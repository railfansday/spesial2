Joe Rogan calls Ilia Topuria vs. Paddy Pimblett a 'big money fight'

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Joe Rogan believes IliaTopuria vs. PaddyPimblett would be one of the Ultimate Fighting Championship's biggest fights and says thepromotion should book it next.Speaking with UFC featherweight TommyMcMillen on "The Joe Rogan Experience," Rogan pointed toPimblett's size, durability and grappling as reasons the matchupwould be compelling.

"That's the big money fight," Rogan said while discussing thepossibility of the two rivals meeting. "That'd be the smart fight.Smart fight would be to do that fight next."Both fighters have lost to JustinGaethje, though Pimblett rebounded by submitting Benoit St.Denis. Rogan cited that performance when assessing Pimblett'schances."He grapples really good, man. Look what he did with Benoit St.Denis," Rogan said. "Paddy's grappling, excuse me, is very, veryunderrated.""Paddy's a big guy," Rogan added. "And he can eat those punches,and he can kind of wear on you, and he grapples really good, man.Look what he did with Benoit. Paddy's grappling is very, veryunderrated. It's just the size difference. Size difference ispretty pronounced."Topuria has said a rematch with Gaethje remains his priority. Theformer two-division champion has also identified Pimblett as apotential opponent if that fight does not materialize.
