Zach Sieler Could Fix Carolina’s Run Defense Before Deadline

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Carolina Panthers are giving up 198.3 rushing yards at home, a number that ranks second-to-last in the entire NFL behind only the Dallas Cowboys. This alarming stat comes after a loss to the Green Bay Packers on Thursday night where the defense collapsed again. While the secondary has four injured corners, the real rot lies up front where the run defense has failed for seven straight weeks. Zach Sieler, a Dolphins defensive lineman, is available.

Sports Illustrated reports the Panthers allow over 140 yards per game, placing them in the bottom three for rushing defense. Two of their home games saw opponents rush for more than 258 yards on the ground, a clear sign that the front seven is broken. Defensive coordinator Ejiro Evero lost his job because of this inability to stop the run. Now the team faces a hard deadline on November 10 at 4 PM ET to find help before the season slips away completely.

Zach Sieler is an established veteran who just signed a three-year extension worth up to $67 million.
