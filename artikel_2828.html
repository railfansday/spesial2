Aaron Rodgers cut by Steelers after torching Mike McCarthy

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Steelers released legendary quarterback Aaron Rodgers after he went scorched earth on head coach Mike McCarthy after getting benched for Friday's game against the Broncos during a rant on "The Pat McAfee Show," according to multiple reports, ending a two-year tenure with the franchise prematurely.

NFL Network's Ian Rapoport reported that the Steelers had initially planned to keep Rodgers after benching him but changed course after the bombshell interview.

Rodgers had slammed the door on any potential return to the Steelers during his appearance on the show.

"At this point, I'm not going back to Pittsburgh," Rodgers said. "That ship has definitely sailed."

Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers (8) reacts after a game at Lincoln Financial Field in Philadelphia on Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

The reunion with McCarthy, with whom he spent 13 seasons in Green Bay and won the Super Bowl in 2011 against the Steelers, came to an explosive end.

"I didn't see this happening. I didn't want it to be the way that it ended," he said.

"… I was surprised that was the way it went. It had a good chance to be one of those really good stories."

Pittsburgh Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy looks on during the first quarter against the Philadelphia Eagles in Philadelphia, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. Getty Images

Rodgers will now go on waivers and can be claimed by another team up until Thursday at 4 p.m. ET. He will become a free agent if he goes unclaimed.

It's unclear if Rodgers will receive interest from other teams, although there are a number of playoff contenders, including the Patriots and Ravens, who could use a quarterback after their starters suffered major injuries.

Rodgers, who turns 43 on Dec. 2, has said all year that this season will be his last in the NFL.

Despite questions from McAfee about a potential return for a 23rd NFL season, Rodgers didn't indicate whether he was considering giving it another go given how his tenure with the Steelers is ending.

Rodgers aired a long list of grievances about McCarthy during his appearance on the popular ESPN program, accusing the coach of falling asleep during meetings, skipping other meetings altogether and failing to prepare for games.

Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers #8 chats with head coach Mike McCarthy before a game against the Philadelphia Eagles on Nov. 22, 2026. Getty Images

The four-time MVP said his breaking point came during halftime of Sunday's loss to the Eagles, when he claimed McCarthy inexplicably broke down the team huddle by saying, "1-2-3, Packers."

Rodgers said he had been privately voicing his concerns to McCarthy for months before confronting him again Monday.

The Steelers shocked the NFL world late Tuesday night by announcing that backup Mason Rudolph would take over as the starting quarterback for Friday's game, with Rodgers set to be inactive, just one day after McCarthy publicly backed Rodgers as his QB1.
