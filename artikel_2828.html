High school football: 5A quarterfinal previews

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

All-time series: Springville leads 5-3 (since 1991)

Last meeting: Bountiful won 36-35 (OT) at Springville (Nov. 3, 2023, 5A quarterfinals)

Springville reached the 5A championship game last year, and it has the defense to make another run. The Red Devils allow 13.9 points per game, third in 5A behind West and Orem. They have held eight of 10 opponents to 14 points or fewer. They have won six straight since their 42-14 loss to Orem in September, including last week's 27-10 first-round win over Olympus. Olympus didn't score in the second half of that game.

Springville's playmakers can change a game quickly. Sam Nadauld opened the second half against Olympus with a 97-yard kickoff return, his second return touchdown of the season. He also has nine touchdown catches and averages 18.3 yards per catch. Siaosi "Leka" Toki leads the team with 637 receiving yards and has eight touchdown catches, and on defense he has five interceptions and a touchdown. Treyton Harris has run for 1,058 yards and nine touchdowns. He also leads the defense with 72 tackles and has three sacks and two interceptions. Beckett Burton has thrown for 1,793 yards and 22 touchdowns. Brooks Johnson leads the defense with five sacks, and the Red Devils have 19 interceptions as a team.

The two teams' common opponents slightly favor Bountiful. Springville lost 38-35 at Alta, and Bountiful won there 29-28. Springville beat Olympus by 17, and Bountiful won 35-0.

To win on the road, Springville has to generate offense against a defense that hasn't allowed a point in three weeks. The Red Devils' offense has struggled at times. They scored nine points against Timpview and 14 against Orem. Harris needs to get going early so Burton isn't throwing into an aggressive Bountiful secondary that has scored three defensive touchdowns. Dave Valeti's team is trying to get back to the semifinals and beyond.

Bountiful's defense is playing as well as any in the state. The Redhawks have shut out their last three opponents: Brighton 38-0, Olympus 35-0 and Granger 47-0 in the first round. They have allowed 54 points in their last six games. They have won eight straight and won Region 6 outright at 5-0. Their only losses came in the first month against two of the best teams outside 5A, 25-14 at Herriman and 37-14 at Morgan. Both of those teams are still alive in the 6A and 3A playoffs. Bountiful also beat two 6A playoff teams, Farmington and Syracuse.

The secondary makes the most plays. Braden Boucha has four interceptions, two defensive touchdowns and a return touchdown. Mason Tidwell has four interceptions and a defensive touchdown, and on offense he averages 20.9 yards per catch with five touchdown catches. Jake Castlberry leads the team with 81 tackles. Manu Tia has 65 tackles and four sacks.

The offense was at its best against Granger. Hudson Malmrose threw four touchdown passes and ran for another, and Carter Brooks added a 59-yard touchdown run. Malmrose has completed 61.8% of his passes for 1,804 yards and 18 touchdowns. Max Feller is his top target, with 38 catches, 603 yards and seven touchdowns. Brooks leads the running game with 528 yards at 6 yards per carry. Bountiful averages 30.4 points per game, sixth in 5A.

The challenge is Springville's special teams and secondary. With Nadauld returning kicks and a defense that has 19 interceptions, Springville can score without a long drive. Bountiful has to win the field-position game. The last time these teams met in the quarterfinals, in 2023, Bountiful won 36-35 in overtime on a two-point conversion and went on to reach the title game. Jason Freckleton's team won the 5A championship a year later. Last season Bountiful lost 14-3 to West in this round. The winner plays the West–Fremont winner.

Last meeting: Fremont won 35-10 at Fremont (Oct. 25, 2019, 6A first round)

No one has found a way to slow West this season, and Fremont has to. The Panthers have outscored opponents 590-59. They have shut out their last four opponents and allowed 13 points in their last six games combined.

Fremont has faced better teams than most 5A clubs. It played two of the top teams in 6A, losing 56-28 to Davis and 49-0 at Corner Canyon. It also beat two 6A playoff teams, 34-3 over Weber and 14-11 at Farmington. The Silverwolves lost the Region 5 title by a single point, 17-16 to Box Elder. The two teams' only common opponent is Roy. Fremont won 55-8, and West won 63-0 in the first round.

Fremont is playing well right now. A month after losing 35-16 at West Field, it beat the Longhorns 43-23 in the first round. Calvin Cooper threw five touchdown passes in that game and ran for another. He has thrown for 1,969 yards and 21 touchdowns. Ky Majors leads the team with eight touchdown catches, and Breckon Phillips averages 20 yards per catch. Jak Masters leads the defense with 11 sacks, and Aisa Tuatagaloa has three interceptions and three sacks.

To pull the upset, Fremont has to keep the score low and limit West's big plays, especially on special teams. Lester Falatea has seven return touchdowns this season. Cooper has to protect the ball against a defense with 16 interceptions and 10 defensive touchdowns. Fremont also has to score on its few good chances, because West's defense won't give it many. The Silverwolves were upset in the quarterfinals last year as the No. 2 seed. This time they're the underdog.

For West, the plan is the same one that has worked all season. The Panthers lead 5A in scoring offense (53.6 ppg) and scoring defense (5.4 ppg). No opponent has scored more than 14 points against them. Their only close games were against out-of-state opponents, 20-14 over Arbor View (Nev.) and 21-13 over Archbishop Riordan (Calif.).

Kamden Lopati leads the offense. He has completed 69.6% of his passes for 2,831 yards and 28 touchdowns, with only four interceptions, and he averages 21.3 yards per completion. He has also run for 661 yards and eight touchdowns, at 11.8 yards per carry, for 36 total touchdowns. Mani Tu'uao has 894 receiving yards and 10 touchdown catches. Naeata Hamilton has run for 872 yards and 12 touchdowns, and Derendrick Dixon has 15 total touchdowns.

Falatea may be the most dangerous player in the state. He has seven return touchdowns, including two punt returns against Roy. He also has six interceptions, three defensive touchdowns and two touchdown catches, for 12 total touchdowns. Takeshi Savery has 12 sacks and Dieter Lotulelei has 11, part of a 41-sack defense.

The challenge is the opponent. Fremont has been in more close games against good teams than anyone West has played. West's last game decided by fewer than five touchdowns was in August. Last year the Panthers lost 34-7 to Orem in the semifinals. Olosaa Solovi's team is chasing the school's first state title since 1992. The winner plays the Bountiful–Springville winner.

All-time series: Orem leads 43-15-3 (since 1931)

Last meeting: Orem won 35-10 at Orem (Oct. 2, 2026, Region 7)

Pleasant Grove got even with one team last week, and it wants to do the same with another this week. In September the Vikings lost 28-24 at Spanish Fork when a late fumble rolled out of the end zone. Last week they went back to Spanish Fork and won 34-10. Corbin Robbins ran for a touchdown and caught another, Cannon Jacobson threw two touchdown passes, and Davian Akwenye put the game away with a 55-yard interception return. Now the Vikings are back at Orem, where they lost 35-10 five weeks ago.

That game was closer than the score suggests. Pleasant Grove led 10-7 until Orem scored with 54 seconds left in the first half. After that, the Tigers scored 28 straight points. The Vikings need to play that first half again and then avoid the turnovers and stalled drives that let Orem take over.

Defense gives Pleasant Grove a chance. The Vikings allow 19.6 points per game, eighth in 5A. They have 23 interceptions and five defensive touchdowns, more of both than any team in these 5A previews. Jake Bills has five interceptions. Ethan Passey has four interceptions and a defensive touchdown. Akwenye has three interceptions, and all three became touchdowns. Clayton Lignell leads the team with 113 tackles, and Jaxon Mortenson has four sacks. Pleasant Grove has also beaten good teams outside its region, shutting out 6A Westlake 14-0 and winning 27-23 at 4A Green Canyon.

The Vikings need big plays to score. Robbins has 14 total touchdowns: eight rushing, two receiving, one on defense and three on returns. Ryder Huntsman has 54 catches for 670 yards. He has also thrown two touchdown passes and has two interceptions. Ben Lyman has made eight field goals. To win, Pleasant Grove has to protect the ball. Jacobson has thrown 10 interceptions, and Orem has scored five defensive touchdowns. A year ago Pleasant Grove lost in the first round. Jeremy Lapan's team has already won a road playoff game, and an upset of the defending champion would get it to the semifinals.

Orem needs to keep doing what has worked for seven straight weeks. The Tigers are the defending 5A champions. They've won seven straight since a 32-26 loss to Mater Dei (Calif.) in August and averaged 48 points over their last four games. Their only other loss was 21-14 at Skyridge, the 6A No. 1 seed. Orem allows 13.3 points per game, second in 5A behind West, and scores 35.5, third. It won Region 7 outright at 6-0.

The common opponents favor Orem. The Tigers beat Springville 42-14 and Spanish Fork 48-0. Pleasant Grove lost to Springville 34-8 and split with Spanish Fork. Orem also beat Maple Mountain 47-0 and Payson 55-28. Pleasant Grove's best result against a common opponent was a 28-21 win over Timpview, a team Orem beat by only three points in September.

Tanner Lewis threw five touchdown passes in last week's 55-7 first-round win over Timpview, four of them to Brooks Ross. Lewis has completed 62.5% of his passes for 1,480 yards and 20 touchdowns and has thrown one interception all season. Ross averages 18.1 yards per catch and has 11 touchdown catches. Po Faumui has 43 catches, seven touchdown catches and two defensive touchdowns. Beckham Curtis has run for 1,295 yards and 12 touchdowns at 8.8 yards per carry. Krew Jones, who scored twice in the first meeting, is a short-yardage option.

The defense has 33 sacks. Major Stokes has nine, Jag Ioane has eight and Jones has five. Kaio Akana has four interceptions and a defensive touchdown, and JaVoni Halalilo has two interceptions and two defensive scores.

Orem has to protect the ball against a defense that turns interceptions into points. Lewis has made that easy all season. In his first year at Orem, Andy Stokes is trying to lead the program to a second straight title. It would be Orem's ninth state title and sixth since 2017. The winner plays the Alta–Woods Cross winner.

All-time series: Alta leads 4-1 (since 1988)

Last meeting: Alta won 27-21 at Woods Cross (Sept. 25, 2026, Region 6)

Woods Cross nearly had its first-round win at Box Elder wrapped up. The Wildcats led through three quarters on two Sam Bahr touchdown runs and held the Region 5 champions scoreless. Then Box Elder came back in the fourth. Judd Howells ran 3 yards for a touchdown, Josh Pugsley returned a kickoff 94 yards, and Kadyn Reyes scored from the 1 on the final play of regulation to tie it. Tytan Hupp's 32-yard field goal put Box Elder ahead in overtime. Bahr answered with a 2-yard touchdown run, and the No. 14 seed won 29-26.

That's how Woods Cross has played all season. All five of its wins came by eight points or fewer: 27-26 over 6A Syracuse, 26-21 over West Field, 60-52 at Brighton, 36-35 over Viewmont and now Box Elder. Its losing record comes from a hard schedule. Five of its six losses came against playoff teams, including 4A seeds Stansbury and Snow Canyon.

The Wildcats nearly beat Alta six weeks ago, too. They led 7-6 going into the fourth quarter and were ahead again in the final minutes, until Lucas Pagel caught a 49-yard touchdown pass with 2:02 left. Xavier Cocci added a 15-yard run, and Tage Ita's 1-yard touchdown with 12 seconds left came too late.

Woods Cross wins by running the ball. Bahr has 11 rushing touchdowns, and he also has 60 tackles and an interception on defense. Ita has run for 522 yards and nine touchdowns and thrown for eight more. Bronson Martin averages 7.9 yards per carry, and Teiyon Halbasch averages 24.5 yards per catch. Abram Webre leads the defense with 95 tackles. Brody Rogers has six sacks and Napa'a Tuakiapimahu has five. Wyatt Hunter has two interceptions and a return touchdown.

To pull another upset, Woods Cross needs another close game in the fourth quarter. Ita also has to protect the ball. He has thrown 10 interceptions, and Alta's defense has 13 interceptions and five defensive touchdowns. Last year the Wildcats shared the Region 6 title and reached this round, losing 35-7 at Orem. Brody Benson, who won a state title at Highland in 2010, has them one win from the semifinals.

Alta's defense made Hillcrest's high-scoring offense look ordinary last week. Hillcrest came in averaging 39 points a game with a quarterback who had thrown 36 touchdown passes, and it scored seven in a 37-7 loss. After a scoreless first quarter, the Hawks scored 30 straight points in the second. Pagel ran for two touchdowns, Cocci threw a 12-yard touchdown pass to Ryland Clayton and ran 8 yards for another, and Hillcrest didn't score in the second half.

Alta has won three straight. It allows 17.8 points per game, seventh in 5A, and its defense has 34 sacks. Charlie Gates has 89 tackles and eight sacks, and Mason Albrecht and Kolt Handren have five sacks each. Thomas Deckart has 103 tackles, three interceptions and two defensive touchdowns. Mason Clark has 98 tackles and a defensive touchdown.

Alta also has the better schedule. It beat 5A quarterfinalist Springville 38-35, 6A Bingham 13-12 and Olympus 23-10. Its losses came against 6A quarterfinalists Davis (27-6) and Syracuse (28-7) and against Region 6 champion Bountiful, 29-28. Most common opponents favor the Hawks. Alta lost to Bountiful by one point, and Woods Cross lost 28-7. Alta beat Olympus by 13, while Woods Cross lost to the Titans 27-6. Alta also beat Viewmont and Brighton far more easily. Syracuse is the exception: it beat Alta by 21, and Woods Cross beat it by one.

Cocci leads the offense. He has thrown for 1,637 yards and 12 touchdowns, and he leads the team with 713 rushing yards and nine rushing touchdowns. Pagel has 11 total touchdowns: seven rushing, two receiving, one on defense and one on a return. Clayton averages 19.9 yards per catch and has five touchdown catches, plus two interceptions and a defensive score. Treven Cooke has 548 rushing yards. Madden McCallister has made seven field goals.

The concern is finishing. Woods Cross led Alta late in September and led Box Elder for most of last Friday, and the Wildcats don't go away. Alta lost in the second round last year. In his third season, Blake Burdette has the Hawks one win from their first semifinal since 2023, when they lost to Bountiful, and closer to the program's first state title since 2008. The winner plays the Orem–Pleasant Grove winner.
