There are worse backup plans than Beaux Limmer

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The starting center for the Los Angeles Rams, Coleman Shelton, is expected to miss four to six weeks with a severe hamstring injury.

He will be replaced by Beaux Limmer, who watched Shelton take his job following a painful end to his Limmer's rookie season.

At one point, Limmer looked like a draft steal. LA swiped the former Arkansas Razorback in the sixth round. He went on to start 14 games and play 1,000 snaps as a rookie, including in the divisional round game against the Philadelphia Eagles.

2025: What do the Rams truly have in Beaux Limmer?

And that is where Limmer's reputation quickly shifted from draft steal to a problem that needed upgraded. On the final two plays of an all-important playoff matchup, Limmer left Jalen Carter unblocked for quick pressures on Matthew Stafford. The result? Two incomplete passes and the Rams flying back to Los Angeles with their heads slumped in defeat.

As a consequence, the Rams prioritized a reunion with Shelton in free agency. Limmer was a nice surprise. He could return to the starting lineup one day. But center is an important position and growing pains for a young player could have jeopardized the remaining window of Stafford's career.

It's a full-circle story. The truth is there are worse backup plans than Limmer. He's capable. In front of him is an opportunity to grab the starting job. This time he might not let go.

Shelton has been an above average starter for LA. Left guard Steve Avila missed the most recent game against the Cardinals.

Limmer earned the start at left guard. When Shelton came out, backup Dylan McMahon lasted nine plays before suffering a significant injury of his own. Limmer slid inside. Bill Murray came in at left guard.

According to Pro Football Focus (PFF), Limmer played 76 snaps and earned a near-elite pass blocking grade of 88.8. He did not allow a single pressure. The run game was also marked at a plus level with a grade of 70.4 for a net offensive grade of 75.5. For comparison, that edges out Shelton's season offensive grade of 74.7.

Limmer is 25 years old. He wasn't a finished product as a rookie. If what he put on tape through 1,000 snaps in 2024 was enough for us to be excited then, it would be foolish to write him off as a future starter moving forward. The Rams have a surplus of players they must re-sign over the next couple of offseasons. Limmer is under contract through 2027.

Limmer holding onto the starting job for the remainder of the season and proving he deserves the job in 2027 would be a welcome development for the Rams.
