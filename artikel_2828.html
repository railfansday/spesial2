Kyrie Irving showed love to his former fan, Warriors rookie Yaxel Lendeborg

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Kyrie Irving showed love to his former fan, Warriors rookie Yaxel Lendeborg originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area

The Warriors continue to struggle without star guard Steph Curry, losing their fourth straight game on Wednesday, a 116-94 loss to the Dallas Mavericks.

Warriors rookie Yaxel Lendeborg, who scored 13 points with five rebounds and four assists in 30 minutes, came into the game with extra motivation, in large part, because he faced his childhood idol, Mavericks guard Kyrie Irving.

Irving and Lendeborg have built a mutual respect in recent months, which led to Irving gifting Lendeborg a signed jersey after the game as a symbol of his appreciation for the Warriors rookie's lifelong support.

Speaking to the San Francisco Chronicle's Luca Evans after the game, Irving praised Lendeborg, while declaring his admiration for his game.

"We had a conversation in the summertime after he got drafted," Irving said. "He's been an advocate and a supporter of Kyrie FC, it's like a fan club that I have, for a minute. But just unconditional support, unconditional love before he got into the league. That was cool. I didn't really know much about him. I watched him in college a little bit and fell in love with his game and his skill set and what he's able to do out there. And obviously, we have [guard Morez Johnson Jr.] here as well like, both of them playing for the championship team.

"It's interesting to see the dynamics. But yeah, it was great to play against him finally. I could tell he was a little nervous. I think he's learning on the fly and he has a lot of pressure on his back right now to be the number one guy, number two guy. I know he has a lot of pressure on his shoulders so I just hope he takes it easy a little bit."

Lendeborg, the No. 11 overall pick in the 2026 NBA Draft, spoke to the media after his selection, and said that as a fan of Irving, he used to "hate" Curry. However, as one of his newest teammates, he has learned to admire and learn from both.

Lendeborg's first season has been impressive, with the 24-year-old among the early Rookie of the Year candidates after averaging 20.3 points, 7.3 rebounds and 3.5 assists per game in 15 contests.

He has multiple 30-point games, and his early season success has cemented his status as Golden State's second star to complement Curry.

Lendeborg and the Warriors face a quick turnaround, traveling to Memphis to take on the Grizzlies on Thursday night. Tip-off is at 5 p.m. PT at FedEx Forum.
