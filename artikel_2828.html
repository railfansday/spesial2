Adonai Mitchell pulls a disappearing act with zero grabs in Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

New York Jets WR Adonai Mitchell had no catches in seven targets in Week 9 against the Kansas City Chiefs.

Why It Matters: Coming off a career day with seven receptions for 142 yards and a touchdown against Las Vegas last week, Mitchell was shut out against the Chiefs. It's hard for fantasy managers to figure out this Jets pass offense; Omar Cooper Jr. saw no targets last week (after recording back-to-back 100-yard games) and 11 this week; Mitchell tallied seven receptions last week and none this week. Mitchell's donut is even harder to explain with Garrett Wilson missing this game due to injury. Mitchell should remain on fantasy managers' radar, but he cannot be considered anything more than an upside WR5 - even against the Bills in Week 10.
