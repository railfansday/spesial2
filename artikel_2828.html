Celtics Must Win Friday To Secure Home Court In NBA Cup Playoffs

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Boston Celtics must win their Friday night game against the Atlanta Hawks to secure the top spot in NBA Cup Group C. A victory clinches the best record in the Eastern Conference for the tournament, which guarantees home-court advantage for the quarterfinals and semifinals. The stakes are high for a team that has traveled all month and needs to find its rhythm at TD Garden. Per The Boston Globe, the Celtics are 3-0 in the Cup with a massive plus-68 point differential, while the Hawks sit at 2-1 but could steal the top seed with a win.

The Celtics have a clear path to the knockout round, but the Hawks are riding a four-game winning streak and are hungry to prove they belong. Atlanta has won eight of their last 10 games and is averaging 116.7 points per game, according to The Boston Globe. The Hawks also outscored their opponents by 29 points in the fourth quarter during their recent run. That late-game surge makes them a threat even if the Celtics build a big early lead.

Playing at TD Garden is the key factor for Boston right now. The team has spent November on the road, and their play has sputtered due to the heavy travel schedule. Per CelticsBlog, locking up home court would allow the Celtics to regain a sense of normalcy and routine while they push toward the playoffs. The difference between hosting games in Boston and playing on the road in the quarterfinals is huge. If the Celtics lose Friday, they would still likely advance as a wild card, but they would face a harder road with away games for the next two rounds.

Paul George addressed the team's recent struggles after the win over the Wizards on Tuesday. He noted that the team had great looks but missed shots during their losing skid. "We shook the rust off," George said. "We shot the ball with confidence." The Celtics need that same confidence against Atlanta. Jayson Tatum led the team with 41 points in the last game, and he is averaging 27.3 points per game, which ranks fifth in the NBA. The Celtics' defense is also a major factor, as they allow only 102.9 points per game compared to Atlanta's 113.6.

The Hawks are without center Jock Landale due to a hip injury and guard CJ McCollum because of a calf issue. Boston has its own concerns, as Mitchell Robinson is listed as questionable with an illness. Despite these absences, the Hawks have shown they can score in bunches, outscoring Detroit 33-12 in the final 12 minutes of their last game. The Celtics must stop Atlanta's late-game runs to secure the win.

Atlanta relies on perimeter defense and quick transitions, with Dyson Daniels proving to be one of the best defenders in the league. Boston relies on a balanced offense and superior shooting, hitting 36.2 percent from three-point range. The Celtics need to execute their offensive sets without turning the ball over. If they can control the pace, they should be able to handle the Hawks' pressure. The game tips off at 8 p.m. on Friday, and the outcome will decide who leads the group.

Boston has a clear opportunity to make history in this young tournament. A win would keep the Celtics as the only unbeaten team in Eastern Conference group play. Even if they lose, they hold a massive point differential that could save them as a wild card. However, the cleanest path to the title is to win Friday and keep the home court.
