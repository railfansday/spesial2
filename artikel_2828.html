'A shared vision': LASA boys pace each other to Class 5A UIL state cross-country title

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

ROUND ROCK - Over two cool, breezy days at Old Settler's Park, more than 1,500 of the state's best high school distance runners competed in the UIL state cross-country championships. And while there were a number of standout performances from Central Texas runners, it was Austin's LASA Raptors who rose to the pinnacle, capturing first place in Saturday's Class 5A race.

The Raptors were led by senior Liam Brandyberry, who finished seventh overall in 15 minutes, 28 seconds on the rolling 5K course.

"It took preparation, drive, commitment, character and courage to do what they had to do today, which is not easy," LASA head cross-country coach Mary Beth Metcalf said of the boys team, which also consisted of seniors Joaquin Gonzalez, John Wilson, Cruz Alcorta and Hugo Radjef, and juniors Ben Vazquez and Sam Smith.

"Not so much a tactical approach, it was more about being committed to one another," Metcalf added. "During the race, when it feels overwhelming, when they feel like maybe they can't hang on any longer, when their brains are telling them to let up, I ask them to think about one another and the love that they have for their teammates, and to let that teammate commitment be what drives them forward. Our team has a shared commitment and obligation to will it into reality. It's a shared vision."

Only the top five runners from each team have their finishing places added together to calculate the team score; the team with the lowest total score wins. LASA scored 94 points, ahead of runner-up Weslaco East's 98 and third-place Dallas Wilson's 113. Weslaco East's Alexander Nava won the overall race in 15 minutes, 13 seconds, while Georgetown's Matthew Morales averaged 4:56 per mile to grab second in 15:18. Liberty Hill's Zander Radziwon was 11th (15:40) and Lockhart's Ethan Herrera (15:52) was 19th.

"These boys, since they've been freshmen, they've always felt like a team and worked together like a team," LASA principal Stacia Crescenzi said. "What makes this so emotional today is not that they won, but that they did it together."

In Friday's Class 6A meet, Vandegrift's Avinash Dewagan had a lot of momentum leading up to the state meet after winning both the district and Region III-6A meets and it continued at state. The sophomore took second overall (14:39) behind Denton Guyer's nationally-ranked junior, Ruel Newberry (14:36).

El Paso Eastwood won the 6A boys race ahead of Southlake Carroll and Keller.

"Coming in second to Newbury is a pretty big deal," Vandegrift coach Colin Sully said. "No question, Avinash pulled our team to fifth place. Though we placed second here at state the past four years, I'm happy with how the boys team ran today."

Flower Mound won the 6A girls race ahead of Conroe The Woodlands and Southlake Carroll. The Vandegrift girls, led by senior Caroline Krupa, placed 11th.

In addition to Dewagan, Round Rock's Andrew Esparza also ran a solid race, taking sixth in 14:52. Other notable finishers included Bowie's Cooper Ford (22nd in 15:34), and Round Rock's Zachary Stevens (25th in 15:39). Lake Travis' Olivia Laudicina was the top Central Texas girls finisher in Class 6A, coming in 17th (18:22). Stony Point's Sophia Lopez placed 22nd in 18:29.
