Midseason 2027 TV Schedules Revealed After Several Changes

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The midseason 2027 TV schedule is stacked — but when is every show coming back after several surprise changes and reduced episode orders?

Fox surprised viewers by confirming that Best Medicine will have a reduced 14 episodes in season 2, which will allow The Interrogator to take over in February.

Meanwhile, over on CBS, Cupertino is moving to Sundays while Eternally Yours is switching to Mondays. Ghosts will air twice a week on Mondays and Thursdays, and Survivor will get newly extended two-hour episodes. FBI and CIA are getting a new time slot as well on Mondays while Sheriff Country has a shift one hour over on Fridays.
