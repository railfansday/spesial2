'DWTS' Champ Ezra Frech Says He 'Blacked Out' During Historic Freestyle

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Ezra Frech made history as the first amputee and youngest man to win Dancing With the Stars alongside partner Daniella Karagach — but the victory still hasn’t set in.

“Last night was crazy. … I can’t believe this happened,” Frech, 21, said on Good Morning America just hours after the season 35 finale aired on Tuesday, November 24. “I can’t believe we got that opportunity to represent [the disabled community].”

For the season finale, the remaining five couples performed three separate dances, including the fan-favorite freestyle. Frech and Karagach, 33, earned a combined score of 90 out of 90 from judges Carrie Ann Inaba, Bruno Tonioli and Derek Hough, landing them in a three-way tie with Amber Glenn and Pasha Pashkov and Maura Higgins and Mark Ballas.

Before being announced as the winners, Frech and Karagach experienced a mishap in their live freestyle when the Paralympian struggled to get his prosthetic blade onto his leg.

“We started our freestyle without the prosthetic, that was a creative choice, and then things went a little off script,” Frech said on GMA. “I wasn’t able to suction my leg on and I knew it wasn’t on. Sometimes when that happens, it’ll re-suction, but it didn’t. It came off. She told me to put it on mid-dance, I kinda blacked out.”

He continued, “When it came off, I was like, ‘I’m doing the rest of the dance without my leg on.’ And I heard [Daniella] go, ‘Put your leg on!'”

Karagach added, “I saw his face, I was like, ‘If I don’t tell him to put his leg on, he’s gonna do the whole dance without it.'”

When asked how he planned on celebrating his historic win, Frech said, “The first thing I’m gonna do is just go home and be with my family, probably, and get some nice relaxation time. I mean, everybody on the cast knows, it’s a crazy ride and there’s so much that goes into it.”

He added, “But honestly, the celebration was getting to spend the night on the private jet coming here with all you guys. I love this cast so much, I have so much respect and admiration for each and every single one of you. We’ve become so close … and so we had a lot of fun on the plane tonight.”

Prior to the finale, some of the finalists admitted they experienced nerves leading up to the finale and questioned whether they would make it to the last episode.

“I was waiting for them to finish the sentence,” Connor Wood said in an exclusive interview with Us after the November 17 semifinals while reflecting on hearing his and Rylee Arnold‘s names called first. “I go, ‘What do we do? You want us to dance again? You want us to do our dance again, you sick freaks?'”

Following the finale, Wood, 31, gushed over the connection he formed with Frech throughout the competition. “If I was a betting man — and God, I wish I had — I would’ve made a lot of money tonight. … He’s been my little brother since day one, did not need an introduction. We immediately just, like, connected,” he told Extra. “A lot like Rylee and I, honestly.”

Wood continued, “I see him, I see his perseverance, the way he carries himself. He talks me through stuff. … Just seeing him tonight, that’s a winner.”

Dancing With the Stars airs on ABC and Disney+ Tuesdays at 8 p.m. ET.
