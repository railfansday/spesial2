Big JUCO OL Godspower Otuonye commits to Oregon State, 'it felt like family'

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Fullerton JC (Calif.) offensive lineman Godspower Otuonye has committed to Oregon State and breaks down why he chose the Beavers.

Otuonye visited Oregon State back in October and the Beavers have been trending for the big tackle ever since.

"There were a lot of reasons why I chose Oregon State," Otuonye said. "The biggest was the family feel I felt on my visit. When I was there, it really felt like I was a part of the family already and that's what I was looking for.

"I also really like the coaching staff and especially Coach (Kirk) Barron, the OL coach. He gets after it and is a very passionate about what he does. He's an awesome guy and I'm excited to play for him."

Oregon State had been watching Otuonye since the Spring and offered in early October.

"Coach Barron came and visited our school in the Spring," Otuonye said. "I had a chance to talk with him and he told me I have the potential to play at the highest level but I had to show it this season. I went out and did it and now I'm a Beaver.

"I really like the fit in the offense. It's similar to what we run here at Fullerton but at a higher level. They're bringing me in as a tackle and I'm a qualifier so I'll be able to graduate at semester and I'll be there for Spring practice. I'm very thankful for the opportunity and excited to get going."

Otuonye is an intriguing player because of his length and natural athleticism. He's a former basketball player, and you can see it in how fluid he moves. At 6-foot-6, 315 pounds, he has a prototype frame and some real pop at the point of contact. As he continues to fill out and get stronger, he has a chance to be a real impact player for the Beavers immediately.
