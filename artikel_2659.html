5 Chargers players to watch in Week 11 vs. Jets

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Chargers are looking to get their second win of the season after grabbing their first in Week 10 against the Ravens.

Now, they'll look for their first win in front of their home crowd at SoFi Stadium on Sunday. The Chargers are home favorites in the contest and will look to build on the win after what's been a disastrous start to the 2026 season.

Los Angeles hopes to turn the tide, and as they do, here are the five players we'll be watching in this Week 11 matchup.

Herbert isn't coming off the best performance of the season, but he did take care of the ball. However, if the Chargers want a shot at a second win, Herbert may need to be a little better than he was against the Ravens. Can Herbert be more productive through the air while still protecting the football? The Chargers need to see their quarterback play complementary football instead of forcing the offense to rely entirely on the running game.

Hampton was the star of the show in Week 10. He was the cowbell for the Chargers' backfield, a position many thought he'd have in his bag since Week 1. Better late than never, as they say. The question now is whether Hampton can take advantage of the Jets' run defense struggles and give the Chargers another balanced offensive performance.

The Chargers cannot afford to let Herbert consistently face pressure, especially against a defense that can create turnovers; that's where Alt comes in. Alt is vital to the offense, particularly with the Chargers dealing with significant injuries along their offensive line. The Jets have one of the NFL's more promising young pass rushers in rookie David Bailey. Alt needs to be able to hold up in pass protection, keep Herbert clean and help the Chargers establish the line of scrimmage.

Tomlinson is coming off a stellar performance against the Ravens; the question is: Can he do it again? The Chargers will need another strong performance from their defensive front against a Jets team with multiple capable running backs that could look to establish the ground game.

McConkey hasn't had the season many envisioned for him. Still, he's a player to watch because his availability could make a big difference for the Chargers' offense. He's missed the last two games with a back injury, and as a result, the Chargers have struggled to consistently generate offense through the air. His return could give Herbert a reliable target who can create separation and make plays after the catch.
