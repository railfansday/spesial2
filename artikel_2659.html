Broncos activate running back to 53-man roster ahead of Steelers game

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Denver Broncos have activated running back Tyler Badie (thumb) from injured reserve to the 53-man roster ahead of Friday's showdown with the Pittsburgh Steelers in Week 12, according to KUSA-TV's Mike Klis. Denver had an open spot for Badie on the active roster after fellow running back J.K. Dobbins was officially placed on the reserve/suspended list on Wednesday.

Badie (5-8, 197 pounds) is now poised to serve as the team's No. 2 running back behind rookie Jonah Coleman against the Steelers. The 26-year-old veteran has been with the Broncos since 2022, rushing 26 times for 131 yards in 25 career games. Badie has also hauled in 29 receptions for 230 yards and a touchdown, and he has returned 21 kickoffs for 557 yards.

Badie is a skilled pass blocker and a capable receiver out of the backfield, which makes him an ideal third-down back in obvious passing situations. He will also serve as a key special teams player for Denver.
