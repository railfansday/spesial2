Europa League Liveblog: Milan-Ferencvaros, AZ Alkmaar-Juventus, Atalanta-Kairat

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Join us for all the build-up and action as it happens from the Europa League and Conference League with Milan vs. Ferencvaros, Juventus away to AZ Alkmaar, and Atalanta in crisis against Kairat Almaty.

We begin at 17.45 GMT (18.45 CET) with two games, as Milan try to build on their Derby della Madonnina success and get back on track in Europe with Ferencvaros.

Atalanta have just two draws in five competitive matches, so cannot slip up against Kairat Almaty.

At 20.00 GMT (21.00 CET), Juventus take a trip to AZ Alkmaar and aim to keep strong Europa League form going, as the Koopmeiners brothers face off.

The Atalanta wait for a victory drags on to seven matches, as they scraped a 1-1 draw with Kairat Almaty in the Conference League thanks to Nikola Krstovic.

It falls to Pasalic, who is the wrong man to score from there and he fires off target!

Milan risk crashing out of the Europa League after a third defeat in four games, capitulating 2-0 at home to leaders Ferencvaros with a Lenny Joseph brace.

Another horrible defensive error gifts it to Madarasz, who dribbles around Torriani and can only find the side-netting…

Into 4 minutes of added time in Bergamo too.

Hutchinson is the last to give up for Milan, cross punched away, then dinked pass for Rabiot, who can’t turn under pressure.

Mrynskiy booked for a late sliding tackle on Zalewski.

Zachariassen and Birmancevic off, Levi and Maiga on

Hutchinson is trying too, his effort is deflected wide of the near post for a corner.

Actual Milan chance with Hutchinson! Low drive parried by the goalkeeper’s outstretched boot!
