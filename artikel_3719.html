While GTA 6 comes out in just a few days, Rockstar Games has remained a little quiet about the 34 songs that make up Grand Theft Auto 6: The Album, but a recent leak seems to have revealed the mystery artists included on the tracklist.

A new video seemingly recorded from a music record store shows off the front and back of the Grand Theft Auto 6: The Album vinyl. We already knew the star-studded album would feature the likes of Travis Scott, Cardi B, Pinkpantheress, SZA and Keith Richards - and you can now add Dua Lipa, Blackpink's Jennie, Lana Del Rey and Zara Larsson to the list of celebs showing up in full force.

Grand Theft Auto 6: The Album isn't to be confused with the GTA 6 original score, which is being composed by dance duo Justice and looks to populate the crime epic with the heavy, bouncy synths we've come to know Vice City for. Of course, the in-game radio stations will also feature a wide range of other real-world tracks as is series tradition.
