Stuttgart have no plans to sell Bilal El Khannouss in January despite interest from Leeds United, sources have told Football Insider.

The 22-year-old still has a key role to play as the Bundesliga club look to continue their progress both domestically and in the Champions League after the turn of the year.

Despite suggestions elsewhere that he could be made available when the mid-season transfer window opens, sources say he is still an important patt of Stuttgart's plans.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, it will be very difficult for Leeds or any of El Khannouss' other suitors to agree terms in Janaury.

A move next summer may be considered more likely, but with plenty of water to pass under the bridge between now and then, he looks set to remain at Stuttgart for now.

Leeds have been long-term admirers of El Khannouss and have continued to keep tabs on his situation, but unless their plans change, he will not be made available in January.

Football Insider revealed on 13 November that Leeds could reignite their interest in El Khannouss when the January window opens, having been long-term admirers of his.

The Whites have been keeping a close eye on the attacking midfielder's progress in the Bundesliga, and could finally look to bring him to Elland Road in 2027.

However, Stuttgart are set to stand firm on their stance that he is not for sale, and will not entertain a mid-season exit as he remains a big part of their long-term plans.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I think it's a real difficult deal to do in January. He's still playing regularly for Stuttgart in the Bundesliga at the moment.

"Stuttgart are competing in the Champions League so I'm sure they don't want to lose somebody like El Khannouss midway through the season.

"Especially if they've got Champions League football to look forward to in the new year, he's going to be crucial to their progress in that competition.

"It's a long-term interest that Leeds have had in him but just think with Stuttgart competing well in the Bundesliga and the Champions League it'll be a very hard deal to do.

"I think obviously his price tag would be quite prohibitive as he's under a long-term contract at Stuttgart as well, so it might be one Leeds have to revisit later down the line."

As previously mentioned, Leeds have been long-term admirers of El Khannouss, and previously missed out when he opted to leave Leicester City in 2025.

The Whites were among a host of Premeir League clubs keen on a deal, but were putt off by Leicester's valuation, before his move to the Bundesliga came at a reduced price.

Meanwhile, Football Insider previously revealed that Leeds were preparing to reignite their interest in El Khannouss if he was made available before the window closed.

However, he opted to remain at Stuttgart while the Bundesliga club were happy to keep hold of him, ending any speculation about a move to the Premier League.

That stance is now unlikely to have changed ahaed of the summer window, and Leeds may have to pause their plans regarding El Khannouss for a third time.
