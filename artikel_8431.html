We both have Medicare part A&B this past year and I had some concerns answered with a Aetna specialist during open season regarding their Advantage plan that gave me the confidence to sign up. It’s been truly amazing both in covered services with zero out of pocket expenses and customer service. I never saw a bill the entire year and I had 4 different specialist, an endoscopy, ultra sound, stress test combo with an ultrasound and blood work. This year because my wife has her own insurance funded by her old company I will pay the Postal Plan costs of 131.00 and 100.00 a month of that goes towards reducing Medicare PartB. I can’t speak about the prescription plan because I don’t take any although they try. It’s a national plan too. I have advised others outside of the federal government to never take an advantage plan for various reasons and instead to pick a supplement plan, this Aetna federal advantage plan I believe doesn’t have some of those concerns. This was my experience but it might not work for others.
