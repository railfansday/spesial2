Football: Pahokee gets revenge, ends John Carroll’s season in Regiom Semis

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

FORT PIERCE — The marathon that was the Region 4-1A semifinal between John Carroll Catholic and Pahokee finally reached its conclusion.

Originally scheduled for Friday, the game officially kicked off at 6 p.m. Saturday and ran its final play at 4:17 p.m. Monday. That final play was a Blue Devils kneel-down, with their fans blowing air horns and chanting, "We got our get-back."

Pahokee advanced to the regional final and ended the Rams' season with a 20-14 victory Monday afternoon at McGuire Stadium. The teams played the second half Monday after completing the first half in a 14-14 tie Saturday.

Uzarus Boswell proved to be the difference. The senior athlete scored the game-winning touchdown on a fourth-and-goal plunge from the John Carroll 1-yard line with 3 minutes, 29 seconds remaining in the fourth quarter.

Postponed Friday because of the threat of severe weather, the game was suspended Saturday following lightning delays totaling more than three hours and a torrential downpour that flooded the sidelines and rendered the field unplayable.

"We've been battle-tested all year," Pahokee head coach Carlos Lammons said. "Lately, we've been falling short, so the message was to finish this week. We got to finish games."

With the ball in its own territory following a Laquiorian Miles interception, the Blue Devils milked the clock in the fourth quarter with a lengthy drive, covering 55 yards in 14 plays. The drive was extended by seven combined penalties between the two teams.

Pahokee marched to the John Carroll 31 before back-to-back holding penalties made it first-and-30 from the Pahokee 49. The Blue Devils managed to get to fourth-and-10, but consecutive Rams offsides penalties gave the visitors a fresh set of downs.

Following a tackle for loss by Jamal Edwards and a false-start penalty, Pahokee (8-4) faced second-and-goal from the John Carroll 20. But a pair of Mari Singleton rushes and two Rams penalties led to fourth-and-goal from the 1.

Boswell was knocked out of the game with an injury in the third quarter. But with the game on the line, Lammons called the team's final timeout and drew up a play for the senior captain. The Blue Devils opted against a short field-goal attempt after missing a 30-yarder in the third quarter.

Boswell's number was called, and he delivered.

"We're going to put it in our leader's hands," Lammons said. "He's a four-year player on varsity. He's got the most experience. So if we're going to go down, we're going to go down on his shoulders."

John Carroll head coach and offensive playcaller Mickey Groody signaled for "Spartan," the team's heavy offensive package featuring senior tailback Jaydien Pittman taking the direct snap and carrying the ball.

The Rams managed two first downs running that package after starting on their own 30-yard line, but without any timeouts, they had to reinsert junior quarterback Carter Cullen and try to move the ball through the air. Cullen then threw three incompletions, resulting in a turnover on downs.

The Blue Devils were the No. 3 seed in the region, while the Rams were seeded second. John Carroll won at Pahokee, 23-20, in a District 10-1A game Sept. 25. By getting their revenge, the Blue Devils advanced to face No. 1 Miami True North on Friday for the region title.

Meanwhile, the Rams (7-4) have a district title and regional semifinal appearance to show for a successful 2026 season. However, in their first completed game in a month, John Carroll came up just short.

"I'm not making any excuses or anything like that, but with the adversity we've faced, I'm just super proud of our guys and how they battled," Groody said. "Playing two quarters the other night, coming back and getting ready today to battle another two quarters, I couldn't be prouder."
