NFL Week 11: Stress-free Sunday open thread

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Washington Commanders have another primetime game this week so that means another Stress-free Sunday to sit back, relax, and watch other teams lose. The will be back in action tomorrow night, hosting the Cincinnati Bengals on Monday Night Football.

There are plenty of games to watch today, including all three of Washington's NFC East rivals. The division-leading New York Giants are hosting the red-hot Jacksonville Jaguars later in the afternoon. The Dallas Cowboys will try to not get embarrassed at home by the Tennessee Titans. The Philadelphia Eagles' tank season continues with a Battle of PA matchup against the Pittsburgh Steelers.
