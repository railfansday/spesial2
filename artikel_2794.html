‘I can’t wait’: Chad Johnson says what he’s going to do to Luka Doncic when he comes to Miami

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Former NFL star Chad 'Ochocinco' Johnson has continued speaking about Los Angeles Lakers superstar Luka Doncic.

It all started when the 48-year-old claimed that he would lock down the Slovenian if he played in the NBA and accused other players of not watching enough film.

Then, Johnson shared a screenshot of his direct message to Doncic on Instagram, challenging him to a one-on-one in June.

Now, the Cincinnati Bengals legend has revealed his next plan.

Johnson has been taunting Doncic but has received no reply from the Los Angeles Lakers star.

Yet, the former Cincinnati Bengals wide receiver has shown no willingness to stop, and his next plan is to have his say face-to-face.

Ochocinco has revealed that he has bought courtside tickets for the Lakers' game against the Miami Heat at the Kaseya Center. He says he will sit right next to the bench.

He said on the Nightcap Show: "I can't wait for him to come here. You see how I was talking trash to Anthony Edwards? Wait until the Lakers come here.

"I got the same seats, Joe. You know, right next to the bench so he can see me."

Shannon Sharpe and Iso Joe Johnson both warned Ochocinco not to rile up Doncic, as it will only backfire on the Miami Heat.

Joe Johnson even claimed that the Lakers star will score at least 55 points against the Heat, which would be his best return of the season.

However, for any of this to happen, we have to wait a long time, as the two teams aren't scheduled to play against each other again until March 2027.
