UConn Men's Basketball: Huskies Fall to Arizona in Tough Road Test

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Dan Hurley had to have known when he scheduled nonconference games against six ranked teams – plus Power Four school Ohio State – that his UConn Huskies would not emerge unscathed.

He gave up the early season "buy" games that could have padded the win total and replaced them with contests against the highest-quality opponents to prepare his team for March Madness and a quest for another national championship.

Hurley wanted tests, he wanted hostile road environments. No. 12 Arizona and a raucous home crowd in Tucson provided just that as the Wildcats led wire-to-wire to defeat the No. 3 Huskies 85-79 on Wednesday night.

Arizona also defeated UConn last season, 71-67, in Storrs, Conn., in the first game of a home-and-away series.

On Wednesday, Arizona came out of the locker room and controlled the tempo from the start, using a swarming defense to stifle the Huskies and get them off their game. The Wildcats took a 10-0 lead, expanded it to 19 and were up 47-29 at halftime.

That defense limited UConn to just 33.9% (23 of 68) shooting for the game.

Still, UConn outplayed Arizona in the second half and cut the lead to eight points five times, but the Wildcats had an answer eavh time before ending with a six-point triumph

Krivas scored 18 points on 7-of-8 shooting and had 12 rebounds and four blocks. Kharchenkov delivered 20 points, five rebounds and seven assists. Three of his rebounds were on the offensive glass.

Perhaps it was fear of foul trouble, but in the first half, Hines and Giltay did not aggressively go after rebounds as they combined for four. Hines grabbed seven in the second half to wind up with nine as UConn outscored Arizona 50-38.

The centers will need to work on being aggressive without getting into foul trouble and hone those skills before the NCAA Tournament.
