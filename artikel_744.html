Cherki salvages draw with Napoli but Man City frustrate

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Manchester City vs Napoli. UEFA Champions League Round 1.

Match report as Manchester City drew 2-2 with Napoli in the Champions League at the Etihad Stadium. Rasmus Hojlund and Noa Lang twice put the Italian side in front but equalisers from Ayyoub Bouaddi and Rayan Cherki earned Enzo Maresca's men a point

Football Features Writer @ghostgoal

Rayan Cherki’s late strike salvaged a 2-2 draw for Manchester City at home to Napoli in the Champions League but Enzo Maresca’s side again struggled to find their fluency.

Former Manchester United striker Rasmus Hojlund opened the scoring for the Italian side and though Ayyoub Bouaddi equalised, Noa Lang's volley stunned the Etihad Stadium as Napoli restored their lead late on. Cherki's goal at least secured a point.

The draw leaves City in sixth spot in the Champions League table but they are likely to drop out of the top eight by Wednesday evening and have now gone 10 games without a clean sheet. Maresca's men look vulnerable ahead of the trip to Arsenal on Sunday.

With little to cheer in the first half, the home crowd took most pleasure in serenading Kevin De Bruyne on his return - King Kev declared one banner - and he gave glimpses of his quality, twice cutting City's defence apart. But the sluggish tempo suited a veteran.

It was all a little too slow from City. Maresca was trying to tighten up in midfield, picking Bouaddi as well as Elliot Anderson and Enzo Fernandez for only the second time. But it left this City side short of thrust in the last third despite Allan's impressive endeavour.

It needed a strong save from Gianluigi Donnarumma to ensure City went in level at the break. Sam Beukema made firm contact with a free header inside the box but the Napoli defender placed it too close to the goalkeeper. The home side did not heed the warning.

Billy Gilmour fed Hojlund inside the box on the angle and he got the shot away under pressure from Marc Guehi to beat Donnarumma low to his left. It was a sweet moment for the Danish striker, 23, whose United links had seen him booed by the City fans.

But Napoli's lead did not last long thanks to Bouaddi. The elegant Moroccan midfielder had yet to score a goal at senior level in his career but his left-footed effort found its way through Vanja Milinkovic-Savic. Substitute Jeremy Doku provided the spark required.

City were pushing for a winner at that stage, but Lang's exquisite volley on the counter-attack was a reminder that Maresca is having trouble controlling counter-attacks. At least he had kept the mercurial Cherki on the pitch to help avoid a damaging defeat.

But after that seven-game winning run in the Premier League, it is one point from four domestically and now this unconvincing effort in front of their own supporters. City are still a work in progress under Maresca. They will need to improve plenty at Arsenal.
