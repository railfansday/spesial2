Jax Taylor Calls Girlfriend Lori Krebs His 'Rock' Amid Cancer Diagnosis

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Jax Taylor is leaning on girlfriend Lori Krebs after being diagnosed with cancer.

“I honestly don’t know how I would have gotten through everything without my amazing girlfriend by my side,” Taylor, 47, wrote via Instagram on Wednesday, November 18. “She’s been my rock through all of this. She’s not just my partner, she’s my best friend and I feel so insanely lucky to have her in my life.”

Taylor revealed his health scare in the lengthy post, sharing that he had a five-hour surgery to remove a tumor.

“This is something I’ve kept private for the past few months because it’s been one of the scariest times of my life,” the reality star wrote. “Back in July, I woke up one morning in horrible pain and ended up in the hospital with kidney stones. What I thought was just a kidney stone turned into something I never imagined. My CT scan also showed a tumor on my left kidney. After many tests and appointments I found out it was cancer.”

Taylor admitted he was “in a really dark place” after learning the news.

“I lost my father to cancer, so hearing that word just broke me,” he continued. “The hardest part was the waiting. Knowing there was a tumor growing inside me while trying to go about my life like everything was fine. It’s crazy how you can act like everything is normal when deep down you’re a mess.”

Taylor is awaiting the results of his procedure, but he’s already learned some valuable lessons. “One thing this experience and the last couple years have taught me is when life gets really hard, you find out fast who’s really there for you,” he wrote. “And believe me.. it’s been eye opening … If there’s one thing I hope people can take away from this, it’s never to take your health for granted. I did for many years and I won’t ever do that again.”

Taylor’s shout-out to Krebs, 42, comes four months after their relationship made headlines when they were photographed getting cozy at a hotel in Mexico. The romance comes amid Taylor’s split from ex Brittany Cartwright, who filed for divorce in 2024 after five years of marriage.

Before Taylor and Krebs began dating, the publicist represented both Taylor and Cartwright, 37. Taylor has since defended his connection with Krebs.

“I love [Lori] to death. You can’t help who you fall in love with. Everybody deserves to find happiness,” he told Page Six in August, noting that he is “in love” with Krebs. “I’m in the happiest place I’ve ever been, and I couldn’t be happier right now. My mental health has been so amazing since I’ve been with her,” he went on, adding that he’s “in love with her.”

Taylor made it clear that he and Krebs “love each other very, very much.”
