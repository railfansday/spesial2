Christina Ricci and Cher Have Been Mad at Each Other This Whole Time?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A 30-year feud might finally be coming to an end — and this is the first time people are hearing it even existed.

Actress Christina Ricci stopped by Watch What Happens Live! With Andy Cohen on Tuesday night to promote the final season of her TV series Yellowjackets. But when Cohen pressed Ricci on her relationship with pop superstar Cher, Ricci revealed the two have been in a tiff since 1999. It’s a grudge Ricci says she’s ready to let go — as soon as she can get Cher to call her back.

“Unfortunately, she’s still mad at me,” Ricci told Cohen and fellow WWHL guest RHONY cast member Sai De Silva. “She holds a grudge. When I was 19, I did some [article] and they interviewed her and she said all these things that were not that amazing about me. And then she left me a voicemail about how journalists are always trying to get the worst things [and] she was so sorry. And I was 19. So I didn’t return that message.”

Ricci’s first film role was in Mermaids, the 1990 dramedy starring Cher and Winona Ryder. Ricci played Kate Flax, the annoying younger sister caught in the midst of Cher and Ryder’s combative mother-daughter relationship. “I’ve reached out to her since and gotten radio silence,” Ricci said. “I adore her. She’s the best. And I absolutely 100 percent can imagine being Cher and being like, ‘Oh my god, they 100 percent misquoted me. I’m going to call this young person and explain to her that they misquoted me.’ And then the gall of that young person to not return the call and be like, ‘Oh my god, Cher, it’s okay. I love you so much.’ I would be so pissed. I get it.”

Maybe Cher will watch the episode and give her a call. Something tells me Ricci will pick this one up.
