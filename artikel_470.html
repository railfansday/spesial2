Far Left Could Force Jeffries to Work With GOP, Says Sarah Chamberlain

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Sarah Chamberlain, president and CEO of Republican Main Street Partnership, said that Democrats will need to win the House by at least a 25 seat majority in order to give Hakeem Jeffries 'wiggle room' to get elected as Speaker and pass legislation. Chamberlain said that the presence of new 'far left' members could mean Jeffries may have to reach across the aisle to get things done. (Source: Bloomberg)
