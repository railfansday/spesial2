If you haven’t evaluated your credit card portfolio lately, now is a great time to do so.

Twice a month, we update this list to feature top welcome bonuses from cards that made the cut for our best credit cards list. Consider bookmarking this page and checking back regularly for the latest bonuses.

Earning new card bonuses is the fastest way to accumulate a stash of points and miles, so if you're working on building up your travel rewards balance, it's likely that one of the options below might be a good fit for you.

Since many issuers have restrictions on how often you can earn a bonus on a card, it’s important to time your application for when there’s a solid offer. Also, higher bonuses don’t always stick around for long, so if you’re considering one of these offers, you’ll want to hop on it sooner rather than later.

If you’re not ready to add a higher-end card, consider these great starter cards or even one with a 0% introductory annual percentage rate.

Best for: United flyers who want to earn bonus miles and enjoy airline-specific perks.

The United Explorer and United Gateway are two solid options for travelers looking to earn United MileagePlus miles, whether you're a frequent flyer looking for travel perks or an occasional traveler who prefers a no-annual-fee card.

The Explorer offers more travel perks, including a free first checked bag for the primary cardholder and one companion on the same reservation (terms apply), priority boarding and two United Club one-time passes each anniversary year. Meanwhile, the Gateway is a good option for more occasional United flyers who want to earn miles without paying an annual fee. It earns bonus miles on eligible United purchases, gas stations and local transit, including ride-hailing services.
