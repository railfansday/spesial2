Leaning on its formidable core and late-game composure, Duke defeated Connecticut, 77-63, at T-Mobile Arena on Wednesday.

The No. 16 Blue Devils were in control from the outset and improved to 7-2. The No. 10 Huskies, meanwhile, fell to 4-2. Duke avenged its one-point loss to Connecticut in the Elite Eight in last season's NCAA Tournament.

John Blackwell led the charge with 25 points, including 5-of-6 three-pointers, and five rebounds. Patrick Ngongba II had 17 points, 11 rebounds, and five assists, while Joaquim Boumtje Boumtje added 12 points and four rebounds.

In the postgame conference, coach Jon Scheyer touted his players' resolve.

"These guys, throughout the first six games of the year, have probably taken as much s***, as much stuff, as any team that I've been a part of," said Scheyer in the video posted by Carolina Blitz.

"We played this gauntlet schedule. These guys will be the first to say we didn't play our best, but the preparation, the practice, we just put our heads down and worked. I'm just really proud."

Before beating the Huskies, the Blue Devils were blown out by Illinois at Cameron Indoor Stadium and bounced back against Washington State at Acrisure Arena.

Scheyer added that they have no time to relax.

"We're gonna enjoy this win. Obviously, it doesn't get any easier for us, but I'm really proud of the effort. This is part of the long-term progression of our team," said Scheyer.

Duke will visit Florida at Tower Hill Arena in the ACC-SEC Challenge on Tuesday.
