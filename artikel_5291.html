Workday Advances EU Sovereign Cloud with New EU-Based Operating Company and Added Safeguards for Customer Data and Services

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Workday EU Sovereign Cloud Builds on the AWS European Sovereign Cloud to Help Customers Meet Their Most Stringent Digital Sovereignty Requirements and Support Continuity of Critical HR and Finance Services

BARCELONA, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Workday Rising EMEA â Workday, Inc. (NASDAQ: WDAY), the trusted agentic platform for HR, finance, and IT, today announced new governance and operational safeguards for Workday EU Sovereign Cloud. These safeguards are designed to give European organizations greater control of their sensitive HR and finance data and the critical services they rely on from Workday.

Together, these steps deepen Workday's existing EU data sovereignty approach and address a broader set of customer priorities: who governs and operates their services, how their data is protected, and what safeguards are in place to help maintain service continuity.

As European organizations adopt AI and other new technology, they are increasingly looking for stronger assurances that their most critical business systems will remain secure, accessible, and resilient. They want to know who can access their data, how government requests are handled, and whether the systems they use to pay their people and run the business will keep working when it counts. Workday EU Sovereign Cloud, announced last year, was built to keep customer data and operations in Europe, and these steps build on that foundation.

"European organizations shouldn't have to choose between adopting AI or sovereign controls for their sensitive HR and finance data," said Gerrit Kazmaier, president, product and technology, Workday. "Workday runs the critical systems that pay people and close the books, so we're bringing sovereign protections to the applications where that work happens, without slowing innovation."

Strengthening European Governance and Operational Control

Workday will establish a dedicated company based in Ireland that will operate Workday EU Sovereign Cloud. Existing operations will move to it gradually. Its management board will be made up of senior Workday leaders who are EU citizens. They will be legally bound to uphold European law and to act in the interest of Workday EU Sovereign Cloud.

Workday also plans to add structural safeguards that support independent governance, so decisions about Workday EU Sovereign Cloud and European customer data will stay in Europe.

The service will run on the AWS European Sovereign Cloud, which runs as a distinct independent partition, separate from the AWS commercial cloud. It will have no critical dependencies on non-EU infrastructure, and will be operated only by personnel who are EU residents. Together, these steps allow oversight and daily operations to be kept in Europe.

Protecting Customer Data and Keeping Services Running

Workday EU Sovereign Cloud is built on the AWS European Sovereign Cloud, which is designed to provide customers with additional assurances on data residency and operational autonomy, and supports continuity of essential HR and finance services.

AWS services support encryption with customer-managed keys that are inaccessible to AWS, giving customers complete control of who accesses their data. Workday's new EU-based company will control those keys, and they stay within the AWS European Sovereign Cloud. There is no mechanism for anyone, including AWS operators, to access the encryption keys in readable form, providing an additional layer of protection against unauthorized access.

"Workday is building on that foundation and bringing the same model to the application layer, where customers keep their most sensitive HR and finance data."

For European organizations that rely on Workday to manage their people and finances, these capabilities are designed to provide additional protections against external disruption, helping safeguard access to the essential HR and finance systems their businesses depend on.

Maintaining Access to Innovation Without Compromising Sovereignty

Workday EU Sovereign Cloud follows the same release schedule as Workday's standard cloud. As a result, customers benefit from the latest HR, finance and AI capabilities across all solutions offered in the sovereign environment, while retaining the European data and operational protections it provides.

Customers can use those AI capabilities knowing their data stays in the EU for AI processing and training and is not used to train global AI models.

That commitment extends to helping customers maintain access to their critical HR and finance services. If an order from a government authority threatens to disrupt these services, Workday and its sovereign suppliers, including AWS, are committed to using all available measures to ensure service continuity, which may include the transfer of operations to an independent European entity.

With these steps, Workday is delivering on its promise to European customers. They can use the latest innovations while keeping control of their data and the services their businesses depend on.

AvailabilityWorkday EU Sovereign Cloud is available today. Workday's new EU-based entity and the new structural safeguards are expected to be operational in early 2027.
