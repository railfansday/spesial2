OCLC and Ecole Solutions partner to bring EZproxy services to libraries across India

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Ecole Solutions is now OCLC's distributor for hosted EZproxy and EZproxyAnalytics, providing local sales, training, and support for Indian institutions

DUBLIN, Ohio and BANGALORE, India, Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- OCLC and Ecole Solutions, the Bangalore-based provider of technology solutions for libraries and academic institutions, today announced a distribution partnership to make OCLC's hosted EZproxy and EZproxy Analytics available to academic and research libraries in India.

Ecole Solutions will market and sell both services and provide local customer support, including implementation, training, and technical assistance. The distribution agreement covers much of the Indian subcontinent, including India, Bangladesh, Nepal, Sri Lanka and Bhutan.

OCLC and Ecole Solutions are partnering to make OCLC's EZproxy available to academic and research libraries in India

"We're delighted to welcome Ecole Solutions as our partner in India," said Eric van Lubeek, Managing Director, OCLC EMEA/Asia Pacific. "Ecole Solutions' local knowledge and technical expertise will help libraries quickly implement and realize the benefits of EZproxy services. This partnership reflects our long-term commitment to supporting the Indian library community."

India's One Nation One Subscription (ONOS) initiative expands access to scholarly research publications across the country. At the same time, libraries continue to invest in databases, e-books, and other digital resources and need reliable information to assess their use and value.

"EZproxy works with virtually any publisher and alongside the national federations and institutional logins libraries already use, giving researchers one way into their collection, from anywhere," said L. S. Venkatesh, Director, Ecole Solutions. "Librarians gain the usage evidence they need for collection decisions and quality reviews. OCLC hosts the service, and our team provides local support."

OCLC and Ecole Solutions leaders will introduce the new partnership and meet with library leaders at the PLANNER 2026 Convention in Guwahati, India, 25-27 November.

Libraries in India interested in learning more can visit https://www.ecoleglobal.com/.

More about EZproxy is on the OCLC website.

Ecole Solutions is an India-based technology services provider specializing in digital transformation for educational institutions. It delivers advanced plagiarism and AI-detection software, alongside e-learning and campus management solutions. These tools empower universities nationwide to enforce strict academic integrity and optimize operations.

OCLC is a nonprofit global library organization that provides shared technology services, original research, and community programs so that libraries can better fuel learning, research, and innovation. Through OCLC, member libraries cooperatively produce and maintain WorldCat, the most comprehensive global network of data about library collections and services. Libraries gain efficiencies through OCLC's WorldShare, a complete set of library management applications and services built on an open, cloud-based platform. It is through collaboration and sharing of the world's collected knowledge that libraries can help people find answers they need to solve problems. Together as OCLC, member libraries, staff, and partners make breakthroughs possible.
