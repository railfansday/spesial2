Nathan Fillion’s ‘The Rookie’ Officially Returns With a Major Schedule Change

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Since its 2018 premiere, ABC's hit procedural The Rookie has performed well for the network. The series, led by Nathan Fillion, was an immediate hit, thanks to its premise of new beginnings and personal reinvention, no matter the challenges along the way. The story started as an exploration of John Nolan, the oldest rookie in the LAPD, but has evolved into one about a sharp police officer keeping the city safe alongside his coworkers in Los Angeles' Mid-Wilshire Police Department. Eight seasons have aired so far, with a ninth set for January 2027. It will also be accompanied by a brand-new spin-off.

This is not the first time ABC has tried to expand The Rookie, with the first attempt coming in 2022 when The Rookie: Feds premiered. Headlined by Niecy Nash-Betts as Simone Clark, the FBI-set spin-off followed a similar premise: Simone leaves her career in guidance and counseling to become the FBI's oldest rookie. The Rookie: Feds ran for one season and was canceled for several reasons, including the 2023 Hollywood strikes. Some of the show's stars have continued to appear in later seasons of The Rookie.

Next year finds the mothership on the move as ABC creates The Rookie Wednesdays. Beginning January 6, 2027, The Rookie will air from 9 p.m. ET, followed by the new spin-off The Rookie North at 10 p.m. ET. It will be an interesting night for ratings, since NBC's juggernaut One Chicago also airs that night from 8 to 11 p.m. ET.

ABC has already released teaser trailers for the upcoming seasons of both shows, and in The Rookie Season 9, Lucy Chen (Melissa O'Neil) and Tim Bradford (Eric Winter) are in trouble after being kidnapped during Tim's proposal. The department jumps into action to save them while they try to save themselves. Meanwhile, a new beginning takes place at 10 pm with The Rookie North. The series introduces Jay Ellis' Alex Holland, whose midlife crisis is activated by a violent home invasion.

He joins the Pierce County Police Department. Rather than a slight refresh of The Rookie, the spin-off aims for a different feel by leaving the city for a slower, rural environment. The Rookie North also stars Janet Montgomery (New Amsterdam), Karen Fukuhara (The Boys), Chris Sullivan (This Is Us), Froy Gutierrez (Teen Wolf), Mya Lowe (My Life with the Walter Boys), and Malik Watson.
