Brad Pitt's Daughter Vivienne Officially Drops His Last Name

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Brad Pitt has officially been dropped from another one of his kids' names ... his 18-year-old daughter Vivienne has legally ditched her famous father's surname ... TMZ has confirmed.

A Los Angeles judge approved Vivienne's petition Monday, changing her legal name from Vivienne Marcheline Jolie-Pitt to Vivienne Marcheline Jolie.

The judge signed off on the request after no objections were filed.

As we reported ... Vivienne filed paperwork in July, shortly after turning 18, citing "personal" reasons for wanting to remove "Pitt" from her name.

She had already been using her mom Angelina Jolie's last name professionally, receiving credit as "Vivienne Jolie" in the Playbill for the Broadway production of "The Outsiders," which she worked on alongside Angelina.

Vivienne is now the fourth of Brad and Angelina's six children to legally drop their dad's surname.

Her older sister Shiloh officially changed her name in 2024, while siblings Maddox and Zahara received court approval for their own name changes earlier this year.

Vivienne's twin brother Knox also left "Pitt" off his high school diploma, though he has not legally changed his name.

As TMZ previously reported ... sources close to Brad have claimed Angelina is responsible for the growing rift between the actor and his children, describing the name changes as part of an ongoing effort to alienate them from their father.

Brad and Angelina finalized their divorce in December 2024, but remain locked in a separate legal battle over their French winery, Château Miraval.
