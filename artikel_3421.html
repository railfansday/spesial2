Sunrise on the Reaping is set 24 years before Katniss Everdeen (Jennifer Lawrence) volunteered as tribute. The prequel follows Haymitch Abernathy, played by Joseph Zada (originally portrayed in the movies by Woody Harrelson), a District 12 tribute selected to take part in the 50th Annual Hunger Games. This time, it's the second Quarter Quell, meaning President Snow (Ralph Fiennes) is upping the stakes by sending double the number of tributes from each district.

The movie introduces a whole new lineup as the children are sent to the Capitol to prepare for their bloodbath. In addition to District 12’s Maysilee Donner (McKenna Grace), Wyatt Callow (Ben Wang), and Lou Lou (Iona Bell), and their dreadful mentor, Drusilla Sickle (Glenn Close), we also meet familiar faces at a very different time in their lives, including Effie Trinket (Elle Fanning), Plutarch Heavensbee (Jesse Plemons), and Wiress (Maya Hawke), Beetee (Kelvin Harrison Jr.), and Mags (Lili Taylor) from Catching Fire.
