Photos: Best images from Thunder's 124-116 win over Cavaliers

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The Oklahoma City Thunder continue to move on with their new NBA life. They had a 124-116 win over the Cleveland Cavaliers on Wednesday. That's two victories in a row against the same opponent.

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander led the way with 42 points and eight assists. Ajay Mitchell stepped up with 23 points and seven rebounds. Bennett Stirtz was also a mild surprise with 16 points off the bench.

Meanwhile, Donovan Mitchell had a sizzling 27 points and six assists. James Harden had 19 points, nine rebounds and six assists. Evan Mobley tallied 16 points and 10 rebounds.
