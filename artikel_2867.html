Jets improved their special teams coverage by having fullback Andrew Beck handle kickoffs

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Only one kickoff has been returned for a touchdown in the NFL this season, and it was allowed by the Jets when kicker Austin McNamara not only failed to make the tackle, but got in the way of his teammates as they tried to run down Raiders return man Dylan Laube. That got the Jets thinking they needed to improve their kickoff coverage.

The solution was taking kickoff duties away from both McNamara, who is the Jets’ punter, and Jason Sanders, who is the Jets’ field goal kicker. Instead, it’s now fullback Andrew Beck, who has been a good special teams player for years in the NFL, who is handling kickoffs. Beck made a tackle last week on what could have been a much longer kickoff return without him, and he said he loves being able to both kick and make the tackle afterward.

“It was the whole reason that I’m out there, and I think it proves that it’s beneficial to have me doing it,” Beck told Newsday. “Instead of just to get some cool pictures, I’m actually out there doing it for a reason.”

Jets coach Aaron Glenn said when players have a skill set like Beck, with the ability to both kick and tackle, teams need to take advantage of that.

“He’s a unique player that has done a little bit of everything throughout his career,” Glenn said. “We want to utilize that as much as we can.”

Beck kicked in high school but not in college, and he didn’t think he’d be kicking again in the NFL. Now that he has the job, however, he hopes it increases his value to the team, and bolsters his career.

“I think I’ve shown that I can be successful in a couple different positions,” he said. “Hopefully the coaches feel comfortable putting me there.”

It’s almost unheard of for a player whose primary job isn’t punter or kicker to handle kickoffs, although with the dynamic kickoff rule promoting returns, it makes sense for a player who can make tackles to be out there if he’s capable of kicking the ball into the landing zone reliably. Saints safety Justin Reid has also kicked off, although he’s only done it once this season. If the Jets have success with Beck, it might be something that other teams try — and it might make players who have the unique skill set of being both good kickers and good tacklers more valuable.
