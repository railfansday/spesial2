I Built Universal Calendar: An Open-Source Calendar That Goes Beyond the Gregorian System

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Most calendar applications are designed around one familiar system: the Gregorian calendar. But what if you want to work with different calendar systems, compare dates across calendars, or even create a calendar with your own months, weekdays and year structure? That's the idea behind Universal Calendar, an open-source Flutter application I developed. 🌍 What is Universal Calendar? Universal Calendar is designed for people who want more flexibility in how they organise, understand and manage time. It brings multiple calendar systems into one application and goes further by allowing users to create entirely custom calendars. ✨ Key Features
