The 10 best Christmas gifts to buy while theyre on sale for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Here we go, people. It's time to start (or finish) your 2026 Christmas shopping. Technically, we still have a few weeks of shopping time left, but December days seem to travel at a quicker speed than most. If you're not looking to add stress to the holidays by wondering if your presents will arrive on time, I suggest shopping soon, like today.

There's no better time to shop for Christmas gifts that during Black Friday sales. You get great deals on tech items, kitchen gadgets, and outdoor gear. Some of our very favorite gifts are on sale, including a few record-low prices.

We're rounded up 10 of our favorite Christmas presents to buy while they're on sale for Black Friday, and we've included budget picks that are excellent stocking stuffers as we as fancy gifts for people you love to spoil.

Yeti makes some of the best buy-it-for-life products, and the Yeti Hopper M Series Backpack Cooler is a terrific gift for nearly anyone on your list. It's excellent for your aunt who's always going camping or your best friend who loves to spend the day at the beach. It's on of the classic Black Friday deals we look forward to raving about each year.

The Kindle Paperwhite is one of Mashable's favorite e-readers, and since Amazon raised prices a few months ago, Black Friday is an ideal time to snag the Paperwhite on sale. The 2024 model with 16GB of storage (that'll fit thousands of titles, btw) is on sale for $129.99. It makes for a great gift for anyone in your life who loves to read or has the best intentions of getting back into reading in 2027.

By now, you've probably seen people carrying around the Owala FreeSip, Mashable's favorite water bottle. Gift this to anyone in your life who needs some motivation to stay hydrated, and you'll be gifting them a healthy present. The FreeSip bottle includes a unique design that allows you to sip from the built-in straw or take a chug from the spout. Plus, it does a great job of keeping drinks cool. A whole slew of Owala water bottles are on sale for 24% off, so take your pick of size, style, and color.

Ready to entertain for story time, the Toniebox 2 makes for a great present. A Tonie character sits atop the Toniebox for an independent playtime full of stories, songs, and games. The starter set comes with Tonie the dog, but the lineup of Tonie characters is immense. Grab the starter box for any kiddo who loves listening to stories, dancing to music, and learning new games. The sale price of $99.97 is the lowest we've ever seen, so hop on this Black Friday deal quickly.

Rated one of Mashable's top Christmas gifts, the Kodak Charmera is a digital camera small enough to fit on a keychain. It's a blind box, so you won't know which colorway design will come with your camera, adding to the fun. The charmingly retro shots are a fun nostalgic throwback, and there's a slot for a microSD.
