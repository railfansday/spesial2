When and Where to Stream the 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade After Its Historic Delay

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

For the first time since its inaugural 1924 march, the annual event was postponed due to weather.

Following a historic two-day delay, the 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade will now be broadcast on Saturday, Nov. 28 at 8:30 a.m. ET/CT/MT and 7:30 a.m. PT on NBC, available to stream live via DirecTV (with a five-day free trial), Fubo (with a one-day free trial), Sling or Hulu + Live TV.

The parade will also stream on Peacock, and while the service doesn’t offer a free trial of its own, fans can claim two complimentary months of Peacock with any of DirecTV’s Signature packages, which offer the aforementioned free trial. The rescheduled parade will now air on Saturday, Nov. 28 at 8:30 a.m. ET/CT/MT and 7:30 a.m. PT on NBC and simulcast on Telemundo.

Since the event airs on NBC, it will also be available to watch live on any streaming service that carries said network, including DirecTV (with a five-day free trial), Fubo (with a one-day free trial), Sling and Hulu + Live TV.

Saturday’s parade will also be available to stream live on Peacock, and while the streamer doesn’t offer a free trial directly, fans can get two months of free Peacock access (thanks to a limited-time promo) through any of DirecTV’s Signature packages, which also offer the standard five-day free trial.

Here’s exactly how to claim DirecTV’s Peacock offer: Select your preferred signature package, then opt to add “Movies Extra Pack + Peacock Premium” where it instructs you to pick your add-ons. As indicated, you’ll automatically get two months of the Movies Extra Pack + Peacock included in your subscription (click here to see everything the add-on offers on top of Peacock). After two months, unless cancelled at any time, DirecTV’s Movies Extra Pack + Peacock will renew monthly at the then-prevailing rate.

For another savvy streaming hack, a subscription to Peacock is available through Prime Video and unlike direct subscriptions on PeacockTV.com, Peacock on Prime Video offers a seven-day trial period for the Premium Plus plan. Following the trial, unless cancelled at any time, the subscription will continue at the standard rate.

To claim the free trial, select your preferred Signature package. Once it prompts you to choose how many Gemini devices you’d like to connect, you’ll select “0 – Stream without the Gemini Air” as the five-day free trial is only available to customers without a leased Gemini device.

Learn more about each plan option, including how to build your own channel lineup (starting at just $20.99 per month), at directv.com.

After the trial, plans start at $54.99 for the first month and $64.99 monthly thereafter.

In select markets, NBC is included in Sling’s budget-friendly Select plan, from $19.99 per month; Sling’s Blue plan, from $45.99 per month; and Sling’s Orange & Blue plan, from $60.99 per month. Visit Sling.com to check station availability.

Watch NBC with Hulu + Live TV. Subscriptions come bundled with Disney+ and ESPN+, and start at $89.99 per month.
