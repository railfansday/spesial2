Jax Taylor Reveals Cancer Diagnosis After 'Private' Health Battle

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Jax Taylor is opening up about a recent health scare, revealing he was diagnosed with cancer.

“This is something I’ve kept private for the past few months because it’s been one of the scariest times of my life,” the Vanderpump Rules alum, 47, wrote via Instagram on Wednesday, November 18. “Back in July, I woke up one morning in horrible pain and ended up in the hospital with kidney stones. What I thought was just a kidney stone turned into something I never imagined. My CT scan also showed a tumor on my left kidney. After many tests and appointments I found out it was cancer.”

Taylor continued, “I lost my father to cancer, so hearing that word just broke me. It put me in a really dark place. The hardest part was the waiting. Knowing there was a tumor growing inside me while trying to go about my life like everything was fine. It’s crazy how you can act like everything is normal when deep down you’re a mess.”

The former reality star noted that he had a five-hour surgery that day to remove the tumor, “but it was a lot larger than they thought so we are waiting on the pathology results.”

Taylor included several photos and videos of himself in the hospital while reflecting on what he learned from the experience.

“It’s been eye opening,” he wrote before giving a shout-out to girlfriend Lori Krebs. “I honestly don’t know how I would have gotten through everything without my amazing girlfriend by my side. She’s been my rock through all of this. She’s not just my partner, she’s my best friend and I feel so insanely lucky to have her in my life. I also want to thank my incredible surgeon Dr. Adhoot and the medical team at @cedarssinai for taking such good care of me.”

Taylor further reminded his followers not to take their health for granted. “I did for many years and I won’t ever do that again,” he added. “Next week on November 28 will mark my 2 year sobriety anniversary and I’m so fucking proud. It’s crazy how things happen…if I hadn’t ended up in the hospital with that kidney stone, I probably wouldn’t have known this tumor was there until it was too late.”

He concluded, “I like to think my dad had a part in that. Life can really change in a second. I’ve been given a lot to think about these past few years and if there’s one thing I know for sure it’s that nothing else matters more than your health your kids and the people that show up for you when it matters the most.”

Taylor, who shared 5-year-old son Cruz with ex Brittany Cartwright, shared more insight into his “really scary few months” in an interview with People.

“The tumor was larger and deeper than originally expected, which made the surgery more complicated. … I have five incisions on my stomach, so I’m pretty sore and know it’s going to take some time to recover,” he said. “More than anything, I just want to be healthy and be here to watch my son grow up.”

The Bravo alum continued to gush over Krebs, who he began dating amid his divorce from Cartwright, 37.

“Having Lori by my side through all of this has meant everything to me,” he told the outlet. “She’s been absolutely incredible, and I honestly couldn’t ask for a better partner. Right now, I’m just so happy to be on the other side of this and taking things one day at a time.”
