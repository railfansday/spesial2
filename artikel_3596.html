Jennifer Lopez Opens Up About 'Disappointing' Ben Affleck Divorce

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Jennifer Lopez is looking back on her “disappointing” divorce from longtime love Ben Affleck.

“It was such a hard, disappointing last situation on every level,” she said on the “Friends Keep Secrets” podcast hosted by Benny Blanco on Tuesday, November 17. “On every level, it was so disappointing. I don’t ever want to delve into that.”

Lopez and Affleck got married in 2022, nearly two decades after they first dated and got engaged, but the actress and singer filed for divorce in 2024 after two years of marriage. The divorce was finalized in 2025.

The “On the Floor” singer said she hopes to find love again, but next time, she plans to do things differently.

“Even if I was in a relationship, I’m not talking about that. I’m done with that part of my life,” she shared, adding that the next time will be “different.”

Lopez, 57, said she realized that she was “a serial monogamist, one after another, trying to find the thing, hoping that this was it,” but following her divorce from Affleck, 54, she sat in her heartbreak and did the internal work that she needed to heal.

She said, “It changed me. I allowed it to change me.”

Lopez also reflected on her multiple divorces and shared her advice for other people in a similar situation. (In addition to Affleck, the Office Romance star was married to actor Ojani Noa from 1997 to 1998, dancer Cris Judd from 2001 to 2003 and singer Marc Anthony — with whom she shares twins Max and Emme, 18 — from 2004 to 2014.)

“They’re hard. They’re disappointing,” she said of her divorces. “My advice for divorce is my advice for everything, which is, always operate in a way that you’ll feel proud of when you look back.”

Lopez continued, “I can look back at these situations and be like, even as painful as some of them were — so bad, life-shifting pain and hurt — I can look back and be like, I loved completely. I acted in a way that I’m proud of as a human. I didn’t treat anybody in any way I would be ashamed of. I always tried in the moment. I did my best in the moment with what I knew.”

“That’s my advice for anything, because when you go through hard things like that, it’s easy to kind of be like, ‘F*** this,’” she added. “You’re in so much pain, you want to hurt somebody else, or somebody has hurt you so much that you’re like … but you can’t. I’ve always kind of held myself to that standard. Be the best person you can be right now, every single time, especially when it’s hard.”
