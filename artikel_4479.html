Highly sociable Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphins with predictable group size preferences may live longer

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Aging can bring changes in sociability, such as decreased network size, increased social selectivity, reduced social influence and connectedness and reduced affiliative interactions. A study published Nov. 24 in the open-access journal PLOS Biology by Kelley Caroline Meehan at The University of Queensland, St. Lucia, Australia, and colleagues suggests a relationship in Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphins between sociability (group size), its predictability and longevity.
