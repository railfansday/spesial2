Man Allegedly Thought About Killing Mom for Months, Confessed to Her Murder

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A New Jersey man allegedly confessed to police he stabbed his mother to death after he had been thinking about murdering her for roughly six months.

Marcus Toth, of Clark, was arrested on charges of first-degree murder, third-degree possession of a weapon for unlawful purposes and fourth-degree possession of a weapon in relation to his mother Ingrid Toth’s death on Thursday, November 19, the Union County Prosecutor’s Office said in a news release that same day.

Police discovered Ingrid dead at her Clark home when officers responded to a report of a medical emergency at the residence the afternoon of November 19, according to prosecutors.

Officers found Ingrid in the backyard after she had been fatally stabbed, per WABC-TV.

Marcus, 26, arrived at the Clark Police Department that afternoon and requested to speak with an officer privately, then allegedly “spontaneously” confessed, “I murdered my mother,” according to a probable cause affidavit obtained by Patch.com.

Marcus, who lived with Ingrid, is accused of stabbing her with a small kitchen knife and a large butcher knife in the kitchen.

While speaking with police, Marcus said was “thinking about killing his mother for approximately six months” before November 19, according to court documents viewed by WABC-TV.

Marcus made a virtual appearance in court on Friday, November 20, when his public defender Todd Hess pleaded not guilty on Marcus’ behalf, per the TV station.

Neighbors who spoke with WABC-TV said Marcus had lived with his mother and father for more than 10 years.

In a statement on the case on Friday, Clark Mayor Angel Albanese said, “I extend my appreciation to everyone involved in this investigation.”

“Above all, our thoughts are with the victim’s family and loved ones as they navigate this unimaginable loss,” the mayor added.

The Union County Prosecutor’s Office encourages anyone with information on Ingrid’s death to call Detective Darius Singletary at (908) 632-0537, Sergeant Nicholas Veltre at (908) 347-7330, or Clark Police Detective Paul Sosnowski (732) 882-8299.
