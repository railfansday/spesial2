Would Lindsay Arnold Ever Return as 'DWTS' Pro? What She's Said

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Lindsay Arnold has taken some time away from the Dancing With the Stars ballroom, but would she ever return?

Arnold made her debut as a DWTS pro for season 16 in 2013, one year after competing on season 9 of So You Think You Can Dance. At the time, Arnold was the second-youngest pro in DWTS history.

Following her first season, Arnold performed with the troupe for seasons 17 through 20. She was bumped up to pro status once again for season 21 in 2015, landing in third place with partner Alek Skarlatos.

Arnold competed as a pro for 10 seasons before announcing in 2022 that she wouldn’t be returning.

“This has been one of the hardest decisions to make, but ultimately, I have chosen what I feel is best for myself and my family,” the choreographer — who shares three children with husband Sam Cusick — wrote via Instagram at the time, adding that she still loved the show “with all of my heart.”

Since then, Arnold has frequently been asked whether she’d ever perform in the DWTS ballroom again. (Fans will see her make a cameo in sister Rylee Arnold‘s freestyle with Connor Wood during the season 35 finale.)

From her 10 seasons as a pro, Lindsay has one mirrorball trophy. She won season 25 in 2017 with actor Jordan Fisher. Lindsay and MLB alum David Ross were runners-up on season 24. She landed in third place with Skarlatos on season 21 and with Calvin Johnson Jr. on season 23.

Lindsay last competed on DWTS with former Bachelor Matt James on season 30 in 2021, ending in 12th place — the lowest ranking in her career.

While speaking with Us in 2024, Lindsay said she “would love” to return to DWTS in some capacity. “[I] definitely have already expressed [that] I’d love to be there if it ever makes sense or needs to happen, or whatever,” she explained.

At the time, Lindsay added that it was “really fun” to support her sister Rylee, who joined the show as a pro in 2023.

“Genuinely I feel like I’m in it with Rylee because I’m like, ‘Send me every video, call me after rehearsal, tell me everything.’ I feel like I’m so in it,” Lindsay said. “It’s funny, because I feel like I even get a little bit more stressed and worried and into it than I ever did for myself because it’s my little baby sister.”

Ahead of season 35, Lindsay told Us she would “never say never” to competing again. “There’s so many aspects of it that I miss,” she said. “In a perfect world, I could do the show and also do all the other things I have and also take my kids to all of their things and also be home. … But I think right now, I am just absolutely loving and embracing this phase of life that we’re in.”

Throughout her own DWTS journey, Rylee has been candid about how much Lindsay has shown her support.

In 2024, Rylee told Us she tried “so hard” to get her older sister back on the show, teasing, “I think there is probably a future with Dancing With the Stars and Lindsay.”

Rylee later revealed Lindsay is “always there” to give her positive feedback. “I call her all the time in rehearsals, and I’m like, ‘Do you like this dance move? Is this good?'” she said amid season 35. “So, she’s a part of this whole process for me, and she’s the best.”
