In Loftia, you get to build your cozy life in the sky. As you explore the floating islands, you’ll be able to farm, craft, decorate, and explore the lands alongside other players. You can enjoy your adventure solo or participate with your friends.

As you explore, you’ll come across different NPCs that will provide you with various quests to complete. These quests will provide you with valuable resources to help you progress on your in-game journey.

Loftia isn’t just about exploration; you get to enjoy new-age farming. From advanced hydroponics to trellis farming, you can craft your own tools using sustainable energy sources.

There’s plenty of scope for in-game customization since you can personalize your characters with unique designs. You can also adopt a pet from the different companions available in the game. While there’s no demo, you can wishlist the game on Steam to stay on top of the launch. With Loftia releasing tomorrow in early access, you can expect to find plenty of updates in the following months.
