ASEAN's Middle Powers Seek Bigger Role in Global Order

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Stratbase Institute Founder Victor Andres C. Manhit believes ASEAN should use its growing economic clout to help shape the global agenda rather than simply choose between the US and China. The Philippines can work with other middle powers while defending a rules-based international order and its maritime rights. He speaks with Haslinda Amin from the sidelines of the "ASEAN Summit 2026". (Source: Bloomberg)
