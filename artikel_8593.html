Tanggal: 2026-11-07

We have a situation that should become increasingly common for Bogleheads.

We have long embraced the concept that equities go in taxable accounts and fixed income in tax-deferred accounts. We now have larger, equity heavy, taxable accounts and smaller, fixed income heavy, tax-deferred accounts. We "struggle" to maintain our 60/40 overall allocation. If equity markets continue their 100-year 10% total return our tax-deferred accounts will soon be all fixed income. Our RMD withdrawals and pension income exceed our spending, so we reinvest (in fixed income now) all of the income in our taxable accounts.

It's a nice "problem" to have.
