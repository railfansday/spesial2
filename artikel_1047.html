Adames to fight Warburton on November 14 live on Sky Sports

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Carlos Adames will fight Gerome Warburton as part of Riyadh Season Zuffa Boxing 13 on November 14, live on Sky Sports; a 10-round bantamweight co-feature will see Jason 'Mayhem' Moloney make his Zuffa Boxing debut against Andres Teran

WBC middleweight world champion Carlos Adames will fight Welsh southpaw Gerome Warburton on Saturday, November 14, live on Sky Sports.

The pair meet in a 10-round main event of Riyadh Season Zuffa Boxing 13, with the bout to be contested at a contract weight of 165 pounds.

Adames, 25-1-1 (18), returns to action less than eight months after his dominant win over Austin 'Ammo' Williams, the third title defence for the Dominican Republic native, who is unbeaten in his last eight bouts.

Warburton, 18-2-2 (2), reignited his career in 2026, earning back-to-back decision victories over former British middleweight champion Nathan Heaney.

The 31-year-old from Colwyn Bay, Wales, is a former Welsh middleweight champion who has challenged for British and Commonwealth honors.

The 10-round bantamweight co-feature will see Australian former world champion Jason 'Mayhem' Moloney, 29-4 (20), make his Zuffa Boxing debut against Mexico's Andres Teran, 19-2 (14).

Moloney, a consensus world-ranked contender, has won two consecutive bouts in Australia to position himself for another championship run. Teran turned heads at Zuffa Boxing 08 in June, knocking out the previously undefeated Floyd Diaz in the sixth round.

Also fighting on November 14, super-middleweight contender Bruno Surace, two fights since his knockout victory over Jaime Munguia, takes on Poland's unbeaten Kamil Bednarek in a 10-rounder.

In an eight-round tilt featuring unbeaten lightweights, Mexico's Ivan Ortiz will battle Dariial Kuchmenov.

Ortiz upset the previously unbeaten Justin Viloria in his last outing, while Kuchmenov upped his KO streak to five with a fourth-round blitzing of Jorge Lagunas.

Two of Uzbekistan's top emerging talents - heavyweight Jakhongir Zokirov and light heavyweight Tullo Boymatov - return following standout Zuffa Boxing debuts earlier this year.

Plus middleweight contender Radzhab Butaev looks to get back on the winning track against Colombian Alexander Castro in a 10-rounder.
