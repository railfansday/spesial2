Nikki Glaser Sets Netflix Partnership As Streamer Officially Unveils Netflix Is A Joke Studios

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

If you’ve been following our reporting closely, you might have noticed a footnote in an October story surrounding restructuring at Netflix: the launch of Netflix Is a Joke Studios, an in-house incubator and production studio that will develop scripted projects and original formats alongside both emerging and established comedy talent.

Now, Netflix has made the official announcement, unveiling a new partnership with Nikki Glaser through the studio, under which she’ll develop an original scripted series in which she;ll star, as well as headline and exec produce two new stand-up specials for the streamer. Gloria Sanchez Productions is aboard to exec produce the scripted series. Glaser has been building her relationship with Netflix for years. She released her first Netflix special, Nikki Glaser: Bangin‘, in 2019, has featured in multiple iterations of Netflix Is a Joke Fest, and took her career to the next level with her work at The Roast of Tom Brady. Subsequently, she’s judged The Roast of Marshawn Lynch on Funny AF, voiced a role in Netflix’s animated feature Steps, and shot the Eva Longoria comedy The Fifth Wheel, due for release on the service in 2027. Watch on Deadline

Robbie Praw, VP, Netflix Standup and Comedy Formats, said: “I first met Nikki Glaser in a small comedy club 20 years ago and have had a front-row seat to her rise ever since. When Netflix’s The Roast of Tom Brady sent her career into overdrive in 2024, we watched audiences embrace what many of us have seen for years. She’s one of the sharpest comedic minds working today, with instincts to build something lasting well beyond stand-up. Netflix Is a Joke has always celebrated comedy in all its forms, and NIAJ Studios is the next step in our commitment to this community. We’re honored to be in Nikki’s corner as she builds what’s next.”

Tracey Pakosta, Netflix VP Comedy Series, said: “Great comedy starts with an unmistakable voice, and few comedians have one as fully formed as Nikki Glaser’s. She’s fearless, funny and disarmingly real, which makes her the ideal first partner for the NIAJ Studios vision. We can’t wait to see the world she’ll build.”

An Emmy, Grammy and Golden Globe nominee, Glaser released her latest special, Nikki Glaser: Good Girl, on Hulu in April. The hour earned three 2026 Emmy nominations, as well as a 2027 Grammy nomination for Best Comedy Album, marking her second nomination in the category.

Glaser has hosted the Golden Globes since 2025, when she became the ceremony’s first solo female host. She earned an Emmy nomination for her work as host of this year’s ceremony and returns to the dais on Jan. 10, 2027.

In film, Glaser is set to co-write, produce and star in an original comedy inspired by the game MASH for Paramount Pictures. She’s also set to star in, co-write and produce a new romantic comedy for Universal Pictures with Judd Apatow producing.

Speaking to her new deal, Glaser said, “Netflix gave me some of my earliest and biggest opportunities in comedy, so I couldn’t be more excited to begin this next phase of my career with them.”

The comedian is represented by WME, Brillstein Entertainment Partners and Ginsburg Daniels Kallis.
