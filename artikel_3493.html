President Trump Defends Friendship With Putin, Says It Keeps America Safe

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

President Trump is doubling down on his friendship with Vladimir Putin ... telling reporters he has a "fantastic" relationship with the Russian leader after their meeting at the China summit.

Speaking at a White House press conference Friday, Trump was asked whether Putin is a friend to America ... and while he said he couldn't speak for the country, he insisted their personal relationship is a major asset, especially given Russia's massive nuclear arsenal.

Trump also claimed Putin treated him well during the "Russia, Russia, Russia hoax" and pointed to his strong relationship with Chinese President Xi Jinping ... declaring, "Our country is safe because of that."

The remarks came during Trump's first press conference since returning from the summit, where he got chummy with Xi and Putin.

As you know ... Trump raised a few eyebrows at the trilateral talks, especially when he appeared to nod off during Xi's address.

Xi and Trump shared a rare hug on the tarmac after he arrived ... before POTUS and a smiling Putin shook hands.

While Trump and Putin apparently chatted privately as the war in Ukraine approaches its fifth year ... the prez called Putin his "friend" at one point during the economic forum.

The trio of world leaders ended up issuing a "peace declaration" centered around nuclear risks, oil supplies, UN reform, and more.

While he was traveling back from the forum, Trump missed his wife Melania Trump's Amazon docuseries premiere in NYC.
