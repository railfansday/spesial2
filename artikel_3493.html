Avengers: Endgame Encore Disney+ Streaming Release Date Officially Confirmed

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

2026 will see Earth's Mightiest Heroes enter their most chaotic era in the Marvel Cinematic Universe franchise.

It has been more than half a decade since the world witnessed Thanos' massive defeat, bringing an epic conclusion to the Infinity Saga, which began with 2008's Iron Man. However, the MCU timeline is getting ready for its next major big bad; Marvel Studios has also been giving the world a chance to relive the iconic 2019 entry from Anthony and Joe Russo in theaters, with additional footage.

With the conclusion of VisionQuest, there will be one more MCU release before Doomsday opens in theaters on December 18, as Avengers: Endgame Encore will arrive on Disney+ on Friday, November 13, with "new scenes and color grading," according to the Disney streaming service's official website. This comes after its run in theaters, which began on September 25, for a limited run.

In ScreenRant's Avengers: Endgame Encore review by Liz Declan, she shared that what was "particularly devastating is that Endgame Encore swoops in to undermine the very conclusions that made Endgame so profound. Again, I'm no fan of Steve's ending in the original cut—he spends the movie underscoring that there's no way to go back and change things, only to...go back and change things—but seeing that (quite literally) disrupted was significantly worse." While addressing that the original Endgame is "a true ensemble piece, and there are many notable arcs in the movie," Declan commented that "the powers that be have taken that away so that his return in Doomsday can make some semblance of sense and get wayward fans to flock to theaters."

Robert Downey Jr., who finished his time in 2019 as Tony Stark, is making a return to the franchise this fall, but not as the iconic Avenger. Instead, he is taking on the villainous role of Doctor Doom, one of the most famous rivals in the Marvel canon. The Oscar-winning star will also bring Victor von Doom back for 2027's Secret Wars, which will serve as the official finale to the Multiverse Saga. Production on the upcoming MCU movie has yet to start, but several key players from the franchise are confirmed to return, including Evans.

Avengers: Endgame Encore arrives on Disney+ on November 13, with Avengers: Doomsday slated for December 18.
