Alex Murdaugh Claims Investigator Lied About Evidence in Wife and Son’s Murders

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Alex Murdaugh’s lawyers are alleging that the lead investigator in his wife, Maggie Murdaugh, and their son Paul Murdaugh’s murder case lied about evidence during his first trial,

During a Friday, November 13, pretrial hearing, Murdaugh’s attorneys and prosecutors presented their latest arguments in front of Circuit Judge Debra McCaslin while requesting the court dismiss the murder indictment against the disgraced former lawyer.

Alex’s attorneys asked the court to dismiss the indictment against him “without prejudice,” claiming that former lead State Law Enforcement Division (SLED) Investigator David Owen lied while testifying before the Colleton County grand jury in 2022, according to the ABC 11.

After his attorneys asked that his murder indictment be dropped, McCaslin said she would try to get a written order on the dismissal request finished by Friday, November 20, according to WCSC. However, she did not rule on the request during the most recent hearing.

Alex, 58, was convicted in 2023 of fatally shooting Maggie, 52, and Paul, 22, on their property in South Carolina on June 7, 2021.

The once-prominent lawyer appealed the double-murder conviction, and the South Carolina Supreme Court overturned his convictions in May. The court then ordered that he receive a new trial after it was determined that former Colleton County Clerk of Court Becky Hill improperly influenced jurors during the initial court proceedings. During the November 13 hearing, Alex’s attorneys shared how they plan to approach his next trial.

“What we’re asking, Your Honor, is a reboot,” attorney Jim Griffin said, according to WIS. “Go back to square one and present the case anew to the Colleton County grand jury.”

Alex’s attorneys said that they wanted to focus on the T-shirt that the defendant was wearing when he said he found Maggie and Paul after they had been fatally shot.

The attorneys claimed that Owen failed to mention an initial report that “no blood” was found on the white T-shirt during his testimony, which they believe misled the grand jury.

A DNA report conducted in June 2021 found Maggie’s DNA on the hem of the shirt Alex used to wipe his hands and face while checking to see if his wife and son were alive.

In February 2022, blood spatter expert Tom Bevel said that stains found on the shirt were consistent with blood transfers but did not appear to be blood spatter from a bullet wound.

However, Bevel later changed his opinion when he said he found “over 100+ spatter stains” after looking at photographs of the shirt that had been altered in Photoshop.

During Owen’s testimony, he acknowledged the presence of blood spatter but not the transfers while speaking to the jury. Additionally, he claimed that both Maggie and Paul’s DNA were found on the shirt.

“SLED Lab says there’s no blood,” Griffin said during the latest hearing, according to News 19. “SLED lab says there’s no DNA. The grand jury didn’t hear that.”

Also during the latest hearing, Griffin claimed that Owen misled the grand jury about ammunition that was found on the property where Maggie and Paul were killed. Owen testified that the same combination of ammunition that was used to kill Paul was found loaded in weapons on the family’s property, which Griffin argued was not true.

“This was not overtly false,” lead prosecutor Creighton Waters said, per News 19. “This is what the agent understood at the time.”

Alex is currently serving a 40-year prison sentence for several financial crimes.
