Netflix’s Long-Awaited ‘Magic: The Gathering’ Series Officially Gets a First Look and Release Window

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Hannah covers horror, sci-fi, and games at Collider, where she writes news and features, reviews new releases, and interviews the people who make them. She’s helping build out Collider’s gaming coverage, with Pokémon as one of her specialties. If she isn’t writing about a monster, she’s probably watching one on a tape from her VHS collection.

The first time Chandra Nalaar escaped to another world, she was about to die. In Magic: The Gathering lore, her spark ignited just in time to take her from her home plane to a monastery where she could learn to control her fire, which is an unusually good way to explain what a planeswalker is. Chandra has somewhere else to go when things get bad enough. The question is how long she can live with leaving other people behind.

Netflix has been trying to bring Magic to television since at least 2019, when it announced an animated project with Joe and Anthony Russo attached. In 2024, the streamer introduced a new creative team led by Terry Matalas, the showrunner behind Star Trek: Picard Season 3 and VisionQuest. Seven years after the first announcement, there’s finally a year to put on the calendar and a clearer idea of whose story will get us into the multiverse.

Netflix has unveiled a first-look image and development diary for Planeswalker, its animated Magic: The Gathering series, which premieres in 2027. The series centers on Chandra, a pyromancer being hunted for her powers. She’ll have to work with other planeswalkers to stop a war that could spread across thousands of worlds. Cindy Appel joins Matalas as co-showrunner, Patrick Osborne is supervising director, and DIGIC is handling animation. Netflix is making the series in collaboration with Hasbro Entertainment and Wizards of the Coast.

Players who know Chandra from the cards have already met her at different points in her life. Magic Origins gave her a card that starts with her as Chandra, Fire of Kaladesh and turns over to reveal Chandra, Roaring Flame. Later versions put her in different worlds and give her new ways to use the same gift. The series has room to spend time on what changes between those cards, especially when using her powers is exactly what makes her a target. That matters when the story gets as big as Netflix’s synopsis promises:

Planeswalker, an epic fantasy saga from the world of Magic: The Gathering, chronicles the fierce clash between formidable mages known as Planeswalkers. At the center of this struggle for power and survival burns Chandra, a volatile pyromancer hunted for her gifts, and haunted by her past. Confronted by an insidious force, Chandra and some of the most legendary Planeswalkers must harness their extraordinary abilities, navigate treacherous alliances, and battle their own inner demons to prevent a cataclysmic war that threatens to consume their very existence. As the line between victory and annihilation grows thinner, a single spark determines the fate of thousands of worlds.

A war across thousands of worlds is hard to picture, but Chandra returning to a world she once fled, as she does in the game’s fiction, makes the cost of that kind of conflict easier to understand. Netflix’s first synopsis gives her a reason to trust other mages whose interests may only line up with hers for as long as they’re in danger. For a character whose first trip across the multiverse was an escape, the more interesting decision is when to stay.

Planeswalker premieres on Netflix in 2027.
