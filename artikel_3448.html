Why VisionQuest Cut A Key Marvel Family Member Clarified By Creator

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

VisionQuest creator Terry Matalas has clarified why the series cut out one of Vision’s family members from the comics.

Five years after he was seen flying away at the end of WandaVision, Paul Bettany’s Vision finally returned to the Marvel Cinematic Universe for Disney+’s VisionQuest in September. In addition to reuniting with his sons Tommy and Billy by the time the series was over, A.I. personas like E.D.I.T.H. and F.R.I.D.A.Y. were incorporated into the android’s family. However, as Marvel fans familiar with the Vision from the comics know, he also has an android daughter named Vivian, but she was nowhere to be seen in VisionQuest.

Matalas explained to The Direct that Vivian never felt like a good fit for VisionQuest because her origins from the comics clashed with the mental space Bettany's starts out with when the Marvel TV show begins. Instead, it was decided that James Spader’s Ultron was more suitable to fill that direct, familial role in the series. As Matalas explained:

No, Viv Vision didn’t seem quite right. It’s a wonderful collection. Everything he did with that obviously inspires— goes back to inspiring even WandaVision, I think. But I don’t know how right that would’ve been for someone who’s mourning to create a robot wife and it just didn’t quite feel like that would be the story. I think Ultron is kind of encouraging him to be more human than he is. So, in some ways, Ultron is a father figure, he’s a therapist, and he’s also the devil on his shoulder. So, it’s an interesting combo that I think was the idea for this show.

Bettany added that while he considers the original Tom King-written Vision comic book series where Vivian “Viv” Vision was introduced to be a “fantastic graphic novel,” it’s also a “different timeline.” In his mind, the MCU has “covered the family story now” for his character, casting doubt on whether Viv will ever be featured in this superhero franchise. Unlike her mother (Virginia) and brother (Vin), Viv made it out of the Vision series alive, and she went on the join the superhero team The Champions.

Despite Viv’s absence from VisionQuest, there was a direct connection drawn between Bettany’s Vision and another one of Marvel’s prominent android characters. It was revealed that him, Ultron, and all the other A.I. personas were descended from J.I.M., better known in the comics as the Golden Age Human Torch), who was created by Dr. Phineas Horton in the image of his late son. Bettany played both those characters in VisionQuest, with Vision encountering manifestations of J.I.M. and Horton when he discovered the former’s source code embedded deep within his programming.

VisionQuest can be streamed in its entirety on Disney+. Bettany is confirmed to reprise Vision in the upcoming Marvel movie Avengers: Secret Wars, which will be released in theaters on December 17, 2027. It hasn’t been announced yet if/when Ultron, Ruaridh Mollica's Tommy, and//or Joe Locke’s Billy will appear in the MCU again.
