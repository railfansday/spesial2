HYBE Makes Major Market Push in Japan With Bid to Acquire KeyHolder

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

HYBE executives believe this deal will strengthen their position in the global music industry.

By Elizabeth Dilts Marshall

In a major push into the Japanese music market, South Korean music company HYBE said its Japanese subsidiary plans to acquire the Tokyo-listed entertainment and media firm KeyHolder, according to a press release issued Wednesday (Nov. 18).

Japan is the second-largest market globally for recorded music, with South Korea as the seventh. HYBE executives say the two countries’ music industries are intertwined, and the deal would create opportunities for both companies by expanding HYBE’s scope in local artist management, content production and advertising and opening up HYBE’s global platform to KeyHolders’ artists.

KeyHolder’s stock closed 3.15% lower on Wednesday at 1,044 Japanese yen ($6.56) following news of HYBE’s takeover bid.

Previously known as an arcade company, KeyHolder expanded into TV and video production, talent management and event and studio operations starting in 2017. It now operates the talent agencies Zest, whose biggest group is SKE48, North River, which together with Sony owns Nogizaka 46‘s holding company, the talent and model agency A.M. Entertainment and the advertising and digital content company allfuz.

“KeyHolder is a proven entertainment company with a highly competitive IP portfolio and extensive infrastructure in Japan,” Jason Jaesang Lee, HYBE CEO, said in a statement. “This acquisition will serve as a catalyst for advancing HYBE’s multihome, multigenre strategy.”

HYBE JAPAN currently operates the subsidiary labels YX LABELS and JCONIC and adapts content and services, including those related to its global stars, BTS and KATSEYE, for a Japanese audience.

“By combining KeyHolder’s leading Japanese artist IP with HYBE’s production capabilities and global platforms, we aim to strengthen our competitiveness in the global music market,” Youngmin Kim, chairman of HYBE JAPAN said in a statement.
