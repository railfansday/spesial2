Hailey Bieber Stuns in Nude 'Vogue France' Photo Released After 30th B-Day

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Hailey Bieber left little to the imagination while posing nude for a Vogue France photo shoot.

Bieber, who celebrated her 30th birthday on Sunday, November 22, graced the cover of the magazine’s December/January issue, which dropped on Tuesday, November 24, and featured a black-and-white photo of her sitting naked on the ground. In the image, Bieber turned her face toward the camera and had her legs curled up under her arms.

The beauty mogul also posed for additional photos in a red latex corset, gold-embellished top and matching pants and a black lace bra with a red skirt. In the interview, Bieber opened up about transitioning from modeling to launching a massive beauty empire with Rhode.

“It’s first and foremost about knowing how you want to present yourself to the world and daring to fully embrace your place in it,” she said, when asked what the word “independence” means to her today. “At 17, I had already left my parents’ house and was earning my own living. It might seem incongruous, but marriage was also a form of emancipation: it’s a decision that completely commits you to who you are.”

She continued, “And then there was Rhode! A space of freedom where I could set my own rules. Ever since I was little, I’ve been obsessed with everything related to beauty. I can still picture myself sitting in a makeup chair during a shoot, thinking, ‘One day, I want to launch my own brand too!’ I knew it would be in beauty, cosmetics, but I had to wait for the right moment. Because I knew that I wouldn’t be a model all my life, that this profession, however enriching it may be, would open up other horizons for me.”

While noting that the modeling industry “imposes its own rules,” the interviewer asked Bieber how she manages to “break free” from them.

“It came quite naturally because I hate being pigeonholed! I obediently follow the rules when it comes to safety on a plane, but definitely not when I’m told what to do… or not to do. I’m a believer in delusional optimism, that slightly crazy optimism that consists of believing anything is possible – and that can’t stand being contradicted!” she said. “Furthermore, I lent my face and name to brands for a long time. And I was able to gauge the impact of my involvement, which gave me the confidence I needed to launch Rhode. Without imagining it would become so big. No matter how much you dream of reaching as many people as possible, nothing is ever guaranteed.”
