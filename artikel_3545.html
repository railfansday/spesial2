Jennifer Lopez Makes Rare Comment About 'Tough' Marc Anthony Divorce

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Jennifer Lopez makes a rare comment about her 2011 split from ex-husband Marc Anthony in her upcoming concert special.

“After my third divorce — that’s when I really started getting good at it,” the “Let’s Get Loud” singer, 57, tells her Las Vegas audience with a laugh in The JLO Show, which premieres on CBS and Paramount+ Thursday, November 26. “It’s not funny. It’s funny! It is funny. It’s not funny.”

Lopez candidly admits on stage that her separation from Anthony, 58, was “a really tough time,” as the couple had welcomed their twins, Max and Emme, only three years prior.

“It was one of those moments in life where you’re just about to give up on it all,” she reflects. “You’re just like, ‘I’ve given up on love. I’m giving up on life, the whole thing.’ But I had these two little baby twins, you know?”

The Grammy nominee tells the crowd that she decided to call one of her “mentors,” motivational speaker and self-help author Louise Hay, for some guidance at the time.

“I said, ‘I don’t know what I’m going to do. I don’t know how to go on,'” she recalls. “And she said, ‘Jennifer, you’re a dancer, right?’ I said, ‘Yes, I am.’ And she said, ‘When you’re learning a dance and you get the steps wrong, what do you do?’ I said, ‘I just keep going until I get the steps right.’ And she said, ‘That’s it, Jennifer. Always keep dancing.'”

Lopez and Anthony wed in 2004 and finalized their divorce in 2014. She was previously married to Ojani Noa from 1997 to 1998 and Cris Judd from 2001 to 2003.

The Hustlers actress went on to say “I do” a fourth time, eloping with Ben Affleck in 2022. The pair, who first dated in the early aughts, called it quits again in 2024 and divorced in 2025.

These days, Lopez is not thinking about dating again.

“I’ve learned a lot over the past 20 years, but also the past couple of years have really been about becoming more self-aware and really understanding what love is,” she recently told Variety. “I had a real idea about love as a little girl, and moving through this crazy life that I found myself in, I realized that what I thought love was really isn’t love.”

The “Jenny From the Block” singer continued, “Love is a secure, safe feeling. Marriages, people can promise you the world and commit and do all the ceremonious things and do all these big gestures, but love is a different thing than that. It’s a very different thing than that. So, the weddings and the marriages, I realize they’re not the thing, and even the promises are not the thing. It’s the actions, it’s the showing up, it’s the understanding. It’s not the leaving. It’s the being consistent.”

The JLO Show: Live in Las Vegas airs on CBS Thursday, November 26, at 8 p.m. ET. An extended version will be available to stream on Paramount+.
