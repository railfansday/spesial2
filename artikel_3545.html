Marvel Officially Debuts New Redesigns For Classic Villains

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Two famous Spider-Man villains are reinvented from scratch in Marvel's new universe, with a tragic twist. Many members of Spider-Man’s classic rogues' gallery share a set of traits that make them instantly recognizable as the wall-crawler's foes. A large number of Peter Parker’s enemies originate from scientific experiments. Doctor Octopus, the Lizard, and Green Goblin are brilliant scientists turned monsters after their ambitions get the best of them, while Electro, Sandman, and Rhino, are ordinary people whose lives are flipped upside down when experimental technology warps their bodies and minds.

Besides, many Spider-Man villains are mirrors to Peter Parker and reflect the monster he could have become without Uncle Ben’s moral lesson. Where Peter uses his arachnid powers and scientific intellect to protect New York without a guaranteed reward, Doctor Octopus and the Green Goblin weaponize their genius to dominate. Electro, and the Lizard constantly lose sight of their self-control, while Sandman and the Spot are motivated by their self-serving goals, and Rhino and Hydro-Man regularly offer their skills to the highest bidder.

Notably, Spider-Man's villains also tend to be based on animals. Apart from the Lizard, Rhino, and Doctor Octopus, Spidey often battles avian and reptilian predators like Vulture, Chameleon, the Jackal, and Puma. Insectoid and arachnid antagonists push the biological mirror even further, from Swarm and the Fly to the Beetle, Shathra, and Doppelganger.

After Peter Parker is bitten by a radioactive spider he accidentally brought home from Oscorp in Midnight Spider-Man #1, Dr. Otto Octavius orders Felicia Hardy to retrieve the arachnid, threatening to transform her into one of his brainwashed assistants. Felicia arrives at the Parkers' home flanked by two agents, Gargan and Rodríguez, who are promptly torn apart by a mutated Peter Parker. "Gargan" is quite clearly Mag Gargan a.k.a. the Scorpion, while Rodríguez is likely Tarantula, as Anton Miguel's daughter is named Jacinda Rodríguez on Earth-616.

Spider-Man: Brand New Day marks the first-ever live-action appearance of Scorpion and Tarantula in costume.

Unfortunately, Gargan and Rodríguez' time in Midnight Spider-Man #2 is short, and the mutated Peter Parker leaves little room for their recovery. After revealing his presence, Peter rips Rodríguez's head with his bare hands, and he pulls Gargan's body apart like a pillow. As Peter jumps out of a window, Felicia Hardy realizes she's been left with a tiny cut on her wrist, which might have been infected by the spider attached to Peter's body. At the end of Midnight Spider-Man #2, Marvel delivers a final surprise with the introduction of Misty Knight, who works as a patrol officer.

Although Scorpion and Rodriguez suffer seemingly fatal injuries moments after their debut in Phillip Kennedy Johnson and ScieTronc’s Midnight Spider-Man #2, both characters still hold tremendous untapped potential inside the Midnight Universe. In a reality defined by grotesque biological experiments, fatal wounds delivered by the newly mutated Peter Parker shouldn't mark the true end of characters who could transform into monstrous creatures themselves. Oscorp could recover their mangled remains from Peter's house and subject them to extreme genetic splicing or cybernetic reconstruction, either to weaponize their bodies or to repurpose them as cyborg assistants for Doctor Octopus.

Spider-Man's classic motto just got a whole lot darker, featuring a truly monstrous twist as a new era for the Webslinger begins from Marvel Comics.

Alternatively, Gargan and Rodríguez' wounds could trigger a natural metamorphosis that transforms them into a duo of literal Scorpion and Tarantula monsters. Although they endure injuries that seem completely incompatible with life, Gargan and Rodriguez could mutate into even more terrifying mutates than Peter Parker himself: Scorpion could spawn a tail, and Rodríguez could sprout multiple legs. If they were to survive, the result might be somewhere around the middle, with mutations that come as a direct result of their wounds and cybernetically restored bodies designed by Oscorp. This concept has already been used before in continuities like The Amazing Spider-Man movie and game, which introduced similar villain reinventions.
