Tencent's founder is willing to move half its workforce into AI-native businesses

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Tencent's public relations director said the 50% figure is an example of organizational change, not a layoff target

Tencent founder Pony Ma told employees he is willing to redeploy half of the company's personnel to AI-native businesses, according to Bloomberg.

Ma spoke at Tencent's 28th anniversary meeting on Wednesday, according to TokenPost. He described the company's main operations as an army, with its new AI work playing the part of a navy. "The army must leverage AI to maximize its efficiency, while we redeploy 50% of our personnel to the navy to support AI-native businesses," he said, according to Bloomberg.

Bloomberg said the speech seemed aimed at getting the entire workforce to adopt AI instead of announcing a major restructuring. Ma said one portion of Tencent, comprising half the company, would work on making current businesses run more efficiently, and the remaining portion would look for new ways to grow.

Tencent's public relations director said the 50% number was an example of organizational change rather than a goal for job cuts, according to TokenPost. Tencent has not publicly outlined any move to cut half of its staff, TokenPost said. Social media posts in China claimed that the company would cut half of its staff.

Ma also pushed for a less hierarchical setup with fewer layers of middle managers, according to Bloomberg. He said the push is meant to reshape the way teams operate and speed up decisions using the current workforce, setting it apart from previous cost-cutting and efficiency campaigns, according to TokenPost. He asked managers to limit drawn-out discussions with staff when handing out new assignments, TokenPost said.

It is not yet known how Tencent intends to restructure, according to Bloomberg. The company's last companywide reorganization came in 2018. Tencent had 115,927 employees as of June, up from 111,221 a year earlier.

Tencent's AI spending has climbed. Capital expenditures for AI projects and computing rose 176% to 52.8 billion yuan ($7.9 billion) in the quarter ended in June, as the company works to close ground on ByteDance and Alibaba.

Tencent stock has fallen about a third so far this year, which puts the company's valuation near $470 billion. Ma described Tencent as a "leaky ship" at a shareholder gathering in May, according to Bloomberg, after the company's Yuanbao chatbot failed to overtake ByteDance's Doubao and its work on large language models fell behind.

Tencent is scheduled to release its third-quarter results on Nov. 18. A Tencent spokesperson did not respond to Bloomberg's request for comment.
