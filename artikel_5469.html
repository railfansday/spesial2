Concrete Partners with Fypher and ether.fi to Bring Institutional Vault Infrastructure to fyUSD

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The collaboration brings Concrete Vaults to fyUSD, launching with $10 million in initial minting and ether.fi as the primary vault depositor.

NEW YORK, Nov. 11, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Concrete, the full-stack institutional operating system for on-chain finance, today announced a collaboration with Fypher and ether.fi to bring institutional vault infrastructure to fyUSD, a new stablecoin built for institutional users in Asia.

fyUSD is expected to launch with $10 million in initial minting, with Concrete serving as the vault infrastructure partner from day one and ether.fi as the primary depositor into the Fypher vault.

The collaboration brings together Fypher's stablecoin infrastructure, Concrete's vault architecture, and ether.fi's capital deployment to connect institutional stablecoins with on-chain financial markets.

The result is an integrated infrastructure stack designed to bring institutional capital on-chain and put it to work.

Bringing On-Chain Yield Infrastructure to fyUSD

As stablecoins become core infrastructure for moving dollars on-chain, the opportunity increasingly extends beyond issuance and settlement to deploying capital productively.

Through this collaboration, eligible fyUSD will have access to Concrete Vaults, creating a direct path from the stablecoin into on-chain yield strategies.

Concrete Vaults provide a single infrastructure layer for deploying assets across on-chain strategies, abstracting away the complexity of allocation, execution, accounting, and rebalancing behind a single vault position.

ether.fi will serve as the primary depositor into the Fypher vault, deploying capital through Concrete's infrastructure. This brings together institutional stablecoin infrastructure and an established DeFi protocol through a unified vault architecture.

fyUSD itself does not generate yield. Eligible holders may separately access yield-generating strategies through Concrete Vaults, subject to applicable terms and risks.
