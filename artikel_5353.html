S&P might downgrade Nvidia's credit over Jensen Huang governance concerns

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

S&P said Nvidia's AA rating, the third-highest level, could drop one notch after a 90-day review of the company's management

Heather Diehl / Bloomberg via Getty Images

S&P Global $SPGI Ratings is weighing a one-notch downgrade of Nvidia $NVDA's credit rating, pointing to unease over how the chipmaker is managed and governed after CEO Jensen Huang's exit and subsequent comeback, according to Bloomberg.

The rating agency said Nvidia's AA grade, which is the third-highest tier, could be lowered once a 90-day review ends, a process that puts the company on CreditWatch with negative implications, according to Bloomberg.

"We expect the potential downgrade to be limited to one notch, unless investigations or disclosures lead us to believe Nvidia's reputation or future business prospects have been harmed," S&P said in a statement on Wednesday, according to Bloomberg.

Nvidia's board brought Huang back as CEO on Monday, less than two weeks after he stepped down following reports of two extramarital affairs. One of the relationships was with a woman suspected of spying for China. Alice Huang, who has no family relationship to Jensen Huang, worked as an executive for Megaspeed, a Singapore-based Nvidia customer.

Nvidia said in a statement that its board "received input from senior levels of the United States government" and is confident the reinstatement "poses no national security risk." The board had opened an independent investigation earlier this month after Jensen Huang disclosed the affair to employees. The board said nothing it has seen suggests Nvidia's trade secrets or U.S. national security were put at risk.

The Justice and Commerce departments said on Nov. 17 that they were looking into whether Megaspeed redirected Nvidia chips in breach of American export rules.

Several Republican lawmakers have criticized the reinstatement and said they would seek answers. Jensen Huang and Nvidia General Counsel Tim Teter have confirmed they will appear for transcribed interviews with House committees on Dec. 1 and Dec. 2. The Senate Banking Committee has announced a public hearing with Huang on Dec. 3.
