The Tilt Engage is a Visa credit card for people with little credit history or a damaged score. Petal is now part of Tilt, and the Engage took the place of the Petal 1 Rise with the same $59 annual fee and the same 1% to 10% cash back at select merchants.

Tilt considers each application for all three of its cards and makes you its best offer, so the Engage suits only people Tilt won’t approve for the Motion. Anyone who can set aside a refundable deposit should open a no-fee secured card instead.
