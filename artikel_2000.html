Mauricio Pochettino has recommended Paul Mitchell as Manchester United's next sporting director to replace Jason Wilcox, Football Insider understands.

Pochettino has arrived in Manchester and is ready to take charge of his first match against Liverpool on Sunday after signing a contract until June 2029.

Football Insider revealed last week that the new United manager wants director of football Wilcox out and that was discussed with the club hierarchy during his discussions about taking over the team.

Wilcox has come under fire for United's poor form over the last few months alongside sacked ex-boss Michael Carrick.

Sources say that Pochettino is keen to see Mitchell replace Wilcox at Old Trafford after the pair previously worked well together at Southampton and Tottenham.

The 45-year-old was reportedly considered by INEOS to be their new director of football in October 2023 before Wilcox took the position in April 2024.

Wilcox is facing widespread scrutiny after United failed to add real quality and depth to their squad in the summer window.

He has overseen successive failed managers in the last two-and-a-half years, and Pochettino is clearly determined not to be the next boss to struggle under Wilcox's watch.

The Red Devils' new appointment believes that Mitchell would be the ideal fit to replace Wilcox above him in their club hierarchy.

They were successful in their work together at both Southampton and Tottenham before the former Wigan defender quit the latter in 2016 after a fallout with Daniel Levy.

Mitchell went on to work in similar roles at the likes of Red Bull Leipzig and Monaco, then spent 11 months as Newcastle's sporting director between 2024 and 2025.

He departed St James' Park after failing to build a rapport with Eddie Howe and is yet to return to football.

The pair worked closely at Southampton from 2013 to 2014 as the club went from strength to strength in the Premier League, then Mitchell followed Pochettino to Spurs.

He identified several gems for the Argentine boss to recruit in North London, but was allowed to leave in 2016 despite their strong ongoing relationship.

Pochettino admitted his upset at Mitchell leaving the club to the press at the time, saying: "I feel very disappointed with his decision. It's his personal choice. You know that we have a relationship from Southampton. I'm very disappointed but when someone takes a decision and it's personal, what can I do?

"Did I try to persuade Mitchell to stay? I think it's obvious that happened. When you take your choice and personal things happen, there's nothing to do other than respect his decision but carry on working."
