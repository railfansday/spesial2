Oliver Glasner tipped to 'go back to Crystal Palace' after leaving Nottingham Forest - expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Oliver Glasner could well return to Crystal Palace if he is sacked at Nottingham Forest.

That is the opinion of experienced former Aston Villa, Tottenham and Everton scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The Austrian only left the Eagles in the summer after seeing out the end of his contract, having enjoyed a hugely successful two years at Selhurst Park.

Glasner was replaced by Pierre Sage, who now appears to be on the brink of departure following just two Premier League wins from the opening nine games of the season.

Nottingham Forest have won just one, with Glasner at significant risk if his side cannot produce a positive result in their final game before the international break.

The Tricky Trees will welcome Manchester City to the City Ground on Saturday, looking to take advantage of a slight blip for Enzo Maresca's team.

The relationship between Glasner and Palace chairman Steve Parish had become exceedingly toxic mid-way through last season.

The Austrian felt that his squad had been abandoned, and made it clear that he would not renew his contract.

With his team on a terrible run of form, Palace considered sacking Glasner before both sides ultimately agreed to end the season amicably, leading to Conference League glory.

"They're a side that are struggling at the moment, Palace", King told Football Insider. "And without any doubt, the coach now must be under pressure.

"If you're talking about [Michael] Carrick and Glasner at Nottingham Forest. There you go - if he gets a sack at Nottingham Forest, he'll go back to Palace.

"The only way he went to Nottingham Forest is because of the money that was on offer. There's no other sane way to go and work for a bloke like that owner, because you know what's going to come. You lose three matches and you're in trouble."

Even after the international break, things do not get easier for Nottingham Forest.

Bournemouth and Chelsea will be up first before a trip to Hull City and the visit of Brighton.

Glasner will be lucky to still have his job by that stage, however. "It's now time not to make compromises, it's now time to show a clear way and everybody has to follow," he said following Saturday's defeat to Brentford.

"And if they don't want to follow, then I guess then the sixth manager will come in."
