9 People Injured During Mass Shooting at Concert Believed to Be Gang Related

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Nine people were shot during a mass shooting at a private concert in California, and one person has been left in critical condition following the horrific incident.

The Tulare County Sheriff’s Office announced in a press release that the shooting broke out around 7:40 p.m. on Saturday, November 21, at a concert in Strathmore, California.

Officials said that an unspecified number of suspects started shooting at the crowd. No arrests have been announced as of time of publication.

One of the victims who was shot was a 26-year-old man, who was reportedly struck in the chest. He sustained severe injuries, which required him to undergo surgery. Officials said that the victim is currently in critical condition.

The sheriff’s office added that two other victims – who are 19 and 22 years old – also underwent surgery, while six people sustained minor injuries in the shooting.

“Initial reports indicated that the suspects opened fire into the crowd, hitting at least nine people. When Deputies arrived, they immediately began rendering first aid to the victims, while several others drove themselves to local hospitals for treatment,” the sheriff’s office wrote in a follow-up press release.

Authorities said that the shooting “may be gang-related,” but they did not elaborate on the theory or share if they have any suspects on their radar.

The Porterville Recorder reported that the shooting took place at a concert that was privately organized by the “norteño gang.” The outlet also reported that “it was also reported investigators had not determined if there was security at the event.”

The Tulare County Sheriff’s Office did not immediately respond to Us’ request for comment when contacted about updates in the case.

The concert was attended by about 150 people, who gathered to watch rappers and other performers from neighboring states take the stage. San Jose-based rap artist Lazy Boy served as the event’s headliner.

An investigation into the incident remains ongoing as authorities work to determine how many shooters there were, who they are and if they had a motive.

The Tulare County Sheriff’s Office has asked that anyone with information regarding the incident contact the office at 559-733-6218.

The recent incident marks the 420th mass shootings in the United States so far in 2026, according to the Gun Violence Archive. The archive defines mass shootings as cases in which four or more victims are either wounded or killed after being shot at.
