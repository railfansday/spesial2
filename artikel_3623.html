A Look Back at the Times Kristen Bell and Dax Shepard Defended Their Marriage

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Kristen Bell and Dax Shepard have never been shy about the realities of their relationship.

Since tying the knot in 2013, the couple — who share daughters Lincoln and Delta — have weathered public scrutiny over everything from controversial social media posts to celebrity hot takes about their pairing. Through it all, both Bell and Shepard have consistently shown up for each other and spoken candidly about the work that goes into their marriage.

Keep reading for a look back at the key moments, from most recent to earliest:

Bell and Shepard became the subject of online discourse for the third time in a year when the Nobody Wants This star revealed the surprising way her husband flirts with her: complimenting other people.

“If we go to a dinner or something, like, there’s a couple things Dax does where he doesn’t have to look up and wink at me, but I know that he’s just being the best boy,” she said during an October 2026 episode of Shepard’s podcast. “We’ll be at dinner, and, like, he’ll say to the waiter or waitress, he’ll go, ‘Wow, you have incredible eyelashes, gorgeous eyes, great job.’”

Bell insisted that this is a form of flirting with her because she loves that “he just made someone else feel good.”

“‘Cause he’s gonna have zero interaction with — let’s say it is a girl. He’s gonna have zero interaction with this girl for the rest of his life,” she continued. “The chances of him ever having another conversation with her are so microscopic. But to me, to know that my partner actively smiles at people and gives them compliments [makes me feel good].”

Days after the clip went viral, Bell gushed about his “commitment to growth” during an appearance on The Drew Barrymore Show.

“He’s committed to growth in all aspects,” she said, adding that he’s a “lifelong learner.”

Bell continued, “I call him a vulneraboy. Because he is vulnerable. He knows a lot of stuff, but he’s also not afraid or ashamed to say, ‘I don’t know, I’ve never experienced this before.’ And that’s very sexy.”

Shepard broke his silence on the flirting claims during a November 2026 episode of his podcast, calling out the “weird narrative” spreading online and painting him as “controlling, abusive, now flirting with people in public, which I don’t do.”

“We’re saying I flirt with Kristen by tipping people a lot, and I flirt with Kristen. Yeah, by complimenting people. If you think I’m complimenting hot young waitresses, you got a problem, right?” he explained. “I’m finding the dude that I don’t think has had a compliment in a while and telling him I love his shoes. It’s always somebody that I want to make their day. And so those compliments [are] flirting with Kristen. … I will offer publicly $10 million to any server you can find that I have given my number to, or hit on.”

Shepard said he found the narrative “deeply insulting” to Bell because it implies that she allows emotional abuse around their daughters, Delta and Lincoln. He also noted that he’s “dedicated his whole life” to breaking the cycle of abuse he grew up around.

“I don’t ever yell at my children,” he said. “I don’t swear at them. I don’t yell at my wife. You know, like, to have given it my all to be the opposite of what now some group of people is saying I am is hard, of course. So hard. I don’t love it.”

Shepard concluded, “Generally, I can brush [these controversies] off, but when we’re now on year two of now, there’s a new [one] … then it just starts making me think like, ‘Oh, is this now my f***ing role going forward is just, like, every four months there’s another thing about me being a terrible man. And that makes me want to go away.”

Nearly six months after Bell stirred up controversy with a wedding anniversary tribute, Shepard finally broke his silence on the drama during the Monday, April 6, episode of his “Armchair Expert” podcast with guest Nikki Glaser.

“I was alerted by someone that this whole thing was happening,” Shepard recalled, brushing off the criticism surrounding their marriage after critics of Bell’s October 2025 Instagram post suggested that she walk away and leave their relationship for good. “I didn’t know, and Kristen knew through her publicist but didn’t tell me. So, I actually didn’t know, and this was brought to my attention.”

Shepard faced backlash after Bell gushed over “the man who once said to me: ‘I would never kill you.’ A lot of men have killed their wives at a certain point. Even though I’m heavily incentivized to kill you, I never would.” She shared the controversial message in honor of the special occasion.

Glaser revealed she had initially planned to poke fun at the situation during the 2026 Golden Globe Awards in January 2026, but cut it because she didn’t know where Shepard stood on the topic.

As she prepared to host The Actors Awards, Bell gushed about her husband in an interview with People, noting that he uplifts her spirits and never tries to put her down.

“There’s nothing that he won’t do to take care of me,” Bell said. “One thing that I love so much about being married to him is that he is never allergic to me shining more and more. He never gets insecure about it.”

She added that being married for over a decade is a “long time,” noting, “We put a lot of work into it and I’m very happy. We’re very supportive of each other and our marriage and our family.”

Cher told Shepard and Bell to their faces that she thought Bell could “do better” in terms of finding a romantic partner during a January 2026 appearance on Shepard’s “Armchair Expert” podcast. The Good Place actress didn’t let it go to her head, instead stating that Shepard was “almost too good” for her, telling them both it was a “slow burn” that’s lasted.

During the press tour for season 2 of Nobody Wants This, Bell drew parallels between her marriage and the show’s central romance. “I feel like my real-life husband and I are ‘opposites attract,'” she told People, noting they have grown stronger as a couple despite facing constant scrutiny in the public eye.

That was the same month Bell’s 12th wedding anniversary post to Shepard sparked backlash. The National Network to End Domestic Violence responded to her controversial post, telling Page Six, “There’s nothing funny about domestic violence ‘jokes’ that trivialize the very real fear, trauma and pain that millions of victims and survivors face each day.”

During a conversation with Alicia Silverstone on “Armchair Expert,” Shepard declared he would never speak negatively about his marriage with Bell, even hypothetically.

“I say this all the time, like, if Kristen and I ever split up, I would only describe this as still an enormously successful relationship,” Shepard said. “Like, we loved each other to death. We had these two beautiful children. We had this great time together.”

During an appearance on The View, Bell defended Shepard’s second date move of taking gum from her mouth and chewing it — a story that “divided the hosts” and audience.

“I personally thought it was very cute and it was the indicator, after we had been talking and chatting for awhile and I was like, ‘Oh, this guy’s kinda cute and special,'” Bell said. “And then I guess I was being flirty and took it out of my mouth and then he took it. And I was like, ‘Well…’ I said, ‘Understood.'”

Bell set the record straight after seeing a negative remark about herself and Shepard. “Every piece of news I see about them talks about how they just can’t f***ng stand each other, constantly fight, now this dude is apparently objectifying her as well. just what’s the point?” a social media user wrote via Instagram in February 2021.

Bell fired back at the commenter, “We adore each other, we just try to always be honest about how marriage, or companionship in any form, is hard sometimes.” She added, “You cant always be in control, or right, and its important to us that we lead with the honesty of your ‘ perfect match ‘ being a myth. You gotta work hard to love yourself, and love other humans. Xo.”
