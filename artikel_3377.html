The holiday season is officially right around the corner, with Thanksgiving this Thursday and Christmas only a few weeks away. As the end-of-year festivities kick into high gear, there is no better time to unwind and catch up on your watchlist from the comfort of home—and free streaming has just the perfect collection. From heartwarming holiday classics to blockbuster movies and binge-worthy new series, you can make the most of the upcoming break with the best free streaming offers, including a new addition: Anya Taylor-Joy's fantasy masterpiece, which boasts a 90% Rotten Tomatoes score.

Best known for playing chess prodigy Beth Harmon in the Netflix miniseries The Queen's Gambit, Taylor-Joy has gone on to build a versatile career spanning thriller, fantasy, comedy, action, and science fiction. One of her most addictive fantasy works is now much easier to watch, arriving on the video-on-demand service Fandango ahead of Christmas and after Thanksgiving, TV Insider can exclusively reveal.

Starting December 3, you can stream The Northman for free on Fandango. This comes four years after its theatrical release, during which it earned rave reviews from critics and found success on VOD, though it underperformed at the box office. The Norse film joins a lineup of familiar holiday favorites arriving on the platform in December, including the 1994 classic Miracle on 34th Street, A Christmas Carol starring George C. Scott, The Family Stone, The Fabelmans, and The Passion of the Christ.

Four worlds and four completely different ideas of what a good life looks like. This is where you belong, what it would ask of you, and what you would get back in return.

You belong somewhere old enough that the mountains have names and somebody can still tell you who gave them. Middle-earth is for people who think the right thing is worth doing even when it will not be finished in their lifetime.

You want the sea, a crew, and absolutely nobody telling you where you are allowed to go. The Grand Line is the world for people whose main ambition in life is finding out what is over there.

You would rather be feared and useful than liked and useless. The Continent is a world where the monsters are the straightforward part of the job and the people are where it all gets complicated.

You want the myths to be real and loose in the actual world, and you want to find out at about thirteen that you were never anywhere near as ordinary as everybody kept telling you.

Starring Alexander Skarsgård as Viking warrior prince Amleth, The Northman follows his vengeful journey and explores how far he will go to avenge his father's murder, King Aurvandill "War-Raven" (Ethan Hawke). The king was apparently beheaded by his brother, Fjölnir (Claes Bang), who staged a palace coup. Amleth narrowly escapes Fjölnir's assassins by fleeing by boat, vowing to kill Fjölnir and rescue his mother, Queen Gudrún (Nicole Kidman). Along his quest for revenge, Amleth meets Olga (Taylor-Joy), a sorceress who shares his goal of defeating Fjölnir.

Directed by Robert Eggers, who co-wrote the screenplay with Sjón, The Northman is among the heaviest revenge movies of all time according to a recent Collider ranking. It is also considered one of the greatest epic fantasy movies, with critics consistently praising its visuals, Eggers's visionary direction, and the central cast's brilliant performances. Similarly, in Collider's review, Ross Bonaime gave the film a B+, commending Eggers's leap to a large-scale epic with a more expansive storyline while maintaining his distinctive style and substance.

The Northman arrives on Fandango next week.
