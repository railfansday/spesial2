Bybit Fiat-to-Crypto Frenzy Returns With 80,000 USDT in Weekly Prizes, Simplifying On-Ramp to Crypto

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

DUBAI, UAE, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Bybit, the New Financial Platform trusted by more than 80 million users worldwide, has relaunched its Fiat-to-Crypto Frenzy rewards fiesta, offering new users a streamlined way to build digital asset holdings with an 80,000 USDT weekly prize pool and a participation threshold as low as 20 USDT.

From November 5, 2026 to March 30, 2027, Bybit's Fiat-to-Crypto Frenzy event distills the crypto onboarding journey into three steps and rewards every milestone along the way.

For many first-time participants, a wide gap between interest in digital assets and actual ownership remains. Complex interfaces, fragmented payment options, and multi-step conversion flows that could take up days continue to slow adoption across the industry.

Bybit's fiat-to-crypto experience has the answer. The platform supports over 65 fiat currencies through three complementary channels: One-Click Buy for instant card and bank purchases, Fiat Deposit for direct bank transfers, and Bybit P2P for flexible peer-to-peer transactions in local currencies for eligible users. Together, these channels give new entrants access to more than 300 digital assets, including BTC, ETH, USDT and popular altcoins, without requiring prior crypto holdings or multi-step wallet setups.

Each completed task generates Lucky Draw tickets, with every ticket representing one entry into the weekly prize draw for a share of the 80,000 USDT pool. The weekly reset means consistent participation throughout the nearly five-month campaign period can compound into multiple chances at rewards.

Bybit is committed to removing friction from the crypto experience. Behind every deposit and trade sits the platform's institutional-grade infrastructure: deep global liquidity, real-time order matching, and a payment rail engineered for speed and reliability across dozens of currencies and geographies. For new entrants, that means less time navigating technical complexity and more time engaging with the market.

Fiat-to-Crypto Frenzy is available exclusively to newly registered Bybit users who meet eligibility requirements during the event period. Terms and conditions apply.
