Why Ballard Power Systems Stock Is Powering Down Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

A prognostication about profitability is doing little to excite investors.

Showing no signs of recovering from its 8.8% slide last week, Ballard Power Systems (BLDP -10.71%) is off to an inauspicious start this week after reporting disappointing third-quarter financial results.

As of 1:51 p.m. ET, Ballard shares are down 11.8%.

Ballard reported third-quarter earnings that failed to meet analysts' expectations. Instead of posting sales of $32.1 million, as analysts expected, Ballard reported $22.5 million on the top line, a 31% year-over-year decrease.

At the bottom of the income statement, Ballard reported a $0.07 loss per share from continuing operations -- worse than the $0.04 analysts anticipated.

Suggesting that profitability is in sight, Marty Neese, Ballard's CEO, stated, "Following the successful closing of our GeoPura acquisition, we have established a pathway to profitability, targeting positive adjusted EBITDA [earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization] by Q4 2027 and full-year profitability for 2028."

Ballard reported an order backlog of $180.6 million as of Sept. 30, 2026, representing a 15.3% increase from the end of June 2026.

As a result of the August acquisition of GeoPure, Ballard has become a vertically integrated provider of hydrogen and fuel cell solutions, including hydrogen production, logistics, refueling, and zero-emission fuel cell engines.

Unsurprisingly, investors are bailing on Ballard stock after the company reported its Q3 2026 financial results. With the company failing to meet analysts' expectations and management suggesting that investors will have to wait until the end of 2027 before it achieves positive adjusted EBITDA, there doesn't seem to be much incentive to hang on to Ballard stock right now, especially when there are other, more compelling hydrogen stocks to consider.
