Collegium Pharmaceutical, Inc. (COLL +6.51%) reported record total net revenue for the third quarter of 2026, primarily driven by a significant transition toward its ADHD franchise. Management reported that ADHD products now account for more than 36% of total revenue, up from 19% in the prior year, as the company integrates Azstarys into its commercial platform alongside Jornay PM. The company stated that while the pain portfolio faced pressure from generic competition and payer dynamics, it remains a primary source of cash flow to support strategic priorities, including debt repayment and share repurchases. During the quarter, the company launched an accelerated share repurchase program and reaffirmed its full-year 2026 financial guidance across all primary metrics.
