Amazon Prime Makes 14 Games Free to Play in November, Including Madden NFL 27

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Check out Kingdom Come Deliverance 2, Mixtape, and more

Each month, Amazon Prime members get two new batches of PC games as part of their membership: one batch to keep, and another to stream for free via Amazon's cloud gaming service, Luna. The Luna library includes some standout titles, and even added the new Star Wars: Galactic Racer last month.

Headlining the newest additions to the Luna library is Madden NFL 27. It's the latest in the long-running football sim series. Steer a franchise, create your own superstar player, or put together a dream team in Ultimate Team mode. New additions for this year's Madden include a personality engine for players that makes negotiating contracts unique and updated interactions for receivers and defensive backs to make passing more realistic. Madden NFL 27 is a fairly new title to land on Luna already, but its arrival is a bit of cross-promotion as Amazon's Nicolas Cage-starring Madden movie, about coach John Madden and the namesake of the game series, lands on Amazon Prime on Nov. 18.

Also heading to Luna are NBA the Run, Disney Epic Mickey: Rebrushed, Mixtape, Kingdom Come Deliverance 2, Police Simulator: Patrol Officers, and Sushi Go. They're all available to stream now, except for Sushi Go, which a news release describes as "coming soon."

Unlike Sony's free PlayStation Plus games, which you can only play while your subscription is active, Amazon's free-to-download games come via redeemable codes from various PC gaming storefronts, like the Epic Games Store and GOG. You'll still own and have access to them even if your Amazon Prime subscription ends.
