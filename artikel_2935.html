Heat star duo Bam Adebayo, Giannis Antetokounmpo set new season-high scoring marks vs. Cavs

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

After opening the season with an 0-4 record, the Miami Heat have started to find their rhythm. On Friday, Miami secured a 133-121 victory over the Cleveland Cavaliers at Kaseya Center. Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bam Adebayo were unstoppable, combining for 74 points to spearhead the Heat's offensive effort.

Both Giannis and Adebayo finished with season-high 37 points, becoming just the second duo in Heat history to record at least 35 points apiece in the same game.

Giannis was particularly efficient, converting 15 of his 20 attempts from the field while adding nine rebounds, two assists and a block. Adebayo also made a significant contribution, recording 14 rebounds while shooting 13-of-19 from the field.

While Miami's frontcourt stars attracted most of the attention, Davion Mitchell also played a crucial part in the victory. The Heat guard recorded a season-high 17 assists along with 22 points.

According to Novig, the Miami Heat became the first team in NBA history to have one player score 35-plus points, another record 35-plus points and 10-plus rebounds with zero turnovers, and a third contribute 20-plus points and 15-plus assists.

Heat head coach Erik Spoelstra was also impressed by the numbers his players put up during the game.

"But those are tremendous numbers, and I want our guys gaining confidence offensively and having that feeling that we had three weeks ago. Coming out of training camp and the preseason, we thought there would be some things that would explode offensively," he said.

Miami has now won six of its last eight games and returned to a .500 record after securing its third consecutive victory. The Heat also improved to 2-0 in NBA Cup play.

Despite the defeat, Cleveland received productive performances from its backcourt stars. Donovan Mitchell led the Cavaliers with 25 points and eight rebounds, while James Harden contributed 20 points and eight assists.

However, Cleveland struggled to keep pace with Miami's offensive production in the final quarter. The absence of Evan Mobley also proved significant for the Cavaliers, as the big man missed the game due to a toe injury.
