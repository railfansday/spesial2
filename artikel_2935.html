UiPath's 2027 Outlook: Agentic AI Orchestration as the Driver of Future Growth

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

This under-the-radar information technology stock boasts a Superscore of 81 from our Hidden Gems Primary database, part of The Motley Fool's Moneyball Database system. Here's why.

By Robert Izquierdo – Updated Nov 24, 2026 at 3:59PM EST

When a developer wants to automate a tedious task, they often reach for a software library, but when a corporation needs to coordinate thousands of workflows across legacy systems, specialized software, and human operators, it turns to UiPath (PATH +1.18%). It provides an automation ecosystem that functions as the connective tissue for digital work, allowing software robots to record, process, and execute tasks alongside human staff. As of November 19, 2026, the stock traded at $11.39, reflecting a 16% decline over the past year as the market grapples with the transition from simple task automation to complex agentic orchestration.

The artificial intelligence boom produced a number of AI-focused companies, but UiPath is one that stands out from the pack. Its long-term prospects look promising thanks to the vision of founder Daniel Dines, who has evolved the company's platform from its roots as a robotic process automation tool to infusing RPA with AI, and now moving toward agentic orchestration.

Agentic orchestration involves using AI to coordinate, control, and optimize AI agents, RPA robots, and people across end-to-end business workflows, such as invoice processing and loan applications. Because UiPath has decades of data from its RPA origins, it is well positioned to have AI coordinate and execute key business processes with accuracy and within a company's guidelines. This gives it a competitive advantage over rivals lacking this data depth.

Moreover, UiPath continues to strengthen its financial health. It reached profitability in its 2026 fiscal year, and expects to improve in fiscal 2027 with an operating margin of 7% in the first half compared to 4% in the prior year. Management is also forecasting adjusted free cash flow of $425 million in fiscal 2027 compared to $372 million in 2026. Now, as long as UiPath can continue to grow its customer sales, it is well positioned for success over the long haul.
