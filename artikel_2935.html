Can $300 a Month in VOO Really Build a $1 Million Portfolio? Yes, But There's 1 Factor to Consider.

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Even small investments can build substantial wealth over time.

By David Dierking – Nov 19, 2026 at 3:00PM EST

A $1 million portfolio sounds like something that only high earners have. The good news is that it's something anyone can have. You just need consistent investing and the necessary amount of time to get there.

Time is the most important factor. It's even more important than the rate of return you see on your investment (although that's important too). The more years you have to invest, the greater the likelihood that you'll hit the million-dollar mark.

What does the path look like? Can most people stay on it?

The answer to this is really just a math problem. If you know you're investing $300 a month and your end goal is $1 million, you need to figure out an estimated average annual return over the life of the investment. With that, you can figure out how many years it takes to get there. Let's use the Vanguard S&P 500 ETF (VOO -1.88%), because it's the largest and one of the cheapest exchange-traded funds offering exposure to the index.

The long-term average annual return for the S&P 500 is 10%. We have no way of knowing for sure, but let's say the index keeps returning that. At that rate, the combination of contributions and compounded growth gets you to $1 million in just under 34 years.

As you can see from the table, not much growth happens in the first 10 years or so. It's not until around year 25 that your account balance really starts to take off.

That can be the frustrating part of investing when you're just starting out. Most of your balance is your own contributions. There's very little growth to be had. It's not until you've been consistently doing it for decades that you start to really see results.

That's why it's so important to stick with it. It takes discipline, and only those who invest consistently throughout their entire lives really get to see the long-term results. But once you get there, you'll see quickly how even small, inconsequential investments can grow into substantial wealth over time.
