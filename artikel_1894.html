Rangers 'working on' deal to re-sign Vaclav Cerny after twist - Transfer expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Rangers are working on a deal to re-sign Vaclav Cerny with Besiktas open to letting him move on in the January transfer window.

The 29-year-old has remained firmly on the radar at Ibrox since his loan spell there in the 2024-25 season, and could finally be set to make a sensational return.

Derek McInnes' side explored a deal to land the winger during the summer window, but missed out as Bestikas priced him out of a move due to his role in their plans.

However, former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Rangers believe Cerny could be made available in January after a difficult run of form.

The winger has made 19 appearances across all competitions this season, buth as scored just four goals and provided two assists, leading to reports he could move on.

Rangers are said to be keeping a close eye on Cerny's situation, and should he become available in the mid-season window, they will be among his leading suitors.

Football Insider revealed back in June that Rangers have been keeping a close eye on Cerny's progress with a potential move in mind should he be made available.

They were unable to get a deal over the line during the summer, but their interest has not faded, and they could be prepared to revisit the deal in the mid-season window.

Rangers are believed to be working on the deal behind the scenes, assessing what it might take to bring him in, and could now be tempted to do a deal.

"It's always something to keep an eye on when there has been long-term interest," Brown told Football Insider.

"Rangers obviously know him well after his previous spell at the club, they liked what they saw from him then, and if the opportunity came about, they would like to bring him back.

"If his performances haven't been up to scratch for Besiktas, Rangers could be tempted to take advantage of that.

"So they'll be working on it behind the scenes, assessing whether he might be available and where he would fit in their plans. I think there's every chance they could do a deal."

Rangers have long been keen to bring Cerny back to the club, and have tried on multiple occasions, with the most recent of those coming in the summer window.

However, they ultimately missed out, delaying their transfer plans and forcing them to look elsewhere before the window closed.

Football Insider revealed that Besiktas had placed a "huge" price tag on Cerny which led to Rangers changing their plans, as the Turkish outfit were reluctant to sell.

Having paid £5million for the winger last summer, and with two years remaining on his contract, Besiktas were hoping to make a significant profit if they were to sell.
