Ferrari is raising its full-year forecast again as earnings top expectations

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Ferrari lifted its 2026 revenue forecast to near 7.65 billion euros and its industrial free cash flow forecast to up to 1.65 billion

Ferrari $RACE raised its full-year 2026 guidance on Tuesday, citing personalizations that outpaced its expectations, a milder drag from currency swings and a runner-up result in the Formula 1 championship.

The company now forecasts net revenue near 7.65 billion euros, up from a prior forecast near 7.6 billion euros. Adjusted EBITDA is expected to reach at least 3 billion euros, compared with at least 2.97 billion euros before. Adjusted operating profit is forecast at no less than 2.285 billion euros, up from 2.26 billion euros, and adjusted diluted earnings per share at no less than 9.85 euros, up from 9.68 euros. Ferrari also boosted its outlook for industrial free cash flow, which it now puts at no less than 1.65 billion euros, versus a previous floor of 1.55 billion euros.

Ferrari said its guidance rests on current visibility into the effects of the Middle East crisis.

For the third quarter, net revenues were 1.896 billion euros, up 7% from a year earlier and up 10% at constant currency. Operating profit rose 12% to 563 million euros, a margin of 29.7%. Net profit was 431 million euros, and diluted earnings per share were 2.44 euros, compared with 2.14 euros a year earlier. Industrial free cash flow rose 43% to 567 million euros.

Analysts expected earnings per share of 2.37 euros and revenue of 1.89 billion euros, according to CNBC. CNBC also noted that Ferrari has now lifted its outlook for 2026 in back-to-back quarters.

Revenue from cars and spare parts rose 7% to 1.586 billion euros on a richer sports car mix and higher personalizations, Ferrari said. The company attributed the profit increase mainly to a positive product mix, supported by the F80, and to higher personalizations. Higher depreciation and amortization and increased marketing expenses partly offset those gains.

Ferrari delivered 3,368 cars in the quarter, down from 3,401 a year earlier, as it carried out a planned model changeover.

"Our order book now reaches into 2028, and we still have two more launches to come this year," Ferrari CEO Benedetto Vigna said in a statement.

Ferrari's U.S.-listed stock was up 2% in premarket trading.
