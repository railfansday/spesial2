Great to see an update on the horse race.

One downside of the contest format -- a completely understandable limitation to prevent the workload of managing the competition from getting completely out of hand -- is that trades during the year aren't allowed. But if they were, a boglehedgefund manager with the conviction that LAZRQ equity is worthless could, as the market starts to catch on & the price begins to decline toward zero, use the gains on their short position to add to their short position and potentially realise a short profit of > 100%.
Yes, I have done this upon rare occasion in practice, although not in the contest for simplicity in the rules as you say. But I remember making 400% gains on my average notional exposure since I kept adding short on the way down. Maybe that was the FRC, the first republic bank failure, last one of the ‘23 bank runs. Then again, you have stuff like Chesapeake that traded at $3-4 until the day it was worth zero, so you don’t always get those opportunities.
