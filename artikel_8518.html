Investing - Theory, News &amp; General • Re: 100% stocks (plus emergency fund): Reasonable or too risky?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Risk is not well understood here.
.. The risk reduction .. from controlling volatility approaches zero .. as portfolio size or the ability to cut spending increase. ..
.. If I can afford to spend less, trading 1 in 1000 chance for 20% less for 300 in 1000 chance for 20% more spending is a good bet on a risk basis. Larger portfolios get better and better odds for these trade-offs, which is why large portfolios with some ability to cut spending do not need to control volatility even in decumulation. Volatility is not a risk. ..
.. I plan to provide some visuals of just how little you are getting from volatility reduction in a future thread.

Volatility reduction can be a fools game. The .. "dollar amount up or down" is .. a terrible measure of risk. It gives the wrong impression, that a larger portfolio has more risk because it goes up or down more on a dollar basis. Comparing volatility/returns is also really bad as it fails to captures the safety of larger and larger portfolio size. .. A correct measure of volatility risk needs to have volatility risk get smaller for bigger portfolios. ..
I can attest that a larger portfolio FEELS safer. A $2M net worth with 95% equities in 2000 felt much riskier than an $10M+ portfolio today with 98% equities [and a large percentage in individual stocks]. I stayed the course and didn't sell in 2000-2003, but I had worries. I don't have worries today.
