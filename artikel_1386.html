Woltemade: ‘I don’t want to act like a good guy’ after giving penalty to Kolo Muani

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Nick Woltemade explains why he let his teammate Randal Kolo Muani take a penalty kick in a 4-0 win over Venezia: ‘We both need confidence.’

Woltemade made an immediate impact, scoring and assisting within 15 minutes of coming off the bench in Juventus’ 4-0 win over Venezia on Sunday.

“It’s an important win. After the international break, it’s always difficult to find the rhythm again. We knew it was a tough game, but I think we did quite well in the second half and scored at important moments,” Woltemade told DAZN.

Woltemade seemed eager to take the penalty kick from which Juventus scored their fourth goal today, but he gave the ball to Kolo Muani, who didn’t miss his chance and sent Filip Stankovic the wrong way to score this second Serie A goal this season.

“Last time, he gave me the ball; we both need confidence,” Woltemade explained.

“When we have the chance to help each other, we help each other. I don’t want to act like I’m a good guy who gave him the ball. It was normal for me. He’s a good guy; he took the penalty, and he took it.”

Woltemade had scored his first Serie A goal in the previous round, just before the break, on an assist from Kolo Muani in a 2-2 away draw at Fiorentina.

Ironically, Woltemade scored his first Juventus goal against NEC in September, when Nico Gonzalez, who was injured today, let him take a penalty kick in a 5-0 Europa League victory.

“It was a gift for me to give me this goal. I’m so happy. He’s my guy,” Kolo Muani said.

“If you play well, I play well, and everyone wins.”

Juventus had a strong reaction in the second half, going to the break with a 0-0 draw as fans booed the players.

“Of course, we always want to win and give good energy to the fans, but sometimes it takes time to get through and create chances. This is football, and we need to be brave,” concluded Woltemade.

“A game takes 90 minutes; you look at 4-0 and think it was an easy game, but no game is easy. We have quality; we will find solutions.”

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
