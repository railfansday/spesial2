Manchester United’s hopes of signing Calvin Bassey in January have suffered a setback after the Fulham captain confirmed discussions are underway over extending his stay at Craven Cottage.

The 26-year-old has emerged as one of Mauricio Pochettino’s defensive targets as United look to strengthen a back line that has struggled for consistency.

Bassey’s pace, physicality and ability to play both centre-back and left-back make him particularly attractive, but Fulham are determined to keep one of their most important players.

According to The Standard, Bassey has confirmed that negotiations over a new Fulham contract are ongoing.

The Nigeria international is in the third year of a four-year deal, although Fulham also hold an option to extend that agreement by another 12 months.

Bassey has played every minute of Fulham’s Premier League campaign this season and was named captain by Alvaro Arbeloa during the summer, underlining just how important he has become.

Fulham have no intention of selling him in January, which significantly complicates Manchester United’s plans.

United’s admiration is well established. A recent CaughtOffside report revealed that Bassey features prominently on Pochettino’s defensive shortlist, with his versatility seen as a major advantage.

Pochettino wants defenders capable of operating aggressively in a higher line, and Bassey has the recovery pace and strength to suit that approach.

He also brings Premier League experience, meaning he should require far less adaptation than many overseas alternatives.

The problem is timing. Fulham are fighting to climb the table and have little incentive to lose their captain midway through the season. A new contract would strengthen their negotiating position even further.

United should therefore avoid overpaying simply to force a January deal.

If Bassey remains interested in a bigger move, the summer could offer a far more realistic opportunity. For now, Fulham hold the advantage.

United are right to keep monitoring him, but unless contract talks collapse, Pochettino may need to look elsewhere for his first defensive signing.
