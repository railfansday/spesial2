Here's Why Vera Therapeutics Stock Surged Today (Hint: It's Not Great News For Vertex Shareholders)

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Some bad news from the developer of a rival drug lifted Vera's stock today.

By Lee Samaha – Nov 27, 2026 at 1:07PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Vera Therapeutics ›

Vera Therapeutics (VERA +16.14%) stock was up more than 16% at midday today, but the increase didn't stem from any direct news from the company. Instead, the news came from a much larger company, Vertex Pharmaceuticals, (VRTX -5.24%) aiming to get a rival drug to Vera's Trutakna (atacicept) approved by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA).

Vera's Trutakna is indicated for the treatment of primary immunoglobulin A nephropathy (IgAN) in adults. It received approval in the summer, and as you might imagine, a small biotechnology company like Vera, competing with a giant like Vertex with a $135 billion market cap, will push hard to establish Trutakna sales before Vertex can get its rival IgAN treatment drug, povetacicept, approved.

Vertex's drug is a real threat, not least as it's taken once every four weeks as opposed to Trutakna's once-a-week dosing.

As such, the recent news that the FDA had issued an extension "to the Prescription Drug User Fee Act (PDUFA) target action date for the Biologics License Application (BLA) for povetacicept for the treatment of IgA nephropathy (IgAN) by up to three months" reads like a shot in the arm for Vera's attempts to build sales.

Not only does it give Vera up to three months to build sales and get patients and physicians used to the treatment, but the fact that the extension was issued at all casts some doubt on ultimate approval.

Vertex's press release noted that the extension was due to an FDA information request, to which it promptly replied, prompting the FDA to require more time to review Vertex's submission. The sell-off in Vertex's stock may well be an overreaction to what could turn out to be merely a delay in approval. Still, that won't worry Vera too much, as it now has a few extra months to build Trutakna's market presence.
