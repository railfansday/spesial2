Coincheck (CNCK) Q2 2027 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Coincheck is funding growth from its Japan marketplace cash engine.

Coincheck Group N.V. (CNCK -0.79%) reported a 43% decline in total revenue for the second quarter of fiscal 2027 as management implemented a new three-phase strategic framework titled fund, grow, and replicate. The company began winding down its Aplo subsidiary's European prime brokerage operations to integrate institutional trading technology into its 3iQ asset management division for a planned product launch in the first half of calendar 2027. Management stated that the core marketplace in Japan and ETF operations in Canada will serve as a cash engine to fund growth initiatives, including the expansion of crypto contracts for difference and institutional partnerships. The company reported that regulatory changes enacted in Japan during the summer of 2026 are expected to provide a long-term opportunity for new asset management products by mid-2027.
