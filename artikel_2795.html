Improbable St. John’s basketball rally falls short after Louisville sinks buzzer-beating three

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Many expected a high-octane heavyweight bout between St. John's and Louisville in Rick Pitino's first meeting with his former program since he was fired in 2017. At first, Thursday's game at the Players Era Festival seemed headed for a no-contest, with the Cardinals running the Red Storm off the floor, but the Johnnies got off the mat and mounted a furious second-half comeback. Ultimately, that rally was all for naught, as the Cardinals delivered an unforgettable knockout blow.

With the game tied at 90 and the seconds ticking down, Louisville's Adrian Wooley drained a three-point jumper on a broken play from the corner to stun St. John's at the final horn, handing the Red Storm their first defeat of the season.

Moments before that shot, St. John's finally came back from a 24-point second-half deficit. Ian Jackson sank a fortuitous pair of technical free throws after officials erroneously ruled Jackson Shelstad called a timeout when Louisville had none remaining.

All indications had pointed to Louisville crushing St. John's most of the night. The Cardinals started quickly with a 7-0 run and quickly gained a double-digit lead.

The Red Storm's normally intimidating full-court pressure was toothless without Tounde Yessoufou, who picked up three fouls in as many first-half minutes. Louisville tore through that pressure with long passes over the backcourt, scoring at the rim with ease or drawing a foul after the defense broke down. Seth Trimble was grilling the Red Storm's defense all night, scoring a career-best 33 points (12-of-19 shooting).

Even when Louisville was forced to operate in the half-court, they still got whatever they wanted offensively, shooting 22-of-37 from the field (59.7%) and 6-of-14 from three. That offensive explosion let them enter halftime with a 57-39 lead, the most points St. John's surrendered in the Rick Pitino era.

There were no signs of life from St. John's out of the locker room either. Although Louisville finally cooled down, the Johnnies kept getting bullied and couldn't close the gap after missing their first 12 three-point attempts. Babacar Sane kept the deficit from growing any larger, posting all 14 of his points in the second half and scoring the Red Storm's first two treys of the game.

Louisville was in cruise control, holding a 17-point lead with 5:02 remaining, but they chose to burn clock instead of maintaining their pace, allowing St. John's to cling to the faintest hope of a comeback. The Red Storm finally came to life and went on an extended 20-3 run heading into the final play of the game, but Wooley shut the door on the comeback bid with that buzzer-beater.

Ian Jackson led the Red Storm with 18 points (4-of-8 shooting, 10-of-11 from free throws), with Keyshawn Hall (17 points, plus ten rebounds), Quinn Ellis (16, a new career high), and Ruben Prey (13) also scoring in double figures.

The Red Storm (6-1) will wrap up their Players Era trip on Friday afternoon as they hope to avenge last season's defeat to Iowa State (4-2). Tip-off from Michelob Ultra Arena is scheduled for 1 p.m. Eastern, and ESPN2 will broadcast the game.
