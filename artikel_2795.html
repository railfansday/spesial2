LaNorris Sellers injury update: South Carolina QB leaves Texas A&M game for locker room

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

LaNorris Sellers left South Carolina's game against Texas A&M on Saturday, Nov. 7, with an apparent injury.

The star quarterback left during the second quarter after taking a sack and fumbling, injuring his ankle. He left the field and briefly entered the injury tent before entering the locker room with a trainer.

"We're concerned, he's got an ankle right now," South Carolina coach Shane Beamer told ESPN at halftime. "Taking an X-ray on it, don't know the results of that right now."

The third-year starter has been fantastic during the Gamecock's three-game win streak and is coming off a five-touchdown performance in a 42-25 rout of Oklahoma in Week 9. Sellers entered the game with 1,849 passing yards and 22 total touchdowns to seven interceptions.

Losing Sellers would be a huge blow to Beamer's squad, especially with South Carolina play some of the best football in the SEC as of late.

"We have a lot of confidence in Cutter Woods," Beamer said. "He's our backup. Total confidence in him. The bigger issue right now is we're getting our butts kicked on the line of scrimmage. We're not running the ball and they're running the ball down our throats right now."

Sellers left the game against Texas A&M in the second quarter with an ankle injury after being sacked. He briefly entered the injury tent on the sideline before entering the locker room with a trainer.

Beamer said Sellers is getting X-rays on his ankle, but that the team is concerned about his status.
