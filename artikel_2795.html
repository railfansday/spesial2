Chargers sign Jake Browning to active roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Chargers' starting quarterback is in the concussion protocol, so they moved to add another healthy one to the active roster on Thursday.

The team announced that they have signed Jake Browning off of their practice squad. Browning signed with the Chargers in October.

Justin Herbert did not practice on Wednesday because of his concussion, which left DJ Uiagalelei to take the first-team reps. Uiagalelei has appeared in one game this season and he went 2-of-7 for 24 yards and a touchdown.

The Chargers placed defensive back Deane Leonard on injured reserve to make room for Browning. He had 13 tackles in six appearances this season.
