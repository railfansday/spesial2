Steelers vs. Broncos: Week 12 Black Friday game chat

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Steelers are reeling after two straight losses have sent them tumbling to third place in the AFC North, and that was before all the Aaron Rodgers-Mike McCarthy drama played out this week. The Denver Broncos, meanwhile, are tied for the AFC West lead and the No. 2 seed as of today.

The Broncos are a bit of a curiosity this season. Their starting quarterback has struggled in stretches and doesn't garner a ton of respect around opposing NFL fanbases. The Denver defense is still talented but nowhere near as dominant as it has been in recent years. And yet, this team has impressive victories over the Chiefs, Jaguars, Rams, and Seahawks on its resume.

If there is one beacon of hope for this struggling Pittsburgh team, it's that this game is at home, where they've gone 4-0 this season. With the Bengals losing in Week 11 and the Browns on bye, the Steelers remain just one game out of first place in the division. A win would get them back on track to securing a playoff spot. Can "new" starter Mason Rudolph provide a similar spark as he did when he took over late in the season in 2023?
