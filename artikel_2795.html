Why Jose Mourinho has benched Vinicius Jr. at Real Madrid as Yan Diomande stars on the left

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Why Jose Mourinho has benched Vinicius Jr. at Real Madrid as Yan Diomande stars on the left originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The time has come to take a hard look at Vinicius Jr's starting position at Real Madrid.

There is no debating that Vini is one of the best left wingers in the world. That has not changed in the first few months of the 2026/27 season.

However, after Vini served his four-game La Liga suspension for pulling the hair of Nathan Saliba, there is now also no debating that Real Madrid are better without the Brazilian on the field. That is just a fact.

Fans have, over the last three weeks, enjoyed the rare opportunity of seeing Real Madrid play with and without Vini in alternating matches, as he was ineligible to compete in league play but eligible to play in Champions League matches. What resulted was a clear juxtaposition — Real Madrid were better in almost every single aspect without Vini.

In the La Liga matches, Jose Mourinho started Yan Diomande at his more natural left wing position, with Arda Guler as an inside forward on the right. This allowed Diomande to dazzle on the ball out wide left, while Guler cut inside as a quasi-midfielder, allowing Federico Valverde or Denzel Dumfries to overlap down the right.

The balance in this setup is exceptional. Diomande's willingness and capability to track back and defend makes the whole thing work, as he stays out of Kylian Mbappe's way up front and helps out Marc Cucurella, giving Real Madrid more dynamism.

While Diomande gets lots of credit for Madrid's excellent performances without Vini, and deservingly so, Guler has also flown spectacularly under the radar in this setup. The Turkey international has been nothing short of sensational this season, flourishing in this inside forward role. His relationship with Mbappe has been especially strong, proven as Mbappe responded to Guler's recent contract renewal by saying on social media, "I will sleep better tonight."

According to Opta, Guler leads La Liga in chances created so far this season with 42, averaging 5.08 per 90 minutes. To put that into perspective: Kevin De Bruyne's best Premier League season in 2019/20, where he racked up an outrageous 20 assists, saw him create 4.32 chances per 90 minutes. What Guler is doing this season is miraculous.

Guler's creativity allows Jude Bellingham to play a more box-to-box role, taking some of the No. 10 duties off his shoulders and allowing him to help stop counter-attacks and assist earlier in build-up. Bellingham has always been a do-it-all star, and he is allowed to shine in this role while also providing a late-arriving option in the penalty area, where he excels.

With Vinicius on the left, things fall apart. Vini drifts more centrally into something that looks like a 4-4-2, failing to defend in any capacity leaving Cucurella alone to handle the entire left touchline. Diomande hugs the touchline, forcing Bellingham alone to build through the middle and thus vacating positions where he can stop opposition transitions. It leaves the entire team stretched and makes the buildup play far more individualistic while they are more prone to being countered.

As a result, the return has been muted at best. Vini Jr. has just one La Liga goal all season. Against AEK Athens, Vini's latest appearance in Europe, Mourinho essentially acknowledged that Vini has struggled. "Vini had an acceptable match tonight," the Madrid manager said after the game, "not extraordinary, just like the whole team."

In that game, with 49 touches including nine in the penalty area, Vinicius Jr. created two chances for teammates, missed the target with both his shots, and most importantly, won two of his nine ground duels including a failure with his one dribble attempt, and made zero defensive contributions plus one loose-ball recovery. Diomande, meanwhile, playing most of the game on the right until the end when he moved to the left after Vini was withdrawn, had 59 touches while winning six of his 12 ground duels including four of seven dribbles, while making two defensive contributions and four recoveries.

Vini has long been a locked-on starter at Madrid, often to great effect. Yet this season, not only has he not been the same superstar player, but he is no longer a good fit at the club. Florentino Perez had a prime opportunity to offload Vini this past summer, a rare chance to move on from an expensive player on big wages who might no longer fit the team's makeup, but flubbed it. It is a roster-building executive's job to foresee these problems, and he completely whiffed.

Now, because Perez failed, it falls to Mourinho to make the bold decisions necessary at a club like Real Madrid. He is no stranger to these type of dilemmas throughout his career, but this one is multi-faceted. Vini is a beloved player at the Bernabeu, and would no doubt make a stink if he were benched. There will be plenty of minutes to go around across all competitions, but the club is already struggling to get the likes of Endrick and Brahim Diaz involved, so adding Vini Jr. to the rotation would complicate things further.

Sure enough, Mourinho made the move, as he started Diomande on the left and Guler on the right against Celta Vigo on Saturday, with Vinicius Jr. beginning the match on the bench.

There are signs that Vini still has something to offer, especially after scoring five goals on the recent November international break, putting his career Brazil struggles behind him under Carlo Ancelotti, who knows a thing or two about getting the best out of Vini. Yet Mourinho's setup is what it is, and there's clearly a pecking order being established.

It's obvious that Vini, in his current form and style, does not fit into the first-choice Real Madrid starting XI. It's time for Mourinho to make the call, and manage his way through the blowback. His expertise in these kind of situations are why he was hired, and it's time he proved he is still The Special One.
