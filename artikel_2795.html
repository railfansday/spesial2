Why a franchise tag may not work for Puka Nacua

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Los Angeles Rams have had a decision to make with Puka Nacua since the beginning of the 2026 offseason, and the longer they hold off on his next contract, the more likely it becomes that the fourth-year receiver won't get one from Les Snead and Sean McVay. Nacua is set to become a 2027 free agent and ESPN's Dan Graziano speculated on Wednesday that the Rams could be too concerned with his off-field issues and/or his injury to commit to him like other teams have committed to number one receivers.

Nacua didn't get the big contract extension that Jaxon Smith-Njigba got last offseason, even though he was eligible. And the Rams don't hold a fifth-year option on him for next year because he wasn't a first-round pick. Nacua also made bad headlines with his off-field behavior in the offseason and could still face league discipline under the personal conduct policy.

Plus, he's banged up again. He missed two games earlier this season with hip and groin injuries, and he's currently on IR with a knee injury that will cost him at least four more games (counting the one he missed Sunday) and possibly more than that. Nacua played all 17 games in his rookie season but hasn't managed to do that since. He's 25 years old and a brilliant player, but his play style makes you wonder about his longevity. All of that is to say: I could at least imagine a scenario in which the Rams let him hit free agency, or franchise him and listen to offers.

Graziano didn't get into the weeds of what a franchise tag would look like and how challenging that would be for the Rams given their slate of free agents in 2027.

OvertheCap.com is projecting a $30.9 million franchise tag for wide receivers in 2027.

The Rams would have to pay Nacua a fully-guaranteed $30.9 million salary in 2027

The Rams would have a deadline to either extend Nacua, pay him the tag, or rescind the tag

The non-exclusive tag means Nacua could negotiate with other teams and if he signs with a team and the Rams don't match the offer, the Rams would get two first-round picks from that team

The exclusive tag means Nacua can't negotiate with other teams

The Rams could still trade Nacua to another team on the exclusive tag

The Rams could still extend Nacua before the deadline, this would just buy them some time

Paying Nacua a $30.9 million salary in 2027 seems exceptionally challenging for L.A..

The Rams only have one starting offensive lineman under contract — AJ Jackson — and only one recent draft pick who might be a contender to start — Keagen Trost — meaning that at best L.A. only has three starting offensive line positions to fill. They will have a hard time replacing anyone in the draft because the Rams don't have a first-round pick and they're not keen on starting rookies anyway.

The Rams also have to make a decision on WR2, as Davante Adams is a free agent.

There are plenty of other free agents, including Kobie Turner, Byron Young, and Ethan Evans. In fact, the Rams also need to pick a kicker and potentially invest more money in special teams next year.

An extension for Nacua could carry a 2027 cap hit of roughly $10-$15 million. That makes a tag 2-3 times more expensive next year at a time when the Rams have to sign almost half of their important starters. A tag would be very difficult for the Rams to pull off.

I highly doubt it. The Rams would only get a third-round comp pick in 2028 if Nacua leaves in free agency. That's not enough compensation for one of the NFL's best players who is only going to be 26. It would be a mistake of absurd proportions.

The Rams will figure something out with Nacua, most likely. Free agency is highly unlikely. But the longer the Rams make us speculate, the more speculation that will occur.
