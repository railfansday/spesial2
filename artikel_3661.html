Glenn Close Recalls Growing Up in a Cult as She Receives Honorary Oscar

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Glenn Close is officially an Oscar winner after eight career nominations.

The All’s Fair actress, 79, received an honorary Oscar at the 2026 Governors Awards on Sunday, November 15, in the Ray Dolby Ballroom at Ovation Hollywood in Los Angeles.

Close was visibly emotional as she accepted the award, discussing her life growing up in what she described as a cult.

“It’s very difficult for me to process this incredible honor, because the truth is, I carry the epigenome of an outsider,” she said. “From the ages of 7 to 22, I was under the thumb of a very restrictive cult — don’t look at yourself in a mirror, no flirting, dress this way, talk this way, say it’s OK when your parents are routinely sent away from you for months on end. It meant that I was always on the outside looking in.”

Close went on to dedicate her award to her daughter and fellow actress, Annie Starke, and spoke about her previous brushes with Academy Awards glory.

“As an outsider, I was never worried about whether I was going to win or not, though some years I thought I might have a chance,” she said. “I have always felt that the great honor is to be in the room. Over the past 43 years, I’ve been in the room eight times … not looking in the window, but in the room, part of this extraordinary community. So to be in this room tonight, embraced by the people who have consistently inspired me, whose work has sustained and challenged me and only made me want to be better, is the greatest honor of all.”

Of Close’s eight Oscar nominations, four were in the Best Supporting Actress category, and the other four were for Best Actress. She was first nominated for Best Actress in 1988 for Fatal Attraction and again the following year for Dangerous Liaisons. Close was up for Best Actress a third time in 2012 for Albert Nobbs, followed by her most recent nomination in the category in 2019 for The Wife.

Earlier this month, Close’s Fatal Attraction costar Michael Douglas said on The Graham Norton Show that she should have won the Oscar for her role in that movie. (The 1988 award was presented to actress and singer Cher for Moonstruck.)

Douglas described his costar’s performance in the thriller as “one of the greatest performances of all time.”

Close, who also appeared on the show at the time, joked of her upcoming honorary Oscar win, “I kind of hate to break my losing streak.”

On receiving the award, she quipped the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences (AMPAS), which hosts the Oscars, “probably don’t wanna feel guilty anymore.”
