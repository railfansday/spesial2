Michelle Randolph Calls 'Landman' Season 3 Return ‘Weird’ After Delay

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Michelle Randolph opened up about the challenges that came with returning to her role as Ainsley in Landman after major filming delays.

“It’s always weird getting back into the groove of a project,” Randolph, 29, exclusively told Us at the premiere of her new Prime Video movie, Clashing Through the Snow. “You’re getting back into the rhythm of how the set works and all the dynamics and the personalities on set.”

Randolph was excited to be reacquainted with Ainsley, adding, “I feel like I’ve almost grown up with [Ainsley] at [this] point, because I was 24 when I booked the show. She’s my better half. I wish I looked at life like she does.”

Based on the “Boomtown” podcast, Landman is part of the Taylor Sheridan TV universe alongside Yellowstone, Mayor of Kingstown, Tulsa King, Lawmen: Bass Reeves and Lioness. The hit Paramount+ series introduced Billy Bob Thornton as a corporate fixer working for an oil titan in a West Texas-set drama with life-and-death stakes.

Landman began filming in Texas in early 2024, premiering its first season that November. Production on season 2 started in April 2025 and ended that August, with the episodes airing in November 2025.

While season 3 was originally scheduled to film in May, it was pushed three months later. Production was delayed again before filming finally began in October. Randolph was seen in character at Texas Christian University earlier this month.

Despite the delay, Randolph remained booked and busy with Clashing Through the Snow. The holiday movie, which premieres on Monday, November 23, follows two rival book editors stranded in a snowstorm who must share a rental car to New York City to land a career-saving author deal.

“When I read the script, I immediately just fell in love with it,” Randolph gushed. “I thought it was funny. I think there’s such value in telling stories that are just meant to bring people joy and don’t ask anything of the audience. And I love rom-coms and I love the holidays.”

Randolph was cast alongside Christopher Briney, who has his own experience with being part of a successful show. After wrapping up his time on The Summer I Turned Pretty, Briney, 28, spoke to Us about what’s next — and even suggested a role on Landman.

“What they’re doing is really great, and I know that they’re still going. I’d audition, [for sure],” he shared with Us. “I’d love to be a f***ing cowboy, dude.”

He added: “With an accent? That’d be fun. I could work with a dialect coach.”

After Randolph found out about Briney’s interest in Landman, she told Us, “He’s ready? Oh, my God! Let me call Taylor. Christopher Briney is going to be a cowboy!”

Clashing Through the Snow premieres on Prime Video Monday, November 23.
