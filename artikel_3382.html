A bingeworthy mystery, 56 Days leads with a whodunnit hook before delivering an intense, steamy, sex-centric relationship thriller.

Perhaps one of the most important queer stories ever told, Fellow Travelers, based on the Thomas Mallon novel, is an emotionally charged, decades-spanning romance. The romantic thriller chronicles the secret love affair between two very different men, State Department official Hawkins Fuller (Matt Bomer) and idealistic congressional staffer Tim Laughlin (Jonathan Bailey), who meet in 1950s Washington, D.C. Beginning at the height of the McCarthy-era Lavender Scare in which the government actively hunted down and purged gay employees, Hawkins and Tim's clandestine relationship is tested by political paranoia, personal betrayal, and societal bigotry. With sweeping historical scope, Fellow Travelers preserves a crucial moment in history through explicit, frank intimacy driven by repressed desire, shifting power dynamics, and high-stakes historical danger.

Created by Ron Nyswaner, Fellow Travelers is a brilliant character-driven drama. The series does a stand-up job at presenting fully-fleshed individuals who must balance their personal desires with a fight to survive. These characters must guard every word and gesture in public. Heated Rivalry examines toxic masculinity and the pressures of staying closeted in athletics while celebrating themes of identity, queer joy, and acceptance.
