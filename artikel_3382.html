Netflix's Near-Perfect 8-Episode Sci-Fi Series Is a Weekend Binge Worth Revisiting

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Perhaps one of streaming's greatest tragedies was the shocking cancellation of the pitch-perfect sci-fi dark comedy The Boroughs. While the logistical reasons are certainly understandable, the series' brilliance may have thrown some viewers for a loop. The eight-episode Netflix series, created by Jeffrey Addiss and Will Matthews and executive produced by the Duffer Brothers, follows a grieving widower who relocates to a seemingly picturesque retirement community, only to discover something sinister lurking underneath. With a dynamic mix of cozy mystery elements and daring sci-fi tropes, The Boroughs moves across genre lines to deliver an emotional, character-driven story.

When Sam Cooper (Alfred Molina), a recently widowed retired aerospace engineer, moves into the idealistic New Mexico retirement community known as The Boroughs, he soon realizes that everything is not what it seems. After noticing mysterious deaths and unexplained disappearances that local authorities ignore, he discovers an ominous threat operating behind the scenes. Sam teams up with a misfit group of fellow residents to stop this otherworldly threat that is harvesting their time and life force.

Though the premise is rooted in sci-fi intrigue, what makes The Boroughs unique are the individuals at the center of the story. Like Stranger Things did with teenage sleuths, The Boroughs focuses on a generation often deemed "too old" to play in the sci-fi sandbox. As with stories like The Thursday Murder Club, unsuspecting underdogs make the most fascinating protagonists. They aren't presented as wise mentors who assist younger adventure-seekers but actually take the lead themselves, investigating the supernatural threats lurking beneath their seemingly idyllic community.

Netflix's inability to create sustained hits could result in long-term issues.

However, The Borough's eight episodes don't shy away from the elephant in the room, with the characters' ages playing a major role in the action. By diving deep into the realities of aging, including managing cancer medication, memory care concerns, and grief, the series presents their daring journey as something bigger than simply letting time get the best of them. When the characters do have moments to reflect and grapple with mortality, loneliness, and purpose, these themes are presented as universal realities that viewers of any age can relate to, emphasizing the brilliance of Addiss and Matthews' creation.

The Boroughs is a beautifully crafted series, with practical and technical worldbuilding playing an equally important part in the storytelling. The show was filmed across the great state of New Mexico, and its cul-de-sac was crafted to feel warm and welcoming, creating an immersive setting. Other parts of the community are imbued with nostalgia, giving the characters a reason to reminisce while also doubling as a means of emotional entrapment.

Visuals aside, The Boroughs taps some heavy hitters to play the story's senior-citizen sleuths. The acclaimed ensemble, featuring the likes of Molina, Geena Davis, Alfre Woodard, Bill Pullman, Clarke Peters, and Denis O'Hare, captures the genuine heart of the show. Molina is quite grounded as the standoffish and guarded Sam Cooper, while Davis gets to lean into the cougar trope, clearly having immense fun as Renee. Woodard and Peters earn the most complex and alluring relationship, and their moments of emotional intimacy are welcome amid the wild sci-fi chaos happening around them. O'Hare is at his finest, bringing nuance to an individual who knows the end is near. Perhaps the most heartbreaking performance comes from Ed Begley Jr. as Edward, a man who lives in The Manor, the long-term care wing.

Given the cast's terrific on-screen chemistry, any scene shared by these elite actors feels like a masterclass in acting. They're so strong that the prospect of more to the story was quite exciting until Netflix pulled the plug. Reported production costs of $10 million or more per episode, the high-caliber cast, and an alleged viewership drop-off were cited as factors in Netflix's decision to cancel The Boroughs after one season. Once you finish this near-perfect series, you'll be stunned that the business side of television got in the way of something truly special.
