Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The FDIC shut down United Fidelity Bank of Indiana at the close of today’s business. First Citizens is taking over the deposits and assets.

https://lite.cnn.com/2026/11/27/busines ... -bank-fdic

Interestingly:

In another likely sign of United Fidelity’s troubled state, First Citizens said it has only agreed to assume $4.5 billion of the bank’s deposits. That is well shy of the $5.4 billion the FDIC said United Fidelity held as of the end of September.

The FDIC said the deposit accounts that transfer to First Citizens exclude “certain brokered deposits” but did not disclose how many or why.
I wonder if the brokered deposits are fintech deposits or brokerage sweep deposits, and what the final disposition of those assets will be.
