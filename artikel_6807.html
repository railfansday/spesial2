Attention everyone confused about “Instant Dances….”

Dancing With the Stars is wrapping up an equal parts messy and incredible season, and the finale is doing things a litttttle differently. From a lineup of incredible performances, to Instant Dances that could turn somewhat chaotic, to a change to the show’s previously established air time, we have all the details on what to expect—and how to watch live!

For the first time ever, the show will air its three-hour finale live on both the East Coast and the West Coast. Typically, DWTS only airs live on the East Coast due to ABC prioritizing “local news and the coveted primetime programming slots,” and West Coast viewers have to avoid spoilers and watch the show late.

The Dancing With the Stars finale will air Tuesday, November 24, at 8 p.m. ET and 5 p.m. PT. It will then air again at 8 p.m. PT for those West Coast viewers unable to catch the live viewing in the late afternoon.
