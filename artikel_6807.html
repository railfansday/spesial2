BBC Issues Formal Apology After British Version of ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Is Interrupted by Sex Noises

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Things took a turn over on Strictly Come Dancing!

Things are getting...interesting over on Strictly Come Dancing, aka the British version of Dancing With the Stars (where Mark Ballas’ mom, Shirley Ballas, is a full-time judge).

According to Deadline, “sex noises” were played during judging on the live show, and could be heard as judge Motsi Mabuse was critiquing Delta Goodrem and Kai Widdrington’s performance.

While Motsi joked, “it’s not me,” host Emma Willis apologized to fans, saying “Before we see the judges’ leaderboard, we’d like to apologize if you heard any audio disruptions earlier in tonight’s show and for any offence caused.”

Meanwhile, the police were called to set, and a spokesperson told The Guardian: “We are aware of an incident that took place during the filming of Strictly Come Dancing in Blackpool. We have been in contact with the event organizers, and a formal complaint has been made to us. A mobile phone has been seized and we are now looking into the matter to see if any criminal offenses have taken place.”

Cut to the BBC, who said, “We apologie to any viewers offended during the live broadcast of Strictly this evening. We are investigating how this happened.”
