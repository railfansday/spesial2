Rodney Rice and Alijah Arenas lead No. 18 USC to 96-51 win over Tarleton State

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

LOS ANGELES (AP) — Rodney Rice had 24 points and seven assists, Alijah Arenas scored 19 and No. 18 Southern California dominated from the outset to remain perfect this season with a 96-51 victory over Tarleton State on Thursday night.

Jacob Cofie added 13 points, Adonis Ratliff had 10 and Darius Ratliff grabbed nine rebounds as the Trojans (5-0) followed up a 76-point blowout against Arkansas-Pine Bluff on Sunday with a 45-point win on coach Eric Musselman's 62nd birthday.

USC shot 53.7% from the floor overall, and 13 of 26 from 3-point range, after shooting 64.3% in the first half. Rice went 5 of 9 from long distance, while Arenas was 5 of 9 overall and has shot 50% or better in all five games.

Franck Emmou scored 14 points and Brett Freeman added 13 for the Texans (4-2), who were facing the first of two power-conference opponents on their schedule, with a matchup at Pittsburgh on Dec. 5.

Tarleton State shot 33.3% from the field, and 13.3% from 3-point range (2 of 15).

The Trojans went on a 9-2 run to open a 19-9 advantage, with Arenas scoring seven points in that stretch. An 18-2 spurt late in the first half gave USC a 49-17 lead.

Arenas had 15 points in the first half while going 4 of 6 from the floor as the Trojans went into the break with a 55-21 lead.

They built their cushion to 80-40 with 6:08 remaining and led by as many as 48 in the second half.

Both losses for the Texans losses have come in Southern California. They were beaten 96-90 in overtime at San Diego on Nov. 6.

Tarleton State visits Weber State next Tuesday.
