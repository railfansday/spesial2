Michigan alumni Yaxel Lendeborg, Morez Johnson Jr. exchange jerseys after Mavericks-Warriors clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Michigan alumni Yaxel Lendeborg, Morez Johnson Jr. exchange jerseys after Mavericks-Warriors clash appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Yaxel Lendeborg and Morez Johnson Jr. may be on different NBA teams now, but the former Michigan teammates made sure to share a moment after the Dallas Mavericks took down the Golden State Warriors on Wednesday night.

After Dallas' 116-94 win at American Airlines Center, the two rookies met on the court and swapped jerseys, with the NBA capturing the moment on video.

The exchange carried plenty of meaning for both players. Lendeborg and Johnson Jr. were key pieces of Michigan's run to the 2026 national championship, which the Wolverines clinched with a win over UConn for their first title in 37 years. Johnson Jr. had 12 points and 10 rebounds in the title game.

Just a couple of months later, both heard their names called in the first round of the 2026 NBA Draft. The Mavericks selected Johnson Jr. with the No. 9 overall pick, while the Warriors took Lendeborg at No. 11. Their Michigan teammate Aday Mara went one pick later to the Oklahoma City Thunder.

Johnson Jr. also reunited with a familiar face in Dallas, as former Michigan head coach Dusty May left Ann Arbor to take over the Mavericks.

On Wednesday, though, it was Johnson Jr.'s team that came out on top. Cooper Flagg led the way for Dallas with 33 points, nine assists and four steals on 11-of-15 shooting, while P.J. Washington added 21 points on five 3-pointers. Johnson Jr. finished with three points, a rebound and an assist while picking up four fouls.

Lendeborg was one of the few bright spots for Golden State, finishing with 13 points, five rebounds, four assists and two steals.

The win snapped a three-game losing streak for Dallas and improved its record to 8-7. The Warriors fell to 8-7 as well.

Whether they're teammates or opponents, Lendeborg and Johnson Jr. clearly still share a bond from their championship days in Ann Arbor.
