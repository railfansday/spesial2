‘Prayers up Man,’ ‘Hope He’s OK’ — Patrick Mahomes, NFL World Deeply Worried As 49ers OT Colton McKivitz Suffers ‘Brutal’ Injury vs. Vikings

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Sunday's international game in Mexico City between the San Francisco 49ers and the Minnesota Vikings took a scary turn when 49ers offensive tackle Colton McKivitz went down with a gruesome leg injury. The replay was certainly hard to watch.

Kansas City Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes quickly spoke up on social media, and several others followed with prayers and well wishes for McKivitz.

Midway through the second quarter, Christian McCaffrey was tackled after a 4-yard run, and he and Vikings linebacker Ivan Pace Jr. rolled into the back of McKivitz's left leg and ankle.

The tackle bent McKivitz backward and left his lower left leg at an awkward angle. He is now out for the season.

After a fantastic performance for the Chiefs in the early window, Mahomes took to X and wrote, "Prayers up, man," along with a prayer hands emoji.

Several others joined Mahomes in sending their support. Grant Cohn, who covers the 49ers for SI, wrote, "I wish Colton McKivitz a speedy recovery. He's such a good player and a real good guy."

Former NFL quarterback Kurt Benkert asked people not to show replays of the injury.

"Please don't show that sideways ankle on the screen ever again. Prayers up for a smooth recovery for Colton McKivitz," he wrote.

Former defensive tackle Breiden Fehoko did share an image of the ankle on X, with the caption, "Viewer discretion advised."

NFL on Fox writerHenry McKenna shared how both teams reacted to the brutal injury.

"Everyone on the field was not okay after the Colton McKivitz injury. Hope he's okay," McKenna wrote.

David Lombardi, who analyzes the 49ers, wrote, "Brutal — starting 49ers' RT Colton McKivitz is down with what looks to be a serious injury, and the cart is coming out. McKivitz has developed into one of the NFL's top right tackles."

McKivitz has allowed zero sacks this season and ranks No. 4 with a PFN OL Impact Score of 87.7. He will be a big loss for the 49ers during what is likely to be a long recovery.

San Francisco bounced back from the injury and beat the Vikings 35-10 to improve to 9-1, the best record in the NFC.

The 49ers return home to face the defending champion Seattle Seahawks at Levi's Stadium in Santa Clara, California. The Week 12 matchup kicks off Sunday, Nov. 29. San Francisco already has a win over Seattle this season, having beaten the team in Week 5.
