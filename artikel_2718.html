FGCU women's basketball team falls in season-opener at Indiana

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

FGCU women's basketball fell in its season-opener on Tuesday night, Nov. 3, losing 79-50 to Indiana at the Simon Skjodt Assembly Hall in Bloomington, Indiana.

The Hoosiers led from the start, opening an 8-0 run through the first five minutes of the game and never looked back. The hosts outscored the Eagles 19-5 in the first quarter and while their offense picked up in the second, the Eagles couldn't cut into the deficit by halftime.

But just as the Eagles thought they had found their footing, Indiana shifted gears in the third quarter, going on a 17-0 run to break the game wide open.

Junior forward Zania Socka-Nguemen led the Hoosiers with 13 points and seven rebounds while senior forward Alisa Williams had 12 points off the bench as the Hoosiers cruised to a lopsided victory.

Junior guard Sinai Douglas led the Eagles with 13 points and senior guard Denika Lightbourne provided support with 10 points. The Eagles shooting struggles continued throughout the night, shooting just 29% from the field as a team and making only 3 of their 26 3-point attempts.

FGCU (0-1) will look to bounce back when the Eagles return to Alico Arena for their home-opener against Arkansas State on Saturday, Nov. 7 at 2 p.m.
