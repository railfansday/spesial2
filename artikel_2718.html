Dan Hicks, NBC Sports' lead golf voice, dies at 64 after acute illness

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Dan Hicks, the longtime voice of golf for NBC Sports, died unexpectedly Nov. 11. He was 64.

Hicks' wife, Hannah Storm, posted on social media that Hicks died following an acute illness.

"Dan was truly one of a kind and, quite simply, the person everyone wanted to be around. When he walked into a room, he brought the energy, the laughter and all the people together. He was endlessly kind, thoughtful and funny, and he made everyone feel like they mattered."

Hicks served as NBC Sports' lead golf play-by-play commentator, called swimming and skiing at the Olympics and returned as the lead play-by-play voice for Notre Dame Football in 2024. He covered the PGA Tour for NBC Sports since 2000.
