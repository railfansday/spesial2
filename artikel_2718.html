‘A fake team’ – JJ Redick predicts LA Lakers will deal with ‘overreactions’ from NBA media after coming games

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Los Angeles Lakers head coach JJ Redick already knows what to expect from the NBA media following their upcoming run of tough games.

The Lakers have a 15-3 record after their first 18 games, but one thing that cannot be denied is that they have played some of the poorer teams in the league. Their next three games are against winning teams, though. They will play the San Antonio Spurs, the Memphis Grizzlies, and the Houston Rockets in quick succession.

The Spurs clash is notable also because it will be a huge early moment in the MVP race between Luka Doncic and Victor Wembanyama. The media will be honed in on the clash, which is sure to lead to quite a few narratives. Head coach JJ Redick already knows what to expect no matter what happens, though.

The Spurs have a 15-4 record, while both the Grizzlies and the Rockets boast a 14-5 record. It is extremely unlikely that LA will beat all three, especially considering that the latter two games are back-to-back and all the games are on the road.

Speaking ahead of this four-game road trip that includes three massive clashes, JJ explained what will happen if the Lakers lose any of these games.

"I said Friday, we're either gonna be contenders or we're going to be a fake team," Redick told reporters. "If we win that game and we lose to Memphis and Houston, we're back to being a fake team. So, it's overreactions, game-to-game. That's what they are. We've gotta, as a group, manage that and manage the noise and that's just part of it. It's all good."

Redick has been on contending teams during his playing career, and a part of the media before he started coaching. Both things mean he knows exactly what to expect in terms of the narrative. For the Lakers, the objective will be clear. If they somehow manage to win all three of these games, they will be able to separate themselves as true contenders at the top of the Western Conference.
