Report: Jaguars C Robert Hainsey week-to-week with knee injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Jaguars center Robert Hainsey left last Sunday’s win over the Titans with a knee injury and it looks like he will need some more time to recover.

Jeremy Fowler of ESPN reports that Hainsey is dealing with a sprain and is considered week-to-week when it comes to a return to action. While Hainsey is set to miss time, he is not expected to be out through the rest of the regular season.

Hainsey has started every game this season and he started all 15 games he played for the Jags last season as well.

Jonah Monheim replaced Hainsey against the Titans and he also started for the Jags when Hainsey missed time in 2025.
