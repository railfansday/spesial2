Lakers vs. Hornets Preview: The winning continues

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

After a successful four-game road trip, the Lakers (12-2) are back home where they will host the Charlotte Hornets (4-9) on Tuesday. This is the first meeting between the teams this season.

Who: Los Angeles Lakers vs. Charlotte Hornets

What's particularly encouraging about this current Lakers' winning streak is that they're learning while stacking up vital wins. Los Angeles may have gone a perfect 4-0 on their road trip, but multiple flaws popped up during this stretch, and they're already working on them.

On top of that list is their inability to maintain leads. While no lead is safe in today's NBA, the purple and gold have the tendency to let go of the rope, especially when their non-starters are in the game. Take the 31-point blown lead against the Phoenix Suns in their previous game as an example. Or even that time against the Raptors when the Lakers thought they iced the game with a 26-point lead, only for the Raptors to come back.

It's a valuable eye-opener and one they have to resolve before it eventually costs them a game down the line.

A good way to assess the purple and gold's progress is to see how they perform in their next matchup against the Hornets. Charlotte may not be the first team that comes to mind as a threat, but they're more than capable of giving LA a tough challenge. Four out of the five members in their starting five can stretch the floor and they have a pesky backcourt in Brandon Miller and Coby White, who will give the Lakers' perimeter defense a tough challenge.

The Hornets are also an excellent rebounding team, which is something the Lakers also have to work on. There's no doubt that the Hornets will go after those rebounds, especially knowing that Walker Kessler won't be playing. Expect the Hornets to attempt as many 3-pointers as possible since they're known to be a high-volume shooting team.

It'll be interesting to see how the Lakers navigate Kessler's absence with this being the first time it's happened this season.

Despite their hole in the front court, the Lakers should still be able to expose the Hornets' lack of offensive firepower, their inability to take care of the basketball and poor defense. Los Angeles has done a good job exploiting these weaknesses in opponents over the last few games.

As long as the Lakers don't let their weaknesses get exploited and continue working to address them, they should be able to keep the wins coming. Let's see if the Lakers can do just that to the Hornets on Tuesday.

Believe it or not, this eight-game winning streak is something Luka Dončić never experienced with the Dallas Mavericks. He's already done so with the Lakers three times.

The Lakers' next two games will be a set of back-to-back games.

For the Lakers' injury report, Kessler (right lower leg soreness) and Austin Reaves (left heel contusion) are out.

As for the Hornets, Kon Knueppel (left hamstring strain) and Dennis Schroder (left hamstring strain) are out.
