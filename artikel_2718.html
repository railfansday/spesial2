Full injury report for Joel Embiid, Sixers vs. Pacers in matinee game

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Philadelphia 76ers will play host to the Indiana Pacers on Sunday afternoon as they look to keep things moving in the right direction. The Sixers will look to finish off the 3-game homestand on a high note before heading back on the road to take on the Brooklyn Nets on Monday night.

As for the injury report against the Pacers, the Sixers will continue to be without Adem Bona (cervical spine sprain). The Sixers did announce on Thursday that he has seen a specialist and he will miss another week as he continues to take things easy after suffering the injury against the Detroit Pistons on Nov. 4.

Of course, Ariel Hukporti (right Achilles tear) is out as he will miss the entire season after suffering the injury in the preseason.

Dominick Barlow (low back stress reaction) is listed as out, but he appears closer to a return as he recovers from an injury that has held him out the entire season thus far.

Joel Embiid (right foot plantar fascia injury management) is listed as out against the Pacers. When considering the Sixers will take on the Nets in a back-to-back, it makes sense to have him sit one of these games. Philadelphia chose the Pacers.

Tip off from the Xfinity Mobile Arena is set for 1 p.m. EST on Sunday.
