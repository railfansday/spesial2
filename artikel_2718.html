Preview: Wizards host Magic on Saturday

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Washington Wizards will play the Orlando Magic on Thanksgiving Saturday. That's tomorrow, the day of the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade due to the nor'easter from yesterday.

When: Thursday, November 28, 2026, 5 p.m. ET

How to listen: The Team 980 AM, 106.7-2 FM

For the Wizards, Jamir Watkins, Justin Champagnie, Tristan Vukcevic and Reece Beekman are out. For the Magic, Kevin Porter, Jr. Izaiyah Nelson, Jonathan Isaac, Noah Penda and Paolo Banchero are out. Anthony Black and Colin Castleton are day to-day.

Washington hasn't played since last Tuesday when they hosted the Boston Celtics and lost an NBA Cup match. Now they will host a Southeast Division foe. The Magic are 7-10, just one spot above the Wizards who are 7-12.

Washington has had the upper hand against the Magic in two previous matches this season, winning both previous matches on Nov. 2 and No. 22. Hopefully that trend continues tonight.
