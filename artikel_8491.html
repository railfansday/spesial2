I've been inclined to view it as the market failing to adequately price in volatility drag, though perhaps a rigorous look would find otherwise. For example, there might be enough laddering that's not really how the price setting works.

In addition to not pricing out volatility properly, there is also the in ability to price out inflation. The bigger threat isn't that the return don't match the risk but that the return gets eroded by inflation. I think it's hard to predict inflation in general.
What is interesting is compared to TIPS there does not seem to be any risk premium for inflation.
