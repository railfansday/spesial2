United Airlines resolved a system outage that delayed departing flights

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The airline said flights already in progress and United Express flights were not affected by the technology issue

United Airlines said on Monday it resolved a technology issue that delayed some departing flights. The airline posted the update on X $TWTR and told customers to check united.com or the United app for the latest status of their flights.

The outage went on for roughly an hour and a half, according to CBS News. United said flights already in progress and United Express flights were not affected, according to CNBC. A United spokesperson said nothing pointed to a cyberattack.

The Air Line Pilots Association told members the breakdown disrupted several applications pilots rely on, and that United was unable to supply final weights for departing aircraft, according to CNBC. A union memo pointed to the system the airline uses to calculate aircraft weight and balance, according to CBS News.

FlightAware data showed 715 United flights running behind schedule late Monday afternoon, though the share tied to the outage was not clear, according to CBS News. About 90 minutes after the fix, delays and cancellations at United were in line with those at competitors.

The outage struck just three days after the Federal Aviation Administration ordered a nationwide ground stop on Friday, Nov. 13. The FAA said a technical problem at about 3:30 p.m. ET led to the stop and that air traffic resumed at 8:15 p.m. It said legacy equipment became unstable when data flooded its network.

Operators canceled about 12,462 flights, though not all cancellations were directly caused by the ground stop, the FAA said. There was nothing to suggest United's outage had any link to the problems that led to Friday's ground stop, according to CNBC.
