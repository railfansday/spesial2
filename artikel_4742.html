Tokyo's hidden methane: Survey pinpoints nearly 100 hot spots

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Carbon dioxide (CO₂) has been the poster child of climate change, but another culprit could be flying just under the radar. Methane is a potent greenhouse gas with a global warming potential approximately 84 times greater than that of CO₂ over a 20-year period. Fortunately, methane remains in the atmosphere for only about a decade, and reducing its emissions could provide rapid climate mitigation effects.
