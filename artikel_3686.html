I Am Obsessed With GTA 6 Jason’s Literary Choices

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

As I start GTA 6, I’m finding myself oddly charmed by Jason Duval. I fully expected to be a committed player of only Lucia for most of the game, but you have to spend a few hours as Jason before Lucia gets out of prison. I like him a lot more than I expected to. His stoic responses in past trailers had led me to assume he might be a bit of a criminal meathead without much of a sense of humor, but I’m actually finding him quite intelligent and sensitive. Especially the way I play him (doing all the home repairs, putting dishes in the sink, and trying not to kill anyone).

But what really won my heart last night was the discovery that Jason Duval is a man of literature.

No, I’m not talking about most of the reading material around his apartment, if you can call it that. Jason has a number of readable books lying around, most of which are weirdly hypermasculine fantasies about military life. There’s a book about GTA 6‘s ORCA military unit, which sounds like a place people go to become supervillains, and a fictional military novel that seems to be about a suicide squad obsessed with teabagging. I also found a fitness magazine with articles only taken seriously by people sick in the head. I have to assume that Jason Duval is intelligent enough to enjoy all these (as I did) as the cheeky satire that they are, because earnest interest in any of this literature should have him locked behind bars instead of Lucia. I got up really close to the table to try and pick it up, but couldn’t see any triggers to grab it and read a few pages like you can the other books.

This is the most interesting novel in Jason’s apartment. It’s so out of line with the rest of his tastes, yet it’s clearly being actively read, and is part of a longer series he’s interested in. Has Jason been a huge sci-fi/fantasy nerd for a long time? Is this something he’s just been trying out recently and is finding that he enjoys? Is this a satire series like the other stuff in his house or is this a more serious piece of literature? The cover looks a lot like the covers on my copies of the A Song of Ice and Fire books, and even the author name, with its multiple initials, seems to hearken to George R. R. Martin, though also sci-fi covers and author names are sometimes just like that.

Anyway, I’m now convinced that Jason Duval is secretly a huge fucking nerd and am totally here for it. We’re gonna read every book in the game and get this bro all the culture he clearly craves.
