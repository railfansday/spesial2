Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Thank you to everyone who commented in this thread. Since it appears that catastrophic plans have been discontinued in my area, there is no reason to submit a hardship application. Instead I will select the cheapest HSA qualified bronze plan. The options are:

HMO HSA qualified with $8,000 deductible for $2,188.38 (a 34% increase over 2026 PPO HSA qualified catastrophic)
PPO HSA qualified with $8,000 deductible for $2,841.88 (a 74% increase over 2026 PPO HSA qualified catastrophic)

My experience with PPO coverage in 2026 wasn't good. I chose PPO because my child attends college out of state and would be out of network with HMO coverage. The PPO supposedly covers them nationwide via the expanded PHCS MultiPlan network, but we couldn't find a single primary care or specialty physician who accepted our PPO coverage in a major city of over 3 million people despite those physicians being listed as eligible on the PHCS website. Neither the physicians, PHCS nor our insurer could resolve the issue despite months of attempts, so we ended up scheduling appointments with local physicians over the holidays when our child was home. After that experience, I am reluctant to pay the additional premium for PPO coverage.
