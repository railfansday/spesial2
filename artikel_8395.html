If you are close to a cliff and can do something to stay below it, do it. If not, do something to take you close to the next one (but not over). That is like getting a 5% discount on the marginal rate for that additional income (perhaps a Roth conversion would be good).
My income (pension, SS, RMDs, interest) also takes me close to an IRMAA cliff (1.4 x standard as a single filer) for 2026. I am embracing the cliff by converting about $30K to Roth this year to bring me close to the next cliff (2.0 x standard), while staying within the 24% marginal federal income tax bracket.

An alternative would have been for me to take more QCDS to satisfy my 2026 RMD withdrawal. This was a "balancing act" because of the need to satisfy the RMD before taking any Roth conversions.

For MFJ filers, there is room to take over $60K extra in MAGI before reaching the next IRMAA cliff.
