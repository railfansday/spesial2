Antonelli completes Qatar Friday practice double for dominant Mercedes

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Kimi Antonelli and George Russell delivered a one-two for Mercedes in Practice Two despite having their soft-tyre runs interrupted by red flags; watch final practice at 2.30pm ahead of Qualifying at 6pm on Saturday live on Sky Sports F1

Kimi Antonelli topped his team-mate George Russell in both of Friday's practice sessions at the Qatar Grand Prix as Mercedes produced a dominant showing.

Antonelli's time of 1:23.077s saw him finish just over a tenth of a second clear of Russell in Practice Two, with McLaren's Lando Norris almost half a second down on the leading effort in third.

The Silver Arrows were clear of their rivals despite both having what should have been their fastest laps of the session - on fresh soft tyres - blown by two separate red flags, with the leading times set on their earlier medium-tyre runs.

Russell had been more than three tenths up on Antonelli's session-topping time after two sectors when a first red flag was thrown to allow the clearance of debris left on track after Ferrari's Charles Leclerc had spun.

Following that stoppage, Antonelli had gone a tenth up on his leading time in the first sector when Pierre Gasly spun at high speed at Turn 14, leaving his Alpine beached in the gravel.

An extra 13 minutes was added to the end of the session to recover some of the lost time, but after the second red flag the field focused on race simulations, during which Mercedes were also extremely impressive.

Friday's action was something of a throwback to Mercedes' dominance during the early stages of the season, which saw Antonelli and the team build leads that have helped them already secure the drivers' and constructors' championships.

The hugely impressive display came as something of a surprise given Mercedes have won just one of the last six races, with Red Bull's Max Verstappen winning four of those.

Verstappen was just behind Norris and the other McLaren of Oscar Piastri in fifth, and marginally ahead of his Red Bull team-mate Isack Hadjar, with all four around half a second down on Antonelli despite having done their fastest laps on the theoretically quicker soft tyre.

A disappointing day for Ferrari ended with Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton seventh and eighth, respectively, with both failing to get soft-tyre flying laps completed.

Leclerc missed 20 minutes of track action after damaging his car's floor, while Hamilton was interrupted by the red flags and errors of his own.

Hamilton suffered an apparent engine failure in the closing moments of the session, but the issue is not expected to leave him requiring new parts and he should therefore avoid a grid penalty for Sunday's race.

Hamilton was also involved in a strange incident with Verstappen that saw the pair almost make contact on the main straight, but the stewards decided a full investigation wasn't necessary after noting the Brit for driving erratically.

High levels of tyre degradation throughout both of Friday's session offered something of an explanation for Pirelli's decision to introduce a 27-lap tyre limit for Sunday's race, which will create a two-stop race.

It is the third time a tyre limit has been implemented for the race at the Lusail International Circuit after punctures at the inaugural 2021 event and safety concerns being raised during the 2023 weekend.

Many had expected McLaren's car to be suited to the characteristics of the circuit, but Norris admitted after Friday's sessions that Mercedes were so far ahead that he doesn't deem them "worth thinking about".

"We are not too bad relative to most but to Mercedes we are a bit further off. They are in a different category compared to us today [Friday], quicker in every single corner by a mile.

"The battle is for P3 and we seem to be in a relatively decent position. We seem similar on pace to Red Bull and similar-ish to Ferrari, so I would say OK.

"There are things we need to improve and things that can be better. But I guess you always look to the top and when you look at ourselves compared to Mercedes, we need to improve a lot everywhere.

"We are probably seven tenths off the Mercedes a lap, so it's not even worth thinking about them."

Antonelli, who is seeking a 10th win of the season, warned that Mercedes' rivals should not be written off ahead of Saturday's final practice and qualifying sessions.

The Italian said: "I think it's going to be very close between me and George. Of course, you can never write off Max and the others.

"There have been weekends when we were very strong at the beginning then in qualifying the others make a big step and get close, so we just need to keep working and improving and try to be ready for tomorrow."
