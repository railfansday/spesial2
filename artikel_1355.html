Van Dijk has ‘no idea’ where he’ll play, as Liverpool veteran targeted by Milan

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Virgil van Dijk has been heavily linked with a move to Milan, and admits he has ‘no idea’ where he will be playing next season, but the Liverpool veteran is confident he can ‘play for many more years.’

The 35-year-old is known to be a primary target for Rossoneri and RedBird owner Gerry Cardinale, who had already tried to sign him last summer.

It is widely reported in Italy that the push was only postponed, and Van Dijk will wear the Milan jersey at San Siro eventually, perhaps even as a free agent if his contract with Liverpool is allowed to run down on June 30, 2027.

“The main thing for me is that I feel good and I’m looking after myself and I still have many more years in my opinion,” Van Dijk told Sky Sports UK.

“Whatever’s going to happen after this season, we’ll see. At this moment of time, if anyone says that or put out that they know what I’m going to do next season, it’s untrue because at this moment of time, speaking to you, I’ve got no idea either.”

The comments and tone of the interview suggest the Dutch international’s time with Liverpool is coming to an end, opening the door for a Milan transfer.

“It’s been an incredible journey, almost 10 years this summer and it’s been a big part of my life and I’ll forever be grateful for anything that happened over those years.

“If it’s getting extended, we’ll see then. If not, then I’m going to say goodbyes in the summer.”

Curiously, Cardinale’s company RedBird also has a minority stake in Fenway Sports Group, which in turn owns 62 per cent of the shares in Liverpool.

Milan’s pursuit of Van Dijk may well depend on whether they manage to qualify for the Champions League next season.
