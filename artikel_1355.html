Confirmed UEFA Euro 2028 Qualifying Draw Pots

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The road to Euro 2028 begins in Belfast in early December with the draw for the qualification groups. With the Nations League group stage behind us, all five pots have been settled as nations eagerly await confirmation of which sides they will have to overcome in order to qualify for the tournament.

This particular qualifying campaign is different from the usual one. Whilst England, Scotland, Wales and the Republic of Ireland are hosting the tournament, they won't qualify automatically. Instead, all four will have to go through qualifying like everyone else, with a safety net in place which will ensure that at least two home nations will make the final tournament.

Here's every team in each pot, plus when and how to watch the draw.

Euro 2028 is set to take place in the United Kingdom and Ireland. We have outlined everything you need to know about the tournament.

Pot one features all of Europe's footballing heavyweights. Having made the last two European Championship finals, England will be hoping to go one step further and win the competition, especially with the backing of the home crowd. Naturally, the biggest contenders for the title are current European and World champions, Spain.

All the Nations League quarter-finalists feature in pot one, with France, Germany and Portugal winning their respective groups, whilst Norway and Greece join the group as the surprise top seeds.

The most important group for the home nations, pot two includes two co-hosts in the Republic of Ireland and Wales, as well as Northern Ireland, who are no longer considered a co-host after the UK government announced in September 2024 that it wouldn't provide funding to redevelop Casement Park ahead of the tournament.

Switzerland go into the draw as the highest ranked team in the FIFA World Rankings, whilst the likes of Austria, Türkiye and Sweden will also be drawn out of pot two.

Scotland, the only co-host in pot three, were placed there after finishing third in their Nations League B group. This gives them a potentially harder draw than the other home nations, as they are guaranteed a pot one and a pot two opponent. It is important to note, though, that all four co-hosts, plus Northern Ireland, will be drawn into separate groups.

Poland, Ukraine and Slovakia all find themselves in pot three, alongside Georgia, who made a surprise appearance in the knockout stages of the 2024 edition of the tournament in Germany.

Pot four features mostly lower-ranked nations, such as Belarus, Latvia and the Faroe Islands. The most recognizable name from the group is Finland, who reached their first major tournament at Euro 2020.

San Marino will likely see themselves become a talking point, albeit, for the wrong reasons, as they enter the tournament as the bottom ranked team in the world according to FIFA's World Rankings. Since their national team's inception in 1990, San Marino have won just three matches, all of which came against Liechtenstein.

With only six teams in pot five, they will all be drawn into the five-team groups, G-L. Given the extra team compared to the other groups, teams that find themselves in group G-L will play more games and start their qualification journey earlier, with games in March 2027.

With the likes of Andorra, Gibraltar and Lithuania in pot five, any home nation in a five-team group will get an extra, theoretically easier fixture, but will have to contend with a busier schedule.

Whilst Northern Ireland are no longer considered a host nation, they have been given the honor of hosting the qualification draw, which will take place at the International Convention Centre in Belfast, on Sunday 6th December, at 17:00 GMT.

And whilst no UK TV broadcaster has been confirmed yet, fans can watch the draw live via UEFA TV. Alternatively, coverage will also be available to stream on the UEFA YouTube channel.
