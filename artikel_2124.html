Micah Richards named EFL ground as the most hostile football stadium he ever played at

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Micah Richards has previously claimed that Stoke City’s Bet365 Stadium was the most hostile he played at during his career.

The former England international has become a huge figure in the media since his retirement, and he is regularly seen analysing games both domestically and in Europe.

However, that shouldn’t detract from the excellent time he had as a player, with Richards winning 13 caps for the Three Lions, and he also won an FA Cup and Premier League with Manchester City.

So, during that period, Richards saw a lot in the game, and his success with Man City meant he played at some of the biggest and best stadiums in Europe.

Therefore, when Richards was previously asked by Kate Abdo on CBS Sports In the Mixer segment about the most hostile stadium he had played at, it’s fair to say he would have a lot of choice.

Yet, he only had one answer, as Richards replied ‘Stoke on Trent’, before then responding to Jamie Carragher quizzing his choice with ‘don’t disrespect Stoke, honestly’.

The 38-year-old came up against the Potters 11 times in his career for both City and Aston Villa, and he didn’t always have it his own way.

Despite City’s class at that time, they struggled at Stoke on occasions, and Richards tasted defeat twice at the ground, including once in 2009 when Tony Pulis’ side beat Roberto Mancini’s men through a James Beattie goal despite playing with ten men.

The clichés surrounding Stoke came about for a reason, as it was a very difficult place to go to for visitors.

Of course, Stoke had a lot of quality players at the time, and even under Pulis after promotion, a lot of the group were underappreciated, even if they were direct.

But it wasn’t just the setup and the style, as the fans also played a big role. They fully bought into the team, and they whipped up a frenzy most weeks, which helped the team to so many good results.

Unfortunately for the Potters, over the years they lost that edge, and there’s no denying that the past decade has been very tough, and the atmosphere that Richards referenced has disappeared.

Yet, there are reasons for positivity under Mark Robins right now, as his side are finally competing at the top end of the Championship.

You can understand why Stoke fans have been frustrated over the years, as the club have made some bad mistakes in the transfer market and with who they appointed.

But, right now, the structure looks good, and the recruitment was very impressive in the last window.

So, the fans are starting to believe that this season could be a memorable one, and if the team can keep building momentum, then it won’t be long before the Bet365 Stadium is rocking once again.
