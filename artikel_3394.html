Tame Impala and Jennie’s ‘Dracula’ Saw a Major Streaming Bump Over Halloweekend

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Could the “Dracula” remix become the new “Thriller”? Tame Impala and Jennie‘s foreboding synth-pop track is well on its way to gaining Halloween staple song status after experiencing a big streaming bump during the spooky holiday. From Oct. 30 to Nov. 1, both the original version and the remix collectively received 16 million global on-demand streams across all platforms, according to music data company Luminate.

Last weekend from Oct. 23-Oct. 25, “Dracula” received 11 million streams during Halloweekend, marking a 44 percent increase . Michael Jackson’s timeless “Thriller” still topped the Global Spotify charts on Oct. 31, but Tame and Jennie’s remix may just beat the song out on Billboard‘s Hot 100 chart. According to early chart predictions, the song’s Halloween streaming surge could help it reach a new peak at Number Three, a slot above “Thriller.”

Since it was released in February, the “Dracula” remix has been inescapable — and quickly became one of the biggest songs in the world, notching nearly 1 billion on-demand global streams and earning both Tame Impala and Jennie their first Top 10 hit on the Billboard Hot 100.

The original song appeared on the Australian music project’s Deadbeat, but a chance encounter between Kevin Parker, the brain behind Tame Impala, and Jennie led them to collaborate on a new version. “I was like, ‘I really love Jennie’s voice.
