Harry Potter Officially Abandons ‘Sorcerer’s Stone’ After 28 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

For nearly three decades, American and British Harry Potter fans have been reading essentially the same book, just with two very different titles. When J.K. Rowling first introduced the Boy Who Lived to the world in 1997, his debut adventure was called Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone but when the book arrived in the United States the following year, publisher Scholastic decided that American readers would be better served by a slightly different name, changing it to Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone. However, finally, it's going back to normal.

Scholastic has officially, finally, dropped the Sorcerer's Stone title for a new American television tie-in edition of Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone, marking the first time the publisher has adopted the original British title for this edition after 28 years. The announcement comes directly from the official Harry Potter website, which confirmed that publishers around the world are releasing the special edition ahead of the show's Christmas premiere. The inside of the book is identical, bar the mentions of the Stone itself.

When Rowling's first novel was published in Britain in June 1997, the title referred to the Philosopher's Stone, a legendary substance associated with alchemy and the supposed ability to turn ordinary metals into gold, and the name also connects directly to Nicolas Flamel, the alchemist plays an important role in the story. But when Scholastic got the American publishing rights, they felt that younger American readers would be confused or put off by the title and went for Sorcerer's Stone instead, deeming it more fantastical and appealing. Accordingly, that move carried over into the 2001 movie adaptation, which was released under different titles depending on the country.

HBO's upcoming adaptation, though, is sticking with the original and best name, with its first season officially titled Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone. The series stars Dominic McLaughlin as Harry Potter, Arabella Stanton as Hermione Granger, and Alastair Stout as Ron Weasley. The cast also includes John Lithgow (The Crown) as Albus Dumbledore, Janet McTeer (Ozark) as Minerva McGonagall, Paapa Essiedu (I May Destroy You) as Severus Snape, Nick Frost (Shaun of the Dead) as Rubeus Hagrid, Lox Pratt (Lord of the Flies) as Draco Malfoy, Warwick Davis (Willow) as Filius Flitwick, and Katherine Parkinson(The IT Crowd) as Molly Weasley.

The new Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone television tie-in paperback was released November 3, 2026. HBO's Harry Potter series premieres December 25, 2026, on HBO and HBO Max.
