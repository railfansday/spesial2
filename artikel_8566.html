Capital One opens it to students enrolled in college or admitted and starting within three months, and it rates the card for fair credit, which its guidelines say includes anyone with under three years of credit history. With the Journey Student retired, this card and the Savor Student are Capital One’s two student cards, and Deserve has stopped offering its EDU student card.

By our math, students who put less than a quarter of their spending in those four categories earn more with this card’s flat 1.5% than with the Savor Student, which pays 1% on everything else. Students who spend more than a quarter there should get the Savor Student.
