Jena Malone, Suzanna Son & Sofia Mortera Wrap On Indie ‘Kismet’ From Giovanna Molina

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

EXCLUSIVE: Jena Malone (The Boroughs) and Suzanna Son (Monster: The Ed Gein Story) have wrapped roles opposite newcomer Sofia Mortera in Kismet, an indie from Radiance Pictures and Distant Films that marks the feature debut of writer-director Giovanna Molina.

Set during the desolate off-season on Fire Island, Kismet follows a melancholic 13-year-old girl (Mortera) who, in the wake of her parents’ separation, claims an alien has taken over her body to save her from her human emotions.

Recently wrapping production, Kismet co-stars Elliot Villar and Riley Vinson, with Zuzanna Szadkowski, Matthew Sean Blumm, Marta Pozzan, Caitlin Hagney, David Staats and Lorena Ramos rounding out the cast. The film is produced by Molina, along with Zoey Pressey, Alexander Spenser Gould and Nuer Taqa. It’s cast by Betsy Fippinger, CSA. Molina told Deadline, “It felt incredibly special to bring this talented cast and crew this fall to Fire Island, where I spent much of my childhood. Jena Malone is a coming-of-age sci-fi icon, so it’s truly full circle to have her supporting a young exciting newcomer like Sofia Mortera in a film that nods to Donnie Darko through a female lens. Throughout our shoot, the island took on a mythical shape that mirrored the uncanny nature of the film: beached whales, nor’easter floods, roaming deer, and a double rainbow, to name a few. Filming against this surreal backdrop was a remarkable experience.” Watch on Deadline

A Golden Globe nominee, Malone has most recently been seen in the Netflix series The Boroughs, exec produced by The Duffer Brothers, as well as Jack Begert’s indie Junk, produced by Darren Aronofsky. Upcoming, she’ll be seen in the horror thriller Pendulum and Arkasha Stevenson’s untitled horror film for A24. She is represented by Entertainment 360, Gersh and Hirsch Wallerstein Hayum.

Son is best known for her breakout turn opposite Simon Rex in Sean Baker’s Red Rocket, for which she was nominated for the Gotham Award for Breakout Performer as well as the Independent Spirit Award for Best Supporting Female. She recently appeared in Monster: The Ed Gein Story and will also be seen coming up in A24’s mystery film from Stevenson. She is repped by Curate, CAA and Jackoway Austen Tyerman.

Mortera voices Young Priscilla in Netflix’s animated feature Steps and has appeared in the films Come Out and Play and The Spirit of Innocence. Additional voiceover credits include Netflix’s CoComelon Lane and Prime Video’s Agent Zeta. She is repped by DDO Artists Agency and Mills Kaplan Entertainment.

A Mexican-Italian-American filmmaker from Brooklyn, NY, Molina’s work explores stories of isolation, belief and surrealism within a female coming-of-age context. She has received grants and fellowships from Sundance, PBS, The Future of Film is Female and the Saul Zaentz Innovation Fund. Her film Quaker had its world premiere at the 75th Berlin Film Festival and won Best Narrative Short at Bend Film Festival. She holds an MFA in Directing from the UCLA School of Theater, Film and Television and a BA from Johns Hopkins University, with degrees in The Writing Seminars and Film & Media Studies. She is represented by Circle Management + Production.
