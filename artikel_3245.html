Grifols, S.A. (GRFS +5.06%) reported financial results for the third quarter of 2026, highlighting growth in its Biopharma segment and the continued deleveraging of its balance sheet. Management stated that stronger operating performance and cost discipline resulted in improved profitability and cash generation, allowing the company to reach a net leverage ratio below 4.0x. While the company confirmed its full-year guidance, it noted ongoing challenges in the China albumin market and a reported decline in Diagnostics revenue following the dissolution of a joint business. The company remains focused on strategic initiatives, including the integration of Egyptian plasma sourcing and the evaluation of a potential public offering for its U.S. Biopharma business.
