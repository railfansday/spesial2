How Peoria-area girls swimmers and divers fared in the 2026 IHSA state finals prelims

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Three Peoria-area swimmers advanced from the preliminary round of the 2026 Illinois High School Association girls swimming and diving state finals on Friday at the FMC Natatorium in Westmont.

Peoria Notre Dame sophomore Kamryn Gardiner will swim in a pair of events, clocking the fastest time in the 100-yard butterfly (54.22 seconds) and the fifth-best in the 200-yard individual medley (2 minutes, 2.79 seconds).

She'll be joined on the final day of the season by LaSalle-Peru co-op senior Samantha Nauman, who swam a 2:04.22 in the 200 IM. Nauman posted the second-fastest time in the 100-yard backstroke (54.47 seconds), while Washington co-op senior Maya Happel is in the 100-yard freestyle finals with the 16th-fastest time of 52.43 seconds.

Each preliminary event sends the top 16 finishers to the finals. The top eight will compete in the "A" final, while the next eight placers are in the "B" final. All 16 places will earn state medals.

Washington senior Ava Gensler will also compete Saturday in three events for athletes with disabilities — the 200-yard freestyle, the 50-yard freestyle and the 100-yard freestyle.

In total, six relays, 10 individual swimmers and one diver in 15 events had qualified for state from the sectional level. Here is how Peoria-area girls swimmers and divers fared during those prelims.
