NFL predictions: Hope, without faith, is how we’re viewing the Panthers until further notice

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Carolina Panthers have been on an odd run of injuries, poor discipline, horrible officiating, and good opponents for too long now. Injury luck, if only for the moment, seems to be a tide turning in the Panthers' favor; discipline may yet prove to be the deciding factor in head coach Dave Canales' tenure; and the quality of their opponent this week is certainly up for debate. Whether we're talking Lamar Jackson or Marlon Humphrey, the Baltimore Ravens are perhaps the first team missing as much talent as the Panthers—if not more—that Carolina has seen on their schedule this season.

Enter Jacoby Brissett. The spry, 33-year old, ten year veteran was acquired by the Ravens via trade on November 6th after Jackson and backup quarterback Tyler Huntley were both lost for the season after only five pass attempts against the Buffalo Bills in Week 8. Brissett started his first game just last Monday, November 16th, for the Ravens and attempted 49 passes in a three-point loss to the Los Angeles Chargers.

We don't know what we're going to see from Brissett tomorrow. He's with a new team, has a history of ups and downs as a starter, and is coming off of a short week of preparation. Brissett and the Ravens are a true wildcard. Maybe Will Lee III and Mike Jackson returning will be enough to iron out any concerns over what Brissett and Ravens team in disarray will bring to the field. Then again, we're pinning a lot of hope there on a rookie corner returning from injury who has shown both promise and a penchant for his own discipline related penalties. That's not to mention the broader penalty issues or quarterback Bryce Young's returning lack of consistency. Maybe that's why, at -2.5, the Panthers are favored over the Ravens in what is tied as the second narrowest spread on FanDuel this week. There are as many reasons to be optimistic about the Panthers in this and future weeks as there are reasons to keep them firmly in the "I'll believe it when I see it" category.
