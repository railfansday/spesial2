Guatemalan Illegal Alien Sentenced For Illegal Reentry of a Removed Alien

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

NEW ORLEANS, LOUISIANA – OSEAS AGUILAR GUZMAN (“AGUILAR-GUZMAN”), age 38, a native of Guatemala, was sentenced on October 27, 2026, by Chief United States District Judge Wendy B. Vitter, after previously pleading guilty to reentry of a removed alien, in violation of Title 8, United States Code, Section 1326(a), announced United States Attorney David I. Courcelle.
