Our long-term friend, Matt Macfarlane, was a very important part of the Monadelphous team for many years. On behalf of the Board and everyone at Monadelphous, I extend our sincere condolences to Matt's family, friends and colleagues. He will be forever remembered fondly and is greatly missed.
