Steelers’ Aaron Rodgers Hit With Brutal Criticism Before Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Pittsburgh Steelers and future Hall of Fame quarterback Aaron Rodgers are set to face off against the Cincinnati Bengals this week. Ahead of the game, the team holds a 6-2 record and they are tied for the AFC North division lead.

Rodgers, who is playing in what he has said will be his final NFL season, is hoping to ride off into the sunset of retirement with a Super Bowl run.

So far this season, the 42-year-old quarterback has completed 60.8 percent of his pass attempts for 1,871 yards, 13 touchdowns, and six interceptions in eight games. He hasn't played poor football, but he certainly isn't the superstar player that he once was.

With that being said, one notable analyst has spoken out with some harsh criticism of the veteran quarterback.

Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers (8) throws a pass during an NFL football game against the Cleveland Browns at Acrisure Stadium. Mandatory Credit: Barry Reeger-Imagn Images

During an appearance on "First Things First," Nick Wright didn't hold back his opinion on Rodgers. He doesn't think Rodgers is doing enough to win it all.

"Aaron Rodgers, who we did not think was lighting the world on fire last year, is worse in everything this year," Wright said. "If he is this, I don't think that's enough."

At the end of the day, Wright may be right. Rodgers hasn't cost the Steelers games, but he also hasn't been the driving force behind wins.

Come playoff time, quarterbacks face moments where they have to put the team on their back. Whether or not Rodgers is still capable of doing that for Pittsburgh is very much a question mark at this point in time.

This week's game will say a lot about the state of the AFC North division race. Both the Steelers and Bengals are 6-2, with the Cleveland Browns right behind them at 5-4.

Kickoff is scheduled this week for 8:20 p.m. ET on Sunday Night Football. It will be interesting to see if Rodgers is able to lead Pittsburgh to a big-time win to take back full control of the division lead exiting Week 10.
