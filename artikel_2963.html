Eli Lilly vs. Novo Nordisk: Not Every GLP-1 Maker Is Enjoying Booming Sales Trends

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Eli Lilly's quarterly sales have outpaced Novo Nordisk's since late 2024, with the gap widening sharply through 2026. Here is how the numbers stack up.

Eli Lilly & Co. (LLY +3.15%) makes medicines for diabetes, obesity, and cancer for a wide range of people.

It won approval for Mounjaro to reduce cardiovascular risk on Aug. 28. It started building an active pharmaceutical ingredient site in Pennsylvania on Nov. 12.

Novo Nordisk (NVO +2.99%) makes treatments for diabetes, obesity, and rare blood disorders.

It raised its full-year guidance for adjusted sales and operating profit on Nov. 4. It filed for Wegovy approval in younger children on Nov. 16.

Revenue here refers to the data provider's standardized income-statement revenue line item. A rise or fall in revenue shows if a business is growing.

Eli Lilly vs. Novo Nordisk Revenue over the last 8 quarters. Figures: Novo Nordisk shown in U.S. dollars, converted from Danish kroner.

The revenue trend is the most obvious metric for investors to examine when considering a stock. The first thing to check for when examining a company's revenue trend is seasonality: some times of the year have stronger buying patterns than others. There usually is some seasonality for every business.

Even in pharmaceuticals, there is seasonality, with the end of the calendar year seeing higher prescription activity as consumers and doctors front-load prescriptions after insurance deductibles have been met, before they reset with the new year.

This chart shows some seasonality, like that for Eli Lilly, with the first quarter of 2025 showing a decline from the last period of 2024. Novo Nordisk shows the same trend, but as we move from left to right on the chart, we see that Lilly's sequential revenue is increasing, while Novo's is choppy. That doesn't necessarily mean Novo Nordisk's business is in trouble, but it does show us Eli Lilly is firing on all cylinders.
