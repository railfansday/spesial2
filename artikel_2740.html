Injury-riddled Rams lose 3 more crucial players as concerns mount

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Los Angeles Rams are running out of healthy bodies faster than they're running out of games.

Already one of the most injury-riddled teams in the NFL, the Rams lost three more players during Wednesday night's 26-13 Thanksgiving Eve victory over the Green Bay Packers at SoFi Stadium, leaving two position groups dangerously thin ahead of Thursday's matchup with the Kansas City Chiefs.

ESPN's Adam Schefter first reported Friday that tight end Tyler Higbee suffered a chest contusion and is considered week to week. Sources confirmed the injury to The California Post, adding that Higbee will need to be reevaluated Monday or Tuesday to determine whether he can absorb contact and potentially play against Kansas City.

Tyler Higbee #89 of the Los Angeles Rams catches a pass against Evan Williams #33 of the Green Bay Packers at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, Calif., Nov. 25, 2026. Getty Images

Fellow tight end Davis Allen suffered an ankle injury, although the severity and recovery timeline remain undetermined. Both tight ends exited Wednesday's game and did not return.

Higbee has caught 32 passes for 318 yards and two touchdowns this season, while Allen has contributed 15 receptions for 124 yards and three touchdowns. With Terrance Ferguson already lost for the season, Colby Parkinson and rookie Max Klare could be the Rams' only healthy tight ends Thursday.

Los Angeles has already been without its two best receivers, and run as much 12 and 13-personnel in the league. Depth at tight end is crucial in running those formations, while also lending a hand to the recent dominance on the ground.

Los Angeles Rams tight end Davis Allen (87) celebrates a touchdown with quarterback Matthew Stafford (9) in Glendale, Ariz., Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. AP Photo/Rick Scuteri

But if you thought depth at the tight end position was a concern, wait until you see the news on the offensive line.

Bill Murray, who started at left guard in place of the injured Steve Avila, was spotted by The Post in the locker room following Wednesday's victory wearing a walking boot and using crutches. Sources told The Post there is fear Murray's injury could be season-ending, pending further testing.

Bill Murray (66) of the Los Angeles Rams stands on the sideline during the national anthem before an NFL preseason game in Inglewood, California, Aug. 27, 2026. Getty Images

Rams head coach Sean McVay is expected to provide updates on all of the injuries, including Allen and Murray on Saturday.

The Rams have already lost Puka Nacua, Poona Ford, Jaylen Watson, Ferguson, Dean Connors, Blake Grupe and Dylan McMahon for the season. Davante Adams remains sidelined with a ruptured hamstring, while Coleman Shelton and Avila are recovering from hamstring injuries of their own.

Los Angeles Rams tight end Tyler Higbee celebrates his catch during an NFL football game against the Green Bay Packers in Inglewood, Calif., Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026. AP Photo/Gregory Bull

Even Myles Garrett, Aaron Donald and Trent McDuffie have missed time this season. Making matters worse, the Rams are running low on injured reserve return designations, handcuffing their roster flexibility.

Remarkably, Los Angeles has won five consecutive games and currently occupies the NFC's seventh and final playoff spot.

But with the NFL's toughest remaining strength of schedule and the three-time Super Bowl champion Chiefs coming to town Thursday, the Rams will need all the healthy players they can get to keep this winning streak rolling.
