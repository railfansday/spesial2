49ers make necessary Mike Evans move to play vs Vikings; not what you think

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

49ers coach Kyle Shanahan revealed the team is hopeful to have Mike Evans back on the field for their Mexico City game against the Vikings by having him travel to meet the team in Colorado Springs, Colorado.

But that doesn't necessarily mean he will be available.

On Monday, Shanahan revealed that the team is "hoping to [get him into practice this week]. Just not sure yet."

Kyle Shanahan standing on the sideline during Sunday's game against the Cowboys. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

"It's still too early. He's flying in tonight with the guys who were left behind who didn't travel with us to Dallas. So we'll see how these next few days go and see if he can get out there sometime this week," Shanahan said.

Evans has been on the 49ers sideline since Week 6, since injuring his Achilles tendon in the second quarter against the Washington Commanders.

Evans went down with a non-contact injury after he hauled in a 16-yard reception while running up the seam.

Evans entered the game recovering from a rib injury and was also dealing with his Achilles hurting.

As he's been working his way back from the injury, 49ers general manager John Lynch gave good news for his star wide receiver on Saturday, by hinting he could've played in Sunday's game against the Cowboys: "If this was his last game or something, he would be able to go out there."

Mike Evans running past a Washington Commanders defender before he injured his Achilles. AP Photo/Kelley L. Cox

Lynch added that the 49ers' philosophy has been focused on making sure he's fully healed up so he won't miss any future time and will be ready to go in the playoffs.

With an 8-1 record, the need to rush Evans back from injury might be the least of San Francisco's concerns as they have sole possession of the NFC right now and control their own destiny.

Mike Evans could miss Sunday's game against the Vikings despite being on his way to travel with the team. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

The six-time Pro Bowler was signed to a lucrative, three-year, $60 million contract with San Francisco over the offseason and has looked to be worth every penny.

Through six games, he and quarterback Brock Purdy found an early connection that should still exist when he does return.

Before Evans was shut down, he was the team's leading receiver, having 27 receptions with 420 yards and three touchdowns.

While he's been absent, San Francisco has been finding production from other players.

Keenan Allen, who was the one player the team traded for at the NFL Trade Deadline last week, had an encouraging 49ers debut in Sunday's 46-40 win over the Cowboys.

Allen had eight targets come his way, while hauling in four receptions for 26 yards.
