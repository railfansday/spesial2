Mike Tomlin calls Steelers a ‘dumpster fire’ after Aaron Rodgers release

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Pittsburgh Steelers have been in the headlines for all of the wrong reasons this week ahead of their Black Friday showdown with the Denver Broncos. Late Tuesday night, Aaron Rodgers was benched, which led to him going on the Pat McAfee Show and going scorched earth, making some wild accusations against Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy.

Rodgers was waived by Pittsburgh on Wednesday after his comments. During halftime of the Bills vs. Chiefs Thanksgiving Night game, longtime Steelers head coach Mike Tomlin sat down with NBC play-by-play broadcaster Mike Tirico to give his thoughts on the developing situation in Pittsburgh.

When Tomlin was asked about the eomtions he was feeling seeing this happen to the team he coached for nearly two decades, he offered a simple but interesting response.

"I'm upset about it," Tomlin told Tirico. "You know, it's a dumpster fire, and I don't say that in a judgmental way. I've presided over a few dumps to fires in my 19 years. But, it certainly is upsetting, and I'm sure Steeler Nation is upset."

Although Tomlin made it clear his use of the words "dumpster fire" were not judgmental, it feels appropriate given the events of the last 48 hours. It feels like this situation is far from over. McCarthy and his team will have to look to quiet the noise after a tumultuous week to take on one of the AFC's best in the Broncos.
