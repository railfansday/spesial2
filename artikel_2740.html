LIVE DISCUSSION: Brooklyn Nets vs. Toronto Raptors, 7:30 PM ET

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

It wasn't pretty, but when you're the Nets you'll take a win any way you can get one. Brooklyn finally took care of business on Wednesday in Milwaukee, snapping its five-game skid with a 104-97 victory. Two days later, they find themselves in the first half of a back-to-back against a shorthanded Toronto Raptors team.

Toronto is coming off a frustrating 116-111 home loss to those same Bucks on Monday night, and enters Friday with both Kawhi Leonard and RJ Barrett sidelined. That leaves Scottie Barnes — posting career highs in usage, field-goal attempts, and scoring — to carry the offense.

Meanwhile, with Nolan Traore still out, Ben Saraf has been called up to give Jordi Fernandez extra backcourt depth alongside Mikel Brown Jr. and the several other players who bring up the ball. We said it on Wednesday and we'll say it again tonight — these are the ones the Nets have to get. If they want anything to show for this abysmal season, let's mention how they're only two games back from a play-in spot.
