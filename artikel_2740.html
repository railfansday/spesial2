Steelers rule out Nick Herbig

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Steelers will not have linebacker Nick Herbig against the Eagles.

Herbig was listed as questionable on Friday's injury report, but the team announced on Saturday that he has been ruled out. Herbig missed practice all week with injuries to his hip and ribs.

Herbig has 23 tackles, three sacks and a forced fumble so far this season.

The Steelers listed five others as questionable, including wide receivers Michael Pittman Jr. and Roman Wilson. Both of those players are on injured reserve and the Steelers will need to make roster moves activating them if they are going to play against the Eagles on Sunday.
