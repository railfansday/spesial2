Kayshon Boutte injures hip against Colts

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Houston Texans WR Kayshon Boutte (hip) suffered a hip injury during the Week 11 game against the Indianapolis Colts. He caught both of his targets for 40 yards and a touchdown before exiting.
