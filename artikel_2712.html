Charlotte Hornets jersey history No. 8 Terrance Roberson 2000-2001

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

After going undrafted in the 2000 NBA Draft, Terrance Roberson secured a deal with the Charlotte Hornets. It didn't last very long, however, as Roberson made only three appearances before being waived in December of 2000. In those three appearances, the Fresno State alum averaged zero points, 0.3 rebounds and 0.3 assists in 4.3 minutes per game. He was unable to reach the NBA again.

Following his stint with Charlotte, Roberson would finish the year in the G-League before pursuing a career overseas. He played for various teams outside of the United States before calling it a career after the 2011 season.
