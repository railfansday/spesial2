Vikings HC reveals positive Jordan Addison news amid threat of IR roster move

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Vikings HC reveals positive Jordan Addison news amid threat of IR roster move originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Minnesota Vikings made the biggest splash at the NFL trade deadline when they acquired quarterback Kirk Cousins from the Las Vegas Raiders for just a third-round pick. Instantly, he proved to be the missing piece for the Vikings.

But the Vikings got some bad news right after the Cousins trade, as wide receiver Jordan Addison suffered a thumb injury during practice and will miss some time. There's even been talk of him going on injured reserve.

However, NFL Network's Ian Rapoport shared at Kevin O'Connell's press conference that Addison is not set to land on injured reserve despite having surgery on his injured thumb.

"No IR for Jordan Addison, per coach Kevin O'Connell," Rapoport shared on Twitter/x from the Vikings' Wednesday press conference.

This is a huge sigh of relief for the Vikings, as they now should avoid the lengthy loss of Addison for the next four weeks, which is the minimum time he'd have to stay on IR.

That would mean Addison would have had to miss Weeks 11, 12, 13, and 14. Considering he's avoiding IR, he is expected to return during one of those weeks, most likely in either Week 13 or 14.

Things could change, especially if his recovery from surgery isn't going as planned. But for now, things appear to be headed in a positive direction, with IR no longer expected for the Vikings' No. 2 WR.

Aside from Justin Jefferson, the Minnesota offense will be relying on wide receivers Jauan Jennings, Myles Price, and Tai Felton in Addison's absence.

Cousins hit Price for a touchdown in Week 10, and Jennings could be in store for a revenge game against the San Francisco 49ers in Week 11. T.J. Hockenson might be back in Week 11 as well after missing Week 10 due to injury.

Addison's absence will continue following thumb surgery, but with all the information the Vikings have now, they're expecting their No. 2 WR back at some point in the next four weeks, so they won't need to put Addison on IR.
