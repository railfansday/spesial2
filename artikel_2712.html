49ers rookie Kaelon Black has developed RB tandem with Christian McCaffrey

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The San Francisco 49ers wanted to develop rookie running back Kaelon Black in order preserve Christian McCaffrey, decreasing the amount of carries he has on a game-to-game basis.

McCaffrey is going to be McCaffrey, no matter what. However, it's Black, the 25-year-old rookie who has filled into that role just as the Niners had hoped when they took him with the 90th overall pick in the third round of the 2026 NFL Draft.

Black has been the perfect complement to McCaffrey throughout the season. Together, they've piled up 1,323 scrimmage yards and 15 touchdowns on 239 attempts through nine games. Black has 373 yards and two touchdowns on 84 carries.

49ers head coach Kyle Shanahan has been impressed with Black's development and how McCaffrey took him under his wings the moment he came into the team facility.

"I also think Christian really respects Kaelon," Shanahan told reporters on Nov. 16. "I remember Christian telling me right when he got here how much of a pro that guy seemed like and seemed like he was made of the right stuff. It's because K.B., similar to Christian, he works at everything. He's very hard on himself. He's very tough and approaches each day to get better. As talents and as people, I think they pair together very well."

The two continued to show why they're quietly becoming one of the better backfield tandems in the league. In the Week 10 matchup against the Dallas Cowboys, Black and McCaffrey combined for 289 yards and four touchdowns.

While McCaffrey ran for 62 yards and a touchdown, he also added four catches on four targets for a career-high 161 yards and two touchdowns.

"I think Christian's the perfect mentor just because even if he doesn't say anything, just how Christian works every second," Shanahan said. "Christian's the most detailed, focused, obsessive player I've ever been around. Just watch how he blocks when [WR] Deebo's [Samuel Sr.] running back and he's playing receiver and just the technique and the effort he goes through on every single play. Just the way Christian plays football is the best example or mentor to anybody who's on our team or anybody who wants to watch how a football player goes about his business."

Black is learning from McCaffrey. He was targeted twice and caught both passes for 13 yards. On the ground, he had 11 touches for 53 yards and a touchdown, scoring on a 25-yard bruising run during which Black trucked through the Cowboys' secondary.

Shanahan praised Black for his physicality on the field whether running the ball or blocking for teammates.

"Oh, it was awesome. The one I got to see live was running over the safety, and the safety brought it too," Shanahan said. "I thought that was a good play by the safety, but K.B. brought it more. Then to finish it on the corner was such an impressive run."

"I didn't get to see his chips on the D-Lineman live," he added. "I saw [Dallas Cowboys DL] Quinnen Williams go out in the game and they told me that was because K.B. hit him and I was trying to figure out how he did because it was on a bootleg. Then he did it another time on another player going through the B-gap, so just the physicality he looks for and then the way his body's wired that when you have that mentality and you're wired that way, obviously when he hits them it feels a little bit different."

While Black did have a mind-blowing fumble towards the end of the game while the Cowboys were desperately trying to mount a comeback, the 49ers retained possession. Shanahan praised Cowboys defensive lineman Quinnen Williams for lodging the ball out, rather criticizing his rookie running back.

"We'll see if he was loose with or if he had his elbow up, if he didn't have his pressure points," Shanahan said. "From what I saw on the sideline 92 is a big man, got his arm in there and just raked it out, so we got to see how he was holding it and stuff, but it was a good play by 92 and I;m glad that we recovered it because it could have been a big one."

Black's play throughout the season has gained the trust of his coaches and teammates. He's showing why the Niners selected him.

McCaffrey had 1,222 scrimmage yards and 8 touchdowns on 229 touches through nine games in 2025. This season in the same amount of games, his touches are way down at 155. McCaffrey has gained 950 scrimmage yards and scored 13 touchdowns through nine games.

Those other touches went to Black, whose progressing along quite nicely as a rookie alongside McCaffrey. They've helped the Niners become one of the best running teams in the NFL.

San Francisco is ranked fifth in rush yards (1,296), third in rush touchdowns (15) and third in yards per carry (4.9).
