‘Childish,’ ‘He’s Rattled’ — NBA Fans Get Vocal After Luka Dončić’s Confrontation With a Fan After Lakers’ Loss to Spurs

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

A courtside confrontation involving Luka Dončić and a San Antonio Spurs fan after the Los Angeles Lakers' 99-96 loss at Frost Bank Center on Friday night drew online criticism of the superstar point guard.

The spectator shoved Dončić as he walked toward the tunnel, but the exchange began moments earlier when the Slovenian guard returned to retrieve an item he had tossed toward a woman sitting courtside.

The incident began as Dončić headed off the floor. The woman, seated courtside along his path, appeared to say something to him. The former Dallas Mavericks guard tossed what appeared to be his wristband toward her but then turned around to retrieve it.

The man seated beside the woman had picked up the item by then. He handed it back to the six-time All-Star, but the spectator then shoved the Lakers star, prompting forward Jarred Vanderbilt and arena security to step between them.

NBA Central shared video of the exchange.

One fan wrote: "Can't see how anyone can defend Luka on this. He threw his sweaty sleeve at a Spurs fan, went back to retrieve it, got shoved, and then played the victim."

A third fan commented: "Understandable response from the fan, Luka is childish."

One reply said: "Lowkey deserved that, why'd he chuck something at his … wife."

Another fan added: "You throw something at my spouse, I'm swinging."

The woman's relationship with the spectator has not been independently established. Still, another viewer had this to say: "Stood on business in front of his wife. Give him season tix."

The courtside incident followed a difficult night for Los Angeles. The Lakers led by as many as 20 points and took a 54-38 advantage into halftime, but San Antonio outscored them 61-42 over the final two quarters. Stephon Castle led the Spurs with 26 points and 9 assists, while Victor Wembanyama added 25 points and 16 rebounds.

Dončić finished with a team-high 31 points on 14-for-31 shooting, along with 8 assists, 6 rebounds, and 3 steals in 38 minutes. The Lakers struggled to protect the ball, committing 17 turnovers as San Antonio completed the comeback.

The collapse began in the third quarter, when Los Angeles managed just 14 points. Keldon Johnson's 3-pointer with 1.8 seconds left tied the game at 68. San Antonio then scored 31 points in the fourth to complete the three-point victory.

The defeat also eliminated the Lakers from the NBA Cup and dropped them to 15-4. San Antonio improved to 16-4 and advanced in the competition.

Dončić had another notable exchange with opposing fans earlier in November, when he traded remarks with courtside supporters during a Lakers win over the Atlanta Hawks. Friday's confrontation in San Antonio added another incident involving the six-time All-NBA First Team selection and spectators.
