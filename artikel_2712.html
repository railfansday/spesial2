Broncos, Riley Moss agree to four-year extension

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Broncos have locked up one of their key defensive players.

Per Ian Rapoport of NFL Network, cornerback Riley Moss has agreed to a four-year contract extension with the franchise.

A third-round pick in 2023, Moss was set to become a free agent in the offseason.

Rapoport notes the deal is worth $67 million.

Moss has appeared in 53 games with 39 starts since Denver drafted him. He’s started all eight games this season, tallying 27 total tackles with eight passes defensed and an interception.

Last year, Moss led the league with 19 passes defensed. He’s put up 35 PDs in his career.
