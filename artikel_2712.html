The polls are open! Vote for the top Tulare County athlete of the week

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Visalia Times-Delta/Tulare Advance-Register readers can now vote for their favorite high school athletes.

The poll closes at noon Friday, Nov. 6. There are no restrictions, so vote now and vote often. Please do not email your votes.

Adan Aldana, football, Woodlake: Aldana rumbled for 122 yards rushing and a touchdown to lift the Tigers to an outright East Sequoia League championship.

Connor Cain, football, Golden West: Cain scored 4 total touchdowns (3 receiving, 1 rushing) and finished with 8 catches for 89 yards as the Trailblazers won their fourth straight Battle for the Saddle rivalry game.

Leonardo Coronado, football, Orosi: Coronado rushed for 128 yards and 3 touchdowns as the Cardinals captured an outright East Sequoia League-Redwood championship.

Emma Crisp, girls flag football, Redwood: Crisp passed for 326 yards and 4 touchdowns in a shutout playoff win over Lompoc.

Mike Ennis, football, Porterville: Ennis was nearly perfect in the Granite Bowl victory, completing 18-of-20 passes for 231 yards and 3 touchdowns. He also ran for 101 yards and scored 3 rushing touchdowns.

J.J. Guereca, football, Lindsay: Guereca caught 3 passes for 125 yards and 2 touchdowns as the Cardinals won the Bell Game.

Nash McElree, football, Redwood: McElree completed 82% of his throws, hitting 19-of-23 passes for 251 yards and 2 touchdowns to lead the Rangers to a win in the annual Cowhide rivalry game.

Landan Seechan, football, Tulare Western: Seechan scored a receiving touchdown to help the Mustangs win the Bell Game for the first time since 2019.

Nominations are based on information available to the Times-Delta.

All high school varsity players from Tulare County can be on the ballot. Nominations must be received by Monday morning following that week's games for consideration.

Anyone can nominate an athlete of the week for every sport. Nominate next week's ballot through email. Readers can email nominations to sports reporter Vongni Yang at vyang2@visaliatimesdelta.com. Please include a player's statistics and a photo if possible.
