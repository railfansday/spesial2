22 Must-See Photos From Travis Kelce’s Chiefs Career

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Travis Kelce was a third-round pick out of Cincinnati in 2013, the 63rd player taken. Fourteen seasons later, he has played in five Super Bowls, won three of them and caught more playoff passes than anyone in NFL history, including Jerry Rice.

This gallery follows him from a 2014 touchdown at Levi's Stadium, in his first full season, to a score against the Arizona Cardinals at Arrowhead on Nov. 22, 2026. Next up for the Chiefs is the Buffalo Bills on Thanksgiving.

Here are the best photos of Travis Kelce through the years.

A second-year Kelce celebrates a first-quarter touchdown against the 49ers at Levi's Stadium on Oct. 5, 2014. The stadium was brand new that fall. Kelce played one game as a rookie and had microfracture surgery on his knee, so this was really his first full season.

Kelce celebrates a first-half touchdown against the Cleveland Browns at Arrowhead on Dec. 27, 2015. Kansas City started that season 1-5. Then it won its last 10 games.

Kelce leaves the NRG Stadium field after the Chiefs blank the Houston Texans 30-0 in the AFC Wild Card game on Jan. 9, 2016. Knile Davis returned the opening kickoff for a touchdown. It was the franchise's first playoff win since January 1994.

Kelce celebrates a first-half touchdown against the Oakland Raiders at Arrowhead on Dec. 8, 2016. Tyreek Hill added a punt return touchdown that night. Kansas City won 21-13.

Kelce warms up before the AFC Divisional playoff game against Pittsburgh at Arrowhead on Jan. 15, 2017. An ice storm pushed kickoff to the evening. Chris Boswell kicked six field goals, and the Steelers won 18-16.

Kelce hauls in a deep catch over the middle in the first quarter of a preseason game at Paul Brown Stadium on Aug. 19, 2017. Cincinnati is familiar ground. He played his college football at the University of Cincinnati.

Kelce celebrates with teammates after a second-half touchdown against the Dallas Cowboys at AT&T Stadium on Nov. 5, 2017. The score kept it close. Dallas still won 28-17.

Kelce celebrates during the AFC Divisional playoff game against the Indianapolis Colts at Arrowhead on Jan. 12, 2019. Snow fell for much of the night. The Chiefs won 31-13 for their first home playoff win since January 1994.

Kelce warms up at Hard Rock Stadium before Super Bowl LIV on Feb. 2, 2020. Kansas City trailed the 49ers 20-10 in the fourth quarter. Kelce caught a 1-yard touchdown in the comeback, and the Chiefs won 31-20 for their first title in 50 years.

Five years after that Browns touchdown, Kelce runs against Cleveland in the AFC Divisional Round at Arrowhead on Jan. 17, 2021. Patrick Mahomes left the game with a concussion. Backup Chad Henne finished it, and the Chiefs held on 22-17.

Kelce gestures to fans before the Chiefs host the Denver Broncos on Dec. 5, 2021. It was a Sunday night game. The Chiefs won 22-9.

Kelce celebrates a first-quarter touchdown catch against the Bengals on Jan. 2, 2022, back at Paul Brown Stadium, where he made that 2017 preseason grab. Ja'Marr Chase had 266 receiving yards that day. Evan McPherson's field goal at the gun won it for Cincinnati, 34-31.

Kelce dives for extra yards after a catch in overtime against the Tennessee Titans at Arrowhead on Nov. 6, 2022. The Chiefs needed the extra period on a Sunday night. They won 20-17.

Mahomes celebrates with Kelce after the tight end's first-quarter touchdown in Super Bowl LVII at State Farm Stadium on Feb. 12, 2023. Travis and Jason Kelce were the first brothers to play against each other in a Super Bowl. Harrison Butker's late field goal gave Kansas City a 38-35 win over the Eagles.

In his first game back from a knee injury that kept him out of the opener against Detroit, Kelce scores a third-quarter touchdown at EverBank Stadium on Sept. 17, 2023. Kansas City beat the Jaguars 17-9.

Kelce reacts to a second-half touchdown in Super Bowl LVIII at Allegiant Stadium on Feb. 11, 2024. The 49ers and Chiefs went to overtime, where Mahomes hit Mecole Hardman for the 25-22 winner. No team had repeated as champion since the Patriots won after the 2003 and 2004 seasons.

Kelce celebrates a first-half score against Denver at Arrowhead on Nov. 10, 2024. Leo Chenal blocked Wil Lutz's field goal on the last play, which saved a 16-14 win and kept Kansas City unbeaten at 9-0.

At Super Bowl LIX Opening Night in the Caesars Superdome on Feb. 3, 2025, Kelce waves to the crowd. Kansas City was trying to win a third straight title. Philadelphia beat the Chiefs 40-22 six days later.

Kelce gets his helmet on during the first half in Buffalo on Nov. 2, 2025, a few months after he and Taylor Swift announced their engagement. That season was the Bills' last at Highmark Stadium, and their new stadium across the street opened in 2026.

Kelce celebrates a first-quarter touchdown against the Indianapolis Colts at Arrowhead on Sept. 20, 2026. The Chiefs drafted him in the third round in 2013. Seven years after that snowy playoff win, the Colts are still seeing him in the end zone.

Kelce beats Arizona linebacker Mack Wilson Sr. and cornerback Kalen King for a first-quarter touchdown on Nov. 22, 2026. Wilson spent his first four NFL seasons with the Browns, the team Kelce scored on back in 2015.

Kelce at GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium after the Chiefs beat the Cardinals on Nov. 22, 2026. He is 12 years removed from that touchdown in Santa Clara. Arrowhead has been home the whole way.
