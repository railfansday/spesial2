Rams release special teams returner Alex Bachman, who will be next up?

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Los Angeles Rams released Alex Bachman as they continue to try and figure out their special teams. They released Xavier Smith earlier this year. They're currently using Brandon McManus for field goals and Joshua Karty for kickoffs. The Rams are trying to adjust their special teams.

Are their changes working? Not yet because they keep making changes. So far, McManus has been perfect, but will he deliver in the clutch? How long will Karty be used for kickoffs? Who will be the next return man for L.A.? Will the Rams go with someone who will just catch or someone with upside on the return?

So many questions for the special teams unit, but as of now, Bachman won't be one of them, and the Rams are still trying to find answers.

"The Los Angeles Rams have made another change on special teams as they head into Week 9. On Tuesday, the Rams released wide receiver and kickoff/punt returner Alex Bachman. Over the past several weeks, the Rams have been working to make improvements to their special teams unit, and Bachman being released is the latest indication of that.

Earlier this season, the Rams turned to Bachman after they released wide receiver Xavier Smith from the same role. While Bachman fielded punts without issue, he didn't offer much as a returner. Among players with at least five punt returns this season, Bachman averaged just 5.2 yards per return, which ranked 31st. At some point, the Rams needed to raise the bar from 'just fielding punts'. Bachman averaged just 22.9 yards per kick return, ranking 62nd out of 75 qualifying returners."

"So allow me to throw a name out there. New England Patriots defensive tackle Christian Barmore makes a lot of sense as a potential addition for the Rams. Los Angeles lost defensive tackle Puna Ford for the rest of the season. Barmore could certainly help the Rams replace some of what they lost in the middle of their defense – and he's reportedly available.

Why Christian Barmore makes sense for the Los Angeles Rams

Barmore is a disruptive presence up front, and he brings plenty of size to the position. We've seen the kind of impact a disruptive defensive interior group is capable of with Seattle's deep playoff run last year. The Rams have Aaron Donald, Braden Fiske, and Kobie Turner (injured reserve) – but Barmore is built a little differently. At 6-foot-5, 315 pounds, Barmore still has disruptive twitch in his game to complement to others on the Rams' front."
