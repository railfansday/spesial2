Real Madrid defender closes in on return after months out injured

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Injuries and physical problems have been among the main factors that have kept Real Madrid from achieving their objectives during the last two seasons. During both these seasons, the Merengues saw a very high number of injuries throughout the season, which kept the team from performing at its best level.

Things seem to have improved significantly this season. There have been no long-term injuries suffered by any of their players so far. However, some players who had already been injured before the start of this season are still continuing with their recoveries.

One such player, Eder Militao, has already fully recovered, and recently returned to the field. Now, another injured player can follow in his footsteps, as Diario AS reports that the fullback Ferland Mendy can return to the squad for the upcoming game against Celta Vigo.

The Frenchman suffered his last injury back in May, during a match against Espanyol. He had to undergo surgery after injuring his rectus femoris tendon of the right leg. This, along with other muscular and ankle problems suffered by the defender, meant that he was able to play only nine games during the last season.

After spending a painful few months on the sidelines, the defender's hard work started paying off in early October, when he returned to train at Valdebebas alone. He then joined his teammates in training six days later. Since then, he has accumulated around a month of preparation to get back to his best level, and can get the medical green light to play in the upcoming game against Celta Vigo.

Hopefully, with this recovery, Ferland Mendy would have left behind the injuries that have troubled him so much in recent years. Jose Mourinho already has Marc Cucurella and Alvaro Carreras to play on the left-back position, so would not be taking any risks if the Frenchman is not at his best level when Real Madrid take on Celta this weekend.
