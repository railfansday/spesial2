Dylan Larkin’s agent breaks silence on Canadiens-Red Wings trade

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Dylan Larkin's agent breaks silence on Canadiens-Red Wings trade appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Montreal Canadiens traded for Dylan Larkin in a stunning deal with the Detroit Red Wings on Sunday night. Montreal was not publicly known as a team that Larkin had interest in. The former Detroit captain has, obviously, had a change of heart.

However, it may not be as big a change of heart as previously thought. Reports surfaced after the trade that Larkin had always seen the Canadiens as a plausible destination. On Sunday, agent Pat Brisson confirmed as much in his first statement after the deal.

"Right on or about mid September when Detroit made Shawn Horcoff the interim GM so teams had someone official to speak to in Detroit, Montreal became an interesting option," Brisson told David Pagnotta of The Fourth Period.

The Canadiens parted ways with three players and four draft picks in this deal. Montreal sent prospect Michael Hage, forward Kirby Dach, goalie Sam Montembeault, two first-round picks, a second-round pick, and a fourth-round pick to the Red Wings for Larkin.

The former Red Wings captain has not played since October 26 against the Edmonton Oilers. In 10 games this season, he scored two goals and six points for Detroit. He went home on October 29, refusing to play another game for Detroit.

Fans of both teams are already circling February 18, 2027 on their calendars. The Canadiens will travel to Detroit that night to take on the Red Wings in Larkin's first game back since the trade. No matter what happens, that game will surely be a must-watch event for fans across the sport.
