Lena Dunham Shares Rare Glimpse of Baby Girl on Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Lena Dunham has a lot to be grateful for this holiday season — especially her baby girl.

Dunham, 40, celebrated her first Thanksgiving as a family of three on Thursday, November 26, sharing a rare glimpse at her and husband Luis Felber’s daughter.

“Thankful,” she captioned the series of Instagram photos, which included a side view of her baby being pushed in a stroller.

A second picture revealed the top of the little one’s head as Dunham went on a walk with Felber, 40, and their dogs ahead of the holiday.

The Girls creator also showed off her Thanksgiving outfit with a mirror selfie. Dunham wore a striped shirt, brown pants, a striped scarf and forest green coat with a fur collar. She had her hair up in a big clip and rocked black sunglasses.

Dunham and Felber tied the knot in September 2021.

The actress struggled with fertility issues amid her battle with endometriosis — and underwent a hysterectomy in 2017 — before she announced in September that the couple welcomed their first baby via surrogate.

Dunham recalled her first words to her baby girl in an essay for Vogue, writing, “Hello. I’m your mama. You look nothing like I thought you would, but exactly right. We’ve been waiting for you, do you know that? It was so nice of you to come.”

She continued, “I meant it. Like a kid who wasn’t sure if their schoolmates would arrive for the birthday party, I was bashful and dizzy with joy.”

Later that month, Dunham praised her husband as they celebrated five years of marriage and their first anniversary as parents.

“Sometimes you get set up on a blind date with a friend’s friend’s friend and you’re like ‘Hey, I know I’ve proven myself an unreliable narrator of love, but I think I’m gonna marry him,’” Dunham wrote via Instagram on September 24. “Everyone who has witnessed your meandering path is kinda like ‘OK, at it again, are we psycho?’ (in a nicer way.) But then you do.”

The Famesick author added, “And he turns out to be a wonderful husband, a beautiful father and an incredibly skilled curator of hair accessories. Don’t quit before the miracle.”

One month later, Dunham had another thing to celebrate: the return of Girls.

She announced in October that the cast of the HBO series, which initially ran from 2012 to 2017, would be reuniting for another season comprising five new episodes.

“We always said we would only return to Girls if there was a specific story we were burning to tell,” Dunham said in a statement on October 13. “I’ve missed these characters, but for a long time I couldn’t quite picture them in the present day. Then, right around my fortieth birthday, they started talking to me in the shower. And, as you may remember, once this fivesome starts talking, it’s pretty hard to get them to stop …”

She added, “It’s rare that you get the chance to return to something that changed your life with the gift of hindsight and a whole new level of gratitude and understanding. I love these girls (and boy), our friends at HBO and all the people who have continued to discover the show and remind us afresh why we do this, which is the joy of being together … and the chance to wear yellow mesh …”

Dunham is set to return as Hannah Horvath alongside castmates Allison Williams as Marnie Michaels, Zosia Mamet as Shoshanna Shapiro and Jemima Kirke as Jessa Johansson. Andrew Rannells will also be back in his supporting role as Elijah Krantz.
