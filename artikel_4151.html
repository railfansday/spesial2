Warframe: Tau sets the date for release and reveals even more in new devstrean

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Warframe: Tau, the newest upcoming expansion to the hit hack-and-slash action game Warframe, is set to be its biggest since Warframe: 1999. Coming packed with new social hubs, a new warframe and more, developer Digital Extremes have revealed even more in their latest devstream, including a December 9th release date.

Of course, for many of you, the headlining addition being showcased is the newest warframe joining the game's already expansive roster. Kullervo Prime boasts high-damage, high-risk manoeuvres and arrives alongside the Rauta Prime heavy shotgun and Victrica Prime heavy blade, two great new weapons to add to your arsenal.

You'll also be able to jump into a new side story that allows you to uncover the criminal conspiracy running throughout Fornax. Digital Extremes promises some exciting story reveals, not to mention plenty of rewards to earn as you play through the story.

With Warframe up for a potential Game Award for best community support, and its release on mobile for Android earlier this year only strengthening the argument for Digital Extremes being an award-winner, there's a lot to look forward to for fans of the Tenno in December!
