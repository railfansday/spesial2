Grammys 2027: The Nominations Are Finally Here

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Grammy nominations are here, announced live on Nov. 16, with Olivia Rodrigo, Ella Langley, Raye, Bruno Mars and Olivia Dean expected to be big contenders.

It’s time for Grammy hopefuls to face the music.

The nominations for music’s biggest night are officially here, coming just three months before the Grammy Awards take place live from LA’s Cyrpto.com Arena on Feb. 1.

Ahead of the nominations being announced on Nov. 16, there were a few frontrunners expected truly strike a chord.

Among them were two Olivias, one by the name of Dean and one Rodrigo. After all, Olivia Rodrigo’s You Seem Pretty Sad for a Girl So in Love and Olivia Dean’s The Art of Loving albums both struck a chord among fans this year.

Other albums that were played by fans until they became a broken record were Noah Kahan’s The Great Divide, Rosalía’s Lux and Ella Langley’s Dandelion, while Taylor Swift’s The Life of a Showgirl and its Encore tracks brought fans to their feet.

As for songs that defined the year, it’s impossible not to mention Langley’s “Choosin’ Texas.” Not only did the country track prove to be an earworm, but it also achieved what seemed to be impossible: It unseated Mariah Carey’s “All I Want for Christmas” from the top spot on the Billboard Hot 100 List.

While the holiday classic had long held the record at 22 weeks at no. 1, Langley’s heartfelt track reached 23 weeks in September.

As for other songs that refused to play second fiddle, Rodrigo’s “The Cure,” Dean’s “Man I Need,” Bruno Mars’ “Risk it All” and Raye’s “WHERE IS MY HUSBAND!” all proved to be fan-favorites, too.

Plus, with Phoebe Bridgers releasing Lost Weekend—her first album in six years—and newer artists such as Geese and Audrey Hobart bursting onto the scene in a huge way, the 2027 Grammy Awards are sure to be a can’t-miss event.

For all the snubs and surprises from the 2027 Grammy nominations, keep reading.

Abrams failed to snag any nominations for her latest album Daughter From Hell, which she submitted for Album of the Year and Best Pop Vocal Album. Her single "Hit the Wall" was also absent from the Song of the Year, Record of the Year and Best Pop Solo Performance lists.

Abrams has previously received two nominations for Best New Artist in 2024 and Best Pop Duo Performance for "Us" which featured Taylor Swift in 2025.

While Grande picked up some surprise nominations for Best Traditional Pop Vocal Performance for "For Good" alongside Cynthia Erivo, as well as Song of the Year and Best Music Video for "hate that I made you love me," and Best Pop Vocal Album for Petal, she was snubbed for Album of the Year.

Although Kahan scored one nomination for Best Contemporary Folk Album, he was not included in the major categories like Album of the Year and Song of the Year for his hit The Great Divide.

Beyoncé is famously the most decorated Grammy-winning artist—the 35-time winner scored her 100th nomination this year— but her Best R&B Solo Performance nod for "Morning Dew (Donk)" was somewhat of a surprise considering its delayed release in celebration of B'Day's 20th anniversary in July.

This year was big for Hobert, who released her debut album Who's the Clown last year, but she failed to score a nod for Best New Artist. (Her brother Malcolm Todd was also left off the shortlist.)

That's actually not all for this much-anticipated sequel. The Devil Wears Prada 2 scored a surprise nomination for Best Compilation Soundtrack For Visual Media alongside Stranger Things season five, Wicked: For Good, The Wizard of Oz at the Sphere and Wuthering Heights.

It's been a long time coming for the first-time Grammy nominee, who scored her first nods for Best Dance/Electronic Recording for "Melatonin" as well as Best Dance Pop Recording for "Too Easy."

Swift wasn't totally snubbed by the Recording Academy—she earned nods for Record of the Year, Album of the Year, Song of the Year, Best Pop Duo Performance alongside Sabrina Carpenter, Best Song Written for a Visual Media and more. But The Life of a Showgirl was not among Best Pop Vocal Album contenders, while "I Knew It, I Knew You" was snubbed for Best Country Song and Best Country Solo Performance.

Still, it was a surprise to see "I Knew It, I Knew You" score a nomination for Song of the Year, a category introduced by Swift herself during the nomination livestream, given Swift's other hits from The Life of a Showgirl.

The hype hasn't died down, Wicked is still oh so very popular. Wicked: For Good scored two nominations including Best Song Written For Visual Media and Best Traditional Pop Vocal Performance.

Disco All The Time, Grammy Occasionally. The "Aperture" singer failed to score recognition for his latest album in any category. Styles has three Grammy wins under his belt, including Album of the Year for Harry's House in 2023.

Her comeback is far from over. The pop star received her first Grammy nominations in 16 years, racking up nods for Best Dance Pop Recording, Best Dance/Electronic Recording, Best Dance/Electronic Album and Best Pop Duo/Group Performance for "Bring Your Love" alongside Carpenter.

Although Lefty rose to massive acclaim this year for hits like "Boston," she failed to receive any recognition by The Recording Academy.

Following a two-year hiatus, West scored a nod for Best Rap Album for Bully.
