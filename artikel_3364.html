33 Years Later, One of the Greatest Westerns of All Time Is Officially Taking Over Streaming

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Many movies are classics from the start, with talented actors and directors working from a perfect script to make Hollywood magic. Some films, however, fight their way to greatness, and few battled quite as hard as those working on the 1993 Western Tombstone. The story of “Wyatt Earp and his immortals” faced a competing biopic, production troubles, and a change in director before it made it to theater screens. But, far from a doomed endeavor, it became a cult classic that is once again climbing streaming rental charts today, and is considered one of the greatest Westerns ever made — and that came from the cast’s determination not to let on-set issues destroy their vision.

The film stars Kurt Russell as Wyatt Earp, the legendary lawman who arrives at the prospecting town of Tombstone in 1879 looking to retire and make his fortune with his brothers, and to reconnect with one of its residents, Doc Holliday (Val Kilmer). His fame precedes him, with many of the townspeople asking him to help with the growing problem of a group of outlaws called the Cowboys, noted for their red sashes and brutal robberies. At first, Wyatt resists the call in order to live the life of a civilian. However, when the Cowboys’ exploits start to spill the blood of those close to him, he heads toward a confrontation that would make history.

Based on real events, the movie features an epic cast that includes Bill Paxton, Sam Elliott, and a cameo from Hollywood legend Charlton Heston. It also faced stiff opposition from none other than Kevin Costner, who made his own film based on Wyatt Earp after originally being attached to Tombstone, and who also reportedly made it difficult for the rival movie to find distribution. That would be the least of the movie’s problems, however, as issues began to pile up just a few weeks into the shoot.

While the finished movie was a success, its road to the big screen was far from simple. The production lost its original director, Kevin Jarre, a month into filming, reportedly due to his being overwhelmed by the schedule. His replacement was George P. Cosmatos, although years later Russell would state that he “ghost directed” the film, giving shot lists and instructions for Cosmatos to execute. The delays and shifting schedules led to frustration among the cast, who wondered if the film would complete production.

Russell persisted, however, and led the stranded production to greatness. While testimonies differ as to whether he truly directed the film, all agree that the star of the movie was the creative force behind its success. “I watched Kurt sacrifice his own role and energy to devote himself as a storyteller,” the late Val Kilmer wrote in 2017, “even going so far as to draw up shot lists to help our replacement director, George Cosmatos, who came in with only two days’ prep.” The finished product is as polished as a movie with twice the resources, featuring an unforgettable shootout at the O.K. Corral which is a masterpiece of tension. There’s also the small matter of Kilmer’s Doc, which is to this day one of the great supporting performances of all time.

"You called down the thunder, well, now you've got it!"

In the same post where he wrote about Russell, Kilmer revealed he briefly lived with Russell during the shoot, so that they would have extra time each day to rewrite the script and hammer out the schedule. “I got all the best lines, and he knew it and still laughed and joked every single day,” he wrote, and that statement has some merit to it. Holliday does indeed have most of the best lines, including the famous response “I’m your Huckleberry,” but the performance was more than one-liners. Around the edges of the main plot, Doc’s journey from ailing scoundrel to selfless hero becomes the highlight of the movie, as if the lead character from another film had wandered in and stolen the show.

Kilmer was at the peak of his stardom in 1993, having become a household name thanks to hits like Top Gun, Willow, and The Doors in the preceding years. He would go on to play the lead in Batman Forever just two years later, but Doc shows what a talented actor he could be even with a supporting character. The idiosyncratic Southern drawl, the dry wit, and his unspoken respect for the stoic, principled Earp make him one of the Western genre’s great characters, and Kilmer one of its most impressive performers.

After overcoming numerous obstacles that would have been fatal for any other production, Tombstone emerged as one of the best Westerns of the 1990s, out-grossing Costner’s Wyatt Earp as well as being the most popular movie culturally. It stands as a testament to the passion, commitment, and love of a cast that, like Earp himself, would never quit on what they believed in.
