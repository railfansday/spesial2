Jon Bernthal’s 132-Minute Crime Thriller 'The Accountant 2' Is the Perfect Watch Before It’s Cut From Hulu

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

It can’t always be award-winning dramas and true-crime documentaries when you’re watching movies at home. Sometimes, you want an entertaining, bullet-riddled rollercoaster, and there are few that fit the bill better than 2025’s The Accountant 2. In the long-awaited sequel to the 2016 original, Ben Affleck returns as Christian Wolff, a neurodivergent accountant and trained killer who comes out of hiding to solve a case involving the death of an old friend, which forces him to reunite with his estranged brother, played by Jon Bernthal. The pair make for a unique buddy movie pairing, filled with action and withering put-downs that made the movie a hit with audiences. With the film leaving Hulu on December 1, your last chance to watch it streaming is November 30.

When Ray King (J.K. Simmons), Christian’s ally and government contact, is murdered and found with the cryptic message “find the accountant” on his arm, treasury agent Marybeth Medina (Cynthia Addai-Robinson) seeks out Christian for help. Keen to avenge his friend, he links the killings to a large conspiracy involving the disappearance of a family and dangerous criminals. Realizing he can’t do this alone, he contacts his estranged brother Brax (Bernthal) to help him. With a difficult past between them, the siblings reluctantly come together to piece together the clues and find justice.

Part mystery thriller, part buddy cop movie, the film features the two stars taking on a variety of bad guys as well as solving numerous puzzles to find their target. While undoubtedly a traditional action movie, there’s also a fair amount of laughs to be had as the Wolff boys slowly begin to trust each other again.

Much of the fun of this sequel is in watching the brothers try to co-exist, and seeing their seemingly opposite personalities lead to some hilarious clashes. Christian is methodical, controlled, and able to point out the smallest details, despite having difficulties with social interaction. By contrast, Brax is emotional, abrasive, and constantly seeking connection. He bellows where Christian mutters, meaning that as partners and family members, it can be difficult to find common ground.

While a lazier movie might have positioned Christian’s neurodivergence as making him the outsider, the enjoyment comes from neither brother being the wildcard. Both characters’ personalities have their strengths and weaknesses, and as the story progresses, there’s a sense of satisfaction in the pair finding out that they may have more in common than they realize. It’s in these interactions where Bernthal becomes the movie’s secret weapon. He has a talent for comedy that might not immediately be obvious given his numerous action-heavy roles, but he's adept at highlighting the ridiculousness of his situation, able to use himself for laughs as well as his co-stars.

The first movie was about uncovering who Christian is, given he has built a life where his footprints are covered and his identity protected. However, The Accountant 2 doesn’t have to do the same amount of digging, making this a movie that can almost immediately dive into the action. With the audience being aware of what Christian is capable of, there’s a shorthand that allows the plot to move forward in a swifter way, and gives him a literal brother in arms.

With a growing number of villains on their tail, the action scenes are more frequent, and evolve as the brothers’ relationship becomes stronger. Christian treats all things in life like an equation, with physical altercations being no different. On the other hand, Brax operates on instinct, able to react and change course depending on what the situation calls for. These two approaches slowly begin to complement each other, leading to a terrific finale where they penetrate a heavily armed compound, outnumbered but never outsmarted.

The Accountant 2 moves the formula of the first movie forward in a way that will satisfy fans who want to see more of what they liked from before, but with the volume turned up. WriterBill Dubuque revealed this year that the film’s central pairing was so successful, that there is interest in a third movie focusing on a road trip between Christian and Brax. If that sounds like something you’d be excited about, make sure you catch their latest adventure before it leaves your Hulu menu.
