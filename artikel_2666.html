Quinyon Mitchell has no injury designation for Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Eagles will have cornerback Quinyon Mitchell in the lineup for Thursday’s game in Dallas.

Mitchell did not receive an injury designation for the Thanksgiving game. He was listed as out of practice on Monday due to a hamstring injury, but moved up to limited participation Tuesday and was a full participant on Friday.

Running back Saquon Barkley, defensive tackle Jalen Carter, tight end Dallas Goedert, and wide receiver DeVonta Smith are also without designations after appearing on the injury report this week.

Linebacker Arnold Ebiketie (oblique) and cornerback Jonathan Jones (ankle) are the only players who did receive designations. Both of them have been ruled out for the Eagles this week.
