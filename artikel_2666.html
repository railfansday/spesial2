MSSU women's cross-country qualifies for NCAA championships

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Missouri Southern State University's women's cross-country team secured a spot at the NCAA Division II Cross-Country Championships with a third-place finish at the NCAA Central Regional Championships Saturday at Yankton Trails Park in South Dakota.

The women's team totaled 120 points, with six of their seven runners recording personal-best performances.

The men's team placed fifth overall with 163 points, highlighted by five personal records.

Ruth Chemutai led the women's team with an individual third-place finish, earning All-Region honors. Viola Johanson also earned All-Region recognition with a 14th-place finish. Both runners recorded personal bests, along with teammates Kyla Weems (25th), Lilah Genel (29th), Isabella Frost (50th), Abbey Brackenbury (59th) and Hailey Nelson (78th).

On the men's side, Kelton Sorrell and Chase Sorrell led the Lions with eighth- and ninth-place finishes, respectively, earning All-Region honors. Both recorded personal bests. Kelby Conner (28th), Caleb Leach (57th) and Michael Kershaw (61st) also established personal records, while Brandon Hammett finished 68th to complete the team lineup.

The national championships are scheduled for Friday, Dec. 4, at Rim Rock Farm in Lawrence, Kansas.
