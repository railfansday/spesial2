Aaron Glenn could become sixth coach to start 0-9 in division

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Jets coach Aaron Glenn has a 5-21 record during his one-and-a-half-year tenure with the team. That’s bad.

Here’s something worse: Glenn is 0-8 in AFC East games.

That’s the longest active losing streak in the division by any team. And it’s one game short of putting Glenn in a small club that no one wants to belong to.

A loss on Sunday to the Bills would make Glenn the sixth head coach since the 1970 merger to start his career 0-9 in division games. The others were Jim Schwartz (0-10), Matt Eberflus (0-9), Pat Shurmur (0-9), John McKay (0-9), and Jim Ringo (0-9).

With each additional loss, the question becomes whether it will be Glenn’s final loss. The clock seems to be ticking toward an in-season termination. Or, if owner Woody Johnson doesn’t decide to make a move before Week 18, an abrupt parting of ways after the season ends.
