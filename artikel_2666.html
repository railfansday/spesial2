Celtics Have Unlocked a New Version of Paul George, and These Stats Prove It

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

There's a reason Paul George has looked so comfortable in Boston.

In the latest edition of his power rankings, NBA.com's John Schuhmann spotlighted two statistics that help explain how well the Celtics star has fit in.

"Paul George scored a game-high 36 points against his former team, shooting 6-for-11 from beyond the arc, with a few of those being wide-open looks," Schuhmann wrote. "He's been assisted on 68% of his field goals, easily the highest rate of his career. His shot quality has been his second-highest mark in the 14 seasons of tracking data."

George has been one of the biggest reasons for Boston's impressive start.

Through 11 games, George is averaging 20.6 points, 5.4 rebounds, 4.0 assists and 2.1 steals while shooting 47.4 percent from the field and 43.2 percent from 3-point range. He already has two 30-point games, including his record-setting debut.

Joe Mazzulla's system has certainly helped create quality looks, but George deserves plenty of credit for buying into his new role. He's made it clear where he stands in the pecking order, and he's wasted little time establishing chemistry with Jayson Tatum.

If George maintains this level of production, he'll have a strong case to earn his 10th career All-Star selection. More importantly, he's giving the Celtics another high-level scoring option without needing the ball in his hands.

So far, it's been a win-win for both sides.
