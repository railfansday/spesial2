49ers’ Kyle Shanahan reveals ‘unc’ package for Keenan Allen, Mike Evans

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The San Francisco 49ers have a nickname for the two veteran wide receivers leading their receiving room. According to head coach Kyle Shanahan, he used to call the combination of Mike Evans and Keenan Allen the "unc package."

The name came up during the team's 35-10 win over the Minnesota Vikings, when NBC commentator Mike Tirico used the term on Sunday's broadcast. Shanahan recently explained its meaning when speaking to reporters Wednesday.

"I don't really have to because they're out there for the most part anyways, just being starting receivers and everything," Shanahan said. "But we mix in so many guys, and I know everybody's name, but when I'm thinking about football, I don't always just spit out names fast, so sometimes it's just easier for me to say unc. Everyone knows who I'm talking about."

Shanahan said he didn't come up with the nickname, describing it as something that came naturally. He also confirmed that the package specifically refers to Evans and Allen.

"I hope they like it," Shanahan added. "I think it's cool. They're not like really uncles. They're in their 30s, they're still young."

The veteran duo has already made an impact in San Francisco's offense. Evans caught six passes for 109 yards and a touchdown against Minnesota, while Allen finished with three receptions for 38 yards.

The two receivers also worked together to create opportunities during the game. On Evans' touchdown late in the second quarter, Allen ran a zig route that helped draw defenders away, allowing Evans to win his matchup and make a one-handed catch in the back corner of the end zone.

They also helped the 49ers move the ball during a 98-yard touchdown drive in the third quarter. Evans caught an 18-yard pass after Allen helped create space against Minnesota's blitz.
