Giannis Antetokounmpo teases ‘another level’ for Heat after loss to 76ers

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

With the Miami Heat turning in their first loss after the seven-game winning streak, dropping to the Philadelphia 76ers on Wednesday, 111-104, one has to wonder how much more the team has to develop this season to reach their highest level. As Giannis Antetokounmpo returned for the Heat after missing one game with a groin injury, he would tease that the best is yet to come for the team.

Antetokounmpo had another standout game, recording 32 points, 14 rebounds, four assists, two steals, and a block as he spoke about how the defense could have been better, and when it does, the offense follows along with it. With that equation, Antetokounmpo believes "there's another level" to Miami.

"Didn't do a good enough job defending. You know, it's easier for teams when they get in the paint to make plays for themselves and for their teammate. And we didn't score the ball. Scored 104," Antetokounmpo said, via The Heat Realm. "We just got to do a better job defending. Got to do a better job scoring the ball…there's another level to us. We've accessed that level. We've seen how that looks…we just gotta do that on the road."

The Heat's offense entered Wednesday's game top of the league in a few categories in points per game (120.7) and half-court offense, though it wasn't on full display against the 76ers.

However, the defensive side of the ball is the proverbial first domino piece for everything to fall into place for Miami, as during the seven-game winning streak, the team shot up from towards the bottom of the league to the middle of the pack in defensive rating.

As Miami looks to climb the defensive rankings, captain Bam Adebayo would say that the communication is what went wrong on that side of the ball.

"We weren't vocal as we usually are, and we need to be better. Being vocal and communicating on defense changes the game," Adebayo said after recording 17 points and 11 rebounds.

The Heat looks to bounce back against the New York Knicks on Friday night.
