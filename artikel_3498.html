GTA 6's Subscription-based Perks are Worrying Players

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The subscription offers an exclusive vehicle, garage, discounts, and other perks in Leonida

Grand Theft Auto 6 has finally arrived to critical acclaim. Millions of players purchased their copies a while ago, and preorders came with a free month of GTA Plus, Rockstar's monthly subscription service. It now offers exclusive benefits in GTA 6's single-player campaign, including a new car, a garage, discounts, and other gameplay bonuses.

Previously focused primarily on GTA Online, GTA Plus is now expanding into Rockstar's latest open-world adventure. The subscription costs $7.99 per month in the United States, and its GTA 6 benefits are only available while players maintain an active membership.

These benefits are tied to an active GTA Plus membership. Canceling the subscription means losing access to the exclusive car and garage, along with the other membership perks. Players who would rather experience GTA 6 without the subscription bonuses can also disable them. Simply open the pause menu, head to the Account tab, find GTA Plus Boosts, and toggle them off.

Not everyone is happy about Rockstar bringing its subscription service into the single-player experience. A Reddit post highlighting the new GTA Plus benefits has attracted thousands of upvotes, with many players criticizing the decision to lock additional content behind a monthly fee.

Some of the most popular comments take issue with the exclusive car and garage, arguing that these features should be included in the base game rather than requiring another subscription. Others have pointed out that even players who purchased more expensive editions of GTA 6 still have to maintain GTA Plus to access the benefits.

Others worry that introducing subscription-exclusive content into the single-player campaign could signal what's coming when GTA Online eventually arrives in GTA 6. While the current benefits are largely optional, the decision has already prompted questions about how much additional content Rockstar might eventually lock behind GTA Plus.
