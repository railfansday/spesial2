New Research, Reports, and Events Address What's Next for Housing

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Cutting-edge resources from Green Builder Media address the most pressing housing issues.

LAKE CITY, Colo., Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Green Builder Media has rolled out new resources to help housing professionals understand the changing market and adapt to the future.

On Dec. 1 at 2 p.m. ET, Green Builder Media CEO Sara Gutterman will share new State of the Industry research in a free webinar. The discussion will explore business pressures, evolving consumer expectations, rising interest in resilience, and the shift from a home's first cost to the full cost of ownership. Click to register.

The conversation continues in person at the Value Per Square Foot Summit: Redefining What Housing Is Worth, Monday, Feb. 1, 2027, from 1 to 5:30 p.m. PT at the Renaissance Hotel in Las Vegas, the day before the IBS/KBIS show floor opens. Leaders from across the housing industry will explore how performance, resilience, health, sustainability, and long-term affordability can reshape how homes are designed, financed, and valued. Click to attend.

Green Builder Media's new report, "Elevated Climate Risk Drastically Changes Homeowner Perceptions on Insurance," reveals how insurance costs and climate risk affect home decisions. Nearly 90% of surveyed homeowners said their insurance costs rose in the past year, and more than half said insurers had left their market. Meanwhile, 59.2% said home hardening reduced damage to their homes. Download it.

Green Builder Media and the National Building Museum invite the public to share what they value in a community and what they hope it becomes. Take the short survey on place, belonging and having a voice in local change and get access to the survey results in a future report.

The latest issue of Green Builder explores what buyers want in 2027, ways to ease the monthly payment squeeze, and how to build homes that can take a hit. VISION House projects in Asheville and Los Angeles show how better design can make homes healthier, more comfortable, and less expensive to live in. Download it.

About Green Builder MediaGreen Builder Media is a North American media company focused on sustainable building. Through content, events, demonstration projects and COGNITION Smart Data, it provides market intelligence to help the housing industry innovate.
