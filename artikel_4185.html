New EU Bureaucracy Index: EU law has more than doubled since 2010

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The new EU Bureaucracy Index measures the volume of EU law in force, and it shows a record high: At the end of 2025, the EU regulations and directives in force comprised around 47,600 standard pages, 118% more than in 2010. The volume of EU law has thus more than doubled in 15 years, and it continued to grow in 2025. The main drivers are sanctions regulations and new sets of rules in environmental, consumer and health law.
