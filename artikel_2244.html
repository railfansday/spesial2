Köln striker Reigan Heskey scores in England U19's five-star win against Belarus

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Reigan Heskey is yet to score for his new club Köln, but he netted three goals in the space of three days for England U19. The 18-year-old scored twice when England started their second round qualification campaign with a 4-1 win over Slovenia on Wednesday.

Ben Futcher's side added another victory on Saturday, with Heskey on target once again in a 5-0 win over Belarus. They will conclude this stage on Tuesday with a game against the group hosts Austria.

Emile Heskey's son has only played 55 minutes for the Billy Goats in all competitions since following Jahmai Simpson-Pusey with a summer switch from Manchester City. His only direct goal contribution was an assist in the 5-0 DFB-Pokal win over Union Berlin.

Germany will play their second group stage game later on Saturday against the hosts of their group Portugal. Both sides recorded narrow wins over Sweden and Lithuania in the opening round on Thursday.
