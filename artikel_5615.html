John Summit Brings Experts Only to Big Sky for Two Headline Shows, March 13-14, 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Event marks Montana debut for John Summit and the exclusive Experts Only ski weekender in the U.S. in 2027

BIG SKY, Mont., Nov. 6, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Outlaw Partners is pleased to announce that Experts Only is heading to Big Sky. John Summit Presents Experts Only Big Sky will take over the Big Sky Events Arena for two headline shows on Saturday, March 13 and Sunday, March 14, 2027. It will be John Summit's first time performing in Montana and the only Experts Only ski weekender in the U.S. in 2027.

Presented by Experts Only and produced by Outlaw Partners, the weekend will bring together some of the world's best DJs, live music, skiing, wellness activities, and more, all in one of the most spectacular mountain settings in North America. The Big Sky Events Arena sits in Big Sky's Town Center, with the 11,166-foot Lone Mountain as the backdrop.

"We're honored to partner with John Summit and the Experts Only team to bring this incredible electronic music and ski lifestyle event to Big Sky," said Megan Paulson, Co-Founder and Chief Marketing Officer of Outlaw Partners. "The caliber of John and the Experts Only artists, set against a one-of-a-kind mountain backdrop and the ski lifestyle that defines Big Sky, makes this a weekend fans will not want to miss. It's a once-in-a-lifetime experience, and we can't wait to share it."

"I've always wanted to go to Montana and throw an event there as I've only heard amazing things," said John Summit. "Big Sky has been on our radar for a while so it was a no-brainer to bring Experts Only there. You know we'll go all out as we always do with parties all over the mountain, It's gonna be a fun one!"

Big Sky is an iconic location, and John Summit has become an icon in his craft. Together they promise a landmark weekend. With no mainstream festivals on John Summit's 2027 schedule, the Big Sky stop will be a unique opportunity to see him.

Tickets include GA, VIP and VVIP options, and fans are expected to travel from around the country for the weekend. Demand is expected to be high and tickets are expected to sell out, so fans are encouraged to secure them early for the best access.
