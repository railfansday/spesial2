'I heard from my man at Villa' - Ex- Aston Villa employee provides Emi Buendia update

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Emiliano Buendia's new contract will see him extend his stay at Aston Villa until 2029.

That is according to former senior Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 29-year-old has been in contract negotiations for weeks with the Villans, with his current terms set to expire next summer.

Buendia has become a key player for Villa over the last 18 months, scoring 11 goals and providing nine assists across all competitions last season.

He has not quite been at the same level so far this season, but remains a superb option available for Unai Emery.

Villa will hope to seal the deal on a new contract for Buendia before the January transfer window, in which he will be free to discuss pre-contract agreements with other clubs.

Buendia joined Villa from Norwich City in 2021 for up to around £40million, and had looked set to leave in 2025.

Injury had left the Argentine on the fringes at Villa Park, but Emery was keen to hand him an opportunity and was rewarded when it was grabbed with both hands.

"I've heard from my man at Villa that negotiations took place earlier in September, where they were talking about an extension for a new contract taking him up to 2029," King told Football Insider.

"You're looking at his current deal. He cost a lot of money to sign from Norwich, didn't he? You're not going to allow him to walk out the door.

"He's at a serious age. You've got [John] McGinn at 32, you've got him at 29. In the next three or four years, they're going to have to be replaced anyway.

"But, Buendia, he's a good player. He's done well for Villa. He's done well for Emery. I would be looking at an improved contract to take him up to 2029, which would keep him under contract until he is 32."

Buendia did not make it off the bench as Villa drew 4-4 with Manchester United before the international break.

He will hope to start against Sunderland on Saturday, and could even have a new deal to celebrate.

"I always show how happy I am here. I show, I think, a lot how I love this club," Buendia said in October.

"I try to enjoy every day and then we’ll see what is happening in the future, but obviously pleased and grateful with all the supporters here.”
