Here's Why Anthropic's IPO Was a Historic Success

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The artificial intelligence leader had a momentous first day in the stock market.

By Joe Tenebruso – Nov 19, 2026 at 8:42PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Anthropic ›

Shares of Anthropic (ANT +17.07%) jumped 17% from its initial public offering (IPO) price of $83 on Thursday. The gains capped off a blockbuster debut for the artificial intelligence (AI) pioneer on its first day as a public company.

Anthropic's stock price opened at $111.50 and rose as high as $115.10 following its listing on the Nasdaq stock exchange. The AI model developer's share price closed at $97.17, placing its fully diluted market capitalization at a staggering $1.8 trillion.

Anthropic sold 980 million shares at $83 per share at its IPO, raising a whopping $81 billion in the process. The AI trailblazer intends to use the proceeds to pay for the enormous amount of data centers, semiconductor chips, and other infrastructure it needs to train its cutting-edge models.

The sizable sum places Anthropic ahead of Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX -3.53%) in the all-time largest IPO rankings. SpaceX raised $75 billion in initial base offering proceeds in June.

Trading volume was also off the charts. Roughly 840 million shares of Anthropic were traded on Thursday, compared to about 520 million for SpaceX on its IPO day.

Anthropic's pace of expansion has been breathtaking. Its revenue rose from $41 million in 2023 to $386 million in 2024 and $4.6 billion in 2025. Those are stunning growth rates of 841% in 2024 and 1,088% in 2025. And for the first nine months of 2026, Anthropic's revenue growth accelerated to 1,206%, surpassing $33 billion.

Although management expects the company's expansion to slow in the coming years, Anthropic is likely to remain one of the fastest-growing businesses in the world.
