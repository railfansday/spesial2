Rams on alert as Packers coach reveals Micah Parsons return timeline

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Rumors have speculated that Micah Parsons would make his return to the football field in Week 12, against the Rams.

On Monday, it appears those rumors are starting to become a reality, as Packers coach Matt LaFleur told reporters the plan is to have Parsons play in the Thanksgiving Eve game.

Packers coach Matt LaFleur reveals Micah Parsons could be returning against the Rams. Getty Images

"Well, that's my plan, but I think my plan's been different than everybody else's the whole time," LaFleur said, via Rob Demovsky of ESPN. "There's still a couple more hurdles for him to clear before we can definitively say he's a go. He's shown progress, but we'll see where he is by the end of the week."

If Parsons plays against Los Angeles, it will be the first time in over 11 months he's been on a football field.

During a Week 15 loss to the Broncos, Parsons' season came to an abrupt end after he tore his ACL in his left knee in a non-contact play.

Since the injury, he's been steadily rehabbing and had his practice window opened earlier this month.

Packers' Micah Parsons had his practice window opened earlier this month. AP Photo/Steve Megargee

Parsons' potential season debut comes just after the Rams' offensive line won their fourth consecutive game, but paid a large price in that effort by losing centers Coleman Shelton and Dylan McMahon in Sunday's 34-6 victory against the Cardinals.

Shelton left the game in the first half due to a hamstring injury, while McMahon suffered from a knee injury just before halftime.

Los Angeles Rams head coach Sean McVay reacts to a play in Sunday's game against the Cardinals. AP Photo/Rick Scuteri

Backup left guard Beaux Limmer, who was starting at the position due to an injured Steve Avila, was shifted over to center in the second half.

According to California Post Senior Sports Reporter Michael Duarte, Coleman Shelton suffered from a severe hamstring strain and is expected to miss four to six weeks, while backup center Dylan McMahon has been placed on injured reserve with a knee injury.

The Rams managed to score 17 more points in the second half, and starting quarterback Matthew Stafford was sacked only two times with the injured offensive line.

"You've got to give those guys credit," Stafford said of his offensive line. "They've done an unbelievable job."
