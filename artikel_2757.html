Cowboys activate S Jalen Thompson

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Safety Jalen Thompson is set for a Thanksgiving Day return to the Cowboys lineup.

Thompson was activated from injured reserve on Wednesday afternoon. He has been out of action since injuring his hamstring in Week 3.

The Cowboys listed Thompson as questionable to face the Eagles earlier in the day, but the move to add him to the roster suggests he's healthy enough to play on Thursday. His presence would help fill out the safety spot with P.J. Locke ruled out due to a concussion.

Right guard Tyler Booker (ankle), tight end Jake Ferguson (knee,ankle), and wide receiver Jonathan Mingo (ankle) have also been ruled out.

The Cowboys have also elevated running back Jashaun Corbin from the practice squad for their matchup with the Eagles.
