Raiders-Seahawks predictions

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

It's time for our weekly tradition of picking the Las Vegas Raiders' games.

The defending Super Bowl champion Seahawks are 5-4. They have not been as strong as they were last year and they are playing without starting quarterback Sam Darnold. But they are still dangerous.
