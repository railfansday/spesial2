Buccaneers add QB Cooper Rush to practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Cooper Rush is headed to a different team in the NFC South.

With starter Baker Mayfield banged up, Rush is signing with the Buccaneers practice squad, according to agency Equity Sports.

Rush was with the Falcons earlier this season, starting a pair of games with poor results. Rush completed 22-of-39 passes for 229 yards with one touchdown, four interceptions, and a lost fumble.

If Mayfield is not able to start this week against the Lions, rookie backup Jalon Daniels would presumably take Mayfield’s place as QB1. Daniels went 1-2 in his three starts while Mayfield was dealing with a thumb injury, completing 61.2 percent of his passes for 501 yards with three touchdowns and five interceptions.
