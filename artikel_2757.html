Matt Patricia makes case to be back with Ohio State despite lost season

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Following Ohio State's Week 11 loss to Nebraska, the Buckeyes now have only a slim chance of reaching the Big Ten Championship Game. Amid the team's struggles, defensive coordinator Matt Patricia has come under scrutiny, but he has made it clear he wants to continue with the Buckeyes.

During a press conference, Patricia was asked about his future with the program and expressed his desire to remain in Columbus.

"I love it here. So I mean obviously, Buckeyes Nation is amazing," Patricia said.

"I love being here. Our family loves being here, and I love coaching these kids. I think for me, my passion for coaching is trying to. Obviously, I always try to do whatever I can to help the team win and, defensively, do that. That's my job and to make sure that our kids are in a great position."

One of the biggest reasons behind Ohio State's disappointing season has been its inability to consistently deliver defensively. More concerningly, the Buckeyes have now allowed at least 30 points in three of their recent losses. That defensive inconsistency has become increasingly difficult to ignore, particularly for a program that entered the season with championship expectations.

However, Patricia acknowledged the team's shortcomings and took responsibility for the defensive unit's struggles.

"When you're in a tough spot, and you haven't really performed the way that you wanted to, and certainly that starts with me from the accountability standpoint, I'm always like, hey, let's get back into the arena. Let's get in the hardest position we can put ourselves in and let's go fight our way out of this thing."

The Nebraska defeat has significantly damaged Ohio State's postseason ambitions. The Buckeyes entered the matchup ranked No. 14 in the College Football Playoff rankings, but the latest setback dropped them to 7-4 overall and 5-3 in conference play.

Ohio State is now facing the possibility of missing the College Football Playoff altogether. Even if the Buckeyes manage to win their final regular-season game, they will have little control over their conference championship destiny.
