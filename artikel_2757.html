Mike Tomlin says Aaron Rodgers is beloved in Steelers locker room

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The last 48 hours have been some of the most insane in the history of the Pittsburgh Steelers. Just two weeks after defeating their division-rival Browns 27-6 to take sole possession of first place in the AFC North, the Steelers announced they were benching quarterback Aaron Rodgers. That announcement was followed by the quarterback making an impromptu appearance on The Pat McAfee Show calling out head coach Mike McCarthy. This led to the Steelers releasing him.

Speaking to Mike Tirico at halftime of Bills vs. Chiefs, former Steelers head coach Mike Tomlin was asked about his firsthand experience coaching Rodgers and how the four-time MVP was perceived in the locker room.

"He was beloved," Tomlin said. "And I'm not over exaggerating that. He worked at it, and people appreciated his openness to getting to know and understand the Steelers culture, to be engaged with teammates. And particularly from my perspective, I work with a lot of veteran players over the years, and at times, they lose a little patience for the process, or they lose patience for relationships with younger guys, et cetera. He seemed to embrace all of that and really embrace it in a real natural way."

Tomlin and Rodgers' relationship was highlighted by Rodgers as a reason he came to Pittsburgh in 2025.
