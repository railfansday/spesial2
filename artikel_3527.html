Zelda: A Link To The Past HD-2D Remake Leaves Gamers Stunned

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Launching back in 1991 for the SNES, The Legend of Zelda: Link to the Past marked the third installment in the beloved Nintendo franchise, and, to this day, it is still remembered fondly despite being more of an underrated entry.

Serving as something of a prequel to the first two Zelda titles, A Link to the Past takes on the role of Link once more as he attempts to defeat Demon King Ganon and save Hyrule once more. However, what makes A Link to the Past as important as it is, is it was the first installment to introduce the Master Sword, which, to this day, is one of the most iconic weapons in video game history.

Over the years, The Legend of Zelda: Link to the Past has received multiple ports and re-releases and, in 2013, a sequel titled A Link Between Worlds also continued the story. However, in recent times, fans have caught wind of a fan-favorite project that aims to create an HD-2D remake of the hit 1991 title.

It was over on r/decomps that a trailer for this unofficial remake was shared, and it is clear to see that The Legend of Zelda: Link to the Past has never looked better, and as one user correctly put it, "Bro, I can’t keep up with these amazing projects. I was just about to start playing the dual-screen mod and now this?"

Shared by user JoggingThruThe6 who is the brains behind this project, they reveal that they have been working on it for the past few days and "tossed" together the trailer to highlight a few things. They continued, "I've been primarily testing and playing on my AYN Thor (I've built an enhanced version of the dual-screen UI)—but I wanted to record sound easily for some scenes and my Mac build chugged with the lighting in the intro... It's been a smooth 60 on my Thor."

Not only that, but the creator shared a list of features that players can expect with the HD-2D remake, including: dynamic lighting and weather effects, a day and night cycle, NPC schedules, an enhanced introduction scene, AI improvements, especially for the guards, seamless overworld and interiors, an improved UI, and sound replacements.

Additionally, JoggingThruThe6 has revealed that they are looking for collaborators to help with the project, so if you want to lend a hand in the creation of this stunning, albeit unofficial, HD-2D remake of The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past, make sure to head over to Reddit and share your interest.
