Magic: The Gathering Animated Series Officially Gets First Trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The long-in development Netflix show seems to have finally escaped development hell

Magic: The Gathering is finally getting a TV show, and publisher Wizards of the Coast just debuted the first official look at the animated series during its MagicCon Atlanta preview panel. While the first half of Wizards' stream focused on next February’s Nauctis: The Sunken Realm set, the second half was targeted at those who “love Magic’s multiverse.”

During the panel, Wizards revealed the official name of the show: Planeswalker.

Planeswalker will focus on a young Chandra Nalaar and her mentor Ajani Goldmane, Wizards said, and the series will feature a variety of art styles based on the planes that Chandra and Ajana visit. The animated series is set to debut in 2027.

“Plansewalker chronicles the fierce clash between formidable mages known as Planeswalkers," the show's synopsis reads. "At the center of this struggle for power and survival burns Chandra, a volatile pyromancer hunted for her gifts and haunted by her past. Confronted by an insidious force, Chandra and some of the most legendary Planeswalkers must harness their extraordinary abilities, navigate treacherous alliances, and battle their own inner demons to prevent a cataclysmic war that threatens to consume their very existence. As the line between victory and annihilation grows thinner, a single spark determines the fate of thousands of worlds.”

In June 2019, Netflix announced that it was working on a Magic: The Gathering TV series that would be executive produced by the Russo brothers. Henry Gilroy, who co-wrote Star Wars: The Clone Wars, and Jose Molina, who worked on Agent Carter, were set as the lead writers. Bardel Entertainment, the studio known for Rick and Morty, Teen Titans Go!, Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (2012), and The Dragon Prince, was going to handle animation. Then came a lengthy radio silence.

Deadline reported in August 2021 that the Russo brothers had departed the project, with a new creative team in place led by executive producer Jeff Kline (Transformers: Prime). Kline was chosen to oversee an all-new storyline focused on Planeswalkers. Snowball Studios stepped in for animation.

Then came yet another restart. In September 2024, Netflix revealed that Terry Matalas, known for showrunning MacGyver, later seasons of Star Trek: Picard, and Marvel's VisionQuest would be helming the rebooted MTG series. Matalas later said that the Planeswalkers Chandra, Ajani, and Jace would be the main characters. The show was previewed at the Annecy International Film Festival in June 2025. Based on today's trailer, Planeswalker appears to be this version of the show. Matalas is listed as a co-showrunner alongside Cindy Appel (MacGyver, Star Trek: Picard, VisionQuest)​​​​​. DIGIC Pictures is animating the series. The studio has mostly handled video game cinematics, VFX, and commercials in the past.

Beyond the long-in-development animated series, Magic: The Gathering is also getting a live-action film adaptation that will reportedly star Maya Hawke. Legendary Entertainment is producing the film, with Matt Johnson (Blackberry, Nirvanna the Band the Show) set to direct. Between the constant set releases and this slew of upcoming adaptations, Magic fans certainly can’t complain about a lack of "content," for better or worse.
