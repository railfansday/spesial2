Dancing With the Stars’ Ezra Frech Addresses Hailey Bills Dating Rumors

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Dancing With the Stars season 35 contestant Ezra Frech is weighing in on speculation he’s dating pro Hailey Bills.

“The internet is crazy,” Frech, 21, told Cosmopolitan in an interview published on Monday, November 23, when asked about the rumors. “Right now, I’m definitely single but not necessarily looking for a relationship. Because, again, once the dance season ends, I’ll be committing myself entirely to track again. I’m enjoying being single, and it’ll probably stay that way for a while.”

Speculation began swirling earlier this season that Frech and Bills, 20, are romantically linked, but neither had addressed the rumors. While reflecting on his DWTS season 35 journey, Frech explained to the outlet how he has been able to block out the outside noise.

“People online — as far as whether they think you’re great or mediocre or suck or whatever the opinions are on social media — shouldn’t influence your day-to-day,” he said. Thankfully, for [partner Daniella Karagach] and I, there haven’t been too many negative comments, but, of course, with every 30 positive comments, you’re going to get a negative comment. It’s all about not letting that little negative comment overshadow the beautiful stuff that people are saying online as well.”

While navigating online chatter, the Paralympian and Karagach, 33, are gearing up to take on the ballroom floor one last time together in the season 35 finale, airing on Tuesday, November 24. The pair are performing an instant dance, a salsa to Don Omar and Lucenzo‘s “Danza Kuduro” and a freestyle to Linkin Park’s “Numb.”

“I’ll miss getting to see everyone at the rehearsal studio,” Frech reflected to Cosmopolitan of the experience being nearly over. “I’ll miss getting lunch with Dani after practice. I’ll miss getting the opportunity to commit myself to something like this so entirely. And I’m gonna miss the show days. You become so close to everybody, and you spend so much time around these people, and then all of a sudden, it’s going to end.”

He continued, “But the good thing is that these people are still going to be in my life. I’m still going to hang out with Dani, and I’m still going to see Connor [Wood] whenever I can, and we’ll get lunch with Guillermo [Rodriguez]. These are people I’ll be with forever.”

Dancing With the Stars airs on ABC and Disney+ Tuesdays at 8 p.m. ET.
