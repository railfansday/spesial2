Capcom Officially Reveals Fourth Mega Man: Dual Override Robot Master

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Capcom continues to build excitement for Mega Man: Dual Override, and following the official reveal of Tank Man earlier this week, it's now lifted the lid on Cook Man.

He is one of the many Robot Masters coming to the game. This master chef is ready to slice and dice Proto Man and the Blue Bomber when his game launches on the Switch 1 & 2 in 2027.

Here's an official description, and you can see this robot master in action in the video above (courtesy of Rockman Corner):

A wandering chef seeking recognition for his culinary prowess, Cook Man prepares to slice and dice our heroes with his Kung Fu Cooking style in Mega Man: Dual Override.

Can you take the heat, or will you get out of his kitchen?

If you're not familiar with Mega Man, these Robot Masters will pop up at the end of each stage and attempt to stop Mega Man and Proto Man in their tracks.

Once again, Cook Man will join Tank Man, Twinkle Girl and Jet Man, with more Robot Master reveals on the way.
