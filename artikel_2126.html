Burnley backed to sign Southampton star who Tonda Eckert has snubbed

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Burnley have endured an incredibly difficult start to the 2026/27 campaign, with genuine fears of back-to-back relegations emerging, as one player has been outlined as their potential saviour.

Burnley, alongside Preston North End and Derby County, occupy one of the three relegation places as things stand, with one win, seven draws, and seven defeats in their first 15 league games.

Nicky Hayen has been replaced at the helm at Turf Moor by Swiss boss Fabio Celestini, who has no choice but to hit the ground running if he wants to guide the club away from immediate danger and follow in the footsteps of the likes of Sunderland, Luton Town, and Leicester City to suffer back-to-back relegations from the Premier League to League One.

With the January window approaching, the former Basel and Lugano boss will be looking to make some changes to the squad ASAP, especially in goal amid Max Weiss and Gregoire Coudert's struggles, as one player has been urged to be on his shortlist.

Football League World's Burnley fan pundit, Aaron Wright, has shared their thoughts on who they would like the Clarets to sign this January, and took little hesitation in their answer.

Aaron said: "Ramsdale, and it's not even close. Aaron Ramsdale, easily.

"He has arms, so that's an upgrade on our current goalkeeper. He has a brain, so that's an upgrade on the goalkeeper at the start of the season. And he's the fourth-choice goalkeeper at Southampton, playing a few Under-21 [games]; I don't know how often that is, but surely, with the way Southampton have him, it can't be a difficult deal to do, even from a cash-strapped position, even if it's just on loan.

"If he comes on loan, somehow keeps us up and puts in a reasonable performance for 23 games, or 22, if it's done early in January, just come in and just perform like hell, it can benefit everybody. Whether that be that we pay Southampton for his services, whether somebody else pays Southampton for his services in the summer, but we need Aaron Ramsdale, or somebody of that ilk. Alex Palmer also comes to mind. Maybe Mark Travers as well at Everton.

"But a goalkeeper is top of the list, and I think we can get a high-quality one that is reasonable for a team with potential money to spend. You never know with Burnley. To be fair, the owners just seem to throw debt at it anyway. So you might as well pile more on, because, let's be honest, you can't pay your way out of the debt we probably have.

"Just get Ramsdale. A goalkeeper who can communicate; he was doing superbly at Arsenal. I remember they just upgraded, as simple as that. They bought David Raya and his confidence got knocked and I hope that we can be a place where he can rebuild almost from point one.

"I think he's only 28. It's a reasonable age; you can get a reasonable amount of time out of them should it become permanent. But I think the only feasible way that we could get him is on loan, with a half-decent loan fee with some sort of option or obligation upon, I can't believe I'm saying this, staying in the Championship.

Whether he would want to come, I don't know. But I think it's the best goalkeeper you can reasonably get. I know some Burnley fans mention Nick Pope. Obviously, I love Nick Pope, but when you've got a goalkeeper potentially of a similar quality, six years younger, with very good experience, you'd probably take him.

"I see no reason why Alan Ramsdale can't come in reasonably easily, and, to be honest, probably save our season. Any goalkeeper with hands and a brain would, but he's the best one I can think of off the top of my head."

Ramsdale has had a unique career, with four relegations, a cumulative transfer fee total of approximately £74 million, five England caps, and now, a fourth-choice shot-stopper in the Championship with Southampton.

The 28-year-old joined the Saints from Arsenal back in the summer of 2024 for around £25 million, but after one season and one relegation, he joined Newcastle United back in the Premier League on loan, battling for the number one jersey at St James' Park with Nick Pope.

Now, he finds himself behind Daniel Peretz, George Long, and Jamie Jones in the pecking order at St Mary's, playing with the club's Under-21s to maintain fitness, in a stark fall from grace.

The Englishman certainly has a lot left to give, and a lot to prove, and Burnley should look to be the beneficiaries of his unfortunate situation this January.

Celestini has been chosen as the man to guide Burnley away from danger, and for at least the first couple of months at the helm, he must make do with the tools he has been provided.

The Swiss boss has found success throughout his career as a head coach, but the Clarets job possesses challenges he has never had to endure, and whether he can guide them away from danger remains to be seen.

A new shot-stopper ahead of Weiss and Coudert must be a priority, and Ramsdale could be the answer.
