Kevin Parker’s Tame Impala Wins ARIA’s 2026 Global Impact Award

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Tame Impala wins silverware ahead of the 40th annual ARIAs.

Kevin Parker won’t go home empty handed from the 2026 ARIA Awards.

The Western Australian singer, songwriter, multi-instrumentalist and Tame Impala spearhead will receive the second annual ARIA Global Impact Award, announced ahead of the 40th annual ARIAs this Wednesday, Nov. 18.

Presented by Spotify, the accolade recognizes Australian recording artists who’ve demonstrated outstanding breakthrough international success and cultural influence, and made an impact for Australian music “through artistry, innovation and global connection” over the previous 12 months. “I just found out I am this year’s recipient of the Global Impact Award at the ARIAs, which is huge, it’s such an honor, obviously,” enthuses Parker, whose career collection of ARIAs now lifts to 14. “I’m going to be at the ARIA Awards to receive it in person, so I’ll see you there. Parker has “spent nearly 20 years making records that the rest of the world can’t stop playing. Rihanna has covered him, Lady Gaga and Dua Lipa have brought him into the studio and this year Dracula sank its teeth into the ARIA Top 10 for 18 weeks,” remarks ARIA CEO Annabelle Herd. “He’s shown a generation of Australian artists that a song made in a home studio in Perth can find its biggest audiences in America, Britain, Mexico and Brazil. Congratulations Kevin from all of us at ARIA.”

Dom Dolla was the inaugural recipient of the award, which is presented at the discretion of the ARIA board to an artist whose international efforts are said to exemplify Australia’s influence in contemporary music worldwide.

Parker will have many more chances to collect silverware on the night. Specifically, eight, which places him equal with Genesis Owusu at the top of the leaderboard. Ecca Vandal is close behind with seven nods.

Spotify’s managing director AUNZ, Mikaela Lancaster, announced this year’s Global Impact recipient during the streaming platform’s Artist Party on Monday night (Nov. 16) in central Sydney, headlined by Owusu. Tame Impala amassed 4.5 billion streams on Spotify in the past 12 months, Lancaster explained. “It’s not just Australia, the U.S. and U.K., he’s growing his fans in Brazil, Mexico, Germany, to name a few.”

Fans worldwide added Tame Impala’s music to their own Spotify playlists more than 141 million times in the past year, and with “Dracula,” Parker enjoyed the biggest hit of his career, nabbing gold certification or better across 16 countries, and hitting the top 10 in 18 markets, including a No. 2 best in the U.K.

For the first time in ARIAs history, more than one million public votes were cast across Spotify and the ARIA website, with fans tipping their picks across four publicly decided categories: song of the year, best Australian live act, best video and most popular international artist.

The 2026 ARIA Awards will be presented at Sydney’s Hordern Pavilion. It’s supported by Destination NSW, in partnership with Spotify, and will be live on Paramount+ and broadcast on 10. Performances and moments will also be available on the @ARIA.official YouTube channel.
