Nobody Prepared 4DX Audiences for Olivia Colman Having Sex With a Basket

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The seat-shaking theater tech is supposed to make audiences feel part of the action. But then came 'Wicker.'

The seats move. The wind blows. Water sprays you in the face. That’s 4DX, the movie theater format designed to make audiences feel like they’re inside the action. Usually, that means a chair-shaking car chase or superhero battle. For those watching Wicker, it meant being bucked in their seats every time Olivia Colman had sex with a basket.

The dramedy about a fisherwoman who commissions a basketmaker to weave her a husband became the first romance to get the treatment since the format was developed in 2009. Afterward came “a beat of stunned silence … while everyone catches their breath.” Then, “the whole room starts cheering and clapping.”
