New EFL rule forces QPR to change home shirt design

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

QPR will have to change the design of their shirt from next season moving forward due to new EFL regulations that will be in place.

The R’s blue-and-white hooped home shirt is arguably one of the most iconic in the Football League, with a host of legendary players having pulled it on over the years at Loftus Road.

However, QPR are now in a position where they will have to alter the design after an EFL update.

That’s because, as explained by West London Sport, the EFL have introduced a new rule that means a ‘number zone’ needs to be free on every shirt.

Essentially, it means that the number on the back of a shirt and the player's name must be free of any patterns or designs.

Of course, the current shirt prevents that from happening, so QPR’s normal design, where the blue-and-white hoop goes all the way around, will need to be changed.

The decision by the EFL was supported by some in the game, with some commentators particularly pleased as it will make reading names and numbers a lot easier. And, the same applies to matchday officials on the pitch, so there is some understanding of the decision.

It should be said that QPR shirts in the past have had a white patch on the back at times, so this isn’t something unique, although most fans in the modern era are used to the blue-and-white hoops going all the way around.

To their credit, the club have interacted with supporters on what next step they will take.

They add that whilst the players must not wear a shirt with hoops that go all the way around, they can still sell shirts to supporters in that way.

So, they have allowed fans to vote on whether the replica shirts next season will have full hoops, or if they want the same design as the players.

It will be interesting to see which vote wins, but there will be frustration among the fans that the club has been put in a position where they have to make changes to their shirt.

This is a new regulation in the EFL, but it’s already in place in the Premier League, so even if QPR do win promotion, they are going to be in the same position.

Again, it’s something that some fans may not like, but you do feel sorry for the club, as they have been left in a position where they need to make changes.
