Celtics’ Jayson Tatum hit with tech at the worst time of the game vs. Nuggets

In the fourth quarter of their Wednesday night tilt against the Denver Nuggets, the Boston Celtics were trying their hardest to not completely throw away the 17-point lead they held earlier in the game. But the Nuggets, buoyed by the support they got from the fans at Ball Arena, stormed back and even took a 104-101 lead with just seven seconds left in the fourth quarter.

The Celtics then called timeout with the intention to draw up a play to try and tie the game and force overtime. However, they didn't even get that opportunity. After Murray drilled the shot to put the Nuggets up by three, Jayson Tatum threw the ball towards an official stationed near midcourt. The referee near the baseline saw that all the way and called a technical foul on Tatum.

Murray would then make the technical free throw to give the Nuggets a two-possession lead, and that was all she wrote.

With both teams headed to their respective benches, Tatum's frustrations clearly got the better of him. Nuggets star Nikola Jokic saw the play the entire way and pleaded for the officials to punish the Celtics star's actions. He got his wish.

Perhaps Tatum didn't take too kindly to the officials' decision to swallow their whistle on the previous possession. Celtics rookie Chris Cenac Jr. fell over on a hard screen from Jokic, which then freed up enough space for Murray to drill a tough midrange shot right in the face of Neemias Queta.

Maybe the Celtics star's frustrations came from the fact that they shot 9-of-24 from the field in the fourth and failed to execute in the clutch.

Tatum tried to explain his actions by saying that he simply wanted to give the ball back to the official. But that official's back was turned against him, and he had another referee closer to him to whom he could have handed the ball to. And a split-second mistake cost the Celtics, as they fell to 11-4 on the season with this 105-101 loss to the Nuggets, marking their second straight defeat.
