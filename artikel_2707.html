Werneck delighted by surprise U17 World Cup call-up, hails team unity

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Gabriel Werneck in action during training in DohaCredits: Nelson Terme /CBF

Goalkeeper Gabriel Werneck greeted his call-up for the U-17 World Cup with surprise and joy. The player said that, although he had not expected to be selected, he had always worked toward earning the opportunity to represent Brazil in the competition.

"I wasn't expecting to be here, but I've always worked toward it. When the squad was announced, I was so happy. It's a dream to represent the greatest national team in the world."

With just days to go before the opening match, the Flamengo goalkeeper also spoke about his expectations for the World Cup. He acknowledged the natural nerves that come with a competition of this magnitude, but believes that once he steps onto the pitch, they will give way to focus and the team's preparation.

"Expectations couldn't be higher, of course. When it comes to the World Cup, we all get nervous, but once we're out on the pitch and the ball starts rolling, those nerves disappear. We're ready for this big moment," he said.

Werneck also highlighted the atmosphere within the squad and the work being done in training in Doha. According to him, the bond between the players has been one of the positives of their preparations.

"It's a close-knit group, a really good group. We're all very supportive of each other, and our preparations are going really well. We're well prepared," he said.

As for Brazil's first opponents, Ireland, the goalkeeper expects a physically demanding encounter.

"It's a very tough match, a very physical one. We already have a good understanding of Ireland's strengths," he noted.

Brazil's men's U-17 national team kicks off its World Cup campaign this Saturday (21) against Ireland at 6 p.m. local time (12 p.m. Brasília time). Brazil is in Group I, alongside Costa Rica and Tanzania.
