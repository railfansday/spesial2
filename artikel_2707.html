RAMalytics: How will Rams deploy their new offensive wrinkle?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Sean McVay looked at the Los Angeles Rams' depth chart following their recent rash of injuries and said "I'm just going to put our best remaining players on the field."

At times Sunday against the Arizona Cardinals, that meant deploying both Blake Corum and Kyren Williams at the same time. Two-back sets, commonly referred to as "pony personnel", unlocked LA's passing game despite a limited corps of pass catchers. McVay's wrinkle was to play with three tight ends in lieu of having a receiver on the field. This wasn't 21 or 22 personnel. It was something else.

The interesting part? 207 (89%) of the Rams' 234 passing yards came with both Corum and Williams on the field while 12 yards and 15 yards came from 11 and 13 personnel groupings, respectively. Matthew Stafford was 13 of 17 on such plays.

In the Rams' bread and butter 13 personnel? He completed just three of six passes for 15 yards and took a sack.

It's mildly strange that the Rams leaned into the passing game with two backs on the field. They had little rushing production to show for it otherwise.

Out of LA's 202 rushing yards, 19 came from the "other" personnel grouping, per SumerSports. They ran 40 times for 183 yards out of 13 personnel, and then netted zero yards between 12 and 21. This is a dichotomy that must be assessed and bridged together.

If McVay's newest offensive wrinkle is going to have staying power, the next step is to also threaten on the ground. There should be plenty of opportunities for changeups and misdirection based on the tape LA left for opponents against Arizona.

It remains to be seen whether the two-back sets are a means to getting by without Puka Nacua and Davante Adams. LA could brush the idea aside once their receiver room returns to health. Or, as we saw last season, the Rams may be finding lightning in a bottle that will eventually be emulated by the rest of the NFL.

At least for now, Sean McVay has our attention. His inventiveness is trademarked. How long is the runway for the newest trick up his sleeve?
