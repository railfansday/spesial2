Jude Bellingham speaks on his 100th La Liga appearance: &#8216;A great honour&#8217;

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Jude Bellingham has quickly developed into one of Real Madrid's most clutch goalscorers, and his return to a free attacking midfield role has seen him blossom under Jose Mourinho.

The direct result of putting him back in his niche has been outstanding, for the player is now one of Real Madrid's most regular goal threats.

Even against Celta Vigo, it was his strike that helped Real Madrid get back on level terms in the second half, and thereby laid the foundation for the eventual comeback win.

The Real Madrid midfielder spoke to the media (h/t Madrid Xtra) after his team's win over Celta Vigo at the weekend and opened up on some of the milestones he hit along the way.

For starters, Bellingham spoke out on his goal against the Galician side and how it unfolded.

Bellingham scored on his 100th appearance. (Photo by Denis Doyle/Getty Images)

"My goal? Just a wonderful play from Vini down the left, came inside and played it into Kylian."

Elaborating on how Kylian Mbappe and Vinicius Jr. combined for the goal-scoring chance that fell to him, the player said,

"Kylian had great awareness to see me there. It was a really intelligent play from them two."

Bellingham also reached 100 La Liga appearances in the game against Celta Vigo, hitting a massive milestone in his career in Spain. Speaking on what the achievement meant to him, he said,

"100 La Liga appearances? It's a great honour to represent this club. Another milestone I'm really proud of."

In surreal form. (Photo by Juan Manuel Serrano Arce/Getty Images)

He also thanked the fans for all the love they showered on him and elaborated on how he feels comfortable at Real Madrid.

"I still feel like I've been here two months. Thanks to the fans for receiving me this way. I feel very loved."

Finally, Bellingham addressed Real Madrid's poor performance against Celta Vigo, and how it was a complicated affair given it was the first game after the international break.

"The game after the international break is always difficult.

"We've got lads flying from all over the place and it's difficult to have enough time to prepare as best as possible."

Finally, Bellingham expressed conviction that the team's performances would only improve.

"I'm sure for the coming games we'll do a better job of that."
