Mike Tomlin questions Cowboys defense's 'hustle' after historically awful day vs. 49ers

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

One of the hot topics during NBC's Sunday Night Football studio coverage was the Dallas Cowboys' latest defensive disaster against the San Francisco 49ers.

Dallas allowed 584 yards, the most the Cowboys have ever allowed at home in franchise history, and looked hapless throughout a 46-40 loss to the rival 49ers. It was the latest brutal outing for a Cowboys defense that has been among the worst in the NFL all season.

Is it a lack of talent, effort or coaching?

Former Steelers head coach Mike Tomlin, who now works on NBC's Sunday Night Football broadcast, thinks there's some effort issues Dallas needs to address.

"To say it didn't look good is an understatement," he said at halftime of Sunday night's Pittsburgh-Cincinnati game. "Not only do they have some structural issues, some fundamental issues, but they also appear to have some hustle issues. On some of those long touchdowns, there's a lack of hustle and so they've got a lot of things to address."

The long touchdowns Tomlin referenced were gut punches for the Cowboys. They included a 74-yard catch-and-run for Christian McCaffrey, a 70-yard run for Deebo Samuel and another 66-yard catch-and-run for McCaffrey.

Head coach Brian Schottenheimer was asked after the game if he believes Dallas' issues are effort-related.

"I don't think so," he said. "The fight in these guys is right. Nope."

Whether it's effort, scheme, talent or anything else, something is clearly rotten within the Cowboys' defense. Sunday's ugly outing was just further proof.
