Packers-Rams Final Score: Blake Corum takes control in 26-13 Rams win

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Green Bay Packers' playoff hopes are officially on life support. The team needed a win over the Los Angeles Rams on Wednesday of Thanksgiving week to stay in the NFC's 7th and final playoff position with six games to go in the 2026 regular season. Instead, another offensive collapse in the second half doomed the team, as Jordan Love and company ran just eight plays, held the football for barely three minutes, and picked up no first downs between the start of the second half and the midpoint of the fourth quarter.

Over that time, the Rams took a lead and extended it out to ten points, which was too much for Green Bay to overcome. Blake Corum ran the football more than 20 times in the second half as the Green Bay defense wore down, and the Rams running back finished with over 180 yards on the ground and scored the go-ahead touchdown early in the third quarter to help the Rams coast to a 26-13 win.

Micah Parsons' return for the Packers saw him record a sack, but it was spoiled by his teammates' inability to stop the run and the Packers' offensive miscommunications. Jordan Love threw two interceptions, both of which appeared to be partially due to receivers running the wrong routes. Tucker Kraft committed an early fumble as well, giving the Packers three turnovers for the second straight game. And all told, the Packers had fewer than 20 minutes of possession in the game and ran just 44 plays on offense.

The unit certainly suffered after Christian Watson left the game with an apparent concussion, while right guard Kevin Zeitler injured his ankle in his first start as a Packer. But the Packers' issues were well beyond any injury issues, and they once again point to more fundamental problems with this team as a whole. With the Rams getting the win — going up by a game in the standings and earning a big tiebreaker with the victory — the Packers' likelihood of making the postseason is now awfully slim.

The Packers went 3-and-out on the game's opening drive, and the Rams immediately took advantage of a bad call, with Brandon Cisse getting flagged for a long pass interference penalty. That put them in field goal range quickly, but Xavier McKinney snuffed out a swing pass on 3rd down to force a 3-point attempt from former Packer Brandon McManus, who hit from 43 yards out to give the Rams an early lead.

Despite a running game that lost six carries on its first two carries, the Packers offense got moving on their second drive. Jordan Love hit Kaleb Johnson for 19 yards out of the backfield for the initial first down of the game, but after he found Tucker Kraft for another first down, the tight end coughed up the football as Nate Landman forced a fumble for the game's first turnover. Another inexplicable penalty on the ensuing drive helped the Rams drive deep into Packers territory, as Devonte Wyatt was called for an absurd roughing the passer penalty. Micah Parsons made his presence known for the first time with a pressure on Matthew Stafford, however, helping to limit the Rams to another field goal.

After another run for negative yardage by Johnson, Love hit Watson for a big 25-yard gain on a crossing route over the middle to get the Packers quickly across midfield. Trey Smack cut the Rams' lead to 6-3 a few plays later, hitting a bomb from 54 yards out to get Green Bay on the board.

The Rams responded with a touchdown drive, picking apart the Packers' secondary with short and intermediate. Stafford went 5 of 6 for 60 yards on the drive, with his longest play going to Konata Mumpfield for 24 yards, but it was Kyren Williams who finished it off, finding the end zone from seven yards out for a score. That gave them a 13-3 lead early on in the second quarter.

Love came up with back-to-back explosive plays on the first two snaps of the next drive, however, as the Packers answered. An end-around flea flicker from Savion Williams back to Love went for a gain of 21 to Watson, then Love hit Matthew Golden for a catch-and-run of 39 yards. A Chris Brooks run of 4 yards and a Love scramble for 7 set up goal to go and two plays later from the 7, Love drifted back and found Watson crossing left to right for a touchdown. Watson's 8th score of the season brought Green Bay back to within three points.

After forcing the Rams to punt for the first time all game, the Packers drove into field goal range once again. They did so thanks to Savion Williams picking up a 3rd-and-short on a dive when lined up as a tailback and Jordan Love finding MarShawn Lloyd for a nice gain out of the backfield for another first down. Matt LaFleur kept the offense on the field for a 4th-and-1 at the 30-yard line, and it proved to be a costly decision.

Love lofted a good pass to Watson up the right sideline for a gain of 15 yards, but Kamren Kinchens was ejected for a hit to Watson's head. Watson walked off on his own power, but the penalty and the catch were erased, offset by an offensive pass interference penalty on Matthew Golden. Watson walked to the locker room under his own power, but he would not return to the game.

Instead of sending the offense out again, Trey Smack came on for a 48-yard field goal attempt and he hit it, tying the game with just under two minutes left in the first half. Micah Parsons then got his first sack of the season on first down of the Rams' next drive, scuttling any real chance for Los Angeles to score before the half as the game went into the break tied at 13.

Coming out of the break, the Rams got cruising on the ground. Blake Corum picked up chunks of yardage as Stafford found Mumpfield for a couple of 3rd down conversions to get inside the Packers' 10. On 3rd-and-2, Mumpfield appeared to have a touchdown, but he was ruled to have bobbled the ball and the play was ruled incomplete. That appeared to set up a 4th down attempt, but instead the Packers were called for defensive holding on the play anyway, giving the Rams a 1st-and-goal from the one. After Corum got stopped on his first rushing attempt, he barely snuck over the goal line on second down to give the Rams a seven-point lead.

The Packers' offense got bailed out on the next sequence with the game hanging in the balance, but then gave the ball right back. After going three-and-out following the Rams' 8-minute drive, Los Angeles looked to put together a long drive again but Ty'Ron Hopper dropped into zone coverage and intercepted a Stafford pass, setting up Love and company in a great spot. However, he and Matthew Golden were not on the same page; Golden appeared to run the wrong route and Love's deep pass to the middle of the field got picked off near the goal line to return the favor.

Corum continued to run the ball effectively into the early fourth quarter, even as they saw a pair of starting offensive linemen and tight end Tyler Higbee leave the game with injuries. Finally, however, the Packers managed to get a stop with the Rams at the 40-yard line, leading to a surprising punt rather than a long field goal attempt.

The Packers still could not move the chains, however, failing to pick up a first down for the third time in three second half drives. Tucker Kraft came up a yard and a half short on 3rd-and-9, and Matt LaFleur called for a punt from deep in Rams territory. Corum gave the Packers a big jab on 2nd-and-long with a 13-yard reception, then delivered a knockout blow with a 35-yard run up the gut on 3rd-and-1. That set up another McManus field goal, making it a two-possession game at 23-13.

Green Bay finally started to mount a bit of a rally, picking up their first first downs of the second half with under six minutes to go in the game. Love picked up a 4th-and-8 from near midfield, but he got crossed up once again on a route, this time with Bo Melton, and threw his third interception of the game to truly put the game on ice. Los Angeles ran out all but the last 30 seconds or so of clock on the ground, adding a short field goal for the game's final points.

The win gave Sean McVay his first victory over his friend and former protege, as Matt LaFleur had been 5-0 against LA heading in. Green Bay now likely needs to run the table to make a run at the playoffs, or at least get a significant amount of help, now that they have fallen out of playoff position.
