Texas football’s Steve Sarkisian desperately wants to chase SEC title, regardless of CFP risk

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

On Saturday, the Texas football program improved to 9-2 on the season with a blowout home win over the Arkansas Razorbacks on Senior Night. The Longhorns now sit with a chance to face the Georgia Bulldogs in the SEC Championship Game, with Alabama and Tennessee also in the running for the final spot heading into the last week of the regular season.

Recently, Longhorns head coach Steve Sarkisian revealed why he wants his team to be in Atlanta in two weeks for the big game.

"I think myself, I think our staff, the people in our organization and the players in our organization; this is a highly competitive building. This is a group of people who have a super high competitive spirit… if we're fortunate enough to compete for an SEC championship, we'll be all-in on that, and then we'll regroup," he said, per Evan Vieth of Inside Texas on X, formerly Twitter.

Some have floated the idea that Texas making the SEC Championship could actually work against the Longhorns' playoff hopes, as they would be looking at a third loss on the season if they were to take a defeat to Georgia in that game.

There are still several tiebreaking scenarios at play heading into the final game of the season, but Texas will at least need to take care of their end of the deal by defeating rival Texas A&M on the road next week in order to keep hope alive for an appearance in the SEC Championship Game.

Kickoff between the Longhorns and the Aggies is set for next week at 7:30 pm ET from College Station.
