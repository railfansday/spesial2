Oregon scares No. 5 Texas Tech before falling 62-59 on Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Oregon Ducks men's basketball team has the potential to be really good. They showed everybody that on Thanksgiving. But ultimately, it was the little things they didn't get done that cost them an upset over the No. 5 team in the nation.

After going on a 19-2 run to give themselves a 59-54 lead with under four minutes left, Oregon went cold from the field and they lost 62-59 to Texas Tech in Las Vegas.

Even though they went down in defeat, the Ducks proved they could play with anybody in the country if they just played some defense. That part of the game has been optional lately, but in the second half, Oregon really buckled down on the defense and held Texas Tech to just 24 points in the second half.

Although the Ducks were great defensively, a missed layup, a missed rebound, and a costly turnover, all under the two-minute mark, led to their demise.

Jasper Johnson led the team with 18 points and Andrew Meadow added 13. Unfortunately for Oregon, those were the only two players with double figures. Donovan Atwell led the Red Raiders with 17 points.

If Oregon had pulled off the upset, the story of the game would have been their defense, especially in the second half. Through the first five games, it seemed as if defense was just an option for the Ducks. But whatever needed to click on defense for Oregon clicked in a big way in the second half. They held the Red Raiders to just 28 percent shooting and 3-of-15 from three-point range in those final 20 minutes.

At one point in the second half, the Ducks found themselves down 46-33. Then came the big 19-2 run. Oregon became more aggressive on offense, driving to the hoop and kicking out for some open threes. Meanwhile, on defense, the Ducks seem to be in the face of every Red Raiders shooter.

Although they couldn't sustain the momentum and lost the game, the Ducks can learn some valuable lessons from this game moving forward. Those lessons can be put into play as soon as Friday afternoon, as they will face Maryland to end their trip to Vegas and the Players Era Festival.

The Ducks will then take a quick jaunt to Los Angeles to open Big Ten play with UCLA Dec. 1.
