Houston Rockets centers Steven Adam and Clint Capela expected to return next week

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Houston Rockets have good news and bad news at the center position.

The good news: Steven Adams and Clint Capela, who have missed the last eight and three games, respectively, are expected back soon. Coach Ime Udoka said Monday that the team was targeting either the Nov. 15 game at the Washington Wizards or the Nov. 16 contest at the Miami Heat for their returns.

Adams, one of the league's best offensive rebounders, missed Houston's season opener with a right calf soreness/tightness, returned to play a little over eight minutes in the second game and hasn't played since with a right soleus (back calf muscle) strain. Capela rolled his ankle on Halloween against the Oklahoma City Thunder.

The bad news? Those absences combined with Oscar Tshiebwe missing Monday's game against the Denver Nuggets due to illness - Udoka said hopefully a one-game absence - meant the Rockets were to face three-time MVP Nikola Jokic with just one healthy center, Alperen Şengün. Jokic is averaging 22.9 points, 10.1 rebounds and 9.1 assists per game, relatively down numbers for him.

"Play some small ball," Udoka said pregame when asked how Houston planned to deal with Jokic. "We have some wings with size and some rugged guys down there that can battle a little bit. … We're very comfortable going small, although Jokic is a tough task for bigs alone, and so we'll mix up some coverages and schemes."

Udoka listed Jabari Smith Jr., Marcus Smart and Jae'Sean Tate when asked who would guard Jokic, saying that they'll try to deny some catches or push the center's catch point out to the perimeter.
