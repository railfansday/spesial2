'Manchester may not have a Premier League team next season' - Keith Wyness drops bombshell verdict after Man United and Man City latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Manchester United’s new manager Mauricio Pochettino has been handed a mountain to climb by chief executive Omar Berrada’s recent comments about the squad.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that there are bigger issues at Old Trafford than just the quality of the first-team group.

This comes after Berrada recently told Sky Sports that they board felt they “fundamentally improved” the squad with the signings of Carlos Baleba, Youri Tielemans and Andrey Santos in the summer, alongside the return of Marcus Rashford.

However, Wyness believes that all that comment has done is heaped more pressure on Pochettino and created an “uphill struggle” for him in his new job.

Tielemans has started 10 Premier League games already, while Santos has featured seven times in the league, but neither have helped to steady the ship.

The Red Devils are sitting inside the relegation zone after the opening 11 games, and Wyness believes that the club hierarchy are still trying to deflect blame elsewhere.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy business advising elite clubs – believes that Berrada has to “stick to” his suggestion that United could have a Premier League-winning team by 2028.

The CEO doubled down on that premonition back in June 2025, but Wyness feels that for now at least, United cannot focus on anything other than a relegation battle.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider’s Inside Track podcast, he explained how Berrada’s recent comments regarding the squad haven’t helped the manager at all.

“He (Berrada) is on record as saying that they expect to get a title winning team in 2028. He's going to have to stick with that, and right now they're looking to avoid relegation as things stand,” he said.

“If they carry on with this record that at the moment they're in line for 28 points, which is pretty much relegation. And there is a scenario where Manchester may not even have a Premier League team next season.

“It's a big problem for United as well because they're under such intense scrutiny. Everything is monitored and it's just going to have to be turned around on results.

“But he (Berrada) has given Pochettino a real uphill struggle. We'll see how he gets on, we'll all be watching.

“But, as I say, it still points to bigger symptoms to me than just the squad itself.”

Former Tottenham scout Bryan King recently told Football Insider that Pochettino is in line to get a “ridiculous” bonus at Old Trafford if he can steer United clear of relegation.

The ex-recruitment man believes that the Argentine is already on a yearly wage of around £15million with the Red Devils.

However, King believes that Pochettino’s wage will climb up to the £20m mark next season, if he can keep the struggling giants in the Premier League.

King does not believe that the former Spurs boss is the right fit for United, and does not expect him to bring success back to Old Trafford during his reign.
