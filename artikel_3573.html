Rob Lowe Reveals Why He Felt Like ‘Huge Cuck’ During Melissa Gilbert Romance

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Rob Lowe opened up like never before about his past star-studded romances, controversial moments and more in his new Netflix documentary, Rob Lowe: The Outsider.

The doc, released Tuesday, November 24, follows the trajectory of Lowe’s life from his complicated upbringing and Brat Pack days to his substance abuse battle and subsequent recovery.

“From his breakout roles in The Outsiders and St. Elmo’s Fire through his Emmy-nominated turn on The West Wing, Lowe has built a career defined by constant transformation,” the official synopsis reads. “Rob Lowe: The Outsider traces that arc in full for the first time, pairing new interviews with Lowe and those who know him best against a career-spanning look back at the moments — public and private — that shaped him.”

Nothing was off the table as Lowe, 62, offered a candid glimpse into his ups and downs. Keep scrolling for the biggest revelations from Rob Lowe: The Outsider:

In the doc, Chad Lowe recalled his brother living the “very fast life” with Gilbert behind the scenes. Rob, for his part, remembered being attracted to Gilbert’s “really fun side” amid his rising fame.

“She would invite me to go to her new friend Warren’s house for movie night and it was Warren Beatty. I was scared to meet him, but finally I went one night with Melissa,” Rob recalled. “I walk in[to the room] and it gets real quiet. I know they’re talking about me and it gets a little uncomfortable.”

Rob said Beatty compared his and Gilbert’s dynamic to Beatty’s relationship with his ex Natalie Wood. More specifically, Beatty recounted how Wood would visit Frank Sinatra’s house, which he didn’t question until later.

“‘I always wondered, what was going on there? Then, years and years and years later, I ran into [her and asked],’” Rob recalled hearing from Beatty. “‘She says, ‘Warren, what do you think was going on? We were f***ing.’”

Rob struggled to understand that Beatty was hinting at a secret affair with Gilbert, adding, “You’re like, ‘Wait a minute. Hang on here.’ But you’re also kind of like, ‘Yeah, hang on here. Somebody you admire is interested in your thing.’ By the way, hearing myself say that either makes me super evolved or just a huge cuck.”

While many consider Rob’s role as Sodapop Curtis in The Outsiders as one of his career highlights, Rob revealed he’s still upset by how much of his screentime was cut. “The first time I saw it, I wanted to cry,” he admitted. “I remember I went to opening night — probably a 3,000 seat theater — and the lights went down. I thought there was a mistake. Movies are 12 reels long or whatever it is. I thought they’d put the fifth reel first. To my horror, I realized it wasn’t a mistake. This was the new opening of the movie.”

Rob was left reeling after the shocking realization.

“All my stuff was basically cut out of the movie, and I never knew it until I showed up at the first screening. It’s all gone. I’ve never truly gotten over it,” he admitted. “I really haven’t. I’m 17 and 18 years old. Somebody can tell me, but no, there was no warning.”

He concluded: “To his credit, C. Thomas Howell was busting Francis [Ford Coppola]’s balls about [how] the two people he decided to cut out of the movie were me and Tom Cruise.”

After The Outsiders, Rob starred in The Hotel New Hampshire with Jodie Foster and Nastassja Kinski. “I am in that period of my life where I had my first — what you think is a serious relationship — and so it’s got all the f*** ups anybody could imagine,” he shared, referring to his romance with Gilbert. “It’s off and it’s on and on and it’s off. I wasn’t gonna let that stand in the way of [being with Nastassja].”

Rob said the movie’s set was “a different world” compared to other projects and claimed,“[Director] Tony Richardson was actively promoting us to have sex with each other.”

According to Demi Moore, who dated Rob after they worked together on St. Elmo’s Fire, it was no secret that he wasn’t looking to settle down in his early 20s. “He just seemed like the perpetual guy out on the town. Not really into settling down,” she said. “I didn’t get any sense that he was ready.”

Gwyneth Paltrow also weighed in on her connection with Rob. “People just really wanted to sleep with Rob Lowe,” she said, quipping, “I never slept with Rob Lowe, just for the record. Because he has slept with everyone. ”

Amid his rise to the top, Rob developed an addiction to both drugs and alcohol. Moore said she got the sense that Rob was not “completely happy with where he was at.” Chad further pointed out that the “nature of his partying” didn’t seem “like it was fun anymore.”

Rob, for his part, recalled feeling like he let down people in his life. He specifically pointed to how he would miss major life events, saying, “I would just go dark and disappear.”

Rob met his now-wife, Sheryl Berkoff, when she was his makeup artist on a project. They crossed paths again later on, with Berkoff admitting in the doc that she didn’t have a “great” experience with Rob at first.

“To say I didn’t like him would be disingenuous,” she noted. “But to say that he got on my nerves would be yes.”

Rob ultimately invited Berkoff to go on a global press trip with him, which she thought was a work offer. She was dating Keanu Reeves at the time, but her connection with Rob progressed when he extended their travels to include a romantic getaway.

“When I got home and had to face reality, I told my boyfriend that I was with Rob and that ended it because I loved what was happening with Rob,” she shared in the doc.

The romance didn’t last long, however, as Rob returned to bad habits.

“I was still drinking. So of course I have a drink and go find some coke and party. Then I had to go chase a girl — it was mathematical for me,” he said, remembering one night when he called Berkoff. “She said, ‘Look, I love you. We will always be friends. I will always be there for you. But I am not gonna do this with you. Your lifestyle is not for me right now.’”

Berkoff hung up on Rob and began to cry. “I felt betrayed. But I knew if I were to be in that relationship at that time, I’d end up hurt,” she recalled. “I was a child of an alcoholic and I couldn’t watch or be a part of him destroying himself.”

Rob cited a missed call from his mom — who was trying to reach him because his grandfather suffered a heart attack — as the reason he got sober. As he ignored the call to keep drinking, he realized how much he was letting down his loved ones.

“It was the proverbial straw that broke the camel’s back. I happened to think it was bigger than straw. It was not being able to be there for my family when they really needed me,” he explained. “But I’d certainly done that before. So what is any different? … I had been carrying in my wallet, for a year and a half, the business card of a drug and alcohol interventionist. If you’d asked me why it was in my wallet, I couldn’t have told you. But it was. I pulled out the card. I made the call and the next morning, I was on a plane to rehab.”

Berkoff was the “only person” who visited Rob when he was in treatment. One year into his sobriety, the pair got engaged. The couple, who share two sons, have been married since 1991.

“When you’re partying together, it can be very superficial. [As Rob got clean], I felt like, ‘Oh, there’s potential here for us to have a very deep relationship,’” Chad shared in the doc. “I felt like I was maybe getting the other side of a relationship with a brother that I hadn’t had.”

Rob hadn’t even made it to his car from his audition for the Aaron Sorkin project before being offered a role — but he initially declined it. “They had called me to offer me the part at a tenth of my salary. Well, I couldn’t afford to do it,” he explained. “But I knew it was great. So I had to pass. And they saw every actor in the world.”

He continued: “They were already building the sets and about to start production. But they hadn’t found anybody to play Sam [Seaborn]. So the day before they started shooting, they came back to me at half my salary. I took it and was happy to do it, because sometimes you gotta take huge pay cuts because the work is so great.”

Rob appeared on the show from 1999 to 2003 before leaving.

“When people want to do a deep dive on The West Wing, I’m always reminded that nobody wants to hear that The Beatles hated each other. It all sounds so petty,” he explained. “What happened was everybody else eventually got raises and I didn’t. I went to [creator] Aaron Sorkin, and I said, ‘Look, I think it’s unfair that everybody else has been rewarded for the success of the show. But the last time you wrote an episode specific for me, I got nominated for an Emmy for it. If you will do that for me — two out of 22 episodes — I’ll stay.’ And he said, ‘I think you have pretty good stuff right now.’”

He concluded: “My kids were just getting old enough to start having relationships. And I was thinking, ‘How could I ever advocate for them to stand up for themselves, if there is somebody that looks like it’s the greatest thing that ever was, but behind closed doors, they don’t value you and maybe even treat you like s***? How could I ever empower my kids to walk away from that if I can’t do it myself?’”

Rob Lowe: The Outsider is streaming now on Netflix.
