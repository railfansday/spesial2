3 Best Movies Streaming For Free This Weekend (November 14-15)

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Tubi is a free streaming service with quite a few exciting movies of different genres for those who want to catch timely releases, especially if you want to prepare yourself for an upcoming theatrical release. On last weekend's Tubi movie recommendations, I talked about why users should consider checking out Bill Skarsgård's The Crow, the sci-fi disaster The Day After Tomorrow, and The Hunger Games: Catching Fire.

At the time of writing, according to data shared by FlixPatrol, the only project among last weekend's recommendations to appear on Tubi's Top 10 Movies list is The Crow, which currently stands as the 2nd most-watched film on the free streaming service. In fact, The Crow has climbed high on the streamer, going from 7th to near the top of the chart right before the new weekend.

The new movies I recommend watching for free on the streaming service come in wildly different genres. One of them is a drama that follows a celebrity many may know, as she deals with a complex relationship with a singer nearly everyone certainly has heard about. Another exciting option is a star-studded comedy to get viewers ready for Christmas.

The other movie that viewers should check out on Tubi this weekend is a dystopian sci-fi that is perfect to watch right before a major theatrical release next week that stands as one of the year's biggest cinematic events. These are the best movies to watch for free on Tubi this weekend:

All the movies on the list arrived on Tubi in November, and Priscilla comes as a timely release for viewers who want to know more about its lead stars given their recent ties to major franchises. After all, Jacob Elordi has frequently been rumored to be among the candidates for the next James Bond, even if the most recent lists do not feature the Euphoria star. As for Cailee Spaeny, she has been linked to Marvel's X-Men movie in multiple roles.

Priscilla is a biopic that has both of the actors at its center, with Elordi playing none other than rock icon Elvis Presley, while Spaeny gives life to Priscilla Presley, with the drama being all about their complicated relationship. Priscilla is worth watching because it shows a different version of Elvis, more flawed and complex, through the eyes of the one who loved him, but grew to not recognize herself as she stood in the singer's orbit.

The Night Before has just entered Tubi's chart, with the movie ranking 10th as one of the films that are most growing in the United States. I felt it would be interesting to include it as we prepare for the Christmas season next month, and there are families already on a holiday film binge. Well, The Night Before is definitely not for viewers of all ages, as the comedy is R-rated, but it is perfect for a laugh in the Christmas lead-up.

The movie follows Seth Rogen, Joseph Gordon-Levitt, and Anthony Mackie as they play a trio of friends who started a tradition of coming together every Christmas Eve, now reuniting for one final holiday as they try to get into an exclusive party, but the night does not play out as expected, with a series of challenges, reveals, and hilarious moments. The Night Before promises good laughs with a star-studded cast that also includes names like James Franco, Nathan Fielder, Lizzy Caplan, and more. It is a funny, heartwarming, and energetic movie to prepare for Christmas and remember what friends are for.

Finally, The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes is the perfect movie to watch this weekend on Tubi before the new release in the dystopian sci-fi franchise. The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping will arrive in theaters on November 20, making this the last weekend to watch the only other prequel in the hit movie franchise thus far, with The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes allowing viewers to understand what to expect.

The dystopian sci-fi movie serves as the origin story for Coriolanus Snow, who was played by Donald Sutherland in the original The Hunger Games movies, with Tom Blyth playing the younger version of the character. With a lot of ambition and something to prove, Snow is a fascinating character in the film, and his relationship with Rachel Zegler's Lucy Gray Baird is simply electric. This is a layered, action-packed, and shocking sci-fi event that ends the weekend on Tubi with a shot of adrenaline.
