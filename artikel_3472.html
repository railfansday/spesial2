GTA 6 Fans Are Hissing Mad About Dolores the Snake

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

After more than a decade of waiting, Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally here. Perhaps most surprising of all, it's actually living up to the world's sky-high expectations, and players everywhere are sharing their delight and awe at the game's immaculate attention to detail on social media. But there's one group of players who aren't so thrilled about the level of realism in GTA 6: snake owners.

Over on Reddit, a post from the r/mildlyinfuriating subreddit about GTA 6 has garnered more than 92,000 likes in just a few days. The user was eager to share the game with a friend from southern Florida. However, rather than being dazzled by GTA 6's intricate attention to detail, the friend was horrified by Dolores the snake's habitat in Jason and Lucia's Key Lento bungalow.

The redditor wrote that their friend "went on this big long 10 minute rant about how the people that designed this game are idiots and terrible people and how they’re gonna cause people to go out and buy snakes and mistreat them and abandon them. Apparently she’s pissed about the lack of a heat lamp and a water dish. And the fact that the tank doesn’t open the right way and the fact that he doesn’t use tongs to feed the thing."

Ultimately, the dismayed snake fan concluded, “They [Rockstar] spent 13 years on this thing and did no fucking research.” If you've actually spent any time at all with GTA 6, you'll realize this is a pretty wild exaggeration. Clearly, this team did all kinds of research, whether its on the behaviors of the American alligator, vintage T-shirts, or telenovelas.

"I find it rather immersive that a Florida scumbag would have a cool snake poorly kept," observed one commenter.

In keeping with the spirit of the subreddit, other GTA 6 players shared their own mildly infuriating experiences with the game, mostly surrounding the home repair sequence in the opening hours. "If you think that is bad, you should try showing a roofer how Jason fixes the roof," wrote one user. "Im not even a roofer and I was complaining about most the home repairs, bro but some floor protection before you paint, what about the light switch 😭," said another.

While GTA 6 might be a remarkably accurate girlfriend simulator, it is sadly not the best example of responsible pet ownership. Given the exhaustive level of detail in other aspects of the game, it is a little surprising that these small details about Dolores's enclosure weren't considered. Then again, this is also a game that forces you to use a left-to-right slider instead of a numerical keypad at the ATM, so the immersion here isn't 100% consistent.
