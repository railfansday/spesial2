Silver Palace, the Belle Époque-style ARPG, sets release date for this winter

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

With so many ARPGs on storefronts these days, a new one can seem a little redundant. But as the debut entry in the genre from Elementa Games, Silver Palace is already shaping up to be an exciting new release. And soon you'll be able to judge for yourself, as Silver Palace is confirmed to land on December 14th for iOS and Android!

Inspired by a mix of Victorian and Belle Époque fashion, Silver Palace is set in a sprawling metropolis called Silvernia, which is undergoing an industrial revolution powered by... you guessed it: Silverium. This means magic and technology intertwine, with brawling melee action accompanied by high-calibre weaponry.

Having played Silver Palace myself, I feel confident in saying that its combat and exploration were extremely engaging. Of course, that was only a vertical slice of everything that's available, so the whole game may hold up very differently.

But overall, for Elementa, Silver Palace is shaping up to be an exciting debut, with an interesting world and exciting gameplay. So keep your eyes peeled for when Silver Palace lands December 14th, and dive into the action and intrigue of Silvernia!
