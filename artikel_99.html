Mexican drug lord Felix Gallardo dies at 80

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Felix Gallardo was considered to be the architect of Mexico's modern drug-trafficking trade. He died of multiple organ failure at the age of 80.

Miguel Angel Felix Gallardo — known as the "godfather" of Mexico's modern drug trade and a notorious trafficker himself — died in a hospital on Sunday. He was 80.

"After remaining under observation with a guarded prognosis, he died on November 15 at the medical facility" after "he suffered multiple organ failure," said a statement from the security ministry of the state of Jalisco, where he was being held.

Having founded the Guadalajara cartel, one of Mexico's first major drug syndicate which would later birth the Sinaloa cartel, Gallardo had been imprisoned since 1989 and was transferred to a private hospital two days ago.

He was arrested and sentenced to 40 years in prison on drug-trafficking and weapons charges tied to the cartel. While already behind bars, he faced a seperate prosecution for the kidnapping and murder of a US drug-enforcement agent. This earned him an addition 37-year prison sentence and his reputation as Mexico's most notorious trafficker.

Felix Gallardo had been an inmate in a maximum-security prison in western Jalisco state even after his lawyers repeatedly sought house arrest on the grounds of his deteriorating health.

Gallardo was born in 1946 in the Northwestern state of Sinaloa, which would later become synonymous with narco-trafficking.

He initially worked as a police officer and bodyguard for a local governer but soon transformed into a power criminal, known as the "boss of bosses."

Gallardo, along with others, set up the Guadalajara cartel becoming the first to move cocaine to the US on a massive scale. He was also the first to forge an alliance with Colombian drug cartels including Pablo Escobar's Medellin cartel.

In the 1970s, Gallardo rose to prominance as his narcotics empire expanded on the back of bribed law enforcers and the use of light aircrafts to move drugs across the continent.

His rise and fall as a Mexican drug lord was ortrayed on screen by Mexican actor Diego Luna in the 2018 Netflix hit series 'Narcos: Mexico.'

In his final years, Gallardo suffered poor health and was blind in one eye, practically deaf and unable to walk on his own. He maintained that he was innocent.
