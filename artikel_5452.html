PFN and SEMIFIVE Collaborate to Develop the MN-Core L2000 Series of Next-Generation AI Inference Chips

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

CHICAGO, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Preferred Networks, Inc. (PFN) and SEMIFIVE Inc. (SEMIFIVE) today announced a collaboration to develop the MN-Core L2000 Series*, PFN's next-generation of chips designed specifically for AI inference.

The MN-Core L2000 Series is the next-generation successor to the MN-Core L1000 Series of AI inference chips, which is scheduled for release in 2027. The new series is planned to deliver more than twice the memory bandwidth of the MN-Core L1000 Series to enable faster and more efficient AI inference infrastructure. The collaboration is already underway, with tape-out (completing the chip design and handing over the final design data to the manufacturer) scheduled in the first quarter of 2027.

The MN-Core L Series (www.mn-core.com) is a family of AI chips that PFN is developing with a focus on memory bandwidth, a key determinant of generative AI inference performance. By vertically stacking logic chips based on MN-Core architecture, which combines high computational performance and low power consumption, with DRAM (dynamic random access memory) in a 3D structure, MN-Core L Series achieves substantially higher memory bandwidth than GPUs and other chips used for AI processing. This 3D-stacked structure enables high-speed AI inference and improves power efficiency for applications such as AI agents, providing a new option for AI infrastructure.

Since beginning development of MN-Core in 2016, PFN has spent more than a decade refining the technology through its use in the company's own services and products. Its proprietary MN-Core architecture combines high computational performance and low power consumption. Supercomputers powered by the first generation of MN-Core have topped the Green500 ranking of the world's most energy-efficient supercomputers three times. PFN is now focusing on developing the MN-Core L series, designed specifically for AI inference. For the MN-Core L2000 Series, PFN is responsible for processor architecture and logical design, leveraging the knowledge and expertise gained through its previous development work.

As a turnkey solution provider for custom semiconductors, SEMIFIVE delivers design implementation and comprehensive supply chain management for the MN-Core L2000 Series, powered by its advanced 3D-IC design solution. "The MN-Core L2000 Series is the next generation of AI chips, designed to deliver even greater memory bandwidth than the MN-Core L1000 Series, taking another step toward overcoming bandwidth limitations, a major bottleneck for generative AI," said Daisuke Okanohara, Co-Founder and CEO of PFN.
