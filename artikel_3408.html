Rebecca Yarros' New Adventure Romance 'Peculiar Stars' Releases Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Just two months after releasing Threshing Day, Rebecca Yarros is back—though not in the way you might expect. The bestselling author of the Empyrean books just released a new adventure romance novel that has nothing to do with her ongoing series.

Yarros’ new novel, Peculiar Stars, is a slow-burn romance epic that caught the attention of Apple TV before it was even released, earning Yarros a deal to develop the novel into a TV series. Though it's not the long-anticipated fourth novel in the Empyrean series, and it's far from Yarros' better-known romantasy fare, fans are already more than happy with this departure.

Unlike Yarros’ series, which fall squarely into the romantasy genre, Peculiar Stars is closer to adventure romance than outright fantasy. It takes a classic adventure novel trope—a protagonist marooned on an island after a shipwreck—and infuses it with plenty of Yarros’ signature steamy slow-burn romance. Think Robinson Crusoe meets The Notebook.

Peculiar Stars follows Callista Moran, a not-so-happily engaged sailor who, after a cyclone, finds herself marooned on a faraway island with her fiancé’s dashing Army medic cousin in tow. With little hope of returning to their lives, this unlikely pair gives in to their unexpected new bond.

When they’re suddenly rescued, and their return becomes the subject of a media circus, they’ll have to decide not just whether their newfound connection is worth giving up the lives they left behind, but whether they can make it off the island at all.

Peculiar Stars has already received rave reviews from critics and fans alike. The book already has an average star rating of 4.52 on Goodreads, while Library Journal gave it 5 stars and called it “so well-written that readers will race to the end.” BookTrib called Peculiar Stars an “epic romance” that “makes us feel all the feelings,” and noted that though the book is unusually long for the genre at 653 pages, Yarros more than justifies the length by filling it with twists and turns.

As for the Empyrean series, there is still no word from Yarros on when exactly Book 4 will hit shelves. However, though Peculiar Stars has no connection to that series, fans shouldn’t fret. This is far from the first time that Yarros has taken a break from Empyrean to publish a standalone novel.

Yarros has published four Empyrean books in as many years: Fourth Wing (2023), Iron Flame (2023), Onyx Storm (2025), and the short story collection Threshing Day (2026). In that time, she’s also published three standalone contemporary romance novels: In the Likely Event (2023), Variation (2024), and Peculiar Stars (2026). Suffice it to say, she’s prolific within and outside of her hit series.

Until then, fans still have another Empyrean ​​​​​​release to look forward to: the graphic novel adaptation of Fourth Wing, releasing in April 2027. Plus, Yarros has already teased that she began drafting the fourth Empyrean novel back in March, meaning it’s certainly on the horizon.
