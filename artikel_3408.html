Chris Pratt’s ‘Reacher’ Replacement Officially Gets New Prequel

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

2026 has been another successful year for Chris Pratt, who has starred in some big-budget projects both on TV and streaming. At the start of the year, Pratt worked with sci-fi legend Rebecca Ferguson for the Amazon-backed AI thriller, Mercy. While the film bombed at the box office, it did go on to become one of the most-watched titles of the year on Prime Video — redemption comes in all shapes and sizes. It didn’t take Pratt long to find box office glory, though, as he reunited with Anya Taylor-Joy as the duo reprised their roles in The Super Mario Galaxy Movie, which was the first feature film of 2026 to gross over $1 billion at the box office. He can also be seen in the second season of The Terminal List on Prime Video.

The first season of The Terminal List premiered on Prime Video all the way back in 2022, alongside the first season of Reacher (starring Alan Ritchson). To put things into perspective, Prime Video has released three full seasons of Reacher in the time that it took to make the second season of The Terminal List. Not long after the premiere of The Terminal List Season 2, Prime Video announced it was bringing back Chris Pratt to adapt a new novel in Jack Carr’s series, Savage Son. This afternoon, news broke that Prime Video is also working with creator Jack Carr to develop a new prequel project around his novel, Cry Havoc. The book centers around James Reece's father, Tom Reece, who is briefly played by David Keeley in an episode of the flagship series. It's set during the Vietnam war.

The first season of The Terminal List bombed with critics, earning a poor 42% on the aggregate site Rotten Tomatoes, but it was a smash hit with fans, registering a strong 94% on the audience-driven Popcornmeter. Season 2 continued the trend of a strong reception from fans, but it began to win over critics, at least enough to score a solid 75% on Rotten Tomatoes. The Terminal List Season 2 may have lost its streaming crown thanks to the release of The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power, but it’s still a force on Prime Video.

Check out the first two seasons of The Terminal List on Prime Video and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage of future projects like Savage Son and Cry Havoc.
