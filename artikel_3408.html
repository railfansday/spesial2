New Legend Of Zelda 40th Anniversary Collection Officially Confirmed For 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Legend of Zelda may have celebrated its 40th anniversary in 2026, but that doesn't mean the festivities won't continue in 2027. Thanks to the latest financial results from Nintendo, we have official confirmation that additional 40th anniversary releases will happen next year, and they will stretch beyond the movie and merchandise we know about already. For Zelda fans who are already looking ahead to see what's next for the beloved franchise, this is great news.

This year is already ending on a strong note for The Legend of Zelda. The Ocarina of Time remake launched on Switch 2 earlier this month, becoming the best-selling physical game of the year and receiving accolades from critics and players alike. This year has also seen the release of some music collections and apparel over the summer, as well as a new LEGO set back in March. Thankfully, it looks like 2027 might be an even bigger year for The Legend of Zelda and its fans.

Nintendo has shared its second quarter financial results and corporate management policy for 2026, and within the 47-page document, Shuntaro Furukawa, President of Nintendo, outlines some surprising news for 40th anniversary releases for next year, even though the 40th anniversary is technically over as soon as the calendar year moves into 2027.

Furukawa states that going into next year, Nintendo "will pursue a variety of initiatives to celebrate the 40th anniversary of The Legend of Zelda. In addition to games, we will expand opportunities for consumers to experience its world through the movie, music, character merchandise, and more."

Furthermore, Nintendo "will make the series even more appealing by offering different ways to enjoy it, both for those who cherish its earlier titles and for those discovering it for the first time.”

While the overview doesn't mention specific releases, it at least confirms that more 40th anniversary items are coming in 2027. And it sounds like that will include more than next year's movie and merchandise. Of course, beyond a new mainline Legend of Zelda game, there's at least a couple of big releases that would get Zelda fans hyped.

For years now, fans have wanted HD remasters of Twilight Princess and Wind Waker on the Switch (and eventually the Switch 2). Thus far, neither game has gotten a re-release since the HD remasters arrived on the Wii U. While Wind Waker at least showed up on the GameCube library for Nintendo Switch Online, it wasn't necessarily what most fans were hoping for. Considering all of this, the timing would be right to release both HD remasters in 2027 as part of the continued 40th anniversary celebrations.

Right now, we don't have any specifics on what else Nintendo is planning for The Legend of Zelda in 2027. This report was for the fiscal year ending in March 2027, so except to see another for Q3 and then Q4 in the near future. Hopefully, we'll be given more specifics when those meetings take place. Perhaps fans will finally get to see Wind Waker HD and Twilight Princess HD on Switch 2 as well.

The Legend of Zelda franchise follows the adventures of Link, an elf-like Hylian, and Princess Zelda as they protect the land of Hyrule from the evil warlord-turned-demon king Ganon. The series is known for its mix of action, adventure, and puzzle-solving elements, often revolving around the collection of the Triforce, a powerful relic left by the goddesses who created Hyrule.
