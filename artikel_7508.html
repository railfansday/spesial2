Inland Empire Man Pleads Guilty to Obtaining $2.1 Million in COVID-19 Jobless Benefits Using Stolen IDs and Prisoner Names

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A San Bernardino County man pleaded guilty today to fraudulently obtaining more than $2.1 million in COVID-19 pandemic relief funds by submitting 124 fraudulent applications for unemployment insurance (UI) benefits in the names of identity theft victims and California state prisoners who were ineligible for jobless benefits.
