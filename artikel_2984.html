Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Pricing reform and AI efficiency drove margins as smaller clients pulled back.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 25, 2026 at 1:37PM EST

Kanzhun (BZ +12.56%) management reported a period of transition in its monetization model and user expansion strategies. The company implemented a pricing reform in Tier 1 cities, replacing free job listings with a paid structure to improve listing quality and user satisfaction. Management stated that blue-collar workers and small-to-medium enterprises in lower-tier cities remain primary growth drivers, while manufacturing demand showed resilience compared to other lagging sectors like automobile and retail. Despite a reported net loss resulting from a noncash fair value adjustment on an investment, the company maintained a strong cash position and authorized an additional USD 500 million in share repurchases, reflecting a commitment to shareholder returns.
