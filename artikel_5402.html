American Pride Bank Opens Winston-Salem Office in the City's Historic Innovation Quarter

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Entrepreneur-founded bank continues its planned expansion across the Southeast

WINSTON-SALEM, N.C., Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- American Pride Bank is celebrating the grand opening of its Winston-Salem office at 450 N. Patterson Ave., Suite 200. The office sits in the Bailey South building in Innovation Quarter, across from Bailey Park and steps from the historic smokestacks of the power plant that once ran the city's R.J. Reynolds factories.

"Winston-Salem has always been a city of builders, and those smokestacks are a daily reminder of what ambitious people can create," said Dominik Mjartan, President and Chief Executive Officer of American Pride Bank. "The people writing this city's next chapter deserve a bank that will keep up with them and be a forward-thinking financial partner every step of the way. That is why we are here."

American Pride Bank is built for business owners building something of their own.

The Winston-Salem team is led by Market President Steve Overcash, a banker known for building relationships based on trust and service. The team works with business owners across the Triad, including manufacturers, service companies, and restaurant and hotel groups, along with the families behind them. Each client works with one banker who knows their name and their business, and who can make decisions quickly.

"Entrepreneurs made Winston-Salem. People like R.J. Reynolds and the Hanes brothers came here in the 1870s and started companies that turned a small Piedmont town into one of the South's great industrial cities," said Overcash. "Today, those same factory buildings are full of founders, researchers and growing businesses. They want a banker who shows up, listens and follows through, and that is how we plan to earn our place in this community."

For American Pride Bank, each new market is a deliberate choice. From its roots in Georgia, American Pride Bank opened a branch in Charlotte in July, opened its doors in Winston-Salem in early October and opened its first South Carolina office in Greenville on Oct. 13. The bank has also opened offices in Tampa and Sarasota, Florida and is exploring opportunities in the Raleigh-Cary area. Over the past year, parent company American Bancorp, Inc. raised $186 million in new capital from a strong base of shareholders and reached $1.3 billion in total assets, guided by careful planning at every step.

"There is so much energy and potential in Winston-Salem," said Michael Sharpton, Executive Vice President and Chief Growth & Revenue Officer. "Our team is here to get to know local business owners, understand their goals and help them through the hard parts of running a business."

The Winston-Salem office is open Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. To meet the team, call 336-422-0164 or visit www.americanpride.bank.

About American Bancorp, Inc. & American Pride BankAmerican Pride Bank is a public benefit corporation, mission-driven community bank and national mortgage leader serving ambitious individuals, entrepreneurs and growing businesses. Founded in 2007 by first-generation Americans and entrepreneurs, the bank offers high-touch, flexible banking that creates pathways to generational wealth.
