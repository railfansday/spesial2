Europe in shock after Doué’s injury, the English press dismantles JJ Gabriel’s entourage

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Last night, PSG picked up another victory in Nice, but no one was really talking about the result after the match. The reason is obviously the serious injury of Désiré Doué. While he had just scored to give the Parisians the advantage, the French international collapsed alone after twisting his right knee. The images are terrible: Doué screams in pain, hides his face and finally leaves the pitch in tears, on a stretcher. But what is also striking this morning is that Doué’s injury is shaking up the European press. In Spain, A.S. explains that “his knee set off all the alarms at PSG“, while Sport title : “Doué’s terrible injury slows down the party“. Mundo Deportivo also mentions “a Parisian victory overshadowed by worry“. In Italy, La Gazzetta dello Sport speaks of a real shock and insists on the parallel with Kvaratskhelia’s injury. Even the English press returns to these images of Doué being evacuated in tears. An emotion which therefore goes far beyond French borders.

A controversy is causing a lot of talk in England: the management of JJ Gabriel’s career. THE Daily Mail publishes a particularly virulent column against Ali Barat, the agent of the young 16-year-old prodigy, announced very close to Real Madrid after his departure from Manchester United. At the heart of the criticism, a promotional video published to announce the arrival of Gabriel in his agency. We see in particular a reference to the Ballon d’Or, before the young player declares on camera: “I am epic“, in other words “I am exceptional“. For the English newspaper, this communication is completely disproportionate for a boy who has not yet played a single minute among the professionals. Email also questions the role of those around him in his departure from Manchester United and the risks of such early exposure.

In Italy, Donyell Malen continues to panic the counters. The Dutch striker absolutely crushed Parma with a quadruple in Roma’s 5-1 win. Four shots, four goals, and now sixteen goals in fifteen matches. Gian Piero Gasperini’s team also has 36 goals after twelve days of Serie A, its best total at this stage since the 1950-1951 season. Enough to start dreaming very seriously of the title. Malen has simply been on cloud nine since his arrival in Italy, a sparkling form which is enough to worry PSG, three days before the clash between the two teams in the Champions League.
