AC Milan: Adrien Rabiot reveals that Ruben Amorim wanted to extend him after a week

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Author of a thunderous start to the season with AC Milan with already 5 goals and 5 assists, the French midfielder has established himself as one of the bosses of the Rossonero locker room. Rabiot highlighted his role as an example for the young Lombard guard as well as the immediate confidence shown in him by his coach Ruben Amorim from the summer resumption at the microphone of Téléfoot : “my successful start to the season with my good statistics, it’s also good to have players who can be decisive without being attacking. It also encourages those around you. We have quite young players at Milan. It’s good that they also have everyday examples. I had been back from vacation for a week, he was already talking to me about extension, I told him: “coach easy (laughing)”. But obviously it’s important for a player to have that confidence. The coach is counting on us. That he has a very good image and that we feel important”.

At the dawn of the home stretch of his career, the Milanese executive also draws his daily inspiration from Luka Modrić, still performing well at 41: “Playing with Modrić is an example to follow. He is 41 years old, he is still here training every day, he has had a huge career. He trains like he’s 21, it’s something incredible. We are lucky even when we move forward to be able to savor certain things. We must take example. The more you advance in your career, the more you realize that you are getting closer to the end and that’s when you start to enjoy it. As I grow, I try to savor it so that I will have something left in a few years. »
