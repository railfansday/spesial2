Nikki Glaser to Develop Scripted Series at Netflix, Inks Partnership With New Netflix Is a Joke Studios

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Nikki Glaser has formed a major partnership with Netflix.

The Emmy-nominated comedian will develop a scripted series to star in, as well as deliver two new stand-up specials, for the streamer as part of its newly announced Netflix Is a Joke Studios.

Glaser is the first talent to join forces with NIAJ Studios, which is an in-house incubator and production studio that aims to develop projects with stand-up talent beyond the stage, crafting scripted comedies and original formats. The studio, which borrows its name from the streamer’s biennial comedy festival in Los Angeles, will partner with both established stars and emerging comedic voices, Netflix said in a press release. Few details were shared about Glaser’s scripted series, but Jessica Elbaum and Will Ferrell’s Gloria Sanchez Productions will serve as an executive producer.

Glaser said in a statement, “Netflix gave me some of my earliest and biggest opportunities in comedy, so I couldn’t be more excited to begin this next phase of my career with them.”

“Great comedy starts with an unmistakable voice, and few comedians have one as fully formed as Nikki Glaser’s,” said Tracey Pakosta, Netflix’s VP of comedy series. “She’s fearless, funny and disarmingly real, which makes her the ideal first partner for the NIAJ Studios vision. We can’t wait to see the world she’ll build.”

Robbie Praw, Netflix’s stand-up boss, added, “I first met Nikki Glaser in a small comedy club 20 years ago and have had a front-row seat to her rise ever since. When Netflix’s ‘The Roast of Tom Brady’ sent her career into overdrive in 2024, we watched audiences embrace what many of us have seen for years. She’s one of the sharpest comedic minds working today, with instincts to build something lasting well beyond stand-up. Netflix Is a Joke has always celebrated comedy in all its forms, and NIAJ Studios is the next step in our commitment to this community. We’re honored to be in Nikki’s corner as she builds what’s next.”

Indeed, Glaser’s career took off after her scathing appearance at Netflix’s 2024 “Roast of Tom Brady,” which led to her recurring gig hosting the Golden Globes. Glaser was a mainstay of the Comedy Central roasts before she released her first Netflix special, “Bangin’,” in 2019. She then went on to put out specials with HBO and Hulu but has continued to expand her relationship with Netflix. She voices a main character in the upcoming Netflix animated comedy “Steps” and she stars in the Eva Longoria-directed film “The Fifth Wheel,” which premieres on the streamer in 2027.

Glaser is represented by WME, Brillstein Entertainment Partners and Ginsburg Daniels Kallis LLP.
