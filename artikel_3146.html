Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Net sales fell 13% as divestitures weighed, while management outlines its fiscal 2027 pricing plans.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 20, 2026 at 12:39PM EST

Post (POST +2.75%) reported fourth quarter and full-year results for fiscal 2026, meeting Adjusted EBITDA expectations despite headwinds from increased freight and diesel costs. Management reported that the company is focusing on execution in fiscal 2027, with earnings momentum expected to build in the second half of the year as pricing actions and productivity initiatives offset inflation timing. The company completed several divestitures during the year, including the Crystal Farms business and the 8th Avenue pasta business, while simultaneously integrating the remaining pet food and private label assets. Management indicated that the company maintains significant financial flexibility, with a focus on prioritizing debt reduction while remaining opportunistic regarding share repurchases and potential acquisitions.
