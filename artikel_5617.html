Zoox Expands Robotaxi Service in Las Vegas

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Zoox, one of the three leading robotaxi companies in the United States so far, has just expanded commercial robotaxi service in Sin City, the city of Las Vegas. “We are expanding our service beyond the Strip into Downtown and the Arts District, while continuing to add access to even more major resort and entertainment venues across Las Vegas. This follows recent service launches at destinations like Harry Reid International Airport, Sphere, and T-Mobile Arena,” Zoox shares. (Ironic that service has expanded to Harry Reid International Airport just after the huge blue tsunami swept across the United States in the midterm elections.)

Zoox keeps going: “The next evolution of our robotaxi has now made its debut in Las Vegas, introducing the lighter interior and increased comfort to riders. We have also expanded our service hours to be 6:00 a.m.-3:00 a.m., seven days a week, giving riders more opportunity to ride with Zoox.

“The latest information about where riders can travel to/from can be found in the Zoox app.”

The company also notes that it launched limited robotaxi service in Las Vegas a little more than a year ago. While it’s still not close to huge, it has expanded greatly since launch and continues to display incremental progress. “On September 10, 2025, we officially launched the Zoox service on and around the Las Vegas Strip. Rides are available through the Zoox app, which can be downloaded on both iOS and Android devices. This moment marked Zoox as the first company in history to provide a fully autonomous ride-hailing service in a purpose-built robotaxi,” the company adds.

Here’s more info on how to ride with Zoox in Las Vegas: “After downloading the Zoox app, riders can choose from a range of destinations on the Strip, into Downtown, and the Arts District. We offer premier, dedicated pickup and drop-off spots at key partner destinations, including Sphere, T-Mobile Arena, Resorts World, AREA15, and others.

“At Harry Reid International Airport, our locations are conveniently situated near the Terminal 1 departures and baggage claim at both Terminal 1 and 3. For all other destinations, Zoox picks up and drops off riders in the designated rideshare areas.

“To help riders identify their robotaxi, riders can use FindMyZoox, a feature in the app where the robotaxi uses distinct lighting and sound cues to help riders identify their specific robotaxi in crowded pickup zones. The app will also include the vehicle’s license plate number, estimated pickup times, real-time arrival updates, and post-ride summaries with feedback options. If riders need support before, during, or after their ride, they can contact a member of our remote Zoox support team through the app or via the “Help” button located on the rider screen within the robotaxi.”

One thing that stands out with Zoox is that it uses a purpose-built vehicle rather than adapting a normal consumer car for robotaxi use. Also, that vehicle is quite a bit different from what we see from others. “Our purpose-built robotaxi creates a fundamentally different cabin experience: carriage-style seating where four passengers face each other, minimal screen distraction, and space to unwind or connect. Whether you’re catching up with friends after a show, celebrating a night out, or just taking in the city, the journey itself becomes part of the experience. That’s what riders in Las Vegas are choosing when they ride Zoox.”

2026 has been a year of significant robotaxi news. One might say it’s been the year of the robotaxi. “The autonomous vehicle industry has made remarkable strides this year, bringing us closer to a future of safer, more accessible mobility. With the launch of our fully driverless ride-hailing service using a purposefully designed robotaxi, we’re thrilled to be part of this groundbreaking journey,” adds Aicha Evans, CEO of Zoox. “Las Vegas is a city famous for unforgettable moments, and it is the ideal location for our debut. Zoox is about transforming the entire ride-hailing experience, making every ride a delightful experience.”
