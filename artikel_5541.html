Atlantic Data Security Partners with LogSeam to Power Harbour AI SOC Managed Service

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

LOWELL, Mass., Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Atlantic Data Security (ADS), a leading cybersecurity solutions and services provider, today announced a strategic partnership with LogSeam to power its Harbour AI SOC offering. The partnership combines LogSeam's agentic AI technology with ADS's cybersecurity expertise to automate routine security operations, accelerate threat investigations, and help organizations make more effective use of their security resources.

As security teams face growing volumes of alerts, increasingly complex threats, and rising operational costs, analysts are spending significant time on repetitive tasks such as alert triage, investigation, and documentation. Harbour AI SOC addresses these challenges by leveraging agentic AI to streamline day-to-day security operations, allowing analysts to focus on more complex threats and strategic risk reduction.

"Security teams don't need another tool generating more alerts. They need the capacity to investigate and resolve threats more effectively," said Mark Ostrowski, CTO of Atlantic Data Security. "With Harbour AI SOC, we're leveraging agentic AI to handle routine investigations and response workflows, freeing analysts to focus on the critical decisions that require their expertise. Our partnership with LogSeam combines powerful technology with experienced security professionals to make AI-driven security operations practical, scalable, and effective."

Unlike traditional automation, which relies on predefined rules and playbooks, Harbour AI SOC uses agentic workflows to automatically create cases, correlate security data, investigate suspicious activity, document findings, and recommend remediation actions. These capabilities operate within defined permissions and human oversight requirements, ensuring security teams maintain control over critical decisions.

The offering also addresses the rising costs associated with security data management. By separating log retention from analytics licensing, LogSeam enables organizations to retain more security telemetry without incurring the high costs commonly associated with traditional SIEM platforms. Customers maintain ownership of their data and control over where it resides.

"Security operations can't scale without efficiency, and the cost of data has been a major constraint. LogSeam removes that barrier," said Dan Wiley, CEO of LogSeam. "Clients retain ownership of all their data, while agentic analysts handle repetitive work at scale. Harbour AI SOC, powered by LogSeam, adds the expertise needed to put that capability to work. Clients get the system and the service, not another tool they have to staff."

Designed to integrate with existing security technologies, Harbour AI SOC combines intelligent automation with ADS's managed security expertise to improve operational efficiency without requiring organizations to replace their current security investments.

Atlantic Data Security is a leading provider of comprehensive cybersecurity solutions dedicated to safeguarding organizations against evolving digital threats. With a deep commitment to excellence, its expert team delivers tailored security strategies, advanced technologies, and proactive threat detection and mitigation.

Atlantic Data Security specializes in threat intelligence, risk assessment, and compliance management, helping clients meet industry requirements while strengthening data protection and overall security posture. The company empowers organizations to navigate an increasingly complex cybersecurity landscape with confidence, strengthen their defenses, and protect critical digital assets.

LogSeam provides a security operations platform that combines security data management with agentic AI capabilities. By separating log retention from analytics licensing and enabling AI-driven investigation and response workflows, LogSeam helps organizations manage security telemetry costs, retain visibility, and improve the efficiency of security operations.
