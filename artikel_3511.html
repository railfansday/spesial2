10 Netflix Detective Shows With Brilliant Mysteries, Ranked

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Most great detective shows do far more than just introduce a crime and follow the investigators as they dig deep into it. The best quality series of the genre wield mysteries that are carefully constructed, filled with unexpected connections, hidden motives, suspicious characters, and small details that eventually become so important they cause viewers to think twice. On the Netflix platform, there are plenty of excellent detective series that take different, more unique approaches to their mysteries as they deliver quite the entertaining investigative story.

Shows like the thrilling anime series PLUTO, which takes the detective genre into a futuristic world where intelligent robots and mankind coexist, and Dept. Q, which centers around a detective and his unconventional team, are just two excellent examples of how detective series can create brilliant central mysteries through complicated investigations, clever twists, and emotional discoveries. Featured on this list are absolutely brilliant detective shows that host masterful central mysteries that prove how rewarding investing in a series found in the genre can be when carefully crafted.

This detective series is one of the most entertaining in Netflix’s brilliant catalog. The British series The Stranger follows a man named Adam Price (Richard Armitage) who is approached by a mysterious woman with a devastating secret about his wife, Corinne Price (Dervla Kirwan).

The Stranger is a masterful, constantly expanding mystery series that offers audiences a fantastic puzzle to unravel. The Harlan Coben mystery, unlike some of his later adaptations, brilliantly balances genuine police investigation with domestic mystery. Considering the show's efforts to explore the consequences of secrets being exposed, The Stranger's moments of blatant uncertainty combined with a steadily growing investigation make it a truly compelling detective series to dive into for anyone looking for a great mystery-driven watch.

Black Spot is an atmospheric international detective show that cleverly uses an isolated French town and its surrounding forest to create a mystery filled with unsettling possibilities. Set in Villefranche, a remote community known for its unusually plentiful murders, the series centers around Laurène Weiss (Suliane Brahim), a police chief who oversees the community.

Black Spot is honestly a truly brilliant pick for this list because it’s a fantastic live-action blend of folk horror and detective noir. The series is careful in leaving questions unanswered despite its offering of uncanny details, which both frustrates and captivates viewers in its mystery. Black Spot may be a pretty unusual detective series, but it stands as effortlessly brilliant because it successfully mixes the uncertainty of supernatural horror with conventional crime-solving, marking it a show especially compelling for viewers who enjoy central mysteries that do so much more than just follow familiar rules.

​​​​​​​This crime drama is an exceptional example of great South Korean detective shows. The 2020 Netflix series The Good Detective focuses on young investigator Oh Ji-hyuk (Jang Seung-jo) and veteran detective Kang Do-chang (Son Hyun-joo) as they revisit a murder case whose conviction may have been devastatingly wrong.

The Good Detective does more than rely entirely on elaborate criminal schemes and shocking murders; the series also dives deep into the complicated process of determining what actually happens when powerful people have reasons to hide the truth. The show’s appeal is definitely its narrative complexity without losing any moral clarity. The Good Detective is an extremely engaging watch, one that delivers clear detective work, strong characters, and ethical ambiguity, making it a true Netflix gem in international crime shows.

PLUTO is an anime series that is often praised as one of the most imaginative detective mysteries on Netflix for its compelling combination of profound questions about artificial intelligence and humanity with the suspense of a serial murder investigation. Set in a future where sophisticated robots live alongside humans, the Japanese series follows Gesicht (Shinshu Fuji), a Europol investigator who is assigned to look into a baffling sequence of killings targeting the world’s most advanced robots and people connected to them.

PLUTO is a mystery-centered show that approaches the detective genre quite differently from other similar series of the genre on the Netflix platform. With both sophistication and emotional depth, the show treats its futuristic setting as far more than a simple spectacle, instead using its medium and sci-fi elements to make the core mystery all the more meaningful and incredibly chilling. PLUTO defiantly earns its place on any list of great watches that stands strong as one of Netflix’s most impressive animated thrillers.

This extremely unsettling Netflix detective thriller artfully builds an elaborate murder mystery around an apparently innocent object. The international watch, The Chestnut Man centers on Europol investigator Mark Hess (Mikkel Boe Følsgaard) and Copenhagen detective Naia Thulin (Danica Curcic), who are thrown together after a brutal murder is coupled with a tiny figure made from chestnuts.

The Chestnut Man is frequently praised as one of the fiercest modern Nordic-noir mysteries. The series is beloved for its puzzles, atmosphere, major surprises, investigative partnership, and signature clue that is both meaningful and memorable. The Chestnut Man is one of the streaming platform’s most effective series, as it uses the smallest hints to slowly reveal the disturbing scale of the larger investigation, making it a rather captivating watch with its strong atmosphere and precise storytelling that rightfully dubs it a Netflix standout with a genuinely brilliant mystery.

The Sinner is genuinely one of Netflix’s most fascinating detective shows that approaches criminal investigations from a truly different perspective. The mystery drama focuses on Harry Ambrose (Bill Pullman), a detective who is less interested in whether someone committed a crime than in understanding why an apparently inexplicable crime happened in the first place.

The Sinner isn’t the kind of detective series that begins with the usual question of “who did it?”; instead, it offers audiences something much closer to the answer, evoking the question of why in its viewers and deepening its central mystery. With its unusual approach to its genre, it completely transforms familiar murder investigation elements into complex psychological mysteries where understanding human behavior becomes just as important as uncovering evidence, making it one of the few thrillers to stand out among Netflix’s ultimate bangers with fantastic mysteries at their core.

This sci-fi drama turns a single murder investigation into an extraordinary mystery spanning more than a century. The quality British series Bodies follows four different eras where a naked corpse with the same wounds mysteriously appears in the exact same location in London. Across the four timelines, detectives investigate their respective cases, gradually uncovering a complicated conspiracy connecting them across time, and revealing a much larger mystery involving political manipulation, time travel, and the fate of the city itself.

Bodies is effortlessly one of Netflix’s most exceptional science fiction crime stories. The premise is a true work of art with a clean conceptual hook that earns the series an exceptionally high ranking among other detective series. The show wields a remarkable premise, one that carefully constructs interconnected storytelling. With its captivating combination of futuristic sci-fi, crime drama, and elaborate conspiracy, Bodies earns itself a high ranking among Netflix’s most imaginative mysteries.

Dept. Q is a remarkable modern detective drama that uses its blend of intricate cold-case investigation and a group of deeply imperfect characters to deliver quite the intriguing watch. The British series centers around Edinburgh detective Carl Morck (Matthew Goode), who, after a catastrophic shooting that leaves one officer dead and his partner severely injured, returns to work traumatized, angry, and barely tolerable to his colleagues.

Dept. Q is a pretty rewarding series, one that effectively transforms an apparently hopeless investigation into an increasingly urgent mystery. The show works so well because it doesn’t actively rely on generic reveals, instead keeping its mystery alive and developing through dread created by missing information. Dept. Q defiantly delivers a refreshing mesh of emotional drama and clever crime-solving, marking it a perfect addition to this list of detective shows with brilliant mystery cores.

This complex watch is easily one of Netflix’s best original series that wields such a complex mystery that a moment looking away from the screen is only an opportunity to miss something important to the central story. Set in the German town of Winden, the international series Dark focuses on the disappearance of a child, which pushes Jonas Kahnwald (Louis Hofmann), police officer Ulrich Nielsen (Oliver Masucci), police chief Charlotte Doppler (Karoline Eichhorn), as well as several members of interconnected families toward an increasingly impossible explanation.

Dark is a complete puzzle box of an investigative show, one that stands out for its complex mystery and overall outstanding story. With its strong combination of philosophical questions about time, free will, and family with history, alongside a thrilling missing-person investigation, the series offers quite the mysterious tale—a tangled web unlike any other that is surely worthy of the fame that Netflix's much bigger hits have received.

Signal is honestly one of the most extraordinary, underrated watches sitting on the back shelf of the Netflix platform. The Korean series follows Park Hae-young (Lee Je-hoon), a cold-case profiler, who discovers an old police walkie-talkie that inexplicably connects him with detective Lee Jae-han (Cho Jin-woong), a man transmitting from decades in the past.

Signal is an underappreciated gem that introduces a fascinating supernatural element into an otherwise rather grounded police investigation. It’s the absolute best choice for this list because its supernatural device is never merely for show. With its fantastic ability to use the connection between two periods to investigate long-forgotten crimes, Signal effortlessly claims a place on this list for its blend of emotional devastation and carefully developed clues to create a mystery that feels both deeply human and imaginative.
