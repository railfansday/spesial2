See Sarah Paulson Hilariously Call Out Costar Kim Kardashian

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

How well does Kim Kardashian really know the All's Fair cast? Watch Sarah Paulson put her costar on the spot over a hilariously inaccurate first impression in E! News' exclusive interview.

Sarah Paulson is putting Kim Kardashian on the stand, er, spot.

While promoting season two of their legal drama All's Fair, Paulson hilariously called out her costar for admitting she had no idea Paulson was speaking in a fake accent while portraying courtroom viper Carrington Lane.

"She thinks that my voice on the show sounds like Elvis [Presley]," Paulson told E! News in an exclusive interview while giggling alongside the cast. "Because I'm doing a character voice and it's like this: 'One for the money, two for the show.'"

And the 51-year-old's inflection left Kardashian all shook up. As the SKIMS founder admitted through laughter, "I thought it was her real voice."

That is, until Paulson laid down the law and proved she doesn't, in fact, have a husky, twangy accent.

"She didn't know it was a voice I was doing," the American Horror Story star recounted to E!. "She's like, 'She's got a really specific voice.'"

So, from her Emmy-winning turn as Marcia Clark in The People vs. O.J. Simpson to iconic witch Cordelia Foxx in AHS: Coven, The Kardashians star confessed, "I thought in your other characters you were holding it back."

But Paulson isn't holding Kardashian in contempt of court over the misunderstanding. In fact, she thinks the mishap is down-right hilarious.

As Paulson added, "You thought that was a real performance because she had to hide her voice."

With the confusion officially cleared up, Paulson, Kardashian, Naomi Watts and Niecy Nash pleaded their case on why season two of the Hulu series is even more thrilling than the last.

For starters, expect major twists and turns for the women of Grant, Ronson & Greene, both in and out of the courtroom.

"I think we're killers in our work and killers outside of work," Paulson teased. "I'm a killer either way."

But when it comes Paulson having a lot in common with her vicious character Carrington Lane, Kardashian has to object.

"I think your character is so insane," the 46-year-old chimed in. "You're not as crazy as her."
