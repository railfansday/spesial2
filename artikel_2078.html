Ex-Sunderland coach updates on Thomas Frank being appointed Regis Le Bris' replacement

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Sunderland have doubts about appointing Thomas Frank if they decide to part ways with Regis Le Bris in the near future.

The former Tottenham boss is one of a number of options believed to be under consideration by the Black Cats as they weigh up a decision over the current manager's future.

Le Bris is facing doubts over his position in the dugout after a dismal start to the Premier League season which has left Sunderland rock bottom of the table after nine games.

Former Sunderland coach Mick Brown told Football Insider that Frank is firmly on the club's radar should they make a change, but may not be a front-runner.

There are doubts about the manager's record at Tottenham, where he was sacked in February after winning just 13 of his 38 games in charge across all competitons.

Meanwhile, there is a feeling that Sunderland are keen to sick by Le Bris, and will not rush into a decision with regard to any potential replacements in the near future.

Brown told Football Insider on 3 November that Sunderland are considering Frank as a potential replacement for Le Bris if they decide to call time on his tenure.

Despite having plenty of credit in the bank from last season, there is a feeling that the board are losing patience with the manager and could soon be tempted into a change.

However, a number of options are being considered if that were to be the case, and the concerns about Frank's Tottenham spell could force them to look elsewhere.

"There are definitely some doubts about Thomas Frank after his time at Tottenham," Brown told Football Insider.

"What about the work he did there was enough to show any Premier League club that he would be the right appointment? There were very few positives to take.

"Obviously he had a good spell with Brentford and that won't be ignored, but you have to look at the most recent evidence of his management.

"In Sunderland's case, I think they'll still want to stick with Regis Le Bris, but they will be considering a number of different managers to come in if they want to make a change.

"Frank is on their list, I'm sure, but I think they would have to look at other options because there are those lingering doubts over him."

Sunderland are not the only Premier League club believed to be keeping an eye on Frank's situation, with the boss heavily tipped to make a return to English football.

Having been out of the game since leaving Tottenham, he is now ready to step back in, and has been linked with a number of potential vacancies in European football.

For example, Football Insider revealed on 4 November that Crystal Palace have already held preliminary talks with Frank about potentially replacing Pierre Sage at Selhurst Park.

There is a growing feeling that Sage could be sacked during the international break if the Eagles do not pick up wins in their next two games.

The Eagles are seriously considering their options at this stage after a difficult start to the season which has seen them drop into the relegation zone in the Premier League.
