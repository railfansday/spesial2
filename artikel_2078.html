Daniel Farke handed blueprint to save Harry Wilson's Leeds career - verdict

Harry Wilson's form has stagnated since signing for Leeds United on a free transfer during the summer.

During the 2025-26 campaign, the attacker notched 11 goals and eight assists in 41 games, helping Fulham to a mid-table finish.

Everyone expected him to carry that form with him to Elland Road, but 10 games later, and he only has a single goal contribution to his name.

In fact, he's only started three Premier League games under Daniel Farke, raising serious questions over what has happened since last season.

Wilson simply isn't the same player at Leeds as he was last season, but all those concerns seem to go out the window on international duty.

Since Gareth Bale and Aaron Ramsey retired, the 29-year-old has become Wales' go-to clutch player, boasting five goals and two assists in 2025.

He is the most beloved player by the Red Wall, maybe other than Neco Williams, and he showed exactly why in his past two appearances.

Despite Craig Bellamy's side losing both games to Norway and Portugal, Wilson is one of the players who can keep his head high.

He may have grabbed an assists against Norway, but against a Portugal side missing Cristiano Ronaldo, the forward was absolutely unplayable.

Deployed in a freeroaming role, Wilson created four chances, had nine (yes, nine) shots, including one from his own half, and had 54 touches, the most of any Wales attacker [FotMob].

Everything went through the Leeds man against the No.5 ranked team in the world, and he absolutely loved having that responsibility at the Cardiff City Stadium.

It's not a surprise either. Every game, he carries the team on his shoulder, and that's exactly how Farke needs to utilise him as well.

Wilson only came on for the final 15 minutes of Leeds' 1-0 win over Tottenham Hotspur before the international break.

He's been in and out of the lineup since signing across the dotted line at Elland Road, but that could change after his international exploits.

If the Whites are to beat Chelsea on Saturday, 21 November, he needs to be in the lineup, but that will mean upsetting one of his teammates.

Either Sean Longstaff or Lukas Nmecha need to give way, allowing Wilson to be next to Dominic Calvert-Lewin, almost as a second striker.
