Cosm is bringing 'Star Wars: A New Hope' to its domes in a Disney film deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The multiyear, multifilm agreement adds Disney as a studio partner, and tickets will go on sale in 2027 in four cities

Cosm announced a multiyear, multifilm partnership with Disney $DIS on Thursday that will bring "Star Wars: A New Hope" to its domes in March 2027. The company said the film headlines the agreement, which makes Disney its next major studio partner.

Financial details of the agreement were withheld, according to CNBC.

Cosm developed the production with Disney, Lucasfilm, Industrial Light & Magic and MakeMake, the company said. It uses the 2019 version of the film and honors the film's 50th anniversary.

The film will keep its native 16:9 aspect ratio, sitting in the middle of Cosm's 12K-plus LED dome, the company said. The surrounding visuals will change to show Star Wars locations and scenes, including Tatooine, the destruction of Alderaan, the Rebel base on the fourth moon of Yavin, the inside of the Death Star and the Millennium Falcon's trench run.

Tickets for screenings at Cosm's locations in Atlanta, Dallas, Detroit and Los Angeles will go on sale in 2027, the company said. "Star Wars: A New Hope" is Cosm's sixth cinematic experience in its Shared Reality format. Its film library covers genres from animated fare and fantasy to horror and science fiction.

Alexis Scalice, vice president and head of Cosm Studios, said the company looks for anniversaries as openings for film programming. "We do look for those opportunities of these like anniversary and commemorative moments," Scalice told CNBC.

Scalice also described demand for the title. "Since we've launched experiential cinema at Cosm, people have been asking for this film for the last year and a half," she told Variety. On the design approach, she said: "The film remains the hero, and how can we expand?"

Disney joins Warner Bros. and Universal as studios with agreements in place with Cosm, according to Deadline.
