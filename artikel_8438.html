I mentioned VHYAX because the OP appears to have a Vanguard account already, and because of its current 30-day SEC yield (2.20%) which is higher than the 30-day SEC yield of VDIGX currently at 1.21%.

I suspect GaryA505 mentioned SCHD because it currently shows a 30-day SEC yield of 3.09%.

In either case, I'd be reluctant to describe the returns of any stock fund as "decent and smooth". While there may be instances of a dividend fund dropping less in a stock market downturn, it's not something I'd be willing to rely on. I suppose you could do backtesting for some of the ticker symbols mentioned, and pay attention to the standard deviation statistics before making your selection. But do keep in mind that backtesting can be like the siren song that draws you in. It's history, not the future. You cannot buy past performance.
