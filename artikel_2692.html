Bears have gone five straight games without ever trailing for first time since 1985

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Bears' win over the Saints on Sunday was their fifth consecutive game taking a first-quarter lead and winning without ever trailing. That gives this Bears team an accomplishment the franchise hasn't achieved in 41 years.

The last Bears team to win five straight games without ever trailing was the 1985 Bears, one of the most dominant teams in NFL history.

That 1985 Bears team lost just one game, a Monday night classic against the Dolphins in the 13th game of the season, and after that loss to the Dolphins they never trailed in the final three games of the regular season, or in the two playoff games they won to earn a trip to Super Bowl XX. In the Super Bowl, the Bears fell behind 3-0 in the first quarter before winning 46-10, snapping their streak of five straight games without trailing.

This Bears team isn't dominating quite like that 1985 team, but the Bears are currently on a very impressive run: They haven't just won five games in a row, but they've scored in every quarter of all five games, the franchise's first streak of 20 consecutive quarters scoring since the 1941 Bears. That team, like the 1985 team, won the NFL championship.

The 2026 Bears are playing like some of the best teams in franchise history. They're jumping out to early leads and never looking back.
