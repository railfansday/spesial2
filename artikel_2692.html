NFL Thanksgiving MEGA-preview: Packers vs. Rams must-win matchup, Lions & Bills defenses get tested

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Nate Tice & Frank Schwab join forces to preview all five mid-week NFL Thanksgiving matchups. After airing out their thoughts on the Washington Commanders' last-second win over the Cincinnati Bengals on Monday night, the duo dive into the Green Bay Packers battling the Los Angeles Rams on Wednesday night. With both teams' playoff odds hanging in the balance, expect a kitchen sink matchup that should be a great appetizer for the rest of the Thanksgiving slate.

Nate & Frank move on to the three Turkey Day matchups, including the Detroit Lions defense getting a true test in Caleb Williams & the Chicago Bears, the Dallas Cowboys and Philadelphia Eagles battling to stay relevant in the NFC East race and the Kansas City Chiefs giving the improving Buffalo Bills defense their toughest test of the season. The guys wrap things up with expectations for a Black Friday rock fight featuring the Denver Broncos & Pittsburgh Steelers.
