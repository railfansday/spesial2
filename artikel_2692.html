Will AJ Surace start for Rutgers football vs. Penn State? What we know

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

PISCATAWAY – AJ Surace's status for Rutgers football's game at Penn State remains unclear.

Coach Greg Schiano revealed after his team's Nov. 14 loss to Nebraska that Surace has three broken ribs and played through the pain after starter Sean Ashenfelder suffered an injury, but whether Surace is able to play on Nov. 21 against the Nittany Lions in State College remains to be seen.

Although Schiano has an idea of what Surace is aiming for.

"Knowing him, he'll do everything possible to play and we'll just see where it falls," Schiano said.

It's been that type of season for Rutgers' quarterback situation, which started with Dylan Lonergan suffering injuries that have cost him the last three games.

Now Surace is dealing with the broken ribs, which kept him from starting against Nebraska only to have to play anyway.

Surace isn't at risk of further injury, so it just comes down to his level of pain tolerance – apparently it's pretty high.

"Very rarely will I share that but I just felt it was necessary because the poor guy – never will we put a player out there that risks further injury so there's protective equipment and the type of fractures he has, but it's very, very painful," Schiano said Tuesday, Nov. 17. "Most humans wouldn't do it. I gave him the option, I told him I would not think any less of you. But he wants to do it for his teammates."

Surace this season is 39-of-65 passing (56.5 percent) for 455 yards with four touchdowns and five interceptions.

Rutgers has two other quarterbacks on the roster in walk-on Rocco Rainone and Xavier Stearn, a 6-foot-6, 220-pound true freshman from Pennsylvania.

It would not be an ideal situation for the Scarlet Knights if either Rainone or Stearn had to play given where they are in their respective careers, but that could be the situation they face.

Schiano deferred all injury statuses – including to Lonergan, Ashenfelder and Surace – to the availability report.

"He got hit a few times as you saw," Schiano said of Surace. "So this week we'll see."
