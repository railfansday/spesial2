Bill Belichick, Robert Kraft advance to final round of Hall of Fame voting

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Bill Belichick and Robert Kraft will have another chance at election to the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 2027.

The Hall announced on Thursday that Belichick has been chosen as the nominee in the coach category while Kraft is the nominee in the contributor category. That was also the case last year, but neither of them were elected.

Belichick’s failure to get enough votes for election created a particular outcry given the six Super Bowl titles he won while coaching Kraft’s Patriots from 2000-2023.

The Hall of Fame overhauled selection procedures in the wake of last year’s election. The size of the selection committee was cut in half from 50 to 25 and there is no longer be a separate category for senior players.

Belichick and Kraft will join 13 player nominees for consideration by the committee. That list of candidates will be reduced to seven before a final vote with 80 percent approval needed for election.
