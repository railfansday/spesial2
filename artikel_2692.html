Kaleb Johnson rumbles for 68 yards, touchdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Green Bay Packers running back Kaleb Johnson rushed 14 times for 68 yards and a touchdown in Week 9 against the New England Patriots, and he was not targeted in the passing game.

Why it Matters: Johnson might be a popular addition off of the waiver wire depending upon the health of Josh Jacobs. The latter left with a fractured finger, although head coach Matt LaFleur said the team would try and find a way for Jacobs to play while healing. If not, Johnson is the next man up.
