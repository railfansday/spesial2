Jonah Coleman is the AFC offensive player of the week

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Broncos rookie Jonah Coleman has moved to the top of the running back depth chart in Denver since returning from injured reserve late last month and he had his biggest game yet against the Raiders in Week 11.

Coleman ran 24 times for 111 yards and three touchdowns to help the Broncos to a 38-24 win. Those were all season-highs for Coleman, who now has five touchdowns in the last three weeks.

The NFL named Coleman the AFC offensive player of the week in recognition of that effort.

Coleman is the first Bronco to win the award since quarterback Bo Nix at the end of the 2024 season and the Broncos will be hoping that the duo is just at the start of a fruitful partnership.
