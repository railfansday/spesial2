Source Tells ‘The Daily Mail’ Taylor Swift Has Enough New Music to Fill Her Next Two Albums

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Casually curious about what Taylor Swift has been up to while we sit around clowning for Debut TV? Aside from getting inducted into the Nashville Songwriters Hall of Fame and making appearances in support of her original Toy Story 5 song (awards season is approaching!), Taylor’s been—allegedly—working on new music.

The Daily Mail reports that the Grammy winner has “enough material to fill her next two albums,” and a source tells the outlet that she “takes notes, hums new melodies and reads a lot of books for inspiration.”

FYI, this quote came from an insider who had a lot to say about Taylor’s post-wedding life with Travis Kelce. Specifically, that “she has told friends that she loves being Mrs Kelce and loves saying she is a married woman. She has found the one. When she is with Travis, she does everything to support him because he is working so hard on football. They work out a ton together. They love to lift weights and stay strong, and they stick to a lean diet. There is a lot of protein in their meals. They see a ton of Travis' family and she loves his mom. Taylor stays super busy.”

Travis is in the thick of it right now with the football, but the source says Taylor “accepts that Travis has to commit so much time to the Chiefs. She never complains because football is his passion. He has been in practice since August, so he has been hyper-focused for a long time. Taylor doesn't mind all the traveling she does for her career and for Travis' career. She hops between their homes and has a great team around her.”

Okay, love to hear alllllll that, but still hyper-focused on the “next two albums” part of this. No word yet on when Taylor might release new music (reminder: she just treated everyone to unreleased tracks from The Life of a Showgirl), but I’ll be here reading into everything per usual.
