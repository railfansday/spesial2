The Aaron Rodgers nightmare continues to worsen for Steelers

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

On Tuesday night, the Steelers dropped the bombshell announcement that they would be benching Aaron Rodgers in favor of Mason Rudolph this week. Then, on Wednesday, Rodgers joined The Pat McAfee Show live to spill every last drop of tea regarding the situation, absolutely obliterating head coach Mike McCarthy for his alleged shortcomings on air. And it appears things are not getting any better as reports continue to roll in.

According to Steelers beat writer Mike DeFabo, who covers the team for The Athletic, the locker room was open to the media while McAfee's show was on the air live on Wednesday, and players in the locker room were watching the show in real time.

DeFabo also reported that one particular allegation made by Rodgers – that McCarthy broke down a team huddle while calling the team the "Packers" instead of the "Steelers" – had been confirmed to him by multiple players on the team, and that one player also corroborated the report that McCarthy is falling asleep in meetings.

These latest tidbits coming directly on the heels of the explosive Rodgers interview gives further insight into the vibes inside the Steelers locker room at the moment, and it's starting to look like this nightmarish situation for the franchise might not end any time soon.
