Here is where the Seahawks are in the NFC playoff picture after Wednesday night

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Seattle Seahawks have fallen in the NFC West standings as a result of the Los Angeles Rams' 26-13 Thanksgiving Eve win over the Green Bay Packers, but there is a silver lining here for the reigning Super Bowl champions.

Green Bay has dropped to 6-5 after losing its third game over the last five weeks. Seattle is now (temporarily, at least) ahead of the Packers in the Wild Card chase. While the Seahawks are behind the Rams by a half-game, they currently own the division record tiebreaker on their fierce rivals. This means that the Seahawks control their Wild Card fate, starting with Week 12 against the San Francisco 49ers.

12. Tampa Bay Buccaneers (3-7, 1-0 vs ATL+NO)

14. New Orleans Saints (3-7, 0-1 vs TB+ATL, 1-5 NFC)

If the Seahawks defeat the 49ers, Seattle is the 7-seed heading into Week 13. A loss? They drop to No. 9 and behind the Packers on the conference record tiebreaker. It's not a dagger to their postseason hopes, but their margin for error would be about as thin as dental floss.

Seattle has a huge opportunity to get back into a playoff spot on Sunday and stay somewhat attached to the 49ers in the NFC West title race.
