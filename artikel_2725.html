A.J. Brown listed as limited Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

New England Patriots WR A.J. Brown (ankle, knee) was limited during practice Wednesday, Nov. 11.

Why It Matters: Brown has missed the last few games because of the injury but seems to have a chance to return to the lineup in Week 10. He should be one of the main options in the passing game when active and can be considered a No. 2 fantasy receiver.
