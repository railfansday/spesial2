The Panthers are in first place in the NFC South

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The Carolina Panthers lost on Sunday to fall to 3-5. Despite being two games under .500, the Panthers are still in first place in the NFC North. Crazy. The Tampa Bay Buccaneers lost on Sunday night and the Atlanta Falcons lost earlier on Sunday, and while the Saints won, Carolina still leads the division thanks to tiebreakers.

Carolina's 1-1 NFC South record is enough to get them past the Saints and Bucs for now.
