Workday Expands Payroll to Seven New Markets, Making It Easier for Global Organizations to Pay Employees

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Workday Combines AI with Its Established Payroll Engine and Human Expertise to Accelerate Expansion Across Asia-Pacific, Europe, and Latin America

New Autopilot Capabilities Will Help Payroll Teams Check Every Employee's Pay Before Payday

BARCELONA, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Workday Rising EMEA â Workday, Inc. (NASDAQ: WDAY), the trusted agentic platform for HR, finance, and IT, today announced it is more than doubling its native payroll coverage by expanding Workday Payroll to seven new markets: Hong Kong, Malaysia, Mexico, the Netherlands, New Zealand, Singapore, and Spain. Workday also announced a renewed investment in its payroll offering in France. The expansion will help multinational organizations run more of their payroll operations on one system, reducing cost and complexity and helping ensure employees are paid accurately and on time.

Paying employees across multiple countries is complex, with different tax laws, labor regulations, and reporting requirements often forcing organizations to manage payroll across multiple providers and systems. Workday is simplifying global payroll by bringing Workday Payroll natively to more markets and helping customers manage payroll in other countries through trusted partners using Global Payroll Connect and Global Payroll Hub.

"Paying people accurately and on time is one of the most important things a company does, and it gets harder with every country you add," said Gina Lange, general manager of payroll, Workday. "By bringing Workday Payroll to more markets, we're helping our customers manage that complexity in one system, so their people can count on getting paid right, wherever they work."

Building payroll for a new country has traditionally been a long and complex process. Workday is now using AI to accelerate development, from researching local requirements and building payroll calculations to creating tests and documentation. Payroll experts, engineers, and local specialists review and validate the AI-generated work, with automated testing to help ensure accuracy throughout development. Workday's unique combination of configurable payroll elements, which serve as the harness for agents that automate country payroll configuration, and human expertise helps Workday bring payroll to new markets faster while maintaining the accuracy, reliability, and control customers expect.

Using this new technical approach, Workday launched payroll for early adopters in Singapore ten times faster than previous country rollouts.

"Workday's expansion of native payroll will help customers manage the complexity of a multinational workforce, while its investment in AI strengthens accuracy, visibility, insight, and control," said Pete A. Autopilot Capabilities in Payroll Agent Can Catch Payroll Mistakes Before Payday

Workday Payroll customers can use Workday Payroll Agent in Sana to ask questions about payroll results, investigate potential errors, and get suggested corrections before employees are paid. The agent works with each organization's payroll data, policies, and settings, and payroll teams stay in control of any changes.

Workday is also adding autopilot capabilities to Payroll Agent that work in the background throughout the pay cycle, automatically checking for issues before and after payroll runs. The agent can review results for every employee, flag potential errors, and prepare suggested corrections for payroll teams to approve. Because HR, time, benefits, and payroll are in one system, Workday can help teams find and fix problems faster, while keeping them in control of every change.
