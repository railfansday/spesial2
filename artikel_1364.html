Nobody Is Talking About Ruthless Matthias Jaissle Decision at Newcastle

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Newcastle manager Matthias Jaissle deserves credit for the brutal decision he made in the 2-2 draw with Premier League champions Arsenal.

The Magpies were on a disappointing run of form heading into the game against Arsenal, who currently top the Premier League table.

Many would have expected a straightforward Arsenal victory, but Newcastle managed to fight back to secure a point at home.

The point takes Newcastle up to 12th in the Premier League table, meaning they've lost just once in their last four matches.

There's no doubt they should be winning more often, but they can certainly take some momentum from a strong point against Arsenal.

Newcastle went 1-0 down in the first half as Eberechi Eze latched onto a through ball from Kai Havertz to fire Arsenal in front.

After the break, it took just four minutes for Newcastle to equalise, as Harvey Barnes levelled the score in for the Magpies.

Newcastle then found themselves in the lead after 62 minutes, as Malick Thiaw pounced in the box to poke home from a set piece.

Arsenal did find an equaliser later in the half through Noni Madueke, but it was certainly a good point overall for Newcastle.

Jaissle deserves plenty of credit for helping Newcastle turn the game around. Being 1-0 down against Arsenal at half-time is far from a disaster, but that didn't stop the Newcastle manager from making a ruthless decision at the break.

Jaissle opted to take Amar Dedic, Jacob Murphy, and Sean Steur off at half-time. On came Tino Livramento, Anthony Elanga, and Jacob Ramsey, with Newcastle finding an equaliser less than five minutes later.

It was a ruthless decision from Jaissle, particularly due to the fact that it was only 1-0 against the side who were sitting top of the Premier League table.

The Newcastle manager knew it wasn't good enough and wanted to do something about it immediately, so he deserves plenty of credit for that.

Although the trio didn't directly influence the game, the brutal nature of the decision from Jaissle clearly gave the players the motivation they needed.

Newcastle have wasted a fortune on one star this summer, with Matthias Jaissle clearly not a fan of an Eddie Howe signing.

Jaissle clearly isn't here to make friends. Many have been calling for more regular starts for Steur, but the Newcastle boss wasn't happy with his performance and dragged him off.

As mentioned, being 1-0 down to Arsenal is far from a disaster, but it shows the mentality of Jaissle that he wasn't satisfied and wanted to see a lot more from his team.

Nobody seems to be talking about the triple change from Jaissle, perhaps due to the fact that none of the three players got themselves on the scoresheet or created a goal.

It can't be understated how important it was for Newcastle to react in the second half, and Jaissle's decision played a huge role in helping that.
