QKS Group defines Vendor Risk Management as the structured and systematic approach that organizations use to identify, assess, monitor, and mitigate the risks associated with third-party vendors. VRM platform protects organizations from various potential risks such as legal risk, reputational risk, financial risk, and cyber risk that can arise when engaging with third parties while ensuring compliance with relevant regulations and industry standards. The VRM platform helps organizations understand the entire risk management lifecycle of the third-party vendors having access to the organization's data while minimizing manual activities and saving operational costs using automation. The VRM lifecycle stages typically start with vendor identification and onboarding and end at vendor termination and offboarding.

Mitratech provides third-party risk management capabilities through its Mitratech Prevalent product, supporting organizations across the vendor lifecycle, from onboarding and risk assessment to continuous monitoring and remediation. The product combines configurable vendor tiering, assessment workflows, AI-assisted response capabilities, and risk intelligence to help organizations evaluate third-party exposure. Its continuous monitoring capabilities provide visibility into cybersecurity incidents, financial risks, geopolitical developments, and other emerging threats that affect vendor relationships. Mitratech further supports risk management through remediation workflows, SLA and performance tracking, analytics, and integration capabilities. Its broader GRC Global Platform and surrounding solutions provide an additional foundation for organizations seeking to connect third-party risk oversight with enterprise-wide governance and risk management processes.
