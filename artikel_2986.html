Southeast and East Asia held up as North America lagged on sales.

Super Hi (HDL +6.93%) reported a 7.1% year-over-year revenue increase for the third quarter, driven by higher guest traffic and an expanded restaurant network despite same-store sales pressure in North America. The company reported a significant increase in net profit due to nonoperating foreign exchange gains, while operating margins were impacted by phased investments in secondary brands and the centralization of management support functions. Management reported that Southeast Asia and East Asia remained resilient, whereas the North American market requires strategic adjustments to menu pricing and guest engagement to reach mainstream local diners. The company continues to advance its multi-brand strategy through the Pomegranate Plan and is leveraging digital tools to refine its loyalty program, which currently contributes 90% of restaurant revenue.
