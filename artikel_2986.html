Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Revenue jumped 40% on AI cloud demand, yet the bottom line slipped into a loss.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 24, 2026 at 3:12PM EST

Kingsoft (KC +3.33%) reported its fastest revenue growth in five years, reaching RMB 3.48 billion in the third quarter as AI cloud services became the primary driver of top-line performance. Management stated that AI cloud gross billings now account for 61% of public cloud revenue, reflecting a shift toward intelligent computing and high-concurrency inference demand. The company reported record adjusted operating margins while executing a significant capital expenditure program to expand domestic computing resources. CFO Yi Li indicated that operating cash flow and customer prepayments covered nearly 100% of the quarter's cash capital expenditures, supporting the company's disciplined capital allocation framework.
