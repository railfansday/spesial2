Harmonic (HLIT) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Revenue surged 64% as Rest-of-Market segment doubled, signaling customer diversification.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Harmonic Inc. (HLIT -0.39%) reported a significant increase in revenue and raised its full-year 2026 outlook for the third time, driven by expansion in the broadband sector. Management outlined a strategy focused on three growth engines: connectivity, intelligence, and edge AI compute. The company reported a record percentage of revenue from its Rest-of-Market segment, signaling a shift toward a more diversified customer base. Strategic moves during the quarter included the launch of the SensAI intelligence fabric and the acquisition of a fiber management platform to enhance end-to-end service capabilities.
