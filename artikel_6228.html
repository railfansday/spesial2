Transfer Chase Points To British Airways, Iberia, Aer Lingus, With 30% Bonus

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Chase Ultimate Rewards has just launched its latest airline transfer bonus. Actually, it’s a trio of transfer bonuses, as it’s valid for British Airways Club, Iberia Club, and Aer Lingus AerClub. This is a popular bonus when available, so I think some members will find this to be worthwhile.

Chase Ultimate Rewards is offering a bonus when you transfer points to British Airways Club, Iberia Club, or Aer Lingus AerClub. Between 12AM ET on November 1, 2026, and 11:59PM ET on December 1, 2026, members can receive a 30% bonus when moving points.

A transfer bonus like this seems to be offered an average of once to twice per year, and it’s typically in the range of 20-30%. So the 30% bonus is as good as it gets, and this is the biggest percentage bonus that we’ve seen so far in 2026.

Keep in mind that this bonus covers three different Avios currencies, and on top of that, you can freely transfer Avios between the various “flavors.” When you go to make a transfer, you should immediately see the 30% bonus reflected. Transfers from Ultimate Rewards to these partners are typically instant.

Keep in mind the various Avios currencies also partner with other major transferable points currencies, and we do sometimes see transfer bonuses with other programs as well.

I think there’s value to be had in taking advantage of a 30% bonus on points transfers from Ultimate Rewards to Avios. That’s not because of any one particular redemption opportunity, but rather because of the collective value of the Avios ecosystem.

Back in the day, the best uses of British Airways Avios were for short haul travel on partner airlines, including on Alaska and American. Unfortunately those redemption opportunities have been devalued horribly.

Avios is one of the most useful points currencies out there, especially given across how many airlines you can redeem these rewards. I think for many people, this could be worth a speculative transfer.
