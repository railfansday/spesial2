Outlook users hoping for a fix at 12:30 pm IST should be careful. That time marks Microsoft’s next scheduled update, not a promised return of services. In its latest statement, Microsoft said it is still working on several fronts to find the root cause of a traffic surge that has overwhelmed its backend servers. Engineers are now taking a slower, more careful approach to repairing faulty systems so they can confirm each fix has actually taken effect. The company is monitoring whether the repaired servers are recovering and performing as expected. Microsoft has not given any estimate for when Outlook, Teams and other Microsoft 365 apps will be fully restored. It again said there is no sign of malicious activity and promised a detailed post-incident report once the problem is resolved. The 12:30 pm IST update, which matches 6 pm in Sydney and 7 am UTC, may show whether that careful repair process is starting to work.
