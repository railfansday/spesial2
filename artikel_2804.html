Jerry Jones backs Mike McCarthy after Aaron Rodgers' rant: 'One of the finest people around football that I've been around'

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Cowboys owner Jerry Jones offered strong praise for Pittsburgh Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy after Dallas' late loss to the Philadelphia Eagles on Thanksgiving.

McCarthy, who coached the Cowboys from 2020 to 2024, has been in the headlines this week after his former quarterback, Aaron Rodgers, leveled a series of accusations against him Wednesday on "The Pat McAfee Show."

Rodgers said McCarthy slept during meetings, didn't watch film and even used the wrong team name while breaking a huddle. Since that interview aired, local beat reporters have cited anonymous sources in the building who confirmed some of the claims.

While the timing likely could have been better, Jones still took the time to address the claims, and he ended up giving McCarthy his full support with effusive praise.

"I offered him [McCarthy] to stay at least another year when he left. I thought he was outstanding as the head coach. Had a great command of, frankly, quarterback, offense. Can't say enough good things about him.

He's, frankly, one of the finest people around football that I've been around. I can't say enough about him.

I never had the type of thing that is being said about him. I never had that in consideration in any of my time with him, extending his contract, any of that it was never on the table. We never had an issue within the organization."

After the 2024 season, the two sides reportedly reached an impasse in contract talks and mutually agreed to part ways.

McCarthy ended his tenure in Dallas with a 49-35 record.

Jones is the latest to defend McCarthy this week. Ahead of each Thanksgiving Day game, hosts on pregame shows defended the head coach and criticized how Rodgers delivered his message.

Former Steelers quarterbacks Terry Bradshaw and Russell Wilson, as well as Hall of Famer Michael Strahan, were among those who spoke out against Rodgers' public delivery of criticism of McCarthy.
