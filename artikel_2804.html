Bay FC's Jordan Silkowitz nominated for NWSL Goalkeeper of the Year: Here's how you can vote

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

After a stellar season in net, Bay FC's Jordan Silkowitz is one of three nominees for the NWSL Goalkeeper of the Year award.

Silkowitz had the second most saves ever in a single season with 115, and became just the 7th keeper to record 100 saves in a season.

She is nominated alongside Ann-Katrin Berger from NJ/NY Gotham FC and Jane Campbell from Houston Dash.

Bay FC goalkeeper Jordan Silkowitz (29) looks down the field as the Angel FC take on the Bay FC during an NWSL soccer match, Saturday, March 21, 2026, in San Jose, Calif. (AP Photo/Scot Tucker)

Voting ends at 5 p.m. Pacific Time on Wednesday, Nov. 11. The winner will be announced on November 18 on ESPN2.
