LIVE UFC Qatar weigh-in video: Prochazka vs. Stirling

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

At UFC Qatar weigh-ins, all 24 fighters on Saturday's UFC fight card step on the scale early Friday morning in Doha, Qatar. Watch ceremonial weigh-in video above, beginning at 10 a.m. ET.

For the main event, Jiri Prochazka and Navajo Stirling can weigh no more than 206 pounds for the non-title light heavyweight fight. Prochazka looks to bounce back after losing via a shocking first-round knockout to Carlos Ulberg in a vacant championship bout, while Stirling recently kept his undefeated record intact with a first-round knockout of former champion Jan Blachowicz this past August.

Former bantamweight champion Aljamain Sterling fights fast-rising featherweight contender Kevin Vallejos in the co-main event. Sterling and Vallejos can weigh no more than 146 pounds for the non-title featherweight fight.
