Vote for Jackson area girls high school athlete of the week from Nov. 2-7

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Voting is now open for The Jackson Sun's area high school girls athlete of the week from Nov. 2-7. The poll closes Nov. 12 at 4 p.m.

Hudson McDaniel, Dresden: McDaniel won the individual Class A cross-country state championship with a time of 18:58.2.

Abagail Wallace, McKenzie: Wallace came fourth at the Class A cross-country state championship with a time of 19:44.4.

Farrah Carson, Trinity Christian: Carson finished 16th at the Division II-A cross-country state championship with a time of 21:09.6.

Hadley Haltom, USJ: Haltom finished 12th at the Division II-A cross-country state championship with a time of 20:28.5.

Alaina Uselton, Westview: Uselton finished third at the Class AA cross-country state championship with a time of 18:57.0.
