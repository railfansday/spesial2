Blues Need Grit To Beat Stars In Tight Central Race

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Central Division is a mess, and the St. Louis Blues are stuck right in the middle of it. Dallas holds second place, but the gap to sixth place is just three points. That tiny margin means every single point matters. The Blues lost their four-game winning streak on Wednesday night in a shootout against the New York Islanders. Now they face a Dallas team that just saw its own five-game run end with a loss to Nashville. Both clubs are tired, but only one will survive Friday's matchup in St. Louis.

Dallas has been nearly unbeatable on the road this season. Away from home, the Stars have posted a 6-1-2 record, having lost just once in regulation time. That is the fewest road losses in the entire league, per Field Level Media. It is a stat that hurts St. Louis right now. The Blues need to stop the bleeding if they want to climb the standings. A loss here would be a massive blow to their playoff hopes in such a crowded division. They cannot afford to let the Stars build a bigger cushion.

The Blues are dealing with a nasty injury bug that could cost them the game. Forward Jimmy Snuggerud leads the team with 14 goals and 20 points, but he is out with an upper-body injury. Pavel Buchnevich is also listed as day-to-day due to a similar issue. These two players drive the offense, and their absence changes everything. Dallas coach Glen Gulutzan admitted his team was flat after losing to Nashville, noting, "This one is going to leave a sour taste in everyone's mouth — players, coaches, everybody." If the Blues can't find a way to score without their top scorers, they will struggle against a Dallas defense that has been solid.

St. Louis showed some heart on Wednesday when they forced overtime against the Islanders. Mason McTavish scored for the Blues, and the team showed they could compete even in a loss. "I loved our effort, I loved our grit," St. Louis coach Jim Montgomery said after the game. He added, "I thought we played an intelligent road game." The Blues need that same energy on Friday. They have a chance to prove they belong in the top half of the Central.
