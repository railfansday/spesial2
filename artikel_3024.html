Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Five long-term partnerships and a Montana Precision stake shape the growth story.

DPC Holdings PLC (DPC +8.03%) reported record financial results for the third quarter of 2026, led by a 39% increase in revenue and a 56% rise in adjusted EBITDA. Management attributed the performance to strong demand in the aerospace and industrial gas turbine (IGT) end markets, which both grew over 40% during the period. The company emphasized its strategic transition toward long-term customer partnerships, announcing a fifth major agreement that includes significant customer funding for capacity expansion. These partnerships, combined with the acquisition of a 50% stake in Montana Precision Products, are intended to provide long-term revenue visibility and enhance returns on invested capital. The divestment of the noncore Ivostud business further focuses the portfolio on high-growth engine products and superalloys.
