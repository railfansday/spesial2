American Shared Hospital (AMS) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Forbearance talks and a Gamma Knife volume jump shape the call.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

American Shared Hospital Services (AMS -7.63%) reported a strategic shift toward its Direct Patient Services segment while managing a forbearance agreement with its primary lender. Management stated that growth was supported by international operations and the Rhode Island facilities, despite overall profitability being impacted by non-recurring professional fees. The company reported improved operating cash flow and a strengthened cash position compared to the previous fiscal year-end. Efforts to resolve debt milestones and expand specialized treatment capabilities in North and South America remain the primary operational focus.
