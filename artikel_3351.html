John Summit's Tour Bus Involved in Deadly Crash in Florida

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

9:42am PT -- A spokesperson for Florida Highway Patrol tells TMZ ... On November 16, 2026, at approximately 8:58 a.m., Florida Highway Patrol Troopers responded to a fatal traffic crash. A tour bus was stopped facing north and a Toyota sedan was traveling east on in an unknown lane of travel.

We're told ... "For reasons currently under investigation, the Toyota sedan traveled off the roadway in a southeast direction and collided with the front of the stopped tour bus. The driver of the tour bus, a 64-year-old Louisiana man, was wearing a seatbelt and was not injured. Nine adult passengers aboard the bus were also uninjured."

The reps tells us ... "The driver of the Toyota sedan, a 40-year-old male from Ocala, was not wearing a seatbelt. He was transported to a local hospital, where he was later pronounced deceased. The crash remains under investigation."

John Summit's tour crew was involved in a deadly crash in Florida ... after a car slammed into their tour bus, leaving one person dead ... TMZ has learned.

A source with direct knowledge tells TMZ ... the bus belongs to the famous DJ's touring team and was carrying members of his production crew when the fatal collision occurred Monday in Ocala.

We're told John was not on board at the time of the crash.

Law enforcement tells TMZ ... a black Toyota Camry ran a red light and collided with the bus ... and the car's driver was killed in the wreck.

Photos from the scene show the Camry's front end completely mangled, with debris scattered across the road and emergency personnel responding to the wreck.

Despite the crash, we're told another bus arrived to pick up the production crew so they could continue their journey to Miami ... where John is scheduled to perform two shows at Kaseya Center on Friday and Saturday.

The terrifying crash comes on the heels of another headline involving John ... who, as TMZ reported earlier Monday, publicly criticized the Grammys after being snubbed by the Recording Academy.
