Paramount Eyeing Sterling K. Brown To Star in New ‘Longlegs’ Movie Opposite Nicolas Cage

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

EXCLUSIVE: Following the excitement that fans of the record-breaking hit Longlegs would be getting a new movie in the universe, Paramount Pictures looks to have now found the actor to lead that film as sources tell Deadline Sterling K. Brown is in negotiations to join Paramount Pictures’ upcoming Longlegs film. Brown will be joining Nicolas Cage who will be reprising his iconic role for the titular character. Osgood Perkins is back to direct and is also writing and will produce.

Cage is also producing along with Brian Kavanaugh Jones through Range Media Partners, Perkins and Chris Ferguson, through their company Phobo; and Dave Caplan under his C2 banner, which originated the rights to the property. The pic will bow on January 14, 2028. When news broke that Perkins and Cage were returning for a new movie this past spring, people were floored that a new movie was not only in the works but was moving over to Paramount after originally being released by Neon. Watch on Deadline

The original 2024 pic starred Cage and Maika Monroe as an FBI agent who investigates a series of murder-suicides in Oregon that are connected to serial killer nicknamed Longlegs.

The film turned into one of Neon’s biggest hits to date grossing $128 million worldwide and also gave us another out of this world performance from Cage who earned some of the best reviews in recent years. Not much is known about this latest film or who Brown will be playing, but it is not a straight sequel and Monroe is not expected to return for this film.

As for Brown, the three-time Emmy-winning actor and Oscar nominee continues to stay busy on both TV and film fronts. The third season of his Hulu series Paradise bows next year. The show is one of Hulu’s more popular series and has already earned him two Emmy nominations for his performance.

He recently starred in Amazon/MGM’s film Is God Is and stars in Roadside Attraction’s upcoming film The Gallerist, opposite Natalie Portman. He is currently in production on Sony’s The Comebacker, opposite Tom Hanks. He will next be seen in Amazon MGM’s Voltron, opposite Henry Cavill, in spring 2027.

Brown is represented by CAA, JWM Entertainment Group and Johnson Shapiro Johnson Shapiro Slewett & Kole.
