Guitar Center Slashes Prices on Exclusive & Limited-Edition Instruments for Black Friday: Shop Here

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Running through Dec. 2, Guitar Center's Black Friday sale offers savings of up to 30% on top-rated instruments and gear.

Ready to strike a chord with the music lover on your holiday shopping list or treat yourself to some new gear? Guitar Center is making it easier to put something special under the tree. The retailer is running one of its biggest sales of 2026, with a long list of instruments and music gear on sale. Until Dec. 2, Guitar Center’s Black Friday sale includes deals on everything from instruments and speakers to travel bags and accessories and shoppers can save on popular brands like Fender, Gibson and Yamaha.

In this list, we’ve rounded up Guitar Center exclusives and limited-edition instruments and gear with reduced prices, along with other deals on standard edition pieces worth checking out, whether you’re shopping for a seasoned musician, a beginner or looking to upgrade your own setup.

If you’re shopping for a first electric guitar or looking for a Christmas gift for someone ready to learn, this limited-edition Squier Stratocaster pack is a great place to start.

A Guitar Center-Exclusive, it includes everything a novice needs, from a Fender amp to a gig bag, cable and picks. Shoppers agreed, with reviewers praising that it had everything a beginner needed to get started.

For someone ready to invest in a special electric guitar, the Gibson ES-335 is worth a closer look.

Firstly, the guitar has a rich musical history, famously played by Eric Clapton during his days with Cream. Secondly, shoppers of varying experience levels praise its quality, tone and overall feel, with one longtime guitarist noting they own 10 guitars but can’t put the ES-335 down.

If you’re looking to splurge on a guitar that can handle a variety of sounds, the Fender Player II Stratocaster is a strong choice. Its combination of pickups makes it suitable for everything from clean tones to heavier rock sounds. Customers say it’s worth the full price, making the 20% discount an especially good opportunity to save on this exclusive model.

This limited-edition Squier Sonic Stratocaster is a particularly standout option for beginners or anyone making the switch from acoustic to electric guitar.

Shoppers love its lightweight body and comfortable neck, which make it easy to practice without feeling weighed down. It also offers a range of sounds to experiment with as playing skills develop.

Want the classic Les Paul look without spending as much on a Gibson? The Epiphone Les Paul Custom Widow is worth considering, and bonus: it’s currently on sale.

Exclusive to Guitar Center, it features a flame maple top, pearloid block inlays and an ebony fingerboard. It also has dual ProBucker pickups for a full sound and comes with a premium gig bag. Shoppers love its affordability, with one calling it “pretty much flawless.”

A Martin guitar is an exciting gift for just about any acoustic player, and this X Series model offers a particularly appealing combination of sound and durability.

Customers appreciate its sound both plugged in and unplugged, so it’s a versatile choice for someone who enjoys playing at home or onstage.

If someone on your gift list regularly performs live or transports their own sound equipment, this speaker bag is a surprisingly useful find.

Designed for 15-inch speaker cabinets, it features generous padding, reinforced stitching and a water-resistant exterior to help protect valuable equipment between gigs. Both DJs and musicians have described it as a necessity in the reviews, making it one of Guitar Center’s most highly rated products.

For anyone interested in learning drums, this Ludwig five-piece set makes getting started much more straightforward, not to mention more affordable.

Available in five color options, it includes the drums, cymbals, stands and pedal, so beginners won’t have to shop for a bunch of additional equipment just to put together a complete kit. Shoppers appreciate that everything is included, making this discounted set a practical choice for a first drum kit or a Christmas gift.

Part piano, part mini music studio, the Yamaha PSR-E383 gives you hundreds of sounds to play with and built-in accompaniments that make playing feel like a full-band experience.

It has 61 touch-sensitive keys along with built-in learning tools and accompaniment styles. Shoppers love the variety of sounds and overall quality, with one calling it a “no-brainer” for the price.

With Guitar Center’s Black Friday sale ending Dec. 2, now is a good time to take advantage of these year-low prices before they disappear. Don’t forget that Guitar Center offers lessons and gift cards, so you can pair a new instrument with lessons for a teen or family member ready to learn.
