Portugal aim to secure their first victory since the retirement of captain Cristiano Ronaldo as they visit Wales in the final round of the Nations League.

Portugal suffered a humiliating 4-1 home defeat to Denmark as they paid tribute to their record goalscorer on Saturday, which also meant they missed the chance to secure top spot in their Nations League group.

Portugal will ensure top spot if they avoid defeat in Cardiff, with Denmark needing to beat Norway in the other game in Group A4 to have any chance of overtaking them. Wales will finish bottom after losing 3-1 to Norway last time out.

Craig Bellamy’s side are safe from direct relegation from League A and will instead enter the relegation/promotion play-offs in March regardless of tonight’s result, but will be playing for pride as they seek to end the Nations League on a winning note.

Follow updates from Wales v Portugal below

20 mins: Denmark have marched into a 2-0 lead over Norway in Copenhagen. So as it stands they will finish Group A4 as runners-up. They need Wales to beat Portugal to take top spot from the Portuguese.

19 mins: Lovely move from Wales! Wilson and Brooks combine and it looks as if Brooks would be breaking into the box. But there’s an excellent recovery challenge from Dalot to stop his in his tracks in the box.

17 mins: Danger for Wales as Portugal find Cancelo in space on the right. The full-back waits to pick his spot but Sheehan is there to cut out his cross.

14 mins: Well that’s certainly sparked the game into life. Wilson grabbed the ball from the net after the equaliser. A message of intent!

AN IMMEDIATE RESPONSE FROM WALES! DAVID BROOKS!

Ruben Colwill plays a lovely pass through to Harry Wilson, whose shot is saved by Diogo Costa. But David Brooks is there to bury the rebound, finding the far corner after Colwill kept the ball alive.

There’s a brief check for offside, but it stands! What a reaction from Wales.

RUTHLESS FROM PORTUGAL AND BRILLIANT FROM FERNANDES!

Portugal’s captain leads by example but Josh Sheehan will not want to see this again. The midfielder lost the ball on the edge of the box, as Wales looked to build possession from the back.

Portugal took full advantage. It was played inside to Fernandes and he rattled an unstoppable shot into the top corner.

Fernandes celebrates by covering his ears. Bellamy looks away in disgust.

7 mins: A first spell of control from Portugal, as Wales sit off. Veiga’s pass over the top looks for Neto and there’s a moment where he looks like he’s in. But Dasilva recovers well to cut it out.

6 mins: Danny Ward, in for Karl Darlow tonight, has been wayward with the ball at his feet on a couple of times already. He is grateful that nothing comes of his loose pass after Neto made the interception in midfield.

3 mins: That’s loose from Silva, the Bournemouth centre-back, and James picks up the ball before bursting into the final third. Can he find a pass with Portugal slightly disorganised? No, it’s cut out as he looked for Brooks.

2 mins: Decent start from Wales as Thomas finds the run from James with a pass with the outside of his foot. Colwill, one the changes tonight, gets an early touch as Wales keep possession against the heavyweight pair of Vitinha and Neves.
