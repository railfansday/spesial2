Academy Museum Unveils Winter Film Series With Colman Domingo, Eli Roth, Jessica Lange and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Academy Museum of Motion Pictures has announced its winter film series and education programs, which include “Sing Sing” (2024), “Blue Sky” (1994) and “Cabin Fever” (2002) among the highlights.

The season includes the museum’s annual 3D-cember series and Present Past, its celebration of film preservation. Present Past will premiere a 4K restoration of William Wyler’s “Wuthering Heights” (1939). Filmmaker and actor Carlos López Estrada (“Blindspotting,” “Raya and the Last Dragon”) will lead a Finding Your Story Workshop. Two-time Oscar nominee Colman Domingo will appear at a screening of “Sing Sing” (2024), presented with the museum’s Teen Council. Domingo was nominated for best actor for his role as John “Divine G” Whitfield, a man incarcerated at Sing Sing Correctional Facility who finds purpose in the prison’s theater program.

“One of the most rewarding things about being an actor is having the opportunity to bring stories and people to the screen that audiences might not be familiar with,” Domingo said. “I am thrilled to revisit ‘Sing Sing’ and share this story at the Academy Museum with the Teen Council and continue the conversations that a film like this can inspire.”

Other screenings include “Blue Sky” (1994) with Jessica Lange, who won the best actress Oscar for the film; “The Family Oppenheim (Semya Oppenheim)” (1938); “Cabin Fever” (2003) with director Eli Roth; and a double feature of “Veteran” (2015) and “Veteran 2: I, the Executioner” (2024) with director Ryoo Seung-wan and star Jung Hae-in. “Kubo and the Two Strings” (2016) will screen with a live orchestra.

Museum members can buy tickets for the winter programs starting Nov. 18. Tickets go on sale to the public on Nov. 19.
