Rodgers benched by Steelers for game with Broncos

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Aaron Rodgers has struggled in the Pittsburgh Steelers' back-to-back defeats to the Cincinnati Bengals and the Philadelphia Eagles; quarterback, who turns 43 next week, has been sacked 31 times this season; Mason Rudolph has started only 19 games in seven seasons

Aaron Rodgers has been benched by the Pittsburgh Steelers for Friday's game against the Denver Broncos with Mason Rudolph to start in the four-time NFL MVP's place.

Rodgers, who turns 43 next week, has struggled during the Steelers' back-to-back defeats to the Cincinnati Bengals and the Philadelphia Eagles.

Pittsburgh confirmed in a statement that the veteran quarterback will be "inactive", despite head coach Mike McCarthy saying on Monday that he remained the starter.

Rodgers is amid his 22nd and final NFL season, having reunited with McCarthy after the two previously won the Super Bowl together at the Green Bay Packers in 2010.

He was key in McCarthy being hired to take over from Mike Tomlin.

Rudolph has started only 19 games in seven seasons with the Steelers and the Tennessee Titans, so this is a big call from Pittsburgh, even considering Rodger's flagging numbers.

Rodgers has completed under 61 per cent of pass attempts this season, marking one of his worst campaigns since he became a full-time starter with Green Bay in 2008.

The quarterback has been sacked 31 times, surpassing last year's total of 21 already, and leads the league with 257 yards lost in those sacks.

Earlier this week, McCarthy said of Rodgers when discussing Pittsburgh's successive defeats: "When you talk about the quarterback position, I think there's things offensively we need to do better and that always benefits and leads into quarterback play.

"I just need him to just keep ploughing away. We got a lot of work to do."
