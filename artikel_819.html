“Many people know my mama, Avie Lee Owen Parton, from my song ‘Coat of Many Colors,’ but my mama gave me a gift that was far more valuable than a colorful coat. She gave me the gift of music.” Explore Billie Eilish Dolly Parton FINNEAS See latest videos, charts and news

That’s how Dolly Parton begins her passage about her mother’s musical legacy in On the Records: 80 Musicians on Beloved and Overlooked Albums. Written before her Aug. 25 death, the country music legend recalled inviting her mother to Fireside Recording Studio in Nashville in 1973 to record some of the Appalachian Mountains songs she cherished. Parton explained that the album was never released at the time, but the tapes were eventually published posthumously in 2024 as Mama Sings (Smoky Mountain DNA Series), 21 years after her mother’s death. Published Tuesday (Nov. 17), the new music book brings together 80 celebrated musicians — including Billie Eilish, Chris Stapleton and Laufey — sharing overlooked albums, hidden gems and personal favorites they believe deserve greater recognition.

A quintessential coffee-table book, the $30 hardcover features full-color photography and artwork for each overlooked album, and is already a No. 1 bestseller on Amazon.

Alongside Dolly Parton, contributors include Erykah Badu on Prince, Billie Eilish on The Dø, Finneas on Feist, Brian Eno on The Consolers, Chris Stapleton on Tim O’Brien and Darrell Scott, Sonny Rollins on Fats Waller, Laufey on Chet Baker, Angélique Kidjo on Bella Bellow and Justin Vernon of Bon Iver on Paul Pena.

Written by Brian Beck with Piotr Orlov, the collection offers music fans a chance to discover underrated recordings through the artists who adore them.
