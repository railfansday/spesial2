Elliot Page and Chris Columbus Board Queer Dark Comedy ‘Callback’ as Executive Producers (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The award-winning short, starring Justin H. Min and Michael Hsu Rosen, is from filmmaker Matthew Puccini.

“The Odyssey” actor Elliot Page and “Harry Potter” filmmaker Chris Columbus have signed on as executive producers of writer and director Matthew Puccini’s award-winning short, “Callback.”

The film is a dark comedy about a gay couple, played by Justin H. Min (“After Yang,” “Beef”) and Michael Hsu Rosen (“Presumed Innocent,” “The Only Living Pickpocket in New York”), whose relationship unravels into competition over the course of a disastrous evening at home. The film was shot by Sam A. “From the moment it begins, ‘Callback’ vibrates off the screen,” Page said in a statement Tuesday. “Quiet and contained, yet brimming with visceral intensity, the film is a painfully funny and incisive portrait of professional jealousy and the complex power dynamics within a relationship. It is an honor to be involved with such a gorgeously crafted film, anchored by two extraordinary performances from Justin H. Min and Michael Hsu Rosen.”

Columbus said, “Matthew has directed a smart, confident and frequently hilarious film. What starts as a simple premise becomes a delightfully dangerous game of one-upmanship that kept me guessing until the very end. I immediately watched it again to savor the razor-sharp writing and superb performances. I’m so glad to have discovered Matthew’s work and to play a part in spreading the word about his wonderful picture.”

“Callback” premiered at the 2026 Sundance Film Festival. It received the Oscar-qualifying award for Best Comedy at AspenShortsFest. The film also received top honors at the San Francisco and Provincetown International Film Festivals, making it the only live-action short film this year to be 3x Oscar qualified and it has received additional honors at HollyShorts, Salute Your Shorts, Nantucket, SCAD, FilmQuest and more.

“I am incredibly grateful for Elliot and Chris’ support in helping Callback to reach a wider audience,” Puccini said. “This film was a true labor of love for all involved, and it’s surreal to have two heroes of mine, each with their own incredible legacy of boundary-pushing work, champion our unhinged gay short.”

Switchboard Magazine is distributing “Callback.” “Like everyone who sees ‘Callback,’ we were hooked from the first frame,” Switchboard Magazine founder Celia Aniskovich said. “The timing, the pacing, the performances and the emotional roller coaster Matthew takes you on in those final minutes are the work of a director who knows exactly what he’s doing. We’re thrilled to welcome Chris and Elliot as we bring ‘Callback’ to audiences.”

Puccini’s previous short films, “Dirty” and “Lavender,” premiered at Sundance and screened at SXSW. “Dirty” was acquired by The Criterion Channel, while “Lavender” was distributed by Searchlight Pictures. He is a Sundance Ignite fellow, an alumnus of the Torino ScriptLab and a recipient of the SFFILM Rainin Grant.

“Callback” is produced by Jeremy Truong and Matt Kazman. Additional executive producers include Aniskovich, Lowell Bagley, George S. Copeland, Richie Jackson, Daphne Kinard, Leland, Natalé Olsen, Jason Rothberg and Tony Yang.
