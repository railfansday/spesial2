College Football 27 predicts Alabama vs. LSU in Week 10 clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Part of a loaded Week 10 slate, Saturday's showdown between Alabama and LSU in Baton Rouge is a massive game for a variety of different reasons.

Not only is this a long-time SEC rivalry, but the latest matchup between the two comes with high stakes attached in terms of making the College Football Playoff. Both teams already have two losses, with Alabama currently ranked at No. 5 in the initial CFP rankings. LSU is ranked No. 14.

Ahead of the huge clash, Roll Tide Wire recently turned to the EA Sports College Football 27 video game to simulate the matchup, as with prior weeks. So far this season, the game simulation has a perfect 8-0 record when predicting Alabama's games correctly.

Here is how this week's College Football 27 simulation predicts Alabama-LSU to play out this Saturday inside Tiger Stadium.

This week's College Football 27 simulation predicts a high-scoring contest Saturday in Baton Rouge between two of the SEC's top offenses, one which Alabama eventually claims a 48-43 win.

In the simulation, Alabama jumped out to an early lead in Tiger Stadium, and eventually held a 34-20 lead going into halftime. The Crimson Tide also held a multi-score lead entering the fourth quarter, with a late LSU comeback falling just short as Alabama safety Bray Hubbard picked off Tiger quarterback Sam Leavitt with under a minute remaining.

Both of Alabama and LSU also had 500+ yards of total offense during the simulation, headlined by a big showing in the passing game from Keelon Russell. Lotzeir Brooks also had 100+ yards receiving, as well as two touchdowns as a receiver from Russell.

Alabama and LSU are set to kickoff this Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:30 p.m. ET on ABC. The game will be played at Tiger Stadium in Baton Rouge, Louisiana.
