Prediction: Lazio vs Lecce

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Marking their return from November’s international break on Saturday afternoon, a high-flying Lazio will welcome Lecce to the Stadio Olimpico.

With the rest of Europe’s elite domestic leagues also making their respective returns this weekend, there are plenty of attractive betting promotions available.

Ahead of Saturday’s Serie A showdown from Rome, you can find all of the key Lazio vs Lecce H2H stats here.

Starting with the hosts, while there might have been plenty of raised eyebrows across the continent over the summer following Gennaro Gattuso’s appointment, Lazio have embarked on a sensational start to proceedings. Receiving a string of plaudits and cementing their spot as one of Europe’s ultimate surprise packages, the Eagles remarkably mark their return from November’s fortnight interval leading the chase at the top of the Serie A standings. Shattering plenty of records since opening up the 2026/27 campaign, Lazio will simply be looking to carry on from where they left off prior to the international break. In fact, the former AC Milan boss has seen his squad register 29 points from their first 11 top-flight appearances and they are yet to suffer a single domestic defeat. With their dominant start underlined by a 3-0 romp away at Juventus on October 18th, Gattuso’s high-flying squad will be confident of making another statement here.

Undergoing knee surgery over the summer and yet to make a maiden appearance this season, Lazio will once again be without Alessio Furlanetto on Saturday afternoon. However, recently returning to first-team training after battling with an extended shoulder issue, Gattuso’s side will welcome back Iceland international Albert Gudmundsson.

Making an immediate impact since completing a season-long loan switch from Inter Milan, Davide Frattesi will start in the middle of the park. Registering eight direct goal contributions in his 10 Serie A appearances this season (four goals, four assists), Dutch attacker Tijjani Noslin could play a leading role in the final third.

As for the visitors, while Lecce might mark their return from November’s international break holding a three-point buffer over the drop zone, Eusebio Di Francesco’s side appear destined for another relegation scrap this season. Signing off October with a nightmare 5-1 drumming at the hands of Frosinone, the Wolves have now registered just one-point from any of their previous four consecutive Serie A appearances. Likewise, putting just a sole top-flight win on the board since the middle of September, Lecce are bracing themselves for a monumental challenge when they travel to Rome this weekend. It should also be highlighted, Di Francesco’s downbeat squad fell to a routine 2-0 defeat when they last travelled to the Stadio Olimpico for a Serie A showdown 12 months ago. Likewise, the Wolves have claimed just a top-flight victory away at the potential title contenders since way back in January 2011.

Suffering what appeared to be a serious calf injury prior to November’s international break, Lecce are once again expected to be without Swiss midfielder Joel Monteiro here. Undergoing surgery back in October following a serious ankle injury, Argentinian striker Santiago Pierotti remains a major doubt this weekend.

While summer recruit Willem Geubbels might still be waiting to open his Serie A account, the former Paris FC marksman could make a first start since October 25th. Now in his fifth campaign at the Stadio Via del mare, ex-Sampdoria goalkeeper Wladimiro Falcone will start between the sticks.

Embarking on what was an astonishing purple patch prior to November’s international break, Lazio will simply be looking to carry on from where they left off prior to the fortnight interval. Unbeaten in all of their first 11 Serie A appearances and marking their return this weekend leading the chase at the top of the table, Lazio will simply be looking to make another statement here. Likewise, landing a 2-0 win when they last welcomed Lecce to the Stadio Olimpico for a Serie A showdown 12 months ago, we are expecting a similar outcome.
