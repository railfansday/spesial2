Shopify Operating Income Jumps 66%: Rapid Earnings Reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Shopify grew sales faster than profit, and its spending discipline closed the gap.

Shopify (SHOP -0.81%) grew third-quarter revenue 32% to $3.74 billion, and operating income jumped about 66% to $568 million. The growth leans on payments rather than subscriptions, which trims gross margin, but tighter spending more than covered the squeeze.

Revenue is outrunning subscriptions. Monthly recurring revenue, a gauge of subscription sales, rose to $230 million from $193 million. Merchant solutions, mainly payments that rise with the dollar volume merchants sell, grew faster and now make up 78% of revenue, versus 75% a year ago. Payments earn thinner margins than software, so gross profit grew only 28%, slower than revenue.

Cost control more than covered that squeeze. Operating expenses grew about 16%, half the pace of revenue.

Guidance says the pattern holds. Management expects fourth-quarter revenue to grow at a low-thirties rate, with gross profit dollars growing at a high-twenties rate. Keeping profit climbing will depend on holding costs down.

Watch the gap between revenue growth and gross profit growth next quarter. If it widens, spending discipline will have to carry even more of the profit story.
