REPORT: Warsofsky’s Job Safe for Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

San Jose Sharks head coach Ryan Warsofsky isn't on the hot seat yet.

That's what Pierre LeBrun reported on TSN's Insider Trading on Thursday.

"The Sharks are near the bottom in goals for. They're near the bottom in goals against. It's ugly. The team's struggles have led to speculation about Ryan Warsofsky," LeBrun said. "My understanding is that Sharks GM Mike Grier is not contemplating a coaching change at this stage."

The San Jose Sharks, after a 6-4-1 start, have slumped, losing five of their last six. They got stomped 7-2 at the Los Angeles Kings on Wednesday, leading to a players-only meeting.

"It's not on the coaches right now," Kiefer Sherwood said afterwards.

Warsofsky, going into the last year of his contract, signed a multi-year extension before the season. Warsofsky and his coaching staff, spearheaded by an MVP-caliber Macklin Celebrini campaign, had led a 34-point improvement in the standings last year. Grier followed that up in the off-season by committing over $100 million to veterans Mason Marchment, Darnell Nurse, Jacob Trouba, and Michael Kesselring, raising expectations for a San Jose playoff berth, which would be their first since 2019.

But dragged down by mostly poor 5-on-5 play, along with injuries to glue center Alex Wennberg and top winger Igor Chernyshov, the Sharks have sputtered recently.

It looks like Warsofsky will get some time to turn the San Jose Sharks around. But for how long?
