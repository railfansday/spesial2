Wolves see off Charlton to climb to third

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Wolverhampton Wanderers vs Charlton Athletic. Sky Bet Championship.

Report and free match highlights from the Sky Bet Championship match between Wolverhampton Wanderers and Charlton Athletic at Molineux on Saturday; Adam Armstrong and Rafiki Said score as Cesar Peixoto’s side climb up to third in the table.

Please use Chrome browser for a more accessible video player

Adam Armstrong scored his fourth goal in five games and Rafiki Said his first for the club as Wolves beat Charlton 2-1 in the Sky Bet Championship at Molineux.

Dominant Wolves put their first-half frustrations behind them to score twice in four minutes shortly after the break - both set up by Matheus Mane - before Matty Godden's 81st-minute consolation rattled a few home nerves.

Victory extended the hosts' unbeaten run to seven games.

Charlton failed to force a save until substitute Godden scored his second goal in as many games.

The home side dominated the first half without breaking the deadlock, forcing just one save from Will Mannion.

Ladislav Krejci twice headed wide, first from Tommy Doyle's cross then going much closer from Kieran Trippier's corner.

In between, Billy Koumetio made a superbly-timed challenge on Armstrong after Said had sent him clear.

Mane continued Wolves' momentum but drove well wide from an angle.

Charlton had an effort disallowed for offside in the 28th minute.

Joe Rankin-Costello tapped home after Tyler Bindon headed the ball back across the six-yard box, but two Charlton players had strayed too far.

Three Wolves chances in as many minutes before half-time threatened a breakthrough.

Mane missed the best opportunity so far when he ballooned a cross from Trippier over the bar from seven yards.

Fer Lopez forced the first save when his 25-yard drive forced Mannion to tip behind before Andre whistled a shot wide from just outside the box.

Charlton had the first chance of the second half. A long throw was flicked on for Rankin-Costello from point-blank range, but Andre nipped in to concede a corner.

Wolves took the lead in the 51st minute through Armstrong.

Mane crossed low from the right and Armstrong steered his finish home from seven yards for his fifth Championship goal of the season.

Mane claimed his second assist as Wolves doubled their lead in the 54th minute.

Armstrong threaded him through on the right, but the 19-year-old still had to outmuscle Amari'i Bell before squaring to Said, who calmly finished from close range.

Lopez mis-kicked in front of Mannion after the goalkeeper's poor pass was intercepted by Tommy Doyle.

Substitute Jordan James arrowed a 30-yard drive over the bar as Wolves threatened more goals, while Krejci's backheel was smothered by Mannion after Mane's cross.

Then Huge Bueno's chip hit the underside of the bar, but, against the run of play, Charlton grabbed a lifeline in the 81st minute.

Godden squeezed home a shot after Jose Sa failed to hold fellow substitute Karlan Grant's shot, but Wolves held on for the win.

"Mateus has a lot of talent. As a technical staff we never give up on him and today he had a great game.

"I said to Mateus, 'Play with confidence and courage, be brave and play to our idea, shoot, cross and if something bad happens, then I am responsible'.

"It's important because he's young and they need these kind of games to improve his confidence.

"We were the better team in the first half, but it was difficult because they stayed with 11 players on the edge of their box.

"In the second half we scored twice and created chances to score more.

"We conceded in the last few minutes so the feeling was not so good because of that, but we controlled the game and deserved the win."

"I'm disappointed because a 10-minute period after half-time killed us.

"After the first goal we needed to defend well to stay in the game, but to concede three minutes later was a real killer.

"The last thing I said to the players before they went out for the second half was that these first 10 minutes are going to be really important so we needed to be aggressive and do the basics really well.

"But we didn't get out to the ball and allowed them to flash it across then we weren't tight enough.

"I was really disappointed with their second goal. Amari'i Bell was in control and just had to defend it properly.

"We rallied and came back really strongly with a good impact from the bench, but you can't come to Wolves and give them a two-goal lead and expect to win."
