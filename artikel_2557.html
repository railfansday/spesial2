Update On Arsenal And Their Pursuit Of This Real Madrid Talent: Should Arteta Move In For Him?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

In a recent report, journalist Pete O'Rourke offered an update on Arsenal and their pursuit of Real Madrid talent Endrick. It is understood that Los Blancos are unwilling to let the Brazilian attacker leave on loan this January amid interest from the Gunners.

Endrick has struggled to earn a regular first-team spot at the Madrid club this season, but has put in a few eye-catching displays for them in the final third. The 20-year-old has registered one goal and grabbed one assist in seven matches for Los Blancos in this campaign so far across multiple competitions.

The Brazilian talent is currently one of the most gifted forwards in La Liga. Hence, it is no surprise that the Gunners are in the mix to sign him next year.

His current contract at the Santiago Bernabeu Stadium will expire in the summer of 2030, which could make it tough for Arsenal to sign him on the cheap this winter or at the end of this season.

Endrick excels at finding a yard of space for himself to get some strikes in at goal. He has the eye to create the odd chance for others around him on the offensive end of the field and is a decent dribbler with the ball at his feet.

The Brazilian sensation can chip in by scoring and creating a few important goals for his team. Endrick is primarily a centre-forward but can also serve as a right-sided wide player if needed. However, he is far from a finished product and will need time to adapt to the physical demands and high intensity of the Premier League if the Gunners manage to land him next year.

We can expect Endrick to bring more quality and depth to Arsenal head coach Mikel Arteta's frontline. He has what it takes to fight for a regular first-team spot at the Emirates Stadium.

At 20, the future looks quite promising for Endrick as long as he continues to improve with each year. Hence, Arteta would be wise to move in for him in the January transfer period or next summer.
