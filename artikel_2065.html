Norwich City fans may be shocked at what's happening with Johannes Hoff Thorup

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Norwich City are embarking on a fifth consecutive year in the Championship, in what has been a frustrating period on the whole for the club.

After years of going up and down the divisions, the Canaries haven’t really sustained a proper promotion push since their relegation in 2022, as the one time they did reach the play-offs, they were embarrassed by Leeds United at Elland Road.

In that timeframe, current boss Philippe Clement is the fifth manager to sit in the dugout, with the previous four all ultimately struggling at Carrow Road.

One man who certainly didn’t live up to expectations at Norwich was Johannes Hoff Thorup, who didn’t finish the 2024/25 campaign, which ended in a disappointing mid-table finish.

The decision to turn to the Dane was a bold one, as Thorup was only 35, and his managerial career consisted of less than 70 games as a head coach in his home country of Nordsjaelland.

Whilst he did very well, creating a dynamic, attacking team that finished second in the Danish Superliga, there were still major doubts about how that would translate to English football.

There were some encouraging signs, and Thorup certainly delivered on the attacking football front, as Norwich scored 71 times and conceded 77 in Thorup’s 47 games in charge.

But, they couldn’t get the consistent results they wanted, and Thorup left the club in April, meaning he failed to see out the full season in charge.

To his credit, Thorup hasn’t let his departure from Norwich impact him in his career, and he didn’t take long to get back into the game, joining Rapid Vienna at the end of December last year.

A very underwhelming end to the previous campaign followed, with Thorup’s side winning just one of their final eight games.

Yet, a full pre-season and a productive transfer window has made all the difference, as the capital city side are enjoying a remarkable 2026/27 season so far.

A 3-0 win over Grazer AK on Saturday made it ten wins on the bounce in all competitions, and the team have picked up 36 points out of 39 in the league, which has left them six points clear at the top.

In that ten-game winning run, they have scored 35 times, conceded just four, and recorded seven clean sheets, so they have been totally dominant over recent months.

There is no animosity towards Thorup from the Norwich fans, as many questioned whether he deserved to be sacked.

As well as that, some felt he didn’t receive the necessary support from the board, who abandoned the project they had outlined with the boss quicker than they should have.

So, it wasn’t a bitter ending, and Thorup would have understood that the results weren’t good enough, and they did keep making the same mistakes in games, so you can understand why the hierarchy may have felt a chance was required.

In some cases in football, it can be the right person at the wrong time, and perhaps Thorup falls into that category with Norwich.

Liam Manning was the man who was named as Thorup’s successor, and that turned out to be a poor appointment by the Canaries.

However, with the club facing relegation to League One, they replaced the ex-MK Dons chief with Philippe Clement, and that has proven to be a masterstroke.

Under the Belgian, Norwich went on a superb run last season that moved them clear of any relegation fears, and, after a slow start in this campaign, they have now started to click into gear.

With Clement producing a stylish, attacking team, Norwich are now firmly in the hunt to win promotion, and fans will be delighted at the style of play he is serving up.

So, whilst Thorup’s progression with Rapid Vienna may have Norwich wondering whether he should’ve stayed longer, the way Clement has got the team performing means there won’t be any regrets at the Dane’s exit anymore.
