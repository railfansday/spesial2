West Ham work on signing Harry Amass with Man United exit 'likely' - Ex-chief scout

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

West Ham United are among a number of clubs keeping close tabs Harry Amass' situation at Manchester United.

The 19-year-old is very highly regarded at Old Trafford, where they have high hopes for his development, but has struggled for regular game time so far this season.

He has yet to make a Premier League appearance, and despite featuring twice in the Champions League, could be allowed to move on in search of more regular game time.

Former West Ham and Man United scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Amass is being tracked by a number of clubs, with the Hammers now "certainly" among them as they work on a potential deal.

The left-back impressed in the Championship last season, where he had spells with both Sheffield Wednesday and Norwich City, and could return to the second tier.

Meanwhile, he is out of contract at Man United at the end of the season, and a permanent move could be entertained in January if they do not make progress in renewal talks.

Football Insider revealed during the summer that Man United were open to letting Amass leave on loan, with clubs including Wolves having made contact over a deal.

However, their failure to bring a new left-back to the club derailed those plans, and Amass was kept around as a capable backup option in a position where they lack depth.

His lack of involvement since then has likely changed both his and Man United's stance, and West Ham could now be prepared to offer him a way out.

"Harry Amass is a very good footballer, and they rate him highly at Man United," Brown told Football Insider.

"The problem is, he hasn't been playing much this season, so then you start asking about why that might be, and whether they feel he's ready to play in the Premier League.

"Personally, I think he needs to play a bit more senior football before he's ready for Man United, and that might be the club's stance as well.

"The thing is, his contract is up in the summer, so they're not going to want to let him go on loan just to lose him for free at the end of the season if he doesn't extend his deal.

"In that case, it looks likely he could be allowed to leave in January for a fee so that they don't let him go for nothing, and I'm sure there will be clubs interested.

"West Ham are certainly going to be keeping tabs on him, because it's a position they want to strengthen in, and he's already proved he can perform in the Championship.

"If he's going to be available, they could do far worse than Harry Amass, because I do think he will be a very good player with a bit more football under his belt."

West Ham have struggled to find a regular left-back this season, since El Hadji Malick Diouf completed a big-money move to Brentford following their relegation from the Premier League.

Amass is firmly on their radar, having been linked with a number of Championship clubs ahead of the January window, after he was subject to offers during the summer.

Brown told Football Insider on 16 November that Scott Parker is already putting transfer plans in place at West Ham and expects to be given the backing of the board.

Despite an overall feeling that the players available should be more than good enough to win promotion from the Championship, recent results have suggested otherwise.

As a result, Parker is aiming to improve his squad with multiple new additions, including a left-back, when the summer transfer window opens.
