19-year-old Wolves star has to kick-on after Charlton Athletic match-winning performance: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Wolves have started the season very well and are just one point off top spot, currently sitting in 3rd. However, despite that start, a player expected to be a driving force for Cesar Peixoto’s side this season has struggled.

That man is Mateus Mane and he finally delivered the kind of performance Wolves supporters have been waiting for against Charlton Athletic, demonstrating exactly why the club continue to place so much faith in the highly rated teenager.

The 19-year-old provided two assists in a 2-1 victory at Molineux on Saturday, helping Peixoto’s side extend their unbeaten run to seven matches. It was a particularly encouraging afternoon for a player who has endured some frustrating moments during his first full campaign of regular senior involvement.

Mane missed a glorious opportunity in the first half, blazing over from close range after Kieran Trippier’s cross, but responded emphatically after the interval. Within three minutes of the restart, he had beaten his marker before delivering a driven cross for Adam Armstrong to open the scoring. He then used his strength to get beyond Amari’i Bell and square the ball for Rafiki Said to register his first Wolves goal.

There is plenty of evidence that Mane’s performance was not an isolated flash of quality. His underlying numbers suggest he has been an important creative presence despite struggling to produce consistent end product.

According to FotMob, he has recorded three assists and created 30 chances in 16 Championship appearances this season, including seven big chances. His expected assists figure of 2.72 also suggests he has been unfortunate not to receive greater reward for his creativity.

He has additionally completed 21 dribbles, won 66 duels and drawn 26 fouls, demonstrating his ability to carry the ball, beat opponents and make things happen in the final third. However, there is still room for improvement.

Mane is yet to score in the Championship this season, despite registering 34 shots and accumulating an expected goals figure of 2.17. His average FotMob rating of 6.95 reflects a campaign containing some encouraging contributions but also inconsistent performances.

Mane has previously admitted that he has not been at his best, explaining after the Charlton victory that he needed a performance like this to get assists into his system. That honesty is encouraging, particularly given his age and the expectations surrounding him.

He has already shown promise at Premier League level, scoring against West Ham in January 2026, and Wolves clearly believe there is considerably more to come. The teenager had plenty of interest from Premier League sides in the summer but remained at Molineux.

Perhaps the most encouraging aspect of Mane’s afternoon was the public backing he received from Peixoto. The Wolves head coach acknowledged the teenager’s talent while recognising that consistency can be difficult for young players.

He insisted the coaching staff would not give up on him and emphasised the importance of allowing him to play with confidence and courage without placing excessive pressure on his shoulders. That is precisely the environment Mane needs.

Wolves are firmly involved in the promotion picture, and Mane could become increasingly important as the season progresses. His performance against Charlton offered a glimpse of his considerable potential, but the next challenge is turning those moments into regular contributions.

If he can add goals to his growing creative output, Wolves could have an outstanding attacking talent capable of making a significant difference to their promotion ambitions.
