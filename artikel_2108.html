"It's no secret" - Wigan Athletic urged to make Bradford City, Stephen Humphrys transfer

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

It has been a tough start to the season for Wigan Athletic in League One.

Gary Caldwell successfully kept Wigan in League One last season after returning for a second spell in charge at the Brick Community Stadium in February, but it seems another difficult year could be ahead for the Lancashire outfit.

If Caldwell was hoping for a positive start to the campaign to banish the memories of last season's relegation battle, it certainly did not work out that way as Wigan lost their opening three games.

The Latics have improved since then, and they have picked up seven points from their last three matches, including crucial home victories over fellow strugglers Bromley and Barnsley, but they remain too close to the bottom four for comfort.

Wigan currently sit 19th in the League One table, two points clear of the relegation zone, but they have played two games more than all the five sides below them, and they are the joint-third lowest scorers in the division, having netted just 13 goals in 15 games.

When asked which player he would like to see the club sign in January, FLW's Wigan Athletic fan pundit Corey Bissell claimed the Latics should look to reunite with their former striker Stephen Humphrys to solve their goalscoring woes.

Humphrys joined Wigan from Rochdale in the summer of 2021, and he went on to score 18 goals and provide six assists in 98 appearances over the next three years before making the move to Barnsley.

After just one year at Oakwell, Humphrys departed for Bradford City last summer, but despite scoring nine goals and registering six assists in 46 appearances last season to help his side to a fourth-placed finish, he has fallen out of favour at Valley Parade this campaign.

Wigan reportedly made a late move for Humphrys on the final day of the summer transfer window, but while a deal did not materialise on that occasion, Corey is hoping the Latics will reignite their interest in the 29-year-old in January.

"Firstly, we'll probably have to shift bodies out before we can bring any in," Corey told FLW.

"We tried for Luca Woodhouse and Stephen Humphrys on deadline day in the summer, if you believe that, and the reason they apparently didn't come off was because we needed to shift bodies out before we could bring bodies in.

"I think Stephen Humphrys, for me, is probably the number one realistic player we should go and get if we can do that.

"Whether that's on loan or permanently, I don't know.

"It's no secret that a centre-forward would completely change the complexion of our season, so I think that should be the priority.

"Stephen Humphrys isn't playing an awful lot at Bradford, if we can convince them to shift him on and we can make that work, I think he will come in and score a few goals between now and the end of the season to hopefully make sure that we stay up.

"He would be an ideal realistic target to go and get after we tried in the summer."

Despite his limited minutes, Humphrys has still managed to score three goals and provide two assists in seven games for Bradford this season, but just two of those appearances have been in the league.

While both of Humphrys' league appearances have come since Michael Duff replaced Graham Alexander in early October, the managerial change at Valley Parade has not significantly improved his fortunes, which means his future is uncertain ahead of the January transfer window.
