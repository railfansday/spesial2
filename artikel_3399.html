The education price of the MacBook Neo just jumped again

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The MacBook Neo was already a bargain by Mac standards when it launched with a starting price of $599, but for students an education price of $499 made it a steal.

Apple has enough cash literally sitting in bank accounts to absorb that MSRP increase of $100 because it's not costing Apple that much.

I would think that after releasing your first ChromeBook Fighter that you would use that as a lost-leader as a way to increase the volume of of other purchases, such as SUBSCRIPTIONS. Create a version of iCloud just for K-12 students that they can then either merge into their existing iCloud accounts when they finish school or use it as their personal account.

Sadly, that changed back in June when both retail and education prices rose by $100. Now, however, the education price has been raised even further …

As Macworld noted, Apple has increased the education price of the MacBook Neo to $649, a saving of just $50 over the standard price – and a full $150 more than it cost students when it first launched.

If you’re looking for a better deal, the site notes that Apple’s refurbished store is currently offering the base model for $599. Another option is Amazon, where you can buy the M1 MacBook Air for $529, or $579 for the 256GB model. You can also keep an eye on 9to5Toys for upcoming deals.
