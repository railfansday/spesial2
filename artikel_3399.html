‘The Rings of Power’ Officially Ends Season 3 With Season 4 Already Confirmed

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Right now is a busy time to be a Lord of the Rings fan, with several new projects in the works. The most exciting new release on the slate is the franchise’s return to the big screen next year with The Lord of the Rings: The Hunt for Gollum, starring Andy Serkis, Elijah Wood, and Ian McKellen. The film has already been set for release on December 17, 2027, the same day as Avengers: Secret Wars. Fans were disappointed earlier in the year when it was confirmed that Viggo Mortensen would not reprise his role as Aragorn in the film, but Jamie Dornan deserves a chance to see what he can do with the role before fans make any assumptions. There’s also The Rings of Power Season 3, which is still dominating watchlists on Prime Video, to consider.

Amazon took a new approach this time around when it came to releasing the third season of The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power. The first four episodes, half of the entire season, were released two weeks ago, with two more episodes following weekly until the Season 3 finale earlier this week. Not that the show needed a viewership bump, as it had already been in the #1 spot for days on Prime Video, but Amazon announced mere hours before releasing the final two episodes of Season 3 that the show had been renewed for Season 4. Still, many fans are hoping to see Prime Video adapt a more traditional release schedule for The Rings of Power next season, so the show will be around for more than just a few weeks before disappearing for another two years.

Prime Video has yet to announce an official release window for The Rings of Power Season 4, but we do have an idea of when the show will return to streaming. Months before the premiere of Season 3, news broke that Amazon had already doubled down on The Rings of Power and opened a writers room for the third season. At this time, the studio also began laying the groundwork to shoot Season 4 in 2027, which should leave little to no trouble for it hitting a 2028 return date.

Check out the entire first three seasons of The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power on Prime Video and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage of future Lord of the Rings projects.
