3 things the Eagles must do to beat the Cowboys in Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Philadelphia Eagles will enter Thanksgiving with an opportunity to build on their first victory since September, but beating the Dallas Cowboys will require a more complete performance than what Philadelphia produced in its Week 11 win over Pittsburgh. Dallas defeated Philadelphia by 17 points in October, controlling the line of scrimmage and generating explosive plays against Vic Fangio's defense.

The Eagles don't need to abandon their identity or ask Joe Milton to match Dak Prescott throw for throw. They need to dictate enough of the game to prevent Dallas from playing to its strengths.

Here are three things Philadelphia must accomplish Thursday.

Philadelphia's success on first down could determine how much of the playbook remains available to both Sean Mannion and Fangio. For the offense, productive early-down runs from Saquon Barkley and efficient completions from Milton would help neutralize Dallas' pass rush. Defensively, Philadelphia must prevent Javonte Williams from consistently creating favorable second-down situations for Prescott.

Controlling those early exchanges would allow the Eagles to dictate personnel decisions rather than reacting to them.

Philadelphia's fifth-ranked red-zone touchdown rate gives it a chance to capitalize when opportunities arise, but the Eagles cannot afford extended stretches without points against Prescott.

The challenge for Mannion is generating enough scoring opportunities through sustained possessions and occasional explosive plays.

Philadelphia needs seven points at the end of long drives, especially if those possessions keep Dallas' offense on the sideline.

CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens can change the game with a single reception, and Williams demonstrated in October that Dallas doesn't need to rely exclusively on its passing attack. Philadelphia must tackle consistently, avoid communication breakdowns, and force the Cowboys to put together extended drives.

The more offensive plays Dallas must execute to score, the more opportunities Philadelphia's defensive front will have to create negative plays or force a turnover.
