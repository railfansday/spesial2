‘Listen to me’: What John Harbaugh ran up to Liam Coen and said after Giants beat Jaguars

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The New York Giants picked up a statement 23-18 win against the Jacksonville Jaguars at MetLife Stadium.

Both teams came into the game with a lot of confidence, but not many people would've put their money on the Giants getting the job done.

That's how good the Jaguars have been this season, which is probably why their coach, Liam Coen, earned some praise from John Harbaugh after the game.

The game between the New York Giants and the Jacksonville Jaguars was a great watch.

Giants defensive coordinator Dennard Wilson's unit intercepted MVP candidate Trevor Lawrence twice and generated constant pressure.

That led to Jacksonville converting just one of six red-zone trips and settling for two field goals early, four overall.

There was some controversy in the end, with a refereeing decision going against the Jaguars, but their head coach Coen still gracefully shook Harbaugh's hand.

Cameras saw that exchange, where Harbaugh was seen running up to Coen, shaking his hand, pulling him closer and offering him and his side some big praise.

The Athletic's Ian O'Connor transcribed the Giants coach's words on X: "Hey, listen to me. You got a hell of a team. And you have done a great job with this football team. You are a very well-coached football team."

Harbaugh is one of the best coaches in the NFL, and he has seen it all in the game over the last few decades. He knows what it takes to build a championship-winning side.

That is why the 64-year-old's praise for Coen should mean so much to the Jaguars coach, who is still very young in his coaching career.

Despite the defeat, Jacksonville are still 7-3 for the season and remain on top of the AFC South standings. It remains to be seen how they will bounce back when they face the Titans next week.
