RAF Abu Dhabi live stream results, discussion and video highlights | Khamzat vs. Weidman

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Real American Freestyle (RAF) returns TODAY (Sat.., Nov. 14 2026), with RAF Abu Dhabi, streaming live on FOX Nation from inside Etihad Arena in Abu Dhabi, UAE.

In the main event, former UFC Middleweight champion, Khamzat Chimaev, takes on fellow former Middleweight kingpin, Chris Weidman, who is stepping in on short notice for Colby Covington.

In the co-main event, top UFC Lightweight contender, Arman Tsarukyan, takes on former Bellator veteran, Neiman Gracie.

In addition, recently jumped Merab Dvalishvili collides with Marlon Moraes, Zabit Magomedsharipov makes his promotional debut against Giga Chikadze and the daughter of Frank Mir and super prospect, Bella Mir, takes on Kennedy Blaydes for the Women's Middleweight Championship.

RAF Abu Dhabi kicks off at 11 a.m. ET, streaming exclusively on FOX Nation.
