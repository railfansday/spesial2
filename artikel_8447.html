Investing - Theory, News &amp; General • Re: AI Discussion and bubble

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

I simply hold mostly index funds and do not worry about concentration or bubbles as they always work themselves out. If one believes that AI is really a new technology and that data centers will be built in outer space and low orbit, then no, there is no bubble. I worked in Systems and Data Processing for nearly 40 years, so I know the subject. The AI hype of today reminds me of the silver mine craze of the Comstock era in that large fortunes were made in a short amount of time and also collapsed in a short amount of time.

On the subject of concentration, the chairs are always shuffling around in indices as the last ‘big shiny thing’ is replaced by the next ‘big shinny thing’ in leadership. One would think that NVIDIA’s 8.39% of the S&P 500 must be historical, but that was matched by IBM in 1973 at a peak of 8.44%. Speaking of IBM, they made a significant announcement and introduction in August of 1972 of VM/370. Yes, the introduction of computer machine virtualization, and it was not Windows that created virtualization. In 1973 IBM also rolled out Virtual Storage Access Management (VSAM) that revolutionized rapid random record access through index keys on large amount of data. It is still in wide usage today. This is why IBM was the top dog in its day. If one goes back eight years earlier the top ten concentration was nearly 40%, none of which can claim top dog status today, and three even went bankrupt to $0.
