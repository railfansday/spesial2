The analysis should be performed for bond subclasses, and for overall stock and bond portfolios, that may contain more than one bond subclass, not for individual bond funds. A total bond index portfolio (whether or not in a single fund) is difficult to analyze for whether the term premium is rewarded. Total bond is exposed to the Term, Credit, and Prepayment bond factors. It is straightforward to look at whether overall risk is rewarded, given some risk measure, but to assess whether duration is rewarded requires separating out the risk and reward associated with each bond factor.
