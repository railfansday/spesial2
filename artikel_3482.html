DWTS’ Derek Hough Alludes to Childhood Sexual Abuse in New Doc

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Dancing With the Stars judge Derek Hough is opening up about dark moments in his childhood in his new documentary, The Symphony of Dance.

Hough, 41, shared in the film, streaming now on Disney+, that dance became his “safe haven” growing up in Utah.

“Derek was my only boy, and he was a bundle of energy. Some of the family videos you look back and start laughing because he just had to be moving,” Derek’s father, Bruce Hough, said in the doc. “You could not look at Derek without wanting to smile. He somehow always managed to put a positive face on things. And he had a lot of things in his life that were traumatizing and difficult.”

Derek reflected on having neighbors who “weren’t great” to him or his family — which includes current DWTS cohost Julianne Hough.

“They pretty much terrorized my family in a lot of ways,” he recalled. “They hung me up in a tree by my ankles, like, put a gun to my head, spat on me, hogtied me up in a field and left me there.”

Derek went on to seemingly allude to one person sexually abusing him. “And then one of my neighbors was, for lack of a better word, just really inappropriate with me physically,” he said.

After Derek’s parents got divorced, the pair moved into separate apartments across from each other and the children spent time in both locations. Derek shared that he felt “relieved” to leave his neighborhood but said it was a chapter of “uncertainty” for him.

“There was a lot of change and a lot of just, I don’t know. A lot of emotion,” he said. “I just didn’t understand where I was going. I didn’t understand what I was doing. I was kind of left to my own devices. I would skip school, I would skip dance, I wouldn’t go. My dad was at work, my mom was off. And just floating. I was just floating around with no direction.”

At age 12, Derek asked to relocate to London to live with competitive ballroom dancers Shirley and Corky Ballas — and their son Mark Ballas — to focus on his craft. While he was initially supposed to be across the pond for six months, Derek wanted to extend his time and ultimately Julianne joined.

“When it was suggested he spend six months in England going to a performing arts school, I was like, ‘Oh yeah, six months we can do that,’” Bruce said. “And then Christmas time came around, and it was like, ‘Oh, we really want Julianne to come around now!”

From Julianne’s perspective, Derek went through a period of transformation in London while putting his energy into dancing.

“He went from a kid in Utah to all of a sudden, this very focused, very competitive, like, let’s rip these heads off of these other competitors kind of vibe,” she said.

While Derek said it would be a “ritual” to argue about where he and Julianne would live, he ultimately returned to the U.S. when he got a gig on Dancing With the Stars season 5. He went on to win a record six mirrorball trophies and has since taken on a new role as a judge, sitting at the table beside Carrie Ann Inaba and Bruno Tonioli. Derek has also brought his 10-month-old daughter, Everley, into the ballroom for a visit.

“I’ve accomplished so many things in that room, but truly my greatest achievement was, like, holding her,” he exclusively told Us in September of his daughter, whom he shares with wife Hayley Erbert. “It was extraordinary to share that space that has held so much history, and now to hold the future, you know, in my hands was really cool and really special.”

The Symphony of Dance is streaming now on Disney+.
