49ers WR Keenan Allen passes Cris Carter, Marvin Harrison on all-time list

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Amid Brock Purdy and Mike Evans' impressive performances during Sunday's game, San Francisco 49ers wide receiver Keenan Allen also managed to steal the spotlight. Allen achieved another significant career milestone during the 49ers' dominant 35-10 victory over the Minnesota Vikings.

ESPN reporter Nick Wagoner revealed that Allen has officially moved past Cris Carter and Marvin Harrison on the NFL's all-time receptions list among wide receivers.

"Allen is up to 1,103 career receptions, moving past Cris Carter and Marvin Harrison for 6th on the NFL's all time list," the report read.

Allen has proven to be an excellent addition to the 49ers. His 1,103 career receptions now place him behind only legendary wide receivers Jerry Rice, who leads the list with 1,549 catches, and Larry Fitzgerald, who has recorded 1,432 receptions, among the players ahead of him.

The matchup was particularly challenging because Minnesota entered the game with one of the NFL's toughest defenses. However, San Francisco disrupted their plan.

Following the game, head coach Kyle Shanahan made sure to recognize both Evans and Allen for their remarkable longevity and ability to continue performing at an elite level.

"I don't think people talk about Keenan Allen enough," the coach said. "He's been one of the best receivers, him and Mike, over these last 15 years. We got two older guys who are playing at an extremely high level."

The victory gives San Francisco another major boost heading into the final stretch of the regular season. At 9-1, the 49ers have established themselves as one of the NFC's strongest contenders and strengthened their position in the conference's playoff race.

They will now turn their attention toward a crucial divisional matchup against the Seattle Seahawks next Sunday at Levi's Stadium.
