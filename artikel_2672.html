49ers get a Brock Purdy injury scare with Seahawks looming

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The San Francisco 49ers had some unexpected news Wednesday as they began their preparation for Sunday's game against the rival Seattle Seahawks.

Quarterback Brock Purdy missed Wednesday's practice due to an ankle injury he suffered in the second quarter of Sunday's 35-10 victory over the Minnesota Vikings, head coach Kyle Shanahan said.

San Francisco 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy (13) throws a pass against the Dallas Cowboys in Arlington, Texas, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

The injury puts Purdy's status in question ahead of Sunday's game as the 49ers look to take a stranglehold of the NFC West. They're 9-1 and enter the weekend with a three-game lead over the second-place Seahawks with a chance to win the head-to-head tiebreaker with six games remaining.

"We'll see. He can't go today," Shanahan said before practice.

San Francisco 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy reacts before an NFL football game against the Minnesota Vikings in Mexico City, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. AFP via Getty Images

Shanahan said he knew Purdy was hurt from the training staff, who cleared him to stay in the game.

"They always tell you when someone has something," Shanahan said. "But then they came back and said, 'Yeah, he checked out, he should be fine.'"

Purdy played the second half with his ankles taped while the 49ers pulled away from the Vikings' top-ranked defense in Mexico City. Purdy completed 21 of 26 for 255 yards and 3 touchdowns, strengthening his case for MVP candidacy.

San Francisco 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy (13) warms up prior to an NFL football game against the Minnesota Vikings in Mexico City, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. Getty Images

Purdy earned NFC Offensive Player of the Week honors for the fourth time in his career. His 146.0 passer rating against Minnesota marked the seventh time he's posted a rating of at least 145, the most by any quarterback in his first five NFL seasons.

Purdy's ability to sidestep pressure and keep his eyes downfield buys receivers time to uncover, an edge San Francisco could lose if his ankle limits him against Seattle.

If Purdy can't play Sunday, the 49ers would turn to Mac Jones, who kept San Francisco's season afloat in 2025 when Purdy missed eight games with a turf toe injury. The 49ers went 5-3 in Jones' starts while finishing the season 12-5.

They were in the mix for the No. 1 seed all the way until the regular season finale when they lost to Seattle, who also beat the 49ers in the Divisional Round of the playoffs to end their season.
