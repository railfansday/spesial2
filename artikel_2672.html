Justin Herbert, Chargers break through. Ravens run nowhere. MNF winners and losers

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

When you become the first team victimized by the previously winless Los Angeles Chargers, "Darkness" has truly fallen.

Thus was the fate of the Baltimore Ravens, who lost 23-20 to the Bolts on Monday, Nov. 16, in front of a prime-time audience − likely kissing goodbye to their already fleeting playoff hopes in the process.

Making his Ravens debut, QB Jacoby Brissett was not able to ignite an offense that's stagnated badly since Lamar Jackson has been (mostly) out of the lineup for the past six weeks. And the defense wasn't all that much better against a Chargers offense that entered the game averaging a league-worst 15.1 points per game.

And the wins and losses from a forgettable "Monday Night Football" affair didn't stop there.

The Chargers and their quarterback finally broke through. While Herbert's numbers weren't mind-numbing, he led the offense to 23 points, tying the Bolts' season high, and didn't serve up an interception for the first time this season. Herbert's 22-yard TD run in the first quarter set a tone, and the Chargers never trailed.

He didn't get to beat his brother, longtime Ravens coach John Harbaugh, but his highly disappointing team at least got off the schneid and might have dealt Baltimore's flickering postseason aspirations a fatal blow.

The Chargers corner dislodged the ball from Ravens WR Zay Flowers with 2:03 to go, allowing the Bolts to milk the clock and effectively end the game − though Brissett's last-second heave to the end zone nearly produced a miracle as the clock hit triple zeroes.

ESPN's Lisa Salters revealed Monday that Baltimore's Pro Bowl tight end had been playing with a broken hand for nearly two months. But he had his best game of the season Monday, his eight catches for 82 yards were both high points for Andrews' 2026 season.

It churned out a season-best 160 yards, 105 (and a TD) courtesy of second-year RB Omarion Hampton.

A team that features RB Derrick Henry, likely a first-ballot Hall of Famer down the road, didn't manage a single yard on the ground in the first half. The Ravens finished with 16 rushing yards ... on 15 attempts.

Baltimore was really excited about its new all-black look when it was revealed during an offseason event for fans. Too bad they were unveiled for such a forgettable game in front of less than a packed house ... and metaphorically signaled the darkness that's descending over Baltimore's playoff hopes as the team dropped to 4-6.

As he recovers from surgery on his broken and dislocated passing thumb, the likelihood that he should hustle to return around Week 16 or so continues to diminish along with his team's relevance − the Ravens basically 1-5 when Jackson essentially doesn't play, including his one-snap appearance at Buffalo in Week 8.

Those teams, which went a combined 0-46, will remain eminent as the NFL's most feckless squads of the Super Bowl era (since 1966), the Chargers no longer a threat to become the league's first-ever 0-17 outfit.
