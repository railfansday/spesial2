Week 11 live discussion: Which games will you be watching today?

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Week 11 morning matchups aren't the greatest, but it's still football, so we will watch.

The Kansas City Chiefs are hosting the Arizona Cardinals. Josh Johnson gets the start for Arizona in a game that shouldn't be close.

Jacoby Brissett and the Baltimore Ravens look to keep their playoff hopes alive against the Carolina Panthers, who are also fighting for a postseason spot. Bryce Young should score, but that defense is banged up and should allow Brissett and company to pull out a victory.

The Bears should do whatever they want to against the Saints. The same is true for the Bills against the Dolphins. Robert Saleh will look to do what the 49ers couldn't and slow down the Cowboys.

The game of the morning is between the Tampa Bay Buccaneers and the Detroit Lions. Baker Mayfield returns. Aidan Hutchinson is out. I think Tampa Bay could pull off the upset, or at least keep it close against a Lions defense that struggles to get stops without Hutchinson.
