Bayern Munich manager wants Barcelona target to remain at the club in January &#8211; Romano

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Almost halfway through the season, Bayern Munich have found themselves embroiled in a series of problems, starting with the Jamal Musiala controversy and the health of head coach Vincent Kompany.

This is coupled with the fact that Bayern Munich are not even leading the Bundesliga table. As a result, the future of multiple stars has appeared uncertain, including Jonathan Tah.

The former Bayer Leverkusen defender has been linked with a potential move to Barcelona, who are on the lookout for a new central defender in the upcoming January transfer window.

According to Fabrizio Romano, Kompany wants Tah to remain at Bayern Munich amid speculation linking him with a move to Catalonia.

Tah is considered a key player at Bayern. (Photo by Adam Pretty/Getty Images)

Kompany believes Tah has done enough to be considered an important part of his project at Bayern. The club, therefore, are unlikely to give him the green light to leave the Allianz Arena anytime soon.

Romano adds that Barcelona, for their part, have not advanced any negotiations surrounding a potential move for Tah.

It must be noted that Barcelona were interested in a move for Tah even during his time at Leverkusen. But Bayern eventually swooped in and signed him on a free transfer in 2025.

Tah is not getting any younger. (Photo by Stuart Franklin/Getty Images)

Missing out on Jonathan Tah might not be such a big blow, as far as Barcelona are concerned.

The club are currently in need of a technically gifted, left-sided centre-back who can operate as a long-term defensive partner for Pau Cubarsi.

Tah, to that end, is known more for his imposing presence in the backline as well as his aerial prowess, rather than his ability to play the ball out from the back. Moreover, he is more comfortable operating in the right-sided centre-back role, much like Cubarsi.

Not to mention, Tah is already 30 and will soon be in the final stage of his career. Barcelona, therefore, are perhaps better advised to look at other options in the market.

The Catalans have been linked with a move for Dutch prodigy Ruud Nijstad, while Chelsea's Levi Colwill and Manchester United's Lisandro Martinez have also emerged as options.
