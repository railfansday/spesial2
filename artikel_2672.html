Brayden Merwin's 4 TDs power Mountain View into Open quarterfinals

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Holes just kept opening up and Mesa Mountain View quarterback Brayden Merwin kept taking advantage of them.

Merwin ran for touchowns of 28, 41 and 79 yards and threw a 46-yard scoring pass, as No. 9 Mountain View stunned No. 8 Phoenix Brophy Prep, 48-34, on Friday night, Nov. 13, in the first round of the Open state football playoffs at Phoenix Central.

Next up is a Nov. 20 quarterfinal date at No. 1 Peoria Liberty.

Afterwards, coach Andy Litten told his players they deserved to be in the Open. The Toros played their most complete game of the season and avenged a 31-28 loss to Brophy earlier this season.

Brophy falls back into the 6A quarterfinals next Friday.

"We always feel we want to win the battle up front with our offensive line and tight ends," Litten said. "Our kids did a great job. We ran a power read scheme and kind of got them tired and it played into our hands. We took advantage of it."

Merwin got great blocks from the linemen, led by Washington-commit Tye Kennedy.

The scoring started with a 64-yard scoring run by tailback Coy Bodily that gave Mountain View a quick 7-0 lead.

"It's one of our power schemes that we run," Kennedy said. "Being able to have an elusive quarterback and an elusive running back, having two threats in the backfield, it's a blessing to block for them."

Merwin said Brophy defenders were keying the edges to try to take away Bodily, after getting burned by him on the Toros' first possession. That opened the middle for him. He ran straight up the middle on his scoring runs.

"Our QB option opened up nice," Merwin said. "The D-ends were crashing on Coy. Shout out to Coy, though, he's a dog. It opened up for me. The O-line doing their thing all night."

Mountain View (9-2) never trailed, as the Toros opened up a 26-3 lead with less then three minutes left in the half on Kash Skousen's 63-yard interception return. It came after Merwin hit tight end R.J. Guarneri down the middle for a 46-yard touchdown.
