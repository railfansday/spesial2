College basketball roundup: USF men romp to win; San Jose State slips past Fresno State

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Gustav Knudsen scored 18 points and USF bolted to a 25-point halftime lead en route to a 77-56 win over visiting Louisiana on Wednesday.

The Dons (4-1), coming off a loss to Portland State in which they were down 19 at intermission, used a 22-2 run in the first half to take a 40-16 lead against the Ragin' Cajuns (1-3). The USF lead stood at 45-20 at halftime, and the Dons boosted the lead to 36 in the second half.

Ben Oosterbaan had 15 points and Dylan Bordon added 12 points and 10 assists for USF. Isaiah Wilks scored 13 points for Louisiana.

San Jose State 92, Fresno State 86: Mekhi Mason scored 30 points, Joey Chammaa added 23 and the host Spartans (3-2) used a 54-point second half to erase a halftime deficit and defeat the Bulldogs (3-2).

San Jose State trailed 43-38 at the break, but took a 55-54 lead on a layup by Chammaa with 14:54 and did not trail the rest of the way. The 54-point period was the Spartans' biggest of the season.

"I thought we were pretty good that second half," San Jose State coach Tim Miles said in a postgame TV interview. "We shot 58% the second half and that's pretty good basketball. … It's a good win. We're happy about it."

Ross Candelino scored 21 points to lead four Fresno State players in double figures.

Cal Baptist 82, San Jose State 68: Cristina Sánchez Cerqueira had her first double-double with 11 points and 14 rebounds, and Erika Mace and Eve Blagrove added 11 points apiece, but the host Spartans (1-3) were outscored by a combined 20 points in second and third quarters against the Lancers (4-0).

The Spartans led by six in the first quarter, but a 42-20 push by Dallas Baptist gave the Lancers a 16-point lead at halftime.

"Our second and third quarters were just nonexistent," San Jose State coach Jonas Chatterton said in a postgame radio interview. "This team's got to figure out, for some reason we just come out in the third quarter flat. … So it has to get changed."
