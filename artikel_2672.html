NBA Cup 2026: Knockout-round bracket officially set after final night of group-play action

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The knockout rounds of the 2026 NBA Cup are settled after the final night of group-play action.

Only the Dallas Mavericks were guaranteed to advance going into Friday, but the field quickly developed and was officially settled once the San Antonio Spurs defeated the Los Angeles Lakers in a wild comeback.

Boston Celtics (4) at Detroit Pistons (1)

Atlanta Hawks (3) at Philadelphia 76ers (2)

San Antonio Spurs (4) at Dallas Mavericks (1)

Memphis Grizzlies (3) at Portland Trail Blazers (2)

Those games will be played on Dec. 4 and 5.

As a reminder, quarterfinal and semifinal games are played at the higher seed's stadium and count toward regular-season standings and statistics. The championship game, however, does not count and will be played at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis on Dec. 11.
