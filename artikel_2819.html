Vikings’ Kevin O’Connell gives 49ers their flowers after SNF blowout

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Vikings' Kevin O'Connell gives 49ers their flowers after SNF blowout appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Minnesota Vikings' vaunted defense was supposed to give the San Francisco 49ers a close fight in Mexico City on Sunday. Instead, Brock Purdy and the 49ers turned it into a scoring fiesta, torching the Vikings for 28 points in the first half en route to the win, 35-10, at Estadio Banorte.

The 49ers improved to 9-1, while the Vikings fell to 7-3, suffering their third loss in four games.

Minnesota entered the game allowing just 14 points per game, the fewest in the NFL. San Francisco scored 21 points in the second quarter.

Coach Kevin O'Connell acknowledged San Fransico's masterful showing, as reported by ESPN's Kevin Seifer.

"They got us tonight. They played better than us in all three phases. We'll tip our cap to them and move on," said O'Connell.

Purdy was not bothered by the high altitude in Mexico City, completing 21 of 26 passes for three touchdowns and zero interceptions.

Kirk Cousins, in his second game since his return to Minnesota, went 26-of-38 for 235 yards, zero touchdowns, and one pick.

The game was played at more than 7,300 feet above sea level, as noted by the Associated Press' Josh Dubow. To prepare, the Vikings used a special elevation room for workouts.

Nothing, however, prepared them for the 49ers' pressure. San Francisco opened the contest with a seven-play, 72-yard drive, which saw George Kittle score on a 17-yard connection with Purdy. It proved to be a bad omen for the Vikings.

But as O'Connell mentioned, there is no need to sound the alarm, as they can bounce back against the Atlanta Falcons in Week 12.
