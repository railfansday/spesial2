Pittsford vs. Rush-Henrietta updates, score from football title game

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Pittsford versus Rush-Henrietta in the Section V football Class AA championship game is a matchup that can be called the "Unfinished Business Bowl."

Both teams had realistic thoughts about winning a Section V title last year, only to see their dreams fade.

The Rush-Henrietta Royal Comets zoomed out to a 4-0 start, outscoring their opponents, 144-28. But in game 5, the Pittsford Panthers came to R-H and left with a 35-21 victory, while Royal Comets quarterback Brendan Mangone suffered a shoulder injury. When the Royal Comets lost to Penfield during the semifinal round of the Section V tournament, their season was over with a 6-3 record.

Pittsford entered the 2025 Section V Class AA tournament with the same number of wins and losses after an up-and-down regular season. The Panthers failed to win back-to-back games until the postseason. Wins over Fairport and McQuaid put the Panthers into the 2025 Section V Class AA final, where Pittsford lost a three-point lead in the fourth quarter, then the game to Penfield.

This time around, both teams enter the championship with just one loss. Pittsford (9-1) and Rush-Henrietta (8-1) meet in a rematch of their Sept. 10 regular-season game, which R-H won 26-19.
