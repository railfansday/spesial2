Iowa State football star Aiden Flora will return to Cyclones next season

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Iowa State football and its fans have one more thing to be thankful for this Thanksgiving.

Former walk-on turned star running back Aiden Flora announced on social media on Nov. 23 that he is returning to Iowa State next season, squashing any worries or concern over a potential departure.

"LOYAL SON FOREVER TRUE!" Flora posted on X. "With God as my guide and my love for the Iowa State football team and Cyclone Nation, I've made the decision to stay home! Go Cyclones and Happy Thanksgiving!"

Flora is relishing a breakout season that has seen him earn national-level success and attention. He currently leads the nation with 1,506 rushing yards. Flora also has 14 rushing touchdowns, a punt return touchdown and has two passing touchdowns. The Adel native is also a semifinalist for the Doak Walker Award, an accolade given to the FBS' top running back in the country, and a finalist for the Paul Hornung Award, which is awarded to the most versatile player in college football.

He has rushed for 170 or more yards and at least one touchdown in each of the last four games for the Cyclones.

Flora's intent to return next season solidifies the Cyclones' backfield, bolsters the offense, and gives a surge of morale and excitement for head coach Jimmy Rogers and the football program. There's still one final regular-season game against Kansas State on Nov. 28, but soon it'll be time for Rogers and his staff to replenish the roster for the 2027 campaign. Having Flora around gives a nice foundation to help build on offensively.
