Sonny Gray Details Why Reunion With Red Sox Did Not Happen

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Sonny Gray signed a two-year, $55 million deal with the Minnesota Twins on Wednesday.

Gray had a great year for the Boston Red Sox, posting his best ERA in a full season in his 14-year career. He was key to the team's success and turnaround last season, but he told reporters on Thursday that a reunion with the Red Sox never got close.

"As far as a reunion, it never really came up," Gray said Thursday on Zoom, per MassLive's Chris Cotillo. "I think the times we did have any type of conversations, it never really got close, it felt like, which is completely OK. As far as a reunion, it felt like it was never gaining any traction."

It does not sound like the Red Sox were losing sleep over Gray leaving. Given what Craig Breslow said to reporters in Las Vegas during the general manager meetings, it's not a surprise.

"I think the rotation is in a really good place," Breslow said on Nov. 9, per Cotillo. "I think it should be a strength of our 2027 team. Whether that's through Garrett (Crochet) being healthy, the continued development of Payton (Tolle), Ranger (Suarez) being a steady, stalwart presence for us, Jake Bennett who emerged and Brayan Bello, who when he came back from being out on option, he looked reinvigorated and was effective in multiple different roles. And getting (Johan Oviedo), Kutter (Crawford) and Tanner (Houck) healthy, I think we should have options and we'll have some competition."

The Red Sox like what they have in the rotation without Gray, but if Bello struggles as a starter, as he did for most of the year, letting Gray walk will be seen as a huge mistake.
