i dont know about stc policies, but the face value of ltc is typically quoted as x# of years derived from $y coverage amount/day for a total face value of $z00,000, but this number assumes using straight and continuous full care. in my late mom’s case she was able to spread out her ltc face value at lower daily payments for reduced care reimbursement to last over a longer term. a locally based social service agency was most helpful explaining the rules.

so for a texas stc policy, are you locked into spending all $40k all within one year, or is the $40k an estimate of what one year of full care will cost, but you can stretch it into say 4 yrs of semi care costing $10k/yr? if the latter why not get the stc policy? please connect with a social worker who is well versed in stc and ltc matters before deciding.
