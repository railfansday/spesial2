Jermaine Haley, WWE Wrestler Known as Bronson Reed, Dies at 38

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Haley died Friday following medical complications, the WWE announced.

Jermaine Haley, the WWE wrestler known as Bronson Reed, has died. He was 38.

Haley died Friday following medical complications, the WWE announced. No further details were reported.

“Jermaine was a larger-than-life figure in WWE. But more importantly, he was a phenomenal person, a devoted family man, and a beloved colleague,” the WWE shared in an Instagram post. “Our thoughts and deepest condolences are with Jermaine’s entire family, including his pregnant wife Paige, their daughter, as well as all his friends, co-workers and fans.” Related Stories Movies Gerald R. Born in 1988 in Adelaide, South Australia, Haley began his professional wrestling career in 2007 under the names Jonah Rock and J-Rock in the Australian independent circuit. He accumulated titles and worked on various promotions before his transition into the WWE.

He officially joined the WWE in 2019, performing on its training ground NXT. He was would become an NXT North American champion, but was released in 2021. Haley continued competing with New Japan Pro-Wrestling and Impact Wrestling.

Haley did not stay away from the WWE for long, as he joined the main roster in 2022, and went on to win the Andre the Giant Memorial Battle Royal in 2024. His most recent WWE appearance was roughly three weeks ago at the Crown Jewel event in Saudi Arabia, where he faced off against Oba Femi.

Haley married his high school sweetheart, Paige, and they welcomed their first child in February 2024. As the WWE announced, his wife was pregnant with their second child at the time of his death.
