Ashley Young Named Surprise Ex-England and Man Utd Defender As His Toughest Opponent

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Former Manchester United and England winger Ashley Young has named the toughest opponent he's ever come up against.

Young retired at the end of last season, calling time on an illustrious career that spanned three different decades and six different clubs. Breaking through at Watford, the Englishman made a name for himself at Aston Villa in the late 2000s before signing for United in the summer of 2011.

Making 261 appearances across nine seasons for the north-west club, Young won the Premier League, the FA Cup, the League Cup and the Europa League during his time at Old Trafford.

Later crowned a Serie A champion with Inter Milan, Young returned to Villa in the latter stages of his career before enjoying late spells at Everton and Ipswich Town.

A hip injury derailed him in his final season before hanging up his boots with the Tractor Boys.

Having played at the top level for so long, including in the Champions League and at international tournaments with England, Young naturally faced up against some of the very best to ever play the game.

He shared the pitch with the likes of Cristiano Ronaldo, Lionel Messi and Neymar, yet none of these players was cited by the former wide man when he was asked who his toughest ever opponent was.

Speaking to Everton's official YouTube Channel back in 2024, Young surprisingly named former United defender Wes Brown as the player he found most difficult to play against.

"Because I'm a winger, aren't I? So obviously, a defender is my toughest opponent, so Wes Brown."

Young coincidentally joined United in the same summer Brown left Old Trafford, meaning their paths may only ever have crossed at club level for a matter of weeks.

That said, Young's early England career did clash with Brown's late Three Lions ventures. The seven-time Premier League winner managed eleven caps from 2007 onwards, which is when the ex-Watford star's international career took off.

The Everton goalkeeper chose a top player.

The pair faced each other many times before Young signed for United, when Brown was representing the Red Devils. With Young formerly a winger, and Brown regularly used as a full-back by Sir Alex Ferguson, the two players would indeed have been directly pitted against each other on a handful of occasions.

The forward also would've had Brown as his opposite number after moving to Manchester, with the ex-Kerala Blasters man spending five years at Sunderland after departing the north-west.
