The Best Pictures From ONE SAMURAI 5

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

ONE Championship returned to Tokyo, Japan, for the fifth installment of its action-packed monthly event series, and it delivered high-paced action from start to finish.

This past Wednesday, November 18, fans inside EBARA WAVE Arena Ota and around the globe watched ONE SAMURAI 5 unfold with a card that had something for every martial arts aficionado.

In the main event, ONE Strawweight MMA World Champion Joshua "The Passion" Pacio put on a dominant display against hometown hero Keito "Pocket Monk" Yamakita. The Filipino star put the Japanese challenger to sleep with an arm-triangle choke in the fourth round to retain his gold and prove why he is the best strawweight MMA fighter on the planet today.

In the co-main event, Ryujin "SHINDO" Nasukawa sent a strong message to every atomweight striker, including ONE Atomweight Muay Thai World Champion Nadaka Yoshinari, with a 36-second knockout of Raichi Nagai. The jaw-dropping finish earned Ryujin a US$50,000 performance bonus.

Also, former ONE Strawweight MMA World Champion Jarred "The Monkey God" Brooks returned to winning ways with a razor-close split-decision victory over former DEEP Champion Tatsumitsu "The Sweeper" Wada.

Before we turn the page to The Inner Circle 40 and ONE Friday Fights 180, take a look at the best pictures from ONE SAMURAI 5, courtesy of our ringside photographers.
