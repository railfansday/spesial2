Chargers-Patriots Game Preview: How will Mike McDaniel warp the offense for D.J. Uiagalelei?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Chargers (1-9) are back at home this week to face the Patriots (5-5) in the hopes of earning their first win inside SoFi Stadium this season. New England is coming off their bye week and have had two weeks to get backup quarterback Tommy DeVito up to speed in the wake of Drake Maye's injury that will sideline him until at least the postseason.

Two inexperienced backup quarterbacks are not what most fans would like to watch during a football game in Novemeber, but sometimes that's the reality of the NFL. To look at this from a more positive view, fans of both teams will get their first look at their backups to see what their team truly has behind the starters. This could be a good or bad experience, but overall everyone will leave the day more enlightened! That's a win, right?

The Chargers are playing for nothing but their own prides while the Patriots are still fighting to right the ship and hopefully find a way into the postseason. Both teams have dealt with injuries this year to impact players, and that goes beyond the quarterback spot. Wideout A.J. Brown will not play in this one which is a good thing for a Chargers defense that could be without half of their starting unit on Sunday.

Being short-handed like that never fun, and it makes the hopes that DJU could somehow lead them to an improbable victory even slimmer.

But hey, when everyone is going poorly, all you can do is throw your hands up and try to enjoy the ride.

Below are the three things I'll be watching closely when these teams take the field on Sunday.

Just like everyone would have predicted before the season began, the quarterback matchup for the Chargers' rematch with the Patriots is D.J. Uiagalelei and Tommy DeVito. Better grab your popcorn!

Uiagalelei is a second-year UDFA who only has one seven career pass attempts to his name, all of which came in garbage time against the Texans. He completed only two passes in those snaps, but one did go for a touchdown to Derius Davis.

DeVito comes into this game with eight starts under his belt and nine career touchdown passes to four inteceptions. He has not started a game this year, but has thrown for 132 yards, one touchdown, and one interception in three appearances.

With two inexperienced quarterbacks on the field, you can fully expected both defenses to rev up their pass rush early and often to rattle them as soon as possible.

It's not like the Chargers were throwing the ball all that much before Justin Herbert's concussion, but it's likely to be even less without the Chargers starting quarterback.

The Chargers have run the football at least 30 times in each of their last three games. They rushed for 138 yards against the Texans, 160 against the Ravens, and 158 against the Texans. Omarion Hampton scored against the Ravens but Herbert has the other two rushing touchdowns over those three games with one coming against the Ravens and Jets, apiece.

Uiagalelei also has the athleticism to produce on the ground if he wants, and it'll be interesting to see if McDaniel uses him in a similar way as Herbert as of late.

In all honesty, I would not be shocked if the Chargers set a new season-high for rushing attempts in a game against the Patriots. It would minimize the pressure on DJU and keep the play-action game alive to make sure the defense can't get to overeager against such a green player.

If all goes well, maybe we get Hampton's second 100-yard game of the season, as well.

The Chargers defense looked the worst they have all year against the Jets in a game where starting linebacker Daiyan Henley and cornerback Tarheeb Still were sidelined with injuries. Star safety Derwin James has also missed the past month and isn't coming back for multiple weeks while on IR.

As of Friday morning, the Chargers were on pace to play a number of backups on defense thanks to a laundry list of a injury report through the first two practices.

Henley and Still have not practiced in any capacity and will likely miss another week. Joining them on the sidelines through Wednesday and Thursday were edge rushers Tuli Tuipulotu and Akheem Mesidor, and defensive tackle Dalvin Tomlinson. If all of those latter three do not practice in some way on Friday, the Chargers could play the Patriots without half of their starting defense.

The secondary is already a shallow group and now the front seven gets even thinner if two of the team's top three edge rushers are forced to miss Sunday. It would be Bud Dupree starting opposite Khalil Mack and Caleb Murphy as the No. 3 pass rusher.

Once again, the linebacker rotation will feature Del'Shawn Phillips, Marlowe Wax, Troy Dye, and veteran Denzel Perryman. No studs by any means and they'll have to do better at stopping the run game which New England will likely try to lean on, especially seeing how consistent the Jets were at getting chunks in Week 11.

Looking at this injury report, the Chargers will have to get a number of depth pieces to play above themselves in over to avoid falling to 1-10.
