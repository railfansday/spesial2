Washington Football WR Duo Questionable For Season Finale at Oregon

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Washington Huskies star wide receiver duo Dezmen Roebuck and Chris Lawson, along with five other players, have an uphill climb to be deemed healthy enough to suit up in Saturday's regular season finale at Oregon.

Both second-year players were listed as questionable on the Huskies' initial Big Ten availability report on Wednesday along with fourth-year defensive lineman Darin Conley, fourth-year edge Logan George, second-year edge rusher Devin Hyde, second-year kicker Hunter McKee and sixth-year running back Trey Cooley.

Second-year offensive lineman John Mills is also listed on the injury report, though his availability at the midweek point is more favorable with a probable designation to play against the Ducks.

Roebuck was also listed as questionable on last Wednesday's availability report ahead of UW's home finale against Indiana before being upgraded to probable on Thursday and Friday. The 5-foot-11, 190-pound sophomore went on to boast a career-high 10 catches in the Huskies' 27-24 overtime victory to go with a team-high 92 receiving yards, only the second time this season he finished with 90-plus yards in Big Ten play.

Lawson and Conley made their first appearance on the availability report this season, both of whom, if ruled unable to go Saturday would deal a significant blow to the Huskies' odds of coming out of Eugene with the eighth win of the season for third-year coach Jedd Fisch.

Conley, a 6-foot-3, 290-pound transfer from Ball State, has come on strong over the past month for second-year defensive coordinator Ryan Walters dating back to UW's 38-26 road victory on October 16 against Purdue.

Over the past five games, the defensive tackle has recorded 3 tackles for loss, all of which have been sacks with one each in back-to-back games at Michigan State and last Saturday against IU, becoming only the second defender to be credited with a full sack in consecutive games in the past two seasons since edge rusher Zach Durfee achieved the same feat last November at Wisconsin and against Purdue.

George, a veteran transfer from Ohio State, appeared on UW's availability report for a third straight week despite playing in both of the past two games against the Spartans and Hoosiers, which should offer a glimmer of hope for Conley when it comes to projecting his availability in Saturday's rivalry game against the Ducks.

The other three players—Hyde, Cooley, and McKee—listed as questionable for the Huskies are each in a different spot with their injuries. McKee was a late addition and ultimate scratch last weekend while Hyde, who missed his first game at UW after appearing in 23 consecutive games, and the veteran Cooley have a chance to play but are completely up in the air for Saturday.

As Fisch noted earlier this month, second-year running back Quaid Carr and third-year back Jordan Washington have been ruled out once again, with only Carr possibly able to return for the bowl game UW has already clinched with a 7-4 record going into the final game of the regular season.
