4 takeaways from Kansas State basketball's humiliating loss to Gonzaga

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The only thing that went Kansas State's way in its blowout loss to Gonzaga is that most people were asleep and didn't see it.

The Wildcats were embarrassed in a 101-61 loss to No. 20 Gonzaga on Tuesday, Nov. 24, at T-Mobile Arena in Las Vegas. The first-round game of the Players Era 16 tournament tipped off at 11:49 p.m. CT and ended at 1:41 a.m.

Kansas State will play No. 15 Alabama in Las Vegas on Thursday, Nov. 26, at 8 p.m. CT in a consolation semifinal.

Gonzaga overwhelmed K-State from start to finish. Here are our takeaways.

The Wildcats tried to match the Bulldogs' size advantage early on, but it didn't work. A starting lineup that included Brock Vice, Timotej Malovec and JT Rock couldn't match up with the Bulldogs' bigs. Gonzaga finished with a 50-22 scoring advantage in the paint and dominated the boards 48-30.

At the same time, Gonzaga's ball movement often had K-State in a blender. Open shots were aplenty, finishing 11-for-28 from beyond the arc.

Xaivian Lee nearly had a triple-double for the Bulldogs, finishing with 11 points, 11 assists and nine rebounds. Gonzaga also had a ton of success in transition, outscoring the Wildcats 30-6 on the fast break.

The Wildcats shot 33.3% from the field and 7-for-30 from deep.

Yes, K-State was picked to finish at or near the bottom of the Big 12, and growing pains are to be expected with a first-year head coach. Patience would be nice, but a loss like this, days after losing to Campbell, only raises concerns.

Alexander has said repeatedly how his roster isn't talented enough to hang with Campbell, let alone Gonzaga, if it has a bad night. This night was as bad as it gets.

We knew this was a possibility when it was shared that K-State would have a $6 million roster, among the cheapest among high-major programs, going into the year.

K-State might have some good nights when it shoots well from deep. But with the Houstons, Arizonas and Kansas of the world on the schedule, more nights like this one could be in their future.

The Wildcats' 89-82 win over Creighton on Nov. 10 looks better by the day. The Bluejays beat defending-champion Michigan on Tuesday, winning 99-73. Creighton has wins over Iowa and Michigan since losing to K-State.

The Wildcats won't have a reunion with former coach Jerome Tang on Thanksgiving. The Baylor associate head coach helped lead the Bears to a 68-63 win over the Crimson Tide before K-State and Gonzaga tipped off.

The only time K-State and Baylor will play during the regular season will be on Jan. 2 in Waco, Texas.

Alabama's 63 points were the fewest it's scored since 2023. The Tide came into the game averaging 99.4 points per game. That probably doesn't bode well for the Cats on Thursday.
