The Week 11 afternoon schedule is short on must-see games

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

As the NFL’s haves and the have-nots continue to declare themselves, it’s becoming challenging at times to find compelling games.

And while the coming week has five of them in national, standalone windows (Vikings-49ers, Packers-Rams, Bears-Lions, Chiefs-Bills, and Broncos-Steelers), the eleventh Sunday is a bit lacking. To say the least.

In the early window, three of the seven games feature double-digit spreads. The 8-1 Bills (-16.5) host the 1-8 Dolphins, who don’t have their starting quarterback. The 7-2 Chiefs (-15.5) host the 3-7 Cardinals, who are down to their fourth quarterback of the season — and the third in three weeks.

The 7-2 Bears (-11.5) host the 3-6 Saints.

None of the early games feature two teams with winning records. Bucs-Lions is arguably the most compelling of the window, given the potential for Detroit stepping into a trap four days before the Thanksgiving showdown against the Bears.

The late time slot is led by the 7-2 Jaguars (-6) at the 5-3-1 Giants. It’s the only game of the weekend featuring a pair of division leaders.

Having six teams on a bye this weekend doesn’t help.

Although it’s impossible in May to know how the various games of a given schedule will look in late November (Steelers-Eagles had the potential on paper to be the best game of the day), it’s a convenient time for a subpar Sunday.
