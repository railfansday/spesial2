Colts surrender 26 unanswered points as Daniel Jones benched in blowout road loss to Texans

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Houston, the problem here is the Indianapolis Colts offense is broken and has not reached the end zone in 17 consecutive drives. In their final primetime appearance on Thursday Night Football, the Colts surrendered 26 unanswered points in a 26-3 blowout loss to the rival Houston Texans.

Outside of the opening drive, there was nothing left from this depleted offensive unit. Backup quarterback Anthony Richardson Sr. relieved Daniel Jones for the final eight minutes of regulation. Richardson trucked Texans' linebacker Jake Hansen on his first snap for a minimal two yards, then nearly delivered Houston an interception before another anticlimactic three-and-out ended his night.

The Colts were poised to establish an early offensive rhythm after rookie Seth McGowan returned the opening kickoff to the 40. Jones found receiver Ashton Dulin wide open on a sit route against Houston's zone coverage, allowing the speedster to bounce outside and race to the sideline for 25 yards. McGowan followed with a tough run up the middle for eight yards, then Jonathan Taylor was patient enough to let a hole develop and surge forward to move the chains.

Texans' defensive tackle Logan Hall blew up McGowan's next run in the backfield, then Jones was forced to throw the ball away on third down. The Colts settled for an early chip shot as kicker Spencer Shrader connected on a 34-yard field goal to give Indianapolis its lone lead.

The Texans opened with a promising drive, but a forced pass ricocheted into an early break for the Colts. C.J. Stroud's throw bounced off the helmet of rookie safety AJ Haulcy before it tipped off Cam Bynum's hands and back into Haulcy's grasp for his first career interception.

Indianapolis could not have asked for a better start as Jones recognized the underneath coverage, went through his progressions and fired a dart down the sideline to receiver Quez Watkins for a 26-yard completion to march right back into Houston territory. The drive stalled after a dropped pass by Taylor was followed by a pass deflection from Hansen, then All-Pro edge rusher Will Anderson chased down Jones and forced him to get rid of it out of bounds. The Colts intended to settle for a kick near midfield, but Shrader's 56-yarder was shanked wide left.

Despite taking a hit to his right bicep from Colts' defensive back Kapena Gushiken, Stroud stepped up into the pocket and launched a missile down the right sideline to receiver Nico Collins, who elevated over cornerback Justin Walley to haul in a 42-yard touchdown and put Houston ahead 7-3 late in the first frame.

The Colts' next possession unraveled inside of Texans' territory after receiver Anthony Gould took a bubble pass through a hole on the field side for 31 yards. Jones' next pass would have been picked off if Texans cornerback Derek Stingley Jr. weren't out of bounds. On the first play of the second quarter, Jones tossed up a meatball sub into double coverage to avoid the sack and was picked off by cornerback Kamari Lassiter over rookie Deion Burks.

Jones finished 12-for-27 for 144 pass yards and one interception, the sixth straight game he's thrown an interception. Shane Steichen has continued to rely on the eighth-year veteran, despite leading the NFL with 15 turnovers and never finishing a single game with more touchdowns than turnovers this season.

The Texans mounted a pair of long drives in the second quarter as Stroud shared the wealth with five different pass-catchers. Facing third-and-2 from just outside the red zone, Stroud delivered a perfect post to Kayshon Boutte near the goal line. Boutte rose over cornerback Kenneth Harris to secure the contested 24-yard touchdown catch, extending Houston's lead to 14-3 midway through the second quarter.

The Texans were able to capitalize on a choice to send Shrader out for a 62-yard field goal attempt, which was blocked by Hall to seize possession inside Colts territory. Houston leaned on its run game to drain most of the remaining clock before settling for a 27-yard chip shot from Ka'imi Fairbairn to take a 17-3 lead into halftime.

The stampede found some life to start the second half as defensive tackle Adetomiwa Adebawore rushed past the left guard to corral Stroud from behind and secure his team-high fifth sack this season, sending Houston behind the sticks. Houston would avenge the hit on Stroud after a precision punt pinned Indianapolis inside its own 5-yard-line as Anderson rushed inside of right tackle Matt Goncalves to sack Jones in the end zone for a safety.

The Texans took advantage of the short field as Stroud connected with Tank Dell on three consecutive throws for a combined 27 yards to breach the red zone. Houston's offensive line gave Stroud a full seven seconds to scramble outside of the pocket, find tight end Marlin Klein near the pylon for a 13-yard TD that capped off a 12-play drive to seal the first home win for Houston.

The Colts could never get their rushing attack in any rhythm, held to just 38 rushing yards on 17 attempts. The rookies continued to impress on the defensive side as C.J. Allen led the game with 16 tackles, while Haulcy forced the takeaway to terminate Houston's opening drive.

Indianapolis (3-8) enters its bye week for Thanksgiving, with 10 days to prepare to host the New York Giants (5-3-1) at Lucas Oil Stadium on Nov. 29.
