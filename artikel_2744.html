UFL's BattleHawks work out a deal to stay at dome in St. Louis

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

As of last month, the UFL had a stadium issue in St. Louis. As of now, it’s resolved.

Via Fox 2 in St. Louis, the UFL and the powers-that-be have struck a deal to create better flexibility in the scheduling of five BattleHawks home games at The Dome at America’s Center.

Under the prior schedule, the BattleHawks would have played four straight home games, followed by five straight road games, capped by a home game. The details of the revised schedule have not yet been announced.

UFL co-owner Mike Repole, who has been aggressively using Twitter to market the league and to squabble directly with critics, took a social-media victory lap.

“We weren’t going to settle,” he posted. “We pushed for better. We got it done!!!! A BIG THANK YOU to our partners at The Dome at America’s Center.”

While the UFL is largely a made-for-TV league, it has been trying to create better visuals by playing in stadiums that are easier to fill. The size of the dome in St. Louis makes that harder. Stacking four home games together in four straight weekends won’t make that any easier.
