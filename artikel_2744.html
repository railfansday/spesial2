‘Love It,’ ‘He Was Afraid of Getting Suspended’ — NFL Fans Take Notice as 49ers Star George Kittle Wears Anti-Cowboys T-Shirt Under His Jersey

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

For George Kittle, last week's buildup to a trip to face the Dallas Cowboys came with a question about an old T-shirt.

The San Francisco 49ers tight end caught six of nine targets for 74 yards in Sunday's 46-40 win over the Cowboys at AT&T Stadium, and by Monday, one viral post claiming Kittle had flashed his anti-Dallas shirt to fans after the game had fans weighing in. The gray tee carries a profane two-word message for Dallas in blue lettering, and Kittle first showed it off during a 2023 win over the Cowboys.

Some of the fans who reacted to Monday's post circled back to what Kittle said when he was asked about it before the Week 10 trip to Arlington, Texas, including his comments on the risk of a suspension.

Asked last week whether he planned to wear the shirt against the Cowboys, Kittle said "potentially," then added, "I don't know if I can show that one again. I think I'd get suspended. So I don't want to do that."

Asked in a follow-up whether he would get a new shirt, Kittle answered, "Why would I do that? The old one is like 3-0. So we'll keep rocking it. It's a nice gray T-shirt."

Fans answered the post through Monday afternoon, and several of them cheered Kittle. One of them wrote, "Love it!"

Another fan summed up Kittle in one word: "King."

Meanwhile, this fan tied Sunday's result to what Kittle said before the game: "Kittle called that shirt 3-0 last week. Make it 4-0. 46-40 in Dallas … Keep rocking it."

Kittle had the shirt beneath his jersey when the teams met at Levi's Stadium on Oct. 8, 2023. He pulled it into view as he celebrated Jordan Mason's 26-yard scoring run during a 42-10 win in which Kittle caught three touchdown passes. He did not catch one on Sunday, so there was no end-zone celebration this time.

Notably, Kittle meant it as a tribute to Gary Plummer, the former 49ers linebacker whose shirt carried the same message when San Francisco faced Dallas in the 1994 NFC Championship Game.

The tribute came at a price. Days after that game, Jeremy Fowler reported that the NFL fined Kittle $13,659 for abusive language on the shirt.

With that in mind, another fan circled back to the suspension remark Kittle made last week, a step beyond the fine he drew in 2023, "He was afraid of getting suspended."

The Cowboys, meanwhile, do not appear again on San Francisco's regular-season schedule this season. Next up, the Niners will take on the Minnesota Vikings in Mexico City in Week 11.
