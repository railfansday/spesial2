16 fascinating images from the Close-up Photographer of the Year awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Published Nov 14, 2026 8:06 AM EST

A split-second, a zoomed in frame, an element you’ve never noticed: close-up photography puts the tiniest details into focus.

The Close-up Photographer of the Year 8 (CUPOTY 8) awards honor the greatest achievements in close-up, macro, and micro photography. A record-setting 15,388 images were entered into this year’s competition before being whittled down to a shortlist of 59 images across 11 categories.

“Close-up photography has a wonderful way of revealing details and perspectives that often go unnoticed,” competition co-founder Tracy Calder said in a statement. “This year’s shortlist is a fabulous showcase of imagination, technical expertise and natural curiosity.”

The overall winner of CUPOTY 8 will be announced in January 2027.
