Fire Emblem Heroes tier list and reroll guide

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

With so many heroes, you might think that a Fire Emblem Heroes tier list should be easy to determine. Surely a certain few characters will appear at the top? Well, given that all the heroes (okay, maybe not all, but most) are good for a specific task, then you'll understand why there are so many different tiers (to give you an example: SS, S, S-).

When travelling between the worlds to free the heroes held captive, a question might pop into your mind: Who are the best characters in Fire Emblem Heroes? That is indeed a very good question, and I started wondering about it ever since I unlocked my first couple of heroes. While this game is extremely fun, you might easily get lost in the numerous characters presented to you. What are the best characters in FEH? There are so many you'll unlock along the way that a newbie might easily get overwhelmed by this alone. But fear not - below, we've prepared a FEH tier list to help you choose some of the best heroes to aid you in battle. And to be completely honest, it wasn't easy at all. There are literally hundreds of heroes, and if you're trying to remember all of them, it might take quite a while.

Also, before we dive in, you need to know that I've split the heroes according to their colour (Red, Blue, Green, and Grey) and divided them each into a separate list. So if you're looking for a colour-specific tier list for Fire Emblem Heroes, that's exactly what we've got.

Blue Heroes in FEH are mainly known to use the Lance as their main weapon. However, other heroes do wield the Blue Breath, Blue Tome or Blue Bow, but more often than not, you will find them with a Lance, since these are the most common weapons for the Colourless Heroes.

The most obvious characteristic of the Red Heroes is their use of bladed weapons, such as daggers and swords. As a rule of thumb, they have a slightly smaller range than other colours, but that depends on each individual character. Swords are typically melee-ranged, but it doesn't apply to every single character on the list.

Most of the Green Heroes are Axe wielders, which should not be confused with the Lance or Sword. The Green heroes are great combatants and can make a neat addition to any team if deployed in the right position.

Colorless Heroes or Grey Heroes are mostly represented by Dagger users and Bow users, which means you should always pay attention to how you deploy them. As you may be aware, Archers are unable to attack the enemies in the spaces next to them - it's always good to pay close attention to where you deploy them.
