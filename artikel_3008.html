Iowa State wrestling takes down Utah Valley in road dual

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Iowa State men's wrestling moved to 2-0 on the season, defeating Utah Valley in the program's first road dual of the season.

The Cyclones won seven of 10 bouts in a 31-11 victory on Nov. 6, including five wins by technical fall from 125-pounder Rin Sakamoto, 141-pounder Evan Frost, 149-pounder Jacob Frost, 165-pounder Connor Euton and Coby Merrill at heavyweight to close the dual.

In his Cyclone debut, Sakamoto cruised to a 19-1 technical fall win over Geronimo Rivera. Sakamoto had just three takedowns in the bout, but 10 near-fall points in a tremendous showing from the top position in his first collegiate victory. However, at 133 pounds, Garrett Grice lost in a 13-2 major decision to unranked Layne Kleimann to make it 5-4.

The teeth of the Cyclones' lineup ran from there. The Frost twins each picked up a win by technical fall, with Evan Frost defeating Smoke McClure 16-1 and Jacob Frost winning 19-3 over Jason Worthley. They both are now 2-0 with four-combined technical falls to start the year. Paniro Johnson closed the first half of the dual with a 10-7 win by decision over Ian Fritz at 157 pounds to make it 18-4. Johnson secured the win by virtue of scoring a takedown late in the third period.

Euton bounced back in a big way after losing by fall in the season-opener, with a 19-4 technical fall over Ryker Fullmar. The Cyclones would take losses at 174 and 184, with Tate Naaktgeboren falling to No. 25 Marcus Espinoza-Owens by an 8-2 decision, followed by a 14-2 major decision loss for Sawyer Bartelt to No. 19 Caleb Ulenhopp.

Nonetheless, the Cyclones would close with back-to-back wins to end on a high note. 197-pounder Brayden Thompson picked up his first win as a Cyclone in a 9-3 decision with a reversal and two takedowns against No. 23 Kael Bennie. Merrill moved to 2-0 as well with a dominant 22-6 technical fall win over Will McCleary, recording four takedowns in the third period.

The Cyclones will return to the mat on Nov. 14 with road duals vs. North Dakota State (noon) and the University of Pennsylvania (2 p.m.) in Bradenton, Florida for the Sunshine Wrestling Duals event.
