FutureFuel (FF) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Customer funding and a strong fourth quarter order book shape the outlook ahead.

FutureFuel Corp. (FF -38.46%) reported a total revenue increase to $104.7 million for the third quarter of 2026, up from $22.7 million in the prior year period. Management stated that the Biofuels segment reached a multiyear utilization high of 83%, while the Chemicals segment results were impacted by planned and pulled-forward maintenance turnarounds. The company is implementing a productivity initiative to improve efficiency at the Batesville facility and has identified opportunities to refine byproduct waste into higher-value products. Management also indicated that it is focusing on reshoring opportunities for European chemical producers seeking lower manufacturing costs in the United States.

Custom chemical revenue was $13.3 million, down 8% from $14.5 million a year ago, primarily due to delayed shipments as a large customer rebalanced inventory and production downtime for a second customer during an extended scheduled turnaround.

Performance Chemical revenue was $3.6 million, up 223% from $1.1 million a year ago, primarily due to higher volumes from a new customer that began production in the fourth quarter of 2025 and from higher glycerin sales. Gross profit was $4.4 million compared to a loss of $4.1 million in the third quarter of 2025. The improvement was driven by higher sales volumes in the energy market and lower indirect fixed cost absorption from operating infrastructure with strong biofuel volumes, partially offset by the customer disruptions Roeland described previously. Business momentum in the Chemicals segment continued to build despite temporary volume disruptions in the third quarter as evidenced by recent customer wins and a robust project pipeline.

Chemical segment capacity utilization was 32% in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 55% a year ago period. Following recent capacity expansions and debottlenecking activities, the company anticipates fourth quarter Chemical segment level capacity utilization above prior year levels. For the Biofuel segment, revenue was $87.8 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to $7.1 million in the same period last year. The increase was driven by greater regulatory clarity, record high renewable volume obligation levels and strong operational execution during the quarter. The Biofuel segment reported a gross loss of $9.8 million in the third quarter compared to a gross loss of $2.4 million in the prior year period.

As Roeland discussed, significantly higher sales volumes and stronger pricing realization were more than offset by elevated input costs and increased plant operating and fixed manufacturing expenses compared to shutdown holding costs in the prior year period. The third quarter gross loss included hedging losses of $2.3 million, which are expected to be offset when the underlying physical commodity positions are sold in future periods and $1.5 million of RIN inventory will benefit Q4 when sold. Market conditions in the biofuel segment have improved meaningfully on a year-to-date basis following improved regulatory clarity around domestic renewable fuel blending obligations, tax incentives and the treatment of domestically produced biofuels.

Biofuels capacity utilization increased to 83% in the third quarter versus 56% in the second quarter, and sales volumes are expected to remain high entering the fourth quarter and into 2027. While demand conditions are expected to remain robust over the near to medium term, input costs for soy oil and other raw materials remain elevated, which is expected to impact Biofuel segment gross profit per gallon sold over the near term. Turning to a discussion of cash flow, balance sheet and liquidity. As of September 30, 2026, the company had total cash and equivalents of $21.3 million, down from $34.3 million at June 30, 2026, and a $35 million revolving credit facility with no outstanding borrowings.

The decrease in total cash during third quarter of 2026 was related to the operating loss in the third quarter of 2026, increased working capital requirements and capital expenditures to support growth. Net cash used in operations was $10.3 million in the third quarter compared to a use of $1.1 million in the prior year period. Capital expenditures were $3.3 million in the third quarter, including $0.6 million and $2.7 million related to maintenance and discretionary programs, respectively. In the first 9 months of 2026, approximately 74% of the $2.7 million in discretionary capital expenditures were customer-funded investments related to capacity expansions and new customer programs.
