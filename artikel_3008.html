Week 10 Vermont high school football semifinal schedule, live score updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Playoffs are here for the 2026 Vermont high school football season. Week 10 offers six semifinal matchups across the three divisions starting on Friday, Nov. 6 and wrapping on Saturday, Nov. 7.

The semifinal winners advance to the championship tripleheader, which is set for Saturday, Nov. 14, at Burlington High School.
