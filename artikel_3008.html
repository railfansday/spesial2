SBC Medical (SBC) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Margins widened sharply as AI and biomarker plans take shape.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

SBC Medical Group Holdings Incorporated (SBC +26.45%) reported revenue growth driven by revisions in management service fee structures and the consolidation of the Waqoo segment. Income from operations increased as a result of operating leverage established during a previous structural reform. Management indicated that underlying clinical performance remained steady throughout the first nine months of the year, with both visit volume and average spend per visit rising concurrently. The company is currently expanding its international clinic network while advancing a technology strategy centered on artificial intelligence and longevity diagnostics.

On the profit side, net income attributable to SBC Medical increased 22% year-on-year, supported by strong growth in our core business. Now to our business strategy update. Our long-term vision is unchanged, which is to become the name that comes to mind when people think of longevity, living young and vigorous for as long as possible.

What is new this quarter is that we are putting a concrete roadmap behind that vision, with initiatives planned in stages through 2028. Longevity requires a complete cycle, which is diagnosis, personalized treatment, intervention, and verification of results. One of the hardest parts of building that cycle is establishing the medical infrastructure to actually deliver treatment, meaning licensed clinics staffed by doctors and nurses at scale. SBC already has that infrastructure. Our medical network spans 293 locations, covering both appearance-based and function-based care, including AGA, dentistry, orthopedics, ophthalmology, fertility treatment, and regenerative medicine. Starting in November 2026, we will begin developing our own blood biomarker testing program, with the aim of eventually expanding it across our clinic network.

Over time, we believe this initiative has the potential to generate recurring revenue while building a valuable foundation of longitudinal health data. In 2027, we aim to establish a longevity center, together with an online coaching and tracking platform. Looking further ahead to 2027 and 2028, we plan to develop AI-generated, doctor-verified treatment protocols and explore opportunities to expand our longevity services into the United States through OrangeTwist, as well as Southeast Asia. By combining the medical infrastructure we already have with new diagnostic capabilities and AI, we aim to build a differentiated and difficult-to-replicate position in the longevity market.

As part of step one, diagnostics, we have begun in-house development of a DNA methylation test to estimate biological age, drawing on the research and technology base our Waqoo Group has built in regenerative medicine. Unlike our underlying DNA sequence, DNA methylation can change with lifestyle and environment, making it a promising way to track changes in biological age over time and potentially assess the impact of interventions. Our development rests on three pillars, which are organ-by-organ mapping of biological age, research into non-invasive measurement methods that do not require a blood draw, and data specific to Japan, one of the most aged societies in the world.

The goal is straightforward but powerful: to make both biological age and the effect of treatment visible, and to turn that into data-driven, personalized care. Next, our AI strategy. We are building our own local large language model foundation that allows us to use patient data safely. Running our own local LLM does two things. First, it lets us develop proprietary features from SBC's unique clinical and customer data that a general-purpose AI cannot easily match. Second, it decouples our inference cost from cloud API pricing, improving the economics. On this foundation, we are developing six AI features spanning cost, risk, and revenue.

On cost, these include voice-driven automatic charting, designed to draft a medical record in about one second, returning time from doctors and nurses to patients, and AI shift scheduling, aimed at optimizing staffing across our medical network of 293 locations. On risk, we are developing AI monitoring of our clinical systems and data access. On revenue, the features include a clinic promotion planner, a treatment plan recommendation engine that draws on 26 years of case data to raise spend per visit, and personalized recommendations for each customer to lift lifetime value.

We have also entered a capital and business alliance with AtomicSoftware, the provider of medicalforce, one of Japan's largest operating platforms for self-pay clinics, adopted by more than 800 clinics with a 99.2% retention rate. The system integrates reservations, electronic charts, payments, customer management, and analytics in a way purpose-built for self-pay medicine, and it is currently used at one of our clinics, Hada no Aozora Clinic. Together, we plan to create value in three steps, which are co-developing AI features, implementing them in our own systems, and extending them to new brands and new formats.

Our goal is to build a more efficient, more standardized operating base that improves the profitability of existing clinics and shortens the ramp-up time for new openings, strengthening the earnings power of our scalable MSO platform as the business expands. Our brand strength is also earning outside recognition. In Newsweek's World's Best Hospitals & Clinics for Medical Tourism 2027, Shonan Beauty Clinic was selected in two plastic surgery categories, liposuction and breast augmentation, each among the top 75 facilities in the world. We believe this reflects the trust we have built in the medical community. In 2025, doctors from our group presented research at 28 medical conferences in Japan and abroad, with a total of 75 physician appearances.

We also offer multilingual support to meet growing inbound demand. We intend to leverage this international recognition to raise our brand awareness overseas and further accelerate our growth. Finally, our capital and IR. Our basic policy is unchanged, which is to pursue EPS growth and a normalization of our valuation in parallel, and so to enhance shareholder value over the medium to long-term. On liquidity and recognition, we are making steady progress. Average daily trading value in October was approximately $1 million, about 4.1x the level this past July. The number of our beneficial shareholders reached 7,300 as of October 2026, representing a 1.6x increase since July 2026.

Let me close with why we are confident in SBC's trajectory. We combine three things that are not often found together in one company, namely high growth, high and rising margins, and a net cash balance sheet that funds its own expansion.

Our priorities are clear, which is to drive sustained EPS growth and earn a valuation that properly reflects our medium-term to long-term potential.
