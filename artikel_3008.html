Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Management details margin targets and memory cost pressures ahead.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Sonos, Inc. (SONO -0.70%) returned to top-line growth in fiscal 2026, driven by a series of hardware launches and the introduction of a next-generation software platform. Management reported that annual revenue increased 7% to $1,546.9 million, while adjusted EBITDA rose 39% as the company realized structural improvements in profitability. The company introduced five new products during the year, including headphones and home cinema components, while transitioning to an annual fall release cadence for its system software. Despite significant headwinds from elevated memory prices, management indicated that component mitigation efforts and pricing adjustments remain on track to deliver approximately 500 basis points of annualized margin improvement by the end of fiscal 2027. The company is prioritizing its installer channel and direct-to-consumer platform to maximize higher-margin sales opportunities.
