Red Sox Make Four Roster Moves Ahead of Arbitration Deadline

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Red Sox Make Four Roster Moves Ahead of Arbitration Deadline originally appeared on NESN. Add NESN as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Boston Red Sox have agreed to terms with four players on one-year contracts for the 2027 season, bringing back a group that gives the club depth at several positions while the offseason roster picture continues to take shape.

The team announced Friday that infielder Andruw Monasterio, outfielders Jahmai Jones and Eli White, and infielder/outfielder Romy Gonzalez have agreed to deals. Boston did not disclose the financial terms. The announcement comes ahead of the arbitration deadline, as the Red Sox work through decisions involving players eligible for salary arbitration.

Jones had the most productive batting line among the four in 2026, hitting .308 with four home runs and a .993 OPS in 37 games. He made the most of a limited workload, finishing with 65 at-bats. White hit .276 with one home run in 27 games, while Monasterio appeared in 107 games and batted .233 with six home runs. Gonzalez played 19 games, hitting .185 with one homer. Those figures are listed on MLB's Red Sox statistics page.

Monasterio played primarily at shortstop for Boston in 2026, according to MLB's statistics, and his return gives the club another option in the infield. Gonzalez brings position flexibility, having been listed by the Red Sox as an infielder and outfielder in the contract announcement.

Jones and White give Boston additional outfield depth. Their 2026 roles were different: Jones appeared in 37 games, while White played 27. Both hit above .270, although their opportunities were limited.

The four agreements settle the 2027 contract status of these players, but the Red Sox still face a broader set of offseason choices. MLB's arbitration eligibility list includes several other Boston players, among them Tanner Houck, Johan Oviedo, Jarren Duran and Wilyer Abreu. The list is based on estimated service-time calculations, which are not official MLB figures. bigleagueservicetime.com
