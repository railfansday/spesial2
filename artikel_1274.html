Ronaldinho’s Highlights Emerges as He Makes Ravenna Debut at 46

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Over 10 years after retiring from professional football, Brazil icon Ronaldinho stepped back onto the pitch, and fans have given a strong reaction.

The 46-year-old came out of retirement to sign for Italian club Ravenna, who play in Serie C. Ronaldinho is one of the most entertaining players in football history and is one of the only men who could have so many people seated to watch an Italian third-flight game.

There was so much anticipation for the 2005 Ballon d'Or winner's return that Sky Sports streamed the match live on YouTube. It was a surreal experience seeing him back on the pitch in his mid-forties.

Days after going viral for his sensational first touch in training, Ronaldinho was named in Ravenna's starting line-up for Sunday's Serie C encounter with Forli on matchday 14 of Group B.

He played 45 minutes, being withdrawn at half-time. In that short time, Ronaldinho managed one key pass, while completing 100% of his long passes attempted.

The Barcelona icon was also credited with the 'pre-assist' as he played a part in the build-up to Ravenna opening the scoring before the break.

Fans were quick to react to his display on social media. While it was accepted that he wasn't in the same shape physically as a 46-year-old, many were quick to praise his technical ability.

One X user said: "No word of a lie when I say this. Ronaldinho looks at home. He’s the most creative player on the pitch lol."
