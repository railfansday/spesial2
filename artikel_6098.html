United Airlines Cancels All Flights To 7 Destinations: See Full List Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

United Airlines, a founding member of Star Alliance, has been continuously reviewing its route network. Just recently, Simple Flying reported that the airline had removed several routes from its third-busiest hub.

Looking at its broader network, however, it has also removed all flights to seven destinations from its summer 2027 schedule compared to this summer. Five of these are domestic destinations, including two airports in Michigan, while the remaining two are international.

According to scheduling data from aviation analytics company Cirium, United has removed all flights to five domestic destinations. These are Nantucket Memorial Airport (ACK) in Massachusetts, Yellowstone Airport (WYS) in Montana, Rhinelander–Oneida County Airport (RHI) in Wisconsin, Pellston Regional Airport (PLN), and Chippewa County International Airport (CIU) in Michigan.

Internationally, the airline has also pulled out of Tulum International Airport (TQO) in Mexico and Blaise Diagne International Airport (DSS) in Dakar, Senegal. All of these services, except Tulum, ended in recent months, as shown in the table below. The Star Alliance member’s last scheduled flight to Tulum is due to operate in January 2027.

None of these destinations currently has United flights scheduled for summer 2027. With that being said, the airline could still bring back some of these flights (such as its seasonal service to Rhinelander) for next summer. However, with the rest of its summer 2027 schedule already on sale, there is very little time for it to add these flights back.

United will operate its final flight to Tulum from Houston's George Bush Intercontinental Airport (IAH) in January 2027. This will mark its complete withdrawal from the Mexican Riviera Maya destination. The airline began serving Tulum in April 2024, just a few months after the airport opened for commercial flights in December 2023.

Following its opening, several airlines were quick to introduce flights, including United, which initially served this Mexican gateway from four hubs: Chicago O'Hare International Airport (ORD), Los Angeles International Airport (LAX), Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR), and Houston. However, the Star Alliance member only operated its Los Angeles service for one season and dropped Chicago last year.

It is now scheduled to end its Newark flights on January 2, followed by Houston two days later. This comes because demand for flights to Tulum has been lower than initially expected. According to US Department of Transportation (DOT) T-100 data, United's flights to the Mexican resort city recorded average load factors ranging from 60 to around 70%.

Other airlines have also reduced their services. JetBlue, for instance, which launched daily nonstop flights from New York's John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK) in 2024, has since discontinued the route. American Airlines and Delta Air Lines continue to serve Tulum, although the overall number of seats available on flights from the US has fallen considerably, as noted above in the table.

All these places have stopped seeing the carrier, and nearly all in September and October.

Furthermore, United's Dakar service also seems to have suffered from weak demand. The airline only began serving Senegal in May 2025, which marked its entry into the West African country. It initially offered three weekly year-round flights from Washington Dulles International Airport (IAD), using its Boeing 767-300ER aircraft.

However, the Chicago-based airline decided to discontinue the service less than a year after its launch. According to US DOT data, the route recorded an average monthly load factor of just 58% across the nine months. In fact, during two of those months, the airline filled fewer than 40% of its seats.

With United's departure, Delta will be the only airline offering nonstop flights between the US and Dakar. The SkyTeam founding member operates five weekly flights from New York JFK using its 767-300ER, although this drops to three weekly during the summer season.
