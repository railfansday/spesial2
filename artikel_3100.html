OppFi: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Management's $150 million platform investment may hinge on whether new credit products can offset tighter lending filters and prove profitable at scale.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 28, 2026, OppFi (OPFI +1.52%) announced it invested over $150 million this year to build a tech-enabled banking platform with a goal of $500 million in long-term adjusted net income. For long-term investors, shifting borrower preferences and tight credit filters require proof that new software and credit products can drive profitable originations.

Management shares three takeaways on how the strategy is unfolding.

Management launched a line of credit product (LOC) to complement traditional installment loans and reach borrowers who prefer flexible options. Built on LOLA, the company's new cloud software architecture that speeds up product development, the structure opens marketing funnels in new states.

Marketing partner screening criteria tightened during the quarter, so origination volume fell 7% year over year. Management designed Model 7, its latest proprietary credit scoring algorithm, to match pre-screening filters with partner banks and boost application conversion.

Management views the current stock price as an unwarranted discount relative to industry peers and long-term earnings potential. It activated a $40 million share repurchase program to buy back equity directly from the open market.

Macroeconomic pressures strain subprime borrowers, yet internal recovery systems cushion credit performance.

Proprietary debt collections, cloud platform migration, and the pending acquisition of BNCC Corp, a bank holding company, anchor long-term efficiency as leadership maintains pricing discipline.
