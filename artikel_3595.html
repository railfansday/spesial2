One of my favorite parts of being a shopping writer is testing products, so Black Friday and the holiday season feel like my personal Super Bowl when they roll around each year. Though splurge products have their place (I won’t give up my Brooklinen and Quince bed sheets or my Ninja Creami for anything), I’m also obsessed with finding items under $50 that give you the most bang for your buck, especially during these shopping-centric seasons!

With so many deals across Amazon, I combed through and pulled out my favorites that I think are worth buying — even if you only have $50 to spend this Black Friday. Whether you’re looking for a wardrobe upgrade, something new for your home, a switch-up to your beauty routine or the perfect gift for someone special, I’ve got you covered.

Everyone needs a go-to sweater for the chilly months, and if you don’t already have an oversized cable knit in your wardrobe, this one is worth grabbing on sale. It’s currently just $30, and it’s made with 60% cotton that feels so breathable and soft. I love the drapey fit — but it still feels polished, with ribbed trim and that intricate, classic texture that feels straight out of The Holiday. I might grab it in multiple colors!

I’ve been lucky enough to try a lot of nail polish, but none impressed me for at-home manicures as much as Dazzle Dry. The brand’s mini nail polish kit is on sale for $30 and includes one color, along with their full prep, base coat and top coat system (plus a bottle of their polish reviver to extend the life of your polishes). A few coats, and you’ll be set with gorgeous nails for, in my experience, at least five days chip-free. Quite a few color options are on sale now, but I’m grabbing Beloved, a bright hot pink shimmer with a subtle flash of blue. So pretty!

I use my Owala water bottle every single day — and I don’t go anywhere without it. I’ve tried dozens of water bottles, but this is the only one I’ve found that never leaks and truly keeps drinks cold all day, even in the wild South Florida heat where I live. Plus, they come in the cutest color combos. It would make a fun holiday gift or stocking stuffer, too!

Some candles are worth savoring, especially if they’re usually around that higher $50 price point. Snif’s candles are some of my favorites, and for Christmas, I’m grabbing their Half Baked Gingerbread Smash scent while it’s on sale for under $40. I might even grab one or two as gifts for those in my life. They’re made with clean, cruelty-free ingredients and burn beautifully…and this one in particular has notes of baked gingerbread, chai, cinnamon swirl, nutmeg, spiked eggnog, frothy vanilla and sugared leather, so it’s perfect for the colder months.

I love gifting fun seasonings and spices — they’re items someone might not treat themselves to regularly, but for foodies or anyone in your life always trying a new recipe, they are sure to impress. I’m currently eyeing this Dan-O’s set, which includes 6 full-size spices in the most fun flavor combinations, all for under $40. The set features Butter Dust, Butter Steak, Blackened Bloody Mary, Sweet Caribbean, Spicy BBQ and Sweet and Tangy BBQ. You can gift the whole set or split it into individual stocking stuffers to get the most mileage out of your budget!
