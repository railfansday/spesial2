Marvel’s Best Avengers: Secret Wars Story Just Became A Lot More Likely

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

New comments from Marvel Studios head Kevin Feige and the Russo Brothers have made one of the biggest Avengers: Secret Wars story hopes that much closer to becoming a reality. While much of the focus is naturally on Avengers: Doomsday releasing next month and the imminent debut of Robert Downey Jr. as Doctor Doom, we're already getting glimpses and teases as to what lies ahead for the final film in the Multiverse Saga.

After multiple movies and shows featuring alternate realities, branching timelines, and variants, Avengers: Secret Wars will be the culmination of the entire Multiverse Saga. However, it seems Marvel Studios intends to give audiences something they've never seen before from the MCU, rather than simply offering more of the same.

Here's what these new comments from Kevin Feige and the Russos likely mean for Avengers: Secret Wars, and why it's potentially a great sign for the end of the Multiverse Saga as a whole.

No matter what, Avengers: Secret Wars is set to be a major turning point for the MCU, as it will mark the end of the Multiverse Saga and the dawn of a brand-new era going forward. However, new comments from Kevin Feige suggest that Secret Wars will likely end up looking very different from what mainstream audiences likely expect. According to the President of Marvel Studios and Chief Creative Officer in a recent interview with Empire Magazine, Secret Wars will be "a whole other genre of story" compared to Doomsday, with Joe Russo agreeing wholeheartedly:

"[Secret Wars] is a really unique world. It’s a really unique story. The only reason to get out of bed every day is to take risks and surprise people and try things that have never been tried before. I think Secret Wars is one of those movies where I hope people will walk out of it and go, ‘I’ve never seen anything like that in my life!’”

Overall, that's a pretty ambitious promise, and Feige's comments about shifting genres are particularly notable considering what we know about the Avengers: Secret Wars title and its namesake from the original comics. With the multiverse already collapsing in Avengers: Doomsday, it stands to reason that Secret Wars will be focused on what comes next in the aftermath, just like in 2015's Secret Wars storyline from writer Jonathan Hickman (a reported consultant on the next two Avengers films) and artist Esad Ribić.

With that in mind, it certainly does seem like a major opportunity exists to rewrite the entire MCU as audiences know it, just like on the page, which in turn could result in a major genre shift to none other than medieval fantasy.

If Marvel Studios really wants to deliver a genre shift, Hickman and Ribić's Secret Wars is the peak blueprint for a major medieval fantasy epic starring all kinds of beloved Marvel heroes and villains.

Following the destruction of the multiverse on the page, it was Doctor Doom who used the power of the Beyonders to create a patchwork realm of various reality remnants known as Battleworld, placing himself as a ruling God Emperor over the land's various fiefdoms and unique territories, with very few subjects remembering existence as it was. Sitting upon a throne made from the Asgardian World Tree itself, the chance to place the biggest MCU heroes in a medieval realm ruled by an omnipotent Doctor Doom would be equally fascinating and terrifying.

Like all MCU adaptations of core comics, what's seen on the page can serve as the basic template for a massive genre shift, with Marvel Studios and the Russos indeed providing a brand-new storyline, ensuring that Marvel diehards and general movie-goers alike can walk out of the theater feeling like "they've seen anything like it before".

While Hickman's Secret Wars in relation to the upcoming MCU movies has been mentioned by Marvel Studios before, neither Feige nor the Russos have confirmed just how much of the epic comics crossover event will serve as the film's foundation, nor 1984's Secret Wars event for that matter. Regardless, it does seem like 2015's more medieval Battleworld setting would serve as the best means to bring about this massive genre shift Kevin Feige has just promised.

Avengers: Secret Wars releases in theaters December 17th, 2027 from Marvel Studios.
