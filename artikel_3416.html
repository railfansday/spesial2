Star Trek: Duty And Honor Officially Brings Back Worf

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Star Trek fans will almost certainly be familiar with the legendary character Worf. The Klingon, first introduced in Star Trek: The Next Generation, has appeared in more episodes throughout the entire show's history than any other character, making him both a fan-favorite staple and a constant fixture. It is for this very reason that his reappearance on multiple shows and subsequent new Star Trek content no longer feels terribly surprising, although it is always appreciated.

Fortunately, for those who cannot get enough of Worf and want a brand-new Star Trek release to tide them over, the iconic character is returning once more in a new release that is targeted at dedicated fans. Titled Duty and Honor, this new Star Trek project offers a different type of experience than the one most fans will be used to, all while putting Worf front and center. Suffice it to say, it could end up being the definitive Worf Star Trek release should its creators play their cards right.

Duty and Honor is the upcoming expansion for the critically acclaimed deck-building game, Star Trek: Captain's Chair. The base game, for those who've missed out thus far, is an in-depth, strategic experience in which players take on the role of one of six captains (either Picard, Sisko, Burnham, Shran, Koloth, and Sela) and must attempt to complete various objectives and obtain the highest score by playing cards from each character's corresponding starter deck and any they obtain throughout.

Widely praised as being a compelling and highly detailed Star Trek experience with strong theming and phenomenal representation of the characters included, Captain's Chair (developed by WizKids) has gone on to get multiple expansions. The latest, the aforementioned Duty and Honor, adds four new captains to the experience, as well as their corresponding decks, thus greatly increasing the level of replayability and variety within the game.

Duty and Honor is adding Captain Worf as well as the fan-favorite Deep Space Nine Cardassian, Gul Dukat. Two additional captains will also be added, although WizKids has not yet confirmed who they will be. Of course, included in their decks will be iconic ships, characters, and locations associated with them, so dedicated fans are in for a treat. While it isn't quite a new series, it should hopefully make up for the canceled Worf series that fans never got the chance to see.

The base Star Trek: Captain's Chair game that is required to play the Duty and Honor expansion costs $59.99. It is currently available for back-order on the official WizKids website and is expected to be shipped in Q4 2026.

The Duty and Honor expansion for Star Trek: Captain's Chair costs $39.99. It is currently available for pre-order via the official WizKids website, as well as other assorted retailers. It is estimated to have a release date of Q3 2027, according to the official store listing, which means it'll release any time between July 1 and September 30. Considering how well-received the other Star Trek: Captain's Chair expansions have been, fans can likely rest assured that Duty and Honor will do Worf proud.
