Another limited practice for Carnell Tate

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Tennessee Titans WR Carnell Tate (shoulder) was limited during practice Thursday, Nov. 19.

Why It Matters: Tate has a chance to play in Week 11 after being limited the first two practices of the week. He is a big part of the passing game when healthy and can be a No. 3 or No. 4 receiver in fantasy leagues if active.
