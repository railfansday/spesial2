New Rumor Suggests NY Giants Can Still Make Jonathan Taylor Trade Today Without a 1st-Round Pick

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

A new report suggests that the New York Giants may still be able to complete a Jonathan Taylor trade before 4:00 PM ET today with an offer very similar to the one they took off the table recently.

The Giants are in a very different position than they were a year ago. On this day last year, they fired head coach Brian Daboll after a 2-8 start and were already pivoting to which players they would target in the upcoming NFL Draft. However, it is a very different story in 2026.

Heading into their Week 10 Thursday night matchup against the Washington Commanders, they own a 5-2-1 record and sit atop the NFC East. They seem to be getting better each week and have reason to be aggressive to improve the roster before today's NFL trade deadline.

The player they have most been linked to in recent weeks is the Indianapolis Colts star. Yet, while rumors suggested they were a favorite to acquire the three-time Pro Bowler, they recently took back an offer of a second- and fifth-round pick after Indy continued to leave it on the table and spoke with other interested teams.

Indianapolis Colts running back Jonathan Taylor against the Tennessee Titans at Lucas Oil Stadium in Indianapolis in Week 6.

That, along with other reports, made it seem like the Colts wanted a first-round pick, or would just hold on to Taylor. Well, maybe not. According to Jordan Schultz, he believes the Giants will put that same offer back on the table before Tuesday's deadline, that the Colts "would strongly consider taking it," and that it is the "offer to watch."

Furthermore, Schultz is under the impression that Taylor is open to being traded and would "welcome a fresh start." However, it must also be noted that the NFL insider claims the Colts don't want to "force" a trade and are completely fine with waiting until the offseason to revisit trade discussions.
