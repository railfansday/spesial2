Foodtastic is making an unsolicited bid of about $720 million for MTY Food Group

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The offer of C$43 a share is a 32% premium to MTY's Oct. 30 closing price, after two earlier approaches were rebuffed

Foodtastic has offered to acquire Canadian restaurant franchisor MTY Food Group for about C$1 billion ($720 million), or C$43 a share, according to Bloomberg. MTY rebuffed two earlier approaches from Foodtastic.

The proposal tops MTY’s Oct. 30 closing stock price by 32% and puts a total valuation of roughly C$1.5 billion on the company once debt is counted, according to Bloomberg. MTY stock gained roughly 1% on Monday to finish at C$32.75, which gives the company a market capitalization near C$748 million.

MTY announced a leadership change on Tuesday, appointing director Victor E. Mandel as chief executive, effective at once. Mandel replaces Eric Lefebvre, who is leaving the company and its board. MTY franchises and operates restaurants under more than 80 banners, with 6,966 locations.

Foodtastic CEO Peter Mammas said in an emailed statement that the company is disappointed with MTY's response, according to Bloomberg. "We are therefore disappointed that MTY has rejected the proposed transaction without engaging in substantive dialogue," Mammas said. He added that Foodtastic is confident MTY's shareholders will see the proposal as "a superior path forward to MTY's standalone strategy."

Mammas also criticized MTY's performance. "MTY is a great company with excellent opportunities, but it has unfortunately not lived up to its full potential in recent years," he said, according to Bloomberg.

The approach follows the conclusion of a strategic review at MTY, which had weighed whether to sell either the whole business or a portion of it, according to Bloomberg. MTY's board, together with a committee made up of independent directors, determined that sticking with its current business plan would generate greater returns for shareholders. On Oct. 9, MTY said it planned to restart share repurchases, weigh a substantial issuer bid, lift its quarterly payout by 35%, to 50 Canadian cents per share, and pare back the portion of its business tied to company-operated restaurants.

Over the 13 weeks ended Aug. 30, MTY's comparable-store sales slid 1.9%, with the U.S. market accounting for a steeper 2.7% drop. Net income dropped to C$24.8 million, compared with C$27.9 million in the same period last year. The company closed a net 74 restaurants over the course of the quarter.

Foodtastic would pay for the transaction together with its financial backers, according to Bloomberg, which cited unnamed sources. Financing is expected to be lined up once a definitive deal is signed, as long as MTY opens its books for closer examination, according to Bloomberg. Foodtastic wants to hold talks with MTY’s board, though it has left open alternative routes if directors say no, Bloomberg said.

Foodtastic, based in Montreal, runs over 1,200 restaurants under roughly 30 brands, among them Milestones Grill + Bar, Second Cup and Freshii, according to Bloomberg. Funds managed by Oaktree Capital Management have backed the company since 2018.
