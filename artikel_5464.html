QUANTUM X AND EVEREST CLOUD PARTNER TO BRING QUANTUM AND AI COMPUTE TO SIX SOUTH ASIAN MARKETS

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Channel partnership MOU gives EntangleX a route to banks, telecom operators, universities and data centers in Nepal, India, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Sri Lanka and the Maldives, backed by a growing operating revenue base

LOCUST VALLEY, N.Y., Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Quantum X, Inc. (OTC: QUTX) ("Quantum X" or the "Company") today announced a channel partnership with Everest Cloud, the Kathmandu-based cloud services brand of Digital Network Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("Everest Cloud"), under a Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") signed on November 6, 2026.

The partnership gives Quantum X's EntangleX quantum computing solution and their first international route to market, through a cloud provider serving enterprises, banks, telecommunications operators, government bodies, universities and data centers in Nepal, India, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Sri Lanka and the Maldives.

Quantum capacity is coming to South Asia. IBM, Tata Consultancy Services and the Government of Andhra Pradesh have announced plans to deploy an IBM Quantum System Two in Amaravati, and India's National Quantum Mission is funding research and startups nationwide. Amazon Web Services and IBM already offer quantum computing as a cloud service.

What remains scarce is access. Most enterprises in the region adopt advanced computing through the cloud providers they already trust. The partnership places EntangleX inside Everest Cloud's offering, alongside the hosting, storage and data-residency services its customers already buy, so that capacity can reach the banks, telecom operators and universities that need it.

"The next phase of quantum and AI will be decided by access, not just by hardware," said Emil Assentato, Chief Executive Officer of Quantum X. "Everest Cloud already serves the banks, telecom operators, universities and data centers we built EntangleX for. This partnership puts our products in front of them through a provider they already rely on."

"Nepal and South Asia are home to fast-growing enterprises, banks and telecom operators that are ready for the next generation of computing," said Ujjwal Roy, Head of Strategy and Chief Scientist of Quantum X. "Working with Everest Cloud gives EntangleX a trusted local channel into these markets, while giving U.S. organizations access to reliable cloud infrastructure from the region. This partnership is a practical step toward building quantum and AI capability across South Asia, and toward stronger technology ties between the United States and Nepal."

"Partnering with Quantum X opens the U.S. market to Everest Cloud and gives our clients in Nepal and South Asia access to quantum computing and advanced AI infrastructure through a trusted local partner," said Arun Khatri, Chief Executive Officer of Digital Network Solutions Pvt. Ltd. "We look forward to bringing EntangleX and Quantum X's broader portfolio to the enterprises, banks, telecom operators and institutions we serve, while introducing U.S. organizations to secure, cost-effective, enterprise-grade cloud services from Nepal. We believe this MOU lays a strong foundation for our vision of becoming a 'Local to Global' company from the Himalayas."

Reach. Everest Cloud will introduce Quantum X products across six South Asian markets. Quantum X will introduce Everest Cloud's services to customers in the United States.

Products. EntangleX, and Quantum X's AI and data infrastructure solutions, with further products to be added over time.

Commercial model. A reciprocal, non-exclusive arrangement in which each party earns commissions on revenue from customers it refers, with registered leads protected for twelve months and the option for either party to resell the other's products.

Go-to-market. Joint sales calls, co-branded proposals, bundled offerings, joint marketing and technical integration between the two platforms, overseen by dedicated partnership managers and quarterly senior leadership reviews.

Status. The MOU has a two-year initial term and is non-binding. The parties intend to sign a definitive Channel Partner Agreement within 90 days. Quantum X has not attributed a revenue estimate to the partnership and will report on referred customers as they are secured.

Quantum X works on the three layers around the hardware that decide whether customers can use it: software that makes it usable, secure sharing that makes it affordable, and distribution that makes it reachable. EntangleX and SliceX cover the first two. Partnerships such as Everest Cloud cover the third.

EntangleX is Quantum X's quantum computing solution: a hosted circuit simulation and labs platform that Quantum X built and operates, live at entanglex.co as a minimum viable product (MVP). Users can already build circuits gate by gate in a visual editor in the browser, send larger simulations to a hosted job queue, and use Agentic Labs to turn a plain-language description of an experiment into a draft circuit. The MVP gives Quantum X a working product to put in front of customers and partners now, and a foundation to extend with their feedback.

SliceX is Quantum X's platform for GPU slicing and security within the slices, designed to let multiple customers and workloads share GPU capacity more efficiently, a direct answer to the cost and scarcity of AI compute. Quantum X identified the opportunity through a preliminary engineering research study. Its development has proven its ability to slice. However, completion of the security issues between the slices are still subject to funding. Data centers are a priority customer group for Everest Cloud, and Quantum X has held preliminary, non-binding discussions with data-center operators in the United States.

Quantum X, Inc. (OTC: QUTX), formerly Two Hands Corporation, is a Locust Valley, New York based technology company building quantum computing and AI infrastructure products, including EntangleX supported by operating businesses in financial technology and telecommunications. For more information, visit https://thequantumx.com.

Everest Cloud is an enterprise cloud services brand owned and operated by Digital Network Solutions Pvt. Ltd., based in Kathmandu, Nepal. Everest Cloud offers cloud hosting, virtual compute, object and block storage, in-country data residency, disaster recovery, AI and managed cloud services to customers in Nepal and the wider South Asia region. For more information, visit everestcloud.com.np.
