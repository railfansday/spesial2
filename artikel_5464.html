Pathlock Now Offered Through GuidePoint Security

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

This new collaboration offers GuidePoint Security customers access to the Pathlock Nexus platform, which governs what identities and AI agents can do inside business-critical applications and proves compliance.

DENVER, Nov. 11, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Pathlock, an innovator in Identity Governance for the Agentic Era, is excited to announce a new reseller partnership with GuidePoint Security, the leading cybersecurity solution provider that helps organizations make better decisions that minimize risk.

28% of organizations already allow AI agents to approve business transactions, and one in four already had an incident that they know about. Through this partnership, GuidePoint Security customers can now acquire the Pathlock Nexus platform and related consulting services, leveraging their current GuidePoint agreements.

"GuidePoint's customers already trust them to run identity programs," said Ben Holcombe, Senior Vice President, Global Sales, Pathlock. "What makes Pathlock unique is that we deliver one governance control plane that manages the activities of what employees, contractors, bots, and AI agents are executing inside business-critical applications, while providing the evidence needed to show compliance."

"Our customers need to govern both human and non-human identities without slowing down the business. Together with Pathlock, we're helping them reduce risk in their ERP and financial systems, prevent fraud, and prove compliance with confidence," said Kevin Converse, Vice President, Identity and Access Management, GuidePoint Security.

Source: Pathlock 2026 AI Governance Gap Report, a May 2026 survey of 286 IT, compliance and security leaders.

Pathlock is an innovator in Identity Governance for the Agentic Era. Global 2000 organizations trust Pathlock to protect their most complex business-critical applications. The Pathlock platform brings identity, governance and compliance together in one place. It helps enterprises govern every human and non-human identity, prevent fraud and prove compliance at scale.
