Kane reveals new Bayern contract is ‘extremely close’

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Harry Kane revealed he is "extremely close" to signing a new deal with Bayern Munich.

The 33-year-old's current contract is set to expire at the end of this season, but he is now preparing to extend his stay at the Allianz Arena.

Kane joined Bayern ahead of the 2023-24 season and has made 160 appearances for the club, scoring 156 goals and providing 39 assists.

61 of those goals came last season as Bayern won the Bundesliga and DFB-Pokal, while reaching the semi-finals of the Champions League.

It was the most by a player in a season in Europe's top-five leagues since Cristiano Ronaldo scored 61 for Real Madrid in 2014-15, and Kane's exploits saw him win the Ballon d'Or for the first time in his career.

The England captain has already directly contributed to 18 goals this season (10 goals, eight assists), and he expects his new deal to be announced soon.

"I know I keep saying we are close, but we are extremely close now. Details are just details in every contract," said Kane.

"A lot of the bigger stuff has been ironed out and we are happy with, but in every contract there is loads of little things that go into it, small details that in the end the lawyers have to thrash out and figure out.

"So, all in all, I would say it is more days than weeks. From what I have heard, the conversations have gone well again and hopefully we will be in a place to do something within the next few days."

Kane has scored in each of his last 10 appearances in the competition, and could equal Ronaldo's record of scoring in 11 consecutive Champions League games.

Kane and Bayern are in the Champions League action on Tuesday as they face Atletico Madrid.

The former Tottenham captain was asked if he wanted to play in LaLiga, to which he responded: "The opportunity never arose. I was at Tottenham for many years and I was happy there.

"When I had the opportunity with Bayern, I wanted to take it.

"If the opportunity comes, it would be fantastic to play in LaLiga and compete with these players, but everyone's career takes them in different directions."
