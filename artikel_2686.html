Section 2 soccer finals: Previewing the Class AA and A girls' title matches

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Today's Section 2 Class AA and A girls' soccer championships at Bethlehem High School feature four teams arriving by very different paths.

Top-seeded Schalmont opens the doubleheader against No. 2 Averill Park, putting its title streak on the line against a battle-tested Suburban Council team that has gone unbeaten against Class A opponents this season.

In the nightcap, No. 5 Ballston Spa looks to follow its comeback win over top-seeded Columbia with another upset against No. 2 Bethlehem. The Eagles won their regular-season meeting 1-0 and are seeking their first sectional title since 2019.

When and where: 7:30 p.m. at Shaker High School

What to know about Ballston Spa: The Scotties (9-5-4) are unbeaten in their past eight games and are coming off a 1-0 quarterfinal win over Burnt Hills-Ballston Lake and a thrilling 3-2 comeback victory over top-seeded Columbia in the semifinals. Ballston Spa registered impressive shutout wins over top Class AAA opponents Shenendehowa (2-0) and Guilderland (3-0) during the regular season and has recorded nine shutouts this season. Led by Leah Szmarz, Haley Wiltsie and Abby Werner, the Scotties are seeking the program's first-ever sectional title.

What to know about Bethlehem: After losing to Mohonasen 4-2 in the regular-season finale, the Eagles (10-4-3) earned a 2-0 semifinal win, led by two goals from Maira Braddock. Bethlehem also secured a 5-0 quarterfinal win over Queensbury, led by a hat trick from June McCarthy and two goals from Braddock, and has 10 shutouts this season. The Eagles beat Ballston Spa 1-0 in late September, with Kayla Janse van Rensburg scoring the lone goal. Bethlehem is seeking its first sectional title since 2019.

When and where: 5 p.m. at Shaker High School

What to know about Schalmont: The defending champion Sabres (14-3) have won six consecutive sectional titles and are playing in their seventh straight title game. Schalmont secured dominant wins over Emma Willard (8-0) and Scotia-Glenville (5-1) to earn a spot in today's final, with Selena Cassano recording seven goals and four assists across the two games. The Sabres have won 10 of their past 11 games, outscoring opponents 49-6 over that span, and have a Section 2 Class A-best plus-53 goal differential.

What to know about Averill Park: The Golden Knights (6-9-3) went winless against Class AAA and AA opponents in the Suburban Council this season (0-7-3), but are 3-0 against Class A teams. Averill Park secured playoff wins against Lansingburgh, 3-0 in the quarterfinals, and Ravena-Coeymans-Selkirk, 4-1 in the semifinals, with Tegan DeLair (two goals), Hailey Pietrafesa and Hannah Premo scoring in the win over Ravena. The Golden Knights seek their first sectional title since 2018.
