Three Rivers Report: A troubling trend could be Steelers downfall

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Pittsburgh Steelers are licking their wounds after a disappointing Sunday Night Football loss to the Cincinnati Bengals. A 20-point loss is troubling, but does it signal a bigger offensive issue for Pittsburgh? The fourth quarter saw the Steelers score two touchdowns, but the game was well out of reach. A 3.8 yards per play average is brutal. As the lead grew, Pittsburgh had to abandon the run, which has been successful this season with a 0.04 EPA/rush and 45.4% success rate.

On the season, the Steelers offense has a total EPA of -37.48. With a -0.07 EPA/play, -0.13 EPA/pass, and a 45.5% success rate through the air are incredibly low for an offense with Aaron Rodgers and an offensive system run by Mike McCarthy. This statistical trend isn't sustainable and looking at the teams in the same range says it all. The Steelers are 18th in total EPA and the Raiders and Jets are ahead of them.
