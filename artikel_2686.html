Josh Allen breaks Peyton Manning's NFL touchdown record against Jets

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Josh Allen needed less than nine seasons to break Peyton Manning's 10-year record.

The Buffalo Bills quarterback scored on a 1-yard run against the New York Jets on Sunday, giving him the most total touchdowns by a player through his first 10 seasons in NFL history.

Allen has accounted for 323 regular-season touchdowns — 232 passing, 89 rushing and two receiving.

He tied Manning's record with a 25-yard touchdown pass to rookie tight end Keleki Latu against the Minnesota Vikings on Monday Night Football.

Manning played all 160 regular-season games during his first 10 seasons. Allen broke the record in his 137th career game.

And he still has the rest of this season and all of 2027 to add to it.

Allen's touchdown against the Jets was also his 10th rushing touchdown of the season. The 1-yard run capped a 12-play, 63-yard drive and gave Buffalo a 7-0 lead with 2:43 remaining in the first quarter.

Allen has rushed for at least 10 touchdowns in four straight seasons.
