Hammond mayor says Bears will "make a lot more money" in Indiana

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Money talks. And the mayor of Hammond hopes the Bears are listening.

At a time when the Bears are trying to get Illinois to pass a law that will lay the foundation for a stadium in the state where the Bears have always played, Indiana interests aren’t giving up.

“The Bears are going to make more money if they come to Indiana, a lot more,” Hammond Mayor Tom McDermott Jr. recently said, via the Chicago Daily Herald. “They’ll be able to have more money every year because we have a better business environment, and if they stay in Illinois, it’s not because they got a better deal. It’s because they’re being nostalgic.”

That’s a good way to put it, if the numbers support it. To the extent that the Bears and/or the league has concerns about going to Indiana, money can alleviate such issues.

For now, it seems that the Bears are still using Indiana to get Illinois to do something. At some point, the Bears need to make a decision — one that at least 23 other owners will support.

If they wanted to go to Indiana, ground would have already been broken. At some point, however, they may have no choice but to get out the shovel. Even if getting out the shovel is the thing that will finally get Illinois to view Indiana as something more than a sham.
