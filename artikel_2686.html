How MNF win over Ravens impacts Chargers’ No. 1 overall pick chances

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

How MNF win over Ravens impacts Chargers' No. 1 overall pick chances appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Los Angeles Chargers finally have something to celebrate after ending their losing streak with a 23-20 win over the Baltimore Ravens on Monday Night Football. The win provided a much-needed boost to their confidence, but it also had an unintended consequence for their draft position.

According to ESPN Analytics, the Chargers' probability of landing the first overall selection dropped from 13% to 7% following their win over the Ravens. This is a six-percentage-point decline, cutting the team's previous odds by nearly half.

There are several games still remaining in the regular season, so the draft order is far from determined. Los Angeles' performances in the coming weeks, along with the outcomes of other struggling teams, will continue to shape the competition for the top selection.

However, the win was a big moment for Jim Harbaugh's squad, marking their first victory in 331 days and ending an 11-game losing streak dating back to last season. Their previous win came on December 21, 2025.

With the Ravens' win, the Chargers became only the second team to win an MNF game after entering with a 0-8 record or worse. The 2013 Tampa Bay Buccaneers were the only other team to achieve the milestone, defeating the Miami Dolphins after starting the season 0-8.

Monday's game also carried a historical distinction, as the Chargers and Ravens entered Monday night with a combined 15 consecutive losses. It set a record for the most combined straight losses by opposing teams heading into a MNF game.

As for the Ravens, the three-point loss to a Chargers team that had yet to win a game this season ranks among the most embarrassing blows in team history. It was also just the second time Baltimore had lost to an opponent carrying a 0-8 record or worse.
