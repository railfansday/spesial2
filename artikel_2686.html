Will Anderson named the AFC defensive player of the week

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Texans hurt their playoff chances with a slow start to the regular season, but they are still in the hunt and having defensive end Will Anderson will help their chances.

Anderson helped them to their fourth win in the last six games last Thursday by recording five tackles, a sack, two tackles for loss and four quarterback hits against the Colts. Anderson’s sack of Daniel Jones came in the end zone for the first sack of the Texans pass rusher’s career.

The NFL named Anderson the AFC defensive player of the week on Wednesday. It’s the second time he’s won the award in his career.

Anderson leads the league with 11.5 sacks and the Texans’ postseason hopes will keep improving as long as he continues to produce off the edge on defense.
