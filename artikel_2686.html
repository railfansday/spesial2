PSG predicted XI v Roma: Ousmane Dembélé to return

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

PSG host AS Roma at Paris' Parc des Princes for Matchda5 of the UEFA Champions League. Les Parisiens have been flawless so far for their second consecutive title defense. Luis Enrique's men have sealed prestigious victories against Manchester City and FC Barcelona as well as dominant wins against Slovan Bratislava and Villarreal. PSG's limits will be tested as they come to terms with Désiré Doué's long-term ACL injury, which adds to Khvicha Kvaratskhelia's similar injury.

In the France international winger's absence, Luis Enrique is poised to give Mika Godts the nod against the Giallorossi, according to L'Equipe. The Belgian winger is poised to accompany Ferran Torres and the returning Ousmane Dembélé, who will start for the first time since injuring his hamstring against FC Barcelona last month. Meanwhile, Luis Enrique is not expected any more surprises in his starting line-up with the usual starters all fit for tonight.

Matvey Safonov; Nuno Mendes, Willian Pacho, Marquinhos, Achraf Hakimi; Fabian Ruiz, Vitinha, João Neves; Mika Godts, Ousmane Dembélé, Ferran Torres. (L'Eq)
