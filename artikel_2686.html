Orange in the NFL week 10 update

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Each week we want to give you an update on former Orange in the NFL. Let's get to this week's recap of the 10th week of the NFL season as two former Orange QB's took snaps.

Williams had a tackle on special teams against the Rams.

Bergeron was Atlanta's highest rated offensive player against the Chiefs according to PFF with an overall grade of 77.9.

Szmyt was 2 for 3 on his field goal attempts against the Texans. He's now 21-24 on the season for the Browns.

Dallas Cowboys: Justin Barron and Alijah Clark

Barron had a tackle on special teams while Clark saw time on defense and special teams against the 49ers.

Cisco made his Lions debut and was credited with a quarterback hurry.

Franklin missed the game against the Vikings due to injury.

Jordan remains on the practice squad in Houston, but with injuries he could be elevated this week.

Jacksonville Jaguars: LeQuint Allen and Trebor Pena

Allen made two tackles on special teams and was used as a blocker on offense against the Titans. Pena remains on the practice squad.

LA Chargers: Oronde Gadsden and Marlowe Wax

Gadsden had a receptions for 7 yards in the win over the Ravens. Wax made one tackle as he saw time on defense and special teams.

McCord was 17-32 for 209 yards and a rushing touchdown in his debut against the Colts.

DeVito replaced an injured Drake Maye and went 12-24 for 81 yards with 1 touchdown and an interception against the Lions in Munich.

Johnathan is on the injured reserve list.

Jobity saw time on special teams against the Bengals.

Cruz, Jr remains on the practice squad in San Francisco.

Tampa Bay Buccaneers: Riley Dixon, Iffy Melifonwu and Sean Tucker
