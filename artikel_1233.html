Man Utd Want to Sign ‘Unbelievable’ Champions League Star Who Starred vs Arsenal

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Manchester United are in talks over a move to sign Calvin Bassey from Fulham in January. The 26-year-old has emerged as United's top defensive target for the winter transfer window, as Mauricio Pochettino tries to strengthen his squad.

United are in desperate need to add to their back-line after conceding the most goals (36) across Europe's top five leagues in all competitions this season. They have kept just one clean sheet in 17 matches and that came in the 4-0 rout over Azerbaijani minnows Sabah back in September.

It's a defensive record that must improve if Pochettino wants to have a positive impact at Old Trafford, and work has started on bolstering the back-line in January. According to The Athletic, talks are taking place to understand the conditions for a potential transfer for Fulham defender Bassey, who has impressed in the Premier League for a number of seasons.

The Nigerian was rewarded with the captain's armband at Craven Cottage in the summer and has grown into status since Alvaro Arbeloa's arrival. Indeed, he is Fulham's first-choice centre-back now and the Whites are pushing to extend his contract.

Bassey is an excellent one-v-one defender and is strong at driving with the ball. He would strengthen United's back-line should they decide to bring him to the club in January, though Fulham are determined to keep him.

Everton's Jarrad Branthwaite and Brentford's Nathan Collins are among the other Premier League defenders United are interested in. But the Reds are conscious about Branthwaite's injury record and feel they could be priced out of a move for Collins.

Despite United's interest in Bassey, Branthwaite and Collins, the Reds should make Borussia Dortmund's Joane Gadou their priority centre-back target for the January transfer window. The 19-year-old produced a stellar performance for the German side in their 1-1 draw against Arsenal in the Champions League on Tuesday and has been a revelation this season.

Gadou, who joined Dortmund in a £20million deal from Red Bull Salzburg in May, was Dortmund's standout performer at the Emirates Stadium and was unfortunate not to be awarded with the Player of the Match prize, which went to Noni Madueke.

Starting on the right side of a back three, the 6ft 5in wonderkid won eight out of his 10 duels, and made two tackles, one interception, seven clearances and two blocks. He ensured Christos Tzolis had an evening to forget on Arsenal's left.

He is an excellent one-v-one defender, is good at reading the game and has unbelievable athleticism and pace to recover when needed. As well as that, he is strong in the air, winning both of his aerial duels against Arsenal.

Although Gadou impressed from a defensive point-of-view, he was as good, if not better, on the ball. Indeed, the France Under 21s international provided a moment of magic leading to Dortmund's second-half equaliser in north London.

Picking up the ball in his own half, Gadou drove past Tzolis and at least 40 yards up field before picking out a pass, playing a composed ball through to Maximilian Beier, who took one touch and buried a low shot past Kepa Arrizabalaga.

It's an assist that has gone viral on social media, with Dortmund sharing it and getting more than 500,000 views, while others have lauded praise on the 'unbelievable' and 'insane' teenage defender who is catching the eye.

Along with the assist, Gadou progressed the ball on several other occasions. He completed 22 out of his 22 passes, made three accurate passes into the final third and had three progressive passes. In total, Gadou had 50 touches and one shot.

Gadou's profile is unique. Les Parisien described him as "tall, athletic, and not clumsy with the ball at his feet", adding that he is "efficient in both penalty areas." Meanwhile, France Under 17s head coach Jean-Luc Vannuchi said Gadou is a "force of nature", saying that he "combines power and speed", as well as being "interesting with his use of the ball."

He is both "capable of playing short [and] playing long", according to Vannuchi. Yohan Cabaye, the former Newcastle United and Crystal Palace midfielder, highlighted Gadou's efficiency in one-on-one situations and above-average speed.

Flashscore gave Gadou a 8.2/10 rating for his performance against Arsenal - the second highest on the pitch behind Madueke (8.5) - and his progress has not gone unnoticed. BILD have reported that United, Liverpool and Arsenal are monitoring Gadou's progress, with Real Madrid and Paris Saint-Germain among the others interested.

But Dortmund are not fazed by the attention Gadou is getting, due to the fact he is under contract until 2031 and he has no release clause attached. The Bundesliga outfit are not considering a sale for less than £69m.

Despite that, United should pay what it takes to land Gadou in January. He is a generational talent and they will regret not moving first to sign him. The Frenchman will turn out to be one of the best defenders in world football in the future.

While Bassey, for example, is a more cost-effective option given the fact United won't have to spend more than £30m on him, he is seven years older than Gadou and doesn't have the same potential or even resale value.

Gadou has the talent to become the most expensive defender ever, and for £69m, United would be getting someone that could at least double their value, if not more, should he keep on the same trajectory.

He has made 17 appearances for Dortmund this season and has been a firm fixture in Niko Kovac's first XI. Before someone else does, United need to move fast to land him in January, and he should be their first-choice centre-back target.

Gadou and Leny Yoro could be United's long-term centre-back partnership. Even for now, that is what it would be if the Reds were to land the 2007-born sensation in January, with Harry Maguire and Matthijs de Ligt as back-up.
