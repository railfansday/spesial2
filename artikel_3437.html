HBO's Game Of Thrones Franchise Successfully Replaced Daenerys After Season 8's Fiasco

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Game of Thrones season 8’s controversial finale risked raising a massive Targaryen problem, but HBO successfully found a Daenerys replacement. The ending of Game of Thrones season 8 was disappointing for a wealth of reasons, chief among them the frustrating conclusion of several character arcs. Be it Jon Snow’s true heritage amounting to nothing of note, Bran somehow becoming king, Jamie Lannister abandoning his seasons-long redemption at the last minute, or Daenerys burning down King’s Landing, to name only a few instances, Game of Thrones season 8 sorely fumbled the many excellent characters it had spent years building.

Daenerys’ ending was perhaps the most controversial; Game of Thrones season 8’s shorter runtime rushed through her descent into the Mad Queen, taking her from a fan-favorite hero to an unbelievable villain right at the last moment. Aside from being an unsatisfying conclusion for such a beloved character and contributing to the general negative reception of the ending of one of the best TV shows of all time, Game of Thrones’ Daenerys fiasco risked the future of HBO’s franchise as a whole.

After all, the Targaryen family permeates every important story in the A Song of Ice and Fire universe. Knowing that the family tree leads to disappointment and essentially dies out via Daenerys’ story, audiences could have found it difficult to invest in other members of that house earlier in the timeline. House of the Dragon faced this challenge with Rhaenyra, a Targaryen queen at the center of a Game of Thrones spinoff set centuries before Daenerys’ time. Thankfully, though, as House of the Dragon nears its end, it has become clear that HBO avoided the possible issues presented by Daenerys’ ending by replacing her effectively.

Due to the problems caused by Daenerys’ ending, HBO needed Rhaenyra to succeed as a protagonist. In the Fire and Blood book, Rhaenyra’s character is a lot closer to how Daenerys was portrayed in Game of Thrones season 8. Book Rhaenyra is much colder, calculated, ruthless, and cruel towards her subjects, which House of the Dragon altered. The show took advantage of Fire and Blood’s intentionally unreliable narration, ultimately depicting Rhaenyra with a lot more sympathy and nuance.

Rhaenyra Targaryen has already been through a couple of rewrites in House of the Dragon, but season 3 is about to lean into a much darker twist.

As a result, the character came across a lot more like early Daenerys: someone who wanted the throne and the power that came with it, but for the honor of her family and, as added in the TV adaptation, for the greater good of the realm in the upcoming battle against the White Walkers. Alongside her more sympathetic moments, Rhaenyra became a much better character to follow than she was in the book, and a much better character than the one Daenerys became. In this sense, House of the Dragon reclaimed the Targaryen name by replacing Daenerys after Game of Thrones sullied it.

Via this reclamation of the Targaryen name and image, House of the Dragon set the franchise up for a promising future. After House of the Dragon came A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms, which also features a Targaryen prince as one of its lead characters. If Rhaenyra had been just another merciless queen like Game of Thrones depicted Daenerys to be in season 8, audiences could have had a harder time connecting with young Aegon, also known as Egg. Now, Egg is beloved, and will continue to be so in future seasons.

The same can be said for another HBO project, the upcoming Game of Thrones movie. Set to adapt Aegon the Conqueror’s, well, conquest of Westeros, the film will depict the very beginning of the Targaryen dynasty that continues through Rhaenyra and ends with Daenerys. Thanks to the former’s success in House of the Dragon and this being fresher in the memory for audiences, this story could be easier to stomach than if it were released close to Game of Thrones season 8’s Daenerys fiasco.
