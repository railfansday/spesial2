Pedro Pascal’s Last Of Us Season 3 Return Gets Definitive Update

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Pedro Pascal's Joel may be dead, but his presence lives on in The Last of Us season 3.

Adapted from the Naughty Dog game of the same name, the HBO series has chronicled the journey of Ellie, a young woman with an immunity to a fungal virus that wiped out the world who strives to survive in the post-apocalypse, all while bonding with Pascal's Joel, a courier with a tragic past. Staying true to its source material, The Last of Us season 2 saw the death of Joel, with Ellie subsequently setting out on a quest for vengeance against Kaitlyn Dever's Abby and her group.

During a recent interview with The Hollywood Reporter for his work on The Terminal List and Dexter: Resurrection, Gabriel Luna opened up about his work on The Last of Us season 3. As the conversation shifted to Pedro Pascal and Joel, a definitive update was given that the former lead star will not return for the HBO show's final season, though Luna assures that "the spirit of Joel runs through everything" and praises Pascal for the presence he brought to the set that is still being carried forward:

Gabriel Luna: Pedro committed such an extraordinary performance to the screen, and you know the audience is certainly holding that flame for him. All the characters are too. In every action, in every life Tommy takes and in every round fired, there is that longing for his brother. I know Bella feels the same way about Ellie and the character’s grief. So, in the world of the show and in our memories of the great time we spent working together, we just carry him with us. But I always say Bella Ramsey is a great general, and we’re in great hands with them at the helm.

It shouldn't come as too much of a surprise that Pascal won't be returning as Joel for The Last of Us season 3 for a few reasons. For starters, with the show's final season set to adapt the second half of Naughty Dog's 2020 sequel game, the plot is now shifting to making Abby the primary character in order to showcase her life before and after killing Joel and the deadly standoff with Ellie and Luna's Tommy in the season 2 finale.

Additionally, Pascal has remained steadily busy all year with a variety of productions. In addition to doing press for the highly-anticipated Avengers: Doomsday, the four-time Emmy nominee was busy with the Todd Haynes-helmed De Noche and Tony Gilroy-helmed Behemoth!, both of which he rescued from being scrapped after their original leading stars departed their productions. He's also expected to return for Avengers: Secret Wars, which is tentatively slated for an early 2027 filming start.

That being said, while his current status may be a definitive absence from the final season, there are still a few ways Pascal could return in The Last of Us season 3. As established in season 2, there are still plenty of gaps to fill with Joel and Ellie's relationship in the five-year time jump from season 1, though it did tackle the ones featured in the sequel game. With Neil Druckmann not co-showrunning the new season, there is a chance Craig Mazin could venture into original territory to do so.

The Last of Us season 2, episode 6 is made up almost entirely of flashbacks across a sprawling timeline. When do all the flashbacks take place?

One of the other most likely ways Pascal could still return for The Last of Us season 3, which still has a quarter of its production remaining, is as a vision to Ellie or Abby. Those familiar with the game know the direction the story is taking both of its characters on the way to an emotional showdown, and Joel's presence in some capacity could be what's needed to keep one or both of them from going into darker territory.

The Last Of Us is a television adaptation of the acclaimed video game series, set in a post-apocalyptic world where a smuggler named Joel (Pedro Pascal) is tasked with escorting a teenage girl, Ellie (Bella Ramsey) across the ravaged United States. Along their journey, they confront various threats from both infected creatures and hostile human factions.
