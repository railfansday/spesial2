Justin Timberlake Makes Rare Comment About Ex Britney Spears in Doc Trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Justin Timberlake looked back at his rise to fame alongside ex Britney Spears in the trailer for his upcoming Apple TV docuseries, Timberlake.

“Our fan base grew so exponentially,” the “SexyBack” singer, 45, recalled of his boy band days in the Monday, November 23, trailer. “And Britney was opening for ‘NSync.”

Timberlake continued, “We were on the road together. And we were all seeing the world.”

Timberlake’s mother, Lynn Harless, also recalled the start of her son’s relationship with Spears, now 44, describing the public attention as “the definition of insanity.”

The trailer featured a handful of clips and photos from Spears and Timberlake’s relationship as the ‘NSync member reflected on “some things that are really hard to talk about.”

Timberlake and Spears met as child performers on The All-New Mickey Mouse Club in 1993. The pair reconnected years later as they both rose to fame in their respective music careers. Their high-profile relationship, which included public appearances at the 2000 MTV Video Music Awards and the 2001 American Music Awards, lasted from 1999 to 2002.

Following their split, Timberlake released his hit song “Cry Me a River,” in which he alluded to Spears cheating on him.

“I’ve been scorned. I’ve been pissed off. I wrote ‘Cry Me a River’ in two hours,” Timberlake recalled in his 2018 memoir, Hindsight: & All the Things I Can’t See in Front of Me. “I didn’t plan on writing it. The feelings I had were so strong that I had to write it, and I translated my feelings into a form where people could listen and, hopefully, relate to it. People heard me and they understood it because we’ve all been there.”

In her own 2023 memoir, The Woman in Me, Spears admitted to being unfaithful to Timberlake and accused him of cheating on her with an unnamed woman. Timberlake never addressed her allegations.

Timberlake and Spears’ relationship has been revisited many times over the years, particularly in a handful of 2021 documentaries about the “Toxic” singer and her conservatorship, which lasted from 2008 to 2021. At the time, Timberlake issued a public apology “for the times in my life where my actions contributed to the problem.”

“The industry is flawed. It sets men, especially white men, up for success,” he wrote via Instagram at the time. “It’s designed this way. As a man in a privileged position I have to be vocal about this. Because of my ignorance, I didn’t recognise it for all that it was while it was happening in my own life but I do not want to ever benefit from others being pulled down again.”

Timberlake continued, “I have not been perfect in navigating all of this throughout my career. I know this apology is a first step and doesn’t absolve the past. I want to take accountability for my own missteps in all of this as well as be part of a world that uplifts and supports. I care deeply about the wellbeing of the people I love and have loved. I can do better and I will do better.”

The first two episodes of Timberlake premiere on Apple TV on Friday, December 11, with two additional installments set for Friday, December 18, and Wednesday, December 23.
