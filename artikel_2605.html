Vikes Views: Who Ya Got? Atlanta Falcons at Minnesota Vikings

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Minnesota Vikings schedule appears to lighten up this week with the low-flying Atlanta Falcons headed north. The Falcons have the third-worst passing defense in the NFL, allowing 239 yards per game through the air. The Vikings offense should be able to move the ball against a very mediocre defense. When Atlanta has the ball, expect a heavy dose of Bijan Robinson. Even with Robinson, the Falcons offense has been average at best.

Kirk Cousins will face Atlanta for the first time since he left. He was 12-10 as a starter for the Falcons.

Former Vikings OC Kevin Stefanski is the Falcons head coach. Stefanski was OC for the 10-6 2019 season. That season ended with a Divisional round loss to the 49ers after beating the Saints in the Wild Card round.

The Falcons have stepped up against good competition this year. They have beaten the Packers on the road and the 49ers in Atlanta.

The Falcons have the 3rd worst turnover margin in the NFL at -10 for the year. The Vikings are tied for 4th best with a +6 margin.

This is a game the Vikings must win if they are going to be taken seriously as contenders. It should be an easy win for the home team. However, this team usually doesn't win easy. If the Falcons offense can get Bijan Robinson going early and their offense can avoid turnovers, it won't be easy. The Packers and 49ers both found that out the hard way. The Falcons are capable of losing to anyone, but every once in a while they surprise people and knock off a good team. I don't expect that to happen this week. The Vikings should return home angry after the loss in Mexico City, and this feels like the perfect opportunity to get back on track. Kirk gets Jefferson going again, the defense forces a turnover or two, and the Vikings pull away in the second half.
