How National Guard units are being deployed around the midterm elections

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

National Guard members in at least seven states will perform some sort of role during the highly charged midterm elections. As Americans cast their ballots, plain clothes National Guard members will help monitor ballot boxes in seven counties across Washington state; monitor and defend election networks in Iowa; and assist with election cybersecurity in Colorado.…
