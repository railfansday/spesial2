Mikah Ballew’s 24 points lead Cal State Northridge past Grace U 108-69

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

LOS ANGELES (AP) — Mikah Ballew's 24 points helped Cal State Northridge defeat Grace U 108-69 on Thursday night.

Ballew added three steals for the Matadors (1-2). Brooklyn Hicks added 21 points and 10 rebounds. Josiah Davis had 14 points and shot 5 of 8 from the field and 4 for 4 from the free-throw line.

Noah Charles led the way for the Talons with 26 points and six assists. Grace U, a member of the National Christian College Athletic Association, also got 14 points and five assists from Caleb Palmer. Ante Topic finished with 11 points and two steals.
