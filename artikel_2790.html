Wisconsin basketball vs Fairfield score, live updates, highlights

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

MADISON – Wisconsin men's basketball faces a team Nov. 10 that won 21 games last year. There's a big catch, though.

Most of Fairfield's wins were against Metro Atlantic Athletic Conference competition. The Stags' 21-13 season ended with a loss to Siena, an eventual NCAA Tournament No. 16 seed, in the MAAC semifinals.

Fairfield (1-1) is visiting the Kohl Center tonight for the second all-time meeting against the Badgers (2-0). (The first meeting was in 1992, when Michael Finley's 24 points and nine rebounds led the Badgers to a 76-55 win at the UW Field House.)

Tipoff is set for 7:30 p.m., and Big Ten Network will broadcast the game.
