C-Falls spikers advance to Western A Divisional

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Columbia Falls Wildkats won a pair of play-in matches, over Browning and Ronan, to claim the fourth and final Northwest A spot into volleyball divisionals.

The Kats beat Browning 25-9, 25-89, 25-10 on Thursday and on Saturday took down Ronan 25-19, 25-15, 25-18.

Riley Sweeney had 11 kills in the win over Browning and added nine against Ronan; Jezanie Brooks 23 assists, six digs and four aces against Browning and 37 assists against Ronan. Anabelle Middlesworth totaled 13 kills and three aces in the two matches; Peyton Ross had 20 combined kills. Ava Scholz had four aces and 16 digs in the win over Ronan.

Columbia Falls' next action is Thursday at the Western A Divisional in Hamilton, against Dillon of the Southwest A.
