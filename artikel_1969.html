Sources: Ryan Naderi's reaction to West Ham interest revealed

Rangers star Ryan Naderi would be prepared to snub a move to West Ham United in January, sources have told Football Insider.

The 23-year-old has established himself as a crucial part of Derek McInnes' plans so far this season, where he has scored nine goals and provided six assists in 17 appearances.

Those performances have attracted interest from a number of top clubs across Europe, including West Ham, who are determiend to strengthen their attacking options.

However, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Naderi is not interested in leaving Rangers to drop into the Championship.

He is playing regularly at a high level at Ibrox, has quickly become a fans' favourite, and is in with a chance of leading his side to major silverware this season.

It might be a different question if a Premier League club comes calling, as he could then be tempted away from Ibrox, but he remains committed to McInnes' plans for now.

Football Insider reported on 9 November that Rangers could find it increasingly difficult to keep hold of Naderi if his terrific performances this season continue.

In the absence of Youssef Chermiti, the 23-year-old has stepped up and taken on the attacking burden, but his displays have attracted high-profile interest.

However, the Czechia international is believed to be happy at Ibrox and has no plans to push for a move away, with West Ham unlikely to be a tempting proposition.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I don't see how Naderi could be prised away from Rangers in the January transfer window.

"Because of his goalscoring form and his performances for Rangers, there's always going to be interest in the forward.

"But I don't see any way that Naderi would be willing to swap life at Rangers, and his role in that side, to move down to the Championship with West Ham any time soon.

"Obviously if there was Premier League clubs interested then that might be a different story in that respect, but look, Derek McInnes won't be looking to weaken his squad in January."

Much of whether Naderi completes a move away from Rangers in January will depend on whether the club are willing to sell, given his prominent role in McInnes' plans.

The forward has quickly become a fans' favourite at Ibrox alongside his impressive performances, which have wowed everybody watching including his suitors.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Rangers are prepared to do everything in their power to keep Naderi at the club long term.

His performances and role under McInnes mean Rangers are reluctant to see him leave, as they have their sights set on Scottish Cup and Premiership success this season.

Regardless of the interest from clubs across European football, Naderi is likely to remain at Rangers beyond the January transfer window, as offers will not be entertained.
