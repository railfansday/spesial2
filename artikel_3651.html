Aaron Rodgers Calls Out Female Reporter for Being ‘Dramatic’ After Blowout Loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Aaron Rodgers didn’t appreciate the line of questioning from ESPN reporter Brooke Pryor following the Pittsburgh Steelers’ blowout loss at the hands of the Cincinnati Bengals on Sunday, November 15.

During a post-game press conference after the 36-18 defeat, Pryor asked Rodgers, “How do you keep a loss like this from changing the trajectory of your season?”

“That’s very dramatic there, Brooke,” the quarterback, 42, said with a smile. Rodgers continued, “You know, I’ve been a part of this league for a long time. There’s big wins, there’s tough losses. We’ll deal with this appropriately.”

Pryor asked Rodgers a follow-up question about the team’s road struggles this season — they are 2-3 away from home, while a perfect 4-0 in Pittsburgh — but he was similarly disinterested.

“Yeah, I don’t know,” he said. “We’ll look at that.”

Pryor, who has worked for ESPN since 2019, managed to have some fun with the viral interaction. She posted a clip from Rodgers’ answer via Instagram, writing, “Am I the drama?”

Rodgers and the Steelers started remarkably slow on Sunday night, with the quarterback racking up only 27 passing yards through the first three quarters. He finished 15-for-29 passing for 143 yards and two touchdowns.

After the game, Rodgers said he was “not happy with the way we performed on offense.”

“Couldn’t get anything going until the fourth quarter,” he continued. “So that’s disappointing.”

Rodgers added, “We kind of shot ourselves in the foot with a couple plays.”

The Steelers (6-3) now sit one game behind the Bengals (7-2) in the AFC North. According to the NFL’s Next Gen Stats, the team currently has a 73 percent chance of making the playoffs.

Up next, Pittsburgh travels to face the Philadelphia Eagles on Sunday, November 22.

The loss to the Bengals came directly on the heels of a surprising social media post from Aaron, which showed him reuniting with his  younger brother, Jordan, after years of estrangement.

“Great to see little bro @jrodgers11 in his element tonight @acrisurestadium,” Aaron shared via Instagram on Friday, November 13. “Really proud of how hard he’s worked to get to where he is now 👊🏻.”

Jordan, 38, was part of ESPN’s broadcast crew for the game between the University of Pittsburgh and Florida State on Friday night.

Aaron and Jordan’s older brother, Luke, commented on the post, “My dudes. ❤️.”

After a lengthy feud, Aaron revealed that he and Jordan recently buried the hatchet.

“I went out to Nashville and met with my older brother,” Aaron said on the September 10, episode of Cam Heyward’s “Not Just Football” podcast, referring to Luke, who lives in Tennessee with his wife and three kids. “A couple weeks after that, I met with my little brother. It was cool.”
