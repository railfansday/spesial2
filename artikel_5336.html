Supreme Court of Pakistan Makes Acquittals of Axact and Founder Shoaib Ahmed Shaikh Final, Ending Eleven-Year Case

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Courts found no evidence, no complainant witnesses and no forged documents

KARACHI, Pakistan, Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Every criminal case brought since 2015 against Axact, a Pakistani AI company, its founder Shoaib Ahmed Shaikh and its management has ended in acquittal. On Aug. 3, 2026, the Supreme Court of Pakistan dismissed as time-barred the State's petitions against the High Court of Sindh acquittals, leaving every acquittal final.
