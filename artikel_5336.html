Trump says U.S. might allow Nvidia AI chip sales to China in two to three years

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Trump said Blackwell chips will be obsolete in a year, while a bill in Congress would bar their sale to China for at least 18 months

President Donald Trump said the U.S. could allow Nvidia $NVDA to sell its Blackwell AI chips to China in two to three years, once newer processors supersede them.

“In two years or three years, I would, if I were here, I would say, go ahead,” Trump said in an interview with Bloomberg during his trip to Shenzhen, China, for the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit. Trump’s term ends in January 2029.

He said Nvidia is working on chips that will replace Blackwell. "In another year, they'll be obsolete," Trump said of the Blackwell line. He called the H200 processors the U.S. allowed Nvidia to sell to China "semi-obsolete" and said, "the 200s are old now."

Nvidia lobbied the U.S. government under Jensen Huang for permission to sell Blackwell chips to China, according to Bloomberg. The Trump administration never granted that request and approved sales of the less advanced H200 instead.

Some U.S. lawmakers want Congress to block Blackwell sales to China for at least 18 months, according to Bloomberg. Sen. Jim Banks, an Indiana Republican, and Sen. Elizabeth Warren, a Massachusetts Democrat, have steered their AI Overwatch Act to the last stage of talks on the yearly defense bill.

Nvidia stopped shipping licensed Hopper data center chips to China, interim CEO Colette Kress said on an earnings call on Tuesday. The Hopper family includes the H200. Nvidia booked $2.4 billion in China sales of licensed Hopper hardware in its fiscal third quarter, which ended Oct. 25. Kress gave no reason for the halt.

The halt came days after Huang, Nvidia's co-founder, resigned as CEO on Nov. 12 after admitting to an affair. The woman is a former executive at Megaspeed, an Nvidia customer based in Singapore. U.S. intelligence officials suspect she is an agent of Beijing's spy agencies. China's Foreign Ministry has called the espionage allegation unsubstantiated.

The Trump administration also issued rules that limit sales of advanced U.S. chips to data centers that plan to give Chinese companies remote access. Kress said Nvidia's backlog of unfilled orders for high-end products such as Vera Rubin means the rules would not affect its top-line revenue.
