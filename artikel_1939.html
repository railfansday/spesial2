Peacehaven and Telscombe hold Crowborough – Isthmian clash in pictures

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

It was the Crows that struck first when on eight minutes Josh Turner put the ball past Charlton Kneath.

That was how it stayed until the interval but five minutes after the break Peacehaven levelled when Marc Noble went down the right wing and cut into the box, where visiting defender Will Samways was ajudged to have brought him down.

Up stepped Connor Tighe to smash home the spot-kick for 1-1.

It was end-to-end for a while and looked like anyone’s game but on 78 minutes, Crows’ Fin Chapman put them 2-1 up.

Lewes Beebee had the last say for The Tye when he levelled it up at 2-2 with a nice finish past Charlie Holmwood in the Crows goal.

The shared spoils left Peacehaven 16th and Crowborough 17th.

The Crows visit Broadbridge Heath on Tuesday (Nov 3) and host Sheppey on Saturday, when Peacehaven visit Herne Bay.
