Boeing's 737 Max deliveries are estimated to have dropped to a nearly one-year low in October

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Free daily briefing on global business news.

An estimate of 32 jets compares with 43 in September; Boeing's official figures are due later this month

Boeing $BA handed over an estimated 32 of its 737 Max jets in October, its lowest monthly tally in nearly a year, according to Bloomberg.

The estimate rests on Cirium data and a note from Jefferies analyst Sheila Kahyaoglu. September's total was 43, and October's count would be the lowest since November of last year, according to Bloomberg.

Ahead of the official release later this month, a Boeing spokesperson would not comment on how many 737 Max jets were delivered in October, according to Bloomberg.

Boeing said last week it would miss its target of delivering 500 737s this year. The company aims to reach a steady output of 47 jets a month in early 2027 and 52 a month in the second half of 2027. Executives had said in May that Boeing would reach the 47-a-month rate this summer.

Wing production is limiting how fast Boeing can ramp up. "Our output of wings is improving, but we need to demonstrate sustained performance at this level and ensure we have a high confidence plan to step up to rate 52 when the system is ready," CEO Kelly Ortberg said on the company's earnings call.

Boeing stock has lost roughly a fifth of its value so far in 2026.

Monthly delivery figures can swing for many reasons and don't necessarily signal weaker output at Boeing's Seattle-area factories, according to Bloomberg. Manufacturers usually ship more planes during the last three months of the year, when they push to hit yearly goals and clear stock.

Airbus handed over roughly 74 aircraft during October, according to Bloomberg.
