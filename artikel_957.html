WBC 'absolutely' backs Kabayel title unification with Fury-AJ winner

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Tyson Fury meets Anthony Joshua for the WBA 'super' heavyweight title on December 11, while Agit Kabayel puts the WBC belt on the line against Nelson Hysa on November 28; WBC president Mauricio Sulaiman will "absolutely" back a unification with the Fury-AJ winner next

The WBC will support a 2027 unification between its heavyweight champion and the winner of Tyson Fury’s vs Anthony Joshua.

Agit Kabayel will defend his WBC heavyweight title against Nelson Hysa on November 28, and if he successfully defends his belt the WBC will "absolutely" be in favour of a meeting with the winner of the huge all-British heavyweight showdown on December 11.

Fury vs Joshua could have been a final eliminator for the WBC title, though Fury and Joshua will now box for the WBA 'super' heavyweight championship.

"It was a natural final elimination for the mandatory, one against two [in the rankings], but the promoters did not request the fight to be a final elimination and they chose to do [it for] another organisation's title," WBC president Mauricio Sulaiman told Sky Sports.

"The industry likes unifications, people like to see undisputed and we don't get in the way. We like to support that."

Kabayel won't be under the obligation of an immediate mandatory defence.

"Kabayel is going to defend at the end of the month and then he is free - we did not mandate any fight [for the winner] because we believe the mandatory contender must be the highest available real mandatory," Sulaiman said.

"We will see if we're going to order a final elimination or if there's a unification or if there's something else that can be done."
