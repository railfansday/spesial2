Glasner ‘no magician’ but expected more Premier League points at Forest

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Oliver Glasner quipped he does not have "magic dust" he can spread to turn Nottingham Forest's Premier League fortunes around after an underwhelming start to the season.

Forest sit 17th in the Premier League ahead of Friday's clash with Chelsea at the City Ground and are only outside the relegation zone on goal difference ahead of Sunderland.

Glasner's side were beaten 2-0 away to Bournemouth last time out, and have now won just two of their last 14 top-flight games (D5 L7), with their problems coming in attack.

The Tricky Trees failed to score against Bournemouth but registered 2.14 expected goals, the highest xG tally by any team not to score in a Premier League match this season.

Forest have failed to score in more Premier League games than any other side this season (six), while only Sunderland (eight) have fewer goals overall than Forest (nine).

Glasner does, however, feel there is room for optimism, though he conceded that he expected to have more points than the nine they currently have after 11 league matches.

"We all thought and wished we would have more points. Performances in games were better than results. We wanted and expected more from us to be honest," Glasner said.

"We have to accept the reality. I'm no magician. I have no magic dust I can spread. We need to improve our individual mistakes.

"We need to improve our efficiency in attack. Still no goals from set pieces. I think I always talked about stability and consistency. This is what we still have to find and do better.

"To really perform consistently on a high level, we get enough points and we get enough wins. Then you climb the table and this is what we're looking for."

Following a 3-1 win at Stamford Bridge in May, Forest are looking to win consecutive league games against Chelsea for the first time since April 1979 (a run of three).

And their hopes of improving in the final third have been boosted ahead of kick-off, with former Chelsea forward Callum Hudson-Odoi back in the Forest fold.

Hudson-Odoi missed Forest's defeat to Bournemouth with an injury picked up during the international break, but he is back involved for the visit of Xabi Alonso's side.

"Yes, Callum Hudson-Odoi trained with us and is fit," Glasner said. "We're just missing Nicolas Domínguez and [Nicolo] Savona. All others are available."

Xaver Schlager was also confirmed to be available for Glasner, having been left on the bench at the Vitaly Stadium after sustaining a slight issue on international duty.

Chelsea are unbeaten in their last five away league games against Forest (W4 D1), with their three consecutive victories their longest run of league wins at the City Ground.
