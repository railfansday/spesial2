Illegal alien receives lengthy federal prison sentence as part of Homeland Security Task Force investigation into fentanyl trafficking in the Eastern District of Texas

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

An illegal alien from Honduras has been sentenced to federal prison as part of a Homeland Security Task Force investigation into fentanyl trafficking in the Eastern District of Texas, announced U.S. Attorney Jay R. Combs.
