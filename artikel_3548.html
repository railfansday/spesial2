Khloe Kardashian Claims Korean Beauty Treatments 'F***ed Up' Her Face

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Khloé Kardashian is recalling what she considers to be a cosmetic treatment mishap in South Korea.

“We did anything and everything,” Khloé, 42, said of the trip during an episode of “Khloé in Wonder Land” with pal La La Anthony. “Kim [Kardashian] and I came back deformed, you came back beautiful and the plane ride just made it so much worse. Kim looked like Stewie from Family Guy.”

Anthony, 44, noted that the group “didn’t go anywhere” on the trip because “nobody could leave the hotel.”

“We couldn’t leave the hotel,” Khloé replied. “Day one, we f***ed up our faces, and we were just sitting in the hotel room. If anyone asks, we don’t know what we did because we just trusted doctors. There was a translator. We were like, ‘OK, sure. This amount, perfect.’ You lay on the table, they do some stuff to you, and you’re like, ‘Oh, that’s what we agreed to?’”

While gesturing to her face, Khloé quipped that she’s “happy everything came back together.” Khloé noted that her mom, Kris Jenner, was emotional when she saw Kim’s face immediately after the treatments.

“My mom [was] crying when she saw Kim’s face like, ‘Did you ruin it? … Stop doing it,’” Khloé recalled. “We’re like, ‘No. That’s why Kim has this plane, we have to try all treatments around the world.’ … It was so fun. We need to do that again.”

Khloé has been open about her beauty regimen and the cosmetic procedures she has undergone through the years. In 2025, Khloé set the record straight after plastic surgeon Dr. Jonny Betteridge posted an Instagram video guessing the work the Kardashians star had gotten done.

“I take this as a great compliment! First off I think these photos are about 15 years apart, But here’s a list of things that I have done,” she wrote. “I’ve been very open in the past about what I have done so here we go.”

Khloé shared that she underwent a nose job courtesy of Dr. Raj Kanodia and had later hair removal “laser Hair for the hairline and everywhere else 🤭” at Sev Laser Aesthetics. The mom of two admitted that she’s had Botox in her face and sculptra, an injection that helps restore facial volume after she had her face tumor removed, done at 7Q Spa Laser & Aesthetic Center. Khloé also tried her hand at soft wave laser treatments “for skin tightening.”

“Filler in the past but not any over the last few years (I hear it never goes away, so I’m sure it’s still there but calmed down 🙏🏽),” Khloé continued, explaining that part of her body transformation is because she “lost 80 pounds over the years.”

For a youthful and glowy look on her skin, Khloé uses “collagen baby Threads underneath [her] chin and neck” as well as “salmon sperm facials/ regular facials, peptides, vitamins and daily skin care.”

“In 2025 there are many other things we can do before surgery but when it’s time, and if I choose to, I know some great doctors 😉💞,” she wrote.
