Kylian Mbappé makes Champions League history (again) 🌟

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

On Tuesday night, Real Madrid thrashed PSV Eindhoven (5-1) on matchday five of the Champions League's league phase.

Trailing 1-0 at half-time, Madrid scored five goals in the second half, including a brace from Kylian Mbappé.

The France captain scored his 75th and 76th career Champions League goals, reaching another historic milestone with his first goal of the evening.

Mbappé became the second-youngest player in the competition's history to reach 75 goals, behind Lionel Messi. He is also the third-fastest player to reach that tally, in just 103 appearances, behind the Argentine and Robert Lewandowski!

With 76 goals now to his name, the Madrid forward sits fifth on the Champions League's all-time scoring list. He is closing in on his former teammate Karim Benzema, who is fourth with 90 goals.
