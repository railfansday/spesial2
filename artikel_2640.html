49ers win in Mexico and Bills stay top - NFL week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The San Francisco 49ers and Buffalo Bills remain the pacesetters in each conference of the NFL after both won again.

The 49ers beat the Minnesota Vikings in the last of a record nine international games this season to take their record to 9-1.

They lead the way in the NFC, with divisional rivals the Chicago Bears and Detroit Lions next best on 8-2.

Buffalo improved to 9-1 for the first time since 1991 and lead the AFC from another pair of divisional rivals, with the Kansas City Chiefs and Denver Broncos on 8-2.

The Bills could be joined by the Cincinnati Bengals, who conclude week 11 at the Washington Commanders on Monday.

Mexico hosted the first regular-season game to be played outside the US in October 2005, and the NFL returned to the country on Sunday following a four-year absence.

After being redeveloped for the 2026 World Cup, the Azteca Stadium staged its sixth NFL game and the third involving San Francisco, who kicked off this season's international series by beating the Los Angeles Rams in Australia in week one.

Sunday's game pitted the league's best offence against the best defence and was the first international game to be played in the primetime slot.

The Vikings defence had not conceded more than 23 points in a game this season, but the 49ers offence outsmarted them in front of 85,612 fans in Mexico City.

The 49ers scored four touchdowns in the first half - two by George Kittle - before running out 35-10 winners.

Mike Evans returned after missing three games with an Achilles problem to finish with 109 receiving yards and a touchdown.

San Francisco face another test of their Super Bowl credentials on Sunday at home to the defending champions, the 6-4 Seattle Seahawks, who were one of six teams on their bye this week.

The Jacksonville Jaguars entered week 11 with the best regular-season record over the past 12 months - they had lost only twice - and had scored more than 30 points in each of their past four games.

Yet the New York Giants managed to stifle the Jaguars for three quarters and led 20-6 heading into what proved to be a dramatic final quarter.

The Jaguars had to settle for a field goal after a controversial call denied them a touchdown, but they still threatened a fightback as they cut the deficit to five.

The Giants offence did enough to stop the Jags getting one more possession, though, and secure a 23-18 home win.

It was the first time the NFC East leaders have beaten a team with a winning record this season and they are now 6-3-1, while the 7-3 Jags remain in charge of the AFC South.

In his first season as head coach, John Harbaugh could lead the Giants to their first division title since 2011, when 'Big Blue' went on to claim their fourth Super Bowl.

They remain clear of the 5-6 Dallas Cowboys, whose rollercoaster season continued with a 51-30 win over the Tennessee Titans, with Dak Prescott throwing for 325 years and four touchdowns.

Cam Skattebo earned a first down to secure victory for the Giants before celebrating with a backflip [Getty Images]

Although Josh Allen threw for more interceptions than touchdowns (2-1), the Bills were still comfortable, beating the Miami Dolphins 26-9 for their sixth straight victory.

The Bills have a mouth-watering Thanksgiving clash on Thursday (01:20 GMT, Friday) at home to the Kansas City Chiefs, who beat the Arizona Cardinals 34-7.

Travis Kelce had two scores for the Chiefs, claiming multiple touchdowns in the same game for the first time since a play-off win over Buffalo in January 2024.

Emmett Johnson registered a combined 143 yards and a touchdown in the absence of fellow running back Kenneth Walker, who is set to return from a minor groin problem against Buffalo.

That game kicks off a crucial five-game stretch for Kansas City, which continues against the Rams, the Bengals, the Patriots and the 49ers.

The Chiefs are joint top of the AFC West with the Denver Broncos, who kept the pressure on by beating the Las Vegas Raiders 38-24 for a sixth straight win.

Fernando Mendoza, this year's first overall draft pick, threw his first three touchdown passes but then a pick-six which allowed Denver to clinch victory, after Jonah Coleman had run in three scores for the Broncos.

The home team won all seven of the early games, and they were favourites heading into all but one of those match-ups.

The only underdogs to emerge victorious were the Philadelphia Eagles (3-7), who beat the Pittsburgh Steelers 19-16 to end a seven-game losing streak.

The Bears and Lions both won to remain joint-top of the NFC North, and they have a Thanksgiving showdown in Detroit on Thursday (18:00 GMT).

The Bears beat the New Orleans Saints 21-13, with Chicago's Jeffery Simmons claiming three sacks, taking his tally to five in three games since his blockbuster trade from Tennessee.

On Thursday he will try to stop this season's rushing leader Jahmyr Gibbs, who rushed for another 98 yards and two touchdowns in Detroit's 31-21 win over the Tampa Bay Buccaneers.

The NFL's highest-paid running back has now tied Barry Sanders for the most rushing touchdowns (52) before turning 25 – and Gibbs' birthday is not until March.
