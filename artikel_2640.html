Aaron Rodgers calls out Tom Brady for comments on locker room ‘sanctity’

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Aaron Rodgers appeared to call out Tom Brady's "sanctity of an NFL locker room" comment in a text to Al Michaels that the Prime Video play-by-play broadcaster read during the Steelers' win over the Broncos on Friday.

"A lot of talk about the sanctity of the locker room, [Rodgers] said, 'The code of sanctity exists to protect the integrity of the locker room, not to provide immunity from accountability for its leadership,'" Michaels said, reading the message from Rodgers, which also included a claim that he was "barred" from the Pittsburgh facility.

Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers (8) looks to pass against the Indianapolis Colts in Pittsburgh, Sunday, Oct. 11, 2026. Getty Images

Rodgers drew the criticism of Brady — and numerous analysts around the NFL — after his appearance on "The Pat McAfee Show" on Wednesday, during which he accused Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy of falling asleep in meetings, breaking down a halftime huddle with "1-2-3 Packers" and missing walkthroughs. The comments followed the Steelers' decision to start Rudolph and make Rodgers inactive, which they announced Tuesday night.

And while Rodgers never referenced Brady by name in his message to Michaels, the "sanctity" line was the exact phrase that the former Patriots and Buccaneers star used.

Former NFL player Tom Brady looks on prior to a game between the Minnesota Vikings and Green Bay Packers in Minneapolis, Sunday, Dec. 29, 2024. Getty Images

"The sanctity of an NFL locker room, it's a very sacred place," Brady said Thursday on the Fox broadcast. "We shouldn't ever lose track of that as players. It's a privilege to be in that locker room. You're fighting with your teammates, you're fighting with your coaches, you're always acting as a team together. Shouldn't always be the place for private things to become public, that's what I'll say."

Brady overlapped with Rodgers in the NFL for most of his career before shifting into broadcasting after retirement, and it's unclear if Rodgers might need to contemplate a similar move after he was released by the Steelers on Wednesday.

The 42-year-old cleared waivers, but according to Prime Video reporter and NFL insider James Palmer on Thursday, teams have expressed little to no interest in signing Rodgers for the remainder of the season.

And for one night, the Steelers thrived without Rodgers, as a pair of defensive touchdowns and one touchdown pass from Rudolph improved their record to 7-4.
