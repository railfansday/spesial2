NFL analyst rips Marlon Humphrey over controversial Baltimore remarks

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Baltimore Ravens cornerback Marlon Humphrey is facing criticism after his podcast comments about the city triggered a strong response from the NFL organization.

The Ravens sent Humphrey home from practice and made him inactive for Sunday's game against the Carolina Panthers. His remarks also drew criticism from Baltimore Mayor Brandon Scott.

Speaking on NFL on CBS, former NFL defensive lineman Leger Douzable questioned Humphrey's judgment and the impact of his comments.

"First and foremost… was drafted in the first round for the Baltimore Ravens," Douzable said. "Legacy is something that players, we all think about while we're playing, whether people want to admit it or not. He's tarnished his legacy in Baltimore with this comment."

"Now, you're on your podcast. You may have been kidding around, but you don't kid around with stuff like this."

Humphrey sparked the controversy during an episode of The Marlon Humphrey Show. Asked what Baltimore was best at making, he joked, "They're best at making criminals."

Douzable called the remark "insane" and "asinine," pointing to Humphrey's connection with the city and its supporters.

"There's so many kids in that community that look up to you," he said. "As professional athletes, we have a duty to the communities that we play in… He's always been a guy that's interacted with the fans, and for you to say this just makes absolutely no sense."

Ownings Mills, MD, USA; Baltimore Ravens cornerback Marlon Humphrey (44) during practice at the Under Armour Performance Center. Mandatory Credit: Mitch Stringer-Imagn Images Credit: IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect (Reuters)

Humphrey later apologized to reporters, but Douzable questioned whether the response showed enough regret. Douzable said Humphrey's apology appeared half-hearted. He also warned that the situation could affect the cornerback's future in Baltimore.

"It was like he didn't even really want to apologize," Douzable said.

The analyst also compared the situation to C.J. Gardner-Johnson's departure from Buffalo. He suggested the Ravens could eventually release Humphrey after initially making him inactive.

"There's players gonna miss games because of injury," Douzable said. "They've already essentially made him inactive for this game coming up. And truth be told, I can see this playing out like C.J. Garner-Johnson, where the Bills kept him for a week and then released him.

"And this just sucks for the legacy that you built in Baltimore because these comments made absolutely no sense."

The controversy comes during a difficult season for Baltimore. The Ravens sit at 4-6 after five consecutive losses. Humphrey has spent his entire NFL career with Baltimore since the team selected him 16th overall in 2017. He has earned three Pro Bowl selections and first-team All-Pro honors.
