Federal, state and local partners launch "Operation Deep Cover" to end violence in Deep Ellum; Convicted felon charged with federal firearm offense

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

U.S. Attorney for the Northern District of Texas Ryan Raybould today announced Operation Deep Cover, a coordinated effort with law enforcement partners to curb violent crime in Dallas’s Deep Ellum neighborhood following multiple shootings on Halloween night. As an example of the type of conduct Operation Deep Cover is designed to target, the U.S. Attorney also announced that a criminal complaint was filed charging repeated felon Treyveion Dionte Demby with being a felon in possession of a firearm.
