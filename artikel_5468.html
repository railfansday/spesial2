Prosper AI Named to the 2026 CB Insights' 50 Most Promising Digital Health Startups

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Prosper AI recognized for its AI workforce that handles patient and payer conversations for specialty practices

NEW YORK, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- CB Insights today named Prosper AI, the AI workforce that turns patient demand into practice growth, to its eighth annual Digital Health 50, showcasing the 50 most promising digital health startups in the world.

"AI is beginning to change not just how healthcare organizations operate, but how they actually deliver care," said Ellen Knapp, Lead Healthcare Analyst at CB Insights. "This year's Digital Health 50 winners are finding new ways to extend clinicians' reach, simplify how patients access treatment, and deliver more continuous care for complex conditions."

"This recognition belongs to our customers, who trust Prosper to fill their schedules with visits that get paid, and to the patients they care for," said Xavier de Gracia, co-founder and co-CEO of Prosper AI. "Practice leaders are heading into 2027 facing an existential financial squeeze, as Medicare cuts pull insurance reimbursements down with them. An AI workforce that confirms benefits before the visit gives practices control over their operations and confidence they'll get paid for the care they provide. The best-run practices that partner with Prosper aren't cutting back or shrinking, they're growing."

The recognition comes 12 days after CMS finalized a 2.31% cut to the 2027 Medicare physician conversion factor, deeper than the 1.68% it proposed in July, putting a severe financial strain on practice owners. Operational complexity also adds to the growing strain. At a time when reimbursements are going down and costs are going up, the one thing a practice leader still controls is how well the practice runs. Prosper AI connects patient access and reimbursement across the front and back office, from scheduling and patient outreach to benefits verification, prior authorization, claims follow-up and patient billing. By handling patient and payer conversations directly within practice workflows, Prosper AI helps practice leaders grow appointment volume, protect reimbursements and recover outstanding revenue, giving teams more time to focus on care.

The list primarily includes companies using AI to reshape care delivery, streamline healthcare operations, and improve patients' access to treatment. The CB Insights research team picked winning companies based on CB Insights datasets, including deal activity, industry partnerships, team strength, investor strength, employee headcount, and proprietary metrics.
