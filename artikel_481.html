David Cameron Sees Hope in Alzheimer’s Research

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

On Bloomberg This Weekend, former UK Prime Minister David Cameron discusses the personal toll of losing his mother to Alzheimer’s and the accelerating search for better diagnostics, treatments and ultimately a cure. Cameron tells hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini about promising drug research, the potential of blood tests to transform diagnosis and why governments should move faster to support clinical trials and expand access to new treatments. Cameron’s Ending Alzheimer’s podcast launches November 16. (Source: Bloomberg)
