Alpha Aesthetics Partners Launches Practice Aesthetics, a First-of-Its-Kind Clinical Education Platform

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

BOSTON, Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Alpha Aesthetics Partners ("Alpha"), a leading medical aesthetics platform and portfolio company of Thurston Group, today announced the launch of Practice Aesthetics, a first-of-its-kind, university-based clinical education platform integrated with Alpha's growing national network of medical aesthetics practices.

Practice Aesthetics builds upon the success of Inject With Lex, the highly regarded clinical education business founded by nurse practitioner Alexa Nicholls Costa and acquired as part of Alpha's partnership with LexRx earlier this year. Under Costa's leadership, Inject With Lex established a strong foundation in university-based medical aesthetics education through partnerships with Boston College, Columbia University, and the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. Since joining Alpha, Costa has continued to play an integral role in growing the platform, expanding its university partnershipsâincluding the University of Pennsylvania School of Nursingâand integrating its educational programs into Alpha's national network of medical aesthetics practices.

Practice Aesthetics brings these programs together under a unified national brand, combining university-based certificate programs, interactive online education, immersive hands-on clinical training, and continuing professional development. By integrating these capabilities directly into its broader platform, Alpha has created a scalable approach to clinical education that benefits both the medical aesthetics specialty and its growing network of partner practices.

John Wheeler, Chief Executive Officer of Alpha Aesthetics Partners, commented:"Through our acquisition of LexRx, we gained an exceptional foundation in university-based clinical education. We recognized the opportunity to build upon that success at a much greater scale, expanding the university network, investing in the infrastructure, and integrating clinical education directly into the Alpha platform. Practice Aesthetics represents what we believe is a first-of-its-kind approach in our specialty, connecting high-quality university-based education with a national network of medical aesthetics practices to support clinicians throughout their careers."

The platform will expand access to high-quality clinical education while providing Alpha's partner practices with additional training, professional development, and recruiting resources. Existing Inject With Lex programs will continue, with courses expected to transition to the Practice Aesthetics brand beginning in 2027.

Healthcare professionals and academic institutions interested in learning more can visit practiceaesthetics.com.

Founded in 2023, Alpha Aesthetics Partners aims to advance the medical aesthetics industry through best-in-class partner support and clinical training. With a vision to build the best operating and biggest network of medical aesthetics practices in the nation, Alpha Aesthetics Partners focuses on making medical spa ownership simpler and more collaborative, allowing partners to concentrate on providing world-class patient care. The company is a portfolio company of Thurston Group, with a rapidly growing presence across 40 locations in 12 states.

Thurston Group is a private equity firm focused on building industry-leading companies in the healthcare services sector. Founded in 1986 by Chairman and CEO Patrick J. Haynes III, Thurston Group has returned over $4 billion of invested capital and has partnered with leading healthcare businesses including Alpha Aesthetics Partners, SGA Dental Partners, ARC Health, and Gen4 Dental Partners.
