A star's 'obliquity' likely has little impact on a given planet's habitability

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

A star's axial tilt as it relates to the circumstellar disk that surrounds it is known as stellar obliquity. Over space and time, there are a myriad of solar-type stars with all sorts of obliquities. But the question heretofore has always been what effect a given star's initial obliquity has on the planetary system that forms around it.
