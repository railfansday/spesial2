Queens Park Rangers v Sheffield United: Liam Kitching brings Blades a better balance

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Harrogate-born defender, on a season-long loan from Premier League Coventry City, has added real class to the Blades' backline, and was captain in the absence of the suspended Japhet Tanganga for Tuesday's 1-1 draw with Southampton.

As well as all his passing and defensive qualities, the 27-year-old former Harrogate Town and Barnsley player brings a characteristic much sought-after in modern-day centre-backs as a player who favours his left foot.

"Always the balance of a left-footer in the team is ideal, and those passes weren't big diagonals, and weren't big long balls," said manager Chris Wilder of Kitching's distribution against Saints. "It was progressive passes into the front to get us off and running and start our attacks.

“We built from the back, but we built in the territory where we wanted to build. We didn't build on the edge of the box; we built over the halfway line, into the midfield, rotated the game, kept the ball, moved it from side to side, moved the ball at good ball speed and progressed.

“(We) tried to hurt their back line at any opportunity and Liam was part of that."

Meanwhile, Wilder has given a nod to the stature of clubs currently fighting it out to make the Championship play-off places by referring to the top ten as "prime Barclays".

The phrase is a nod to the days when the bank sponsored Premier League football, and teams like QPR were regulars in it. The Rs are in seventh, the Blades ninth, a place outside play-off qualification.

“We're a fighting club," he said. "We're a proud club, and we want to be in the mix; of course we do, and you can't get away from that.

“I can't sit here and go, yeah, 12th, 13th, 14th, even though our data company predicts us to be that. That ain't for me; we're always trying to overachieve, always trying to push on, and there's a huge prize.

“You want to go and mix it with the big boys. Financially, what it does to a football club, as we know, how it affects everybody.

“The fight to get in there is going to be intense, but for me the fight isn't over after 17 games; it's over after 46 games."

Tom Cannon is in contention to play after Wilder insisted the striker's half-time withdrawal on Tuesday was a tactical decision.”
