Personal Investments • Re: Self-Directed in Retirement

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

You may have already done this and not included the info but good planning starts with goals ( what are my needs/wants, annual income from all sources, desires related to Long term care, heirs, etc.).

One old saying is: No one will care more for your money than you will. Self- learning of the benefits of simple, low cost, diverse indexes can have a high ROI.

Once you know your goals, then you can set an asset allocation and place those assets in tax efficient locations and funds. An advisor might be able to help you with that but many are trained in complication and it makes it difficult to see the beauty and profit in simplicity.

There is a sticky note with the best method to share your situation if you desire advice but if you are not yet clear on goals, I would start there.

FWIW, I serve on a couple small endowment investment committees and one started with an advisory. They originally proposed a maddeningly complex investment plan and when we pushed back, they returned with direct investing. My colleagues thought that was a good compromise and I set up a spreadsheet comparing the equity side to VG Total Stock Market. It turned out to be an somewhat expensive compromise and I reported the results quarterly. After a couple of years, we switched to Vanguard and TSM.
