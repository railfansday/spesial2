Outdoor Holding (POWW) Q2 2027 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Post-election volume surged as revenue grew for a fifth straight quarter.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Outdoor Holding Company (POWW +3.28%) reported results for the second quarter of fiscal 2027, highlighting the transition to an asset-light marketplace model following the sale of its ammunition manufacturing segment. Management stated that the business achieved its fifth consecutive quarter of year-over-year revenue growth despite the period being seasonally the softest of the fiscal year. The company reported a leaner cost structure and higher monetization through value-added services such as Federal Firearms License (FFL) transfer integration. Strategy focused on removing transaction friction and improving searchability through technology investments. Management indicated that capital allocation priorities include maintaining a strong balance sheet and returning cash to shareholders through an expanded share repurchase program.

Compliant firearms commerce at scale is difficult, and GunBroker has spent more than 25 years building a specialized marketplace, dealer network and infrastructure to facilitate it. Second, the platform. Our strategy remains to remove friction from each step of the transaction, listing, discovering, buying, paying and transfer. We intend to attach services that make commerce easier, safer and more efficient so that take rate rises because the platform is more valuable, not because the base final value fee is higher. We think about it as 2 levers. GMV and revenue per dollar of GMV. Every initiative on our road map pulls 1 of those 2 levers and we have evaluated each on that basis. We will measure our AI investment by visible improvement in productivity, response times and transaction completion. Universal payments remain a priority, and we continue to navigate the complex compliance hurdles associated with such an initiative. Nearly 30% of transactions on the marketplace are not paid by credit card today.

Card checkout for those transactions is the single largest piece of friction left in the buying process and removing it should help buyers complete more purchases and sellers close more sales. We are evaluating the commercial arrangements with payment providers, the technical integration, the underwriting and compliance requirements and the availability of payment services for firearms-related commerce. One more point on the platform on the supply side. Trailing 12-month active buyers grew 2.6% to approximately 410,000 and GMV per active seller increased 12%. Active sellers at approximately 36,000 were down 1.6% year-over-year. Sellers with unique inventory are the lifeblood of this marketplace, and we are not satisfied with a flat seller base.

Strengthening seller participation by attracting new sellers, reactivating former sellers and supporting our top sellers is a focus area for the rest of the fiscal year. Third, capital allocation. Our priorities are unchanged: keep the balance sheet strong, invest selectively in high-return platform enhancements and return excess cash to shareholders. Disciplined growth means investing in initiatives that strengthen the platform, expand monetization through value-added services and create durable long-term shareholder value. Every major decision we make fits within one of those 2 principles. Our priorities for the balance of fiscal 2027 are clear, grow marketplace activity and revenue, protect the reset cost structure while reinvesting where returns are visible, scale FFL transfer services and advance universal payments, convert technology investments into measurable productivity and turn earnings into cash and shareholder value.

Second quarter results demonstrate the earnings power of the model.
