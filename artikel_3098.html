Best Buy: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Television replacement demand from 2020 buying may keep carrying sales, but whether premium hardware can keep offsetting higher component costs remains an open question.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 24, 2026 at 9:51PM EST

On its third-quarter fiscal 2027 earnings call, held on Nov. 24, 2026, Best Buy (BBY +2.70%) announced comparable sales growth of 6.1%, its highest in five years. That growth shows how demand for customer tech upgrades is insulating the retailer against broader volatility in consumer spending. Three things management said stand out.

Best Buy is expanding beyond traditional retail into high-margin revenue streams like digital advertising and its third-party online marketplace. These higher-margin channels help protect profitability even when standard product margins face pressure.

Home entertainment unit sales peaked during pandemic buying in 2020. Upgrading those aging sets creates a multiyear tailwind for retail sales demand.

Higher component costs pushed up average selling prices (ASPs), yet customer appetite for premium hardware offset lower unit volumes. Demand held up because shoppers prioritized high-end device upgrades.

Demand is building across major product categories as customers replace aging hardware, and management isn't hiding its satisfaction with the trajectory. Best Buy relies on faster delivery networks, interactive vendor partnerships, and trained store staff to maintain pricing discipline and defend its category leadership. Execs are watching customer demand, inventory positioning, and promotional execution to capture holiday market share.
