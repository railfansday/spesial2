Chris Evans Took Secret Marvel Meetings for Years After ‘Avengers: Endgame’ to Discuss His Return Despite Telling the Press He Was ‘Happily Retired’ From the MCU

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Chris Evans’ Marvel Cinematic Universe character Steve Rogers was written out of the franchise in 2019’s “Avengers: Endgame,” but the trailers for the upcoming “Avengers: Doomsday” have his return front and center.

In a new feature in Entertainment Weekly about “Doomsday,” the team behind the franchise reveals that Evans was never too far away — despite giving various interviews after “Endgame’s” release saying he was “happily retired” and “sad to not be back with the band.” In reality, Evans “took multiple meetings with Marvel in secret in the years following ‘Endgame.’” Related Stories 'White Tiger Column' Review: Young Resistance Fighters in Myanmar Are Trained for War, but Not Yet for Life, in a Delicate Doc Study ‘Dig’ Review: Amy Poehler’s Archeology Comedy Is Shockingly Unfunny

“He’s very committed to saving the experience for the movie theater,” “Doomsday” co-director Anthony Russo said in the story when asked about Evans’ cagey comments to the press. “He’s like, ‘Why would we ever compromise that?’ Of anybody involved, as far as the cast goes, he may be the most committed to that aspect.”

Evans also recalled a dinner with Marvel Studios president Kevin Feige and co-president Louis D’Esposito while the team was still filming “Endgame.” The topic of playing Nomad, Steve’s alternate identity after giving up the Captain America title, came up.

“They said, ‘We’d love to continue making movies with you. Steve would no longer be Captain America, he would be Nomad,’” Evans said. “They sent me a pitch, and it was a great pitch. It really was. But for whatever reason at the time, I was very happy with how things wrapped up. It didn’t feel like it was the thing I was looking to do at the time.”

When audiences last saw Steve, he chose to travel back to the past to live a full life with his love, Peggy Carter (Hayley Atwell). At the end of “Endgame,” he appears in the present day as an old man to give the Captain America shield to Sam Wilson (Anthony Mackie). But in footage for “Doomsday,” Steve and Peggy look young again and have a child, so who knows what time shenanigans the movie has in store for audiences.
