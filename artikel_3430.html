Prime Video's 10/10 Fantasy Series With Perfect RT Score Returns In 4 Months & The Time To Binge It Is Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

One of Prime Video's best TV shows is officially returning in just four months after a new update, and this high fantasy series is worth a binge, with now being the perfect time to do so. The fantasy genre, and particularly the high fantasy subgenre, is on a high right now. After all, Prime Video's most ambitious TV show to date has returned.

The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power expands on the lore created by J. R. R. Tolkien, with each season being better than the last. Rings of Power season 3 is mature, dark, hard-hitting, and quite shocking, having all the necessary elements to be one of the best seasons in fantasy TV show history. That said, Prime Video has other genre shows.

Rings of Power is not the only major fantasy series on Prime Video, with the streaming service also having hits in that department in both live-action and animation. The latter is particularly important for genre fans, as Prime Video is the home of a growing high fantasy animated universe that has now set its next release, and it will come soon.

The Mighty Nein is one of the Prime Video TV shows that are based on the famous Critical Role campaigns, having come after the success of The Legend of Vox Machina. Recently, Prime Video confirmed when the show will be back with a new season, and this update makes it the perfect time to binge one of the best fantasy series in recent memory.

The Mighty Nein season 2 will officially debut on Prime Video on March 24, 2027. This new release will mark a crucial change in format for the hit series, as the animated show will release all of its eight episodes on the same day on the streaming service, which differs from season 1's release model. After a three-episode debut on November 19, 2025, The Mighty Nein season 1 went on to release its remaining episodes weekly. Well, Prime Video now seems to be embracing the show's status as one of the most bingeworthy fantasy series on streaming, and new viewers should too.

The Mighty Nein is easily one of the best TV shows on Prime Video, standing at a rare perfect 100% critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes, also boasting a stellar 89% audience score, making it one of the streamer's universally beloved shows. The key element playing in the show's favor comes from how The Mighty Nein focuses on a cast of colorful and complex characters, each coming with their unique set of quirks, abilities, and complicated and/or tragic backstories, all played by some of the best voice actors in the business, such as Laura Bailey, Ashley Johnson, Matthew Mercer, and more.

The Mighty Nein is the latest D&D adaptation from Critical Role with a more mature and evolved story that feels like they have perfected their craft.

Other major names involved with the show include Lanterns star Nathan Fillion, Hollywood icon Ming-Na Wen, VisionQuest's T'Nia Miller, and many more. With different and intricate magic systems, intense fights, many plot twists, characters audiences will quickly fall for, and a high fantasy world that is both immersive and expansive, The Mighty Nein's eight episodes make for the perfect binge before season 2's release on March 24, 2027.

For anyone who is only learning now about the show and decides to binge The Mighty Nein and — inevitably — ends up enjoying the high fantasy series and its world, there is another must-watch on Prime Video's catalog. In the months leading to The Mighty Nein season 2, there will be plenty of time to watch all four seasons of The Legend of Vox Machina to help in the wait, getting new fans caught up for that show's upcoming fifth and final season.

The Legend of Vox Machina takes place in the same world as The Mighty Nein, aka Exandria, only a couple of decades before the TV show that is set to return in 2027. The Legend of Vox Machina also boasts the same core voice cast as The Mighty Nein and overall tone, action, and style of the TV-MA animated series, making it a necessary binge to get the most out of this growing shared universe on Prime Video.
