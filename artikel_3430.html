The 89-Minute Movie That's Essential For Calvin & Hobbes Fans Is Officially Streaming For Free

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Calvin & Hobbes is among the most impactful comic strips in the history of the medium, and a fantastic 2013 film about it is now streaming for free. Filled with fantastic quotes about love and friendship, the popular strip has remained an important pop culture product even now, more than 30 years after ending its tremendous publication run.

Depicting the relationship between a precocious young boy and his stuffed tiger, Calvin & Hobbes uniquely accesses and depicts the incredible power of the imagination. The wholesome series of comics is filled with colorful characters and manifestations, drawn with some of the most striking and textured art that has ever been seen in newspaper strips.

Calvin & Hobbes ran from 1985 until 1995, and was published internationally in more than 2000 newspapers. Since then, many of the compilation books have gone on to become bestsellers in their own right, republishing the best strips from the initial run. Despite this, Calvin & Hobbes has never been directly adapted into an animated or live-action film.

The magic of the strip works best directly on the page, and creator Bill Watterson never saw the appeal in turning his comic strip into animation, even if this has been effective for the strips that influenced him, including Peanuts. However, there is still one important film about Calvin & Hobbes, and it is currently available to stream for free.

Dear Mr. Watterson is a charming 2013 documentary that celebrates Calvin & Hobbes. Directed by Joel Allen Schroeder, the film examines the enduring power of the popular strip even years after it completed its newspaper run through interviews with other well-known media figures who have a deep love for the characters.

Actor Seth Green appears in the film, alongside cartoonists including Bloom County's Berkeley Breathed. The film outlines the impact that Calvin & Hobbes had on the comic medium, while also going into how much the industry has changed in the years since.

Crowdfunded on Kickstarter, Dear Mr. Watterson is a labor of love, and primarily focused on being a celebration of the wonderful strip. And while there are some issues to be had with the film, which is fairly light on substance, it is hard to deny that it is one of the most compelling fan-made tributes ever produced.

Dear Mr. Watterson is currently free to stream on a number of services domestically, including Tubi, as well as free services that can be accessed with a library card, such as Hoopla and Kanopy. The film is well worth the watch on these ad-supported services.

While not the most groundbreaking documentary, Dear Mr. Watterson channels the love for the popular strip, and turns that into an 89-minute exploration of the wonderful work of Bill Watterson.

Those who love the Calvin & Hobbes comic strips will find much to enjoy in this feel-good documentary that remains an essential watch. With that in mind, there are a handful of notable reasons why this film is so important.

There will likely not be a Calvin & Hobbes movie anytime soon. While the strip and its underlying intellectual property remain valuable and beloved, Bill Watterson has expressed no interest in having an adaptation commissioned. Due to this, it is unlikely that any project for film or television will be made until the distant future, when the copyright changes.

If that is the case, and a studio decides to commission something in the future, any adaptation will feel hollow, and against the spirit of the original strip.

However, this documentary is a fantastic achievement in capturing the passion around the Calvin & Hobbes strips, and highlighting their cultural importance. These features make it an essential watch for fans of the comic.

Dear Mr. Watterson is a wholesome film that does not look to adapt the popular comics. Instead, this small and charming documentary adeptly captures the passion and the love that many share for these beloved characters. It is the closest thing to a Calvin & Hobbes movie that audiences could ever hope to see.
