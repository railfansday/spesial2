Bitunix Exchange Joins CodeVASP Network to Strengthen Travel Rule Compliance

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Bitunix, a global cryptocurrency derivatives exchange, has joined the CodeVASP network, a South Korea-based Travel Rule compliance provider. The move connects Bitunix to a group of more than 350 virtual asset service providers (VASPs) across 60 countries, enabling the exchange to share required transaction information with other exchanges and platforms securely.
