Chase credit cards: Best time to apply based on offer history

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

With many of the best credit cards on the market, earning a generous welcome bonus is a quick way to receive a lot of value. These cards from Chase are no exception.

It's important to know, however, that welcome offers often change.

Let's say you're considering adding a new Chase card to your wallet. When is the best time to apply so you can earn the best offer? We have you covered.

Read on to learn more about the welcome offer history across both Chase's cash-back cards and those earning Chase Ultimate Rewards points.

Below are some of the most popular Chase credit cards, along with their highest-ever offers and our recommendations for when to apply.

Note that these are all publicly available offers; some individual, targeted bonuses may be higher than the offers listed here. Additionally, some of these offers may no longer be available.

Also, keep in mind that Chase factors in its 5/24 rule when you apply. Other factors can dictate whether or not you're eligible for a Chase welcome bonus.

Consider your current wallet setup and cross-check with the terms of whichever card you decide to apply for.

The Chase Freedom Flex is a solid no-annual-fee, cash-back option for cardholders who want to maximize rotating quarterly bonus categories (on up to $1,500 in combined spending each quarter; activation is required each quarter).

If you have a card that earns Ultimate Rewards points, the cash back you earn on the Freedom Flex (and any Chase cash-back card, with the exception of the Ink Business Premier, which doesn't combine rewards with any other Chase card) can be turned into Ultimate Rewards points.

Here are the recent offers we have seen on the Freedom Flex.

The best bonus we've seen on this card is the current one: Earn a $250 bonus after spending $500 on purchases in the first three months from account opening.

We suggest applying for this card when the bonus offers at least $200 cash back.

The Chase Freedom Unlimited offers a flat 1.5% cash back on most purchases, with simple bonus-earning categories for certain everyday purchases. The card doesn't charge an annual fee.

The Freedom Unlimited's bonus offer has matched the offer on the Freedom Flex in the past, but they aren't always identical. So, it's worth comparing the two if you decide to apply.

Here are the recent offers we have seen on the Freedom Unlimited.

$200 plus up to $600 on grocery purchases

This card has offered two welcome bonuses that could be considered the highest, and it depends on what you prioritize.

One bonus offered unlimited double cash back for the first 12 months from account opening. The other offered a $200 bonus after spending $500 on purchases in the first three months from account opening, plus 5% cash back on grocery store purchases (not including Target or Walmart; on up to $12,000 spent on these purchases) during the first year.

This card could be worth applying for when the bonus offers double cash back for the first year, or at least $200 plus a bonus category for extra earnings during the first year.

Welcome bonuses often fluctuate, so if you're interested in applying for a new card, it's worth checking out its offer history and comparing it to the present offer.

Since so much of a card's value is found in the welcome offer, you may find that the best strategy is to hold off until a lucrative limited-time bonus appears so you can earn a large number of bonus points or cash back.
