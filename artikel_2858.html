Christian Parker loses play-calling as Cowboys’ defensive coordinator

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Back on October 13, we noted how Christian Parker seemed to be getting Sean Desai'd with the Dallas Cowboys bringing Mike Pettine in as a defensive assistant. That prophecy has now fully come to fruition with Parker losing play-calling duty to Pettine.

It's not hard to understand why the Cowboys are making this move; Dallas has the worst defense in the NFL. They just allowed 46 points and 584 yards in a home loss to the San Francisco 49ers. The Cowboys are looking for a spark that can get them pointed in the right direction with the NFC East crown still within reach.

The Desai change famously didn't work well for the Eagles in 2023. Their defense was even worse under Matt Patricia. But the Cowboys don't really have any room to go but up from here.

On one hand, it's funny to watch the Cowboys scramble like this. On the other hand, it's unfortunate that Parker — a contributor to the Eagles' Super Bowl LIX victory — is floundering and now being publicly embarrassed by being demoted. It would probably be more respectful for the Cowboys to fire him as opposed to keeping up this charade where he's the defensive coordinator in title only.
