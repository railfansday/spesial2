Naugatuck football rolls past Seymour with potent ground game, much-improved defense

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

NAUGATUCK - Going into this high school football season, there was some uncertainty in Naugatuck as to how good the Greyhounds could be. Although the team had some offensive talent, coach Chris Anderson needed to see something more from his players. He wanted to see them play with heart, with conviction for one another and with an attitude befitting a champion.

"Our team motto is, 'Earn it,'" Anderson said. "Since Day 1, we have told them the only thing we are entitled to is the air we breathe. Everything else we have to go earn. We felt like we lost that a little bit last year. We had a little entitlement and issues like that. We wanted to get back to old-fashioned hard work, and 'Earn it' has been our rally cry."

That rallying cry could be heard loud and clear throughout Craig Peters Field on Friday night as the Greyhounds remained undefeated with a dominant 41-12 win over a very good Seymour team that had won five games in a row.

Qwentarvous Lee scored two touchdowns and threw for another. Brandon Andoh rushed for a game-high 145 yards, scored two rushing touchdowns and caught a 68-yard touchdown pass, and the Naugatuck defense held a potent Wildcats offense in check to complete the total team effort.

Seymour had scored 44 points or more in each game of its winning streak, but Naugatuck intercepted three passes and forced the Wildcats to turn the ball over on downs three times while the Greyhounds ran out to a 21-0 lead and cruised to their eighth win of the season without a loss.

The Naugatuck defense set the tone early, as the Greyhounds stopped Seymour on a 4th-and-9 play at the Naugy 34-yard line on the Wildcats' first possession of the game.

Then Naugy needed only five plays to score on a 57-yard pass play from Sean DiBona to Malachi Kinchen.

Seymour drove deep into Naugatuck territory on its second possession, but again came away with nothing when Kinchen intercepted Max Brito's pass on fourth-and-8 from the Greyhounds' 18-yard line, creating a major momentum swing.

Eric Harriott and MJ Delpino also intercepted passes for the Naugatuck defense, which was led by inside linebacker Josiah Loyd, Raily Hernandez and Isaiah Portalatin.

"I can tell you that we have definitely improved on defense from the beginning of the season," Kinchen said. "Our defense looked a little shaky in the beginning of the season, but our coaches made adjustments and put players in spots they needed to be, and they made plays."

Quarterback Max Brito led Seymour offensively with 77 yards on 17 carries, while Connor Shea finished with 58 yards on 11 carries. They each scored a touchdown.

They couldn't keep up with Naugy's combination of Lee and Andoh.

Lee made the plays that really made the difference in the first half when the game was being decided. He connected with Andoh on a 68-yard TD pass to give Naugy a 14-0 lead and then scored on an 18-yard touchdown run to make it a three-score game with 3:46 to play in the half.

"I am always trying to find an alley," Lee said. "We ran to the right, it was kind of closed up a little bit, so I cut back and guys on the other side of the play are always blocking and they were there for me blocking. It is all about giving 100% on every play and never letting your foot off the gas."

He also scored on a 29-yard run for the first points of the second half to put the Greyhounds up, 28-6.

While Lee started the Naugatuck offense rolling, Andoh made certain that Seymour wasn't going to be able to recover in the second half. On the final scoring drive of the game, he had runs of 36 yards and 16 yards.

The dual threat of Lee and Andoh kept Seymour off balance the entire game as they took turns making big plays to sustain drives.

"Great players make coaches look good for sure, and they are a nice 1-2 punch," Anderson said. "They are also very humble, and they complement each other very well. As a coach I never want to be a one-trick pony so I want to get other people involved. Malachi Kinchen was involved tonight and Sean DiBona did some nice things. It was a true team effort."

Friday's game marked the 74th meeting between the two teams, with Naugatuck leading the series 38-33-3.

Qwentarvous Lee, Naugatuck: The Greyhounds' quarterback/running back had a 68-yard touchdown pass and TD runs of 18 and 29 yards while finishing with 142 yards on 14 carries.

"I have to give a big shout out to our O-Line. We probably have 2,000 pounds on our O-Line. They are huge, and I want to thank them for blocking and creating gaps for us." - Qwentarvous Lee on the play of the massive offensive line that includes Raily Hernandez, Isaiah Portalatin, Dallas Autore, Logan Washenko and Llir Mamudov.
