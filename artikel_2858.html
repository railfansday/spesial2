This may sound insane, but leading a touchdown drive.

Since the miracle win against the Dallas Cowboys, the Arizona Cardinals have not scored a touchdown. They have three field goals.

So, against the third ranked scoring defense, let's just shoot for a touchdown and improving as a unit as they head into a more realistic schedule from a competitive standpoint after this debacle in Kansas City.

There is also now a chance the Cardinals don't even start Beck as he is dealing with back spasms.

Man, talk about an organization talking out of both sides of their mouth.

The trade for Marv was after a complete dumpster fire to start the season for him, then they moved Brissett because he was benched and wanted out.

Then the cavalcade of injuries has led to Josh Johnson likely starting a game in the year 2026 in the NFL.

You had two veteran quarterbacks that would keep you competitive. You drafted a running back third overall because you needed to get a piece that fit what Mike LaFleur wanted to do on offense; you paid Trey McBride and Michael Wilson super high-end money because this wasn't a rebuild.

And within 10 weeks, it is not only a clear rebuild, but a complete teardown… except trading a 30-year-old safety on a bottom-three defense in the NFL.

McBride was always the focal point, but the injury to Wilson made him the only legitimate target. The Cardinals wide receivers remaining to throw to:

Devin Duvernay – six receptions 57 yardsSimi Fehoko – two receptions 38 yardsHarrison Wallace – one reception nine yardsReggie Virgil – three receptions 54 yardsZay Jones – no catches no yards

The Cardinals entire wide receiver room for the season: 12 receptions 158 yards

Those are not the stats for last week, those are for the entire season.

So, it'll be a lot of Trey McBride, but the Rams and Seahawks have put the gameplan out on letting him get receptions and not gaining yards. And now it could be Josh Johnson starting… yay!

They seem to be okay covering, but they are terrible at rushing the passer, and covering for four seconds in the NFL is not ideal. They cannot stop the run, but I genuinely think Emmett Johnson may not be needed as much because the Chiefs will likely be up by so much.

The Chiefs will be able to throw the ball if they want, run the ball if they want, score as much as they want. It will be a complete cluster on Sunday.

The Cardinals have not scored a touchdown in two games. I don't see that changing with Josh Johnson as the quarterback after just 10 days with the team.

Good luck and good health to the Chiefs in this one.
