Gilmore Girls' Milo Ventimiglia Gives Rare Nod to Ex Alexis Bledel

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Gilmore Girls alum Milo Ventimiglia made a rare reference to his former girlfriend Alexis Bledel, whom he dated while filming the Amy Sherman-Palladino series.

Milo Ventimiglia has been out on the road for a long time.

But when he thinks back to his time on Gilmore Girls and his relationship with costar Alexis Bledel, he feels anything but lonely and so cold.

“What were those days like on set?” he reflected to Us Weekly in an interview published Nov. 2. “Hanging out with Jared Padalecki, being with Alexis—the experience is something that I always lean into, that really fills me up.”

The This Is Us star and Bledel’s onscreen chemistry as Jess and Rory found legs off camera, with the pair quietly dating for almost four years in the aughts before their 2006 breakup. But while their romance wasn’t destined to last, the pair’s relationship was and has always remained amicable.

In fact, some of the memories that stand out clearest for Ventimiglia—who shares two kids with wife Jarah Mariano—involve his former girlfriend.

“I remember shooting on the back lot of Warner Bros. with Alexis and getting on the bus and carrying a bag that the prop guy wanted to make really light,” he said. “But I [said], ‘Yeah, but it looks full and it’s huge, so it should be heavy. Put a sandbag in it.’ Those are the things that I think about.”

But while the Gilmore Girls fandom loves to examine the minutiae of every line, scene and episode—including the famously emphatic way Jesse asked Rory, “Why did you drop out of Yale?”—it’s the more general memories that fill him up like a cup of coffee.

“The episodes are for an audience to pick apart, dissect,” he added. “For us to make it, [it’s] the experience of making it.”

And loving it the way he do, Ventimiglia is ready to go to the ends of the earth to recall every beautiful moment for the upcoming HBO Max documentary about the Amy Sherman-Palladino show.

“I think the world of Amy, and Lauren [Graham],” he said, adding of the doc, “I have no idea what we’re going to talk about. It was 26 years ago that I made the show.”

He added, “It’s funny: I’ll have people run up to me and be like, ‘Hey, do you remember that time on Gilmore when you said this?’ I’m like, ‘That was 26 years ago.’ I’m like, ‘Tell me something about your life from 26 years ago.’ They’re like, ‘I wasn’t born.’”
