4 bold predictions for Chargers in Week 11 vs. Jets

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Chargers will look to build on their momentum. After recording their first win of the season six days ago against the Ravens, the Bolts will look to stay in the win column.

The Chargers will host the Jets in Week 11. The Jets are in a lull of their own, having lost five straight games.

Combined, the Chargers and Jets are 3-16. These two teams haven't played their best football for most of the season, but Los Angeles hopes its win last Monday marks a turning point.

In the meantime, here are four bold predictions for the Chargers against the Jets.

The Chargers made the mistake of waiting this long to give Omarion Hampton the touches he deserves. I don't think they'll backtrack, but rather double down. Hampton reached 100+ yards on the ground last week, and I think he will do it again against a Jets rushing defense ranked third-to-last in rushing yards allowed.

It was a mistake-free game for Justin Herbert against the Ravens; however, he didn't record a touchdown. Herbert attempted only 18 passes and completed 12. While I believe the Chargers will stick to the run game, Herbert will record two touchdown passes against a Jets defense ranking 27th in opponent touchdowns per game.

Dalvin Tomlinson gets to Geno Smith twice, like he did to Jacoby Brissett on Monday. The Jets are ranked 12th in opponent quarterback sack percentage at 7.32. Tomlinson has a chance for another big game.

The Chargers will build on the momentum from Monday night. One win is all it takes to turn the tide, and I think Monday night's win propelled them in the right direction. Los Angeles has a great chance to prove that victory wasn't a one-week fluke.
