Cardinals can become first team mathematically eliminated from the playoffs on Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

We all know the Cardinals are not making the playoffs this year. As soon as Sunday, that can be a mathematical certainty.

The Cardinals can be mathematically eliminated from playoff contention this week, if they lose and the right combination of other games results in NFC teams winning or tying.

The exact combination of results necessary probably won’t happen. It would require some unlikely results, including a tie in either the Bears-Lions or Packers-Rams game. But it is possible.

Cardinals coach Mike LaFleur said this week that he knows his team won’t win the Super Bowl this year. That’s obvious, and it will soon be official.
