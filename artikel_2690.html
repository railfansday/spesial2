Michael Pittman Jr. on track to play in Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Pittsburgh Steelers WR Michael Pittman Jr. (foot) fully participated in practice Thursday, Nov. 26, and is not on the injury report for Week 12.

Why It Matters: Pittman Jr. has activated from the Injured Reserve list and will be available to play in Week 12. He should see a handful of targets during the game and can be considered a No. 4 or No. 5 receiver in fantasy leagues.
