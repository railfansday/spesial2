New Saints go to Haverfordwest in Welsh Cup

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Cymru Premier leaders The New Saints will go to top flight rivals Haverfordwest County in the fourth round in December.

In more top-flight encounters, Cambrian United head to holders Caernarfon Town, Barry Town United host Connah's Quay Nomads and Trefelin travel to Cardiff Met.

Tier four sides Holyhead Hotspur and Rockspur Fords are the lowest-ranked teams still left in the competition.

But their rewards for reaching the fourth round are away trips to Cymru Premier opponents. Hotspur will journey to Penybont while Rockspur Fords will venture to Llandudno.

Meanwhile another top tier side, Colwyn Bay, will welcome Cardiff side Caerau Ely hoping to avoid an upset.

Ties to be played on the weekend of 11-13 December.
