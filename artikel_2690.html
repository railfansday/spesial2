'Sad to see:' Steelers players, NFL world weigh in on Aaron Rodgers breakup

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Aaron Rodgers has long had a cannon for an arm. As it turns out, he also has a flamethrower for a mouth.

On Wednesday, the Rodgers-Pittsburgh Steelers era ended after the quarterback was released following his unceremonious benching on Nov. 24. Rodgers not only lost his starting gig, but also an active roster spot, a day after the team announced the passer would be inactive for the Week 12 Black Friday game vs. the Denver Broncos.

Prior to his release, Rodgers reaction to the benching wasn't the typical PR-talk reserved for veterans. Instead, the passer aired dirty laundry of the Steelers' inner workings underneath head coach Mike McCarthy on the Nov. 25 edition of the "Pat McAfee Show," highlighted by the fact that McCarthy would allegedly fall asleep in meetings or fail to attend them altogether.

Rodgers' release and subsequent accusations – which the quarterback says can be corroborated by a number of players on the team – drew plenty of reaction, skepticism and some verification from plenty around the NFL sphere.

Drew Allar, the rookie fourth-round pick of the Steelers, kept it amicable when discussing Rodgers, deciding not to weigh in on the release, instead wishing the passer the best in his future endeavors.

"For me, it's kind of sad to see, because I have a lot of respect for Aaron," Allar told reporters on Wednesday. "I've learned a lot from him since he's been here. I think, again, I'm just gonna leave it at that."

The tight end, who Rodgers says broke the news of his benching and deactivation to him on Wednesday, tried his best to keep questions related to the Steelers' upcoming matchup vs. the Denver Broncos on Black Friday.

"Like I said, I'm just gonna focus on answering questions on the Broncos and the game on Friday, I really have nothing to add," Freiermuth said when questioned about McCarthy's sleeping habits.

When asked about his conversation with Rodgers on the evening of Nov. 24, he added: "I was just checking in on a close friend from what I heard. That's all I have to say about that."
