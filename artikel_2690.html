‘I am…’: Luka Doncic explains what has changed since he stopped playing with LeBron James

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Luka Doncic became the undisputed star of the Los Angeles Lakers following LeBron James' departure in the summer.

The two superstars played together for a season and a half, and although there were some great signs, they never quite lived up to all the hype.

James left in the summer, and since then, Doncic has exploded into the best player in the NBA and the favourite to win the MVP award.

On paper, a James-Doncic duo felt like an unstoppable force.

Both players are world-class at what they do, and along with Austin Reaves, they did impress for a brief period with the Los Angeles Lakers.

However, the general opinion is that the partnership failed, and seeing Doncic perform brilliantly now has made many, like Skip Bayless, claim that the Lakers do not miss James one bit.

But what has changed for the Slovenian? 'Not much', he says, although he admitted that he was probably 'more open' when they played together compared to now.

He said in his press conference, as per Duane Rankin on X: "I just try to play my own basketball. I don't think there are that many differences.

"Obviously, with LeBron, you can learn a lot of things. I am probably more open then than now, but just trying to play my game."

Doncic may not be as open anymore, but his numbers tell a whole different story.

The 27-year-old scored 43 points against the Phoenix Suns while recording nine assists and three rebounds on Sunday.

That takes his season averages to 39 points, 7.2 rebounds and 7.8 assists per game so far. Those are incredible numbers, and it is no surprise that he is the favourite for the MVP award.
