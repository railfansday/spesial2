Kovač backs Arsenal loanee Nwaneri after impressive England display amid “fierce competition” at Dortmund

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Ethan Nwaneri impressed in England U21's 3-2 friendly victory over France earlier this week – and his performance did not go unnoticed by Borussia Dortmund head coach Niko Kovač.

"We're delighted about that. It was a friendly in which he did really well. And that's why we signed him," Kovač said at a press conference on Thursday, speaking about the Arsenal loanee.

After initially enjoying regular playing time following his summer loan move from Arsenal, Nwaneri has seen his minutes dwindle in recent weeks. The 19-year-old managed just five minutes of action across Dortmund's final three matches before the international break.

As Kovač explained on Thursday, competition for places in attack is fierce. Nevertheless, the Dortmund boss is confident that Nwaneri will get plenty more opportunities during the remainder of the season.

"We have some very good players in those attacking positions. Kosta [Karetsas] has also returned now. The competition is fierce. He (Nwaneri) has played a lot of games and, at just 19 years of age, he will certainly play many more games for us," Kovač said.

Nwaneri could get another opportunity to impress when Borussia Dortmund travel to face Borussia Mönchengladbach in the Bundesliga on Saturday.
