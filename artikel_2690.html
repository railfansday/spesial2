It’s time for Basketball: San Antonio Spurs vs Los Angeles Lakers

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Welcome to the Game Thread. Veterans of the Game Thread know how we do things around here, but for all you newbies we have a few rules. Our community guidelines apply and basically say be cool, no personal attacks, don't troll and don't swear too much.

The Spurs lost their first road game and NBA Cup game of the season on Wednesday night, in a 111-106 thriller in front of an enthusiastic Moda Center crowd as both Dame and Deni Avdija went nuclear and out-executed the Silver and Black down the final stretch. Some missed free throws, a lane violation and an 8 second violation were costly as the Spurs ran the gamut of rarely enforced reasons to lose points at a key juncture of the game. The Spurs missed having De'Aaron Fox available, as his quickness and steady administration of the offensive sets was missed, especially in crunch time. Dylan Harper's absence was apparent, too, with the Spurs were missing his ability to get points even when the offense was not creating any space. The Spurs became too reliant on three point shooting, which isn't good for them, because only Champagnie is a good three-point shooter among the rotation players, and everybody in the Moda Center knew that, especially the Trail Blazer defenders. The good news is that Fox should be back in tonight's lineup. The better news is that Harper is listed as questionable on the injury report, which means that he could return to play tonight, just 10 days since fell on the court and injured his hip with less than a minute left in the Pistons game. Harper's injury was initially estimated to keep him out from 1-3 weeks (or 2-3 weeks, depending on where you read the news) and he's definitely on the lower end of that estimate.

Tonight, they face the Los Angeles Lakers in their final game of the Emirates Cup group stage. The math is pretty simple. In order to advance to the knockout rounds, they will have to beat the Lakers tonight. Because the Trail Blazers won on Wednesday, they will win the group and automatically advance if they win tonight's game against the Warriors, which tips off at the same time as the Lakers/Spurs game. In that case, a win for the Silver and Black would send them to the quarterfinals as the group winners, with a home game on December 4 or 5. If the Blazers win tonight, then the Spurs would probably advance to the semis as the Western Conference wild card, because they would have the best point differential among the second place finishers, barring some unlikely scenarios like the Grizzlies defeating the Timberwolves by 40+ or the Nuggets routing the Jazz by 50+ [OK, the second one could happen]. If the Spurs are the wild card, they will play on the road against the top team in the west, which would probably be the Wolves or Mavericks.

Is that all clear? Probably not. But if the Spurs lose tonight, it's even simpler. They will join the also-rans and have two more games added to the schedule in the second week of December against other non-qualifying teams. Although it's probably marginally better for the Spurs overall record, having two games against mediocre opponents added to the schedule than having to play two teams against top four teams in the conference, it would be a disappointment to a team that's looking to prove that they're ready to compete for the NBA title again this season. Last year, the top two teams in the NBA Cup were the teams in the NBA Finals, and that could happen again this season.

A lot of times, you can refer to the best player on the opponents' team as "the head of the snake." That's not exactly true of Luka Doncic. He's not the head of the snake, he's the whole damn snake. His usage rate is insanely high, and the rest of the team is pretty much relegated to watching him work on the offensive end. If the Spurs can force him to give up the ball early, and make the Lakers run the offense through someone else, especially if it's not Austin Reaves [questionable with a bruised heel], then the Spurs can hold down their scoring. The Lakers defense is decent, but the Spurs offense will be fine if they move the ball and don't just settle for three point shots. Let's GO SPURS!! Castle must dominate Luka.
