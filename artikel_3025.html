Resideo Technologies, Inc. (REZI -2.99%) completed its transition to a stand-alone building technologies company following the official spin-off of its distribution business. Management reported that total revenue growth was driven by volume and pricing power across core sales channels despite a challenging housing market. The company is shifting its strategic focus away from lower-margin outside design manufacturing and toward its own branded security and comfort products. Operational initiatives, including manufacturing site consolidations and SKU rationalization, are intended to reduce the cost structure and improve utilization across the global footprint.
