U2’s ‘Carnaval De Luz’ Becomes Its 12th U.K. No. 1 Album, Moving Band Up All-Time Leaderboard

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

It's the group's first No. 1 album of original material in 17 years.

U2 is No. 1 on the U.K.’s Official Albums Chart published Friday (Nov. 20) for a 12th time, this go-round with Carnaval De Luz. With this latest achievement, the band is third on the all-time rankings for groups.

The Irish group is now tied with Bruce Springsteen on the all-time list with 12 each, trailing only Elvis Presley (13), Madonna (13), Taylor Swift (14), The Rolling Stones (15), The Beatles (15) and Robbie Williams (16).

U2’s first No. 1 came in 1983 with third LP Boy, followed by The Unforgettable Fire (1984), The Joshua Tree (1987), Rattle and Hum (1987), Zooropa (1993), Pop (1997), The Best of 1980-1990 (1998), All That You Can’t Leave Behind (2000), How to Dismantle an Atomic Bomb (2004), No Line on the Horizon (2009) and Songs of Surrender (2023).

Carnaval De Luz includes “Torn”, a duet with Dolly Parton and the final vocals she recorded before her passing in August. The LP outsold the rest of the top five combined.

Olivia Rodrigo’s you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love lifts one spot to No. 2, and Noah Kahan’s The Great Divide rises nine places to No. 3 as his U.K. arena tour dates begin. Olivia Dean’s The Art of Loving closes at No. 4, followed by Michael Jackson’s The Essentials greatest hits collection at No. 5.

Becky Hill’s third full-length, Rebecca, lands at No. 6 to give the dance act her third top 10 album; her peak came in 2024 with Believe Me Now? (No. 3)

Jake Bugg scores his sixth top 10 album with Alamo (No. 8), joining his self-titled debut (2012), Shangri La (2013), On My One (2016) Hearts That Strain (2017) and Saturday Night, Sunday Morning (2021).
