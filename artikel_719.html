Doja Cat Blasts Rockstar Games for Leaving Her Music Out of ‘GTA 6’: ‘I’m So F–kin Bitter’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Grand Theft Auto VI arrived on Nov. 19, with the album hitting streaming services the next day.

Doja Cat was looking forward to a call from Rockstar Games, but the phone never rang. The Grammy-winning rapper vented on X on Thursday (Nov. 19) as Grand Theft Auto VI hysteria was sweeping the globe. The reason? Doja was frustrated she didn’t get to be a part of the iconic video-game series.

“I would’ve loved to have my music on gta6 i’m so f–kin bitter,” she wrote, before adding in a second post: “@RockstarGames eat my d–k all yall i love your stupid f–kin b—h a– game.”

Doja continued: “@RockstarGames you broke my heart pinche culero.”

Billboard has reached out to Rockstar Games for comment.

Fans pointed out that music was later added in updates online to Grand Theft Auto V, so not all hope is lost for Doja to make an appearance in GTA 6. “You actually a gamer too why i feel bad,” one Doja fan replied, pointing to the 31-year-old’s avid gaming history and GTA fandom.

Rockstar didn’t hold back when it came to the music budget. It recruited Morgan Wallen, Lana Del Rey, Bad Bunny, Burna Boy, Trina, Trick Daddy and PinkPantheress to helm radio stations in the video game.

There is also an original soundtrack album for Grand Theft Auto VI set to arrive on Friday (Nov. 20), which is loaded with 34 tracks and features the likes of Dua Lipa, Playboi Carti, Don Toliver, Travis Scott, Cardi B and others. Justice also created the score for the game.

Doja Cat is currently on the North American leg of her Tour Ma Vie. The trek continues with upcoming shows in Baltimore, D.C., Boston, Toronto, Montreal and Philadelphia, and will wrap up with a final date in New York City on Dec. 1.
