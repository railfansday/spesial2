Larne beat Glens to reach BetMcLean Cup quarter-finals

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Larne moved into the quarter-finals of the BetMcLean Cup as they triumphed 3-2 against Glentoran at the Oval.

Holders Linfield also booked their place in the next round with a narrow 1-0 victory over Championship side H&W Welders at the Blanchflower Stadium.

Adam Brooks scored a hat-trick as Coleraine thrashed Dungannon Swifts 5-1 at Stangmore Park while Ballymena United, Cliftonville and Limavady United also progressed.

Championship side Queen's University are through to the last eight after they beat Loughgall.

The game between Carrick Rangers and Glenavon at Taylors Avenue has gone to extra time.

Glentoran went ahead on 14 minutes when Nathan Shaw's deep free-kick to the back post was knocked back into the danger area by Zeno Rossi and Pat Hoban was ideally placed to stab the ball home from eight yards.

Larne levelled nine minutes before the interval when Matt Ridley powered home a header from a Sean Graham corner via the underside of the crossbar.

Larne went in front just after the hour when Kevin O'Hara cut the ball back from the right and the ball slipped under defender Daniel Larmour's foot with Greg Sandiford alert to slide home from close range.

But Glentoran restored parity on 71 minutes when James Douglas crossed from the right and substitute Cal Weatherup directed an excellent header beyond the reach of Carney.

Larne had the final say, however, with eight minutes left when James Simpson's crossed looper over the head of goalkeeper Crellin and Paul O'Neill was on hand to head home from virtually on the goal-line.

Adam Frizzell's first-half penalty was enough to send Linfield through, with the spot kick awarded on 18 minutes after Matthew Fitzpatrick was fouled in the box.

At Stangmore Park, Brooks scored his first as he rifled a shot past Declan Dunne after getting on the end of a long ball over the top.

Senan Devine then fired home the rebound after Brooks saw his initial shot blocked, before Junior pulled one back for the Swifts before the interval.

After half-time, Joe Thomson restored Coleraine's two-goal cushion with a header from Devine's free-kick.

Brooks seized on a loose ball and drilled past Dunne with 10 minutes remaining and then executed a fantastic finish from Joel Cooper's exquisite pass for his third of the game.

At Mill Meadow, Ballymena eased past Moyola Park 3-0.

Chris McKee scored from the penalty spot after Igor Rutkowski had been fouled and the latter added his side's second before the break when he turned home Charlie Allen's low cross into the box.

Finley Thorndike netted the third in the second half when the ball ricocheted into his path and he dinked home.

In-form Cliftonville also defeated Championship opposition to progress as they saw off Warrenpoint Town 2-0 at Milltown.

Joe Gormley broke the deadlock on 53 minutes as he pounced to score from close range while Liam McStravick added a second with a strike on 67 minutes.

Limavady United were always in control against Rathfriland Rangers and a remarkable four first-half goals from Oisin Duffy gave them a 4-0 home victory.

The hosts had goalkeeper Fintan Doherty sent-off in the second half.

Queen's University picked up a 2-0 win over Loughgall courtesy of goals from Jamie Glover and Caoimhin Bonnar.
