Josh Allen's 10th rushing TD of season puts Bills up 7-0

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Bills quarterback Josh Allen has given his team an early lead at MetLife Stadium.

Allen pushed his way into the end zone from the 1-yard-line to put the Bills up 7-0 with 2:49 to play in the first quarter. The touchdown is Allen's 10th rushing score of the season and the 89th rushing touchdown of his career.

Allen has also converted a pair of fourth downs with runs, although one of them came on Buffalo's opening possession and they wound up punting the ball a few plays later.

The Jets went three-and-out on their first possession. Wide receiver Adonai Mitchell created a lot of separation from Bills corner Christian Benford on third down, but Geno Smith underthrew Mitchell for an incompletion. They also saw wide receiver Omar Cooper leave with an ankle injury and he is called questionable to return.
