Former Barcelona forward urges patience with Real Madrid&#8217;s South American stars

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Vitor Roque has been enjoying life ever since his return to Brazil. Since joining Palmeiras in 2025, the forward has racked up 29 goals, and his market value has also gone up.

This comes on the back of a disastrous spell at Barcelona, where the player struggled to prove himself and eventually left the club permanently in 2025.

In light of his success, the former Barcelona forward was recently asked about the lack of patience from European clubs when it comes to talents from South America.

Roque believes players like Endrick need more time. (Photo by Juan Manuel Serrano Arce/Getty Images)

When asked about the South American talents, the interviewer particularly referenced two Real Madrid players: Endrick and Franco Mastantuono.

Both players had joined Real Madrid in the recent past. While Mastantuono had to settle for a loan at Fiorentina, Endrick is also struggling with a lack of game time despite being a part of Jose Mourinho's setup this season.

Keeping that in mind, Roque urged more patience with these players.

"People believe that young people arrive and we have to start playing 100%, giving everything, doing our best football, but it is not. It takes patience. Sometimes we don't have the adaptation time we need," he said (h/t Diario AS).

The former Barcelona forward pointed out how language and culture can also be a problem for young South American stars.

"We are very young and speaking a new language is very difficult. Sometimes it is important to have the support of the coach and I when I went to Barcelona I did not have it. They barely talked to me, you know?

"So I think you have to keep that in mind and a lot of people don't see it that way, they think you have to be prepared from the first minute," he added.

Mastantuono's fate will be decided at the end of the season. (Photo by Gabriele Maltinti/Getty Images)

Fortunately, it appears Real Madrid are ready to bide their time with both Endrick and Mastantuono.

Especially in the case of Endrick, the club have not shown any indication of permanently parting ways with the forward. They are, in fact, ready to block a January exit for the Brazilian international, who is rated highly by coach Mourinho.

As for Mastantuono, the Argentine has shown glimpses of his talents at Fiorentina. But unless he breaks out in a big way like Nico Paz, it is unlikely Real Madrid will want to make him a part of the first-team setup next season.
