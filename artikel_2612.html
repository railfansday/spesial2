Where to watch Rams vs. Packers today: Channel, live stream, time, schedule for NFL Thanksgiving Eve game

Is Thanksgiving Eve a real holiday? In the NFL's eyes, it is.

The league is debuting a Thanksgiving Eve game for the first time this season, getting the holiday actin started early with a highly-anticipated battle between the Rams and Packers in Los Angeles.

The race for the NFC's final playoff spot could very well come down to these two teams. Both got off to slow starts and have recovered well, but each has landmines on their schedule. The Rams face the Chiefs and 49ers in consecutive weeks after this battle, and they will have to do so without Puka Nacua.

The Packers, meanwhile, have no room for error as they sit last in the NFC North with more divisional games ahead.

Here's your guide to watching the Thanksgiving Eve matchup between the Rams and Packers.

Rams vs. Packers can be live streamed exclusively on Netflix and will not air on traditional television outside of local markets.

Rams vs. Packers will kick off at 8 p.m. ET (5 p.m. local time) from SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles on Wednesday.
