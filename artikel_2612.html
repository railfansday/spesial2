UFC 334 Post-Fight Press Conference

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Following tonight's UFC 334 at Madison Square Garden in New York, the winners will answer questions during the post-fight press conference.

The fight card featured two world title bouts. A heavyweight title bout between champion Ciryl Gane and undefeated Josh Hokit headlined the event. In the co-main event, bantamweight champion Kayla Harrison faced former two-division titleholder Amanda Nunes.
