Blake Corum workout video goes viral after destroying Packers in crucial Rams win

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

For the second week in a row, the Los Angeles Rams were able to count on Blake Corum.

And if you paid attention to what the Rams' third-year running back was doing during the offseason, you wouldn't have been shocked.

Blake Corum's offseason workout video goes viral after dominant performance against the Packers on Wednesday night. X / @barstoolsports

Blake Corum running past a group of Packers defenders in Wednesday night's game. Getty Images

After the Rams' 26-13 victory against the Packers on Wednesday night, Barstool Sports posted one of Corum's workout videos from over the offseason on X, where it showed him moving as fast as lightning in the gym.

Corum was seen doing multiple workouts that incorporated weightlifting and plyometrics. In addition to doing agility workouts, he also did slower workouts that focused on keeping himself balanced.

Rams running back Blake Corum running past a Packers defender. AP Photo/Ryan Sun

Within 30 minutes after it was posted, the video garnered over 52,500 views with 15 comments, 13 reposts, 330 likes, and 34 saves.

NFL fans were seen gathering in the comment section of the video and were praising the running back.

@on_brando 20 posted a GIF of Terrell Owens' iconic "That's my quarterback" clip and captioned his post "That's my fantasy rb."

Corum, who is a former Michigan standout and won a National Championship, received some love from the Wolverines fan base, as @MrDrunkBear posted a GIF of him and J.J. McCarthy after he scored a touchdown and captioned the post "Michigan Man

Corum had a career-high running the ball 32 times for 184 yards, averaging 5.8 yards per play with one rushing touchdown.

Blake Corum falling short of scoring a rushing touchdown against the Packers. Getty Images

Corum's performance happened on the same evening of his birthday, and it was the fifth consecutive game he's scored a rushing touchdown.

According to Sara Barshop of ESPN, Corum's performance was the first time a Rams running back had 30 carries in a game since Todd Gurley did so in his rookie season in 2015 — when the team was still located in St. Louis.

With the Rams' victory against the Packers, head coach Sean McVay also accomplished a rare feat, which was beating Packers head coach Matt LaFleur.

McVay was 0-5 against LaFleur in his career, and was 0-6 against the family, as the Rams came up short against the Arizona Cardinals [who are coached by Matt's brother, Mike LaFleur] in Week 6 of the season.

The last time the Rams took the field, was Week 10 on the road against the Cardinals, and it was a breakout game for Corum, having 17 carries for 100 yards and a touchdown.
