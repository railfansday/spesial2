Juwan Johnson remains sidelined Thursday

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

New Orleans Saints TE Juwan Johnson (hamstring, wrist) did not participate in practice Thursday, Nov. 26.

Why It Matters: Johnson's status for Week 12 is uncertain after missing the first two practices of the week, so fantasy players can monitor the situation over the next few days. He will see a few targets if he is cleared to play and can be a No. 2 tight end in fantasy leagues.
