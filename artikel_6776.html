Gracie Abrams Channels <i><em>Romeo and Juliet</em></i> With Armor, Angel Wings, and Tons of Chanel

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

She put a high-fashion spin on the ’90s looks

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Yesterday evening, as people everywhere traded their typical night-out fits for spooky, theatrical costumes, Gracie Abrams dressed up for a special Halloween edition of Saturday Night Live. Serving as the musical guest for the October 31 installment, which saw Inde Navarrette as the host, the “That’s So True” star took the stage in a handful of on-theme looks.

For her two-song set, in which she performed the lead single from Daughter From Hell, “Hit the Wall,” and gave her latest release, “Here You Come, There You Go,” its live debut, the singer pulled inspiration from Baz Luhrmann’s Romeo and Juliet (1996). More specifically, she looked to the pivotal Capulet mansion costume party, where the young lovers meet and fall madly in love at first sight.

The film, which helped launch the careers of leads Leonardo DiCaprio and Claire Danes, was defined by its vibrant, modern take on the classic text, combining traditional Shakespearean language with contemporary music, fast-paced editing, markers of youth culture, and custom looks from Prada and Dolce and Gabbana. Its most famous scene saw Romeo dressed as a prince in shining armor and Juliet as an ethereal angel.

Putting her own spin on the two iconic looks, Abrams took the stage as Luhrmann’s Romeo, wearing armor layered over a ’90s Hawaiian shirt. Second, she gave Danes’s angelic Juliet a modern high-fashion update, turning to Matthieu Blazy’s Chanel for a long white silk dress. Pulled from the 2026 Métiers d’Art collection, the piece took over 600 hours to complete, and featured a low scoop neckline, delicate pleating, and intricate embroidery—with 30,000 glass beads, 2,000 crystals, and 33,000 silver sequins. The musical guest paired the piece with angel wings, of course.

Later, for the after party, she slipped into a lacy white maxi dress and slathered on the fake blood, turning into a gory vampire.
