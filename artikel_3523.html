Inside Chris Brown and Usher's 'Tension' on Tour: 'Egos Started to Show'

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The R&B Tour has been anything but smooth sailing for Chris Brown and Usher behind the scenes.

“There has been tension between Chris and Usher throughout the tour,” a source exclusively tells Us. “Their teams tried to keep it under lock and key, but everyone knew it was only a matter of time until it became public.”

Brown, 37, and Usher, 48, kicked off The R&B Tour at Empower Field at Mile High in Denver on June 26. The tour — featuring supporting acts Tank, Mario and Eric Bellinger — was initially considered a success, expanding from 33 to 59 dates due to fan demand and grossing $77.4 million in September alone, according to Billboard Boxscore.

However, rumors of a feud between Brown and Usher quickly spread on social media after they began their November 5 show at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta approximately two and a half hours late. TMZ subsequently reported that the coheadliners had gotten into an argument backstage, delaying their usual start time for their 50-plus-song set. Brown and Usher have since canceled concerts at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles on November 16 and Hard Rock Stadium in Miami on December 6 without offering an explanation to ticket holders.

“Chris and Usher have worked together a few times over the years, but they never had a genuine brothership beyond that,” the insider explains to Us. “It was more of a professional respect for each other’s artistry than anything else. Planning and executing this tour forced them to spend more time together, and eventually their egos started to show and they clashed.”

Despite their lingering beef and two scrapped performances, Brown and Usher appear to be forging on with the remainder of The R&B Tour, which is currently scheduled to conclude with back-to-back nights at Raymond James Stadium in Tampa, Florida, on December 11 and 2. In fact, the “New Flame” collaborators have continued to put on a united front on stage, even hugging it out during a separate Atlanta concert on November 10.

“One thing they can agree on is that they want to finish out the tour because the fan response has been amazing and they still have a few surprises up their sleeves that have been in the works for a while,” the source tells Us. “Their teams remain hopeful that can happen, but it won’t be easy.”

Us has reached out to reps for Brown and Usher for comment.
