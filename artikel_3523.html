Pittsburgh Steelers Announce Dramatic Aaron Rodgers News Amid Struggles

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Aaron Rodgers will be spending Black Friday on the bench.

The Pittsburgh Steelers announced that Rodgers, 42, will be “inactive” against the Denver Broncos on Friday, November 27, with backup quarterback Mason Rudolph taking his spot.

The surprising move comes after two straight losses for the Steelers, both with Rodgers behind center. Pittsburgh lost in disappointing fashion to the Philadelphia Eagles, 19-16, on Sunday, November 22. Rodgers finished 30-for-44 passing with 227 yards and one touchdown.

Pittsburgh is now 6-4 on the season tied for second place in the AFC North with the Cleveland Browns. Both teams are one game behind the first-place Cincinnati Bengals.

After the loss to the Eagles, head coach Mike McCarthy scoffed at the idea of demoting Rodgers.

“Aaron Rodgers is our quarterback,” McCarthy told reporters on Monday, November 23.

However, ESPN reported on Wednesday, November 25, that the move to bench Rodgers was a coach’s decision.

Sources told NFL Network’s Ian Rapoport that Rodgers was not in attendance at the Steelers’ 8 a.m. meeting Wednesday, and it is not clear whether he will attend Wednesday’s practice at noon.

“The Steelers essentially believing that Mason Rudolph gives them the best chance to win,” Rapoport, 46, said on Wednesday. “So the big question is: What happened? Here’s my understanding of where the Steelers have been the last couple of days. They’ve had meetings — offensive meetings, staff meetings, meetings with players, meetings with Aaron Rodgers, among others — trying to figure out the best way forward, trying to figure out the issues with the offense.”

ESPN’s Steelers reporter, Brooke Pryor, called the decision to bench Rodgers “a stunning reversal.”

“Everything on Tuesday was business as usual,” Pryor, 35, explained on Wednesday. “I was in the building. They had a walk-through. It was only an hour long. If they had known at that point that they were definitively going with Mason Rudolph, safe to say that practice would have been longer than just an hour.”

Pryor said Rodgers “gave no indication that this was coming down the pipe.”

“What I can tell you is, as I was leaving the locker room at about 4:15, I saw Rodgers, Mason Rudolph and [quarterback] Will Howard sitting together in front of Mason Rudolph’s locker,” Pryor explained. “They were having a conversation. … It did feel notable because the rest of the locker room had cleared out.”

Rudolph, 31, is currently playing in his seventh NFL season, six of those with the Steelers. He has appeared in four games this season.

With Rodgers previously confirming this will be his last season, there’s a strong possibility he’s played in his final NFL game.
