Isaiah Washington Reveals He's Dating Someone New After Wife's Death

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Grey’s Anatomy alum Isaiah Washington has found love again after his wife’s sudden death.

“I have known many incredible women in my life, but not a single one of them can hold a candle to the deep Southern charm of @keibenet,” Washington, 63, wrote via Instagram on Monday, November 16.

Washington reflected on how he met Kei Benett, adding, “I am not only thrilled to have gotten to know her in the last 9 months talking to her for hours every single day. I am absolutely baffled how any man in her past could have harmed her, belittled her, disrespected her, or even betrayed her in the most deplorable ways.”

In his social media post, Washington gushed over their connection.

“She has an incredible mind, heart, and spirit and I have grown to respect her intelligence, green thumb, hair stylist talent, entrepreneurship, holistic approach, and her black business owner’s business acumen immensely,” he wrote. “Yes, she has earned her place at my creative table and I love her dearly. I am so glad that all of the men who treated her like she was less than a Queen, all left her heartbroken, but not broken.”

He concluded: “Don’t worry I will take it from here and I will never let her down as a friend and partner in love and creativity. There has been no other woman who has made me laugh and has encouraged me as she has. Yeah, I don’t play about Kei Bennett, and just know that she doesn’t play about me. Yeah … she is all mine now and I am all hers. Deal with it. Expect great things to come from both of us soon! Keep Watching.”

Washington’s insight into his dating love comes one month after he confirmed his wife, Jenisa Marie Garland, died suddenly last year.

“Yes … the rumors are true. My dear wife of 29 years collapsed suddenly on May 31, 2025 and passed away on June 9, 2025,” he wrote via Instagram in October. “It came as a huge shock to me and our entire family. She succumbed to a massive hemorrhagic stroke and never recovered.”

The actor mourned the loss of his life partner, writing, “Listen, ya’ll … some headaches are not just headaches or migraines, they can be much worse and a warning sign. Get a brain scan if you can and take those headaches seriously.”

He added: “She has been gone 13 months now, but she left 3 incredibly amazing children behind to continue her legacy. I don’t know if this is an appropriate post, because Jenisa Washington was extremely private, but I guess I just have to be reprimanded from her on the other side.”

Washington was briefly married and divorced at 21 before being introduced to Garland in 1995. The pair exchanged vows one year later. They went on to welcome three children: sons Isaiah and Tyme and daughter Iman.

“For me, being married and having children did change a lot of my trajectory,” Washington told Fox News Digital in 2022 about how his professional life took a back seat to his family. “That’s why I didn’t want to get married 25 years ago, but I’m glad I did because the wife and children really make you the best human being that you can be.”
