World of Warcraft Forever release time in your time zone

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

22 years later, it's time to start WoW again

The release of World of Warcraft: Forever brings a third — or, depending on how you count them, a sixth or seventh — way to play World of Warcraft to everyone with a WoW subscription. Joining the modern game and the Classic servers, Forever is based on WoW as it was at launch in 2004, but with new content, rebalanced classes, graphical enhancements, and lots of modernization.

You'll be able to delve into WoW as it felt like 22 years ago, but never actually was, this week on Nov. 4 (or Nov. 5, depending on your time zone). Here's when World of Warcraft: Forever releases in your time zone.

WoW: Forever is a kind of retro remix of WoW, based on the content and design philosophy of the classic game but with some new stuff, some modernizations, and some changes.

The core of Forever is the original nine character classes and eight playable races, split into two warring factions who can never communicate with each other or group up together: The Horde and Alliance. All the adventuring zones, dungeons, raids, and cities of the original game are present across its two massive continents.

Access to WoW: Forever is included in a World of Warcraft subscription, but the Skyborne (and access to their starting zone) are sold separately.
