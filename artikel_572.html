Fubo Plans Now Include Free Disney+, Hulu, ESPN Unlimited and Fox One

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, The Hollywood Reporter may receive an affiliate commission.

In an effort to combat industry-wide streaming inflation, Fubo has introduced a deal-savvy way to access additional services for your same dollar — four more services, to be exact.

Following the standard free trial, Fubo Pro has a promotional rate of $63.99 for the first month, then costs $88.99 monthly thereafter, while Fubo Elite is $68.99 for the first month, then $98.99 monthly thereafter. The former features 201 channels, while the latter includes 265 channels and 4K.

Visit fubo.tv to further compare plans and sign up for the free trial period.
