Daniel Farke needs to keep a close eye on Mateus Fernandes ahead of Leeds United's upcoming clash with Tottenham Hotspur.

The two teams have experienced very different starts to the 2026-27 campaign, and their forms have completely flipped in recent weeks.

While Spurs are unbeaten in four matches, moving to ninth in the Premier League table, the Whites have fallen to 12th with no wins in five.

Some fans have even suggested a turnaround on Daniel Farke's new contract, but the German has more than enough success in the bank.

Dominic Calvert-Lewin's injury is a major concern ahead of Saturday, 7 November, especially when goals have been hard to come by at Elland Road.

There's one player who has yet to face Leeds in a Tottenham kit, but based on his previous performances, he has some kind of personal vendetta against the club.

And that player would be Mateus Fernandes, who has come alive under Roberto De Zerbi in recent weeks.

Initially, he struggled to break into the team. Now, he's one of the most undroppable players in the lineup, and deserves to start again.

Looking at his stats against the Whites, De Zerbi would be foolish to drop him either way.

While Fernandes has been on the losing side twice in three games against Leeds, he loves to cause havoc in the final third.

For West Ham United, he notched two goals and an assist, with both goals coming in the 90th minute or later, and his assist coming in the Hammers' most recent win against them.

In each of the last three meetings between Leeds and West Ham, Ethan Ampadu and Ao Tanaka made up at least 66 per cent of the midfield.

Sean Longstaff and Brenden Aaronson made up the midfield trio in two of the games, while Farke deployed just a midfield duo in the other.

It was the introduction of Aaronson when West Ham claimed victory. Calvert-Lewin was joined by Lukas Nmecha inthe final third as well.

The Leeds manager has plenty of thinking to do, but stopping Fernandes needs to be top of his list, especially given his recent form.
