Inkera Brings Full-Color 3D Printing to the Desktop With the C1

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Newcomer debuts at Formnext 2026 with a UV material-jetting printer offering more than 10 million colors

FRANKFURT, Germany, Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Inkera, a new name in full-color additive manufacturing, will introduce its first product at Formnext 2026 in Frankfurt. The Inkera C1, a desktop full-color UV 3D printer, will be shown in Hall 12.1, Booth B11.

The C1 is built to turn digital ideas into vivid physical objects, including personalized figurines, character models, creative gifts, classroom projects, product concepts and small-batch prototypes.
