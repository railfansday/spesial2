Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO after affairs and spy scandal

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The move capped a remarkable fall from power for an executive who had been one of the faces of the generative artificial intelligence boom

Jensen Huang has resigned as CEO of Nvidia $NVDA, the company said Thursday, capping a remarkable fall from power for an executive who had been one of the faces of the generative artificial intelligence boom. CFO Colette Kress will serve as interim CEO, the company said, after revelations of multiple extramarital affairs by Huang, including one with a suspected Chinese spy. Huang also left the board, effective immediately, Nvidia said.

Nvidia’s board appointed Harvey C. Jones, a former CEO and executive chairman of Synopsys $SNPS, as its first-ever chair. The board is starting a search for a permanent CEO and will consider internal and external candidates. Kress will keep her CFO role while she serves as interim CEO.

"The Board has full confidence in Colette and the leadership team, and we expect the company's operations, customer commitments and product roadmap to continue without interruption," Jones said in a statement. Kress said in a statement that she is committed to building on the foundation Huang created over 33 years as Nvidia's founder and CEO.

Nvidia stock was down about 7% in Thursday morning trading. It began a sharp fall in after-hours trading Wednesday evening after reports that the board was meeting to consider ousting Huang.

The resignation follows reports of two extramarital affairs. Huang, 63, admitted to infidelity in a note sent to employees on Sunday. That relationship was with Alice Huang, a former executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed, a major Nvidia customer. U.S. intelligence officials suspect she is an agent of Beijing’s spy agencies. (Jensen Huang and Alice Huang are not related.)

The second affair was with Kimberly Powell, according to The Wall Street Journal and other outlets. Powell, who runs Nvidia's healthcare business, has not commented publicly.

Jensen Huang rejected claims that his actions put U.S. security or the chipmaker's confidential information at risk once the reports about Alice Huang surfaced. The Justice Department is heading several separate probes that touch on Alice Huang, according to The Journal. Megaspeed and Nvidia have denied wrongdoing in a U.S. probe of suspected chip smuggling into China. And China's Foreign Ministry has called the espionage allegation unsubstantiated.

Nvidia’s board opened an independent investigation on Sunday, overseen by a committee of independent directors. The company said Tuesday that the directors retained Joon H. Kim, a former acting U.S. attorney, and the law firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz to conduct it. The board said it had no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised. Three House Republican committee chairs have asked Jensen Huang to appear for a private session with lawmakers on Nov. 18.

The company is set to report third-quarter earnings on Tuesday.
