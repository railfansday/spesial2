Trump nominates Chris LaNeve as army chief after Hegseth fired predecessor

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

LaNeve, who served as Hegseth’s senior military commander, nominated to permanently replace Gen Randy George

Donald Trump has nominated Gen Chris LaNeve to be the US army’s top officer, making permanent his interim service after defense secretary Pete Hegseth unexpectedly fired his predecessor in April.

LaNeve had been serving as Hegseth’s senior military commander before his nomination for a fourth star and service as vice chief of staff of the army in February.

Two months later, Hegseth fired army chief of staff Gen Randy George. While the firing has never been explained, George had insisted on an official investigation into an unauthorized 28 March army helicopter flyover near the Tennessee home of musician Kid Rock, which Hegseth blocked.

LaNeve’s relatively short tenure as a four-star general has raised questions among Democrats and Republicans, according to reporting from the Wall Street Journal.

LaNeve, 59, graduated from the University of Arizona in 1990 and later earned master’s degrees from Central Michigan University and the US Army Command and General Staff College. He has served in a wide variety of command roles, including a rotation as the commander of the 82nd Airborne Division and the 8th Army in Korea.
