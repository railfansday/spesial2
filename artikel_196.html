Macys Thanksgiving Day parade rescheduled for Saturday

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade will not be happening on Thanksgiving. Yes, you read that correctly.

The annual event, which is celebrating its 100th anniversary this year, has been rescheduled for Saturday, Nov. 28, due to severe weather.

According to the Weather Channel, New York City is bracing for a nor'easter on Thanksgiving Day. Before the Macy's Thanksgiving Day parade was rescheduled, there was discussion about how the parade could continue. After a 1997 incident where a balloon in the parade crashed into a lamppost and critically injured a bystander, New York City rules state that the parade balloons cannot fly in weather with "sustained winds top 23 mph or gusts top 34 mph."
