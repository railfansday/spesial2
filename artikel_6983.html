Taylor Swift Had the Best Reaction to a Joke About Her “Arena” Wedding During the CMAs

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Taylor Swift showed up at the CMAs for the first time in 13 years last night, performing a medley of “I Knew It, I Knew You” and “Our Song” that has fans convinced DebutTV is incoming. But she was also spotted taking in host Lainey Wilson’s opening monologue with Brad Paisley, where Brad joked, “We have to talk about the biggest story of the year. Somebody got married. Blonde singer, football player, it was such a big deal.”

Lainey—who’s married to NFL quarterback Devlin Hodges—replied, “Thank you for remembering, I sure did,” while Brad shot back, “What arena did you get married in?” Lainey then joked, “I’ll never forget what my husband said to me when we went looking for a venue,” and started singing “I beg your pardon, I never promised you Madison Square Garden.”

Cut to Taylor Swift, who was seen laughing in the audience alongside Brittany Mahomes, and mouthing “That was good.”

Travis Kelce actually opened up about choosing Madison Square Garden as a wedding location back in September, saying on New Heights, “We wanted to do it somewhere where we could have a very intimate and genuine setting without a lot of distractions. We needed it to be a private feel. MSG went above and beyond to give us that. I can’t thank everybody enough that showed up but also that helped put the show on. It was a very magical, magical night.”
