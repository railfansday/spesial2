Miley Cyrus Got Married at Frank Sinatra’s Midcentury Palm Springs House

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The home still has its piano-shaped pool—and you can rent it for the weekend.

Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando just got married in Frank Sinatra’s Palm Springs backyard. The singer and the Liily drummer, who met on a blind date in 2021, exchanged vows on Saturday, November 21, beneath an arch of white flowers at the edge of Sinatra’s former swimming pool. As far as wedding backdrops go, this one makes sense for two musicians: The pool is shaped like a grand piano.

Sinatra’s former home takes its name from the two palms that still stand on the property. When he hired architect E. Stewart Williams in 1947, he wanted a Georgian-style house, but Williams talked him into a single-story design with wide stretches of glass shaded by deep eaves. It was his first residential commission in Palm Springs, and he arranged the house around the pool. From the living room, sliding glass doors open onto a terrace bordered by the house, a pergola, and a cabana.

For a house built for Sinatra, a pool shaped like a grand piano sounds almost too on the nose. But Williams said the resemblance was incidental: He intended the curve to draw the eye toward the mountains. Sinatra lived at Twin Palms for about six years, including part of his marriage to Ava Gardner. In 2011, Palm Springs granted historic status to the house and parts of its grounds.

Yes, though it’s a private residence, not a museum. The estate is available as a vacation rental, with four bedrooms and room for up to eight guests. It also offers private guided tours for groups of 20 or more.
