Russell Brand's Sentence Could Be Extended Over 'Not Guilty' Prison Message

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Russell Brand’s prison sentence could possibly be extended as U.K. authorities investigate whether he broke prison rules by sharing a public message via his wife declaring he was “not guilty” of rape and sexual assault.

On Sunday, November 15, Brand’s wife, Laura Brand, read out a statement from the convicted comedian and broadcaster via the couple’s YouTube channel. “Beloved brothers and sisters in Christ, God bless you for your support. As you know, I am not guilty for the offenses I have been jailed for, so something strange is happening,” Brand, 51, declared in the message.

Brand’s missive is being investigated to determine whether it violates U.K. law prohibiting prisoners from making statements that may cause distress to victims.

“This is clearly appalling and is being urgently investigated. We expect action to be taken if he is found to have broken the rules,” a spokesperson for U.K. Prime Minister Keir Starmer said in a statement on Monday, November 16, per The Guardian and Reuters.

“Prisoners are strictly banned from making statements that cause distress to victims, and speaking to the media is only permitted in exceptional circumstances,” the spokesperson added. “Prisoners cannot get around these rules by having someone else publish their comments.”

According to the U.K. government website, inmates who break prison laws may be punished by being “kept in their cell for up to 21 days” and/or “given up to 42 extra days in prison on top of their original sentence.” Prisoners may also lose certain privileges, such as “removing a TV from a cell.”

Brand was convicted of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault at London’s Southwark Crown Court on November 5. He was sentenced to 10-and-a-half years behind bars on November 9.

In his message on Sunday, read by wife Laura, 39, Brand said he was incarcerated at HM Prison Littlehey in Cambridgeshire, England.

“Little as in small, and hey as in, ‘Hey, I’m in the biggest sex offenders prison in Europe.’ The good news is that I have already seen that the staff here are strong, they are kind and they are dedicated,” he said. “And as for my fellow prisoners, I now know that I am in no position to judge anyone.”

On Monday, Mia Latter, who alleged Brand raped her when she was 16, told the U.K.’s Jeremy Vine Show that she was “shocked” by his public message from prison.

“I was shocked that YouTube put it out, because I feel like they have some kind of responsibility not to let these things be broadcast,” said Latter. “And it was an insult to all the women that stood trial, and it was an insult to the jurors that gave their time. It’s an insult to the criminal justice system.”
