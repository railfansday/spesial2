‘NCIS: Origins’ Officially Sets Season 3 Finale Date Just 10 Episodes In

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

All good things must come to an end, and, as NCIS: Origins viewers have known for a while, the end will be coming sooner than usual in Season 3. While the first two runs of the prequel series focused on a younger Leroy Jethro Gibbs (Austin Stowell) coming into his own at Camp Pendleton under the leadership of Mike Franks (Kyle Schmid) lasted 18 episodes apiece, the latest season was designed to wrap up in just ten episodes. It ultimately fit with CBS' expanded schedule, allowing room for NCIS: New York to welcome LL COOL J back into the procedural game and still give NCIS: Sydney space for Season 4 in the new year. Creatively, the decision also made for the perfect opportunity to get Mark Harmon back in front of the camera for one final mission bridging the past and present.

With the network's holiday break only a few weeks away, the date has officially been set for the spin-off's Season 3 finale. CBS announced midseason premiere dates for its biggest shows this week, confirming that NCIS: Origins would return alongside the mothership series and New York on January 12. After that, it would air its finale on January 19, leaving Sydney to take its place starting on January 26. The arrangement is slightly awkward, with so few episodes remaining for 2027, but it'll nonetheless ensure Gibbs gets the space to wrap up the ongoing mystery stemming back to his younger years.

Season 3 opened with a shocker when two gunmen arrived at Gibbs' secluded Alaskan property and shot him. Unbeknownst to them, however, he survived the attack and used the opportunity to investigate in secret to learn who his assailants were, why they came after him, and who the other target they mentioned was. It all ties back into his Camp Pendleton days, but, fortunately, he's not alone in figuring out what's going on. Throughout the season, Gibbs has received help from a few familiar faces, including Michael Weatherly's Tony DiNozzo, using his newfound authority as NCIS director, and Emily Wickersham's Ellie Bishop, whose off-the-books work allowed her to accompany him in his search for answers. With four episodes remaining after tonight, there are still many questions left unanswered and more cases from the 1990s to explore before the big finale.

Tonight, the past will once again come back to haunt Gibbs once more, though in a much more surreal way. Episode 6, "Shot Through the Heart," will see the former Major Case Response Team leader searching for answers as to what really happened to Lala (Mariel Molino) after learning she was never aboard the Cessna that crashed in the Sierras. In the 90s, meanwhile, the series will spotlight an unexpected bond forming between her and Franks (Kyle Schmid) while the team tries to expose a mole after FBI Agent Casey Bloom (Sean Harmon) is shot. An interesting wrinkle to the episode, however, is that Muse Watson will be guest starring as the late Franks, reprising his role from NCIS. As it turns out, not properly addressing his gunshot finally catches up to Gibbs and, upon passing out, he reunites with not just his old mentor, but his younger self and Lala in a delirious dream.

Speaking to Deadline, showrunner David J. North teased how the episode will be a nostalgic one for NCIS fans of all stripes, invoking some of the old dream sequences that dove into Gibbs' mind in the past. It's a rare and creative opportunity to allow Harmon to play directly alongside the younger cast and reunite with an old friend, while also having a bit of fun. He teased a few of the other surprises in store, adding:

“Let’s just say fans of Top Gun won’t be disappointed. Plus, Austin’s Gibbs and Lala aren’t the only blasts from the past visiting Mark. Also, Franks and Lala make a decision that will later change everyone’s lives forever. Oh, and Randy meets his brunette doppelgänger, which is a lot of fun. NCIS fans, old and new, won’t want to miss this one."

NCIS: Origins will air its Season 3 finale on January 19. Stay tuned here at Collider for more updates on the series as the date draws closer.
