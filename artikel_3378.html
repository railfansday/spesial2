Florence Pugh’s Netflix Hit Sends a 74-Year-Old Classic Back to No. 1

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Netflix has brought us plenty of adaptations that have people reading once again. This year, three of their adaptations have brought sales to the source material back in a big way. One of those adaptations is the television series East of Eden. Starring Florence Pugh as John Steinbeck's complicated Cathy Ames, the series has brought the Steinbeck novel back to the #1 spot, reaching Amazon's list 74 years after its publication. East of Eden is not the Steinbeck novel that is often taught in schools, that goes to Of Mice and Men, but the Pugh series has people itching to read Steinbeck's original work.

Comparatively, other Netflix adaptations have been giving their book counterparts a boost. Both The Whisper Man and Remarkably Bright Creatures have similar stories after their adaptations. Sales of Remarkably Bright Creatures were up by 560% and the same can be said for The Whisper Man, with the added bonus of its audiobook also gaining a listening bump of 400+%. It has helped books like The Whisper Man find its way back into the spotlight and the rise in sales for East of Eden is not surprising, given the success and love that audiences have been experiencing with the Zoe Kazan-created series.

Kazan's East of Eden was helped by its all-star cast as well. Adam and Charles Trask were played by Christopher Abbott and Mike Faist respectively with Joseph Zada, of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping fame, playing Cal Trask, made famous by James Dean in the 1955 film. Kazan's adaptation is also one of the more faithful looks at Steinbeck's novel, that gives Cathy more nuance and layers to her rather than turning her into a straight forward movie villain as the 1955 film did.

Four Netflix characters, four completely different ways of showing up for somebody. This is the one who fits how you actually do friendship, and what having them in your life would be like.

You do not do friendship at half volume, and neither does he. What you want is somebody who says yes to the plan before you have finished describing the plan.

You are the friend who makes things happen, and so is she. Together you would be mildly exhausting to everyone around you and completely delighted with yourselves.

You have no patience for pleasant lies, and she has no ability to tell them. What looks like coldness from the outside is the most reliable friendship on this list.

You look after whoever is having the worst day. So does he. It is the least strategic way to move through the world and the best possible quality in a friend.

Prior to Pugh becoming the household name she is now, she was in a string of adaptations that helped her star rise. In 2016, her role in the film Lady Macbeth, which is adapted from Nikolai Leskov's novella Lady Macbeth of the Mtsensk District, put Pugh on the map. She then went on to star in Greta Gerwig's Little Women before tackling Princess Irulan in Denis Villeneuve's Dune franchise. Her work has inspired so many to pick up the books that these stories are inspired by and that now includes getting Steinback in the number one slot, 70+ years later.

You can see Pugh, Abbott, and Faist in Netflix's East of Eden.
