2 Months After GTA 6, Prime Video Drops The Closest Thing To Its Live-Action Series

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Grand Theft Auto VI is finally here, and, unsurprisingly, it is already making waves across the globe.

What is surprising, though, is that despite the game series' worldwide reach and long-running legacy, it has not been adapted for the screen yet. Given how the game in itself allows one to have a completely immersive and serialized storytelling experience, pulling off its live-action adaptation will not be an easy feat.

However, considering the growing demand for prestige video game adaptations, a GTA live-action adaptation might eventually see the light of day. While only time will tell whether that will eventually happen, an upcoming crime drama could perfectly qualify as a live-action GTA. Based on Ed Brubaker's comics of the same name, Prime Video's Criminal has many ingredients that would appeal to GTA fans.

While Ed Brubaker's Criminal comics may not be as popular as GTA, they, too, have a solid legacy of their own. They are highly acclaimed and have won multiple accolades. Prime Video's adaptation of the comic series also has a lot going in its favor, and it is set to premiere precisely two months after GTA 6 on January 13, 2027.

Similar to the original comics, Prime Video's Criminal will not follow a single protagonist. Instead, like the recent Grand Theft Auto games, it will switch between the perspectives of many different outlaws whose lives are intertwined through crime.

The show's first season will be split into two four-episode arcs. The first arc, titled "Coward", will center on a cautious but masterful thief named Leo Patterson (Charlie Hunnam).

In Part 2, "Lawless," the series will follow Tracy Lawless (Luke Evans), an AWOL soldier who sets out to investigate his brother's murder. Just like GTA's main characters become main leads in the DLCs, the Criminal comics give enough room to all characters to shine. Some of the best story beats in GTA are also driven by a fully developed crime ecosystem.

The overarching ecosystem involves a diverse set of players, including kingpins, cops, and fixers. Even in Brubaker's Criminal universe, everything from gambling clubs to local bars radiates outwards. The show's character archetypes will also read GTA's mission-giver roster.

Compared to GTA, the Prime Video crime drama will be far grittier in tone and less satirical. In fact, it will likely be completely bereft of the humor that defines the games. However, like GTA, Criminal, too, will completely strip away the romanticism of crime and deliver a realistic portrayal of a generational web of crimes and criminals.

Dan Houser is not only one of the co-founders of Rockstar Games, but he also serves as the lead writer and creative director for most titles in the GTA series. He is best known for his work in games like GTA, Red Dead Redemption, Bully, and Max Payne. However, he also serves as the writer of the acclaimed American Caper comics that are seemingly ripe for adaptation.

In terms of tone, the American Caper comics are even closer to GTA than Criminal with their acidic satire of American access. They also feature some bizarre and unhinged characters in their stories, reminding one of the NPCs from the GTA games. If Prime Video's Criminal proves to be successful, there will certainly be an audience for American Caper's TV adaptation.

A live-action movie or television adaptation of Grand Theft Auto VI may not be completely off the cards. However, it is certainly not happening anytime soon. Even if it does not see the light of day, however, fans of the games can always read comics like Criminal and American Caper and check out their TV adaptations.

Based on the comic series, Criminal is an action crime series developed by Ed Brubaker and Jordan Harper for Prime Video. This adaptation follows various characters within and adjacent to a bustling criminal underworld and comprises several arcs that occasionally intersect. The series was greenlit by Amazon in 2024.
