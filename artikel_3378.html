Star Trek's Brand New Crew Officially Met With Mixed Reviews

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Any new Star Trek adaptation lives or dies by several key facets, not least the crew who take to the stars on our behalf to explore the galaxy. That's why shows like The Next Generation did so well, because Picard et Al were compelling characters who were able to deliver the narrative in a way that felt real and grounded, despite it being so fantastical in nature.

It's also the same reason that other adaptations have struggled over the years, even if they had a solid story that fans would have easily been able to get into otherwise. That same rule also applies to adaptations into other mediums, and it's why so many draw on recognizable faces like Spock, just to lower the risk if their new characters don't prove to be as popular.

Now, another adaptation has just gone live and, while it has plenty of potential, fans aren't particularly impressed with plenty besides just the crew.

In case you missed its release in October, Star Trek: Outposts Unknown is a narrative-driven outpost builder that tasks a fledgling crew of Starfleet officers with building, researching, and exploring, all in a strange unique world. On the face of it, it's the perfect premise for a new game tied to the franchise, but it seems the execution has left a lot to be desired, with the game currently sitting on Mixed reviews overall.

"I wanted to love this as a modern successor to Star Trek: New Worlds, but in its current state, I cannot recommend it. The game feels less like a finished PC strategy title and more like a rushed, half-baked mobile port with the microtransactions stripped out, leaving only the tedious waiting mechanics behind," one review read.

While another added, "Yet another promising idea that was bady executed. It certainly looks pretty, but they needed a lot more polish on the actual gameplay mechanics. The developer itself says that this is a resource management game. If that was their goal, then it was a massive failure. The game is actually just you sitting around waiting for your crew to eventually generate a bit more Poly Alloy."

"I really wanted to like this game. I wanted it to be the spiritual successor to Stranded Alien Dawn. I have given it time to get its act together, there have been many patches, and I have come back and its still bad. Its a great concept but what they have made is just dumb and boring," a third said.

With all of that in mind, the game does continue to receive regular updates from developer Magic Fuel Games, so they may well be able to bring it around and turn it into the game that everyone was hoping for in the first place.

Star Trek: Outposts Unknown is currently available for $32.99 on Steam, though it does regularly get discounts as part of sales and wider events, so it might be worth waiting for one of those to crop up before committing to the purchase if you aren't sure.
