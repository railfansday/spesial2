Apple Hit With Outages in iCloud, Apple Pay and More Services

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Apple reported outages across several of its platforms on Tuesday, including iCloud, Apple Pay, the App Store and more, the latest company hit by technical difficulties after a string of nationwide issues.

The company’s system status page as of 2:30 p.m. ET showed full or partial outages affecting platforms like the iCloud website, Apple’s business services, on-device AppleCare support and more. Users reported outages earlier on Tuesday across services like Apple Pay and the App Store, according to the tracking service Downdetector, though those appear to have resolved. The outages were not caused by a cyberattack, according to a person familiar with the matter. An Apple spokesperson said in a statement on Tuesday that the outages were caused by “an internal update.” “We’re working to get the remaining systems online as soon as possible,” the spokesperson said.

The outage is the latest in a series of tech malfunctions across several industries in the last week.

Microsoft saw a massive outage impact its Outlook email service on Thursday, a disruption it blamed on “an issue during an earlier network capacity upgrade that surfaced following a recent network change.” A day later, the Federal Aviation Administration issued its first nationwide ground stop in 25 years after it said a massive data traffic surge at two possible locations caused “instability with aircraft communications and radar feeds.” Both incidents are believed to be unrelated and are under investigation.

Several major U.S. banks, including JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America and U.S. Bank, saw outages on Monday that affected customers’ access to their accounts, though those were resolved later that day. Marriott, the world’s largest hotel company by rooms, also saw users report outages with its website and app, according to Downdetector.
