After two close-call overtime wins, Indiana will have time to refresh and refocus with a bye week.

The Hoosiers had a Halloween scare on Saturday, being forced into extra time by unranked Minnesota. While IU ultimately secured a 23-16 win, this marked the second week in a row that they couldn't pull ahead by the end of regulation. Sitting at No. 6 in the national rankings, these performances definitely draw some concern for the remainder of the season.

A common thread in both of these games? Quarterback Josh Hoover. The transfer star has not met his standards in recent weeks, citing a shoulder injury as the reason for his lower output.

7th-year senior backup Grant Wilson stepped in to spark the Hoosiers' offense against Minnesota, raising questions about who will start for the team against USC in Week 11.

Here's everything you need to know about Indiana's next game, including TV channel and streaming options for Buckeyes football.

Indiana won't play during Week 10 of the college football season. The Hoosiers will return to action against USC on Saturday, Nov. 14. The game will be played at Memorial Stadium in Bloomington, IN.

Fans can watch the game live on CBS or stream the game with Fubo, which offers a free trial for all new users.
