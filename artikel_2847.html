Cam Schlittler unanimously wins 2026 AL Cy Young Award after historic season for Yankees

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

New York Yankees starting pitcher Cam Schlittler unanimously won the 2026 AL Cy Young Award. He received all 30 first-place votes from the BBWAA, the league announced Wednesday.

Schlittler started 33 games for New York this season, posting a 14-6 record and a 1.95 ERA. He struck out 239 batters across 193 2/3 innings.

The 25-year-old's ERA was the lowest in the American League and second-lowest in MLB, with only Milwaukee Brewers ace Jacob Misiorowski (1.80) besting him. Schlittler was tied with Dylan Cease for third in the majors in strikeouts, behind Misiorowski (252) and Gavin Williams (248).

Schlittler led the AL in pitching WAR (6.2) and opponent on-base percentage (.244) while posting the third-lowest ERA in Yankees franchise history, trailing only Spud Chandler (1.64 in 1943) and Ron Guidry (1.74 in 1978). Guidry won the AL Cy Young Award in 1978.

In a rotation expected to be led by Max Fried and Gerrit Cole, Schlittler instead cemented himself as the team's ace, becoming an All-Star for the first time along the way.

The Yankees starter was a finalist for the award along with Toronto's Cease and Cleveland Guardians starter Parker Messick. Cease had the most second-place votes at 22, ahead of seven for Messick and one for Tampa Bay Rays ace Drew Rasmussen.

After watching Schlittler all season, Yankees manager Aaron Boone was confident his ace would win.

"He's going to win the Cy Young. I think he's just continued to get better year in and year out. This is largely in line with who we saw down the stretch when he got called up last year.

"He's been the best pitcher in the league. When you have that going all year at the top of your rotation, that's massive. And that's what he's been."

Schlittler was drafted in the seventh round of the 2022 MLB Draft. At the start of the 2025 season, the right-hander was with New York's Double-A affiliate in Somerset.

After injuries to other starters in the rotation, the Yankees promoted Schittler. He made his MLB debut on July 9, 2025, and earned a win over the Seattle Mariners. In his debut, the righty allowed three earned runs over 5 1/3 innings and struck out seven.

Overall, Schlittler made 14 starts as a rookie, posting a 4-3 record and a 2.96 ERA. He then stunned the Red Sox in the playoffs, striking out 12 in a wild-card victory.

Barring an extension, Schlittler has one more pre-arbitration year left, then three years of arbitration. He made $801,000 in 2026, per Spotrac.
