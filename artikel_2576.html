Lions place Skyler Gill-Howard on IR, elevate DE from practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

As expected, the Detroit Lions have placed rookie defensive lineman Skyler Gill-Howard on injured reserve, ending his 2026 season. Earlier this week, Lions coach Dan Campbell indicated that Gill-Howard suffered a knee injury during a late fourth quarter goal line play, and he will require surgery to repair the damage.

"I hate that for him. And he's had a heck of a season up to this point," Campbell said.

Gill-Howard appeared in all 10 Lions games this season, holding a significant role as a pass rusher and disrupter in the run game. Despite being a sixth-round pick, Gill-Howard worked his way up and played 316 snaps—fifth among defensive lineman in Detroit this season.

Gill-Howard is the third Lions defender to be placed on injured reserve in the last four weeks, joining linebacker Jack Campbell (neck) and Aidan Hutchinson (torn ACL).

In his place, the Lions will rely upon the likes of Alim McNeill, Tyleik Williams, Tyler Lacy, and Levi Onwuzurike to step up in his place. They could also play Mekhi Wingo, who shares a lot of physical traits with Gill-Howard, but hasn't been active for a single game yet this season.

The Lions did not immediately fill Gill-Howard's spot on the 53-man roster, but they did temporarily elevate defensive end Anthony Lucas from the practice squad for Thursday's game against the Chicago Bears. This is the second time Lucas has been elevated, playing in 12 defensive snaps against the Miami Dolphins a few weeks ago. Lucas is an undrafted rookie out of USC who brings a ton of intriguing athletic traits, but is still growing as a defender.
