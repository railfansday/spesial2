Mavericks’ Blowout win over Nuggets had Kyrie Irving feeling unguardable

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Dallas Mavericks are on a roll right now, and Kyrie Irving knows defenders still have no answer for him. Following Tuesday night's 120-101 Emirates NBA Cup blowout win over the Denver Nuggets at Ball Arena, Irving joined NBA legend and NBC studio analyst Carmelo Anthony to discuss his rhythm after missing all of last season with a torn ACL. Irving kept his message simple.

"I don't feel like a lot of people can guard me," Irving said.

Anthony immediately smiled and replied, "There you go! There's the Kyrie we missed!"

Even Denver coach David Adelman saw that threat coming before tipoff. Adelman called Irving "one of the best one-on-one players" ever and raved about his three-level scoring package, via Grant Asfeth of the Dallas Hoops Journal.

"It can be a mid-range night. It can be a three-point night. It can be the crazy finishes around the rim," Adelman said.

Interestingly, Irving didn't even need a massive scoring night to wreck Denver's game plan. He finished with 11 points on 5-of-19 shooting, 6 assists and 5 rebounds while posting a plus-21 rating across 34 minutes. His gravity opened the floor for a balanced Dallas attack that saw seven players reach double figures and shoot 49% from the field.

Cooper Flagg led the charge with 24 points, 12 assists and 6 rebounds, seamlessly playing off Irving's perimeter pressure. P.J. Washington and Naji Marshall chipped in 14 points apiece, while Daniel Gafford and Caleb Martin added 13 each, with Martin swiping 5 steals. Zaccharie Risacher rounded out the surge with 12 points as Dallas piled up 68 paint points and 35 fast-break points.

On the other end, the Mavericks stifled Denver into 40% shooting and 18 turnovers. Jamal Murray paced the Nuggets (10-6) with 20 points, and Nikola Jokic recorded 18 points and 11 rebounds.

With four straight wins, an 11-7 record and a 3-0 mark in West Group A, Dallas looks dangerous, and Irving's confidence is clearly all the way back.
