Who is Clemson QB vs Georgia Tech? Trent Pearman in after Tait Reynolds injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

CLEMSON — Trent Pearman has entered the game at quarterback for Clemson football.

The Tigers (6-3, 4-2 ACC) are playing Georgia Tech on Nov. 14 (The CW).

Starting quarterback Tait Reynolds was injured on the first series of the game and headed to the Clemson locker room.

According to Clemson, this is the third game Pearman has entered this season. He's a Clemson native.
