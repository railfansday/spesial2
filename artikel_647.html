Watermelon Pictures Buys Zach Woods’ ‘The Accompanist,’ Starring Susan Sarandon and Aubrey Plaza

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Watermelon Pictures has acquired North American distribution rights to “The Accompanist,” co-written and directed by actor, writer and comedian Zach Woods in his directorial debut.

The film, which premiered at the Tribeca Film Festival, stars Oscar winner Susan Sarandon (“Dead Man Walking,” “Bull Durham”), alongside Emmy and Golden Globe nominee Aubrey Plaza (“The White Lotus”) and Everly Carganilla (“The Chair”). Written by Woods and Brandon Gardner, “The Accompanist” centers on Emily (Carganilla), a nine-year-old girl living with her loving grandfather in New Jersey as his advancing dementia makes it increasingly difficult for him to care for her. After a young child-welfare agent (Plaza) intervenes, Emily is placed in the care of Sylvia (Sarandon), an eccentric, mischievous 70-year-old foster parent whose unconventional world initially leaves Emily uncertain of what to expect.

“People casually use words like ‘honored’ and ‘family’ all the time in press releases and it often sounds kind of insane to me,” said Woods. “But. And I mean this. I really am honored to be partnering with Watermelon Pictures on ‘The Accompanist.'”

Added Sarandon: “So happy that our little jewel of a film is in the hands of Watermelon Pictures. I’m grateful for their enthusiasm and commitment.”

The distribution deal was initiated by Audrey Delaney, marketing executive at Watermelon Pictures and was negotiated by Munir Atalla, EVP of production and acquisitions for Watermelon Pictures, and Jessica Lacy of Gersh on behalf of the filmmakers.

“We fell in love with ‘The Accompanist’ because beneath its humor, mystery and wonderfully strange edges is an incredibly moving story about the people who find one another when they need it most,” said Hamza Ali, Co-Founder of Watermelon Pictures. “We’re thrilled to bring this beautiful film to audiences across the country.”
