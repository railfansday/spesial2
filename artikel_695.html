Zach John King Crowns the Country Airplay Chart for the First Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The singer-songwriter hoists "Get to Drinkin' " to No. 1.

Zach John King earns his first No. 1 on Billboard’s Country Airplay chart with his first entry as “Get to Drinkin’” rises a spot to take the lead on the list dated Nov. 28. The song attracted 31.8 million audience impressions (up 9%) Nov. 13-19, according to Luminate.

King becomes the fifth artist to earn a first No. 1 in 2026, joining John Morgan (“Kid Myself,” October), Stella Lefty (“Boston,” September), Josh Ross (“Hate How You Look,” July) and Tucker Wetmore (“Brunette,” May). That’s already more than in 2025 and 2023, when three acts apiece did so, although well shy of 2024’s decade-best 10, a crop that included Post Malone, Shaboozey and Ella Langley.

Cowritten by King and four others, “Get to Drinkin’” summits in its 39th week, seven faster than the decade’s average. It’s from his 20-track debut album, I’m What You Get, released in August. The Fayetteville, Ga., native worked as a behavioral therapist before moving to Nashville in 2021 to pursue songwriting. He later caught the attention of Morgan Wallen through TikTok, leading to an opening slot on the superstar’s 2025 stadium tour.

Trey Pendley, from tiny Toad Suck, Ark., charts his first Country Airplay title as “Family Man” debuts at No. 59 (730,000). He becomes the second artist on Leo33’s roster to reach the ranking, following Zach Top. Cowritten by Pendley, Jacob Bryant and John Davidson, the song celebrates life with his wife and children, and appears on his 13-track debut album, Signs of Life, released in August.

Pendley first drew attention with 2021’s “Daddy’s Son,” whose homemade music video, reportedly produced for just $50, amassed more than 10 million YouTube views. He also cowrote Luke Combs’ 2025 Hot Country Songs entry “15 Minutes,” inspired by an encounter with an inmate at a Louisiana prison where Pendley’s father worked. Pendley signed with Leo33 last year.

All charts dated Nov. 28 will update Tuesday, Nov. 24, on Billboard.com.
