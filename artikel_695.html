‘The Gilded Age’ Scores Season 5 Renewal at HBO

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The period drama from Julian Fellowes is tracking for its most watched season to date.

Like a train on one of railroad magnate George Russell’s lines, The Gilded Age will keep rolling.

HBO has renewed the period drama from creator Julian Fellowes for a fifth season. The Gilded Age will reach the midpoint of season four with its Nov. 22 episode and is on pace for its most watched season to date.

“Julian Fellowes and the cast of The Gilded Age have once again captured the zeitgeist this fall, delivering another captivating, unmissable season, and we love how our growing audience has continued to embrace the series year after year,” said Francesca Orsi, president HBO drama series and films. “We can’t wait to return to 61st Street, as the Russells, van Rhijns and Kirklands continue to wrestle with the cost of ascent, while we expand the canvas and deepen the operatic stakes for these beloved characters.” Related Stories Movies Parker Posey Knows Career Highs and Lows. The first three episodes of season four are averaging 5.9 million cross-platform viewers in the United States (measured from the show’s Nov. 1 premiere date), up 20 percent from the same point in season three. The Nov. 15 episode drew 4.3 million viewers over three days.

“It’s very exciting and deeply satisfying to see audiences fall more in love with The Gilded Age every season,” said Erin Underhill, president of Universal Television, which produces the series with HBO. “Our remarkable cast, writers led by Julian Fellowes, and the entire creative team continue to make this world feel both timeless and resonant while keeping us on the edge of our seats season after season. We can’t wait for audiences to see what’s in store for season five.”

The show’s sprawling cast is led by Carrie Coon, Christine Baranski, Cynthia Nixon, Morgan Spector, Louisa Jacobson and Denée Benton. The current season, writer and executive producer Sonja Warfield told The Hollywood Reporter after the season premiere, will be a series of trials for the characters.

“We wanted to test all of the relationships within the show. So marriages, romances, familial relationships,” Warfield said. “I think the social mobility that is inherent to America is also something that’s tested, and we get to see who gets to move up socially, where they get to go.”

Fellowes executive produces The Gilded Age with Salli Richardson-Whitfield (who also directs), Warfield, Gareth Neame, David Crockett, Robert Greenblatt, Kate Churchill, Davita Scarlett and Elaine Aronson. Erica Armstrong Dunbar and Holly Rymon are co-EPs.
