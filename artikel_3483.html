How to play pool in GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

To play pool in Grand Theft Auto 6, you must find a table and know the rules. You'll likely play your first game of pool during the "Coming Home" mission at the Rusty Anchor, but the game assumes some familiarity with the game of pool. Don't worry; playing pool (and winning!) is easy once you get the hang of it.

Here's where and how to play pool in GTA 6.

Pool isn't listed as an activity on the map legend, but if a location contains a pool table, you will see a small 8-ball icon when you hover over it on the map. To find new pool tables, you'd best look for bars, clubs, and gyms. One pool table isn't different from another, but it can be hard to find one with a challenger present. If you're up for some competition, you'd best go to the pool table at The Rusty Anchor.

Playing pool in GTA 6 doesn't yield much money, but it will boost your focus by a few percent per game.

While you can practice solo anytime you want, if there's an opponent waiting at the pool table, you can accept their challenge (which requires cash). Don't expect to walk away rich; the prize pot is usually no more than $20. Alternatively, you can invite your partner to play pool with you, which will improve your relationship.

To get started, pick up a cue — it's usually on the wall, near the pool table. Now, take your break shot; hold L2/LT to aim, then either use the left trigger to manually hit the white cue ball (pull it back, then move forward) or hold and release R2/RT. For now, aim forward in a straight line and hit the cue ball with enough power to scatter the rest of the balls.

From this point onward, you and your opponent will take turns trying to push the colored balls into the six holes on the edges of the table, via the cue ball. Initially, you and your opponent are free to score with any type of ball except the black 8-ball — if you pocket it, you'll lose the game. Beware that the first person to pocket a ball is assigned that type of ball. So, if you score first by pocketing a striped ball, you'll have to pocket every other striped ball to win the game. Your opponent, in that case, must pocket every solid ball.

If you hit no balls, your opponent's balls, or the 8-ball first, your opponent can place the cue ball in a better spot at the start of their next turn. If you successfully pocket a ball from your own group, you get an extra shot. Once you've pocketed every ball within your group, you only need to pocket the 8-ball to win the game. It's important to note that you do not need to call which pocket you'll hit the 8-ball into to win.

While GTA 6's version of pool is very realistic, there are still a few exploits to help you win every game.
