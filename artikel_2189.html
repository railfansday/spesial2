What Emile Heskey has noticed about Sheffield Wednesday this season

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Former Leicester City, Aston Villa and England striker Emile Heskey believes Sheffield Wednesday must be more consistent if they are to win promotion from League One this season.

After David Storch completed his takeover earlier this year, it has been a turbulent start to life back in League One for Wednesday, and the club have made an early season managerial change, with Dave Challinor replacing Henrik Pedersen.

Wednesday were on a disappointing run in the league prior to Challinor's arrival, winning just one of their previous eight games under Pedersen and caretaker manager Matt Hamshaw, and the 51-year-old's reign got off to a difficult start.

The Owls were knocked out of the FA Cup by League Two side Oldham Athletic in Challinor's first game before a much-changed team were beaten 4-0 by Accrington Stanley in the EFL Trophy, but he recorded his first victory at the third attempt with an emphatic 3-0 win over Plymouth Argyle at Hillsborough on Saturday.

Goals from Billy Mitchell, Davis Keillor-Dunn and Di'Shon Bernard sealed a crucial three points for Wednesday against their fellow promotion hopefuls, moving them up to fifth in the League One table, just six points behind second-placed Stockport County and league leaders Huddersfield Town.

Challinor boasts the impressive record of finishing in the play-off places or higher in each of his 15 full seasons as a manager, winning seven promotions during that time, and the Owls will be hoping that the ex-Stockport boss can add an eighth to his CV this season.

As his son Jaden returned to Hillsborough for a second loan spell from Manchester City this summer, Heskey has been watching Wednesday closely this season, and the 48-year-old has identified one thing that Challinor must improve.

While Heskey admitted he is pleased with the amount of game time Jaden is receiving in South Yorkshire, he claimed that the Owls must show greater consistency if they are to mount a serious promotion push.

"I've been doing ambassador work for Liverpool, and watching the boys play mainly, one's at Sheffield Wednesday and one's in Germany, so a bit of travelling," Heskey said on ex-Wednesday striker Jamie Vardy's podcast, Jamie Vardy's Having A Party.

"He plays quite regularly for Sheffield Wednesday, so that's good.

"Sheffield Wednesday are a bit up and down at the minute, they need a couple more wins to cement themselves and try to get back up into the Championship.

While Wednesday are currently in the play-off places, many supporters feel that their side have not performed to their best for much of this season, so one of Challinor's main priorities will be to improve the consistency of his new squad.

As things stand, it seems Huddersfield Town, Stockport and Oxford United are the teams to beat in League One this season, but with no standout side in the division, automatic promotion could still be a realistic target for the Owls if they are able to put a run together in the second half of the campaign.
