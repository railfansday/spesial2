Falkirk beat St Johnstone to extend winning run to six

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Falkirk vs St Johnstone. Scottish Premiership.

Report as Falkirk beat St Johnstone 2-1; Jacob Murrell opened the scoring with Finley Barbrook doubling their lead; Josh Campbell pulled a goal back for Saints; Falkirk remain third in the Scottish Premiership; St Johnstone are seventh

Falkirk clung on to clock up six top-flight wins in a row for the first time since 1966 with a 2-1 home victory over St Johnstone.

John McGlynn's team scored early in each half to remain third in the Scottish Premiership.

Jacob Murrell's eighth goal of a superb debut season sent the Bairns on their way inside six minutes and Finley Barbrook's second goal in as many games on 48 minutes doubled the advantage.

Josh Campbell nodded in a late reply to serve up a scare but the hosts held firm thereafter.

McGlynn's well-oiled machine continued to operate at a high level and their surprise bid for a European place shows no sign of stopping.

They went in front when a long Deniss Melniks throw-in was flicked on by stand-in skipper Liam Henderson and Murrell netted on the volley from close range.

Saints created a flurry of chances in response. The pick of them saw Campbell sent clear by Joel Cotterill. The on-loan Hibs forward opted for an early shot and skewed the effort a yard wide.

Top-scorer Josh Fowler found plenty of penalty-box space, too, but his strike was deflected wide.

Falkirk were next to threaten as play swept from end to end.

Toby Steward fumbled a Murrell header from Scott Tanser's delivery but recovered to deny Ethan Ross on his goal line.

Dan Finlayson bravely got his head in the way of a rocket shot from Owen Eames.

Falkirk 'keeper Scott Bain batted away from Taylor Steven and Fowler dragged a great chance wide - both set up by Kai Fotheringham.

Crucially for Falkirk, they were more clinical so close to goal.

Their second, three minutes into the second half, was sparked by Ross feeding Eames out wide. The Derby loan winger's rapid burst of pace did for Liam Smith. Eames fired across goal to award Barbrook with a tap-in from a yard out.

The game should have been over as a contest on 55 minutes but Ross miscued right in front of goal from Murrell's cross.

Simo Valakari made a trio of attack-minded changes, including Danny Schmidt for Fowler.

Within a minute, the German spurned a glorious chance. Campbell's pass was a touch behind the unmarked Schmidt and a weak shot was saved by the legs of Bain.

Falkirk managed the game well until conceding on 83 minutes.

Campbell gave Saints hope by ghosting onto substitute Logan Chalmers' delivery from deep and heading past Bain.

Saints 'keeper Steward joined the attack in the final seconds as a free-kick was launched into the box to no avail.

"I wouldn't have believed it if someone had said we would win six in a row this season.

"We only managed two in a row last season and thought we were doing well.

"To do six is beyond our wildest dreams, really.

"Since coming here, we have always tried to create history and break records.

"That has been unbeaten runs, the 'Invincible' season in League One and games against other teams.

"Winning at Easter Road for the first time in 20 years, beating Celtic for the first time in 19.

"Not many teams have won at Tynecastle recently and we were the first to win there in 18 months.

"We thrive on that. We are always the underdog and we are trying to prove people wrong."

"We didn't give ourselves a chance today. For virtually the whole match we were chasing the game after conceding a goal early in the first half.

"We gifted them a platform and momentum. Then, after conceding a goal early in the second half, it was almost impossible to get back into the match.

"Our players didn't think it was impossible, though. They thought they could do it. But to go 2-0 down away from home against a top team is very hard.

"We are a strong team but we faced a team today that took its chances. We didn't, so we cannot complain."
