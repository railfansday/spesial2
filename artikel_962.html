Clyde ready for 'heavy metal football' as rock band sponsors club

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Metalcore band Bleed From Within have designed Clyde's new third kit

The former Liverpool manager Jurgen Klopp once described the high-octane style of his teams as "heavy metal football."

Now Scottish League Two side Clyde have gone a step further than Klopp's metaphor - by agreeing a sponsorship deal with a rock band.

Metalcore act Bleed From Within have also co-designed the new third kit for the club nicknamed "the Bully Wee", who now play in the band's home town of Hamilton, South Lanarkshire.

The group, who have released seven albums, said the new partnership was "an opportunity not to be missed".

The band's singer Scott Kennedy said: "Working with Clyde FC was a no brainer.

"Given our shared connections to Glasgow, where we played our first shows and Hamilton, where the band was formed, we knew this made sense.

"We couldn't be happier with how the shirts turned out and we look forward to supporting the guys throughout the season and beyond."

The new shirt features colour tones from the group's 2020 record Fracture and is designed to appeal to "communities in football and music".

Clyde chief executive David Alexander said: "We are beyond excited to work with Bleed From Within on our third shirt for the 26/27 season.

"As a club who thrive on living outside the box and doing things differently, we had no hesitation when given the option to team up with an iconic name in the metal industry from Glasgow.

"A number of staff members already familiar with the artist, gave immediate excitement at the potential to turn heads and raise visibility of the club through such a great band."

Alexander added that since the project was floated the club had "enjoyed watching the band storm across the European festival circuit".

Bleed From Within worked on the shirt design with Clyde's technical partner Terrace, who said the group had shown great enthusiasm for the idea.

The band join a long line of music acts who have become involved with football clubs.

Ed Sheeran - who was also a minority owner of the club - sponsored Ipswich Town for four seasons, starting in 2021.

Last year rock band Franz Ferdinand backed youth football team Symington Tinto 2014s, with the group's logo appearing on kits.

In October US rock band Wheatus agreed to sponsor a Worcestershire youth football team.

And in the 1990s Clydebank were sponsored by the pop band Wet Wet Wet, who came from the West Dunbartonshire town.

Other acts like the Libertines, Fontaines D.C and Mogwai have also been involved in sponsorship of teams in the past.
