Meta announced Direct, a new messaging app from Instagram that it’s currently testing in Turkey and Thailand on iOS and Android. Direct is essentially Instagram’s direct messaging without Instagram’s other features. Meta says your Instagram chats will sync across both apps if you login with the same account. You can also sign up with an email or phone number, but Meta says if you use Direct without an Instagram account, “only your contacts can start a chat with you or add you to a group.”

This is far from Meta’s first messaging app — it also has Messenger, which requires a Facebook account to use, and WhatsApp, which Meta (then Facebook) acquired in 2014. Meta also recycled the “Direct” name, which it previously used for a Snapchat-like app it shut down several years ago.

The new Direct app has a UI similar to Instagram’s messaging tab, but with extra features for customizing chats and sending different types of media, including:

• Draw: Draw and sketch directly inside your chat threads.

• Notes: Catch quick updates from friends right at the top of your inbox.

• Stickers: Create custom stickers to express yourself and share with friends and family.

• Voice Effects: Add fun audio effects like crowd laughter or underwater sounds to your replies.

• Themes: Personalize your chat backgrounds with custom visual themes.

Meta says users have to be at least 13 years old to use Direct, and teens who log in with their Instagram account will have the same Teen Account messaging protections and restrictions on Direct.
