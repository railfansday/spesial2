The 2026 ADWEEK Innovator 50: Building Relevance for Tomorrow

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

“If you can dream it, you can do it” has never been more true.

From advancing emerging technologies to delivering breakthrough ideas that drive real business impact, the 2026 ADWEEK Innovator 50 recognizes the executives shaping the future of marketing and advertising by pushing the boundaries of what’s possible.

These innovative leaders across disciplines at agencies, brands, tech firms, and media companies are moving at the speed of technological and cultural change, ensuring their businesses remain relevant tomorrow.

SVP of Digital Shopping Experiences, Albertsons Companies

With over 2,200 stores in 35 states, Albertsons Companies knows how to connect with consumers in the aisles. Jill Pavlovich has brought the same rigor to its digital storefront, advancing the ecommerce side of the business by integrating AI into the shopping journey through conversational search and personalized recommendations using first-party data. Among her biggest advances have been enhancing product discovery through AI and expanding shoppable recipe capabilities to reduce the daily stress of meal planning. Customers using Albertsons’ new AI-powered tools generated up to 26% higher average order values, demonstrating AI’s commercial potential in retail by helping to move customers more easily from inspiration to purchase.

Global Chief Marketing & Innovation Officer, APR

Having watched the AI revolution begin at Accenture, Edmond Handwerker recognized genAI’s potential to fundamentally reshape the marketing operating model. Since joining APR in 2024, he’s repositioned the company from a production consultancy to an AI-enabled production intelligence firm that gives clients more control by combining proprietary technology and one of the industry’s richest intelligence datasets, collected from 400 global brands. Instead of reviewing individual hero campaigns, APR now analyzes production investment across a client’s entire marketing ecosystem through its Acero platform, then operationalizes its insights at scale through its Capri system. With these assets, APR has won nine of 10 competitive pitches in the past year, while client engagements have generated $75 million in annual savings and cut production costs by 84%.

This is the year beehiiv grew beyond newsletter advertising, becoming a broader creator media platform that spans monetization, distribution, audience control, and paid access, pushing the industry to take creator-led media seriously. Under Tyler Denk’s leadership, beehiiv’s ad network doubled revenue to $15 million in ARR, publisher payouts topped $1 million monthly, and the platform grew to more than 50,000 active users. He rebuilt the network from a largely manual operation into a self-serve, increasingly programmatic system serving everyone from independent writers to major publishers. With advertisers including Roku and HubSpot investing in the channel, he’s established a model competitors are racing to match, strengthening the economics of creator-led publishing.
