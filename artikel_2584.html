Alex Campos reveals Hansi Flick&#8217;s advice after Barcelona debut against Sabah: &#8216;Have the same confidence I have in training&#8217;

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Alex Campos made his Barcelona first-team debut against Sabah in the UEFA Champions League, becoming the latest La Masia graduate to receive an opportunity under Hansi Flick.

The 18-year-old defender came on as a substitute in the 64th minute, with Barcelona leading 4-1 and approximately half an hour remaining.

Campos became the 12th youngster to make his first-team debut under Flick, marking an important milestone in his development at the club.

The defender spoke to the media after the match and admitted that the experience was difficult to comprehend while he was on the pitch.

"It's a dream come true; I couldn't believe it whilst I was on the pitch. All I can do is thank the team and everyone at Barça," the Barça defender began.

Alex Campos made his Barcelona debut against Sabah. (Picture credit: X/@FCBarcelona)

Campos also revealed the advice Flick gave him before sending him onto the pitch.

Rather than asking the youngster to change his approach, the Barcelona manager encouraged him to trust the qualities he had demonstrated during training.

"He told me to have the same confidence I have in training and to do what I know how to do.

"Barça has a lot of faith in La Masia, and that motivates all of us young players to work hard so that we can make our debut one day."

His introduction also came in a situation that offered valuable European experience without placing him in the starting line-up from the opening whistle.

With Barcelona already holding a three-goal advantage, Campos had the opportunity to experience the intensity of Champions League football and begin adapting to the demands of first-team competition.

The defender was asked whether he could eventually become Barcelona's first-choice centre-back.

Campos avoided making ambitious predictions about his future, instead acknowledging that establishing himself at the highest level will require continued effort.

For what it is worth, a first-team debut is indeed an important step, but it does not guarantee a permanent place in Flick's plans. Campos will now need to build on the experience through his performances in training and future opportunities.
