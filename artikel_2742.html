What became a profound strength is now an issue for Manchester City

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Under Pep Guardiola, Manchester City were a force in possession. Playing out from the back became their forte and it formed the backbone of their play. It became a regular occurrence to see Pep Guardiola's team play their way out from the back as their build-up play suffocated their opponents. City's success under Guardiola was based upon their ability to control matches and in essence strangle their opponents. However, Manchester City are now in the Enzo Maresca era. What was a strength for City for a decade isn't as pronounced as it once was for a variety of reasons.

Last season, Pep Guardiola used Bernardo Silva and Rodri to drop deep to receive possession to help his team play out from the back. After Gianluigi Donnarumma replaced Ederson as Manchester City's first-choice keeper, Guardiola found a solution to Donnarumma's shortcomings in the build-up. Of course, this solution worked. Bernardo Silva and Rodri knew Guardiola's demands intimately and could play their way out of any situation in possession. However, Bernardo Silva and Rodri are gone and Enzo Maresca doesn't have the security blanket that the pair provided to call upon.

Now, Manchester City's opponents are not scared to press Enzo Maresca's side. After being such a strong weapon of their play, possession play from the back is a weakness for Manchester City. Ruben Dias, Marc Guehi and Gianluigi Donnarumma aren't adept in possession. Not in terms of playing through an opponent. Too often, City are pressed into trouble and a long ball to Erling Haaland is the outlet ball. The issue is that the long ball from Donnarumma, Guehi, or Dias isn't a quality one, and Haaland is fighting a losing battle. From there, Manchester City squander possession and their possession play breaks down. Somehow, Enzo Maresca has to find a solution and it has to come from the players he has at his disposal.

Perhaps it is time for Enzo Maresca to shake up his back four. How he can do this to set up his Manchester City defence to better in terms of playing out from the back seems to have one or two options. The first would be to slide Josko Gvardiol across from left-back to centre-back. Gvardiol is Manchester City's best ball-playing defender. Starting Nico O'Reilly and Matheus Nunes at left and right-back would help City's build-up play. Also, Abdukodir Khusanov has been an option in possession. Khusanov isn't afraid to drive forward in possession and he can play a pass and isn't afraid to attempt a risky ball into midfield. Perhaps that tweak will help Manchester City's play out from the back.

Also, which Manchester City midfielders can drop deep to receive possession in the manner of Bernardo Silva and Rodri? The answer to this question cannot always be Elliot Anderson. He has enough work to do. Ayyoub Bouaddi can. He often drops deep in City's build-up. Rayan Cherki is another option. He did this last season at times under Pep Guardiola. However, Cherki's best work is done in the final third. Perhaps placing this responsibility on Enzo Fernandez may unlock his game for Manchester City. Phil Foden can drop into midfield to link up City's play from the back to midfield. The options are there for Enzo Maresca. He has to find the right balance to counter what Manchester City's opponents are doing when City attempt to play out from the back.

What was a strength for Manchester City for so long under Pep Guardiola is no longer so far this season. This is to be expected given the upheaval that has taken place at the club. Manchester City's play out from the back is an issue that is impossible to ignore. Their opponents are no longer afraid to get in their face when City attempt to build-up. Enzo Maresca may need to tinker with his set up to unlock an answer to a conundrum his team is facing. Whether or not he will remains to be seen.
