Germany 5-0 Netherlands: Initial Observations and Reactions

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

It took six games for Jürgen Klopp to revitalize the culture around the national team. Give the man his flowers.

On the other side of the pitch, Xavi Hernández ran his mouth all weekend. Instead of backing him up, his Dutch side laid an absolute goose egg and visibly quit during the final 30 minutes.

Tonight was the epitome of Florian Wirtz. Perhaps it was the tougher opponent or the short three-day turnaround, but he was a non-factor for 60 minutes. Then all of a sudden Wirtz produced 15 seconds of individual glory to complete change the tone of the match. Ten minutes later, Wirtz added a second goal for good measure and walked off the pitch full of confidence.

It is painful that Germany cannot find a better option at striker than Kai Havertz. He consistently fails to press effectively, create space in possession, or produce quality shots on target. When Jamal Musiala returns from injury, he has a very realistic shot at claiming the number nine position for his country.

Thanks for hanging out! Germany finishes the UEFA Nations League group stage in first place on a three-way tiebreaker. Next up for Germany is the quarterfinal stage (the draw is this Friday). Next up for BFW is Bayern Munich's Bundesliga match against Köln on Friday.

As expected, both teams brought a conservative game plan into the first half. Thanks to that rocket from Derry Scherhant, the onus falls to the Netherlands to find the next goal to regain the top spot in the group.

Germany's backline got sucked too high on a few occasions but the Netherlands failed to capitalize on the opportunities. In particular, Philipp Treu gave Cody Gakpo acres of free space to run and dribble.

Germany was much better in possession, especially in the midfield. Germany's wingers had very little success winning their dribbles against the Dutch outside backs. Clever move by Scherhant to feint and beat his man to the inside for the shooting angle.
