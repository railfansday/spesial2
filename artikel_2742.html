Broncos' updated 53-man roster following NFL trade deadline

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Following the 2026 NFL trade deadline, the Denver Broncos have set their 53-man roster going into their Week 10 bye.

Last week, Denver made a series of roster moves over the weekend. First, the Broncos placed defensive lineman Eyioma Uwazurike (shoulder) on injured reserve, ruling him out for at least four games. To fill Uwazurike's old spot on the active roster, Denver activated wide receiver Pat Bryant (ankle) from IR to the 53-man roster.

Second, the Broncos re-signed safety Taylor Rapp to the practice squad after releasing him from the active roster last Friday. To fill Rapp's old spot on the 53-man roster, Denver promoted cornerback Sean Fresch from the practice squad.

This week, the Broncos acquired linebacker Daiyan Henley in a trade with the Los Angeles Chargers. After that trade, Denver cut Fresch to make room for Henley.

In addition to the active roster, Denver has 17 players on the practice squad going into Week 10.
