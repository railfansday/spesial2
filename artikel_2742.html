Arroyo called up and set to miss Cruzeiro's Brasileirão match

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Forward Keny Arroyo was named in Ecuador's squad for the upcoming international window, announced this Sunday (8).

The call-up is expected to rule him out of Cruzeiro's next Brasileirão match, against Palmeiras at the Mineirão.

Ecuador have two friendlies scheduled for November. They face Costa Rica on the 13th and Bolivia on the 17th.
