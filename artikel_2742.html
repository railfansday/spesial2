Bills news: Previewing Buffalo’s prolific rushing attack against Jets’ porous run defense

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Buffalo Bills (7-1) take on the New York Jets (2-7) on Sunday afternoon in a battle of teams sitting at opposite ends of the AFC East.

What can fans expect in Week 10, when the Bills and Jets meet up for the 132nd time? Today's edition of Buffalo Rumblinks leads off by sharing the key matchups to watch and positional analysis as Josh Allen and the Bills look to beat the Jets for the 11th time in their last 13 meetings.

Check out the assessments below and then let us know your thoughts on the game in the comments section.

Linked below: Breaking down the key story lines to watch, including previewing how, in recent years, Buffalo's defense has been terrific in keeping the Jets from scoring a touchdown on their opening drive.

The Bills are looking to improve to 5-0 on the road this year, and on Sunday, they will have a great chance to exploit a Jets defense that has surrendered 140.5 rushing yards per game over the last month. Conversely, we explain why James Cook and the Bills will need to focus on ball security against a Jets defense that leads the league in forcing fumbles.

We also explore how, despite their record, the Jets have built up a sneaky-good offense, led by a talented trio of wide receivers who are great at creating separation downfield, and why Buffalo's secondary will need to be up to the challenge of containing the Jets' pass-catchers. Plus, we break down the potential impact safety Amani Hooker could have in his Buffalo debut; discover how the Bills have built a pipeline for producing talented offensive linemen; learn how linebacker Dorian Williams has blossomed into a valuable member of the defense; and more!
