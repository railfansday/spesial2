Ole Miss' Will Echoles runs back infamous peeing dog celebration in Egg Bowl

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

There's always some nonsense going on at the Egg Bowl. Add a defensive player being on the field for a touchdown, and you get a situation where no one knows how to act.

After Will Echoles was a lead blocker for an Ole Miss touchdown in the fourth quarter against Mississippi State to give the Rebels a 41-17 lead, the defensive lineman pulled out an old classic and lifted his leg in celebration, simulating a peeing dog.

Echoles, a junior, showed up to his news conference podium earlier this week with the Golden Egg, showcasing what this rivalry means to him. He got a moment to show up his hated in-state rivals, and he seized it.

It's continuing a tradition that, while not proud, is tradition all the same. Echoles joins DK Metcalf in 2017 and Elijah Moore in 2019, the latter of whom apologized after the celebration.

Moore's demonstration of the dog peeing celebration was particularly notorious, as it came on what would have been a game-tying touchdown. But the ensuing penalty moved the point-after attempt 15 yards back, and Ole Miss missed its attempt, resulting in a 21-20 Bulldogs celebration.

That was not the case on Friday, Nov. 27, with Ole Miss winning in dominant fashion. It gives the Rebels their fourth straight win in the rivalry, the longest such streak since 1983-86. While not fighting for a CFP berth at this point, they should end up with a good bowl draw, should they accept it.
