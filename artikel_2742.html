Recap, highlights: Lions crush Patriots in Germany, but lose Hutchinson

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Detroit Lions had one of their best performances of the year in Munich, Germany with a beatdown of the New England Patriots, but it came at a significant price. In the first quarter, Lions star defensive end Aidan Hutchinson celebrated a sack on Drake Maye and landed awkwardly on his leg. He went down grasping his knee, and he did not return to the game. We're providing updates on Aidan Hutchinsons injury here.

Coincidentally, Patriots starting quarterback Drake Maye was also injured on the play, and did not return with an ankle injury.

With Maye out of the game, the Patriots' offense had no life with Tommy DeVito behind center. After Maye's opening field goal drive, the Patriots went scoreless until midway through the fourth quarter—when Detroit was already four scores ahead.

The Lions offense had themselves a day. Amon-Ra St. Brown put on a show for his German fans, finding the end zone twice on 28 and 56-yard plays. Meanwhile, no one could stop Jahmyr Gibbs, who nearly put up over 200 rushing and receiving yards—finishing with 153 rush and 98 receiving, along with two touchdowns, before Detroit pulled him from the game.

With the 35-10 win, the Lions moved to 7-2 on the season, and now hold first place in the division. However, the injury to Hutchinson could be serious and will give this team tough questions about whether they can handle injuries to two key defensive players, after losing Jack Campbell two weeks ago.

The Lions won the deferred. So Drake Maye and the Patriots offense took the field first. They had very little trouble marching down the field. A couple of passes to Hunter Henry picked up 10 and 12 yards. Then, the Patriots pulled off a flea flicker for 26 yards to Mack Hollins.

The Lions had a chance to hold New England to three with a third-and-goal from the 14-yard line (after two Patriots penalties), and Aidan Hutchinson sacked Maye to end the drive. However, Hutchinson went down with an injury after celebrating.

Hutchinson was initially declared questionable to return with a knee injury.

The Patriots made the field goal to go up 3-0 Pats.

Apparently on the play, quarterback Drake Maye was also injured. He was listed as questionable to return with an ankle injury, but he was later downgraded to out. Tommy DeVito took for the rest of the game..

The Lions quickly struck back to take an early lead. On a third-and-long, Jared Goff found Amon-Ra St. Brown on an out route that he turned upfield for a 56-yard touchdown. 7-3 Lions.

The Patriots got to midfield quickly with a third-down, 30-yard pass interference penalty on Ennis Rakestraw. But on the subsequent third down, Lions rookie Derrick Moore sacked DeVito to end the drive.

Patriots punter Mitch Wishnowsky successfully pinned the Lions at their own 3-yard line with a nice punt.

The Lions were able to get out of the shadow of their own end zone with a third-and-1 conversion via a Goff sneak. Then Jahmyr Gibbs ripped off a 25-yard run. Sione Vaki followed that with an 8-yard run that earned another first down, as the first quarter came to an end.

Vaki caught a pass on the first play of the new quarter, made a defender miss, and picked up 17 yards. Two plays later, Gibbs picked up another 11 to move down into the red zone. Isaac TeSlaa found the end zone with an impressive grab for 12-yard touchdown and a 14-3 Lions lead.

Detroit's defense was able to quickly force a three-and-out, thanks to a 5-yard tackle for loss from DJ Wonnum on first down. On third-and-long, DeVito scrambled for just 6 yards, but Detroit went three-and-out on their own after Goff was sacked on third down by Milton Williams.

Taking over on their own 33-yard line following a poor Jack Fox punt, DeVito still couldn't get anything going. After a run for loss, and a sack from Alim McNeill, the Patriots faced a third-and-19.

A checkdown only gained a handful of yards and the Patriots punted back.

Gibbs kicked off the next drive with a 15-yard gain around the right side of the line. But on third-and-9, Goff tried to force one to St. Brown and he was blanketed by Christian Gonzalez, who picked off the pass at his own 39-yard line.

Again, the Patriots went three-and-out. Branch had a nice pass knockdown on a blitz, and then Rock Ya-Sin had perfect coverage to knock down a third-down attempt from DeVito.

Detroit avoided a three-and-out of their own with a wheel route to Gibbs that picked up 22 yards. Then, on third-and-10, Goff sidestepped pressure and found a wide-open Gibbs, who caught the short pass and picked up 50 yards.

The Lions went back to him for a 5-yard touchdown run to make it 21-3 Lions with 2:06 left in the half.

Bates put the kickoff out of bounds, giving the Patriots good field position (own 40) to run their two-minute offense. But that possession was quickly squandered. After a sack from DJ Wonnum, DeVito tried for force a ball into coverage and Roger McCreary was right there for the easy pick. He returned it to the Patriots' 42-yard line to get one more opportunity to score.

It took just two plays for the Lions to get into the red zone: a 7-yard pass to St. Brown and a toe-tapping 19-yard catch by Jameson Williams. After two more plays, the Lions had a first-and-goal from the 3-yard line with 20 seconds and no timeouts. But on third down, Goff made a critical mistake and was picked off by Kevin Byard.

The Lions opened the second half with a pair of play-action passes to Gibbs and Brock Wright, setting up a third-and-2. Goff couldn't connect with TeSlaa on the next play, forcing Detroit to punt after just three plays. The Lions defense, though, continued to shut down the Patriots and DeVito. After two unsuccessful runs, DeVito tried a deep shot to Kyle Williams, who couldn't come down with the pass and took a punishing hit from Lions safety Chuck Clark.

Detroit's offense finally started to click again while leaning on Gibbs and the running game. After Gibbs and Vaki got down into field goal range, the Lions called a nifty middle screen to St. Brown, and he took it to the house for his second touchdown of the game and a 28-3 Lions lead.

For the fifth time, the Patriots went three-and-out on offense. On third down, Lions safety Brian Branch had an outstanding pass breakup to force the punt.

Detroit moved to midfield, but a holding penalty on Tate Ratledge erased a big run and forced a third-and-12. Goff couldn't connect with LaPorta, and Detroit punted back with just over four minutes left in the quarter.

The Patriots finally put together a somewhat successful drive. On a third-and-3 play, DeVito hit A.J. Brown on a quick slant that he took for 22 yards down to Detroit' 47-yard line. Then, DeVito scrambled for an 11-yard gain, and tight end Mack Hollins picked up 6 yards to set up a second-and-4 from Detroit's 30-yard line as the game moved over to the final quarter.

It looked like the Patriots had finally found the end zone, but they were called for offensive pass interference on a screen play, setting up a third-and-18. However, the Patriots were given life with a pass interference call on Nick Whiteside that set them up at the 15-yard line. Three plays later, DeVito found Lan Larison for a 10-yard touchdown to make it 28-10 Lions with 12:40 left.

The Lions took over on their own 40-yard line after an out-of-bounds kick, with a chance to really put the game away. Jameson Williams helped with gains of 9 and 14, the latter of which came on a third-and-4. Then, Gibbs took a neat counter play 28 yards and to the house. 35-10 Lions.

With the game well in hand, the Patriots still couldn't move the ball consistently, and the Lions threw in their backups on offense.

Along the way, Moore picked up another sack for Detroit—his second of the game and fourth in the last two weeks.

Ultimately, the game ended with nothing too dramatic, and Detroit walked away 35-10 winners.
