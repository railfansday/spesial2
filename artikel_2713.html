Philadelphia Eagles Quickly Hire Fired New Orleans Saints Coach

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Philadelphia Eagles are having an unusual season by their standards. Typically, under general manager Howie Roseman and head coach Nick Sirianni, the Eagles have been jockeying for playoff positioning in November. Instead, they're currently in position to land a top-five pick in the 2027 NFL Draft instead.

Even though they started hot with a 2-0 record, the Eagles have lost seven in a row since. While they have a chance to emerge as an improved team coming out of their Week 10 bye, expectations are that the Eagles' season will continue on their downward trend. Though, now there's been a curious change to the Eagles' coaching staff.

T.J. Paganetti worked for the Eagles from 2013 to 2014, and then again from 2017 to 2024, most recently as their run game specialist/assistant offensive line coach. The 37-year-old won two Super Bowls with the Eagles. Yet, once Kellen Moore received the opportunity to be head coach of the New Orleans Saints, he took Paganetti with him as their run game coordinator.

However, after getting less than desirable results over the past two seasons, the Saints relieved Paganetti of his duties on Nov. 4. It didn't take him long to find a new opportunity.

According to ESPN's NFL insider Adam Schefter, the Eagles have now re-hired Paganetti in a consulting role. As Schefter included in his report, the Eagles previously set an NFL record with 3,866 rushing yards with Paganetti serving as their run game specialist/assistant offensive line coach.

Backed by coach Sirianni's trust, it may not be long until Paganetti secures a more specific role on the Eagles' coaching staff. But for now, he'll help wherever he sees fit.
