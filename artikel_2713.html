49ers likely won't cut Brandon Aiyuk until they have to

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

With a roster exemption that lasts until November 24 and with no requirement to pay him a penny until the exemption expires, the 49ers have shown no inclination to release receiver Brandon Aiyuk until they absolutely have to.

Nine days since Aiyuk was reinstated from the reserve/left squad list, the 49ers haven’t cut him. At this point, with only six days to go, the 49ers surely will kick the can as long as they can.

They gain nothing from delaying Aiyuk’s return, other than the relatively minor strategic advantage of keeping him away from a competitor. With all signs pointing to Aiyuk landing in Washington, he’s no threat to San Francisco’s playoff interests.

Of course, the receiver-needy Rams could get involved. Or perhaps the Vikings, who are currently without Jordan Addison. Or maybe the Seahawks, who don’t have a clear No. 2 option behind Jaxon Smith-Njigba.

If the 49ers are simply squatting on Aiyuk out of spite, that’s a bush-league move that creates bad karma. Let the guy get back in the league. They apparently still have the ability to pursue upwards of $13 million in bonus money from him; use that process as the vehicle for getting a pound of flesh.

Regardless, the 49ers apparently won’t be letting him go until the exemption expires. At that point, he’ll pass through waivers, his contract won’t be claimed, and he’ll become a free agent.
