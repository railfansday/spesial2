Lakers forward has ‘a lot of special things’ planned being back in LA

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

SAN ANTONIO — Ziaire Williams is no stranger to giving back to the community that raised him.

The Lakers forward has hosted a free annual youth basketball camp in his hometown of Lancaster the last five years.

Ziaire Williams takes pride in giving back to the local community. NBAE via Getty Images

He once again hosted a Thanksgiving community event at Inglewood High last week for about 150 kids from the Boys & Girls Clubs of Metro Los Angeles, which he's done every year since being the No. 10 pick in the 2021 NBA Draft.

But the community events have landed differently for Williams since joining the Lakers as a free agent this past summer.

Ziaire Williams shoots a 3-point basket. NBAE via Getty Images

"I've been talking with my guy Mike, who does my foundation stuff; we got a lot of special things [planned]," Williams said after the Lakers' shootaround Friday afternoon ahead of the game against the Spurs. "Definitely want to take advantage of this time while I can. I love the kids, love helping the kids. It's nothing like seeing smiling faces. So that's the first of many events that'll be taking place back home."

After starting his NBA career with the Grizzlies (2021-24) and playing for the Nets (2024-26), Williams is looking to take advantage of the opportunities that'll come with being closer to home.

"I don't want to share it yet until they're official," he said, "but, yeah, we got some nice little Christmas giveaways, some school stuff, a lot of good things at work, for sure."

Another perk of being back in Southern California for Williams was that he was able to spend time with family leading into Thanksgiving before the Lakers started their four-game road trip against the Spurs.

The Lakers will also play the Grizzlies, Rockets and Pelicans as part of the trip.

"Luckily for me, I'm from LA, so I was able to get a little early Thanksgiving Eve dinner," Williams said. "Shoutout to my chef. I ain't get grandma's cooking back home, but I'm grateful I was able to see my fam the day before Thanksgiving."
