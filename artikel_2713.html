Move over, volleyball: Central Texas girls basketball is off to a hot start this year

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

In what was another strong season for Central Texas high school volleyball, 10 Austin-area teams finished ranked in the top 10 in state polls, headlined by UIL state silver medalists Westlake and Wimberley.

The Chaps fell in four sets in Saturday's Class 6A Division II state final while Wimberley was swept by Sunnyvale in Friday's 4A DII championship match.

Westlake, Anderson girls top our Central Texas girls basketball power polls

Joining Westlake and Wimberley in the state's final four were Georgetown (5A DI) and Lake Travis (6A DI), both of whom fell in the state semifinals and finished third in state. Round Rock (6A DI) and Glenn (5A DI) finished fifth as regional finalists. And four more Central Texas teams - Rouse in 6A DII, Anderson in 5A DII, Weiss in 5A DII and Thrall in 3A DI also advanced to their regional tournaments.

The Chaps fell down by two sets, rallied for the third-set win and came within a service point of forcing a fifth set against Southlake Carroll, 25-20, 25-18, 21-25, 25-23. It was Southlake Carroll that beat Austin High for the 2025 state championship. Geralynn Byers-Veal led the Chaps with 10 kills, Annabel McDuffee had nine and both were named to the all-state tournament team. Sunny McWilliams and Clayton Kelly combined for 26 digs and Kyrksen Cook and Ellie Ogden combined for 34 assists.

Wimberley: The Texans got 20 kills from sophomore standout Trinity Laney, but overall struggled with their passing in a 25-23, 25-16, 26-24 loss to Sunnyvale. Bianca Mohr had 29 assists. Laney and Mohr made the all-state tournament team. It was Wimberley's second straight runner-up finish after winning the state in 2024. The Texans have gone 137-19 over the past three years.

Georgetown: Six-rotation standout Amelia Albright was named to the Class 5A DI all-state tournament team after leading the Eagles to the final four. She averaged a triple-double in assists, digs and kills during the playoffs.

State-ranked Westlake blasted perennial playoff team Vista Ridge 65-28 behind Peyton Hensle's 15 points. Molly Frazer scored 12 points, which also put her over the 1,000 career points mark, and Wynn Thorne added 11 points. The Chaps were able to use their complete bench and were still playing without star basketball recruit Byers-Veal, who was playing in the state volleyball finals in Garland.

Area 5A teams are hot, too: Georgetown remained undefeated with an impressive tournament championship in Forney. And Anderson has rolled off 13 straight wins to open the season. Bastrop is also undefeated.

Georgetown Eagles head coach Rhonda Farney and McNeil Mavericks head coach Matthew Butler chat before the game at the non-district-6A girls basketball game on Tuesday, Nov 21, 2023, at McNeil High School - Austin, TX. (John Gutierrez, John Gutierrez For American-Stat)

Georgetown went 4-0 to win the Forney Invitational, sparked by a banner weekend from Eagles freshman Alicia Strickland. Georgetown beat Lindale 38-36, then beat host Forney 58-56, then blasted Lewisville 51-20 and then Canton 44-35 for the title. Strickland scored 18 points against Lindale, 25 points against Forney, 20 points against Lewisville and 17 points and six assists in the championship game. Sophomore Tayza Peavy also had a strong weekend.

"We are young, but talented," Georgetown coach Rhonda Farney said. "We have much to work on."

Liberty Hill crushed Bowie 70-17 as Kallahan Erps scored 19 points. ... Vandegrift had a strong week with an early win over Class 5A power Glenn and then a dominant 57-24 win over Temple. Vipers guard Lexi Augustine set a school record with 12 steals and notched 18 deflections and six rebounds. Saania Khawaja led the Vipers with 19 points, three rebounds, four steals and three assists while Maddie Broxterman chipped in 18 points. ... Second year coach Kaidence Glenn has Anderson (13-0) on a roll. The Trojans' five most impressive wins - in order: 47-35 over Round Rock 47-35, 54-36 over Weiss, 63-27 over Killeen Ellison 63-27, 51-41 over Cedar Ridge 51-41 and 66-29 over McNeil.

Other local teams off to good starts are Bastrop (7-0), McCallum (8-2), Crockett (8-2), Liberty Hill (5-2), Weiss (8-4), Hays (7-2), Austin High (7-2), Wimberley (4-1) and Thorndale (4-0).
