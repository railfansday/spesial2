‘Just Stop It,’ ‘This Is Eternal Hate’ — NFL Fans React As Aaron Rodgers Gets Accused of Being ‘Selfish’ by Former Packers Teammate

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

In one of the most shocking sagas in recent memory, Thanksgiving week in the NFL was shaken up by the drama surrounding the Pittsburgh Steelers and Aaron Rodgers. After the Steelers benched him for Mason Rudolph, the four-time MVP went nuclear on the franchise, especially on head coach Mike McCarthy.

Rodgers' comments looked like a move that could burn every bridge he had left. One former teammate from his Green Bay Packers days found the whole thing selfish, but a lot of fans weren't buying his assessment.

During his appearance on "The Pat McAfee Show" on Wednesday, Rodgers went scorched earth on McCarthy. He accused the coach of sleeping through meetings and of shouting "One, two, three, Packers" after a huddle, as if he had forgotten which team he was on.

The Steelers have since released the quarterback. Reacting to the release and Rodgers' rant, Greg Jennings, a former Super Bowl champion and two-time Pro Bowler, was not happy with how Rodgers handled the whole thing.

Appearing on "First Things First," Jennings said, "I actually am surprised by a lot of it… It was very distasteful that he went out there and did it in this fashion. I'm not surprised at that."

His biggest issue was the spot Rodgers left the rest of the locker room in. Instead of focusing on the games ahead, Jennings said, the roster now has this hanging over its head.

"When you go out and you put all of this out there, you have an entire team that you are friends with, that you understand how the locker room works and how the media works. They have to answer every single question… If you truly care about more than just yourself, then you don't do this to everybody in that locker room that you just called brothers."

Many fans did not think Jennings was the right messenger, given his own history with Rodgers. Because of how things ended for him in Green Bay, they questioned whether he could be unbiased.

"I ain't heard the name Greg Jennings in like 5 years, bruh. This [expletive] only pops out when it's time to slander Aaron Rodgers," one fan wrote. "This is eternal hate."

Jennings also said he couldn't verify some of Rodgers' allegations based on his own time in Green Bay. However, he made clear that he wasn't accusing Rodgers of making them up and believed there was "some truth" to his account. That explanation didn't convince everyone.

Some fans questioned Jennings' credibility and pointed to other former players, including Ryan Clark, who had backed up parts of Rodgers' account.

"Ah, yes, Greg Jennings. The guy who didn't get the big deal from the Packers and then became a complete bum in the NFL after," one fan mocked. "Then you have guys like Ryan Clark who can't stand Rodgers basically saying it's all true. Funny how this all works."

Another fan added, "This hater is so sad. Literally all Steelers players agree with what Aaron said. Just stop it, we get it, the Packers let you go 13+ years ago," while one fan joked, "These dudes gonna have beef forever."
