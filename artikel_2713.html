Despite everything, the Packers still have their goals within reach

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

I'd like to say 2026 has been a uniquely stupid year of Packers football, but I don't know if that's true. Certainly there have been uniquely stupid aspects of it; I can't think of another year where the Packers have taken such drastic steps to find someone, anyone capable of playing replacement-level football on their offensive line during the season. I can't think of another season where the Packers have had a comparable cavalcade of injuries, except for maybe 2025. Maybe that's just modern football. I don't know. I can't think of another season where so many games have been, if not just affected, seemingly outright steered toward an outcome by the officials.

But handwaving all of that, let's just say it's been a stupid year. If not uniquely so, I think we can land on that.

And yet! Here the Packers sit entering Week 12 with everything they want still within reach.

If the season ended today, the Packers would make the playoffs, occupying the seventh seed in the NFC field, a position that will surely one day be named for Matt LaFleur. The Rams and Seahawks are hot on their tails, but a win this week — certainly very achievable against an injury-diminished Rams squad — would solidify their lead over both teams. They'd widen their lead over the Seahawks by strengthening their conference record and put a serious dent in the Rams' chances by taking ownership of the head-to-head tiebreaker.

And if the Packers make the playoffs, who knows what could happen? Right now, the playoff field would see them matched up against the Detroit Lions in the wildcard round. That, too, is an eminently winnable game. The Packers had the Lions all but dead to rights in Week 7 but for their semi-permanent kicking woes. And even so, if Josh Jacobs takes the small but significant step of simply catching the ball in the end zone, there's a very good chance they beat the Lions.

I think outside of the Vikings, the Packers could at least be competitive against pretty much any team in the NFC. There are certainly some talented teams in the field, but none of them have the borderline psychopathy of Brian Flores. If that's what it takes to beat the Packers (other than a kicker meltdown), you have to at least think the Packers have a chance.

And I suppose that's the point I'm trying to make here. The Packers still have virtually everything to play for and all of it is within their control. The NFC North title might be out of reach, but the playoffs are still fully achievable. The Packers control their own destiny on every front; keep winning and they're in the playoffs. Stumble and their odds grow more remote by the week.

But it's all up to the Packers. They've shown this year that they can beat anyone — or lose to just about anyone, too. Their weaknesses are almost entirely self inflicted, but the solutions to those weaknesses are all self-driven, too.

There are a lot of different ways you could spin this Packers season, but I think the ultimate story is that this is a team that had everything within its grasp, despite whatever weirdness befell them. And that fact may end up being the biggest indictment anyone can level against the 2026 Green Bay Packers.
