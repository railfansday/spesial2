Florida’s Jon Sumrall changing QBs again after Oklahoma train wreck

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Florida's Jon Sumrall changing QBs again after Oklahoma train wreck appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Jon Sumrall admitted that his Florida football team feels "lost" on offense after they were thumped 23-7 by the Oklahoma Sooners. As a result, Sumrall is making a quarterback change yet again.

Per Matt Zenitz of CBS Sports, Sumrall is set to start Aaron Philo against No. 11 Kentucky on Saturday. Philo had been benched in favor of Tramell Jones Jr., but he is back in after Jones' disastrous start against the Sooners.

Jones went 10 of 22 for 133 yards with no touchdowns and two interceptions against Oklahoma. That was a far cry from his impressive showing filling in for the injured Philo in the 27-24 win against Texas. He completed 63% of his passes for 155 yards with two touchdowns in that game. He had also acquitted himself well filling in for Philo in their 27-8 loss to Georgia. He completed 72% of his passes for 172 yards and had a rushing touchdown.

Jones' failure to take advantage of his opportunity has opened the door once again for Philo, who has been largely unspectacular as the team's starting quarterback this season. While he's completed 70% of his passes, he has 1,367 yards with nine touchdowns and five interceptions.

Granted, Philo was coming off a shoulder injury, which may have played a part in his poor start against Georgia. However, Philo won't be helped by the fact that Vernell Brown III will miss his sixth straight game due to injury. Before his injury, Brown had been on pace to be the Gators' top receiver. In four games, he has 17 catches for 271 yards and two touchdowns.
