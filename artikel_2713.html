Here’s where the Broncos stand right now in the division and the AFC playoff picture

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Josh Allen and the Buffalo Bills hosted their AFC rivals, Patrick Mahomes and the Kansas City Chiefs, on Thanksgiving Night in cold, rainy, and windy Buffalo and struggled to do much in the elements. However, in the end, the Bills outlasted the Chiefs and won on a 57-yard field goal by kicker Tyler Bass as the clock hit zero. It was a crazy back-and-forth game that featured sloppy play from both sides, but in the end, the Bills defeat the Chiefs during the regular season once again.

The Buffalo Bills improved to 10-1 on the season while the Kansas City Chiefs fell to 8-3 and remain the 5th seed in the AFC.

The Denver Broncos cannot secure the number one seed in the AFC this week; however, a victory over the imploding Pittsburgh Steelers on Black Friday will give them some much-needed cushion in the AFC West. Also, the Chiefs are in the midst of several tough games that include road games vs. the Los Angeles Rams and the Cincinnati Bengals and a home game vs. the 49ers. So, this loss will sting for them moving forward.

Looking ahead to the rest of the games this weekend, the Broncos play the Steelers on Friday, the Bengals play the Saints, the Jaguars play the Titans, and the Browns play on Sunday Night vs. the Raiders. Meanwhile, the Patriots, Texans, Raiders, and Ravens will look to get back on track and get themselves back into the AFC Playoff conversation.
