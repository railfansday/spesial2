This newsboard constantly demands 3% COLAs for pensions and for annuities. But they are vanishingly rare today, and too costly to fund, so insurance companies are not interested in offering them. You are not going to find one.
While it's true that TIAA has stopped offering COLA in their Traditional annuitization product, it's easy to find multiple other companies that offer a SPIA with COLA from 1% to 4%. Of course they cost more because insurance companies are not in business to lose money.
