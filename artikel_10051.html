One aspect of my work and personal life that has remained stubbornly able-bodied has been, surprisingly, my terminal use. Reading, manipulating, and editing the terminal has required keyboard and mouse use at every step:

I am fairly convinced there is no true voice first terminal experience-- this is something I am hoping to change. I feel like my approach is deeply inefficient and must be missing something obvious...? We've had terminals since before mice existed, surely there are better ways.

Friend, let me tell you about the fc command. fc -l lists the last few commands. fc and a number edits that command in your favorite $EDITOR and then when you save/exit it runs it!
