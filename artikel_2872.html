Bears Super Bowl Odds Surge As Simmons Trade Pays Off

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Chicago Bears are no longer a project team waiting for the future to arrive. They are a live threat right now, and the market knows it. After a dominant 34-17 win over the Seattle Seahawks on Monday night, the team's Super Bowl odds took a massive leap. This shift isn't just hype; it is based on cold, hard production from a player who arrived just days ago. Jeffery Simmons changed the entire feel of the defense before he even packed his bags in Tennessee.

Most trades take weeks to gel. The deal for Simmons broke that rule. On Oct. 29, he discovered a trade was pending and quickly picked up the defensive scheme. By the time he stepped on the field for his first primetime game in three seasons, he was already a force. He recorded a sack, six tackles, multiple tackles for loss, and a pass deflection. Bears coach Ben Johnson told ESPN that his impact was profound, noting that the team had a high-character locker room that welcomed him immediately.

The numbers back up the coach's praise. Chicago held the defending champion Seahawks to just one conversion on 11 third-down attempts. That 9.1% conversion rate stands as the fifth-lowest by any team this season. When Simmons was on the field, Seattle averaged only 3.0 yards per play. When he sat on the bench, that number jumped to 13.3 yards per play. The difference is not subtle. It is a gap that wins championships.

The offense did its part to seal the deal, led by quarterback Caleb Williams. He tossed a touchdown on the opening drive, tallying 247 yards and hitting 71% of his passes. He committed no turnovers and took just one sack. This efficiency was crucial against one of the NFL's stingiest defenses. Williams threw for a score to Luther Burden and set up three short touchdown runs by D'Andre Swift. The result was a blowout that left Seattle reeling.

Sam Darnold struggled to find any rhythm against this new Chicago front. He completed less than 50% of his passes when Simmons was on the field. The Seahawks suffered their third loss in four games, breaking a 30-game run in which they had never lost by more than four points. This streak ranked as the fourth longest for any team during the Super Bowl era. The Bears snapped it with authority, forcing the league to pay attention.

Seahawks trail Bears 34-17 with 4:41 to play. Seattle has gone 30 straight games in regular season and playoffs without losing by more than 4 points. Only other teams with streaks that long in Super Bowl era: Chiefs 40, 11/1/2021-10/22/2023Packers 33, 9/12/2010-12/11/2011…

Chicago is now 6-2 and sitting squarely in the NFC playoff hunt. They have the best third-down defense in the league and a quarterback who is finally playing with total confidence. The Super Bowl odds jumped because the team finally found its missing piece. Simmons proved he can elevate the whole group from day one. If this defense stays this hot, the Bears are a team no one wants to face in January.
