Commanders injury updates: Four questionable vs Bengals on Monday

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Washington Commanders got some bad news at the end of Friday's practice. Starting cornerback Amik Robertson suffered an ankle injury on the last day of practice. He did not practice on Saturday and the Commanders listed him as questionable on the final injury report for Monday night's game against the Cincinnati Bengals.

Losing Robertson would be significant. He's been Washington's best and most consistent defensive back. His competitiveness sets the tone for the entire team. According to head coach Dan Quinn, Robertson could be a game-time decision on Monday.

"So, yeah, [Amik's injury] on the last play of the day [Friday], so it was sore today, so we held [him] out. I'll monitor over the next, you know, 48 hours," Quinn said.

In some good news, no players were ruled out for Monday night's game. However, three others were listed as questionable:

Croskey-Merritt had his 21-day window opened last week. Quinn indicated on Saturday that things were trending positively for Washington's starting running back to return on Monday.

White has handled the bulk of running back duties while Croskey-Merritt is out, but Quinn seemed to indicate that he believed White could play against the Bengals.

Finally, Patrick is listed as questionable, but practiced fully on Saturday and Quinn said he'd be back in the starting lineup. He suffered a concussion two weeks ago against the Rams and missed last week's win over the Giants. Former starter Nick Allegretti played well in Patrick's absence.
