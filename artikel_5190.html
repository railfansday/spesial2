Mutsamudu, Comoros, November 3, 2026 – MEXC, a pioneer in 0-fee digital asset trading, announced an upgrade to Futures Earn. This product helps retail traders use their capital more efficiently by allowing eligible futures account balances to earn interest while users continue trading. The upgrade is designed to improve the overall user experience and make the product easier to understand and use.

Futures Earn involves several aspects, including interest calculation rules and reward calculation. To help users better understand and navigate Futures Earn throughout their user journey, MEXC has made three key improvements to the user experience.

Dedicated futures earning products remain relatively uncommon across the industry. Among the offerings currently available, some are limited to a single stablecoin, require minimum trading volumes, or operate as time-limited campaigns. MEXC Futures Earn combines broader asset coverage, higher earning potential, and lower barriers to participation. It supports eligible USDT- and USDE-margined futures pairs, with no minimum trading volume requirement or fixed end date. USDT-margined pairs offer a 5% base APR plus up to 15% in additional rewards, bringing the maximum APR to 20%, while USDE-margined pairs offer a 4.75% base APR. Eligible futures account balances of at least 100 USDT automatically accrue interest hourly, with rewards settled daily, while remaining available for trading.

Institutional market participants have long prioritized capital efficiency and typically have access to more sophisticated tools for managing and deploying capital. MEXC believes retail traders should also have greater opportunities to improve how they use their funds. MEXC remains committed to putting retail traders at the heart of its product and service development, offering innovative products and services such as 0-fee trading, deep liquidity, exclusive VVIP benefits, and Futures Earn to optimize trading costs, trade execution, and capital utilization. Looking ahead, MEXC will continue to break down barriers and empower more retail traders to achieve greater capital efficiency and unlock more value from their capital.

To learn more about MEXC Futures Earn, visit the official product page.

Founded in 2018, MEXC is a leading global multi-asset trading platform built as your 0-fee gateway to infinite opportunities. Serving users across 170+ markets, MEXC provides simple and efficient access to crypto, stocks, tokenized assets, derivatives, and a growing range of TradFi-linked opportunities through one account and one gateway.

With 0 trading fees, deep liquidity, broad asset coverage, and a high-performance trading experience, MEXC is designed for retail users who want to discover earlier, act faster, and trade with fewer barriers. As crypto and traditional finance continue to converge, MEXC is committed to making global opportunities more accessible, helping users trade freely and MEXCmize every opportunity.
