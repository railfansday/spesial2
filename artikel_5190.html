One Polymarket user is betting heavily that more revelations about former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang are coming. On Sunday, the trader put $15,127 on “Yes” in a contract asking if a fourth affair will be reported by December 31, buying in at 39% odds. A correct call would bring in roughly $24,083 in profit. Traders have grown more cautious since the market opened, with the probability now at about 40%, down from 57% last week. Activity remains strong, with $80,072 traded in the past day and $286,675 in total. A separate outcome for a report by November 20 sits at 12%. Three relationships have been reported so far, including Huang’s own admission and reports about healthcare vice president Kimberly Powell and a second Nvidia employee. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has since urged Nvidia to hire an outside CEO, Axios reported, while Nvidia plans an all-staff meeting on November 18. The bet comes as Nvidia prepares to report third-quarter earnings after the US market closes on Tuesday, its first results under interim CEO Colette Kress, with revenue expected near $108 billion.
