Nicole Scherzinger and Thom Evans Split After 7 Years of Dating

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Nicole Scherzinger and fiancé Thom Evans have ended their relationship after seven years together.

“It is with a heavy heart that I share that Nicole and I are no longer together,” Evans, 41, wrote via his Instagram Story on Friday, November 27. “After seven years of love, commitment and believing in a future together, this is an incredibly difficult time.”

He continued: “Whilst this is not the outcome I had ever hoped for, I respect Nicole’s decision and genuinely wish her every happiness in the next chapter of her life. Thom x.”

Scherzinger, 48, for her part, has yet to publicly address the breakup.

The former couple met in 2019 on the set of The X Factor: Celebrity. They began dating later that year but didn’t go public with their romance until a Golden Globe Awards afterparty red carpet in 2020.

“It wasn’t even in my thought process to try it on with Nicole because it would have been unprofessional, and she’s so lovely and stunning in every possible way,” Evans told The Sun in 2021. “I was just like, ‘She’s out of my league.'”

He added: “It was only after [my band Try Star] sang an awkward performance of ‘Swing Low, Sweet Chariot’ and she kindly saved us in the show that we, as a band, went up to her dressing room to thank her. That was the first time I met her, and I’ve never looked back.”

Three years later, the former rugby player popped the question. Scherzinger teased that the pair were planning on having a wedding in her native Hawaii with a reception in Europe.

“The reception will be somewhere in Europe. Beyond that, I haven’t even thought about anything,” she said during an interview with radio host Chris Evans in 2023. “We’re in love with Portugal obviously, so I think we would have a reception there maybe. I didn’t know Portugal before Thom introduced me to it. I’m in love with it.”

Scherzinger also opened up about their plans to start a family.

“I’ve never shied away from that. I can’t wait,” Scherzinger told The Sunday Times in 2024. “It’s like the clock is ticking. I want to have a baby but work calls. But I’m going to have to make time because, yes, I cannot wait to have children.”

Scherzinger noted that her mom understood why parenthood wasn’t something the duo was rushing, adding, “No, because my mother knows how hard I work and how passionate I am about what I do. She is a faithful woman and she just knows, everything in God’s timing.”
