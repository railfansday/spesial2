Minimum wage has not gone up with inflation,
and the federal minimum wage has been $7.25/hr since July 2009.

Adjusting for inflation, that would be $11.36/hr. now.

Inflation is also bad for those on SS, because the calculation
for how much SS benefits are taxed depends on dollar amounts
set in the 1980's, and never adjusted for inflation.

Also, there is not one figure for inflation.

Inflation in the average price for the things that I buy is not the
same as inflation for the things you buy. We just have some
ball park averages, like CPI-U and CPI-W etc.

It's also hard for me to get used to new prices for things I haven't bought in a while.

I had done repairs done, and I had to make a calculation to believe
that I was not being ripped off,because the bill was so large.
