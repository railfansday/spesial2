Amtrak Completely Halted in Washington, D.C. by Municipal Order

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Washington, D.C., has halted all Amtrak trains going in and out of the city ... and reports indicate it's because a person was run over in a different state.

Here's the deal ... on Monday afternoon, Amtrak posted on X that service around D.C. has stopped due to local municipal officials placing a hold on the tracks.

The company stated the municipal government issued the hold ... and it couldn't say when trains would start running again. Amtrak also didn't give a reason for the hold.

However, a local D.C. NBC transportation reporter, Adam Tuss, got to the bottom of the story ... revealing that a trespasser came into contact with an Amtrak train heading from D.C. to New York City. The trespasser's condition is unknown at this time.
