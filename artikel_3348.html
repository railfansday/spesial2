British Independent Film Awards: ‘Elsinore’ Leads Nominations

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Simon Stone’s Elsinore, starring Andrew Scott, leads this year’s British Independent Film Awards nominations with 12 nods. Scroll down for the full list of nominees.

Elsinore earned nominations for Best British Independent Film, Best Director for Simon Stone, Best Screenplay for Simon Beresford, Best Lead Performance for previous winner Andrew Scott, and Best Supporting Performance for Billie Piper. The film also received seven craft nominations, including Best Cinematography, Best Casting, Best Costume Design, Best Editing, Best Make-Up & Hair Design for Nadia Stacey, Best Original Music, and Best Production Design for Helen Scott. Clio Barnard’s I See Buildings Fall Like Lightning trails with eight nominations, including Best British Independent Film, Best Director, Best Screenplay for Enda Walsh, and Best Ensemble Performance. Craft nominees for the film include past BIFA winners and nominees Shaheen Baig (Casting), Connie Farr (Music Supervision) and Maya Maffioli (Editing). Watch on Deadline

Ashley Walters’ debut feature Animol has seven nominations, including Best Debut Director Film for Walters and Breakthrough Producer for Tom Hawkins. The film’s acting leads, Tut Nyuot and Sekou Diaby, have two nominations each: Best Lead and Best Supporting Performance, alongside Breakthrough Performance Netflix.

This year’s nominees for Best International Independent Film are Ryusuke Hamaguchi’s All of a Sudden, Lee Chang-dong’s Possible Love, Javier Ambrossi and Javier Calvo’s La Bola Negra, Jordan Firstman’s Club Kid, and Marine Atlan’s La Gradiva.

BIFA craft winners will be announced on 24 November on BIFA’s social channels. The full BIFA ceremony takes place on 6 December at Roundhouse in Camden.
