Air Canada Cancels All Flights To 3 International Destinations: See Full List Now

Canada's largest airline, Air Canada, has cut three international routes since mid-year, axing its services to New York, Charleston, and its soon-to-be short-lived service to Singapore.

Air Canada operates an extensive network of international and domestic flights from its three main hubs in Canada, Vancouver, Toronto and Montreal, with a fleet of more than 240 aircraft.

Air Canada became the only North American airline to fly to Singapore Changi Airport (SIN) in April 2024, operating the route with the Boeing 787-9 Dreamliner from Vancouver International Airport (YVR). The service is set to be axed on January 24, with its last pushback from Vancouver for the Lion City. The return flight from Singapore will depart on January 26.

The route, served four times weekly, was the airline's longest flight and the only nonstop link between Canada and Singapore. The airline has suggested that the route failed to meet commercial sustainability targets, despite it running for three years.

Air Canada's withdrawal leaves Canada with no nonstop links to Singapore; the only other airlines operating nonstop between North America and Singapore are Singapore Airlines and United Airlines (both served from the mainland US).

Vancouver International Airport (YVR) –Singapore Changi Airport (SIN)

January 24 (Vancouver), January 26 (Singapore)

Air Canada terminated its nonstop flights to John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK) over the summer, suspending service on June 1. This move removed up to four daily rotations, including two flights from Toronto and two from Montreal. The airline's withdrawal from the airport saw a loss of up to 480 seats per day.

Toronto Pearson International Airport (YYZ) –John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK)

Montréal–Trudeau International Airport (YUL) –John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK)

For the Star Alliance carrier, its decision to stop flying to New York-JFK came as the airline consolidated its services to LaGuardia Airport (LGA) and Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR), and was influenced by the severe spike in the cost of jet fuel following the US and Iran conflict and the blocking of the Strait of Hormuz.

This now sees Air Canada operate all its nonstop services to New York, to LaGuardia and Newark, where the airline provides services from six Canadian cities, equating to 34 daily departures.

Flight tallies show sudden withdrawals from midwest and northeast airports after years of steady Toronto and Montreal links.

Charleston International Airport (CHS) lost its seasonal nonstop flights to Canada when the airline axed its service on September 6. The airline had launched the service from Toronto in March 2024, and despite starting with up to 200 annual departures, this dropped to 92 flights (up to six weekly) before the summer seasonal route was eventually axed.

Toronto Pearson International Airport (YYZ) – Charleston International Airport (CHS)

The service to South Carolina has now been dropped completely, as part of a five-route cut for the 2027 summer, including select services that were Toronto Billy Bishop Airport (YTZ) to Boston, Montreal to Seattle, Vancouver to Nashville, and Vancouver to Raleigh.

This leaves Charleston with just one international destination on its network map, Cancun, where Breeze Airways operates the route seasonally.
