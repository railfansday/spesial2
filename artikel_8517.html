Personal Consumer Issues • Re: Water dispenser on GE GSS23WSTASS side by side refrigerator not working

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

We had a GE side-by-side for years that constantly had the same problem. The issue was that the line would freeze between the ice maker and the door dispenser. Usually the ice maker would still work, but no water from the front. Pretty much every vacation of more than a few days would trigger it, when it wasn't being used. We tried a lot of the tricks and tools, but usually, we wound up leaving the fridge open for a few hours(Moved everything to another fridge or a cooler) to thaw it out.

Ultimately, my research led me to see this was a fairly common problem, not just for this model, and ultimately an inherent risk/flaw when the water and ice dispensers are built into the door. We ultimately bought a fridge with the water dispenser inside the fridge and the ice maker in the bottom freezer section, no external access. Now we have to open one fridge door to get water, and open the freezer and scoop out ice when needed. I wondered if it would feel inconvenient, but once we got used to it, opening the door for water isn't even noticeable. Opening the freezer and scooping out our own ice actually works better for getting the exact amount we want, versus trying to time the old dispenser in the door just right.
