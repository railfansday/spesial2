1 Unstoppable Stock to Buy Before It Joins NVDA and AAPL in the $1 Trillion Club

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

AMD looks poised to reclaim a $1 trillion market cap as Nvidia turmoil creates an opportunity.

By Marc Guberti – Nov 19, 2026 at 7:32PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Advanced Micro Devices ›

Advanced Micro Devices (AMD -7.72%) briefly became a $1 trillion company in September, but it lost that milestone after a prolonged artificial intelligence (AI) stock correction. However, the chipmaker is set to rejoin Nvidia (NVDA -3.30%) and Apple (AAPL -0.92%) as a trillion-dollar company. These are the catalysts that can drive the stock higher.

AMD has been viewed as the No. 2 graphics processing unit (GPU) chipmaker for several years, with Nvidia comfortably holding on to the top spot. However, Nvidia suddenly faces significant uncertainty with former CEO Jensen Huang out of the company and angling for a comeback.

Nvidia currently doesn't seem receptive to a reunion, and that can cost the company. Some investors argue that Huang's departure will make it difficult for Nvidia to continue innovating despite the competitive edge it has already obtained.

The AI industry is moving rapidly, and leadership complexities can cost Nvidia valuable time. It can be the difference between missing out on the latest technology and watching hyperscalers turn to alternatives.

This scenario positions AMD as the chipmaker with the most to gain from the confusion, and the company has been taking major steps to close the gap. For instance, AMD is making its chip fleet more attractive by making it much easier for developers to port existing Nvidia CUDA applications to its HIP platform.

Nvidia's CUDA was previously a major moat. Moving to another software essentially meant rewriting vast amounts of code, but that's no longer the case. If AMD can win over more businesses with an easier migration, it creates more headaches for a company already full of them.

Although Nvidia has captured most of the headlines throughout the AI boom, AMD has been quietly accelerating its growth. Data center revenue surged by 97% year over year in the third quarter, while overall revenue reached $13.4 billion, up by 45% year over year. That also represents a 16% sequential boost.

AMD CEO Lisa Su told investors that demand for its AI products is still accelerating. The chipmaker also remains confident that revenue growth will surge in the coming years, along with higher net profit margins.

AMD trades at a 30 forward price-to-earnings (P/E) ratio, which may be higher than what some investors are willing to handle. However, this same company almost doubled its net income year over year. AMD wrapped up the quarter with an 18.2% net profit margin, and if it can get closer to Nvidia's 62% net profit margin, the stock should have an easy time reclaiming a $1 trillion market cap and marching toward new highs.
