Pochettino Wants Man Utd Chiefs to Sign Balogun, He's Ready to Explode

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Manchester United are ready to back new manager Mauricio Pochettino in the January transfer window.

Pochettino officially landed in Manchester on Wednesday to officially take charge of Man United, having managed the United States to a 2-0 win against Haiti in the early hours of Wednesday morning, UK time.

Now, the Argentine will get to work to try and pick up a positive result when the Red Devils travel to Anfield to face Liverpool in the former Tottenham boss' first match in charge of the club.

United have made a terrible start to the Premier League season and lie in the relegation zone after 10 matches, winning just one of those at home to newly-promoted Ipswich Town.

They could actually be bottom of the table by the time they kick off on Merseyside, should Sunderland register at least a draw at Aston Villa and Crystal Palace beat Coventry City under new manager Thomas Frank.

Pochettino knows he must use his current squad to get out of trouble until the New Year, with the potential that Luke Shaw, Harry Maguire and Marcus Rashford could all be available at Anfield after various injury issues.

Beyond that, it appears as though INEOS are ready to back the 54-year-old in the market, as they consider strengthening three different positions.

According to GIVEMESPORT sources, United are looking at signing a new centre-back in January with Fulham's Calvin Bassey a player who is appreciated.

The Nigeria international is able to play in central defence and at left-back, another position the Red Devils want to strengthen, and GMS has also revealed that Chelsea right-back Malo Gusto is 'one to watch' ahead of January.

As well as the defence, United chiefs are also looking at bringing in a new striker for Pochettino, given they have navigated the majority of this season without Benjamin Sesko because of injury, which has seen the likes of Bryan Mbeumo, Matheus Cunha and even Bruno Fernandes fill in as makeshift number nines.

United hold an interest in Leeds United's Dominic Calvert-Lewin, although GMS revealed that the centre-forward is set to sign a new contract to stay at Elland Road instead.

Meanwhile, Monaco's Folarin Balogun could be on the move in January with the Ligue 1 outfit needing funds, and there will be links with United given Pochettino knows him so well and got the best out of him in a United States shirt.

According to the Telegraph, the Argentine head coach is actually ready to push United to sign the 25-year-old in the New Year, according to US sources.

There would be some uncertainty over whether Balogun would be a hit at Old Trafford if he joined mid-season, but there won't be many better striker options on the market in January than the former Arsenal man.

What is clear from Balogun's career so far is that he has largely been prolific wherever he has been, from Arsenal youth sides to the United States national team.

The 25-year-old, who was labelled 'insane' by teammate Christian Pulisic, is fast and has shown he is capable of spectacular finishes, and he can give the Red Devils what they have been missing in recent months in the absence of Sesko.

His movement is good, and you'd hope he could thrive from the creativity of Fernandes and others, and he is also clinical.

The other big benefit of signing Balogun is that, because he knows Pochettino's methods and tactics so well, he shouldn't need much time to bed in at Old Trafford.

Pochettino clearly has confidence in him and that has boosted the striker's confidence in front of goal, and he would be ready to explode at the Theatre of Dreams if he did make the move in January.

With Monaco needing the money, it will be interesting to see how much the 25-year-old will cost in January, given he came close to completing a £40m move to Everton on deadline day in the summer.

From United's point of view, a loan with an option to buy would probably be their preferred deal, but Monaco would likely want an obligation to buy to agree to that structure of deal.

Now we must see if Pochettino and United chiefs agree on the direction they should go in when it comes to identifying their number one striker target.
