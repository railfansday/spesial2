Cathie Wood Says AI Isn't Even in the First Inning Yet of Its Long-Term Technological Revolution, Pointing to Palantir's 123% U.S. Revenue Growth as Evidence the Market Is Underestimating the Trade. Is Her Concentrated AI Bet Still the Right One?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Ark Invest's ETFs are heavily invested in AI-related stocks.

Late last month, Ark Invest founder Cathie Wood told a Bloomberg interviewer that she doesn’t even believe the artificial intelligence (AI) trade is in its first inning yet — she thinks the sector is “still in the parking lot before we entered the baseball game.” To support that upbeat thesis, she pointed to Palantir's (PLTR -2.21%) 123% year-over-year U.S. revenue growth as evidence of the long-term opportunity in AI. She has also put a lot of money behind that statement through Ark’s exchange-traded funds.

Most Ark Invest funds focus heavily on AI stocks. Although it's been a bumpy month for many of them, those companies have been trending higher in recent years, and many have improved their fundamentals.

AI isn't just chatbots, but looking at how AI models have evolved over time offers hints at what can happen to the broader industry. When ChatGPT first came out, it had no connection to the live internet, which meant it was outdated on current events. You also weren't able to upload images, PDFs, and other documents to ChatGPT and ask it questions about those files.

Anthropic's Claude was similar in its early days, but it has turned into an autonomous digital coworker. Claude Code can create entire apps and websites from scratch just with a few prompts.

ChatGPT and Claude are still evolving, introducing new features that have attracted many consumers and business owners. The evolution of this technology will increase the demand for various inputs, including AI chips, optical cables, liquid-cooling solutions, and electricity.

Furthermore, customers and businesses will end up with higher expectations as this technology evolves. Within the next five to 10 years, that could lead to new features and capabilities that make current versions of ChatGPT and Claude look archaic.

The artificial intelligence boom won't end with software models. Humanoid robots and self-driving vehicles are among the next big opportunities. Self-driving vehicles are already in limited use, with Waymo establishing itself as the clear leader in the robotaxi space.

Tesla (TSLA +0.10%) is working to gain ground with its Cybercabs, which are now in limited production, but its Optimus humanoid robots could become a lucrative business segment for it once AI robotics technology is ready for broad commercialization. Each of these products could command significant demand and change how people engage with the world.

These examples show that the possibilities of artificial intelligence resemble those of the early internet. Just as many investors in the 1990s underestimated the impact the internet would have on our lives, a similar pattern may be taking shape with AI. That seems to be part of the reason why Wood is heavily focused on AI stocks throughout Ark Invest funds.

Agentic AI is another hot category within the AI umbrella. It's one area where Palantir excels, which helps explain how the stock has crushed the S&P 500 over the past five years. This technology can perform various tasks for consumers and businesses that save time and money.

Innovation is good and can get people excited about the future, but investors must still consider a company's fundamentals and long-term opportunities. Not every humanoid robot company, self-driving car manufacturer, or AI model provider will produce positive returns for its investors over the long term.

Analyzing these businesses’ fundamentals helps investors assess the opportunities more rationally. However, their fundamentals have actually improved a lot. Chipmakers continue to report exceptional growth rates. The robust results and enthusiasm for more AI growth in 2027 do not align with the sector’s recent stock price performance.

It's these types of setups that create asymmetric risk opportunities that can position investors to multiply their wealth. However, they also require investors to accept a lot of volatility. Wood's Ark Innovation ETF (ARKK -0.83%) has been quite volatile, experiencing many downturns of 20% or more.

The fund has not recently experienced the same upward momentum it enjoyed from the mid-2010s to 2021, after which it slumped by more than 80% from its February 2021 peak to a December 2022 cyclical low. And despite some promising periods during the intervening years, it’s still down by more than 50% from that peak. But its heavy AI stock exposure gives this fund a shot at getting back on track to deliver positive long-term returns.
