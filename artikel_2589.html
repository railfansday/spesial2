Hamilton won't accept what Leclerc 'normalised' at Ferrari says ex-F1 team boss

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Lewis Hamilton refuses to accept the strategic mistakes that Charles Leclerc has 'normalised' at Ferrari, according to ex-Formula 1 team principal Otmar Szafnauer.

Ferrari's hierarchy this season has faced much criticism for its handling of their two drivers who have had a number of on-track clashes, most notably on the opening lap at both the Italian and Mexico City Grands Prix. The drivers resolved tensions during a chat before the Brazilian Grand Prix weekend, although team principal Fred Vasseur insists that the guidelines between the two drivers at Ferrari remain the same.

However, former Alpine and Aston Martin team principal Szafnauer, and former Ferrari race engineer Rob Smedley, have addressed the team's strategic mistakes while speaking on the High Performance Racing podcast. In Singapore, Hamilton refused to pit when Ferrari called him in, suggesting a lack of faith in the decisions made by the team, even though he ultimately dropped from P2 to P6 as a result.

Former Ferrari race engineer Smedley said: "If you look at, say Mercedes and Red Bull's race management against Ferrari's over the last four years, it will have been better."

In response, Szafnauer said: "It could very well be that Lewis came from an environment where they got it right most of the time. Leclerc only knows Ferrari. So, Lewis is not venting his frustration but saying, 'Hey, I know it can be done better', whereas Charles is used to it, normalised [it]."

Leclerc is in his eighth season with Ferrari, with the team's strategic calls consistently coming under question during his time with the team. Hamilton meanwhile joined Ferrari in 2025, after 12 years with Mercedes in which he won six of his seven drivers' titles, and the team claiming eight consecutive constructors' titles.

Smedley believes that Hamilton's recent grievances towards his team come off the back of 'two years of frustration'.

He said: "If you've got a team which is really working well together, you know, brilliant people, brilliant technology, brilliant tools, you've got to be getting it right eight out of 10 times. And I think he's come from an environment [at Mercedes] where they've got it right eight out of 10 times. So, this is now his frustration that he's wearing on his sleeve."

Meanwhile, Smedley has also dismissed claims thatHamilton has received preferential treatment at Ferrari from chairman John Elkann.

Speaking on the High Performance podcast, the Brit said: "There aren't factions inside Ferrari. I can tell you that. I know them. John Elkann is not favouring Lewis."

Smedley explained that Ferrari's internal structure places Elkann at the top, followed by team principal Fred Vasseur and Lorenzo Giorgetti, who oversees the commercial side of the organisation. He emphasised that the drivers are part of the same system rather than operating within competing groups, with everyone ultimately expected to contribute to Ferrari's overall objectives.

"There is a hierarchy, which is John Elkann, Fred, and then the two drivers, and everybody just works for the team, including John. So, there are no factions there. Let's not make stuff up."
