NFL predictions: Jets look for first AFC East victory under Aaron Glenn against tanking Dolphins

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The New York Jets are on the road in Miami, Florida to take on the Miami Dolphins in the Jets' 12th game of the 2026 season. The Jets will be looking to get their first win against an AFC East opponent in the Aaron Glenn era, as well as some payback for their loss to the Dolphins earlier this season.

These two teams last met in Week 7, when a then winless Dolphins team never trailed in an embarrassing 28 – 25 Jets loss. The Dolphins have won seven of the last eight and 17 of the last 21 games between these two teams. The Jets have not won in Miami since 2014. The Dolphins lead the overall series 64 – 57 – 1.

The Dolphins are clearly tanking this season in hopes of a top draft pick in 2027. They have won just one game all season. Unfortunately, that one win came at the Jets' expense. The Jets hope to return the favor on Sunday against a Dolphins team that will be starting backup quarterback Kyle McCord. If the Jets can't find a way to beat Kyle McCord, well, the 2027 NFL draft can't come soon enough.

Let's make a few predictions for the game on Sunday.

The Jets hold the Dolphins offense under 180 passing yards and under 270 total yards.

The Jets defense stifles the Dolphins' running game and holds them under 90 rushing yards.

No Dolphins receiver has more than 50 receiving yards in the game.

Geno Smith passes for 250+ yards and 0 interceptions.

The Jets cruise to a double digit victory.
