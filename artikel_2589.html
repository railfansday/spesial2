Chuba Hubbard receives day off Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Panthers RB Chuba Hubbard (non-injury) did not participate in practice Wednesday, Nov. 18.

Why It Matters: Hubbard was receiving the day off and should return to the practice field in the next day or two. He is coming off an outstanding Week 10 performance where he posted 172 total yards. He will continue to receive plenty of touches during games and should be a No. 1 running back in fantasy leagues.
