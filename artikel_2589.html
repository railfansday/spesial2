The Outlook - Week 12 (GB-LAR, CHI-DET, PHI-DAL, KC-BUF, DEN-PIT)

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Welcome in, folks! It's Week 12 and we're here during Thanksgiving week, wishing all of you reading this a happy, healthy and, most importantly, safe holiday!

Thanksgiving week in the NFL always feels like a bit of a whirlwind. This week features a game on Wednesday (Packers-Rams), three games on Thursday (Bears-Lions, Eagles-Cowboys and Chiefs-Bills) plus an afternoon game on Black Friday, pitting the Broncos against the Steelers. It's also an unofficial "two-minute warning" of sorts for the fantasy football regular season, as there are just a couple of weeks left for most fantasy gamers. We certainly hope that you're in a good position to make the playoffs and be in the hunt for your league championship by the time you're reading this column.

We're already reflecting on some of the main fantasy beats of the season and the major storylines, whether it's the tight end position being a major wash, the huge rookie running backs dominating like Emmett Johnson and Jonah Coleman or the major quarterback injuries to players like Jalen Hurts, Lamar Jackson and Drake Maye. We'll certainly be talking about a lot of these storylines closer to the new year, but just enjoy this week however you choose to. Just don't neglect your fantasy lineups! We have all 32 NFL teams playing this week before we hit the final two bye weeks of the season. After that, it's fantasy playoff time!

Block 1: GB-LAR, CHI-DET, PHI-DAL, KC-BUF, DEN-PIT

Block 2: NO-CIN, TEN-JAC, NYG-IND, NYJ-MIA, ATL-MIN, BAL-HOU

"The Outlook" will give you the details, stats, analysis, recommendations and everything else you need to make informed fantasy football decisions. Every fantasy-relevant player on each team will be profiled in this article. We want everyone reading this to bring home championships in their fantasy leagues!

I'm incredibly excited to bring this article to all of you this season and I hope you enjoy the ride. If you have any questions, feel free to find me on Bluesky (@ktompkinsii.bsky.social) or tag me in a post with any lineup questions. I'll do my best to answer them for you.

All game and implied point totals listed were compiled via DraftKings Sportsbook on Monday for GB-LAR, CHI-DET, PHI-DAL, KC-BUF and DEN-PIT.

Implied Point Total: Rams 23.75, Packers 20.75

Must-Start Players: WR Christian Watson, WR Matthew Golden

After a week off, the Packers have to saddle up and play on Wednesday in Los Angeles against the Rams. After getting shell-shocked against the Vikings in Week 10, it was not the tone-setter the Packers wanted. Love has been phenomenal this season, ranking as QB7 in fantasy points per game with 20.3. He's been feeding the air yards machine with deep shots and more targets to Watson and Golden. Honestly, what more do you need? The Rams have been a brutal defensive matchup for offenses this season, as they rank third in the NFL in defensive DVOA and seventh in the NFL in fantasy points per game allowed to quarterbacks. That said, we know what the Packers are capable of. In a week when every team is playing, Love needs to be in lineups unless you also have Joe Burrow, Josh Allen or another top quarterback option.

Fantasy Advice: Fire up Love as a QB1 this week. We're hoping some big plays can provide the Packers with offensive fireworks in Week 12.

In Weeks 7-8, Kraft had six targets and a touchdown in each game. In the last two weeks, Kraft has six targets TOTAL with just two receptions for 20 yards. Kraft is just so hit-or-miss right now. With the Packers so dependent on Watson and Golden to move the ball, the gap between those two and Kraft feels like an ocean. He feels incredibly risky to put in your lineup right now. If you don't have better options or you drafted him, just know the Rams allow the second-fewest fantasy points per game to opposing tight ends. Among tight ends with 100 or more routes, Kraft ranks TE16 in targets per route run and TE28 in yards per route run, behind actual second tight ends like Elijah Higgins, Tommy Tremble and Noah Gray. He currently ranks as TE12 for the season in total points, which is a big disappointment considering where he came off the board on draft day.

Fantasy Advice: Kraft is a touchdown-hunting TE2 right now because volume in the Packers' offense is so heavily concentrated among the wide receivers.

Also on a bye in Week 11, Stafford put up a QB11 performance without Puka Nacua and Davante Adams against the Cardinals, throwing for 249 yards and three touchdowns. Well, the Packers are NOT the Rams and Green Bay will also have Micah Parsons back for his first game of the season. All of this means Stafford will still be throwing to a bare-bones receiving corps against a solid defense that is getting one of the best players in the NFL back. In Week 10 against Arizona, the Rams posted a -11.2% PROE, their second-lowest percentage of the season and the second-lowest in the league that week. Granted, they didn't have to do a lot of heavy lifting against the woefully undermanned Cardinals, but with their running game, they could opt to use their backs to set up the passing game rather than the inverse approach we've grown accustomed to seeing from these Rams.

Fantasy Advice:Stafford has a history of doing more with less than most quarterbacks, but fantasy managers should be able to find a better starting option in a week where all 32 teams are playing than a quarterback without his top three options.

Blake Corum has been on a massive heater for the Rams and has really picked up the slack in the running back room with Kyren Williams a bit banged up with a toe injury and less efficient in recent weeks. With the injuries to Nacua and Adams, it makes much more sense to feature the running game anyway. The Packers have been very susceptible to the run, giving up the third-most rushing touchdowns per game to backfields this season. Corum very much feels like the lead back here, but it's not like Williams is uninvolved; he's definitely a big piece of the offense. It's just that head coach Sean McVay is riding the hot hand. Even Ronnie Rivers got 15 carries in their blowout of the Cardinals, but all of that came late in the game and is not indicative of increased utilization outside of a blowout. It's still a two-man backfield here, but Corum is more of the focus right now.

Fantasy Advice: Corum is nearly an RB1 against the Packers, while Williams is a solid RB2. Both are startable and should be plenty involved and productive on Wednesday.
