Vikes Views: Who is Missing the Playoffs in the NFC?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Minnesota Vikings got off to a hot start and were the last undefeated team in the NFL. However they've lost three of the last four and are currently the sixth seed in the NFC. There are going to be good teams that will miss. If we assume the 49ers with the NFC West and the NFC South and East will only have the divisional winners. That leaves the NFC North Winner and 3 Wild Card Spots for the Lions, Bears, Vikings, Packers, Rams, and Seahawks. Two of those teams are going to miss the playoffs.

The Lions face the Bears in Detroit on Thanksgiving Day. They follow that up with a few winnable games and then three NFC North games in the last four weeks. They have to go on the road against Minnesota, Green Bay, and Chicago to finish. Aiden Hutchinson going down is also a huge blow. It seems like injuries always take away their steam.

The Bears are at Detroit and then host the Jaguars the next two weeks. They easily could go 2-0 or 0-2. They have a short reprieve against the Dolphins before finishing with the Bills, Packers, Lions, and Vikings. The schedule is tough, but Jeffrey Simmons has proven to be a difference maker. Chicago has a real chance to make a run, if they can overcome their schedule.

The Vikings have an easier stretch with the Falcons and Panthers at home before going to the Drake Maye-less Patriots. The final four include home games against Detroit, Chicago, and Washington and a final road game at the New York Jets. The schedule is in their favor to make a run for the NFC North. They will need to win against the bad teams and at least split the Lions and Bears games.

The Packers are 1-3 in the NFC North, so the division is just about out of reach. They're on the road this evening against the Los Angeles Rams. Games against the Bills, Bears, Lions, and Texans are all tough matchups. They a small break with the Saints and Dolphins mixed in. It's very likely they end up at 9-8.

Next up for the Seahawks are the high-flying San Francisco 49ers. They have a chance to control some of their destiny with two games against the Rams. 49ers, Cowboys, Giants, Eagles, Rams, Panthers, and Rams is a rough end to the season. We'll see if Sam Darnold can get healthy and recapture some of that magic.

Packers, Chiefs, 49ers, Cowboys, Seahawks, Buccaneers, and Seahawks is the hardest schedule remaining of the bunch. Combine that with the Puka Nacua injury, and it seems like this season has gotten away from the once-favored Los Angeles Rams.

I think the Packers and Rams are the two that will be left out. The season is long and the toughness of the schedule changes rapidly. I believe the Vikings have the easiest path remaining and that should allow them to remain in contention for the NFC North crown.
