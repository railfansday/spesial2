Jimmy Pitaro told what he must do right away to end the Troy Aikman and Joe Buck drama

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The drama between Troy Aikman and Joe Buck has been one of the biggest stories in the NFL lately.

The two legendary voices of the game fell out recently after Buck signed a new contract with ESPN without saying anything to his long-time broadcast partner.

That has created a huge rift, and former Miami Marlins president David Samson thinks there's only one solution now.

The Super Bowl is not too far away, and at a time when all the talk should be about what happens on the field, everyone's invested in the drama in the booth.

Aikman and Buck have been the voices of the NFL for over two decades now, and the expectation was that they would call the Super Bowl in 2027 as well.

However, Aikman has taken his feud with Buck public by claiming that the latter has always wanted to have a one-man booth.

Samson believes things have gone way too far now and claimed that ESPN chairperson Jimmy Pitaro must begin the process of finding Aikman's replacement right away.

He said, as per Awful Announcing: "Troy Aikman has gotten to the 'I don't give a f*** stage' of his career with ESPN because he keeps doing these things. And here's the problem: Jimmy Pitaro has to end this.

"I don't know whether he ends it today, tomorrow, next week or next month. But ESPN cannot have Joe Buck and Troy Aikman call the Super Bowl. They have invested too much money to have any distraction that they know about entering into the lead-up to the Super Bowl.

"There's always going to be something that happens. But if I am Jimmy Pitaro, I am making sure that I find someone to call the Super Bowl right now, and that I start the process. So that the audience, the media, the gossipers, we get it all out of our system.

"We deal with the fact that Aikman is gone from Monday Night Football, and we deal with it now. So by the time the playoffs and the Super Bowl comes, it's no longer part of the conversation."

Pitaro definitely has a big decision to make now, and it's not an easy one at all.

Buck and Aikman have been incredible to listen to over the years, and their voices are what people want to hear when they tune in to watch the Super Bowl.

However, if the two broadcasters cannot work in an amicable and professional manner, a difficult decision has to be made. It remains to be seen what Pitaro will end up doing in the coming days.
