Anthropic Shares Pare Gains After Rocketing in Debut

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Bloomberg's Bailey Lipschultz said that initial trading action of Anthropic was not as 'robust' as people would have hoped for or anticipated as Anthropic shares soared at the beginning of trading, only to pull back sharply. Lipschultz was joined by Bloomberg's Ryan Gould, who said the AI company has made it clear that is is prioritizing existing investors who understand Anthropic's goals and what CEO Dario Amodei is trying to do with the company. (Source: Bloomberg)
