Colts’ Defense Takes Crushing Hit Before Dolphins Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Indianapolis Colts will face the Miami Dolphins with holes at multiple levels of their defense. Their latest injury update also leaves the offense and special teams searching for answers, creating a difficult personnel challenge before Sunday's game.

Indianapolis ruled out six players, according to The Athletic's James Boyd. Linebackers Jaylon Carlies and Akeem Davis-Gaither will miss the matchup, along with defensive tackle Jerry Tillery. Tight end Mo Alie-Cox, long snapper Luke Rhodes and right tackle Jalen Travis are also unavailable.

Carlies is dealing with a neck injury, while Davis-Gaither has a knee issue. Tillery will sit because of a calf injury.

Those absences put particular pressure on the middle of Indianapolis' defense. Losing two linebackers simultaneously limits the coaching staff's flexibility. Tillery's absence adds another complication up front, where the Colts must distribute his workload among their available defensive linemen.

The Dolphins now have an opportunity to challenge those replacements. Indianapolis must prevent its injury problems from becoming a weakness Miami can repeatedly exploit.

The concern extends beyond simply filling three spots on the defensive depth chart. Indianapolis also needs enough available players to rotate, handle different packages and respond if another injury occurs.

At linebacker, communication will be especially important. The Colts cannot afford confusion over assignments while adjusting to the losses of Carlies and Davis-Gaither. Miami can test that coordination through motion, misdirection and throws into the middle of the field.

Tillery's absence could make that job harder. Defensive linemen help keep blockers away from linebackers, allowing them to pursue the ball. Indianapolis needs its remaining options to hold up without asking the players behind them to compensate for repeated breakdowns.

That does not guarantee a productive afternoon for Miami. However, it gives the Colts less room for mistakes.

The available defenders have an opportunity to earn additional trust. A strong performance could help their cases for larger roles beyond this matchup. First, Indianapolis needs them to execute against an opponent that can study exactly where the lineup has changed.

The defensive injuries account for only half of Indianapolis' six-player list.

Alie-Cox will miss the game with a groin injury. His absence removes an experienced tight end who can contribute as a blocker and receiving option. The Colts must cover those responsibilities through their remaining personnel.

Travis' knee injury creates another issue for the offense. Indianapolis needs a replacement at right tackle, putting added attention on the protection around quarterback Daniel Jones.

The Colts cannot let those changes disrupt both their passing game and rushing attack. Sustaining drives would also help a defense working through its own personnel losses.

Meanwhile, Rhodes is unavailable because of a hamstring injury. Long snapping rarely draws attention when everything works properly, but Indianapolis needs a reliable replacement for punts and field goals.

Six absences do not decide a game before kickoff. They do, however, leave the Colts managing problems across all three phases against Miami on Sunday afternoon.
