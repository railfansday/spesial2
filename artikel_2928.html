&quot;I am convinced that the team will give their best version tomorrow.&quot;

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Girona will play tomorrow at Montilivi against Almeria, in a challenging match against a solid team in good form. "It's a tough and complicated game, but I trust that we will give our best version," said coach Quique Álvarez, who emphasized the importance of winning: "The standings at the top are very tight, and we are coming off a loss. We have things to improve."

The coach warned that Almeria is capable of causing trouble at any moment, both when they dominate and when they play on the counterattack. "They have gone nine games without losing and have players who can hurt you at any moment." Additionally, he pointed out that the upcoming series of matches, against mid-to-high table teams, will be a good measure to assess the team's situation.

To face the challenge, Girona wants to increase their aggressiveness in pressing. "Except for the match in Córdoba, what has hurt us the most has been the two red cards, against Castellón and Real Sociedad B." Despite this, the coach expressed confidence that the team will respond well: "I am convinced that tomorrow we will give our best version."

The match will also have a special component for the coach, who will reunite with a childhood friend on Almeria's bench. About García Pimienta, he explained that they share a philosophy of play based on taking the initiative and being protagonists, although experience has allowed him to adjust some aspects.

The coach also wanted to send a message of encouragement to Jastin, who is going through a tough time after working very well. "He has been unlucky. We can only encourage him and help him so he can have continuity."

In his press conference, the coach expressed Girona's support for the family, friends, and fans of Tenerife. He explained that the squad would have understood if the match had not been played and that he understands that those closest to the situation might decide not to participate. "Now we have to focus on the tribute, which will be very beautiful."

Finally, he confirmed that Girona will only lose three players (Vanat, Asprilla i Holmes Junior) due to the international break, but he insisted that all attention is now focused on tomorrow's match against Almeria.
