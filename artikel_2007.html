Liverpool 'doing due diligence' on Luka Vuskovic deal, asking price revealed - expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Liverpool are working behind the scenes on a deal to sign Brighton defender Luka Vuskovic as their top target next summer.

The 19-year-old has enjoyed an outstanding season on the South Coast since his £46million move from Tottenham, and has been a crucial part of Fabian Hurzeler's side.

He is already regarded as one of the best defenders in the Premier League based on this season's displays, and has sparked interest from elite clubs across Europe as a result.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Liverpool are "huge admirers" of Vuskovic and view him as a long-term replacment for Virgil van Dijk.

Work is already being done in the background to assess what it might take to land the Croatia international, although Brighton will be extremely reluctant to lose him.

The Seagulls have been tipped to demand a fee of at least £100million for Vuskovic, and while it remains to be seen if that will be met, Liverpool are among the leading contenders.

Football Insider revealed last month that Liverpool and Real Madrid are among the elite clubs keeping tabs on Vuskovic with a big-money move in mind for the future.

While there is still a belief he may need a full season of Premier League football under his belt, they will continue to keep a close eye on his performances for Brighton.

Vuskovic's age and obvious talent have made him a leading target for Liverpool, who are aiming to bolster their defence and plan for a future without Van Dijk.

"I would be more surprised if Liverpool weren't looking at Vuskovic, to be honest," Brown told Football Insider.

"He's really emerged as one of the best talents in his position, there's no doubt about that, he looks like a terrific player and somebody who would improve most teams.

"Liverpool want to sign a centre-back because they're looking long-term, so he obviously ticks that box because he's still only young.

"They've been doing their due diligence on him, finding out all of the details you need to know, his strengths, his weaknesses, his attitude, how he trains, these sorts of things.

"I'm sure they've been very impressed by what they've seen, because a lot of clubs will have been after the season he's had so far.

"At the same time, his age would be a bit of a concern for me because he doesn't have that much experience playing in the Premier League or at the highest level.

"So Liverpool will be watching closely to see how he gets on this season, and if his performances do not drop off and he continues playing at this level, they could make a move."

Much of whether Vuskovic will be allowed to join a top club in the summer will depend on Brighton, and whether they are prepared to entertain offers for their star defender.

The Croatia international has been a key player for Hurzeler's side as they have pushed up the Premier League table, just months after his big-money arrival from Tottenham.

Football Insider revealed last month that Brighton believe Vuskovic is already a £100million player, despite having secured him for less than half of that during the summer.

That valuation comes as a result of the growing interest in his signature, and the Seagulls have shown in the past they can demand significant fees for their top talents.

Should Liverpool or any other club want to come forward with an offer, they will have to make it a very good one to tempt Brighton to sell.
