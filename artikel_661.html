MrBeast Settles Lawsuit Against Ex-Dev Director Over Allegedly Swiped Files and Hidden Cameras

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The former employee oversaw issues ranging from content production to fundraising to political advocacy.

MrBeast YouTube, a content arm for the social media creator, has settled a lawsuit from a former development director who allegedly stole internal company documents and installed hidden cameras at company offices.

Lawyers for MrBeast and the ex-employee, Leroy Nabors, told the court on Friday of a deal to resolve the case. Under the agreement, he’ll submit to a forensic inspection of his devices and accounts to ensure that sensitive information has been removed and not been disclosed.

“Upon satisfactory completion of the Protocol, Beast will voluntarily dismiss this action,” states the filing. The dismissal will be “without prejudice,” meaning the lawsuit can be refiled.

Nabors started at the company in 2023 as an IT contractor before becoming head of the development team. Upon learning that he’d be fired, he allegedly swiped thousands of documents containing financial data and sensitive information regarding business transactions and company investors, plus blueprints detailing how the social media superstar develops megahit videos, among other intellectual property.

Nabors later wiped his laptop to cover his tracks, according to the complaint. When he was confronted by the company about the alleged theft, he said that the mass download was a routine “backup” he conducted in the “normal course of business” and refused to return the documents, the lawsuit said.

In its investigation, MrBeast said it discovered multiple hidden cameras across its offices and a device attached to its servers containing an application that allows users to remotely access and control its network. The company suspects Labors to have installed them. Nabors’ daughter contracted with MrBeast to manage its entire IT network and had access to the camera feeds, according to the complaint.

MrBeast brought claims for misappropriation of trade secrets and breach of contract, among others. It sought unspecified damages. The YouTube star, whose real name is Jimmy Donaldson, wasn’t a party to the lawsuit.

The lawsuit detailed some of the innerworkings of the media empire controlled by MrBeast, whose real name is Jimmy Donaldson. It described the company’s development team as “deliberately flexible and multi-purpose, tackling issues ranging from content production to fundraising to political advocacy.” The company maintains strict security protocols, limiting access to trade secrets to certain employees and storing such information in a separate network, according to the complaint. It routinely audits user access to critical shared folders and directs employees to regularly change their passwords, said the lawsuit, which noted that applications on company devices require user authentication and have a session timeout mechanism.
