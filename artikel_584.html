How to Watch the 2026 Thanksgiving Day Parade: A Dolly Parton Tribute, Broadway Medley and Mystery Harry Potter Float

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Variety may receive an affiliate commission.

Dolly Parton will be honored with a special musical tribute at this year’s Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade, which celebrates its historic 100th edition on Saturday after being postponed from Thanksgiving Day due to weather conditions.

Tony-nominated Broadway star Megan Hilty will lead the tribute to the country music icon, who died in August at age 80. The performance is among several high-profile musical numbers planned for the milestone celebration, alongside appearances from RAYE, Teddy Swims, Megan Moroney, Queen Latifah and “KPop Demon Hunters” breakout EJAE. The 2026 Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade will air Saturday, Nov. 28, from 8:30 a.m. to noon in all time zones on NBC and Telemundo, with an encore presentation on NBC at 2 p.m. ET/PT. Those without cable can stream the festivities live on Peacock, in addition to a variety of live TV streaming services that carry NBC, including DirecTV, Hulu + Live TV and Sling TV in select markets.

“Today” show personalities Savannah Guthrie, Hoda Kotb and Al Roker will return to host the Emmy-winning telecast.

The parade’s 100th edition coincides with NBC’s own centennial anniversary, marking a particularly significant year for the network. More than 11,000 volunteers and participants will descend on Manhattan for Thursday’s festivities, which will span 2.5 miles from the Upper West Side to Macy’s flagship store in Herald Square. In a nod to the anniversary, Macy’s has assembled a record 100 inflatable creations, including new balloons depicting Bluey, Sorcerer’s Apprentice Mickey Mouse and Snoopy’s Joe Cool alter ego. The parade will also bring back balloons designed by some of the art world’s biggest names, including Tim Burton, Keith Haring, Jeff Koons and Yayoi Kusama.

Hollywood will be well represented among this year’s new floats, too, including a Hogwarts-themed creation from HBO. A commemorative Macy’s All Stars float will bring together familiar characters from throughout the parade’s history, from Bugs Bunny and Scooby-Doo to Sonic the Hedgehog.

Broadway, a fixture of the annual telecast, will get an expanded showcase this year. A 10-minute musical medley introduced by Julianne Hough will bring together performers from nine productions, including “Hamilton,” “Wicked,” “Rent,” “Chicago,” “The Book of Mormon,” “Aladdin,” “Schmigadoon!,” “The Lost Boys” and “The Rocky Horror Show.”

Other musical performers on this year’s roster include Natasha Bedingfield, Jimmy Fallon and the Roots, Keke Palmer, JoJo, Wyclef Jean, Leslie Odom Jr., Anthony Ramos, Indigo Girls, DEVO, Smash Mouth and K-pop boy band RIIZE. The Radio City Rockettes will also return before Santa Claus closes out the festivities.

Stream the 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade live on Peacock, or watch for free with an eligible streaming trial through DirecTV.
