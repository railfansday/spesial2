CFP, ESPN reportedly agree to 5-year, $1.7 billion deal expanding playoff field to 24 teams starting in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

ESPN and the College Football Playoff (CFP) have reportedly agreed to a 5-year, $1.7 billion deal, per ESPN's Heather Dinich.

The deal expands the current 12-team CFP field to 24 teams and keeps ESPN as the broadcast partner.

An official announcement is expected soon.
