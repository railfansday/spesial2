GTA 6 PS5 Pro Features Revealed, 30fps Target on Both Consoles

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Improved clarity and ray traced shadows on PS5 Pro

Rockstar Games has today revealed what sort of benefits playing GTA 6 on a PS5 Pro will provide.

No matter whether you play on a base PS5 or a PS5 Pro, though, the game will target 30 frames-per-second, a new post on the developer's website confirms:

Grand Theft Auto VI has been fully developed from the ground up to provide the best possible experience for the current generation of consoles, targeting 30 frames per second while pushing both platforms to their absolute limits to bring the vast and rich world of Leonida to life — while maintaining the density and visual fidelity of all its environments and inhabitants.

The improvements are slim on PS5 Pro; the article mentions "improved clarity" thanks to PSSR, and there'll be ray traced shadows that are only available on the upgraded console.

There is no 60fps mode, or even a 40fps mode for GTA 6. This also applies to the Xbox Series X|S versions.
