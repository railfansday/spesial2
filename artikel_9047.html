If there's a writer in your life, you'll know they can be difficult to buy for. All they need is something to write with and something to write on, right? Unlike your photographers and your designers (who are covered by our guide to the best Black Friday deals for creatives), it isn't immediately obvious exactly what expensive kit to spoil a writer with. But just like everybody else, we authors love receiving gifts.

With that in mind, I've rounded up the best Black Friday deals for writers, from genuinely useful bits of tech (a reMarkable tablet and an excellent Logitech keyboard) to the accoutrements of a tortured literary genius. Even the most Serious Literary Author will find something to enjoy here.
