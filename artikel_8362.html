1. I need cash for whatever reason. [This hasn't happened yet.]

3. I was wrong in buying the stock. This happens every few years. I make mistakes. Everybody does.
4. I have more than 20% of my net worth in a single stock. [This hasn't happened yet, but it got close with Costco.]
4. I want to buy a better stock. This hasn't happened yet, but it could since I am retired and spend all of my income. [Essentially no new money.]
