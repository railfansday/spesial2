Mercari Global App to Offer Mercari Japan Authentication and Auction Feature, Making it Easier Than Ever Before to Purchase Japanese Items From the US

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Platform has surpassed 1 million cumulative registered usersâShipping discount promotion to be held in conjunction with Black Friday

TOKYO, Nov. 27, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Mercari, Inc. ("Mercari") has announced plans to begin offering Mercari Japan Authentication[1], a service that authenticates select items before they are shipped overseas from Japan, as well as an auction feature that will allow users in the US to bid on and purchase select auction items listed on the Japanese Mercari marketplace. Both Mercari Japan Authentication and the auction feature will be available through the Mercari Global App ("Global App"), a worldwide app that allows users to purchase items listed in Japan from overseas.

The cumulative number of registered users for the Global App surpassed 1 million[2] on August 28, 2026. As overseas usage expands, Mercari will create an environment where US users can have select items authenticated after purchase and before they are shipped overseas, allowing them to make purchases with peace of mind. By giving US users access to auctions, Mercari will also expand the number of opportunities for buyers to discover and purchase more Japanese items.

Additionally, in line with Black Friday and Cyber Monday in the US, Mercari will hold a promotion from November 24 to November 30, 2026, offering shipping discounts of up to 50 USD for select orders.

The Safe Appraisal service has been offered in Japan since March 2024.[3] Now, Mercari is starting to gradually offer the service for crossborder transactions for the US, allowing select items to be authenticated before they are shipped overseas. In the US, the name of the service is Mercari Japan Authentication. â

After a user purchases a trading card, pair of sneakers, fashion/luxury item, or another select item, it is sent to a designated authentication center in Japan, where expert staff verify the item according to category-specific authentication standards. Items that are found to satisfy the standards are shipped overseas. However, if an item does not satisfy the standards or cannot be authenticated, the transaction is canceled, and the buyer will be refunded for any expenses they paid such as the purchase price of the item, authentication fees, shipping costs, and taxes. Note that authentication does not guarantee the authenticity of the item. â

Once the item arrives at the authentication center, authentication typically takes three to five business days. This does not include the time required to ship the item. For more information on such things as what items are eligible for this service, authentication fees, and conditions for free authentication, check the information page of the service for the US. â

- Service information page for the US https://japan.us.mercari.com/en/pages/service/authentication/index.html

Since January 2025, Mercari has offered its auction feature[4] to users in Japan as a sales method that can be selected when an item is listed. This is a system where the seller sets a starting price, prospective buyers place bids during the auction period, and the user who places the highest bid at the end wins the auction.

Mercari is now rolling out a feature in the Global App that allows users in the US to search for, view, bid on, win, and purchase select auction items listed on Mercari in Japan. In addition to fixed-price items, buyers will now be able to bid on and purchase select auction items as well.

Mercari will offer the fun of searching for, bidding on, and winning items such as trading cards and character merchandise to users in the US, while also expanding opportunities for sellers in Japan to deliver their items to users overseas.

Crossborder transactions on Mercari kicked off in 2019 with the start of partnerships with crossborder e-commerce operators. Following the launch of the web version of Mercari in Taiwan in August 2024, and in Hong Kong in May 2025, Mercari launched the Global App in Taiwan and Hong Kong in September 2025. The app launched in the US in June 2026.

With the Global App, users overseas can browse and buy Japanese items from Mercari and Mercari Shops. Mercari offers a platform where overseas users can purchase Japanese items by using translation features and support for payment methods in each country and region.

The gross merchandise value (GMV) of Mercari's crossborder business reached 112.2 billion JPY in the fiscal year ending June 2026, expanding approximately 19 times over the past four years.[5] â In addition, the cumulative number of registered users of the Global App surpassed 1 million on August 28, 2026.

For the period from April 2025 to March 2026, the US market ranked second in both transaction value and number of transactions for Mercari's crossborder business. Mercari launched the Global App in the US in June 2026, and by adding these latest two features, the company will further enhance the experience of purchasing Japanese items.

In November 2026, Mercari also made the decision to partner with SURUGA-YA Inc. to establish "Mercari Surugaya Inc.," a joint venture that will operate in the US. The joint venture plans to purchase, procure, and sell items in the Japanese Entertainment & Hobbies categories on the ground in the US.[6] Mercari will expand the touchpoints between US users and Japanese items through both the functional expansion of the Global App, which supports purchases of items from Japan, and local business operations in the US.

Mercari will hold a promotion on the Global App that discounts shipping costs for eligible orders by up to 50 USD. The promotion will coincide with Black Friday and Cyber Monday in the US.[7] During the promotion period, users who place an order totaling 50 USD or more will receive a discount on their shipping costs of up to 50 USD. The discount is automatically applied to eligible orders and can only be used once per user.

Having surpassed the milestone of 1 million cumulative registered users, Mercari will continue striving to improve the features and purchasing experience of the Global App. The company will continue to build a platform where overseas users can enjoy shopping more easily and with greater peace of mind, from discovering Japanese items to purchasing and having them from Japan.

By connecting individual sellers and businesses in Japan with overseas users, Mercari will expand opportunities for items that are no longer needed by one user to cross borders and become of value to another. In doing so, Mercari will accelerate the circulation of all forms of value.
