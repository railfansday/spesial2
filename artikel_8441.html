I struggle with this as well, being 6' 3" and 170 lbs. American Tall's website offers plenty of clothing in sizes that fit but alas, most of their stuff is not made in America, contrary to their business moniker.

I've never had much luck with long-sleeve t-shirts from Lands End or Eddie Bauer, but LL Bean's medium tall are usually long enough in the sleeve, even if they're not cut for slim torsos.

I also struggle finding casual hoodies long enough in the torso length; a lot of them are long enough in the sleeves but ride up above my waist in the back. American Giant makes some USA-made items that fit better but they're very expensive and again, American Tall offers better toro length hoodies but they're not made in the USA.
