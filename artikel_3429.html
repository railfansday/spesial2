3 Best New Netflix Series To Binge-Watch This Weekend (November 27–29, 2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The 3 best new Netflix shows to binge this weekend, November 27–29, 2026, include an erotic mystery, a gripping true crime, and a new medical drama. This Thanksgiving weekend, Netflix has plenty of notable new streaming options that are perfect for all members of the family, whether you're looking for something thrilling that will keep you on the edge of your seat or your next favorite feel-good comfort show.

Earlier this month, some of the best new shows to binge-watch on Netflix were Black Doves season 2, The Body, and Lupin season 4, all of which are still top trending on Netflix's worldwide viewership charts. That said, these three shows are the best on the platform that were just released within the past few days. Also, check out ScreenRant's TV binge guides for Prime Video, Paramount+, and HBO Max this weekend.

Always On Call is a brand new medical drama series that just premiered on Netflix on Thursday, November 26. Consisting of just seven episodes, Always On Call follows a brilliant yet complicated surgeon named Sofia who is assigned to one of the most chaotic emergency rooms in her city. Determined to make an immediate impact and improve her professional reputation, Sofia navigates the endless stream of medical emergencies while forming a tight-knit bond with her new coworkers.

Always On Call is the perfect weekend binge on Netflix this weekend for fans of The Pitt and Grey's Anatomy and is already being called one of Netflix's best new medical dramas by viewers. Featuring a talented Norwegian cast, Always On Call has flown under the radar since its global premiere on Thanksgiving but is positioned to become a new medical drama franchise for the streamer. Full of human emotion and intense situations, Always On Call is worth a quick 7-part binge on Netflix right now.

The Trials of Karen Read is officially the biggest new streaming hit on Netflix heading into the holiday weekend, currently ranking #2 worldwide following its premiere earlier this week. The 3-part true crime series examines the death of Boston police officer John O'Keefe and the controversial murder trial of his former girlfriend, Karen Read. The Trials of Karen Read refers to Read's two murder trials, the first ending in a hung jury in 2024, and the second resulting in her being acquitted of several charges, including second-degree murder.

The Trials of Karen Read is the newest viral true crime docuseries on Netflix following Taken: Natalee Holloway, which was previously the #1 most popular series on the platform earlier this month. The new Netflix docuseries has a movie-length runtime of 2 hours and 25 minutes, making it extremely easy to start and finish in one night. Don't miss out on Netflix's latest streaming sensation this weekend.

The Hunting Wives just returned with its highly anticipated second season on Thursday, November 26, making it the best new show to binge on Netflix this weekend. The erotic mystery thriller follows the tension-fueled dynamic between Sophie O'Neil, played by Brittany Snow, and her new socialite friend Margo Banks, played by Malin Akerman, after she moves to East Texas. Margo pulls Sophie into a twisted secret world of seduction and even murder, centered on soapy twists, high-stakes affairs, and full-blown obsession.

All 32 episodes of Netflix's gangster thriller masterpiece deliver gripping crime drama, unforgettable characters, and relentless suspense.
