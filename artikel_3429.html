A Knight Of The Seven Kingdoms' Dunk & Egg Officially Return In February 2027 Collection

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms has been a breath of fresh air in terms of live-action adaptations in the Game of Thrones universe. While House of the Dragon certainly has its merits, fans have become more and more divided with each season as the show takes more liberties with the source material. AKOTSK has so far stayed very true to George R.R. Martin's Hedge Knight novellas, and the series also harkens back to the character-driven storytelling that popularized the original Game of Thrones adaptation.

Much of that success comes down to Peter Claffey and Dexter Sol Ansell, who seem perfectly cast as Ser Duncan the Tall and his young squire, Egg. Claffey captures Dunk's awkward nobility and rough-around-the-edges demeanor, while Ansell brings the perfect blend of wit, mischief, and heart to Egg. Their chemistry is easily one of the show's biggest strengths, especially during the quieter moments where no dialogue is even required to make a scene work.

While we're eagerly awaiting their next on-screen adventure in Season 2, Dunk and Egg are officially reuniting in a brand-new February 2027 release that hardcore fans will definitely appreciate.

Dunk and Egg are officially returning in February 2027, with two brand-new Funko Pop! figures celebrating the beloved A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms duo. The collectibles recreate Peter Claffey's Ser Duncan the Tall and Dexter Sol Ansell's Egg, giving fans an opportunity to bring both characters home ahead of their next on-screen adventure.

Both figures are currently available to preorder through Entertainment Earth, with an estimated arrival window of February 2027 and a price roughly $15 each. They're being sold separately, although fans can reunite the unlikely companions for just under $30 before taxes and shipping.

The first is the Dunk Funko Pop! Vinyl Figure #1901, which measures approximately four inches tall. The collectible features Dunk's modest appearance, complete with his sword, cloak, and simple traveling attire: "Ser Duncan the Tall is determined to prove his mettle" and fans can reunite the hedge knight with his faithful squire.

Meanwhile, the Egg Funko Pop! Vinyl Figure #1899 is appropriately smaller at about three inches tall, recreating the young Aegon Targaryen with his distinctive shaved head and modest clothing. It's a fitting tribute to the young prince's adventures masquerading as an ordinary squire, far removed from the royal comforts of House Targaryen.

Both figures arrive in traditional Funko window display boxes, making them appealing options for collectors who prefer keeping their figures in their original packaging. The February arrival dates are estimates, however, and could change before release.

Dunk and Egg aren't the only A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms characters receiving the Funko treatment, either, with Tanselle and Lyonel Baratheon also getting figures and currently expected in April 2027.

As for the HBO series itself, Season 2 is officially scheduled for 2027, with filming having wrapped in August 2026. The upcoming season will adapt George R.R. Martin's second Dunk and Egg novella, The Sworn Sword, continuing the pair's adventures across Westeros. HBO chief Casey Bloys has also indicated that Season 2 will arrive within the next Emmy eligibility window, which ends on May 31, 2027. While an exact premiere date hasn't been confirmed, fans should be reunited with A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms' Dunk and Egg on-screen in the first half of 2027.
