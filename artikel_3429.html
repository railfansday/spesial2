Dinosaur Western Mash-Up Series Confirms Filming & Cast For Alternate Universe Action Thriller

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Ryan Northrup is a Senior Staff Writer on ScreenRant's Movie & TV News team, where he has authored nearly 5,000 articles, including features and reviews. A graduate of McMaster University, Ryan began his career in public relations before attending Vancouver Film School. There, he built lasting industry connections and optioned a feature film screenplay.

EXCLUSIVE: Dinosaurs of the Wild West just took a major step forward.

After filmmaker Luke Sparke found success with Primitive War in 2025, the creator returned earlier this year with an ambitious idea for a Western that imagines cowboys and dinosaurs living side-by-side. Inspired by illustrations by Shaun Keenan, Dinosaurs of the Wild West launched on Kickstarter in July, becoming the most successful campaign for a TV series on the platform in Australian history.

Now, ScreenRant can reveal that Dinosaurs of the Wild West has officially entered production in New Zealand and Australia, with a release for the first episode (of a planned eight) set for 2027. Accompanying the news that filming has begun is confirmation of the series' cast. Casper Van Dien (Starship Troopers) leads the series as Sheriff Virgil Ross, with Lincoln Lewis, Jesse Kove, Travis Jeffery, Mark Ricci, Tony Bonner, and M.C. Huff rounding out the rest of the ensemble. Previously announced cast members include Martin Kove, Rachel Pizzolato, and George Kittle.

The series is said to combine "sweeping Western landscapes, practical environments and extensive creature visual effects to bring Sparke’s ambitious vision to the screen." Sparke shared the following statement about the start of filming:

“We’ve been building this world for years, and to finally be bringing it to life with this cast is incredibly exciting. The Kickstarter showed us that there is an audience hungry for original stories and worlds they haven’t seen before. This isn’t just cowboys riding dinosaurs. We’re creating an entire alternate history with its own characters, conflicts and mythology, and we’re approaching it with the same cinematic ambition as our feature films.”

On top of a wide release for episode 1, Dinosaurs of the Wild West's 2027 launch will also include a new role-playing game. This game is described as "an ambitious tabletop RPG and collectibles project designed to expand the universe beyond the screen." The show's expanded universe will be built out using illustrated sourcebooks, collectible dinosaur miniatures, and territory guides, with a separate Kickstarter for the companion materials set to launch next year.

Production start on Dinosaurs of the Wild West follows Sparke's successful venture into dino territory last year with Primitive War. The action horror film served as a fun alternative to the blockbuster Jurassic Park franchise, following a team of American soldiers as they encounter dinosaurs while searching for a missing Green Beret platoon during the Vietnam War.

Primitive War reviews were broadly positive, with critics and audiences alike praising the film's fun premise and impressive visual effects. The film was quick to develop a cult following, paving the way for Sparke's continued exploration of dinosaurs during different periods of history. Though Primitive War was an ambitious project given its small budget, the official synopsis for Dinosaurs of the Wild West, included below, suggests Sparke is about to go even bigger:

Set in an alternate 1879, Dinosaurs of the Wild West imagines a frontier where humans and dinosaurs have built civilization together. Massive Brontosaurs haul entire towns across the plains. Raptors are ridden into battle. Pterosaurs rule the skies. Against this extraordinary backdrop, pioneers, outlaws and adventurers navigate a dangerous frontier where prehistoric creatures have reshaped the course of human history.

It's unclear how Dinosaurs of the Wild West will fare with audiences, but the show is evidently intended to be just the beginning of a much larger cinematic universe. Just how far this universe expands may depend on the reception to the first episode next year, but clearly those looking for more dinosaur action are about to have an exciting 2027.
