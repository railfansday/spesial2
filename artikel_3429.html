Netflix’s 10/10 ‘House of Cards’ Replacement Is Officially Taking Over the World

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Three years ago, Netflix released a highly rated political thriller that has become one of the streamer's best since House of Cards. With a 93% average critic score on Rotten Tomatoes, the show has received universal acclaim for its story, acting, and direction. While the first season received some mixed reviews and managed an 83% score, subsequent seasons have received near-perfect scores, peaking at 97%. It helps that The Diplomat has escaped the 2-year curse and delivered a packed season every year.

The most recent season premiered last month, on October 15. The show quickly climbed into the top ten, peaking at number three globally. In its debut week, The Diplomat managed 5.5 million, while in the second week, that number dropped to 4.8 million. The series has accumulated over 14 million views so far and spent 4 weeks in the top ten. The Diplomat has been renewed for a fifth season, expected in 2027.

Four Netflix characters, four completely different ways of showing up for somebody. This is the one who fits how you actually do friendship, and what having them in your life would be like.

You do not do friendship at half volume, and neither does he. What you want is somebody who says yes to the plan before you have finished describing the plan.

You are the friend who makes things happen, and so is she. Together you would be mildly exhausting to everyone around you and completely delighted with yourselves.

You have no patience for pleasant lies, and she has no ability to tell them. What looks like coldness from the outside is the most reliable friendship on this list.

You look after whoever is having the worst day. So does he. It is the least strategic way to move through the world and the best possible quality in a friend.

Created by Debora Cahn, the series is set in the volatile world of international relations and follows America's ambassador to the UK, Kate Wyler (Keri Russell). The series explores the intensity required to keep countries on good terms, even when interests don't align. From assassinations to nuclear threats and rogue heads of state, The Diplomat's tight narrative makes for one of Netflix's most thrilling binge-watches. In Season 4, Ambassador Wyler tries to keep things from imploding at home and at work when America's new president finds an equally charged vice president: Wyler's husband, Hal (Rufus Sewell). Like its predecessors, the season ends on a massive cliffhanger as Kate, her husband, and other members of staff resign during a G7 summit to protest the president's new policy. Other cast members include Ali Ahn (Eidra), Rory Kinnear (Trowbridge), Allison Janney (President Penn), Nana Mensah (Billie), and more.
