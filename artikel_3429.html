Prime Video’s ‘The Chosen’ Season 6 Surges to No. 1

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

One show that Prime Video has also won big from investing in is The Chosen, the faith-based series created and directed by Dallas Jenkins. Earlier this month on November 15, Prime Video finally brought back The Chosen for its sixth season, releasing the first three episodes of the new season on streaming. Episode 4 was released on November 22, and Episodes 5 and 6 are already confirmed to follow on November 29 and December 6, respectively. Still, Prime Video needed only a few episodes of The Chosen to draw fans in, as it has swiftly become the singular most-watched title on the platform worldwide. What makes this climb to the top even more impressive is that The Chosen had to claw past one of Prime Video’s most popular properties in The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power to hit #1, but it has taken a convincing lead over the Tolkien-inspired series.

Four worlds and four completely different ideas of what a good life looks like. This is where you belong, what it would ask of you, and what you would get back in return.

You belong somewhere old enough that the mountains have names and somebody can still tell you who gave them. Middle-earth is for people who think the right thing is worth doing even when it will not be finished in their lifetime.

You want the sea, a crew, and absolutely nobody telling you where you are allowed to go. The Grand Line is the world for people whose main ambition in life is finding out what is over there.

You would rather be feared and useful than liked and useless. The Continent is a world where the monsters are the straightforward part of the job and the people are where it all gets complicated.

You want the myths to be real and loose in the actual world, and you want to find out at about thirteen that you were never anywhere near as ordinary as everybody kept telling you.

Among the most-watched titles on Prime Video right now is the new Madden movie directed by David O. Russell and starring Nicolas Cage opposite Christian Bale. Stephen King’s new horror series, Carrie, has drifted closer to the bottom of Prime Video watchlists over the last week, but the show has already been picked up for a second season. The Hunger Games movies are also charting on Prime Video’s global charts alongside the theatrical release of Sunrise on the Reaping.
