Stefan Borson reveals how much Unai Emery will be earning at Aston Villa after agreeing new terms

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Aston Villa will make Unai Emery one of the highest-paid managers in Europe with his imminent new contract.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that the Spaniard's new deal is also likely to include an eye-watering release clause.

Despite a tough start to the 2026-27 campaign, the Villans have risen to sixth in the Premier League table, with Manchester United up next on Sunday, 8 November.

Emery's side are also thriving in the Champions League, only seeing their European streak come to an end at the hands of Barcelona in midweek.

Given all of this, the 55-year-old is expected to sign across the dotted line this season, extending his stay at Villa Park until the summer of 2029.

It is reported that Emery currently earns £8million-a-year, but that figure could rise to upwards of £15m with a new deal in B6.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson has predicted the Villa manager to earn between £10m and £15m after signing his new deal.

"It's probably pretty big, actually. I suspect he's right up there in terms of European managers," he explained.

"He's built that credibility at Villa, even beyond where he already was. He's respected as one of the best managers around, one of the most experienced managers around.

"And he's talked about for all of the biggest jobs. So he'll be asking why he shouldn't be paid at the very highest level. They're doing pretty well in the Champions League.

"Everything points to a very, very big European salary, one of the biggest I would think. He could be on £10m to £15m a year, I would think, with a very big release clause."

This is clearly a statement of intent from the hierarchy at Villa Park, with Emery being heavily linked with the Man United job.

Michael Carrick is under massive pressure right now, but Eddie Howe and Julian Nagelsmann appear to be the more likely options for the Red Devils.

Undoubtedly, Emery deserves his new deal at Villa Park. But can the club keep hold of him right until the end of his deal?

Mick Brown, speaking to Football Insider, certainly doesn't think so, stating that his new deal won't do anything to rule out an exit in the future.
