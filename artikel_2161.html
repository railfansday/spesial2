Middlesbrough's £3m winger has hit trouble after surprise Turkey move

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Middlesbrough have flown out of the traps in Kim Hellberg's first full season at the Riverside Stadium, showcasing exactly why they were viewed as automatic promotion potential before a ball was kicked.

The Teesside outfit have spent almost a decade at Championship level under several names, but the Swede is certainly the one who has made the biggest impression in terms of having such an immediate impact during said timeframe.

Hellberg will mark one year at the Riverside Stadium at the end of the month - one which his current chargers have begun two points clear at the top of the table, having lost just one of their first 13 matches.

In the past 12 months, Boro have adopted an unrelenting, high-pressing style in which several chances are created and, indeed, now being put away, largely by big-money summer recruit, Will Lankshear, whilst other creative forces were added to Hellberg's ranks throughout an eye-catching summer window.

Whilst such strong quality was added, a plethora of players on the fringes of the squad saw exits sanctioned, as well as the high-profile departures of Hayden Hackney and Morgan Whittaker.

However, there still remains a big question mark over Sontje Hansen's long-term future, albeit one which is highly unlikely to lie in the North East.

With Ajax famed for their infamous production line, Hansen was a player seen to have a fairly high ceiling in terms of potential, representing the Netherlands across all youth age groups and scoring the Golden Boot in the Under-17 FIFA World Cup of 2019, which saw him linked with Manchester City.

Having scored 12 times in 64 appearances for NEC Nijmegen, there was excitement in the air when Hansen penned a four-year contract under Rob Edwards last summer.

Despite scoring his first Boro goal in his fourth Championship outing against Preston North End, it soon became apparent that the midfielder was still somewhat of an enigma to his then-new head coach.

"Sontje is a real talent. I think we’re still working out everything he has got. I think he’s someone we’re going to be able to use in different positions and ask him to do some different things," Edwards said 13 months ago.

Despite starting Hellberg's first game in charge against Derby County, Hansen was barely utilised by the new head coach despite a brief period with illness in the early weeks of his tenure.

Between New Year's Day and Good Friday, the Dutchman played just two minutes of Championship football and was an unused substitute in 13 of 15 league outings.

With minutes hard to come by despite a 14-minute cameo in the play-off final, speculation was rife over his immediate future, with Hellberg making a rather honest admission as to why Hansen didn't feature in the EFL Cup success over Wrexham at the start of the competitive season.

“Sontje was not involved, but it was no injury or anything like that. He played a game [for the Under-21s] because we looked at his physics," he revealed.

At 24, the seven-time Curacao international still has much of his senior career in front of him, but there is a risk of stagnation if Hansen is unable to turn his current fortunes around.

Shortly after Hellberg's admission, it was confirmed that a loan agreement had been struck with Turkish Süper Lig outfit, Gaziantep, which reportedly includes an option-to-buy at the end of the season.
