'Star Trek' Icon Patrick Stewart's Cause of Death Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

New details surrounding Patrick Stewart’s death have been revealed less than a month after the Star Trek icon died.

In a Los Angeles County Medical Examiner report obtained by TMZ on Thursday, November 19, Stewart died from Alzheimer’s disease and Lewy body dementia at the age of 86.

According to the Mayo Clinic, Lewy body dementia is the second most common form of Alzheimer’s disease. The condition causes a decline in mental abilities that gradually gets worse over time.

Stewart’s death certificate also lists cardiopulmonary arrest (commonly called cardiac arrest) and a neurocognitive disorder with Lewy body, a progressive form of dementia that affects memory, thinking and movement.

Stewart’s remains were handled through alkaline hydrolysis, which is also known as water cremation, on November 3. The process uses water and chemicals instead of flames.

News broke on October 29 that Stewart had died. The actor was 86.

“He has been held in the warm embrace of family and friends, surrounded by live music, prayer, tears, laughter and the kind of profound emotion that only he could inspire after a lifetime of devotion to us all,” his spokesperson shared in a statement to Us. “We will forever cherish the peaceful ease and reflection on his face, an enduring reminder of his invincible grace.”

In lieu of sending flowers, Stewart’s Instagram account encouraged fans to donate to the Johns Hopkins Alzheimer’s Disease Research Center Fund and Alzheimer’s Foundation of America Caregiver Resources.

Since Stewart’s death, the former X-Men star has been celebrated by his costars and friends in Hollywood.

Stewart’s widow, musician Sunny Ozell, has also paid tribute to the Hollywood legend on social media.

“The grief continues to come in waves. But I’ve also been experiencing such deep, profound gratitude. We left nothing unsaid,” Ozell, 47, wrote via Instagram on November 10. “He knew I loved him, and I know he loved me. Patrick and I truly, authentically saw one another, and we amplified each other’s best qualities.”

She continued, “I’ve been overwhelmed by the public response to Patrick’s death, and while I’m not at all surprised that he touched so many, I’m sincerely moved by the outpouring of sadness and loss. Thank you all for your condolences, they mean so, so much to me. My heart had to grow to contain my love for Patrick, and he changed me for the better, forever.”
