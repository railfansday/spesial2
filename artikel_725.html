Chris Brown Agrees to Increase Child Support — For Now — to Ex Diamond in Bitter Custody Fight

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Breezy will almost double monthly support payments temporarily ahead of a big settlement hearing that could finally resolve a nasty fight over his four-year-old daughter.

Chris Brown has agreed to temporarily boost his child support payments to his ex Diamond Brown amid a bitter legal battle over their four-year-old daughter, Lovely.

In legal filings obtained by Billboard, the star singer agreed Wednesday (Nov. 18) to pay $12,000 per month in child support, as well as continue paying other non-cash support — at least until a big hearing next month that his lawyers say could potentially resolve the entire dispute.

The new figure represents a hefty increase from the $7,000 her lawyers say Breezy was previously paying in child support. He has also agreed to pay $25,000 to retroactively cover support back to July and has advanced her $55,000 to cover her legal bills, per the filings.

Brown and Diamond, who split up in 2024, have been in a nasty fight since February, when she accused him on social media of threatening her current boyfriend. She went to court in May, suing him for child support and physical custody.

Brown has opposed her request for sole custody, and the pair have since exchanged bruising statements on social media — including Chris saying he already pays for “everything” even though he hasn’t seen Lovely since March. Diamond fired back that she had offered visits but Chris hadn’t shown: “If you wanted to see your daughter, you would.”

The dispute in court has been no nicer. Last month, she claimed in court filings that Brown had “made death threats against my most recent boyfriend.” In another filing, she said Brown makes between $5.5 and $7.3 million per month and requested he pay roughly $45,000 per month in total support payments to her and their daughter.

Throughout the dispute, C. Breezy has been on the road for his expansive R&B Tour with Usher, which heads to New Orleans this week and will wrap up next month. On the music front, CB released his Brown album in May, which debuted at No. 7 on the Billboard 200.
