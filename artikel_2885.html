‘Hands down’ – Rival NBA head coach makes strong Luka Doncic MVP claim

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Luka Doncic's start to the season has been so good, even his opponents are giving him MVP shouts at this point.

The Los Angeles Lakers are off to a 7-2 start through their first nine games. A lot of this is thanks to the absolutely incredible start that Luka Doncic is off to. The Slovenian superstar is averaging 38 points, eight rebounds, and eight assists a game while being extremely committed on the defensive end.

Doncic has never won the NBA MVP award despite being in contention for it for half a decade. This season seems like it might be the one if he keeps going like this. And despite the fact that his Portland Trail Blazers have to face Luka on Friday, head coach Micah Nori has given him a huge compliment.

The MVP is far from decided after just 10 games, Victor Wembanyama has been making quite the claim for himself in the early going as well. However, the current discussion is less to do with the award itself and who the best player in the league is.

And speaking ahead of his team's game against Los Angeles, Nori was incredibly complimentary about the Lakers man.

"This is the best shape I've seen him in," the Portland head coach told the media. "What I mean by that is, back in the day… It was like, pick him up full court. You know he's going to play a lot of minutes. Wear him down and later in the game, you could maybe switch on to him and he might have a harder time.

"But I don't see that. He's playing at an elite level… If you let him get to his left, the stepback is deadly. With his right hand, he can do anything in the world… To me, if I were to answer it, it looks like he is in elite shape, he's playing with a great amount of confidence. He's got shooters around him and he has a rolling big. For him, it's a perfect offensive storm. And he's the MVP right now. Hands down."

This is a ringing endorsement coming from one of the people who have to find a way to stop and beat Doncic. Giannis Antetokounmpo and Zach LaVine have similarly complimented Luka in recent weeks, too. It seems that the league is appreciating the incredible level he is currently playing at.
