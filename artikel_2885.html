NFL Week 10 expert picks, score predictions: Patriots vs. Lions

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Detroit Lions fanbase is known for being great travelers. Last week against the Miami Dolphins, it felt like a home game for the players, but how will the fans travel overseas? For the first time in over a decade, the Lions are going across the pond, this time to Munich, Germany, to take on the New England Patriots.

"Real good team here. The record is not the true indication of what this team is," said Lions head coach Dan Campbell. "There's a number of things that go into that, that Green Bay game the other day. I mean, (Packers QB Jordan) Love was on a heater, and you know it was just kind of one of those things that happens. You look at the totality of what this team is capable of, the firepower they have, and they're a good team. They're dangerous, and they're going to clean a lot of things up."

"I think (Mike) Vrabel's a hell of a coach and his staff. Josh (McDaniels) and those guys have done a hell of a job. Drake (Maye), he's as good as they come getting out of the pocket right now. That's where he's very, very dangerous. Buying time, these five-plus-second throws, he's dangerous, and then the skill he's got around him. You know, three (WR DeMario Douglas) and 18 (WR Kyle Williams), and then they got (Mack) Hollins back now and the tight ends. It's potent; it's very potent. We got our hands full. Defensively, they're a lot like us. They don't want you to run the ball on them. They're going to make sure that they stop the run. They are disruptive, they're long, they got versatility at the D line. There's a wave of guys. They got two or three deep wave of guys that are good football players across the board that create havoc, and then here comes the pressure, here comes a lot of sticky man coverage, make you make accurate throws with people in your face, and make contested catches."
