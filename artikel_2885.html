Nick Sirianni is pulled away from fans after comeback win

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Good or bad, right or wrong, Eagles coach Nick Sirianni has had from time to time the demeanor of a fan who won a contest to be the head coach for a day.

On Thursday in Dallas, he displayed those qualities after a stunning come-from-way-behind win over the Cowboys.

While celebrating the 39-38 win (they trailed 31-10, late in the third quarter), Sirianni approached fans in field-level suites. He eventually had to be pulled away by Dom DiSandro.

It's unclear whether he was interacting with Cowboys fans or Eagles fans. He appeared to be making an aggressive movement toward someone before DiSandro intervened.

The Eagles contend that Sirianni was celebrating with Eagles fans, and that Sirianni was pulled away so that he could shake the hand of Cowboys coach Brian Schottenheimer after the game.

There was never any contact (at least none that the cameras captured). Still, it's a bad look — and it's definitely on brand.

While Sirianni has yet to cross the line, it seems like he will, one of these days.

Regardless, the Eagles have now won two in a row after losing seven in a row. They're 4-7 and on the fringes of contention in the NFC East.
