Taylor Swift Supported a Fellow WAG With Her Sunday Chiefs Game Outfit

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Taylor Swift won’t miss a football Sunday. The pop superstar was back in her VIP suite yesterday to cheer on her husband, Travis Kelce, as he and his Kansas City Chiefs went up against the Arizona Cardinals.

And she certainly wasn’t alone. It was a full family affair at Kansas City, Missouri’s Arrowhead Stadium, as Swift’s parents, Andrea and Scott, joined Kelce’s family, including mom Donna and sister-in-law Kylie.

Everyone repped the Chiefs in their own way for the occasion, and the Swift family coordinated in colorful jackets. Andrea went for a bright red zip-up while Scott opted for a colorblock ’90s-style windbreaker. Meanwhile, the “Fortnight” singer took a cue from both of her parents, choosing a preppy red, white, and black windbreaker from Wear by Erin Andrews. At $100, the piece has a high neck with a button detail, and slouchy, sporty silhouette. A small Chiefs logo is embroidered onto one side of the chest.

Andrews, like Swift, is a WAG. The designer is married to former NHL center Jarret Stoll. She first came up with the idea for Wear at the time of the 2016 Super Bowl, after she noticed a lack in stylish sports merch for women, and launched the brand in 2019. She has since expanded to represent nearly 200 teams across major leagues, including the NBA, MLB, NHL, NCAA, WNBA, NWSL, US Soccer, and WWE.

Swift has been supporting Andrews for a while, ever since she received a care package from the designer in 2023. In October of that year, Swift first wore a red-white-and-black striped Wear zip-up to a Chiefs game against the Denver Broncos, and the piece immediately sold out. She also wore Wear to Super Bowl LVIII in 2024, and to a Chiefs game this September 2026.

Now, as the NFL season continues, you too can get her WAG look.
