Cineverse Building Upon Social Video Revenue Growth with a First-Time Spanish-Language Dog Whisperer Channel on YouTube + a Yu-Gi-Oh! Live Stream

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Revenue Across YouTube, Facebook, and Twitch up 55% YOY, with Social Video Now a Multi-million Dollar Business for the Company

LOS ANGELES, Nov. 11, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- In June, entertainment technology company and studio Cineverse (Nasdaq: CNVS) recorded its highest-ever gross revenue from social video in a single month, up 82% from the biggest month in 2025.  Now a multi-million dollar business, revenue from video across YouTube, Facebook, and Twitch is up 55% year-over-year (January through June).

This double-digit growth comes as the company announces two new launches: a new Yu-Gi-Oh! live stream on the iconic anime series' official YouTube channel, and a Spanish-language version of Dog Whisperer with Cesar Millan (El Encantador del Perros con Cesar Millan) on YouTube and Facebook.

YouTube and other social video have become a strong revenue-driver for our business," said Cineverse Senior Vice President, Partnership & Business Development Alexandra Viglione.

With Yu-Gi-Oh! live, fans of the popular anime franchise can now tune-in 24/7 to watch their favorite episodes.

Also driving growth of Cineverse's social video business is the expansion of successful relationships with third-party monetization partners, and the recent relaunches of Cineverse's Lone Star (westerns) and Midnight Pulp (cult films and all things strange) brands on YouTube.
