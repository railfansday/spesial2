David Moyes is “very attracted” to becoming the next Scotland manager and it is his “dream last job”, sources have told Football Insider.

The 2026-27 campaign is likely to be Moyes' last at Everton unless their impending takeover changes his circumstances.

American owners The Friedkin Group (TFG) recently announced their intention to seek outside investment or a full sale of the Merseyside club.

Moyes is in the final year of his contract with the club, which expires next June, and talks over a new deal are yet to progress amid uncertainty over the future of their owners.

He is not believed to be on the same page as TFG after feeling like he was left well short of new signings in the summer transfer window.

Sources say the Scotland job is hugely appealing to Moyes and he would like to manage his country before retiring from the dugout.

Moyes could be relieved of his duties by Everton's potential next owners, but they may hand him a new deal if he continues to do an impressive job at the Hill Dickinson Stadium.

He has split opinions among fans since returning to the club, but has the Toffees sat sixth in the Premier League and into the quarter-finals of the Carabao Cup right now.

The 63-year-old will not be short of suitors if he is forced out of Everton, with sources revealing to Football Insider that Celtic are keen on appointing Moyes to succeed Martin O'Neill.

He is looking to return to his home country, but would prefer to manage the national team above a Premiership club.

Sebastien Pocognoli is coming under increasing pressure as Scotland boss after winning just two of his first six games in charge since taking over after the World Cup.

Moyes would like to be in the frame to follow the Belgian manager if he is relieved of his duties in the coming months.

After being asked by talkSPORT co-host Jeff Stelling if he would like to take charge of his country, ex-Scotland star McCoist put Moyes forward as his number one pick to succeed the struggling Pocognoli.

He said: "It's an obvious answer and it ain't going to happen: David Moyes. I think it's a no-brainer. What he's doing at Everton right now is absolutely remarkable.

"Moyes could have the Scotland job tomorrow if he wanted it. And I think one day it might suit him to take it. To be brutally honest with you, Jeff, I think he'd be perfect for it. But not now. He would be my number one choice."
