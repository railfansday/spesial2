Wade Forster Dominates 2027 Golden Guitar Nominations

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Australia's flagship country music awards ceremony will be presented on Saturday, Jan. 23 during the Toyota 55th Tamworth Country Music Festival.

With 10 nominations, Wade Forster is in the fast lane heading into the 55th annual Golden Guitar Awards, Australia’s flagship country music awards ceremony.

Forster is coming off a career year during which he made his Grand Ole Opry debut, sold out a run of U.S. shows, released his third studio album The Aftermath (via Community Music), and completed a coast-to-coast Australian tour in support of it. The singer, songwriter and guitarist is in solid form having collected two Golden Guitar trophies earlier in 2026 (male artist of the year and new talent of the year), off the back of his sophomore 2025 release Gooseneck Party.

In what is believed to be a first for the Golden Guitars, Forster has two albums nominated in separate categories for the 2026 event, to be presented in Tamworth, New South Wales, Australia’s home of country music; Gooseneck Party, produced by Simon Johnson, is up for alt country/Americana album of the year and album of the year, while The Aftermath, also produced by Johnson, is a chance for contemporary country album of the year and competes for album of the year.

Nominations in 16 categories were called out Tuesday (Nov. 17) at a lunchtime party in central Sydney, part of the ARIA Awards week of events. Among the next best-placed artists are Brooke McClymont & Adam Eckersley and James Johnston, with six nominations, while Mack Geiger collected five.

Close behind is Catherine Britt, Dean Perrett, Felicity Urquhart & Josh Cunningham, Morgan Evans and William Alexander, all with nominations in four categories. Meanwhile, Beccy Cole, Sons of Atticus, Kingswood, The French Family Band and newcomer Tyla Rodrigues each scoop three nominations, while Fanny Lumsden leads a bunch of artists with two chances.

Guests at the launch function were treated to performances by Jake Whittaker, Zac and George, and opened with Beccy Cole and Duncan Toombs.

“The future of Australian country music is certainly in great hands, and the Golden Guitar Awards continue to celebrate the extraordinary talent, creativity and diversity that make our industry so special,” remarks awards executive producer Peter Ross who, along with CMAA chair Dobe Newton, was one of the guest speakers on the day.

All told, 580 entries were accepted for awards, which will be presented Saturday, Jan. 23 during the Toyota 55th Tamworth Country Music Festival, which is expected to host some 30,000 country music fans on each of its 10 days.

Tamworth Country Music Festival is supported by the NSW government through its tourism and major events agency, Destination NSW. Tamworth Regional Council will present the ceremony, Australia’s longest-running music awards show, at the Tamworth Regional Entertainment and Conference Centre.
