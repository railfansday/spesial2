Derby County have been in preliminary contact with Steve Cooper about replacing John Eustace as manager, sources have told Football Insider.

The Rams' current boss is expected to depart in the coming weeks, with his side sitting 23rd in the Championship table after 14 games.

Football Insider already revealed that upcoming games against Bolton Wanderers and Southampton could be Eustace's final ones in charge.

Cooper is a leading contender to take over at Pride Park, but it is understood that the 46-year-old is not 100 per cent convinced after his time at Leicester City.

During his time at the King Power Stadium, where he won just 27 per cent of his games, the Welshman struggled to win over the fans due to his strong links with Nottingham Forest.

That could certainly be an issue at Pride Park this season, given the very strong rivalry between the two clubs.

This isn't the first time Cooper has been linked with the Derby job, with his previous mention coming in November of 2020.

As the Rams parted company with Phillip Cocu, the Welshman was quickly installed as the favourite to take over at Pride Park.

He was with Swansea City at the time, and he responded to the links in a very public manner, showing loyalty to those at the Swansea.com Stadium.

Right now, though, the 46-year-old has been out of work since May, having been sacked by Denmark side Brondby at the end of last season.

Eustace still has time to turn things around in the East Midlands, but it is looking increasingly likely that a change is on its way.

While Cooper appears to be the favourite right now, there are several managers currently out of work.

According to one major bookmaker, the Welshman is the most likely at 2/1, with Rob Edwards up next at 5/1 and Steven Gerrard at 6/1.

Football Insider understands that Edwards is also being discussed by Wolves' hierarchy, with recent doubts over Cesar Peixoto's tenure.

Eustace will be in charge for Derby's clash against Bolton on Friday, 6 November, and potentially still in the dugout against Southampton as well.
