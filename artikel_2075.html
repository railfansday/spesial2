Top 20 most valuable EFL Championship strikers right now

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Championship campaign is already nearing its halfway point, and many individuals have made headlines so far this season for differing reasons.

Several sides will now have one eye on achieving a play-off berth come May with the second tier table extremely congested, whilst for others, their immediate aim will be to stave off relegation to League One.

Of course, having a defence which is extremely tough to break down, as well as a combative midfield, can go a long way to achieving such short-term goals, but, ultimately, those at the top end of the pitch are paid top dollar for a reason.

Some of the Championship's forwards have already hit double-figures, and interest is already swirling over their services ahead of the January window, but are they among the 20 most valuable in the division according to Transfermarkt's current market values? Football League World take a look...

This is list is NOT based on FLW's opinion, it's based on Transfermarkt's valuations. All figures converted from Euros to GBP

Getting this list underway is Burnley's Swiss international forward, Zeki Amdouni.

The 25-year-old signed a five-year contract at Turf Moor in July 2023, but only recently hit a double-figure goal tally for the Clarets in just over 50 appearances.

At present, he holds a market value of £5.8m.

West Bromwich Albion's impressive start to the season has come about for many reasons, whilst Aune Heggebo continues to spearhead James Morrison's attack.

The Norwegian signed from SK Brann last summer for £4.75m, and his 13 goal contributions proved vital in the Baggies' eventual survival last term, before continuing to lead the line well alongside summer recruit, Jimmy-Jay Morgan.

Heggebo's market value has increased to £5.8m as a result.

However, one man whose market valuation has decreased to £5.8m is Sheffield United forward, Tom Cannon.

The five-time Republic of Ireland international set the Blades back a reported £10m from Leicester City in January 2025, but the striker's form has left such a figure rather unjustified.

Despite scoring six times last term, Cannon was the subject of reported summer interest, but remained in S2 and has since endured mixed runs of gametime.

The last striker on this list with a £5.8m market value is Birmingham City's highly-rated Dane, August Priske.

Priske, 22, signed from Djurgardens IF for £6m in January, and after an adaptation period, has started to come into his own at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park.

The well-rounded centre-forward is already closing in on a double-figure tally in all competitions despite missing a handful of games in recent weeks through injury.

George Hirst is the first of five strikers on this list with a current valuation of £6.6m.

There were question marks over Stoke City's decision to part with potentially £10m to secure the Scottish international's signature from newly-promoted Premier League side, Ipswich Town.

The 27-year-old will be hoping, though, that his recent winner against Birmingham provides a platform for goalscoring success in the Potteries.

David Strelec also holds a £6.6m valuation at present, with the Middlesbrough forward still yet to convince many at the Riverside Stadium.

It was reported that the deal which took the Slovakian to the North East from Slovan Bratislava could be worth up to £8.5m with potential add-ons, but after a return of eight goals in 32 appearances last season, the 25-year-old's start to the current term has been one of frustration due to injury.

Luca Kjerrumgaard of Watford is also said to hold a £6.6m valuation, with the 23-year-old's initial loan from sister club, Udinese, being made permanent over the summer.

The Danish striker scored 10 times in 43 appearances at Vicarage Road last term as the Hornets finished 16th after a campaign led by three different head coaches.

A disastrous start to the new season means the striker will soon be under his fifth boss in just over a year following the departure of Alessio Dionisi.

Richard Kone signed for Queens Park Rangers from Wycombe Wanderers for an initial £2.75m fee, which could rise to an overall £5m if various add-ons are met.

Following on from a 21-goal campaign in Buckinghamshire, the Ivorian found the target 10 times in his first 43 Championship appearances in West London, and has started the current season in encouraging form, seeing his valuation increase to approximately £6.6m in the process.

The fifth and final striker on this particular ranking with a £6.6m market valuation right now is Norwich City's Jovon Makama.

Makama moved to Carrow Road from Lincoln City last summer for £1.2m, and there are certainly signs that such a figure will be a coup for the Canaries.

Despite a long-term injury lay-off, the England youth international is already closing in on 20 strikes for the Norfolk side in under 50 appearances.

Millwall parted with a club-record fee of more than £5m last summer to sign Josh Coburn from Middlesbrough on a permanent basis, and it certainly looked an inspired piece of business last season for the Lions.

The 6"4 man led Alex Neil's line extremely well last season in just 26 appearances, during which he scored nine times but also demonstrated fine link-up play with his fellow attackers.

The current season has been one of injury frustration for the 23-year-old, although it is claimed he is still worth £7.5m in the current market.
