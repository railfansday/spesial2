Stefan Borson: Aston Villa 'well placed' to benefit from NBA expansion

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Aston Villa could benefit from a new revenue stream due to the owners' connections with the National Basketball Association (NBA).

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that discussions over the NBA's European adventure are likely already advanced.

Sky Sports reported on Monday, 16 November, that the NBA is looking to expand into Europe, backed by billions of dollars worth of funding.

There is a lot of excitement around a bigger basketball culture coming to Europe, and the Villans could certainly benefit from it.

Villa co-owner Wes Edens has plenty of experience in American sports, having owned the Milwaukee Bucks since 2014.

Many at Villa Park have expected Edens to recreate the same success in the Premier League, and the Europa League title has been a close second.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson believes that the links between Edens and the NBA could almost certainly benefit Villa as talks over a European expansion begin.

"There's already a lot of relationships around Europe with the local football clubs, like Fenerbahce or Barcelona. They all have their own basketball teams," he said.

"I would think there's a really good chance that if the European NBA is serious and wants a number of teams in England, Villa are going to be very well placed to join.

"To be honest, we're probably quite a long way down the line of discussions, and I suspect a lot of certainly exploratory conversations are happening with the NBA if they press ahead with this European NBA."

Hosting games for the European NBA would be more than exciting, that is if Villa can build a venue to hold such sporting events.

Villa's owners continue to play a key role behind the scenes in B6, while several other Premier League clubs are wanting a change at the highest level.

Their latest move has been to tie Unai Emery down to new terms at Villa Park, as the talented manager continues to be linked with deals elsewhere.

Despite the inability to splash so much cash, Emery never panics and always brings Villa back to where they should be in competitions.

The owners are only going to continue making these smart moves behind the scenes, and that could be the case with this European NBA venture.
