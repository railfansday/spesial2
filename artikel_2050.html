Birmingham City need to assess 25-year-old ahead of Bolton Wanderers clash after Burnley omission

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Birmingham City left the right-back out of their trip to Burnley on Wednesday night after he picked up a knock against Stoke City last weekend. BCFC beat the Clarets 2-0 at Turf Moor with striker August Priske on the scoresheet, whilst Jaydon Banel scored an own goal.

Laird, 25, has made 13 appearances for the Blues in all competitions this season, 12 of which have come in the Championship. He is under contract with his current club until the summer of 2028 and has been with them since 2023.

The Midlands outfit will need to assess him ahead of their clash against Bolton Wanderers at home on Saturday. In this latest update regarding his condition, his head coach Davies has said, as per a report by BirminghamLive: “I don’t think he’s long. It’s a knock. My understanding is it’s one or two games max.”

Birmingham City are positioned in 4th place in the table after their first 16 fixtures of the campaign. They are in the play-offs along with Wolves, West Brom, Southampton, QPR and Swansea City as they chase down Middlesbrough and Stoke City.
