Microsoft has not said when Outlook, Teams and other Microsoft 365 services will be fully working again, as the global outage continues past 10 hours. Its next update is due at 5 pm Australian Eastern time, which is 11:30 am IST on Friday. In its latest statement, Microsoft said its fixes triggered a sharp rise in traffic from its routing systems to backend servers, which could not route requests properly. The company is adding more virtual machines to ease the load and trying to force a recent network change onto servers stuck in a faulty state. However, many of those machines are not accepting the update as expected. Microsoft said some services have partly recovered, but the improvement has not held, and the outage is not believed to be caused by malicious activity. For now, SharePoint users can still open, edit and save files, though shared mailboxes and calendars that rely on Exchange Online may not work properly.
