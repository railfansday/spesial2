Sharon AI Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Total Contract Value1 of ~$9.6bn over 5.5 years2, including $2.5bn contracted since the second quarter of 2026

ARR3 of $50m in the third quarter of 2026, anticipated to increase to $400-$450m in January 2027 as the Company continues to deploy successfully

212MW of total capacity expected in 2027 across Australia and New Zealand, including ~155MW4 expected to be available for sale into the high-demand, short and long-term GPU compute market

NEW YORK, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- SharonAI Holdings Inc. (NASDAQ: SHAZ) and its subsidiaries ("Sharon AI" or "the Company"), a leading Australian Neocloud, today reported its financial and operational results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

All amounts are in U.S. dollars unless otherwise indicated.

In the fourth quarter 2026 to-date, the Company has demonstrated continued business momentum:

"Since December 2025, Sharon AI has secured approximately $2.6 billion of capital, including our inaugural GPU-backed facility valued at $356m, establishing a repeatable financing structure supported by customer contracts," said Anuj Goel, Chief Financial Officer of Sharon AI. "Further GPU-backed facilities to fund the next stage of the build-out are well advanced. The market remains open and highly interested in our secured GPU-backed debt which we intend to raise against signed take-or-pay contracts, secured against the GPUs dedicated to them. The majority of our TCV1 is with customers that hold investment-grade credit ratings, and we expect greater than 75% of our aggregate secured debt packages to stem from the NVIDIA AI Compute Program."

"In the third quarter, we advanced the conversion of our contracted commitments into live AI infrastructure at scale, delivering and achieving customer acceptance of the initial deployment for our global technology customer," said James Manning, Co-founder and Chief Executive Officer of Sharon AI. "We also achieved SOC 2 Type 2 attestation, an important security and compliance milestone.

"Globally, demand for AI compute is outpacing the power available to deliver it, and this is where Australia, and Sharon AI, stand apart. Our TCV1 has increased to ~$9.6 billion over 5.5 years2, with this TCV only including the floor price of the NVIDIA AI Compute Program for ~40,000 NVIDIA GB300s online in 2027.

"Over the course of 2026 we have seen pricing on our long-term contracts strengthen from ~$15 million to more than $19 million annualized revenue per MW, with new customer prepayments at up to 30% of TCV or 60% of capex. In relation to the NVIDIA AI Compute Program, we expect to sign shorter-duration contracts at over $30 million annualized revenue per MW.

"With power in place, we are executing on delivery alongside our deployment partners, and that is our focus in the fourth quarter and beyond. We expect to complete commissioning, testing and NVIS verification of the ESDS and global technology customer deployments in December 2026 and January 2027, taking ARR3 from approximately $50 million for September 2026 to an anticipated $400-450m ARR3 for January 2027, with our additional deployments, including the NVIDIA AI Compute Program, following through 2027. With a strong long-term contracted customer base, live deployments and ~155MW4 of capacity available for sale, strengthening short and long-term GPU pricing and access to institutional capital, we are building a leading sovereign AI compute platform at increasing scale across Australia and New Zealand."

The Company defines Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss) adjusted to exclude: (i) interest expense (income), net; (ii) income tax expense (benefit); (iii) depreciation and amortization; (iv) stock-based compensation expense; (v) fair value adjustments on convertible notes; and (vi) other non-cash or non-recurring items that management does not consider indicative of the Company's ongoing operating performance. Adjusted EBITDA is not a substitute for net income (loss) or any other measure of financial performance prepared in accordance with U.S. GAAP and may not be comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies. Management believes Adjusted EBITDA is useful to investors because it provides a supplemental measure of the Company's core operating performance by excluding the effects of capital structure decisions (such as interest expense and fair value changes on convertible notes), non-cash charges (such as depreciation, amortization and stock-based compensation), and tax impacts that can vary significantly between periods and across companies. Management uses Adjusted EBITDA to evaluate the Company's performance, compare performance across periods, and assist in the allocation of resources. Investors are cautioned that Adjusted EBITDA has limitations as an analytical tool and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of the Company's results as reported under U.S. GAAP.
