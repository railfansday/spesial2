Korean Entertainment Hybe and CAA Form Strategic Partnership

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The company is home to K-pop groups like BTS, Le Sserafim, Cortis and Seventeen.

Korean entertainment giant Hybe has entered into a strategic partnership with CAA, the talent agencies announced Monday. The move aims to expand the creative and commercial opportunities for Hybe artists and the company’s IP.

CAA and Hybe will collaborate across content, brand partnerships, sports, licensing and merchandising, using live experiences to “build new businesses and create greater opportunities for Hybe and the company’s artists,” according to a release. The partnership will work to build initiatives across Hybe’s key Asian markets, including Korea, Japan, China and India, along with the United States. “This partnership with CAA marks an important step in expanding creative possibilities for Hybe artists,” the company’s CEO Jason Jaesang Lee said in a statement.

“By bringing together our complementary strengths and global networks, we aim to extend the reach and value of our artists and their IP across content, brands, and live experiences,” Lee continued. “Together, we will explore new creative opportunities that transcend borders and formats, bringing audiences closer to our artists through more immersive and meaningful experiences.”

Hybe is home to some of the biggest K-pop groups both domestically and globally. BTS, Le Sserafim, New Jeans, Cortis, Seventeen, Enhypen, Illit, Boynextdoor and TWS are among some of the acts under Hybe’s sublabels.

“Hybe has created a cultural movement,” CAA CEO and co-chairman Bryan Lourd said in a statement. “Hybe artists do not just entertain; they connect with audiences in extraordinary and innovative ways that define the future of our industry and the intersection of art, influence, and fandom. We are honored to help carry that forward, building on CAA’s experience and relationships across the region, to show the world what is possible when two organizations that believe in creative talent come together.”
