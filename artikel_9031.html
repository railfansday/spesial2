New Eames Museum in California Funded in Part by Trump Appointee

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Executive Editor, ARTnews & Art in America

A new museum devoted to design legends Charles and Ray Eames in the Bay Area of Northern California received more than $25 million in initial funding from Joe Gebbia, an Airbnb cofounder who is currently a Tesla board member and previously worked with Elon Musk’s Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE), according to a report by SFGATE.

“Gebbia is an unexpected benefactor behind the museum in Marin County,” the publication wrote, after citing the $25-million figure from the North Bay Business Journal. “After co-founding Airbnb in San Francisco in 2007, which helped him to eventually earn over $9 billion, Gebbia turned to politics and joined DOGE last year. President Donald Trump later tapped Gebbia to become the chief design officer of the United States and redesign government websites. The Eames Institute of Infinite Curiosity is slated to take over the former campus of the Birkenstock shoe company, with a building design by the Swiss architecture firm Herzog & de Meuron and landscape design by Field Operations, the firm behind New York’s High Line park.

Gebbia’s support of the nonprofit represents a considerable chunk. The Eames Institute raised over $165 million in contributions from 2021 through 2024, anchored by Gebbia’s $25 million.

Asked about Gebbia’s connection to the planned museum—with his $25 million making up a significant portion of the $165 million raised between 2021 and 2024, Eames Institute president and CEO John Cary told SFGATE that donors have no outside influence. “Joe’s views are his own and do not reflect the Eames Institute,” he said.
