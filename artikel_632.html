Sebastian Stan Says ‘Thunderbolts’ Had ‘More Nuance’ Than ‘Some of the Recent’ Marvel Movies and Was a ‘Success’ Despite Disappointing Box Office

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Marvel veteran Sebastian Stan is sticking up for last year’s “Thunderbolts,” despite it being one of the lowest-grossing MCU movies.

In the team-up movie, he reprised his role as Bucky Barnes, aka the Winter Soldier, alongside Florence Pugh as Yelena Belova and David Harbour as the Red Guardian to form the Thunderbolts, a new group in the MCU. After releasing in May 2025, “Thunderbolts” ended its box office run with $382 million worldwide, ranking as the fifth lowest total out of all 38 MCU films. The only movies below it are “Black Widow” ($379 million), “Captain America: The First Avenger” ($370 million), “The Incredible Hulk” ($264 million) and “The Marvels” ($206 million). They’re all a far cry from this summer’s “Spider-Man: Brand New Day,” which is the second biggest MCU movie with $2.5 billion (behind the highest-grossing film ever, “Avengers: Endgame” with $2.94 billion).

“I think it’s just when you’ve had a studio and you’ve had Marvel and it’s been so many years where they’ve just been knocking it out of the park, $350 million global, something like that, it just suddenly doesn’t seem that much,” Stan said on “Next Best Picture.” “But it’s insane how much money that is.”

He added, “For us, it felt like a success. I know a lot of care went into that and it had more nuance to it in terms of some of the recent ones, from my point of view at least, dealing with real issues like mental health and depression and so on.”

“Thunderbolts” was praised by fans and critics for its darker tone and how it elevated its cast of misfit, lesser-known characters. Its director, Jake Schreier, was tapped by Marvel to helm its 2028 “X-Men” movie, with a cast including Sadie Sink, Adam Driver, Inde Navarrette, Kit Connor and more.
