Bowen Yang Apologizes for Calling Regina Spektor an Antisemitic Slur, Didn’t Know It Was ‘Popularized by Far-Right Movements’

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Bowen Yang has apologized after he received backlash recently for calling singer Regina Spektor an antisemitic slur on a past episode of his podcast, “Las Culturistas.” A resurfaced clip from his Oct. 14 podcast came under fire when Yang referred to Spektor as “Zio.”

“I want to genuinely apologize for using a word on the podcast without knowing its antisemitic history, especially since we’ve repudiated antisemitism on the show as recently as the last two weeks,” Yang wrote on Instagram on Friday. “Particularly upsetting to realize that I adopted language popularized by far-right movements whose rhetoric we should be wary of and act against.” Related Stories Tony Award Winner Lois Smith Joins Colin West’s ‘Outside of Cleveland’: ‘She’s an Icon’ (EXCLUSIVE) 'Chasing Summer' Director Josephine Decker Says That ‘Filmmaking Is Lonelier Than Ever,’ So It’s Time to Build a Community

“Zio,” short for “Zionist,” has been used as a derogatory slur toward Jewish people and Israelis. Its origins trace back to Ku Klux Klan leader David Duke and white supremacists.

On the Oct. 14 episode of “Las Culturistas,” Yang, his co-host Matt Rogers and guest Edy Modica were discussing Spektor, her alma mater SUNY Purchase and her music when Modica added, “And now she’s out to lunch.” Rogers asked if Modica was “upset with Regina lately,” to which she responded, “she’s just like a little Israel hardcore.” Yang added, “yeah, she’s Zio.”

In recent days, social media users found the resurfaced clip of the podcast and called out Yang for using the slur. “Bowen Yang using antisemitic slang that was popularized and mainstreamed by KKK leader David Duke is really just chefs kiss for the guy who got Shane Gillis fired from ‘SNL’ for racism,” wrote Meghan McCain on X. “Well done buying this hate mongering podcast Disney.” “Las Culturistas” began streaming on Disney+ and Hulu on Wednesday.

“Bowen Yang, Matt Rogers & soooo many people LOVE preaching inclusion, compassion & protecting the dolls. UNTIL IT COMES TO JEWS,” Michael Rapaport posted. “They dismiss the GREAT Regina Spektor for supporting Israel. Funny how it’s ALWAYS Jewish Zionists they single out. Never Black, Christian, Muslim or LGBTQ Zionists. The self-appointed COOL KIDS CLUB is judgmental, mean & needs fixing Girl….Faye Dunaway would NEVER.”
