This Black Friday shopping hack could help you save $15 on GTA 6 for PS5

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

SAVE $15 TO $40.01: Costco members can buy bundles of Nintendo eShop or Sony PlayStation digital gift cards at a discount, giving you some extra funds to buy popular video games.

Costco members always have some exclusive Black Friday deals that make the rest of us jealous. Sometimes it's the largest pack of energy drinks you've ever seen, and other times it's a deal on an Apple MacBook. You can check out our full guide to Costco Black Friday deals, but today we're highlighting a gift card deal that lets you get an extra $15 to spend on the PlayStation 5 game of your choice (or up to $40.01 to spend at the Nintendo eShop).

Costco is currently offering members bundles of digital gift cards for the Sony PlayStation Store and Nintendo eShop — at a discount. That means you can buy a package of two $75 Sony PlayStation gift cards (a $150 value) for only $135. You can also get four $50 Nintendo eShop gift cards (a $200 value) for only $159.99. That gives you an extra $15 to spend on a game like, say, GTA 6.

If you've been suffering from the generational FOMO that comes with not playing GTA 6, then maybe this is the push you need to give it a shot. The new Grand Theft Auto is an instant classic, and it still has no official PC release date.

Of course, you don't have to spend your extra $15 on any particular game. Though, we do have a recommendation for Nintendo gamers as well. The new The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake is probably the second-most-anticipated game of the year, and it's available now for the Switch 2 console.
