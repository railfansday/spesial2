Johan Manzambi slammed, John McGinn threatened: Four things you may have missed from Aston Villa v Galatasaray

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Correspondent AUTHORITY Football journalist with experience at Reach PLC and PA Media; University of Central Lancashire graduate. FOCUS Fan-engagement content and match analysis across the Premier League and Championship. THE INSIGHT Oliver utilises a network of club and industry contacts to deliver verified reporting and fan-first content. He provides the human stories behind the results to ensure fans get the full picture as it happens.

Johan Manzambi was among the Aston Villa stars to struggle as they were beaten 1-0 by Galatasaray in the Champions League on Tuesday.

Deniz Gul's late header sealed all three points for the hosts as Villa suffered a second loss of the Champions League campaign.

The first half was full of endeavour from both sides, but neither could muster a shot on target before the break.

Galatasaray went close through Aleksey Batrakov and Yunus Akgun in the early stages of the second half before Gul netted in the 77th minute.

The game was full of flashpoints and controversies that were lost to the intense atmosphere inside RAMS Park.

Football Insider have picked out four moments from the clash that some Villa supporters may have failed to spot.

Villa sometimes let the intimidating Istanbul atmosphere get to them in the first half, officially misplacing 26 passes as a team.

One of those nearly resulted in a Galatasaray opener, as Swiss starlet Mazambi gave the ball away to Akgun in the 20th minute, who was ultimately thwarted by Mings in the area.

Mings then stood with his arms outstretched towards Manzambi and made it clear that he was unhappy with his teammate for his sloppiness, in an unseen moment that showed just how tough the hosts made it for Villa.

The cameras initially failed to capture Jackson's 41st-minute booking, which was the first of the evening to be produced by referee Daniel Siebert.

It was quickly revealed that the Villa striker was shown a yellow for slowing down the chance for Gala's players to take a free kick after Ian Maatsen had committed a foul while trying to attack on the left wing.

Jackson threw the ball over for Davinson Sanchez to boot it upfield, but then quickly ran to kick it further back to where he believed the offence had taken place.

He then stood in front of the ball to block the free-kick and was correctly booked for delaying the restart.

It was a tense and tetchy night at RAMS Park that threatened to boil over on several occasions in the second half.

The first of those moments came in the 53rd minute when Villa captain McGinn crashed into Gala midfielder Batrakov before the pair got to their feet and clashed as others rushed in.

While they each earned bookings, replays showed that Batrakov very clearly kicked out at the Scottish star while on the floor before a scuffle ensued.

It was easy to miss and could have landed him in hot water had the officials been more diligent.

Under IFAB's new law changes, a substituted player must leave the pitch within 10 seconds at the nearest boundary line, or their replacement must wait until the next stoppage after at least one minute of play

Gomes made Siebert enforce the rule in the 66th minute as he took too long to come off for Leon Goretzka.

Villa were then forced to play with 10 men for the next 60 seconds with Gala on the attack, and will have been relieved not to go behind before they did shortly after.
