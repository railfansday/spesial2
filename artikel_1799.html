How to watch Wales vs Portugal: TV channel and live stream for Nations League today

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Wales take on Portugal this evening in the last of their Nations League group matches.

Craig Bellamy’s side are bottom of Group D with just three points, with Jorge Jesus’ side top of the table.

They will be without talisman Cristiano Ronaldo, who retired from international football last month.

They were thrashed by Denmark 4-1 last time out, suffering their first defeat of the competition this term.

The two-time winners were battered in Braga, with just Bernardo Silva’s strike as consolation.

Former Tottenham and Southampton midfielder Pierre-Emile Hojbjerg netted the opener, with ex-Manchester United striker Rasmus Hojlund scoring a brace.

Joachim Andersen, the Fulham centre-back, put the final cherry on top in a massive three points to close the gap.

Wales, meanwhile, suffered a 3-1 loss to Norway last time out, with Antonio Nusa and Andreas Schjelderup on target before Erling Haaland’s late penalty.

David Brooks pulled one back for the visitors, but it was too little, too late for them at the Ullevaal Stadium in Oslo.

TV channel: You can watch this game live and for free on BBC One, with coverage starting at 7.15pm GMT ahead of the 7.45pm GMT kick-off.

Live stream: You can also stream the game live on the BBC iPlayer app and website.
