Aptera Motors Has Finally Delivered Its First Customer Car

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

After nearly 20 years of starts and stops, the first solar-powered EV hits the road.

“Car company sells a car” seems like an unusual headline, but when it comes to Aptera Motors, things are different. For two decades, the company has been showing off concepts and prototypes, changing names and deadlines, and not actually delivering anything. That all just changed with the first delivery of a customer car.

The customer in question is Martin Hartono, an Indonesian CEO who is the largest early investor in Aptera. That investment puts him first in the queue for the production car, and he has now taken delivery. Reportedly, the car will be shipped overseas, presumably to Indonesia.

The Aptera is a three-wheeler that looks more like an aircraft than a car, capable of seating just two people. It has a 44-kWh battery pack and uses the NACS-style charger. Light weight was a target for engineers, with the stated curb weight of 2200 pounds making it more like a Mazda MX-5 than a Tesla Model 3.

The calling card for the Aptera is its set of onboard solar panels, which are said to be capable of returning as much as 40 miles of range per day. That’s 10 percent of the planned 400-mile total range and should be measurably useful in someplace sunny like California. Beyond this ability to charge on the move, the slippery aerodynamics and light weight should make it very efficient.

Hartono’s vehicle was spec’d out in a matte black finish that makes it look like something that should shoot out of a larger Batmobile. It’ll be the first of its kind on the roads once it hits them and should look especially unusual in Indonesian traffic.

A single car does not make a company profitable, so Aptera Motors still has a long way to go. However, having taken so long to arrive at this moment, perhaps it deserves a little celebration.
