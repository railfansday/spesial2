Swansea City face significant blow as European club make approach for Vitor Matos

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Swansea City have been a real surprise package to start this Championship season. The Swans have build on how Vitor Matos started his career in Wales well and have led the second tier for much of the opening months of the year.

Now, with the Welsh outfit sitting in the Championship play-off spots, they could be set for a major blow. According to BBC South Wales reporter Gareth Vincent, Matos could be about to move on from the club with Ligue 1 side Strasbourg keen on the 38-year-old.

Matos has been approached by the French club already despite their current boss Huge Oliveira still being in the job. However, it looks likely that he will be sacked in the coming days with Matos as his replacement.

Swansea City have made efforts to keep Matos at the club but he is keen on the move, according to David Ornstein. Sporting CP had been linked with a move for the 38-year-old last month, while Crystal Palace had also had their eye on him before making the move for Thomas Frank.

The French side appointed Portuguese coach Oliveira in July 2026 following Gary O’Neil’s departure to Ipswich Town, but results have failed to improve. Three wins from 10 Ligue 1 matches, including five defeats in six, have left them 13th, just above the relegation zone.

They have also conceded around 22 goals, highlighting their defensive struggles. Matos has emerged as an attractive candidate after transforming Swansea’s fortunes.

The Swans’ impressive defensive record to start this year will no doubt attract Strasbourg given their struggles. Taking charge in November 2025 with the club 20th in the Championship, he guided them to 11th before establishing them as promotion contenders this season.

The 38-year-old also brings five years of Liverpool coaching experience under Jurgen Klopp, with his high-intensity pressing philosophy and reputation for developing young players appealing to BlueCo. While it would be a blow to Swansea City, Matos and BlueCo do feel like a perfect fit.

Swansea City made a bold move when appointing Matos last season. He had very little experience as a head coach but it has proved to be a brilliant appointment and one that will earn them a hefty compensation fee at the very least.

With his departure looking quite likely, given his interest in the Strasbourg move, the Swans should be given trust when it comes to replacing him. Not only was the Matos appointment itself proof, but their recent transfer windows have also been strong.

All in all their recruitment with both players and staff recently has been very good and transformed them as a side. Their is no reason to believe that their next appointment will not also be a success.
