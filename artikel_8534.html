1) To be a little pedantic, "taxable bond" is a technical term that means a bond subject to tax, not "bonds in taxable." So you have no taxable bonds in taxable you say "cash like bonds" but SUB has a duration of 1.95 and VTEB of 7.32. Even factoring in your MMF, your overall duration of your bonds in taxable is 3.1 years. So you have more duration than you think.

There's nothing necessarily wrong with what you are doing here. It depends how involved you want to be in watching interest rates, and your target duration. You could always shorten up by selling VTEB (likely with little/no tax consequences) closer to retirement and going into something else.

2) This depends on what you want from your bonds specifically. However I will note that, historically, an intermediate treasury fund would have been a better choice than total bond.

3) Seems fine.

4) I don't think I'd count your single stock positions as part of your retirement assets. Consider it a buffer at best.

Also, some on this site will raise an eyebrow at the plan to retire at 52 with 25x saved. This is considered a fairly early retirement and your approach to retirement will be different than the generic advice on this site. Building up a nice buffer of muni bonds will help with this, but you will at least need a flexible spending plan and a plan to access tax-sheltered accounts if needed.
