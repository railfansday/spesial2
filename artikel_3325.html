Jon Bernthal’s 100-Minute Taylor Sheridan Thriller Is Officially Cut From Netflix

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

If you’ve binged Taylor Sheridan’s TV universe twice over, it’s time to check out one of his movie projects that will be leaving Netflix on December 1, making November 30 your last chance to watch. Those Who Wish Me Dead is a 2021 action thriller starring Angelina Jolie and Jon Bernthal as characters who face the worst of nature and humanity during one wild fight for survival. It features all that’s good about the Yellowstone creator’s storytelling, combined with two stars who know how to make you feel every step of their journey.

The Oscar-winning star plays Hannah Faber, a smokejumper (wildland firefighter) in the Montana wilderness who is haunted by a past failure that cost lives. Now living in isolation, her simple routine is shattered when she encounters Connor (Finn Little), a young man who witnesses a murder and now has the killers on his tail. Hannah must use all of her experience to navigate a forest which is being ravaged by a wildfire, in order to evade capture and confront the demons of her past.

It is the third film to be directed by Sheridan, who also co-wrote the script, and has been compared to cult ‘90s survival thrillers like Cliffhanger and The River Wild, where nature becomes as foreboding as the villains. Hannah and Connor’s every turn is blocked by a new life-or-death challenge, with the emotional stakes being raised constantly. It’s an entertaining story of redemption, elevated by the traits that made Sheridan a TV mogul.

Sheridan has shown strength as a director of mid-budget dramas and thrillers intended for adult audiences.

Taylor Sheridan had already scored big with Yellowstone at the time of the film’s release, starting down a path that would see him become synonymous with big stars, big ranches, and even bigger plot twists. Those Who Wish Me Dead has a different setting, but there’s so much in the story that is typical of what Sheridan does best. His signature themes of blue-collar heroics, the burden of responsibility, and frontier morality are all present, as are low-key protagonists who are reluctantly drawn back into a world that forces them to deal with the past.

He delivers action free of the fantasy trappings of genre cinema. You won’t find any special powers or enchanted weapons here, just the will to survive and the force of ordinary people pushed to the limit. Sheridan would write more celebrated movies, such as the crime thriller Hell or High Water and the Sicario films, but if you’re a completist of his work, then this is a cinematic adventure his fans won’t regret seeking out.

Since his breakthrough on the TV show The Walking Dead, Bernthal has specialized in gritty, working-class characters who have more depth than their gruff exteriors suggest. Whether it’s the vigilante fury of The Punisher, the calculating criminality of his role in Baby Driver, or his striking appearance in The Odyssey, he is someone who can bring a lot of impact no matter how high he is on the call sheet.

Those Who Wish Me Dead is no exception, as he holds his own with one of Hollywood’s biggest stars. He plays Ethan Sawyer, a sheriff’s deputy who becomes part of the chase and an ally to Hannah. In many ways, he is a classic Taylor Sheridan hero – a quiet man who does the right thing and gets the job done in order to save innocent lives. He is a direct counterpoint to the villains, in that he is a thoroughly decent man who puts himself in danger to do the right thing. It’s not a huge role in the movie, but it gives an extra human element to a set-piece-heavy movie.

Those Who Wish Me Dead didn’t find the audience it deserved upon release, arriving as the world was still recovering from the 2020 pandemic and people were still reluctant to return to theaters. However, given Sheridan’s status only seems to go from strength to strength, people who want to see even more from him will find everything they love about his TV work in 100 action-packed minutes.
