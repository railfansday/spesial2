Patrick Stewart Cause Of Death Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

We now have the official cause of death for Sir Patrick Stewart. The star of Star Trek: The Next Generation, Star Trek: Picard, the X-Men film franchise and much more died October 28 from Alzheimer’s disease and Lewy body dementia, according to his death certificate obtained by TMZ.

The death certificate also lists cardiopulmonary arrest and a neurocognitive disorder with Lewy body — a progressive form of dementia that affects memory, thinking and movement, according to TMZ. Watch on Deadline

The family statement announcing his death noted that his passing followed “a brief and poignant journey with Alzheimer’s disease and Lewy body dementia.” Stewart was 86.

Stewart, whose career spanned nearly six decades, was a three-time Olivier Award winner and a Tony Award nominee for his stage work, including numerous Shakespeare productions. He won a Grammy in 1995 and received multiple Emmy, Golden Globe and SAG Award nominations for his work across television and film.

In 1987 he took on the role of Captain Jean-Luc Picard in Star Trek: The Next Generation, ensuring his place in TV history and the pop culture landscape. He played the USS Enterprise captain on the popular syndicated drama for seven seasons from 1987-94 and several film installments, beginning with Star Trek: Generations in 1994. He later reprised the role in the global hit Paramount+ series Star Trek: Picard. The series concluded its third and final season in April 2023.

Stewart also was praised for his portrayal of Charles Xavier aka Professor X, a role he originated in the 2000 film X-Men and reprised throughout the franchise’s many hit sequels including the hit Logan, opposite Hugh Jackman as Logan/Wolverine. Stewart also reprised the role in the upcoming Avengers: Doomsday, set for release on December 18, marking his eighth time portraying the character.

Stewart’s many additional screen credits include Coda; L.A. Story; Green Room; Blunt Talk; Robin Hood: Men in Tights; Conspiracy Theory; I, Claudius; Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy; The Kid Who Would Be King; Match; The King of Texas; Jeffrey; Dune and Excalibur. Stewart also did voice work on The Simpsons, Seth MacFarlane’s Family Guy and American Dad! as such films as The Prince of Egypt, Chicken Little and Jimmy Neutron: Boy Genius.
