Bitcoin fell to $68,617, its lowest level in three months. The sell-off forced out hundreds of millions of dollars in leveraged bets within an hour, and XRP dropped to the $1 mark. Bitcoin is down 6.6% over 24 hours and 9.5% over the week. Ethereum fell 6.0% to $1,926.48 and has lost 13.7% in seven days. About $60 billion was wiped off the total crypto market value in four hours, according to reports.

The drop started when Bitcoin broke below $72,000. It then fell through $70,000 and $69,000, and each break set off more forced selling.

Most of the losses fell on traders betting on higher prices. When the market moves against them, their positions are closed automatically, and that selling adds to the drop.

XRP is trading at $1.00, down 4.5% in 24 hours and 17.5% over the past week. That is the steepest weekly loss among the top 10 coins. Its market value has shrunk to about $63.8 billion.

The token was near $1.05 to $1.06 earlier in the week. It is now sitting right on the $1 level, which traders watch closely as a psychological floor.

Selling from spot Bitcoin ETFs in the United States has added to the pressure. From November 5 to November 11, the funds recorded five straight days of net outflows:

That comes to about $1.44 billion in five sessions. BlackRock’s IBIT alone lost $269.7 million on November 11, its ninth outflow session in a row, which brings its total for the run to about $2 billion.

The crypto sell-off is part of a wider pullback from risk:

Bitcoin’s market value has fallen to about $1.38 trillion and it now sits about 46% below its record high of around $126,198, set in October 2025.

On the chart, Bitcoin keeps posting lower highs. Buyers first need to win back $70,000 and then $72,000, the level where the breakdown started. Until then, buying into the drop carries high risk while prices are still falling.
