Baidu, Inc. (BIDU -5.38%) reported a return to year-over-year revenue growth for its general business segment, driven by the rapid expansion of its AI cloud infrastructure. Baidu intends to declare its first-ever cash dividend before the end of 2026 and continues to execute on its share repurchase program, balancing growth investments with capital returns to shareholders.
