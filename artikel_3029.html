The Best Recession-Proof Consumer Stock to Buy With $1,000 Before 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

This company often does even better in rough economies.

By Jennifer Saibil – Nov 18, 2026 at 2:45PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Costco Wholesale ›

Is there a recession coming? There may be economic volatility on the way. Inflation has remained stubbornly high, and rising interest rates meant to combat it may also restrict the booming economy.

No matter what happens, there are certain companies that can continue to outperform, and at the top of that list is Costco Wholesale (COST -0.55%).

Despite continued inflation, Costco has been demonstrating outstanding growth. In the fiscal 2026 fourth quarter (ended Aug. 30), total sales were up 11.2%, and comparable sales were up 9.4%.

Costco has a unique operating model that sets it apart from most other retailers. It charges an annual fee for the privilege of shopping in its store while offering the lowest prices it can on products. That creates loyalty from members and drives higher volume, since shoppers want to get the lowest prices as well as make the most of their memberships.

It basically makes profits from the membership fees, marking up goods just enough to cover associated costs, and everyone wins.

While members love shopping at Costco in any environment, in tough times it becomes essential, and growth rates have accelerated. Gas has become a major growth driver, and Costco's lower gas prices are attracting sales both at the pump and in stores. Management plans to open stand-alone stations, too, to grab the opportunity.

It's also benefiting from e-commerce, which it calls digitally-enabled sales. It doesn't actually do any shipping, but customers can purchase online and pick up curbside or in-store, and it partners with delivery companies for grocery. Digitally-enabled sales increased 20% year over year in the fourth quarter.

Costco stock had been middling for most of this year. The market was worried about slowing membership growth and slightly lower-than-usual renewal rates. But it got a major boost when management announced a special dividend last week.

This is the fifth time in 11 years that it's issuing a dividend, but it's the highest it's ever been at $30 per share. Costco's dividend yield is generally low, but these special dividends make up for that in a big way. The dividend goes to any shareholders on record as of Nov. 24.

Costco stock rose 5% after the announcement, and it's now trouncing the market, up 30% year to date. It's become extremely expensive again, trading at 53 times trailing-12-month earnings.

However, the market sees it as a reliable investment in any environment, especially in the current uncertain economic climate. If you have $1,000 to invest right now, Costco could be an excellent, recession-proof stock to hold for the long term.
