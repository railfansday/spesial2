Canaan Inc. (CAN) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Canaan shifts focus to energy and AI infrastructure as mining demand lags.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Canaan Inc. (CAN -19.15%) reported its results for the quarter ended Sept. 30, 2026, and announced a strategic focus on energy and artificial intelligence high-performance computing (AI HPC) infrastructure. Management reported that the company achieved a milestone for its 1 gigawatt power project in Duval County, Texas, and is advancing a second 1 gigawatt project in Zapata County. The company stated that it is implementing strict capital discipline, reducing research and development spending, and prioritizing shareholder returns through a new share repurchase program. Management indicated that while the bitcoin mining hardware market remains challenged by weak demand and elevated costs, the company intends to utilize its power procurement and site development expertise to participate in larger infrastructure projects.
