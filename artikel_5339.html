AeroBand Brings the Beat to Thanksgiving with $149 Black Friday Deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The portable air-drumming kit brings hands-on music play to holiday gatherings, with vibrating drumsticks, 12 built-in sound kits.

NEW YORK, Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- As families gather for Thanksgiving and the holiday shopping season gets underway, AeroBand is offering the PocketDrum 2 Ultra full set for US$149 on its U.S. online store, US$40 below its US$189 suggested retail price.

AeroBand's PocketDrum 2 Ultra is definitely a playful musical gift from after-dinner fun on Thanksgiving to unwrapping gifts on Christmas morning, AeroBand PocketDrum 2 Ultra brings people together through music. Friends and family can take turns playing the motion-sensing drumsticks, exploring different sounds and feeling every hit. With Auto Groove, even first-time players can trigger a full groove with a simple strike, making it easy for everyone to join in and get the party going.

And when the party is over, the fun doesn't have to stop. PocketDrum 2 Ultra offers an easy way to explore a new hobby and make music part of everyday life.

The first surprise of the holidays is a beat you can feel. PocketDrum 2 Ultra responds to 127 levels of strike intensity. With built-in haptic feedback, the sticks pulse with each hit, letting players feel the rhythm in their hands as they hear it.

At a holiday get-together, friends can take turns playing the same beat with a rock kit, then hear how it changes with jazz or electronic sounds. PocketDrum 2 Ultra has 12 built-in sound kits, so players can pick a different sound as they pass the sticks around. The AeroBand Drum app makes it easy to explore rhythms, have fun, and develop a passion for drumming at your own pace.

Bring It to the Party or Practice at Home

Whether you're heading home for Thanksgiving, traveling for Christmas or simply enjoying a cozy night in, PocketDrum 2 Ultra is easy to take along.

Two sticks, two foot pedals and an adapter fit in a backpack. Each stick weighs around 2 ounces (a large egg). Players can hook up a wired speaker to share the sound at a get-together or plug wired headphones into the adapter for quieter practice at home.
