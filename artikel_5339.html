Microsoft’s Satya Nadella: Jensen Huang ‘a singular person,’ but it’s up to Nvidia to decide whether to bring him back as CEO

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella praised ousted Nvidia founder Jensen Huang as “a singular person” who has contributed immensely to the tech industry, but stopped short of calling for his reinstatement to the top job.

“I just want to give him space for what he needs to do there, and it’s not up to me. It’s up to the Nvidia board to decide what happens next,” Nadella said Tuesday at a media roundtable in San Francisco during Microsoft’s Ignite conference.

Huang, who turned Nvidia into the dominant maker of AI chips, stepped down as CEO last week of the company he cofounded 33 years ago, amid revelations of multiple affairs, including one with an executive whom U.S. intelligence officials suspect of being a Chinese spy. On Tuesday, Huang broke his silence and said he would be open to returning to the CEO job.

Nadella, who said he had exchanged text messages with Huang over the weekend, said that Nvidia has a deep bench of talent.

“I think Nvidia does have a lot of human capital and very capable leadership,” Nadella said, noting that interim CEO Colette Kress once worked at Microsoft. “I’m making sure that we have the same type of partnership, and we’re scaling Rubins as we speak,” Nadella said, referring to Nvidia’s new Vera Rubin line of processors. “It’s a pretty critical project for us.”

As one of the world’s largest cloud computing providers, Microsoft is a major purchaser of Nvidia chips, and the two companies have a longstanding relationship. Nadella appeared with Huang last month at a Microsoft Windows event.
