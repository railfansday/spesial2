Roll Tide Wire staff predictions for Alabama vs. Vanderbilt in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

As the regular season enters its' home stretch, Alabama certainly appears in the drivers seat to eventually make the College Football Playoff (CFP).

Ranked at No. 3 in the latest CFP rankings, Alabama has three games remaining on their regular season schedule, a stretch which will see the Tide favored in each. As a result, all Alabama has to do to make the 12-team CFP field is to simply win out, and that starts Saturday in Nashville against the Vanderbilt Commodores.

Vanderbilt is a team that given Alabama troubles each of the last two seasons, even handing the Crimson Tide a 40-35 upset loss the last time the teams met in Nashville. Alabama avenged that loss a year ago in Tuscaloosa, 30-14, a game that was originally tied at halftime before the Crimson Tide eventually pulled away.

This season though, the Commodores have struggled mightily, especially in recent weeks where they have dropped five of their last six games. Vanderbilt's record sits at 4-5 (1-5 SEC), and are heavy underdogs against Alabama on their home field.

So, can Alabama avoid another huge upset in Nashville? Follow along as the Roll Tide Wire staff make their picks for Saturday's showdown between Alabama and Vanderbilt.

Yes, this game is played in Nashville, and yes, Alabama did drop their last game played inside of FirstBank Stadium. However, this is not the same Vanderbilt team that has given Alabama problems the last two years, and as a result, the pick is a Crimson Tide win by multiple scores.

Vanderbilt does have a number of players back from the last two matchups, but the Commodores have also missed Diego Pavia at quarterback this season, a position which is now filled by a true freshman in former top recruit Jared Curtis. The Crimson Tide should be extremely motivated following that shocking 2024 loss, and will look at make a statement Saturday in Nashville.

Sometimes teams coming off a huge win like Alabama had at LSU struggle a bit coming out of the gate the week after. I could see Alabama doing that here, but I could also see the opposite where the Tide play a version of "joyous murderball," land some punches early, and never look back. I thought it was interesting listening to Kalen DeBoer this week talk about how some people haven't really watched Vanderbilt on tape. That tells me he's doing all the right things to keep his players locked in and focused on their opponent.

Vanderbilt is a shell of the teams we saw the past two years, even though it does have a promising quarterback in five-star recruit Jared Curtis. I don't think there's any question Alabama wins this game, but keep an eye on how Keelon Russell looks this week and potentially Austin Mack.

Vanderbilt has taken a sizable step back this season without Diego Pavia. I think five-star true freshman Jared Curtis has talent and potential, but he is not on the level yet to take down Alabama, nor is the rest of the Vanderbilt roster at the level it was two years ago when the 'Dores stunned the college football world by beating Alabama.

Alabama is slowly getting healthier, and I think they cruise to a blowout win over Vandy and move one step closer to locking up their spot in the CFP.
