Luca Williams-Barnett was the star of the show as Tottenham Hotspur beat Ipswich Town 2-0 on Saturday.

The 18-year-old got on the end of Pedro Porro's cross to open the scoring on his first ever start in the Premier League.

And he was not done there, driving into the box and past his man in the second half before finding Dominic Solanke, who could not miss.

Williams-Barnett has had to wait for his opportunities this season, but he has grabbed them with both hands when they have arrived.

Despite the huge money spent on the likes of Savinho in the summer, the teenager now looks to be a central option for Roberto De Zerbi.

Here, Football Insider has rated Williams-Barnett and every other Tottenham player's performance against Ipswich on Saturday.

There is no longer any way that Tottenham cannot be aware of the huge talent on their hands in the form of Williams-Barnett.

The winger turned 18 just last month and already looks more than ready for senior football.

Brimming with talent and confidence, the youngster tortured the Ipswich defence, running at Darnell Furlong and getting past him time and time again.

Williams-Barnett's goal contributions were the highlights, but he took more touches in the opposition box than anyone else as well with eight, and took four shots on target.

The winger was also fouled twice, won three duels, made a tackle, two blocks and three recoveries.

Tottenham have not had a lot to be excited about in recent years, but in their latest academy product, they may just have stumbled upon a gem.

With Sandro Tonali missing from the Tottenham squad through illness, Conor Gallagher had an opportunity to stake his claim for a starting role.

The midfielder was largely tasked with following danger man Julio Enciso, and did make it difficult for him.

But far too often was Gallagher beaten by the 22-year-old in midfield, allowing him to run onto the Tottenham defence.

With Tonali likely to return next week, De Zerbi has not been given much to think about.
