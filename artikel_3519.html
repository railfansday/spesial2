The new movie, Sense and Sensibility, takes an old-fashioned approach to the material. Also starring Esmé Creed-Miles, George MacKay, and Caitríona Balfe, now holds a "Certified Fresh" 78% score on the aggregator website Rotten Tomatoes, where the consensus reads, "Elevating the Dashwood sisters' relationship to the forefront of Jane Austen's classic tale of courtship, Georgia Oakley's Sense and Sensibility may not best previous adaptations but achieves a wistful identity of its own."

The new Sense and Sensibility debuted in 2,545 theaters before dropping to 1,888 theaters in its third week. Following the conclusion of a rather short theatrical window, the movie was removed from a further 1,357 theaters in its fourth week. It's now playing in 531 locations nationwide. It's also available on the PVOD market, having debuted in the top 10 of the domestic iTunes viewership chart. Sense and Sensibility was a modest hit, considering its short run.
