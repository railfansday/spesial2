Arsenal right-back Jurrien Timber enjoyed a sensational evening as the Netherlands crushed Greece 7-0 in the Nations League on Friday.

Incredibly, the 25-year-old netted a brace on the night - the first two goals of his international career.

After a 2026 that has been heavily hit by injuries, the defender has returned and began to look back to his best in North London.

Timber is widely considered among the best full-backs in world football when fully fit, and Arsenal will hope to keep him firing throughout the campaign.

The fitness of Ben White will be a significant boost for the Dutchman, with it having been a rarity for both players to be fit at the same time over the past few years.

Timber's quality and utility was on full show on Friday, with the natural right-back, in fact, enjoying his fantastic performance while positioned at left-back.

Timber had been almost ever-present for Arsenal last season until he picked up an injury in March.

White's return made the loss easier to swallow, before he was ruled out as well, with Cristhian Mosquera stepping into the role.

The Spaniard did well but was caught out in the Champions League final, giving away the penalty that would ultimately lead to Arsenal's heart-breaking defeat.

Timber was rushed back for the game and finished it, but it became clear after that he had done himself further damage and would miss the World Cup after requiring surgery.

The Netherlands missed him and that was made more clear by his display on Friday. The Arsenal man's first goal saw him drive into the box like a striker to finish, before reacting well to turn in from Denzel Dumfries' cross.

Dutch outlet SoccerNews rated him Timber with a 10 out of 10 for his display and wrote: "Jurrien Timber can play anywhere in defence.

"It seems clear that a position on the flanks, whether on the right or left, suits the Arsenal defender incredibly much better than a spot in the centre.

"Timber was fantastic. From two pre-assists to the fifth and sixth goals: Tijjani Reijnders wasn't the only one flawless on Friday night."

Voetbal Primeur gave him a nine, and added that he "crowned his masterclass with his first two goals for the Dutch national team."

Mee Met Oranje shared that rating for Timber, and described his performance as "exceptional".

Despite playing on the left of the defence, Timber was a menace throughout the game.

Alongside his two goals, he was also heavily involved in two other goals and could have had an assist when he created a big chance for Crysencio Summerville, who was denied.

Timber was supreme defensively, too, making a tackle, a block and an interception, helping to cut out the limited attacks Greece enjoyed.

The Dutchman took four touches inside the opposition area and won half of his duels before he was substituted in the 74th minute.
