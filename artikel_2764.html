Some of you might be recovering from Thanksgiving like your hoddler-in-chief. Fear not, Tottenham don't play again until Sunday. That gives us all one extra day of rest

It's pretty rare that I treat myself to a nice bottle of scotch, but I thought it'd be good to do that this year. I've been waiting for too long to pick up a bottle of Oban Distillers Edition. I first tried this at the distillery in Oban four years ago and really enjoyed it.

This kind of scotch is a little different in that it's finished in a sherry cask, giving it a sligthly sweet and complex finish. The Oban Little Bay is still an absolute workhouse, but I enjoy the additional notes of vanilla, fruit and caramel in this one.

I was always disappointed with the Oban 14 year. There was nothing especially spectacular about it, and you can get a better Oban Little Bay for far less. This one is a bit more premium, but it tastes more premium too, making it a lovely holiday-time liquor.

It took me a surprisingly long time to find it back here in the US. And I'm glad I finally did.
