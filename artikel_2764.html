Is Hunter Long playing today? The latest on the Cardinals TE

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Los Angeles Rams and Arizona Cardinals play Sunday in Week 10 at State Farm Stadium in Glendale, Arizona. Both teams have a number of players injured and some who are questionable to play.

Long has been out since Week 4 with a knee injury he suffered against the New York Giants. He was placed on injured reserve and then, this week, was designated to return so he could practice.

He was a limited participant in practice all week and listed as questionable on the team's final injury report of Week 10.

Long was activated from injured reserve on Saturday by the Cardinals. They did not elevate any tight ends from the practice squad, so he is expected to play.

He will resume the role he had as the No. 2-No. 3 tight end, whose role is primarily to block.

Long missed five games with the injury. He is good to go now and will face his former team.
