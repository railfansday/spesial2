Jose Mourinho adopts PSG&#8217;s rest strategy to keep Real Madrid stars fresh &#8211; report

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Jose Mourinho is introducing a new workload management strategy at Real Madrid, taking inspiration from Luis Enrique's approach at Paris Saint-Germain to ensure his players remain physically and mentally fresh during the decisive stages of the season.

According to MARCA, the Portuguese manager is not only looking at traditional squad rotation, but is also incorporating individual rest periods, additional days off and measures designed to help players recover away from football.

Simply put, Mourinho's desire is to prevent fatigue and reduce injury risks during a demanding campaign.

With Real Madrid competing across multiple competitions, managing the physical condition of key players has become an important part of his planning, which is quite crucial given Real Madrid's history with injuries.

The outlet mentions that Luis Enrique has regularly managed the workload of his regular starting players at PSG, giving them opportunities to rest during the first half of the campaign rather than waiting until fatigue becomes a problem.

Mourinho is now looking to introduce a similar philosophy at Real Madrid, although the approach will be adapted to the demands of Spanish football.

Notably, Arda Guler and Aurelien Tchouameni were among the first players to benefit from the strategy during the match against AEK.

Mourinho is planning to copy Luis Enrique's strategy. (Photo by Juan Manuel Serrano Arce/Getty Images)

In fact, the Portuguese manager intends to extend the approach to other members of his squad as the season progresses.

Simply put, the plan involves not just substituting players or changing the starting XI. Rather, selected footballers will receive additional time away from training and matches to recover mentally and physically.

Mourinho is working alongside different departments at Valdebebas to monitor the workload of his players and determine when they need additional recovery.

Rather than applying identical restrictions across the squad, the coaching staff is assessing individual requirements.

Minutes played, physical demands and the needs of upcoming fixtures will influence decisions about training intensity, selection and rest periods.

This approach is particularly relevant for players who regularly feature across competitions.

Guler and Tchouameni were rested against AEK Athens. (Photo by Angel Martinez/Getty Images)

Managing their involvement throughout the campaign could help Real Madrid avoid entering the crucial second half with key performers carrying excessive physical fatigue.

For what it is worth, Mourinho will have to be extremely careful in deciding which players will get additional rest.

This is because there is an important difference between the environments at PSG and Real Madrid.

PSG operate in Ligue 1, where the competitive demands and intensity of the domestic title race can differ from those experienced in La Liga.

Real Madrid face a particularly demanding domestic schedule, meaning Mourinho has less flexibility when deciding which players can be rested.

This means the plan has a significant chance of backfiring, and if this happens, Mourinho will be caught in the crossfire.
