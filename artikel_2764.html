Christian McCaffrey scores in otherwise quiet performance

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

San Francisco 49ers RB Christian McCaffrey ran the ball 12 times for 49 yards and a touchdown against the Minnesota Vikings in Week 11. He also caught two passes (on three targets) for 13 yards.

Why It Matters: San Francisco's plan on Sunday night was to attack the middle of the field with Brock Purdy and sprinkle in the ground game. It resulted in only 14 touches for McCaffrey, which was just one above his season low. It's been interesting to see Kyle Shanahan adjust CMC's role in his age-30 season as in years past the Niners would ask McCaffrey to shoulder a heavy load. It's worked to this point, and though he's not challenging Jahmyr Gibbs on a weekly basis, McCaffrey continues to generate RB1 value.
