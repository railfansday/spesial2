Taylor Swift, Kylie Kelce watch together as Travis Kelce scores for Chiefs

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Taylor Swift didn't have to wait long, her fans did.

Travis Kelce caught an 8-yard touchdown pass from Patrick Mahomes on the Chiefs' first drive against the Arizona Cardinals on Nov. 22. Swift was watching from a suite at Arrowhead Stadium with her sister-in-law Kylie Kelce.

The broadcast, however, didn't show the singer after the score. It was much later when she was sighted.

The touchdown was his first scoring catch since Sept. 27 and his fourth of the season.

The timing was a nice touch for Swift and Kelce to celebrate. It was Travis Kelce's 100th game at Arrowhead Stadium. He is just the fourth player in Chiefs history to play 100 games there.

Swift is coming off her first CMA Awards performance in 13 years on Nov. 18. Kelce couldn't make it to the show because of Chiefs practice.

Kylie Kelce, a successful podcast host in her own right, is married to Travis' brother, former Eagles center Jason Kelce. She hosts the "Not Gonna Lie," podcast.
