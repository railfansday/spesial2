Aaron Rodgers released by Steelers: Revisiting his high school football career

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Aaron Rodgers has never been one to shy away from speaking his mind.

The four-time NFL MVP made headlines Wednesday, Nov. 25, after appearing on The Pat McAfee Show, where he publicly criticized Pittsburgh Steelers coach Mike McCarthy following the team's decision to bench him ahead of Friday's game against the Denver Broncos.

Rodgers accused McCarthy of repeatedly missing team meetings, falling asleep during a quarterback meeting and falling behind on film preparation. He also detailed disagreements over the team's offensive approach and said his relationship with the Steelers had reached its breaking point.

Rodgers made it clear he does not expect to return to Pittsburgh. Shortly after appearing with McAfee, the Steelers announced they would be releasing the soon-to-be Hall of Fame quarterback.

But long before becoming one of the most accomplished quarterbacks in NFL history, Rodgers was a relatively overlooked high school prospect in Northern California.

Here's what to know about his high school football career:

Rodgers attended Pleasant Valley High School in Chico, California, where he played quarterback for the Vikings.

A two-year varsity starter from 2000-01, Rodgers established himself as one of the top quarterbacks in Northern California. He passed for 4,419 yards during his junior and senior seasons and earned all-section honors twice. As a senior, Rodgers set a school record with 2,303 passing yards.

Despite his production, Rodgers did not attract significant attention from major college football programs. He was considered undersized at the time and did not receive a Division I scholarship offer coming out of high school.

Pleasant Valley went 17-7 during Rodgers' two seasons as its starting quarterback, reaching the CIF Northern Section playoffs in consecutive years. He also played basketball and baseball, while pitching for Pleasant Valley as a senior.

Without a major college scholarship offer coming out of Pleasant Valley, Rodgers took the junior college route. He enrolled at Butte College in Oroville, California, where he became the starting quarterback for the Roadrunners.

Rodgers led Butte to a 10-1 record, a NorCal Conference championship and a No. 2 national ranking in 2002.

His performance caught the attention of California coach Jeff Tedford, who offered Rodgers an opportunity to play for the Golden Bears. Rodgers transferred to Cal and became the team's starting quarterback during the 2003 season. He played two seasons in Berkeley before entering the 2005 NFL Draft.

The Green Bay Packers selected Rodgers with the No. 24 overall pick in the first round of the 2005 NFL Draft. He spent 18 seasons in Green Bay, succeeding Brett Favre as the franchise's starting quarterback in 2008.

Rodgers went on to win four NFL MVP awards, earn 10 Pro Bowl selections and lead the Packers to a victory over the Pittsburgh Steelers in Super Bowl XLV. He later played for the New York Jets before joining Pittsburgh in 2026.

Rodgers returned to his high school roots in April 2026 when he was inducted into the Pleasant Valley High School Athletics Hall of Fame.

The four-time NFL MVP was part of the inaugural class honored at the school's Viking Legacy Dinner.
