Report: Bills may miss out on playing A.J. Brown again

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Buffalo Bills weren't able to beat the Patriots without A.J. Brown in Week 4, and they'll get another opportunity to do so in a few weeks inside Gillette Stadium.

According to ESPN's Adam Schefter via Mike Reiss, Brown will be out for an extended period with a hamstring inury:

The Bills and Patriots play in Week 13 on Sunday, December 6. That's 16 days from now. There's been no further indication of now that Brown will be placed on IR — he already had a stint there — but it appears somewhat unlikely he'll be on the field for New England in its second meeting with Joe Brady's team this season.

The team returns to play the Chargers at 4:25pm on Sunday of Week 12 before hosting the Bills the following Sunday in the same time slot.

While leg injuries have severely hampered Brown this season, he's been largely ineffective when on the field. He has 11 catches for 128 yards without a touchdown in four games on a total of 24.8% of New England's offensive snaps to date.

This is certainly something worth monitoring for the Bills over the next few weeks.
