Steelers drop Aaron Rodgers, Mason Rudolph gets nod vs. Broncos

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Steelers drop Aaron Rodgers, Mason Rudolph gets nod vs. Broncos originally appeared on NBC Sports Boston

The Pittsburgh Steelers are making a major change.

The team on Tuesday night announced Aaron Rodgers is being dropped for the Black Friday home game vs. the Denver Broncos.

Mason Rudolph will get the starting nod, with Rodgers set to be inactive.

It's a stunning move for a 6-4 team still in the AFC playoff mix, though recent poor results have left Mike McCarthy's side searching for answers.

Pittsburgh lacks needle-moving offensive weapons, making this decision quite a questionable one. A 42-year-old Rodgers is no longer one of the league's best quarterbacks, but dropping him likely doesn't solve anything.

Rodgers said prior to the start of the season that it would be his last.

Rudolph's last full season as a starter was in 2024 with Tennessee, where he played eight games and threw nine touchdowns and nine picks for 1,530 yards on a 64% completion percentage.

This season, Rodgers has thrown 16 touchdowns and six picks for 2,241 yards on a 60.9% completion percentage. DK Metcalf and rookie Germie Bernard are the top receiving options.

Behind Rudolph on the depth chart are Will Howard and Drew Allar, two inexperienced options for a team stuck in the league's middle tier.

Pittsburgh has a tough upcoming stretch where it hosts Denver and the Houston Texans before travelling to the Jacksonville Jaguars. The end of the schedule is lighter, but how the team fares during this three-game stretch will most likely make or break its playoff hopes.

And as of right now, Rodgers' 22-year career is on the line.
