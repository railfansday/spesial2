Tottenham Hotspur are staying calm over the future of midfielder Lucas Bergvall amid interest from Barcelona, sources have told Football Insider.

The La Liga giants have got their sights set on the Swedish star at the moment, with Serie A side Napoli also interested in his services.

However, Spurs have no plans to sanction a sale any time soon, with Bergvall understood to be far "happier" with his situation at the club than he was in the summer.

Sources say that Spurs have "big aspirations" for the 20-year-old long-term, and believe that he can become a "mainstay" in their starting XI.

Roberto De Zerbi persuaded the starlet to sign a new contract in the summer, and there is no temptation for a move away as a result.

Tottenham remain aware that Bergvall will naturally attract further interest as he develops, but they are not concerned about losing him for now.

Bergvall spent a large chunk of the summer pushing to leave Tottenham, as he was unhappy with the level of game time that he received last term.

The youngster felt that his development was being stunted in North London, only for De Zerbi to then convince him to stay put.

He ultimately signed a new long-term contract with Spurs, and the club were very happy that they were able to avoid a significant crisis.

Tottenham view Bergvall as a player with elite potential, and they are not surprised that he is already attracting attention from sides like Barcelona.

However, with the player now far more settled than he was in the summer and also contributing to the team, they are not prepared to sanction a sale any time soon.

Former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness told Football Insider last weekend that Tottenham are likely to hand a "heavily appearance-based" contract to James Maddison in the coming months.

The 29-year-old is out of contract in the summer of 2028, but the club still feel that he has a role to play, depsite consistent injury issues.

Wyness expects Spurs to issue a bonus-heavy new deal to Maddison, with a lower basic wage that will then improve with the more games he plays.

The attacking midfielder recently scored his first goal since May 2025, and has been edging towards a return to top form of late.
