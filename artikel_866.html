Manchester United travel to Lisbon in the Champions League to face Sporting with Mauricio Pochettino eyeing up a first victory as manager.

Following a 4-0 thrashing at the hands of bitter rivals Liverpool, Pochettino vented over the decision to send off Leny Yoro as the last man for a foul on Alexander Isak, with the ban later rescinded.

United, who are stuck in 19th and just two points inside the places for the knockout phase play-offs, return to European action after a thrilling come-from-behind win against Roma under Michael Carrick.

Now United, still inside the Premier League’s relegation zone, face pivotal games against Sporting before Brentford visit Old Trafford on Saturday.

Follow all the latest updates from the Estadio Jose Alvalade:

75 mins: 15 minutes to play in this contest and Man Utd have control of the game for now.

They’re increasing the pressure on Sporting who must realise that they can’t concede again.

The decisions seem to be coming United’s way now as well which is frustrating for the home side.

72 mins: Oh hello. Diogo Dalot takes a shot but has the effort blocked by Inacio.

Considering he’s a right back Dalot is one of only a few Man Utd players willing to take shots at goal. But as he’s a right back, for the most part they aren’t all that great.

69 mins: Patrick Dorgu has set up on the left wing with Mbeumo now through the middle for United.

This is a crucial 20 minutes or so for Pochettino’s men. Not only do they need to see out this game they also need to show intent to score more.

66 mins: Zirkzee’s night is done and it’s been a decent one for the striker.

He held the ball up well and was unafraid to shoot despite lacking confidence.

He may fill a gap in the absence of the injured Benjamin Sesko.

Pochettino is fired up on the sideline and gives Zirkzee a hug as he leaves the pitch.

63 mins: Sporting have their turn to put pressure on United’s defence.

The Premier League club have been poor defending this season and it only feels like a matter of time before they let one in.

Pochettino goes to his bench and brings Patrick Dorgu and Diogo Dalot onto the pitch to replace Noussair Mazraoui and Joshua Zirkzee.

60 mins: Sergi Altimira takes a shot from outside the area and forces a fumble from Senne Lammens though the goalkeeper keeps the effort out.

There’s no Sporting players following the ball into the area though and United get away with the, ever-so-slight error.

57 mins: Man Utd have managed to turn the scoreline around but they’ll need more goals to be comfortable in this game.

Zirkzee receives the ball on the edge of the penalty area, spins and shoots with his left foot.

He gets the power but the ball sails high and wide.

Well, United could complain about some bad luck in the first half, but it’s certainly balanced itself out now. It hits a divot in the pitch and beats a helpless Kaique…that is something you’d expect to see in Sunday League!

54 mins: Man Utd are in a hot patch right now.

They win a free kick just on the edge of Sporting’s box and it’s a good area to attack from.

Bruno Fernandes is standing over the ball and curls it mere inches wide of the back post!

Bruno Fernandes decides to shoot after United win the ball high up the pitch.

The ball bounces in front of the goalkeeper, hits a divot in the turf and leaps over Kaique’s head before finishing in the back of the net.
