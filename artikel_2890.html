Thursday Night Football: Colts vs. Texans discussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Week 11 of the 2026 NFL season will get started with an AFC South matchup between a couple Philly alumni, Shane Steichen and the Indianapolis Colts (3-7) and DeMeco Ryans and the Houston Texans (3-6).

Both teams are pretty much out of the postseason discussion, especially with the Jaguars holding a 7-2 record at the top of the division. Still, it feels like Steichen and Ryans could both be on the hot seat after underperforming this season, so stringing together some wins could impact their futures.

The Colts lead the all-time series between these teams, 33-15-1, but the Texans have won five of their six most-recent meetings. They last faced off at the beginning of the 2026 season, with the Colts narrowly winning, 19-17, in Indianapolis.
