Boston Celtics at Washington Wizards: Stream, lineups, injury reports, broadcast (11/24)

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The NBA regular season can be like a roller coaster. The Boston Celtics currently find themselves on the part of the ride that points straight down — except there's no fun in this steep drop. The C's have lost four games in a row, which is enough for their longest losing streak since the 2020-21 campaign. They'll look for a reprieve in the regular-season roller coaster on Tuesday night, when they face the Washington Wizards in D.C.

It's no secret that the Celtics have had the Wizards' number in recent years. They've beat their Eastern Conference foe 11 consecutive times, with the last win for the Wizards dating back to March of 2023. But, these aren't the same Wiz the C's have crushed in the past. Washington has prevailed in three straight contests after a slow start to the season and seems to have found a stud in forward AJ Dybantsa, the first overall pick in the 2026 NBA Draft (and a former Celtics fan who grew up less than an hour outside of Boston). Meanwhile, the Celtics are reeling and need to play desperate in the nation's capital to avoid going 0-4 on their rough road trip.

The Celtics have lost four games in a row and, for the moment, sit in third place in the Eastern Conference with an 11-6 record.

The Wizards have won three games in a row and own a 7-11 record. They're just barely in Play-In Tournament territory, as they hold ninth place in the East.

For the third consecutive night, Celtics center Mitchell Robinson is out with an illness.

As for the Wizards, Reece Beekman (right AC joint sprain), Justin Champagnie (right foot surgery), Tristan Vukcevic (left hip impingement), and Jamir Watkins (left ACL repair) are out on Tuesday evening. Two-way center Felix Okpara is listed as questionable.
