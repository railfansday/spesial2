Chargers-Patriots in Week 12 flexed out of Sunday Night Football

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Just like we all could have predicted, the Chargers' Sunday Night Football matchup against the Patriots has been flexed out of primetime by the NFL and replaced by Raiders-Browns. The Chargers and Patriots will now play at 1:05 p.m.

The news was expected after Patriots starting quarterback Drake Maye went down with an ankle injury against the Lions in Germany that looks to be season-ending. Behind Maye, the Patriots have Tommy DeVito as their backup. Turns out, the NFL does not think it would be a good idea to keep the 1-8 Chargers and the DeVito-led Pats in one of the week's three primetime slots.

After this flex, the Chargers only have one other primetime game remaining on the schedule and that comes on December 16th for Thursday Night Football. There's only been one other Thursday Night Football game that's ever been flexed, so it's likely the Chargers get to keep this one against a very good opponent.
