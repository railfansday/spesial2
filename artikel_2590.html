Ole Miss basketball rallies to beat BYU, make Maui Invitational final

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Ole Miss basketball continues to shine in its first trip to the Southwest Maui Invitational in Hawaii.

The Rebels (6-0) won 73-63 over No. 21 BYU (6-1) on Nov. 24 to advance to the Maui Invitational championship game.

Coach Chris Beard's team will play the winner of Providence (5-1) vs No. 8 Arizona (5-1) in the championship game on Nov. 25 (1:30 p.m. CT, ESPN).

The Rebels beat Clemson 82-62 in the first round Nov. 23.

Ole Miss used a big comeback to get the job done. The Rebels were down 14 at the half but outscored the Cougars 47-23 in the second half. Ole Miss ratcheted up defensive pressure in the second half, and it stifled BYU's offense. The Rebels forced 23 BYU turnovers and committed only eight.

Ole Miss racked up 27 points off turnovers. BYU managed only eight.

Point guard Budd Clark led the way for the Rebels. He scored a team-high 20 points on 8-for-15 shooting. He also had a team-high five assists. Forward Corey Stephenson added 18 points, including an authoritative transition dunk in the final minutes.
