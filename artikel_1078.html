Leaders Celtic ease to victory against St Mirren

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Celtic vs St Mirren. Scottish Premiership.

Report as Celtic beat St Mirren 4-1; Alex Oxlade-Chamberlain opened the scoring; Camilo Duran and Luke McCowan made it three before the break; Callum McGregor fired in a fourth for Celtic; Killian Phillips pulled a goal back from the penalty spot

Football Journalist @AlisonGConroy

Celtic looked refreshed after the international break as they eased to a 4-1 win against St Mirren.

Injuries meant Alex Oxlade-Camberlain and Luke McCowan were drafted into the starting XI and both scored in a dominant performance at Celtic Park.

Camilo Duran scored in between the above pair after he had two early finishes ruled out for offside.

McGregor added a fourth with a low strike into the bottom corner from the edge of the box.

Celtic's perfect afternoon came undone when Sam Johnstone caught Calvin Ramsay inside the box, and Killian Phillips scored from the penalty spot.

Martin O'Neill also introduced former PSV Eindhoven midfielder Joel van den Berg for a debut in the second half and the 19-year-old showed some good touches.

Celtic are seven points clear of Rangers before their trip to Motherwell later on Saturday, live on Sky Sports. St Mirren are ninth, one point above the Steelmen.

Celtic boss Martin O'Neill speaking to Sky Sports News:

"I thought we played really well, particularly in the first half. We carved out quite a number of chances in the game.

"There are games here that we have played where some things haven't come off and the opposition get a taste of that there, but that wasn't today.

"I thought that we were very, very strong. St Mirren still had, in parts of the game, decent possession, but overall we were clinical as well too.

"Really pleased, really, really pleased with the side because with the international boys not getting a break at all, but they're coming back very, very late.

"Delighted with the performance and it really couldn't be better."

St Mirren head coach Craig McLeish speaking to Sky Sports News:

"The scoreline is really disappointing from our point of view. We have to be disappointed and frustrated at losing games, but we have to take some positives from it. There was a lot of good within our game.

"I actually thought we started the game well. We looked like we were well within the game and then it can happen here at times. Celtic were clinical and in a nine-minute spell they scored three goals.

"That's when the damage is done. The damage is done in that nine-minute spell.

"Throughout the rest of the game we did a lot of positive things. I felt we got into some really good areas. Similar areas to Celtic that they get into, but the difference is that quality in and around the penalty box.

"They were ruthless and they were clinical and we weren't. One of the biggest bits we have to take from it is the attitude in the second half."
