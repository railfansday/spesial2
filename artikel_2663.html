Seedy K’s Peerless Pigskin Prognostications: Week XII

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

We are single minded and laser focused here at Pigskin Prognostication Plaza.

Certainly not tomorrow's release of Grand Theft Auto VI, despite its use of Roman Numerals.

Nor even those leftover slices of Impellizzeri's* waiting to be heated for dinner.

*Original crust. Sausage, Green Peppers, Mushrooms, Onions.

Thus, it is with full understanding and compassion, we name Oklahoma AD Roger Denny our Pigskin Personality of the Week.

When the nation was suffering an air traffic crisis of significant inconvenience for hundreds of thousands of travelers around the country, Denny, single minded, stood up for truth, justice and Boomer Sooner.

The Ole Miss football team wasn't able to get to Norman reasonably in advance of Saturday afternoon's scheduled kickoff, so the SEC rescheduled the game for Sunday afternoon.

To paraphrase Denny's response to the league's "inexplicable" decision:

"This is absurd. Unfathomable. Does Greg Sankey not comprehend how this will inconvenience the entire state, the University of Oklahoma, and how it will unfairly burden our student athletes and our fans? Not to mention the spirit force of the once and true Supreme Commander Sooner Nation, David Boren."

To Roger, a true and unwavering acolyte of Bronconagurskius, we simply say, "We feel your pain."

Speaking of pain, your prognosticator non pareil was feeling none when reviewing Week XI's results.

Sure, I picked Kentucky to beat Florida at home. But the Wildcats didn't, suffering their second loss in a row. I'll take that prediction miscue with a smile.

DePauw is ringing the Monon Bell. Indiana fashioned its most impressive W of the year. Louisville escaped Tobacco Road.

And it might have taken Santa Anna thirteen days to make the stalwarts in the Alamo succumb, but Wake Forest's Demon Deacons only needed thirty minutes to corral and slaughter SMU's Mustangs.

So, yeah, four right, one wrong. 46-29 on the season.

CoachSpeak of the Week: "I don't know, I sort of always like playing them that second game, because you could always count on two or three players suspended." — Steve Spurrier on playing Georgia early.

UCLA @ Michigan. Though it shall have zero bearing on this upcoming encounter in the Big House, a bit of research advises these two heralded college football franchises have to my surprise only met once in the Rose Bowl Game. The Bruins, coached by Terry Donahue — not to be confused with Hollywoodland star Troy Donahue ( née Merle Johnson Jr.) — bested Bo's Wolverines, as they had earlier in the campaign in Ann Arbor. Which nonsense brings us to this week with the schools on dissimilar paths. Bob Chesney, having drunk the magic elixir in Harrisonburg, Va., home of James Madison U., has the domiciliaries of Westwood undefeated so far in his inaugural season there. Meanwhile previously successful Kyle Whittingham is not so. 5-5, Ls in 3 of last 4. QB tsouris. Go Blue is just blue. I love me some ocean blue and sunset gold. UCLA.

Stanford @ California. The Big Game. (A designation the NFL actually tried to trademark. Unsuccessfully. These two top academic institutions got some lawyers.) First contested in 1892. The '82 battle with its cockamamie finish is one of the most memorable in college football lore. Look it up if unaware, not going into details here. That this is an ACC game boggles a rational mind (Fortunately mine is otherwise.) New coaches at both schools. Tavita Pritchard with the Cardinal, 5-5. W last time out at Virginia Tech. Tosh Lupoi in Berserkeley. Uhhh, Ls in 6 of last 7. But, ya know, strangeness appears in these rivalry affairs. On a hunch, to the chagrin of one of my most loyal of readers, Golden Bears.

Louisiana State @ Tennessee. We can't turn our eyes away, can we? The state of Louisiana is ever fascinating, the sports programs of its flagship university especially. They just seem to lean into the unlikeable down there. It is a state where a fellow won the gubernatorial race over a KKK leader, using the slogan, "Vote for the Crook." Which is how some Bayou Bengal fans must be talking of their new and very expensive football coach. That guy, last name Kiffin, has to be grateful that Clemson, Louisiana Tech and McNeese State were on the schedule. Had LSU been playing schools of even legitimate competence, he wouldn't be able to see bowl eligibility with binoculars, even were it just at the other end of the Huey P. The Vols are kinda sorta under the radar. A loss at South Carolina more than offset by that W over the Crimson Tide. They seem to be accumulating some momentum. Rocky Top shall be played. Tennessee.

Indiana @ Washington. 47 seconds into his weekly press conference Monday, IU's Curt Cignetti reached down to his Adam's Apple area and pulled off a mask, revealing his inner Rodney Dangerfield. "No respect, I tell ya, we get no respect." Which is kinda sorta true, given their CFP status. But kinda sorta understandable, given that this year's Hoosiers aren't last year's Hoosiers. But the dude at 25-1 is a misstep away from being undefeated in his last 26. I needn't google him to know. He wins. And, with a replacement 7th year QB who started his career at Fordham, shall do so again against the Huskies. IU.

Kentucky @ Missouri. Am I finally willing to stop munching Sissy Cake, to jump the Will Stein Hype Train? Once legitimately on the playdown chart, the Wildcats have dropped two in a row. To the Vols, understandable. To the Gators, not so much. Meanwhile Mizzou's promising opening isn't quite so glossy in retrospect. Add to it losses in three of their last four. All to ranked teams. Both squads are skidding. Home team prevails. Tigers.

Pittsburgh @ Louisville. Both of espn.com's bowl projectionists have the Cards slated for the Pop Tarts. Which would be a tasty conclusion to the season. (See what I did there?) The Panthers shall not be an impediment. The toaster is plugged in. Louisville..
