12 takeaways from NFL Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

This Philadelphia Eagles season hasn't been a ton of fun. With the Birds on their bye, it was nice to get a break from them and watch other teams play instead. Here are some Eagles-related takeaways from the NFL Week 10 games.

If you're holding out hope for the Eagles to come back and win their division, Week 10 was a good one for this rooting interest.

The first-place New York Giants and second-place Dallas Cowboys both lost.

Accordingly, Philly's playoff chances (via FTN Fantasy) increased from 0.4% … to 0.7%!

The Eagles are still massive long shots to win the NFC East:

But the division would've effectively been sealed with a Giants win on Thursday night. They would've clinched a head-to-head tiebreaker over Washington in addition to gaining ground on the Cowboys.

Instead, the Giants choked away a 93.2% win probability with 2:15 remaining in the fourth quarter. Props to Washington for capitalizing and keeping their season alive, if only for a little longer.

I'm curious, which team do you think is going to win the division?

The Commanders are suddenly not a bad bet; they have the easiest remaining schedule in the NFC East and seventh-easiest overall in the NFL.

The Giants, meanwhile, have the toughest remaining schedule in the NFC East and fourth-toughest overall.

The Cowboys' defense is absolutely dreadful but their offense might do enough to help them win some games.

The Eagles can be ignored in this context until they provide reason to be relevant again.

I'm still going with Dallas. But I don't feel great about it.

As for the team that Eagles fans should prefer to win the division, assuming it's not going to be Philly?

I previously felt like the Giants were the answer to this question because I can see them beating their chests about barely winning a bad division and overestimating how close they are to being serious contenders. But I actually think Washington might be the preferable choice. Maybe the Commanders keep Dan Quinn and put too much stock into coming back to win the NFC East, only to then struggle again in 2027.

I do not feel bad for the Cowboys, obviously, but I do feel bad for the former Eagles passing game coordinator/defensive backs coach.

He deserved a defensive coordinator job after his success in Philly. It's too bad for him that he looks completely in over his head in his new role. I'm not sure how he hasn't already been fired (as of me typing this sentence) after the San Francisco 49ers dropped 46 points and 584 (!!!) yards on a Dallas defense that ranks dead last in basically any relevant rankings.

In defense of Parker, this level of defensive ineptitude isn't simply on one person. And this unit also stunk terribly last year with Matt Eberflus at defensive coordinator.

Still, keeping him at this point seems hard to justify. If he's dismissed, the Eagles shouldn't rule out a return to Philly (as a position coach).

Brown suffered a high ankle sprain injury in Week 1 that landed him on injured reserve. It seems like he returned too early since he was pretty ineffective upon playing again and had to miss more time before giving it another go in Week 10. Now he's dealing with a new hamstring injury that could keep him out of action.

Many seem to enjoy Brown's struggles. I can understand why that's the case after his less than charming finish to his Eagles career. Also because screw the Pats.

I don't begrudge anyone for feeling that way, though I do think it's pretty corny how some of the same people now delighting in Brown's failures previously insisted any idea that the Eagles were going to trade him was purely a media fabrication.

I'm not among those delighted by Brown's downfall. I enjoyed watching him be a dominant receiver and Super Bowl champion with the Eagles. I'm fairly nostalgic, so, I like to think of the good times more than his bad ending in Philly.

Regardless of how you feel about him, there's no denying that the Eagles are looking like big-time winners in this trade. Despite Howie Roseman seemingly not having much leverage in a Brown trade, he extracted the Patriots' 2027 fifth-round pick and 2028 first-round pick.

It's only too bad Roseman didn't manage to get New England's first-rounder in the 2027 NFL Draft with the Pats now likely to fade down the stretch. Drake Maye suffered a potential season-ending injury in Week 10 and Tommy DeVito stunk up the joint in relief duty. At least that 2027 fifth-rounder could be improving.

I don't know that the Pats will definitely rebound in 2028. But I do think it's typically tough to be the team coming off a Super Bowl-losing season (as we saw with the 2023 Eagles). So, they could be better. I also think Brown could be much more effective if he's able to get healthy and stay that way, though the latter part of that equation is a question mark.

The Eagles should be rooting for the Houston Texans in an effort to knock the Jacksonville Jaguars (who owe their 2027 first-round pick to Philly after the Jordan Davis trade) out of the top spot in the AFC South.

But that much is probably not going to be happening now.

It's no surprise that the Jags managed to win as 10.5-point road favorites against the Tennessee Titans.

But the Texans lost as 4.5-point road favorites against the Cleveland Browns. And this despite having a 17 to 0 lead in the second quarter.

Had the Texans won, maybe they could've won their division. Instead, the Jags are overwhelming AFC South favorites via FanDuel:

The Jags are also now -30000 to make the playoffs.

If Jacksonville makes the postseason, their first-round pick owed to the Eagles will officially be outside of the top 18 selections. And it could be as low as No. 32 if they win the Super Bowl.

But here's to hoping they somehow win out and the Jags lose out, or something close to that.

If we're to assume the Jags are not losing the AFC South, you've gotta root for the likes of the Buffalo Bills, Kansas City Chiefs, and Cincinnati Bengals to win enough to drop the Jags to the No. 4 seed in the AFC playoff picture. At that point, they'll be hosting the top wild card team in their conference in the first round.

Returning from their bye, the Eagles opened as 4.5-point home underdogs to the Pittsburgh Steelers in Week 11.

Might the Eagles actually beat this team?!

But the Steelers hardly seem unbeatable at the moment. They just allowed 38 points and 484 yards in a loss to the Bengals. Old ass Aaron Rodgers is predictably struggling.

It's not like they're a terrible team. Prior to the Bengals loss, they won four in a row. They could very well rebound in Philly.

Maybe the Eagles make it interesting, though? That's probably the most you can ask for at this point.

But they're certainly in a good position to represent the NFC in the Super Bowl.

It would be funny if they made it only to lose to the Kansas City Chiefs once again.

Still, I'd rather see another NFC team make it over them. The Eagles-49ers beef isn't as hot as it used to be but I don't find them to be likable. At least in contrast to the alternatives, such as the Detroit Lions.

Fred Johnson should be remembered fondly in Philly as a Super Bowl LIX champion and credible swing tackle. Not to mention his fun personality that was often on display in the Eagles' official social media content channels.

Hollywood Brown … didn't lack effort. He just wasn't very good in limited opportunities. His departure also came with a non-injury settlement that offered the Eagles some financial relief.

It's good to see the team is realizing it's a lost season. No need to keep players who aren't particularly in the short-term and certainly won't be around beyond this season. Give some younger/newer options some chances.

To a bigger point, it's a relief that we can be honest about what the Eagles are: not a good team. It stinks as it relates to this season. But, hey, at least we're not Cowboys fans, who get their hopes up just enough only to be totally let down.

Since then, the Eagles have dropped to 2-7. The Rams have advanced to 6-4. LA has undoubtedly benefited from a light schedule recently. But their underlying numbers suggest they're even better than their record indicates, which was always the case:

LOS ANGELES CHARGERS at BALTIMORE RAVENS: Two reasons to root for the Bolts: 1) the Eagles want to move ahead of LAC in the draft order and 2) thanks to the Cam Jurgens trade, the Eagles own Baltimore's fifth-round pick in the 2027 NFL Draft. Root for the Chargers.
