Rams announce 2 practice squad promotions for Sunday vs. Cardinals

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

There may not be a team in the NFL that has shuffled its roster more than the Los Angeles Rams this season. They've had a revolving door at several positions due to injuries, so Sean McVay and Les Snead have had to get creative with their practice squad elevations to round out the game-day roster.

On Saturday, the Rams announced their two practice squad promotions for Week 10 against the Arizona Cardinals. For the third week in a row, they've called up long snapper Jake McQuaide, which is an indication that Joe Cardona will not be able to play; he's currently listed as questionable.

Additionally, they elevated cornerback Damarion Williams. Sean McVay said on Monday that he's "optimistic" Trent McDuffie will be able to play, but he's questionable and uncertain to suit up. Williams' promotion could be insurance in case McDuffie can't play.

The Rams and Cardinals square off at 4:05 p.m. ET on Sunday in what is virtually a must-win game for Los Angeles in order to keep its playoff hopes alive.
