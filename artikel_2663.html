Brandon Aiyuk finishes visit with Commanders, but decision is not expected on Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Brandon Aiyuk may still join the Commanders soon, but it sounds like that’s not imminent.

Drew Rosenhaus, Aiyuk’s agent, said the receiver’s visit with Washington is over, via ESPN’s Adam Schefter.

Rosenhaus added that “Discussions are ongoing and positive,” but there probably will not be a decision today.

Aiyuk, who has not played since 2024, was released by the 49ers earlier this week and cleared waivers, making him free to sign with any team.

The Commanders have long been thought of as the frontrunner to land Aiyuk

We’ll see if Aiyuk takes more visits before joining a club.
