Knicks have no late-game magic as they’re eliminated from NBA Cup title defense

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Captain Clutch had a chance to tie the game with three free throws after Ryan Conwell's boneheaded foul on Brunson's corner 3-pointer with 9.8 seconds left. But Brunson's first free throw clanked off the rim — a rare shortcoming for him in a big moment.

Brunson made the next two, and the Knicks trailed by one. But they weren't able to foul on the subsequent Heat possession, as Pelle Larsson's dunk put Miami back up three with 7.2 seconds left. Brunson — while trying to draw another foul — missed a 3-pointer on the other end, and the Knicks' six-game winning streak came to an end as they fell 108-105 to the Heat on Friday night at Madison Square Garden.

Miami Heat forward Giannis Antetokounmpo pulls down a rebound away from New York Knicks guard Josh Hart on Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, at Madison Square Garden. JASON SZENES FOR NEW YORK POST

"Very," Brunson said when asked how frustrating it was to miss the free throw.

Friday's loss meant the Knicks were officially eliminated from the NBA Cup. They needed to win by at least seven points and get a Pacers win over the 76ers to advance. Neither happened.

Brunson's missed free throws came on the possession after James Wiseman similarly came up short from the line.

Trailing by two with 36.8 seconds left in the game, the Knicks needed a stop and seemed to have bottled up Giannis Antetokounmpo, who tried to throw the ball off the backboard to himself. But Karl-Anthony Towns fouled him on the play.

Antetokounmpo missed both free throws, however, and Bam Adebayo fouled Wiseman going for the rebound after the second miss. Wiseman could have tied the game with 12.5 seconds left by making both. He made the first, but his second free throw rimmed out.

So, the Knicks overcoming a 23-point deficit was all for naught.

New York Knicks guard Jalen Brunson reacts to referee Scott Foster after a call at Madison Square Garden in New York, Friday, Nov. 27, 2026. JASON SZENES FOR NEW YORK POST

"We did not have a great shootaround today with our focus," coach Mike Brown said. "We have to be better in our shootarounds, coming into games — especially games like this. It was evident with how sloppy we played."

There will be no defense of the Knicks' NBA Cup title. They didn't even make it to the knockout rounds after going 1-3 in the group stage.

But being eliminated could end up a blessing in disguise. Brown — even before his team was knocked out — said that this time around, he values the extra rest the Knicks will get by not advancing in the tournament. And with how banged up they are, he's probably right — the Knicks (12-7) were without OG Anunoby (left calf strain), Miles McBride (sports hernia), Jordan Clarkson (right plantar fasciitis) and Andre Drummond (concussion protocol) on Friday. Towns played but has been battling an abdominal ailment.

Miami Heat forward Giannis Antetokounmpo pulls down a rebound away from New York Knicks guard Jalen Brunson on Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, in New York. JASON SZENES FOR NEW YORK POST

Now the Knicks will get easier opponents (other teams who were eliminated) and play just two games — one home and one on the road — over a nine-day stretch in the beginning of December. Advancing would have meant better opposition (other teams who advanced) and the potential for up to three more road games if they reached the Final.

Friday was their first matchup against the Heat since Miami acquired Antetokounmpo over the offseason. And the Greek Freak delivered his first 20-20 game for the Heat with 22 points and 23 rebounds. Adebayo, his partner in crime in a dominant frontcourt, had 25 points and seven rebounds. Towns recorded his 19th straight double-double to start the season with 19 points and 10 rebounds, but he couldn't take on the Giannis-Bam duo by himself.

"One guy has the second-most points in a game ever and the other guy, he's an MVP, a former MVP," Towns said. "It goes back to execution."

Brunson had a game-high 27 points — but the Knicks needed 28 out of him.

The Heat like to utilize a zone defense with their dominant frontcourt and an elite perimeter defender in Davion Mitchell. And it gave the Knicks offense fits in the first half — they shot just 39.5 percent from the field across the first two quarters.

They were able to largely figure it out in the second half, but were let down by their end-of-game execution.

Last year, the Knicks' NBA Cup triumph was a precursor to their actual championship. They better hope this year's failed Cup run isn't a preview of anything else.

"Obviously disappointed we can't defend the Cup championship," Towns said. "But we still have a lot more games to get ourselves ready to defend the world championship."
