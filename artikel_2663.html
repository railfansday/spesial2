Aaron Rodgers benched vs. Broncos; Steelers will start Mason Rudolph at QB instead

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Pittsburgh Steelers announced Tuesday night that Mason Rudolph will start at quarterback on Friday against the Denver Broncos. Rodgers will be inactive.

NFL insider Tom Pelissero reported after the announcement that the decision was head coach Mike McCarthy's. There are no reports that Rodgers is dealing with an injury.

The Steelers didn't offer any explanation beyond a statement from team spokesman Burt Lauten announcing the roster move:

"The Pittsburgh Steelers have named Mason Rudolph the starting quarterback for the game this Friday against the Denver Broncos.

"Aaron Rodgers will be inactive for the game on Friday."

Rodgers, a future Hall of Famer, may have played his last NFL game. He announced before the season started that this would be his last in the league. It's a stunning development for the 6-4 Steelers and a quarterback who is one of the best to ever play the game.

Aaron Rodgers is no longer the starting quarterback for the Pittsburgh Steelers.

Rodgers, 42, is in his 22nd NFL season and well beyond his prime. He's in his second season with the Steelers, who hired McCarthy in the offseason in part because of his previous relationship with Rodgers.

McCarthy coached Rodgers for 13 seasons during Rodgers' prime and the pair won a Super Bowl together in Green Bay. Rodgers won two his four NFL MVP's while playing for McCarthy.

But with a playoff berth in the balance, McCarthy and the Steelers have decided 10 games into their season that Rudolph gives them a better chance at making the postseason.

Tuesday's news drops one day after McCarthy gave Rodgers what appeared to be clear vote of confidence.

"Aaron Rodgers is our quarterback," McCarthy told reporters in his Monday news conference.

That statement came on the heels of Sunday's 19-16 loss to the Philadelphia Eagles. The loss was the second straight for Pittsburgh and snapped a seven-game losing streak for an Eagles team that's been in a tailspin for most of the season.

Even with the consecutive losses, the Steelers remain on the right side of the playoff picture. If the postseason started this week, Pittsburgh would play as the final wild-card team and the No. 7 seed in the AFC.

Rodgers had displayed continued signs of decline this year with statistically his worst career season. But there are worse starting quarterbacks in the NFL. And it's questionable whether Rudolph, an established NFL backup, gives the Steelers a better chance at winning.

In 10 starts, Rodgers has completed 60.9% of his passes for 6.2 yards per attempt and 224.1 yards per game with 16 touchdowns and 6 interceptions. The Steelers rank 23rd in total offense, 22nd in passing offense and 22nd in scoring offense with 21.4 points per game.

Rudolph is a seven-year NFL veteran in his sixth season with a Steelers team that selected him in the third round of the 2018 NFL Draft.

In 38 career appearances, Rudolph has completed 64.2% of his passes for 6.6 yards per attempt with 30 touchdown and 22 interceptions. He's made 19 career starts, but has never established himself as an NFL starter.

With their postseason hopes on the line against a stout Broncos defense, the Steelers are ready to give Rudolph another chance in favor of their aging future Hall of Famer.
