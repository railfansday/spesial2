Kyle Whittingham previews first Michigan vs. Ohio State rivalry game

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

ANN ARBOR, Mich. -- In less than a week's time, we'll find out for sure whether embattled Michigan football head coach Kyle Whittingham gets the Ohio State rivalry or not.

Whittingham had been under fire in the offseason for potentially downplaying the rivalry, which he claimed wasn't the case. He noted that hating the Buckeyes more wouldn't necessarily result in a win. But former OSU head coach Urban Meyer's maniacal obsession with the Wolverines led him to an undefeated track record. Current head coach in Columbus, Ryan Day, hasn't exactly had the same results.

Called the greatest rivalry in all of sports by many, Whittingham is about to be out of the frying pan and into the fire. And whether he burns is up to him.

After the improbable win over UCLA in triple overtime on Saturday, Whittingham shared his thoughts on The Game, with less than a week to go before kickoff.

"I can envision it," Whittingham said. "I've never been in the Shoe, never played in that stadium. We did play Ohio State in the Rose Bowl five years ago, four years ago. So we've had a taste of that. We know they're a very good football team, very talented. I guess they got beat today at Nebraska. But I think the environment I'm anticipating could be very hostile, but I've been in hostile environments before. So I don't know if it's going to blow all those away or not. We'll find out."

If Whittingham doesn't get it, his interim offensive coordinator, Jim Harding, does. On Wednesday, in the last regular season coordinator media availability of the year, Harding, a Toledo native, shared his excitement for getting to be involved in The Game for the first time.

"I'd be lying if I didn't think when this whole thing transpired last week that I will be calling an offensive team in the Ohio State-Michigan game," Harding said. "That, to me, is crazy, but we gotta worry about this week. But in the back of my mind, it's the greatest rivalry in all the sports.

"I think I literally get goosebumps even talking about it. But again, not to avoid, we gotta focus on sending these kids out the right way this week, and then when next week comes, then there's more time to sit and reflect on it, but it's a great opportunity. You're right, I grew up, that's what you did. You watched Michigan football on Saturday afternoons growing up, and that being one of the games is pretty cool, and that's all I'll say."

Ohio State has four losses entering The Game for the first time in 15 years, with the last time being when Luke Fickell was the interim head coach following the dismissal of Jim Tressel. The Buckeyes stumbled into that iteration of The Game at 6-5, before losing two straight. At the moment, OSU is 7-4, and is in real danger of potentially having the same record as the Wolverines should Michigan find some offensive footing this next week.
