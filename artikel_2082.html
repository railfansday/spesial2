Bradford City told to sign Charlie Webster who is "a class above the rest" at League One rivals

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Bradford City are still settling into life under Michael Duff, and it'll be intriguing to see how the new manager is backed in the upcoming January transfer window.

The Bantams have still been as defensively brilliant as they were under Graham Alexander, keeping clean sheets in seven of the eight games in all competitions under Duff, but haven't packed much of a punch going forward in league action.

Indeed, they've scored just two goals in five games, both against Stevenage, and were beaten 3-0 by West Yorkshire rivals Huddersfield Town in their last league game back on Halloween.

Dominant wins by an 11-0 aggregate scoreline against Marine and York City in the FA Cup and Jersey Mike's Trophy hopefully indicate a removal of the safety at Valley Parade under the ex-Wycombe Wanderers boss.

Nevertheless, the Bantams are currently outside of the top six and will be looking for ways to get back in, both by improving league results before the end of the calendar year and by improving the squad come January.

It's coming to that time when fanbases fantasise over their dream January transfer window, and FLW's Bradford fan pundit, Boris Thompson, wants to see Burton Albion's Charlie Webster make the switch to Valley Parade.

The 22-year-old has been one of the more underrated players in League One for quite some time now, since he signed for the Brewers in the summer of 2024 from Chelsea, where he spent a decade in the academy.

Injury has always seemed to hold Webster back, but he's started every league outing thus far for Gary Bowyer's side, impressing as he usually does. With a rocky injury history perhaps no longer there, he could be ready to make that step up into becoming a Championship player, which could happen at Bradford.

"One player I'd love us to make a move for, and who is someone who has proved himself to be a class above the rest in recent years when we've played them, is Charlie Webster at Burton Albion," Thompson said.

"When I first saw him play, I felt he had a bit. Then I looked him up and realised that he's a Cobham Academy graduate; coming through at Chelsea isn't something your average player can do, even if it doesn’t reflect the standard of Chelsea’s first team at present.

"He looks a class above every time we play them, and a really dynamic box-to-box midfielder is something we've been missing. We had it a bit last year with Jenson Metcalfe, but obviously, we know what’s happened to him since, so he’s one I’d love us to sign."

It's not a surprise to see Thompson impressed by Webster and wanting to see him join the club in January.

The 22-year-old has only played the Bantams twice in his career, but hasn't ever come out on the losing side, and scored in a 2-1 win at Valley Parade last November, handing Bradford their first loss at home back in League One, and just their second in the entirety of 2025.

Webster's performances aren't just saved for Bradford, though. The former Chelsea academy man has 13 goals in just 70 League One appearances at the Pirelli, while also being one of the best defensive number 10s in the division.

He sits in the top 10% of attacking midfielders for recoveries, interceptions and possession won in the final third, per FotMob. Evidently, he'll be key to Burton remaining above the drop zone once again in League One this season.

However, football is a business, and Webster's contract doesn't lend itself well to remaining at Burton past January. The 22-year-old's deal expires at the end of the season, meaning that unless he extends, this upcoming winter window serves as the last chance for the Brewers to make some money via a sale.

While reluctant, Gary Bowyer's side may be open to a sale, and if Bradford come in with a good offer, they could almost be forced to accept.

Last January, Bradford were in a strong position to possibly go back-to-back into the Championship. At the turn of the year, they sat third, one point off Lincoln City in second and with a game in hand. They were four points off Cardiff City in top spot.

Unfortunately, the winter window was as big a dud as you could have imagined. Seven new people came in, but only Kayden Jackson remained a Bradford player heading into this season.

Lee Evans and the loan deals for Louie Sibley, Harrison Ashby, Joe White, Ethan Wheatley and Paul Mullen were all massive failures and didn't contribute to their second half of the season at all.

This time around, Bradford could be in a similar position, especially if they make good on their current games in hand. They currently sit on the fourth-best PPG record, even if they sit eighth in reality.

A good winter window could be the difference between pushing for the top two again or remaining outside the top six altogether. Webster would be the perfect candidate to maintain strong performances and would be a great way to kick off the January dealings at Valley Parade.
