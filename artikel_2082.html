Derby County fans slammed for "utterly ridiculous" David Clowes claims

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Despite pushing the Championship play-off places close in 2025-26, Derby County have been one of the big strugglers of the current campaign.

The Rams finished just four points adrift of the top six in May, but despite the backing in the transfer window of manager John Eustace, who was able to spend money on the likes of Bobby Clark, David Ozoh and Alex Mowatt, has struggled to get a tune out of his squad.

Injuries of course have not helped matters at Pride Park, but they sit inside the second tier relegation zone with Burnley and Preston North End, with no end in sight to their struggles.

Whilst the fanbase has been divided on Eustace's role as manager, with some wanting him gone and others blaming injuries for their rotten form as of late, chairman David Clowes is now getting it in the neck.

Local businessman Clowes rescued County in 2022 from administration, not long before they were close to liquidation after failing to find a buyer for some months, and just two years later, Derby were back in the Championship.

Clowes has made no secret about his search for external investment, but with Turki Alalshikh's buyout collapsing in August, it's left the 57-year-old on his own again.

And some Derby fans, like this one via X, are calling for Clowes to sell the club and depart to allow someone else to take them forward.

Whilst another believes that sticking with Eustace as manager and the results that have followed means that Clowes is ruining the legacy that he created a few years ago.

It's fair to say though that not every Rams fan is in agreement with the complaints and criticism that Clowes is getting.

When asked on the current situation at Pride Park right now, Football League World's Derby fan pundit, Shaun Woodward, believes that Clowes is getting an unnecessarily hard time from the fans.

"Some of the criticism David Clowes is receiving online is utterly ridiculous," Shaun told FLW.

"I'm not going to say that he should be immune to it, because he is the chairman of the club, and when a team is doing badly, the finger is going to be pointed at the manager and the chairman, it comes with the territory. I'm not going to sit here and say he should be immune from ever having any negativity thrown at him. We can't forever say because he saved the club, he's got a free pass, so to speak.

"But, the guy is a legend at Derby, and I do believe he deserves a little bit more respect on his name.

Clowes has Woodward's backing at Derby, and likely thousands of other fans, as it's not too long ago that he took a big risk to bring them out of liquidation danger.

But it's clear to see that everything is not alright on the pitch. County are struggling to pick up points, and to the surprise of many from the start of the season that would've predicted them to be challenging for the top eight.

Many clubs would have changed manager by now, and whether or not Clowes is sticking by him or is being indecisive is a matter of opinion, but Woodward believes he needs to make the big call as soon as possible - whilst also being thankful that Clowes isn't another Mel Morris who throws his money around unnecessarily.

"Yes, he's making mistakes right now, and I do believe he should be getting rid of John Eustace, Stephen Pearce and quite a lot of the backroom staff - they don't seem to be doing their jobs," Shaun admitted.

"He should be surrounding himself with better footballing people that will make better decisions either with him or for him."

"Who knows what is going on behind the scenes, I don't know whether he's actively out there trying to find those people, but it does seem at the moment that he is struggling to make a decision, and the longer we stay in this no man's land, where Pearce and Eustace are still around and we are losing every week, he's going to take a lot of flak.

"I still believe we should be thankful for what we have, and some of our fans have got short memories. We don't want to go back to the days of a Mel Morris-type chairman, who on the surface looked great by bringing in all these players, but behind the scenes he was lowering the club into the ground.

"It's frustrating right now results-wise, but that to me is on John Eustace. I think Clowes has backed him the best he can, but he's probably struggling to make that big call about whether he should fire him or not, and I do feel for him.

But ultimately he's got to start making these big calls sooner rather than later, or he will continue to get some stick, but some of it is ridiculous in my opinion, and it needs to stop."
