Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Hafnia spins out its TORM stake and rewrites its dividend policy.

Hafnia (HAFN -4.03%) reported a net profit of $170.8 million for the third quarter and announced a strategic exit from its investment in TORM via a special share distribution. Management stated that the company will distribute its entire 19.81% stake in TORM to shareholders, a move valued at approximately $686.6 million, intended to satisfy dividend obligations for the remainder of 2026. The company reported that market fundamentals for product tankers remain supported by low global inventories and a structural shift of LR2 vessels into dirty trades. Management confirmed expectations for full-year earnings of approximately $1 billion, supported by high fourth-quarter coverage rates. The company also announced an updated dividend framework effective in 2027 that scales payouts based on net loan-to-value ratios.
