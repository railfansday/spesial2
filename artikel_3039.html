1 Number Reveals Which Is the Better Buy Between Nvidia and AMD

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Is AMD really worth three times as much as Nvidia?

The Nvidia (NVDA -4.12%) versus AMD (AMD -5.58%) argument has gotten a bit more intense over the past week. A great debate between the two was who the better leader was: Jensen Huang (Nvidia's former CEO) and Lisa Su (AMD's CEO). Both were legendary leaders and had great technical minds. However, with Huang out as CEO, this score firmly flips toward AMD and Lisa Su, at least until a full-time Nvidia CEO is appointed.

So, which is the better buy between the two right now? I think there's one number that easily defines it, and it all comes down to how the market is valuing Nvidia and AMD stocks right now.

You can dig into the financials to see which stock has a better overall growth rate or a better growth rate in its data center division. Nvidia has a slight edge there, stemming from its class-leading CUDA software and the lead it established early in the artificial intelligence boom, when Nvidia's technology far surpassed AMD’s.

However, both companies are growing their data center business at or above 100%, so it's splitting hairs to say which is doing better, because they're both doing great.

So, where can investors find a difference between the companies? I'd argue you need to look no further than their respective valuations.

Nvidia and AMD are similar enough businesses that comparing their valuations head-to-head is a fair analysis. With 2026 about wrapped up, I think valuing the stock based on next year's earnings projections makes for a smart move as well.

NVDA PE Ratio (Forward 1y) data by YCharts

I'd buy the argument that AMD deserves a premium valuation over Nvidia right now given what’s going on with its leadership role. However, that is only worth so much. The reality is that Nvidia has a massive lead in the data center industry, and its software and products are the best available. Furthermore, most of next year's revenue is already locked in, as Nvidia has high visibility into what's coming down the pipeline, since clients have their spending planned far in advance.

So, this gives Nvidia plenty of time to right the ship and appoint a CEO to continue in Huang's footsteps.

With AMD trading at such a higher premium, I think Nvidia is the smarter stock to buy here. It could be a rocky few months for Nvidia shareholders, but I think after a year, this will all have blown over, and Nvidia will emerge as the stronger of the two companies to invest in.
