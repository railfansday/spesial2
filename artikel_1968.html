Expert reveals Andoni Iraola's tweak to 'unlock' Florian Wirtz at Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Liverpool manager Andoni Iraola deserves all the credit for attacking midfielder Florian Wirtz's sharp upturn in form.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that there has been a "change of sentiment" surrounding the German.

Wirtz has two goals and four assists so far this season in the Premier League, with four of those six goal involvements coming in November alone.

Wyness believes that Iraola is getting the 23-year-old pressing higher up the pitch, which in turn has led to him picking the ball up in more dangerous areas.

Wirtz has been heavily criticised since his £116million move from Bayer Leverkusen in 2025, but he has become one of Liverpool's key men of late.

OptaJoe recently revealed that he is either top or joint top of all Premier League players for goal involvements, expected assists, chances created, line-breaking passes and possessions won in the final third for November.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy business advising elite clubs – believes that Wirtz is really "starting to relish" his role within Iraola's system at Liverpool.

Discussions surrounding the starlet's ability to adapt to the Premier League have felt like a distant memory in recent weeks, such is the form he has shown at Anfield.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness hailed Wirtz's "determination" to keep going while he was struggling for form.

"Well, there's been rumours, was it (Jurgen) Klopp that unlocked him when he went on international duty? I don't believe that. I think it is Iraola," he said.

"What I've observed is that he's pressing much higher up the pitch and becoming the first line of defence. As a result, Wirtz is getting the ball much higher up the pitch, and that's where he's doing really well.

"To me, that's the big change. And I credit Iraola with that. I think that's the difference that we're seeing. Certainly, there's a change in sentiment around him.

"Look, he's a quality athlete, and a quality player. He's got the right determination, the right personality from what I've seen and I think he is starting to relish his position.

"I give Iraola all the credit for this in terms of getting him to click, but we always know that players need a bit of time to adapt from the Bundesliga. I think that time has come now and I think he's going to contribute to a very strong second half of the season for Liverpool."

Football Insider revealed on Thursday that Liverpool will explore a "very expensive" move for Bayern Munich attacking midfielder Jamal Musiala if he can prove his fitness ahead of next summer.

Recent reports in Germany have suggested that the 23-year-old has a release clause that will become active next summer.

However, Musiala is still suffering from a neurological issue that is causing him to have occasional seizures during games.

Bayern are considering his future as a result, but Liverpool will need to see that he can get back to full fitness before they make any sort of move.
