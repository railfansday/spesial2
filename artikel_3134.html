Okta vs. SentinelOne: Which Technology Stock Is a Better Buy for 2027?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Okta's cloud identity platform serves over 20,000 customers, while SentinelOne's AI-driven security grew revenue more than 21%, leaving the valuation gap worth a closer look.

By Jake Lerch – Updated Nov 18, 2026 at 1:45PM EST

As digital threats grow more sophisticated, enterprises are prioritizing identity management and endpoint security to protect their networks. Choosing between Okta (OKTA +11.05%)and SentinelOne (S +9.98%)requires weighing established profitability against rapid growth.

Okta focuses on securing identities, ensuring only authorized users can access sensitive data across cloud applications. SentinelOne uses artificial intelligence to detect and respond to threats across devices and cloud workloads. Both companies are essential players in the tech stocks landscape, providing critical infrastructure for a cloud-first world.

Okta provides a cloud-based platform that manages and secures identities for both workers and customers. The company serves over 20,000 organizations, ranging from small businesses to large government agencies and universities. Its ecosystem includes the Okta Integration Network, which features over 7,000 pre-built integrations with providers like Microsoft (MSFT +0.33%), Amazon (AMZN +3.68%), and Salesforce (CRM -2.90%).

In the 12 months ended July 31, 2026, revenue reached nearly $3.1 billion. This figure represents revenue growth of approximately 11.2% compared to the prior twelve-month period. For that same time frame, the company reported net income of roughly $296.0 million, reflecting its position as an established player in its industry.

As of its January 2026 balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio was nearly 0.1x, a measure of total debt relative to shareholders' equity. The current ratio, which indicates the ability to pay short-term obligations using short-term assets, was approximately 1.4x. In the fiscal year ended Jan. 31, 2026, free cash flow was nearly $863.0 million, though note that stock-based compensation accounted for roughly 61.5% of operating cash flow, inflating reported cash generation.

SentinelOne offers the Singularity platform, an AI-powered solution that protects endpoints, cloud workloads, and network-connected devices. The company relies on an extensive network of channel partners to fulfill sales, including resellers and distributors. Recently, it expanded its footprint through partnerships with firms such as N-able (NABL +1.06%)and Lenovo to integrate security directly into enterprise devices.

In the 12 months ended July 31, 2026, revenue reached nearly $1.1 billion. This was driven by revenue growth of approximately 21.1% over the preceding twelve months. Despite this top-line expansion, the company reported a net loss of roughly $340.1 million for the period as it continues to prioritize market share gains.

As of its January 2026 balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio was close to 0.0x, meaning the company carries virtually no debt relative to its equity. The current ratio was approximately 1.4x, indicating a liquidity profile similar to its peers. In the fiscal year ended Jan. 31, 2026, free cash flow was nearly $75.9 million, but note that stock-based compensation represented roughly 388.4% of operating cash flow, meaning reported cash generation is heavily inflated by this non-cash add-back.

Okta faces intense competition from larger, well-established entities like Microsoft, which offers integrated security suites. The business relies heavily on retaining and expanding existing customer subscriptions to maintain its revenue trajectory. Furthermore, any significant cybersecurity incident involving Okta could harm its reputation and create liability, especially as it works to integrate evolving AI technologies.

SentinelOne operates in a crowded cybersecurity market and competes for market share against rivals such as Palo Alto Networks (PANW +7.24%). Macroeconomic conditions and shifts in IT spending can significantly impact demand for its platform, which is sold through third-party partners. Additionally, the company must manage the risks of integrating new AI initiatives and potential flaws that could arise from its automated response technologies.

SentinelOne maintains a lower P/S ratio, which measures market value against sales over the past twelve months, while Okta offers a lower Forward P/E relative to future earnings estimates.

Valuation metrics include those sourced from Financial Modeling Prep (FMP) and may differ from those of other data providers.

When comparing Okta and SentinelOne, investors should consider a few key factors. Let's have a look at them and see what that tells us about each stock.

To begin, we should consider revenue growth. Over the last three years, SentinelOne has averaged 27.1% year-over-year revenue growth, although its growth has slowed in recent quarters, falling to 20.6% in its most recent quarter. Okta, meanwhile, has averaged 13.6% revenue growth over the same period, with growth slowing to 10.6% in its most recent quarter.

Another factor to consider is each company's profitability. Here, Okta has a significant edge. It has delivered a positive operating margin since 2025, whereas SentinelOne's remains below zero.

Finally, there's valuation. Since both companies are not consistently profitable, we'll examine each's price-to-sales (P/S) ratio. Okta has a P/S ratio of 10.8x, while SentinelOne's P/S ratio is 7.0x. That gives SentinelOne a slight advantage, as its shares are more affordable.

To sum up, both of these technology stocks have positive fundamentals, but they each also carry some risk. In a head-to-head matchup, more aggressive investors may favor SentinelOne due to its faster revenue growth and lower valuation. However, Okta may be favored by those who prioritize current profitability.
