Ancient skull reveals an early relative of the largest bone-cracking hyena

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A newly identified prehistoric hyena from the northeastern edge of the Tibetan Plateau aids in the quest to trace the origin of a lion-sized bone crusher. Its partial skull also records a remarkable recovery: The animal lost a canine tooth and survived long enough for bone to fill the socket.
