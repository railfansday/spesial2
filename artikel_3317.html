Why Micron Stock Is Up Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Micron is bouncing back after Thursday’s chip rout, but the reason it fell- a possible slowdown in data center construction- hasn’t gone away.

Micron Technology (MU +5.47%) stock is bouncing back, up 4.1% as of 2:14 p.m. ET on Friday, Nov. 6, after chip stocks got hammered on Thursday.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC +0.73%) and the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC +1.48%) are up 0.5% and 1.1%, respectively.

Thursday was the market's worst day since April 2025, with the Nasdaq sinking 5.5%. Micron had it worse than most -- the stock fell 17.5% as investors worried that Democratic victories in Tuesday's midterm elections could curb data center construction.

Micron's growth narrative hangs almost entirely on the data center build-out.

Today, the market seemed to feel yesterday could have been an overreaction, especially given a better-than-expected jobs report that showed the U.S. added 165,000 jobs in October.

Micron's latest numbers were huge: Q4 sales jumped to $54.23 billion from $11.32 billion a year earlier, and the company set forward guidance for $61.5 billion in the current quarter. Wall Street was only looking for $57 billion.

The continuation of this incredible pace of growth is mostly baked into the company's share price. That makes the question of a data center slowdown so important. I think it's likely we will see some moratoriums on new construction that could lead to a somewhat of a pullback in AI capital expenditures.
