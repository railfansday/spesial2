Elon Musk Blasts ‘SNL’ For Jeremy Culhane’s Impression Of Him: “They Need To Try Harder”

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Elon Musk was not impressed with the impression of him on Saturday Night Live this past weekend.

The Tesla founder took to X, the social media platform he owns and formerly known as Twitter, to blast Jeremy Culhane’s impression.

“I’d give them maybe 5/10 for me,” the controversial personality said, rating Culhane’s performance.

Musk then added, “They need to try harder.”

The divisive figure was replying to an X user who rated the SNL impressions from the cold open for the episode that aired on Saturday, October 14. The sketch featured guest host John Mulaney as a CIA officer coaching tech billionaires and testing their national security training.

I’d give them maybe 5/10 for me. They need to try harder.

Mulaney’s character was featured alongside Palantir cofounder Peter Thiel (James Austin Johnson), OpenAI CEO Sam Altman (Ben Marshall), Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei (Jane Wickline), Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg (Andrew Dismukes), and SpaceX CEO Elon Musk (Jeremy Culhane). At the end of the sketch, former SNL cast member Bowen Yang makes a surprise appearance as Jensen Huang, whose scandal inspired the sketch to begin with. Watch on Deadline
