Jennifer Lawrence Ranks Her Onscreen Kisses, Details ‘New’ Implants and Says Her ‘Hunger Games’ Bow and Arrow Was ‘Stolen’ in Andy Cohen Interview

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Jennifer Lawrence wasn’t afraid to spill tea during her appearance on the Nov. 19 episode of “Watch What Happens Live With Andy Cohen.”

She appeared alongside her “The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping” co-star Josh Hutcherson and, in her trademark honesty, fielded plenty of questions, ranging from her on-screen roles to her opinion of the “Real Housewives” franchises. At one point, Cohen asked Lawrence to rank her on-screen kisses from best to worst, including Hutcherson (whom she smooched in “The Hunger Games: Catching Fire”), Liam Hemsworth (“The Hunger Games: Mockingjay — Part 1”), Chris Pratt (“Passengers”), Timothée Chalamet (“Don’t Look Up”) and Bradley Cooper (“Silver Linings Playbook” and “Serena”).

Her ranking? Hutcherson was the best, and then Hemsworth, Cooper, Chalamet and Pratt at the bottom.

Elsewhere in the episode, she was candid about her breast implants. During a segment where she was discussing hot topics in the “Real Housewives” universe, Lawrence was asked about a storyline in which “Real Housewives of New York” star Hailey Glassman said her nose was broken by a woman’s breast.

“Is it a mystery to you how a boob could break Haley Glassman’s nose?” Cohen asked.

“Yes, and I have implants,” Lawrence said.

She then went on to say that “they’re new” and shouted out Dr. Steven Teitelbaum in Santa Monica for his work. Cohen then asked about the size, and she said, “It’s a full C, I think. It’s the same size that they were.”

Elsewhere, when Cohen asked if she ever snagged a bow and arrow from the “Hunger Games” set as a souvenir, she said that she did but couldn’t locate it.

“I think I lost it, or I guess it was stolen,” she said. “Where am I going to lose it? I’m just actually learning this in real time that they did.”
