‘Sunset P.I.’ Sets NBC Release Date, Jake Johnson Comedy Series Drops New First-Look Photos

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

NBC has set the premiere date for its new comedy series “Sunset P.I.”

The single-camera comedy will debut Monday, Feb. 8 at 8:30 pm ET/PT. Episodes will be available to stream the next day on Peacock. NBC has also released new first-look images from the series, which can be seen below.

The series stars Jake Johnson, Jane Levy, Langston Kerman, Mary Shalaby and Keith David. “Sunset P.I.” was created by Dan Goor and Luke Del Tredici, who also serve as executive producers. Akiva Schaffer directed and executive produced the pilot. Universal Television is the studio.

“Sunset P.I.” was originally developed as part of NBC’s most recent pilot season and officially picked up to series in May. The show was seen as a strong contender for a series pickup from the jump, and was one of four new series the broadcaster ordered for the 2026-2027 season. Others include the cop drama “Line of Fire,” a reboot of “The Rockford Files” with David Boreanaz, and the multi-cam comedy “Newlyweds.”
