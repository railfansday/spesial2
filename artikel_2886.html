Lions fan chokes out Bears supporter in vicious Thanksgiving brawl

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Chicago Bears got the better of the Detroit Lions when the two teams faced off on Thanksgiving Day, winning a thriller, 39-35.

But a Lions fan got the best of a Bears supporter in a wild brawl that took place inside Detroit's Ford Field while the game was underway.

A Detroit Lions fan fighting with a Chicago Bears fan inside Ford Field. Instagram/@metrodetroitnews

The video was initially posted on Instagram by Metro Detroit News and started with a Lions fan raining punches down on the Bears fan, who he was already on top of despite numerous people nearby trying to pull him off.

After several punches, the Lions fan wraps his arm around the Bears fan's neck and seemingly tries to choke him out.

Detroit Lions fan puts a Chicago Bears fan in a chokehold in the stands. Instagram/@metro

The two men fell to the floor a few seconds later but seemed to be in the same position.

Then another fan wearing a black jacket appears to try and stomp on one of the fans before getting pulled away, at which point the video ended.

A man in a black jacket stomping a fan on the floor during the Chicago Bears vs. Detroit Lions game.

It's unclear what sparked the fight between these two fans.

But the Lions and the Bears are bitter rivals, and this is surely not the first time fans of the two teams have resorted to blows.

Brawls between fans at NFL games have been a recurring theme over the past several weeks, with multiple videos of these ugly scraps going viral every weekend.

Apparently, even on a day of thanks, Thanksgiving, some fans are unable to hold their emotions in check. A wild video surfaced late Thursday of an Eagles fan manhandling two Cowboys fans in a fracas.

That came on the heels of Rams and Packers supporters going toe-to-toe on Wednesday night.
