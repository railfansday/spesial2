Fall sports season is back, which means it's time for women's college volleyball. As always, one of the nation's top programs is ready to dominate — get ready for Nebraska to hit the court.

Even with a lethal core squad, the Huskers fell short of a title last year, losing to eventual champions Texas A&M in the Elite Eight. Prior to the regional final, Nebraska had sat strongly at the top of the national polls as it won 33 consecutive matches.

But a curtain call is looming for this era of Nebraska volleyball, as many of the program's star players are heading into their senior year. Outside hitter Harper Murray, middle blocker Andi Jackson, setter Bergen Reilly and libero Laney Choboy will all have an extra sense of determination as they look to secure a national title before graduating.

Nebraska ranked No. 1 in the preseason AVCA coaches poll. Can Dani Busboom-Kelly lead the Huskers to a title in her second year at the helm?

Here's how to watch Nebraska volleyball this year, including TV channels and streaming options for each game on the schedule.

TV channel: Fox, FS1, TNT, BTN, Nebraska Public Media

Nebraska volleyball matches will be broadcast on a variety of networks this season. Fans will be able to watch the Huskers on Fox, FS1, TNT, Big Ten Network and Nebraska Public Media. See the full schedule below.

Televised matches will be available to stream on Fubo or DIRECTV. Select matches also will be available to stream via B1G+.
