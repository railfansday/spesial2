Back in the old days of Black Friday shopping, you had to fight other members of your community—your kids’ bus driver, the people that sat next to you in church, a friend’s personal trainer—to score a good TV on sale. You were all in tents, only able to use whatever porta-potty they had set up in the parking lot. You were, effectively, animals—full survival mode.

Thankfully, Black Friday—which Amazon was hellbent on making even longer shopping holiday this year with it’s “Turkey 12,” 12-day event—is slightly more dignified these days. You’re still a pawn in some executive’s scheme to show a big green number to the shareholders, but at least it’s a little more dignified. You can shop in your PJs, and you don’t even have to spend money on gas.

Our favorite deal of the day week is Samsung’s Frame TV, which is on a handsome little discount.
