Thomas Castellanos exits Texas Tech football game after big hit

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Texas Tech football starting quarterback Thomas Castellanos exited Saturday night's game against Oklahoma State after taking a brutal hit in the fourth quarter.

Castellanos took a run to the outside and headed for the end zone before being driven into the back of teammate Kenny Johnson. The play was nullified by a holding call but Castellanos and Johnson both stayed down after the hit.

Johnson got up under his own power while Castellanos came out of the game for the remainder of the drive, which resulted in a 54-yard field goal by Stone Harrington to put Texas Tech up 45-20 with 8:59 left.

Castellanos was being looked at by trainers and headed to the locker room.

This is a developing story and will be updated when information becomes available.
