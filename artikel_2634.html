Bavarian Loan Works: Chávez scores first goal of season

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

As club matches resume after the international break, Bayern Munich loanees had a mixed week. Arijon Ibrahimović and Bryan Zaragoza, who are almost always in the starting lineups, were both on the bench. Two 2. Bundesliga loanees scored goals—unfortunately, one of those goals was an own goal. See how these loanees fared, who made it onto the pitch, and who was left out.

Arijon Ibrahimović is the only player on loan in the Bundesliga.

Ibrahimović started on the bench for Augsburg as it hosted Mainz in Bundesliga play. He came into the match in the 64th minute after all the goals had been scored. Ibrahimović had a shot—off target—and recorded just one interception and three recoveries on defense. Luckily, Augsburg and he were able to coast to a 3–1 home win.

Augsburg is away to Hoffenheim on Sunday.

Bryan Zaragoza is the only player on loan to Spain's LaLiga.

Zaragoza was on the bench for Espanyol's 1–0 away win against Athletic Club Bilbao, but did not make it onto the pitch.

Felipe Chávez is the only player on loan from the first team to Germany's 2. Bundesliga.

Chávez started for Magdeburg on the road against Arminia Bielefeld and played the full 90 minutes in midfield. He opened the scoring in the 12th minute with his first goal of the season. On the day, he had two shots—both on target—and created two chances for his teammates to shoot, including one scored as a big chance created. Chávez also tracked back to help on defense with two tackles, a clearance, an interception, and seven recoveries. Magdeburg doubled its lead before halftime; Bielefeld pulled one back in the second half, but Magdeburg held on for a 2–1 road win.

Magdeburg hosts Energie Cottbus on Sunday.

There are two players from the second team on loan to Germany's 2. Bundesliga: Javier Fernández and Deniz Ofli.

Fernández started for Nürnberg at home against Eintracht Braunschweig. In attack, he had three shots—one off target and two blocked—and did not create a chance for his teammates to shoot. Unfortunately, he put the ball in the net, though it was his own. His own goal equalized the match in the 61st minute. Fernández tried to make up for it with a tackle, three clearances, an interception, and eight recoveries, but it was not enough to keep the visitors from finding a winner. He left the match in the 88th minute with his team down 2–1. Nürnberg was unable to find an equalizer and fell at home by that scoreline.

Ofli started for Karlsruhe and played the full 90 minutes at home against VfL Osnabrück. He did not have a shot, but created two chances for his teammates to shoot. He contributed mostly on defense, recording a tackle, a blocked shot, two clearances, and an interception to help the home side keep a clean sheet (or shutout). KSC was unable to find a goal and earned a scoreless draw at home, lifting it out of the relegation zone for now.

KSC travels to Berlin to face its close friends and league leaders, Hertha BSC.

Richard Meier is the only player on loan to Germany's 3. Liga.

Meier was not in the matchday squad for Meppen for its 2–1 road loss against Fortuna Köln.

Meppen is away again to Wehen Wiesbaden on Sunday.

Magnus Dalpiaz is the only player on loan to Austria's Bundesliga.

Dalpiaz started for Hartberg away to LASK and played the full 90 minutes on the left wing. He neither took a shot nor created a chance for his teammates to shoot. He mostly sat back on defense and filled the stat sheet, recording two tackles, four clearances, an interception, and five recoveries. He also received a yellow card in the first half for a bad foul. Hartberg fell 2–1 on the road.

Hartberg is on the road against SV Ried on Sunday.

Julien Yanda is the only player on loan to Spain's LaLiga2.

Yanda was on the bench for Eldense's 2–0 loss away to Castellón, but did not make it onto the pitch.

Anna Wellmann is the only player on loan to Spain's Primera División Femenina.

Wellmann was on the bench last weekend for Eibar as it advanced in the Copa de la Reina with a 1–0 win away to Levante. She was back in goal this weekend as Eibar hosted Atlético Madrid in league play. She recorded six saves, but also conceded three goals—at least one of which stemmed from an error by a teammate. Her teammates could only score once at home, and Eibar fell 3–1.

Eibar hosts Logroño United after the upcoming international break for the women's national teams.
