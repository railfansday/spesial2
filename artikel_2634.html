Brock Bowers not at open of practice Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

It appears Las Vegas Raiders superstar tight end Brock Bowers will continue to be "day-to-day" moving forward as the team prepares to play at the Denver Broncos on Sunday. Kickoff is set at 1:25 p.m. PT.

According to The Athletic, Bowers was not seen during the practice window that was open to the media Wednesday morning. It's not a major surprise.

Bowers injured his ankle Sunday in a home loss to the Seattle Seahawks. He left the game twice and for good in the second quarter. Monday, Las Vegas coach Klint Kubiak labeled Bowers' injury status as "day-to-day." Thus, he still could have a chance to play in Denver if he gets on the practice field by Friday.

Also, The Athletic noted that defensive tackle Tonka Hemingway wasn't at practice Wednesday, either. He was hurt late n third quarter, but did return.

This post will be updated after Kubiak speaks and the injury report is released later Wednesday.
