‘Dreadful Officiating,’ ‘Need To Be Investigated’ — NFL Fans Rip Refs Over Controversial No-PI Call Ends Up Costing Ravens MNF Win

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Baltimore Ravens entered the game with a 4-5 record and were looking to take advantage of a struggling Los Angeles Chargers team that had lost 11 consecutive games, dating back to last season. However, the Ravens had another frustrating night on Monday, suffering a 23-20 loss to the Chargers.

The Ravens have now suffered their fifth consecutive loss and dropped to 4-6 on the season. Meanwhile, the Chargers finally ended their losing streak, improving to 1-8 with their first win of the season.

Adding to Baltimore's frustration is a controversial officiating decision that has become a major talking point among fans.

The Ravens' loss was overshadowed by a controversial officiating decision involving wide receiver Chris Moore. As Baltimore attempted to fight its way back into the game, officials chose not to call pass interference on a play where Moore appeared to be brought down before the ball reached him.

The missed call triggered outrage among NFL fans, with many questioning the officials' decision.

"If anyone knows what pass interference is, then please let the NFL officials know because they did not call this PI on the Chargers," Rate the Refs wrote.

"Refs completely miss a PI on that potential Chris Moore TD. He was tackled well before the ball got there," Ravens reporter Sarah Ellison wrote.

The criticism was loud as the play involved a potentially big scoring opportunity. Baltimore, which ranks No. 22 in the PFN Offense Impact with a 70.5 grade, was trailing 23-13 when Brissett targeted Moore from the Chargers' 4-yard line. The Ravens scored four plays later on Derrick Henry's 1-yard touchdown run to cut the deficit to 23-20.

As a pass interference penalty could have given the Ravens a more favorable position inside the red zone and potentially changed how the drive unfolded, fans kept slamming the officials.

"No pass interference called on this throw to Ravens WR Chris Moore," Kevin Oestreicher, host of the "Locked On Ravens" podcast, noted.

"They need to be investigated!!!!" a fan added.

Beyond the controversial call, the loss was another difficult night for Baltimore, as losing by three points to a Chargers team that entered the game winless this season made the result especially damaging. It also marked just the second time in franchise history that the Ravens had lost to an opponent with a 0-8 record or worse.

Baltimore's offensive struggles were evident from the opening half, managing just six rushing attempts for zero yards before halftime and setting a franchise record for the fewest rushing yards in a first half. Their overall ground production was hardly better, finishing with 15 carries for 16 yards, the third-lowest single-game rushing total in franchise history.
