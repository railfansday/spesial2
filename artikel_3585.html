Honkai: Star Rail version 4.7 release time in your time zone

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Here's when you'll be able to play version 4.7

Honkai: Star Rail's 4.7 patch is coming soon, adding the first playable Aeon, Aeon ★ Aha (the artist formerly known as Nihilux). Older characters will also be getting buffs, and the usual slew of events will be taking place, allowing you to earn Stellar Jade and other rewards.

Below, we explain when Honkai: Star Rail maintenance starts and ends, alongside the release time of version 4.7. We’ve also added some details about what to expect from Honkai: Star Rail version 4.7.

Make sure to burn your fuel before maintenance starts, if you wanted to. As a reminder, the new battle pass will start once the patch launches, so you may want to hold on to some of your stamina to farm out new materials and whatnot for that.

The servers may come up early or late, depending on how maintenance goes, but these are the approximate times 4.7 should go live. Usually Hoyoverse is pretty good by abiding by maintenance times. We don’t usually get extended maintenance, but it does end early sometimes!

Now that we know Nihilux's true identity, the fun is really beginning. Nihilux, now known officially as Aeon ★ Aha, will be available to pull as a five-star Quantum Elation character during this patch. Acheron, Dan Heng Imbibitor Lunae, and Jing Yuan will all be getting buffs, though it'll be through an "Affinitive Eidolon" system. Everyone who logs in during the patch will be able to pick one of the Affinitive Eidolons to have for free, so make sure to grab one while you can.

There'll also be a Relic Recon event, allowing you to earn relics and roll a nice set for a character of your choosing, so long as you play the game and participate in other types of content during this patch.
