Brothers Sentenced for Torturing and Killing Family of 4, Setting Home Ablaze

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Two brothers will spend the rest of their lives in prison for torturing and killing a family of four inside their upstate New York home.

Julio and Luis Soriano received life sentences without parole on Monday, November 16, after they were convicted of murdering their cousin Fraime Ubaldo, his girlfriend and the couple’s two young children at their Monroe County home in 2024, Spectrum News reported.

“A family was tortured and murdered,” Monroe County District Attorney Brian Green said in a Monday statement on the case prosecuted by Wayne County prosecutors.

“The Sorianos caused the deaths of an Irondequoit family — a father, 30-year-old Fraime Ubaldo, a mother, 26-year-old Marangely Moreno-Santiago, and their two children, four-year-old Evangeline Ubaldo Moreno, and two-year-old Sebastian Ubaldo-Moreno,” Green added. “The extended families of these victims will forever grieve the enormous hole left behind, and these crimes will continue to have a devastating impact for years to come.”

Julio, 36, and Luis, 34, allegedly traveled to the family’s home outside Rochester to kill Fraime, Marangely, Evangeline and Sebastian on August 31, 2024.

That day, the family of four was discovered dead in their basement following a fire at their home, WHAM-TV reported. They each had been fatally stabbed.

According to prosecutors, the blaze was caused by the Soriano brothers. Julio and Luis allegedly ignited eight fires to try to conceal evidence of the murders, according to the TV station.

Prosecutors said the killings occurred after an argument related to drugs. They noted Fraime and his girlfriend allegedly were involved with the Soriano brothers in connection with efforts to obtain drugs from Puerto Rico, the TV station reported.

Julio and Luis Soriano were both found guilty in September on charges including murder, kidnapping, burglary and arson, WROC-TV reported. Prior to their convictions, their defense counsel had argued that evidence didn’t directly link them to the murders.

At trial, prosecutors showed surveillance footage of Julio buying zip ties that the brothers allegedly used to restrain Fraime, according to WHAM-TV. The brothers dragged Fraime into the basement before beating him, according to doorbell footage shown in court.

“Today’s sentencing closes a long, dark chapter, prompting a family and community to look toward a path to healing, even as they still process their grief,” Green said. “There is no value that can be placed on a life, but there is no doubt that these two men should ever breathe the air outside of a prison.”

During the brothers’ sentencing hearing, a special prosecutor read a victim impact statement written by Marangely’s mother, Michelle Santiago, in which she recalled her initial reaction to the murders.

“It felt as if my heart had just been ripped out of me,” Santiago wrote in her statement, WHAM-TV reported. “The pain was so excruciating that I could not speak or move.”
