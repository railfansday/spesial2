GTA 6 Official Sales Numbers Are In — And They're Unbelievable

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Rockstar Games' new release is 'the biggest entertainment launch in history'

Grand Theft Auto 6 is off to a staggering start mere days into its release. Rockstar Games and Grand Theft Auto publisher Take-Two Interactive announced Friday that GTA 6 sold 32.5 million copies during its pre-release period — a new record across all platforms, Take-Two says — and generated $3.79 billion in revenue.

Those figures are based on Take-Two's internal estimates and account for worldwide sales through the end of the day on Nov. 19, the day of Grand Theft Auto 6's launch.

“It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI,” said Sam Houser, president and founder of Rockstar Games, in a news release. “We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.”

Take-Two Interactive boasted that GTA 6's launch has set "a new record for entertainment across any medium," and that the game is now the biggest entertainment launch in history. Take-Two and Rockstar also bragged, rightfully so, about the game's near-perfect critical reception; GTA 6 has an incredibly rare 99 average critics score on Metacritic and OpenCritic, making it the highest rated video game of all time.

In the lead-up to release, Take-Two said that pre-orders for Grand Theft Auto 6 were tracking "far above" the publisher's initial expectations. Third-party data estimates put GTA 6's early sales at around 25 million copies, resulting in $2.36 billion in revenue. Those estimates were clearly well below what Take-Two has officially announced.

Other estimates, based on pre-order data, put GTA 6 on pace to earn somewhere between $3.3 billion and $5.2 billion in cumulative global sales by the end of launch week. Analysis firm Newzoo said this summer that GTA 6 was expected to earn $4.5 billion in sales by launch week and sell as much as 51 million units.

The official day-one sales for GTA 6 obliterated records for the previous entry in the series, Grand Theft Auto 5. When that game launched on PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360 in 2013, publisher Take-Two Interactive boasted $800 million in sales on the game's first day of release. At the time, it was the highest launch-day total for any GTA game and any game in Take-Two history.

Three days after launching, Grand Theft Auto 5 became the fastest entertainment property to hit $1 billion in sales, according to Take-Two. GTA 5 has since sold nearly 235 million copies worldwide across all platforms.

Rockstar has not officially disclosed GTA 6's development budget. But Rockstar previously shot down the idea that GTA 6 cost $1.5 billion to develop, though the game likely cost over $1 billion to make, based on the long-awaited sequel's development cycle and the sheer number of employees and contractors hired to work on the game. It is likely the most expensive video game ever made.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is available on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X. A PC version has not been announced.
