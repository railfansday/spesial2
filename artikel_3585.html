Christine Quinn Makes Rare Chrishell Comment After 'Selling Sunset' Feud

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Ahead of her return to Selling Sunset, Christine Quinn is reflecting on her past feuds — starting with Chrishell Stause.

“I personally never had a problem with Chrishell,” Quinn, 38, exclusively told Us before the season 10 premiere on December 4. “I don’t hold grudges. It’s just not who I am.”

Quinn noted that the “animosity” between her and Stause, 45, “wasn’t from” her side, adding, “I was willing to be in rooms with her. I was willing to work — and I really feel like I never had a problem. Even though we had disagreements, I could still show up to work. We could still work together.”

The reality star confirmed she “wouldn’t have a problem filming with” Stause again “at all,” adding, “Would it be comfortable? Probably not for her. But I’d be OK with that.”

The duo faced their fair share of ups and downs since Selling Sunset debuted in 2019. Quinn was initially close to fellow real estate agents Mary Bonnet, Maya Vander and Heather Rae El Moussa before Stause joined the Oppenheim Group in season 1.

In season 5, Quinn ultimately remained at odds with everyone except newcomer Chelsea Lazkani before leaving the brokerage. She is now coming back amid a cast shakeup — with Stause not returning for the first time since the show’s premiere.

“I think where [Chrishell is] at in her life, she’s doing her own thing,” Quinn told Us. “She was on it for so many seasons. What she wants is to probably not be on the show anymore.”

Despite their differences, Quinn didn’t rule out them working things out in the future after both going through big changes in her personal life. (Stause announced in October that her four-year relationship with G Flip has come to an end while Quinn had her own messy divorce from Christian Richard since her time on Selling Sunset.)

“I can definitely bond and relate to anyone who’s going through a breakup. I know it’s a horrible situation, and I don’t wish that on my worst enemy,” Quinn told Us. “I’m just kidding. I don’t wish it upon anyone. I really don’t. I wish her all the best.”

Before her reality TV return, Quinn reflected on the decision.

“A lot has changed since viewers last saw me, and I think they’re going to be pleasantly surprised,” she shared. “I’ve grown so much. I’m still the same Christine, just a little softer and more vulnerable.”

She concluded: “I really never wanted to leave. But mentally, I had to for myself. I had my son, and my marriage was falling apart. It was a lot. Even though it’s [only] been four years, it feels like so long ago. And then when I got back into it, it was like no time had gone by. I’ve always had a special place in my heart for the Oppenheim Group and for real estate. So the timing was right. It felt good. I was thrilled.”

Selling Sunset premieres on Netflix December 4.
