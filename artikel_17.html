Philippines police ask prosecutors to charge Thomas Markle over child sex abuse material

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Thomas Markle has separately been charged in the US

Police in the Philippines have asked prosecutors to charge Thomas Markle Sr, the father of Meghan, Duchess of Sussex, with possession of and intent to distribute child sexual abuse material.

Cebu province prosecutor Graeme Elmido told the BBC that police had filed documents requesting that Markle be charged on Monday.

Earlier this month, Markle, 82, was separately arrested and charged in the US with possession of child sexual abuse material. The FBI said agents seized about 78 items, including film negatives, slides and books that they allege contained child sex abuse material, from storage units Markle rented.

Markle, who is in custody in the US, has previously denied the allegations. The BBC has contacted Markle's attorney for comment.

Elmido told the BBC that Philippine National Police had asked prosecutors to charge Markle with "two counts of intent to distribute CSAEM [child sexual abuse and exploitation material], and one count of possession of CSAEM material".

Last month, police in the Philippines obtained a warrant to examine devices allegedly removed from Markle's former home in the country.

Four boxes of material, including computers, phones, hard drives and old video tapes, were handed to officers by Meghan's half brother Thomas Markle Jr.

Earlier this month, Markle Sr was arrested and charged in the US. He had initially been placed under house detention at his assisted living facility in Florida, but was taken back into custody last week after prosecutors alleged he removed his ankle monitor.

Meghan Markle has been estranged from her father since 2018. The BBC understands she broke contact with him last year, telling him she and the authorities knew he had abused young girls.

In a previously shared statement after the allegations against her father emerged, Meghan wrote: "I am deeply distressed... my thoughts are with any children who may have been harmed."

She said she hoped it could be understood why she had largely cut off contact from her father and did "not allow him to have contact with our children".
