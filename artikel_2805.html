Rice men hold off Lamar's rally to win opener at Jacksonville Classic

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

JACKSONVILLE, Fla. - Rice built a big first-half lead and held off Lamar's second-half rally for an 85-81 victory Sunday night in the opening game of the Scout Jacksonville Classic.

The Owls (4-2) led by 11 at halftime, but Lamar (4-2) cut the margin to one several times in the second half. Jacob Dar answered one late push with a 3-pointer, and Rice closed out the win at the free-throw line.

Dar finished with 23 points and 11 rebounds for his first double-double of the season. Jaxson Thompson scored 20 points and had a season-high six assists. Jimmy Oladokun Jr. added a season-high 15 points with seven rebounds and three blocks.

Rice next faces Illinois-Chicago in the tournament at 12:30 p.m. Tuesday.
