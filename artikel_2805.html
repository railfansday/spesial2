KenPom predicts winner of Player's Era 8 matchup between Auburn and UNLV

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Auburn basketball lost a tough game to West Virginia on Tuesday in the Player's Era 8 quarterfinals in Las Vegas and will aim to bounce back Wednesday against UNLV.

How does KenPom see Wednesday's game between the Tigers and Rebels shaking out?

KenPom gives Auburn a 73% chance to beat UNLV on Wednesday, with an implied score of 81-74. If this prediction becomes true, it will snap Auburn's two-game losing streak and lock down a spot in Thursday's fifth-place game against either Rutgers or Notre Dame.

Auburn has the advantage on both ends of the floor. The Tigers rank No. 33 in offensive efficiency and will battle UNLV's 84th-ranked defense. On the other side, UNLV's offense ranks No. 107 and will look to navigate its way through Auburn's No. 37-ranked defense.

Auburn also relies on its bench more than UNLV and has a slight height advantage. One advantage that could work in UNLV's favor is tempo. According to KenPom data, the Rebels create two more offensive possessions per game than Auburn.

Auburn faces UNLV in the Player's Era 8 consolation game on Wednesday at 9 p.m. CT on ESPN2.
