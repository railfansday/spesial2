Dan Quinn: Brandon Aiyuk won't play vs Cardinals

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

It's a short week for the Washington Commanders with a holiday and travel out west also making for a funky practice schedule. Reporters weren't onsite for yesterday's practice due to the Thanksgiving holiday. Practice in Wednesday was a walkthru, and today's practice will be shorter as they team prepares to fly to Arizona to take on the Cardinals on Sunday.

There's also a new face on the practice fields today, and he's wearing his old number. Brandon Aiyuk was officially signed to the practice squad and has worked out a deal with WR Luke McCaffrey for the No. 11 jersey. Dan Quinn said he won't be activated for the Cardinals game, but he looked good in practice, and he's looking forward to seeing him on the field when he's ready.

Amik Robertson will miss a second game due to an ankle injury suffered in practice. Quinn was asked if he's going on injured reserve or there are long-term concerns. He said they're not there yet, and Robertson is still battling to get back on the field.
