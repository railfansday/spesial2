Eagles Injury Report: DeVonta Smith on track to play against Steelers

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Philadelphia Eagles issued their second official injury report of the week on Thursday in advance of their Week 11 game against the Pittsburgh Steelers.

The Eagles listed one player under DID NOT PARTICIPATE: Zack Baun and Smael Mondon.

Baun missed practice for the second day in a row. He's tracking to miss Sunday's game. The Eagles will start Jeremiah Trotter Jr. and Jihaad Campbell at off-ball linebacker.

Mondon was downgraded from full on Wednesday to DNP on Thursday. If he doesn't practice on Friday, he likely won't play this week. If both Baun and Mondon can't play, the Eagles will be down to Trot Jr. and Campbell as their only two healthy linebackers. They'll likely temporarily elevate Chance Campbell and/or Anfernee Orji from the practice squad for more depth.

The Eagles listed six players under LIMITED PARTICIPATION: Dontayvion Wicks, Will Shipley, Nolan Smith, Michael Carter, Britain Covey, and Jonathan Jones.

All six of these players were limited for the second day in a row. We'll learn more about their statuses on Friday.

Smith seems likely to play since he's been limited due to illness, not injury.

The Eagles listed seven players under FULL PARTICIPATION: DeVonta Smith, Saquon Barkley, Jihaad Campbell, Jonathan Greenard, Jalyx Hunt, Andrew Mukuba, and Tanner McKee.

Smitty was notably upgraded from limited on Wednesday to full go on Thursday. He's on track to play for the first time since Week 6. Good for the Eagles to be getting their top wide receiver back!

Barkley, Campbell, Greenard, Hunt, Mukuba, and McKee were full go for the second day in a row.
