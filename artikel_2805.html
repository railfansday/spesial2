Vote for the Detroit Free Press Boys Athlete of the Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

It's time to vote for the Detroit Free Press Boys Athlete of the Week.

This week's poll includes three players who had standout performances during the week of Nov. 2-8.

Voting will close at 3 p.m. on Thursday, Nov. 12.

Here are the nominees (listed in alphabetical order by last name):

De La Salle's all-time leading scorer led the Pilots to a historic victory on Saturday, Nov. 7, as they defeated St. Joseph, 3-2, to win a third consecutive Division 2 state championship and the program's eighth overall. Corder scored two goals in both the semifinal and final and added an assist in the championship match. The victory secured De La Salle's place in state history, making the Pilots the first program in Michigan's two largest boys soccer classifications to win three consecutive state titles.

Ecorse advanced to the regional tournament with a 26-6 district championship victory over Warren Michigan Collegiate on Friday, Nov. 6. Senior running back Massey powered the offense for the Raiders as he finished with 188 rushing yards and three touchdowns on 17 carries.

Romeo improved to 11-0 on the season in a dominating 42-2 victory against Rochester on Friday. Siracoff, a senior running back, rushed the ball five times for 164 yards and four touchdowns. He also had two receptions for 30 yards.
