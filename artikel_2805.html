Verstappen wins in Las Vegas as Ferrari rivals clash again

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Max Verstappen delivered a performance for the ages to win a thrill-a-minute Las Vegas Grand Prix - as Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc collided after the British driver had earlier warned his Ferrari team-mate to "back the f*** off."

On a fiery night in Sin City, Verstappen and George Russell exchanged positions 12 times as they duelled for the lead before Lando Norris and then Kimi Antonelli muscled their way into the conversation.

Norris and Antonelli both had spells in the lead, but Verstappen refused to budge at the sharp end of the pack despite the powerful and unprecedented slipstream rotating the leaderboard like a slot machine.

By the end of 50 laps of breathless racing - the sort of which not seen before in modern Formula One - Verstappen crossed the line six tenths clear of runner-up Norris, with McLaren team-mate Oscar Piastri third.

Antonelli had to settle for fourth as Russell's challenge fell apart when he pranged the barrier with just two laps left. He finished ninth.

Hamilton's relationship with Leclerc was already fractious heading into Saturday's contest following a string of altercations between the warring Ferrari team-mates in the second half of this season.

And it is now in full-on crisis mode after Leclerc crashed into his team-mate with just 13 laps left.

Leclerc was slapped with a 10-second penalty for the collision, demoting him to 10th. Hamilton, who was also earlier forced to serve a 10-second sanction for a tussle with Norris - a decision the seven-time world champion described as "b******t" - was seventh on another torrid evening for Ferrari.
