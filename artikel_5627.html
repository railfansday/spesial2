MAVERIX Welcomes Real Estate Industry Veteran Anna Visioli as Strategic Advisor

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

MAVERIX, a strategic advisory firm serving the real estate and PropTech industries, has announced that Anna Visioli has joined the firm as a strategic advisor. Visioli brings more than two decades of experience spanning technology, operations, digital marketing and enterprise strategy, most recently serving as senior vice president of Strategy and Implementation across the franchise brands of what is now Compass International Holdings. Throughout her 21-year career with Coldwell Banker and its parent organizations, including Realogy and Anywhere Real Estate, Visioli held numerous leadership positions, helping develop and implement technology platforms, operational programs and strategic initiatives across some of the industry’s most recognized brokerage brands. Her experience navigating complex organizations and bringing new programs and technologies to market adds valuable enterprise-level expertise to MAVERIX’s growing advisory team. “Anna brings something incredibly valuable to MAVERIX and our clients: a deep understanding of how major real estate organizations operate,” said Jeff Kennedy, founder and CEO of MAVERIX. “She’s spent more than 20 years on the brokerage side, working at the intersection of technology, strategy, and execution. That perspective is invaluable for the companies we advise, and we’re excited to have her on the team.” “Real estate has no shortage of great ideas. The challenge is turning those ideas into results,” said Visioli. “Helping brokerages execute on their priorities and develop technology that fits how they work has been the most rewarding part of my career. I’m excited to bring that experience to MAVERIX clients.” As a strategic advisor, Visioli will help MAVERIX clients navigate enterprise relationships, refine go-to-market strategies, and better understand the operational and technology needs of large brokerage organizations. For more information, visit https://www.maverixadvisory.com/.
