XRP Ledger Hits Highest Level of 2026

XRP Ledger activity has jumped to its highest level this year, while the price of XRP slips lower. Active accounts, which count the unique senders on the network, reached 32,604 on October 27. That is the highest reading since the start of 2026 and well above the 30-day average of 19.4K.

For most of the past year, daily active accounts stayed between 13K and 20K. A few short spikes pushed above 30K, but they faded quickly. The latest jump is larger and cleaner than those moves. It also follows a deep drop in late August and early September, when activity fell to nearly 4K accounts.

The numbers have cooled since the peak. The latest reading is 30.5K, down 6.3% in 24 hours, and the chart shows a sharp pullback toward 18K. Even so, the network is far busier than it was a few weeks ago. The all-time high of 106K is still far away, so the ledger has a long way to go before it reaches record levels.

The price chart does not match the surge in usage. XRP trades near $1.29 and has been falling since early October, when it was close to $1.50. The coin is now below the 50-day and 100-day averages, which sit around $1.33 and $1.36. The 200-day average near $1.37 is also above the price. All of these now act as resistance.

Support is near $1.25, where buyers stepped in twice in recent weeks. If that level breaks, the next area to watch is $1.20, followed by the August breakout area near $1.10 to $1.15. Volume has stayed low throughout the decline, and the RSI sits just below 50. This indicates the market is neutral and lacks strong momentum in either direction.

A rise in active accounts can mean more people are using the network for payments, trading, or moving funds. That is a healthy signal for the long term. But network activity does not always push the price up in the short term. Spikes can also come from bots, airdrops, or one-time events, and they often fade quickly, as past peaks this year have shown.

For XRP to turn bullish, the price needs to climb back above $1.33 and then $1.37. Until that happens, the trend remains weak, and the busy ledger stays a positive sign that the market has not yet rewarded.
