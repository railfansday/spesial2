HRC’s 1300-HP Trophy Truck Is the Most Badass Honda Passport Ever

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

This new cutting-edge machine features a powerful hybrid system and a twin-turbo V-6 like Acura’s IMSA race car.

If you want your kids to think the Honda Passport parked in your driveway is more than a run-of-the-mill three-row mid-size SUV, show them a picture of this new race truck, which not only wears the Passport name, but features a front end that faithfully resembles the crossover’s mug. “Daddy’s/mommy’s car is based on a race car,” you can tell them. That’ll be a lie; the Baja Spec shares nothing more than a name with the mainstream Passport. But it’ll also give you street cred in your kids’ mind, ‘cause your car will be associated with the most badass Passport ever made.

The Honda Passport Baja Spec is a new state-of-the-art racing machine that was developed to compete in the top Trophy Truck class of the 2027 SCORE World Desert Championship. If you didn’t know, Honda has a long history with Baja racing dating back to the early ‘60s, and it played a role in the first official running of the Baja 1000. Following 22 overall victories in that legendary race, Honda Racing Corporation (HRC) will make its new trophy truck the first hybrid vehicle to compete in California’s premier off-road endurance racing series.

The Passport Baja Spec is powered by a mid-mounted twin-turbocharged 2.4-liter V-6 that’s based on the engine in Acura’s ARX-06 IMSA race car, and it’s paired with an 800-volt hybrid system. The latter features a lightweight battery pack with a carbon-fiber shell that protects pouch cells similar to the ones used in Formula 1 racing. A front-mounted five-speed gearbox sends over 1300 horsepower to all four wheels through an all-wheel-drive transaxle and dual rear driveshafts.

HRC says the Passport Baja Spec introduces new tech to the series, with its roster of innovations including dual-axle brake-by-wire, adaptive traction control, and power that’s deployed by AI. It’s all hidden under a carbon-fiber body that’s adorned with a Red Bull livery; underneath is a 145-gallon safety cell made from carbon Kevlar and developed by Mason Motorsports. To help manage airflow, HRC added side channels behind the front wheels, and tunnels around the cab help reduce aerodynamic drag.

The trophy truck’s ride is controlled by a custom long-travel suspension that features Fox racing dampers. A set of large Toyo tires highlights all four corners, and there’s a backup pair of rubber shoes sticking out the back of the Baja Spec Passport. And speaking of things in the back, HRC positioned fixed-mass components like coolers, batteries, and fluid tanks there to better distribute weight and lower the center of gravity for improved handling.

While the Honda Passport Baja Spec was revealed today at a private event in downtown Los Angeles, California, it’ll make its first public debut next week at the SEMA show held in Las Vegas. The trophy truck will make its competition debut next June at the 2027 SCORE Baja 500, where it will be driven by two-time Baja 1000 winner Bryce Menzies.
