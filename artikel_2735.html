'Monaco unrest as they prepare to face Hearts' - gossip

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Speculation surrounds the future of two top Monaco players as they prepare to face Heart of Midlothian, while the Scottish Premiership's top summer free agents are highlighted.

Hearts' Conference League opponents, Monaco, have been plunged into uncertainty with striker Folarin Balogun saying he wants to leave in January amid links to Manchester United, while midfielder Mamadou Coulibaly is attracting interest from Napoli. (Edinburgh Evening News)

Monaco's 25-year-old American striker, Folarin Balogun, whose deadline-day move to Everton collapsed, is in talks with AC Milan and Juventus over a January deal. (Calciomercato)

Celtic winger James Forrest and right-back Tony Ralston, Rangers defender Dujon Sterling, Motherwell forward Tawanda Maswanhise and left-back Emmanuel Longelo, Heart of Midlothian striker James Wilson, Dundee United wing-back Will Ferry, St Mirren midfielder Killian Phillips, Hibernian wing-back Nicky Cadden, Falkirk full-back Keelan Adams and Kilmarnock striker Tyreece John-Jules are among Scottish Premiership players out of contract next summer who could attract January transfer interest. (The Herald)

Former Celtic centre-half Ramon Vega has revealed he had agreed a transfer switch to Real Madrid only for the deal to be ripped up while on a flight to Spain because manager Fabio Capello was sacked. (Glasgow Times)

Dunfermline Athletic head coach Neil Lennon is happy to entrust Sam Cleall-Harding's rehabilitation to parent club Dundee United as the 20-year-old on-loan defender recovers from a serious hamstring injury. (The Courier)
