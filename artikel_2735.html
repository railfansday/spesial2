Lions vs. Bucs: Live score updates, highlights, injury news

The Lions are rolling on a five-game winning streak, showing they can win in multiple ways. They've toughened up for a couple of defensive battles (19-15 over Vikings, 19-9 over Dolphins), but they can also still put up a lot of points, as they did last week in their 35-10 blowout win over the Patriots in Munich.
