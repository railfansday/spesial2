Joe Mazzulla shifts blame to Celtics, not refs after Nuggets loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Nobody buys a ticket to an NBA game to watch the referees take over in the second half. Nonetheless, the officiating down the stretch was a factor in the Boston Celtics' 105-101 loss to the Denver Nuggets on Wednesday night.

Boston got into foul trouble to start the third and fourth quarters, and Celtics wing Paul George even fouled out for the first time this season with 6:14 remaining in the final frame. Moreover, Denver attempted 22 free throws in the second half while Boston received just 3. One of those attempts for the Nuggets came with only 6 seconds to go in a one-possession game, as Celtics star Jayson Tatum was whistled for a technical foul during a late-game timeout.

Despite the fact that this final free throw gave the Nuggets a four-point lead with a few ticks left (essentially sealing the win for them), Celtics head coach Joe Mazzulla refused to blame the refs during his postgame press conference.

"We just can't put ourselves in that position to even give the refs an opportunity to interpret (it) a certain type of way," Mazzulla said, via NBC Sport Boston. "So, we just got to be better."

After Nuggets star Jamal Murray cashed a clutch jumper to put the Nuggets up 104-101, Tatum appeared frustrated and passed the ball down the court to a ref before entering the Celtics huddle. That must've been enough for referee Eric Dalen, who then gifted Tatum the aforementioned tech for perhaps bouncing the ball to an official with a little too much force.

This is partially speculation, though. Mazzulla said the controversial call wasn't explained to him, so a pool report might be necessary in order to clear things up for everybody.

Technicals and free throw disparity aside, the Celtics contributed to their own demise at Ball Arena. They shot under 40 percent from the field in the fourth quarter and missed multiple open shots. Tatum struggled to break Denver's harassing defense at times and George arguably had his worst performance as a member of the Green Team.

The C's have now lost back-to-back games for the first time this season and will finish 2026 without a single win against the Nuggets, who they haven't defeated since March of 2025. The 11-4 Celtics will look to bounce back from the frustrating loss on Saturday night, when they face the 1-13 Sacramento Kings on the road.
