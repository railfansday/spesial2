Rayuan Lane III named Jaguar’s 2026 Salute to Service Award nominee

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Jacksonville Jaguars have officially named safety Rayuan Lane III as their nominee for the 2026 Salute to Service Award presented by USAA, recognising his outstanding commitment to supporting military personnel, veterans and their families.

Now in its 16th year, the NFL's annual award celebrates individuals across the league who go above and beyond in honouring the United States military community. Lane is a particularly fitting candidate, having combined his emerging NFL career with a commitment to military service of his own.

A 2025 graduate of the United States Naval Academy, Lane was selected by the Jaguars in the sixth round of the 2025 NFL Draft. Alongside his football commitments, he serves as a second lieutenant in the U.S. Marine Corps Reserve.

Since arriving in Jacksonville, Lane has become actively involved in supporting service members, veterans and military families throughout Northeast Florida, participating in several initiatives alongside the Jaguars and veteran-focused organisations.

Presented in partnership with USAA, the Salute to Service Award recognises members of the NFL community who demonstrate an exceptional commitment to supporting the military. Its reach extends beyond players, with coaches, executives, staff and former NFL figures all eligible for recognition.

The award forms part of the league's wider Salute to Service initiative, which aims to honour, empower and connect with active-duty service members, veterans and their families throughout the year.

The selection process begins with one nominee from each of the NFL's 32 teams. Fans can then vote for their preferred candidates, with the public vote helping determine the three finalists. A panel featuring representatives from the military community, the NFL and USAA ultimately selects the winner.

Previous recipients include some familiar names from around the league. San Francisco 49ers running back Christian McCaffrey received the 2025 award for his work supporting veterans through his Christian McCaffrey Foundation and its 23 and Troops initiative.

Other recent winners include 49ers tight end George Kittle (2024), New England Patriots long snapper Joe Cardona (2023), former Washington head coach Ron Rivera (2022) and former Denver Broncos tight end Andrew Beck (2021).

The award also recognises the contributions of people beyond the playing field. Previous winners include former Atlanta Falcons executive Steve Cannon, former NFL linebacker Donnie Edwards and (then) Baltimore Ravens head coach John Harbaugh.

There is also a financial contribution attached to the recognition. USAA will donate $25,000 in the eventual recipient's name to official aid societies representing all branches of the U.S. military, with the NFL Foundation matching that contribution through a further $25,000 donation to the recipient's chosen military charity.

Lane's nomination is particularly meaningful because his connection to the military goes considerably further than supporting it from the sidelines.

His involvement with the Wounded Warrior Project (WWP) has been a significant part of his community work since joining the Jaguars.

He welcomed participants in the organisation's Soldier Ride 250 to the Jaguars' Miller Electric Center for dinner ahead of their cycling journey, providing an opportunity to connect with veterans and those who have experienced the physical and psychological challenges associated with military service.

Lane also visited Wounded Warrior Project headquarters in recognition of the 25th anniversary of September 11, learning more about the organisation's mission and the ongoing work it does to support wounded veterans and service members.

His involvement has also extended to inspiring the next generation of military personnel.

Lane has administered the Oath of Enlistment to future Marines during a Military Entrance Processing Station ceremony and visited Recruiting Sub Station Jacksonville Beach, where he shared his experiences and offered motivational advice to prospective recruits.

He has also participated in physical training activities involving aspiring Marines and local high school students, helping prepare them for the demands of boot camp.

Away from his own military responsibilities, Lane has continued to support the Jaguars' Salute to Service programming, including a movie night recognising active-duty and retired military personnel and their families. It's a commitment that carries particular significance for a player who understands the responsibilities of military service firsthand.

Lane is one of 32 nominees representing their respective NFL franchises, with fans having the opportunity to help determine the award's three finalists.

Voting is open until November 30, 2026, with supporters permitted one vote per day for their preferred nominee.

Jaguars fans can cast their vote for Lane through the official NFL Salute to Service Fan Vote.

The three finalists are scheduled to be announced in January 2027, with the eventual recipient recognised at NFL Honors during Super Bowl week.

For Lane, the nomination represents recognition of a commitment that began long before he arrived in Jacksonville. Balancing the demands of an NFL career with his responsibilities as a Marine Corps Reserve officer is no small undertaking, and his continued involvement with veterans, military families and future service members demonstrates that his dedication to the military community extends well beyond his playing career.
