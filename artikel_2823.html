Jets vs Bills: Garrett Wilson is back; Jelani Woods headlines Jets' inactives

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Garrett Wilson will be suiting up for the New York Jets when they take on the Buffalo Bills today. Wilson was in the concussion protocol and missed his first game of the season last week. Wilson's presence, along with Omar Cooper, Adonai Mitchell, Kenyon Sadiq and Breece Hall, gives the Jets' offense a full set of weapons for one of the few games this season. They'll need every weapon in the arsenal if they want to keep up against the Buffalo Bills' #1 in the NFL offense.

Cornerback Nahshon Wright will also be active today for a change. Wright signed with the Jets in the offseason after earning a Pro Bowl nod last year with the Chicago Bears. He has been active for just three games for the Jets. He has played just 22 snaps, all on special teams. Needless to say this has not worked out as planned for either Wright or the Jets.
