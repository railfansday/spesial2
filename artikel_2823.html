Aston Villa Eye Bayern’s 17-Year-Old Keeper: Is He Really An Option?

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Leonard Prescott looks increasingly likely to leave Bayern Munich, according to Sky Sport Germany, after contract talks with the 17-year-old goalkeeper stalled. Aston Villa, Chelsea, Arsenal and Brighton are all interested in the New York-born German youth international, whose deal expires on 30 June 2027.

The sticking point is the pathway. Bayern see Jonas Urbig as Manuel Neuer's long-term successor, so Prescott has no clear route into the first team. He trained with the senior squad at 15 and came close to a Champions League debut against Atalanta in March during an injury crisis, but he has yet to make a first-team appearance.

His strengths are technical. Bayern's under-19 coach Peter Gaydarov has praised his ability on the ball, and Vincent Kompany reportedly rates him. The weaknesses are experience and fitness. He had knee surgery in July for a meniscus injury and should return around Christmas, so he has no competitive football this season to judge him on.

Chelsea look the most active. Sky reports they have held initial talks and could offer a loan at sister club Strasbourg, which answers his need for regular football.

Villa settled the position in August. Zion Suzuki, 24, arrived from Parma for a fee that can reach £30m on a contract until 2031, replacing Emiliano Martinez. Marco Bizot, 35, is the deputy, and his deal also expires next summer, while 21-year-old academy graduate James Wright is the third choice.

Yes, but only as a loan-first project. Suzuki is signed for five years, so the first-team pathway Prescott wants does not exist at Villa Park, the same complaint he has about Bayern. What Villa can offer is a development route through Annecy, the Ligue 2 club in which owners V Sports hold a minority stake and where academy players already go on loan. Chelsea's Strasbourg play a level higher, so Villa start second.

There is also a rule problem. Premier League clubs cannot sign overseas players under 18, and Prescott turns 18 on 23 September 2027, so any move to Villa Park would have to wait or run through a partner club like Annecy.

The logic is still sound. He costs no transfer fee, and Bizot's contract ends in June, so Villa must rethink their number two anyway. But Prescott should be a long-term bet rather than Bizot's replacement, because Emery needs an experienced deputy on Champions League nights.
