Penn State vs. Minnesota live updates: Score, highlights, analysis

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Penn State (5-4, 2-4 Big Ten) welcomes Minnesota (5-4, 3-3 Big Ten) to Happy Valley for a chilly night game in Beaver Stadium on Saturday evening. The Governor's Victory Bell and bowl eligibility will be awarded to the winner of this Week 11 matchup, and Penn State is looking for its third straight win after a tough stretch to open the Big Ten schedule.

Penn State is coming off a road win at Washington last week that saw the Nittany Lions hold a decisive lead at halftime but go scoreless as they held on for a narrow victory in the second half. Minnesota has lost two straight games after tough battles with highly-ranked Indiana and UCLA in its last two outings. The Gophers also dealt with some travel nightmares just getting to Happy Valley this week after being impacted by the nationwide FAA ground stop on Friday.
