Rams’ 29-Year-Old Undrafted WR Shares Message Following First NFL TD

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

GLENDALE, ARIZONA - NOVEMBER 15: Xavier Smith #14 of the Los Angeles Rams scores a touchdown late during the second quarter of a game against the Arizona Cardinals at State Farm Stadium on November 15, 2026 in Glendale, Arizona. (Photo by Mike Christy/Getty Images) Getty

The Los Angeles Rams brought Xavier Smith back from a division rival five days ago. On Sunday, Nov. 15, he gave them a reason to celebrate.

Smith scored his first NFL touchdown in the Rams' 34-6 victory over the Arizona Cardinals, catching a 22-yard pass from Matthew Stafford late in the second quarter.

The moment mattered for the 29-year-old receiver, who had been released by Los Angeles earlier this season.

"It meant a lot … sometimes you got to get brought down to get put back up," Smith told CBS Los Angeles following the win, as shared by Sports Central LA.

The touchdown came less than a week after the Rams signed Smith off the San Francisco 49ers' practice squad.

Los Angeles signed Smith on Nov. 10, adding the receiver to an offense depleted by injuries.

Smith had spent his first three NFL seasons with the Rams before the team waived him Oct. 2. San Francisco signed him to its practice squad five days later.

His second stint with Los Angeles got off to a memorable start.

With 20 seconds remaining in the first half against Arizona, Stafford found Smith on a screen pass. The receiver followed his blockers, broke a tackle and raced into the end zone.

The score extended Los Angeles' lead to 17-6, completing a dramatic turnaround before halftime.

Smith's touchdown followed an interception by safety Kamren Kinchens, who returned the ball 33 yards to set up the scoring drive.
