More on flexed Week 12 game at Cleveland

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

This week, the NFL flexed the Las Vegas Raiders' game at the Cleveland Browns on November 29 has been flexed into Sunday Night Football. Kickoff is set for 5:20 p.m. PT.

It's a bit surprisisng that a game featuring a 4-6 team against a 6-4 team would be selected to be moved into prime time on the final Sunday of November. But there there were few choices and it's better a Drake Maye-less New England Patriots against the one-win Los Angeles Chargers, which was the original choice. Because there are five games in a Wednesday-Friday window, there were few choices. So, this was a top option.

There's no doubt the fact that No. 1 overall draft pick Fernando Mendoza is now the Raiders' starting quarterback was part of the appeal for NBC for this choice. There will be more more prime-time opportunities in his future.

The Browns are definitely part of the ideal for this flex as well. They are 6-4 and are currently in the playoff mix. The Dawg Pound will be alive in the Browns' first Sunday night affair in five years.

This game was originally slotted for a 10 a.m. West Coast start, The Raiders are 1-1 in that time slot this season, but traditionally has struggled. So, the later start time can't hurt.

It could be cold and rainy. The current forecast calls for low 40s and a chance of rain on the shores of Lake Erie.

Could the Raiders' get awarded a second flex? There are not many chances. The Raiders do host the Denver Broncos in Week 15, but the scheduled prime-time games Sunday and Monday games don't look like they will be moved. However, if the Raiders beat Denver this week, perhaps the Week 15 game could be flexed to Thursday night. But the deadline for that switch will be next Tuesday. There will be three stand-alone games on Sunday of Week 18 when the Raiders play at Kansas City and the Sunday night game is open as well. So, that final game may be the Raiders' best chance for a second stand-alone game.
