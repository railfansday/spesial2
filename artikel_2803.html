Apple Valley’s Brent Lanning, Chase Higgs headline All-MRL football selections

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Apple Valley's return to the top of the Mojave River League football standings came with a sweep of the league's three major individual awards.

Quarterback Brent Lanning was named Offensive Player of the Year, Chase Higgs earned Defensive Player of the Year honors and Lucas Arceo was selected Lineman of the Year when the All-Mojave River League football selections were recently announced.

The trio helped lead Apple Valley to an undefeated league record, snapping Oak Hills' five-year reign as MRL champion.

Lanning put together an impressive five-game league campaign, completing 60 of 88 passes for 791 yards and nine touchdowns with just one interception. He also rushed for 219 yards and four touchdowns, accounting for 13 scores during league play.

The Sun Devils quarterback finished the season completing 136 of 207 passes for 1,621 yards and 14 touchdowns with five interceptions.

Higgs made his presence felt on both sides of the football.

Defensively, he led Apple Valley with 47 tackles in five league games, including five for a loss, two sacks and an interception. He also contributed seven rushing touchdowns on 43 carries during league play.

Higgs finished the season with a team-high 84 tackles, including 11 for a loss, two sacks, an interception and a fumble recovery.

Both Lanning and Higgs earned All-MRL first-team recognition for the second consecutive season.

Apple Valley had seven additional first-team selections, giving the league champions 10 honorees, including Erom Rohrbaugh, who was selected at linebacker and earned first-team recognition for the second consecutive season.

Oak Hills finished with eight first-team selections, highlighted by two players earning the honor for the third consecutive season.

Defensive lineman Sione Felila and defensive back Royalton Allen were both named to the first team for the third straight year.

Both Felila and Allen are both headed to Power Four college football programs. Felila is bound for Oklahoma, and Allen to Wisconsin.

Allen has now received All-MRL recognition in all four years of his high school career after earning second-team honors as a freshman.

Defensive lineman Ritchie Avei, who is committed to Fresno State, earned first-team honors for the second consecutive season, along with teammates Haime Finau and Rudy De La Fuente as repeat selections.

Hesperia had seven first-team honorees after finishing second in the MRL standings. Serrano had five players earn first-team recognition, with Jamal Larry receiving honors on both sides of the football at wide receiver and defensive back.

The Diamondbacks also received the league's sportsmanship award.
