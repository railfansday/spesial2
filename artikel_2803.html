Barcelona see €70 million summer signing as &#8216;the new Raphinha&#8217; after impressive start

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Barcelona are comparing Anthony Gordon to Raphinha because of his work rate and defensive contribution, according to Mundo Deportivo.

The report states that the England international has settled quickly into Hansi Flick's squad and has impressed not only with his numbers, but also with the way he approaches the matches.

On that note, Gordon's pressing, running and willingness to track back are considered valuable in Barcelona's structure, where forwards must contribute without the ball.

The outlet mentions that Gordon, who signed for Barcelona for €70 million past summer, has built strong relationships with the likes of Eric Garcia, Dani Olmo and Pedri, who have helped him adapt to life at the club.

Garcia and Olmo speak English well and have been important points of support, while Pedri has also helped him connect with the group.

Anthony Gordon has impressed everyone at Barcelona. (Picture credit: Barcelona website)

His integration matters because Flick's system depends on coordinated movement and trust.

Moreover, Gordon sees Barcelona as a project where he can compete for titles, establish himself among Europe's elite and strengthen his standing internationally.

Gordon is a skilful and powerful winger who combines attacking ability with a high level of physical effort. He presses opponents, tracks back and continues running throughout matches.

These qualities have drawn comparisons with Raphinha, whose commitment without possession has become a defining part of his game at Barcelona.

Anthony Gordon is called 'a second Raphinha'. (Picture credit: X/@FCBarcelona)

In fact, those within the club call him "a second Raphinha" thanks to his similarities with the Brazilian style, where his defensive efforts help the team maintain its intensity.

Despite his promising start, Gordon recognises that he still has room to develop.

One of his priorities is improving his decision-making, particularly identifying when to take on an opponent and when to choose a pass or another option.

For what it is worth, his experience in English football has largely been influenced by a more direct, box-to-box style, but at Barcelona, he is learning to operate within a possession-based system that requires careful reading of space and movement.
