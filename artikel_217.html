Is it time to change the UK age of consent? – podcast

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

After Russell Brand’s conviction and accusations against David Walliams, campaigners are calling for a staggered age of consent. Nosheen Iqbal speaks to the columnist Polly Hudson and the educator Michael Conroy about what needs to change

Send your comments and questions to tif.questions@theguardian.com

Anger, disgust, shame. In the last couple of weeks, as news of famous older men being convicted and accused of crimes against very young women and girls has emerged, the public mood has shifted.

The conversation has moved from “how did society let this happen?” to “how do we stop it from happening again?”. In that space, a campaign has grown for the consent gap to be closed.

The government is seriously considering the move, which is backed by two women who have made allegations against Russell Brand and David Walliams.

They’re fighting to change UK law to introduce a staggered age of consent under which 16- and 17-year-olds would still legally be able to have sex, but only with people no more than three years older than them.

Nosheen Iqbal speaks to the Guardian columnist Polly Hudson about why the culture and the law has left girls vulnerable to exploitation by older men for so long. And the educator Michael Conroy explains what needs to be done to ensure change is made.
