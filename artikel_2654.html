IPL 2027: RCB retention list, released players, remaining purse and squad details

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Defending champions Royal Challengers Bengaluru (RCB) have retained the bulk of their title-winning squad ahead of the IPL 2027 auction, keeping 20 players and releasing six on the retention deadline day, November 17. Bengaluru have opted for continuity, with only a handful of changes to their squad ahead of the new season.RCB will head into the IPL 2027 auction, scheduled to take place in Goa on December 16, with a remaining purse of Rs 14.15 crore and five slots to fill, including one overseas position.Among the notable players released by the franchise are England batter Jacob Bethell. Bethell, who was bought for Rs 2.6 crore, has been let go after struggling with a knee injury that has kept him out of action since July. The 23-year-old was expected to return during the ongoing white-ball series in Australia but experienced pain and swelling and is now expected to undergo surgery for a cartilage issue.RCB have also released Mangesh Yadav, who was acquired for Rs 5.2 crore, along with Swapnil Singh, Satvik Deswal and Vihaan Malhotra. Richard Gleeson, who joined the franchise as a replacement for Nuwan Thushara towards the end of the previous season, has also been released.The six departures have freed up Rs 9.15 crore. Added to the Rs 50 lakh remaining from the previous auction and an additional Rs 5 crore made available to the franchise, RCB will have Rs 14.15 crore to spend at the upcoming auction.One of the significant decisions by the defending champions has been their decision to retain left-arm pacer Yash Dayal. The bowler, who was retained for Rs 5 crore ahead of the 2025 mega auction, did not feature in RCB's title-winning campaign last season. Director of Cricket Mo Bobat had earlier cited Dayal's 'personal situation' while explaining his absence.Dayal has since returned to competitive cricket, turning out for Uttar Pradesh in the Ranji Trophy in October before featuring in the ongoing Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy.RCB have seven overseas players in their retained squad and will therefore have room for just one more overseas signing at the auction as they look to fine-tune their title-defending combination.

Abhinandan Singh, Kanishk Chouhan, Suyash Sharma, Bhuvneshwar Kumar, Krunal Pandya, Tim David, Devdutt Padikkal, Nuwan Thushara, Venkatesh Iyer, Jacob Duffy, Phil Salt, Vicky Ostwal, Jitesh Sharma, Rajat Patidar, Virat Kohli, Jordan Cox, Rasikh Dar, Yash Dayal, Josh Hazlewood and Romario Shepherd.
