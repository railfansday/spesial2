Where Every ‘Love Is Blind’ Season 11 Couple Stands as Of Right Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Reminder: the show was filmed almost 2 years ago.

A brand-new batch of aspiring reality-star singles gathered in Boston for the annual social experiment known as Love Is Blind, and the final installment of season 11 is officially out on Netflix. This week’s reunion (which was filmed just a few weeks ago) caught up with the couples after multiple breakups and just one wedding—here’s where things stand circa right now!

Despite managing to work through some of their communication issues, Aneha’s parents refused to attend her wedding to Cam—and her sister made it abundantly clear she wasn’t on board either. Ultimately, Aneha turned down Cam at the altar, but confirmed at the reunion that he was also on board with not saying “I do.” In fact, Cam ended up breaking things off completely a few days later during a hotel getaway.

Currently: still split...but kinda vibing?

Jax might have ghosted Stew immediately after their engagement, but they reunited with the cast at a bar and spent the entire time joking around with each other at a table and watching the mess unfold. I swear, they had chemistry. Weird chemistry, but still chemistry! Though, for what it’s worth, Jax insisted nothing was going on during the reunion.

Wilnie and Wilson ended things after their bachelor/bachelorette parties. During a tense couch convo, Wilson called out Wilnie for not...cooking enough (?), and Wilnie pointed out Wilson’s lack of affection. Ultimately, the pair were on the same page about not being right for each other, and rehashed some of their issues during the reunion (which made it abundantly clear they’re not back together, though they are kinda in touch).

Sarah and John said “I do” in front of their friends and families, only to get divorced a year later. The exes opened up about what happened during the reunion, confirming that John went out for a “boys’ night” and started chatting with an escort he met. Sarah found the texts, which were the push she needed to leave the relationship after a rough year of marriage. More details this way.

Michaella broke up with Rob after he went off during a group bar hang, telling everyone he didn’t care about their relationship, pointing out they’d only known each other for a few weeks, and acting generally messy. Michaella told Erika that Rob had left her at home that night, and while he did call to make amends, she ended things. The couple’s reunion was pretty tense, but they don’t seem to have bad blood. In fact, turns out Michaella went on to date Beekeeper Matt, and they had the most awkward exchange of all time about their breakup during the reunion.

Much like Wilnie and Wilson, Erika and Blake had a pre-wedding couch convo and decided to part ways. The pair ended things very amicably, and seem to still genuinely love each other—which was abundantly clear during the reunion. Since spending time apart, both of them moved on with other people, but they appear to be single and still into each other as of now...just saying!
