InventHelp Inventor Develops Power-Generation System for Electric Vehicles (BKC-1015)

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

PITTSBURGH, Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- "I wanted to create a special adapter that would generate electricity while traveling in an electric vehicle," said an inventor, from Canton, Mass., "so I invented THE FIFTH WHEEL. My design enables you to travel longer distances without having to stop and recharge."

The invention provides an auxiliary battery-charging feature for electric vehicles. In doing so, it eliminates the need to stop and recharge the battery at home or at a public charging station. As a result, it increases efficiency. It also helps avoid delays. The invention features an innovative design that is easy to employ so it is ideal for manufacturers of new-production electric cars and light trucks and owners of existing electric vehicles. Additionally, a prototype model and technical drawings are available upon request.

The original design was submitted to the Boston sales office of InventHelp. It is currently available for licensing or sale to manufacturers or marketers. For more information, write Dept. 25-BKC-1015, InventHelp, 100 Beecham Drive, Suite 110, Pittsburgh, PA 15205-9801, or call (412) 288-1300 ext. 1368. Learn more about InventHelp's Invention Submission Services at http://www.InventHelp.com.
