Elo Unveils New Elo Pay M61 Mobile POS Computer and Enhanced Elo Pay 22" Tap to Pay Solution

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Expanded Android platforms, integrated contactless payments and Zebra integrations streamline transactions across mobile POS and self-service environments.

KNOXVILLE, Tenn., Nov. 5, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Elo, now part of Zebra Technologies, today announced a dual expansion of its enterprise payment portfolio: the launch of the next-generation Elo Pay M61 Mobile POS Computer and a significant capability update to the Elo Pay 22" Tap to Pay self-service solution. Together, these offerings bring greater flexibility and secure payment capabilities, and seamless Zebra integrations into retail, hospitality, entertainment, and other commercial environments.

"Businesses need more than standalone hardware; they require a flexible, unified platform that developers can easily integrate, and IT teams can deploy and manage at scale," said Dan Ludwick, chief product officer, Elo. "The new Elo Pay M61 and updated Elo Pay 22" bring customer interaction and payment together, helping businesses deliver seamless, consistent experiences across mobile, fixed countertop, and self-service environments while simplifying lifecycle management."

Next-Generation Elo Pay M61 Mobile POS Computer

Building on the successful Elo Pay M60, the new Elo Pay M61 Mobile POS Computer is a handheld payment solution designed to simplify application development and support enterprise commerce wherever transactions take place.

Updated Elo Pay 22" Tap to Pay Self-Service Solution

To complement handheld mobility, Elo has updated its Elo Pay 22" Tap to Pay solution, combining a Full HD interactive touchscreen with built-in contactless payments to streamline self-service deployments.
