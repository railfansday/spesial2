Who Is Grace Brassel? What to Know About Shane Gillis' Ex-Girlfriend

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Grace Brassel made headlines when she spoke out about her relationship with ex Shane Gillis.

The pair originally sparked romance rumors in July 2024 when they attended Christian McCaffrey and Olivia Culpo‘s wedding together. Brassel and Gillis later confirmed their relationship and were together for more than a year before calling it quits in late 2025.

At the time, Brassel fueled speculation that Gillis had cheated on her after she posted a TikTok video with Lily Allen‘s song “Madeline,” which is about infidelity. In response, one fan wrote, “If THAT man did something to YOU I’m gonna freak out,” to which Brassel replied, “😁.”

Gillis later came under fire after he called women who go to the gym in shorts “whores” during his Saturday Night Live monologue in October. He addressed the controversy one month later, shortly before Brassel penned a revealing essay for The Cut about their relationship.

“I felt plucked out of my ordinary existence like some kind of chosen one, dropped straight into the orbit of the coolest guy with the flashiest life. It felt like a dream,” she wrote. “I was so excited for my and Shane’s life together that I didn’t even notice my agency was slipping through my fingers.”

According to her LinkedIn, Brassel is “a lifestyle, beauty, fashion and UGC creator” who specializes in “authentic storytelling, relatable short-form content, and social-first brand partnerships that feel native to the feed.” She listed comedy skits, street interviews and more as some of her areas of expertise.

In January 2023, Brassel appeared on the Dr. Phil show to speak about the harms of vaping after being hospitalized one year prior for a collapsed lung.

“This experience has completely changed how I view the world, pain, and nurses — literal [angels] from heaven,” Brassel said in a subsequent TikTok video. “This is the craziest thing I’ve ever experienced.”

Brassel messaged Gillis via social media in 2024 after watching him on Netflix’s Tires. She flew to meet him in Texas before the pair, who had a 12-year age gap, started dating.

“Shane was rich, he had a cool car, and his house was lined floor to ceiling with awards. He thought I was beautiful and treated me with care,” Brassel wrote in her November 2026 essay. “He said all the right things. … His world was so fast and extravagant. I was just happy to be part of it.”

Brassel agreed to move in together after two months of dating before their relationship dynamic took a turn.

In her essay, Brassel recalled noticing — and ignoring — red flags in her relationship with Gillis. She found herself dressing more conservatively and changing her behavior due to Gillis’ alleged comments about other women. Brassel also said she felt isolated after moving around with Gillis, who was often not around due to his work.

Their relationship came to an end when Brassel allegedly found explicit texts between Gillis and other women.

Gillis addressed the claims in a statement to Us, saying, “Grace and I had some good times together. Breakups are tough, and I wish her the best.”

Brassel wrote in her November 2026 essay, “Until last August, I was in a year-long relationship with Shane, and his comments didn’t faze me.”

She continued: “After our time together, how do I think he feels about women? He doesn’t like them much.”
