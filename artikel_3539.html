Anthony Mackie, 48, Says Dating Is Difficult: 'Women Don't Like Older Men'

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Marvel star Anthony Mackie believes dating is “hard” because “women don’t like older men.”

The Captain America actor, 48, was quizzed on whether he had “any plans to get married again” during an appearance on the Monday, November 16, episode of Watch What Happens Live With Andy Cohen, and he gave a surprising response.

“You know, I’m an older man and I feel like being an older man, women don’t like older men,” Mackie said.

Both host Andy Cohen and fellow guest Jamie Dornan — who costars with Mackie in Apple TV heist drama 12 12 12 — strongly disagreed with the actor’s position.

“What? That’s absolutely not true!” Cohen, 58, told his guest.

Dornan, 44, added, “Older men with Marvel money?”

Cohen weighed in, agreeing with Dornan, “Yeah, right! Excuse me… older, jacked men who look like him?”

Despite Cohen and Dornan both attempting to boost Mackie’s confidence, the Avengers: Doomsday actor did not budge.

“It’s hard as an older man. Women don’t like older men. They like younger, fruitful men,” he said.

Mackie was previously married to childhood sweetheart Sheletta Chapital from 2014 to 2018. The former couple share four sons.

In February 2025, Mackie opened up about his love life in an interview with People, admitting that he found dating “hard.”

“How do you trust someone? How do you put yourself in a position to be vulnerable?” he said. “How do you know what someone really [wants] from you when all you want is to just be loved and appreciated? What does that look like?”

At the time, Mackie said he wasn’t on dating apps like Tinder or Raya. “I’m older and I don’t do the apps,” he shared.

Mackie has largely remained private about his personal life over the years, though he has revealed that his four sons aren’t too impressed by having a Marvel superhero for a dad.

“They’re pretty much really not into me as an actor at all,” he told People in 2021. “It’s shockingly funny how much they couldn’t care less about me being in movies.”

During a 2024 roundtable interview, he shared, “I have four boys. I tell my boys, I’m like, ‘Yo, I’m the coolest mother. … I’m the dad everybody wants.’ And my sons don’t watch Marvel movies. They have no idea. I’ll FaceTime Sebastian [Stan]. I’m like, ‘Yo, man. Say what’s up to my son.’ And my son’s like, ‘Hey?’ They don’t [care]. To them, I’m Dad.”
