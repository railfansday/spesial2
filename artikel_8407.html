Tanggal: 2026-11-03

How big of a risk is it to have a non ACA compliant plan that uses the United Healthcare Choice Plus network regarding emergency care. I currently have a Cigna $5k/$10k HDHP/HSA plan with a $35k OON OOP through work and was looking into a non compliant HDHP/HSA plan…the family is healthy and on no meds with no preexisting conditions so medical underwriting would be no big deal. The network and coverages look great. However, it has an unlimited OON OOP and covers 60% out of network costs but no “No Surprises Act” protections . My biggest concern would be getting hauled off to an ER either out of network or being billed by OON providers at the in-network ER/OR (if I needed emergency surgery). I do have a decent chunk currently in HSA. ($100k). How big of a risk is this really? How much could realistically be racked up before I could be taken to an in network hospital? How much in OON charges could realistically be racked up at an in-network hospital by an OON provider regarding emergent care? Once again, my main concern is insuring against catastrophic emergencies/diagnosis. Obviously a diagnosis would be much more predictable to manage through an in-network provider. The non ACA compliant plan is about $750/mo vs about $1050 for the plan through work. The ACA bronze plan when I checked a few years ago was $12-1500 and basically no one was in network close by …laughably horrible and expensive.
$100k is a rounding error with emergency care.

Back in 2005, when I went into the ER with appendicitis, the ER was $75/minute.

The total bill, at the negotiated insurance rates, which included being admitted for surgery the day was about $17,000. I walked into the ER and out of the hospital in about 18 hours.
