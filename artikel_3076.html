Lifeway Foods (LWAY) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Demand outpaced production while milk costs squeezed margins.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Lifeway Foods, Inc. (LWAY -7.20%) reported record quarterly net sales and continued a multiyear growth streak while navigating capacity constraints and elevated input costs. Management stated that consumer demand for probiotic-rich foods, including kefir and high-protein cheeses, reached levels that exceeded current production capabilities. To address these limitations, the company is executing a facility expansion in Wisconsin designed to increase manufacturing capacity by the first quarter of 2027. Management indicated that rolling price increases and operational efficiencies from modernized automation are expected to drive margin expansion in the coming fiscal year.
