SerpApi Joins The Internet Infrastructure Coalition & Earns Ethical Web Data Collection Initiative Trust Seal

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

AUSTIN, Texas, Nov. 5, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- SerpApi has joined the Internet Infrastructure Coalition (i2Coalition) and earned the Ethical Web Data Collection Initiative (EWDCI) Trust Seal.

i2Coalition brings together companies that build and operate internet infrastructure to support policies that protect an open, secure, and resilient internet. SerpApi and its leadership team share that commitment.

SerpApi has earned the Ethical Web Data Collection Initiative (EWDCI) Trust Seal.

Web data collection has become critical infrastructure for LLMs, AI agents, research and innovation, giving them real-time, structured access to publicly available information. The industry is still relatively young, and EWDCI brings together companies in the field to set ethical standards for how that work is done.

"Transparency is a core value at SerpApi," said Julien Khaleghy, founder and CEO of SerpApi. "We believe a more open world is a better one and that transparency leads to integrity. That's why we're especially excited to be part of i2Coalition and earn the Ethical Web Data Collection Initiative Trust Seal. We want to help champion ethical guidelines and build public trust among data collectors, because this work is critical infrastructure for technology and innovation."

"Web data collection supports research, competition, and innovation, but that work comes with responsibilities," said Christian Dawson, Executive Director of the i2Coalition. "The EWDCI Trust Seal gives companies a way to demonstrate their commitment to responsible practices. We're glad to welcome SerpApi and look forward to working with their team to strengthen those practices and help policymakers understand this industry."

Through i2Coalition and EWDCI, SerpApi will contribute to industry discussions around responsible web data collection and help inform policymakers as questions about access to public web data become increasingly important to AI, research and digital innovation.

To learn more, visit https://policy.i2coalition.com/i2coalition-member-spotlight-serpapi/

About SerpApi SerpApi is the world's leading provider of search data, pioneering APIs that turn search engine results into structured, developer-ready information. Founded in 2017, the company enables developers, researchers, Fortune 500 companies, and organizations around the world to integrate live search insights from sources like Google, Bing, and YouTube into applications, analytics, and research workflows. With more than 100 specialized APIs, SerpApi supports use cases ranging from AI development and machine learning to SEO monitoring, cybersecurity intelligence, and e-commerce analysis, helping teams build reliable, data-driven products at scale. Learn more at https://www.serpapi.com

The Internet Infrastructure Coalition (i2Coalition) brings together companies that build and operate Internet infrastructure to support policies that protect an open, secure, and resilient Internet. Its Internet at Risk series provides educational resources on technologies and infrastructure at the center of public policy debates. Learn more at https://i2coalition.com/.
