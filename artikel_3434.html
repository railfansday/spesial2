‘The Pitt’ Season 3 Status Officially Addressed by Noah Wyle

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Whether it's fantasy masterpieces like Game of Thrones, sci-fi epics like Dune: Prophecy, or even high-class dramas like Succession, HBO has already found a winning formula when it comes to TV shows. Through its streaming service, HBO Max, the studio continues to release some of the most-watched shows on TV year after year across various genres, and the empire is only growing as time ticks by. Last year in January, HBO took one of its biggest risks in platform history with the launch of The Pitt, the medical drama starring Noah Wyle, who spent years recurring as Dr. John Carter on ER. It didn't take long for it to become clear that HBO's gamble has paid off, and with billions of minutes watched through its first two seasons, The Pitt is officially one of the most popular shows in the world.

After the success of The Pitt Season 1, HBO renewed the show for Season 2, but the studio didn't wait nearly as long to double down on the future of The Pitt beyond its second season. HBO announced mere days before The Pitt Season 2 premiere that the show had already been picked up for Season 3, and cameras on the third season began rolling earlier in the year, assuring that it would once again come back on a swift timeline. Collider's Taylor Gates recently attended a FYC (For Your Consideration) event with the cast of The Pitt, and when asked how things were going in production for Season 3, Noah Wyle shared an in-depth update, revealing how many episodes are fully cut and even which episode the team is filming right now:

"We're shooting Episode 12 currently. I think I'm the only one working tomorrow... It's going really, really well. We've got nine episodes cut and locked, and we're really, really proud of this season. It's not easy to come back after a first season and deliver a strong second [season]. It's especially hard in the third year because you really have picked all the low-hanging fruit, and you have to dig a little bit deeper to find who your characters are, even deeper than you've explored them before, and everyone was up for the challenge this year."

The cast of The Pitt was also asked to give one word to describe their characters in Season 3 during the FYC event that took place. Gerran Howell, who plays Dr. Dennis Whitaker, said "faith" is the apt word to describe Whitaker's journey in Season 3, while Dr. Trinity Santos actress Isa Briones used two words, describing Santos' journey as "cracked open" in Season 3. Noah Wyle followed behind, saying that Dr. Robby is "climbing" in Season 3, while Dr. Mel King actress Taylor Dearden says that her character will be "processing."

As for Sepideh Moafi, who was a newcomer in Season 2 as Dr. Baran Al-Hashimi, she said that her Season 3 journey will be "porous," and Dr. Langdon star Patrick Ball teased a "tumbling" journey for Dr. Langdon in Season 3. Katherine LaNasa, who plays the glue in the Pittsburgh Trauma ER, Dana Evans, said that she will be "teetering" in Season 3, while Fiona Dourif says Dr. Cassie McKay will be "outraged" in the third season. Creator R. Scott Gemill jokingly teased that he's "tired" while working on Season 3.

Check out the first two seasons of The Pitt on HBO Max and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage of Season 3.
