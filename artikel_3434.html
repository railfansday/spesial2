‘Epic Zombie Christmas,’ Family Comedy About Senior Citizens and Teenagers Making a Horror Film, Begins Production

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Loam Entertainment has started production this month on “Epic Zombie Christmas,” the follow-up to their summer camp film, “Bad Counselors.” Descried as a “heartfelt comedy,” the family film will focus on an unexpected intergenerational connection between teen filmmakers and the eccentric senior citizen stars of their Christmas zombie movie. Despite the generational delta, the group bonds over their emotional, creative journey that eventually bonds them.

Nathan Frankowski (“Te Ata”) directs from a script written by Frankowski and Dexter Masland (“Bad Counselors”). The film’s teen stars include Alex J. Montero (“Law & Order: SVU”), Carson Bolde (“Godzilla”), Chandni Shah (“Jailed Bird”), Indi Star (“Charmers”), and Jacob Buster (“Ballers”). They join a cast of veteran actors that includes Tom Kemp (“Manchester by the Sea”), Bobbie Elzey (“First Lady”), Megan McFarland (“Walden”), Lauren Halperin (“Stranger Things”), and Erik Estrada (“CHiPs”). Filming at Trilith Studios, the production will also collaborate with Trilith Institute, a film and media education and workforce development nonprofit, to provide opportunities for students and alumni to gain experience on a working film set. Alumni of Trilith Institute’s professional education and Semester at Trilith programs will have opportunities to work in production roles and shadow filmmakers and key crew.

“Our films aim to encourage audiences to live for something greater, and we look to craft those stories by the next generation of great American storytellers. The Institute is developing those filmmakers, and we have an opportunity to amplify their mission while also benefiting from it,” stated Tom Evans, Chairman of Loam.

“Working on a feature film gives students the opportunity to connect what they’re learning in the classroom with the collaboration and problem-solving that happen on set every day,” stated Jeffrey Stepakoff, Co-Founder and CEO of Trilith Institute. “We’re pleased to work with Loam to create those opportunities for our students and help our alumni take the next steps in their careers.”

Loam Entertainment will fully finance EZC and produce in partnership with Wild Chapel and Trilith Institute. Kris Galuska and Rodney Eldridge, who worked closely together on “Bad Counselors,” will produce with Chris Fenton (“Blockers,” “Chappaquiddick”). Loam’s Evans will executive produce along with Robert Crotty.
