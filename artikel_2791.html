Peyton Manning gets revenge on Eli Manning by gifting Richard Sherman interception painting on ManningCast

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Over the last few weeks, it has become obvious that Peyton Manning's younger brother Eli likes gifting paintings, and the tables were turned when the elder Manning gifted former NFL cornerback Richard Sherman one of his own on the ManningCast.

Sherman was the fourth-quarter guest on the ManningCast during the Week 8 game between the Chicago Bears and the Seattle Seahawks. With Thanksgiving and Christmas coming up, Peyton Manning ESPNNFL/status/2117469431957692867?a=KkX-bNEDoWx_Map70Jc&s=20" rel="noopener">was feeling extra giving, gifting Sherman a painting of one of his two interceptions—which he called a "Picasso"— off of his younger brother from the Seahawks' 2013 game against the New York Giants.

This was at the height of the Legion of Boom. They shut out the Manning-led offense and picked him off a career-high five times. In the painting, there's a speech bubble over Manning's intended receiver Hakeem Nicks' head that reads, "No! Not you!"

Of course, Peyton Manning was having a ton of fun at his younger brother's expense. He even joked that Sherman would put it "right on the mantle" in his office. Sherman promised that "it will make the wall."

Sherman intercepted Eli Manning twice in his career, both in the same game. That was part of the four-time Pro Bowl quarterback's worst season ever, during which he threw 18 touchdowns against 27 interceptions.

While he was on the Seahawks, Sherman also faced Peyton Manning a couple of times, most notably in Super Bowl 48. The Seahawks destroyed the Broncos 43-8. However, in all of their encounters, he never intercepted the elder Manning brother.

During his career, Sherman played for the Seahawks, the San Francisco 49ers, and the Tampa Bay Buccaneers. He had 37 interceptions and 116 passes defended in his legendary career. He was a five-time Pro Bowler and five-time All-Pro (three First-team and two Second-team).
