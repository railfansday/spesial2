Did Wemby's attempts to 'show off' for Selena Gomez fuel his 50-point game?

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

If it weren't for the team's previous 12-game winning streak, people might say a certain good luck charm pushed the San Antonio Spurs to victory Saturday night.

Cheering from a courtside seat was Texas native and longtime Spurs fan Selena Gomez.

Gomez has said she and her family used to make the pilgrimage from her home in the Dallas suburb Grand Prairie to San Antonio to root for their chosen team. She returned this weekend, repping a silver-and-black T-shirt under her black blazer at the game where the Spurs soundly defeated the New Orleans Pelicans 114-91.

The presence of the actress and singer-songwriter wasn't lost on the players. When asked whether he flashed a grin toward a certain someone after hitting a corner 3-pointer, Spurs forward Victor Wembanyama responded immediately: "Of course."

"I had to show off for Selena, you know," said the NBA All-Star, who scored 50 points in Saturday's game.

The Spurs recognized Gomez in a post of the star's Jumbotron appearance. "Welcome back to the 210," the caption said.

Saturday's Spurs game wasn't the first that Gomez, named after Tejano star Selena, has attended since becoming a Hollywood star.

The NBA posted a clip of young Gomez dancing in her seat during a 2016 game, with a video from the Frost Bank Center on Saturday juxtaposed above it. The younger Gomez laughed and covered her face with her hands, while the current Gomez smiled and accepted a custom jersey reading "RARE" on the back and emblazoned with No. 1.

A 2016 image of Gomez showing off her Spurs jersey has been so widely shared over the years that it's become a GIF used by Spurs fans with something to boast. And the NBA has used the GIF in its promotion of NBA Jersey Day.

It appears that Gomez took in some of the Alamo City over the weekend. She was spotted Sunday morning at Blanco Cafe, where she posed for a photo that the iconic local eatery shared on social media.

Gomez, 34, launched the cosmetics company Rare Beauty in 2020. She recently embarked on an advertising campaign dubbed "Still Making Each Other Blush" with her childhood friend and pop star Demi Lovato to promote her new blush collection.

Gomez moved from Texas to Los Angeles around age 13 to pursue acting. She has since starred in "Wizards of Waverly Place," "Selena + Chef" and "Only Murders in the Building," and she has won numerous awards for her acting, music and makeup company.
