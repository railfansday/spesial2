The Bears' offense has been one of the NFL's best behind Ben Johnson and Caleb Williams this season, but after making a huge swing for defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons, Chicago's defense looked just as impressive in Week 8.

Heading into Week 9, the Bears are coming off a win over the defending Super Bowl champions — and they'll get another prime-time matchup, aiming to continue proving why they're title contenders on Sunday night.

Here's what's more to know about this week's "Sunday Night Football" matchup.

The Bears will host the Buccaneers in this week's "Sunday Night Football" matchup, with Chicago hosting Baker Mayfield and company at Soldier Field.

The "Sunday Night Football" game between the Bears and Buccaneers will kick off from Soldier Field in Chicago, Illinois, at 8:20 p.m. ET.

As always, NBC will have the broadcast for "Sunday Night Football," with Mike Tirico and Cris Collinsworth on the call. Alternate streaming options include DIRECTV and Fubo, which offer free trials, along with Peacock.
