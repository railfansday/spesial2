Jeffery Simmons, Luther Burden, Kyle Monangai have no injury designations for Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Bears' final injury report ahead of Sunday's game against the Saints brought good news on both sides of the ball.

Defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons is on track to play after being limited in practice with a knee issue. Simmons was up to full participation on Friday and has no injury designation for the game.

That's also the case for wide receiver Luther Burden (concussion) and running back Kyle Monangai (toe). Both players were full practice participants all week and Sunday's game will mark Monangai's first appearance since he ran for 146 yards in a Week 4 win over the Jets.

Wide receiver Zavion Thomas (ankle) missed practice on Friday and is listed as questionable. Cornerback Kyler Gordon (shoulder) has been ruled out.
