Nebraska volleyball vs Purdue live updates, score and highlights

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Nebraska Cornhuskers (29-0, 17-0) will face the Purdue Boilermakers (20-8, 11-6) in the semifinal round of the Big Ten Volleyball Tournament on Tuesday. The Huskers swept the Indiana Hoosiers in the quarterfinal round while the Boilermakers swept the Oregon Ducks to advance to Tuesday's showdown.

Nebraska is the tournament's No. 1 seed and clinched the outright Big Ten regular-season title with a sweep against Illinois. It was the program's 38th conference title and seventh Big Ten crown.

The Huskers ranked No. 1 in the latest AVCA Coaches Poll, released Nov. 16. It marks Nebraska's 136th week at No. 1 in the AVCA Coaches Poll, the most of any program in the nation.

This is the 36th all-time meeting between the two schools, with Nebraska leading the series 27-8. The Huskers have won eight consecutive matches against the Boilermakers.

These two played on October 17, with Nebraska defeating Purdue (25-13, 20-25, 25-19, 25-14) in four sets in Lincoln.
