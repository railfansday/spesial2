Packers, Rams embark on shortest possible bye weeks

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

In Week 11, six teams will have a bye. Four will have a full, two-week break. Two will have the shortest possible bye weeks.

It’s the latest byproduct of the effort to find more places in the pizza into which cheese can be crammed (and/or from which cash can be siphoned).

The NFL will play its first-ever game on the night before Thanksgiving. The easiest, and more equitable, approach to scheduling entailed giving the Week 12 Wednesday assignment to teams that don’t play in Week 11.

Still, it’s not a real bye week. The Rams and Packers will play 10 days after their pre-bye games. The usual in-season break is 14 days.

It’s possible that a team could play on Monday night and the next Thursday, 10 days later. We’re not aware of that happening since the NFL embraced full-season Thursday night games; usually, the league tries to have the same amount of down time for both teams playing on a Thursday night.

Actually, the Packers will get more time off on the back end than the front end of their off week. Green Bay’s Week 13 game comes 11 days after the Week 12 game.

The Rams won’t be so lucky. They play the Chiefs on the following Thursday night, resulting in 10 days, 8 days, and 10 days between L.A. games.

But that’s how it will go in the new and improved NFL. More windows. More prime-time games. More international games.

All with the objective of the ultimate more.
