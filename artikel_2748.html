Hertha extend lead, Magdeburg move up to third, Heidenheim in crisis – 5 things we learned from 2. Bundesliga Matchday 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Hertha BSC continued their outstanding start to the season, while Heidenheim are now without a win in seven 2. Bundesliga encounters.

Hertha BSC recorded their biggest win of the season on Saturday as a 4-0 victory in Cottbus extended the gap with second-placed Nürnberg to ten points. After the first 12 matches, Stefan Leitl's men only dropped points in mid-October's goalless draw at bottom-placed Holstein Kiel.

The hosts played the majority of the second-half with ten players, but they had chances to reduce the two-goal deficit before conceding two more late goals. Nürnberg lost at home to Eintracht Braunschweig at the same time, which further cemented Hertha's surprising lead at the summit of 2. Bundesliga.

Kiel stay bottom after Kniat's difficult debut in Wolfsburg

Michel Kniat held promotion hopefuls Wolfsburg goalless for 50 minutes on his Kiel debut, but the hosts secured a flattering 3-0 victory by the end of the game to climb to fourth in the standings. The result left the DFB-Pokal quarter-finalists four points off of the relegation playoff spot.

Meanwhile, there is only a two-point gap between Wolfsburg and an automatic promotion place. On his first start back from a serious injury, Fraser Hornby finally scored his maiden goal for the side from Lower Saxony.

Fabian Reese had a difficult evening back at his former stomping ground Olympiastadion before the international break, but he was back amongst the goals on Saturday to reach 12 G/A in as many games.

Magdeburg up to third place after hard-fought win in Bielefeld

Third in our list, Magdeburg climbed to third in the 2. Bundesliga for the first time this season. The overachievers maintained their unlikely promotion bid with a 2-1 win at Arminia Bielefeld on Friday.

It was the first time Magdeburg won back-to-back games since matchday 3 as FC Bayern loanee Felipe Chávez scored his first professional goal. Emmanuel Iyoha added the second before the half-time break. The versatile wide player already tied his combined tally from five years at Fortuna Düesseldorf with his first 12 games for Magdeburg.

Sandro Wagner had a difficult start at Hannover, but they've now recorded back-to-back home wins. On matchday 12, the underachievers came from behind to see off relegation-threatened Greuther Fürth.

Waniss Taïbi's first goal of the season won it for the hosts in injury-time after the captain Stefán Teitur Thórdarson brought them back in the game in the second-half. The visitors missed former Hannover winger Felix Klaus as the league's joint top-scorer was unavailable for the first time this season.

Heidenheim are the only team to take points from Hannover from the last three matchdays, but their recent form left plenty to ponder. Frank Schmidt already confirmed his long-term tenure will come to an end at the end of the season as last season's Bundesliga club only picked four points from their last seven games.
