Adeyemi, Gavi react after Sabah 1-4 Barcelona: &#8216;We want to win every match&#8217;

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Karim Adeyemi continued his impressive UEFA Champions League form as Barcelona defeated Sabah 4-1 in Baku, while Gavi used the opportunity to underline his importance to Hansi Flick's squad despite limited playing time this season.

Adeyemi replaced the absent Lamine Yamal and delivered another influential performance, scoring after a superb assist from Alejandro Balde.

The German forward has now scored in three of Barcelona's five Champions League matches, strengthening his case as an important attacking option during the club's demanding schedule.

Adeyemi's goal was a positive contribution after Flick trusted him to cover for Yamal, and after the match, the forward said he was pleased to help Barcelona secure the three points.

"The goal means a lot to me; I'm pleased for the team that I was able to help secure the win, and we can be happy with the three points," he began.

The German also recognised the challenge of playing away from home and praised Anthony Gordon, who scored twice against Sabah.

"It's always difficult to play away from home; they did a great job and were very motivated. Gordon? He had a very good game; I'm happy for the team and for him, and I hope he keeps scoring more goals."

Karim Adeyemi scored from Alejandro Balde's assist. (Picture credit: X/@FCBarcelona)

Adeyemi then insisted Barcelona have enough quality to cope without their teenage star.

"He's an important player and we have the quality to replace him. At Barça, there must always be replacements, regardless of the position.

"We mustn't rely on anyone or be afraid of anyone. We're here to win and we want to win every match."

Gavi made his third start of the season against Sabah and played 86 minutes, offering Flick another opportunity to assess the midfielder's readiness for a greater role.

The Spain international acknowledged his importance within the squad while recognising that every player must understand their responsibilities.

"Every player is important and everyone needs to be clear about their role. I consider myself important even though I'm not getting as many minutes at the moment, and I must always contribute to the team," Gavi emphasised.

The midfielder also reflected on the difficult experiences that have shaped his mentality, including serious injuries and his early days at La Masia.

Gavi made his third Barcelona start of the season. (Picture credit: X/@FCBarcelona)

"It might sound a bit boastful coming from me, but I think my mentality is unique.

"Despite the serious injuries I've suffered and the difficult years I've had in the past – such as when I was on my own at La Masia, which wasn't easy either – I've always pulled through on my own, by my own efforts."

Gavi made clear that competition for places is part of representing Barcelona and that he has never considered himself bigger than the team.

"Ultimately, there's competition. That's what it means to be at the best club. Everyone's good and the manager gives everyone their chance.

"I've never been a star; I'm a team player and always have been, and I'll be there for the team whenever it needs me."

Lastly, he refused to use Barcelona's difficult journey, the cold conditions or the pitch as excuses for the performance.

"It wasn't easy because of the journey we'd made, the cold weather, and the pitch was a bit dry… but you have to get used to that sort of thing. It's never an excuse," he concluded.
