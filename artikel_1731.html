Manchester United want Nathan Collins

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

According to talkSPORT, Manchester United have identified Brentford’s Nathan Collins as a January transfer target as the club looks to strengthen its defence following a difficult start to the season.

United’s plans for a defensive rebuild were initially expected to begin next summer, but ongoing problems at the back are forcing the club to act sooner. As a result, Collins has emerged as one of the key names being considered ahead of the winter transfer window.

New United boss Mauricio Pochettino wants to add both a centre-back and a striker during the winter transfer window. The club’s recruitment staff are reportedly focusing on experienced Premier League players with leadership qualities, and Collins is viewed as fitting that profile.

However, any move for the 25-year-old Ireland international is expected to be complicated by the fact that the defender remains under contract with Brentford until 2029, which obviously puts the Bees in a strong negotiating position.

Calvin Bassey and Jarrad Branthwaite have also been linked with United, but Bassey is expected to sign a new contract with Fulham, while Everton’s Branthwaite is likely to prove too expensive.
