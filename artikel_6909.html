Wonderskin vs. Sacheu: Which Viral Lip Stain Is Better?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

After testing both for a full month, I have thoughts.

We may earn commission from links on this page, but we only recommend products we love. Promise.

Hot take: Lip stains are the most important lippies to have in your makeup routine—especially if you hate reapplying and want the color to stay all day. There is no shortage of lip stain options, but two in particular have been duking it out on TikTok. Both the Sacheu Lip Liner Stay-n and the Wonderskin Lip Stain have gone super viral. If you’ve been wondering if one is better than the other, you’re not alone. This is why, in the name of beauty journalism, I decided to test them both to give you all the deets and my honest reviews.

For those of you who are unfamiliar, these two lip stains stay put for literal HOURS. They're not as opaque as lipstick—the color is more sheer than saturated. Plus, there are multiple ways to use each formula: They can be worn as long-lasting lip liners (because, let’s be real, I have yet to find a pencil liner that truly lasts all day) or as an all-over tint under a shimmery lip gloss or lip balm.

The real question is, which one deserves a spot in your makeup bag? Keep reading to find out. The answer may surprise you!

Wonderskin Wonder Blading All-Day Lip Stain

Confession: I rolled my eyes when I first saw this deep purple formula on TikTok, but once I tried it, I was hooked. This lip stain applies just like a typical lip product—except for its unique color. Don’t worry, a stunning flush is revealed after letting it sit for one minute. Once set, you simply wipe it off with a damp cloth or tissue to reveal a gorgeous lip color.

This pairs seamlessly with any lip oil, gloss, or balm, so you can layer on your favorite hydrating formula without worrying about the color fading away. The only downside? You need a wet tissue or cloth to remove the purple layer, so be sure to apply this at the start of your makeup routine to avoid accidentally wiping away other products.

I really love this stain when I know I have an event or long meal ahead of me (filled with multiple drinks, greasy food, lip gloss applications, and maybe some smooches). Somehow, it’s always still perfectly intact by the end of my night. And of course, we have some more thoughts from Cosmo’s beauty editor, Beth Gillette:

"Wonderskin is definitely the better lip stain if you want to cover your entire lip. It's also available in a gazillion shades—32 to be exact!—which is def helpful if you want a wide range, like a red lip that won't budge. I definitely prefer Sacheu’s peel-off format; however, wiping this lip stain off definitely helps me avoid any mistakes I might make when applying."—BG

Do you know how much your favorite singer sweats on stage? It’s a miracle any makeup stays put under those intense lights. I knew this lip stain was the real deal with Billie Eilish shared that she uses it. In her now mega-viral GRWM video, she over lines her lips for a plump, defined pout that still looks pristine by the end of her concert.

The formula of this lip stain goes on as a deeper version of your chosen shade. I recommend applying it at the start of your routine because it needs to dry down for about 10 to 15 minutes for the magic to work. Once set, you simply peel it off in one satisfying go to reveal a wash of long-lasting color.

I applied the stain every morning for weeks and at the end of every day—after three meals and countless snacks—the color looked just as good as it did 12 hours prior. There was zero smudging, smearing, or feathering either. Need more convincing? Check out what contributing beauty editor Mary Honkus has to say:

“Not gonna lie, I was skeptical about every aspect of this lip stain. The peel-off application was totally foreign, and I’ve never ever experienced a lip product that lasts all day. It took a few tries to really understand the magic behind this. I grew to love the fun (and satisfying) peeling step that reveals the prettiest stain. It truly lasts all day, too—through workouts and multiple meals—without fading or feathering. I will say this works much better as a liner than an all-over lip color. I don’t get an even peel when I applied it all over my lips. As a lip liner, though, I can’t live without it.”—MH

Both the Sacheu and Wonderskin lip stains are truly amazing products, fully worthy of their viral hype. The Sacheu applicator offers more precision and is an excellent value at just $14, while Wonderskin wins when it comes to shade range. The only setback to both? Removal. Sacheu’s stain needs a full 15 minutes before you can peel it off, while Wonderskin's requires a wet tissue to wipe away—so you have to pick your struggle! That said, both deliver impressive, all-day wear, so the best choice comes down to your personal preference and budget.
