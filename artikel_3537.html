Kaitlyn Bristowe Slid Into Sam Hartman's DMs Before He Became 'The Bachelor'

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Kaitlyn Bristowe has a confession: she once slid into NFL star Sam Hartman’s DMs.

“Before he was announced as The Bachelor,” Bristowe, 41, shared via Bachelor Nation on Wednesday, November 18. “This was, like, as soon as I got out of my last relationship. I was like, ‘That guy’s hot.’”

Little did The Bachelorette alum know at the time that Hartman, 27, would one day be announced as the lead for season 30 of The Bachelor.

“I realized his age, and he didn’t respond, and I was like, ‘Oh, get out of here Kaitlyn,’” she said. “I deleted it real quick.”

On November 13, ABC shocked fans when the network announced that Hartman would be the new Bachelor when the show returns in March 2027.

“When people are saying ‘I do,’ they don’t ask where you met,” the Washington Commanders quarterback shared on Good Morning America after ABC’s casting announcement. “They just ask if you wanna do it forever. I don’t see any problem with that.”

Bristowe, who was the lead for season 11 of The Bachelorette in 2015, assured fans that she would not be applying for the new season.

“Do I have a boyfriend? I do,” she said on a panel hosted by Jared Freid. “It feels a little more intentional, a little more honest. I don’t feel like I have a stage mom as a partner.”

Bristowe has been keeping her love life much more private in recent months after having her fair share of public breakups.

While speaking on a panel aboard Bachelor Nation’s Vacation at Sea, Bristowe referenced her past relationships with Shawn Booth, Nick Viall and Jason Tartick when she was asked to rank her exes “on how much you would fix them up with another single woman.”

“I would fix up Shawn Booth with a really nice therapist. I actually believe in him,” she said. “If he did some therapy, I think he would be a really amazing guy.”

As for Tartick, 38, the “Off the Vine” podcast host tried to keep it nice.

“I’ve tried so hard to move past being an a**hole on this one,” she said. “Let me just say I’m allowed to be bitter about all of my exes. Let me have it!”

In case there was any doubt as to how much fun Bristowe was having on the cruise, the reality star summed up her experience with a carousel of photos on social media.

“Honestly, I should have more content,” she wrote, “but we have all been so in the moment hanging out just GIGGLING on this bachelor nation cruise.”

Season 30 of The Bachelor premieres on ABC March 21, 2027, at 8:00 p.m. ET. New episodes will stream the next day on Hulu.
