All About Brad Pitt’s Girlfriend, Ines de Ramon

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

In November 2022, photos came out of Brad Pitt at a Bono concert on November 13 with Ines de Ramon, a Swiss jewelry professional in her thirties. People reported that de Ramon works for the jewelry brand Anita Ko. Her LinkedIn lists her position as vice president of the company.

In photos, Pitt appeared to be putting his arms on de Ramon and introducing her to his friends at the concert, including Cindy Crawford. In the years since, they’ve become more serious. As of October 2026, the pair is still going strong.

So, who is de Ramon? Here’s what to know about her connection to Pitt.

After dating rumors with Pitt first broke, de Ramon appeared to set her Instagram to private. It’s now public, and she has over 114,000 followers. She posts on occasion, sharing glimpses of the events she goes to.

De Ramon has managed to keep public information about herself very limited, despite her marriage to Paul Wesley and current ties to one of Hollywood’s biggest stars. Pitt is likely to keep details of their relationship private too, as the actor has not spoken much about any woman he has dated since his 2016 divorce from Angelina Jolie.

De Ramon is a graduate of the University of Geneva, earning her bachelor’s in business administration. Her LinkedIn indicates that she speaks five languages, with French, English, and Spanish indicated at a bilingual or native proficiency, and German and Italian listed at a professional working proficiency. Prior to working at Anita Ko in Los Angeles, de Ramon spent four years working in the retail department of jewelry company de GRISOGONO.

While neither de Ramon nor Pitt initially commented on reports that they’re dating, photos came out of them at a Bono concert together on Nov. 13, 2022. The outlet reported they arrived together in Pitt’s Tesla at 8 P.M. They left separately, de Ramon in Pitt’s bodyguard’s SUV and Pitt in his Tesla.

Interestingly, the source was indicated as close to de Ramon rather than Pitt. The source said, “[they] have been dating for a few months,” and that “Brad is really into” de Ramon. “They met through a mutual friend. She is very nice.”

A second source, this one close to Pitt, confirmed they were dating, but casually. “[They] recently started dating. [But] it’s not an exclusive relationship. Ines is cute, fun and energetic. She has a great personality. Brad enjoys spending time with her,” the source said.

People reported on Dec. 16, 2022, that de Ramon and Pitt were acting particularly coupley at the after-party for the Los Angeles premiere of his film Babylon the night before. The couple was seen “mingling with guests, occasionally with their arms around each other,” the outlet wrote. It marked de Ramon and Pitt’s first big public sighting together since dating reports broke in November.

People reported that the two spent New Year’s Eve together in Cabo. A source told the outlet on January 2, “They are dating and having fun. They have a good thing going on. There is no stress. Brad is enjoying it.”

“[He] is not seeing anyone else right now,” the source added, suggesting that things may be becoming a little more serious. “He is spending more time with Ines. He is very happy.”

A few days after their vacation, a source told Entertainment Tonight that the new couple is “excited about where things are headed.”

They continued, “Ines took a bunch of time off from work during the holidays to celebrate with Brad and spend New Year’s Eve with him. They had a great time in Cabo just relaxing and having fun together.”

“They’re a great match and have a good thing going,” they added. “They have a lot in common and Brad likes how chill and low-key Ines is. They’re excited about where things are headed.”

Pitt isn’t the only actor that de Ramon has been with. De Ramon and The Vampire Diaries’ Paul Wesley announced their separation after three years of marriage in September 2022. Wesley and de Ramon were first romantically linked in July 2018.

On Friday, February 17, Wesley filed for divorce in Los Angeles listing the reason for their split as “irreconcilable differences,” according to People. The divorce petition says that the former couple’s assets split will be decided at “trial or by stipulation.”

In March 2024, Entertainment Tonight confirmed Wesley and de Ramon had finalized their divorce. Their divorce papers were signed off on February 23 and filed in Los Angeles County Superior Court on Wednesday, March 6. She did not contest Wesley’s divorce filing, and for division of property, they “have entered into a written agreement regarding their property and their marriage or domestic partnership rights.”

There will be no spousal support and de Ramon requested “the restoration of the former name as set forth in the proposed Judgment.”
