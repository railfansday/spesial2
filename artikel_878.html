Cadillac confirm Bottas to stay with team for 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Please use Chrome browser for a more accessible video player

Cadillac have confirmed Valtteri Bottas will stay on at the team for the 2027 Formula 1 season, ending speculation about the Finnish driver's future.

Bottas returned after a year's absence from the grid for this season, Cadillac's first in the sport, as one of two hugely experienced drivers for the all-new American team's first line-up alongside Sergio Perez, who signed a contract extension last month until at least 2029.

But, Bottas' future has been up in the air recently as Cadillac pushed for Formula 2 driver Colton Herta to earn a Formula 1 super licence, which he secured two weeks ago, by running him in practice sessions.

The team have committed to Bottas for another year though, citing the 37-year-old's "extensive Formula 1 experience, technical insight and race-winning pedigree to the team".

"To whom it may concern, I'm staying!" said Bottas, in reference to his team radio after winning the 2019 Australian Grand Prix with Mercedes.

"I'm excited to continue this journey with Cadillac into 2027. We knew building a new Formula 1 team from the ground up wasn't going to be easy, but that's exactly what makes this challenge so special.

"We've laid the foundations, we're learning and improving, and there's still plenty more to come, I'm hungry for more, and I believe in what we're building together. The story is only just getting started."
