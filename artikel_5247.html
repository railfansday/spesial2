Alice Huang, also known as Huang Le, is a Chinese citizen and former senior executive at Singapore-based data centre firm Megaspeed, which bought more than $4.8 billion worth of Nvidia chips over two years, according to the New York Times. She is not related to Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang. A Chinese private fund database lists her as a former TV reporter at Hunan TV and state broadcaster CCTV, an assistant at HSBC’s private banking arm and a vice president at CITIC Securities. The New York Post, however, could not find a CCTV profile for her, and a Hunan TV executive said he had never heard of her. She was first photographed beside Jensen Huang at a Taipei bar in June 2024. A classified memo alleges that China’s Ministry of State Security recruited her to gather information on Nvidia. These claims have not been publicly proven. President Trump called the matter very complex and said officials would look into it closely.
