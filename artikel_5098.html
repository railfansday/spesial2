Grayscale says Bitcoin and major cryptocurrencies are showing clear signs of oversold conditions.

Bitcoin has suffered a 56% drawdown from its October 2025 peak, while ETP record $2 billion in weekly outflows.

Grayscale warns AI-related risks could continue driving crypto market volatility near term.

Grayscale Head of Research Zach Pandl says Bitcoin and other major cryptos are in oversold territory. However, Bitcoin failed to hold above $64,500 even after Nvidia and other US tech stocks rallied following Jensen Huang’s return as CEO, slipping back toward $60,000.

He warned that AI-related developments could have a greater impact on prices in the near term than blockchain fundamentals.

In a report released on November 23, Zach Pandl said Bitcoin dropped to around $55,000 on November 21, falling below its previous June low of $58,000. He added that the decline has pushed major digital assets into oversold territory.

The current Bitcoin bear market has now lasted at least 411 days, making it the longest in Grayscale’s historical comparison.

However, the 56% drop from Bitcoin’s peak of around $126,000 is also the shallowest drawdown among the market cycles studied.

Grayscale also pointed to weak fund flows. Global crypto exchange-traded products recorded more than $2 billion in outflows last week, while stablecoins saw significant redemptions.

Meanwhile, Bitcoin and Ethereum futures open interest fell back toward summer lows. In the options market, the implied volatility curve turned deeply inverted, with traders paying higher premiums for downside protection.

CoinGlass data also shows weakness across the market. Its November 24 RSI heatmap showed an average crypto RSI of 35.23, with 12 assets in oversold territory and only one in overbought territory.

Bitcoin’s RSI stood near 26, while BNB was around 29 and several other major tokens remained below 40.

Despite these oversold signals, Grayscale says the next market move may depend more on AI developments than blockchain fundamentals.

Pandl pointed to uncertainty around US data center construction, Jensen Huang’s removal and reinstatement as Nvidia CEO, and Anthropic’s IPO.

Grayscale said public blockchain fundamentals remain solid and current prices look attractive for medium- and long-term investors.

“Bitcoin and other leading assets are oversold, but the near-term market outlook will depend more on AI-related risks than blockchain fundamentals.”

However, it warned that near-term uncertainty remains high.
