Martin Zubimendi is finished at Arsenal, dismal stats in latest showing speak volumes - verdict

Martin Zubimendi's future at Arsenal is looking bleak after zero Premier League starts in the first 10 games.

Mikel Arteta has seemingly decided the 27-year-old is no longer part of his best starting XI, with the former Real Sociedad midfielder needing to change his opinion.

This international break was the perfect chance for the Arsenal man to do just that, with him starting both of his national team fixtures.

While he did impress against Czechia, Arteta would have been much more interested to see how he got on against England.

With many Arsenal players involved in the action, the Gunners' manager is likely to have been watching.

But Zubimendi failed to take the chance in front of him.

While Zubimendi's technical quality remains unquestionable, the off-ball side of his game was lacking against England.

England were able to find space and create chances too easily, exposing an area of his game that Arsenal will also be keen to monitor.

As per FotMob, Zubimendi was good on the ball, as shown by his 92 per cent passing accuracy.

But that is to be expected of a player of his on-ball abilities.

England were dangerous in attack, with the Three Lions creating five big chances throughout the game.

Zubimendi was unable to help break up the play, with a lack of defensive contributions no doubt adding to that.

He made just two throughout the game, including one block and one interception.

Additionally, he was dribbled past twice, and his stats in his duels left a lot to be desired.

Zubimendi won just 25 per cent of his four ground duels, highlighting just how difficult an evening he found it against Thomas Tuchel's side.

To make matters worse for the defensive midfielder, after 72 minutes, he had to be withdrawn from the action.

The Spanish international was seen holding his hamstring as he was taken off the field.

For Zubimendi, his inability to impact proceedings against one of the tougher nations, and this new injury blow, will only make things far more difficult for him at Arsenal.

Ultimately, his time as a starter looks to be over, as there are no clear pathways back into the Arsenal midfield.
