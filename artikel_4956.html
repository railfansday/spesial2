SBI brings Japanese stocks to Solana but investors face a five-day wait to cash out

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

SBI’s Solana expansion brings Japanese equities to more investors through USDC, with redemptions taking five dealing days.

SBI Solana Global began full-scale operations on Nov. 25, with plans to bring Japanese financial assets to more markets.

Eligible professional investors can use USDC to buy exposure to a Japanese dividend stock fund through Hong Kong’s HashKey Earn. To exit, they submit a redemption request, and HashKey expects settlement on T+5 under the issuer’s dealing calendar and settlement arrangements.

SBI Holdings, Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group and the Solana Foundation said they completed agreements for a new joint-venture structure and that the former SBI R3 Japan began operating under its new name.

The partners plan to use Solana to distribute Japanese assets and develop services in stablecoin payments, financial and real-world asset tokenization, and on-chain asset management.

The partners intend to start with practical uses in Japan before expanding across Asia and linking up with global markets.

DigiFT and SBI Global Asset Management announced the token JX on July 15. DigiFT issued the token on Solana as a depositary receipt linked to the SBI Japan High Dividend Equity Fund’s growth type, managed by SBI Asset Management.

The fund invests mainly in listed Japanese companies and seeks returns from dividends and rising share prices. DigiFT says the growth structure makes no distributions at either the fund or token level.

Qualified investors first gained access through Singapore-based DigiFT on July 14, according to SBI Global Asset Management. On Nov. 5, SBI Global Asset Management announced a second distribution channel through HashKey Earn, a service of the licensed Hong Kong platform HashKey Exchange.

That gave eligible investors a second place to buy JX while SBI Asset Management continued to run the underlying portfolio.

Clients must qualify as professional investors under HashKey and issuer rules and complete applicable onboarding and suitability checks.

The public dealing terms list a minimum subscription of 5,000 USDC and a minimum redemption of 750,000 JX tokens, and the platform lists a 0.1% redemption fee.

The daily cutoff is 9 a.m. Hong Kong time. HashKey expects settlement in five dealing days for subscriptions and redemptions after the applicable dealing date.

The final price and timing depend on the product’s valuation and settlement arrangements. A non-dealing day in Japan, Singapore, or the US can affect processing or settlement.

Payouts are in USDC, while the underlying stocks are mostly denominated in yen, so exchange-rate movements can still affect returns.

Both instruments were issued for the trial, and the test paired delivery of the bond token with payment in the stablecoin.
