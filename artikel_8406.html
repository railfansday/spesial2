Checkwriting on the VG taxable brokerage account works okay. But I'd still worry about check fraud via the various means.

I read somewhere that checks issued by bank 'billpay' services are safer because they cannot be washed as part of a fraud attempt and there is no connection to your personal bank account. I'm testing that notion shortly.
The "no connection to your personal bank account" varies by the bank. The Fidelity BillPay attached to the CMA account uses and shows the account number on the paper checks. One of my other banks uses a different number not connected to my personal account number.
It can also vary for each payment from the same bank. Fidelity BillPay uses FiServ CheckFree. CheckFree sends payments either 1) via ACH (if the recipient has their ACH details on file with CheckFree and the payment amount meets requirements for that payee), or 2) via a "laser draft" or check drawn on the customer's account, or 3) via a check drawn on a CheckFree account. In that case, the money is first withdrawn from the customer via ACH. The decision to use 2 or 3 is made based on amount and recipient.
