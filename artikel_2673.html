Four grabs for Konata Mumpfield against Packers

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Los Angeles Rams WR Konata Mumpfield caught four of his five targets for 69 yards against the Green Bay Packers in Week 12.

Why it Matters: Injuries have thrust Mumpfield in to a larger role, and he has responded with back-to-back decent outings. He has caught seven passes for 150 yards in the last two games, and Mumpfield is emerging as a legitimate flex candidate. Expect him to be one of the team's main receivers again in Week 13 against the Chiefs.
