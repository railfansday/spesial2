Who is ‘Lennox’ the Corgi? Viral dog steals the show during NFL’s Panthers vs. Broncos clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

NFL social media is abuzz with a highlight play from the Carolina Panthers' 26-21 home loss to the Denver Broncos, but it wasn't from a human athlete.

People aren't talking about the Broncos improving to 7-2 and establishing themselves as genuine Super Bowl threats after a slow start to the season following their Sunday afternoon win on November 8.

That is entirely because of the viral hit of this game at halftime when one of the corgis broke free from the group and started sprinting around the field, breaking the ankles of four pursuers before finally being corralled.

The video of the corgi evading the pursuers effortlessly while running around the field has naturally attracted a lot of attention, with millions already engaging with the clip.

The dog was referred to as 'Lennox' throughout social media memes, especially after Barstool Sports shared a meme graphic with the dog's name.

Turns out, the dog's real name is Quinn, and she was selected to race at the halftime show among a group of corgis also featuring corgis Winnie, Sausage, Daisy, Colorado, and Ollie.

The dogs were a part of the Charlotte Corgi Club, with the group reacting to Quinn's famous NFL moment on their Facebook page, where they confirmed her name as well.

"Go Quinn Go! We had the best time at the Carolina Panthers game today and always love being a part of halftime!"

The race was dubbed the 2026 Carolina Cup, with Ollie being crowned the winner ultimately, before Quinn stole the show with her post-race dash around the field.

The Panthers might have fallen to a 3-5 record with this loss at home, but at least the fans left entertained with the close game and the incredible corgi show at halftime.
