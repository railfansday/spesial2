Chiefs give Hunter Nourzad the start at right guard vs. Cardinals

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

In the lead-up to the Kansas City Chiefs' Week 11 game against the Arizona Cardinals, former Chiefs offensive lineman Tim Grunhard indicated that there would be more change in the team's starting five than this week's injury report showed.

We knew the Chiefs would be entering the game without starting right tackle Jaylon Moore (ankle) and right guard Trey Smith (concussion), indicating another week of starts for Joshua Ezeudu and Mike Caliendo — but the team had third-year lineman Hunter Nourzad in for Smith, instead of Caliendo. It's Nourzad's second start of his career; he was also a starter in last year's season finale.

Kansas City had the ball to begin the game and showed No. 64 Nourzad between Humphrey and Ezeudu. On the first play from scrimmage, rookie running back Emmett Johnson ripped off a 16-yard run to the right side; Nourzad got hands on a linebacker to open up space for the run.

Nourzad was originally a fifth-round pick from Penn State, where he primarily played center (801 snaps) and left guard (535) over his two seasons there, according to Pro Football Focus. He transferred there from Cornell, where he primarily played offensive tackle.
