What Glenn Schumann said after being named FSU's coach

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Georgia Bulldogs ware looking for a new defensive coordinator after the Florida State Seminoles named Glenn Schumann as their head coach. FSU signed Schumann to a five-year contract, but Schumann will be serving as Georgia's defensive coordinator for the remainder of the college football season as UGA looks to make a run in the College Football Playoff.

Schumann, 36, is an excellent recruiter and has served under coach Kirby Smart during the entire Smart era at Georgia. The two were previously together at Alabama before coming to Athens.

Schumann will have a major challenge at Florida State. The Seminoles have struggled defensively and along the offensive line for much of the past decade-plus. Florida State has a rich history, but has not lived up to the high standards set during the Bobby Bowden and Jimbo Fisher eras.

"Being named the head coach at Florida State is an incredible honor and responsibility," Schumann said after Florida State hired him. The Seminoles have not made the playoff since the 2014 season. "I am thankful to President McCullough, Peter Collins, Jeff Purinton and the rest of the university's leadership for their belief in me. We are going to build a program that meets the championship expectation at Florida State through hard work, high standards and a commitment to each other.

"I want to thank Coach Smart and Coach Saban for their trust in me and supporting me with opportunities to develop as a coach and leader. I will be forever grateful for the impact they have had on my life. My family's connection to Florida State is deep, and I know what this program can be. I'm excited to bring my wife and children to a place that means so much to my family."

Schumann will be working double duty as he works to chase a national championship with Georgia while also trying to secure a quality signing class with Florida State. The early signing period is Dec. 2-4 while the transfer portal will be open Jan. 4-13 (with teams in the playoff having a five-day period after their final game to enter the portal).
