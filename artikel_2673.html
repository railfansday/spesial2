Steelers sign familiar face at WR

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Steelers have signed wide receiver Brandon Smith to the practice squad, the team announced Tuesday.

Smith has already appeared in five games for the Steelers this season. He started the year on the practice squad and was elevated the maximum of three times before he was signed to the active roster, appearing in two games before being waived ahead of Pittsburgh's Week 11 matchup with the Eagles.

Now back in Pittsburgh, Smith fills the practice squad spot vacated by edge rusher KJ Henry, who the Steelers signed to their 53-man roster ahead of the Eagles game.

Smith has four catches for 39 yards on the season. According to Steelers beat writer Chris Adamski, Smith cannot be elevated from the practice squad again this season; If Pittsburgh wants to play Smith again, he will have to be signed to the 53-man roster.

However, with Michael Pittman Jr. expected back in Week 12 and Roman Wilson returning to the Steelers' lineup last week, the team's wide receiver depth looks stronger than it has been for much of the season ahead of a Black Friday matchup against the Denver Broncos.
