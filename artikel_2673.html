Tarik Skubal has in-person meeting with Mets amid high-profile free agency

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Tarik Skubal is expected to command plenty of interest in free agency as the best starting pitcher available on the open market. While it will always be difficult to financially outmuscle the Los Angeles Dodgers, the team Skubal ended the 2026 season with, the New York Mets are expected to give them a run for their money.

According to Jon Heyman of the New York Post, "a high-powered Mets contingent made its in-person pitch" to Skubal in Orange County, California. The Mets are among six finalists for Skubal's services, joining the Dodgers, San Diego Padres, Toronto Blue Jays, and two unnamed teams.

Coming off a season in which the Mets won just 74 games, they need every bit of talent boost they can get. Their starting rotation outside of Nolan McLean and Christian Scott didn't inspire much confidence, and adding Skubal would give the Mets the legitimate ace to front the rotation to ease the burden on McLean's shoulders.

It's not quite clear if being first to meet with Skubal gives them the leg up. But at the very least, getting a meeting with Skubal is a good sign for a Mets team that knows it can compete financially with the Dodgers.

In fact, the Mets spent more on player payroll last season than the Dodgers did, and under owner Steve Cohen, they have never been shy to splash the cash. Skubal is an ace-caliber pitcher in his prime, and he is coming off yet another strong season.

In 2026, Skubal posted an ERA of 2.72 while splitting time with the Dodgers and Detroit Tigers. He struck out 186 batters in 158.2 innings, which was good for an fWAR of 5.2

Experts project Skubal to sign a contract well worth over $300 million, which would potentially take him up to his late-30s. Skubal has a 3.02 ERA for his career, and his consistency from the mound should make him worth the huge investment.
