LSU fans in shambles after 1 point loss to Texas

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Coach Lane Kiffin's LSU Tigers took on the Texas Longhorns in a game they led until the very end, faltering in a soul-crushing 22-21 loss that saw Arch Manning engineer a final drive comeback. Dilin Jones' epic run gave LSU a late lead, but the defense ceded a TD pass to Raleek Brown that had fans beside themselves in Baton Rouge, Louisiana.

Fans commiserated on social media after the game as they vented frustrations together.

"Maybe LSU did not account for a scenario where hiring Lane Kiffin results in them becoming a laughing stock," one person said.

Kiffin's team led 14-10 at halftime as the Tigers' defense continued to harass Manning. The Longhorns were outscored 7-3 in the third quarter as the tide appeared to ebb and flow in the Tigers' direction.

As the game continued to progress, LSU continued to rack up yards, led by Sam Leavitt at quarterback. Leavitt and Manning's fortunes both took wild turns as each player was intercepted three times. Leavitt ran the ball 11 times, drawing the wrath of fans on X as they questioned his continual decisions to tuck the ball and run.

Manning eventually got one last chance to take on the LSU defense and managed to make the most of it. His swing pass to Brown to win the game was perfectly timed, and more the result of a great play than poor coverage.

Now 6-4, LSU will have to regroup and see what hand fate deals them headed into Week 12. Fans are justifiably upset, and now the healing work begins for Kiffin's team.
