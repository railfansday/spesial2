Hooved mammals that migrate may be at greater risk of extinction than those that stay put

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Over the past 100,000 years, hooved mammals that migrate have experienced a higher rate of extinction compared to nonmigratory species, according to a study being published Nov. 5 in the journal PLOS Biology by Joel Abraham at Princeton University, U.S., and colleagues.
