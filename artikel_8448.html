Also look at the plan details regarding tax treatment.

If you sell immediately, whatever the bonus you get count as earned income, you will pay your regular tax and ss tax on it.
If you hold for a year or 2, depends on the plan, it becomes qualified disposition, which maybe treated as LTCG. If that's the case, you may want to hold whatever the qualification period maybe, then sell the old one when the new one is bought. The downside is you will always hold 1 or 2 years worth of purchase which could go down in value.

Again, look at the plan detail to determine what's the best.
