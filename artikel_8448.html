Is there a trick, or are we seriously talking about tying up 333k for 5 years?
I think most of us are talking about tying up vanilla assets we don’t trade much and are going to hold somewhere anyway. I don’t think anyone would fund these with cash.

Still, five years is a long time.
$250k in assets moved between a few places every 90 days can net like 1.4% annualized even if not maximizing the number of times that can be done with those assets in a year. For instance, $250k ACATS to WF for $2,500 bonus in 90 days then ACATS to Citi or Merrill for another $1,000 bonus each for 90 day holds ... or even some one offs like wire $250k cash to HSBC for $2,000 bonus in 90 days. Annualized even at only two bonuses / yr., to avoid churning too fast, while only tying up the 250k for about 6 months (actually hold periods can be less in some cases that have 30-45 days to fund) could net 1.4% boost ($3,500/$250,000).
