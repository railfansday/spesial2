Air France-KLM Flying Blue Promo Rewards: Save 25% On Select Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Air France-KLM Flying Blue program has its monthly Promo Rewards promotion, whereby members can save 25% on select award tickets over a limited travel period. The program has just published its new Promo Rewards list for November 2026, offering discounted short haul and long haul awards for travel over the coming months.

There are quite a few destinations bookable with this promotion, including several in the Americas, with options in business class, premium economy, and economy. This has the potential to be a great deal.

I’m a big fan of the Air France-KLM Flying Blue program, especially for long haul premium cabin redemptions. Not only does Flying Blue largely have attractive award pricing for business class travel, but the program also has access to more Air France and KLM award space than other programs.

The Promo Rewards offer is published every month by the Flying Blue program, and offers 25% discounts off select awards for travel on Air France and KLM. The Flying Blue program has dynamic award pricing, and this promotion specifically applies for the lowest, saver level award costs.

Flying Blue has published its November 2026 Promo Rewards, valid for bookings between November 1 and November 30, 2026. Most of these Promo Rewards have long travel windows, and can be used for flights all the way through April 30, 2027.

Let me emphasize that with the recent Flying Blue devaluation, the entry level pricing is now for a “Light” fare rather than a Standard fare, so it comes with fewer inclusions. For example, a long haul business class “Light” fare doesn’t offer any flexibility, and also doesn’t offer lounge access.

However, if you have Flying Blue Platinum status, then you still receive most of the perks of a “Standard” fare automatically. So as an example, a Promo Rewards ticket that costs 45,000 miles in business class for a “Light” fare would now cost 56,250 miles for a “Standard” fare.

All of the lowest priced awards in the above markets through April 30, 2027, should be bookable with the Promo Rewards pricing (though not necessarily the extra award availability that Flying Blue Platinum members receive). You can use all of these awards for travel to and from destinations across Europe, so you can travel beyond Amsterdam and Paris.

If you’re having trouble finding availability, remember to use Flying Blue’s secret award calendar. Also keep in mind that children ages two to 12 receive 25% off Flying Blue award tickets, including Promo Rewards.
