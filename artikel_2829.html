Caleb Williams stars as Chicago Bears beat Seattle Seahawks

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Caleb Williams threw for one touchdown and set up three short touchdown runs for D'Andre Swift as the Chicago Bears beat the Seattle Seahawks 34-17.

Williams threw for 247 yards and committed no turnovers as the Bears improved to 6-2.

Chicago kept Seattle to 252 yards and one third-down conversion on 11 tries.

Sam Darnold connected with Jaxon Smith-Njigba on a 43-yard pass with 30 seconds left in the second quarter and found Cooper Kupp to reduce the deficit to 20-17.

However, Darnold was intercepted as the Bears answered with another touchdown drive to put the result beyond doubt.
