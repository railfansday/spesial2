Ravens QB Lamar Jackson undergoes thumb surgery, expected to be out 6-10 weeks | Yahoo! Sports

Jackson, who had been out since Week 4 with an ankle injury, suffered the injury during his first pass in Week 8, making contact with the hand of Bills linebacker Gregory Rousseau. The quarterback left the game immediately and did not return. He was placed on injured reserve later that week with the hope that he could return later this season.

The Ravens have eight games remaining in the regular season and still have their bye week awaiting them in Week 13. A six-to-10 week recovery timeline certainly allows for Jackson, who reportedly told teammates to "just stay alive," to return this season. But that could also depend on where the team is in the AFC playoff hunt when he's approaching 100% healthy and ready to play.

Fernando Mendoza, Carson Beck, Kyle McCord set to make NFL history with first starts, debuts | CBS Sports

This week, we have more to look forward to: Three QBs each get their first starts and make history while doing so. Mendoza is set to make his first start for the Raiders, facing the Seattle Seahawks, and rookie third-rounder Carson Beck will make his NFL debut with the Arizona Cardinals. Another quarterback joining the group of first-timers is 2025 sixth-rounder Kyle McCord, who is making his NFL debut this week as the Miami Dolphins starter.

If the three of them do start, 50 quarterbacks will have made at least one start through Week 10 this season, tied for second-most in the past 20 seasons. Only 2019 saw more quarterbacks, when 51 quarterbacks had at least one start through Week 10.
