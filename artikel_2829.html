Tom Brady makes bold prediction Raiders will win Super Bowl in short order

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

While Tom Brady built his legendary playing career with the New England Patriots and the Tampa Bay Buccaneers, he's now more involved as a partial owner of the Las Vegas Raiders.

He and general manager John Spytek are responsible for building a competitive roster. Brady believes they are on track to make a strong statement about the franchise.

Brady returned to Gillette Stadium in Foxborough, Massachusetts, as part of the FOX Sports crew that covered the Week 9 Patriots-Green Bay Packers game. Coincidentally, it was also the day the Patriots inducted former Brady teammate Rob Gronkowski into the team's Ring of Honor.

Brady, Gronkowski, and Super Bowl 53 Most Valuable Player Julian Edelman reunited when the former Patriots wideout asked Brady some quick questions as part of the "Gronk and Jules Show."

One of the questions was, "True or False: The Raiders will win the Super Bowl by 2030." To which Brady answered "true" without hesitation.

The seven-time Super Bowl winner believes that the Raiders will get their fourth Vince Lombardi Trophy in four years. In that case, there's much work left to be done by the front office.

They may have a generational player in quarterback Fernando Mendoza. But as it stands, there are gaping holes that must be addressed. In addition to having a weak running game, the Raiders' defense is currently giving up the most points per game. Improving on those departments could boost the team's chances to become Super Bowl champions again.
