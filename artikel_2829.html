Hawks Rookie Kingston Flemings Changes Eastern Conference Power Rankings

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Atlanta Hawks are climbing the Eastern Conference standings. While teams like Milwaukee and Chicago flounder with losing streaks, Atlanta found a new engine on Wednesday night. The rookie guard Kingston Flemings made his first career start.

Atlanta needed a reliable facilitator after trading away Trae Young, and Flemings arrived with the answer. He scored 14 points on 6-for-9 shooting while adding four assists and three rebounds in the win over the Wizards. According to Sports Illustrated, Flemings manages the offense more effectively than certain veterans, a distinction that may represent the greatest praise possible for a 19-year-old rookie. This level of control means stars like Jalen Johnson and Nickeil Alexander-Walker no longer have to force every shot. The offense flows through him, making the entire system simpler and more dangerous for opponents.

The Big Lead noted that the Milwaukee Bucks have lost four straight games and sit at 1-7, a record that offers little reason for optimism. Chicago Bulls fans are watching their team lose six straight games after a tough schedule started to bite. Even the Charlotte Hornets are in trouble, having lost five straight games with their young core failing to find consistent production. These teams are dealing with injuries and roster questions while the Hawks seem to have found a solution.

It is easy to forget about a team that does not have a top-ten player in the league, but Flemings changes that math. Soaring Down South wrote that if this rookie continues to display incredible two-way impact, the Hawks could instantly reach the next level of excellence as a unit. The team now has a playmaker who fits their timeline. The Magic and Nets are also struggling to find their footing with records of 2-6. Atlanta is the only team in this group that looks like it has a clear path forward.

Dyson Daniels recorded 17 rebounds, a Hawks record for a guard, and Onyeka Okongwu added 16 rebounds in the win.
