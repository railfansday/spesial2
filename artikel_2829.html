Vincent Kompany in awe of Bayern Munich’s Michael Olise

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Michael Olise has quickly established himself as one of the most exciting players in world football, and Vincent Kompany has no hesitation in putting him among the very best.

The Bayern Munich head coach has praised the Frenchman's extraordinary talent, mentality, and attention to detail while explaining that his job is not to turn Olise into a world-class player, but to make sure he continues developing the qualities that already make him special.

Speaking about his contribution to Olise's development, Kompany highlighted the winger's ability to see things on the pitch that others simply cannot.

"I think a player with talent like Michael Olise will always make it. He will always be one of the best players. It's not just his talent, but also his mentality and his eye for detail. He always seems to have the vision to see something that others might not see, and that's true even in training. And that's why this boy has already become a truly special player in world football," Kompany told Sky Germany journalist Torben Hoffmann (as captured by @iMiaSanMia).

Asked whether he considers Olise world-class, Kompany was unequivocal.

"Yes, absolutely. Without a doubt. Especially because this is now confirmation of what he has already achieved. He has taken many steps forward over the past few years. But the most important thing for a coach in a situation with players like this—and I say this not only from my experience as a coach, but also from my experience as a teammate of players like that—is that the hunger remains. And even when the guys come into an environment where that praise is no longer constantly there. With us, you get praise for what you deliver and how you work in training, for what you do and produce in a game. And if that is no longer there in a game, then we immediately have that discussion about how we can continue to take the next steps. That's the role we play. But it's not about us turning a player into a world-class player. That's impossible. He already has that within himself," Kompany said.

Kompany's message is clear — Olise already possesses everything necessary to be among the world's elite.
