East Bernstadt woman arrested following reported assault on Wayne County football player

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

MONTICELLO — A woman from East Bernstadt was arrested Tuesday following an investigation into a reported assault involving a Wayne County football player during last Friday's football game against North Laurel.

According to a press release from the Monticello Police Department, officers were dispatched to the Wayne County Emergency Room on Oct. 30 in reference to a reported assault that occurred during the game. A Wayne County football player was being evaluated for injuries sustained during the incident.

Upon arrival, K-9 Officer Bertram conducted an investigation and determined that the player had allegedly been strangled from behind by a female spectator during the game, according to police.

With assistance from the Kentucky State Police, investigators identified the suspect as Kayla Barrett, 36, of East Bernstadt. A criminal complaint was filed in Wayne District Court, and an arrest warrant was subsequently issued.

On Tuesday, Barrett was arrested by Kentucky State Police and charged with second-degree strangulation. She was transported to the Laurel County Detention Center and released Wednesday morning.

The Monticello Police Department thanked Kentucky State Police for their cooperation and assistance throughout the investigation.

In its statement, the department emphasized the importance of maintaining a safe environment at high school athletic events, saying student-athletes should be able to compete without fear of violence or intimidation.

"While emotions can run high during competitive sporting events, there is absolutely no justification for physical violence against players, coaches, officials, or spectators," the department stated.
