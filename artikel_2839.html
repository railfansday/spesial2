NFL not suspending DK Metcalf for his actions vs. Eagles on Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

NFL not suspending DK Metcalf for his actions vs. Eagles on Sunday originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

The NFL is reportedly not suspending DK Metcalf for his attempt to poke Eagles cornerback Cooper DeJean in the eyes on Sunday afternoon.

Metcalf was tossed in the third quarter after his second personal foul. He might (and probably will) face fines from the NFL, but will not be suspended, per ESPN.

After the game, DeJean said Metcalf "almost" got him in the eye. Despite video evidence, Metcalf denied that he tried to poke DeJean's eyes.

Metcalf was held to just 1 catch for 2 yards against the Eagles before getting tossed. The Eagles snapped their seven-game losing streak with a 19-16 win.

Quinyon Mitchell locked up Metcalf all afternoon. Mitchell lined up against Metcalf 14 times and gave up 0 catches on 2 targets. DeJean matched up against Metcalf 9 times and gave up just that one 2-yard catch.

"He wasn't getting the ball a whole lot during that game and some receivers, they let that get to them," DeJean said. "Obviously, he tried to take it out on me."

DeJean claimed he wasn't doing anything to try to get under Metcalf's skin, but Metcalf indicated that wasn't the case.

Even though Metcalf dodged Steelers reporters after the game, he didn't show much accountability when they finally caught up with him on Monday.

"We need him," Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy said after the game. "We need him in those games and that's not acceptable. He is one of the 48. It is about everything we do in the week to put those guys in position to be successful. We have receivers up today. We need all of our guys."
