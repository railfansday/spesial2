Bears TE Colston Loveland week-to-week with knee injury, reportedly expected to land on IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Chicago Bears tight end Colston Loveland is considered week-to-week with a knee injury, the team announced on Friday.

Loveland is reportedly going for a second opinion on the injury, which was suffered during Thursday's 39-35 Thanksgiving Day win over the Detroit Lions. He is expected to miss time with what is reportedly a knee sprain, according to NFL Network's Ian Rapoport.

A stint on injured reserve is expected, reports ESPN's Adam Schefter, which would rule him out for at least four weeks. The Bears are hopeful that Loveland will return before the end of the regular season and be ready for the playoffs.

Loveland was carted off the field in the first quarter after Lions linebacker Malcolm Rodriguez rolled up on the 22-year-old's left leg as he was engaged in a block for Bears running back D'Andre Swift.

As the second quarter got underway, Loveland was ruled out by the Bears.

Bears head coach Ben Johnson was unsure of the severity of Loveland's injury after the game.

"Nope, don't know yet, no," Johnson said. "We'll find out."

Prior to the injury, Loveland had lost a fumble on Chicago's opening drive, after the ball was punched out by Lions defensive end Derrick Moore. Detroit went three-and-out on the ensuing drive.

Rookie tight end Sam Roush and veteran Stephen Carlson stepped up to fill in the gap left by Loveland: Carlson had two huge catches in the Bears' touchdown drive in the second quarter. The Bears went on to rally for a thrilling win via a go-ahead touchdown in the final seconds.
