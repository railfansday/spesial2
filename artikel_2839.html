Washington football game pushed back due to FAA's flight groundings

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Washington Huskies football game at Michigan State has been moved to 2:30 p.m. Pacific Time on Saturday, Nov. 14, a delay in the kickoff necessitated by the nationwide grounding of flights by the FAA on Friday.

The Huskies were originally due to depart Boeing Field in Seattle around 1 p.m., until all plane flights in the country were halted. The team waited in a hangar initially, then returned to campus.

Washington eventually was cleared to fly and left Seattle just before 8 p.m. on Friday night, according to On Montlake, an independent site focused on Washington athletics. That departure time would have slated the team to arrive in East Lansing, Michigan, where Michigan State University is located, around 2:30 a.m. Eastern Standard Time.

The original kickoff time was 1:15 p.m. Pacific, or 4:15 p.m. EST in Michigan.

The game will be broadcast on the Big Ten Network.
