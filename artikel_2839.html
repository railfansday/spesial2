NFL honors John Madden with Thanksgiving-themed coins, Madden uniform patch for Thursday slate

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Five years after the death of John Madden, the NFL is keeping the memory of the broadcaster's connection to Thanksgiving alive.

The league announced last week that a Madden coin, featuring Madden's silhouette on one side and a six-legged "turducken" (a chicken stuffed in a duck, stuffed in a turkey) on the other, will be used for coin flips for the three NFL games on Thanksgiving Day.

All players in those games — Detroit Lions-Chicago Bears, Dallas Cowboys-Philadelphia Eagles and Buffalo Bills-Kansas City Chiefs — will wear patches on their uniforms with the Madden silhouette. The Los Angeles Rams and Green Bay Packers, who played on Wednesday (or "Thanksgiving Eve," as the league calls it), also wore the patches.

Stencils honoring Madden will be placed in the end zones and along the sidelines of the three home fields during the matchup, and panels on the stadium wall will feature Madden in his coaching days with the Oakland Raiders.

Along with those Madden-themed additions, the MVP of each game will receive a trophy fashioned to look like a turkey leg-football amalgamation. The Thanksgiving MVPs will also earn a $10,000 donation from the NFL Foundation to a high school or youth football program in their name.

Madden, one of the defining figures of the sport as both a coach and broadcaster, died in 2021 at the age of 85. Alongside longtime broadcasting partner Pat Summerall, Madden called NFL games for decades on CBS and later Fox, becoming associated with Thanksgiving games thanks to his enthusiasm for turduckens and turkey legs.

The league first introduced the specialized Madden patches and coins in 2023, two years after the football legend's death, and have continued the tradition over the past few years.
