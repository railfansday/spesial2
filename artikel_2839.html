Vote for the Detroit Free Press Girls Athlete of the Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

It's time to vote for the Detroit Free Press Girls Athlete of the Week.

This week's poll includes four volleyball players who had standout performances during the week of Nov. 2-8.

Voting will close at 3 p.m. on Thursday, Nov. 12.

Here are the nominees (listed in alphabetical order by last name):

Sophomore Noell Hughes turned in a standout two-match performance for Trenton, totaling 48 digs, 32 kills, five blocks, three assists and one ace. Hughes helped the Trojans defeat Woodhaven in five sets in the district semifinals before falling to Temperance Bedford in another five-set battle the following night.

Bloomfield Hills advanced to the regional round of the MHSAA volleyball tournament for the second consecutive season with victories over West Bloomfield and Orchard Lake St. Mary's. Emily Nwabueze dominated at the net, recording 38 kills, 12 blocks and one dig across the two-game stretch.

Lutheran North punched its ticket to the regional semifinals for the first time since the 2019-20 season with victories over Armada and Notre Dame Prep. VanSteenkiste paced the Mustangs, totaling 47 receptions, 23 kills, 22 digs, five assists, five aces and three blocks across the two-match stretch.

Romeo defeated Port Huron, Utica Eisenhower and Port Huron Northern to advance to its fourth consecutive regional tournament appearance. Williams recorded 47 kills, 22 digs and five aces for the week.
