Browns rookie Denzel Boston’s toe injury deemed ‘minor’ in latest update

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Browns rookie Denzel Boston's toe injury deemed 'minor' in latest update appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Cleveland Browns are now 5-4 for the season and will enter their home game against the Houston Texans off the back off two straight losses. Denzel Boston, who picked up a toe injury during the 23-22 overtime loss to the New Orleans Saints, returned to practice Friday after missing Wednesday and Thursday, per a post on X by Mary Kay Cabot.

In response, ESPN insider Ian Rapoport claimed that his return means that the injury was minor. Head coach Todd Monken had initially described the injury as a minor toe issue and anticipated Boston would return to practice Thursday, but the rookie remained sidelined for that session before getting back on the field Friday. His return suggests the injury may not be serious, although it does not mean that he will be available against Houston.

The rookie wide receiver caught six passes for a career-high 98 yards, including a 60-yard reception against the Saints. Boston has emerged as an important part of Cleveland's attack, leading the team with 507 receiving yards through nine games on 32 receptions and three touchdowns.

Boston may still end up receiving a questionable designation, and has dealt with multiple minor injuries in recent weeks. Regardless, his potential return is not the only positive for the Browns, as Deshaun Watson is also expected to return as the starting quarterback after missing the Saints game following a concussion.

Cleveland will look to avoid a third straight defeat at Huntington Bank Field and have multiple other injury concerns for this one. Elgton Jenkins was expected to return to practice Thursday and is sidelined with a knee issue.

Meanwhile, safety Ronnie Hickman also sat out of practice on both Wednesday and Thursday due to concussion protocol, while fullback Michael Burton's availability is also under doubt due to a calf strain.
