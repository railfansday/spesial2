UCLA Guard Earns Big Ten Freshman of the Week Honors

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Support Local Media - Click to Add LA Sports Report as a Preferred Source on Google

UCLA Bruins guard/foward Nikola Kusturica earned Big Ten Men's Basketball Freshman of the Week for his efforts from Nov. 2-8.

Kurustica, a 6-foot-9 guard from Serbia, had incredible showings during his two games last week, averaging 19 points, 5.5 rebounds and 2.5 assists over the course of UCLA's season-opening wins against Arizona and North Florida.

His better game of the two came against North Florida, during which he shot 10-of-12 with 29 points, six rebounds and three assists. Against No. 5 Arizona four days prior, Kusturica had nine points, five rebounds and two assists.

The 17-year-old also had stunning defensive displays in both games, logging a total of give blocks and three steals across those contests.

Kusturica also had a strong showing against Arkansas-Pine Bluff in UCLA's on 106-37 rout on Wednesday, logging 17 points with three rebounds and three assists, shooting 7-of-9 from the field with a block and two steals.

His 18.3 points and 2.0 blocks per game lead the Bruins, and his 4.7 rebounds per game rank second on the team begin forward Eric Dailey Jr. He's shooting 62.5% from the field through three games, also hitting 42.9% of his shots from deep.

The freshman has played a huge part in the Bruins' 3-0 start and helped them on their way to ranking No. 9 in the Associated Press' weekly top-25 poll.

Kusturica may be just 17, but he has played at the professional level for several years. He came from overseas after playing with FC Barcelona, becoming their youngest ever player in the process, and also represented Serbia at FIBA U16 EuroBasket in 2025 and the U17 World Cup this year.

He proved he had star potential at the World Cup, during which he averaged 24.6 points, 6.9 rebounds and 2.7 assists per game throughout the tournament.

UCLA hosts each of their next two games against teams in the area, as they will face off against Long Beach State on Monday, Nov. 16 and Cal State Fullerton two days later on Wednesday, Nov. 18. Long Beach State currently has a 1-2 record, winning their most recent game against Pacific, and Cal State Fullerton is 1-1 after splitting games against San Diego State and South Dakota.

The Bruins will look to remain undefeated as they take on Long Beach State on Monday at 7:00 p.m. PT.
