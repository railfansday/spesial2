Dianna Russini reveals whether she’ll release tell-all book on Mike Vrabel scandal

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Dianna Russini just kicked off her first show in her comeback to sports media, but is a book now in the works?

Rumors that Russini will be taking the scandal involving her alleged relationship with Mike Vrabel to a publisher ignited when 98.5 The Sports Hub's "Big" Jim Murray shared that she would be writing about the rumored affair.

"She said she'll be talking about everything that's gone on, is what she told my friend," Murray said.

Photos of Russini and Vrabel were published by Page Six back in April of the two at a resort in Arizona where they were seen getting cozy.

However, if you're looking for more details, you won't be getting it from Russini, as her publicist has received several offers for her to pen some pages about her rumored affair with the New England Patriots coach but will not be accepting, according to TMZ. The publication's sources also shared that Murray's claim about his friend was untrue. Sources tell TMZ that Russini is prioritizing getting back to reporting on sports, which is currently her sole focus.

The Russini Report kicked off on Wednesday, Nov. 11 which marked her official comeback to sports media.While she didn't address the scandal directly, she did talk about her personal life at the beginning of her show.

"Over the last few months, I have had a lot of time to think about my life and my priorities and the person that I want to be moving forward, and being home in New Jersey with my husband and our two little boys and being truly present, it gave me so much perspective that I didn't even know I needed," Russini said.

"It gave me so much perspective that I didn't even know I needed. I've also had a lot of long conversations with people all around football. You know, some were about the game, and others were just about life to the people who picked up the phone and checked in and offered both encouragement and honest advice, from the bottom of my heart, thank you," she added.

The Russini Report airs weekly on Wednesdays at 12 pm ET and streams on BetUS TV's website and X account.
