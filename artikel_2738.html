Packers activate Micah Parsons from PUP list to 53-man roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

That's the amount of time it took for Micah Parsons to return to the Green Bay Packers' active roster after suffering a torn ACL (and other damage to his knee) late in the 2025 season. As of Tuesday, Parsons is back on the 53, with the Packers officially processing his return to active duty this afternoon. Green Bay had an open spot on the roster ready for Parsons, having released running back Pierre Strong, Jr. a few days ago, so no corresponding move was necessary.

Despite missing three games in 2025, Parsons was named a first-team All-Pro in his initial season with the Green and Gold. His 12.5 sacks led the Packers last season and was the highest total for a Packers defender over a full season since Za'Darius Smith put up 13.5 in 2019. Eight of Parsons' sacks came in the fourth quarter or overtime as well, demonstrating his value to the team in crunch time.

Without Parsons, the Packers' defense took a significant step back at the end of 2025, giving up at least 360 yards in each of the four games he missed. However, the unit — and the pass rush in particular — has taken a giant leap forward in 2026 under new coordinator Jonathan Gannon. The Packers rank first in the NFL in turnover rate and they are third in the league in net yards per pass attempt allowed. Furthermore, their sack numbers have skyrocketed despite Parsons' absence; 2025 saw the team record just 36 sacks over 17 games, including Parsons' 12.5, but this year, the team is already up to 29 through just 10 games.

And now, the biggest veteran addition that the Packers have made in decades is back and ready to wreak havoc in backfields once again.

Parsons is officially listed as questionable for Wednesday's game against the Los Angeles Rams, but that should be a formality. His ramp-up period is now behind him, and by all accounts, he's ready to go give a boost to a squad that's looking to go on a strong stretch run.
