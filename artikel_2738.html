Alvin Kamara should be available for Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

New Orleans Saints RB Alvin Kamara (non-injury) fully participated in practice Friday, Nov. 20, and is not on the injury report for Week 11.

Why It Matters: Kamara was productive in his limited playing time last week but should continue to see a limited amount of touches during games. He can be a possible No. 3 running back or flex option in a majority of fantasy leagues.
