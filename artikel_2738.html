FedEx Cup Fall Bubble Watch: Who's projected in and out with only two events to go

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

It will be small solace after losing a sudden-death playoff on Sunday at the World Wide Technology Championship, but Ben James improved from No. 62 to No. 53 in the FedEx Cup Fall standings. That means that with just two events remaining on the schedule, he clinched a spot in the first two Signature events in 2027: Pebble Beach and Riviera. Not a bad consolation prize.

James moved on the right side of the cut off as did Michael Kim, who jumped 10 spots to No. 55 after finishing third. Those moves pushed Max Homa back four spots to 'bubble boy' status, while Jordan Spieth dropped off the pace to No. 63 (though he's only played once this fall at a no-cut event in Japan).

That wasn't the only movement as players jockey to finish from Nos. 51-60 for signature event status, inside the top 100 to be fully exempt next season, and earn some level of conditional status for finishing Nos. 101-110 and Nos. 111-125. Anything to avoid a return to Q-School and a possible demotion to the Korn Ferry Tour, where the purses are a fraction of those on the PGA Tour.
