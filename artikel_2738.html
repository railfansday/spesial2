Ja’Marr Chase joins Bengals legend AJ Green in elite club amid Steelers beatdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

With Joe Burrow delivering a stellar performance, the Cincinnati Bengals continued their remarkable showing when they took the field against the Pittsburgh Steelers on Sunday. However, Burrow wasn't the only Bengals star making headlines, as wide receiver Ja'Marr Chase also enjoyed an outstanding night and reached another significant career milestone with his performance.

According to Sports Illustrated's Jay Morrison, the Bengals wideout became the first receiver since his former teammate AJ Green in 2014 to record more than 200 receiving yards against the Steelers.

"Ja'Marr Chase is the first receiver to top 200 yards against the Steelers since … the #Bengals' A.J. Green in 2014 (224 in a 42-21 loss). The most the Steelers have ever given up is 258 by Qadry Ismail in 1999," Morrison wrote.

Chase was arguably Pittsburgh's biggest defensive nightmare throughout the game. The Bengals wide receiver finished with 231 receiving yards on 11 receptions.

For the first time in his career, Chase has now surpassed 100 receiving yards in three consecutive games. His explosive plays repeatedly put Pittsburgh's defense under pressure, helping Cincinnati maintain control of the matchup and eventually secure a 38-18 victory.

According to The Athletic's Paul Dehner Jr., Chase has accumulated 526 receiving yards over his last three games. That total ranks as the second-most receiving yards by any wide receiver over a three-game stretch in a single season in the last decade.

Additionally, Chase has surpassed Justin Jefferson for the second-most receptions through a player's first six NFL seasons.

Meanwhile, Burrow was another major driving force behind Cincinnati's dominant victory. The Bengals quarterback completed 35 of 44 pass attempts for 409 yards and four touchdowns.

Burrow and Chase's connection continues to be one of Cincinnati's biggest offensive strengths. Sunday's performance provided yet another reminder of how dangerous the duo can become when both are operating at their best.
