Northern Oil and Gas, Inc. (NOG +3.12%) reported record total production and adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter, driven by operational leverage and improved pricing realizations across its multi-basin portfolio. The company achieved its goal of reducing leverage below 1.5 times net debt-to-adjusted EBITDA a full quarter ahead of schedule while generating its 27th consecutive quarter of positive free cash flow. Management reported that the company's assets are self-funding their own growth, providing the flexibility to return capital through dividends and share repurchases while maintaining a robust $1.6 billion liquidity position. The company raised its full-year production guidance and tightened its realization outlooks, reflecting strong well performance in the Permian, Uinta, and Duvernay regions.
