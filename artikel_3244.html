Scott Speedman To Guest Star On ‘Landman’ Season 3

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: Scott Speedman (R.J. Decker, Running Point) has been cast in a guest-starring role in the Paramount+ series Landman‘s third season. Details regarding his character are under wraps. Production is currently underway on Season 3 in Texas.

Co-created by Taylor Sheridan and Christian Wallace, and set in the boomtowns of West Texas, Landman is a modern tale of fortune-seeking amongst roughnecks and wildcat billionaires – fueling an oil boom so big it’s reshaping the climate, the economy and geopolitics.

The series stars Billy Bob Thornton, Demi Moore, Andy Garcia, Sam Elliott, Ali Larter, Jacob Lofland, Michelle Randolph, Colm Feore, Paulina Chávez, Kayla Wallace, Mark Collie, James Jordan, Mustafa Speaks, Bobbi Salvor Munuez, Francesca Xuereb, and Michael Kelly. Landman is executive produced by Sheridan, David C. Glasser, David Hutkin, Ron Burkle, Bob Yari, Wallace, Thornton, Geyer Kosinski, Michael Friedman, and Stephen Kay. Dan Friedkin and Jason Hoch for Imperative Entertainment, and J.K. Nickell and Megan Creydt for Texas Monthly, also executive produce. The series is produced by Paramount Television Studios, 101 Studios, and Sheridan’s Bosque Ranch Productions. Watch on Deadline

Speedman most recently starred as the titular character in ABC’s R.J. Decker, which concluded its second season in late October. He also recently appeared in the network’s popular series Grey’s Anatomy, with more yet to come in Season 23, and Netflix’s Running Point. Speedman is perhaps best known for his roles in the coming-of-age story Felicity and John Wells’ Animal Kingdom. His additional credits include Peacock’s Teacup, Netflix’s You, and ABC’s Last Resort. In film, he has appeared in David Cronenberg’s Crimes of the Future, Cellar Door, Sharp Stick, Best Sellers, Run This Town, Underworld and Underworld: Evolution, The Strangers, The Vow, Out of the Dark, Dark Blue, and Duets. Speedman is repped by CAA, Untitled Entertainment, and Johnson, Shapiro, Slewett, & Kole.
