Allegiant Air Launches 16 New Routes To Florida: See Full List Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

For obvious reasons, Allegiant Air revolves around Florida. Cirium Diio data shows that the Sunshine State accounts for about 65% of the carrier's flights this year, with an average of 115 daily departures throughout 2026. As days are far from equal, activity varies from as few as seven departures (!) on Tuesdays to as many as 190 on Sundays.

Despite serving 12 airports in Florida, Allegiant is far from being the state's leading domestic operator. It ranks sixth, behind American (influenced by its Miami hub and huge numbers of flights from Charlotte), Southwest, Delta, JetBlue, and United. It is still sixth when Sun Country's operations are included.

Analysis of the carrier's schedule for November 2026 through 2027 shows that a total of 16 Florida routes are due to begin or resume, all of which were previously revealed. They are from nine airports in the state, with six routes being from Orlando. They're from both Sanford (Allegiant's most-served airport in its entire network) and International.

After an absence of 22 months, Allegiant will reintroduce service between Sanford and North Dakota's Grand Forks shortly before Valentine's Day. When the carrier previously served the market, the US Department of Transportation data indicates that 202,325 round-trip passengers were transported, with an overall load factor of 80.1%. The source indicates that Allegiant generated $21.9 million in revenue, although it is unclear if this includes all the various add-ons (bags, seat selection, etc.)

Many routes mentioned in this article have very time-limited operations in February for school vacations and Valentine's Day and April for spring break. Most in this section fall into this camp.

Exceptions include Sarasota to Minneapolis, with Allegiant returning to Minnesota's busiest airport next year. The airline last flew there in August 2025, with one of its routes being Sarasota. It will now return and replace Sun Country, which originally served the market between 1999 and 2001 and again from 2019.

Until now, all the markets mentioned in this article will take off in 2027. This is not the case for Tampa St. Pete to Columbia, Missouri (not South Carolina), with that market's inaugural flight beginning exactly a week before this article was written. The launch was historical in various ways, including because it was Allegiant's debut route between Florida and Columbia.
