How to plan a SaaS product demo video that people actually finish

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

A product demo video answers one question: "how does it work, and will it fit what I need?" It is for people who already understand the problem and are deciding whether to trust your product. If they do not understand the problem yet, make an explainer first.

A demo that shows everything shows nothing. Choose the single task a prospect most wants to see done, such as importing data or sending a first report, and the one person who will watch it. Write both in a sentence before you record anything.

Keep narration to what the screen cannot say by itself.

Raw recordings show cursor wobble, loading screens and small text. Zoom into the part that matters, add short callouts and captions, cut the waiting time and keep the pace steady. If the real interface is cluttered or still changing, UI animation can replace the recording and keep the screens sharp and on brand.

A feature clip can be under a minute. A task walkthrough usually runs one to three minutes. A longer product tour works better with chapters. These are starting points, so test what your viewers actually finish.
