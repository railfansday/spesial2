Mexican illegal alien with prior heroin conviction sentenced to 11 years in prison for new drug, immigration crimes

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

COLUMBUS, Ohio – Luis Cabrales-Guerra, 32, was sentenced in U.S. District Court today to 132 months in prison for conspiring to distribute fentanyl and for illegally reentering the United States following his previous federal conviction for an aggravated drug felony.
