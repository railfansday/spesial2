Real Madrid await FIFA green light to complete JJ Gabriel transfer

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Even though the team has not been able to win anything significant in the last two years, the last few months have been very exciting for Real Madrid fans. This is mainly the case because of the club's activity in the transfer market.

Last summer, the Merengues brought in six new players to reinforce almost all the areas of their team, and Jose Mourinho is already taking advantage of these new signings. Meanwhile, Los Blancos have also not lost sight of young talents emerging around the world.

During the summer, they bought Sergio Martinez from Racing Club, and now, only a few months after the end of the transfer window, they are close to signing one of the most promising young talents in Europe, i.e., the English forward JJ Gabriel.

Recent reports indicate that Real Madrid have already won the race for his signature, beating other big European sides like Barcelona, Paris Saint-Germain, and Bayern Munich. Now, Diario AS has provided an update on this transfer, and reports that one final step is remaining before this move can be finalized, i.e., the approval from FIFA.

The top global football body is ultimately responsible for transfers of this kind, i.e., when the player involved is still a teenager. The Merengues had always been aware of the legal complications that this move would have entailed, and have already worked on this aspect. Thus, there are no problems expected to come from FIFA in finalizing this transfer, though this can delay the completion of the youngster's move.

Meanwhile, JJ Gabriel is already training in Spain, and recently visited Valdebebas. Everything indicates that he will be training and playing for Real Madrid in the near future, and hopefully, he will live up to the expectations he has generated with his great potential and turn out to be a great player for the Merengue side.
