Omar Cooper Jr. will not play in Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

New York Jets WR Omar Cooper Jr. (ankle, groin) did not participate in practice Friday, Nov. 27, and has been ruled out for Week 12.

Why It Matters: There is no timetable for Cooper Jr.'s return, so fantasy players can check on his status after the Week 13 bye. Garrett Wilson and Adonai Mitchell will likely see a majority of the targets among the receivers with Cooper out. Wilson can be considered a No. 1 fantasy receiver, while Mitchell should be seen as a No. 5 receiver.
