Brian Robinson remains limited on Thursday injury report

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Playing in Spain on Sunday, the Falcons held a full practice overseas on Thursday after staging a walk-through on Wednesday.

Notably, running back Brian Robinson (hamstring) remained limited on the report.

Robinson is No. 2 on the team with 211 rushing yards, behind league-leader Bijan Robinson, who has 780.

Linebacker Yasir Abdullah (hamstring) and linebacker Divine Deablo (forearm) were also limited on Thursday.

Defensive back Billy Bowman Jr. (hamstring), receiver Beaux Collins (hip), and offensive tackle Cameron Williams (ankle) were all full participants.

Receiver Zachariah Branch (hamstring) did not practice as he’s already been ruled out for the Week 9 matchup.
