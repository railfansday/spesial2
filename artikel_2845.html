Cowboys HC Brian Schottenheimer can’t shift the blame forever

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

When Brian Schottenheimer was announced as the Dallas Cowboys head coach, most fans and people around the football world were not very excited with the hire.

After hearing him speak, many got on board with giving him a chance. In year one, the Cowboys offense showed some positive signs of improvement, but historically bad defensive results resulted in the team missing the playoffs and finishing the season with a disappointing 7-9-1 record.

Over the offseason, the Cowboys fired long-time defensive coordinator Matt Eberflus and elected to hire Christian Parker. That decision was met with plenty of praise around league circles and the expectations that the defense would make some major improvements in 2026.

As we know now, that was not the case and this season is feeling eerily similar to 2025. With Eberflus fired and Christian Parker now stripped of his play-calling duties, head coach Brian Schottenheimer is running out of people to blame for his own shortcomings.

It's widely known that Eberflus was more of a front office hire given his familiarity with the Jones', his veteran experience as a defensive coordinator, and his recent experience as a head coach to support first-time head coach Brian Schottenheimer.

After that did not work out, Schottenheimer almost bragged up his process in landing Christian Parker and talked plenty this offseason about how the 2026 Dallas Cowboys had a "championship roster" coming into the season. With Matt Eberflus' unit being blamed for the 2025 Cowboys struggles, Christian Parker is now taking the blame for the 2026 Cowboys struggles.

Like most teams, the Cowboys have dealt with their fair-share of injuries on the defensive side of the ball, but most of their key players on defense have remained healthy this season. The defensive results in 2026 have just about matched the results the defense had in 2025, and even performing worse by some metrics.

Even with the defense being the main cause of their struggles in 2026, Brian Schottenheimer's inconsistency as a play-caller on offense and in-game management blunders have been just as detrimental to the team's success.

Schottenheimer was able to win a lot of people over with his words in press conferences and radio hits, but given how his first two seasons as a head coach have gone, the time for talk is over. The Cowboys need results and so far the team's 11-15-1 record with a healthy Dak Prescott is simply not good enough. Schottenheimer's last ditch effort move to name Mike Pettine the defensive play-caller may work, or it may not, but currently it feels like a panic move to help take some of the heat off himself.

We have just seven weeks left of the regular season to figure out if it was the right move or not. If it is, and the Cowboys somehow make the playoffs, expect to see Schottenheimer back in 2027. If it doesn't, it's hard to imagine the Jones' will have belief in the head coach's ability to win the NFC East given the state the division is in this year.
