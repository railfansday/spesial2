Personal Investments • Overcoming Brokerage Account Consolidation Inertia

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

63-Year old single male, retired in May 2025 with mid 7-figure assets. I inherited about 50% of these assets after my mother passed away in late 2023. Prior to inheriting most of my investments were with Vanguard and Fidelity (401k). Due to personal time considerations, I simply set up new accounts at the same financial providers where my mothers' assets were, and received the inherited assets in these new accounts. Post inheritance I now have five new accounts: Vanguard, TIAA, and Merrill Edge inherited IRAs, an LPL individual brokerage account, and an LPL inherited IRA. She also had Vanguard brokerage assets; those I just moved to my own Vanguard brokerage account. Inherited IRAs are around the very high six figures.

Recently, announced only in their monthly statement fine print, LPL stated they will now charge a $100 yearly per account maintenance fee starting in early December. The LPL accounts are neither advisory or AUM accounts. LPL has only charged a few very small fees when I purchased several fixed income assets (LPL's default sweep account pays next to no interest). I know LPL is not widely used by Bogleheads, but LPL's very limited fees so far contributed to my inertia / lack of urgency about thinking about moving any assets. In the last year LPL has gone back and forth about annual IRA maintenance fees, and never seems to communicate clearly about any changes.

Several months ago, I had it the back of my mind to consider moving the LPL assets (mid-to-high 6 figures) to a different lower-cost brokerage, such as Fidelity or Merrill Edge. My reasoning was the LPL accounts have over 40 stocks, ETFs, bonds, and other assets, and at LPL it will likely be costly to sell these and rationalize the holdings into fewer, larger holdings. Some of the stocks are small lots from spin-offs. I have also considered moving low six-figure individual stock holdings I have at Computershare to a better brokerage.

I contacted Fidelity to ask questions about moving assets - some Computershare assets were ESPP stock purchases- and they were (obviously) interested and helpful. They have checked back with me several times since then. Fidelity also handled several technical questions/issues efficiently when I recently set up a HSA there. I could potentially go with Merrill Edge, but I have somewhat less experience with them. I did not get off to a good start with Merrill in early 2024 when I told them I wanted a Merrill Edge account rather than an expensive Merrill AUM advisory account. Merrill has called me once every six months to "see if I have questions or need any help".

I know $100 is not a lot, but I dislike paying fees when better value alternatives exist. Is it realistic to get at least the LPL accounts moved by the end of November? If anyone has done similar asset moves, what was your experience - good, bad, or indifferent? Thanks as always!
