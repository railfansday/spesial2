Kaelon Black goes nowhere in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

San Francisco 49ers RB Kaelon Black ran the ball six times for eight yards against the Minnesota Vikings in Week 11. He added a seven-yard reception.

Why It Matters: Given the caliber of Minnesota's defense and Black's recent issues with ball security, it wasn't a huge surprise that his role was marginalized on Sunday night. Don't read too much into it. The rookie should continue to see expanded opportunities against lesser opponents, giving him RB3/flex value going forward. That being said, the 49ers' Week 12 matchup with Seattle is a good time to leave Black on your bench -- in the first meeting between the two, Black had just 16 yards on six carries.
