Giants think underwhelming high pick has ‘turned the corner,’ and could be re-signed

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Giants are still very much in the thick of coach John Harbaugh's fascinating first season.

They're 5-3-1 and still alone atop the NFC East with eight games remaining.

But it's never too early to look ahead to offseason storylines. For the Giants, one of those will involve outside linebacker Kayvon Thibodeaux.

The fifth overall draft pick in 2022, Thibodeaux has flashed potential at times — but has yet to become a star. He is playing this season on his fifth-year option — the final year of his rookie contract.

Entering the season, it seemed possible that the Giants might trade Thibodeaux by the Nov. 10 deadline. But that didn't happen, since the Giants are winning and had to replace Brian Burns after Week 3.

At first glance, it appears unlikely that Harbaugh (who calls the shots in East Rutherford) will re-sign Thibodeaux, since he has Burns and Abdul Carter (plus Arvell Reese, who can rush the passer quite well).

But for what it's worth, NFL insider Jordan Schultz recently told Big Blue View that he thinks Thibodeaux re-signing is possible.

"There is a real sentiment inside that building that Kayvon Thibodeaux has turned the corner," Schultz said. "I do believe ... there is a scenario in which Kayvon Thibodeaux is there [beyond 2026]. And I never would have thought that previously."

OK, so what about Thibodeaux's production? (That, of course, will be a big factor in his potential return, along with the Burns/Carter/Reese factor.)

In his first four seasons, he had four, 11.5, 5.5 and 2.5 sacks. The past two years, he was limited to 12 and 10 games, which impacted his overall numbers. His yearly pressure totals: 45, 43, 38 and 32.

Thibodeaux posted solid but not spectacular Pro Football Focus pass rushing grades his first three years: 67.3, 62.9 and 72.0. Then he dipped to 58.0. This season, he is at 58.1, with 18 pressures and zero sacks.

Thibodeaux began the season as a backup, with Burns and Carter starting. The first three weeks, he played 46%, 51% and 66% of the snaps. Then Burns suffered a season-ending knee injury late in the Week 3 win over the Titans. So Thibodeaux was thrust back into a starting role.

Let's see what he can do with it down the stretch.

Thibodeaux will get a second contract from someone next offseason. If it's not the Giants — and if he gets paid enough elsewhere — Harbaugh could land a 2028 compensatory draft pick tied to Thibodeaux leaving.
