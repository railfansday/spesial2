Jordan Love struggles in Week 12 loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Green Bay Packers QB Jordan Love completed 17 of his 29 passes for 198 yards, a touchdown and two interceptions against the Los Angeles Rams in Week 12. He also had a seven-yard carry.

Why it Matters: Love had been on a bit of a hot streak, but he has struggled the last two weeks, throwing just a single touchdown in each game. He also had his first game of the season with multiple interceptions Wednesday night. Fortunately for Love, a Week 13 meeting with the New Orleans Saints should be just what the doctor ordered.
