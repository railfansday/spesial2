Bengals QB Joe Burrow cooked Steelers so hard, fans got a Joe Flacco-Aaron Rodgers faceoff

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Sunday Night Football showdown became a showcase of 40-and-older quarterbacks toward the end. Joe Flacco and Aaron Rodgers wound up having a faceoff in the end. But that's because Cincinnati Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow cooked the Pittsburgh Steelers.

Flacco stepped in for the red-hot Burrow in the fourth quarter. And after Burrow shredded Pittsburgh with 409 yards and four touchdowns. Cincinnati rolled to the 38-18 rout of the previous first place Steelers inside Paycor Stadium.

So Flacco stepping in brought back throwback AFC North vibes when he led the Baltimore Ravens. And he stepped in facing an old rival in the Steelers. Though Flacco holds the claim of playing for three of the four teams in the division.

Rodgers, meanwhile, endured a rough night on the passing end. He didn't complete a pass on his first seven attempts. Rodgers finished with 15 completions out of 29 attempts, hitting only 143 yards. Rodgers tossed two touchdowns but both came in the fourth quarter; one to DK Metcalf for 22 yards and the other to tight end Pat Freiermuth on a five-yarder.

How dominating was Burrow? ESPN Insights dropped this startling stat after the game.

"Joe Burrow had 21 completions tonight before Aaron Rodgers recorded his first," the account posted on the social media website X, formerly Twitter. "That is tied for the most completions by a team before their opponent recorded their first completion since 1978 (as far back as play-by-play data is available)."

Now Burrow has the Bengals thinking playoffs again with their 7-2 mark. And now the AFC North belongs to them for now.
