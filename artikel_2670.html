How Jason Kidd is approaching his ESPN NBA Countdown debut

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

How Jason Kidd is approaching his ESPN NBA Countdown debut appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The former NBA star Jason Kidd is preparing for a new chapter in basketball, stepping into the broadcasting world as an ESPN analyst. Kidd is set to make his debut on NBA Countdown, bringing his experience as a player and coach to a new role on television.

Kidd shared his excitement in a post on X ahead of his first appearance on Wednesday. He explained that he plans to approach the opportunity with the same mindset he had when entering the NBA as a player. "I'm looking at it like I'm a rookie in the league. New team, doing something I haven't done before," Kidd wrote. He also praised Malika Andrews, Kendrick Perkins and Brian Windhorst for helping him adjust, adding that the ESPN production team has made the transition easier. "I'm learning from the best," he said.

The opportunity gives Kidd another way to stay connected to the game after building a lengthy career as both a player and a coach. His experience across different eras of the NBA should also bring valuable insight to the ESPN broadcast team.

Fans were quick to congratulate the NBA legend and share their excitement about his television debut. One fan recalled watching Kidd during his playing days, writing, "You were my favorite player to watch, still have all your rookie cards. Im sure you will be great."

Another supporter praised his approach, saying, "Congrats on the debut, Kidd. Going in as a rookie again is the right mindset."

Kidd's comments suggest he understands that success in broadcasting requires a willingness to learn. Rather than relying solely on his basketball résumé, he is embracing the chance to start fresh. His first NBA Countdown appearance will give viewers an early look at what he can bring to ESPN's coverage.
