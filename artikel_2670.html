Pirates Add OF Chas McCormick on Minor-League Deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Pittsburgh Pirates have agreed to a minor-league deal with outfielder Chas McCormick, according to Ari Alexander of 7 News Boston. McCormick gets an invite to spring training.

McCormick, a native of West Chester, Pa., totaled five big-league seasons with the Houston Astros from 2021-25 and won a World Series in 2022. His best season came in 2023, when he batted .273/.353/.489 with 17 doubles, two triples, 22 home runs, 70 RBI and 19 stolen bases in 115 games.

Through his first three seasons with the Astros, McCormick compiled a robust .259/.336/.449 batting line with 41 doubles, four triples, 50 home runs, 164 RBI and 27 steals in 342 games. But from 2024-25, he batted just .211/.273/.301 with 11 doubles, one triple, six homers, 32 RBI and 10 steals in 158 games.

The 31-year-old spent the 2026 season with Triple-A Iowa in the Chicago Cubs organization. He slashed .285/.383/.468 with 23 doubles, 14 home runs, 59 RBI and 17 steals in 102 games.

McCormick has experience in all three outfield positions in his career. He's totaled 245 games in left, 193 in center and 121 in right and has combined for a .995 fielding percentage and 13 defensive runs saved.

The Astros originally drafted McCormick in the 21st round of the 2017 draft out of Millersville University of Pennsylvania.
