Nat Geo Explores The Unabomber’s Brother, Somali Pirates & Hantavirus In New Doc Slate

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

EXCLUSIVE: National Geographic is exploring the people and decisions behind pivotal events of the past three decades with its latest documentary slate.

The Disney-owned brand has greenlit three documentaries about the brother of the Unabomber, Somali pirates and the Hantavirus.

My Brother The Unabomber, Hantavirus: The Inside Story and Hijacked: The Real Captain Phillips will all premiere on Disney+, Hulu and Nat Geo.

“These are names and events everyone recognizes,” said Tom McDonald, EVP, National Geographic Content. “But behind each name is an extraordinary story. My Brother The Unabomber comes from David Abraham’s Wonderhood Studios. It tells the the story of a man faced with the impossible choice of turning in his own brother during the final year of the Unabomber manhunt. It features access to David Kaczynski, the brother of Unabomber Ted Kaczynski and previously unseen archive, including letters between the brothers and explores the decision that he and his wife Linda faced when they read his manifesto in the papers before reporting their suspicions to the FBI. Daniel Vernon directs, Jessie Versluys and Samantha Anstiss exec produce for Wonderhood Studios and Carolyn Payne exec produces for Nat Geo.

Hantavirus: The Inside Story comes from Passion Pictures and Muck Media and tells the story of the outbreak aboard the MV Hondius in the summer of 2026. Featuring hours of personal archive, the doc follows the passengers and scientists racing to find safety for the ship, trace the outbreak to its source and stop its global spread.

Darren Foster directs, Will Anderson and Hamish Fergusson exec produce for Passion Pictures, Foster and Yuna Ma exec produce for Muck Media and Sean D. Johnson exec produces for Nat Geo.

Hijacked: The Real Captain Phillips comes from Curious Films. The three-part series revisits the 2009 hijacking of the Maersk Alabama, when Somali pirates seized the American cargo ship, and the military rescue operation that followed. It brings together the experiences of Captain Richard Phillips and his crew with the rarely heard stories of the Somali pirates at the heart of the hijacking, and explores who the Somali men were, the circumstances in Somalia that drove them to sea, and how events unfolded from their point of view. It also features never-before-seen archive, including U.S. Navy footage.

Christian Collerton is series director, Dov Freedman, Jessie Versluys and Jamal Osman exec produce for Curious Films and Carolyn Payne exec produces for Nat Geo.
