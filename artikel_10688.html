Rockstar Issues Official Statement On GTA 6's PS5 Exclusive Features

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Rockstar finally tells PS5 Pro fans what they can expect on Sony’s fancier console

Grand Theft Auto 6 fans are so excited about the game that many of them already bought a PlayStation 5 Pro in anticipation of Rockstar's open-world adventure. Funnily enough, Rockstar hadn't really told anyone what PS5 fans could expect on Sony's souped-up platform until now, just over a week before the game's official Nov. 19 release.

First, let's discuss the standard features and tech specs all PS5 players will have access to. Rockstar says the game will include high-fidelity haptic feedback via the DualSense controller, and players can fine-tune those settings. The game will come with improved spatial audio rendering, which the developer says will take "full advantage of Tempest 3D audio."

This is the news GTA 6 fans have waited months to hear. Rockstar claims that the Pro platform will offer "breathtaking visuals" that take advantage of PlayStation's Spectral Super Resolution as well as ray-traced shadows.

For Xbox, the studio only noted vaguely that it would utilize Xbox's graphics settings. What that means is anyone's guess!

"And as with all cloud streaming, connection speeds and other technical factors may influence game performance in ways that would not impact local gameplay," Rockstar added.

Both platforms will support Remote Play. As Rockstar (and Sony) have done throughout GTA 6's marketing campaign, the developer outright says that GTA 6 plays "best" on PS5. This new feature breakdown for both platforms does little to dissuade that argument.

It gives fans a quick look at some of the game's party scenes and car chases. Otherwise, you can begin preloading GTA 6 in anticipation of its impending release.
