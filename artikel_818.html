Rodgers to be released after claiming Steelers coach was 'sleeping in meetings'

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers claims that head coach Mike McCarthy was 'snoring' in a quarterback meeting in an explosive interview; Steelers are set to release the four-time NFL MVP; Rodgers had been benched after back-to-back losses with Mason Rudolph taking his place

Aaron Rodgers is set to be released by Pittsburgh Steelers after making explosive claims about head coach Mike McCarthy.

Speaking on the Pat McAfee Show, Rodgers claimed that McCarthy had been "falling asleep in meetings" and insisted that the entire team felt a change was necessary following the veteran quarterback's decision to bench him for Friday's game against the Denver Broncos. Mason Rudolph is set to start in place of the four-time NFL MVP.

Pittsburgh confirmed in a statement that Rodgers would be "inactive", despite McCarthy having said on Monday that the 43-year-old remained the team's starting quarterback. Rodgers subsequently voiced his frustrations in a scathing interview, which has reportedly led to his release, according to US media reports.

"Mike and I talked for a long time the last few days, and that word [benched] never even came up," Rodgers said on the ESPN sports talk show.

"I want to take you back through my last week, and this is not an abnormal week what I'm about to tell you, but these are some of the issues that I've been dealing with, which ultimately led to where we're at now.

"Last Wednesday, we were in a quarterback meeting. Mike came in late, sat down, and within 10 minutes began snoring.

"He woke himself up, was rattling his papers, we all looked around. Five minutes later, you hear the snoring again. And less than five minutes after that, he walks out of the room like nothing happened.

"Thursday, he misses an offensive meeting. Friday, he doesn't come to the walkthrough. We only have a walkthrough, really, on Friday.

"He doesn't come to the protection walkthrough on Thursday. Saturday, he misses the morning meeting, and we don't have a team meeting Saturday night, for whatever reason, in Philly.

"Then comes to Sunday, when obviously we struggled and the product wasn't great. And there was some guys early on that didn't like the way things were going, especially defensive guys. And Mike didn't want to get in the middle of defense.

"At one point, I told him that he really needed to make more of an effort to connect with the defensive players and engage with them. His response was, 'Well, I don't think I need to.' I disagreed, and I think that attitude had already caused him to lose the support of a lot of those players.

"I think the final straw for many of us came at half-time. Towards the end of the team talk, with the offence on one side and the defence on the other, he was rambling. Then, without any warning or context, he suddenly shouted, 'One, two, three, Packers!'

"It was such a strange, out-of-the-blue moment. Nobody had even mentioned the Steelers; it was just an awkward, disjointed speech. But he said it loudly enough for everyone across the entire locker room to hear. It was one of those moments that really stuck with people.

"We all looked around going: 'What is going on? Where's his head at?'"

Rodgers is currently in his 22nd and final NFL season, having reunited with McCarthy after the pair previously won the Super Bowl together with the Green Bay Packers in 2010.

He also played a key role in McCarthy's appointment as Mike Tomlin's successor.

"We've had these conversations for months now, and it began as a concerned friend about somebody who's unhealthy," Rodgers continued.

"If you're sleeping in meetings, you're unhealthy. You're not coming to everything you should be. How many quarterbacks around the league are dealing with the same type of stuff?

"This guy's calling the plays. And so Monday we're having this conversation and I just said: We've got to make a change for the betterment of the team.'

"The entire team, and offensively think that a change is needed because they see it."

Rudolph has started only 19 games in seven seasons with the Steelers and the Tennessee Titans.

Sky Sports has contacted Pittsburgh Steelers for a response to Rodgers' comments.
