NASA, European sea level satellite prepped for new role

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

As of Oct. 30, the Sentinel-6B satellite has been calibrated to serve as the world's go-to data source on sea level rise. The satellite has now passed all its initial system checks to ensure that its data meet the strictest quality and accuracy standards. In January, it will become the reference satellite that other sea-level missions use to calibrate their data.
