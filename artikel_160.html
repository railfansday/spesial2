If you’re tired of constantly scrounging around for your lost belongings, Apple’s second-gen AirTags are on sale for an all-time low of $18 at Amazon, Walmart, and Best Buy. The handy location trackers have a list price of $29 each, but have been consistently available for around $24 since Amazon Prime Day in late June.

The upgraded ultra-wideband chip and improved Bluetooth in the latest AirTag should provide more precise location tracking, with a range 1.5x wider than the first-gen model. The internal speaker is also 50 percent louder than the original, making it easier to find whatever item your AirTag is attached to, whether it’s buried deep in your couch cushions or at the bottom of your bag. Even without those improvements, though, AirTags are still a great option for Apple users looking for their keys or luggage, with up to a year of battery life on a single coin cell battery and compatibility with both iPhones and newer Apple Watch models.
