Veteran goalkeeper Alisson Becker hopeful of signing a new contract with Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

LIVERPOOL, England (AP) — Veteran goalkeeper Alisson Becker wants to extend his contract with Liverpool and win more trophies with the club.

The 34-year-old Brazilian has made 347 appearances for Liverpool since joining from Roma in 2018, winning two Premier League titles, one Champions League, and one Club World Cup. He can leave as a free agent next June.

"I'm focused on playing every game but in my mind I'm happy to stay," he said after Liverpool's 3-1 win against Crystal Palace in the Premier League on Sunday. "That depends on a lot of aspects and different situations. But my will is to stay, I'm happy here and my family is happy here."

Although he has not been offered a new contract he hopes that will be the case, with his agent currently in discussions.

"I'm still hungry for titles and to delivering good performances," he said. "I still want to play at the top level for as long as I can, so that's my thinking." ___
