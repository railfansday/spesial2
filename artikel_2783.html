Luke Moga Shines in First Career Start as Lobos Secure Second Straight 9 Win Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

ALBUQUERQUE, N.M. (KRQE) –The New Mexico Lobos are making history. With their latest victory, the Lobos have secured their second consecutive nine-win season for the first time in program history and clinched a spot in the Mountain West Championship Game.

A major storyline was quarterback Luke Moga, who earned the start over Toa and delivered in his first career start.

Moga helped lead New Mexico on an opening drive that converted six of nine third-down opportunities, capping it off with a five-yard touchdown.

He finished the game completing 22 of 40 passes for 234 yards — a career high and the second-most passing yards by a Lobo quarterback this season.

Head coach Jason Eck praised Moga's performance, particularly his ability to protect the football while executing a game plan built around his strengths. Eck also emphasized that the young quarterback has plenty of room to improve after his first full college game.

The Lobos will close out the regular season at home against Air Force on Friday at 2:30 p.m. before turning their attention to the Mountain West Championship Game.
