Pederson’s game-winning goal leads Reign to third consecutive win

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

ONTARIO, CA. -- For the second night in a row, the Ontario Reign gave their fans something to smile about - another home win.

In a back and forth competition, it was Lane Pederson who scored the game-winning goal to lift the Reign to a 4-2 victory over the Colorado Eagles.

For a team who came into the matchup 10-for-63 on power-play goals, tonight Ontario would prove that their power play had some refinement.

The Reign went 2-for-4 on the power play and killed three of four penalties.

After Ontario's recent six-game losing streak, to finally have a winning streak again - especially at home - is meaningful to the success of the team this season.

And having players loaned back from the Los Angeles Kings didn't hurt either.

"I think we learned some valuable lessons during that stretch (of six losses)," Reign coach Andrew Lord said. "I think we're playing the right way right now. I think we're defense first, I think our puck management's pretty good. Obviously a couple power-play goals helped tonight and it feels like a confident group."

Carter George earned his fourth win tonight with 23 saves.

Although George is only 20 years old, Lord said talking to him feels like talking to someone five years older.

"(George) is just so solid, so calm, so composed," Lord said. "(He) reads the game at an elite level, doesn't seem like anything ever phases him, it's really impressive."

Koehn Ziemmer found the back of the net 9:24 into the first period, with help from Scott Perunovich and Taylor Ward, the Reign had a 1-0 advantage.

But the Eagles (8-8-1-1; 18 points) weren't behind for long.

Two minutes following Ziemmer's goal, Sean Behrens and Cooper Gay passed the puck to Nikita Prishchepov who evened the score with a goal of his own.

Taylor Makar's power-play goal that came halfway through the second would be Colorado's last score of the game, with Ontario's defense shutting them out for the remaining 27 minutes.

Perunovich tied the game as he found the back of the net for the first time this season.

Kaleb Lawrence and Taylor Ward earned assists on Perunovich's power-play shot.

The Eagles took their goalie off the net in the final minutes, in hopes to tie the game after Pederson's go-ahead goal.

But with 54 seconds left, assists from Francesco Pinelli and Angus Booth gave Cole Guttman an empty-net shot as cheers erupted for Ontario's third consecutive win.

Colorado goalie Trent Miner saved 22 shots on goal and the Eagles went 1-for-4 on the power play.

Ontario (8-7-1-0; 17 points) will face Abbotsford (9-4-0-3; 21 points) for the fourth time in 12 days on Wednesday at 7 p.m. at Toyota Arena.

The Reign defeated the Canucks 5-2 Saturday night and they're 2-1 against Abbotsford this season.
