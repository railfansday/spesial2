Who is playing in NFL Week 12? Here's the full schedule

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Who is playing in NFL Week 12? Here's the full schedule originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

Let's get ready for a week of family, food and football.

The NFL is taking over Thanksgiving week with a packed schedule of 16 games across five days and no teams on bye. There will be games on Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Sunday and Monday as the league filled the calendar with star-studded matchups.

What's in store for Week 12? Here's the full schedule ahead:

The week opens with the first-ever Thanksgiving Eve game between two teams coming off their bye. Matthew Stafford and the Los Angeles Rams (6-4) will host Jordan Love and the Green Bay Packers (6-4) in the Wednesday night affair, streaming on Netflix.

As is tradition, the Thanksgiving lineup has three games with two familiar hosts in the afternoon slots. First up, the Chicago Bears (8-2) will visit the Detroit Lions (8-2) with first place in the NFC North on the line. The Dallas Cowboys (5-6) will then host their division rival Philadelphia Eagles (3-7) before the primetime game on NBC and Peacock — Patrick Mahomes and the Kansas City Chiefs (8-2) visiting Josh Allen and the Buffalo Bills (9-1).

For the fourth annual Black Friday contest of Prime Video, Aaron Rodgers and the Pittsburgh Steelers (6-4) will host Bo Nix and the Denver Broncos (8-2) in a critical conference matchup.

Sunday's action will include six games in the early window and three in the late window.

The standout games on Sunday afternoon are the New Orleans Saints (3-7) at Cincinnati Bengals (7-3) and Baltimore Ravens (4-7) at Houston Texans (4-6) in the 1 p.m. ET slate, then the Seattle Seahawks (6-4) at San Francisco 49ers (9-1) in the late window.

NBC's Sunday Night Football will be a battle of surprise AFC teams. No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza and the Las Vegas Raiders (4-7) will visit Deshaun Watson and the Cleveland Browns (6-4).

Week 12 will conclude with an NFC South showdown on Monday Night Football. Baker Mayfield and the Tampa Bay Buccaneers (3-7) will look to sweep the season series against Bryce Young and the division-leading Carolina Panthers (5-5).
