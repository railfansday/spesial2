NFL Injury News: 49ers’ Brock Purdy dealing with ankle injury ahead of Seahawks game

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

As the Seattle Seahawks prepare to welcome Sam Darnold back into the starting lineup, their Week 12 opponents could be missing their MVP favorite quarterback.

San Francisco 49ers star Brock Purdy torched the Minnesota Vikings in a magnificent showing last Sunday in Mexico City, but it did come at somewhat of a cost. Head coach Kyle Shanahan told reporters that Purdy won't practice on Wednesday due to an ankle injury, which he described as not a high ankle sprain, but one that will limit his reps for the week.

Shanahan also added that the injury didn't affect him during the Vikings game.

It's the first of three practices during a holiday week, so there could be a lot more erring on the side of caution mixed in with some gamesmanship from Shanahan regarding Purdy's Sunday availability. This is nevertheless something that should be on Seattle's radar heading into Sunday afternoon in Santa Clara.

If Purdy cannot go, then the very capable Mac Jones would replace Purdy as the starter. Jones was 5-3 as a starter last year and is comfortable running Shanahan's offense, so this level of backup QB against the Seahawks defense would be a lot like Marcus Mariota in terms of a veteran presence.
