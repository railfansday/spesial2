Carnegie, Woliczko headline Iowa women's basketball reactions in opener

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

No. 10 Iowa women's basketball (1-0) opened its 2026-27 campaign inside Carver-Hawkeye Arena with a 73-45 win over Towson (0-1) on Monday night.

Dani Carnegie led the way with 21 points on 7 for 15 shooting. Point guard Chit-Chat Wright scored 14 points and forward McKenna Woliczko added 10 points to round out Iowa's double-figure scorers.

Woliczko added a team-leading eight rebounds, three blocks, one assist, and one steal in 24 minutes in her collegiate debut.
