Napoli show interest in Paris FC midfielder Patrick Zabi

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

SSC Napoli are reportedly interested in Paris FC's Patrick Zabi (20), according to Sky Sport Italia.

Struggling at the start of the season, the Neapolitans – who sit 10th in the Serie A table after 11 matches – are keen to use the winter transfer window to strengthen their squad.

With this in mind, the Azzurri are said to have identified the Ivorian midfielder as one of their potential targets. Having joined PFC from Stade de Reims for the – hefty – sum of €20m last summer, Zabi, despite being regarded as a player with great potential, is currently struggling to establish himself in Liam Rosenior's first-team line-up.

Although he has made eight appearances (providing one assist) in the first 10 matchdays of the Ligue 1 season, the Yopougon-born player has yet to make a single start. This is a situation Napoli would like to capitalise on to convince the Ivorian international, who has earned two caps, to move to southern Italy.
