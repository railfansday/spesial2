Dez Bryant goes off on Cowboys in NSFW rant after implosion vs. Eagles: ‘Get everybody the f–k out’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Dez Bryant didn't hold back after watching his former Cowboys team author the largest Thanksgiving collapse in NFL history.

The legendary wide receiver, who was in attendance in Texas, was calling for Cowboys owner Jerry Jones to clean house as the team was in the process of blowing a 21-point third-quarter lead with a calamity of a fourth quarter.

Bryant doubled down after Jake Elliott's 59-yard field goal with three seconds remaining gave the Eagles an improbable 39-38 win at AT&T Stadium on Thursday.

Dez Bryant Getty Images for Fanatics Sports

"Jerry, Jerry, Jerry, everybody got to get the f–k up out of here," Bryant, who played eight years on Dallas from 2010-2017, said in a video posted to social media. "F–k that. That's completely unacceptable. That is unacceptable. F–k that

"Get everybody the f–k up out of here. Fire everybody. Fire'm all. F–k it. Fire'm, get everybody the f–k out. F–k that. Fire everybody."

The Cowboys were as much to blame for losing the game as the Eagles were for winning, as they surrendered a blocked punt for a touchdown early in the fourth quarter, watched usually automatic kicker Brandon Aubrey miss from 40 yards on a kick that would have given them a five-point lead with 3:27 remaining in the game.

Dallas (5-7) chose to try to put the game away by going for it on fourth and 1 from the Eagles' 41, but Dak Prescott was sacked, setting up Philly's last-gasp effort.

Dallas head coach Brian Schottenheimer speaks at a press conference after the Cowboys' 39-38 stunning loss to the Eagles on Nov. 26, 2026, in Arlington, Texas. AP Photo/Sam Hodde

The loss denied the Cowboys a chance to get to .500 and keep themselves in the thick of the NFC East race, which should put coach Brian Schottenheimer further on the hot seat.

Jones called the loss a "bitter disappointment" to reporters. He said that a coaching change is "not a thought on my mind at this particular time" and that there are "several more games to go and we have to have our mind on that."

Dallas owner Jerry Jones speaks to the media after the Cowboys' stunning Thanksgiving loss to the Eagles. AP Photo/Sam Hodde

Former Cowboys QB Joe Milton threw for 331 yards, two touchdowns and one interception for the Eagles (4-7), who kept their slim playoff hopes alive.

DeVonta Smith caught 11 passes for 200 yards and two scores as the Dallas defense continues to struggle.
