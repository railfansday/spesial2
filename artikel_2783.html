Chicago Bears 1-on-1: Can Theo Benedet neutralize the Saints’ Chase Young?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

On paper, the New Orleans Saints might be the worst team the Chicago Bears have faced since Week 4 against the New York Jets. The Saints rank 15th in the league in scoring offense at 22.4 points per game. They are 25th in the league in scoring defense at 26.2 ppg allowed.

They are an average to below-average team, but they might yet surprise the Bears.

The Saints still have a lot to play for, despite their 3-6 record. They remain only one game back in the paltry NFC South, and remain in the upswing of a franchise rebuild eager to get a taste of the postseason.

Their star player right now is WR Chris Olave, who is 8th in the NFL with 764 receiving yards. He has turned into a true WR1 in his age 26 season.

If after Chris Olave you are struggling to name many Saints players, that's understandable. Their roster is young and lacking in top-end talent. But there is something lurking beneath the surface that the Bears need to take very seriously.

New Orleans has two of the top four sack leaders in the NFL right now, with DE Carl Granderson boasting 8.5 sacks and his foil Chase Young sporting 8 sacks. 16.5 sacks between two players 9 games into a season is not a coincidence.

While Granderson may have the higher count presently, he typically lines up on the right side and will face Darnell Wright, who is… really freaking good. It's no given, but I think that Wright should be able to hold his own.

On the other side, though, is the far more interesting matchup. Chase Young, the revived 2nd overall pick in the 2020 draft, has found a new home in New Orleans and has really turned into one of the premier pass-rushers in the league. He gets the opportunity to line up on the Bears left side, which will likely be manned by Theo Benedet.

Benedet is hitting a stride himself and holds the 3rd best OT pass blocking win rate in the league entering Week 11 at 92%. He has earned the right to start at LT until Ozzy Trapilo can prove himself to be healthy enough to play like he did in 2025.

Still, Chase Young's physicality is elite. Just look at what he did against highly touted rookie Spencer Fano.

Benedet, as we know, was not a high-end draft pick. And while he has improved drastically in his third season with the Bears, he will have his hands full going up against Young. If the Bears are to win on Sunday, devising a plan to help Benedet will be crucial. It will also be important for Theo to step up and win 1-on-1s when his number is called.
