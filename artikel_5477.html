Pfizer is raising its sales forecast after beating earnings expectations

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The drugmaker's full-year revenue outlook is now $62 billion to $64 billion, up from $60.5 billion to $62.5 billion

Pfizer $PFE raised its full-year 2026 guidance for revenue and adjusted earnings per share on Tuesday after reporting third-quarter results that included higher sales of launched and acquired products.

The company now expects revenue between $62.0 billion and $64.0 billion, compared with the $60.5 billion to $62.5 billion range it had forecast earlier. That is a $1.5 billion increase at the midpoint. Pfizer also raised and narrowed its adjusted EPS guidance to $2.95 to $3.05, from $2.80 to $3.00. The EPS range absorbs a single $650 million charge tied to an in-licensing and collaboration deal with Innovent Biologics, which weighs on the figure by roughly 10 cents a share.

Third-quarter revenue was $16.6 billion, flat from a year earlier on an operational basis. Excluding Comirnaty and Paxlovid, revenue grew 7% operationally. Adjusted EPS was 87 cents, unchanged from the prior-year quarter. Net income was $3.24 billion, or 56 cents a share, down from $3.54 billion, or 62 cents a share.

Analysts expected adjusted earnings of 73 cents a share and revenue of $15.87 billion, according to CNBC. Pfizer's results exceeded both figures.

Revenue from launched and acquired products rose 32% on an operational basis. Eliquis revenue rose 25% operationally and Padcev revenue rose 55%. Paxlovid revenue fell 63%, Comirnaty revenue fell 29% and revenue from the Prevnar family fell 10%.

Chief Financial Officer Cecile Guegan said in a statement that the raised guidance reflects the company's year-to-date performance and confidence in its business. Chairman and CEO Albert Bourla said in a statement that the obesity program continued to advance and that recent milestones in oncology, immunology and vaccines show the potential of the pipeline.

Pfizer raised the midpoint of its full-year revenue guidance in August, when it set the $60.5 billion to $62.5 billion range. Guegan became interim CFO that month after Dave Denton left the role. The company said her appointment as CFO took effect Nov. 1.

Pfizer will host a conference call and webcast at 10 a.m. ET on Tuesday.
