SpaceX selected Qualcomm to supply technology for Starlink Mobile's U.S. 5G ground network

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Qualcomm said SpaceX plans to begin its nationwide U.S. rollout in 2027, with tens of thousands of sites under a multi-year agreement

SpaceX has selected Qualcomm $QCOM Technologies, a subsidiary of Qualcomm, to supply cellular infrastructure platforms for the terrestrial 5G network behind its Starlink Mobile service, Qualcomm said on Wednesday.

Qualcomm will provide its Dragonwing QRU100 and QDU100 platforms and commercial-grade Layer 1 software for Starlink Mobile's base stations, the company said. SpaceX plans to begin the U.S. nationwide rollout in 2027, with tens of thousands of sites deployed over the multi-year agreement.

Starlink Mobile will use Massive MIMO radio units with 64 transmit and 64 receive paths, according to Qualcomm. The company said the design is meant to improve coverage, capacity and spectral efficiency. Qualcomm described the collaboration as a commercial step toward a broader vision with SpaceX for AI-native 6G infrastructure.

"We shouldn't live in a time where phone calls are still dropping whether you're at home or on-the-go," Michael Nicolls, senior vice president of Starlink, said in a statement. He said SpaceX will use Qualcomm's expertise and platform "to accelerate our timeline to develop our base station end-to-end architecture."

Durga Malladi, executive vice president and general manager of technology planning, edge solutions and data center at Qualcomm Technologies, said in a statement that the companies are "advancing 5G today, with an opportunity to lay the foundation for AI-native 6G in the years ahead."

Malladi told reporters the network will rely on large cell towers instead of small cells, and that it marks Qualcomm's largest contract of this kind since 3G, according to Light Reading. He also said SpaceX will build the Layer 2 and Layer 3 software, up to the core network, on top of Qualcomm's foundation, according to Light Reading.

Qualcomm's earlier deployment for Vietnamese operator Viettel covers a few thousand sites, according to Light Reading.

The agreement's financial details were not made public, according to CNBC. SpaceX has yet to identify any tower-company partners, and it has not given an estimate of the additional towers required, CNBC said. Mobile carriers usually rent room on towers owned by firms including American Tower and Crown Castle.

Last month, SpaceX said it had agreed to acquire nationwide spectrum holdings covering the 800 MHz band, according to CNBC.
