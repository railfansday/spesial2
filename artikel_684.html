Charlie Collier and Anjali Sud Exit Fox As New Digital Structure Set at Company

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Sud is leaving for another opportunity amid the changes, which will see Mustafa Ozgen become Roku CEO, and Pete Distad add oversight of Tubi and Frndly TV.

Fox is restructuring its senior executive ranks in the wake of its Roku deal closing.

The changes will see some high-profile names exit the company, including Roku president of media Charlie Collier, and Tubi president Anjali Sud, who Fox says is leaving for another opportunity.

The changes were announced in a memo to staff from Anthony Wood and Paul Cheesebrough Friday.

Among the other moves will be elevating Mustafa Ozgen to CEO of Roku, with Pete Distad, CEO of Fox direct-to-consumer, adding Tubi and Roku’s Frndly TV to his oversight. Mike Bidgoli will become the new president of Tubi. Lisa Holme, head of content for The Roku Channel, will also report to Distad while also working with the programming team at Tubi.

Chris Handman, senior VP and general counsel at Roku, and Robyn Peterson, executive VP of AdRise, will also exit the company.

And to everyone across FOX and Roku who made this possible: thank you.Together we have premium live sports and news, a growing streaming portfolio, and a leading connected TV platform. More importantly, we have talented teams who know how to build, innovate, and deliver for viewers, partners, and advertisers. Grow Platform Share and Engagement: Expand connected TV device sales and platform engagement through affordability, simplicity and creative partnerships.2. Lead in Free Ad-Supported Streaming: Be a top destination for movies, TV shows, podcasts and creator content.3. Lead in Premium Subscriptions: Build a compelling destination and bundling ecosystem for premium subscriptions.4. Grow our Portfolio Globally: Use our combined strengths to expand on and off platform in the U.S. and internationally.For our first 100 days, we’ll organize around three core teams while we identify the biggest growth opportunities and determine the right long-term structure.RokuMustafa Ozgen will lead Roku as CEO.
