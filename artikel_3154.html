Orion S.A. (OEC -24.79%) reported third quarter results including net sales of $472.8 million and adjusted EBITDA of $66.8 million. Management stated that results included a $17 million benefit from European emission allowances that were finalized during the period. The company prioritized cash flow generation, reporting $46 million in free cash flow and reducing its net leverage ratio to 4.1x.
