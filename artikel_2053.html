Ex-scout shares verdict on Arsenal target Junior Kroupi leaving Bournemouth in January

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Eli Junior Kroupi is more likely to leave Bournemouth next summer than he is in January.

That is the opinion of experienced former Aston Villa, Tottenham and Everton scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 20-year-old attracted huge interest when he scored 13 Premier League goals last season, before a significant foot injury ruled out any potential summer move.

Kroupi has now returned to action and interest has begun to mount once more, with Arsenal among the most heavily-linked suitors.

The France youth international is under contract until 2030, with the Cherries under no pressure to sell him yet.

Marco Rose will hope that keeping hold of the talented forward will boost Bournemouth's chances in both the Premier League and the Europa League this season.

Arsenal will not be alone in their pursuit of Kroupi, should they attempt to sign him in January or next summer.

The Frenchman is also a target for European champions PSG, as well as several other European powerhouses.

"Well, they've certainly made an awful lot of money. Why should he leave in January? The kid was injured," King told Football Insider.

"He had an operation in the summer. I think they're bringing him back slowly, not rushing him back, because they certainly don't want to antagonise that injury that he got.

"It'll be slowly, slowly with him, but he showed what he could do, certainly against Leeds.

"I wouldn't have any intent in selling him in January at all. I'd want him to get back to full fitness and let's see his full potential at Bournemouth and then maybe look in the summer, see how the finances are.

"'We've brought him in reasonably. If he continues to progress as he is, he's going to bring us a lot of money.'"

Decision-makers at Bournemouth will be well aware of the growing number of rivals sniffing around their brightest assets.

Not just Kroupi, but Alex Scott is expected to leave in the coming months with Chelsea reportedly leading the race.

Antonio Silva, Adrien Truffert, Marcus Tavernier and Rayan are also attracting interest ahead of what could be a difficult year for the Cherries.

Bournemouth know they cannot keep all of their stars, but they will hope to squeeze as much as possible out of them.
