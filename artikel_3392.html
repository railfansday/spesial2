Nathan Fillion’s 9-Part Crime Thriller Officially Returns in First Footage for New Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

It's been six months since ABC's favorite procedural, The Rookie, aired its eighth season finale. And while many of ABC's shows have returned for the 2026-2027 TV season, The Rookie is one of those held for a midseason premiere. Fans still have several more weeks to wait, but the good news is that The Rookie Season 9 has an official premiere date. ABC is pairing the long-running procedural with its new spin-off, The Rookie: North, and they will air from 9 to 11 pm on Wednesdays beginning January 6.

When the flagship series returns for its ninth season, the LAPD squad will immediately jump into a high-stakes, two-part premiere to rescue newly engaged officers Tim Bradford (Eric Winter) and Lucy Chen (Melissa O'Neil) following their shocking kidnapping in the Season 8 finale. Alongside the main cast, Season 9 welcomes notable guest stars including Troian Bellisario. Immediately following at 10 pm, The Rookie: North introduces Jay Ellis as Alex Holland, a middle-aged man who joins the Pierce County Police Department in the Pacific Northwest after surviving a violent home invasion. Nathan Fillion is set to make a crossover appearance in the spinoff's debut.

The Rookie Season 9 is largely about Chenford, at least for the first two episodes. The duo was kidnapped in the Season 8 episode "The Bandit" by Heath Everett's (Jeffrey Vincent Parise) people. New footage reveals what comes next for the couple, and it's not pretty. The video, which you can watch below, is set to Wesley's narration as he tells his children a bedtime story, but the events mirror what's happening to Lucy and Tim. After the "princess" and "prince" are kidnapped, they are tortured by the "evil lord." Luckily for them, they have friends who love them, and those friends come to their rescue. But don't write the princess off just yet, because Lucy has been in a similar position before. You pick up things when you've been kidnapped by a murderous lord. You started late and you are not embarrassed about it. Nolan walks into the academy at forty-five surrounded by people half his age, and the show is honest that this is both his biggest disadvantage and the only reason he is any good at the job.

You are better at this than people expect and you intend to keep proving it. Chen goes from rookie to the best undercover officer in the division, which works because she is genuinely good at becoming somebody else and genuinely bad at pretending she does not care.

You are hard on people because you have decided that is what caring looks like. Bradford is introduced as the worst training officer in the building and slowly revealed as the one who takes the job most seriously, which are the same fact seen from two angles.

You said what you wanted and then you went and got it. Lopez wanted detective from the first episode, said so out loud, and got there by being better at the work than the people who were quieter about wanting it.

Bellisario is switching sides by becoming a rookie after playing a TO in On Call. She also steps behind the camera, directing on The Rookie, to play Stephanie Monroe, a thirty-something single mom looking to reinvent her life by becoming a police officer. She's paired with Nolan, who has done that before. Psych alum Dulé Hill will also guest star as Lt. Hughes, a Watch Commander in Tim's absence. Meanwhile, The Irrational's Molly Kunz is Quinn, a small-town girl chasing her EMT dreams in Los Angeles.
