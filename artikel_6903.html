Kim Kardashian Gives New Life to Elizabeth Taylor’s Iconic Emerald-and-Diamond Bulgari Necklace

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The dazzling piece was gifted to Taylor on her 30th birthday

If there’s one thing Kim Kardashian loves, it’s an Old Hollywood icon. There was, of course, the time she wore Marilyn Monroe’s “Happy Birthday, Mr. President” dress to the Met Gala (one of many nods she’s made to the blonde bombshell). But her references extend further, to other classic figures of the silver screen, like Audrey Hepburn, Grace Kelly, Sophia Loren, and Lauren Bacall. Still, her biggest muse may be Elizabeth Taylor.

In a new Instagram video, Kardashian documented a trip to the Bulgari store in Rome, Italy, where she was given the opportunity to try on the emerald-and-diamond necklace that was gifted to Taylor by Richard Burton on her 30th birthday.

Joined by her mother, Kris Jenner, Kardashian is seen in the clip trying on the pendant and its matching earrings, both of which she later wore for a photoshoot that was organized by her stylist, Law Roach, as a part of the ongoing All’s Fair season two press tour.

As she talks with the Bulgari associates, Kardashian reveals that she actually got the chance to see this necklace in person immediately following Taylor’s death. “When she passed away, I was brought into her home [and] shown her safe with all of her jewelry before they cleared it out,” Kardashian says.

“So, I was able to see everything in her home and her jewelry still in her safe and all of her stuff still [there], her furniture. I was able to go in there like right after she passed,” she continues. “I know that sounds insane, but I was able to see this stuff in her purple closet.”

Taylor has seemingly long been an inspiration for Kardashian, who, just last year, wore the late actor’s Lorraine Schwartz 15-carat diamond and platinum earrings while walking in Demna’s final couture show for Balenciaga.

Prior to Taylor’s passing, she and Kardashian even sat down together for a joint conversation with Harper’s Bazaar, where the Skims founder declared: “I am a huge Elizabeth Taylor nerd.”

Talk about a pinch-me moment. In the video, Kardashian even calls Law Roach to thank him. She says, “But this is like the best birthday present ever… Now I can start my year with this Elizabeth energy.”
