Some of my experience using AI for financial concerns:
- it came up with the same numbers for army tax return as the accountant
- it had the same answers to my questions about what is deductible, to what extent, and how various gains/losses would affect my return.
- it identified a series of mistakes in my 709's. When the first mistake was acknowledged and corrected, he introduced additional mistakes.
- it did a far better job of coming up with and analyzing tax optimization strategies than my accountant was able to do, and was able to present them in a manner that was far more useful and informative.

The last two issues has resulted in me starting a search for a new accountant. Current accountant is a senior person at a respected mid sized firm, has a CPA, significant experience in this area, and has a team of analysts who back him up, and he personally reviews all information.

I will still continue to use an accountant but I will always check their work with an AI.

AI's make mistakes. So do people. Use both.
