Matt Damon’s 172-Minute War Epic Is Officially Returning to Theaters

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

There should be no doubt that 2026 was the year for movies. Every few months delivered the next big thing, from sci-fi to drama to war movies. One of the most anticipated was The Odyssey, a new feature from Christopher Nolan that may be considered his magnum opus in years to come. Finally dispensing with the dead wife narrative, he wrote an emotionally compelling part for Anne Hathaway while also adapting one of the most famous stories ever told.

One of the biggest hurdles was making Matt Damon’s version of Odysseus palatable for a modern audience. The Odyssey takes huge archetypes and molds them to be relatable and more human than their divine counterparts. Acting, music, and cinematography all came together in a story that is miraculously heading back to theaters. On December 11, fans can relive the horror of war when The Odyssey returns to 10,000 screens in IMAX and IMAX 70mm.

There have been many attempts at adapting The Odyssey before, but none have quite got to the heart of what it means to be human. Though experts were free with the criticism of Christopher Nolan’s interpretation of Homer’s epic poem, the director was free to develop a story that would speak to modern viewers. Many aspects wouldn’t translate; the biggest of all was investing in a belief that Odysseus would really not find a way home for 20 years.

This question is answered in the emotional character arc that takes the Trojan War hero from place to place. One of the best sequences of the film was the infamous invasion of Troy and the iconic horse that comes with it. Sneaking into Troy via a hollow wooden horse was Odysseus’ brainchild, and he becomes traumatized by what his decision wreaked. The scene shows the destruction of the city and the looting and murder that always come with war.

Zendaya’s performance as a priestess who gets beheaded in the siege is one of the most emotional moments of the movie. Odysseus never forgets the lives he was responsible for taking, even if it was indirectly. He feels that he doesn’t deserve to get home because of his crimes, putting a fine point on the PTSD narrative that runs through the film. The Odyssey provides a modern context for viewers to see these characters not as archetypes, but as people.

While Nolan certainly took creative liberties with the material, there is no denying that this is one of the best movies of the decade. The performances from Matt Damon, Zendaya, Anne Hathaway, Robert Pattinson, and Tom Holland were a masterclass of acting that comes together in the final act. Even in an age where movie length fatigue is real, not one minute of The Odyssey’s close-to-three-hour runtime is wasted. It all culminates in Odysseus finally accepting that he can come home after the horror of war as well as the loss of his men. The Odyssey will prove to be a modern classic that fans can see once again on the big screen.
