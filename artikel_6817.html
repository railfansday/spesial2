Here Are the 6 Remaining Couples Going Into Semifinals on ‘Dancing With the Stars’

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

We only have a few more weeks until Dancing With the Stars season 35 wraps things up, and the show is heading to semifinals! Grammy Night delivered an incredibly shocking elimination, and if you need a reminder of who’s left in the competition, these are the remaining six couples (in no particular order):

With only six couples left, there’s obviously plenty of time for multiple routines in Dancing With the Stars’ two-hour window. So yep, next week dancers are expected to perform a Ballroom and Latin dance (presumably of their choosing), which means they only have seven days to learn two routines instead of just one per usual. A pretty huge task!

As a reminder, Jenna Dewan and Val Chmerkovskiy were voted off the show during Grammy Night, a shock that Jenna speculated came down to “online noise.” As she put it to Extra, “I know it’s hard to know how this all plays into it. I know this was a particularly tough week for a lot of the online noise and all stuff that comes with the show. I don’t know, I’ll never know how, or if, any of that plays into this. But I know that we went out there and had the best time of our life.”

Next week’s DWTS episode airs Tuesday night, November 17, at 8 p.m. ET on Disney+ and ABC.
