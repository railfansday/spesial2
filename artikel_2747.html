Browns Week 12 game gets flexed versus the Raiders

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

As expected by many, the Cleveland Browns winning record and AFC playoff standing, their lack of primetime games is no more. The NFL has decided that the Week 12 matchup between the New England Patriots and Los Angeles Chargers was no longer worthy of Sunday Night Football. Instead, the Browns will face the Las Vegas Raiders at 8:20 PM on Sunday, November 29th.

Cleveland's schedule was loaded with 1 PM Sunday games, except for their Thursday Night Football contest with the Pittsburgh Steelers early in the year. Few expected the Browns to compete for anything more than a top 2027 NFL draft pick, especially after the team traded away Myles Garrett.

Now, heading into their bye week, Cleveland is 6-4, squarely in the AFC playoff hunt and a team that is wanted in primetime:

The Browns bye week now gets extended by about eight hours, with the eyes of the nation falling upon them, while fans will be "waiting all day for a Sunday night."
