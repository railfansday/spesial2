Falcons list inactives: Who's in, who's out vs. Bengals in Madrid?

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

We're 90 minutes away from the Atlanta Falcons and the Cincinnati Bengals' Week 9 matchup in Madrid, Spain, and both teams have officially announced their inactives list for today's game. The Falcons won't have starting kick returner Zachariah Branch or defensive back Billy Bowman Jr.

Branch has a hamstring injury and was ruled out by head coach Kevin Stefanski on Monday. Look for veteran wide receiver Olamide Zaccheaus to fill in for Branch. James Pearce Jr. returned to the team this week following his eight-game suspension for violating the NFL's personal conduct policy.

Pearce suffered a back injury during Friday's practice and was downgraded to questionable for Sunday's game. However, the star edge rusher is active. Pearce led the team with 10.5 sacks last season.

Sunday's game kicks off at 9:30 a.m. ET at Bernabéu Stadium in Madrid, Spain.
