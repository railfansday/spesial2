Buffalo Bills activating veteran WR off IR, per Joe Brady

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

There's been a development in the Buffalo Bills wide receiver room — Tyrell Shavers is set to make his 2026 debut after tearing his ACL in the wild-card win in Jacksonville last January.

Shavers, 27, burst onto the scene in 2025 playing 38% of the snaps after hardly seeing the field in his first two seasons with the Bills.

In Week 11 against the Buccaneers, he had four catches for 90 yards and a touchdown. Shavers blocked on 232 run plays a season ago as well. While not especially thick-framed, the former Alabama and San Diego State wideout is sizable at 6'4" and 211 pounds.

Shavers will be added to the 53-man roster. Ar'maj Reed-Adams being played on IR Tuesday made room for this move.

We'll see if Shavers, now 10 months removed from his ACL tear, is quickly inserted into the receiver rotation within Josh Allen's offense as early as Sunday against the Dolphins.
