NFL news, live updates: Aidan Hutchinson reportedly suffers torn ACL on sack celebration; Patriots QB Drake Maye out indefinitely with ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Another NFL Sunday is in the books, with just one game left in Week 10 as the Baltimore Ravens host the LA Chargers on "Monday Night Football."

Sunday kicked off with a bang as the Detroit Lions and New England Patriots faced off in Munich, Germany, with injuries to two stars that will have all eyes drawn as the new week plays out. Lions star defensive end Aidan Hutchinson suffered what is feared to be a season-ending ACL tear while celebrating a sack against Patriots quarterback Drake Maye … who suffered an ankle injury on that very sack, which knocked him out of the game and could cost him the season.

Week 10 also offered us our first look at Las Vegas Raiders No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza as a starter. He led scoring drives on each of the Raiders' first two possessions and entered halftime with a 10-7 lead over the Seattle Seahawks, but Drew Lock rallied with two passing touchdowns in the third quarter to take the lead, improving to 3-0 as a starter for Seattle this season. Raiders star tight end Brock Bowers exited the loss with an ankle injury.

Follow along with Yahoo Sports for the latest NFL news, injury updates and more coming out of Week 10.

The Steelers are expecting some wide receiver reinforcements soon.

Head coach Mike McCarthy announced earlier that the team would be opening the 21-day return windows for both Michael Pittman Jr. and Roman Wilson.

Pittman Jr. has been dealing with plantar fasciitis in his left foot and has been on IR since Week 5.

The veteran wide receiver has played in three games this year, coming down with seven of his 12 targets for 89 yards. Pittman is in his first year with Pittsburgh, after the franchise acquired him from the Colts and signed him to a 3-year, $59 million deal this offseason.

Wilson dislocated his finger in Week 5, and was added to IR. He is first eligible to return this week against the Eagles.

He had finally been building rapport with Aaron Rodgers when the injury happened. Wilson has brought in 14 of his 25 targets for 188 yards and two touchdowns.

During Wednesday's press conference, head coach Kevin O'Connell provided an update on wide receiver Jordan Addison.

O'Connell confirmed that Addison's thumb surgery was successful. He added that the wideout is not currently expected to have to go on short-term IR. Suggesting that the plan is for him to return within four weeks.

The Buccaneers could be without quarterback Baker Mayfield for Sunday's game against the Lions.

Mayfield is dealing with an injury to his foot that saw him sporting a walking boot last week during the team's bye week.

Head coach Todd Bowles did say that Mayfield is improving.

The Colts released their final injury report for Thursday night's game against the Texans.

TE Will Mallory, T Bernhard Raimann, DT Jerry Tillery, T Jalen Travis, and CB Charvarius Ward Sr. have all been ruled out.

Cornerback Sauce Gardner is listed as questionable, but did not practice this week. WR Anthony Gould, T Charlie Heck, and LS Luke Rhodes are also questionable.

Walker sat out Sunday's win over the Falcons with groin tightness. That opened the door for rookie Emmett Johnson to post a 101-yard rushing day

As far as Walker's availability for Week 11 against the Cardinals, head coach Andy Reid said Wednesday that the running back's groin is "getting better" and that they won't rush him back if they don't have to.

Nick Sirianni confirmed that Joe Milton III will make his second start of the season on Sunday against the Pittsburgh Steelers.

Milton is the third starting QB that Philadelphia has used. Starting quarterback Jalen Hurts remains sidelined indefinitely with blood clots in his lungs. Tanner McKee stepped into the starting role, but after going 0-3, Sirianni pivoted to Milton, whom Philadelphia signed off the Dallas Cowboys' practice squad.

In his first career start last week against the Giants, Milton finished 18 of 34 passing for 169 yards. He also added 64 yards on nine carries, but his effort wasn't enough to keep the Eagles from losing 19-11. Milton also lost a fumble.

In a corresponding move, the team is signing wide receiver Xavier Restrepo to the active roster.

Robinson suffered the ankle injury in the team's Week 10 loss to the Jacksonville Jaguars and could not return to the game. Restrepo was activated from the practice squad on game day and finished with 31 yards on three receptions.

Restrepo may be a familiar name to college football fans. He played for the University of Miami through the 2024 season, his final year with Cam Ward at quarterback. He finished that year with 1,127 yards and 11 touchdowns.

Parker's job as Dallas Cowboys defensive coordinator is safe for now, but he will reportedly no longer be calling the plays, according to ESPN's Todd Archer and Kimberly A. Martin.

Mike Pettine, who was added last month as a senior defensive assistant to support Parker, will have play-calling responsibilities going forward.

The decision comes after another poor showing by the Dallas defense during Sunday's 46-40 loss to the San Francisco 49ers. After the game, head coach Brian Schottenheimer didn't exactly show support for the 34-year-old Parker, who was hired in the offseason after two seasons on Nick Sirianni's staff with the Philadelphia Eagles.

Hainsey left Sunday's win over the Titans with a knee injury and he will now miss Sunday's game against the Giants, head coach Liam Coen said Wednesday.

According to ESPN's Jeremy Fowler, Hainsey is dealing with a knee sprain and is considered week-to-week. The injury is not expected to keep him out for the rest of the season.

Hainsey, 28, joined the Jaguars in March 2025 via free agency after three seasons in Tampa.

A decade of cold calls, patent applications and low-budget prototypes later, Rasmussen has made believers out of a growing contingent of NFL players. Dozens are now choosing to wear Caddix cleats on game days, a stunning breakthrough for a company that didn't begin selling cleats to the public until December 2024 and that Rasmussen says still has only four employees.

Caddix rocketed into the national spotlight in late October when Bears superstar quarterback Caleb Williams not only wore the brand's cleats but also refused to abide by NFL policy requiring he cover up a non-league sponsor's logo. The story ricocheted across the internet, especially after Rasmussen shrewdly offered to pay Williams' fines — $11,941 for the first "infraction," $23,882 for the second — and bought billboards around the Chicago area bearing the message, "Caleb, we'll pick up the tab."

When we last looked at the potential playoff field a month ago, the Minnesota Vikings sat in the No. 2 hole — and we had our share of questions about them.

Now the Vikings find themselves as the No. 5 seed in the projected playoff field, if the regular season ended today. Thankfully for Minnesota it does not. And yet, now that it has Kirk Cousins at quarterback, it feels like its potential is greater than ever this season.

On the flip side, the Detroit Lions sat in the seventh slot just a month ago. They're now on a five-game winning streak and have zoomed up to the No. 2 seed.

That part isn't the bad news, but Aidan Hutchinson's injury is, and it's hard to imagine Detroit not feeling the effects of it in a significant way, especially with the other three NFC North teams all currently in the playoff field.

Pittsburgh Steelers guard Mason McCormick is reportedly dealing with a dislocated ankle and could return in time for the playoffs, per NFL Network.

McCormick was carted off the field in Week 10, but reportedly did not sustain a fracture of his ankle.

McCormick will not need surgery, giving him a chance to return if the Steelers make a playoff run.

NFL Network's Ian Rapoport gave an update on some injuries to watch on Tuesday. It is still early in the practice week, but Rapoport mainly spoke about Kenneth Walker III and Baker Mayfield.

Walker missed last week's game due to a groin injury, but rookie Emmett Johnson balled out, so the Chiefs may not be rushing Walker back to the field.

Mayfield is coming off a bye, but was spotted in a walking boot last week at the facility. Rapoport said that he thinks Mayfield could miss this week's game, in which undrafted rookie QB Jalon Daniels would be back under center for Tampa Bay.

The Tennessee Titans are adding wide receiver Wan'Dale Robinson to injured reserve due to an ankle injury, per team reporter Jim Wyatt.

In a corresponding move, the team is signing wide receiver Xavier Restrepo to the active roster.

Robinson suffered the ankle injury in the team's Week 10 loss to the Jacksonville Jaguars and could not return to the game. Restrepo was activated from the practice squad on game day and finished with 31 yards on three receptions.

Robinson has played in all nine games for Tennessee this season. He has brought in 38 receptions for 272 yards and a team-high three receiving touchdowns.

Head coach Robert Saleh has not revealed the extent of Robinson's ankle injury. If the wideout returns this season, he would first be eligible to be activated off IR in Week 15, ahead of the team's game against the Indianapolis Colts.

Tennessee will be without Robinson for at least the games against the Dallas Cowboys, Jacksonville Jaguars, Washington Commanders, and Detroit Lions.

Miami Dolphins defensive tackle Jordan Phillips suffered a torn biceps in last week's loss to the Indianapolis Colts that will sideline the 2025 fifth-round pick for the remainder of the season, according to ESPN's Marcel Louis-Jacques.

Phillips is the third Miami DT to sustain a season-ending injury in the past two weeks, per Louis-Jacques.

The Dolphins are 1-8 under first-year head coach Jeff Hafley. That said, their young core has flashed promise, especially on the defensive side of the ball.

The Commanders announced Tuesday that they are opening the 21-day practice window for running back Jacory Croskey-Merritt.

He has been sidelined due to a groin injury since Week 5. The running back remains on IR and must be activated before he can play in a game for Washington.

The Commanders take on the Cincinnati Bengals this week for "Monday Night Football," giving JCM an extra day of rest to get up to speed.

Croskey-Merritt has had a rather unproductive season so far, but at times has shown how effective he can be. Through five games, he has 239 yards and two touchdowns on 67 carries.

The NFL has utilized another flex to switch around the Week 12 schedule, per ESPN's Adam Schefter.

The New England Patriots and the Los Angeles Chargers were previously scheduled for that week's "Sunday Night Football" matchup.

With the injury to Patriots QB Drake Maye, and the Chargers' 1-8 start, the NFL has moved the game out of primetime.

New England-Los Angeles will now kick at 4:05 p.m. ET. Moving the Titans and Jaguars game into a 1 p.m. ET kick.

The new SNF kick will feature the Las Vegas Raiders taking on the Cleveland Browns.

The bye week came at a good time for Chicago. Wide receiver Luther Burden III was placed in the league's concussion protocol following the team's last game against the Buccaneers.

Coming out of the bye week, though, ESPN's Courtney Cronin reports that Burden has cleared concussion protocol.

Cronin also added that Kyler Gordon is considered "day-to-day" by head coach Ben Johnson due to a shoulder injury also suffered against the Buccaneers.

The Texans are on a short week this week, taking on the Colts on Thursday night in an AFC South divisional matchup.

It's a tough time for Houston to not have many days to get some much-needed rest, as the Texans are looking like they could be down some impactful players.

KPRC2 Houston's Aaron Wilson reported on Tuesday that defensive end Danielle Hunter, RB Woody Marks and WR Marvin Harrison Jr. are currently expected to miss Thursday night's game.

The good news for Texans fans is that running back David Montgomery was practicing, and it looks like he is in a good spot to start Thursday night for the first time since injuring his groin in Week 5.

The Cleveland Browns have been through this type of season before. It has just been a long time.

In 1979, the Browns had seven fourth-quarter drives to win games. In the 1980 season they were 11-5 and nine of the wins came by one score. That group got the nickname of the Kardiac Kids. Brian Sipe became a Cleveland legend, and won the 1980 NFL MVP award.

The 2026 Browns aren't at that level yet, but they're getting close. After rallying to beat the Houston Texans on a touchdown with 53 seconds left, the Browns are the first team in NFL history to win each of its first six games by four or fewer points, according to ESPN Insights. Every one of those wins has included a game-winning drive in the fourth quarter. It has been a magical, improbable run.
