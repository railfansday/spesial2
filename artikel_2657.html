Seahawks' Nick Emmanwori vs Raiders' Bowers is marquee matchup of W10

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Seattle Seahawks safety Nick Emmanwori's matchup against Las Vegas Raiders tight end Brock Bowers will be the marquee individual battle of the game in Week 10. Emmanwori faces a difficult assignment against one of the league's most dangerous offensive weapons as Seattle looks to slow down Bowers in the Raiders' passing attack.

Emmanwori has recorded 34 total tackles, 23 solo tackles, two sacks and two passes defended this season. Meanwhile, Bowers has totaled 48 receptions, 658 receiving yards and six touchdowns in seven appearances.

Bowers is among the NFL's elite tight ends, presenting matchup problems with his combination of size, athleticism, route-running ability and reliable hands. His ability to create separation and make plays after the catch may put significant pressure on Emmanwori throughout the contest.

However, Emmanwori will have plenty of help. Seattle's defense features playmakers at every level, giving the young safety support in his efforts to limit Bowers. A coordinated defensive effort, particularly from the pass rush and secondary, may be crucial to keeping the Raiders' top receiving threat in check.
