Bills pull off fake punt, extend lead to 6-0

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Bills reached into their bag of tricks in the first quarter of Sunday's home game against the Dolphins and it paid off for them.

After a Josh Allen incompletion left them with a fourth-and-8 on their own 34, the Bills chose to roll the dice with a fake punt. Punter Tommy Doman arced a pass to an open Keleki Latu for a 30-yard gain.

The Bills would not move the ball any closer, but kicker Tyler Bass was good from 54 yards to extend Buffalo's lead to 6-0 with 5:27 to play in the opening quarter.

While the Bills lead, their offense has yet to get in gear. Allen is 2-of-8 and James Cook has four yards on four carries.
