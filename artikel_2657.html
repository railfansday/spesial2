Lighting error briefly leaves Commanders-Bengals ‘MNF’ game in dark as Roger Goodell looks on

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Billy Joel once sang about seeing the lights go down on Broadway, and now football fans can say they saw the lights go down at Northwest Stadium.

The "Monday Night Football" game between the Bengals and Commanders was briefly delayed after the lights went out in the middle of the third quarter just as Jayden Daniels was lining up to hike the ball on third-and-14.

Stadium lights turn off during the third quarter between the Cincinnati Bengals and Washington Commanders at Northwest Stadium. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

The ESPN broadcast initially reported that the issue was "power-related and not the stadium" and later added that it had occurred due to "regularly scheduled maintenance for 10 p.m." by the "lighting vendor" that was never disabled.

"It's a weekday thing, and they forgot it was a Monday night game," ESPN broadcaster Joe Buck said on the broadcast.

All the lights in the stadium went out at first, causing the refs to blow the whistle with 11:59 left in the third quarter. After several seconds, the electric signage that wraps around the stadium and the video board came back on, providing some light inside the Landover, Maryland venue.

"Somebody turned out the lights," Buck said.

The telecast showed Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow throwing a football around to keep warm.

NFL commissioner Roger Goodell was also on hand in the suit of Commanders owner Josh Harris.

ESPN threw to commercials after several moments and when the network returned, the lights also returned.

The Bengals were holding a 10-7 lead at the time of the power outage and when play resumed, Commanders quarterback Jayden Daniels was forced to throw the ball away. The Commanders rallied to win the game 22-20 on a walk-off field goal that came with some clock controversey.

The Cincinnati Bengals and the Washington Commanders stand on the field after the lights went off during a game in Landover, Md., Monday, Nov. 23, 2026. Getty Images

NFL commissioner Roger Goodell looks on as the lights go out during Commanders-Bengals. ESPN/X

Washington settled for a field goal that briefly tied the game at 10-10.

Monday's incident is just the latest to occur at the outdated home of the Commanders.

On Jan. 2, 2022, a railing broke at what was then known as FedEx Field sending several football fans tumbling in a near-tragic incident.

Players look on after the lights go out during the third quarter of the game between the Cincinnati Bengals and Washington Commanders at Northwest Stadium. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

Raw sewage soaked fans in one section of the stadium during a game against the Chargers after a pipe burst on Sept. 12, 2021.

The Commanders are building a new stadium at the site of the former RFK Stadium in Washington, D.C., that is scheduled to open in 2030.

Speaking with ESPN ahead of the game, Goodell expressed excitement over the new stadium.

"I'm a kid who grew up here. And so I remember going to RFK Stadium. And I think the work that they've done just on the architectural side of it and the construction of it, I think it's going to be remarkable," the NFL commissioner said, according to Front Office Sports.." And having it on the same site, I think it's going to have a wonderful emotional connection. Being in the District is special. And I just think that's going to be a wonderful moment, not only for the league but for the fans."
