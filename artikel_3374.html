Elden Ring Officially Reaches End Of An Era On December 28

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Lands Between are as alive as ever thanks to the recent release of Elden Ring: Tarnished Edition on Nintendo Switch 2, introducing a whole new generation of players to FromSoftware's beloved RPG. With new starting classes, equipment, and customization options for Torrent, it's brought veteran players back into the fold as well

There's also plenty more to look forward to beyond Tarnished Edition and the DLC, particularly with Alex Garland's highly anticipated live-action Elden Ring adaptation on the horizon. Backed by A24 with a massive reported budget approaching $200 million, the upcoming film could introduce the franchise to an even larger audience when it hits theaters in 2028.

However, while the future of Elden Ring looks brighter than ever, one short but sweet chapter in the franchise's recent history is coming to an end. December 28 will officially mark the conclusion of an incredible era for Elden Ring, and fans won't want to miss it.

December 28, 2026, will mark the end of Elden Ring's unique new Community Quests, a four-part event that has brought players together for a series of unique challenges in the Lands Between in celebration of the Tarnished Edition launch. The final quest, Path of Shadows, kicks off on December 1, giving fans one last opportunity to participate in the ambitious community-driven celebration before it comes to an end.

Path of Shadows focuses primarily on Shadow of the Erdtree, with players collectively tasked with gathering 1 million Scadutree Fragments, reaching the Abyssal Woods 15,000 times, drinking St. Trina's Nectar 10,000 times, collecting 70,000 Larval Tears, and obtaining 500,000 Revered Spirit Ashes.

Like the previous quests, completing these objectives will unlock exclusive commemorative artwork, with a special Twitch chat badge also available as a reward. Here's a look at the Path of the Tarnished and Path of the Cardinal Sin artwork:

These quests have been a fantastic way to encourage players to revisit Elden Ring, especially following the recent launch of Tarnished Edition. They've also given the community something to work toward collectively, with exclusive artwork and Twitch badges providing a unique reward beyond the game itself.

Although December 28 will be the last currently scheduled quest, it's entirely possible Bandai Namco could introduce another series of Community Quests in 2027, especially considering the opportunities to keep players engaged ahead of the upcoming film and based on the popularity of these inital four.

That said, nothing has been officially confirmed just yet, but fans should keep their eyes peeled for additional announcements in the new year. Hopefully, this won't be the last time Tarnished unite for a special journey through Elden Ring's Lands Between.
