Karol G Gets Record Room Entries to Her Celebration of ‘No Me Arrepiento de Sentir Tanto’ on TikTok Live

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The Colombian superstar's massive party featured guests including Greg González, John Summit and more.

Karol G celebrated her album No Me Arrepiento de Sentir Tanto with a massive party in Mexico City, an exclusive event streamed on TikTok on Wednesday night (Nov. 4), where millions of fans tuned in simultaneously. The Colombian superstar performed a variety of songs during the show and featured special guests such as Greg González, the group La Obsesión and DJ John Summit, who announced the remix of her hit “BbY WOW.”

“La Bichota” achieved a historic milestone as the artist with the highest number of simultaneous viewers on a TikTok Live, reaching 4.8 million stream entries on the platform and recording 3.6 million unique viewers, according to a press release.

Held at Quarry Studios with about 2,000 fans in the venue, the performance served as a prelude to the Latin American leg of her current Viajando Por El Mundo Tropitour, which kicks off with six shows in two of Mexico’s biggest stadiums: Nov. 6, 7 and 8 at Monterrey’s Estadio BBVA, and Nov. 13, 14 and 15 at Mexico City’s Estadio GNP Seguros.

The nearly hour-long celebration began at 8 p.m. local time with the songs “Maybe” and “Bebiendo lágrimas.” It followed with “Eclipse,” a piano rendition of “Final Feliz” and “Matadora.” Joined by Greg González, leader of the rock band Cigarettes After Sex on guitar, she performed “Después de Ti.”

“This is my favorite song from the entire album, so I hope you sing it really loud,” Karol G said as she introduced “Con Quién Andará?” while dancing with her fans.

Released Aug. 7 via Bichota/Interscope/Capitol, No Me Arrepiento de Sentir Tanto debuted at No. 1 on Billboard’s Top Latin Albums chart and spent 11 weeks at the summit. It also entered the Billboard 200 top 10, peaking at No. 8.

Karol performed a total of 11 songs during the event, including “Si Lo Ven,” a mind-blowing remix of “BbY WOW” alongside DJ John Summit. The original track, recorded with Judeline and rusowsky, has spent three weeks at No. 1 on the Latin Airplay chart and six weeks atop the Billboard Global 200 and Billboard Global Excl. U.S. charts. The setlist also included a remix of “AltaFarra,” featuring Mexican band La Obsesión and DJ Maracuya.

Following her North American trek, the Viajando Por El Mundo Tropitour now begins its journey in Latin America, with Mexico as one of its key stops before continuing to Europe.

Karol’s arrival in the country comes after a year of significant milestones in her career. At the 2026 Billboard Latin Music Awards, she took home four honors: Hot Latin Songs Artist of the Year, Female; Top Latin Albums Artist of the Year, Female; Latin Pop Song of the Year for “Coleccionando heridas,” with Marco Antonio Solís; and Fan Favorite Artist, a new category based on public voting.

These achievements add to her seven nominations at the 27th Annual Latin Grammy Awards, including in general categories such as album of the year for Tropicoqueta, as well as record of the year and song of the year for “Coleccionando Heridas.”
