Grammy Awards 2027 Nominations: See the Complete List

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The countdown to Music’s Biggest Night is on, now that the 2027 Grammy Awards nominations have been revealed.

Grammys CEO Harvel Mason Jr. and the Recording Academy’s Board of Trustees chairperson Dr. Chelsey Green revealed the nominees on Monday, November 16, with a star-studded crop of past honorees including Alan Menken, Carly Pearce, David Guetta, Kehlani, Olivia Dean, Pharrell Williams and Ziggy Marley.

The 2027 Grammys will be presented on February 7, 2027, at Los Angeles’ Crypto.com Arena, airing live on ABC. The broadcast will also be streamed on Disney+ and Hulu.

During the 2026 ceremony earlier this year, Lady Gaga, Bad Bunny, Kendrick Lamar, Billie Eilish and Lola Young took home trophies. Dean, for her part, won the coveted Best New Artist category.

“I never really imagined that I would be up here, let alone nominated, so thank you so much,” Dean tearfully said in her February acceptance speech. “I guess I wanna say I’m up here as a granddaughter of an immigrant.”

She continued at the time, “I’m a product of bravery and I think those people deserve to be celebrated. So, yeah, we’re nothing without each other.”
