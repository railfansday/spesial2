Eastchester, Putnam Valley, John Jay-East Fishkill win NY cheer titles

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Three Section 1 teams won state championships at the New York State Public High School Athletic Association Game Day Cheer meet Nov. 8 at Hudson Valley Community College in Troy.

In the competition, which was divided by enrollment, Putnam Valley won in Class C, Eastchester won in Class B and John Jay-East Fishkill won in Class A.

The victory for Eastchester was their third consecutive state title - a first in the sport since its inception at the state championship level in 2023. The win for Putnam Valley was its second straight state title and third in four years. And John Jay-East Fishkill won its first state title and the first ever for a Dutchess County school.

Cheer has two different competition seasons in New York. Game day cheer is for activities and routines - as is evident in the name - that happen on a game day. The other season is competitive cheerleading, more based on routines, which takes place in the winter, with state championships held in March in Binghamton.

Eastchester, as it was in its other two state championships, is coached by Stephanie Leone.

"What made this season so strong was our ability to evolve while maintaining the foundation of a successful program," Leone said. "We graduated nine seniors and welcomed 13 new athletes, but our depth and strong feeder program gave us an incredible foundation. Many of our athletes have already experienced high-stakes competition at the state and national levels, so they understand what it takes to compete at this level."

Eastchester's assistant coaches are Nicole Sallustio, Christine Roth, Danielle Pennachia and Amanda Bernardi.

Putnam Valley is coached by Kelly Wilson and assisted by D'Addona and Joseph D'Addona.

"The coaching staff is incredibly proud of what this group has accomplished, both on and off the competition floor," the staff said in a news release. "Their success is a testament to the athletes' dedication, the support they provide one another, and the commitment they bring to the program every day.

"The Putnam Valley Varsity Cheerleading Game Day Team has continued to raise the bar this season, demonstrating an incredible commitment to hard work, dedication, and excellence," Putnam Valley athletic director Rob Webb said.

John Jay-East Fishkill is coached by Alice Granger, with assistants Alyssa Martinez and Brielle Maxwell.

Rosters of all three championship teams are available on NYSPHSAA's web site.

Phil Strum is local sports editor for The Journal News/lohud.
