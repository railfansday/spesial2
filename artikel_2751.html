How to follow Las Vegas Grand Prix on the BBC

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The final triple header of the 2026 Formula 1 season starts in the United States with the Las Vegas Grand Prix.

First practice was a washout on Friday as rain stopped drivers getting any meaningful running on track. In the second hour with conditions still wet but drying, George Russell was quickest ahead of Alpine's Pierre Gasly and Ferrari's Charles Leclerc.

With the drivers' championship already won by Kimi Antonelli, second place in the standings is still up for grabs.

Max Verstappen holds that position and is 17 points clear of Russell, who moved back up to third place and one point ahead of Lewis Hamilton following his P4 at Interlagos.

Mercedes were also crowned constructors' champions in Brazil last time out to move level with Williams on nine titles.

Lights out for the 50-lap race on the Las Vegas Strip is at 04:00 GMT on Sunday (20:00 on Saturday local time).

Commentary of the race will be available across BBC Radio 5 Live, 5 Sports Extra, BBC Sounds and the BBC Sport website and app.

You can also ask your smart speaker to "play 5 Live", for example.

Make sure to listen to every episode of the Chequered Flag podcast. For the first time this year, the post-race show for every grand prix is available to watch on BBC iPlayer and YouTube.

Third practice: 00:30-01:30 - 5 Sports Extra

Race: 04:00 - BBC Radio 5 Live with build-up from 03:30

After a wet start to the Las Vegas weekend, the rest of the track action looks set to be dry.

Saturday's qualifying session and Sunday's grand prix both take place at night on the famous Strip, and conditions should be clear with light winds and temperatures about 13C.
