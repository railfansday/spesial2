Raiders surprising move bestows major respect on Fernando Mendoza

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Two starts into his NFL career, Fernando Mendoza has earned the distinction of being named a Raiders captain.

And he's in some pretty elite company, joining Maxx Crosby as one of three captains for Sunday's game against the Cleveland Browns. Special teams ace Dylan Laube is the third.

Las Vegas Raiders quarterback Fernando Mendoza celebrates a touchdown pass against the Denver Broncos in Denver, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. AP Photo/David Zalubowski

The Raiders select their captains on a weekly basis, based on a set of criteria established by first-year coach Klint Kubiak. Among the attributes Kubiak emphasizes are work ethic, performance, diligence and how players conduct themselves throughout the practice week.

That Mendoza has already checked enough of those boxes to earn the designation speaks volumes about how he's handled himself since taking over as the Raiders' starting quarterback.

Mendoza will make just his third career start Sunday night in Cleveland, where he'll look to build on the significant progress he made last week against the Broncos.

The first overall pick in April's draft completed 18 of 37 passes for 267 yards and three touchdowns while adding 22 yards rushing against one of the NFL's most formidable defenses.

Among his highlights were a 68-yard touchdown pass to Ashton Jeanty, a 19-yard scoring strike to tight end Ian Thomas and a seven-yard touchdown pass to Jack Bech.

Maxx Crosby of the Las Vegas Raiders reacts during a game against the Seattle Seahawks in Las Vegas on Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. Getty Images

Perhaps most impressive was how Mendoza kept his composure after the Raiders fell behind 17-0, engineering three consecutive scoring drives to pull them even in the third quarter.

There were some growing pains, including five sacks and a late interception that was returned for a touchdown. But the improvement from his starting debut against the Seahawks was unmistakable.

Now comes another significant challenge against a talented Browns defense in his first NFL prime-time start.

Dylan Laube (23) of the Las Vegas Raiders celebrates a tackle against the New York Jets in East Rutherford, New Jersey, Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026. Getty Images

The captaincy is noteworthy because, under Kubiak, it isn't automatically bestowed upon the starting quarterback. It's something that has to be earned, irrespective of salary, draft status or position.

The Raiders have entrusted Mendoza with the future of their franchise. He's already demonstrating an understanding of the responsibilities that come with it.
