Seahawks injury updates: Quenton Nelson, Abe Lucas ruled out of 49ers game

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Seattle Seahawks are set to release their first injury report for Week 12 against the San Francisco 49ers, but we can already rule out a couple of players before Wednesday's practice is even completed.

Mike Macdonald told reporters that left guard Quenton Nelson (knee) and right tackle Abe Lucas (ankle) are both out. Macdonald described Nelson as "close" to returning from his recent procedure, whereas Lucas' ankle injury suffered in Week 10 against the Las Vegas Raiders is not severe enough to land him on injured reserve. He considers Lucas' timetable in the range of "days to weeks."

Josh Jones, who started for Charles Cross at left tackle when Cross was out with his own ankle injury, will slide over to right tackle in Lucas' absence. Cross is expected to be back at left tackle after a two-game absence. It's worth noting that right guard Anthony Bradford (knee) will be limited on Wednesday, although that was what transpired for the Raiders game before he had full participation status on Friday.

Seattle signed Bobby Hart from the practice squad earlier this week to serve as additional swing tackle depth, while Chase Lundt was poached off the Buffalo Bills practice squad for guard/tackle versatility.

Stay tuned for Seattle's initial injury/practice report later this afternoon.
