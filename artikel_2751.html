Big 12 football power rankings after Week 12: Shuffling in the middle

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Who plays Utah in the Big 12 title game will be decided in the final week of the regular season, after Week 12 action confirmed that the Utes will play for the conference championship. Three conference games went to overtime to decide a winner, making it an exciting week across the conference.

Utah maintained its hold on the No. 1 spot with a convincing 38-17 road victory over TCU, while Texas Tech remained at No. 2 after defeating Baylor 20-10. BYU wasn't nearly as comfortable, needing three overtimes to escape Kansas with a 31-29 victory and keep its College Football Playoff hopes alive.

UCF made the biggest jump after a dominant 48-17 win over Iowa State that clinched the Knights' bowl eligibility. Cincinnati also climbed two spots after defeating Colorado 35-28, officially eliminating the Buffaloes from bowl contention.

With just one week remaining in the regular season, here's how all 16 Big 12 teams stack up in our latest conference power rankings following Week 12.

Utah Utes (38-17 win at TCU) -- No change

Texas Tech Red Raiders (20-10 win at Baylor) -- No change

BYU Cougars (31-29 3OT win at Kansas) -- No change

Arizona Wildcats (35-28 OT win at Kansas State) -- No change

Houston Cougars (34-31 win at West Virginia) -- No change

Oklahoma State (30-27 OT win at Arizona State) -- No change

Arizona State (30-27 OT loss vs. Oklahoma State) -- No change

Baylor Bears (20-10 loss vs. Texas Tech) -- Down 1

Cincinnati Bearcats (35-28 win vs. Colorado) -- Up 2

TCU Horned Frogs (38-17 loss vs. Utah) -- Down 2

West Virginia (34-31 loss vs. Houston) -- Down 1

Kansas Jayhawks (31-29 3OT loss vs. BYU) -- No change

Colorado Buffaloes (35-28 loss at Cincinnati) -- No change

Kansas State (35-28 OT loss vs. Arizona) -- No change
