Wirtz Continues His Good Form for Germany — and Other Nations League Group B News

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Florian Wirtz and the wider German side shone against a Virgil Van Dijk-less Holland in Tuesday's Nations League match — though both sides are headed to the Championship Playoff regardless of outcome. Wirtz scored a brace in Germany's confident win against a weakened Dutch side. His second goal, which made it 4-0 for Germany, was a confident strike: in off the crossbar, smashed in at close range. Wirtz also put one shot off target and saw a shot from distance blocked. The match finished 5-0 with Wirtz playing through to the 75th minute.

Cody Gakpo started for the Netherlands and played 79 minutes, managing just one shot from outside the box — though it was on target. Though he was far from the worst on the depleted Holland side, he failed to make much of an attacking impact and didn't make great choices when he was on the ball. Though he's semi-recently returned from a fitness issue, watching the Dutch side play made me wish he was taken off on 63' alongside Crystal Palace's Quinten Timber, as the added minutes seemed unlikely to add to his playing rhythm.

Elsewhere in the group, Kostas Tsimikas' Greek side beat Serbia, the team firmly at the bottom of Group 2. Though the win does put Greece level on points with Germany and the Netherlands, their goal difference keeps them in the relegation qualifiers rather than line to progress.
