Browns roster: 6 players on IR, 3 who could return in 2026 after the bye week

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Not much more than Cleveland Browns fans could ask for in 2026 than to be heading into Thanksgiving with the team at 6-4, in a playoff spot, and among the second tier of AFC contenders. Getting to enjoy the bye week while looking forward to Week 12's Sunday Night Football game adds to the peace and joy in the middle of November.

We've used the bye week to catch up on some things while also looking ahead. The Browns active 53-man roster has been mostly steady this year, while the 16-man practice squad has seen a lot of changes. GM Andrew Berry will likely continue to look for changes that he believes could help the team, which could include players returning from injured reserve.

As of now, Cleveland has six players on the IR list, but only three could return:

OL Teven Jenkins – A back injury limited him in training camp and then into the season before being placed on IR. Jenkins could return

FB Michael Burton – Placed on injured reserve last week, Burton's calf injury is not considered major, and he might be able to return this season, if needed

RB Raheim Sanders – A hip injury earlier in the year knocked the Browns backup back out, but a return is possible and even expected

DE Alex Wright – Out for the year due to a ruptured Achilles

DT Kalia Davis – Cleveland thought they would get Davis back, opened his practice window, then found an injury in practice that ended his possibility of playing for the team this season.

CB Damarri Mathis – A roster hopeful, Mathis injured his knee in training camp and is out for the year
