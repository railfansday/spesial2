'The Challenge' Star Tyler Duckworth's Cause of Death Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Challenge star Tyler Duckworth’s cause of death has been revealed.

The North Dakota State Forensic Examiner’s Office confirmed to Us on Friday, November 27, that Tyler died of hypertensive cardiovascular disease. His manner of death was ruled natural.

The Cleveland Clinic states that hypertensive heart disease can cause problems because of long-term, unmanaged high blood pressure. The added strain on one’s heart can lead to heart failure. (TMZ was first to report the news.)

News broke on August 13 that Tyler had died at the age of 44.

“My son, Tyler, passed away earlier this week,” Tyler’s mom, Jodi Duckworth, wrote via Facebook at the time. “Cause of death has yet to be determined. I will post on Facebook about services as soon as his dad and I figure it out.”

The Grand Forks Police Department later confirmed to Us that its officers had responded to a welfare check on August 11. When they arrived, Tyler was found unresponsive and determined to be deceased.

Tyler’s death came nearly two weeks after he appeared at The Challenge Mania event in Minneapolis. In his final social media post before his death, the reality star expressed how excited he was to interact with fans and meet with fellow competitors, including Paulie Calafiore, Mark Long and Rachel Robinson.

“So proud to represent my hometown of Minneapolis along with these other Challenge legends,” Tyler wrote via Instagram on August 2. “The fans are what keep us coming out! Hearing your stories of courage, inspiration & joy that you’ve got being a fan of the franchise is humbling each and every time.”

Fans met Tyler when he appeared on MTV’s The Real World: Key West in 2006 shortly after graduating from Tufts University.

He went on to compete on six seasons of The Challenge, including The Duel, The Gauntlet III, Cutthroat, Rivals, All Stars 2 and All Stars 3. He was crowned the champion on both Cutthroat and Rivals.

“The Challenge community is gutted to hear of Tyler Duckworth’s remarkably untimely passing. The young man who first came into our universe as a comical and sensitive personality developed into a force who would become a two-time champion, and he will be sorely missed,” showrunner Justin Booth said in a statement to Variety. “We are devastated, and we send our most heartfelt condolences to his family and friends. Rest in peace, young man.”

In the wake of Tyler’s death, many of his former costars — who turned into dear friends — have honored the reality star who left a former mark on The Challenge franchise.

“We shared something special being part of The Challenge family,” veteran and champion Brad Fiorenza wrote via Instagram on August 13. “I’ll always be grateful for the memories we shared. … Tyler will be deeply missed by so many. May his amazing soul live on in the lives he’s touched. RIP my dear friend 🕊️🙏.”

In a separate Instagram post, The Real World alum Melinda Collins wrote, “I’m so grateful I got to know you, to laugh with you, and to have you as a part of my life. You were truly one of a kind, and you will be missed more than you know.”
