Gypsy Rose Blanchard's Ex Ken Urker's Cause of Death Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The official cause of death for Gypsy Rose Blanchard’s ex-fiancé, Ken Urker, has been revealed.

The Lafourche Parish Coroner’s Office confirmed on Monday, November 16, that Urker’s death was the result of an accidental drug overdose.

“The final cause of death has been determined to be accidental poly substance drug toxicity,” a statement read. “The case is now complete, and no further testing is pending.”

Us confirmed in October that Urker was found dead inside a Louisiana home on his 34th birthday. Further details were not immediately revealed, but foul play was not suspected. In addition to Blanchard, 35, Urker is survived by the pair’s 22-month-old daughter, Aurora, and his other relatives.

Urker first connected with Gypsy Rose as a pen pal in 2017 while she was incarcerated following her role in the murder of her mother, Clauddine “Dee Dee” Blanchard. The pair were briefly engaged in 2018, but they went their separate ways later that year. They reconciled in 2024 after Gypsy Rose’s prison release and her divorce from ex-husband Ryan Scott Anderson.

Breaking her silence on Urker’s death, Gypsy Rose told People that he died at home in his bed.

“I’m in disbelief. I can’t think,” she told the outlet. “I’ve screamed until my throat burns.”

Gypsy Rose later shared an emotional tribute to Urker via her social media account.

“Ken was an amazing father and a man who was deeply loved,” she wrote. “He will forever be my soulmate and my red string, a connection that time, distance, and even death can never erase.”

Gypsy Rose continued, “Ken endured an unimaginable level of public scrutiny and relentless cyberbully across social media platforms, including TikTok and Reddit. The cruelty directed toward him was something no human should have to endure. I believe the tremendous weight of that scrutiny and harassment had a profound impact on him, and I hope his passing serves as a reminder that there is a real person behind every screen, every headline and every comment.”

Gypsy Rose further revealed that she planned to keep Urker’s memory alive for daughter Aurora.

“Above all, Ken loved our daughter, Aurora. She is a beautiful reflection of his enduring spirit, and through her, a part of him will always remain with us,” she added in her statement. “I will make sure she grows up knowing how deeply her father loved her and who he was beyond what strangers on the internet chose to say about him. He will be deeply missed, forever loved and never forgotten.”

In an emotional message of her own, Urker’s sister Autumn revealed that his final words to her were “I love you.”
