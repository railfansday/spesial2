‘Ted Lasso’ Renewed For Season 5 By Apple TV

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Ted Lasso fans have something extra to be thankful for this Thanksgiving: the popular Apple TV comedy series has been officially renewed for a fifth season. Its targeted production start date is April 14, I hear.

The renewal, which has been pending since the Season 4 finale was released in early October, was never in doubt — four seasons in, Ted Lasso remains Apple TV’s biggest global hit.

But because Ted Lasso has been such a big juggernaut, comes from an outside studio, Warner Bros. Television, and has an executive producer and star, Jason Sudeikis, who is very protective of his creation, deals on the show have never been quick and easy, especially when it comes to Sudeikis’ contract. Further complicating the process this time was the fact that the Season 4 finale dropped the same day as Warner Bros. TV parent Warner Bros. Discovery’s acquisition by Paramount closed, so the renewal conversation was going on with the post-merger integration in the background.

Sudeikis signing on for Season 4 paved the way to the renewal, with a writers room now starting up. As Deadline reported, the Ted Lasso team opted not to open a writers room ahead of the Season 4 renewal so the stories for next season have not been fleshed out. Watch on Deadline

Because of that, character story arcs are yet to be locked in but it’s safe to assume that stars/co-creators Sudeikis (Ted Lasso) and Brendan Hunt (Coach Beard) would be back, joined by fellow OGs Hannah Waddingham (Rebecca), Jeremy Swift (Higgins), Brett Goldstein (Roy) and Juno Temple (Keeley) as well as at least some of the new Season 4 additions, most notably breakout Tanya Reynolds as Coach Alice Chilton.

The renewal comes with a question about the extent of the involvement of Goldstein, who has two other series, Apple TV’s Shrinking, on which he is co-creator, executive producer and recurring guest star, and Prime Video’s upcoming Escorted, which he created, executive produces and headlines, in addition to his movie career. The uncertainty extends to how much we will see of Temple, who plays Roy’s girlfriend — and Rebecca’s BFF.

In the Season 4 finale, Roy (Goldstein) left AFC Richmond after accepting an offer to become a manager of his former team Chelsea as he and Keeley (Temple) are expecting their first child together. The cliffhanger was an insurance policy, according to Hunt.

“No, not necessarily,” he told Deadline last month when asked whether the plot twist means that Goldstein would no longer be a series regular on Ted Lasso. “It is acknowledging that he’s a busy man and giving us some wiggle room I would say. But no, there’s no expectation of losing Roy and Keeley. But they are two very busy people, so wiggle room is a smart thing.”

Back after a three-year break, Ted Lasso delivered the most viewed season for a returning Apple TV comedy over the first 54 days, according to the streamer. In the U.S., Ted Lasso set series highs for monthly viewership, drawing 3.6 billion minutes across September 1–30, and premiere week (August 3–9) with 866 million minutes.

And, despite some midway wobbles, Ted Lasso ended strong creatively, with Season 4 averaging 90% on Rotten Tomatoes.

The season expanded the Ted Lasso universe to AFC Richmond’s Lady Greyhounds, introducing new characters and stories.

In it, Ted (Sudeikis) returned to Richmond, taking on his biggest challenge yet: coaching a second division women’s football team. In addition to Emmy winner Sudeikis, the fourth season also starred Emmy Award-winner Waddingham, Temple, Emmy Award winner Goldstein, Hunt and Swift, along with Reynolds, Jude Mack, Faye Marsay, Rex Hayes, Aisling Sharkey, Abbie Hern and Grant Feely.

The finale shook up the team’s leadership, with Ted (Sudeikis) stepping back to become assistant coach alongside Coach Beard (Hunt) while promoting Alice Chilton (Reynolds) to take over as head coach from him.

“I’m certainly interested in finding out like, is this a bit pie in the sky for Ted to think he can just shift down to backseat and have someone telling him what to do and where to be and making demands of him?,” Hunt said about the new dynamic. “We’ll see how how he adjusted that. It might not be as easy as he thinks.”

Season 4 of Ted Lasso is executive produced by Sudeikis alongside Hunt, Joe Kelly, Jane Becker, Jamie Lee, Bill Wrubel and Jack Burditt. Goldstein serves as writer and executive producer alongside Leann Bowen. Bill Lawrence executive produces via his Doozer Productions, in association with Warner Bros. Television and Universal Television, a division of Universal Studio Group. Doozer’s Jeff Ingold and Liza Katzer also serve as executive producers. The series was developed by Sudeikis, Lawrence, Kelly and Hunt, and is based on the preexisting format and characters from NBC Sports.

The first season became the most Emmy Award-nominated comedy series, and the series went on to land rare back-to-back Emmys for Outstanding Comedy Series for its first two seasons on air.
