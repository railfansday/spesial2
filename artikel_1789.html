Chelsea monitor Jules Kounde situation at Barcelona

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

According to TEAMtalk, Chelsea are closely monitoring Jules Kounde’s situation at Barcelona ahead of the January transfer window, with the French defender emerging as a leading target if Malo Gusto leaves Stamford Bridge.

The report states that Chelsea are prepared to sanction Gusto’s departure but only if they can secure a suitable replacement. Kounde has been identified as a strong candidate due to his versatility, with the ability to play both at right-back and in central defence. That flexibility is said to be particularly appealing to manager Xabi Alonso.

While Kounde is currently dealing with an ankle issue, the report claims his representatives have informed interested clubs that the problem is not considered a long-term concern. Chelsea have therefore continued assessing what a potential deal might involve, though they are likely to face competition from other Premier League clubs.

Barcelona are also reportedly open to considering the defender’s future as they seek to raise funds for their own transfer activity. Kounde is apparently aware that the Catalan club are evaluating alternative defensive options, prompting his camp to explore possibilities elsewhere.

The report adds that clubs from the Saudi Pro League are interested in the 28-year-old, but Kounde’s preference is believed to be remaining in Europe, with the Premier League viewed as his preferred destination. A move to London is also said to appeal to the defender, who previously came close to joining Chelsea before moving to Barcelona from Sevilla in 2022.
