How To Increase Your Stamina In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Here is how to increase your stamina in Grand Theft Auto 6.

Grand Theft Auto 6 has finally been released, so grab your gun, load up on C4, and get ready to, checks notes, make a daily habit of going to the gym to improve your health. Leaning even more into maintaining a daily routine than the previous entries, a decent chunk of GTA 6 is all about maintaining Lucia and Jason's well-being.

Doing so gives you several benefits, with some of the biggest being increased health and stamina. Given how important stamina is, this is something you will want to always make time for when you can if you want to have a smoother experience during missions or just completing heists. Here's what you need to know about working out and increasing your stamina.

To improve your stamina, fill the radial stamina meter in the Fitness app on your phone. Keep in mind, both Jason and Lucia have separate stats, so increasing the stamina (or making progress towards it) on one will not impact the other. Stamina can be improved by either completing specific exercises or performing in-game actions for an extended period of time.

Health, Stamina, and Focus all have four separate levels, with four being the max amount of stamina you can have at any time. However, you can lose levels if you don't work out daily and maintain a healthy lifestyle for Jason and Lucia. Meaning, even if you reach Level 4 Stamina, you can easily drop to Level 3 if you neglect exercising.

You can only work out so much each day before you enter a cool off period. The length of this can be cut short if you drink a Protein Shake. It will take multiple days of consistently performing Stamina-focused exercises to raise this stat.

Finally, you can temporarily buff your Stamina bar by either consuming specific foods or doing certain actions. This includes things such as drinking coffee, sleeping, or showering. Look for the green lightning bolt icon, as this indicates what food or activity will give you a small bump in your Stamina for the day.

Staying up all night playing GTA 6 will make you tired in real life and in-game.

To raise your stamina level, you will need to focus on completing cardio exercise routines or just performing actions that are tied to cardio (running, swimming, diving, kayaking, cycling).

Starting with the former, once you can wander around Jason's home, you will discover that he has a home gym on his porch. Unfortunately, all the machines on his porch will only increase his health. To start working on his stamina, head down to the shed below his home, grab the punching bag, and hook it up to the stand.

Hitting the punching bag will make progress towards increasing your stamina. You can do this up to twice a day; however, as you further explore Leonida, you'll discover gyms that you can visit. These will have additional exercise equipment like treadmills you can use to improve your stamina.

Alternatively, you can improve Jason and Lucia's stamina through normal gameplay. This takes a bit more time than working out, but if you're planning on diving for sunken treasure or just want to use a bike to get around, it will help contribute to increasing your overall stamina. There is a kayak at Jason's house that you can use near the water to the right of the docks.

All that being said, I still recommend making use of things like the punching bag or treadmill as your main method for improving your stamina. These will be quicker and ultimately more consistent.
