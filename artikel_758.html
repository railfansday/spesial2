The Top Gabb Music Songs Chart Turns 2, Malcolm Todd’s ‘Earrings’ Reigns Again

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Todd's top-20 Hot 100 hit leads the monthly survey for a third time.

Malcolm Todd’s “Earrings” joins rarified air as one of just four songs to spend at least three months at No. 1 on the monthly Top Gabb Music Songs chart, reigning again on the October 2026 tally. Explore Malcolm Todd See latest videos, charts and news

Billboard has partnered with Gabb Wireless, a phone company for kids and teens, to present a monthly chart, which launched in October 2024, tracking on-demand streams via its Gabb Music platform. Gabb Music offers a vast catalog of songs, all of which are selected by the Gabb team to include only kid- and teen-appropriate content. Gabb Music streams are not currently factored into any other Billboard charts. The rule of “Earrings” comes amid the chart’s second birthday (the inaugural ruler, Benson Boone’s “Beautiful Things,” continues to appear on the monthly survey, rising two spots to No. 10 in October). Having led for the preceding three months beginning in August, it joins Alex Warren’s “Ordinary,” Forrest Frank’s “Your Way’s Better” and Lady Gaga and Bruno Mars’ “Die With a Smile” as the only songs to lead Top Gabb Music Songs for three months or more.

“Earrings” reached a new peak of No. 17 on the weekly Billboard Hot 100 in October; it appears at No. 23 on the latest list (Nov. 21).

Each month of the track’s Top Gabb Music Songs reign, a new challenger reaches No. 2. This month, it’s Alyssa Grace’s “Bloodstream,” which rises to a new peak of No. 2; it debuted at No. 20 in August, followed by a then-new best of No. 4 in September.

“Bloodstream” peaked at No. 45 on the Hot 100 in August and returned to the spot in September. Its best rank in October was No. 46, and it’s at No. 59 on the latest edition.

The month’s big mover on Top Gabb Music Songs is also the top debut: Taylor Swift’s “Patient Zero,” which bows at No. 14 following its Sept. 25 premiere as part of the deluxe edition of her album The Life of a Showgirl. It’s Swift’s best rank on the tally since her Toy Story 5 soundtrack contribution, “I Knew It, I Knew You,” also debuted at No. 14 on the June list.

“Patient Zero” became Swift’s 16th Hot 100 No. 1 upon release and spent three weeks atop the ranking in October.
