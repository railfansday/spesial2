Happy Friday! Bitcoin is trading below $60,000. CryptoQuant said USDT supply needs to start growing again for a sustained bitcoin price recovery.

Also in today's newsletter, K33 assesses potential threats to Bitcoin from AI math breakthroughs, several Asian countries plan to form a working group on digital payments and crypto regulation, and more.

Bitcoin may see short-term price rebounds as USDT supply falls, but a lasting recovery requires supply to grow again, CryptoQuant said.

Recent advances in AI-assisted mathematics warrant closer attention to Bitcoin's security, but there is no evidence yet that its cryptography has been compromised, K33 analysts said.

China, Japan, South Korea and the 11 ASEAN member states have agreed to form a working group focused on cross-border digital payments and crypto regulation.

Ethereum core developers agreed to activate the Glamsterdam upgrade on the Hoodi testnet on Dec. 15, following its deployment on Sepolia last month.

Bank of Japan Executive Director Kazushige Kamiyama said stablecoins need strict oversight of their reserves and operations, as well as links to central bank money, to prevent financial fragmentation.
