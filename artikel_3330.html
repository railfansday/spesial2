Tyra Banks Taps Andy Vallentine To Direct Sally Hansen Film

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

EXCLUSIVE: Tyra Banks’ Smize Productions has tapped Andy Vallentine (The Mattachine Family) to direct its Sally Hansen feature film, which Deadline announced was in development last year.

In addition to his directing work for Hollywood, Vallentine has a background creating visually bold work for major beauty brands including Sephora and MAC Cosmetics, touts a release from SMiZE Productions. His experience fits into the Sally Hansen world of vintage Hollywood glamour, beauty, fashion and color.

The film, as yet untitled, follows a team determined to uncover the real Sally Hansen — chasing clues, rumors and myths — while juxtaposing her untold story against today’s global nail obsession. Blending beauty with business intrigue, the film explores how Hansen built one of the industry’s most enduring empires decades before “beauty influencer” was even a phrase — and how her legacy still shapes the $50 billion nail market. Watch on Deadline

“I’ve always been drawn to tenacious, impossible stories. Sally Hansen built a beauty empire, and like so many women before and after her, she was nearly written out of history anyway,” Vallentine said. “I’ve spent my career directing campaigns for beauty brands like Sephora and MAC, and now I can’t wait to turn the camera around to tell her unbelievable story and the real-life search to find her.”

Added Banks, “Andy has this incredible visual imagination and a deep understanding of storytelling, plus a real connection to the beauty world, to bring all of that to life. I want people to know Sally the woman, not just Sally the bottle.”

Smize Productions’ global slate includes scripted, unscripted, live theater and ice-cream-themed entertainment projects in the U.S., Canada and Australia, with additional announcements slated through 2026.

Vallentine is repped by Heroes and Villains.
