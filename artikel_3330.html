Star Trek: Divergent Timeline Officially Brings Back The Kelvin Era Crew

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Star Trek can boldly go wherever it pleases, but right now there doesn't seem to be a heading. Following the conclusion of multiple Star Trek series in recent history, there isn't a clear mainline direction, and all the spinoffs seem short-lived as Paramount retools its entire media division.

That said, there are plenty of Star Trek stories to enjoy throughout the decades. The series has been around for so long that countless creatives have taken cracks at it, some of whom are better at navigating deep space than others. But if you're a fan of the Kelvin Timeline, you're in luck, as Star Trek: Divergent Timeline is bringing them back with a recent release.

In case you haven't been following Star Trek, the Kelvin Timeline was introduced in 2009's film simply titled Star Trek, directed by J.J. Abrams and starring Chris Pine as James T. Kirk.

In essence, in the year 2387, the Romulan Nero travels back to 2233 and destroys the USS Kelvin, along with First Officer George Kirk. This splits the timeline, creating the alternate universe many refer to as the "Kelvin Timeline." It's garnered a mixed reception since its original release (especially in terms of its sequel, Into Darkness), but it still has fans, and it's part of Star Trek's decades-long history.

That timeline is now making it into Magic: The Gathering, as shared by Wizards of the Coast officially:

Experience 60 years of Klingons, Vulcans, Tamarians, and more with Magic: The Gathering® | Star Trek™! Magic's latest set puts the stories and characters that have captivated generations front and center, celebrating the legacy and future of the Star Trek franchise with Booster Fun treatments like borderless scene cards. Each scene card is part of a larger, multi-card piece that depicts a corner of the Star Trek universe, whether that's an iconic episode or an especially chaotic moment on the bridge. Here are all the scenes you can find in this set!

Multiple crew members have made it onto the set, topped off by a release called Divergent Timeline, which reunites the film trilogy crew in scene cards. So here's what we're getting with Divergent Timeline:

JB Casacop's scene aboard the Enterprise features Kirk, Spock, Uhura, and more, but they look a tad different here! This is the Kelvin timeline, depicted in 2009's Star Trek and other films. You'll also find these incarnations of the crew on the set's Stardates cards.

In other words, we're getting Captain Kirk, as well as Spock, Uhura, Chekov and McCoy, Scotty, and Sulu, as they appear in their Kelvin Timeline forms in the feature films. They bear the likenesses of the films, in line with the rest of the sets.

The other big scene card addition includes Enterprise-Q, depicting the chaos of this particular episode when Q meddled with the Enterprise. Depicted by ALexandaar Gering, it gives us a close look at the Next Generation crew, including the iconic Jean-Luc Picard. Even if Star Trek is rudderless in the years to come, it's had plenty of celebrations so far that are worth remembering.
