Dude Wipes’ New ‘Dingle Bells’ Scent Brings Holiday Cheer and a Cleaner Rear

There’s no such thing as too much holiday cheer. From yard decorations and ugly sweaters to special beverages and Christmas music, we like holiday-themed gear almost everywhere in our homes. However, one place where the festive spirit is sorely lacking is the bathroom.

Dude Wipes is here to fix that. The personal hygiene brand just released its best-selling flushable wipes in a Christmas-y “Dingle Bells” scent, ensuring some holiday cheer for your rear. The adult wipes are made with clove, balsam, and other jolly scents, meaning you’ll leave the commode smelling better than you did on the way in. User reviews are stellar for Dude Wipes’ new product: The Dingle Bells wipes boast a near-perfect 4.8/5-star rating on Amazon with more than 350 reviews. “Move over, Rudolph,” writes one reviewer. “There’s a new holiday hero in town, and it’s here to wipe the slate clean in the most festive way possible!”

Pick up a pack of 144 Dude wet wipes (for all your Yule logs, this holiday and beyond) at Walmart for just $13. Since wet cleans better than dry, that’s a great deal.

Another great option from Dude Wipes this holiday season is their Pocket Packs. These mini, travel-size containers include eight wipes each — ideal for the office, music festivals, day trips, or your dopp kit while traveling. Get a pack of six (48 wipes total) for $5 at Walmart.

No matter which Dude Wipes you choose this holiday season — the festive “Dingle Bells” wipes or the Pocket Packs — your rear and your plumbing will be in good hand(s). The wipes (which are great for everyone, no matter their gender) are plastic-free, made of 99% water and plant-sourced fibers, and sewer- and septic-safe, so they break down when flushed,* unlike typical baby wipes. They’re also dermatologically tested and soaked with soothing vitamin E and aloe, which makes them great for sensitive skin. Plus, they’re larger than a standard sheet of toilet paper for all your full-coverage needs.

*Independent lab testing shows these wipes meet Industry Flushability Specifications. Not all systems can accept flushable wipes. Ignoring Disposal Instructions may lead to clogs, property damage, or regulatory violations.
