In Ramayana: Rise of a Legend, we’re introduced to a new on-screen adaptation of one of the world’s most epic tales, starring Ranbir Kapoor, Yash, and Sai Pallavi, and featuring a score from Academy Award-winning composers Hans Zimmer and A.R. Rahman. Coming in at just under three hours, Part 1 boasts stunning visuals and sets us up for an all-out celestial war after the immortal demon king Ravana (Yash), driven by vengeance, takes control of the kingdom of Lanka. Not far off, Prince Rama (Kapoor), the human avatar for the god Vishnu, is exiled to live out his days in the forest with his wife (Pallavi) and brother (Ravi Dubey). It’s here that these two deities’ fates cross in a destiny long foretold.

In the last six years, sweeping, cinematic epics and flashy blockbusters have given audiences a reason to return to the theaters. In 2023, the viral Barbenheimer double feature sent people to the theaters in droves, and just this year, Christopher Nolan turned out again with his legendary take on The Odyssey. In fact, 2026 was packed with a slate of tentpole releases, with Avengers: Doomsday set to take over movie theaters in December. But November belongs to two colossal international features: Japan’s Godzilla Minus Zero and India’s Ramayana: Rise of a Legend.

This poster features an extraordinary clash of gods, as Rama and Ravana face off in brilliant stars and nebulas.
