It should be quite the atmosphere at a sold-out Windsor Park as Northern Ireland host Ukraine in a crunch Saturday-night fixture in Belfast.

Michael O'Neill's side know a victory would keep them top of Nations League Group B2 heading into the final round of fixtures, while they could even seal an historic first promotion to the top tier if they beat Ukraine and Hungary lose to Georgia.

Teenage Arsenal winger Ceadach O’Neill has withdrawn from the squad with an injury, while Conor Bradley, Ali McCann, Dan Ballard, Eoin Toal and Jamie McDonnell remain unavailable for the same reason.

Ukraine will be out for revenge after losing 3-0 to Northern Ireland in their 'home' fixture in Slovakia, in which Oxford United defender Ciaron Brown scored his first two international goals and West Bromwich Albion's Isaac Price also found the net.

They have the added incentive of knowing that a victory would put them right back in the hunt for automatic promotion, although they would likely then be relying on O'Neill's men to beat Hungary in Budapest on Tuesday.

AEK Athens winger Oleksandr Zubkov has dropped out of the squad due to injury, with Dynamo Kyiv's Mykola Shaparenko and Vladyslav Vanat, of Spanish side Girona, called up in his place.

We expect O'Neill's side to take all three points, roared on by their passionate fans.
