'Never doubted you': College football fans praise Arch Manning, Longhorns after LSU win

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Texas football walked into Tiger Stadium and despite some ugliness at times, the Longhorns gutted out a win.

The No. 6 team in the College Football Playoff rankings, UT was down 11 points at one point but pulled off the rally for a 22-21 win against the No. 21 Tigers. Both quarterback struggled with turnovers but made some key plays.

Texas kicker Gianni Spetic (44) celebrates after a field goal during the game against LSU at Tiger Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 14, 2026 in Baton Rouge, Louisiana. (Aaron E. Martinez/Austin American-Statesman)

LSU's Sam Leavitt threw for two touchdowns while Arch Manning had Texas' lone rushing touchdown from the first quarter and threw a touchdown late in the game. Each quarterback threw three interceptions and senior defensive back Jelani McDonald caught all of Leavitt's to join many Longhorns (8-2, 5-2 SEC) who had multiple picks in a single game.

Down almost 11 at one point in the game, Texas got the ball back after the two-minute timeout and Manning went to his receivers to march down the field. Manning rushed for a 1st-and-Goal inside the five-yard line and he finds running back Raleek Brown who walks it into the end zone to beat LSU (6-4, 3-4).
