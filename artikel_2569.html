What channel is Lions vs. Bears on today? Time, TV schedule

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

First place in the NFC North will be on the line when the Detroit Lions host the Chicago Bears in their annual Thanksgiving Day game Thursday, Nov. 26, at Ford Field.

The Lions (8-2) have won six consecutive games, the longest active winning streak in the NFL, after defeating the Tampa Bay Buccaneers, 31-21, on Sunday. Jared Goff completed 20 of 28 passes for 279 yards and two touchdowns with no interceptions, while running back Jahmyr Gibbs scored two fourth-quarter touchdowns.

Detroit's defense has held each of its past six opponents to 21 points or fewer. The Lions recorded four sacks against Tampa Bay in their first game since losing All-Pro edge rusher Aidan Hutchinson to a season-ending knee injury.

The Bears (8-2) have won five consecutive games under former Lions offensive coordinator Ben Johnson, including a 21-13 victory over the New Orleans Saints on Sunday. Chicago hasn't trailed in any of its past five games.

Johnson led the Bears to an NFC North championship last season, his first as Chicago's head coach, but lost both games against the Lions. Detroit has won four consecutive meetings against Chicago.

Thursday marks the 87th Thanksgiving Day game for the Lions and their 21st against the Bears. The teams will also meet Jan. 3 at Soldier Field in Chicago.

Here's how to watch Lions vs. Bears in Week 12 of the NFL season:

Streaming: Paramount+; NFL+ on eligible mobile devices

Radio: 97.1 FM The Ticket in Detroit; Detroit Lions Radio Network

Jim Nantz will handle play-by-play for CBS, with J.J. Watt as analyst and Tracy Wolfson reporting from the sidelines.

Dan Miller will have the Lions radio call alongside analyst Lomas Brown.

The Lions' Thanksgiving Day tradition dates to 1934, when Detroit hosted Chicago in a game the Bears won, 19-16. The Lions have played annually on the holiday since 1945.

Detroit defeated Chicago, 23-20, in the teams' most recent Thanksgiving Day meeting in 2024, snapping a seven-game holiday losing streak. The Bears hold an 11-9 advantage in Thanksgiving Day meetings.

The Lions and Bears will open the NFL's Thanksgiving tripleheader, followed by the Philadelphia Eagles at Dallas Cowboys at 4:30 p.m. on FOX and the Kansas City Chiefs at Buffalo Bills at 8:20 p.m. on NBC.

The Bears were 2½-point favorites, according to BetMGM. Betting lines are subject to change.
