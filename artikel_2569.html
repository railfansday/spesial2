Dennis Allen on how Jeffery Simmons has impacted Bears defense

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Chicago Bears made a franchise-defining move trading for All-Pro defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons this season, and his impact has already been massive in just a short span, further elevating Chicago as Super Bowl contenders.

In just three games with the Bears, Simmons already has five sacks, which is more than he had in the seven games he played in with the Tennessee Titans before the trade. Chicago had six sacks in their Week 11 win against the New Orleans Saints. They also have 13 sacks in the past three games.

Bears defensive coordinator Dennis Allen has always been creative in his playcalling. However, Simmons gives him even more freedom because Chicago can now get pressure on the quarterback without having to send an extra rusher.

"In each and every game, we have a game plan going in, and then really it comes down to a feel of when you feel like you need to bring the extra rusher and what you feel like you can still affect the quarterback with a four-man rush," Allen said. "It's a good problem to have as a play caller knowing that you don't have to rush five, six, seven guys to be able to get to the quarterback. It's comforting to know that when you call a four-man rush, more often than not the quarterback's not going to be just standing there patting the ball."

Unfortunately, Allen lost one of his favorite blitzers, cornerback Kyler Gordon, who is currently dealing with a shoulder injury. In the four games Gordon has played in so far this year, he has blitzed 14 times and has four sacks, including three in his season debut against the Atlanta Falcons.

The Bears will need to get creative on defense to stop running back Jahmyr Gibbs and the rest of the Detroit Lions offense on Thanksgiving. Chicago has not defeated the Lions since the 2023 season, although Detroit does not have the best track record on Thanksgiving. It will also be their first matchup against the Bears this season. Both teams come into the game 8-2; the winner will take sole possession of first place in the NFC North.
