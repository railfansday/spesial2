Barcelona step up interest in 17-year-old Dortmund winger amid competition from Manchester United

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Barcelona have reportedly stepped up their pursuit of Borussia Dortmund's teenage winger Mathis Albert, with the Catalan club taking fresh steps in recent weeks to position themselves for a possible transfer in 2027 amid competition from Manchester United.

According to SPORT, Barça are actively monitoring the 17-year-old American, who is regarded as one of Dortmund's promising young attacking talents.

Albert joined Dortmund from the LA Galaxy academy in 2024 and made history in April 2026 by becoming the youngest American to feature in the Bundesliga.

The right-footed winger is primarily used on the left, where his pace and ability to cut inside make him an intriguing prospect for Barcelona's attacking plans.

As stated before, Barcelona are not alone in their interest, with Manchester United also among the clubs pushing to sign Albert.

Barcelona are interested in signing Mathis Albert of Borussia Dortmund. (Photo by Alex Pantling/Getty Images)

For United, their appointment of manager Mauricio Pochettino is a massive advantage.

Having previously worked with the teenager in the United States national team setup, the Argentine manager is familiar with Albert's talent, and even the player appreciates the coach.

Apart from Barcelona and Manchester United, it is crucial to note that Albert has also attracted attention from several other major European clubs, including Manchester City, Bayern Munich, Chelsea, Paris Saint-Germain, Arsenal and Liverpool.

Dortmund continue to view Albert as an important part of their future and have offered him a contract extension.

His current deal runs until June 2027, giving the German club a limited period to secure his commitment before the final year of his contract becomes a significant factor in negotiations.

Barcelona face competition from Manchester United for Mathis Albert. (Photo by Christof Koepsel/Getty Images)

The winger has struggled to secure consistent first-team minutes, but Dortmund have previously rejected loan interest because they believe he has a role in their senior squad, which suggests they are not keen to lose control of his development, even with several major teams monitoring his situation.

For now, a move during the winter transfer window is considered unlikely, with even Barcelona not too keen or focused on the player.

At the same time, any potential transfer to England would also face FIFA's restrictions on international transfers involving players under 18, subject to limited exceptions and prior approval.

For Barcelona, Albert would be a high-potential investment and certainly not an immediate solution.

His explosive running, right-footed profile from the left flank and ability to attack inside channels fit the type of young winger clubs increasingly seek to develop before he reaches his prime, although there is no urgency from their side despite intensifying their scouting of the 17-year-old.
