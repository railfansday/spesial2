Titans vs. Cowboys live updates, score: Dallas first on the board with Javonte Williams rushing TD

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Sunday's matchup between the Tennessee Titans and the Dallas Cowboys looks poised for high-scoring madness, as the two worst defenses in the league face off.

Dallas and Tennessee are one-two in total offensive yards allowed this season, with both defenses boasting more than 3,900 yards allowed by opponents. But while the Cowboys' offense has been able to salvage a 4-6 record, the Titans are 1-8, with just a surprisingly good offensive line holding the team together.

Dallas tried to reset the issue by stripping first-year defensive coordinator Christian Parker of play-calling duties, and having senior defensive assistant and veteran coach Mike Pettine call the plays going forward.

Tennessee, meanwhile, appears to be actively tanking. But the team did opt to keep star running back Tony Pollard, who should provide some offensive entertainment against a Cowboys defense that, in the words of their own head coach, "tackled like s***" last week.

Follow along with Yahoo Sports for live updates, highlights and more as the Titans face the Cowboys in Week 11, airing on Fox.

Rookie wide receiver Carnell Tate will return to the active lineup for Tennessee in Week 11, officially active for the first time since Week 7 after dealing with a shoulder injury.

Despite having missed two games, Tate leads the team with 413 receiving yards this year with a single receiving touchdown. His presence will be key this week, especially after teammate Wan'Dale Robinson was placed on season-ending injured reserve with an ankle injury this week.

Christian Parker's job as Dallas Cowboys defensive coordinator is safe for now, but he will no longer be calling the plays, head coach Brian Schottenheimer said on Wednesday.

Mike Pettine, who was added last month as a senior defensive assistant to support Parker, will have play-calling responsibilities going forward.

"A different perspective [will be] good for us," Schottenheimer said. "Play-calling is a very collaborative thing."

The decision comes after another poor showing by the Dallas defense during Sunday's 46-40 loss to the San Francisco 49ers. After the game, head coach Brian Schottenheimer didn't exactly show support for the 34-year-old Parker, who was hired in the offseason after two seasons on Nick Sirianni's staff with the Philadelphia Eagles.

"At the end of the day, we tackled like s**t in the second half," Scottenheimer said after the 49ers recorded 584 yards of offense, the most the Cowboys have allowed at home in franchise history.
