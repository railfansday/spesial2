Versatile Barcelona star expected to start against Villarreal despite just returning from injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Barcelona are increasingly confident that Eric Garcia will start Sunday's La Liga meeting with Villarreal at the Spotify Camp Nou, with the defender's recovery providing Hansi Flick with an important boost ahead of the league's resumption.

According to a report by SPORT journalist Sergi Capdevila, the 25-year-old versatile defender has made significant progress during the international break and is now in contention to start at right-back on November 22.

The development could also change Barcelona's defensive selection, particularly with Pau Cubarsi facing a fitness test after returning from international duty with muscular discomfort.

Eric's improved condition therefore gives Flick a stable first-team option at a time when injuries have complicated his choices across the back four.

The outlet affirms that Garcia has impressed the coaching staff with his performances in recent training sessions, showing improved physical condition and a stronger competitive rhythm after recovering from the muscle injury that sidelined him for several weeks.

Notably, his appearance against Atletico Madrid before the international break was an important stage of that recovery.

Eric Garcia is likely to start against Villarreal. (Photo by Fran Santiago/Getty Images)

The defender featured in the second half, following a carefully managed return designed to increase his playing time gradually rather than immediately expose him to a full match.

Barcelona's medical staff intended to use the international interval to complete that process, and his progress has apparently been encouraging enough for Flick to consider handing him a starting position against Villarreal.

The situation surrounding Cubarsi remains an important consideration.

Barcelona's medical examinations identified an overload in his iliopsoas muscle after he experienced discomfort during Spain's match against England, leaving his availability dependent on his recovery.

As a result, the defender's participation against Villarreal will be assessed based on his progress this week.

Pau Cubarsi's availability is uncertain for Villarreal clash. (Photo by Pedro Salado/Getty Images)

If Cubarsi is unavailable, Andreas Christensen could partner Gerard Martin in central defence.

The Danish defender has also been working towards full fitness after his own injury absence, making his condition another factor in Flick's selection.

Eric's expected return at right-back would give the manager more flexibility in that scenario and, in turn, make Brian Farinas, who provided cover during the defensive injury crisis, more likely to start on the bench.

However, if Flick needs Garcia in a central role instead, Farinas could remain an option on the right.

Lastly, Jules Kounde's fitness is another talking point. His availability is still uncertain, making Eric's return a crucial development for Flick.
