Travis Kelce questionable to return with ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Chiefs tight end Travis Kelce is questionable to return to Sunday's game against the Falcons with an ankle injury.

Kelce had to exit the game after making his first catch in the contest, which went for 23 yards. Kelce has been nursing an ankle injury.

The tight end tried to get back on the field, but wasn't able to do so. He was examined briefly on the sideline before walking back to the locker room for further testing.

Fellow Kansas City tight end Jared Wiley was also carted from the sideline to the locker room with an ankle injury. He's been announced as questionable to return.

The Chiefs lead 7-0 midway through the first quarter.
