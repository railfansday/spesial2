New details emerge from Aaron Rodgers' ugly Steelers release

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Some new and interesting details have just emerged from Aaron Rodgers' abrupt exit from the Pittsburgh Steelers, and it appears the 42-year-old quarterback may have left out a point or two.

After acknowledging that some of what Rodgers said on The Pat McAfee Show about McCarthy was true, NFL insider Tom Pelissero reported that Rodgers wasn't completely innocent in this — some coaches and teammates were frustrated with him as well.

"But I am also told that frustration had been building among coaches and some teammates about Rodgers, who had been increasingly calling plays himself in recent weeks," Pelissero said. "Rodgers and the offense were struggling, and getting further from their identity — which is running the football. So after back-to-back losses, and tension increasing, Rodgers went to McCarthy on Tuesday to demand more changes, including to the play-calling process.

"McCarthy was open to it, but the feedback he'd gotten from some coaches and some of the locker room was that's not what they wanted. Those discussions ended with an ultimatum from Rodgers: 'Run things my way, or I quit.' And the Steelers called his bluff."

Pelissero added that Rodgers was called directly by Steelers general manager Omar Khan and was told he'd be benched in favor of Mason Rudolph for the Week 12 matchup against the Denver Broncos.

It appears that neither Rodgers nor McCarthy was completely guilt-free in the fiasco the Steelers found themselves in, but the team ultimately decided to support their long-term head coach over a soon-to-retire quarterback.
