Cris Collinsworth is out for Browns-Raiders. Here’s who replaces him

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

There will be a different voice in the booth when the Cleveland Browns host the Las Vegas Raiders on "Sunday Night Football." Cris Collinsworth will be off Sunday, with Jason Garrett replacing him alongside Mike Tirico, according to NBC Sports.

The change is part of NBC's unusual Thanksgiving week schedule. Collinsworth and Tirico will call the Buffalo Bills-Kansas City Chiefs game Thursday night. Tirico will then work Sunday's game with Garrett as the analyst, while Melissa Stark reports from the sideline, NBC Sports announced Tuesday.

It is not the first Thanksgiving week that Garrett has filled Collinsworth's seat. Yahoo Sports reports that this will be the fourth Thanksgiving week game Garrett has called with Tirico in place of Collinsworth.

Garrett is a familiar face to NFL viewers. The former Dallas Cowboys head coach now works for NBC and regularly appears on "Football Night in America." Yahoo Sports notes that Garrett coached Dallas from 2011 through 2019.

The Browns-Raiders game was not originally scheduled for prime time. The NFL flexed the Cleveland-Las Vegas matchup into "Sunday Night Football." The move gives Cleveland a national spotlight late in the season.

Sunday will also mark a rare moment for Browns fans. NBC says it will be Cleveland's first home "Sunday Night Football" game since 2019. Coverage begins at 7 p.m. ET, with kickoff scheduled for 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC and Peacock.
