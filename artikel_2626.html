Shane Steichen says Colts have not shut down Alec Pierce for the season

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

A report from NFL Media on Sunday morning indicated that the Colts have decided to pull the plug on hopes that wide receiver Alec Pierce will return to action this season, but it appears to have been premature.

Pierce has not played since aggravating a heel injury that required offseason surgery in Week 2 and the Colts’ recent roster moves have made it clear that they have shifted their attention to the 2027 season. While that may be the case, head coach Shane Steichen said on Monday that the team has not made any call about whether Pierce will return in their final six games.

“I know there’s reports out there on Alec Pierce being done for the season,” Steichen said, via Stephen Holder of ESPN.com. “We have not made that determination. He’s still going through his rehab process. The only way we’ll put him out there is if he’s 100 percent. We gotta be really smart.”

Pierce has a short window to get to 100 percent before the season is over and bringing him back from a long layoff may not be the best way to ensure that he’s at full strength for next season, so it may just be a matter of when rather than if the Colts ultimately decide to shut the wideout down until next year.
