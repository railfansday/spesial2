Aaron Rodgers tosses a pair of consolation touchdowns in blowout loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Pittsburgh Steelers QB Aaron Rodgers completed 15 of 29 passes for 143 yards and a pair of touchdowns versus the Cincinnati Bengals in Week 10.

Why It Matters: When these teams met in Week 3, Rodgers threw for 292 yards and three TDs. On Sunday night, the veteran couldn't get anything going until the game was out of reach. In fact, nearly all of his modest 143 yards and two scores came after the Steelers were down 31-3. Rodgers had a nice three-game run earlier this season, but his numbers have dipped recently. Although he remains a midrange/low-end QB2 -- and has a plus matchup on tap next Sunday against the Eagles -- given his recent play it'd be tough to trust him in your lineup.
