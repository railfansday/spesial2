UConn lands a new men’s soccer coach with college, pro championship credentials

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Caleb Porter, who built a dominant, championship program at Akron, then went on to two MLS Cups, will be the new men's soccer coach at UConn.

"Caleb brings a rare combination of experience at both the collegiate and professional levels," UConn AD David Benedict said, in a statement announcing the hire. "He has a proven record of building championship-caliber teams and achieving success across different organizations and levels of the game."

Porter, 51, who replaces Chris Gbandi, let go following the Huskies' 8-7-3 finish this past season, vows to "reignite" the UConn soccer program.

One of only three coaches to win NCAA and MLS championships, Porter played for Indiana, where made the All-Big Ten team three times and was runner up for the Herman Trophy (national player of the year) in 1997. He was an assistant coach there before going to Akron in 2006. Porter's 2009 team went 23-1-1, undefeated until losing the championship game to Virginia on penalty kicks. The following year, the Zips 22-1-2, beating Louisville to win the national championship. When he left Akron in 2012, Porter had a record of 123-18-17.

Porter moved to MLS in 2012, and led the Portland Timbers to the title in 2015, joining Bruce Arena and Sigi Schmid in winning championships at both levels. He moved on to Columbus and won another MLS Cup in 2020. Porter coached the New England Revolution in 2024 and '25.

He was clearly UConn's top target on the decision was made to change coaches Nov. 13.

The Huskies won national championships in 1948 (pre-NCAA), in 1981 under Joe Morrone and 2000 under Ray Reid, but have reached the NCAA Tournament only once since 2018. Gbandi, one of the greatest players in UConn history, replaced Reid five years ago and led the Huskies to the NCAA Round of 16 in 2025, but the Huskies disappointed after starting the 2026 season in the national rankings. Meanwhile, New England schools like Vermont, the 2024 national champ, and Bryant have risen in national profile in recent years.

"I'm incredibly honored to be named the head men's soccer coach at the University of Connecticut," said Porter. "UConn's rich tradition in college soccer speaks for itself. I look forward to reigniting that success and helping create an exciting future for the program. I'm eager to get to know our players, work alongside our staff and become part of the UConn community. Our vision is to establish a program driven by passion, purpose and a shared commitment to competing at the highest level of college soccer. We will recruit and develop elite talent, play an aggressive and proactive brand of soccer, and relentlessly compete for championships.

"We want our players, alumni and supporters to take tremendous pride in what the program represents and what we will strive to accomplish together. I couldn't be more proud to join the UConn family. I look forward to getting started and creating a culture of excellence that will serve the program well for many years to come."

Porter will be introduced at a later date.
