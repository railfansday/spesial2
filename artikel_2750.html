U2 honors Dolly Parton at NFL Thanksgiving Eve halftime show

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

U2 just wrapped up its Thanksgiving Eve halftime performance at SoFi Stadium, and in the middle of the performance, the band paid tribute to a legend.

During the middle of its performance, U2 transitioned to its next song while multiple banners in the stadium began showing Dolly Parton's face.

Banners at SoFi Stadium start showing Dolly Parton's face as U2 honors her at the NFL Thanksgiving Eve halftime show by playing "Torn." AP Photo/Gregory Bull

The band paid tribute to Parton, who died earlier this year at 80 years old after a brief battle with cancer, and started playing the song "Torn."

U2 Singer Bono singing during the NFL Thanksgiving Eve halftime show at SoFi Stadium. AP Photo/Gregory Bull

As Bono started singing, Parton's voice followed along, echoing off the speakers to create a memorable performance for fans.

"Torn" was the final song Parton recorded before her death and is featured on U2's new album, "Carnaval de Luz," which came out a day after the song was originally released Nov. 13.

While Parton had multiple legendary performances in her career, she only performed during the NFL's Thanksgiving halftime show once — when she was 77.

In 2023, Parton performed at AT&T Stadium during halftime of a game between the Cowboys and Commanders.

In her set, she wore a Dallas Cowboys cheerleader outfit and sang some of her classic hits, including "9 to 5" and a cover of Queen's "We Are the Champions."
