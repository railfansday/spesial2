UM's Marsh, facing misdemeanor indecent exposure charge, out vs. UCLA

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

ANN ARBOR — Michigan receiver Andrew Marsh, who is facing a misdemeanor charge for indecent exposure stemming from an incident in early February, will not play against UCLA, according to the Big Ten availability list issued two hours before kickoff.

Michigan (5-5, 2-5 Big Ten) is preparing to face unbeaten No. 4 UCLA on Saturday, Nov. 21 in the final home regular-season game at Michigan Stadium. The Wolverines also will be without leading receiver JJ Buchanan, who appeared to injure his left shoulder at Oregon.

Marsh had been listed as questionable on the first three Big Ten availability lists that were first issued on Wednesday, Nov. 18, and then was shifted to "out" for the game. News of the charge against Marsh broke Thursday, Nov. 19, but Michigan had not issued a status report on Marsh.

"We were just made aware of an incident involving Andrew Marsh," Michigan football spokesman Dave Ablauf said in a statement Thursday ."We will continue to monitor the situation as more information becomes available, but we are unable to comment further due to the on-going case."

Also out for the Michigan-UCLA game is defensive end Nate Marshall, who was indefinitely suspended by the program on Nov. 11 for a violation of team rules, and right tackle Blake Frazier is missing his second straight game after suffering an injury against Michigan State on Nov. 7.

Running back Jordan Marshall is a game-time decision as is defensive tackle Enow Etta.

The Washtenaw County Prosecutor's office authorized a misdemeanor complaint of indecent exposure on Oct. 14, according to a press release on Thursday, Nov. 19, from the Pittsfield Township Police and Fire Department. The press release did not name Marsh, but a notice for him to appear in court for a misdemeanor case in Washtenaw County was generated on Wednesday, Nov. 18, according to online court records. Marsh is scheduled for a pre-trial hearing on Dec. 2 in 14A District Court.

A misdemeanor indecent exposure charge is punishable for up to a year in prison and up to $1,000 fine, or both. Marsh is presented by Ann Arbor attorney Joe Simon, who has not responded to requests for comment.

Marsh, on his Instagram story Friday, Nov. 20, shared an on-field photo of himself with eye black that reads: "Keep Going" and Psalm 18:39, "For you have armed me with strength for the battle; You have subdued under me those who rose up against me."

▶ Kickoff: 3:30 p.m., Saturday, Nov. 21, Michigan Stadium

▶ Records: Michigan is 5-5, 2-5 Big Ten; UCLA is 10-0, 7-0.

▶ Series: Michigan leads the series, 8-3. Last meeting: UCLA beat Michigan 23-20 on Sept. 16, 2000 at the Rose Bowl.
