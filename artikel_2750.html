LSU guard Milaysia Fulwiley arrested; accused of hitting officer with vehicle, fleeing scene

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

LSU guard Milaysia Fulwiley was identified by the East Baton Rogue Sheriff's Office as a person arrested on Saturday night after allegedly striking a officer with a vehicle, then fleeing the scene, according to arrest records.

Fulwiley was driving a vehicle weaving through traffic after 10 p.m. local time when an officer directing traffic yelled for her to stop. The driver stopped as ordered and handed her license to officers. However, when she was asked to back the vehicle up, she drove forward and hit an officer with the front of the automobile and left the scene, WBRZ reports.

The vehicle eventually drove through a parking lot to elude pursuit before an officer responding to the call stopped the car by cutting it off. Fulwiley and a passenger were then removed from the automobile, and she was taken into custody, arrest records said.

"We are aware of the situation and have no further comment at this time as it's an ongoing legal matter," a statement from an LSU spokesman said, via On3's Talia Goodman.

Fulwiley was booked in the East Baton Rouge Parish Prison on multiple charges, including aggravated battery, flight from an officer, aggravated assault and resisting an officer, according to arrest records. She is currently being held and faces $20,000 bond on the charges.

The 21-year-old is in her senior season at LSU. In the Tigers' first four games this season. she is averaging 17.8 points per game — third among LSU's leading scorers — with 3.5 rebounds, 4.3 assists and 4.3 steals. She is in her second season at LSU after playing her first two college seasons at South Carolina.

LSU last played on Friday, defeating Denver 101-37 at home to improve to 4-0 this season.
