Juve's attack, Stones ⚠️ Napoli-Varela and Roma-Lookman: today's news 🔥

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Serie A is on hold for the international break, but the transfer market is already heating up. Juventus are looking for a new centre-forward and weighing up several options for January, while Milan are exploring two ways to strengthen their attack. Inter are waiting for Stones and considering their next moves, Napoli are dreaming of Varela, and Roma are looking at Lookman again. There are also updates from Lazio and Fiorentina.

Here are today's top stories, Wednesday, November 11.

Juventus want to bring Luciano Spalletti a new centre-forward in the January transfer window. According to the latest reports, the Bianconeri are primarily considering Gianluca Scamacca, Alexander Sorloth and Folarin Balogun.

The Atalanta striker appeals for his physical and technical qualities, while Atletico Madrid's Norwegian forward and Monaco's American striker represent other potential opportunities. A move for Mateo Retegui looks more complicated: the former Atalanta player remains highly regarded, but currently appears intent on continuing his spell in Saudi Arabia.

Milan have already identified one of their January priorities: signing a striker who can play alongside Gonçalo Ramos and give him a breather. According to Corriere dello Sport, the Rossoneri's management are considering two strategies. The first involves a low-cost loan for a player on his way out of a major European club.

The second centres on Nicolò Tresoldi, born in 2004 and particularly admired by Gerry Cardinale. The striker is valued at around €40 million, a figure that makes a summer deal more likely. An early move in January cannot be ruled out, however.

The return of John Stones could also prove decisive for Inter's transfer strategy. The English defender, who joined on a free transfer in the summer, has played just 144 minutes due to fitness problems, but is now close to returning. Cristian Chivu hopes to have him back after the break to bring greater solidity to the defence.

According to La Gazzetta dello Sport, however, the club could consider signing another defender in January if he suffers any further setbacks. That remains a hypothetical scenario, and would also require freeing up a place in the squad lists.

Napoli are already looking to the future and have set their sights on Gustavo Varela, Monza's Portuguese striker, who is impressing in Serie A. Born in 2005, he has already scored nine league goals and 11 in as many appearances across all competitions, catching the attention of Napoli's management.

According to Corriere dello Sport, signing him could cost around €30 million, but there are two obstacles: Benfica retain a sell-on percentage and the right to match any offers from other clubs. Those two clauses complicate Giovanni Manna's plans.

Roma are looking for a new winger, and a particularly intriguing name is back on their shortlist: Ademola Lookman. According to Repubblica, the Giallorossi are monitoring the Nigerian forward's situation as he struggles to find the consistent playing time he had hoped for at Atletico Madrid.

A potential deal would allow Gian Piero Gasperini to reunite with one of the key players from his time at Atalanta. Endrick remains the top target, but Lookman could offer a high-quality alternative in attack.

Transfers are not the only talking point at Lazio. The future of sporting director Angelo Fabiani is also making headlines after he was asked about his contract situation in an interview with Gazzetta dello Sport.

The Lazio executive does not seem particularly concerned by the uncertainty over a renewal and has put off any discussion: "My contract is expiring? We'll talk about it when the time is right. Football is more than just a contract". Fabiani also expressed satisfaction with Gennaro Gattuso's work at the start of the season.

Fiorentina are preparing two major investments for the future. The first concerns Alieu Njie, who is enjoying an excellent run of form with three goals and two assists in his last three matches. The obligation to buy the player, born in 2005, from Torino for €12 million will be automatically triggered after Fiorentina's first match following February 5.

The management are also reportedly considering signing Pedro Gonçalves permanently, having brought him in from Sporting Lisbon with an option to buy for €22.5 million. The club could decide to complete the deal even if the conditions triggering the obligation to buy are not met.
