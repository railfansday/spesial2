Todd Golden talks Florida's home win over Merrimack on Friday night

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Florida basketball earned its fourth win of the young 2026-27 campaign on Friday night at the O'Connell Center with an 83-54 victory over the Merrimack Warriors.

After the game, head coach Todd Golden spoke to the media about his team's performance. Among the topics of discussion were the respect he has for Merrimack and the Warriors' effort against the Gators, as well as Alex Lloyd's performance in the starting role, an update on Boogie Fland's status, Florida's defensive effort, a look ahead to the three games coming up in Las Vegas, and more.

Below is a look at everything Golden told the press on Friday night after the Gators' home win.

I thought we did a good job. You know, credit to Merrimack. I think Coach Gallo, he just does a really good job, and his teams always play with great intent and great purpose, and they're just tricky to prepare for. They do a good job with that 2-3 zone, not something that we see a lot. Thought it took us the first couple of mini-games to get comfortable, and after that I thought we did a good job attacking it.

Defensively, they run good stuff. They make the chase. I thought when we started playing a little backdoor pressure to slow them down was good for us. It made us not have to chase them around so much in the half-court, and in the middle 20 of the game I thought we defended really, really well. I thought we did a great job.

I think we only gave up four points in the first ten minutes of the second half. Really pleased with our free-throw shooting. I thought we did a good job sharing the ball, and it's a good solid win for us tonight.

Yeah, you know, Boog was a little sore this morning again, and so instead of risking putting us behind the eight ball for the rest of the year, we thought it would make more sense to nip it in the bud right now and just make sure he's ready for Vegas. So that's the plan.

I think he'll be good for Vegas, and then I thought Lloyd-o did a good job taking advantage of the opportunity. He hit his first couple shots, did a really good job stepping up in those moments. I thought he defended well, I thought he was physical, and I thought he played a pretty good game tonight.

I think it can help a lot, you know. He sees the ball go through the hoop a lot in practice, but it's a different feeling when it's in a game. And he just hadn't played his best in those opportunities. He hadn't had a lot of opportunities, and so we felt like as a staff that, with Boogie being out, that he should be rewarded for his consistent effort and his great attitude that he brings every day.

And I think he was probably a little surprised when I told him this morning that he was going to start. But again, he deserved it. He deserved the opportunity. It allowed us to keep Urb in that role that he's very comfortable in and kind of see if we can help Alex gain some confidence.

I think we'll need him this year. He's a really good player, and I think he proved that tonight.

I don't think our group in the last eight minutes defended very well. You know, I think it was a tough ask. One through four for them moves a lot offensively. We play pretty big, so at the four it was Artie or Mikic, and we're asking them to switch and chase and do some things that Alex and Tommy are able to do as seniors, and guys that have been in the program.

But I think for the younger guys, that was more of a challenge. And I think that's why they were getting so many open looks. It was kind of an issue with our frontcourt not being able to guard some perimeter players and stay attached or switch when they come together.

But they were trying; they were competing. It was just probably a tough ask of them tonight.

I mean, I expect this to be one of, if not the best defensive teams in America this year, with our personnel and our continuity. So I think we've been good. I think we've been very good defensively, but I would say we're kind of where we thought we would be.

We have, in our opinion, the best defensive frontcourt in America. I think Boog's one of the best on-ball guard defenders in America. 'Zel's improved, and our other guys are pretty good on their assignments. So the expectation is for us to be pretty elite that way, so I'll be disappointed if we're not.

Well, it's more important to just keep them off the line, make them make skilled shots. And to their credit, they did make some threes tonight. I thought they hit four in the first half, and a couple of them were tough shots that you have to tip your cap a little bit. They got us late from the three-point line as well.

But a big part of our scout tonight was not allowing them to stay in the game by parading them to the foul line. And the reality is they don't provide a lot of rim pressure the way they play. A lot of their actions are on the perimeter, so they don't put themselves in advantage/disadvantage positions at the rim where they might get more of those calls.

I think it's going to be a great opportunity. It's going to be a great measuring stick for where we are. It's a great challenge for us, because we like to play pretty physical, and we play three days in a row. We're going to have to see more bodies. We're going to have to see our bench step up, for sure. We're not going to be able to play guys 36 minutes a night three days in a row, so it's going to provide an opportunity for others to step up.

But it's going to be a great event; but as you can imagine, we're pretty focused here on Notre Dame and making sure that we get off to a good start, because they're a very good team. As I explained to the guys, Notre Dame's a high-major version of Merrimack in a sense of they have a lot of skill, a lot of shooting, they cut a lot, they screen a lot, and this, in a way, can be a good precursor for that game and a good warm-up.

Absolutely. I mean, we prepared knowing that Merrimack was going to show us 40 minutes of zone. It's what Coach Gallo does, and they have, it's a great zone. It's a really difficult zone to go against. They're just not that big, right? And so there were some times where we were able to take advantage of them size-wise, but it's a challenging defense to go against, and I do think we handled it well.

We wanted to focus on — we call it hitting singles: completing passes, making sure that we were moving, and not playing a stagnant style against the zone. I thought we cut well. I thought we moved on penetration well, and we didn't shoot great from the perimeter, but we hit some shots.

And again, I thought we were really effective. We were really, really good from two-point field goal range, finishing shots, so I was pleased with the way we attacked it.

I was just pleased with our focus and our effort. We had a good run at the end of the first half. We got up 17 when it was nip and tuck for a little bit, and we were able to push it out. And we challenged our guys to make sure that we competed the right way coming out of halftime, and we did that, and we held them to four points in the first ten minutes in the second half.

I think the game was 64-30 with about ten to go when we got to our bench. So it was a really good effort. It says a lot about their competitiveness and their effort.

Just going back there and having a better showing?

I mean, it was a different time. That was the first game of the year for us. We had a lot of new players. Well, we had Boog and X trying to figure out, get comfortable, got Tommy playing a new position, and we played okay. We didn't play great, we didn't play bad. Arizona ended up being really good.

So I do believe that we're in a much healthier place going out there this year, and I'm not sure how much we'll draw on that experience, but I do think getting away from home a little bit, leaving the state, will be good for us — to kind of get into our own team bubble, so to speak, and kind of see what we got being away from home for a week or so.

We'll see how he continues to do. I feel pretty good about where we're at right now with our rotations, and as I said earlier, I do think there are going to be times for him where he'll get opportunities to help this team. And if it's a long year — whether it's injuries or foul trouble, whatever it may be — he's going to have opportunities.

So I think tonight was a great step in the right direction for him.

We're a lot further along rotation-wise this year than we were last year. And the exhibition season probably helped us with that a little bit, trying some things out. And we feel like we pulled the right levers in terms of our rotation going into that first game, and we played well against Miami; we played well at Florida State.

So going in next week, we'll be pretty consistent with that. But I do think we'll play a deeper bench next week because we have three games in three days.

It's just a different game because of the way they guard. We don't get a lot of transition opportunities, we don't get a lot of ball-screen opportunities, so I think he did a good job until late valuing the basketball and keeping us organized. In the game where you're seeing a 2-3 zone for 40, to me it's kind of hard to show much of a floor game or have an opportunity to penetrate the defense and make plays.

But I think in his role and what our expectation of him was tonight, I thought he did a really good job.
