Titans fans grade the Jeffery Simmons trade return

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

It is amazing what a good performance by a quarterback can do for confidence in the team. More on that in a minute. This week, I asked you to grade the return Mike Borgonzi got on the Jeffery Simmons trade to the Chicago Bears. 64% of you gave the return an A:

The question was to grade the return. For the 14% that gave it an F, I'd love to hear your reasoning. My guess would be that you are mad that the Tennessee Titans traded him, because if you are just grading the return, I'm not sure how you can give an F when a defensive tackle had never been traded for two first round picks before.

The real surprise this week was that 60% of you voted that you are confident in the direction the Titans are headed. As I said above, Cam Ward's 300-yard, two-touchdown performance against the Cincinnati Bengals probably had a lot to do with that.

If we can see Ward stack a couple of those games, that number will probably continue to climb, as it should.
