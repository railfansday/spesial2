'Gary Neville was very critical of him and I agree' - International is 'not good enough' for Man United

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Leny Yoro has set off alarm bells at Manchester United as the inquest into their defensive failties continues behind the scenes.

The 20-year-old was sent off after just eight minutes during the 4-0 defeat against Liverpool after a basic error at the back saw him drag Alexander Isak to the ground.

This was not the first mistake the centre-back has made this season, and amid United's ongoing defensive issues, his displays have been highlighted as a major concern.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Yoro is "not good enough" to start every week at Old Trafford as a result of his inconsistent performances.

Brown is not the only one to share these views, as Gary Neville ranted: "He makes a lot of these types of mistakes... It's as bad defending from two centre-backs as you will see."

While Yoro's age will allow him some slack from the criticism, more is expected of him as a full France international and a regular for Man United, and he has not done enough.

Brown told Football Insider last month that Man United were having doubts about Yoro and whether he was at the required level to play regularly in the Premier League.

Since then, he has been thrust into the starting XI more regularly, but the spotlight remains on him for the wrong reasons as his error-prone displays have raised concerns.

After his performance against Liverpool, those concerns have become clear once again, and while Yoro is not solely at fault, he has highlighted a major issue at Old Trafford.

"Leny Yoro is not good enough to be starting for Manchester United every week," Brown told Football Insider.

"Yes, he's only young, but he's also a France international and a regular in the Man United team, so I would be expecting a lot more from a player who has those credentials.

"It's not his fault that he's having to start every week, it's being forced upon him because of the situation United have found themselves in defensively.

"But they cannot continue to play defenders who make mistake after mistake, because this isn't the first time he's been at fault this season, and that's part of the problem.

"Gary Neville was very critical of him on commentary, and I have to agree, you can't just not say anything because he's young and it's not down to him.

"The players do have to take some responsibility as well, and there are going to be some major concerns about Yoro and his performances during this terrible run."

Concerns about Yoro's performances have come ahead of what is expected to be a busy January transfer window for Mauricio Pochettino and Man United.

Defensive improvements are expected to be the priority, with additions across the back line required as they work to ease their nightmare start to the season.

Brown told Football Insider on 12 November that Man United want to sign a physically dominant centre-back amid concerns about the weakness of their current options.

This came following the 4-4 draw against Aston Villa, in which Yoro partnered Ayden Heaven in the closing stages, with Harry Maguire withdrawn late on.

When Maguire is not on the pitch, United lack physicality, which is another concern that has been levelled at Yoro, and they are determined to add a new option in that respect.
