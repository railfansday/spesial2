Ella Langley and Tucker Wetmore Walk Red Carpet Separately at 2026 CMA Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Ella Langley and Tucker Wetmore walked the red carpet separately at the 2026 CMA Awards amid their low-key romance.

Nine-time nominee Langley, 27, arrived at Bridgestone Arena in Nashville in a beaded, plunging Elie Saab dress while New Artist of the Year contender Wetmore, also 27, looked dapper in a striped suit and a black cowboy hat at the 60th annual ceremony on Wednesday, November 18.

A source exclusively told Us in September that Langley and Wetmore had been quietly dating “for more than six months but less than a year” after meeting through mutual connections in Nashville. (Langley and Wetmore both currently live in Music City, but they are from Alabama and Washington, respectively.)

“It started casually, so they kept it on the DL at first, but they’re more serious now,” the insider revealed at the time. “They’ve met some of each other’s family and friends. It’s going really well.”

Wetmore broke out in the country music industry in 2024 with hits including “Wind Up Missin’ You.” While Langley started to find success of her own that same year, she didn’t become a household name until her song “Choosin’ Texas” broke Mariah Carey‘s record for the longest-running No. 1 single in Billboard Hot 100 history in September.

“Ella has been grateful for Tucker’s support and guidance [as she navigates fame],” the source told Us.

Dating rumors first swirled around Langley and Wetmore in December 2025 after they were spotted on vacation in the British Virgin Islands. Less than three months later, a TikTok user caught the “Be Her” singer getting on the “Brunette” crooner’s tour bus following a joint charity appearance in Chattanooga, Tennessee.

Langley and Wetmore fueled romance speculation when they exchanged flirty Instagram comments in March, days after he said on Kristin Cavallari‘s “Let’s Be Honest” podcast that he had “sworn off blondes.” They were most recently seen leaving the 2026 Nashville Songwriter Awards together in September before returning to the Virgin Islands weeks later.

Neither Langley nor Wetmore has publicly addressed their relationship — not even when a concertgoer directly asked the “Sunburn” singer about it during a Q&A last month. Instead, he simply responded, “Next question.”

Langley previously sparked dating rumors with her “You Look Like You Love Me” duet partner, Riley Green, though they both denied being an item. Wetmore, meanwhile, was last linked to model and influencer Bryana Ferringer, whom Green, 38, notably brought as his date to the CMA Awards last year.
