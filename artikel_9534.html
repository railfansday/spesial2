Angkot Simulator Indonesia: Game Design Takeaways From a New Android Minibus Sim 🚐

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Angkot Simulator Indonesia is a new Android driving sim that turns a daily work shift into the core loop. I recorded a no commentary playthrough and looked at the design choices that make it work as a small mobile game. These are my observations as a player, not a look at the game's code.

You drive an angkot, the shared minibus used in Indonesian cities. Riders wave you down, you pull over, and when someone calls "Kiri!" (Indonesian for "left," the traditional way to ask the driver to stop), you drop them off and collect the fare. At the end of the shift, you pay the owner's daily deposit, called the setoran. What is left is your income. 💰

The loop is simple: pick up, drop off, settle up. Each shift ends with a breakdown of fares, fuel, the deposit, and income, plus a rating of 1 to 3 stars, so the player gets clear feedback every time.

The game plays offline with no account or login, and progress saves automatically. Low, Medium, and High graphics options and an adjustable frame rate target lower-end phones. 📱

According to the Google Play listing, there are no ads while driving. Banners appear only on menu, garage, shop, and shift result screens, and full-screen ads are described as rare. Rewarded-ad bonuses and one-time purchases are optional. The listing also shows that location and app activity are among the data types that may be collected or shared, so check the Data Safety section before installing. Download size can vary by device and change with updates.
