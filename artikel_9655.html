Xiaomi-OCR-0 is a unified 0.8B-parameter OCR-focused vision-language model for document parsing and OCR-centric understanding. It supports text, tables, formulas, OCR-oriented visual question answering, and key information extraction. The model is described as handling scanned, warped, photographed, skewed, and recaptured documents. Maintainer: xiaomi-research.

Use it for parsing document images into text and structure, recognizing formulas and tables, answering questions about text-rich images or documents, and extracting structured fields. The supplied results also cover historical handwriting and calligraphy, including Clerical, Cursive, Running, and Regular scripts.

The supplied evidence does not specify supported image dimensions, languages, maximum input size, hardware requirements, or a reproducible inference example for the original checkpoint. Benchmark results are reported by the model source; they do not establish performance on every document type or deployment setup. Community GGUF and NInfer conversions are separate from the original checkpoint, and their reported results should not be treated as original-model results.

On the reported document-parsing benchmarks, Xiaomi-OCR-0 scores 96.83 on OmniDocBench v1.6, 95.24 on Real5-OmniDocBench, and 87.94 on Wild-OmniDocBench. The source lists TeleOCR at 96.87 on OmniDocBench v1.6 and 88.53 on Wild-OmniDocBench, and OvisOCR2 at 96.58, 92.29, and 87.91 respectively; Real5 is not reported for TeleOCR. On five OCR-centric VQA benchmarks, Xiaomi-OCR-0 has a reported mean of 83.2, compared with 72.9 for Qwen3.5-0.8B, 80.9 for Qwen3.5-2B, 84.9 for Qwen3.5-4B, and 82.6 for MiniCPM-V-4.5. These are source-reported benchmark figures, not a general quality ranking. The supplied alternatives include TeleOCR, MiniCPM-OCR-1.0, Unlimited-OCR, and Qianfan-OCR; their short descriptions do not support broader capability comparisons.

The exact model is a 0.8B vision-language model based on Qwen3.5-0.8B-Base. Its training recipe uses Q-Mask text anchoring, OCR-centric continued pretraining, and mixed-task reinforcement learning. The source describes an approximately 170M-sample OCR-centric training corpus. Metadata identifies Transformers and Safetensors, and lists the Apache-2.0 license. Reported scores include 97.84 on UniMER-Test for formula recognition, 92.27 on PubTabNet for table recognition, and 73 on Chronicles-OCR Mature Scripts. No hardware, context length, or measured inference performance is supplied for the original checkpoint. The 4B teacher figures in the source describe separate models, not this 0.8B checkpoint.

The model is an image-text-to-text vision-language model. The source supports document images and OCR-oriented visual question answering, but does not specify an exact input schema, image-size limits, or prompt controls for the original checkpoint.

Supported tasks include document parsing, text and structure recognition, formula and table recognition, OCR-oriented question answering, and structured key information extraction. The source does not define a required output schema or decoding controls for the original checkpoint.
