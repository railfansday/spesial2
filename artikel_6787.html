When it comes to shopping for chilly fall weather wardrobe staples, pieces that are super versatile, easy to style and useful for countless occasions are what we're on the look out for.

And now that fall dress season is in full swing, we need no excuse to treat ourselves to a chic piece that we'll wear on rotation all the way into spring. If, like us, you're keeping your eyes peeled for a new capsule wardrobe addition that you'll get loads of wear out of and will keep for many autumns and winters to come, we've found something you're going to want to see.

Zara's Faux Suede Midi fits the bill if you're after a piece that can be dressed up or down and makes pulling a chic outfit together so easy. Plus, with a $70 price tag, it won't hurt your bank account too much.

The faux suede dress boasts a gorgeously sleek silhouette and a stylish slit in the midi-length hem. The v-neck design and thick shoulder straps of the sleeveless number give a seriously elegant vibe. And the neutral camel-toned beige shade (one of the biggest color trends of the year, btw) just radiates timeless fall energy.

Wondering how you'd wear it? Teaming it with a standout pair of knee-high heeled boots would be our first move. Weather that be in bold black leather or rich brown suede. Or, go for a cute pair of ballet flats or streamlined trainers for something a little more casual and comfy.

This Zara offering holds plenty of layering opportunities, too. We're imagining a cosy yet lightweight cashmere rollneck worn underneath, or a chunkier oversized knit chucked on top. Add a chic longline coat, a statement scarf and your favorite winter handbag and you're good to go.

And don't rule it out for an outing that requires something on the more glamorous side. A pair of court stilettos and a cute clutch and you've got an easy go-to for an autumnal evening. Convinced? We don't blame you.
