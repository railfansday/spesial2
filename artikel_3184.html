Baytex Energy Corp. (BTE -0.83%) management reported an acceleration of its three-year production growth outlook to 8% to 10% annually, supported by efficiency gains in the Duvernay and heavy oil portfolios. The company stated that it reached record Duvernay production during the quarter and is moving to commercialize the asset 1.5 years ahead of the original schedule. Management indicated that capital discipline remains a priority, with a target to achieve a WTI breakeven of $50 per barrel in 2027. The company is advancing enhanced oil recovery initiatives, including polymer floods and waterflood pilots, to moderate base decline rates and reduce future sustaining capital requirements.
