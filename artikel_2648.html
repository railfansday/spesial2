Lions strike first with touchdown pass from Jared Goff to Amon-Ra St. Brown

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The 7-2 Lions are on the board against the Buccaneers.

After stuffing the Bucs on a fourth-and-one play from the Detroit nine, the Lions went 90 yards for the first points of the game.

The 14-play drive was capped by a five-yard touchdown pass from quarterback Jared Goff to receiver Amon-Ra St. Brown.

Per the Lions, it was the 50th touchdown pass from Goff to St. Brown.

Along the way, cornerback Jacob Parrish suffered a back injury. He has headed to the locker room, and he's questionable to return.
