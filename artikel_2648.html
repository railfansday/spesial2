Caitlin Clark’s Future Hinges on Fever GM Pick After Latest Exit

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Indiana Fever must hire a general manager quickly or risk losing Caitlin Clark to a restricted free agent status. Matt Riccardi has been removed from contention for the front-office role.

This leaves the team with only two reported finalists while critical roster decisions sit on hold. The delay creates a massive hole in the team's ability to lock down its biggest star and fill out the roster for the 2027 season.

The search for a new leader opened up last month when the Fever parted ways with Amber Cox. Matt Riccardi, a former executive with the Dallas Mavericks and Brooklyn Nets, is now officially out of the mix. He apparently seeks to return to an NBA front office.

Consequently, the Indiana Fever has two reported finalists: Allison Feaster, vice president of the Boston Celtics, and Phil Jabour, an executive for the Sacramento Kings. Every major roster decision remains on hold until one candidate secures the position. Sports Illustrated notes that the incoming general manager will face numerous significant choices upon starting.

The 2027 season marks the final year of Caitlin Clark's current contract, which she is set to play. This offseason, she qualifies for an EPIC contract extension that might secure her a max deal. Without signing an extension, she could turn into a restricted free agent following next season.

The Fever would then have the ability to match any offer she receives or Core her to get exclusive negotiating rights. Clark previously stated she would collaborate with the organization to assemble the best possible roster. Her stance on the deal remains open as the team figures out the financial path forward.

That team awaits a massive to-do list featuring free agents Kelsey Mitchell and Sophie Cunningham. Both are unrestricted free agents. The new GM must also decide if head coach Stephanie White will remain under new management. Clark knows the stakes involve more than just her own check. The Fever needs a leader who can solve these puzzles before the window closes.

Clark is not going anywhere regardless of the contract outcome. The Fever must act fast to turn that goal into a reality with a new front office in place.
