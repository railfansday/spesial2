Nick Nurse reveals Joel Embiid is not 100% just yet after dealing with an illness

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Nick Nurse reveals Joel Embiid is not 100% just yet after dealing with an illness originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Joel Embiid has been dealing with an illness for the past week, which has led to him missing a couple of games. He returned to the Philadelphia 76ers lineup on Black Friday, but it seems that he was not yet at 100%.

The 76ers beat the Indiana Pacers 111-87, as Embiid played 28 minutes. He had a decent performance, getting 14 points, 11 rebounds, and three steals. It was an impressive performance, but 76ers head coach Nick Nurse knew that he had to be careful with Embiid's minutes.

For a star player like Embiid, it is important for a team to handle his minutes well. He has a long history with injuries and illnesses that will naturally need better treatment from the coaches.

Nurse was definitely thinking about that because he knows that Embiid will need to be 100% when the games become even more important.

It is still early in the season, which is why Nurse is allowing Embiid to build his rhythm moving forward. Fortunately for Embiid, he has a good coach who will take care of him when he needs it.

"Obviously, he was a little winded. Some short stints there, but I thought he looked good tonight... It was, cause he asked to come out a couple of times. That's the effect of the illness and conditioning. I thought he had some good plays, Nurse said after the 76ers' win against the Pacers.

Embiid will need to ramp it up soon if he wants to contribute for the 76ers. Thankfully, he has a lot of time left because the 76ers have an immense amount of talent with their players.
