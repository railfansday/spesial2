'It's bittersweet': Mike Elko comments on Texas A&amp;M AD's departure

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Texas A&M is now on the hunt for its fourth athletic director in the last decade, as Trev Alberts has agreed to terms for the same position at Michigan, and it couldn't come at a worst time.

The Aggies' football program are on their worst stretch under third-year head coach Mike Elko, boasting a 4-6 overall record on the gridiron, including a 1-6 mark in Southeastern Conference play. As a result, much speculation of potential firings and departures among the sitting coaching staff have been sparked by the disappointing outcomes by the Maroon and White.

Now, Texas A&M has much larger issues on its hand. Amidst a lackluster football campaign, the Aggies will be forced to search for a new athletic director to try and turn things around in all sectors of athletics and maintain the success Alberts produced during his time in the position.

During today's SEC Teleconference, Elko commented on the matter and gave high praise for what Alberts did in College Station.

"Just a ton of respect for Trev. Him and Angie have become family friends of ours, and I appreciate the time that we spent here together. He was very supportive. He was a great friend and certainly a great ally here as we tried to build this program. So it's bittersweet. I'm happy for him and the opportunity that he decided to take. Certainly going to miss him around here," Elko said.

Alberts will replace former Michigan athletic director Warde Manual, after interviewing for the job earlier this week. His contract with the Wolverines is expected to be finalized soon. The deal will run through June of 2031 and projects out to be more than $4 million annually.
