All 45 Episodes Of Netflix's 10/10 Sci-Fi Masterpiece Are Perfect From Start To Finish

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Netflix's 45-episode science fiction TV series is a masterpiece of anthology storytelling, and every episode is worth watching. Netflix's science fiction offerings are not of the same quality or even quantity as a streamer like Apple TV, but there are a few that are absolutely worth checking out.

Netflix doesn't always appear to have a specific game plan when it comes to greenlighting new series. It seems as willing to pursue trusted IP like The Chronicles of Narnia as it is to pursue a German-language sci-fi series from an unknown creative team, like Dark. This can result in some rough viewing, but it can also lead to totally unexpected successes.

Love, Death & Robots is one of the strangest hit series you'll find anywhere. This sci-fi anthology premiered in 2019 and came to an end in 2025 after 4 seasons and 45 episodes, running anywhere from 6 to 21 minutes each. Every episode is produced by a different team of animators and directors, though some have taken a stab at the series more than once.

The only seeming requirement of a Love, Death & Robots episode is that it includes one or more themes of the title. Therefore, nearly every episode of the series is a sci-fi or fantasy extravaganza, with lots of gore and violence, sex and eroticism, immature jokes and humor, or all of those combined.

Each episode is different, and what excites one viewer may not be another's preference at all. You can't say that any of the episodes are bad, though. Even the ones that might be distasteful, over-the-top, or mean, you still have to admit that they are impressive works of science fiction storytelling.

Love, Death & Robots made the very wise choice to unlink its stories. Only a handful of times are episodes even in the same style of animation. There are one or two times that episodes could be called part 1 and part 2, but these are more like mini anthologies with only a few characters retained across the episodes.

Anthology TV shows can often be hit or miss, with terrible episodes following standout ones. Even in an acclaimed series like The Twilight Zone, not every episode is great. That's just what happens when a singular creative force is asked to come up with so much story. Love, Death & Robots spreads the workload around.

Each episode is unique in just about every way. You would not know that two episodes were from the same series if the opening titles didn't indicate it. Every episode is fresh and keeps you invested with its short, compact stories. It's a lot easier to sit with an episode you're not vibing with when you know that in six minutes, something completely different is going to appear on-screen.

There's a Love, Death & Robots episode for everyone. Even people who don't like science fiction or horror or fantasy will have something they will enjoy. It's one of those series where everyone's top 20 episode list is going to look a lot different from the next person's.

If you like music, check out David Fincher's Red Hot Chili Peppers' music video where the band and fans are rendered to look like marionettes. If you vibe more with holiday horror, "All Through the House" is a stop-motion monster short. Whatever it is, Love, Death & Robots has something for you.
