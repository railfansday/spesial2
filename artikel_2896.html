NHL Rumours: Montreal Canadiens Hoping to Sell High On Oliver Kapanen

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

NHL rumours continue to knock on the door of the Montreal Canadiens, with the latest revolving around players who may be moved as regular roster players return to health. According to one pundit, the Habs have identified the player they would like to sell high on. Without further ado, let's get into the details.

Rumour: On Tuesday, on the Starr and D'Amico Show, a discussion unfolded about players returning to health for the Montreal Canadiens. They noted the roster clog that will result when everyone is healthy, and that based on how a couple of guys have been playing, there is an opportunity to sacrifice quantity for quality. Marco D'Amico said that from what he has been hearing, Oliver Kapanen is the player that the Canadiens are trying to sell high on at this point based on his play last season.

Analysis: As injured players return to health, the Canadiens will have to concern themselves with both roster spots and staying within the 23-man limit, and managing their cap space. While key players have been away, youngsters have proven themselves, and their presence will force management to assess their best options moving forward, and to use some of their extras as trade chips to upgrade their roster. Kapanen is a young player with value that can be packaged for that purpose.

D'Amico discussed the issue that arises when Ivan Demidov returns to action, which is expected to happen in the near future. According to NHL rumours, the Montreal Canadiens have been trying to acquire a top-six asset to complement Demidov dating back to last trade deadline when they tried to acquire Matthew Knies, and in the summer when they pursued Kirill Marchenko, and more recently in their inquiries about Dylan Larkin.

According to D'Amico, the Canadiens are hoping teams would see in Kapanen a player that can shine as a complement to skilled players, as he did with Demidov last season. In his efforts to acquire a top-six player, Hughes is hoping Kapanen can be a key piece in a package. D'Amico also mentioned Kirby Dach's contract as an option to move to create cap space to acquire that player. Dach's name has been in rumours previously, and with his improved play of late, and this may increase the chances of such a deal.

Perhaps the most controversial player returning to health, and a continued subject of NHL rumours, is goaltender Sam Montembeault. The Montreal Canadiens approached the season with a netminder conundrum. They had three NHL-ready goalies and multiple options to address their crowded net. Ultimately, the decision was made to loan Jacob Fowler to the Laval Rocket, and to run a tandem of Montembeault and Jakub Dobes, who had won the starter's net last season. But Montembeault was injured early, and Fowler called up to replace him.

Fowler has been excellent in relief, and the belief is that the Habs are not prepared to demote him again. Now Montembeault is near ready to return and decisions await. On Tuesday, General Manager Kent Hughes disclosed to the media that they may have Montembeault complete a conditioning stint in Laval before returning to Montreal.

D'Amico addressed this in the podcast, noting that they could place Montembeault on retroactive injury relief and he would be eligible for such a conditioning stint if the player was open to the arrangement. No doubt Montembeault will be open to any steps that would get him closer to regular playing time in the NHL.

On Wednesday morning on The Morning Show on TSN690, Darren Dreger discussed what he was hearing about Montembeault. He said he knew Hughes had been having conversations with clubs that had inquired about the veteran goalie in the past few weeks. He confirmed there was interest in Montembeault, but he wasn't sure the market was strong just yet. Dreger was asked if a couple of strong games in Laval might be enough to generate interest, and he said it might be. Certainly, the Habs would like to find a new home for Montembeault, and removing his contract would provide cap space for future moves.

The Montreal Canadiens have already traded one defenceman this season, when they dealt Jayden Struble to Nashville. There have also been NHL rumours of Adam Engstrom being showcased, and of Montreal's interest in Simon Edvinsson out of Detroit. Prospect David Reinbacher is also playing very well in Laval. With Mike Matheson close to a return, NHL rumours are circulating about whether a Montreal Canadiens defender may be close to a trade.

Earlier in the week, Hockey Insider Frank Seravalli joined Flames Now with Pat Steinberg and discussed the season-ending injury to Kevin Bahl, and Calgary's search for help on defence. He immediately mentioned the Montreal Canadiens depth on defence as an option for Calgary. D'Amico discussed the awkwardness of sending one of the young players who are performing well to Laval when Matheson returns, mentioning Engstrom, along with Owen Beck and Florian Xhekaj.

The Montreal Canadiens continue to be at the centre of NHL rumours. With their strong start to the season, despite multiple injuries, and with the glut of prospects who are forcing their way into permanent roles, the Habs have decisions to make as players return to health. The return of each of Demidov, Montembeault and Matheson all trigger decision points for the Habs, and opportunities to further shape their roster as they rise to contention.
