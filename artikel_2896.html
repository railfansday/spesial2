Rumor Opens Door For Memphis Grizzlies To Make Game-Changing Trade For Jamal Murray

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

With the Memphis Grizzlies' rebuild ahead of schedule in the new season, a rumor opens the door to a potential trade for Jamal Murray to supercharge the team's return to being a perennial playoff contender.

It was just a few years ago when the Grizzlies were viewed in the same way the San Antonio Spurs are today. They were a fast-rising team with a talented young core, and led by a budding superstar. However, over the last three seasons, what was once a very promising roster has been completely dismantled.

Once it became apparent that Memphis couldn't trust Ja Morant to make smart decisions off the court or be healthy enough to step on it, the Grizzlies slowly broke up their big three of him, Jaren Jackson Jr., and Desmond Bane. When they traded the gifted guard this summer, it officially put the final nail in the coffin of that era and began a total rebuild.

Heading into the 2026-27 season, the expectation was that this would be a rough year in Memphis as the team looked to develop their current crop of young talent. However, to the shock of the basketball world, the Grizzlies have been one of the best-scoring teams in the NBA (120 PPG) and have a 6-2 record.

Their impressive play has been led by rookie Cam Boozer, 2025 lottery pick Cedric Coward, and a career month from veteran guard Ty Jerome. It has created a narrative that the team is actually closer to being a force in the West far sooner than anyone expected. And maybe they are just one big piece away from getting back to being a top five team in the conference.

Nov 2, 2026; Denver, Colorado, USA; Denver Nuggets guard Jamal Murray (27) reacts after a play in the first quarter against the Portland Trail Blazers at Ball Arena. Mandatory Credit: Isaiah J. Downing-Imagn Images

Well, that piece could potentially be available in the near future. In a Friday report, an NBA executive explained to Heavy's Sean Deveney why the Denver Nuggets must seriously consider trading Murray this season.

"The guy who has value right now is Murray, and if they move him, they can start making real changes with how that team operates," the executive claimed. "You can get better role players around Nikola Jokic. You can reset, get draft picks.

"It's a difficult thing, but that is where your value is, so you have to explore what you can get back. The Nuggets are in a no-man's-land, and they need a seismic kind of move to get them out."

The Nuggets are off to a 4-3 start and are stuck with the team they have because of several hard-to-trade contracts. However, after having a career year in 2025-26 and a great start to the new season, Murray's value is at a new high point. Under the current collective bargaining agreement, it is difficult to build a consistent playoff team with a pair of max contract players. The Nuggets are finding that out the hard way.

Nov 4, 2026; Denver, Colorado, USA; Denver Nuggets guard Jamal Murray (27) drives to the basket against Toronto Raptors guard Ja'Kobe Walter (14) during the second half at Ball Arena. Mandatory Credit: Christopher Hanewinckel-Imagn Images

Due to the trades they have made over the last couple of years, the Grizzlies have 11 first-round picks in the next six NBA Drafts. So they certainly have draft assets the Nuggets would love to have. To make the money side of a deal work, they can send Jerome and Jerami Grant back in the deal. Both Grant and Jerome have player options next season that they will likely decline to get new multi-year deals, especially Jerome.

Memphis has all the chips to make a deal possible. Yes, there is risk in a trade because Murray has had injury woes in the past and is still owed $110 million over the next two seasons. However, at 29, he is right in the middle of his prime and is showing it on the court by averaging 25 points, 5 rebounds, and 7 assists a night over the last season plus.

Murray, as the primary playmaker and another top scorer, could be huge for the development of Boozer, Coward, and rookie Karim Lopez over the next few years. It would remove overpaying Jerome in free agency or trying to persuade a top free agent from their summer to-do list and instead set them up to be a team with major upside over the next two years.
