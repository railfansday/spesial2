Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo and U.S. Bank Users All Reporting Outages

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

11:42 AM PT -- According to CBS News ... Chase Bank's services are being interrupted by a technical issue, not a cyber issue.

Multiple banks have been experiencing outages, according to user reports.

Insider Wire posted on X Monday that users are saying Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo and U.S. Bank are all down.

According to Downdetector, the banks have all been experiencing a spike in user-reported problems since 9 AM.

This comes just days after the FAA issued a ground stop on Friday ... claiming legacy equipment had become unstable and caused "instability with aircraft communications and radar feeds."

The ground stop was called off after a little more than four hours -- with Secretary of Transportation Sean Duffy and Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin assuring Americans in an emergency press conference that no one's safety was at risk.

Just one day before the ground stop, Microsoft experienced an outage ... with its popular Microsoft 365 services, such as Exchange Online and other email services, coming to a screeching halt.

The Federal Aviation Administration ground stop and Microsoft outage were reportedly unrelated ... despite the close timing, which raised suspicions.
