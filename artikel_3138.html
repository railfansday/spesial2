‘The Odyssey’, ‘Widow’s Bay’ Among Top Nominees For AARP’s Movies & TV For Grownups Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Odyssey, Wild Horse Nine, The Invite, Being Heumann, and Tender Loving Care are the top nominees for Best Movie For Grownups in today’s announcement of this year’s contenders for the upcoming Movies & TV for Grownups Awards.

Tom Cruise, John Malkovich, Matt Damon, Andrew Scott, and John Turturro make up the list for Best Actor and all are considered strong bets when Oscar nominations are announced in January. The big difference with the list compiled by AARP for their awards is eligible candidates must be 50 or over. Scott literally just squeaked into eligibility this year. Nominees for Best Actress are Julianne Moore, Sophie Okonedo, Marion Bailey, Meryl Streep, and Helen Mirren. The organization also hands out a few awards in TV as well with The Diplomat, DTF St. Louis, Hacks, The Pitt, and Widow‘s Bay on the list for Best Series or Limited Series. Watch on Deadline

“This year’s nominees are proof that experience is one of the most powerful creative forces in film and television,” said Dr. Myechia Minter-Jordan, CEO of AARP. “From legends like Meryl Streep and Helen Mirren to acclaimed creators like Christopher Nolan and Chris Rock, these artists are doing some of the best work of their careers and reminding audiences and the industry alike that great stories have no age limit.”

Hosted by five-time Emmy winner Jane Lynch, The AARP Movies & TV for Grownups Awards will take place on Saturday, January 9, 2027, at the Beverly Wilshire, Beverly Hills. It will be broadcast on PBS, Great Performances Friday, February 26, 2027.
