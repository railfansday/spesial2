Cloudflare Sales Up 39%, Gross Margin Falls: Rapid Earnings Reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Cloudflare is growing quickly, and the release shows where the cost of that growth landed. We look at margins, cash flow, and what the GAAP numbers add.

Cloudflare (NET -8.28%) grew third-quarter revenue 39% to $782.5 million. Shares jumped about 15% in after-hours trading. Growth is strong, and each new dollar of sales now leaves less behind. Gross margin fell about 3 points, and free cash flow margin slipped to 10% from 13%.

Cost of revenue grew 54%, far faster than sales, which alone explains the lower gross margin. The release does not say why. Cloudflare runs its own global network, so added capacity hits cost of revenue and capital spending before the sales that fill it.

Equipment purchases rose 44%, faster than operating cash flow, so free cash flow grew far less than sales.

Non-GAAP operating margin improved, but GAAP shows a wider loss. The gap is mostly stock-based pay and related payroll taxes, which rose 45%, faster than revenue.

Watch whether gross margin stabilizes as revenue heads toward the $845 million to $846 million fourth-quarter guide. Also look for the cost and headcount of the workforce reduction plan, which the release mentions only in its forward-looking statements.
