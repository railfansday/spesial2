Oil to Remain at $100 Per Barrel Through 2027, Oil Analyst Sankey Warns

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Paul Sankey, president at Sankey Research, explains the factors that will keep crude above $100 per barrel for the year ahead and how that could impact diesel prices. Sankey also offers his outlook for European energy supplies, saying, “it’s gonna be an expensive winter for the average European.” He speaks on “Bloomberg Surveillance.” (Source: Bloomberg)
