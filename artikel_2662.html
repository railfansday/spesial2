Pierre completes perfect 12-0 season with 45-21 win over Watertown

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

VERMILLION - A ninth state Class 11AA championship in the last 10 years and the completion of a perfect 12-0 season didn't come as easy as expected for Pierre's football team.

The top-seeded and top-rated Governors used a 227-yard, five-touchdown rushing performance by junior Porter Hunsley to defeat third-seeded Watertown 45-21 on Friday night in the DakotaDome.

A dominant season for Pierre included a 55-14 victory at Watertown in mid-September. The rematch nearly two months later wasn't as convincing.

"Our guys learned a lot about who they are this season and what they're capable of and in this game, they came out and showed it," Watertown head coach John Hodorff said.

The upset bid didn't start well for Watertown (8-4) when Pierre recovered an onside kick to start the game. The Governors drove deep into Watertown territory on their first two possessions but were stalled by a combination of penalties and Watertown's defense.

The two teams remaining scoreless through the opening quarter before Hunsley kicked off his big night with a 30-yard TD run with 7:42 left in the half.

Watertown needed only a couple of minutes to pull back even on Kaeden Himmerich's 4-yard TD pass to Tyson Miller before Pierre used perhaps the biggest play of the game to turn the tide back in its favor.

Facing a third-and-22 play late in the first quarter, Pierre quarterback Jerren Deal connected with Penn State tight end recruit Cooper Terwilliger for a 26-yard gain. Hunsley followed with a 13-yard TD run on the next play to put the Govs back on top 14-7 with 1:15 left in the half.

The Governors then opened the second half with another scoring drive that ended with a 4-yard TD run by Hunsley.

Watertown never was able to make up the two-score deficit, but did pull within 21-14 on Himmerich's 7-yard TD pass to Kye Paulson late in the third quarter and within 31-21 on Himmerich's 66-yard scoring strike to Gavin Robison with 8:28 remaining.

Each of the scores were followed by two more TD runs by Hunsley, a 5-yarder in the final minutes of the third quarter, and a 66-yard gallop on the first play from scrimmage following Robison's scoring reception.

Pierre closed out the game's scoring with Deal's 19-yard scoring pass to Carter Schiefelbein with 1:39 left.

Stopping the Governors, who rushed for 339 yards and passed for 171, wasn't easy for Waterown or any of the Class 11AA teams this fall.

"They're a good football team," said Hodorff. "You try to stop the run and they're going to throw it on you. You try to stop the pass and they're going to run it on you."

Hunsley was named the game's outstanding back and also received the Joe Robbie MVP award. Deal completed 12 of 21 passes for 171 yards, connecting with Terwilliger four times for 66 yards and Hudson Stoeser three for 48.

Eli Nickels made 12 tackles and Samuel Larson 10 to lead Pierre's defense, which got interceptions from Sutton Sonnenschein and Terwilliger.

Watertown, which had a five-game winning streak snapped, rushed for 171 yards and threw for 121. TYson Miller gained 108 yards on 23 carries. Himmerich threw for 81 yards and North Dakota State recruit Hunter Peterson completed a 40-yard halfback pass. Robison had two catches for 79 yards.

Charles Whiting was involved in seven tackles and Miller, Gage Robison and Caden Corey six each for Watertown's defense. Whiting and Mason Anderson each recorded sacks.

"I think our guys competed for a lot of the football game. It was 7-7 after the first quarter, could have been tied at halftime and was 31-21 in the fourth quarter before a couple of missed assignments led to scores."

It was the second time in three years that Watertown and Pierre met for the state Class 11AA title. The Arrows slipped past Pierre 14-13 in 2024.
