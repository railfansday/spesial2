Bryce Young sacked for safety, Jacoby Brissett throws TD as Ravens lead Panthers 9-0

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

It wasn't a good start for the Panthers today against the Ravens.

Carolina started its first drive deep in its own territory after a Baltimore punt, and on the Panthers' third play, Bryce Young dropped into the end zone and was sacked by Mike Green for a safety.

That made the score 2-0 Ravens in the first quarter.

On the subsequent Baltimore drive, the Ravens pulled out all the stops: Derrick Henry took the snap with quarterback Jacoby Brissett lined up as a wide receiver. Henry gave the ball to Justice Hill, who ran to the outside and gave it to Brissett, who launched it downfield to a wide open Rashod Bateman for a 52-yard touchdown.

Brissett has now thrown touchdown passes for seven different teams in his career, and it's now 9-0 Ravens.

The Panthers enter today's game with a 4-5 record and the Ravens are 4-6, but the Panthers are actually in first place in the NFC South, while the Ravens are probably out of playoff contention. That didn't stop the Ravens from coming to play.
