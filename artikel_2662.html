49ers activate Nate Hobbs, re-sign Brandin Cooks to practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Cornerback Nate Hobbs has not played in a game since signing with the 49ers in March, but that could change against the Seahawks this weekend.

Hobbs was activated from injured reserve on Wednesday as part of a handful of roster moves announced by the team. Hobbs landed on the list on September 1 with a groin injury and was at the end of his 21-day window to be activated.

The 49ers opened a roster spot by placing offensive tackle Colton McKivitz on injured reserve. He will miss the rest of the season after fracturing and dislocating his ankle last Sunday.

The 49ers also announced that they have re-signed wide receiver Brandin Cooks to their practice squad. He was dropped from the active roster ahead of the Niners' Week 11 win over the Vikings.
