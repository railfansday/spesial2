Recursive tree queries in Drizzle without raw SQL

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

I'm not sure.. when was the last time I posted here.. its prolly been years.. but I built something that was long overdue, and had to share. So, most apps have at least one tree hiding in a table. Product categories, comment threads, folders, org charts. They usually look like this:

Storing a tree this way is easy. Reading it back is where it gets awkward. "Give me every subcategory of Clothing, however deep it goes" needs a recursive query, and Drizzle does not have a builder for that yet. The feature request has been open since 2023.

The idea / inspo comes from laravel-adjacency-list, which has been the standard way to do this in Laravel's Eloquent for years. (And its one of my favorite repos) I wanted the same thing in TypeScript :)

The repo is sabira-khan/drizzle-tree. If you store trees in a different shape, such as closure tables or materialized paths, I'd like to hear about it in the issues.
