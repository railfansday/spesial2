Stock Market Midday, Nov. 20: Nasdaq Plunges Nearly 3% as AMD and Micron Lead a Brutal Semiconductor Sell-Off

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Mounting regulatory concerns, elevated valuations, and uncertainty surrounding interest rates are putting some of Wall Street's biggest winners of the AI boom under considerable pressure.

By RJ Fulton – Nov 20, 2026 at 12:00PM EST

As of 11:31 a.m. ET, the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -4.23%) had plunged 2.80% to 23,210, while the S&P 500 (^GSPC -2.77%) fell 1.54% to 7,065, approaching the psychologically important 7,000 level. The Dow Jones Industrial Average (^DJI -1.01%) fared considerably better, edging 0.10% lower to 49,048.

Selling pressure extended beyond equities, with gold tumbling 3.73% to $3,547.90, while the 10-year Treasury yield eased three basis points to 5.04%. Consumer staples led sector performance as investors gravitated toward more defensive areas of the market, while technology suffered the heaviest losses.

Semiconductor stocks were among the hardest hit on Friday, with Advanced Micro Devices (AMD -12.98%) tumbling 11.03% as concerns surrounding firmware security and alleged ties to blacklisted Chinese companies continued to weigh on shares. Micron Technology (MU -12.64%) plunged 12.03% amid the intensifying semiconductor sell-off, reflecting growing investor concerns surrounding chip valuations and the outlook for memory demand.

Meanwhile, Humana (HUM +3.67%) gained 4.63%, recovering some of its losses from earlier this week following proposed changes to Medicare Advantage regulations. Walmart (WMT +2.32%) also bucked the broader market trend, climbing 3.18% as investors evaluated the retailer's performance and outlook heading into the holiday shopping season.

For much of the past two years, investors have rewarded companies for their exposure to AI and ability to grow revenue. However, with financing costs remaining elevated, the market is increasingly likely to favor companies generating tangible returns from AI over those committing billions of dollars to infrastructure with uncertain payback periods. Profit margins, free cash flow, and capital efficiency could become more influential in determining which technology stocks outperform.

Beyond technology, the next several weeks of holiday shopping will provide an important indication of whether consumer spending can withstand persistently restrictive financial conditions. Any meaningful deterioration in consumer demand could prompt analysts to lower corporate earnings expectations for 2027, leaving stocks vulnerable to further valuation compression.

Another development worth monitoring is the simultaneous weakness in stocks, gold, and cryptocurrencies, which suggests investors may be reducing their overall risk exposure. With the S&P 500 trading below its 200-day moving average, the next meaningful recovery will ideally be accompanied by broader sector participation, easing borrowing costs, and stabilizing earnings expectations. Without improvement in these conditions, even a substantial market rebound could prove short-lived.
