Graybar Expands U.S. Logistics Network with 1.7M Square Feet of Additional Capacity

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

ST. LOUIS, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Graybar, a leading distributor of electrical, industrial, automation and connectivity products and provider of related supply chain management and logistics services, has announced the addition of more than 1.7 million square feet of warehouse space across the United States. The investment expands Graybar's logistics network and strengthens its ability to support customers' specialized supply chain and project needs.

Building on Graybar's existing footprint of more than 355 North American locations, the expansion includes new regional distribution service centers, new STAR (Storage, Transport and Readiness) Centers supporting complex construction projects, and larger branch facilities in key metropolitan areas.

Graybar is also enhancing the capabilities of its logistics network with investments in automation and artificial intelligence, including robotics, inventory management and software solutions designed to improve speed, visibility and the customer experience.

"As our customers take on larger, more complex projects, they need a trusted distributor that can grow with them," said Kathleen M. Mazzarella, Graybar's chairman, president and chief executive officer. "We are investing in capacity, technology and automation to strengthen our capabilities and deliver greater value across the supply chain. We look forward to continuing these investments to better serve our customers, support growth, and position Graybar for long-term success."
