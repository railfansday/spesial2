The Bachelor’s Kelley Flanagan Explains Why She Hasn’t Dated in 2 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Bachelor alum Kelley Flanagan hit pause on her dating life two years ago — and she hasn’t looked back.

“I haven’t been on a date in almost two years, and let me take you back to what was going on in my life two years ago,” Flanagan, 34, began in a Sunday, November 22, Instagram video.

“I was dating a guy who I found out was cheating on me, and then about a month later, my dad passed away,” she recalled. “There have only been two times in my life where I felt like I was not at a good place mentally. One was after The Bachelor, and the second was after losing my dad.”

Flanagan added that the death of her father was “one of the hardest things” she has ever experienced.

“For months, I just felt like I was surviving,” she admitted. “Wake up, do what you need to get done, go to bed. That was my life for months, and eventually my friends started asking me, ‘Are you seeing anyone? Are you going to start dating again?’ And I just didn’t have the mental capacity to do it.”

Flanagan continued, “I didn’t want to meet new people. I didn’t want to put myself in new environments. I just wanted to stay in my own little comfort zone, and I remember thinking that my glass was already so full that even the smallest thing could make it overflow. And I just knew I needed to get myself back to a place where I just felt OK again.”

Flanagan said she considered “three things that genuinely make me happy” to break free of her rut.

“Astrology, interior design and gardening. So that’s what I started doing,” she explained. “Went on long bike rides, listening to astrology podcasts. I started helping my brother redesign his place and I created an entire orchard at my mom’s house. And somewhere along the way, without even realizing it, I started to feel like myself again.”

Now that Flanagan has “created so much peace” in her life, she’s worried that reentering the often tumultuous dating pool will “disrupt it.”

“And don’t get me wrong, I absolutely want to get married. I absolutely want to have kids,” she said. “But I also don’t want someone to come into my life and disrupt everything I’ve worked so hard to rebuild. Because for the first time in a really long time, there’s no drama, there’s no chaos.”

Flanagan concluded, “I think that’s where I’m struggling with dating now because I do want to find my person. I just don’t want to lose the piece I finally found while looking for him.”

In the caption, she added, “To everyone heading home single this year, you’re not behind 🤍 finding your peace first is never wasted time.”

Flanagan noted another reason for her dating break in the comments.

“My last relationship was with a narcissist, and if you’ve been there, you know how draining it is. It takes time, but your peace comes back 🤍,” she wrote.

Flanagan dated The Bachelor season 24 star Peter Weber on and off between 2020 and 2023. Later that year, she moved on with businessman Ari Raptis, whom she dated until late 2024.
