50% of Roma’s goals scored by defenders and midfielders

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Roma has become a beautiful team to watch and a difficult team for opponents to contain.

When the Giallorossi decide to step on the gas, they tend to crush anyone who tries to get in their way, whether it’s Real Madrid or Udinese, it makes no difference.

Gian Piero Gasperini is giving every player in the squad a chance, and they’re responding on the pitch with top-notch performances.

Everyone can score for Roma; it’s reductive to focus solely on the uncanny performances of Donyell Malen, who has already reached double figures (11) in just 12 games.

Roma have already scored 36 goals this season, 28 of which came in the league and eight in the Champions League in just 13 matches.

As Corriere dello Sport reports, the offensive avalanche doesn’t just come from the forwards, but also from the midfielders and defenders ready to make an impact in front of goal. Gasp’s team boasts 14 different scorers, a statistic that reflects the manager’s ability to involve the entire squad.

Roma seeks and finds the goal in every way: 18 of the 36 goals (50%) have come from Pisilli (4), Cristante (2), Koné (2), and De Roon (1), as well as the central defenders and full-backs: Hermoso (2), Balerdi (2), Lulli (2), Molina (1), Ndicka (1), and Ghilardi (1).
