Introducing Nonprofit: A Free Theme for Backdrop CMS

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Building a professional website for a nonprofit organization should not require a complicated setup process or extensive custom development.

That's the idea behind Nonprofit, a free and open-source theme for Backdrop CMS, built with Tailwind CSS.

Backdrop CMS provides powerful tools for managing content, layouts, and configuration. However, creating a polished website often requires considerable time configuring these features.

Nonprofit aims to simplify that experience while preserving the flexibility that makes Backdrop CMS valuable.

One of the most interesting parts of this project is its integration with Backdrop Theme Setup.

This reusable, JSON-driven PHP library helps theme developers configure their themes without manually recreating sample content and site settings.

The library uses Backdrop's existing APIs and Configuration Management system rather than introducing a separate configuration framework.

The goal is to reduce repetitive setup work while keeping content management and presentation responsibilities clearly separated.

Nonprofit is also a starting point for a larger project: a Backdrop CMS distribution designed specifically for nonprofit organizations.

The long-term goal is to provide organizations with practical tools for managing their websites and operations, while keeping the system affordable, flexible, and accessible.

Backdrop CMS has a welcoming open-source community, and feedback from designers, developers, and nonprofit organizations is always valuable.

If you're interested in the project, I'd love to hear your thoughts, suggestions, and ideas for improvement.

Nonprofit is free and open source. Explore the source code, try the theme, and share your feedback.

Nonprofit Theme: https://backdropcms.org/project/nonprofit

Backdrop Theme Setup: https://github.com/shabib-m/backdrop_theme_setup
