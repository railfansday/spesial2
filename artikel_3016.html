Indianapolis Colts release QB Daniel Jones

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Mike Garafolo of the NFL Network is reporting that the Indianapolis Colts released quarterback Daniel Jones on Friday. Jones has played very poorly since returning from the torn Achilles that ended his 2025 season, prompting the release

As pretty much everyone will remember, the New York Giants drafted Jones out of Duke with the sixth overall pick in the 2019 NFL Draft. The Colts will now turn to Anthony Richardson, and he'll be the quarterback for whom the Giants will need to game plan when the two teams face off next week.

The Giants' decision to draft Jones was something of a surprise on draft night, as the 2019 quarterback class was not seen as a strong one at all. Jones would go on to replace Giants' legend Eli Manning as the starting quarterback for the Giants game against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers in Week 3 of that year.

Though Jones won his first game as a starter, his time with the Giants was tumultuous, to say the least. There were flashes of the potential that caused GM Dave Gettleman to fall in "full bloom love" with Jones in his Senior Bowl MVP performance, but there were also far too many turnovers and injuries. The Giants had hoped Jones turned the corner when the team surprisingly made the playoffs in 2022, and rewarded him with a rich 4-year, $160 million contract in the spring of 2023. Unfortunately, the injuries and poor play returned that season Jones was ultimately benched following the Giants' 2024 loss to the Carolina Panthers in Munich.

Jones requested his release after being benched and landed with the Minnesota Vikings. He backing up Kirk Cousins for that team's own playoff run. He then signed a two-year, $88 million contract with the Colts as a free agent in 2025.

Jones initially played well for the Colts, taking advantage of the talents of RB Jonathan Taylor, TE Tyler Warren, and WR Alec Pierce in the Colts' play-action and RPO-heavy offense. However, his play fell off beginning with the Colts loss to the Pittsburgh Steelers and his season was ended early by a torn Achilles.

It's anyone's guess where Jones will land next, however he should be comfortable in the meantime. Jones has, so far, made a combined $172.8 million as a quarterback for the Giants, Vikings, and Colts.
