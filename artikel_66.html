Italy fines Meta €805m over accessing users' photos

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Italian regulators have fined social media giant Meta â¬805m (Â£670m; $887m) for accessing Android users' photos without their specific consent.

Italy's Data Protection Authority said Meta did not properly inform about 20 million smartphone users that it had access to their photos and videos via a feature of the Facebook app.

It has given Meta, which also runs WhatsApp and Instagram, 60 days to disable the feature by default and seek users' consent separately.

The fine is among the largest an Italian authority has ever imposed on a tech company.

A Meta spokesperson told the BBC that it planned to appeal.

The charge is related to a feature that suggests photos and videos from a user's phone gallery as ideas for posts.

Facebook requested access to users' photo galleries through a single app-wide authorisation. A preceding notice did not make clear that this would also allow Facebook to generate suggestions based on photos in their gallery, according to the Italian regulator.

The regulator said it had taken into account "the high number of people affected" and the fact that the feature was "still ongoing for those who have not updated the app and for those who have already given their consent to the system".

It also cited "Meta's lack of co-operation" in its decision.

Meta told the BBC: "We strongly disagree with the [regulator's] assessment of the facts, its application of the law and the disproportionate level of the fine.

"This is a straightforward feature which helps people post photos and videos to Facebook. People must give permission on their device before it can operate and they can change this permission at any time via their settings."

Last month, Meta was hit with its largest fine ever for its business practices.
