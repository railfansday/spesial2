Nvidia gave up its early gains on Wednesday, slipping 1.05% to $153.82, down $1.64 from Tuesday’s close. The reversal is striking because shares had jumped as high as $159.45 before the opening bell after a strong earnings beat, meaning the stock has dropped about 3.5% from that peak. The turn came as a ransomware group called The Gentlemen claimed it had hacked Nvidia and placed the company on its leak site with a 10-day countdown. No stolen files have been published, and Nvidia has not confirmed any breach, so it is unclear how much the report is weighing on the stock. Other concerns remain in play. Nvidia’s upbeat $130 billion forecast includes an extra week and assumes zero data centre sales to China, where new US rules now block chip rentals. Investors are also still waiting on a permanent CEO after the board ruled out Jensen Huang’s return. The stock has already lost about 24% this month.
