Brighton unearth new Luka Vuskovic weapon as unusual Croatia stats emerge

Brighton centre-back Luka Vuskovic is proving there is more to his game than simply defending.

The Croatian has been a standout star for manager Fabian Hurzeler since arriving in the summer window from Tottenham.

His performances have been a large part of the Seagull's strong start to the campaign which has them sitting in second place.

And he continued to impress on his latest international outing for Croatia.

In Croatia's game against Czechia, Vuskovic has showcased another side of his abilities, with some unusual data for a centre-back.

As he topped plenty of creative stats despite being in the backline.

It was only a narrow win for the Croats, defeating their fellow European opposition 2-1, with Vuskovic right at the heart of it.

As per FotMob, despite playing at centre-back, the 19-year-old defender managed to create the most chances of the game between Croatia and Czechia.

He created four in total, with two of those coming as big chances for his nation.

The Brighton ace was also influential, as one of those chances did result in a goal, handing him an assist.

Vuskovic also completed the most passes into the final third (nine), while maintaining a total passing accuracy of 94 per cent.

Defensively, he also performed well, winning six out of his nine duels - a 66 per cent success rate.

But nonetheless, seeing a centre-back dominate those creative stats is a rare sight, and perhaps a quality that could be honed back in England.

A Brighton centre-back being capable on the ball is no surprise and his qualities there are likely part of why he was signed in the first place.

However, his ability to produce chances from centre-back and put the ball into the final third is a rare sight.

This season in the Premier League, over his 10 appearances, Vuskovic has only managed to create one chance for Brighton.

While, in terms of getting the ball up the pitch, he ranks mid-range for line-breaking passes with 10.95 per 90. This places him in the 58th percentile.

If this latest performance for Croatia gives him more confidence to try for those final passes, that would add another edge to an already exciting Brighton side.

Whether or not this was just a one-off good day at the office remains to be seen, but he showcase a new possible weapon.
