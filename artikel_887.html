Saturday tips: Kap Vert fancied to lift Coral Gold Cup crown at Newbury

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Wendigo heads the market for a typically competitive renewal of the Coral Gold Cup at Newbury, with Mr Hope Street, Road To Home and Kap Vert among the challengers; Panic Attack back to defend her crown for in-form Dan Skelton team

With a hugely exciting renewal of the Coral Gold Cup on Saturday, Kate Tracey, Declan Rix and Sam Boswell headed to Newbury for a very special episode of Weekend Winners.

The familiar trio have each had a close look at the famous handicap clash as well as a brilliant supporting card, all live on Sky Sports Racing from 11am.

Jamie Snowden's Wendigo is the pre-race favourite and promises to bring the house down if scoring for the well-supported Racing Club syndicate, while last year's winner Panic Attack returns with 10lb claimer Dominic Lewis on board for the in-form Dan Skelton team.

"My horse is Kap Vert. He's only a six-year-old but for a novice to go and win the Scottish National I thought was a big effort.

"All he's done is progress and improve. This horse's big asset is his jumping and he's got a prominent style of racing, which I like for the track.

"If the rain does come, we know he's going to handle that and it puts more emphasis on his stamina.

"It's a big ride for Sean Houlihan and if he can bounce him out early and get into a rhythm, his jumping will keep him in it."

"I don't want too much rain to come down for my pick: Miami Magic.

"He had the perfect prep for this, in my opinion, in a Pertemps Qualifier here on November 5.

"It didn't exactly go to plan early on in his chasing career but I think he's got his act together now.

"I'm hoping it will be fair ground for him to bounce off."

"It's unoriginal, but I'm with Wendigo. It's competitive from a betting angle but I think it looks a little bit shy of real top-class future chasers.

"This is the one horse who could maybe be that and he's 40/1 for the Grand National.

"The trip is the one thing people might throw at him because he's an old-fashioned chaser who needs a bit of getting after but once he does, he'll stay on really nicely.

"For me, he's the standout. He's a really strong and obvious contender. For Jamie Snowden and the Lambourn trainers, this is their Cheltenham Gold Cup. This is a special race and I think it will go to a local trainer."
