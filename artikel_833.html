Billboard Live Music Summit 2026 Recap: VENU Honors Jorge Juárez with the Disruptor Award

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

J.W. Roth presented the honor to the executive who helped carry Música Mexicana to some of the biggest stages

Billboard’ Live Music Summit presented by VENU, brought the live entertainment industry together at the 1 Hotel West Hollywood on Nov. 10 for a celebration of the artists, executives and innovators shaping the future of touring.  Explore Hilary Duff Izzy Escobar Weezer See latest videos, charts and news

The festivities began the evening before, when VENU® hosted its Money & Music Cocktail Hour at The Sunrose in West Hollywood. The event set the stage for conversations about the changing business of live entertainment. It included an intimate fireside chat with J.W. Roth, Founder, Chairman and CEO of VENU, and Ron Bension, former President and CEO of ASM Global, veteran entertainment executive and strategic advisor to VENU, moderated by Billboard co-chief content officer Jason Lipshutz. Reflecting on the company’s continued growth and the challenges ahead, Roth said, “We believe that we have a significant calling to build fan-centric and artist-centric venues. Venues that have never been built before. At the end of the day, we see that as our challenge, and we embrace it with vigor.”

The night also featured pop-soul artist Izzy Escobar, who commanded the stage and opened her set with “These Eyes.”

Billboard Live Summit continued the next morning with a full day of programming curated by Billboard senior editor Eric Renner Brown, along with networking that connected every corner of the touring world. Leila Cobo, Billboard’s chief content officer, moderated the International Touring Panel. Executives from OCESA, Live Nation, Oak View Group and AEG Presents joined her to discuss global venue infrastructure and the push into new live markets.

Among the honorees were Weezer, recognized with the Legends of Live Award; Hilary Duff, who received Outstanding Pop Tour of the Year; and MSG Entertainment president Josephine Vaccarello, who was presented with Billboard’s 2026 Touring Executive of the Year honor by Cobo.

VENU’s Disruptor Award also returned for its second year recognizing the visionaries challenging convention and creating new possibilities for live entertainment. .

Presented by VENU’s Founder, Chairman and CEO, J.W. Roth, the honor went to Jorge Juárez, CEO of Westwood Entertainment and founder of Novus Media, whose work has helped propel Música Mexicana onto some of the world’s most prominent stages.

As Carín León’s manager and business partner in Socio Music, Juárez has played a pivotal role in expanding the artist’s reach beyond traditional Latin markets. From Coachella and Stagecoach to the Grand Ole Opry, León’s presence across genres and stages reflects an approach that continues to challenge expectations of where Música Mexicana belongs.

That vision reached another milestone with León’s historic Sphere headlining announcement, making him the first Latin artist announced to headline the venue. For Juárez, the achievement represents more than a career milestone: it underscores how ambitious live strategies can open doors for artists, audiences and entire genres.

Receiving the award, Juárez said, “I’m happy to accept this award after 25 years of selling out shows. I’ve discovered that being a disruptive promoter isn’t about celebrating when things go well. The best part of this business is when things don’t go as planned, and having the opportunity to turn those challenges into opportunities.”

Roth reflected on Juárez’s career and his work with León: “Jorge didn’t ask for permission. He went out and proved them all wrong. Fans don’t just want a ticket; they want to be part of something. It takes vision, guts and hard work to build something that lasts.”

For VENU (NYSE American: VENU), disruption is the business model. Built on a fan-founded, fan-owned, artist-inspired philosophy, VENU is reimagining what a venue can be, developing next-generation, immersive destinations across the country. Each pairs world-class stages with elevated hospitality and signature Luxe FireSuites®, giving fans new ways to own a piece of the experience and become part of the concert journey. With every new space, VENU advances its mission, seeking to redefine how fans and communities experience live music.

Similarly to VENU, Juárez has long argued that today’s fans don’t just want to buy a ticket; they want an experience, a belief that shaped everything from León’s Sphere production to the 100-day rollout leading up to it. VENU is built on the same premise, betting that fans in growing and underserved cities deserve top-tier live destinations of their own. Both are expanding the map of where live music happens and who it’s for.

Looking ahead, JW expressed his excitement about the company’s upcoming venues. “We’re looking forward to opening our first major venue in the Tulsa market in just three days. We’ll follow that with the next venue in our collection in McKinney, Texas with the largest full-season, multi-configurational venue ever built. Think of a next-generation amphitheater meets the Sphere,” he says. “We’re building venues that are our way of saying thank you to both fans and artists.”
