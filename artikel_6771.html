Amazon’s Black Friday MacBook Deals Are Pretty Impressive

Considering the price of RAM, we’ll gladly take $500 off a new MacBook.

If there’s one Apple product line where the recent price hikes hit the hardest, it’s the MacBook. Rising RAM and storage costs have pushed laptop prices up across the board, which makes scoring a new MacBook on sale all the more mission critical.

As luck would have it, Black Friday deals on Apple tech have landed on Amazon, with up to 23 percent off MacBooks. The brand-new M6 MacBook Pros and Apple’s first touchscreen laptop are on sale for the first time ever. Given that these prices have reached record highs lately, these discounts are certainly nothing to sneeze at. In fact, now that the MacBook pricing status quo has been reset, we’d say this is probably as low as MacBook prices will get these days.

Of course, not all MacBooks were created equal. While the standard MacBook Air is great for most, those who are planning on running hardcore software might benefit from a beefed-up MacBook Pro. And if budget is your primary concern, we’re of the opinion that the MacBook Neo is a worthy buy, even if it’s only $50 off right now.

The best MacBook Air deal you’ll find right now is this $300 discount on the 13-inch model. With this laptop, you’ll get Apple’s M5 chip in a fanless, 2.7-pound frame that handles heavy multitasking and light video editing without breaking a sweat. Base storage doubled to 512 GB this year—a great upgrade for storing more apps, photos, and files—and the Liquid Retina display is sharp and vivid. You also get a 12 MP Center Stage camera that’s great on video calls, Wi-Fi 7 for fast downloads, and up to 18 hours of battery, enough for a busy workday and then some.

If you’d prefer a larger laptop, 15-inch models are up to 20 percent off. They’ve got everything the 13-inch models have, just with more screen real estate. The rule of thumb for choosing between a Pro and an Air? The slimmer Air won’t replace a MacBook Pro for serious creative work or gaming, but for school, business, and general use, it’s the one to get.

Given that Apple just dropped a new slate of M6 MacBook Pros, in addition to the company’s first-ever touchscreen laptop, we were anticipating discounts on the old range of M5 models to clear out inventory. Sure, those M5s are on sale, but if you don’t mind paying just $50 more, you can scoop up the shiny new M6 on sale, complete with improved speed and boosted AI capabilities. It’s extremely rare for Apple to discount its latest laptop so soon after release, and even more rare for it to be $200 off. And if you really want Apple’s latest and greatest, the 16-inch touchscreen MacBook just went on sale for the first time.

Why go Pro over the Air? The GPU is fast, which makes a difference when editing video, working with high-res photos, or gaming, and the XDR screen is significantly brighter. If your daily workload has started becoming too much for the Air to handle, this is where you should look next. If you want the biggest, most powerful model possible, souped-up MacBook Pros with more RAM are up to $500 off. And no matter if you choose an M5 or an M6 chip, your new MacBook will feature a Liquid Retina XDR display, up to 24 hours of battery, three Thunderbolt 4 ports, HDMI, an SD card slot, and up to 2 TB of storage.

The MacBook Neo is Apple’s most affordable laptop, even with the recent price hike from $599 to $699. Right now, the Neo is $50 off, sitting smack in the middle between its debut price and post-increase price, at $649—making it far and away the cheapest MacBook. The 512 GB version, which offers Touch ID in addition to double the storage, is $100 off, making it $699.

The Neo is an easy recommendation for anyone looking for their first Mac. It runs on Apple’s A18 Pro chip and is surprisingly quick for browsing, sending email, and streaming video. The 13-inch Liquid Retina display looks great for the price, and the battery lasts a solid 16 hours. It also comes in four fun colors, with the yellow-green citrus being a fan favorite.

There are some trade-offs with going with Apple’s budget model, including storage that starts at 256 GB, just 8 GB of RAM, and no keyboard backlight. You also get only two USB-C ports, so getting a USB-C hub is a must if you plan to connect a lot of accessories. For students or anyone who mostly sends emails and browses the web, the Neo is hard to beat.
