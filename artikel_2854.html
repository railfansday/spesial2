Andrew Marsh, facing criminal charges, out for Michigan vs UCLA

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Michigan football starting receiver Andrew Marsh is out for Saturday's matchup with undefeated No. 4 UCLA (3:30 p.m., CBS).

Marsh, a sophomore and one of the top weapons in the pass game, finds himself in the spotlight as he faces a criminal charge in connection to allegedly exposing himself to a female food delivery driver earlier this year.

Washtenaw County prosecutors issued a misdemeanor indecent exposure complaint against Marsh, 19, in October, court records show. However, the misconduct allegedly occurred in February.

A food service delivery driver contacted the Pittsfield Township Police Department on Feb. 2, 2026, regarding a report of indecent exposure in the 5000 block of E. Spaniel Drive, according to a media release.

A sophomore from Katy, Texas, he leads the Wolverines with 48 receptions, is second with 459 yards (9.6 yards per reception) and has one touchdown. Marsh, however, has fumbled three times this year, twice on special teams early in the season and was replaced by Salesi Moa.

Marsh figured to be an even more important part of the attack with fellow wide receiver JJ Buchanan ruled out with an apparent shoulder injury suffered against Oregon.

Defensive end Nate Marshall, serving a suspension, is also out.

Other notable updates: running back Jordan Marshall and defensive tackle Enow Etta are both game-time decisions. Marshall has run 93 times for 346 yards (3.7 yards per carry) and five touchdowns this season. He has been overtaken by freshman Savion Hiter, carrying the ball just 12 times the past three games.

Etta, meanwhile, has 30 tackles including 1½ sacks and one pass deflection.

UCLA standout linebacker Jalen Woods has been downgraded to out.
