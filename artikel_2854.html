Florida basketball vs Florida State live updates, TV channel, live stream for rivalry game

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

TALLAHASSEE - Florida basketball is about to have another in-state rivalry game.

The No. 1 Gators (2-0) go on the road to take on Florida State (2-0) at Donald L. Tucker Center on Tuesday, Nov. 10.

UF recently defeated Jacksonville 88-57, as five Gators scored in double figures, including a double-double by Alex Condon.

The Seminoles finished 18-15 under Luke Loucks' first season. FSU opened the year with a win over Florida A&M and most recently earned a 91-68 victory against Troy.

"I think Luke has done a great job in a short period of time getting those guys playing really hard, playing together, and playing without fear," Florida coach Todd Golden said. "I think that's something that makes them uniquely scary. When a team isn't afraid to fail, you can get pretty good performances from them.

"We're going into enemy territory as the No. 1 team in America. We're gonna get their best shot, and they're gonna be well prepared and we have to bring our best effort. It's definitely one we're looking forward to."

Florida State is led by forward Sebastian Rancik, who averages a team-leading 21.5 points per game.

UF beat Florida State 78-76 last year and leads the all-time series 48-28.

Florida starters: G - Boogie Fland, G - Denzel Aberdeen, F - Thomas Haugh, F - Alex Condon, C - Rueben Chinyelu

Florida State starters: G - Anthony Robinson II, G - Kameron Taylor, F - Shon Abaev, F - Sebastian Rancik, C - Marcis Ponder
