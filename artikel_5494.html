New York Times stock pops as digital subscriber additions accelerate

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Digital-only subscription revenue rose 14.6% to $421.3 million and total revenue grew 9.7% in the quarter

Total revenue rose 9.7% to $768.5 million from $700.8 million a year earlier. Net income rose to $97.4 million, or 60 cents a diluted share, from $81.6 million, or 50 cents. Adjusted diluted EPS came in at 68 cents, up from 59 cents.

Analysts surveyed expected adjusted earnings of 65 cents a share and revenue of $763.5 million, according to The Associated Press.

Operating profit came in at $121.9 million, a 16.4% increase. Adjusted operating profit totaled $149.6 million, up 13.9%. Operating costs totaled $646.6 million, an 8.5% increase, while the adjusted measure grew 8.7%.

For the fourth quarter, the company is forecasting a 12% to 15% increase in digital-only subscription revenue from a year earlier, and a 9% to 11% increase in total subscription revenue. It forecasts digital advertising revenue growth in the high-single-digit to low-double-digit range, with total advertising revenue growing at a mid-single-digit pace. Adjusted operating costs are expected to rise 6% to 8%.

The company repurchased 1,158,652 shares of its Class A common stock for about $79.1 million in the quarter. About $146.0 million remains authorized for repurchases as of Oct. 30.
