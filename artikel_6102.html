Ras Al Khaimah’s airport is betting on Wynn’s new casino resort and other luxury hotels to bring in five-star travelers. For now, it is upgrading the terminal it has and holding the new one until airlines commit. The resort’s delay pushes any payoff to late 2027.

Ras Al Khaimah International Airport in the UAE is upgrading its existing passenger terminal and building a dedicated terminal for ultra-wealthy travelers. Both will be ready for the opening of the Wynn casino resort. The resort’s opening has been delayed from spring 2027 to September.

“We are doing a soft uplift in our current passenger terminal by improving processes and using new technology as we increase our share of leisure passengers. This will be ready by the Wynn opening,” Manish Seth, CEO of Ras Al Khaimah International Airport, told Skift. “Our current terminal is built for 1.2 to 1.3 million passengers a year, and we aim to take this number up to 1.4 to 1.5 million.”
