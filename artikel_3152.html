Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Existing leases are ramping cash rent toward a $250 million annualized run rate.

Ionic Digital Inc. (IOND -10.83%) reported a strategic transition toward becoming a high-performance computing and artificial intelligence data center developer, supported by a 32% year-over-year revenue increase. Management reported that the company's primary focus is commercializing the remaining 500 megawatts of capacity at Ward County and Midland, while maintaining a debt-free balance sheet with $628 million in total liquidity. The company is pivoting its operations from Bitcoin mining toward digital infrastructure, expecting to move its Midland sites off mining over the next six to 12 months. Management confirmed its full-year 2026 guidance, noting that cash rent from existing leases began in Aug. 2026 and is expected to reach an annualized run rate of $250 million following the delivery of 89 megawatts of incremental capacity.
