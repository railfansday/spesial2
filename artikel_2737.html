NFL analyst blasts Steelers over Week 10 implosion

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Dreadful hardly does the Pittsburgh Steelers' Week 10 performance justice, as the team was completely and utterly outclassed by the Cincinnati Bengals on Sunday Night Football—and one NFL analyst couldn't help but put the Steel City brand of football on blast for their embarrassing showcase.

According to NFL analyst Nick Wright, the Steelers put on one of the worst offensive performances in the past "17 years".

"What the Steelers offense did, through 3 quarters … is the worst by any team in 17 years," Wright said via FS1's First Things First. "They had 1 yard a play for 3 quarters. ... I had not been as wowed by Aaron Rodgers' season leading up to this game and I think we saw some of the reason why."

Wright finished by stating the Steelers were an "absolute cross-off" when it comes to playoff contention, and it's hard to argue against that claim after what we saw from Pittsburgh during the Week 10 blowout loss.

The Steelers managed to finish the contest with 222 total yards of offense and 18 points, but most of those stats can be chalked up to the garbage-time portion of Sunday Night Football when the Bengals took their foot off the gas.

Hopefully, Wright's comments will serve as bulletin board material for the Steelers moving forward as the team looks to rebound in the second half of the 2026 season.
