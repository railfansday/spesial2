Keisean Henderson's first UH win is particularly sweet, thanks to honey buns

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

MORGANTOWN, W.Va. - How did Houston quarterback Keisean Henderson celebrate his first win as a starter Saturday?

As he walked off the field after the No. 20 Cougars' 34-31 come-from-behind victory at West Virginia, Henderson was spotted eating one of the snacks that have become part of his game-day routine.

Henderson, who started for injured fifth-year senior Conner Weigman, said his mother and grandmother would often pack him honey buns.

"That's just something I take with me every day," Henderson said during a postgame interview with TNT.

Earlier Saturday, Henderson posted a photo on Instagram of three small honey buns on his bed, along with his motto: "Stay Down."

In a video posted on UH's social media accounts, Henderson celebrated with a similar message: "And that's how the Coogs do it. Stay down, honey bun club!"
