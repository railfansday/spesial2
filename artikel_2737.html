FC Barcelona Coach Flick Reacts To Villarreal Remontada

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

FC Barcelona head coach Hansi Flick said he was "very happy" with his team's second half as the La Liga champions came back from behind to beat Villarreal 3-2 at the Spotify Camp Nou on Sunday afternoon.

The Catalans showed worrying signs of ring rust following the international break, and got off to a nightmare start by going 0-2 down in seven minutes.

When Lamine Yamal missed a penalty, it seemed as though Barca might be doomed and sentenced to stay level with Real Madrid on 33 points at the top.

In an inspired 45 minutes after the break, however, Flick's men showed what they are made of by scoring three unanswered goals.

"They took the lead in the first two plays," Flick reflected at full time. "The first goal was a clear mistake on our part and they scored directly. But in the first half, after trailing by two goals, we did very well.

"We tried to create chances and had the chance to score with the penalty. I'm very happy because in the second half we played even better, with more structure. We deserved the win today," Flick added.

With Gerard Martin outstanding and starting the off the remontada with his second strike as a culer, Flick said that the centre back slash left back is "always ready to play" and had done fantastically.

"His evolution and impact on the team is incredible. I'm happy with him because he is very professional and very important to us," Flick said about the number 18.

"In modern football, full-backs are very important, both in attack and defense. In the second half they were excellent, always helping the wingers," the German also remarked regarding Eric Garcia and Joao Cancelo.

With Sabah up next, Flick noted that this was a "a great victory against a Champions-level team". Yet harder tests await the Blaugrana in Atletico Madrid in under a fortnight then Manchester City - both at home.
