Real Madrid&#8217;s next signing spotted at the club&#8217;s training ground

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The next incoming signing at La Liga giants Real Madrid was on Wednesday spotted in attendance at the club's training base in Spain's capital.

Youngster Gabriel is of course expected to be announced as a new Real Madrid player any day now. After the 16-year-old recently made known his intention to depart Manchester United, a host of Europe's leading clubs were quickly credited with an interest in his signature.

Gabriel, an explosive attacker boasting quick feet and an eye for goal, is widely considered one of the standout up-and-coming talents in world football at present.

In the end, it was the aforementioned Real Madrid who beat out the competition, to sew up an agreement with the Englishman and his entourage on the terms of a contract.

And as alluded to above, on Wednesday, Gabriel was afforded a first look at the setting which he will soon call home. As per a report from journalist Fabrizio Romano:

'JJ Gabriel has visited Real Madrid training ground and facilities today together with his family and camp. The move is set to be completed in the upcoming days, decision made.'
