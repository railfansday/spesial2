&quot;Should the opportunity arise...&quot; – Köln chief still dreaming of permanent Mikey Moore transfer

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

When 1. FC Köln signed Mikey Moore on a season-long loan from Tottenham during the summer, an option to make the transfer permanent wasn't included.

It's been reported that Tottenham want the 19-year-old to use this season to further his development before potentially integrating him into their first-team squad next summer.

However, Köln have not completely given up hope of signing the England youth international on a permanent basis.

"Should the opportunity arise next summer to sign him permanently, we would of course sit down with Tottenham," 1. FC Köln managing director for sport Thomas Kessler told Bild.

He was quick to temper expectations, though, adding:

"As things stand today, I consider that scenario rather unlikely."

Nevertheless, according to Bild, Köln are still holding out hope of making the unlikely happen, with Moore's long-term future having "long been a topic of internal discussion" at the club.

The newspaper also identifies another potential scenario, namely extending Moore's loan spell for an additional season.
