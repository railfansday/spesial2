College basketball roundup: Stanford women beaten by 55, most lopsided loss in program history

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Brynn McGaughy scored 21 points, Avery Howell added 20 and No. 16 Washington hit 17 3-pointers en route to a 105-50 win over Stanford on Friday night in Seattle, the most lopsided loss in program history for the Cardinal.

Stanford (4-2), which had won four in a row since a season-opening loss to Arizona, saw the Huskies (5-0) post a 12-0 run to take a 20-4 lead in the first quarter. The Cardinal trailed by 15 after the first quarter, by 30 at halftime and by 41 entering the fourth quarter.

Hailee Swain, who had scored at least 20 points in each of Stanford's first five games, led the Cardinal with 12 points.

Stanford's 55-point margin of defeat topped a 49-point loss to Notre Dame on Feb. 6, 2025. Washington also matched the most points ever scored against the Cardinal, first achieved by Tennessee in a 105-69 win on Dec. 1, 1994.

Cal 70, Hawaii 49: Albina Syla scored 16 points, Zara Russell added 14 and Naya Ojukwu had 10 as the Bears (6-0) beat the Rainbow Wahine (1-4) in the North Shore Showcase in Laie, Hawaii.

The Bears, playing without leading scorer Lulu Twidale and Mjracle Sheppard because of injuries, led by four at halftime but pushed the margin to 10 on a three-point play by Ojukwu with 1:27 left in the third quarter. Cal led by double figures the rest of the way.

"We had a lot of people step up with Lulu being out," Cal coach Charmin Smith said. "I love how Albina stepped up. She just shows up every day and does all the right things. She's a vocal leader for us and she's always in the right spot."

Ritorya Tamilo scored 16 points for Hawaii.

Arizona State 76, Santa Clara 63: The Broncos (4-2), who trailed by four at halftime, saw the game get away as the host Sun Devils (6-0) went on a 13-3 run in the third quarter.

Delainey Miller led Santa Clara with 19 points. Maia Jones scored 14 points and Sophie Glancey added 13.

Rashunda Jones scored 22 points for Arizona State.
