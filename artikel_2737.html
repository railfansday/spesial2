Texans estimate David Montgomery as a full participant, but Woody Marks doesn't practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Texans did not practice on Monday, but they were required to release an injury report ahead of Thursday night's game against the Colts.

Running back David Montgomery was estimated as a full participant again, putting him on track to return this week. A groin injury has sidelined him the past four games.

Montgomery returned to practice Thursday and was a full participant Friday, but he was inactive again Sunday.

The Texans estimated linebacker Azeez Al-Shaair (groin), wide receiver Marvin Harrison Jr. (hamstring), defensive end Danielle Hunter (hamstring), tight end Brevin Jordan (foot/illness) and running back Woody Marks (concussion) as non-participants. Al-Shaair, Harrison, Hunter and Jordan did not play Sunday, and Marks was hurt in the loss to the Browns.

Linebacker Jake Hummel (groin) was listed as limited Monday.

Safety Reed Blankenship (hand), wide receiver Tank Dell (hand/shoulder), offensive tackle Blake Fisher (thumb), cornerback Kamari Lassiter (wrist), offensive guard Febechi Nwaiwu (knee), offensive lineman Keylan Rutledge (shoulder), cornerback Jaylin Smith (hamstring) and offensive guard Wyatt Teller (back) were full participants.
