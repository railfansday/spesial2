Steelers EDGE Nick Herbig OUT vs. Eagles

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Pittsburgh Steelers have downgraded edge rusher Nick Herbig to out for Sunday's Week 11 game against the Philadelphia Eagles, per Adam Schefter.

Herbig has been dealing with a rib injury and didn't practice at all throughout the week. This will likely give more opportunities to Jack Sawyer.

Herbig signed a four-year, $100 million extension this offseason, keeping him in Pittsburgh for the long-term future. He has three sacks so far in the 2026 season, as well as seven tackles for loss and a forced fumble.

The Steelers haven't won in Philadelphia since 1965, and will need a win to get to 7-3 and keep pace with the 7-2 Cincinnati Bengals and the rest of the leaders in the AFC as we hit the second half of the season and get deep into the playoff push.
