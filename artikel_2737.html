LeRoy vs East Rochester score, updates from Section V girls volleyball title game

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

BLOOMFIELD – Only one team will hold the coveted sectional championship block.

No. 1 LeRoy and No. 2 East Rochester will soon battle for the 2026 Section V Class C1 girls volleyball title. The winner will play Section V's C2 champion for a spot in the state tournament.

LeRoy seeks its third consecutive sectional crown in a rematch of a 2025 Class C1 semifinal which spanned five sets.

Follow below for updates throughout the match.

06:10 PM ET, November 3 2026, Jeff Uveino

LeRoy and East Rochester are scheduled for a 7 p.m. start on Tuesday, Nov. 3.
