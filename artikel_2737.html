Braves prospect Owen Carey makes Baseball America’s AFL Hot Sheet

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

On the final hot sheet of the 2026 Arizona Fall League season, Atlanta Braves outfield prospect Owen Carey has played his way onto the list. Carey hit .444/.688/1.132 with a homer, double, four runs scored, and eight batted in to put himself on the list as the #6 prospect of the week.

For his entire time in the AFL Carey hit .328/.438/.492 with three doubles, a pair of triples, and a home run with 10 walks to just seven strikeouts over his 73 plate appearances. Carey started out a bit slow in the AFL, but ended very strong to post that stat line.

With Carey spending his last 35 games in Double-A in 2026 and his strong 18 games in the AFL, the expectation for him in 2027 is likely to open back in Columbus with a chance to receive a promotion to Triple-A at some point this coming season. Depending on the status of big league ball and the upcoming lockout, he could be positioning himself to make his big league debut at some point in the next year.
