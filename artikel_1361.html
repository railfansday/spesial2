Leeds United's Most Underrated Player: Ao Tanaka

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Leeds United produced a brilliant second-half performance to come from 1-0 down to beat Chelsea 4-1 at Stamford Bridge.

Emmanuel Emegha had put the home side in front in the 25th minute, smashing the ball past James Trafford. But Leeds provided an instant response, levelling soon after as Noah Okafor slotted into the bottom corner.

From that point, it felt as though Leeds were the more dominant side and went ahead for the first time just after half-time. After Morgan Rogers handled the ball inside the box, Dominic Calvert-Lewin slammed home from the spot to make it 2-1.

Okafor doubled Leeds' lead with a thumping finish before Brenden Aaronson put gloss on the scoreline and made it 4-1 late on. The American turned the Chelsea back-line inside and out, and tucked a low shot beyond the hapless Emiliano Martinez.

It was a fine result for the Whites as they recorded back-to-back wins for the first time this season and moved up to sixth in the Premier League table, just five points behind second-place Brighton & Hove Albion.

A spot in Europe is not out of the question for Leeds, who have had quite the resurgence after battling against relegation for most of last season, and there are some players who have got the most credit for their up-turn in form.

Calvert-Lewin, Ethan Ampadu, Okafor, Trafford and Tarik Muharemovic are among those who have got a great deal of praise from the Leeds supporters, but there is one player who appears to be going under the radar for his impact.

Ao Tanaka is the man in question, and he has been outstanding for Leeds this season. Since coming on for the second half of the 1-1 draw against Brentford back in August, the Japan international has since started every Premier League match.

He has partnered Ampadu in a midfield two with Farke deciding to go with two attack-minded midfielders and Calvert-Lewin up front. It's a system that has worked and Tanaka's impact is evident.

Indeed, Leeds have lost just 10 of the 66 games Tanaka has started following his move from Fortuna Dusseldorf in 2024. He helped Leeds win promotion back to the Premier League in his first season, before having a major impact on their return to the top flight last campaign. But he has taken his game to a new level this term and has been excellent for Farke.

To further back Tanaka's impact, out of the 308 players with more than 50 starts in Leeds' history, only Alan Peacock (12 per cent) and Mick Jones (14 per cent) have lost a lower percentage than Tanaka (15 per cent). That is a remarkable statistic and one that proves how important Tanaka is to Farke and his system right now.

Tanaka was Leeds' outstanding performer in the win at Chelsea, with Yorkshire Evening Post giving him a 9/10. His excellent work out of possession, winning the ball back and stopping Cole Palmer from having an impact, stood out. But he was equally as good with the ball and helped Leeds progress up the pitch and create chances.
