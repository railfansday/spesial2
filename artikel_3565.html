If you’re anything like Us, you’re obsessed with nearly everything Katie Holmes wears. She’s perfected the art of casual yet chic styling, which is why her outfits are undeniably relatable (and possible to copy). Her latest look to catch our eye? An unexpected version of a double-breasted trench coat — only her choice is made with luxurious leather. It’s only right that we went on a hunt to find the outerwear style, and we totally nailed it with these 15 picks.

The Happy Hours actress wore her effortlessly sleek Milan get-up at the Tod Spring Summer 2027 Fashion Show, and while her outfit felt fairly simple, the buttery-soft material of her coat made Us zoom in. At first glance, it looks like a classic gabardine; however, the natural sheen of Holmes elevated the piece tenfold. It’s a fall coat style we didn’t realize we needed . . . until we saw how instantly it upgraded a basic white tee and beige trousers. We don’t know where she got her exact trench, but these faux and real leather options deliver the look. Psst, you’ll also find picks that go a little out of the box, featuring seasonal colors, cropped hems and even animal embossed textures.
