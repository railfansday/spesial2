Personal Consumer Issues • Re: Costco Enterprise Car Rental

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

This is more general than Enterprise who I have not used in a long time. But I do use costco car rental a lot.

Usually, the final price is the same as the costco quote. But at the counter, they typically charge your card more than that and tell you your final price will be X when you return the car. X should be what Costco said unless you dont return it with a full tank of gas.

They always ask about fuel service, insurance, toll both packages, etc. I think most people turn all of this down and they are used to being turned down. Rarely are they a bargain, but the toll package can be if you are incurring 10 to 20 tolls a day. I just put avoid tolls in my Maps app, but in some places they can be hard to bypass.

Beware of the fuel service charge if you dont return a full tank. They charge a lot per gallon. My last rental was Budget and they read the gas guage electronically. The gauge looked full and I filled it 20 miles from where I returned the car because it is inconvent to get gas close to the return center. They charged me for 0.8 gallons of gas at I think $10.99 per gallon. They always seem to have a way to nickel and dime you.

Finally, most rental car places have no problem putting you in a bigger car for the same price if they are out of what you reserved. This was a problem last time as I got a larger SUV than the small one I wanted. My 91 year old mom had a hard time getting in to it (too high). The car I had the time before was too low and she couldnt get out easily. So I need a goldilocks size car for seat height.
