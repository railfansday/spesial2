‘GTA 6’ Hits Massive $3.79 Billion in First-Day Sales

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Crime pays: Rockstar Games’ “Grand Theft Auto 6″ preorder and first-day sales reached $3.79 billion, the game developer announced Friday.

Per, Rockstar Games parent company Take-Two Interactive, that total sets “a new record for entertainment across any medium, and becoming the biggest entertainment launch in history.”

A spokesperson for Take-Two Interactive declined to comment on number of “GTA 6” copies sold. For the sake of comparison, Sony Pictures’ “Spider-Man: Brand New Day” reaped $2.52 billion at the global box office in 2026, the No. 1 movie of the year to date. Christopher Nolan’s blockbuster “The Odyssey” has taken in just over $1.8 billion to date.

On its first day of release, “GTA 6” made roughly as much as James Cameron’s last two “Avatar” movies, “The Way of Water” and “Fire and Ash,” have grossed combined. With just Day 1 sales, “GTA 6” has already out-grossed the highest grossing movie of all time, “Avengers: Endgame” ($2.9 billion), in one day.

“GTA 6” launched Thursday (Nov. 19) for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S gaming consoles and was also available on Xbox One via Xbox’s Cloud Gaming service. The game retails at $80 for the standard edition and $100 for the ultimate version. Preorders for “GTA 6” opened June 25.

With a reported total gameplay time of approximately 80 hours to complete its main storyline, “GTA 6” follows duo Jason and Lucia as they navigate their way through criminal life in the fictional Leonida.

“GTA 6” has reached a 99 Metacritic score (tying it for the highest-rated video game of all time) and received a nomination for Game of the Year at The Game Awards this December.

“It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of ‘Grand Theft Auto VI,’” Rockstar Games president and founder Sam Houser said. “We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.”
