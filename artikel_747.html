From the Olivias & Madonna to RAYE & Rosalía, Women Dominate 2027 Pop/Dance Grammy Nominations

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

On the Billboard Pop Shop Podcast, we're breaking down the pop/dance categories, while Katie takes Keith inside the Rock Hall ceremony.

If you’re a pop singer/songwriter named Olivia, the 2027 Grammy nominations were kind to you.

Olivias Rodrigo and Dean fared well during Monday’s announcement, with Rodrigo picking up seven nominations and Dean picking up six — including three Big Four nods for each. On the new Billboard Pop Shop Podcast, Katie & Keith are taking a closer look at those top prizes as well as the pop/dance categories, where RAYE, Rosalía, Madonna, Tinashe and Ariana Grande also lead the way. Plus, Katie takes Keith (and listeners!) inside Saturday’s Rock & Roll Hall of Fame induction ceremony, where Cynthia Erivo and Travis Barker paid striking tribute to Phil Collins with “In the Air Tonight,” the Red Hot Chili Peppers donned full long white hair and beard wigs to honor Rick Rubin, and more highlights from a nearly five-hour show.

Also on the podcast, we’ve got chart news on Christmas songs surging on the Billboard Hot 100, with eight in the top 50 (including a debut from a very current superstar artist) and how one — or more — of them could jump into the top 10 next week. Plus, Rodrigo’s you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love captures a fifth week atop the Billboard 200.

The Billboard Pop Shop Podcast is your one-stop shop for all things pop on Billboard‘s weekly charts. You can always count on a lively discussion about the latest pop news, fun chart stats and stories, new music, and guest interviews with music stars and folks from the world of pop. Casual pop fans and chart junkies can hear Billboard‘s executive digital director, West Coast, Katie Atkinson and Billboard’s managing director, charts and data operations, Keith Caulfield every week on the podcast, which can be streamed on Billboard.com or downloaded in Apple Podcasts or your favorite podcast provider.
