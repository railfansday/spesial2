Three grabs for Tucker Kraft against the Rams

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Green Bay Packers TE Tucker Kraft caught three of his five targets for 24 yards and lost a fumble against the Los Angeles Rams in Week 12.

Why it Matters: Kraft has been scuffling lately, and over the last three games, he has a total of five catches for 44 yards. Coming off knee surgery, Kraft may be wearing down a bit, but it is also worth noting that the Packers' offense has struggled in general in the last two games. At this point, Kraft is a risky TE1 option.
