Bills HC Joe Brady applauds Buffalo for finding ways to win on Josh Allen’s off night

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Buffalo Bills didn't need a vintage Josh Allen performance to take down the Kansas City Chiefs on Thanksgiving night, and head coach Joe Brady couldn't be prouder of how his team found a way.

Allen endured one of the roughest outings of his career, but Buffalo still escaped with a 13-10 win at Highmark Stadium thanks to Tyler Bass' 57-yard field goal in the final seconds.

After the game, Brady pointed to the gritty victory as a sign of what his team is made of.

"To win a game like this when Josh Allen had 80 passing yards says a lot about our football team," Brady said, via Matthew Bove.

Allen completed just 10 of his 21 passes for a season-low 81 yards with no touchdowns, two interceptions and an 18.3 passer rating. Both picks came in the red zone, with safeties Alohi Gilman and Nohl Williams snuffing out what could have been scoring opportunities for Buffalo.

It was the second straight game in which Allen threw two interceptions. The star quarterback was quick to take accountability. "I have to be better; I need to be better; I will be better. Thankful for my team," he said.

Fortunately for the Bills, the rest of the roster picked up the slack. James Cook carried the load on the ground, piling up 117 rushing yards and a touchdown on 32 carries. As a team, Buffalo ran the ball 43 times and controlled the clock for nearly 37 minutes.

The defense also held up its end of the bargain, limiting Patrick Mahomes to 16-of-29 passing for 174 yards and no touchdowns.

Bass then delivered in the clutch. After tying the game with a 54-yarder early in the fourth quarter, he drilled the game-winner to send the home crowd into a frenzy.

The win extended Buffalo's winning streak to seven games and improved its record to 10-1, keeping the Bills in sole possession of the AFC's top spot.
