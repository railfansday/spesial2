FC 27 Pumpkin Seeds SBC solutions and rewards

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Pumpkin Seeds SBC it is now live in FC 27, and you can complete this fodder challenge practically for free. In return, you get a bonus pack that could provide you with something valuable. You also come one step closer to completing the completionist objective, which will reward you with ultimate scrim tokens and more packs.

Let’s first find out how to complete this challenge easily at the lowest possible fodder costs.

The Pumpkin Seeds SBC has only one task to complete, so it’s not very difficult. However, the solo task has specific requirements you’ll need to meet to complete the challenge.

Ideally, you will use cards that are already in your Ultimate Team squad. When you supply fodder from your own club, you do not need to spend any coins on the market. This saves your coins and increases the valuation of whatever rewards you get.

However, the requirements mentioned above could force you to get stuck. To complete the challenge, you can then use the following solution:

When you complete the challenge, you will get a Large Gold Player Pack. This pack includes 12 gold player items, and two of them are guaranteed to be rated 82 or higher. It has one of the best statistical chances for getting walkout or promo items.

While the cards are all untradeable, you can always find something valuable in the pack. At worst, you can use the cards to complete more challenges in the future. For that reason, do not miss out on the Pumpkin Seeds SBC while it’s live in Ultimate Team.
