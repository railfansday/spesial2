Crestview Man Pleads Guilty to Distributing Methamphetamine

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Pensacola, Florida –John Martin Elmore, 37, of Crestview, Florida, pleaded guilty in federal court to one count of conspiracy to distribute more than 50 grams of methamphetamine or 500 grams of a mixture and substance containing methamphetamine and one count of possession of more than 50 grams of methamphetamine or 500 grams of a mixture and substance containing methamphetamine. The plea was announced by John P. Heekin, United States Attorney for the Northern District of Florida.
