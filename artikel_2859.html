Kansas State report card: Grading the Wildcats' flop vs Oklahoma State

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

MANHATTAN — The downfall of Collin Klein's first season as Kansas State's football coach hit its latest low point in its 66-28 loss to Oklahoma State.

The Wildcats didn't stand a chance. The Cowboys' high-powered offense got what it wanted, and the Wildcats' offense turned the ball over too many times to make it a shootout.

You can probably guess how our grades for this game turned out.

It doesn't matter that Kansas State played backup quarterbacks; the Wildcats should have played better against what's been a mediocre Oklahoma State defense. Everything was bad outside of the two times when the Cowboys forgot to cover Derrick Salley.

You can't give up 66 points and not get a failing grade, even if you forced a pair of turnovers.

At least they didn't allow Oklahoma State to return a punt for a touchdown? The hold on a good kickoff return to start the game set the tone. At least Monterrio Elston looks like a solid choice to return kicks.
