GTA 6 Boss Gives Official Update On GTA 7

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

GTA 6 was such a monumentally anticipated release that everything dealing with it was under a microscope. This was the case for several other GTA games before it, once GTA 3 brought even more fame to the series. But with GTA 6, and expectations that it would be one of the biggest entertainment releases in any sector, it made sense there would be even more scrutiny.

That's ultimately part of the reason why Rockstar (under the ownership of Take-Two) decided to delay the game multiple times. Since everyone in the world would be watching when it came out, it needed a level of polish that many AAA games don't get anymore.

So imagine our surprise when GTA 7 was already mentioned, less than a week after the release of GTA 6. Speaking to Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick, TBPN got an unexpected response regarding GTA 7 [via X].

Talking to Zelnick, when asked if "GTA 7 would be a live version of the entire world," Zelnick had this to say in response:

I can't help myself. We at Take-Two just went live with GTA 6 on November 19, wait let me check my watch, it's still November 19, and we're talking about GTA 7...I mean guys, really? I don't know, my father-in-law had a line whenever...he had many great business achievements...and if you ever said anything nice, he'd say those are yesterday's headlines.

“Once you get through several hundred hours of the most incredible fun you’ve ever had, ask the question again," he also noted. While it sounds rough on paper, Zelnick took the response in stride and talked about the achievements of GTA 6.

Once again, GTA 6 has entered the collective consciousness, and it's pretty much all the entertainment industry is talking about right now. There are so many moving parts in GTA 6; it's wild, especially when it comes to the Easter Eggs, secrets, and detailed recreations of South Florida's infrastructure.

Having played through the entire game, it's a true testament to how much money Rockstar is willing to throw at their game budgets. We saw this with most mainline GTA games, but Red Dead Redemption 2 also tested the limits of what AAA games could offer. With its expansive and dynamic open world, it whisked players back to a bygone era.

But with GTA 6, it's much different. The team had to recreate a modern aesthetic but also give it its own unique spin, which I think they succeeded at. After all, the world of GTA is slightly askew compared to ours, with alternate acronyms for organizations and a lot more nuances.

GTA 7 might be over a decade away at this point, but people will be talking about GTA 6 for that long, if not longer. That's definitely going to be the case whenever Rockstar gets to the next iteration of GTA Online, which is bound to happen at some point, along with the inevitable PC port.
