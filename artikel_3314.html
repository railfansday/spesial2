Teagan Meredith & Jacob Batalon Join Disney’s ‘Lilo & Stitch 2’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

EXCLUSIVE: Disney Live Action has added two to the cast of Lilo & Stitch 2: young up-and-comer Teagan Meredith (Fallout) and the Spider-Man franchise’s Jacob Batalon.

The studio also confirmed returning cast, including Maia Kealoha as Lilo, along with Courtney B. Vance, Kaipo Dudoit, Amy Hill, Tia Carrere and Billy Magnussen.

Marcell the Shell‘s Dean Fleischer Camp directed the 2025 film, which delivered the biggest Memorial Day weekend opening in box office history and became Hollywood’s first billion-dollar movie of that year. Chris Sanders, co-writer and co-director of the original film and the original voice of Stitch, reprised the voice role. Watch on Deadline

Sanders is writing and directing the new film, with Jonathan Eirich producing. Ryan Halprin and Tom Peitzman are executive producing, with SVP of Production Stephen Feder overseeing the project, reporting to Disney Live Action President Daria Cercek. The film is now in production in Hawaii.

Meredith is a rising actress who has previously been seen in Prime Video’s Fallout and AMC’s The Terror: Devil in Silver. She made her professional stage debut at just 7 years old in Lilliana Padilla’s How to Defend Yourself at New York Theatre Workshop and will next appear in the film Claws, written and directed by the Adams Brothers. She is represented by Nouveaux Talent Management and Zuri Agency.

Batalon is best known for his breakout role as Ned Leeds in Marvel/Sony’s Spider-Man franchise, most recently reprising the fan-favorite character in Spider-Man: Brand New Day, the highest-grossing film of 2026 and the third-highest-grossing film of all time. He also recently starred in the action comedy The Wrecking Crew, opposite Jason Momoa and Dave Bautista, and in the action thriller Novocaine. Previously, he exec produced and starred in the titular role of Syfy’s Reginald the Vampire. He is represented by Independent Artist Group and Brecheen Feldman Breimer.
