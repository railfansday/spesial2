Crystal Palace tipped to accept '£50m' Ismaila Sarr offer after initially rejecting Liverpool - expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Crystal Palace could accept a huge offer for Ismaila Sarr in an attempt to raise funds for reinvestment in the January transfer window.

The 28-year-old was heavily linked with a move away from Selhurst Park during the summer, contributing to a turbulent start to the season in which he has hardly featured.

Following the appointment of Thomas Frank, questions have been asked about the forward's long-term future, and he could be allowed to move on if he still wishes to leave.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Frank could green-light the sale of Sarr to raise funds as he looks to improve his squad.

Liverpool made a £50million offer for the Senegal international during the summer, and a similar offer could tempt Crystal Palace to cash in when the mid-season window opens.

Frank is in need of investment in January, and hopes he will be backed to improve key areas of the team, with Sarr's sale potentially freeing up space and funds.

Brown told Football Insider on 19 November that Frank would have the final say on Sarr's future, as he weighs up his long-term role in this Crystal Palace side.

The Senegal international has started just one game so far this season, with ongoing injury issues and his previous desire to leave both playing a part in his absence.

Should the decision be made to let him go, much will be decided by the fee involved, and a significant offer could allow Palace more room to spend big in January.

"I think there is a real chance Crystal Palace could cash in on Sarr if they get another £50million offer," Brown told Football Insider.

"Does Frank see Sarr as part of his plans? Or does he see him as a sellable asset who could be moved on to make space in the squad and maybe raise some funds?

"A fee like the one Liverpool offered in the summer would give Palace more room to spend in January, and to sign players that the new manager wants to bring in.

"It depends how they want to look at it, is Sarr such an important player that they can't afford to let him go, or would it be worth it to improve in other areas?

"That's the decision they have to made in January, but if a big offer comes along, I wouldn't be surprised if they let him go.

"There are areas in this squad where Crystal Palace are not good enough, Frank will be looking to change that, and that means they will need to invest in new players."

There is no doubt that Frank will want to have the backing of the Crystal Palace board ahead of what could be a crucial transfer window for their future in the Premier League.

Having sunk to bottom place, there are clear weaknesses in the squad, particularly at the back where they have failed to replace some key players in recent windows.

Brown told Football Insider that Crystal Palace have likely already agreed to back Frank as part of the deal to bring him to Selhurst Park in the first place.

Frank's appointment was officially confirmed on 16 November, after Pierre Sage was dismissed from his role, and he will now be tasked with turning their season around

To do that, he will need investment in his squad, and he has likely already identified players and positions which are in need of improvement.
