Wolves finally unlock £50m-rated winger - Middlesbrough should be on red alert in promotion hunt

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It's taken a little while for Mateus Mane to warm up to the Championship, but the talented teenager is starting to come to the boil for Wolves, which can only be a concern for their fellow promotion hopefuls.

Wolves have gone under the radar for the most part in their second-tier return so far. Cesar Peixoto is still not the most popular manager in Wolverhampton, but the Old Gold are cruising through certain games this season.

They momentarily climbed into the top two on Tuesday night after a convincing 3-0 win over a Derby County side in disarray, and have now won five of their last six, including both after coming back from the international break.

What's more exciting, though, is that Mane has come back from the break on red-hot form and is finally seeing things go his way. For someone who had a £50 million price tag slapped on him earlier this year, we're finally starting to see why.

Mane was a rare bright spark in an otherwise dismal season at Molineux last year. The Portuguese wideman was brought to the forefront by Rob Edwards, and gave a Wolves side who were doomed after two points through 18 games a much-needed boost.

He scored and assisted in their first league win of the campaign against West Ham United back in January, and followed that up with another goal in a 1-1 draw against Everton just days later.

At that point, Wolves were destined for the drop, but now they had an 18-year-old superstar who could continue his career at the top level. Liverpool and Manchester United were two of the big clubs interested, but reports in late February hinted that the Old Gold wanted £50 million for him.

In the end, a combination of that price tag putting suitors off, and Mane himself being happy to develop in the Championship for a year, meant that he stayed at Molineux, and after five goal contributions in a terrible Premier League side, he was primed for a big year in a second-tier outfit expected to challenge right at the top.

A year in the Championship can be good for a side to reset and return to the Premier League in a stronger position. For Mane, it would have given him the opportunity to be one of the best players in the league, if things worked out, and build his confidence ahead of a career in the top flight.

Ultimately, that wasn't the case, and the second-tier proved difficult to begin with for the 19-year-old.

The first game of the season against Blackburn Rovers was a bit of a culture shock for the club as a whole, but definitely for Mane, who was often found needing to offer more defensively against Ryan Alebiosu off the Rovers' right, and was subbed after an hour with the side 2-1 down.

From then on, he began to get into his groove, completing dribbles and getting into shooting positions, but not having the end product needed to profit from those, which was a big frustration and a little alarming for those who expected him to go from strength to strength at this level.

Peixoto even dropped Mane after back-to-back defeats against Middlesbrough and Bolton Wanderers, with new arrival Rafiki Said taking the left-wing spot after he returned from an early injury, and the 19-year-old found himself coming off the bench four times in a five-game run.

Said has kept his place on the left, but Mane has now been deployed on the right for the last two games, and it's starting to work wonders for the Portuguese starlet.

Mane is finding that end product both in terms of creating chances and finally scoring his first goal of the season with what was his 39th shot of the campaign in the win against Derby on Tuesday night.

But the excellence began against Charlton Athletic on Saturday. The 19-year-old played off the right and began the game by spurning a big chance. At the time, he had yet to score, and many were feeling he was simply cursed at this level.

Mane responded by just setting up goals instead, though. He assisted both in the 2-1 win over the Addicks, taking his tally to three for the season, and created seven chances and four big chances in a Man of the Match display.

He was able to follow that up against the Rams, too. It was his incredible skill which led to Adam Armstrong's opener; he could have grabbed at least one assist, too, before he then got his goal to put Wolves two to the good.

That's now three goal involvements in two games, when he had just one in the previous 15. If this is a sign of things to come, fellow title-challengers, such as Middlesbrough, Birmingham City, Stoke City and Southampton, need to be aware.

Granted, these performances have come against two of the poorer sides in the division so far, and with Lincoln City on the horizon on Saturday, we won't know if Mane is now a force to be reckoned with against top teams, but it's promising for the 19-year-old.

As mentioned, his strike against Derby was his 39th shot of the season. Only 10 players in the entire league have had more, per Opta. So, it's not been for a lack of trying, and he's been the victim of some bad luck, too.

Hopefully, that goal can open up the floodgates and the Portuguese wideman can be more of a threat.

In terms of chance creation, too, the 14 he's made over the past two games have shot him up those ranks. Now, only four players can say they've created more, and only Finn Azaz and Jarrod Bowen can say they have in open play.

Time will tell whether this is the start of a real breakthrough into Championship superstardom for Mane. If it is, a Wolves side who were already projecting as one of the best sides in the league could get even better.
