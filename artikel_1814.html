What channel is Rangers v Falkirk? TV and live stream plus team news, referee and VAR

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Something has to give when Rangers host Falkirk in the Scottish Premiership at Ibrox on Saturday.

While Rangers go into the match protecting a 14-game unbeaten run stretching back to August, Falkirk are seeking a seventh consecutive victory in the top flight for the first time since 1907. Only one of those sequences will remain intact at full-time.

The excellent form of both clubs makes for an interesting fixture, with Rangers having the opportunity to move top of the table for at least 24 hours before Celtic play Hearts at Tynecastle on Sunday. Derek McInnes’ side are one point behind Martin O’Neill’s league leaders but will need a victory to knock them off top spot due to Celtic boasting a superior goal difference of +21 compared to their +14.

Falkirk meanwhile can move four points clear of Hearts in third if they extend their winning streak, but to do so the Bairns will need to break a 101-year Ibrox hoodoo having gone 61 league visits to Rangers (D10, L51) without a victory since a 3-2 victory in November 1925.

A high-scoring affair could be on the cards given the past three league meetings between the teams have served up 19 goals, 13 for Rangers and six for John McGlynn’s side, with Rangers winning all three matches.

The Scottish Premiership fixture between Rangers and Falkirk takes place at Ibrox Stadium, Glasgow, on Saturday, November 28, 2026. Kick-off is 8pm.

The match has not been selected for live TV coverage in the UK. Highlights will be shown on Sportscene

Rangers pair Cammy Devlin and Lawrence Shankland are back in partial training after hamstring injuries, while Youssef Chermiti (knee) remains out for the long term.

Falkirk are still without captain Brad Spencer (knee), Ryan Strain, Leon McCann (both thigh) and Aodhan Doherty (foot).

Ryan Lee is the match referee. He will be joined on the pitch by assistant referees Ross Nelson and Colin Drummond, while Alastair Grieve has been appointed as the fourth official. Duncan Nicolson is the VAR official with Andrew Dallas as the assistant VAR.
