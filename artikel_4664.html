Cells can adjust gene activity to partly offset extra or missing chromosomes

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

When a cell loses or gains an extra chromosome, the activity of hundreds or even thousands of genes is affected. A new study published in Nature Communications shows that cells have a much greater ability than previously thought to compensate for such changes. The findings provide new insight into how genes are regulated and may help improve our understanding of how cancer cells cope with major changes in their genetic material.
