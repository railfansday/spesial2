Sneak Peeks of All Tonight’s ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Finale Freestyles Just Dropped

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Dancing With the Stars is airing its Season 35 finale tonight, and the remaining couples will perform three routines each: Instant Dances, the Judges’ Choice Round, and the Freestyle Round.

The Freestyle Round is undeniably the most exciting since the couples are in total control of their dances and get to be extremely creative. And we just got a bunch of sneak peeks at rehearsals!

These two are doing a Freestyle to “Numb” by Linkin Park, and several moving mirrors seem to be involved.

Amber and Pasha are doing a Freestyle to “Like a Prayer” and “Music” by Madonna, seemingly inspired by Amber’s short program at the 2026 U.S. Nationals.

Maura and Mark are dancing to “Diamonds Are a Girl’s Best Friend” by Marilyn Monroe and “The Parting Glass” and “Flowers of the Red Hill,” which are traditional Irish folk songs.

Connor and Rylee are pulling a Tom Holland and doing a freestyle to “Singin’ in the Rain” by Gene Kelly and “Umbrella” by Rihanna. And naturally literal umbrellas are being used.

Tatyana and Jan will dance to “Minnie the Moocher” by Cab Calloway and “Over the Rainbow” by Judy Garland. It looks gorgeous so far.

Tune in to the Dancing With the Stars finale tonight at 8 p.m. ET, and reminder: it’s live on both the East and West coasts, which means voting should be at an all-time high.
