Swimply: Regulatory Affairs Manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Swimply is the marketplace that lets homeowners rent out their private pools, hot tubs, courts and backyards by the hour. Thousands of hosts across the United States and Canada use Swimply to share space they already own with families and small groups in their community.

Because hourly backyard rentals are new, most local codes were written before they existed. Towns, counties, health departments and homeowners associations regularly try to fit Swimply into rules written for hotels, public pools, home businesses or overnight short-term rentals. We are building a dedicated regulatory function to manage those matters at scale and to move the rules in our favor, state by state.

You will own the day-to-day operation of the program and work directly with our General Counsel, our CEO, our Head of Operations and the outside lobbyists and local counsel we retain.

The Regulatory Affairs Manager runs Swimply's regulatory program end to end. Half of the job is operational: every notice a host or the company receives is logged, answered and tracked to a close. The other half is strategic: finding the bills, partners and officials that let us fix the rules for a whole state at once instead of town by town.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.

🇬🇧 United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland
