Legend Of Zelda: Tears Of The Kingdom Sequel Gets Official Update From Nintendo

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom remains one of the last mainline Zelda games, with the far smaller-in-scope Echoes of Wisdom serving as the final entry, thus far, a year after its release in 2024. Since then, fans have been wondering what the future of the Zelda franchise looks like, and whether it would continue down the same open-world path as TotK or go in an altogether new direction. Nintendo has, for the most part, remained fairly tight-lipped about the whole affair, which has only further frustrated fans.

However, recently, Nintendo has begun to be a little more coy regarding the future of the Wilds era and, indeed, Zelda altogether. Across numerous interviews, Eiji Aonuma and co. have begun sprouting the seeds of what fans can expect from the next mainline Zelda game. Crucially, they've been discussing how it may be more connected to Ocarina of Time than one might have first imagined, and how Zelda as a whole could be going in a fresh and exciting new direction.

In previous interviews, Eiji Aonuma has hinted that the next mainline Zelda game could more closely resemble the Ocarina of Time remake, in large part thanks to the younger talent working on it, who are set to spearhead the future of the franchise. These comments have been further supported by a recent Ask the Developer hosted by Nintendo for the aforementioned Ocarina of Time remake. During the interview, Aonuma further hinted at how the remake is shaping the future of the series.

When asked how, 15 years after the release of the 3DS version of Ocarina of Time, development for the Switch 2 remake was sparked, Aonuma discussed how he questioned whether the new staff who have only worked on more recent Zelda games "truly understand just how significant The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time was to the series as a whole." This, naturally, pushed the team to work on remaking the game, and it was through that process that they realized insights gained from its development could be utilized in future Zelda games. Aonuma explained:

By learning the game structure and development style behind the original The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, and by actually rebuilding it as a full remake, it may trigger new insights that they can carry forward into future development. If we could achieve that, I thought it would be incredibly valuable. That’s what led us to the conclusion that now is the right time to revisit The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time once again.

The "new insights" that the team "can carry forward into future development" have obviously yet to be confirmed by Nintendo and Aonuma. However, one can imagine that it relates to the more linear, dungeon-focused progression of the earlier titles, a facet of The Legend of Zelda that the Wilds era moved squarely away from. It would certainly be interesting for Nintendo to return to this style of Zelda design, even if it was, prior to Breath of the Wild's release, proving not to be working as sales for the series dropped significantly.

It is unlikely that we'll learn much about the future of the mainline Zelda games for some time. With a Majora's Mask remake heavily rumored to be in the works, a full-blown mainline release is likely still years away. One can hope that Nintendo will be a tad more forthcoming over the next few years, but it's also entirely possible that the first we'll hear about it will be through its own dedicated trailer. For now, fans' only recourse is to enjoy the remakes and revisit Tears of the Kingdom whenever they get a hankering for the Wilds era.
