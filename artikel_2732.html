Alabama softball reveals full 2027 schedule

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Alabama softball faithful will have 28 chances to see the Crimson Tide at home in 2027, per the full schedule released Wednesday, Nov. 25.

The Crimson Tide will begin its 31st season in program history and 29th under coach Patrick Murphy on Thursday, Feb. 11 at the Sarasota Softball Classic in Florida.

Alabama returns to Tuscaloosa for its home opener with the Easton Bama Bash featuring games against Georgia Tech, Lindenwood and Southern Miss from Feb. 26-28, the first of three tournaments at Rhoads Stadium. The Tide will also host Winthrop, St. John's and Nicholls for the T-Mobile Crimson Classic from March 5-7 before welcoming former national champions Cal State Fullerton, Fresno State and Florida State for the Tournament of Champions from March 12-14.

Conference play begins March 19 against Texas A&M at home. Alabama's remaining home SEC opponents include Ole Miss, Florida and LSU. The Crimson Tide will travel to Texas, Mississippi State, Georgia and Oklahoma this year.

Alabama will wrap up its regular season with a three-game series against the Sooners from May 6-8 in Norman.

The Crimson Tide will also get an early look at the new SEC Tournament venue when it faces North Alabama on March 9 at Toyota Field in Madison before the tournament takes place May 11-15.
