Social media reacts to Commanders' huge win over Giants on TNF

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Washington Commanders showed some resiliency on Thursday Night Football, overcoming Jayden Daniels' first two interceptions of the season in the fourth quarter to come back and defeat the New York Giants 24-21.

Daniels played well and was looking to tight end Chig Okonkwo for a score, but Giants safety Jevon Holland picked him off. A good play by Holland. Washington's defense did its job, giving the ball back to Daniels and the offense. On fourth-and-1 near midfield, Daniels inexplicably throws another interception, seemingly ending the game. Fortunately, the Commanders had all their timeouts remaining and the two-minute warning. After a huge Sonny Styles stop on third down, the defense again got the ball back.

Daniels made them pay this time, leading the game-winning drive and finding Stefon Diggs for a 32-yard touchdown. Still, the Giants had 57 seconds remaining. After a big first-down play to get the ball around the 50-yard line, Washington defensive coordinator Daronte Jones had seen enough. He came after New York quarterback JJ McCarthy on three straight plays, and on the final play, cornerback Rasul Douglas intercepted him to end the game.

It was another up-and-down night on X — formerly Twitter — for Washington fans. At times, it looked like another painful, heartbreaking loss. But this time, Daniels responded, leading his team to a huge, season-saving (at least for now) win.
