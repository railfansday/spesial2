Raiders trade deadline 2026: Trackings rumors

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The NFL trade deadline is a week from today, Nivember 10, at 1 p.m.

The Las Vegas Raiders could be active in the trade market in the next week, whether it's as buyers, seller or maybe even both.

Las Vegas general manager John Spytek could be busy, trying to add key pieces to the Raiders' roster. And, of course, there may be speculation the Raiders could trade some players such as defensive end Maxx Crosby, quarterback Kirk Cousins or guard Jackson Powers-Johnson.

You can follow the rumor mill here up until the deadline. The rumors will appear in reverse chronological order:

It's the morning of the trade deadline and the Cousins-Vikings rumors won't die, especially after Carson Wentz's horrible outing Monday night.

ESPN is reporting the Colts could trade wide receiver Josh Downs for a fourth-round pick. Perhaps the Raiders could be involved.

ESPN is reporting the Raiders haven't gotten any calls for Cousins and have no plans to trade him.

Albert Breer thinks the Vikings want Kirk Cousins back, but the Raiders are not into the idea.

The Kirk Cousins and Minnesota Vikings rumors won't die.

The 49ers are rumors to be interested in Colts' running back Jonathan Taylor. If that happens, Christian McCaffrey could change teams next year. Could a reunion with Kilt Kubiak make sense?

Adam Schefter examines available running backs and mentions and AFC West team in that hunt.
