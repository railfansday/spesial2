Buckle (BKE) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Fashion pants grew nearly 60% as denim demand shifted.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 20, 2026 at 1:27PM EST

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Buckle (BKE +6.19%) reported third quarter net income of $48 million, maintaining profitability despite unseasonably warm weather that impacted early denim sales. Management reported a 2.6% increase in net sales, supported by significant growth in the women's and kids segments and a shift toward new fashion silhouettes. The company continued its strategic expansion into premium and outlet centers, completing 14 new store openings year to date while managing merchandise margins through reduced markdowns and private label growth. Operating margins were pressured by increased labor and marketing costs, but the company ended the quarter with $332.2 million in total cash and investments.

Thomas Heacock: Good morning, and thanks for joining us this morning. Our November 20, 2026 press release reported that net income for the 13-week third quarter, which ended October 31, 2026, was $48 million or $0.94 per share on a diluted basis compared to net income of $48.7 million or $0.96 per share on a diluted basis for the prior year 13-week third quarter which ended November 1, 2025. Year-to-date, net income for the 39-week period ended October 31, 2026, was $139.3 million or $2.73 per share on a diluted basis which compares to net income of $128.9 million or $2.55 per share on a diluted basis for the prior year 39-week period ended November 1, 2025.

Net sales for the 13-week third quarter increased 2.6% to $329.2 million compared to net sales of $320.8 million for the prior year 13-week third quarter. Comparable store sales for the quarter increased 0.6% in comparison to the same 13-week period in the prior year, and our online sales decreased 1.4% to $52.2 million. Year-to-date net sales increased 4.3% to $937.8 million compared to net sales of $898.7 million for the prior year 39-week fiscal period. Comparable store sales for the year-to-date period increased 2.5% in comparison to the same 39-week period in the prior year, and our online sales increased 1.1% to $144.5 million.

For the quarter, UPTs decreased approximately 0.5%, the average unit retail increased approximately 3% and the average transaction value increased about 2.5%. Year-to-date, UPTs decreased approximately 0.5%, the average unit retail increased approximately 4% and the average transaction value increased approximately 3%. Gross margin for the quarter was 47.8%, down 20 basis points from 48% in the third quarter of 2025. The decline was driven by a 60 basis point increase in buying, distribution and occupancy costs, primarily related to continued growth in the number of both new and remodeled store locations, and this increase was partially offset by a 40 basis point improvement in merchandise margin.

Year-to-date gross margin was 47.3%, down 10 basis points from 47.4% for the same period last year. And again, the year-to-date decline was the result of a 60 basis point increase in buying distribution and occupancy expenses, partially offset by a 50 basis point increase in merchandise margin. Selling, general and administrative expenses for the quarter were 29.8% of sales compared to 29% for the third quarter of 2025. And for the year-to-date period, SG&A was 28.7% of net sales compared to 29.5% for the same period in the prior year.

The third quarter increase was due to a 40 basis point impact from compensation expense related to realized gains on our deferred compensation plan investments which was equally offset by investment income recognized in other income and also to a 35 basis point increase related to continued marketing investments, a 25 basis point increase in store labor related expenses and a 10 basis point increase in certain other SG&A expenses. And these increases were partially offset by a 30 basis point reduction in incentive and equity compensation accruals. Our operating margin for the quarter was 18% compared to 19% for the third quarter of fiscal 2025.

And for the year-to-date period, our operating margin was 18.6% compared to 17.9% for the same period last year. Income tax expense as a percentage of pretax net income for each of the current and prior year quarter and year-to-date periods was 24.5%. Our press release also included a balance sheet as of October 31, 2026, which included the following: inventory of $180.5 million, which was up 8.9% from the same time a year ago, and $332.2 million of total cash and investments. We ended the quarter with $195 million in fixed assets, net of accumulated depreciation. Our capital expenditures for the quarter were $10.8 million, and depreciation expense was $7.2 million.

For the year-to-date period, capital expenditures were $55.3 million and depreciation expense was $20.6 million. Year-to-date capital spending is broken down as follows: $34.1 million for new store construction, store remodels and technology upgrades and $21.2 million for capital spending at the corporate headquarters and distribution center. During the quarter, we opened 4 new stores and completed 4 full store remodels, one of which was a relocation to a new shopping center. And following quarter end, we opened 2 additional new stores which brings our year-to-date counts through today to 14 new stores, 14 full remodels and 2 store closures.

For the remainder of the year, we now anticipate completing 2 additional full remodeling projects, both of which will be completed in January. Buckle ended the quarter with 450 retail stores in 42 states compared to 442 stores in 42 states at the end of the third quarter of 2025. And now I'll turn it over to Adam Akerson, Vice President of Finance.

Adam Akerson: Thanks, Tom, and good morning. Our women's business continued its growth during the quarter, increasing 4% on top of a 19% increase in the third quarter of 2025. The women's business represented 52% of total sales for the quarter up from 51% last year. The biggest win in denim was the continued shift into new silhouettes. Wide-leg is now our largest women's denim fit, while barrel and low-rise styles also grew quickly. Total women's denim sales were down about 0.5% against a 17.5% increase last year, with average denim price points increasing from $86.95 to $92.15 during the quarter.

That same shift in silhouettes also drove the fashion pants category, our fastest-growing segment of the women's business, which was up nearly 60% year-over-year. This expanding category gives us a second path to earn our guest business as she looks to fill in her wardrobe beyond traditional denim. Combined with denim, total full-length women's bottoms for the quarter were up 3.5%. Women's tops grew approximately 4.5%, led by fashions, prints and graphics, along with strong growth in bralettes and sleeveless styles. In tops, our private brands did much of the work, growing 16% and highlighted by performance in BKE, Buckle Black, Willow & Root, and Gilded Intent.

Our men's business increased approximately 0.5% for the quarter, representing 48% of total company sales compared to 49% last year. Men's denim sales declined approximately 5% year-over-year. We are pleased with the response to relaxed fits, which grew double digits as demand shifted away from slimmer fits. Average denim price points increased from $88.15 to $88.40 for the quarter. Similar to our women's business, men's also complemented our denim selection with strong performance in denim alternatives in both performance and cotton fabrics. We also saw unseasonably strong performance in our shorts selection throughout the quarter. Men's tops continued its strong growth trend within the men's business, increasing approximately 6% year-over-year.

Our expanded polo assortment, short-sleeve, button-fronts, quarter-zips, hoodies and fashion solids each showed strong growth for the quarter. Graphic tees, which is our largest men's tops category also continued to grow. On a combined basis, accessory sales for the quarter increased approximately 1% against the prior year, while footwear sales decreased about 3%. These 2 categories accounted for approximately 10% and 4%, respectively, of third quarter net sales. This compares to 10% and 4.5%, respectively, for the third quarter of fiscal 2025. For the quarter, average accessory price points were up approximately 4.5%, and average footwear price points were up 8.5%.

Our kids business delivered another outstanding quarter, increasing 14% on top of a 22% increase a year ago as it remains one of our consistent growth engines. Growth was led by denim, casual and knit bottoms, tees and hoodies. The same silhouette and wash trends driving our adult business resonated with kids and parents alike. For the quarter, denim accounted for approximately 43.5% of sales, and tops accounted for approximately 30%, which compares to 46% and 29% for each in the third quarter of fiscal 2025. Our private label business for the quarter represented 48% of sales versus 47.5% in the third quarter of 2025. And with that, we welcome your questions.
