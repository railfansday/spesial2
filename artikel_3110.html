Gladstone Capital (GLAD -2.86%) reported fiscal year 2026 results highlighted by an 11.7% increase in total investment income and a strategic focus on the lower middle market precision manufacturing sector. Management reported that net investment income remained flat year over year at $2.00 per share as growth in average earning assets was offset by increased financing costs and higher management fees. The company reported a decline in net asset value to $20.77 per share, primarily driven by unrealized depreciation in nonaccrual assets and specific equity positions. Strategic initiatives for the coming fiscal year include returning nonearning investments to earning status and managing leverage within a target range of 1.1 times net asset value.
