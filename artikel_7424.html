Settlement means LA keeps control of federal homelessness funding

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Trump administration wanted to control how millions of dollars in federal money for homelessness was spent. Instead, LA County will now control it.

The U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development claimed victory, trumpeting the fact that according to the settlement, the beleaguered Los Angeles Homeless Services Authority, or LAHSA, is being permanently cut off from federal funding starting in 2028.
