‘Mission accomplished’ – Hallgrimsson revels in Ireland’s second-place finish

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Republic of Ireland manager Heimir Hallgrimsson declared it was "mission accomplished" for his team after they secured second spot in Nations League Group B3 with a 3-0 win over 10-man Kosovo.

Ireland went into the game knowing they needed to win to leapfrog Kosovo in the table, and they were given an early boost when Kreshnik Hajrizi was shown a straight red card 26 minutes in for a handball on the line.

It was Troy Parrott's header that he kept out, and the striker stepped up to convert the resulting penalty.

Ireland continued to dominate but had to wait until the 50th minute for their second goal when Parrott was felled in the box by Arijanet Muric, and the striker again confidently swept in from the spot.

Kosovo, who had made a bright start before the red card, offered little in terms of a fightback, and Jack Moylan made sure of the win in the 75th minute when he glanced his header past Muric.

And Hallgrimsson was thrilled his side managed to accomplish their initial goal.

"Mission accomplished, task completed," he told RTE.

"It's nice to say that, as it doesn't happen often for these guys, but now they need to get used to that.

"They [Kosovo] started throwing themselves down, shouting, screaming, but we talked about that before the game, to stay focused and switched on – don't get over ambitious. We didn't score in the beginning but kept on going.

"Even in the second half, sometimes when you're 1-0 up, you start to cheat and don't go when you need to go, but we went man for man with a high press. I think that was a good decision, and the players executed it perfectly."

Ireland could have had more goals, having 28 shots overall, with 12 of those hitting the target, as they accumulated 4.6 expected goals (xG).

They also had seven Opta-defined 'big' chances, and 40 touches in the opposition box, while facing just five Kosovo attempts at the other end worth 0.2 xG.

The victory means Ireland will enter the League A/B play-offs in March as they aim to try and gain promotion to the top tier of the competition.

"The goal for this Nations League was to achieve that second place and pot two in the draw," Hallgrimsson added.

"To have a play-off game against a good nation is a bonus, and hopefully we will be in a group of four teams for the Euro [qualifiers], and this makes it more likely, not a group of five."
