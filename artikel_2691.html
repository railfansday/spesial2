Browns make seven roster moves after Steelers loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Cleveland Browns have changed their roster up a bit after losing to the Pittsburgh Steelers 27-6 in the rubber match on the road.

Their practice squad saw activity on Tuesday.

The Browns waived/released WR Bub Means, P Wes Pahl, OG Vershon Lee, and OT Andrew Coker from their practice squad. In corresponding moves, they signed WR Xavier Guillory, OG Austin Blaske, and OT Xavier Truss to the practice squad. This leaves one roster spot on the practice squad. Estime will not be filling that spot, as he was claimed by Cleveland's Week 9 opponent, the New Orleans Saints.

Guillory (6'-2", 210 pounds) went undrafted in 2025 out of Arizona State, then signed with the Baltimore Ravens and was placed on their practice squad last year and this season until he was waived on October 13. He runs a 4.58 and has never been on a main roster.

Blaske (6'-5", 310 pounds) played for North Carolina and went undrafted this year. He then signed with the Los Angeles Rams and was a last-day cut, then signed to their practice squad.

Truss (6'-7", 320 pounds) played for Georgia, where he was part of two National Championship teams and went undrafted in 2025. He spent time with the Denver Broncos, New Orleans Saints, the Browns, and the Philadelphia Eagles. He has spent time on practice squads and played in seven NFL games while with the Saints. The Eagles waived him on October 27. Truss played for Cleveland head coach Todd Monken while with Georgia.
