Boise State loses stunner vs SDSU after head-scratching final sequence helps Aztecs kick walk-off FG

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Group of Six College Football Playoff race has been shaken up.

Leader Boise State was stunned by San Diego State 22-20, losing in improbable fashion that could drastically hurt its shot at returning to the playoff. Having already clinched a spot in the Pac-12 Championship game and the only ranked Group of Six team in the CFP rankings, the Broncos had plenty of wiggle room to absorb a loss.

But now that it's happened, it's made things a lot more uncomfortable in Boise.

The Broncos were in a battle with a resurgent San Diego State team, but looked to be heading toward a close win when it stopped the Aztecs' go-ahead 2-point conversion attempt with just under two minutes left.

All Boise State had to was get ball and run out the clock.

The play call was questionable, and on a third down, Maddux Madsen threw it to Keawe Browne for a 5-yard catch at the sideline, with Browne going out of bounds to stop the clock and let San Diego State keep its final timeout. On fourth down and just past midfield, coach Spencer Danielson kept the offense on the field to end the game right there.

San Diego State got the ball at its 46-yard line, and in a flash, moved into Boise State territory, getting to the 24-yard line.

The Aztecs were able to set up Cooper DiLeva for the game-winning 42-yard kick, and he drilled it with no time left to pull off the upset.
