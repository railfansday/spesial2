Brock Purdy wins fourth career NFC offensive player of the week award

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Brock Purdy is an MVP candidate and had another strong performance in Sunday’s victory over the Vikings.

Now he’s been named NFC offensive player of the week.

Purdy finished the game 21-of-26 passing for 255 yards with three touchdowns and no interceptions — good for a 146.0 passer rating.

This was Purdy’s seventh career game with a passer rating of at least 145, which is the most by a player in his first five seasons in league history.

Purdy is one of two quarterbacks with a passer rating of at least 100 in eight games this season, the other being Cincinnati’s Joe Burrow.

Purdy has now won four career offensive player of the week awards.

The current No. 1 seed in the conference, Purdy and the 49ers will host the Seahawks on Sunday afternoon.
