Malen makes Roma history scoring 26 Serie A goals in less than a year

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Blessed was that January 14, 2026. It was just 11 months ago, in fact, that Donyell Malen first set foot in Rome. Two days later, his transfer to the Giallorossi would be made official.

Today, 311 days later, Bobby is rewriting Roma history week by week. With this afternoon's hat-trick against Parma at the Tardini, the Dutch striker has reached 26 Serie A goals in 2026.

And, as reported by OptaPaolo, Malen is only the second player in the club's history to score at least 26 goals in a single Serie A calendar year. Rodolfo Volk (25 in 1930) has already been archived, and Pedro Manfredini (26 in 1960) has now joined him. And Bobby can still improve on this fantastic tally.
