Fremantle U.S. COO Suzanne Lopez Exiting After Over 20 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

EXCLUSIVE: Suzanne Lopez, who has been with American Idol producer Fremantle for nearly 23 years, is exiting the company.

Lopez has most recently been Chief Operating Officer for Fremantle’s North American operation, a role that she was promoted to in 2019. In that position, she oversaw legal and business affairs, HR, production, technology and security.

Before that, she was EVP, Business and Legal Affairs.

Jennifer Mullin, CEO, Fremantle, said, “Suzanne has made an exceptional contribution to Fremantle North America in her 22 years with the business. She has been integral to Fremantle’s success and growth, and we are grateful for her leadership, commitment, and the significant impact she has had.” Watch on Deadline

Lopez’s departure comes after former Endemol Shine North America chief Sharon Levy was hired as CEO, Fremantle Americas and Australia, reporting to Mullin. Levy, who previously ran the company behind Fox’s MasterChef and Fear Factor: House of Fear, will oversee shows such as American Idol and America’s Got Talent in her role running the company’s production operations across the United States, Mexico, and Australia. She starts in January.

Lopez has been with Fremantle since March 2004.
