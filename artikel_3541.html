Battlestar Galactica Cerberus & Prometheus Collection Officially Announced

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

It was on 17 September 1978 that Battlestar Galactica premiered for the first time, following the theatrical release of its pilot movie earlier that year. However, over the past 48 years, the beloved sci-fi franchise has been the subject of many renewals and sequels, including 1980's Galactica 1980, and more recently, the prequel series, Caprica, which aired in 2010.

Set in a distant part of the universe where human civilization has colonized a group of planets known as the Twelve Colonies, the story follows their migration from their ancestral homeworld of Kobol, and their ongoing war with the Cylons, a cybernetic race who wishes to exterminate the human race.

With so many sequels, prequels, and even a reimagined television series, it is no wonder why the sci-fi franchise is still as popular as it is today, and those who were a fan of the original series, will be pleased to learn that they can get their hands on both the Cerberus and the Prometheus.

It was during 1978's Battlestar Galactica that viewers were told of twelve battlestars, despite only eight ever being mentioned on screen. It was during this time that three more battlestars - Solaria, Cerberus and Prometheus - were waiting in the wings, with plans to add them to the Colonial fleet over time.

Now, two out of three of these battlestars will now be released courtesy of Master Replicas: the Cerberus and Prometheus. As for the Cerberus, it is available as part of a limited-edition, with only 500 being made. As the official description reads: "One of those long-rumored additions to the fleet, she makes her debut here — a new name among the ranks of the Twelve Colonies, brought to life for the collection for the first time."

Made from die-cast and ABS, and coming in 10.5 inches in length, the Battlestar Galactica (Classic) Cerberus Limited Edition retails at £74.99, and has already begun shipping to customers as of October 2026. The same can be said for Battlestar Galactica (Classic) Prometheus Limited Edition. Coming in at the same price and length, it is also available for purchase now and will make an excellent addition to your collectible shelf.

It is clear to see that both replicas feature a staggering amount of detail, and manage to capture the battlestars in all of their canon glory. If you are not sure which one to purchase, it is safe to say that they look pretty similar, so if you are only able to get one, you need not worry about missing out on the other. Thanks to an accompanying stand, they will no doubt be a talking piece once displayed in your homes, and better yet, they will not break the bank to purchase.

So, if you are feeling nostalgic for the original Battlestar Galactica television series, and want a way to showcase your love, look no further than these stunning new releases which aim to highlight the beloved science-fiction franchise in all its glory. So, Cerberus or Prometheus?
