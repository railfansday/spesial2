On its second-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 28, 2026, Fomento Económico Mexicano (FMX -0.69%) announced that positive customer traffic helped drive 9.2% same-store sales growth at OXXO Mexico, the company's flagship convenience store chain. Protecting store-level transaction volume preserves long-term retail power across core consumer categories. Scaling digital financial tools and discount store models builds additional channels for long-term expansion.

Management adjusted OXXO Mexico's price pack architecture, which is its strategy of offering smaller sizes and lower-cost product alternatives, to win back value-seeking shoppers. Rebalancing shelf assortment to focus on customer needs drove positive traffic after eight consecutive quarters of declines.

Spin by OXXO, the company's digital financial services platform, expanded its active user base by 22% year over year. Management is now pivoting the digital platform toward monetization through a strategic lending partnership with QED Investors, a financial technology venture capital firm.

Bara, the company's discount proximity grocery store chain, is emerging as a powerful growth driver as consumers seek value in everyday groceries. Unit economics are improving alongside store expansion, supported by strong private label sales.

Operational momentum across retail formats is carrying the business through a sluggish Mexican consumer environment. Massive store density, an agile supply chain, and proprietary customer data protect long-term market leadership. Executive focus remains on pricing discipline, supply chain efficiency, and store-level execution as temporary tailwinds subside.

Disciplined capital deployment and flexible operational controls keep the long-term thesis intact across core and emerging channels.
