Sources: Rangers have rejected ‘big’ Djeidi Gassama offers as ‘only way’ for exit revealed

Rangers have no intention of cashing in on winger Djeidi Gassama in January amid interest from Monza, sources have told Football Insider.

The Italian side are the latest club to take an interest in the 23-year-old, but Derek McInnes has no doubt that he is not for sale.

Rangers are understood to have turned down “big offers” for the former Sheffield Wednesday man in previous transfer windows, and still view him as a key part of their long-term plans.

Sources say that the “only way” they would let Gassama out of the door in January is if an offer came in that was too good for them to refuse.

However, that is not expected to come from Monza, who are struggling at the foot of the Serie A table at the moment.

Rangers fully expect that Gassama will still be part of their squad in the second half of the season, and they are happy for that scenario to play out.

Gassama endured an inconsistent start to life at Ibrox last term, but still managed to notch nine goals and five assists in all competitions.

He already has three assists in the Premiership so far this term, and is currently winning the battle for regular minutes in Rangers’ wide areas.

McInnes is blessed with a wealth of options on the flanks, with Gassama, Findlay Curtis, Minsu Kim, Badredine Bouanani and Daisuke Yokota all pushing for starts.

However, it is Gassama who is emerging at the top of that group at the moment and appears to be winning the boss over with his performances.

That has reduced any chance of him being sold even further in recent weeks, with McInnes viewing him as a potential difference-maker for his team.

Football Insider revealed on Wednesday that Dinamo Bucharest winger Danny Armstrong is keen to return to Scotland and join Rangers in January.

The 29-year-old is flourishing in Romania, and already has 16 goal involvements from just 15 games in the top flight this season.

Sources say that Armstrong would find it “hard to turn down” a move to Ibrox this winter, and is keen to reunite with McInnes.

He previously played under the Gers boss at Kilmarnock, where he became a firm favourite of the manager.
