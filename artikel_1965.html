Sources: Monaco to open door for Folarin Balogun move after Man United news

Folarin Balogun moving to Manchester United could be a move that suits all parties in January, sources have told Football Insider.

The Monaco striker has emerged as a surprise target for the Red Devils, with new manager Mauricio Pochettino a “big fan” of the American international.

Balogun is also understood to be keen to reunite with the Argentine and would be keen to make the move to Old Trafford this winter.

Sources say that Monaco are even happy to sell the 25-year-old, in a bid to raise funds for their own mid-season business.

The French giants are set to open the door for Balogun to depart, with United hoping to exploit the “ongoing relationship” between him and Pochettino.

The Manchester giants are hoping to add a new striker to their group this winter, with Benjamin Sesko currently out injured.

Balogun had been set to join Everton in the latter stages of the summer window, only for him to ultimately fail a medical on Merseyside on deadline day.

Monaco were keen to cash in on either him or Lamine Camara, but ultimately ended up keeping both stars at the end of the window.

However, that plays into United’s hands ahead of January, and they are unlikely to face any issues when it comes to bringing Balogun in.

Monaco still need the money, and with the player also open to the move, the door is firmly open for United to make a deal happen.

Balogun has made a slow start to the new campaign by his standards, but still managed to net 19 goals in all competitions last term.

Five of those came in the Champions League, and he already has significant experience at the top table of European football.

Football Insider revealed last weekend that United are chasing a deal for Brentford centre-back Nathan Collins ahead of the January window.

The Irishman is understood to “tick a lot of boxes” for Pochettino’s side due to his ample Premier League experience and leadership skills.

However, sources say that Brentford are determined not to lose their club captain in the middle of the season, and are likely to try and block any approaches.

United want the 25-year-old to be part of their defensive overhaul, but they have work to do if they are to convince Brentford to do business.
