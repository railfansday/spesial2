ETFs Across the AI Ecosystem

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Ryan Issakainen, SVP & ETF Strategist at First Trust, Matt Orton, Chief Market Strategist & Portfolio Manager at Raymond James Investment Management, and Hepsen Uzcan, CEO of Deutsche Asset Management Americas, join Bloomberg Intelligence's Emily Pierce at the Bloomberg ETFs in Depth conference in New York on November 19, 2026. The panel examines whether recent volatility has changed the long-term investment case for AI and where opportunities are emerging beyond the biggest technology names. They also discuss concentration risk, the infrastructure buildout behind AI and how investors are using ETFs to gain more diversified exposure to the theme. (Source: Bloomberg)
