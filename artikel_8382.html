Congratulations to These 2026 Appalachian Trail Finishers (Part 8)

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Every year, thousands of hopeful thru-hikers set off on a journey of nearly 2,200 miles. The Appalachian Trail stretches from Springer Mountain in Georgia to Mount Katahdin in Maine.  Hikers encounter unforgettable scenery ranging from sprawling mountain vistas to dense forests. The trail is as challenging as it is beautiful,  pushing hikers to their limits both physically and mentally.

Of all the ambitious hikers who embark on this trek, less than half will successfully complete a thru-hike. Finishing the AT is a life-changing accomplishment that requires physical determination, mental toughness, and a little bit of luck. Hikers from all walks of life are impacted by their time spent on the trail, and we are thrilled to spotlight the incredible hikers who completed the AT in 2026!

Have you just finished your AT thru-hike? If so, please celebrate your finish with us by submitting a photo through the form below so we can feature you in a subsequent post. We can’t wait to share your incredible achievement!

“I fell in August coming down Mount Madison, and thought my hike was over. Tried to hike out of Gorham and made it to the last shelter in NH before bailing. 32 zero days later, I was able to hike again and salvaged the hike via a flip-flop!” -Catapult

“Great times – Great people – Great memories” -WARHammer

Congratulations to all the 2026 AT finishers — your achievements are remarkable and worth celebrating!

Anna is from western North Carolina and grew up romping through hollers and hills. Her love of the Appalachian mountains drew her onto the Appalachian Trail in 2021 where she became "Magic". The AT was her first thru hike and the place where she would meet her future husband, Truffles. After completing the AT, the pair moved to east Tennessee to work as backpacking guides in Great Smoky Mountains National Park. Between guiding gigs, Magic hiked the Arizona Trail in 2023, the West Highland Way in 2024, and the Pacific Crest Trail in 2025. When she isn't in the woods, Magic enjoys writing, playing disc golf, and teaching yoga. You can follow along with all of Magic's adventures on her Instagram (@annamckinneysmith) or by checking out her blog, magicinthemountains.blog.
