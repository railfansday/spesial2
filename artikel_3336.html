American Society Of Cinematographers Focuses In On 2027 Career Honorees

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The American Society of Cinematographers has its 2027 career award recipients.

Newton Thomas Sigel will receive the organization’s Lifetime Achievement Award, Robert McLachlan will be honored with the Career Achievement in Television Award, and Dejan Georgevich is set with the President’s Award.

They will be feted during the 41st annual ASC Awards on March 7 at The Beverly Hilton, along with Julianne Grosso who will receive an ASC Award of Distinction, and the Tiffen Company, which will be recognized with the Curtis Clark Technology Award, spotlighting its creative excellence and cultural influence. Read more about all the honorees below.

Sigel has established himself as one of the industry’s most inventive visual stylists. He photographed Bohemian Rhapsody, which earned him a BAFTA nomination and became one of the highest-grossing music biopics in history. His work on The Usual Suspects for director Bryan Singer marked the first of 10 films they made together, including Superman Returns, Valkyrie and four X-Men titles. Sigel’s use of unconventional film stocks and innovative processing techniques on David O. Russell’s Three Kings opened new creative pathways in modern cinematography. “Tom Sigel has consistently elevated the art of cinematography through his bold visual storytelling and deep commitment to collaboration,” said ASC President Shelly Johnson. “His contributions span an extraordinary range of films and genres, each marked by a clarity of vision and a profound respect for the craft. This Lifetime Achievement Award celebrates not only his remarkable accomplishments but the enduring legacy he has built within the cinematography community.”

Robert McLachlanHe has built a wide-ranging career across genres and formats, earning numerous awards and hundreds of credits. He has received three Emmy nominations for his work on Ray Donovan and multiple seasons of Game of Thrones. He has earned five ASC Award nominations for Game of Thrones, High Noon and three for his work on Millennium. McLachlan has collected 16 Canadian Society of Cinematographers nominations, with wins for the theatrical features Impolite and Willard. His diverse television credits include series such as The Commish, Strange Luck, Harper’s Island, Human Target, The Secret Circle, Westworld, The Affair, Lovecraft Country, American Gigolo and, most recently, Taylor Sheridan’s 1923, Landman and Lioness.

Dejan GeorgevichWith a career spanning feature films, documentaries, commercials and major television productions, including The Blacklist, Gossip Girl, 3lbs., Cupid, Mercy and The Education of Max Bickford, he has established himself as a versatile and respected visual storyteller. He was recently appointed chair of the School of Visual Arts BFA Film program following a 30-year tenure as a highly regarded professor, where he taught master classes in cinematography and mentored generations of emerging filmmakers. Georgevich served as national vice president of the International Cinematographers Guild, Local 600 IATSE. In recognition of his exemplary commitment to education, the Guild honored him with the 2012 Bud Stone Award for Outstanding Educational Contributions to the art and craft of cinematography.

Julianne Grosso Grosso will receive the ASC Award of Distinction for her pivotal role in co-founding Cine Gear Expo with Karl Kresser and shaping it into a cornerstone event for the cinematography community. Her leadership helped create a space where filmmakers and innovators could connect, collaborate and advance the craft. Cine Gear’s influence continues to elevate the tools, techniques and creative dialogue that define modern cinematography.

The Tiffen CompanyTiffen will receive the Curtis Clark Technology Award in recognition of its nearly nine decades of innovation and its enduring impact on cinematography. From the Golden Age of Hollywood to today’s digital production environments, Tiffen’s technologies have helped shape the look and movement of countless films and television productions, providing filmmakers with the tools to bring their creative visions to life. Renowned for its pioneering optical filtration technology, Tiffen’s contributions extend beyond the lens to include more than 25 years of advancing Steadicam camera stabilization systems and the continued evolution of Lowel lighting solutions since 2009. Together, these innovations have empowered generations of cinematographers to shape, capture and illuminate their images with greater creative freedom. The company has been recognized with numerous honors from the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences, as well as multiple Emmy Awards for its contributions.
