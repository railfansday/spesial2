Hilary Duff & More Take the Stage at Billboard Live Music Summit 2026 | Billboard News

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The 2026 Billboard Live Music Summit took place on Tuesday, Nov. 10, and Hilary Duff took center stage along with Weezer to recount their careers and touring. Keep watching to see everything you missed at the summit!

Hilary Duff: When you dream, and you have the right people around you with a really good plan, and you work your a– off, things like this can happen.

Tetris Kelly: The millennial pop star who has refined the word “comeback” shared what raced through her mind, returning to the stage in an exclusive conversation at this year’s Billboard’s Live Music Summit and Awards. Here’s a recap of the event presented by AEG Presents.

Hilary Duff: My biggest fear was being present and not feeling like I was in my body to give a show that represented who I am now and reached people the way that I have before. That was my biggest fear. I wasn’t like, “Oh, if I miss this dance move. Oh, if I f–k up this song,” or you know, I was more like, I want to give it my everything and like lay it all out and give people what they came for and what, maybe, you know, they missed for 20 years.

Tetris Kelly: Hilary Duff was honored with Outstanding Pop Tour of the Year for her Lucky Me Tour, the biggest in her 25-year career.

Tetris Kelly: The annual event unites the ultimate power players, creatives and influential voices in the live music industry. Weezer received the Legends of Live Award for their three-plus decades of touring, rocking stages around the world. Earlier in the event, the band — currently on The Gathering world tour — reflected on their career touring and why they still love what they do.
