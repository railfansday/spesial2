Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Re: Break Even Point on Receiving Amount in Social Security Benefits that one Paid-in Through Taxes

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

For someone who anticipates earning the maximum earned income amount each year that is taxable by Social Security for their 35 highest earning years, what is the general break even point after collecting benefits at age 67 at which one will receive back in benefits what they paid-in in taxes for those 35 highest earning years?
Max monthly payment assuming you turn 67 in 2027 would be $4188. Assuming you contributed the max from 1992-2026, that would be a total of ~$225k not accounting for any assumed growth during that time. This also excludes employer contributions.

The payback would be about 4.5 years so you’d be 71.5.

Exercise to the reader: redo the analysis assuming a per-year return on the social security withholding “investment” from 1992-2026, factor in inflation, etc.
