MercadoLibre vs. Shopify: Comparing Recent Revenue Scale and Growth Trajectories

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

MercadoLibre's quarterly sales have outpaced Shopify's in every period shown, but Shopify's year-end surges raise a question about whether the gap keeps widening.

MercadoLibre (MELI -1.75%) primarily earns its revenue by operating a prominent digital commerce marketplace alongside a complementary financial technology platform that facilitates secure digital transactions and credit services for users across the broader Latin American region.

While managing a planned leadership transition for its global chief executive officer position, it successfully completed a $1 billion issuance of senior unsecured notes to institutional investors and expanded its marketplace delivery and pharmacy capabilities across Brazil.

Shopify (SHOP -3.89%) generates its revenue by providing an extensive digital commerce technology platform that equips independent global merchants with the necessary software tools to build digital storefronts, manage inventory, and fulfill their merchandise sales.

While navigating a California federal judge's recent refusal to dismiss a class action lawsuit regarding consumer data collection, it rolled out an extensive software update featuring new artificial intelligence integrations designed specifically for various merchant store checkouts.

Revenue here refers to the data provider's standardized income-statement revenue line item, and it functions as a primary financial measure that allows investors to track the overall monetary volume of goods and services a business successfully sells to its customer base.

The first thing to check for when examining a company's revenue trend is seasonality: some times of the year have stronger buying patterns than others. There usually is some seasonality for every business.

We see this seasonality in both MercadoLibre and Shopify stocks, with the fourth calendar quarter of each year showing an uptick, followed by a drop-off into the first quarter. In these cases, comparing a quarter to the prior year's comparable quarter provides a better sense of the longer-term trend and helps adjust for seasonality.

But even with seasonality, we see two companies with strong sales trends looking right to left. The better results come from MercadoLibre, which shows its sales growth is so strong it almost totally offsets its seasonality. The company's push into financial services, moving beyond its traditional business of online retail business, is clearly paying dividends.

Interestingly, Shopify's revenue is growing faster quarter by quarter than the overall retail sector it serves. That implies it's grabbing market share.

The revenue trend is the most obvious metric for investors to examine when considering a stock. The chart shows two thriving businesses, though one is clearly accelerating faster in its sales generation.
