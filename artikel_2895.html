Eagles-Cowboys final score: Joe Milton leads comeback victory over Dallas!

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

HAPPY THANKSGIVING, PHILADELPHIA EAGLES FANS!

The Eagles are 4-7 after beating the Dallas Cowboys on Thursday. Final score: 39 to 38.

Man, I can't believe the Eagles won that game! The Birds were looking left for dead after going down 31 to 10 with about three minutes remaining in the third quarter. Their defense was getting carved up by the Cowboys' high-powered offense.

But Joe Milton never said die! The Eagles' quarterback led his new team to a winning effort against his former team, ultimately finishing 21/31 for 331 yards, three touchdowns, one interception, and a 121.8 passer rating. A lot of that production came through DeVonta Smith, who had 11 catches for 200 yards (!) and two touchdowns.

Ty Robinson, who blocked a punt to create an Eagles touchdown earlier in the game, came through with a much-needed fourth down sack that ultimately set up a game-winning Jake Elliott field goal!

And suddenly the Eagles' season is not totally dead?! Could they actually win the NFC East?! Probably not … but they weren't supposed to win this game, either!

Next up for the Eagles: a road game against the Arizona Cardinals, which the Eagles will probably be favored in!

Read on for a recap and stay tuned for BGN's postgame coverage.

The Eagles won the coin toss and elected to defer, per usual. The Cowboys took over at their own 23-yard line and Quinyon Mitchell was flagged for pass interference on the first snap from scrimmage. Tacky call on Q going up against George Pickens, who was also pretty handsy on that play. Facing 3rd-and-3, a quick dump off to Javonte Williams resulted in a first down. Cooper DeJean was hurt after tackling Williams. The Cowboys picked up another first down into field goal range with Andrew Mukuba missing a tackle in the hole. Facing 3rd-and-8, Dak Prescott's first down scramble was called back by Jonathan Greenard drawing a holding penalty on Terence Steele. Facing 3rd-and-18, Prescott hit CeeDee Lamb wiiiiide open in the middle of the field for an easy first down at the seven-yard line. Great resistance there. Not! Jihaad Campbell seemed to get lost in coverage. The Cowboys then went with three straight runs to reach the end zone. 11 plays, 77 yards, 6:10 off the clock. EAGLES 0, COWBOYS 7.

The Eagles started at their own 6-yard line after Britain Covey mishandled Brandon Aubrey's bouncing kickoff. The Eagles picked up their first first down with a Joe Milton completion to Dallas Goedert. Facing 3rd-and-7 after a blitz forced a Milton incompletion, Milton ripped a nice ball to DeVonta Smith for another first down. A Cowboys player signaled incomplete, though, and Brian Schottenheimer threw his red flag to challenge the play. After commercial, it was announced that the Cowboys did not challenge in time before the Eagles got a play off. I don't know if the review would have been conclusive enough to overturn the ruling on the field anyway. Milton hit Elijah Moore (!) in rhythm for a 25-yard completion to put the offense past midfield. Milton balling early! Facing 3rd-and-6 after two Saquon Barkley runs, Milton hit DeVonta just shy of the sticks. Facing 4th-and-short, the Eagles seemed like they might go Brotherly Shove but they instead tried a fullback dive with Barkley and it was stopped for a loss. The decision to go for it was correct. Overthought it on the play call. Just use the tush push. Disappointing end to an otherwise encouraging 11-play, 57-yard drive.

The Cowboys took over at their own 37-yard line. Prescott's deep shot to Pickens with Riq Woolen trailing bounced off of the receiver for an incompletion. Fortunate break for the Eagles' defense. Facing 3rd-and-6, Prescott took off running for a first down but it was wiped out by another holding penalty on Javonte Williams grabbing Marcus Epps. Facing 3rd-and-16, Prescott hit Pickens for a contested grab against Woolen. Another favorable opportunity for the defense to get off the field and they just can't do it. One play later, Prescott hit KaVontae Turpin for a 31-yard catch-and-run touchdown. Too easy. Meanwhile, Nick Sirianni was shown arguing with Will Shipley on the sideline. OK, then. EAGLES 0, COWBOYS 14.

Facing 3rd-and-5, Milton ran out of bounds for no gain. Three-and-out. Honestly, should've gone for it there, even though it was on the Eagles' own 25-yard line.

Turpin bobbled the ball, though, and Sirianni threw his red flag to challenge the play. After review, the call was overturned and the Cowboys had to punt. Hey, a stop! If only barely.

Facing 3rd-and-12 after Markel Bell had a real rough rep to allow a Rashan Gary sack, Milton rolled right and hit a wide open Lemon for a first down! Milton got the juice?! Facing 3rd-and-6, Milton was blitzed and threw behind DeVonta for an incompletion. Jake Elliott came in for the 48-yard field goal make. I would've gone for it there, you're not beating the Cowboys by kicking FGs. But not a bad nine-play, 52-yard drive. EAGLES 3, COWBOYS 14.

The Cowboys took over at their own 40-yard line after their special teams made a smart play to draw an Eagles penalty. They picked up a first down with two runs, with Mukuba getting blocked out of the play on one. The Cowboys quickly moved into field goal range. And then into the red zone. Williams then went 12 yards easily up the gut for a touchdown. Zero resistance. EAGLES 3, COWBOYS 21.
