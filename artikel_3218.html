LGI Homes (LGIH) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Net orders fell sharply, but margins held up despite affordability pressure.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

LGI Homes, Inc. (LGIH -8.24%) reported growth in total revenue and home closings for the third quarter, even as the housing market faced significant headwinds from elevated interest rates and stretched affordability. Management reported that the company achieved its results by expanding its community count to 151 and leveraging its in-house land development capabilities to maintain margins. While net orders declined 61.2% year over year, the company strengthened its balance sheet by reducing its net debt to capital ratio and extending its revolving credit facility. Management narrowed its full-year closings guidance to between 4,600 and 4,800 homes, focusing on balancing sales pace with profitability in a high-rate environment.
