LG Display joins VALORANT Esports EMEA as Official Partner

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

SEOUL, South Korea, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- LG Display, the world's leading innovator of display technologies, announced today it is joining VALORANT Esports EMEA as an Official Partner for the 2027 season.

VCT EMEA and Game Changers EMEA represent the pinnacle of VALORANT esports across Europe, the Middle East, and Africa, uniting a vast territory where elite tactical masterminds and decorated champions clash on the stage. Players competing across VCT EMEA and Game Changers EMEA will use LG Display's newly launched 24.5-inch FHD 720Hz Tandem WOLED panel, which is the fastest Tandem WOLED panel tailored specifically for esports. The panel is featured in ASUS's ROG Swift OLED PG259QWS Ace gaming monitor.

By using this 24.5-inch FHD panel, professional gamers get to benefit from the world's highest refresh rate (720Hz) among gaming OLED displays, as well as the fastest response time (0.02ms). As a result, it supports the ultimate gameplay experience in fast-paced, first-person shooter titles such as VALORANT.

For professional VALORANT players, every fraction of a second matters. From identifying an opponent in an instant to reacting to plays quickly, players rely on display performance to succeed when pressure is at its highest.

By harnessing self-emissive pixels, LG Display's Tandem WOLED technology also offers highly vivid picture quality, maximizing player immersion.

"We are excited to partner with LG Display for the upcoming season and have their powerful technology used on stage at the Berlin arena, and when we take the competition on the road to Warsaw and Istanbul," said Charlie Allen, EMEA Director, Commercial Partnerships & Business Development of Riot Games. "VALORANT players need certain qualities from their monitors to perform at their best: fast response times, high-quality display performance and, of course, clear visibility when competing under the bright lights of an arena. This partnership will ensure the players can always compete at their fullest potential," he added.

"We are delighted with this opportunity to showcase the advantages of our panel, which is the world's fastest Tandem WOLED display tailored specifically for esports. By providing exceptional clarity and smoothness, it can clearly capture even fast-paced moments such as the flash of a gun muzzle, making it a highly immersive window into the world of gaming," said Hyeon-woo Lee, Head of the Medium and Large Display Business Unit at LG Display.

LG Display Co., Ltd. [NYSE: LPL, KRX: 034220] is the world's leading innovator of display technologies, including thin-film transistor liquid crystal and OLED displays. The company manufactures display panels in a broad range of sizes and specifications primarily for use in TVs, notebook computers, desktop monitors, automobiles, and various other applications, including tablets and mobile devices. LG Display currently operates manufacturing facilities in Korea and China, and back-end assembly facilities in Korea, China, and Vietnam. The company has approximately 53,000 employees operating worldwide. For more news and information about LG Display, please visit www.lgdisplay.com.
