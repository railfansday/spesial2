Mortgage firms add sales leadership and expand lending teams

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Mortgage and housing finance companies, including NEXA Lending and New American Funding, have announced several personnel moves, including new sales leadership, mortgage team additions and an industry appointment.  AI Summary

NEXA Lending has appointed Susie Cheatham as national sales director. Cheatham previously worked at Bay Equity, where she managed a Kansas City branch and later oversaw regional operations. She will focus on recruiting, business development and coaching loan officers at NEXA.

New American Funding has added The Brandon Drew Group, a Southern California-based mortgage team founded by Robert and Colleen Olivas. The family-run team, which includes six of the couple’s 10 children, two daughters-in-law and Robert Olivas’s mother, specializes in serving first-time homebuyers, veterans, active-duty military members and refinance borrowers.

Better Home & Finance has added Josh Burruss and The Home Team as a producing branch of NEO Home Loans, bringing $82.5 million in 2025 funded volume to its platform.

Burruss, who has 24 years of mortgage industry experience, will continue originating loans while serving as NEO’s head of retail innovation, focusing on coaching, training and product development.

Angel Romero, the housing finance agency relationship manager for Down Payment Resource, has been named vice chair of the Technology Impact and Enablement Community of Practice at the Mortgage Industry Standards Maintenance Organization (MISMO).

Romero, who joined Down Payment Resource in 2023, will help guide discussions on mortgage technology standards and represent the interests of down payment assistance programs, and the housing finance agencies and lenders that offer them.

Carter Bank has added mortgage loan officers Carl Garnett and Tom Ulbrich to its mortgage lending team, expanding its coverage in Virginia. Garnett, who has nearly 30 years of mortgage industry experience, will primarily serve Charlottesville and Lynchburg, while Ulbrich, who has more than 30 years of experience, will serve Bedford and Smith Mountain Lake.

West Capital Lending has hired former Better executive Scott Davis to lead its new direct-to-consumer retail lending division, bringing about 50 loan officers with him. The company is opening a sales office in Irving, Texas, to serve the Dallas-Fort Worth market, adding to its existing corporate locations in Irvine, California, and Southfield, Michigan.
