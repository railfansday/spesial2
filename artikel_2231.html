Confirmed Frauen-Bundesliga Lineups: VfL Wolfsburg vs. FC Bayern

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

With only three points separating them in the standings, Frauen-Bundesliga leaders FC Bayern face VfL Wolfsburg away from home on Saturday night. The visitors are yet to drop a point in the league as last week's win against Werder Bremen extended their unbeaten record to 50 games.

Both sides won their respective European games in midweek, with Bayern awaiting another one on Thursday against Austria Vienna in the UEFA Women's Champions League.

Here are the lineups for this evening's La Clasica clash at Volkswagen Arena.

Wolfsburg W: Johannes; Bjelde, Carleer, Küver, Linder; Minge, Vallotto, Peddemors, Huth ©; Zicai, Prašnikar

Bayern W: Mahmutovic; Gwinn, Eriksson ©, Ballisager, Naschenweng; Amani, Tanikawa; Dallman, Harder, Bühl; Demnjanović
