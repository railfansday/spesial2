While the Black Fridays of yore involved a sea of people rushing into a big-box store to claw each other’s eyes out over a new TV or gaming console, the biggest shopping holiday of the year looks a bit different these days. Now, the madness is largely confined to the Internet, which means you can get all of your shopping done from the comfort of your couch.

Case in point: Amazon’s Black Friday sale historically offers the year’s best Apple prices, including impressive deals on AirPods. Right now, the AirPods Pro 3 are a record-low $149. It’s rare to see 40 percent discounts on Apple gadgets—these already sold out once at this price, and we wouldn’t be surprised if it happened again. It’s just like those Black Friday mad dashes, except from the comfort of your own couch.

In other news, at $130 off, the AirPods Max 2 are hovering close to their lowest price ever. They’re a great buy if you prefer over-ear headphones. And if you’re looking for the cheapest possible pair, the brand-new AirPods 5 are still at full price, but considering that’s just $129, it’s still a solid deal—especially because they’re Apple’s first standard AirPods to include noise cancellation. That said, we wouldn’t be mad if they went on sale for the first time. We’ll keep you posted if their price drops come Cyber Monday. Amazon, your move.

AirPods Pro 3 are Apple’s most advanced earbuds yet. We called them “pretty damn cool” back when they first dropped in September 2025, and more than a year later, we stand by it.

Not only do they come armed with the noise cancellation Apple is famous for, but they also boast both adaptive and transparency audio features that adjust to the environment around you. Krista Jones, senior commerce director, is partial to the adaptive mode, which she says is “slightly less noise cancelling and always tunes into my surroundings to turn things up or down accordingly.”

Jones said that the Live Translation feature has become particularly useful in her bilingual home. Heart Rate Sensing is also of note—it keeps you plugged into your heart rate during workouts without having to wear a fitness tracker.

While a fourth generation of AirPods Pro were rumored to drop in fall 2026, there’s been no sign of them in Apple’s most recent slate of releases, so if you’re looking for the most sophisticated AirPods on the market to date, these are it. Right now, they’re just $149, besting their previous record-low price of $169. In other words, if you need new AirPods, you’d be a fool not to take advantage.

Should you prefer over-ear headphones, the AirPods Max 2 are $130 off as part of Amazon’s Black Friday sale. Back when the first generation of these dropped in 2022, editor-at-large Dave Holmes called them “Apple’s best product in years.” Four years later, Apple’s second stab has only improved the superb noise cancellation and sound quality we already loved, and added in a slate of beloved features from the AirPods Pro (think Live Translation, Conversation Awareness, and Personalized Volume), without sacrificing the sleek build.

Since the second generation was released in March 2026, the lowest we’ve seen them go is $398. That said, a $130 price cut is nothing to scoff at, especially in an age where our favorite tech has fallen prey to price increases. Note that these tend to sell out pretty quickly when they’re more than $100 off.
