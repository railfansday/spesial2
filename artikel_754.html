Ryan Adams Engaged to 22-Year-Old Kate Farr 6 Months After Meeting: ‘She’s My Best Friend’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

"I've never been happier in my life," the 52-year-old musician said.

Ryan Adams is engaged to Kate Farr, a 22-year-old writer who is 30 years younger than the singer-songwriter.

TMZ reported the news on Tuesday (Nov. 17), sharing a photo of what appears to be Adams’ and Farr’s overlapping hands showing off her new engagement ring. The couple told the outlet that they’d known each other for just six months when Adams proposed in October.

“I’ve never been happier in my life,” Adams shared with TMZ. “Kate is the most fascinating, kindest nerd I’ve ever met. She’s my best friend, and I’ve never been happier. Here’s to love.”

A few weeks prior to the news, Farr seemed to quietly confirm that they were planning to tie the knot by sharing selfies of herself and Adams on Substack and writing, “a pretty accurate representation of our relationship (happy late bday to my dork fiancé <3).”

Both Adams and Farr have shared snaps with each other on Instagram over the past few months, including pictures of their vampy Halloween costumes on the musician’s account. On Nov. 1, Adams — who’s notched 21 entries on the Billboard 200 — left a comment on an older collection of photos posted on Farr’s account in July, reading, “The week we fell in love.”

Adams was previously married for six years to Mandy Moore, who accused him of controlling and psychologically abusive behavior — which he denied through his lawyer — in a 2019 New York Times exposé, four years after they separated in 2015. Several other women also shared similar allegations against him, including Phoebe Bridgers, who said that Adams had started pursuing her after first promising mentorship and career opportunities. During their brief romantic relationship, Bridgers claimed he was controlling and emotionally abusive.

She also claimed that Adams had brought up getting married just one week into their relationship. Adams denied Bridgers’ accounts, characterizing it instead as “a brief, consensual fling.”

Additionally, the NYT piece spoke to a young woman who provided evidence of communication between herself and Adams from when she was 15 and 16, which included sexually explicit messages from the singer. That same girl alleged that Adams would expose himself to her on Skype while having “phone sex.”

The rocker’s lawyer told the newspaper at the time, “Mr. Adams unequivocally denies that he ever engaged in inappropriate online sexual communications with someone he knew was underage.”

When the article came out, Adams tweeted, “To anyone I have ever hurt, however unintentionally, I apologize deeply and unreservedly … But the picture that this article paints is upsettingly inaccurate. Some of its details are misrepresented; some are exaggerated; some are outright false. I would never have inappropriate interactions with someone I thought was underage. Period.”

The following year, he shared a letter of apology. “There are no words to express how bad I feel about the ways I’ve mistreated people through my life and career,” Adams wrote in July 2020, noting he was prioritizing sobriety in an effort to become a better person. “Having truly realized the harm I’ve caused, it wrecked me, and I am still reeling from the ripples of devastating effects my actions triggered. No amount of growth will ever take away the suffering I had caused.”
