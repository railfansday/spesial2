EVE Energy Unveils Full-Scenario Energy Storage Solutions for AI Data Centers at RE+ 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

New solutions address power needs across the source-grid, power-distribution and cabinet-side layers of AI data center infrastructure

LAS VEGAS, Nov. 26, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- As AI workloads scale, data centers are placing new demands on power infrastructure, making reliable electricity supply essential to continuous operations. At RE+ 2026, part of North America's Smart Energy Week, EVE Energy introduced a full-scenario energy storage portfolio for AI data centers (AIDC), with solutions designed to support power requirements from the source-grid side through power distribution to cabinet-level backup. The company also showcased energy storage solutions for commercial and industrial, residential and telecommunications applications, alongside emerging all-solid-state and sodium-ion battery technologies.

From the source grid to the cabinet: an integrated AIDC power architecture

During the exhibition, EVE held a dedicated AIDC product launch and presented solutions for three application layers: the green zone, or source-grid side; the gray zone, or power-distribution side; and the white zone, or cabinet side. The portfolio includes rack-level battery backup solutions and is designed to address requirements ranging from millisecond-scale load smoothing to long-duration energy management.

Source-Grid Side: High-Power Energy Storage and Frequency Regulation. On the green zone's source-grid side, EVE Energy introduced the 1P 5MWh ESS, which the company describes as the industry's first 20-foot, 5 MWh, 1P high-rate energy storage system. The system balances 5 MWh of energy capacity with 1P power capability in a standard 20-foot container. It is rated at 1P, with a peak rate of up to 1.2P, and delivers 20,000 equivalent cycles in frequency-regulation applications. System efficiency reaches 93.6%, while auxiliary power requirements are reduced by 20%, according to EVE.

The solution integrates DC, AC and EMS capabilities at the plant level. At a 100 MWh project scale, the configuration can reduce the number of cabinets by three, lower transportation costs by more than 40% and reduce land use by more than 13%. EVE reports annual system availability of 99%, along with higher returns over the system's full lifecycle.

The system is equipped with what EVE describes as the industry's first 300+ Ah dedicated frequency-regulation cell, featuring a capacity of 315 Ah and a volumetric energy density of 395 Wh/L. According to EVE, no lithium plating occurs at the cell interface under high-rate operating conditions, helping ensure cell safety and long service life.

Also on display was Mr.Giant 3.0, EVE Energy's flagship long-duration energy storage system. Following 700 days of demonstrated operation, the system achieved efficiency of more than 95.5% and a cycle count reaching 10,000 cycles. EVE says the system can deliver lifecycle benefits equivalent to an additional two years.

Power-distribution side: backup for transient loads. EVE presented its Fortron 6C/10C backup power cabinet, designed to support short-duration, high-power load events. The cabinet supports continuous discharge at up to 10C and can deliver 660 kW for five minutes. It has a 15-year design life and is compatible with uninterruptible power supply (UPS) backup systems, with support for high-voltage direct current (HVDC) applications.

The modular architecture is designed for factory pre-integration and rapid deployment. Safety provisions include a reinforced cell design, compartmentalized thermal isolation, staged overcurrent protection and coordinated fire-suppression systems. These features are intended to help protect the system while supporting fast-response backup applications.

Cabinet side: rack-level support for dynamic AI loads. EVE also introduced a rack-level energy storage system combining a battery backup unit (BBU) with a capacitor backup unit (CBU). The BBU uses lithium iron phosphate (LFP) high-power cells in a high-density 3U form factor and supports discharge rates of up to 48C. The system is designed to smooth load fluctuations across a 1-millisecond-to-10-second window, while providing backup power transfer in less than 2 milliseconds.According to EVE, The LIC hybrid supercapacitor has a response time of 1 millisecond or less.

Building a full-scenario energy storage platform for AIDC

Spanning the green zone on the source-grid side, the gray zone for power distribution and the white zone at the rack level, EVE Energy's newly launched AIDC energy-storage portfolio reflects years of sustained investment, development and expertise across the company's full range of battery products. With one of the world's most comprehensive lithium battery product portfolios, EVE Energy is among the few companies capable of directly supplying both battery cells and systems across the entire AIDC power chain. The company's end-to-end capabilities extend from battery cell development through system integration.

The integrated approach is intended to simplify procurement and operations for customers. A single-source offering can reduce the number of vendor relationships and streamline purchasing and project decisions. Coordinated development of cells, BMS and system integration can also clarify accountability when issues arise. A unified technical platform and service contact are designed to reduce maintenance complexity and operations and maintenance (O&M) costs for data centers that operate around the clock.

EVE says it ranked No. 2 globally in energy-storage cell shipments in the first half of 2026. The company reports cumulative global shipments of 230 GWh and inclusion in S&P Global's Tier 1 list of energy storage system integrators. Building on its strategy of "global manufacturing, global cooperation and global service," EVE says it will continue developing grid-forming capabilities and energy storage products for the full range of AIDC applications. The company aims to become a trusted global energy partner for AI computing infrastructure, supporting reliable and lower-carbon power for the AI era.
