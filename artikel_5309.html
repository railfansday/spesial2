Nvidia’s CEO meltdown, who could replace Jensen Huang, and our latest AI tips and tricks

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

by Todd Bishop on Nov 14, 2026 at 8:30 amNovember 14, 2026 at 8:30 am

This week on the GeekWire Podcast, we react to the sudden downfall of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, who resigned Thursday after reports of extramarital affairs, including one with a woman suspected of being a Chinese spy.

We discuss the governance gaps at the world’s most valuable chipmaker, the long but low-profile Microsoft career of interim CEO Colette Kress, and who could take over permanently, including candidates from Microsoft, Amazon and the chip industry.

In the second segment, Todd explains how AI tools are changing the way he reports, with help in live interviews, research in news archives and an analysis of GeekWire’s coverage that shows Pacific Northwest M&A and IPO activity picking up this fall.

Inspired by Colette Kress’s new role, the GeekWire Trivia Challenge looks at what some of Microsoft’s former finance executives have done after leaving the company.
