CAMBRIDGE, England, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- FocalPoint today announced the expansion of its S-GNSSÂ® technology into the smart cities and robotics markets, extending its positioning technology beyond automotive to a growing range of autonomous and connected systems. The move brings reliable GNSS positioning to emerging physical AI systems, including delivery drones, mobile robots and micromobility platforms.

The expansion addresses a common challenge across these applications: maintaining reliable positioning in challenging environments. In cities, tall buildings reflect satellite signals, creating multipath that degrades accuracy. Under thick foliage and around other obstacles, satellite signals can be weakened or obstructed. Receivers are also increasingly vulnerable to jamming and spoofing. And the compact form factor of many robotic platforms often leaves antennas poorly placed.

FocalPoint's SupercorrelationÂ® signal processing addresses these challenges at the GNSS receiver level. It improves accuracy in multipath-heavy environments, strengthens resilience to spoofing and jamming, and helps recover performance where antenna placement is compromised. It works with existing receiver hardware, with no additional sensors required.

As location-aware systems become increasingly autonomous, reliable positioning is fundamental to navigating their surroundings, supporting location-based services and operating safely around people. A delivery robot deciding which way to cross or a robotaxi receiving false positioning information from a spoofer must make decisions based on the information available to it. There is no driver to catch the error.
