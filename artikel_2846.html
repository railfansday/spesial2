Swansea 'acknowledge' Matos' move to Strasbourg

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Swansea City have belatedly confirmed that head coach Vitor Matos has left the Championship club by saying only that they "acknowledge" Strasbourg's statement over his move to the French side.

Strasbourg announced the appointment of Matos as their new boss on Wednesday evening.

Around 23 hours later, Swansea issued a statement of their own featuring only one line on Matos.

"Per RC Strasbourg's announcement, Swansea City acknowledges that Vitor Matos and his coaching staff have left the club," the Welsh club said.

Swansea's statement initially misspelled Matos' first name, as Victor rather than Vitor. The same error was made in Strasbourg's English language social media announcement of his appointment on Wednesday.

The 'mistake' was quickly updated on the Swansea's website. When asked whether the misspelling had been deliberate, a Swansea spokesperson declined to comment.

Swansea say former captain Ashley Williams will lead a temporary coaching team tasked with filling the void left by Matos' departure.

"Swansea City can confirm club greats Ashley Williams, Leon Britton, Joe Allen and Kris O'Leary – a quartet boasting over 1,400 appearances in black and white – will be at the helm for Saturday's game against West Bromwich Albion," the club's statement added.

"Additionally, head of academy goalkeeping Paul Whitfield has stepped up to the senior set-up, with the group taking today's (Thursday) training session at Fairwood.

"The club wants to place on record its thanks to Ashley, Leon, Joe, Kris and Paul for making themselves immediately available without hesitation, and we are grateful to have people with such a great passion for Swansea City guiding us through this period."

Ex-captain Ashley Williams has been assisting Wales boss Craig Bellamy since September, but has never held a senior managerial role [FAW]

Strasbourg are expected to pay Swansea a compensation fee thought to be in the region of £5m for Matos, who has left despite agreeing a new contract running until 2030 in July.

The 38-year-old spent just under a year at Swansea, having left his role as Maritimo boss - after only 12 games in charge - to succeed Alan Sheehan as the Championship club's head coach in November 2025.

Four members of Matos' coaching team - Diogo Medeiros, Toze Mendes, Goncalo Ricca and head of goalkeeping Pedro Pereira - are also expected to leave Swansea.

Allen and Williams return to the club to join an interim coaching team alongside fellow former players O'Leary and Britton.

O'Leary and Britton are the only remaining members of Matos' backroom staff, with academy goalkeeping coach Paul Whitfield temporarily stepping up to first-team level.

The hastily assembled coaching group will lead Swansea while the club searches for Matos' permanent successor, with their first game in charge coming when West Bromwich Albion visit on Saturday.

Swansea then go to Middlesbrough next Tuesday before hosting Welsh rivals Cardiff City on Sunday, 29 November.

"Our focus is firmly on this weekend's fixture against West Bromwich Albion and collectively working to continue to drive the club forward," the Swans' statement added.

Swansea's long-awaited statement on Vitor Matos' exit – or should we say Victor – is about as cold as they come.

The Welsh club are unhappy over various aspects of Matos' move to France.

Without saying as much, they have made that abundantly clear.

Barely a week ago, Matos was his usual chirpy self at Swansea's Fairwood training base, as he chatted to BBC Sport Wales about Marko Stamenic's three-match suspension and which of his injured players might be fit to play against West Bromwich Albion this Saturday.

By last weekend, Strasbourg had made their move for the Swansea head coach – and suddenly Matos' plans for West Brom were forgotten.

His surprise departure all happened very swiftly and, as Swansea's statement shows, has left a sour taste for many within the club.

The wait for the Swans to say anything was not a result of bitterness towards Matos.

BBC Sport Wales understands the Championship side - unusually in this sort of situation - did not know Strasbourg's announcement was coming on Wednesday, and they were particularly surprised when it did given that they did not believe the process of finalising Matos' move had been completed.

It all amounts to a sorry end to a reign which brought plenty of progress, and had promised much more.
