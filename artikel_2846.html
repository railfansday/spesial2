Cops reveal surprising outcome for brawling fans at Niners vs. Cowboys game

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

None of the Niners and Cowboys fans seen brawling at AT&T Stadium on Sunday were arrested for their violent behavior, authorities told the California Post on Monday.

The Arlington Police Department said in a statement to The Post that while five people were taken into custody during San Francisco's big 46-40 win over Dallas, "none of those were related to fights inside the stadium."

"They were primarily for Public Intoxication," the APD added.

People supporting both the Cowboys and the Niners got into fistfights throughout the teams' matchup, according to videos posted to social media.

CeeDee Lamb of the Dallas Cowboys catches a fourth-quarter touchdown against Deommodore Lenoir of the San Francisco 49ers in Arlington, Texas, Nov. 15, 2026. Getty Images

In one of the clips that was shared on X, four fans — including two women — were seen wrestling in a concourse inside Dallas' home stadium.

In another video that went viral on social media, several men clad in 49ers jerseys could be seen throwing punches at one another during a heated argument at their seats.

The APD said "about a dozen fans were ejected during the course of the game," however, "we did not end up arresting or charging any fans in connection to any fights yesterday."

A San Francisco 49ers fan and a Dallas Cowboys fan fight at AT&T Stadium. Instagram/@dtxtvmedia

Fan-on-fan violence has been an issue for the 49ers for two straight weeks, as on Nov. 8, several of the team's supporters were captured on video getting into wild altercations in and around Levi's Stadium during San Francisco's matchup with Las Vegas.

The Santa Clara Police Department said 11 people were arrested at the game while 45 ejections were made. It also announced on Nov. 10 that the 32-year-old seen on video disturbingly striking an unconscious man was taken into custody for felony assault.

Niners general manager John Lynch called the scenes "a shame," while the team said in a statement it has "a zero-tolerance policy for violence at Levi's Stadium."
