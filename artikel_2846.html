Previewing the New York Jets’ opposition: Los Angeles Chargers

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The last time the Jets beat the Chargers, they were still representing San Diego and Plaxico Burress scored three touchdowns. Since that time, New York have lost five straight times to them, including a 27-6 drubbing in their most recent meeting in 2023.

Let's break down the Chargers' roster going position by position:

Justin Herbert was a pro bowler last season, but he's having a rough year in 2026, with career-worst numbers in almost every category. In his first six seasons, he averaged 27 touchdown passes and 11 interceptions per year. However, over halfway through the 2026 season, he has thrown for just six touchdown passes and already has 11 interceptions.

He didn't have a very productive game as a passer against the Ravens on Monday night, but at least he managed not to throw an interception for the first time all year as the Chargers finally won their first game to improve to 1-8. He also rushed for a season-high 51 yards and a touchdown.

DJ Uiagalelei is the backup but he's completed just two NFL passes in his career.

Incredibly, five of the Chargers' eight active offensive linemen are rookies, although this is hardly by design. They have five offensive linemen on injured reserve, including well known names like Rashawn Slater, Tyler Biadasz, Trevor Penning and Trey Pipkins. Pipkins was activated for practice last week, though, so his return is possible on Sunday. His official game status is questionable.

Unsurprisingly, this has led to the Chargers struggling to protect Herbert, who has been sacked 33 times. Only Deshaun Watson has been sacked more often that that this season.

The only two players to start every game so far have been second round rookie Jake Slaughter at center and veteran right guard Cole Strange.

At the moment, Joe Alt is at left tackle, with sixth round rookie Logan Taylor at left guard. Slater and Penning had started off the year on the left side, but Alt moved across from right tackle and Taylor stepped in when each of them got hurt. Since Alt's move, fourth round rookie Travis Burke has been starting at right tackle.

Alt has a team-high seven penalties and Strange, who has also given up a team-high three sacks, has six.

The three players currently on the bench are veteran guard Kayode Awosika, sixth round tackle Alex Harkey and the undrafted center Jacob Spomer. Awosika and Harkey have started two games each this season.

Los Angeles' top running back, Omarion Hampton, last season's number one pick, is still averaging less than four yards per carry behind the banged-up line, although the Chargers rank 10th in the NFL for rushing offense. He's coming off his first 100-yard rushing performance of the season in the win over the Ravens.

Keaton Mitchell and Kimani Vidal both get work as backups to Hampton but they combined for just four yards on five carries in the Ravens win. They have caught 30 passes and scored three touchdowns between them, though.

Scott Matlock is also on the roster as a fullback with Alec Ingold on injured reserve.

The leading receiver so far this year for the Chargers is last year's second round pick Tre Harris who has 26 catches for 348 yards but no touchdowns.

You would have expected Ladd McConkey and Quentin Johnson to produce more alongside Harris but they've only started eight games between them and combined for just 31 catches. However, they've flashed big play potential with Johnson averaging 19 yards per catch and McConkey averaging 17. On Monday, McConkey – who is questionable for Sunday – was inactive and Harris was the only Charger with more than 16 receiving yards.

At tight end, Oronde Gadsen II has caught 20 passes and Charlie Kolar has caught 15, but David Njoku has been a non-factor. Njoku could play more this week, though, because Kolar has been dealing with a concussion and is listed as questionable.

The backups at wide receiver are Theo Wease Jr., KeAndre Lambert-Smith, Derius Davis and fourth round rookie Brenen Thompson. They've combined for 12 catches, half of which were by Lambert-Smith. Thompson is listed as doubtful for Sunday.

The Chargers operate with a 3-4 system that employs Jamaree Caldwell, Dalvin Tomlinson and Teair Tart as starters. They are poor against the pass, but middle of the pack against the run, and held the Ravens to 16 yards on 15 carries on Monday night.

Fifth round rookie Nick Barrett and Justin Eboigbe are the backups inside. All five have played at least 140 snaps with Tomlinson having contributed 2.5 sacks.

The Chargers drafted Akheem Mesidor in the first round but he's currently coming off the bench behind veteran Khalil Mack and 2025 pro bowler Tuli Tuipolotu. While none of the three has more than three sacks, they've combined for 8.5. Mack leads the team with six tackles for loss, while Tuipolotu leads them with 16 quarterback hits.

At inside linebacker, Daiyan Henley plays a full-time role and wears the headset. He leads the team with 62 tackles but has been bothered by a knee issue all year, which even nixed a potential trade to Denver. The knee issue has ruled him out for Sunday.

The other starter has been former Jet Del'Shawn Phillips, although Henley was sometimes the only off-ball linebacker on the field. Backups Troy Dye and Marlowe Wax don't get to play much but Wax should have a bigger role with Henley out.

Bud Dupree and Caleb Murphy are the backups on the outside but neither has played much.

Aside from all their injuries on the offensive line, the Chargers have also been deeply impacted by pro bowl safety Derwin James being on injured reserve.

Tony Jefferson and Elijah Molden are the current starters at safety, although RJ Mickens and fourth round rookie Genesis Smith have also played over 200 snaps each.

Kendall Williamson is also on the roster but hasn't played on defense and has been listed as out anyway.

Tarheeb Still and Donte Jackson are the starting cornerbacks with Still reverting to the slot in nickel packages and Cam Hart entering the game on the outside. Hart has two interceptions and a team-high eight pass breakups.

Still practiced all week but showed up on the injury report on Friday and is listed as questionable.

Backups Rodney Shelley, Isas Waxter and Deane Leonard have all played less than 100 snaps at cornerback. Shelley is another undrafted rookie but he's been ruled out, while Waxter is also listed as questionable.

The Chargers' kicking game is pretty solid with kicker Cameron Dicker having only missed two kicks all season. He also has the third-lowest opponents' kickoff return average in the NFL. While punter JK Scott's raw numbers are not eye-popping, Pro Football Focus currently ranks him first in the league based on their grades.

Davis, Mitchell and Vidal have all seen work on kickoff returns, with nobody managing a 35-yard return so far this year. The Jets should be wary of Davis, though, as he had an 87-yard punt return for a touchdown against them in 2023. He's averaging 10 yards per return on punts with Thompson and McConkey listed as his backups.

Jets fans may recall what a good special teams contributor Phillips was, and he's continued in that vein by leading the Chargers in special teams tackles again despite moving into the starting unit. He led the entire NFL last year.

Wax, Murphy and Dye are also productive, while the primary gunners have been Lambert-Smith and the backup cornerbacks.
