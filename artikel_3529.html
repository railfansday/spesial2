Arnold Schwarzenegger Shares His Secret to Staying Young at 79

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Arnold Schwarzenegger is sharing his candid thoughts on aging.

“I don’t think that you have the time, nor do I have the time, to go through the list,” the Terminator actor, 79, told Woman’s World in a cover story published on Wednesday, November 18, when asked what he “could no longer do that he could at 29.”

While the outlet noted that Schwarzenegger is “more cautious about physical risks” these days after years of weightlifting and movie stunts took a toll on his body, he’s not ready to give up on his workout routines completely.

“The training keeps us going,” he shared. “When I leave this gym, everything turns into a — it goes from black and white to a color movie. The whole world looks different afterwards.”

Schwarzenegger also noted the importance of staying connected to others.

“We need human contact. It’s the thing that makes you happy,” he shared.

Another thing that brings Schwarzenegger pride and joy is being a grandfather. His and ex Maria Shriver’s daughter Katherine Schwarzenegger shares three children of her own with husband Chris Pratt: Lyla, 6, Eloise, 4, and Ford, 2. (Arnold and Shriver, 71, also share kids Christina, 35, and Patrick, 33. Meanwhile, the actor is also a dad to son Joseph, 29, with Mildred Baena.)

“I’m so impressed by what great parents Katherine and Chris are,” Arnold, whom Woman’s World crowned its first-ever World’s Hottest Grandpa, told the magazine. (Katherine, 36, and Pratt, 47, tied the knot in 2019.)

Comparing being a grandfather — or an Opa, the German translation of the word, as his grandkids call him — to being a dad, Arnold added, “I’m not responsible now for disciplining them. That’s Katherine’s job. To me, with them in my house, it’s all for their enjoyment.”

Spending time at Arnold’s house, where he has several farm and rescue animals, is a favorite activity for his grandkids — even if it means cleaning up after the animals go to the bathroom.

“With every pleasure there comes also some, maybe misery, something tough,” Arnold recalled telling his grandkids. “Now the first thing they do when they come over to the house is ask, ‘Can we go and shovel the poo?’”

When asked what his grandkids love most about their action star grandfather, he told the outlet, “I think that they know they get a laugh when they’re with me.”

Arnold also shared what he hopes the children will remember most about him.

“I think that they should know that Grandpa was a hardworking person,” he said. “There’s no shortcut.”

The latest issue of Woman’s World hits newsstands nationwide on Friday, November 20.
