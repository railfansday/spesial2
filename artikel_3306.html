Matthew McConaughey Is Hype for U2’s Thanksgiving Eve NFL Halftime Show

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Matthew McConaughey waxes lyrical about the thrill of seeing U2 live in a new trailer for the band’s upcoming halftime performance at a special NFL game the night before Thanksgiving.

The new trailer features black-and-white shots of U2 and their devoted fans, while McConaughey intones in a voice-over: “I don’t know how many times they’ve been the soundtrack to my story. But it’s when you see them live that you really understand. Thousands of people feeling exactly what you’re feeling: hope, meaning, a little bit of magic. Every time.”

At this point, U2’s “Beautiful Day” kicks in as the rest of the trailer plays out, and McConaughey concludes, “That’s what U2 is to me. And there’s nothing quite like feeling it live.”

U2 will take the stage during halftime of the NFL’s first Thanksgiving eve game, Nov. 25. The game will feature the Green Bay Packers versus the Los Angeles Rams, and will air on Netflix. The streamer has been organizing special halftime performances since it began airing select NFL games a few years ago. Beyoncé performed at Netflix’s first NFL game on Christmas Day 2024, while Snoop Dogg took the stage last year. U2 recently released their 16th studio album, Carnaval de Luz, marking their first album of all new material since 2017’s Songs of Experience. (In 2023, they released Songs of Surrender, featuring newly recorded versions and interpretations of older songs.) In a recent Rolling Stone cover story, Bono said the album was “made through, probably, the most disturbed period any of us have lived through,” noting he didn’t mean personally, “but globally.”

He added, of the band’s intentions, “It’s not putting your head in the sand. It’s just saying, ‘These people can’t control our mood.’ We don’t even have to name them, because everyone has their own version of who that is.”
