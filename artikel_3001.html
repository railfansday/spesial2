Stephen A. Smith makes ‘bandwagon’ Super Bowl prediction

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

With Week 11 in the window, the NFL playoffs get closer, and the real contenders are starting to set themselves apart.

At the start of the season, the expectations for the Los Angeles Rams were based on previous years' success, the addition of Pro Bowl defensive end Myles Garrett, and the return of former Super Bowl champion Aaron Donald. But the team's slow start deterred most critics from holding onto any hopes that they once had for the Rams.

Now, ESPN's Stephen A. Smith has claimed the Rams as his pick to be Super Bowl contenders this year. On a recent episode of First Take, the crew discussed the hurdles that remain for the Rams to secure a ticket to Los Angeles as late-season action intensifies.

"They come out of the Week 11 bye at home against Green Bay, which holds the final wild card spot," Smith said. "I think they can beat Green Bay, then they host Kansas City, and they visit San Francisco, and two of their final three games are against Seattle, so I'm looking at it from that standpoint. And it's hard to imagine they beat Seattle twice, maybe once, but not twice."

The Rams were a heavy favorite this season, but early injuries to Garrett and Donald, as well as several offensive pieces, including star receiver Davante Adams, have made it a little harder for the team to adjust. Now on a four-game win streak, the Rams have switched gears and are tied for second in the NFC West, and "bandwagon" fans have resurfaced.

"But I'm not jumping off the bandwagon; I pick Aaron Donald and Myles Garrett to be at SoFi for the Super Bowl, and I'm staying on that bandwagon until further notice," Smith added.

The Rams have grabbed wins against the Arizona Cardinals, the Washington Commanders, the Las Vegas Raiders, and the Los Angeles Chargers, but these teams are all near the bottom of their respective divisions. With a heavy schedule ahead after their Week 11 bye, the Rams have a lot to think about before they can consider Super Bowl LXI.
