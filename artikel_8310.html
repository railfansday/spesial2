Personal Consumer Issues • Re: HAM Radio Operators?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Amateur radio has changed a lot over the years and it continues to evolve.

The number of traditional voice and CW contacts has been in decline in recent years. The digital modes are very active with a great deal of activity in FT8. Activity on the HF bands is affected by the eleven year sunspot cycle and the resulting changes in propagation.

Contests have become very popular. There are contests running just about every day of the year.

There is a lot of activity on VHF and UHF with many repeaters supporting both narrow band FM and the digital voice modes.

Amateur radio today spans a wide range of activities. You can operate RTTY with an actual radioteletype machine or a computer. Amateur TV is available with several different modes supported. You can work amateur radio satellites which have a separate uplink and downlink.

You can launch a helium balloon and track its location with APRS.
