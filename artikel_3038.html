Offerpad Solutions Inc. (OPAD -4.61%) reported a shift in its timeline for reaching positive adjusted EBITDA to the end of the second quarter of 2027. Management stated that a rapid increase in mortgage rates to approximately 7.4% in early October contributed to a more cautious market environment, impacting home sales conversion. The company is responding by widening its expected margins on acquisitions, reducing its annualized operating expenses, and expanding its asset-light, fee-based channels. Management indicated that these actions are intended to reduce the transaction volume required to break even while maintaining capital discipline across its active listings in key markets.
