Celtic will retain patience with Kasper Hogh despite concern over his struggle for goals, sources have told Football Insider.

The 25-year-old joined in the summer transfer window from Bodo/Glimt in a club-record deal worth around £11million, but has struggled to find his feet.

Despite scoring a hat-trick on just his second appearance for the club, the Dane has failed to find the net in any of the 16 games since.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Celtic are still confident that they will see the best of Hogh.

The Denmark international starred in Norway, attracting particular attention when he netted against Manchester City, Atletico Madrid and Inter Milan during last season's Champions League.

Celtic will hope to see that quality eventually emerge at Parkhead, with Martin O'Neill's team yet to be at their consistent best so far this season.

Football Insider revealed in October that Celtic held concerns over an injury Hogh suffered just weeks after joining the club.

The Dane's thigh issue came in late August and kept him out for three games, with it unclear if he has fully returned to fitness in the months following.

Hogh has not scored since, although he was cruelly denied by VAR after appearing to have broken his duck against Kilmarnock on Sunday.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I wouldn’t say they’re concerned, obviously there’s a bit of alarm, maybe at his lack of goals.

"Celtic, as you said, he was their big summer transfer, they spent a lot of money to sign him from Bodo/Glimt after he had a great time there, scoring goals in the Champions League and everything else.

"He had a bright start, scoring that hat-trick in his second game against Kilmarnock but hasn't scored since. He did pick up an injury soon after that game, so maybe that set him back a bit as well.

"He had a goal disallowed last time out as well against Kilmarnock, but I just think maybe Martin O’Neill is still trying to work out his best team as well and maybe we’re not seeing the best of Kasper Hogh because of that."

Despite his struggles, O'Neill has continued to persist with Hogh in the hopes that one goal will see him explode into form.

"We thought Kasper had got that goal and then it was chalked off," the Northern Irishman said after Sunday's game.

"Then a few minutes later he had another chance. And I think if the first goal had counted then I think perhaps he then gets a second goal.

"It’s maybe a lack of confidence in front of goal at this minute. But I thought he worked exceptionally hard for us during the game – as he did on Thursday night. The goals will come."
