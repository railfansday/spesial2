Small Caps and Blue Chips Outpace Broader Market Through Midweek Session

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

VANCOUVER, BC, Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Stock Preachers News Commentary - Stocks were mixed in afternoon trading Wednesday as the Russell 2000 and Dow Jones Industrial Average pushed to gains while the S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite slipped on the day. The small-cap and blue-chip benchmarks remain the strongest performers for the week, with volatility easing sharply from last Friday even as it ticked higher on the day. Treasury yields fell and gold firmed modestly as traders weighed a batch of new corporate headlines. Active Companies from around the markets with current developments this week include: Deere & Company (NYSE: DE), Palo Alto Networks (Nasdaq: PANW), Alimentation Couche-Tard Inc. (TSX: ATD), Amgen (Nasdaq: AMGN), and PayPal Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: PYPL).

The Dow Jones Industrial Average traded at 50,318.97 as of 2:54 p.m. ET Wednesday, up 300.27 points (0.60%) on the day and up 2,171.64 points (4.51%) on the week. The Russell 2000 stood at 2,707.27, up 22.33 points (0.83%) on the day and up 125.48 points (4.86%) on the week, its strongest weekly gain among the major benchmarks. The S&P 500 was little changed, down 9.70 points (0.13%) on the day at 7,191.39, though it remains up 281.00 points (4.07%) on the week. The Nasdaq Composite fell 124.20 points (0.52%) to 23,609.34 on the day while holding a 991.61 point (4.38%) weekly advance.

The CBOE Volatility Index rose 1.45 points (5.50%) on the day to 27.81 but remains down 8.08 points (22.51%) on the week. The PHLX Semiconductor Index fell 96.39 points (1.09%) on the day to 8,736.11, still up 327.52 points (3.90%) on the week. The US 10-year Treasury yield slipped 6 basis points on the day and 7 basis points on the week to 4.84%.

Gold rose 0.10% on the day to $3,565.50 per ounce, up 1.43% on the week. WTI crude oil rose 1.68% on the day to $83.43 per barrel but remains down 0.65% on the week. Natural gas jumped 10.05% on the day to $3.657 per MMBtu, up 7.21% on the week. The US Dollar Index slipped 0.17% on the day and on the week to 103.63.

Deere & Company (NYSE: DE) Deere & Company reported net income of $1.051 billion, or $3.89 per share, for the fourth quarter ended November 1, 2026, compared with $1.065 billion, or $3.93 per share, a year earlier. For fiscal year 2026, net income attributable to Deere was $4.860 billion, or $17.95 per share, versus $5.027 billion, or $18.50 per share, in fiscal 2025. Worldwide net sales and revenues decreased 2% to $12.115 billion for the quarter and increased 4% to $47.704 billion for the full year.

Net income attributable to Deere for fiscal 2027 is forecasted to be in a range of $4.50 to $5.25 billion, which includes an estimated one-time, non-cash pension settlement charge of $0.25 to $0.40 billion. Read the release.

Palo Alto Networks (Nasdaq: PANW) Palo Alto Networks reported fiscal first quarter 2027 total revenue of $3.44 billion, up 39% year over year. Next-Generation Security ARR grew 66% year over year to $9.73 billion, and remaining performance obligations grew 35% year over year to $21.0 billion. GAAP net income was $482 million, or $0.57 per diluted share, compared with $334 million, or $0.47 per diluted share, a year earlier.

The company also announced its intent to acquire Applied Compute, which it said will allow it to deliver secure open weight models to customers.

For fiscal 2027, the company expects total revenue in the range of $14.52 billion to $14.57 billion. Read the release.
