Hamilton flag football coach Matt Stone resigns

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Chandler Hamilton flag football coach Matt Stone has resigned, athletic director Brett Palmer confirmed to The Arizona Republic on Nov. 24.

Stone, who was a central figure in flag football getting sanctioned by the Arizona Interscholastic Association, was the first coach in school history for Hamilton.

During Stone's tenure over the past four seasons, Hamilton posted a 55-11 record and won back-to-back 6A state championships in 2024 and 2025 to establish the sport's first dynasty in Arizona. Hamilton won 40 consecutive games from 2024 to 2026, a state record.

In 2025, The Republic followed along with Hamilton's team for the video documentary, "Beyond The Gridiron," which recently won a 2026 Rocky Mountain Emmy Award.

Under Stone's helm, Hamilton was nationally recognized as well. Hamilton was frequently featured in the MaxPreps top 25 and USA TODAY national rankings. At one point, Hamilton was ranked as high as No. 1 in the nation by USA TODAY.

This past season, following the graduation of key players and the transfer of record-breaking junior quarterback Marlie Phillips, was a tough one for Hamilton. The Huskies went 5-8 and missed the playoffs, the first time that happened with Stone as coach.

With Stone leaving, the Hamilton flag football coaching position becomes the top opening in the state. The job opening was posted on the CUSD website earlier Tuesday.

The Republic reached out to Stone for comment but had not heard back as of the afternoon of Tuesday, Nov. 24.
