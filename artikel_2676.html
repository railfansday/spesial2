Buffalo Bills at New York Jets, Week 10 LIVE game chat

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Buffalo Bills made it to New Jersey without concern after a wild Friday that saw the United States' aviation industry grind to a halt due to Air Traffic Control maintenance. Like those who had infrastructure to attend to, so, too, do the Bills by way of their Week 10 game against the New York Jets.

Entering today at 7-1, Buffalo can take a real stranglehold on the AFC East with a win today. The 2-7 Jets want nothing to do with that, and aren't likely to just disappear the same way the nation's air traffic patterns did Friday into Saturday.

With their loss early today over in Munich, Germany, the New England Patriots have opened up the door for both the Jets and Miami Dolphins to begin clawing their way toward second place in the division. The Bills must take care of business in the tri-state area this afternoon, not just for the AFC East crown, but to also remain in first place among the entire AFC.

This is only Buffalo's second divisional game of the season, and they're still looking for their first AFC East win. The Bills are the only undefeated road team (4-0) left in the NFL this season. They've won four games in a row, while the Jets are on a four-game skid.

Will those trends continue today? You know the answer if the Bills are as good a team as fans hope is true. With that, here's your open thread for Bills-Jets. As always, be kind to one another — and in all things… Go Bills!
