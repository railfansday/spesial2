No. 5 Texas Tech Beats San Diego State 78-75 For 5th-Place Finish in Las Vegas

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Darrion Williams had 18 points, six rebounds and four assists to lead No. 5 Texas Tech past San Diego State, 78-75, in the fifth-place game of the Players Era 16 tournament in Las Vegas late Saturday night, with the final buzzer extending beyond the midnight hour local time.

Despite shooting 41 percent from the field, the Red Raiders used a 17-of-19 showing from the free throw line and 13 points scored off 17 turnovers forced to avoid going 1-2 on the weekend after losing their first game of the season.

Texas Tech fell to No. 25 Louisville on Tuesday. The Cardinals will play for the tournament championship against Creighton on Saturday evening.

Three other Red Raiders joined Williams in double figures, with veteran sharpshooter Donovan Atwell hitting four of his seven 3-pointers to score 17 points.

"Three games in four days is about grit," Tech head coach Grant McCasland said pregame. "You don't get points for style; you get them for wins. That's what we're moving towards and we're excited about having an opportunity to keep doing it."

The Red Raiders' first 13 shots were from behind the arc, and more than half of them went down for a first-half lead that grew to as large as 10.

Williams was 3-of-3 from 3-point range to start the game with 13 first-half points.

San Diego State, however, answered with a 6-0 run. Texas Tech was 1-of-11 from the field and missed their last seven shots from downtown to end the first half.

The Aztecs were led in scoring by Tae Simmons, who scored a game-high 21 points on 7-of-9 shooting. Bear Cherry added 16 points on 70 percent shooting from the field.

The Red Raiders, however, held onto a 30-28 lead at the break.

Texas Tech maintained the first-half lead despite being without its top interior force, Jamichael Stillwell, who was limited due to foul trouble. Josiah Moseley picked up the slack on the inside to give Texas Tech its first 2-point field goal with 8:25 to go in the half, a layup establishing a 25-15 lead.

Stillwell returned and hit a fadeaway hook shot, and Williams converted a 3-point play to begin the second half offensively for the Red Raiders. Dra Gibbs-Lawhorn hit a pair of free throws to extend Texas Tech's second-half surge to 15-8 a few minutes later.

Gibbs-Lawhorn's nifty layup gave Texas Tech a 49-40 lead by the under-12-minute media timeout in the second half. The Red Raiders briefly stretched their lead back to double digits on a 3-pointer by freshman DaKari Spear, but Stillwell spiked the ball and was called for a technical foul on an and-one basket on Arizona State's next possession.

Simmons' fast-break slam brought the score to 52-49 as San Diego State sprinted to a 9-0 run in response with 9:04 to go. The dunk followed a block by Cherry, who rejected nine shots in the Aztecs' last game, a 4-point win over Maryland.

The Red Raiders scored off a turnover as Spear's triple grew Tech's lead back to nine at 60-51. Atwell, the NCAA's active leader in made 3-pointers, hit a pair of triples to extend Tech's lead to 11 a few possessions later.

Chance Gladden's second 3-pointer of the game came from the Players Era logo near center court, trimming the Red Raiders' lead down to six with 3:44 to go as Tech coach Grant McCasland called a timeout. Cruz Davis' layup built on the Texas Tech lead out of the timeout, but Moseley then fouled out for the team's already-slim frontcourt.

With just over 2 minutes remaining, Stillwell missed a short layup, which then led to the Aztecs' two free throws that cut the Tech lead to four points.

At the 56-second mark, Elzie Harrington hit a pull-up 3-pointer to bring San Diego State to within three at 73-70. Atwell responded with a 3-pointer for the Red Raiders to push the lead back to double digits.

The Aztecs hit a 3 off an offensive rebound to draw within one, but Atwell sealed the Texas Tech win with just seconds remaining.

The Red Raiders host Omaha on Dec. 8 at United Supermarkets Arena in Lubbock. Texas Tech is 5-0 at home this season and has yet to play a true road game before this week's holiday trip to Las Vegas.
