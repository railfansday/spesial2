Darren Waller, Tommy Tremble return to Panthers practice Thursday

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Panthers got a pair of key offensive contributors back on the practice field Thursday.

Tight ends Darren Waller and Tommy Tremble were both listed as limited participants. Both players are dealing with knee injuries and the team will issue any injury designations for their game against the Ravens on Friday afternoon.

Linebacker Bobby Okereke (foot) and safety Isaiah Simmons (knee) remained out of practice. Okereke has missed the last two games.

Running back Chuba Hubbard (rest) and linebacker Trevis Gipson (ankle) moved up to full practice. Cornerback Mike Jackson (groin) remained a full participant after saying on Wednesday that he expects to return from injured reserve this weekend.

Cornerback Will Lee (hamstring), offensive lineman Brady Christensen (hamstring), running back Jonathon Brooks (groin), quarterback Bryce Young (foot), linebacker Jackson Kuwatch (shoulder), and tight end Feleipe Franks (wrist) were also full participants.
