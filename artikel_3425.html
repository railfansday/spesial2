The Legend Of Zelda: Twilight Princess Officially Returns For New 40th Anniversary Collection

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess proved to be an enormous success at launch despite (or likely as a direct consequence of) its drastic change in art style and tone. Twilight Princess' darker atmosphere and deeper exploration of The Legend of Zelda's previously restrained eerie elements have since stood the test of time, offering an experience unlike any other in the series that has yet to be replicated by Nintendo. It is widely considered to be one of the best Legend of Zelda games of all time, and will likely remain so for quite some time.

However, as aforementioned, Nintendo has never really revisited Twilight Princess and its bold approach to the Zelda formula. As it is looking increasingly likely that the next mainline Zelda game will skew closer to the Ocarina of Time remake in tone and gameplay style, it appears even less likely that we'll get something akin to Twilight Princess any time soon. Fortunately, to ease the blow just a tad, a brand-new range of Zelda 40th anniversary merchandise is dropping, and it includes a notable nod to Twilight Princess.

Takara Tomy, the Japanese toy manufacturer behind a number of Legend of Zelda merchandise and gachapon toys, is releasing a new range of Zelda collectibles. The Legend of Zelda Historical Charms+ collection features 12 different keychain accessories, each of which is based on one of the mainline Zelda games released across its 40-year history. Styled after the Triforce, each keychain features an image from the respective Zelda game's box art inside a triangle attachment, as well as the name of said game.

While it isn't quite as exciting as a Twilight Princess Switch 2 remake, or even a more traditional port, the Twilight Princess Historical Charm+ keychain is a fun collectible for fans of the game to add to their ever-growing collection of merch. These keychains will be made available in gachapon machines across Japan, and it'll cost 300 yen (about $2) per capsule. Of course, you're not guaranteed to get any specific keychain with each attempt, so it could end up costing quite a bit in order to get just the Twilight Princess one, for example, if you're unlucky.

Unfortunately, the Legend of Zelda Historical Charms+ line of collectibles is exclusive to Japan and currently does not have an international release. It is likely that resellers will make these available to fans outside of Japan through secondhand sites for a substantial markup. However, if you wish to avoid paying any extra fees, your best bet is to buy them in Japan. This set appears to be a spiritual successor of sorts to Takara Tomy's previous keychain-focused gachapon collection from 2017. The keychains from that release were nearly identical to this new set; however, there were only six of them, and the set covered just The Legend of Zelda, A Link to the Past, Ocarina of Time, The Wind Waker, Twilight Princess, and Breath of the Wild. If you happened to get the Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess keychain from the last set, then you can probably skip out on this 40th anniversary collectible.
