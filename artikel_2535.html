NFL Power Rankings: Week 11 Edition

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Now that Week 10 of the 2026 NFL season is in the books, it's time for an incredibly meaningful and serious exercise: NFL Power Rankings! What differentiates these rankings from all others is that they're THE only truly accurate ones in the entire universe. Hard to believe, I know. But it's true. Let's take a look at how all 32 teams stack up. (Here's a link to last week's version.)

1 – Chicago Bears (Last Week: 1) – The Bears return from their bye to host the Saints. That game is certainly winnable; they're 11.5-point home favorites. Despite this, Chicago has THE most difficult remaining strength of schedule in the NFL. They're going to be majorly tested down the stretch.

2 – San Francisco 49ers (LW: 2) – The Niners own the Cowboys. They're 5-0 in their last five meetings against Dallas … while averaging 32 points per game. For that much, Eagles fans can appreciate this team.

3 – Buffalo Bills (LW: 3) – The Bills are really good. They've also been fortunate to face the following quarterbacks in recent weeks: Joe Fagnano, Carson Wentz, Max Brosmer, and Cade Klubnik.

4 – Kansas City Chiefs (LW: 4) – So much for any concern about Patrick Mahomes' health; he threw five touchdowns and zero interceptions in KC's most recent win.

5 – Detroit Lions (LW: 5) – Don't sleep on the Lions. They've won five in a row and they're 2-0 in division games. By contrast, the Bears are 0-2 in NFC North action. Unfortunately, they did take a major hit with Aidan Hutchinson suffering a season-ending injury while celebrating a sack.

6 – Jacksonville Jaguars (LW: 6) – The Jags are riding a three-game winning streak. Their average margin of victory in this stretch is 25 points. They're looking tough to beat and they have the NFL's fifth-easiest remaining strength of schedule.

7 – Seattle Seahawks (LW: 7) – Are we sure Drew Lock shouldn't be the full-time starter? He's 3-0 and has a 125.6 passer rating this year. Sam Darnold is at 79.5.

8 – Cincinnati Bengals (LW: 8) – Joe Burrow is currently the betting favorite for MVP at +100. The dude is on fire. He's above Josh Allen at +350 and Brock Purdy at +400. The Bengals are also now the No. 1 NFL team by DVOA.

9 – Minnesota Vikings (LW: 9) – So far, so good on Kirk Cousins' return to Minny. Given how strong their defense is, the Vikings needed mere competency at quarterback and it seems like they got it.

10 – Los Angeles Rams (LW: 10) – After starting the season 2-4, the Rams have rebounded to win four straight … albeit against lesser competition. They're probably better than their 6-4 record indicates.

11 – Denver Broncos (LW: 11) – The Broncos return from their bye to host the Raiders. It's the first meeting of these two AFC West teams this season. Denver is favored by 9.5 points at home.

12 – Green Bay Packers (LW: 12) – We've entered a weird stretch in this week's power rankings where all the teams above this mark did not lose in Week 10 … and five of the following six teams did. So, that's why there's little movement here. Anyway, the Packers deserve to be bumped down for a decisive home loss to Minny. They seem less ready to hang with the elites.

13 – Las Vegas Raiders (LW: 14) – Fernando Mendoza wasn't particularly sharp in his NFL debut, which was to be expected going up against a tough Seattle defense. We'll see if he can show up bigger against the Broncos in Week 11.

14 – Pittsburgh Steelers (LW: 15) – A blowout loss to Cincy dropped the Stillers to second in the AFC North. Aaron Rodgers isn't inspiring much confidence at the moment.

15 – Cleveland Browns (LW: 19) – Todd Monken has earned Coach of the Year consideration. The Browns entered this season with a projected win total of 5.5 games. Cleveland erased a 17-point deficit against Houston to pick up their sixth win of the season on Sunday.

16 – Dallas Cowboys (LW: 16) – We shouldn't let this dreadful Dallas defense (46 points and 584 yards allowed!) distract us from talking about how Dak Prescott is an elite garbage time stat padder.

17 – New York Giants (LW: 17) – The Giants had a chance to effectively wrap up the NFC East by beating Washington at home. They failed to do so. It's possible they'll still win the division anyway … but they'll be kicking themselves if they don't.

18 – New England Patriots (LW: 13) – The Pats' season is probably over with Drake Maye going down. A.J. Brown is also hurt … again. We'll see if this group has any resiliency before totally writing them off.

19 – Tampa Bay Buccaneers (LW: 20) – The Bucs return from their bye to play in Detroit. Tough matchup for Tampa … but they'll be rested while the Lions are coming off international travel.

20 – Carolina Panthers (LW: 21) – It was a narrow win for the Panthers but they beat the Saints to put themselves on top of the NFC South … with a 4-5 record. For all the jokes about the NFC Least, at least their division leader isn't under .500.

21 – Baltimore Ravens (LW: 18) – Oof. Baltimore's quarterback situation obviously isn't ideal but you've still gotta find a way to beat the winless Chargers while at home. Instead, the Ravens are slipping out of the AFC playoff picture.

22 – Washington Commanders (LW: 26) – Give the Commanders credit: Thursday was a must-win game for them to keep their season alive … and they out and beat the Giants. I'd argue that the result was more about New York blowing it than Washington asserting themselves. Nevertheless, the Commies are alive … and face the NFC East's easiest remaining strength of schedule. Not impossible that they make a run.

23 – New Orleans Saints (LW: 22) – No team is worse at running the ball. They're not going anywhere at this rate.

24 – Houston Texans (LW: 23) – Any chance of the Texans coming back to win the AFC South is over now after Houston choked away a 17 to 0 lead in Cleveland. Just not a team that deserves to be taken seriously.

25 – Atlanta Falcons (LW: 24) – We've seen enough to say that Michael Penix isn't the answer. The Falcons need a new quarterback in 2027.

26 – Arizona Cardinals (LW: 25) – Carson Beck logged a 38.4 passer rating in his NFL debut. That's rough.

27 – Indianapolis Colts (LW: 31) – The Colts were competitive against Dallas in Week 9 and beat the Dolphins in Week 10. That's not much. But it's more than nothing.

28 – Miami Dolphins (LW: 27) – They lost to Indy.

29 – Los Angeles Chargers (LW: 32) – No longer do the Chargers hold the NFL's longest losing streak! The Bolts are on the board. They're also favored in their next two games: vs. NYJ and vs. NE. Can you say … winning streak?!

30 – New York Jets (LW: 28) – Make that five straight losses for the J-E-T-S. The last two have been blowouts, too. Aaron Glenn's seat is only getting hotter.

31 – Philadelphia Eagles (LW: 29) – The Eagles' bye was one of the most enjoyable weeks of their season. They return to host the Steelers as 4.5-point home underdogs. We'll see if the time off actually helped at all or if the Birds will continue to struggle.

32 – Tennessee Titans (LW: 32) – The Titans are currently projected to have the No. 1 overall pick in the 2027 NFL Draft. It'd be their second No. 1 overall pick in three years.
