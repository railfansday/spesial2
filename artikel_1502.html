Man United consider shock Dominic Calvert-Lewin move as club launch search for new striker after Benjamin Sesko injury blow left them with NO fit centre-forward

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Published: 15:44 EDT, 29 October 2026 | Updated: 20:15 EDT, 29 October 2026

Manchester United are exploring the possibility of a move for Leeds striker Dominic Calvert-Lewin as the club looks to strengthen in attack in January.

Daily Mail Sport understands a move is viewed as difficult given the fierce rivalry between the two clubs.

Calvert-Lewin has revitalised his career at Leeds after joining on a free transfer in the summer of 2025, following an injury-plagued end to his time at Everton.

The 29-year-old has scored 20 goals in 49 appearances for Leeds, including five in ten games at the start of this season.

Calvert-Lewin scored against Man United when the two sides played out a 1-1 draw earlier this month.

The forward’s form earned him an England recall during the last international window, ending a five-year absence from the national team set-up.

Calvert-Lewin, who has started 38 times in his 43 Premier League appearances for Leeds, is currently contracted until 2028.

Man United are currently operating without a recognised striker with Benjamin Sesko having been ruled out until at least January with a shin injury.

The 23-year-old was struggling with the issue at the beginning of the season and saw his game time carefully managed as the club attempted to nurse him back to full fitness but those efforts now appear to have been in vain.

Sesko has not featured for the club since their defeat by Brighton in the Carabao Cup on September 16, when he was substituted in the 66th minute.

He missed all of pre-season with the complaint, which also plagued him last season and resurfaced against Liverpool in May, causing him to miss the final three Premier League games.

The Slovenian, who pulled out of international duty last month, now looks set to be sidelined until the new year, dealing a major blow to United during a difficult run of form and leaving Michael Carrick without a recognised striker.
