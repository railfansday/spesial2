MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR vs MacBook Pro: Here’s every difference

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Apple unveiled its new MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR this week (pre-order here), which joins the lineup alongside the existing MacBook Pro using the same M5 Pro and Max chips. Here’s an in-depth comparison of the two models.

The MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR has an all-new industrial design. It’s the first design overhaul for the MacBook Pro since 2021.

You still get a 14.2-inch regardless of whether you choose PROLED XDR or not. On the 16-inch model, PROLED XDR is 16.3 inches, slightly larger than the standard 16.2-inch.

Apple’s new design is a bit sleeker and significantly lighter-weight. It also brings back the glowing Apple logo on the lid that was an iconic element in previous generations. Additionally, the PROLED XDR has a newly designed hinge that provides greater stability.

Here’s a breakdown of how each model compares in size and weight:

As the numbers show, the overall size difference is fairly minor, but the reduced weight should be obvious to anyone who upgrades.

The new MacBook Pro’s display technology is so important, Apple put it in the branding. MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR features a tandem OLED that Apple says is “the world’s best laptop display.”

Again, it’s available in 14.2-inch or 16.3-inch sizes that are nearly identical to the standard MacBook Pro.

The PROLED XDR display is touch-enabled, with macOS 27 introducing a variety of optimizations for touch input. So if you want a touchscreen on your Mac, that’s a big advantage.

Tandem OLED means you’ll get deeper blacks, true-to-life color, and also a new max peak brightness of 2,000 nits (up from 1,600). And Adobe RGB is now supported, along with a host of industry-standard reference modes.

There’s also a Dynamic Island on the new MacBook Pro. It replaces the notch and serves as the home to software features like Spotlight, Live Activities, and Siri AI.

Apple is using the exact same M5 Pro and M5 Max chips in its two MacBook Pros, so performance should in theory be the same across models.

That said, real-world testing will reveal whether the new MacBook Pro design has an impact on sustained performance—for better or worse.

MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR largely retains the same storage options as the standard MacBook Pro, with one exception.

The only change here is that there’s now a high-end 16TB option when you choose M5 Max. Previously, 8TB was the most storage available in any MacBook Pro.

MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR offers identical battery life and fast charging features as the standard MacBook Pro. That means:

All MacBook Pro models can fast charge up to 50 percent battery in 30 minutes.

Apple’s MacBook Pro models all feature the same 12MP Center Stage camera with support for Desk View, 1080p HD video recording, and an advanced image signal processor with computational video.

However, because of touchscreen support, the PROLED XDR model adds one new feature: Advanced Video Stabilization.

Essentially, this exists so that if you’re touching your display during a video call, it won’t have an impact on your camera feed.

The MacBook Pro PROLED XDR features largely the same speaker system as the standard model, but with a pair of key upgrades. Per Apple Newsroom:

The industry-leading six-speaker audio system in the new MacBook Pro features an all-new woofer design that provides 50 percent deeper bass and all-new tweeters that shape sound dispersion for remarkable clarity…And for the first time, dedicated audio tuning for closed-lid use delivers exceptional sound quality when MacBook Pro is connected to an external display, so users can enjoy a premium audio experience however they work.

The MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR starts at $2,899 for the 14-inch model and $3,399 for the 16-inch. That’s a $400 premium over the standard MacBook Pro.

Apple’s standard MacBook Pro starts at $2,499 for the 14-inch size and $2,999 for the larger 16-inch.

Warm fuzzies for the return of the glowing Apple logo but I won't need the other features so it's not worth the extra $400 to me.

Overall, the MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR offers a lot of upgrades over the standard model, but with the same basic performance and battery life. If you want a better display, touch support, a Dynamic Island, and various other smaller improvements, PROLED XDR is the way to go.
