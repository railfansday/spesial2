All 2 DC TV Shows & Movies Releasing On HBO Max In December 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

DC fans can get ready for a new movie and the latest season of a long-running series to arrive on HBO Max in December to end 2026 in style. What a ride this year has been for DC Studios, who ends it in a much different place than when it started. After all, 2025 was the height of James Gunn's DCU.

Superman's release finally allowed DC to have the highest-grossing superhero movie of the year in a long time, beating three Marvel Cinematic Universe films. However, with Supergirl becoming a major box office bomb and Clayface not turning into a hit like other 2026 horror movies, sentiment regarding the DCU soured, and as Paramount closed its deal to acquire Warner Bros. Discovery, everything changed.

Gunn and Peter Safran are out as DC Studios co-CEOs, with former Marvel Studios executive Jeremy Latcham now set to update the plan for DC's characters moving forward. Amid this period of uncertainty, there are no new DCU projects set to drop in December. The shared universe will return next year with Gunn's Superman movie sequel, Man of Tomorrow, and shows like Mister Miracle and The People v. Gorilla Grodd.

While the DCU has ended its slate of releases for 2026, there are other Elseworlds franchises, which exist outside the shared universe's canon, with projects that superhero fans can enjoy in December. Both of them are animated, including a movie and a TV show, though they are completely different in tone. These are the two DC movies and TV shows coming to HBO Max in December:

Batman: Knightfall Part 1 is the first film in a new animated trilogy that adapts one of the Dark Knight's most iconic stories from DC Comics. This is the classic story that saw Bane break Batman both emotionally and physically, which has been adapted in a Batman movie before. After all, one of the best directors of all time, Christopher Nolan, used elements of the Knightfall arc for his final adventure with the DC hero, The Dark Knight Rises. However, the Batman movie was not all that accurate to the comic book storyline, which is where Batman: Knightfall Part 1 comes in.

The animated Knightfall trilogy's plan is to be as close to the comics as it can be, with key characters like Azrael, Nightwing, Robin, and more being part of this new movie franchise. In Batman: Knightfall Part 1, Bane breaks Batman's Rogues Gallery out of Arkham Asylum, creating a gauntlet of villains for the Dark Knight to face until he is exhausted, which is when Bane proceeds to break Batman himself. This is the DC release on HBO Max in December for older viewers, as the Batman movie is R-rated, allowing the Dark Knight's fights with villains to be visceral.

Batman: Knightfall Part 1's cast comes with major stars, as Star Trek and Marvel Cinematic Universe actor Anson Mount voices Bruce Wayne, while Spider-Man: Brand New Day's Scorpion star, Michael Mando, gives life to one of the most haunting versions of Bane to date. Batman: Knightfall Part 1 will be released on HBO Max on December 7, just in time for viewers to get ready for its sequel, Batman: Knightfall – Part 2: Knightquest, which releases on digital on December 15.

Teen Titans Go! is simply the longest-running DC series of all time, which is an impressive feat, and its latest batch of episodes, season 9, releases on HBO Max on December 20. The Teen Titans make up one of DC's best teams, perhaps second only to the Justice League, with charismatic characters and a nice balance of powers. Teen Titans Go! also stars the same cast of the original 2003 Teen Titans animated series, with Scott Menville (Robin), Hynden Walch (Starfire), Tara Strong (Raven), Greg Cipes (Beast Boy), and Khary Payton (Cyborg).

Teen Titans Go! Season 9 will also become available on Hulu on December 28.

Teen Titans Go! is fun for the whole family, making for a great introduction to the wider DC universe for kids, while also being written for adults in a sense. There are a lot of references and jokes that older viewers will be able to spot, with the show's humor being suited for viewers of all ages. Season 9 features a wide range of DC characters besides the Teen Titans, with Harley Quinn, Black Canary, The Penguin, Swamp Thing, Hawkman, Lex Luthor, Superman, The Joker, Darkseid, and more appearing. These are all the DC movies and TV shows being released on HBO Max in December 2026.
