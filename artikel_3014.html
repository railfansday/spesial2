Over time, the relationship can turn from a project into a pipeline. Now let me spend some time on what makes our Direct Breeding platform different. Every crop in the world is the product of plant breeding and breeding has always been indirect. To add a trait, a breeder crosses its best variety with a donor plant that carries the trait.

The offspring is only half the variety the breeder wanted, and so it is crossed back round after round until the parents variety genetics are fully recovered with the trait included. That takes most of a decade in development and testing before years of growing enough seed to sell. The first generation of gene editing technology shortened the search for the right change, but most commonly, its editing machinery was introduced into the plant's DNA, so the machinery had to be bred out again through those same rounds. And if several changes were made at different places in the genome, some of those were lost while the machinery was being bred out.

Every one of those routes spends its years growing whole plants season after season. Cibus takes a different approach. We take a single cell from the seed company's own best variety. We make the changes in that cell, several edits at once if the customers want them and with editing tools that do their work and then break down. None of our editing tools are inserted into the plant's DNA. So our customers have nothing to breed out afterwards. We confirm the changes while there are still cells in a petri dish. The edited cells grow into small clumps and from those, we grow back whole edited plants.

The variety that comes out is the variety that went in now carrying the new traits, and it goes back to the seed company for its own testing and commercial development. We call this Direct Breeding. And I want to highlight 2 very important outcomes that differentiate Direct Breeding from the waves of the past because they are why I believe this platform has the potential to generate revenue at scale. The first is speed. In canola, we are able to return a customer's germplasm with trait edits within 12 to 15 months, and our goal is to achieve that similar turnaround of edits in other crops. The second is several edits at once.

When inserted editing machinery has to be bred out, every extra edit multiplies the cleanup. Our platform leaves no machinery, just highly targeted edits, so a customer can ask for several edits at once. And we do all of this inside our own partner's elite varieties. Unlike some larger companies, we sell no seeds and no chemicals. So we do not compete with our customers for seed sales and seed companies of any size can work with us.
