Grocery Outlet Holding Corp. (GO +1.08%) reported a return to positive comparable store sales in the third quarter, driven by a strategic pivot back to its core opportunistic procurement model. Management stated that the company successfully transitioned away from the broad-based synthetic promotions utilized earlier in the year as the volume of high-value branded deals in the supply chain recovered. The company reported a sequential acceleration in traffic and maintained its expansion pace with 14 new store openings. While gross margins faced pressure from elevated freight and a regional produce health event, management indicated that underlying trends in October suggest momentum is building heading into the fourth quarter. The company revised its full-year guidance to reflect these improved sales trends while tightening EBITDA expectations to account for lumpy maintenance costs and supply chain headwinds.
