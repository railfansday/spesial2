Northern Technologies (NTIC +0.13%) reported record fiscal 2026 consolidated revenue of $95,981,956, although management indicated that profitability did not keep pace with top-line growth. The company appointed Gautam Ramdas as Chief Executive Officer in Sept. 2026, who has prioritized improving operating profitability and cash flow as central objectives for the upcoming fiscal year. Management reported that while segments like ZERUST industrial and oil and gas achieved record annual sales, margins were compressed by volatile raw material costs and a shift in product mix. To address these challenges, the company is focusing on global coordination of research and development, the introduction of higher-margin adjacent products, and the restructuring of joint venture payment models to accelerate cash repatriation.
