Why Dec. 9 Could Be a Huge Day for Rivian Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Rivian's post-earnings rally is fading, but this upcoming event could kick it back into high gear.

On Dec. 9, Rivian Automotive (RIVN -4.84%) hosts its second annual Autonomy and AI Day. Taking a page from the Tesla playbook, Rivian hosts this event to showcase its latest autonomous-vehicle innovations to investors.

Last year's event elicited mixed reactions, but Wall Street could sing a different tune this year if it perceives that Rivian's Autonomy Plus, its subscription-based driver-assistance platform, will help the electric car company continue to scale up and add to its recent R2-related success.

The market knows about Point-to-Point, but one analyst, Stifel's Stephen Gengaro, believes that the event, plus the increasing importance of autonomous driving features as a product differentiator, could make further details of its launch an important catalyst for the stock. Gengaro gives shares a "buy" rating and a $22-per-share price target, which is around two-thirds above Rivian's current stock price.

Rivian's autonomous driving events come on the heels of its well-received third-quarter 2026 earnings release. For the quarter, the company reported nearly $2.2 billion in revenue, a 38% year-over-year increase, driven largely by the success of its R2 midsize SUV. Rivian's top line exceeded Wall Street expectations, as did its reported gross profit of $176 million. Analysts expected $39 million in gross profit on $2 billion in sales.

Rivian also beat expectations on Q4 delivery guidance. Although shares rallied immediately after earnings, this rally has since lost momentum. Investors' focus may be shifting back to other concerns, such as Rivian's timeline to reach full profitability. While such concerns could persist after Dec. 9, this event could still shift focus back to the growth story, triggering another rally for what remains one of the most popular electric car stocks.
