Matt LaFleur on Packers' run-game struggles: &quot;We're trying&quot;

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Packers weren't able to stop the run on Wednesday against the Rams. To apply a twist to a classic line from John McKay, they made up for it by not being able to run.

"Right now, we're a pass-heavy offense and we're trying to sneak in some runs there to at least keep the defense honest," coach Matt LaFleur told reporters after the 26-13 loss when asked about his team's offensive identity.

"We're trying, for sure," LaFleur said. "We're trying."

He also was asked whether they tried to run it enough.

"Obviously not, not when you lose the game like that," LaFleur said. "So I would say the answer to your question is no. I mean, you've got to convert third downs too. You've got to get those opportunities to get some first downs. And the second half, I think we had one drive that was a seven-play drive or whatever and everything else was like three-and-out or two-and-out and we turned the ball over."

On a night when the Rams had 241 yards rushing against only 132 yards passing, the Packers mustered only 55 rushing yards on 14 attempts.

Not having Josh Jacobs didn't help matters. Without Jacobs (who's recovering from a broken finger), the Packers' backfield consisted of Kaleb Johnson (eight carries for 13 yards), MarShawn Lloyd (one carry for 10 yards), and Chris Brooks (one carry for four yards).

Receiver Savion Williams ran the ball twice for 18 yards, making him the leading rusher for Green Bay. Quarterback Jordan Love added one for seven, and receiver Bo Melton ran one time, for three yards.

And, no, that's not good enough. Especially on a night when receiver Christian Watson was lost early to a concussion.

Even if the Packers can muster enough wins over the final six weeks to qualify for the postseason, it will become very hard to win in January if they can't run or stop the run.
