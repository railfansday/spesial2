Steelers open practice window for Michael Pittman Jr, Roman Wilson

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Pittsburgh Steelers suffered their most embarrassing loss of the season on Sunday Night Football to the Cincinnati Bengals in Week 10, with the Bengals gaining control of first place in the AFC North. But with the division and playoffs still very much in reach for the Steelers, they got great news on Wednesday with the return of four players to practice.

Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy announced Wednesday that the team opened the 21-day practice window for four players: Michael Pittman Jr., Roman Wilson, Gennings Dunker, and Max Hurleman.

Pittsburgh later announced that Hurleman was activated to the practice squad, with wide receiver Isaiah Hodgins being released to make room.

Pittman Jr. returns to practice after suffering a plantar fasciitis injury that landed him on injured reserve on Oct. 10. The wide receiver was seen working off to the side last week and running with the team during warmups. He even went as far as to say that he felt healthy enough to return last week. Now he will get that chance to provide a boost to the Steelers' receiver room.

Also a boost to the room, Roman Wilson is set to return after suffering a finger injury in Week 5. Wilson and Pittman Jr.'s return should bode well for a Steelers passing game that has looked lackluster over the course of the last month.

Max Hurleman is also a name to watch, after he was the subject of a lot of praise from coaches and teammates last week. After several years of string training camp performances, the team made it clear how they feel about Hurleman. Now he returns to practice and will fight for an opportunity to get a helmet on game day.

After the injury to starting right guard Mason McCormick, getting Gennings Dunker back provides some nice depth. Dunker was one of the Steelers' three third-round picks last April, and is viewed as more of a developmental player than anything. Even with his return and the injury to McCormick, fans should still expect Spencer Anderson to hold down the right guard duties.

The Steelers still have to activate Pittman, Wilson and Dunker before they can play. Hurleman has been activated to the practice squad but would have to be elevated to appear in a game. Particularly for Wilson and Pittman Jr., keeping track of their participation in practice this week will be key to whether or not they play as soon as this weekend against the Philadelphia Eagles.
