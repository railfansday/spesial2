Sam Darnold did not practice Wednesday; Drew Lock was limited

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Seahawks have already ruled out Sam Darnold for Week 10, with the starting quarterback's return potentially coming in Week 12 after the bye. He did not practice on Wednesday, with ribs and quad injuries.

Drew Lock will start against the Raiders.

Lock was limited in practice as he navigates right elbow and quadriceps injuries.

Lock completed 19 of 28 passes for 175 yards with a touchdown in the 17-3 victory over the Cardinals. He also rushed five times for 16 yards while playing every offensive snap.

Offensive tackle Charles Cross (ankle), offensive guard Quenton Nelson (knee), defensive tackle Brandon Pili (back), defensive tackle Jarran Reed (personal), wide receiver Jaxon Smith-Njigba (rest) and defensive tackle Leonard Williams (stinger) were the others who did not practice.

Offensive guard Anthony Bradford (knee), linebacker Derick Hall (back), linebacker Uchenna Nwosu (ankle) and linebacker Patrick O'Connell (toe), linebacker Connor O'Toole (foot) were the others who were limited.
