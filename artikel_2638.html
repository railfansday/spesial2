Frank Gore And Richmond Webb Get 2027 Hall Of Fame Nod

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Pro Football Hall of Fame trimmed its candidate list for the 2027 class down to just 52 names on Tuesday, and two former Miami Dolphins are still in the running. Frank Gore and Richmond Webb earned spots on this short list, keeping their legacy hopes alive for the next year. Yahoo Sports reported that the selection committee made this cut to narrow the field before the final voting process begins.

Richmond Webb, now 59, was a massive force for the Dolphins in the early 1990s. He played 11 seasons in Miami after the team took him with the ninth overall pick in the 1990 NFL Draft. Webb appeared in 164 games while wearing the teal and orange, earning seven Pro Bowl nods and four All-Pro selections. Two final seasons with the Cincinnati Bengals concluded his career.

Running back Frank Gore brings a different kind of durability to the conversation. The 43-year-old spent 16 seasons in the league, rushing for a staggering 16,000 yards and scoring 81 touchdowns. Gore also caught 484 passes for 3,985 yards and 18 more scores over his long career. His time with Miami was brief, consisting of just 14 games where he rushed for 722 yards.

Making the initial 52-man list is a huge hurdle for any player. It means the committee sees these athletes as top-tier candidates worthy of serious debate. The final class will be much smaller, likely around five or six players who get the gold jacket. Gore and Webb have cleared the first major gate, but the real work starts now.

The numbers show why both men deserve to be here. Webb was an All-SWC standout at Texas A&M before becoming a first-round pick. Gore won a BCS national championship at the University of Miami before being drafted by the San Francisco 49ers. Their combined stats and accolades prove they were among the best at their positions for over a decade.

Both players have strong claims based on their time in the NFL. Gore earned five Pro Bowls and one All-Pro team, whereas Webb secured seven Pro Bowls. These honors highlight their consistency and impact on the field. The Hall of Fame process is slow, but these stats keep the pressure on voters to recognize their careers.

The final decision will come later in the year after more deliberation. The 2027 class will be announced in early February, marking the end of this long nomination process.
