AJ Brown compared to one of New England Patriots’ worst ever NFL Draft picks after Lions loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The New England Patriots made a major move this offseason by trading a first-round pick for former Philadelphia Eagles wide receiver AJ Brown. The 29-year-old was expected to level up their offense and give Drake Maye an elite weapon in the passing game.

However, Brown has had a quiet start in New England, recording just nine receptions for 101 yards and no touchdowns across three games. He struggled again in Sunday's 35-10 defeat to the Detroit Lions in Munich, prompting Bill Simmons to make an unflattering comparison.

AJ Brown's difficult time in New England has been made worse by injuries, having already missed time this season due to knee and ankle problems. He returned for Sunday's clash against Detroit in Germany but failed to make much of an impact before another setback ended his afternoon early.

The former Eagles receiver has had fewer than 50 receiving yards in his first three Pats games and is yet to score a touchdown. His performances have already attracted criticism, and Bill Simmons was particularly unimpressed by what he saw against the Lions.

The Patriots fan compared Brown to N'Keal Harry, who was selected with the 32nd overall pick in the first round of the 2019 NFL Draft. Simmons tweeted: "AJ Brown can't even get open by one foot – it's like having N'Keal Harry back."

Harry spent three seasons in New England before being released. He managed only four touchdowns for the Patriots and became one of the franchise's biggest draft disappointments.

Simmons also questioned why Mike Vrabel kept Brown on the field while trailing heavily against Detroit. He added: "Let's keep a not-100% AJ Brown out there in a 28-3 game on a bad field; What could go wrong?"

Brown eventually left the game in the fourth quarter after picking up a hamstring issue. The Patriots now have fresh injury concerns over two of their most important offensive players, as Drake Maye was also forced off early with an ankle problem.
