Thunder’s Mark Daigneault addresses ‘common theme’ after loss to Timberwolves

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

OKLAHOMA CITY — Oklahoma City Thunder head coach Mark Daigneault addressed a common thread in his team's shortcomings after a 113-106 loss to the Minnesota Timberwolves. Oklahoma City has dropped four of its last five games, including a 125-120 blowout loss to the Indiana Pacers amid a three-game skid.

Daigneault and the shorthanded Thunder are without Chet Holmgren, Jalen Williams, Cason Wallace, Aday Mara, and Jaylin Williams, among others, which continue to create more obstacles. Still, Daigneault sees ways for the Thunder despite their slim margin for error.

"The common theme is: it's a 48-minute game, and we need to execute a few more plays in a game that comes down to one or two possessions in order to be on the other side of the result," Daigneault said. "And that can happen in the second quarter. It can happen in the first quarter, and it can happen late. So, we just got to fight for those margins right now. Improve on the things that we know the game is going to come down to. There's plenty of controllable things that we can handle in these games.

"And that shows up in the first quarter, shows up in the fourth quarter; shows up throughout. We just got to continue to invest in those things. Trust that process and allow the wind to turn for us a little bit."

Thunder head coach Mark Daigneault watched his team hang around the Timberwolves before a 24-10 third-quarter run helped keep Oklahoma City at bay throughout the second half. After the Thunder pulled to within four (110-106) late in the final frame, the Timberwolves closed out the win on both ends of the floor. Oklahoma City didn't score another point.

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander led the Thunder with 32 points, five assists, and two blocks. Ajay Mitchell finished with 25 points, three rebounds, three assists, and one steal.
