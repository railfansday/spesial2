Elementary School Teacher Arrested for Alleged Possession of Child Pornography

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A community in Texas was left in shock after an elementary school teacher was arrested for allegedly possessing child pornography.

On Friday, November 20, physical education teacher Bryan Rasco was booked on three felony charges of possession of child pornography.

Authorities learned of the allegations against Rasco, 58, when Amazon flagged files that were uploaded to an Amazon Photos account earlier this year, according to an arrest affidavit viewed by Us. Rasco possessed or accessed the files with intent to view child sexual abuse material on or about May 3, according to the complaint.

The lead investigator in the case was assigned to look over tips that the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children made available to the Houston Metro Internet Crimes Against Children Task Force on May 6. Investigators then used records to link Rasco to the account.

On November 9, the lead investigator contacted a Conroe ISD police detective, who confirmed that Rasco worked for the district.

Investigators are still working to identify Rasco’s potential victims. However, the Montgomery County Precinct 1 Constable’s Office said that authorities currently do not believe any of Rasco’s alleged offenses took place on school property.

Following Rasco’s arrest, Conroe ISD’s Executive Director of Communications Andrew Stewart told Fox 26 that the school district “first learned of the investigation” on Friday morning.

“The district placed Mr. Rasco on administrative leave and notified San Jacinto Elementary families in writing today. Families seeking support may contact the campus counselor or an administrator,” he said. “The district will continue to cooperate with law enforcement.”

Not only has the district spoken out, but parents and relatives of children at the school have also expressed how upset they are over the situation.

“I was very shocked,” Suzanne Bruner told the Fox 26. “I had two sons that went here that now are in college, and I had two sisters that went there. So I’ve been in this school for a really long time.”

Meanwhile, Lynn Lager – a retired teacher and grandmother – said her family had already been talking about how to explain the news to her younger family members.

“And even on the way over here, we were talking in the car, like, how do you talk about that with your kid, you know?” she said.

It’s not currently clear if Rasco has entered a plea or retained legal counsel following his arrest. The Montgomery County District Attorney’s Office had no additional updates to share when contacted by Us about the case.

As the investigation remains active, authorities have asked that anyone with information regarding the case call the Montgomery County District Attorney’s Office Internet Crimes Against Children Task Force at 936-538-7800.
