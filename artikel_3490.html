Final Fantasy 10 Officially Returns November 30 In New Collection

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Final Fantasy 10 is one of the most beloved entries in the long-running RPG franchise from Square Enix. In the years since the game originally launched on PS2, the love story between Tidus and Yuna has especially resonated with fans. With a loveable cast, compelling narrative, and turn-based combat, Final Fantasy 10's popularity only continues to grow. And thanks to a number of remasters and re-releases in the decades since, new generations continue to get to experience Final Fantasy 10 for the first time.

Final Fantasy 10 is celebrating its 25th anniversary, and that means a number of official new releases from Square Enix and its partners. This includes new Yuna and Tidus figures, plushes, shirts, and other merchandise. It also includes the recent release of Final Fantasy 10/10-2 HD Remaster on Switch 2 and Xbox. Now, another official collection has been announced, and this one arrives on November 30, 2026.

To celebrate Final Fantasy 10 turning 25, fans can try to get their hands (pun intended) on FINAL FANTASY X - Tendence De'Color Watch Tidus Mode. A watch for Yuna is releasing as well. Both run for around the same price, with Hobby Genki listing Tidus' watch for ¥78,550, which comes out to around $495 USD. The site currently lists the watch with a release date of November 30, although it's currently sold out. Considering the limited availability of the items, restocks may not happen.

FINAL FANTASY X - Tendence De'Color Watch Tidus Mode, which can be viewed below, "captures the essence of Spira's oceans with a striking ice-blue and white gradient." This is a 50mm watch that is part of the Tendence's De'Color series. The officially-licensed timepiece features the logo of the Zanarkand Abes (the team, you might remember, that Tidus plays for in Final Fantasy 10 before he winds up in Spira).

As you might expect, the rest of the Tendence watch is also designed around items that fans would associate with Tidus. There's a Blitzball second hand, and there are additional custom hands that are modeled after the Brotherhood and Ultimate Weapon swords, according to the official listing. Tidus is famously holding the Brotherhood sword on the cover of Final Fantasy 10 on PS2, so that's a pretty cool detail to include here.

This is an admittedly costly collector's item, but considering it's currently sold out, that doesn't seem to have stopped it from selling like hotcakes. Final Fantasy 10 is one of the most popular Final Fantasy games of all time, so there's no shortage of fans out there. If you're looking for something a little less expensive, Square Enix is currently selling matching Yuna and Tidus plushes, as well as scale figures of the iconic duo. These are also a part of the game's 25th anniversary collection.

If you're looking for other ways to celebrate, then consider checking out Final Fantasy 10/10-2 HD Remaster on Switch 2 or Xbox. I've been replaying the games on Switch 2 and have really enjoyed them. As Square Enix and fans continue celebrating the 25h anniversary, we'll hopefully see some fun additional bits of merchandise announced for Final Fantasy 10 in the upcoming months.
