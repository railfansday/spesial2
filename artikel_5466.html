Pine AI Open-Sources an AI Filmmaking Studio and Brings Real-World Phone Calls to Pine Computer

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Weeks after launching its AI-native computer, Pine AI unveils new applications and capabilities built on the platform, starting with open-source filmmaking and autonomous phone calls.

SAN FRANCISCO, Nov. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Pine AI today released Pine Drama Studio, an open-source AI filmmaking studio built on Pine Computer. It also brought Pine Phone into Pine Computer, so AI agents can make real-world phone calls as part of their work.

The announcements mark Pine Computer's next step: from a computer built for AI to a platform for AI-native applications. Pine Computer drew about 24 million views on its Oct. 9 launch day. Developers in early access were already building on it, and production teams now use it for video creation and other long-running work. Pine Drama Studio shows that agents can run an entire creative production. Pine Phone takes them beyond software, into conversations with real businesses.

"Pine Drama shows what agents can create. Pine Phone shows how they can act in the real world," said Stanley Wei, co-founder and CEO of Pine AI. "We gave AI a computer. Now we're seeing what people can build with it."

Pine Drama Studio: agents run the production, creators direct it

Pine Drama Studio grew out of what developers built on Pine Computer. AI short-drama production was the first workload to take off, and professional teams brought the hardest requirement: whole series, not single clips.

One of those teams, an AI short-drama studio in Australia, moved its production onto Pine Drama Studio in October, after a September beta. Each member of its team used to produce 2 to 3 minutes of video a day. The same people now produce 30 to 50 minutes a day, with several episodes in production at once.

"We used to spend most of our time generating clips, fixing inconsistencies, and stitching everything together. With Pine Drama Studio, the same team can now produce 10 times more video while running multiple episodes in parallel." said Alec, CTO of an Australia-based AI short-drama production company.

Pine Phone makes the phone call a native capability of Pine Computer, not a separate voice assistant. An agent changing a booking in a browser may find that the last step needs a call. It phones the business, talks it through, and finishes the task, without switching apps.
