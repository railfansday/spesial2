CoreWeave's bond yield tops 16% as AI credit stress spreads wider

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Free daily briefing on global business news.

CoreWeave's credit default swaps spiked above 1,289 basis points on Thursday, higher than their October peak

CoreWeave saw the yield on its 9.625% bond due in 2032 climb above 16%, according to CNBC, as investors demand more protection against default across AI-linked borrowers.

The company's five-year credit default swaps rose beyond 1,289 basis points on Thursday, a level higher than their peak in October, according to CNBC. The contracts eased 8% on Friday to around 1,155.

Default protection has also grown more costly for larger issuers over the past month, according to CNBC. Swaps on Nvidia $NVDA debt rose 76%, Broadcom $AVGO’s rose 49% and Amazon $AMZN’s rose 44%. Those contracts remain low in absolute terms: Nvidia’s sits near 139 basis points and Oracle $ORCL’s around 298, compared with about 1,155 for CoreWeave.

CoreWeave's bonds slid on Wednesday after Democratic candidates who want to curb data center construction took governorships, Texas among them, according to Bloomberg. Its 2031 unsecured bonds hit a low of 79.5 cents per dollar of face value, and its 2030 and 2032 notes also declined, with yields topping 15%. CoreWeave stock dropped 9.9% on Wednesday to close at $48.42, its weakest finish since May 2025.

Texas Gov.-elect Gina Hinojosa has pressed for a moratorium on building data centers, according to Bloomberg. Democrats Amy Acton in Ohio and Keisha Lance Bottoms in Georgia have also advocated slowing construction. Moratoriums could touch 45% of the data center capacity in the nationwide pipeline, and Texas holds a big share of that, according to CNBC, citing Goldman Sachs $GS.

"We do not currently anticipate any impact to CoreWeave's existing customer commitments or associated backlog as a result of shifts in the political landscape," a CoreWeave spokesperson said in a statement, according to Bloomberg.

Financing for AI projects has not stopped. Goldman Sachs counts five bond sales tied to AI, worth a combined $67 billion, dating back to Oct. 29, according to CNBC. National Economic Council Director Kevin Hassett told CNBC on Friday he anticipates that data centers will keep expanding at the projected pace.

CoreWeave's borrowing costs were already rising in October, when its 2032 bond carried a yield to maturity of 14.8% and its stock fell 16% in one day. The company reported net interest expense of $640 million in the second quarter, more than double the $267 million a year earlier. During that quarter it raised more than $10 billion in unsecured debt and convertible bonds, plus a $3.1 billion term loan.
