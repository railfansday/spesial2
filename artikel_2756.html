C.J. Stroud, Texans take care of business vs. cratering Colts: TNF winners, losers

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

If you tuned in to stream the Houston Texans vs. Indianapolis Colts on "Thursday Night Football" to kick off Week 11 of the 2026 NFL season ... we're sorry for you. Hopefully you had the good sense to log out early.

If not − or if you're looking for a (very quick) recap − the Texans unsurprisingly put the Colts down in a 26-3 romp, though one that should give Houston added relevance moving forward.

But while we wouldn't suggest one of the worst games of the season will have massive ramifications across the league's landscape, there are certainly going to be some ripples. Your winners and losers from "TNF" on Nov. 19:

Four days after what could eventually prove a seismic collapse at Cleveland, the Texans improved to 4-6 and jumped up to ninth place in the conference − two games (and a tiebreaker) behind the Browns for the AFC's final wild-card berth. Still, Houston's schedule does have the potential to continue vaulting this team past some of its collapsing conference brethren.

An efficient night for Houston's QB1, who launched three TD passes and once again looked like the linchpin of this team. Stroud, who turned down an extension in the offseason, could find the market renders him the league's top-paid player in a few months ... if this keeps up.

If you were smart − or obsessed − you steered clear of football in Houston in order to start running roughshod in Vice City (don't catch any strays, Dolphins). We wish we'd been there to join you, getting fresh tags on that boosted Lambo.

Playing for the first time in nearly six weeks after recovering from a groin injury, the veteran tailback had his best outing in a Houston uniform, rushing for a season-high 107 yards on 26 carries. And a balanced offense − meaning a sustained running game for a team that's had one 1,000-yard rusher since the start of the 2020 season − could really make this team dangerous if it finds its way into the postseason field, especially given how a ground attack would reduce the need for Stroud to carry the offense.

The NFL's sack leader was a menace, adding to his league-best total (11½) with one takedown of Colts QB Daniel Jones while getting four licks on him Thursday. Houston's All-Pro pass rusher should very much be in this season's Defensive Player of the Year conversation, regardless of how the Texans finish.

You guys are so lucky you were liberated from Indianapolis prior to the league's trade deadline.

Houston broke out its new, predominantly (Liberty) white Nike "Rivalries" uniforms for prime time. Meh? Make no mistake, the team put a lot of thought into the threads and tying them into Houston's culture. But not sure they're going to make many non-Houstonians clamor for Ka'imi Fairbairn jerseys.

So sorry you fellas are consigned to Indy for two more months. All bad things must end ... hopefully. (In case you missed it, Taylor rushed 11 times ... for 21 yards.)

The Colts' QB1 (for now) had another dreadful night, completing 12 of 27 passes for 144 yards and an INT − while operating behind a makeshift line and throwing to a depleted wideout corps. Jones wasn't playing especially well under fairly optimal circumstances this year, but it's only degrading now. Heading into a mini bye, worth wondering if QB2 Anthony Richardson, who mopped up in Houston, gets a shot to play more in the coming weeks.

Bit of a dead man walking vibe around Indy's head coach, who continues forging ahead in his fourth season ... the latest without consistently reliable quarterback play. Whether Steichen is fired next year or retained, he won't be out of work for long − but hopefully he gets a reasonable chance to showcase his ability to generate offense by playing with a full deck in 2027.

The AFC teams most at risk of getting left in the also-ran dust if the Texans get a full head of steam. Beware, too, Browns ... though your margin is pretty cushy right now.

Over the past three weeks, they've provided this game, Commanders-Giants, and the Jaguars rolling through the Ravens and rookie QB Joe Fagnano. The league's schedule-makers might have to consider extending their flex window for "TNF" in the future if this keeps up ... not that you should feel sorry for Jeff Bezos.
