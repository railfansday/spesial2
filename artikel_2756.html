Steelers vs. Eagles: Week 11 game chat

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

After a blowout loss to the Bengals in primetime last week, the Steelers will hope to get their season back on track Sunday against the 2-7 Eagles.

On paper, facing a hapless Eagles roster without its starting quarterback looks like the optimal rebound for 6-3 Pittsburgh. But the game will be in Philadelphia— a city the Steelers haven't won in since the 1960s. The Steelers won't just be hoping to shake a dismal game last week, but a long history of subpar showings in The City of Brotherly Love.
