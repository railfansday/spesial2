Ravens cut WR LaJohntay Wester after 2 muffed punts in Carolina

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Baltimore Ravens are waiving second-year wide receiver/returner LaJohntay Wester after two muffed punts in Sunday's loss to the Carolina Panthers, per Jeff Zrebiec of The Athletic.

Wester, 24, could not handle his first two punts from Sam Martin on Sunday, both in the second quarter. On the first, he signaled for a fair catch but never settled under the ball, instead attempting a diving grab and letting the ball fall through his arms. The Panthers took over at the Ravens' 23-yard line and scored a go-ahead touchdown two plays later.

The second was significantly worse. Wester had ample time and space to make a clean catch, but the ball ricocheted off his helmet and rolled toward Baltimore's end zone. Ravens cornerback Keyon Martin managed to corral it at the 1, forcing the offense to battle out of their own end zone with a minute left in the half instead of pushing for another score.

This is a breaking story that will be updated with more information.
