Giants rumors: San Francisco has obvious demand for potential Logan Webb trade return

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Giants rumors: San Francisco has obvious demand for potential Logan Webb trade return appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The San Francisco Giants reportedly have a firm expectation regarding what they would want in return if they decide to trade Logan Webb this offseason. With multiple teams looking to boost their starting rotations, he could be an elite option on the trade market, but San Francisco is unlikely to consider moving him unless it receives a big package in return.

The Giants showed little interest in trading the three-time All-Star earlier, and Buster Posey was reportedly unwilling to entertain the possibility of dealing him last summer. But a new report from The Athletic's Ken Rosenthal and Will Sammon says the situation may have changed.

"San Francisco would want a 'very strong return' for Webb, according to a person briefed on the team's plans who was not authorized to discuss trade conversations publicly. And while a number of clubs are desperate to add starting pitching, some rival executives are skeptical the Giants will get their price," the report said.

The expectation of a big trade package is unsurprising given Webb's track record as one of MLB's most dependable starting pitchers. The Athletic's report also follows FanSided's Robert Murray, who revealed that the Giants are now "open to listening to offers" for him.

Webb established himself as an innings-eating starter over the previous three seasons, recording at least 204.2 innings in each season from 2023 through 2025. However, he is coming off a down year, finishing with a 4.31 ERA across 160.2 innings, marking the first time in nearly five years that he failed to reach 190 innings.

Webb remains an attractive trade target because of his contract situation, as he has two seasons remaining on his five-year, $90 million deal. His salary is relatively affordable for a starting pitcher of his caliber, and so there should be plenty of teams looking for rotation help.
