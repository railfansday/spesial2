Red Sox make shocking trade with division rival Rays

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Boston Red Sox and Tampa Bay Rays have completed an unexpected transaction involving two American League East rivals. The move comes during an offseason in which both organizations are making changes to their rosters following a 2026 MLB season that saw Tampa Bay reach the World Series.

It is unusual to see division opponents make a deal, particularly when both teams are competing for the same postseason opportunities. However, the latest transaction gives Boston another option behind the plate while Tampa Bay adds a young pitching prospect.

The Red Sox are acquiring catcher Nick Fortes from the Rays, sources told ESPN's Jeff Passan on Wednesday. Boston is sending minor-league right-handed pitcher Angel Roa to Tampa Bay in exchange for the veteran catcher.

Fortes spent the 2026 season with Tampa Bay after joining the organization in a trade with the Miami Marlins at the 2025 trade deadline. He appeared in 109 games for the Rays, recording a .258 batting average, .304 on-base percentage and .376 slugging percentage. He also finished with six home runs and 35 RBIs.

The 29-year-old catcher gives Boston another experienced option at the position. The Red Sox already made a significant move behind the plate earlier this season by acquiring Adley Rutschman, and Fortes provides additional depth at the position.

For Tampa Bay, trading Fortes represents a notable roster decision just weeks after the club's run to the World Series. The Rays are sending a player who appeared in more than 100 games during the 2026 regular season to a division rival.

Fortes' departure also comes with some context regarding Tampa Bay's catching situation. The Rays acquired Liam Hicks during the 2026 trade deadline, giving them another option behind the plate and making Fortes' role less certain heading into the offseason.

The deal also allows Tampa Bay to add Roa, a right-handed pitching prospect from Boston's minor-league system.

Although the Red Sox and Rays will continue competing against each other in the AL East, the two clubs found common ground on a trade that addresses needs for both organizations.
