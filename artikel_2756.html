The wicked Raiders’ Devon Witherspoon blitz that had Fernando Mendoza ‘in a blender’ during NFL debut

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Las Vegas Raiders took a loss against the Seattle Seahawks in Week 10, as it was the beginning of the Fernando Mendoza era. It started off well for Mendoza as he ran in for a touchdown for the Raiders' first offensive series, but there wasn't much else that they could do to make things interesting.

Mendoza definitely had his welcome-to-the-NFL moment after getting sacked by Devon Witherspoon, and Sam Warren of The Athletic explained what happened to the rookie quarterback.

"Fernando Mendoza told Mike Silver that this Devon Witherspoon sack was his 'In a Blender' moment from his first NFL start. Seattle shows overload blitz to the weak side, so Mendoza checks the protection to slide that way. But the look was Mike Macdonald preying on his inexperience, as the four perceived blitzers all dropped into coverage and Witherspoon blitzed opposite, creating a free rush that Mendoza never saw coming," Warren wrote on X, formerly Twitter.

Mendoza was also sacked six times, which didn't make his day any better. In all, he finished completing 17 of his 29 pass attempts for 143 yards.

It was probably a good thing that Mendoza saw the Seahawks in his first career start, as they are one of the best defenses in the league. He'll have some easier days against some teams, but for him, it's all about looking back at the film and figuring out how he can get better.

It'll be interesting to see how Mendoza looks as the season continues, and there's no doubt that he should get better the more reps he gets on the field.
