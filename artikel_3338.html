‘Upgraded 2’ Film Casts Tanner Zagarino, Christopher McDonald, Andrea Arcangeli & Alessandro Preziosi

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

EXCLUSIVE: Andrea Arcangeli (Trust, The Saints), Alessandro Preziosi (ChiaroScuro), Tanner Zagarino (The Summer I Turned Pretty), and Christopher McDonald (Hacks, Happy Gilmore 2) have joined the cast of Upgraded 2 from director Becca Gleason, Gulfstream Pictures, and Honor Role.

They join the previously announced returning star and producer Camila Mendes, Leslie Bibb, and Marisa Tomei. The film is currently in production in Italy.

Plot details for Upgraded 2 are under wraps. Amazon MGM Studios has the worldwide distribution rights for Upgraded 2, penned by returning screenwriters Luke Roberts and Justin Matthews. The original film follows aspiring art intern Ana (Mendes), who is invited on a last-minute work trip to London by her brilliant but intimidating super boss Claire (Tomei), meeting the handsome and wealthy William (Archie Renaux) on the plane. Set in both London and New York City, the rom-com chronicles the trials and tribulations of balancing work, romance, and following your dreams. Watch on Deadline

Producers include Gulfstream Pictures’ Mike Karz and Bill Bindley, Honor Role’s Mendes and Rachel Matthews, Luber Roklin Entertainment’s Lena Roklin, and Tempo Production’s Piers Tempest.

Zagarino will next star in The Land, a new Hulu drama from creator and showrunner Dan Fogelman, which premieres February 15. His breakout role was in the third and final season of Prime Video’s hit series, The Summer I Turned Pretty. Additional TV credits include Apple TV+’s Shrinking and Fox’s 9-1-1: Lone Star. Film credits include Netflix’s Aftermath and The Real Bros of Simi Valley: The Movie. Zagarino is represented by CAA, Canopy Media Partners, and Yorn, Levine, Barnes, Krintzman, et al.

McDonald most recently starred as Marty on HBO Max’s acclaimed comedy Hacks, for which he received Emmy nominations for Outstanding Guest Actor in a Comedy Series in both 2022 and 2026. He also reprised his Happy Gilmore role as Shooter McGavin opposite Adam Sandler in Netflix’s Happy Gilmore 2. Additional film credits include Thelma & Louise, Quiz Show, Requiem for a Dream, The Iron Giant, and Broken Flowers.

Arcangeli’s credits include Trust, directed by Danny Boyle, The First Omen, The Saints, produced by Martin Scorsese, Casi el paraíso, directed by Edgar San Juan, and the Netflix biopic Baggio: The Divine Ponytail. Most recent credits include Prima di Noi, Deep Blue – L’ora più bella, and the TV series 500 Battiti for HBO Max. He is represented by Gianni Chiffi.

Preziosi’s credits include Aeschylus’s Oresteia trilogy (Coefore, Agamemnon), King Lear, and Hamlet, and directing and starring, among other productions, in Cyrano de Bergerac and Van Gogh: The Deafening Smell of White. TV credits include a lead role in Elisa di Rivombrosa, Saint Augustine, Edda Ciano and the Communist, the Medici saga, and Sandokan.
