The best shopping holiday of the year is back. Black Friday — which has basically become the entire week of Thanksgiving — is underway at hundreds of top retailers. Just like years past, it’s the time of year to score the steepest discounts, whether you’re shopping for yourself or buying gifts.

Tech historically gets the most attention on Black Friday, and it’s still one of the best categories to save on in 2026, with tech deals at sites like Amazon, Sonos, Bose, Best Buy, and others. However, clothing might take the cake for the best deals this year. Department stores like Macy’s and Nordstrom are offering up to 70% off thousands of items, and individual brands like Levi’s, Dr. Martens, and Abercrombie & Fitch are also running some great sales. Black Friday has also become a great time to save on streaming subscriptions, with services like Disney+, HBO Max, and Paramount+ all running deals.
