HBO Max Officially Revives ‘Better Call Saul’ Star’s ‘John Wick’ Replacement

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Bob Odenkirk is among a handful of actors who have reinvented themselves as an action star. He gained national attention with Breaking Bad as Saul Goodman and went on to lead his own spin-off, Better Call Saul. The shows catapulted him to worldwide fame and various accolades, and soon he came out with Nobody, a movie nobody could imagine him leading.

Directed by Ilya Naishuller from a script by John Wick vet Derek Kolstad, Odenkirk plays a docile family man, Hutch, who slowly reveals his violent tendencies after his house gets burgled, which eventually leads him to take on a Russian crime boss. The film was a breakout success for its brilliant craft, punchy stunts, and Odenkirk’s stoic performance. Continuing his collaboration with Kolstad, he then went on to make Nobody 2, which had a similar premise but takes Hutch and his family on a vacation where he clashes with the town's corrupt officials and a ruthless crime boss. Sadly, the Timo Tjahjanto-directed dark action comedy couldn’t replicate the box office success of Nobody, putting an end to speculations about further installments, for now.

Last year, Odenkirk continued his action streak with Normal, which he co-wrote with Kolstad. We follow Odenkirk as a world-weary lawman Ulysses, who accepts a temporary posting as interim sheriff in the snowbound, seemingly idyllic town of Normal, hoping for a quiet respite. However, those plans come to a rest when two bungling drifters attempt to rob the local bank, accidentally exposing a massive underground vault filled with illicit gold bullion. Ulysses soon discovers that the entire community, from the friendly mayor (Henry Winkler), local deputies, to ordinary neighbors — all are secretly on the payroll of the Japanese Yakuza. Cornered and exposed, the heavily armed townsfolk turn on their new sheriff, forcing Ulysses into a relentless, snow-soaked fight for survival.

The feature was a modest box office success, grossing roughly $6.1 million worldwide against an estimated $17 million. But since then, it has become a streaming favorite, appearing on various streaming charts. The 78% Rotten Tomatoes-rated feature has critics consenting that the feature thrives on “Bob Odenkirk's gruff yet playful presence and director Ben Wheatley's over-the-top carnage, delivering simple, ridiculous, and consistently entertaining thrills despite its familiar premise.” Now that love for the film has catapulted it to new heights, Normal has landed on HBO Max’s global top 10 list. Other titles on the list include The Batman, The End of Oak Street, The Hunger Games, and more.
