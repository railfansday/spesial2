Is Realty Income the Easiest Way to Collect Monthly Income in 2027?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

There are several options available, but only one is best right now.

By James Brumley – Nov 18, 2026 at 2:27PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Realty Income ›

Several stocks pay monthly dividends rather than the more common quarterly dividend. These include business development company Main Street Capital (MAIN +0.79%) and real estate investment trust (or REIT) Stag Industrial (STAG +1.11%). And if you own or were to buy either one, you'll be fine.

But is there an easier way to collect monthly dividend income in the year ahead? There arguably is one, called Realty Income (O +0.37%).

Don't misunderstand. Buying a stake in Realty Income is no more difficult than stepping into any other ticker. The "ease" comes in how the company in question manages to maintain the underlying cash flow needed to support its continued dividend payments.

See, like Stag Industrial, Realty Income is also a REIT. Unlike Stag, however, Realty Income isn't heavily reliant on economically fickle tenants that experience long cycles of growth and contraction. Realty Income's specialty is brick-and-mortar retailing. Although its tenants -- which include resilient stalwarts like 7-Eleven, Dollar General, and Home Depot -- experience their own cyclical ebb and flow, once these organizations go to the time, trouble, and expense of establishing a store, they tend to stick with their investment, continuing to pay rent.

This is evident in Realty Income's numbers, too. Its occupancy rate is consistently about 99%.

This REIT's head-turning metric, however, is the dual reliability of its dividend. Not only has the company paid a monthly dividend like clockwork since 1970, but it has also raised its full-year payout for the past 31 consecutive years.

Again, there are other monthly dividend payers. You may also want to consider owning more than one monthly dividend-paying name.

The first one to consider buying in this uncertain environment, however, is the one best positioned to weather stubborn inflation and high interest rates. Thanks to high-quality tenants and a resilient dividend, that's Realty Income by a country mile.
