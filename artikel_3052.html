Anthropic’s IPO Is Reportedly 6 Times Oversubscribed and Could Start Trading Nov. 19. Should You Buy on Day 1?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The AI lab is growing quickly, but its expenses are set to grow quickly, too.

With Anthropic expected to set its final price for shares on Wednesday and start trading on Thursday, plenty of retail investors may be wondering whether they should consider buying shares, too.

At the very least, investors should expect Anthropic to list its stock at the high end of its pricing range of $70 to $78 per share, giving the company a fully diluted market cap of around $1.4 trillion. That's still well below the $2 trillion valuation that many estimated the company would target with its IPO earlier this year. But after a broad sell-off in AI stocks, Anthropic’s leadership may be being more conservative with its pricing in order to ensure a successful IPO.

That said, the fact that some big market participants are telegraphing that Anthropic stock looks attractive at up to $78 per share doesn't make it so. Investors should consider the fundamentals of the business and whether it has a competitive moat before they ask their broker for an allotment of shares in the upcoming IPO.

So, there's no clear indication that Anthropic will overcome negative market sentiment around AI stocks and concerns about financing and valuation in the near term. Your decision to buy the stock should ultimately come down to whether you think $78 per share is a fair price to pay for the company in the long run. If you had bought Meta Platforms at its $38 IPO price and held on through all the turbulence that followed, you'd be sitting on a massive gain despite the early challenges for the stock.

Anthropic is producing some phenomenal financial results. Revenue climbed more than tenfold year over year in the third quarter, reaching about $17 billion. What's more, it produced a positive adjusted operating margin in each of the last two quarters. The company is seeing broader adoption both by attracting new customers and increasing spend from existing customers. Its revenue retention rate of 900% is massive.

Anthropic is growing quickly, but its expenses are set to grow quickly as well. Its compute costs could skyrocket, and it's already committed to leases totaling over $900 billion. The capital raised in the IPO, combined with its cash and investments of $132 billion, should provide a nice cushion to absorb those planned expenses, though.

Considering the growth potential for Anthropic, a valuation of around $1.4 trillion, about 27 times 2026 sales estimates and just over 10 times 2027 sales expectations, isn't an unreasonable price. I'd be hesitant to pay significantly more than that, though.
