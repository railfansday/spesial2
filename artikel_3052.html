Yatsen (YSG) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Skincare now drives most sales as color cosmetics shrinks and margins hold steady.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 25, 2026 at 1:08PM EST

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Yatsen (YSG +1.20%) reported a total revenue decrease of 11.4% to RMB 884.5 million in the third quarter, as the company continued its strategic transition from color cosmetics to skincare. Management prioritized brand equity and long-term quality of growth over short-term scale, resulting in skincare brands accounting for 67.8% of total revenue. The company reported a net loss of RMB 129.0 million, influenced by a RMB 30.6 million impairment loss and a RMB 21.4 million noncash mark-to-market loss. For the fourth quarter, the company projected revenue growth between 0% and 10%, supported by performance during the Double 11 shopping festival.
