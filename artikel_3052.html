Petrobras (PBR) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Raised guidance and record refinery output mark a strong quarter for the oil major.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Petróleo Brasileiro S.A. - Petrobras (PBR +1.02%) reported record operational performance in the third quarter of 2026, leading management to increase guidance for annual production, capital expenditures, and operating cash flow. The company emphasized a strategy of accelerating production by expanding the capacity of existing platforms without additional capital investment, while simultaneously reducing the annual production decline rate from 10% to 3%. Management indicated that higher-than-expected Brent prices, which reached levels between $90 and $100 per barrel, provided the liquidity to pull forward drilling and platform construction milestones originally planned for later years. The company remains focused on its 2027 to 2031 strategic plan, scheduled for release on Nov. 24, 2026, which will detail future investment cycles and the integration of refining with petrochemical operations.
