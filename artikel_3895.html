Crazy Taxi Makes a Stop in India for the Final Leg of Its World Tour

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

SEGA has confirmed the fifth and final destination in Crazy Taxi: World Tour’s global adventure: India!

A new trailer shows off the mix of rural and urban locations you can expect in the full release, and teases some of the missions you’ll experience – like shooting a Bollywood movie.

That means the full roster of locations looks like this: West Coast, Germany, Japan, Brazil, and India.

I’m really surprised the UK didn’t make it in as I feel like London would be a perfect fit for this series, but maybe it’ll be DLC.

I’ve enjoyed what I’ve played of Crazy Taxi: World Tour so far, but I do have some concerns about it – namely, I just don’t think The Offspring’s All I Want is going to work while drifting through Indian open air markets.

I hope each location has its own licensed soundtrack to suit the mood.

The game’s officially due out in 2027, so hopefully we get to see a lot more of it soon.
