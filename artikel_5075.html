What Awaits XRP Holders in December If the 4-Year Cycle Works

XRP is on track for its worst November on record and has lost 48.3% in 2026 so far. The token also ended 2014, 2018 and 2022 with annual losses.

CryptoRank data shows that XRP has lost 24% in November so far, as of Friday. Before 2026, the token's worst November was in 2019, when it lost 23.3%. The month will close on Monday, Nov. 30.

According to CryptoRank, XRP's high for 2026 was $2.40, and its low was $0.87 in November. In the fourth quarter, the token has lost 36.1% so far, and in 2026, XRP has lost value in seven of 11 months so far. On Friday, XRP rose as high as $0.987 on Binance and was trading at around $0.95 later in the day, up 0.5%, according to TradingView.

Veteran trader Peter Brandt has also used the four-year cycle in his forecasts for Bitcoin. In April 2026, Brandt said that if the cycle holds, Bitcoin's next peak could come in September or October 2029 at $300,000 to $500,000.

In 2014, XRP lost 10.7% over the year, but the token gained 130.4% in November and 118.1% in December, according to CryptoRank.

In 2018, XRP lost 84.5% over the year. The token also lost 19.3% and 2.25% in November and December of that year, respectively. In 2022, the annual loss was 59.2%, and XRP lost 11.8% in November and 16.9% in December.

XRP's median December return is -4.07%, and its average December return is 63.1%, according to CryptoRank data. In December 2017, XRP gained 818.9%. The token closed December in the red in eight of the past 13 years, including December 2025, when it lost 14.8%.

XRP's largest December loss came in 2020. The token lost 66.5% that month, after the SEC sued Ripple on Dec. 22, 2020.

XRP ended 2015 with a 75.3% loss and 2019 with a 47.1% loss, according to CryptoRank. In 2023, the token gained 82.5%.

In the first quarter of 2015, XRP lost 68.2%. The token lost 12.8% in the first quarter of 2019 and gained 58.8% in the first quarter of 2023.

On Dec. 1, Ripple's escrow will unlock another 1 billion XRP.

The SingleAssetVault amendment on the XRP Ledger reached a majority on Nov. 25. According to XRPScan, it can activate as early as Dec. 9 at 7:38 p.m. UTC.

According to Ripple, lenders will deposit funds into a Single Asset Vault, and loan brokers will use that capital to issue loans and track repayments on the ledger.

On Friday, the LendingProtocolV1_1 amendment reached 29 of 35 validator votes, according to XRPScan. Its earliest activation date is Dec. 11 at 2:22 p.m. UTC, and its support has to stay above 80% until then.
