SUI price is testing the crucial $0.66 support zone as bearish momentum weakens, but the broader daily trend remains bearish.

A breakdown below $0.65 could expose the SUI price to $0.60, while reclaiming $0.70 could strengthen recovery prospects.

SUI price is approaching a critical support zone after surrendering most of its gains from the September rally, with its prolonged downtrend bringing the token back towards levels last seen during its July–August consolidation. After climbing to nearly $1.29 in late September, SUI has declined by approximately 48%, with the price hovering around $0.666. The $0.65 to $0.66 region has emerged as a crucial demand zone, potentially determining the token’s next directional move.

SUI’s daily chart highlights a sharp shift from the September rally to a prolonged decline. After trading largely between $0.66 and $0.76 during July and August, the token broke out in September and climbed to nearly $1.29 before losing momentum. Sellers gradually took control, driving the price into a pattern of lower highs and lower lows. The decline accelerated in November as SUI slipped below the $0.85 support region and reached $0.59 before rebounding to the current levels.

Besides, the MACD histogram has contracted, indicating that the gap between the two lines has narrowed considerably, heading towards a bullish crossover. The volume profile also highlights a potentially important hurdle for buyers. A substantial concentration of historical trading activity sits around $0.70 to $0.85, suggesting that this region could act as resistance during a recovery. The first hurdle lies around $0.70 to $0.72, followed by the $0.73 to $0.74 region. A sustained move above these levels would offer a stronger indication that buyers are regaining control.

While SUI’s price action remains weak, recent ecosystem developments offer a different perspective on the network’s progress. Recently, Sui announced that Hashi had gone live on mainnet, bringing Bitcoin-focused financial infrastructure to its ecosystem. The launch included more than 20 partners across custody, retail and decentralised finance. The initiative aims to make Bitcoin available for use in lending and other financial applications within Sui’s ecosystem.

Another development came when USDC transfers on Sui became available through Samsung Wallet for eligible US Galaxy users. The integration allows users to transfer USDC between Samsung Wallet accounts on Sui without a transfer fee, with the reported potential reach of approximately 82 million eligible devices in the United States.

Both developments could expand Sui’s reach across Bitcoin-based financial applications and stablecoin payments. However, their long-term impact on SUI’s token demand remains to be established. Greater network activity does not automatically translate into immediate price appreciation.

SUI’s next move largely depends on whether buyers can defend the $0.65 support region and push the token back above its immediate resistance levels. Although the MACD suggests that bearish momentum is easing, the broader daily trend remains weak. This leaves room for both a recovery and another round of selling.

Bullish scenario: If SUI holds the $0.65 to $0.66 support zone, buyers could push the price toward the $0.70 to $0.72 resistance zone. A sustained move above this region could open the way towards $0.75, where stronger selling pressure may emerge. Reclaiming this resistance would provide a more convincing indication that buyers are regaining control. A further recovery towards $0.85 could bring $1 back into focus.

Bearish scenario: However, a decisive daily close below $0.65 could weaken the recovery outlook and expose SUI to further losses. The $0.63 to $0.64 region is the next key area to watch, followed by the deeper support zone around the local lows below $0.60. A failure to reclaim the broken $0.65 level could reinforce the existing bearish structure and encourage further selling.

For now, the SUI price remains at a critical juncture. The slowdown in selling pressure offers some encouragement, but buyers have yet to establish a clear reversal. Until the token reclaims key resistance levels with stronger trading volume, the broader outlook remains cautious.
