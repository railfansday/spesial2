Rams TE Terrance Ferguson to undergo season-ending ankle surgery

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

One of the Rams’ young pass-catchers will not be able to return in 2026.

Via multiple reporters, head coach Sean McVay said on Monday that tight end Terrance Ferguson will undergo season-ending ankle surgery.

Ferguson was on track to return earlier this month before suffering a hamstring injury at practice.

But now his ankle has not responded to treatment the way he or the team anticipated.

“Didn’t necessarily feel great, but he had a really good week of practice but you could still see he was kind of getting comfortable,” McVay said of the process with Ferguson, via Adam Grosbard of the Southern California News Group. “Took some meds to be able to do that. Gets the hamstring on a Friday, and then, as he’s really been attacking both of these injuries, you get another look at it, and it was like something in his deltoid and his ankle that really is just not healing the way that you want.

“I don’t know if it was necessarily a specific setback as much as it’s just not coming along, and he wouldn’t be able to be himself and put his foot in the ground, and a lot of the things that his game is predicated on is being that twitchy athlete, some of the change of direction, moving him across the formation.”

Ferguson is now set to remain on injured reserve for the rest of the year.

A second-round pick in the 2025 draft, Ferguson was able to appear in just three games this year, catching seven passes for 63 yards with a touchdown. He caught 11 passes for 231 yards with three TDs in 14 games as a rookie last year.
