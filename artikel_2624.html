Matthew Golden held in check by Rams

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Green Bay Packers WR Matthew Golden caught one of his five targets for 39 yards against the Los Angeles Rams in Week 12.

Why it Matters: Golden came into the game ranked inside the top 10 in both receptions and receiving yards, but he was unable to find a rhythm against the Rams. Golden should rebound nicely against the Saints in Week 13, and if Christian Watson (concussion) is unable to play, Golden could produce a huge state line.
