Rams open to free-agent ‘upgrade’ with just four receivers left on roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

When Sean McVay sends three wide receivers onto the field against the Packers on Wednesday night, he can look down the sideline and find exactly one more.

That's where the Rams stand before any practice squad elevations.

A veteran who can give Matthew Stafford another dependable target would help. So would a fifth receiver who can spell one of the first four.

Los Angeles Rams receiver Puka Nacua (12) celebrates a touchdown with head coach Sean McVay in Landover, Md., Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026. Getty Images

Asked Monday whether the Rams would pursue a veteran free agent, McVay said, "We've talked about all those types of things," according to the L.A. Times.

"I would never rule out opportunities to upgrade," McVay said, adding that his current receivers did "a great job" in their last game.

There's room to agree with McVay on both counts.

Xavier Smith caught his first NFL touchdown in the win over Arizona. Konata Mumpfield had already shown what he could do in an expanded role, catching four passes for 93 yards and a go-ahead touchdown in Denver while Puka Nacua was sidelined.

Both have earned work. The trouble is how few players are behind them.

Los Angeles Rams head coach Sean McVay leaves the field after defeating the Las Vegas Raiders at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas on Sunday, Oct. 25, 2026. Getty Images

Monday's depth chart lists rookie C.J. Daniels on one side and Tutu Atwell on the other, with Mumpfield in the slot and Smith behind him. Neither outside spot has a backup listed.

The Rams have already had to reach outside the organization to get to four. Smith was on San Francisco's practice squad before Los Angeles brought him back when Jordan Whittington went on injured reserve.

At the trade deadline, the Rams had reason to think the numbers would improve. They passed on trading for a receiver in part because they expected Nacua and tight end Terrance Ferguson back this season.

San Francisco 49ers wide receivers Deebo Samuel, left, and Brandon Aiyuk take part in an NFL football practice in Santa Clara, Calif., Tuesday, June 6, 2023. AP

Nacua has since undergone knee surgery, Ferguson needs season-ending ankle surgery and Davante Adams is still recovering from hamstring surgery. Ferguson's absence also costs McVay a receiving option when he wants to give one of his wideouts a breather and put another tight end on the field.

Spotrac lists Brandin Cooks, Hunter Renfrow and Tyreek Hill among the available veteran receivers. Brandon Aiyuk could join them if he clears waivers after the 49ers waived him Monday, ending a lengthy standoff.

Availability on paper only tells the Rams so much. Hill's agent said earlier this month that the receiver was still awaiting medical clearance, and McVay didn't single out a target Monday.

A practice squad call-up can get the Rams through Wednesday. With seven games left and two expected reinforcements lost for the year, there's a job here for someone who can stick beyond one game day.
