Jahmyr Gibbs up to three touchdowns, Lions up 35-24

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Lions running back Jahmyr Gibbs scored a pair of short touchdowns on Thursday and he decided for something a little more extravagant for his third trip to the end zone.

Gibbs broke free for a 70-yard touchdown with 8:20 left to play in the game and the Lions now have a 35-24 lead over the Bears. It is Gibbs's second three-touchdown game of the season and he is up to 18 total touchdowns for the season.

The Bears could have gone to a 12-point lead, but Cairo Santos missed an extended extra point after Christian Mahogany was flagged for taunting on the touchdown run.

Gibbs's score came after the Lions forced the first punt of the second half by either team and the Bears will now have to scramble in order to find a way to end the day on top of the NFC North.

If they can't, their inability to contain Gibbs will be a big reason for the loss. He has 20 carries for 171 yards and four catches for 41 yards as the Lions try for their seventh straight win.
