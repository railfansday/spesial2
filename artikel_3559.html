Jack Nicholson's Son Didn't Watch Any of His Dad's Movies Until He Was 18

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

It’s difficult for Ray Nicholson to name his favorite film featuring dad Jack Nicholson — in part, because he didn’t watch a single one until he was an adult.

“It’s tough. I’m a sucker. It’s like every PTA or Kubrick movie you watch, you’re like, ‘That’s my favorite,’” Ray, 34, said during an interview with Men’s Health published on Monday, November 23. “I didn’t watch my dad’s movies until I was 18. I love Cuckoo’s Nest. I love The Shining. I always come back to The Last Detail. For some reason, I really f***ing love that movie. I think it’s because he loves it so much and he’s like, ‘It’s underappreciated.’ So I’m like, ‘But I love it, Dad!’ I love watching his movies. It’s a cool thing, and I want my kid to watch my movies someday.”

Ray added, “I just want to make people happy and smile. And break their hearts a little bit.”

Jack is the father of six children from five relationships. The Academy-Award winning actor shares Ray and Lorraine, 36, with ex Rebecca Broussard, as well as daughter Jennifer, 63, with Sandra Knight, son Caleb with Susan Anspach, daughter Honey with Winnie Hollman and daughter Tessa with Jennine Gourin. From Ray’s perspective, his father is his “hero.”

“I think he’s magic,” Ray said on Monday. “I love him more than anything. When I was first doing a Meisner exercise and you’re knocking, coming through the door, I was comparing myself to, Is that worthy of three Oscars and a 50-year career? So much of my practice has just been learning to be present. And so now I have less moments when I’m in my car driving, crying, thinking about I’m getting an Oscar, and my dad… And that’s a good thing. I’ve just gotten way more present about it.”

While reflecting on his future, Ray shared that he’s “open to the idea that this might not be right for me.”

“The world might take me in another direction,” he explained. “I was really closed off to that before. Because I thought that if I didn’t do something, I was unworthy of my life. I think a lot of that is just therapy. And I’m finding the real joy in it.”

He continued, “I lost football and I was really lost in my life, and I didn’t know what to do. Acting has slowly become the thing that saves my life. It’s not only what gives me a purpose, but it’s the tool I meet myself with. I don’t know who the hell I am. I don’t really remember anything from my life. And then somehow, when I read a script, I’m like, ‘Oh, that is you.’”

Ray admitted that when he first started, he “didn’t really have those kind of goals.”

“I don’t want to say because it’s bulls***, but that’s the industry,” he said. “That’s beautiful, and I love the industry, but I’m more focused on how I approach every day.”

He continued, “That was the key thing, and that’s all personal life stuff. But the key is being of service, and I believe that ultimately, I’m in the service industry. When I think about my dad, he makes me smile every time I see him on-screen. I want some kid somewhere to smile every time he sees me.”
