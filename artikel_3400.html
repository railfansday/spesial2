Red Dead Redemption 3 Perfect Protagonist Unites Gamers

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

It may not have been officially announced just yet, but now that GTA 6 is finally out in the wild, we're back to dreaming about our perfect protagonist for Red Dead Redemption 3. With the Red Dead franchise being one of the most successful in gaming history, it's hardly a surprise that Rockstar is under pressure to deliver the next installment. Although with GTA 6's Online mode still to come, and the 13 years between GTA 5 and GTA 6, we may be in for a wait.

Regardless, with Rockstar giving us iconic protagonists like Jason, Lucia, Michael, John Marston and Arthur Morgan over the years, whoever takes over in Red Dead Redemption 3 has some pretty big shoes to fill. Luckily, fans have one beloved character in mind, and I have to say I back it all the way.

Over the years, fans have been pretty split when it comes to who should lead Red Dead Redemption 3 when it eventually comes. Some reckon it should be a pre-established character that we already know and love, while others think it should be handed over to a brand-new arrival with no ties to the original Van der Linde gang. You have to remember fan-favorite Arthur Morgan was a completely original creation that we never met in Red Dead Redemption. It helped that he was dead, but still.

However, over on r/reddeadredemption, it seems as though fans are finally coming together for once with one major candidate we have met before: Sadie Adler.

First introduced in Red Dead Redemption 2, Sadie went on to become one of the most beloved characters in the franchise as she became a fully-fledged member of the Van der Linde gang after being rescued by them at the start of the game. Despite featuring in a few missions alongside Arthur, she mostly took a backseat until playing a larger role alongside John Marston in the epilogue. Now, though, fans are hoping that Red Dead Redemption 3 will put her in the leading role, with one gamer proclaiming to Rockstar that their "my life is yours" if they make Sadie the star. Not everyone is convinced, though.

"I love Sadie, but I struggle to think of how this would work, considering most of her 'redemption'/significant aspects of her story already take place in RDR2," one user shared. "By the end of RDR2, Arthur identifies himself and Sadie as "more ghosts than people"- and I think there is little left to do with her storyline after RDR2." Another agreed, adding, "How would that fit with redemption theme? Sadie herself said that kind of quiet life is behind her. It's like you didn't pay attention to her story. Sadie is a broken, angry woman who has already killed all the people she has a grudge against. What would be the point?"

Whether you agree with Sadie Adler being the next protagonist or not is up to you, but it is also worth remembering that Red Dead Redemption 3 is still a long way away. At the time of writing, Rockstar will be putting the finishing touches to its biggest project yet, with Grand Theft Auto VI finally expected to launch this November. Following this, there will be heaps of support needed, not to mention content for its Grand Theft Auto Online component. Maybe then, it will be able to turn its attention to the Red Dead franchise. However, when this will be is anybody's guess. For now, all we can do is hope that Red Dead Redemption 3 will arrive in our lifetime.
