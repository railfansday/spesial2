Prime Video keeps finding ways to continue its spectacularly successful detective thriller series, Bosch, based on the novels by Michael Connelly and starring Titus Welliver in the lead role. After the original show ended following its seventh season, a spin-off that effectively served as a sequel was released in 2022. Titled Bosch: Legacy, the new show ended its run after three seasons, paving the way for a proper spin-off titled Ballard. Now, a new prequel series is in production, with Cameron Monaghan playing the titular character in his younger days as an LAPD officer. The prequel is set during a particularly controversial period in the LAPD's history, and will serve as a neat companion piece to a largely forgotten movie that's currently streaming for free in the United States.

The movie was headlined by Woody Harrelson and released in 2011, just a few years before Bosch's debut on Prime Video. The film follows Harrelson's character, a corrupt LAPD officer who gets entangled in a widely publicized scandal during the late 1990s. Bosch: Start of Watch follows a young Harry Bosch as he navigates police corruption in 1991 Los Angeles. The show will presumably address the scandal in later seasons, provided it continues as long as the original.
