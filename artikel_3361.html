‘NCIS’ Star Teases Potential Ziva Return After Spin-Off Cancellation

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

NCIS has been welcoming back a ton of familiar faces from across the franchise throughout this fall. The two biggest, however, have been Mark Harmon jumping back in front of the camera for a third season of NCIS: Origins that puts an older Gibbs back in the line of fire, and Michael Weatherly rejoining his old colleagues at the Washington Navy Yard as Tony DiNozzo. Following the end of NCIS: Tony & Ziva on Paramount+, the latter is set up for a longer return to the flagship series after moving back into the area to establish a new office for his security company and instead landing an entirely new job as the new director of NCIS in place of the late Leon Vance (Rocky Carroll). Because of that, it feels inevitable that Ziva David (Cote de Pablo) will eventually make an appearance too.

As the series explains, Tony's longtime love interest is just a stone's throw away, with their daughter Tali attending boarding school in Boston. However, showrunner Steven D. Binder previously ruled out a return in Season 24, meaning an on-screen reunion between the couple after their spin-off's cancelation isn't imminent. Still, the groundwork is set for her to join the team on a mission like old times at a moment's notice. One person who has long been waiting for that opportunity is Katrina Law, who feels it's more a matter of when than if she steps back into the field.

Law recently spoke to Cinemablend about how Season 24 was unfolding so far and was questioned about the possibility of a guest appearance by de Pablo. Jessica Knight has a particular connection to Ziva, as it's revealed the two are directly in contact to keep tabs on Tony and his diet while he's at work. For Law, that fully opens the door to a future Knight and Ziva mission that'll allow the two women to combine their skills both as highly trained special agents and in driving Tony crazy. "I fully expect Ziva to come onto our show and Knight and Ziva team up and just make Weatherly extremely uncomfortable, but then go off and, like, have a massive fight scene together to take down some bad guy."

Weatherly was initially brought aboard for a season-long arc for Season 24, but the fact that he's the team's new man in the chair seems to indicate his return as Tony isn't just a brief visit. He's working well with the current team, from Knight to his old pal McGee (Sean Murray), and the former agent even got to reunite with his old boss during a poignant crossover with NCIS: Origins. Binder, for his part, is loving having an NCIS legend back in the fold, though he previously shared that they're taking things "one season at a time" as far as the story and Weatherly go. "Certainly, it’s been really great having him in that chair, and I don’t see any reason why it should slow, but I don’t know that we’re looking quite that far yet." It seems the hope would be for Tony to stick around a while in a recurring role, making the chances of Ziva returning to D.C. in Season 25 or beyond all the more likely.
