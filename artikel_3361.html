10 Greatest Netflix Shows of 2026, Ranked

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

2026 has been a year of growth and expansion for Netflix. With its intriguing plots, fantastic production, and well-designed characters that leave an impact, Netflix has rolled out several great shows this year. While there has not been a monumental rise in critical quality, the streamer’s lineup of shows has been much stronger and more positive than its original movies.

On the one hand, the streaming service has released multiple fresh new originals like The Boroughs to cure the Stranger Things hangover. At the same time, the year has also seen the returns of acclaimed shows like Beef and Black Doves, which have thrilled fans even more than last time. So, before we close the book on this year, here’s our ranked selection of the top 10 best Netflix shows of 2026.

Based on the American literary classic by Laura Ingalls Wilder, Little House on the Prairie follows the Ingalls family, parents Charles (Luke Bracey) and Caroline (Crosby Fitzgerald), and their daughters, Laura (Alice Halsey) and Mary (Skywalker Hughes), as they move from Wisconsin to a newly developing settlement in Kansas to start a new chapter for the family. Developed by Rebecca Sonnenshine, the Netflix original series marks the third television adaptation of the beloved book series.

Heavily compared to the NBC classic, Little House on the Prairie has had mixed reviews since it premiered in the summer of 2026. The show earned praise for its grounded historical context and sweeping visuals and period-accurate aesthetic, but has also been criticized for lacking the gritty realities of frontier life that the older adaptation captured so well.

Among the first major releases of 2026, Netflix’s Regency-era epic romance drama returned with an all-new fourth season, based on the novel An Offer from a Gentleman, the third in Julia Quinn’s titular novel series. Bridgerton Season 4 follows a classic Cinderella-inspired romance centering on Benedict Bridgerton (Luke Thompson) and Sophie Baek (Yerin Ha).

After a divisive third season, Bridgerton Season 4 was a welcome return for the show, which has become a global streaming sensation since its premiere in 2020. The new chapter brings back the high-stakes, slow-burn romance narrative that has defined Bridgerton as a sensation of modern romance television. Critics and reviewers have praised Bridgerton Season 4 for its emotionally grounded storytelling, compelling romance plot, and better visual aesthetic compared to its previous seasons.

Two years after its first season premiere, The Gentlemen, Guy Ritchie’s black comedy crime drama reboot of his 2019 film returns for an action-packed, explosive second season, as Duke Edward “Eddie” Horniman (Theo James) deepens his grip on this underground cannabis business. As Eddie transforms into a ruthless power player in tandem with Susie Glass (Kaya Scodelario) and her father, Bobby (Ray Winstone), he finds himself facing new rivals and the consequences of unlimited ambition.

Though the new season arrived after a two-year gap, The Gentlemen Season 2 makes it worth the wait for fans and offers everything you expect from a Guy Ritchie caper. In fact, the second season of the crime thriller is darker, sharper, sleeker, and more high-stakes than the first. The second season of The Gentlemen trades the lighthearted, absurd tone and style of Season 1 for a punchier, snappier second chapter in Eddie’s evolution, while maintaining the original visual flair and cinematic style.

Four Netflix characters, four completely different ways of showing up for somebody. This is the one who fits how you actually do friendship, and what having them in your life would be like.

You do not do friendship at half volume, and neither does he. What you want is somebody who says yes to the plan before you have finished describing the plan.

You are the friend who makes things happen, and so is she. Together you would be mildly exhausting to everyone around you and completely delighted with yourselves.

You have no patience for pleasant lies, and she has no ability to tell them. What looks like coldness from the outside is the most reliable friendship on this list.

You look after whoever is having the worst day. So does he. It is the least strategic way to move through the world and the best possible quality in a friend.

Lee Sung Jin’s multi-award-winning black comedy anthology returned this year with a second season featuring an all-new cast in a new story of class satire and the unpredictable human condition. Beef Season 2 explores the escalating feud between couples Ashley (Cailee Spaeny) and Austin (Charles Melton), employees at an elite country club, and their boss and his wife, Josh (Oscar Isaac) and Lindsey (Carey Mulligan). After Austin and Ashley see the latter in a questionable confrontation, it sets off a wild story of blackmail, sabotage, and coercion, upending everyone’s lives.

After the raw and unabashed exploration of rage and existential crisis that Beef introduced in its first season, the second season expands to a more philosophical and spiritual exploration of human nature. While the new story does not really match up to the phenomenal success and critical acclaim of the first season, Beef Season 2 anchors itself in the crackling performances of its lead cast and sharp class satire.

Created by Haley Z. Boston, Something Very Bad is Going to Happen is a horror-thriller miniseries that tells the story of a newly engaged couple, Rachel (Camilla Morrone) and Nicky (Adam di Marco), who are all set to get married. In the week leading up to the wedding, Rachel begins to experience several sinister, supernatural events that turn her wedding stress into a dark nightmare, making her seriously question her decision.

One of the most highly anticipated horror series of 2026, Something Very Bad is Going to Happen puts an eerie psychological and supernatural spin on pre-wedding jitters. The dark gothic horror drama has been praised for its atmospheric tone and surreal treatment, which takes inspiration from horror classics like Rosemary’s Baby.

An adaptation of Jo Nesbø’s novels featuring the titular central character, Detective Hole is a gritty Nordic crime drama that follows the homicide detective, a brilliant but troubled man who is in search of a horrific serial killer. Starring Tobias Santelmann as Detective Hole, the series follows his relentless and often dangerous attempts to catch the killer while also trying to bring his corrupt fellow officer, Tom (Joel Kinnaman), to justice.

Directed by Oystein Karlsen and Anna Zackrisson, Detective Hole is a grim but stylish crime thriller with unexpected plot twists that can keep you hooked from start to finish. The Netflix original miniseries joins the long list of Nordic noir thrillers that Netflix has been dishing out over the last few years. Intricate and intense, Jo Nesbø’s Detective Hole is a deeply layered serial killer mystery any genre fan would love.

Debora Cahn’s political thriller series returns for a fourth season that somehow manages to improve upon the first three excellent installments. Keri Russell and Rufus Sewell lead the show as Kate and Hal Wyler, a couple who attempts to juggle a marriage that’s often just as complicated as diplomacy. Things are more complex than ever this time around, as President Grace Penn (Allison Janney) begins making riskier and riskier moves, and a new Prime Minister in the form of Bethan Vaughn (Jodie Whittaker) throws alliances into question.

The Diplomat Season 4 has earned rave reviews from critics, currently holding an impressive 97% on Rotten Tomatoes. Janney’s shocking but well-deserved Emmy win this year seems like it could be the first of many more accolades in the show’s future. Season 4 both tackled the fallout from last season’s jaw-dropping cliffhanger and introduced a new twist in the finale’s last moments; needless to say, Season 5 can’t come soon enough.

Starring Alfred Molina, Geena Davis, Bill Pullman, Alfre Woodard, Clarke Peters, and Denis O’Hare, The Boroughs is a sci-fi adventure involving a group of residents at a retirement community out in the deserts of New Mexico. When a new resident named Sam (Molina) witnesses the shocking death of a fellow retiree and begins investigating, his search leads him to an unearthly, uncanny conspiracy that threatens the lives of the residents in the seemingly perfect community.

Executive produced by the Duffer Brothers and developed by Jeffrey Addis and Will Matthews, The Boroughs is a great follow-up to the Duffers’ five-season sci-fi epic. But the show surpasses its perception of “Stranger Things with older adults” and stands on its own merit as a heartfelt exploration of later-life challenges told as a chilling sci-fi mystery. The series has been widely praised for its standout cast, poignant storytelling, and excellent production values.

Zoe Kazan's East of Eden is undeniably one of the best shows of the year. The adaptation of John Steinbeck's epic 1952 novel, the show centers around the Trasks and the Hamiltons, two families whose lives intersect in California's Salinas Valley. The novel takes heavy inspiration from the story of Cain and Abel, tackling complex themes of good versus evil.

Florence Pugh’s performance as Cathy Ames is widely considered one of the best in her career, which is saying something considering she has everything from Midsommar to Little Women on her resume. The rest of the cast, which includes Christopher Abbott, Mike Faist, Hoon Lee, and Ciarán Hinds, is impressive in their own right. The series earned 6.5 million views in its first four days alone and only increased in popularity over time, solidifying itself as one of the year’s most enduring hits.

Joe Barton’s genre-bending spy thriller returned this year after a two-year interval for a brand-new season with Keira Knightley and Ben Whishaw reprising their roles as spies from the titular agency, Helen and Sam, respectively. In Black Doves Season 2, the duo faces new threats after discovering a conspiracy involving their handler, Reed (Sarah Lancashire). At the same time, Helen finds herself on increasingly uncertain ground after her husband becomes the Prime Minister. The new season also adds Ambika Mod, Neve Campbell, Babou Ceesay, and Samuel Barnett to the cast.

Black Doves Season 2 has proved itself to be darker, sharper, and faster than the first season, with higher stakes and a higher comedic quotient. While the new season successfully maintains the show’s original themes of love, friendship, and espionage, it arguably lacks the moodiness and charm of classic British spy thrillers, which Season 1 achieved excellently. Despite that, Black Doves Season 2 has earned rave reviews, surpassing the acclaim of the previous season, with critics praising the more grounded plotlines and Knightley and Whishaw’s chemistry.
