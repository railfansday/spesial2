Neosho tops Branson in high-scoring affair, advance to district finals

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

NEOSHO, MO – The Neosho Wildcats extended it's historic season on Friday night, besting the Branson Pirates in district semi-final action, 34-31.

The Neosho offense started hot, as the Wildcats used a long scamper from quarterback Blayne Patterson to collect an early 7-0 lead on the first drive of the game.

Branson would be quick to respond with a score of it's own, fullback Noah McCright bowled over several Wildcats in a goal line scenario to knot things up at seven in the first.

Neosho collected another pair of touchdowns later in the first half, one coming on the ground from Patterson, his second of the night, and another coming through the air with Patterson connecting with Mason Spiva in the first quarter's final seconds.

Branson would knock through a field goal to close out the first half with a 21-10 deficit, but the second half was worth the price of admission. Branson collected two quick scores out of the break to establish a lead of it's own, but Neosho executed when it counted to keep their season alive.

The diesel-powered offense of Neosho now faces Republic in the district finals next week.
