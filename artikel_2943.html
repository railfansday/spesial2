“Nobody Said Anything About Saints”: Fans Side With Shedeur Sanders as Carl Granderson Accuses Browns QB of ‘Chirping’ Before Big Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Shedeur Sanders looks set to start for the Cleveland Browns against the New Orleans Saints on Sunday after Deshaun Watson suffered a concussion. However, the build-up has been dominated by reactions to the quarterback's cryptic comments in a press conference.

Saints EDGE Carl Granderson took aim at Sanders before the game, suggesting the team knew about Sanders' words and was motivated to play its best on the field.

"They've been chirping a little bit. We see what the quarterback said. […] It's going to be a good game for sure," he told reporters (via ESPN Cleveland).

Shedeur wasn't laughing at the Saints defense btw. Cause what QB in the league is getting asked if they're studying the opposing teams defense?

When asked whether he was studying the New Orleans defense, Shedeur Sanders gave an answer that seemed to rub Saints players, including Granderson, the wrong way.

"Yeah, I've seen a lot," Sanders said to reporters. "When I smile, just know that means something. I'm maturing. I'm maturing. Like, I could have given a decent answer, but now I'm just going to smile whenever it's just, like, some questions."

Sanders had a mixed rookie season, posting a 3-4 record. He threw for 1400 yards, seven touchdowns and 10 interceptions. However, he has played just five snaps this season, serving as a backup to Deshaun Watson.
