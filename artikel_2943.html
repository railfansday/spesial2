Plexus: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Capacity gains without heavy capital spending let Plexus expand margins while chasing new market share in semiconductors.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 25, 2026 at 9:27PM EST

On its fourth-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 29, 2026, Plexus (PLXS +0.37%) announced full-year revenue of approximately $5 billion, representing a 23% year-over-year increase. The company provides electronic manufacturing services (EMS), assembling complex printed circuit boards and systems for industrial, medical, and defense customers. Higher operational efficiency and new customer wins are fueling faster revenue and profit expansion. Three things management said stand out.

Plexus is raising its non-GAAP (adjusted) operating margin goal, which measures profit from operations excluding one-time items, to a range between 6.5% and 7%. Equipment efficiency improvements exceeding 10% over two years let the business expand capacity without massive capital spending.

New contract awards in aerospace and defense reached $490 million for the fiscal year, expanding the long-term backlog. Sector momentum is accelerating as defense programs ramp up and commercial aviation demand gradually recovers.

Plexus is expanding its footprint in semi-cap, short for semiconductor capital equipment used in chip fabrication. The business historically gained market share during cyclical downturns, but recent gains demonstrate customer wins during an industry expansion.

End-market demand across healthcare, industrial, and defense sectors is accelerating. Automated supply chains and strong execution provide operational advantages while management monitors material availability and pricing discipline.

Disciplined capital deployment and factory-floor automation position the business to generate free cash flow exceeding $100 million for the year.
