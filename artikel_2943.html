Axe Compute (AGPU -5.19%) completed its transition to a pure-play AI infrastructure provider during the third quarter after selling its legacy oncology business. Management reported the company's first profitable quarter on an operating and net income basis, driven by sequential revenue growth and gains on digital assets. The company stated that its business model utilizes customer prepayments to fund the acquisition of dedicated GPU clusters, which avoids the need for corporate debt to finance growth. Management indicated that it has secured a multiyear contract book expected to generate over $700 million in annualized revenue once global AI factories reach full deployment.
