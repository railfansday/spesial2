Opera's 2027 Outlook: AI-Driven Browser Ecosystem Maintains Rapid Growth

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

This under-the-radar internet software stock boasts a Superscore of 83 from our Hidden Gems Primary database, part of The Motley Fool's Moneyball Database system. Here's why.

By Robert Izquierdo – Updated Nov 27, 2026 at 9:20PM EST

When you click to open a browser, you expect a portal to the web. In the case of Opera Limited (OPRA +3.17%), that portal has evolved into a specialized engine optimized for high-value user niches, such as the gaming-focused Opera GX browser. The company, based in Norway, has successfully pivoted its platform toward agentic AI integrations and higher-ARPU (average revenue per user) Western markets. As of Nov. 19, 2026, the stock traded at $13.62, representing a 5% gain over the past year despite a broader 21% decline in the last six months.

Our proprietary Hidden Gems scoring system assigns Opera an overall Superscore of 83 out of 100, placing it in the Strong category. The Superscore is an AI-powered score that evaluates a company's overall strength by combining financial performance, product market position, technological capabilities, leadership quality, and relative valuation. It represents the unification of all our scores into a single score for public companies, with five rating bands: Exceptional (90-100), Strong (75-89), Above Average (60-74), Average (40-59), and Cautious (0-39).

This score places Opera in the Top ~6% of all companies we score. This analysis offers a starting point for your due diligence, pairing the company's clear strengths with the constraints that prevent a higher score so you can weigh both sides of the coin.

I believe Opera stock is poised to deliver a solid return in the coming years. Several factors drive this. One is that its trailing P/E ratio of about 9 is at a low point for the past year, suggesting shares sport an attractive valuation.

Adding to this is Opera's ability to grow user engagement. The company crossed 100 million monthly active users on PCs in October, representing a 16% year-over-year increase, while its Opera One mobile offering for Apple's iOS rose 20%. As these results reveal, the browser giant continues to benefit even amid changing user habits due to the artificial intelligence boom.

It's also seeing success in its push into advertising. Ad sales grew 22% year over year to $116.8 million in the third quarter of 2026, and now represents 64% of total revenue. This helped drive total sales to a 20% year-over-year increase to $182.8 million, contributing to a whopping 48% year-over-year increase in net income to $27.6 million.

The many successes Opera is experiencing, combined with a compelling valuation and bottom-line growth that it puts toward rewarding shareholders with dividend payments and share repurchases, indicates the company's stock is poised to deliver in the coming years for investors.
