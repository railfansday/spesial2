49ers’ Kyle Shanahan shouts out Mexican fans after win in Mexico City

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

49ers' Kyle Shanahan shouts out Mexican fans after win in Mexico City appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The San Francisco 49ers are alone in the penthouse of the NFC after a 35-10 domination of the Minnesota Vikings that included a feat not seen this season against Brian Flores' defense. Brock Purdy picked apart blitzing Vikings defensive schemes as he had another big day with three touchdown passes.

After the game, the 49ers' fanbase got a big shout-out from head coach Kyle Shanahan, as shared by David Lombardi of The San Francisco Standard.

"That Mexican fan base is unbelievable. They show up in San Francisco. They show in LA. And they definitely show up when we go to Mexico City," Shanahan said.

The 49ers were in a celebratory and jovial mood as they embraced local customs in a video shared by the team's official X account. They were inspired to dominate from the get-go, and wound up with one of the biggest wins of the NFL season so far in one of the season's biggest games.

Purdy had just five incompletions on the night as he passed for three touchdowns, two to tight end George Kittle and one to receiver Mike Evans, who fought through Achilles tendonitis to get back on the field and thrive against a fierce Vikings defensive unit. Evans made a confident Brock Purdy claim after the game that added to MVP rumblings surrounding the former Iowa State star.

The 49ers are looking like the NFC's team to beat after easily dispatching of Kevin O'Connell's Vikings, but there is still much work to do. November 29's home game against the Seahawks is a big one, and another chance for Shanahan's team to impress its fans, and NFL fans in general, heading toward the playoffs.
