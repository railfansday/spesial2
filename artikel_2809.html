Pittsburgh Steelers vs. Denver Broncos, Black Friday game thread

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Well, here we go, Rumblers! Fans of the Buffalo Bills are still waiting to come back down from their emotional high following a crazy win at home against the Kansas City Chiefs. It's been a Thanksgiving to remember with a trio of turkey day games that weren't decided until the final play.

Now there's today's game, which might be more interesting due to the drama unfolding in Pennsylvania. The 6-4 Pittsburgh Steelers are set to host the 8-2 Denver Broncos in a matchup with pretty big playoff stakes. However, all anyone can talk about is Aaron Rodgers snitching on head coach Mike McCarthy. However you may feel about the entire situation, it's indisputable that Rodgers appears to have violated an unspoken NFL code.

Now, Rodgers is left to wallow in the darkness while waiting for the ayahuasca to take effect and numb away the pain — self-inflicted pain, mind you. Has Rodgers permanently tarnished his certain and future Pro Football Hall of Fame inclusion? They say time heals all wounds, but Rodgers is prone to endlessly tearing away beneficial scabs.

For many in Bills Mafia, this soap opera is a bit personal now. Instead of showing fans the Goo Goo Dolls' halftime performance at Highmark Stadium, NBC decided to conduct an interview between Mike Tirico and Mike Tomlin focused on Rodgers and the Steelers. This, despite every other game from Wednesday night until late-day Thursday showing their game's halftime performance.

It was an unusual public relations misstep by the network, and one that came during one of the nation's biggest holiday viewing windows. Yet you know that's why NBC ran with the Rodgers-Steelers drama over a feel-good moment for Buffalo and Western New York.

Savvy internet users will have found the Goo Goo Dolls' halftime show by now, but here's a high-def video shared by One Bills Drive for those of you who wanted to see it:

Back to today's game. Guess who Bills fans should root on to victory: Pittsburgh. Yes, it's true. A Broncos loss would drop them to 8-3, thus creating a bit of separation between Buffalo's 10-1 first place AFC record and another potential playoff foe.

During his halftime interview, Tomlin called the Steelers a dumpster fire more than once. It's true, and most should have seen it coming with Rodgers in the picture. Now, Pittsburgh will try to rally a once-promising season back on track toward a playoff birth. Instead of the over-the-hill Rodgers, quarterback Mason Rudolph has been tasked with righting the ship.

This one could go one of two ways: The recent drama could serve to completely unravel the team, and usher in a move to can McCarthy before season's end. Or, McCarthy and the team could prove Rodgers and the court of public opinion wrong by using this nightmare as a chance to do big things.

I wouldn't expect Broncos head coach Sean Payton to oblige the ask. Denver is in a fight to win the AFC West, and victory today sends them to first place over the Chiefs. How do you see this one playing out?

With that, here's your open thread for Steelers vs. Broncos on Black Friday. As always, be kind to one another — and in all things… Go Bills!
