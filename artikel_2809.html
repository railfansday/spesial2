Red Sox land backup catcher for Adley Rutschman in trade with Rays

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Red Sox land backup catcher for Adley Rutschman in trade with Rays originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Before today, the Boston Red Sox had two catchers on their active roster heading into next season: Adley Rutschman and Connor Wong.

Wong has spent the previous six seasons with the Red Sox, but his playing time as Boston's backup catcher could be in jeopardy. (Note: Wong was designated for assignment on Wednesday)

According to ESPN's Jeff Passan, the Red Sox acquired backstop Nick Fortes from the Tampa Bay Rays in an offseason trade.

"Trade News: The Boston Red Sox are acquiring catcher Nick Fortes in a deal with the Tampa Bay Rays, sources tell ESPN," Passan wrote on X.

In return, the Red Sox are sending minor league right-hander Angel Roa, a 17-year-old who spent this past season in the Dominican Summer League, to the Rays. Roa's involvement in the deal was reported by MassLive's Chris Cotillo.

"Red Sox-Rays trade is a 1-for-1 swap sending Nick Fortes to Boston for Angel Roa," Cotillo wrote on X. "Boston will make a 40-man move to fit Fortes on."

Fortes, 30, has spent parts of seven seasons in the Majors with the Miami Marlins (2021-25) and Rays (2025-26). In 2026, the right-handed hitter batted .258/.304/.376 with six home runs, 35 RBIs, and a .680 OPS.

Fortes has typically posted below-average defensive metrics with a slightly above-average pop time behind the plate. His best season was in 2024, when he recorded eight blocks above average with the Marlins.

As for the Rays, they land a young controllable arm who has a long way to go to reach the Majors. They can fill the backup catcher spot internally with No. 3 prospect Caden Bodine, or they can sign a free agent/make a trade.
