'There will come a day when you won't make a comeback!' - Jose Mourinho reveals tactical risks and why Kylian Mbappe and Carlos Espi cannot start together

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Jose Mourinho praised match-winner Carlos Espi's attitude while warning Real Madrid cannot keep relying on late comebacks after edging Celta Vigo 2-1. The Portuguese boss also explained why his current wingers prevent him from starting Espi alongside Kylian Mbappe in a traditional 4-4-2 system.

Jose Mourinho hailed three "golden points" after Real Madrid overturned Pablo Duran's sixth-minute opener to beat Celta Vigo 2-1 and claim their ninth straight victory. Following Kylian Mbappe's missed first-half penalty, Jude Bellingham equalised on 66 minutes before substitute Carlos Espi netted an 85th-minute winner.

Mourinho revealed his squad was physically drained after several players endured 12-hour flights and disrupted routines during international duty, noting that rivals Arsenal, Manchester City, Chelsea and Bayern Munich all failed to win on the same day. "I've seen people physically struggling, I've seen people who weren't at their peak, and then I've seen people who surpassed themselves and found strength in mentality, in the group, in the will to win," Mourinho said. "As I joked on Realmadrid TV, it's not an isotonic drink at half-time that gives them energy. It's the group."

Mourinho heaped praise on Espi's penalty-box instincts and humility, crediting the forward's former Portuguese coach at Levante for his development while explaining why the Spaniard is currently deployed as a specialist impact substitute rather than partnering Mbappe from the start.

"He's a guy with great quality in the box, a guy who's good in the air, a guy who plays well with his back to goal," Mourinho said. "He's the kind of player who doesn't get carried away by stories about needing to play more, about how Kylian should be on the bench so he can play. Playing with two pure attackers means your wingers have to defend really well inside. With the characteristics of our wingers, we're not set up to play in a pure 4-4-2."

Having used all his substitutions to chase victory—including introducing Marc Cucurella to tuck inside and cover Iago Aspas—Mourinho admitted Real Madrid finished the match with an unbalanced shape featuring four pure attackers alongside Bellingham and Arda Guler in midfield.

"There will come a day when you won't make a comeback. Obviously, that worries me," Mourinho warned. "After the 2-1, Celta obviously tried to equalise. We didn't have any more substitutions to make. Today, I would've created a sixth substitution to take out Bellingham or Guler and bring in Tchouameni, but that option doesn't exist. The way we finished the match, if we had scored 15 minutes before the end, I don't think we would have won."

Mourinho also urged his players to embrace the demanding expectations of the Bernabeu crowd, calling it a defining trait of wearing the white shirt, while taking a moment to send a warm message to the grieving family of Jesus de Miguel in attendance.

Looking ahead to Tuesday's Champions League clash, Mourinho ended any debate over his team selection early: "I said yesterday that Vini Jr. would either come off the bench or start. So you don't ask me the question on Monday and to give you less to talk about over the next couple of days, I'll be the bad guy and end the story now. Starter on Tuesday."
