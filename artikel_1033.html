Parker still winless as West Ham held by Preston

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

West Ham United vs Preston North End. Sky Bet Championship.

Report and free match highlights from the Sky Bet Championship match between West Ham United and Preston North End at the London Stadium on Saturday; Konstantinos Mavropanos heads home in second half to earn a point for the Hammers but Scott Parker stays winless.

Scott Parker's stuttering start as West Ham manager continued in his first match at the London Stadium as bottom club Preston battled their way to a 1-1 draw.

Parker is yet to taste victory in three games since he returned to the club he once graced as a player.

After Konstantinos Mavropanos headed home on the hour to cancel out Liam Lindsay's early goal for the visitors, it seemed that West Ham's attacking power would force a victory to keep pace in the Championship promotion race.

Instead, in spite of 27 efforts on goal, they could not break Preston's resistance and are now winless in their past five games.

If the second half was one-sided, the first period was anything but. The only surprise was that it contained just a single goal.

Joel Piroe ought to have done better than shoot into the legs of Preston keeper Lee Nicholls after being sent clear by Arne Engels before Lindsay fired the visitors ahead in the 18th minute.

An inswinging Alfie Devine corner was met by Piroe whose flicked header looped backwards and inadvertently struck his own crossbar before dropping down for Lindsay to lash it over the line from two yards out.

West Ham responded with an Edson Alvarez shot into the side netting following a corner of their own.

The Preston goal had an extraordinary escape in the 27th minute when Joel Veltman set up Jarrod Bowen whose shot cannoned back off the left-hand post and into the path of Veltman who instantly struck the other upright.

If that was unfortunate, the home side had a lucky escape minutes later when Thierry Small opened up space for himself in the West Ham area but then lifted his shot wastefully over the bar.

A frantic game became even wilder as half-time approached.

Divine Mukasa dragged a shot wide for West Ham before their 20-year-old keeper Finlay Herrick justified Parker's decision to hand him a Championship debut.

Herrick threw himself to his left to parry away a Callum Lang shot, before springing up to save Devine's follow-up.

The beginning of the second half was just as hectic with Edvin Austbo jinking inside and curling his shot against the same part of the crossbar that featured in Preston's goal.

Leaving just one man up, the visitors dropped deeper and deeper, challenging West Ham to force their way through for the equaliser.

Bowen neatly set up Alvarez only for the Mexican to drag a weak shot wide. Eventually the Preston defence gave way with Mavropanos rising highest to head home an Engels cross.

Having drawn themselves level, West Ham continued to drive forward only for their play to become more desperate at times, mirroring the anxiety among their fans.

Their bombardment of the Preston goal almost brought its reward deep into injury-time only for Nicholls to brilliantly tip over a Tomas Soucek header.

"Everything that I was thinking about last night as I was looking at a ceiling, everything that I was feeling about today, what took place was never this scenario. It was a different vision.

"I got here really early today and I had a vision of ending the day with three points and a brilliant performance.

"I got the brilliant performance from the players, but not the three points, so in that sense, I am devastated. The thing that went missing today was the result.

"But with a rational head, there were a lot of good things, and if we played that game a hundred times, we would win every other time.

"Did we do a lot of good things today? One million per cent, so I have to keep the players on a consistent pathway."

"I don't think anybody would have looked at two games against Southampton and West Ham and expected us to get four points.

"We knew the challenge would be tough coming here, but we pressed them high in the first half, took the lead and could have scored more.

"The pressure that West Ham put on us told in the end with the equaliser, but I thought the attitude of the players was magnificent.

"The biggest test today was after their equaliser. If we have conceded at times this season we have gone a little bit to pot.

"So that was a massive challenge. We'll now see if we've learnt anything from the last couple of weeks about how we have handled ourselves, when negative events like conceding goals happen."
