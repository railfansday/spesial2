See Week 11 Texoma high school football final scores

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Texas high school football regular season wrapped up Friday, Nov. 6, with district championships and playoff positioning decided across Texoma.

Windthorst senior quarterback Brock Belcher moved into third place on Texas high school football's all-time passing touchdowns list as he led the Trojans past Munday, while Wichita Falls Legacy secured its first playoff berth in program history with a victory over Memorial.

Elsewhere, Graham, Jacksboro, Bowie and Henrietta were among the area teams closing out the regular season with wins.
