PennantPark (PFLT +0.48%) reported core net investment income that supported its base dividend and allowed for the declaration of a supplemental distribution. Management stated that the company is prioritizing the expansion of its second senior secured loan joint venture toward a $1 billion target while maintaining a portfolio heavily weighted toward first lien floating rate debt. The company reported a significant concentration in the government services and defense sectors, citing durable federal funding priorities and resilient cash flow profiles as key investment drivers. Management indicated that the core middle market remains a source of attractive risk-adjusted opportunities despite uneven M&A activity and ongoing macroeconomic uncertainty.
