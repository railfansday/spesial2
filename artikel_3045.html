Legacy Ed (LGCY) Q1 2027 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Record enrollment rose, but an unplanned legal settlement pressured profits.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Legacy Education Inc. (LGCY -5.23%) reported a 3.1% increase in revenue for the first quarter of fiscal 2027, reaching a record student population of 3,684. Management noted that profitability was impacted by $1 million in planned investments in faculty and facilities, alongside an unprojected legal settlement of $687,500. The company continued its expansion strategy by preparing to open its first campus outside California in Houston, Texas, and launching three new associate degree programs at its Contra Costa location.

We classify our starts in two categories: Title IV programs and cash pay programs. Our Title IV programs generate $24,000 in annual revenue per start on average.

Our cash pay programs run less than 12 weeks and generate an average of $3,000 per start over the full program. Cash pay programs is nonmaterial. It represented 1.3% of first quarter revenue, but yet accounted for over half of the year-over-year decline in total starts. Our total starts declined by 9.9% in the first quarter. while the Title IV starts only declined 5.6%. This is important because had the 105 MRI and cardiac sonography starts that shifted into the second quarter occurred in the first quarter, Title IV starts would have increased approximately 5.5% year-over-year.

We saw the similar difference in the fourth quarter in fiscal 2026 when the total starts declined by 3.6%, while the Title IV starts increased 5.8%. This quarter, we had 1,006 new student starts. Of those, 897 were in Title IV programs. We continue to see the growth in the other parts of the portfolio. Ultrasound technician, sterile processing and surgical technology generated 338 new starts, nearly double the 172 a year ago. And in MRI, we had 99 more active students than a year ago despite fewer first quarter cohorts. Those are two measures we watch closely as the programs grow and we execute.

In vocational nursing, some health care employers have been looking to develop more nursing talent in-house, which is changing how they recruit from schools. We've been proactively responding to those changes. Two years ago, we updated the curriculum to better prepare students for the NCLEX-PN, to strengthen the student engagement and improve student retention, so more students stay in the program. In November 2025, I shared with you that we also introduced a new entrance exam to help us identify applicants with the academic foundation to succeed in a rigorous program. By taking this action over the past three quarters, our starts were 264 below our original expectations.

In the 12 months ended September 30, 144 of the 168 first-time NCLEX-PN candidates passed for a rate of 85.7% across Legacy. In July through September quarter, the first-time pass rate was 88.5%. Lancaster, our largest vocational nursing program, achieved 92.3% first-time pass rate over the same 12-month period.
