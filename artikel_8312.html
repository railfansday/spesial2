"Vanguard funds double distributions in late December?"

Great news, if true!!
It's not an extra distribution; it is the distribution that would normally be paid in January but that is paid in December. (And even if it is paid in January 2027, it would still be taxable in 2026; this is an IRS rule to prevent a fund from delaying tax by distributing 2026 income in 2027.)
That’s normal for bond funds, not stock funds with quarterly distributions like VTI.
