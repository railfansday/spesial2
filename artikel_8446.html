Still, five years is a long time.
$250k in assets moved between a few places every 90 days can net like 1.4% annualized even if not maximizing the number of times that can be done with those assets in a year. For instance, $250k ACATS to WF for $2,500 bonus in 90 days then ACATS to Citi or Merrill for another $1,000 bonus each for 90 day holds ... or even some one offs like wire $250k cash to HSBC for $2,000 bonus in 90 days.
