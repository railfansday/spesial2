US stocks tumbled on Thursday despite Anthropic’s blockbuster market debut, as the AI trade came under fresh pressure. The S&P 500 fell 2.02%, the Dow lost 2.19% and the Nasdaq dropped 2.25% after swinging from an early 2% gain. The Dow closed below 50,000 for the first time since June, and more than 90% of S&P 500 stocks ended lower. Anthropic, which raised $81.3 billion at $83 a share, opened at $111.50 but closed at $97.17, still 17% above its offer price. AMD sank 7.72% after a short seller alleged links to a blacklisted Chinese firm, a claim AMD denied, while Nvidia fell 3.3% as new US chip rules targeted China. Cybersecurity stocks bucked the trend, led by a 6.42% jump in Palo Alto Networks after strong results. Inflation fears also weighed, with the Philly Fed prices index at its highest since April and traders pricing about a 95% chance of a December rate hike. The 10-year yield held near 5.05%, gold fell 2.7% and Bitcoin slipped to about $61,600.
