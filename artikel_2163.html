David Storch breaks Sheffield Wednesday silence as Dave Challinor arrives

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Sheffield Wednesday are now under new management for the first time since Arise Capital Partners' takeover at Hillsborough back in May - and popular Owls owner David Storch has broken his silence on the freshly-sealed appointment of Dave Challinor.

Storch was left with his most significant decision at the Sheffield Wednesday helm to date when a parting of ways with Henrik Pedersen, who had been in charge since the beginning of the previous campaign, was sought following consecutive defeats to Notts County and Doncaster Rovers last month.

With the promotion-aspiring Owls having been positioned in eighth place amid growing calls for Pedersen's sacking from supporters, Storch was ultimately left with little choice after previously retaining strong backing in the Dane's services.

It was, however, a relatively straight-forward recruitment process to both find and thrash out an agreement with the Owls' next manager, which was perhaps always destined to be Challinor following previous reports citing the ex-Stockport County figure's strong interest in succeeding Pedersen if provided the opportunity.

Now, after leaving League One promotion rivals Stockport upon last season's play-off final defeat to Bolton Wanderers, Challinor will be making his return to management in the blue-and-white quarters of the Steel City, and Storch has made his thoughts clear on his first appointment as Wednesday owner.

Following the 51-year-old's official unveiling at Hillsborough, Storch has explained exactly why Challinor was appointed ahead of other candidates and voiced an evident delight to have landed his man.

Challinor, of course, enjoyed a fruitful working relationship under current Owls sporting director Simon Wilson while at Stockport, whom he guided from the National League to two successive League One play-off finishes in under five years.

Storch described how Wednesday's new manager has the "unanimous support" of the club's hierarchy, and trusts him to make further strides on what he believes is an "ambitious journey" ahead for the third-tier promotion hopefuls.

Speaking to the Owls' official media channels, Storch stated: "Dave's appointment has the unanimous support of our board, following a thorough recruitment process expertly led by Simon Wilson, and reflects our shared commitment to the long-term future of Sheffield Wednesday.

"We are still at the beginning of an ambitious journey. Significant progress has been made in rebuilding the foundations of the club, but we recognise there is much more work ahead."

"Our ambitions remain clear, but our progress cannot be defined by the outcome of one moment or season alone.

"We must establish a clear footballing identity, consistent standards and a culture capable of delivering sustained success.

"That journey will require patience, resilience and collective commitment. Progress will not always be straightforward, but we believe Dave has the leadership, character and determination to help us achieve our ambitions.

"We are delighted to welcome Dave to Sheffield Wednesday and look forward to building the next chapter together.”

Since completing his takeover of Wednesday following the club's well-documented turmoil under ex-chairman Dejphon Chansiri, Storch has not been one to mince his words or provide sheer lip-service.

Instead, his thoughts have always been reflected in his actions and that's exactly why his faith in Challinor's services is highlighted by a significant contract package, which will see Pedersen's replacement tied down at Hillsborough on terms running until the summer of 2030.

That should, of course, give Challinor plenty of time to realise Storch's lofty long-term ambitions for Wednesday, as they will be expected to successfully seal promotion under a boss who has already achieved that feat on seven occasions in his career.

Challinor knows exactly what it takes to compete in the top echelons of League One, even if he ultimately fell short of taking Stockport over the finish line - although his strong work in taking them through the divisions at breakneck speed to third-tier promotion contenders allows for plenty of credit in the bank.

Now, it's time for Challinor to go one better and take Wednesday to the places where he couldn't quite reach with Stockport.

Challinor, who was in attendance for Wednesday's 1-1 draw against Blackpool at Hillsborough on Sunday afternoon, will lead the Owls for the first time when they travel to League Two outfit Oldham Athletic in the FA Cup first-round proper this weekend.

He will have to wait to make his league bow, though, with a Hillsborough debut beckoning in the form of next Wednesday's EFL Trophy group stage clash with Accrington Stanley.

Indeed, that is scheduled to come on November 14 through a potential baptism-of-fire at home to fellow promotion-chasers Plymouth Argyle, which could yet be postponed owing to impending international call-ups.

After that, Wednesday will have to grit their teeth in preparation for successive away trips to South Yorkshire rivals Barnsley and then Stevenage before returning to S6 to face Reading at the end of November.
