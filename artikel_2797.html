Former Steelers cornerback finds new home with UFL team

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Former Pittsburgh Steelers cornerback Beanie Bishop is heading to the UFL. The West Virginia product is signing with the St. Louis Battlehawks, the team announced on Thursday.

Bishop signed with the Steelers as an undrafted free agent in 2024. He appeared in all 17 games as a rookie, picking off four passes and recovering a fumble. He didn't make the 53-man roster in 2025, instead being assigned to the practice squad. He was released in November, 2025 before being signed by the New Orleans Saints to their practice squad.

He joined the Chicago Bears this past August, picking off two passes in the preseason. He was suspended for the first three games of the season for violating the NFL's substance abuse policy, and was released by the Bears ahead of Week 1. He currently remains a free agent.
