Brian Schottenheimer goes full NSFW on Cowboys’ defense vs. 49ers

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Brian Schottenheimer didn't hold back on the defensive issues the Dallas Cowboys suffered all evening in their 46-40 loss to the San Francisco 49ers on Sunday.

Schottenheimer is halfway through the second season of his head coaching career with the Cowboys. He has delivered mixed results as playoff aspirations continue to be a challenge for Dallas to the many lapses the defense has sustained in many games.

Dallas failed to contain Christian McCaffrey and Brock Purdy, giving up multiple touchdowns to them. Schottenheimer reflected on the loss after the game, calling out the lack of tackling as it played a big role in how things went down.

"There's a lot of things we got to figure out because I haven't been around a situation like this where the defense was that poor in the second half," Schottenheimer said, via ESPN.

"It's something we've got to study. I mean, we got to look at the film, but very disappointed, very frustrated. I thought the first half we did some really good things. I think that's a very good offense, right? But what got us in the second half was the tackling, or the lack of tackling. You've got to get guys on the ground and, yeah, they're good players, but at the end of the day, we tackled like s**t in the second half."

Brian Schottenheimer and the Cowboys have a lot of issues to figure out on defense. The offense will deliver with the talent they have, but they need the other side of the ball to come through in crucial moments.

Dak Prescott was active with his throwing throughout the game. He completed 30 out of 51 passes for 343 yards and five touchdowns and one interception.

Javonte Williams led the run game with 16 carries for 63 yards. George Pickens commanded receivers with 11 catches for 145 yards and three touchdowns. Meanwhile, CeeDee Lamb caught seven passes for 72 yards and a touchdown.

The Cowboys will look to bounce back in their next matchup, remaining at home. They host the Tennessee Titans in Week 11 as kickoff commences on Nov. 22 at 1 p.m. ET.
