Trieu: In-state targets highlight Michigan State visitor list for Oregon

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Michigan State has Oregon coming in on Friday, Nov. 20. With many high school players done for season or playing on Saturday, there will be more recruiting visitors at this contest than at the previous Friday Big Ten games.

One of the headliners is Birmingham Brother Rice junior wide receiver Tyler Trusel, who has an offer from the Spartans as well as many other schools around the country. Likewise, River Rouge receiver Jerrimiah Cross has a Spartan offer, but Michigan, Virginia Tech and Arizona State also are making strong pushes for him.

One of the state's top sophomores, Saline quarterback Case Campbell, will visit. Campbell is a 6-foot-2, 200-pound prospect who had an excellent season, throwing 33 touchdowns to just one interception in his first season as a starter.

Some other standout Class of 2029 visitors include Plymouth (Minnesota) Robbinsdale Armstrong wide receiver Kyson Wanjara, a long 6-foot-2 pass catcher who had over 1,000 yards receiving this season. Grand Blanc defensive back Noah Cooper is a Michigan State legacy (his father was Spartan defensive back William Cooper). Cooper has played two seasons of varsity already, earned some early offers as well as looks from P4 schools like MSU.

Brighton safety Logan Moore had a very good sophomore season. His 6-foot-3 frame and versatility are drawing college coaches his way. Teammate Anthony Storey, a 6-foot-3 H-back who plays three sports at Brighton, also is planning to visit.

Elijah Armstead-Jackson, a hybrid pass-rusher from Cleveland Lutheran East, was a nice find by the staff. The 6-foot-3, 205-pound junior had 56 tackles (28 for loss) and 14.5 sacks this season. Another Ohio pass-rusher coming is KIPP Columbus junior Anthony Jemison, a 6-foot-5, 205-pound two-way standout who most schools like on defense where he had 62 tackles (18 for loss) and 7.5 sacks this season.

Cardae Brown is a long 6-foot-3 defensive back from Ontario (Ohio) who has early offers and recruiting attention. He had 10 touchdowns on offense and five interceptions on defense this season.

Other in-state prospects visiting include Haslett junior lineman Garrett Goerger, Grand Haven offensive lineman Matthew Fuller, and St. Louis defensive lineman Merrick Brock.

Cincinnati Withrow defensive backs Mister Achoe and D'Maurie Riley will be in East Lansing. Achoe already has a handful of offers including Louisville. Teammate Joshua Erkins, a linebacker with four Division I offers, also plans to come.

A senior Michigan State is still in contact with is Grand Rapids Catholic Central receiver Jace Cummings, a Michigan State legacy who may get a look as a preferred walk-on.
