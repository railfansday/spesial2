Chiefs-Falcons: Game Thread with Live Updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

In Week 10 of the NFL season, the (6-2) Kansas City Chiefs hit the road to take on the (3-6) Atlanta Falcons as they head into the second half of the schedule. The game kicks off at noon Arrowhead time at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta and will be broadcast on CBS.

The Falcons have had incredible highs this year, including a 29-20 home win over the San Francisco 49ers a few weeks ago; it's still the 49ers' only loss of the season. Yet, Atlanta has a losing record and is coming off two losses with a combined score of 50-17.

Even after trading starting safety Jessie Bates to the Jacksonville Jaguars and starting center Ryan Neuzil to the Baltimore Ravens, the Falcons still have a lot to play for; they're firmly in the race for the NFC South title, while Kansas City is looking to stay alive in its own divisional race without starting running back Kenneth Walker (groin); he was ruled out on Saturday.

Stay on top of every Chiefs update and debate every snap with other members of Chiefs Kingdom. Game-day discussion is smoother in the SB Nation app.

The Chiefs won the opening coin toss and elected to defer; Atlanta started with the ball.

The Chiefs' defense forced a three-and-out right away after Atlanta dropped back on three consecutive plays to start the game. Two incompletions preceded a pass to tight end Kyle Pitts that cornerback Nohl Williams stopped short of the sticks. Newly acquired Chiefs receiver Josh Downs went back for the punt return and let the ball bounce into the end zone for a touchback.

Kansas City took the ball from the 20-yard line and started the Emmett Johnson show with a 10-yard gash. He was the highlight of the opening drive, gaining 48 total yards on the 10-play, 80-yard drive and capping it off with a third-down catch-and-run touchdown. The Chiefs lead 7-0 early.

The Chiefs went back on defense and kept Atlanta down, forcing another three-and-out after defensive end George Karlaftis wrapped up Falcons quarterback Michael Penix on second down for his fifth sack of the season. On the ensuing punt, Downs muffed it deep, recovered, but was tackled at the four-yard line.

Second-year tight end Jake Briningstool took a short pass off play action for 20 yards to open the drive and give the offense breathing room. However, the Chiefs ran into a wall from there and punted from their own 32-yard line a few snaps later.

Kansas City's defense continued to be all over Atlanta's run game, stuffing running back Bijan Robinson with authority and setting up a third-and-17 by allowing little room to work. The Falcons would punt for the third time.

Patrick Mahomes opened the drive with a 21-yard completion to Josh Downs, moving Kansas City out from its own 13. The Chiefs gained just two more yards on an Emmett Johnson catch before Mahomes' third-down pass to Johnson fell incomplete. A false start backed up the punt unit five yards, but Matt Araiza still booted it 60 yards to the Atlanta 9. Olamide Zaccheaus returned it 20 yards, giving the Falcons possession at their own 29.
