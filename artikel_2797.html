Florida has five players out, one game-time decision for Kentucky game

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The final SEC availability report ahead of Florida football's Week 11 matchup with the Kentucky Wildcats in Lexington has five players still left out for the game, while one was upgraded to a game-time decision, and another is now cleared to play.

Kofi Asare, a JACK who has played in nine games, logging seven total tackles, 2.5 tackles for loss, 1.5 sacks, a forced fumble and a quarterback hit, is now a game-time decision.

Defensive lineman DK Kalu, who has also played in nine games including a pair of starts, logging seven total tackles, 2.5 tackles for loss, 1.5 sacks, a forced fumble and a quarterback hit, is now cleared to play.

Wide receiver Vernell Brown III, safety Bryce Thornton, inside linebacker Jaden Robinson, tight end Evan Chieca and cornerback Dijon Johnson are the five Gators who will not play on Saturday.

Brown will miss his sixth straight game with a grade-1 PCL strain he suffered against Ole Miss in Week 4 that was re-aggravated ahead of Week 9's Georgia game. The second-year receiver recorded 17 receptions for 271 yards and two touchdowns over four appearances before being sidelined with his injury.

Thornton's injury was not disclosed after playing in 55 of 77 defensive snaps in last week's loss to Oklahoma. The fourth-year defensive back has amassed 28 tackles and three defended passes in eight games this season.

Robinson will miss his third consecutive game, also due to an undisclosed injury. The fourth-year ILB has 26 tackles with four for loss, plus 1.5 sacks and two defended passes over seven appearances for Florida this fall.

Johnson, who was previously listed as doubtful earlier in the week, has appeared in six games this season, recording 10 total tackles (three solo, seven assisted) along with an interception and three passes defended.

Kentucky still has four players who are listed as out for Saturday: wide receiver Nic Anderson, inside linebacker Antwan Smith, and defensive backs Jaden Smith and Grant Grayton.
