The Chiefs have bounced back in a major way this season, starting 8-2 and putting themselves back in Super Bowl contention, but the real gauntlet is just about to begin.

Starting on Thanksgiving, Kansas City is facing the Bills, Rams and Bengals on the road in three consecutive weeks. While a Week 15 matchup with the Patriots is much less daunting than it once looked, the Chiefs then get the NFC-leading 49ers.

If Taylor Swift is going to attend some more regular season games this year, there might be none better than Thursday night's blockbuster showdown against the Bills.

Here's what you need to know about Swift's whereabouts for the Chiefs' Thanksgiving game.

Swift was at the Chiefs' game against the Cardinals in Kansas City in Week 11, but she has much less frequently attended road games, making her chances of traveling to Buffalo for Kelce's Thanksgiving game against the Bills uncertain.

If there were ever a road game to attend in the regular season, this might be the one. The Bills and Chiefs have one of the best modern-day rivalries in the NFL, and Thursday's game could go a long way toward determining the No. 1 seed in the AFC.

If the Chiefs win, it could make attending games much easier for Swift when the playoffs arrive. Kansas City needs to keep pace in the AFC West to have home-field advantage of any kind in the playoffs, and a loss would improve the Broncos' chances of keeping the division crown.

It's still unclear whether Swift will attend Thursday's game, but she has been seen at games this season with everyone from Kelce's family to actor Tom Cruise.

Given this game is in Buffalo, it could be a more subdued entourage if Swift does indeed attend.
