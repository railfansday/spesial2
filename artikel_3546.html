DWTS' Connor Wood Was 'Nervous' About Finale Odds After Lower Scores

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Connor Wood is still “pinching” himself after sailing through to the Dancing With the Stars season 35 finale.

“I was waiting for them to finish the sentence,” Wood, 31, joked in an exclusive interview with Us after the semifinals on Tuesday, November 17, sharing his reaction after hearing his and Rylee Arnold‘s names called first. “I go, ‘What do we do? You want us to dance again? You want us to do our dance again, you sick freaks?'”

Wood noted the semifinals were “the hardest week” of the competition so far because each couple was tasked with performing two dances.

“Obviously, you see the competition. … This is the only time the competition feels competitive is when we are in the ballroom competing. Otherwise, this is our family,” he continued. “And it was just so cool to do this with everyone. It’s so cool to be going to the finale with the group that we’re going to the finale with.”

Wood and Arnold, 21, were consistent with their scores on Tuesday night, earning 8s across the board for each of their dances. The pair performed a jive to Elvis Presley‘s “Blue Suede Shoes” and a quickstep to Fall Out Boy’s “Dance, Dance,” both of which scored 24 out of 30 points — the lowest of the night.

Following their second routine, Carrie Ann Inaba got out of her seat to give Wood a hug, saying she could tell he was “a little nervous.” She also urged him not to lose his confidence.

“I mean, the nerves were higher just because we were scored lower, obviously, tonight,” he told Us. “I felt pretty good about the first dance. And when I saw other people’s scores, I was like, ‘Now I gotta really bring it for the second dance,’ and I felt really good about the second dance. And so going in, yeah, I was a little nervous to make sure that one was that much better, and that’s not the headspace that we operate really well in. I kind of left our zone for a second, and I wish I wouldn’t have. But hindsight’s 50-50. Now we are going to the finale.”

Wood joked, “And bygones, you know what they say about them? Bye, you’re gone!”

Despite the nerves, Arnold gushed that she was “so proud” of how far Wood has come this season, calling him “the best partner ever.”

Wood insisted Arnold “can’t get rid of me” once the competition ends, even joking that he wants to make a comeback next season.

“Getting in touch with ABC as we speak,” he quipped. “The curveball next year: Connor Wood is back, and he’s still learning.”

Wood and Arnold will compete in the supersized season finale Tuesday, November 24, alongside Maura Higgins and Mark Ballas, Tatyana Ali and Jan Ravnik, Amber Glenn and Pasha Pashkov and Ezra Frech and Daniella Karagach.

Dancing With the Stars airs on ABC and Disney+ Tuesdays at 8 p.m. ET.
