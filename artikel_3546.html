Corpse Bride's Live-Action Remake Chances Get Major New Tim Burton Update After 21 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Tim Burton has addressed whether Corpse Bride could finally be resurrected over 21 years after the original movie.

While Tim Burton's name has been synonymous with Disney's The Nightmare Before Christmas film since its 1993 release, he was credited for the story and characters and served as an executive producer, not the director. It wasn't until 2005 that Burton directed his first full-feature stop-motion animated movie with Corpse Bride, a fantasy musical about a shy groom who accidentally marries a deceased woman while practicing his vows. The critically acclaimed film has remained an annual Halloween season staple, and fan-driven demand for a live-action adaptation has stayed just as strong.

In an interview with Vanity Fair about Tim Burton's new The Nightmare Before Christmas collection with fashion company Loewe, the filmmaker was asked about whether a live-action Corpse Bride movie will ever come to fruition. While Burton didn't give a clear affirmative, the magazine noted that he also didn't say "no," with Burton instead stating that he's "gone through too much to go, 'Okay,'" as other projects have "mutated" and changed throughout the years:

Tim Burton: "I’ve got thoughts swirling around and all sorts of things going on, but I’ve been through too much to go, 'Okay.' I keep it open, and I’m just finishing something, so I’m in an open spot now, and I’m really excited to look for what the next thing is."

Burton didn't give a concrete answer about whether a live-action Corpse Bride movie is one of the potential projects "swirling around," but his response is more hopeful than his stance on a possible The Nightmare Before Christmas sequel, prequel, or remake. In previous interviews, Burton has given a more solid "no" to questions about bringing back the 1993 animated film for another screen adaptation, noting how "important" and personal those characters and the story are to him.

Throughout over a four-decade career, legendary director Tim Burton has only remade one of his previous films. In 2012, he collaborated with Disney to release a feature-length stop-motion animated remake of his 1984 live-action short film Frankenweenie. Additionally, Burton has only ever directed two sequels to his projects, with the first being Batman Returns (1992) and the latest being Warner Bros.' hit fantasy Beetlejuice Beetlejuice (2024). As such, though a live-action Corpse Bride remake wouldn't be unprecedented, it would be a rare move for Burton.

Currently, the only Tim Burton project in post-production awaiting release is Wednesday season 3, with the super-hit Netflix series having wrapping filming on its latest episodes at the end of September. Netflix has yet to officially announce a release date for the Addams Family spinoff's third outing, but the new season is expected to premiere in 2027. Meanwhile, Burton has a few other films reportedly in the works, including a post-World War II movie with Kazuo Ishiguro and a remake of the 1958 sci-fi cult classic Attack of the 50-Foot Woman.

The original Corpse Bride movie's voice cast included several frequent Tim Burton collaborators, being led by Johnny Depp as Victor Van Dort and Helena Bonham Carter as the eponymous character, Emily. The vocal ensemble also featured Emily Watson, Richard E. Grant, Albert Finney, Joanna Lumley, Tracey Ullman, Paul Whitehouse, Christopher Lee, Michael Gough, Deep Roy, and Danny Elfman. However, a potential live-action Corpse Bride adaptation would be expected to feature a brand-new lineup of actors.
