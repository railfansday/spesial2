Brielle Biermann Wants to Break Out From Behind Mom Kim Zolciak’s Shadow

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Brielle Biermann got candid about the pros and cons of growing up in the public eye with her Bravo star mom, Kim Zolciak.

“I definitely want to be my own person. I think that was kind of the theme here today with everybody,” she exclusively told Us on set of Celebrity Weakest Link’s “Next Generation” episode, which also included Joseph Baena, Sailor Brinkley-Cook, Cori Broadus, Corinne Foxx, Prince Jackson, Hailie Jade Scott and Melissa Rivers. “I am Brielle Biermann first and Kim’s daughter second.”

Biermann, who recently tied the knot with Billy Seidl in Florida, is determined to pave her own way.

“I want people to remember that and know that I have my own identity,” she noted. “So I’m trying to be more conscientious about those types of shows or opportunities and decisions going forward so that way I can really branch out and be my own person.”

Before competing for her charity of choice — World Central Kitchen — on the Fox game show, Biermann and her family were featured on The Real Housewives of Atlanta from 2008 to 2012. Zolciak, 48, subsequently received her own spinoff show titled Don’t Be Tardy for the Wedding, which focused on her nuptials with Kroy Biermann. The series was later renamed to Don’t Be Tardy and continued to air until 2020.

Having her life featured on reality TV taught Brielle some important lessons.

“I think resilience and being a survivor. You can go through and grow through anything. So nothing ever knocks me down,” she told Us. “I’ve been to hell and back many times — as people can read. And here I am.”

Celebrity Weakest Link allowed Brielle to show a new side of herself — but it was still a challenge.

“Oh, I am 100 percent competitive. But I also have a lot of empathy,” she noted. “So it’s hard because I feel really bad sending somebody home.”

The experience also made Brielle want to do more reality shows.

“Special Forces is a little scary, but sure I’ll do it,” she said. “Gia [Giudice]’s experience is a little crazy. I saw a couple of her episodes. I’m down for anything, honestly. This year is all about embracing awkwardness and saying yes. I want to do something scary every single day. So here is my scary [thing] and it was actually really amazing.”

Celebrity Weakest Link airs on Fox Mondays at 9 p.m. ET.
