Sami Sheen Jokes About 'Screaming Fights' With Family During the Holidays

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Denise Richards and Charlie Sheen’s daughter Sami Sheen is keeping it real this holiday season.

“Wait, you’re telling me people actually are excited for Thanksgiving,” Sami, 22, asked via TikTok on Tuesday, November 24. “And you guys don’t get into screaming fights with your family, and you dress nice and have a great time, and you eat really good food not on paper plates? What the f***?”

Sami later captioned the post, “I hate the holidays.”

One day later, the influencer poked fun at all of the attention her TikTok video was receiving on the Internet.

“Every single time I talk about my family issues or anything on my account — because it is my account and I feel like it’s a safe space, sometimes it’s not,” she said in a separate video on Wednesday, November 25. “It’s a safe space for you guys. For me, my own account is not safe for me because every little thing I say gets picked up … and then I get a phone call saying, ‘Why the f*** would you say that? Why would you f***ing post that?’”

Sami continued, “I’m going to give this 30 minutes until I get a phone call about it.”

Fans of Sami and her family know that dynamics between relatives can get a little wild.

During the inaugural season of Denise Richards & Her Wild Things in 2025, cameras rolled as Sami and her younger sister, Lola Sheen, often butted heads.

While chatting with Us earlier this month, however, Denise, 55, shared a promising update on her daughters’ relationship.

“My older girls are doing great. They’re sisters,” she exclusively told Us on November 14, while celebrating Dorit Kemsley’s new beverage partnership with Maison d’Or. “There are times they’ll have a little, you know, little fit here and there, but then they’ll go shopping or out to lunch after. My sister and I are a year and a half apart, so I very much understand.” (In addition to Sami and Lola, Denise is also mom to daughter Eloise, whom she adopted in 2011.)

While Lola, 21, and Sami may have stepped away from reality TV, Denise is expected to make cameos in the upcoming season of The Real Housewives of Beverly Hills.

“I’m really, in real life outside the show, I am good friends with many of them, and it makes sense that I would be invited to certain things,” she told Us. “When I’m able to go, I go. And you know, I love a lot of the women, and some of them not so much, but most of them I do.”
