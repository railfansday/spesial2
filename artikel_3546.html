Khloe Kardashian Reveals If She Would Ever Consider a Lavender Marriage

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

After going through several messy breakups, Khloé Kardashian revealed whether she would explore a lavender marriage.

During the Thursday, November 26, episode of The Kardashians, content creator Colt Paulsen asked Kardashian, 42, at an event whether she would enter a marriage with a gay man, to which she replied, “I’m down.”

Kardashian elaborated about the possibility of being in a platonic partnership in a confessional.

“There’s times [where] yes [I do consider it] and then other times, I’m like … to take care of one more person,” she explained. “I don’t know. It depends on what that gay man can do for me.”

Kardashian continued: “Will you make me look pretty every day? Sold. That’s all it takes.”

The reality star has previously made headlines for her personal life when she dated former NBA star Lamar Odom for one month before getting engaged in 2009. The pair got married later that year and Kardashian filed for divorce in December 2013 for the first time. Kardashian later postponed her court motion after Odom — who struggled with substance abuse during part of their marriage — was left comatose following an overdose at a legal brothel in Nevada.

“Lamar was and is definitely someone that I felt was the love of my life,” Kardashian said on The Kardashians in a February 2025 episode. “I got married when I was 24 and although him and I got married 30 days to the day we met, I loved him with all of my heart and soul. I took my vows very seriously and I was married for about seven years. But there were bigger things that were not fixable in that marriage. So the last time I saw him was in 2016.”

After Odom, 47, recovered, Kardashian went through with their divorce. She later moved on with Tristan Thompson and the duo welcomed daughter True in 2018. Days before her arrival, Thompson, 35, cheated on his then-pregnant girlfriend with multiple women.

Kardashian and Thompson initially stayed together but split after he kissed her sister Kylie Jenner’s BFF Jordyn Woods in 2019. Thompson and Kardashian continued to date on-and-off before their son, Tatum, was born via surrogate in 2022.

“I get offended, whether it be Tristan or Lamar, where these people do these outrageous things and then they see me again and act as if nothing happened,” Kardashian told Kendall Jenner and Kylie in an April 2025 episode of their Hulu show.

She continued: “Does it say ‘f***ing doormat’ on my forehead? And it probably does to these people because I have been in so many different ways. Then it gets taken advantage of. It is really insulting to me.”
