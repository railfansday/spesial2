’X-Men ‘97’ Stars Officially Return for New 2027 Rogue and Gambit Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Hannah covers horror, sci-fi, and games at Collider, where she writes news and features, reviews new releases, and interviews the people who make them. She’s helping build out Collider’s gaming coverage, with Pokémon as one of her specialties. If she isn’t writing about a monster, she’s probably watching one on a tape from her VHS collection.

For all the trouble Rogue and Gambit can get into on an X-Men mission, sitting close enough to hold hands is still one of the riskier things they could do. Rogue can take someone’s memories, powers, or even their life with a touch, and Remy has never been especially good at keeping his distance. The flirting is fun because he knows exactly what she’s afraid of, and she knows exactly how much he means it.

That tension is associated with a particular pair of voices for anyone who watched X-Men ’97. Rogue can put a whole conversation into one “sugah,” and Gambit can make a line sound like he’s testing his luck even when he’s saying something perfectly ordinary. Marvel’s new romance gives them a trip to Remy’s hometown, a vampire problem, and a whole book’s worth of chances to say what they’ve been dancing around.

Collider can exclusively reveal that X-Men ’97 stars Lenore Zann and A.J. LoCascio will narrate the Rogue and Gambit chapters, respectively, in the audiobook of Rogue x Gambit (An X-Men Love Story). The novel by Louangie Bou-Montes and Aabria Iyengar, in collaboration with Marvel, releases from Random House Audio on February 9, 2027. With each actor reading their own character’s point-of-view chapters, listeners will get to hear what Rogue and Remy are thinking when the other one is out of earshot.

Four powers, and four completely different kinds of person behind them. Here is what you would wake up with, what it would cost you, and why the MCU keeps handing it to somebody exactly like you.

You get the power that is really about taking the hit so that nobody else has to. Strength in the MCU always lands on people who were already carrying too much, which is exactly why it suits you.

You get the power that never switches off. Spider-sense is a gift and a nervous system on permanent alert, and the people who get it in these films are always the ones who were already worrying about everybody.

You get the quietest power in the building, and quietly the most useful. Invisibility comes with force fields for a reason, because the people who prefer not to be seen are usually the ones holding a shield around everybody else.

You get the biggest and least comfortable power on the list, the one that runs directly on feeling. Chaos magic is not really a superpower in the MCU so much as an emotional state with consequences attached.

Remy heads home with the feeling that New Orleans needs him, though he’s less certain that the people he left behind want him showing up as an X-Man. Rogue goes with him, and the two discover that someone is buying up pieces of the city in the aftermath of a devastating hurricane. There are actual vampires involved, which makes the question of who’s profiting from the city’s pain a little more literal than either of them might have expected.

Giving both characters a point of view is a good fit for a couple who can spend an entire conversation flirting with the thing they actually want to say. Rogue worries that wanting to be close to someone makes her dangerous, while Remy trusts her with his life and would like her to trust herself half as much. That’s a fine setup for a romance, especially when the person asking Rogue to take a chance is a man who makes a living out of knowing exactly how much he can get away with.

Rogue x Gambit (An X-Men Love Story) releases February 9, 2027, from Random House Audio. Stay tuned at Collider for more.
