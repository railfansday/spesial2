Bachelor's Peter Weber Reveals He Secretly Married Pregnant Fiancee Jadeyn

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Bachelor’s Peter Weber is officially off the market after secretly marrying his pregnant fiancée, Jadeyn Carr.

“I was debating if I should reveal this here, but you guys have all been here following my journey for a very long time,” Peter, 35, shared via Bachelor Nation on Tuesday, November 17. “I appreciate that — all the support — and I can officially say that on 11/11, I married the love of my life.”

ABC’s former Bachelor continued, “I can honestly say I am so happy now with where life’s taken me.”

Peter’s announcement came when he was speaking on a panel as part of Bachelor Nation’s Vacation at Sea. Guests sailing on the luxurious Norwegian Joy are having the opportunity to vacation with dozens of past Bachelor and Bachelorette stars.

The news also comes less than a week after Peter announced he was engaged to Jadeyn, 26.

“Marry Me @jadeyncarr ❤️,” he wrote via Instagram on November 11 as Jadeyn put her oval diamond engagement ring on full display.

It’s been a year of big announcements for Peter and Jadeyn. After confirming their relationship in August, the pair went on to announce they are expecting twins.

“Pregnancy is romantic ❤︎,” Jadeyn wrote via Instagram on October 10. “There’s something so precious and raw about what our bodies are capable of. About how much we happily sacrifice for a little soul we haven’t even met yet.”

She continued, “Through the exhaustion, the body I’m still learning to recognize, the raging hormones (sorry Peter). This experience has given me a whole new level of admiration for the extraordinary strength and love that comes with growing a baby (or two). Womanhood is a superpower. And I can’t think of anything more romantic than that.”

Peter later commented, writing, “My Superhero ❤️.”

Fans met Peter when he competed on season 15 of The Bachelorette with Hannah Brown in 2019. He later became the lead of The Bachelor for season 24.

Although he didn’t find his happily ever after during the season, which aired in 2020, The Traitors alum still received his final rose.

“I have faith in due time that’ll work out,” he told People in February 2025. “I never lost faith, so I know it’ll happen soon and I’m just enjoying the journey along the way.”

Peter went on to say he wants a woman with “a kind heart, adventurous spirit” and “someone close to family.”
