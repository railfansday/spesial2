Liberace’s Palm Springs Estate Hits the Market for $6.9 Million—Plus 4 More Luxury Homes for Sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Also for sale this week: Annette Bening and Warren Beatty’s onetime home and the most expensive house in Charleston.

One of the most legendary entertainers in American history, Liberace dazzled audiences for decades with his over-the-top piano medleys, flamboyant costumes, and cheeky banter. The self-described “Mr. Showmanship” might be synonymous with Las Vegas, where he had a popular residency, but he also had a taste for Palm Springs, owning no fewer than four homes in the California desert oasis throughout his life.

Now, perhaps the most exceptional of those properties, a 1930s Spanish Revival estate—dubbed the Cloisters due to its network of arcades and courtyards—has hit the market for $6.9 million. Elsewhere in Southern California, the longtime Beverly Hills home of Hollywood power couple Annette Bening and Warren Beatty is also newly for sale, asking almost $15 million. And on the East Coast, Lady Gaga’s former penthouse atop Manhattan’s Hit Factory building is available for $7 million, while the most expensive home in Charleston just listed for $35 million. Check them all out below.

Acquired at the height of his career in the late 1960s, Liberace’s walled Spanish Colonial Revival estate in Palm Springs just hit the market for nearly $6.9 million. The famed entertainer turned the 1930s bed-and-breakfast, known as the Cloisters, into an extension of his opulent stage set, brimming with signature candelabras, opulent furnishings, and splashes of gold. He lived on the half-acre property until his death in 1987, after which new owners have completely remodeled the 8,000-square-foot home to bring it back to its original splendor. HGTV host Brett Waterman spearheaded the most recent update, filling the five-bedroom home with handcrafted details and understated furnishings. Perks include a heated in-ground pool, courtyards, and a spa facing the San Jacinto Mountains. It’s listed with Keith Markovitz and Rick Distel of TTK Represents at Compass.

On Wednesday, ELLE Decor exclusively reported that the longtime Beverly Hills home of Warren Beatty and Annette Bening was hitting the market for the first time since they sold it. The Hollywood power couple custom-built the gated residence in 1992 and raised their four kids there, selling it in 2012 to hotelier Jonathan Baker and his wife, Jenny. They spearheaded their own revamp to the tune of $5 million, bringing a contemporary spirit to the interiors of the nine-bedroom compound, which includes a guest house, pool, and tennis court. Its new asking price is $14.995 million.

Last year, a 225-year-old mansion smashed the record for the most expensive home sold in Charleston, fetching a little over $21 million. Now, another estate is vying for the title. The Sword Gate House, located in the South Carolina city’s historic South of Broad neighborhood, just listed for $35 million with Leslie Turner of Maison Real Estate. Revamped by interior designer Cortney Bishop, the 17,140-square-foot mansion dates to the early 1800s and features preserved millwork, exposed brick, and an original mural in the dining room depicting historic Charleston harbor. There are also uncommon perks like a triple-decker piazza, a ballroom, a pool, and an acre of manicured gardens. The whole compound is secured behind preserved wrought-iron gates made by local ironworker Christopher Werner, giving the landmark its moniker.

Forget a “Million Reasons”—we’re looking at $7 million. The New York City penthouse where Lady Gaga once lived just hit the market for nearly $7 million—and she’s not the home’s only celebrity connection. The 3,700-square-foot duplex was also once home to former NBA star Carmelo Anthony and 30 Rock actor Tracy Morgan, per People.

With its organic silhouette, cantilevered roofline, and clever use of space, Leuthold House by Allan Gelbin bears the hallmarks of his mentor, legendary architect Frank Lloyd Wright. Completed in 1966 on five rolling acres in New Canaan, the five-bedroom house spans roughly 5,700 square feet and underwent a restoration by Hobbs Inc. and JMK Architects in 2014. The interiors feature long expanses of glass and mahogany, while the grounds come with a spring-fed pond, a pool, a one-bedroom guest house, and 150 specimen trees. Listed for $6.2 million with John Engel and Melissa Engel of Douglas Elliman, the two-story house is on the market for the first time in 29 years.
