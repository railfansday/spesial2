Sergej Jakirovic open to more Hull City signings and finds striker's behaviour 'unacceptable'

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Irogebunam underwent hamstring surgery on Tuesday, with a predicted recovery time of three months.

But despite six of the 18 players Hull signed in the summer – so many, three senior players could not even be registered for the squad – being central midfielders, they may dip back into the market to cover for the 23-year-old.

They could also be on the lookout for a striker after Vaz was arrested in France for not making payments on his rental car. It has not gone down well with Jakirovic, who had not been won over by Vaz's football.

There is not understood to be a break clause in Vaz's season-long loan from Roma, but having only played 50 minutes of Premier League football – all from the bench – Jakirovic's words about the 19-year-old did not sound too promising if he returns.

"The club is dealing with this together with Roma, so we will find a solution for what we need to do right now," explained Jakirovic, whose side are at Crystal Palace on Sunday.

"For me, it's unacceptable as a head coach because I have 27 people in the building. Imagine that somebody else is doing this? What message am I sending there?

"He will be in training. We will organise everything and that's it. But the club is dealing with this.

"For me, everything is clear in this case. You (Vaz) are too young – I know everything, la-la-la – but you are a professional player, so you cannot behave like this.

Asked if Vaz had sought permission to go to France, Jakirovic replied: "Nobody asked me about this, but it's a day off, you can fly wherever, you just have to show up in the training session."

In addition to Vaz, fellow striker Ilyas Ansah is yet to play for the club since a spinal injury was discovered in his medical, although the German is building up his fitness in the under-21s having recently begun full training with the Tigers. Mohamed-Ali Cho was a third forward signed in the last transfer window, and he has scored once.

Likewise, Hull are still waiting for a significant return from their midfield additions. Irogebunam had just forced his way into the side, the last four games witnessing his first Premier League starts for Hull, but Luca Gourna, Hidemassa Morita, Jens Hjerto-Dahl and Christos Mouzakitis have kicked off a combined three league matches.

"Maybe we will bring one more player," said Jakirovic, who as coach is not in charge of recruitment.

"Maybe we will buy a £30m players, maybe they will surprise me."

If not, he may have to consider registering Darko Gyabi, Oscar Zambrano or Kieran Dowell, who are unable to play before January. Gyabi was de-selected partly because he was injured, but has since returned to play for the under-21s. "We are using this for Darko, for Dowell, (so) that they are also ready maybe for the next move, to other clubs, to home,” said Jakirovic. “People can see they are alive, they are healthy.

"Together with the club, we will discuss this. Maybe we will take other players on loan, maybe.

"It depends how we will be active in the transfer window in January, which is very difficult. It's easier to get a loan player."
