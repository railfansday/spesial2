Tory Horton’s heads-up play earns him NFC Special Teams Player of the Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Seattle Seahawks have found themselves in several batted-ball controversies, but none broke new ground like wide receiver Tory Horton's effort on Sunday against the Las Vegas Raiders.

Horton's bat mirrored that of a home run-robbing catch as he knocked down the Raiders' kickoff so his teammate, Rashid Shaheed, could run the game past the two-minute warning and clinch a Seahawks victory.

For his efforts, the Seahawks special teamer was first flagged on the play, but has since been honored by the league. The Colorado State alum earned the NFC Special Teams Player of the Week Award, creating one of, if not the funniest 'stat' graphic of all time.

The award is Horton's first and just the latest in a string of honors for the Seahawks' special teams group. Shaheed himself earned the player of the week award after his punt return touchdown against the Los Angeles Rams in Week 14 last season. He also becomes the first Colorado State position player to earn the award, per the NFL, which is perhaps the only thing CSU Rams fans will be able to celebrate this season.

While the second-year wideout has struggled to find a consistent role in the offense, like nearly every pass catcher outside of Jaxon Smith-Njigba, he has been a core special teamer this season. Horton has filled in while Shaheed has battled injuries and recorded a 37-yard kick return against the Kansas City Chiefs.

The Seahawks' wide receiver's tremendous play helped Seattle improve to 6-4, keeping their playoff hopes alive heading into their bye week.
