Why is Rodri not playing for FC Barcelona vs. Atletico Madrid?

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

FC Barcelona heads to the Spanish capital on Sunday for a showdown with Atlético Madrid, but it will do it without one of its marquee summer signings.

Rodri won't make the trip for the match, with Barcelona forced to replace the midfielder. Barca tops the table on goal difference, with Real Madrid drawing level on 30 points.

A win against third-place Atleti would put the Balugrana back into sole possession of first place, while a draw or defeat would see both Madrid clubs buoyed by the chance of a true title race.

The club noted that Rodri felt something off in Barcelona's 3-0 UEFA Champions League win over Aston Villa during the week. He exited the contest at halftime, with scans showing he suffered an injury to his right hamstring.

In addition to missing the trip to face Atleti, Rodri may miss additional matches depending on how the injury heals.

Where: Metropolitano Stadium(Madrid, Spain)

Channel/streaming: ESPN/ESPN Deportes (Watch on ESPN+)
