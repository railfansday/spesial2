Steelers EDGE Alex Highsmith OUT vs. Broncos, Nick Herbig will play

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Pittsburgh Steelers edge rusher Nick Herbig was a full participant in Thursday's practice and he will play Friday against the Denver Broncos. Safety DeShon Elliott and Michael Pittman were also full participants and will play.

Linebacker Cole Holcomb is questionable with a heel injury. He was limited in practice.

Guard Gennings Dunker was a full participant, but was ruled out with a knee injury. Edge rusher Alex Highsmith is out with a knee injury, and wide receiver Ben Skowronek is out with a calif injury, and will head to IR.

The Steelers currently hold the No. 7 seed in the AFC playoff picture, sitting one game ahead of the New England Patriots and two games ahead of the Houston Texans. Pittsburgh will face Houston in Week 13, which gives the next two weeks an extra sense of urgency as they look to hold onto a spot in the postseason.
