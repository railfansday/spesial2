Hornets history: Kemba Walker sets single-game scoring record (2018)

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

On this day eight years ago, Charlotte Hornets franchise history was made by former No. 9 overall pick Kemba Walker. In a matchup against the Philadelphia 76ers, Walker broke the franchise single-game scoring record, tallying 60 points on an incredible 21-34 shooting. He also tallied seven rebounds, four assists and four steals in what ended as an overtime defeat. Walker's 60 points were over half of Charlotte's total, as the Hornets fell 122-119.

Walker, the Hornets' all-time leading scorer, owns many of Charlotte's single-game scoring records, having tallied 52 points, 47 points (on three separate occasions) and 46 points. Overall, the former UConn guard spent eight seasons with Charlotte, averaging 19.8 points, 5.5 assists, 3.8 rebounds, 1.3 steals and 0.4 blocks over 605 games with the franchise.

After finishing his career with the Celtics, Knicks and Mavericks, Walker played one season overseas before returning to Charlotte as a player enhancement coach.
