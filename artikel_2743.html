Jared Goff: Jahmyr Gibbs is a special player, special guy, special teammate

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Back in August, the Lions signed Jahmyr Gibbs to a three-year contract extension.

The 24-year-old running back currently leads the league in rushing yards (1,000), rushing touchdowns (11), yards from scrimmage (1,384), and total touchdowns (13).

In his Wednesday press conference, quarterback Jared Goff praised Gibbs for his outstanding performance so far in 2026.

“You look at the numbers he’s doing, it’s like you hear his name mentioned with [Hall of Famer] LaDainian Tomlinson it seems like every week. And obviously we know about that guy and yeah, he’s unbelievable,” Goff said, via transcript from the team. “He really is. He’s a special player. He’s a special guy, special teammate. The work he puts in I think gets unnoticed sometimes. He’s a worker and has elevated his game from last year to this year in a lot of ways that you guys have obviously seen and it’s paid off. It’s been great for him and he’s playing really well.”

Goff added that there’s plenty the public doesn’t see when it comes to Gibbs’ competitiveness.

“He’s very competitive,” Goff said. “Anytime there’s a competitive situation in practice, he wants the ball, he wants to make someone look silly, he wants to make somebody miss and he wants to make the play. And when he doesn’t, he gets upset and that’s kind of what you like to see when he makes a mistake or misses a block. He’s truly upset about it and that’s a sign of a good player and a guy who’s striving for greatness.”

It’s also worth noting that Gibbs leads the league in rushing attempts (199) and touches (240). His previous career high in touches came last year with 320, which he appears poised to blow through.
