Live updates, score from Florida basketball vs Milwaukee

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Florida basketball returns home Tuesday to face Milwaukee after winning two of three games at the Players Era 8 tournament in Las Vegas.

Tipoff is scheduled for 7 p.m. ET at Tower Hill Arena in Gainesville, with streaming coverage on SEC Network+ through the ESPN app. The Gators (6-1) closed their trip with a win over West Virginia and now host the Panthers (2-4) in just their third home game of the season.

Urban Klavzar brings a hot hand back to Gainesville after averaging 18.3 points in Las Vegas and making 10 of his 15 attempts from three. Boogie Fland will not play after dealing with a shoulder injury for most of last week.

Florida shot better than 50% from beyond the arc in all three tournament games, giving the offense something to build on before next week's matchup with Duke.
