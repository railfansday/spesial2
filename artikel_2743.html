Updating Eagles' 2027 draft pick after win over Steelers

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Updating Eagles' 2027 draft pick after win over Steelers originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

The Eagles were able to snap their seven-game losing streak on Sunday with a 19-16 win over the Pittsburgh Steelers.

The bad news is that the win has dropped the Eagles a few slots in the 2027 draft. The Eagles went from No. 5 to No. 8 in Week 11, per Tankathon:

Not only did the Eagles win on Sunday but most of the teams ahead of them lost. The only team in the top 10 that won on Sunday was the Jets and they beat the Chargers. Both teams are still ahead of the Eagles in draft order.

The Commanders (3-5-1) are hosting the Bengals (7-2) on Monday Night Football. They are currently in the No. 13 slot.

By winning on Sunday, the Eagles snapped the longest losing streak in the NFL. The Eagles had lost seven in a row entering Week 11. The longest losing streak in the league now belongs to the Lamar Jackson-less Ravens at six in a row.

Since becoming Eagles GM in 2010, Howie Roseman has made just four picks in the top 10: Lane Johnson (No. 4 in 2013), Carson Wentz (No. 2 in 2016), DeVonta Smith (No. 10 in 2021) and Jalen Carter (No. 9 in 2023).

The Eagles will have two first-round draft picks in 2027 after trading Jordan Davis to the Jaguars before the deadline. The Jaguars lost to the Giants on Sunday but are still way out in front in the AFC South.

In addition to the two first-round picks in 2027, the Eagles also have a second-round pick, a projected third-round compensatory pick, a fourth-round pick, three fifth-round picks and a seventh-round pick.
