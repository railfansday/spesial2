Rico Dowdle limited to seven touches in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Pittsburgh Steelers RB Rico Dowdle had five carries for 26 yards against the Cincinnati Bengals in Week 10. Dowdle also had eight yards on two receptions.

Why It Matters: With the Steelers unable to possess the football, Dowdle finished the night with a season-low seven touches. It was a disappointing step backwards after he logged 86 yards in Week 8 and 56 yards with a touchdown in Week 9. Dowdle is stuck behind Jaylen Warren, but he should get around a dozen touches most weeks. That's enough to give him value as a middling flex candidate.
