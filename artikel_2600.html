Things get physical as Spurs fan shoves Luka Doncic in wild exchange with Lakers star

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Los Angeles Lakers couldn't hold on to a 20-point lead Friday night, and Luka Doncic couldn't hold on to his temper.

Moments after the San Antonio Spurs stunned the Lakers with a 99-96 comeback victory in their NBA Cup Group C matchup, Doncic got into a heated altercation with two courtside fans that required security to intervene.

Video captured by Amazon Prime Video's postgame broadcast showed Doncic walking toward the visitors' locker room when a woman sitting courtside appeared to say something to the Lakers superstar.

Luka Doncic of the Los Angeles Lakers reacts during a game against the San Antonio Spurs in San Antonio, Texas, Friday, Nov. 27th, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

Doncic routinely tossed his white wristband at the woman before continuing toward the tunnel. Seconds later, he doubled back to retrieve it.

Los Angeles Lakers guard Luka Doncic #77 is shoved by a Spurs' fan sitting courtside in San Antonio. Amazon Prime

The woman's male companion, who had picked up the wristband, handed it back to Doncic before shoving the six-time All-Star with his right hand as he walked away.

Doncic stopped in his tracks and turned around, but Lakers teammate Jarred Vanderbilt restrained him before leaning toward the fan and exchanging words.

Los Angeles Lakers guard Luka Doncic #77 is shoved by a Spurs' fan sitting courtside in San Antonio.

Security quickly intervened, and the male fan was subsequently ejected while Doncic retreated to the Lakers' locker room.

It's unclear what was initially said to Doncic, but throwing his wristband at a spectator was an unnecessary lapse in judgment. That, however, doesn't give a paying customer license to put his hands on an NBA player.

With the entire incident captured on camera, Doncic could face a fine or suspension from the NBA. It's unknown whether the fan will face additional punishment.

The altercation punctuated a spectacular collapse by the Lakers, whose 20-point first-half advantage evaporated like water on hot pavement. San Antonio outscored Los Angeles 30-14 in the third quarter, tying the game entering the fourth before pulling off the upset.

The Lakers lost their composure long before Doncic reached the tunnel.
