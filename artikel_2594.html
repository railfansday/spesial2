Exclusive CelticPlayer interview with Martin O'Neill

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Celtic returned to action following the international break with an impressive 4-1 home win over St Mirren at the weekend.

Goals from Alex Oxlade-Chamberlain, Camilo Duran, Luke McCowan and Callum McGregor delivered another league victory for the Hoops, delighting Martin O'Neill.

The Celtic manager was pleased with the performance and the result, and with a further eight fixtures to follow before the end of 2026, he's looking for the team to use the win over St Mirren as a springboard going into these games.

Almost 48 hours after the victory at Celtic Park, Martin O'Neill sat down with CelticPlayer to reflect on the game and also look ahead, specifically to the next game on the horizon – a UEFA Europa league clash with Portuguese side, Torreense, on Thursday night.

"I think the coordination of the side was very, very good, but obviously first half, excellent," he said.

"We didn't feel hard done by, getting a couple of goals chopped offside, and just kept going. And we played some really lovely football too.

"We got the important first goal and then followed up with two more goals before half-time. I thought we should have really pushed on. Sometimes that's easier said than done, but the overall performance was really pleasing."

And on the return to Europa League football this week, the manager added: "We had a terrific win against Besiktas, and to win so late and win in the fashion in which we did was terrific.

"So it gives us a little bit of a chance, and we have to go and try and do something now for these next two away games.

"We've got to take confidence from the recent performances now and really take that into the game on Thursday night against Torreense."
