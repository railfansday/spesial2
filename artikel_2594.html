Moneyline Betting Explained: Definition, Examples, and FAQ

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Moneyline betting involves wagering on which team or player will win. The margin of victory doesn't affect the payout. Some markets also let you bet on a draw.

In American odds, a negative number shows how much you'd stake to win $100. A positive number shows how much a $100 stake would win. At -150, a $15 bet earns $10 in profit if it wins. At +150, a $10 bet earns $15.

A moneyline bet wins when your side wins the game outright. Score, spread and margin don't matter. A one-point win pays the same as a 30-point blowout.

The favorite has the shorter price. A -178 selection requires a $178 stake to win $100. A +149 selection pays $149 in profit on a winning $100 bet. In a close matchup, both teams can have negative odds. At -110 on each side, both teams carry the same price.

A standard full-game, two-way NFL moneyline includes overtime. If a regular-season game ends tied after overtime, the bet is a push. Fanatics, theScore Bet, FanDuel and DraftKings return the original stake on a straight cash bet.

If the market offers a draw, check the settlement period on the ticket. A draw at the end of that period wins the draw selection, and both team selections lose.

A two-way moneyline offers two choices, one for each side. A three-way moneyline adds the draw. You can also combine eligible moneyline selections into a single bet called a parlay.

A two-way moneyline offers one selection per team. Standard full-game NFL, NBA, MLB and NHL moneylines use this format. Check the market label for the period that counts toward settlement.

A three-way moneyline offers three selections: either team to win or a draw. In a standard soccer match, this market settles after 90 minutes plus stoppage time. At +250, a winning $10 draw bet earns $25 in profit.

A parlay combines two or more selections, called legs, into one bet. On a standard parlay, one losing leg loses the ticket. A $10 parlay with two -110 moneylines on separate games earns about $26.45 in profit if both legs win. A push can remove a leg and reduce the payout. Check the house rules for same-game parlays.

Say the Bills are -175 against the Ravens at +150. A $17.50 bet on Buffalo earns $10 if it wins. A $10 bet on Baltimore earns $15 if it wins. If the regular-season game ends tied after overtime, a standard two-way moneyline pushes.

Implied probability is the break-even win rate at a given price. Use the odds without their plus or minus sign in these formulas.

Negative odds: Implied probability = odds ÷ (odds + 100). Profit = stake × 100 ÷ odds.

Positive odds: Implied probability = 100 ÷ (odds + 100). Profit = stake × odds ÷ 100.

Total payout on a winning cash bet equals the original stake plus profit. Use our sports betting odds calculator to check potential payouts for single bets and parlays.

At -110 on both sides, the implied probabilities add up to 104.76%. The 4.76 percentage points above 100% are the market's overround. Overround measures how much the quoted probabilities exceed 100%.

If you're new to moneylines, compare the same team and market at two sportsbooks. A winning $10 bet at -130 earns $7.69, compared with $6.90 at -145. Check whether the price is for the full game or regulation before you compare.

If you already track your bets, record the price and its break-even percentage. At -110, that's 52.38%. If your estimated win rate is higher, check which assumptions drive the difference. A lineup change or a scratched starter can change your estimate.

Confusing payout with profit. A winning $10 bet at +150 pays $25 total. That includes your $10 stake and $15 in profit.

Overvaluing a big payout. At +300, one win in four breaks even. If exactly 20 of 100 bets of $10 win at that price, you lose $200 overall.

Comparing different markets. An NHL full-game moneyline includes overtime and shootouts. A three-way regulation line settles after 60 minutes. Check the market name before comparing prices.

Assuming a push refunds the whole parlay. A pushed leg can drop off while the other legs stay active. Check the sportsbook's rules, especially for same-game parlays.

Assuming every pitching change cancels a bet. If your MLB moneyline lists one required starter, check that pitcher. If it lists both, both must start. A bet marked action can stand after a pitching change.
