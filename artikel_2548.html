Week 11 fantasy quarterback rankings: Top QB streamers, starts

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Hello fantasy football managers, and welcome to bye-mageddon. A season-high six NFL teams are on a bye this week, so that's causing more than a few headaches for those looking to fill holes in their starting lineups.

Nowhere is the pain felt more acutely than at quarterback, where injuries have already taken several starters off the board − even before the byes start factoring in.

One quarterback who's seen his stock rise througout the season is the Texans' C.J. Stroud. He threw for 384 yards and five touchdowns in Week 9 and followed that up with 211 yards and three more scores last week. Back home on Sunday against the Colts, Stroud is comfortably inside the top 10 at the position.
