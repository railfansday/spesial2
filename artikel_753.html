U.K. Labels Veteran Ferdy Unger-Hamilton Joins Gotobeat Advisory Board

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

With Unger-Hamilton on board, says Max Busin, CEO and founder of Gotobeat, the business enjoys a "significant step" forward.

Ex-Columbia Records U.K. president Ferdy Unger-Hamilton joins the advisory board of Gotobeat, a business that describes its mission as “building a new model for live entertainment.”

With Unger-Hamilton on board, enthuses Max Busin, CEO and founder of Gotobeat, the company enjoys a “significant step” forward. “What a moment this is for Gotobeat,” he remarks. “We’re building a company that combines the experience and relationships that have always driven the music industry with technology that allows us to operate in a completely different way. Ferdy understands that vision, and I’m really looking forward to building the next chapter of Gotobeat with him.” Related CL Signs With THEBLACKLABEL, Reuniting With Longtime Producer Teddy For 'Full Circle' Moment Jeff Benjamin

A decade ago, in 2016, Unger-Hamilton was promoted to lead Columbia Records’ U.K., following senior leadership roles at Virgin and Polydor Records, where he helped launch Ellie Goulding, The 1975, Lana Del Rey, Haim, Years & Years and Michael Kiwanuka.

Earlier, in 2014, he founded Promised Land Music, where he helped launch the career of Fred again…

Based in London, Gotobeat presents itself as a “next-generation” live music promoter and ticketing platform that uses proprietary data and artificial intelligence to design, price, and launch tours and festivals across the United Kingdom and Europe.

Unger-Hamilton takes his seat as the company prepares for its next phase of growth in markets, including projects focused on Latin music events.

“What excites me about Gotobeat is that it understands both sides of the music industry: the importance of relationships, instinct and experience, and the opportunity to use data and technology to make better decisions,” he remarks in a statement. “Live music is constantly evolving, and there is real potential to rethink how artists reach audiences and how touring businesses grow. Gotobeat has an ambitious vision for what a modern live entertainment company can be, and I’m excited to bring my experience to the team as it enters this next stage of accelerated growth.”
