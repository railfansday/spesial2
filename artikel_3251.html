Kevin James Doyle Releasing Edinburgh Fringe Show ‘After Endgame: A Comedy About Chess’ As Special

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

EXCLUSIVE: After Endgame: A Comedy About Chess, the Fringe solo show from comedian Kevin James Doyle, will become his fourth comedy special, out February 11 on his YouTube channel.

After Endgame is a lesson in both life and chess from someone who has taught over 6,500 chess lessons. Doyle takes the audience on a journey across the globe as he is flown to the vibrant city of Singapore at the behest of a mysterious investor. There, he finds himself imparting chess wisdom to some of the world’s wealthiest individuals. Tensions rise as he navigates these high-stakes players, both on and off the chessboard. Doyle became a chess instructor in New York City 15 years ago, around the same time he started doing stand-up comedy. Following his experience in Singapore, he knew he had to tell the story on stage — a tale of real-life game of deception, with feints and intrigue, which he analyzes with lessons he learned from studying chess. In constructing the show, he drew inspiration from films like House of Games and The Color of Money, as well as the Off-Broadway sleight-of-hand magic show Ricky Jay and His 52 Assistants. Doyle performed After Endgame to critical acclaim at the Edinburgh Fringe in 2024, followed by 2025 dates in Chicago, Columbus, Denver and Los Angeles, as well as four weeks off-Broadway at the Soho Playhouse in New York City. Both the show and special are directed by his longtime friend and collaborator, Cory Cavin.

Doyle is Creative Producer at SoHo Playhouse, where he founded and leads SoHo Playhouse Jr., the theater’s children’s theater and education program. He previously co-wrote and starred in the long running Off-Broadway production How to Be a New Yorker and has also performed at the Edinburgh Festival Fringe with The 30 Year Old Virgin and Diary of a Bald Kid — which were taped as specials — and Wild Card.

Doyle is otherwise known for co-hosting The Bradshaw Boys, a Sex and the City podcast featuring appearances by all four principal actresses, and co-creating Great Times with Cory and Kevin, a New York City comedy show running since 2014.
