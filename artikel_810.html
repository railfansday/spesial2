Country Star Maggie Rose Brings a Rock 'n' Roll Edge to Coastal Style

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Statement coats, vintage-inspired denim, cheeky graphic tees and cozy knitwear give Maggie Rose’s curated collection a laid-back California feel with a cool-girl edge.

Country star Maggie Rose has teamed up with Carve Designs on a curated collection that feels like a vintage rocker found her way to the California coast. Think statement outerwear, cool girl denim, playful T-shirts, and cozy knitwear with an easygoing, Gold Coast feel.

Launched November 2, the collection brings together Maggie’s personal style and the California-based brand’s laid-back aesthetic. Its versatile pieces are made for the many roles women move between every day, whether that means heading onstage or spending time with family.

“When I feel comfortable, I feel confident. And when I feel confident, I can be fully present,” Maggie says about the collaboration. “That’s how I feel in every piece of this collection. Whether I’m performing in front of thousands of people or reading bedtime stories to Graham, I just want to feel beautiful, comfortable, and completely like myself.”

With their shared love of nature, life and a little rock-and-roll edge, Rose and Carve Designs make a perfect pairing.

“This partnership felt so natural because Carve is a women-run brand that understands that women are multi-faceted, dynamic and complex beings,” she said. “We’re artists, moms, businesswomen, athletes and travelers—We wear a lot of hats…and pants and dresses! I love that Carve celebrates all of those different sides of who we are, instead of asking us to choose just one.”

From vintage-inspired silhouettes to statement prints and graphics, the fun capsule makes it easy to bring a little rock-and-roll attitude to everyday dressing—without losing that polished, retro California spirit.
