AFC Wimbledon v Barnsley FC: Daniel Stendel on importance of dark arts

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Barnsley head coach Daniel Stendel, inset, knows it and his more studious players do too.

Speaking after Tuesday's 3-0 loss at Bradford City, Sam Gale spoke admirably about the home players' ability to defend their box and be 'savvy' with blocks, pushes and nudges at both ends of the pitch.

It all boils down to game intelligence and being streetwise. In games like Saturday which sees basement club Barnsley visit sixth-from-bottom AFC Wimbledon – where the result is everything and a good performance is a bonus – this importance is magnified.

Stendel, whose side are winless in 11 league matches and conceded three soft goals as a result of long balls in midweek, said: "I think we have the quality to defend long balls, it's not like we don't have the players who can do it.

"Sometimes, it is not just your individual quality on the technical or tactical side, but also the desire to be ready in the duels and push this (opposing) guy or defend.

"On Tuesday, the push was from the other guy (Bradford player for the first goal) and here you can improve and learn.

"But in professional football, you do not have a long time to learn, you must learn quick or you are not ready for this game."

Having the mindset to cast aside mistakes and stay mentally strong is another facet which the Reds' young players must develop fast in particular.

Stendel, whose side have taken a pitiful two points from the last 33, with their last league win coming almost three months ago, continued: "We have to accept that we make mistakes maybe more than other teams, but as a team, we need more of a mentality to say: 'ok, we accept that'.

"When we miss a chance, it's the same as a mistake in the defence. It's part of our development in this time and we have to improve individually and with the decision-making situation.

“We have to develop, improve and stay together."
