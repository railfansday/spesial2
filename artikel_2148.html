Amar Dedic is among three Newcastle players who should be axed for their upcoming clash against Fulham.

Matthias Jaissle is facing increasing pressure as Newcastle struggle at the wrong end of the Premier League table.

The Magpies have lost four of their last five outings in league and cup and sit 13th in the top-flight right now.

Jaissle will know he needs to ring the changes this weekend, as a defeat at fellow strugglers Fulham could see him out of a job.

Bosnian right-back Dedic deserves to be dropped to the bench after a string of poor performances and off-field issues.

Football Insider have picked out two other Magpies starters from last week's loss to Everton who should be axed from the first eleven at Craven Cottage.

Dedic has been the only right-back option for Jaissle in recent weeks after injuries to Tino Livramento and Lewis Miley.

The 24-year-old struggled in each of their recent losses to Everton, winning just two duels and playing just one accurate long ball from seven attempts (17 per cent) in last Monday's clash.

He has also embarrassed the club away from the pitch in midweek by sparking a major police operation after being caught with illegal weapons in a public area.

Miley is back fit for the trip to Fulham and should be back in at right-back, where he has impressed so far this season.

Barnes has come in for massive criticism among Magpies fans this season, and it is easy to see why.

He failed to create a chance or have a shot on goal against Everton last time out, while also winning one of nine attempted duels and completing one accurate cross from eight attempts (12 per cent).

The 28-year-old was also dispossessed more times (3) than any player on the pitch as he continued his recent poor performances on the left wing.

It is time for Jaissle to drop him for the first time in the league this season, especially with Bazoumana Toure still untested and ready to make his mark.

Osula looked to have turned a corner with a goal against Crystal Palace two weeks ago, but has since failed to impress.

He was hooked off at half-time against Everton in the Carabao Cup after taking just seven touches of the ball and failing to win a duel.

Jaissle then incredibly handed the Dane a full 90 minutes four days later against the Toffees, but he took the fewest touches (13) and completed the fewest passes (3) of any starter, with one duel won from four attempts.

Yoane Wissa and Matias Fernandez-Pardo are each ready to come in for Osula in attack, and Jaissle needs to switch things up again if he wants to stay as Newcastle manager for much longer.
