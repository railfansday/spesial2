13 players set to miss QPR vs Burnley ft £20m striker

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

It's an action-packed week in the Championship before the November international break, with a full slate of midweek games on the horizon.

It's been a real feast-or-famine month for QPR since the return from the last break. After a 1-1 draw against West Ham United in their first game back, Julien Stephan's side have alternated between big wins and defeats in their last four, all by at least a three-goal margin.

After a 4-1 defeat at Southampton on Saturday, they return to Loftus Road to hopefully continue that pattern against a struggling Burnley side.

The Clarets year-long wait for a win at home continued against Portsmouth in Fabio Celestini's first game in charge following the sacking of Nicky Hayen, but it was a promising performance which arguably warranted all three points and at least saw them keep a clean sheet.

Now they're on the hunt to climb out of the bottom three, but against an unpredictable QPR side, grabbing just their second win of the season may be quite difficult.

Nevertheless, it promises to be an exciting matchup in the capital, but these 13 players won't be available for it.

Once again, QPR were forced to end the game at Southampton with ten men due to injury, as Alfie Lloyd, who had just come on 14 minutes earlier, came off with what seemed to be a hamstring issue.

The 23-year-old had just returned from a couple of games on the sidelines, too, which must be incredibly difficult to take. It's unknown how long this fresh setback will keep him out for.

QPR boss Julien Stephan revealed in late October that a trio of key players will be out until after the international break. One of whom is Nicolas Madsen, who has been one of their more impressive players since the Frenchman took over ahead of last season.

The Danish midfielder picked up his first three goal involvements of the campaign against West Ham and Derby County, but after being unused from the bench in the defeat at Birmingham City, it was revealed he'll be on the sidelines for a few weeks.

Injury issues are the main reason why someone with the quality of Tariq Lamptey has found himself playing in the second tier this season. The former Chelsea and Brighton and Hove Albion man signed for QPR off the back of an ACL injury picked up at Fiorentina.

The 26-year-old returned from an ankle problem against West Ham, but managed to re-injure it in his 21-minute cameo off the bench. He will also be out of action until after the international break.

Striker Rumarn Burrell has battled injury issues throughout 2026, clouding what was a brilliant start to his life at Loftus Road last season. His ten league goals came in 26 games before he went down in January.

Now, an issue picked up during the last international break with Jamaica will see him out of action until the following break. Richard Kone has done a good enough job stepping up in his absence, with four goals in that time.

Amadou Mbengue will miss the remainder of the 2026/27 campaign after he was forced off early in their win over Derby with a devastating ACL injury.

The right-back started seven of the opening ten games this season, and will be a big miss over the coming year at Loftus Road.

Dastan Satpaev has had a really frustrating loan spell at Burnley so far. The 18-year-old arrived with plenty of promise from Chelsea and has looked exciting, but hasn't been able to stay fit for lengthy periods.

New Clarets boss Fabio Celestini confirmed that he faces yet another spell on the sidelines with a hamstring issue, after he missed the goalless draw with Portsmouth.

Armando Broja hasn't enjoyed the best of stints at Burnley since arriving from Chelsea, either. The Albanian international was bought for up to £20 million by the Clarets ahead of their Premier League return last summer, but has scored just one goal.

He's yet to find the net this season, and that wait looks set to continue, as he faces time out with a groin injury he picked up in Nicky Hayen's final game at Lincoln City.

Celestini admitted he doesn't know how long Broja will be on the sidelines for, but it will be a few weeks at least.

In what is currently an injury crisis in the forward areas, Zeki Amdouni's absence due to an ankle injury he picked up midway through October is the biggest frustration at Turf Moor.

The Swissman had finally started to find his feet in a Burnley shirt after a stint which has so far seen him sent out on loan the last time they were in the Championship, only to pick up an ACL injury upon his return last summer.

But after three goals in four games, he's been out and will remain out until later this month.
