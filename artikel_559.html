Hila Klein Exits ‘H3 Podcast’ Following Ethan Klein Affair: ‘I’m Devastated That Things Have Come to This’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Hila Klein is officially exiting the “H3 Podcast” a month after it was revealed that her co-host and husband, Ethan Klein, had been having a six-month affair. The news follows Hila’s October announcement that the show would be on an “indefinite hiatus” amid the scandal’s fallout.

She continued: “I’m devastated that things have come to this. I love this community we’ve built together so much, and I love the crew like my own family. Walking away from something that has been such a huge part of my life is incredibly painful.” “I will miss you all more than I can put into words,” she concluded.

When Ethan’s affair was made public, the podcast’s production team unanimously agreed to cut ties with the host, issuing a statement that read: “We stand with Hila and will support her every step of the way. We’re disgusted by Ethan’s actions and the hurt he has caused. We all deserved better: Hila, us, and you.”

“We are all shocked, angry, and heartbroken to learn about Ethan’s ongoing affair,” the team’s statement continued. “As soon as we found out, we all agreed: we would not continue working with Ethan involved or profiting in any way. We don’t yet know what that means for the future. There’s a lot to work through, and we wish we had more answers for you.”
