Dodge Skips The Press Conference For A Celebrity Stock Car Brawl

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Dodge is skipping the boring press conference to mark its return to NASCAR Cup racing. The brand will unveil its new Cup Series car before a celebrity race on December 4. This event, called the Dodge Sno-Brawl, takes place at Five Flags Speedway in Pensacola, Florida. It happens right in the middle of the 59th annual Snowball Derby weekend. Per PR Newswire, the company chose this high-energy setting to show off the car to real fans instead of hiding it behind a curtain.

The race features a field of 10 drivers who know how to handle a vehicle but need help on the track. Former NFL quarterback Jay Cutler and tight end Delanie Walker will drive Dodge Charger late-model stock cars. They will face off against UFC champion Quinton "Rampage" Jackson and X Games legend Brian Deegan. The group also includes social media star Demi Bagby and drift racer Matt Coffman. These athletes are racing for a $50,000 check that goes to the charity of their choice. The winner takes home a unique trophy and lifetime bragging rights for winning at one of the most famous short tracks in the country.

Racing legend Tony Stewart is on hand to teach these celebrities how to drive in circles. Stewart fields Dodge teams in the NHRA and knows exactly how to handle a stock car on a short track. Per FloRacing, he will use his decades of experience to help his wife, Leah Pruett, who is also competing. Pruett has 19 NHRA wins, but this is a different type of racing. Stewart's advice matters because the cars have a Direct Connection-produced Charger Scat Pack Nose that changes how they handle the turns.

Dodge has not raced in the Cup Series since 2012 when Brad Keselowski won the championship. The company is bringing back gasoline-powered performance as a core part of its identity. "Forget the flashy reveal. We're back, and we're bringing the future of Dodge in the NASCAR Cup Series where it belongs, to a legendary racetrack and the passionate, grassroots fans who have fueled our brand in NASCAR for decades," said Bob Broderdorf, Dodge CEO. "Next year, for the first time in 15 years, Dodge will take the green flag in a NASCAR Cup Series race. Gasoline-powered performance is back at the center of the brand, and it's time for Dodge to go NASCAR Cup Series racing."

The new Cup car will make its actual race debut at the 2027 Daytona 500. Before that, fans can see the car and the celebrities during the Sno-Brawl on December 4. The reveal happens during a break in the race action at Five Flags Speedway. Per Motorsport.com NASCAR Cup, the event is part of the Kaulig Racing umbrella for the upcoming season. AJ Allmendinger and Ty Dillon are named as the drivers who will pilot the cars next year. The race on December 4 is just the beginning of a return that started with Ram in the Truck Series in 2026.
