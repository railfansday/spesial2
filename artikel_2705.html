What Alijah Vera-Tucker returning to practice means for the Patriots

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The New England Patriots returned to work on Monday, officially ending their bye week. When they did, they saw a member of their starting offensive line make his comeback: left guard Alijah Vera-Tucker participated in practice for the first time since suffering a hamstring injury in Week 6.

Vera-Tucker's return means that he has now officially been designated to return from injured reserve and is eligible to rejoin New England's active roster as soon as this week's game against the Los Angeles Chargers. Let's find out how this impacts the Patriots.

The actual on-field impact of Vera-Tucker returning to the 53-man roster will be discussed once he is actually activated. For now, however, let's focus on the return designation itself and it's impact on player and team.

First of all, the timeline of his return. At the moment, Vera-Tucker remains on injured reserve albeit with the "designated for return" attachment next to his status. This signifies that his window to come back has now opened and will remain so for the next 21 days. He can be activated at any point during those three weeks, and if not would revert to regular IR for the remainder of the season.

Obviously, with return designations limited — more on those in a second — the expectation is that the 27-year-old will return to the 53-man roster during that 21-day period. That activation might happen as early as Friday, when the Patriots are set to fly to L.A. for their game against the Chargers.

The Patriots currently have 53 players on their active roster, the maximum allowed by the NFL during the regular season. This means that whenever the team decides to actually activate Vera-Tucker, a follow-up move will become necessary.

What that move will look like is anybody's guess at this point in time. It could be as simple as cutting a player from the roster, possibly from an offensive line that would be 11 players deep once Vera-Tucker is fully back.

Vera-Tucker has extensive starting experience at both left and right guard, but the belief is that he will return to his previous position in the Patriots lineup when all is said and done. While this should help solidify the left guard spot — he looked solid in his first six games of the season — the right guard position is where the real intrigue now starts.

With Mike Onwenu sidelined and increasingly unlikely to return at any point this year, the Patriots realistically have three starting options available: Greg Van Roten, Ben Brown and Matt Pryor. Van Roten was the next man up at right guard after Onwenu broke his ankle in Week 2; Brown took over for him when he went to IR one game later; Pryor has started the last four games.

Having Vera-Tucker back allows the Patriots to use their interior O-line resources differently, and the process of doing so is already getting started this week.

Every NFL team is allowed to bring up to eight players back from injured reserve during the regular season, and Vera-Tucker has now taken the sixth of those spots.

Previously, the Patriots designated wide receivers A.J. Brown and Mack Hollins, fullback Reggie Gilliam, and offensive linemen Marcus Bryan and Van Roten for activation. All of them have since made their way to the 53-man roster, but even if they hadn't their return spots would still count against the eight available.

This also means that the Patriots have only two spots remaining to be used on a group of players consisting of RB TreVeyon Henderson, WR Romeo Doubs, RG Mike Onwenu, ED Quintayvious Hutchins, LB Khalil Jacobs and S Dell Pettus. QB Drake Maye, who is expected to miss at least six weeks after fracturing his fibula is not even part of that group; he remains on the active roster for now.

His future is uncertain. What seems like a safe bet, on the other hand, is that the seventh returnee will be Romeo Doubs.
