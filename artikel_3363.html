Prime Video's 30-Episode Action Thriller Series Is The Better Version Of A 5-Movie Franchise

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

While one Prime Video action thriller series already had an existing set of movie adaptations, the small-screen take on the story somehow managed to outdo its blockbuster competition. It is rare for a TV show’s take on a franchise to gain more popularity than its big-screen counterpart, but this phenomenon is not unheard of. For some viewers, Netflix’s Addams Family spinoff Wednesday is the definitive screen take on the macabre clan, even though creator Tim Burton’s show is heavily influenced by the earlier 1990s live-action movies.

Similarly, even though 1994’s Anne Rice adaptation Interview with the Vampire featured an array of A-list stars including Brad Pitt, Tom Cruise, Antonio Banderas, and Kirsten Dunst, AMC’s two-season adaptation of the same Gothic romance novel is viewed by most fans and critics as the superior take on the story. With more screen time to devote to Louis and Lestat’s doomed, destructive love, the show develops a deeper understanding of the characters without losing its urgent pacing and dark sense of humor.

Likewise, Netflix’s under-seen From Dusk til Dawn: The Series is viewed by some as a stronger alternative to director Robert Rodriguez’s original 1996 thriller, especially for fans who felt that iconic characters like Salma Hayek’s Santanico Pandemonium were underused in the original movie. However, arguably the most impressive instance of a TV show outdoing its movie predecessors comes in Prime Video's Tom Clancy's Jack Ryan, a spy series that had to outclass five movies to become the franchise’s definitive screen outing.

Jack Ryan is the character at the core of author Tom Clancy’s Ryanverse, and the appeal of the eponymous CIA operative is easy to articulate. In a genre full of slick super-spy secret agents like James Bond, Jack Ryan is an average guy with a normal desk job who is thrust unexpectedly into the world of espionage and must rely on his wits and quick thinking to succeed in globe-trotting missions from the Czech Republic to Venezuela to the Middle East.

If the appeal of Prime Video’s Reacher is seeing an almost indestructible macho hero fight his way through criminal syndicates, then the appeal of Tom Clancy's Jack Ryan is seeing a far meeker, more unassuming everyman take on even bigger, more existentially threatening terrorist cells, foreign governments, and entire regimes. This appeal kept Clancy’s character on the bestseller list in all 12 Jack Ryan books, released between 1984 and 2003.

The first Jack Ryan novel from Clancy, 1984’s The Hunt for Red October, was made into a hit movie of the same name starring Alec Baldwin as Ryan, with supporting roles for Sean Connery, Sam Neill, and Gates McFadden. The second, Patriot Games, was made into a 1992 movie starring Harrison Ford as Ryan, and featuring Anne Archer and James Earl Jones.

Patriot Games was followed by 1994’s Clear and Present Danger, also starring Ford, before 2002’s The Sum of All Fears recast Jack Ryan with Ben Affleck in the role. 2014’s Jack Ryan: Shadow Recruit recast the character all over again, with Chris Pine taking over the role this time alongside supporting stars Keira Knightley and Kevin Costner.

With so many actors playing the role in five different movies, it would seem like Prime Video’s show had a lot to live up to when Tom Clancy's Jack Ryan debuted in 2018. However, across its four seasons and 2026’s spinoff movie Ghost War, the show justified its name choice. Krasinski truly is Clancy’s take on the character, as his definitive version of the role is arguably more impactful than all the previous theatrical movies.

Even though they had so much star power, none of the earlier blockbusters succeeded in capturing the everyman appeal of Clancy’s character. With more screen time to devote to the spy, Tom Clancy's Jack Ryan gave viewers the definitive take on Jack Ryan.
