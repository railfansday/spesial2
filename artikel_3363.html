Amelia Dimoldenberg Tears Up in Final Episode of ‘Chicken Shop Date’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Don’t cry because it’s over. Amelia Dimoldenberg aired the emotional final episode of her long-running YouTube interview series Chicken Shop Date, concluding the show by wiping away a few genuine tears.

Instead of enlisting a celebrity to join her on the series, Dimoldenberg opted to make acclaimed Belgian relationship therapist Esther Perel her guest for the finale. “For my final date, I wanted to do something different,” the host shared on social media. “I wanted to end Chicken Shop Date by showing another side of myself, in the most truthful way I could think of.”

For the episode, Dimoldenberg shed her usual persona and got real with Perel, explaining why she had decided to bring the show to its conclusion.

“I think I have to end the show to meet somebody,” Dimoldenberg told Perel. She added, “I definitely would love to meet someone that wasn’t a celebrity, if I’m being honest. It seems very intense to go out with a celebrity.”

Perel encouraged Dimoldenberg to imagine she is having a conversation with Chicken Shop Date itself, which leads to an emotional monologue and a tearful farewell.

“Thank you for allowing me to be where I am today,” Dimoldenberg told Perel as the show. “I’ve learned so much. I’ve grown up so much and I’ve had so much fun. But I feel now trapped in a cage of my own making and I’m ready to try something else. I never thought I would be able to say goodbye. I thought you would be part of my life forever.”

She added, “It was quite a shock to realize that doesn’t need to be the case. And now I feel kind of liberated in the farewell, in thinking there could be another path.”

Since 2014, Chicken Shop Date has shown the British hostess going on “dates” (read: cringy interviews) with Daniel Kaluuya, Ed Sheeran, Charli XCX, Phoebe Bridgers, Cher, Billie Eilish, Sabrina Carpenter, and many other celebrities. In September, she announced this would be the last season. “I never thought I’d stop Chicken Shop Date,” Dimoldenberg wrote. “I thought maybe it would be a part of me forever, and in some way it always will, but I feel like it’s now time to move on from this adventure and onto a new one.”

“To me, the show is my very own romcom and I have loved creating so many stories for you to watch,” she continued. “I am so grateful to everyone who has followed me on this journey and found some joy in these episodes.”
