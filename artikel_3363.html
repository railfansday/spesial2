Connor Storrie Sci-Fi Thriller ‘April X’ Sets HBO Max Streaming Release Date

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

April X, Michel K. Parandi’s psychological sci-fi thriller starring Heated Rivalry‘s Connor Storrie and Lily Krug (Plane) begins streaming on HBO Max on Friday, December 18. The film will premiere on HBO linear the following day, on Saturday, December 19 at 8 p.m. ET. April X was released theatrically in the UK on September 18 and in limited release in the U.S. on October 2.

The near future thriller follows twins Bax (Storrie) and April (Krug). When April goes missing, Bax searches every dark corner of the post Soviet cityscape trying to find her, ultimately descending into madness.

April X is Storrie’s first film vehicle to be released since his hugely popular romantic drama Heated Rivalry and rise to global stardom. Watch on Deadline

Cast also includes Tudor Chirila, Serban Pavlu, Tourialay Akbari, and Lulian Postelnicu.

The film is directed by Parandi, written by Michel K. Parandi and Jack Coulton, and produced by Lavinia Postolache and Michel K. Parandi. The executive producers are Melissa Schon, Lavinia Postolache, Alfred DeCarolis, Andrew Robl, Eddie Ting, Andrew Sasson, Berite Labelle, Tracy Paul and Ben Barenholtz. April X is produced by Lavinia Postolache under U.S. banner Lavi Star.
