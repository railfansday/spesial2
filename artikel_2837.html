PSG joins Arsenal in the race for Champions League star

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Nico Paz has emerged as one of the standout players in the Champions League this season, with the Argentinian playing a major role in Como's impressive performances against some of the top clubs in the competition.

Cesc Fabregas has done an excellent job since taking charge of the Italian side, and Paz has been one of the players responsible for their progress. The midfielder has established himself as an important member of the team and continues to demonstrate the quality that made him such a highly rated prospect.

Como signed Paz from Real Madrid's academy and has given him the opportunity to showcase his ability at the highest level. He has taken that opportunity impressively and is now regarded as one of the most sought-after players in Europe in his position.

Arsenal are among the clubs interested in signing him, with the Gunners hoping he will choose them when he eventually decides to leave Como. However, they are facing competition from several sides, and a new contender could make their pursuit considerably more difficult.

Paz has reportedly dreamed of returning to Real Madrid, but Arsenal are not the only club hoping to secure his signature. According to Fichajes, Paris Saint-Germain have now entered the race for the Argentinian, adding another major competitor to the transfer picture.

PSG's interest could complicate Arsenal's plans, particularly given their recent success in the Champions League. The French club have established itself as a major force in European football, and their involvement could give Paz another attractive option if he decides to leave Italy.

Several other clubs are also interested in the midfielder, meaning Arsenal could face a difficult battle to convince him to join their project.

Despite the growing interest, Paz is comfortable working under Fabregas at Como and is not in a hurry to change clubs. He continues to receive opportunities to develop and play an important role in the team's progress.

His situation means interested clubs may have to wait before making a serious attempt to sign him. Although a return to Real Madrid remains an ambition, PSG could offer an alternative capable of attracting the young midfielder.

Arsenal will therefore have to contend with increasing competition if they want to bring Paz to the Emirates.
