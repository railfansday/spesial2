UFC Qatar early weigh ins results | Prochazka vs. Stirling

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC) will stage its early and official weigh-ins at 9 a.m. local time for this weekend's UFC Qatar mixed martial arts (MMA) event on Paramount+. Unfortunately for stateside fight fans, Qatar is eight hours ahead of Eastern Standard Time, so most of us will be asleep by the time the first fighter hits the scale at 1 a.m. ET.

No worries, we got some West Coast homies to get the job done.

UFC Qatar will be headlined by a light heavyweight showdown between former champion Jiri Prochazka and undefeated contender Navajo Stirling, a five-round clash that follows the featherweight co-main event pitting former bantamweight champion Aljamain Sterling against Kevin Vallejos.
