Purdue soccer takes home six Big Ten honors after record-breaking season

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

A banner season for Purdue soccer resulted in six All-Big Ten honorees, including the league's top offensive award.

Junior Jaiden Anderson transferred from USC and in her first season with the Boilermakers set the program's single-season record with 18 goals.

That record-breaking mark led the Big Ten and earned her the conference's Forward of the Year, Purdue's first since Sarah Griffith in 2021.

In all, Purdue scored a program-record 66 goals, going 15-2-2 along the way, including nine conference wins, also a program record.

The team success helped highlight Anderson and five teammates. Anderson's first-team all-conference recognition was complemented by second-team All-Big Ten honorees Megan Santa Cruz and Irene Campo, both of whom contributed 9 goals and 6 assists each. McKenie Carle, who scored 8 goals to go with 4 assists, and defender Tanna Schornstein, who helped Purdue allow just 17 goals in 19 matches while also scoring 2 goals with 2 assists, were third-team All-Big Ten.

The Boilermakers are the No. 3 seed in the Big Ten Tournament and face Penn State in the first round on Thursday at Energizer Park in St. Louis.
