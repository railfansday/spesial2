Steelers Week 11 power rankings roundup: Pittsburgh slides after loss to Bengals

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Pittsburgh Steelers were embarrassed by the Cincinnati Bengals at Paycor Stadium on Sunday night. It was by far the Steelers' worst showing of the season, as their defense was shredded for 409 yards through the air while posting 222 total yards themselves. The Bengals gained sole possession of first place in the AFC North, putting the Steelers in a tough spot to repeat as AFC North champions.

As always, let's take a look around the internet to see where the Steelers fall on this week's power rankings roundup.

"In the words of former Steelers coach Mike Tomlin, speaking on the Sunday Night Football postgame show, "they got smashed" by the Bengals. They really did. They were smashed into a 31-3 hole in the third quarter before Aaron Rodgers and the offense showed a pulse. In fact, they had 23 total yards through three quarters, including minus-17 in the third quarter alone. And their defense wasn't any better. It was a good, old-fashioned butt-kicking. And it proved that the Bengals, not the Steelers, are far and away the power team in the AFC North this year." – Ralph Vacchiano

"The fifth-year running back was disappointing Sunday night, but so were all of the other Steelers. For the season, Warren has been great. He has 126 carries for 719 yards and leads all running backs with 5.7 yards per carry. He's second in the league in yards per carry after contact (4.04)." – Chad Graff and Josh Kendall

"The offense couldn't keep up with the Bengals as the defense was shredded by Joe Burrow. That was not a good look Sunday night." – Pete Prisco

"They're not ready to compete with the best teams in the conference." – Mike Florio

"I expected more from the Steelers on Sunday night. I knew the offense had its issues, but I did not expect them to allow the Bengals to cruise right through their defense, especially after Pittsburgh had the bye week to prepare for this massive AFC North showdown. It may have just been a bad day at the office, but I tend to believe it might also be a reminder of this team's flaws and overall limitations." – Nick Shook

"Was Sunday's outing just a bad night against a hot Bengals team or a bad sign for the Steelers going forward? It's OK to excuse it as a one-off. Before the 38-18 blowout loss, the Steelers were 6-2 and showing improvement. It's also OK to worry that Pittsburgh was exposed, though Aaron Rodgers didn't think so when asked if the loss changes the season's trajectory."

"That's very dramatic," Rodgers said, via the team's site. "I've been a part of this league for a long time. There's big wins, there's tough losses, so we'll deal with this properly."

"One thing that is an issue for the Steelers is their receiver room. DK Metcalf continued to have minimal impact, as he was held without a catch before garbage time in the fourth quarter. Germie Bernard was blanked and has just 57 yards in Pittsburgh's last three games. No other healthy receiver on the roster has more than 39 yards this season. Roman Wilson and Michael Pittman Jr. could both return in the next couple weeks, and that is badly needed." – Frank Schwab

Another chapter in this franchise's storied history, the Steelers now with the distinction of being the first team coming off a bye week to lose to an opponent coming back from a game in Europe. – Nate Davis

"Even after a quiet night in the Steelers' blowout loss to the Bengals, Warren is still far and away the Steelers' MVP. He ranks sixth among all players in yards from scrimmage (1,039) this season and has a league-best 5.7 yards per carry. Warren also ranks fifth in rushing yards after contact (364). Given Pittsburgh's inconsistent and ineffective passing game, Warren is the Steelers' most reliable option to move the ball." – Brooke Pryor

As humiliating as the loss was for the Steelers, it seems as though the national media is giving the Steelers some leeway. Of the eight publications we examined today, only two of them moved the Steelers multiple spots down in their rankings. Half of them kept the Steelers in the same spot. The Steelers are viewed as a good team that can't quite play with the top-of-the-line teams in the NFL, and that seems like a pretty spot-on evaluation at this point in the season.
