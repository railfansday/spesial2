Rangers rumors: The ‘issue’ in Texas’ possible Corey Seager trade push

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Texas Rangers could make one of the offseason's biggest moves by trading Corey Seager. However, even if the organization wants to part ways with its star shortstop, getting a deal done will require more than finding an interested team. Seager still has considerable control over where he plays next.

According to ESPN's Jeff Passan, the Rangers are open to moving Seager, who has reportedly shown some willingness to consider a trade. However, Passan noted that his interest is limited to a small number of destinations. "Seager has indicated an openness to moving, but it's to fewer than a handful of teams, and there is no current momentum on a deal," he reported.

That restriction presents a major obstacle for Texas. Seager has a full no-trade clause, meaning he can reject any proposed destination. The Rangers cannot simply send him to the highest bidder without his approval. Even if a team meets their asking price, Seager would still have the final say.

His contract could also complicate negotiations. The 32-year-old has five years remaining on his $325 million deal, carrying an average annual value of $31.5 million. Any potential trade would require a team willing to take on a significant financial commitment.

Still, moving Seager could give Texas more flexibility as it reshapes its roster for 2027. President of baseball operations Chris Young has indicated that the club expects a payroll similar to its 2026 figure, leaving limited room for major additions.

The Rangers could revisit discussions before the expected December 1 labor deadline. A deal is far from certain, but Corey Seager's limited willingness to waive his clause gives the possibility some life.
