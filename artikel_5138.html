Tata Steel shares touched a 52-week low of ₹159.75 on Wednesday before recovering about 1% to an intraday high of ₹164.65 on BSE, as investors weighed the cost of its push into APL Apollo Tubes. The steelmaker said it has completed the purchase of a 9.91% stake in APL Apollo for about ₹5,825 crore through a block deal on BSE on November 23. It bought 2,77,38,075 shares at ₹2,100 each from S Gupta Holding, part of APL Apollo’s promoter group. The stake is part of a larger plan to take control of the steel pipe maker, which also involves moving Tata Steel’s tubes business to APL Apollo for ₹11,700 crore and selling its stake in Tata Steel Colors for ₹16,300 crore. Tata Steel shares are down 8% in a week and 22% over six months.
