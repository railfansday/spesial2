Worried About Nvidia? 3 Artificial Intelligence (AI) Stocks That May Be Safer Buys Right Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

These stocks have tremendous growth potential due to AI, modest valuations, and have less risk than Nvidia these days.

Nvidia has been in a tailspin over the past week as the uncertainty around who will run its business now that Jensen Huang has resigned as president and CEO is weighing on its valuation. In just the past month, it has declined by nearly 30%. It's an unnerving situation, and there are no guarantees things will get better in the weeks ahead.

For artificial intelligence (AI) investors, there are three tech stocks that may be more attractive options to consider right now: Alphabet (GOOG -1.38%)(GOOGL -1.32%), Amazon (AMZN -0.07%), and Broadcom (AVGO +1.78%). Here's why I'd go with these stocks today, rather than Nvidia.

Alphabet is an underrated tech stock that I don't think the market is valuing fairly. Given how diverse its business is and how many opportunities it has related to AI, it's a bit surprising to see it trail Nvidia in valuation.

While Alphabet is among the most valuable companies in the world, its market cap of $3.4 trillion remains lower than Nvidia's $3.7 trillion valuation.

The company has a dominant position in online search (Google) and video sharing (YouTube), plus exciting opportunities related to robotaxis (Waymo) and AI chatbots (Gemini).

There's so much potential for Alphabet that it can tap into related to AI that it virtually makes it a no-brainer buy, especially when you consider its modest valuation. At a forward price-to-earnings (P/E) multiple of a little under 20, this is a stock that trades in line with the average S&P 500 stock. Except, Alphabet is clearly not average, and it's worthy of a far higher premium.

Another AI stock that I believe should be worth more is Amazon. At $2.8 trillion in market cap, its valuation is far lower than Alphabet's. It too is a stock that may have far more room to rise higher.

Amazon's business dominates e-commerce and sets the trend for fast shipping speeds. It's shown that it can fend off low-cost competition by offering its own competitive service, Amazon Haul, to help appeal to price-conscious shoppers.

Its business continues to perform well, with Amazon reporting 12% revenue growth for the September quarter and its cloud business, Amazon Web Services, performing even better with a growth rate of 39%.

Amazon's been getting into the chip-building business and may one day carve out some decent market share for itself. CEO Andy Jassy said if it were a stand-alone operation, it could be bringing in $50 billion in annual sales. Amazon, however, could likely scale that significantly higher.

This is another cheap-looking stock worth buying right now, as its forward P/E multiple is just 24. That's higher than Alphabet, but it still offers great value for the growth opportunities the business possesses.

Chipmaker Broadcom is another stock that I think is a good buy. I haven't always felt that way in the past, as I felt its valuation was too inflated. However, with the stock declining in recent months, I believe there's much more value here now, and it's a much safer investment with less downside risk.

Broadcom builds custom chips that have been in high demand amid the AI tech build-out. Its operations aren't as diverse as Amazon's or Alphabet's, but given how well the business has been doing, it's hard not to like Broadcom's stock.

In its most recent quarter, which ended on Aug. 2, revenue rose by 86% year over year, totaling $29.6 billion. And its growth rate is expected to rise to 93% for the last quarter of its fiscal year. It's a growth beast, and with demand for custom chips on the rise, this could be one of the best AI stocks to own today.

Broadcom's stock is down 2% this year, but it's fallen more than 30% from its 52-week high of $495. It's trading at a forward P/E of 18, making this another attractive stock to buy and hold.
