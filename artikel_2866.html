2. Bundesliga: Bayern loanee Chávez helps Magdeburg to huge win as Bielefeld’s unbeaten home record come to an end

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Despite the hosts dominating the first ten minutes, Magdeburg took the lead with their first chance. FC Bayern loanee Felipe Chávez scored his first 2. Bundesliga goal after a brilliant one-two with Moritz-Broni Kwarteng. Including against his parent club in the cup, the Peruvian previously had three assists for Magdeburg.

Fellow summer signing Emmanuel Iyoha had the next chance for the visitors as they settled into the game. The fourth-placed side did very little until the last action of the half, which resulted in their second goal of the night. Iyoha converted his opportunity to a league-high seventh goal after a brilliant through pass from Magdeburg's assist king Barış Atik.

Bielefeld started the second-half with a couple of unsuccessful shouts for a penalty-kick. At the hour mark, their goalkeeper Jonas Kersken was taken off after a collision with Mateusz Żukowski. Former Borussia Dortmund midfielder Oliver Kirch also brought Leo Oppermann and Benjamin Boakye with triple substitutions.

A few seconds later, the latter halved the deficit with his first touch. The hosts nearly leveled the score almost immediately, but Dominik Reimann made a great save to deny Joel Grodowski, who also has seven goals to his name.

The visitors resorted to defending their lead for the rest of the half as Reimann saw a yellow card for time wasting. However, the 29-year-old barely tested in the closing stage of the game as Bielefeld failed to build on their momentum.

The hosts mounted the pressure once again in injury-time with a couple of chances for Isaiah Young as the US striker also felt undone by a refereeing decision that denied Bielefeld a late free-kick. Kirch's men lost at home for the first time as there was a huge protest aimed at the referee Florian Lechner after the final whistle.

Magdeburg momentarily climbed to third in the standings before Kaiserslautern's game against Dynamo Dresden on Sunday afternoon.

The two teams will not be in action for the next nine days as their matchday 13 fixtures will be played on Sunday. Greuther Fürth will host Bielefeld, while high-flying Magdeburg entertain newly-promoted Energie Cottbus.
