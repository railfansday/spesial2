HoYoverse Announces Cosy Galactic Life Sim 'Petit Planet' For Switch 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

HoYoverse, the company known for games like Genshin Impact and Zenless Zone Zero, has announced it's bringing the cosy free-to-play galactic life sim Petit Planet to the Switch 2 in 2027.

It shared news about the Nintendo version of the game at the end of its latest "pre-launch special program" broadcast. Petit Planet is launching for other platforms on 4th December 2026.

You can see the Switch 2 version in motion around the 28 minute mark in the video above. Here's the announcement:

"We're excited to announce that Petit Planet is coming to Nintendo Switch 2 in 2027. We can't wait to welcome to you the Starsea."

Petit Planet allows you to make your planet your own, live alongside fuzzy neighbours, farm, fish, cook, and even explore the vast Starsea, beyond your planet. Here's an official description and trailer:

"Your Little Planet. Your Own Universe. Petit Planet is HoYoverse's cozy galactic life sim, available as a free download on multiple platforms. Make a little planet your own, live alongside fuzzy Neighbors, and discover cozy wonders beneath the stars.Step into the Starsea and make a little planet your own. Shape the land and skies, befriend fuzzy Neighbors, explore new worlds, and visit a bustling bazaar where galactic travelers gather. Create, discover, build lasting friendships, and watch as your own planet begins to teem with life."
