Watford vs Blackburn Rovers predictions with ‘problems’ expected to continue despite Dionisi sacking

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Watford head into this midweek Championship clash with Blackburn Rovers sitting 21st in the table. The Hornets have collected just 15 points from 16 matches, winning four, drawing three and losing nine. Their struggles continued last time out with a heavy 4-0 defeat at Sheffield United, which led to Alessio Dionisi’s sacking on Sunday.

There have been more encouraging signs at Vicarage Road, though. Watford are unbeaten in three of their last four home league matches, including a 1-0 victory over Lincoln City and a goalless draw against league leaders Middlesbrough.

Blackburn Rovers sit in 13th place with 22 points from 16 matches, having won six, drawn four and lost six. Rovers responded to Tony Mowbray stepping away from first-team duties on health grounds with an encouraging 2-2 draw against Burnley on Sunday, twice coming from behind in the derby.

They have collected 17 points from their last 10 league outings and remain comfortably clear of the relegation zone, although their away record remains a concern, with just one victory on the road this season.

“Watford’s home performances suggest they could make this a difficult evening for Blackburn. Their defensive record at Vicarage Road has been encouraging, particularly against strong opposition, but Rovers have demonstrated greater attacking quality throughout the campaign.

“Andri Gudjohnsen is the obvious player to watch. The Blackburn Rovers striker has scored 11 goals and provided one assist in 15 Championship appearances, accumulating 1,032 minutes and emerging as one of Europe’s most clinical forwards.

“His finishing has been particularly remarkable, with those 11 goals coming from just 4.35 expected goals and 24 shots, including 13 on target. He has also created eight chances, highlighting his growing influence in the final third.

“Watford should make life uncomfortable for Rovers on home soil, but Gudjohnsen’s exceptional goalscoring form could prove decisive. I am backing the visitors to edge a competitive encounter.”

“Once again, Watford are changing boss. Edward Iordanescu looks set to take the reins, and although it remains to be seen if he’s in charge on Wednesday, he will be eager to make an immediate impact. But, everyone knows the problems at Vicarage Road run far deeper than the manager.

“There’s attacking talent to get more out of like Iker Bravo and Othmane Maamma, but the midfield isn’t creative and the defence regularly concedes chances. Against players like Andri Gudjohnsen and Todd Cantwell, the hosts simply have to tighten up defensively, or they will be punished by Blackburn Rovers.

“The away side are a really united one right now. Tony Mowbray had forged real cohesion after his summer arrival, and his withdrawal from head coach duties feels like it has only brought the group closer. Away form is a bit of a weakness though, with only one win in eight on the road this term.

“Despite that, I am leaning towards a Rovers win. I think this game will show just what a big task awaits Iordanescu in the Watford dugout.”
