Top 9 friendliest EFL League Two fanbases named and ranked by AI

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Football fandom can shine through in many different ways with many clubs throughout Europe and beyond taking pride in creating a hostile and difficult atmosphere for their opponents.

Hooliganism, born in England with the so-called ‘English disease’ is something that can get confused with that desire for hostility and an intimidatory atmosphere but it must be said English football and the passion of its supporters in that regard has dwindled in the last 30 years or so.

Regardless of that, though, the sheer depth of the English football pyramid and large crowds as low as the eighth and ninth tiers, unparalleled across the world, is something that remains a source of pride for English football supporters.

Attracting huge crowds lower down the leagues can be reliant upon a variety of factors but a crucial couple involve the welcoming and community-like nature of a fanbase.

The fourth-tier is the best mix of English football with some fairly big spending and ambitious ‘new’ clubs in the EFL that have risen up from non-league, old and storied outfits wanting to restore their reputation and teams punching above their weight by even being in England’s top four divisions.

As a result, we have consulted AI chatbot ChatGPT to rank the nine friendliest fanbases in League Two. It could even be that many clubs pride themselves on having a more unwelcoming and hostile atmosphere, too, to try and rattle the opposition – and so this may not be the most overly positive ranking that it first appears, albeit all of that and what it feels and how it is perceived also remains subjective.

First up on the list, the ninth friendliest fanbase in League Two, according to AI, are Cheltenham Town’s supporters. Down in Gloucestershire, The Robins have been deemed to have such a ‘relaxed’ and ‘friendly match day experience’ that they are among the friendliest fans in the fourth-tier.

Under the management of Steve Cotterill, Cheltenham will be expecting to be steady enough by the spring that they can have a tilt at the top seven and the play-off places. The Gloucestershire-based outfit have reconnected with supporters after a difficult couple of years.

In the heart of Lancashire, it may be stereotypical for Accrington Stanley supporters to be the archetypal friendly Lancastrians but they are ranked eighth on the list by AI. A fun away day, the ‘close knit community’, as described by ChatGPT, marks Stanley out as having one of the friendliest fanbases in League Two.

A look at the 25 biggest clubs in England according to AI chatbot ChatGPT.

Accrington have punched above their weight pretty much throughout their history and they are again doing so this season with a fun to watch team in England’s fourth-tier. The openness of owner Andy Holt has also helped Accrington form their connection with supporters, albeit some did become critical of Holt’s outspoken nature on social media.

Once again up in the North West for ChatGPT’s list, Crewe Alexandra have been deemed the seventh friendliest fanbase in League Two with the AI chatbot describing The Alex has having a ‘community feel’ and a ‘welcoming atmosphere’. It is often another fun away day, too, with Gresty Road right by Crewe train station, hence their The Railwaymen nickname.

Having been League Two play-off finalists in the 2023-24 campaign, Crewe look to be back towards the level of that team this time around with Lee Bell’s side pushing for promotion once again.

Whether travelling from below the midlands north or vice versa, the Bescot Stadium is famous for its ubiquity in terms of being spotted on a longer away day for supporters. The home of Walsall has also been marked out as the home of the sixth friendliest fanbase in League Two with AI suggesting The Saddlers boast a family-friendly fanbase.

Walsall supporters remained patient in recent years with successive collapses away from promotion, having been top at Christmas in both of the last two seasons, and they have been rewarded with another strong side but nothing can be taken for granted.

Back in the EFL, York City have been inserted as the fifth friendliest fanbase in League Two according to AI. The Minstermen, now at the York Community Stadium since 2021 after moving from Bootham Crescent, have managed to maintain a ‘passionate fanbase’ despite that move, as suggested by ChatGPT, and that has them in the top five for the chatbot.

Like many clubs coming straight up from the National League in recent seasons, York are putting themselves in the promotion picture in League Two with that fervent fanbase behind them.

Ranked fourth in ChatGPT’s list of friendliest fanbases, Chesterfield are up next. According to AI, The Spireites have ‘strong connection’ with their supporters and the local community and ‘excellent support’, as it explains, marks it out as one of the friendlier groups of supporters – and being able to see the famous crooked spire from the away end is also a bit of a boon.

National League winners in the 2023-24 campaign, Chesterfield have been play-off semi-finalists in each of the last two seasons with a now patient Chesterfield fanbase hoping to take the next step this time around.

Heading into the top three, ChatGPT has ranked Merseyside outfit Tranmere Rovers as having one of the friendliest fanbases in League Two. Described as being a ‘traditional club’ by AI, Tranmere are said to have a ‘welcoming culture’ that has the chatbot putting them third in this list.

Tranmere are a steadily run club and will hope to punch themselves clear of the fourth-tier this season, having survived relegation on the final day last season, as they push towards the top seven and the play-off places.

Up to second on the list of the friendliest fanbases in League Two are Grimsby Town – which Manchester United supporters may disagree with given the way in which The Mariners fans managed to help their side overwhelm and eventually produce one of the EFL Cup’s biggest upsets last season. Those ‘passionate’ fans, as explained by AI, are described as being able to create a ‘fantastic atmosphere’ with Grimsby’s travelling support also given as a reason for Town being so high on the list.

Dave Artell is one of the most well-regarded coaches in the EFL and, after missing out in the League Two play-off semi-finals last season, Grimsby will be hopeful of pushing themselves into the third-tier this year with the backing of an impressive fanbase, both in terms of their support and attitude – according to AI.

And now we come to the top of the list with AI chatbot ChatGPT deeming Gillingham to have the friendliest fanbase in League Two. According to ChatGPT, Gillingham are described as having ‘loyal supporters’ with a ‘strong community’ between the club and players marking them out as top spot in this list in Kent, a county self-anointed as ‘England’s back garden’.

Many away supporters may find it a surprise that Gillingham rank so high on this list with the narrow streets leading up to the Priestfield Stadium occasionally presenting quite an intimidatory build up if circumstances dictate, perhaps on a game-by-game basis. AI has opted not to take that into consideration in its ranking though.

Gills have started steadily and will want to find themselves in the hunt for a promotion challenge again in the fourth-tier, under the management of Gareth Ainsworth, and they will hope to be one of League One’s friendliest fanbases next season, well, at least one of the friendliest fanbases in the eyes of an AI chatbot next season.
