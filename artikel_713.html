‘The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives’ Star Jen Affleck Files for Divorce from Zac Affleck

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

“The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives” star Jen Affleck has filed for divorce from Zac Affleck after seven years of marriage, according to People.

The news comes ahead of the premiere of the “Secret Lives” spin-off, “The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County,” on Nov. 12, in which Affleck stars.
