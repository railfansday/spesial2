Knotch Launches Ace for Agents to Help Brands Compete in the Age of AI Search & Personal Agents

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A new end-to-end solution for LLM visibility, GEO/AEO and personal Agent communication, as Scott Brinker predicts traditional websites will disappear by 2030

NEW YORK, Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Knotch, the AI experience infrastructure company, today announced the launch of Ace for Agents, a new solution within its Agentic Content Engine (Ace) that helps enterprises make their existing content more accessible, understandable, and actionable for AI search and personal agents. As AI increasingly influences how consumers discover and evaluate brands, Ace for Agents enables companies to optimize their digital content for AI systems without rebuilding their websites or disrupting the experience for human visitors.

The launch is accompanied by a new whitepaper from Scott Brinker, the marketing technology analyst behind chiefmartec, developed in partnership with Knotch. In The Future of Websites: A Brand Context Model to Power Human & Agent Web Experiences, Brinker predicts that by 2030, the traditional website as we know it will have disappeared, replaced by dynamic digital experiences that respond to the needs of both human visitors and AI agents acting on their behalf.

"AI won't make brand sites irrelevant. It will make them more strategically important than ever, even as traditional web pages fade away," said Brinker. "By 2030, the website as we've known it, a predetermined experience everyone has to navigate, will have disappeared. Instead, websites will dynamically assemble experiences around what each visitor wants to accomplish."

"The digital-native era was built on pages, structure, and templates. The AI-native era is built on data, context, and experience," said Anda Gansca, Co-founder and CEO of Knotch. "Brands now serve two customers: people, and the AI agents acting on their behalf. Both need the brand's knowledge, not its page layout. Ace turns a brands existing content into data, so it can be delivered to an agent as a citable answer and to a person as an experience built around what they came to do."

Brinker's whitepaper identifies several fundamental shifts in enterprise digital strategy. With Ace for Agents, Knotch is already addressing key elements of the transformation to an AI-native infrastructure. .

As AI reshapes how consumers discover, evaluate, and interact with brands, the enterprise website is evolving from a destination customers navigate into a source of knowledge that can serve them wherever decisions are made. Ace for Agents gives enterprises a way to begin that transition today, ensuring their content can be understood and used by AI systems while maintaining control over how their brands are represented.

Knotch is the AI experience infrastructure that Helps Brands Win Every Audience. As AI reshapes how consumers discover, research, and engage, Ace makes brands AI-Native, so they can seamlessly adapt to every audience in real time, whether that's an AI system crawling for answers or a consumer arriving with sharper intent than ever before.
