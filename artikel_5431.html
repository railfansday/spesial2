Better adds 3 brokerages to platform, lifts cost savings to $38.2M

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Better said its wholesale platform added 3 mortgage brokerages, reaching 46 companies and 1,801 brokers in total with $517M in trailing 12-month volume. The firm reported $38.2M in annualized cost savings under its Better 2.0 plan and outlined new HELOC and capital markets initiatives.  AI Summary

Better Home & Finance reported in its sixth weekly scorecard that it added three mortgage brokerages to its wholesale platform and increased its annualized cost savings to $38.2 million as it continues its turnaround effort under the Better 2.0 plan.

The company said Tuesday that its wholesale platform now includes 46 brokerages and 1,801 brokers, representing $517 million in trailing 12-month origination volume. Better also reported an additional $4.1 million in annualized savings since its previous weekly update, bringing it closer to its $64.2 million target.

The latest savings include $1.1 million from mortgage operations efficiencies and approximately $3 million from marketing expense reductions. Better said it expects to generate another $940,000 in annualized savings through additional efficiency initiatives between Nov. 17 and Nov. 24.

The company also reported progress in its wholesale home equity line of credit business. Customer testing of its AI-powered HELOC with a large existing partner has been completed, and partnership volume continues to expand.

November brokerage locks for the product had already doubled October’s full-month total within the first 16 days of the month, said Vishal Garg, Better’s founder and head of product, platform and innovation.

“Brokers are looking for a faster, simpler way to help their clients tap home equity, and they’re finding it on our platform,” Garg said.

Better also said it is developing products tied to previously announced partnerships with a rental credit management platform and a home inspection company. It is advancing discussions with a prospective lead generation partner, with terms expected to be finalized in the coming weeks, while reportedly pursuing three other major partnerships.

On the capital markets front, Better launched an options strategy within its existing share repurchase program. The strategy allows the company to purchase call options and sell put options on its Class A stock.

The company is also continuing discussions with prospective financing partners after receiving five term sheets for approximately $50 million in debt financing tied to the planned sale of Birmingham Bank, its U.K. bank subsidiary.

Better said it will continue publishing its weekly management scorecard until it reaches adjusted EBITDA breakeven.
