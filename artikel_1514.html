Europe’s top 10 chance creators in 2026-27: Real Madrid starlet leads, Yamal third

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Stars from Roma, Barcelona and Real Madrid feature prominently among Europe’s leading chance creators so far in the 2026-27 season, alongside some surprise names from across the continent.

We’ve taken a look at the 10 players who have registered the most key passes (the final pass before a team-mate shoots at goal) across Europe’s top five leagues this season.

Note: If two players are tied on the number of key passes, we’ve listed them in order of superior key passes per minute ratio.

The experienced Germany international looks like he should be playing the villain in a low-budget action film.

In this realm, Raum is making life miserable for Bundesliga defenders. The RB Leipzig left-back has registered 26 key passes in just seven appearances, averaging one every 24 minutes.

An outrageous return, really. How he is yet to notch an assist yet this season is beyond us.

This season it’s been more about chance creation – and he’s only taken eight games to get into this top 10, considerably fewer than most of the other names he’s brushing shoulders with.

Now in his second season with Atletico Madrid, Baena only has two assists to show for his 24 key passes, but can take solace from scoring five goals for himself.
