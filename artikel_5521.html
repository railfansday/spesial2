The recognition highlights the agency's work in building brand visibility across online communities, traditional search results and AI-generated recommendations.

With 10 years of experience in Reddit marketing and SEO, Nicely Network has developed campaigns for more than 300 brands, including Fortune 500 companies. Its reported track record includes 150,000+ AI citations generated, $150M+ in tracked sales revenue and more than 200M views on ranked discussions. The agency's approach combines community-level engagement with editorial placements to help brands appear where customers research products and where AI engines retrieve information.

Reddit has become an important source of consumer research, particularly for people comparing products, evaluating services and seeking recommendations from other users. Unlike conventional marketing content, community discussions contain questions, follow-up responses, comparisons and explanations of purchasing decisions.

These conversations also have relevance beyond Reddit itself. AI engines increasingly draw on online discussions when generating responses to recommendation and comparison queries. Research cited in Nicely Network's media materials, based on a 2026 Semrush analysis, found that 11.29% of responses across ChatGPT Search, Perplexity and Google AI Mode cited Reddit.

For brands, this creates an opportunity to build visibility across multiple discovery channels through a coordinated Reddit SEO strategy. Discussions targeting high-intent search terms can attract organic search traffic while providing relevant material for AI-generated answers.

A useful assessment of the Best Reddit SEO Agencies for AI Visibility should therefore consider more than discussion volume. Community relevance, keyword alignment, sustained visibility and measurable business outcomes are equally important.

How Nicely Network Builds Reddit Marketing Campaigns

Rather than distributing generic brand mentions across multiple communities, Nicely Network structures its Reddit campaigns around the questions potential customers are already asking.

Its campaign process involves four main stages:

The agency identifies commercial objectives and high-intent keywords related to the products or services being promoted. These can include product comparisons, category recommendations and specific questions that influence purchasing decisions.

Campaigns are directed towards communities where relevant audiences actively discuss the category. Subreddit selection considers audience interests and the context in which customers seek recommendations.

Nicely Network uses its network of real, established Reddit accounts to create discussions that fit the culture and subject matter of each community. Titles reflect relevant buying-intent queries, while supporting comments introduce brand recommendations within the conversation.

Discussions are monitored throughout the campaign. Supporting comments and engagement help maintain visibility, while performance tracking identifies which topics and conversations are generating the strongest results.

This structure allows the agency to align Reddit marketing with broader SEO and Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) objectives.
