Recap: Wedgewood superb in 4-0 shutout of Ducks

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Colorado Avalanche delivered a strong performance at Ball Arena on Thursday night, defeating the Anaheim Ducks 4-0 behind a two-goal performance from Brock Nelson and a 25-save shutout from Scott Wedgewood.

With Nathan MacKinnon and Cale Makar sidelined by injuries, Colorado needed contributions from throughout the lineup, and several players stepped up in a big way.

One of those being Brock Nelson as he continued his hot streak, recording two goals and an assist in the victory. The veteran forward now has four goals in his last five games, providing a much-needed offensive spark for an Avalanche team navigating injuries to two of its biggest stars.

It was a cautious at times scoreless opening period in which the Avalanche outshot the Ducks 9-4, however, Colorado wasted little time getting on the scoreboard in the second period.

Just eight seconds into the second frame, the Avalanche capitalized on an Anaheim turnover following the face-off. Nazem Kadri made a quick play, dishing the puck to Nicolas Roy, who slipped into the low slot undetected. Roy finished with a backhand past Ducks goaltender Lukas Dostal to give Colorado a 1-0 lead in one of the quickest goals to start a period this season.

The goal provided an immediate spark and set the tone for Colorado's dominant second period.

Nelson got in on the action scoring on the power play at 3:25 of the second period, finishing a beautiful passing sequence with a one-timer from the slot. T.J. Hughes delivered the feed, and Nelson made no mistake, doubling Colorado's lead.

After a couple lackluster power plays in the first period, this time Colorado moved the puck beautifully, executing a tic-tac-toe passing sequence that ended with T.J. Hughes feeding Nelson for a one-timer from the slot. Nelson buried the opportunity, showcasing the precision and chemistry that Colorado's special teams have been looking for.

Nearly seven minutes later, Nelson struck again with a terrific snipe, showcasing his scoring touch and helping the Avalanche take control of the game up 3-0 at that point.

The contest was all but over in the third period but Devon Toews also contributed offensively, recording a goal to go along with an earlier assist. That tally came at the midpoint of the period helping Colorado put the game firmly out of reach and at the final score of 4-0.

Scott Wedgewood was outstanding between the pipes, stopping all 25 shots he faced to secure the shutout. The performance was particularly impressive considering Wedgewood had left practice early in recent days after feeling under the weather. Despite the concern surrounding his health, he was ready to go when the puck dropped and delivered a composed, reliable performance.

Adam Beckman, who was recalled earlier in the day, made his Avalanche debut against Anaheim and showed flashes of what he could bring to the lineup. Before the halfway point of the first period, Beckman had a promising scoring opportunity. Logan O'Connor delivered a slick pass, allowing Beckman to fly into the offensive zone and fire a shot toward the net. Unfortunately, Ducks goaltender Lukas Dostal denied him with the blocker.

A lengthy break of five days and then a rematch with the Montreal Canadiens at Ball Arena on Wednesday, November 25th. Puck drop is at 7:00 p.m.
