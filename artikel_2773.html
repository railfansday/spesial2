Patriots fans would start Tommy DeVito at quarterback

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The New England Patriots will have a new face under center when they return from their Week 11 bye week.

After starting quarterback Drake Maye suffered a fractured fibula in last week's loss to the Detroit Lions, he will be sidelined for an extended period and perhaps the rest of the season.

In Maye's absence last Sunday, the Patriots handed things over to backup quarterback Tommy DeVito. While DeVito struggled in relief, head coach Mike Vrabel expressed confidence in the 28-year old moving forward and said the team did not have current plans to add another quarterback.

That is where things still stand now, as the Patriots have not signed a quarterback during the bye week. That sets the stage for DeVito to start next Sunday in Los Angeles, a move Patriots fan agree with.

In the latest SB Nation Reacts survey, 41% of participants said that DeVito should be the starter moving forward.

All in all, this week's survey results look as follows:

While DeVito was the leading vote getter, 25% of fans would prefer to turn things over to rookie Behren Morton. 24% of voters would then go sign former Patriot Jimmy Garoppolo to start in place of Maye, while 10% voted for another option, although voting was done before the Indianapolis Colts released Daniel Jones.

Maye's absence coupled with back-to-back blowout losses has expectedly hit fan confidence, with just 11% of fans confident that the team is now headed in the right direction.

The injury has also hit the Patriots odds of hoisting the Lombardi Trophy this season. The Patriots are now tied with the 20th best odds to win the Super Bowl on FanDuel Sportsbook.
