Avoid your family, catch the game: Rams release more Thanksgiving Eve tickets

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Los Angeles Rams host the Green Bay Packers at SoFi Stadium on Wednesday.

In honor of the Thanksgiving Eve matchup, the Rams have opened up more tickets for fans in the Standing Room Only sections of the stadium. President of team and media operations Kevin Demoff took to social media to share the news.

Matthew Stafford #9 of the Los Angeles Rams celebrates with Puka Nacua #12 after a touchdown pass against the Carolina Panthers on Jan. 10, 2026, in Charlotte. Getty Images

"For @RamsNFL fans who are trying to avoid family for one more day, or if you don't want to run into your high school ex at the bar, we've opened up more Standing Room Only tickets for tonight to join us @SoFiStadium for Thanksgiving Eve football!" Demoff wrote in a post on X.

Wednesday's game marks the first-ever Thanksgiving Eve matchup at SoFi Stadium. Tailgating in Inglewood will begin at 1 p.m. with a 5 p.m. kickoff.

Los Angeles Rams quarterback Matthew Stafford (9) throws a pass during the third quarter against the Green Bay Packers at SoFi Stadium. USA TODAY Network via Reuters Connect

Fans taking Demoff up on his offer can skip the family gathering and still get their turkey fix. SoFi's Thanksgiving menu includes smoked turkey legs with cranberry barbecue sauce and turkey pot pie. For those who'd rather wear the bird, a limited number of Rams turkey hats will be available starting at 3 p.m.

The Rams will also debut their "Fearsome White" uniforms in honor of the Fearsome Foursome, the revered Rams defensive line of the 1960s, featuring Deacon Jones, Lamar Lundy, Merlin Olsen, and Rosey Grier.

Grier, the only living member from the unit, will be in attendance for Wednesday's game.

Los Angeles Rams mascot and team cheerleaders hand out turkeys and Thanksgiving meal essentials during a drive-through food distribution held in partnership with the Los Angeles Regional Food Bank. AFP via Getty Images

Irish rock band U2 will perform at the Thanksgiving Eve halftime show. The Rams and Packers enter Wednesday's game with the same record at 6-4.

Given the loaded NFC West, a short-handed Los Angeles squad will have to do its very best to execute against Green Bay.
