Who was the Murfreesboro area TSSAA athlete of the week for Nov. 9-14?

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Who was the Murfreesboro area TSSAA athlete of the week for Nov. 9-14?

Corbin Garrett, Cannon County football: Was 16-of-18 passing for 273 yards and three touchdowns in a 34-20 win over Tellico Plains in the Class 2A state playoffs.

Cayden Maloney, Eagleville football: Had 13 carries for 89 yards and a touchdown and added a 29-yard TD reception in a 29-27 loss to Greenfield in the 1A playoffs.

Cooper Jones, MTCS football: Had 13 tackles and a forced fumble in a 23-13 loss to USJ in the DII-A playoffs.

Jacob Vaughn, Oakland football: Had 14 carries for 175 yards and two touchdowns and added a 52-yard reception in a 22-21 loss to Maryville in the second round of the Class 6A playoffs.

Nate Lamb, PCA football: Passed for 96 yards and rushed for 15 yards and a touchdown in a 38-7 loss to USJ in the DII-A quarterfinals.

Gavin Johnson, Riverdale football: Had three receptions for 93 yards and two touchdowns in a 32-6 win over Science Hill in the 6A playoffs.

Yonni Morales, Smyrna: Had two interceptions, including one returned for a touchdown, in a 48-6 win over Dobyns-Bennett in the 6A playoffs.

The poll will close at 2 p.m. on Nov. 19.
