Lions quietly had $60 million clause put in Aidan Hutchinson’s contract before devastating injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Lions made sure they didn't leave themselves exposed when they signed Aidan Hutchinson to a $180 million extension last year.

In the aftermath of Hutchinson's season ending after he tore his ACL celebrating a sack Sunday, the Lions are set to receive salary cap relief due to injury insurance.

The four-year pact includes a clause protecting against injuries in the 2026-28 seasons up to $60 million, according to the Detroit Free Press.

Aidan Hutchinson of the Detroit Lions falls with an injury while celebrating a sack in Munich, Germany, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. Getty Images

The Lions reportedly were allowed to insure up to $45 million for injuries suffered between May 31, 2026 and May 31, 2027, including $12 million of his $15 million signing bonus, his $28.41 million option bonus that registered earlier this year and his $1.2 million base salary for this season.

That insurance can ultimately help the Lions since teams can receive future cap credits for insurance payments that cover injured players' contracts, per the Free Press.

The outlet projects the credit for the remainder of the 2026 season would be more than $5 million.

The Lions (7-2) have $32.9 million available in cap space for this season and are projected for $36.6 million in cap space in 2027 before any credits could be applied, per overthecap.com.

Teams insuring mega contracts like Hutchinson's is a common practice across sports leagues but it's not a given.

Aidan Hutchinson of the Detroit Lions falls with an injury in Munich, Germany, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026, during an NFL game against the New England Patriots. Getty Images

The Lions, for example, reportedly did not insure quarterback Jared Goff's or linebacker Jack Campbell's extension in recent years, according to the Free Press.

"It's becoming more and more expensive, I think, because the insurance companies have had to pay out on a few of these things," ex-Lions president Rod Wood said last year, per the outlet. "So it gets into a financial decision about how much. It's like any other insurance, you don't ever want to have to collect on it, but the premiums are real expensive while you're waiting to collect on it."

Hutchinson is a sensational talent but has struggled to stay on the field over the last three seasons.

His 2024 season ended after five games due to a fractured left tibia and fibula, and he appeared in all nine games this season before being shut down.

Hutchinson had six sacks for the NFC North leaders, and they face the Buccaneers on Sunday.
