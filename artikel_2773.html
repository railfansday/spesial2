Christian Watson ruled out with a concussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Packers wide receiver Christian Watson will not return for the second half of Wednesday night's game against the Rams.

Watson went to the locker room for a concussion evaluation shortly before halftime and the Packers announced that he has been ruled out at the start of the second half. Watson had four catches for 65 yards and a touchdown prior to his injury.

The Rams also lost safety Kamren Kinchens for the rest of the game on the same play. Kinchens was ejected for a helmet-to-helmet hit on Watson.

Rams tight end Davis Allen has also been ruled out with an ankle injury.

The two teams went to the break tied 13-13 and that remains the score early in the third quarter.
