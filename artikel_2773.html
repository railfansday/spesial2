Luke Schoonmaker questionable for Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Dallas Cowboys TE Luke Schoonmaker (illness) is considered questionable to play in Week 11's game versus the Tennessee Titans after missing the team's final two practice sessions of the week.

Why It Matters: Regardless of whether or not Schoonmaker plays, the Cowboys will already be shorthanded at tight end because Jake Ferguson has already been ruled out for this weekend with knee and ankle injuries. If Schoonmaker does suit up, he could serve as a backup to TE Brevyn Spann-Ford but is not a recommended fantasy lineup option.
