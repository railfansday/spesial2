Brittany Mahomes goes off on refs in Instagram Story rant after Chiefs-Bills clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Kansas City Chiefs were served humble pie on Thanksgiving Day after losing to the Buffalo Bills in a down-to-the-wire affair, 13-10, at New Highmark Stadium.

Tyler Bass was the hero for the Bills, kicking a 57-yard field goal as time expired. Buffalo improved to 10-1, while Kansas City fell to 8-3.

Brittany Mahomes, the wife of Patrick Mahomes, ranted about the officiating after the Chiefs' defeat.

"WHAT A JOKE. Guess we can't even play football without getting it called against us. Not sure what an actual catch is after that, I guess? I don't wanna hear s*** anymore from anyone," wrote Mahomes on her Instagram Story.

Guess she was not thankful for the referees.

She was referring to Rashee Rice's 11-yard catch with 1:47 left in the fourth quarter, which was overturned after a review. The referees ruled that the ball touched the ground and Rice, who was hit by Amani Hooker, did not maintain possession.

It is not the first time Brittany Mahomes, who has never refereed a game in the NFL, has called out the officiating. Most recently, in Week 8 against the Denver Broncos, she slammed the referees over the false start call on Jaylon Moore with four seconds left in the fourth quarter. The Chiefs lost to the Broncos, 28-24.

"You can't tell me you call that on the game-winning TD. Oh wait, yeah I can cause it's us," said the fitness entrepreneur.

At the height of the Chiefs' dynasty, fans accused the team and Patrick Mahomes of always being favored by the referees.

Patrick Mahomes went 16-of-29 for 174 yards and zero touchdowns against the Bills. He had three carries for two yards.
