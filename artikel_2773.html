Bears TE Colston Loveland carted into locker room, ruled out with knee injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Chicago Bears tight end Colston Loveland has been ruled out with a knee injury after being carted into the locker room.

Loveland appeared to suffer the injury early in the Thanksgiving matchup against the Detroit Lions, and was taken into the medical tent in the first quarter. He was later transported to the locker room on the cart. The tight end, who put a towel over his head, was clearly emotional as he left the field.

Loveland appeared to suffer the injury on Chicago's second drive, with a Detroit defender rolling up on the 22-year-old tight end's left leg while he was blocking for Bears running back D'Andre Swift.

Shortly after the start of the second quarter, the Bears ruled Loveland out for the rest of the game.

Prior to the injury, Loveland had lost a fumble on Chicago's opening drive, after the ball was punched out by Lions defensive end Derrick Moore. Detroit went three-and-out on the ensuing drive.

Rookie tight end Sam Roush and veteran Stephen Carlson stepped up to fill in the gap left by Loveland: Carlson had two huge catches in the Bears' touchdown drive in the second quarter.

Loveland is a key piece of the Bears' offense, with the third-most receiving yards on the team this season. Chicago already lost tight end Cole Kmet to a season-ending knee injury last month, leaving glaring holes in the Bears' depth at tight end going forward.
