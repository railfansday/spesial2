Al Gore To Purchase Hometown Paper Carthage Courier

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Former Vice President Al Gore has purchased the Carthage Courier, a 110-year-old weekly newspaper that covers his Tennessee hometown and surrounding area.

The current owners, the family of Hershel and Geraldine Lake, said that the sale will be finalized in the coming weeks.

Gore said in a statement, “I’m so grateful to the Lake family for their decades of leadership in supporting independent news here in Smith County. Gore said that when the paper came up for sale, “I saw an opportunity to help preserve the Courier as an independent voice for Carthage and Smith County. I look forward to supporting the Carthage Courier team, many of whom have served this community for decades, as they continue to provide vital information and serve this community for generations to come.” Watch on Deadline

Carthage is about one hour east of Nashville.

The publication also announced that Roger Wells would become publisher, succeeding Scott Winfree, who will serve in a part-time role as adviser due to health issues. The Courier also said that the current staff would be retained.

Gore previously was co-owner of Current TV, the cable channel that initially specialized in user-generated content and later switched to news programming and a lineup of progressive hosts. The channel was sold to Al Jazeera Media in 2013.
