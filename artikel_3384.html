Bravo Joining Channel Lineup Of Low-Cost Pay-TV Service Philo

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

NBCUniversal and Philo have reached a carriage deal adding Bravo to the low-cost pay-TV operator’s lineup.

Along with the network home of reality franchises like The Real Housewives and Below Deck, the agreement will also bring a selection of NBCU FAST channels to Philo, the companies said.

Philo, which carries more than 70 linear channels focused on entertainment and lifestyle programming, offers a $25-a-month subscription plan as well as a $33 tier that includes HBO Max, AMC+, Discovery+ and All Reality. (Virtual MVPDs like YouTube TV or Hulu + Live TV are more than twice the price because they also carry more expensive news and sports fare.) After operating for several years as a targeted service on college campuses, Philo became a general consumer offering in 2017.
