‘GTA 6’ Officially Defeats Christopher Nolan’s ‘The Odyssey’ in Just 1 Day

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

2026 has been a great year for the box office, nearly rivaling 2019 with as many successful movies that have been released since the start of the year. The highest-grossing of the bunch is Spider-Man: Brand New Day, which is now the highest-grossing movie in domestic box office history and one of the few films to reach the $1 billion mark from American earnings alone. Brand New Day launched in theaters on the last day of July, only two weeks after the premiere of The Odyssey, the acclaimed historical epic from director Christopher Nolan. Both Jon Bernthal and Tom Holland star in The Odyssey as Telemachus and Menelaus, and they teamed up in Spider-Man 4 as well to star as Peter Parker and Frank Castle, aka The Punisher.

While The Odyssey may not have earned as much at the box office as Spider-Man: Brand New Day, it still shattered records on its way to becoming the highest-grossing R-rated movie in history with a cumulative total of more than $1.8 billion. The film beat the record previously held by Deadpool & Wolverine, which occupied the top spot for less than two years. Still, as successful as The Odyssey was, there's always a project lurking around the corner that is ready to take its crown. For Nolan's historical epic, that project is Grand Theft Auto VI, the new video game from Rockstar, the same studio behind Red Dead Redemption 2. News broke this morning that, after only one day, GTA 6 has already surpassed the entire box office total of The Odyssey, as Rockstar's new entry has collected more than $1.84 billion from pre-orders and first-day earnings.

While the previous game in the Grand Theft Auto series featured three protagonists, Trevor, Michael, and Franklin, Grand Theft Auto VI makes the game a love story between its protagonists, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Maintaining a relationship between the two affects the overall story, and doing so requires effort, like doing heists and missions together, and even going on dates. The new game is much more akin to a modern-day Red Dead Redemption 2 than it is a sequel to Grand Theft Auto 5. There's no word yet about when GTA online will be added to the game, or if there are even plans to add the feature.
