GTA 6's Best Weapons For Avoiding Police

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally here, offering players the most immersive experience Rockstar has ever presented. Naturally, that comes with some pros and cons, since realism can sometimes get in the way of running from the cops, but overall, you can still expect to see some wacky and weird stuff across Leonida. As with previous GTA games, you're bound to have police chasing you at some point. GTA 6's map is massive, though, and if you really want to avoid police at all costs, then certain weapons can help you do that better than others.

You'll come across some weapons in the main story missions, and others while taking part in side missions. Other weapons you can buy, upgrade, and customize at Ammu -Nation. There are basically three broad weapon types in GTA 6: owned weapons have a blue icon, unowned weapons have a white icon, and unique weapons have a golden handgun icon. Owned weapons are those purchased at the Ammu-Nation store.

The more you buy from Ammu-Nation, the more options you'll unlock as you progress through the main story. Weapons, body armor, and more can be purchased at Ammu-Nation stores across Leonida. This also includes items that allow you to modify your weapons, which include grips, magazines, muzzle attachments, optics, and suppressors.

Check out all of our guides for GTA 6 here.

In order to customize and upgrade most weapons, you'll have to visit an Ammu-Nation store. However, some items like flashlights you can remove and fix onto your weapon while on the go. Based on what we've seen, the inventory varies depending on the location of the Ammu-Nation store and the size of the store itself.

While we haven't found the single best weapon for helping you avoid police, we have found the best type of weapon. This is mostly related to police response times, which will differ based upon the crime being committed in GTA 6. For instance, crimes against a gang has a slower response time than if you're assaulting some random person on the street, in broad daylight, and with plenty of witnesses.

The best weapons to help you go unnoticed by police in GTA 6 are ones that use suppressors to silence the weapon. Without sound, people won't call the police unless they see you commit the crime. In theory, this means you can use silenced weapons while staying hidden and never have to worry about police being notified.

This is, of course, Grand Theft Auto 6, so if you get a large police tail after you, don't stress too much. It's bound to happen at some point, no matter how much you try to obey laws in the game. And even if you're using a silenced weapon, if someone sees you, the odds are high that the police will be after you soon, no matter how much noise you avoid making.
