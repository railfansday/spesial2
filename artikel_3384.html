‘9-1-1’ and ‘9-1-1: Nashville’ Will Officially Return in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The 9-1-1 franchise returned for its second year with two shows on ABC's schedule. The flagship series is in its tenth season, which featured a massive volcano in Los Angeles in its first several episodes. Meanwhile, the second offshoot, 9-1-1: Nashville, introduced a new character, Rogue (Ryan Phillippe), just as Nashville came under threat from a killer called the Pharaoh (A.J. Buckley). Both shows are now on hiatus until next year, and ABC has already set return dates.

When the franchise returns, each show will focus on its own storylines. 9-1-1 signed off with a massive cliffhanger that saw Athena (Angela Bassett) pass out while being interviewed. It is unclear what is wrong with her, and the show will explore that in two episodes. Could it have something to do with being underground during the volcano? The event has left some injuries whose symptoms have yet to show up for characters like Harry, who watched someone die. Meanwhile, 9-1-1: Nashville also closed out with danger as the Pharaoh targeted Don (Chris O'Donnell) and Rogue for being firstborn sons.

When the shows return on Thursday, January 7, and resolve these hanging storylines, fans can also expect a crossover. 9-1-1 showrunner Tim Minear confirmed that the story originates in 9-1-1: Nashville. The spin-off's showrunner, Rashad Raisani, also confirmed that the crossover will introduce one of Rogue's sons, who will appear in the LA crossover with 9-1-1. "We want to let people know that these people have lives off the few minutes of screen time they get in an episode, that they do have rich backstories and lives, and we will be bringing them in," Raisani told Collider. Meanwhile, ABC has also unveiled its winter schedule.

The Rookie started as a show about a man starting over at forty-five and turned into a proper ensemble drama somewhere around season three. Here is which of them you are, and the case for yours being the best of the four.

You started late and you are not embarrassed about it. Nolan walks into the academy at forty-five surrounded by people half his age, and the show is honest that this is both his biggest disadvantage and the only reason he is any good at the job.

You are better at this than people expect and you intend to keep proving it. Chen goes from rookie to the best undercover officer in the division, which works because she is genuinely good at becoming somebody else and genuinely bad at pretending she does not care.

You are hard on people because you have decided that is what caring looks like. Bradford is introduced as the worst training officer in the building and slowly revealed as the one who takes the job most seriously, which are the same fact seen from two angles.

You said what you wanted and then you went and got it. Lopez wanted detective from the first episode, said so out loud, and got there by being better at the work than the people who were quieter about wanting it.

The second half of the 2026-2027 season kicks off on Tuesday, January 5, on ABC. The much-awaited new seasons of Will Trent (Season 5) and High Potential (Season 3) debut at 8 pm and 9 pm, respectively. Shifting Gears returns for Season 3 on Wednesday, January 6, with a two-episode premiere, followed by The Rookie (Season 9) at 9 pm and the new offshoot The Rookie North at 10 pm. The 9-1-1 universe returns on Thursday, January 7, followed by Grey's Anatomy's Season 23 midseason return at 10 pm. Abbott Elementary resumes Season 6 on Wednesday, January 13, at 8:30 pm.
