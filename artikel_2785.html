Steelers Owner Speaks Out on Ugly Aaron Rodgers Split, Backs Coach After QB’s Scathing Comments

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Pittsburgh Steelers owner Art Rooney II says he has "100% confidence" in head coach Mike McCarthy following the messy fallout with Aaron Rodgers

The Steelers released Rodgers after he publicly lambasted McCarthy on The Pat McAfee Show

"The big thing, what happened this week, is insubordination can't be tolerated in any organization," Rooney said

Pittsburgh Steelers owner Art Rooney II has backed Mike McCarthy after Aaron Rodgers publicly lambasted the coach on The Pat McAfee Show.

Rodgers, 42, who had been the team's starting quarterback before McCarthy, 63, benched him this week, was released by the Steelers in the aftermath of his explosive interview with McAfee on Wednesday, Nov. 25.

Speaking to the Pittsburgh Post-Gazette after Rodgers was released, Rooney, 74, said "a change had to be made."

"I just want to say that I have 100% confidence in Mike McCarthy and I expect he'll be our coach this season, next season and beyond," Rooney told the paper. "The big thing, what happened this week, is insubordination can't be tolerated in any organization. When you start getting ultimatums, you can't live with that."

Aaron Rodgers, left, and Mike McCarthyCredit: Michael Owens/Getty

Speaking to McAfee on Wednesday, Rodgers said he believed McCarthy was "unhealthy" and claimed that the head coach had fallen asleep during team meetings.

"Mike came in late, sat down and within 10 minutes, began snoring," Rodgers said. "Woke himself up, was rattling his papers ... five minutes after that, he walks out of the room like nothing happened."

The quarterback and coach have a long history together, with McCarthy coaching Rodgers in Green Bay from 2006 to 2018, when the former was fired.

Rodgers remained with the Packers for another four seasons, before leaving to join the New York Jets and eventually signing in Pittsburgh ahead of the 2025 season.

McCarthy was reunited with Rodgers when the Steelers hired him as head coach in 2026.

On Wednesday, the four-time MVP quarterback described another alleged incident during which he claimed McCarthy said "1-2-3 Packers," rather than Steelers, while breaking down a huddle.

The situation escalated on Tuesday when, according to a report from Gerry Dulac of the Post-Gazette, Rodgers demanded that he be allowed to call all plays on offense or that he be released. Rodgers was benched that day.

During a press conference Wednesday, following the Rodgers interview, McCarthy told reporters that there had been an "ultimatum" made "clearly out of his job description."

The Steelers are set to play the Denver Broncos on Black Friday and now turn to quarterback Mason Rudolph, who has been named the starter. Rooney reiterated his confidence in the team and McCarthy in a statement to ESPN.

"I am disappointed in where this went with Aaron Rodgers, but I have 100% confidence in Mike McCarthy," Rooney told ESPN. "And I have tremendous confidence in Mason Rudolph and the other players in our locker room."

Rodgers will turn 43 on Dec. 3 and has said he plans to retire after the 2026 season.
