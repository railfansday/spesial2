Previewing the 2026 Brooks XC Northeast Championship Hosted at Holmdel Park

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The long-awaited 2026 Brooks XC Championships presented by Fleet Feet are here, with the Brooks XC Northeast Championships set for this Saturday at Holmdel Park in New Jersey. The Northeast will be the first of four Region Championships, with 10 athletes on both the boys' and girls' sides advancing to the coveted Brooks XC National Championships in San Diego, California.

All four Brooks XC Region Championships, as well as the National Championship, will stream LIVE on FloTrack. Audiences can watch the Northeast championship starting at 9 AM this Saturday, November 21. The Girls' and Boys' Championship races are slated for 12:00 PM and 12:45 PM, respectively.

How to Watch the Brooks XC Region Championship LIVE on FloTrack

Every Brooks XC Northeast Region Championship Winner Ever

The Fleet Feet Girls Championship 5K sees two of last year's top 10 return. Leading the returners is Amelia Avila, who placed fourth last year to qualify for the National Championship, where she recorded a 20th-place finish. This season, Avila has posted three sub-17:10 5Ks, with her season best of 17:06 occurring at the VA XC Showcase. The Princeton commit currently ranks eighth in the Northeast.

The other top returning athlete is Lexie Cole, who claimed the final National qualifying spot last year. This season, the Long Island native has raced at Holmdel once, placing fourth at the Shore Coaches Invitational. She lowered her season best at her most recent race this past weekend, clocking 17:43 for another fourth-place finish. With experience on the course and a top-10 finish under her belt, Cole will look to build on that momentum heading into Saturday.

Another top contender is Alexandra Klein, who is ranked seventh in the Northeast this season. Of the aforementioned competitors, Klein has the most experience on the championship course, having taken on the infamous Bowl four times this season, including two victories. She enters the championship in the best form of her career, running a personal best of 16:57 this season at the Shore Conference Championships at Ocean County Park.

Other contenders include Lauren Parrett, Daniella Cameron, and Addison Sheehy, ranked 11th, 19th, and 26th, respectively, in the Northeast this season. With 10 qualifying spots up for grabs, these athletes have a real chance to punch their tickets to Nationals.

The Fleet Feet Boys Championship 5K features only one top-10 returner from last year, opening the door for a new set of athletes to earn national qualifying spots. The lone returner is Tycen LaBelle, who finished ninth last year in 15:29. The senior owns a personal best of 15:05, set at the 91st Annual New England Interscholastic Cross Country Championships.

One of the top entries is New Jersey's Matthias Fontao. Fontao started his 2026 season with a personal best of 15:23 and has continued to make significant improvements. In October, he dropped a 14:46 at the Bergen County/Lou Molino MOCs, moving up to No. 2 in New Jersey and No. 6 in the Northeast. Having competed at Holmdel Park twice this year, he will be the strong favorite to claim the regional title.

Ethan Fischer is another name to watch. The junior has a personal best of 15:07, which he ran against Tycen LaBelle at the New England Interscholastic Championships. Fischer will be making his first attempt to qualify for the Brooks XC National Championships, and a strong performance could put him on the national stage.

All four of the Brooks XC Region Championships, as well as the Brooks XC National Championships, are streaming LIVE on FloTrack.
