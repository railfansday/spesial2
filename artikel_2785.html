Daniel Jones goes unclaimed on waivers after surprising release by Colts

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Daniel Jones will have more time before making his next step in the NFL.

The quarterback went unclaimed on waivers Monday, Nov. 23, three days after the Indianapolis Colts announced they would be parting ways with their starting quarterback.

Jones prefers to wait before choosing on a team to sign with, ESPN reported Sunday.

Any team that had claimed Jones on waivers would have had to take on $2 million this season along with a $10.51 million injury guarantee were he to get hurt at any point this season. He also had been due $38 million in non-guaranteed money for 2027.

Now a free agent, he can pick his landing spot and negotiate agreeable terms on a new deal.

Jones, 29, was released just 11 games into a two-year, $88 million contract extension he signed in March. The Colts opted to embrace a new direction near midseason amid their persistent struggles, as the team fired longtime general manager Chris Ballard on Oct. 19.

But Indianapolis opted to keep Jones at the Nov. 11 trade deadline. The Baltimore Ravens made an offer for the veteran passer, and the Minnesota Vikings inquired about his availability, according to ESPN.

Nine days later, Jones was released in the aftermath of a 26-3 loss to the Houston Texans.

"Can't say enough good things about Daniel, just the ultimate competitor, ultimate teammate, the toughness that he brought to our football team. Can't say enough good things about him," Colts coach Shane Steichen said Friday. "I mean, the guy played on a broken leg for us last year for three or four games, snaps his Achilles, came back in eight and a half months and gave this organization everything he had. Couldn't be more thankful for the time I had with him."

In 2026, Jones has thrown for a league-leading 13 interceptions along with 1,974 yards and seven touchdowns.
