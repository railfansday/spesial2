Jon Cryer Joins ‘Cupertino’ As Judge In Top-Secret Court

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

EXCLUSIVE: Jon Cryer has been set as a guest star on CBS‘ Cupertino, playing the role of Judge Van Linkerspleen, a federal judge working in a top-secret court. He’s an individual thinker who tries to judge fairly in an environment that only values compliance.

Cupertino is a David vs. Goliath legal drama set in the heart of Silicon Valley that follows a lawyer (Mike Colter) who is being cheated out of his stock options by his former employer, a tech start-up. Refusing to back down, he joins forces with another recently fired attorney (Rachel Keller) to represent those taken advantage of by the tech elite and help them fight back in a high-stakes battle against the Goliaths controlling Silicon Valley. The series also stars Ella Stiller, Nik Dodani, Busy Philipps, and Renée Elise Goldsberry. Watch on Deadline

“Jon Cryer was perfect, playing a judge you couldn’t tell was a good guy or a bad guy. We needed someone who could deliver the comedy but also dig into the moral dilemma of a judge who needs to follow the law in a deeply flawed system,” shared Robert King and Michelle King, showrunners, co-creators and executive producers of Cupertino.

The series airs on Thursdays at 10 PM ET/PT on CBS. Beginning January 17, Cupertino moves to Sundays at 10 to 11 PM.

In addition to the Kings, the series is executive produced by Liz Glotzer and Sam Hoffman.

Cryer earned two Emmy Awards for his portrayal of Alan Harper on the CBS comedy series Two and a Half Men, first in the Supporting Actor category and then going on to win for Lead Actor. He followed the series with work on NBC’s Extended Family, in which Cryer starred and served as executive producer, and played the villain Lex Luthor on the CW’s Supergirl. Additional credits include The Famous Teddy Z, Getting Personal, Partners, The Trouble with Normal, No Small Affair, Pretty in Pink, Hot Shots, and Hiding Out. Cryer is repped by UTA, Artists First, and Myman Greenspan Fox.
