NBC Pulls Ad That Criticizes U.S.’ Israel Policy & Aired During ‘SNL’ Thanksgiving Special

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

NBC has pulled from its air a controversial ad that is critical of the United States’ policy of sending financial aid to Israel. The ad ran Wednesday night during the network’s Saturday Night Live Thanksgiving special.

Titled “Every Three Seconds,” the half-minute spot shows a young girl working to save $400 to buy a coveted bicycle. It follows her as she does such chores as moving lawns and shoveling snow until the girl proudly takes a jar filled with coins and small bills to the store. After showing her riding the new bike, it then cuts to a girl of similar age who is missing a leg and on crutches standing next to the rubble of a building.

The narrator then says: “Every three seconds, our government sends $400 of taxpayer money to do this.”

A person familiar with the matter told Deadline that NBC won’t re-air the ad, which comes from The IMEU Policy Project aka the Institute for Middle East Understanding Policy Project.

The spot drew backlash from multiple groups including the Anti-Defamation League, whose CEO called it “indefensible,” and B’nai B’rith International, which said in a social media post, “Shame on NBC.” The Israeli-American Council also weighed in, saying it “condemns NBC’s decision to air anti-Israel propaganda that echoes Hamas’s narrative and fuels antisemitic hatred.”

Senior management was not aware of the ad until after it aired, the person familiar with the matter told Deadline, and NBCUniversal will be evaluating its process to ensure appropriate levels of review.
