Orthodox Cleric Wanted in Connection With International Forgery Scheme

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Greek police has arrested one man in the $1.85 million art trafficking case.

A 44-year-old man was arrested during an undercover operation into an art forgery scheme in Athens, Greece, on Saturday, November 21, exposing an international network allegedly overseen by a Florida-based Orthodox priest. The arrest subsequently yielded the discovery of 35 forged paintings falsely attributed to prominent Greek and international artists, among other valuables.

The man, who remains unidentified to the public, was arrested on his way to make a sale to an undercover cop who posed as a client online. He had six paintings that he was going to sell to the undercover agents for €5,150 ($5,613). The investigation began in early October, when an art specialist tipped off authorities after encountering an online listing of two paintings attributed to famous 20th-century Greek expressionist George Bouzianis with counterfeit certificates of authenticity for sale.
