Sheffield Wednesday confirm key details of Dave Challinor's arrival at Hillsborough

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Sheffield Wednesday have confirmed that Dave Challinor has signed a long-term deal to take over as the new first team head coach.

The former Stockport County boss has officially taken charge at Hillsborough in place of Henrik Pedersen, and has agreed to a contract until June 2030, with Antony Sweeney and Clint Hill also taking on roles in his backroom staff.

Pedersen oversaw a busy summer transfer window alongside new sporting director Simon Wilson, with as many as 18 players joining the club.

However, an inconsistent start to the League One campaign led to the decision to dismiss the Dane after back-to-back losses to Notts County and Doncaster Rovers.

Matt Hamshaw was in charge for draws against Cambridge United and Blackpool, but now Challinor is preparing for his first game in the dugout this weekend.

Sheffield Wednesday will be hoping that the 51-year-old will be able to guide the Yorkshire outfit to a promotion push this season.

Wilson has claimed that Sheffield Wednesday are not targeting a short-term solution with their appointment of Challinor, who they see as the ideal man to take the club forward on a longer-term period.

The pair worked together at Stockport, where the coach led the team to consecutive play-off place finishes in League One before departing last summer, and the sporting director has highlighted his talents from their time together at the Hatters.

"Dave was the outstanding candidate following a detailed and comprehensive recruitment process, and we're delighted to welcome him to Sheffield Wednesday,” said Wilson, via the club’s website.
