Why There's No New Eternally Yours Tonight (Nov 26) & When Episode 8 Releases

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Laura Hurley has been writing about the entertainment industry for more than twelve years, after a decade as a content producer and editor at CinemaBlend after writing for WhatCulture and Examiner. Attending events like SCAD TVfest and San Diego Comic-Con over the course of her career, she has been immersed in the world of network, streaming, and cable TV.

CBS has had one of network TV's strongest nights of comedy for several years, and freshman sitcom Eternally Yours joined that lineup this fall. The show is going strong despite breaking one of TV's biggest comedy rules after just seven episodes, and it has been a solid replacement for the beloved sitcom that was delayed to 2027. The first bout of bad news for the family comedy (with a vampiric twist) is that fans won't get a new episode this week.

Starring Ed Weeks as Charles and Allegra Edwards as Liz, Eternally Yours took an unconventional approach to a family-centered sitcom. The two leads are a married couple of vampires, who start out the series in need of a spark to their relationship after half a millennium together. Suffice it to say that their version of a coven is very different from what any other supernatural TV show (including Supernatural itself) ever brought to the small screen.

It was enough of a hit right off the bat in CBS's fall 2026 schedule that it was network TV's #2 comedy during premiere week in early October. It only came in behind Georgie & Mandy's First Marriage, another CBS comedy. The success followed the network's divisive decision to bump Ghosts from its usual Thursday night time slot to make room for Eternally Yours, except for some holiday specials at Woodstone Mansion before the end of the year. For tonight, however, CBS' Thursday night is going to be missing all of its usual sitcom action, including Eternally Yours.

After seven straight weeks of Eternally Yours on Thursday nights at 8:30 p.m. ET, the new comedy is taking its first break of the fall. The consistency over the past two months, as well as the pairing with Georgie & Mandy's First Marriage, undoubtedly helped Eternally Yours carve out a niche in primetime despite replacing Ghosts. That said, CBS pulling Eternally Yours from primetime this week isn't a sign that the show is running out of steam.

Many network TV shows skip new episodes during the week of Thanksgiving, and Eternally Yours isn't the only CBS show that's not airing a fresh installment tonight. Georgie & Mandy and Cupertino are entirely off this week, and Elsbeth will air a rerun. The network's entire Thursday night lineup will be missing new episodes. Instead, CBS will broadcast The JLo Show, a concert special showcasing Jennifer Lopez, from 8 p.m. to 10 .m. ET.

Fortunately, Eternally Yours isn't taking a long time off. The next new episode will air on Thursday, December 3 in the usual 8:30 p.m. ET time slot on CBS. Called "Guess Who's Coven to Dinner," the episode will feature more of the family than usual. Charles and Liz likely won't be too thrilled, but it should make for some solid comedy before the end of the fall season. CBS' logline reveals:

When Max’s parents arrive for a surprise visit, it leads to the discovery of a surprising and uncomfortable connection with one of the coven members. Meanwhile, now that his human wife, Phyllis, has been brought into the circle of trust, Jesse worries she’ll want to become a vampire too.

In a fun twist, another sitcom star will return to the network as a guest star in the eighth episode of season 1. Less than a year after CBS cancelled DMV, Tim Meadows will appear on Eternally Yours as Dr. Edwards. He's not just any normal doctor, however. The former DMV scene-stealer is a dentist who also happens to be a vampire. The network released a first look at him in the next episode:

It remains to be seen if Dr. Edwards is the kind of character who could recur on Eternally Yours. Despite DMV only lasting a single season, Meadows was one of the highlights. The Saturday Night Live alum landing a new recurring role on CBS could be a treat, if the stars align for it to happen. As another treat, "Guess Who's Coven to Dinner" won't be the final episode of the fall.

The fall finale will air the next week, on December 10, and CBS has already confirmed when Eternally Yours will be back in 2027. For now, fans can just look forward to what the new episode brings after the comedy's Thanksgiving break.
