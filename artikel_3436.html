Quentin Tarantino’s First Movie in 7 Years Is Officially a Near-Perfect Hit

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Quentin Tarantino's last movie debuted to rave reviews and boffo box office seven years ago, in 2019. And while he's still hemming and hawing over his tenth and reportedly last movie in the director's chair, he's set to return to theaters next week with a new film. That movie just had its Hollywood premiere earlier this week, and the reactions are in.

The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth is a sequel to Tarantino's last film, Once Upon a Time in Hollywood, focusing on the onetime stuntman of the title (Brad Pitt). Tarantino penned the script, but he didn't direct: that honor went to David Fincher, in what will be the final act of his groundbreaking contract with Netflix. Tarantino had previously penned a number of films that were helmed by other directors, including True Romance, Natural Born Killers, and From Dusk Till Dawn, but this is the first time it's happened in 30 years.

And now, the reviews are in, and it's looking like this adventure is well worth undertaking. The movie has an 89% Fresh rating on Rotten Tomatoes, which puts it in the upper echelon of films for both Tarantino and Fincher. In fact, it outdoes its predecessor, which currently holds an 86% Fresh rating. That should bode well for the film: its predecessor grossed $410 million USD at the box office and garnered 10 Oscar nominations, winning two (Best Supporting Actor for Pitt and Best Production Design).

ScreenRant's Graeme Guttman raved about the film, calling it a hangout movie that takes a look at a darker side of Los Angeles than its predecessor:

...the reason Further Mis-Adventures works so well is, in part, because Fincher is able to visually tap into the sadness of the character and the time period at the heart of the film. Sure, Cliff is wily and spirited and Hollywood is booming, but there's an underlying sense of melancholy that follows him around town: in interactions with his father, in the way he pursues Karen, and even in his relationship to the world of film, which he so clearly loves even if it doesn't always love him back.

Jake Kleinman at Polygon smells a sequel, and thinks this could be part of an extended franchise for Tarantino:

...it's easy to imagine a world where we get to keep hanging out with Cliff for even longer. Like Benoit Blanc returning to solve each new Knives Out mystery, Brad Pitt's fixer could easily resurface a few years from now for another misadventure, ideally with Tarantino still writing the script.

Collider's Robert Brian Taylor, meanwhile, doesn't agree with the critical consensus: he calls it "a surprisingly shrug-worthy affair."

The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth is a lark, a throwaway, a side quest. If that's all you want out of it, you'll find things to enjoy here. But if you're hoping for something on the level of Once Upon a Time... in Hollywood (or any of Tarantino or Fincher's masterworks, really), I'd suggest realigning your expectations.

Rare for a Netflix release, The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth is getting a worldwide launch in theaters: it will hit the big screen for two weeks only, starting on November 25, before coming to the streamer on December 23.

Pretty soon, he gets mixed up with a TV star (Scott Caan) and his wife (Carla Gugino), a high-powered talent agent (Elizabeth Debicki), a football star turned Blaxploitation actor (Yahya Abdul-Mateen II), and some sadistic Filipino mobsters.
