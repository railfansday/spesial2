5 Near-Perfect Netflix Series With Less Than 20 Episodes

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Netflix has a shaky history when it comes to keeping its best shows alive, given that the streamer has rarely renewed shows beyond a third season. While the obvious reason for this is a cynical justification of not wanting to give bonuses to writers, it does result in many shows that are either cut off before their prime or forced to reach their finales at too early a date.

The Netflix library is filled with shows that have a shorter number of overall episodes, but determining which ones are truly of value can be a challenging ordeal. While Netflix might not have the same consistent level of quality across all of its programming as rival streamers like HBO Max or Apple TV do, it nonetheless has produced exceptional television shows which left a sizable impact, regardless of how many episodes they had.

Mindhunter is a captivating true crime thriller from the brilliant filmmaker David Fincher, which is loosely based on FBI Agent John Douglas and his research into psychopathic killers in the 1970s, which helped to coin the phrase “serial killer.” The show follows the FBI investigators Holden Ford (Jonathan Groff) and Bill Tench (Holy McCallany) as they interview and profile some of the most famous killers in American history, all whilst helping local cops solve puzzling crimes.

Mindhunter is crafted with the same meticulous style that defines Fincher’s best work, while also offering deep subtext about the extent to which empathy can make someone vulnerable. While the show’s looming expenses and Fincher’s notoriously detail-oriented approach to capturing historical detail may have prevented Mindhunter from getting a third season, it remains one of the best things that Netflix has ever produced.

Bodyguard was a true phenomenon of a summer streaming sensation that became a massive hit in the United Kingdom on BBC during 2018 before making its American debut on Netflix, where it picked up even more steam. In only six episodes, Bodyguard offered an unforgettable mystery and served as a starmaking performance from Richard Madden in his first major role after his run as Robb Stark on Game of Thrones.

Bodyguard is more than just a murder mystery, as it works as a conspiracy thriller, a political drama, a dark romance, and a feat of non-stop action. There has been no shortage of spy shows released within the past decade on streaming, but Bodyguard is the rare instance of a series that operates with the scale of a studio blockbuster while offering the type of intimate interpersonal relationships that would only be fit for the small screen.

Midnight Mass is the crowning achievement of Mike Flanagan’s career and the most deeply idiosyncratic and personal work that he has ever made. While the two previous horror shows that Flanagan had made for Netflix succeeded in offering classical family drama within a gothic atmosphere, Midnight Mass is an equally gripping character study that goes to supremely dark places, including the type of graphic content that would have a hard time making it on any other streaming service.

The brilliance of Midnight Mass is that Flanagan weaves a historical understanding of the occult into a grounded story of religious fanaticism and the monopolization of fear, suggesting that the pandemonium that gripped the past is not entirely the stuff of legends. Hamish Linklater had already been a reliable character actor up to this point in time, but Midnight Mass turned him into a total icon within the horror community.

Four Netflix characters, four completely different ways of showing up for somebody. This is the one who fits how you actually do friendship, and what having them in your life would be like.

You do not do friendship at half volume, and neither does he. What you want is somebody who says yes to the plan before you have finished describing the plan.

You are the friend who makes things happen, and so is she. Together you would be mildly exhausting to everyone around you and completely delighted with yourselves.

You have no patience for pleasant lies, and she has no ability to tell them. What looks like coldness from the outside is the most reliable friendship on this list.

You look after whoever is having the worst day. So does he. It is the least strategic way to move through the world and the best possible quality in a friend.

Lord of the Flies is not the first adaptation of the classic William Golding novel that is often taught in schools, but it is the most comprehensive interpretation of its dark coming-of-age story because the four-part series is divided between the perspectives of the four main characters: Ralph, Piggy, Simon, and Jack. While this version of Lord of the Flies is still set in the novel’s original setting of the 1950s, the freshness came from the writing by Jack Thorne, who had also created Adolescence.

Lord of the Flies captures the way that kids actually talk and act in a way that none of the previous adaptations have shown themselves capable of, but the empathy that extends to the entire cast of characters does not prevent the show from getting to the heart of Golding’s prophetic message about collapsing society.

Black Doves is the most bingeable spy show on the air right now because it takes a very different approach to the idea of former operatives being called out of retirement. Keira Knightley plays the wife of a politician who is placed in the middle of a conspiracy after her past as a covert assassin is threatened to be revealed, and Ben Whishaw is equally convincing as her former partner.

The first season of Black Doves is the perfect show to watch over the holidays because it is a straight shot of adrenaline that is set entirely over the Christmas season, and the second season was a major jump up in quality that raised the stakes while adding more character depth, action, and surprising humor. Although it has not yet been revealed whether the show will get a Season 3, the 12 episodes that exist are already riveting.
