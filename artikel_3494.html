‘Dune’ Director Officially Reveals Why He Cast Robert Pattinson as the New Bond

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Many worries about the future of the James Bond franchise, following its $1 billion acquisition by Amazon, were calmed when it was confirmed who would be directing the next movie. The brilliant Denis Villeneuve was officially announced as the Bond director, and he will work alongside writer Steven Knight, best known for creating the Peaky Blinders franchise. Villeneuve's track record of producing high-quality, ambitious, big-screen favorites is arguably unrivaled in the modern era, so there is no doubt that the franchise is in safe hands.

But Villeneuve would not be able to get started on the Bond film in earnest until his latest big project had been completed. Dune: Part Three, the final installment in the director's adaptation of the Frank Herbert novels, will make its debut on December 18, as audiences watch in awe as Paul Atreides' (Timothée Chalamet) empire is threatened by the evil Scytale. This terrifying villain is played by Robert Pattinson, who just so happens to be the new James Bond.

Pattinson called the casting "the honour of a lifetime," with the British Royal Family even wishing him well, posting "Welcome to His Majesty’s Secret Service, Mr Bond" to their social media. "I am overjoyed to welcome Robert Pattinson as our new James Bond. He has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new. I cannot wait to begin," Villeneuve also wrote in a statement celebrating the announcement of his Bond, but exactly why was Pattinson picked? There's no shortage of discussion online, but the director himself has now answered these questions in an interview with GQ.

"At the end of the day, the truth is, no matter who I choose, some people will be excited and others will be disappointed," Villeneuve admitted in the interview. The likes of Callum Turner and Jack Lowden were considered the frontrunners, and many have expressed their frustration that neither was picked. But Turner and Lowden must've lacked the perfect style and substance Villeneuve was searching for. "The first quality is that I need to believe that this man is a killer, because more than a spy, Bond is an assassin," he continued. "I need that kind of anger deep in the eye. It’s not everybody who has that." He expanded on his criteria:

"I need someone who has that kind of unapologetic panache, someone that when he comes into the room, everything starts to gravitate around him, that kind of strong charisma and someone that has a lot of sensuality as well and that people are attracted to… someone who has dark humor, someone that has pleasure in being in deep shit, someone who is not afraid to put his hand in the mud. I need someone that is strong, muscular, someone that is a force. And someone, of course, who has a deep intelligence in the eyes, someone that is always ahead of us."

Pattinson's Bond is expected to begin filming in 2027. For more Bond stories, stay tuned to Collider.
