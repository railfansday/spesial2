Donna Kelce Unloads Remodeled Florida Condo For Sweet Profit, Moves Into Bigger Pad

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Donna Kelce’s remodel of her Florida pad paid off, with Taylor Swift’s mother-in-law making a nice profit after selling the home ... TMZ has learned.

According to official records obtained by TMZ, Donna sold her 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom, 1,434-square-foot home for $485K. She purchased the home for $355K in May 2019, meaning she made over $100K on the sale.

As TMZ first reported, Jason and Travis' mom remodeled the Orlando, Florida, pad earlier this year. She paid to replace 9 windows and 6 doors.

The sale paperwork notes Donna’s new address is a 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom, 2,549-square-foot lakefront home in the same city.
