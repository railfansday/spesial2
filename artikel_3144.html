Howard Hughes Holdings Inc. (HHH -1.56%) reported a net loss of $1.1 million for the third quarter, a result primarily influenced by the transition of the Vantage insurance portfolio and a non-cash loss from the sale of municipal receivables. Management reported that the integration of the Vantage platform is complete, with the insurance segment now providing permanent capital to support the company’s real estate development. The company stated it is shifting toward an asset-light model for its real estate holdings, intending to use joint ventures and asset sales to unlock equity for redeployment into insurance operations or preferred stock redemption. Management noted that despite higher interest rates, demand for land in its master planned communities remains strong, supported by consistent homebuilder interest and increasing price per acre.
