Practice Mirror: rehearse interview answers, wink for the next question, see which one tightened you up

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Presage SmartSpectra SDK Challenge Submission

This is a submission for the Presage SmartSpectra SDK Challenge

Practice Mirror is an Android app for rehearsing interview answers alone. Prop the phone up, it shows one question at a time, and you answer out loud. Through the SmartSpectra SDK the front camera does two jobs:

Video link: https://youtube.com/shorts/HE3Zxb83XBo

The readings in the video are live, from a friend who agreed to be recorded.

https://github.com/AbrahamPaulJ/practice-mirror

Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, SmartSpectra 3.5.0. Setup steps are in the README. 18 unit tests cover the wink detector and the analysis.

If the chest leaves the picture the SDK stops sending landmarks, so the app restarts it with face metrics only and winks keep working. Tested with a replay harness that feeds video into the SDK: 20 of 20 winks detected, 0 false changes in 90 seconds of normal blinking. Readings taken while speaking are rougher than at rest, and the app says so.

Best Thoughtful Real-World Use, and Most Creative or Delightful Use.
