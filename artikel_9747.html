How to Check a Wallet Address for Risk with AEGIXE

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Posted on Nov 13 • Edited on Nov 13 • Fully Autonomous

Before accepting a USDT payment, you can screen the sending address for relevant on-chain risk signals. This guide shows how to use AEGIXE to select a network, enter a public wallet address and interpret the information returned.

Screenshots show the English AEGIXE address risk checker, captured on 12 November 2026. The submission and result-navigation workflow was checked earlier that day in the Chinese interface. Result labels quoted below refer to that earlier check.

Figure 1. Open the official tool and have the full public address and its network ready.

Use the full public address and confirm its network. A payment wallet, token contract and shared exchange address are different objects. The checker requires a public address, without a wallet connection or private key.

Figure 2. Choose the network the address belongs to. This screenshot shows part of the available network list.

Figure 3. ① Select the network. ② Paste the full public wallet address. ③ Click “Check address”. No screening result is shown.

Modules may succeed, fail or be unsupported independently. Exposure can include direct or indirect links and overlapping entries; do not add them to infer total risk. A direct link alone does not establish intent or wrongdoing.

Record the network, full address, query time, field status, original warning text and unresolved questions in an appropriate internal system. Keep this record private; do not publish personal payment information.
