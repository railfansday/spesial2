Alphabet is Buying Around $10 Billion More Anthropic Shares on Day 1. Should You Follow?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Alphabet already owns a huge chunk of Anthropic. It wants more.

By Keithen Drury – Nov 18, 2026 at 3:05PM EST

Anthropic is going public Thursday, and it won’t just be financial institutions and individual investors buying shares. Major public companies like Alphabet (GOOG +1.35%) (GOOGL +1.46%) will be, too.

But why is Alphabet buying more shares when it already owns a big stake in the AI lab? Let's take a look.

Alphabet already owns a fair bit of Anthropic right now, but it wants more exposure. That shows how bullish it is on the company's prospects.

Because it has been a longtime partner of Anthropic, Alphabet has access to more information about it than the average investor does. Anthropic doesn't own any computing power; instead, it chooses to rent cloud computing capacity from various providers. Two of its primary vendors are Alphabet’s Google Cloud and Amazon (AMZN +3.68%) Web Services (AWS). By not owning any data centers, Anthropic can stay asset-light and focus on training its AI models. This also gives it the flexibility to direct workloads to the most efficient computing units for them.

One of the most cost-effective ways emerging to train and run AI models is to use a type of custom chip called an application-specific integrated circuit (ASIC). These chips are designed by hyperscalers to handle a narrow range of workloads, but their lack of flexibility compared to GPUs makes them cheaper to build and operate. One of the most popular ASICs is the Tensor Processing Unit, which Alphabet designed in partnership with Broadcom (AVGO +4.14%). This has become Google Cloud’s secret weapon. Anthropic has taken full advantage of this and utilizes TPUs heavily. Alphabet knows this, and to deepen its ties with Anthropic, it's choosing to purchase more shares of the company via a $10 billion private placement deal when Anthropic goes public.

Alphabet has a higher degree of insight than the average investor about the growth Anthropic may have in store over the next few years because the deals they make show how the AI lab plans to expand its computing usage. Because of that, it may be wise to follow Alphabet's lead and buy some shares of Anthropic soon, because the growth figures it has already delivered are truly astounding.

Anthropic’s revenue rose by around 1,200% year over year during the first nine months of 2026.

But what kind of valuation does that give Anthropic stock?

We only know its results for the first nine months of the year, and its ludicrous growth rates make it harder to project where the business is going, so these are all going to be guesses that will be mostly wrong. But they’re the best estimates we can make.

Over the past nine months, Anthropic generated $33.2 billion in revenue. In the first six months of 2026, this figure was $16.3 billion. So during Q3, it brought in $16.9 billion, or just over what it brought in during the prior two quarters combined. If we assume it can double its revenue again on a quarter-over-quarter basis, that would put its full-year revenue estimate at about $67 billion. That's a huge mark for any company. If Anthropic goes public as expected at around $1.4 trillion, that prices the stock at around 21 times sales — an expensive premium.

However, by next year, Anthropic could easily be producing over $100 billion in annual revenue, which would bring its price-to-sales ratio down to a pretty reasonable level at that market cap. The biggest question is how long Anthropic's runway is. If it can put up multiple years of rapid growth, then it could be a brilliant buy now. Alphabet anticipates that will happen, and it’s choosing to invest accordingly. I think retail investors would be well advised to stay patient for a few days after the IPO to let the early hype cool off. But then, it could be time to pounce.
