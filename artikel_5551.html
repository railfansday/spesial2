Breach Secure Now Launches MCP Connector, Putting Security Program Data to Work in ChatGPT, Claude and Microsoft Copilot

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

New connector helps MSPs prioritize client needs, create customized reports and demonstrate service value, while giving business leaders and employees greater visibility into security progress.

MORRISTOWN, N.J., Nov. 10, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Breach Secure Now (BSN), a provider of cybersecurity, AI awareness, productivity and compliance solutions delivered to small and midsize businesses (SMBs) through Managed Service Providers (MSPs), today announced the Breach Secure Now MCP, a new integration that brings BSN Portal Data directly into ChatGPT, Claude and Microsoft.

MSPs manage valuable security program data across their clients' portfolios, but finding the right information often means navigating multiple dashboards, pulling individual reports, and spending time organizing results. The Breach Secure Now MCP changes this process by allowing users to ask questions about their data in plain language, directly within familiar AI assistants.

The connector extends beyond MSP administrators, giving their clients business leaders and employees access to information appropriate to their BSN account permissions. MSPs can prioritize client needs and prepare for business reviews, leadership teams can evaluate security program performance, and employees can track their own security awareness progress.

Helping MSPs Turn Program Data into Action

For MSPs, the Breach Secure Now MCP provides a practical and highly impactful way to review security program data across their managed clients, identify accounts that need attention, prioritize outreach and prepare for Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs).

Rather than reviewing individual client reports, MSPs can ask questions such as, "Which of my clients I call first, and why?"  The AI assistant can quickly retrieve and analyze available BSN Portal Data, helping partners identify priorities and turn information that previously required extensive manual reporting into useful summaries in minutes. MSPs can also generate customized client reports that bring together relevant security program information metrics, helping them demonstrate going value and have more productive client conversations.

"MSPs have a tremendous amount of valuable information available through the security programs they manage, but turning that information into meaningful client conversations takes time," said Art Gross, CEO of Breach Secure Now. "With the Breach Secure Now MCP, we're continuing our commitment to first-to-market innovation by making it easier for partners to understand where their clients need attention, show the progress they're making, and have more productive conversations about security. Just as importantly, we're giving business leaders and employees a way to better understand their own results."

Greater Visibility for Business Leaders and Employees

The benefits extend beyond MSP administrators to the organizations and employees they support.

Business leaders can use their AI assistants to review their organization's training participation, Employee Secure Score and aggregate phishing results. They can summarize program performance for leadership, identify areas that need attention, and determine where additional follow-up may be needed.

Employees can use their AI assistants to check their Employee Secure Score, review completed training and access their personal phishing history. They can also see how their score has changed over the past six weeks, giving them a clearer picture of their security awareness progress and where they may need to focus their attention.

Chris Gross, Sr AI Product Director, adds, "For years, partners have asked us for more flexibility in how they access and present their security program data," said Chris Gross, Senior Director of AI Product at Breach Secure Now. "With the Breach Secure Now MCP, they're no longer limited to predefined reports. They can ask their own questions, create tailored summaries, and use their AI assistants to build reports around what matters most to their clients. This opens up a whole new way for MSPs to put their data to work."

Secure, Role-Based Access to BSN Portal Data

The Breach Secure Now MCP uses Model Context Protocol (MCP), an open standard that enables AI assistants to connect securely with applications and data sources.

Users sign in with their existing BSN credentials, and access is limited to the information permitted by their BSN account role. MSP administrators can view data for their managed clients, client management can access organizational information, and employees can view their personal results.

The connector is read-only and does not change records within the BSN Portal.

"We're meeting our partners where they already work. BSN's MCP server brings portal data into everyday AI tools, so partners, company managers, and individual users can get the answers relevant to their role just by asking," said AL Lowenstein, CTO of Breach Secure Now.

Available Now, with Additional Capabilities Planned

The Breach Secure Now MCP is available now to BSN users with the appropriate account permissions and supports ChatGPT, Claude and Microsoft Copilot.

Development is already underway to expand the BSN Portal Data and reporting capabilities available through the connector, with additional enhancements planned for future releases.
