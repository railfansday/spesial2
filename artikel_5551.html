Finch AI Receives CMMC Level 2 Certification

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

HERNDON, Va., Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Finch AI, developer of artificial intelligence-driven solutions that deliver entity intelligence in order to uncover risk and take action, today announced that it has achieved Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) Level 2 certification following an independent assessment by Ace of Cloud, an authorized Certified Third-Party Assessment Organization (C3PAO).

"This is a reflection of our sincere commitment to security and the most stringent protections of informational assets â both ours and our customers'," Finch AI Chief Executive Officer Steve Baldwin said. "We thank the team involved in helping us attain this certification as well as Ace of Cloud, our C3PAO assessor, and KTL solutions, who helped guide us through this process."

The CMMC framework is a Department of Defense (DoD / Department of War) framework designed to verify that Defense Industrial Base (DIB) contractors and subcontractors properly safeguard sensitive government information. It is designed specifically for contractors and suppliers who handle Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) and requires full implementation of 110 security practices and requirements from the National Institutes of Standards & Technology (NIST) SP 800-171, which spans 14 control families.

By achieving a Level 2 certification, Finch AI becomes one of only a small subset of federal government contractors to achieve this level of certification.
