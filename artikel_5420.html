ArchiPro Officially Launches in the U.S., Introducing Its All-In-One Architecture and Design Platform to the American Market

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

On November 1, 2026, the New Zealand-based platform ArchiPro, trusted by over 3,000 professionals and 4.2 million annual users in ANZ, expands fully to U.S. professionals and homeowners.

NEW YORK, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- ArchiPro, the leading all-in-one platform connecting architecture and design professionals with the products, projects, and people they need to build better spaces and homes, has publicly launched in the United States. After more than a decade transforming how the architecture and building industries operate across New Zealand and Australia, where the platform has grown to more than 3,000 professional members and 3.9 million annual users actively planning building projects, ArchiPro is bringing its integrated marketplace model to one of the world's largest and most dynamic design markets.

The platform offers architects, interior designers, landscape architects, builders, and product suppliers a single destination to showcase work, discover materials and products, connect with collaborators, and reach clients, while giving homeowners and design enthusiasts an elevated, editorial-quality experience for finding the professionals and inspiration behind their next project.

From Soft Launch to Full Platform: A Community Built on Craft

Following a deliberate soft launch in June 2026, ArchiPro America has spent the past several months doing what it does best: building the right community before scaling it. The soft launch brought together a founding group of architects, interior designers, and firms whose work reflects the quality and range ArchiPro is known for, including Specht Novak, Citizen Artist, Massuco Warner, PBW Associates, Desai Chia, KODA, and Roger Ferris + Partners.

Quality of membership has driven every decision in this phase, not speed of growth. ArchiPro has grown its U.S. architecture and design community through a selective invitation and application process, the same approach that earned the platform its reputation in New Zealand and Australia. Every firm and professional who joined has strengthened the network for everyone on it: more projects, more products, more connections, and more value for the homeowners and developers seeking them out.

"The U.S. design industry has platforms built for volume, but few built for craft. ArchiPro was designed from the start to be the opposite, a curated home for the studios and brands shaping the future of the industry, where exceptional work is the standard for inclusion," says Milot Zeqiri, Co-Founder and CEO.

What's Here Now: A Full Platform Launch on November 1, 2026

ArchiPro's November 1 launch marks a significant expansion of the platform's U.S. offering across two fronts.

Products. ArchiPro has introduced a curated product layer to the U.S. platform, connecting homeowners and design professionals directly with the materials, finishes, and furnishings featured in the projects they love. Products on ArchiPro are endorsed by the platform's professional members, grounding discovery in real-world specification rather than generic search. Foundation brand partners include Gaggenau, Roche Bobois, Poliform, Kravet, Artistic Tile, and Oggetti. Product brands interested in partnering with ArchiPro can learn more at ArchiPro America.

Editorial. A robust original editorial program is now live, produced by ArchiPro's in-house editorial team under the direction of Brittany Chevalier McIntyre, Editorial Director for the Americas. The program is built on a simple belief: that the right story, told well, inspires homeowners to taking the step of engaging an architect, interior designer, or other design professional for their next project. Through original features, project spotlights, and honest coverage of the building process, ArchiPro's editorial closes the knowledge gap that keeps too many homeowners from pursuing the homes they actually want, from understanding realistic cost expectations to knowing how to find and work with the right design professional.

The United States represents the largest and most influential architecture and design market in the world, and it has never had a platform built specifically for it: one that treats professionals as the talent rather than the inventory, connects the inspiration, the people, and the products behind a project in a single cohesive experience, and gives brands and professionals the performance visibility to know whether their presence is actually working. ArchiPro is that platform, and the timing of its U.S. arrival is deliberate.

For homeowners and developers, ArchiPro solves an equally critical problem: finding the right design professional for the right project. The search for an architect, interior designer, or other design professional has long been opaque, relying on referrals, geography, and luck. ArchiPro changes that by giving end users a curated, searchable window into the work, aesthetic, and expertise of professionals across the country, making it possible to find not just a qualified designer, but the right one. It is, at its core, a platform built on the belief that the right match changes everything.

ArchiPro is a three-sided platform, and that architecture is what sets it apart. Founded in Auckland in 2014, the company was built from the ground up to serve architects and interior designers, high-net-worth homeowners, and luxury product brands within a single cohesive ecosystem. For architects and interior designers, it is a stage built with editorial intention: a place where work is presented with the craft and context it deserves, and where the right audience is already assembled. For those homeowners, it is the only destination where inspiration, expertise, and curated product discovery exist in one seamless experience. And for luxury product brands, it is direct access to the specifiers and clients who matter most. Most platforms serve one constituency and ask the others to adapt. ArchiPro was designed from the start so that each side makes the others more valuable.
