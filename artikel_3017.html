Sociedad Química y Minera de Chile: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Management's view that lithium prices sit below the level needed to attract new supply sets up a price case the capex plan is built to capture.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 18, 2026 at 2:45PM EST

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 18, 2026, Sociedad Química y Minera de Chile (SQM -4.94%) reported net income of nearly $1.6 billion for the first three quarters of the year. That figure represents almost a fourfold increase compared to the same period in 2025. The results demonstrate how low production costs and volume scale buffer profitability during volatile commodity cycles. Three key takeaways from management's comments highlight the long-term outlook.

Lower lithium spot prices don't reflect long-term market fundamentals. Management projects global lithium demand to reach 3 million metric tons by 2030, driven by electric vehicles and energy storage systems.

SQM continues to expand its production efficiency in Chile while keeping costs low. The company expects to produce 290,000 metric tons of lithium this year and push beyond 300,000 metric tons next year.

Management isn't cutting investment plans to react to temporary market weakness. The company is maintaining its multiyear capital expenditure budget to fund projects where it holds structural cost advantages.

Lithium demand is expanding toward 15% to 20% growth next year. Low-cost Chilean brine reserves and expanding refining operations provide structural cost advantages, while leadership monitors pricing discipline across energy storage markets.

Capital allocation remains disciplined as multiyear investments proceed on schedule.
