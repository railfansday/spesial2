White and Vancouver out to ‘finish the job’ in MLS Cup playoffs

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Brian White has made it abundantly clear that he and the Vancouver Whitecaps are out to avenge their near-miss in the MLS Cup playoffs last season in 2026.

The Whitecaps lost to Inter Miami in last season's MLS Cup final, which was their first ever appearance in the showcase match, but they have bounced back superbly.

Jesper Sorensen's side enjoyed their best MLS regular season, winning the Western Conference, with their points tally (64) and total wins (19) both higher than their previous best, set in 2025 (63 points, 18 wins).

Vancouver are also looking to become just the second Canadian side to win the MLS Cup, after Toronto FC's success back in 2017.

They will also hope to follow the template set by Toronto that season too, as the last team to lose an MLS Cup final in one season (2016 vs Seattle Sounders) and win it the next (2017 vs the same opponents).

"Losing finals is never easy," White told MLSoccer.com this week. "Then to go through two of them and to lose both is obviously a very difficult thing to process and deal with.

"But as players and as professionals, I think it's even more motivating to want to get back to those spots and, in a sense, redeem yourselves and finish the job.

"That's what everyone was focused on going into this season, was getting back to the MLS Cup, and winning it."

The Whitecaps had endured a difficult stretch as early as last month, going four MLS matches without a win (D3 L1) before eventually finishing the season with straight victories.

"We had ups and downs, especially after the World Cup. We struggled a bit with our results," said Vancouver midfielder Thomas Muller.

"In the beginning, I had the feeling we wouldn't struggle with our playing style. We were kind of dominant but not really effective.

"In between, after that, we also sometimes struggled with the games we played. But now we are on the upslope.

“And maybe teams adapted a bit to us. We were at the top of the table almost the whole season, and they looked a little bit more into it: How can we hurt them?

"How can we maybe avoid their strengths? And maybe that's also what happened in one or two games. But we are always a team that kind of fights back."

Vancouver will face Real Salt Lake this weekend after Pablo Mastroeni's side overcame the Seattle Sounders 4-2 on penalties following a 1-1 draw in normal time.

Albert Rusnak opened the scoring in the 36th minute, having missed a penalty five minutes earlier, before Morgan Guilavogui brilliantly scored the equaliser just after the hour.

Rafael Cabral came to RSL's rescue in the shoot-out, saving penalties from Rusnak and Dejan Joveljic to seal his team's place in the next round.

White is Vancouver's main threat. He scored a career-best 20 goals in the regular season, his fourth straight season with 15 or more goals.

He is the fourth player in MLS history with four straight regular seasons with 20+ goals, joining Bradley Wright-Phillips (five), Denis Bouanga and Robbie Keane.

If it had not been for Cabral's heroics against Seattle, RSL would have found themselves putting their feet up. Instead, they are on their way to BC Place.

Cabral made five saves, his joint-most in a match this season (also five against Inter Miami), while also keeping out three penalties, two in the shoot-out and one in the match.

At BC Place this season, the Whitecaps have secured 35 points from a possible 51 on offer in 17 MLS contests. They will also be out to prolong a three-match home unbeaten streak dating back to September 27 against D.C. United.

Vancouver have dominated recent meetings against RSL, winning the last four in a row by an aggregate scoreline of 9-3.

RSL have four wins, three draws, and 11 losses on their travels this season in league matches, and will need to win away at Vancouver for the first time since February 2023, when Justen Glad and Damir Kreilach were both on target, to give themselves a chance.
