The big winner from Stoke City v Birmingham City could be 165 miles away in Middlesbrough

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Stoke City, Birmingham City, and Middlesbrough are all firmly in the promotion picture as the 2026/27 Championship campaign approaches its busiest period.

Birmingham City currently sit top of the table, with Stoke City occupying third place and Middlesbrough sandwiched between the two in second. There is very little separating the leading sides, and the automatic promotion race is beginning to take shape.

With the November international break now behind us and no further interruption until March, the coming weeks could prove to be hugely influential in determining who finishes the calendar year in the strongest position in the Championship.

The winter period is arguably the biggest test of any Championship promotion contender. Fixtures come thick and fast, the colder weather can take its toll on players, and the increased risk of injuries means squads are often stretched to their limits.

Those who can navigate this period successfully will be extremely well positioned to attack the second half of the season. For Birmingham and Stoke, that challenge begins for them now, with the pair meeting on Saturday in a huge clash at the top of the table.

Blues currently lead the way, while the Potters are just behind them on goal difference, meaning both have an opportunity to strengthen their promotion credentials in a classic six-pointer for promotion.

The 25 top scorers in the Championship since the second tier was rebranded back in 2004/05, including Billy Sharp and Aleksandar Mitrovic.

However, Middlesbrough could be the biggest beneficiaries, 165 miles away, of the pair going head-to-head. Kim Hellberg's side sit second, also on goal difference, and have an opportunity to capitalise on the outcome, provided they take care of business against an out-of-form Bristol City.

At least one of the two sides around them is guaranteed to drop points, giving Boro a potentially valuable opportunity to move into an even stronger position. Michael Skubala and co. have won just one of the last seven Championship games as well.

Middlesbrough are not the only promotion hopefuls who could benefit from the two leading sides taking points off one another in the race to the Championship's summit.

Southampton host a struggling Derby County and will be desperate to take advantage, while Wolves welcome Charlton Athletic, and West Ham United have Preston North End visiting.

All three have favourable opportunities on paper and cannot afford to waste them while rivals are guaranteed to drop points elsewhere. There is another potentially significant fixture for each of these four clubs, too.

That particular game involves Swansea City, who sit sixth and host fifth-placed West Bromwich Albion. That guarantees another promotion rival will lose ground, with the possibility of a draw benefiting those around them even further.

It could be a pivotal weekend in the automatic promotion race, particularly for Middlesbrough, Southampton, Wolves, and West Ham if they all deliver in their respective fixtures. However, it is important to remember the very nature of the Championship.

Stoke have home advantage on Saturday, but Birmingham have demonstrated their quality both home and away, while the Potters actually boast a stronger record on their travels.

The underlying numbers also raise some questions about Mark Robins' side, with Blues ranking fifth for both expected goal difference and expected points compared to Stoke's 17th-place standing for both, per FotMob.

Perhaps more concerningly, Stoke have lost all four of their matches against sides currently in the top nine, despite winning nine and drawing two of their other 11 Championship fixtures.

That suggests they have been excellent against much of the division but have yet to prove themselves against the strongest opposition. This is the perfect opportunity to change that narrative.

A victory would be a huge statement of intent, but another defeat against a promotion rival would raise legitimate questions about their credentials in the long-term. Either way, Middlesbrough should be the winners if they continue to add to Skubala's woes.
