John Eustace has one last chance to save his job – Derby County must beat Watford today

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Derby County are rooted to the bottom of the Championship table with 17 games down. We are hurtling towards the halfway point of the season and the hectic winter schedule, and DCFC have shown little sign of turning around their form.

The 3-1 triumph over Bolton Wanderers was viewed as a crucial one for John Eustace, but defeats in tough away games at Southampton and Wolves since the return from the international break has brought new pressure on the boss. As it stands though, the Rams chief is expected to lead his side for Saturday’s game against Watford.

Eustace has retained the backing of David Clowes and co to date. However, the failure to win on home turf against a fellow Championship struggler could spell the end of the Derby County boss.

Eustace was arguably one of the managers of the season in the Championship last term. After fighting relegation in the 2024/25 campaign, Derby County were able to push for the play-off places and only came up short on the final day, finishing 8th.

A failed takeover from Saudi boxing supremo Turki Alalshikh disrupted the summer transfer window. Despite the expansion of the play-offs, few were touting the Rams for another push for the top eight given the level of competition in the Championship and their far-from-ideal preparations for the campaign.

However, few expected DCFC to have just two wins to their name after 17 games. 14 goals scored is the second-lowest tally in the league, and 35 goals conceded is the joint-worst. Abject performances across the board mean the Pride Park faithful are almost unanimously calling for a change in management.

But, Eustace is in place for today’s clash with Watford, and it might just be his last chance.

The Derby County hierarchy appear to have given Eustace the benefit of the doubt for the last two losses given the strength of Wolves and Southampton. Watford are a different matter though – they are a beatable opponent, with just 15 points from 17 games amid a lack of creativity and a glaring weakness for conceding chances.

At home, they are a team that the Rams can beat. Today’s hosts have to expect a battle, as it is Watford’s first game under the new management of Edward Iordanescu, and he will be determined to start with a victory.

You don’t need to go into too much depth to explain why Watford are beatable. They struggle to score and concede too many chances, hence why they’ve lost 10 of 17 Championship matches this season.

One specific positive for Derby County is the set-piece matchup. The Hornets have scored just twice from set plays this term, so they shouldn’t pose too much of a threat to the hosts’ poor defensive record from such situations (eight goals conceded from set pieces).

DCFC can find success with direct football, and perhaps even score their first set-piece goal of the campaign. It remains to be seen who Iordanescu picks at the back, but none of Watford’s four centre-backs – Mattie Pollock, Soumaila Coulibaly, Kevin Keben or Branimir Mlacic – average better than a 60.3% aerial duel win-rate, putting all of them in the bottom half of Championship centre-backs (FotMob).

That could make powerful strikers Carlton Morris and Lars-Jorgen Salvesen dangerous, and Shane Duffy can offer a threat from set pieces as well. Energetic forwards Rhian Brewster and Mo Fuseini can get in behind and pick up knock-downs.

If the midfield stays compact and safe in possession, they can easily limit Watford’s chance creation too. David Ozoh will have to be on the top of his game, as will his likely partner Alex Mowatt.

Watford have just one point from eight away games, losing all of the last seven and failing to score in five of them. They are a dismal team on their travels, and Derby County simply have to make that count.

If not, Eustace may well bid farewell to his job later this weekend.
