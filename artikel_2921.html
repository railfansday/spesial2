Why Barcelona handed Real Madrid a free run at JJ Gabriel signing

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

An insight into the reasoning behind Barcelona's lack of a concerted push for the signature of Manchester United talent JJ Gabriel has late this week surfaced online.

This comes amid the understanding that the Blaugrana were warded off by the costs involved in the deal.

Youngster Gabriel has of course seen his name dominate the headlines across Spain over the course of the week to date. After making the call to depart the aforementioned Man United in search of a new challenge, the 16-year-old is widely reported to have chosen Real Madrid as his next landing spot.

Los Blancos are expected to announce a deal for his signature any day now, ahead of Gabriel's arrival in January.

Upon word first surfacing that the young attacker had made the call to depart Old Trafford, the aforementioned Barcelona were also credited with a serious interest in his services.

But as alluded to above, Deco and co. ultimately made the call not to move ahead with any potential offer. As per a report from journalist Nil Sola, speaking to El Larguero:

"They had their eye on him and were monitoring him, but the numbers didn't add up for Barça. It would have cost practically 10 million."
