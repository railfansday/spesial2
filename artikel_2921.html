InterDigital: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Video codec adoption and legal wins are opening new royalty streams beyond its core smartphone licensing business.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 27, 2026 at 2:24AM EST

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 29, 2026, InterDigital (IDCC -1.00%) announced revenue of $193.4 million, exceeding its guidance range of $154 million to $158 million. Annualized recurring revenue, or ARR, reached an all-time high of $629 million as the company secured a patent license with a major personal computer maker and expanded beyond smartphones.

High-margin intellectual property, or IP, licenses provide the business with predictable cash flows to fund ongoing research into wireless and video standards. Three things management said on the earnings call stand out.

InterDigital signed a patent license with a top personal computer, or PC, manufacturer, bringing 70% of the global PC market under license. Revenue from consumer electronics and internet of things programs nearly doubled year over year to $55.7 million.

Major chipmakers and operating systems are building hardware support for Versatile Video Coding, or VVC, an advanced video compression standard. InterDigital holds foundational patents in VVC, allowing it to collect incremental royalties as video streaming adoption grows across mobile devices and platforms.

European courts issued multiple injunctions against The Walt Disney Company over video encoding and casting patents, finding that Disney acted as an unwilling licensee. Legal victories validate the strength of the patent portfolio and push reluctant media companies toward binding arbitration or settled commercial terms.

Revenue from existing contracts is tracking ahead of full-year expectations, and management isn't hiding its satisfaction with operational execution.

Predictable recurring revenues, low capital intensity, and an internal research engine that produces six new patents daily position the business to generate steady free cash flow. Management is watching capital returns, legal enforcement outcomes, and hardware adoption of new video codecs. Long-term fixed-fee contracts protect profit margins and support ongoing research investments alongside share buybacks and dividend payments.
