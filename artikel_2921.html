Capitals star Alex Ovechkin sets new franchise record in Sabres clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Alex Ovechkin has spent more than two decades as the face of the Washington Capitals, captaining the franchise to its first Stanley Cup in 2018 and becoming the NHL's all-time leading goal scorer along the way. On Thursday night against the Buffalo Sabres, he added yet another piece of history to his legendary resume, even if the night ended in disappointment.

Ovechkin picked up an assist in Washington's 6-3 loss to Buffalo at Capital One Arena, giving him 763 for his career, all of them in a Capitals uniform. That moved him past longtime teammate Nicklas Backstrom (762) for the most assists in Capitals history.

Ovechkin had pulled even with Backstrom on Nov. 10, when he set up Cole Hutson's overtime winner in a 3-2 victory over the Ottawa Senators. Now, he stands alone atop the franchise leaderboard, ahead of Backstrom, John Carlson (605) and Michal Pivonka (418).

The milestone carries even more weight given the timing. Ovechkin will retire at the end of the 2026-27 NHL season, making this campaign his farewell tour after 22 seasons with the only NHL franchise he has ever known.

Unfortunately for the Capitals, the record-setting night didn't come with a win. Buffalo jumped out to a 4-1 lead after the first period and never looked back. Jack Quinn led the way for the Sabres with two goals, including an empty-netter, and an assist, while Rasmus Dahlin chipped in a goal and an assist. Zach Benson scored the game-winner, and former Capital Beck Malenstyn also found the back of the net against his old team.

Washington outshot Buffalo 37-18, but Sabres goaltending held strong. Boone Jenner and Ryan Leonard each scored for the Capitals, with Leonard's tally coming on the power play.

The loss extended Washington's skid to four straight games.

Even so, Thursday served as another reminder of just how much Ovechkin has meant to the Capitals, and how much he will be missed once he hangs up his skates.
