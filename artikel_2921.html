SpaceX vs. Anthropic: Here’s Which Mega-IPO Stock I’d Buy After Anthropic Goes Public.

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Anthropic will be a brand-new type of company in the market.

One of the biggest companies ever is planning to go public on Nov. 19: Anthropic. The current record for the largest IPO is held by Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX +3.16%), which went public earlier this year. In a short period, investors will have a chance to buy either (or both) of these two blockbuster 2026 IPOs. But the question is, should you?

Of the two, I'm leaning heavily toward Anthropic, as its valuation is pretty reasonable for the growth it's delivering. While I'm still a SpaceX fan, it's hard to ignore what Anthropic brings to the table.

At first glance, SpaceX and Anthropic don't seem like competitors, but they are. SpaceX is far more than just a rocket and satellite internet company. In fact, by revenue, it is more of an artificial intelligence stock.

Before going public, SpaceX acquired xAI, which owns X (formerly known as Twitter) and also the Grok family of AI models. This division has exploded over the past few months since SpaceX went public, and it now accounts for over half of SpaceX's revenue. Furthermore, it's growing at an incredibly fast pace, rising 830% year over year in Q3. That far outpaces anything that its rocket division or Starlink internet delivered, and it's easy to see that SpaceX is now becoming an AI investment rather than purely a space one.

While the Grok product lineup for AI is quite popular, it has nothing on Anthropic. Anthropic's Claude models have become one of the most popular to deploy in a business setting. That's a big deal, because that's where the vast majority of revenue will be made for AI firms. Anthropic released its figures before going public, and its growth outpaced SpaceX's by a wide margin.

During the first six months of 2026, Anthropic's revenue rose a jaw-dropping 1,305% year over year to $16.3 billion. It's hard to compare that figure to SpaceX's AI division, as it's based on a different time frame and length. But with SpaceX's AI division generating $7.6 billion in revenue during Q3 alone, it's likely closer in size to Anthropic than many people give it credit for.

Another factor some may be surprised to learn is how close these companies are to breaking even. SpaceX's AI divisions generated a $431 million operating profit. Anthropic was also close to breaking even, as it posted a $693 million operating loss during the first six months of the year.

So, these aren't the deeply unprofitable companies that some make them out to be, but which is the better buy between the two?

The question that investors must wrestle with when deciding which one to invest in boils down to whether they want a stock that is 100% exposed to AI proliferation. If you do, then Anthropic takes a slight lead. It will be the only AI lab player on the market, and its success will be directly tied to the AI movement’s success. It's also likely that Anthropic will grow rapidly over the next few years, and regardless of the valuation at which it goes public, that figure will change dramatically over the next five years.

SpaceX has a more diversified business, although it's diving headfirst into AI because it's where the money is now. It has a good product, but it isn't the leading one in the AI realm. This difference can make or break whether SpaceX is a good stock pick for you.

Personally, I think investing in both is a smart idea because these two are among the fastest-growing companies in the market -- they are exposed to a generational growth catalyst. But with Anthropic's great exposure, I'm leaning that way.
