GTA 6 Players Report Issues with Physical Version Pre-Order Codes

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Some GTA 6 buyers say their code-in-a-box editions releases without... a code in the box

With its launch less than a week away, players all over the world have started preloading Grand Theft Auto 6 to ensure they're able to play as soon as it drops. Reports from several fans indicate that some code-in-box copies of GTA 6 include a faulty redemption leaflet, preventing buyers from claiming the game.

Rockstar opted for an all-digital release for Grand Theft Auto 6 for practical reasons. Instead of a disc, physical editions include a cover with the code printed on the back — and a poster for those who pre-ordered the game. You're supposed to see the code from inside the box, as it's made of translucent plastic.

On Nov. 12, retailers started distributing physical pre-orders to allow players to preload GTA 6 and boot it up once it launches on Nov. 19. However, it seems that when some players opened the box, there was no code to be found. There was only a grey strip that looked similar to scratch paper but did not have the right texture, making it impossible to scratch off to reveal the code.

This issue seems to be occurring on copies of the game for both Xbox and PlayStation. According to reports, certain Best Buy locations have refused to refund players, while others have been understanding and solved the problem. Amazon has also returned players' money. GameStop has reportedly told players to reach out to Rockstar, as it is a manufacturing defect. In a Reddit thread about this matter, users Scottish Pancakes and InPartis both said that Rockstar support dismissed the issue, allegedly closing their support tickets without providing any solution.

The issue does not appear to be widespread, given prelaunch sales of GTA 6 thus far and the number of players affected.

The biggest concern for players right now is that this issue won't be solved within the few days that are left until GTA 6 comes out, and they will find themselves unable to play on launch despite buying their copy in advance. According to Take-Two boss Strauss Zelnick, Grand Theft Auto 6 didn't get a disc release because digital media is "much more convenient" than physical media. Ironically, if the game had been distributed using discs rather than codes, these issues could have been avoided.

Grand Theft Auto 6 will be released on Nov. 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X.
