9-1-1 has long been a show known for putting its characters in jeopardy, but with the series' fall finale leaving more questions than answers about what's next for Athena Grant's (Angela Bassett) health, showrunner Tim Minear has spoken up.

While the ABC procedural has built a reputation on its high-intensity disasters and harrowing rescues, 9-1-1 season 10 is looking at a somewhat different story for Athena when the series returns from its fall hiatus. After accusations of being involved in the murder of Detective Ben Hooks (Josh Stamberg), Athena was being questioned by the FBI when she suddenly collapsed after being shown a photo of someone potentially involved in the case.

According to Minear, who spoke to TVLine about the fall finale, there's more going on with Athena than the stress of being investigated for her alleged involvement in Hooks' murder. "There is something physically happening with her, but also when she saw that photograph, she knew that she was kind of screwed," the 9-1-1 showrunner explained, making it clear that he wasn't ruling out any possibilities.

"I'm not saying she didn't have a heart attack," Minear shared, teasing that viewers will "find out [what's happening] quickly enough" upon 9-1-1 season 10's return. The series is slated to be back on ABC on Thursday, January 7 with new episodes, but the wait won't be an easy one for confused viewers.

Minear's answer about Athena's health may be taking the character down an entirely different path than viewers anticipated this season, as there may be something bigger to explore. With the ambiguous answer setting Athena up for some major complications, her potential involvement in the crime could be the least of her worries.

Though 9-1-1 season 10 has consistently brought Athena's stories to the forefront, the series has been struggling since the death of Athena's husband, Bobby Nash (Peter Krause), at the end of 9-1-1 season 8. While Athena has gradually moved into being the focal point of the show, the series has struggled at times to figure out what to do with her character as the stakes of the series continuously increase with each passing episode.

While repeatedly putting Athena in danger is something of a tradition for 9-1-1, seeing something happen with her health wasn't on most viewers' bingo cards for the season, and could unearth a much larger story for the long-running series.
