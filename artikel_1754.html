Scaloni and Fernandez urge calm around Argentina’s ‘new process’

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Argentina boss Lionel Scaloni and midfielder Enzo Fernandez believe the team is in "a new process" following their loss to Uruguay.

In their first match since Lionel Messi's retirement, Argentina lost 2-1 to rivals Uruguay in an international friendly on Friday.

Diego Forlan's side took the lead early in the second half after an own goal from Leonardo Balerdi, before Maxi Araujo quickly doubled their advantage.

Julian Alvarez pulled a goal back for Argentina, but they were unable to secure a draw at Estadio Alberto Jose Armando.

Scaloni's side were on top for much of the contest, winning the expected goals (xG) battle 1.5 to 0.4 after retaining 72% of possession.

Younger players such as Gianluca Prestianni, Agustin Giay and Roman Vega featured off the bench, and Fernandez insisted the future is bright for Argentina.

"I think we were the better team, and the team responded well despite the goals we conceded," said the Manchester City midfielder.

"We were in control from start to finish, which is why we’re leaving frustrated.

"It's a new process, and we're looking to build a great team. I really liked the second half, and some partnerships worked well. There's a future ahead."

Scaloni shared the same sentiment as Fernandez, insisting his players need time to gel as a team.

"Uruguay is tough for Argentina, for Brazil, for Spain and for everyone. They're a good team, and that's why it's good to face them," said Scaloni.

"We knew it was going to be a tough opponent. Unfortunately, we couldn't find the right moment for the players to enjoy their football, but we have to be patient. They're young guys who are going to deliver for us.

"Their team-mates will surely help them do that at their clubs. But let's stay calm, because we can't put all the responsibility on them alone.

"We'll probably make some changes on Tuesday, but it's also important that some players get to play again so they can find their rhythm in this kind of match."

Argentina will face Costa Rica in their second game of the international break on Tuesday.
