Netflix’s ‘3 Body Problem’ Gets Season 2 Premiere Date, Trailer Coming Tomorrow

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The sci-fi epic also released a new description for its highly-anticipated return season.

The sci-fi epic 3 Body Problem is set to return to Netflix.

The adaptation of Liu Cixin’s acclaimed novels is set to continue on Jan. 14. The announcement was made at Netflix’s first appearance at the Texas Book Festival in Austin.

The second season from showrunners David Benioff, D.B. Weiss and Alexander Woo also has a new official description: “The alien invasion nears and humanity prepares to meet them — on Earth and beyond. While a space fleet embarks on a mission that spans centuries, a secret locked inside of one man’s mind might be humanity’s only hope for survival.”

The second season is reportedly shorter than the first, with six episodes instead of eight, though Netflix hasn’t confirmed this. Two years ago, Netflix renewed the series for second and third final seasons which will complete the adaptation.

A teaser trailer for the new season will debut Tuesday.

The first season spent seven weeks in the streamer’s global Top 10 list, reaching the Top 10 in 93 countries and hitting No. 1 in 44 countries, with 46.6 million views.

The cast of 3 Body Problem season two includes returning stars Jess Hong, Benedict Wong, Jovan Adepo, Eiza González, Liam Cunningham, Marlo Kelly, Sea Shimooka, Saamer Usmani, Alex Sharp and Josh Brener. New cast members include Claudia Doumit, Ellie de Lange, Alfie Allen, David Yip and Jordan Sunshine.
