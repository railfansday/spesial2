Roadside Attractions Acquires U.S. Rights to Thriller ‘One Second After’ Based on William R. Forstchen Novel (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Roadside Attractions has acquired U.S. distribution rights to “One Second After,” Scott Rogers’ action thriller based on the 2009 novel by William R. Forstchen. The film is set for theatrical release on April 30, 2027.

“One Second After” marks Roger’s directorial debut. J. Michael Straczynski and Nicole Roewe penned the screenplay. Rob Pfaltzgraff, Nick Reid, Lana Link and Matt Kennedy serve as producers. Executive producers include Richard Masson, Sue Masson, Kenneth Horowitz, Barbara Horowitz, David Gore, Cecilia Gore and Forstchen. “One Second After” is an MPI Original Films production in association with Startling. According to an official logline, “One Second After” follows “former Army Colonel turned college professor John Matherson as a devastating electromagnetic pulse attack instantly disables the nation’s electrical grid, communications and transportation systems. With modern civilization brought to a standstill, Matherson must rally his neighbors to protect their families and community as dwindling resources, mounting desperation and violent outsiders threaten their survival.”

Josh Holloway stars alongside Hannah John-Kamen, Steven Ogg, Famke Janssen and Mary McDonnell.

“’One Second After’ takes a very real concern about the vulnerability of our infrastructure and brings it to life through the story of one family,” said Howard Cohen and Eric d’Arbeloff, Roadside Attractions co-presidents. “We live in a world where we take so much for granted, and the film asks what happens when those systems suddenly disappear. We love that the film can explore something so relevant while being a gripping, wildly entertaining ride.”

Roadside Attraction’s other upcoming films include “The Gallerist,” a crime comedy starring Natalie Portman, Jenna Ortega, Da’Vine Joy Randolph, Sterling K. Brown and Zach Galifianakis, and “Babies” starring Seth Rogen, Anna Kendrick, Issa Rae and Dan Stevens.
