Newcastle fans ‘cannot delete the history I have with the club’, says Guimaraes

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Bruno Guimaraes says he cannot control how Newcastle United fans feel about him after he was booed on his return to St. James' Park.

Guimaraes joined Arsenal for a reported £75m fee from Newcastle in the summer and played against the Magpies for the first time since then on Saturday.

He was loudly jeered by the home contingent before, during and after the 2-2 draw, in which Arsenal had to come from behind to salvage a late point.

Guimaraes spent five seasons on Tyneside, making 195 appearances in all competitions, and becoming the first club captain in 56 years to lift a trophy after helping them to the EFL Cup in 2024-25.

The Brazilian had been a popular figure at Newcastle before his departure, but he said he has no say over how the fans view him now.

Asked about the contrast between that reaction and the instant backing from Arsenal supporters, Guimaraes said: "The welcome has been amazing.

"I think they have been so good for me. I want to thank them for their support.

"For the Newcastle fans, I cannot control the fans. They can do whatever they want. The only thing they cannot do is delete the history I have with the club. But I cannot control the fans, and that's all."

Guimaraes has made a successful start to life at Arsenal, making 15 appearances in all competitions so far and helping them to sit top of the Premier League table.

They are also unbeaten across their first four Champions League matches this season ahead of facing Borussia Dortmund on Tuesday.

But the 29-year-old is confident he is still not yet at his best level for Arsenal, claiming that he thinks Mikel Arteta will help him hit his best.

"Of course, because I scored two goals, I think some fans want me to score every game, but it's not like this; I'm definitely adapting myself.

"I've been here three or four months, and there's still a lot to go. But now I definitely understand my role in the team, the way Mikel wants me to play in the team, so I think I still can play much, much better.

"Mikel is unbelievable. As a manager, he is so focused on the small details: the way you control the ball, the way that you cannot be aligned with your opposite side – some details that I never heard before.

"I just found out, listening to him here, very small details, he is so crazy a little bit with these small details and I try to take as much as I can because he is so passionate on the small details.

"He always says to the midfield to not be aligned with the opposition, to give you two or three seconds ahead of them.

"He showed some clips of me at Newcastle and some clips of me here where he thinks I could do different [things]. I like to keep it a bit more private, but he is trying to take me to another level."
