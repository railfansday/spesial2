Lord Of The Rings: Ascension Officially Announced For 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Lord of the Rings is entering a whole new era with an abundance of spin-offs that are attempting to flesh out the fascinating lore of Middle-earth or simply bring fans back to previously explored key moments. From the Rings of Power TV show to one of the worst Lord of the Rings video games of all time, these spin-offs may vary in quality, but there's no denying that it's always an exciting affair whenever one is announced or indeed successfully launches.

Joining this pantheon of mixed releases is a new title that has a lot of promise, one that has already garnered the affection of fans and looks set to deliver an exciting and ambitious new look at the original trilogy. Serving as a precursor of sorts to the new Lord of the Rings film, Lord of the Rings: Ascension looks to take the series in a whole new direction, offering an expansive and immersive experience that utilizes the strengths of an existing IP to bolster its offerings.

Lord of the Rings: Ascension is an upcoming tabletop deckbuilding game from developer Stone Blade Entertainment. These are the same folks behind the incredible Ascension series, which cemented itself as one of the best deckbuilding games on the market, and of which this new Lord of the Rings title now finds itself a part. LOTR: Ascension was originally crowdfunded on Gamefound, where it found an enormous amount of success, earning $2,410,467 from across 8051 backers, and is now in the throngs of production.

The Ascension deckbuilding games have their own unique set of rules that Lord of the Rings: Ascension largely draws from, but ultimately reimagines. Playable solo or with up to three other people, Lord of the Rings: Ascension sees players go through each of the three original books, completing objectives and attempting not to be corrupted by the power of the One Ring. Featuring recruitable heroes, iconic items, and unique abilities, Lord of the Rings: Ascension aims to be the definitive exploration of these legendary stories in deckbuilding form.

The Gamefound page for Lord of the Rings: Ascension lists the shipping date for backers as December 2027. However, this is obviously subject to change and, as with a lot of crowdfunded projects, could potentially be delayed into 2028. What fans can be guaranteed is that LOTR: Ascension will feature stunning art and high-quality components, just like the many Ascension releases before it. If you're interested, you can still back the Lord of the Rings: Ascension and grab your own copy via the aforementioned Gamefound page.

Each book has been given its own box, which needs to be bought individually. They all feature unique rules and can be experienced standalone. However, there is a trilogy bundle that features all three in one package. Each standalone box costs $60, whereas the trilogy bundle combines them all for $150. Of course, it's yet to be seen whether LOTR: Ascension will hold up under scrutiny. Yet, for now, at least, this Lord of the Rings spin-off release seems to have everything going for it.
