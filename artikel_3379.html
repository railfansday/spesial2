Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade Officially Postponed Until After Holiday

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade has been postponed.

The annual parade held in New York City is a longtime Thanksgiving staple that airs on NBC during the morning of the holiday. From Spider-Man to Grogu, the floats often feature many beloved pop culture characters, and even hints of what's to come in upcoming projects, including Shrek 5's first look at the ogre's sons last year. As the 100th year of the parade, 2026 marks a particularly notable milestone for the event.

Due to severe weather, Macy's shared with NBC News that the parade will take place on Saturday, November 28 at 8:30am ET, instead of on Thanksgiving Day this year. A storm with heavy rain, strong winds, and coastal flooding is expected on the day of the actual holiday. Read Macy's official statement about the delay below:

"The safety of our participants and spectators is our top priority, and after close consultation with our City and agency partners, we have made the decision to move this year’s celebration. Due to the expected severe weather, the 100th March of Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade will move to Saturday, Nov. 28. Our team is working through the operational logistics for this change and will share details as they are finalized. We expect the Parade to kick off at 8:30a.m. on Saturday. Thank you to the thousands of volunteers, performers and partners who create the magic millions enjoy each year. We look forward to celebrating together on Saturday.”

After debuting in 1924, the parade has only been canceled three times, specifically in 1942, 1943, and 1944. This was not due to weather but because the World War II effort required the materials that are needed for the giant balloons. In terms of when the parade's balloons were last grounded because of weather, that was in 1971 as a result of severe weather and rain. New York City regulations prohibit balloons from flying if sustained winds go above 23 miles per hour.
