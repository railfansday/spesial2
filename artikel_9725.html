Antigravity Pet — a free pixel desktop pet that shows what your Antigravity agent is doing, live

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

I built a pixel desktop pet for Google Antigravity that sits on your desktop and shows what your agent is doing, live. It's free and open source.

Repo: https://github.com/Wlaeg/antigravity-pet

It watches your Antigravity session through the official hooks and reacts to it. When the agent is working, the pet works. When it's waiting on you, the pet tells you. When a background task finishes, you get a quiet nudge instead of having to check the window.

Everything runs locally on your machine through Antigravity's official plugin hooks. No account, no server, no data leaves your computer.

One-tap share cards are next, and it's launching on Product Hunt on Dec 1. Tell me which pet you picked.
