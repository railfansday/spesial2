Solstice Advanced Materials Declares Dividend of $0.075 per Common Share

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

MORRIS PLAINS, N.J., Nov. 3, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Solstice Advanced Materials (NASDAQ: SOLS), today announced that its Board of Directors has declared a regular quarterly dividend payment of seven and a half cents ($0.075) per share of the Company's common stock. The dividend will be payable on December 10, 2026, to shareowners of record as of the close of business on November 25, 2026.
