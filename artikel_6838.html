Is It Just Us? Or Was 2026 the Year of Whimsy?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

In an age marred by burnout and algorithms, people are once again making room for the weird, the joyful, and the wonderfully unproductive. We tapped 18 tastemakers to learn how they’re doing it.

Let’s face it: Growing up is hard. One day, you’re listening to The Cheetah Girls soundtrack on the way to school with an armful of Silly Bandz and the next, you’re worried about making rent, hours lost to Google Sheets, and an unexpected medical bill when you still have a $52 request floating in a sea of Venmo transactions.

As for the world we’ve grown into? Nearly two years into Trump’s second presidential term, with AI raising questions about how much of our thinking we’ll hand over to machines, adult life can feel relentlessly optimized and not as chic or shiny as we imagined it as kids. But even if the future feels uncertain, we continue to hope. 2026 has revived the desire to make room for the colorful, playful, and wonderfully impractical again, and that impulse has a name: whimsy.

“As more of our everyday experiences are shaped by technology and algorithms, people are finding value in things that feel more personal, creative, and unexpected. Inspiration is still incredibly important, but so is the freedom to take what you see and make it your own,” Sydney Stanback, global trends and insights lead at Pinterest, tells Cosmopolitan.

“People are craving more imagination and fun in their everyday lives. There is a real appetite for aesthetics that feel less polished and predictable and more personal, and whimsy gives people room to try something they might not have considered before.”

And the numbers don’t lie. Pinterest searches for “bedazzling patterns” have increased by a whopping 9,187 percent this year, while “bedazzled phone case” searches are up 8,012 percent. Even “whimsical office outfits” have jumped 885 percent.

From Kylie Jenner’s bejeweled birthday cake and “princess kitty”–themed fete to the deconstructed silhouettes and glittery garments taking over spring/summer 2027 runways, we’re all finding increasingly creative ways to inject personality into our days. In an increasingly automated world, doing things simply because we can is the ultimate act of rebellion. At its core, whimsy is about making magic out of the mundane, whether we’re revisiting childhood obsessions now that we (sort of) have adult money or taking the DIY approach to life to make it more vibrant.

That’s why Cosmopolitan invited 18 colorful, charismatic, and unapologetically authentic tastemakers to share how they’re romanticizing life and embracing their eccentricities. Because to be whimsical is to be human!
