Personal Consumer Issues • Re: How often do you buy a new car?

Tanggal: 2026-11-14



We have a bunch of new Hondas. None of them require the use of the touchscreen to adjust the climate controls. There are knobs and switches for all of that. There are physical controls for everything just like a car that doesn't have a touchscreen. The touchscreen is only used to configure certain options that only need to be set once. When I am driving, the touchscreen is used for Android Auto (Apple Car Play for Apple users). I don't touch the screen while driving.
FWIW, my spouse's 2021 CRV requires touchscreen use to adjust some climate control stuff. AC on/ off, vent choice, etc. however, it does have physical fan and recirc buttons and temp knobs. So sort of a hybrid interface.
I'm really hoping there will be a trend back to physical interfaces (muscle memory requires zero attention away from the road) for anything meant to be touched during driving.
It's terrific to have a screen interface to adjust clock, display settings, and deep functions (lane departure behavior, ACC sensitivity, etc.) But basic car and cabin functions should be physical interfaces, ideally different in shape and size, so you don't need to look down to adjust them.
Just my opinion.
