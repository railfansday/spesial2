House Of The Dragon: Syrax Officially Releases June 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

House of the Dragon has had a really interesting run so far. After the fairly showstopping Season 1, the series primarily focused on the adult versions of its main characters, much to the chagrin of some fans. Season 2 was also a stopgap of sorts, meant to bridge several stories together.

The main issue was the wait between seasons, but that won't be too much of a problem with this new release around the bend. Now, thanks to a reveal from Iron Studios, we know that House of the Dragons is getting an official release in June 2027, and fans of the show will definitely want to take note.

Coming in June 2027, Iron Studios is producing a Syrax statue based on the design from House of the Dragon, as part of the "Icons" line. It's made out of polystone (as well as resin, PVC, metal, and fabric), containing a hand-painted version of Syrax, clocking in at 11.5"(H) x19.3"(W) x8.9"(D).

Here are all the full details from Iron Studios:

"With golden-yellow scales and a fierce temperament, Syrax is the dragon of Princess Rhaenyra Targaryen in House of the Dragon. Named after a goddess of Old Valyria, she carries Rhaenyra through the skies of King's Landing and Dragonstone and becomes one of the great symbols of the Black faction during the Dance of the Dragons, the civil war that tears House Targaryen apart.

Part of the Iron Studios Icons line, dedicated to the most imposing creatures and figures of pop culture, this statue celebrates Syrax in a large-format display piece. Every scale, horn and membrane of the wings is sculpted and hand-painted to bring the dragon's presence to your collection."

Syrax is a female dragon, and commanded by Rhaenyra Targaryen herself. Typically depicted as yellow, Rhaenyra started riding on Syrax from the age of seven, and gave the dragon her name, based on a Valyrian goddess. It's a pretty huge part of the source material, and has appeared frequently in the show.

As for House of the Dragon, it's just starting to wrap up after being on the air for four years and counting. The first season dropped in 2022, and primarily focused on the friendship between a young Rhaenyra Targaryen and Alicent Hightower. As the political intrigue deepens and the years go on, we're whisked 19 years into the future to see how their relationship plays out amid a battle for the crown.

Season four, currently planned for release in 2028, seems to be the end of the series, as the source material only covered a specific period in time. But that isn't game over for Game of Thrones, as Knight of the Seven Kingdoms was renewed for a second season, and will arrive in 20276, well before House of the Dragon.

For any House of the Dragon fans out there, Syrax is a huge presence (literally and figuratively), so it makes sense that Iron Studios would prioritize making it a focus. The other big statue from Iron Studios is Caraxes, if you're interested in picking that up as well.
