‘They Can Take My Stage’: Macklemore Teases New Song Benefiting Palestinian Mothers & Children

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

"They can't take my faith/ When love is the call," he raps on "Where's Our Love."

Macklemore is continuing his fight for peace in Gaza with a new song called “Where’s Our Love” that will benefit Palestinian mothers and children.

The rapper announced Wednesday (Nov. 18) that the track would drop on streaming services Friday (Nov. 20), sharing a song and music video teaser. Over stirring, reflective piano and clips of both Palestinian suffering and resilience in Gaza, Macklemore spits, “We circled the sun three times/ Have you heard the olive tree cry?/ What have we done?/ Where’s our love?”

The lyric refers to the three years of violence that have unfolded since the Oct. 7, 2023, attacks, in which terrorist organization Hamas killed approximately 1,200 Israelis. More than 74,000 Palestinians have died in Israel’s ensuing war on Hamas, according to Gaza’s Health Ministry via the Associated Press, and the United Nations has classified Israel’s actions as a genocide, something the country and its ally United States deny.

“All proceeds will go to the Mother of Hind Fund, to support mothers and children in Gaza,” Macklemore announced in his caption.

The musician has been one of the most outspoken artists in the stance that Israel is committing genocide, and incorporates “Free Palestine” demonstrations into his performances. This led to his dismissal from Ed Sheeran’s Loop Tour earlier this year as stadium owners such as Robert Kraft — who owns Gillette Stadium — threatened to cancel their scheduled concert dates unless the British pop star’s team dropped the rapper from the lineup.

Macklemore went on to launch his own Free Palestine Tour, selling out arenas in Dublin, Paris and London. When he tried to keep the trek going in the U.S., however, he alleged that multiple venues had more or less turned him down.

“They can take my stage/ They can taint the cause,” he raps on “Where’s Our Love.” “They can’t take my faith/ When love is the call.”

The “Thrift Shop” artist has previously released music in support of Palestinians, dropping “Hind’s Hall” in 2024 and “F–ked Up” in 2025.
