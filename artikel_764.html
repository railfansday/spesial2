Blink-182 and Pierce the Veil to Headline When We Were Young 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Blink-182 and Pierce the Veil have been announced as the headliners of When We Were Young 2027, returning to the Las Vegas Festival Grounds after taking a year off.

The event, presented by 7-Eleven, will take place on October 16, 2027. It features a robust lineup for its fifth edition including Limp Bizkit, Third Eye Blind, The Used, All Time Low, Simple Plan, 5 Seconds of Summer, Circa Survive, Taking Back Sunday, Sleeping With Sirens, The Psychedelic Furs, Hot Mulligan, New Found Glory, Movements, Senses Fail, Neon Trees, Cartel, Plain White T’s, Thursday and many more. The lowest-priced general admission tickets will be available during the first hour of the presale, which starts on November 20 at 10 a.m. PT. A general onsale follows at 11 a.m. PT for any remaining tickets.

The announcement of the 2027 festival comes after organizers announced in this past February that they’re taking a year off before picking back up next year. A specific reason for the delay was not given. “After an unforgettable run in Las Vegas, we’ve decided to take 2026 off to give this festival the care it deserves and to make sure what comes next feels just as special as what came before,” read the statement. “When We Were Young Festival will return to Las Vegas in October 2027. Thank you for showing up with your whole hearts every year. This isn’t goodbye — it’s just a pause.”

When We Were Young had its inaugural year in 20 at Santa Ana, CA’s Observatory. It then moved to Las Vegas for its second iteration in 2022, where it has returned every year through 2025. Headliners in the past have included Blink-182, Panic! at the Disco, My Chemical Romance, Paramore and more.
