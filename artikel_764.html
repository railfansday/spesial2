DWTS' Disney Elimination Is Not the Fairest of Them All

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Dancing With the Stars bid a fond farewell to one couple on the Nov. 2 Disney Night episode as the duo enjoyed their final magical moment on the dance floor.

Despite a magical evening, one couple had to leave the happiest ballroom floor on Earth during the Nov. 2 Disney Night episode of Dancing With the Stars.

Although they wowed the judges with a Paso Doble set to “Satisfied” from the musical Hamilton, Julia Stiles and her partner Ezra Sosa would no longer be in the room where it happens.

“It's been an incredible experience. I pulled things out of myself that I didn't realize were there,” Stiles said through tears following the elimination. “And Ezra is just an amazing person. I'm going to be sad not to see him every day.”

Sosa was equally downhearted to be saying goodbye, especially because he had seen Stiles grow so much as a dancer throughout the experience.

“Just to see your growth in and outside the ballroom has been an absolute honor,” he told the Save the Last Dance actress. “I'm so lucky that I got to share this experience with you. I love you so much.”

Still, plenty of couples had no worries for the rest of their days during the evening, as Harry Shum Jr. and partner Jenna Johnson received a perfect score for their jazz routine to “Supercalifragilisticexpialidocious” from Mary Poppins, as did Jenna Dewan and Val Chmerkovskiy for their quickstep to “Be Our Guest” from Beauty and the Beast.

Amber Glenn was also ready to shout it from the mountaintops with her partner Pasha Pashkov as the two quickstepped to “A Star Is Born” from Hercules, as Ezra Frech and Daniella Karagach let the audience be part of their world, dancing a samba to “Under the Sea” from The Little Mermaid.

Disney’s Broadway adventures also got a moment on the dance floor, as Tatyana Ali and her partner Jan Ravnik took a page from the Broadway book, dancing a contemporary routine to “Easy as Life” from the Disney-produced musical Aida.

Disney Night was certainly a wonderland away from last week’s Horror Movie Night, where the dancers said goodbye to two couples: Savannah Bananas baseball player Jackson Olson and his partner Emma Slater, as well as Bachelor Nation alum Tyler Cameron and pro dancer Sharna Burgess.

And luckily, the dance pros would always have a friend in their partners, with Burgess raving to E! News how close she and Cameron had become outside the ballroom.

"There's been a lot of things happening in my life that he's been an incredible friend through. He's so grounded for his age. He's so well beyond his years that he surprises me sometimes with his wisdom and his depth,” she shared. "I found an amazing human that I just want to keep in my life."
