Solana Walks a Tightrope as ETF Outflows Persist and Spot Volume Turns “Heating”

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Solana is under renewed pressure after dropping below $107, as ETF outflows and spot sellers have pushed the market into a defensive phase.

Solana is under fresh pressure after falling below the important $107 support level. Analysts have pointed to heavy ETF outflows and plummeting spot trading volume as the latest developments pushing the market back into a defensive phase.

SOL is currently caught between a rock and a hard place, hovering around $95 on Monday, Oct. 19, 2026. The price has fallen 5.81% over the past week as short-term weakness battles a longer-term recovery. The asset is facing pressure to move above the crucial $107 support zone. Analysts believe this could decide whether the market falls further or renew its recovery.

The asset is facing massive liquidity pressure as persistent outflows from spot ETFs create sustained downward pressure on the fifth-largest cryptocurrency. Recent analysis from analyst Sovereign Yield shows Solana is trapped below its critical support region of $107, suggesting potential volatility ahead for SOL investors.

Persistent ETF outflows signal that institutional demand for SOL has weakened significantly in October, according to data from Sovereign Yield. U.S. spot Solana ETFs registered $25.52 million in net outflows in the week ending Oct. 16. This follows another $ 24.81 million in outflows the previous week. This marks two straight weeks of unprecedented withdrawal levels and net outflows. According to Sovereign Yield’s report, this shift has significantly weakened institutional buying appetite that previously supported price rises.

Meanwhile, a report from CryptoQuant analysts indicates further difficulties in the Solana market. According to the CryptoQuant data, spot Solana trading volume shows a bearish shift, as spot selling volume has surpassed spot buying volume for the first time in more than six months. This trend reversal signals a shift in market sentiment.

According to the data, Solana’s spot volume bubble map signals weakening buying interest, rising investor caution, and more common risk-off positioning in the market.

The two key metrics above (from Sovereign Yield and CryptoQuant analysts) show that demand for Solana ETFs and spot selling has weakened in recent weeks. This indicates lower investor confidence.

Bearish signals have risen sharply, rising concerns about a greater correction. If selling pressure continues, the analysts believe Solana could drop below the $100 level soon.
