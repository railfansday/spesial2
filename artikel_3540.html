Rams’ Kyren Williams Jokes About Matthew Stafford’s Glow-Up: ‘Like a Fine Wine’

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Los Angeles Rams running back Kyren Williams acknowledged that his quarterback, Matthew Stafford, has undergone a noticeable glow-up.

“He’s looking good,” Williams, 26, exclusively told Us about Stafford, 38, via his partnership with Invisalign. “He’s like a fine wine.”

In addition to Matthew’s increasingly youthful physical appearance, Williams explained how the quarterback’s love for his wife, Kelly, and their four daughters positively influences the entire organization.

“It’s so cool because not only do you see them on game days, you also see them throughout the week,” Williams said of the Stafford family. “Sometimes they’ll be in the facility. I remember one time I was working out — it was such a cool moment, I told my whole family about it — I was working out one morning and he and his family were there shooting a podcast for the Rams. All of his daughters came up to me as I was working out and asked me questions and whatnot. I was like, ‘Man, that’s what it’s all about.’”

Matthew and Kelly, 37, share twins Sawyer and Chandler, 9, Hunter, 8 and Tyler, 6.

“At the Rams, we’re a family,” Williams continued. “We’re family-oriented. That has allowed us to stay connected throughout even the hard times, throughout the rough start and the things that didn’t go our way.”

Widely regarded as the odd-on favorite to win the Super Bowl at the start of the season, it’s been a roller-coaster thus far for the Rams. Heading into a Thanksgiving Eve game against the Green Bay Packers on Netflix, Los Angeles is 6-4 and three games back of the first-place San Francisco 49ers in the NFC West.

“Being able to have a guy like Matthew, not only is he a great father off the field, but he’s an even better leader,” Williams said. “He’s on the field always making calls, always putting us in situations where he can see us succeed. Not only does he do that, but he also motivates the guys around him.”

He added, “It’s just been a really cool blessing to be able to play with Matthew, because I remember back in the day being back at my grandma’s house on Thanksgiving and the Detroit Lions were always on. I’ve always watched Matthew Stafford and idolized his game, just how tough he was and how hard he played.”

Matthew played the first 12 seasons of his NFL career with the Lions before being traded to the Rams in 2021.

Away from football, Williams has partnered with Invisalign and their “SHIFT Happens” campaign.

“My whole life, my teeth haven’t been the way I wanted them to,” he admitted. “When I was able to get around the NFL and get around guys that have been through the Invisalign treatment, I was able to see the results of how their smiles changed and you can see how confident they walk around the locker room.”

Williams continued, “I was like, ‘OK, bet.’ This was my chance to be able to get with a great company and be able to fix a thing that I’ve always wanted to fix my whole life. It was a no-brainer.”
