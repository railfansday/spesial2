Vote for the Knoxville TVA Employees Credit Union girls athlete of the week for Nov. 16-20

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Here are the candidates for the Knoxville TVA Employees Credit Union girls athlete of the week. Voters have until noon on Nov. 25 to submit their responses.

Maddie Braden, CAK: Braden opened the season with a 42-point performance, along with three rebounds, to lead CAK to an 87-77 win over Stone Memorial.

Zoe Chömpff, Carter: Chömpff won the girls Region 1 bowling individual qualifier tournament with a total score of 1,127.

Paisley Leach, Carter: Leach totaled 36 points, 10 rebounds and five steals in an 80-53 win over Greenback.

TSSAA BASKETBALL: CAK's Maddie Braden opens TSSAA basketball season with 42 points

Jailie Rodd, Clinton: Rodd had 23 points, three rebounds, two assists and two steals in Clinton's 62-58 win over Tellico Plains.

Abby Roberts, Grace Christian: Roberts recorded 17 points, eight rebounds, four assists, two steals and a block in Grace's 49-35 win over Lenoir City.

Maria Lyde, Greeneville: Lyde had 23 points, six rebounds, two assists and a steal in a 67-42 loss to Maryville.

Katie Graves, Heritage: Graves had 22 points, two rebounds and two assists in a 75-39 win over Halls. She went 6-of-9 from 3-point range.

Lilly Woodruff, McMinn Central: Woodruff had 19.5 points, 7.5 rebounds, 4.0 steals and 3.0 rebounds across wins over Copper Basin and Cumberland County.

Kennady Phillips, Morristown East: Phillips had 28 points, 17 rebounds, three assits and a steal in a 56-53 loss to Daniel Boone.

Ashlyn Finch, Pigeon Forge: Finch had 18 points, 10 rebounds, six assists and two steals in a 58-31 win over Union County.

Katelynn Reano, Rockwood: Reano recorded 19 points, 18 rebounds and three blocks in Rockwood's 46-27 win over Sweetwater.

Neylan Wilcox, West: Wilcox tallied 36 points, three rebounds and two steals as West defeated Carter, 68-63.
