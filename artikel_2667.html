‘The 49ers Are the Best Team in the NFL,’ ‘The Super Bowl Favorite’ — 5 NFL Analysts React to San Francisco’s Statement Win Over Vikings

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The San Francisco 49ers and Minnesota Vikings faced off in front of a roaring crowd at the historic Estadio Banorte in a highly anticipated clash between two of the NFL's most explosive teams. San Francisco entered the game with an 8-1 record, while Minnesota arrived at 7-2.

However, the 49ers came out on top, securing a comfortable 35-10 win on Sunday night. Minnesota had been seen as one of San Francisco's biggest obstacles in the race for the NFC's No. 1 seed, and the 49ers have now strengthened their position as the conference's leading contender.

San Francisco's performance prompted several NFL analysts to weigh in on the team's Super Bowl prospects.

San Francisco established control from the opening possession in Sunday's game, putting together an efficient seven-play, 72-yard drive to find the end zone. The Vikings struggled to slow the 49ers' offense, allowing 28 first-half points as the game quickly slipped away from reach.

PFN analyst Jason Katz believes the win answered an important question about the 49ers' championship credentials.

"It's not as if the 49ers don't have quality wins. But proving they can not only win, but put up a number on the best defense in football, firmly establishes them as the NFC frontrunner," Katz said.

"The foundation is certainly tenuous. This is the oldest offense in football, and they've already dealt with several injuries. They will need their players to stay upright, but as long as that happens, they will be the team to beat."

PFN analyst Kyle Soppe even identified the 49ers as his current Super Bowl favorite. "This is the Super Bowl favorite in my eyes right now. They've won ugly, they've won shootouts," Soppe said. "Their lone loss saw them caught up in a one-score game at the two-minute warning despite a -4 turnover differential."

PFN's Brandon Austin also argued that San Francisco should currently be considered the favorite to win the Super Bowl.

"As it stands right now, the 49ers should be the favorites to win it all," Austin said. "With Kyle Shanahan at the controls, Brock Purdy playing at an MVP level, and a defense trending up, the sky is the limit. The numerous injuries haven't slowed them down, so why can't the Niners get over the hump this year?"

Brock Purdy dismantled Minnesota's defense with three touchdown passes in the opening half. He finished 21-for-26 for 255 yards and a 146 passer rating, ranking No. 1 in the league with a 94.1 PFN QB Impact Grade.

Christian McCaffrey recorded 49 rushing yards and a touchdown on 12 carries while adding two receptions for 13 yards. His six-yard touchdown run in the fourth quarter extended his scoring streak to nine consecutive games.

PFN analyst Brandon Astreicher considers San Francisco the NFL's best team, but he shared Katz's concerns about the team's ability to stay healthy.

"Someone is going to have to play near-perfect to take down this 49ers squad. The Colton McKivitz injury is going to hurt, but they should be okay. This offense made the best defense in the NFL look average at best.

"Brock Purdy is balling, CMC is doing what he does, and Mike Evans is playing some elite ball. Fred Warner and this defense are good enough to get them to a Super Bowl and potentially win it. It might be Shanahan's time."

PFN analyst Jacob Infante also declared San Francisco the best team in the NFL.

"The 49ers are the best team in the NFL. The way that they utterly dismantled the best defense in the league like it was nothing is nothing short of mesmerizing," Infante wrote. "Kyle Shanahan has shown that he can get the most out of what he has to work with, injuries at wide receiver be damned.

"Brock Purdy is also playing at an incredibly high level. Combine that with the defensive growth we've seen from this unit, and you have a recipe for disaster for the rest of the NFL."

The last two times the 49ers played the Vikings, they were dominated by the Minnesota defense, with San Francisco failing to score more than 17 points in either matchup. However, that changed on Sunday, with Kyle Shanahan's offense dominating from start to finish.

The 49ers will now seek to continue the momentum in the upcoming game against the Seattle Seahawks.
