Patriots vs. Packers live updates: Score, news, discussion for NFL Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Going up against the NFC has not been a pleasant experience for the New England Patriots so far this season. The hope is that this will change on Sunday against the Green Bay Packers.

The team of head coach Mike Vrabel is 0-2 against the conference so far, losing in Week 1 to the Seattle Seahawks and again in Week 7 to the Chicago Bears. So, why might Week 9 be different? For starters, the Patriots are playing in front of their home crowd after playing both the Seahawks and Bears on the road.

Ultimately, however, the performance on the field will decide whether the crowd — home or road — will have something to cheer for.

Let's find out together if that is going to be the case. Welcome to our Patriots-Packers live tracker and community discussion.

Sunday, Nov. 8, 4:25 p.m. ET | Gillette Stadium (Foxborough, MA) | Broadcast information | Game day roster | Inactives | Weather forecast

Starting the game on offense, it took the Patriots only two plays to turn the ball over against one of the NFL's most opportunistic defenses. An apparent miscommunication between Drake Maye and Mack Hollins resulted in an uncontested interception, and a return that set the visitors up at the New England 9-yard line.

Just two plays later, Green Bay quarterback Jordan Love found an opening up the middle and scrambled 13 yards for the game's. first touchdown. | Patriots 0 : 7 Packers

New England's second possession of the game went better than the first, but only barely. While the unit did not turn the ball over this time around, it went three-and-out after a Drake Maye sack on third down.

Whereas the Patriots offense struggled to move the ball to start the game, the Packers had no such issues. Their second possession included back-to-back big pass plays to Matthew Golden for 16 and 49 yards to put them into into the red zone again, as well as an 11-yard Christian Gonzalez pass interference penalty to negate an interception. Golden capped off the series with a 5-yard touchdown when he got wide open on a busted coverage. | Patriots 0 : 14 Packers

Drake Maye hit his first pass of the day to start the Patriots' third series, connecting with Mack Hollins for a gain of 5 yards. The successful drive starter set up New England's first conversions of the day on throws to Hunter Henry and Efton Chism, the second putting the home team in Green Bay territory. Once there, however, things went wrong again: a Rhamondre Stevenson run on first down was blown up for a loss of 7, with a subsequent deep ball on 3rd-and-17 falling incomplete when Kyle Williams seemingly failed to locate the football in the air.
