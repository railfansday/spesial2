Rams vs Packers live updates, TV channel, start time, where to watch Wednesday NFL game

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Who says Thanksgiving football has to wait until Thursday?

For the first time in league history, the holiday festivities are kicking off Wednesday night as the Los Angeles Rams host the Green Bay Packers in a Thanksgiving Eve showdown.

And while the novelty of a Wednesday night NFL game is certainly worth talking about, there's plenty more on the line than turkey-day bragging rights. Both teams enter at 6-4, fighting for valuable positioning in an increasingly crowded NFC playoff race.

The Rams came into the season as the Super Bowl favorites, but things haven't exactly gone according to plan. Injuries have piled up, and superstar receiver Puka Nacua is sidelined for the rest of the season, leaving Matthew Stafford and head coach Sean McVay plenty of work to do. Still, don't count Los Angeles out. The reigning MVP can keep pace with just about anyone, and the Rams boast a formidable defense featuring Myles Garrett, Aaron Donald and Trent McDuffie.

On the other sideline, Green Bay gets a massive boost with All-Pro pass rusher Micah Parsons returning for the first time since tearing his ACL on December 14, 2025. The Packers' offense is spearheaded by a passing game revolving around Jordan Love, Matthew Golden and Christian Watson.

Between the playoff implications, star power and historic scheduling, this one has all the ingredients for a Thanksgiving Eve treat.

Only fans in the local markets of the Rams and Packers can watch the Thanksgiving Eve game on cable television. Here's where those fans can tune in:

TV channel Los Angeles (Local only): KNBC

TV channel Green Bay (Local only): WGBA (NBC 26)

TV channel Milwaukee (Local only): WTMJ (TMJ4)

The Week 12 Wednesday night game between the Rams and Packers will stream on Netflix at 8:00 p.m. ET.
