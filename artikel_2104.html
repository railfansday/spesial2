QPR handed double injury boost ahead of Millwall clash as Julien Stephan confirms Tariq Lamptey is back training

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

QPR head into the November international break on a positive note after securing a 2-1 home victory over Lincoln City. Richard Kone gave the Hoops an early lead, but Dom Jefferies restored parity for the visitors. However, QPR had the final say as Glen Kamara struck in the 80th minute to secure all three points.

Julien Stephan’s side have started this season well and are set for a major boost as they return from the break. As reported by West London Sport, the Hoops will welcome back duo Rumarn Burrell and Nicolas Madsen against Millwall.

Stephan confirmed that the pair have been back in training and while they may not be fit to start, they will can be involved. The QPR boss also revealed that Tariq Lamptey is also closing in on a return and began full training again on Tuesday.

QPR’s hopes of sustaining a Championship play-off challenge have received a significant boost with the return of Madsen, Burrell and Lamptey. Madsen is arguably the most influential of the trio, having previously earned Player of the Season recognition.

The midfielder has also contributed goals against West Ham and Derby this season, offering midfield control, energy and an additional threat from deeper areas. Burrell provides valuable attacking depth after scoring 10 goals and registering three assists in his first Championship campaign.

He had added two goals and an assist this season before suffering an injury on international duty with Jamaica. Richard Kone’s recent hat-trick against Swansea has been encouraging, but Burrell offers further pace and competition, particularly with Alfie Lloyd sidelined long-term.

Lamptey’s availability is equally important given Amadou Mbengue’s season-ending ACL injury. The summer free signing brings Premier League experience, pace and attacking quality, strengthening a position where options have been limited by injuries.

QPR sit eighth after 15 games, with 24 points, and these returns give Stephan greater flexibility ahead of a demanding period, starting with Millwall away. None are guaranteed to walk straight into the starting XI, but having three experienced players available should help sustain their play-off ambitions.

QPR have had a good start to the season, but they have also had some concerns. Stephan’s side have struggled to pick up results consistently with three of their six wins coming in their opening four games.

Having these extra three options is a massive boost to the squad as a whole. The trio give Stephan a lot more variation to keep things fresh as they look to get a streak of good results on the go.
