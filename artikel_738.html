Busta Rhymes Breaks Down His Billboard Chart History: ‘I Know What You Want,’ ‘Don’t Cha’ & More

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Busta Rhymes takes us through his Billboard Chart History, from the “Woo Hah!! Got You All in Check” breakthrough to hitmaking moments such as “I Know What You Want” and “Touch It.” Busta breaks down how a Sugarhill Gang lyric sparked the “Woo Hah!!” hook, why he sat on the record for months and how his album visuals helped define the iconic Hype Williams era.

He also reflects on the nerves (and the excitement) of working with Janet Jackson, the behind-the-scenes of building a blockbuster video and how “Touch It” turned into a culture-shifting moment. Plus: Busta reacts to The Pussycat Dolls story about getting him on “Don’t Cha” and why it made perfect sense at that moment in his career.

Busta Rhymes: Hey, yo, what’s good? This is Busta Rhymes and this is my Billboard Chart History.

My man Rashad Ringo Starr Smith produced it and, um, I had just recently done the album cover photo shoot. When I got the beat from Rashad, I actually sat on the record for about seven months. I couldn’t really come up with nothing to it. Red Alert was DJing the “Old School at Noon” at the time, when he was still on Hot 97 and, um, he played Sugarhill Gang’s “8th Wonder” and it, it just kinda hit me when I heard that. That sparked the idea because there’s a line in that song that ac- actually became the hook with the “woo-ha, got you on the check.”  Once I had that hook, I then took the album package photo shoot to Hype and said, “I want the video to look like the album package.” So all of the outfits matching the environments was exactly what the photo shoot was for the album. So we just kinda replicated that and that’s how me and Hype, the magic started from there with us.

We was in the studio at the same time, but I, I wrote mines in a different room. I di-I didn’t wanna crowd her space and I wanted her to feel comfortable when she was recording her … her parts and, um, I also didn’t want anything to go wrong. You know what I’m saying? So I … I, I gave her her space and, um, it was like an anxiety and a torture at the same time. Because I’m like, yeah, she’s right next door and I don’t wanna go in there and interrupt if she in the middle of cutting her parts.
