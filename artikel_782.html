Dawson: Rashid is still England's No 1 white-ball spinner

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Liam Dawson has been preferred to Adil Rashid in ODIs of late but spinners play together in T20s, helping England into a 2-1 series lead over Australia with two matches left; Dawson hails two more spinners in James Coles and Rehan Ahmed ahead of Sunday's fixture in Sydney (8.15am UK)

Liam Dawson insists Adil Rashid remains England's No 1 white-ball spinner - even though he has seemingly taken the Yorkshireman's place as first-choice pick in ODIs.

Stalwart Rashid, 38, has been left out of England's last six 50-over encounters - three in Pakistan and then three in Australia - with Dawson the frontline slow bowler for those games.

The duo form a potent pairing in T20 internationals, though, and bowled economically - and in Dawson's case prolifically, as he took 3-16 from three overs - in Brisbane on Friday as England took a 2-1 lead over Australia in the five-match series.

Speaking ahead of Sunday's fourth fixture in Sydney (8.15am UK), Dawson, 36, said: "I'm not sure I've knocked him out [of the team] because it's all conditions dependent, but he's still England's best white-ball spinner, without doubt," he said.

"I wouldn't look too far into what's happened on this trip, or in Pakistan before that. He'll certainly come good again because he's a world-class performer."

On his own ascent into a key player for England, Dawson said: "Having the backing of [captain Harry Brook] for the past 18 months, and to play as much as I have, has been brilliant. I've really enjoyed it and hopefully I can carry on and keep performing.

"It's obviously nice but I'm very realistic from a personal point of view. I know how good Rash is, everybody in world cricket knows how good he is."

Dawson also hailed two more England spinners, in Sussex's James Coles, who made his debut against Australia in this T20 series, and Rehan Ahmed, who has been added to the squad for the final two fixtures following Will Jacks' trip ending early due to a broken hand.

The Hampshire bowler added: "Colesy has done so well for someone so young, I think people forget that, and he's got a hell of a lot of cricket ahead of him.

"Rehan's a great lad, I've known him a couple of years now and he's one of the hardest workers - always wants to bat, always wants to bowl, always wants to field. He's got a fantastic attitude.

"I've played a lot of cricket and I'm a lot older than those guys so I'll chat to them in training.

"You've always got to find your own way as well, work it out a little bit, but I'm very happy to speak to them both and help, of course."

All T20 internationals to start at 8.15am UK time
