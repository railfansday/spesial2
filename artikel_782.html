Oprah’s Favorite Things 30th Anniversary List Includes Actor-Owned Apparel, Celeb-Approved Skin Care and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

It’s been three decades since the beloved host launched her annual list, and we’re celebrating by shopping this year’s picks.

The year was 1996. Bill Clinton won his second presidential election. Dolly the sheep became the first barnyard clone. Hotmail launched (and worked). And Oprah Winfrey, host of The Oprah Winfrey Show, and arguably the biggest star on daytime television, introduced her first-ever “Favorite Things” segment.

It all happened by accident. A producer on the show gave the host a pair of Karen Neuburger pajamas. Oprah loved them so much that she bought them for her entire staff. Then, she decided to gift them to her entire audience. On Nov. 27, 1996, Oprah came onstage in her red-and-white checkered PJs, dancing to “My Favorite Things” from The Sound of Music, and told everyone in the crowd that they’d be taking their own pair home. And with that, history was made (as were household names). Oprah’s Favorite Things became a way for the star to introduce her viewers to new or lesser-known brands. Many, including Spanx, Ugg and Tory Burch, attribute their early success to their inclusion on Oprah’s annual list.

This year, Oprah celebrates 30 years of her Favorite Things with a roundup that includes must-haves from both well-known brands (Shark and Ninja) and under-the-radar names (Maison Wheat and McEvoy Ranch). The TV host’s picks include skin care saviors, home accessories, kitchen gadgets and stylish accessories. Even better: all of them are available to purchase online on Amazon, so you can save yourself the trip (and line up) at the mall.

Whether you’re starting your holiday gift shopping or just looking for yourself, here are a few of our favorite things from Oprah’s Favorite Things.

Uggs aren’t just for your feet. These stunning Sheepskin UGGbraid Gloves are an elevated way to stay warm all winter long. Not only are they made from genuine sheepskin to keep your hands toasty, they’re also touchscreen-friendly, so you don’t have to take them off every time you text.

Plus, they’re available in black, chestnut, and anchor grey, so there’s a pair to match every style. Cozy, tech-savvy, and chic? Sign us up.

Maison Wheat made the list for the second year, with Oprah selecting the brand’s Helena Long Sleeve and Hampton Jogger as two of her favorite loungewear pieces.In fact, the talk show host said she wore these pieces for days on end because they were so soft and comfortable.

Maison Wheat co-founder and Pretty Little Liars actor Huw Collins says it was “a great honor” to have the TV legend’s stamp of approval. “Oprah’s word is gold, and someone whose impact reaches far beyond the TV screen. Her love of Maison Wheat last year led to a slew of new fans for the brand, so naturally we’re thrilled to have been selected by her for a second year in a row. Nothing makes us happier that she feels as comfy in her pieces as thousands of other people do in theirs.”

Oprah discovered Black-owned beauty brand Noël Jolié while appearing on Keke Palmer’s podcast, and she quickly became a fan. Started by Kela Nabors and named after her youngest daughter, the wellness brand focuses on products that nourish your skin and well-being. This body cream has a creamy, whipped texture and a formula that leaves your skin feeling soft and smooth.

Inspired by the stylish women she saw in Paris, Oprah loves these Echo Half and Half Oversized 50″ Silk Squares. Available in two colorways, the 100 percent silk scarves are just as versatile as they are luxurious. Their generous size means they can be worn around the waist, shoulders, neck, and more. Oprah calls them “so chic,” and we agree.

Is there anything more relaxing than a long, hot bath? Oprah thinks not. She says that bathing is her hobby, so she knows what she’s talking about when she says this BADESOFA Back Bath Pillow is a must-have.

Not only does it provide ergonomic head, neck, and back support in the tub, it also has so-called “CleanDry+ Technology,” which prevents it from soaking up the water (I.e. it dries fast). Available in three sizes, this smart pillow fits in any tub, and it comes in a wide range of soothing colors to up any bathroom’s aesthetic appeal.

The Shark ChillPill 3-in-1 Fan, Mist and Cooling Plate is perfect for your “hotflashing friends” (Oprah’s words, not ours). Drop 16 degrees in seconds with this flexible little gadget, which can be worn, attached, or twisted for hands-free tabletop mode.

The personal cooling fan has ten speeds and an option to “dry-touch” mist without soaking your clothes or hair. With an 11-hour battery and USB-C charger, it’s a wearable, portable, packable must-have for anyone who runs hot.

Oprah isn’t the only celebrity fan of the ChillPill — Justin Bieber dropped an orange-colored version in conjunction with his SKYLRK brand during Coachella this year.

Who knew Oprah was a Mackenzie-Childs girlie? She chose the brand’s Electric Salt and Pepper Grinder set for this year’s list. The ceramic grinders can be adjusted to match your meal (think coarse salt on your avocado toast and powdery salt on your popcorn) and are crafted from sturdy stainless steel.

While the set is available in four colorways, our pick is the iconic black and white check. It’s a great way to add a little whimsy—and a little flavor—to your kitchen.

The Artisan Olive Oil Gift Set from McEvoy Ranch, a family-owned, 550-acre working farm in Petaluma, California, known for the world’s best estate-produced extra virgin olive oil, might just be the ultimate hostess gift from the ultimate (talk show) host.

Complete with fig bruschetta, savory fig jam, fig vinegar, and organic extra virgin olive oil, it’s a delicious way to say happy holidays.

The coffee lover in your life deserves the best brew, and this Terra Kafe Demi is the way to make it. This intuitive espresso machine (which also makes Americano, lungo, and drip) serves up barista-quality coffee in three easy steps. Just grind the beans, choose your preferred beverage, and let it brew its thing. Not only does it make delicious coffee, it serves looks on the counter. Available in four stylish shades (Oprah has “dune” in her kitchen, seen here), it looks just as good as the coffee tastes.

Oprah is a busy woman. She doesn’t have time to be running two cycles in the air fryer. That’s why she loves the Ninja Crispi DualZone Glass Air Fryer. The double-basket style allows home cooks (or their private chefs) to cook two foods two ways and finish at the same time. Each side has a four-quart capacity, allowing room to cook meat and veggies or dinner and dessert simultaneously. The baskets even double as storage containers, so leftovers don’t have to go to waste.

Oprah has always been open about her fitness journey, so it’s no wonder she’s a fan of the Withings Body Scan Scale. More than just a way to track your weight, it’s a whole-body checking device that tracks your health and wellness in one compact place.

Capture your essential health biomarkers; see a breakdown of your fat and muscle across five body zones, monitor your heart with a 6-lead ECG, and track your electrodermal activity for cues on how your body handles stress and recovery. The scale even pairs with smartphones to connect users to medical professionals, turn data into a health plan, optimize sleep, and so much more. It’s no wonder Oprah calls it an “ingenious machine.”
