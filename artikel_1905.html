Stefan Borson drops verdict on 49ers exit in full Leeds United takeover

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

49ers Enterprises are unlikely to consider a full sale of Leeds United in the foreseeable future.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that the American owners are much more likely to seek further investments.

Leeds have done incredibly well under Daniel Farke, currently sitting seventh in the Premier League table after 11 games this season.

This comes just one season after the Whites went on a great run to the FA Cup semi-finals before losing 1-0 to Chelsea at Wembley Stadium.

Recently, rumours have sparked of the 49ers potentially looking into a sale of the club after it was learned that secretive bank, Allen and Co., had been hired.

Their main job was to take a valuation of Leeds, with former Aston Villa CEO suggesting that an exit is not off the cards at Elland Road.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, though, Borson has squashed the idea of Leeds' owners wanting to seek a complete sale in West Yorkshire.

"Well, it won't... I wouldn't see it as a fear. That, I think, is much more along the lines of expected co-investment," he said.

"We've talked about it in terms of whether it's going to be possible [a full takeover], given all the competition for American co-investment.

"But, I think if they can get it, if they can get another partner on board, then it's probably positive."

Leeds' valuation could soar even higher if the team's performances continue on the pitch, but that's a pretty big if.

Qualification for European football would be an incredible achievement for the Whites, and at this rate, it is certainly a possibility.

Farke's side have won 17 points from a possible 33 so far, and are just four points off the top four spots ahead of facing Coventry City.

Opta has predicted Leeds to finish sixth in the Premier League come the end of the season, with a 15.83 per cent chance of claiming a Champions League spot.

The Europa Conference League looks like a far more achieveable target for the Whites, but even that would boost the club's valuation significantly.
