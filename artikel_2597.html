Chiefs-Bills Week 12 Tuesday injury report: Kenneth Walker returns limited

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Each practice day this season, the Kansas City Chiefs release an official injury report ahead of the next game. In Week 12, the Chiefs play the Buffalo Bills on Thanksgiving night.

Here is the Chiefs' second official injury report of the week, courtesy of the team's official website.

After practice estimations on Monday's report, Tuesday featured the teams actually on the practice field preparing for the game.

Running back Kenneth Walker (groin) was upgraded to a limited participant and practiced on Tuesday for the first time since injuring his groin prior to the Week 10 game against the Atlanta Falcons.

This is what head coach Andy Reid said during his press conference on Tuesday about Walker potentially playing this week.

"Let's see how he does today," Reid said. "I just want to get him out there and see—really, he wants to get out there and see, but we'll see how he moves around. He's feeling pretty good though."

Starting right tackle Jaylon Moore (ankle) did not participate in practice again and hasn't since leaving the Week 9 game against the New York Jets in the third quarter with an ankle injury.

Right guard Trey Smith (concussion) was a full participant in practice again and appears to be set to return after entering concussion protocol during the first quarter of the Week 10 game. Andy Reid Smith "will probably be in" the game this week on Tuesday.

Second-year defensive tackle Omarr Norman-Lott (knee) was a limited participant in practice again. He was activated from the Reserve/Physically-Unable-to-Perform list last week, but was scratched on gameday.

Tight end Noah Gray (rib) was upgraded to a full participant in practice after missing on Monday after leaving the game against the Arizona Cardinals late with the injury.

Fullback Ben VanSumeren (knee) was placed on the Reserve/Injured list on Tuesday.

Starting cornerback Christian Benford (groin) was estimated as a did not participate in practice on Monday and missed last week's game. In eight games played this season, Benford has 33 tackles and eight passes defended. In his career against Kansas City, Benford has 2 passes defended, one sack and a forced fumble.

Starting tight end Dalton Kincaid (thumb/ankle) was estimated to be limited in practice on Monday. He missed last week's game and has been banged up for a few weeks. This season, Kincaid has seen the second-most catches on the team (32) and has 487 yards and two touchdowns; Kincaid has one touchdown in his career against the Chiefs.

Check back in for Buffalo's official injury report after Tuesday's practice.
