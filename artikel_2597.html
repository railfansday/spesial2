3 potential X-factors for the Jets offense vs. Dolphins in Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The New York Jets have an extremely winnable game against the Miami Dolphins in Week 12. Head coach Aaron Glenn's squad will attempt to stack consecutive victories for the first time this season.

After losing to the Dolphins (1-9) earlier this season, the Jets (3-8) should feel motivated to avoid the regular-season sweep.

Jets Wire has identified three potential X-factors on offense in Week 12:

Braelon Allen was very involved in last Sunday's win over the Los Angeles Chargers. The former Wisconsin standout rushed for 75 yards via 16 carries. It was more of a timeshare in the backfield with Breece Hall. Expect offensive coordinator Frank Reich to establish the run through Hall and Allen versus the Dolphins.

With injuries at wide receiver, veteran Sterling Shepard made quite the impression last weekend. Both of Shepard's catches resulted in touchdowns. That's the definition of efficiency. Starting quarterback Geno Smith clearly trusts him, as the two spent the 2017 season together with the New York Giants.

Star wide receiver Garrett Wilson recorded just three receptions on five targets for 39 yards and zero touchdowns against the Chargers. That's pretty unacceptable given how talented he is. Coach Reich has to make a concentrated effort to ensure Wilson is consistently involved, and Smith should pepper him with targets versus the Dolphins.
