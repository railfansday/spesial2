Punches and beers fly in wild Thanksgiving Eve brawl at SoFi Stadium between Rams-Packers fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Apparently, the Los Angeles Rams and Green Bay Packers weren't the only ones throwing their weight around at SoFi Stadium on Thanksgiving Eve.

A massive fight broke out in the stands during the Rams' 26-13 victory Wednesday night, and oddly enough, a Tampa Bay Buccaneers fan appeared to be at the center of it.

In a video posted on X, a man wearing a red Tom Brady Buccaneers jersey and a black hat sticks out like a stop sign in a sea of Rams blue and Packers green. He's seen throwing a punch at someone to his right before the entire section erupts into chaos.

Men and women wearing both Rams and Packers jerseys get involved, with punches flying from seemingly every direction.

Security eventually intervenes as a woman clutching a young child attempts to escape the melee.

Fed-up spectators began showering the combatants with beer. Considering a beer at SoFi Stadium can cost $22, that's an expensive way to express your displeasure.

It's unclear what sparked the altercation or whether anyone was arrested.

There was plenty of fighting on the field, too, where the Rams bullied the Packers at the line of scrimmage, rushing a whopping 47 times for 241 yards, including a career-high 184 from Blake Corum.

The Rams squeezed the life out of Green Bay's offense, possessing the football for 23:45 of the second half and 40:40 overall compared to the Packers' 19:20.

Los Angeles improved to 7-4 with its fifth consecutive victory, climbing back into the NFC playoff picture.

The Rams gave their fans plenty to be thankful for heading into Thanksgiving. Apparently, some were more interested in serving knuckle sandwiches than turkey.
