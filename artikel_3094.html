Intrusion Inc. (INTZ -7.27%) reported a 36% year-over-year revenue increase to $2.7 million for the third quarter, supported by the integration of VigilAigent and a contract with the State of Texas. Management indicated that the company is developing an AI-driven cybersecurity platform while implementing cost-reduction measures, including executive pay reductions and headcount adjustments. The company secured a 180-day extension from NASDAQ to meet minimum bid price requirements and completed a debt-for-equity exchange to strengthen its balance sheet. Strategic focus remains on converting a growing pipeline of government and critical infrastructure opportunities into recurring revenue.
