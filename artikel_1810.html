Prediction: Everton vs Coventry City

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Looking to skyrocket their way into the European spots on Friday night, Everton will welcome Coventry to the Hill Dickinson Stadium.

With all of Europe’s elite turning their attention back to domestic duties this weekend, there are plenty of eye-catching betting promotions available.

Ahead of Friday’s showdown from Merseyside, you can find all of the key Everton vs Coventry City H2H stats here.

Starting with the hosts, enjoying a successful week, Everton now have their sights fixed on finding a shock route into the European spots. Along with Extending on their EFL Cup adventure thanks to a thumping 3-1 victory against Newcastle last week, David Moyes’ men clinched a gutsy 1-0 win at St. James’ Park on Monday night and have certainly handed themselves a real boost on either side of opening November. Leaping up the Premier League standings, the Blues will be confident of making it three consecutive victories against top-flight opposition here. Smelling a real opportunity when they return to Merseyside on Friday night, Everton should also be excited by their extended success on home soil. Heading into September’s international break with back-to-back 1-0 wins against Ipswich and Wolves, Moyes’ side have won three of their previous four showdowns at the Hill Dickinson across all competitions.

Once again missing out away at Newcastle on Monday night following a high-profile breach of club discipline, it remains to be seen if Manchester City loanee Jack Grealish will return to Everton’s squad here. However, ahead of Friday’s showdown, Moyes’ men have reported a clean bill of health.

Bagging the winner at St. James’ Park earlier in the week and notching a fourth Premier League strike of the season, Thierno Barry will spearhead Everton’s attack. Academy graduate Harrison Armstrong will keep his spot in the middle of the park.

As for the visitors, only making their iconic Premier League return this season, it is no secret that Coventry’s sole focus is on maintaining their top-flight status this time around. Last seen making a much-welcomed return to winning ways as they signed off October with a thumping 3-1 victory at home against Sunderland, Frank Lampard’s men have found a route out of the relegation zone. Winning three of their previous five Premier League appearances and marking their return from September’s extended international break with a dominant 4-1 win against Newcastle, Coventry will now be hoping to find some essential stability on their travels. Opening up the 2026/27 campaign with a routine 3-0 loss at the hands of reigning Premier League champions Arsenal on August 21st, the one-time Everton boss has seen his squad lose three of their four top-flight showdowns outside of the Midlands.

All still dealing with respective injury issues, Coventry will travel to Merseyside on Friday night without the quartet of Aurele Amenda, Josh Eccles, Luke Woolfenden and USA international striker Haji Wright. Landing that 3-1 victory against Sunderland last weekend, Lampard could be tempted to name an unchanged starting XI.

Bagging a maiden Premier League strike last week, summer recruit and former Nottingham Forest marksman Taiwo Awoniyi will lead Coventry’s attack. Now finding the net in three of his previous four appearances, Jack Rudoni could play a leading role in the final third.

Putting in a gritty performance away at St. James’ Park on Monday night as they clinched a 1-0 victory against Newcastle, Everton suddenly find themselves on the cusp of skyrocketing into the European spots. Winning three of their previous four appearances at the Hill Dickinson across all competitions, Moyes’ men will be confident of making another statement here. While Coventry might have claimed their own respective Premier League boost last weekend, Friday’s guests have failed to find their feet outside of the Midlands.
