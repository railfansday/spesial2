The First Review For Zelda: Ocarina Of Time Switch 2 Is In

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

It's a huge week in gaming with the highly anticipated launch of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for the Switch 2. The first review from the famous Japanese outlet Famitsu has now officially gone live. So, what did it think of Nintendo's remake?

If you aren't familiar with this publication's unique scoring system, four critics each deliver a score out of ten, and these scores are then totalled. For Ocarina of Time on the Switch 2, it's received 9/9/9/10, giving it an overall score of 37/40. So, it's been well received by the outlet!

In case you need a reminder, the original 1998 Nintendo 64 game was awarded a "perfect" score of 40/40. As for the 2011 3DS release, it was also given a score of 37/40. We'll be delivering our own final thoughts here on Nintendo Life later today, and be on the lookout for a review round up.
