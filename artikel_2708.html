NFL Thanksgiving week games: Ranking the 5 weekday games by what's on the line

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The NFL went out of its way to schedule five compelling matchups for Thanksgiving Eve, Thanksgiving and Black Friday.

The schedule comes out in May, and things can change dramatically over six months. Yet four of the five matchups have held up very well. That's not a bad percentage, even if the Eagles and Cowboys have let us down. Everyone will watch those two marquee franchises anyway.

There's a lot on the line in terms of the playoff race during this week. We rank the most important games, in terms of what's at stake.

We could look back on this game as the one that determined where the AFC championship game is played. The Bills lead the conference at 9-1, but the 8-2 Chiefs can pull even with a win and also clinch the head-to-head tiebreaker. The 8-2 Broncos also will have a big say in the race for the No. 1 seed, as could the Jaguars and Bengals who are each 7-3. But a big part of the playoff picture will be revealed in the primetime Thanksgiving game.

The Bills are trying to win their first Super Bowl, and a No. 1 seed would help that quest. The Bills are 0-4 in the playoffs against the Chiefs in the Josh Allen/Patrick Mahomes era but three of those games came in Kansas City. A win Thursday night would probably ensure the Bills don't have to play at Arrowhead Stadium in January.

Josh Allen has consistently felt the pain from the Chiefs in big games.

The game will go a long way in determining the NFC North race. The Bears and Lions are tied for first place at 8-2, and the winner Thursday will have a one-game lead and an edge in the tiebreaker. They meet again in Week 17, but Thursday will shape the race over December.

The Vikings aren't out of the race either at 7-3, and the Packers are still lingering at 6-4. The NFC North is really good, and whoever wins it will have earned it.

The Lions keep overcoming injuries and have won six in a row. The Bears are on a roll too; they haven't trailed during a five-game winning streak. There was a long stretch in which the Lions' traditional Thanksgiving game didn't matter much because they weren't very good. Detroit has gotten a lot better and this game is one of the most important of the season in terms of division races.

The NFL practically invented a Thanksgiving Eve holiday as an excuse to put a game on Wednesday. At least it made it a good matchup.

This game is huge in a competitive NFC wild-card race. The Rams' chances of going to the playoffs would rise from 34% to 47% with a win, according to The Athletic, while the Packers' playoff odds would fall from 40% to 25%. If the Packers win they'll have a 61% chance of making it while the Rams' odds would fall all the way to 16%.

It's stunning to think that with a loss, the overwhelming preseason Super Bowl favorite would have worse than a one-in-five shot of even making the postseason field.

The teams are on opposite ends of recent injury news. The Rams found out that receiver Puka Nacua (knee) will miss the rest of the season. Meanwhile, the Packers will get edge rusher Micah Parsons back into the lineup for the first time since he tore his ACL last December. But it might not matter for Green Bay if it loses a key game in a tough NFC wild-card field.

The Broncos and Steelers are going in different directions. The Broncos improved to 8-2 and have a very easy remaining schedule after facing many top teams in the first half of the season. The Steelers were 6-2 a couple weeks ago, but then were blown out by the Bengals and took a bad loss to an Eagles team that had lost seven in a row. At 6-4, the Steelers are suddenly more vulnerable in the wild-card race, especially with a Week 13 matchup against the 4-6 Texans coming up. A loss to Denver would knock Pittsburgh closer to the glut of teams trying to stay in the mix for a playoff spot.

The Broncos' game against the Steelers is one of their toughest remaining, and a win would help them solidify their spot atop the AFC West. The Chiefs have a much tougher remaining schedule (second-toughest to only the Rams, according to DVOA), including a game at Buffalo on Thanksgiving. It's hard to imagine the Broncos running away with the division title, but there's a possibility they could find themselves with a one-game lead after Week 12 with a much easier path the rest of the way.

Of the 10 teams playing between Wednesday and Friday, nine have legitimate playoff hopes. The Eagles' chances of making it to the postseason are less than 1%, according to NFL.com. They're a very disappointing 3-7.

Still, it would be nice to ruin their division rival's playoff chances. The Cowboys have had plenty of defensive issues but still have a 22% chance to make the playoffs because the NFC East isn't very good this season. They can't, however, afford a loss to the Eagles.

There is some playoff intrigue to the middle game on Thanksgiving. Just not nearly as much as any of the other four games during this week.
