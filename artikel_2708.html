Michigan football’s Kyle Whittingham responds to accusations he’s trying to get fired with dumb quotes to media

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

To say Kyle Whittingham's first year as the Michigan football coach hasn't gone well would be an understatement. But he still claims he's not trying to get fired.

After a second straight loss and the fifth in a trying first season in Ann Arbor, Whittingham spoke to the media, and he was asked what he thinks about people believing he is intentionally making comments that would lead to his dismissal.

"I don't know who spun it that way, but like I said, you can take anything and spin it any way you want and try to get the desired outcome you're looking for. That was not the intention," Whittingham said [h/t 247Sports' Alejandro Zuniga].

Specifically, Whittingham, who also called the accusation "bizarre," recently made a comment in which he seemed to forget about The Game, Michigan's highly anticipated rivalry vs. Ohio State, after a loss to Michigan State, another major Wolverines rival.

Additionally, Whittingham's suggestion that Oregon "outbid" everyone for its roster in the lead-up to the Ducks' 31-7 win vs. Michigan on Saturday was not well-received by Oregon head coach Dan Lanning or the Wolverine fanbase.

Ultimately, though, the comments, as well as Whittingham's seemingly unfazed demeanor on the sideline and at the podium, stem from fan outrage as Michigan endures a rough year.

After firing Sherrone Moore last December, Michigan hired Whittingham, who had spent his entire career out west, including the last 30-plus years at Utah. The fit has proven to be less than ideal, with the longtime Utes coach immediately struggling to lead the Wolverines.

Following a 3-0 start, including a controversial season-opening 13-12 win vs. Western Michigan, Michigan lost one-score games to Iowa and rival Minnesota, recovered for a one-score loss to Minnesota, and then lost in overtime to Indiana.

In the last three weeks, Michigan beat Rutgers and then fell to Michigan State and Oregon, bringing the Wolverines to .500 on the season entering the final two weeks, which could be the toughest part of their schedule.

Michigan, which needs one win to become bowl-eligible, hosts undefeated No. 4 UCLA on Saturday before traveling to Columbus next week for The Game vs. the vulnerable but ranked Buckeyes.
