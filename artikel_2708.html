Caleb Williams (the least intercepted QB ever) has moved on from that 4 pick anomaly

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Chicago Bears are 7-2 and coming off a much-needed bye week, which allowed them to regroup and refresh after a first half of the season in which they led the league in time of possession.

They'll also get back one of their key offensive players, running back Kyle Monangai, who hasn't played since Week 4. Also, wideout Luther Burden III, who exited their last game with a concussion, has cleared protocol during the bye and will be good to go this Sunday.

Chicago's bye really came at a perfect time for the team, although it also came after Caleb Williams threw four interceptions against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers. Caleb's competitiveness and drive are well chronicled, so he was asked earlier this week if that performance has lingered with him at all during the bye.

After breaking down the four picks as one "bad decision," two of them "funky," and one where the ball "popped up in the air," he explained what stuck with him during the week.

"The biggest thing is, the frustration comes from the feeling of, for me, personally, I always don't want to let my teammates down," Williams said from Halas Hall. "That's always big for me. And in that situation, in those situations, I feel like I did. And so, that's the frustrating part that lingers."

You can tell Williams is itching to get back out there and put that game behind him, but did you realize that even with those 4 interceptions, he's still the least-intercepted quarterback in league history?

Okay, technically Williams doesn't have enough attempts to qualify for the all-time leaderboard, but his 1.36 interception percentage is lower than Aaron Rodgers' 1.42%.

Williams needs 175 more passing attempts to reach 1,500, and if he stays under Rodgers' interception percentage mark, he'll be the new and official number-one least-intercepted QB, per Pro Football Reference. Then again, Rodgers is still active, so he'll have a say in the number, but regardless, Caleb is in rare air with how safe he is with the football.

"I'll move on from it," he said about his 4 pick game anomaly. "I've moved on from it," he clarified. "You know, it's more of a how can I get better? What can I be better at? Just finding ways to be more consistent. And in those other situations where, you know, I'm not making a dumb decision like the first one. I'm trying to either get it out of bounds or, you know, anything like that — just juice it out of bounds."

The Bears host the New Orleans Saints on Sunday, and FanDuel Sportsbook has Chicago at -11.5. The Bears' moneyline is -720, and the game's over/under is 48.5.
