Report: Mike Pettine takes over as Cowboys' defensive play-caller

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

This one was inevitable, perhaps from the moment Mike Pettine arrived last month as an assistant.

Via ESPN, Pettine will be taking over as the play-caller for the Dallas Cowboys defense.

Pettine supplants defensive coordinator Christian Parker, a Pettine protege who is in his first year as the coach in charge of an NFL defense. Parker will remain the team’s defensive coordinator.

It was obvious that something was coming. Sunday’s 46-40 loss to the 49ers included an embarrassing performance from the defense, highlighted by multiple players running the wrong way when receiver Deebo Samuel rumbled 70 yards for a rushing touchdown.

Some would say Pettine is part of the problem, given that he has been there for multiple weeks. Short of firing everyone and hiring someone off the street, however, there’s not much that can be done right now.

The first test comes on Sunday, when the Cowboys host the one-win Titans. Four days later, Dallas faces the two-win Eagles. If the Cowboys don’t win both, they’ll likely be de facto done for 2026.
