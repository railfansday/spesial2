Bears vs. Saints Recap: Good teams don’t always play good games

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Their 21 to 13 win over the New Orleans Saints was not a good game.

Good teams don't always play good games, and good teams sometimes get beat when they aren't at their best. But yesterday, the Bears (for whatever reason) went into their game as a big 11.5-point favorite and then slogged through the afternoon. Whether or not that was by design, we'll never know, but it's not the type of effort or planning that will allow them to win against other good teams.

Ben Johnson and Caleb Williams set the bar high, and now the addition of Jeffery Simmons has raised it even higher, so anything perceived as less than championship-caliber play will raise concerns.

I should clarify that Chicago's defense, for as bare-bones as the game plan looked, did play championship-caliber football yesterday. We didn't see much blitzing from Dennis Allen, but he ensured one-on-ones for his defensive line by threatening a blitz on several occasions, and the front four harassed the Saints all afternoon.

Chicago's much-maligned running game managed 146 yards, their most since October 4 and their third-most in a game this season. So while the offense is still searching for consistency, it's still performing like a top-10 unit.

The Bears have a top-10 offense and defense, but with the bar set so high, it's okay for us to want more.

I guess these are the types of problems fans who root for good teams have.

I'll take that over whatever problems we had during the 'Flus era.
