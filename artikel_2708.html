Where will Tennessee football rank in CFP poll? Live updates of selection show

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Tennessee football will move up in the second College Football Playoff rankings of the 2026 season, when it's released on Nov. 10, but the question is how far.

The CFP selection committee will release its top 25 teams at 9 p.m. ET on ESPN.

The Vols were ranked No. 13 in the first CFP rankings, and then they beat No. 6 Kentucky 34-6 win on Nov. 7. Four teams ranked ahead of Tennessee lost in Week 10, including Kentucky, so the Vols should climb into the Top 10.

Tennessee (7-2, 4-2 SEC) plays at Texas A&M (4-5, 1-5) on Nov. 14 (3 p.m. ET, ESPN). The rankings will be updated each Tuesday with the final CFP field announced on Dec. 6.

The 12-team CFP field will include automatic bids for the Power 4 conference champions (ACC, Big Ten, Big 12, SEC); an automatic bid for the highest ranked Group of Six team (American, Conference USA, MAC, Mountain West, Pac-12, Sun Belt), regardless of whether it's a conference champion; and at-large bids to the seven highest ranked remaining teams.

Seeds 5 through 12 will have first-round matchups. Seeds 5 through 8 will host a first-round game on campus sites.

College Football Playoff selection committee: Hunter Yurachek (chair, Arkansas athletics director); Troy Dannen (Nebraska athletics director), Mark Dantonio (former Michigan State, Cincinnati coach); Doug Gillin (Appalachian State athletics director); Mark Harlan (Utah athletics director); Ivan Maisel (former sportswriter); Gus Malzahn (former Auburn, Arkansas State, UCF coach); Chris Massaro (Middle Tennessee athletics director); Randall McDaniel (former Arizona State All-American player); Mike Riley (former Oregon State, Nebraska coach); Jeff Tedford (former California, Fresno State coach); Wesley Walls (former Ole Miss All-American player); Carla Williams (Virginia athletics director).
