Geno Smith will be limited in Wednesday's practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Jets quarterback Geno Smith avoided a major ankle injury in last Sunday’s loss to the Bills, but his Week 11 status remains uncertain.

Head coach Aaron Glenn said at his press conference that Smith will be a limited participant in Wednesday’s practice as the team works to get ready to face the Chargers. Glenn said that the team will see how the week plays out before making any determination about Smith’s availability for the game.

Fourth-round pick Cade Klubnik made his NFL debut against the Bills and will make his first pro start if Smith is unable to play.

Wide receivers Omar Cooper (ankle) and Isaiah Williams (ribs) will not practice on Wednesday due to injuries suffered in Week 10.
