Sea Of Stars Is Getting A 'Sunset Edition' Switch 2 Physical And A Gorgeous Final Merch Drop

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Sabotage Studio's Sea of Stars is no stranger to a physical release or merch drop, and today, iam8bit has announced that it has one final collection in store.

A brand new Switch 2 physical, the Sea of Stars: Sunset Edition ($49.99), is the star of the show, launching on 2nd April 2027. This one packs the full game and all of its expansions on the cartridge (what a concept!), and comes with some sweet sticker sheets and a download code for the game's soundtrack.

There's a beefier option in the Exclusive Edition, which also boasts an exclusive sleeve and an in-box manual, for those after a little throwback.

The beefiest of the bunch, mind you, is still to come. The Collector's Edition contains all of the goodies outlined above, and throws in a bunch of extra merch too. We're talking a double-sided poster, a tote bag, a reversible bandana, a Rainbow Conch keychain, and seven pins, all housed in a Rainbow Conch Chest. Come on, that's sweet.

The Collector's Edition comes in at a pricey $129.99 (or you can just get the merch for $79.99). All of the above are now available to pre-order from the iam8bit website until 11th January, before shipping in April.

As if all of that wasn't enough, iam8bit has also announced that it has two vinyl LPs in the pipeline, one all about the main game soundtrack, and one featuring the tunes from the Throes of the Watchmaker DLC. You'll also find pre-order information about these on the iam8bit site.
