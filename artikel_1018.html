WBC open door for Canelo to fight Hutchinson for light-heavy title

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The WBC did not put in place a mandatory challenger for new light-heavyweight world champion Willy Hutchinson; that leaves the path open for Mexican superstar Canelo Alvarez to return to 175lbs and take on Hutchinson; Canelo returned from Terence Crawford defeat to reclaim WBC 168lbs title

The door is open for Canelo Alvarez to move back to light-heavyweight and challenge new world champion Willy Hutchinson.

Scotland's Hutchinson won his rematch with Joshua Buatsi on November 7 to secure the vacant WBC light-heavyweight title.

Mexican superstar Alvarez reclaimed the WBC's super-middleweight belt when he outscored Christian Mbilli at the end of last month.

Although 168lbs is his natural weight, Canelo has previous boxed at light-heavyweight, beating Sergey Kovalev but losing to Dmitry Bivol.

The WBC would approve Hutchinson making a first title defence against Canelo.

"Yes, absolutely," WBC president Mauricio Sulaiman told Sky Sports.

"Canelo's win is very fresh after a year of huge difficulties, dealing with a loss against [Terence] Crawford, dealing with an injury and a surgery, then having to come back.

"The way he conquered the title and he celebrated, he's taken some time off."

WBC haven't ordered a mandatory challenger yet for the Briton, leaving that path to Hutchinson open for Alvarez.

"We will see what happens in the following days. At the convention we left that division open without a ruling. We're going to wait and not anticipate and we will see what happens in the following weeks and Canelo Alvarez certainly is a possibility [for Hutchinson]," Sulaiman said.

"If he decides to challenge at light-heavyweight, it would certainly be approved, champion against champion, if he decides to stay at super-middleweight there are certain alternatives."

The WBC president added: "I'm very proud of Hutchinson. He proved us right, there were many complaints about him fighting for the title but he proved us right and he won and he's deservedly the WBC champion."
