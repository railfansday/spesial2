Jack McBrayer To Host NBC’s ‘Countdown To Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade’ Featuring Interviews With Leslie Odom Jr & McKenna Grace

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

EXCLUSIVE: NBC is gearing up for the 100th edition of its annual Thanksgiving celebration and will be bringing back the hourlong Countdown to Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade the night before.

This year, the special will be hosted by Emmy-nominated actor and comedian Jack McBrayer and feature interviews with parade performers Mckenna Grace and Leslie Odom Jr., Deadline can reveal exclusively.

The episode will air November 25 at 8 p.m. ET and also simulcast on Peacock.

On the eve of the 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade, the countdown special will give audiences an all-access look at the Macy’s Parade Studio to discover exactly what it takes to create the famous floats and balloons prior to their journey through the streets of New York, including the iconic characters making their grand return to the parade after decades away.

Creator Taylen Biggs will serve as a special 34th Street correspondent at Herald Square, providing behind-the-scenes coverage of Parade rehearsals on the eve of the 100th march. Watch on Deadline

The special will also feature superfans whose lives have been changed by the parade, as well as a preview of the The Divine Nine Unity Step Team’s parade performance.

Countdown to Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade is produced by Done + Dusted. Katy Mullan and Jim Cantiello serve as executive producers.
