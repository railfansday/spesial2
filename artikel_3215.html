‘Bunker’ Trailer: Penélope Cruz & Javier Bardem Clash In Florian Zeller’s Venice Breakout; UK Release Date Set

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

“Do you keep up with the news?” the billionaire asks the UK’s most prestigious architect via video call. “So you’re talking about some kind of revolution?” Miguel Moreno replies.

“Uh, full-scale collapse of the system we live,” he’s told.

That’s the setup for Bunker, Florian Zeller‘s thriller starring Javier Bardem and Penélope Cruz that wowed the Venice Film Festival. Check out the trailer above.

Here’s the logline: When an architect (Bardem) accepts a morally ambiguous project — building a survivalist bunker for a tech billionaire (Paul Dano) — his wife (Cruz) begins to question their marriage after 17 years together. The dramatic thriller examines the emotional and moral challenges faced by a couple as the anxieties of an increasingly uncertain world begin to reshape their marriage, exploring the fears, doubts and ethical dilemmas that define our time.

Stephen Graham and Patrick Schwarzenegger also star in the film, which got a boisterous 16-minute ovation after its Venice premiere.

During the Bunker panel at Deadline’s recent Contenders Film Los Angeles event, Cruz said she was concerned that the fragile relationship between the husband-and-wife leads might bleed over into her rock-solid union with real-life husband Bardem. ““We’ve worked together every five or six years – we don’t plan it like that, but I think in a way we both want to protect and we feel like if we would be working together too often, it would be tricky and risky,” she said “But when Florian came to us, even before we read the script, we looked at each other and we knew none of us would want to say no … because we were huge fans and now even more after working with him.”

StudioCanal on Tuesday also set a February 19 UK and Ireland release date for the film, which is generating awards buzz. See the UK/Ireland poster for Bunker below.
