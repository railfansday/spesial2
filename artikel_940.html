Why Dancing With the Stars’ Adele Zaikman is Missing Disney Night

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Dancing With the Stars pro Adele Zaikman, who made her debut on the series in season 35, is missing Disney Night on Nov. 2 after coming down with an illness.

Even a wish upon a star won’t get Adele Zaikman into the ballroom.

While the Dancing With the Stars pro, who was partnered with Connor Leavitt before their week one exit, has been a key member of the eliminated cohort that performs during show breaks, she's recently been sidelined by an illness.

“I’m sadly feeling unwell and won’t be performing in the opening number on Dancing With the Stars tomorrow,” the 25-year-old wrote in a since-expired Instagram Story Nov. 1. “I’m so sad to miss a show day, but I’ll be taking this time to take care of myself. Sending so much love to everyone on that ballroom floor. I’ll be cheering you on from home.”

Because she’s under the weather, Zaikman—who earned a season 35 spot by placing first on Dancing With the Stars: The Next Pro—will miss the Nov. 2's Disney-themed night.

And even though the road is looking rough ahead, Zaikman’s got many friends willing to stick together and help her see it through.

“I think that I'm the luckiest human on earth,” she said in a video shared to her Story later that day, adding that her friend Brenda “just stopped by just to give me this superfood honey and things that help me with my cough and some special teas.”

Not only that, but the good friend also dropped off some blueberry muffins. “How good do they look?” Zaikman stressed. “I mean, I am so lucky that I have people like that in my life.”

But while Zaikman will hopefully be back in the ballroom faster than you can say, “Supercalifragilisticexpialidocious,” she will indeed miss week eight of Dancing With the Stars.

Instead, the other eliminated pros—including Hailey Bills, Alan Bersten, Britt Stewart, Brandon Armstrong, Witney Carson, Sharna Burgess and Emma Slater—will be there to cut a rug when the episode airs live on Monday, as opposed to its usual Tuesday slot. (The episode was moved due to the midterm elections on Nov. 3.)

As for Zaikman, a position as one of the pros isn’t the only thing her time on DWTS: The Next Pro earned her: She also walked away with a new boyfriend.

Indeed, one month after the series aired its finale on Aug. 31, it was revealed Zaikman and her fellow contestant Allen Genkin’s onscreen bond had taken a turn for the romantic offscreen.

For Zaikman, her time alongside Genkin on the series cemented him as someone she wanted to be with.

“When I got to know Allen on The Next Pro, I immediately felt that he was one of the kindest souls I’d ever met,” she told E! News in October. “Having such a strong bond and friendship first makes our relationship now feel so right.”

She added, “There’s so much trust because we built it before we were romantically involved. It’s that feeling of knowing the person, knowing his heart.”
