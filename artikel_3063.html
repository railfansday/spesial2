Root (ROOT) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Root traded direct-channel growth for returns as its new pricing model came online.

Root, Inc. (ROOT -17.18%) reported profitable third quarter results characterized by geographic expansion and the deployment of updated pricing technology. Management emphasized a disciplined capital allocation strategy, intentionally trading off incremental policy growth in direct channels when expected returns fell below internal thresholds. The company reported that 52% of new writings now originate from the partnership and independent agent channels, up from 44% a year ago. Strategic initiatives included the renewal of the Carvana partnership through 2028 and the launch of a new pilot program with Hyundai. The company reported it is focusing on automating the insurance lifecycle end-to-end through proprietary systems as it targets a national footprint by the end of 2027.
