Bukayo Saka will be itching to return to action for Arsenal as he watches Noni Madueke thrive in his position.

The 25-year-old has been missing since October's trip to Bayern Munich, where he suffered an injury to his quad.

In his 11 appearances before the injury, the England international had scored four goals and provided an assist, but was still some way off the levels he can reach.

Madueke replaced him in the starting line-up and has been superb on the right of the attack, putting in another superb display as Arsenal beat Hull City 1-0 on Saturday.

The 24-year-old, who joined the Gunners in a £52million deal in the summer of 2025, has not always been convincing, but finally looks to be showing why Mikel Arteta targeted him.

Should his form continue, Saka may even face a serious battle for his position when he does return from injury.

Noni Madueke has been in fine form for Arsenal and leads the way in the Premier League for chances created per match 👏📊 pic.twitter.com/p3hxg9FWHR

Madueke's strong form has come at exactly the right time for Arteta, who can use it to light a fire under Saka.

The winger has struggled with injuries over the last two years, but he remains a hugely capable player and has not been able to show it.

Yet with him now having been forced to sit at home and watch as Madueke takes the Arsenal acclaim from his position, there should now be new motivation for Saka to get back to his best.

His position for England is in threat, too, if he does not. Thomas Tuchel opted against selecting Madueke for November's camp, but he will undoubtedly be involved should his strong form continue.

Saka had been set to miss around eight weeks of action with the quad injury, but will hope to return ahead of schedule.

Arteta will be well aware of the effect Madueke's form may have on Saka because he is encouraging the same competition throughout the squad.

William Saliba's return will see him compete for Ezri Konsa's place, Piero Hincapie is targeting Riccardo Calafiori's spot and there are several other examples in the Arsenal squad.

"We have really good options because they're in the right mindset," Arteta said last month. "I'm extremely proud of the chemistry and the desire we have created among the players who both start and finish the game.

"We've seen a great example with Ebz [Eberechi Eze], who could have been upset and certainly was when he was substituted after 45 minutes at Forest, and then came in and made the impact he did on Wednesday.

"That's exactly what you need - you have a bench that can be strong as strong as you want, but they have to give you what you need."
