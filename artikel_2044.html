Middlesbrough warning shot sent to 'entitled' Southampton ahead of first post-Spygate clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Over six months on from their infamous play-off clash, Middlesbrough and Southampton are preparing to meet again on Friday night.

Middlesbrough faced Southampton in the play-off semi-finals last season, but events on the pitch were overshadowed after a Saints intern was spotted observing their opponents' training session ahead of the first leg at the Riverside Stadium, and the saga had significant consequences for the south coast outfit.

After a goalless draw in the first leg, Southampton booked their place in the play-off final with a 2-1 victory over Middlesbrough in the second leg at St Mary's, but that was not the end of the story.

Having been caught watching Boro's training session, the Saints also admitted to spying on Oxford United and Ipswich Town during the regular season, which resulted in them being kicked out of the Wembley showpiece and docked four points for the current campaign.

Unfortunately for Middlesbrough, they were unable to take advantage of their reprieve as they were beaten 1-0 by Hull City in the final, and, with both clubs remaining in the Championship, they are battling each other for promotion to the Premier League once again.

Ahead of the game at St Mary's on Friday night, Boro are top of the Championship table after an excellent start to the season, while Southampton currently sit sixth, with seven points separating the pair.

Saints head coach Tonda Eckert avoided an FA ban for his role in the Spygate affair, but it seems any bad blood between him and Hellberg has been firmly put to bed, with the German revealing on Thursday that he will invite his opposite number for a drink after the game.

When asked if he still feels animosity towards Southampton, FLW's Middlesbrough fan pundit Liam Day insisted that he would love to see Hellberg's men have the last laugh at St Mary's on Friday night, but he admitted that he would be happy with a point.

"I think any fan that says there's nothing behind this game is completely lying," Liam told FLW.

"Southampton fans want to win it more than most games.

"The way their fans acted throughout the whole Spygate thing was entitled and just a bit embarrassing really.

"They cheated, they know they cheated, but they refused to admit it, so it was really embarrassing.

"I really want to beat them, I'm not going to shy away from that.

"Do I think we will? That's a different question.

"I think it's such a tough place to go, they're a very decent team, especially at home.

"If you asked me right now, I'm absolutely taking a draw.

"We've been playing well this season, so you never know, but, right now, I'm taking a draw.

"There's definitely animosity, and I think any fan who says there isn't is lying."

Having been expelled from the play-off final and given a four-point deduction, some felt that Southampton's punishment for Spygate was disproportionate, while others believed that the sanction did not go far enough after Eckert escaped a ban from the FA.

After speaking on the phone and meeting at an EFL event in the summer, it appears that Eckert and Hellberg are keen to move on from the controversy, with the duo expressing a desire to focus on football during their pre-match press conferences on Thursday.
