Top 20 most valuable EFL League Two goalkeepers right now

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The League Two season is now well underway, and as expected, English football's fourth tier hasn't disappointed when it comes to the drama which has already been provided.

It will come as no surprise to learn that the top-half of the table is extremely congested as the campaign nears its halfway point, with several sides now hopeful that they can be the ones who mount a successful promotion bid to League One, with three automatic spots up for grabs as well as the eventual play-off victors at Wembley Stadium.

In order to achieve such strong short-term goals, a full squad effort will be required right until matchday 46, and many individuals have already been in the limelight for their exploits this term.

It comes as no surprise that the majority of those come in the form of attacking stars, but having a resolute goalkeeper certainly goes a long way for any head coach.

With that in mind, according to Transfermarkt's current valuations, which 20 League Two shot-stoppers are the most valuable? FLW takes a look.

This list is NOT based on FLW's opinion, it's based on Transfermarkt's valuations. All figures converted from Euros to GBP.

Will Brook is now in the thick of his second campaign at Shrewsbury Town, having joined from Leamington FC in August 2025.

The Shropshire outfit have endured mixed fortunes since the 23-year-old's arrival, but after signing a fresh two-and-a-half year contract in November 2025, his current market value is said to be £104,711.

Next up is Newport County's mainstay between the sticks, Jordan Wright, who moved to the South Wales outfit in the summer of 2025 from divisional rivals, Grimsby Town.

The Nottingham Forest academy graduate is certainly well experienced in both League One and League Two, having made over 100 appearances across the two divisions.

Since joining the Exiles, Wright has already accumulated a double-figure tally of clean sheets, contributing to a current £125,621 valuation.

Another shot-stopper valued at £125,621 right now is 27-year-old Taye Ashby-Hammond, who signed for Barnet on a free transfer in June.

The former England youth international previously won promotion out of the division with Stevenage, and given the Bees' bright start under Dean Brennan, similar exploits could be achieved in May at The Hive.

Zach Jeacock is also worth £125,621 at this stage of the season after joining Northampton Town on loan from current Championship side, Lincoln City.

The former England youth international and Birmingham City academy graduate has made a solid start to life at Sixfields, conceding the joint-second fewest goals per 90 minutes on average.

Harvey Cartwright returned to Grimsby Town on loan this summer from Premier League newcomers, Hull City, having spent time at Blundell Park during the 2023/24 campaign.

After keeping five clean sheets in 25 League Two matches back then, the 24-year-old is currently a deputy in David Artell's squad, but has still retained a market valuation of £125,621.

Ahead of Cartwright is his Mariners teammate and the vastly experienced Christy Pym, with the 31-year-old's current market valuation sitting in the region of £146,540.

The man, who is closing in on 300 appearances at League Two level, signed a two-year deal in North-East Lincolnshire last summer, and has retained his starting berth despite just six clean sheets in all competitions last term, as well as a remarkable display in the historic EFL Cup triumph over Manchester United.

Alfie McNally is also worth £146,540 in the current market, with the 21-year-old signing for York City on loan from Premier League side, Fulham, this summer.

The Londoner has certainly performed to an admirable standard in what is his first taste of senior football, whilst he remains under contract at Craven Cottage until June 2028.

Cheltenham Town's Wyll Stanway is one of three goalkeepers on this list with a valuation of £167,490.

The brother of England Lioness, Georgia, signed for the Robins this summer from Barrow after the Bluebirds' relegation to the National League, and has certainly been busy, averaging the third-highest number of saves per 90.

Bristol Rovers' campaign has been one of frustration, with Steve Evans' position under increasing pressure as a result.

However, the Gas could be in an even worse position were it not for the services of £167,490-valued goalkeeper, Brad Young, who signed from Leicester City in June 2025.

The 24-year-old currently ranks as one of the best in the division when it comes to clean sheets.

Also valued at £167,490 is Crewe Alexandra goalkeeper, Tom Booth.

The 24-year-old has progressed through the Railwaymen's highly-rated academy system and into the first-team fold, and since his debut in April 2023, has amassed over 60 appearances.
