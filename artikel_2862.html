Kyren Williams hopes GTA VI release distracts other NFL teams

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Kyren Williams made it clear that he does not want the massive release of Grand Theft Auto VI to be a distraction to the Los Angeles Rams as they prepare for perhaps their biggest game of the year against the Green Bay Packers in Week 12, and go from there.

Williams was asked on ESPN how the trendy release of GTA VI could or would impact the Rams? Williams responded during the interview with laughter and glee at the thought of the game coming out, and he confessed to Peter Schrager that he hopes he and his teammates stay focused on winning, while taking some healthy time to away from football to check out the new video game.

On the flip side, Williams hopes other NFL teams play the new game all day, all night and twice on Sunday.

Said Williams on the release of GTA VI during L.A.'s bye week:

"I hope it impacts everybody, but the Rams, you know? And so, I hope the Rams, I hope my teammates and I are able to game a little bit, but still, you know, get to bed and be on time for practice and you know be alert and whatnot. But I am super excited. I'm glad that you brought that up cause I gotta go pre-load it, I gotta go pre-download it, you know, when we get done here. But yah I'm super excited for GTA and we're on a bye week, so it's like, bro this is the best week ever."

NFL players across the league and people all over the world have been waiting for the pop culture phenomena that is the new Grand Theft Auto, and it's releasing in the thick of the NFL season.

Cleveland Browns' quarterback Deshaun Watson was asked about the playoffs and the Browns chances. Watson gave the classic answer about taking it "one game at a time" before basically stating that at this point in the season, players are thinking more about the new Grand Theft Auto than playoffs. And to be fair, playoffs are still a while off while GTA VI is out now. Keep your feet where they are.

Of course, players like Williams and Watson do have a few million reasons to put down the controller and stick with the football. While many gamers all over will be celebrating, gamers like Williams and others are in the busy part of their work season. And gamers like Williams hope they can stay focused on football and a potential Super Bowl run now, and focus on Grand Theft Auto later.

Ask any gamer, focusing on the task at hand while worrying about a new game later: Easier said than done. We'll see how Williams and the Rams do.
