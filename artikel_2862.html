FILM ROOM PODCAST: Jayden Anglin joins Season 3 Episode 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Welcome to Season 3 Episode 10 of the Film Room Podcast! The Times-Tribune Athlete of the Week Abington Heights football player Jayden Anglin joins hosts Joe Baress and Joby Fawcett to discuss his school-record performance in the District 2 Class 5A title game, what it means to capture another championship, what it took for the team to bounce back from a 2-4 start and rattle off five straight wins, what it's like balancing playing offense and defense, being compared to Saquon Barkley and more.

We also discuss the District 2 football, volleyball and soccer playoffs and the PIAA championships in tennis and cross country.
