New Ravens QBs taking team pictures at practice sums up hopeless QB situation

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The entire Baltimore Ravens quarterback room is currently dealing with injuries, creating a major concern for the team ahead of its upcoming matchup against the Jacksonville Jaguars. The Ravens have reportedly reached a point where all three of their quarterbacks are nursing injuries. The situation has become so concerning that Baltimore has brought in two additional quarterbacks, Kedon Slovis and Austin Reed, to provide some much-needed depth.

Both newcomers arrived in Baltimore shortly before practice and had to have their photographs taken before joining the team's activities. Their last-minute arrival highlights just how urgently the Ravens need reinforcements at quarterback.

Lamar Jackson suffered a right thumb injury during Sunday's 36-10 defeat against the Buffalo Bills. Head coach Jesse Minter confirmed that the former MVP would be placed on injured reserve, with surgery also being considered.

Meanwhile, backup quarterback Tyler Huntley exited the same game with a toe injury, and the possibility of placing him on injured reserve is also being considered.

Baltimore rookie Joe Fagnano made his NFL regular-season debut against the Bills after Jackson and Huntley went down. However, he is now dealing with an ankle injury of his own. The undrafted rookie took a low hit from Bills defensive tackle Ed Oliver but managed to remain in the game and finish the game.

Baltimore's quarterback problems could not have arrived at a worse time, particularly considering the team's disappointing start to the season. The Ravens are coming off a heavy defeat against Buffalo and must now prepare for another challenging matchup against the Jaguars without their franchise quarterback.

With their quarterback room severely depleted, the Ravens have wasted little time bringing in Reed and Slovis. However, the two newcomers have very little time to familiarize themselves with Baltimore's offensive system before facing the Jaguars.

Minter announced Tuesday that Fagnano will be under center for the upcoming game. Amid the rookie's own injury concerns, Baltimore's immediate priority is to get its available quarterbacks prepared for Thursday night.
