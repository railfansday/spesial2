NFLPA names Steelers LS Christian Kuntz their Community MVP for Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The NFL Players Association announced on Friday that they have named Steelers long snapper Christian Kuntz their Community MVP for Week 9 of the 2026 season.

Kuntz recently donated $10,000 to Pittsburgh Morrow school to help open a resource room that will benefit more than 200 students every month. Kuntz also helped stock that room with clothing and food while his foundation hosted its inaugural Kuntz 4 Ki6s Halloween Bash for 80 children from nine local nonprofit organizations.

“I am incredibly honored to be named this week’s NFLPA Community MVP,” Kuntz said. “Pittsburgh is home to me, and being able to make a positive impact in the community that I grew up in is extra special. Football has given us this platform, and I believe it’s important to use that to make a difference beyond the field.”

The NFLPA will donate $10,000 to Kuntz’s foundation and he will join the other weekly MVPs in being eligible for the Alan Page Award at the end of the season.
