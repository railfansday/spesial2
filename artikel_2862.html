Can Arsenal Stun Barcelona Femení Again in Camp Nou UWCL Showdown?

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

After their narrow 2-1 win over Liverpool at the weekend, Arsenal Women's next fixture sees them take on Barcelona Femení in what is a matchup of previous Champions League winners against the current Champions League holders in the next league phase game of the competition.

The two teams have not faced each other since the Gunners ran out 1-0 winners in the final of the competition in 2025, and there is no doubt Barcelona will be out to get revenge and pile more pressure on Renée Slegers in what seems to be a miserable season so far for the ladies, in the Women's Super League at least.

Arsenal Women's Champions League clash against Barcelona, at Camp Nou, kicks off at 5.45pm UK time. The match will be shown live on Disney + in the UK.

Arsenal's run in the Champions League so far has seen them win two and draw one, whereas Barcelona have won three out of three, scoring 19 goals and conceding just two in the process.

Arsenal, on the other hand, have scored six and conceded two. So it is clear to see who is the more free-scoring side, although the similarity in opponents would have suggested similar results, but this wasn't the case.

Arsenal's three games have seen them come up against Benfica, winning 3-0, Danish side Køge, winning 1-0, and Paris FC, whom they drew 2-2 with.

Barcelona, on the other hand, have come up against Køge, winning 8-0, Roma, winning 6-0, and Paris FC, winning 5-2.

But given the lack of goals Arsenal are scoring, one thing is for sure: the defence has done its job since the initial 2-2 draw with Paris FC, and against Barcelona it needs to be as tight as ever to stop their attackers.

And maybe, just maybe, we can hope that Arsenal get one counter-attack that could wrap up a win for the women.

And if the previous game between the sides is anything to go by, then maybe it won't be as free-scoring as Barcelona hope, and maybe Arsenal can get another win that will give them a much-needed boost.

After their narrow win over Liverpool at the weekend, here's hoping they can build on that result.
