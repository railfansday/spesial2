Fluffernutter Cookies

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Preheat the oven to 350°F. In a large bowl, cream the shortening, peanut butter and sugars until they’re light and fluffy, five to seven minutes. Add the eggs, one at a time, beating well after each addition. Beat in the vanilla.

In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda and salt. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, mixing until they’re combined.

Scoop the dough by tablespoonfuls and arrange the portions on a parchment-lined baking sheet or work surface. Use your palms or the bottom of a drinking glass to flatten each scoop into a disk.

Pipe or spoon about 1 teaspoon of marshmallow fluff onto the center of half the disks, leaving a little space around the edges. Top each with another dough disk, pinching the edges together to seal in the filling.

Editor’s Tip: A piping bag or zip-top bag with the corner snipped off makes it easy to add neat, even dollops of marshmallow fluff. If using a spoon, lightly coat it with cooking spray to help the sticky marshmallow release.
