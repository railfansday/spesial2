‘Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma’ Comes to VHS in Special Pan-and-Scan Edition from Mutant

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Jane Schoenbrun’s “Teenage Sex and Camp Miasma” is now available to purchase in the format that inspired it. IndieWire can exclusively announce that MUBI and Mutant have partnered on a new VHS release of the slasher homage, available in both a widescreen and a pan-and-scan format. Both editions, along with a new limited edition poster, are live on Mutant’s website and will soon be available in the MUBI shop.

Schoenbrun’s film, which premiered at Cannes before hitting theaters in August, makes no attempt to hide its affection for the VHS medium. The film follows a young director tasked with reviving a dated slasher franchise that has produced a seemingly endless string of mediocre sequels. Images of past “Camp Miasma” films lining video store shelves establish the film’s world and illustrate Schoebrun’s ongoing love for seemingly disposable pieces of pop culture that take on outsized meaning for the people who love them. The director has previously described “Camp Miasma” as the kind of film they would have wanted to find on a video store shelf. That dream might still be impossible, but placing it on your own VHS shelf at home is the next best thing. “I make movies I wish existed when I was a kid,” Schoenbrun said in a 2025 statement when the project was first announced, “and ‘Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma’ is my best attempt at the ‘sleepover classic,’ an insane yet cozy midnight odyssey that beckons to unsuspecting viewers from the horror section at the local video store. I couldn’t be more excited to be heading to sleepaway camp this summer with the mad comic genius Hannah Einbinder, the legendary Gillian Anderson, and the daring folks at MUBI and Plan B, who, by greenlighting this movie, have summoned a plague-like flood of blood, guts, and various other fluids to rain down on us all.”

“Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma” is the latest indie hit to capitalize on audiences’ love for analog formats with a VHS release. Films like “The People’s Joker,” “Hundreds of Beavers,” and “This Is How the World Ends” have all enjoyed recent success with the format, suggesting that cinephiles are increasingly open to embracing the old-school technology.

Schoenbrun recently released their debut novel “Public Access Afterworld” and will be making their TV debut with the upcoming Netflix series “Black Hole.”
