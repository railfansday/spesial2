Ridley Scott's 8-Episode Sci-Fi Thriller Is An S-Tier Cyberpunk Show

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

It took a while for Ridley Scott’s most iconic sci-fi franchise to reach the small screen, but Hulu’s Alien streaming series exceeded expectations when the show did arrive in 2025. The Alien franchise has lived many lives since director Ridley Scott’s original sci-fi horror blockbuster was released in 1979. Director James Cameron’s 1986 sequel Aliens upped the action quotient of the franchise, downplaying the horror elements in the process.

While Aliens was the most action-packed of the Alien movies, 1992’s divisive Alien 3 took the exact opposite approach. Grim, downbeat, and dour, future Se7en director David Fincher’s sequel was certainly dark and disturbing, but lacked the fun and thrills of Alien and Aliens. 1997’s Alien: Resurrection was something of a course correction, but Scott’s prequels Prometheus and Alien: Covenant divided the franchise’s large fan base further.

Even without mentioning the dubious canon of the Alien Vs Predator movies, the Alien franchise is one with vertiginous highs and embarrassingly misguided lows. As such, it is no surprise that the first Alien streaming series came laden with decades of hype and high expectations. What was surprising is the fact that Fargo show runner Noah Hawley’s Hulu series Alien: Earth managed to not only live up to, but even exceed these expectations with its subversive, spooky sci-fi storytelling.

Taking place after the events of Prometheus and Alien: Covenant, Alien: Earth followed 2024’s theatrical reboot Alien: Romulus with a story that offered a fresh perspective on the franchise. Alien and Aliens had supporting characters, like Ash and Bishop, who turned out to be Weyland-Yutani Corporation’s cyborgs, while Prometheus and Alien: Romulus had lead characters who were synthetic humans in David and Andy.

However, Alien: Earth’s intriguing spin on the Alien franchise mythology was the show’s decision to focus primarily on hybrid characters, synthetic bodies that had real human consciousnesses transferred into them. While there were human characters too, Hawley’s show spent most of its time with these synthetic “Lost Boys” as they navigated the disastrous escape of one of the Weyland-Yutani Corporation’s Xenomorphs.

Scott returned as an executive producer on Alien: Earth, but Hawley is the creator and showrunner and the Hulu show benefited dramatically from his loose approach to existing franchise lore and canon. As the USCSS Maginot brings back samples of extraterrestrial life to Earth for the Corporation’s top-secret experiments, this process exposes the main characters to the lethal Xenomorph, as well as a shady conspiracy involving their infamously shadowy employers.

Since Alien: Earth has a far longer runtime than any of the movies in the franchise, the show could explore the world of the series in more depth and detail than ever before. Much like Netflix’s ambitious sci-fi series 3 Body Problem. Alien: Earth spares no expense in creating an immersive reality that looks as lush and cinematic as any big-screen genre outing.

While Prime Video’s The Peripheral and Netflix’s Altered Carbon were similarly ambitious cyberpunk shows, both critically acclaimed series were canceled in their prime before they could fully fulfill their potential. The Peripheral lasted one eight-episode season on the streaming service, while Altered Carbon made it to a mere two seasons before the adaptation was axed.

In recent years, Prime Video’s expensive spinoff Blade Runner 2099 and Apple TV’s upcoming William Gibson adaptation Neuromancer have hinted at an openness to exploring the cyberpunk genre on the small screen once again. However, no matter how well these shows do, Ridley Scott’s Hulu spinoff Alien: Earth will always deserve credit for pioneering this trend as Noah Hawley’s cyberpunk show expanded the sparsely seen setting of the Alien movies into a lived-in world.
