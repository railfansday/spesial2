‘Only Murders in the Building’ Gets a Christmas Spin in Netflix‘s Near-Perfect Mystery

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

With a massive wave of mysteries and crime thrillers taking over movies, television, and even the world of podcasts, it was only a matter of time before somebody twisted the formula to make a lighter, more fun experience out of it. Only Murders in the Building did exactly that, and the Hulu series has unsurprisingly been on a real hot streak for the past half-decade. Going into its sixth season, Only Murders is still at the top of its game, delivering both mystery and comfort in equal measure.

While Only Murders is probably the best of these lighter-hearted murder mysteries, it is far from the only one nowadays, and Netflix just added another that will likely share a lot of the same fan base. Mistletoe Murders, which is currently in its third season, shares a lot of the same energy as Only Murders in the Building, but the entire thing is gift-wrapped in holiday cheer.

Mistletoe Murders, for those who aren't familiar, is about a woman named Emily Lane who moves to a small town and opens a year-round Christmas store — just in time for a series of murders to strike the usually quiet town. The series stars Sarah Drew as Emily, while Peter Mooney stars as the mayor of Fletcher's Grove, Sam Wilner.

It's a simple premise, but it has understandably become a holiday season staple for many over the past three years. It began airing on Hallmark Channel back in 2024, and has released a new season each year as the holidays begin. Unlike the original movies on Hallmark that have to fit their stories into right, 82-minute windows each time, Mistletoe Murders has made great use of its format and allowed the mysteries themselves to simmer a lot more. It's the perfect blend of Hallmark's Christmas tone and the TV mysteries that air year-round.

While Mistletoe Murders is very much still an original to Hallmark Channel and Hallmark+, the company struck a streaming deal to send previous seasons over the Netflix, likely hoping to get more attention on the show for its third season. Earlier this month, the first two seasons of Mistletoe Murders arrived on the Netflix lineup in the U.S. and have popped up on the streamer's Top 10 TV list since being added. So, in that regard, it appears Mistletoe Murders is already making plenty of new fans, making the move from Hallmark seem like an instant win.

The timing, of course, was no coincidence, as the third season of Mistletoe Murders just kicked off on November 5th. The night of Thanksgiving will see the fourth episode of Season 3 air on Hallmark, with two more episodes set to be released in December. The folks discovering the series are Netflix will be able to head over to Hallmark while Season 3 is still airing, and potentially remain fans for the inevitable Season 4 next year.
