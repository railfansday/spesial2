Broncos’ injury report includes running back being listed as questionable

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Broncos' injury report includes running back being listed as questionable appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Of course, the talk of the Denver Broncos vs. Pittsburgh Steelers game on Friday has centered around the drama in Pittsburgh. The team benched and then released Aaron Rodgers after his tirade about head coach Mike McCarthy. The quarterback claimed that McCarthy had fallen asleep in meetings and even gave a 1, 2, 3, Packers chant at halftime.

The drama bodes well for Denver, who hasn't had many easy matchups despite being 8-2. Denver isn't drama-free in their own right, though. The Broncos suspended J.K. Dobbins for the game against Pittsburgh for conduct detrimental to the team. While it isn't clear exactly what Dobbins did, it is likely connected to his demotion on the running back depth chart.

Rookie ball carrier Jonah Coleman has broken out in a big way in recent weeks, and Dobbins has expressed frustration at multiple points over his role this year. However, without Dobbins, the Broncos are all of a sudden thin at RB. RJ Harvey is currently on the injured reserve with a knee injury. The team has designated Tyler Badie to return from the IR, but he is being listed as questionable for the Steelers game with a thumb injury that has kept him sidelined in recent weeks.

Currently, Coleman is the only active player without a designation on Denver's 53-man roster. Regardless of whether Badie is or isn't able to go, Denver seems likely to elevate Cody Schrader off the practice squad. The Missouri product received a carry last week and played a crucial role as a special teams return man.

When Harvey, Badie, and Dobbins are all back, and if Coleman is truly a star, the Broncos will have a deep backfield for their playoff push. In Week 12, though, the team just may be thin at running back.
