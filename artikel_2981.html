Anthropic Just Went Public. Here’s the 1 Thing I’m Watching.

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The company's valuation depends on one ratio, and it went in the wrong direction last quarter.

By Lyle Daly – Nov 23, 2026 at 2:00AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Anthropic ›

Anthropic (ANT -8.50%) went public on Nov. 19, and its valuation drew the most attention. The artificial intelligence company closed its first trading day with a basic market cap of about $1.3 trillion, or about $1.8 trillion on a fully diluted basis.

The question for anyone investing in Anthropic, or considering doing so, is whether it can justify that valuation. There's one thing I'll be watching.

The key factor is compute spending as a share of revenue. In early 2025, this was 251%, meaning Anthropic spent $2.51 for every $1 in sales. By Q2 2026, it had fallen to 48% because revenue had climbed so quickly. But in Q3, the ratio climbed back up to 58%. Although revenue rose 47% from the previous quarter, compute costs increased 77% to $9.9 billion. Anthropic attributes this to buying $5 billion in short-term compute to keep up with demand, as this is much more expensive than its long-term contracts.

Anthropic has committed to spending more than $900 billion on compute through 2045, and about half of that is expected to come due by the end of 2030. At the company's current valuation, the market is betting that it will be able to bring down the ratio of compute spending to revenue. If it can't, either due to rising model training costs or a slowdown in revenue growth, the bull case for Anthropic gets harder to make.

The compute spending-to-revenue ratio is the first factor I'll consider when Anthropic releases its Q4 earnings. If the ratio comes back down toward Q2 levels, then the Q3 numbers were probably a one-off. If it keeps rising, that's a red flag.
