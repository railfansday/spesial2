Building a quiz application is one of the best ways to practice core React concepts. When users enter their name on a welcome screen, answer questions on a second screen, and view their final score on a third screen, your app needs two essential features: client-side routing and global state management. In this guide, we will walk through how to build a complete, three-page React quiz app using react-router-dom and React's built-in createContext hook—and cover common pitfalls to avoid along the way.
