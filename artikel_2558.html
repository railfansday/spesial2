Kobel on Dortmund's bravery and "deserved" draw in Munich

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

MÜNCHEN (Bulinews) Speaking to the media after their 2-2 draw with Bayern Munich, Gregor Kobel feels Borussia Dortmund were full value for their point tonight.

"I think we are happy with the draw, also how the game was played out. It came very late in the 90th minute and we fought for this draw," Kobel told Bulinews.com in the mixed zone.

"I think it was a very good performance from us, we believed in our ability and it was a good result for us in the end.

"When you look at the chances Bayern had, they had some very good opportunities. Still, I think it was a deserved draw for us," he explained.

Regarding difficult past trips to Munich, Kobel feels the weight of expectation and previous criticism didn't weigh on Dortmund against the German record champions.

"Of course we have some hard games and performances at times. But we knew before the game that maybe not a lot of people believed in us.

"We know what quality we have, even when we play them in Munich. I think it was very brave for that Niko [Kovac] played four at the back against them.

"Of course our mindset was we want to beat them today. We didn't beat them but I think the game was very eye-to-eye. We kept fighting and we got a point," Kobel concluded
