10 Years Later, Netflix's Greatest Sci-Fi Masterpiece Has Officially Racked Up 1.4B Streaming Hours

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Netflix's catalog is full of memorable shows, but even after 10 years, one series still stands above the rest. Stranger Things kicked off a massive franchise for the streamer, delivering terrifying mysteries, lovable characters, and a heavy dose of nostalgia. Since its 2016 premiere, the sci-fi show has become one of Netflix's most recognizable properties. Although the series finale aired at the end of last year, Stranger Things has continued to draw an audience throughout 2026, with fans watching more than 1.4 billion hours, and it's easy to see why.

Stranger Things' five seasons have their share of ups and downs, most notably the divided reaction to the finale, but 2026's viewing numbers show just how much love fans still have. By following a supernatural mystery that threatens the small town of Hawkins, Indiana, and the unlikely group that forms to stop it, Stranger Things blends horror with coming-of-age drama — an irresistible combination that's only one of the reasons why the show continues to endure on streaming a year after its original ending.

Over the last decade, Stranger Things has been impossible to avoid because of its undeniable cultural impact. The Netflix series thrives as a nostalgic '80s mystery that draws people in quickly, but soon reveals a story that isn't so easily defined by one genre. With lighthearted moments of friendship between the main characters, murder mysteries, and villains straight out of a horror movie, the show appeals to a broad audience. From middle school bullying to government conspiracies, the story's unpredictability is one of Stranger Things' major strengths. Each season delivers more mysteries, starting with Will Byers' (Noah Schnapp) disappearance from Hawkins and growing to include Eleven's (Millie Bobby Brown) background and the looming threat of Vecna (Jamie Campbell Bower).

Stranger Things ultimately thrives by raising the stakes. The town's safety is at risk, and from Season 1 onward, the show makes it clear that not everyone is safe. Throughout the series, fan-favorite characters are killed off permanently, providing real consequences for the story, and these emotional losses make fans want to return to the series and revisit their favorites. With its major twists and heart-wrenching moments, Stranger Things' story is so intriguing that fans are clearly struggling to say goodbye to the show forever.

Although the series has plenty to offer in its storytelling, Stranger Things' greatest strength is its characters. The series has a huge cast, and many have become fan favorites, but the core group is where the story thrives. Eleven, Mike (Finn Wolfhard), Will, Dustin (Gaten Matarazzo), Lucas (Caleb McLaughlin), and Max (Sadie Sink) are the heart of the narrative as their friendship grows and changes, and the team they form, as well as the older characters who join them, add the necessary humor and heart.

Stranger Things would never have gained so much attention without its lovable characters, and it continues to develop them thoughtfully as the story unfolds — focusing on elements like Eleven's self-discovery, Hopper's (David Harbour) grief, and Steve Harrington's (Joe Keery) transformation from a self-centered teen to a loyal protector. These character arcs add depth to the series and are best seen through rewatching the entire series.

Stranger Things is one of the most influential shows of the last decade, and it clearly still holds that distinction. Based on the viewing numbers, fans continue to return to the original Netflix series, which has spawned a franchise that includes the spin-off Stranger Things: Tales from '85, a stage play, and several tie-in novels that expand the story. Stranger Things may have ended last year, but with so many dedicated fans still watching, it's not going away any time soon.
