Leeds 'working on' Calvert-Lewin deal after 'behind-the-scenes' talks - Transfer expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Leeds United are working hard to reach a full agreement with Dominic Calvert-Lewin over the offer of a new contract.

All parties are believed to be keen to extend the 29-year-old's stay at Elland Road, and talks are said to have progressed to a stage where a deal is close to being confirmed.

Daniel Farke views the striker as a crucial part of his long-term plans and has been pushing for him to extend his stay beyond his current deal's 2028 expiry date.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that it is a no-brainer for Leeds to tie Calvert-Lewin down and end any speculation about his long-term future.

The England international has been linked with a host of other Premier League clubs in recent months, but was never likely to entertain a move away from Leeds.

From Calvert-Lewin's point of view, he is said to be very happy at Leeds and is keen to extend his stay, having finally found a club where he can establish himself.

Former Everton CEO Keith Wyness told Football Insider last month that Calvert-Lewin would be loyal to Leeds despite the whispers of interest from rival clubs.

The striker is now set to show that loyalty and commitment my signing a new long-term contract to extend his stay at Elland Road for the foreseeable future.

Calvert-Lewin remains a big part of Farke's ambitious plans, and the club are delighted with his performances since they took a gamble on signing him in 2025.

"Leeds have been working on a new contract for Calvert-Lewin, it was a no-brainer for them really," Brown told Football Insider.

"They've been talking about it behind the scenes for quite a while, so it really has been something they've wanted to pull off.

"He's a big part of the manager's plans there, he's proving himself to be an important player and one they can rely upon, which he's never really been able to do before.

"From his point of view, he's found a team at Leeds where he can settle as a regular, he's been consistent and they've put their full trust in him.

"He hasn't had that at former clubs, and Leeds will be delighted with the gamble they took to bring him in, because it really has paid off for them.

"There was talk about interest from a few different clubs, but Leeds were never going to let him go easily, and now it looks like he's going to commit his future to them."

Part of the reason Leeds have been so determined to tie Calvert-Lewin down is that he has attracted interest from a number of Premier League clubs with his performances.

Alongside earning a return to Thomas Tuchel's England squad, a number of clubs in need of a striker have been keeping tabs on his recent form.

Brown told Football Insider that Man United had considered a move to sign Calvert-Lewin, but any deal was unlikely to be formalised beyond an initial admiration.

His aerial presence, ability to lead the line, and recent improvement in front of goal would, on paper, make him a valuable asset to their attacking options.

However, despite the positive assets to his game, the striker has lingering question marks over his fitness and consistency, and isn't seen as being the level required for Man United.
