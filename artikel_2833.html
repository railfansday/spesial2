Chris Braswell injury update: Texans player carted off after being hurt on opening kickoff

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Chris Braswell suffered a serious injury on the first play of the Houston Texans vs. Indianapolis Colts game. The Texans edge rusher was elevated from the practice squad for Thursday's game and injured his right knee after being blocked by Caden Curry while Seth McGowan returned the ball.

Braswell was taken off the field on a cart and had an air cast covering the entirety of his right leg as he left the field. He appeared to be in tears while being taken off.

Amazon Prime Video sideline reporter Kaylee Hartung reported the Texans were checking Braswell's injured leg for a pulse, indicating they may be concerned about the circulation in the 25-year-old's leg.
