Broncos sign CB Riley Moss to a four-year contract extension worth $67 million

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

According to NFL insider Tom Pelissero, the Denver Broncos are finalizing a four-year contract extension with cornerback Riley Moss. It is a four-year deal worth up to $67 million dollars as the Broncos lock up one of their key starters in their secondary.

The story heading into the season was: which player would the Broncos re-sign? Cornerback Riley Moss, whom the Broncos spent a 3rd-round pick on, or cornerback Ja'Quan McMillian, a former undrafted free agent this coaching staff inherited? Well, at least for now, it appears Moss is their choice. He signed a four-year extension, which makes him the 23rd highest-paid cornerback in the league right now.

While many wanted the team to re-sign Ja'Quan McMillian, and honestly, they may still, Moss is worthy of this extension. He has one of the hardest jobs in the NFL, which is playing opposite star cornerback Pat Surtain II. Teams avoid going after Surtain II, which means more targets go Moss's way. Yes, he has had some struggles, and the refs, for whatever reason, love to call phantom penalties on him, but he has been a key part of the success of the Broncos' secondary these past few years. Now, both he and Surtain II are locked up and will keep their secondary strong for the foreseeable future.

Now, all eyes go to star slot corner Ja'Quan McMillian. He remains a pending free agent next season, and the narrative always was that the Broncos will likely have to pick between him and Moss. Well, Moss has an extension, so was the decision made? Perhaps, but there is some room for optimism, in my opinion. Slot corners are typically cheaper than outside corners, and the struggles of Jahdae Barron recently could have the Broncos wanting to bring back both. With that said, you do have Jahdae Barron, a former first-round pick who profiles as a slot corner and someone who many assumed would take McMillian's job when he was drafted. Could they still have faith in their first-round investment and risk letting McMillian walk?

At the very least, the Broncos have Pat Surtain II and Riley Moss locked up for the foreseeable future, and that should keep the Denver Broncos' secondary strong moving forward. We will just have to see what this Moss extensions means for Ja'Quan McMiillian and his future with the team.
