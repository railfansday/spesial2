Jaxon Smith-Njigba limited Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Seattle Seahawks WR Jaxon Smith-Njigba (ankle) was a limited participant in practice Wednesday, Nov. 25.

Why it Matters: The ankle injury is new for Smith-Njigba, so his status will be worth monitoring in the coming days. He currently leads the NFL in both receiving yards and receiving touchdowns, and assuming he suits up, he will be facing a San Francisco defense that he had 102 yards and a touchdown against earlier this year.
