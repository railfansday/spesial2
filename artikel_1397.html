Mourinho blocks Endrick’s exit: “Doors are closed.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The future of Endrick, the Real Madrid striker linked to Roma ahead of the January transfer window, remains tied to José Mourinho’s assessment.

The Portuguese coach has expressed his desire to keep his squad intact during the winter transfer window.

Speaking at a press conference on the eve of the match against Alavés, as reported by Real Madrid’s official website, the former Giallorossi coach stated: “I don’t want anyone coming in or going out. Doors are closed in both directions. I don’t want to be involved in the transfer market.”

Mourinho didn’t directly mention Endrick in this passage, but his stance on transfers represents an obstacle to the Brazilian’s potential move to the capital.
