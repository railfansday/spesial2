Comparing how long it took Kylian Mbappe to reach 75 Champions League goals with Messi & Ronaldo

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Kylian Mbappe has reached 75 Champions League goals, putting him alongside Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo in one of the competition’s most exclusive clubs.

Robert Lewandowski and Karim Benzema are the only two others in the history of the European Cup to have reached that number of goals in the competition.

The Real Madrid superstar is already chasing down the two greatest goalscorers in Champions League history – but could he one day overtake them in the all-time charts?

He’s on the right track. He hasn’t quite done reached the milestone in as emphatic style as Messi, who still holds the record for getting there at the youngest age and in the fewest games, but he has a considerable headstart on his idol, and the Champions League’s all-time top scorer, Ronaldo.

Mbappe scored his 75th and 76th goals in Madrid’s statement 5-1 comeback victory over PSV, adding another landmark to an already remarkable European career – albeit one that still lacks him getting his hands on the Champions League trophy.

The Frenchman has needed 103 appearances to reach 75 Champions League goals, putting him somewhere between Messi and Ronaldo for speed.

Messi needed eight games fewer to reach the milestone of 75 Champions League goals. Ronaldo, meanwhile, needed eight games more, having scored his 75th Champions League goal against Schalke in March 2015.

Mbappe also reached the landmark at a considerably younger age than Ronaldo, although Messi was still the youngest of the three, having done so about six months younger than the Los Blancos Galactico.

However, Mbappe has already surpassed both players for assists at the same stage, registering 30 compared to Ronaldo’s 24 and Messi’s 20. Considering that the two era-defining icons are also the all-time top assist-providers in Champions League history, the 27-year-old France international finds himself on the right trajectory to one day smash that record too.

His overall rate of goal contributions is almost identical to Messi’s, although the Barcelona legend was more prolific when it came to goals per minute.
