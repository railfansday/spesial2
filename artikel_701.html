KATSEYE’s Megan Is Seeing Pink in Warby Parker & Rare Beauty’s New Eyewear Collection

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The singer stars in her first solo campaign for the limited-edition collaboration, featuring five frames inspired by Selena Gomez's viral Soft Pinch Liquid Blush.

Selena Gomez‘s Rare Beauty Soft Pinch Liquid Blush has already taken over makeup bags and social media feeds. Now, the viral beauty favorite is making its way into the eyewear world, and KATSEYE‘s Megan is helping make it happen. Explore KATSEYE See latest videos, charts and news

Warby Parker and Rare Beauty have teamed up for a limited-edition collection of glasses and sunglasses inspired by five shades of the bestselling blush. Starting at $95, the collaboration brings some of Rare Beauty’s most recognizable colors to five new frames, giving fans another way to wear their favorite shades. And who better to show off the collection than Megan Skiendiel? The KATSEYE singer stars in her first solo campaign for the collaboration, giving fans a first look at the blush-worthy frames. It’s also a new milestone for the singer, whose fans are used to seeing her alongside her KATSEYE bandmates.

The collection itself is all about color, taking the same shades that made Rare Beauty’s Soft Pinch Liquid Blush a fan favorite and transforming them into eyewear. Rather than sticking to traditional black or tortoiseshell frames, the collaboration plays with color in a more wearable take, with styles designed to complement the blush shades.

Each of the five frames takes inspiration from a different Soft Pinch Liquid Blush shade. Franny is inspired by Resilience, Elwell by Encourage, Millie by Worth, Gillian by Hope and Albie by Faith. The lineup includes both glasses and sunglasses, giving shoppers a few ways to try the trend.

The best of both worlds, Franny pairs an oval silhouette with the subtle lift of a cat eye, all in a red-chestnut shade inspired by Rare Beauty’s Resilience blush.

These aren’t your usual black sunglasses: Albie swaps dark lenses for a rich berry tint inspired by Rare Beauty’s Soft Pinch Liquid Blush in Faith.

The Gillian pairs oversized round lenses with a slim acetate design for a look inspired by Rare Beauty’s Hope blush.

Rare Beauty’s Worth blush looks just as pretty off your cheeks with Millie, a pair of rounded sunglasses featuring wide rims and a soft rose-colored finish.

A little color goes a long way with Elwell, which combines a blush-inspired finish with darker details for a look that’s anything but ordinary.

The limited-edition Warby Parker x Rare Beauty collection is officially available as of Thursday (Nov. 5), with prices starting at $95.

All five styles are available online at Warby Parker’s website, through the Warby Parker app and at Warby Parker stores. You can also shop the Rare Beauty blush that inspired it all at Rare’s website, Sephora and Ulta (both online and in stores).
