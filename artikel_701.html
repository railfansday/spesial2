Don Toliver Announces Sixth Album ‘Nitrous,’ Due Next Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Fresh off receiving five Grammy nominations, Don Toliver has announced that his sixth album “Nitrous” will be released on December 4 via Cactus Jack/Donnway & Co/Atlantic Records.

“Nitrous,” which Toliver has been teasing for some time, was recorded, produced and engineered entirely by himself. Toliver announced the album on social media with a clip that shows him working on music in something of a futuristic studio laboratory. To coincide with the album’s release, Toliver will make his “Saturday Night Live” debut on December 5. “Nitrous” features the new single “Party With the Boys,” which dropped this past Friday along with a Tyler Ross-directed video featuring Bam Margera.

Toliver is coming off of a red-hot year in the wake of his fifth album “Octane,” which was released this past January and bowed atop the Billboard 200. The project featured the hit single “E85” that reached No. 3 on Billboard’s Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs chart and No. 15 on the Hot 100. The Houston rapper spent much of the year on the road as part of his “Octane World Tour,” which had a first run of arena shows from May to July before picking back up for a “Nitrous: New World Tour” addendum to the trek from August through the end of November, when he’ll wrap the shows at London’s O2 Arena.

Yesterday, Toliver made waves with a substantial five nominations for the 2027 Grammy Awards. In addition to best new artist, Toliver was nominated for best rap album with “Octane”; best rap performance with “Body”; and best rap song and best melodic rap performance with “E85.”
