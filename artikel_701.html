Lance Broadway, Pitcher Turned ‘Olympus Has Fallen’ Actor, Dies at 43

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

He starred at Texas Christian University before a brief stint in the big leagues with the White Sox and Mets.

Lance Broadway, who pitched in the major leagues with the Chicago White Sox and New York Mets before becoming an actor, has died. He was 43.

Broadway died unexpectedly Tuesday, according to a reporter for the Texas Christian University sports website HornedFrogBlitz.com (he pitched for the school in 2005). No cause of death was revealed.

After leaving baseball, the 6-foot-3 Texan made his film debut as a Secret Service agent under President Benjamin Asher (Aaron Eckhart) in Olympus Has Fallen (2013), the Gerald Butler action film directed by Antoine Fuqua. He then appeared on such TV shows as Kevin From Work, Lucifer, Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D., Real Husbands of Hollywood and Colony.

Lance Daniel Broadway was born on Aug. 20, 1983, in Bryan, Texas. He attended Grand Prairie High School and Waxahachie High School, then spent two years at Dallas Baptist University.

The right-hander transferred to TCU for his junior year and posted a 1.61 ERA with 151 strikeouts in 117 innings in his only season with the Horned Frogs as the Conference USA Pitcher of the Year.

Selected by the White Sox with the 15th pick in the 2005 MLB Draft, Broadway made it to the big leagues with Chicago in September 2007 and pitched briefly for the team the next two seasons and also for the Mets in 2009. He ended his baseball career with the Toronto Blue Jays organization.

Broadway took an acting class in November 2010 and “loved it and stuck with it,” he said in a 2013 interview.

Joining the cast of Olympus Has Fallen “was the same feeling as walking into a big-league locker room with guys that you knew were established, had earned the right to speak, and you do not have that right,” he said.

“I try to take that same approach on set of, you’ve never done this before, you don’t know what you’re talking about, so just be seen and say ‘Yes sir, Yes ma’am’ and do not be heard unless there is an appropriate time.”

More recently, he was a coach with Canes Baseball, designed to develop high school players to perform at the collegiate level.
