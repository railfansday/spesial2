Pat McAfee floored by Aaron Rodgers remarks about ‘unhealthy’ Mike McCarthy that got him cut

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Pat McAfee was completely stunned while Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers unleashed some serious allegations against his now former head coach Mike McCarthy on Wednesday.

The four-time NFL MVP appeared on "The Pat McAfee Show" after the Steelers announced Tuesday night he would be inactive for Friday's game against the Broncos and replaced by Mason Rudolph.

McAfee's jaw dropped when Rodgers claimed he confronted McCarthy about the coach sleeping and snoring during team meetings and missing walk-throughs — adding that it's allegedly a known behavioral pattern exhibited by McCarthy in the Steelers' building.

Pat McAfee reacts to Aaron Rodgers discussing his issues with the Steelers on his show, Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026. X

The Steelers released Rodgers shortly after he went scorched earth on-air.

Rodgers explained that "the nail in the coffin" occurred during Pittsburgh's 19-16 loss to the Eagles, when McCarthy purportedly broke up the halftime huddle by saying, "1-2-3 Packers."

"'1-2-3 Packers' is crazy," Rodgers said. "On the bus and in all of our group chats it was talked about, like how was that possible."

McCarthy coached Rodgers with the Packers from 2006 through December 2018 and they won a Super Bowl by beating the Steelers to cap the 2010 season.

Rodgers claimed things have been contentious behind the scenes for months — and that the team thought there needed to be a change with the offense because of McCarthy.

He explained that he confronted McCarthy as a "concerned friend" because he believed the coach was "unhealthy" due to him allegedly dozing off in meetings.

Rodgers said he found out he was going to be inactive from tight end Pat Freiermuth on a phone call Tuesday night, and that being benched was never a discussion with McCarthy.

"I'm standing on the truth, that's why it's not emotional for me anymore," Rodgers said. "Everyone knows it and everyone talks about it all over the building."

The 42-year-old signal-caller challenged the Steelers to release him if changes weren't implemented — and they took him up on his offer after his shocking interview.

Aaron Rodgers discussing his issues with the Steelers during an appearance on "The Pat McAfee Show," Nov. 25, 2026. X

"Obviously the team has chosen to back Mike," Rodgers said before his release. "We know where the players stand. Not even a question."

He also accused the franchise of trying to spin a narrative of benching him to steer the attention away from McCarthy's alleged issues.

McAfee was visibly taken aback by Rodgers spilling the tea on what's allegedly happening behind the scenes in Pittsburgh.

Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers (8) talks with head coach Mike McCarthy during an NFL game on Sept. 27, 2026, at Acrisure Stadium in Pittsburgh. USA TODAY Network via Reuters Connect

"At training camp when we were up there it felt good … Mike McCarthy coming back to [coach in] his hometown," McAfee said, noting that "after the bye week [Week 9] it feels like things have gotten a bit different."

Rodgers and McCarthy had a heated exchange that was caught on a hot mic during a 27-24 loss to the Browns in Week 4 when the quarterback became upset during the first quarter of the Thursday game while waiting for his longtime coach to make a play call.

Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers (8) reacts after a game at Lincoln Financial Field in Philadelphia on Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

A Prime Video mic picked up Rodgers yelling, "What the f–k? Call it!" while looking at McCarthy on the sideline.

Rodgers, who turns 43 next week, returned to Pittsburgh on a one-year, $22.5 million contract for what he said will be his final NFL season.

He told McAfee that he felt responsible for the Steelers' issues because he vouched for McCarthy's hire in Pittsburgh, which happened in January.

The Steelers are 6-4 heading into Friday's game against Denver.

It's possible that Rodgers has now potentially played in the last game of his career.
