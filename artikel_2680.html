Cook, Gibbs, Swift lead Week 11 fantasy football running back rankings

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

MIAMI, Nov. 18 (UPI) -- James Cook, Jahmyr Gibbs and D'Andre Swift lead my Top-50 fantasy football running back rankings for Week 11.

Chuba Hubbard and Christian McCaffrey also are inside the Top 5 of my rankings, which are listed below. Emmett Johnson, Jaylen Warren, Javonte Williams, Derrick Henry and Tony Pollard are inside my Top 10.

Players from the Atlanta Falcons, Cleveland Browns, Green Bay Packers, Los Angeles Rams, New England Patriots and Seattle Seahawks, who are on bye, should be benched -- or dropped in some cases.

My Week 11 waiver wire/free agent targets can be found here. My Week 11 wide receiver rankings also are available.
