PGA Tour’s Youngest Winner Blades Brown Reveals Funny Truth About His Older Sister

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Blades Brown has revealed a funny truth about his older sister, Millie, days after winning the World Wide Technology Championship in Mexico. Brown may be the youngest PGA Tour winner in 95 years but the 19-year-old's sister still finds ways to remind him that he is not as grown-up as he might think.

Brown said Millie, who is 22, has always treated him as her little brother and does not hesitate to put him in his place. She often reminds him that she went to college while he turned professional at 17, giving her an easy way to claim that she is smarter than him.

Despite his recent success, Brown enjoys having someone who treats him like a normal teenager. He shared the story while speaking to reporters ahead of the Austin Championship, explaining how his sister helps him stay grounded during a busy time in his career.

Brown spoke warmly about his relationship with his sister, explaining that she also makes time for simple activities that help him enjoy life outside golf. He said Millie regularly asks him to go out for ice cream and do the kind of things most teenagers enjoy.

"My sister is one of the people that helps me understand my age," he said. " She's 22, her name's Millie. I'm 19 so she's three years older than me and she's always seen me as her little brother and she humbles me real fast. She's like, I went to college and you didn't, so I'm smarter than you. She's awesome. She's always the one that says, hey, Blades, do you want to go out for ice cream? Kind of do typical teenage things."

He added that having Millie around allows him to relax and enjoy moments when he does not have to think about golf or behave like a professional.

Brown's comments came just days after he produced a remarkable comeback to win the World Wide Technology Championship. He started the final round seven shots behind but carded a nine-under-par 63 to force a playoff with fellow American Ben James.

The pair finished regulation on 23 under par before Brown secured the title on the second playoff hole in Cabo San Lucas. His victory made him the youngest PGA Tour winner in 95 years at 19 years and 171 days.

He became the first teenager to win on the PGA Tour since Jordan Spieth claimed the John Deere Classic in 2013. Brown also secured his PGA Tour card after finishing among the top 20 in the Korn Ferry Tour standings earlier this year.
