Report: Saints, 143-game veteran to 'discuss his future with the team'

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Here's something. Veteran safety Justin Reid is taking this week to "discuss his future with" the New Orleans Saints, per NewOrleans.Football's Nick Underhill. Ironically, it's been a year to the day since wide receiver Brandin Cooks was listed on the injury report for "personal reasons" and asked for his release, which was granted a few days later. Reid had the same designation on Wednesday.

The two players found themselves in similar situations. Both Cooks and Reid were some of the first big investments Kellen Moore made in free agency, but they each found the reality of their new team to be lacking. Cooks wasn't happy with how few targets he was seeing, and that a younger quarterback than Derek Carr was the one throwing them (in other words, he felt like this wasn't what he signed up for).

It's less clear what Reid has to gripe about. He was named a team captain in 2025 and played almost 100% of snaps in every game when healthy, at least until recently. He suffered a concussion against the Pittsburgh Steelers and returned to see 76.7% and 78.6% of snaps during the next two games. He's had a starting role in the defense but has delivered with impact plays. Reid only has one takeaway in 24 games with New Orleans while missing 22 tackles, per Pro Football Focus charting.

What's disappointing is that the Saints failed to trade either Reid or Cooks when they had the chance at the NFL deadline. Instead of taking offers on the table for Cooks, he was let go and left behind $3.2 million in dead money on this year's books. Cutting Reid (who we know teams were interested in) would leave $13.26 million on the 2027 salary cap, up from his current cap hit of $13.14 million. That's because the Saints restructured his contract earlier this year. They expected Reid would be a positive influence on his less-experienced teammates, lead the defense, and be someone they could count on. That hasn't been the case. We'll see if the situation changes and whether Reid is let go -- and what he would have to give back, if anything, to make it happen.
