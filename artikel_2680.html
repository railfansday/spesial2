Sayvia Sellers scores 16 points and well-balanced No. 16 Washington women top Oklahoma State 73-62

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

FORT MYERS, Fla. (AP) — Sayvia Sellers scored 16 points, two teammates had double-doubles and No. 16 Washington defeated Oklahoma State 73-62 on Friday in the Blue Heron Division of Coconut Hoops.

After leading by one point through three quarters, Washington scored the first 17 points of the fourth period to take control, 66-48.

Twice the Cowgirls got within 10 points, the second time at 69-59 with about three minutes remaining. The teams traded free throws the rest of the way.

Brynn McGaughy had 14 points and 10 rebounds, and Avery Howell added 10 points plus 11 rebounds for the Huskies (7-0). Tilda Trygger had 13 points and nine rebounds and Amayah Garcia scored 10 points off the bench. McGaughy, Howell and Trygger had 30 of Washington's 34 rebounds.

Stailee Heard scored 19 points, Yuting Deng 11 and Audi Crooks 10 for the Cowgirls (7-2).

Sellers scored seven points in the opening five minutes of the second quarter and the Huskies pushed their 15-10 first-quarter lead to 27-19. A 3-pointer by Brooke Carlson gave them their first double-digit lead, 30-20, and they would go on to lead by 14 with three minutes left in the half.

The Cowgirls cut it to 36-26 at halftime.

Oklahoma State held Washington scoreless over the final three minutes of the third quarter and a 3-pointer by Heard, who made four 3s in the period, made it 49-48 heading to the final quarter.

The Blue Heron Division continues on Saturday when Oklahoma State will play UMass. Washington will face UMass on Sunday. ___
