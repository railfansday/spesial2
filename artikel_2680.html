CFP rankings live updates: Show start time, TV channel for new top 25 reveal

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The second set of College Football Playoff rankings are set to be unveiled Tuesday, Nov. 10, in what should be an informative top 25 for the remainder of the season.

Has Notre Dame sealed a spot in the 12-team bracket following its win over No. 1 Miami? How far will the Hurricanes fall, considering their own schedule has not been overly challenging, particularly moving forward?

Elsewhere, will Brigham Young's second loss of the season preclude from the Cougars from the next set of brackets? And how high will Utah be following its win over its hated rival?

USA TODAY Sports will provide live reaction from Tuesday night's College Football Playoff rankings reveal. Watch Blake Toppmeyer and John Brice for their instant analysis on the CFP's second top 25.

In the SEC, Alabama, Texas and Tennessee have created something of a conundrum for the CFP selection committee, with the Crimson Tide ranked well ahead of a Volunteers team that trounced them in Neyland Stadium in Week 7. Following wins by each team in Week 10, will the committee dare move the Vols past Alabama — and a Texas team that has a head-to-head win over Josh Heupel and Co.?

That's to say nothing of the big news from Monday, Nov. 9, that the CFP will expand to 24 teams beginning in 2027, at the cost of Power Four conference championships.
