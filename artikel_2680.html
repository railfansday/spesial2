Game Recap: Saints host Panthers in Week 10 for divisional lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The New Orleans Saints had the chance to take sole position atop the NFC South when they faced off against the Carolina Panthers on Sunday. Here's a recap of all the action from the Caesars Superdome.

The Saints received first, and Tyler Shough got off to a positive start, completing his first pass to the returning Travis Etienne Jr for 11 yards.

After a few more short gains, the Saints went for a 4th-and-1, but Shough's throw intended for Chris Olave was deflected and picked off by Devin Lloyd.

With a short field after the return, runs from Chuba Hubbard and a 6-yard pass from Bryce Young to Tommy Tremble set up 3rd down from the 3-yard line.

But Young was incomplete on 3rd down, before being sacked by Carl Granderson on 4th down to keep the game scoreless.

New Orleans proceeded to three-and-out and punt from deep inside their own territory before Carolina were soon forced to punt themselves, on a drive hampered by penalties.

Shough completed a few more short throws to Etienne on the ensuing series, but the Saints had to punt again.

The Panthers moved the ball past midfield as Hubbard broke off a 17-yard run, before three straight incompletions would follow.

Ryan Fitzgerald's 48-yard attempt was wide left, and the game remained scoreless.

Shough found a chunk gain of 20 yards to Juwan Johnson, but fumbled a play later to return possession to Carolina.

Young then found Tremble twice for a total of 41 yards as Carolina entered the red zone. He connected with Darren Waller for 13 more, before Hubbard ran in the 2-yard score to give the Panthers the lead.

Shough kicked into gear with a 20-yard pass to Jordyn Tyson, followed by a 17-yard scramble.

But the Saints rattled off multiple runs for no yardage before Audric Estime was stuffed on 4th-and-1 to turn the ball over.

Despite a 20-yard pass to Tetairoa McMillan to move the drive to midfield, the Panthers had to punt with just over a minute on the clock.

Shough was then sacked twice as New Orleans failed to move into scoring position before halftime.

Carolina had to punt to begin the second half, with Colby Wooden sacking Young on a 3rd down.

The Saints then embarked on a 64-yard drive, highlighted by an 18-yard catch by Tyson and an 11-yard pass to Olave to convert a 3rd down.

Alvin Kamara would run in a 3-yard touchdown to tie the game at 7-7.

Young returned to the field and continued to move the ball, connecting with a variety of receivers as the Panthers moved into field goal range before coming up short on a 3rd-and-9.

Fitzgerald converted this time from 55 yards to give the visitors a 10-7 advantage.

The teams would then exchange three-and-outs, with New Orleans getting the ball to begin the fourth.

Shough completed a number of short throws to Tyson, but was incomplete on 3rd down to force the black and gold to punt again.

The Panthers didn't fare any better, punting back to the hosts after finding just 16 yards on their series.

Shough then spread the ball across the field as Carolina were guilty of a number of penalties, quickly moving the drive into the red zone.

A pass interference penalty cost the Panthers 17 yards, setting up Kamara for his second touchdown of the day with a 4-yard touchdown catch.

But trailing 14-10, Carolina responded in a big way.

Young found Hubbard on 29- and 34-yard passes as he drove into the red zone in rapid fashion. Hubbard then ran in a 1-yard touchdown to give the Panthers the lead.

Shough found Olave, Tyson, and Kamara as the Saints sought a response, but came up short on a 3rd down. Charlie Smyth's 55-yard kick tied the game with just under a minute left.

Looking to get into field goal range and win the game, a pass interference penalty against Kool-Aid McKinstry gave Carolina the opportunity to do exactly that.

Fitzgerald's 54-yard effort was good as time expired, giving the Panthers the 20-17 victory.

The Saints are now back on the road as they pay a visit to the Chicago Bears this weekend.
