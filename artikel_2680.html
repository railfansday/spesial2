Why this version of Arch Manning can lead Texas football to special places | Golden

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Is Arch Manning in the middle of another second-half resurgence?

Texas Longhorns quarterback Arch Manning (16) celebrates the win over the Mississippi Rebels at Darrell K Royal-Texas Memorial Stadium in Austin on Saturday Oct. 24, 2026. (Jay Janner/Austin American-Statesman)

Texas' QB1 was asked after Saturday's 34-20 win at No. 18 Missouri if this was his best individual performance of the season.

"I don't know," he said. "I've got to watch the film."

Hey Arch, those two mice in sunglasses standing on the sideline at Faurot Field all agreed they saw it as your best game and their third visually challenged brother co-signed after watching through his readers.

Manning doesn't shy away from a challenge and even during the darkest moments of his fourth year on campus, he has maintained a stiff upper lip and a quiet confidence that has sustained him from the tough times to this nice little heater he has been on since the Florida loss. He played one of the cleanest games of his career with 297 yards 28-of-38 passes with three touchdowns and no interceptions or lost fumbles.

Texas Longhorns head coach Steve Sarkisian reacts to defeating Missouri at Faurot Field at Memorial Stadium in Columbia on Saturday, Nov. 7, 2026. (Mikala Compton/Austin American-Statesman)

His play has admittedly been spotty at times this season but Manning never lost the respect of the men in that locker room who have kept their faith in him strong throughout the season.

At it's not just the offensive players who believe in him. Manning commands the respect of all and one need only check out what was happening on the sideline in the moments before the defense took the field to seal it on Warren Roberson's forced fumble and Graceson Littleton's scoop, 44-yard return and kneel down at the four-yard line.

Manning had lifted the Texas Longhorns from a 20-13 deficit to a 27-20 lead but wasn't over on the bench with his head in a tablet. Instead, he was in the defensive huddle imploring his guys to get one more stop to bring a win back to the 512.

"Yeah, he's a team dude, not just offense," said defensive tackle Hero Kanu. "It's everybody. We feed off his energy. His energy was electric, him staying with us on the d-line, talking and getting us hyped."

With three games remaining, Manning is leading and just as important, is him starting to play like the big cheese that led the Horns to a 6-1 finish to the 2025 season. This was his sixth straight win over a top 25 opponent dating back to last season.

"It was a long week," Manning said. "I'm glad we had a little gritty road win."

Texas Longhorns defensive back Graceson Littleton (29) intercepts a pass during the game against Missouri at Faurot Field at Memorial Stadium in Columbia on Saturday, Nov. 7, 2026. (Mikala Compton/Austin American-Statesman)

Sure, he's the most polarizing quarterback around these parts since Chris Simms, who knows a thing or 10 about living up to the family standard of quarterbacking amid heavy scrutiny both from the stands, the press box and the social media monster.

Unlike Simms' stay here where he played in front of a fan base split on if Mack Brown should be starting him or the more popular Major Applewhite, Manning isn't embroiled in any quarterback controversy. Those few murmurs about possibly getting back K.J. Lacey some run after the Florida game have gone away amid this three-game winning streak that has come despite the Horns missing some key contributors due to injury.

In my annual preseason prediction piece, I projected Manning to pick up where he left off in the 2025 season when he finished the last six games averaging 285.6 passing yards while accounting for 19 touchdowns with only two interceptions in his final 315 attempts.

I wrote that he would finish what I believed to be his final college season with 3,775 passing yards and 36 touchdown passes. Why not expect him to take it to another level? After all,  he was only the third quarterback in program history - Vince Young and Colt McCoy were the others - to top 3,000 yards passing, 25 touchdown passes, 500 rushing yards and 10 rushing touchdowns in the same season.

The current numbers -  2,024 passing yards, 18 TD passes and two rushing scores - are well off the pace unless the Horns keep this streak going through a sustained CFP run that would include two or more games added to the 12-game regular season slate.

It could happen, especially if wideout Cam Coleman's shoulder heals up and if the nation's top sack master Colin Simmons can add to his workload after toughing out a dozen or so plays with a high ankle sprain.

Things broke their way Saturday with losses by two teams ahead of them in the rankings - No. 1 Miami and No. 4 Kentucky - which paves the way for a move up.

Wins aside, Manning just provided a nice blueprint to success entering his Louisiana homecoming at LSU Saturday: play clean, take what the defense gives you and go downtown when given the opportunity.

A win over the Bayou Bengals would be a national best fifth over a ranked team and all but guarantee a spot in the big tournament, assuming they don't suffer an epic collapse in winnable games against Arkansas and the reeling Texas A&M Aggies.

This version of Manning could lead the Horns to special places.
