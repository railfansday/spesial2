Battle Town sink Sidley United

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

In the Premier Division, Battle Town claimed an excellent 3-0 home victory over Sidley United. Harris Thorogood-Swoffer opened the scoring in the 11th minute before Trystan Mayhew doubled the advantage midway through the first half. Substitute Sam Herget completed the scoring after 31 minutes as Battle secured all three points with a commanding performance.

Bexhill Amateur Athletic Club continued their impressive goalscoring form with a 5-2 victory away to Ninfield. Harley Thomas led the way with a brace, while Taylor Field, Daniel Goergen and substitute Reuben Davis were also on target for the visitors.

Sandhurst came from behind to record a 3-1 home victory over The Junior Club Tackleway. Trailing 1-0 at the interval, Sandhurst turned the contest around through second-half goals from substitute Sam Hall, who struck twice, and Ethan Reynolds.

Rye Town maintained their momentum with a hard-fought 2-1 away victory against Victoria Baptists. Ole Reader and Ben Reader were the goalscorers for Rye as they secured another valuable three points.

The division's remaining fixture saw Wadhurst United and SC Pass & Move Arrows share the points following a goalless draw, with neither side able to find the breakthrough.

In Division One, Little Common II continued their excellent recent form with an emphatic 5-1 away victory over Herstmonceux. Igor Wydra was the star of the show with a hat-trick, while Cyrus Abbott and Attilio Field also found the net.

Bexhill Amateur Athletic Club II recorded an impressive 5-0 home victory over Robertsbridge United. Frankie Deeprose scored twice, with Ben Fergusson, Dominic Sinden and substitute Stanley Salter completing the scoring.

There was no shortage of entertainment at St Leonards Social, where the home side edged a remarkable nine-goal thriller against Mountfield United. Social eventually emerged 5-4 winners, with Damien March scoring twice and Jack Buckley, Stanley Dhaura and Allan McMinigal also on target.

The Junior Club Tackleway II and Pevensey & Westham could not be separated in a closely fought 1-1 draw, with both sides finding the net after a goalless first half.

In Division Two, Parkfield continued their remarkable goalscoring form with another commanding performance, recording a 6-2 away victory over SC Pass & Move Arrows II. Jamie Salvidge led the way with a hat-trick, while Bobby Bowles, Jack Lugg and substitute Josh Harris also scored. Che Davis and Kyle Wilson replied for the hosts.

Battle Town II enjoyed a successful afternoon as they claimed an impressive 4-2 victory away to Sedlescombe Rangers. Alex Southall and Frankie Walbrin both scored twice for Battle. Spencer Sharkey and Liam Whicker found the net for Sedlescombe.

Ticehurst returned to winning ways with a convincing 3-0 victory away to Catsfield. Joshua Jevon opened the scoring after 15 minutes before Matthew Goldsmith doubled the advantage in the 38th minute. Finlay Brown added a third in the 80th minute to complete a comfortable victory.

In Division Three, Icklesham Casuals continued their outstanding run of form with a comprehensive 5-1 away victory over Hollington Hawks. Alfie Hillman scored twice, while Greg Stoodley, substitute George Stoodley and Callum Hadaway were also on target. Chris Sanders scored the consolation goal for Hawks.

Northiam 75 II also produced an impressive display as they swept aside Hollington United III 6-1 away from home. Alfie Trainor Wood scored for the hosts, but Northiam proved far too strong as they secured one of the biggest victories of the afternoon.

Hawkhurst United returned to winning ways with a narrow 2-1 home victory over Sedlescombe Rangers II. Marco Palumbo and Joe Waters were the goalscorers for Hawkhurst, while Alexander Nuttney replied for the visitors.
