DeVonta Smith logs full practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Philadelphia Eagles WR DeVonta Smith (hamstring) was a full participant in practice Thursday, Nov. 19.

Why it Matters: Smith has been sidelined for weeks with a hamstring injury, but it looks like he has a chance to suit up in Week 11. The quarterback situation isn't ideal, but if Smith does return, he is worth a look as a WR3.
