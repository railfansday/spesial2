What we learned as Darius Acuff Jr.‘s 36 aren't enough in Kings' loss to Suns

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

What we learned as Darius Acuff Jr.'s 36 aren't enough in Kings' loss to Suns originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area

SACRAMENTO – The Kings continue to make their dismal start to the 2026-27 NBA season even worse.

They lost Wednesday night to the Phoenix Suns, 128-107, falling to 1-13 on the year and extending their franchise-record home winless streak to begin the season to nine games.

The defeat capped their longest home stand of the season at Golden 1 Center at 0-7, with many members of the lifeless 13,016 in attendance leaving early.

The game followed a familiar pattern. It was close at halftime – Phoenix led by five at the break – the Suns pulled away and opened a 21-point lead in the third quarter, and the Kings were unable to pull off the unlikely comeback while a handful of fans boo'd at the final buzzer.

Their 1-13 start ties the worst start to a season in franchise history.

Another pattern continued as prized rookie Darius Acuff Jr. showed off his scoring chops, finishing with a game-high 36 points and six assists.

The 2026 NBA Draft's No. 7 overall pick got off to a hot start, scoring 10 points in the first quarter and adding 11 more in the second quarter. His 21 first-half points were the second-most of any half this season (he scored 22 on Oct. 23 against Portland). He made 7 of 10 shots including 4-of-7 from 3-point range and all of his 2-point attempts.

Acuff continued his torrid scoring with 13 of the Kings' 24 points in the third quarter while Phoenix's lead ballooned. He finished 11 of 22 from the floor including 6 of 11 from deep. He made 8 of his 10 free throws (no other King shot more than two).

Acuff finished two points shy of his career-high 38 he scored on Halloween against the Memphis Grizzlies when he made nine threes. He entered the night leading rookies in total points, 30-point games, assists and made threes.

He got little help Wednesday. Zach LaVine was second on the Kings with 18 points. Keegan Murray (12) was Sacramento's only other player in double digits.

Acuff hit a 3-pointer midway through the third quarter against Dillon Brooks and immediately started talking trash down the court. It pushed Acuff's total 26 points.

Brooks responded with a step-back three just over a minute later, giving him 25, and leading to a timeout. Brooks was animated and yelled things towards fans seated near the visiting bench.

Brooks tied for the Suns lead with 29 points while Phoenix outscored Sacramento by 26 points during his 31 minutes. Brooks led the team with five made 3-pointers. Devin Booker matched with 29. The two combined to shoot 22 of 36 and (61 percent).

Brooks has been a known irritant throughout his career. He often got into it with former Kings guard/forward DeMar DeRozan and appears to be continuing his Sacramento beef with Acuff.

The Kings scored the first six points of the fourth quarter with three straight baskets from Keegan Murray, who had only four points in the first three quarters combined. It trimmed a 15-point lead to nine and injected some much-needed life back into Golden 1 Center.

But the Suns responded with a Jalen Green dunk out of a timeout and pushed the lead back to double-digits. Phoenix went on to outscore the Kings 29-23 in the first half and 63-47 in the second half.

Domantas Sabonis sat the entire fourth quarter while the Suns went small, using Miles Bridges as their center late. Sabonis sat despite having eight points, 13 rebounds and eight assists.
