Investing - Theory, News &amp; General • Re: TIPS ladder placement (taxable vs trad vs Roth)?

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Just because the TIPS ladder is in traditional does NOT mean you have to take the money out of traditional to use it.

If you have assets (like equities) in taxable, sell them there to get the money you need and then sell the TIPS rung in traditional and buy equities there. Watch out for wash sale rules.

Eventually, you will have to sell something in traditional and pay the tax on it. But until then, he taxes on the equities you sell in taxable will likely be less than the ordinary rate you would pay on all of the TIPS rung. When you eventually sell the equities in traditional and take the money out you will pay tax at ordinary rates on all of it (including the gain that would have been taxed as LTCG in taxable).
