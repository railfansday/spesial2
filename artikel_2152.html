'Disaster' - Stefan Borson drops verdict on Sunderland sacking Regis Le Bris

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Sunderland are making the right decision by backing Regis Le Bris despite the serious risk of relegation this season.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that the manager deserves time to turn things around.

The Black Cats are currently sitting bottom of the Premier League table after nine games, with Europa League football also to contend with.

It is understood that, despite the situation, Sunderland's owners are more than happy to back the manager who took them to seventh in the league last season.

After defeat to Dinamo Zagreb on Thursday, 5 November, the Black Cats still stand a very good chance of securing a play-off spot in Europe.

Not everything is doom and gloom at the Stadium of Light, but success in Europe will mean much less if they cannot remain in the Premier League as well.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson backs the decision to keep Le Bris in charge, but did admit that relegation would be a "disaster".

"Relegation would be a disaster, obviously. And a bit of a shock. It's hard to say it, but probably, qualifying for Europe was bad news for them," he said.

"They just weren't ready, and probably didn't have a big enough squad to cope with it. I think they've been quite unlucky so far.

"Maybe a reversion to the mean will come, and they will recover. I don't think they're the worst team in the Premier League by any means.

"It's a complicated situation. I do not think they will change the manager. I think it would be a mistake to do so, given the job he did last year."

For now, it would seem that Le Bris' future at the Stadium of Light is safe, but that could quickly change as nerves get worse this season.

Performances have certainly disappointed so far, and that needs to change. The squad has some very talented players.

Managers can only live off previous accomplishments for so long, and it would appear that plans are being forged in the worst case scenario.

Mick Brown told Football Insider understands that Sunderland have concerns over Thomas Frank, but it would suggest contingency plans are being put in place.
