Ella Langley Breaks Out of a Tie With Carrie Underwood for This Record on Country Airplay Chart

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Ella Langley’s “Loving Life Again” claims a third week at No. 1 on Billboard’s Country Airplay chart, leading the list dated Nov. 21 with 33.8 million audience impressions Nov. 6-12, according to Luminate.

The latest frame ups Langley’s 2026 total to 12 weeks atop Country Airplay across four songs — “Choosin’ Texas,” “Be Her” and the Morgan Wallen duet “I Can’t Love You Anymore” previously led — breaking her out of a tie with Carrie Underwood (2006) for the most by a woman in a single year. Wallen holds the record with 17 weeks on top in 2023. He also logged 16 in 2025 and 13 in 2022, and Luke Combs had 13 in 2019 and 2021, while Kenny Chesney totaled 12 in 2007.

In 2026, Langley’s time atop Country Airplay outpaces runner-up Combs’ nine weeks and Wallen’s seven. Combs has led with “Sleepless in a Hotel Room” and “Be by You,” and Wallen with “20 Cigarettes,” “Don’t We,” his Langley collaboration and “Been by Now.”

Meanwhile, “Choosin’ Texas” has spent 49 weeks and counting atop Hot Country Songs, two shy of the all-time mark held by Bebe Rexha and Florida Georgia Line’s “Meant to Be” (2017-18).

Lainey Wilson pockets her 10th Country Airplay top 10 and John Mayer his first as “Phone Keys Wallet” rises two spots to No. 9 (16.4 million, up 4%).

Plus, Russell Dickerson reaches the top 10 (12-10; 15.6 million, up 9%) for the seventh time, with “Worth Your Wild.”

Thomas Rhett scores his second-highest Country Airplay debut as “Be There in a Minute” opens at No. 19 (9.3 million). Only “What’s Your Country Song” (No. 18 in 2020), one of his 21 No. 1s, started higher.

Meanwhile, U2 makes its Country Airplay debut as “Torn,” featuring Dolly Parton, launches at No. 28 (4.8 million), marking the late Parton’s highest ever entrance on the chart. With “Son of Jolene,” alongside Belles, likewise on the list at No. 56 (1.1 million), two Parton songs place simultaneously for the first time since 2006; “Torn” is also her first top 40 entry on the chart since then.
