Joshua Kimmich holds off on Jamal Musiala comments during fan club visit, promises Wednesday response

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Bayern Munich midfielder Joshua Kimmich declined to address the ongoing public debate surrounding teammate Jamal Musiala during a fan club visit on Sunday, though he confirmed he will break his silence on Wednesday.

Appearing at the Stadthalle in Viechtach to meet with members of the Red Ritters Kollnburg supporter group, Kimmich kept the focus strictly on the club's traditional fan festivities rather than engaging with the intense scrutiny enveloping Säbener Straße. After sidestepping questions regarding Musiala's current situation, the German international assured reporters, as captured by @iMiaSanMia, that he would share his perspective on Wednesday following Bayern's UEFA Champions League fixture away against LOSC Lille.

Pointed remarks from board member Karl-Heinz Rummenigge and club leadership regarding Musiala's protracted return to top form—after battling serious physical and neurological setbacks—have drawn wide criticism from across the footballing world. CEO Jan-Christian Dreesen has since sought to temper external pressure by insisting that Musiala will be given all the time he needs to recover, though the public discourse has drawn sharp pushback from players union FIFPRO and German media alike. As one of Bayern's foremost dressing-room leaders, Kimmich's upcoming remarks on Wednesday will carry significant weight as the squad tries to maintain focus ahead of a crucial trip to France.
