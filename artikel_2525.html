Kevin Durant's night vs. Suns: A milestone, a clash with Dillon Brooks and a win for Rockets

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Kevin Durant scored his 33,000th career point Wednesday and spent a chunk of a late-game possession - with the Houston Rockets' 116-104 win over the Phoenix Suns all-but secured - jawing with former Rockets forward Dillon Brooks.

The two got in each other's faces as the game wound down, a heated discussion that ended with both players earning technical fouls - Brooks' was his second of the game, leading to an automatic ejection.

"I don't mind if somebody got a grievance with me. I don't mind addressing it right there in the moment. I'm sure (Brooks) has some," said Durant, who scored 20 points. "I said some things he probably didn't like, and he addressed it right there and I respect that as a man. It didn't get further than that.

"But if it does get further than that, I'll be ready for that too."

Durant wasn't the only Rocket to trash talk with Brooks, who popped into Houston's locker room after the Suns beat the Rockets in Phoenix on Nov. 5 but didn't do so after the loss at Toyota Center.

Brooks, one of the players Houston traded for Durant, also got into it with former teammates Amen Thompson, Fred VanVleet and Alperen Şengün.

"It's going to be that every night with (Brooks). We know that and for good or bad, it can help your team or hinder your team at times," said coach Ime Udoka said. "I think our guys did a good job of keeping it cool, playing through it, although quite a few guys got technicals."

Houston led by just three with under five minutes left but went 6-for-9 down the stretch to pull away for its second straight win. Şengün led the Rockets with 23 points and added 11 rebounds and 11 assists for his 15th career triple-double - passing Hakeem Olajuwon for second in franchise history.

"Hopefully I play with the Rockets for long, long years and pass James Harden," Şengün said. The former Houston guard leads the franchise with 46 career triple-doubles. "That's going to be my next goal."

The Rockets recognized Şengün for his achievement and Durant for his - the latter received a game ball, Udoka said.

"I try not to make it about myself in there. I understand that it's rare to hit these type of accomplishments and I don't ever want to undersell it," Durant said. "But we playing a team game and it's not about me most of the time."

Asked what he does with the numerous balls he receives, Durant said he gives them to his mom, close friends and agent.

"I'll lose track of them if I don't give them to them. I don't ever want to undersell some of that stuff, but I'm about the experience," he said.

Durant scored the milestone bucket on a 3-pointer right before halftime and gestured towards Brooks afterwards. At 33,006 points, he remains at fifth all time - 638 points away from passing Kobe Bryant.

"A part of me is supposed to be doing this," Durant said. "I had expectations since I was kid to be great and to last long. So when I'm doing stuff like that it's cool. … I'd be more upset that I didn't accomplish this stuff. It's just what I'm supposed to be doing at this point."

"I'm sure he will (recognize it) at the end of his career," Udoka said. "But yeah … he's surpassing so many things, it's almost become second nature to him."

The first bucket Houston scored after Phoenix got within three came on an action they'd found success with all season.

VanVleet brought the ball up and tossed the ball to Şengün in the post before screening for Durant on the perimeter in what's called a post split action (ball in the post, two perimeter players screening for each other). Both defenders rushed to Durant, leaving VanVleet wide open. Şengün waited for a beat before hitting the guard for a triple that kicked off the game-sealing run.

Those off-ball actions have been a crucial part of the Rockets' offensive improvement - they entered Wednesday's game with the league's best offensive rating, per Cleaning the Glass. Having increased movement on the backside makes isolation attacks from Durant, Şengün and others more dangerous.

The Rockets entered Wednesday's game third in the league in points per game off cuts, per NBA.com. A lot of them have come off Şengün passes."Scoring is fun but … I don't get tired with passing like that," he said.

Şengün said he's always been able to see those actions but said he worked this summer with coaches Cam Hodges, Walker Miller and Orhun Gungoren to continue improving at them.

Durant said the beauty of those off-ball actions comes in part from randomness. That can lead to turnovers if the passer isn't patient, he said.

"Playing with Draymond (Green), he's making quick passes. As soon as he sees something open for a split second, he's throwing it. He didn't care about turnovers," Durant said. "I think Alpy takes a little more time and waits for the play to process a little bit. … It's on the passer really, to take his time and figure out what reads he wants to make.

"And Alpy is one of those guys who pretty much gets it right every time."

This closes out Houston's NBA Cup group stage schedule with a 2-2 record.

The Rockets were already eliminated entering the game when Dallas defeated Denver, but their win eliminated the Suns.

The Mavericks advanced to the knockout round by winning West Group A.

During the NBA Cup's knockout rounds, the Rockets will be scheduled two regular-season games - one on Dec. 6 or 7 and one on Dec. 9 or 10 - to give them a full 82-game schedule.
