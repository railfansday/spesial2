Texas football film study: This Arch Manning move drove winning TD vs. Mizzou

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Arch Manning's faliure to diagnose the opposing defense cost Texas football in a game-ending turnover against Florida last month. The Longhorns won at Missouri on Saturday in part due to Manning's mental growth.

Texas sat tied with Missouri at 20-20 with 5:13 remaining, facing a 2nd-and-13 from the Missouri 23-yard line. The stakes were clear. Take the lead and win, and Texas would add a fourth top-25 victory this season. Lose late, and a third loss could crush the Longhorns' College Football Playoff hopes. Manning delivered with the season potentially in the balance, hitting running back Raleek Brown on a wheel route for a touchdown down the right sideline.

First watch of the touchdown shows a simple, albeit impressive, throw and catch for a score. But the play-and the preceding moments-illustrate the mental development of Texas' quarterback. Let's watch the tape, and see Manning's growth from a frustrating October to a promising start to November.

Take a look at the Missouri defense before the ball is snapped on the second -and-13 in question. The Tigers only have one safety atop the screen. Texas' three receivers to the left will command significant defensive attention, and Missouri appears to have a four-man pass rush planned. The Longhorns have one receiver set to the far right side, and Brown is to Manning's right in the backfield.

Arch Manning properly recognized single coverage on Texas running back Raleek Brown before a go-ahead touchdown pass in a 34-20 win at Missouri on November 7, 2026. (YouTube)

All four of the outside wide receivers run inside the right hash mark in the first seconds of the play. The only players to Manning's right? Brown and Missouri linebacker Dante McClellan. Manning recognizes the lack of help for McClellan. He then sees Brown even with McClellan shortly after the snap. Manning is already ready to pull the trigger.

Raleek Brown was even with a Missouri linebacker, and the ball was leaving Arch Manning's hands in this go-ahead touchdown in Texas' 34-20 win at Missouri on November 7, 2026 (YouTube)

Brown's speed and Manning's touch do the rest, as the running back sprints past a flat-footed linebacker and the quarterback floats the ball right into the pocket of his wide-open running back. Touchdown Texas, a score both Brown and Manning saw coming as soon as they saw the defensive coverage in question.

"I saw that (Missouri) was playing (man-to-man coverage) right before and I got happy," Brown told the media in Columbia postgame regarding his touchdown reception. "I just took off."

Brown, a fifth-year senior and transfer from Arizona State, is increasingly the lead running back in a three-man Texas running-back unit. His impact as a pass catcher might be a separating factor. Texas' win at Missouri marked Brown's second straight game with a receiving touchdown. He caught a TK-yard touchdown from Manning in Texas' 31-3 win over Mississippi State, the product of an out route from Brown out of Texas' backfield.

Manning currently giveth and taketh away with his processing, especially with regards to his running backs. He took a crunching sack on a 3rd-and-16 against Mississippi State, a play in which he said he should have recognized the open pass to Brown in Texas' backfield. Brown's touchdown vs. the Bulldogs brought the inverse, in which Manning immediately recognized Brown as the uncovered pass catcher, then tossed the ball to an open receiver for an easy score.

Manning made a slate of maddening mistakes in losses to Oklahoma and Florida last month. He's played clean football of late, with six touchdown passes, zero interceptions and two wins in Texas' last two games. Manning's embrace of his Brown is a driver of his recent success, in which a mind meld with his lead running back paid dividends in back-to-back weeks.
