‘A crucial win’: Swansea City vs Cardiff City predictions

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Swansea City come into this Sunday’s derby match against Cardiff City on a run of one win in the last seven Championship games. The Swans’ unassailable form at the start of the season has waned, but they have given a good account of themselves under the interim management of Ashley Williams after Vitor Matos’ exit.

After a 2-2 draw with West Brom last weekend, the South Welsh side were narrowly beaten 2-1 by Southampton in midweek. Williams is set to remain in charge for this weekend’s game, but talks to appoint Freyr Alexandersson as the new head coach are progressing.

As for Cardiff City, they come into the crucial game off the back of a heavy midweek defeat. The Bluebirds’ unbeaten home record was brought to an end in shock fashion as Preston North End – who were winless away from home beforehand – thumped them 4-0 at the Cardiff City Stadium.

Brian Barry-Murphy‘s side have come up from League One playing some impressive football, but style doesn’t matter on a day as big as this. Away form will be a big worry for the visitors, having drawn two and lost six of their eight Championship games on the road this term.

“This is the first game Swansea City and Cardiff City fans will have looked for when the fixtures were announced. The importance of this game in the context of their seasons is nothing compared to the weight it holds as a standalone rivalry in South Wales.

“Swansea City’s form and managerless status mean they’re not preparing for this in ideal fashion. But, with passionate club legend Ashley Williams in the dugout, the supporters can be confident that the players will be fully aware of the importance of this fixture.

“The Bluebirds’ away form and bruising midweek loss mean they’re there for the taking too. They’re on more stable footing with a permanent manager – and an impressive one at that – in place, but the visitors’ record on the road is a big concern for me.

“I’m leaning towards a crucial win for Williams and the Swans.”

“Swansea City have seen their form slump over recent weeks and need to get themselves back on track. They risk slipping out of the play-offs if they aren’t careful.

“The Swans are winless in their last four games. Therefore, they will be itching to return to winning ways this weekend.

“Cardiff City are still adapting to life back in the Championship. They have been a bit hit and miss so far this season and have conceded too many goals.

“The Bluebirds need to tighten things up at the back to ensure they don’t get sucked into any trouble. I think they will suffer a defeat in this one but it could be a close one.”
