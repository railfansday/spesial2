My Green Condo Inc. Expands "Association as a Service Organization" Consortium, Inviting Management Firms and Vendors to Co-Lead Zero-Friction Blockchain Operations

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Powered by U.S. Patent No. 12,443,952, MGCOne's 16-portal marketplace eliminates client onboarding bottlenecks and preserves vendor relationships across management transitions.

HIGHTSTOWN, N.J., Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- My Green Condo Inc. (MGCOne) today announced the next phase of its industry-wide Founding Partner Consortium, inviting forward-thinking community management companies, accounting firms, attorneys, and maintenance vendors to co-develop and deploy the industry's first decentralized "Association as a Service Organization" standard.

Protected by U.S. Patent No. 12,443,952, the MGCOne marketplace model reverses a century-old operational bottleneck. Instead of management firms bearing the heavy expense of maintaining proprietary software stacks and migrating historical records every time they win a new HOA or Condo contract, service providers plug directly into a clean, immutable ledger owned by the association.

"Management companies should win on service excellence, not get bogged down in software administration and painful data migrations," said Joanne Gambino-Borrell, Director of Operations at My Green Condo Inc. "Through our patented 16-portal architecture, an association's rules, financial ledgers, and vendor histories remain intact on the blockchain. When a management firm is hired, it can begin work immediately, and vendors, auditors, and legal counsel keep uninterrupted workflows without chasing down missing records."

My Green Condo Inc. is a PropTech company delivering end-to-end community management solutions for Condo, HOA, and Co-op associations. Founded by former Wall Street bankers and seasoned association board members, the company is dedicated to modernizing property management.

The company's flagship platform, MGCOne, is the industry's first patented, association-centered operating system. Engineered to elevate community living and modernize governance, MGCOne empowers property managers, board members, and residents through advanced automation and seamless communication. The platform grants communities' absolute control over their data, financial ledgers, and operational continuity.

Leveraging advanced blockchain infrastructure and next-generation AI, MGCOne centralizes operations into an ultra-secure "System of Record." Inspired by the Amazon marketplace model, it delivers unprecedented transparency, robust security, and independence for residents, board members, and service providers alike.
