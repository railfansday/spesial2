Sources reveal trigger for Rayan Ait-Nouri's Man City exit

Barcelona may only move to sign Manchester City left-back Rayan Ait-Nouri if they can free up space in their squad in January, sources have told Football Insider.

The Spanish giants are trying to offload Alejandro Balde this winter, which would create room for Ait-Nouri to slot in on the left side of their defence.

Hansi Flick's side are clear admirers of the 25-year-old, but are likely to need Balde's exit to provide the trigger for them to make a move.

Sources say that it is currently an "open race" for Ait-Nouri's signature, with Juventus and Napoli also keen to take him to Italy.

The final decision is expected to hinge on the player himself and where he would like to move for the next stage of his career.

City splashed out a fee of around £36million to sign Ait-Nouri back in 2025, but he is yet to make a single Premier League appearance so far this season.

Barcelona's difficult financial situation has provided a hurdle for plenty of deals in recent years, and it is likely to affect their pursuit of Ait-Nouri as well.

Football Insider revealed on Monday that the Blaugrana would prefer to strike a loan deal for the Algerian in January, rather than a permanent move.

Barcelona are "not willing" to meet City's £30m asking price in the middle of the campaign, but could settle for a loan-to-buy deal.

City are likely to be prepared to do business with the La Liga outfit, rather than risk the possibility of Ait-Nouri moving across the city to join their bitter rivals Manchester United.

The Red Devils saw an approach for the defender knocked back in the summer, and have been heavily linked with another move for him of late.

City would far prefer to sell Ait-Nouri to a foreign club, meaning the interest from Barcelona and the Serie A clubs is a welcome development.

Football Insider revealed on Tuesday that City's recent interest in Chelsea right-back Malo Gusto is being driven by Enzo Maresca.

The boss worked closely with the Frenchman during his time at Stamford Bridge, and is a "big admirer" of what he brings to the table.

However, sources say there is "no way" that City will meet the Blues' lofty £75m valuation in January.

Chelsea are prepared to cash in on Gusto if the right offer came in, but City are not ready to break the bank for him in that manner.
