Elon Musk Thinks Jeremy Culhane Needs to “Try Harder” With His ‘SNL’ Impression

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The actor has portrayed the tech billionaire in the last two episodes of NBC's sketch comedy show.

Elon Musk isn’t laughing at Jeremy Culhane’s impression of him on Saturday Night Live.

During the cold open for the most recent episode of the NBC sketch comedy program, a CIA officer, played by host John Mulaney, gathers the nation’s most prominent tech billionaires to test their national security training. The sketch references the resignation of former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, who stepped down following revelations of an affair with an alleged Chinese spy.

Among the tech billionaires spoofed in the skit were Musk (Culhane), Mark Zuckerberg (Andrew Dismukes, Sam Altman (Ben Marshall), Peter Thiel (James Austin Johnson), Dario Amodei (Jane Wickline) and Huang (Bowen Yang). Following the episode, a user on X shared their personal scores of the SNL castmembers’ impressions, giving Culhane’s impersonation a 7/10.

However, Musk disagreed. The Tesla CEO replied to the tweet with his own score, writing, “I’d give them maybe 5/10 for me. They need to try harder.”

In another X post, Musk replied to SNL‘s post of the sketch with a Venn diagram with “HUMOR” written in one circle and “SNL” written in the other. “The good sketches” was written in the small overlap.

Saturday night wasn’t the first time Culhane had spoofed the SpaceX founder. During the Nov. 7 episode, hosted by Druski, Culhane played the mogul on “Weekend Update” with Colin Jost.

During the skit, which took place a few days after Democrats won big in the 2026 Midterms despite Musk’s major campaign investments, Jost said to the actor, “You’re in the news because you wasted half a billion dollars on the midterms… almost all the candidates you backed lost.”

In an attempt to change the subject, much like the billionaire has done in real-life interviews, Culhane’s Musk replied in an enthusiastic tone, “I’m in the news because my car did a flip… it was like a freaking Hot Wheel.”

Prior to Culhane joining SNL during season 51, Mike Myers previously portrayed Musk in 2025. The X owner also hosted the show in 2021.
