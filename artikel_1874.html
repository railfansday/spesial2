Callum Hudson-Odoi has 'no future' at Nottingham Forest under Oliver Glasner - Transfer expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Callum Hudson-Odoi could push to leave Nottingham Forest in the January transfer window amid concerns about his playing time.

The 26-year-old has yet to start a Premier League game so far this season, and has played just 75 minutes combined across all competitions since Oliver Glasner's arrival.

It has now become clear that the former Chelsea star is not part of the new manager's plans, with his 3-4-3 system meaning there is no place in the XI for out-and-out wingers.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Hudson-Odoi has no future under Glasner and will remain on the sidelines at Nottingham Forest.

That could force the forward to push for a move away if he wants to play more regularly, and could place a number of Premier League clubs on high alert as a result.

Hudson-Odoi remains highly regarded in the top flight, but after it has become clear he won't have a role to play under Glasner, Nottingham Forest could cash in.

Football Insider reported on 17 November that Hudson-Odoi's career has stagnated at Nottingham Forest, and there is a growing chance he will be allowed to move on.

Glasner's system has not helped his cause, with the lack of out-and-out wingers meaning there is no real place for him to stake a claim for a more regular role.

As a result, both Hudson-Odoi and Nottingham Forest could open the door to a January exit with a growing likelihood that he will look to move on.

"Honestly, it's pretty clear that Callum Hudson-Odoi has no future under Oliver Glasner," Brown told Football Insider.

"We know how Glasner's teams set up, we saw it at Crystal Palace as well, he likes to have attacking midfielders in those areas who can drift wide and play in the pockets.

"Hudson-Odoi is not that type of player, he's an out-and-out winger, and that's why he hasn't been playing. There's no space for him in the side.

"He's obviously going to be frustrated, any player would be when they're not playing, so from his point of view I'm sure he will be tempted to push for a move away in January.

"From Forest's point of view as well, he's a sellable asset, there are going to be club interested in the Premier League and in Europe because he is still held in high regard.

"So if they can get a good fee for him in the January window, it's something that would make sense for everybody involved."

As previously mentioned, there is no shortage of interest in Hudson-Odoi whether that's within the Premier League or across Europe, as he is held in high regard.

Despite never having reached the heights once expected of him at Chelsea, he has proven his worth in the Premier League in recent years, and could be set to land a new club.

Football Insider revealed on 24 November that Roma are plotting a move to sign Hudson-Odoi on loan in the January transfer window, with a potential option to buy.

The Italian outfit are aiming to add another winger to their squad in the mid-season window, and the Nottingham Forest man is high on their list of targets.

However, there are doubts about whether they would be willing to spend the fee Nottingham Forest will demand, with a loan move more likely at this stage.
