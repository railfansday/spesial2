Prime Video Unveils Trailer for Final Season of ‘Maxton Hall‘

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

School is back in session for one last time as Harriet Herbig-Matten and Damian Hardung‘s Ruby and James fight for justice and love in the conclusion of the hit series.

School is back in session for one last time.

Prime Video has unveiled the trailer for the third and final season of Maxton Hall, starring Harriet Herbig-Matten and Damian Hardung.

The series‘ third season, produced by UFA Fiction, is based on Save Us, the third installment of the bestselling trilogy by author Mona Kasten, published by LYX.

The final season will follow the cliffhanger of season two, which left Herbig-Matten‘s Ruby learning she has been suspended from Maxton Hall College after being accused of having an affair with a professor. The synopsis for the third season reads: “Her dream of going to Oxford is slipping away, but she refuses to give up. Together with James, they fight to prove her innocence. The closer they get to the truth, the more it threatens to tear everything apart. Friendships are tested and secrets they never expected come to light. In the end, they must decide – is love enough?“

In addition to Hardung and Herbig-Matten, Sonja Weißer, Ben Felipe, Fedja van Huêt, Runa Greiner, Justus Riesner, Andrea Guo, Frederic Balonier and Eli Riccardi also star in the series.

Martin Schreier returns to direct with Markus Brunnemann, Eike Adler, and Ceylan Yildirim, who is also the head writer of the series, executive producing. Sandra Stöckmann serves as head writer alongside, Yildirum, with additional writers Catharina Junk, Marlene Melchior and Aylin Kockler. Valentin Debler returns as producer. The series is supported by the German Motion Picture Fund (GMPF).

Following its release last year, Maxton Hall became Amazon’s biggest-ever international launch and the most watched international show of all time in its first week. The show‘s second season hit the number one spot in 42 key markets including the U.S., U.K., Germany, France, Australia, Italy, Spain and Canada.

Amid the show’s success, Kasten’s German-language book trilogy has been translated in English and published in America for the first time.

“Watching Damian and Harriet bring my book characters to life has been truly special. They perfectly captured Ruby’s determination and eagerness, as well as the many layers of James’ personality,” Kasten told The Hollywood Reporter.

The first two episodes of the six-part season will be available on Prime Video starting Dec. 9, followed by a new episode each week until the finale on Dec. 23.
