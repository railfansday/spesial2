Netflix to Adapt ‘Grendel’ Comic Books Into Adult Animated TV Series

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

'Widow’s Bay' producer Bobak Esfarjani will showrun, write and executive produce the series based on Matt Wagner’s dark vigilante creation.

The long-running comic book series Grendel is getting a TV series adaptation from Netflix.

The streamer announced the upcoming adult animation series on Wednesday as it revealed Widow’s Bay producer Bobak Esfarjani will showrun, write and executive produce.

Grendel, created by veteran comics artist and writer Matt Wagner, follows the author Hunter Rose, whose world is turned upside down after the murder of the woman he loves. But as he becomes the revenge-obsessed vigilante Grendel, he faces the choice of whether to return to a more normal life or become something closer to a villain than a hero. While Comico debuted the series in 1982, Dark Horse Comics has recently published the title. “Grendel has been at the forefront of independent comics for over forty years, and we’re thrilled to bring Matt Wagner’s creation to Netflix,” Netflix’s vp of animation series and kids and family John Derderian said in a statement. “Like the comic, this animated series will break the traditional superhero mold just as Matt’s time-honored comic book has always done. Bobak, Dark Horse Entertainment and Bobbypills are bold storytellers who love this world as much as its fans do, and we’re so excited to partner with them to bring this story to screen.”

Netflix’s adaptation of the series will be executive produced by Chris Tongue, Tim Wiesch and Jay Komas for Dark Horse Entertainment as well as Lydia Antonini and Wagner. Bobbypills will serve as the studio.

“It’s incredibly exciting to see my creation finally make this leap to the animated screen,“ Wagner added in a statement. “Netflix’s dynamic new series retains the source material’s dark adult themes and also brings a fresh and thrilling approach to the narrative. This is Grendel as I’ve always wanted to see the character portrayed — a hyper-kinetic level of action, bracing explosions of violence and a poignant engaging storyline.”

When it is released, Grendel will join Netflix’s library of adult animated shows including BoJack Horseman, Big Mouth, Love, Death & Robots and Mating Season.
