How ‘Twilight’ Predicted Robert Pattinson’s James Bond Casting

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

In 2008, Pattinson debuted his skin of a killer. Nearly two decades later, he's gotten his license to kill.

Robert Pattinson really does have “the skin of a killer“… but in a new form.

For Twilight diehards, Monday’s announcement of Pattinson’s casting as the new James Bond might not have come as a surprise. That’s because the 2008 vampire romance film predicted it.

In the film, which launched Pattinson’s acting career, he plays the brooding Edward Cullen, a 108-year-old vampire who falls in love with human Bella Swan (Kristen Stewart). About halfway through Twilight, the first installment in the beloved film saga, Swan’s high school friend group is seen discussing the theme for the upcoming prom, which is revealed to be Monte Carlo. “Gambling, tuxedos, and Bond, James Bond,” Eric (Justin Chon) quips in the scene. A shocked Angela (Christian Serratos) then says, “Oh my God,” as the camera cuts to a smirking Edward, opening the car door for Bella as they make their couple debut walking into school together.

In the 2008 film, Pattinson‘s Edward may have warned Stewart‘s Bella that he has “the skin of a killer“ but less than two decades later, the actor is now getting his license to kill. The Primetime actor is taking over the iconic role from Daniel Craig, who concluded his run with 2021’s No Time to Die.

The Hollywood Reporter previously reported that Pattinson was among the finalists being considered for the role, in addition to Callum Turner, Jack Lowden, Josh O’Connor and Nicholas Hoult. But director Denis Villeneuve and producers Amy Pascal and David Heyman ultimately thought Pattinson was perfect as the next 007.

“James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honor of a lifetime,” Pattinson said in a statement about his casting. “I’m deeply grateful to Denis, Amy, David, Tanya and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me. To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.”

Following Monday’s news, the official X account for Twilight also posted the scene referencing James Bond and added in the caption, “It all comes back to Twilight.”
