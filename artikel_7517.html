Four Convicted for Defrauding Federal Program Intended for Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Businesses in Connection with Construction Contract for Cancer Treatment Center at Bay Pines VA Medical Center

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Tampa, Florida – A federal jury found Matthew West (55, Lantana) and Kevin Kutina (64, Ramona, CA) guilty of conspiracy to commit wire fraud, wire fraud, and major fraud, for the conspirators’ roles in fraudulently obtaining a multi-million-dollar construction contract. Each faces a maximum penalty of 50 years in federal prison. Sentencing hearings are scheduled for February 4, 2027. West and Kutina conspired with Robert Gonzalez (61, Palmetto Bay) and William Gonzalez (54, Orlando). Robert Gonzalez and William Gonzalez previously pleaded guilty to conspiracy to commit wire fraud. Each faces a maximum term of 20 years’ imprisonment. U.S. Attorney Gregory W. Kehoe made the announcement.
