PSG’s Champions League ‘obsession’ cost them league title under Tuchel, says Florenzi

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Former Paris Saint-Germain defender Alessandro Florenzi believes their "obsession" with winning the Champions League cost them the Ligue 1 title under Thomas Tuchel.

Florenzi spent the 2020-21 season on loan at PSG, who lost in the Champions League final to Bayern Munich in the previous campaign.

Tuchel won 74.8% of his games in charge of PSG (G127 W95 D13 L19), his best win percentage at any club, and led them to back-to-back titles in 2018-19 and 2019-20, but things unravelled the following season.

PSG sacked Tuchel in December 2020 after they were falling behind in the Ligue 1 title race to Lille, who would go on to win the league by one point.

It was only the second season since 2012-13 in which PSG failed to win the league title, after Monaco won the French top-flight in 2016-17.

However, Tuchel was hired by Chelsea a month after being sacked by PSG and went on to win the Champions League with the Blues in that same season.

Florenzi described Tuchel's Champions League win as "karma", but the Italian was not surprised to see PSG eventually win the competition.

"At the time, PSG hadn't won the Champions League yet, but you could see the president wanted to build something big," Florenzi told L'Equipe.

"Their obsession was the Champions League. And that's undoubtedly what cost us the league title; that season, we only thought about the Champions League.

"We lost in the semi-final against Manchester City, and Tuchel won it in the final. That's karma! But I have great memories of Paris, and I won my first trophies there.

"I told myself that PSG would end up winning the Champions League, yes, but something had to happen. And that something is Luis Enrique.

"With him, it's no longer the same club."
