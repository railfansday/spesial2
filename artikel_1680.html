Prediction: Celta Vigo vs Bournemouth

Turning their attention back to their iconic Europa League adventure on Thursday evening, Bournemouth will travel to Spain when they meet Celta Vigo.

Starting with the hosts, marking their return from November’s international break with a collapse away at Spanish icons Real Madrid on Saturday night as they eventually fell to a 2-1 defeat, Celta Vigo have endured a real struggle since opening up the 2026/27 campaign. Remarkably holding just a two-point buffer over slipping into the La Liga drop zone, Claudio Giraldez has serious pressure mounting on his shoulders. Likewise, last seen in Europa League action getting hit with another 2-1 loss at home against Belgium outfit Royale Union, the Sky Blues have mustered just a single win from any of their previous six appearances across all competitions. Facing what should be another stern examination at Balaidos in midweek, it should also be highlighted that Celta Vigo have notched just a sole strike in Galicia in almost 300 minutes of action.

Yet to feature over the past couple of months and still sidelined with a knee injury, Celta Vigo will once again be without Spanish midfielder Miguel Roman. Likewise, veteran striker Borja Iglesias is recovering from a calf issue and won’t play any part on Thursday evening.

Bagging what turned out to be a consolation La Liga strike away at Spanish heavyweights Real Madrid over the weekend, academy graduate Pablo Duran will lead the line. One-time Chelsea star Marcos Alonso will start at the heart of Celta Vigo’s defence.

As for the visitors, while Bournemouth might have endured a stuttering start to proceedings when opening up the 2026/27 campaign back in August, the Cherries have come roaring back in a record-breaking fashion. Embarking on an astonishing run of form, Thursday’s guests are clearly relishing life under Marco Rose’s guidance. With the mood around the Vitality at a red-hot high, Bournemouth will be relishing another trip to Spain in midweek. Marking their return from November’s fortnight interval with a 2-0 win at home against Nottingham Forest over the weekend, Rose’s high-flying squad have won each of their previous nine consecutive appearances across all competitions. Likewise, continuing to shine on their maiden European adventure this season, the Cherries have won all of their first four group stage battles by an aggregate score of 11-4 and they are just one of three clubs who can boast a 100% start.

Yet to make a maiden appearance of the season and still sidelined with a long-term thigh injury, former LA Galaxy defender Julian Araujo will once again miss out here. Making a cameo appearance off the bench over the weekend, England international Alex Scott will get some more minutes under his belt.

Once again finding the net against Nottingham Forest on Saturday and now getting on the scoresheet in all of his previous five consecutive appearances across all competitions, the red-hot Evanilson will spearhead Bournemouth’s attack. Italian shot-stopper Michele Di Gregorio should return between the sticks in Galicia.

Winning all of their opening four Europa League adventures by an aggregate score of 11-4, Bournemouth are in the midst of what has become an iconic purple patch. Shattering a string of records this season, the Cherries will be relishing another famous trip to Spain on Thursday evening. Now remarkable winning all of their last nine straight matchups across all competitions, Bournemouth should be smelling another potential opportunity here. Watching Celta Vigo lose three of their first four group stage showdowns, Rose’s high-flying squad will be confident of laying down another public marker on foreign soil.
