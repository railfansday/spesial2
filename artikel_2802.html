Gisele moved by first Brazil women’s call-up: “I didn’t expect to be here”

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The call-up to Brazil's senior national team marks a new chapter in Gisele's career. After representing Brazil at the U-20 World Cup this year, the forward received her first opportunity to join the senior squad. The news came unexpectedly and was met with emotion and the feeling of living a dream.

"I wasn't expecting it, because it's the last FIFA international window of the year. It was a very special moment for me, and my heart is overflowing with joy. I believe it was the result of a lot of hard work, and I want to make the most of this opportunity, contribute as best I can and learn as well," she said.

The squad announcement was one of those moments that stay with you forever. Gisele was watching at home after a day of training, never imagining her name would be among those selected.

Gisele during a women's national team training session in Hiroshima, JapanCredits: Luciana Vermell/CBF

"I got home from training, sat down on the sofa and watched the squad announcement. I was the last one called, and I was so happy. I cried so much. I really wasn't expecting it. To me, it was something that still felt a long way off. The Gisele who played for the U-20 national team never expected to be here, but it's been a dream come true."

In October, Gisele was at Beira-Rio in Porto Alegre, Rio Grande do Sul, to watch Brazil beat Argentina in a friendly. It was an opportunity to watch coach Arthur Elias's team in person and get even closer to a setting she had always dreamed of being part of.

The forward said she had greeted the coach on that occasion, but that was the extent of their contact. Upon arriving in Hiroshima, Japan, for her first FIFA international window with the senior women's national team, she thanked Arthur for the opportunity.

Gisele greets Arthur Elias upon her arrival in HiroshimaCredits: Luciana Vermell/CBF

Gisele also recognizes the importance of the players who were part of her journey through Brazil's youth national teams and contributed to her development. Now, as she joins the senior team, she brings with her the lessons she learned and gratitude for those who were part of that journey.

"I learned a lot from them, and if I'm here today, it's because they helped me in some way. I have so much affection and gratitude for the girls I met during my time with the youth national teams," she concluded.
