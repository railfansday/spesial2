Titans vs. Jaguars odds: Opening lines for Week 10 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Unfortunately, the Jacksonville Jaguars are really good this year. Unfortunately, part two: the Tennessee Titans play them twice in the next three weeks. The Titans opened as a 9.5-point home underdog in the Week 10 matchup. I'm probably crazy, but there is a world where the Titans can cover that number.

Cam Ward had the best game of his career in Week 8 against the Cincinnati Bengals. The Jaguars pass defense is pretty comparable to the Bengals'. If Ward and Brian Daboll actually found something that works against Cincinnati, we could see another solid performance here.

The Titans' defense is going to give up points. They were doing that before they traded Jeffery Simmons. That means the offense is going to have to put up 24+ per game to keep them in games for rest of the season. Can they do that in back-to-back games?

The Sparkle Kitties have the worst fans in the NFL. While most weeks I am cheering for the Titans to lose in an exciting game, I will be cheering for nothing but a win against the Jags.
