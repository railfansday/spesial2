Colts CB Justin Walley named the AFC defensive player of the week

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Colts ended a five-game losing streak with last Sunday's win over the Dolphins and a defensive touchdown played a big role in the 24-22 win.

Cornerback Justin Walley got home on a blitz deep inside Miami territory and separated Dolphins quarterback Kyle McCord from the ball. Defensive tackle Adetomiwa Adebawore recovered the ball for a touchdown.

Walley had five tackles, two tackles for loss and two passes defensed overall in the win. The third-round pick has 40 tackles, two sacks and three forced fumbles to this point in his rookie season.

The NFL named Walley the AFC defensive player of the week on Wednesday. It's the first time a Colt has won the award since early in the 2024 season.
