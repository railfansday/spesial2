NASCAR Star Tries Bull Riding, Immediately Regrets It

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

NASCAR driver Noah Gragson thought trying bull riding would be an exciting change of pace. Instead, his trip to Davis Rodeo Ranch in North Carolina left him sore for an entire week. The Front Row Motorsports driver climbed aboard two bulls and quickly discovered that handling a race car and staying on a bucking bull require very different skills.

Speaking on This Cowboy Life podcast, Gragson described how the soreness began shortly after the experience. "I was pretty good there for a couple hours, and then I got to Olive Garden. We went to Olive Garden afterward in Salisbury, and my neck started getting kind of sore," he said.

Gragson said the discomfort worsened the following day. He struggled to raise his arm and described his shoulder as "killing me for the last week." The soreness also spread through his abdomen and groin, involving what he called "muscles I've never used before."

His introduction to bull riding involved two attempts. The first bull offered little resistance, but the second was considerably tougher. Gragson lasted approximately two seconds before getting thrown off. He also described being dashboarded during the experience. By the time he spoke on the podcast, about a week later, he said he was finally somewhat back to normal.

Gragson even found an unusual way to get some relief during a visit to the Cosmopolitan Casino. He described receiving a massage while leaning over a blackjack table, paying $2 per minute for treatment focused on his neck and back. He credited the massage with helping his traps and neck finally feel better.

Gragson finally recovered enough to move freely again after a week of pain. His experience on the ranch in North Carolina remains a painful memory for the NASCAR star: "That was the craziest experience I've ever had."
