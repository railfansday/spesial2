Turns out Raiders Kirk Cousins trade saved Vikings from themselves

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

While most have celebrated the trade that sent Kirk Cousins from Las Vegas to Minnesota, many have wondered how the Raiders didn't get anything more than a low third round pick out of the deal.

Granted, the Raiders were going to be turning to rookie QB Fernando Mendoza within a few weeks and everyone knew it. But the Vikings were desperate for a quarterback to help lead them and their dominant defense on a playoff run. And Kirk Cousins was by far the best option to do that.

The only thing resembling leverage the Vikings had was their next option which was Colts QB Daniel Jones. Yeah, about that...that is now former Colts QB Daniel Jones because he was just cut. And with Jones being out in Indy come reports that, had the Vikings not reached their deal with the Raiders, they were set to pivot and attempt a trade for Jones instead.

According to the report, the Colts turned down an offer from the Ravens for a mid-round pick swap. Which means any offer from the Vikings would have to exceed that one. And most likely Jones would not have been the answer to their issues.

They were saved from that mistake by because the Raiders settled for what was somewhat less than what they probably should have gotten.

After all, the Vikings had two third round picks -- their own, which figures to be at the tail end of the round, and the Eagles which figures to be near the top of the round. For that reason, many think the Raiders should have at least insisted on the high third rounder. Or perhaps made the pick conditional in that it could become a second round pick should the Vikings go to the Super Bowl.

It's all neither here nor there at this point. The Vikings got their guy, the Raiders got a third round pick -- albeit barely -- and Kirk Cousins got the ideal situation for him. Everyone is happy.
