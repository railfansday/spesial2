Buyer behavior has changed. Before booking a call or filling out a brief, crypto project teams now open ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Claude and ask which agency to hire. AI has become the first touchpoint in the vendor selection process — and the agencies it recommends are not random. The engines surface those who have invested in AI visibility and optimized for how these models index and cite content. We ran the same buyer-intent queries across all three platforms in October 2026 to see who actually appears — and where.

We submitted five main queries to ChatGPT, Perplexity (standard and Deep Research), and Claude with web search enabled in October 2026. Queries covered three buyer intents: best crypto marketing agency, best blockchain marketing agency, and best crypto marketing agency for token launch. Agency name, position, and source URLs were recorded for all responses.

Positions below reflect the best crypto marketing agency query. Coverage Score = total appearances across all three platforms and all three query types (max: 9). Agency entries are ordered by that score.

ICODA is the top result on both Claude and ChatGPT for “best crypto marketing agency” — the only agency to hold both platforms simultaneously on that query.

What the engines say: Claude: #1 for crypto marketing and token launch, citing AI search visibility as a “2026 differentiator” (source: marketerhire). ChatGPT: #1 for both crypto and blockchain queries, tagged “best for integrated growth.” Perplexity standard: absent from the crypto shortlist, but #1 for token launch.

Where it wins: Quick-answer queries on Claude and ChatGPT. Both engines explicitly name AI search visibility as a differentiator — the only agency in this audit cited for it.

Notable strength: Full-cycle coverage — SEO, PR, KOLs, paid, community — with stated focus on each type and stage of crypto project.

Watch-out: ICODA’s own content is cited by Perplexity Deep Research to rank a competitor (MarketAcross), an unflagged self-citation loop.

MarketAcross is the most consistently cited PR-specialist — present on Claude, ChatGPT, and Perplexity across crypto and blockchain queries.

What the engines say: ChatGPT: #3 for both crypto and blockchain queries. Claude: #6 for crypto — “best for media authority and thought leadership.” Perplexity: present in both standard and Deep Research, ranking #4 in the blockchain Deep Research final table (sources: coincodex, icoda).

Where it wins: Institutional PR positioning. All three engines describe it in near-identical terms: media relations, thought leadership, credibility-building.

Notable strength: Verified client work with Polygon and Splinterlands (source: coincodex).

Watch-out: Perplexity Deep Research cited ICODA’s own agency comparison page to position MarketAcross as an “institutional PR leader.” The citation reads as independent; the source is commercially motivated.

Every token launch query on Claude and Perplexity returns Lunar Strategy in the top three.

What the engines say: Claude: #2 for blockchain marketing (“best for token launches, DeFi and NFT”) and #3 for token launch. ChatGPT: #3 for token launch, citing its TGE Accelerator. Perplexity standard: #3 for token launch (sources: Lunar Strategy + one additional). Absent from all standard crypto marketing shortlists on Perplexity.

Where it wins: Token launch and pre-TGE context — a rare cross-engine agreement on positioning.

Notable strength: Dedicated TGE Accelerator product; described as combining marketing with tokenomics consulting, differentiating it from PR-heavy agencies (source: marketerhire).

Watch-out: Query-type dependent visibility. Lunar Strategy does not surface on Perplexity’s standard crypto marketing shortlist.

NinjaPromo appears consistently on Claude and ChatGPT but is entirely absent from Perplexity across all query types tested.

What the engines say: Claude: #3 for both crypto and blockchain marketing, positioned for “performance and execution speed” (source: cbinsights). ChatGPT: #4 for crypto — “a broad digital marketing partner combining creative, social, paid campaigns, and branding.” Zero Perplexity appearances.

Where it wins: Claude positions NinjaPromo most strongly. GameFi and fintech are cited as strong-fit verticals by both engines.

Notable strength: Positioned as a full-stack outsourced marketing department — suited to teams that need multi-channel output without building in-house.

Watch-out: Buyers using Perplexity as their primary AI research tool will not encounter NinjaPromo on any query tested.

theKOLLAB surfaces in KOL-heavy contexts — not in general crypto marketing queries on all platforms.

What the engines say: Perplexity: #2 on the crypto shortlist — ahead of MarketAcross — citing Trust Wallet, Aptos Labs, and Tron as clients and 50M+ reported reach (source: coinranking). Claude: #7 for crypto. Absent from ChatGPT on all queries tested.

Where it wins: Perplexity’s crypto shortlist specifically, for KOL-driven token launches.

Notable strength: 250+ vetted crypto creators including Altcoin Daily and Crypto Lark. Claude cites a 1,230% token price increase for Orange Crypto at launch (source: dailycoin).

Watch-out: That 1,230% figure traces to dailycoin, not independent verification. All performance metrics are self-reported.

The agencies in this audit appear in AI search results because they have working AI visibility methodology — not by accident. If AI search visibility is a priority for your project, the practical starting point is agencies that already demonstrate it for themselves: they have the expertise and a proven process.

One caveat applies across the board: AI engines can surface inaccuracies, outdated information, and self-cited sources as independent ones. Before shortlisting any agency from an AI-generated result, verify their cases and results manually.

No single agency dominates all three platforms in this audit. In the crypto marketing agency 2026 results, ICODA leads standard results on Claude and ChatGPT — the two engines that drive the majority of quick buyer research. Perplexity runs its own shortlist. The same agency can rank #1 on two platforms and be absent from the third. AI search visibility is a real asset — and an engine-specific one.
