Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Medicare Part A and B and took a first time try with Aetna Advantage opt. In under PSHB federal. It’s been fantastic. No deductible or copays ( unlike Advatage plans outside FEHB ) which I had concerns about hidden costs. Another concern was who controlled the method of testing and treatments which is done under Medicare guidelines with your doctor , another concern did the plan meet part D requirements, it did and is also enhanced which gives a little more coverage ( not exactly sure what that means) also the Medicare out of network providers are handled 1 of 3 ways as I understood in my call with a Aetna rep who specializes in the plan, you can pay out of pocket and get reimbursed, see if the provider will bill Aetna , or call Aetna and they will see if they can work it with the provider, they also say it rarely happens that the insured has to get reimbursed. Also they can’t bill for more than original Medicare part B . Eye exams are free even without a medical condition and tomorrow I go for a hearing aide that gets reimbursed up to 2500 every 30 months. I’m giving it another try this year but of course this may not be for everyone.
