USMNT: Pep Guardiola’s Verdict on Donald Trump Looks Interesting Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Pep Guardiola looks to be the dream replacement for Mauricio Pochettino as the manager of the United States of America's national team.

Pochettino left for Manchester United after Tuesday night's victory over Haiti, and the USMNT is now searching for his successor. The Argentine has left big shoes to fill after an impressive showing at the 2026 World Cup, where the host nation reached the last 16.

However, the US have big dreams and are set for talks with one of the most successful managers in football history. Guardiola ended his 10-year stay at Manchester City in the summer and is now available.

Donald Trump, the President of the United States, came under strong criticism in early 2026 for his immigration policies. More specifically, the actions of Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) agents caused outrage.

ICE agents fatally shot 37-year-old Renee Good and intensive care nurse Alex Pretti in January. Those incidents received global recognition, with then-Man City boss Guardiola even giving his thoughts publicly.

The Catalan-born coach was visibly emotional as he passionately spoke about the ongoing situation in America. He questioned how any administration could be comfortable in such circumstances.

As quoted by Goal, he said: Look what happened in the United States of America: Renee Good and [intensive care nurse] Alex Pretti have been killed.

"Imagine the NHS, five, six people around him, go on the grass and 10 shots. Tell me how you can defend that?"

He had often stayed out of political issues until that point, when he felt silence was no longer an option. Guardiola, who has also spoken out about the conflict in Gaza, added:

"Today we can see it; before we could not see it. It hurts me

"If it was the opposite side, it would hurt me. I'm sorry, I will stand up, always I will be there. Always. Completely kill thousands of innocent people? It hurts me. It's no more complicated than that."

It could be interesting to see whether Guardiola continues to speak about political matters in the US if he becomes the new national team boss.

Despite being targeted for the position and tipped as an ideal candidate by USA icon Alexi Lalas, Guardiola isn't the front-runner when it comes to the bookmakers.

He's currently the third favourite, while the likes of Thierry Henry, Jesse Marsch and Antonio Conte are seen as outside shots. Leading the way is BJ Callaghan, the current Nashville SC coach. Steve Cherundolo of the USMNT's Under-23s is also ahead of Pep as things stand.

Guardiola might be a long shot for the US, but he previously spent a sabbatical year in the country and spoke positively about the future of the nation. When Pochettino took over the role in 2024, the ex-Man City and Barcelona manager said (per Sports Illustrated):

"The USA is fascinating to try to build a project for many years and being able to get the national team to the next step. The potential with the USA is there for the amount of people and sports and so on. If he has decided, it’s because it’s the best for him."
