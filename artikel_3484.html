Thomas Markle Sr. Indicted on 2 Counts of Possession of Child Pornography

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Thomas Markle Sr. has been indicted by a Los Angeles federal grand jury on two counts of possession of child pornography.

According to Bill Essayli, First Assistant U.S. Attorney for the Central District of California, Markle, 82, is in federal custody in Florida and faces a statutory maximum of 20 years in federal prison on each count if convicted.

“According to the indictment, Markle knowingly possessed 35 mm film negatives of sexually explicit material involving children,” Essayli, 41, alleged via X on Tuesday, November 24, with a copy of the report. “Federal law enforcement was notified last month that Markle possessed child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in the Philippines and at two storage units in Los Angeles. A judicially approved search warrant was executed, and the materials were seized.”

Markle is expected to be arraigned in a Los Angeles courtroom in the coming weeks. (Us has reached out to Markle’s attorney for comment.)

Essayli stated the case remains an ongoing investigation and anyone with knowledge of those who receive, possess, distribute or produce child sexual abuse material should report them to the FBI at (800) CALL-FBI.

Earlier this month, Markle was arrested for possession of child pornography and taken into FBI custody near Tampa, Florida.

“Mr. Markle is accused of possessing child sexual abuse material, including photographs depicting himself with minor girls,” Essayli said in a separate press release on November 6. “These allegations are extremely serious, and we will prosecute Mr. Markle to the fullest extent of federal law.”

Thomas Sr.’s arrest comes after his son, Thomas Markle Jr., told The Sun on October 22 that he had discovered child sexual abuse material in boxes at his father’s home in the Philippines and alerted authorities. Thomas Sr., however, denied his son’s claims.

“I’m shocked, I’m hurt, and I’m very upset that my son is making these accusations against me,” Thomas Sr. told the Daily Mail on October 23. “I could never do what I’m being accused of. It’s the worst thing anyone could accuse me of. This is bulls***. It’s just a bunch of lies. It’s sick. I have no idea why Tom is trying to destroy me with these lies.”

On November 19, an independent competency evaluation by a psychiatrist reportedly determined that Thomas Sr. has “sufficient ability to understand the nature and consequences of the charges against him” with a reasonable degree of medical certainty.

Meghan Markle, who has been estranged from her father since her 2018 wedding to Prince Harry, previously spoke out about allegations that Thomas Sr. was found to have been in possession of material depicting child sex abuse.

“I am deeply distressed at the news that has come to light in the last 24 hours, and my thoughts are with any children who may have been harmed,” she said in a statement to Us on October 23. “I hope it can now be understood that, despite certain media publications’ distortion over the years, any distance from my father and my choice to not allow him to have contact with our children, was for one reason: to protect them.”

If you or someone you know is experiencing child abuse, call or text Child Help Hotline at 1-800-422-4453.
