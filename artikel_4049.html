Housemarque Issues Saros PS5 Patch for Bug Fixes, Update Out Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Here's a quick one: Saros developer Housemarque has today updated the PS5 exclusive and issued version 02.000.003, available to download now.

It's a very simple set of patch notes that predominantly focuses on bug fixes, like issues in the Overlord Rush mode and problems affecting Trophy unlocks. Perhaps the most interesting note in relation to changing when the Saros: Zenith splash screen appears.

"Instead of appearing the first time the player enters Passage, it will now appear after returning to Passage after making a certain amount of progress through Shattered Rise." Exciting!
