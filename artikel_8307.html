I am using the same method, we had only HSAs at Fidelity, but when I started looking for ways to transfer funds discovered that Fidelity allowed this, so we have a CMA account there now. Since we now keep a little cash there, the CMA account also gives us a second source for free cash withdrawal, should we ever need it. All the other places that we have accounts did not allow transfers to others unless their accounts were also at that institution.
I ended up with a Fidelity CMA for the same reason. I wanted a better HSA and an easier way to get more interest on free cash. Doing this allowed me to do both without a net increase in the number of financial institutions I worked with. So far, it has been great.
