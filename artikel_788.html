Parker still winless as Portsmouth beat West Ham

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Report and free match highlights from the Sky Bet Championship match between Portsmouth and West Ham United at Fratton Park on Tuesday night; Odin Bailey scores the only goal as Pompey beat Hammers to leave Scott Parker without a win.

Scott Parker's winless start to life as West Ham boss continued as Odin Bailey netted his first Portsmouth goal in a 1-0 victory at Fratton Park.

Parker has lost two and drawn two since replacing Nuno Espirito Santo in the Hammers dugout, with the east Londoners without victory in six and 11 points off the top of the table.

The Championship's top scorers West Ham failed to scored for the first time this season, despite hitting the woodwork twice, as Bailey's first-half strike was enough for Portsmouth to rise to 10th.

Academy graduate goalkeeper Finlay Herrick was drafted into the Hammers starting XI at the weekend in place of Mads Hermansen, but the 20-year-old had a first half to forget after keeping his spot in the team.

He got away with flapping at Bailey's fourth-minute corner as Ebou Adams scooped the ball over the bar from close range.

But Herrick was punished for a weak punch at Tyrese Shade's dangerous cross from the right in the 31st minute and his poor connection went straight to Bailey, who thumped his finish under the keeper.

Bailey's first goal since arriving from Stockport was a bad omen for the Hammers, who had failed to win on the previous nine matches they had conceded first this season.

There had been a general air of nervousness about the visitors - without a win since October 17 - but they still created strong openings.

Jarrod Bowen showed pace on the right before his shot was pushed on to a post by Nicolas Schmid in the early stages.

Portsmouth goalkeeper Schmid has come under scrutiny of his own and did well to deny Joel Piroe twice but also had his own punching gaffe.

The Austrian managed to punch Arne Engles' corner behind him, but Madiodio Dia spared his blushes on the line as Mohamadou Kante lurked for a tap-in.

The Hammers had a claim for a penalty at the start of the second half when Konstantinos Mavropanos flung himself to the ground under Adams' tackle, but Andrew Kitchen waved the shouts away.

Engels struck a free-kick into Schmid's midriff before the Belgian was set up with a golden headed chance by Edvin Austbo, but his effort flew over the arriving Tomas Soucek and wide.

The hosts still offered a threat and almost doubled their lead in extraordinary style when Abu Kamara volleyed a raking through-ball wide.

It seemed to add a desperation to West Ham's attacking as Austbo headed at Schmid before the keeper produced a showstopping save to push Soucek's rasping shot on to the crossbar, but they could find an equaliser.
