Giants injury report: Abdul Carter continues to sit out

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The New York Giants took the field on Thursday for their second of three practices ahead of a Week 12 game against the Indianapolis Colts at Lucas Oil Stadium in Indianapolis, Indiana, this Sunday at 1:00 p.m. ET.

Thursday's practice was closed to reporters given the Thanksgiving holiday, but went on as planned in East Rutherford.

Linebacker Abdul Carter sat out on Wednesday with ankle and calf issues, but neither was deemed serious by head coach John Harbaugh.

"Abdul, he's got a little thing. Day-to-day, he should be fine for the game," Harbaugh said.
