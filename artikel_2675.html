Devontez Walker wins AFC special teams player of the week

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Ravens didn’t beat the Panthers on Sunday, but they did have one individual do enough to win a player of the week award.

The NFL named returner Devontez Walker special teams player of the week.

Walker had an 86-yard kickoff return for a touchdown in the second quarter of the contest. He ended up returning seven kicks for 211 yards, becoming the second player to have at least 200 kickoff return yards in a game this season.

His touchdown return was just the second kick returned for a TD this season.

This is Walker’s first player of the week award.
