Bayern Munich predicted XI vs Lille: Who replaces Harry Kane?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Bayern Munich will be without Harry Kane for the next three months after England's leading goalscorer underwent successful surgery on a torn syndesmosis ligament in his left ankle, sustained during their Bundesliga draw against Cologne on 20 October. And with Ismael Saibari, Serge Gnabry and Michael Olise all sidelined, options in attack remain short for Vincent Kompany.

Olise also sustained an injury during their 1-1 draw against Cologne, but the Frenchman only suffered bruising and should return for their Bundesliga game against Hamburg on Saturday. However, Bayern must first navigate a difficult Champions League tie against French outfit Lille on Wednesday night.

Kicker suggest that Lennart Karl will lead the attack, supported by Konrad Laimer, Tom Bischof and Nathaniel Brown. Luis Diaz is expected to return after an injury lay-off, but only to the bench, while Jamal Musiala is also expected to feature on the bench.
