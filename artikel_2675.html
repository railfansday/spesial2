A.J. Brown heads to locker room with hamstring injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

It’s been another tough day in the disappointing first year as a Patriot for wide receiver A.J. Brown.

Brown limped to the locker room with a hamstring injury in the fourth quarter of today’s game with the Lions. Brown had two catches for 27 yards before exiting.

Officially the Patriots initially were calling Brown questionable to return before ruling him out. It’s hard to imagine that he ever would have been back in a game the Lions are winning 28-10 with 10 minutes left to play.

Injuries have been a big part of the story today in Germany, with Patriots quarterback Drake Maye getting knocked out of the game with an ankle injury when Aidan Hutchinson sacked him — and then Hutchinson getting knocked out of the game with a knee injury celebrating the sack.
