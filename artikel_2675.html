Mason Rudolph hits DK Metcalf for touchdown pass as Steelers take 14-0 lead over Broncos

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

More than anyone else, the two players the Steelers needed to step up today were Mason Rudolph and DK Metcalf. And they did just that in the first quarter against the Broncos.

Rudolph threw a well-placed pass toward Metcalf, who leapt into the air over Broncos cornerback Patrick Surtain, reeled it in and landed in the end zone for a 22-yard touchdown.

It's the first start of the season for Rudolph, who has replaced Aaron Rodgers as the Steelers' quarterback. And it's the first game for Metcalf since his ejection last week against the Eagles drew him a great deal of criticism — not just for his two personal foul penalties, but for the poor play he has exhibited all season.

There's still plenty of time left, but in the first quarter, the Steelers look like they're firing on all cylinders. They're beating the Broncos 14-0.
