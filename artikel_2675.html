Steph Curry hints at retirement as injuries continue taking their toll

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Stephen Curry is beginning to think more about how much longer he can continue playing in the NBA as injuries become a bigger part of the conversation surrounding his career.

The Golden State Warriors star has acknowledged that his latest contract could be his last, while explaining how the physical toll of playing at the highest level has changed his approach to the game.

Curry recently discussed his future in an interview with ESPN's Anthony Slater, explaining how deteriorated cartilage in his right knee has affected his ability to train and recover.

The knee problem contributed to a difficult stretch last season, when Curry missed 27 consecutive games. He explained that the cartilage deterioration led to a bone bruise, making even routine shooting workouts painful.

The Warriors have since adjusted his workload to help preserve his health. Curry has been limited to approximately 28 minutes per game, held out of back-to-back contests, and encouraged to reduce the intensity of his workouts.

Those changes have made him more aware of the physical realities of the later stages of his career. Curry is also currently dealing with an oblique abdominal tear that is expected to keep him out for several weeks.

The four-time NBA champion also addressed his future when discussing the two-year, $115.9 million contract extension he signed with Golden State last summer.

"I assumed that it would probably be one of, if not the last, contract I signed. I'm not afraid to say it. But maybe I'll surprise myself and want to keep going," Curry told ESPN.

The extension includes a player option for the 2028-29 season, which would potentially allow him to play his 20th NBA season with the Warriors. However, Curry made clear that he has not made a retirement decision and is not necessarily planning to walk away when the contract expires.

He also indicated that he would be comfortable taking on a smaller role if that is what it takes to continue competing for championships.

Curry remains focused on enjoying the time he has left in the NBA rather than looking too far ahead. His comments offer a reminder that even one of basketball's most accomplished players is beginning to consider what life beyond the game might look like, although retirement is not an immediate decision.
