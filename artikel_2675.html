What was going on with the heated Nick Sirianni-Will Shipley exchange during Eagles-Cowboys?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Last week, there was fiery exchange between coaches on the Eagles sideline, and on Thursday against the Cowboys, there was a heated moment caught on camera between head coach Nick Sirianni and RB Will Shipley. The coach and player were seen yelling back and forth after the Eagles failed to convert on fourth down after choosing to hand the ball to Saquon Barkley instead of calling a QB sneak.

Sirianni wasn't asked about the situation during his post-game press conference, but has likened similar moments to two guys being passionate about the game. These things do happen, but it's still notable considering suggestions that the head coaches job is on the line this season — there's been little to suggest that this is true from the organization's perspective, but the rumors won't subside as the team claws back from a 2-7 start to the year.

As for Shipley, after the game, he chalked it up to any other disagreement that families have on Thanksgiving, but said it will all just make them closer.

"I got so much love for Coach Nick and as the leader of this football team. Emotions were running high and there was no love lost…We got after it for a little bit, but it's just going to allow our relationship to continue to grow. With that being said, I love him to death."

Still, Shipley wouldn't get into exactly what the conversation was about. Regardless, the RB ended up getting a majority of the carries the rest of the game as Saquon Barkley was sidelined with a stinger, and there didn't seem to be any lingering negativity to worry about.
