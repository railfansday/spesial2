Packers list Micah Parsons, Josh Jacobs as questionable for Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Micah Parsons has been cleared to make his return from last December’s torn ACL, but the Packers didn’t make anything certain about the defensive end’s status for Wednesday night’s game against the Rams on their final injury report.

Parsons is officially listed as questionable after another three days of limited practice participation. The Packers will have to make a roster move to activate Parsons and all signs point to that happening in time for him to face Los Angeles.

Running back Josh Jacobs (finger) was not expected to return this week, but he’s been able to practice the last few days and he also earned a questionable tag for the NFL’s first Thanksgiving Eve game.

Six other Packers are also listed as questionable. They are linebacker Zaire Franklin (shin), offensive lineman Travis Glover (shoulder), wide receiver Bo Melton (thigh), wide receiver Skyy Moore (ankle), center Sean Rhyan (ankle), and guard Kevin Zeitler (triceps).
