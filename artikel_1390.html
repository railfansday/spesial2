Man Utd Transfer News: European Club Make Move To Sign £100,000-A-Week Star

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Besiktas are eyeing up a move for Manchester United winger Amad Diallo as they look to strengthen their front line during the January transfer window.

Amad, 24, has endured a difficult spell at United, having failed to score in this calendar year. Indeed, the Ivory Coast international last found the net in a 4-4 draw against Bournemouth in December 2025, meaning he has gone 25 matches without a goal for the Reds.

Due to this, some United fans have called for Amad to be dropped and Shea Lacey to take his place in the XI, after impressing off the bench in recent games. Lacey made an instant impact off the bench in the 3-2 comeback win against Roma, and is pushing for a first Premier League start.

Mauricio Pochettino has a huge decision to make on Amad going into Sunday's trip to Liverpool, but, even if he does retain his place in the starting line-up, there is talk that he could be SOLD in January. According to Turkish outlet Sabah, Besiktas have made a move to sign Amad in January as they look to add another attacker to their squad.

It's believed that Besiktas are prioritising the Premier League market while monitoring potential signings from Belgium and Portugal, and Amad is right at the top of their shopping list.

Vincenzo Italiano's side could sell Milot Rashica to fund a move for Amad, with the ex-Norwich City man struggling for form and fitness this season. Meanwhile, Vaclav Cerny's long-term future is uncertain, having seen his game time reduced in recent matches.

Amad is thought to be valued at around £40m, a fee that would represent Besiktas' record signing. Orkun Kokcu, who left Benfica to join the Turkish side in a £26m deal in the summer, is their most-expensive ever recruit, with Leandro Trossard (£15m) coming in as second.

It's rare that Besiktas splash the cash, but they might need to if they want to continue competing alongside Galatasaray and Fenerbahce at the top of the Turkish top fight. Both sides have spent big in recent years and it has threatened to leave the likes of Besiktas behind.

Despite talk around his future, Amad's immediate focus will be helping new United head coach Pochettino as the Reds prepare for the daunting-looking trip to Liverpool on Sunday.

Pochettino's side aim to end an eight-match winless run in the Premier League when they face Andoni Iraola's Reds, who are flying high near the top of the table.
