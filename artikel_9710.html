How to get job listings from Greenhouse job boards with an API (Python, Node or no code)

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Thousands of companies, from startups to Airbnb, Discord and Robinhood, host their careers page on Greenhouse. If you track hiring at a list of companies, or run a job board or newsletter, checking each careers page by hand doesn't scale.

This tutorial uses Greenhouse Jobs Scraper, an Apify actor I built. It returns every open job from any company's Greenhouse board: title, company, locations, departments, posted date, the salary range when the company publishes one, the full description and the apply link. You can filter by title, location, department and date, and an only-new mode suits scheduled runs. The full examples are on GitHub: auto-actor/greenhouse-jobs-scraper.

You only need the board name: airbnb in job-boards.greenhouse.io/airbnb, or the full URL.

$0.0012 per job ($1.20 per 1,000), paid per result, with no monthly fee. Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month, which is enough to try it. For companies that hire on Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workday or Workable instead, there's a matching Career Site Jobs Scraper.
