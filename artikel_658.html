Bowen Yang Apologizes for Antisemitic Remark About Regina Spektor

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A resurfaced ‘Las Culturistas’ podcast had the comedian under fire for using a KKK-linked slur about Jewish people to describe the singer and artist.

Bowen Yang has issued an apology following backlash for using an antisemitic slur, reportedly coined by the KKK, to describe Jewish singer Regina Spektor in a resurfaced episode of the Las Culturistas podcast.

“I want to genuinely apologize for using a word on the podcast without knowing its antisemitic history, especially since we’ve repudiated antisemitism on the show as recently as the last two weeks,” Yang posted on Instagram on Friday.

“Particularly upsetting to realize that I adopted language popularized by far-right movements whose rhetoric we should be wary of and act against,” Yang added. In the offending podcast, the veteran comic called Spektor a “Zio“ (short for “Zionist“), a pejorative term said to have been first used by former KKK Grand Wizard David Duke and then as an antisemitic slur adopted by white supremacists. Yang used the pejorative term to describe Spektor, a Jewish singer and artist, in his Oct. 14 podcast alongside co-host Matt Rogers and guest Edy Monica. The popular podcast produced by Will Ferrell’s Big Money Players Podcast Network and iHeartRadio also streams on YouTube.

Meghan McCain was among those to speak out against Yang for his comment, writing on social media, “Bowen Yang using antisemitic slang that was popularized and mainstreamed by KKK leader David Duke is really just chefs kiss for the guy who got Shane Gillis fired from SNL for racism.“

For their Las Culturistas podcast, Yang and Rogers give their takes on pop culture moments with celebrity guests and recurring segments. The show launched in 2016 and has featured guests including Mariah Carey, Michelle Obama, Lady Gaga, Reese Witherspoon, Ariana Grande, Charli XCX, Chappell Roan and Nicole Kidman.

The podcast has become known for its “I Don’t Think So, Honey” segment in which the celebrities join Yang and Rogers in ranting about something in modern culture for one minute as well as the question asked to every guest: “What is the culture that made you say culture was for you?”
