Diddy's Daughter Love Wishes Him a Happy Birthday Amid His Prison Sentence

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Diddy's daughter Love is sharing a very special birthday message to her father as he serves his prison sentence ... asking him, "When are you going to come visit me?"

The 4-year-old's Instagram account posted her message ... with Love saying, "Hey, daddy! It's me, Love! I want to wish you a happy birthday!"

Love tells her father she misses him "so much" ... and she asks Diddy how old he is turning and what he ate today.

The rapper's daughter finished her message by singing a giggly version of "Happy Birthday" to the incarcerated music mogul ... and sharing a painting she made for her dad.

Love wasn't the only one of Diddy's kids to send their dad a message as he turned 57.

The rapper's twin daughters Jessie and D'Lila Combs wrote on their Instagram Stories ... "Happy birthday to the best dad ever!!! We love and miss you so much and we wish we could celebrate with you on your special day!"

We reported earlier this week ... Diddy's prison release date was moved forward by 3 weeks to February 6, 2028 ... so it looks like he'll spend at least on more birthday in the clink.
