Harlan Coben’s Most Famous Hero Is Officially Filming a New Netflix Series

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Harlan Coben fans have had several exciting new projects to watch this year, but the most popular of them all was I Will Find You. Starring Sam Worthington (Avatar: Fire and Ash) and Britt Lower (Severance), Netflix added all episodes of I Will Find You to streaming in June, and the show spent months as one of the most-watched titles on the platform worldwide. Fans have asked questions about a second season of the show following the success of Season 1, but Coben and creator Robert Hull have said they’re content to leave the story where it lies. The duo is reuniting soon for a new adaptation of another Coben novel, The Innocent, so fans looking for another watch akin to I Will Find You have something to be excited about.

However, before The Innocent hits streaming, fans have another Harlan Coben-inspired show to look forward to, one based on his bestselling book of all time. We’re talking, of course, about Myron Bolitar, which is now in production in New York under co-showrunners David E. Kelley and Kyle Long. The new series is led by KJ Apa, who can be seen in theaters starring in the new Jimmy Stewart biopic, with Collin Woodell and Diane Guerrero also recurring. No word yet on when Myron Bolitar will be released, and while a 2026 release date can safely be ruled out as the show is still filming, it’s likely it will premiere within the first few months of 2027. The show is about a former college star athlete who becomes an agent after leaving sports life behind only to get entangled in a world of murder, deceit, and blackmail.
