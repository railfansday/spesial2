Brad Pitt’s Gritty New Thriller Officially Releases On Digital This Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Brad Pitt's new survival thriller is finally finding its way home this week.

Throughout 2026, movie star Brad Pitt has continued his streak of high-octane thrillers and dramas. In recent years, Pitt has only had a few major leading film roles with varying critical and commercial success, such as with the star-studded Bullet Train in 2022, crime comedy Wolfs in 2024, and Best Picture-nominated sports drama F1 in 2025. Now, Pitt is set to close out this year by reprising his Oscar-winning titular role in Netflix's The Misadventures of Cliff Booth. But before the Once Upon a Time in Hollywood spinoff hits theaters this month, he'll be celebrating the digital release of his earlier critically acclaimed 2026 thriller.

Approximately six weeks after its theatrical launch on September 25, the David Ayer-directed and Brad Pitt-starring movie Heart of the Beast is set to drop on digital platforms. As listed on Prime Video and the Apple TV Store, Heart of the Beast will be released on PVOD on Tuesday, November 10 at 12 a.m. ET.

The new survival thriller film is now available to pre-order through digital vendors such as the Apple TV Store, Prime Video, Fandango at Home, and YouTube Movies & TV. Heart of the Beast is currently set at the premium new release purchase price of $24.99 for SD, HD, and UHD resolutions.

Written by Cameron Alexander and directed by Ayer, Heart of the Beast follows a retired Special Forces veteran (Pitt) who must undertake an arduous 58-mile trek to safety alongside his traumatized combat German Shepard after their plane crashes in the remote Alaskan wilderness. In addition to Pitt as James Belmont, the tight cast of Heart of the Beast includes J. K. Simmons as Watkins and Anna Lambe as Amber.
