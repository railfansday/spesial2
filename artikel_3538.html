Rep. Jimmy Gomez Resigns After Alleged 'Unwanted Sexual Contact' With Aide

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Democratic Rep. Jimmy Gomez is resigning from Congress after a House Ethics Committee report determined he allegedly made “unwanted sexual advances” and “sexual contact” with a congressional aide.

“I have strong disagreements with the committee’s report and its conclusions. Among other significant deficiencies and omissions, the report does not include key portions of my testimony that are essential to a full and fair understanding of what occurred,” Gomez, 51, said in a statement to CNN on Monday, November 23. “I also have serious concerns about the process. In my interview, I answered the Committee’s questions only to see my statements selectively quoted, disparaged and disregarded throughout the final report, to which I was given no opportunity to respond.”

The California politician added, “With this in mind, I see no other alternative than to resign, effective December 1, to allow for my staff to close out important constituent casework and needs.”

Us has reached out to Gomez’s team for comment regarding the report and his resignation.

The ethics panel made the Gomez report public on Monday. According to CNN, the report found that Gomez — who lost his bid for re-election this year after serving more than nine years in congress— “failed in his obligation to behave in a manner that reflects creditably on the House, and his conduct was exacerbated by his failure to appreciate the inherent power imbalance between House Members and House staff.”

In its report, the committee also called for “zero tolerance of unwanted sexual advances toward House staff.”

MS Now also reported that a woman, identified only as Witness 1 in the report, alleged to the committee that she had more than one physical encounter with Gomez and did not consider them to be consensual.

She described two incidents, which allegedly occurred in 2024, to the committee, claiming the incident was “nonconsensual whatsoever” and said she remembered “feeling very uncomfortable” and “mortified.”

Attorneys representing the woman praised the committee for its “thorough and sensitive” handling of the investigation.

In January 2023, Gomez — who represented California’s 34th Congressional District — went viral when he appeared in Washington, D.C., to cast his vote for House Speaker with his then 4-month-old son, Hodge.

“In the end, we have to normalize dads taking their kids with them, be it stay-at-home dads or working dads,” Gomez told Today.com at the time. “When I took him to the floor, I think people were surprised — but it wasn’t a big deal. I think it does send a powerful message that us guys need to do our part. We don’t risk our lives bringing children into the world — women do.”
