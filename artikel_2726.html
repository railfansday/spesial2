Kirk Cousins’ latest gesture toward Fernando Mendoza shows why he’s class act

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The NFL is a league where quarterback competition, roster decisions, and the pressure to win can sometimes overshadow the relationships players build along the way. That makes the connection between Kirk Cousins and Fernando Mendoza particularly noteworthy, especially after the two quarterbacks went their separate ways following a midseason trade.

Despite no longer being teammates, Cousins continues to show an interest in Mendoza's development, offering support as the rookie navigates the early stages of his NFL career.

Cousins reportedly called Mendoza following the rookie quarterback's first NFL start against the Seattle Seahawks on Sunday. The veteran wanted to speak with his former teammate, hear his thoughts, and discuss how the first starting experience went.

"Just wanted to debrief with him, get his thoughts," Cousins said, according to NBC Sports' ProFootballTalk. "I'm a big fan of his, and me and my boys will be following him for a long time. When I'm long done playing, we'll be cheering for Fernando and for his team. So just wanted to get his thoughts."

Cousins also reflected on his own first NFL start, recognizing how significant that experience can be for a young quarterback beginning his professional career.

"I remember my first career start, and that's one that kind of always [will] stick with you, but I think he did some great things and he's got a bright future ahead," Cousins said.

The gesture is particularly meaningful considering Cousins was traded to the Minnesota Vikings just days before Mendoza made his first start for Las Vegas. The two are no longer teammates, but Cousins clearly has no intention of abandoning the relationship they developed during their time together.

Mendoza also spoke positively about Cousins, acknowledging that the veteran remains someone he can lean on despite no longer being in the same quarterback room.

"He's awesome," Mendoza said. "He's been a great mentor of mine, and [even though] he's in a different building … I still lean on him, and still lean on all the great mentors that I have in this building with the coaching staff and other players and leaders on our team."

Cousins was brought to Las Vegas in part to help Mendoza develop before eventually taking over as the franchise's quarterback. Even after the trade, that mentorship appears to be continuing naturally.

The NFL can be a business, and Cousins is now focused on a new opportunity in Minnesota. However, taking the time to call a young quarterback after his first start speaks volumes about the kind of teammate and mentor he was in Las Vegas.

Cousins didn't have to make that call. He didn't have to check in or offer advice. The fact that he did is another reminder that his impact on Mendoza may extend well beyond the time they spent as teammates.
