The Rams have no choice but to win Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

If the Los Angeles Rams want to make the NFL postseason, they'd better find a way to beat the Green Bay Packers on Wednesday night.

Otherwise, we're likely barreling toward one of the biggest single-season flops in modern league history.

That's harsh, to be sure, but it's also the reality the Rams are currently facing. This was the heavy preseason favorite to win the Super Bowl, currently sitting on the outside looking in at the playoff picture as we enter Week 12. If they can't edge out a team in a similarly precarious position, and one with a devastating weakness, at that, reality will get even more grim.

This overall point is fleshed out by the math. There is massive playoff leverage on the line for whichever wins (and, subsequently, whichever one loses). According to RBSDM.com, Los Angeles currently has a 42% chance to make the playoffs. With a win on Thanksgiving Eve, the odds are bumped up to 56%. With a loss? They plummet to merely 20%.

Compared to The Athletic's playoff predictor, RBSDM's modeling is actually rosy for the Rams. That outlet gives L.A. just a 34% chance of reaching the postseason as things currently stand. It increases to 47% with a win and drops off to 16% with a loss.

The obvious storylines and narratives, and simply looking at the upcoming schedule, flesh out what the models are indicating.

Right now, the Packers are clinging to the No. 7 seed in the NFC playoffs. The Seattle Seahawks are one spot back of them, followed by the Rams and they're all 6-4 entering this week's action. The Packers and Seahawks don't play each other, so that potential tiebreaker is a little more complicated than simply head-to-head results, but Green Bay obviously is in control for the time being. Los Angeles gets at least one crack at both, however, with two meetings against Seattle. Blowing a chance to win the tiebreaker over the Packers would be devastating.

Per Tankathon, the Rams have the second-most challenging schedule left in the NFL, only behind that of the Chicago Bears. The Seahawks are fourth in that metric while the Packers are seventh. None of those home stretches are particularly easy, but it's an especially difficult road for the Rams. Throw in a lost tiebreaker and it gets scary quickly.

It isn't impossible for the Rams to make the playoffs with a loss on Wednesday night, but it becomes exceptionally difficult and it takes their fate out of their hands. Even if Los Angeles sweeps Seattle late in the season, a loss this week means it would need help to crack the top seven in the NFC.

This team has already had to battle through so much, and we're only 2/3 of the way through the season. It's only getting more difficult with each passing devastating injury.

From an objective viewpoint, that desperation is what makes this week's matchup such incredible theater, especially considering the Packers are essentially in the same boat. They're almost certainly toast for the postseason with a loss.

The Rams would love to make that their opponents' reality this week. For their own sake, they have to.
