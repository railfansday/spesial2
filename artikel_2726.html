Packers linebacker Ty’Ron Hopper questionable to return with chest injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

FOXBOROUGH, MA − The Green Bay Packers already are thin at linebacker, and that group took another hit early in the game with the New England Patriots.

Ty'Ron Hopper is listed as questionable to return with a chest injury. He briefly went into the blue medical tent.

Hopper as well as Isaiah McDuffie have stepped up at linebacker as Edgerrin Copper (Achilles) is out for the season and Zaire Franklin (shin) has missed the last three games.

Nick Niemann replaced Hopper during the second defensive series.
