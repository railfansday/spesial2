Carson Beck ruled out for Week 12, Josh Johnson to start

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Josh Johnson will once again start at quarterback for the Cardinals.

Head coach Mike LaFleur formally ruled out rookie Carson Beck for Sunday’s game against the Commanders, as Beck continues to deal with a back issue.

LaFleur noted in his Friday press conference that Beck may be able to return for Week 13 when the Cardinals host the Eagles.

Johnson started for Arizona in last week’s loss to the Chiefs, completing 18-of-31 passes for 155 yards with one touchdown.

The Cardinals have also ruled out offensive lineman Jon Gaines II.
