Wild headbutt, bodies flung down stairs in new video of Rams Packers fan brawl

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

On Thanksgiving eve, the Los Angeles Rams won their fifth straight game, defeating the Green Bay Packers emphatically, 26-13. In the win, the Rams imposed their will with physicality, rushing for well over 200 yards.

That adrenaline-pumping aggression was not only seen on the field, but also in the stands at SoFi Stadium.

A video surfaced of a wild brawl breaking out between Rams fans and someone wearing a red Tom Brady Buccaneers jersey, of all things.

Los Angeles Rams cornerback Emmanuel Forbes Jr. (1) intercepts a pass against the Green Bay Packers in Inglewood, Calif., Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026. AP Photo/Gregory Bull

Several punches were thrown, but the initial camera angle was unable to capture much of the craziness. However, another video has surfaced on social media showing the brawl, which featured headbutts and fans being flung down a flight of stairs.

The video is shot from above the stairwell, looking down on the action. A Rams fan wearing a #17 Davante Adams jersey and the guy in a Brady jersey are exchanging haymakers, while holding each other with their off-arms, akin to a fight in the NHL.

Loud screams and gasps can be heard around the fracas.

Rams and Packers fans fight in stairwell at SoFi Stadium on Wednesday, Nov. 15. TikTok/@@MLFootball

Rams and Packers fans fight in stairwell at SoFi Stadium on Thanksgiving eve. TikTok/@@MLFootball

Security guards are seen attempting to hold the combatants off each other, to no avail. As the two reach the top of the stairs, the Rams fan attempts a head butt, coming within inches of the other man's face.

That opened the door for the Brady fan to gain some leverage, shoving his dancing partner backward. Both men tumbled and began falling down the flight of stairs.

Meanwhile, a fan wearing a Packers Jordan Love jersey is seen throwing a punch at another Rams fan before he also gets pushed down the stairs.

Green Bay Packers wide receiver Christian Watson (9) is hit by Los Angeles Rams safety Kamren Kinchens (5) during an NFL football game in Inglewood, Calif. AP Photo/Gregory Bull

In a game that had tons of physicality, fans took it to another level. It is unknown at this point whether any arrests were made.

This fan brawl comes amidst videos surfacing across the internet in recent weeks of similar behavior across the league. The 49ers fans have found themselves most consistently in the middle of the growing controversy, but they are far from the only fan base participating in such behavior.
