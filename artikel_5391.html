MassMedia Earns Two Honors at 2026 PRSA Las Vegas Pinnacle Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Award-winning campaigns for Sam & Ash Injury Law and Nocturno Cocktail Bar demonstrate the agency's strategic approach to building brands, strengthening reputations and driving meaningful results

LAS VEGAS, Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- MassMedia, a strategic growth partner for ambitious brands ready to grow, scale and perform, earned two honors at the 2026 Public Relations Society of America (PRSA) Las Vegas Valley Chapter Pinnacle Awards, recognizing its work for Sam & Ash Injury Law and Nocturno Cocktail Bar.

Presented October 28 at Siena Golf Club in Las Vegas, the awards recognize excellence in public relations, communications strategy and execution across Southern Nevada. MassMedia received a Pinnacle Award in Community Relations for its Safe Teen Driving campaign on behalf of Sam & Ash Injury Law and an Award of Excellence in Reputation and Brand Management for its work with Nocturno Cocktail Bar.

"Great public relations starts with understanding the business, not simply the assignment," said Paula Yakubik, founder and CEO of MassMedia. "Whether we're helping a client build a stronger connection with its community or establish a reputation in a competitive market, our role is to look beyond the immediate brief, uncover opportunities and develop strategies that make a meaningful difference. These awards reflect the thinking, creativity and collaboration our team brings to our client partnerships every day."

For Sam & Ash Injury Law, MassMedia developed a community relations campaign that connected the firm's commitment to public safety with an issue affecting Nevada families: teen driving safety. The Safe Teen Driving campaign invited students ages 16 to 22 across Nevada to share what safe driving means to them through an essay competition and pledge initiative, with opportunities to receive scholarships and prizes. The program generated nearly 1,000 essays and safe-driving pledges, up from 400 in its inaugural year, and secured more than 50 earned media placements with a combined potential audience reach of 68.7 million. Beyond increasing awareness, the campaign created opportunities for meaningful engagement with young drivers and reinforced Sam & Ash Injury Law's commitment to the communities it serves.

For Nocturno Cocktail Bar, MassMedia developed a sustained public relations and reputation-building strategy designed to establish the emerging Las Vegas Arts District destination within a highly competitive national cocktail industry. The program combined local, national and industry media relations, executive positioning, journalist experiences, strategic collaborations and industry award opportunities. The campaign generated more than 180 earned media placements and a combined potential audience reach of 1.38 billion. Nocturno also earned a James Beard Award nomination and four major industry recognitions, helping elevate its visibility among leading cocktail destinations nationwide.

Together, the award-winning programs reflect MassMedia's approach to communications: understanding each client's business objectives, identifying opportunities to build meaningful connections and developing strategies that extend beyond publicity to support broader brand and business goals.

Founded in Las Vegas nearly 30 years ago, MassMedia has expanded its capabilities and geographic reach while maintaining its commitment to working as an invested extension of its clients' teams. Today, the agency brings together strategy, creative, media, public relations, social, events and performance expertise to help brands navigate challenges, uncover opportunities and grow.
