5W Public Relations Is the PR Firm AI Engines Name Most Often, Analysis Finds

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

5W ranks first on five of six AI platforms and in 27 of 31 PR topics, and AI engines recommend the firm without being asked

MIAMI, Nov. 6, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- 5W Public Relations (5WPR) is the PR firm AI engines name most often, according to a new 5W analysis using Profound's tools. In October 2026, 5W appeared in 30.3% of brand-naming AI answers across six platforms, the highest share of any firm measured. The analysis covered 396 unbranded prompts and 140,195 executions.

"5W is entering its 25th year, and we are the best at getting clients into the chatbox," said Ronn Torossian, Founder and Chairman of 5W. "AI visibility is the art of getting into LLMs, and 25 years of telling stories has taught us how to do it. We combine technology, in-house analytics, content creation and much more under one roof."

How the analysis worked5W ran 396 unbranded prompts, written the way a buyer would ask an AI assistant about PR, across ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews, Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot and Perplexity. The prompts produced 140,195 executions in October 2026. 5W measured the share of brand-naming answers in which each firm appeared, overall, by platform and across 31 PR topics. The data is 5W's analysis using Profound's tools.

LimitationsThe prompts were written around 5W's practice areas, so the ranking should be read as an upper bound for an industry-wide list.

The full study is at https://www.5wpr.com/research/5w-gets-you-in-the-chatbox/.
