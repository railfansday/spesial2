Daniel Farke 'seriously looking' at long-term Leeds tactical change - expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Leeds United manager Daniel Farke is desperately searching for a way to spark more life from his team away from home.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the Whites have little change from their style when playing away, compared to at Elland Road.

This comes after the news that Farke has won just four of his 24 away league games since Leeds' promotion back to the Premier League in 2025.

Wyness believe that the German must now prove his worth in West Yorkshire, and show that he is a top manager by finding a way to change his team's fortunes on the road.

The 50-year-old's record in away games has plagued him for years now, with his time in the Bundesliga also providing similar issues.

Leeds haven't won away from home in any competition since 25 August, and Wyness believes that it is now time for a drastic change of strategy from the boss.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that Farke needs to drop Leeds' slow build-up away from home, and try to employ a more fast-paced, counter-attacking style.

He pointed out that all of the Premier League's elite bosses pride themselves on their ability to find solutions to any issues that emerge throughout a campaign.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained how the responsibility now lies with Farke to make a change in the team's tactics.

"Well, when I looked at it, Farke's actually got a pretty bad history of away records with clubs he's been at before as well," he said.

"They're trying to replicate that style of play when they're playing at home, and their build-up initiative isn't quick enough, and I think they've got to start getting more counter-attacking away from home rather than just doing what they're doing right now.

"I think they need a result before the international break for sure, otherwise they'll start lingering at the wrong end of the table.

"He's got to be seriously looking at the tactics away from home and the style of play, and not trying to replicate what he does at home because it's a different style of football when you're away.

"He's an experienced manager now, Farke, and he's got to be able to change and make those moves. That's what makes the difference in good managers: those who can adapt and make that change."

Former Manchester United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider on Thursday that there is a "disconnect" between Farke and the Leeds board over winger Jean-Matteo Bahoya.

The wideman was signed on loan from Eintracht Frankfurt on deadline day, but Farke has hardly used him so far, despite the club having a purchase option for him.

Brown believes that Bahoya's lack of game time is a sign of issues behind the scenes at Elland Road, given that the club hierarchy clearly think he has potential.

Farke was recently asked about Bahoya during a press conference, and suggested that he didn't understand the "hype" surrounding the 21-year-old.
