HireQuest (HQI) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Pro forma sales grew, but a $1.3 million write-down clouded the quarter.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

HireQuest, Inc. (HQI +1.53%) reported growth in its core commercial staffing brands as total revenue rose 4% to $8.8 million. Management stated that the franchise model demonstrated resilience during a period of improving labor market demand, with pro forma system-wide sales increasing 11.4%. The company completed the divestiture of its MRINetwork assets earlier in the year, which impacted year-over-year comparisons but allowed for a focus on more profitable commercial segments. Management reported that momentum built throughout the quarter and accelerated into early November, with weekly system-wide sales up high teens year over year. The company remains focused on disciplined capital allocation, including dividends and share repurchases, supported by a credit facility with over $40 million in availability.
