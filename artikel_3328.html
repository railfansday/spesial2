Edgar Barrera Signs With Fortress Talent Management

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Grammy winner Edgar Barrera has signed with Fortress Talent Management for film and television representation.

Barrera’s signing comes after he recently made history as the most-awarded individual at the Latin Grammys, with a record 33 wins.

The songwriter and producer has helped craft global hits, collaborating with some of the industry’s biggest artists, including Bad Bunny, Karol G, Shakira, Peso Pluma, Grupo Frontera, and Maluma, among others.

Barrera is looking to transition into film and television to expand his creative footprint and bring a fresh perspective to screen composition, combining his deep understanding of Latin music with a versatile approach spanning pop, regional Mexican, urban, and beyond.

“We are thrilled to be working with Edgar as he crosses over into composing and songwriting for film andtelevision,” said Rich Jacobellis, Partner at Fortress Talent Management. “He’s developed incredible relationships with distinguished artists that will be invaluable to producers and directors.” Watch on Deadline

Fortress Talent Management’s roster includes Oscar-, Emmy-, and Grammy-winning and nominated composers such as Daniel Pemberton, Ariel Marx, David Fleming, Bryce Dessner, Gustavo Santaolalla, and Este Haim.

Barrera won his first Grammy in 2015 in the Best Tropical Album category for his work on Carlos Vives’ Más Corazón Profundo. He recently received a nomination at the 69th Annual Grammy Awards in the Songwriter of the Year, Non-Classical category for the fourth consecutive year. The songs he worked on include “Broke My Heart”, “Dando Vueltas”, “Después De Ti”, “For u My lova”, “Lighter”, “Ojitos Bellos”, “Pvta Luna”, “7-3” and “Velas.”
