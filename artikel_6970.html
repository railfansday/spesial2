Victoria Beckham’s Fiery “Chili-Pepper Nails” Are Winter’s Most Expensive-Looking Manicure

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Traditional red nails just got a hot upgrade for the colder months

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Victoria Beckham has many signature looks, but when it comes to her nails, she’s almost always sporting a neutral, minimalist pink manicure or a subtle French manicure; they’re practically a part of her uniform, and her fingertips are rarely painted with bright colors. However, in her latest Instagram post, Beckham’s signature manicure was nowhere to be seen. Instead, the fashion designer opted for elegant, chili-pepper red fingertips.

The fiery red hue marked a significant departure from her trademark look, but it still read equally sophisticated. As Beckham showed, red nails are classic for a reason: confident, polished, and endlessly versatile. Even in fashion, red has the power to elevate an entire outfit. While bright red isn’t everyone’s cup of tea (Posh Spice possibly included), there are a variety of red hues that are more subtle but still carry the power.

It’s a sentiment echoed by nail artist Jessica White, who previously told Bazaar, “A clean red manicure is timeless because it’s powerful, but understated at the same time. It doesn’t need embellishment—it’s a statement on its own.”

As the perfect antidote to the incoming winter’s dark, damp nights, it’s the perfect time to take inspiration from Beckham and embrace a simple but bold refresh. The best part: Red is foolproof and works no matter the shape or length of your nails and always feels fashion-forward. After all, Beckham wouldn’t co-sign it otherwise.
