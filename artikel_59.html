COP31 deal draft foreshadows difficult route to consensus

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

COP31 negotiators in Turkey released a draft agreement on Monday to advance talks on fossil fuels and climate finance before this week's deadline. The pressure to reach a deal is intense after a year of environmental challenges including  catastrophic glacier flooding in Nepal and European wildfires, even as global conflict and cost-of-living protests distract leaders from climate action.
