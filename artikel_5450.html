Ai2 gets $5M from Google to turn AI weather model into a tool for projecting climate change

Tanggal: 2026-11-05


Google is giving the Allen Institute for AI about $5 million, plus $1.8 million in cloud credits, to turn NeuralGCM, a Google AI weather model, into an open-source tool for projecting climate change over the next five to 50 years. Ai2 plans to add the ocean to the model, work on making it reliable in a warmer world and release the code for training it. Read More
