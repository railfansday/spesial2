Men’s Wearhouse Kicks Off Black Friday With Designer Discounts on Suits and Tuxedos — Just in Time For Awards Season

Just in time for holiday parties and the upcoming awards season, Men’s Wearhouse has kicked off its Black Friday sale early, slashing prices across its expansive selection of suiting, formalwear and everyday wardrobe staples.

The menswear retailer is offering discounts on top brands such as Calvin Klein, Joseph Abboud, Kenneth Cole and Nautica, with particularly steep savings on sport coats, wool overcoats and tailored separates. The early Black Friday deals, which run through 11:59 p.m. CT on Nov. 24, include 40% off select suits, sport coats and dress shirts, up to 50% off outerwear and casualwear and up to 40% off shoes.

Standout deals include a Calvin Klein slim-fit wool-blend suit for $299.99 and a Joseph Abboud navy wool-blend blazer for $149.99 (normally $249.99). Those looking to spend a little less can snag a classic black Nautica suit for just $119.99, down from $199.99.

Of course, you don’t need a black-tie invitation to take advantage of the retailer’s Black Friday offerings. Some of the biggest discounts are on cold-weather staples, including tailored overcoats, sweaters and casual trousers. A structured wool overcoat is an especially worthwhile investment this time of year, versatile enough to throw over a suit on the way to a premiere or pair with jeans and a sweater on the weekend. A pair of Joseph Abboud stretch denim jeans is currently going for just $14.99, down from $69.99.

The sale also extends to the retailer’s more giftable offerings. Leather belts, ties and dress shirts make practical presents for the men on your list, while those willing to spend a little more can choose from an assortment of dress shoes, loafers and boots.
