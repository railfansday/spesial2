Texans OC Nick Caley on Dell's and Boutte's lack of catches vs Browns

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

After playing a prominent role in the Houston Texans' Week 9 win against the Los Angeles Chargers, wide receivers Tank Dell and Kayshon Boutte were hardly involved in Houston's loss against the Cleveland Browns.

Dell and Boutte each had over 100 yards and a least one touchdown against Los Angeles, but combined for only 21 yards against the Browns. Boutte was targeted just once and Dell was only targeted four times. Offensive coordinator Nick Caley attributed their lack of involvement to the flow of the game, especially because Houston was not able to get things going in the second half.

"Just the flow. Three-and-outs, three-and-outs, three-and-outs, three-and-outs, and you really can't get into working your different groupings and sustaining those drives," Caley said Monday. "The first half, I think most of those drives- not all of them had six-plus plays and we were able to get into a good rhythm and be able to mix groupings, et cetera. The second half of this game, not so much."

Instead of targeting Dell or Boutte, quarterback C.J. Stroud was primarily trying to get the ball to Nico Collins, who finished the game with 94 yards and a touchdown. Collins was target a career-high 16 times in this game, although he only had one catch on two targets in second half. He also had a catch for 16 yards on fourth down that did not count due to a holding penalty. That penalty may have cost the Texans a chance to go win the game or at least go to overtime.

Houston will be looking to get back on track on Thursday night against the Indianapolis Colts. The Texans lost to Indianapolis earlier in the season, but the Colts have traded away numerous players since then, including their top two wide receivers.
