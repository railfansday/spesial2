IPL 2027 transfers: List of confirmed swaps, trade rumours as Hardik Pandya linked to CSK, DC, KKR move

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The IPL trade window has generated a lot of hype in the off-season, especially with franchises looking to strengthen their squads before the 2027 season.

Apart from the mini-auction that generally steals all the limelight, trades among various teams can be equally important since it allows them to fill their gaps without engaging in a bidding war.

With several prominent names already mentioned in the media, including Mumbai Indians captain Hardik Pandya, the trade period promises to be quite exciting.

While the transfer period is still on, The Sporting News takes a look at all the IPL 2027 confirmed transfers and the biggest rumours making the rounds.

At the time of writing, only the trade deal between Delhi Capitals and Lucknow Super Giants for Rishabh Pant and Kuldeep Yadav has been confirmed.

Hardik Pandya has undoubtedly turned out to be the most talked-about player when it comes to any deal being done.

Reports have claimed that both CSK and DC are showing interest in acquiring the Mumbai Indians captain.

The CEO of CSK, Kasi Viswanathan, has outrightly denied that there are any talks of recruiting Pandya to their team. However, reports are claiming that the five-time champions are indeed trying to explore all possibilities and Delhi Capitals are also a possible destination for him.

According to reports, Punjab Kings have emerged as the latest team in running to sign the Mumbai Indians captain. They are reportedly closing in on a deal to sign Pandya.

Chennai Super Kings / Kolkata Knight Riders

MI reportedly wanted Shivam Dube + Ayush Mhatre. Talks later shifted to Dube + Dewald Brevis, but MI sought an additional ₹10 crore, ending negotiations. KKR are also in the race, hoping to lure him with the offer of captaincy. The three-time champions could also include Cameron Green as part of a swap deal.

Recent reports suggest MI wanted Harshit Rana and Rinku Singh in exchange for Hardik Pandya, but KKR reportedly rejected the offer.

Moreover, Hardik could even stay put in Mumbai, but the franchise could take away the captaincy from him.

Reported interest and DC could offer Axar Patel in exchange, according to Cricbuzz.

RR could offer Yashasvi Jaiswal in exchange to sign Hardik Pandya.

LSG are also in the fray for Hardik Pandya transfer as they could offer Nicholas Pooran as part of swap deal.

Possible swap involving Romario Shepherd has been speculated.

Possible exchange involving Washington Sundar or Sai Kishore coming to CSK.

Possible swap involving Gurnoor Brar moving to Punjab Kings.

Pandya reportedly reached out to GT for a potential trade, but his captaincy demands led the franchise to back off from the table.

Mumbai Indians, Gujarat Titans; PBKS and Delhi Capitals

Handing out captaincy to Ishan Kishan might have miffed the opener, and he has been reportedly seeking new pastures.

CSK could reportedly release the ₹14.2 crore all-rounder to free up funds and attempt to buy him back at a lower price at the Goa auction.

"The Ricky Ponting-coached franchise has advanced considerably in its negotiations with Mumbai Indians, with India pacer Arshdeep Singh and wicketkeeper-batter Vishnu Vinod likely to move in the opposite direction," the Sportstar reported on Tuesday, November 3.

IPL 2027 mini-auction is set to take place in Goa on December 16, 2026, with the franchise briefing on December 15.

The one-day auction would be the first IPL auction to be conducted in India after 2022. The deadline for all 10 franchises to submit their list of players to retain or release is November 15, 2026.
