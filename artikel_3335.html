David Walliams Being Investigated By Police

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

London police have launched an investigation into David Walliams.

A Metropolitan Police statement said the force had “received a number of allegations of non-recent serious sexual offences involving a man now in his 50s,” and that these allegations are distinct from those reported by ITV News and The Guardian on Tuesday. “However, as part of their investigation, detectives are also proactively reviewing that reporting to establish whether there is any evidence of offences,” added the police.

Tuesday’s article and documentary, which the Little Britain co-creator tried to block via the courts, carried allegations of sexually exploitative activity towards young women and girls. The women accuse Walliams of using his celebrity to prey on teenagers as young as 15, making contact with them through work, including on Britain’s Got Talent, to pursue them for sexual purposes. After the publications, Britain’s Got Talent producer Fremantle urged anyone who “feels they have been the subject of wrongdoing” to come forward, although it hasn’t launched a formal investigation.

Deadline has contacted Walliams’ legal representatives for comment on The Guardian and ITV News’ allegations. In a statement to ITV News, Walliams denied all allegations against him. When trying to prevent publication, Walliams’ lawyers revealed that he had made two attempts to take his life because of the allegations.

“I am not a sexual predator… I have not broken the law,” Walliams said. “I am devastated that some of the women I met ten to twenty years ago have now expressed hurt or anger towards me.”

Today, Metropolitan Police Detective Superintendent Mike Cagney, whose team is leading the investigation, said: “We would encourage anyone who has been the victim of a sexual offence to come forward and speak to us, no matter when it happened. We know reporting allegations of this nature can be daunting, but specialist officers are on hand to provide support, answer questions and guide you through the process.”

The news comes in the same week that British TV personality Russell Brand was sentenced to more than 10 years in prison for rape and sexual assault. The Times reported earlier on more allegations against Brand from another half-dozen women.
