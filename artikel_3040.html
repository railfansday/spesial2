Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Pricing gains and a sharp Plant Nutrition margin rebound powered the turnaround.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 24, 2026 at 2:41PM EST

Compass Minerals (CMP +14.51%) reported a return to net income for fiscal 2026, driven by improved production efficiency and pricing discipline. Management stated that the company implemented a back-to-basics framework to reduce expenses and strengthen core mining operations. The company reported margin expansion in the Plant Nutrition segment and reduced net leverage through debt repayment. Management indicated that priorities for fiscal 2027 include asset utilization and converting increased highway salt pricing into higher margins.
