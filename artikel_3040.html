TOYO Co., Ltd. (TOYO -8.94%) reported a decrease in third-quarter gross margins as the company shifted its sales mix from solar cells toward modules while navigating a complex U.S. trade regulatory environment. Management stated that shipments from its Ethiopia facility were impacted by customs examinations and a countrywide circumvention inquiry, though the company reported that all detained shipments have since been released. The company reported that its module manufacturing capacity in Texas reached 2 gigawatts and is substantially committed for 2027. Management indicated it is pursuing a vertically integrated strategy, including a planned 1.5 gigawatt heterojunction cell line in the United States and the potential for domestic wafer production to qualify for federal incentives. The company reported it has secured non-FEOC cell supplies to fulfill its existing module delivery commitments through 2027 to mitigate risks associated with the Ethiopia trade inquiry outcome.
