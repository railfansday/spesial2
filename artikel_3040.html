Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Record EBITDA and a Bahamas expansion anchor a busy third quarter update.

Excelerate Energy, Inc. (EE +3.19%) reported record quarterly results for the third quarter as management advanced a growth strategy centered on integrated downstream infrastructure and LNG supply. Management reported the development of the Bahamas LNG Hub as a primary expansion of its Caribbean platform, utilizing existing Jamaica-based assets to initiate service before deploying dedicated storage infrastructure in 2028. The company stated that its vessel deployment strategy for upcoming projects in Iraq and Colombia has been adjusted to avoid transit through the Strait of Hormuz, prioritizing operational security while maintaining targeted startup dates in early 2027. Management indicated that its financial position and low leverage support a multiyear capital expenditure program, including the conversion of an LNG carrier to a Floating Storage and Regasification Unit (FSRU) to capture a projected wave of new global LNG supply starting in 2028.
