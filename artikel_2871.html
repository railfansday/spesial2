When the Browns hired longtime NFL assistant Todd Monken as their head coach this offseason it really didn't move the radar. It wasn't considered a sexy pick. But the no -nonsense offensive minded job has his team playing well at 6-4. He seems to be a fit for the city and for team franchise. The first-time NFL head coach, 60, is a dark horse NFL Coach of the Year candidate

The Browns known for being a woebegone franchise that has never been to a Super Bowl. But there are looking to go to the payoffs for the third time this decade. Nothing much was expected from this team, but they are winning more than they are losing.

The Browns have had six fourth-quarterback comebacks and they are a perfect 4-0 at home. Do you think the Dawg Pound will be fired up for their first Sunday Night Football game in five years?
