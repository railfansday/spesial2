Warren Sapp says it’s ‘unreal’ how bad one Tampa Bay Buccaneers player looks

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Tampa Bay Buccaneers are having a difficult 2026/27 NFL season so far, which Warren Sapp has been discussing.

Tampa were most recently in action against the Chicago Bears, losing 27-24 after Caleb Williams managed 279 passing yards and two touchdowns.

That defeat means the Buccaneers are 3-6 at present. Now, Sapp has highlighted the struggles of Kameron Johnson.

In a video shared by 'NFL on NBC' on YouTube, Sapp described Johnson as looking like he was trying to catch a ball for the first time when he watched him.

Sapp said: "I haven't seen Godwin. Has Godwin played this year? And then did you see my kid Johnson on the first ball?

"He tried to catch it. I said, 'My God, was that the first time you ever tried to catch a ball, young man?' Unreal, Chris. Unreal."

Tampa have been given a bye for the latest round of action and will next take to the field on Sunday, November 22 against the Detroit Lions.

But despite their struggles, the Buccaneers may still have a chance to top the NFC South. The Carolina Panthers and the New Orleans Saints are currently above them with 3-5 records.

However, one of those teams is very likely to win this weekend, unless there is a tie, as they are going head-to-head on November 15.
