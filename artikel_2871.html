Real Madrid star in talks over AC Milan move; club will demand €70m fee with €80m buyback clause

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

In a major development, ESPN Brazil (h/t Madrid Xtra) reports that Endrick is now in talks with AC Milan over a potential transfer in January from Real Madrid.

The 20-year-old striker has found minutes hard to come by this season under Jose Mourinho, leading to speculations over his future. And, now, it appears that the youngster might be considering a possible move away to Italy.

The report states that Endrick has already begun talks with AC Milan over a potential switch in early 2027.

The young attacker left Real Madrid on loan in January earlier this year and enjoyed a productive spell at Olympique Lyon.

However, this time around, if he were to leave the Santiago Bernabeu, it will not be on loan, but instead a permanent transfer away.

Endrick in talks with AC Milan. (Photo by Angel Martinez/Getty Images)

Indeed, in case Endrick opts to move on from the Spanish capital club, he will be sold permanently and not offloaded on another temporary loan deal.

The report further claims that in case of a sale, Real Madrid will demand a transfer fee of around €70 million for the attacker.

Furthermore, Los Blancos also plan to insist on inserting a buyback clause worth €80 million in the agreement, so that they can bring Endrick back to the Bernabeu at a later date.

Despite the mounting speculations around his future, Real Madrid manager Jose Mourinho continues to insist that Endrick remains an important player for him and will remain at the club.

Mourinho wants to keep Endrick at Real Madrid. (Photo by Juan Manuel Serrano Arce/Getty Images)

Just earlier today during his press conference ahead of the Celta Vigo clash, the Portuguese tactician discussed the youngster's situation, saying:

"He's very happy here. For me, Real Madrid is more important than Endrick. I love him very much and I have a lot of affection for him. I need him, you already have the answer."

"To the clubs, to the agents, then Real Madrid decides something different, and we have to respect that. But I and Real Madrid know that he's important."

Endrick, for his part, has also maintained that he is very happy at Real Madrid and would love to remain at the club for a long time. But with minutes proving hard to come by, he could decide to move on come January.
