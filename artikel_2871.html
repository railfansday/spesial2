‘End This Ridiculous Soap Opera’ — Media Reporter Urges ESPN To ‘Sign the Best NFL Game Analyst’ Troy Aikman to New Deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The uncertainty surrounding Troy Aikman's future at ESPN remains a recurring storyline. The network has yet to offer him an extension beyond the 2026 season, and Aikman has confirmed he'll become a free agent next year, leaving his future alongside Joe Buck on Monday Night Football very much up in the air.

Still, given his track record and standing in the sport, there is no shortage of people who want to see Aikman stay. One prominent media reporter, in particular, has made a strong case for why ESPN should make sure the longtime broadcaster doesn't go anywhere.

Since hanging up his cleats, Aikman has become a fixture in the NFL broadcasting world. His knowledge of the game, experience, and natural presence in the booth have made him one of the most respected voices in football. He's also spent more than two decades working alongside Buck.

The longtime broadcasting duo moved to ESPN in 2022 to take over Monday Night Football, signing five-year deals. However, while Buck received a contract extension this offseason, Aikman's future with the network remains unresolved.

"Right from the jump, ESPN features Troy Aikman on 'Monday Night Countdown,'" Michael McCarthy of Front Office Sports pointed out.

The veteran media reporter has covered the sports media industry from New York since 2019 for Front Office Sports and has also spent more than a decade teaching at Rutgers University. His journalism background includes stints at USA Today and The Sporting News, along with contributions to The New York Times and The Wall Street Journal.

A four-time national award-winning journalist, McCarthy knows the sports media business well. And when it comes to Aikman's future, his message to ESPN was about as clear as it gets: The network should do what it takes to keep him.

"Again, I'm on the record here, ESPN: Sign the best NFL game analyst to an extension, and end this ridiculous soap opera with him and Joe Buck during the worldwide leader's 'Year of the Super Bowl,'" McCarthy tweeted.

The "soap opera" he's referring to may be reports of tension between the long-time broadcast partners. A few weeks ago, Buck and Aikman reportedly had a heated confrontation in the booth at SoFi Stadium before the matchup between the Buffalo Bills and Los Angeles Rams.

Reports have said Aikman suspected Buck played a role in changes to their production crew, while Buck denied responsibility and reportedly pointed instead to Aikman's treatment of staff.

It's unclear who, if anyone, is to blame for the situation, but ESPN has yet to put a new contract on the table for Aikman.

If broadcasting opportunities don't come his way, the Dallas Cowboys legend could always find a role within an NFL organization, particularly the franchise where he made his name as a player.

During an appearance on "105.3 The Fan," owner Jerry Jones acknowledged that while there are no immediate plans to bring Aikman into the organization, a future role with the Cowboys is "very possible" and should "not be ruled out."
