Steelers KR Kaden Wetjen could be benched for undrafted free agent

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Pittsburgh Steelers selected wide receiver Kaden Wetjen in the fourth round of the 2026 NFL Draft out of Iowa. Listing Wetjen as a wide receiver was a bit of a stretch, though, as he was really known for being one of the nation's most electrifying return specialists during his time with the Hawkeyes.

To this point in his NFL career, Pittsburgh has yet to reap the benefits of selecting the return man in the fourth round. This could explain why one Steelers insider believes that Wetjen could be upended by an undrafted free agent as soon as this weekend.

Appearing on 93.7 The Fan on Friday, Ray Fittipaldo of The Pittsburgh Post-Gazette suggested that the Steelers could replace Wetjen in favor of the highly recommended Max Hurleman.

Fittiplado reported earlier in the week that Rodgers had mentioned that he would like to see Hurleman to get some reps on special teams, a sound explanation for his belief that the undrafted free agent could get a helmet this Sunday.

Many members of the Steelers organization have sung the praises of Hurleman in the past week, including Aaron Rodgers, Mike McCarthy, and DK Metcalf. Earlier in the week, the team opened up Hurleman's 21-day practice window, clearing the path for him to return from injured reserve.

The receiver had put together two strong training camps with the team but has yet to appear in a game. There seems to be too much smoke to ignore right now. Pittsburgh has plans for Hurleman, and he could get a helmet as early as this week.
