Paul Anka, Stevie Wonder, Cher & More Kennedy Center Honorees Who Were Teenagers When They First Hit the Billboard Hot 100 (Full List)

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Most of the artists on this list weren't teen idols, as such. They simply knew what they wanted to do from an early age and got off to a fast start.

Paul Anka was just 15 – two weeks away from turning 16 – when he first made Billboard’s pop chart in July 1957 with the smash “Diana.” (This was more than a year before Billboard introduced the Hot 100.) Last week, Anka was announced as a 2026 recipient of the Kennedy Center Honors, one of the most prestigious career cappers in the entertainment field.

Anka is the 11th artist who later received the Kennedy Center Honors who first hit the Hot 100 as a teenager. Anka was a bona-fide teen idol when he started out, with such teen-targeted hits as “Put Your Head on My Shoulder” and “Puppy Love,” but most of the artists on this list weren’t teen idols, as such. They simply knew what they wanted to do from an early age and got off to a fast start.

In addition to these 11 artists who received Kennedy Center Honors as individuals, two artists who received the Honors as part of a group first made the Hot 100 as teenagers. Pete Townshend was 19 when The Who first made the Hot 100 with “I Can’t Explain” in March 1965. The Who received the Kennedy Center Honors in 2008. Verdine White was 19 when Earth, Wind & Fire first made the Hot 100 with “Love Is Life” in July 1971. EWF received the Kennedy Center Honors in 2019.

This year’s other Kennedy Center Honorees are songwriter/producer David Foster, actor/director Andy Garcia, playwright David Mamet, and country star Barbara Mandrell. The awards will be presented on Dec. 7 at Capitol One Arena in Washington, D.C.
