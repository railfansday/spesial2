Motor City win first overtime match in WTGL history against Woad's Boston

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Motor City Golf Club secure 7-6 overtime victory over Boston Common Golf in dramatc WTGL victory - the first ever to go to overtime; England's Lottie Woad part of beaten side, but chipped in sensationally to force overtime session; Albane Valenzuela then beat Woad to clinch victory

Albane Valenzuela narrowly topped England's Lottie Woad in the last leg of closest-to-the-pin to give Motor City Golf Club a 7-6 WTGL overtime victory over Boston Common Golf in one of the most thrilling matches SoFi Center has seen.

Motor City are in full control at 2-0-0 but things weren't nearly as easy this time as they were for an 8-3 win over Los Angeles Golf Club in their opener.

Boston (0-0-1) overcame an early 3-0 deficit and never stopped fighting in their debut match, earning one point in the standings for the overtime loss.

Detroit hasn't wasted any time getting acclimated around SoFi Center. The new team has already put up 15 points in their first two matches and proved itself as a serious contender on Monday with Lexi Thompson, Jeeno Thitikul and Valenzuela staying poised when Boston rallied down the stretch.

They also have plenty of confidence and a firm grasp of strategy, throwing the Hammer before players teed off at No 14 and leaning on world No 2 Thitikul to deliver. She did just that, winning the hole to earn two points and give Detroit a 6-4 advantage amid a wild singles session.

Boston had the advantage off the tee at the par-three third and threw the Hammer looking for a quick 2-0 lead. When Lexi Thompson made a 30-foot birdie putt and raised her putter in celebration, Detroit threw a Hammer of their own and Khang's nine-footer didn't fall as MCGC took a 3-0 lead for a big momentum swing.

It was the perfect example of how quickly things can change in WTGL with this format, and a sign of things to come later in the match.

Woad, who came out with an homage to her home country sporting a Leeds United Premier League jersey, was part of a Boston side who put forth an outstanding effort in their first go-round at SoFi Center.

Boston and Motor City were still scoreless entering the par-three third, where Thompson rolled in a 30-footer for birdie and gave Motor City a 3-0 lead with two Hammers in play.

Khang cut into the lead at the next, nailing a seven-foot birdie putt to make it 3-1. But MCGC continued to pour it on with a 19-foot birdie make from Valenzuela at No 5, giving her team a 4-1 advantage.

Boston continued to fight back and keep the match within reach, earning another point at the sixth on an eight-foot birdie make from Iwai to cut the deficit to two. Iwai came through with the flatstick again at No 9, rolling a perfect lag putt from 63 feet for a conceded birdie to make it 4-3 Motor City entering singles.

With the deficit down to one, Khang stepped up and made birdie at No 10 to best Thompson and tie the match at 4-4.

Iwai and Thitikul traded birdies at the 11th with impressive up-and-downs as the intensity continued to build down the stretch. Things continued to ramp up at the 12th, where Valenzuela drained a 25-footer for birdie to match Woad and keep it tied as Detroit and Boston continued to go shot-for-shot.

Thompson and Khang traded pars at the 13th and Detroit threw the Hammer before players teed off at the 14th, meaning Iwai had to accept. That showed how much confidence MCGC has in Thitikul and she delivered with a beautiful pin-high tee shot while Iwai's tee ball bounced off the bridge of The Last Toll and into the penalty area.

Boston eventually conceded the hole and Detroit regained the lead, 6-4.

Boston threw a Hammer at the last, needing to win two points to stay alive. Needing to get up-and-down from off the green to have a chance, Woad instead chipped in to win the hole and send the match to overtime in spectacular fashion.

Thompson hit it inside nine feet to start the best-of-three, closest-to-the-pin overtime session, giving Motor City the early advantage.

Khang narrowly inched out Thitikul in the second round as the match came down to the final shots.

Valenzuela came through for Detroit in the end, besting Woad to cap an epic victory for MCGC that could have gone either way down the stretch.

The WTGL follows on from the success of the Tomorrow's Golf League (TGL), founded by Tiger Woods and Rory McIlroy's TMRW Sports, in partnership with the PGA Tour.

The women's edition of the tech-infused golf league will see LPGA Tour stars feature in a unique team format, with five teams (representing five different cities) of five-player rosters confirmed for the inaugural season.

The season-long competition takes place across November and December at the SoFi Center in Florida, a purpose-built indoor venue, with every shot live and all the players mic'd up for the made-for-TV format.

Both the 2026 and 2027 editions of WTGL will be broadcast exclusively on Sky Sports as part of an expansion of its golf rights, which also includes a further two years of TGL presented by SoFi.

Every match will be exclusively live on Sky Sports Golf, Sky Sports+ or Sky Sports Mix, while repeats will be available the following day on Sky Sports Golf. You can also download the Sky Sports App for news, highlights, scores and reports.
