CBS Midseason 2027 TV Schedule Revealed Amid Surprise Changes

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

CBS has released its midseason 2027 TV schedule with new additions and returning shows — but there are a few changes audiences should be prepared for.

New shows that are rounding out the slate include Stacks of Cash — hosted by Mike O’Malley — and Drew Carey‘s Undercover Boss. Meanwhile, Matthew Gray Gubler and Melissa Fumero‘s long-awaited Einstein series is set to air on Fridays.

Established programs will also be going through shakeups when Cupertino moves to Sundays and Eternally Yours shifts to Mondays — while Ghosts will air twice a week on Mondays and Thursdays as Survivor gets new extended two-hour episodes. FBI and CIA are getting a new time period on Mondays while Sheriff Country shifts one hour over on Fridays.

Keep scrolling for a full schedule for CBS shows airing in midseason:

Wednesday, January 6 The Price Is Right at Night 8 p.m. Stacks of Cash 9 p.m.

Friday, January 8 Sheriff Country 8 p.m. Fire Country 9 p.m.

Tuesday, January 12 NCIS 8 p.m. NCIS: NY 9 p.m. NCIS: Origins 10 p.m.

Thursday, January 14 Georgie and Mandy’s First Marriage 8 p.m. Elsbeth 9 p.m.

Sunday, January 17 60 Minutes 7 p.m. Marshals 8 p.m. Tracker 9 p.m. Cupertino 10 p.m.

Thursday, January 21 Georgie and Mandy’s First Marriage 8 p.m. Ghosts 8:30 p.m. Elsbeth 9 p.m. Matlock 10 p.m.

Monday, January 25 Ghosts 8 p.m. Eternally Yours 8:30 p.m. FBI 9 p.m. CIA 10 p.m.

Sunday, January 31 NFL on CBS: AFC Championship 6:30 p.m. NCIS: NY (one time only) 10 p.m.

Friday, February 12 Einstein 8 p.m. Sheriff Country 9 p.m. Boston Blue 10 p.m.

Wednesday, February 24 Survivor 8 p.m. Undercover Boss 10 p.m.
