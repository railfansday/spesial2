United States Antimony Corporation (UAMY -6.73%) reported a transition to volume-based guidance to better account for volatile antimony market prices and the timing of government contract acceptances. Management stated that the company achieved a record monthly sales volume of 310,000 pounds in October 2026, signaling increased throughput at its processing facilities. The company is advancing its vertical integration strategy through the expansion of the Thompson Falls smelter and the start-up of the Radersburg flotation facility. Management indicated that the company remains the only domestic producer of antimony in North America, a position supported by federal executive orders requiring domestically sourced minerals for defense applications.
