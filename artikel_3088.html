Real REMAX Group Inc. (REAX -12.18%) reported its first combined financial results following the acquisition of RE/MAX, focusing on organizational simplification and cost structure improvements. Management reported actioning $40 million in annualized gross cost savings and identified over $28 million in synergies impacting adjusted EBITDA within the first two months post-closing. The company provided a preliminary outlook for 2027 that anticipates continued weakness in the U.S. housing market, including declining transaction volumes and home prices. Despite these headwinds, management stated that the combined platform is positioned to generate substantial free cash flow and increase market share through technology-driven productivity tools and franchise operational improvements.
