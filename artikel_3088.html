IRSA Inversiones (IRS) Q1 2027 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Currency-driven loss hides a 20% jump in dollar rental EBITDA.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

IRSA Inversiones y Representaciones Sociedad Anónima (IRS +0.72%) reported a net loss for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 as Argentine inflation outpaced the rate of currency devaluation, impacting the fair value of its investment properties. Management reported that despite the currency-driven loss, the rental segment achieved a 20% increase in EBITDA when measured in U.S. dollars. The company is currently executing a $150 million capital expenditure plan, which executives identified as one of its most significant investment cycles in a decade. This program focuses on expanding the shopping mall portfolio, revitalizing the Edificio Del Plata residential project, and advancing the large-scale Ramblas del Plata mixed-use development.
