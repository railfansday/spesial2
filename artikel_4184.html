Here's What The Metro Redux Next-Gen Upgrades Look Like On Xbox Series X|S

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Last night, we covered the story about Metro 2033 Redux and Metro Last Light Redux getting free upgrades for Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S, and now we've also got some footage of them to bring your way.

This is provided by the YouTube channel Fuzion Xbox Testing, as is typically the case, and it reveals some interesting details that we weren't previously aware of, such as how the two upgrades look and perform on Xbox Series S.

So, with Metro 2033 Redux, we're getting an output of 4K in both the 60FPS and 120FPS modes on Xbox Series X. The 60FPS mode seems to dip into the 50s and even 40s sometimes, but the 120FPS mode appears largely stable according to this initial footage. In both cases, it looks a fantastic upgrade from a visual perspective.

The Xbox Series S version, meanwhile, seems to run at 1080p and at a mostly solid 60FPS.

With Metro Last Light Redux, we're getting basically the same situation - two 4K modes at 60FPS and 120FPS on Xbox Series X, although the footage for this game suggests the frame rates are even more stable.

Xbox Series S is once again 1080p at 60FPS, and appears to run extremely well overall.
