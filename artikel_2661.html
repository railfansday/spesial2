Titans sign CB Corey Thornton off of the Panthers practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Titans have made an addition to their ailing defense.

They announced that they have signed cornerback Corey Thornton off of the Panthers’ practice squad. There was no corresponding move needed to clear space for Thornton on the roster in Tennessee.

Thornton appeared in two games for the Panthers this month. He had an interception in their Week 9 loss to the Broncos and also recorded eight tackles and three passes defensed.

After last Sunday’s 51-30 loss to the Cowboys, the Titans rank 30th in the league in points allowed and dead last in yards allowed. They’ll be hoping Thornton helps them improve on both fronts.
