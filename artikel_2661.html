Will Chargers face Geno Smith? Jets QB provides latest injury update

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Chargers will look to remain in the win column after getting their first taste of victory on Monday night.

It was long overdue for the Chargers; however, they are no longer winless. Los Angeles improved to 1-8 and has a strong chance to earn their second win on Sunday when they take on the Jets.

The two teams are a combined 3-16 this season. It's been a rough road for both teams, and while the Chargers are dealing with injuries, the Jets may be without Geno Smith.

Smith suffered an ankle injury last Sunday against the Bills and was walking with a noticeable limp on Wednesday. That's not an encouraging sign; nonetheless, Smith said he plans on playing in Week 11 against Los Angeles.

"That's the plan," Smith said. "Obviously, you've got to take it day by day, but that's the plan."

The Jets are riding a five-game losing streak despite Smith playing efficiently under center.

In the season, Smith has 2,226 passing yards, a completion percentage of 67.1%, 12 touchdowns and three interceptions. He also has a 99.2 passer rating this season.

The Chargers have played well against Smith in his career. Los Angeles is 3-1 against Smith, limiting him to a 66.4 passer rating, 582 passing yards, four touchdowns, and six interceptions over four games.

Los Angeles is the home favorite in this contest at 3-point favorites.
