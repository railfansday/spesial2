Steelers Provide No Update on Mason McCormick After Ankle Injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Steelers Provide No Update on Mason McCormick After Ankle Injury originally appeared on SportsNet Pittsburgh. Add SportsNet Pittsburgh as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Pittsburgh Steelers suffered a significant injury concern along their offensive line during Sunday's matchup against the Cincinnati Bengals.

Starting left guard Mason McCormick was carted off the field late in the second quarter with an apparent right ankle injury. The Steelers quickly ruled him out for the remainder of the game.

The injury occurred on a third-and-8 play when Bengals defensive tackle Jonathan Allen drove McCormick backward into quarterback Aaron Rodgers while recording a sack. McCormick remained on the ground after the play, prompting Rodgers to signal for medical personnel.

Medical staff placed McCormick's right ankle in an air cast before he was helped onto the cart by teammates Dylan Cook and Ryan McCollum. Several Steelers players gathered around the third-year lineman before he was taken off the field.

The concern surrounding McCormick's injury continued after the game, with several Steelers acknowledging the difficult situation. Rodgers called it "a tough injury," while linebacker Patrick Queen expressed hope that the outcome would not be the worst-case scenario.

"I just hope it's not, you know, the worst-case scenario," Queen said.

Head coach Mike McCarthy did not provide an update on McCormick's condition, saying, "We'll speak on that when the time is right."

According to NBC Sports' Pro Football Talk, McCormick had played every offensive snap at left guard for Pittsburgh this season before suffering the injury.

Spencer Anderson took over at left guard following McCormick's departure. Anderson entered the season as Pittsburgh's starting right guard but was replaced in the lineup earlier in the year.

Losing McCormick would be a significant setback for an offensive line that has relied on continuity throughout the season. The 2024 fourth-round pick has developed into an important piece of Pittsburgh's young offensive front.

The Steelers have not announced the full extent of McCormick's injury or provided a timetable for his return.

Pittsburgh ultimately fell to Cincinnati, 38-18, adding to an already difficult night for the team.
