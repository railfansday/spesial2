NFL Betting Advice: Giants-Commanders picks and props

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Welcome to the New York Giants betting angle, Week 10 edition! Each week, I'm giving you my three favorite wagers for that week's New York Giants game. My one rule: I won't ever give you wagers that go beyond -120. No gimmes!

The 5-2-1 Giants host the 2-5-1 Commanders on Thursday Night Football, and it's Big Blue's first rematch of the season. Fans will remember that the first contest between these teams resulted in the NFL's only tie this season, with each team blowing an easy chance to win at the end of overtime. I'm guessing we'll get another close game this time around. Jameis Winston was the Giant's quarterback in that Week 5 contest, but now he's backing up J.J. McCarthy, who gets his third start for New York on Thursday.

The Giants are coming off their first win in Philadelphia in 13 years, and they continue to lead a weakened NFC East. It wasn't pretty, but a win is a win. Not only did the Giants break their long losing streak at the Linc, but they also broke a strange streak of scoring 27 points in three straight games, starting with that Week 5 tie with Washington. They scored 19 against the Eagles, and their only touchdown drive was led by Winston, after McCarthy was briefly knocked out of the game. McCarthy is good to go on Thursday and Big Blue should also have Cam Skattebo back.

The Commanders fell at home to the Rams last week, 20-13. Jayden Daniels continues to play mistake-free football, but Washington struggled to move the ball and score. For the second straight week, the Giants have an opportunity to effectively put away a division rival for the 2026 season.

My picks were 1-2 in Week 9 and I'm 13-11 on the season. I did go 3-0 when these teams met the first time, so I'll try to replicate that this week.

Let's get to this week's picks. All lines and odds are from Fanduel, as of Wednesday, November 11.

The line for the game is Giants -1.5, with a total of 41.5.

Giants -1.5 (-120). Looking at the teams' records, and the fact that this game is being played at MetLife Stadium, this line feels surprisingly low. Is it a trap line? Maybe. But throw in that the Giants are coached by John Harbaugh while Dan Quinn is on the other side and I'm jumping on the value. Outside of the quarterback position, it's hard to find a spot where Washington is clearly better. Both teams have dealt with a lot of injuries, but Washington is not only without its top receiver and running back, but now both of its starting defensive tackles (DaRon Payne and Javon Kinlaw) are out. This won't be an easy game for New York by any stretch, but I think they're the better team and will come away with the win. The Giants' moneyline (-135) offers similar value.

Jayden Daniels OVER 34.5 rushing yards (-114). This was my favorite prop is Week 5, and it hit big-time, with Daniels rushing for 103 yards and two touchdowns. New York will need to do a much better job of trying to contain him this time around, and I'm sure Dennard Wilson has a plan. Maybe Arvell Reese will spy him some? I still think Daniels should easily get to this total. New York's secondary is playing great football, and Daniels won't hesitate to take off when there's nothing there.

Malik Nabers longest reception OVER 23.5 yards (-106). I'd really like to take Nabers over 5.5 receptions, but at -134 its not eligible. I'll take this prop. The Commanders have a porous secondary, and they rank 30th in the league in passing yards allowed per game (240). McCarthy struggled to get Nabers the ball last week but that shouldn't be the case on Thursday. Puka Nacua absolutely demolished Washington last week (14 catches for 150 yards and a touchdown, on 19 targets), even though everyone in the building knew he was the first read on most dropbacks. Nabers just needs one big play for this to hit, and I don't think that's a problem. He's had a catch of at least 22 yards in five straight contests.

Those are the picks for Week 10. Good luck with your wagers! And if you play fantasy football, be sure to check out my Week 10 fantasy preview, with start/sit advice and more, coming soon.
