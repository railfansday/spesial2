Prince William and Kate Middleton's Sandringham Estate Home Broken Into

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Prince William and Princess Kate Middleton‘s home at the royal family’s Sandringham estate has been broken into.

Us understands the royal couple were not at the residence when the incident occurred on Monday, November 23.

The Sun was first to report the security breach at Anmer Hall in Norfolk, England, revealing authorities launched an investigation. Norfolk Police reportedly arrested two men on suspicion of burglary and trespass.

“Police were called shortly before 6pm last night following a report of suspicious activity at Anmer Hall on the Sandringham Estate,” officers told The Sun. “Officers attended the scene and deployed specialist resources, including police dogs, drones, and the National Police Air Service helicopter, to assist with searches in the area.”

The two suspects are still in police custody, per authorities.

William and Kate, both 44, were given their Sandringham residence by the late Queen Elizabeth II in 2011 to celebrate their wedding. The couple and their children — Prince George, 13, Princess Charlotte, 11, and Prince Louis, 8 — now spend most of their time in Windsor.

“The kids can play outside and be normal there,” a source told Us earlier this year about the family’s Forest Lodge home.

Of the pair’s parenting styles, the insider added, “Kate brings patience and structure, and William has a fun outlook and energy. [He] likes to play sports [with the kids]. They both do school runs and bedtime, and they both enjoy cooking and make family dinner a priority. The kids also have chores.”

This isn’t the first time the British royals have faced a security breach. A man was charged in January after allegedly breaking into the grounds at Kensington Palace twice in the days leading up to Christmas.

Derek Egan was charged with trespassing on a protected site and breaching bail conditions. He was sentenced to 32 weeks in prison.

William and Kate were reportedly not on the premises when the incidents occurred in December 2025.

A separate break-in was attempted at Windsor Castle in June 2025, with the Thames Valley Police noting at the time that the trespasser was quickly apprehended.

“He did not enter the inner grounds of Windsor Castle,” authorities stated. “He was arrested on suspicion of trespass on a secure site and possession of class A drugs. He has been bailed and an investigation is ongoing by Thames Valley Police.”
