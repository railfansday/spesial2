Your Probation Has Been Extended by Three Months

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Your six month probation review was this afternoon. You expected a short chat and a form to sign. Instead your manager said they would like to extend your probation by three months. They mentioned communication and pace, said it was nothing to worry about, and the meeting ended before you had properly taken it in.

It is normal to feel embarrassed, or angry, or both. Give yourself an evening. Then treat the extension for what it usually is. Feedback, delivered late, with a deadline attached.

Many contracts allow an employer to extend probation, and it is common. Managers often use it when they are not ready to confirm someone but do not want to end things either. It is a real warning, but it is also a real chance. You have three months, and you now know someone is paying attention.

Your first job is to turn vague words into something you can act on. Ask for a follow up meeting this week, and go in with questions. What specifically has worried you about communication? Can you give me two recent examples? What would good look like by the end of the extension? How will we both know I have got there? Write down the answers and send them back in an email afterwards, so you both have the same version.

Ask for regular check ins, perhaps every two weeks. Short ones are fine. Their purpose is to hear about problems while there is still time to fix them, rather than all at once at the end.

Then work on the specific things, and make the progress visible. If the concern was pace, agree smaller tasks and finish them. If it was communication, send a brief written update at the end of each week. Say what you finished, what is blocked and what is next.

Look for help too. A colleague you trust may tell you things your manager has not. And check whether the job matches what you were told when you joined. Sometimes an extension reveals a mismatch, and it is better to see it now.

Many people whose probation is extended go on to pass it. The ones who do usually asked for clarity early.
