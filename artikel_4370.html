Every New Lego Set Releasing In November 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Lego releases new sets at the start of each month, and although November tends to be a quieter time for the world-renowned toy brick seller, there are some big ones coming your way this month, both in terms of IP and literal size. A first-ever set for one of the biggest anime in history, and another that will break the world record for the biggest Lego has ever sold. Below you'll find everything you need to know about both, including prices and links to their store pages, along with every other new set releasing throughout November.

Dragon Ball's first-ever Lego set is finally here. A 1,764-piece build of Shenron, the dragon who puts the dragon in Dragon Ball, and it comes with the series' first-ever minifigure too, with Goku getting that honor, of course. You can only buy the set today if you are a Lego Insider, with it going on general sale for everyone from November 4. It's free to become an Insider, and not only can you buy certain sets before everyone else when you sign up, but you also accrue Insider points that can then be used to buy other sets, some of which are never made available to non-members, such as the Pokemon Center set.

Dragon Ball isn't the only big IP getting its first Lego set this month. Finally, Godzilla, the king of the monsters, has been turned into Lego too, although you'll have to wait a little longer for this massive set's arrival. Lego's Godzilla is this year's Black Friday set, which means it won't be available until November 27. Its store page is already live, though, confirming some of the finer details about the set, including its 5,363-piece count, $449.99 price tag, and that it will stand at an impressive 27.5 inches tall once complete. Lego has also revealed that those who buy the set before November 30 will get the Godzilla Logo & Footprint GWP for free.

It's official, the record for the biggest commercial Lego set ever has been broken. That's thanks to the release of the Sagrada Familia set, which is available now, taking the record away from the massive Lego World Map that was retired years ago. The Lego version of the iconic Catalan cathedral will require an incredible 12,060 pieces to complete, breaking the old record by almost 400 pieces. Despite that, it isn't even close to being the most expensive set ever, likely due to the fact that it isn't tied to an IP. Still pretty pricey, though, at $799.99.

It's a big month for big additions to Lego's collection of buildable landmarks, and also on sale starting today is the Venetian Neighborhood set. Not an exact replica of a specific landmark like the Sagrada Familia, but a build that has been designed to capture the essence of Venice, one of the most unique cities in the world. It's also not quite as big or as expensive, although at 5,682 pieces, it's still a set that will likely keep you busy throughout the rest of the year until Lego's release season picks back up again in the new year.

Don't panic, there is one set releasing this November that won't require you to set aside days to build, or need you to spend the equivalent of what it would cost to buy a new console to get it, and it's an adorable one. The Cozy Plants set, a new addition to Lego's growing range of buildable shrubs, and these cute ones with the little faces are the best. I'm a big fan of Lego's planters, not only because they're small and cheap, but because real plants don't last long in my house since I forget to water them for weeks at a time. No risk of that here. I'd actually recommend you don't water them at all; it will just make a mess.

Rather than kicking the month off with a new GWP, Lego is bringing October's into November. The Build, Paint, and Play set will remain available as a GWP through November 8. It's free when you spend a total of $130 or more, so any of the sets above will qualify, aside from the Cozy Plants set. The Venetian Water Taxi is this month's new GWP, but it will only be given away with the Venetian Neighborhood set, and as noted above, for the last four days of the month, the new Godzilla set will come with a free Godzilla Logo & Footprint set.
