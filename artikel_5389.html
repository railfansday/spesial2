Minelab Invests in the Future of Metal Detecting With Original YouTube Competition Series: Race to the Realm Finals

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

15 contestants emerge from The Realm app to compete for $100,000 in a four-episode adventure series premiering Dec. 2, with Bass Pro Shops as official basecamp sponsor

CHICAGO, Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Minelab, the global leader in metal detecting technology, launches Race to the Realm Finals, an original YouTube competition series that showcases the skill, discovery, and adventure of metal detecting. The four-episode series premieres Wednesday, Dec. 2 on Minelab's YouTube channel with new episodes released every Wednesday through Dec. 23.

Race to the Realm Finals premieres Dec. 2 on Minelab's official YouTube channel @MinelabDetecting

The competition began in The Realm, Minelab's digital community mobile app, where metal detecting enthusiasts competed for a chance to become one of 15 finalists. Now, those contestants will face a series of challenges designed to test their technical skills, instincts, endurance, and ability to perform under pressure. At stake: a $100,000 grand prize and the title of Race to the Realm champion.

The series marks a new chapter in Minelab's investment in the metal detecting community, bringing the passion and expertise of everyday enthusiasts to the screen while introducing new audiences to the hobby.

"Metal detecting has always been driven by curiosity, skill, and the thrill of what you might discover next," said Ben Harvey, president and executive general manager of Minelab. "Race to the Realm is an investment in the people who make this community extraordinary and in the future of the hobby itself. We're creating new ways for enthusiasts to connect, challenge themselves, and share their passion, while showing a much broader audience how much adventure and skill metal detecting has to offer."

From Digital Community to Real-World Competition

Race to the Realm connects Minelab's digital community with a high-stakes, real-world adventure. Through The Realm app, participants engaged in a three-round national competition, earning points through digital challenges, community engagement, and real-world activities. Five competitors from each round advanced, securing their spots among the 15 final contestants.

The original YouTube series takes that competition further with skills and adventure in the spotlight. Across four episodes, contestants must adapt to unfamiliar challenges, make strategic decisions, and put their detecting expertise to work as the stakes rise.

The series also gives viewers a closer look at metal detecting as an evolving, skill-based outdoor pursuit, combining technology, problem-solving, exploration, and the possibility of unexpected discovery.

Bass Pro Shops Joins as Official Basecamp Sponsor

Bass Pro Shops, a leading national outdoor retailer, served as the official basecamp sponsor of Race to the Realm Finals, supporting the competition and connecting the series to the broader outdoor adventure community.

The partnership connects the series to the broader outdoor recreation community and reinforces Minelab's commitment to expanding the reach of metal detecting.

A New Chapter for the Metal Detecting Community

Race to the Realm is part of Minelab's broader effort to build a more connected community and create new opportunities for enthusiasts to share their passion.

Through The Realm app, users can connect with fellow detectorists, share finds and engage with the community. The app will also feature all four episodes of Race to the Realm Finals, along with exclusive finalist interviews and behind-the-scenes content that takes viewers deeper into the competition.

Together, the series and The Realm create new ways for longtime enthusiasts and newcomers to experience metal detecting.

Race to the Realm Finals premieres Dec. 2 on Minelab's official YouTube channel, with new episodes every Wednesday through Dec. 23.

Meet the 15 contestants, follow the competition, and watch the adventure unfold at the Race to the Realm Hub starting Dec. 2.
