NOAH HOLDINGS LIMITED ANNOUNCES UNAUDITED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

SINGAPORE, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Noah Holdings Limited ("Noah" or the "Company") (NYSE: NOAH and HKEX: 6686), a leading and pioneer wealth management service provider offering comprehensive one-stop advisory services on global investment and asset allocation primarily for global Chinese high-net-worth investors, today announced its unaudited financial results for the third quarter of 2026.

Our Mainland China operations are organized into three reportable segments: mainland China public securities, mainland China asset management, and mainland China insurance. Each segment operates under a dedicated brand and serves a distinct client need in the mainland China market.

Mainland China public securities, operating under the Noah Upright brand, is the business that distributes mutual funds and private secondary products in mainland China. This segment operates under an "online-first, offline-supported" business model, with the goal of facilitating global asset allocation through RMB-denominated products.

Mainland China asset management, operating under the Gopher Asset Management brand, is the business that manages RMB-denominated private equity funds and private secondary products. Current focus areas include managing primary market exits on existing vintages and growing cross-border ETF products in the secondary market.

Mainland China insurance, operating under the Glory brand, is the business that distributes insurance products in mainland China, consisting mainly of life and health insurance products. The business has been undergoing a strategic shift toward a commission-only broker model and comprehensive family succession planning services. The net revenues for the third quarter of 2026 were RMB1.3 million (US$0.2 million).

Our international operations are organized into three reportable segments: international wealth management, international asset management, and international insurance and comprehensive services. The Company operates booking centers in Hong Kong, Singapore and key U.S. markets including New York, Los Angeles and Silicon Valley.

International wealth management, operating under the ARK Wealth Management brand, is the business that provides offline and online wealth management services to global Chinese high-net-worth investors outside mainland China. Currently we are dedicated to providing comprehensive services using our booking centers in Hong Kong and Singapore.

International asset management, operating under the Olive Asset Management brand, is the business that manages USD-denominated private equity funds and private secondary products, with a dedicated U.S. product center and partnerships with top-tier global managers across structured products and hedge funds. We are building our offices in Hong Kong, Singapore, Japan and key U.S. markets, including New York and Silicon Valley.

International insurance and comprehensive services

International insurance and comprehensive services, operating under the Glory Family Heritage brand, is the business that provides comprehensive international services such as insurance distribution, trust services and other family office-style services. With offices in Hong Kong, Singapore and Los Angeles, we provide global coverage to clients.

Headquarters reflects revenue generated from corporate operations at the Company's headquarters in Singapore and office in Shanghai, as well as administrative costs and expenses that are not directly allocated to the aforementioned six business segments, including investments in platform-wide technology, AI infrastructure and corporate functions.

"In the third quarter of 2026, global markets became more volatile, and many high-net-worth clients shifted toward caution, liquidity and diversification," commented Ms. Jingbo Wang, Co-founder and Chairwoman of Noah. "We used this period to reposition the business. We stayed with our decision to step back from insurance distribution and put our resources into investment products and global asset allocation, where our long-term strengths lie. Even as markets pulled back, demand for our investment products held up, and our overseas business continued to deliver steady contributions."

"This quarter's results also include several one-off costs from streamlining our organization. We see these as investments in a leaner, more focused Noah."

"Building on the foundation we set out in the first half of the year, our use of AI is enhancing both the quality of our client service and the efficiency of our organization. It allows our relationship managers to serve more clients with greater consistency and helps our four global booking centers work together more seamlessly. Looking ahead, we will keep strengthening our global platform, risk management and technology capabilities to create long-term value for our clients and shareholders."

Net revenues for the third quarter of 2026 were RMB509.0 million (US$75.9 million), a 19.6% decrease from the corresponding period in 2025, primarily due to lower one-time commissions from international insurance distribution and lower recurring service fees from mainland China private equity products, partially offset by higher performance-based income from mainland China private secondary products.

Operating costs and expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were RMB445.1 million (US$66.4 million), a 3.5% decrease from the corresponding period in 2025. Operating costs and expenses for the third quarter of 2026 primarily consisted of (i) compensation and benefits of RMB270.0 million (US$40.3 million); (ii) selling expenses of RMB54.1 million (US$8.1 million); (iii) general and administrative expenses of RMB87.4 million (US$13.0 million); (iv) provision for credit losses of RMB2.9 million (US$0.4 million); (v) other operating expenses of RMB31.0 million (US$4.6 million); less (vi) income from government subsidies of RMB0.3 million (US$0.05 million).

Operating margin for the third quarter of 2026 was 12.6%, compared with 27.2% for the corresponding period in 2025.

Interest income for the third quarter of 2026 was RMB29.5 million (US$4.4 million), a 2.8% increase from the corresponding period in 2025.

Investment income for the third quarter of 2026 was RMB85.5 million (US$12.7 million), compared with RMB20.6 million in the corresponding period in 2025, primarily due to higher fair value gains on investments held by consolidated funds.

Income tax expense for the third quarter of 2026 was RMB62.1 million (US$9.3 million), an 8.0% decrease from the corresponding period in 2025.

Non-GAAP Net Income Attributable to Noah Shareholders

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had RMB3,891.7 million (US$580.5 million) in cash and cash equivalents, compared with RMB4,322.7 million as of June 30, 2026 and RMB3,837.4 million as of September 30, 2025.

Net cash inflow from the Company's operating activities during the third quarter of 2026 was RMB175.0 million (US$26.1 million), compared with a net cash inflow of RMB316.9 million in the corresponding period in 2025. The decrease was primarily due to lower income from operations and decreases in contingent liabilities.

Net cash inflow from the Company's investing activities during the third quarter of 2026 was RMB32.3 million (US$4.8 million), compared with a net cash inflow of RMB253.7 million in the corresponding period in 2025. The decrease was primarily due to lower net proceeds from redemptions of held-to-maturity investments.

Net cash outflow from the Company's financing activities during the third quarter of 2026 was RMB612.0 million (US$91.3 million), compared with a net cash outflow of RMB549.9 million in the corresponding period in 2025, primarily due to higher dividend payments.
