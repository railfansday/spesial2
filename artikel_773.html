Jamie Dornan Admits He “Loved Shoplifting” as a Kid

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The actor made the revelation on 'Watch What Happens Live With Andy Cohen,' where he appeared alongside Anthony Mackie to promote their new heist thriller series '12 12 12.'

Jamie Dornan is getting candid about his illegal hobbies as a child.

The actor, along with Anthony Mackie, made a guest appearance on Monday’s episode of Watch What Happens Live With Andy Cohen to promote their new heist thriller series 12 12 12 for Apple TV.

During the segment “Dirty Dornan,” the host asked Dornan, “In honor of your role in 12 12 12, did you ever engage in a five-finger discount in your youth?” In the show, the actor plays Paul Galvin, a master thief and criminal mastermind. “I loved shoplifting,” Dornan candidly replied, drawing laughter from Andy Cohen.

However, he clarified that he “would only steal from the big corporations. Never like an independent store. I’m not paying for like lip balm in, you know, CVS or something.”

12 12 12 follows a daring heist in Zurich, Switzerland, and the ensuing European manhunt across three non-chronological timelines.

Elsewhere on Watch What Happens Live, the Fifty Shades of Grey star also revealed that Viggo Mortensen gave him the “stamp of approval” before he took over the role of Strider, aka Aragorn, in the upcoming Lord of the Rings: The Hunt for Gollum.

“I haven’t spoken directly to Viggo,” Dornan said, “but he gave the stamp of approval and that’s all I care about. They released a thing that he sort of agreed to, to say that he was thrilled that I’d be taking [the character] over from him.”

Cohen also asked the Belfast actor if he grew his hair out for the role, but Dornan revealed that he wore “an amazing wig” instead.

Last week, he told Entertainment Tonight that he “actually cried” after learning he was cast as Aragorn in the upcoming installment of the beloved epic fantasy franchise.

“It’s like a dream,” Dornan added. “It’s an absolute dream and an honor to be entering that world in any capacity, let alone playing a character of such significance and such admiration, and that we all love and love what Viggo did with that role in those three films.”
