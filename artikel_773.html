Walmart’s Viral “Birkin” Dupe Still Makes a Luxe-Looking Gift for Less

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Hermès taste on a Walmart budget? This Birkin dupe delivers a designer feel without the price tag.

When Walmart unveiled their under-$100 Birkin dupe over a year ago, it got the internet talking. And while the conversation has since died down, the Hermès lookalike (or "Wirkin" as TikTok calls it) still remains a great holiday gift.

Less elusive and expensive than the actual designer bag, Walmart's affordable alternative is chic, versatile, and less than $60.

Walmart's Hermès Birkin dupe bears a striking resemblance to the real thing, only without the hefty designer price tag. Available in 17 colors, including a faux ostrich feather texture, this spacious tote boasts a similar boxy satchel silhouette, complete with the designer's signature gold-toned lock detail.

Reviewers praise its sturdy construction and elegant design, noting that it looks and feels more expensive than it is.The bag also includes a handy matching pouch that doubles as a wristlet.
