Arsenal winger Christos Tzolis endured a disappointing evening as Greece were smashed 7-0 by the Netherlands on Friday.

The forward's Gunners teammate Jurrien Timber was on the score sheet twice, with Tijjani Reijnders also netting a hat-trick in the Nations League destruction.

Greece had been unbeaten in group two before the clash, but have now had their goal difference decimated and will find themselves under pressure from Germany.

Tzolis arrived at Arsenal in the summer transfer window, joining from Club Brugge after a deal worth around £35million was agreed.

It was thought that the 24-year-old would not be the last attacking signing to arrive in North London, with Leandro Trossard, Gabriel Martinelli and Gabriel Jesus all going the other way.

Yet moves for the likes of Vinicius Junior, Julian Alvarez and Morgan Rogers all ultimately failed to come to fruition, with Mikel Arteta left light in attack.

Tzolis is Greece's star man. The winger has netted 10 goals in 38 caps and has been tipped to ultimately become their all-time leading goal-scorer.

But on Friday, he was powerless to respond as the Netherlands launched wave after wave of attacks on his team's goal.

The 24-year-old did not help by delivering a poor individual display himself, too.

Tzolis completed just 23 passes in his 73 minutes on the pitch, with eight of his efforts missing their marks.

The forward also failed to create a single chance, with his one shot causing no trouble for the goalkeeper.

But there's more - Tzolis completed just one successful dribble and was dispossessed once, winning just three of his seven duels.

If Arteta was not already placing pressure on those above him to act in the January transfer window, he should well be now.

While Tzolis has produced good performances since arriving at Arsenal, he is clearly under huge pressure.

While the 24-year-old is fit, he is the only natural option for the left of Arteta's attack and will continue to play there.

But after such a big move from the Belgian league, Tzolis would have hoped to have time to bed in behind another winger.

Arsenal must provide that relief for the Greece international in January if they want to get the best out of him.
