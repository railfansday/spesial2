Steelers Trade Rumors Tracker: Latest News Ahead of 2026 NFL Trade Deadline

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

All things considered, it's been a strong start to the 2026 season for the Steelers.

Sitting at 6-2 and contending for the NFC North title entering Week 10, Pittsburgh's offense has been a pleasant surprise, while its run defense has led the way for the NFL's No. 7-ranked total defense. But that isn't to say the Steelers aren't without their weaknesses.

Ahead of the 2026 NFL trade deadline, which arrives Tuesday at 4 p.m. ET, Pittsburgh could be in the market for a wide receiver or secondary help. With Michael Pittman Jr. and Roman Wilson sidelined, an additional weapon for Aaron Rodgers could make some sense.

Below, you can follow along with the latest rumblings, rumors and official moves made by the Steelers at the trade deadline. Here's the latest surrounding Pittsburgh.

Schultz also reports that Pittsburgh is interested in adding not just a wide receiver, but is also looking at the cornerback market.

The Steelers head coach said he won't be involved in trade deadline plans much.

"I know Omar and his staff are doing their due diligence and so forth, but I haven't really spent a lot of time down there," McCarthy said.

According to Pelissero, Pittsburgh joins Kansas City and San Francisco among the teams who have been "calling around" for a wide receiver, with a "good bet" that multiple players from the position will be moved by the deadline.

The NFL trade deadline is Tuesday, Nov. 10. Trades must be submitted to the league office before 4 p.m. ET.

Jonathan Taylor and Josh Downs, Colts: Indianapolis has lost five games in a row, so the franchise is hitting the rest button. The Colts should sell high on Taylor and Downs with multiple teams searching for offensive help.

Brian Thomas Jr., Jaguars: After a breakout rookie season in 2024, Thomas hasn't been able to make a consistent impact in Jacksonville. He is just 24 years old, though, and may benefit from a change of scenery.

Maxx Crosby, Raiders: Las Vegas tried to finalize an offseason trade that would have sent Crosby to Baltimore, but the Ravens reportedly backed out because of medical concerns. Will the Raiders attempt to move Crosby again?

Khalil Mack, Chargers: It has been a disastrous campaign for the Chargers, the only team in the league without a win. Los Angeles should trade Mack now before he hits free agency.

Budda Baker and Josh Sweat, Cardinals: Jacoby Brissett is gone, and Gardner Minshew is hurt. The Cardinals simply don't possess enough firepower to compete in the AFC West, so Arizona should be discussing deals involving Baker and Sweat with contenders looking for a defensive boost.
