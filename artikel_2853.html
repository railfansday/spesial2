Indianapolis Colts discussion: Week 11 live chat during the Texans game

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Indianapolis Colts are on the road to take on the Houston Texans on Thursday Night Football for week 11 of the 2026 season, kickoff is at 8:15 p.m. EST.

Week 10 saw our Colts pick up their third win of the season against a terrible Miami Dolphins team starting a backup quarterback. Normally you would assume the Colts would easily handle such a text but that wasn't the case. Quarterback Daniel Jones posted a 1.6 QBR and Indy escaped with the victory.

That 1.6 QBR is impressive for all of the wrong reasons. Jones week 10 QBR was the lowest recorded QBR in any game by a starting quarterback this season. Every backup that has started a game during this NFL season has posted a higher mark on the metric. Even Baker Mayfield's zero completion performance on November 1st managed to score higher. Please understand, I'm not trying to sell QBR as some sort of be-all end-all metric for quarterback play. I don't even think it's that great of a metric, but you never want to have the lowest advanced metric score of the season in any category. And this week, Daniel Jones, will once again start at quarterback for the Indianapolis Colts.
