Mike Vrabel says Patriots to be without Drake Maye for 'short time'

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The New England Patriots aren't giving up hope.

Despite losing Drake Maye to injury in Week 10, the Patriots don't seem to be convinced the quarterback will be out for the remainder of the season. That is notable since reports indicated Maye suffered a fractured ankle, something the team has yet to confirm.

ESPN Insider Adam Schefter reported Friday, Nov. 20, that tests showed Maye fractured his right fibula but won't require surgery.

However, head coach Mike Vrabel didn't close the door on his star quarterback returning this season during an interview with the Patriots' YouTube channel on Nov. 19.

"I think he's gonna be unavailable here for the the short time, and we'll see where that goes down the road, but you know, we'll have to prepare to win without Drake in the short term," Vrabel said.

Vrabel noted that adversity hits a lot of teams in the NFL, but the Patriots still have a lot to play for right now.

New England is 5-5 after a loss to the Detroit Lions in Germany, and the team now heads into its bye week with some time to reset at quarterback. Tommy DeVito is expected to take over for Maye in the immediate future, but there has been speculation about adding a new quarterback.

The head coach said he doesn't know if there are plans to do that right now.

The Patriots trail the seventh-seeded Cleveland Browns by a game in the AFC playoff picture.

Vrabel said he is "looking forward" to working with DeVito and building a game plan for him. The coach expressed a belief that the backup improved last season, but that will have to continue going forward.

"Made great strides last year, great improvements," Vrabel said. "But also there's going to need to be some things here going into the games that's going to have to improve. I know that he's an elite competitor. Great toughness and he'll do everything that he can to give us a chance to win."

Maye is going to unavailable for the "short time," according to Vrabel.

It's unclear just how short that time is, but the Patriots haven't provided a concrete update on the quarterback since he suffered an ankle injury in Germany. Schefter reported Friday that a return at the end of the season is a "possibility." Maye's leg was caught underneath Aidan Hutchinson during the first quarter of the Patriots' loss to the Lions in Week 10.

Hutchinson tore his ACL on the same play, which will cost him the remainder of the season.
