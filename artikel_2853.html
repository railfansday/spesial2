Scottsdale Christian flag beats Ben Franklin to repeat as 3A champions

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

After taking a loss to Ben Franklin in the regular-season finale and watching his quarterback, junior Campbell Reader, struggle, Scottsdale Christian Academy coach Ryan Fisher decided to tweak the playbook and game plan.

The changes worked wonders as Scottsdale Christian avenged the loss from two weeks ago to beat Queen Creek Ben Franklin in the 3A state high school flag football championship 28-6 at Corona del Sol on Nov. 21, successfully defending its title. Reader finished that first game against the Chargers with 168 yards for one touchdown and three intereceptions; she took lessons applied to complete 21-of-35 passes for 230 yards and four touchdowns this time around.

"I was content to dink and dunk a little bit," Fisher said. "We have some good athletes. I want us to be like some horses and catch the ball and run. That's what I wanted today. Campbell really elevated the game. We did put in quarterback run today, we wanted her to run a little more."

Reader also ran the ball nine times for 80 yards, which led to the passing game opening up and a big day from senior wide receiver Nola Bynum. In her final game in an SCA uniform, Bynum, one of the best players all of 3A and the entire state, finished with six catches for 115 yards and two touchdowns.

Bynum had a 70-yard touchdown right before halftime that really shifted the momentum for SCA to go up 21-0. On defense, Bynum also had an interception.

"I don't know how to give it justice," Fisher said about Bynum. "I mean, she is a great person. 4.7 GPA, probably the valedictorian of the school, great athlete, great leader, three-year captain. She's so good, I just expect it. I mean she makes a great play, I forget how great a play it is. I'm gonna miss her."

It was a fitting end for Bynum and Fisher, who grew up together.

"Our parents have been friends since we were so little," Bynum said. "We have a little small group, we meet with her every week and I've gone to school with her my whole life. It's just so amazing to get to play on the team with her for three years. It's so sad that it's my last game, but it's been so fun playing with her. She's such an incredible person and carries our team."

In the early years of the 3A conference, SCA has established itself as the premier school and becomes the first to go back-to-back and win multiple state titles. It was a banner year for SCA (16-4), also beating two 6A schools at the Copper State Invitational to make the semifinals.

"I can't say enough good things about the girls in the program and what they do," Fisher said. "God's given us all a gift and our girls are showing it out there in the field, on and off the field, quite frankly. It is really an honor and privilege for me, to watch it and see them grow up and be a part of it."

It was a season of growth from Ben Franklin (17-4) and coach Jake Thomas, making the first title game in school history.

"We started in the first year that AIA had it and in the first year, we made playoffs in 5A," Thomas said. "In the second year, we won our first playoff game in 4A. Last year, we made semis. This year, we made the championship. It's awesome to see our program just growing at the junior high level and at the high school level."
