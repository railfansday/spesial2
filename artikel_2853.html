Cardinals QB issues continue: Beck or Johnson to start?

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

During training camp this year, both the quarterback and receiver rooms were overflowing with talent and capable bodies. Now, the pass catchers are backups to backups, while the QB room is down to two rookies and a guy who is being paid by his 17th NFL club.

This weekend as the Arizona Cardinals are preparing to play the Kansas City Chiefs, one of the best teams in the AFC, the coaching staff just might be starting its fourth quarterback this season. Rookie Carson Beck may not start.

Beck started his first NFL contest last week in the 34-6 beatdown from division foe Los Angeles Rams. He was scheduled to start again this weekend as center Hjalte Froholdt has been released from concussion protocol.

But Beck has had some flare-ups with back spasms. Although the team hasn't ruled Beck out for the Chiefs game, journeyman Josh Johnson is being prepped to start instead. If Johnson does play, that will become four starting quarterbacks in four games. What a QB turnstile.

Cardinals head coach Mike LaFleur stated that the team is taking Beck's situation one minute at a time, which made it sound serious since he avoided the classic "one day at a time" standard answer. Beck did not participate in practice on Friday while the coaching staff is waiting to see how the young signalcaller responds to treatment. The rookie QB was previously sidelined with back spasms earlier in the season.

"We're not gonna shut the door on him, but we're gonna proceed as if Josh [Johnson] is gonna be starting on Sunday."

Beck was taken in Round 3 of this year's NFL draft and basically was the emergency quarterback for the majority of the year. Then Jacoby Brissett was shipped off, and suddenly Beck was the backup to Gardner Minshew against the Seattle Seahawks. Minshew became injured in that game and then was placed on IR after surgery to repair his () in his throwing arm.

Which meant, the next man up. Beck started against the Rams as the Cardinals offensive line did not provide much protection and sacked Beck five times, which could just as easily been double digit numbers. Beck finished going 11 for 18 for 108 yards and two picks, of which one was the receiver's fault.

Johnson is 40-years old. He was a fifth-round selection of the Tampa Bay Buccaneers way back in 2008. The Cardinals are his 17th NFL team. He has played for three clubs twice, plus was a roster member of an UFL team, an AAF franchise, and the XFL where he was the passer rating leader. He played in five games for Washington in 2025 with two starts.

Beck was seven years old when Johnson was drafted by Tampa Bay. Johnson has always been the backup, or the backup to the backup. He has played in just 50 NFL games with a mere 11 starts. His total career passing yards is 2,669, what most starters eclipse in a single season. Johnson has a career 58.7% completion ratio with 94 rushing attempts for 481 yards, so he is mobile.

Which means he has experience. If Beck can't go, rookie Joey Aguilar will become the backup with WR/KR Devin Duvernay the emergency QB.
