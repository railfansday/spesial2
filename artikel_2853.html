Former IVC volleyball star returning to Purdue team for fifth year

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Purdue volleyball All-American announced via social media on Friday, Nov. 27 that she will be back for her fifth season. Wollard, a three-time Journal Star Player of the Year, missed all of the 2026 season due to treatment for Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma.

"I'll be back for my fifth year, and I'm so grateful I get to say that!!!!" Wollard posted on Instagram. "Thank you to everyone who's been in my corner. I love playing for Purdue, and I'm ready to give this program and my teammates everything I have next season."

The 2023 Illinois Valley Central grad had revealed in October that her treatments for Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma were working and she was on track for remission. She was a first team All-Big Ten and second team All-American pick following the 2025 season. That season she posted 577 kills — third-most in program history.

Wollard will be joined by Taylor Anderson, Big Ten all-conference first team, and Dior Charles, who also will be coming back for their fifth years.

Purdue ended its regular season with a loss to top-ranked Nebraska in the Big Ten Conference Tournament semifinal. The Boilermakers, who have a 20-8 overall record, await their NCAA Tournament fate with Sunday's selection show.
