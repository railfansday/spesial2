7 Mystery Shows That Are 10/10 Masterpieces

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

There are good mystery shows, and then there are genius mystery shows that transcend the simple whodunnit puzzle and become true masterpieces. These shows are simply great because they don’t just focus on the murder and the mechanics thereof, but prioritize character, theme, and structure over crime. A masterfully written, acted, and designed mystery television series would compel its audience to care deeply about its people and their stories, becoming a part of the mystery and wanting to resolve it before it unfolds in the finale.

In simpler words, a masterpiece mystery show ought to feel like reading a visual book, like how ITV’s Poirot gave life and form to Agatha Christie’s famous Belgian detective and his adventures. Similarly, shows like Only Murders in the Building earned the attention and affection of their fans with their deeply felt characters and charming atmosphere. From sci-fi mysteries to complex crimes and grisly murders, here’s our selection of mystery shows that are 10/10 masterpieces.

A modern reimagining of Sir Arthur Conan Doyle’s Sherlock Holmes stories created by Steven Moffat and Mark Gatiss, BBC’s Sherlock transports the legendary Victorian-era detective to 2010s London, reimagined as a self-proclaimed high-functioning sociopath, completely stripped of the grace and charm of the traditional Englishman. In the show, Sherlock makes the most of the latest technology and his eccentric mind to solve unsolvable cases for Scotland Yard, alongside his roommate/friend, Dr. John Watson. Benedict Cumberbatch and Martin Freeman star as the modern Sherlock and Watson, roles that made them pop culture icons.

BBC’s Sherlock is a brilliant modern update on the 19th-century literary landmark. By swapping the Victorian setting with 21st-century London and combining classic mysteries with inventive visual design and a standout cast, the show thrills and awes with every case. While most of the cases are adaptations of originals, their reimagined visual and narrative styles make each mystery feel new, even for ardent fans. The show’s fantastic cinematography, perfectly directed action scenes, and the unmatched chemistry between Cumberbatch and Freeman make Sherlock one of the best screen translations of Conan Doyle’s literary classics.

Agatha Christie’s Poirot, or simply Poirot, is a serialized adaptation of Christie’s crime fiction novels and short stories featuring the titular detective, played by David Suchet in the show. Created by Clive Exton and Brian Eastman, the series follows the iconic Belgian sleuth, renowned for his meticulous methods and eccentric nature, as he goes around solving various crimes in England and around the world in the early 20th century. Other recurring main characters are portrayed by Hugh Fraser, Phillip Jackson, and Pauline Moran.

Spanning 13 seasons, Agatha Christie’s Poirot adapts almost every Hercule Poirot novel and short story written by Christie, giving life to her bestselling crime novels and cementing them with a visual form. The show also immortalizes the persona of the famous fictional detective with David Suchet’s pitch-perfect depiction of Hercule Poirot and his unique mannerisms, which have become a defining image of the character. A mystery television landmark, Agatha Christie’s Poirot rarely attempts to reinvent the original, instead emphasizing the darker psychology of the novels through gorgeous, well-acted productions.

Netflix’s first German original series, Dark is a time-and-space-defying sci-fi mystery thriller that follows the sudden disappearance of children in a small German town and explores how it sends four families on a desperate search for answers. In the aftermath of a teenager’s disappearance, an investigation reveals the dark secrets about the town and a time travel conspiracy that spans generations. The main characters are played by Louis Hofmann, Karoline Eichhorn, Lisa Vicari, Maja Schöne, and Moritz Jahn.

With its intricate writing and atmospheric visuals, Dark goes beyond standard crime thrillers and operates more like a puzzle. It transforms a simple missing-person’s case into a complex, multi-generational, multidimensional mystery involving time travel, determinism, and interconnected family tragedy. A fine balance of hard sci-fi and profound philosophical conversations, Dark is intense and immersive in every episode and is widely considered a modern thriller classic.

Britain makes more detective television than any country has any business making, and somehow almost all of it is good. Here is the one you belong in, what it says about you, and the case for it being the best of the four.

You want the tea, the gossip and the vicar behaving oddly. Midsomer is a county where the scenery is beautiful, the fete is on Saturday and the murder rate is roughly that of a small war, and absolutely nobody involved thinks this is strange.

You feel other people's grief as if it were handed to you personally. Broadchurch is less a whodunnit than a study of what a small town does to itself when it finds out that the answer was always going to be somebody everybody already knew.

You go through things rather than around them. John Luther is the best detective in London and a liability to everybody who works with him, and the show is completely honest that those two facts are the same fact.

You say the thing and you do not dress it up. Vera Stanhope turns up in a hat and a mac, is rude to almost everybody, and is the only person in the room prepared to ask the question that the family has been waiting years for somebody to ask.

One of the most iconic and defining crime dramas of the 2010s, Chris Chibnall's Broadchurch stars David Tennant and Olivia Colman as detectives Alec Hardy and Ellie Miller, who solve two brutal crimes in the titular English coastal town. In the first two seasons of Broadchurch, the duo solves the mysterious death of an 11-year-old boy named Danny and the involvement of the community, and in the second, they investigate the rape of a local woman, as well as the aftermath of Danny’s death. The show also stars Jodie Whittaker, Andrew Buchan, Carolyn Pickles, and Jonathan Bailey in notable roles.

Anchored by Tennant and Colman's affecting performances and the stunning cinematography, Broadchurch ranks among the most outstanding British mystery shows of our times. It defies the classic mystery traditions by contrasting its cozy setting with brutal crimes, elevating the simple whodunit structure into a profound study of grief, trauma, and a fractured community. Gripping and often emotionally devastating, Broadchurch is a pure masterpiece of British mystery television with intense and well-crafted storytelling.

Since its premiere, Apple TV’s Severance has quickly become a sci-fi mystery favorite and one of the most successful shows in the genre. Created by Dan Erickson and Ben Stiller, it revolves around an elite and enigmatic biotech company where employees are subjected to the titular procedure that separates their consciousness between personal and professional life, until Mark, a newer employee, smells a conspiracy. Adam Scott, Britt Lower, Patricia Arquette, John Turturro, and Christopher Walken star as the main characters amid a recurring ensemble cast.

Severance is not just a high-concept sci-fi mystery, but also a black comedy, absurdist satire, and a psychological thriller that revisits the classic sinister corporation trope through a dystopian lens. Dark and surreal, the show intrigues episode after episode with its narrative style, stylish aesthetics, and terrific cast, headlined by Adam Scott’s award-winning performance as Mark. With its intelligent script, sharp satire, and incredible production values, Severance is undoubtedly one of the finest sci-fi shows made in the 2020s.

A dark comedy-drama anthology created, written, and directed by Mark White, The White Lotus follows mysterious events surrounding the staff and guests at the titular luxury global resort chain. Set in a different exotic vacation destination, each season tells a new story involving a (largely) new set of eccentric characters and a mysterious, unexpected death. The series features a massive rotating ensemble of TV and film’s finest, including the likes of Tom Hollander, Parker Posey, and Molly Shannon, with Jennifer Coolidge and Natasha Rothwell appearing in more than one season.

An HBO original with no bad seasons, The White Lotus is unlike any traditional whodunit or howcatchem, as the murder is more of an afterthought in the storytelling and serves more as a dark punchline to the season’s twisted plot. The juxtaposition of the story’s idyllic, picturesque setting in every season against a shocking murder and sharp social commentary is what makes this show distinctive. Three seasons and 10 Emmy Awards later, The White Lotus is a unicorn of its genre, defying and bending the rules of mystery drama TV as we know it.

With five wonderful seasons and counting, Steve Martin and John Hoffman’s quirky, colorful crime comedy series is undeniably one of the best TV mysteries of the 2020s. Starring Martin, Martin Short, and Selena Gomez, Only Murders in the Building follows their misfit trio of neighbors — Charles, Oliver, and Mabel — whose shared passion for murder mysteries brings them together to solve the wildest murders right in their own New York apartment building, making a podcast while they’re at it. The show has become popular for its stellar ensemble cast every season, which includes the likes of Meryl Streep, Paul Rudd, Tina Fey, Nathan Lane, and Richard Kind.

While Only Murders has occasionally taken the trio out of New York City and the comfort of their apartment, every season has successfully retained the core essence of the series, which is pretty rare in multi-season series. The Hulu original has broken several records since its premiere in 2021 and swept accolades every season, including several Emmys and Golden Globes. The show beautifully balances complex crimes with a wholesome narrative about friendship, love, and life in general, which is what really makes it so worthwhile.
