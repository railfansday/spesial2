We Bought Licences for Our Summer Yard Staff on a Yearly Term and Paid for Them Until March

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Builders' merchants are seasonal. From April to September we take on around a hundred and seventy temporary yard staff, drivers' mates and counter assistants, and each of them needs a Microsoft 365 account for email, rotas, training and the handheld apps that sign in with it.

For years we bought those licences month to month and cancelled them in the autumn. In 2022 Microsoft moved its business subscriptions to new commercial terms. A licence bought for a year became cheaper than one bought month by month, by about a fifth, but it could only be reduced in the first seven days after purchase or renewal. After that, the number is fixed until the year ends.

Our licence reseller recommended the yearly term to save money, and the procurement team, sensibly, agreed. The extra licences were bought in March, alongside our annual renewal. In October we tried to remove a hundred and seventy of them and were told we could not until the following March.

So we paid for six months of licences that nobody was using, about seven thousand pounds. It is not a fortune, but it was money spent to save money, and it happened because the people choosing the term knew the price and not the pattern of our year. HR knew we hire seasonally. My team knew which licences the seasonal staff needed. Procurement saw a cheaper line on a quote.

We now buy licences in two layers. The number we need all year is on yearly terms and renewed once. The seasonal top up is bought month to month, which costs more per licence and can be cancelled when the season ends. Since April 2025, paying monthly for a yearly licence also costs more, so we pay the yearly ones in one go. Every licence purchase now records which layer it belongs to, and HR's seasonal hiring plan comes to us in January rather than when the first starter is waiting for an account.

We then checked our other software contracts and found two more with the same kind of term.

A discount for commitment is only a discount if you were going to need the thing for the whole commitment. Before choosing a term, ask who knows how many you will need in October.
