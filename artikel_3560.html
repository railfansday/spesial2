'Nobody Wants This' Star Reveals If She's Spoken to Adam Brody Since Drama

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Nobody Wants This star Tovah Feldshuh revealed what Adam Brody told her off camera after drama surrounding his public support for Palestine.

“After he said his comments, he asked to see me. So we’ve talked privately about it,” Feldshuh, 77, exclusively told Us ahead of PaleyImpact’s “One-on-One With Tovah Feldshuh” panel at the Paley Museum in New York City on Monday, November 23.

Feldshuh clarified there was “no animosity” between her and Brody, 46, adding, “But [there is] profound disagreement. We’ll see, hopefully, if he will — based on fact and history — change his point of view.”

The actress stood by her perspective on the situation.

“I’ve never directly addressed Adam in public, nor will I. We are colleagues, so we can disagree without being disagreeable,” she explained. “However, I’m sure everybody is very clear about how I stand and this isn’t a political statement. This is a statement based on whether or not I have a right to live.”

Feldshuh concluded: “I’m not Israeli and neither is [Nobody Wants This creator] Erin Foster. But fortunately for me, she agrees with me and that’s lucky. I will not cross swords with Adam. He plays my son, and you know what else? He’s been an incredible friend to me. When they showed my documentary at the temple that hosted us, who came out of all the cast? Adam Brody. He sat next to me.”

Brody, who plays a rabbi on the hit Netflix series, recently made a rare comment about his political views when he voiced his support for Palestine amid the ongoing conflict with Israel.

“I have spoken a lot about it privately. I haven’t spoken much about it publicly,” he told GQ in October. “Put me down as thinking it’s a genocide. Put me down as wanting a free Palestine.”

He continued: “It’s awful that it’s taken so much death and misery, but it does feel like it’s reaching a breaking point that it should have a while ago. I’m hopeful that another administration can really do some good and correct some of these policies that are helping that situation be as awful as it is.”

In response, Foster, who converted to Judaism in 2019, spoke out about their differing perspectives.

“I think our work environment, our set is very similar to dinner tables around the world where people are having debate and discussion and disagreeing,” she said on the “Not Skinny But Not Fat” podcast at the time. “While I love Adam, I also disagree with him. And that’s OK.”

Feldshuh also spoke out after her onscreen son’s comments made headlines.

“To accuse the Jewish people of genocide is wild,” the actress told The New York Post in October. “It’s right out of [Adolf] Hitler’s playbook. You take the victim and you make the victim the perpetrator.”

After Nobody Wants This was renewed for season 4, Feldshuh later clarified that there were no issues on set. “Tensions are NOT RISING,” she wrote via Threads. “Profound differences of opinion are. Please do not conflate the two — And none of it ￼will be brought to the set. I deeply enjoy filming NWT and hope we are chosen to go on for a fourth season.”

Feldshuh spoke to Us on Monday before joining a panel hosted by Judy Gold highlighting how she has celebrated Jewish identity and community throughout her career as part of Paley’s ongoing series, Media’s Role in Combating Antisemitism.

“There is nothing more dangerous than an ignorant mind and an uncurious mind,” she told Us. “So don’t get swept away by group speak. Go look at books and go study. After all, I love being Jewish. We’re people who kiss books.”

Nobody Wants This is streaming on Netflix.
