Mormon Wives' Mayci Neeley 'Embarrassed' Over 'F*** Them Up' Text Message

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Mayci Neeley is taking accountability for her actions.

The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives star caused a stir during season 1 of spinoff show The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County when she inserted herself into drama between her sister and Mormon Wives OC cast member, McCall DaPron, and costar Bobbi Althoff.

In a text message shown on screen, Mayci, 31, wrote to friend Jen Affleck, “These girls making my sister… I’m gonna f*** them up.”

But in a video shared via her Instagram Stories on Tuesday, November 17, the Babymama cofounder admitted that her text to Jen, 27, wasn’t appropriate.

“To be honest, I’m embarrassed,” Mayci said. “I’m genuinely embarrassed that I said that. I don’t remember the intention behind it because I know I wasn’t trying to get into a street fight, like a physical fight. It’s genuinely embarrassing. I don’t know why the hell I said that.”

The mom-of-three continued, “I was just seeing red in that moment and I said that. I said that to a friend over text. Did I know that Jen was going to go tell everyone I said that? No. Should I have said that? Absolutely not.”

Mayci added, “I am very sorry I said that. I did regret saying that. That is definitely out of character for me. … I’m just going to be accountable for the things that I was upset with myself for. I’ve apologized for it, and moving on.”

Mayci’s text to Jen became a point of conversation on an episode of Mormon Wives OC, which dropped its first season Thursday, November 12, on Hulu.

Cast member Salomé Andrea commented, “Is that a joke? Like, I’m not dealing with that. Are we 15?”

“Personally, it feels very middle school-ish, but I think that’s what Mayci’s used to,” Salomé, 34, further shared in a confessional. “She’s not part of this group, so she should stick to her own MomTok.”

Jen reasoned with the girls that “Mayci wants this to be a good experience for her sister,” referring to McCall, 36, noting that it’s the first time “she’s doing something for herself outside of being a mom, and so I think she’s just protective.”

In her own confessional, Bobbi, 29, said, “What the f*** is this message from Mayci? That is so dramatic.”

Bobbi recently called on fans to “stop bullying McCall,” with whom she clashed during season 1 of Mormon Wives OC.

“I’m going to need all you people to stop bullying McCall,” the podcaster wrote via her Instagram Stories on Saturday, November 14. “It’s really out of control. I understand she made some mistakes, but your response to calling her a mean girl is being mean? It’s not necessary.”
