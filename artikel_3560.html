Jenna Johnson Admits She Was 'A Little Taken Back' by 'DWTS' Elimination

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Jenna Johnson is still in shock after being eliminated from Dancing With the Stars in the semifinals with partner Harry Shum Jr.

“You know what? It’s very bittersweet,” Johnson, 32, told Us exclusively at the L’Oréal Paris Women of Worth Celebration on Wednesday, November 18. “I think this season — you never know what’s going to happen, I think we’ve seen that from the very beginning. It’s so exciting that it’s so celebrated. Everyone’s getting to watch dance, but you never know what’s going to happen, and so last night, obviously, I think I was just like a little taken back. But I am so incredibly grateful for my relationship and partnership with Harry, and that goes far beyond these just three months of Dancing With the Stars.”

Johnson said she “will cherish” the friendship she built with Shum, 44, “forever” despite their elimination.

“That far outweighs any of the heartache I’m feeling right now of not being in the finals and getting to do a freestyle,” she added.

Johnson and Shum were early frontrunners when season 35 premiered in September, but their DWTS journey was cut short just one week before the finale. After receiving perfect scores for both of their semifinal dances on Tuesday, November 17, the pair were ultimately sent home.

While speaking with Us, Johnson opened up about how Shum reacted in the moment. “He kind of just held me,” she said. “He feels like a brother, like a long lost brother. … He just said to me, like, ‘This is not the last time we will create art together,’ and I firmly believe that.”

Johnson hinted that she’s “excited” about the possibility of doing another project with Shum in the future. “I think also getting to work and collaborate with one another for three months, we’ve learned so much about how each other works, and I think we work really well together,” she added. “So I don’t think this is the last of Harry and Jenna.”

The ballroom pro also pointed out that “a lot of people” have been asking her and Shum to film their freestyle like Mark Ballas and Whitney Leavitt did after their season 34 elimination.

Following Tuesday’s results, Shum reflected on the online chatter surrounding DWTS this season, claiming “there’s a good amount of bullying” from fans on social media. Johnson weighed in on Wednesday, noting that there are “a lot more eyes” on the show now.

“I guess it’s to be expected,” she said of the criticism. “I wish it could be a bit more positive and just celebrate how much effort all of these beautiful celebrities are putting into their dances each week. So I think that’s my biggest wish, is that it can go back to the focus of the effort, the hard work, the blood, sweat and tears … and what this journey is meant to be about.”

Johnson also opened up about facing the critics with her husband, Val Chmerkovskiy, by her side. (Earlier this season, Chmerkovskiy, 40, was briefly suspended from DWTS after making a controversial remark toward judge Carrie Ann Inaba.)

“I think because he is such a big support system for me, I am able to, like, get through it or get past it,” she told Us. “It is very wild to work with your spouse, but in these moments, it is really beautiful because we get to lean on each other and we know what each other are experiencing, so that is the beauty of it.”

Johnson added, “He is my biggest cheerleader and always lifts me up.”

Being uplifted was a central theme of Wednesday’s event. For 21 years, L’Oréal Paris Women of Worth has recognized extraordinary women leading grassroots nonprofit organizations and creating meaningful change in communities across the country. The brand hosted its annual celebration to honor this year’s 10 inspiring honorees, who each received a $25,000 grant in partnership with Points of Light.

“I feel so lucky to have some incredible supporters, especially women, in my life. … First of all, my castmates, the female pros. I feel like we really rally behind each other, but also I have two sisters who are my world,” Johnson told Us. “They have raised me, trained me in dance, and so I feel like they’ve shaped a lot of my life. Obviously, my mom is a huge supporter. You know, especially after last night, they were in the ballroom, and so I think just having that support, that love, means everything to me.”

Dancing With the Stars airs on ABC and Disney+ Tuesdays at 8 p.m. ET.
