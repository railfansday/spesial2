Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Re: Roth account or stepped-up basis for inheritance?

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Generally speaking, leaving taxable with step-up basis would be a better than roth
Let's say you have 20k each in taxable and roth, and you have to spend 10k today before dying tomorrow
Assuming the tax basis for the 20k taxable is 10k

Case 1: Spend Roth, no tax due, 20k taxable and 10k Roth passed to heir
Worst case, heir holds onto the money for 10 more years, it becomes 40k taxable and 20k Roth, pay 20k x 15% = 3k for CapGain Tax, net 57k

Case 2: Spend taxable, in order to get 10k, you have to sell 10.81k worth of stock, 9.19k taxable and 20k roth passed to heir
Best case: heir holds onto the money for 10 more years, it becomes 18.38k taxable and 40k roth, pay 9.19k x 15% = 1379 in CapGain Tax, net 57k

If the heir has to use any money before the 10-year is up, the result favors case 1, also if the cost base in your taxable is less than half, it will also favor case 1.

Only when your cost base in taxable is higher than half and the heir hold onto it for the full 10 years will case 2 come out ahead.
