You didn't state what your intentions are for the money. It doesn't sound like you plan on spending it all. Do you have heirs or is it going to charity or something else?

If you have charitable intentions for at least some of the money, it might make sense to NOT convert some amount.

I get that no one likes paying taxes or IRMAA or anything else that leaves them with less. I too was very upset about IRMAA when I first heard about it (still don't like it or how it is implemented, but do understand it better now). Even at the top IRMAA rate, the total fee doesn't cover the total cost. It might be more palatable if instead of IRMAA, there was a discount like the way ACA handles it. Of course, that might be worse given the hoops some people go through to avoid the various levels for ACA subsidies.

The other thing that helped me is to remember way back when I started saving money in tax-deferred accounts and the big discount I got on my taxes for making a deal with the government to share in any profits. Of course I started making that deal decades ago. The government is very patient, but still expects it's due with interest/gains when the bill finally comes due.

There was a poster here a little while ago extolling the virtues of reinvesting capital gains and dividends and how his portfolio was up so much, but most of it had a high basis. He was happy and didn't care about tax drag, because his portfolio was up. It felt like he had won (and to be clear he had done very well and felt good about it, so he really did win) but he probably would have had a higher portfolio balance had he invested more tax efficiently.

Tax efficiency is great, doing it really well is hard after the fact. It sounds like you have a lot of unrealized gains that have a tax liability. The only way you can change that tax liability is to gift the assets so the liability moves to someone else. If that someone is a charity they don't owe anything. You don't get to "spend" the money but you do get to do good with it.

So, figure out your intentions for the money. Is it to not pay taxes on it? Is it to have the most money to spend (over what timeframe)? Is it something else?
