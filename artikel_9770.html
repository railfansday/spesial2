I built a free pet memorial page with a small three.js garden that stays smooth on phones

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

I made Bridgelight, a free, ad-free memorial page for a pet. Each pet gets one page: photos, a short video, letters, candles friends can light without an account, and a small 3D garden where the pet sits with you. Here is what went into the garden, since that was the hard part.

The dog is procedural, not a downloaded model: an adult skeleton (three-joint hind legs), shell fur rendered as stacked layers, and a few behaviours (wander, pant, sit by the bench). Lesson learned the hard way: big head, big eyes and round proportions read as a toy. Adult proportions read as a pet.

A frame-time watchdog steps quality down in order: render resolution, then grass density to 55%, then shadows off, then grass to 25%. Desktop pixel ratio is capped at 1.5. A garden with 20 visitors went from about 2.4M to 1.35M triangles after capping visitor count and sharing geometry.

Public, link-only or private per memorial. Candles and letters need no account. If you lose the secret link, recovery goes by email, twice a day at most.

Happy to answer questions about the rendering or the choices above. The garden is at https://www.bridgelight.pet/?utm_source=devto
