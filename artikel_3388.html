All 50 Episodes Of Apple TV’s Space Sci-Fi Masterpiece Are Perfect From Start To Finish

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

All 50 episodes of Apple TV's space sci-fi masterpiece deliver an extraordinary alternate-history adventure that's perfect from start to finish. Following its premiere back in 2019, this acclaimed sci-fi series has become one of Apple TV's all-time streaming franchises and has featured one of the best ensemble casts on the platform. With its elite story execution, rich worldbuilding, and thoughtful character development, this is one sci-fi series on Apple TV that you don't want to miss, and it can truly be called a modern streaming masterpiece.

For All Mankind is one of Apple TV's most universally celebrated sci-fi series that deserves its masterpiece status. Spanning five seasons and 50 episodes, with a sixth on the way and the spinoff, Star City, already released earlier this year, For All Mankind delivers peak sci-fi throughout its entire multi-generational alternate history narrative, led by elite performances by Joel Kinnaman, Michael Dorman, Coral Pena, and more. With its near-perfect 91% Rotten Tomatoes score, For All Mankind belongs in the same prestigious league as Apple TV's other masterful sci-fi hits like Severance and Silo.

In the same vein as other alternate history series like The Man in the High Castle, For All Mankind is built on the historical fantasy premise in which the Soviet Union beat the United States in the 1969 Space Race to the Moon. After Soviet cosmonaut Alexei Leonov becomes the first person to walk on the Moon and defeats NASA's Apollo 11 mission, the entire geopolitical landscape shifts greatly, forcing the United States into a compromising position as they look to regain their presence in outer space.

Losing the Space Race actually accelerates the American space program, which launches an epic sci-fi saga that serves as the foundation of For All Mankind. Rather than setting the new global standard for space missions, the U.S. follows the lead of the Soviets, which includes recruiting more female astronauts and building permanent infrastructure on the moon. The series explores how these alternate historical events reshape American politics and culture, even down to the fabric of everyday life, which creates a fascinating viewing experience.

What's more, For All Mankind goes even further than Moon operations in its later seasons and eventually expands to Mars, adding another compelling angle to the epic space sci-fi saga. Essentially, losing the initial Space Race catapults technological advancement in the United States' space program, so much so that the Space Race never actually ends. By the time season 5 arrives, a large colony has been firmly established on Mars in the year 2012, sparking interplanetary conflict as seen in other exceptional sci-fi masterpieces like The Expanse.

The imaginative premise of For All Mankind plants a seed that develops into a complete revision of 20th-century American history, affecting everything from the Cold War to the militarization of off-planet assets. By season 2, the Moon becomes somewhat of an active war zone, which enables scientists and astronauts to actually bear arms as an imperialistic conflict develops. Determined to recover from losing the race to the Moon, the United States shifts its gears to rapidly inhabit Mars, which only thickens the plot and raises the stakes in the series.

The sixth and final season of For All Mankind will jump into the 2020s and chronicle the growing situation on Mars, following the first human ever born off Earth. Because Apple TV has confirmed that For All Mankind will be the last, viewers can expect a conclusive ending to the decades-long space race portrayed in the series, likely bringing the series to even new heights in terms of stakes and tension. Given all this, the current five seasons of For All Mankind should be considered must-see TV for all space sci-fi fans ahead of its 2027 finale run on Apple TV, as each new season is better than the one before it.
