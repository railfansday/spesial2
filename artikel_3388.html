Established Artists Signs Actress & Former Pure Flix Entertainment Co-Owner Andrea Logan

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

EXCLUSIVE: Andrea Logan, the actress, podcaster and former co-owner of Pure Flix Entertainment, has signed with Established Artists.

This comes as she launches Season 2 of her podcast, The A-List with Andrea Logan, with guests from film, music, faith, music, wellness and culture just confirmed for the run.

They include I Can Only Imagine producer Cindy Bond, actor Josh Murray, The Voice Season 10 winner Alison Porter and songwriter Nitanee Paris.

Logan’s acting credits include Moms’ Night Out, God’s Not Dead, The Space Between Us, Revelation Road, Double Threat and Christmas Manger and she was a series regular on Malibu Dan and Hitting the Breaks. Pure Flix, the faith-based streaming service she co-founded with her former husband, David A.R. White, and others, was sold to Sony Pictures in a $100M deal in 2020. This marked one of the largest M&A deals in faith-based media M&A and remains the case. Watch on Deadline

For Established Artists, the signing comes days after that of British model and Geek Girl actress Bella Glanville.

In recent months, it has also added actress, writer, director and producer Natasha Ofili, who acted in Creed III and The Politician, and Anne-Marie MacKay, veteran writer-producer and co-founder of Propaganda Films, for management.

Patrick Havern launched Established Artists in 2021 as a full service talent management company.
