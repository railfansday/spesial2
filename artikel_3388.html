Director David Leitch's new movie is shaping up to be a major box-office underperformer, receiving the weakest reviews of the filmmaker's career. Leitch served as an uncredited co-director on the original John Wick movie and went on to chart his own path in Hollywood, leaving his former creative collaborator Chad Stahelski to oversee the John Wick franchise. After going solo, Leitch directed the hit franchise films Deadpool 2 and Fast & Furious Presents: Hobbs & Shaw. However, his subsequent films have been rather hit-or-miss.

In 2022, he directed the action-thriller Bullet Train, starring Brad Pitt at the helm of an eclectic ensemble that also included Aaron Taylor-Johnson, Brian Tyree Henry, Joey King, Andrew Koji, and others. Produced on a reported budget of $90 million, the film grossed around $240 million worldwide at the box office and received a 53% score on the aggregator website Rotten Tomatoes. Two years later, Leitch released the acclaimed action-comedy The Fall Guy, an homage to his previous career as a stunt performer and coordinator.

Starring Emily Blunt and Ryan Gosling, the film grossed around $180 million worldwide against a reported budget of $150 million. Although it reportedly lost Universal $50 million, The Fall Guy received an 81% score on Rotten Tomatoes. The reception for Leitch's latest film, a heist comedy starring Nicholas Hoult, has been considerably worse.

We're talking, of course, about How to Rob a Bank, which debuted theatrically alongside Johnny Depp's comeback vehicle, Ebenezer, this week. The film holds a 35% score on Rotten Tomatoes, where the consensus reads, "Frenetic entertainment with a lot on its mind, How to Rob a Bank fights the law but ultimately gets busted less so for its provocative ideas and more for its inability to make them cohere into a satisfying message."

While Ebenezer topped the domestic box-office chart with more than $20 million, How to Rob a Bank underperformed with less than $5 million against a reported budget of $40 million. This is about as much as Jason Statham's recent films have cost to make. Incidentally, Leitch is working with the action icon on his next film, Jason Statham Stole My Bike. Also starring Zoë Kravitz, Anna Sawai, John C. Reilly, and Pete Davidson, How to Rob a Bank is playing in theaters. It's the second major heist movie of the year after the star-studded Crime 101, which underperformed with $73 million against a reported budget of $90 million.
