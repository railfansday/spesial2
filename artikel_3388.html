Harry Potter’s New Era Begins With Major Release Days After HBO Premiere

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

HBO's upcoming Harry Potter reboot series is getting brand-new releases exactly one week after its debut.

LEGO has been producing Harry Potter-themed products since 2001, when the franchise made its big-screen debut with Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone. While previous sets have featured characters, buildings, and memorable moments from the movies, the upcoming releases are specifically based on HBO's adaptation of JK Rowling's popular book series.

HBO's upcoming Harry Potter TV series is planning ahead. The franchise is officially expanding its reach with new merchandise ahead of its highly anticipated premiere. LEGO has officially revealed four new sets inspired by the adaptation, giving fans an opportunity to recreate some of the most recognizable locations and characters from the Wizarding World. Check out the sets below:

The latest additions to the LEGO Harry Potter collection are scheduled to launch on January 1, 2027, seven days after the HBO series premieres on December 25, 2026. Two of the four newly announced items are already available to pre-order on LEGO's official website. However, it is unclear when the other two sets will become available for pre-order.

The first season of HBO's Harry Potter will adapt the first novel of the series. The new LEGO sets will feature several locations and characters who will be introduced in the HBO show's television series, which will depict Harry's first year at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.

LEGO Harry Potter: Privet Drive recreates the house where Harry grew up with his Aunt Petunia, Uncle Vernon, and cousin Dudley, before discovering that he is a wizard. The build features the Dursley family's home, including the small cupboard under the staircase where Harry slept for most of his childhood. The set costs $59.99 and is currently available for preorder.

Also available for preorder is LEGO Harry Potter: Gryffindor Common Room, which costs $89.99. The set brings one of Hogwarts' most iconic locations to life. It includes a fireplace and five minifigures. It also incorporates a Quidditch from HBO's adaptation, making it different from previous LEGO sets from the collection.

The most expensive product of the lineup is LEGO Harry Potter: Hogwarts Express & Platform 9¾. Selling for $99.99, the set recreates the famous train that students take to get to Hogwarts. It also includes minifigures of Harry, Hermione Granger, Ron Weasely, Molly Weasley, and Draco Malfoy.

Finally, the LEGO BrickHeadz: Harry, Draco, and Hagrid figures were also revealed. Unlike the other three releases, this product focuses on buildable figures instead of recreating a particular setting from the TV series. The BrickHeadz collection will cost $24.99, making it the least expensive set.
