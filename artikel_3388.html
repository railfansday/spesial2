‘Home Alone’ Sequel With Macaulay Culkin In Early Works With Seth Rogen & Evan Goldberg Writing & Producing

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Ever since Macaulay Culkin pitched his idea for a Home Alone sequel to Disney Motion Pictures Boss David Greenbaum, that being from an older dad’s POV, there’s been some heat on a continuation of the Christmas comedy franchise which has been a gold mine for 20th Century Studios since its inception 36 years ago.

Culkin, we heard, spoke with Greenbaum over the summer after there was some buzz online about him returning. As a father now, Culkin had an interesting take as to where his Kevin McCallister would go now that he’s older and wiser, having survived abandonment and burglars as a child.

We’ve confirmed with sources that a sequel starring Culkin and with Point Grey‘s Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg writing and producing is in early works at the Disney owned 20th Century Studios. The duo are working with Culkin on his idea. Watch on Deadline

Chris Columbus directed both the first movie in 1990 and the sequel Home Alone 2: Lost in New York which combined grossed $836.3M. No word if he’s coming back yet. A 1997 threequel sans Culkin was made taking the franchise to $915.7M WW. Home Alone was back on the Nielsen top 10 streaming movies chart this week at No. 9 with 177M minutes viewed on Disney+.

Disney declined to comment on the Home Alone news.
