Pandora is raising its 2026 sales forecast ahead of the holiday season

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Pandora lifted the low end of its 2026 organic growth range to 2% from 0% and updated its 2027 margin target

Pandora narrowed its 2026 guidance toward the upper end of its previous ranges on Wednesday, lifting the low end of its organic sales growth forecast to 2% from 0%. The update comes as the company heads into the festive shopping season, according to The Wall Street Journal.

The jewelry maker expects 2026 organic growth of 2% to 3% and an EBIT margin of 22.5% to 23%. The previous ranges were 0% to 3% for organic growth and 22% to 23% for the margin.

Pandora stock climbed 8.7% in early trading across Europe.

Third-quarter revenue was 6.69 billion kroner, up from 6.27 billion kroner a year earlier, the company said. Organic growth was 6%, made up of 4% like-for-like growth and 2% from network expansion and other factors. Operating profit (EBIT) was 1.06 billion kroner, compared with 880 million kroner a year earlier. The EBIT margin was 15.9%, up from 14.0%.

Revenue came in above the 6.58 billion kroner analysts were expecting on average, based on a company-supplied survey, according to Reuters.

A one-off partial refund on Pandora's U.S. tariff claim added 370 basis points to the quarter's EBIT margin and 340 basis points to the gross margin, which was 83.7%. The underlying EBIT margin was little changed from a year earlier.

Like-for-like growth was 3% in North America, 1% in Europe, the Middle East and Africa, 20% in Asia-Pacific and 6% in Latin America. Like-for-like growth in October was 1%, which the company said was hurt by lower promotional activity. It put underlying trends at around 3% like-for-like growth.

"Q3 delivered 4% LFL growth, with positive LFL across all regions, an encouraging step in a challenging consumer environment," CEO Berta de Pablos-Barbier said in a statement.

Pandora updated its 2027 EBIT margin target to 14% to 15% including one-offs and 15.5% to 16.5% excluding one-offs. The previous targets were above 12% and above 14%, respectively.

A pilot of platinum-plated jewelry that began in the Netherlands in July has been expanded to markets including the U.S., U.K. and Australia, the company said. Pandora said the broader switch to platinum plating remains on schedule to begin in 2027.
