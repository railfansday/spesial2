Steelers place Mason McCormick on injured reserve

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Steelers got good news regarding offensive lineman Mason McCormick early Wednesday, with the starting left guard suffering a dislocated ankle instead of a fracture — avoiding surgery after a gruesome-looking injury on Sunday versus the Bengals.

However, McCormick will still be missing the Steelers' next four games at minimum, with the team placing him on injured reserve Wednesday, Pittsburgh announced.

With McCormick now on IR, the Steelers have an opening on their 53-man roster. After Pittsburgh opened the 21-day practice window for wide receivers Michael Pittman Jr. and Roman Wilson, as well as rookie guard Gennings Dunker, earlier in the day, the team could leave the vacant spot available for one of those three players' eventual activation.

Steelers utility lineman Spencer Anderson is expected to start at left guard for the team while McCormick misses time. The earliest McCormick can return to the lineup would be the Steelers' Dec. 20 matchup against the Baltimore Ravens, although a report from NFL Network's Ian Rapoport on Wednesday indicated the Steelers hope McCormick can return for the playoffs, which don't begin until mid-January.
