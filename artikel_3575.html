‘High Potential’ Season 3 Officially Sets 2027 Release Date After Major Delay

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Network television's fall slate has yet to run its course, but it's already time to start looking forward to midseason premieres. For ABC, it's expected to be an especially big winter and spring, with some of its heaviest hitters, like The Rookie and Will Trent, returning alongside new spin-offs The Rookie: North and the Texas-based Grey's Anatomy offshoot. Looming largest, however, is High Potential. Television's top-rated drama in the key 18-49 demographic was held back for 2027 for the first time ahead of Season 3, and all eyes are on what's next for Kaitlin Olson's genius detective Morgan Gillory.

While ABC may have delayed High Potential from its usual arrival time, it isn't going to keep fans waiting much longer. The Disney-owned network announced its 2027 premiere dates today, including a much earlier-than-expected launch for Season 3 of the megahit crime procedural. As previously announced, the series will have a special post-Super Bowl episode airing on Valentine's Day, February 14, but it'll first return even sooner on Tuesdays starting on January 5. That date will also mark the overall kick-off of the ABC midseason schedule, with Will Trent leading off the night in the 8 p.m. ET/PT time slot and Surviving a Scandal after High Potential at 10 p.m.

When Morgan and the LAPD Major Crimes unit do return, it'll be under new management. Todd Harthan departed as showrunner after Season 2, leaving the door open for former Poker Face helmers and new Scream 8 writers Nora and Lilla Zuckerman to take the reins. A little extra time off might serve the series well given the high-profile change at the top, but the series is expected to stick to its same winning formula nonetheless. Alongside Olson, Daniel Sunjata, Javicia Leslie, Judy Reyes, Deniz Akdeniz, Amirah J, and Matthew Lamb are all slated to return.

One person who won't be around for High Potential's triumphant return is Steve Howey. Season 2's finale left Captain Wagner's fate in limbo following a brutal stabbing that Morgan discovers upon going to meet up with him in the park. It appears to have been a setup by his father, Wagner Sr., building him up as the next potential big threat facing the department going forward. The series also has yet to properly reveal Morgan's ex-boyfriend and Ava's father, Roman. While the mystery of what happened to him was central to Season 2, the closest viewers got to seeing his face was when a mysterious figure implied to be him showed up at Ava's art show. Expectations are sky-high after two straight runs lauded by critics and audiences alike, boasting some of ABC's highest viewership numbers in years.
