AI Is Both Canva’s Biggest Bet and Its Biggest Threat

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Design platform Canva is looking down the barrel of declining valuation, rocky macroeconomic conditions for the software industry writ large, and intensifying competition from AI.

Despite those headwinds, even as it pushes through a major reorg announced this week, the company could still have a competitive advantage, some experts say.

The company is reshuffling or laying off 22% of its staff, a change that impacts 1,271 people, the company said Tuesday. In a blog post, CEO Melanie Perkins characterized the shakeup as “the hardest moment” in the company’s 13-year history.

As part of the change, Canva will consolidate its product, growth, and platform teams under one division, cut certain specialist positions, and adopt a flatter management structure.

Kendra Barnett is Adweek's senior tech reporter.
