F1 Las Vegas Grand Prix 2026: Celebrity Sightings, the Biggest Parties and Where to Watch, Stay, Shop and Dine (Updating)

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

From Wynn’s Charles Leclerc cafe and Lewis Hamilton’s new pop-up to a Jay-Z-hosted celebration and the Backstreet Boys at Sphere, the world’s flashiest street race electrifies with major moments — plus last-minute deals on tickets, hotels and more.

Formula 1 has already crowned its 2026 world champion, Mercedes driver Kimi Antonelli, but Las Vegas has another race in mind: seeing which famous faces turn up trackside and who throws the party everyone is talking about. When the fourth annual Las Vegas Grand Prix returns to the Strip Nov. 19 through 21, the competition for a place on the podium will share the spotlight with a packed calendar of designer pop-ups, caviar and Champagne-drenched viewing experiences, high-roller megasuites and A-list-hosted afterparties and concerts.

At Wynn, Charles Leclerc’s CL16 takes over Café Al Teatro with Café Leclerc (featuring a bag charm of Leo in a tuxedo in homage to his high-profile pooch), Lando Norris’ LN4 opens a vintage-lounge-inspired Clubhouse and Lewis Hamilton’s +44 returns with another limited-edition collection. The recently reimagined XS features the Chainsmokers headlining Sports Illustrated’s Circuit Series, Calvin Harris taking race night and Rüfüs Du Sol closing the weekend with a DJ set. Jay-Z hosts Roc Nation and Tao Group’s celebration at Omnia Dayclub & Skybar, Kacey Musgraves headlines Uber and Zoox’s invite-only Disco Rodeo party and the Backstreet Boys take the official post-race concert to Sphere. Dominique Crenn, Izu Ani, Tetsuya Wakuda, Tyler Florence and Guy Savoy lead the dining calendar, and the world’s best bars — including New York’s Double Chicken Please — pour the drinks.

The prize? Louis Vuitton has created a bespoke trophy trunk for the Las Vegas Grand Prix, handcrafted at its historic atelier in Asnières-sur-Seine, France. Its signature monogram canvas is crossed with colored Damier strips that form a V in white gold leaf for both “Victory” and “Vuitton” (and also perhaps V for Vegas), while a Victory patch will bear the winner’s initials. The season’s championship may be settled, but whose initials go on the Las Vegas trunk remains an open question.

Below, The Hollywood Reporter rounds up where to stay, dine, shop and watch, along with last-minute deals and the parties and performances worth planning around. Check back for celebrity sightings and updates as race week unfolds.

The Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix takes place Nov. 19 through 21 on the Las Vegas Strip Circuit, with the main race starting Saturday, Nov. 21, at 8 p.m. PT. Thursday brings the first two practice sessions, followed by Friday practice and qualifying.

Race tickets and hospitality packages are available through the Las Vegas Grand Prix website, with options varying by day and viewing area. The Heineken Silver Main Grandstand faces the start-finish line and pit lane, the Lewis Hamilton Grandstand overlooks the approach to the final turn along the Harmon Straight and the T-Mobile Zone at Sphere combines racing with nightly concerts.

For access beyond a grandstand seat, F1 Experiences offers packages that combine hospitality with pit-lane walks, a guided paddock tour and an optional hotel stay.

Alternatively, some of the best ticket deals can be found on third-party resale platforms, including Ticket Network, Vivid Seats, StubHub, Ticket Liquidator, SeatGeek and Gametime. Plus, THR has exclusive promo codes for select sites (all listed below).

Apple TV, Formula 1’s U.S. streaming home, is making the entire Las Vegas Grand Prix weekend available free, with no subscription required. Watch all three practice sessions, qualifying and Saturday’s race through the Apple TV app or at tv.apple.com.

Race-week travelers can compare scheduled flights into Harry Reid International Airport with private-terminal options connecting Los Angeles and Las Vegas. The following services offer alternatives for getting to town and navigating the weekend.

Theos, the members-only aviation service, operated by Cirrus Aviation Services, offers seats aboard a Challenger 850 from Van Nuys to Las Vegas on Friday, Nov. 20, with return service Sunday, Nov. 22. Seats are quoted at $1,950 each way or $3,900 round trip, allowing travelers to book a place on the private aircraft without chartering the entire jet. Book or apply for membership at gotheos.com.

The Santa Monica-Las Vegas JSX route connects the Westside with Las Vegas using hangar departures. Travelers can arrive 20 minutes before their flight. Check race-week schedules and fares at jsx.com.

Zoox’s robotaxi service serves selected areas of Las Vegas, operating from 6 a.m. to 3 a.m. Check the Zoox app for available trips and pickup locations during race week, when circuit closures may affect access.

Waymo’s driverless service now includes Harry Reid International Airport, with pickups and drop-offs at designated autonomous vehicle zones on the Ground Zero level of Terminals 1 and 3. Las Vegas access is still rolling out to riders, so download the Waymo app and confirm eligibility, your destination and available pickup points at waymo.com.

Travelers picking up a rental car on Friday, Nov. 20, can meet Hertz Gold Squad, the four-legged welcome committee. Trained golden retrievers will greet visitors from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. at the Hertz rental-car area serving Harry Reid International Airport.
