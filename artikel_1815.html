Arsenal: Ryan Gravenberch angers Liverpool fans with Jurrien Timber interaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Liverpool midfielder Ryan Gravenberch has faced criticism from supporters after appearing to like an Instagram post celebrating Arsenal’s victory at Anfield.

The Gunners secured a 1-0 win on Sunday, with Kai Havertz scoring the decisive goal to give Mikel Arteta’s side their first Premier League victory at Anfield since 2012.

Following the match, Arsenal defender Jurrien Timber shared a photograph on Instagram showing him involved in a heated exchange with Liverpool midfielder Alexis Mac Allister.

Timber accompanied the picture with the caption: “Proud of the team! Enjoy this one, Gooners.”

Liverpool fans were quick to notice that Gravenberch had appeared to like the post, prompting an angry reaction on social media.

Some supporters questioned the midfielder’s loyalty, with others suggesting he should be sold or disciplined by the club.

Gravenberch, 24, was an unused substitute during Liverpool’s defeat. He and Timber are close friends, having played together at Ajax for two years before continuing their relationship as team-mates with the Netherlands national side.

It is not clear whether Gravenberch personally liked the post or whether the interaction was made by someone managing his social media account.

The incident has nevertheless caused frustration among sections of the Liverpool support following a disappointing result against their Premier League rivals.
