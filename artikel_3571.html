‘9-1-1’ Is Officially About to Go On Hiatus

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Since moving to ABC nearly three years ago, 9-1-1 has been one of the network's top performers. The procedural proved to be a smart investment for the network, with an established fanbase and strong ratings. The show performed so well that ABC invested in a spin-off series, and 9-1-1: Nashville premiered last season and has been successful. The franchise occupies the 8 pm to 10 pm slot on Thursdays and is still doing well.

Nielsen ratings for the season premiere episodes revealed that 9-1-1 Season 10, Episode 1 earned 8.14 million total viewers after seven days of viewing on ABC, Hulu, Hulu on Disney+, and digital platforms. This increased viewership from the previous season, which averaged 7.82 million viewers. 9-1-1: Nashville earned 6.76 million total viewers after seven days, outperforming the show's Season 1 finale as well as its previous season average of 6.72 million and nearly doubling its initial live and same-day audience. The tenth season of 9-1-1 opened with a volcanic eruption in the middle of Los Angeles that demanded all hands on deck. Everything concluded after three episodes, and now the show is back to regular storylines, with new episodes airing on Thursdays.

However, 9-1-1 and 9-1-1 Nashville will go on hiatus sometime in the next few weeks as the holidays approach. These breaks are common for shows that air in the fall, especially when a season has many episodes. The good news is that ABC has already announced the return date for both shows, and thankfully nothing much has changed. 9-1-1 and 9-1-1: Nashville will return on Thursday, January 7, 2027, to their regular time slots. The network has also announced the return dates for anticipated shows like The Rookie, The Rookie: North, and High Potential. In the meantime, there's still more to explore in the coming episode before the hiatus.

It's officially Detective Athena Grant (Angela Bassett) now, and no one is stopping her when she has a hunch. ABC has released the promo for 9-1-1 Season 10, Episode 5, "Who's Your Daddy?" which finds Athena investigating a suspicious death after a jogger stumbles on a body. The episode features a guest appearance by Arrow's David Ramsey, whose character collides with Athena because she sees something everyone else doesn't. Meanwhile, the 118 responds to an emergency when a woodchipper accidentally turns on and drags a father into it. The damage is extensive, and Chim (Kenneth Choi) doesn't think the victim will survive.

Catch new episodes of 9-1-1 and 9-1-1: Nashville on Thursdays on ABC, and stay tuned to Collider for more updates.
