New Lucario 'Pokémon' Set Quietly Changes Everything For TCG Fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Flowing Fists is the first Pokémon TCG set to include Mega Z cards

After weeks of apparent early sales leaks, The Pokémon Company just revealed its slate of products for the upcoming Flowing Fists Pokémon Trading Card Game expansion set. The fighting-themed set looks like a banger based on early card reveals, which feature fan-favorite monsters and art by popular illustrators. TCG fans shouldn't get too wrapped up in the pretty pictures or shiny wrappers, though: there's an important asterisk to the news that will impact the hobby at large.

The new set, which marks the debut of Pokémon Legends: Z-A's Mega Z Pokémon, contains over 110 cards and is set to launch on Feb. 6, 2027, according to The Pokémon Company. Japan gets the set a little earlier, so we already know what coveted products like the set's Elite Trainer Box will look like. The ETB's Riolu promo card, illustrated by Yoshioka, is guaranteed to make ETBs fly off shelves. Collectors can look forward to over 30 lush art cards as well.

In the announcement, The Pokémon Company also revealed that Flowing Fists will be the first set to officially release on Saturday. Typically, new Pokémon sets release in North America on Friday — although shipping issues and pre-release events can cause the release date at local card shops to vary.

"Subsequent Pokémon TCG expansions will continue to release on Saturdays," The Pokémon Company said in a press release.

So, why does this matter? Well, Saturdayis a more convenient day to release a new product for people who work or study during normal business hours. Pokémon cards have been in a bit of a price slump lately​​​​​​​, but even standard sets like Delta Reign are difficult for average collectors to find on shelves without lining up early on release day.

​​​​​​​If TCG releases move to Saturdays, that opens the door​​​​​​​ for a wider range of people to vie for the same products. Competition for an already scarce product is about to get much fiercer.​​​​​​​ And let's not forget, Lucario is one of the most beloved creatures in the entire franchise. People are guaranteed to show up in droves for the first set to introduce a Mega Lucario Z card. The set has multiple Lucario cards spread out across illustration cards and ex-cards.

Hopefully, that's only bad news for scalpers who treat reselling like their primary job. Everyone else will likely welcome having a real shot at buying Pokémon products​​​​​​​ for a price closer to the manufacturer's suggested retail price.
