Cody Johnson Pulls Out of 2026 CMA Awards After 4 Nominations

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Cody Johnson is taking a step back from the 2026 CMA Awards after receiving four nominations.

“To the COJO Nation, my peers and the country music industry, I won’t be attending the CMA Awards tomorrow and I want to be honest about why,” Johnson, 39, wrote in an Instagram statement on Tuesday, November 17. “After getting sick at the end of my UK Tour earlier this month, I realized much of it stemmed from mental and physical exhaustion. I want to be open about this because I believe we help one another by revealing our truths, and this time my truth isn’t in the words of a song; it’s real life.”

He continued, “At the end of the day, I’m a human being, no matter how much I tell myself and others I’m superman. I have everything to be thankful for, but sometimes I put too much pressure on myself. I’m going to spend some time recouping at home; being grounded by family is what I need most right now. I’ve waited my whole life to be where I am in my career, and I wouldn’t have it any other way. It was well worth the wait and the hard work it took to get here.”

Johnson, however, noted that going “100 miles per hour all the time” isn’t a “healthy” way of living — which he said he now can see.

“Again, thank you for understanding and for your love, support and grace during this downtime through the end of the year. I will see you all in 2027,” he concluded, signing his message with “Cody.” Johnson added in the caption, “Thank you, COJO Nation, for your continued love and support.”

Johnson was up for four nominations — including Entertainer of the Year, Male Vocalist of the Year and Single of the Year and Music Video of the Year for “The Fall” — at the awards show, which is taking place on Wednesday, November 18, at Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena.

He was also announced as one of the performers of the awards show, taking the stage on the same night as fellow artists including Taylor Swift, Ella Langley, Kacey Musgraves and host Lainey Wilson.

The “Horseback” hitmaker took home the prize for Male Vocalist of the Year at last year’s CMAs.

Earlier this month, Johnson had to cancel shows in Belfast and Dublin after facing a “viral infection” and being advised by doctors not to perform.

“I am sorry for any inconvenience this may cause and with the late announcement but wanted to do all I could to try and proceed with the shows. I am extremely disappointed not to be able to play as I have been looking forward to the Irish shows for a long time,” he wrote via Instagram at the time. “I will do all I can to come back to Ireland as soon as I can and play these shows for you all … Thank you for your understanding.”
