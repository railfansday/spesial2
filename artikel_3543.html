Man Arrested After He Shot Coworker, Claimed It Was a 'Freak Accident'

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

A man was arrested after he killed his coworker with a Glock 19 while they were staying in an Airbnb for a work trip, and he’s now claiming the fatal shooting was a “freak accident” and that he didn’t know the weapon was loaded.

The Jacksonville, Texas, College Station police announced in a Tuesday, November 24, press release that Landon Wyatt King was arrested earlier that day and charged with aggravated assault with a deadly weapon in connection with the death of Jordon Aguilar.

Authorities learned of the incident when College Station police officers responded to an Airbnb in College Station, Texas, around 1:15 a.m. after they received reports of a shooting. Aguilar, 26, was found suffering from a single gunshot wound, and first responders began life-saving efforts on him. Aguilar succumbed to his injuries and died at the scene.

Police said that Aguilar and King, 21, were in College Station for work and they were staying at the Airbnb on Tuesday night.

“During that time, both had been consuming alcohol through the night,” the police department wrote in the statement. “Preliminary investigation resulted in statements from King, admitting to owning the firearm, pointing the firearm believing it was unloaded, and firing a single, fatal shot to the victim.”

Aguilar reportedly handled the weapon himself before he gave it to King, according to WTAW. King claimed to investigators that he did not think there was a round in the chamber of the weapon, though he admitted to knowing that a magazine had been loaded into the handgun.

A third coworker, who was also staying at the Airbnb, was sleeping in a bedroom at the time that King allegedly shot Aguilar, according to KBTX.

While speaking to officials, King allegedly said that the shooting was a “freak accident,” according to KBTX. He also said that he and Aguilar got along and did not have any sort of altercation or conflict in the moments leading up to the shooting.

King also allegedly told officers that he owned the firearm that was used in the shooting.

The defendant was booked into custody on Tuesday and was issued a $100,000 bond, according to the Brazos County Sheriff’s Office.

It’s not currently clear if King has entered a plea or retained legal counsel following his arrest. The College Station Police Department did not immediately respond to Us’ request for comment regarding updates in the case.
