Why There's No Sheriff Country Tonight (November 20) & When New Season 2 Episodes Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

No new episodes of Sheriff Country season 2 will air tonight, raising some questions about what will be broadcast by the Eye Network instead and when Sheriff Country returns. In its maiden season, Sheriff Country had already achieved the rare feat of consistently scoring better ratings than Fire Country, its parent series. Its incredible success continued as Sheriff Country returned, with its season 2 premiere earning a series high of 10.52 million viewers between live and 7+ delayed viewers, and its crossover episode with Fire Country drawing 5.54 million live viewers, according to Nielsen ratings.

Story-wise, Sheriff Country season 2 had already so much to tackle. Starting right when Sheriff Country season 1’s finale left off forced the CBS drama into the biggest investigation ever seen in Edgewater and to focus on the aftermath of Mickey discovering Wes’s betrayal and that her mother had always been alive. Things didn’t go any slower after the Stoyanovs case was wrapped, as the massive crossover event with Fire Country immediately followed, ensuring Sheriff Country season 2 was as packed as it could be.

Sheriff Country season 2, episode 6 offered some respite in the form of less attention to Mickey and the Foxes so that the CBS show could focus on its supporting characters. Still, its end hinting at Cassidy’s ex’s return and his appearance in the trailer for Sheriff Country season 2, episode 7, all promise new danger leading up to Sheriff Country season 2’s winter finale, making this hiatus a chance to prepare for its inevitably high drama.

Sheriff Country season 2 is on its first midseason hiatus, with the last episode that aired being season 2, episode 6, “Close to the Chest.” This will be the longest midseason break among those already announced, and a reason justifying its occurrence now and its length has yet to be provided.

Network TV having a strict schedule to follow makes breaks inevitable. Usually, the reason behind a specific break has to do with networks taking advantage of sweeps weeks, which set the ad rates for the rest of the year, or with special programming happening instead. Given that taking advantage of the sweeps weeks usually results in pulling out all the stops to raise the ratings through plot twists and celebrity cameos, and this year’s fourth period ends on November 25, it’s unlikely that is the reason for Sheriff Country season 2’s current break.

A reason for the hiatus could nevertheless be Thanksgiving falling on November 26, 2026, as holidays usually call for special programming. Instead of a new episode of Sheriff Country, a College Basketball game between Kentucky and Indiana will air at 8 pm ET tonight, followed by a special broadcast of Sheriff Country season 2’s premiere, “Cyclone,” at 10 pm ET.

Sheriff Country season 2 is set to return with episode 7, “Stand Your Ground,” on Friday, December 11, 2026. The CBS crime drama will then air its midseason finale the following week, on December 18. This timeframe makes it possible for Sheriff Country and Fire Country to maximize the opportunities for crossovers until Fire Country season 5’s finale on February 5, 2027.

Despite the fact that Fire Country preceded Sheriff Country, the original CBS show's biggest weakness is becoming its spinoff's best quality.

Sheriff Country season 2, episode 7’s trailer focuses on both Mickey and Boone’s investigation into a violent assault and the introduction of Cassidy’s ex to the story, whose return can cause a lot of chaos, especially for Cassidy, as she worked hard to come to terms with what he put her through. The synopsis for “Stand Your Ground” reads as follows:

Mickey investigates a violent assault that rattles the community while mounting public pressure and a controversial new technology threaten to derail the case. Cassidy is forced to confront a dangerous chapter from her past.

Mickey is also set to appear in some capacity in Fire Country season 5, episode 7 when it returns, as its trailer shows her informing Manny of his brother being involved in a stabbing. Everything that is known thus points to Sheriff Country season 2 packing so many drama-worthy developments before its second hiatus hits after its midseason finale of December 18, 2026.
