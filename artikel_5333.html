Emplifi Appoints Claire Wilson as Chief Marketing Officer

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Ten-year Emplifi leader and B2B SaaS marketing executive will accelerate brand awareness and growth as AI reshapes social media marketing and CX

NEW YORK, Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Emplifi, a leading Autonomous CX platform, today announced Claire Wilson as its new Chief Marketing Officer. Wilson will lead the marketing organization, fostering a dynamic environment that prioritizes teamwork, performance, collaboration, and respect, while building on Emplifi's customer-centric culture and innovative approach to social media marketing, commerce, and care.

Wilson's appointment to CMO comes on the heels of Emplifi launching Fuel AI, an agentic AI solution for social media marketing and CX leaders that removes the barriers between social media marketing, commerce, and care. According to EMARKETER, 44% of marketing leaders ranked their marketing technology stack as a top AI investment this year, with 42% citing "data" as a top AI investment in 2026. With the launch of Fuel AI, Emplifi is giving marketers a powerful, unified AI platform that can analyze data, and then create and orchestrate campaigns based on that data, eliminating the need for a complex martech stack with multiple tools.

"Marketers are struggling to find steady ground right now. More than 80% of the marketers we surveyed say AI has boosted their productivity, but only about a third say it has made a significant impact on results," said Wilson. "That gap is where Emplifi comes in. I've had a front row seat to our customers' success for more than a decade, and my goal as CMO is to make sure every social media marketing and CX leader understands what Emplifi can do for them, and to measure our success by the growth we help them deliver. I want to help brands turn AI from a productivity tool into a real growth engine."

Wilson brings more than 20 years of B2B marketing leadership to the role. She joined Socialbakers in 2016, before its combination with Astute formed Emplifi, and has since led the company's corporate and marketing communications, most recently as Vice President of Marketing Communications.

"Claire has been with Emplifi since the early days, and few people understand our customers, our platform, and our market as well as she does," said Ohad Hecht, CEO, Emplifi. "As AI changes how brands engage their customers, we need a marketing leader who can turn that depth of knowledge into growth. Claire is that leader."

Emplifi is the only Autonomous CX platform unifying social marketing, commerce, and customer care for more than 22,000 brands worldwide. Powered by Fuel AI, built on more than 5T of data, and recognized as a leader by leading industry analysts, Emplifi helps enterprise teams orchestrate connected customer journeys and deliver personalized experiences at scale, turning every customer touchpoint into a competitive advantage.
