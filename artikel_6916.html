‘Dancing With the Stars’ Pro Jan Ravnik Reveals His Iconic Dream Guest Judges

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

We’re mere weeks away from Dancing With the Stars season 35 wrapping up, and you know what that means: it’s almost time to start fan casting Dancing With the Stars season 36, and that definitely includes guest judges.

The show always invites a roster of guests to join the judges’ panel, from former Dancing With the Stars pros to random celebrity fans—and Jan Ravnik has a suggestion.

Speaking to Entertainment Weekly, Jan mused, “Oh, who would be my dream? You know what would be cool, if there would be the Kelce brothers here, one of them.”

He added, “They would be incredible. They would know nothing about dance, but somehow judge great.”

Reminder: Jan was a backup dancer for Taylor Swift during the Eras Tour, which means he presumably knows the Kelce brothers. So, if he says they’d be good judges, we have faith. And honestly, it could happen. Dancing With the Stars has managed to book some pretty major celebrities, including Cynthia Erivo, who showed up for Disney Night a few weeks ago. On top of that, the show introduced a Super Bowl night this season, and if they do so again, having Jason or Travis guest judge would be an obvious choice!

Meanwhile, according to The Sun back in 2024, Jason Kelce is a fan of the show and even considered joining. “He looks forward to being on TV when the NFL season starts. But he also wants to do multiple things and loves TV shows,” their source said. “He been asked to appear on plenty of shows in the last few months, and he is really interested in being on some of them. Jason is sure to have fun on Dancing with The Stars and hopes it will work out in the coming season.”
