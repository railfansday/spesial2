Pick the checking account that charges you nothing for the way you bank. For most people that’s Capital One 360 Checking, with no monthly or overdraft fees and free cash deposits at stores. Choose Chase Total Checking for branches in 48 states if you’ll meet its fee waiver, or nbkc to earn interest on every dollar.

An account’s overdraft rules can cost you more than its monthly fee. The National Consumer Law Center estimates people paid about $12.4 billion in 2025 in fees for overdrawing their accounts or having payments returned unpaid. So each pick below is judged by what it costs someone it suits, from its monthly fee and how easily that’s waived to its overdraft and ATM fees.
