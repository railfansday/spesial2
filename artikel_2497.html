Christian Watson's absence noticeable to Packers fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Green Bay Packers wide receiver Christian Watson took the impact of a vicious hit in the second quarter from Los Angeles Rams safety Kamren Kinchens and was able to walk off the field under his own power.

Flagged for a defenseless hit, Kinchens was ejected from the game. But the loss of Watson also appeared to drain the energy from the Packers' offense during their 26-13 loss, Nov. 25, at SoFi Stadium.

With about two minutes remaining in the first half, the Packers decided to go on a 4th and 1 and quarterback Jordan Love threw to an open Watson near the sidelines. Kinchens lowered his shoulder and leveled Watson near his helmet. He was able to get up and walk off the field, but the momentum of the game immediately changed.

When the down was replayed due to offsetting penalties, Packers coach Matt LaFleur chose to take the field goal instead of attempting to continue the drive. Trey Smack made the kick to tie the game at 13, but the Packers would not score again.

The loss of Watson certainly wasn't the lone reason that the Packers were unable to avoid a zero in the second half. They ran only 15 second half plays and were outgained on the ground 241 yards to 55.

But it was clear that Love wasn't the same without Watson, who had four catches for 65 yards and a touchdown before leaving the game. The Packers had only two first downs after Watson was put into concussion protocol.
