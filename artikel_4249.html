How To Unlock The Hall Of Fallen Foes In The Ocarina Of Time Remake

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake is finally here, so it's time to once again experience one of the greatest video games ever made. While this is a mostly faithful recreation of the original, Nintendo has added some new content, adjustments, and features for players to enjoy.

Perhaps the biggest addition is the Hall of Fallen Foes, which acts as both a museum to look at all the enemies in the game and a way to replay Ocarina of Time's bosses. However, like many things in this game, it is hidden, and you are only given minor hints as to where it can be found. So if you're struggling to unlock or locate this new area, here is everything you need to know about the Hall of Fallen Foes.

To unlock the Hall of Fallen Foes, you will need to progress through the main story and beat the Forest Temple. This will be the first dungeon you can complete as adult Link, so make sure to get it done right away if you want to check out the Hall of Fallen Foes as soon as possible.

Keep in mind, you won't need any actual equipment or gear to access the Hall of Fallen Foes. This isn't a separate dungeon or area in Hyrule that can only be opened if you have an item like the Megaton Hammer or Fairy Bow. All you need to do is complete the actual dungeon and then return to Kokori Forest while in the future.

With the Hall of Fallen Foes unlocked, you will now need to return to Kokiri Forest and go to Link's House. Previously, you would only be able to rest in Link's bed, which would both advance time ands restore any health or magic you were missing. After the Forest Temple, there will be a small, swirling green circle on his mattress.

Interact with the circle to fall into a dream state, which causes Link to be teleported to the Hall of Fallen Foes. Once you visit the House of Fallen Foes for the first time, various teleport points will unlock throughout Hyrule. This includes places such as Impa's House in Kakariko Village, the Lakeside Laboratory in Lake Hylia, and the Carpenter's Camp in Gerudo Valley.

When you arrive, Kaepora will greet you and explain that this is a place where you can examine all the previous enemies you've fought so far. There are no puzzles or exploration needed, and if you want to leave, just talk with Kaepora.

Once you arrive in the Hall of Fallen Foes, you can examine any of the 3D Models for every enemy you have currently defeated at least once in the game. This means, the hall will gradually fill up as you play through the game, adding to this museum of enemies. This also includes all of the bosses in the game, which are displayed near the back on larger podiums.

Interacting with one of the statues will not only show where the enemy is commonly located, but what tools are best to use against them. There is also a small written segment about the monster, but this isn't anything particularly interesting. Additionally, you can replay any boss fight in the game by interacting with the boss' statue and selecting the "Rematch" option.

Not only will this fight have a timer for those trying to speedrun their boss battles, but you'll be heavily limited by the equipment available along with your health. Meaning, if you fight Phantom Ganon you will only have access to the Fairy Bow, Hookshot, and a single bottle with a Red Potion in it. This can make these fights more challenging than their story counterparts, since you can make way fewer mistakes during the fight.

Additionally, players can interact with the Kaepora statues to fight all the bosses in sequence for both young and adult Link. As for the normal enemies, these cannot be fought and there are no additional combat challenges tied to them.
