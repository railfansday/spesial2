Why Broadcom Stock Took a Wild Ride on Thursday

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

By Danny Vena, CPA – Nov 19, 2026 at 2:41PM EST

Shares of Broadcom (AVGO -2.44%) were all over the map on Thursday. After starting the day as much as 4.3% higher, the stock reversed course, down 1.5% as of 2:25 p.m. ET.

The semiconductor specialist stood to profit from a record-breaking initial public offering (IPO).

All eyes were on artificial intelligence (AI) start-up Anthropic (ANT +15.56%) when the company made its public debut today. After initially proposing a price range of $70 to $78, Anthropic priced the stock above the high end of that range at $83 per share. The company sold more than 980 million Class A shares, raising more than $107 billion after accounting for several private placements with some of the biggest names in technology (more on that in a minute).

Investors were keen to get in on the action, with the stock opening at $111.50 and climbing to nearly $115 per share, pushing its market cap briefly above $2 trillion. Then, the luster began to fade, and Anthropic gave up some of its early gains, trading at about $97 as I write this.

So what does this have to do with Broadcom? The chipmaker was one of the parties to Anthropic’s private placements. Broadcom agreed to purchase more than 13 million shares of Anthropic's Class N common stock for a cool $1 billion. When the stock spiked more than 38% in early trading, Broadcom's position was worth roughly $385 million more. However, as Anthropic's gains moderated, so too did Broadcom's stock price.

Eventually, the excitement preceding the IPO gave way to profit-taking and concerns about the broader economy and the sustainability of AI.

Those concerns aside, at less than 18 times forward earnings, Broadcom stock is worth a look.
