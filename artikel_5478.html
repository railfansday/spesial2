Anthropic's IPO and public placement could be the biggest ever

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The IPO and a $26 billion private placement would raise $90.4 billion at the top of the range, as U.S. equity raising tops 2021's total

Anthropic plans to price its IPO on Nov. 18 in an offering that, combined with a private placement, would bring in $90.4 billion if it lands at the high end of the range being marketed.

That total would top SpaceX’s June listing, which raised $86.2 billion, according to Bloomberg. If underwriters exercise the over-allotment option after trading begins, the total would exceed $100 billion. That would eclipse the approximately $98 billion stock offering that Meta $META Platforms unveiled last month to finance its artificial intelligence push.

Anthropic said in an updated filing on Tuesday that the IPO carries a price range of $70 to $78 a share. At the top of that range, the IPO would raise up to $64.35 billion before costs. The company also struck deals with Advanced Micro Devices, Broadcom $AVGO, Google $GOOGL, Micron $MU, Microsoft $MSFT, Nvidia $NVDA and Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. to sell them $26 billion of Class N shares through a privately negotiated placement. The price would equal the IPO price.

Anthropic could carry a valuation as high as $1.405 trillion once every potential share is counted, according to CNBC. The company announced a $965 billion valuation in a funding round in May. Some shareholders told CNBC last week that even a $1.5 trillion valuation was in doubt.

The deal would cap a record year for U.S. equity raising. More than $425 billion has flowed to U.S.-listed firms via initial public offerings, finished at-the-market programs and marketed follow-on stock offerings, according to data compiled by Bloomberg. That tops the roughly $408 billion raised in 2021.

Bloomberg said the three largest U.S. IPOs on record have arrived within a few months of one another, fueled by the heavy capital demands of AI firms. SK Hynix's U.S. listing in July, at $26.5 billion, was the biggest ever by a firm headquartered abroad.

Anthropic has drawn orders for multiple times the stock on offer. The demand stemmed from large, anchor-style expressions of interest, and the terms may still change. The company launched its roadshow on Tuesday. It includes a live-streamed event on Friday with CEO Dario Amodei and JPMorgan $JPM Chase CEO Jamie Dimon.

Goldman Sachs $GS and Morgan Stanley $MS are leading the IPO with 20 other banks. The stock is expected to list on the Nasdaq $NDAQ Global Select Market under the ticker ANT and begin trading on Nov. 19.
