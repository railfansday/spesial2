5 Chargers players to watch in Week 10 vs. Ravens

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Chargers are set to play in their first primetime game of the 2026 season.

It's finally arrived, but the Chargers are far from the team many predicted they'd be this season. Los Angeles is the only winless team in the NFL and ranks among the bottom in many major offensive categories.

Although the Chargers sit at 0-8, Monday offers a chance to get their first win of the season. If the Chargers want any chance at a win, these five players may need to have one heck of a game.

Here are the five players to watch for in Monday night's contest.

All eyes will be on Henley, the only player who was traded prior to the deadline, before the deal fell through. After a whirlwind week for the linebacker, we'll watch how he responds.

Herbert has been abysmal thus far through the first eight games of the season. Frankly, the whole offense has been atrocious. However, Herbert and that side of the ball have a chance to turn things around against his former defensive coordinator, Jesse Minter. Their familiarity is no small thing. It'll be interesting to see if Herbert can play well and finally put together a complete offensive performance.

Mack didn't play his final game as a Charger last Sunday. This season could be Mack's last, and it will likely come without a playoff win. With Lamar Jackson out and Jacoby Brissett in, the Chargers have a chance to make life difficult for the Ravens' offense. Mack needs to generate pressure without allowing the Ravens to exploit LA's defense on the ground.

With Ladd McConkey out, the Chargers' young wide receiver could become Herbert's go-to target. Harris ranks second in receiving yards (262) and first in receptions (21). If McConkey is unavailable or limited, Harris could get more targets and a chance to establish himself as one of Herbert's most dependable receivers.

Tart has been one of the Chargers' better run-stopping defensive linemen, recording 12 run stops and 17 total tackles through eight games. He and the defensive line will have their hands full with Derrick Henry in the Ravens' backfield. If Tart continues his stellar play and keeps the Ravens' offense in check, stopping Henry must be a top priority.
