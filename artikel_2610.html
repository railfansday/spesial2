Bucs get on the board to trail Lions 10-7 at half

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Buccaneers are keeping it close in Detroit, so far.

After falling behind the Lions, 10-0, the Bucs scratched and clawed their way to a touchdown just before halftime.

A 23-yard touchdown pass from quarterback Baker Mayfield to receiver Ted Hurst III provided the first six points of the game for the 3-6 Bucs.

Mayfield has completed 11 of 17 passes for 104 yards. He has added 27 rushing yards on three carries, leading the Tampa ground game.

Detroit running back Jahmyr Gibbs has been limited to 18 rushing yards on six carries, an average of only 3.0 yards per attempt. He has one catch for minus-three yards.

The Lions get the ball first to start the third quarter.
