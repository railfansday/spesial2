Tottenham Hotspur Are Keeping Tabs On This Championship Forward: Should De Zerbi Bring Him Back?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

In a recent report, The Sun revealed that Tottenham Hotspur are keeping tabs on Middlesbrough forward Will Lankshear. It has been stated that Spurs are facing competition from Newcastle United and Leeds United in the race to land the English striker next year.

Lankshear is enjoying the form of his life at Middlesbrough this season as he has banged in goals for fun for them in the EFL Championship. The 21-year-old has scored 15 goals in 16 matches for the Boro in this campaign so far in multiple competitions.

The English talent is currently one of the most talented centre-forwards in the English Championship. Hence, Spurs would do well to bring him back to the North London club next year.

Lankshear's current contract at the Riverside Stadium will run out in the summer of 2031, so Tottenham would have to launch a convincing offer if they want to recruit him this January or at the end of this season.

Lankshear excels at finding a yard of space for himself to get some strikes in at goal. He has the eye to play some decisive passes in and around the opponent's penalty area and is a good dribbler with the ball at his feet.

Standing at 1.89m, the English talent can use his strong frame to win a few 50-50 aerial duels for his side. Lankshear has proven himself to be a reliable goal-getter in the Championship. However, it remains to be seen whether he can adapt to the physical side and high intensity of the Premier League if Spurs manage to get him on board next year.

We can expect Lankshear to bring more firepower to Tottenham head coach Roberto De Zerbi's attack. He is playing at a high level right now and could be well-equipped to make an instant impact at the North London club.

At 21, the future looks quite promising for Lankshear as long as he continues to improve with each season. He is undoubtedly a prospect to watch out for in the long run, so Spurs would be wise to bring him back in the January transfer window or next summer.
