CIAC football playoff picture is set for 2026, as final nine teams clinch berths unofficially

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Rematches were the special of the day when the final Connecticut Thanksgiving high school football results came in to finalize the unofficial 2026 CIAC football field. But there were a few surprise qualifiers, at least.

Six of the 24 quarterfinal games are rematches of regular season games, another two (both in Class SS) are rematches of last year's quarterfinals.

Here's how the points shook out and the matchups were decided Thursday.

The final points and pairings are unofficial until the CIAC football committee meets Friday morning to approve the final standings. The CIAC quarterfinals will begin Tuesday, Dec. 1 at the sites of the higher seeds, 6:30 p.m.

Hamden's 24-22 victory over Notre Dame-West Haven clinched a playoff spot for the Green Dragons. Despite its 20-14 loss to New London, NFA clinched the final spot on bonuses over South Windsor.

Southington (No. 1) and New Britain (No. 2) grabbed the top two seeds. Greenwich earned the No. 3 with its victory over Staples, setting up a home quarterfinal vs. No. 6 Fairfield Prep. The loss dropped Staples to No. 4 and set up a rematch vs. No. 5-seeded Danbury, which beat Staples 17-10 on Oct. 9.

New Canaan's victory over Darien, coupled with Maloney's 27-7 victory over Platt in the Stoddard Bowl, clinched a playoff spot for Maloney.

Darien (6-4), though, nearly got outpointed by Cheney/Windham Tech (7-3). But Stamford's 41-6 victory over Westhill denied the Chargers the 5 strength-of-schedule points that would have put them in over the Blue Wave.

Instead, Darien now travels to Fairfield to face FCIAC rival and top-seeded Ludlowe in the first round. Ludlowe beat Darien 16-0 in Week 2.

Notre Dame-West Haven's loss to Hamden ended the Green Knights' playoff hopes.

Notre Dame-West Haven's loss to Hamden opened the door for Wilton, which earned enough points to clinch the No. 8 spot.

The Warriors get a rematch with FCIAC rival and No. 1 (seeded and GametimeCT ranked) St. Joseph, a game they lost 31-0 on Oct. 17.

Torrington's comeback win over Watertown clinched a spot and the No. 6 seed for the Raiders, who will travel to Southbury to face No. 3 Pomperaug.

Newtown, the No. 5 seed, travels to face CTC co-champion and No. 4 seeded Thames River co-op in Groton. Middletown, which was idle, slid into the No. 7 seed, and travels to face No. 2-seeded Holy Cross.

Ledyard's victory over Fitch eliminated the Falcons from contention, allowing Bunnell to capture the final spot as the No. 8 seed. The Bulldogs get a quarterfinal rematch with SWC rival and No. 1 seeded Masuk, which beat them 41-26 on Oct. 16.

Branford wiped out East Haven 56-0 to clinch a berth as the No. 7 seed and earned a trip to unbeaten New London, which secured the No. 2 seed with its victory over NFA.

Lyman Hall's victory over Sheehan - its first Carini Bowl win since 2011 - gave the Trojans the No. 3 seed and (yet another) a home quarterfinal rematch with SCC rival and No. 6-seeded Guilford. Lyman Hall hung on to beat the Grizzlies 20-13 on Oct. 23.

No. 8 BUNNELL (6-4) at No. 1 MASUK (10-0)No. 5 NOTRE DAME PREP (8-2) at No. 4 ROCKVILLE (8-2)No. 7 BRANFORD (7-3) at No. 2 NEW LONDON (10-0)No. 6 GUIILFORD (7-3) at No. 3 LYMAN HALL (8-2)

Waterford, which clinched when Ellington lost to Rockville Wednesday night, earned the No. 8 seed and a date with Hand at the Surf Club - a rematch of last year's Class SS quarterfinal game won by eventual champion Hand, 43-19.

No. 6 Ledyard also got a playoff rematch at No. 3-seeded Law, which it defeated in last year's quarterfinals, 32-29, also at Law.

No. 7 Berlin faces unbeaten and No. 2-seeded Plainville in an intriguing interdivisional CCC game. No. 6 Griswold/Wheeler heads to Jarvis Field to take on No. 3 Ansonia.

No. 8 WATERFORD (7-3) at No. 1 HAND (10-0)No. 5 LEDYARD (8-2) at No. 4 LAW (8-2)No. 7 BERLIN (7-3) at No. 2 PLAINVILLE (10-0)No. 6 GRISWOLD/WHEELER (8-2) at No. 3 ANSONIA (9-1)

Seymour and Coginchaug came into the morning both depending on the result of the Oxford-Derby game and Coginchaug hoping Watertown could beat Torrington to clinch outright.

Oxford hung on to win, 21-20, giving Seymour the needed boost to outpoint Coginchaug for the final spot by 5 points.

The No. 8-seeded Wildcats, however, get a rematch with No. 1-seeded and unbeaten North Branford. The T-Birds wiped out Seymour 41-6 in an Alliance Week 2 game.

Montville/St. Bernard, which clinched Wednesday night, takes their No. 7 seed to face No. 2-seeded Tolland. Bloomfield, the No. 6 seed, travels to face No. 3 Valley Regional/Old Lyme. Woodland earned the No. 5 seed and travels to face No. 4-seeded Granby/Canton.
