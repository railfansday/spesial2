Colorado drops in ESPN FPI rankings after Week 10 loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

A sixth loss and fifth Big 12 loss, this time a road loss against Arizona State, the Colorado Buffaloes saw their standing in ESPN's Football Power Index fall slightly.

The Buffs fell one spot and remain the Big 12's last-ranked team, eight spots below Kansas State. CU is ranked No. 74 nationally with a -1.8 FPI score.

FPI dropped Colorado's projected record from 4.3-7.7 to 3.9-8.1, suggesting even one more win is a slight stretch for the 3-6 program. The probability of reaching six wins, which would guarantee a bowl game, fell to its lowest mark this year, from 10.9% down to 2%. Colorado needs to win out to reach six wins.

If Colorado wins out, it will finish 6-6 (4-5 Big 12), which FPI currently views as a 2% possibility.

With three games left, the Buffaloes' toughest game is this week against No. 14 Houston, which FPI views as the fifth-best team in the Big 12 and the No. 21 team nationally
