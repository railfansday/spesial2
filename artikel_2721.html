Here’s where the Chiefs sit in the AFC playoff standings after their Thanksgiving loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Kansas City Chiefs fell to the Buffalo Bills 13-10 on Thanksgiving night in a game with a lot of playoff positioning on the line. The Bills strengthened their grip on the top spot in the conference while the Chiefs fell a game behind the Denver Broncos in the AFC West.

The Broncos have a chance to further cement the lead in the division with a game against the Pittsburgh Steelers on Friday, who just benched starting quarterback Aaron Rodgers in favor of veteran backup Mason Rudolph.
