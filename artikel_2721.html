Celtics outworked by Magic, lose 115-105 on back end of back-to-back

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Orlando Magic visited Boston looking for their fifth straight win at TD Garden Monday night. The game was close until the final five minutes when the Magic overran the Celtics for their first win in Beantown since 2022, 115-105. Boston was paced by Jayson Tatum with 24 points, Paul George had 22 points, and Payton Prtichard dropped 19 points.

Luka Garza was handed the start for Boston, alongside Derrick White, Jordan Walsh, Jayson Tatum, and Paul George. Jalen Suggs, Anthony Black, Desmond Bane, Franz Wagner, and Wendall Carter Jr. started for the visitors. Neemias Queta was ruled out for Boston prior to the tip, and for Orlando, Paolo Banchero, Jonathon Issac, Noah Penda, and Izaiyah Nelson were also unavailable.

Paul George continued his stellar start to the season, scoring the first eight Boston points of the game. The veteran wing hit two 3-pointers and a pullup jumper in the first three minutes of play. The rest of Boston's starters were struggling to hit the target, going 0-5 from downtown after six minutes of play.

Payton Pritchard hit a pull-up jumper for Boston on a curl screen, with Boston up 12-9 in the 1st quarter. After destroying the Mavs last night thanks to a first quarter scoring explosion it appeared that Boston had come crashing back to Earth in this game, going just 19% from the field. The entire team had made just 4 field goals in the first eleven minutes of play.

Boston ended the quarter going just 3 of 20 from the field. Boston were still close, however, because Orlando was shooting just 33% from the field themselves. Orlando had a six-point lead with two seconds left in the first quarter before Payton Pritchard hit a buzzer-beating triple to cut it back to just 3 points. Magic up 20-17 after the first quarter.

Chris Cenac Jr. started the second quarter in the middle for Boston as Garza and Tatum went to the bench. Derrick White got cooking to start the second with 6 straight points for the home team. Sam Hauser and Payton Pritchard hit back-to-back 3-pointers for Boston as scores were tied 31-31 midway through the second quarter.

Jayson Tatum returned to the game with 5 minutes to go in the half; he scored 4 straight points in the midst of a 14-5 run by the home team. Orlando had 11 fast break points and was paced by Anthony Black, Goga Bitadze, and Franz Wagner in the first half, who combined for 30 points. The visiting Orlando Magic were up at the half by 9 points.

Derrick White, Sam Huaser and Paul George all converted on 3-pointers for Boston to start the second half as the C's cut the Magic lead back to 4 points. Franz Wagner and Jayson Tatum traded baskets as the TD Garden crowd started to get loud.

Jayson Tatum was working his way into the game after a slow start by his standards, the MVP candidate was attacking the basket relentlessly in the third, putting pressure on the Magic defenders. He drove by three blue shirts to scoop home a nasty lay up past an outstretched Bitadze, his 17th point of the game.

Paul George was solid all night for Boston, his 19th point of the night came at the free throw line with 5-minutes left in the third quarter. Jayson Tatum splashed home a triple for his fourteenth point of the quarter. Bitadze and Mitchell Robinson got tangled up and needed to be separated as things got chippy between the two big men in the third quarter.

Payton Pritchard drove the length of the court and dished out a perfectly weighted pass to rookie Chris Cenac Jr., who had a wonderful backdoor cut and a loud alley-oop dunk for Boston.

Sam Hauser splashed home a pair of 3-pointers late in the third to give Boston the lead 77-75. Boston outscored the Magic by 12 points in the third quarter behind the great play of Jayson Tatum, Sam Hauser and Derrick White.

Payton Pritchard was playing the role of creator in this game, tallying his fifth assist, finding Mitchell Robinson on the roll to the basket who converted with a two-handed flush to start the 4th quarter. Pritchard followed that up driving into the paint for a layup and took a headshot on the play.

The Magic kept coming at the Celtics, they took a lead once more with 9 minutes to go in the game, Anthony Black splashing an uncontested corner triple. The Magic kept the pressure on Boston as Tristan Da Silva hit a three of his own extend the lead to four points.

The Magic scored on 9 of 10 straight possessions down the stretch of the fourth. Sam Hauser's fourth triple of the game cut once more into the Magic's lead. Orlando was relentless on the offensive glass in the fourth, they netted plenty of second chance points. With 2 minutes to play, the Magic were up by 9 points and in complete control. The Magic would hold for the gritty win. Desmond Bane had 24 points, Franz Wagner 21, and Anthony Black 20 points for the visitors.

The Boston Celtics next game is on the road against the Denver Nuggets Wednesday night at 9:30 pm EST, 18th of November, the first of four games on the road.
