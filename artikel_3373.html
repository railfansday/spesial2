Why There's No Scrubs Tonight (November 18) & When New Reboot Episodes Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Sorry, Scrubs fans, there won't be a new episode tonight, November 18, 2026, but here's when the show will return. The Scrubs revival premiered in February 2026 on ABC and ran for nine episodes, bringing back all the characters of the original series and introducing a new main character, Dr. Samantha "Sam" Tosh (Ava Bunn).

Bill Lawrence is back in the showrunner's seat, and the series has even accomplished the fan-service feat of erasing season 9 from canon. The series picks up right after Scrubs season 8, which, if you remember, saw J.D. (Zach Braff) leaving Sacred Heart Hospital and watching an imagined, though more likely than usual, scenario of what his future holds.

In the revival, set 16 years after "My Finale", J.D. is divorced from Elliot (Sarah Chalke) and working as a concierge doctor for the elderly. A chance visit to Sacred Heart reunites him with his old pals and coworkers and leads to him being named Chief of Medicine at the hospital, now in charge of Elliot, Turk (Donald Faison), Carla (Judy Reyes), and Dr. Cox (John C. McGinley).

Season 1 earned a 90% on Rotten Tomatoes, with a lot of critics and viewers celebrating the return of the madcap, heartfelt hospital comedy. A second season was quickly ordered in April 2026, and shooting began soon after. It wasn't long before Scrubs season 2 was on the way.

The 10-episode second season premiered with two episodes on September 30, 2026, with a few new characters. It's off to a strong start, with a 100% on Rotten Tomatoes, though that is likely to change after the season finale. So far, eight episodes of season 2 have aired.

There's been a new episode every week since, with episodes airing on Wednesdays on ABC at 8 PM Eastern and 7 PM Central. Episode 8, "My Emergency", aired last Wednesday, November 11, 2026, but sadly, there won't be any Scrubs tonight. The show is taking a short break before resuming.

The Scrubs revival is going on its first series hiatus in 2026, with a few weeks' break in between episodes 8 and 9. On November 18th at 8 PM, ABC will air the 60th annual Country Music Association Awards on November 18, with Lainey Wilson serving as host for her third consecutive year.

The CMA Awards have aired on ABC since 2006, and since 2024, the awards have always been held on the third Wednesday in November. In 2026, that's November 18. Then the next week is Thanksgiving in the United States, a holiday that always interrupts normal broadcast schedules. On November 25, the day before Thanksgiving, ABC will air Mickey's Christmas Carol as part of ABC's holiday lineup (via ABC).

With the next two weeks booked with other programming options, Scrubs season 2, episode 9, "My Worthless Peon", is set to air in its usual 8 PM time slot on December 2, 2026. There is no episode description yet, but there is a little more information about the season finale, which, as of writing, is a series finale until we hear about a renewal from ABC.

Scrubs season 2, episode 10, "My Wonderful Life", is set to premiere on December 9, 2026, and will be a Christmas-themed episode with J.D. and Dr. Cox working on a challenging case, and Elliot dealing with a packed emergency room and getting some Christmas cheer from unexpected sources.
