Personal Investments • Re: What to expect when opening J.P. Morgan brokerage.

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

If you do decide to go ahead with opening the brokerage account be aware that there is no UI to set the cost basis or default account liquidation method. You have to send support a message requesting a change and there is a time limit (i think 6 months or a year) after which you are locked in to average cost.
