Trump Brushes Off FAA, Microsoft, Bank Outages: ‘It’s Life. It’s Fine’

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Shit happens, folks, get over it. Fresh off a series of government and corporate crises, Donald Trump arrived in Manila on Tuesday to kick off his trip through Southeast Asia, which will culminate in a trilateral meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin. He knows stuff may be a mess at home, but you should get over it.

Trump’s departure from the United States comes just two weeks after a devastating showing in the midterm elections, and on the heels of a nationwide emergency aviation ground stop that continues to disrupt air travel ahead of the Thanksgiving holiday. The FAA fiasco was only one of several significant technical outages in recent days. Last week, Microsoft’s email service, Outlook, experienced a global outage that has not yet been wholly resolved. Several major banks, including Wells Fargo and JPMorgan Chase, experienced widespread system disruptions on Monday. United Airlines also reported a “widespread system outage” this week that further disrupted travel in the aftermath of the FAA ground stop.

So what did the president have to say about all of this to the international press?

Q: “Are you concerned about all these outages?”Trump: “No. It's life” pic.twitter.com/DJPjY8mzYG

Speaking at a press conference in the Philippines, Trump responded to questions about the outages by dismissing them. “It’s life. When you’re big and powerful, and you have all the money, things like that happen,” he said. “It’s fine.”

It’s not really just “life” for everyday Americans. Some have been stranded for days awaiting flights, out thousands of dollars, or unable to access work resources and banking information for their everyday needs. The Trump administration has largely botched the response to the flight ground stop, and Trump took days to address it himself. He was reportedly meeting with Jon Voight when the crisis came to a head on Friday. The string of subsequent outages across critical systems is more than a little concerning for Americans, even more so when the country’s leadership seems wholly incapable of taking them anything seriously.
