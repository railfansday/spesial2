Yahoo Top 10: Wild Week 12 shakes up college football rankings

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The most mundane weekends in college football can often turn into mayhem.

Two of the Big Ten's best playoff challengers (UCLA and Indiana) lost, preseason No. 1 Ohio State took a fourth 'L' this season and LSU — its high-priced head coach, Lane Kiffin, and $40 million-plus roster — dropped a fifth game.

The Bruins and Hoosiers served up the biggest surprises with overtime losses at Michigan and Washington, respectively, tossing the conference into a bit of chaos and uncertainty. Can the Big Ten win a fourth straight national championship? At this point, the odds seem steep for even getting more than two teams into the playoff. Also in the conference, Ohio State became the first preseason top-ranked team to lose at least four games since USC in 2012 as the Buckeyes dropped a game at Nebraska.

In the SEC, Tennessee kept its playoff hopes alive with a throttling in Knoxville of LSU, and Georgia survived the always-scary trip to Columbia, South Carolina. The Big 12 race is Utah, BYU and Texas Tech. Meanwhile, Miami and Wake Forest are on a collision course for a rematch in Charlotte (the Hurricanes won Round 1, 33-20) for all the ACC marbles.

The Utes blew open a 17-17 tie with three consecutive touchdowns in the final six minutes of the game. QB Devon Dampier scored the last two TDs — one passing and another rushing — on a night when he led the team on the ground and through the air. Dampier had more than 325 total yards and Larry Simmons caught three passes for a whopping 114 yards as Utah clinched a Big 12 championship game appearance.

Is anyone playing better football than Kirby Smart and crew? Smart's team turned a tight game into a blowout in the second half, even recovering a surprise onside kick in the third quarter. Gunner Stockton continues to be one of the nation's leaders in efficiency (19 of 23) and the Bulldogs know how to run the ball (Nate Frazier had 94 yards). But DC Glenn Schumann's defensive unit was great from the start in Columbia on Saturday night.

Ethan Barbour and the Georgia Bulldogs have plenty of reasons to flex these days. (Jeffrey Vest/Getty Images)

After beating Miami and bludgeoning SMU, there is little argument that the Irish, with a season-ending win over Syracuse next week, will make the 12-team postseason. The loss to BYU stings, sure, but Notre Dame QB CJ Carr continues to be one of the biggest challengers in the Heisman Trophy race. He threw for more than 300 yards against the Mustangs.

Down 14-0? No problem. The Hurricanes mounted a comeback at Hard Rock Stadium to solidify a trip to Charlotte for the ACC championship game — despite QB Darian Mensah not throwing a single touchdown pass. In fact, he had two interceptions to match his season total to this point. But Miami's defense rose to the challenge after the early hole, forcing Tech into five punts on its next seven drives.

If there was still any doubt that the Demon Deacons are real playoff challengers, those should be put to bed. Jake Dickert's team has outscored its last seven opponents 363-67. QB Gio Lopez threw for two touchdowns and ran for two more in the shellacking of the Yellow Jackets. Wake is barreling toward an ACC championship game appearance and a playoff spot.

The Tide rested Keelon Russell and why not? The 7-5 FCS Mocs didn't stand a chance. Seven of Chattanooga's 10 drives lasted three plays or less. Kane Wommack, a candidate for several head coaching gigs, and his defensive unit has allowed 20 points the last three games. Bama is headed to the playoff with a win in the Iron Bowl.

Michigan QB Bryce Underwood completed just 11 passes and was sacked four times. And while that doesn't sound like the stat line of a winner, the Bruins offense couldn't muster enough to beat the Wolverines. Ty Dieffenbach was sacked four times, threw an interception and lost a costly fumble that set up Michigan for the go-ahead score.

Arch Manning had four incompletions and three touchdowns in the win over the lowly Razorbacks. There's no hiding the Longhorns' deficiencies, but Texas has the talent to win it all and a victory over the Aggies will send them to the 12-team playoff. After back-to-back losses to Florida and Oklahoma, the Horns have won five straight.

No one benefitted from Saturday's Big Ten upset bonanza quite like the Hawkeyes. They are now in striking distance to advance to the playoff. And who would want to play Phil Parker's defense and an offensive unit that moves the ball better than any Hawkeye version in years. Iowa shut out the Illini in the second half and backup RB L.J. Phillips, in for the injured Kamari Moulton, ran for 154 yards — this third straight 100-yard outing.

When in doubt, run QB Faizon Brandon. That's what the Vols did. He ran for 112 yards on just 12 carries — nearly 10 yards a pop — and scored two touchdowns in the fourth quarter. It was more than enough for Tennessee to re-enter our Top 10 ahead of a big game in Nashville next week. Win and they're (likely) in the postseason.
