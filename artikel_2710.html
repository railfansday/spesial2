After landing Jeffers and Gray, GM Zoll says Twins aren't done adding

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Twins have already had a surprisingly splashy offseason. They retained catcher Ryan Jeffers for another season on the $23.1 million qualifying offer, then went out and bolstered their rotation by signing Sonny Gray to a two-year, $55 million deal.

Give credit where it's due. Owner Tom Pohlad is backing up his talk of investing in the team's roster in an effort to build a winner. General manager Jeremy Zoll is delivering. And it sounds like the Twins aren't done adding.

Zoll told reporters on Thursday that strengthening the bullpen and lineup are both still priorities for the front office, with less than two weeks until the looming lockout takes effect. "We're working both paths right now," he said, via Aaron Gleeman.

Gray also spoke to the media, and one thing he made clear is that his decision to sign with the Twins was at least partially driven by the team's pledge to keep adding and put a competitive roster on the field next season.

"There's no way we sign in Minnesota without believing and knowing there are good things coming and we will win," Gray said. "It just wouldn't have happened."

"There is still other things they're looking to do and looking to add," he noted, via The Athletic's Dan Hayes.

There's already a lot to like about what the Twins have on the roster for next season, especially in the starting rotation. But there's more work to be done. If the Twins can add at least one more bat and a bullpen arm or two, they'll be in business in 2027.
