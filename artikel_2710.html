Detroit Lions elevate LB Owen Pappoe for Week 10 vs. Patriots

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Detroit Lions announced they have elevated practice squad linebacker Owen Pappoe for Week 10 against the New England Patriots in Munich, Germany.

Pappoe signed to the Lions' practice squad just 10 days ago, after Jimmy Rolder (and eventually Jack Campbell) were placed on injured reserve. A former third-round pick of the Arizona Cardinals in 2023, Pappoe has made a career out of primarily playing special teams (49 games), but does have some experience on the defensive side of the ball as well. A highly athletic linebacker with 4.39-second speed, Pappoe can contribute in all five phases of special teams and act as an emergency option at WILL linebacker.

It's not unusual for the Lions to roster seven linebackers on game day, given how much they value special teams, and this elevation likely reflects his quick acclimation to the Lions system and ability to contribute this Sunday.

That said, with running backs Sione Vaki (ankle, questionable) and Jacob Saylors (neck, not listed with a designation) dealing with injuries, and Vaki's increased usage on offense, the Lions could certainly use a player like Pappoe to help fill gaps on special teams.

We will have to wait until inactives are announced ahead of the game for more details. Of note, with the Lions vs. Patriots game starting at 9:30 a.m. ET, inactives will be announced at 8 a.m. ET.
