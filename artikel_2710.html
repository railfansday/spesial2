WATCH: Xavier Hutchinson scores Houston’s first TD vs Chargers

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

On the Houston Texans' first drive of the game against the Los Angeles Chargers, quarterback C.J. Stroud led them on an eight-play, 73-yard touchdown drive that ended in a 17-yard touchdown pass to wide receiver Xavier Hutchinson to give them an early 7-0 lead.

Here is a look at Hutchinson's touchdown, courtesy of the Texans' official social media account:

It is the second game in a row Hutchinson has discord a touchdown after not scoring in the first six games of the season. Los Angeles responded with a field goal on their opening drive, cutting the Texans' lead to 7-3. The Chargers are looking for their first win of the season, while Houston is looking to get their third win of the year.
