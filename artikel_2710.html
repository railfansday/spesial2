Kirk Cousins Could Make Wildly Unique NFL History After Reuniting With The Vikings

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Kirk Cousins is headed back to the NFC North, as he will now be running it back with the Minnesota Vikings.

Minnesota took a swing at a trade for Cousins ahead of Tuesday's deadline, acquiring the veteran quarterback from the Las Vegas Raiders in exchange for just a third-round pick in the 2027 NFL Draft. The move comes on the heels of a horrendous start by Carson Wentz in the Vikings' loss to the Buffalo Bills on Monday.

The timing of the trade also puts the former Michigan State Spartans quarterback in a position to pull off something no other quarterback in the history of the league has managed to do.

"Kirk Cousins could become the first QB in NFL history to start 18 regular season games. Vikings already had their bye, Raiders have not," Steve Palazzolo of Check the Mic with Steve Palazzolo & Sam Monson shared on X.

Las Vegas Raiders quarterback Kirk Cousins (8) throws a pass against the Kansas City Chiefs during the third quarter at Allegiant Stadium. Mandatory Credit: David Brytus-Imagn Images

The Vikings are three weeks removed already from their Week 6 bye, while the Raiders will have theirs in Week 13. For someone being paid this season by three teams, Cousins can be just fine with playing more football. At 38 years old, Cousins can feel lucky that he's still playing and a starter in the NFL, so what's another start for him?

Cousins can make his first start in his return to Minnesota as soon as Sunday, when the Vikings visit the Green Bay Packers at Lambeau Field in Green Bay.

In nine starts for Las Vegas, Cousins passed for 2,340 yards and 20 touchdowns (second in the league) against eight interceptions on a 64.1 percent pass completion rate.

During his first stint with the Vikings, Cousins gathered 23,265 passing yards and found the end zone 171 times with his passes, while getting intercepted 55 times through 88 starts covering the 2018 to 2023 seasons.
