UIW earns at-large berth to FCS playoffs, will face No. 15 Montana

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Incarnate Word is headed back to the FCS playoffs for the fourth time in the past six years, earning an at-large selection and a trip to face No. 15 Montana at 7 p.m. Saturday in Missoula, Montana.

The game will air on ESPNU, and the winner advances to face No. 2 Tarleton State.

Northwestern State safety Christian Williams pushes UIW wide receiver Jamir Roberts out of bounds after Roberts scored a touchdown during a football game at Tom Benson Stadium, Saturday, October 31, 2026. (Cody Duty/Contributor)

The Cardinals opened the year 2-4 but caught fire in the second half of the season, closing with six straight wins by an average margin of 34.8 points.

UIW entered the day regarded as a bubble team and had to wait as the drama built. The Cardinals were announced as part of the final matchup unveiled during the selection show Sunday morning on EPSNU.

UIW will be appearing in the FCS playoffs for the fifth time, and the second time as an at-large selection.
