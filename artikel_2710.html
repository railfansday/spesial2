Bears DT Jeffery Simmons gets huge sack in Chicago debut en route to win over Seahawks

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

SEATTLE — Just four days after heading to the Chicago Bears, Jeffery Simmons immediately made an impact.

Simmons, who was acquired by the Bears on Thursday in a big trade with the Tennessee Titans, made his debut in blue and orange in a 34-17 win over the Seattle Seahawks on Monday night. And in the first quarter, the defensive tackle made a huge play, sacking the Seahawks' Sam Darnold for a 6-yard loss.

Unfortunately for Chicago, it didn't get to reap the rewards of the tackle due to a penalty. Grady Jarrett was flagged for a facemask that took place after the play, resulting in Seattle gaining 15 yards. But the sack counted in the books, giving Simmons his first big stat since changing teams.

Simmons finished with six total tackles, including three solo, in the massive win over Seattle. He also tipped a pass from Darnold that was collected by James Lynch for a game-sealing pick in the fourth quarter.

"It means a lot to me," Simmons said on the broadcast after the win, "to be able to play meaningful football."

The Bears paid up in order to get Simmons, sending Tennessee two first-round draft picks (a 2027 and a 2028) as part of the trade package. Chicago's defense has had its struggles, with the team allowing the most yards per attempt (8.7) in the league.

Despite the high cost, Bears general manager Ryan Poles said Friday that the team doesn't think of it as not caring about draft picks — or, as he calls it, the "F them picks" mentality. Instead, he touted Simmons' ability to make a difference both on the field and within the team's culture.

"I don't think it can be either or when you talk about a move this size," Poles said. "You need to have both. And that's what Jeffery does. We believe he's going to impact us at a high level on the grass but also in the locker room, too."
