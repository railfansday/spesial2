Pistons vs. Spurs predictions: Odds, recent stats, trends and best bets for November 17

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Cade Cuningham and Victor Wembanyama headline Tuesday's schedule in the NBA when the Pistons (8-4) visit the Spurs (12-2). Primarily due to injury issues and subsequent rotation dilemmas, the Pistons are a pedestrian 6-4 over their last 10 games and as a result sit third in the Eastern Conference. There is nothing average about San Antonio, though, to this point in the young season. The Spurs have won 12 straight and own the best record in the NBA. Detroit is averaging 115.7 points per game, but San Antonio has been superior at both ends, scoring 118.8 per night while allowing just 105.9. As mentioned Cade Cunningham is Detroit's superstar, averaging 25.7 points and 7.9 assists. Victor Wembanyama has been exceptional as well producing 28.6 points, 11.6 rebounds and 4.3 blocks per game for the Spurs.

Detroit comes in looking to rebound from a 137-117 loss at Miami on Saturday, a game in which the Pistons started quickly with 39 first-quarter points before being overwhelmed the rest of the way. Miami outscored Detroit 120-78 over the final three quarters, enjoyed a 28-11 advantage in fast-break points and blocked 14 shots. Giannis Antetokounmpo led Miami with 34 points and 14 rebounds as Detroit's two-game winning streak ended with a thud. The Pistons had previously survived a wild 144-140 double-overtime game in Washington. As mentioned, though, the Pistons have been forced to endure their share of injuries with starting center Jalen Duren out because of a hamstring injury, while Duncan Robinson and Isaiah Joe are listed day-to-day.

The Spurs extended their streak to 12 with a 110-102 victory at Utah on Sunday, following a dominant 123-85 rout of Golden State two nights earlier. During their current run they have repeatedly separated themselves with defense and size, and they enter Tuesday ranked first in the league in rebounding at 48.6 per game and averaging an NBA-best 7.1 blocks. Wembanyama is naturally at the center of everything, shooting 53.5% while combining elite scoring and rebounding with his game-changing rim protection. Rookie Dylan Harper has also emerged as a major contributor, averaging 18.3 points over his last 10 games, while De'Aaron Fox leads San Antonio with 5.8 assists per game. Fox, however, is day-to-day with a hip issue.

San Antonio controlled Detroit last season. Their most recent meeting came March 5, 2026, when the Spurs beat the Pistons 121-106 in San Antonio; San Antonio also won 114-103 in Detroit on February 23 to sweep the 2025-26 season series.
