Surreal scenes inside Steelers locker room as Aaron Rodgers drama exploded ‘in real time’

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The fallout is only beginning to hit the Steelers.

Following a late-night decision Tuesday to bench quarterback Aaron Rodgers, the veteran returned to his old stomping grounds on "The Pat McAfee Show" to fire allegations at head coach Mike McCarthy on Wednesday.

According to The Athletic, Steelers players watched the show "in real time" in the locker room, many on their phones, as Rodgers went to town on their coach.

Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers reacts at a news conference. AP Photo/Chris Szagola

The tension was palpable as the room opened to media, with TribLive reporting safety DeShon Elliott signaled to Drew Allar — now the No. 3 quarterback behind Mason Rudolph and Will Howard following the end of Rodgers' time in Pittsburgh — that he should stop speaking with media.

Those who did speak didn't have much to say, with tight end Pat Freiermuth noting he had "really nothing to add toward that."

"I'm just going to focus on answering questions about the Broncos and the game on Friday," he said.

Late Tuesday night, the Steelers announced they were benching the 42-year-old veteran in a shocking move the QB said was delivered to him by Freiermuth and not McCarthy.

In response, Rodgers went scorched earth, taking the opportunity Wednesday to submarine the man he won the 2011 Super Bowl with in Green Bay.

Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers (8) checks with head coach Mike McCarthy after calling a time-out. USA TODAY Network via Reuters Connect

"That ship has sailed," Rodgers said of his year-plus stint with the Steelers, going on to allege that McCarthy slept and snored in team meetings, and that the coach lost the locker room by ending a Pittsburgh halftime speech with "1-2-3 Packers."

Team sources corroborated both claims to The Athletic and ESPN.

On Monday, McCarthy said his longtime QB was slated to be the starter for Friday's game against the Broncos at Acrisure Stadium.

Aaron Rodgers went in on Steelers coach Mike McCarthy on Wednesday's "Pat McAfee Show." ESPN

Rodgers even addressed the media as part of his QB1 duties.

Then came Tuesday night's bombshell, the McAfee appearance and then the team waiving Rodgers on Wednesday.
