Pi Network price is approaching a crucial turning point as its first-ever Ecosystem Summit begins today, November 17. The three-day event promises developer announcements, AI integrations, technical demonstrations and a $200,000 USDT hackathon, raising expectations for fresh ecosystem activity. However, PI is entering the event under significant market pressure, having slipped to a new record low near $0.05678.

With investors looking for signs of real adoption, can the Pi Ecosystem Summit revive demand for PI, or will the token’s decline continue despite the upcoming announcements?

Pi Network’s first major developer-focused summit begins today, bringing renewed attention to its efforts to expand the ecosystem beyond mobile mining. The online event runs from November 17 to November 19, with each day focusing on a different development milestone.

The first two days will feature technical demonstrations, developer conversations and presentations from the Pi Core Team. The third day will introduce Pi Next, a new hackathon designed to encourage developers to build applications on the network.

The event comes as Pi attempts to strengthen its developer infrastructure and create more practical uses for its native token. The challenge is turning announcements into applications that attract regular users and generate economic activity.

AI development is emerging as one of the most important themes for Pi Network, with the November 18 programme expected to focus on artificial intelligence and ecosystem applications.

The project has already introduced tools aimed at making application development easier. These include Pi App Studio, SoloHost, its Developer Portal and software development kit. Pi says its ecosystem provides access to more than 60 million engaged Pioneers, over 300 Mainnet applications and a network of more than 420,000 Nodes. It also reports more than 18.1 million fully KYC-verified users.

These figures provide a potential distribution advantage for developers building applications on Pi. However, the next challenge is demonstrating that these users will actively engage with applications, make payments and generate recurring demand for the network.

The AI announcements could therefore become an important test of Pi’s broader strategy. New developer tools may attract attention, but actual adoption will depend on whether they produce useful applications and sustained user activity.

The final day of the summit will introduce Pi Next, a hackathon featuring a $200,000 USDT prize pool. The initiative is designed to attract developers and startups to build applications using Pi’s infrastructure. It could also encourage new projects across payments, AI applications and other ecosystem services.

For Pi, the hackathon is more than a developer competition. It represents an opportunity to expand the number of applications available to users and strengthen the network’s utility.

Investors will be watching whether the summit delivers measurable progress rather than another round of ecosystem promises.

PI price remains under heavy pressure despite the timing of the Ecosystem Summit. The token was trading around $0.059 on November 17, after touching a new all-time low near $0.05678. Its market capitalization has fallen toward $675 million, while daily trading volume remains relatively modest at approximately $6.5 million.

The $0.056–$0.057 region is the immediate support area to watch, given the latest record low. A sustained breakdown below this zone could extend the decline into untested territory.

On the upside, PI needs to reclaim the broken consolidation range to signal that buyers are returning. A recovery without stronger trading volume may struggle to sustain momentum. The summit could provide a short-term catalyst, but the price structure suggests that investors still need convincing evidence of renewed demand.
