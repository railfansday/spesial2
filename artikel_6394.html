Taco Bell Is Expanding Its Cantina Menu With Six New Steak Creations

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Your usual order could be getting some serious competition.

Taste-testing Taco Bell's new FLAMIN' HOT menu items should've bought my stomach at least a few days off. Instead, the chain has already given me another reason to start thinking about my next order.

Taco Bell is testing a new carne asada protein across six menu items, giving its Cantina lineup another round of additions. But of course, there's a catch. As of November 12, it's only available in Nashville, Tennessee, and the Minneapolis-St. Paul area. So if you're lucky enough to live in either market, congratulations. I'll just be over here drooling over the pictures.

The steak itself sounds pretty different from what Taco Bell currently serves. It gets a marinade with citrus and savory seasonings before being cooked sous vide, then seared and cut into small chunks. Taco Bell says it's going for a deeper, more grilled flavor, and rather than giving it one or two menu appearances, the chain has come up with six ways to try it.

The Cantina Carne Asada Chalupa Lime Bowl is probably the most loaded of the bunch, piling steak, rice, black beans, guacamole, pico de gallo, cheese, and purple cabbage slaw together with chile-lime seasoned Chalupa Chips. There's also a Cantina Carne Asada Burrito, which wraps the meat in a new Sonoran-style flour tortilla with lettuce, cabbage, cheese, pico, and Spicy Ranch Sauce before getting grilled.

If tacos are more your speed, there are three options. The Cantina Carne Asada Crispy Taco comes in a fried corn shell with cheese grilled onto the outside, while the Cantina Carne Asada Soft Taco uses that same new flour tortilla with cabbage slaw and Spicy Ranch. Then there's the smaller Cantina Carne Asada Street Chalupa, which has a fried shell lined with crispy cheese.

Rounding things out is the Cantina Carne Asada Rolled Quesadilla, stuffed with steak, a three-cheese blend, and Spicy Ranch before being grilled and sliced. All six items also come with an Avocado Verde Salsa packet.

The rollout follows Taco Bell's push to give its Cantina menu more attention, including the slow-roasted chicken that's already made its way into items like the Cantina Chicken Mexican Pizza. This latest test could eventually put carne asada on the permanent menu, although there's no word yet on whether it'll expand beyond those two markets.
