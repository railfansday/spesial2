Ring Energy (REI) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Front-loaded drilling pushed free cash flow negative even as debt fell.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Ring Energy, Inc. (REI +0.45%) reported a third quarter defined by the divestiture of noncore assets and the acceleration of a co-developed long-lateral program in the Central Basin Platform. Management reported that the company deliberately front-loaded capital expenditures to transition toward a more capital-efficient development model, which resulted in negative adjusted free cash flow for the period. The company indicated that proceeds from asset sales were used to reduce debt and acquire leasehold interests adjacent to existing operations. Management stated that the development strategy focuses on increasing production and reserves per dollar spent by drilling multiple zones from single pads. Furthermore, the company established a preliminary outlook for 2027 that targets organic production growth while maintaining cost discipline and strengthening the balance sheet.
