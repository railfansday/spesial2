Lil Baby’s ‘FRESH’ Starts at No. 1 on Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums Chart

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

His "Dead Fresh" single finally reigns on Hot Rap Songs after 14 weeks at No. 2.

Lil Baby claims his fifth No. 1 on Billboard’s Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums chart as FRESH launches atop the list dated Nov. 21. The set, released on Quality Control/Motown/Interscope Capitol, begins with 59,000 equivalent album units earned in the United States for the tracking week of Nov. 6-12, according to Luminate.

Plus, the set’s de facto title track, “Dead Fresh,” rises to No. 1 on the Hot Rap Songs chart for the first time, capping a 17-week journey to the penthouse.

For FRESH, streaming activity powered nearly all the debut sum, with 55,000 units, representing 56.8 million official on-demand streams of the album’s songs. (Thanks to that figure, it opens at No. 1 on the Top Streaming Albums chart.) Four thousand units derived from traditional album sales, with a negligible number of activity from the final metric, track-equivalent albums. (One unit equals the following levels of consumption: one album sale, 10 individual tracks sold from an album, or 1,000 paid/subscription tier or 2,500 ad-supported tier official on-demand audio and video streams for a song on the album.)

With FRESH, Lil Baby scores his fifth leader on Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums. He first reigned via 2020’s My Turn, which posted six weeks in the summit and was later named the chart’s top album of the first 25 years of the century, and followed with the one-week champ The Voice of the Heroes, a 2021 collaboration with Lil Durk, three weeks with 2022’s It’s Only Me and one week with 2025’s WHAM.

Elsewhere, FRESH becomes the Atlanta rapper’s sixth No. 1 on Top Rap Albums — all five of the Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums No. 1s, plus 2025’s The Leaks — and arrives at No. 2 on the all-genre Billboard 200.

The triumph continues with the album’s single “Dead Fresh,” which ascends to No. 1 on Hot Rap Songs. The track marks Lil Baby’s fourth chart-topper, following three in 2022 that each ruled for one week: “Do We Have a Problem,” with Nicki Minaj, via his and Drake’s features on DJ Khaled’s “Stayin Alive” and his own “California Breeze.”

“Dead Fresh” seemed nearly destined to come tantalizingly close to the summit, tracing to its No. 2 debut on the chart dated Aug. 1, and holding the rank for 14 non-consecutive weeks, all behind Drake’s “Janice STFU.” By toppling its main competitor, “Dead Fresh” completes its trek in its 17th week on the chart, one of the four longest waits of the 2020s. It matches Lil Uzi Vert’s “Just Wanna Rock” in time to the top, with only Latto’s “Big Energy” (22 weeks) and BigXthaPlug’s “All the Way,” featuring Bailey Zimmerman (21) requiring more.

Over on Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs, “Dead Fresh” leads a parade of 17 Lil Baby tracks on the 50-position list this week. The new arrivals include “Mr. Do Something” (No. 8) and “Top Uh It” (No. 10), pushing the rapper to 40 top 10s in his career.
