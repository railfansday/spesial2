Tanggal: 2026-11-03

After everything Mizu survived in Blue Eye Samurai’s first season, the prospect of an ocean crossing almost sounds restful. Maya Erskine’s swordswoman fought her way through an extraordinary number of people, repeatedly treated devastating injuries as minor inconveniences, and remained spectacularly resistant to accepting help. Anyone hoping the trip might give her time to reconsider her priorities should probably lower their expectations.

The second season takes her beyond Japan, following a finale that left several characters facing lives they hadn’t necessarily planned for. Mizu has another destination and more names to pursue. The people she leaves behind have their own problems, including the consequences of deciding what they want and discovering what getting it might cost.

Netflix has announced that Blue Eye Samurai Season 2 will premiere January 19, 2027, with six new episodes. Created by Amber Noizumi and Michael Green, the Emmy-winning animated series picks up with Mizu traveling to London to find the two remaining men who might be her father. The new season adds Jonathan Pryce, Joel Kim Booster, and Sherry Cola to its voice cast. Animation studio Blue Spirit returns to bring the story to life, with Mizu facing new allies, familiar enemies, and the personal demons that won’t conveniently stay behind when she reaches another country.

London puts Mizu in an unfamiliar position beyond having to find her way around. Her revenge is inseparable from the hatred she has endured for being mixed-race, and she is now entering a society that could challenge her assumptions about how others see her. That makes the move more interesting than supplying her with different streets to fight through. Her skill with a sword can get her out of plenty of situations. It cannot settle every question she has about herself. Back in Japan, Akemi and Taigen must navigate life at Edo Castle under a dangerous new shogun. Ringo, meanwhile, searches for a new purpose. Brenda Song, Darren Barnet, and Masi Oka return to voice the characters, whose stories continue alongside Mizu’s overseas pursuit.

Akemi’s next steps are especially worth watching after her determination to gain control over her life became one of the first season’s strongest stories. Reaching a position of influence brings its own dangers, particularly within a court where power offers little guarantee of safety. Ringo’s search carries a different emotional weight: helping Mizu gave him direction, but his future deserves to amount to more than waiting for her return.

With six episodes, the new season has two very different worlds to explore and plenty of unfinished business in both. Mizu may be focused on reaching the next person on her list, but the people around her have ambitions that won’t stop moving while she’s away.

Blue Eye Samurai Season 2 premieres January 19, 2027, on Netflix.
