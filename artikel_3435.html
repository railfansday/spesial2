Karin Slaughter Teases The Good Daughter Sequel Plans After 6-Part Mystery Series Adaptation

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Warning: SPOILERS lie ahead for The Good Daughter!Karin Slaughter may have more stories for the Quinn sisters after The Good Daughter.

Adapted from the 2017 novel of the same name, the Peacock series centers on sisters Charlie and Sam Quinn, whose teenage years were upended by the murder of their mother and attacks on them during a home invasion, and are reunited after being estranged for a decade when their defense attorney father's latest case forces them to confront their past. Led by Rose Byrne, Meghann Fahy, Brendan Gleeson and Harper Steele, The Good Daughter has scored generally positive reviews from critics.

Now, in an interview with Entertainment Weekly, Slaughter has addressed the chances of The Good Daughter getting a season 2 or published sequel. The author/creator began by humorously stating that if the Quinn sisters are "gonna be in a new story by Karin Slaughter, it's never gonna be for a good thing." Even still, she did confirm that she has been formulating thoughts on a potential follow-up:

Karin Slaughter: Don’t think they would be really happy in the beginning. Then, bad stuff would happen, and then they would get through it and be happy again. Charlie and Ben moved to Atlanta, and start jobs there and start over. I have given a lot of thought to [what’s next].

Slaughter is certainly no stranger to writing follow-up stories to her works, her most notable being that of the Will Trent novel series, which has thus far comprised of 12 novels, and the Grant Country series, which ran for six books. Even some of her seemingly standalone stories would later get follow-ups in various fashion, including 2018's Pieces of Her, which was the first of Slaughter's work to be adapted for the screen, and got a sequel book in 2022 with Girl, Forgotten.

As for The Good Daughter itself, the show, much like its source material, did largely tie up the threads of its core plots. Sam and Charlie got closure regarding their mother's death and their attacks as they learned that Jake McDornan's Huck was the one who orchestrated the event, wanting revenge for his sister's suicide, which he blamed on Gleeson's Rusty for his part working against her in her previous trial accusing a man of sexually assaulting her.

Moreover, a series of monthly payments went to the son of Huck's accomplice by his parents so that he'd never be named, only for Huck to learn about this and confess directly to Rusty, who took over the payments and forced him to write a confession for if anything changed. The Quinn sisters would turn Huck in for his hiding the gun involved in The Good Daughter's other big mystery, the school shooting seemingly carried out by Shayna Bernardo's Kelly.

Will Trent is naturally often compared to the Karin Slaughter books, but a lot of ABC’s most notable changes were ultimately for the better.

The show's ending further saw them and Charlie's estranged husband, Michael Dorman's Ben, determine that it was done by Paula Malcomson's Judith forcing Kelly's hand after learning her husband, Douglas, was having a sexual relationship with Kelly and got her pregnant. While attempting to, and succeeding in, getting Judith's confession, Charlie also witnesses her take her own life out of guilt.

That being said, there is still some room for Slaughter to develop a follow-up to The Good Daughter, be it on screen or on the page. The show ends with Charlie and Ben starting to reconcile and plan for a move to Atlanta, where Sam's law firm has a satellite office, and the sisters have similarly begun rebuilding their bridges after sharing and learning the truths of their tragic pasts, while also still leaving plenty for the other to unpack from the years they spent apart.

The Good Daughter is now streaming in its entirety on Peacock.
