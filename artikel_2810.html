Breaking down Auburn football's blowout of Samford: Advanced stats

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Auburn football finally has it. A bowl berth was already delivered the week prior, and the program secured a winning season in its latest contest, beating FCS Samford 59-17 on Saturday, Nov. 21, at Jordan-Hare Stadium.

It was the seventh win of the season, which Auburn hasn't been able to say since 2019 when it last reached that mark. It hadn't mustered a record above .500 since 2020, and in blowout out lesser competition a week out from the Iron Bowl, Alex Golesh has Auburn moving with momentum late in November.
