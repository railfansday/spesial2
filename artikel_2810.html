NFL power rankings, updated entering Sunday Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

We will officially be halfway through the NFL season once Week 9 is in the books. This past weekend the Vikings fell to the Lions, ensuring that the 1973 Dolphins will remain the only team to achieve a perfect season in NFL history. (We're sure Nick Buonaconti and Don Shula popped a champagne bottle somewhere over the rainbow.) With zero undefeated teams remaining, who will sit atop our power rankings as we prepare for the final Sunday of the first half of the season? Let's dig in and find out.

They're the easiest team to rank on this list since they are the only team in the league without a win. Coach Harbaugh has sent Mike McDaniel to the booth to see if that changes anything. It feels a lot like rearranging the deck chairs on the Titanic right after it split in half, but we'll see.

Another week and another loss, right? Not exactly. They took the Bengals to overtime in what could have been Cam Ward's best game of his career. It's a step in the right direction and a sign that Robert Saleh has this team playing hard despite the loss of Jeffrey Simmons.

Malik Willis has looked impressive, and his receiving targets also look to be emerging as real NFL offensive threats. Things seem to be moving in the right direction.

Last week's game against the Raiders was exciting, but at the end of the day it was another loss for Aaron Glenn and the Jets. That last pass from Geno Smith had many Jet fans wishing the network cut the feed to air "Heidi".

The roster purge isn't quite over yet, and it will be interesting to see what's left of this team after Tuesday's trade deadline. Whoever is left in Indy will likely have to endure a painful rest of the season.

Now coming to the stage….Joe Milton!!!! This should be fun to watch. It might not be pretty, but Milton brings a combination of size, speed, and arm strength that no other QB outside of Josh Allen can.

Coming off of their bye at 2-5, the Saints need to step up against Shadeur Sanders and the Browns this weekend if they still have any hope of getting into this NFC South race.

They win when you don't expect it, and then look like one of the worst teams in the league. Sometimes they do both in the same game. Now, they are handing the reins to Gardner Minshew for a few weeks. That should last until their Week 13 bye, and then we should see Carson Beck. Either way, this team is a rare combination of bad and dangerous.

They beat the Eagles, which means they aren't quite the worst team in their division (at this point in the season). The NFC East is just ugly enough that these guys could get hot and get in the mix.

They clearly did not like playing in the rain. At 3-5 they are firmly in the mix for the NFC South crown, but they are going to have to play better than they have the last couple of weeks.

They have now won 2 straight with Baker under center. They have also won 2 straight without needing Baker to do a darn thing. Something tells us that will change when they face the Bears this weekend.

They may be the best team in the NFC South, but that still doesn't mean that they're a top 20 team in the league. This week they get Denver at home.

They have mastered the art of playing down to their opponents. It's either that or they just plain stink. We'll know more after this week's game against the reeling Colts.

Losing their top 2 QBs showed us how terrible a team can look without a QB. They literally didn't have a QB for the better part of their last 2 games, and it has cost them. Now Jacoby Brissett will come to town to stop the bleeding, but it may be too late in a competitive AFC North.

We're giving this team a bit of the old benefit of the doubt right now as they come off of their bye with a disappointing 2-5 record. This defense needs to figure things out in a hurry or this season will be over. This week they get a Charger offense that has been stuck in Neutral all season.

Last week's loss to the Steelers might have told us more about the Browns than it did about Pittsburgh. Shadeur Sanders will look to get this team back in the win column this Sunday as they take on the Saints.

They had a great bye week as they sat at home and watched the Cowboys and Eagles lose. They get Philly this weekend, and if they can handle Joe Milton, this team might actually be able to roll through this season and win the East handily.

Kirko is playing at a near-elite level this season, leading this Raiders offense to wins without much of a running game to back him up. They have a tough one this week against the 49ers.

They are sitting in 1st place in the North, and Aaron Rodgers has the offense looking decent. The defense has done a good deal of the heavy lifting for this team, and now they get a week off to rest as the team has a Week 9 bye.

The defense has been getting better despite Micah Parsons still being on the shelf. The offense still struggles to run the ball, but Jordan Love is having an incredible season thus far. If they can get the run game going, this team will be a strong playoff contender.

They now have 3 straight wins as Jared Goff and this offense continue to show that they are one of the best units in the league. Last week they were able to shine despite facing a tough Vikings defense.

Drake Maye is keeping this team afloat despite a lack of weapons. This week, they take on the Packers without AJ Brown, Treveyon Henderson, Romeo Doubs, and Hunter Henry.

Joe Burrow is making a strong case for league MVP honors as he has been lighting up the scoreboard on a weekly basis. If they can get the running game going, they have a good enough offense that they can just outscore their opponents every week.

Sam Darnold looks to be out for a couple of weeks, so the team will turn to Drew Lock to lead the offense. The defense and the running game will have to be enough to handle teams like the Cardinals and Raiders before they get Week 11 off with their bye.

They've managed to even their record at 4-4, and Myles Garrett is coming back. They are still missing a receiving option outside of Puka Nacua, but Matthew Stafford has still been able to make things work.

The offense has been sputtering, and they have now lost 2 straight games. Patrick Mahomes will need to right the ship, and this week's opponent (the Jets) feels like the perfect punching bag for Kansas City to work out their frustrations on.

They are still managing to win games in ugly fashion. Last weekend's victory over the Chiefs was yet another ugly feather in Sean Payton's hat. Those feathers are all adding up to a potential home playoff game, so you won't hear Broncos fans complaining.

They lost their first game of the year and now will look to Carson Wentz to fill in for an injured Kyler Murray. If Wentz can get this offense going, there's a chance this change could be permanent.

Josh Allen and this offense are still banging away at opponents every week. It remains to be seen if this defense is good enough for Buffalo to be a serious playoff contender.

A week off came at a perfect time for this banged-up offense. Unfortunately, they need a bit more time before they can get Mike Evans and Deebo Samuel fully healthy.

They look like the most complete team in the AFC right now. The offense is clicking, and the defense is one of the top units in the league.

The only question we had about this team was their defense. Jeffrey Simmons joined them last week, and that seemed to have an immediate impact. With Simmons wrecking offensive lines every play, this secondary isn't quite as leaky as it was a week ago.
