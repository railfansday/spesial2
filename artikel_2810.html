Colston Loveland injury update: Bears TE ruled out with knee injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Chicago Bears tight end Colston Loveland is off to a bad start to his Thanksgiving.

On Chicago's first possession of the Thanksgiving matchup vs. the Detroit Lions, Loveland's fumble in the red zone gave the ball back to Detroit. One possession later, the tight end had to be carted back to the locker room with an apparent lower body injury.

Loveland appeared to sustain his injury on the first play of the Bears' second offensive drive. While blocking a Lions defender on D'Andre Swift's short run up the middle, the tight end had his ankle rolled up on by Swift and Lions defensive tackle Tyleik Williams.

CBS cameras showed Loveland receiving attention from trainers on the sideline, and he was eventually carted back to the locker room.

The Bears ruled Loveland out for the remainder of the Thanksgiving game against the Lions with a knee injury.

The second-year tight end appeared to sustain the ailment when his lower leg got rolled up on while he was making a block. One of Detroit's defensive lineman, Williams, tackled Swift, the Bears' running back, and both players got tangled up with Loveland, who was making a block.

Loveland had to be carted back to the locker room, and CBS sideline reporter Tracy Wolfson reported he was in tears.
