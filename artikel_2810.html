South Carolina QB LaNorris Sellers is spending fall semester working at a golf course?

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

South Carolina football quarterback LaNorris Sellers needs a quiet place when he's not leading the Gamecocks. Even amid boosting his NFL Draft stock after shredding Oklahoma on Saturday.

But his peaceful venue happens to be his side hustle: Working at a local golf center, which he shared to ESPN.

Sellers is employed at Par Tee Golf Center in nearby West Columbia. The links also keeps him away from the classroom on the South Carolina campus. Because Sellers doesn't have a regular class schedule like the rest of his teammates.

Instead, Sellers is using the golf center for his required internship before officially graduating. It's all part of his sports and entertainment management degree he's pursuing.

So what duties does Sellers perform when he's not dissecting defenses and going after them with his arm and legs? He's performing the traditional labor involved with golf course workers: Picking up golf balls from the range, plus cutting grass.

Now Sellers is almost complete with his internship at the driving range. Perhaps a spot at The Masters in Augusta, Georgia could be in his future, especially if that renown event needs additional help.

But outside of seeing golf swings, Sellers and South Carolina is taking a big swing at staying alive in the College Football Playoff race. The Gamecocks managed to sneak into the top 25 of the CFP poll released on Tuesday. South Carolina's upset over a previously No. 11 ranked Tennessee team certainly helps the Gamecocks' case. Sellers and SC are one of three teams with three losses to make it inside the postseason rankings, joining Florida and Oregon.
