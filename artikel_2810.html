Matt LaFleur pushes back on Packers’ right guard criticism after Week 8

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Green Bay Packers opted to start Laken Tomlinson at right guard in Week 8, even though Kevin Zeitler was available. The decision drew questions after the position struggled during the game. When reporters asked Matt LaFleur to explain the choice, the head coach pushed back against their assessment of the right guard play.

"You didn't think our play at right guard was that great?" asked the Packers head coach.

"Okay, you and I can agree to disagree on that, so I guess that is your opinion."

LaFleur stopped short of confirming whether Tomlinson would remain the starting right guard in Week 9. Still, he stood by the veteran lineman's performance, noting that Tomlinson handled his first snaps at the position since 2016 well.

Packers head coach Matt LaFleur, left, talks with defensive coordinator Jonathan Gannon during the fourth quarter of their preseason game Friday, August 28, 2026 at Lambeau Field in Green Bay, Wisconsin. The Packers beat the Arizona Cardinals 42-38.

"Yeah, potentially, it is a week-to-week league. But I think Laken went out there and did a pretty good job. There were some looks where, especially in the run game, we need to be better on our details. But it did not fall on the individual. It was on the collective, and there were some bad plays called in there as well," LaFleur added.

Tomlinson earned a 72.3 PFF grade in pass protection but posted just 47.5 as a run blocker. Those numbers appear to support the concerns raised about his overall performance at right guard.

Packers head coach Matt LaFleur is shown before their game against the Dallas Cowboys Sunday, Oct. 18, 2026 at Lambeau Field in Green Bay, Wisconsin.

LaFleur's comments suggest Green Bay is willing to sacrifice some effectiveness in the running game to prioritize its passing attack. The approach has helped the Packers reach a 5-3 record, making it difficult to argue too strongly against the strategy.

Zeitler could help strengthen Green Bay's rushing attack, but the coaching staff seems to favor Tomlinson's ability to protect the quarterback. His lower marks as a run blocker have fueled criticism of his play at right guard, yet LaFleur appears more focused on his pass protection. That difference in priorities explains why the head coach views Tomlinson's performance more favorably than the media does.
