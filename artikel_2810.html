Yale bounced back from consecutive losses with a 28-0 win over Princeton on Saturday. Quarterback Dante Reno threw three touchdown passes, connecting twice with Lucius Anderson and once with Graham Smith as the Bulldogs built a 14-0 halftime lead. Yale's defense was just as impressive, holding Princeton scoreless and forcing two interceptions. Abu Kamara finished with eight tackles, four tackles for loss and a sack before returning an interception 36 yards for a touchdown in the fourth quarter. Running back Hugo Djeumeni Kemeni added 113 rushing yards, his seventh consecutive game reaching 100 yards.

Harvard secured at least a share of its fourth consecutive Ivy League championship with a 38-24 comeback win over Penn on Saturday. The Crimson jumped ahead 17-3 in the second quarter before allowing 21 unanswered points to enter halftime trailing 24-17. Quarterback Charlie Smith helped turn things around with a 34-yard touchdown pass to Nathan Bailey in the third quarter, tying the game before Harvard's rushing attack took over. Xaviah Bascon scored the go-ahead touchdown early in the fourth quarter, and Kise Flannery added another score to seal the victory. Smith finished with three touchdown passes, while Bailey caught 13 passes for 113 yards.
