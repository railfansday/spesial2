Pakistan head coach Mike Hesson loses cool over Babar Azam question during presser - Watch

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

NEW DELHI: Pakistan head coach Mike Hesson lost his patience at a press conference after being repeatedly questioned about Babar Azam's poor form and whether fans should be concerned ahead of next year's World Cup.Hesson defended the star batter and made it clear that he wanted journalists to focus more on Pakistan's performance against Sri Lanka rather than repeatedly discussing Babar.The questions came after Babar scored 92 runs in three innings during the ongoing Test series against Sri Lanka. Although the batter has shown some good performances, his consistency has remained a concern among fans."In all honesty, I've had enough of those questions. You ask the same question at every single press conference. Babar is averaging close to 50 in Test cricket, so we're certainly not worried about him. I'll give you the same answer I gave last time," Hesson said after the second day's play in the second Test.

The Pakistan coach further urged reporters to stop asking the same questions about Babar and discuss the team's performance instead."Can we stop talking about the same questions every time and actually ask about the cricket that's being played? Pakistan are playing some good cricket here, so how about we start focusing on that? Next question, please," Hesson added.Watch:

Babar scored 50 and 34 in the two innings of the first Test, which ended in a draw. However, he managed just eight runs in the first innings of the second Test before being dismissed by Lahiru Kumara. His total of 92 runs in three innings has come at an average of 30.66.Meanwhile, Pakistan strengthened their position in the Rawalpindi Test after bowling Sri Lanka out for 98 and scoring 325 in reply. Ghazi Ghori top-scored with 54, while Razaullah contributed an attacking 42 off 32 balls.Sri Lanka ended Day 2 at 74/3 in their second innings, trailing Pakistan by 153 runs.
