Jonathon Brooks continues to be a full participant

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Carolina Panthers RB Jonathon Brooks (groin) fully participated in practice Thursday, Nov. 19.

Why It Matters: Brooks has been a full participant for the second straight week, so there is a decent chance that he will be activated before the Week 11 game. He will see a limited workload if he is cleared to play again but could be a possible flex option in some deeper leagues.
