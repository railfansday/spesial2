Citing hearing was 'extra stress' for Prendergast

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Ireland's Sam Prendergast has said head coach Andy Farrell was "unbelievably supportive" while the fly-half waited to find out whether he would be available to face South Africa last weekend.

The Leinster playmaker had a citing complaint against him dismissed before starting in the 20-17 victory over the back-to-back world champions, but the alleged hip-drop tackle was brought up in media conferences by Springbok coaches Rassie Erasmus and Mzwandile Stick.

After the game, Farrell said he had not liked the comments made by his South African counterparts.

"It was a little bit hard," Prendergast said of facing a hearing during the build-up to such an important match.

"In terms of you're preparing for the game, but you also have that in the back of your mind - 'will I be playing?'

"After meeting with everyone at the start of the week, we felt pretty confident in the case. It added a little bit of extra stress, but that was early in the week.

"Once I got it out of the way it was pretty easy just to focus on the game."

Despite winning five of their six games in the Nations Championship, Ireland finished third in the northern hemisphere table.

They will finish the competition against Australia on Friday night (20:10 GMT) in what is expected to be a sparsely attended fixture in Twickenham.

Prendergast, however, believes Ireland will put just as much into the game as they did against South Africa.

"All I know is that we'll be taking it incredibly seriously," he added.

"We've taken every game as it comes this autumn, in terms of first game up [against Argentina], and then Fiji were such a tough Test.

"Last week was obviously a huge game, but I think this week is just as big."
