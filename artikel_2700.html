Warriors receive good Steph Curry news amid oblique injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Warriors evaluated Steph Curry Friday.

The superstar has missed the last six games with an oblique abdominal tear, but he is reportedly making good progress.

While there is not a firm timeline for his return, he will be re-evaluated next Wednesday and has started individual on-court workouts.

Golden State Warriors guard Stephen Curry, right, confers with head coach Steve Kerr during an NBA basketball game in Denver, Monday, Oct. 26, 2026. AP Photo/David Zalubowski

Curry will miss the next three games as he continues the recovery process.

Across 10 games this season, Curry averaged 27.8 points, 3.9 assists and 3.7 rebounds. The Warriors were 7-3 when Curry was healthy.

Golden State has evidently missed its veteran star as the team holds a 10-8 record. The 38-year-old tore his oblique in the first quarter of a Nov. 11 loss to the Rockets and the Warriors proceeded to go winless on its five-game road trip.

The expectation was that Curry would miss at least a month with the oblique injury, but the Warriors have recently shown some resilience to his absence with wins over the Kings and Celtics.
