Wizards’ Anthony Davis, Trae Young catch strays from NBA insiders scouting AJ Dybantsa

The Washington Wizards came into the new season with lofty expectations. They drafted AJ Dybantsa first overall in the 2026 NBA Draft. They also were expecting full seasons from Trae Young and Anthony Davis, former All-Stars who the team acquired last season, but neither of whom played much or at all with the team in 2025-26.

However, the Wizards haven't gotten off to a great start. Dybantsa started of the season sidelined because of an ankle injury, and Young and Davis haven't looked quite like their old selves. It has all resulted in just a 6-11 record for the Wizards. Now, Dybantsa has gotten better as the season has progressed. The BYU product has surpassed the 20-point mark in each of the past three games, finally starting to show his megastar potential. He is a freak athlete who thrives in transition and as a slasher, but his jumper hasn't been falling as hoped. Dybantsa is shooting just 23.5% from deep, and some members around the league think his situation is to blame for that.

Brian Windhorst and Tim MacMahon talked to executives and scouts around the league about this rookie crop, and an Eastern Conference scout said, "He's in a tough environment for a rookie. There's not a high level of accountability there for their top players, so there isn't down the line."
