Georgia Football Surges To No. 2 After Crushing Florida

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Georgia Bulldogs are back on top of their game, climbing to the number two spot in the latest AP Top 25 poll. This rise comes after a dominant 27-8 victory over their SEC rival, the Florida Gators. The win fixes a rough patch for the team, which had lost to Auburn just two weeks ago. Now sitting at 7-1 overall and 5-1 in conference play, the Bulldogs have restarted their win streak with authority.

While Georgia moves up, the Ohio State Buckeyes fell hard. They lost to USC and dropped nine spots in the rankings, leaving a wide-open path for Atlanta to challenge for the top seed. Miami still holds the number one rank after a narrow 38-35 win over North Carolina, but the gap between the Hurricanes and the Bulldogs is shrinking fast.

Every fan knows the sting of a bad loss. The Bulldogs suffered one against Auburn, a game that stalled their momentum and shook their confidence. That loss made the path to the playoffs look much harder for the entire season. But football is about how you bounce back, not just how you start.

According to Yahoo Sports, the win against Florida proves the team has learned from that mistake. The Bulldogs showed they can still dominate their biggest rivals when they play with focus. This is the kind of performance that convinces voters to move a team up the ladder, even with one blemish on their record.

The debate for the top spot is fierce right now. Miami is unbeaten at 8-0, but their wins have been messy, and their resume is not as strong as it looks on paper.

Georgia has a better argument for the top spot if they keep winning. They have beaten Alabama and Florida, two of the toughest teams in the country. The only thing stopping them from being number one is that early loss to Auburn. If they can stay perfect for the rest of the season, the voters might finally switch their minds. The Bulldogs have strong wins over Oklahoma and Alabama, which give them a solid case for the crown.

Resting on a ranking is never a good idea. The Bulldogs face their next challenge this Saturday, November 7, at 7:30 p.m. ET against the Ole Miss Rebels. Ole Miss is already ranked 12th and enters the game with a 6-2 record. This is another huge SEC showdown that could define the rest of the season.

The Bulldogs have to win this game to keep their title hopes alive. A loss here would hurt their chances more than the Auburn game did. The team has to play like the number two squad they are ranked as, not the team that lost to Auburn.
