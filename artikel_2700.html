Where Florida football lands in USAT Sports' latest bowl projections

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Florida football is coming off a much-needed victory over Kentucky, and with the regular season winding down, the Gators' postseason outlook begins to take shape.

Following an inconsistent stretch during Jon Sumrall's first season, Florida has managed to still comfortably clinch a spot in the postseason. In USA TODAY Sports' latest bowl projections, Florida is set to face the Arizona Wildcats in the Las Vegas Bowl on Dec. 31 at Allegiant Stadium.

Arizona has put together a strong 2026 campaign, sitting at 7-3, as they enter the latter part of their schedule. If this projection holds, it would mark the first meeting between the two programs.

Florida made its first and only appearance in the Las Vegas Bowl in 2022, under former head coach Billy Napier's first season. The trip to Sin City ended in disappointment when the Gators suffered a 30-3 defeat against No. 17 Oregon State, in which UF's offense failed to generate any production on the ground, finishing with only 39 rushing yards.

A return to Las Vegas would give the Gators a chance to avenge their blowout loss and claim their first Las Vegas Bowl victory to cap off Sumrall's first season in Gainesville.
