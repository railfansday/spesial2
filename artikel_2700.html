Internet Invitational biggest studs & duds: Who gets invited back?

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The 2026 Internet Invitational featured several notable players that deserve more love, but some others don't deserve an invite next year. 64 major YouTube golf stars, celebrities and notable athletes competed in the second edition of the massive internet challenge.

The $4 million competition didn't entirely match the drama that Barstool Sports' Dave Portnoy was able to capture during Year 1. This year's three winners, Fat Perez, Riggs and Bobby Ray, were all deserving of the massive prize.

However, the 2025 series simply included more drama that added an extra layer to the incredible on-course storylines. During this year's six-episode release, there's only been one major breakout star beyond the champions; Big John.

His incredible putting and "all heart" mantra has earned him a permanent place in the competition. That's especially the case considering his history with Portnoy, which includes backing out of several other Barstool shows at the last minute.
