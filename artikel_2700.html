Breece Hall, Bhayshul Tuten & more fantasy football RB injury updates for Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The NFL injury report is already creating plenty of questions for fantasy football managers heading into Week 9. Several notable running backs are dealing with injuries, while others are facing significant changes to their availability.

Aug 14, 2026; East Rutherford, New Jersey, USA; New York Jets running back Breece Hall (20) carries the ball against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers during the first half at MetLife Stadium. Mandatory Credit: John Jones-Imagn Images Credit: IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect (Reuters)

There is encouraging news for fantasy managers waiting for Hall to return to the Jets' backfield. According to NFL Network's Ian Rapoport, Hall is now good to go and "should play" in Week 9 against the Kansas City Chiefs. Hall has missed the last five games with a quad injury, so his return would be a significant development for New York's offense. However, fantasy managers will want to monitor his workload after an extended absence.

Love is facing a much longer absence after being placed on injured reserve. The Cardinals running back suffered a right high-ankle sprain during Sunday's comeback win over the Dallas Cowboys. He's expected to miss four to five weeks, leaving Arizona without a key offensive contributor for the foreseeable future.

Etienne's status remains uncertain after he underwent an evaluation Monday. Head coach Kellen Moore is expected to determine whether the running back can return to practice this week. Etienne is eligible to return from injured reserve, and a positive evaluation could open his 21-day practice window. Fantasy managers should watch his participation throughout the week.

Oct 11, 2026; Tottenham, ENG; Jacksonville Jaguars running back Bhayshul Tuten (33) reacts during the second half against the Philadelphia Eagles at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium. Mandatory Credit: Nathan Ray Seebeck-Imagn Images

Tuten is dealing with ankle and foot injuries after an impressive Week 8 performance. The running back did not practice Monday, leaving his availability for Thursday night's matchup against the Baltimore Ravens uncertain. Tuten recorded 159 total yards and two touchdowns against Indianapolis, making his injury situation particularly important for fantasy managers.

Skattebo was seen working off to the side with trainers during Monday's practice as he continues dealing with a hamstring injury. The Giants are coming off their bye week, so his official practice status will provide a better indication of his availability for Sunday's matchup against the Philadelphia Eagles. His participation later this week will be worth monitoring.

Brooks could be moving closer to a return, with the running back potentially resuming practice this week. His availability remains something to monitor as the Panthers determine the next step in his recovery. A return to practice would be an encouraging development, although it would not necessarily mean he is ready to contribute in Week 9.
