Bills open as enormous favorites over Dolphins for Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Buffalo Bills are now 8-1 and remain in first place in the AFC playoff race. The change at head coach hasn't slowed down Buffalo one bit, thanks in large part to the consistency on offense and the fact that quarterback Josh Allen and running back James Cook III are among the best in the NFL.

The Bills have a firm hold on the AFC East, and are in prime position to re-claim the division title this season. This, despite a .500 record while having only played two games against division rivals.

Week 11 is upon us, and with that comes Buffalo's first chance to take on the Miami Dolphins in 2026. It's been another mostly forgettable season for the Dolphins, who will head to Highmark Stadium with a 1-8 record.

As might be expected, the Bills enter Week 11 as heavy favorites over the Dolphins. As of publishing, FanDuel Sportsbook listed Buffalo as 16.5-point favorites (-16.5) over Miami, with an over/under set at o/u 45.5. It's the biggest points spread in NFL action this week.

Buffalo and Miami are two teams headed in very different directions. Though not mathematically eliminated yet, the Dolphins' chances of making the playoffs are on life support. The team has looked listless much of the season, and now that quarterback Malik Willis is injured, things have been turned over to rookie quarterback Kyle McCord.

The Bills-Dolphins rivalry is a heated one that goes back generations, beginning back on September 18, 1966. Miami actually holds the advantage in the all-time series record, 63-61-1, but they're at real risk of allowing Buffalo to even things up this season.

Though one-sided in favor of the Dolphins through the entirety of the 1970s, this series has belonged almost entirely to the Bills since Josh Allen came to Orchard Park, NY. Since 2018, Allen and the Bills are 14-3 (including playoffs).

If you were to poll a room of Bills fans, it's likely that most of them well remember those three losses, which brings us to the last time Buffalo and Miami played. That game was also the most recent loss for the Bills against the Dolphins. During head coach Sean McDermott's final season with Buffalo, the Bills dropped an uncharacteristic-by-their-standards 30-13 decision in Miami. That ended a seven-game win streak dating back to 2022.

Buffalo has its sights set on a major goals in 2026, which includes earning the one seed and a bye to begin the AFC playoff race. Expect Allen and the Bills to be locked-in and ready to defend their status as enormous favorites this weekend.
