Kübler clinches Freiburg's Conference League victory at Trabzonspor

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Freiburg picked up a second win in their Conference League campaign thanks to Lukas Kübler reacting quickest in a 1-0 win at Trabzonspor on Thursday.

The Germans, among the favourites for glory having been runners-up in the Euopa League last term, went ahead on 14 minutes when André Onana could only parry Igor Matanović's header from a corner and full back Kübler pounced.

Kübler started as Philipp Treu, included in the Germany squad again on Thursday, was only fit enough for the bench after a knock.

Mo Salah had a couple of chances but the Turks failed to avoid their first defeat in the league phase. Trabzonspor lie eighth, just inside the automatic last-16 berths with three games to go.

Freiburg are 11th in a seeded play-off place.
