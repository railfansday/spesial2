Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The veteran poured in 29 points against his old team, but they got the last laugh as the Nets suffered a 142-116 loss. But Randle got a standing ovation from the Target Center crowd, and another Minnesota memory. "I had so much fun here. My family had a lot of fun here, made a lot of great memories, played some good basketball, a lot of good people here. I was at a point in my career where it was needed. I needed to reset and it was great. It was open arms and I'm extremely happy that I got the opportunity to do that," said Randle, who insisted there was no beef or bitterness with the Timberwolves. "It's fine. You understand the business I'm in long enough, so you understand it, you get it. "The organization was great to me. So it really wasn't any hard feelings. You move on and I'm here now trying to make the best of the environment here. So I just gotta keep competing here, just enjoying the moment, keep playing hard. … And I was going to a place I was familiar with."
