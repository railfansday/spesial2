Transfer news LIVE: Arsenal begin January talks; Man Utd target Toney; Scott to Chelsea twist; Liverpool latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

With the January transfer window approaching, Arsenal, Chelsea, Manchester United, Liverpool and Tottenham are among the clubs expected to pursue major signings. Arsenal have been linked with a surprise move for Jamal Musiala and could also seek a goalkeeper following David Raya’s injury. The Gunners face competition from Paris Saint-Germain for Eli Junior Kroupi and are reportedly interested in Tornike Kvaratskhelia, brother of Khvicha.

Manchester United are tipped to target Ivan Toney, while Mauricio Pochettino is reportedly keen on Fulham defender Calvin Bassey. Chelsea could pursue Alex Scott and Lamine Camara to strengthen midfield, with both the Blues and Arsenal linked with Cesc Fabregas as a potential managerial candidate.

Liverpool are reportedly interested in Real Madrid’s Endrick, while Tottenham are said to have agreed a new deal with teenager Luca Williams-Barnett. Follow the latest transfer news, rumours and gossip live below!

Mauricio Pochettino will have just £50m to spend in January as he looks to overhaul his Manchester United squad and dodge relegation.

The Argentine is said to be keen to add a striker to his squad, with Benjamin Sesko struggling for fitness and Joshua Zirkzee for form.

Per reports in the Daily Mail, he will hope to add two players to his squad in the winter window.

Arsenal have begun talks with RB Leipzig over signing winger Antonio Nusa in a deal which could be worth £67million.

The 21-year-old has impressed this season, registering six goals and eight assists in 15 appearances, prompting Arsenal to send scouts to watch him in action against Manchester City.

According to Fabrizio Romano, Leipzig would be reluctant to sell Nusa midway through the season following the departure of Yan Diomande last summer.

Any January deal would likely require an exceptional offer, although interest in the winger is growing ahead of 2027.

Arsenal are viewed as a strong potential destination given their need for a left-sided attacker, but Paris Saint-Germain are also monitoring his progress.

Chelsea may have cooled their interest in Alex Scott and Lamine Camara.

The Blues were expected to at least make a swoop for Bournemouth stars Scott, and have been linked with reigniting their pursuit of Monaco man Camara.

But The Athletic’s David Ornstein is not so sure.

“When you look back to the end of the summer window, Chelsea didn’t get let’s say a Kone, a Camara and Alex Scott but I’m not aware of them going for those players this January, maybe that’s something for the summer.

“When you look at the rise of Romeo Lavia now he appears to be staying fit, he’s one of the best midfielders in his position available.”

Paris Saint-Germain could step up their pursuit of Bournemouth forward Eli Junior Kroupi after Desire Doue suffered a serious knee injury, Teamtalk claims.

The injury comes just a month after Khvicha Kvaratskhelia suffered an ACL injury that is expected to keep him out for at least 6 months.

Kroupi is seen as the next starlet in France and PSG could act in January given their injuries.

Paris Saint-Germain are preparing to offer midfielder Vitinha a new contract until 2032, according to Nico Schira.

The Portuguese’s agent, Jorge Mendes, is thought to be braced for an approach from the European champions.

Sky Germany reports that Bayern Munich are weighing up a move for Schalke 04 goalkeeper Loris Karius.

However, the 33-year-old says he hasn’t spoken to anyone about a potential transfer.

Diario Sport claims that Barcelona now face competition from Chelsea, Manchester United and Manchester City for FC Twente defender Ruud Nijstad.

Barca have been leading the race for the 18-year-old, but Twente’s asking price of over €20million could complicate a January move.

Manchester United have hired former Arsenal coach Sam Wilson, who has already started work as part of Mauricio Pochettino’s backroom staff, writes Matt Verri.

Wilson left Arsenal in the summer, having been at the club for 12 years.

He was the lead physical performance coach under Mikel Arteta up until the end of last season, playing his part in Arsenal’s Premier League title-winning campaign.

Wilson left the Gunners as part of the club’s overhaul of their medical department, with significant changes made in an attempt to address the injury problems that have hindered the squad in recent seasons.

Chelsea could potentially sign both Alex Scott and Lamine Camara in January.

The Blues are on the hunt for midfield reinforcements after losing Enzo Fernandez to Manchester City on summer deadline day and seeing a move for Monaco’s Camara fall through at the 11th hour.

Bournemouth’s Scott has become their No1 target amid a fierce transfer battle with Liverpool, while both clubs are also said to retain an interest in Camara.

TEAMtalk report that Chelsea have not ruled signing both players in January.

Manchester United are said to be pondering a January move for Ivan Toney.

The England striker is believed to be keen on returning to the Premier League in 2027 after a year-and-a-half in Saudi Arabia with Al-Ahli.

Several clubs are said to be interested in Toney, with Football Insider stating that Manchester United are being kept closely informed of the situation.

TEAMtalk claim that Arsenal, Chelsea, Tottenham and Crystal Palace have also been made aware of the availability of the former Brentford frontman, who could be signed on an initial loan deal that includes an option to buy.
