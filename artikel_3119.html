Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Observatory visits slumped while leasing and debt metrics held steady.

Empire State Realty Trust, Inc. (ESRT +1.20%) reported a third quarter focused on capital recycling, operational efficiency, and executive transition. Management highlighted the completion of the $217.5 million sale of 1359 Broadway, utilizing proceeds to fund share repurchases and strengthen the balance sheet. While the commercial office and retail portfolio maintained high occupancy and positive rent spreads, the Observation Deck segment faced significant headwinds from declining visitation. To address this, the company appointed new leadership to modernize customer acquisition through digital and AI-driven platforms. Financial results were supported by disciplined expense management, with the company remaining on track to meet its multiyear general and administrative cost reduction targets.
