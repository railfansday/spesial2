FedEx plane overshoots Miami runway, causing brief suspension of flights at airport

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

FedEx aircraft FedEx plane sends dirt flying while overshooting Miami runway dirt in the air while overshooting Miami runway

The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) temporarily halted flights bound for Miami International Airport in Florida after a FedEx aircraft overran a runway on Wednesday evening.

Departures heading to Miami were grounded just after 20:15 local time (01:15 GMT) and all but one runway at the airport was closed, according to the FAA advisory. Flights resumed again about 90 minutes later.

The airliner that ran past the runway arrived from Memphis. No injuries have been reported. The FAA is investigating the incident.

The ground stop came on the day before Thanksgiving, one of the busiest travel days of the year in the US.

Flight delays in Miami reached one hour but appeared to be declining later Wednesday night.

At 21:41 local time, the FAA confirmed that "flights will be resuming shortly".

Passengers scheduled to travel on Wednesday night were encouraged to check directly with their airlines for the latest flight updates before heading to the airport.

"We appreciate our passengers' patience as we work to safely resume normal operations," Miami-Dade Aviation Department spokesman Greg Chin said in a statement to the BBC.

The airport is among the top five domestic destinations for travellers in the country over this holiday week, according to the American Automobile Association.

The Miami-Dade area is also under a flood watch until late Wednesday night. Periods of heavy rain throughout the day are expected to cause flooding, according to the US National Weather Service.

A nationwide ground stop earlier this month halted flights across the US for hours - the longest such event since the terror attacks on 11 September 2001.
