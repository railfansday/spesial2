North Korea expels Swiss diplomat in sanctions row

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Switzerland is one of the few Western nations to maintain dialogue with Pyongyang

North Korea has ordered Switzerland's diplomatic representative to leave the country within 48 hours and called for the office to close amid a row over sanctions.

It said the move was in response to Switzerland's decision to place sanctions on the Songdowon international children's camp. State media described this as "a blatant hostile act".

That camp is at the centre of claims that it was involved in transferring children from Russian-occupied areas of Ukraine.

North Korea's foreign ministry said "any act of violating the sovereign rights" of the country and the interests of its citizens "can never be welcomed".

Switzerland is one of the few Western nations to maintain dialogue with Pyongyang.

Swiss authorities said relations between the two countries would be maintained through the North Korean embassy in Bern, and the Swiss embassy in Beijing.

The diplomatic rebuke comes weeks after Swiss foreign minister Ignazio Cassis made a rare visit to North Korea, where he officially reopened the office and discussed normalising relations.

Diplomatic links with North Korea were first established in 1974 and Switzerland opened a co-operation office in Pyongyang in 1997.

A Swiss delegation, on left, including Foreign Minister Ignazio Cassis, met with North Korean officials in Pyongyang last month

Switzerland's federal department of foreign affairs said the country "distinguishes between its sanctions policy and its commitment to maintaining dialogue with all countries".

It added: "The reopening of the office in Pyongyang did not in any way affect Switzerland's implementation of UN sanctions related to North Korea's nuclear programme, nor its implementation of EU sanctions targeting North Korea in the context of Russia's war against Ukraine."

Thousands of North Korean soldiers have been deployed to help support Russia's war with Ukraine.

In August, Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelensky accused Moscow of using North Korean missiles.
