Best cloud phones for AI agents (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Posted on Nov 14 • Originally published at mobilebox.dev

Disclosure: we build Mobilebox, one of the services in this post. Written with AI assistance from our own published data; every number carries its date and source, and corrections go to hello@mobilebox.dev.

The best cloud phone for an AI agent depends on the job. Limrun gives an agent a working Android screen fastest, about 20 times faster to a first screenshot than Mobilebox in our test (0.98 s against 19.4 s), and adds iOS simulators and remote Xcode. Phone Harness and Mobilerun rent real iPhones; Mobilerun also gives agents phone numbers and SMS. Clousd lists the lowest price per minute. Mobilebox fits when every task or pull request gets its own phone that is started, checked, screenshotted and stopped for a few cents.

A cloud phone for an agent is a phone in a data center that your agent drives over an API or MCP; see what a cloud phone for AI agents is. We make Mobilebox, so it is one row below, judged like the rest. Facts come from each vendor's own pages, reviewed on November 10, 2026; speed numbers are ours, from the November 8 benchmark.

Three of these let us measure on a free tier, with the same steps and the same APK, 10 sessions each, from one client in Nuremberg, Germany.

Medians; the benchmark has p90s, the CSV and the scripts. Limrun was fastest on every speed metric, very likely because its phones are booted before you ask. Mobilebox was the cheapest for 10 minutes and the only one where we measured park and resume: 11.5 seconds back to a screenshot, the installed app still there in 10 of 10 runs. For a one-minute task a 20-second start is a third of the wall clock; for a 30-minute session it hardly matters.

We didn't measure Mobilerun (sign-up is Google, GitHub or Discord only), phonebox.dev (the free week needs a card), Clousd or Ramus. Their rows are as published.

Device farms rent real phones to run test suites across many models. They are built for QA teams more than for an agent that acts step by step.

BrowserStack's terms forbid publishing performance results, so we didn't time it. If your app checks for real hardware, use a physical phone; see real devices or cloud phones.

A local emulator with mobile-mcp costs nothing on a laptop with hardware acceleration and a screen. In CI it is slower: on a GitHub-hosted runner the emulator took 50 seconds to a first screenshot with KVM and 13.5 minutes without. See cloud phone or local emulator.

A service is on the list if an agent can create and drive a phone in the cloud through it today, over MCP or an API, with public prices. Each row quotes the vendor's own page as of November 10, 2026; prices are list prices that day, as published. Speed numbers are only the ones we measured.
