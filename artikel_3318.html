Jorrybell Agoto Dies: ‘Filipiñana’ Breakout Actress Was 32

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Jorrybell Agoto, the star of Rafael Manuel’s award-winning debut feature Filipiñana, died November 17 following a battle with lymphoma, her family said. She was 32.

Epicmedia Productions, the Philippine production company behind Filipiñana, posted on its Instagram account: “We love you, Belle. No more pain.”

Manuel posted: “The tragedy of this loss cannot be put into words. The world has just lost one of its brightest stars. We have just lost a great friend. But our thoughts and prayers go out especially to Jorrybell’s family who have just lost a loving older sister and a devoted daughter.”

Filipiñana premiered at this year’s Sundance Film Festival, where it won the Special Jury Award for Creative Vision in the World Cinema Dramatic Competition. It is also the Philippines’ entry for the Best International Feature category of next year’s Oscars. Watch on Deadline

Agoto played the lead role of a quiet but determined resort worker at a high-end golf club in the film, which explores themes of gentrification, class and globalization. She also starred in the original short film, Filipiñana (2020), that the feature was based on. Also directed by Manuel, the short won a Silver Bear Jury Prize at the Berlinale.

Born on September 25, 1994, in Ilocos, Philippines, Agoto started acting in community theater at a young age before graduating with a BA in Theater Arts from Polytechnic University of the Philippines. She also had roles in short films including Cross My Heart and Hope t Die and such features as Jun Robles Lana’s Sisa, which premiered at the 2025 Tallinn Black Nights Film Festival, and Lawrence Fajardo’s Silenced Nights, which premiered at this year’s Toronto Film Festival.

The Film Development Council of the Philippines also issued a statement following the news of her passing: “The Film Development Council of the Philippines extends its deepest condolences to the family and loved ones of Ms. Jorrybell Agoto — Lead Actress, Filipiñana (1994–2026). Your contribution to the Philippine film industry will remain etched in our hearts. Thank you so much, and farewell, Jorrybell.”

Kino Lorber, which is distributing Filipiñana in the U.S., also remembered the young actress and paid tribute to her talent: “While we never met Jorrybell, we felt like we had through the light she brought to Isabel in Filipiñana. Cinema is poorer for having lost her so young.”

Filipiñana is being released in the UK and Ireland this weekend by BFI Distribution. Kino Lorber released the film in the U.S. in August and is organizing a series of screenings as part of its Oscar campaign.
