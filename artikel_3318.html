Pearl Jam Plan ‘Big-Boy Conversation’ About New Drummer

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

It’s been a little under two months since Pearl Jam performed at Ohana with Abe Laboriel Jr. behind the drum kit, but it turns out the band still isn’t sure if he’s actually their new drummer. “At the first of the year, we’re gonna have a big-boy conversation about what we’re doing next year, ’cause we all wanna play some shows next year,” Pearl Jam bassist Jeff Ament recently told Lipps Service. “So, hopefully by then – I mean, hopefully it’s Abe. Abe has to sort of figure out what his schedule is.”

Ament didn’t spell out the exact nature of Laboriel’s scheduling difficulties, but it’s likely related to his role in Paul McCartney’s band. Laboriel has been with Macca since 2002, and is a pivotal part of the ensemble since he also provides background and harmony vocals on songs like “Eleanor Rigby” and “Hey Jude.” There have been no changes in McCartney’s band since that 2002 tour. It’s unclear if Laboriel is under contract to him, simply doesn’t want to part ways with a Beatle, or is trying to figure out if it’s possible to do both gigs.

Ament hopes he finds a way to make it happen. “It was so fun to play with him, and it was so completely different for me,” he said. “I’m really trying to understand his tendencies and sort of how he enters and exits fills is, like, different than any drummer I’ve ever played with. There’s a push and pull. There’s so much power. Entering a fill, he’s back, and then the fill pushes forward, and then at the end of the fill, it gets really back again. Man, once I figured that out, I was, like, ‘Oh, this is so fun, man. There’s so much power in this.’”

Despite months of fan speculation, Ament said they didn’t even audition any other drummers. Laboriel has been in Edder Vedder’s solo band the Earthlings for several years, and they were all familiar with his work. “There’s a real [John] Bonham quality to the way that he plays,” Ament said. “So, there’s a part that I think about, like, man, if it did work out with Abe and we could make a record with him, it could be fucking amazing.” Editor’s picks The 250 Greatest Albums of the 21st Century So Far The 100 Best TV Episodes of All Time The 500 Greatest Albums of All Time 100 Best Movies of the 21st Century

Pearl Jam have been without a drummer since Matt Cameron amicably parted ways with them last year following a 27-year run. “Much love and respect to Jeff, Ed, Mike, and Stone for inviting me into the band in 1998 and for giving me the opportunity of a lifetime, one filled with friendships, artistry, challenges, and laughter,” he said at the time. “I am forever grateful to the crew, staff, and fans the world over. It’s been an incredible journey. More to follow. I thank you all from the bottom of my heart.”

The newest member of Pearl Jam, whether it’s Laboriel or someone else, will be the band’s sixth drummer since forming in 1990. It was Matt Cameron for the vast majority of that time since they had a Spinal Tap-like situation in the early days of the group where drummers came and went very quickly.
