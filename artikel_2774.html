Johnny Walker evicts Waldo Cortes Acosta from Heavyweight Top 10 in latest UFC rankings update

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Johnny Walker predicted 2027 will be "the year of the heavyweights."

The Brazilian is certainly doing his part, racking up two straight knockout wins along with a pair of $100,000 post-fight performance bonuses. That includes his destruction of Waldo Cortes Acosta atop the UFC Vegas 124 fight card last weekend in Las Vegas, which earned Walker (24-10, 1 NC) a spot in the heavyweight Top 10.

Elsewhere on the charts, Tatiana Suarez (13-1) ascended to the top spot in the strawweight division by turning away Brazilian bruiser Virna Jandiroba. In addition, Karine Siva rose two places in the women's flyweight division by defeating Gabriella Fernandez on the UFC Vegas 124 preliminary card.
