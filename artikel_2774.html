Olympic marathon champion Peres Jepchirchir hit with a 3-year ban after EPO test

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

MONACO (AP) — World and former Olympic marathon champion Peres Jepchirchir has been suspended for three years over a positive drug test for the banned substance EPO, the Athletics Integrity Unit said Thursday.

Jepchirchir won women's marathon gold at the Tokyo Olympics in 2021 and the world title last year. The 33-year-old Kenyan runner is also a former world record holder in the half marathon.

The AIU, which investigates doping in track and field on behalf of the sport's governing body World Athletics, issued a statement saying Jepchirchir admitted to breaching anti-doping rules.

She returned an adverse analytical finding for EPO from an out-of-competition sample collected in Kenya prior to her Sydney Marathon victory in late August.

The Sydney Marathon issued a statement Thursday saying Irine Cheptai was set to be elevated to its 2026 women's champion.
