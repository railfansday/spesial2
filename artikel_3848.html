BLEACH Mirrors High character tier list

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Fulfilling every fan’s dream, BLEACH Mirrors High lets you join forces with some of Tite Kubo’s most iconic characters in an original storyline taking place after the series’ Thousand-Year Blood War.

But the game is also a gacha, where understanding which characters excel can be key, especially when pondering a reroll or investing in the right backups. Here’s our latest BLEACH Mirrors High tier list.

We divided our list into four tiers, from S to C, and selected the three-star versions of all characters as our baseline for evaluation while taking all of their Limit Break passives into account. Due to their customizable nature, the protagonist was not included.

True to what any fan can imagine, Ichigo and Aizen are the staple S-Tiers of Mirrors High and the game’s best Combo picks. Rukia and Rangiku then shine as its best Chain supports, with the latter being by far the best partner for Hitsugaya.

Although unable to perform at the same level as the above, those in the A-Tier can be just as destructive when under the right conditions or compositions. True to that, many of them are excellent wild card picks or secondary DPSs.

The B-Tier houses characters who are either unable to compete with the above or lack overall deck variety/kit versatility. This does not mean they cannot excel, just that their utility and ceiling will be limited by the opposition.

There’s no easy way to say this: the C-Tier houses those either unable to match their competition if not equipped with the right Special Effects or who are simply outclassed within their role.
