710 Pounds Of Linemen: Frontier Airlines Signs 2 College Football Stars To Launch First Class

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Frontier Airlines has signed a name, image, and likeness partnership with Juan Gaston and Jahzare Jackson, who are University of Georgia offensive linemen. These college football giants are promoting the airline's new 2-2 configuration first class product.

The full first class product will be available in the first two rows of Frontier, offering legroom up to 37 inches (94 cm). Extra legroom seating will also be an option in rows three to eight, with 34 inches (86 cm) of pitch. The seats are also wider and more comfortable, resembling premium economy on a long-haul airline.

The reason for choosing the college football stars to promote the first class product is obvious. It shows that even the United States' biggest athletes can be comfortable while flying with the low-cost airline. The pair weigh in at a combined 710 pounds (322 kg) and have 13 feet and five inches (409 cm) of height between them.

Frontier launched a promotional video named 'It Comes Her,' as embedded above. It shows the pair sitting on their contracts. Jackson said “more room, no middle seat, and a contract you sign sitting down? I’m in." Gaston gave his endorsement for the seat, telling Frontier:

“When Frontier called, I didn’t expect my seat to be part of the deal. But at 6-foot-7 and 360 pounds, I take having enough room pretty seriously. This is a partnership I can get comfortable with.”

Gaston and Jackson's promotion is for Frontier's new two rows of first class that will be at the front of the aircraft. The seats have more legroom and width, and have a more comfortable construction. The 2-2 layout means there's no middle seat. Passengers can access this product with the first class bundle, starting at $129 per passenger one-way.

It also includes two checked bags, a carry-on bag, and priority boarding, and is available for flights from March 5, 2027. Alongside the first class product, the airline is also introducing extra legroom in rows three to eight. The UpFront Plus product guarantees that the middle seat is empty.

This product is also available from March 5, 2027, and can be purchased with the Premium bundle from $90 one-way.

You'll get a carry-on bag and priority boarding. Another cabin upgrade planned is at-seat power for the first eight rows, with installation beginning in 2027, with progression scheduled throughout the year. Starlink Wi-Fi installation will also be conducted throughout 2027.

The new class will include eight seats with improved legroom, more comfortable seats and priority boarding.

Since 2021, US college athletes have been allowed to earn money through NIL deals. Athletes can use their personal brand for endorsements, social media posts, appearances, and merchandise without impacting their ability to play. Some of these deals have been in the aviation sector, albeit not for such a significant brand as Frontier.

An example is Marcel Reed's partnership with aircraft charter firm ENG Aviation Group, signed in 2025. Reed is a quarterback for Texas A&M University. As reported by ENG on LinkedIn, the partnership promoted the company's organ recovery flights and raised awareness for organ donation.

In 2024, Simple Flying reported on Noah Fifita and Tetairoa McMillan's NIL deal with business jet company Alerion Aviation. Both athletes played American Football for the University of Arizona, with Fifita playing as quarterback and McMillan as a wide receiver. The exact deal was not disclosed.
