Why SpaceX Stock Is Up Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

SpaceX stock is bouncing back after a two-day slide pushed it below its $135 IPO price on fears that Democratic wins in Texas could hit its data center plans.

Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SPCX +7.54%) stock is recovering, up 7.3% as of 1:56 p.m. ET on Friday, Nov. 6, after a two-day slide that took it below its IPO price of $135.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC +0.72%) and the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC +1.49%) are up 0.8% and 1.4%, respectively.

Shares of the rocket, satellite, and AI company fell about 13% through two trading sessions following Tuesday's sweeping midterm victory for the Democratic Party.

The wins, which included a U.S. Senate seat and the governorship in Texas where SpaceX is based, raised the odds that Elon Musk's companies would face increased scrutiny. The SpaceX CEO donated a whopping $474 million to Republican efforts across the U.S., including $224 million in Texas.

And critically, SpaceX's data center building is key to its growth plans. The now governor-elect of Texas ran, in part, on the promise of pausing new data center construction.

Today's bounce appeared to be from investors who believe the previous two days were an overreaction.

Still, none of what spooked investors this week has gone away, though to be clear, it is all speculation at this point. I think it's a legitimate fear and one more substantial risk to add for SpaceX stock.
