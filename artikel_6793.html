You Need a Funnel-Neck Coat This Winter

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

They’re cozy, polished, and way less fussy than a scarf.

If there’s a style that’s having its moment, it’s the funnel neck. Between sweater tops and coats, I’ve been seeing this trend all over my feed and I am quite obsessed. Especially as the winter months creep closer, to me, a funnel neck is a perfect style for the lazy-fashion girls. It’s not as restrictive as a turtleneck, but allows you the freedom that a scarf can’t while still keeping you warm.

Funnel necks are a balance between two necessary winter staples, while adding a little edge to your late fall and winter outfits. So, if you’ve also been seeing the funnel neck everywhere, I’ve gathered a few styling options in coat form below, so you can map out your own look next.

Contrary to the masses, I love a brown leather coat more than black. There’s something so chic about it, and with the added funnel neck it makes the whole look more interesting and even a bit mysterious.

A really good, thick coat is a necessity during the colder months. And as someone who always struggles to find a scarf that fits the vibe perfectly, rocking a coat like this does the job perfectly.

As I said, brown leather is my personal fave, but most people love a good black leather coat. It’s the easiest and sharpest monochromatic outfit you’ll be able to pull together. Pairing a good funnel-neck coat with a sleek pair of black trousers hits every time.

For the on-the-go Gen Z girlies, this feels as quintessential 20-something as it can get. not only are you on trend with the funnel-neck coat, but having it be belted with a pair of baggy jeans just reads as Cool Girl to me.

There’s nothing I love more than a monochromatic outfit, so if you’re feeling colorful this winter (to negate how gloomy and cold it is) leaning into a matching look like this is a trendy way into the funnel-neck style.
