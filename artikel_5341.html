Attorney general calls Nvidia a 'good corporate citizen' after Jensen Huang's return

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Todd Blanche declined to say whether the Justice Department is examining Huang, a day after Nvidia reinstated him as CEO

Attorney General Todd Blanche called Nvidia $NVDA "a good corporate citizen" on Tuesday but declined to say whether the Justice Department is examining the company or its newly reinstated CEO Jensen Huang.

Blanche spoke at a news conference in Washington a day after Nvidia's board brought Huang back as CEO. Reporters pressed him on whether authorities are scrutinizing Nvidia or Huang. "Obviously, if Nvidia was the target or was subject to some criminal investigation or they were obstructing or thwarting our investigation, then that wouldn't be the message that the president is saying to the American people," Blanche said.

President Donald Trump said on Truth Social on Monday that Blanche "confirmed" the government "does not possess evidence that he has engaged in any unlawful conduct." Trump told CNBC that an investigation showed Huang had "bad judgment having to do with somewhat unrelated incidents, but he did nothing wrong."

Blanche would not expand on Trump's remarks or address whether Jensen Huang's affair with Alice Huang raises security concerns for the country, according to Bloomberg. Asked what the Justice Department's espionage probe of Jensen Huang found, Blanche said Trump "talked to a lot of people" before urging the board to reinstate Jensen Huang. "I'm not going to get into communications I have with the president," Blanche said. FBI Director Kash Patel did not say if his agency has had contact with Jensen Huang.

The Justice Department and Commerce Department said on Nov. 17 that they were investigating Megaspeed, a Singapore-based Nvidia customer, over suspicions that Nvidia chips were illegally redirected, a potential breach of American export rules. Alice Huang, who is not related to Jensen Huang, was an executive at Megaspeed.

Questioned about ruling out any criminal exposure for Jensen Huang while that probe continues, Blanche replied, "When you say the export investigation is still ongoing, I don't know what you're talking about." He said the department has "hundreds, if not more, export investigations going at any time."

The Justice Department's espionage probe includes Alice Huang's relationship with Jensen Huang, who is not a target of U.S. law enforcement probes, according to Bloomberg. Blanche said on Nov. 12 that the department had reached no conclusions on who Alice Huang "is or is not." Beijing has denied trying to spy on Jensen Huang.

Nvidia's board reinstated Huang on Monday, less than two weeks after he stepped down on Nov. 12 following revelations of multiple affairs. U.S. officials suspect Alice Huang of relaying information to China's intelligence services. Nvidia said the board consulted high-ranking U.S. government officials and concluded that bringing him back does not endanger national security.

Nvidia stock rose 31% on Monday before falling on Tuesday.
