The Best Black Friday iPad Deals On the Latest Models

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

There's no denying that 2026 has been a slower year for the iPad lineup. Only the iPad Air and iPad Mini received updates (with nothing new for the iPad or iPad Pro since 2025), and in June, the entire lineup was subject to a painful price hike of either $100 or $200, depending on the model. But that's not to say you need to abandon your dreams of getting a good deal on a great iPad this year.

With Black Friday here, there are some solid sales that make now a smart time to shop for an iPad. For more shopping advice from our team of product testers and deal hunters, check out our Best Black Friday Deals, Best Amazon Black Friday Deals, Best Black Friday MacBook Deals, and Best Black Friday Laptop Deals.

I was delighted to see that Apple's brand new iPad Mini is already on sale—especially by this much. There's a lot I loved about the latest iPad Mini, which comes with the A20 Pro chip and an OLED screen, but the price was a tough pill to swallow. But for $100 off, it feels completely worth the money. It's better than the previous model in every way, including a thinner tablet, a slightly larger screen, and the front-facing camera has been moved to landscape for better video calls.

If you're trying to save the most cash, the base iPad is the absolute cheapest. You might appreciate the larger screen over the iPad Mini, too. The standard $50 discount has been upped to $70 off for Black Friday, which makes it great time to buy the base iPad. It's particularly attractive if you need something for an upcoming flight or to keep kids busy on a long holiday road trip.

The iPad Pro is a bit of a niche tablet, due to the price alone. But if you really want an iPad that can properly replace a high-end laptop, the 13-inch model is big enough (and fast enough) for the job. Strangely enough, the configuration that comes with a Silver chassis and 512 GB of storage is $50 cheaper than the 256-GB model in Space Black. That's how good of a deal this is!

The iPad Air is the in-between iPad. It's not as cheap as the base iPad, nor does it have the Tandem OLED screen and high-end features of the iPad Pro. And yet, I find that it's the iPad people should buy if they intend to use it for work. That's primarily because it has both a powerful M4 chip and can be connected to a Magic Keyboard with full trackpad support. The most recent versions of iPadOS make windowing much easier, and Apple even made the cursor work more like macOS. After all, most people don't need an OLED screen to do some word processing, answer emails, and take the occasional Zoom call.

When you're ready to use it more like a traditional tablet, just pull it off its magnetic cover, and it's ready for your fingers or an Apple Pencil. The 11-inch model is still small enough to not feel overwhelming in your hands. And while the $649 sale isn't particularly rare, it's not a bad price for a tablet that functions pretty well as a laptop replacement.

iPad Pro 11-inch for $1,099: It's a little surprising that the iPad Pro hasn't been discounted at all for Black Friday. At $1,099, it's cheaper than the 13-inch MacBook Air, which uses the same M5 chip. The difference? You still have to buy the Magic Keyboard separately to pair with the iPad Pro. All in all, the iPad ends up being a much more expensive device.

iPad Mini (2024) for $499: Like the newer iPad Mini, the 2024 model is also on sale for $100 off. The upgrades in the 2026 model are worth the money, but if you just need something simple to take notes or read books on, this will do the trick.
