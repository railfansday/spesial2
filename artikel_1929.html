Chelsea star Cole Palmer 'out in the cold' after confirmed news

Cole Palmer is facing an uphill battle to regain his place in the England squad any time soon, sources have told Football Insider.

The 24-year-old has now missed the last two England camps, after he controversially dropped out of the September squad due to apparent injury concerns.

While there are two sides to the story, and Morgan Rogers has since insisted his Chelsea teammate is fully committed to the Three Lions, the manager has serious doubts.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Tuchel has been left unhappy with Palmer who now has multiple options ahead of him.

After he pulled out of a previous camp, the boss insisted the Chelsea star has missed too many opportunities to impress for his country, and he may now have lost his place for good.

England captain Harry Kane is also believed to have grown frustrated by the players who opt against representing the Three Lions, as tension grows behind the scenes.

Former Man United coach Mick Brown warned Football Insider back in September that Palmer may already have played his last game for England after angering Tuchel.

The 24-year-old was included in the original squad for the September international break, but pulled out on medical grounds and instead remained at Chelsea.

This is not the first time Palmer has dropped out of doing international duty, leaving the manager frustrated, and he will now find it difficult to return to his plans.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "It's going to be difficult for Cole Palmer to break back into Thomas Tuchel's plans.

"Tuchel wasn't happy when he pulled out of the squad before these recent internationals, he missed out on the squad for these games against Croatia and Spain as well.

"The problem for Palmer is England are blessed with so much attacking talent right now as well it's gonna be hard for him even if he performs well for Chelsea to break back in.

"You've got the likes of Morgan Rogers, Anthony Gordon, Rio Ngumoha all performing well, and the front six for England are among the best in the world.

"There’s not a great relationship between Palmer and Tuchel has been made clear by some of the comments that Tuchel has made.

"It's going be very hard for to try and win a place back in that England squad as well. Tuchel is happy with the squad he's got and he feels the players who are committed to the cause in that respect.

"While England continue to impress and get results, it's going be hard for Cole Palmer to break back into the fold."

Part of the reason why Palmer was left out of the most recent England squad was the abundance of attacking options available for Tuchel to call upon at the moment.

England's star-studded attacking options include Kane, Rogers, Anthony Gordon, Jude Bellingham, Eberechi Eze and Morgan Gibbs-White.

Meanwhile, Brown told Football Insider on 13 November that Liverpool sensation Rio Ngumoha could soon establish himself as a regular part of Tuchel's England plans.

The 18-year-old made his fourth senior appearance for the Three Lions during their 2-1 win against Croatia on Thursday night, where he came off the bench late in the game.

However, despite his quick feet, speed and ability to beat a man, the winger must still add goals and assists to his game as he is being primed to make a difference in the attacking third.
