Heidi Klum Teases Her Latest Halloween Costume

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Halloween is just around the corner—and what does that mean? It means that Heidi Klum, the undisputed queen of the scariest night of the year, will soon reveal her incredible new costume. After dressing up as an anthropomorphic worm, an incredibly realistic ET, and a chimpanzee complete with prosthetic facial features and ears, the supermodel is ready to amaze us once again.

Back in September, she revealed to People that she was already hard at work planning her next big costume. While she didn’t reveal any details—which is perfectly understandable—she did hint that it would be a “very, very bloody” look and that it “goes very well with red wine.” She’s given a few clues; the latest one she shared on her Instagram profile.

“What’s my costume?” the supermodel asked her followers, attaching a spooky close-up of some prosthetics ready to transform her into who knows what. The image appears to include a shiny brain, a blue tube resembling a sea creature, fleshy tissue, and a yellow sac that could be a honeycomb, coral, earwax—in short, anything.

Of course, many have tried to come up with an answer. Some suggested it might be Ursula from The Little Mermaid; others noted similarities to the vanity monster portrayed by Demi Moore and Margaret Qualley in The Substance, while another user pointed out that the image resembles the appearance of the zombies in The Last of Us.

In an interview with NBC’s Hollywood ReelTime, special effects makeup artist Mike Marino—who has created some of the model’s most iconic Halloween looks—revealed that this year’s costume is the most complicated one he’s ever worked on.

It seems almost impossible for Klum to follow last year’s incredible costume; on the 25th anniversary of her famous Halloween party, she appeared as the mythological Medusa. Her body was entirely covered in green scales; she had a long tail and moving snakes instead of hair, while her husband, Tom Kaulitz, dressed as a Greek soldier turned to stone.

It’s quite the challenge to top that legendary getup. Yet we’re certain the former Victoria’s Secret Angel won’t disappoint. So mark your calendar for this weekend to find out whom Heidi Klum will be impersonating.
