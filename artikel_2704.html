Washington State blown out by Texas State, 42-14

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

In their final road game of the season, the Washington State Cougars were dominated by the Texas State Bobcats, 42-14.

WSU (2-9, 1-6 in Pac-12) got in front early with the games' first points on a Daniel Blood touchdown reception, then proceeded to allow 42 unanswered to TSU (7-4, 5-2).

The Cougars got out to a promising start, forcing a three-and-out and converting the first offensive drive into points. They did it in chunks too, starting with a 12-yard run and 10-yard reception from Kirby Vorhees. A roughing the passer on TSU to set WSU up on the TSU-23 yard line, which led to WSU's lone offensive touchdown on the day. Owen Eshelman found Blood on the short curl route, and Blood did the rest. Dodging and dragging Bobcats into the end zone for a 7-0 WSU lead.

It ended up being one of the very few WSU highlights of the night.

The Bobcats immediately answered, scoring on a eight-play, 75-yard drive to tie the game up. WSU's offense went backwards in their response, committing a holding penalty and taking a sack, forcing a quick three-and-out. On the ensuing punt, TSU's Jordan Martin was able to get around the edge and block the punt, giving the Bobcats the ball at WSU's five-yard line. The Cougar defense was able to hold strong on the first two plays but relented on the third on Davian Jackson's third rushing attempt to put the Bobcats up 14-7 early. Despite a promising return by Tank Hawkins Jr., the Cougar drive ended up stalling out. They had a 4th and 3 at the Texas State 37-yard line, but a false start forced their hand into punting from the Bobcat 42.

The Bobcats grinded their way down the field on their fourth drive, finishing it off with a 44-yard QB keeper by Brad Jackson to make the score 21-7.

WSU tried to get back into the game with a long drive that ended up stalling at the Texas State 13-yard line. Jack Stevens trotted out for a field goal attempt from 31 yards out, but the Bobcat special teams came up big again, getting a hand on the field-goal attempt to block it. The Cougar defense would come up big on the ensuing Bobcat drive with a stop on 4th and 5 from the WSU 33-yard line. With 1:12 to go, the Cougar offense more or less decided to run the clock out and get to halftime down 21-7.

The Cougars opened the half with another promising drive that yet again failed around the Bobcat 30-yard line. A 4th and 5 from the TSU 31-yard line ended in an incompletion from Eshelman to Jack Pedersen that officially ended the drive. Following a pair of punts, the Bobcats put together a nine-play, 79-yard drive to put the Bobcats up 28-7.

Another WSU punt gave TSU the ball right back, and another touchdown drive from the Bobcats to put them up 35-7 more or less ended things. WSU did end up getting one more touchdown on the defensive end with a scoop and score to make things look a little closer.

The Cougars may get another shot at Texas State next week depending on the results of the San Diego State vs. Boise State game, as the Pac-12 may flex the Bobcats to Pullman for the final regular season game.
