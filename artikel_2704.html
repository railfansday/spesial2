CeeDee Lamb brushes off Quinyon Mitchell challenge despite Cowboys star’s previous struggles

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

CeeDee Lamb has dismissed concerns about facing Philadelphia Eagles cornerback Quinyon Mitchell ahead of the Dallas Cowboys' Thanksgiving showdown, insisting he does not view any individual matchup as a challenge.

The Cowboys wide receiver has enjoyed another productive season and already has more receiving yards than he managed throughout the whole of the previous campaign.

However, Mitchell will provide a difficult test for Lamb, having established himself as one of the Eagles' most reliable defensive players.

Speaking to reporters ahead of the divisional meeting, Lamb made it clear that he is not concerned about who lines up against him.

"I don't really face any challenges, to be honest," Lamb said via All City DLLS' Clarence Hill Jr.

It was a confident response from the Cowboys star, particularly given how their previous meeting unfolded.

Mitchell got the better of Lamb when the teams last faced each other, limiting the receiver to just one catch for five yards when matched up against him.

According to NFL Next Gen Stats via the Philadelphia Inquirer's Devin Jackson, Lamb finished that game with three receptions for 24 yards on eight targets from Dak Prescott.

Mitchell has continued to impress during the current campaign. Through 10 games, the Eagles cornerback has allowed just 31 receptions for 309 yards and one touchdown while also recording his first regular-season interception.

He was limited in practice ahead of the Thanksgiving fixture but is expected to be available for the game.

Lamb, meanwhile, has recorded 1,108 receiving yards in 2026, already surpassing his 1,077-yard total from the previous season with six games still remaining.

That production has made him a central figure in the Cowboys' passing attack, but Mitchell will be looking to repeat his previous success against the Dallas receiver.

Lamb's confidence is clear. However, the Eagles cornerback has already shown he can make life difficult for one of the NFL's leading wide receivers.

Their latest meeting will offer Lamb an opportunity to prove his comments on the field while Mitchell looks to maintain his impressive record against the Cowboys star.
