Warriors' Stephen Curry to miss at least 3 more games, will have oblique tear re-evaluated again

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Stephen Curry will miss at least three more games for the Golden State Warriors with an oblique tear. He will have the injury re-evaluated next week, the team announced on Friday.

Curry, 38, has missed the Warriors' past seven games with the abdominal injury, At the time, the Warriors announced that their star guard would be sidelined for at least two weeks before being re-evaluated. So this latest update approximately follows that timeline.

The two-time NBA MVP has begun individual on-court workouts and is making good progress based on Friday's evaluation, according to the Warriors' release (via the San Francisco Chronicle's Luca Evans).

Curry sustained the injury during the first quarter of Nov. 11's 115-108 loss to the Houston Rockets. After hitting a 3-pointer, he began reaching for his side. Curry's frustration seemed to indicate that he knew he was injured, though no contact was made with him on the play. He did not come out of the game immediately, but left midway through the quarter.

Following the game, Warriors coach Steve Kerr told reporters that Curry had tweaked something in his abdominal area and "had never done it before."

In 10 games this season, Curry is averaging 27.8 points, 3.7 rebounds and 3.9 assists. He's shooting 46% on 94 3-point attempts. If he maintains that scoring average, it would be his highest in the past four seasons.

In his absence, the Warriors have gone 2-5, giving them a 10-9 record after Friday's NBA Cup losss to the Portland Trail Blazers (13-6). Golden State went 2-2 in the Western Conference's Group C play and was eliminated.

Following Friday's game, the Warriors host the Los Angeles Clippers (4-14) on Sunday and the Utah Jazz (3-17) on Tuesday. Curry will be re-evaluated the next day.
