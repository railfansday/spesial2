Memphis’ Penny Hardaway finds silver lining from his players binging GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Grand Theft Auto VI was released on Thursday. A touchstone moment that gamers have reveled in ever since the GTA franchise hit the market in 1997. The Memphis men's basketball team got their hands on it, much to head coach Penny Hardaway's delight.

The NBA legend takes some comfort in his players playing GTA 6, per Run It Back on FanDuel TV. Essentially, he doesn't fully know what it will do to them overall, but he points out a notable caveat.

"I am worried. … I know they'll be at home, they won't be out, so I can handle that part" Hardaway said.

Another thing is, maybe the players deserve a breather.

Memphis is coming off a blowout win over No.23 Louisville on Sunday, November 15. The Tigers are currently 3-1 and will face off against the Evansville at home on Saturday.

Hardaway has coached the Tigers since March 2018. Along the way, he accumulated many accolades, including the 2021 NIT Championship. He also led the Tigers to two AAC Tournament titles in 2023 and 2025. That year, they also won the AAC regular-season title, and Hardaway was named AAC Coach of the Year.

Moreover, Hardaway led the Tigers to three NCAA tournament appearances in 2022, 2023, and 2025.

Hardaway famously played in the NBA from 1993 to 2008. He is best known for playing alongside Shaquille O'Neal with the Orlando Magic in the 1990s.

It was then that Penny became a prominent pop culture figure alongside Shaq. He was in commercials, movies, television shows, and video games, including NBA Jam and NBA 2K.
