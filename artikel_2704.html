49ers monitoring Brock Purdy ankle injury before Seahawks game

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The San Francisco 49ers have been the class of the NFL in 2026, thanks in large part to the pristine play of QB Brock Purdy. Now the Niners will have to monitor their star's ankle ahead of the NFC West clash with the Seattle Seahawks on Sunday.

ESPN's Nick Wagoner reports Purdy will not participate in the team's practice on Wednesday, thanks to an ankle injury sustained in the second quarter of the 49ers' 35-10 win over the Minnesota Vikings on Sunday.

Purdy was again a dominant force on the field in Week 11, throwing for 255 yards on 21-for-26 passing with three touchdowns and nary an interception.

San Francisco improved to 9-1 on the season and is running away with the division, with annual stalwarts the Los Angeles Rams and Seattle Seahawks sitting at 6-4 heading into Week 12 play.

It will be a pivotal crush of games, with the Rams and Seahawks battling for postseason scraps as the playoff picture slowly comes into focus.

As ESPN reports, Purdy suffered an ankle injury Sunday. According to that report, the team will monitor the progress of the injury through the coming days, with coach Kyle Shanahan stating that it is not a high ankle sprain and that the status is day-to-day.

Asked by a reporter on Wednesday whether Purdy would be able to go on Sunday, Shanahan replied, "I mean he can't go today, so that's always a possibility."
