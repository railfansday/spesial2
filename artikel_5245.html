Zcash is consolidating after a sharp rally that pushed its price toward $1,700 in late September. The token is trading near its short-term moving averages, keeping the immediate outlook mixed.

The $1,200–$1,220 support zone is crucial for maintaining the current recovery structure. A sustained hold above this region could help ZEC retest resistance around $1,340–$1,400. A breakout above $1,400 would strengthen the bullish outlook and potentially open the way toward $1,500. However, a breakdown below $1,200 could expose the next support near $1,100–$1,120, followed by the broader demand region around $850–$900.

For November, ZEC’s outlook remains cautiously optimistic as long as buyers defend the $1,200 region. However, continued ETF outflows and fading post-upgrade momentum could limit its recovery.

Zcash’s 2026 outlook has shifted sharply after the asset suffered a major breakdown from its rising channel, erasing weeks of bullish momentum and triggering renewed downside pressure. Unlike earlier recovery attempts, the latest move reflects weakening buyer conviction, with ZEC losing a key support zone following panic-driven selling tied to fresh network concerns.

Technically, holding above the $350 support zone remains critical. If buyers maintain control, the next levels to watch were $450 and $550, with a recovery move potentially opening the path toward the psychological $600 level later in the cycle. However, a break below the 200 day EMA move would push ZEC price toward the $250 zone in the coming sessions. Meanwhile, the longer-term targets of $1000 for the year 2026 was still intact.

Zcash activated its NU7 network upgrade on November 6, 2026, introducing changes to its blockchain infrastructure. The upgrade reduces block production time from 75 seconds to 25 seconds, alongside adjustments to block rewards and network fee distribution. ZEC gained around 10.7% on November 6, briefly reaching $1,371 as the upgrade went live.

The development has renewed attention on Zcash’s network utility and long-term adoption. However, the price reaction has remained volatile, with ZEC trading near $1,230 on November 9. Investors are also monitoring ETF flows and proposed changes to the network’s development funding structure. Whether the upgrade can sustain demand will be an important factor in ZEC’s November recovery.
