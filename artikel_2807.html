Real Madrid midfield star rediscovers his best form under Jose Mourinho

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Real Madrid have enjoyed an excellent resurgence under Jose Mourinho over the past couple of months.

Several individual performances have been crucial for Los Blancos, but none has been more striking than Jude Bellingham's return to his devastating best.

After a couple of difficult seasons in the Spanish capital, Bellingham is back to the form that took Spanish football by storm during his debut season at the club.

Having a fully fit and firing Bellingham is great news for Los Blancos, especially with Madrid aspiring to win trophies this season.

Arsenal legend Thierry Henry has praised Bellingham's form and has identified Real Madrid as title contenders this campaign.

"Real Madrid put in a great performance; playing like that, they're contenders for the Champions League.

"They're improving a lot as the weeks go by. Something's happening at Real Madrid – watch out," he explained.

His influence on the team is now beginning to extend beyond just his ability to score goals and provide assists.

The midweek clash against PSV Eindhoven provided the perfect demonstration of what Bellingham can add to this team.

Bellingham has returned to his very best this season. (Photo by Tullio M. Puglia/Getty Images)

The Englishman came on at half-time with Los Blancos struggling to impose themselves and trailing 1-0.

He immediately turned the game on its head, scoring a spectacular goal that will be remembered for a long time.

Bellingham has already scored seven goals this season, just one fewer than he managed throughout last season.

The numbers are moving closer to the kind of attacking output he produced in his debut season in the Spanish capital.

Bellingham scored 23 goals and provided 13 assists from midfield to help Los Blancos complete the La Liga and Champions League double.

Under Jose Mourinho, Bellingham has combined his attacking instincts with greater defensive discipline.

He has always been an intelligent presser, but the Englishman is also taking more responsibility in possession and influencing play.

The best example of this came in the Clasico that Real Madrid won back in October. With the game heading for a draw, Bellingham helped his team secure all three points.

Bellingham is evolving into a complete midfielder under Jose Mourinho. (Photo by Denis Doyle/Getty Images)

He picked up a loose ball following Gabriel Jesus' mistake, escaped several Barcelona players with an extraordinary turn and released Eduardo Camavinga, who set up Mbappe.

It was a moment of individual brilliance, but also one that showed just how decisive he has become in big games.

In Kylian Mbappe, Madrid have a player who can score goals for fun. However, there is a case to be made that Bellingham is their most important player.

Bellingham's resurgence has put Real Madrid on the perfect path to compete for all trophies this season, and it remains to be seen whether he can sustain this form over the course of the campaign.
