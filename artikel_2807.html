Bhayshul Tuten misses practice on Monday

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Jacksonville Jaguars RB Bhayshul Tuten (ankle, foot) did not practice on Monday, Nov. 2. The team noted in the practice report that it was a walkthrough and a practice estimation.

Why It Matters: Tuten has been on a hot streak and is a borderline RB1 at this point in the season. His status will need to be monitored by fantasy players, but if he is active, he should be in lineups as an RB2 at a minimum.
