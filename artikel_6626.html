Carrie Coon Stuns in a Plunging Black Gown at ELLE’s 2026 Women in Hollywood Event

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Carrie Coon is having a moment, and she’s dressing the part. On Monday night, the actress stepped out for ELLE’s Women in Hollywood celebration in Los Angeles, wearing a black Ralph Lauren gown with a deep-cut neckline. The velvet number cascaded down to the floor with a train, adding some extra drama. Coon wore spiky gold statement Fred Leighton earrings and was joined by her husband, Tracy Letts, on the carpet.

Coon is among this year’s honorees, following her acclaimed performances in The Gilded Age this fall and The White Lotus in 2025. In her recent ELLE cover story, she discussed her new film, The Liberation, in which she plays a woman who leaves her conventional life behind after becoming involved with a women’s commune.

The role also prompted Coon to reflect on the limited opportunities available to actresses in Hollywood.

“Something like The Liberation is so exciting for me, because I don’t have access to leading roles in film. I have to fight for every [role],” she told ELLE. “Which is fine; I’m a fighter. But it reflects the reality of how little work there is to go around.”

Coon also spoke about the women who have inspired her throughout her career, singling out her Gilded Age co-star Christine Baranski.

“I love the way Christine Baranski moves through the world,” she said. “She’s been in the business a long time. She raised her family while she was at the height of her powers, and I really value her input. She’s an old-school dame. She’s both political and classy. She always meets the moment, and I love having those conversations around the business with her.”
