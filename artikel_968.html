Alcaraz defeats Shelton to reach championship match at ATP Finals

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Carlos Alcaraz advances to the championship match at the Nitto ATP Finals for the second consecutive year; you can watch the climax to the ATP Finals in Turin this weekend on Sky Sports Tennis

Carlos Alcaraz made it a lucky 13 wins in a row against Ben Shelton to reach Sunday's showpiece of the ATP Finals in Turin.

Alcaraz, who eased concerns about a hamstring ‌injury by completing an unbeaten group-stage campaign, defeated American third seed Shelton 3-6 6-3 7-6 (7-1) despite heavy strapping on his upper left leg.

The Spaniard was beaten by Jannik Sinner in last year's final in Turin but will be determined to go one better this time around and claim his first year-end title. He will face either Alexander Zverev or Daniil Medvedev in Sunday's championship match.

Carlos Alcaraz has reached a seventh final at ATP level in 2026, surpassing Jannik Sinner, Ben Shelton and Taylor Fritz for the most of any player this season

Alcaraz, beaten by Shelton in a memorable US Open quarter-final clash which ended at 3.33am New York time, stretched his winning run ‌to 13 matches after back-to-back titles in Vienna and Paris.

"I was a little bit frustrated at the beginning because I didn't start the match the way I wanted to, to be honest," Alcaraz said.

"And again, someone like Ben, big serve, big playing from the baseline, I think he just pushes you to the limit every time. I didn't start that well. And there was that break early. It was tough for me to deal with.

"But just happy that I came back in the second set playing such great tennis, step by step, focused on all my things and ended up playing good tennis.

"The third set was even closer. I think he raised the level a little bit from the second set. Myself as well. Great, great points, great games and just happy to play a great tennis in the tie-break. I think this win matters. Excited for tomorrow, and happy with everything today."

Shelton, making his second ⁠consecutive appearance in the season finale, ⁠grabbed an early break to race to a 3-0 lead.

Alcaraz, whose fitness had ‌come under scrutiny after he left the court with heavy strapping on his left leg following Wednesday's win over Medvedev, ‌upped his game and started moving the American around more, engaging him in longer rallies.

He earned two break points at 4-2 ⁠but could not convert either of them, with ​Shelton's booming serve getting him out of ​trouble.

The world No 4 fired a second-serve ace ​on his first set point to take the lead ⁠in the match, but Alcaraz kept at it and got his well-deserved break to move 5-3 up before serving ⁠out the second set.

The pair traded ​blows in the third set, refusing to yield serve, ​and took it to a tie-break where Alcaraz cruised through, sealing the win with a ‌return winner.

Former British No 1 Tim Henman, speaking on Sky Sports Tennis: "I thought it was a brilliant match and Shelton most definitely played his part. He made a fast start and his serving was as good as I've seen in a long time but when push came to shove in that final-set tie-break it was Shelton that blinked."

Jamie Murray said: "Shelton got off to that fast start and Carlos wasn't quite there with the energy levels and seemed so emotionally engaged in the match. He obviously brought that to the table as the match went on. In sets two and three, Alcaraz dominated on serve and it was Shelton who blinked in the tie-break."

Meanwhile, Britain's Neal Skupski and American Christian Harrison were beaten 7-6 (12-10) 6-7 (7-9) (10-7) by Marcelo Arevalo and Mate Pavic in the semi-finals of the doubles.
