Foo Fighters, Noah Kahan, the Who Lead New Orleans Jazz and Heritage Festival Lineup

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Foo Fighters, Noah Kahan, and the Who are among the headliners of the 2027 installment of New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival. Other boldfaced names on the event’s poster include Brandi Carlile, Gwen Stefani, James Taylor, Jack White, the Red Clay Strays, LL Cool J, Buddy Guy, the Meters, and Wyclef Jean.

The event features more than 5,000 artists performing on 14 stages over two weekends in spring, between April 22 and May 2, at the Crescent City’s Fair Grounds Race Course. Tickets go on sale at 10 a.m. local time on Friday via the festival’s website. Discounted admission is available to Louisiana residents. Unlike Coachella, the lineup doesn’t repeat on the second weekend, so concertgoers need to check the lineup to decide when they’re going.

“Almost went to Tulane,” Kahan wrote in an Instagram story. “Certainly going to Jazz Fest.”

The festival is also notable for being the first time Roger Daltrey and Pete Townshend have performed together as the Who since concluding a run of shows they’d called The Song Is Over: The North American Farewell Tour. In a Rolling Stone interview earlier this year, Daltrey said that even though the farewell tour was done, it didn’t mean the Who was done. “We are not retired,” he said. “We don’t want to do another tour of America. For the Foo Fighters, Jazz Fest is a return. They headlined the festival, alongside the Rolling Stones and Chris Stapleton, in 2024. They were also headliners in 2012.

The 2027 iteration of Jazz Fest will be the 56th edition of the event, which began in 1970.
