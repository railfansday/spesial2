Ares Commercial RE (ACRE) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Management eyes nonaccrual resolutions and office exits to redeploy capital

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Ares Commercial Real Estate Corporation (ACRE +11.87%) is focusing on the resolution of nonaccrual loans and the reduction of its office exposure to drive future earnings growth. Management reported that the company exited an office asset in Charlotte at a gain and is taking steps to acquire title to a significant office property in Chicago to gain direct control over its resolution. The company is also adjusting its liability structure by repaying its secured term loan in favor of floating rate financing alternatives like repo facilities. CEO Donohoe stated that resolving the remaining $160 million in nonaccrual and REO assets is a significant priority to allow for the redeployment of capital into earning assets.
