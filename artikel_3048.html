A wild week, a pricier turkey, and a Buffett reminder.

By Amanda Kish, CFA, CFP® and Robert Brokamp, CFP, EA – Nov 27, 2026 at 1:08PM EST

Happy Thanksgiving week from Advisor Insights, brought to you by The Motley Fool! We're serving a lighter plate this time so you can get to the leftover turkey. The market, meanwhile, spent the past week on a roller coaster with the safety bar optional: the S&P 500 sank 8.6% last week, its 12th-worst week since 1952, and then jumped 4.7% on Monday when Nvidia's co-founder walked back into the CEO's office. Your clients' plans didn't change in either direction, and that is the message worth carrying into this week's calls.

Trying to dodge the market's worst days without missing its best ones is like leaving a movie during the slow part: the plot twist always lands right as you reach for your coat. Last week's 8.6% slide and Monday's 4.7% snap-back were a very short course in exactly that.

Client-ready line: "Last week was ugly, and Monday was a reminder that the best days tend to show up right next to the worst ones. Your plan was built for weeks like this, so let's stay with it and revisit only if your goals or timeline change."
