Here’s How to Find Rick Ross’ Mansion in ‘Grand Theft Auto VI’

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Miami rapper took to social media and urged fans to find his house in the game's fictionalized version of South Florida.

You can visit Rick Ross‘ Miami mansion in Grand Theft Auto VI.

The rapper took to his Instagram Story and encouraged fans and gamers to find his home in the game and to send him proof in the DMs when they do. “Who can pull up, Vice City, pull up to Star Island and see the muthaf—kin BOAT?” he asked fans in a video. “And everybody that’s playing GTA VI right now, DM it to me when you pull up on Star Island and I need to see if you muthaf—kin see the biggest. The BOAT of all time.” Players can search for Ross’ house on Starfish Island, Grand Theft Auto‘s version of Star Island where many celebrities have property in real life.

YouTuber M3RKMUS1C made a 12-minute tutorial showing gamers the exact location of not only Rozay’s pad but The Weeknd‘s as well, who posted a picture of the Vice City version of his estate on his gene.hackerman finsta recently. He starts his video off by heading straight there. You can find the Toronto singer’s mansion in the Peacock Estates, just south of Peacock Bay, located in the South Vice-Dade County area. He then heads to Starfish Island where he visits Rick Ross’ mansion, which is located on the northeastern side of the island (you can’t miss it), and immediately encounters one of the rarest cars in the game. We don’t want to spoil which specific car it is, so check out the tutorial if you’re curious.

Both Rick Ross and The Weeknd have music that you can hear on the game’s many radio stations, while The Weeknd also contributed original music for the main score on Justice’s song “My Only Vice,” which the streets (social media) are saying plays an important role in the game. You can check out an in-depth look at how the game’s score and soundtrack came together in Billboard‘s cover story.
