Hatton paired with McIlroy: R3 tee times for Race to Dubai season finale

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Pairings and tee times for the third round of DP World Tour Championship, held on the Earth Course at Jumeirah Golf Estates, Dubai.

Rory McIlroy heads into the weekend tied for the lead, with the world No 2 posting rounds of 69 and 70 to sit on five under alongside Tyrrell Hatton.

The Masters champion is looking to win the event for a fourth time and also move above Eugenio Chacarra in the Race to Dubai standings, which would see him match Colin Montgomerie's record with an eighth Order of Merit title.

First-round leader Joost Luiten is a shot back alongside Norway's Kristoffer Reitan, with Jon Rahm three off the pace and Chacarra heading into the weekend four strokes behind.

Live coverage continues Saturday from 7am on Sky Sports Golf, with McIlroy teeing off alongside Hatton in the final group at 8.40am UK time.
