Wrexham earn narrow late win at Lincoln

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Lincoln City vs Wrexham. Sky Bet Championship.

Report and free match highlights from the Sky Bet Championship match between Lincoln City and Wrexham at the ProAmpac Stadium on Saturday; George Thomason scores the only goal as Red Dragons claim a narrow late victory.

George Thomason grabbed a late winner as Wrexham secured a welcome 1-0 victory away at Lincoln.

It was a game lacking real quality in the final third at Sincil Bank.

But Thomason kept his cool when the moment arose to fire Wrexham to victory with his 82nd-minute strike.

It was a first away league victory for Phil Parkinson's charges since the start of September when they beat Millwall 2-0.

The Red Dragons have now gone three games without defeat and will be hoping to use their recent upturn in form as a catalyst towards their play-off push.

For Lincoln, it was the latest bump in the road as the reality of being minnows in the Championship is really beginning to hit home.

Any hopes the international break would bring about a change in fortunes were dashed with a fourth loss on the spin, whilst it is their seventh defeat in nine.

Their only victory in that time was the 4-1 hammering of relegation rivals Burnley, which led to Nicky Hayen's sacking.

The first real chance of the contest fell to the visitors as Callum Doyle nodded wide of the mark from Thomason's delivery into the box.

Down the other end, Rob Street saw his own header well kept out by stopper Anthony Patterson.

Defender Demi Elerewe produced a great double block when he prevented Ollie Rathbone from breaking the deadlock.

Patterson was then called into action once more to deny Adam Reach's effort on the half hour mark after good link up play between Reach and Dan Oyegoke.

Street came close to changing the complexion of the respective half-time team talks when he agonisingly fired wide on the stroke of the break.

The second half was a poorer showing than the first with meaningful chances at a premium after the restart.

George Wickens got down low to keep out Rathbone's tame strike from outside the box in a routine bit of keeping.

Patterson barely had to move to stop Andrei Coubis' effort down the middle of his goal.

Dom Jefferies' free-kick did not threaten the visitors, whilst Oyegoke was kept out by another routine Patterson stop heading into the final quarter of an hour.

But the door was eventually unlocked when Thomason snatched the winner with eight minutes to go.

The 25-year-old pounced on a poor back pass and easily slotted home a left-footed effort beyond the on-rushing Wickens for his second goal of the campaign and most importantly to secure the points for the Welsh outfit.

Lincoln chucked on reinforcements to try and salvage something from the game.

Bailey Cadamarteri was denied Wrexham's second, with Patterson keeping out Jefferies right at the end.

"It's a very disappointing outcome. I thought we went toe-to-toe with Wrexham on the whole.

"The result has been decided by an error. It will be big learning for us as a group. We were trying to force the game, try to find a way to win it and unfortunately we've come away with zero points.

"The feel leading up to the goal, we got a bit too comfortable with the ball. A few passes went a little bit off script. We were perhaps irresponsible at times.

"But the people, the players, the group involved will learn really quickly that you can't indulge at any moment at this level and we got punished.

"Our performance as a whole was competitive enough. We were just starting to tip a nothing game, but unfortunately it's a really tough lesson for us all to learn.

"We looked physically good, but we just didn't quite connect around the penalty area.

"The game was there for us if we really wanted, but when we got that flow we made a big mistake and that sums it up."

"As any win, it's a huge one. We stood strong as a team. There was a group understanding of what was required.

"I spoke to the lads on Monday about the importance about getting our away form back on track.

"You can never guarantee a result, but the performance and the determination of the group…the supporters could see that.

"When the moment came, Thommo took it brilliant. But the defending is as important as that goal.

"I think we're targeting performances on a weekly basis. Each game is different and you need different qualities every game.

"Each win is so hard. It was great to celebrate with our supporters at the end. The players are pleased with that one.

"The lads who have got the shirts at the moment are putting in a great shift. We've had injury problems before, we've pieced teams together at a lot of levels. It's about being in a good position when those players come back.

"We've got good heart and good leadership in this team."
