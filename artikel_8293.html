Investing - Theory, News &amp; General • Re: Chasing Yield. Is there a point where it is worth the risk?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The float on XCCC is 6.35M shares, has lost about 22% since inception, and averages about 258,000 shared traded per day, so about 4%. I do not use funds containing the word “dividend’ ‘yield,’ or ‘high’ in the name. Just because a stock or a fund ‘yields’ a high percentage there is usually a reason, such as big price drops with no correction to the dividend distribution per share (yet).

Some in my circle own those products with the “9% yield” that they enjoy getting but then find out they have a difficult if not impossible time getting a distribution from their principal investment. One has to wait to see if the quarterly 'window' for withdrawals is open and then it may only be about 4% of the account value as documented in the 288-page prospectus. They do not appreciate it when I say that it “…really yields 13%.”
