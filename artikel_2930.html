76ers’ LeBron James reveals what went wrong in NBA Cup loss to Cavs

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

LeBron James had not scored fewer than 10 points in Cleveland across 396 consecutive regular-season games until Friday's disappointing loss against the Cleveland Cavaliers. Following the loss, James opened up about Philadelphia's struggles and explained what went wrong during the NBA Cup matchup.

Speaking to reporters in the locker room after the game, James weighed in on the team's offensive shortcomings while taking responsibility for some of Philadelphia's mistakes.

"I definitely did not feel a good rhythm offensively," James said. "We didn't look like much offensively. We had a couple spurts. We were able to cut it, I think, to three in the third quarter, but too many turnovers on my part. I didn't feel any rhythm offensively."

The Cavaliers' defense completely neutralized Philadelphia's biggest stars on Friday night. During his homecoming to Cleveland, James and Joel Embiid combined for just 14 points.

Embiid finished with only six points on 0-of-9 shooting from the field, along with seven rebounds and two turnovers. James also struggled to make an offensive impact, recording eight points on 3-of-11 shooting while committing six turnovers.

The atmosphere before the game was much more welcoming for James, as he received the familiar cheers from the Cleveland crowd when his name was announced in the starting lineup. LeBron also reflected on the warm reception he received from Cavaliers fans on Friday night.

"At the end of the day, nothing can get as worse as December 2nd, 2010 from a reception standpoint," he said. "So I've seen the near depths of hell when it comes to reception here all the way to the heavens above. So anything in between is easy money."

The loss moves Philadelphia to 8-2 on the season while bringing its five-game winning streak to an end. The Sixers will now aim to put the disappointing loss behind them and get back on track in their upcoming matchup against the Chicago Bulls.
