Measles is endemic in the U.S. again. A panel at the Pan American Health Organization reached that conclusion, and PAHO announced that the country no longer holds measles elimination status. The two labels describe opposite conditions. A country with elimination status is recognized as having eliminated measles. A country where measles is endemic has the virus circulating within its own population.

The panel's finding moved the U.S. from the first category to the second. The country had previously held elimination status, which is why the panel described measles as endemic again rather than endemic for the first time.
