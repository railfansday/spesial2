Chicago Fed chief calls October inflation 'borderline disturbing'

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Austan Goolsbee said services inflation is not coming down, as the consumer price index rose 0.4% in October

Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago President Austan Goolsbee called the October consumer price report “borderline disturbing” in an interview on “PBS NewsHour” on Tuesday.

“It’s another distressing report, borderline disturbing,” Goolsbee said, according to Bloomberg. “We have been hoping to get progress.”

The Bureau of Labor Statistics reported that the consumer price index rose a seasonally adjusted 0.4% in October. Core CPI, which excludes food and energy, rose 0.3%. Economists surveyed by Dow Jones had forecast increases of 0.3% and 0.2%, according to CNBC.

Goolsbee said the effect of tariffs on goods prices should have run its course at this stage, yet it is still being felt. He called services inflation, including health care, "perhaps the most troubling part" and said it is probably not mostly driven by oil prices or tariffs.

"We have got a real inflation issue on our hands that has been developing, and we're going to have to deal with that," he said.

Goolsbee said demand tied to the AI data center build-out has added a demand-side push to prices. He said he fears the overheating is spreading into services and other categories, with prices rising far above the Fed's 2% target. He said the job market has been steady and stable.

Pressed on the prospect of a bigger hike in December, Goolsbee said he would rather not box in policymakers before they sit down to meet.

"I will say it's literally the biggest table I have ever seen in my life in that room. So everything is always on the table, and we must get inflation back down to target, and it's going the wrong way at this point," he said.

Goolsbee is not among the officials holding a vote on policy this year, according to Bloomberg.

Fed Governor Christopher Waller said in a London speech on Tuesday that a half-point rate increase should be considered at the December meeting if October core CPI came in around 0.3% or higher. The CPI release came out later that day.

The Fed held its benchmark rate at 3.75% to 4% at its October meeting. Its next meeting is Dec. 8-9.
