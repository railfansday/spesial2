Kirk Herbstreit makes revised national championship pick

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

ESPN college football analyst and "College GameDay" personality Kirk Herbstreit has made a revised national championship prediction ahead of Week 11 college football action. Herbstreit shared his pick on "The Pat McAfee Show" this week.

Herbstreit thinks the Georgia Bulldogs will play the Notre Dame Fighting Irish in the national championship based on what he's seen thus far.

"Right now, if you had to pick it over, the national championship I've got Georgia versus Notre Dame," Herbstreit said. Herbstreit was impressed with Georgia's win at Ole Miss and Notre Dame's win over Miami a week ago.

Pat McAfee agrees with Herbstreit's pick. "I think I've got the same as you," McAfee said before acknowledging that Georgia and Notre Dame have the best odds to the win national title according to sports betting odds.

"Both got knocked out of the playoff, both have a chip on their shoulder," Herbstreit said on Notre Dame and Georgia. Before the season, Herbstreit predicted Indiana to play Notre Dame in the national title game.

Georgia has a few more regular season tests before the Bulldogs can think about postseason play. Georgia has a home game against the No. 22 Missouri Tigers and a road game against the No. 17 South Carolina Gamecocks before the Bulldogs conclude the season at home against Georgia Tech. Georgia is in the driver's seat to return to the SEC championship.
