Jaguars Rule RB Bhayshul Out For Sunday's Game Against Titans

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

After their worst game of the season last week against the New York Giants, the Jacksonville Jaguars now must contend with the absence of two of their most vital offensive pieces.

Wide receiver Parker Washington is considered "week-to-week" with a shoulder injury and has been ruled out for this Sunday.

Joining him on the sidelines for the Jaguars' Week 12 game against the Tennessee Titans will be Bhayshul Tuten, who has enjoyed a breakout season in his first year as the team's top running back.

Tuten, who has been dealing with foot and ankle injuries over the last three weeks, has also been ruled out against the Titans.

Though Tuten has played through both ailments in the Jaguars' last three games, his play has clearly been affected. Tuten hasn't averaged more than 3.4 yards per carry in any of the last three games and gained only 33 rushing yards against the Giants last week and 29 yards against the Baltimore Ravens in Week 9, his two lowest totals of the season.

Jacksonville will need a healthy Tuten for the season's stretch run and hopes sitting him this week will get him back to 100%.

Even without Tuten and Washington, the Jaguars are still 14-point favorites over the 1-9 Titans.

With Tuten out, Chris Rodriguez Jr. will finally get his chance to lead Jacksonville's backfield.

Rodriguez was signed as a free agent in March and was expected to be in an intense competition with Tuten for the RB1 job. Unfortunately for Rodriguez, an ankle injury sidelined him until training camp, allowing Tuten to take the job and not look back.

Rodriguez's role has been much smaller than anticipated through the first 10 games. He has only 63 carries for the season, barely averaging six per game.

Now, the bruising back gets his opportunity to show what he can do as the team's top back.

It is smart for the Jaguars to finally decide to sit Tuten for at least one game. He has excelled as Travis Etienne's replacement while making the many doubters who believed the team's running game would be its biggest weakness look foolish.

Hopefully, the week off will allow Tuten to get fully healthy for the team's final six games. Jacksonville is currently 2.5 games behind the Buffalo Bills for the AFC's top seed, but the Jaguars have an opportunity to pull even with the 8-3 Kansas City Chiefs and Denver Broncos, who have both already lost this week.

Even without Tuten and Washington, the Jaguars should be able to defeat the rebuilding Titans, who punted on this season weeks ago.

The race for seeding in the AFC will be a multi-team battle that will go down to the wire, and Jacksonville needs its top running back healthy if it wants to challenge Buffalo for home-field advantage throughout the playoffs.
