‘Which?’ Wednesday: Who is getting the No. 1 seed in the AFC?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Past the halfway point of the 2026 NFL regular season, the Kansas City Chiefs are one of five AFC teams with two or fewer losses:

The ultimate question is: who will earn the conference's No. 1 seed at the end of the schedule, earn a postseason bye and have homefield advantage over the rest of the AFC?

Chiefs Kingdom knows a matchup with the Bills awaits on Thanksgiving night, which is a pivotal matchup in this discussion. Buffalo still has to play the Broncos and the (6-4) Green Bay Packers on the road, and host the (7-2) Chicago Bears.

Jacksonville has already lost to Denver and the Houston Texans; the Bears and (6-3) Pittsburgh Steelers still await.

The Bengals have won five games in a row and should be favored to win the next three games before welcoming the Chiefs for a Week 14 battle. Kansas City and the Cleveland Browns (twice) are the only two opponents with winning records left on Cincinnati's schedule.

Denver — also riding a five-game win streak — is coming out of the bye in Week 11 with games against the Las Vegas Raiders and Steelers. Pittsburgh and the Bills are the only two opponents with winning records left on the Broncos' schedule.
