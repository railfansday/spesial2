Kansas State report card: Grading the Wildcats' latest loss at TCU

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

FORT WORTH, Texas — Kansas State dropped its fourth consecutive game after a 20-10 loss to TCU at Amon G. Carter Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 14, in Fort Worth, Texas.

The loss dropped K-State to 4-6 with two games remaining.

A lack of run game didn't help Dillon Duff when making his second career start. An explosive play taking advantage of TCU's blown coverage helped the Wildcats avoid an F.

The Wildcats' defense played well enough to win with a competent offense. The pass rush was fun to watch, but, unfortunately, the Wildcats didn't have a competent offense.

Simon McClannan didn't have his best day punting the ball, and Monterrio Elston had a head-scratching return on a kickoff that gave the Wildcats the ball at their own 11. Luis Rodriguez booted a career-long 55-yard field goal, saving the day for the special teams.

Jordan Peterson's defense looked much improved after last week's disaster. It's worth questioning the move to Sean Gleeson as the team's play-caller and the team's decision to start Dillon Duff over Blake Barnett. Klein's decision to punt on fourth-and-15 from the TCU 40 when down by 10 with 6:15 left was... something.

The Wildcats' defensive tackle led a strong day for the Wildcats' defensive line with 1.5 sacks.

It wasn't the absolute worst, but it was bad.
