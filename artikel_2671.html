Why Miranda Lambert is performing during halftime at Eagles vs. Cowboys NFL Thanksgiving game

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Why Miranda Lambert is performing during halftime at Eagles vs. Cowboys NFL Thanksgiving game originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Cowboys have positioned themselves as country music's team on Thanksgiving Day, regularly hosting artists belonging to the genre. Reba McIntire, Toby Keith, Sheryl Crow and Dolly Parton all basked in the AT&T Stadium spotlight on Turkey Day at one point or another. In 2026, another country music supernova will take the leap.

Miranda Lambert will headline proceedings when the Eagles and Cowboys vanish into the locker room on Thursday afternoon. Like her predecessors, she has a rolodex of noteworthy tunes certain to delight the masses of supporters who will pool into Dallas' stadium come Thanksgiving Day.

With that, here's more on the NFL's move to give the microphone to Lambert during Thursday's NFC East clash.

A Lindale, Texas native, Lambert could be considered an ideal choice to grab the microphone for Eagles vs. Cowboys. After all, Dallas has hosted a number of noteworthy Texas-based acts during its Turkey Day festivities, from Jessica Simpson and Destiny's Child to Post Malone.

Lambert, a country music superstar with three Grammys to her name, will give the spectacle a hometown flavor, a common element in fixtures held in Dallas.

"There's something special about how music brings people together, especially when it's to spread joy and hope to families who need it most," Lambert said in a statement. "The Salvation Army and the Dallas Cowboys have built an incredible tradition with the Red Kettle Kickoff, and I can't wait to be back home in Texas this Thanksgiving to help carry it forward."

Lambert remains relevant two decades into her career, releasing her 10th solo album, "Crisco", in October. The project, which features cameos from Ella Langley and Chris Stapleton, was fairly well-received, climbing to the No. 4 spot in Billboard's Top Country Albums list.

Her friendship with Langley adds to her notoriety. Lambert co-wrote and co-produced Langley's hit song, "Choosin' Texas", which recently broke Mariah Carey's record for longest-running No. 1 single. There's a non-zero chance that Langley joins Lambert on stage come Thursday afternoon. If she does, that will be certain to draw some additional interest from masses of hardcore and casual NFL fans alike.

All told, Lambert has the artistry and audience to drive viewership for the NFL during Thanksgiving Day. Her hometown roots simply add to her appeal. She should have the crowd in the palm of her hand come Thursday.
