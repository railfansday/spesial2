16.27 million watched Commanders-Giants on Thursday night

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Thursday night's game became surprisingly entertaining, except for Giants fans who still don't quite understand how the Commanders won the game.

The audience wasn't surprising — a good-not-great 16.29 million on average watched the game on Prime Video.

It was a 17-percent increase over last year's Week 10 Thursday night game between the Raiders and Broncos, at 13.93 million. Which continues to be a standard basis for comparison, even if it still seems to be not fully apples-to-apples.

The audience fell below the season-to-date average for Prime Video of 17.06 million.

This week's audience surely will, too. The game is Colts-Texans. Which is one of only two Week 11 games featuring teams that, at this point, aren't really playing for anything.

It could be worse. Jets-Chargers could have been the Thursday night game.
