New York Jets WR Garrett Wilson out vs. Kansas City Chiefs

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Nov. 6 (UPI) -- New York Jets wide receiver Garrett Wilson will not play in Week 9 against the Kansas City Chiefs, coach Aaron Glenn told reporters Friday.

Wilson did not practice this week due to a concussion. He caught a team-high 43 passes for 527 yards and three scores through the Jets' first eight games.

"I think it speaks for itself," Glenn said of Wilson's absence. "Anytime you lose a player of that magnitude, it's always tough. But we do have other players.

"Those guys look forward to the challenge."

The Jets also will be without linebacker Trevin Wallace, who is dealing with a groin injury. Defensive back Nashon Wright (back), edge rusher Joseph Ossai (ankle) and defensive lineman Harrison Phillips (ankle) are questionable for Sunday's matchup.

Glenn said running back Breece Hall, tight end Mason Taylor and edge rusher David Bailey each will fully participate in practice for the second consecutive day Friday.

In the wake of Wilson's absence, the Jets are set to turn to Adonai Mitchell, Omar Cooper Jr., Tim Patrick, Isaiah Williams and Malik McClain as their top wide receivers.

Patrick was inactive for the Jets' 38-33 loss to the Las Vegas Raiders on Sunday in East Rutherford, N.J. Mitchell led the Jets with 142 yards and a score off seven catches in the loss.

The Jets (2-6) will face the Chiefs (5-2) at 1 p.m. EST Sunday in Kansas City, Mo.
