Gophers men’s basketball: Minnesota stays unbeaten, picks up another Power 5 win over Oklahoma State

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Gophers remained undefeated by picking up another potentially critical non-conference victory on Friday, and impressed doing it.

Minnesota downed Oklahoma State, 85-70, in a matinee in Charleston, South Carolina.

Kyan Evans recorded a double double — his third in his last four games — with 15 points and 10 assists, Isaac Asuma scored 17 points to go with eight rebounds and Jaylen Crocker-Johnson buried three triples. Five Gophers scored in double figures.

Minnesota led 48-44 early in the second half, then used a 13-4 run to pull away and never looked back.

The Gophers (6-0) now own power-conference wins over Cincinnati and Oklahoma State, both of which came away from The Barn.

Minnesota remains in Charleston, where it will play Utah at 5 p.m. Sunday.
