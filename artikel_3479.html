Pokémon Officially Gives Charizard An Amazing New 30th Anniversary Redesign

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Pokémon's 30th anniversary has been a massive celebration of everything that made the franchise a worldwide phenomenon, from the original games to the iconic characters who've stuck with fans for three decades. One of the best parts of the anniversary for many fans, however, has been the various callbacks to the beloved Kanto era where it all began.

And out of that era came Charizard, who may be one of the most popular Gen 1 Pokémon of all time, second only to the face of the franchise, Pikachu. The final evolution of Charmander has been a fan favorite since 1996, appearing on everything from trading cards to collectibles while continuing to turn heads whenever Pokémon finds a new way to spotlight it.

Now, as the franchise's milestone anniversary continues, Charizard is getting yet another fresh design that gives fans a new way to celebrate the icon. It might not be the kind of reveal fans were expecting, but it's a fitting tribute to one of Pokémon's most recognizable characters.

Sideshow's latest Charizard Statue gives one of Pokémon's most iconic creatures an absolutely stunning new overhaul, capturing the beloved Fire/Flying-type in action. Inspired by Charizard's appearance in the original animated series, the statue features the Pokémon soaring above a swirling column of flames with wings spread wide as it prepares to unleash its power.

The collectible was originally announced in 2025 and was among the Pokémon statues showcased at PokémonXP 2026, held in San Francisco this past August as part of the franchise's 30th-anniversary celebrations. The statue is priced at $640, with a $12.80 tariff fee that brings the list price to $652.80 before shipping and taxes. It's also a limited edition of just 850 pieces, which means it's likely to sell out fast among serious collectors.

That said, Charizard isn't the only Gen 1 favorite getting the premium collectible treatment. Sideshow also offers an impressive life-size Pikachu figure and a charming Snorlax Diorama featuring several Pokémon relaxing together peacefully. There's also the incredible Gengar Diorama, which brings Gastly, Haunter, and Gengar together in a single display. With all these beloved originals receiving premium statues, Sideshow is giving longtime Pokémon fans plenty of ways to celebrate as the huge anniversary year comes to a close.
