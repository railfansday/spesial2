Patriots vs. Chargers Thursday injury report: No major changes on either side

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

After practicing for three straight days coming out of their bye week, the New England Patriots opted to give their players a day off for Thanksgiving. Accordingly, the injury report released on Thursday is merely an estimation what it would look like had the team practiced. And if it did, the list would look exactly like it did on Wednesday.

As opposed to the Patriots, their Week 12 opponent did hold a practice on Thursday even though there were no major changes for the Los Angeles Chargers either. And with that said, let's take a look at the two teams' second injury reports leading up to Sunday's 4:05 p.m. ET kickoff at SoFi Stadium.

The Patriots projected no changes to their list of absentees, with Drake Maye headlining the group after fracturing his fibula in Week 10 versus the Detroit Lions. The expectation is that the MVP runner-up will miss most if not all of the remaining regular season, with backup Tommy DeVito being prepared to take over as the starter in his absence.

More interesting that New England's estimation, however, is the Chargers' report after they practiced Thursday. The first name that stands out is Justin Herbert, the team's starting quarterback who remains in concussion protocol and has now missed both sessions this week; seemingly in anticipation of him being inactive, L.A. promoted practice squad QB Jake Browning to the 53-man roster on Thursday. Browning would serve as QB2 behind second-year passer D.J. Uiagalelei in case Herbert is unable to go.

Herbert is joined on the DNP list by four defensive starters: defensive tackle Dalvin Tomlinson, edge Tuli Tuipulotu, linebacker Daiyan Henley and cornerback Tarheeb Still.

LG Alijah Vera-Tucker (hamstring — IR return)

The Patriots estimated a limited level of participation from both of their recent designees to be brought back from injured reserve, Romeo Doubs and Alijah Vera-Tucker. This leaves their status for Sunday's game in question; New England still has a respective 20 and 18 days to activate them to the 53-man roster.

One of Los Angeles' injured players, meanwhile, graduated from sidelined to limited participation: reserve safety Kendall Williamson.

DT Teair Tart (not injury related — rest)

LB Khalil Mack (not injury related — rest)

Ben Brown would have been a full practice participant again on Thursday, which suggests he is on track to factor into the interior offensive line mix this week. Depending on Alijah Vera-Tucker's outlook, Brown could either start at left or at right guard, or serve as a versatile backup behind whoever gets first billing on Sunday.

For the Chargers, veteran. defenders Teair Tart and Khalil Mack were back in action on Thursday. Both were given a veteran's day of rest on Wednesday and are in line to play on Sunday.

After taking the day off on Thursday, the Patriots will return to the practice fields at Gillette Stadium on Friday morning for another regular session. They will fly out to Los Angeles later that same day.
