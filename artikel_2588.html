Jonas Urbig is ready to succeed Bayern Munich goalkeeper Manuel Neuer per Sven Ulreich

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Sven Ulreich has served Bayern Munich as the backup goalkeeper for over a decade. Being the deputy to one of the greatest goalkeepers in Manuel Neuer, Ulreich was called into action only during injury spells for the GOATkeeper. Ulreich opined that Urbig possesses the consistency and sheer skill that saw Neuer rule the sport.

In a recent interview with Kicker's Frank Linkesch and Mario Krischel, Sven Ulreich was asked what he thought of Jonas Urbig becoming Bayern's primary goalkeeper after Neuer eventually hangs up his boots.

"He has everything it takes and has already put in some excellent performances for Bayern. He has superb goalkeeping technique, quick reflexes, and is very good with the ball at his feet. He simply has tremendous quality," answered Ulreich (as captured by @iMiaSanMia).

Urbig undoubtedly has the talent to reach the very top, as seen in his performances during his days at 1. FC Köln and since donning the Bayern shirt. Most recently, he made an incredible double save against his old club on matchday 10. Köln took the game to the Bavarians and managed to create lethal chances with a rapid counterattack strategy. Urbig really saved the game for Bayern and was vital in preventing a loss.

But the wily Ulreich, who has held onto the backup keeper spot for what feels like forever at Bayern, knows what it takes to remain at the top once you get there. "I believe he is already very mentally mature for his young age. Ultimately, that's what makes the difference at Bayern: how do you handle the mental demands when you become the number one and have to deliver consistently, week after week? I have confidence in Jonas because he strikes me as a very level-headed person, always performs at a high level in training, and has already played very well," added Ulreich as he heaped praise on the 23-year-old.

Neuer's latest injury has given Urbig uninterrupted game time to further hone his craft and prepare to take up the number one spot soon.
