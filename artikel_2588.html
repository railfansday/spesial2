Lack of snow forces cancellation of World Cup skiing races in Canada next month

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

OBERHOFEN AM THUNERSEE, Switzerland (AP) — Two World Cup giant slalom races scheduled for Mont-Tremblant in Canada from Dec. 5 have been canceled because of a lack of snow, the International Ski and Snowboard Federation said Wednesday.

FIS said it was "working on a potential rescheduling."

Tremblant, in Quebec, was due to be the fifth stop on the women's World Cup calendar, and the third venue for giant slalom races after Soelden, Austria — won by Paula Moltzan — in late October and Killington, Vermont, this weekend, where Mikaela Shiffrin is competing.
