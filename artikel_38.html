Xi, Putin meet ahead of Trump's arrival in China for historic trilateral summit

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

China's leader Xi Jinping and his Russian counterpart Vladimir Putin met on Wednesday ahead of US President Donald Trump's arrival in Shenzhen for a high-stakes trilateral meeting. Trump has said Ukraine will be on the agenda, despite Xi vehemently seeing the war as not China's problem, stoking fears that Kyiv's fate could be on the negotiating table.
