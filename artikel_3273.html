Jeff Bezos Says He Was ‘Heavily Involved’ in Casting Robert Pattinson as James Bond

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A day after Amazon MGM announced Robert Pattinson as the next James Bond, Amazon founder and chairman Jeff Bezos said that the was “heavily involved” in the decision.

When speaking at ‌Semafor’s Silicon Valley and the World conference on Tuesday, per Variety, Bezos said that his team was initially “slightly biased” against Pattinson “because he’s already so famous.” He added, “We sort of thought, you know, maybe we should have somebody a little more unknown.” However, during the rigorous screening tests it became “so obvious.” When referring to Pattinson, Bezos said, “He is James Bond.”

The billionaire said that the final decision was made last Friday and that they had narrowed a large group down to five finalists who went through “real, old-fashioned screen tests.” The process involved full costumes, real sets, and other actors with the finalists, said Bezos, who called all five actors gunning for the coveted role “amazing.” He added, “But there was something special about Rob and his performance. It was immediately undebated. Denis [Villeneuve], the director … we all gravitated toward Rob.”

In 2022, Amazon acquired MGM Studios. However, the Bond series’ longtime producers Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson still retained a significant hold on the direction of the franchise. It wasn’t until last February that Amazon MGM struck a deal with Broccoli and Wilson that would allow them to retain the intellectual property rights to Bond, while giving Amazon MGM Studios full creative control. The legendary spy franchise is among the longest-running and most lucrative in Hollywood history. The last Bond film, No Time to Die, was released in 2021, with that movie capping off Daniel Craig’s five-movie run as 007. Pattinson is set to work with filmmaker Denis Villeneuve, who was announced as the next Bond director last year. There’s still no firm timeline for when Villeneuve’s Bond movie will go into production and when it will be released.
