Expert: Newcastle have exposed Arsenal's 'major weakness', Mikel Arteta should be worried

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Newcastle United's second-half resurgence exposed a major weakness in Mikel Arteta's Arsenal side at St James' Park.

The Gunners dominated the first half of their Premier League clash on 21 November, and believed they were cruising to victory, before Matthias Jaissle's side turned things around.

In the end, it took a late strike from Noni Madueke to ensure the game ended 2-2, but concerns have been raised about the Arsenal players' performances.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Arsenal's stars looked complacent and like they were not up for a battle against Newcastle.

Newcastle can be a tough side to deal with, especially with the fans on their side at St James' Park, but the Gunners appeared to capitulate under the growing pressure.

That has not typically been a criticism labelled at Arteta's side, but their failure to put games away when they are on top could lead to further issues going forward.

Football Insider reported on 22 November that Jaissle inspired his Newcastle players to turn around their performances after half-time with some strong words at the break.

However, their second-half displays exposed a major weakness at Arsenal, who looked to shrink in the face of the pressure put on by the players and fans at St James' Park.

Similarities have been drawn to when Brighton beat Arsenal 3-0 weeks ago, when they also appeared unable to keep up with the intensity of their opponents, raising new concerns.

"I think Arsenal and Mikel Arteta should be worried by what happened against Newcastle," Brown told Football Insider.

"They were all over them in that first half, it was easy for them, they went 1-0 up and then they controlled the game and it seemed like it would be another straightforward result.

"But that all changed in the second half, Newcastle came out with plenty of intensity, high pressure, they got the fans on their side, and Arsenal couldn't cope with them.

"If there's one thing this Arsenal team prides itself on, it's being resillient and solid at the back, but I think Newcastle have exposed a major weakness now.

"Arteta's side looked complacent, they went a goal ahead and they were cruising, they thought they could stay in second gear and just ease themselves to a 1-0 win.

"As soon as the intensity changed and they came under pressure, they looked miles off it, and that's not the first time that's happened.

"When they go 1-0 up, they need to start putting teams away, they can't just assume they'll control the game and it'll be easy from there, it's never going to be that simple."

Growing concerns about Arsenal's recent weaknesses have led to questions about what can be done to resolve these issues under Arteta's guidance.

One boost for the Gunners will be the return of William Saliba, who is nearly ready to come back to action and bolster their defensive line once again.

Former Tottenham scout Bryan King told Football Insider that Saliba's return could force Arteta into a tough call but will be positive for the team's performances.

Ezri Konsa has shone alongside Gabriel at the back since joining from Aston Villa, and has made the position his own in the absence of his injured teammate.

However, it is expected that Saliba will come straight back into the side in his usual position, given his importance to Arsenal and to Arteta's plans.
