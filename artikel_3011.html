Tanggal: 2026-11-13

U.S. Global Investors, Inc. (GROW +0.99%) reported a return to operating profitability for the first quarter of fiscal 2027, supported by an 18% increase in operating revenues and a 14% rise in average assets under management. Management stated that growth was primarily driven by increased investor interest in gold and precious metals strategies, which saw average assets rise 45% year over year. The company reported that its thematic exchange-traded funds, specifically those focused on defense technology and global shipping, experienced significant asset growth despite a volatile market environment. Capital allocation remained focused on shareholder returns through monthly dividends and an algorithmic share repurchase program that has reduced outstanding shares by 20% since December 2019.
