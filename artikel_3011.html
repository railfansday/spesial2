Missed the Anthropic IPO? These 3 Stocks Give You Immediate Exposure.

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Nvidia, Amazon, and Alphabet are all benefiting from Anthropic's success.

By Keithen Drury – Nov 19, 2026 at 1:57PM EST

Anthropic (ANT +15.56%) is Wall Street’s newest public AI stock, and there is a ton of excitement surrounding it. However, not everyone wants to be a Day 1 investor. Stocks frequently behave oddly and erratically over their first few months of trading, and many investors don't want to invest in a company until it has released a few quarterly earnings reports’ worth of data about the business.

I fall into that more-cautious category, but there are a few stocks that don't have new-stock risk that are well positioned to benefit from Anthropic going public. Three of those that I'm eyeing are Nvidia (NVDA -2.57%), Amazon (AMZN -3.92%), and Alphabet (GOOG -2.49%) (GOOGL -2.66%).

These three benefit in different ways, but I think each of them serves as an excellent alternative to investing in Anthropic right away.

Nvidia is well-known in the tech world for having developed the best accelerated computing ecosystem available. It has no rival when it comes to its combination of hardware prowess, controlling software, and other attributes.

Anthropic recognizes this, which is why it has chosen Nvidia to be one of its primary computing unit suppliers. Now that Anthropic has secured more funding for computing resources via its IPO, Nvidia will be a big beneficiary.

Additionally, Nvidia owns Anthropic shares and planned to buy more during the IPO process. If Anthropic thrives, then so will its investment.

Nvidia is currently undergoing some leadership turmoil, and the stock has sold off heavily as a result. This could be an excellent buying opportunity, as the business model is still intact and revenues are growing quickly, as evidenced by its most recent quarterly results.

Nvidia is still a great company and a force to be reckoned with in the AI computing world. As more and more computing power is needed to run a growing number of AI workloads, Nvidia will be one of the primary beneficiaries.

Amazon and Alphabet are two of Anthropic's biggest partners. Anthropic doesn't own any of its computing power; it chooses to rent it from cloud computing providers like Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Alphabet's Google Cloud.

From these two, Anthropic selects the best computing unit for the workload, whether it's an Nvidia graphics processing unit (GPU) or one of the custom AI chips available on either of these cloud platforms. Moreover, Amazon and Alphabet have invested heavily in the AI lab; between them, they own nearly 30% of Anthropic.

Between their relationships as compute providers and their massive ownership stakes, both companies have plenty of potential for upside if Anthropic ends up being the AI lab of choice and proves a profitable operation long term.

So, even if you choose not to invest in Anthropic directly, by owning shares of these two, you're indirectly investing in Anthropic. It is a wise move for investors to hold stakes in Amazon, Alphabet, and Nvidia, and to be patient as we wait to see what happens with Anthropic stock.

Indeed, even without the Anthropic connection, these three look pretty attractively valued right now relative to next year's expected earnings.

GOOG PE Ratio (Forward 1y) data by YCharts

With Nvidia trading at a dirt-cheap 9.5 times next year's expected earnings, it's easily the lowest-priced stock of this trio. Alphabet trades at a very reasonable 18.5 times forward earnings, especially considering that the S&P 500 (^GSPC -1.91%) trades at 18.8. Amazon is the most expensive at 24.5 times next year's earnings, but I still think that's a fair price tag for a company of its dominance.

These stocks will benefit immensely from Anthropic's IPO. They serve as great alternatives to investing directly in it right now, and investors may be better off buying these three instead of Anthropic, at least until more is known about the business.
