Uber Bookings Jump 20% as Profit Grows Faster: Rapid Earnings Reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Uber's third quarter paired strong demand with rising profitability, but its revenue line and adjusted profit figures both need a second look.

Uber Technologies (UBER -0.17%) grew gross bookings 20% in the third quarter, its fifth straight quarter above 20%. Shares were up about 0.8% in pre-market trading. GAAP operating income rose 30% to about $1.4 billion. Uber is growing fast, and more of each booking dollar is now turning into profit.

Revenue grew half as fast as bookings, but that isn't a demand problem. Uber says business model changes cut 8 percentage points off revenue growth. Mobility revenue even slipped while Mobility bookings grew. The release doesn't explain the changes, so bookings and trips are the better gauge of demand for now.

The profit story is stronger. Corporate overhead and platform R&D grew much more slowly than bookings, so those fixed costs got spread over far more rides and orders. That's how non-GAAP operating income hit a record 3.9% of bookings.

The adjusted figures deserve a closer look, though. Uber left $857 million in charges out of non-GAAP operating income. The biggest piece, reserves for legal and regulatory matters such as driver classification, was about the same size a year ago. Charges that keep coming back look more like a cost of doing business than a one-time hit.

Watch whether Uber delivers its guided 34% to 39% growth in Q4 non-GAAP EPS. Also watch whether about 700 driverless vehicles are on the platform by year-end, as management expects.
