Celtic Face Hurdle Over Chelsea Teenager: Should O’Neill Even Bother?

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Celtic's pursuit of Chelsea winger Kendry Paez faces an uphill battle, according to Pete O'Rourke in Football Insider. The club have shown recent interest in the 19-year-old Ecuadorian for a January loan, yet Martin O'Neill is happy with what he already has on the flanks.

Jota, Yang Hyun-jun, Camilo Duran, Sebastian Tounekti and Haissem Hassan all compete for wide places, and Duran scored the winner at Hibernian last month. The manager sees no need to add another unless further injuries force his hand.

Chelsea add a second obstacle. They want regular first-team football for a player who has not had it, and Football Insider reports heavy competition from European and Championship clubs, so the Premier League side could place him elsewhere quickly.

The club already host two Chelsea loanees, Shumaira Mheuka and Landon Emenalo, so the working relationship exists, but it doesn't solve the minutes problem.

Paez is a right-sided attacking midfielder who can also operate as a No. 10, and the 27-cap Ecuador international brings creativity more than finishing. His loan record shows the gap. One goal in 21 appearances at Strasbourg and one in 14 for River Plate. This season he has managed 229 minutes across five Premier League 2 games, with one assist. FotMob values him at about £6m, well below the roughly £16m peak of August 2025.

Away from transfers, former Aberdeen chief executive Keith Wyness has criticised the club for releasing £299 Lisbon Lions watches, warning that the product exploits that history for minimal profit.

No, not unless injuries change the picture. The problems are not on the wings. Hogh had gone 15 matches without scoring before the 3-2 Europa League win over Besiktas, and O'Neill said after the 2-0 defeat at Falkirk, their sixth loss in ten matches, that his side could easily have scored six or seven. That points to a finishing and defensive issue rather than a creativity shortage. Paez is a creator, so he wouldn't fix either.

He would also sit behind five wingers, which defeats the purpose of a loan Chelsea want built around minutes. The club would take on a player lacking rhythm, with two goals in 35 loan games, for little visible gain.

A proper centre-forward or a defensive reinforcement deserves the January effort. If two wide players get injured, Paez becomes a reasonable emergency option on loan. Until then, passing is the sensible call.
