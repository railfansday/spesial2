‘He Has Adapted to It': Mekies Hails Max Verstappen's Masterclass With Flawed RB22 in Las Vegas

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Max Verstappen overcame an unresolved problem with his Red Bull RB22 to win the Las Vegas Grand Prix. Team principal Laurent Mekies has praised the four-time champion's ability to adapt despite the car's limitations.

"There is a car characteristic that he is certainly unhappy with, and we all realize that we will not be able to fix it this weekend, and he has adapted to it, as opposed to us fixing it," Mekies said.

His comments revealed that Red Bull knew Verstappen was unhappy with the car but could not make the changes he wanted. The team instead relied on his ability to adjust and find a way around the problem.

Verstappen said the issue had followed him since the Brazilian Grand Prix and continued throughout Las Vegas. He described it as a problem with the car's feel through the steering wheel, rather than its balance. He also said it made attacking corners harder, although it was not dangerous.

Despite that setback, Verstappen started from pole and led 34 of the race's 50 laps. He repeatedly lost the lead in heavy slipstream battles, then found a way to take it back. Mekies praised his calm decisions, defensive driving, and communication with the pit wall.

The Red Bull team principal has further admitted that Verstappen's car problems could continue into 2027. The team has made progress, but it cannot fully solve the issue with this year's RB22.

Max Verstappen might have to deal with the car issues in 2027 as well. (Source: MEGA)

Mekies confirmed that Verstappen was right about the car losing performance during sessions and races. He admitted the solution will not come soon, so this is likely to play a role until next year. Mekies also described the problem as complex, explaining why Red Bull had struggled to find a complete fix.

Verstappen has consistently complained that the RB22 becomes less predictable as a session or race develops. The problem has often affected the rear of the car, including during qualifying at Monza. He has also said these struggles are not new and have affected him for several seasons.

Max Verstappen has deemed the RB22 an unpredictable car. (Source: MEGA)

Red Bull has tried different approaches and made some progress during the Madrid weekend. However, Mekies warned that the team still needs more time to understand and solve the problem. The issue could continue affecting Verstappen during the remaining races and into next season.

Verstappen has written a new chapter in his rivalry with George Russell after winning the Las Vegas Grand Prix. The Red Bull driver took another swipe at the Mercedes star following their latest disagreement that began at the Vegas FP3.

Russell had criticized Verstappen after contact during final practice. He questioned the Dutchman's driving and said he had lost respect for him. Verstappen later dismissed those comments, claiming that the No. 63 was far below his level.

Their tension was on display during Sunday's race, with both drivers fighting near the front. Russell later dropped down the order after an incident that saw him lock up his front right tire and drive into the wall. Verstappen admitted he enjoyed watching the incident unfold in his mirrors.

The latest exchange adds another chapter to their strained relationship. Their previous disagreements include heated moments at the 2024 Qatar and 2025 Spanish Grands Prix. Their rivalry remains one of the biggest talking points heading into the final races, with Russell potentially hoping to exact his revenge.
