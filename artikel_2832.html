GametimeCT girls soccer power rankings, week 8 (Nov. 6): CCC champ Glastonbury moves up to No. 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The CIAC girls soccer tournaments begin Friday, and the GametimeCT power rankings are set heading into the final two weeks of the 2026 season.

St. Joseph remained No. 1 after winning its fourth FCIAC championship in five years. Glastonbury made the biggest move forward, up three spots to No. 2 after winning the CCC championship. Ridgefield lost its first game in the FCIAC final and remained at No. 3.

Greenwich dropped to No. 4 after falling to Ridgefield in the FCIAC semifinals. Notre Dame Prep won its second straight SWC title and is now fifth. Simsbury is sixth after being upset by Farmington in the CCC semifinals. Amity, No. 7 this week, is looking for a Class LL championship after winning the SCC crown.

Two teams returned to the poll this week: Pomperaug, the SWC finalist, at No. 9 and Farmington at No. 10. Undefeated East Hampton moved up two spots to No. 8 after going undefeated in the Shoreline Conference.

The final GametimeCT rankings will be released after the conclusion of the CIAC tournaments.
