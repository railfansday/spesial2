NFL predictions: Fernando Mendoza’s chapter in Las Vegas Raiders-Denver Broncos rivalry begins in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Week 11 feels kind of late for the first matchup between the Las Vegas Raiders and Denver Broncos of the season. At the same time, it's kind of fitting that the game will be rookie quarterback Fernando Mendoza's first chance to make his mark on the Raiders-Broncos rivalry in just his second career start. Also, we're at the point of the year where the contenders start to separate from the pretenders in Silver and Black Pride's Pick'em Competition!

Odds below are provided by FanDuel Sportsbook and are subject to change.

Holder's picks: LV +9.5, Over 41.5, DEN ML

Not that it was much of a secret, but last week's game against the Seahawks was a not-so-subtle reminder of how important Brock Bowers is to the Raiders' offense, especially with a young quarterback. Bowers' status for this Sunday is up in the air at the time of writing; however, he did return to practice on Thursday to provide some optimism.

Either way, I think Vegas is going to have a tough time pulling off the upset on the road, especially since Denver had more time to prepare with the bye last week. But if Bowers can suit up, I like Mendoza and the Raiders to cover the spread.
