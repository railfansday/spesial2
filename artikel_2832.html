Florida State's offensive keys vs. NC State

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Florida State got the win against the Pittsburgh Panthers last Friday. However, it wasn't the best performance for them offensively as they only produced 260 yards of offense and 16 points.

This Saturday, FSU will face the NC State Wolfpack, who have a defense that can be taken advantage of if the Seminoles execute correctly. They allow over 28 points per game while ranking 70th in rush defense and 88th in total defense. So, Florida State should have plenty of opportunities to score.

Let's dive into five things FSU needs to do offensively to pull out a win against NC State.

Lopez has really started to come along as a true freshman. After just four receptions for 56 yards in the first seven games, he's caught 13 passes for 178 yards and a touchdown over the last three, and his yardage totals have increased each game. One could argue that he's looked like best receiving option for Florida State lately, and they need to keep getting him the ball.

NC State's defense struggles against the pass, ranking 110th in the nation in pass defense at 237.6 yards a game. Daniels needs to take full advantage and keep feeding the ball to Lopez, Micahi Danzy, and Jayvan Boggs. This can set up the running game for FSU which will need all the help that they can get with several backs being listed as out.

The Wolfpack have allowed 34 trips inside red zone defensively, and opposing offenses have scored points 30 times with 24 touchdowns. Florida State's offense has scored on 35 of their 40 red zone opportunities with 27 touchdowns (87.5%). So, they need to keep up that efficiency on Saturday.

One thing NC State's defense does well is stop teams from converting on third down. They've only allowed conversions on 35.1% of the ones they've faced. Of course, there's no avoiding third downs, but the more FSU's offense can move the chains in two plays or less the more they can put up points on a subpar unit.

NC State scores over 29 points a game on offense. They'll be on the road against Florida State, and getting turnovers can create great scoring opportunities and take the crowd out of the game. The Seminoles must protect the football and turn their possessions into scores to keep the pressure on the Wolfpack.
