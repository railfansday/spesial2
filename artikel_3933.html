Feature: 25+ Of The Best Discounted Games From Xbox's Pre-Black Friday Sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

We're counting down the days until Xbox's Black Friday sales! They're likely to begin next Wednesday, however we've spotted a plethora of massively discounted games on sale right now. So, we're back to rummage through them and pick out a banger or two for you.

Xbox is currently running a couple of sales - a 'Sports & Racing' BOGOF, and an ID@XBOX Publisher Spotlight sale. We've thrown in a few other notable deals from outside of those sales that we think are worth checking out.

It's a hefty job sifting through Xbox Sales, we get it. So, instead of having you waste your precious gaming time doomscrolling through the Xbox Store, we've handpicked over 25 deals from Xbox's sales. These recommendations come complete with trailers, descriptions, Game Pass availability, and whether each title supports Xbox Play Anywhere!

Of course you can find all of these games, and then some, by going to the "Deals" section of the Microsoft Store app on your Xbox console.
