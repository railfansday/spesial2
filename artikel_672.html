Kristin Chenoweth to Honor Late Dolly Parton With Gay Men’s Chorus of Los Angeles at Holiday Shows

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

"Celebrating my queen Dolly Parton with the Gay Men's Chorus of Los Angeles? It just doesn’t get any better," said Chenoweth, who will hit the stage at Saban Theatre on Dec. 12-13.

The Gay Men’s Chorus of Los Angeles will honor the late Dolly Parton at back-to-back holiday concerts next month and the group has recruited a very special guest to join and pay respects through song.

Emmy and Tony Award winner Kristin Chenoweth will hit the stage with GMCLA for A Very Dolly Holiday!, which will take place Dec. 12 and 13 at Saban Theatre in Beverly Hills, Calif. The chorus, which is kicking off its 48th season, will honor the iconic Parton by performing some of her hits and beloved favorites like “Coat of Many Colors,” “I Will Always Love You,” “Light of a Clear Blue Morning,” “Islands in the Stream,” “Hard Candy Christmas” and “9 to 5” along with holiday favorites like “Happy Xmas,” “Where Are You Christmas?,” “O Holy Night,” “The Christmas Song” and “Let It Snow.” Related Stories Music Dolly Parton Lands Three Posthumous Grammy Nominations Lifestyle Hollywood's Latest Must-Have Luxury? “Kristin Chenoweth is one of the great theatrical and vocal talents of our time, a true ally to our community, and someone we’ve been hoping to welcome for quite some time,” said executive director and producer Lou Spisto. “We are absolutely thrilled to have her join us, particularly now, as I can’t imagine a more perfect occasion than A Very Dolly Holiday! Kristin’s deep affection for Dolly Parton, both as an artist and as a person, makes this collaboration especially meaningful. To have a Broadway legend join us in celebrating the ultimate icon and sharing the joy, love and spirit that Dolly represents is something truly special.”

Said Chenoweth: “Celebrating my queen Dolly Parton with the Gay Men’s Chorus of Los Angeles? It just doesn’t get any better. I’ve been a fan of the chorus forever, and I’m so excited to get to take the stage with these fabulous men for the first time. We’re going to give the audiences a show they won’t forget.”

Most recently, Chenoweth, best known for a range of roles from Wicked to Pushing Daisies, most recently produced and starred in Broadway’s The Queen of Versailles. She can be seen on the upcoming fifth season of Peacock’s The Traitors. Tickets are available here. GMCLA’s nearly 200-voice chorus is under the leadership of artistic director Ernest H. Harrison.
