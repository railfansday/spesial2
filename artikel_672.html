NYC Mayor’s Office Hires First Executive Director of Music

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Rachael Pazdan will help the city support musicians by serving as a liaison between the government and the music industry.

The NYC mayor’s office of media and entertainment (MOME) hired Rachael Pazdan as its first executive director of music. She will report to Rafael Espinal, commissioner of the NYC MOME.

“New York City has shaped the sound of music for generations,” Espinal said in a statement. “But being a great city for music should also mean being a great city for the people who make it. That’s why building our music portfolio is a priority for me. Rachael Pazdan brings the experience and relationships to help us get it right. Together, we’ll listen to the industry and work toward making live performances easier, expanding opportunities across all five boroughs, and connecting young New Yorkers to careers in music. Our goal is simple: make New York City the best place in the world to create music, perform music, and make a living in music.”

Pazdan will build on the city’s New York Music Month and its newly launched Music Production Bootcamp, as well as Mayor Zohran Mamdani’s commitment to increase access to entertainment experiences for NYC residents.

Since launching in 2017, New York Music Month has grown to host 38 talks, 21 performances, a conference and a free rehearsal series that offers more than 2,000 hours of free rehearsal space across the city in four locations.

Parzdan has worked with indie music promoter AdHoc, helping the company become a full-service creative agency that served clients including Bryant Park, Pitchfork, A2IM and BandLab. While at the company, she also helped promote events including Global Citizen in New York City and Johannesburg.

She also served as a booker for numerous independent venues across New York City, including Public Records, The Bell House and Le Poisson Rouge, and produced the short-form docuseries Stories from the Stage, which shared stories from New York City’s independent venues and promoters during the pandemic.
