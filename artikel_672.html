‘Dune: Prophecy’ Star Tabu Joins YRF Entertainment’s Netflix Rescue Drama ‘Tunnel Men’ (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Indian star Tabu, whose international credits include “Life of Pi” and “Dune: Prophecy,” has signed on to “Tunnel Men,” a Netflix series from YRF Entertainment, the streaming banner of studio Yash Raj Films.

The show is her first original Hindi-language series for streaming.

“Tunnel Men” is a fictionalized drama that takes its cue from the 2023 rescue in the northern Indian state of Uttarakhand. That November, a tunnel collapse left a group of workers trapped underground, and India watched anxiously as the effort to free them dragged on. The series sets out to capture the grit and cooperation that carried the operation through. “The Railway Men” director Shiv Rawail is back on board as showrunner.

Over a career of more than 30 years, Tabu has moved freely between commercial and independent cinema, working in Hindi as well as in regional-language films. She won Indian National Film Awards for “Maachis” and “Chandni Bar,” while “Maqbool,” “Haider” and “Andhadhun” proved her versatility.

Outside India, she played Ashima Ganguli in Mira Nair’s “The Namesake,” appeared in Ang Lee’s “Life of Pi” and took on the role of Sister Francesca in the series “Dune: Prophecy.”

The series is Saugata Mukherjee’s first since he became head of content at YRF Entertainment, reporting to CEO Akshaye Widhani. An experienced content executive, Mukherjee has overseen shows including “Scam 1992,” “Maharani,” “Tabbar” and “Rocket Boys.” Three series he worked on – “Aarya,” “Rocket Boys” and “Real Kashmir Football Club” – earned International Emmy nominations.

The project extends a relationship between YRF Entertainment and Netflix that began in 2023 with “The Romantics.”
