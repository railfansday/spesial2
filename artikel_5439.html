S&P 500 falls 0.8% and Nasdaq 1.5% as Nvidia sinks after Huang’s resignation

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Nvidia stock fell more than 11% after CFO Colette Kress was named interim CEO, while oil rose on President Donald Trump's Iran remarks

Nvidia $NVDA said on Thursday that CEO Jensen Huang has resigned and named CFO Colette Kress interim CEO, and the company's stock fell more than 11%.

The S&P 500 fell 0.8% and the Nasdaq $NDAQ Composite lost 1.5%. The Dow Jones Industrial Average gained 0.2%, a rise of 108 points.

Nvidia stock slid by up to 14% when trading began. Kress will serve until the company finds a permanent successor. Huang co-founded Nvidia in 1993 and has previously dismissed the value of mapping out a successor, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Semiconductor shares and other artificial-intelligence plays declined in Asia. According to The Wall Street Journal, Nvidia’s board had been planning to oust Huang. The Journal also said the slide put Nvidia on track to have lost just shy of $750 billion in market value since the scandal broke.

Huang had acknowledged an affair with a woman suspected of spying for China, according to CNBC.

Investors shifted money toward other chipmakers. Shares of Advanced Micro Devices and Broadcom $AVGO each rose around 2%. Oracle $ORCL stock lost 4%.

Oil prices climbed following a Truth Social post in which President Donald Trump declared, "We don't need a deal with Iran. Those days have long since passed." U.S. West Texas Intermediate futures were up by over 1%, changing hands at more than $87 a barrel. Brent crude futures also were up over 1%, topping $94.

Chevron $CVX and ExxonMobil $XOM stock each rose roughly 1%. Eli Lilly $LLY stock gained more than 1%.
