Reolink Releases Much-Anticipated OMVI X16 PoE - The Flagship of the OMVI Series with A Triple-Lens Camera and 16x Optical Zoom

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The award-winning OMVI X16 PoE pairs a panoramic view with long-range 4K zoom and 360-degree endless pan - and no subscription required

LOS ANGELES, Nov. 3, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Reolink, a global smart home security brand, today released the award-winning OMVI X16 PoE, the flagship of its OMVI series. Designed for larger properties and high-security environments, the OMVI X16 PoE keeps the entire scene in view while bringing faraway details into sharp focus.

The OMVI X16 PoE pushes performance further with its total 24MP triple-lens system: a 16MP dual-lens panoramic bullet camera that blends two images into a single 180-degree ultra-wide view, paired with a 4K (8MP) pan-tilt-zoom dome camera with 16x optical zoom. The PTZ dome delivers 360-degree, high-speed, endless panning with 95-degree vertical tilt for expansive coverage and intuitive tracking.

"When it comes to security, seeing the full picture is just as important as being able to focus on the details," said Annie Potter, Head of Marketing at Reolink North America. "The OMVI X16 PoE brings those capabilities together, giving homeowners and businesses greater visibility and confidence in what's happening around their property. And with local storage and no mandatory subscription fees, users have more control over their security and their footage."

Starting today, it is available for purchase on Amazon and Reolink.com for $679.99 in the U.S.  For more information, visit Reolink.com.

Reolink offers smart security solutions for homes and businesses, aiming for a seamless security experience with its wide range of products. Trusted by millions of users globally and available in over 5,000 stores through leading retailers worldwide, Reolink provides video surveillance and protection, standing out for its commitment to innovation in security technology.
