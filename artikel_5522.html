Veho Launches Pulse AI to Unlock Post-Purchase Intelligence From Every Delivery

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Built on billions of data points from deliveries facilitated through the Veho platform, Pulse AI helps brands understand what matters after checkout - and turn that insight into stronger loyalty and growth.

NEW YORK, Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- VehoÂ® today announced Pulse AI, a new platform that enables e-commerce brands to turn delivery data into actionable post-purchase intelligence. Pulse AI gives teams a new way to understand what happens after checkout, how those experiences shape customer behavior, and which actions can strengthen the relationship.

At the center of the platform is Pulse Agent, a conversational AI experience that lets teams ask questions about their deliveries in plain English and receive answers, analysis, and visualizations in record time. Today, that includes questions about service performance.

Every delivery reveals much more than whether a package arrived on time. It captures the promise made to the customer at checkout, the experience they received, and how they responded. Because Veho's platform facilitates both the delivery network and the customer-facing experience around it, Pulse AI brings those signals together into a clearer view of the post-purchase journey.

"Veho has been built on the belief that delivery is a key 'moment of truth' in the e-commerce customer experience," said Itamar Zur, Co-founder and CEO of Veho. "Facilitating the entire supply chain of a package gives us a real-time view of what's happening from checkout all the way to the customer's door. Pulse AI turns that data into intelligence that teams across the business can use, which unlocks an enormous opportunity: turning millions of delivery moments into insights that empower brands to better understand their customers, make smarter decisions, and create more personalized e-commerce experiences that drive loyalty and growth."

Turn post-purchase signals into answersOver time, Pulse Agent will give teams a simpler way to explore delivery data and connect post-purchase experiences to customer outcomes:

Ultimately, Pulse AI can help brands answer the bigger and most strategic questions about their customers - what they value, which experiences build trust and loyalty, and what brings them back.

Bring delivery and customer signals togetherPulse AI draws on data across deliveries facilitated through the Veho platform, including shipment events, service performance, delivery exceptions, and more. Together, those signals connect operational performance with the customer experience it creates. Pulse Agent makes that data accessible through a conversational interface instead of separate reports and dashboards.

Pulse AI is designed for enterprise use, with controls designed to protect how client and operational data is accessed and processed.

Turn insights into growthOver time, Veho plans to connect delivery data with additional signals such as customer sentiment, support interactions, and commercial outcomes, giving brands a comprehensive view of which post-purchase experiences drive satisfaction, repeat purchase, loyalty, and long-term customer value.

Pulse AI and Pulse Agent will be available in beta to select Veho clients. Clients can request access through their Client Success Manager.
