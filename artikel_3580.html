RHOBH's Dorit Kemsley Teases 'Fresh Start' in New Home After Eviction

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Dorit Kemsley is embracing a new era and a brand new home.

In August, TMZ reported the Real Housewives of Beverly Hills star, 50, and estranged husband PK Kemsley were ordered to sell their marital home in Los Angeles amid speculation earlier this year that they faced foreclosure due to missed mortgage payments.

Speaking to Page Six in an article published Tuesday, November 17, Dorit confirmed that she and the couple’s children — son Jagger, 12, and daughter Phoenix, 10 — are indeed moving out and have already found a new home.

“So as everyone knows, we were going to court to be evicted by a certain date, which is pretty crummy,” the Beverly Beach founder said. “But I did find another house. The kids and I are moving into a new home imminently. Fresh beginnings, fresh start. We are all welcoming it.”

According to Page Six, Dorit’s deadline to leave the marital home was November 13.

In March, Dorit addressed a report that she and PK owed over $800,000 in mortgage repayments. (Dorit and PK first bought their six-bedroom home in Encino for $6,475,000 in August 2019.)

“That is something that I learned a few weeks before everyone else learned [about it],” Dorit said at the time. “It’s something that PK was in charge of. I didn’t know it was not being paid. We’ve since spoken and he agreed to bring it up to date.”

In June, she told Us, “Where we are now, I mean, I’m hoping that we both have the same goal. The house should come out of foreclosure. If PK and I are not together, it makes sense for us to sell the home and for the kids and I to move somewhere else.”

Dorit filed for divorce from PK, 59, in April 2025, nearly one year after the pair announced their separation.

“I really, really do hope and wish for this to be over,” Dorit told Page Six of the divorce proceedings. “It has been very, very taxing. I have found it incredibly unnecessary. I never felt that we should go down this road. I never felt that PK and I should be enemies. There’s never a reason to be, and I’m very, very saddened.”

On Saturday, November 14, Dorit exclusively spoke to Us about her new chapter at the launch of her new beverage partnership with Maison d’Or.

“I have to apologize because I haven’t been very present on social media,” she said. “I was navigating a lot of big changes in my life, and it was enough just to really put one foot in front of the other — let alone go on social media. And I’m not exactly going to show that life is great when, quite frankly, it wasn’t.”

“But I will say life is looking a lot better, and I’m very, very excited about this next chapter,” the Bravolebrity told Us.
