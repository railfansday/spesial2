Over a decade of atmospheric data from NASA's Curiosity rover hints at small amounts of surface water on Mars

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

NASA's Curiosity rover has been collecting data about Mars' thin, carbon dioxide–rich atmosphere since 2012. Now, a study published in the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences has analyzed over a decade of this data to determine whether the daily and seasonal changes in Mars' atmosphere could be explained by known processes, including the freezing and release of carbon dioxide at its poles. What they found suggests a process involving water either attached to soil grains or in extremely thin, liquid-like films at the surface.
