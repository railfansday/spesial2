Sunderland 'turn attention to' 53-year-old manager as Regis Le Bris sack plan revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Sunderland are considering Thomas Frank as a potential target to replace Regis Le Bris amid growing pressure on his position.

Le Bris is coming under fire for a poor start to the Premier League season, with the Black Cats currently bottom of the table with just one win from nine games so far.

Despite having plenty of credit in the bank from last season, there is a feeling that the board are losing patience with the manager and could soon be tempted into a change.

Former Sunderland chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that a number of potential replacements are alerady being assessed, with Frank, 53, now firmly on their radar.

The former Tottenham boss' experience in the top flight and immediate availability have made him a target, although it is unclear how high he ranks on their list.

Contingency plans are being put in place behind the scenes, with Frank one of a number of big-name bosses on their radar should they be forced to sack Le Bris.

Brown told Football Insider on 2 November that Le Bris may have just two games to save his job at Sunderland, with the upcoming international break causing concerns.

The Black Cats will face Dinamo Zagreb in the Europa League next time out, before their Premier League clash against Chelsea, with both games at the Stadium of Light.

While reports elsewhere have suggested Le Bris will be backed for now, they must start winning games in the Premier League, or they will be forced to make a change.

"If Sunderland do make a change then the next question is about where they're going to look," Brown told Football Insider.

"It's not as simple as just saying 'yeah, let's change the manager', because you have to be sure that whoever comes in next is going to make the positive difference that you want.

"There's no point making a change of manager and then you're still struggling on the pitch, and that's a risk every club has to take.

"Thomas Frank, for example, is somebody who is available and I'm sure will be very much on Sunderland's radar if they do decide to make a change.

"He's out of work at the moment and looking to get back into the job by all accounts, so I'm sure taking over at Sunderland would be something that appeals to him.

"I'd have my doubts after his time at Tottenham, but he is still held in very high regard for what he did at Brentford, and that will be the case at Sunderland I'm sure.

"But there are other managers who they could target as well if they do decide to make a change, so it depends where he ranks on their list.

"For now, it seems like they want to stick with Le Bris, because are any of the options available going to do a better job than him? It would be difficult, that's for sure."

Much of whether Sunderland will target Frank could depend on whether he is willing to step back into the dugout, as he is currently taking time out of the game.

There is a growing feeling he could now be lured back in, and if Sunderland opt to sack Le Bris, the former Tottenham boss could be keen to move to the Stadium of Light.

This comes after Brown told Football Insider back in September that Frank is looking to return to Premier League management and is interested in discussing opportunities.

However, Sunderland are not the only club keen on Frank, as he continues to be linked with the Crystal Palace job amid growing pressure on Pierre Sage's position.

Should the Eagles opt to a change, the former Brentford boss is believed to be firmly on their radar, and there will be stiff competition for whichever club makes an approach.
