'CBS Mornings' Coanchor Gayle King's Family Guide: 2 Kids, Grandchildren, More

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Gayle King‘s family has long been a focal point of her life and career, from her 1980s marriage to William Bumpus to her evolving role as a mother and grandmother.

The CBS Mornings broadcaster has long been candid about how parenthood shaped her career ambitions.

“I knew from the time that I was pregnant that I was not going to be a stay-at-home mom,” she told Huffington Post in 2012. “I love working and I happen to believe that if you’re happy in your life and you’re happy in what you’re doing, it makes you a better person, it makes you a better mother, it makes you a better partner and it makes you better at your job.”

William Bumpus, an attorney, was married to King from 1982 to 1993. They welcomed daughter Kirby in 1986, followed by son William Jr. in 1987. They ultimately divorced after the TV broadcaster discovered her spouse was having an affair with a friend.

“I’m not a huge fan of the woman I caught naked with my now ex-husband on June 24, 1990, at 9:16 p.m.,” King told Vanity Fair in July 2016. “But I don’t remember the details.”

The couple initially attempted to reconcile through counseling before divorcing in 1993. Speaking on the “Call Her Daddy” podcast in May 2026, King said she realized Bumpus “hadn’t really changed” and that she “didn’t like” who she was becoming during that time.

“I would be out in the garage feeling the hood of the car to see if it was warm. I was going through his phone. And I thought, ‘I don’t want to live like this,’ only to find out he was cheating again,” she said.

Bumpus publicly apologized to King in 2016, calling himself “haunted” by his choice, and again after the “Call Her Daddy” episode aired.

“My deepest apologies to Gayle, to our daughter Kirby and her husband, Virgil, to our son William and his wife, Elise, and to our three grandchildren, for the pain I caused decades ago,” he said. “Those actions were mine.”

Kirby, King’s firstborn, arrived in 1986. She married Virgil Miller in 2020. Miller died in November 2026 after a private battle with cancer.

Further details about Miller’s diagnosis were not immediately publicly shared.

In addition to Kirby, Miller is survived by their two children: son Luca, born in 2021, and daughter Grayson, born in 2024. Luca, for his part, holds a special place in his grandmother’s world.

Before her April 2025 Blue Origin flight, she turned to Luca for emotional support — in stuffed-animal form.

“I just asked my favorite grandson, ‘Could you pick out your favorite animal, one of your favorite stuffed animals, that could go with me to space and keep ‘Gyla’ company?'” King exclusively told Us in April 2025. “So he’s looking.”

William Jr., born in 1987, is King’s second child and only son. He is married wife Elise Smith, with whom he shares one child.

While William Jr. has largely stayed out of the spotlight, King has often spoken about how important it was to her that both of her children grow up with a strong relationship with their father, even after the divorce.

“Once it is an issue, you have to balance what’s most important to you,” she said on “The Pivot” podcast in 2024. “I don’t care how successful you are, if your kids aren’t happy and healthy and adjusted. All this success means nothing.”
