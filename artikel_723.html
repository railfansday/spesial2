Jamie Miller’s ‘Babydoll’ Scores Fifth Consecutive Week as U.K.’s No. 1 Single

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Justice scores its best-ever placing with GTA VI hit "Flower in a Hurricane."

Jamie Miller is No. 1 for a fifth week on the U.K.’s Official Singles Chart with breakthrough single “Babydoll” on the list published Nov. 20.

The Cardiff-born singer first hit the top spot in October, becoming the first male Welsh solo act to hit the summit of the charts since Shakin’ Stevens in 1984. “Babydoll” is currently up to No. 37 on the Billboard Hot 100, a new peak.

Speaking to Billboard in a recent Chartbreaker story, Miller commented on the song’s rapid rise. “It became this kind of phenomenon on TikTok,” he said of the track. “It feels like the song has kind of unified people, and it’s been so amazing to see how fun it’s been for everybody — from kids in Africa and people in Sweden to these amazing dance groups in America and TV shows in the U.K.”

Ashe’s “Stop the Wedding!” comes in at No. 2 and is a non-mover in the spot, her best-ever placing on the charts. Sombr’s “My Body Isn’t Ready” lifts two places to No. 3, and comes in the same week he was announced for a major slot at 2027’s Glastonbury Festival.

Tate McRae’s “Housewife” closes at No. 4, followed by Sienna Spiro’s previous chart-topper “Great Expectation” (No. 5).

As GTA VI launches globally, Justice’s “Flower in a Hurricane — a collaboration with SZA, Tame Impala and King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard that appears on the soundtrack – hits a new peak at No 9, the duo’s best-ever placing on the charts to date.

A number of festive songs leap up the chart. Wham!’s “Last Christmas” is up to No. 12, Mariah Carey’s “All I Want for Christmas Is You” is at No. 18 and Olivia Rodrigo’s “Take Me Home (4 Christmas)” sits at No. 24.
