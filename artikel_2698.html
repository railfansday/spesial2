Flick talks Lamine&#8217;s penalty miss, Pedri, De Jong, Rodri, Balde after Barcelona 3-2 Villarreal

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Barcelona manager Hansi Flick highlighted Pedri's influence, Frenkie de Jong's versatility and the team's resilience after his side recovered from a two-goal deficit to beat Villarreal 3-2 in La Liga.

The victory at Spotify Camp Nou kept Barcelona three points ahead of Real Madrid at the top of the standings, but the German coach also addressed several individual performances and selection decisions in his post-match press conference.

The result was particularly significant given Barcelona's difficult opening seven minutes, during which Tajon Buchanan and Tani Oluwaseyi put Villarreal 2-0 ahead.

Lamine Yamal subsequently missed a penalty, leaving Flick's side with considerable work to do. However, three goals between the 57th and 67th minutes completed an impressive turnaround.

Flick acknowledged that his players struggled against Villarreal's aggressive pressing but praised their ability to regain control rather than allow the early goals to dictate the outcome.

"Looking at the first ten minutes, we didn't start well at all. We knew they were a team that would press us. It was tough, and we conceded two goals.

"The team's reaction was incredible, even in the first half. We had chances like the penalty. In the second half, we had a clear game plan, and playing like that, the opponent gets tired, which is normal.

Lamine Yamal missed a penalty against Villarreal. (Photo by David Ramos/Getty Images)

"We managed to score three goals in 10 minutes. Incredible," he explained.

The missed penalty also raised questions about Barcelona's penalty-taking arrangements, with Yamal failing to convert in the 23rd minute.

Flick explained that the decision had been made by the players on the pitch rather than imposed by the coaching staff.

"Lamine has also scored a few. It was decided on the pitch. I'll speak to them and we'll see how we handle it in future. Both of them can score from the penalty spot."

Pedri's influence in midfield was another major talking point.

The midfielder helped Barcelona dictate the tempo after the interval, while Frenkie de Jong impressed despite being deployed in an unfamiliar defensive position.

When asked about the Spaniard, the German manager said, "It was one of our best matches. The way we controlled the midfield – the key moments of the match, the tempo, the pace of play.

"De Jong and Gerard, in particular, did a fantastic job at the heart of the defence. That position isn't an easy one for Frenkie.

"If you look at the statistics, you could say it was two shots on target and two goals. But I think we deserved the win."

The Barcelona manager also explained why he considered De Jong's deployment in defence a worthwhile option, particularly given his ability to progress possession.

'It's an option, of course. It's incredible how he's played with the ball. If you have to be in that back line, it's not easy.

"It's more comfortable doing it from midfield because you always have someone behind you. His attitude in that position helps us, and he's shown that, above all, with the ball.

"We were discussing whether to play Eric as a centre-back and De Jong as a full-back… and I think we made the right decision.'

Alejandro Balde was an unused substitute against Villarreal. (Photo by Alex Caparros/Getty Images)

Flick also discussed his midfield options, acknowledging the contribution of players who have continued to improve despite difficult moments during the match.

"Rodri is an incredible player, but the rest of the midfielders are also doing very well. Fermin has improved a lot since his mistake. It's not easy when you're two goals down."

Meanwhile, Alejandro Balde's reduced involvement prompted questions about his position in the squad and a possible departure.

Flick dismissed the suggestion that he had been discussing the situation with the player's representatives.

"I haven't spoken to him and I don't speak to agents either. We need them all. We've got eight matches before the next international break and we need them all.

"Eric and Cancelo have played brilliantly. It's not easy for him to get playing time, but the important thing is that he's in the right frame of mind, although it's true that if you're not playing, you can't be entirely happy," he concluded.
