USC men's basketball destroys Arkansas-Pine Bluff 122-46

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

After two games away from Galen Center, the USC men's basketball team returned home for a matchup with Arkansas-Pine Bluff Sunday evening. Facing off against one of the worst teams in the country, the Trojans entered the game as massive favorites.

The game went as expected and then some, with USC absolutely dominating Arkansas-Pine Bluff 122-46. The 76 points broke the record for the largest margin of victory in program history.

The Trojans had eight different players finish with nine or more points. Alijah Arenas led the way, scoring 21 while also adding six assists in just 17 minutes of action.

With the game out of hand early, no USC player played more than 24 minutes. That is quite unusual for head coach Eric Musselman, who typically loves to have his starters play nearly the entire game.

The Trojans are back in action on Thursday night at Galen Center against Tarleton State. Tipoff is set for 8:00 p.m. Pacific time on Big Ten Network.
