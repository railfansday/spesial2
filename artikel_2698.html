Ravens send CB Marlon Humphrey home, will not play him this week due to comments about Baltimore

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Ravens cornerback Marlon Humphrey will not be in the lineup for the team’s game against the Panthers on Sunday.

Head coach Jesse Minter told reporters on Thursday that Humphrey has been sent home for the rest of the week after saying the city of Baltimore is “best at making criminals” on the most recent episode of his podcast. Humphrey apologized for the comments on Wednesday, but Minter said the team determined this was the best choice for the organization.

“Internal conversations obviously with myself and the leadership of the organization, we’ve decided to deactivate Marlon for this game, this upcoming week,” Minter said. “I know there’s gonna be a lot of questions. I said this yesterday, these conversations are very personal and private. To me, [they] need to remain that for the good of the team.”

Minter was asked if Humphrey will have to do anything else in order to return to the lineup for their game against the Texans in Week 12. He said that was one of the conversations that will remain in house as the Ravens move forward.
