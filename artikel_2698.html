Eagles vs. Steelers: Live score updates, highlights, injury news

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Philadelphia Eagles are 0-7 in their last seven games after starting the season with a 2-0 record. Can they beat the Pittsburgh Steelers to snap their losing streak?!

It'll be interesting to see if the Eagles have some answers to the problems that plagued them prior to their bye. Especially on offense. Getting DeVonta Smith back should help. But would anyone really be surprised if it's more of the same struggles? Probably not.

Follow along here for live score updates, highlights, injury news, analysis, and more before this article ultimately turns into our game recap. The best place to actually discuss the game as it happens is still in our game thread, which you can find here.

The Steelers began at their own 31-yard line. Facing 3rd-and-4, Aaron Rodgers completed a pass to DK Metcalf just short of the sticks. Facing 4th-and-1, the Steelers kept their offense on the field and Jeremiah Trotter Jr. tackled Jaylen Warren short of the marker! Big stop to start the game. Check the timestamp:

The Eagles took over at the plus 40-yard line. Saquon Barkley took a carry to the left edge for an easy first down run. Joe Milton then got strip-sacked … and just looked at the ball on the ground … before Jordan Mailata jumped on it for the Eagles to retain possession. What are you doing, Joe? Facing 3rd-and-8 after a completion to DeVonta Smith, Milton threw short of the sticks to Makai Lemon. Jake Elliott came in for the 41-yard field goal make. Five plays for 17 yards and three points. Might've been good to be more aggressive and try to get seven points by converting that 4th-and-5. EAGLES 3, STEELERS 0.

The Steelers took over at their own 20-yard line. Facing 3rd-and-8 after Jihaad Campbell made a nice run stop in the hole, Trot Jr. tackled Pat Freiermuth short of the sticks to force a Steelers punt. Strong start by Vic Fangio's defense out of the bye.

The Eagles took over at their own 31-yard line after a 17-yard Britain Covey punt return. Facing 3rd-and-13 after Milton was sacked on a blitz, Milton had a clean pocket but held onto the ball to invite pressure … and rolled left … to throw a strike on the move to Darius Cooper (who else but the birthday boy?) for 29 yards and a first down. Great ball. Barkley ran for another first down but it was called back due to an illegal shift on Dallas Goedert. Milton helped the Eagles pick up another first down through the air with a touch pass to Goedert for an over-the-shoulder grab for 38 yards. What is this offensive success?! Facing 3rd-and-9 after Barkley was tackled for a loss, Makai Lemon dropped a Milton pass short of the sticks and Mailata was flagged for illegal formation anyway. Elliott came in for the 29-yard field goal make. EAGLES 6, STEELERS 0.

Byron Young got the start at nose tackle next to Jalen Carter and Moro Ojomo.

John Ojukwu was the sixth offensive lineman (in place of Fred Johnson, who was released).
