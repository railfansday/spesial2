NFL Thanksgiving schedule: Chiefs-Bills, Eagles-Cowboys, Bears-Lions on the menu

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

NFL Thanksgiving schedule: Chiefs-Bills, Eagles-Cowboys, Bears-Lions on the menu originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

NFL fans will be treated to a football feast this Thanksgiving.

For two decades, the NFL has brought three Thanksgiving games to the table each year. That will continue in 2026 with a three-course schedule filled with intriguing matchups.

That's not all this year, though. The league introduced a Thanksgiving Eve game for the first time, and it will also have its fourth annual Black Friday.

Here is a look at the NFL's loaded Thanksgiving menu in 2026.

This year's holiday festivities will kick off on Thanksgiving Eve, as the Los Angeles Rams will host the Green Bay Packers on Wednesday, Nov. 25.

That leads into a stacked menu of three Thanksgiving games on Thursday, Nov. 26: the Chicago Bears at the Detroit Lions as the appetizer, the Philadelphia Eagles at the Dallas Cowboys for the main course and the Kansas City Chiefs at the Buffalo Bills for dessert on NBC and Peacock.

The holiday action isn't done there, as the Denver Broncos will visit the Pittsburgh Steelers on Nov. 27 for a Black Friday contest.

The NFL will also have 10 games on Sunday, Nov. 29, and a Monday Night Football game between the Carolina Panthers and Tampa Bay Buccaneers on Nov. 30.

Here is the Thanksgiving schedule for 2026:

Chiefs vs. Bills: 8:20 p.m. ET, NBC and Peacock

Broncos vs. Steelers, 3 p.m. ET, Prime Video

The Packers defeated the Lions 31-24, the Cowboys beat the Chiefs 31-28 and the Bengals rolled past the Ravens 32-14 on Thanksgiving in 2025.
