Jaelan Phillips wins first NFC defensive player of the week award

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Panthers moved into sole possession of first place in the NFC South with their victory over the Saints on Sunday.

Now one of their players has been recognized for his performance in the win.

Edge rusher Jaelan Phillips has been named NFC defensive player of the week.

Phillips had five total tackles with three for loss, a sack, and a fumble recovery that led to a Panthers touchdown in the 20-17 victory.

According to the league, Phillips is the second player in the conference to have three TFLs and a fumble recovery in a single game in 2026.

This is Phillips’ first career defensive player of the week award.

In his first season with Carolina, Phillips has tallied 3.5 sacks, five tackles for loss, and eight QB hits so far in 2026.
