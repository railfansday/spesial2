PowerWash Simulator 2 DLC ‘Barbie Pack’ launches December 3

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

PowerWash Simulator 2 downloadable content “Barbie Pack” will launch on December 3 for $7.99 / £6.50 / €7.99, developer FuturLab announced.

Here is an overview of the downloadable content, via its Steam page:

With Barbie, You Can Be Anything! Turns out, “anything” includes a PowerWasher Barbie. Step outside of the box and power wash your way through Malibu in this clean-up adventure spanning five levels. Spray away muck, revealing sparkling, pristine plastic beneath!

Show dirt who’s boss in a sleek new look that takes power dressing to a whole new level. Accessorize with a range of practical and chic tools and attachments that make cleaning up a dream. Trade in the pink convertible and take your new abseiling kit and scissor lift for a spin to help hunt dirt high and low.

It’s not always about beach parties on the Dream Boat, sometimes, Barbie just wants to chill out to the soothing sounds of high-pressure water. PowerWash Barbie knows that no task is too big to tackle solo, but there’s always room for friends—pair up with your bestie in split-screen or play with up to four in Free Play!

Ever wanted to ride the DreamHouse elevator from the point of view of Barbie? Or chill by the fire in front of the Dream Camper? Now you can. Escape to the ideal destination where the only pressure is in the water, as you blast away dirt from five new levels teeming with Barbie playsets and accessories.

PowerWash Simulator 2 is available now for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, and PC via Steam, Epic Games Store, and Microsoft Store.
