TSSAA announces initial football region assignments for 2027, 2028 seasons

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The TSSAA has released its initial high school football region alignments for the 2027-2029 classification cycle.

The high school governing body released its final classification and enrollment numbers for football on Oct. 5.

Schools wishing to appeal its football region placement must do so at the TSSAA Board of Control meeting on Nov. 19. All appeal requests must be submitted no later than Nov. 16.
