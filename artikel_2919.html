Sixers head to Orlando for showdown with Franz Wagner and the Magic

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Philadelphia 76ers are coming off three straight victories against the shorthanded Toronto Raptors and Indiana Pacers and rebuilding Brooklyn Nets, sitting at the top of the Eastern Conference with a 12-3 record. They'll now look to keep their early-season momentum going as they travel to Orlando to face off against the Magic.

The Magic, similar to seasons past, sit at a 6-7 record. Notably, the franchise has been without Paolo Banchero for much of the season, while Jalen Suggs and Anthony Black have also missed time. Despite the injuries and offensive struggles, there has been a major silver lining in the Magic's season in the form of Franz Wagner, who has been lighting it up to start the year.

Through 13 games, Wagner has averaged 24.2 points, 6.8 rebounds and 8.3 assists while shooting 46.4 percent from the field. The forward has been instrumental on both ends of the floor, with his playmaking particularly standing out. He should present a challenge for the Sixers defensively, especially with Jaylen Brown out (more on that below).

The Sixers, on the other hand, have enjoyed plenty of success early in the season. They've faced one of the toughest schedules in the league, and despite a few disappointing performances, have taken care of business against lesser opponents while picking up some notable victories against the better teams in the conference.

Joel Embiid, in particular, is coming off his best scoring performance of the season, amassing 37 points on 59.1 percent shooting in his last game. Embiid has had a roller-coaster season in terms of production, but if his latest performance is any indication, he may finally be starting to find his rhythm.

LeBron James sat out the latter half of the back-to-back against Brooklyn but is expected to return for this matchup. His presence should provide another offensive boost for Philadelphia as the Sixers look to maintain their momentum. With the aforementioned Brown out, James will need to step up his offensive production. Defensively, the Sixers will likely throw a combination of Dean Wade and Kentavious Caldwell-Pope at Wagner in an effort to slow him down.

Update, 1 p.m.: Dean Wade is now listed as questionable with left hamstring tightness/soreness.

The Sixers will be without Brown (hamstring strain), Adem Bona and Dominick Barlow. As things stand, neither Embiid or James is on the injury report. For Orlando, Banchero, Jonathan Isaac, Noah Penda and Izaiyah Nelson are out.

Orlando's size, physicality and defensive versatility should provide a solid test, particularly for a Sixers offense that has experienced its share of inconsistencies. If Philadelphia can establish an offensive rhythm early and limit the Magic's opportunities in transition, it should be in a strong position to extend its winning streak.
