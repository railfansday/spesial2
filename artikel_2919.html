Cardinals’ Carson Beck pops up on injury report in latest QB setback

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Cardinals fans may be holding their breath going into the weekend over whether Carson Beck will be able to play on Sunday against the Chiefs.

Beck was placed on the Cardinals' injury report on Thursday as a limited participant due to a back issue, raising concerns about whether he will be available for the Week 11 game, which would reignite the quarterback shuffle that Arizona has gone through this season.

The rookie quarterback had been dealing with back spasms a few weeks ago, according to Cardinals reporter Dani Surreck.

Arizona Cardinals quarterback Carson Beck (19) warms up before an NFL football game against the Los Angeles Rams in Glendale, Ariz., Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. Getty Images

Arizona Republic Cardinals reporter Theo Mackie noted in a post on X that Beck had been in the team locker room after practice and "seemed to be walking around normally."

An update on Beck's status will take place on Friday when the team releases its final injury report of the week.

While Beck's stat line didn't appear to be anything special, reviews of his first NFL start — a 34-6 loss to the Rams — were more complimentary.

He finished completing 11 of 18 pass attempts for 108 yards and had two interceptions.

"I think the overall operation of our offense was really good," Beck told reporters after his first game. "That's something that [head] coach [Mike] LaFleur talks about a lot is the operation [and] getting in and out of the huddle. There were a couple communication things that [for] the first time being out there — calling plays again in a live game and live action and just getting better, being more clear and concise with the huddle, being able to get in and out quickly and get on the ball and snap it right.

"Everything that you can control before the ball is actually snapped and there are live bullets flying, but I think that was good. Just going where the defense told me to go, reading the defense, reading out our plays and trying to give guys opportunities to make plays. For me as a quarterback, that's what I'm here to do. Run the offense, have great operation, get in and out of the huddle and then ultimately play clean football. I think that's something that this offense can do."

If Beck isn't able to go on Sunday, Josh Johnson and Joey Aguilar are the Cardinals' backup options.
