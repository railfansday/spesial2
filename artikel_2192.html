Cesar Peixoto must manage Wolves’ creative machine carefully with star potential emphasised in Cardiff City win

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Wolves continued their improved run of form on Saturday as they defeated Cardiff City 3-0 at Molineux. The triumph made it 10 points from the last 12 available for Wanderers, continuing their fine response to back-to-back losses in the first two games after the September-October international break.

Cesar Peixoto‘s side were two up within 15 minutes thanks to goals from Fer Lopez and Adam Armstrong, Ladislav Krejci made it three just before the hour mark to really secure the points, heading home from a Kieran Tripper corner.

Alongside assisting the third goal, right-back Trippier was a standout creative threat for Wolves with the joint-most chances made at three – tied with Bluebirds winger Chris Willock. The 36-year-old and captain on the day showed why he has a key role to play at Molineux through his technical ability and leadership.

Trippier was Wolves’ leading creator on Saturday with his three opportunities made. He hugged the right-hand side for the vast majority of the match and saw a lot of the ball, with his 74 touches more than any other player for the Old Gold.

He completed 35 passes, 11 of which were into the final third and 14 of which were line-breaking. He made two accurate crosses too, showing how he mixed up his work in possession with deliveries into the box and others into spaces occupied by Lopez, Jean-Ricner Bellegarde and Armstrong.

Trippier did his defensive duties too, with 12 defensive contributions the joint-most in the game. He made four tackles, five clearances and three interceptions, so it was a very well-rounded display from the veteran right-back.

Creatively, his display saw the theme of his performances continue. Trippier currently ranks in the top 4% of Championship right-backs for successful passes (52.53), accurate long balls (4.35), line-breaking passes (16.39), chances created (3.01) and chances created (3.01) per 90 (all stats via Fotmob).

From deep areas on the right-hand side, Trippier is a major creative outlet and a go-to player in possession. This paired with his vast experience and leadership from a storied career to date make him a crucial asset for Wolves.

There are few doubts over what Trippier can contribute at Wolves. His past at the very top of club and international football means he provides a level of pedigree few in the Championship can offer, and he sets an example on and off the pitch as a vocal leader.

He’s a standout creatively too, serving as a key asset in possession and from set-pieces. Such traits mean that, in an ideal world, Trippier could play every minute of every match.

However, at 36 and with injury issues arising over his last two years at Newcastle United and early on this term, Peixoto faces the challenge of managing his workload. Trippier can not be expected to play every minute of a Championship season, so they must be careful not to demand too much of the right-back to limit the risk of injury.

It will be a tough balance to strike, but it is key to getting the best out of him all season. Trippier needs to be rotated with Jackson Tchatchoua to keep him fit and at the top of his game.
