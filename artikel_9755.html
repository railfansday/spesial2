I scanned 4 developer-platform homepages for AI-agent readiness (llms.txt, MCP, x402, OpenAPI)

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Posted on Nov 22 • Originally published at manhourplan.com

AI assistants and agents read websites differently from people. Most of them don't run JavaScript, they look for files such as llms.txt, and they follow robots.txt rules written for their own user agents. Agents that do work for someone can also call an API or pay for it per call, but only if they can find it.

I built a small, free scanner that checks ten of these signals and gives a score out of 100: Agent-Ready Scan. Disclosure: it's my tool, and the page also sells a done-for-you setup. The scan itself is free, needs no account, and doesn't store results.

The MCP, x402, API-catalog and OpenAPI checks only matter if you offer an API or data. A brochure site can be in good shape without them.
