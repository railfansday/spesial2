Ella Langley’s ‘Choosin’ Texas’ Overtakes Mariah Carey for Most Weeks Atop Streaming Songs Chart

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

"Choosin' Texas" reigns for a record-setting 27th week amid the beginning of the 2026 holiday season.

Nearly two years after Mariah Carey’s “All I Want for Christmas Is You” set a new mark for the most weeks at No. 1 on Billboard’s Streaming Songs chart, a new song has assumed the throne — and it’s the same one that broke Carey’s Billboard Hot 100 longevity record earlier this year.

That’s right: Ella Langley’s “Choosin’ Texas” adds another notch to its belt on the Nov. 21-dated Billboard charts, reigning on Streaming Songs for a record-setting 27th week since the tally’s 2013 inception. In the week ending Nov. 12, “Choosin’ Texas” earned 17.8 million chart-eligible streams in the United States, down 5%, according to Luminate.

The song first topped Streaming Songs on the Jan. 31 survey and has led the ranking over multiple periods since, most recently on the Oct. 24 tally prior to its return to No. 1. That week, it tied Carey’s mark of 26 frames, which she set during the 2025 holiday season (Jan. 3, 2026).

Speaking of the holidays, “All I Want for Christmas Is You” returns to Streaming Songs’ top 10 on the latest chart, thanks to 10.1 million streams, a boost of 42%. It first reached No. 1 in 2019 and has topped the ranking for anywhere between three and five weeks every year since, except for 2023, when it was unable to pass Brenda Lee’s “Rockin’ Around the Christmas Tree” for the duration of the season.

It’s worthwhile to bring up 2023, when a holiday song other than Carey’s standard was the most streamed of the season, because the Nov. 21 chart features Wham!’s “Last Christmas” ahead of Carey at No. 4 (11 million streams, a gain of 48%).

However, if “All I Want for Christmas Is You” is able to pass “Last Christmas” once the holiday season kicks into full gear, it’s possible the former could retake its Streaming Songs longevity record from “Choosin’ Texas” — especially as holiday tracks continue to rise while Langley’s current recordholder falls, albeit just 5% on the latest ranking.

For now, “Choosin’ Texas” is the new all-time leader in weeks at No. 1, a record it also added to on the Nov. 21-dated Hot 100, spending its 28th week atop the tally, as previously reported.
