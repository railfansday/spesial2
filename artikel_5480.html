Forum Systems Launches QS Platform 10, a Sovereign AI Platform for Running Enterprise AI Agents Across Private and Public Models

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Unifies API security, AI orchestration and governance in one platform that deploys on-premises, in private cloud or hybrid, licensed by capacity rather than tokens. Domain solutions for healthcare, contracts and finance follow, beginning in Q1 2027.

BOSTON, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Forum Systems and its subsidiary Quantum Gears today announced the immediate availability of QS Platform 10, a sovereign AI platform that lets enterprises and governments run AI agents and intelligent workflows on their own terms, across both private and public large language models.

QS Platform 10 unifies QS Sentry, QS Cortex and QS DevX into one foundation for connecting enterprise systems, orchestrating AI and governing every action AI takes. Organizations can deploy it on-premises, in private or public cloud, or across a hybrid of all three, and build on the applications, APIs and information assets they already have instead of standing up a separate stack for AI.

For most enterprises, sovereignty does not mean giving up public models. It means deciding where each workload runs and enforcing that decision consistently, across every application and agent.

QS Cortex routes each request to the appropriate private or public model based on the customer's data policies, quality requirements and cost, and records where each request ran. Sensitive workloads can stay on infrastructure the customer controls, while others draw on leading commercial models where they add value. The same governance applies everywhere: private where it matters, public where it helps, governed throughout.

For organizations that need dedicated sovereign compute, QS Platform 10 can run on QS Nano, Quantum Gears' AI infrastructure deployed within the customer's own facilities.

"Enterprises shouldn't have to choose between the capability of public models and control over their own data, systems and costs," said Mamoon Yunus, President and CEO of Forum Systems. "QS Platform 10 lets them use both, under one set of policies, on infrastructure they choose. That is what sovereignty means in practice."

"We expect 2027 to be a year of reckoning," Yunus continued. "Boards will scrutinize the cost, risk and maintainability of AI initiatives, particularly in regulated industries like healthcare. Generating code with AI has become easy; operating it securely and accountably for years has not. We believe the pendulum will swing back toward proven enterprise software vendors that combine decades of product engineering with AI to deliver that accountability."

QS Platform 10 is licensed by capacity, not by token. As AI adoption grows, customers expand usage within their licensed capacity without a per-token platform bill, and QS Cortex's routing between comparable private and public models is designed to keep AI economics predictable.
