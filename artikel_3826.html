It's not a cheap upgrade, but if you're as sick as I am holding down a button to sprint in Pokopia or Ocarina of Time, it's worth investigating some Joy-Con alternatives with back buttons. These extra clackers can be mapped to pretty much any command you usually use in-game, but they serve best when replacing face buttons. That way you can keep your thumbs on the sticks themselves, maintaining control of your camera.

There are a few Joy-Con alternatives that include these back button additions, but the best we've tested so far is the CRKD Nitro Deck 2. The controller wraps around the Switch 2, taking the Joy-Con out of the equation completely, and comes with four clackers to program.
