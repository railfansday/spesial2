Former MLB All-Star, manager Bob Boone dies at 78

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Nov. 10 (UPI) -- Former-All-Star catcher and manager Bob Boone has died, his family announced Tuesday.

Boone, the father of New York Yankees manager Aaron Boone and former All-Star infielder Bret Boone, was 78. His family did not disclose his cause of death.

"With a heavy heart, I'm here to confirm the passing of my dad [Bob Boone] today," Bret Boone wrote on Facebook. "He was a man of great integrity. He was a great dad, husband, and patriarch.

"It was a privilege to call him my father. Baseball lost a great one today. I love you dad."

Boone made his MLB debut in 1972 for the Philadelphia Phillies. The seven-time Gold Glove winner and four-time All-Star also spent time with the California Angels and Kansas City Royals. He won a World Series in 1980 with the Phillies. Boone hit a career-high .295 in 1988. He hit .254 with 105 home runs over 19 seasons.

Boone went on to manage the Royals and Cincinnati Reds. He owned an overall record of 181-206 as an MLB manager. He never posted a winning record or led a team to the postseason.

Boone's sons combined for four All-Star appearances. His father, Ray Boone, also was a two-time All-Star. He won the 1948 World Series with the Cleveland Indians alongside Hall of Fame teammates Bob Lemon, Bob Feller, Satchel Paige, Lou Boudreau, Joe Gordon and Larry Doby, among others.
