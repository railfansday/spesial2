Brock Purdy’s embarrassing viral moment resurfaces with Vikings twist

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Brock Purdy has become one of the NFL's most efficient quarterbacks thanks to his ability to execute.

But that doesn't mean he hasn't had a misstep or two with the 49ers.

San Francisco 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

One notable miscue resurfaced this week ahead of Purdy's return trip to Mexico City, where he last played during garbage time of a blowout win over the Cardinals in 2022 when he was Jimmy Garoppolo's backup.

The 49ers were leading by 28 points with just over seven minutes remaining, and Purdy was sent in to relieve Garoppolo and salt the game away.

On one play, Purdy took a shotgun snap and turned left to hand off the ball to running back Jordan Mason, who went to the right and forced Purdy to keep the ball. The play was botched, but Purdy wound up scrambling for a 6-yard gain while "Monday Night Football" broadcasters Joe Buck and Troy Aikman poked fun at the rookie who was the last pick in the NFL draft.

Buck noted the 49ers called Purdy, at the time, "Baby Bosa" because of his thick legs.

Aikman, laughing, added, "Baby Bosa needs to know which way to turn to hand off."

The play came up this week because Purdy was asked about it by reporters on Thursday ahead of San Francisco's trip to play the Vikings at Azteca Stadium. Oddly enough, the running back in the play, Mason, will be on the other sideline Sunday night after the 49ers traded him to the Vikings in March 2025 for fifth- and sixth-round draft picks.

Jordan Mason #27 of the Minnesota Vikings runs the ball against the Green Bay Packers in the fourth quarter of a game at U.S. Bank Stadium on Sunday, September 13, 2026, in Minneapolis, Minnesota. Getty Images

"It's funny now," Purdy said Thursday. "In the moment when I'm a rookie, I'm trying to prove myself to Kyle [Shanahan] and my teammates. So I was stressing over it after that. But it's funny that I got an opportunity after that, and we went and had success."

That game was Purdy's third career NFL appearance. He didn't throw a pass as the 49ers ran the ball to kill the clock in the 38-10 win.

But history changed two weeks after that gaffe when Garoppolo injured his foot against the Dolphins, forcing Purdy to become the team's starting quarterback. He completed 25 of 37 for 210 yards with a touchdown and interception in relief helping the 49ers win, 33-17.

Purdy went on to win his next five starts and two playoff games against the Seahawks and Cowboys before suffering a torn UCL in his throwing elbow early in the NFC Championship game against the Eagles.

The 49ers lost that game but made it to the Super Bowl the next season with Purdy under center, and he hasn't been called "Baby Bosa" since.
