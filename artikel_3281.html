‘The Pitt’ Sets Season 3 Premiere Date

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The holidays have arrived early, as HBO Max has revealed that its critically acclaimed medical drama, The Pitt, will premiere its third season on January 7, 2027.

The news dropped today via the show’s Instagram account, with actors Taylor Dearden, Isa Briones, and Gerran Howell dressed as their characters Mel, Santos, and Whitaker, respectively. The trio is in the locker room, with little reveals shown in their compartments: Santos’ says The Pitt returns on a pink sticky note, and Mel finds a small package that also has a pink sticky note that reads January 7.

The Pitt chronicles the urgent challenges U.S. frontline healthcare professionals face through the lens of the fictional Pittsburgh Trauma Medical Center’s emergency department. Offering a real-time format, the series traces, hour by hour, the medical cases and personal crises physicians and nurses must navigate as they reckon with an overabundance of patients and a lack of resources.

The cast includes Noah Wyle (Michael “Robby” Robinavitch), Patrick Ball (Dr. Langdon), Katherine LaNasa (Dana Evans), Fiona Dourif (Dr. McKay), Taylor Dearden (Dr. King), Isa Briones (Dr. Santos), Gerran Howell (Dr. Whitaker), Shabana Azeez (Javadi), Ayesha Harris (Dr. Ellis), Sepideh Moafi (Dr. Al-Hashimi) and Shawn Hatosy (Dr. Abbot). Watch on Deadline

New additions for Season 3 in recurring roles: Stephen Chang, Barry Clifton, Chris Diamantopoulos, Lawrence Kao, Brent Sexton, JB Tadena, and Hannah Ware.

Regarding the plot for Season 3, Wyle told Deadline, “Season 3 is about doctors benefiting from being patients. We’re going to see that [Robby]’s begun the therapeutic road; it’s had a positive effect. It’s fragile, and it’s not without risk and vulnerability, but at least there’s forward progress.”
