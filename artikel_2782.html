West Lyon stunned by Aplington-Parkersburg in semis

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Cedar Falls, Iowa (KELO) — The West Lyon Wildcats fell to the Aplington-Parkersburg Falcons, 27-22, in the 1A Iowa High School state football semifinals.

The Wildcats had one play to reach the end zone with five seconds left inside the five yard line, and unfortunately for them, that pass was intercepted.

West Lyon ends the season with a 10-2 record.
