Top Performers from Day 3 of Minnesota High School State Football Semifinals

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Minnesota state football semifinals wrapped up Saturday with five games at U.S. Bank Stadium. Springfield and Minneota won in Class A, Pierz and Waseca won in Class 3A and Cretin-Derham Hall won in Class 5A.

Here are the top performers from each game.

Parker Kuehn, Springfield: Kuehn completed 10 of 17 passes for 224 yards and five touchdowns with no interceptions. He also threw a two-point conversion pass and caught a 38-yard pass.

Braxton Brown, Springfield: Brown caught three passes for 83 yards and two touchdowns, including a 61-yarder in the third quarter. He finished with 101 all-purpose yards.

William Schafer, Upsala/Swanville: Schafer ran 31 times for 106 yards and completed 16 of 26 passes for 160 yards. He also ran in a two-point conversion.

RJ Sorensen, Minneota: Sorensen carried 34 times for 211 yards and finished with 219 all-purpose yards.

Riley Kappes, Breckenridge: Riley Kappes ran 11 times for 91 yards, including a 56-yard run, and threw a 6-yard touchdown pass to Joey Lopez.

Brayden Foust, Pierz: Foust scored six touchdowns. He ran nine times for 244 yards and five scores. He finished with 314 all-purpose yards.

Tyler Kimman, Pierz: Kimman caught two passes for 60 yards, including a 51-yard touchdown. On defense, he had nine tackles and a forced fumble.

Elijah Larson, St. Croix Lutheran Academy: Larson ran 24 times for 148 yards and returned a kickoff 92 yards for a touchdown. He finished with 293 all-purpose yards.

Samuel Selthun, Waseca: Selthun ran 23 times for 149 yards, including a 52-yard run. He also caught a 20-yard pass and finished with 177 all-purpose yards.

Jace Baumgartner, Dilworth-Glyndon-Felton: Baumgartner ran 18 times for 125 yards and ran in a two-point conversion.

Dawsen Johnson, Dilworth-Glyndon-Felton: Johnson led all players with 11 tackles and had 1.5 sacks.

Gavin Russell, Cretin-Derham Hall: Russell completed 15 of 24 passes for 236 yards and three touchdowns.

Antonio Barajas, Cretin-Derham Hall: Barajas caught five passes for 150 yards and two touchdowns

Jackson Loreno, Brainerd: Loreno caught seven passes for 74 yards and ran for 39. He also intercepted a pass and returned it 20 yards, finishing with 145 all-purpose yards.
