Arsenal Are In The Running To Land This Gifted Winger: One For The Future?

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

In a recent report, TyC Sports mentioned that Arsenal are in the running to land Benfica winger Gianluca Prestianni. It has been mentioned that the Gunners would have to compete with Newcastle United, Aston Villa, Paris Saint-Germain and Bayern Munich in the race to sign the Argentine wide player next year.

Prestianni has enjoyed a good run of form at the Portuguese club in this campaign so far as he has put in a run of impressive displays for them in the final third. The 20-year-old has scored 12 goals and grabbed six assists in 20 matches for Benfica this season on all fronts.

The Argentine talent is currently among the most gifted young wingers in Liga Portugal. Hence, it is no surprise that the Gunners are hoping to sign him next year.

His current contract at the Portuguese club will run out in the summer of 2029, which could make it tough for Arsenal to land him on a cut-price deal next year.

Prestianni is a good dribbler with the ball at his feet and can make a few driving runs with it out on the flanks. He has the vision to engineer some decent chances for his teammates in the final third and can shoot the ball with power from long range.

The Argentine talent is primarily a right-winger but can also operate as a left-sided wide player or play in the number ten position if required. However, there are some question marks on whether he can cope with the physical side and high intensity of the Premier League if the Gunners manage to get a deal done for him next year.

We can expect Prestianni to bring more quality and depth to Arsenal boss Mikel Arteta's attack in the long run. He is good enough to challenge for a regular first-team spot at the Emirates Stadium in the coming years.

At 20, Prestianni has a bright future ahead of him. He is undoubtedly a prospect to watch out for in the long run, so the North London club would be wise to try everything they can to lure him to the Emirates Stadium in 2027.
