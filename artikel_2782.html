Buccaneers at Lions: Thursday injury report for Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Tampa Bay Buccaneers received some encouraging news Thursday as they continued preparations for their Week 11 matchup against the Detroit Lions.

After missing Wednesday's practice, quarterback Baker Mayfield (foot/right thumb) returned to the practice field Thursday as a limited participant. It's a positive development for the veteran quarterback, whose availability for Sunday has been in question since suffering a foot injury against the Chicago Bears.

Mayfield revealed Thursday that the injury occurred early in the game against Chicago during a scramble that resulted in a completion to running back Kenny Gainwell. He played through the discomfort for the remainder of the contest.

When asked about his readiness to play against Detroit, Mayfield responded, "I feel ready."

While his return to practice is certainly encouraging, the Buccaneers are expected to make a determination on his availability Friday. Rookie quarterback Jalon Daniels remains the next man up should Mayfield be unable to play.

Unfortunately for Tampa Bay, safety Antoine Winfield Jr. (ankle) missed his second consecutive practice, while outside linebacker Yaya Diaby (knee) remained limited.

Wide receiver Chris Godwin Jr. and running back Bucky Irving were added to Thursday's injury report as non-participants, while defensive lineman A'Shawn Robinson was limited. All three received rest designations.

Cornerback Josh Hayes (knee), tight end Cade Otton (thumb) and left tackle Tristan Wirfs (toe) remained full participants.

For Detroit, there were no changes to its injury report from Wednesday. Center Cade Mays (ankle) remained sidelined, while six players, including cornerbacks D.J. Reed (hip) and Ennis Rakestraw Jr. (shoulder), remained limited. Safety Kerby Joseph (knee) was once again a full participant.

Both teams will release their final injury reports Friday, with much of the attention in Tampa Bay continuing to surround Mayfield and whether he will be healthy enough to lead the Buccaneers' offense Sunday in Detroit.
