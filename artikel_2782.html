5 SEC Rivalry Week Storylines That Could Change Everything Before Championship Saturday

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

BIRMINGHAM, Ala. — The Southeastern Conference enters Rivalry Week with plenty of unfinished business.

Georgia has already secured a place in the SEC Championship Game, but the Bulldogs still do not know their opponent.

Meanwhile, Alabama, Texas and Tennessee remain involved in a complicated race for the second berth.

Several programs also have College Football Playoff positioning to protect.

With Thanksgiving weekend approaching, here are five storylines that could define the SEC's final regular-season games.

The Egg Bowl has produced enough unexpected results to remind fans that records mean little when Mississippi State and Ole Miss meet.

This year, the Rebels enter with seven victories, while Mississippi State needs another win to reach bowl eligibility.

However, the rivalry carries implications beyond Mississippi.

The result could influence the SEC Championship Game tiebreaking process through opponents' conference winning percentages.

Consequently, Alabama, Tennessee and Texas fans may have an unexpected reason to follow Friday's noon kickoff.

For Ole Miss, the goal is to finish the regular season with another rivalry victory.

For Mississippi State, the opportunity involves extending its season and damaging its biggest rival's momentum.

Georgia has already clinched its place in Atlanta.

However, Kirby Smart's Bulldogs must first navigate their annual rivalry against Georgia Tech.

The Yellow Jackets enter the matchup following a disappointing loss to Wake Forest, but that result does not eliminate their ability to challenge Georgia.

Furthermore, the Bulldogs have national championship aspirations to protect.

A defeat would not change their SEC Championship Game qualification, but it could complicate their College Football Playoff seeding.

Georgia must remain focused on the opponent directly ahead rather than the championship game awaiting in December.

Alabama enters Rivalry Week with nine victories and an opportunity to strengthen its postseason résumé.

However, Auburn has an opportunity to make the Crimson Tide's final regular-season weekend considerably more difficult.

The Tigers cannot reach the SEC Championship Game themselves.

Nevertheless, they can damage Alabama's conference title hopes and potentially complicate its playoff position.

The Iron Bowl has repeatedly produced extraordinary results, including the Kick Six and Grave Digger.

Another unexpected finish would immediately reshape the national conversation surrounding Alabama.

Tennessee enters Rivalry Week tied with Alabama and Texas in the conference standings.

The Volunteers must defeat Vanderbilt to remain in contention for the SEC Championship Game.

However, Tennessee also needs favorable results elsewhere.

That creates an unusual situation for a program that has remained involved in the conference race throughout the season.

Furthermore, Tennessee has a College Football Playoff résumé to protect.

A loss to Vanderbilt would eliminate its SEC title hopes and create additional postseason uncertainty.

A victory, meanwhile, would keep the Volunteers in the conversation until the remaining conference results become clear. The Vols need Texas to lose.

The conference's biggest unresolved question involves its championship matchup.

Georgia has already clinched one berth, leaving Alabama, Texas and Tennessee competing for the other.

All three enter the final weekend with identical 6-2 conference records.

However, their paths to Atlanta differ because of the SEC's tiebreaking procedures.

Texas holds important advantages in several scenarios.

Meanwhile, Alabama and Tennessee need particular combinations of results to advance.

Additionally, games involving teams outside the championship race could influence the final outcome.

That means the Egg Bowl and other matchups may carry consequences that extend far beyond their participants.

Rivalry Week traditionally delivers emotional finishes and unforgettable moments.

However, this year's SEC schedule also features significant championship implications.

Georgia knows it will compete in Atlanta on Dec. 5.

The rest of the conference must determine which program will join the Bulldogs.

Meanwhile, several teams are fighting to protect their postseason positions or finish the year with victories over their biggest rivals.

By Saturday night, the SEC's championship picture should finally become clear.

Until then, nearly every result could have consequences somewhere else in the conference.
