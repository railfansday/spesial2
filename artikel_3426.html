All 32 Episodes Of HBO’s Crime Masterpiece Deliver A Masterclass In Blending Genres

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

HBO's Barry delivers a masterclass in blending genres, between its hitman premise, comedic tone, and psychological thriller elements. It's also incredibly easy to binge with 32 sharply paced episodes, and is one of HBO's all-time great modern series with 10 Emmy wins and a near-perfect 98% Rotten Tomatoes score. Featuring performances by Bill Hader, Stephen Root, Henry Winkler, Sarah Goldberg, and more, Barry belongs in the same elite category as Veep, Curb Your Enthusiasm, and The Righteous Gemstones as one of the most prolific HBO comedy series ever made.

Few series available to stream on HBO Max blend various distinct genres as masterfully as Barry, which primarily combines dark comedy with crime drama and psychological thriller. Bill Hader stars as the titular protagonist Barry Berkman, a professional hitman who is sent on a contract killer assignment to Los Angeles, only to end up falling in love with the craft of acting. The former Marine-turned-assassin takes an acting class taught by Gene Cousineau (Winkler) and is enamored by the idea of escaping his own identity by assuming those of fictional characters. While Barry continues to work as a hitman, he gets a new lease on life by throwing himself into the world of acting.

Several key supporting characters also challenge the traditional expectations within the crime genre, specifically Anthony Carrigan's NoHo Hank, who is simultaneously a serious but hilarious member of an organized crime operation. Barry and NoHo Hank defy the norms of the standard criminal characters seen in countless crime series, which makes Barry a triumph of genre-blending technique. What's more, the series itself rests on its contrasting tone of crime and comedy, while also diving into Barry's psychological profile as a hitman with a moral compass, which exacerbates his repressed personal feelings of guilt. These elements elevate the series beyond a simple crime comedy.

Barry is a series worth investing in on HBO Max because it truly does get better and more complex with each new season without ever sacrificing the qualities that made its debut season such an instant success. By the time Barry reaches its third season, which earned a perfect 100% Rotten Tomatoes score and is often considered the best of the series, it reaches new heights in terms of both story and character development, especially in how the past comes to affect Barry and his growing ambitions to correct his previous mistakes. The psychological elements become more integral in the narrative, while the series also hits impressive achievements as an action thriller.

All 50 episodes of Apple TV's space sci-fi masterpiece deliver an extraordinary alternate-history adventure that's perfect from start to finish.

Hader also evolved as a visual storyteller and director in Barry's final season, taking complete creative control of the series by directing all eight episodes while also remaining its star. Through Hader's lens, the unconventional crime-comedy hybrid develops into a full-bodied psychological drama that touches on darker and deeper themes than the debut season, including Barry's relationship with violence and the myriad ways in which he has altered his identity, lied to himself, and hurt other people. Because of this, Barry delivers a phenomenal showcase of not only how to pull off a genre-blending series, but how to utilize various genres to enhance the show's original premise.
