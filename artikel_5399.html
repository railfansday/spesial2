RushDATA™ Enables Wet-Lab Validation of AI-Designed Antibodies Generated by Molence Bio's Mol-1

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

CAMBRIDGE, Mass., Nov. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Biointron Biological USA Inc. ("Biointron") is pleased to have supported Molence Bio's Mol-1 project. Mol-1 demonstrates performance comparable to the leading closed-source model IsoDDE in antibody-antigen structure prediction and is capable of designing functional nanobodies from scratch. To experimentally evaluate these AI-designed candidates, Biointron conducted developability assays using RushDATAâ¢, its wet-lab validation service for AI-designed antibodies.

The results: 23 out of 30 AI-designed candidates met at least three of the four developability criteria, demonstrating promising developability profiles.

AI-designed antibodies require experimental validation to demonstrate their potential as viable drug candidates. Biointron ran the developability panel on Molence Bio's VHH-Fc candidates, measuring properties that predict manufacturability and safety, including hydrophobicity, thermal stability, self-interaction, and non-specific binding.

Each assay was benchmarked against Biointron reference antibodies, including infliximab, bevacizumab, bococizumab, gantenerumab and trastuzumab, so the designs could be compared directly with approved and clinical-stage antibodies.

Bridging AI Design and Wet-Lab Validation

Why This Matters for AI-Driven Antibody Discovery

AI can now generate antibody binders at scale. The next challenge is proving which designs work in the lab and behave like drugs. Mol-1 shows what happens when a strong open model meets high-quality experimental data, and Biointron is proud to provide that data.
