Nvidia said its AI data center loan guarantees won't kick in until facilities are built

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The company's maximum gross exposure on guarantees totaled $118 billion as of Oct. 25, and it has made no payments under them

Nvidia $NVDA said on Tuesday that its guarantees for data center buildouts take effect phase by phase, once each related building is completed and ready for service.

The largest guarantee, entered into in August, provides credit support on the land, power and shell buildout for about 4.25 gigawatts across nine buildings at SB Energy's PORTS-Pike Technology Campus in Ohio, the company said. The campus will host Nvidia infrastructure under 20-year leases to OpenAI, subject to limited exceptions.

The first guarantee is expected to take effect in fiscal 2029, Nvidia said. Its exposure builds as each building comes online, up to a maximum of $105 billion, then declines over each 20-year lease term as OpenAI makes lease payments. Nvidia has the option to provide credit support in phases for about 3.8 additional gigawatts as the site scales.

Maximum gross exposure on all of Nvidia's guarantees totaled $118 billion as of Oct. 25. That figure includes $105 billion in SB Energy credit support guarantees, $9.5 billion in power and systems credit support guarantees and $3.5 billion in land, power and shell guarantees for neoclouds. Nvidia said it has made no payments under its guarantees.

Nvidia said separate commitments to neoclouds take effect once the neoclouds secure financing to buy the data center infrastructure and the related compute is deployed, made operational and accepted by Nvidia. If those conditions are not resolved on time, certain commitments could be reduced or eliminated, the company said.

Interim CEO and CFO Colette Kress told analysts on Tuesday's earnings call that a guarantee ends if a customer is delayed or runs into trouble building, according to TechCrunch. "Our guarantee doesn't exist anymore," she said. Asked whether the terms were new, Kress said they simply spelled out conditions that already applied and did not signal a shift in direction.

Kress said Nvidia will treat help with land, power and shell for data centers as a top priority while reviewing which types of companies it will help finance, according to TechCrunch.

Nvidia reported fiscal third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year earlier, on Tuesday. The results were its first since co-founder Jensen Huang resigned as CEO last week after admitting to an affair with a former executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed, a major Nvidia customer. Kress took over as interim CEO while keeping her CFO role, and the board began a search for a permanent chief executive.
