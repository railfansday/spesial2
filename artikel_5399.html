Fluidstack Launches Modular 400 MW Data Center Products Designed to be Built in Six Months

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Fluidstack's proprietary modular build, Blackbird, is made in its more than 1.1 million square foot facility in Glendale, Arizona from globally sourced components, supporting 1,250 permanent manufacturing and skilled trades jobs.

GLENDALE, Ariz., Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Fluidstack, which builds AI data center infrastructure, today announced the launch of its Blackbird 400 MW modular data center product for customers that need rapidly deployed compute infrastructure at their own site. Each Blackbird is designed to be built at a customer site within six months of groundbreaking, against an industry standard of 18 to 24 months. Blackbird modules are made in Fluidstack's Glendale, Arizona manufacturing facility from globally sourced components and are designed to ship to data center campuses as integrated and tested units. Fluidstack expects this to reduce build time by two-thirds or more. In addition to bringing compute capacity online faster, remote fabrication and on-site modular assembly are expected to minimize local disruption from data center construction and bring tax revenue and permanent jobs to host communities 12 to 18 months sooner.

Fluidstack's new plant in Glendale, Arizona, will supply all three products. The facility spans more than 1.1 million square feet and will support 1,250 permanent manufacturing and skilled trades jobs.

"We're bringing industrial capacity back to the United States for the components that foreign supply chains can't deliver fast enough. Fluidstack engineers and designs data centers in the U.S., then assembles them on construction sites across the country, so American companies can build AI infrastructure here at speed," said Gary Wu, CEO of Fluidstack. "That's how we keep American leadership in AI. And we're just getting started."

Fluidstack designs, engineers, integrates, and sells all products in the United States and plans to deliver them finished and tested from a single source.

More information is at https://fluidstack.io/
