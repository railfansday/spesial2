Pokémon Officially Unveils New Eeveelution Design

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Pokémon is celebrating its 30th anniversary throughout the year, and even as 2026 is winding down, we're still seeing the franchise debut new releases and show off some fun surprises. One way the franchise has done this is through new designs of popular Pokémon like Espeon and Charizard, as well as through games like FireRed and LeafGreen coming to Nintendo's eShop for Switch and Switch 2. From the 30th Celebration collection in the Pokémon TCG to the new McDonald's Happy Meals, it's a great time to be a fan. And if you love Umbreon, then this latest design could be just what you're looking for.

Debuting in Pokémon Gold and Silver, Umbreon quickly rose to popularity as one of the most beloved Pokémon from Gen 2. In fact, Umbreon and its counterpart, Espeon, are some of the most popular Pokémon of all time, regularly featured in the games, anime, and on official merchandise. Even recently, Umbreon was a part of Halloween products thanks to various items like porcelain jack-o-lanterns and yard statues. In other words, new designs for Umbreon aren't all that surprising, but they're always nice to see.

The "Umbreon Together for the Holidays Ornament" is back in stock at Pokémon Center, offering fans a new design of a fan-favorite Pokémon from the Johto region. With a price tag of $16.99, it's also one of the most reasonably priced items that is part of the holiday collection from the official Pokémon Center storefront. Orders over $20 get free shipping, so if you spend a bit more, you won't have to worry about paying any shipping fees.

This is a resin ornament of Umbreon wearing a sprig of holly while playing with a toy train. The figure comes with a festive green ribbon, so you can hang it from a Christmas tree or simply use its flat base to display it on a shelf or windowsill. This adorable Umbreon ornament is a part of the Together for the Holidays collection, which is exclusive to Pokémon Center. It measures 3.2 x 2.9 x 3.2 in, putting it right on par with most other holiday ornaments from Hallmark and other popular companies. You can check out an image of the new Umbreon design below.

Umbreon is one of the most popular Pokémon of all time, even winning a fan vote for most popular Gen 2 Pokémon, so it's never surprising to see this Eeveelution being featured on official merchandise and apparel. In 2026 alone, Umbreon has been featured on shirts, cards, skateboards, and playmats for the TCG. Plushes and holiday items like this ornament can also make nice gifts for Umbreon fans at this time of year.

And Umbreon isn't the only Eevee evolution getting some attention this holiday season. Both Espeon and Glaceon are also featured on multiple items from Pokémon Center's collection. If you prefer the base Pokémon, then there's a lot of Eevee merch to choose from as well. We've gotten some cool new looks for Umbreon over the years, but the "Umbreon Together for the Holidays Ornament" is certainly a great one for Pokémon fans this Christmas season.
