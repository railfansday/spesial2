Marvel’s Biggest Director Is Officially Ready to Adapt ‘House of M’ After ‘Avengers: Doomsday’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Marvel has had another successful year in 2026, mostly with TV releases like the second seasons of Daredevil: Born Again and X-Men '97 and the debut season of Wonder Man. Although Wonder Man was canceled after only one season, both Daredevil: Born Again and X-Men '97 will be back for new seasons in 2027. Marvel also found success on the big screen with the release of Spider-Man: Brand New Day, the blockbuster starring Tom Holland that grossed over $2.5 billion at the global box office to become one of the highest-grossing movies in history. However, despite the success of Spider-Man 4, Marvel has another movie coming in just a few weeks that could surpass Holland's fourth Spider-Man man at the box office and become another Marvel phenomenon to reach $2 billion globally.

We're talking, of course, about Avengers: Doomsday, which stars Robert Downey Jr. as the titular villain, Doctor Doom, with tenured MCU veterans like Chris Evans and Chris Hemsworth returning to their roles as Steve Rogers and Thor, respectively. The film is being directed by Joe and Anthony Russo, who have also worked on other MCU projects in the past, such as Captain America: The Winter Soldier, Captain America: Civil War, Avengers: Infinity War, and Avengers: Endgame. The duo is also directing Avengers: Secret Wars, which is currently scheduled to be released on December 17, 2027, the same day as The Lord of the Rings: The Hunt for Gollum, but their Marvel future beyond Secret Wars is unclear. During a recent interview with SFX Magazine to promote the upcoming release of Avengers: Doomsday, co-director Joe Russo was asked if he would be interested in adapting House of M, the X-Men/Avengers crossover comic, in the future. His answer might surprise you:

"I mean, they dabbled with it [in Avengers: Doomsday], but there's certainly a more robust House of M interpretation that could be done. There's quite a number of really great stories from over the years. But, I'd say that, especially with the X-Men emerging over the next year or two, that's certainly a story that could be built towards, or elements of that story."

House of M was written by Brian Michael Bendis, and it's set in a new reality created by the Scarlet Witch where mutants are the alpha species and Magneto is their leader. Marvel has more of a leg to build towards this, especially following the recent revelation that Elizabeth Olsen's Scarlet Witch is confirmed to reprise her role in Avengers: Doomsday, according to the VisionQuest post-credits scene. Ian McKellen's Magneto from the Fox X-Men movies is also confirmed to star in Doomsday, and with an X-Men movie already launching in 2028, Marvel could be laying the groundwork for a House of M adaptation sooner than we realize.
