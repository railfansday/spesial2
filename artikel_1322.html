Harry Kane Names the Major Trophy He'd Happily 'Trade' His Ballon d'Or For

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

It's been a fantastic few seasons for Harry Kane, culminating in his 2026 Ballon d'Or win.

The English striker scored goal after goal for Tottenham Hotspur, but never managed to get his hands on a major trophy with Spurs. In the pursuit of glory, he made the move to Bayern Munich in 2023, and that has proved to be a sensible transfer.

The 33-year-old, one of the oldest Ballon d'Or winners, has now won two Bundesliga titles and the DFB-Pokal, while continuing to score at an electrifying rate. Indeed, Kane scored 73 goals for club and country in 2025/26, which helped him win the 2026 Ballon d'Or.

Lamine Yamal may have won, but some former Ballon d'Or winners didn't even include him in their top three.

Having been named the world's best player, the Englishman could not hide his delight, saying he was 'speechless' as he claimed the award in London. However, in the short time since that historic night, Kane has revealed that he would swap the Ballon d'Or for one major honour.

Speaking to reporters via Goal, he claimed that he'd happily 'trade' his prize if it meant winning the Champions League with Bayern. Kane explained:

"Team trophies are why we play football. The team aspect of our sport is really, really important. The Ballon d'Or last week was one of the best feelings I've had in my life and my career... but to have moments with the team, celebrations with the club, to be able to lift the Champions League with Bayern would be an incredible feeling.

"Of course the Ballon d'Or is a highlight, it puts me on an even bigger level, but you want to have these big finals, the Champions League, having that feeling of emotion with your teammates as well. I would choose the Champions League, but I have the opportunity to do both, let's see what happens."

That's not to say Kane isn't appreciating the value of his award, as he went on to add: "Winning the Ballon d'Or was a huge achievement for me. I've had to overcome many challenges in my career, some people didn't think I'd get this far..."

Kane most notably made it all the way to the Champions League final with Spurs in 2018/19, but was unable to get across the line, as his team lost 2-0 to Liverpool. At the time, he dejectedly said:

“It was a tough one to take. It’s probably one of the biggest games we’ll ever play in our careers and to let it slide by is always a tough one to take. It’s an experience we need to learn from."

At Bayern, Kane then made it to the semis twice, and the quarters once, but still hasn't been able to make another final. He's also suffered more disappointment in cup competitions with England.

Once again, his goalscoring record at that level has never been questioned – he is the Three Lions' all-time top scorer after all – but he's lost two finals with his country, suffering defeat at both Euro 2020 and Euro 2024. He also made it to the semis with England this summer at the 2026 World Cup, but once again fell short when it came to pushing for glory.

With all that disappointment in mind, it's little wonder Kane would happily give up his Ballon d'Or if it meant finally getting his hands on a major team honour such as the Champions League.
