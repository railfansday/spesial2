'I'd be surprised if it doesn't happen' - Arsenal tipped to present 'big figure' for Antonio Nusa in January

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Arsenal will be able to sign Antonio Nusa from RB Leipzig in January if they offer a fee attractive enough.

That is according to experienced former Tottenham, Aston Villa and Everton scout and expert on Norwegian football, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 21-year-old is being chased by several European giants after a fantastic start to the campaign that has seen him score six goals and provide eight assists across all competitions.

Leipzig hope to resist any offers and keep Nusa in January, with the player currently under contract until the summer of 2029.

The German club will then hope to spark a bidding war in the summer transfer window as they look to extract the maximum value from the Norway international.

But Arsenal are in need of a left-sided winger immediately, and may well think to jump the queue in January.

Nusa's career began in Norway before he moved to Belgium with Club Brugge in 2021 and began to catch the eye.

There, he almost signed for Tottenham and Brentford before eventually joining Leipzig, with this season marking his real breakthrough.

Nusa now looks set to join the Premier League, following the same path as his international teammates, Martin Odegaard and Erling Haaland.

"His next move will be big and it'll be Premier League. He's practically followed in Haaland's footsteps really, coming from Norway," King told Football Insider.

"Now he's in Germany, [next will be the] Premier League. I'd be very surprised if something does not happen even in the January window.

"How many times have we heard that, 'they're not going to let him go, they're not going to do this.'  Look at Newcastle and [Alexander] Isak. That was a prolonged thing, and they weren't going to sell [Bruno] Guimaraes.

"Arsenal come in with a big, big figure. Money's the talking point here. It's the time to sell and the way the boys playing, you're going to get £80million for him."

Despite the mounting speculation surrounding Nusa, he has continued to keep his focus on the present.

"I see everything, then! Whatever comes. I'm not blind, after all, so I get it. That's why I try to take a step back," the winger said last week.

"I have good people around me. It's not a stress. I just try to enjoy the everyday life I have in Leipzig. It's a fantastic team with a fantastic coach.

"Next to it, I have an agent who works with all other parts, and just lets me focus on the football. That's the most important thing for me."
