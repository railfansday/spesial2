Jones, Russell, Tuipulotu return in Scotland's quest for first win over All Blacks

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Scotland face New Zealand on Saturday at Murrayfield (2.10pm kick-off), seeking a first ever win over the All Blacks; Centres Huw Jones and Sione Tuipulotu, and fly-half Finn Russell return to start; Scotland also face Australia (Nov 15) and Japan (Nov 21) this month

Gregor Townsend has welcomed back Huw Jones, Finn Russell and Sione Tuipulotu as Scotland seek a first-ever victory over New Zealand in Saturday's Nations Championship.

Scotland's winless record against the All Blacks stands at 33 Tests, but a strong selection gives them hope ahead of the weekend's contest at Murrayfield (2.10pm kick-off).

The backline sees a Jones-Tuipulotu British and Irish Lions centre partnership restored, with Russell guiding the side from fly-half. The latter is partnered by another Lion in scrum-half Ben White.

Kyle Steyn and Jamie Dobie start on the wings, with Kyle Rowe at full-back, leaving Blair Kinghorn on the bench as a replacement.

In the forwards, Rory Sutherland starts ahead of Pierre Schoeman at loosehead prop, with hooker Ewan Ashman and tighthead Zander Fagerson completing the front-row.

Gregor Brown and Scott Cummings start in the second-row, while the back-row is made up of Matt Fagerson at blindside flanker, Rory Darge at openside flanker and Jack Dempsey at No 8.
