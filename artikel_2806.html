ACC Football Week 11 Predictions: Picks for Every Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The ACC has one of the more interesting slates of games going into Week 11. Miami is about to get a massive test from Duke. Wake Forest and SMU will play one of the most important games of the ACC season, and teams like Virginia, Louisville, and Virginia Tech are trying to make a good season even stronger. Below are our quick previews and predictions for every ACC game.

The focus will be on the Duke showdown at Miami, but Wake Forest at SMU could be the bigger moment for the ACC's playoff pecking order.

Virginia Tech is bowl eligible, and now it's looking to crank up the offense against Stanford.

Boston College has a tough task against Notre Dame, but it has a chance to make a program-changing statement.
