Half a million! Bayern reach historic membership milestone among other records

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

FC Bayern Munich have announced that they now have 500,000 registered members, making them the world's largest sports club by registered membership. Their members come from 152 different countries.

The announcement was one of several highlights at Saturday's Annual General Meeting, where the club also revealed record financial results for the 2025/26 financial year.

Bayern generated a turnover of €988.2 million, an increase of approximately €10 million compared to the previous year. Operating profit rose to €190.8 million, while the club recorded a consolidated net profit of €22.8 million.

CEO Jan-Christian Dreesen described the figures as another record year, emphasising the importance of financial discipline and sustainable investment.

He also addressed ticket prices, insisting that football must remain affordable for supporters despite the growing commercial demands of the sport. A regular Bundesliga ticket at Bayern currently costs around €80, a price the club has maintained for several years.

Meanwhile, president Herbert Hainer praised the club's growing international appeal and highlighted the importance of its partnership with German family-owned business Viessmann.

There was also a special message from head coach Vincent Kompany, who thanked the supporters for their backing and described how his family had quickly come to feel at home in Munich.
