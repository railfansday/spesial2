FedEx Cup Fall bubble watch: Who's projected in, out for 2027 tour card with only 1 event to go

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Chad Ramey won the ultimate prize at the Austin Championship on Monday but Trace Crowe went home with the ultimate consolation prize – he locked up his PGA Tour card for 2027 with a third-place finish.

The 54-hole leader was seeking his maiden Tour title and while that day will have to wait for now, he still shot 3-under 69 at Barton Creek Resort and Spa's Fazio Canyon Course on Monday. After starting the FedEx Cup Fall at No. 153 in the season-long standings, he arrived at the 18th hole projected at No. 99 in the standings but reached the par-5 18th in two shots and made birdie to finish alone in third.

That lifted him to 87th in the FedEx Cup Fall standings and secure his finish in the top 100 with just one event remaining this season.

South Africa's Christiaan Bezuidenhout, who played on the International Team at the Presidents Cup in late September, heads to the Fall finale in Georgia as the Bubble Boy.

Let's take a look at the FedEx Cup Fall bubble heading to the RSM Classic, which begins on Thursday.
