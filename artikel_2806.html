Juve, Ait-Nouri says yes! Milan-Demirovic, Thuram renewal: morning news☀

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The January transfer market is heating up, and Serie A's biggest clubs are planning their next moves. Ait-Nouri is open to Juventus, Milan are considering Demirovic to strengthen their attack, and Inter are working on Thuram's contract extension. Meanwhile, Napoli are exploring several alternatives to Varela, Roma are looking to Brazil to strengthen their left flank, and Lazio are considering Gatti. Keep an eye on Fiorentina too, where Vanoli is preparing a defensive overhaul.

Here are the main stories on the morning of Friday, November 20.

Juventus are stepping up their pursuit of Rayan Ait-Nouri. According to La Gazzetta dello Sport, the Algerian full-back has reportedly indicated that he would be open to moving to Turin as early as January, after an initial deal fell through over the summer.

The 2001-born player has seen very little action at Enzo Maresca's Manchester City: no minutes in the Premier League and just 168 in total across the Champions League and League Cup. That situation could pave the way for a loan move, Juventus's preferred option given the financial restrictions imposed by their agreement with UEFA. The decision now rests with City, who intend to consider offers for a permanent transfer. Tottenham's Udogie and Barcelona's Balde remain alternatives.

Milan are looking for a striker in January and are exploring a new option. According to Tuttosport, Stuttgart's Ermedin Demirovic has joined the Rossoneri's shortlist of potential signings, amid difficulties in negotiations for Nicolò Tresoldi and Endrick.

The Bosnian, who has already scored nine goals this season across the Bundesliga and Champions League, is particularly admired by Markus Krösche and would represent a cheaper alternative to their other targets. His versatility would also allow Ruben Amorim to play him either alongside Gonçalo Ramos or in a front three. Milan have already sent scouts to watch him closely, while continuing to assess the future of Francesco Camarda, who could leave on loan in search of regular playing time.

Inter are preparing to extend Marcus Thuram's contract, but a financial agreement has yet to be reached. According to La Gazzetta dello Sport, the French striker wants a raise on his current net salary of six million a season, while the club are aiming to keep fixed costs under control.

His contract expires in 2028, and Inter's management are reportedly considering an extension with a substantial variable component linked to individual and team performance. Both sides want to continue together, but further meetings will be needed to reach an agreement. A summer sale also remains a possibility if no deal is reached. Meanwhile, Inter are working on new contracts for Carlos Augusto, Dimarco and Calhanoglu, with the Brazilian increasingly close to signing until 2031.

Napoli are preparing for a particularly busy January transfer window, especially in attack. According to Corriere dello Sport, Monza's Gustavo Varela remains one of Giovanni Manna's main targets, but his 30–35 million valuation and strong international competition are complicating the deal.

Juventus and Liverpool are also interested in the Portuguese player, as are Benfica, who retain certain rights over him. Napoli are therefore assessing alternatives, particularly in the Premier League. Joshua Zirkzee could become available on loan from Manchester United, while Jamie Bynoe-Gittens, Patrick Dorgu, Geovany Quenda and Mason Mount are also on the list. Much will depend on departures: Lang, Lucca and Neres could make way for new arrivals.

Roma are looking to Brazil to strengthen their wide options. According to Corriere dello Sport, sporting director Tony D'Amico is monitoring Kauã Pascini, Atletico Mineiro's 2008-born left-sided player, who is regarded as a highly promising talent.

Gasperini currently has several right-sided players who can fill in on the opposite flank, but lacks a natural left-footer capable of reaching the byline and providing better crosses. Pascini would fit that profile: he has already made 18 first-team appearances, scoring in the Copa do Brasil, and is part of Brazil's Under-20 setup. His contract until 2030 and competition from European clubs are the main obstacles, but Roma are considering an investment to get ahead of their rivals.

Lazio are looking for a centre-back following Josip Sutalo's injury and have set their sights on Federico Gatti. According to Tuttosport, the club have reportedly made initial enquiries about the Juventus player, who has fallen down Luciano Spalletti's pecking order.

The 1998-born defender has played just 183 league minutes and could consider a move to secure regular playing time. Lazio, who will be without Sutalo until early February, would represent an appealing option. Juventus, however, value Gatti at around €20 million and would be unlikely to accept a loan with an option to buy from a direct rival for Champions League qualification. Napoli, Porto, Galatasaray and several Premier League clubs also remain interested in the defender.

Fiorentina are seeking a defensive turnaround after an extremely difficult start to the season. According to La Repubblica, Paolo Vanoli is reportedly preparing changes for the away game against Monza, aiming to cut down on goals conceded: already 25 in the first eleven rounds.

The coach is banking on the return of Luca Ranieri, who could partner Radu Dragusin at centre-back. Jimenez remains a reliable option on the right, while De Gea needs to rediscover his consistency after several mistakes. The situation could prompt further moves in the transfer market: Fabio Paratici is considering signing a left-back, with Milan's Estupinian among the players proposed, while the arrival of a centre-back has not been ruled out either. Much will depend on the defence's performances and Pongracic's future.
