Mr. Rocket finally hits his target to ice a much-needed win

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Mr. Rocket finally hits his target to ice a much-needed win originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

Joe Milton missed a bunch of throws on Sunday. It was far from a perfect game against the Steelers in his second-career NFL start.

But with the game on the line, Milton came through in the Eagles' 19-16 win.

With just 1:09 remaining, the Eagles were facing a 3rd-and-6 from their own 39-yard line. They could have run the ball again, forced the Steelers to use their final timeout, and punted the ball away. Instead, Nick Sirianni got aggressive and put the ball in Milton's hands.

Milton said it meant a lot that the coaching staff trusted him in that situation. He repaid that trust when he hit DeVonta Smith with a perfect pass for a seven-yard gain to ice a win that snapped a seven-game losing streak.

"I told [Smith] in the huddle that I'm going to come back (to him)," Milton said. "Just keep running your routes hard. Those small things that he's coming back this week, just small things that we have to clean up and get better at and get more dialed in. That just takes more practice and reps, just completing the ball to him and getting him a friendly football."

Before that completion, Milton had completed just 10 of 20 passes in the game as the Eagles' offense completely dried up in the second half. To make matters worse, Milton missed several throws that he really needs to make.

But there's something special about a guy who can keep his confidence after mistakes and can make a play like that after a less-than-stellar game. It's something Milton has struggled to do at times.

"Every play you go through out there, that's the only play that matters," Milton said. "Once that play is over with, you have to know how to flush it. Like I talked about previously, I've been through a lot of adversity. I've been in a lot of situations where you put me in that situation six years ago, and I probably would've still been thinking about the [previous] play and not being able to flush it. Being in the moment now, I'm being where my feet are, I'm able to flush the play and move on with the next one."

Milton is known for his strong right arm — his nickname is Mr. Rocket — and we saw that again on Sunday. Sometimes that was good, sometimes that was bad.

In the first half, Milton was able to scramble to his left before throwing a cross-body pass on a rope to Darius Cooper for a 29-yard gain on 3rd-and-13. But there were other plays where Milton couldn't harness his velocity.

On one play, he nearly threw a ball through Makai Lemon. And on the Eagles' first offensive play of the second half, he overthrew DeVonta Smith on what would have been a 68-yard touchdown.

"On throws like that, I usually say my daughter's name because it helps me relax," Milton said. "I didn't at that time, obviously. … I saw it, and I just got real excited because before that play I just told him we're gonna go home. It's time to go home. It's time to go score. When I've got the look that I wanted, I just got overly excited. But yeah, you just got to hit those, and especially in those moments."

Milton says he really does sometimes say his daughter's name — Maleia was born on Oct. 9, 2025 — before unleashing a throw. It helps him relax.

"Basically it kind of tells [me] in my mind that I'm throwing the ball to her, so it can't be as hard," Milton said. "Show a little love."

The Eagles are now 3-7 as they head to Dallas to face the Cowboys on Thanksgiving. The Eagles signed Milton off the Cowboys' practice squad on Oct. 16. A little over a month later, he's going to start against them.

The Cowboys scored 51 points on Sunday, so if the Eagles are going to win, they're probably going to need Milton to show off that arm again in a few days.

And if you needed any reminder that Milton has a strong arm, he was wearing a rocket pendant around his neck after his first NFL win as a starter. He bought that chain for himself when he went to the Combine a couple years ago.

"Yeah, that's me, Mr. Rocket," Milton said. "It's on my wall in my game room. That's me, that's my name. I love it. I'm blessed to be able to throw the ball far, and however strong my arm is. Yeah, Mr. Rocket, I love it. It's me."
