The revamped Grand Slam of Darts begins this month, with 48 players competing for one of the sport's most coveted titles.

The event has some key changes, so it's important to know the details before watching the action unfold.

The Grand Slam of Darts is a 48-player tournament (16 more players than 2025), with various qualification routes to get there.

The seeded players consist of the top-16 players, who are each assigned to a separate group, and then have two additional players assigned to their group in the event's draw (one player from pool #2 and #3, with #2 having higher-ranked qualifiers than #3).

The first four days of the tournament (Nov. 14 - 17) will be purely group stage games, as each player will play the other two players in their group (round-robin format). These games will be played to best-of-9 legs.

The winner of each group will advance to the last 16. If there were to be a three-player tie, it would be determined by leg difference and then legs won, legs won against throw, tournament average and lastly a nine-dart shootout.

The knockout stage (last 16) will begin on Nov. 18. The first round of the knockout stage is played to best-of-19 legs.

The final three rounds (quarterfinals, semifinals & final) of the tournament will take place from Nov. 18 - 22 and will all be played to best-of-31 legs.
