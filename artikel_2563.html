Racing Louisville FC 'actively working' to sell club to new ownership

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Racing Louisville FC is "actively working through a process to sell Racing to a new ownership group," the team announced on social media Tuesday, Nov. 17.

"As the NWSL has grown, so have the financial demands," the club said in a statement. "Additional capital is needed to operate the club moving forward, and we are in an active process to sell Racing to a new ownership group."

The statement did not identify a prospective buyer or clarify whether the team would remain in Louisville under new ownership.

Talks of Racing being possibly being sold initially ramped up in the spring. In May, ownership group Soccer Holdings confirmed to The Courier Journal that it was exploring a partial sale in hopes of raising interest and investment.

In August, NWSL commissioner Jessica Berman acknowledged the ownership was prepared to sell, and in September, reports surfaced that Major League Soccer's Cincinnati FC was a possible franchise looking to purchase Racing.

The club began play in 2021 and made its first-ever playoff appearance in 2025. But Racing finished 7-20-3 this season, 15th in the 16-team NWSL, and averaged approximately 5,500 fans per game, a number lagging behind the rest of the circuit.
