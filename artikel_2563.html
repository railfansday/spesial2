Packers’ Micah Parsons unhappy after brutal no-show against Rams’ run game

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Packers' Micah Parsons unhappy after brutal no-show against Rams' run game appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Green Bay Packers took on the Los Angeles Rams to kick off Week 12 on Wednesday night. It was a matchup between two NFC playoff hopefuls on the night before Thanksgiving. For Micah Parsons and his teammates, it wasn't a celebratory night.

The Packers lost to the Rams on Wednesday night in Los Angeles. Quarterback Jordan Love threw two interceptions in this game. Meanwhile, Los Angeles ran the ball all over the field. Parsons shouldered the blame for this loss on Wednesday night.

"If someone can line up 50 times and run it down your mouth and you don't have an answer for it, that's accountability," he said, via ESPN's Rob Demovsky.

As he alluded to, Green Bay had no answer for the Rams run game. Los Angeles handed it off 47 times against the Packers on Wednesday. As a team, they ran for 241 yards.

Blake Corum was the star of the show, though. The former Michigan star carried the ball 32 times, racking up 184 yards and a touchdown. He added two catches and 21 yards, giving him more than 200 yards from scrimmage.

The Packers offense could not solve the Rams defense, either. They gained just 244 yards of offense on Wednesday. They were especially brutal on third down. Green Bay converted two of its eight third-down attempts.

Green Bay has fallen to 6-5 on the 2026 campaign. This is going to set them back a bit in the NFC North standings. They are last place in the division, and are hoping to avoid finishing there at the end of the year. Green Bay will attempt to bounce back on December 6th when they take on the New Orleans Saints in Week 13.
