Roseanne's Michael Fishman Reacts to Roseanne Barr's Criticism

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

A Lighthouse in the Dark and Fish's Call Sheet podcast host Michael Fishman revealed where he stands with Roseanne costar Roseanne Barr after she criticized his comments on the reboot's cancellation.

Michael Fishman will always have a seat at the kitchen table for Roseanne Barr.

The Roseanne alum reflected on his relationship with his TV mom after the two first starred together on the series from 1988 to 1997 and again on the 2018 reboot (which later became The Conners following Barr’s exit from the series).

“I loved Roseanne from the first moment I met her at the audition, and she and I bonded,” Fishman told E! News in an exclusive interview. “And that just grew over the course of the show.”

“I'm always going to love her,” he added, “I’m always going to care about her.”

Still, that’s not to say the current status of their relationship is without its difficulties, especially after Barr recently called out her former TV son for saying that the 2018 reboot of Roseanne had a good reason for getting cancelled following offensive social media posts from Barr.

“Look, what I'm going through in a public sense is something a lot of people are going through in the private sense, which is we all love our family members,” Fishman explained. “It does not mean we agree all the time. And the truth about that is just because you don't agree, it doesn't mean you stop loving each other.”

However, the 45-year-old noted that in those situations it’s important to “set your own boundaries” and to “have clear delineation about what you believe and what you stand for.”

“The public comments are what they are,” he continued. “The reality is I've never said anything negative publicly about her. I don't have a reason to. I'm always going to care about her.”

And regardless of how his relationship with Barr unfolds over the years, Fishman expressed that his “love for her has never wavered.”

“I’ve reached out to give her support and care about her,” he shared. “That doesn't mean we agree. And we have to be very clear where we stand and responsible for how we stand. She always knows how to reach me. There's never a time I'm going to refuse communication.”

Part of Fishman’s ability to care for Barr comes from an understanding of the human experience, a topic he touches on with his podcast A Lighthouse in the Dark.

“The world feels dark for a lot of people right now,” the podcaster admitted. “So it's an opportunity to talk with people who are doing something beautiful in the world and have really deep conversations, because I feel like we've lost an element of our ability to even disagree and to have deep conversations and connect.”

And after spending over three decades in Hollywood, Fishman is also taking a moment to put the spotlight on some lesser known figures with his podcast Fish's Call Sheet.

“Fish's Call Sheet really is about celebrating the people who make production possible and telling their stories,” he told E! News, “because rarely do we tell the stories of the people who are behind the camera.”
