Trump Hits New Low With Bizarre Nvidia CEO Meme

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The president shared an image featuring the philandering businessman in Trump’s own pose after the attempted assassination in Butler, Pennsylvania.

The news that Jensen Huang has been reinstated as the CEO of the world’s fourth most valuable company has been met with both applause from investors and derision and confusion from those who thought the leather-jacket-clad adulterer was being dragged to the trash bin. His return to Nvidia was facilitated by Vice President JD Vance and celebrated by the White House’s social media influencer-in-chief, President Donald J. Trump, with a post on Truth Social, which is hardly truthful, quite anti-social, and rife with the AI slop that has come to characterize the current lame-duck à l'orange administration.

As news of the Huang turn by the Nvidia board caused gasps the world over, including among Congressional Republicans who decried Trump’s involvement in the decision to bring the philanderer back into the fold, Trump shared a meme watermarked by a well-known right-wing meme brand, @GRANDOLDMEMES. In it, the businessman mimics Trump’s own pose after the attempted assassination in Butler, Pennsylvania, as captured by AP photojournalist Evan Vucci, but in Huang’s hand is a poorly AI-generated — or is it photoshopped? — brassiere as stiff as it is bright. It seems to say: Sure, the man is a lech, but he has dodged a bullet, even if blood is smeared across his face. The American flag is replaced with the corporate logo, and hell, considering the way the government is being run, a corporate takeover of this nation doesn’t seem all that outlandish if you think about it.

But what does the red mean? In Nathaniel Hawthorne's 19th-century novel, the titular scarlet letter marks an adulterer's public shame, but here the echo of Trump's 2024 comeback points to redemption. There’s another reading, because it was reported that the billionaire, now apparently worth $144.1 billion since the stock jumped 31% at the news of his reappointment, slept not only with a subordinate but also with a woman who is allegedly a Chinese spy, though Attorney General Todd Blanche has hedged on that claim. Is it a wink to the red of the Chinese flag?

We have come to expect so little from Trump and his edgelord memesters, but this is just truly and utterly bizarre. Perhaps the man at the helm of the world’s most powerful country thought it was funny that Huang is being ushered away by a blonde Secret Service agent who hugs his torso, or he sees himself in the once disgraced and now restored businessman — very likely.

The phrase “let your freak flag fly” has now lost all meaning when billionaires brazenly act like they’re above the law and a call to the president can make all the bad things go away. The man who makes the chips to churn out slop is now rendered in a mock-heroic pose. Once we immortalized the powerful in paint; nowadays we humor them with pixels.
