Margot Robbie & Kristoffer Borgli Team On ‘The Week Of Pain’ From A24 And Square Peg

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

EXCLUSIVE: Even with production on her Ocean’s prequel winding down, Margot Robbie isn’t looking to slow down anytime soon as sources tell Deadline she is set to star in the A24 and Square Peg pic The Week of Pain, with Kristoffer Borgli on board to direct.

A24 will finance and produce the pic with Lars Knudsen and Ari Aster’s Square Peg producing along with Dilemma Films and LuckyChap. Borgli also penned the script.

As with any Borgli and Square Peg project, plot details are being kept under wraps. The project reunites Borgli with the studio and Square Peg following the success of The Drama, which A24 released in April. Starring Zendaya and Robert Pattinson, it became one of the studio’s biggest films of 2024 with $134 million at he worldwide box office. He had also directed 2023’s Dream Scenario for the studio.

Robbie has been a fan of Borgli’s work for some time. She began discussing potentially starring in his next project awhile ago and recently committed to shoot the film sometime in 2027. Watch on Deadline

Robbie is repped by CAA, Entertainment 360 and Aran Michael Management and attorney Jeff Bernstein. Borgli is repped by UTA.
