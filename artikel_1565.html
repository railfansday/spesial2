Player Ratings: AC Milan 5-1 Frosinone – Rabiot unstoppable; super attack

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

AC Milan clinched a comfortable win against Frosinone and individual brilliance certainly had a hand in that. Below are the player ratings for the Rossoneri.

Ahead of what will be a busy month, Milan knew that a win tonight would do them a lot of good, so that’s exactly what they managed to achieve. Goals from Goncalo Ramos, Ardon Jashari, Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic set them up nicely, as well as an early own-goal.

If you haven’t already, make sure to check out how we decide the player ratings. From the scale to what affects the rating, we have compiled everything there.

🥇 Rabiot (8 – MOTM): He just can’t help himself from getting goal contributions! Two more assists today, three if you count the own-goal, and his running is becoming a proper weapon for Milan. He’s just unstoppable when he gets going.

👔 Amorim (7.5): I think this was more or less a home run tactically. Frosinone were always going to try to have a high line, so the head coach exploited that with the help of Rabiot and the passing abilities of his teammates. Benching Cisse was bold, but it turned out to be just the right move.

Maignan (6): I’m not sure he could have done better on the goal, as it was really hard to predict where the cross would end up. Anyway, he made a couple of decent stops and was good with his feet, even if there was a scare in the first half.

Gila (6.5): Let me just say straight off the bat that he wasn’t far away from a 7. However, I think he could have done better to stop the cross on the goal conceded, and he had a few really poor passes as well. On the plus side, he was decent in possession overall and had a key clearance in the second half.

De Winter (6.5): He continues to marshal the defence well, though he lacked concentration on a few occasions today. It didn’t prove to be costly and similarly to Gila, there was definitely more good than harm, but the rating is affected a tad.

Diawara (6.5): I don’t think he could have got to the ball quicker on the goal, not to mention that it’s hard to anticipate such a deflection. As far as I’m concerned, it was solid defending from him, including some nice clearances to stop a few dangerous crosses.

Bartesaghi (6): Defensively he did okay, which was all that was required of him, but the main contributions were in attack. I think we saw some really good runs forward from him, although the crosses had mixed results. It seems fair with a 6 for him.

Moreira (7.5): His run before the second goal was crucial, of course, and he also managed to get an assist (good pass in the end, as VAR confirmed). For a higher rating, I would have liked to see more sharpness in the final third, as he had a couple of very good shooting opportunities that he missed.

Modric (8): My word, the pass before the first goal was just magnificent! That alone shows just how much he means to this team, but he also continued to do well with his passing and movement after that. For me, the rating does deserve to be that high, but maybe I’m blinded by the aforementioned pass.

Musah (7): He did really well to unleash Moreira just before the second goal, playing it perfectly. On the goal conceded, it was just unlucky in my opinion, he did everything right by getting a toe on it. The block at the start of the second half was just brilliant too, showcasing good defensive skills.

Pulisic (7.5): Amorim always says he lets the pitch do the talking and that was the case today. He wasn’t flashy by any means, but did very good work in silence (such as the movement before the own-goal) and was eventually rewarded with the goal. Stunning finish by the way. An 8 perhaps?

Rabiot (8 – MOTM): He just can’t help himself from getting goal contributions! Two more assists today, three if you count the own-goal, and his running is becoming a proper weapon for Milan. He’s just unstoppable when he gets going.

Ramos (7.5): Finally, back on target! Overall, it was a very encouraging one for him as the goal was the result of great positioning and hard work, which helped on the other goals too (opening up space). He could have had a second too if he hadn’t been stopped by the referee.

Jashari (7): That’s one way to make an impact! The finish was fantastic and really surprised the goalkeeper. He had a few more good runs after that, so this was definitely a performance that he can build on.

Cisse (7): It was an easy finish on the goal, it has to be said, but he still timed the run very well. He also showed off some good understanding in the centre-forward, which was nice to see. It wasn’t nice to see the unnecessary yellow card at the end, though.

Pavlovic (N/A): I didn’t see enough to give him a rating.

Estupinan (N/A): I didn’t see enough to give him a rating.

Hutchinson (N/A): I didn’t see enough to give him a rating.
