What should you watch this weekend? (Oct 30 – Nov 1, 2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

A mystery lover’s guide to what’s new to streaming this weekend.

Dexter: Resurrection, season 2 (Paramount)

If you haven’t been keeping up on the expanded Dexter universe, this is the spinoff / continuation series that’s actually really good! (For a full guide to the spinoff universe, Olivia Rutigliano has you covered here.)  Resurrection has a knack for using guest stars extremely effectively (like the original series did, season to season) and this time around, it’s Brian Cox bringing a jolt of electricity to the story of Dexter’s days in New York City.

Liev Schreiber and Stephen Graham star in this new serial killer / FBI agent cat-and-mouse thriller. It’s a very Hannibal Lecter setup, but with a few dark twists of its own along the way. For fans of the genre, this is well worth the time: a high-quality production with all the trappings we expect from a story about a serial killer mastermind.

It’s Halloween weekend, so I’m assuming most people looking to stream a movie at home will be looking toward the horror and supernatural ends of the genre. But I’m going to get way more specific than that and recommend a duo of witchy Massachusetts set movies, starting with The Crucible (Prime), the 1996 film from director Nicholas Hytner, working off a script by Arthur Miller himself. starring Daniel Day-Lewis as John Procter and Wynona Ryder as Abigail Williams. Filming was done in Massachusetts and Nova Scotia, and the whole production has that wind-swept woebegone Bay Colony atmosphere down perfectly. From there, it’s a short trip over the marshlands to The Witches of Eastwick (HBO), starring Jack Nicholson, Cher, Susan Sarandon, Michelle Pfeiffer. It’s of course based on the John Updike novel, back in a simpler time when America was still fascinated by adultery and middle aged sexual awakenings. Also, there’s some magic involved, and rituals, so it works for Halloween viewing.

Born on the 4th of July (1989) (HBO Max) Harlem Nights (1989) (Tubi) Once Upon a Time in Mexico (2003) (Tubi) Casino (1885) (Prime) Fletch (1995) (Prime)
