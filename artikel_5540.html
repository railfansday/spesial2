Loyalty Juggernaut and On Point Loyalty Launch fidivio, the Loyalty Industry's First Integrated Financial Planning, Analysis and IFRS-Compliant Accounting Platform

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

fidivio connects loyalty operations with enterprise finance to strengthen financial control and improve program economics

PALO ALTO, Calif., Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Loyalty Juggernaut, Inc., a global leader in enterprise loyalty technology, and On Point Loyalty, a leading consulting firm specializing in loyalty strategy and finance, today launched fidivio, a purpose-built SaaS platform for the financial management of loyalty programs. Jointly developed by the two companies, fidivio combines IFRS-compliant loyalty accounting, financial planning and analysis, and fidi, an AI-powered financial assistant.

"Enterprises are asking loyalty to contribute more to growth, and that requires greater discipline in how they invest in and manage their programs," said Shyam Shah, CEO and Co-Founder of Loyalty Juggernaut. "With fidivio, we are extending our commitment to helping brands turn loyalty into an enterprise growth engine. Our ambition is to give finance and loyalty leaders the financial confidence to pursue growth opportunities while maintaining control over program economics."

"Loyalty programs have evolved into substantial financial businesses, yet their financial management often remains fragmented across systems and spreadsheets," said Evert de Boer, CEO of fidivio. "This limits executives' visibility into performance and their ability to assess the financial consequences of program changes. We built fidivio to address that gap, embedding specialist financial models and methodologies into everyday accounting and planning. fidi AI makes that expertise more accessible, helping teams understand the drivers behind financial outcomes, evaluate scenarios, and make more informed decisions."

fidivio combines Loyalty Juggernaut's enterprise technology expertise and experience powering loyalty programs for iconic global brands including Emirates, Liverpool, WestJet, Global Hotel Alliance, Shoppers Stop and Deutsche Telekom through its flagship GRAVTYÂ® platform with On Point Loyalty's specialized expertise in loyalty economics, financial modeling and program valuation, drawing on experience advising more than 40 airlines worldwide.

fidivio is already in production at Nexxia, a Mexico-based loyalty and data solutions company serving brands across Latin America, bringing the platform's capabilities into a live enterprise environment.

"As our loyalty business grows, we need accounting processes and financial controls that can grow with it," said Liliana Galeano Pardo, Chief Financial Officer of Nexxia. "fidivio helps us maintain a consistent view of deferred revenue and program liabilities, with reporting that supports reconciliation and financial oversight. It gives our team a stronger foundation for managing the obligations associated with our programs."
