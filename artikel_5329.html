Wendy's is sued for allegedly coordinating menu prices across its franchise network

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A proposed class action in Maryland also names NCR Voyix and seeks unspecified damages and an order halting the alleged pricing scheme

Wendy's $WEN and restaurant software provider NCR Voyix face a proposed class action in U.S. federal court in Maryland that accuses them of colluding to drive up what customers pay for menu items, allegedly by consolidating price-setting and pooling sales figures throughout Wendy's franchise system, according to Reuters.

Andy Garcia, a California resident, filed the suit on Monday, representing a group of Wendy's customers that numbers in the tens of thousands at minimum.

The complaint alleges that Wendy's pulls in and circulates register data on individual transactions via NCR Voyix's Aloha system. It says Wendy's relies on that data to align prices among outlets that ought to be rivals. The suit also alleges Wendy's obliges franchisees that compete against each other to run the Aloha software and hand over granular figures on their sales and prices.

Garcia alleges that Wendy's acts as a "common decisionmaker" across a network of thousands of independent franchisees plus the restaurants the company owns itself, controlling what consumers pay in breach of U.S. antitrust law. The lawsuit says the scheme dates to 2016 or earlier.

Allen Neumark, a lawyer for Garcia, said in a statement that Wendy's relies on a system for setting prices to rig the market for what it sells.

"Wendy's tells its investors that franchisees set their own prices. But this is false," Neumark said. "And when businesses that should be competing stop competing on price, consumers pay more."

The complaint asks for an unspecified amount in damages, along with a court order that would halt the alleged coordination of prices. Wendy's and NCR Voyix had not responded to requests for comment, according to Reuters.

The case follows a similar suit against McDonald's $MCD. That company has denied wrongdoing in a suit filed in October that accuses it of improperly using technology tools to fix prices, according to Reuters. The Wendy's case is one of a rising number of legal actions that question the way businesses use data and technology to set prices.

Wendy's is also dealing with falling sales. Franchisee Meritage Hospitality Group, which operates 314 Wendy's locations, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in September. Same-store sales across Wendy's U.S. system had fallen for six consecutive quarters, down 7.8% in the first quarter of this year and 7.0% in the second.
