Genspark Launches SecondBrain VoicePad, the Fully Customizable AI Gadget

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

PALO ALTO, Calif., Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Genspark.ai, the company building the all-in-one AI workspace, today launched SecondBrain VoicePad, a customizable desktop device that lets people work with the press of a key or the sound of their voice. It pairs with your PC or Mac and combines programmable keys with dual microphones, so you can open apps, dictate text, and trigger custom actions. Speak normally or whisper, and your words appear as text in whatever app you're using.

For most people, getting started on a task means a series of interruptions: finding the right app, locating the right tab, and typing out thoughts that are already fully formed. VoicePad eliminates those detours. A single press opens a favorite app or shortcut, while another turns speech into text instantly. The result is a faster, more natural way to work, with more of the day spent on what matters most.

Set a key to dictation, pick VoicePad up, and speak at a normal volume or even a whisper. VoicePad types clean, polished text directly into whatever app you're using, automatically removing filler words and false starts. For people who spend their day writing AI prompts and messaging teammates, that can add up to hours saved each week. Because the dual microphones sit close to your mouth, VoicePad performs reliably in loud or shared offices where built-in laptop dictation often struggles.

VoicePad connects to Windows and macOS over Bluetooth, and is housed in a CNC-machined aluminum body that weighs just 85 grams.

SecondBrain VoicePad is available for preorder now. The first production run ships in January 2027. Learn more and order at shop.genspark.ai/pages/voicepad.

Genspark.ai is a Palo Alto, California-based AI company building agentic AI for knowledge workers worldwide. Founded by veterans of Microsoft, Google, Meta, YouTube and Pinterest, Genspark orchestrates more than 70 leading AI models to turn business objectives into finished deliverables, from boardroom presentations and financial models to client-ready documents and full-stack applications. Its products work across web, desktop, mobile, and hardware. Genspark is valued at $2.6 billion and has raised $645 million in total funding, from investors including Emergence Capital Partners, LG, SBI, UpHonest, and Temasek's Pavilion Capital. The company partners with OpenAI, Anthropic, AWS, Microsoft, and other industry leaders, and serves both individual users and business customers. Learn more at genspark.ai.
