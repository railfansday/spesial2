CHILDREN'S MIRACLE NETWORK WELCOMES CHUCK E. CHEESE AS NATIONAL PARTNER, LAUNCHING CHECKOUT GIVING AT 450-PLUS LOCATIONS

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Guests can support local children's hospitals by making a donation at participating Chuck E. Cheese locations through Jan. 31.

SALT LAKE CITY, Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Chuck E. Cheese is joining Children's Miracle Network (CMN) as its newest national partner, launching a fundraiser at 454 participating U.S. locations where guests can donate at checkout to benefit their local children's hospital.

For nearly half a century, Chuck E. Cheese has been known as the place "Where A Kid Can Be A KidÂ®," bringing families together through play, celebrations, and lasting memories. Now, through its partnership with Children's Miracle Network, Chuck E. Cheese is building on that legacy by helping support the health and well-being of kids in communities across the country.

"Chuck E. Cheese has spent decades being part of some of the happiest days in a kid's life," said Kristen Schavemaker, Chief Partnerships Officer at Children's Miracle Network. "Now its guests can help make the hardest days a little easier. Every donation at checkout goes straight to a children's hospital in that family's own community, and we're thrilled to welcome Chuck E. Cheese to the Children's Miracle Network family."

The partnership's first fundraising campaign runs Dec. 1, 2026, through Jan. 31, 2027. Guests can add a $1, $3 or $5 donation at checkout through FreedomPay's Gateway to Givingâ¢, a charitable program that enables seamless giving at the point of sale. Each hospital directs the funds to its most pressing needs, from life-changing treatments and equipment to research and family support.

"For nearly 50 years, we have helped kids be kids, and that mission does not stop at our front doors," said Alejandra Brady, Senior Director of Communications and D&I at CEC Entertainment. "Partnering with Children's Miracle Network lets our guests and team members support the children and families who need it most, right in the communities we serve."

Beyond checkout donations, Chuck E. Cheese and Children's Miracle Network will also bring the partnership to life through special experiences for patients and families. These include visits from Chuck E. Cheese at local children's hospitals and opportunities for up to 50 CMN hospitals to bring patients and families to nearby Chuck E. Cheese locations.
