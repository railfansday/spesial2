Battlestar Galactica Franchise 2027 Return Officially Confirmed

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Despite welcoming many reimaginings since it first premiered in 1978, Battlestar Galactica fans have been waiting a while to see what is next from the beloved sci-fi franchise.

After all, it was in 2010 that the prequel series, Caprica, premiered, and before that, the series that ran from 2004 to 2009 gave us a reimagining of the original television show. However, with a legion of fans still dedicated to the story which takes us to a group of planets known as the Twelve Colonies as we follow the exploits of the survivors who engage in an ongoing war with the Cylons, 2027 is the year when it is set to make a major return.

Unfortunately, back in 2024, Peacock broke fan's hearts when it announced that it would be scrapping its planned Battlestar Galactica reboot. Despite being in development for five years and announced back in 2019, the project, created by Mr. Robot's Sam Esmail, was rumored to follow the same vein as the 2004 reboot, but was reportedly canceled due to high production costs, creative friction, and a tighter streaming market.

However, it is not all bad news as Battlestar Galactica is expected to return in the new year thanks to one of the franchise's most beloved entries.

This is all courtesy of Battlestar Galactica: The Board Game as it is set to make an official comeback after almost two decades. Earlier this year, Ghost Galaxy revealed a new licensing agreement with Universal and Fantasy Flight Games and, as such, an updated edition of the beloved tabletop classic will begin a crowdfunding campaign in early 2027.

For those who missed out on its original iteration, Battlestar Galactica: The Board Game first launched in 2008 courtesy of Fantasy Flight Games. Created by Corey Konieczka, it swiftly became one of the most successful hidden-traitor games on the market, which makes it all the more exciting that it is set to return, and with a modern twist.

The original game is based on the 2004 reboot and will allow players to head to the Galactica in an attempt to secure humanity's dwindling resources, all whilst surviving Cylon attacks and various events which could see your ship damaged, or even destroyed. However, what made Battlestar Galactica: The Board Game all the more exciting is that it allowed some players to secretly be Cylons with the aim of sabotaging the other players.

The board game experience was so successful that it welcomed multiple expansions, including Pegasus, Exodus, and Daybreak, and even though it eventually stopped being created, secondhand copies of Battlestar Galactica: The Board Game soon became highly sought-after collectibles.

Whilst we do not yet know the specifics of the upcoming crowdfunding campaign, we do know that the updated Battlestar Galactica: The Board Game will feature an updated design, but what this will look like remains to be seen. Nevertheless, if you were a fan of the original experience, or just so happen to be a Battlestar Galactica fan who is eager for a return to the sci-fi world, make sure to keep up-to-date with this exciting new project.
