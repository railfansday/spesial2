Sebastian Stan’s New Thriller Officially Removed From 500 Theaters

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

In his time at Collider, he has also helped drive diversity by writing stories about the multiple Indian film industries, with a goal of introducing audiences to a whole new world of cinema.

While some Oscar contenders have emerged as massive box-office hits already this year, others have struggled to break out of niche circles. Two films at opposite ends of this spectrum are currently playing in theaters, having delivered drastically different results. The three-hour documentary You Can See Everything has defied the odds to gross nearly $100 million at the domestic box office, and thanks to the film's incredible reception, A24 is gearing up to campaign for a Best Picture nomination. On the other end of the spectrum is Neon's seventh consecutive Palme d'Or winner, which is on its way out of theaters after a so-so box office run.

The movie premiered at the Cannes Film Festival earlier this year, where it won the festival's top honor, continuing Neon's incredible streak. The distributor has picked up the American rights to seven Palme d'Or winners in a row — Parasite, Titane, Triangle of Sadness, Anatomy of a Fall, Anora, and It Was Just an Accident. Two of these movies — Parasite and Anora — ended up winning the Best Picture and Best Director Oscars.

It remains to be seen if Neon's latest can score a Best Picture nod as well, although it has already been selected as Romania's official entry in the Best International Feature category. The movie was directed by Romanian auteur Cristian Mungiu, who now has two Palme d'Ors to his name. It stars Sebastian Stan and Renate Reinsve in the lead roles of a married couple whose conservatism attracts attention when they move to a progressive Norwegian town.

We're talking, of course, about Fjord. Unlike Parasite, which grossed more than $50 million domestically, Fjord has so far struggled at the box office. The movie has generated $1.8 million domestically after nearly 40 days of release, during which its peak theater count stood at 540. The film is now playing in just 44 locations nationwide.

The film received positive reviews and now sits at a "Certified Fresh" 92% score on the aggregator website Rotten Tomatoes. The site's consensus reads, "Presenting a knotty moral dilemma and refusing to provide easy answers, Cristian Mungiu's superbly well-acted Fjord has a chilly surface that belies a heated humanity beneath the frost." Reinsve is coming off the blockbuster horror film Backrooms, which grossed $400 million worldwide, while Stan will reprise his role as the Winter Soldier in the upcoming Avengers: Doomsday.

Fjord follows the Gheorghius, a Romanian-Catholic family who move to a remote Norwegian village hoping to build a quieter life. As they grow close to their neighbors, the Halbergs, cultural differences and local suspicion begin to turn that welcome into hostility, leading to accusations that threaten both families and expose the fragile line between trust and fear.
