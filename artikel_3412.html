Paramount+ is heralding November 2026 with an avalanche of fresh titles landing on the platform. As it does every month, the streamer drops the bulk of its buzziest additions right on the first, serving up a sprawling menu of movies and shows to carry your watchlist straight through the weeks ahead.

Because wading through that catalog can get overwhelming fast, we here at Tom’s Guide have done the heavy lifting to narrow the pile down to a prime trio worth clearing your schedule for. If you are feeling the holiday spirit, there is a cozy seasonal double feature ready to roll: “It’s a Wonderful Life,” the gold-standard Christmas classic, alongside “Surviving Christmas,” a goofy Ben Affleck crowd-pleaser tailored for easy holiday viewing.

Prefer something considerably colder and nastier? Paramount+ has that angle covered as well, headlined by David Fincher’s razor-sharp thriller “Gone Girl,” anchored by Rosamund Pike’s chilling, Oscar-nominated turn as Amy Dunne. Without further delay, let’s dive into the three newly added Paramount+ movies you need to be streaming right now.

What’s it about? On the morning of their fifth wedding anniversary, Nick Dunne (Ben Affleck) comes home to find his wife Amy (Rosamund Pike) gone and the living room trashed. As the media descends and the police dig in, their perfect marriage starts to look a whole lot darker, and everyone looks to Nick as the prime suspect behind Amy's disappearance. But nothing here is what it seems, and the truth turns out to be far nastier than anyone suspects.

Why you should watch it: “Gone Girl” is one of Fincher's best, and Rosamund Pike delivers one of the most chilling performances of the last decade as Amy, earning a Best Actress Oscar nomination in the process. It's well worth reading the novel of the same name or going on blind -- it'll surprise you either way.

What’s it about? You’ve almost certainly seen it, but on the off chance you haven’t: James Stewart stars as George Bailey, a man who keeps setting aside his own dreams to do right by his hometown of Bedford Falls. When a financial disaster threatens to ruin him one Christmas Eve, George hits his lowest point and wishes he’d never been born. That's all until a guardian angel named Clarence (Henry Travers) shows him just how many lives he’s touched, and how different the world would be without him.

Why you should watch it: Nearly 80 years later, “It’s a Wonderful Life” remains the gold standard of Christmas movies, and for good reason. Stewart gives one of the great performances of his career, and the film’s message about the value of one life still feels like a warm hug in the middle of what many may find a lonely season.

What’s it about? Ben Affleck plays Drew Latham, a wealthy, workaholic advertising exec who hates the thought of spending Christmas completely alone. His solution is to drive out to his childhood home, where he finds a working-class family (led by the late greats James Gandolfini and Catherine O’Hara) living there now. He offers them $250,000 to let him move in and play the part of their son for the holidays. Naturally, forcing a bunch of strangers to become his instant family doesn’t go quite as smoothly as he’d hoped.

Why you should watch it: This one’s pure, goofy holiday comfort food. It's the perfect accompaniment for a huge cup of cocoa and a big blanket on the couch, and plenty silly with tons of holiday cheer. If you're ready to move past Halloween, this is going to do it for you immediately.
