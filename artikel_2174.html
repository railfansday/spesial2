Scott Parker dubbed 'underwhelming' as he readies to replace Nuno at West Ham

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

West Ham United have decided to sack Portuguese head coach Nuno Espírito Santo following two defeats on the spin to Southampton and West Bromwich Albion.

West Ham are on the hunt for a new permanent head coach after making the difficult decision to part ways with Nuno Espírito Santo.

The 52-year-old remained in charge at the London Stadium despite overseeing the Hammers' demise into the Championship, and now, despite the club sitting in the play-off places after 13 games, has seen his time at the helm in East London come to a disappointing end.

After six wins, four draws, and three defeats in 13 games, the West Ham hierarchy have decided it's time to part ways with Nuno.

The former Wolverhampton Wanderers boss, who led the Old Gold to the Championship title in 2018 before enduring spells of varying success with Tottenham Hotspur, Al-Ittihad, and Nottingham Forest, will not get the chance to add another English second-tier title to his trophy haul.

Following their relegation from the Premier League, it was largely expected that West Ham would be in the title fight throughout the entire campaign, but having failed to win less than half of their league games at this point, the club's hierarchy has decided it's time for a change.

West Ham have made a swift move for their Nuno replacement, as, according to The Athletic's David Ornstein, former Fulham, Bournemouth, and Burnley boss Scott Parker is set to take the reins in East London, with an agreement in place to appoint the former Hammers midfielder.

With three promotions from the Championship as a head coach already in his managerial career, Parker has emerged as the frontrunner, and a deal could be announced quickly.

The news of Nuno's dismissal and Parker's impending appointment will be rippling throughout the West Ham fanbase right now, as supporters have taken to social media in droves to react to this shock news.
