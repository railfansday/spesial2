Leeds United put in one of their best performances of the season against Chelsea on Saturday.

The Whites were dominant at Stamford Bridge on 21 November and beat the Blues 4-1 after goals from Dominic Calvert-Lewin, Noah Okafor and Brenden Aaronson.

Emmanuel Emegha gave the hosts the lead in the 25th minute, but the momentum stayed with the West Yorkshire outfit from there on.

Supporters at Stamford Bridge had an early headloss as they watched Daniel Farke's team dominate their side.

Xabi Alonso had no answers for the Peacocks after the break and was punished for it.

Here, Football Insider look at some of the moments that you may have missed from the game.

Chelsea and Leeds' team buses arrived outside Stamford Bridge at the same time, leading to some delay for the visitors.

The Whites' squad were made to wait outside as Chelsea players made their way in first.

Leeds Live journalist Isaac Johnson shared on X that some of the West Yorkshire outfit's staff weren't happy with the way things unfolded.

Chelsea had a decent opening 20 minutes in the game, but Leeds quickly turned the tide.

The Blues took the initial lead but couldn't build on it.

The West Yorkshire outfit had the better of the chances before the break and could've been ahead before half-time.

As the London club's players walked towards the tunnel at the end of the first half, boos rang out at Stamford Bridge, likely reflecting disappointment with Alonso's team's performance.

After the historic victory at Stamford Bridge, Leeds players made sure to celebrate.

Miguel Delane of the Independent was in the press room at Stamford Bridge, which is right beside the away dressing room.

Farke's team were playing the Luke Littler song and banging on the walls.

Aaronson added the final nail in Chelsea's coffin in the 92nd minute.

The USMNT international went off celebrating with the away fans towards the corner.

James Trafford was seen running and jumping in on the substitute with the rest of his teammates.
