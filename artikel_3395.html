Netflix’s New 8-Part Psychological Thriller Series Passes Major Streaming Milestone Ahead Of Season 2 Fate

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Netflix's new psychological thriller series is one of its most popular releases of the year, and it continues to impress, now leaving a key milestone behind as season 2 hopes loom. Netflix has had many successful thriller shows over the years, with high-profile projects coming from the psychological thriller subgenre. Parallel to that, Netflix's list of TV shows in 2026 includes quite a few genre shows that have returned with new seasons, such as major releases like The Night Agent, The Lincoln Lawyer, Black Doves, and more. Now, there is another TV show hoping to join the list of multi-season thrillers.

That would be The Body, a new Netflix psychological thriller series that focuses on a Catholic high school dance team as the teenage girls jokingly perform a sacrificial ritual to win their competition, only to find that strange things start to happen after that, with plenty of blood and paranoia to go around. The Body released all of its eight episodes on Netflix on November 11, with the show immediately becoming a hit on the streaming service, both nationwide and around the world. Now, The Body has gone on to find impressive success on Netflix, with the thriller hitting a milestone.

The Body has been trending on Netflix in the United States for over 10 days, currently at 12, according to data from FlixPatrol. Right now, the longest trend on the streaming service's nationwide top 10 belongs to the rom-com Nobody Wants This, at a whopping 107 days. That said, the Adam Brody and Kristen Bell romance series is currently the 10th most-watched on the streamer, ranking well below the new psychological thriller. The Body is still holding strong as the most popular TV show on Netflix in the US, almost two full weeks since it debuted on the streaming service.

The new hit series is showing no signs of slowing down. Netflix's Top 10 TV Shows chart in the US also includes projects like Taken: Natalee Holloway, The Altruists, Black Doves, and many more. According to FlixPatrol data, The Body also ranks high as the #1 Netflix series worldwide, with the platform's latest streaming report claiming that The Body is trending on Netflix in 88 countries, with the thriller also standing as the streamer's most-watched in 35 of those nations. The new hit series' streaming success bodes well for The Body season 2's chances at Netflix, with the show waiting for a renewal.

Another major factor that could help season 2 become a reality is the positive response the show has had from viewers. On Rotten Tomatoes, The Body has achieved a near-perfect 95% audience score. Fans have also banded together to start a petition for Netflix to renew The Body for season 2 on change.org, with 59.052 signatures accrued at the time of writing. As the petition states, the show needs to answer the major cliffhanger left by The Body season 1's ending, aka what Grace's "I am God" moment really means. The Body season 2 grows more likely with the show's continued streaming success.
