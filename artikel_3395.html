5 Must-See Cheesy Christmas Movies Coming Out in 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Every year, dozens and dozens of cheesy Christmas movies are released. The Hallmark Channel alone often releases over two dozen each year. This means anyone looking for a quick, cheery holiday fix is likely to find themselves swimming in options with no idea where to turn or what might be worth watching. Which is tricky because there can be a very fine line between a cheesy movie that's fun versus one that's too over-the-top to enjoy.

2026 is no exception to the deluge of holiday films, with just about every streaming service trying to cash in on the holiday spirit, from Netflix to Disney+. But there will always be movies that rise to the top for one reason or another, whether it's a household name starring in the movie (Arnold Schwarzenegger) or it's produced by a company with a stellar track record for Christmas movies. There are five Christmas movies that stand out of the lineup this year, and everyone who loves the holidays should be clamoring to watch them with a cup of cocoa in hand.

In The 12 Dates of Christmas, Lucy Hale plays a woman named Christina who has found herself rather unlucky in love, but agrees to go on 12 dates set up by her best friend in the hopes of finding someone worthy of bringing to her family's Christmas dinner. She's willing to do anything to get her mom to stop pestering her. What ensues is a string of shocking dates that highlight the modern horrors of dating. Anyone who didn't get married before Tinder rocked the world will be able to relate to Christina's woes.

This holiday romcom can be found on Disney+ starting November 6. Alongside Lucy Hale, other starring actors include Paul Ben-Victor, and Penelope Ann Miller. Disney+ has more than proven they can craft fun holiday movies, and this one isn't likely to disappoint. Titles from previous years include Noelle (2019), Godmothered (2020), and the slightly weird yet fun musical A Very Jonas Christmas (2025).

No holiday movie list would be complete without a Hallmark movie and this year's My Christmas Cowboy looks like it'll be worth the watch. The movie stars Amy Acker as Meg Marshall and Luke Mcfarlane as Billy Kane. The story follows Meg, a news producer from a small town. Meanwhile, Billy, her college crush and a famous Hollywood presenter, has come back to town to see family. Meg and Billy's paths seem to keep crossing despite their best efforts.

My Christmas Cowboy is great for anyone looking to watch a new movie that centers around the holidays while breaking certain expectations of the genre — like ubiquitous snow and a serious lack of cowboy hats. But the romcom tropes will still be hard to ignore. The movie is set to air for the first time on the Hallmark Channel on November 13.

Guarding Stars has whipped up plenty of traction on social media and with good reason. This Netflix movie stars Jared Padalecki and Leighton Meester in a holiday comedy with a bodyguard crush trope. The best part? The movie turns the trope on its head by having Meester play Hannah Brooks, the bodyguard to famous star Jack Stapleton (Padalecki). Oh, and there's fake dating too, because Stapleton refuses to let his mom know he's under protection.

Netflix's Christmas movies are often quite similar to Hallmark movies, but with a better budget and scripts that are more polished. So, if you're hoping for butterflies caused by a wintery love story, but fewer small-town values being pushed, this film should be at the top of your list. It also may be worth checking out some of their other offerings from previous years if you haven't before, like The Princess Switch (2018) starring Vangessa Hudgens or A Castle for Christmas (2021) starring Brooke Shields and Cary Elwes. Guarding Stars will be available for streaming starting on November 18.

The Man with the Bag is a family action movie starring Arnold Schwarzenegger as Santa, Alan Ritchson as his begrudging helper, and Awkwafina as a naughty elf. There's no romance in this movie, so if anyone is in the mood for holiday cheer without seeing couples kissing under mistletoe, this would be an excellent choice. The movie explores what happens when Santa loses his infamous bag and reaches out to a criminal named Vance (Ritchson) for help getting it back. The trailer for The Man with the Bag is filled with high-energy scenes, plenty of quips, and a cozy sense of fun.

Anyone who enjoyed Netflix's 2018 movie The Christmas Chronicles should be making sure they watch The Man with the Bag this holiday season. The movies appear tonally similar and both feature larger-than-life Santas who find themselves on an unexpected adventure with civilians in an effort to prevent Christmas from being ruined. If Schwarzenegger breaks out into a jazz solo, it'll be hard to ignore the parallels, but that's unlikely. Probably. The Man with the Bag will begin streaming on December 2 and can be found on Prime Video.

Anyone who is a sucker for time loop stories will want to save the date for Netflix's In a Holidaze. It's based on a 2020 novel of the same name, written by Christina Lauren. The movie, directed by Tiffany Paulsen, will be available for streaming starting December 2. The movie stars Maddie Ziegler, Patton Oswalt, and Rob Lowe.

In the movie, Mae (Ziegler) is going to her family cabin for Christmas along with her dysfunctional family. For reasons the trailer doesn't provide, her lifelong crush will also be there. Mae is determined to finally reveal her feelings but chickens out and even kisses his brother. Panicked and frustrated with herself, Mae wakes up the next day on an airplane. The exact same airplane she was on yesterday — and her family are saying and doing the exact same things. If she can pull herself together, maybe she can finally work up the courage to make her feelings known and find a way out of the time loop she's in.
