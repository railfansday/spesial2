2022 Bestseller Returns Today as a Netflix Holiday Rom-Com With Supernatural Star

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Many major book adaptations like Klara and the Sun or Sunrise on the Reaping are instantly recognizable as on-screen renditions of their source material. But sometimes, movie titles don't match their source material, hiding their bookish roots from more casual viewers. That's the case for Netflix's latest book adaptation, Guarding Stars, which leans on its star-studded cast and holiday vibes more than its bookish roots.

Guarding Stars arrived on Netflix on November 18, delivering an on-screen take on Katherine Center's best-selling novel, The Bodyguard. The title change has more to do with avoiding confusion with Kevin Costner's 1992 thriller than hiding the project's relationship to Center's book. But it still makes the movie's adaptation status a little less obvious, except to major fans who've been eagerly waiting to see the book make its way to the screen.

The Bodyguard was a massive breakout hit even for an already beloved rom-com author, hitting multiple bestseller charts when it released in 2022. Even with the movie's name change, Netflix certainly isn't trying to bury that lede. But the bookish connection isn't this new holiday rom-com's only claim to fame. The Netflix adaptation boasts an impressive cast list, featuring talent from Gossip Girl and Supernatural in its starring roles. And that nostalgic casting, along with its pivot to a Christmas holiday theme, has certainly taken center stage for the hype cycle.

Trailers for Guarding Stars make two things very clear. The movie has managed to snag some familiar faces for its leading roles, and it will be giving the book a holiday-themed makeover. The November release timing is no accident, as Guarding Stars rebrands Katherine Center's book as a Christmas rom-com, landing it firmly in Netflix's lineup of holiday programming. It's not the only bookish rom-com Netflix is treating readers to this season, either: In a Holidaze, based on the Christina Lauren holiday rom-com, releases December 2.

The Bodyguard does incorporate holiday elements, as it's set around Thanksgiving. But it's hardly a holiday rom-com. The book leans into more typical contemporary romance tropes like fake dating, forced proximity, and, of course, celebrity romance. The movie, on the other hand, features all the Christmas trees, mistletoe, and holiday cheer. That could be a win for readers who love a good holiday love story, but it could also reframe the story a bit too much for adaptation purists eager to see a more faithful version of The Bodyguard on screen.

The movie does keep the overall premise for The Bodyguard, even if it dusts it with a heavy dose of holiday cheer. Jack Stapleton needs a bodyguard to keep his stalker at bay, but he doesn't want his mom to worry. So, Executive Protection Agent Hannah Brooks will pose as his fake girlfriend so she can stick close by while he heads home to help out on the ranch. Your typical fake-dating turns into a real romance arc ensues, to delightful effect. The book has garnered a 3.93 average on Goodreads with over 473K ratings, solidifying its place among the pantheon of beloved contemporary romance novels from the last decade. That gives the adaptation some big shoes to fill, and it's no surprise Netflix came in swinging with its casting choices.

In case a holiday romance isn't enough of a selling point, Netflix also threw a few familiar faces in the mix. Jared Padalecki of Supernatural and Gilmore Girls fame steps into the boots of our romantic lead, actor Jack Stapleton. Though readers might not have initially pictured the Supernatural actor as a holiday rom-com lead, he certainly fits the bill. And it's not likely many romance fans will be sad to see the actor who portrayed both Dean Forester and Sam Winchester playing a heartthrob actor in Guarding Stars.

Padalecki is joined by another familiar face. Leighton Meester, best known for the role of Blair in Gossip Girl, portrays the book's protagonist and titular bodyguard, Hannah Brooks. This is quite a different role from the spoiled, bossy Blair, but Meester's acting chops are clearly up to the task from the trailer alone.

Seeing two major stars from 2000s TV hits come together for a holiday rom-com is no doubt a strong selling point for the adaptation. And the cast boasts a few other big names, including Andie MacDowell of Groundhog Day fame. But for readers who love the original story, the movie runs the risk of leaning too heavily on its casting and holiday rom-com rework at the expense of hitting key points from the story we know and love.

Indeed, early reviews, including ScreenRant's own, suggest that Guarding Stars feels much more like a stereotypical Hallmark movie than a robust adaptation of its source material. Even so, readers who enjoyed The Bodyguard will no doubt want to experience its adaptation, if only to see how Meester and Padalecki pull off the starring roles. Thankfully, it's out now for your holiday viewing pleasure.
