Brooklyn Building Collapse: New Angle Shows Apartment Crumble

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The deadly Brooklyn building collapse was caught from yet another terrifying angle ... with new footage showing the massive structure crumbling as city traffic moves just yards away.

Video captured by an MTA camera shows an ordinary afternoon at a busy Flatbush intersection ... with a city bus passing through and pedestrians walking along the sidewalks ... moments before tragedy strikes.

Suddenly, without warning, a huge section of the building gives way ... crashing down near a parked white truck and sending an enormous cloud of dust and debris billowing into the street.

Within seconds, the dust spreads across the roadway, obscuring much of the block. Watch the footage ... it's a chilling look at just how quickly the disaster unfolded.

As TMZ previously reported, the three-story apartment building collapsed on November 11 near Flatbush and Bedford Avenues, killing eight people and injuring 18 others.
