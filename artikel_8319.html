As a long time independent traveler I wasn't sure I'd like guided tours but I also grew to appreciate having someone else take care of all the planning, logistics, and troubleshooting. Strikes? Riots? Floods? Someone else will deal with it and do their best to give us a nice vacation.

There are a lot of choices but for daily rate you have to look at the hotel and the included meals as that becomes one of the differentiators and ends up giving you a lot of choices. It's one of the first things I look at when I compare tours to a specific destination along with the typical daily mileage and time in transit each day.
After a coach tour thru Alaska I'm feeling teh same way about letting someone else handle the headaches and scheduling. We had a great time and the guide and driver were excellent. Both had very insighful perspectives on the tour.
I'm expecting the same from this tour in Switzerland. Driving in other countries could be challenging I found on our last trip to the Azores. We have always driven ourselves and made our own itinerary but getting older I find I don't mind being a passenger taking in the sights.
