Match Awards from Germany’s magical 5-0 win over the Netherlands

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Germany are so back. In a troubling scenario heading into this match, Germany required all three points to have any chance of qualifying for the UEFA Nations League quarter-finals. In what was supposed to be a tightly contested match, Jürgen Klopp found some magic in his new squad scoring five against a Netherlands side that just came off a 7-0 thrashing of a solid Greek side.

This one always feels harsh giving out but simply no one stood out from the pack in this Dutch side. What was actually a decent start for Xavi's side in the first half was squandered by some missed scoring chances that really should have been put in the net. Thankfully for Germany, the Dutch were simply not clicking in this one.

While Nico Schlotterbeck might not have that illustrous aerial presence that Malick Thiaw offers, the Dortmund defender certainly showed off his heading with a brilliantly placed goal. Schlotterbeck was reliable defensively and offers much more going forward than other defenders.

While Germany's defense was never going to be the storyline of this match, it was ruthlessly efficient in thwarting anotherwise excellent Dutch attack, as shown in recent matches.

The midfield was arguably the weakest side of the German team in this game and yet there was no bad performance to be found. Aleksander Pavlović and Kimmich were perhaps equally effective in this match and did a great job at picking up on the mistakes of a shaky Dutch performance.

While many were disappointed to not see the recent duo of Tom Bischof and Pavlović share the midfield over this international break, it is clear that Kimmich and Pavlović are the main cast for the time being.

While Wirtz was perhaps outshined by a certain winger by the end of the match, it was he who got things going for the Germans in the second half and created the momentum that they would ride for the rest of the match.

Wirtz is the face of this team and Klopp is going to build something special around their star.
