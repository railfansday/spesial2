Rams injury report: Myles Garrett, Trent McDuffie questionable vs. Cardinals

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Los Angeles Rams are hoping to avoid another letdown against the Arizona Cardinals this week. They'll face their division rivals without some of their top players, which is nothing new for this team.

Heading into Sunday's game, the Rams will have three players listed as questionable: Myles Garrett, Trent McDuffie and Kam Curl. Garrett isn't practicing on Friday, as Sean McVay said the Rams are just "being smart" with him. McVay expects Garrett to play, saying that he's "feeling good" and building on a strong performance last week.

McDuffie and Curl will be limited in practice, and the Rams will see how they feel before determining their statuses for Sunday's game. McDuffie has missed three of the last four games but McVay said earlier in the week that he's "optimistic" about a return on Sunday. Curl didn't practice at all last week due to a foot injury and still managed to play against the Commanders.

Finally, Terrance Ferguson and Steve Avila have both been ruled out, which isn't surprising. They're both expected to miss multiple weeks with their hamstring injuries.

The Rams will release their full injury report after practice on Friday, at which point this story will be updated.
