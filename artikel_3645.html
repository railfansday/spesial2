Kelly Osbourne Looks Unrecognizable With Waist-Length Vampy Dark Hair

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Kelly Osbourne channeled The Little Mermaid’s Ariel with her latest hair transformation.

The reality TV alum, 42, posed for photos on the red carpet at the ZYN Rolling Stone U.K. Awards at the Roundhouse in London on Thursday, November 19. Osbourne traded in her short, platinum blonde locks for long, auburn tresses as she showed off her figure in a plunging blue sparkling gown. She finished off the edgy look with silver and gold statement necklaces and a bold dark red lip to match her hair.

Osbourne shared several snaps of her new look via her Instagram Stories on the way to the event, including one where she compared herself to Ariel.

“It’s [giving] … you want thingamabobs I got 20,” she captioned a selfie, setting the photo to “Part of Your World.”

Kelly’s hair transformation comes two months after the mom of one opened up about losing a significant amount of weight as she mourned the loss of her dad, Ozzy Osbourne. (The Black Sabbath legend died at age 76 in July 2025 after suffering cardiac arrest.)

“I looked like the f***ing Crypt Keeper,” Kelly told the Daily Mail in September of her appearance at the 2026 Grammys in February. “I said, ‘This is scary. I didn’t know I looked like this.’ My spine — you could see every single bone. I couldn’t see what other people saw because I was in so much pain. I just saw a broken person.”

She continued, “I wore a wig to cover my face, so you couldn’t see how skinny my cheekbones really were. They knew I was going through something. I was scared. I wanted to be there to support my mum and pay tribute to my father’s legacy. But in all honesty, when I reflect on it, I don’t think I should have gone. I wasn’t very well.”

Weeks later, Kelly revealed that she had gained back 30 pounds after prioritizing her health.

“I didn’t know what I looked like that until I saw the pictures,” she recalled in an interview with The Sun in October. “When I saw them, I couldn’t f***ing believe it. I was as shocked as everyone else. It wasn’t an eating disorder. I just couldn’t do anything for myself. Eating made me feel like I wanted to throw up.”

Kelly added, “In so many ways, I can’t even explain to you. But it’s also been really beautiful because I’ve learned that my grief is an honor, as I truly love someone that much.”
