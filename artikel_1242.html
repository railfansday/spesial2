He's Spot On: Carragher Can't Believe What He's Seeing From Liverpool Star

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Liverpool legend Jamie Carragher has been blown away by what he's seen from Alexander Isak this season, and he's absolutely spot on.

It was a difficult first campaign for Isak as he made the big money move from Newcastle to Anfield.

It's never easy for a player to switch clubs for a huge fee, as it automatically adds a ridiculous amount of pressure.

Isak also struggled with injuries during his first season at Liverpool, with many questioning whether Liverpool had wasted their money.

The Swedish forward, one of the best strikers in the Premier League right now, produced some phenomenal numbers for Newcastle, despite them not being on the same level as Liverpool in terms of stature.

Now we're slowly starting to see that from Isak this season, already reaching double figures for goals in all competitions.

It's not been a straightforward period for Isak, but Liverpool are finally starting to see the player they thought they'd bought.

Former Liverpool defender Carragher has been blown away by Isak so far this season, praising the forward's improvement outside of scoring goals.

"He's looking really, really good. I was impressed with him at the weekend, and he missed a lot of chances against Man United, but what I'm seeing more from Isak is the goals. We judge on goals, but as Thierry [Henry] knows and says more than anybody, you want to see a striker not just score but be involved in the game a little bit more."

Carragher suggests that that is something we saw from Isak at Newcastle, but we've only seen that at Liverpool recently.

"When I saw him at Newcastle, he was more than just a goalscorer. He was involved in the play. Over the last few weeks, it's not just about the goals, but about being involved in the game.

"I liked that again tonight. I liked it at the weekend. I wasn't bothered that he missed chances at the weekend because, more often than he scores, he gets his chances tonight, and he is looking the real deal."

Many judged Isak too quickly after a difficult start to his Liverpool career, but it was incredibly harsh when he had only started eight Premier League games last season.

Of course, Liverpool supporters will predominantly care about how many times Isak hits the back of the net, but his improvement in all aspects benefits Liverpool immensely.

The Reds have now won three games on the bounce, and Isak has played a crucial role in all of them.

Many will only look at how many goals Isak has scored this season to praise him, but Liverpool's attack is functioning a lot better with the Swedish striker heavily involved.

When you pay so much for a player, just scoring goals won't be enough, especially if you want the team to start competing for titles and climbing the Premier League table.

Andoni Iraola needs Isak to be contributing in multiple different areas, and he's seriously starting to show that at the moment.
