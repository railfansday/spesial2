See all 14 winning interiors selected in the Dezeen Awards public vote

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The results of the interiors public vote for Dezeen Awards 2026 have been announced, with 14 projects selected as winners by Dezeen’s readers.

Projects voted as readers’ favourite include a co-working space in Berlin that offers “more than a boring desk”, as well as a “quietly magical” Toronto cafe that provides a family-friendly third place.

Also winning this year’s public vote is an office in Kyiv featuring vintage furnishings, artworks, and quirky touches such as leftover paint swatches.

Dezeen Awards 2026 public vote winners revealed this week

Dezeen Awards 2026, in partnership with Trimble, will reveal all public vote winners this week. The architecture winners were announced yesterday and the design winners will be revealed tomorrow.

Readers were invited to choose their favourite projects out of the 392-strong shortlist in this year’s vote.

The vote took place between 28 September and 16 October, spanning shortlists in each of Dezeen Awards’ three main category sectors. This year’s interiors category sponsors include Framery and Programa.

All winning interiors projects are listed below, each with a link to a dedicated page on the Dezeen Awards website where you can find an image and more information about the project.

The public vote is separate from the main judging process, in which entries are assessed by our panel of industry-leading judges. The overall winners of this year’s programme, including the overall interiors project of the year sponsored by Gaggenau, will be announced at the Dezeen Awards 2026 party on 16 November at the Royal Opera House in London.

Read on to see the list of this year’s public vote winners for the interiors categories:

The Atrium, Sydney, Australia, by Anna Carin Design

Cateto Club, Costa Del Sol, Spain, by Cateto Cateto

Lost Villa Huanglong Island Lighthouse Hotel, Zhejiang, China, by SZ-Architects

Banda Agency Office, Kyiv, Ukraine, by Ater Architects

Coworking Spaces at C1 Alexanderplatz-Berlin, Berlin, Germany, by Bruzkus Greenberg

Messa House, Almaty, Kazakhstan, by Up2date Architects

The Willow Play Cafe, Toronto, Canada, by Denizens of Design

Unraveling: New Spaces, Venice, Italy, by Serbian Pavilion

Deco House, London, United Kingdom, by Farid Karim Design

William King Apartment, Athens, Greece, by Kan Atelier

Cosy House, London, United Kingdom, by Enbee Architecture and Design
