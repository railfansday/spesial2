Apple's iCloud and App Store face disruption as an internal update causes an outage

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Apple said the problems did not stem from a cyber attack and that many systems are back online as it works on the rest

Apple $AAPL is trying to bring its systems back online following Tuesday outages, triggered by an internal update, that disrupted iCloud, the App Store and other services, according to Bloomberg.

"Many of our systems are back up and running after an internal update caused some outages this morning," Apple said in a statement, according to Bloomberg. "We're working to get the remaining systems online as soon as possible." The company said a cyberattack was not the cause of the problems.

Apple's System Status page listed a resolved outage for the App Store and an issue with iCloud account and sign-in. It also showed a performance problem for iCloud web apps and an outage for AppleCare on Device. Apple Business, Apple School Manager and Schoolwork were listed with issues.

According to 9to5Mac, users said iCloud logged them out of iPhone and Mac without warning, and attempts to sign back in then met with unknown errors or failed verification. Others said they could not get the App Store to open or to fetch apps. Apple Music and device activation also ran into trouble, and developers said App Store Connect was hard to get into, according to 9to5Mac.

Apple Support, Apple Pay and iCloud drew the most outage reports on Downdetector at about 2 p.m. ET on Tuesday, according to MarketWatch. Downdetector collects user-submitted reports of service problems.

The outages reached inside the company as well, according to Bloomberg. Internal services that went down included AppleWeb, which works as the employee portal, and AppleConnect, which handles sign-ins inside the company. VPN access for staff working remotely also failed. Employees struggled to download internal software builds, check company calendars and reserve conference rooms.

Many employees were locked out of any systems that needed their Apple login details for several hours, according to Bloomberg. Apple stores worldwide experienced interruptions in selling products, handling repairs and helping customers.
