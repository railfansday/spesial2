Raiders’ Klint Kubiak gives honest review of Fernando Mendoza’s second start

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Raiders' Klint Kubiak gives honest review of Fernando Mendoza's second start appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Las Vegas Raiders quarterback Fernando Mendoza is still looking for his first win in the NFL after losing to the Denver Broncos, 38-24, at Empower Field at Mile High on Sunday.

Mendoza made his first start on the road and looked a little better than his forgettable showing in Week 10 against the Seattle Seahawks. He went 18-of-37 for 267 yards, three touchdowns, and one interception. He was sacked five times.

Raiders coach Klint Kubiak said Mendoza played more comfortably, especially in a raucous venue, as reported by the Las Vegas Review-Journal's Sam Gordon.

"Playing on the road, in a really tough environment, one of the loudest environments in football, he had some good reps there," said Kubiak.

"He was very well-prepared, and he just got that first round out of the system. On to the next one."

Las Vegas fell to 4-7, including 3-3 on the road.

The 23-year-old Mendoza admitted that the heavy noise was a factor, as reported by the Associated Press' Arnie Melendrez Stapleton. He, however, gleaned a positive from the defeat.

"Those fans are crazy loud. Played in some loud environments in my college days, but college is nothing like the NFL, and they were very loud. There was some helmet miscommunication as well, so that was another challenge that prevented itself," said the No. 1 pick.

"But just happy to work through those challenges, and I think it was a great experience."

The learning curve is high for Mendoza, who led Indiana to the national title in January, and as long as he keeps his chin up, he will get better.
