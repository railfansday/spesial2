NFC Playoff Picture: Chicago Bears fall to 5th with the Detroit Lions win

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Chicago Bears are on their bye this weekend, but with the Detroit Lions beating the New England Patriots in Germany, 35 to 10, the Lions have leapfrogged the Bears into first place in the NFC North. That also dropped the Bears down to the fifth seed in the conference.

The Packers and Vikings are playing right now, and a Minnesota win would move them up to the fifth seed, so adjust your rooting interests accordingly.
