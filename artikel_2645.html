Hawks advance to knockout stage after 87-86 win over Celtics

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Atlanta Hawks were in Boston on Friday night to face the Celtics. It was a big game for both teams regarding the NBA Cup, and the objective was clear: win and you advance to the knockout round. There could be some other things that could happen for the Hawks to get in if they lost, but winning would make everything better for them.

The Hawks had all of their main guys available for the game, with CJ McCollum, Jock Landale, Asa Newell and Mouhamed Gueye still out. For the Celtics, Mitchell Robinson was out with an illness.

Offense was hard to come by from both teams to start the game, and the only thing the Hawks had to show for it was a 3-pointer from Dyson Daniels.

The good news for the Hawks is that the Celtics couldn't get much going either, and the score stayed 8-3 for some time. Both teams started to heat up just a little as the quarter continued, but the Hawks still found themselves fighting from behind.

Lu Dort came in off the bench and was aggressive for the Hawks, and he led the team in points in the quarter.

Outside Dort and Daniels, Jalen Johnson was the only other player for the Hawks who scored in the quarter. The Hawks shot 5-of-23 from the field and 1-of-14 from the 3-point line. Going into the second, the Hawks trailed 29-14.

Aaron Wiggins started to heat up for the Hawks in the second quarter to try to get their deficit down, but the Celtics continued their hot run. They led by as much as 17, and it looked like the Hawks were still trying to find a rhythm on both sides of the ball.

The Hawks never let the game get too out of hand, and they had several chances to get it within double digits, but the shots just weren't falling. When the shots weren't falling, Johnson used his physicality to make things happen in the paint.

After a rough start, Nickeil Alexander-Walker was able to get a 3-pointer to go down.

The Hawks were able to keep things interesting going into halftime despite the poor shooting, and they trailed the Celtics 51-39.

It was a good start to the third quarter for the Hawks, as Kingston Flemings was able to get on the board with the layup off the high glass.

The Hawks then cut down their deficit to single digits after this 3-pointer from Johnson.

The Hawks continued to push the pace, and Johnson got another easy two.

The Celtics were able to respond to the Hawks' run, and it was their work on the boards that helped them extend their lead again. The Hawks kept fighting, and they went on another run to cut their deficit again. It started with Johnson finding Onyeka Okongwu for an alley-oop.

The second unit that has been strong for the Hawks over their last few games was able to keep the momentum going and keep cutting their deficit.

It was a good way to end the quarter, and they came out with their first lead of the game. Going into the fourth, the Hawks led 70-69. The second unit continued to play well going into the fourth, and Dort hit a deep 3-pointer to give the Hawks the lead.

There's no doubt that this game got physical down the stretch, and it affected both teams on the offensive end. Points were hard to come by, which meant every possession was important.

Putting the ball in Daniels' hands was a good idea down the stretch because he was able to find Johnson for this dunk.

With less than almost a minute remaining, the game was tied at 83. Alexander-Walker broke the tie with a layup to give the Hawks a two-point lead, and on the other end, Johnson got a big stop on Jayson Tatum.

The Hawks turned the ball over, but the Celtics gave it right back to them with seconds remaining, and they fouled Alexander-Walker, which sent him to the free-throw line. He made both free throws, and it gave the Hawks a four-point lead. The Celtics didn't call a timeout from there, ultimately accepting defeat, and the Hawks advanced to the knockout stage of the NBA Cup.

Johnson finished with 24 points and 12 rebounds, Alexander-Walker finished with 20 points and Dort finished with 14 points.

The Hawks will be back in action on Sunday to face the New York Knicks.
