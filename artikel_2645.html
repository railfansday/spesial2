Andy Reid gives 2 injury updates after loss to Bills

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Kansas City Chiefs only officially ruled one player out of Thursday's sloppy 13-10 loss to the Buffalo Bills.

Wide receiver Jalen Royals left the game with an ankle injury in the second half. The second-year pro was quickly ruled out.

Then, in the fourth quarter, right guard Trey Smith was seen heading into the locker room in frustration. The team never provided an update on the two-time All Pro selection, but he appeared to suffer a similar injury to Royals.

"As far as the injuries go," said head coach Andy Reid after the game, "Jalen Royals hurt his left ankle. He sprained it. Trey Smith likewise."

Tight end Travis Kelce took a frightening hit to the back in the second quarter but stayed in the game and led all Chiefs receivers with four catches for 71 yards.

The Chiefs will play a second consecutive Thursday night game in Week 13 when they travel to face the Los Angeles Rams. We will watch for updates on Royals and Smith — and players such as running back Kenneth Walker and tackle Jaylon Moore who did not play against Buffalo — when the team releases its first official injury report on Monday.
