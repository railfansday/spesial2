Fantasy Football Waiver Wire: Justin Boone's top kickers to stream for Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Welcome to Week 11 of the 2026 fantasy football season. It never gets any easier week-to-week as injuries pile up. Plus, now we have six teams on bye in Week 11 — the Packers, Falcons, Browns, Seahawks, Rams and Patriots. That's a few notable kickers off the slate, so you may be in need of a streaming option. Not to worry, Justin Boone has you covered with some of his favorite options off the waiver wire.

Santos vs. Saints: The Bears' kicker had at least nine fantasy points in four straight games prior to the team's Week 10 bye. New Orleans also allows the fifth-most fantasy points to opposing kickers this season. Santos makes a ton of sense, and has decent long-term value.

Butker vs. Cardinals: The Chiefs are set up nicely again in Week 11, going up against a struggling Arizona squad. Butker hasn't been good this season, but Kansas City should be in the red zone plenty at home.

Lutz vs. Raiders: The Broncos are coming off their bye and have a solid draw against the Raiders, who are losers of six of the past seven games. Lutz has a great floor in this matchup having scored at least seven points in six straight games.
