InventHelp Inventor Develops New K- Pack Charger for Vehicles (BKC-998)

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

PITTSBURGH, Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- "I wanted to create a convenient battery charger for electric vehicles," said an inventor, from Iberville, La., "so I invented the K- PACK CHARGER. My design eliminates the need to sit at a charging station or wait at home for your vehicle to charge."

The invention provides an improved way to charge an electric vehicle in case of an emergency or on long trips. In doing so, it eliminates the need to stop at a charging station. As a result, it saves time and effort. It also increases safety and convenience. The invention features a portable design that is easy to use so it is ideal for the owners of electric vehicles.

The original design was submitted to the National sales office of InventHelp. It is currently available for licensing or sale to manufacturers or marketers. For more information, write Dept. 25-BKC-998, InventHelp, 100 Beecham Drive, Suite 110, Pittsburgh, PA 15205-9801, or call (412) 288-1300 ext. 1368.
