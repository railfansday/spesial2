Helios Technologies: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Its first data center cooling order targets a roughly $1 billion market, but whether margins hold into 2027 remains the open question.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 26, 2026 at 11:29PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Helios Technologies ›

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 6, 2026, Helios Technologies (HLIO +0.09%) announced sales of $230 million and raised its full-year revenue outlook. Expanding beyond traditional hydraulics and electronics markets into data center cooling gives the company room to grow even as inflation and soft agriculture demand pressure near-term margins. Three things management said on the call stand out.

The company secured its first customer order for data center thermal management, introducing liquid cooling quick-release couplings to an estimated $1 billion addressable market.

Distributors and equipment manufacturing partners accelerated buying before planned price increases took effect, shifting revenue into the third quarter and setting up tougher fourth-quarter comparisons.

Management completed a targeted asset purchase to add audio systems and establish a multiyear relationship with Indian Motorcycle, demonstrating its disciplined acquisition strategy.

Order backlog, which refers to unfilled customer orders, and new product releases are pushing full-year sales toward historical highs. Top-line expansion continues despite persistent input cost pressures in freight, utilities, and electronic components.

Manufacturing footprint consolidation in Ohio and production transfers to Mexico give the company structural cost flexibility. Management is focusing on pricing discipline, customer retention, and inventory positioning to sustain cash flow momentum into 2027.
