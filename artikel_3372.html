Ole Miss Student Who Caught Schoolmates Making Monkey Noises Speaks Out After One Withdraws

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The University of Mississippi freshmen who captured the viral video of their classmates making monkey noises outside their apartment are responding to one of the women withdrawing from the school.

The three young women, who are Black and live together, and one of their moms sat down Wednesday with CBS correspondent Jericka Duncan for an interview after Alaina Kunes, who is white, called it quits on her education at Ole Miss following the racist incident.
