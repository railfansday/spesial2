Ibrahim Sangare now has the perfect chance to save his Nottingham Forest career - verdict

Ibrahim Sangare has simply been a spectator during a rollercoaster 2026-27 campaign at Nottingham Forest.

The 28-year-old has only started one Premier League game so far, which came in the season-opener against Leeds United in August.

Last season, under multiple managers, he was a consistent figure in the starting lineup, but that has changed completely under Oliver Glasner.

James McAtee is simply undroppable, starring next to Xaver Schlager, who has made an impressive impact since signing on a free transfer.

And in Forest's surprise 3-1 win over Manchester City, Ryan Yates was brought in, as McAtee was deployed further forward at the City Ground.

At this point in time, the future looks very bleak for Sangare. However, he may be given a chance at redemption against Bournemouth on Saturday, 21 November.

McAtee absolutely needs to start against the Cherries, but if Yates were to start, Forest would undoubtedly be overrun in the middle of the park.

Normally, Schlager would be the ideal partner for the former Man City gem, but he appears to have picked up an injury on international duty.

Glasner revealed in his latest press conference that the Austrian has suffered a hamstring injury and is now a "doubt" for Saturday's game.

Even if he can make it to matchday, it seems foolish to risk him at the Vitality Stadium with a perfectly capable replacement waiting in the wings.

There is one catch, though. Sangare has yet to return from international duty because of travelling delays, so that provides a new level of confusion.

During the summer, both Liverpool and Manchester United were understood to be keeping an eye on the situation.

No moves were made for the midfielder's signature, but that could change if his situation at the City Ground doesn't before January.

Besiktas are the latest to stake an interest, with the Turkish side exploring Forest's asking terms ahead of the next transfer window.

His value almost certainly would have dropped over the last few months, but the Reds would still need a decent fee to let him go this season.
