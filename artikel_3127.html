TeraWulf Inc. (WULF +10.66%) reported third-quarter results that demonstrate a transition toward long-term contracted high-performance computing revenue as the primary driver of its financial profile. Management reported that the company achieved a critical milestone with the CB3 building at Lake Mariner reaching full operation, while construction remains on track for the CB4 and CB5 buildings. The company stated it has secured the necessary equity and investment-grade credit support to move the Justified Data Campus into full-scale construction, supported by a definitive engineering, procurement, and construction agreement with Fluor. Furthermore, the expansion of contracted power at the Muskie site provides a path to 1 gigawatt of capacity, reinforcing the firm's focus on securing scarce energy infrastructure to meet robust demand for artificial intelligence workloads.
