What's the series history between FSU and Boston College

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Florida State's ninth game of the season is against the Boston College Eagles. The Seminoles and Eagles have been playing for many years, but what is the full series history between these two? Here's the full breakdown.

Florida State's record vs. Boston College is 16-6, as they meet nearly every year in ACC play. In their last appearance, the Seminoles lost to Boston College 28-13 in 2023. Some guy named Tommy Castellanos threw for 106 yards and two touchdowns for the Eagles. On the other hand, D.J. Uiagalelei went for 272 yards, one touchdown, and one pick.

Largest margin of victory: FSU 51, Boston College 7 (2012)

Smallest margin of victory: FSU 22, Boston College 21 (2018)

The Seminoles have a positive record against ACC teams since joining the conference in 1991 after being independent in football for 40 years. Per StatMuse, Florida State is 226-90-2 against the ACC. They average 31.7 points per game when facing an ACC team and have a strong record against many of their conference opponents.

FSU will look to get its first win on the road since 2023 and move to 4-5 this season. Fans can catch the game on Saturday at noon ET on the ACC Network.
