Indiana basketball score vs. Arkansas tonight: Where to watch, time, IU roster, radio

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

NEW YORK — The Indiana Hoosiers (4-0) head to Madison Square Garden tonight, Nov. 17, to take on the Arkansas Razorbacks (3-1). The Hoosiers held off Syracuse in a close game last week, and Arkansas is coming off a blowout loss to top-10 Virginia.

Six IU players average at least 9.5 points: Bryce Lindsay (13.8 points), Darren Harris (13.5), Markus Burton (13.3 points with 5.0 assists), Jaeden Mustaf (10.5 points, 4.0 rebounds), Aiden Sherrell (9.5 points, 5.3 rebounds) and Samet Yigitoglu (9.5 points, 6.0 rebounds).

Jeremiah Wilkinson (14.5 points, 46.4% 3-pointers), Billy Richmond III (14.0 points, 5.5 rebounds, 42.9% 3s) and Jordan Smith Jr. (13.5 points) power the Razorbacks.
