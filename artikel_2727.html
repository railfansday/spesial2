After record-breaking contract, Cowboys kicker Brandon Aubrey is having his worst season

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

In April, the Cowboys signed kicker Brandon Aubrey to a four-year, $28 million contract, making him the highest-paid kicker in NFL history. That decision has not aged well.

Aubrey, who missed an extra point in Sunday's loss to the 49ers, is having the worst season of his NFL career this year.

Aubrey has made just 79.2 percent of his field goals this season, the worst mark of his career. And he's made just 92.6 percent of his extra points this season, also the worst mark of his career.

Those numbers aren't just bad for the highest-paid kicker in the league, they're bad for an average kicker. The league average field goal success rate this season is 84.5 percent, and the league average extra point success rate this season is 95.2 percent. Aubrey ranks 21st in the NFL in field goal percentage and 24th in extra point percentage.

Aubrey does still have a strong leg, as evidenced by the 60-yarder he hit against the Ravens. But in today's NFL, plenty of kickers have strong legs, and many of them are kicking long field goals more accurately than Aubrey is: On field goals from 50 yards and longer, Aubrey is 5-for-10 this year. There's nothing special about making 50 percent of your kicks from 50 yards out; there are 25 kickers in the NFL who have made more than 50 percent of their field goal attempts from beyond 50 yards this season.

Ups and downs for kickers aren't unusual, but this is by far the lowest "down" of Aubrey's career. And it's happening after the Cowboys made him the highest-paid kicker in NFL history.
