Ravens K Tyler Loop could be headed for injured reserve with groin injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

When it comes to injuries, the Ravens have far bigger issues than having a healthy kicker. Still, their kicker isn’t healthy.

Coach Jesse Minter told reporters on Monday that Tyler Loop has “been dealing with something,” and that he could “potentially may go on IR.”

If he has been dealing with something, the Ravens haven’t disclosed it. Loop had appeared on none of the injury reports issued through eight weeks of the 2026 season.

The first injury report released in advance of Thursday night’s game against the Jaguars reveals that Loop has a right groin injury.

Loop missed a field goal in Buffalo on Sunday, his fourth of the season.

Jake Moody is currently on the Baltimore practice squad. He’d presumably be signed to the 53-man roster if/when Loop ends up on injured reserve.
