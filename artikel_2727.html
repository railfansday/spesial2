Recap: Wizards drop eighth straight with 136-120 loss to Grizzlies

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Washington Wizards couldn't hold onto a 12-point second-quarter lead Monday night, as the Memphis Grizzlies outscored them by 24 in the third quarter to pull away for a 131-120 victory and hand Washington its eighth consecutive loss.

Washington opened the game with a 35-33 lead, fueled by 10 first-quarter points from Anthony Davis who connected on 4-of-6 field goals and knocked down a pair of free throws.

AJ Dybantsa and Trae Young each knocked down a three-pointer, while Khris Middleton added two more from beyond the arc. Young, who went scoreless in Sunday's game against the Houston Rockets, also dished out five assists in the opening quarter, helping set the pace for Washington's offense.

Seven different Wizards scored in the first quarter, including 15 points from the bench, and Washington shot 55.6% from distance.

Washington went back and forth with Memphis to start the second quarter, but Young continued to orchestrate the offense. He added five more assists to reach 10 for the half, becoming the second player in the NBA this season to record 10+ assists before halftime.

Dybantsa put together an impressive second quarter. He caught a lob from Young, threw down a put-back dunk and picked Cam Spencer's pocket before finishing the ensuing fast break with a two-handed slam. The rookie scored 12 of his 15 first-half points in the second quarter, helping Washington build a 69-59 halftime lead with a 16-4 run.

But that lead quickly evaporated as Memphis broke out to a 12-4 run to start the third quarter, which was escalated by two three-pointers from Cameron Boozer and one from Coward. Washington's defensive strategy was to give Boozer, who entered the game as a 28.4% shooter from distance, but he connected on six triples at a 50% clip midway through the third to help Memphis take an 81-79 lead.

The Grizzlies outscored Washington 42-18, and contributed 19 points from the bench to enter the fourth quarter with a 101-87 lead. For the second time in two games Washington gave up 40+ points in a quarter after giving up 46 points to the Rockets in the third quarter on Sunday night. Memphis shot 60% (6-10 3PT) from distance in the third and outscored Washington 20-6 in the paint.

Memphis maintained its lead through the final buzzer, remaining undefeated on the road after Washington failed to recover from the Grizzlies' third-quarter surge. Boozer led Memphis with a season-high 29 points, and recorded seven rebounds, seven assists and shot 7-of-13 from distance. Zach Edey (23 points, 10 rebounds) and Javon Small (12 points, 12 assists) each recorded double-doubles.

The Grizzlies finished with 42 assists on 53 made field goals.

Anthony Davis led the Wizards with 23 points, four rebounds and two blocks. AJ Dybantsa added 21 points, five rebounds and two steals, while Kyshawn George finished with 18 points and six rebounds.

Trae Young recorded an 13-point, 12-assist double-double, his sixth of the season, Bilal Coulibaly added 11 points and Tre Johnson and Khris Middleton each contributed nine points off the bench.

Riding an eight-game losing streak, Washington travels to Toronto to face the Raptors at 7:30 p.m. ET on Wednesday night on Monumental Sports Network.
