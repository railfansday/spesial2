Greg Rousseau ties Mario Williams for seventh in Bills history

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Buffalo Bills outside linebacker Greg Rousseau moved into a tie for seventh place on the team's all-time sacks list on Thanksgiving night. Rousseau sacked quarterback Patrick Mahomes on a 3rd & 16 midway through the third quarter on Thanksgiving night to bring his career total to 43, catching former Bills defensive end Mario Williams.

Williams spent four seasons (2012-2015) in Buffalo, recording double-digit sacks in each of his first three years with the team. His best season came in 2014 when he recorded 14.5 sacks and earned first-team All-Pro honors.

Rousseau, now in his sixth NFL season, has spent his entire career in Buffalo after the Bills selected him with the 30th overall pick in the 2021 NFL Draft. Having already established a new personal single-season record for sacks this year, the 26 year old is now working his way through some familiar names in Buffalo's history books.

Once Rousseau gets past Mario Williams, there's another Williams waiting for him. Former defensive tackle Kyle Williams (48.5), who spent all 13 of his NFL seasons in Buffalo, sits sixth on the franchise's all-time list.

With six regular-season games remaining, and the way Rousseau has been getting to opposing quarterbacks lately, we might not have to wait until next season to talk about him catching another Williams.
