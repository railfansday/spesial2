‘They could’: DeMarcus Cousins says what could happen to Warriors now after Steph Curry’s injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Golden State Warriors have confirmed that Steph Curry has suffered an injury.

The veteran superstar looked to be in pain against the Houston Rockets, and medical checks were subsequently performed.

An MRI showed that Curry had suffered an oblique abdominal tear, and the official statement from the Warriors claimed that he will be reevaluated in two weeks.

That could be a huge blow for Golden State, and DeMarcus Cousins believes it could even have a big impact on their season.

Curry has had an incredible start to the new season.

The 38-year-old has averaged nearly 28 points per game this season and has produced multiple incredible performances for the Warriors. He has undoubtedly been their best player.

Now, the Warriors will be without him for at least the next two weeks, which means he will miss a minimum of six games in that period.

Cousins believes that is a huge problem for the Warriors and has suggested that they could go on a lengthy losing run without their talisman.

He said on the Run It Back show: "This was kind of the writing on the wall, especially with the way he had been playing. You have your oldest guy on your team who's putting up historic numbers this late in his career.

"Obviously, if he was to go down, it's going to plummet the rest of the team. You just can't go find 30 to 35 points on the bench on a random night. So, it's going to be tough for the Warriors. This is part of the issue I had with how they built this team.

"I don't think at this stage of his career, he's supposed to have this much responsibility to carry this team every night. He should have better company around him to carry the load. Obviously, him going down is going to change the look of the Warriors.

"They could possibly go on a pretty long losing streak. From a team that was top-three, top-four, five in the West, you could easily see them drop down to the 9th or 10th seed."

Curry's absence will increase the pressure on the other players on the roster, like Yaxel Lendeborg.

The rookie has been unbelievable so far, but if he can fill Curry's void until his return, Lendeborg will be viewed very differently.
