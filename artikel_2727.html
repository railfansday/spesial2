Where Duke soccer is seeded in NCAA Tournament

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Duke women's soccer earned a No. 3 seed in the 64-team 2026 NCAA women's soccer tournament bracket announced Monday, Nov. 16.

The Blue Devils (10-3-5) will host UNC Wilmington in the first round at Freeman Field at Koskinen Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 21, at 2 p.m. ET. The winner will advance to face either No. 6 Michigan or Western Michigan.

Duke is making its 31st NCAA Tournament appearance after reaching the College Cup in each of the past two seasons. The Blue Devils fell in the semifinals both times and have advanced to the College Cup six times in program history.
