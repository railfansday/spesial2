With the 50th anniversary of “Star Wars” approaching in 2027, Disney and Lucasfilm are preparing for a yearlong celebration of the franchise, complete with new merchandise, immersive experiences and a theatrical re-release of George Lucas’ original 1977 film.

Lego is releasing seven commemorative sets on Jan. 1 of next year, spanning collectible helmets, vehicles and display models inspired by the original trilogy. Hasbro, meanwhile, has opened preorders for new figures across its Black Series, Vintage Collection and Retro Collection lines. The larger rollout will also see collaborations with brands including Adidas, Aston Martin and Steinway & Sons.
