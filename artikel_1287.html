Richards: Harry Maguire Won't Fit Into Mauricio Pochettino Plans at Man Utd

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Manchester United have confirmed that Mauricio Pochettino will be the new first-team manager, replacing Michael Carrick, following the international break.

The Argentine coach has agreed a two-and-a-half-year deal at Old Trafford to finally become the boss, having been considered for the role a number of times since 2016, but always being overlooked.

Pochettino will officially take over on November 18th, after taking charge of the USMNT's Nations League double-header against Haiti on Saturday and Tuesday, before jetting into Manchester to prepare for his first game in charge against Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday afternoon.

The 54-year-old will take training for the first time on Thursday, with the full squad expected to be available barring any injured stars, following the international break, and will immediately start to look to implement his style of play and philosophy.

That usually includes high-intensity running, pressing high, and a high defensive line to squeeze the pitch and dominate possession. And while discussing his appointment on The Rest is Football, former Man City and England defender Micah Richards admitted that he is worried for one star's fit in that system.

"In terms of style of play, have they got the players? They've got a few players who can compress high. I think.

"I think it would worry me. Someone like Harry Maguire, I love Harry Maguire in terms of his organization. And when he was really good at Leicester, I thought was when it was a little bit deeper, and then he could maraud forward.

"You know, I think if you're asking Harry Maguire to play a high line, it doesn't suit his game."

Man Utd's season so far has been awful. They sit 18th in the Premier League table with just one win from their 10 games, while they have also lost two of their four Champions League games, leaving qualification up in the air.

Carrick was someone who wasn't the most astute tactically, and he seemed to rely more on trust and belief to win games. That ran out, and results fell off a cliff as a result. Pochettino must now come in and rock the boat a bit, and the changes that are needed must be made no matter what.

That means he will need to be ruthless with dropping players from the team and making certain demands, and if he feels that Maguire can't help his team, then he shouldn't play. Lisandro Martinez, Matthijs De Ligt, Leny Yoro, and Ayden Heaven are all more than capable of filling in if that is the case, and Pochettino must do whatever it takes to get the team winning games again.

Maguire is currently in the final year of his contract at Old Trafford, and the chances of a renewal at this stage seem slim. If that is the case, then moving on from him six months earlier than his departure may be best for everyone involved too.
