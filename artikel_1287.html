Cole Palmer’s ‘Private’ Stance on Joining Man Utd Already Emerged

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Mauricio Pochettino's appointment at Manchester United has re-ignited speculation that Cole Palmer could be a transfer target for the Red Devils in the future.

The boyhood United fan signed for Chelsea from Manchester City during Pochettino's only summer transfer window in west London. Palmer's only season under the Argentine coach was incredible, as he bagged 22 goals and 11 assists in 33 Premier League games.

Palmer has been open about the fact that he supported Man Utd as a child, and he has previously been linked with a move back to Manchester. But what has he said about a potential transfer previously?

Back in April 2026, Palmer was directly asked about links to his boyhood club. The England international laughed off the suggestion, as he said:

"When I see it I just laugh. Obviously Manchester is my home. All my family are there, but I don’t miss it. Maybe I’ll miss it if I don’t go for three months or something. But then when I get home I think there’s nothing there for me anyway."

He went on to state that he had no intention of leaving Stamford Bridge, adding: "I’ve got no plans to move from Chelsea. We’ve still got a lot to play for. We’ve got the FA Cup semi-final [against Leeds] and if we finish in a Champions League spot it puts us in a good position to sign players that we need."

Mauricio Pochettino could have Man Utd back at the top of he's allowed to build this XI.

Chelsea ended up missing out on European football altogether, but Xabi Alonso's appointment brought new excitement. Palmer confirmed that talks with Reece James and the owners left him feeling confident about the future.

"We spoke to the owners, and they’re sure of the players that are gonna do it. Reece won’t sign a six-year contract if he’s not spoken to the owners and the directors.

"Me and Reece spoke a lot. About things we need, players we need to sign and how things need to be. He wouldn’t sign a new contract if he didn’t know what was going on."

He has since put pen to paper on a new contract, meaning any potential deal would be incredibly difficult to pull off. However, the Daily Mail reported over the summer that Palmer would privately be open to a switch to Old Trafford if it became a possibility.

Speaking at the 2024 London Football Awards, Palmer waxed lyrical about his manager at the time. "He speaks to you about anything, like family issues,” the midfielder said (per The Standard).

He continued: "I think if he knows what's going on off the pitch, it can affect how he is with you on the pitch. He's really great at that."

The 2023-24 campaign under Pochettino remains the standout year of Palmer's career to date. It's no surprise to hear the Englishman was 'loving' life under his old boss. He added:

"He's given me the freedom to play my stuff and I like it when he frees you because I feel I can express my stuff more.

"I'm loving it. Every day he's talking to you about different things, not just football things but life things as well. He's good at making you feel good and trying to get the best out of you."
