We want Argentina fans to fall in love with us in post-Messi era – Emi Martinez

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Emiliano Martinez says Argentina's squad wants supporters to fall in love with the team all over again in the post-Lionel Messi era.

Argentina's all-time leading goalscorer Messi announced his retirement from international football earlier this year after 20 years representing his country.

Messi won one World Cup and two Copas America with La Albiceleste, while he also played in two further World Cup finals in 2014 and 2026.

A new-look Argentina thrashed Costa Rica 7-0 in a friendly in Cordoba on Tuesday, with Nico Paz, Julian Alvarez, and Franco Mastantuono each scoring twice, and Alexis Mac Allister also on target.

Speaking after the victory, Martinez – back in action after a broken jaw suffered playing for Chelsea – said: "We want to make the fans fall in love with the team all over again.

"We knew we had to give a little extra effort, and we wanted to start winning in this new phase now without Leo."

— Selección Argentina in English (@AFASeleccionEN) November 18, 2026

Paz has been in excellent form for Como, and the attacking midfielder is fast establishing himself as one of the most talented footballers in world football.

There have been suggestions the 22-year-old could be next in line to succeed Messi by wearing the famous number 10 jersey for Argentina.

But Paz was reluctant to comment on that, saying: "The truth is, I don't think about that [the chance to take the vacant number 10 shirt.

"What I think about is enjoying and having fun on the pitch because what I love most is playing football, and on top of that representing the national team and so many people.

"I am very happy; representing these colours is the ultimate goal. Especially on days like today, with all the people supporting us and all the love that was felt today."
