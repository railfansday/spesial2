Easiest way to get the Pirate Token Shop in Fortnite Treasure Hunters

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Treasure Hunters mini-season has sent all Fortnite players on a wild hunt, as the Pirate Shop has several premium items for you to redeem at zero cost. Typically, you’ll need to spend V-Bucks to buy these items, but you can get all of this for free for a limited period of time. It depends on you accessing the Pirate Token Shop first.

To raise your chances, you will have to open as many regular chests as possible during your adventures. You can, however, accelerate the process with a few simple tricks.

To get the tokens, you have to open as many chests as possible. That’s the only way to obtain it. However, here’s how you can strategize the whole process.

First, you have to drop on the map and look in areas that have a higher concentration of these chests. If you visit a spot that has a limited number of chests, chances are that someone else might have opened them and received the rewards. More chests provide you with more opportunities to find the tokens.

Next, you’ll want to get the Plundarggh Sprite. This sprite increases your movement speed whenever you open a chest. This fits nicely into our loop since we will open as many chests as possible. Doing so will increase your speed, and this allows you to save time while traveling between different chests.

Finally, you’ll want to play in squad fill if you don’t have friends. All members of the party will get the access tokens if one of you gets it from the chest. Hence, playing in a party makes complete sense since you can open the number of chests three times in a round, which you might not be able to do alone.

Ultimately, it will boil down to your luck when it comes to unlocking the Pirate Shop. Once done, you’ll have 14 days to redeem all the available rewards and applicable cosmetics.
