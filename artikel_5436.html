VT Markets Integrates Global Market Insights Directly into ChatGPT and Claude

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

SYDNEY, Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- VT Markets, a leading multi-asset broker, today announced the launch of its official plugin for ChatGPT and Claude, bringing real-time asset tracking and economic intelligence directly into conversational AI platforms.

The new integration allows traders, investors, and market observers to analyse global assets without leaving their chat interface or switching between financial dashboards.

Designed to streamline daily research, the VT Markets plugin provides comprehensive data and visual analytics across forex, commodities, global indices, and equities. Users can instantly surface price movements, highlight top gainers and losers, track market sentiment, and generate visual charts on demand.

The plugin also helps traders stay ahead of market catalysts. From central bank decisions to corporate earnings, users can immediately check upcoming schedules and prepare for potential price movements ahead of major market shifts.

With this integration, VT Markets puts comprehensive market intelligence right where daily research happens. By removing friction from financial analysis, the ChatGPT and Claude plugins equip market participants worldwide to evaluate global assets, track macro trends, and turn raw data into actionable insights instantly. Looking ahead, VT Markets plans to roll out secure account connectivity for the plugins, allowing traders to view their portfolios and prepare orders directly within the chat.
