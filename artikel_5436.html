Nvidia stock falls about 8% as CEO Jensen Huang steps down

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Nvidia's stock decline buts the company on track to have lost almost three-quarters of a trillion dollars in market value since the scandal broke

Nvidia $NVDA CEO Jensen Huang resigned on Thursday, and the chipmaker's stock fell about 8% in morning trading.

Huang stepped down as CEO and as a member of the board, Nvidia said in a statement. Colette Kress, the chief financial officer, will step in as interim CEO while a successor is found. Harvey C. Jones, who previously led Synopsys $SNPS, becomes Nvidia's first board chair and will direct the effort to find a permanent CEO, which will weigh candidates from inside and outside the company, the company said.

Huang's resignation follows reports of two extramarital affairs. Huang, 63, admitted to infidelity in a note sent to employees on Sunday. That relationship was with Alice Huang, a former executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed, a major Nvidia customer. U.S. intelligence officials suspect she is an agent of Beijing’s spy agencies. (Jensen Huang and Alice Huang are not related.)

The second affair was with Kimberly Powell, according to The Wall Street Journal and other outlets. Powell, who runs Nvidia's healthcare business, has not commented publicly.

Nvidia stock was off about 12% in premarket trading before recovering some of those losses to trade down about 8% shortly after the opening bell. The stock had already slid 7% for the week through Wednesday's close and has fallen by more than a fifth in the past month.

The Nasdaq $NDAQ was off about 0.6% and the S&P 500 was down 0.2%. Shares of AMD $AMD and Broadcom $AVGO gained.

Nvidia's stock decline buts the company on track to have lost almost three-quarters of a trillion dollars in market value since the scandal broke.

Huang co-founded Nvidia in 1993. The company's revenue for fiscal 2026 reached $215.9 billion, a 702% increase over the $26.9 billion it booked in fiscal 2022.

The company is set to report third-quarter earnings on Tuesday.
