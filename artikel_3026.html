Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Used electronics demand surged as new device prices climbed, lifting profits

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 19, 2026 at 1:39PM EST

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

ATRenew (RERE +0.63%) management reported a significant expansion in both total revenue and profitability during the third quarter, with non-GAAP operating income rising 50.7% year over year. The company stated that growth was primarily supported by its first-party product sales and the continued expansion of its compliant refurbishment business. Management indicated that the pre-owned consumer electronics market is currently benefiting from rising prices of new devices, which has improved the value retention of used products. The company also announced it has submitted a confidential application for a listing on the Main Board of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange to broaden its investor base across Asia.
