Here Are All the Surprise Star Cameos in Taylor Swift’s 2026 CMA Awards Performance

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

From host Lainey Wilson to Sugarland and beyond, country stars took part in Swift's "I Knew It, I Knew You" and "Our Song" set.

Man, it’s been awhile – 13 years to be exact – since Taylor Swift last performed at the CMA Awards, but when she returned to take the stage at the Bridgestone Arena in Nashville on Wednesday (Nov. 18), she did it in style and made sure it sparkled not only thanks to her sequined outfit, but with literal stars – country music stars, that is.

The whimsical performance – a mashup of her Toy Story 5 hit (which has topped the Billboard Hot 100 for two weeks so far) “I Knew It, I Knew You” and “Our Song” (No. 16 peak) from her self-titled 2006 debut album – kicked off backstage in her dressing room. Eagle-eyed Swifties likely noticed that quite a few of her debut-era outfits were strategically placed around the space. But look a little closer, and viewers might see that she had a very small but special – albeit not breathing – guest in the room: Jessie the doll from the Toy Story franchise.

That was just the first of numerous surprise sightings throughout the performance, which included several nods to the film series, including the backup dancers who were an homage to Woody, the blue sky and fluffy white clouds on the front of her guitar that harken back to the wallpaper in Andy’s room, the tree on stage that brought viewers back to Tire Swing Hill, and of course, the 14-time Grammy winner’s glittery, Jessie-inspired cowgirl outfit.

And as Swift left her dressing room and moved through the backstage area as she effortlessly sang “I Knew It, I Knew You,” country greats such as Little Big Town, Brooks & Dunn, host Lainey Wilson, Eric Church and “Whispering Bill” Anderson all greeted her on the way, with Keith Urban waiting on the Toy Story-inspired stage to perform alongside her.

Take a closer look and you’ll see that the Jessie doll from the Toy Story franchise is sitting on the couch between some of the pop superstar’s outfits from her debut era.

Swift kept Paisley busy, as the “Whiskey Lullaby” crooner donned some rubber gloves to polish her CMA Awards trophies.

Cheese! The pop superstar struck a pose with Little Big Town for a quick snap.

Sugarland made a short and sweet appearance.

The singer helped move Brooks & Dunn’s card game along as she made her way through the backstage area during her set.

Swift plopped Kix Brooks’ burgundy cowboy hat on Lainey Wilson’s head, while the CMA Awards host handed the singer a pair of sunglasses.

Swift stopped for a quick and cool fist bump with Eric Church.

“Whispering Bill” was all smiles as he handed the superstar artist her sparkling Toy Story-themed guitar.

Urban was the only country artist to join Swift for the onstage part of her performance.
