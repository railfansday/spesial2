‘Furious’ Creator Elizabeth Meriwether to Receive ATX TV Festival’s 2027 Showrunner Award

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Elizabeth Meriwether is set to receive the 2027 Showrunner Award at next year’s ATX Television Festival, Variety has learned.

Meriwether will take part in a conversation onstage at the festival, which runs June 3-6, 2027, in Austin, TX. It will be her first time appearing at the festival.

The Showrunner Award is meant to recognize those who “foster an environment of collaboration and creativity in their role as showrunner, and whose creative voice has a singular and expansive influence on TV,” per the official description. Past recipients include David E. Kelley and Bill Lawrence. “ATX TV Festival is such a special gathering of people who truly love television and truly love Texas BBQ, and I am deeply grateful for this honor,” Meriwether said.

Meriwether most recently created and served as showrunner on the Hulu series “Furious” which starred Emmy Rossum as an FBI agent hunting a serial killer played by Lola Petticrew. The show was loosely inspired by the 1987 film “Black Widow” and was renewed for a second season in August.

Meriwether is also known creating the hit Fox single-cam comedy “New Girl,” which ran for seven seasons. She is also behind the Emmy-winning Hulu liminted series “The Dropout” starring Amanda Seyfried, which delved into the life and career of Elizabeth Holmes. Her other credits include the acclaimed series “Dying for Sex” at FX.

“From the pure joy of ‘New Girl,’ to the righteous anger of ‘Furious,’ to the sharp surreality and cultural relevance of ‘The Dropout,’ there are few writers in television that are willing – and able – to consistently challenge our expectations in the way that Liz’s stories do,” said Jenn Morgan, head of programming for the ATX TV Festival. “As a showrunner, she continues to prove that creative risks are worth taking, and that collaboration is essential to producing TV that stands the test of time. We’re thrilled and honored to have Liz join us next summer, and to celebrate her remarkable career thus far.”
