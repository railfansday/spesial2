Why Jordan Klepper thinks were living in "dumb McCarthyism"

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

On Mashable's Say More, hosts Kristy Puchko (Mashable's Entertainment Editor) and Mark Stetson (Senior Creative Producer) bring humor and their trusted insights to the biggest shows, films, digital trends, and cultural moments. From viral-worthy rants and passionate raves to smart recaps and first-look teasers, they cover what everyone is talking about. In this episode of Mashable's Say More, Executive Producer Mark Stetson sits down with comedian and Daily Show host Jordan Klepper to discuss his new special, The Daily Show Presents: Jordan Klepper Fingers the Pulse: Red Flags. Klepper shares the improv techniques he uses to disarm attendees at Trump rallies, unpacks the right's obsession with the "communism" boogeyman, and reflects on the exhausting reality of what he calls "dumb McCarthyism." From his character-driven days on The Opposition to his current need for comedic authenticity in a post-truth world, Klepper offers a candid, behind-the-scenes look at the challenges, frustrations, and absurdities of political satire today.

The special is now available on Paramount+, The Daily Show’s YouTube channel, and VOD.
