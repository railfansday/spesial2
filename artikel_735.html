‘Cliff Booth’ First Reactions: Brad Pitt’s Sequel Is an ‘Absolute Triumph’ and a ‘Wildly Entertaining’ ‘Love Letter’ to Hollywood

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

David Fincher, Brad Pitt and Quentin Tarantino‘s long-awaited “Once Upon a Time in Hollywood” sequel movie, officially titled “The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth,” has been unveiled to members of the film press, who are calling the Netflix-backed tentpole a “wildly entertaining” “hangout movie” that is already one of the year’s best.

“David Fincher’s The Further Mis-Adventures of #CliffBooth pops, throbs and crackles,” wrote film critic Simon Thompson. “Like Once Upon a Time… this is a love letter to LA. An eclectic, ragtag cast make this a slick and sexy romp. Tomatometer critic Zach Pope called the film “a PURE cinematic trifecta of Tarantino’s signature chaos, Fincher’s meticulous attention to detail, and Brad Pitt’s effortlessly charismatic Cliff Booth.”

He continued: “All wrapped into a stylish love letter to 1970s Hollywood cinema that reminds us sometimes all you need is a laid-back, wildly entertaining misadventure with a character you just LOVE spending time with. That just might turn into insanity. It’s cool, chaotic, charming, and never tries to be more than it needs to be. If you loved Once Upon a Time in Hollywood, this one’s absolutely for you. Just as it was for me.”

The Wrap’s Senior Film Reporter Drew Taylor “absolutely loved” the film. “Tarantino and Fincher are like peanut butter and chocolate (the peanut butter is profane and the chocolate dipped in blood). Stop watching anything, go see it on the biggest screen around, have the time of your life. An absolute triumph. The best,” he wrote.

“The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth is an absolute banger,” wrote Critics Choice member Ricky Válero. “An aesthetically rich film that beautifully captures the relationship between Los Angeles and the movies. Brad Pitt is as charismatic as ever. Truly one of the year’s best films.”

Variety’s Clayton Davis described the film as a “wild, hilarious hangout movie.” “Brad Pitt slides back into his beloved Oscar-winning role and gives his best performance since “Once Upon a Time in…Hollywood.” Elizabeth Debicki brings an intoxicating sensuality, and Scott Caan and Peter Weller are such a treat that they could have had triple the screen time,” he continued. “The cinematography is gorgeous. If the 2019 film was a love letter to L.A., this one is a full-on sonnet.

“The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth” features the return of Pitt as the eponymous stuntman. The movie is set in 1977, eight years after the events of “Once Upon a Time in Hollywood,” and follows Cliff as a Hollywood studio fixer who gets in way over his head while working for a popular movie actor. The supporting cast includes Brad Pitt, Elizabeth Debicki, Scott Caan, Yahya Abdul-Mateen II, Carla Gugino and Peter Weller, among others.

Pitt won the Oscar for best supporting actor for playing Cliff Booth in “Once Upon a Time in Hollywood,” which Sony released in theaters in June 2019. The movie was acclaimed and earned a strong $410 million at the worldwide box office. “Hollywood” went on to earn 10 Oscar nominations, including best picture and best director.

“The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth” features a screenplay by Tarantino, who opted not to direct the movie because, per his retirement plan, he only plans on directing one more feature film before he leaves the director’s chair for good. The first draft of the sequel script clocked in at a whopping 230 pages, Fincher revealed to Empire magazine last month. The director cut 60 pages.
