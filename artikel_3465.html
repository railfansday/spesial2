Boy Was ‘Begging for His Life’ Before Father Killed Family in Murder-Suicide

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Authorities in Florida revealed additional heartbreaking details of a 911 call before a father killed his wife and three young children in a murder-suicide.

The Hillsborough County Sheriff’s Office confirmed that Isaac Luechtefeld, and his wife, Raquel Luechtefeld, were found dead alongside their children, Enoch, 5, Hannah, 3, and Job, 1, at their home in Plant City, Florida, on Thursday, November 19.

Investigators said they believe Isaac, 34, fatally shot his family inside their home before he died by suicide.

Dispatchers received a 911 call from the home around 3:30 p.m. on Thursday and reported hearing gunshots in the background.

“It’s an open line, and we can hear a male’s voice say, ‘Sorry, buddy.’ And we hear a loud bang,” Hillsborough County Sheriff Chad Chronister said during a news conference on Thursday.

An open-line call connects to 911 without the caller speaking, which allows dispatchers to hear background noises.

“In the distance, we can hear a small child begging for his life,” Chronister continued.

The child was then heard begging his father to spare his life. “Daddy, please don’t shoot me. Daddy, please don’t kill me,” the sheriff said of the child’s pleas.

Chronister said that the child repeated the pleas three or four times before dispatchers said they heard another loud bang.

Deputies arrived at the home just 11 minutes after the 911 call came through at 3:40 p.m. Once they got to the home, officers spotted one of the children lying lifeless on the floor and they forced their way into the residence.

Chronister went on to say that all five family members were found dead with gunshot wounds.

“When you become a deputy sheriff, you expect to see awful things, but what the deputies encountered here today is nothing short of pure evil,” Chronister added.

Despite confirming details of the horrific killings, Chronister did not state if authorities have established a motive. He also said that they are still trying to figure out what incidents led up to the murder-suicide.

Many of the first responders and the 911 dispatcher were left in shock over the killings, and they were required to “take a time out.” Those employees were also connected with the department’s peer support team and were given other resources to help them process the tragedy.

“There are no words that can capture the depth of this tragedy,” Chronister said in a press release when the sheriff’s office first announced the murder-suicice “A family has been lost, and our hearts are with their loved ones, friends, and everyone in our community who knew them.”
