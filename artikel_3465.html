The Netflix slate for the week ahead has been confirmed, and as November gets underway, Netflix isn’t waiting any longer to bring the festive cheer. Over the next seven days, there will be plenty to watch on the world’s biggest streaming service, so I’m picking out the highlights.

Before we get into Netflix’s first batch of holiday content, there’s “Mike Tyson: Return of the Mike,” a special that sees the boxing legend take to the stage for a one-man show full of comedy, reflections and untold stories.

Netflix will kick off its Christmas movie slate this week with “A Dog’s Perfect Christmas,” a comedy-drama in which the star of the show isn’t Santa, but man’s best friend. Or for a different breed of seasonal streaming, check out winter-set spy thriller “Black Doves” season 2.

If you love true crime, then “Taken: Natalee Holloway” will be right up your street; it chronicles the decades-long search for a missing girl. Finally, there’s “Carolina Caroline,” a “Bonnie and Clyde” style romantic drama about two lovebirds on a criminal spree across the United States.
