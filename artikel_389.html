China’s Trade Surplus Passes $1 Trillion at Record Pace

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

China’s factories are finding eager buyers worldwide, but the export boom is raising concerns about its impact on trading partner nations.

China’s trade surplus surged past $1 trillion in the first 10 months of this year, eclipsing last year’s record-setting pace as overseas demand for Chinese goods continued to climb steeply, the government announced on Tuesday.

From solar panels to electric cars, China has found strong global interest for green energy products, as Iranian attacks on ships in the Strait of Hormuz have driven up international oil prices. China has also benefited from the boom in artificial intelligence data centers, for which it manufactures much of the equipment, apart from the most powerful semiconductors.

China’s trade surplus reached $1.04 trillion in the first 10 months of the year, the General Administration of Customs said, with exports exceeding imports by $105.3 billion in October. The monthly gap narrowed from September, however, as crude oil import prices remained high and China’s crude oil purchases jumped to the highest level since the start of the Middle East war.

At the same time, China restricted shipments of diesel, jet fuel and other refined oil products to nearby countries last month to conserve domestic supplies.Overall exports climbed 35.2 percent last month from a year earlier, driven in part by strong shipments of cables, racks and other equipment for data centers overseas. But imports jumped 41.6 percent, including substantial demand of electronics for China’s own A.I. infrastructure buildout.

“High-tech products continued to underpin export strength, while import volumes delivered the larger upside surprise,” said a research note from ANZ, a major Australian bank.

The figures came a day after Washington and Beijing took steps to ease tariffs and restrict the use of export controls through Jan. 10, putting into effect an agreement reached by senior officials in late September ahead of a Washington summit between President Trump and Xi Jinping, China’s top leader.
