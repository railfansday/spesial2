The Velvet Flats Trend Is Taking Over Fall 2026—Shop Our Favorite Pairs

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Everyday opulence—with its brocade jackets, tassel-trimmed handbags, and menswear-inspired cummerbunds is one of fall 2026’s biggest trends, and it was only a matter of time before sumptuous velvet flats would enter the wardrobe mix. Celebrities and the street style set are already on board with the look, confirming the decadent footwear trend is having a moment this fall.

An early champion, just recently Jennifer Lawrence stepped out in New York City wearing velvet slippers with a matching velvet housecoat from Colleen Allen and slinky black silk pants. Elsewhere in Los Angeles, Dakota Johnson paired hers with a leather trench and a classic white tee for a low-key outing.

But Lawrence and Johnson’s flats—both likely from The Row—aren’t the only velvet styles to choose from this season. There’s also Reformation’s low-heeled ballerina, or Khaite’s high vamp shoe in a rich burnt orange. There’s even a pair of pointed-toe flats in royal purple from Gianvito Rossi. For a more slipper-like look, Toteme’s velvet flats stay true to the classics, while mules from Dôen and Staud could easily double as your favorite indoor-outdoor shoes.

The trend’s surge feels especially fitting with the holidays right around the corner, as velvet is a texture synonymous with the latter half of the year. Styling-wise, they’ll look chic with an LBD and sheer tights or a festive matching set. Truth be told, though, we love the “everyday” of it all—worn with your trusty 501s and a chunky sweater or tailored trousers and a great piece of fall outerwear.
