Why Marvell Stock Is Down Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Investors appear to be stepping back from AI stocks ahead of Nvidia's earnings, and Marvell's pending all-stock Lumentum deal adds dilution risk on top of that.

Marvell Technology (MRVL -7.27%) stock is down 6.4% as of 1:15 p.m. ET on Monday, Nov. 16, as chip stocks continue to slide in the wake of Jensen Huang's Nvidia (NVDA -4.12%) departure.

The S&P 500 and the Nasdaq Composite are down 1.6% and 2.2%, respectively.

Jensen Huang stepped down as Nvidia CEO last week after multiple reports of extramarital affairs surfaced. One of these was with a woman suspected of being a Chinese spy who helped billions of dollars worth of Nvidia chips find their way into China, in violation of U.S. export controls.

There is a lot riding on Nvidia's earnings report, released tomorrow after the bell. For now, investors are apparently rotating out of the whole AI trade until more clarity emerges. Marvell is far from the only chip stock sliding: Micron (MU -7.62%), Intel (INTC -7.02%), Advanced Micro Devices (AMD -5.58%), and Broadcom (AVGO -3.92%) are all down 4% or more today so far.

Marvell stock has also been under pressure for a few weeks now after announcing a $115 billion all-stock deal to acquire Lumentum (LITE -8.48%) -- a supplier of optical parts for AI data centers. That kind of deal will lead to serious dilution for Marvell shareholders.
