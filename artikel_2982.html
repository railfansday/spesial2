Nextpower: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Supply chain headwinds are pressuring near-term margins, but a backlog-driven Q4 positions the company for cash profitability.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 19, 2026 at 2:30PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Nextpower ›

On its second-quarter fiscal year 2027 earnings call, held on Oct. 28, 2026, Nextpower (NXT -1.65%) announced record quarterly revenue exceeding $1.1 billion alongside a backlog of orders committed by customers worth over $6 billion. The expanding order book and shifting product mix show how the business is evolving beyond its core solar tracker products, which turn panels to follow the sun, into a broader clean energy technology platform.

Three takeaways from the call highlight what matters most for long-term investors.

The company is expanding beyond solar trackers into energy storage and electrical balance of system components, which include the cabling and switches that connect solar arrays to the power grid. Expanding these higher-growth offerings increases revenue per project and deepens customer relationships.

Management completed the acquisition of Zimmermann PV-Steel Group to gain ground-mount structural products and agrivoltaics, which pair solar panels with agricultural farming land. The transaction immediately adds established distribution networks across 15 European countries.

Management projects near-term gross profit margins in the high 20% range due to rising transportation and raw material expenses. A localized manufacturing footprint helps absorb shipping spikes, but elevated logistics expenses will remain a short-term headwind.

Project deliveries are shifting toward the fourth fiscal quarter, positioning Nextpower for record quarterly operational cash profitability. Localized manufacturing networks and expanding product lines give the business a structural advantage as it manages freight inflation and scales domestic inverter production.

Long-term value creation depends on maintaining disciplined capital allocation while expanding cross-selling opportunities across solar and storage product lines.
