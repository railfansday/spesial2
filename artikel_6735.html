Why ‘DWTS’ Fans Think Harry Shum Jr.’s Elimination Ties Back to Some Behind-the-Scenes Drama

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

We’ve finally made it to the Dancing With the Stars finale—and fans aren’t entirely pleased with last night’s results.

The five celebs who are still in the running for the Mirrorball Trophy are Connor Wood, Ezra Frech, Maura Higgins, Amber Glenn, and Tatyana Ali. Which means Harry Shum Jr. has officially been booted from the ballroom—even after his touching homage to his late Glee co-star, Naya Rivera.

While fans are distraught by Harry’s exit, some are convinced that his elimination was driven by some behind-the-scenes drama from earlier in the season.

“Personally, I liked Harry and his dancing,” one fan wrote on Reddit. “Yes, he had dance experience but he was very good and he made things his own. I think he had a great story to tell and genuinely is a good guy.”

They then theorized what might have contributed to his elimination on Tuesday night’s episode—and it all ties back to Jenna Johnson and Val Chmerkovskiy’s drama earlier in the season.

The fan alleged that Val’s Mariah Carey Night braids and social media activity played a part, as well as Jenna and Harry’s LiftGate debacle, which led to Val’s “Fresh Prince” comments toward Carrie Ann Inaba.

“I think because of Jenna’s inability to take criticism from a judge and then Val’s social media (getting dreads and referencing hitting Cari Ann) Harry got voted off. Val only ‘apologized’ when he got pushback from the public,” they pointed out before separately suggesting that Harry might have survived longer with a different pro partner.

While some agreed, others weren’t immediately aligned with the theory.

“Honestly, for both Jenna Dewan and harry I didn’t feel the connection to the viewers as compared to the other dancers,” someone added. “So for me they were easy to not vote for because I didn’t feel anything towards them emotionally.”

Another added, “Harry got voted off because he wasn’t people’s favorite. It’s really not necessarily that deep. Not everyone is chronically online and know what’s going on.”

While none of this is confirmed and it probably came down to votes, Harry seems to be content with his DWTS journey, as he wants to “make more art” with Jenna outside of the show.

“I want to make more art with her, and that's what it's really about. I like making art that people enjoy,” he told E! News after his elimination. “So, to me, I felt that energy [in] the people that I did see and the people that really loved what we were able to do in this dance. I’m now a trained dancer because of her.”
