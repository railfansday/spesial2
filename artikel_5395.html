California AG says Nvidia scandal has raised 'red flags' for his office

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Rob Bonta said his office is weighing whether the law and facts justify action over Jensen Huang's affair and Nvidia's fallout

David Paul Morris/Bloomberg via Getty Images

California Attorney General Rob Bonta said his office is examining parts of the Nvidia $NVDA controversy that touch former CEO Jensen Huang, speaking in an interview on Bloomberg Television on Tuesday.

Bonta characterized the matter as one that "very possibly" falls within what his office might investigate. He said his office is determining whether "the law and facts justify" taking any action, according to Bloomberg. He did not elaborate on his concerns.

"It's something that we're interested in. Red flags have been raised. We're aware and we're looking," Bonta said. "Of course, we can only act when the actions fall within our jurisdiction."

Huang's disclosure of extramarital affairs sent Nvidia stock sharply lower and led to his resignation as CEO, according to Bloomberg. The disclosure also prompted potential national security worries, because reports linked him romantically to a woman American officials suspected of working as an intelligence asset for China.

Beijing has rejected claims that it sought to surveil Huang, according to Bloomberg. Huang said Tuesday that he is willing to return as CEO, though the board is not considering the idea, according to Bloomberg.

Bonta, a Democrat, was returned to office in the vote held earlier this month. He has taken a wider look at Big Tech, according to Bloomberg, and this week sued OpenAI. The suit alleges that the company broke California's unfair-competition statute because it built its AI products in an unsafe manner.

Huang resigned as Nvidia's CEO last Thursday after revelations of two extramarital affairs. One was with Alice Huang, a former executive at Megaspeed, a major Nvidia customer, whom U.S. intelligence officials suspect of being an agent of Beijing's spy agencies. The two are not related. Nvidia CFO Colette Kress is serving as interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent replacement.

China's Foreign Ministry has called the espionage allegation unsubstantiated. Nvidia's board opened an independent investigation, overseen by a committee of independent directors, and retained former acting U.S. Attorney Joon H. Kim and the law firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz to conduct it. The board said it had no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised.

House committees have requested a private session with Jensen Huang on Nov. 18.
