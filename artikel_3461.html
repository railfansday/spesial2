The Foundation Franchise Has Officially Returned With A New Free Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

With the Galactic Empire falling and old powers scrambling to hold on, fans now have another way to explore Isaac Asimov’s Foundation universe. Foundation: Galactic Frontier, the free-to-play strategy RPG from FunPlus, has arrived on Steam, giving PC players another route into a story built around the science-fiction franchise.

There is a small detail worth clearing up, though: this isn’t a new game making its debut. FunPlus first released Galactic Frontier on iOS and Android on October 21 last year, and PC players could already access it through the game’s official website. The recent release brings it to Steam in particular, where it’s listed as free to play. The studio had announced on October 30 that the Steam launch was scheduled for November 6.

Foundation: Galactic Frontier takes place in the Foundation setting, as the Galactic Empire begins to weaken and new groups compete for influence. It draws from both Asimov’s novels and the Apple TV+ series, but narrates an original story within that universe. Players step into the role of an interstellar trader, moving between the Empire, the Foundation and other factions. You’d see familiar concepts like Hari Seldon’s plan and psychohistory in here, but the game’s main story is designed to introduce newcomers to them without requiring prior knowledge of the books or show.

Galactic Frontier makes sure you don’t have to take charge of an entire civilization from the start. Instead, it gives you a ship to build up and a crew to manage. The flagship works as a mobile base, with rooms and facilities that can be expanded. Players recruit different characters, take on trade and bounty missions, and gradually build fleets. The combat is split between space battles and missions on the ground. Fleet fights use three weapon types—Beam, Kinetic and Ion—while ground missions put a squad of three champions into third-person battles on planets and in abandoned space locations.

As an MMO, Foundation: Galactic Frontier, naturally, has a strong multiplayer component. Players can join Commerce Guilds, work with other members to protect trade routes, and compete over ports and territory.

It’s also the perfect time for Foundation: Galactic Frontier to arrive on Steam since its second season (content) began in September for its mobile and native PC version. Called The Usurper Rebellion, the theme introduced the Sivana region, a rival group known as the Ascendancy Court, and magnetic storms that can interfere with fleet performance.

Galactic Frontier is free to download at its core, but don’t forget: It does come with optional in-game purchases. These cover cosmetic changes, progression boosts and quicker access to some characters or ships, but the main story and core systems can be experienced without investing real money.

Coming to how Steam reacted to the release, the early reception has been mixed: the store showed 63 percent positive out of 149 reviews on November 16. While players have praised the game’s story and visuals, many have criticized its pay-to-win mechanics and noted that the overall experience still feels very much like a mobile port.

The verdict may change as more players try the game, but it gives the new PC release for Foundation: Galactic Frontier a more measured start than what its free price tag alone might suggest.
