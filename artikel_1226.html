Rasmus Hojlund Slammed For 6 Words He Shouted at Man City Fans After Goal

Manchester City's struggles continued on Tuesday evening as they were held to a draw by Napoli in their Champions League encounter at the Etihad Stadium.

Enzo Maresca's side went into the match looking to pick up victory for just the second time in six matches.

Man City fell behind in the 55th minute, though, when former Manchester United striker Rasmus Hojlund netted the opener.

Ayyoub Bouaddi restored parity 12 minutes later, only for Napoli to take the lead once more in the 76th minute when Noa Lang found the back of the net.

Rayan Cherki levelled proceedings once more with four minutes remaining but, despite City's best efforts, they couldn't find a winner and the match ended all-square.

After netting the opener, Hojlund, looked up at the Man City supporters behind the goal and decided to taunt them. He appeared to mouth: 'There’s only one club in Manchester.'

Hojlund joined United in 2023 in a £72m move from Atalanta. He played 95 matches for the club and scored 26 times before completing a permanent move to Napoli this summer.

When asked whether that was what he said to the City fans in his post-match interview, he responded:

"Indeed (that's what I said). Some good banter, you know. There's the rivalry between City and United and I have a past with United. That's my club. Nothing against Man City, but United is a big club. It was good to score here."

While United fans would no doubt have loved Hojlund's comments, his words have not gone down well with CBS Sports pundits, Mike Grella, Nigel Reo-Coker and Troy Deeney.

Reacting to the 23-year-old's interview, Grella said: "I'm dumbfounded by that... He's playing for Napoli, who by the way, are in absolute shambles. They may not qualify for the knockout stages. They didn't even win tonight.

"You're also wearing the Napoli badge, they're paying you at the moment. And you're talking about the time that you spent at Manchester United, which, by the way, was another shambolic for you and Manchester United. I think he's really trying to be harmless and funny, but really poor timing and not reading the room."

Deeney added: "They [Manchester United] got rid of you because you're not good enough. You're at a massive club. You're not playing for Mickey Mouse FC down the road. You're playing for Napoli, a huge club with history. You've not only infuriated the rest of us, you've infuriated Napoli fans who are now going to go, this wekeend, after you've come out and questioned the manager's tactics the last time out in the Champions League, you're now saying that that's your club? Off you pop, mate. See you later."

Reo-Coker chimed in: "When footballers make stupid decisions, that's the perfect example. That moment right there. It makes no sense. It makes you look absolutely silly with everything that's gone on. Crazy. He didn't need to say anything like that."

Hojlund has scored four times in 16 matches for Napoli this season. The Italian club are currently 27th in the Champions League league phase table and at risk of failing to qualify for the knockout stages.
