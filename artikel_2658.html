Marlon Humphrey takes part in Wednesday's Ravens practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Cornerback Marlon Humphrey returned to the Ravens’ facility on Monday and he returned to their practice field on Wednesday.

Humphrey was sent home last week and deactivated for the team’s loss to the Panthers after saying that Baltimore was the best at “making criminals” on an episode of his podcast. Humphrey apologized for that comment, but was criticized by the city’s mayor and many others for the way he characterized local residents.

The Ravens welcomed Humphrey back this week, but head coach Jesse Minter did not commit to playing him in this Sunday’s game against the Texans.

Reporters at the open portion of practice also noted that defensive tackle Nnamdi Madubuike and safety Jaylinn Hawkins are not practicing. Both players were out in Week 11.
