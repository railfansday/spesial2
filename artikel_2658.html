PHOTOS: Two NASCAR Tracks Look Almost Unrecognizable as Offseason Begins

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The NASCAR season has barely ended, but two of the sport's most recognizable racetracks already look like completely different places.

Just one week after the 2026 season concluded at Homestead-Miami Speedway, Pocono Raceway and Watkins Glen International are offering fans an unexpected glimpse of what life looks like without stock cars roaring around their famous layouts.

And judging by the latest photos, the offseason has wasted little time making its presence felt.

Both tracks received an early dose of winter weather, leaving grandstands, surrounding hillsides and familiar landmarks covered in snow.

The images, shared by NASCAR on Friday, Nov. 13, capture a side of these facilities that fans rarely get to see during the racing season.

At Pocono Raceway in Pennsylvania, the grandstands that normally welcome thousands of NASCAR fans are sitting empty beneath a fresh blanket of snow.

One particularly striking image shows rows of snow-covered seats stretching alongside the track, with not a spectator in sight.

Another captures snowfall around Pocono's sweeping third turn, where drivers typically battle for position on the way down the frontstretch.

The transformation extends well beyond the racing surface.

Photos also show the facility's entrance surrounded by snow-dusted trees, while the familiar tunnel leading into the infield has taken on an entirely different appearance.

It's a far cry from the sights and sounds of NASCAR's annual summer visit to the 2.5-mile Pennsylvania track.

At Watkins Glen International in upstate New York, the offseason has brought an equally dramatic change of scenery.

The road course, famous for its sweeping corners and elevation changes, is now surrounded by patches of snow stretching across the surrounding landscape.

One photo captures the view beyond Turn 2, where the white-covered grounds stand out against the rolling hills in the distance.

Another shows The Glen Club overlooking a snow-covered property, while a close-up reveals fresh snow resting on the circuit's distinctive light-blue guardrails.

For a track typically associated with high-speed road racing, the quiet winter setting makes for quite a contrast.

The images offer a timely reminder of just how quickly things change once NASCAR's season comes to an end.

Only days ago, the sport was celebrating its newest champions in South Florida. Now, hundreds of miles north, two of its most familiar venues are settling into the offseason.

The engines may be silent for now, but these photos prove NASCAR's racetracks can still put on quite a show without a single car on the track.
