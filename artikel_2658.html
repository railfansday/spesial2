Monday Night Football live updates, TV channel, start time, where to watch NFL game tonight

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

It's mentor vs. mentee to close out Week 10.

"Monday Night Football" brings an AFC matchup as the Los Angeles Chargers visit the Baltimore Ravens, two teams that have faced significant adversity in 2026.

Ravens first-year head coach Jesse Minter spent four seasons in Baltimore as a defensive assistant (2017-20) under John Harbaugh before joining Jim Harbaugh at Michigan as defensive coordinator (2022-23). He followed Harbaugh to the Chargers in 2024, serving in the same role for two seasons (2024-25) before taking over as Baltimore's head coach this year.

The Chargers remain the NFL's only winless team at 0-8, and despite their disastrous start, head coach Jim Harbaugh and the front office stood pat at the trade deadline. That's a surprising decision for a team with several potentially attractive trade chips, including wide receiver Ladd McConkey and pass rusher Khalil Mack.

On the other sideline, Baltimore head coach Jesse Minter faces his former team and mentor, Harbaugh, with the Ravens still fighting to stay in the thick of the AFC playoff picture. However, they'll have to do it without two-time MVP Lamar Jackson, who landed on injured reserve following surgery on his injured right thumb.

Baltimore moved quickly to address the quarterback position, acquiring veteran Jacoby Brissett from the Arizona Cardinals early last week. The move comes after Joe Fagnano struggled mightily in Week 9 while filling in for Jackson and backup Tyler Huntley. Brissett is expected to provide some stability to an offense that will lean heavily on running back Derrick Henry and receiver Zay Flowers.

Can Justin Herbert finally get the Chargers' offense and season moving in the right direction, or will Minter and Brissett spoil Harbaugh's Monday night?
