Patrick Mahomes has quiet Week 12 performance

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Kansas City Chiefs QB Patrick Mahomes completed 16 of his 29 passes for 174 yards and rushed three times for two yards against the Buffalo Bills in Week 12.

Why it Matters: Thanks to some harsh weather conditions, it was a rough showing from the passing attacks on both sides in this one. After throwing 10 combined touchdowns in the previous four games, Mahomes failed to throw a single touchdown for the third time this season. He should have a chance to return to his QB1 form against the Rams in Week 13.
