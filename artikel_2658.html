NFL news, live updates: Chiefs RB Kenneth Walker III out for Week 10, per reports

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The 2026 NFL trade deadline is officially in the rearview mirror, and we're on to Week 10, where we'll see a couple of players in new uniforms this week — and some in their old uniforms.

Kirk Cousins rejoined the Minnesota Vikings this week via a trade with the Las Vegas Raiders. He will officially don purple this weekend against the Green Bay Packers after head coach Kevin O'Connell officially named him the team's Week 10 starter on Friday. He'll make his return to Minnesota against the very team he last faced as a Viking, when he suffered a season-ending Achilles tear. He'll look to give the offense a much-needed boost to complement Brian Flores' defense after the team's 6-2 start.

With the trade of Cousins, the Raiders officially named 2026 No. 1 pick Fernando Mendoza as their starter moving forward. He'll face a tough schedule to kick off his career as a starting quarterback, with the Seattle Seahawks and Denver Broncos on deck in Weeks 10 and 11.

Follow along with Yahoo Sports for the latest NFL news, injury updates and more ahead of Week 10.

After a great deal of speculation was made that Jonathan Taylor could be moved ahead of the NFL trade deadline, the Indianapolis Colts are reportedly standing pat.

The team will not trade him ahead of the deadline, NFL insider Tom Pelissero reported Tuesday afternoon.

Pelissero reported that the Colts had received multiple offers for Taylor of a second-round pick-plus, but instead, the team will move forward with him as a foundational piece within the locker room as they look to rebuild.

Kansas City Chiefs running back Kenneth Walker III will not play in Week 10 after suffering a groin injury in practice this week, NFL insider Jordan Schultz reported Saturday.

Walker leads the NFL with 109 rushing yards per game and is averaging a career-high 5.5 yards per carry. He has seen a heavy workload this season, just 69 carries short of his previous career high for a season, despite being just eight games into the season.
