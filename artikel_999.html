Scotland crumbled to an embarrassing 4-0 defeat to a second-string Switzerland that ended their slim hopes of reaching a Nations League promotion play-off.

With Switzerland already through as group winners, the Tartan Army needed to win in St Gallen while hoping that Slovenia slipped up at North Macedonia to grab a play-off spot.

But a promising start from the visitors was punctured by Lewis Ferguson’s own goal before Sebastien Pocognoli’s team unravelled at the start of the second half, conceding twice more in the space of five minutes.

A late fourth for Switzerland, through Zeki Amdouni’s deflected effort, meant a historic night for Andy Robertson, who overtook Kenny Dalglish to become Scotland men’s record appearance holder with his 103rd cap, ended in disappointment.

Scotland will also fall into Pot 3 of next month’s Euro 2028 qualifying draw, while Pocognoli’s tenure is off to an underwhelming start – with just two wins over North Macedonia after six games.

To add to the ignominy, Switzerland were without many of their first-choice players, with the World Cup quarter-finalists starting with a much-changed line-up, yet were comfortable winners in what was essentially a dead-rubber for them.

Scotland had a decent opening 15 minutes. Their heads went down after Lewis Ferguson’s own goal but the hope ahead of the second half was there was plenty still to play for.

But Scotland were so, so poor after the restarting, sloppy from the whistle and conceding two goals in five minutes. There was no way back from that, and the fourth Switzerland goal was salt in the wounds.

A bad Nations League campaign finishes on the worst possible note for Sebastien Pocognoli. There are big questions to answer now ahead of Euro 2028 qualifying.

Zeki Amdouni is left all alone on the edge of the box and his shot takes a deflection off Graham and beats Gunn.

87 mins: A good save from Gunn to prevent further embarrassment. Sanches worked the space to shoot but the goalkeeper made a smart stop to his right.

86 mins: A McTominay header from a Robertson cross drifts wide as a lone piper solemnly plays the Flower of Scotland.

81 mins: Good grief, how is it not 4-0. Gunn races off his line to reach the long ball to Embolo and gets there first, but then plays a pass straight to Sanches. With the goal open, Sanches puts his effort from long range wide.

76 mins: A nice touch from Gannon-Doak releases Patterson but he looks for Bowie in the box instead of shooting.

It’s been that sort of evening for Scotland.

71 mins: WHAT A MISS! Tierney’s cross falls to McTominay on the volley but he blazes over.

He can’t believe it. Sums up Scotland’s night.

69 mins: Ferguson has a big chance at the back post after Robertson’s deep cross lands favourably, but he pokes the volley over the bar.

65 mins: It could quite easily be 4-0. It may have been ruled out after Ballet caught Tierney in the face, but Ballet could not beat Gunn after breaking through on goal.
