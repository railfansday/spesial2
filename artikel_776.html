SpaceX's Starship Flight 15 is the 15th test flight of Starship, the world's largest and most powerful rocket. SpaceX has designed the rocket to be fully reusable, and has landed its massive Super Heavy first stage booster several times.

Flight 15 is SpaceX's first attempt to land both the Super Heavy booster and its Ship upper stage by returning them to their launch pad and catching them in the pad's metal "Chopstick" arms. The landings will be about 22 hours apart, with Super Heavy landing minutes after launch and the Ship upper stage returning to Starbase on Saturday afternoon.
