Emma Roberts Is in a New League Debuting Trading Card Business

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Emma Roberts and her husband Cody John are ripping into a new business venture, launching a channel to open up trading card packets.

Emma Roberts is ready to rip a pack or two.

The American Horror Story actress revealed that she and husband Cody John are starting a new business venture together: a live channel called Summer Breaks where they will be opening up trading cards packets.

“New job alert,” Roberts posted to Instagram Oct. 31. “Trading cards have become a real passion of mine over the years, and now my husband and I are turning that love into our own trading card show.”

The 35-year-old also joked about her obsession in an Oct. 29 video, quipping, “If there is one thing people know about me, it's that I'm obsessed with cards. Oh, yeah, and I guess I act or whatever sometimes too.”

“That's just a way to buy packs,” she teased. “Thanks, Hollywood.”

She and John—who wed in June—also showed off their love of cards as the Grounded actor excitedly ripped a pack off camera, with Roberts exclaiming, “He really is my soulmate.”

The duo plan to officially launch their channel Nov. 4, although it remains to be seen if any of the cards they pull will be as meaningful as the ones Roberts already has, which she walked fans through in an Oct. 31st Instagram video.

“This card is the love of my life. It's Star Wars Galaxy 2021 Leap of Faith,” Roberts explained. “My husband got me this card, and I literally cried when I opened it.”

Another favorite? A sketch of the Jungle Cruise ride from the Disneyland card set as it’s a favorite of her son Rhodes, 5, whom she shares with ex Garrett Hedlund.

Of course, Roberts is no stranger to putting her surprising obsessions in the spotlight, like when she revealed her penchant for collecting dolls.

"These are the dollykins," Roberts shared with Architectural Digest in April. "I'm madly obsessed with them, in love with them."

And while she keeps her card collection perfectly preserved in card sleeves, she’s far more comfortable taking her dolls out of their boxes, which came as a surprise to John. But as Roberts explained to AD, “I feel like if you're gonna collect dolls, you might as well enjoy them."
