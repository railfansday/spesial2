Brad Pitt Explains Why Leonardo DiCaprio’s Cameo in ‘Cliff Booth’ Movie Fell Apart

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The actor told The Hollywood Reporter at the L.A. premiere that a Rick Dalton appearance in the sequel would have "required a lot of prosthetics."

Though Brad Pitt and Leonardo DiCaprio both starred in Once Upon a Time in Hollywood, only Pitt appears in the sequel, The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth — and the star is shedding some light on why.

Cliff Booth is a follow-up to 2019’s Once Upon a Time, this time centered on Pitt’s stuntman character and set eight years after Quentin Tarantino’s original film. Tarantino wrote the script for the new film with David Fincher taking over as director, and DiCaprio confirmed to Deadline last year that his leading man Rick Dalton is not along for the ride this time, even in a cameo capacity. “There were some talks about it early on. Ultimately, I cannot wait to see the Cliff Booth story, but I’m not in it,” he told the outlet. “I think David Fincher’s the perfect man for the job. Quentin is a huge fan of his work, I’m a huge fan of his work, and there’s nobody better to carry on that lineage and tell that story. I think it’s the next phase of Cliff Booth’s life. I’m excited to see it.”

At the Los Angeles premiere on Monday, Pitt told The Hollywood Reporter that a DiCaprio appearance “would have been great fun, but it required a lot of prosthetics, which is a good couple of weeks prepping to get him. It’s hardly worth the cost for a cameo.” There is a joke in the trailer where Booth explains he no longer doubles for DiCaprio’s character because “Rick got too fat,” which is likely where prosthetics would have come into play.

“It was talked about for a while, but it just seemed like a big ask,” he added, teasing that they still make a couple of “sweet tributes” to Rick Dalton in the sequel.

Both Pitt and DiCaprio earned Oscar nominations for their Once Upon a Time performances, with Pitt going on to win. The follow-up film sees Cliff Booth having left Rick and his stunt career behind, working as a bartender, a projectionist and a Hollywood fixer in the town’s dark underbelly. Elizabeth Debicki, Scott Caan, Carla Gugino, Yahya Abdul-Mateen II and Peter Weller round out the cast.

Also on the carpet, Pitt said he did not expect to reprise the character, but “Quentin was talking about for his 10th one he wanted to do this again — his last film, as he says — and he just wanted to work with friends and do something he loved and I was really flattered. And then he went, ‘Nah!'”

Tarantino has previously put together The Movie Critic as his so-called final film, which later evolved into a Once Upon a Time in Hollywood spinoff that he put on the shelf.

“I don’t bemoan that all because it’s such a commitment, especially for a director. Two years of their lives,” Pitt continued. “And then things sat around for about nine months and Fincher and I were talking about doing something again and it just happened. It occurred to me, gave it to him, called him, and within 24 hours,” they had the movie in motion.

The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth starts its IMAX run on Nov. 25 and starts streaming Dec. 23 on Netflix.
