Nasdaq-100 futures jumped more than 2.3%, leading a broad recovery across U.S. stock markets.

S&P 500 futures climbed above 7,300 as buyers returned after Wednesday’s sharp market swings.

Bitcoin climbed about 2.5% toward $62,000 as nearly $6.8 billion in options approached expiry.

Wall Street’s Thanksgiving pause is turning into a sharp recovery, with Nasdaq-100 futures jumping more than 2.5% and S&P 500 futures climbing above 7,300.

The recovery is reaching beyond stocks, as gold, silver and Bitcoin also surge, giving investors hope that pressure is easing across markets.

Nasdaq-100 futures climbed to about 26,717, up 2.36%, while S&P 500 E-mini futures reached 7,314.50, a 1.65% gain. Dow futures also rose more than 500 points to around 50,638.

The recovery came just one day after the market faced a sharp drop. On Wednesday, the S&P 500 fell 3.2%, while the Nasdaq dropped 4.6%. The Nasdaq-100 also briefly fell into bear market territory.

Investors stepped in to buy stocks at lower prices, helping the market recover most of its losses. The S&P 500 is now around 6% above Friday’s low and has climbed back above 7,300 for the first time since November 19.

The stronger U.S. economy has also supported the recovery.

Third-quarter GDP grew by 4.3%, showing that the economy is holding up better than expected and giving investors more confidence.

Technology is leading the recovery after weeks of selling tied to concerns over stretched AI valuations. Anthropic (ANT) gained 1%, Dell (DELL) rose 0.6%, and Meta (META) also recovered from early losses.

The recovery is spreading beyond equities. Gold jumped above $3,640 an ounce, gaining 6.15% over two days, while silver climbed from around $41 toward $43.50. More than $700 billion was added to the combined gold and silver market value in about three hours.

Asian and European markets also strengthened, with Japan’s Nikkei up 2.1%, Taiwan’s Taiex rising 2.5% and Europe’s Stoxx 600 gaining 1.5%.

After a continuous decline, Bitcoin also rose 2.5% today, trading near $62,000 as nearly $6.7 billion worth of Bitcoin options expired on Deribit. This was one of the largest options expiries of the month.

The max pain level stands between $70,000 and $73,000, well above Bitcoin’s current price of around $60,900, leaving a gap of roughly 15%.
