Change of Date for Sivers Semiconductors' Q3 2026 Report and Invitation to Presentation

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

KISTA, Sweden, Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Sivers Semiconductors AB (STO: SIVE) today announced that the Board of Directors has decided to move the publication of the interim report for the third quarter of 2026 from Thursday, November 26, 2026 to Monday, November 30, 2026. The report will be published at approximately 6:00 PM CET (12:00 PM ET), followed by a webcast presentation at 6:30 PM CET (12:30 PM ET).

The original date coincides with the U.S. Thanksgiving holiday, and the new date has been chosen to facilitate participation by the Company's growing base of U.S. investors and analysts. As previously communicated, Sivers publishes its quarterly reports and hosts its webcasts after the close of trading on Nasdaq Stockholm, supporting broad participation across international markets.

The presentation will be held in English by Sivers' management and will be followed by a Q&A session. The report will be available at www.sivers-semiconductors.com, and the webcast can be followed at:
