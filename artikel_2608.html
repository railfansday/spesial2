What a world: Raiders-Browns will be on Sunday Night Football

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Congratulations, we all have lived long enough to see a Las Vegas Raiders-Cleveland Browns game be flexed into prime time.

For various reasons, the NFL announced Tuesday that the game in Cleveland in Week 12 will be moved to Sunday Night Football. The game will be broadcast on NBC. Kickoff is set for 5:20 PT p.m.

The original Week 12 Sunday night game was the New England Patriots at the Los Angeles Chargers. That game is now schedeled for 1:05 p.m. PT. The Patrots are without standout quarterback Drake Maye and the Chargers won their first game on Monday night.

Because there are five games from Wednesday through Friday of the Thanksgiving weekend slate, there were few replacement options.

The Raiders-Browns game was appealing because the presence of No. 1 overall pick, Ferrando Mendoza in the starting lineup and with the Browns being a6-4 and a surprise playoff contender.

The Raiders didn't have any prime-time games on the schedule when it was released in May.
