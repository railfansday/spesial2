Oregon men's basketball offense stalls late in loss to Texas Tech

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Oregon's offense went cold and was unable to hold a late lead as No. 5 Texas Tech rallied to win Nov. 26 during a Players Era 16 Tournament men's basketball game at Michelob ULTRA Arena in Las Vegas.

The Ducks didn't make a field goal in the final five minutes as the Red Raiders overcame a five-point deficit to win 62-59, sending Oregon to its third straight loss.

Jasper Johnson tied his season high with 18 points to lead the Ducks (3-3), and Andrew Meadow scored 13.

Oregon trailed by 13 in the second half but went on 19-2 run to take a 54-49 advantage with 7:24 to play.

A layup by Fred Payne with 5:19 left in the game put the Ducks ahead 56-51, but that was their last field goal of the game as they missed their final five attempts, including a 3-point miss by Johnson with three seconds to play that would've tied the score.

Oregon outshot Texas Tech 42%-38.9% but was outrebounded 34-32 and allowed the Red Raiders to get 14 offensive rebounds and score 17 second-chance points.

The Ducks did have a season-high seven blocks, with Taylor Bol Bowen and Pharaoh Compton getting three each.

Oregon trailed by double digits at halftime. The score was 25-24 Texas Tech with just under four minutes to play in the first half, but the Ducks were outscored 13-3 over the final 3:24 and trailed 38-27 at the break.

The Ducks have one more game in Las Vegas at 12:30 p.m. Friday when they get an early season look at Big Ten opponent Maryland. The Terrapins are 3-4 after a 75-71 loss to San Diego State Thursday.
