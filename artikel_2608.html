Ravens' fans hope Jacoby Brissett can be a boost, not just a Band-Aid

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Before Baltimore Ravens fans get their hopes too high, they may want to take a realistic look at the 2026 season statistics of newly acquired quarterback Jacoby Brissett. The team's upcoming showdown with the Los Angeles Chargers may seem like a pending cakewalk, but the implications of another potential loss might send fans into an uproar.

Brissett hasn't played great in the 2026 NFL season, and his gut-wrenching interception throw to the New York Giants on Oct. 4 is not a play Arizona Cardinals fans will soon forget. Statistically, Brissett is riding a season QBR of 49.1, has thrown 10 touchdowns, and has tallied seven interceptions. Sure, Brissett might be an upgrade from the novice tendencies of quarterback Joe Fagnano, but the Ravens' ceiling makes the acquisition seem more like desperation than diligence. Will Brissett add a real boost, or is he just a Band-Aid to stop the bleeding?

Ravens fans want to see the franchise win, not fluster, and even with a hypothetical win this upcoming Monday, the Ravens still have to compete in a very competitive AFC North division. Nevertheless, Brissett did defeat the Chargers earlier this season on Sept. 13, so he might be well prepared to shake the team out of their losing streak.

Ultimately, the immediate gravitation to Brissett has been presented as a breakthrough, but the reality is the Ravens are at a breaking point. On Monday, Brissett will need to evade the blitz to bring in a win at M&T Bank Stadium. Hopefully, Brissett's services will bring some optimism on offense.
