NFL yanks fine for 49ers player’s controversial hit on Jaxon Smith-Njigba

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Seattle Seahawks and San Francisco 49ers will renew their rivalry this Sunday in a game that has massive implications for Seattle's playoff hopes and San Francisco's bid for the No. 1 seed in the NFC. San Francisco finds itself in the NFC West driver's seat in large part due to its 32-29 win at Lumen Field back in Week 5, but one play from the closing stages has just been drudged up again.

One of the pivotal moments of the game (well, at least after the fact) was Jaxon Smith-Njigba getting rocked on an incomplete pass by 49ers safety Malik Mustapha. No flag was thrown, otherwise Seattle would've been in game-tying field goal range. Mustapha was fined $6,998 for a hit on a defenseless receiver, which can be subject to appeal…

Rescinding the fine also presumably means rescinding the first of three strikes for Mustapha toward a suspension. Three violations of certain offenses would result in an automatic one-game ban, so Mustapha's strike is taken off the board.

Mustapha also had a heavy collision with Rashid Shaheed which drew neither a flag nor a fine.

That's one way to stoke some more rage heading into a must-win rivalry game!
