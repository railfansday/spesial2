‘No-brainer’: Ex-EFL man reacts to Dave Challinor move to Sheffield Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Sheffield Wednesday have turned to the ex-Stockport County man as their new head coach. SWFC have picked him as their replacement for Henrik Pedersen, who was sacked at the end of last month after back-to-back away defeats against Notts County and Doncaster Rovers.

Challinor, 51, parted ways with the Hatters in the summer after five years at Edgeley Park. He guided his former team from the National League to League One during his time in the North West.

Virgo, who played for the likes of Brighton and Hove Albion and Bristol Rovers, has delivered his verdict on his arrival at Hillsborough on the InsideThe72pod on YouTube: “It’s a no-brainer, it’s an absolute no-brainer for me. I thought he deserved an opportunity at Burnley but they’ve decided to go left-field.”

He added: “Dave Challinor in every single way makes sense. He knows League One, he knows that kind of level of football. I think he deserves the opportunity, I really, really do. The one thing I notice about his managerial style is he always sits back in the dugout a lot and he trusts his players and his team.”

As a player, Challinor was a centre-back for the likes of Tranmere Rovers, Stockport County and Bury. He played over 450 games before he hung up his boots in 2008.

The Chester-born man subsequently started his managerial career in non-league with spells at Colwyn Bay and AFC Fylde. Hartlepool United came calling in 2019 and he guided the Pools back up to the Football League.

Stockport County managed to lure him away from the North East and he dropped back down into the fifth tier. Challinor then oversaw the Hatters’ rise up two leagues.

He wasn’t quite able to get them into the Championship though and they lost in the play-offs two years in a row. He cut ties with them earlier this year and has since been weighing up his next move in the game.

Sheffield Wednesday are under pressure to gain promotion at the first time of asking in this campaign. SWFC were relegated earlier this year after finishing in the bottom three along with Leicester City and Oxford United.

The Owls are sat in 9th place in the table after their first 13 fixtures. They are two points off the top six behind Reading in 6th.

They drew 1-1 at home against Blackpool last time out. Davis Kellior-Dunn opened the scoring for them against the Seasiders before Jerry Yates levelled things up after Sil Swinkels was sent off.

Sheffield Wednesday are back in action as they aim to return to winning ways. They face an away trip to Boundary Park to take on League Two side Oldham Athletic in the first round of the FA Cup as Challinor looks to start with a win.
