West Ham backed to seal ‘dream’ appointment of man Sir Alex called ‘incredible’

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

This article is part of Football League World's 'Terrace Talk' series, which provides personal opinions from our FLW Fan Pundits regarding the latest breaking news, teams, players, managers, potential signings and more…

Pressure is mounting on Nuno Espírito Santo after back-to-back home defeats in the Championship.

The Hammers were beaten comprehensively by fellow promotion candidates West Brom last weekend, with fierce criticism directed at the West Ham boss for their struggles.

Despite being backed to win the division convincingly and return to the Premier League at the first time of asking, the East London outfit have struggled to assert their dominance, with consecutive losses in front of their own supporters leaving the club languishing in eighth – a position nowhere near acceptable for the level of quality their squad possesses.

And it’s raising big questions about whether a change in the dugout is needed, with such slowness out of the traps contributing to their demise.

One of those candidates who could be in a strong position to replace Nuno is Kieran McKenna, who departed Ipswich Town at the end of last season to take a break from management.

But a few months on after guiding the Tractor Boys back to the Premier League, he may be interested in the challenge of managing such a big club like the Hammers, so it’s a deal that could be very much on the cards.

So with this in mind, FLW’s West Ham fan pundit has commented on the recent speculation surrounding McKenna and whether he would welcome the 40-year-old to the London Stadium.

It’s safe to say that Nuno’s position at West Ham is becoming increasingly bleaker, with the Hammers reportedly considering a change in the dugout following an unexpectedly poor start on their Championship return.

Successive defeats at home to Southampton and West Brom has left many connected with West Ham fearing that Nuno isn’t the man to lead the club back to the promised land of the Premier League and that a change is needed in order for the club to get back competing towards the automatic promotion places.

And according to journalist Alan Nixon, West Ham have drawn up a three-man managerial shortlist of candidates should they decide to part ways.

It is believed that the shortlist consists of Kieran McKenna, Rob Edwards and Scott Parker, who are all out of work and possess previous experience of getting clubs promoted from the Championship to the Premier League.

So with this development, FLW’s West Ham fan pundit has reacted to McKenna being linked with a move to the club.

Speaking to Football League World, West Ham fan pundit Ryan Horn has urged the Hammers to appoint Kieran McKenna to replace Nuno, insisting he would be the ‘perfect fit’ for the club to move towards a more front-footed and modern style of play.

When asked whether he would like to see Kieran McKenna become the new West Ham boss, Ryan told FLW: “100%. I think Kieran McKenna should be who we go for.

“I know he’s taken some time out for personal reasons, I believe, and I know he’s studying some stuff with UEFA to up his game, but I think what he brings is something that the older style managers like Nuno, (Sean) Dyche, maybe (David) Moyes just don’t have in their locker, that modern-day footballing approach.

“The game has changed so much in the last decade, and we want to move from the old-style approach that West Ham have been on. We’ve been so sideways stepping, sometimes even backwards, when it comes to managerial choices.

“We should have gone for someone like McKenna after Moyes, not sidestepped backwards in a way to Lopetegui, but now, with Nils Koppen, Mark Noble and the changes around the board that are still coming into play, I think this is a great time to bring in someone, back them and build a team for them because they have done it with less of a budget, less pressure in a way, other than by pressure from their own fans.

“Ipswich fans loved McKenna, he did such a great job there, and I think he’d be the perfect fit for us. I think he’s one of those managers that can come in and have that Arteta effect at Arsenal, so yeah, I think McKenna replacing Nuno, that would be my dream, that’s the best outcome.

“Nuno needs to go either way, but we need to not make sidestep approaches like we’ve done in the past and McKenna is forward-thinking, has a modern-day footballing approach, and we haven’t had that at West Ham at all.”

Named an ‘incredible young talent’ by Sir Alex Ferguson during his early coaching days at Manchester United, Kieran McKenna has certainly lived up to all the hype and expectation, doing a fantastic job at Ipswich Town by leading them from League One all the way into the Premier League, as well as driving them back to the big time for a second time last season.

Not to mention McKenna achieved these incredible feats with Ipswich on a limited budget too, so working with much better players at the London Stadium and having the cash to bolster the squad in January are added bonuses that could see the Northern Irishman work wonders, should he be given the role.

After some time away from management, McKenna may well be keen on getting his teeth stuck into another Championship project at the London Stadium, with West Ham always an appealing club given their size at this level.

McKenna’s modern style of play and way of working could be exactly what is needed to turn the West Ham ship around following an extended period of malaise under Nuno, with the rot clearly setting in and the Portuguese boss set for the exit door if these performances and results carry on for much longer.

If West Ham want to avoid seeing their promotion hopes go up in smoke, they must act quickly with McKenna still available, with the 40-year-old having every chance of delivering success to this football club given his previous exploits in Suffolk.
