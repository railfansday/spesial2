Martin Boyle's goosebumps as Hibs hero salutes fans after downing Hearts

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Jubilant Martin Boyle dedicated Hibernian's stirring Premier Sports Cup semi-final victory over Hearts to the supporters who have stood by the team through a difficult campaign.

The Easter Road hero was emotional at full-time after helping fire Hibs into the final at Hampden, where they will face Rangers on December 13.

Boyle cancelled out Claudio Braga's opener before Jamie McGrath netted the winner in a dramatic Edinburgh derby comeback that sparked wild celebrations among the green-and-white half of the national stadium.

With Hibs sitting bottom of the Premiership, Boyle believes the result can provide a major boost heading into the rest of the season.

Speaking to Premier Sports, he said: "Unbelievable. I thought we were brilliant in the first half, apart from the corner. After that, the reaction from the boys was great and I thought we probably deserved it.

"In the second half we dug in and defended the box really well. It's a derby, we knew form would go out the window and we stuck together. We always believed we could win this game and we have."

Asked how a side at the foot of the table could produce such a performance on the big stage, Boyle insisted confidence has never disappeared within the squad.

He said: "Nothing's changed, we've always had that belief.

"We're going through a bit of a transition phase at the moment and the squad is quite young, so us senior boys really need to step up.

"The experienced boys coming on helped us at the end. It's brilliant and the fans deserve it for sticking with us."

Boyle reserved special praise for the travelling support, who created a thunderous atmosphere throughout the afternoon.

He said: "Hopefully we can enjoy a cup final and get ourselves up the league table. That's the main objective.

"The noise behind us here was unbelievable."

The 33-year-old, who has made more than 400 appearances for the club, admitted the occasion brought back memories of some of the greatest days of his Hibs career.

"It's a special feeling," he said. "I've got goosebumps. It's a great day out.

"Moments like this don't come around often and you've got to create them. We did that today and the fans have stuck by us.

"I know the league performances haven't been great, but thank you to everyone who came to support us today. They can go home with a smile on their face and enjoy their night out."

Hibs will return to Hampden next month looking to lift the trophy, but Boyle is refusing to get carried away ahead of the showdown with Rangers.

He smiled: "I'm not getting carried away, fingers crossed.

"I've still got some legs in me at the tender age of 33. As long as I'm playing and contributing to this club, I can enjoy moments like this and we can all enjoy the day out in the final.":
