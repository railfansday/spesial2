Cowboys legend joins Blake Corum, and Myles Garrett for a turkey celebration after Rams stengthen playoff hopes

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Rams are celebrating a 26-13 Thanksgiving Eve victory against the Green Bay Packers on Wednesday night in Los Angeles, and everyone is joining in on the festivities.

Including Dallas Cowboys legend Michael Irvin.

As Myles Garrett, Blake Corum, and Nate Landman were seen wearing turkey-inspired overcoats during their Netflix postgame interview, the trio was handed turkey drumsticks, and they included Irvin in their celebration.

Landman was initially handed a turkey leg, but dropped it, leaving the Netflix broadcast team screaming "No!" and Irvin came in to save the day by providing Landman a new drumstick while he was wearing the turkey overcoat.

With Irvin, the trio evolved into a quartet and brought the drumsticks into a circle to cheer for the celebration.

"Imma get in with these boys. This is the only thing I got," Irvin said.

The four of them savored the moment and bit into the turkey legs to cap off the Rams' fifth consecutive victory, keeping their goals of reaching the playoffs alive.

Rams' Nate Landman, Blake Corum, and Myles Garrett join Michael Irvin and the Netflix broadcast crew for a turkey following Wednesday's win over the Packers on Nov. 25, 2026, in Los Angeles. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

"It was very big," Corum said to Jaime Maggio of Sports Central LA. "There was no doubt that we were going to come in here and win. Just our preparation, our mindset. I'm super proud of my teammates."

As the Rams are preparing to enjoy the holiday by having the rest of the week off, Corum had a night he'll never forget.

Blake Corum breaking past a group of Packers defenders during the Rams' win over the Packers on Nov. 25, 2026, in Los Angeles. Getty Images

Celebrating his 26th birthday, the Rams running back had a career night by becoming the first Rams running back to have over 30 carries in a game since Todd Gurley did so in his rookie season in 2015.

"I can't wait to go home. My parents and two of my sisters came out and celebrate with my wife, my daughter, my mother-in-law," Corum said. "It's kinda like our first family Thanksgiving since I left to go to college. … So it will be a good time."

Corum finished the night with 32 carries for 184 yards, averaging 5.8 yards per carry and having one rushing touchdown.

Blake Corum being congratulated by Sean McVay following Wednesday night's victory against the Packers. AP Photo/Gregory Bull

"We knew coming in we needed to be a physical team. I would say we were the more physical team tonight," Corum said. "That's what coach was preaching all week: 'The most physical team would win this game."

The Rams' rushing attack was mixed between Corum and Kyren Williams who both scored two touchdowns, while the team finished running the ball for 241 yards.

Myles Garrett rushing off the edge during the Rams' home win over the Packers. Getty Images

While the Rams offense was brilliant, the defense was just as good.

Though the Rams only managed to land one sack on Packers quarterback Jordan Love, they had two interceptions from Jaylen McCollough and Emmanuel Forbes, while Landman had a forced fumble.

The Rams defense held Green Bay to only 189 passing yards and 55 rushing yards.
