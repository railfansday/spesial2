NFL rescinds Malik Mustapha’s fine for hit on Jaxon Smith-Njigba ahead of 49ers-Seahawks rematch

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

NFL rescinds Malik Mustapha's fine for hit on Jaxon Smith-Njigba ahead of 49ers-Seahawks rematch appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The San Francisco 49ers are gearing up for their rematch against the Seattle Seahawks on Sunday, and Malik Mustapha might want to treat his teammates to a nice dinner for a chemistry-boosting activity.

According to ESPN's Adam Schefter, Mustapha successfully appealed his $6,998 fine for his hit on Jaxon Smith-Njigba in Week 5, when the 49ers beat the Seahawks, 32-29, to avenge their loss in the divisional playoffs in January.

"Upon appeal, the NFL rescinded the $6,998 fine it assessed to 49ers safety Malik Mustapha for this hit earlier in the season on Seahawks WR Jaxon Smith-Njigba. Jordy Nelson was the hearing officer," reported Schefter on X.

Mustapha was penalized by the NFL for unnecessary roughness on Smith-Njigba. The incident occurred with 1:13 left in the fourth quarter, with the Seahawks attempting to tie the game.

The 24-year-old Mustapha called the ruling "soft" on his Instagram Story.

Despite the positive development, Mustapha has no time to celebrate, as he will have his hands full again versus Seattle.

The voided fine might also give more fuel to the 24-year-old Smith-Njigba, who is on pace to break Calvin Johnson's 14-year record for most receiving yards in a season.

Mustapha had seven tackles and one pass defended against the defending champions in Week 5. Smith-Njigba, meanwhile, tallied five receptions for 102 yards and a touchdown.

The 49ers will enter the rematch against the Seahawks with a three-game winning streak, including a 25-point demolition of the Minnesota Vikings in Mexico City on Sunday. Mustapha recorded four tackles.
