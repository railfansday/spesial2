Lakers blow 20-point lead in NBA Cup loss to Spurs in biggest test of season

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

SAN ANTONIO — JJ Redick, and the rest of the Lakers, acknowledged the four-game road trip would be a "massive challenge" because of different tests each team would present.

The biggest test of the trip, and the season so far, came Friday night at Frost Bank Center in the form of Spurs, the reigning Western Conference champions.

And for the first 19 minutes, the Lakers looked like a team ready to ace the test. But the problems they had to answer evolved, and they didn't find enough solutions, leading to the Lakers blowing a 20-point lead and suffering a 99-96 loss to the Spurs to close out NBA Cup group play.

Victor Wembanyama, who scored 25 points and grabbed 16 rebounds, celebrates during the Lakers' 99-96 NBA Cup loss to the Spurs on Nov. 27, 2026, in San Antonio. AP

The Lakers fell to 15-4 with the defeat, with the Spurs improving to 16-4 in a battle between the top two teams in the Western Conference.

And though neither Luka Dončić nor Victor Wembanyama had an efficient offensive performance, the Spurs star big man got the bragging rights, at least for one night, in the battle between two of the top contenders for MVP.

Dončić had 31 points, eight assists and six rebounds, but made just 1 of his 13 3-point attempts (14 for 31 from the field) on a night the Lakers struggled with their outside shooting — especially in the second half.

The Lakers shot 28.6% on 3s (6 of 21) in the final two quarters, with the returning Austin Reaves being the only Laker who found a consistent rhythm from beyond the arc.

Reaves (21 points, eight rebounds on 8-of-15 shooting) made all five of his 3s in the second half, including two late in the fourth to keep the game close after struggling with his ball security (seven of Lakers' 20 turnovers).

Wembanyama led the Spurs with 25 points (10-of-25 shooting) and 16 rebounds, while Stephon Castle had team-highs of 26 points and nine assists.

Luka Dončić, who scored 31 points, dribbles up court during the Lakers' 99-96 road loss to the Spurs. NBAE via Getty Images

The Lakers dominated the Spurs early, with nearly flawless execution of their defensive game plan to play off San Antonio's struggling 3-pointer shooters and shrink the floor against Wembanyama.

They held the Spurs to 38.9% shooting in the opening quarter, including 0 for 6 on 3s after the Spurs missed their final six shots (four 3s) of the first.

The Lakers' defensive intensity carried over into the second, with their stout halfcourt defense helping them take a 45-25 lead midway through the quarter before the Spurs cut their deficit to 54-38 going into halftime.

But the script flipped going into the third for the Lakers, who experienced their own offensive struggles. The Lakers scored 14 points on 6-for-24 shooting (2 for 13 on 3s) in the third, with the Spurs tying the game at 68 apiece when Keldon Johnson made a corner 3 with two seconds left in the third.

The Spurs led by as many as eight points in the fourth, but Reaves and Dončić made big shots late to keep the game close before the Spurs closed them out.
