As for extras, the CopperFlex Essential boasts the most generous benefits: a year-long sleep trial, a lifetime warranty, and free shipping. EGOHOME and SweetNight also provide free shipping, but their trials and warranties are slightly shorter. However, that's not a complaint — a 10-year warranty and a 100-night sleep trial are perfectly sufficient.

The 12" CoolNest Memory Foam Mattress is $399.99 in a queen right now, which isn't anything new. However, there is a chance that you may snag some freebies as we get closer to Black Friday, as we saw free pillows during Labor Day. Whenever you buy, though, expect a 10-year warranty and 100-night sleep trial.

With the EGOHOME code PREVIEW 10, you can take a queen size EGOHOME Black 12" Foam mattress from $379 (MSRP:$559) down to $341.10. This is excellent value for money, and you can also receive a 10-year warranty and a 100-night sleep trial.

Brooklyn Bedding has ditched its standard 25% discount for Black Friday and rolled out its big 30% off sale. This dunks a queen size CopperFlex Essential under $500, with a queen now $489.30. Thanks to a huge shakeup to 3Z brand's mattress extras, the CopperFlex now comes with a lifetime warranty and one-year trial.

Like the best memory foam mattresses on the market, all three beds are made entirely of foam (not a spring nor coil in sight) and are fiberglass-free.

The 12" SweetNight's design is focused on cooling, with an airflow-enhancing '3D' cover that my fellow testers and I found to be extremely cool-to-the-touch (but more on that later). Its foam is also infused with Phase Change Material (PCM), a specialist cooling fabric found in some of the best cooling mattresses. PCM has been proven to improve human heat dissipation up to 25.6% compared to conventional mattresses

The 12" EGOHOME does have a graphene-infused cover that the brand calls "cooling", but this is very basic. That said, the EGOHOME has an excellent quality build, packed with dense layers of foam (including a EGOHOME's trademarked BraceSleeper Support Foam, which is high-density for extra durability).

Despite being mid-range rather than budget, the CopperFlex has the least sophisticated design out of the three mattresses, with just a copper-infused foam for temperature control. However, its cover does boast a "Nano Stain Terminator Protector" cover to safeguards against stains and spills, plus it comes in a wide range of sizes (including an Olympic queen and RV sizes).

I found all three beds to be good choices for anyone seeking the best mattress for side sleepers (and I say that as a primary side sleeper), but each did boast different levels of pressure relief.

In my SweetNight CoolNest mattress review, I found that the CoolNest provided excellent pressure relief for side and back sleeping (both in objective and subjective tests). It has enough give in the shoulder and hip region to relieve the side-sleeper pressure points, but its ultra-quick response time means it's easy to move around on and avoids that "quicksand" feel that some memory foam mattresses have.

I conducted the EGOHOME Black 12" Foam mattress review for seven months (the longest I've tested a mattress at home) and found it delivered excellent medium-firm support for all sleep positions. However, another tester (a stomach sleeper) considered it a little too soft for front sleeping, proving that mattress firmness is subjective. That said, back and side sleepers should feel both comfortable and supported.

As for the CopperFlex Essential, it's clear from our tests that it's strictly for side sleepers. I found it incredibly cushioning when sleeping on it for my Brooklyn Bedding CopperFlex Essential mattress review, which is surprising as it doesn't have a pillow top. It scored the highest pressure relief score out of the three mattresses, but my fellow testers and I agreed that it's too plush for stomach sleeping.

From the moment I unboxed the SweetNight CoolNest, I found it to be incredibly cool to the touch. I even slept on it through summer heatwaves without AC and it still delivered optimal temperature regulation. My experience was backed up by our thermal imaging camera tests, which found that the SweetNight has a rapid cooling rate of 2.1°F per minute (a cooling rate even quicker than the Bear Elite's 1.82°F per minute).

In contrast, I found that both the CopperFlex Essential and the EGOHOME Black retained heat, especially in the back and shoulder region. Out of the three mattresses, the EGOHOME absorbed the highest amount of heat (15.6°F ), which was definitely noticeable to both me and fellow tester, Ruth Jones. And while the CopperFlex Essential absorbed up to 11.1°F in comparison, its temperature control will pose a problem for hot sleepers.

✅ You want cooler nights: The SweetNight boasts a cool-to-the-touch cover and heat-dissipating PCM which provides a cooler sleep even during hot weather.

✅ You sleep on your side or back: Its medium-firm foam is soft enough to soothe side sleepers but supportive enough for back sleepers. Plus, it's responsive enough for you to change position without feeling 'stuck' in the foam.

✅ You want the best value for money: The EGOHOME Black 12" Foam is the cheapest option, with promo codes adding extra discount to already cheap sale prices.

✅ You're a combination sleeper: I found it to be supportive in all sleep positions, and the medium-firm feel is ideal for side and back sleeping.

✅ You want the most cushioning: Its plush pressure relief is ideal for dedicated side sleepers, even if it's not such a great choice for back and stomach sleepers.

✅ You want generous extras: With a lifetime warranty and a trial that lasts a full year, the CopperFlex boasts the best benefits.
