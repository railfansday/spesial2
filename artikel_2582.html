Fans hit back at Jason Whitlock’s ‘worst halftime choice in the history of the NFL’ claim

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Jason Whitlock was heavily critical of the famous hip-hop crew Wu-Tang Clan's halftime performance for the Detroit Lions' game against the Chicago Bears.

After the high-octane first half of the game, fans were treated to a classic hip-hop experience by the Wu-Tang Clan, which has 9 members currently.

They rose to prominence in 1993 after the release of their single 'Protect Ya Neck.'

Their set included classics like Triumph, Da Mystery of Chessboxin, Method Man, Shimmy Shimmy Ya, Ice Cream, and C.R.E.A.M.

The decision to bring the Wu-Tang Clan back to the stage was taken by legendary Detroit rapper Eminem and his manager, Paul Rosenberg. Eminem signed a deal with the Lions as their executive producer and wanted to see the Wu-Tang Clan bring back their 90s energy.

While many 90s Hip-Hop fans enjoyed the experience of seeing the crew during the halftime show, analyst Jason Whitlock was not happy.

He wrote on X: "What percentage of NFL fans can name a single Wu-Tang song? Let alone 2 or 3? Is this the worst holiday halftime choice in the history of the NFL?"

He didn't stop there and expressed his displeasure once again, saying: "Is everybody ready to pretend old men shouting profane rhymes into microphones is art? Wu-Tang Forever!!!!"

Whitlock wasn't even happy to see Ian Rapoport praise the halftime show. He retweeted Rapoport's thoughts on the Wu-Tang Clan's performance and stated: "I like Ian. He does good work. But…"

However, fans were not going to allow Whitlock to criticise the famous crew.

One fan said: "Wu-Tang is forever. I usually skip halftime shows, but I was looking forward to this one. Happy Thanksgiving, Jason." While another added: "Jason, your lame a** probably wanted Maroon 5. Sit down."

One fan took up Whitlock's challenge and named a few songs from the Wu-Tang Clan. "Protect ya neck C.R.E.A.M. Method man Triumph Reunited Can it all be so simple Bring da ruckus I can keep going," he said.

Someone questioned Whitlock's music taste. They wrote: "Because you don't listen to rap nobody else knew the songs?"

"You are dead wrong, probably the worst take of all time," remarked another fan.

The Wu-Tang Clan's performances are all over social media, as unlike Whitlock, fans loved their performance during the halftime show.
