Mike Florio demands answers for what happened with Eagles QB Jalen Hurts’ scary blood clot situation

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Philadelphia Eagles have been without Jalen Hurts since the 19-17 Week 5 loss to the Jacksonville Jaguars in London. It was announced shortly after that contest that Hurts played through a blood clot in his leg and was eventually diagnosed with blood clots in his lungs. However, since then, things have been relatively mute from the organization, and Mike Florio wants answers.

During a guest appearance on "The Dan Patrick Show," Florio questioned why the NFL and the NFLPA are taking so long to discuss what transpired during the Eagles' trip overseas, as the investigation has yet to begin. Florio wants the timeline of the investigation to speed up because there are multiple questions he feels have been left unanswered for too long.

"The last word on this was that the league and the union were in communication about the parameters of the investigation that has yet to begin," Florio said. "How much do you need to talk about what you're going to do? And why does it have to be some complicated negotiation?

"It's pointless if the results of this investigation aren't made available for everyone to scrutinize because we still have questions that haven't been answered," Florio continued. "Fair, obvious questions that the Eagles have refused to answer. That the league has refused to answer… The extent of what happened to Jalen Hurts could happen to another player. Don't you want to get to the bottom of it and learn whatever lessons need to be learned?"

Florio also claimed that the last he's heard about Jalen Hurts is that the 28-year-old signal-caller was at the Eagles' facilities "a few weeks ago," and stated that is a good sign for Hurts' recovery. But until the NFL and NFLPA finish their discussion and officially launch the investigation, Florio is going to have to wait for those answers he's seeking.

In the meantime, Hurts will continue his progression through the blood clot, while the Eagles continue with the 2026-27 season. Philly is scheduled to take on the Pittsburgh Steelers next in Week 11.
