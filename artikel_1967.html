Chelsea's January 'priority' revealed as Xabi Alonso alarm bells ring - Transfer expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Xabi Alonso is prioritising the addition of new centre-backs ahead of the January transfer window to ease pressure at Chelsea.

The Spaniard is facing doubts about his future in the dugout at Stamford Bridge after a miserable run of form which culminated in a 4-1 defeat against Leeds last time out.

The Blues have kept just three clean sheets across 14 games in all competitions so far this season, and must make changes in January to turn their recent form around.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that new centre-backs have to be the priority for Alonso if he wants to keep his job as Chelsea manager.

Concerns have already grown about the performances of Levi Colwill and Wesley Fofana, while a serious lack of depth in the position has left Chelsea with little option to change.

As a result, big plans are being put in place for the January transfer window, with at least one new centre-back expected to be brought in to support Alonso's plans.

Brown told Football Insider on 24 November that Colwill has exposed a "serious weakness" in Chelsea's defence after his struggles against both Leeds and Sunderland.

The lack of physicality in the absence of Maxence Lacroix has seen Chelsea's defence bullied by big, physical strikers in Brian Brobbey and Dominic Calvert-Lewin.

As a result, the addition of more capable centre-backs has risen to the top of Chelsea's list of priorities, and work is ongoing in the background to strengthen their side.

"Chelsea are so weak defensively and that is now becoming clear every single week," Brown told Football Insider.

"They conceded four goals at home against a Leeds side who have struggled to score much at all this season, and that has got to be a concern for everybody involved.

"Defensively, they are all over the place, and it's becoming clear that the options they've got at the back are not good enough for where they want to be.

"Nobody ever wins anything if they cannot defend, it might not be pretty or fashionable, but it's the most important part of any successful team.

"There's no way Chelsea are going to be able to compete for any major silverware if they have such clear issues at the back.

"So, I'm sure they will already be working to identify players who can come in and improve them at centre-back, because it has got to be a priority for them in January.

"If they don't address their weaknesses, results aren't going to get any better, and then Xabi Alonso will be under serious pressure."

Chelsea's recent results have piled pressure on Alonso's position in the dugout, with large sections of the fans already asked questions about the manager's role.

Issues surrounding the Blues' tactical setup and player selection have led to growing concerns, while recent results have done little to ease the calls for his head.

Meanwhile, Football Insider revealed on 25 November that Chelsea will be forced to sack Alonso if they fall short of their expectations to secure Champions League football.

The Blues' hierarchy are aiming to give the boss time and support to get his ideas across, but should their results continue to fall short of expectations, he will be let go.
