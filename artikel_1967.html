Sources: Malo Gusto to Man City update ahead of January

Enzo Maresca is likely the driving force behind Manchester City interest in Chelsea defender Malo Gusto, sources have told Football Insider.

The Citizens were interested in the 23-year-old in the summer transfer window, and could be set to revive their interest in January.

The defender has made it clear to decision-makers at Stamford Bridge that he is keen to explore a move away from the club in the coming months.

But as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Man City may struggle to pull off a deal for Gusto in January.

Chelsea's price tag is significant, although there may well be breathing room with the France international clearly not in Xabi Alonso's plans.

The right-back has started just three Premier League games this season, with one of those coming in midfield amid an injury crisis.

Football Insider revealed in the summer that Chelsea had put a £75million price tag on Gusto's head amid interest from Man City.

The defender made 72 appearances for the Italian during the time he was in charge at Stamford Bridge, and was a favourite.

In fact, over the course of Maresca's managerial career so far, only seven players have made more appearances under him than Gusto.

One of them is Enzo Fernandez, who joined Man City from Chelsea in a £125m deal on transfer deadline day.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Enzo Maresca is well aware of Malo Gusto, having worked with him at Chelsea, and he is a big admirer him.

"I think still it’s a hard deal to do for this January. It won’t be cheap because of City’s valuation, which I think was around £75m.

"There's no way Man City will pay that. Look, Chelsea aren't actively pushing him out of the door, but if they got a good offer then it's something that they would consider.

"It'll be up to any interested club if they can try to persuade Chelsea to cash in on Gusto in January but I would imagine because of the price tag, it'll be a problematic deal to do."

In December 2025, Maresca chose to praise Gusto in a press conference that would ultimately mark the beginning of the end for him at Chelsea.

"it's the effort from the players. We said many times that Reece [James] and Malo, they are both full-backs and today they were both midfielders," he said after a 2-0 win over Everton that came just weeks before he was dismissed.

"So the effort, the open mind, the way they want to learn has been fantastic and this is the reason why I praise the players because with so many problems, they are doing very well after a complicated week.

"Since I joined the club, the last 48 hours have been the worst 48 hours since I joined the club because many people didn't support us.

"So I'm very happy for Malo in that moment that the effort from Malo and from the rest show that they are all there and they want to help this club."
