'Crystal Palace are making a mistake over Sean Dyche, it could come back to bite them' - Expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Crystal Palace have been warned they may be making a mistake by not hiring Sean Dyche to help turn their season around.

Pierre Sage was officially dismissed from his role earlier this week following a disastrous start to the new season which has the Eagles bottom of the Premier League after ten games.

Dyche has been heavily tipped as one of the leading contenders for the role, but now looks set to miss out, with Thomas Frank emerging as the clear front-runner.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that hiring Frank over Dyche could be a mistake which costs Crystal Palace their place in the Premier League.

Defensive issues have been the main concern for Crystal Palace this season, and the former Everton boss has been backed as the man to ease those worries.

Meanwhile, Dyche would bring a clear and recognisable style of play, while questions have been asked about Frank's tactical setup after his struggles as Tottenham manager.

Brown told Football Insider last month that Dyche was the front-runner to land the Crystal Palace job and was seen as the ideal candidate to turn their season around.

The Eagles are in desperate need of defensive structure, and that would be guaranteed under Dyche, who has a proven track record of making his sides hard to beat.

Palace, however, seem to have opted against pursuing Dyche in favour of Frank, despite concerns about the latter's difficult spell at Tottenham which has raised red flags.

"If I was in charge at Crystal Palace, Sean Dyche is the man who I would be going with," Brown told Football Insider.

"Their biggest issue is the defence, they concede far too many goals, leave far too many gaps, and Dyche would come in and immediate tighten that up and make them hard to beat.

"They've got the attacking talent to score goals, more than Dyche has had elsewhere, so it's just about stopping the leaks and being more rigid.

"With Dyche, you know what you're getting, he demands a lot of the players, he wants them to work hard, they all have to pull in one direction as part of the team.

"It's a clear style, and I don't see that from Thomas Frank. I look at what he did at Tottenham, how his side set up, and I don't see that structure or that clear way of playing.

"That's not always a bad thing, but Palace are in a situation now where they need to have a clear direction, I don't think Frank brings that.

"So I think, if they appoint Frank and decide not to go forward with Sean Dyche, they could very well regret that decision come the end of the season."

Crystal Palace hope Frank will be the right man to lead them to success both in the Premier League and Europa League, as they aim to turn their season around.

The boss' work with Brentford is still highly regarded, but concerns have started to creep in from outside the club about his Tottenham spell, which was less than impressive.

Brown warned Football Insider that Crystal Palace could be in big trouble if Frank suffers a repeat of his time in North London, and must make sure they get this call right.

The 53-year-old was dismissed by Spurs in February after an awful spell in which he won just seven of his 26 Premier League games, leading to fears they would be relegated.

Those fears have now emerged at Crystal Palace, and serious questions will be asked about whether Frank is the right man to lead them out of a potentially perilous situation.
