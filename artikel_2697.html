Despite overall struggles, Texas QB Arch Manning leads one long drive against Texas A&M

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

COLLEGE STATION - Earlier this week, Texas quarterback Arch Manning downplayed his role in the Longhorns' 17-7 win at College Station during the 2024 season.

"I only played in like three plays that game," Manning told reporters.

What Manning omitted was that one of those plays was a 15-yard touchdown run so it wasn't like his introduction to this rivalry was a nothingburger. But the point remains, Manning didn't play much during the Longhorns' last trip to College Station. That wasn't the case Friday.

Manning threw for 143 yards and a touchdown as Texas recorded a 16-6 victory at Kyle Field. Sixteen of Manning's 34 passes fell incomplete and he had a deflected pass get intercepted near the end zone in the fourth quarter.

The Texas offense, which posted season-low totals in yards and points, produced points on four of its 11 drives. But three of those scores were Gianni Spetic field goals. Texas A&M, however, was held to 234 yards by the UT defense.

"That pick, I can't do that in that situation. I've got to run and try and get in the end zone. If not, kick the field goal," said Manning, who also led UT to a 27-17 win over Texas A&M in Austin last year. "But defense had our back all night, and they're going to continue to do that going forward."

Manning did direct Texas on a 12-play drive that ended with Cam Coleman's 3-yard touchdown catch. During an 88-yard drive that accounted for 36.4% of its offense for the evening, Manning completed all six of his attempts and also converted a 3rd-and-3 attempt with a 4-yard run.

"I think we had just a really good rhythm in that drive," Texas center Connor Robertson said.

For the season, Manning has thrown for 2,628 yards, 22 touchdowns and 11 interceptions while completing 64.9% of his passes. Manning will next attempt to join Paul Campbell (1949), Walter Fondren (1957), Bobby Lackey (1958) and Sam Ehlinger (2018) as the Texas quarterbacks that have led the Longhorns to a win over Georgia. Texas lost to the Bulldogs twice during the 2024 season with Quinn Ewers as its starter, and Manning was leading Texas during last season's 35-10 loss in Athens.

"(Winning a conference title was) one of our goals before the season, and we're going to make the most out of it," Manning said. "Excited to go to Atlanta and play Georgia. They're kind of the standard. They've been in the SEC championship a lot of times. So we're excited for the challenge."
