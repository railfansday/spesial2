Manchester City 2 Napoli 2: What went right &#038; wrong for City as they drew once again

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Manchester City have drawn 2-2 tonight with Napoli at the Etihad Stadium tonight. Enzo Maresca's side have now won one match in seven in all competitions. Tonight's match featured a lacklustre City performance against a stubborn and resiliant Napoli side that does pose more questions than answers.

Enzo Maresca spoke yesterday of his bid to find balance with his team. Maresca has only been in charge of City for four months and his team is taking time to gel and it is taking time for his ideas to come across. In keeping with Maresca's bid to find the right lineup for his team, he made five changes to the starting lineup which drew 1-1 with Fulham. At the back, Josko Gvardiol stepped in at a centre-back. Elliot Anderson, Ayyoub Bouaddi and Enzo Fernandez started in midfield. Rayan Cherki, Allan and Erling Haaland were Manchester City's front three as Enzo Maresca gave his team a new look against Napoli.

First things first, it was fantastic to see Manchester City legend Kevin De Bruyne back at the Etihad. De Bruyne's legacy at the club will live on forever and it is always a special moment seeing him back home. Secondly, the orange kit that City wore for tonight's match just didn't seem right. I get that in the era of revenue being key why such kits are made but it did give the match an odd feel to begin the game.

Manchester City did make a bright start in the games opening 7 minutes. There was a crispness to City's possession play with Ayyoub Bouaddi, Enzo Fernandez and Rayan Cherki all featuring prominently. Elliot Anderson saw a low drive saved by Napoli keeper Vanja Milinkovic-Savic in the second minute and Enzo Fernandez saw a left-footed shot blocked from close range after a well-worked City short corner routine.

In the seventh minute, Napoli carved out an opening that saw Scott McTominay see a shot from a great position blocked behind for a corner. Napoli's chance came after a Gianluigi Donnarumma ball to Erling Haaland was seized upon by Napoli which allowed them to break forward.

Enzo Maresca would have been pleased with some of the possession play from his side in the opening half-hour. The move of Josko Gvardiol to centre-back was helping City's build-up play and there was energy to City's play. Enzo Fernandez was lively in midfield as was Elliot Anderson, who was drifting all over the pitch in an advanced midfield role. Anderson popping up on the right and swinging balls into the box did look to be a dangerous attacking option for City. Rayan Cherki was lively on the right wing and was drifting centrally to add another attacking option. However, Manchester City were frustrated by a well-organised Napoli defence who were keeping their shape well and being resolute at the back.

Of concern for Manchester City was the occasions that Napoli did cut open City's defence. Kevin De Bruyne was having joy picking out passes through Manchester City's left that Napoli didn't take full advantage of. A cross from Matteo Politano from the left almost found Scott McTominay for a header in the 38th minute. Nico O'Reilly was having a tough time at left-back. Defensively, O'Reilly was being caught out which was a cause for concern for City.

Gianluigi Donnarumma made a strong save in the 43rd minute to keep a Sam Beukema header. Beukema got a strong header on target from six yards out after a Napoli corner. Donnarumma reacted quickly to keep out Beukema's header. Napoli were enjoying their best spell of the match. Fortunately for City, Donnarumma was on hand to keep the score locked at nil-all.

Sam Beukema's header would be the final significant moment of the first half. Manchester City did have bright moments, but their attacking play lacked any really cutting edge. Napoli defended resolutely and were having moments in attack. The match was evenly poised at the interval. One positive for Enzo Maresca was that his team did seem to be stronger in the middle of the pitch. Perhaps Maresca has found the midfield solution for his team. However, City do need more life in attack which was impossible to miss during the first half.

Manchester City did begin the second half brightly. They succeeded in dropping Napoli but were frustrated by the Serie A sides defence. However, despite City dominating possession and territory it would be Napoli who would take the lead. Enzo Maresca's side played themselves into trouble when playing out from the back. Napoli seized upon the opening and worked the ball to Billy Gilmour on the edge of the penalty area. Gilmour played a ball to his left to Rasmus Højlund. Højlund turned Guehi inside and out and then beat Donnarumma with a quality finish across the Italian keeper to give Napoli a 1-0 lead.

After Napoli took the lead, Enzo Maresca's side were struggling to play with any intensity or break Napoli down. Jeremy Doku replaced Allan in the 53rd minute and Doku's introduction did add much-needed pace and creativity to Manchester City's attacking play. This would pay dividends in the 67th minute. Rayan Cherki played a short corner to Jeremy Doku inside the Napoli area on the left. Doku stood up two Napoli defenders before squaring the ball to Ayyoub Bouaddi. Bouaddi got a strong shot on target from just inside the area which beat Vanja Milinkovic-Savic to make it 1-1. Doku's introduction had brought life to Manchester City's attacking play which was impossible to miss.

As the game entered the final 15 minutes, Manchester City were pushing for a winner. Napoli had dropped into their own area and were struggling to get out. City were moving the ball quicker in the final third and hopefully that would pay dividends.

However, Napoli would retake the lead in the 76th minute. Napoli worked the ball from right to left and Scott McTominay played a ball to Matteo Politano who beat Nico O'Reilly in a crucial duel to set Leonardo Spinazzola free on the left-hand side. Spinazzola played in a great cross to the back post which Noa Lang volleyed home with aplomb. It was a simple goal for City to concede and summed up their frailties.

Josko Gvardiol would smash a shot into the post in the 81st minute. Rayan Cherki floated a corner from the left to the back post. Cherki's corner found an unmarked Gvardiol who took a touch and unleashed a stinging low shot on goal from 8 yards out which struck the post and rebounded away.

Manchester City would make it 2-2 in the 86th minute. Enzo Maresca's side were patient with their build-up and Iliman Ndiaye received possession on the right. Ndiaye picked out a surging run from Rayan Cherki who took a touch and beat Vanja Milinkovic-Savic with a crisp right-footed finish from a tight angle to make it 2-2. Outside of Jeremy Doku, Cherki had been Manchester City's most dangerous attacking player and he deserved his goal.

Outside of a Rayan Cherki shot that flew miles over the bar no side would come close to finding a winner. The final whistle blew and another frustrating City performance would be the major talking point out of tonight's match.

There wasn't a lot of positives to take from Manchester City's overall performance tonight. City started brightly but they were too easily contained until the introduction of Jeremy Doku and Rayan Cherki's drive offered City a threat up front.

My first thoughts are that Enzo Maresca has attempted to solidify his team's midfield which is working in stages. However, the left-hand side is now a major weakness for City. Napoli proved away down the left and Noa Lang's goal did come via this area of concern for City.

There is a lack of energy in Manchester City's play both in and out of possession. City aren't pressing as it appears that Maresca has attempted to tighten up his midfield. This is working in a sense, but City are far too susceptible to attacks down the left. Tonight's match was a continuation of City's lacklustre showing against Fulham and you wonder when it goes from being a trend to a pattern for Enzo Maresca's side.
