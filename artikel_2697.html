Dan Quinn gives injury updates on Bill, Laremy Tunsil, and Lucas Patrick

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Washington Commanders are on a mini-bye as they have 10 days between their Thursday and Monday Night Football primetime games. They took care of business in New Jersey against the Giants last week, and are now preparing to host the Cincinnati Bengals, a much more talented team. Dan Quinn spoke to reporters for his weekly "Tell the Truth Monday" presser, and gave sone injury updates.

RB Bill Croskey-Merritt (groin) is eligible to return from injured reserve this week, but the team hasn't made a decision on whether they'll open his 21-day practice window. Those announcements are typically made on the first full practice day of the week which is Thursday due to playing on Monday Night Football this week.

Reporters also asked about two other players on IR, LT Laremy Tunsil (triceps) and LB Leo Chenal (neck). Tunsil had a positive update from his doctors, and will begin strength training as he recovers from surgery. Chenal has been back in the building since his surgery, but its too early to give updates on either player's return to play. Center Lucas Patrick remains in the concussion protocol, and Nick Allegretti would get the start again if he cant play on Monday night.
