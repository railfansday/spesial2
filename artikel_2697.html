D.J. Reed off injury report, 2 defensive starters questionable vs. Bears

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Detroit Lions (8-2) and Chicago Bears (8-2) have declared their injury designations ahead of their Week 12 matchup on Thanksgiving Day. The Lions will be down at least one defensive lineman, as it's been reported that rookie Skyler Gill-Howard will require season-ending knee surgery, but they also appear to have several other players heading to game-time decisions on Thursday.

Let's take a look at what we know heading into the Lions Thanksgiving Day game.

Note: Any changes from the most recent injury report will be bolded

Gill-Howard's season is over, according to Lions coach Dan Campbell, as he'll need surgery after a significant knee injury against the Bucs.

"You hate to lose a player like him," Campbell said on Tuesday. "He's had a heck of a rookie season. We've mentioned this, but for a young guy, he's a pro. He's conscientious in what he did, just the right approach."

Rakestraw injured his shoulder in Germany, missed last week's game against the Bucs, and has regressed from limited practices to no practice this week. He has unsurprisingly been ruled out for another game.

Wonnum and Clark are both estimated to be game-time decisions according to Campbell and both got in limited practices on Wednesday. Clark was also estimated to have been limited on Tuesday, but both starting defenders seem like coin flips at this point.

LaPorta was estimated to have been limited on Monday but returned to full practices the rest of the way, so he should be good to go Thursday.

Reed has been getting in full practices, but both he and Campbell have reiterated that they're taking things slowly with him, and suggesting he may still need more time. However, with him not being listed with an injury designation, there's a good chance he is ready to play in this game.

Dorsey managed to play through his wrist injury last Sunday and has been estimated to have been in full practices this week, so he should be good to go against the Bears.

CB Tyrique Stevenson (groin) — added to the injury report on Wednesday
