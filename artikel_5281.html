Skanska signs an additional award to build data center in Georgia, USA worth USD 275M, about SEK 2.8 billion

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

STOCKHOLM, Nov. 26, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Skanska has received an additional award with an existing client to build a new data center in Georgia, USA. The additional award is worth USD 275M, about SEK 2.8 billion, which will be included in the US order bookings for the fourth quarter of 2026.

The project involves constructing a new 48MW data center facility with a total square footage of 244,730 square feet (about 22,700 square meter). The work includes constructing the shell and interior fit-out for technical spaces, support areas, and office functions. Additionally, there will be construction of an 860 square foot (about 80 square meter) ancillary water treatment building.

Construction began in November 2026 and is expected to be completed in October 2028.
