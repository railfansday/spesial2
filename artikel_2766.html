Jeffery Simmons says knee injury won't keep him from playing

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Bears defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons emerged from the bye week that has had him limited in practice this week. But Simmons has full confidence that he'll be ready to go on Sunday against the Saints.

"Like 99 problems, but this ain't one," Simmons said Thursday, via Courtney Cronin of ESPN. "I'll be out there Sunday."

Bears defensive coordinator Dennis Allen expressed similar confidence in Simmons's availability for Week 12.

"I don't anticipate any issues with him," Allen said, per Cronin.

It will be a busy week for the Bears. After hosting the Saints, they have a short-week visit to the Lions for the early game on Thanksgiving.

Simmons has played two games for the Bears since being traded from the Titans. He has a sack in both of those contests.

If Simmons gets another sack on Sunday, he'll be the first Bears player to get a sack in each of his first three games since Khalil Mack in 2018.
