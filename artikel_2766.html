Giants Must Avoid 49ers, Bears And Vikings In 2026 Playoffs

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Following a strong victory against the Jacksonville Jaguars, the New York Giants sit comfortably atop the NFC East with a 6-3-1 record. GMEN HQ identifies three specific NFC contenders that Big Blue should dread facing in the 2026 playoffs.

The San Francisco 49ers stand as the best team in the entire NFL right now. Even with numerous injuries and an older roster than most in the league, this squad continues to dominate. Brock Purdy leads the offense as an MVP contender while Kyle Shanahan calls the plays. According to GMEN HQ, San Francisco will probably secure the NFC's No. 1 seed. The Giants might not meet them until the championship game, but a January clash remains a real possibility.

The Chicago Bears rank as the second-best team in the conference and are legitimate Super Bowl contenders. Their offense ranks eighth in the NFL in total yards per game and scoring per game while holding the league's top rushing attack. Quarterback Caleb Williams is having an exceptional 2026 campaign with a career-best 68.3% completion rate. He also posted a solid 95.9 passer rating during this strong season. To strengthen the unit further, the Bears defense brought in Jeffery Simmons, a Pro Bowl pass rusher from the Tennessee Titans.

The Minnesota Vikings possess a championship-caliber defense led by coach Brian Flores. The Giants offense has struggled to find its identity since losing Jaxson Dart back in Week 2. A playoff matchup against Minnesota would test the Giants' current attack. The Vikings have lost to the Giants before.

The Giants face the Indianapolis Colts on Sunday at Lucas Oil Stadium for their next game.
