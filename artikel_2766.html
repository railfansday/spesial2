Former Bills coach arrested after allegedly running over pedestrians' feet in hit-and-run

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Former Buffalo Bills coach Rex Ryan was arrested Wednesday after police say he ran over two pedestrians' feet with his pickup truck in Nashville, argued with them when they confronted him and drove away.

Ryan, 63, faces six misdemeanor charges stemming from the Nov. 5 incident, which left both pedestrians with minor injuries.

According to the Metropolitan Nashville Police Department, the pedestrians were crossing Broadway with a walk signal shortly before midnight when Ryan allegedly turned right onto the street from Fourth Avenue, running over both pedestrians' feet and striking one person's leg.

Police said Ryan continued driving before stopping at a red light, where the pedestrians caught up to him.

The pedestrians told police Ryan got out of his truck and argued with them before getting back in and driving away.

They photographed the driver and the truck's license plate, which police said was registered to Ryan.

Ryan turned himself in to Nashville police Wednesday morning on six outstanding misdemeanor warrants.

He is charged with two counts each of leaving the scene of an accident, failure to provide information or render aid, and failure to obey a traffic control signal.

His bond was set at $6,000, but he qualified for release without posting bond. He is scheduled to appear in court in December.

Ryan's attorney, David Raybin, told Nashville television station WSMV that Ryan turned himself in after learning of the allegations and is cooperating with the investigation.

Ryan coached the Bills for two seasons from 2015 to 2016, compiling a 15-16 record before being fired with one game remaining in his second season.

Buffalo went 8-8 in Ryan's first season and 7-8 in his second, missing the playoffs both years.

Ryan previously coached the New York Jets for six seasons, leading them to consecutive AFC Championship Game appearances following the 2009 and 2010 seasons.

He joined ESPN as an NFL analyst in 2017 and currently appears on "Sunday NFL Countdown."

"We are aware and are in touch with Rex," ESPN said in a statement Wednesday.
