NFL Expert Picks for Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The NFL rolls on into Week 11, and there's some fascinating football on the slate, assuming people can pull themselves away from Grand Theft Auto VI for long enough this weekend to watch it.

Only three games on the schedule are divisional matchups, with the bulk of those being settled in the finals weeks of the season. What this week does have a lot of is potential trap games. This is a week that looks like a lot of chalk, but we know something is going to happen somewhere on the slate that will throw a wrench in the works.

Easily the best game of the week will come on Sunday Night Football between the 49ers and the Vikings. These could be the two best teams in the NFC right now, and it's a chance to see if the Minnesota defense can stop Brock Purdy and Christian McCaffrey, paired with what Kirk Cousins will bring to the table in his second start since returning as the Vikings' QB.
