Jack Whittaker should make Sunderland debut vs Jagiellonia Bialystok

Sunderland youth star Jack Whittaker must be in line for his first-team debut in their upcoming Europa League clash against Jagiellonia Bialystok.

The Black Cats' priorities have changed somewhat since the start of the campaign due to their recent struggles.

Regis Le Bris has been juggling endless domestic games with the Europa League so far, and the competition has taken a backseat as they fight off early Premier League relegation fears.

Their return to European action on Thursday against Polish side Jagiellonia provides a chance to rotate and bring new players into the fold in search of a surprise spark.

Young midfielder Whittaker has been knocking on the door of the first team all season and should now be handed his senior Sunderland bow this week.

Le Bris needs to prioritise league games and using the 20-year-old to rest more experienced players would make perfect sense as he impresses in their academy.

Whittaker has been at Sunderland since he was seven, and he signed a new two-year deal in the summer after scoring 11 goals and registering four assists for their under-21s last season.

He was also exposed to the first-team environment on several occasions, being named on the bench in the Black Cats’ March Premier League clash against Brighton, and has continued to impress in this campaign.

Whittaker has bagged four goals in 12 U21 games in 2026-27, and is among the top-performing Premier League 2 midfielders for several key metrics.

He has an 87.5 successful pass percentage (76th percentile), 66.7 successful cross percentage (95th percentile) and has won 65.3 per cent of his attempted duels (90th percentile).

Whittaker shone on the wing last time out against senior opposition, with the most chances created (3) and ground duels won (8) in the youth side's 3-2 EFL Trophy loss to Salford City.

Le Bris has continued to hint towards a senior bow this season, with Whittaker sitting on the bench for three of the Black Cats' Europa League matches in this campaign so far.

He is one of their youth stars who is most deserving of a first-team debut, and Thursday's clash against the 29th-placed Europa League side seems like an obvious place for it to happen.

Whittaker will feel like he deserves a chance to show what he can do, especially as those in his most likely position struggle for form at senior level.

Sunderland's recent starting wingers, Chemsdine Talbi and Trai Hume, are each in poor form, with just two goals and no assists in 22 combined appearances this season.

Talbi is in the 16th percentile for chances created (0.71 per 90), seventh percentile for line-breaking passes (2.49 per 90), 25th percentile for successful passes (18.9 per 90) and 13th percentile for successful dribbles (0.36 per 90) against other Premier League attacking midfielders and wingers.

While Hume ranks in the 13th percentile for xG (0.07 per 90), 12th percentile for chances created (0.61 per 90), 27th percentile for successful cross percentage (14.3) and 16th percentile for xA (0.04 per 90) in the same category.

Sunderland need fresh impetus and Whittaker could be the man to provide it on Thursday.
