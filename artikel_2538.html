Dak Prescott, Dallas Cowboys hoping for back-to-back wins for first time in 2026, stay playoff hunt

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Dallas Cowboys quarterback Dak Prescott is playing as well as any quarterback in NFL. The same can be said about Quinnen Williams and defensive tackle position and CeeDee Lamb at receiver.

But none of it will truly matter if the Cowboys (5-6) don't beat the Philadelphia Eagles (3-7) on Thursday in the annual Thanksgiving Day affair at AT&T Stadium, notch their first two-game win streak of the season and get back to .500 to stay in playoff contention.

A loss would likely be final deathknell on what has been a disappointing start to the 2026 season.

But the Cowboys remain hopeful because of a Prescott-led offense that ranks second in the NFL in scoring at 30.6 points per game, coming off back-to-back outings of 40 points in more. A 46-40 loss to the San Francisco 49ers was followed by a 51-30 victory against the Tennessee Titans in what was the team's best performance of the season.

With 7 games left, the Cowboys are in officially in must-win, playoff mode from here on out to keep their fledgling hopes alive as they they are second in the NFC East behind the New York Giants (6-3-1).

"Yeah, absolutely," Prescott said when asked if the Cowboys were in playoff mode against the Eagles. "I mean, I think we put ourselves in that position a couple of weeks ago, frankly, right? Yeah, you said it — last week was, and this week is, and I would imagine every week going forward is, given how the division is."

It's been a full calendar year since the Cowboys won back-to-back games. As well as they played last week, they have to find a way to keep it going against the Eaglees.

"I spoke before about how big momentum is," Prescott said. "And when you can grasp it and use it, and also write how tough it is when it's against you, and right now, yeah, we've got it on our side. We've got to go make sure that we use it, and it's not just me saying it, but we've gotta start fast and continue to build off of what we just did this past Sunday."

Said Lamb: "In order for us to get into the playoffs, we've got to win back-to-back games because, I mean, it's what you need to do."

The Cowboys haven't lost to an NFC East team at home when Prescott is at quarterback since November 19, 2017 _ a span of 20 games.

Prescott is currently playing the best football of his career with 668 passing yards and 10 total touchdowns against San Francisco and Tennessee the past two games. He has thrown for over 1,000 yards with 12 touchdowns and just one interception over his last three games.

Prescott is currently second in the NFL in passing yards (3,049) and second in passing touchdowns (25).

Is Prescott playing the best football of his career?

"Sure," Prescott said. "Because I do. I mean, it's all work. I mean, it is. It's all working. It's not just me, it's the 10 other guys, the game plan around and healthy. Thank God."

"Just the confidence is real and the confidence is not overconfidence," Cowboys coach Brian Schottenheimer added. "He's playing with great command and control man. And I've been here a long time and watched him play a lot of football. And I can honestly say I can't remember a stretch that he's played this well and I think he's in a really good spot right now."

The same can be said about Lamb and Williams.

Lamb has caught 88 passes, which leads the NFL, for 1,108 receiving yards (2nd in the NFL) and nine touchdowns. With seven receptions against the Eagles, he will pass Marvin Harrison Sr. (665) for the most receptions by any player in NFL history through the first seven seasons of a career.

"I feel like just being open and being there, and being available for my quarterback is putting me in a position to even be having this conversation," Lamb "Obviously, that's a huge honor. I'm appreciative of all the little accolades that's going on, but I really want to win too, so that would make it more cherishable."

Williams leads the Cowboys with eight sacks and he's recorded at least one sack in five of the last six games. Williams is also the NFL's leader in tackles for loss this season with 18 to put himself in considering for the NFL Defensive Player of the Year of the Cowboys can keep winning.

"I feel really good, I feel like I'm going in the right direction to kind of what I want to achieve, what I want to be," Williams said. "But like I said, the biggest thing is about stacking wins. That's kind of the thing I'm focused on, just doing everything I can to help this defense be better, affect the game and win football games because that's the only thing that truly matters."

Williams said he can do even more personally and help improve a run defense that ranks at the bottom of the league. That is even more acute on Thursday against Eagles running back Saquon Barkley.

"It's a lot of ways to go individually," Williams said. "It's a lot of things I can do when it comes down to affecting the game, taking the ball away and just being consistent up front. Consistent hand usage and things like that. We gave up 30 points last week, and playing good teams, playing in the playoffs and playing in the Super Bowl that we kind of achieve to do, you can't really do that, and you shouldn't have to do that, especially being the defense that we have. Just honing in on what I can do personally, looking in the mirror to change that…"

"Being consistent in the run game, being consistent in the pass game because I'm getting a bunch of one-on-ones right now, and just winning them at a higher level that I feel like I can. Like I said, just affecting the game and doing the things I can do on the field to help us win."
