Riverdale safety Hudson Fuqua decommits from Mississippi State football

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Riverdale senior defensive back Hudson Fuqua decommitted from Mississippi State on Nov. 17.

The 6-foot, 190-pound Fuqua, a three-star safety who is ranked by 247Sports Composite as the No. 36 player in Tennessee and No. 96 safety nationally, announced his decision on the X platform.

The decision came just over a year after he committed to the Bulldogs early last November. When he committed he said his relationships with the Bulldogs coaching staff was a big factor in his decision.

Bulldogs head coach Jeff Lebby fired two assistants ― offensive line coach Phil Loadholt and special teams coordinator Cliff Odom ― on Nov. 15, following a 41-9 loss to Auburn.

Fuqua has offers from Ole Miss, Arkansas, Vanderbilt, Maryland, Purdue, Indiana, Virginia Tech and MTSU, among others.

His most recent visits have been to Purdue, an unofficial coming on March 7 and an official visit on June 5. The Boilermakers offered him in February.

Fuqua has helped lead Riverdale (11-1) to the TSSAA football Class 6A state quarterfinals, where the Warriors will play at Cleveland on Nov. 20.

He was one of three Riverdale players committed to the Bulldogs, including senior quarterback Gavin Hampton and senior tight end Sutton Gutting.

He is third on the team with 74 tackles while adding a sack, a team-leading four interceptions (one returned for a touchdown) and two forced fumbles.

Fuqua was the 3-6A Co-Defensive Back of the Year in both 2025 and 2024. He had 87 tackles, three interceptions and two caused fumbles last season and 78 tackles and a forced fumble in 2024. He is a two-time first-team all-area performer.
