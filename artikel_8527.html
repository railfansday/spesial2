Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Re: When to let go of a problematic inherited co-op in NYC ?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

When a home doesn't sell, it's because the market looks and decides they can buy better for the same money. AKA, your price is too high. When FIL passed, we did $0 in renovations because you never know what the next person wants. We cleaned it out and put it on the market at the price the real estate agent suggested. At the first open house, we received 2 offers and one was above asking and a cash buyer. Only following inspection did we spend money correcting things in the inspection. Some plumbing and replacing knob and tube wiring, which the buyer even paid part of. The house next door was identical when built in 1920. But that house had been renovated. They had it listed for less than ours and it sat for months after we closed. So I guess it's too late but spending a dime on an inherited home before selling can end up being a waste of money. I also won't ever forget looking for our first house. With our income, we could afford $125k. So if something was perfect and in the perfect location and newly renovated for $130k, we didn't even consider looking at it. The lower your price, the bigger the available market. Drop your price.
