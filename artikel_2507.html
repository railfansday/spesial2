Confirmed 2. Bundesliga lineups: Greuther Fürth vs. Holstein Kiel

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Heiko Vogel makes one change from the heavy defeat to Osnabrück last weekend as Omar Sillah replaces Jordan.

Tim Walter makes two changes after the penalty victory over Hoffenheim in the cup during the week. Timon Weiner and Giorgi Kvilitaia come in for Lio Rothenhagen and Ikem Ugoh.

Greuther Fürth (4-2-3-1): Hellstern; Dehm, Heuer, Blank, Itter; Banse, Will; Klaus, Appelkamp, Pohlmann; Sillah

Holstein Kiel (4-4-2): Weiner; Sekine, Nekic, Madsen, Parduzi; Kaprálik, Davidsen, Meffert, Therkelsen; Kvilitaia, Harres
