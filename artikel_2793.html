Cardinals sign QB Josh Johnson

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Cincinnati Bengals QB Josh Johnson was signed by the Arizona Cardinals off their practice squad on Monday, Nov. 9.

Why It Matters: Johnson was added to the roster following the injury to starter Gardner Minshew. Carson Beck will assume the starting role, and Johnson will serve as backup. Neither should be considered by fantasy players at the moment.
