NFL news, live updates: Rams' Myles Garrett reportedly in line to return Sunday; Shedeur Sanders to start for Browns with Deshaun Watson in concussion protocol

We're officially into Week 9 of the NFL season (and the looming NFL trade deadline), where teams will have to make a decision on the intended course of the remainder of their season — are they going all in? Or is it time to tear it all down and look toward the future?

NFL fans also await news on the fate of star running back Jonathan Taylor, who has been touted as a potential trade-deadline mover as the Indianapolis Colts kicked off their franchise reset with a fire sale. "Recently, I experienced an unexpected and unusual medical emergency involving a series of unexplained neurological symptoms that required hospitalization for several days," Henderson wrote. "During that time, I underwent extensive medical testing and evaluation. While the results of my comprehensive medical testing have come back positive and reassuring, the cause of this remains unknown and unexplainable.

"I will continue working closely with specialists and physical therapists to focus on my recovery, as these symptoms have affected my strength, coordination, and some of my physical functions. I am committed to doing everything necessary to return to full health. My health and long-term well-being remain my foremost priorities."
