Panthers TEs Darren Waller, Tommy Tremble drawing concern with knee injuries, miss Wednesday's practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Carolina Panthers may be down a pair of pretty important tight ends this weekend.

Darren Waller and Tommy Tremble were both listed as non-participants at Wednesday's practice due to knee injuries. Following the session, head coach Dave Canales was asked if there's a level of concern regarding their statuses for Sunday's matchup against the Baltimore Ravens.

"Certainly a concern," Canales replied. "But just wanna take it a day at a time and see how these guys progress throughout the week. And so tomorrow, we'll get out there and run around, see if we can get them out there to do something. But we have to make that determination tomorrow."

Waller enters Week 11 as the team's third-leading receiver, with 25 catches for 323 yards and four touchdowns. Tremble ranks fifth, at 25 grabs for 227 yards and three scores.
