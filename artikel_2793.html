The Las Vegas Raiders are now officially Fernando Mendoza's team following Kirk Cousins' move to the Minnesota Vikings. The rookie quarterback finally got the opportunity to take charge of the offense after spending the opening portion of the 2026 season learning and developing behind Cousins. For fans who had been waiting for Mendoza to share a career update on LinkedIn, the wait is finally over.

Mendoza has already made his first start for the Raiders against the Seattle Seahawks. Now, the rookie signal-caller has finally updated his LinkedIn profile following his promotion to the starting quarterback position.

"Promoted," Mendoza said. "A lot to learn from my first NFL start. Grateful for my family, friends, anN Raiders nation for the support! Looking forward to getting better and earning the trust of my teammates and coaches every day."

Mendoza has developed a reputation for using LinkedIn to celebrate important milestones in his football career. Unlike many professional athletes who primarily rely on Instagram or X to share career developments, the rookie quarterback has become unusually active on the professional networking platform. His LinkedIn activity has even caught the NFL's attention.

The Raiders selected Mendoza with the No. 1 overall pick in the 2026 NFL Draft following his remarkable college career with the Indiana Hoosiers. However, head coach Klint Kubiak initially preferred to give the rookie time to develop and learn behind the experienced Cousins before handing him the starting responsibilities.

Now, following Cousins' departure, Mendoza has the opportunity to lead the entire Raiders team as their starting quarterback. However, his first outing didn't go exactly as expected. Las Vegas suffered a 21-13 loss against Seattle, while the rookie completed 17 of 29 passing attempts for 143 yards without throwing an interception or recording a passing touchdown.

Despite the disappointing result in his NFL debut as a starter, Mendoza has the opportunity to grow into the role with the support of his teammates and coaching staff.
