Slow start? No problem. The Bills gave us plenty to Shout! about!

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Someone once said, "It's not how you start, it's how you finish." Maybe they were previewing the Buffalo Bills' Week 10 matchup with the New York Jets, because that start was uneventful at best and downright ugly at worst.

But somewhere along the way, the Bills did what the Bills do. They made some big plays, broke a few records, set some personal bests, and found a way to get the job done. And when it was all over, Buffalo headed back to Western New York with an 8-1 record and gave us plenty to Shout! about along the way.

So, let's hand out some Shout!outs this week.

_____________________________________________________________________________

Nine games into his head coaching career, Joe Brady is already keeping some pretty impressive company. And, for the rookie head coach, taking his offense on the road hasn't been much of a problem, either.

_____________________________________________________________________________

Another week, another handful of NFL records for Josh Allen. This time, he's passing Patrick Mahomes on one list, joining Jalen Hurts on another, and adding two more milestones to a résumé that's getting pretty crowded.

Allen also passed Peyton Manning for the most touchdowns in a player's first 10 seasons and tied Eric Dickerson for 14th on the NFL's all-time rushing touchdown list. Not a bad afternoon.

_____________________________________________________________________________

James Cook must have decided that one Shout!out wasn't going to be enough. So, he tied a Hall of Famer, matched his own franchise record, and threw in a career-long touchdown run for good measure.

_____________________________________________________________________________

DJ Moore is a difference maker when he's on the field with Buffalo's offense. His 45-yard touchdown against the Jets was his longest since joining the Bills and one of the longest of his career.

_____________________________________________________________________________

How about a little love for the rookie? T.J. Parker got to the Jets quarterback twice on Sunday, putting himself in some pretty good company among recent Bills rookies.

_____________________________________________________________________________

Dorian Williams decided he wanted in on the sack party, too. He brought down Geno Smith for the first sack of his NFL career.

_____________________________________________________________________________

And while we're handing out individual Shout!outs, let's not forget the entire defense. Buffalo has now gone 28 consecutive defensive drives without surrendering a touchdown, something the Bills haven't done in more than a quarter-century.

_____________________________________________________________________________

It wasn't pretty from start to finish, and there were definitely a few moments that had Bills Mafia yelling at their televisions for all the wrong reasons. But Buffalo found its rhythm, took care of business against a division rival, and added another win to the standings.

Now it's back home to face the Miami Dolphins, with an 8-1 record, a five-game winning streak, and a whole lot of history being made along the way. Plenty of reasons to keep Shout!ing!
