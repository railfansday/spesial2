Lakers release injury report for Wednesday's game versus Kings

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

With a 6-2 record, things are starting to look increasingly optimistic for the Los Angeles Lakers. While they have some issues to rectify, they have one of the NBA's most prolific offenses, and they have been adept at forcing turnovers and turning them into easy baskets.

After scooting past the Miami Heat 138-117 on Monday, they will take a quick trip up north to face the Sacramento Kings on Wednesday. The Kings are 1-6, but they do have potent players in center Domantas Sabonis, a three-time All-Star, veteran wing Zach LaVine and terrific rookie point guard Darius Acuff Jr.

After forward Ziaire Williams returned to action for the Lakers on Monday, Adou Thiero remains their lone injured player. He will still be out of the lineup on Wednesday as he continues to deal with a knee ailment.

Thiero has yet to play in the regular season, and he missed long stretches of action last season as a rookie due to knee problems. Lakers coach JJ Redick said on Monday that the hope is that Thiero will be able to return by the end of this week.

The team will host the Portland Trail Blazers this Friday. It will then start a four-game road trip with a matchup versus the Detroit Pistons on Sunday, and it will then face the Atlanta Hawks on Monday. The Lakers will return home from that trip to host the Charlotte Hornets on Nov. 17.
