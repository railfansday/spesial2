Harbaugh Reverses Giants’ Coin Toss Plan Due To Injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The New York Giants broke their own tradition on Thursday night by choosing to receive the opening kickoff. Most teams in the NFL defer their choice to the second half when they win the coin toss. The Giants did not wait. Head coach John Harbaugh made a quick decision to flip the script just minutes before the game started against the Washington Commanders. A sudden injury to a key defender drove this change in strategy.

Cornerback Tae Banks was not ready to play when the team took the field for warmups. He hurt his shoulder right before the game began. Harbaugh explained the situation, "we had a little issue come up right before, in warmups, where … actually Tae got dinged on his shoulder, and he was getting looked at." The coaching staff realized they could not start the game on defense with Banks out.

The roster situation made the decision even more urgent. Backup Colton Hood was already injured with a dislocated finger. Another backup, Paulson Adebo, was a healthy scratch and not available to play. With the secondary depleted, the Giants could not afford to start on defense. Harbaugh noted the impact of the injury on the lineup. "So we weren't going to have him for the first series. So that being the case, we figured hopefully he'll be out here for the second series," Harbaugh said.

The offense got the ball first and managed to move it down the field. After the opening kickoff, Gregory Desrosiers returned it for 24 yards. The unit then picked up two first downs before punting the ball away. Harbaugh credited the offense for buying time while the defense got ready. "Offense was gracious enough to at least put some plays out there and buy some time," Harbaugh said, "and [Banks] was able to play. So that determined that decision."

The team faced a tough loss to Washington, falling 24-21 on Thursday night. The Giants now look ahead to their next matchup. They will host the Jacksonville Jaguars at MetLife Stadium on Sunday, Nov. 22, for a 4:25 p.m. kickoff.
