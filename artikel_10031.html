As the second generation of Sony's older XM4 flagship headphones, the XM4Cs just hit shelves this fall. And according to Mashable's Bethany Allard, they're a "really well-rounded pair of headphones that stands on its own among other pairs."

They come with most of the same interior tech as the original XM4s, but the big upgrades are in the audio. Sony enhanced the sound quality with a 10-band equalizer, an updated sound profile, and a gaming equalizer preset. We do wish the noise-cancellation got a boost, as they house the same QN1 processor as the originals. But to be fair, it still holds up in 2026. Check out Allard's review of the headphones if you want the full breakdown.

If noise-cancelling headphones are on your Black Friday wishlist or you're seeking a great gift option for someone in your life, the XM4Cs are certainly worth a look. At less than $200, the value is undeniable.
