Patriots rooting guide for NFL Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The New England Patriots are on their bye this weekend, and it is a much-needed one. The team has struggled with injuries throughout the season, including most recently starting quarterback Drake Maye. While Maye's outlook remains uncertain, the team as a whole is able to catch its breath after an excruciating 10 weeks of football.

This means that we are also able to do that. And so, without further ado, let's take a look at the football that is being played in Week 11 and who the Patriots and their fans should be rooting for.

Before doing so, however, a quick explainer: as long as the Patriots are still theoretically alive in the race for the AFC East and a playoff spot, we will root with that possibility in mind.

Indianapolis Colts (3-7) at Houston Texans (3-6): Go Colts! The Colts have seemingly given up on the season, which in turn makes them far less a threat to a potential Patriots playoff run. And while the Texans have also not shined so far this year, they are a talented team capable of making a push over the second half of the season. | Prime Video

Miami Dolphins (1-8) at Buffalo Bills (8-1): Go Dolphins! The Patriots' chances of repeating as AFC East champions are slim, but until they are mathematically eliminated from the division race — as mentioned above — we will keep rooting against their rivals from Buffalo. To be fair, we would probably root against them in any scenario, but you get the point. | CBS

Baltimore Ravens (4-6) at Carolina Panthers (4-5): Go Panthers! The Ravens' season has gone haywire after a 4-1 start, but they are still a theoretical player in the competition for playoff spots in the AFC. So, we will go with the classic NFC over AFC pick in this one. | FOX

New Orleans Saints (3-6) at Chicago Bears (7-2): Go Bears! There are two factors at play here. A Bears win would improve New England's strength of schedule tiebreaker, and also help with the positioning of the 2027 sixth-round pick acquired from the Saints in the Ja'Lynn Polk trade. But don't the Patriots also have Bears pick in the fourth round after sending them Garrett Bradbury? Yes, they do, but the higher of the two picks will transfer to Philadelphia as part of the A.J. Brown trade, so that selection essentially doesn't matter. | FOX

Tennessee Titans (1-8) at Dallas Cowboys (4-6): Go Cowboys! Dallas losing would be fun, but the first overall pick in next year's draft not going to an AFC East team would be as well. Hence, a pick against Tennessee in this one to keep them well-positioned in that particular race. | FOX

Tampa Bay Buccaneers (3-6) at Detroit Lions (7-2): Go Lions! Four words: strength of schedule tiebreaker. | FOX

Arizona Cardinals (3-7) at Kansas City Chiefs (7-2): Go Cardinals! The Patriots need to get all the help they can get if they want to qualify for the playoffs, and their conference rivals losing what on paper is winnable games would be a good start. | CBS

Pittsburgh Steelers (6-3) at Philadelphia Eagles (2-7): Go Eagles! See above. | CBS

New York Jets (2-8) at Los Angeles Chargers (1-8): Go Jets! Welcome everybody to the Putrid Bowl. The Jets look destined to remain a non-factor in the playoff race even if they win in Los Angeles on Sunday, while a win would further remove them from the No. 1 draft pick. Meanwhile, the Chargers' momentum after their first win of the season would be halted just before the Patriots come to town in Week 12. | FOX

Las Vegas Raiders (4-6) at Denver Broncos (7-2): Go Raiders! The Patriots hold the head-to-head tiebreaker over the Raiders, so they shouldn't worry too much about Las Vegas making up some ground — especially if it comes at the expense of a team currently in the playoff picture. | CBS

Jacksonville Jaguars (7-2) at New York Giants (5-3-1): Go Giants! The Jaguars are likely to finish the season as AFC South champions, which in turn wouldn't impact the Patriots' own playoff outlook. That said, it's b better to be safe than sorry when it comes to seeding and qualifying possibilities, so let's go with the Giants here. | CBS

Minnesota Vikings (7-2) at San Francisco 49ers (8-1): Go Vikings! There is one reason we are picking Minnesota here: the Vikings are on the Patriots schedule, and the 49ers are not. This is purely a pick with tiebreakers in mind. | NBC

Cincinnati Bengals (7-2) at Washington Commanders (3-5-1): Go Commanders! We are back in NFC over AFC territory for that one. | ESPN
