Report: Colts with trade offer on the table for WR Josh Downs

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Indianapolis Colts reportedly have a trade offer on the table for wide receiver Josh Downs.

According to ESPN's Adam Schefter and the NFL Network's Ian Rapoport, the Colts have "told other teams that they already have received an offer of a fourth-round pick for wide receiver Josh Downs."

Schefter and Rapoport add that the Colts were initially looking for a third-round pick in return for Downs.

Downs requested a trade last week. He is currently in the final year of his rookie deal and is set to be a free agent this upcoming offseason.

A third-round pick by the Colts in 2023, Downs has caught 30 passes this season for 330 yards with one touchdown.

Schefter and Rapoport also note that Colts' WR Keenan Allen and DT Grover Stewart have received trade interest as well.

The Colts are also reportedly listening to offers on QB Daniel Jones.
