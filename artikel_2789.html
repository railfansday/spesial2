Tennessee Titans discussion: Week 10 live chat during the Jaguars game

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

If we are being honest, the Tennessee Titans don't have much of a chance against the Jacksonville Jaguars today. They are just two teams heading in opposite directions. The Titans were the biggest seller at the 2027 NFL trade deadline, while the Jaguars were the biggest buyer.

All eyes are on Cam Ward in this game. The Titans need him to build off of the game that he had against the Cincinnati Bengals two weeks ago, when he threw for 300+ yards for the first time in his career. He doesn't have to throw for 300+ again, but he does need to show the same command of the offense that he did in Cincinnati. If he can string together a few of those performances, the Titans can feel confident he is the guy moving into next season.

Somehow, the Titans got less healthy during their bye week. They will be without Carnell Tate, Peter Skoronski, Anthony Hill, and Cody Barton in this game.
