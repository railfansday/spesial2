Manchester City star forced to withdraw from England duty as replacement named

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Dan Burn has been called up to England's squad following Marc Guéhi's withdrawal due to injury. The Newcastle United defender received the call for the UEFA Nations League double-header after Guéhi was ruled out with a hip problem.

Manchester City's Guéhi was substituted at halftime during his side's loss at Nottingham Forest and subsequently underwent scans, leading to his withdrawal, with the remainder of the squad reporting for duty at Tottenham Hotspur's training facility on Monday.

It is a welcome development for Burn, who played a significant role in England's FIFA World Cup run before missing out on the last two squads.

England manager Thomas Tuchel stated on Friday that Burn remains in his plans, even as they focus on developing the next generation of players ahead of the 2028 Euros.

Meanwhile, Tuchel confirmed that Jordan Pickford retains his status as first-choice goalkeeper, while acknowledging that James Trafford is pushing hard for the position. Pickford and Trafford could share goalkeeping duties across the fixtures against Croatia and Spain, mirroring the arrangement from the previous international window.

"No, he is," Tuchel said on Pickford being first choice, via The Mirror. "But nothing has changed. The competition is on there as well. Like for the outfield players. And Traff gets regular starts now, that's what we wanted.

"James Trafford did well. They shared the matches last time. I'm not sure what we will do. There's an opportunity that we maybe do this again.

"I have to speak with our goalkeeper coach. But the goalkeepers, they are a bit of a unit for themselves, and rightly so.

"Yes, it's slightly different, but when do I have a chance to see another goalkeeper, if not in Nations League? If not in high-pressure matches?

"So we had James against Spain, we asked ourselves is this fair for James Trafford to straightaway go against Spain, but I think it's exactly what we need. Because what happens if Jordan Pickford is injured? What happens if he's not available? So it's better to test it."
