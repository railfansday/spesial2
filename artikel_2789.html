Eagles make a roster swap, add LB to 53-man roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Eagles make a roster swap, add LB to 53-man roster originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

The Eagles on Tuesday afternoon made a roster swap, adding LB Chance Campbell to the practice squad and waiving OL Jake Majors.

Campbell, 27, played in one game with the Eagles (against the Bears) earlier this season as an elevation but was then placed on the practice squad injury list. Campbell was brought back to the practice squad on Nov. 16 and is now on the 53-man roster.

The Eagles are in need of linebackers with Zack Baun (back) on Injured Reserve and with Smael Mondon (ankle) on the shelf.

Against the Steelers on Sunday, the Eagles started Jeremiah Trotter Jr. and Jihaad Campbell, who both played well. But when Jihaad Campbell had to leave the game briefly, the Eagles were down to Anfernee Orji for a couple plays. So it'll help to have Chance Campbell in the mix.

"Yeah, he played good," defensive coordinator Vic Fangio said about Jihaad Campbell on Tuesday. "Played good, hurt his finger, but played really good. Made a lot of people nervous when he hurt his finger because we were out of ILBs at that point and he came back, played good and strong."

Chance Campbell was drafted in the sixth round of the 2022 draft out of Ole Miss by the Titans. He has played in five career games. Campbell has been known for his special teams ability. So he'll help out on teams and provide depth at linebacker.

Majors, 24, started at center against the Jaguars and Panthers in place of Drew Kendall but hasn't played since. In fact, Majors has been inactive in three of the four games since then.

If Majors clears waivers, the Eagles will likely try to re-sign him to the practice squad.
