How Titans' Tony Pollard can earn $200,000 on one play in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

How Titans' Tony Pollard can earn $200,000 on one play in Week 11 originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Tennessee Titans might be 1-8 in the regular season, but there are a lot of things to love about how this team has played as of late.

Tony Pollard, Cam Ward, and the receiving room have really impressed as of late. Pollard, especially, has been on a heater for Tennessee in the final year of his deal. Speaking of his contract, he has a chance to earn a hefty payday in Week 11.

According to Michael Ginnitti of Spotrac, the Titans running back has a chance to score a $200,000 payday on one play this week. How? All he has to do is score a touchdown.

"Titans RB Tony Pollard is 1 rushing TD away from a $200,000 bonus," Ginnitti shared on Twitter/x before Week 11.

The Titans are set to face off against the Dallas Cowboys in Week 11, and it's a fantastic opportunity for Pollard to have a revenge game against his former team.

With how badly the Cowboys' defense has played, ranking 32nd in rushing yards allowed per game, the Titans running back has a real chance for a big game.

Pollard has five rushing touchdowns in his last three games, but he had just 33 yards and no touchdowns on nine attempts in Week 10 against the Jacksonville Jaguars.

But in a good matchup against the Cowboys, Pollard has a chance for a big day and a chance at securing a $200,000 bonus.

He has six rushing touchdowns this season, and according to Ginnitti, one more touchdown from Pollard on the ground and he'd secure $200,000 this season.

Pollard is having a good season so far, and in a good matchup that is also a revenge game, he can make $200,000 with one rushing touchdown, which would make for quite the lucrative touchdown.
