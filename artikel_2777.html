Bills Mafia’s confidence in Buffalo dips ahead of Week 11 vs. Dolphins

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Something strange happened here at Buffalo Rumblings following the Buffalo Bills' win against the New York Jets. It was the team's first win against an AFC East foe this season (now 1-1), and the victory allowed them to retain their lead in the AFC playoff race thanks to an 8-1 overall record.

Yet for some reason, there was a drop in confidence among some Rumblers who responded to our latest poll. Heading into the game versus the Jets, fan confidence was at 94%. A week later, just ahead of a home game with the Miami Dolphins, that confidence number has dropped to 89%.

I blame the quality of opponent, rather than the way in which the Bills won. After all, winning, no matter how it looks, is the only thing that matters in the NFL.

I'm left wondering if it's the situation in the rest of the AFC that has some in Bills Mafia concerned about the season. There are four other teams that remain serious threats to Buffalo's one-seed goal. We asked readers about that this week, wondering which AFC team is the biggest threat to the Bills' hopes of securing a playoff bye.

I think recency bias prevail here in the results. Leading the way are the Cincinnati Bengals (43%), a team that doesn't face Buffalo in the regular season. In second with 39% of the vote are the Jacksonville Jaguars — another team the Bills don't face in the current regular season.

There's a precipitous drop after those teams, surprisingly. Just 10% of people saying that the Kansas City Chiefs are the biggest threat to Buffalo. Meanwhile, only 8% of those polled say the Broncos remain the Bills' biggest challengers to the one seed.

Why do I find surprise in these results? Yes, the Bengals and Jaguars are red-hot right now. Yet, I wonder if some people thought about the direct threat the Chiefs and Broncos represent to the Bills. Remember, Buffalo will face Kansas City (at home) on (equally) short rest/prep for Week 12. They play a road game at the Denver Broncos in Week 16, another major U.S. holiday.

The Chiefs and Broncos have already played each other twice in 2026, splitting the series 1-1, which means there's no longer a chance either team can acutely unseat the other before the playoffs.

We'll close up shop here with a quick glance at the confidence level of 'Phins fans over at The Phinsider. The Dolphins lost a close game against the Indianapolis Colts in Week 10, but put together a decent game behind rookie quarterback Kyle McCord.

Currently, 84% of fans who responded to the poll gave a vote of confidence in Miami.
