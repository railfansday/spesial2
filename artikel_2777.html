Jon Wood Won’t Ask Josh Berry If He Meant It but Says ‘It Does Take a Man to Own Up’

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Wood Brothers president Jon Wood backed Josh Berry after the contact that ruined Ryan Blaney's day at Martinsville but said "it does take a man to own up."

Wood spoke on SiriusXM NASCAR Radio before NASCAR fined Berry $50,000 and docked him 25 points over an alleged brake check on Blaney.

"I'm not going to ask Josh if he did it on purpose," Wood said. "It doesn't matter. You know, the intent at this point was one race to go, and then he's on to the next opportunity, and we're on to our next opportunity."

"It's uncharacteristic of Josh to do something like that. So, you know, you try to weigh the totality of evidence, and it's just not like him," he added.

The contact happened on Lap 252, late in Stage 2. Blaney's No. 12 Ford suffered front-end and radiator damage, and he accused Berry of brake-checking him after finishing 35th.

Berry said Blaney "got into me pretty good" and that he was trying to save his car. He went on to finish fourth.

The Wood Brothers have a technical alliance with Team Penske, which also runs IndyCar and sports car programs across motorsports. Wood called Blaney "a teammate of sorts."

"And then for it to be a teammate of sorts in Ryan Blaney, a guy that, you know, you really wish no ill will toward. It's hard to think it was on purpose," Wood said.

Wood said he did not want to know whether Berry meant to do it.

"But it happened, as I said a dozen times, and, you know, you can't really take it back," Wood said. "I know Josh and Blaney have spoken, and I'm sure it hasn't changed Ryan's perspective of being upset about it."

"But it does take a man to own up and say, look, I ruined your day; whether it, again, was on purpose or not, it happened," Wood added. "And I have the utmost respect for both of those guys and, again, it's just not typical of what Josh Berry does."

NASCAR announced Berry's penalty the same day it fined Carson Hocevar $100,000 and took 25 points for his brake checks on Austin Cindric. Hocevar was also suspended by his team for the finale.

Berry has one race left with the Wood Brothers before he moves to Legacy Motor Club in 2027. Blaney's title hopes ended at Martinsville.

Founded in 1950, the Wood Brothers are the oldest active team in NASCAR. Their No. 21 Ford runs its final race of the season with Berry on Sunday at Homestead-Miami Speedway.
