Suns’ Miles Bridges is ‘good now’ after injury scare vs Rockets

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Concern crept into the Phoenix Suns' roster during Wednesday's 116-104 loss to the Houston Rockets. The most concerning element had to be a potential Miles Bridges injury.

He tweaked his ankle during a second-quarter post-up with Alperen Sengun. Bridges subsequently fell on top of him after Sengun was fouled on the play.

The Phoenix forward only played 26 minutes, and signs of a potential injury were there. He wasn't moving the same way he usually does. There wasn't the same amount of bounce and pep in his step moving up and down the court.

Although Bridges and the Suns were riding high during the road trip, this potential injury was a question mark. Luckily for the fanbase, he clarified his current condition ahead of Friday's game against the Dallas Mavericks.

"It was the 2nd quarter when (Alperen) Sengun tried to post up on me, and I pushed him, and he kind of flopped, and I fell over him," he said via AZCentral's Duane Rankin on X (formerly Twitter).

That's when I kind of tweaked it. I couldn't move the same way the whole game, but I'm good now."

The recently acquired Phoenix forward leads the team in rebounds per game (7.4) and is the third-leading scorer. Not to mention, Bridges's playmaking has been impressive, averaging the second-most on the team (3.7).

His downhill and driving ability is always a guarantee, but when the shot is falling, that's when he's at his most dangerous. That being said, there are still some concerns that he can address with his team.

The rebounding is a byproduct of the team's lack of size, but also not handling physicality the best.

Facing off against Cooper Flagg and the Mavericks can be a good test for observing their own physicality. For them to win, it needs to match up and be elevated.

Having Bridges fully healthy only helps the Suns in that department.
