What Would Warren Buffett Do With Nike Stock Down 79% From Its Peak?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Oracle of Omaha would probably agree that sentiment toward this business is characterized by extreme uncertainty.

By Neil Patel – Nov 20, 2026 at 12:00PM EST

Based on its long-term industry leadership position, most investors would view Nike (NKE -2.64%) as a high-quality business. And there aren't many people in this world who could size up a company like Warren Buffett could to figure out if this is true. He's adept at making thoughtful decisions about what course of action to take.

So, what would he do with this consumer discretionary stock that's trading a gut-wrenching 79% (as of Nov. 13) off its record?

Nike shares have been hammered to the point where they might be too hard to ignore for investors who focus on finding cheap businesses. The current stock price is the same as what it was selling for about 13 years ago, despite revenue in fiscal 2026 being 83% higher than in fiscal 2013. And the price-to-earnings ratio of 18.2 is close to a 17-year low.

Despite this beaten-down setup, I believe there's zero doubt that the Oracle of Omaha would quickly pass at the chance to buy Nike shares right now. "Turnarounds seldom turn," he once wrote.

The company is now two years into its strategic overhaul under CEO Elliott Hill, a more than 30-year Nike veteran. He admits that improvements are taking longer than expected.

When it comes to financial trends, there's so much uncertainty surrounding Nike's trajectory. Buffett wanted to avoid situations like this that make it impossible to confidently forecast earnings five or ten years into the future.
