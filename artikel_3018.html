Why Alphabet Stock Fell Today (Hint: It's AI-Related)

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The AI enterprise sector came under pressure as investors worried about the rising costs of building frontier AI models in a highly competitive market.

By Lee Samaha – Nov 25, 2026 at 1:32PM EST

Alphabet (GOOG -2.02%) (GOOGL -2.13%) came under pressure today, with a more than 4% decline at midday. The move can't be divorced from overall weakness in the AI-related sector, with all other hyperscalers also firmly in negative territory.

The decline comes as the market digests news reports that Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX +1.30%) CEO Elon Musk believes that Grok is falling behind models developed by rival AI enterprises.SpaceX stock has also come under pressure recently.

Such reports serve to highlight a major concern among companies like Alphabet. Specifically, the costs associated with running and maintaining frontier AI models, and what those costs imply for Alphabet's balance sheet and profitability.

As you can see below, the rising cost of Alphabet's AI infrastructure buildout means that it's now burning cash, and the Wall Street consensus, according to S&P Global Market Intelligence, is that Alphabet won't generate free cash flow again until 2029 as it continues to ramp capital spending.

GOOG Free Cash Flow (Quarterly) data by YCharts

The current negative sentiment is understandable, but it may prove entirely necessary over the long term, as weaker players in the AI market are shaken out in a correction. That could be a plus longer term for Alphabet, as its core businesses of Google advertising and Google Cloud are strong cash generators. Alphabet has the underlying strength to emerge victorious from industry consolidation.
