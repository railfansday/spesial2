Herbalife (HLF) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Herbalife Ltd. (HLF +1.76%) reported its fifth consecutive quarter of net sales growth, supported by a 8.6% increase in global volume and significant outperformance in the Indian market. Management detailed a strategic roadmap focused on the establishment of a dedicated Nutrition Club vertical and a modular technology platform designed to capture 50% of distributor transactions within three years. The company attained a net leverage ratio of 2.0x, meeting its long-term leverage objective ahead of schedule while authorizing a new 250 million share repurchase program. CFO Scott Schaefer reported that the April refinancing resulted in a $19.1 million reduction in interest expense during the quarter.
