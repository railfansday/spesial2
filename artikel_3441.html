Game Of Thrones: The Rise Of Dragons Officially Unveiled

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

While House of the Dragon has managed to give Game of Thrones fans a live-action rendition of one of George R.R. Martin's most important time periods when it comes to dragon lore in the A Song of Ice and Fire universe, there's a vast amount of history there left to explore. For example, the upcoming Aegon's Conquest film will take us back to the Golden Age of dragons, when legendary beasts like Balerion, Vhagar, and Meraxes helped establish the Targaryen dynasty in Westeros.

By the time Daenerys entered the picture centuries later, dragons had been extinct for generations, with her three hatchlings being a miraculous return for the species. Aegon's era, meanwhile, saw fully grown dragons reshaping the political landscape, setting the stage for generations of Targaryen dragonriders and the much larger population of dragons seen in House of the Dragon.

Fortunately, it looks like fans will have another opportunity to celebrate these legendary creatures, with the newly announced The Rise of Dragons bringing them back into the spotlight.

Warner Bros. Games has confirmed that Game of Thrones: Conquest is bringing back The Rise of Dragons later this month, with the limited-time event scheduled to run from November 23 through November 25, 2026. It's the second time the event has appeared in November, following its initial run from November 9 through November 11.

For those unfamiliar, The Rise of Dragons is one of Conquest's recurring Power-Up Events, specifically focused on developing players' dragons. These events reward players for investing in their dragons, allowing them to earn points through several activities, including:

Earning points will equate to players earning unique rewards, making this a nice opportunity for anyone looking to beef up their dragons. Specific reward milestones haven't been detailed in the November announcement, although players can check the in-game event window for additional information.

The Rise of Dragons is also just one part of Conquest's much larger Highgarden Harvest Festival, which runs from November 3 through November 27. The monthly update introduces two new playable heroes, Hotpie and Mace Tyrell, alongside the Rose of the South gear set, which specializes in ranged combat bonuses.

There's also the new Terror of the Tundra Dragon Gear set, beginning with a Saddle that offers several useful bonuses, including improvements to Dragon Attack, Dragon Health, and Dragon Ranged Health against other players.

Additional activities include The Great Hunt from November 24 through November 28, the Arena from November 23 through November 30, and the Festival of the Maiden, beginning November 26.

For those who haven't checked it out, Game of Thrones: Conquest is a free-to-play mobile strategy game that lets players build their own kingdoms, raise dragons, assemble armies, and compete for control of Westeros. First released in 2017, it's been quite popular among mobile gamers, boasting over 10 million downloads on Google Play, along with impressive user ratings of 4.3/5 on Android and 4.4/5 on Apple's App Store.
