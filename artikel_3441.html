Barbara Eden, 95, Breaks Down Her Fitness Routine Away From the Gym

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Barbara Eden’s secret to looking and feeling great at the age of 95 involves some quality time with a personal trainer.

“I go for about, I don’t know, 35 minutes on the bike. It’s good,” Eden shared with Fox News Digital in an interview published on Sunday, November 22. “I don’t go to the gym anymore.”

When she did, the I Dream of Jeannie actress committed to a “spinning class for an hour, and then I worked with a trainer for a half hour, lifting weights.”

These days, Eden still lifts weights because she believes it’s especially good for women and their bones.

“You have to be disciplined about being active when you’re an actor,” she said with a laugh. “My husband [of 35 years, Jon Eicholtz] and I both have a trainer that comes in and helps us out.”

As any wellness expert would argue, fitness is only part of the equation to staying healthy. Diet is also important to longevity.

Eden said she is careful about what she eats, “but I’m not overly stupid about it, you know?”

“I’ve never smoked. I don’t know if that means anything. But I think one of the things that’s most important in your life is that you recognize other people,” she said. “You realize that they’re not so different than you are, and you have to get along. And when you do that, you find life is much more interesting, and people are much more interesting.”

Eden became a household name when she starred in I Dream of Jeannie from 1965 to 1970. She also guest starred in projects like Sabrina the Teenage Witch and George Lopez.

As she calls Beverly Hills home, the actress said she’s “glad I’m here, and I’m glad I’m still working.”

As she watches the younger generations grow up, Eden also wanted to share her beauty tips. While her evening routine involves washing her face and putting on moisturizer, she warns fans to watch the sun.

As she explained, Eden wished she had known “the damage the sun could do to your skin.”

“We didn’t know that when I was growing up,” she explained. “And I think it really helps, if you know, to put sunscreen, you know, on your face during the day. Or if you’re working.”
