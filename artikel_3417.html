Steam Officially Drops 4 New Free Games You Can Keep Forever (November 16)

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

You can discover plenty of games on Steam without spending money, whether browsing its growing collection of free-to-play releases or trying out demos before committing to a purchase. However, running into a worthwhile experience that doesn’t require you to spend real money is always a welcome surprise. Recently, four new titles have joined the platform, giving players a fresh set of additions to explore across different genres.

All of these games arrived between November 10 and November 12, and are currently available to download for free. While there are a lot of free titles to check out on Steam, my selection below includes everything from a short survival experience on Mars to an idle RPG that runs alongside your desktop activities. Players can also explore an online crafting sandbox or uncover a story through a simulated computer interface. Here’s what each game has to offer.

A survival simulation, 687 Days on Mars gives players a brief introduction to life on the Red Planet. In the 30 minutes of gameplay you get here, you are tasked with building your first shelter, collecting essential resources, and finding ways to survive in an environment where even a short trip outside can be dangerous.

The game includes several survival mechanics, requiring players to manage oxygen, water, food, and energy while keeping radiation levels under control. You can mine iron and copper, construct equipment, research new technologies, and grow crops such as potatoes and lettuce. Dust storms and solar storms can also damage equipment, making it important to prepare your shelter before venturing out. Overall, it’s a short, challenging survival experience that you can run through in just half an hour, and it’s free, so why not?

DPS: Desktop Pension Squad takes a different approach to RPGs by allowing players to keep their adventure running while they work, study, or watch videos. It features a monster squad that automatically fights enemies and collects chests in the background. Players can keep the game running in a corner of their screen or switch to Icon Mode when they need more desktop space.

The objective is to recruit monsters, combine their abilities, and recover Dungeon Cores stolen by humans. Each character has eight unique skills, allowing players to experiment with different combinations and equipment. Rewards collected during battles can be used to improve characters and craft better gear. The game is currently in Early Access, which the developers expect to last around two months.

The developers have confirmed that generative AI tools were used for parts of its artwork, sound, and translation work, but much of the final artwork has been reworked. Well, players are surely happy with this background RPG as is evident from its 81% positive reviews.

Loamward is an online sandbox game where players can build settlements, gather resources, and shape the world around them. It doesn’t follow a traditional class or level system. Instead, players improve their skills by performing different activities, whether that means mining underground, crafting equipment, farming, or constructing houses. You can also work with other players to establish settlements and decide how the shared land is managed.

There are several activities to explore, including fishing, cooking, animal breeding, and farming. The world also features changing seasons and weather, with wildlife such as wolves and bears roaming outside settlements. The developers have confirmed using AI-generated content for some artwork, item icons, voice lines, and sound effects. AI tools were also used for store artwork and the narrator’s voice in the trailer. However, these assets were edited by hand, and the game does not generate AI content during gameplay.

Love, Death & My Daughter is a single-player narrative adventure that uses a simulated desktop interface to tell its story. It puts players inside an old software system where they must repair a damaged memory program. As you progress, you gradually recover memories from the past and uncover the story of a mother’s love. The developers estimate that completing the game takes around two to three hours.

Gameplay revolves around exploring the old computer system and managing two resources: Heart and battery power. Both are needed to repair damaged memories and progress through the story. The experience also features illustrated scenes that provide more details as players uncover the past.

You can claim all four of these Steam games for free and keep them forever, so check out their store pages and add them to your library. In case you’re wondering whether they are worth it and can’t make up your mind, it doesn’t hurt to try them out since they don’t cost you a penny.
