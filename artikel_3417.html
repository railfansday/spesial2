GTA 6 Guides - Locations, Progression, Relationships, & More

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

If any game has ever been fundamentally overwhelming, it's Grand Theft Auto 6. While the main missions of the game tend to be fairly straightforward, that's only the tip of the iceberg. The latest acclaimed installment in the iconic Rockstar Games franchise is packed to the brim with activities, locations, and secrets, and there's a fair bit of learning involved if you want to make the most of your trip to Leonida.

To that end, ScreenRant's guide coverage answers plenty of pressing questions about GTA 6. Whether you're trying to figure out fast travel or simply waiting to figure out when you can play as Lucia, these guides can clear up all the confusion.

With missions and activities strewn about across a massive map of Leonida, plenty of time in Grand Theft Auto 6 will be spent going from one place to the next. Sometimes, the journey matters more than the destination, and there's nothing wrong with taking a roundabout cruise. If you're itching to get things done, though, these guides should help with efficient navigation of the game.

Of course, navigation isn't the only critical part of progression in Grand Theft Auto 6. Building up finances will also open up more opportunities, for example, and it can also be helpful to get a general idea of how much progress you're making throughout the main story, especially in the extended opening material with Jason. In old-school fashion, there are also plenty of cheats that will help you manipulate the game to your own means.

The relationship between protagonists Jason and Lucia is at the center of Grand Theft Auto 6, although it does take a minute before you can play as both characters. Even at the beginning of the game, though, there are important steps you can take to increase the bond between Jason and Lucia. It's ultimately easy to build a strong relationship between the characters — and some missions will simply increase the bond without any extra work — so there's little reason not to ensure that Jason and Lucia are a rock-solid pair.

As GTA 6 has just landed, this is just the start of ScreenRant's guide coverage, with plenty more to follow as more and more of the game's secrets are uncovered.
