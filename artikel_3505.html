A Jesuit Priest Explains How He Facilitated Bob Dylan’s Meeting With the Pope

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

You’ve all seen the pictures by now. But now we know a little bit more about this week’s meeting between Bob Dylan and Pope Leo XIV, thanks to an email interview in Flagging Down the Double E’s, the Bob Dylan deep-dive Substack run by Dylan expert Ray Padgett.

According to Spadaro, it somehow became apparent that Dylan was interested in meeting the pope, and vice versa. “Leo was completely open to it,” said Spadaro.

The pope is, among many other things, an American baby boomer who, earlier this summer, referenced in a footnote in a treatise on peace a Dylan lyric so obscure that not even Padgett had ever heard of it.

This isn’t the first time Dylan fans have learned new information about the singer-songwriter from Padgett, a self-described fan whom Rolling Stone spoke with last year about his never-ending Dylan scoops.
