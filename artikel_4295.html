Pathogen P. aeruginosa uses protein to limit entry of last-resort antibiotic

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Bacteria have many strategies for defending themselves against antibiotics. Some destroy the drug, others pump it out or alter its target. They can also, more simply, prevent it from getting in. A team at the University of Geneva (UNIGE) has discovered how Pseudomonas aeruginosa, a bacterium responsible for infections that are often difficult to treat, very rapidly restricts the entry of imipenem, an antibiotic reserved for the most critical cases. Published in the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, these findings reveal a mechanism of antibiotic resistance and open up new avenues for eventually counteracting it.
