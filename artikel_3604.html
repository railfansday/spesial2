Sega Officially Reveals Huge Black Friday Sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Yakuza, Space Marines, blue hedgehogs, and more

Black Friday seems to get earlier and earlier every year. A week before the main festivities, Sega has launched its Black Friday 2026 sale, featuring discounts of up to 80% on certain titles. The sale is running across the Epic Games Store, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Steam, and Xbox — and it includes Sega and Atlus franchises such as Sonic the Hedgehog, Persona, and Like A Dragon.

Perhaps the best deal of the bunch is Like a Dragon: Infinite Wealth, the latest mainline entry in the long-standing Yakuza series, which is$13.99 (80% off). This 2024 turn-based RPG from Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio is a lengthy epic that takes 60 to 100 hours to complete. It’s the second game in the series centered on former yakuza Ichiban Kasuga, who this time sets out to find his estranged mother in Hawaii. The game received positive reviews, with critics praising its characters, turn-based gameplay, and ridiculous side missions.

Another major standout is Persona 3 Reload, the recent remake of a 2006 RPG, which is on sale for $20.99 (65% off). The game follows a high schooler who finds they are capable of summoning a Persona, a physical embodiment of their psyche (that is totally a JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure reference). The game earned strong reviews for its visuals and smart quality-of-life updates, even if it was criticized for omitting content from Persona 3 Portable, including its female protagonist.

Sega’s Black Friday sale also offers a significant discount on Total War: Warhammer 3, which is going for$8.99 (85% off). This 2022 strategy title is the latest Total War: Warhammer game from Creative Assembly. It received positive reviews for its deep strategy and interesting endgame, but eventually faced criticism over steep DLC prices. Creative Assembly has continued releasing add-ons for the game, putting out new content as recently as September.

Beyond these particularly deep discounts, there are also smaller savings on several well-received games from 2025, such as Two Point Museum, Shinobi: Art of Vengeance, and Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds.
