It's that time of year again — holiday shopping season.

I’m the friend everyone comes to for gift ideas, and I take that VERY seriously (in fact, my nickname during the holiday season is "Santa Bella"). Between way too many hours scrolling on my FYP and my group chats, I always know what's trending and actually worth the hype. This year, I'm also lending my gift giving expertise to all of you with a guide on all of the trending gifts you should get the chronically online loved one in your life. These are all gifts I guarantee won’t end in the back of someone’s closet.

You'll find everything from viral skincare, plushies everyone is collecting and a few wellness upgrades people have been eyeing all year. And since the upcoming Black Friday/Cyber Monday holiday is the best time to get a deal, every gift also comes with a TPG tip to help you get rewarded on the gifts you give.

Best for: Skincare lovers or those who need a hydration boost

The Medicube PDRN Collagen Gel Mask is viral for a reason. Put it on, leave it for a few hours, and peel it off once it turns clear. I absolutely love using this before a big event or after a long flight. It leaves my skin looking plump and hydrated. You can purchase this on Ulta, Amazon, Target, or on Medicube’s site.

TPG tip: Sign into Ulta Rewards before checking out as every purchase counts towards dollars you can redeem. Bonus tip: make sure you use Rakuten for extra cash back.

Best for: The plushie lover and collector

Jellycats are everyone’s favorite plushy, and definitely not just for kids. I have a ton of friends who collect them. The designs are adorable and the quality is great and some are country-exclusive (I really want the aviation one you can only get in London). Buying in person is half the fun, especially at FAO Schwarz in NYC. You can find them also in Nordstrom or Bloomingdales.

TPG tip: Join Nordstrom’s Nordy Club and log in before you buy to earn points towards a future purchase.

Best for: Anyone who wants to be wrapped up in a ball all winter.

This might be the most viral blanket ever! They have a 4-way stretch that makes it softest blanket you will ever own. They often offer discounts throughout the year, including a big discount when you sign up, so be sure to get one then. Bonus: the founders donate blankets every month to women going through breast cancer. Shop the blankets on their site or at Bloomingdales.

TPG tip: Wait for it to go on sale or sign up for their site to get 35% off the first order. Also make sure to use Rakuten to get cash back!

We grew up with Legos and somehow never outgrew it. Legos are the perfect gift for ANYONE. There are so many options from plants to planes, so there is one for literally everyone. I love that they can also act as decor in your home once you complete it. You can buy these almost anywhere, from the Lego Store itself to Best Buy.

TPG tip: Join LEGO Insiders to earn points on purchases from LEGO directly. If you are buying elsewhere, look at Rakuten’s cash back offer and check your card offers for a bonus.

Best for: Skincare lovers ready to level up their routine.

A face-sculpting tool for your jawline, cheekbones, and under-eyes. Hot and cold contrast therapy across six temperatures helps depuff, and a ton of people use it for muscle tension too! You can get it from Sephora, Ulta, Best Buy, Kohls, Amazon, and more!

TPG tip: Make sure you are logged into any of the above loyalty programs and get cash back from Rakuten.

Best for: The person who finally wants to tackle their wellness in 2027

Some even wear it all day as an accessory. I love the brand too: science-backed, sustainability-first, and made with every skin tone in mind.

TPG tip: Sign up for their rewards program to earn perks like birthday rewards and even a shot at one of their iconic brand trips. Sephora Beauty Insiders members can also purchase through Sephora.

Best for: Someone who loves to document every moment

You can’t scroll on Instagram or TikTok without seeing a sign to get a digital camera. Viral for a reason: the soft, nostalgic look, perfect sunset shots, and it’s small enough to toss into your bag. I shoot on the Canon G7x, which is nearly impossible to find right now, but there are plenty of comparable options for a fraction of the price.

TPG tip: Purchase from whatever store gives you the highest cash back on Rakuten. Since these cameras are offered in a ton of stores, check your card offers to see if a store offers a larger mile multiplier.

The designs and colors are so cute, and since wellness is at the top of everyone’s mind, having a glass or stainless steel one is key. TPG tip: Make sure to use a credit card that earns on online shopping to maximize your purchase.
