Beat me to it. Avalon's a big, boring, higher-end Camry type sedan. I'd watch Car Care Nut YouTube channel since he goes into details on which years have the fewest issues re: engine, transmission, brakes, etc.

I'm biased in favor of Toyota (if it's within the budget) since I'm done with unreliable headaches. Older Avalon or Camry in the right year/engine/transmission combo that has the fewest issues.

I rented a 2025(?) Chevy Malibu with like 40K miles recently and it was big and boring, but it felt cheap and rattly and didn't inspire confidence in long term reliability. I'd hit the gas at a stop sign and it would take 2+ seconds to move -- not sure if it was the Turbo or the Auto Start/Stop that I forgot to disable, but sometimes you need to go since cars are coming in the distance. The glovebox fell to the floor because of a known issue.

Maybe Hyundai Sonata but I'd do a deep dive on reliability and any known issues.

Not sure when the Ford Crown Victoria / Mercury Grand Marquis stopped production, but getting a used Crown Vic is a running joke on the [now defunct] FatWallet Finance forums. When I saw a Crown Vic beater on the road, I always thought, "I bet that person's a multi-millionaire."

Ford Fusion is out of production and not a full-size big sedan, but that might be okay to look into.
