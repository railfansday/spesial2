Asset Panda Expands IT Asset Tracking Software With Launch of Auto Discovery and Voice AI Capabilities

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

New features simplify IT asset tracking and introduce hands-free asset management through conversational AI

DALLAS, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Asset Panda, the leading asset management software, announced the launch of Auto Discovery, a new capability that automatically identifies devices connected to an organization's network and adds them to their Asset Panda Pro platform. The company also introduced new voice capabilities for its Ursa AI platform, enabling users to interact with their asset management system through natural, spoken conversations.

Together, the new capabilities are designed to reduce manual administrative work, improve IT asset visibility and make asset management more accessible for organizations managing growing inventories of technology and equipment.

"IT teams are responsible for managing increasingly complex networks of devices, yet many still rely on spreadsheets and manual processes to maintain accurate asset records," said Rex Kurzius, CEO of Asset Panda. "With Auto Discovery, our IT asset tracking software gives organizations a faster way to identify and track their technology assets, while our new Ursa AI voice capabilities make it easier than ever to access information and manage everyday tasks."

Auto Discovery enables organizations to scan their networks for connected devices and automatically add them to Asset Panda Pro, eliminating the need to manually create individual asset records or import spreadsheets. By providing a more comprehensive view of active IT assets and their statuses, the feature helps organizations strengthen asset control, improve inventory accuracy and make more informed decisions about resource allocation.

Alongside Auto Discovery, Asset Panda is expanding the capabilities of Ursa AI with new speech-to-text and text-to-speech functionality, allowing users to communicate with the platform without relying on a keyboard or mouse. Speech-to-text functionality supports approximately 100 languages and dialects, while text-to-speech capabilities offer a wide selection of voices and accents tailored to different languages and regions. Administrators can enable or disable voice functionality based on company policies and configure access for all users or designated groups.

"AI should make technology easier to use and help people get more done with less effort," Kurzius said. "By combining automated asset discovery with conversational AI, we're continuing to remove barriers between users and the information they need. Whether someone is managing an entire IT inventory or asking a simple question about an asset, our goal is to make those processes as intuitive and efficient as possible."

The launch builds on Asset Panda's continued investment in AI-powered asset management, including the introduction of Ursa AI and its visual inspection capabilities. By integrating automation and conversational AI directly into Asset Panda Pro, the company continues to help organizations streamline workflows, reduce manual data entry and improve visibility into the assets they rely on every day.

For more information about Asset Panda Pro and Auto Discovery, visit assetpanda.com.

Founded in 2012, Asset Panda is an asset tracking, inspection, audit, and compliance platform. Designed to help the world work smarter, Asset Panda Pro helps businesses track asset lifecycles, check items in and out, perform inspections, and more across web and mobile platforms. With Ursa AI, teams can set up customized accounts, fields, and workflows in minutes, saving valuable time and simplifying tasks. Asset Panda is committed to providing clients with outstanding customer service and constantly improving its technology. To learn more about Asset Panda, visit assetpanda.com.
