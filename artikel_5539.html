CobbleStone® Launches Compliance+ Module to Streamline Third-Party Compliance Screening

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The new Compliance+ Module consolidates leading compliance screening data sources into a single, flexible solution for vendor and client due diligence.

PRINCETON, N.J., Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- CobbleStone Software, a recognized leader in contract lifecycle management (CLM) and contract artificial intelligence, today announced the launch of its new Compliance+ Module, a streamlined compliance screening solution designed to simplify third-party due diligence and compliance management across organizations.

Organizations face growing regulatory obligations and increased scrutiny of vendor, supplier, healthcare provider, and client relationships. In these struggles, compliance teams often rely on multiple screening tools and data sources to conduct proper due diligence.
