Jarrett Sidaway Named CEO of Slickdeals

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

SAN MATEO, Calif., Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Slickdeals, the community-powered platform helping millions of shoppers discover the best deals online, today announced that Jarrett Sidaway has joined the company as Chief Executive Officer. Sidaway brings extensive experience across consumer shopping and digital commerce, with a deep understanding of how product discovery is evolving as shoppers turn to trusted communities and AI for guidance.

Sidaway previously served as CEO of RetailMeNot and General Manager of Ziff Davis Shopping. Earlier in his career, he served as General Manager of Coupons at Groupon and held leadership roles at Updater and Raise. Throughout his career, he has led businesses operating at the intersection of consumer shopping, savings and digital commerce.

As CEO, Sidaway will lead Slickdeals into its next phase of growth, building on what makes the company distinctive: a community of shoppers who find, evaluate and share deals. Their collective knowledge separates real value from noise and helps millions of people make confident buying decisions.

"Jarrett brings a strong track record of leading and growing digital businesses, along with a deep understanding of the deals industry," said Albert Lee, Managing Director of Crescent Capital Group. "Slickdeals has built something difficult to replicate: a highly engaged community that helps shoppers discover and evaluate deals. We believe Jarrett can build on that advantage and help the company reach its next stage of growth."

"What drew me to Slickdeals is the strength of its community and the product-level focus that sets it apart from other shopping sites. Those are difficult assets to build, and Slickdeals has both," said Sidaway. "I've spent my career helping businesses build on what they do best, and I see a clear opportunity to do that with Slickdeals. I look forward to learning from our community and team as we build on those strengths together."

Slickdeals is a community-powered shopping platform where millions of shoppers share, vote on and discuss deals from leading retailers and brands. Since 1999, its community has put deals to the test, surfacing the best offers and calling out the rest, and helped shoppers save more than $10 billion. Through its website and mobile app, Slickdeals connects shoppers with relevant deals they wouldn't discover on their own. Shop smarter, together.
