Bolton hit back to hold Portsmouth

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Bolton Wanderers vs Portsmouth. Sky Bet Championship.

Report and free match highlights from the Sky Bet Championship match between Bolton Wanderers and Portsmouth at the Toughsheet Community Stadium on Saturday; Josh Murphy nets early for Pompey before Sam Dalby hits back for Trotters as spoils are shared.

Please use Chrome browser for a more accessible video player

Bolton nemesis Josh Murphy helped Portsmouth secure a 1-1 Championship draw at the Toughsheet Community Stadium.

Murphy's first goal of the season put Pompey in front but Sam Dalby ensured Wanderers avoided a fourth successive home defeat with his seventh goal of the season and third in four games.

Despite this frenetic opening 12 minutes neither side could celebrate a first game back after the international break with three points.

Murphy ensured the worst case of deja vu for Wanderers, now unbeaten in 15 second-tier games against Pompey but still adjusting to life after leaving League One last term.

He scored the two first-half goals that saw Oxford stun the Trotters in the 2024 League One play-off final at Wembley.

This time it took him seven minutes to register his opening goal of the campaign and first since March 2025, heading in a deep right-wing cross from Northern Ireland international Terry Devlin.

But Steven Schumacher's side crucially levelled five minutes later. Max Conway, atoning for giving possession away for the game's first goal, crossed from the left and Dalby side footed home inside the six-yard box.

Goalkeeper Nicolas Schmid, however, should have done better as he allowed the striker's shot to go through his legs in front of his own supporters.

Top scorer Dalby should then have put Bolton in front in first half stoppage time.

Excellent work by Sam Iling-Junior fashioned the chance but Dalby could not convert the winger's cross on the slide from four yards.

Portsmouth, beaten only once now in seven outings, started the second half positively with Murphy seeing a shot deflected for a corner.

Bolton though were more than a match for their visitors as they looked to improve this season's dismal home form.

Schmid saved from midfielder Xavier Simons' shot as both sides rang the changes in an attempt to make the breakthrough.

Portsmouth's hopes of a second goal though were repeatedly dashed by a succession of offsides while Wanderers

Teenager Max Lott headed wide shortly after entering the fray while defender Chris Forino tested Schmid in the final stages of normal time with a close-range header.

Forino was the focal point for Bolton's late raids but again Schmid comfortably dealt with his latest attempt via a Lewis Brunt cross.

"It was a decent performance apart from the first 10 minutes when they got in front.

"We looked a bit edgy and made a few unforced errors. But I thought after that we settled down really nicely, we played well for the rest of the first half, deserved to be level and should have been ahead before half time.

"And I think it would have been a deserved lead. He (Iling-Junior) had to hit the pass hard to get it past the keeper but the ball just bobbled up and Sam couldn't get his right boot on it.

"There were more positives than negatives," added Schumacher of a display that nudged his side upto 17th, eight points above the relegation zone.

"Defensively, we improved and limited Portsmouth to only one shot on target. That takes a whole team effort.

"And we have a setback but the lads always try and respond. We try and stay calm, don't lose our minds and start pointing fingers and screaming at each other.

"It just looked like there was one team wanting to win the game."

"We came away with a point having not played particularly well in the first half. So, there is much more to come from the group.

"We were inconsistent. There were some good bits, some poor bits and everything in between.

"The overall message to the group is 'well done' because we have come away to a tough place against a good side and taken a point.

"I put out a side I thought could win us the game. But when you strip it back because of what we are contending with the squad, I think it is a decent achievement.

"But I still think we can be better both as individuals and as a team unit."
