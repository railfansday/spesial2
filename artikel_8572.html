Are you aware about the limitations of the Rürup Rente?
Generally high administrative fees, which is a nogo for this Boglehead, limitations on inheriting, no lump sum distribution- only monthly pension payments after age 62, fully taxable( not as investment income), cannot be terminated ever.
There will be new legislation in 2027 for a new Altersvorsorgedepot ( pensionplan). I would wait to see what investment vehicles for self employed will be available. Do you want to use Rürup to lower your current tax burden?
No suggestion on the Qef statement. They are not available.
