Tanggal: 2026-11-06

If your parents are only withdrawing RMDs, their overall portfolio withdrawal rate right now is ~2.5% which is likely perpetual.

I think they/you are on the right track in moving their portfolio to Fidelity using low-ER diversified funds/ETFs and away from the high fee/ER advisor. But to get the best feedback on a new Fidelity portfolio, it would be helpful to post their financial situation as there is a lot of information not in your post - desired AA, marginal tax rate, etc. Use the template in “Asking Portfolio Questions”:
https://www.bogleheads.org/wiki/Asking_ ... _questions

Include in your revised post the amount of unrealized gains in their current Taxable account by holding. The annual ER savings will help offset the one-time taxes on any Taxable gains realized. But depending on the amount of unrealized gains, they may want to stagger the sales over 2 tax years (late 2026/early 2027) if it saves them $ on taxes. Also keep in mind the AGI impact of such realized gains as it may increase their Medicare premiums (IRMAA) which is a one year impact in two years, if applicable.
