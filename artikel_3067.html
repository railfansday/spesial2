Sociedad Química (SQM) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Management says lithium demand keeps climbing despite cooler prices.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 18, 2026 at 2:25PM EST

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Sociedad Química y Minera de Chile (SQM -4.94%) reported a significant increase in net income during the first nine months of the year, driven by steady demand across its primary lithium and iodine business lines. Management highlighted that while lithium prices have moderated from earlier peaks, global demand continues to expand through electric vehicle adoption and the growth of battery energy storage systems. The company is maintaining its long-term capital expenditure plans and production efficiency initiatives despite recent pricing fluctuations in the fertilizer and lithium markets. Executive commentary focused on the strategic positioning of Chilean and Australian assets to meet projected global demand growth through the end of the decade.
