US blacklists Megaspeed, the chip firm at the centre of the Nvidia scandal

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Entity List addition came the same day Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive.

The US Commerce Department has put Megaspeed, the Singapore cloud company at the centre of the Nvidia scandal, on its trade blacklist, after officials and lawmakers spent weeks asking why it was taking so long.

Moreover, the Entity List filing also caught six firms seen as Megaspeed affiliates in Malaysia, Singapore, and Thailand.

A place on the list means US firms need a licence to sell it goods, software, or tech, and such requests are usually refused. Listed firms also struggle to get spare parts and updates for gear they already run.

US investigators have spent more than a year looking into whether Megaspeed, long one of Nvidia’s bigger customers, funnelled advanced chips into China, where export rules bar their sale. The Justice Department has opened a criminal inquiry, and Nvidia says it chose to stop selling to the firm in June 2025.

Why the listing took so long had become a sore point at the top of the US government. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent confronted Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick about it at a White House meeting this month, Axios reported, though Commerce says that never happened.

Four departments had wanted Megaspeed listed for months, but “commerce has the pen,” Bessent told CNBC this month.

The scandal runs through the company’s former boss, Alice Huang, who had a relationship with Nvidia chief Jensen Huang. US spy agencies reportedly came to suspect her of working for Chinese intelligence, a claim that was not confirmed until now.

Jensen Huang resigned as CEO on 11 November, after 33 years running the company he co-founded. On the same day Megaspeed was blacklisted, Nvidia’s board voted unanimously to reinstate him.

Megaspeed denies doing anything wrong and says Alice Huang left the company in August 2025. The rule took effect on Monday, when it was filed for public inspection, and formal publication in the Federal Register follows on Wednesday.
