Starwood-Trez venture targets $1B a year in residential land loans

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Starwood Capital Group and Trez Capital have formed a strategic partnership to originate residential land acquisition and development loans for single-family communities across the United States, the firms announced Nov. 3.

The new venture pairs Starwood’s institutional capital and investment platform with Trez’s residential land financing business. The relationship will expand Trez Capital’s lending capacity while giving Starwood another channel to finance land deals that support its long-running investment focus in residential land and master-planned communities.

The partnership is expected to be seeded with a $450 million portfolio of U.S. residential land acquisition and development loans originated by Trez Capital and will target more than $1 billion in annual loan originations, according to the announcement.

Executives at both firms framed the tie-up as a way to scale into what they see as a durable opportunity created by the U.S. housing shortage and steady demand for new single-family homes, especially in markets with sustained population growth and diverse employment bases.

“Trez Capital has a strong track record in U.S. residential land lending, and we look forward to leveraging our collective strengths through this relationship,” said Anthony Murphy, managing director at Starwood Capital. “Bringing together Starwood’s institutional capital, deep expertise in real estate financing and master-planned community development with Trez Capital’s capabilities in residential land financing will uniquely position us to capitalize on sector tailwinds as a premier lending platform.”

“This strategic partnership represents a significant endorsement of Trez Capital’s financing expertise,” said John D. Hutchinson, co-chief executive officer and global head of origination at Trez Capital. “Combining Starwood’s institutional investment capabilities with Trez Capital’s origination, underwriting, loan servicing and asset management platform will enable us to scale our U.S. residential land development lending business significantly.”

John Maragliano, Trez Capital co-CEO and chief financial officer, said the partnership advances the firm’s push to deepen its institutional investor base and should support “stronger returns and liquidity” for Trez investors.

For homebuilders, developers and land bankers, the Starwood–Trez vehicle adds another large-scale source of capital at a time when traditional construction and A&D lending standards remain tight and carry higher interest costs.

By targeting more than $1 billion in annual originations, the platform could influence pricing and availability of horizontal development capital for single-family projects, especially in growth markets where public and large private builders are still actively buying lots despite affordability challenges.

For public and large private builders that rely on optioned lots and third-party land developers, expanded A&D capacity from institutional platforms like this can support lot pipelines without tying up as much balance sheet capital, but it may also increase competition for well-located tracts.

Miami-based Starwood Capital Group, founded in 1991, has raised more than $95 billion of capital and manages over $130 billion in assets across global real estate and related strategies. In June 2026, the firm closed its latest opportunistic real estate fund, Starwood Distressed Opportunity Fund XIII, with more than $10.2 billion in commitments from more than 300 investors in about 20 countries.

Vancouver-based Trez Capital, founded in 1997, focuses on commercial real estate debt and equity in Canada and the U.S. Trez Corporate Group oversees more than C$4.7 billion in assets under management and has funded over 1,800 transactions totaling more than C$23 billion since inception, according to the company.
