Ben Jacobs Drops Update on Everton Move to Sign Man Utd Star Joshua Zirkzee

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Joshua Zirkzee could leave Manchester United in January, despite his re-emergence into the first team fold. Zirkzee's future at Old Trafford looked grim in the summer, and he was close to leaving after falling down the pecking order under Michael Carrick.

However, an agreement could not be reached and Zirkzee remained at United. But his game time stayed the same, and he had played just 186 minutes of first-team football, scoring one goal, this season before Carrick was sacked during the November international break.

Mauricio Pochettino then arrives as United's new head coach and Zirkzee has another chance. With United 2-0 behind and down to 10-men at Liverpool, Zirkzee was brought on at half-time and impressed with limited service during the second period.

That, alongside others struggling, gave Zirkzee a second start of the season for United's trip to Sporting CP in the Champions League on Wednesday. Pochettino is a huge fan of the Netherlands international and started him up front at the Jose Alvalade Stadium.

Although Zirkzee didn't score or assist, he did well in the lone striker position. He was a physical presence, held the ball up and had a couple of shots before being replaced by Patrick Dorgu in the 63rd minute.

There is a real chance that Zirkzee could keep his place in the XI for United's crucial home game against Brentford on Saturday, as it's clear Pochettino rates him as he looks to revolutionise Man Utd. But that isn't expected to change United's long-term stance on Zirkzee.

Indeed, according to Ben Jacobs, Zirkzee could still leave United in January for the 'right price', in a window where United have made a £60m France international their 'dream target'.

He continues to add that the Reds might be tempted to sell if they find an adequate replacement, with Everton one of the sides who could look to make a move for the Netherlands international.

"There’s a possibility, by the way, that Joshua Zirkzee could leave at the right price, and at the right time, and if a replacement is found, and Zirkzee and Serie A might be one to watch," Jacobs said, while speaking in his latest newsletter.

"Zirkzee and Everton, potentially at the right price and the right structure, but again, Manchester United would need a replacement, and like I said before, the player seems very open to moving to Italy."

While there is considerable talk about his future, Zirkzee's main focus will be on United and helping them turn their fortunes around following a dismal start to the season. The Reds go into Saturday's game against Brentford down in 19th in the table, having won just once in the Premier League all campaign.
