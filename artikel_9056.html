Trump Staffer Unwittingly Reveals Smithsonian Takeover Plan

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Reviews of the institution's exhibitions, termination of the American History Museum director, and financial restructuring are all on the line.

With no sign of ceasing its attacks on the Smithsonian Institution, the White House could further upend the museum and research network, according to a supposed Trump staffer's documents for a tentative overhaul strategy, which were photographed on a Metro train in Washington, DC.

The photos, which a commuter took and dispatched to ProPublica, outline 25 items under the "Smithsonian Institution Renewal Plan," including the firing of National Museum of American History (NMAH) director Anthea Hartig, planned exhibitions on American nationalism, a redesigned logo and brand, and new institution-wide guidelines. If implemented, the measures could give the White House unparalleled authority over the Smithsonian's operations.

While the identity of the staffer has not been confirmed, ProPublica's report from today, November 23, speculates that it was Sam Bock, an associate policy adviser for the Trump-appointed Domestic Policy Council, which produced a draconian 162-page report in July that accused museum leadership, including Hartig, of promoting “extreme political activism."
