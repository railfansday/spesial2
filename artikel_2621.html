Jazz Chisholm Jr., Blue Jays reportedly finalizing contract in free agency

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Two-time MLB All-Star Jazz Chisholm Jr. is finalizing a deal with the Toronto Blue Jays in free agency, FanSided's Robert Murray reports.

The 28-year-old infielder has spent parts of the last three seasons with the New York Yankees following a 2024 trade from the Miami Marlins. Over 313 regular-season games in the Bronx, Chisholm recorded 61 home runs and 158 RBI. He set career highs with 31 home runs and 80 RBI during the 2025 season, which helped earn him his second All-Star nod and first Silver Slugger Award.

It was in the playoffs where Chisholm struggled at the plate, however. The lefty batted .182 in each of the past three postseasons for the Yankees, totaling just three home runs and three RBI, while striking out 21 times in 99 plate appearances. He also had issues defensively, registering 34 total errors in New York -- 13 at third base and 21 at second base.
