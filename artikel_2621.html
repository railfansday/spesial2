Sergey Bilostenniy outpoints Vadim Nemkov to become PFL heavyweight champion

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

SergeyBilostenniy was the superior fighter in the main event ofPFL Dubai at Coca-Cola Arena in Dubai, UAE, on Saturday, as hewrested the PFL heavyweight championship from reigning champVadimNemkov.Bilostenniy (16-4) controlled the pace for much of the five roundsand kept Nemkov (20-3, 1 NC) at bay with timely head punches, mixedwith several leg kicks. Nemkov, a former Bellator light heavyweightchampion, tried to use his grappling prowess early and nearlylanded a belly-down armbar in the first, but Bilostenniy was wiseand kept the fight off the canvas the rest of the way.Bilostenniy busted up Nemkov's face with jabs and wore his fellowRussian countryman down, and when the final sounded, there was nodoubt as to who the victor was. In the end, two of the cagesidejudges favored Bilostenniy with scores of 48-47, while the thirdsaw it for him 49-46, ending Nemkov's 10-year winning streak.

Rising welterweight contender PatrickHabirora soared to 10-0 with an impressive first-roundtechnical submission of Egypt's Omar ElDafrawy. The two fighters tore into each other early and often,but after a wild scramble on the ground, "The Belgian Bomber"locked in a guillotine, stood up, squeezed harder, and put ElDafrawy (16-7) to sleep. The end came at 3:11 of the stanza, givingHabirora his biggest win thus far.OlegPopov made it look easy against fellow heavyweight PouyaRahmani, dominating him for three rounds. Popov (24-2) landed afew decent strikes on the feet, but most of the fight took place onthe ground, where Rahmani (5-1) was overwhelmed. All three judgesfavored Popov with scores of 30-27, 30-26 and 30-25.JojiGoto scored arguably the biggest win of his MMA career bydominating longtime UFC veteran SergioPettis for three rounds at bantamweight, winning a lopsidedunanimous decision. Goto (21-9) landed the far better strikes,controlled the pace, and dominated Pettis (25-9) on the ground. Inthe end, Goto won the contest via tallies of 30-27 across theboard.LewisMcGrillen-Evans landed dozens of punches to the head and bodyand then landed a knee to NawrasAbzakh's face as the first round ended, and that was all theEnglishman needed to soar to 14-2.
