Super Bowl LXI will be shown in IMAX theaters

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Super Bowl will be shown on some very big screens this season.

ESPN’s broadcast of Super Bowl LXI will be shown live in IMAX theaters as part of an agreement among the NFL, Disney and IMAX.

The game will air live in IMAX theaters in the U.S., Australia, New Zealand and Brazil. The show will include the game itself, the halftime show, pregame and postgame.

IMAX has grown increasingly popular for its huge, curved screens and immersive sound. The format is known for movies like The Odyssey, not for live sports, but the opportunity to watch the Super Bowl on the biggest screen in town will draw plenty of fans who will be curious to see what the game looks like on a screen so big that the players are larger than life.
