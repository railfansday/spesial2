The 2027 Rogue Hybrid is the first Nissan sold in the United States to utilize the brand’s e-Power hybrid powertrain.

The new Rogue uses a series-hybrid setup.

That means the turbocharged 1.5-liter three-cylinder engine serves exclusively as a generator and never powers the wheels.

Power instead comes from a pair of electric motors, with one motor situated at each axle.

The mid-grade SR trim will arrive this year, with the SV and Platinum grades scheduled to arrive in January 2027.

Pricing for the new Rogue Hybrid starts at $37,065.
