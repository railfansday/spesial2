Rosalind Eleazar Brings the Frills to ELLE’s 2026 Women in Hollywood Event

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Rosalind Eleazar walked the red carpet at ELLE’s 2026 Women in Hollywood event in a look fit for a star.

The Slow Horses actress arrived at Monday night’s event, which took place at The Four Seasons Beverly Hills, wearing a tailored Ralph Lauren ensemble, featuring a patterned beige tailcoat over a ruffled blouse and matching dark trousers. She wore her signature curls in an updo, revealing her dainty dangling earrings.

In Eleazar’s recent ELLE cover story, she opened up about working with Chris Rock on their film, Misty Green, and revealed that she deeply appreciated him giving her creative control over the movie’s titular character.

“He has this expression, ‘My movie, your character,’” she recalled. “He allowed me to really explore Misty, because she’s unique and wild and untethered. In order for me to do that, I needed to also feel untethered and without boundaries.”

Misty Green, released in October, earned Eleazar widespread critical acclaim and potential Oscar buzz. The actress, however, is hoping that the role simply allows her more options in Hollywood.

“I do hope that it will give me more choice,” she added. “But our job is not like a lawyer, where you start as a junior associate, and you can see your trajectory to partner. It’s just not like that. It depends on what’s around. It depends on where you are in your career. It depends on your age.”
