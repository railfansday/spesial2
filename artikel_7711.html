Weil, Gotshal &amp; Manges creates AI-centric 1L summer program

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

“I don’t believe that to be a great lawyer that is AI-enabled someone needs to have deep technical history,” Andrew Simon, the chief AI and innovation officer at Weil, Gotshal & Manges, told Law.com. (Photo from Shutterstock)

Weil, Gotshal & Manges is launching an artificial intelligence-based program for 2027 1L summer associates that will pair law students with the firm’s AI and innovation team, the law firm announced Wednesday.

The 10-week program, now accepting applications, includes coding lessons in Python, developing skills to build a neural network, and working on active legal projects. Two members of the cohort will work directly with a member of the Lawyer Augmented Solutions Engineering and Research team, and the students will get offers to return for the following year’s 2L program.

“I don’t believe that to be a great lawyer that is AI-enabled someone needs to have deep technical history,” Andrew Simon, the chief AI and innovation officer at Weil, Gotshal & Manges, told Law.com. “Anyone who wants to learn this stuff—sufficient enough to be an advanced AI builder as a lawyer—can do so.”

The focus on learning the Python programming language and the PyTorch machine learning framework aims to help students develop usable tools.
