Russian ship hit in Benghazi by suspected Ukrainian drone attack, say Libyan officials

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

No one has claimed responsibility for the strike on the vessel, which had docked on its way back to Russia

A suspected Ukrainian drone attack caused an explosion on a Russian warship docked at the naval base in the Libyan port city of Benghazi, Libyan officials have said.

The strike severely damaged the Merkury, a Russian navy warship, and ignited a fire at the naval base, they said. Several Ukrainian sea drones were launched at the ship from a base run by Ukrainian forces in western Libya, according to an initial investigation into the attack. At least four drones were intercepted and destroyed, while two hit the warship, the officials told the Associated Press.

The officials were affiliated with the self-styled Libyan National Army, which controls eastern and southern Libya.

There was no immediate reaction from Russian authorities, and no immediate claim of responsibility for the strike, but it comes as Ukrainian forces have stepped up their long-range barrages against Russia far beyond the frontlines.

Authorities in divided Libya’s east said in a statement that the vessel had stopped at Benghazi port to refuel and ⁠resupply on its way back to Russia, ​under maritime cooperation agreements between ‌the two ‌countries.

Two medical sources told Reuters no deaths had been reported, but there were injuries. The ⁠number and nationalities of those injured are not yet known.

In a separate attack, another sea drone hit a commercial vessel off the port of Benghazi on Sunday evening, Libyan military sources told Reuters.

The ‌eastern-based Libyan authority said port and naval base facilities, including warehouses, were safe and that ​normal operations would resume once the fire was brought under control and the site secured.

It condemned the suspected attack as a violation of Libya’s sovereignty and ⁠a threat to maritime safety, saying it would take ​legal and ​diplomatic measures against ​anyone found responsible. “The Libyan government will not ​allow Libyan ‌ports, territory or territorial ​waters ​to be turned into an arena for settling scores or exporting international conflicts,” it said.

The specialist website Naval News also identified the ship attacked as the Merkury, a Russian corvette – the smallest class of warship, smaller than a frigate – and said it was almost certainly destroyed.

HI Sutton, a naval analyst, said the attack was probably conducted by sea drones, after a photograph of one emerged apparently taken off the Libyan coast. The drone, he said, “is also likely the type used in the subsequent attack”.

If it is confirmed that Ukraine has been able to blow up or seriously damage a Russian warship in Benghazi, it would represent one of its most successful naval strikes during the almost five-year war.

But it also represents a risky extension of the fighting far beyond eastern Europe, in an incident that could affect civilians in another continent and the working of the port.

A small unit of Ukrainian special forces operatives has been based at Misrata, western Libya, and is believed to have coordinated drone attacks on Russian tankers and transport ships in the past year.

Initial reports and footage suggest the Merkury was struck from above, potentially by a drone, launched no more than a few kilometres away from the target in port.

Russia is close to the Benghazi authorities that control eastern Libya. It uses the port, which has a growing importance since it lost bases in Syria, to supply Russian mercenary forces in Africa and as a base in the Mediterranean.

Separately, a fire onboard a Russian cargo ship that was ⁠struck by drones in the ⁠Baltic Sea continued to smoulder ​on Sunday, Finland’s border guard said, with emergency services from Finland, Russia and Estonia working together to put out the blaze.

The Finnish military said the strike on the Sparta II ⁠on Saturday was probably carried out by Ukraine and that the vessel later drifted into Finnish waters, where it remained at anchor. The 16 crew members were evacuated on to a nearby Russian ship. One crew member was slightly ⁠injured.

On Sunday, visible flames had been put out, with operations focusing on cooling down the containers and stabilising the vessel, Finnish authorities said.

The ship, bound for the Russian exclave of Kaliningrad, had been damaged in international waters west of Russia’s Gogland Island, Finland’s defence forces said, corroborating an account by Russian officials.

Kyiv has sent waves of military drones towards the Leningrad region on Russia’s north-west coast this year, including some that strayed into the airspace of nearby Baltic countries, leading to fears that the war was spilling on to Nato territory.

The risk of ⁠further attacks in the Gulf of Finland, a narrow arm of the Baltic Sea, could not be ruled ​out, Ivo Värk, the Estonian navy commander, told the public ​broadcaster ERR on Sunday.

Finland’s military said last ​week it had detected a strike on a Russian warship in the same area during Ukrainian drone attacks.
