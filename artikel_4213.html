New quasar record tightens constraints on black hole formation scenarios

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A team of astronomers led by Julien Wolf, a postdoctoral researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Astronomy (MPIA) in Heidelberg, Germany (now at the European Southern Observatory in Garching bei München, Germany), discovered the most distant quasar to date. First identified in the Euclid Wide Survey (EWS), the object was confirmed to be a quasar by subsequent observations with some of the most powerful telescopes on the ground and in space.
