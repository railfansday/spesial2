Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade Postponed to Saturday Due to Storm

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Thanksgiving morning will not feature the Macy’s Parade this year due to extreme weather in New York.

“The safety of our participants and spectators is our top priority, and after close consultation with our City and agency partners, we have made the decision to move this year’s celebration. Due to the expected severe weather, the 100th March of Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade will move to Saturday, Nov. 28,” Macy’s said in a statement to NBC News on Monday. “Our team is working through the operational logistics for this change and will share details as they are finalized. We expect the Parade to kick off at 8:30a.m. on Saturday. Thank you to the thousands of volunteers, performers and partners who create the magic millions enjoy each year. On Thanksgiving Day, NBC will instead telecast the 2025 Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade at 8:30 a.m. in all time zones.

Ahead of the announcement, organizers behind the parade had been monitoring the weather situation all week, worrying that strong winds in New York City would ground the iconic balloons. Parade rules prohibit giant balloons from flying if sustained winds exceed 23 mph or if gusts are 34 mph or stronger.

Over 100 balloons and balloon handlers were set to be on display in celebration of the parade’s 100th anniversary. Buckaroo Bob the Minion, Joe Cool, Mickey’s Sorcerer’s Apprentice and Billie Brightpaw were among the balloon characters that had to be grounded.

Preparations for the parade balloons take months, with a village of creatives working behind the scenes to bring everything together. As early as July, the parade’s concept artist begins working on both balloon and float designs. By September, the parade’s costume designer, Kimberly Montgomery, starts piecing together over 5,000 costumes and collaborating with partners to ensure she has the right colors for the balloon handlers and accurate designs.

Float performances on the schedule include Natasha Bedingfield, McKenna Grace, Jimmy Fallon & The Roots, Queen Latifah and a tribute to Dolly Parton led by Megan Hilty.

“Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade brings the extraordinary together: iconic entertainers and emerging talent, artistry and imagination, hometown pride, cherished traditions and the thrill of discovering something new,” said Sharon Otterman, Macy’s chief marketing officer. “For our 100th Parade, we’re creating a celebration of unprecedented scale and will deliver an unforgettable Thanksgiving for millions across the country.”

NBC and Peacock are still set to air “Countdown to Macy’s 100th Thanksgiving Day Parade” Wednesday at 8 p.m. ET.
