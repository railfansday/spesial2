‘South Park’ Takes Aim at Jensen Huang, Nvidia Spy Scandal in Next Episode

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The town hunts for a Chinese spy amid a series of mysterious power outages in Wednesday's episode.

The espionage allegations surrounding Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang and America’s recent series of software outages and cyberattacks appear to be the next cultural targets to be mocked on South Park (rebranded South America for the current season).

Wednesday’s episode is titled “City Endings.” The official description reads: “When mysterious power outages strike South America, the townspeople fear Chinese cyberattacks. It’s up to the boys to discover what’s really going on.”

In a brief promo for the episode (watch it below), a local declares, “Someone in this town is a Chinese spy.” In another scene, Tuong Lu Kim, also known as the City Wok Guy, is announcing the grand opening of his new massage parlor called City Massage (a name which takes on a different meaning when you hear how co-creator Trey Parker voices it). The new episode will have its debut on Comedy Central at 10 p.m. ET and will stream on Thursday on Paramount+.

South Park is coming off last year’s Emmy-winning season, which also set some modern-day ratings records for the show. The show has made appearances in the Nielsen streaming Top 10 this season but has come down a bit from last year’s highs, when the show pivoted into a series of topical attacks on the Trump administration. The series rebranded as South America to mock President Trump’s penchant for trying to rename geographic features.
