Princess Kate Wows in Even More Red on Her Final Day in Mumbai

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

She’s ending her overseas tour on a high note

Today marks the end of Princess Catherine and Prince William’s three-day trip in Mumbai, India, and for one of her final appearances, the Princess of Wales brought out yet another striking—and meaningful—look.

Early this morning, the royal pair stopped by the Chanakya School of Craft to see a presentation of local, artisan work, before Kate made a solo trip to the Nowrosjee Wadia Maternity Hospital.

For these engagements, the princess looked resplendent in a bright-red wool crepe dress from one of her go-to British brands: Emilia Wickstead.

The design was conceived to look like separates, with a short-sleeve hourglass jacket stitched to a matching A-line skirt with ruffles. The bodice closed with a row of buttons in front, and it featured two decorative pocket flaps and notch lapels along the collar.

This isn’t the first time Kate has worn red on their overseas tour. Just yesterday, she attended the 2026 Earthshot Prize Awards in a vermilion anarkali gown, custom-made by Indian designer Mahima. Traditionally, red has been used to denote purity and power in Indian culture, and it is often a color worn by brides.

To contrast her latest red design, the princess accessorized with chocolate suede slingbacks from Manolo Blahnik, while carrying the custom DeMellier x SurpRice handbag in mocha that she debuted on the first day of their trip.

Finally, she stacked a few golden bangles on her wrist, including a Sézane cuff and a Halcyon Days torque bracelet, which matched her chandelier Cassandra Goad earrings. For a meaningful touch, she also wore a beaded macramé bracelet that was reportedly gifted to her by a local.

For her first overseas tour with Prince William in over four years, Kate has certainly not disappointed with her wardrobe.
