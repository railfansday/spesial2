These five names make sense for Georgia defensive coordinator job

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Georgia football will have a new defensive coordinator in 2027.

Florida State is expected to hire Glenn Schumann as its head coach, a source confirmed to the Athens Banner-Herald on Monday. Schumann replaces Mike Norvell.

Schumann was defensive coordinator for four seasons after serving as co-defensive coordinator with Will Muschamp in 2002.

History tells us Smart may look in-house for the replacement.

Five of the six times he's replaced an offensive or defensive coordinator, he hired someone already on staff.

That includes promoting outside linebackers coach Dan Lanning to defensive coordinator in 2019 and inside linebackers coach Glenn Schumann and assistant Will Muschamp to co-defensive coordinators when Lanning was hired by Oregon in December of 2021.

The only outside hire Smart made for a coordinator was Todd Monken from the NFL in 2020 to replace offensive coordinator James Coley.
