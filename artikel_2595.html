Elic Ayomanor posts career-high 79 yards receiving vs. Jaguars in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Tennessee Titans WR Elic Ayomanor caught five of 10 targets for 79 yards in Week 10 against the Jacksonville Jaguars.

Why It Matters: Ayomanor, who had a big play of 50 yards, set career highs in catches and yards in this one - the team's second straight game with Carnell Tate. The effort comes one game later than fantasy managers hoped, as it was hoped that Ayomanor would fill in for Tate better than he did in the team's last game (Week 8), when he could muster only three catches and 34 yards. If Tate misses another game with an injury in Week 11, Ayomanor could be worth using as a flex option against a vulnerable Dallas Cowboys defense.
