Luka Dončić shoved by fan in bizarre wristband-tossing incident after Lakers' loss to Spurs

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Los Angeles Lakers blowing a 20-point lead against the San Antonio Spurs on Friday was bad. What happened immediately after was ugly, with Lakers star Luka Dončić getting shoved by a courtside fan.

The incident began when Dončić tossed an armband at a female fan who appeared to be jeering him as he walked by. Dončić took a few more steps, then turned around and got the wristband back from a male fan, who picked up the armband then gave Dončić a one-armed shove as the All-Star started walking away.

Dončić immediately turned around, but Lakers forward Jarred Vanderbilt stepped in front of him while a few staffers immediately surrounded the male fan.

It's unknown what was said before and after the shove, but the NBA will certainly be looking into it.

For his part, Dončić told reporters after the game that he went back to the fans because they told him they didn't want his wristband:

"I threw my wristband, like I do almost every game, and then I heard they didn't want it, so I went to try to grab it and he just pushed me. Then I gave it to somebody else."

Before the incident, Dončić posted 31 points on 14-of-31 shooting with 8 assists, 6 rebounds, 3 steals and 3 turnovers in a 99-96 loss for the Lakers, who had won 11 of their last 12 games entering Friday. The loss officially eliminated the Lakers from the NBA Cup, as they finished with a 2-2 record in group play.
