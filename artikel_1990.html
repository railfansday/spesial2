Sources: Barcelona outline formula for Rayan Ait-Nouri deal after Man City January decision

Barcelona would prefer to sign Manchester City left-back Rayan Ait-Nouri on loan in January rather than agreeing a permanent deal, sources have told Football Insider.

The Spanish giants are battling sustained financial issues at the moment, and are “not willing” to meet City’s £30million asking price in the middle of the season.

Nevertheless, a deal between the two clubs could still take place this winter, with Ait-Nouri starved of football at the Etihad Stadium right now.

Sources say that Enzo Maresca’s side would prefer to move Ait-Nouri abroad, rather than sanction a deal with Manchester United, who have also shown a consistent interest.

Deals between the two clubs have been few and far between in recent years, and City are not keen on the idea of strengthening their bitter rivals.

Barcelona’s interest is a welcome development for the Citizens, as it could get them out of a potentially tricky situation.

Ait-Nouri has been firmly on the fringes of City’s squad so far this term, and is still waiting for his first minutes of the campaign in the Premier League.

He finds himself firmly below the duo of Josko Gvardiol and Nico O’Reilly in the left-back pecking order, and has shown no signs of winning a place any time soon.

City are open to the idea of letting him move on this winter, but only if the deal is right for them.

United made attempts to sign the 25-year-old in the summer, but City view their rivals as a last resort when it comes to any potential sale.

They feel a possible move abroad for Ait-Nouri far more favourably, and Barcelona could be set to provide the ideal escape route for the player.

The Algeria international made 31 appearances in all competitions last year, and remains highly regarded, with Barcelona viewing him as someone who could become a valuable part of their plans.

Former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness told Football Insider last week that City could potentially consider letting goalkeeper Gianluigi Donnarumma depart in a “structured loan” deal within the next 12 months.

The struggling gloveman has seen mistakes creep into his game in the early stages of the season, raising speculation over his long-term future with the club.

However, Wyness pointed out that Donnarumma’s wages are so hefty, that City may only be able to offload him through an initial loan deal, where they made a significant contribution.

The Saudi Arabian market is also seen as an option for Maresca and Co., if they decide that a change is needed between the posts.
