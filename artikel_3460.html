Every 'DWTS' Season 35 Cast Member Who Returned for Finale Night

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Dancing With the Stars ballroom was filled with familiar faces during the highly-anticipated season 35 finale.

Before one of the final five couples clinched the coveted Len Goodman Mirrorball Trophy, they competed in three rounds of competition. The pairs were tasked with completing a “Judge’s Choice” routine to revisit a style they performed earlier this season, the Instant Dance and the often show-stopping freestyle.

The cast were joined by a number of season 35 castoffs — and even more franchise alumni.

“Oh hey,” former pro Lindsay Arnold wrote via her Instagram Stories on November 19, snapping a selfie from the rehearsal studio with sister Rylee Arnold and dance partner Connor Wood. “My winners right here.”

Rylee and Wood are vying for the championship against Amber Glenn and Pasha Pashkov, Ezra Frech and Daniella Karagach, Tatyana Ali and Jan Ravnik, as well as Maura Higgins and Mark Ballas.

“Obviously, you see the competition. … This is the only time the competition feels competitive is when we are in the ballroom competing. Otherwise, this is our family,” Wood exclusively told Us after the semifinals earlier this month of making it to the finale. “It was just so cool to do this with everyone. It’s so cool to be going to the finale with the group that we’re going to the finale with.”

As for Rylee, she told Us she was “so proud” of Wood’s ballroom progress and that he is “the best partner ever.”

Keep scrolling to see which DWTS season 35 alums returned for the finale:

The first contestant eliminated, Leavitt kicked off the opening number with first-time pro Adele Zaikman before the other couples joined in. The duo, much like the other eliminated pairs, also performed in bumpers between competitive numbers.

Nader’s DWTS journey with new pro Hailey Bills was cut short, but they returned for the finale’s opening number.

The celebrity chef danced with pro Alan Bersten once more in the opening.

Hanson was back to boogie with Britt Stewart after joining forces for his “Criminal” music video.

The Summer House star busted out tango moves with partner Brandon Armstrong in the opening.

Rodriguez was back with reigning champ Witney Carson — who showed off her bare baby bump — for a brief solo in the beginning.

Cameron returned for the DWTS finale to perform with Sharna Burgess just days after he disembarked from the inaugural Bachelor Nation cruise.

Olson and pro partner Emma Slater joined forces for the group’s finale opening.

DWTS superfan Stiles couldn’t hide her enthusiasm to perform with pro Ezra Sosa once more, cha cha-ing in the opening.

Dewan returned to the ballroom for a special moment in the opening routine with Val Chmerovskiy.

After Shum’s shocking semifinals elimination, he returned for the finale with Jenna Johnson as part of the opening.
