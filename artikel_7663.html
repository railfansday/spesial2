Nithya Raman wins LA mayor’s race, toppling incumbent Karen Bass

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The 45-year-old Raman, a former urban planner who founded a homeless services nonprofit, will take office with seemingly few allies in City Hall.

LOS ANGELES (CN) â Progressive City Councilmember Nithya Raman will be the next mayor of Los Angeles, ousting Mayor Karen Bass after only one term in office.

Bass called Raman Thursday to concede the race.

“While this is not the outcome we hoped for, the people of Los Angeles have spoken,” Bass said in a written statement. “I have called Councilmember Nithya Raman to congratulate her on her victory, and I have committed to ensuring a smooth and seamless transition. Moving this city forward is what Los Angeles needs from all of us.”

The 45-year-old Raman currently leads Bass by roughly 35,000 votes, or 2%. But the momentum of the ongoing count points to a comfortable victory by the challenger. Though the early count put her just behind the older and more centrist Bass, every subsequent tranche of counted ballots has favored Raman. Past elections followed the same pattern: the early vote-by-mail ballots trended more conservative, while the ballots cast on Election Day leaned more progressive.

It’s hard to imagine a less likely mayor than Raman, an Indian-American with degrees from Harvard and MIT, an introvert and a policy wonk, a former urban planner who founded a homeless services nonprofit. She surprised the establishment in 2020 when she defeated an incumbent for a spot on LA’s City Council, becoming the first of a number of left-wing candidates backed by the Democratic Socialists of America, or DSA.

Despite that growing contingent, Raman was an unpopular figure inside the clubby world of City Hall. But Bass was always unpopular. Voters blamed her for traveling out of the country, to Ghana, just before the deadly wildfires of 2025 devastated the neighborhoods of Altadena and the Pacific Palisades. And although her approach to homelessness, the centerpiece of her 2022 campaign, appeared to making headway, the most recent homeless count showed the numbers climbing, an indication that her tactics were failing.

“Angelenos made up their mind about Bass last year, right after the wildfires,” said Dan Schnur, a political science professor at the University of Southern California. “Bass ran a very successful campaign to be mayor of 20th-century Los Angeles. Raman targeted younger voters who normally don’t turnout as heavily as older generations. But she got just enough of them to pull this off.”

Raman will become the first challenger to topple an incumbent mayor in more than 20 years, and only the second in half a century. She won’t just face resistance from the council. Many of her policy positions are either unpopular or are opposed by entrenched special interests.
