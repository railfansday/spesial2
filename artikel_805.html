‘The Odyssey’ to Receive Ensemble Performance Award at Palm Springs International Film Festival (Exclusive)

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Cast members Matt Damon, John Leguizamo and Samantha Morton as well as casting director John Papsidera, who will also be honored with the award, are expected to attend the ceremony.

Christopher Nolan’s The Odyssey will receive the Ensemble Performance Award at the Palm Springs International Film Festival, the Palm Springs International Film Awards announced on Tuesday.

Cast members Matt Damon, John Leguizamo and Samantha Morton, as well as casting director John Papsidera, are expected to attend the ceremony. The Film Awards will take place on Jan. 7, 2027, with the festival running from Jan. 7-18.

The Ensemble Performance Award recognizes the “casting director in alignment with the Academy’s newly established best casting award.” Previous recipients include Conclave (2025), King Richard (2022), Mary Poppins Returns (2019), Hidden Figures (2017), The Big Short (2016), The Imitation Game (2015), American Hustle (2014), Argo (2013), The Social Network (2011) and Babel (2007). “The Odyssey is one of the most ambitious films ever made, and its ensemble of performances is nothing short of extraordinary,” Festival chairman Nachhattar Singh Chandi says. “From Matt Damon’s weathered turn as the hero Odysseus, to Samantha Morton’s captivating and terrifying portrayal of Circe, to John Leguizamo’s faithful guidance as Eumaeus, these are just a few of the many performances that bring this world of antiquity to vivid, undeniable life. It is a privilege to celebrate this remarkable company of actors and the casting director who brought them together with the Ensemble Performance Award.”

The Odyssey was the first feature film to be entirely shot in Imax. It also stars Tom Holland, Anne Hathaway, Robert Pattinson, Lupita Nyong’o, Jon Bernthal, Charlize Theron and Elliot Page, among others. Nolan wrote and directed the script and produced alongside Emma Thomas.

Previously announced honorees at the fest include Andrew Garfield (Spotlight Award, Actor); The Invite’s Seth Rogen, Olivia Wilde, Penélope Cruz, Edward Norton (Vanguard Award); La Bola Negra’s directors Javier Calvo and Javier Ambrossi, as well as actors Guitarricadelafuente, Miguel Bernardeau and Penélope Cruz (International Star Award); Martin McDonagh (Outstanding Achievement in Directing Award); Inde Navarette (Rising Star Award); and Andrew Scott (Desert Palm Achievement Award, Actor).
