Motley Crue's Vince Neil's Girlfriend Arrested on Domestic Assault Charge

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Rain Hannah Andreani, the longtime girlfriend of Mötley Crüe lead vocalist Vince Neil, was arrested on a domestic-related assault charge, Us can confirm.

Andreani, 45, was booked by the Williamson County Sheriff’s Office in Tennessee on Sunday, November 1, and released on a $5,000 bond the following day, per arrest records viewed by Us. TMZ was first to report the news.

Andreani is due to appear in court in connection with the domestic-related assault charge on December 3 at 1 p.m. local time.

Us has reached out to Neil’s representative for comment.

In February 2025, Andreani was injured in a plane crash while aboard Neil’s private jet. The pilot of the plane, Joie Vitosky, died after Neil’s plane veered off course and collided with another jet at Scottsdale Airport in Arizona. Neil was not on board the plane.

TMZ reported at the time that Andreani suffered five broken ribs in the crash. A friend also sustained injuries. Both women and the jet’s co-pilot were taken to the hospital. Two dogs aboard the plane also survived.

“At 2:39 p.m. local time, a Learjet aircraft Model 35A owned by Vince Neil was attempting to land at the Scottsdale Airport. For reasons unknown at this time, the plane veered from the runway causing it to collide with another parked plane,” read a statement from Neil’s representative shared via the Mötley Crüe Instagram page at the time. “On board Mr. Neil’s plane were two pilots and two passengers. Mr. Neil was not on the plane.”

The statement continued, “More specific details regarding the collision are not available as this is a rapidly evolving situation and there is an ongoing investigation. Mr. Neil’s thoughts and prayers go out to everyone involved, and he is grateful for the critical aid of all first responders assisting today.”

In September 2025, Neil, 65, opened up about recovering from a stroke, which he suffered in December 2024.

“I had to learn to walk again, and that was tough,” the musician told the Las Vegas Review-Journal. “The doctors said they didn’t think I’d be able to go back on stage again. I go, ‘No, no, I’m gonna do it. Watch and see.’”

“I went from people carrying me to the bathroom, because I couldn’t walk myself, finally to a wheelchair,” he recalled. “I graduated to a walker, and then I had a cane. Now I don’t need anything. But it’s like a full-time job getting back to where you feel good again.”

Neil added, “It takes a while to get your brain to start moving your legs, for them to do what your brain wants to do. You try to walk, but it doesn’t come out right.”
