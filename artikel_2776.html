Carnell Tate injury update: Titans rookie WR will practice today

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Carnell Tate has missed the Tennessee Titans' last two games with a shoulder injury he suffered in the loss to the Cleveland Browns. Robert Saleh said today that the rookie wide receiver will practice today. It sounds like he has a good chance to be back for the game against the Dallas Cowboys on Sunday.

Tate has been really good this season with 34 catches for 413 yards and a touchdown through 7 games.

Saleh also said that Anthony Hill and Cody Barton will return to practice today. It will be interesting to see what that means for Cedric Gray. For reasons that aren't exactly clear, Gray spent most of his time on the sideline when both Hill and Barton were healthy. Gray, who might be the best defender left with Jeffery Simmons and Amani Hooker both gone, was really good last week against the Jacksonville Jaguars.

In addition to getting those guys back on the practice field, Fernando Carmona will get his first practice in since he injured his ankle against the New York Giants.

Saleh also said that Wan'Dale Robinson will miss the rest of the season. The team placed him on IR yesterday.
