Browns rookie Emmanuel McNeil-Warren tops CLE’s 2026 draft class grades; offensive guys lead the NFL

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Prior to the 2026 NFL season, almost everyone believed that the Cleveland Browns hit another home run with their 2026 NFL draft class. Little did anyone know that the Browns rookies would be a part of a season that has started out special with a 6-4 record heading into their bye week.

Much of the excitement has been around WRs KC Concepcion and Denzel Boston, for good reason, but safety Emmanuel McNeil-Warren, OT Spencer Fano, and guard Austin Barber have also had very good seasons to help Cleveland's record. In many ways, the rookie class is meeting or exceeding expectations, which is pretty impressive given what many thought before the year.

It was a game that saw him finish with a 77.6 PFF grade that was the second-highest of his career, and best in a game where he has played more than 30 snaps. On the season, McNeil-Warren has recorded eight tackles resulting in a defensive stop, and all eight have come over the past five games.
