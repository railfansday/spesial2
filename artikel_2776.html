Vikings’ Kirk Cousins gets real on brutal pick-6 in 49ers loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Minnesota Vikings walked into Mexico City looking to prove they belong atop the NFC, only to leave with a 35-10 reality check from the San Francisco 49ers on Sunday night. Afterward, quarterback Kirk Cousins didn't dodge responsibility for the second-quarter mistake that blew the game wide open.

Trailing 21-10 with just over two minutes left in the first half, Cousins tried to squeeze a short throw over the middle. San Francisco safety Ji'Ayir Brown read it the whole way, jumped the route at the 18-yard line and sprinted into the end zone for a backbreaking pick-six.

"The backside safety made a great play," Cousins said after the loss. "I need to feel him and progress, and not try to fit it in there."

That turnover encapsulated a rough night for Minnesota's offense against a relentless 49ers pass rush that hit Cousins seven times. Cousins finished 26-of-38 for 235 yards with no touchdowns and one interception, taking one sack before backup Carson Wentz closed things out late.

Minnesota showed flashes early. Running back Aaron Jones powered in an 11-yard touchdown run in the first quarter, and Cousins later dropped a 29-yard loft to wide receiver Justin Jefferson inside 49ers territory. Jefferson led the Vikings with seven catches for 86 yards, while tight end T.J. Hockenson added six receptions for 55 yards and Jordan Mason chipped in 38 rushing yards. Still, Jefferson's third-quarter fumble, forced by cornerback Deommodore Lenoir, erased another promising drive.

Meanwhile, San Francisco quarterback Brock Purdy carved up Minnesota's blitz-heavy defense, completing 21 of 26 passes for 255 yards and three touchdowns. Tight end George Kittle caught two scores, wide receiver Mike Evans piled up 109 yards and a touchdown and running back Christian McCaffrey added a rushing score.

The loss drops the Vikings to 7-3, while the 9-1 49ers strengthen their grip on the conference.
