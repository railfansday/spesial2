Here’s what happened in crypto today

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Need to know what happened in crypto today? Here is the latest news on daily trends and events impacting Bitcoin price, blockchain, DeFi, Web3 and crypto regulation.

Today in crypto, 42 Macro CEO Darius Dale said in an interview last week his firm is waiting for signs that Bitcoin’s sell-off has run its course before buying again, Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO despite an ongoing intelligence probe and OKX and New York Stock Exchange parent ICE launched a tokenized securities trading platform despite objections from dozens of major companies.

Darius Dale, founder and CEO of 42 Macro, says his firm has trimmed its Bitcoin exposure and is waiting for signs that the sell-off has run its course before it considers buying again.

“We’ve already de-risked out of Bitcoin,” Dale said in an interview with Cointelegraph last week. “Our stress levels are low. We’re just waiting to buy.”

Dale’s comments came just days before Bitcoin fell to a two-year low around $55,100 on Saturday, raising questions about whether Bitcoin has finally found its bottom this cycle.

However, Dale warned that persistently high inflation could prompt further Fed tightening that could drain the liquidity that supports Bitcoin. The US Federal Reserve is widely expected to increase rates by 25 basis points at its next meeting on Dec. 9.

“Bitcoin doesn’t generate earnings. Bitcoin needs liquidity to go up,” he said.

Bitcoin was trading at around $64,000 at the time of the interview. Dale said it could fall another 30% to 40% if the S&P 500’s decline from its Oct. 6 peak reaches 20% by Christmas, which would put it at around $38,400. The S&P500 is currently down 7.4% from that peak.

Nvidia’s board unanimously voted to reinstate Jensen Huang as president and CEO after consultations with senior US government officials, concluding that his return posed no national security risk despite an ongoing investigation into an alleged foreign intelligence operation targeting him.

In a Monday announcement, Nvidia said its directors were satisfied with Huang’s candor and ability to lead following a series of discussions. The independent investigation will continue despite his reinstatement.

Nvidia shares surged 28.91% to $161.82 shortly after US markets opened on Monday, according to Yahoo Finance data.

Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress, who served as interim CEO, will return to her previous role, while Harvey Jones will remain chair of the board.

The chipmaker also said it had recently formed a National Security and Export Control Committee comprising independent directors to oversee compliance and security risks. Nvidia plans to appoint a chief compliance officer and said it would continue cooperating with government investigations.

OKXICE, a joint venture between cryptocurrency exchange OKX and New York Stock Exchange parent Intercontinental Exchange (ICE), launched a venue Monday for round-the-clock trading of tokenized US securities, with 113 assets set to roll out in stages.

The lineup includes stocks, exchange-traded funds and other exchange-traded products, according to an announcement shared with Cointelegraph. OKXICE named Coinbase, Circle, Fidelity Investments and Robinhood among the issuers and asset managers taking part.

Before the launch, 67 companies, including Meta, Tesla and Netflix, sought to keep tokenized versions of their shares off the venue.

Each token represents a security entitlement backed one-to-one by an underlying share, OKXICE said. Holders are entitled to dividends and voting rights where applicable.

OKXICE said the venue operates under the US Securities and Exchange Commission’s Innovation Exemption. Access is limited to eligible US customers of OKX Inc., who must verify their identity, activate a self-custodial wallet and complete onboarding before trading through the OKX app.
