Germany news: Metalworkers go on strike as pay dispute heats up

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Germany's biggest union is calling for more strikes over heated pay disputes with car makers like Mercedes and shipyards among the companies impacted. Meanwhile, a Berlin hospital fire leaves many injured. Follow DW.

Read below for more on these stories and all other things Germanyon Monday, November 23.

Police are investigating a suspected case of aggravated arson after a fire broke out at a hospital in Berlin.

Investigators believe a patient set fire to his bed in a room on the first floor of one of the hospital buildings in the Lichtenberg district on Sunday evening.

One nurse and the suspected arsonist suffered smoke inhalation and had to be treated in hospital. Overall, 15 people were injured with another 13 suffering from minor injuries.

While firefighters managed to put out the flames, smoke spread to other parts of the hospital which then had to be evacuated.

The country's largest and most powerful union, the IG Metall, has called for a strike this Monday over an ongoing pay dispute.

The union has called on some 23,000 workers to down tools in as many as 19 factories in northern Germany. Among the companies affected likely to be impacted are Siemens, Mercedes Benz, and shipyard Meyer Werft.

At a similar strike on Friday, more than 12,000 workers had taken part.

IG Metall hopes that the strikes will increase pressure on employers to yield to its demands, which include a 5% ‌wage increase.

Employers have so far ⁠offered a one-off payment next year and a 1.3% wage increase from July 2028.
