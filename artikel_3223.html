Clairo Covers Beloved MF Doom Song With El Michels Affair

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Here’s a contender for best headline of the week: Clairo just dropped a cover of MF Doom‘s “Lavender Buds.” Teaming up with Brooklyn music collective El Michels Affair, the singer-songwriter provided vocals to a faithful rendition of Doom’s beloved Special Herbs beat. Listen to the song via Bandcamp now.

“Lavender Buds” originally appeared on Doom’s instrumental album Special Herbs Vol. 5 & 6, which samples the Blackbyrds’ dreamy 1974 song “April Showers.” Clairo sings the sample’s lyrics recalling a lost romance.

El Michels Affair and Clairo’s Doom cover features synth player and flautist Leon Michels, drummer Homer Steinweiss, bassist Nick Movshon, and pianist Marco Benevento. The collective also played on Clairo’s third album Charm, with Michels and Clairo co-producing the record together. The B-side of their “Lavender Buds” cover is an El Michels Affair adaptation of Doom’s Special Herbs Vol. 1 & 2 beat “Nettle Leaves,” sans any Clairo contributions. This isn’t the first time Clairo aligned her sound with hip-hop: In 2024, she sang on Freddie Gibbs and Madlib’s “Crime Pays” live at a series of concerts for their Piñata 10-year anniversary tour. As for El Michels Affair, bandleader Michel was heavily inspired by Metal Face on the group’s most recent album 24 Hr Sports, which was released in 2025. Clairo sang on that album’s song “Anticipate.”

MF Doom died in 2020 at age 49 from angioedema, a sudden swelling often caused by an allergic reaction after he was hospitalized for respiratory issues.
