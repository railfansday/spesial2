Griffon (GFF) Q4 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Debt fell to 2x as leadership laid out its capital allocation plans.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Griffon Corporation (GFF +3.94%) completed a strategic transformation in fiscal 2026, transitioning into a pure-play building products company through the divestiture and reorganization of its tool businesses. Management reported that the company utilized proceeds from these transactions and free cash flow to reduce debt by $364 million and lower its net leverage to 2x. The company reported market share gains in the residential garage door segment, specifically within the high-end market, despite broader industry volume declines. Leadership stated that the current capital allocation strategy prioritizes share repurchases and dividends, supported by a long-term projection of over $1 billion in free cash flow through fiscal 2030.

Ronald Kramer: Thank you, Brian. Good morning, everyone, and thank you for joining us. 2026 was a transformational year for Griffon. We completed a comprehensive series of strategic actions, which have strengthened and transformed our company. In June of 2026, Griffon formed Veritage Brands through the merger of Venanpri Tools and the AMES Company, North American businesses creating a global leader in professional tools, lawn and garden, home organization and outdoor living. This transaction provided us with $100 million in cash and $161 million in 10% pay-in-kind notes. Additionally, we've retained a 43% ownership in Veritage Brands, so Griffon can participate in its future success.

We also completed the sale of a 51% interest in AMES Australasia to an investor group led by our AMES management team, along with independent investors. This venture since renamed Griffon ANZ provided us with $181 million in cash and a $49 million 10% pay-in-kind note. We're excited about the prospects of this business and will share in its value creation through our ongoing 49% retained ownership. Using the cash proceeds from our strategic actions, along with our free cash flow, we took the opportunity to significantly strengthen our balance sheet. We reduced our outstanding debt by $364 million, refinanced our bonds and pushed out our maturity to 2034 and secured a $500 million new credit agreement.

These changes have resulted in our net debt-to-EBITDA leverage being reduced to 2x compared to 2.4x at the end of fiscal 2025. In August, we lowered our target leverage by 1 turn to a range of 1.5 to 2.5, reflecting our new debt structure and our commitment to maintaining a strong balance sheet. We're currently at the midpoint of that range. As a confirmation of the importance of completing these strategic actions, on August 10, Griffon's corporate family credit rating was upgraded one notch by Moody's and S&P to Ba2 and BB, respectively.

Our strategic actions have transformed Griffon into a pure-play building products company with modest leverage, excellent free cash flow and EBITDA margins among the strongest in the industry. Fundamentally, we are well positioned to continue building shareholder value in the current business environment, and we expect our growth to accelerate as building activity and economic conditions improve. Turning to our results for the year. We are pleased with our operational and financial performance, which was highlighted by our strong free cash flow generation of $267 million. Revenue increased to $1.5 billion, and adjusted EBITDA was consistent with the prior year at $460 million.

These results reflect our team's ability to manage through the challenging global economic environment, including the soft U.S. housing and commercial construction markets. We continue to focus on innovation and productivity, which has driven our performance and has resulted in notable industry recognition. In 2026, the Clopay Avante garage door with C-Power enabled Click-to-Conceal panels was the winner of both the International Door Association's Product Innovation Award and the Best of IBS Award in the Window and Door category at the International Builders' Show. The patented C-Power technology delivers electrical power directly to the garage door panels, opening up a new world of potential for building openings, including allowing the door to instantly transition its windows from clear to opaque.

This recognition, along with last year's Best of IBS Award for the VertiStack product, demonstrate Clopay's industry-leading innovation and product vitality. We expect this momentum to continue with many new product and service launches in the coming years. We continue to innovate in our ceiling fan business as well, including the launch of HunterSMART, a new family of smart ceiling fans and home accessories. HunterSMART products use Matter home network connectivity to integrate with leading smart home ecosystems while maintaining simple installing and intuitive controls. This launch represents an important milestone as homeowners increasingly embrace connected home technology to enhance convenience and energy efficiency. Turning to capital allocation.

In fiscal 2026, we continue to take significant actions to deliver shareholder value through stock buybacks and cash dividends, while also reducing debt and strengthening our balance sheet. During the year, we repurchased 1.6 million shares at an average price of $83.14. Since April 2023 and through the market close yesterday, our share repurchases totaled 12.6 million shares of common stock or 22.1% of the April '23 outstanding shares for a total of $713 million or an average of $56.48 per share. Earlier today, we announced an increase of $400 million to our share repurchase authorization.

As of market open today and including the $400 million increase, we now have a total of $545 million available under our authorized repurchase program. Also this morning, we announced that the Griffon Board authorized a 14% increase to our regular quarterly dividend to $0.25 per share, marking the 61st consecutive regularly quarterly dividend to shareholders. Our dividend has grown at an annualized compounded rate of 18% since we began paying dividends in 2012. Overall, during fiscal '26, Griffon returned a total of $177 million to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases and reduced debt by $364 million while also reducing leverage by 0.4 of a turn and making substantial investments in our businesses.

Since April 2023, we've returned a total of $950 million to shareholders in the form of dividends and share repurchases while reducing our leverage from 2.5x to 2x. These actions reflect the ongoing strength of our businesses as well as our confidence in our strategic plan and outlook. Adjusted EBITDA increased 4% to $128 million, driven by increased revenue and partially offset by the unfavorable impact of decreased volume and overhead absorption as well as increased material, labor and distribution costs. Fourth quarter GAAP selling, general and administrative expenses were $113 million compared to $109 million in the prior year. Excluding adjusting items from the prior period, SG&A expenses were $106 million or 22.4% of revenue compared to the current year of $113 million or 22.9% of revenue. Fourth quarter GAAP income from continuing operations was $67 million or $1.49 per share compared to the prior year of $20 million or $0.43 per share.

Excluding all items that affect comparability from both periods, current quarter adjusted income from continuing operations was $72 million or $1.61 per share compared to the prior year of $65 million or $1.42 per share. Net capital expenditures were $14 million in the fourth quarter compared to $9 million in the prior year quarter. Depreciation and amortization totaled $10 million for the fourth quarter, consistent with the prior year. Regarding our balance sheet and liquidity, as of September 30, 2026, we had net debt of $947 million and net debt to EBITDA leverage of 2x as calculated based on our debt covenants.

As Ron mentioned earlier, during the year, we generated $267 million of free cash flow and paid down $364 million of debt, which contributed to reducing leverage 0.4 of a turn compared to the prior year-end. In terms of share repurchases, for the full year, we bought 1.6 million shares of common stock for a total of $136 million or $83.14 per share. Since September 30, we have repurchased 0.2 million shares of common stock for the total of $16.9 million or $96.22 per share.
