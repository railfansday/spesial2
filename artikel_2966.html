Celtics guard lost 10 pounds while sick, but returns to make Boston debut

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Devin Carter, then still fighting for a roster spot, showed exactly why he was a first-round pick just a couple years ago. Carter flashed a stellar debut for the Celtics in a preseason win over the Cavaliers back on Oct. 8, putting up 17 points and five rebounds off the bench.

But then Carter was shut down because of a bout with mononucleosis that knocked him out for weeks. Carter revealed that he lost 10 pounds, but he's slowly getting himself back into game shape. He even got to make his C's debut last Sunday in the blowout win over the Mavericks. Now, he's trying to entrench himself in Boston.

"I still feel fatigued," Carter said, via CLNS Media. "Just energy low. Moment you kind of play two minutes, everything kind of shuts down. Just building that back up. Obviously, the way I play, is kind of tough to not be tired. On top of the way I play, dealing with the sickness is tough. Not in a rush, but I definitely feel myself getting back into shape, playing a lot better."

Carter, 24, originally signed an Exhibit 10 contract with the Celtics ahead of training camp. He started his career with the Kings, where he was drafted 13th overall in 2024 out of nearby Providence. But after Sacramento traded him to Atlanta, the Hawks waived him shortly before training camp.

But the Celtics wanted a closer look at Carter, so they signed him to the training camp deal. Even though Carter missed the majority of preseason, he showed enough in practices for the team to ink him to a two-way contract. Still, the timing was tough considering he was just gaining steam.

"I was definitely upset at the time when it happened," Carter said. "I had a good game in Cleveland and I was looking forward to playing the rest of preseason and showing what I worked on all summer. Just everything got stripped quick. But I'm working back into it."

Carter will get his reps in the G League with the Maine Celtics, suiting up alongside fellow two-way signees Dillon Mitchell and Amari Williams. Prior to joining the Celtics, he recorded 74 games of NBA experience in Sacramento through two seasons.

The Celtics have a pair of guards in front of Carter in Derrick White and Payton Pritchard. But relatively speaking, there could be an opportunity for playing time for Carter throughout the course of the season. He's certainly a guy worth monitoring as a guy who could contribute to the main club once he's back to tip-top shape.

"Picking up full court," Carter said. "Playmaking. Sharing the ball. Getting teammates involved. Doing the little things that helps the team win games. We have superstars on the team. Just come in and play as hard as you can in the time you get."
