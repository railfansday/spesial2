Huddersfield Town backed for £10m+ Joe Taylor windfall as Burnley & Swansea City lurk

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Heading into the busy festive period, Huddersfield Town remain firmly in the automatic promotion mix.

There’s not been too much to complain about for those connected with the Yorkshire outfit, with the Terriers already netting 28 goals so far this term with such a sharp front line, while they have also conceded the fewest goals in the division, a recipe for success when it comes to getting back into the Championship.

From their opening 15 league games, Martin Drury’s men have only tasted defeat twice so far this campaign, with eight victories in the process, leaving them in a handy position to challenge and get into top spot over Christmas.

You’ve got to feel Huddersfield stand a great chance of returning to the Championship come the end of the season, but a big hurdle to navigate in order to achieve that is by hanging on to their most prized assets, with some of those bound to be subject to transfer speculation in January after producing superb performances week in, week out.

One in particular is Huddersfield forward Joe Taylor, who has dazzled so far this season and heavily contributed to the Terriers being in a promising position to achieve automatic promotion.

But with links to some Championship clubs at the turn of the new year, an exit for the 24-year-old has the potential to shake up the promotion race completely, with the Terriers' hierarchy having a big decision to make if a concrete offer is tabled.

From a Huddersfield Town perspective, so much credit has to go to Welsh forward Joe Taylor for the start the Terriers have made this season.

Versatile enough to flip between playing as a number 10 and as a lone striker, Taylor has already got into double figures for goals from the 14 league games he’s played in, nailing himself down as one of the first names on the team sheet due to such a clinical nature in the final third.

Taylor has been part of such an electric forward line, with himself and Derensili Sanches Fernandes at the heart of most of their good work, with that duo in particular needing to sustain their form over the whole of the campaign if they are to get the club back into the second tier.

And with goals flowing from Taylor in the early stages of the term, it’s no surprise to see him linked with making a step-up back to the Championship, with some transfer speculation emerging about the forward heading into the January window that may worry the Yorkshire outfit.

The potential sale of Huddersfield’s Joe Taylor could really shake up the League One promotion race.

An exclusive report from us here at Football League World has revealed that second tier duo Burnley and Swansea City are both eyeing up moves for Taylor to bolster their frontlines heading into the second portion of the season.

Swansea, of course, lost head coach Vitor Matos to Strasbourg last week, but it will not stop them eyeing up targets ahead of January, and Taylor is on their radar, as they are looking at adding him to a strike-force consisting of Adam Idah, Ross Stewart and Zan Vipotnik.

Burnley, meanwhile, find themselves in a relegation battle under Fabio Celestini and may need some further additions at the top end of the pitch to get them out of the mire, with current centre-forward options like Jamie Vardy, Zeki Amdouni and Andreas Hountondji, failing to provide a consistent return of goals.

So with this in mind, FLW’s Huddersfield fan pundit has reacted to the latest links and how much the club should demand for Taylor's services in January.

Speaking to Football League World, Huddersfield Town fan pundit Graeme Rayner has demanded that the Terriers accept nothing less than £10m from Burnley or Swansea City for Joe Taylor in January, admitting that while it is a ‘laughably big’ fee for a League One player, they can’t lose a player who has such an impact on their automatic promotion ambitions.

Graeme told FLW: “Until this season, Joe Taylor’s career at Town hadn’t come to life. He went on loan to Wigan for the second half of last season, demonstrated what he can do if you use him in the right way and if you supply him in the right way, and since then he’s come back and been absolutely superb this season for Huddersfield.

“He’s on 10 goals already, and we’re only in November, late November, he’s been featured in the Wales squad, although he hasn’t got any minutes, but he’s kicked on, and he is one of the reasons, and there are many of them, but one of the key reasons why Huddersfield are right in the hunt for automatic promotion and definitely for the play-offs.”

Asked about the fee Huddersfield should demand for Taylor, Graeme added: “On Saturday we played at home to Leicester, he played, and neither him nor Derensili Sanches Fernandes were on their best game, and it showed, and it showed how reliant upon them both we have become for that cutting edge up front, so to answer your question, if somebody wants to come in January and take away our top goalscorer, then they’re going to have to pay through the nose for that.

“Strikers in January are always extra expensive, British strikers carry a premium as well, and both of those factors, plus the likely impact on us, mean it would have to be silly money and a fee that’s so big that it’s laughably big and we have to take it.

“I would say it would have to be an eight-figure sum. If I’m the chairman of Huddersfield and I’m thinking we’ve been in League One for too long, and have to get back in the Championship, the value of us keeping Joe Taylor to get us back in the Championship is more than his actual paper value, so don’t offer us anything less than £10m.

“Now that might sound fanciful, but Birmingham’s signing of Jay Stansfield for £15m the other season in League One has possibly shifted the goalposts anyway, but it’s about him being worth more to us than just his transfer value, so if they came in with less than £10m then I’d laugh them out the door.

“But I do hope that we’ve already been scouting out possible replacements just in case someone comes in with an offer too good to refuse.”

An exit for Joe Taylor in January would bring about a massive swing in the League One promotion race, with Huddersfield Town undeniably weaker without their star forward on the books.

Such a fundamental player in Huddersfield’s free-flowing attack, their promotion push could be in tatters if he was to move back to the Championship, with it so hard to find an ample replacement in January.

Therefore, unless a ridiculous offer comes in from a second tier side, around the £10m mark, then they should be instantly rejecting it, with Taylor’s form and impact on the side a major factor as to why Huddersfield are challenging for top spot in the League One table at this moment in time.

If the Terriers were to sell the 24-year-old, then there wouldn’t be any guarantee of a play-off place, let alone a spot inside the top two, so the hierarchy must stand firm when it comes to his future, with their place back in the Championship at stake if they move him on.

While it’s nervy times for those associated with Huddersfield, you would hope those at the top of the club would make the sensible decision of keeping him, with this choice critical towards Huddersfield ending a period outside of the Championship that has already stretched on for far too long.
