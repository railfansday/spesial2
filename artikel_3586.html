Daphne Zuniga Addresses Future of 'Melrose Place' Podcast Amid Lengthy Pause

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Daphne Zuniga is offering a rare update on her Melrose Place rewatch podcast with Courtney Thorne-Smith and Laura Leighton amid its ongoing pause.

“We did the first season and then our schedules all kind of split up,” Zuniga, 64, explained during an exclusive interview with Us on the set of Fox’s Celebrity Weakest Link.

Zuniga hinted that they also “didn’t get the followers that we really needed.”

“We had passionate [listeners], but iHeartRadio had a quota and they just needed to make that,” she recalled about the podcast, which premiered in summer 2024. “And we’re like, ‘What about that theory of things will build?’ Then [Courtney and Laura] got busy. I was off shooting Spaceballs: The New One and everyone’s [working on something].”

The actress, who played Jo Reynolds, didn’t rule out returning to the podcast project. “But we did get a call about would we want to revisit it? That was [a few] months ago,” she said. “So when things calm down, we might [return].”

She continued: “And it was just getting good because Melrose Place really got good when you get into all the big drama stuff, which was just happening at the end of the first season.”

Melrose Place, which ran from 1992 to 1999, followed a group of young adults living in an apartment complex in West Hollywood. After filming her episode of Celebrity Weakest Link — alongside fellow ’90s stars Mario Cantone, Zac Hanson, Jasmine Guy, Jonathan Lipnicki, Sisqo, Kel Mitchell and Stephen Baldwin — Zuniga had a few suggestions for which of her Melrose Place costars should compete next.

“The entertainment would be way up there because we all still know each other still,” she told Us. “I think Courtney would be a threat. I think Laura would be a threat.”

Zuniga confirmed that the cast remained quite close over the years. “Laura and Courtney and I, obviously, had a text chain for [the podcast]. Grant [Show] just texted us all the other day about something else,” she continued. “So I would say Grant and Laura and Courtney. But Laura and I talk all the time.”

While Zuniga didn’t have any official news about a potential Melrose Place revival, she hinted at some kind of return, adding, “It came to a standstill just because of the strike and also because of companies buying each other. But there was a deal made, so we’re trying to see what’s going to happen now.”

For now, Zuniga is focused on other projects and her philanthropic work while competing for Insight Meditation Society on the Monday, November 16, episode of Celebrity Weakest Link.

“I started about 23 years ago doing these meditation retreats and silent retreats. It came at a time that was very difficult for me. What I got out of it was — out of this very difficult but simple routine over and over — was that I am allowed to be my own best friend and that the stories that I tell in my head that your mind is always going are just add-ons to actual reality,” she recalled. “But we believe them and so we just run by that. So I got to clear my head, get close to my heart and I left there and things started to pop with my career, with my love life and with just my life in general.”

She concluded: “I really believe in that. And they provide scholarships for everyone. They don’t turn anyone away. So if anyone’s interested in going to a meditation retreat in Massachusetts, they just do wonderful work. It’s not religiously affiliated. It’s just mindfulness and meditation in the woods.”

Celebrity Weakest Link airs on Fox Mondays at 9 p.m. ET.
