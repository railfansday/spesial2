Alfred Johansson hails returning stars as Motherwell end seven-game wait for victory

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Motherwell boss Alfred Johansson insists welcoming back a host of first-team regulars was the icing on the cake in their William Hill Premiership victory over St Mirren.

The Fir Park boss was delighted to break a seven-game winless run and he believes an easing injury list is another cause for celebration.

He said: “It was so good to be manager of Motherwell for this game and being able to put that quality onto the pitch in the second half. Wow, that makes a massive difference. Regan Charles-Cook came on and was involved in the penalty situation.

“Oscar Priestman had a huge influence as did Eythor Bjorgolfsson and Callum Hendry, so that is a big joy and also something we will be able to build on.

“There was a lot of joy and happiness in the locker room after the game. After the draw in midweek at St Johnstone there was a lot of anger, fire and frustration, so the emotions were different and we are delighted to get the win.

“Even more than relief I felt there was determination and this group have been doing very well recently. To get four points from our last two games brings only praise to the players.

“The most important thing was we never gave up anything, we were very solid defensively and we pressed well.

“The solidity is something we need to build on and offensively we need to create even more from the advantages we have.

“There were spells when we were going too direct at times and making the game a bit more 50-50. In the second half we had to focus on the things we needed to take care of to win this game.”

Saints boss Craig McLeish admits his side will get back on the training ground in a bid to rediscover their early-season form and regain some belief.

He said: “There is a lack of belief and we will get back in on Monday and work hard on regaining that freedom of play which marked our performances at the start of the season

“We didn’t do enough to test Motherwell, I don’t think their goalkeeper had a save to make.

“We got into some promising areas but lacked a bit of quality in the final third. There were some really promising periods and we got into promising areas but didn’t manage to take advantage of them.

“Motherwell are a good side and will maybe feel a bit aggrieved that they didn’t take more of their chances.”
