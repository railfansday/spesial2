Eve Rackham Watt records milestone win at Tennessee

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

No. 10 Tennessee (17-5, 8-4 SEC) defeated Mississippi State (17-6, 7-5 SEC), 25-20, 25-14, 29-27), Friday at Food City Center.

Friday's victory was the Lady Vols' 13th sweep of the 2026 season. Tennessee also ended a two-match losing streak. Head coach Eve Rackham Watt recorded her 100th home win and 100th conference victory.

Tennessee totaled 55 kills and a .354 hitting percentage against the Bulldogs. The Lady Vols committed 10 errors on 127 attacks.

Kennedi Rogers led Tennessee with 19 kills, while recording a .341 hitting percentage. Jalyn Stout had a double-double with 15 digs and 10 kills. She also recorded two blocks.

Chelsea Sutton recorded nine kills, two blocks and two assists, while Maggie Dostic totaled 12 digs and two aces.

Caroline Kerr had 34 assists and eight digs for Tennessee, while Anni Tang led the Lady Vols with five blocks.

Mississippi State recorded 39 kills and a .223 hitting percentage. The Bulldogs committed 12 errors on 121 attacks.
