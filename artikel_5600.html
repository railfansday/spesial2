New-home mortgage applications fall 13.6% in October

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Mortgage applications for new-home purchases fell 13.6% year over year and 7% from September, according to MBA’s Builder Application Survey. Rising mortgage rates and affordability pressures contributed to the decline.  AI Summary

Mortgage applications for new-home purchases fell 13.6% year over year in October as rising mortgage rates and affordability pressures weighed on buyer demand, according to the Mortgage Bankers Association’s (MBA) Builder Application Survey.

Applications also declined 7% from September. The figures are not adjusted for seasonal patterns.

The 30-year fixed mortgage rate climbed about 60 basis points over the two months leading up to October, reaching 7.5% last month, said Joel Kan, MBA’s vice president and deputy chief economist.

“As a result, applications for the purchase of newly built homes declined year-over-year for the fourth consecutive month,” Kan said.

MBA’s estimate of new-home sales increased slightly in October, but the annualized sales pace for the past two months remained at its weakest level since July 2022. Affordability pressures and uncertainty surrounding mortgage rates have prompted prospective buyers to delay purchases, even as builders continue offering incentives, Kan said.

MBA estimated that new single-family home sales ran at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 592,000 units in October, up 1.4% from September’s pace of 584,000 units.

On an unadjusted basis, MBA estimated 44,000 new-home sales in October, down 6.4% from 47,000 in September.

Conventional loans accounted for 47.9% of new-home mortgage applications in October, while Federal Housing Administration (FHA) loans made up 36.9%. U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) loans accounted for 14.1%, and  U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) loans represented 1%.

The average loan size for new homes declined to $368,934 in October from $374,724 in September.

MBA’s Builder Application Survey tracks mortgage application volume from homebuilder-affiliated mortgage companies nationwide. The association uses the data, along with other market information and assumptions, to estimate new-home sales at the national, state and metropolitan levels.

The survey provides an early indication of new-home sales because mortgage applications typically coincide with contract signings, when the Census Bureau records new-home sales in its monthly report.
