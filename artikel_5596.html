Deals, hires, and 2027 trend signals across real estate brokerages

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

This roundup covers brokerage growth through affiliations and acquisitions, recruiting activity, and leadership changes. It also highlights 2027 luxury and consumer trend reports and recognition for top-producing agents.  AI Summary

Luxury real estate trends, changing homebuyer preferences and brokerage expansion are among the latest developments across the industry. This roundup also highlights top-producing agents, an association leadership change and a Central Florida brokerage joining a national platform while retaining its independence.

The Agency has released its 2027 Red Paper, examining the wealth dynamics, buyer preferences and design innovations shaping luxury real estate markets worldwide.

Drawing on insights from its global network of agents and industry leaders, the report identifies trends expected to influence luxury real estate in 2027.

“The global luxury real estate market is moving in more than one direction at once,” said Mauricio Umansky, CEO and founder of RealTrends Verified brokerage The Agency. Read the Red Paper report.

Affordability pressures are changing how Americans think about homeownership, with buyers prioritizing flexible living spaces, personal expression and long-term functionality, according to the 2027 Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate Trends Report.

The report identifies three trends: distinctive design, creative uses for existing space and longer-term homeownership.

Buyers are embracing color. More than half of respondents (55%) said distinctive color choices would make them more likely to purchase a home. Younger buyers were particularly receptive, with 79% of Gen Z respondents and 64% of millennials favoring distinctive colors, compared with 38% of baby boomers and older consumers.

Homeowners are maximizing existing space. With higher housing costs making moves and additions less practical, 53% said they had repurposed underused space within the past one to three years. Another 72% plan to optimize unused areas in the coming year. Common conversions include home offices, storage, fitness areas and guest accommodations. Additionally, 81% said outdoor amenities could influence their choice between similarly priced homes.

Buyers are thinking longer term. Consumers are considering how homes can accommodate multiple life stages. Nearly one-third (31%) would accept an older home needing updates, while 30% would compromise on square footage.

Nearly half (47%) favor smaller improvements over time, compared with 26% who prefer major renovations.

The findings suggest affordability challenges are shifting consumer priorities toward adaptability, comfort and long-term livability.

Trifecta Real Estate, a locally owned brokerage in Brandon, Mississippi, has affiliated with Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate (BHGRE).

Now operating as Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate Trifecta, the firm was founded in 2023 by brokers Tye Densford and Alex Alexander.

Densford brings 20 years of experience as an agent, developer and former homebuilder. Alexander also has a homebuilding background and more than a decade of experience as an insurance agent.

Three Summit Sotheby’s International Realty RealTrends Verified agents — Sheila Hall, Michael LaPay and Chris Maddox — have been named among Sotheby’s International Realty’s Top 100 agents worldwide.

The distinction recognizes agents based on annual sales volume across a global network of more than 26,000 associates in 1,100 offices across 86 countries and territories.

Hall, earning her fourth Top 100 recognition, serves as director of real estate sales and exclusive listing agent for Deer Valley East Village, the first new alpine village developed in the United States in more than 40 years.

LaPay, a five-time honoree, has more than four decades of Park City real estate experience and has ranked as Summit Sotheby’s top-producing agent since 2016.

Maddox brings nearly 20 years of experience, including 13 in greater Park City, specializing in master-planned golf communities and luxury properties.

Canopy Realtor Association has appointed Rob Nanfelt as its next CEO, effective Jan. 4, 2027. He succeeds Anne Marie DeCatsye, who is retiring after 26 years of leadership. Nanfelt will also serve as CEO of Canopy Housing Foundation.

Nanfelt brings more than 25 years of experience in executive leadership, association management, strategic planning, government affairs and public policy.

As executive director of the Real Estate & Building Industry Coalition (REBIC), he has worked with business executives, elected officials and community leaders on economic development, infrastructure and transportation initiatives in the Charlotte region.

Lively Real Estate has joined RealTrends Verified ENRG Realty as an independent brand, allowing the Central Florida boutique brokerage to retain its identity and leadership while gaining access to ENRG’s national platform.

Gernelle and Dan Bokuniewicz will continue to own and lead Lively, with expanded operational support, national resources and revenue-sharing opportunities for agents.

“For a change to be worth it, Dan and I needed full ownership of Lively and the freedom to shape its future. We had worked hard to build a brand our community knows and trusts, so any move had to support that vision. It also had to offer meaningful opportunities for our agents, support for their businesses, and additional ways to build long-term income,” said Gernelle Bokuniewicz, founder of Lively Real Estate.

ERA Property Elite expands into Morgantown with KLM Properties acquisition

ERA Property Elite, based in Charleston, West Virginia, has acquired KLM Properties Inc., expanding its reach into Morgantown and North Central West Virginia.

Founded in 1994, KLM Properties will operate as KLM Properties ERA Powered, retaining its local identity while gaining access to ERA Real Estate’s technology, marketing resources and referral network.

Chaille and Whitney Adkins, who purchased KLM Properties in 2022, bring experience in residential and commercial real estate and construction. Chaille will continue overseeing daily operations in Morgantown.

Ming Tree Realtors, a family-owned brokerage serving California’s Humboldt County since 1972, has affiliated with Century 21 Real Estate.

Now operating as Century 21 Ming Tree, the brokerage maintains offices in Eureka and Fortuna under broker/owner Larry Doss.

A second-generation real estate professional, Doss joined the family business in 1995 before taking over leadership from his father, company co-founder Larry G. Doss. He previously served as president of the Humboldt Association of Realtors in 2007 and was named the Eureka Chamber of Commerce Business Person of the Year in 2015.

Christie’s International Real Estate has named Art Tobani national vice president of affiliate development to accelerate the luxury brokerage network’s expansion across the United States.

Tobani will also help identify growth opportunities for @properties, which has affiliates in six states and Washington, D.C.

He brings more than 20 years of real estate business development experience, including 12 years in brokerage affiliate sales and advisory services. His work in affiliate recruitment, mergers and acquisitions, and recruiting has generated approximately $12 billion in real estate sales volume.

Naples, Florida-based John R. Wood Properties has launched a wellness initiative providing more than 850 agents and staff with individualized coaching focused on personal well-being, resilience and professional development.

Developed in partnership with HatchPath, a Naples-based coaching and wellness platform, the program expands beyond traditional real estate sales coaching to support agents’ personal and professional needs. Unlike conventional coaching focused on production and prospecting, the brokerage is investing in broader well-being services for its workforce.

Compass has added New York City new development specialist Doron Zwickel and his three-person team, who bring more than $1 billion in career sales volume.

Previously affiliated with boutique brokerage CORE, Zwickel has more than 20 years of experience in residential new development and resale. The move expands his team’s reach across Manhattan.

Take Action Team, a Florida-based real estate team led by CEO Mari Parks, has joined Keller Williams Realty Florida Partners in Port Orange, Florida. The team, which includes 13 agents and two staff members, closed 122 transactions totaling $49 million in sales volume in 2025. Parks said the move will provide agents with additional resources and growth opportunities while maintaining the team’s local expertise and personalized service.
