SpaceX launched a Falcon 9 rocket carrying a classified US Space Force satellite from Vandenberg Space Force Base in California on Tuesday. The mission, called USSF-266, lifted off from Space Launch Complex 4E at 8:08 am local time, or 9:38 pm IST, within a four-hour launch window. The first-stage booster was making its 29th flight. It has previously flown 16 Starlink missions and a range of government and commercial launches, including NASA’s Crew-7 and CRS-29 missions, the PACE and EarthCARE climate satellites, and payloads for the National Reconnaissance Office, which operates US spy satellites. After separating from the upper stage, the booster was set to land on the Of Course I Still Love You droneship in the Pacific Ocean. Officials warned residents of Santa Barbara, San Luis Obispo and Ventura counties that they might hear sonic booms. The launch follows a $985 million increase in the combined ceiling for US military launch contracts shared by SpaceX, Blue Origin and United Launch Alliance.
