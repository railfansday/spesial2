AOI Sales Up 127%, Gross Margin Stuck at 28%: Rapid Earnings Reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Datacenter demand is lifting sales at this optical networking company, but profit is not keeping up. We look at what supports the bottom line and what the outlook requires.

Applied Optoelectronics (AAOI -20.89%) grew third-quarter revenue 127% to $269.9 million, led by datacenter sales. Shares fell about 8% in after-hours trading. Profit is not keeping pace with sales. Gross margin is stuck near 28%, operations still lost money, and the GAAP profit came from other income.

GAAP net income rests on $10.1 million of other income, because operations lost money. The largest piece, a line called "other income (expense), net," gets no explanation in the release.

Gross margin barely moved even as revenue more than doubled. Ramping new transceiver lines usually costs yield, which holds margin down until volumes settle. Guidance for next quarter is 29.5% to 31% on a non-GAAP basis, only a modest step up.

Guidance calls for fourth-quarter revenue about 49% above this quarter at the midpoint. The company also says its 2027 US capacity expansion will take longer, because it is waiting on electrical power and facing other construction issues, though it kept its end-of-2027 target.

Watch whether GAAP operating income turns positive in the fourth quarter as revenue rises toward the guided range. Also track whether the US expansion gets its electrical power in time for the end-of-2027 capacity target.
