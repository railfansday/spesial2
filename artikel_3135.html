Gerdau: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Brazil's import headwinds are proving manageable as North American strength funds shareholder returns without straining the balance sheet.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 27, 2026, Gerdau (GGB -0.11%) announced a North American EBITDA margin, or earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization, of 27.7%. Long-term investors should care because high profitability in North America is offsetting persistent steel import pressures in Brazil.

Heavy spending on data centers and energy projects keeps order volumes elevated in North America, helping insulate overall operating results from broader market choppiness.

Surging foreign steel imports continue to weigh on domestic Brazilian pricing, prompting management to shift focus toward internal productivity and lower capital expenditures, or cash used for physical asset upgrades.

Strong free cash flow generation allows management to prioritize direct shareholder returns through quarterly dividends and share repurchases while maintaining low debt leverage.

Orders from artificial intelligence infrastructure and power transmission lines continue piling up across North America, and management is capitalizing on that operational momentum. Internal iron ore production from the Miguel Burnier mine expansion in Brazil will soon lower raw material costs for blast furnaces, while specialized rolling mill capacity expands in Texas. Management is watching domestic trade defense decisions and global scrap price stability.

Capital spending is trending down toward essential maintenance as large expansion initiatives wrap up. Low leverage gives the balance sheet flexibility to manage interest rate shifts while maintaining ongoing shareholder distributions in Brazilian reais, abbreviated as BRL.
