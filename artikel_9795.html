Where to Publish Articles Online for Free: 7 Best Platforms for Writers

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Creating valuable content is only the first step toward becoming a successful writer. Finding the right place to share it is equally important. Fortunately, there are several websites where beginners, bloggers, freelancers, and professionals can publish articles online for free without purchasing hosting or building their own websites.

Whether you want to share personal experiences, write educational content, promote your business, or improve your online visibility, free publishing platforms make it easier to reach readers.

In this guide, we will explore seven platforms where you can publish articles online for free and start building your online presence.

Medium is a popular publishing platform for writers who want to share stories, tutorials, opinions, and educational articles. It has an established reading community, making it a useful starting point for new writers.

Best for: Bloggers, beginners, storytellers, and professional writers.

WordPress.com allows users to create blogs and publish articles without managing their own hosting. Its free plan provides a simple way to start writing and organize content.

Best for: Bloggers who want to build a long-term online presence.

Blogger is a free blogging service from Google. Users can create a blog, publish articles, customize its appearance, and manage their content without paying for hosting.

Best for: Beginners and personal bloggers.

LinkedIn is not just a professional networking website. It also allows users to publish long-form articles and share industry knowledge with a professional audience.

You can write about business, career development, marketing, leadership, and professional experiences.

Best for: Professionals, freelancers, entrepreneurs, and business owners.

Substack is a publishing platform that combines blogging with email newsletters. Writers can publish articles and build an audience through email subscriptions.

It is especially useful for people who want to regularly share knowledge with interested readers.

Best for: Newsletter creators, independent writers, and industry experts.

DEV Community is a publishing platform designed primarily for developers and software professionals. Writers can share programming tutorials, coding experiences, development guides, and technical knowledge.

Best for: Developers, programmers, and technology bloggers.

If you are searching for a simple place to publish articles online for free, PostNest is another platform worth exploring.

PostNest is a free blogging platform where writers, students, developers, entrepreneurs, and content creators can publish their knowledge and ideas online.

You don't need to build your own website or maintain separate hosting to start sharing articles. Whether you want to write technology tutorials, business insights, educational guides, lifestyle content, or personal experiences, PostNest provides a place to publish your work.

Best for: Beginners, bloggers, developers, freelancers, and content creators.

Choose a platform that matches your writing goals and target audience.
