Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Every week CISA adds vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalogue. The bar for getting on that list is not "severe on paper" but "attackers are using it now". For a small team with limited patching hours, that makes KEV the most useful priority list there is.

Below: every entry added between 16 November and 22 November 2026, copied from the catalogue and linked to its NVD record, plus what Romania's national cyber security directorate and Have I Been Pwned published in the same days. Nothing is estimated or rewritten.
