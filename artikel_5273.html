Ethereum is trading well below its highs, but SharpLink CEO Joseph Chalom says the long-term case for ETH is getting stronger. Chalom, a former BlackRock executive who helped launch its tokenized fund BUIDL, told the Milk Road podcast that crypto no longer needs a single big catalyst to move forward.

What matters now, according to Chalom, is how quickly Wall Street moves on-chain, and he named three numbers to track it.

Chalom offered an analogy. The internet is open, neutral and hard to censor, but it never had a token. Ethereum, he said, is closer to a huge public highway with toll booths:

He recalled that BlackRock CEO Larry Fink showed a slide at Davos in January saying Ethereum is “the toll road to tokenization.”

Fees on Ethereum are low today because there is still plenty of block space. Chalom expects that to change as stablecoins, tokenized assets, on-chain finance and AI agents bring a surge of transactions. When that happens, he says, demand for ETH as both a store of value and a security asset will rise.

Chalom said he agrees that crypto does not need one specific trigger. The drivers are already in place: stablecoins, tokenized assets, DeFi and AI agents. The real question is how fast they grow.
