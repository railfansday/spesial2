Chemed: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Brand strength and operational scale may offset near-term margin pressure from strategic acquisitions and rising marketing costs.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 28, 2026, Chemed (CHE -0.38%) announced that it expects full-year adjusted earnings per share (EPS), a measure of net income excluding one-off costs, to finish at or slightly above the top end of its previous guidance. The company combines end-of-life care with nationwide residential plumbing services, generating steady operational cash flow to fund share repurchases and strategic franchise buybacks.

Chemed spent $96 million in 2026 to reacquire Roto-Rooter franchise territories across California and Texas. While these acquired territories initially operate at lower earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA) margins, management expects top-line revenue to expand rapidly as operations integrate.

VITAS Healthcare, Chemed's hospice subsidiary, drove revenue growth through higher patient days of care and back-office cost discipline. Targeted recruitment and electronic staffing tools allowed management to meet rising patient demand without experiencing severe hiring friction.

Roto-Rooter faces persistent marketing headwinds as internet search platforms charge higher fees per lead while reducing non-paid organic placement. Management relies on brand equity, direct commercial accounts, and higher average prices per job to offset marketing expense pressures.

Patient census growth is accelerating across healthcare settings, and Chemed benefits from brand recognition alongside centralized administrative scale. Management is watching lead acquisition costs at Roto-Rooter and admission mix at VITAS Healthcare to maintain long-term profitability.

Strategic reacquisitions of independent plumbing territories will expand owned branch coverage over the next 12 to 18 months. As operations normalize, management expects operating leverage to assert itself across the branch network.
