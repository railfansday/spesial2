Champions League Talking Points: De Bruyne's return, Como's rise and Bayern's Kane crisis

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Champions League offers plenty of intrigue this week, from emotional reunions and emerging European contenders to injury crises and managerial pressure.

Manchester City prepare to welcome back a club legend, while Como's remarkable debut campaign is beginning to demand serious attention.

Elsewhere,Paris Saint-Germain's pursuit of a third successive title faces an unexpected test, Bayern Munich must find a way to cope without Harry Kane, and Mauricio Pochettino looks to put a disastrous Manchester United debut behind him.

With the league phase beginning to take shape, there is plenty at stake across Europe. Here are five talking points to watch in theChampions League.

Few footballers have had as indelible an impact on Manchester City asKevin De Bruyne. A spine of Vincent Kompany, Yaya Toure, David Silva and Sergio Aguero helped establish the Cityzens as a force, but De Bruyne was central to the later highs.

A Centurion, Domestic Treble Winner, Treble Winner, and assist machine, De Bruyne was a master of his craft and, undisputedly, among the greatest Premier League midfielders of all time. He returns resembling something of a fading force, and has struggled to make the desired impact since a move toNapoli.

At 35, his future is unclear, but his past is sure to be celebrated during an Etihad homecoming on Tuesday night.

Como's first Champions League campaign has been one to remember.

The tournament debuts are nestled between European giants near the top of the league phase standings, and trail only Paris Saint-Germain for goals scored. The Serie A side have thrashed RB Leipzig and Manchester United, before humiliating Lens by a record-equalling 9-1 scoreline.

AEK Athens are next up for Cesc Fabregas's talented team, who are fifth domestically but thriving in Europe. A win would be a giant step towards the knockout rounds, before real acid tests against Barcelona and PSG to conclude the group.

Paris Saint-Germain have been the best side in Europe over the last two seasons, becoming only the second team to win back-to-back Champions League titles in the modern era. The holders have started the league phase in ominous fashion, winning all four games, scoring 20 goals goals in the process.

However, PSG have been rocked by Desire Doue's season-ending knee issue, with the 21-year-old joiningKhvicha Kvaratskhelia in the treatment room. Neither will feature again this season, leaving the champions without two major stars in the final third.

Mika Godts, Ferran Torres and Maghnes Akliouche all arrived to provide depth in the summer, but is that group enough?

Another side facing a significant spell without a key man is Bayern Munich. Harry Kane is set to be sidelined for the festive period after suffering an ankle ligament tear.

It piles on the pressure for the German champions, who are already without Luis Diaz, Alphonso Davies, Manuel Neuer, Serge Gnabry and Ismael Saibari. Michael Olise was also forced off against FC Köln, potentially leaving Bayern without their entire first-choice frontline.

With Kane and his most likely deputies in Saibari and Gnarby sidelined, Bayern have a striker crisis.

Mauricio Pochettino's start as Manchester United could not have gone much worse. A 4-0 hammering at your biggest rivals, featuring an eighth-minute red card, was a nightmare start to his tenure.

Mick McCarthy might disagree, but it can't get worse from here, can it?

Manchester United head to Sporting aiming to keep alive hopes of progress, with Pochettino's first task to fix up the most porous defence in Europe. The decision to start Matthijs de Ligt at Anfield backfired, while a shape shift to a back five might be considered from the start here.

Sporting are also under new management, but have had a positive start. Paulo Fonseca has won each of his first two games in Lisbon.
