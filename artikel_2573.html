Memphis takes on Golden State, looks for 5th straight victory

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Golden State Warriors (8-7, 10th in the Western Conference) vs. Memphis Grizzlies (11-4, fifth in the Western Conference)

LINE: Grizzlies -8.5; over/under is 225.5

BOTTOM LINE: Memphis is looking to keep its four-game win streak going when the Grizzlies take on Golden State.

The Warriors have gone 6-7 against Western Conference opponents. Golden State ranks fifth in the Western Conference with 27.5 assists per game led by Draymond Green averaging 6.5.
