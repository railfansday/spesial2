Texas defensive back Kobe Black out for rest of season with knee injury but Hero Kanu could return

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

AUSTIN - Texas safety Kobe Black has been ruled out for the rest of the season, but defensive tackle Hero Kanu could possibly return to the team in the playoffs after his arm surgery a week ago, Texas head coach Steve Sarkisian said on Monday.

Asked if there's any chance Kanu could get back after surgery for a torn biceps, Sarkisian smiled and said, "Potentially. I'll use that word."

Black, who was moved from cornerback in the offseason and played 341 snaps, left Saturday's game against Arkansas early with a knee injury. His absence will deprive Texas of one of its top backups.

"I hate it for him," Sarkisian said. "It's disappointing. He was having a really good year for us with the position change. But the other guys need to step up."
