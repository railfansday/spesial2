Binance withdrawals are back after October’s hack and here’s why yours might still be blocked

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Binance customers can check network maintenance and account restrictions to identify what is delaying their crypto withdrawals.

On Nov. 26, Binance said it had lifted the withdrawal freeze imposed after its October hack, but customers moving crypto to their own wallets may still encounter pauses on individual networks or account restrictions.

In its recovery update, the exchange said withdrawals on a small number of networks were still paused for maintenance or planned upgrades.

Binance described those pauses as routine and separate from the work it did to restore withdrawals after the hack.

Open Binance’s withdrawal screen for the asset you want to send and check the status of the network you plan to use. You can also check that asset and network on Binance’s public network status page.

Binance’s withdrawal suspension FAQ says wallet maintenance can temporarily stop withdrawals, and a network upgrade can make withdrawals of a particular asset unavailable.

For an upgrade-related pause, the FAQ says the withdrawal screen shows the reason and an estimated recovery time. Customers can set a reminder on that screen to get a status update.

A restriction on your account’s withdrawal function requires a different check, so the next step is to review the account alert for the reason and next steps.

An asset may show an available withdrawal route while the account still has a hold. In that case, the status page answers the route question, and the notice on your account tells you what action Binance needs from you.

Binance’s account guide says some security actions, including changing a password, can trigger a temporary withdrawal hold. Depending on the restriction, the alert may ask you to complete verification or offer an appeal.

The Nov. 26 notice also warned customers about messages promising to speed up a withdrawal. Binance says it will never ask for your password, two-factor authentication code, or wallet seed phrase. If someone contacts you about a delay, go directly to the Binance app or website for support.

The latest update closes a phased reopening that had already begun before Nov. 26. On Nov. 13, Binance restored access on some networks and was still working to reopen other routes, even after the exchange said it had replaced the customer assets lost in the hack.
