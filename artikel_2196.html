Camilo Duran rails against Celtic crisis as star man vows 'everything will change'

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Camilo Duran has issued a Celtic rallying cry in the wake of Sunday's nightmare defeat to Falkirk.

The striker has been one of the few bright sparks in an increasingly bleak season for the Premiership champions, with their alarming dip in form threatening to derail their campaign.

Celtic remain top of the Premiership, but supporters can no longer ignore the problems affecting the team both on and off the pitch.

Despite the growing scrutiny, Martin O'Neill remains bullish, and Duran is convinced the team can turn things around despite losing six of their last 10 matches.

Posting on Instagram, the £6million summer signing from Qarabag said: "The tough times are also part of the journey. Today we need to resist, work in silence and keep the faith. This is not the end, this is just another test. Everything will change, because those who never stop fighting always find a way to get up."

Duran was withdrawn in the second half against Falkirk, but he remains Celtic's key man after scoring 11 goals in 18 appearances this season.
