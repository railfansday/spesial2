Qatar GP: UK schedule and how to watch on Sky Sports

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Find out the race-week schedule, weather and how to watch the Qatar Grand Prix at the Lusail International Circuit; watch every session live on Sky Sports F1 from Friday, with qualifying at 6pm on Saturday and the race at 4pm on Sunday

Formula 1 heads to the Middle East for the last two rounds of the 2026 season with the Qatar Grand Prix hosting the penultimate event this weekend.

After a dramatic Las Vegas Grand Prix which saw 34 lead changes and Max Verstappen pull off a defensive masterclass, the F1 circus has moved to Qatar and the high-speed Lusail International Circuit.

Verstappen, who is set to finish runner-up in the Drivers' Championship behind Kimi Antonelli, won in Qatar last year after McLaren made a poor strategy call when in position for a one-two.

Like last year, there is a tyre limit which will create a minimum two-stop race with no driver allowed to do more than 27 laps on a set of tyres in the 50-lap race on Sunday.

This is due to the heavy loads which the tyres are put through in the fast corners which come one after another. The combination of this and this year's 2026 cars could create another fascinating race, particularly with high tension at the front between Mercedes and Red Bull as Verstappen and George Russell's rivalry escalated in Las Vegas.

Qualifying on Saturday is at 6pm UK time with the Qatar Grand Prix on Sunday at 4pm, all live on Sky Sports F1.

The 3.367-mile Lusail International Circuit joined the F1 schedule in 2021 and is one of the highest speed tracks on the calendar due to its fast, flowing corners which punish the tyres but also makes following the car in front difficult.

In the first four editions of the event, which took place when DRS was still in use, most of the overtakes came at Turn One where drivers can make moves on the outside and inside, so it will be interesting to see whether the 2026 regulations create any additional opportunities.
