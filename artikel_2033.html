7 players set to miss Norwich City v QPR ft £4.3m star

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Midweek action returns in the Championship this week, and Norwich City will be looking to continue this impressive run of form they find themselves on against play-off challengers QPR.

The Canaries have benefitted from their schedule easing up slightly, winning five games in a row against sides in the bottom half of the table, including on Saturday against Cardiff City.

They're now being tasked with beating a side in the top eight, as QPR make the trip to Norfolk buoyed by back-to-back wins either side of the break against Lincoln City and Millwall.

Julien Stephan's side are proving to be the real deal in the play-off race this season, putting in great performances per the underlying numbers and taking points off of fellow strong sides, too. This will be another test, with Norwich beginning the gameweek just three points behind them.

All eyes will be on who prevails at Carrow Road, but these seven players won't be available.

The main absence at Norwich continues to be Mohamed Toure, who has now missed two months with the shoulder injury which forced him off after 15 minutes against Bolton Wanderers.

The free-scoring Aussie has 16 goals in 21 Norwich games since arriving last January from Randers, but has been plagued by injury ever since making the move. Canaries fans will be hoping that he can stay healthy for the remainder of the campaign once he makes his return.

Papa Diallo, who the Canaries signed for a reported £4.3m, has also had some terrible luck with injuries ever since making the move to Norwich from French side Metz ahead of last season.

He only played 11 times last season due to a couple of setbacks, and has missed over two months now with a quad injury. The Senegalese international hasn't been seen since coming off at half-time in their defeat against Manchester City in the EFL Cup.

Speaking ahead of the win over Cardiff, Norwich boss Philippe Clement did say that he'll be back in a few weeks, though.

Young Gabe Forsyth has now gone two years without a senior appearance for Norwich. He suffered a horrific knee injury which kept him out for 21 months, but has since made three PL2 appearances for the Under-21s, scoring against Birmingham City.

Ali Ahmed hasn't featured yet this season, and will be out until the summer after rupturing his ACL on the eve of the new campaign.

The Canadian international scored four and assisted three in the second half of last season after making a January move from MLS side Vancouver Whitecaps.

QPR will continue to be without their captain, Jimmy Dunne, after an injury picked up on international duty with the Republic of Ireland saw him miss out on Saturday against Millwall.

Speaking ahead of the trip to Norwich, R's boss Julien Stephan admitted that it's "not a bad" injury, but he won't be involved at Carrow Road.

QPR received a big boost at the top end of the pitch against Millwall, with Rumarn Burrell making his return after missing the last two months.
