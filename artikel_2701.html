Williams to stay in charge of Swans for Boro trip

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Ashley Williams will remain at the helm for Swansea City's trip to Middlesbrough on Tuesday after taking a point against West Bromwich Albion in his first game as interim boss.

Swansea started life after Vitor Matos by coming from behind twice in a chaotic 2-2 draw with the Baggies at the Swansea.com Stadium.

Williams, who was seen having a lengthy discussion with Swansea chief executive Tom Gorringe after the game, says he has been told he will remain in charge for the midweek trip to face Championship leaders Boro.

"I will go to Middlesbrough with the team," Swansea's interim boss said.

"I will be concentrating on the build-up to Middlesbrough and the Middlesbrough game, and that is it at that point.

"I don't know what happens [after that]. I'm not too interested, if I'm being deadly honest, because all eyes are on Middlesbrough after this. I can tell you that much, and that's it at this point."

Williams is leading a caretaker management team also featuring fellow Swansea old boys Leon Britton, Joe Allen and Kristian O'Leary following this week's shock departure of Vitor Matos.

The 42-year-old, who has primarily worked as a pundit since retiring as a player, said he "absolutely loved" the experience of leading Swansea from the touchline.

"To be back here as well was special, and to be with this set of players at this football club is special," he added.

"When you're in it, you're concentrating so much that that's all you're thinking about. But when the dust settles and I digest it, maybe this evening or whenever, it's a really proud moment for me.

"Even when we went a goal down, I was enjoying it. I was in it with the players.

"I wanted them to be aware of me. I wanted them to see me and hear me. If we're suffering for a minute, I'm with them. If we score, I'm with them and I'm happy. It was really nice to feel connected with the team and the whole stadium."

Williams' side endured the worst possible start as Isaac Price gave West Brom a seventh-minute lead, but they quickly replied through Moussa Yeo.

Rabby Nzingoula then restored Albion's lead before Baggies forward Isaac Price's Panenka penalty was saved by Lawrence Vigouroux.

Eli Just swiftly made it 2-2, with the New Zealander also hitting the bar after Melker Widell had struck the post, though Swansea ended up feeling relieved to claim a point as West Brom pushed hard for a second-half winner.

"It was end-to-end and very frantic," Williams said.

"But my natural thought is just how proud I am of the players and the fans.

"To respond after a week like this, an uncertain week, was impressive. Maybe we saw that uncertainty in our team at the start, in the first few minutes, but they recovered themselves."

Swansea will assess Yeo after he was forced off in the second half with a lower leg injury.
