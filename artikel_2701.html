San Francisco 49ers continue to haunt Kirk Cousins

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Week 11 saw the Las Vegas Raiders continue to struggle against the playoff contenders on their schedule, falling 38-24 to the Denver Broncos on Sunday. But let's check in on a few old friends and see how the weekend went for a few former Raiders: Kirk Cousins, Tre'von Moehrig and Marcus Epps.

Stats: 26/38 (68.4%), 235 yards, 0 TDs, 1 INT

Cousins' last start with the Raiders was a 38-17 loss to the San Francisco 49ers, and his second start with the Minnesota Vikings this season was a 35-10 loss to the 49ers. Clearly, San Francisco just has his number, as facing the same team/defense twice in three weeks didn't make a difference the second time around. There's no shame in that since San Francisco looks like a Super Bowl favorite, asserting its dominance over another contender for the top seed in the NFC.

Considering how bad the Baltimore Ravens have been without Lamar Jackson, this game was way too close for the Carolina Panthers. But the Panthers managed to get a big win that gives them a two-game lead in the NFC South. Moehrig was part of that, finishing third on the team in tackles. This week's former Raider check-in is paying extra attention to the Raiders' 2023 safety duo. Epps hasn't done much since leaving Las Vegas and is more of a special teamer in Philadelphia this season. However, he received more playing time this week and made the most of it, ranking second on the Eagles in tackles. Also, all eight tackles were solos as the Birds got a much-needed win over their in-state rivals, the Pittsburgh Steelers. That win also helped the Raiders' playoff odds (slightly).
