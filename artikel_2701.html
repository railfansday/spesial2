Jahmyr Gibbs wins second NFC offensive player of the week award of the season

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Lions running back Jahmyr Gibbs continued his terrific 2026 season in Sunday’s win over the Patriots in Munich.

Now the league has recognized him for his performance in that contest.

Gibbs has been named NFC offensive player of the week.

Gibbs put up 252 yards from scrimmage (153 rushing, 99 receiving) along with two rushing touchdowns in the 35-10 win.

According to the league, Gibbs is just the second player under the age of 25 in NFL history with at least 250 yards from scrimmage and two rushing touchdowns in multiple games, joining Hall of Famer LaDainian Tomlinson. He also scored his 61st and 62nd touchdowns, which puts him past Hall of Famer Terrell Davis for the most touchdowns by a player in his first four seasons in league history.

The 252 yards were the most by a player in a single game so far this season. They were also the second most in an international game in league history.

Gibbs now has five career offensive player of the week awards. He last won it for Week 5 of this year.

Through nine games, Gibbs leads the league with 1,000 rushing yards, 1,384 yards from scrimmage, 11 rushing touchdowns, and 13 total TDs.
