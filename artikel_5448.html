Replit Launches Replit & Co., a New Creator Program Bringing B2B Voices Into the Era of the Self-Driving Company

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Replit marked the launch with an event this week featuring Marina Mogilko, known as "Silicon Valley Girl."

The program brings together 45 B2B creators to show enterprise audiences how to put AI agents to work

SAN FRANCISCO, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Replit, the agentic software creation platform that enables anyone to build applications without learning to code, today announced Replit & Co., a new B2B creator program that brings together business and technology creators to demonstrate how enterprises can build software and put agents to work with Replit. The program supports Replit's vision of the self-driving company, where people define goals and remain accountable while agents execute work within clear boundaries.

Replit & Co. launched with 45 creators, including Marina Mogilko, known as "Silicon Valley Girl"; April Little, named to the 2025 TIME100 Creators list; and Carly Taylor, engineering leader and founder of Rebel Data Science.

The program gives creators access to Replit's product updates and opportunities to work directly with its team. Through content and demonstrations, creators can show their communities how to use Replit's AI agents to build applications, automate workflows and get more done across their businesses.

"The people closest to the work usually have the best ideas about what needs to change. Our vision of the self-driving company is about making it easier for them to act on those ideas, with AI agents handling more of the execution," said Haya Odeh, co-founder and Head of Design at Replit. "Replit & Co. brings that vision to business audiences through creators they already trust and learn from."

As agents take on more of the execution, employees across companies can turn ideas into working software without waiting on traditional development cycles.

"Business audiences don't need another prediction about AI. They need to see what it can actually do," said Nick Silver, VP of Marketing at Replit. "We're looking for creators who understand how businesses work and can show people how to solve real problems. The goal is for someone to watch a demo and think, 'We could build that for our team.'"

Members of Replit & Co. receive Replit account benefits, regular product updates and opportunities to connect with Replit's team and broader ecosystem. Additional opportunities include early access to new capabilities, member showcases, sessions with Replit leadership and co-branded events.

The program is open to creators with expertise in business, technology and related fields. Replit is looking for creators with credible voices and engaged audiences, not simply large followings. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.

Replit is the agentic software creation platform that enables anyone to build applications without learning code. Founded in 2016, the company has more than 60 million users worldwide, including users at 85% of the Fortune 500. Teams at enterprises including Atlassian, Accenture, Adobe, and Databricks use Replit to build apps, and the company has partnerships with Google, Stripe, and Slack, among others. Replit is headquartered in San Francisco with offices in London and Tokyo.
