Sunrise on the Reaping Makes Hunger Games A Box Office Blockbuster Franchise Again

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping raked in the dough its opening weekend

The opening weekend box office estimates for The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping are in, and it's off to a strong start. While The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes had a somewhat muted opening in 2023 compared to the franchise's original films, Sunrise on the Reaping has had the best opening weekend of any movie released this November.

Deadline reports that Sunrise on the Reaping has grossed $120 million at the North American box office during its opening weekend. While its haul doesn't quite reach the heights of the $150+ million openings of the first two Hunger Games films, it's similar to Mockingjay — Part 1's opening weekend ($121.9 million) and better than Mockingjay — Part 2 ($102.7 million). Sunrise on the Reaping's performance is significantly better than fellow prequel The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes' $44.6 million opening weekend gross.

The Haymitch-focused prequel has been received well by critics and audiences. Polygon's review called it "an incredibly entertaining blockbuster with the feel of a bleak, existential horror movie." Now, its opening weekend gross shows that The Hunger Games franchise can still reach blockbuster status at the box office, too. It's another big theatrical win for Lionsgate, which had another hit on its hands earlier this year with Michael.

Outside of Sunrise on the Reaping, Sylvester Stallone biopic I Play Rocky had an expanded theatrical release this weekend and brought in $9.3 million. The viral You Can See Everything also crossed the $100 million mark this week, an impressive feat for an unusual documentary.
