AI-enabled attacks surge 89 percent as global security innovators warn that governing autonomous AI agents will become a defining cybersecurity challenge in 2027

Dubai, UAE: Autonomous AI agents are emerging as the next major cybersecurity challenge as they gain access to sensitive data, execute transactions and operate beyond direct human oversight. At GITEX Cyber Valley 2026, taking place at GITEX GLOBAL 2026 from 8–11 December at Dubai Exhibition Centre, Expo City Dubai, cybersecurity leaders will spotlight the growing imperative to govern these autonomous systems as businesses prepare for a new era of AI-driven risks in 2027 and beyond.

Headline exhibitor CrowdStrike’s 2026 Global Threat Report underscores the escalating threat landscape, recording an 89 percent increase in attacks by AI-enabled adversaries in 2025. Meanwhile, the World Economic Forum’s Global Cybersecurity Outlook 2026 found that 87 percent of respondents identified AI-related vulnerabilities as the fastest-growing cyber risk, highlighting the need for stronger AI governance, identity controls, and enterprise data protection.

Cloudflare, a global connectivity cloud company, predicts that AI agents will overtake humans as the internet’s primary users by 2030, transforming how businesses interact and transact online while creating new challenges around identity and authorisation.

At GITEX GLOBAL, Cloudflare will showcase technologies for building and securing AI agents, including shadow AI visibility, workload protection and a unified gateway for managing AI models. The company is also expanding its UAE data centres to support local control over AI workloads.

Forcepoint, a cybersecurity specialist in data protection and AI security, warns that autonomous agents will increasingly function as a digital workforce, creating challenges beyond traditional IT oversight.

The company predicts that by 2030, a business employing 40,000 people could operate millions of AI-powered working entities, raising questions about who controls them and what information they can access.

said Samer Diya, Senior Vice President, EMEA, Forcepoint.

Forcepoint will showcase its AI Data Security platform, which protects sensitive information across cloud, networks, endpoints and AI environments while governing autonomous agents and unsanctioned AI activity. Its ARIA AI Assistant enables security teams to create and enforce protection policies using natural language.

Keypasco, a cybersecurity and digital identity specialist, believes AI security must evolve beyond protecting models to governing what autonomous agents can do, who authorises their actions and who remains accountable.

At the event, the company will introduce the next evolution of its Trust Identity Platform, extending Zero Trust governance beyond employees and devices to agents that access enterprise systems and execute business workflows.

Rapid7, a global cybersecurity company specialising in exposure management, threat detection and incident response, identifies greater accountability for AI agents as a major cybersecurity development expected in 2027.

said Gopan Sivasankaran, Regional Director – Middle East and Africa, Rapid7.

He stressed that significant agent actions must be logged, human approval required where appropriate and access revoked quickly when necessary.

Rapid7 will demonstrate its AI-enabled Command Platform at Mindware's stand, using agentic AI workflows to gather evidence, support investigations and automate repetitive security tasks.

The focus on AI governance comes as the UAE Cyber Security Council reports more than 800,000 cyberattack attempts daily.

The Cybersecurity Stage, one of several stages within the wider conference programme, will address sovereign security, nation-state threats, supply-chain vulnerabilities, IT/OT convergence and post-quantum readiness.

Leading the discussions will be H.E. Dr. Mohamed Al Kuwaiti, Head of Cybersecurity, UAE Government; Michelle Bordachar Benoit, National Director, National Cybersecurity Agency (ANCI), Chile; and Ioannis Alexakis, Director General, National Cybersecurity Authority, Greece.

International speakers include Mikko Hyppönen, Chief Research Officer at Sensofusion and a globally recognised cybersecurity researcher; Kevin T. Shin, Head of Information Security, Samsung Semiconductor; Corey Deng, Chief Security Officer, Huawei; and Christian Reilly, Field CTO, Cloudflare.

The 46th edition of GITEX GLOBAL, organised by inD under the theme ‘Global Reset 2027’, opens with the GITEX Summit at Dubai World Trade Centre on 7 December, followed by the exhibition at Expo City Dubai alongside Expand North Star, which connects the global startup and investment community. Together, the events will feature more than 6,000 enterprise and startup exhibitors, attracting government leaders, investors and technology buyers worldwide.
