IPL 2027: Punjab Kings retention list, released players and remaining purse

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Punjab Kings (PBKS) have announced their list of retained and released players ahead of the IPL 2027 auction, with the franchise making six changes to its squad following the previous season.Nehal Wadhera and Lockie Ferguson are among the notable names released by the franchise.Punjab Kings will enter the auction with INR 18.60 crore remaining in their purse and six slots to fill, including two overseas positions.
