Seth McGowan unable to practice Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Indianapolis Colts RB Seth McGowan (hamstring) did not participate in practice Wednesday, Nov. 25.

Why It Matters: McGowan is expected to miss some time because of the injury, so fantasy players should plan on him missing at least one game. Jonathan Taylor should receive nearly all of the carries in the running game this week and can be considered a No. 2 running back in fantasy leagues.
