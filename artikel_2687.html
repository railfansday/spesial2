NFL Picks Against the Spread: Week 11 Games

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Before the eleventh Sunday of the 2026 regular season kicks off, let's have some fun by taking a look at the lines for the NFL Week 11 games.

You can CLICK HERE for straight up NFL picks without regard to the spread.

My overall record this season is 71-76-2, which is slightly ahead of the collective BGN community at 69-78-2. We're slowly but surely trying to creep above .500.

Here are suggestions when trying to beat the odds.

NEW ORLEANS SAINTS at CHICAGO BEARS (-11.5): The Bears are coming off their bye. The Saints only have two losses this season by more than one possession … and those were back in early October. But Chicago's offense is capable of scoring enough to cover this line. PICK: Bears -11.5

BALTIMORE RAVENS at CAROLINA PANTHERS (-2.5): The Panthers aren't really worth believing in. The Ravens have enough fight in them to pick up a much-needed win in order to try to keep their season afloat. Jacoby Brissett has had more time to get comfortable with the offense. PICK: Ravens +2.5

MIAMI DOLPHINS at BUFFALO BILLS (-16.5): That's … a lot of points! But am I really going to bet on Kyle McCord in his return to upstate New York over Josh Allen? No. PICK: Bills -16.5

TENNESSEE TITANS at DALLAS COWBOYS (-7.5): The Dallas defense is truly dreadful. That being said, I'm not so sure this Titans team has a pulse. Maybe going away from Christian Parker can actually help a little. Even if it won't, the Cowboys should be able to score enough to cover. PICK: Cowboys -7.5

ARIZONA CARDINALS at KANSAS CITY CHIEFS (-16.5): Josh Johnson can do enough to make this game competitive for the Cards … albeit in a double-digit loss. PICK: Cardinals +16.5

TAMPA BAY BUCCANEERS at DETROIT LIONS (-4.5): The Lions have found their groove. But this is a tough spot for Detroit, now without Aidan Hutchinson, as they return to the United States after playing in Germany … while the Bucs are coming off their bye. Tampa's run defense is up to the challenge of slowing down Jahmyr Gibbs. The Bucs will make this game tough. PICK: Buccaneers +4.5

PITTSBURGH STEELERS (-3.5) at PHILADELPHIA EAGLES: Interesting to see that this line moved in Philly's favor with the Steelers originally being listed as 4.5-point road favorites. I refuse to pick the Eagles to win given how incompetent they've looked. But Aaron Rodgers has been struggling and DeVonta Smith is back for Joe Milton to work with. Saquon Barkley is healthier. I don't know, maybe the Birds mess around and make it a close loss? PICK: Eagles +3.5

NEW YORK JETS at LOS ANGELES CHARGERS (-3): These teams stink. I guess I believe in the Chargers' upside more than the Jets' upside? PICK: Chargers -3

LAS VEGAS RAIDERS at DENVER BRONCOS (-10.5): The Broncos are worse than their record indicates. Fernando Mendoza is going to give Denver a scare. PICK: Raiders +10.5

JACKSONVILLE JAGUARS (-5.5) at NEW YORK GIANTS: There's really no reason to bet against this Jags team that's been rolling. The Giants have done some nice things this year but they've yet to prove they belong with the big dogs. PICK: Jaguars -5.5

MINNESOTA VIKINGS at SAN FRANCISCO 49ERS (-3.5): If there's someone who could give the 49ers' offense problems, perhaps it's Brian Flores. PICK: Vikings +3.5

CINCINNATI BENGALS (-5.5) at WASHINGTON COMMANDERS: It's impossible to bet against Joe Burrow right now. Besides, the Commanders winning in Week 10 was more about their opponent losing. PICK: Bengals -5.5
