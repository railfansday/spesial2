Chiefs-Bills Week 12 Monday injury report: Ken Walker remains sidelined

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Each practice day this season, the Kansas City Chiefs release an official injury report ahead of the next game. In Week 12, the Chiefs play the Buffalo Bills on Thanksgiving night.

Here is the Chiefs' first official injury report of the week, courtesy of the team's official website.

Editor's Note: The practice designations are estimations due to the short week.

Running back Kenneth Walker (groin) did not participate in practice again after missing the last two games. His status on Tuesday and Wednesday will be worth keeping a close eye on.

Starting right tackle Jaylon Moore (ankle) did not participate in practice and hasn't since leaving the Week 9 game against the New York Jets in the third quarter with an ankle injury.

Right guard Trey Smith (concussion) was a full participant in practice; he seems to be on the mend after entering concussion protocol during the first quarter of the Week 10 game against the Atlanta Falcons.

Second-year defensive tackle Omarr Norman-Lott (knee) was a limited participant in practice again. He was activated from the Reserve/Physically-Unable-to-Perform list last week, but was scratched on gameday.

Tight end Noah Gray (rib) did not practice on Monday after leaving the game against the Arizona Cardinals late with the injury.

Fullback Ben VanSumeren (knee) did not practice on Monday after leaving Sunday's game late and being ruled out from returning.
