Should you start QB Kirk Cousins in fantasy football in Week 10?

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Vikings offense is loaded in every way, at every position. Of course, with a high-powered offense like the Vikings', they come with some notable fantasy football assets.

During the 2026 season here on Vikings Wire, we will review all fantasy-relevant skill players for the team and share whether they are worth a start.

Each position has players that can help or hurt your fantasy team, so this will be a form of start or sit. In this piece, we will be looking at quarterback Kirk Cousins.

The returning Cousins is expected to bring a boost to the Vikings' offense overall at a time when they desperately need it. He showed in Week 9 that he can be a liability with his turnovers, but he still can push the ball, be accurate, and that's more than Kyler Murray and Carson Wentz have been.
