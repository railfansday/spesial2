Thanksgiving is the perfect time for family, food and a little college basketball. The 2026 Emerald Coast Classic will bring hoops down to Florida before the celebrations begin.

The Emerald Coast Classic is annual D1 women's college basketball tournament, featuring four programs every year in Niceville, Florida. The event was founded in 2014, and has typically run with an eight-team format, though this year's competition will only include four.

Michigan State, Virginia Tech, UT-Martin and Alcorn State are all set to compete in this week's event, which will take place on Monday and Tuesday. After all is said and done, only one program will be named an Emerald Coast Champion.

Here's everything you need to know about the Emerald Coast Classic 2026, including TV channel and streaming options for the women's college basketball tournament.

The 2026 Emerald Coast Classic will not be broadcast on national television. Instead, fans can stream each game of the tournament on FloCollege.
