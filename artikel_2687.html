The Packers’ bye week will have a unique structure

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Green Bay Packers will be the first in NFL history to do something. Not only will the team be part of the initial duo to debut the league's new Thanksgiving Eve window, but they also have to figure out how to do it coming off a bye week.

Generally, a bye week is a vacation for players. They're given the week off, allowed to travel to wherever they please during that period.

For the Packers, though, they're also getting ready for a game that will kick off on next Wednesday, meaning that they don't have a full week to give their players.

According to Green Bay head coach Matt LaFleur, the solution the team found — after discussing it with management, the team's players and the NFL Players Association — was to give the players three days off this week, leading up to their matchup against the Los Angeles Rams, and then four days off after the team plays Los Angeles.

The Packers' players were in the building today, so that the team could "flush" the loss to the Minnesota Vikings, according to LaFleur. They'll have the next three days off, returning on Friday for preparation against the Rams.

Green Bay will travel on Tuesday, meaning that we can expect the Packers' practices this week to be on Saturday, Sunday and Monday. On usual bye weeks, players would be returning to the facility on Tuesday — after the Packers would have concluded their three practices for the Rams game — so you can see how this Sunday game-bye week-Wednesday game schedule presented issues for just about everyone involved.

In the media, we've already talked about this period as consecutive "mini-byes" for the Packers going into and coming out of the game against Los Angeles. Now we know that Green Bay's practice schedule and days off will also reflect that.
