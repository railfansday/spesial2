President Donald Trump Ranks His Cabinet in Terms of Hotness

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

President Donald Trump was asked to rank the most attractive members of his current administration after he mentioned men’s asses on the campaign trail multiple times.

“Who is the best-looking member of your administration?” podcast host Miranda Devine asked on the Monday, November 2, episode of the “Pod Force One” podcast before listing Vice President JD Vance, Secretary of State Marco Rubio and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent. “Which one of those three? Or is it somebody else? Pete Hegseth?”

After letting out a little chuckle, Trump, 80, said that Devine, 65, was possibly going to get him in trouble with his response.

“Pete’s a good-looking guy,” he shared. “You’re going to get me in big trouble when you ask me those questions. Why do you do that? I think they’re all great looking.”

Trump continued, “I’ll tell you what, Marco’s really a great hot politician in the sense that he’s done great. JD, the same thing. They’re all doing great. Scott Bessent is central casting, right? If I was going to do a movie on the Treasury, I’d pick him for the lead. They’re all good.”

The podcast episode’s release comes less than 24 hours before millions of Americans will cast their vote on Election Day.

Depending on the results in key states, the Democrats could take back control of both the Senate and the House of Representatives as Trump continues his second term as commander in chief.

In the weeks leading up to Decision Day, Trump made headlines for his comments about Ohio Sen. Jon Husted’s physique.

At an October 12 rally in Youngstown, Ohio, Trump said the former football coach and All-American defensive back at the University of Dayton had a “great ass.”

“I watched up and down,” Trump shared. “I looked at his ass, and I looked at the whole — and then I looked at his hands.”

In a separate rally on October 25, the commander in chief said, “I looked at his ass, and I’ve never done that with a man before. It’s true. Off the record: Women, yes, men, no, OK?” he said. “By the way, I looked at one hell of a beautiful ass before, Nicki Minaj. … She’s been with us for a long time.”

Trump later expressed his belief that his comments about Husted’s butt helped him climb in the polls.

When campaigning for Rep. Mike Rogers, who is running for Senator in Michigan, Trump said, “I’d say that about your ass too, Mike, if it gets you a guaranteed victory.”

Trump’s comments about asses have grabbed the attention of late-night hosts and the ladies of The View.

As cohost Ana Navarro shared on the October 26 episode of the ABC talk show, “I can’t believe that Trump’s closing message on an election that is so close in so many places is about asses.”
