DP World Tour Championship: Pairings, tee times for final day

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Tyrrell Hatton plays alongside Victor Perez on the final day in Dubai; Rory McIlroy and Eugenio Chacarra are both four off the lead as they battle for the Race to Dubai; watch the final day live on Sunday from 6.30am on Sky Sports Golf

Pairings and tee times for the final round of DP World Tour Championship, held on the Earth Course at Jumeirah Golf Estates, Dubai.

Tyrrell Hatton takes a one-shot lead over Victor Perez after a third-round 68, with the Englishman on nine under after outscoring playing partner Rory McIlroy on Saturday.

McIlroy is looking to win the event for a fourth time and also move above Eugenio Chacarra in the Race to Dubai standings, which would see him match Colin Montgomerie's record with an eighth Order of Merit title.

Both McIlroy and Chacarra head into the final day four strokes back, with the world No 2 needing a solo fourth finish or better to have any chance of overtaking the Spaniard in the season-long standings.
