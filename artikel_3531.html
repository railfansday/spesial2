Alan Ritchson’s ‘Reacher’ Is Officially Prime Video’s Most-Watched Show of 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

2026 has been a benchmark year for Alan Ritchson, who has truly come into his own as one of the biggest action stars in the world right now. Back in March, Ritchson made his Netflix debut in War Machine, the big-budget sci-fi thriller that went on to earn over 135 million views during its first 90 days on streaming. Not only was this enough to pass other movies, such as The Gray Man, to earn a spot as one of the top 10 most-watched Netflix movies ever, but Netflix has also confirmed that a sequel to the film titled War Machines is officially in the works with Ritchson confirmed to return. Later in the year, Ritchson also starred in other films such as Motor City and Runner, which underwhelmed at the box office but have since found success on streaming.

For Ritchson, all of his newfound stardom can be traced back to Prime Video's decision to cast him in the lead role in Reacher, the hit series based on the novels by Lee Child. As the show has continued to pick up more viewers throughout its four season, Ritchson has become more and more popular, which has opened the door for him to star in plenty of other big-budget projects.

Back in August, Prime Video finally released the highly anticipated fourth season of Reacher, but there was little to no doubt that the show was going to be a smash hit. It was renewed for Season 5 months before the Season 4 premiere. After continuing a run of streaming success weeks beyond its Season 4 finale, Alan Ritchson's Reacher has officially become Prime Video's most-watched show of 2026.

Prime Video has yet to announce when Reacher Season 5 will be released, but with production on the new season set to wrap any day now, it's all but confirmed that it will premiere before the end of 2027. The next season will be based on the Jack Reacher novel, Make Me, which is the 20th book in the series. Reacher has also already been picked up for Season 6, but there's no word yet on which book the sixth season will adapt. Even Reacher's flagship spin-off, Neagley, has also been picked up for a second season.

Check out the first four seasons of Reacher on Prime Video and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage of Alan Ritchson's future projects.

When retired Military Police Officer Jack Reacher is arrested for a murder he did not commit, he finds himself in the middle of a deadly conspiracy full of dirty cops, shady businessmen and scheming politicians. With nothing but his wits, he must figure out what is happening in Margrave, Georgia. The first season of Reacher is based on the international bestseller The Killing Floor by Lee Child.
