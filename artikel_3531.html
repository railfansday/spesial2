Taylor Swift Wears 'Toy Story' Yellow at 2026 CMA Awards After 10-Years Away

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Taylor Swift lassoed up a chic red carpet outing — and brought the sunshine with her bold Toy Story-approved gown — at the 2026 CMA Awards.

All eyes were on Swift, 36, as she hit the scene on Wednesday, November 18, solo at Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena, where she was dressed to the nines in a bright yellow strapless gown from the Oscar de la Renta Spring/Summer 2027 Ready to Wear collection.

For glam, the pop star wore her blonde locks twisted up in a chic updo with her wispy bangs framing her face. She rocked sexy, gold high heels that gave a bit of a snake-style vibe and finished her look with an orange-red lip. As one red carpet host said, the singer was “dripping”in diamonds, head-to-toe.

Swift, who last attended the CMAs in 2016 and is set to perform on Wednesday, is nominated for “Single of the Year” alongside Ella Langley, Lainey Wilson and Cody Johnson.

“Looks like I haven’t yee’d my last haw,” Swift wrote via her Instagram Stories at the time, celebrating her nomination. “See you there Nov 18.”

Swift previously won 12 CMAs trophies, hoping to add another for her song “I Knew It, I Knew You.” The singer wrote the track with longtime collaborator Jack Antonoff for the Toy Story 5 soundtrack, inspired by the backstory of cowgirl figurine Jessie (voiced by Joan Cusack).

“For Jessie, I feel like the line, ‘I watched you drive around the bend for what I thought would be the last time I saw my friend,’ that hits me really hard because that movie just broke my heart,” Swift recalled during the film’s 20/20 special in June. “So I’m really happy to, like, give her a happy moment. ”

According to Swift, she was particularly “grateful” to follow in composer Randy Newman’s footsteps. (Newman, 82, created the music for the entire Toy Story franchise. Swift is the only other musician who lent her vocal styling to the animated franchise’s soundtrack.)

“I felt wonderful knowing that I had Randy’s blessing to write for this film, given that I’m the first songwriter to get to partake in the Toy Story world outside of Randy,” Swift added at the time. “I was absolutely dazzled by [the movie]. It is my favorite Toy Story movie.”

“I Knew It, I Knew You” marked Swift’s return to the country music genre.

“I love my country roots more and more as I go further in this path,” she told attendees during a Grammy Museum event in August. “I wanna honor them more and more, the artist who shaped who I am, and that all happened in Nashville.”

Swift famously began her career as a country artist, eventually shifting toward pop music with 2014’s 1989. To date, Swift has released 12 studio albums and rerecorded four of her early albums.
