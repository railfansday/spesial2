TSITP's Chris Briney Addresses [Spoiler]'s Surprise Cameo in Christmas Movie

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Summer I Turned Pretty‘s Christoper Briney broke down new details about the surprise cameo in his new Christmas movie, Clashing Through the Snow.

In the festive film, which is streaming now on Prime Video, Briney’s character, Ryan, ends up at a house party with Paris Hilton. He then runs into New York Knicks star Karl-Anthony Towns and his wife, Jordyn Woods, and teammate OG Anunoby — but doesn’t know who they are.

“Dude, those producers. They just told me [before] it happened,” Briney, 28, exclusively told Us.

The actor, who is known for attending Knicks games, clarified he didn’t make the call, adding, “No, I wish I made it happen. But no, that was producers. They did it. It was cool and I appreciate them all showing up. It was really sweet.”

Director Carlson Young also spoke to Us about the surprise appearances.

“Was it Chris? Was it a producer [who set it up]? It was probably both, but it was amazing,” she shared. “We have this really funny picture of all of us on set, and I am literally this tall and they are to the roof. It was so cool to have them. We’re really lucky.”

Clashing Through the Snow follows two rival book editors who must share a rental car to New York City to land a career-saving author deal after becoming stranded in a snowstorm. Michelle Randolph plays Ryan’s work nemesis, with Lukas Gage, Julia Fox, PJ Byrne and Andrew Schulz also starring.

“When I read the script, I immediately just fell in love with it,” Randolph, 29, gushed to Us. “I thought it was funny. I think there’s such value in telling stories that are just meant to bring people joy and don’t ask anything of the audience. And I love rom-coms and I love the holidays.”

Briney, meanwhile, took on the holiday movie after starring on another Prime Video project, The Summer I Turned Pretty. While speaking to Us, Briney teased what could come next for him — and revealed whether he would want to join Randolph on her show, Landman.

“What they’re doing is really great, and I know that they’re still going. I’d audition, [for sure],” he shared with Us. “I’d love to be a f***ing cowboy, dude.”

He added: “With an accent? That’d be fun. I could work with a dialect coach.”

After Randolph found out about Briney’s answer, she told Us, “He’s ready? Oh, my God! Let me call Taylor. Christopher Briney is going to be a cowboy!”

Clashing Through the Snow is streaming now on Prime Video.
