Penélope Cruz Arrives in a Red-Hot Ralph Lauren Dress for ELLE’s Women in Hollywood Event

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Penélope Cruz knows how to turn heads with a red carpet look, and for ELLE’s 2026 Women in Hollywood event, the honoree opted for a breathtaking red Ralph Lauren gown. The La Bola Negra actress paired the flowing red number with gold earrings.

She also posed with her The Invite co-star Seth Rogen:

Cruz has been acting for decades and expects to finish her directorial debut next year, a documentary on a subject she hasn’t revealed yet. While becoming a female director is a milestone, “it’s sad that it still has to be a conversation,” she told ELLE in her Women in Hollywood cover story. “[But] we cannot ignore it and look the other way and pretend like there is any kind of equality in those terms.”

One of the worst parts of being a woman in Hollywood, she said, is “the fact that when you have a writer-director who is a woman, it is commented on in the interview or is talked about as something extraordinary, something that is not just normal. You don’t say that about women being in other departments in film, but you get asked about it when it’s about your director or writer-director. So that is unfortunate, that it’s evolving at such a slow pace.”

As AI continues to change many industries, including the film business, Cruz candidly reflected on its development. “I’m sure there are benefits, for example, for medicine, for finding ways and tools to protect our planet [amid] climate change,” she said. “For things like that, it can be incredible. But the problem is that at the speed it’s going, there’s not enough time to regulate things so it doesn’t become something that can become a real threat.

“That’s where it gets so tricky: Who’s creating that balance? Who’s creating a boundary and the limits of what we all can handle? They try to sell the benefits of it, saying, ‘People will have all this free time on their hands.’ That always freaks me out, because I’ve been happy in my life because I’ve had purpose. Imagine if young people a few years from now won’t have to get prepared for something, or dream about something and work hard to achieve their dreams, because they would be treated like robots in a cycle of: You wake up, you eat. But what do you do with your time? What is your purpose? What do you have to invest your time and your passion into?”
