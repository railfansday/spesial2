An unidentified EU entity is suspected of losing more than $106 million from multiple CEX accounts.

Blockchain investigator ZachXBT reports that an unidentified European Union (EU) entity may have lost more than $106 million in cryptocurrency after an attacker withdrew funds from multiple centralized exchange accounts.

The suspected attacker converted much of the stolen stablecoins into Ether and Monero.

In a Telegram post on November 2, ZachXBT flagged the suspected theft, reporting that the attacker withdrew funds primarily in USD Coin (USDC) and Tether (USDT) from multiple centralized exchange accounts.

The victim has not been identified, and no affected exchange has publicly confirmed the incident. The term EU entity could refer to a company, trading firm, or investment fund. ZachXBT also did not disclose how the attacker gained access to the accounts.

ZachXBT published 11 Ethereum wallet addresses linked to the suspected theft to help track the movement of funds.

On-chain transactions show the attacker used wallet address 0x9EcA to buy about 27,553 ETH, worth about $62.97 million, at an average price of $2,286.

The attacker then moved portions of the funds through several instant exchange and cross chain services, including WellWallet, Kyrrex, Wagyu, SwftSwap, and Rango.
