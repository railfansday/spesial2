On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 16, 2026, KE Holdings (BEKE -0.38%) announced that non-GAAP net income, a profit metric excluding non-operating items, doubled year over year. The housing platform expanded profit margins by optimizing operating efficiency rather than expanding its physical network.

Existing home transaction volume per agent increased while total store counts remained flat. Management is focusing on individual workflow efficiency rather than adding retail locations.

In new home services, third-party sales support is shifting from simple traffic routing to targeted buyer matching. Using historical transaction data allows the company to capture broader developer marketing budgets.

Lower personnel costs and store rent savings expanded contribution margins, the revenue remaining after direct expenses, across primary segments. Combined with net-basis product shifts in rental management, profit growth outpaced revenue.

Housing market conditions are stabilizing across core cities as lower interest rates ease ownership costs. Operational focus centers on service efficiency and phased resource allocation.

Integrated solutions across transactions, home renovation, and rental services provide a structural advantage. Strategy prioritizes pricing discipline and third-party developer service adoption.
