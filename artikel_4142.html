It's officially the spookiest day of the year, but do you know what you're playing this Halloween weekend?

October has been a pretty wicked month for gaming. And so, as we play out its final hours, we'd like to know what it is you're having a crack at. This is the space to have a chat with your fellow PXers and let us know what you're up to. Happy Halloween, everyone! Or, as I'm a Manxie, I should be saying Hop tu Naa! 🎃

My plans for this weekend have changed a smidge. Originally, my goal was to crack on with the phenomenal Silver Pines, as well as aim for its Mythic cheevo. Toss in a bit of Gears of War: E-Day's Halloween event and fully revel in the spooks.

Instead, I am now getting properly stuck into Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 in order to bring you a lovely little review of the sucker. The whole N/A for Xbox on Metacritic got me all bothered, and so I've decided to get Pure Xbox in the review mixer.

I'd expect it won't take me all weekend to get through the Campaign, play some DMZ and vibe with Multiplayer, though. So, who knows? Maybe I will get to play something spooky for Hop tu Naa!

I've been really, really tempted to pick up the new Evercade Nexus alongside the Banjo-Kazooie and Banjo-Tooie Double Pack, but I've held off for now. Maybe I'll just play on my Evercade Alpha this weekend instead.

On Xbox, I've still got tons of Star Wars: Galactic Racer I want to play, while the new release of Tape to Tape is definitely on the list as well. I've been waiting so long for that game to arrive on Xbox, and now it's finally here!

I’m playing the appropriately spooky Seance of Blake Manor on Game Pass.

It’s set in 1897. You play a detective summoned from Dublin to investigate a disappearance at a remote hotel in the west of Ireland, where a group of guests have arrived to participate in a seance. You’ve got to speak to them and also snoop around to find clues as to what’s happening. All the guests and staff also have their own backstories as to why they are there.

It’s great fun once you get to grips with it. The best detective game I’ve played since the Golden Idols and probably better than them.
