Sources reveal Everton's January signing plan as David Moyes' future takes new twist

Everton's potential transfer plans have been widely debated by a frustrated fanbase ahead of the January window.

The Friedkin Group's decision to put the Toffees up for sale has thrown a spanner in the works, though the owners are confident of an exit.

As soon as the summer, there could be new owners at the Hill Dickinson Stadium, which would spark yet another new era on Merseyside.

For the most part, fans are still huge supporters of TFG due to what they've achieved since coming in, but others aren't so impressed by their recent actions.

The summer transfer window was a tough one to take, and the backtrack on Harrison Armstrong's exit likely saved a riot amongst the fans.

And despite David Moyes playing with a weakened squad, the Toffees continue to defy expectations, sitting seventh in the Premier League right now.

Due to TFG's situation, those at the Hill Dickinson Stadium have been left with two major questions this season.

Will Everton sign anyone in the January window? And what does this mean for Moyes' future as the manager?

Well, according to iNews (5 November), the owners are willing to back the manager in January, with the addition of a striker, on loan or permanently, seen as a priority.

That will be music to the fans' ears, and so will be the source's reveal that figures inside the club want Moyes to sign a contract extension soon.

One source said: "He wants to stay beyond the summer but he wants to have a proper go at it," and that is all the supporters need to hear.

There is absolutely no doubt that a new striker is needed. Thierno Barry is shining, but he can't play every single minute of every single game.

Football Insider understands that Minnesota United's Kelvin Yeboah is of serious interest, but there are fears of an increased price tag.

Clubs are likely to take advantage of Everton's desperation, especially in January, which could see Moyes and TFG back in the same situation.

After a failed move in the summer, it is understood that a return for Folarin Balogun's signature is unlikely. Maybe Yeboah is the answer.
