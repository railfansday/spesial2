Sources: Graham Alexander to terminate multiple Preston deals as new signings lined up

Preston North End manager Graham Alexander is set to cancel the deals of all players currently in on loan, sources have told Football Insider.

Every player on loan at Deepdale will be sent back to their parent clubs in the January transfer window to make room for his own potential signings in the winter.

Alexander has inherited a below-par squad since taking over from Paul Heckingbottom in September, with the team currently sat at the bottom of the Championship table.

The boss is set to receive the backing of the board to turn around their dismal start to the season, and is prepared to take drastic measures when the window opens.

Preston currently have four players in on loan: Caleb Wiley, Delano Burgzorg, Jusef Erabi and Liam Gibbs, on loan from Chelsea, Middlesbrough, Genk and Norwich respectively.

All of them are now set to be sent back to their parent clubs, who will make decisions about their futures, while Alexander targets players who are better suited to his style.

Alexander has been forced to take sudden measures in an attempt to turn around Preston's form this season, with growing concerns about their future in the division.

The Lilywhites have conceded the second-most goals in the Championship this season, and have scored the joint-fewest, raising alarm bells about the quality of their squad.

While the current crop of loanees cannot be to blame for their poor start, they were brought in for the previous manager, and have not lived up to expectations so far.

As a result, they will be sent back to their parent clubs, leaving space and funds for Alexander to target new additions, both on loan and permanently in January.

The boss is hopeful that he will be backed in the mid-season window, with new additions required if he is to turn around Preston's form and save them from relegation.

Despite Alexander putting preparations in place to cancel Preston's current loan deals, he has not ruled out adding short-term players to his squad in the January window.

The loan market could be a valuable asset for the Championship's bottom club, who could utilise their relationship with Premier League clubs to land some talented players.

Former Everton scout Bryan King told Football Insider that both Preston and the Toffees could utilise their relationship to agree a short-term deal for youngster Harvey Foster.

Following Harrison Armstrong's spell at Deepdale, which gave him a platform to return to Everton, there is a belief that Foster could follow in his footsteps.

Preston's struggles, meanwhile, mean Foster could make a key impact on their season if given the opportunity to make the switch in January.
