Apple Music Outage: Company Says Streamer Is Back Online After Brief Disruption

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The outage, which Apple says was caused by an "internal update," is the latest in a recent rash of similar incidents affecting companies and government agencies.

Apple Music and other Apple services were experiencing outages on Tuesday (Nov. 17), Billboard has confirmed, with the company citing an “internal update” as the cause.

“Many of our systems are back up and running after an internal update caused some outages this morning,” said an Apple spokesperson in a statement to Billboard. “We’re working to get the remaining systems online as soon as possible.”

In addition to Apple Music, the App Store, Apple Pay, AppleCare and Apple Business were among the services experiencing outages or other access issues on Tuesday. As of press time, the spokesperson says Apple Music is back online.

The Apple outages are the latest in a rash of similar incidents over the past week. On Thursday (Nov. 12), Microsoft Outlook experienced an outage that lasted nearly 24 hours, causing disruption to business operations across the globe; Microsoft has since attributed the issue to “a latent defect” following a fix deployed for Microsoft 365 networking gateway servers, among other contributing factors. On Friday (Nov. 13), flights were grounded across the U.S. after the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) said it was experiencing technical issues. The FAA later claimed that “legacy equipment became unstable when data began flooding the FAA FTI network,” which “disrupted the system and created instability with aircraft communications and radar feeds.”

So far this week, similar outages have affected companies including JPMorgan Chase, Wells Fargo, Bank of America and United Airlines. JPMorgan Chase has attributed its issues to a software bug while United Airlines blamed a “widespread system outage.” No companies or government agencies have cited cybersecurity incidents.
