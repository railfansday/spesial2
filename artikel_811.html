Louis Carr Out as BET President Less Than One Year After Assuming Role

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Carr, who succeeded Scott Mills last December, oversaw a reboot of the Soul Train Awards during his tenure.

Louis Carr has exited his post as president of BET less than a year after he was elevated to the role, with the executive saying in a letter to staff that he has “decided to chart a new path” as a result of “recent events within our community and our country.”

“For 40 years, I have had one of the best jobs in the media and entertainment industry,” continued Carr, who has worked at BET for four decades. “Having a position that gives one the opportunity to carry out a purpose that is bigger than oneself is a privilege. Working for a brand whose mission from day one was to uplift and empower the Black community is an honor I’ll never forget.”

“BET has never been just a job to me. It has been a community, a culture and a place to serve,” he added. “When I reflect on this journey, I am incredibly proud of what we have accomplished together. Over the years, we helped transform the multicultural advertising landscape, built meaningful relationships with some of the world’s most recognized brands and served as the launching pad for today’s top Black creators. We demonstrated the undeniable value, influence, and purchasing power of Black consumers while helping companies better understand and invest in our communities.”

In his own note sent to staff on Monday (Nov. 16), Skydance co-chair/chief content officer George Cheeks wrote that Carr “has been one of BET’s greatest champions, helping shape the brand, its business and its connection to Black audiences and culture. Louis built lasting partnerships with some of the world’s leading brands and helped cement BET as a cultural force. As President, he led BET with clarity and purpose, creating renewed momentum for the brand. His passion, vision and heart have been front and center in everything he does, and it has been a privilege to partner with him.”

Cheeks added that Carr will remain at BET through the end of the year and that more will be shared about the company’s “leadership and structure” at a later date.

Last December, it was announced that Carr would succeed Scott Mills, who served as BET president/CEO for 23 years. Carr was previously president of media sales. His appointment followed Paramount Global’s $8 billion merger with Skydance Media in August 2025. Paramount had been shopping BET since 2023, before announcing it was no longer for sale ahead of the Skydance merger.

Just last month, Carr spoke with Billboard about the revamped Soul Train Awards, which is set to air on Nov. 27 following a two-year hiatus. The show was taped Nov. 5 in Chicago.

“It’s an iconic brand,” Carr told Billboard at the time, citing R&B’s current popularity as a factor in the reboot. “When we paused it two years ago, consumers in the community spoke and very loudly, asking, ‘Why would we do that?’ So I always knew it was one of the main things on my agenda to bring back because it’s important to our culture, our community and our industry. And I wanted to bring it back to its hometown Chicago, the best place to revitalize [the brand].”

“We find ourselves in a space where there aren’t a lot of people celebrating Black people, Black history and Black entertainment,” Carr added. “And that’s our job.”

That said, even as I write this email, I wrestle with my decision that the time has come to make a transition. Based on recent events within our community and our country, I’ve decided to chart a new path and my last day will be December 31.

BET has never been just a job to me. It has been a community, a culture and a place to serve. When I reflect on this journey, I am incredibly proud of what we have accomplished together. Over the years, we helped transform the multicultural advertising landscape, built meaningful relationships with some of the world’s most recognized brands and served as the launching pad for today’s top Black creators. We demonstrated the undeniable value, influence, and purchasing power of Black consumers while helping companies better understand and invest in our communities.

I am particularly proud of the momentum we have created and the work we have undertaken to strengthen the brand, expand our business and reaffirm BET’s place at the center of Black culture. Stepping into the role of President gave me the opportunity to see just how much the BET brand is loved by our talent, our consumers and the next generation.

We established our brand positioning as “Something You Can Believe In,” reinforcing the trust, purpose, and cultural connection that have always defined BET. We established a trusted Board of Advisors, one that would be envied by any Fortune 500 company, to help inform our direction and ensure that the voices and perspectives of our community remain part of the conversation.

And that was just the beginning. In 2026 alone, we:

The work of this incredible BET team has afforded me the opportunity to earn an abundance of professional accolades and recognition, including five Lifetime Achievement Awards, induction into the American Advertising Federation’s Advertising Hall of Fame, and more than 14 community awards. I have also been privileged to sit on board seats across the industry, including with the International Radio and Television Society (IRTS), the Advertising Council, and the American Advertising Federation (AAF). Additionally, I am proud to be the longest-serving board member of the Video Advertising Bureau (VAB).

Thank you for your partnership, your commitment, and, most importantly, for allowing me to be part of your journey. It has been one of the greatest honors of my life.

I am proud of what we have built and proud of what we have accomplished.
