Jesus Owono Update Emerges After Jesus de Miguel Dies

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Spanish media have now provided an update on Jesus Owono, the goalkeeper who collided with Jesus de Miguel, following the striker's tragic passing on Tuesday morning.

Monday evening's Spanish Segunda Division contest between CD Tenerife and FC Andorra was abandoned in stoppage time after De Miguel appeared to lose consciousness following a collision with Owono, who was shown a straight red card.

The scores were level at 2-2 when the hosts cleared the ball, sending De Miguel on his way towards goal. However, the goalkeeper clattered into the 30-year-old with a horrific challenge, with his knee appearing to make contact high on De Miguel's chest.

De Miguel was rushed to hospital, and after a worrying update at 4am local time revealed that his condition was worsening, he was confirmed dead just four hours later after suffering a cardiac arrest. While FC Andorra's statement paid tribute to the player, further updates have now emerged on how his opponent has been dealing with the situation.

Reports in Spain from AS say that Owono was 'crying and praying' in the dressing room after the match. It's added that he was 'visibly shaken' throughout the journey back to Andorra.

As for his teammates, there was also a continued sense of concern for their opponent. The players closely monitored De Miguel's condition through sporting director Jaume Nogues and a member of the medical staff, who were in direct contact with Tenerife.

The club had previously stated that the player, before making a public statement, intended to contact De Miguel and his family to apologize, offer his support and encouragement, and make himself available for anything they might need.

Of course, the situation for the 25-year-old Equatorial Guinea international and his teammate has changed significantly since, and there has been no official update on what happens next.

Comments were initially limited on his Instagram account. Now his account has been closed following a wave of abusive messages, which included death threats and racial abuse.

Owono has also deactivated his X (Twitter) account following the amount of online abuse he was receiving. Those close to the goalkeeper told AS, as per the Mirror: "He is a caring, very kind person. He has no malice."

The club have reinstated that the collision was unintentional, with Owono not intending to hurt his opponent. Video footage shows the goalkeeper turning to check on the player while others turned away, clearly in distress.
