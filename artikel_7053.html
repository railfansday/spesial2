Video Editor at Winona

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Winona is one of the leading telemedicine companies providing HRT for women in menopause. We’ve built all of our technology in-house including an EHR, patient care admin system, patient portal, website, marketing technology etc. Winona has two compounding pharmacies and a team of in-house physicians providing world-class care via a fully vertically integrated business model.

Winona is looking for a creative Video Editor to produce eye-catching videos that drive engagement across our social media channels. You'll be responsible for editing and producing high-quality content that aligns with our brand and resonates with our target audience across platforms like Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, and LinkedIn.

Edit a variety of video content, including User Generated Content (UGC), explainer videos, and short-form digital content (15s, 30s, and 60s).

Apply consistent branding across all video assets while allowing for creative flexibility.

Manage multiple video projects simultaneously, ensuring timely delivery while meeting project deadlines.

Collaborate effectively with project managers, marketers, and other team members to bring creative visions to life.

Stay up-to-date with the latest video editing trends, best practices, and technologies.

3+ years of professional experience in video editing.

A strong portfolio showcasing your video editing expertise; experience with graphic design projects is a plus.

Expert-level proficiency in Adobe Premiere Pro and Adobe After Effects.

Strong understanding of the post-production process, including offline editing, online editing, 2D motion design, sound design, and color correction/grading.

Excellent interpersonal and communication skills.

Ability to work independently and collaboratively within a team.

Proactive, adaptable, and able to manage multiple projects simultaneously.

Access to a personal computer with a stable internet connection capable of efficiently running the required tools and applications.

Ability to work full-time with flexible hours, with requirement to attend key team meetings as scheduled.

Experience working in a commercial advertising, creative, or digital agency is highly preferred.

Proficiency in Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, Figma, and other creative design tools is a strong advantage.

Ability to create unique designs and visuals from scratch without relying solely on templates.

Familiarity with the women's health industry or related telehealth products/brands.

As a full-time independent contractor, you’ll enjoy:

Paid non-working holidays per country of residence

Pro-rated 13th-month bonus in select regions

Employee engagement activities and virtual gatherings
