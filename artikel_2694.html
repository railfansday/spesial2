Mammoth: Overreaction trade Utah must make amid poor play

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Mammoth: Overreaction trade Utah must make amid poor play appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Utah Mammoth have not had the best run of form as of late. Utah entered play on Wednesday night on a brutal six-game losing streak. They are winning against the Colorado Avalanche at the time of this writing. The win will be nice, but it's clear something isn't clicking in the early going.

The Mammoth have an exciting young team they are building around. At the same time, there are clear weaknesses they can address. Goaltending is one, as their .867 team save percentage is the third-worst in the NHL entering play on Wednesday.

However, there aren't many goaltending options out there for them to pursue. One of the goaltending options they pursued in the summer, rookie Sebastian Cossa, is contributing to the team's goaltending woes.

The trade market does have some impactful players, though. While they may not fix the main issues with this team, they could give them the jolt of energy they need moving forward. If the Mammoth are serious about contending this year, it may be worth taking a risk on the trade market sooner rather than later.

There is one player on the market who makes a ton of sense at this time. This player won't be easy to acquire, as he is making an impact for his team and he is one of the younger stars in the league. But his situation with his current club has made a trade a distinct possibility.

The Philadelphia Flyers are having a horrific season at this time. They made the Stanley Cup Playoffs last year. However, a repeat performance looks quite unlikely in the early going of this year. And it could result in Matvei Michkov leaving the team, as well.

There are conflicting reports on whether Michkov wants a trade. Some indicated that the Flyers star had requested a move away from the City of Brotherly Love. The team itself has denied these rumors.

Still, it's clear that Michkov is unhappy with the team's current situation. He has gone viral for shouting matches on the ice he has had with two teammates this year. His ice time has also fluctuated, though he is being used more in recent games.

This, combined with the drama with head coach Rick Tocchet last year, points to friction within the organization. Michkov is a restricted free agent next summer. More than this, he is offer sheet eligible. Michkov has a lot of say over his NHL future.

The Mammoth should jump on this if Michkov becomes available on the trade market. Utah has a very good forward group, as mentioned. But Michkov would fit in quite well with this team. He has 11 goals and 19 points in 24 games, leading the Flyers in both categories.

What the Mammoth would give up for the 20-year-old is up for debate. He certainly wouldn't come cheap. Michkov is producing at nearly a point per game and is due for a contract extension. There is a lot of value and team control there that Utah will need to factor into the trade cost.

This also doesn't mention how reluctant Philadelphia would be to do it this season. They don't need to trade Michkov at all if they don't want to, at least during the season. If a trade is best for both sides, then they can figure that out in the summer. No matter when this comes to a head, the Mammoth are certainly an ideal landing spot.
