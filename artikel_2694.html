WR Ryan Flournoy continues showing up in clutch situations

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Dallas Cowboys continue to disappoint at just about every turn. They've underperformed in all three phases of the game this season and show no signs of coming anywhere close to the team many believed could be Super Bowl contenders. Their latest loss to the San Francisco 49ers was yet another example of how far this team has fallen.

With everything that is happening in the Cowboys 2026 NFL season, it's really difficult to appreciate some of the good things that have taken place thus far this year. But, that's exactly what we want to do today by shining the spotlight on wide receiver Ryan Flournoy, who quietly having a career year.

Now in Year 3 with the Cowboys, the former 2024 sixth-round pick has gone from someone trying to stick around on the roster to a reliable weapon in one of the most talented offensive units in the entire league. No. 19 is currently having himself a career year and has been absolutely clutching important situations here recently.

To date, Flournoy has caught 38 passes for 466 receiving yards and three touchdowns. That already just about equals his 2025 totals of 40 receptions for 475 yards and four touchdowns. In just over the halfway point of the 2026 season, there's no telling what his statistics will look like at the end of the season if he continues to produce.

Flournoy's success is a testament to his work ethic. He's put in the work both on and off the field and earned the trust of his QB, Dak Prescott. It's obvious Prescott trusts him in any given situation, even if the game is on the line, as evidenced by some clutch plays over the past couple of weeks.

Two weeks ago against the Indianapolis Colts, he made arguably the most impactful play of the game. With 97 seconds left on the clock he made a one-handed catch with All-Pro CB Sauce Gardner draped all over him. That reception was pivotal in the Cowboys regaining the lead and ultimately winning the game.

Although the 49ers ultimately won, Flournoy once again made a couple of clutch plays to keep the Cowboys in the game last week. He ended the game with six catches for 71 yards and a touchdown, but made two key plays. First a big catch in the third quarter down the sideline to move the ball in the 49ers territory and later with his TD catch in the fourth.

With players like CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens ahead of him on the depth chart, Flournoy is never going to be the go-to WR for the Cowboys. That seems to be okay though because he's thriving as Dallas' WR3 this year and most importantly is answering every time his number is called.

It's a little too early to start thinking about what his future may hold with the Cowboys next season, but it's looking bright nonetheless. They were wise to lock him up for another year, especially with George Pickens future with the organization up in the air.

It's going to be really interesting to see how the remainder of the season plays out for Ryan Flournoy. If he continues to produce like he has recently he should finish the year with 60-70 receptions for around 800 receiving yards and a half a dozen touchdowns. Not bad numbers for WR who's third on the Cowboys depth chart.
