No winter shopping? Bayern Bosses reveal their stance after injury setbacks

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Bayern Munich are not planning to enter the January transfer market despite their recent injury problems, according to club president Herbert Hainer.

Speaking at Saturday's Annual General Meeting, Hainer admitted that the current situation was not anticipated but insisted that the club does not believe immediate reinforcements are necessary.

Luis Díaz is expected to return soon, while Alphonso Davies could be back to full fitness within two weeks. Serge Gnabry is also gradually returning to training.

"Therefore, I don't think we need to react in the winter transfer window," Hainer explained.

However, the Bayern president acknowledged that circumstances can change quickly, particularly after the club suffered two further injury setbacks, Harry Kane and Michael OIise are both unavailable for the time being, ahead of the AGM.

Hainer made it clear that Bayern would be financially capable of reacting if necessary, but stressed that this was not their preferred approach.

The club's leadership also remains cautious about January business, with Hainer pointing out that Bayern have not enjoyed much success with signings made during the winter window or in the final days of transfer periods.

For now, the Bundesliga leaders are hoping their injured players can return soon enough to avoid the need for additional reinforcements.
