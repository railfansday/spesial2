Arizona volleyball loses to No. 10 BYU in 3 competitive sets

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Competing. It's what Arizona volleyball head coach Rita Stubbs wants from her squad over their final few matches of the season.

"I feel like I've said that since August," Stubbs said before the team headed to Provo to play No. 10 BYU. "I do know that you the BYU gym is a pretty active gym…so my hope is that we are able to go in and compete in a hostile environment."

The Wildcats absolutely competed against the Cougars, but they came away with the 3-0 (25-21, 25-20, 25-21) loss.

Arizona started strong with 17 kills on .342 hitting in the opening set, but the Wildcats couldn't replicate that in the next two. Despite seeing both their kills and efficiency go down as the match wore on, they didn't go away.

Arizona had late leads in the first and second sets. It was within two points of BYU at 23-21 in the third. It just couldn't overcome the hurdle of a top 10 Cougars team.

BYU got two players into double-digit kills with Elli Mortensen ending with a match-high 14 and Claire Little Chambers finishing with 11. Arizona could only get Destiny Ndam-Simpson above that threshold with 12 kills.

Ndam-Simpson led Arizona in points with 14.5. She added one ace and a team-high three total blocks. She started the match extremely efficient on the attack with a hitting percentage over .400 in the opening set. That began to take a hit later in the match and she ended at .171.

In addition to competing, Stubbs also wants to see her young players improve with the time they have left on the court.

"Part of what happens is when you don't get a chance to go through the grind and understand what it takes to win," Stubbs said. "If you just say it, it's way different than being in a position to understand it because you've done it. You know you can. I can say things to them until I'm blue in the face, but if they never experience those situations, it's pretty hard to understand what actually can and will and needs to take place. So that part has been good for us. A lot of reps."

One of the players who continues to show that is middle blocker Marina Vosloo. The freshman led the team with a .500 hitting percentage. She had six kills on 12 swings without an error. She also had three digs and tied Ndam-Simpson with three total blocks to lead the team. Vosloo's 7.5 points were second on the team behind the senior outside hitter.

Like Ndam-Simpson, Kennedy Osunsanmi started very efficiently with a first-set hitting percentage over .400. Also like her teammate, that started to drop as the match wore on. Osunsanmi ended tied with Vosloo for third on the team with six kills, but she did it on .100 hitting.

Carlie Cisneros was second on the team with seven kills. She also had 10 digs.

Arizona heads to Salt Lake City to play Utah on Saturday at 3 p.m. MST. The Utes are 15-11 overall and 7-8 in the Big 12, but they dropped a home match to Cincinnati on Thursday evening. They will be looking to regroup when the 7-19 Wildcats come to town.
