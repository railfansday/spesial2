Stefan Borson expects Man United takeover - Glazers and INEOS exits are 'inevitable'

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Manchester United's change in ownership looks "inevitable" as supporters continue to rip into the Glazers and INEOS.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that Man United could be one of the Premier League clubs currently up for sale.

A new study found that eight Premier League clubs are for sale, either officially or unofficially, though it is unclear who they might be.

The Red Devils' owners are often in the spotlight, and always for the wrong reasons, especially after the latest summer transfer window at Old Trafford.

Michael Carrick's sacking and replacement by Mauricio Pochettino has been yet more evidence of failure from the current ownership.

For years, supporters have been calling for change, and that might not be too far around the corner anymore, or so it is believed.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson has tipped Man United for a change of owners in the not-too-distant future.

"There are a few clubs where change looks inevitable, just because something's got to give," he explained.

"Manchester United are probably one of those teams where I don't know how long it can go on for with the Glazers and INEOS if they're underperforming.

"And if they're not providing sufficient cash into the club to get them back up the league. So I don't know whether they're in or out of the list.

"But I think some of the clubs that are on the list are not going to get deals."

Man United are sitting 18th in the Premier League table, inside the relegation zone after the first 10 games of the 2026-27 campaign.

The supporters are quite rightly furious with the current situation. Man United are not a club that should be worried about relegation.

Pochettino won't be taking training until USA's two upcoming games against Haiti, a situation that has amused Borson, to say the least.

The appointment has been greeted with a mixed reaction, and only time will tell if Pochettino can make do with what he's got at Old Trafford.
