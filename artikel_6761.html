Kim Kardashian and Jennifer Lawrence Get Their Slumber Party Dressing On

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Kim Kardashian and Jennifer Lawrence might occupy opposite ends of the celebrity style spectrum, but it turns out they have more in common than a shared appreciation for archival fashion and an Hermès handbag. That is, a penchant for a good old-fashioned slumber party.

The longtime friends stepped out in New York City for a girls’ night out following Kardashian’s appearance on Late Night With Seth Meyers to promote the second season of All’s Fair, catching a late dinner and drinks at CUT. For the occasion, both women stayed true to their respective sartorial sensibilities. Kardashian, who rarely misses an opportunity to dip into the fashion archives, wore a sculptural black crocodile jacket from Jean Paul Gaultier’s fall 2004 collection for Hermès, sourced from Arch Couture. The standout jacket features a plunging zippered neckline, dramatic flared peplum, and tendril-like detailing at the cuffs. She paired it with sheer black tights, pointed pumps, and an oversized chocolate-brown leather east-west bag.

Jennifer Lawrence, meanwhile, brought her own particular brand of off-duty cool to the GNO. The actor and now-beauty founder wore a slouchy, cream-colored coat from The Row’s pre-fall 2018 collection, layered over a vintage ’70s Morris the Cat T-shirt and relaxed trousers. She added a shaggy, icy-blue scarf added a flash of color, and wore some white-and-brown mink fur flip-flops by Valentino—an irreverent footwear choice on a November evening in NYC. She carried a black patent Dior Malice bag from the John Galliano era; an archival flourish Kim Kardashian would definitely have appreciated. While their approaches to getting dressed might seem worlds apart—Kardashian’s high-octane glamour versus Lawrence’s studied nonchalance—the two share a fondness for rare archival finds and a litany of Hermès bags.

But the evening’s most unexpected fashion moment was still to come. Later, the pair were photographed at the Four Seasons hotel having traded their going-out clothes for matching chocolate-brown velour Skims robes, festive floral and deer print waffle pajama sets, and oversized sunglasses. A slumber party? Some conveniently timed holiday content? Why not both? Between two actors, mothers, and business founders, there was surely plenty to catch up on.

Of course, Kardashian is the founder of the shapewear and loungewear behemoth Skims, which recently unveiled its Christmas campaign. But pajama dressing is hardly unfamiliar territory for Lawrence, whose relaxed street style has increasingly blurred the boundaries between getting dressed and staying in. Just last week, she stepped out in New York wearing a floor-sweeping chartreuse velvet housecoat by Colleen Allen, paired with fuzzy slippers from The Row and a lime-green Miu Miu cardigan.

Whatever the occasion, it seems these two have found some common ground: the best part of a night out is getting into your pajamas afterward.
