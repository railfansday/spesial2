Rex Ryan brutally roasts Cowboys after awful performance: ‘Worst-coached defense I’ve ever seen’

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Rex Ryan crushed the Cowboys' defense in a rant on ESPN New York Monday morning, with the former Jets coach claiming that he could turn Dallas' unit around in one week.

The bravado-filled statement came a day after the Cowboys lost to the 49ers despite scoring 40 points, as the defense allowed nearly 600 yards for a 46-40 loss in another disappointing day for the franchise.

San Francisco 49ers running back Kaelon Black (26) rushes past Dallas Cowboys cornerback Cobie Durant (2) on Nov. 15 in Arlington, Texas. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

It also came less than a week after Ryan was arrested for an alleged incident in which he ran over two people's feet on a Nashville street and fled the scene in his pickup truck.

Ryan resumed his regularly scheduled media appearances over the weekend despite the legal issues and had no problem criticizing the Cowboys on Monday on the radio.

"It's the worst-coached defense I've ever seen," Ryan said of the Cowboys unit led by coordinator Christian Parker. "It really is. And I've seen some horrible defenses, this one's awful, and it starts with coaching. … They make more mental mistakes than any defense I've seen, as well as the physical, they're not a tough physical defense."

Ryan was credited with having some of the best defenses in the NFL during his run with the Jets, especially in his first two years, when he coached the Jets to back-to-back appearances in the AFC title game.

Former NFL coach Rex Ryan called the Cowboys' unit the worst-coached defense he has ever seen following Dallas' loss to the 49ers. Penske Media via Getty Images

Ryan has also never been shy about the confidence he has in himself, leading to a bold statement while speaking with Rick DiPietro and Dave Rothenberg on his weekly spot on their show.

"I could go there today and say, 'All right, we can have an average ass defense.' I'll promise you they'd be average, I'd probably make them top 10," Ryan boasted. "But I'll take that group right now, in one week, I'd go in and turn this thing out. Instead, they're going to play this and have no freaking clue. They gave up 584 yards in this game [against the 49ers]. We got issues with the Jets, we do, but are you kidding me?

Former NFL coach Rex Ryan appears in a booking photo released Nov. 11 after his arrest on six misdemeanor charges in Nashville. Metro Nashville Police Department via AP

"I don't think anybody thought the Jets were going to make the Super Bowl and this team comes in there, you scored 40 points and got beat by six. It's unreal, man."

Ryan appeared on ESPN's "Sunday NFL Countdown" as an analyst just days after his arrest and has not publicly addressed the allegations.

The alleged incident occurred on Nov. 5 when two people claim Ryan hit them with his vehicle as they were crossing the street and had a walk signal.

Ryan is facing six misdemeanor charges and is due back in court for a hearing on Dec. 18.
