Week 11 NFL picks

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Week 11 brings another exciting slate of NFL games, with playoff pictures beginning to take shape across both conferences. While the Los Angeles Rams will be sitting out on their bye week, that doesn't mean their playoff hopes are taking a break. Several games across the NFC could have a direct impact on the Rams' positioning, giving Los Angeles an opportunity to gain ground without stepping onto the field. With plenty of intriguing matchups, potential upsets, and teams fighting to stay alive, Week 11 offers no shortage of opportunities to make bold predictions. Let's take a look at this week's picks and see which teams come out on top.

(Bye: Los Angeles Rams, Seattle Seahawks, Green Bay Packers, Atlanta Falcons, Cleveland Browns, New England Patriots)

The Texans have been one of the biggest disappointments in 2026. It's a shame that they are wasting the talent of Will Anderson Jr. With that being said, I think they manage the Colts with ease.

The Chiefs are gaining steam while the Cardinals have used up all of their "upsets".

The Panthers need a win to try and salvage their place in the NFC South.

The Dolphins give the Bills some competition for two quarters but the Bills will pull away in the second half.

We're going to see what the Detroit Lions look like without Aidan Hutchinson. I think Detroit can beat Tampa without him but we're going to see the cracks in Detroit's armor that will likely show up through the final month and a half of the season.

If Cam Ward can't produce points against the Cowboys defense then they might want to think about taking another quarterback. The Cowboys should get a win just to keep themselves relevant in the NFC East playoff picture.

There's a strong chance that Nick Sirianni and the Eagles make it 8 straight losses.

Ew. I guess whoever wins this game will no longer have a shot at No. 1 pick in the 2027 NFL Draft.

The Jaguars have not slowed down once under Liam Coen. They have maintained a high level of play under Trevor Lawrence and Coen's offense.

Tough draw for Mendoza to get the Seahawks defense in his rookie debut and then the Broncos defense the following week.

Brian Flores meets Kyle Shanahan. We'll see if Shanahan can crack the code on Flores's defense or if the league's best defense can continue to stifle league offenses.

Burrow's Bengals are purring right now and there should not be a reason that they stop on Monday Night.
