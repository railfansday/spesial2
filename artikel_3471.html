Elden Ring: Path Of Shadows Officially Gives Players A Reason To Return To The Lands Between

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

After the recent release of Elden Ring: Tarnished Edition, many new players have entered the Lands Between thanks to the game being available on Nintendo Switch 2 consoles for the first time. Further, the accompanying DLC that was released alongside the Tarnished Edition has given veteran Tarnished new content to be excited about, including two new classes, new weapons and weapon arts, Torrent skins, and more.

Beyond the game itself, there's plenty more for Elden Ring fans to look forward to as well, especially the highly anticipated live-action movie from A24, which is being written and directed by Alex Garland. However, players won't have to wait until the film's 2028 release for another excuse to revisit the Lands Between.

This December, Elden Ring's Path of Shadows is giving players an exciting reason to jump back into the game, specifically in the Realm of Shadow, the Abyssal Woods, and other key areas in the Shadow of the Erdtree DLC.

Elden Ring's Path of Shadows event officially begins on December 1, 2026, and runs through December 28, giving players nearly an entire month to complete a series of community-wide challenges. It's the fourth and final event in Bandai Namco's Community Quests initiative, which launched in September to celebrate the arrival of Elden Ring: Tarnished Edition.

Unlike traditional in-game quests, Path of Shadows encourages players across all platforms to work toward shared objectives, with their combined progress contributing to several massive milestones. It's a particularly interesting initiative from Bandai Namco, especially following the arrival of Tarnished Edition. With an influx of newcomers joining longtime veterans, these challenges offer a fun way to bring the community together without requiring everyone to play cooperatively.

Even better, Path of Shadows gives players a reason to explore areas they might have otherwise forgotten, revisit some of Shadow of the Erdtree's most memorable locations, and collectively celebrate one of FromSoftware's greatest achievements.

Before Path of Shadows begins in December, Elden Ring players still have time to participate in the currently running Path of the Elden Lord, the third installment in Bandai Namco's Community Quests initiative. The event officially began on November 2, with players having until November 28, 2026, to contribute toward four community-wide objectives.

It's also a great excuse to revisit the Lands Between following the release of Elden Ring: Tarnished Edition, which introduced the game to Nintendo Switch 2 players alongside additional content through the new Tarnished Pack DLC.
