Birmingham City urged to seal Blackburn Rovers deal for 'different' 10-goal ace

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Birmingham City have certainly flexed their financial muscles during Knighthead Capital Management's ownership at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park, and similar feats could be in store this new year.

Chris Davies' side have enjoyed a remarkable start to the Championship season, as 12 months on from a period in which many expected the club to mount a serious promotion bid, they are showing the signs of doing exactly that.

A run of six wins in seven games has Birmingham top of the league on goal difference ahead of pre-season promotion favourites, Middlesbrough, as well as Stoke City.

Much has been made of exactly how the West Midlands side find themselves in such a position, and soon enough, attention will turn to how Davies can add further depth and quality to his squad in a bid to see Blues return to the Premier League for the first time in 16 years.

With that in mind, Football League World quizzed our Birmingham City fan pundit, Jason Moore, as to who his 'dream' signing would be in a window notoriously difficult for doing business in.

It is fair to say that not every high-profile transfer in recent times has worked out for Blues, even though the club remains an extremely attractive prospect for potential incomings.

Less was clearly more for Davies this summer as only seven additions came through the door at St. Andrew's, although Moore would like the club to revisit deals for two previous targets, as well as an ambitious move for Blackburn Rovers' 10-goal star, Andri Gudjohnsen.

He told FLW: There are three players I'd love us to get.

"I would love us to go back in for (Adrian) Segecic, because I do think there's a player there, and I think that's what we need.

"We need a proper 'number 10'. I would still like us to explore the (Anis) Mehmeti move because, again, there's a real player there," Moore explained.

"But I think if we were going to go and get someone, I would love something different to Priske, a striker. The one I want at the moment is Gudjohnsen from Blackburn.

"What a player he looks. He's just something totally different to August Priske.

"You can have two really good strikers in this division. I'd be curious to see how much Blackburn want, but it wouldn't be cheap," he added.

"I'd love one of them three. I'm probably leaning more towards Gudjohnsen with the way we're playing at the moment."

Mehmeti and Segecic, of course, were heavily linked with moves to St. Andrew's in the final days of the summer window as Davies sought to add further attacking firepower and Championship experience to his Birmingham squad.

The former would have provided both in equal measure, having been instrumental to Bristol City's 2024/25 play-off finish before winning promotion with Ipswich Town in May.

Anis Mehmeti snubbed Championship moves to try and become established in Ipswich Town's Premier League fold, but hasn't been able to

Mehmeti would have reportedly cost as much as £9m in the summer before the Albanian ultimately opted to snub Blues and Wrexham, which, in hindsight, could have been the wrong call given his diminishing gametime under Gary O'Neil at Portman Road.

Birmingham's newly-appointed director of football, John Murtough, recently revealed the club's strategy ahead of the upcoming window, and whilst it remains to be seen if Gudjohnsen aligns with such visions, a move for the Icelandic forward would be a real statement of intent.

The 24-year-old is one of just three players to have already hit double-figures in this season's Championship, with six of those coming in the Blackburn striker's last five appearances.

Rovers' £2m signing was recently valued at £15m by EFL pundit, Tom Garratt, and given his recent form and potential, such figures aren't entirely unrealistic.

It is also a sum of cash which Birmingham haven't been afraid to pay before, having coughed up at least said fee for the club-record deal which brought Jay Stansfield back to B9 from Fulham during their record-breaking League One season.

Gudjohnsen's poacher's instinct means he certainly would offer different attributes to Priske, who, as well as his goals, brings other attackers into play.

However, the Dane has already missed four games so far this season, with striking availability key when mounting a promotion push, whilst Gudjohsen's sole absence for the East Lancashire side came against Sheffield United on September 8th.
