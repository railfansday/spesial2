Steven Spielberg Officially Takes On a New Genre for Next Movie

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Cillian Murphy and Jessie Buckley are heading to the Wild West in a new film from the legendary director

Steven Spielberg is finally getting his chance to make a Western, and he's bringing some serious star power along for the ride. The legendary filmmaker is developing an adaptation of The Heart in Winter, with Jessie Buckley (Hamnet), Cillian Murphy (Oppenheimer), and Mark Rylance (Bridge of Spies) set to star.

As reported by Deadline, Spielberg will direct the movie for Focus Features, with an adapted screenplay by Paul Thomas Anderson (One Battle After Another). The project is based on Kevin Barry's novel of the same name and is expected to arrive in theaters sometime in 2028.

The story follows two star-crossed fugitives trying to find a place for themselves in the American West during the late 19th century. Spielberg and Anderson will also produce alongside Kristie Macosko Krieger and Sara Murphy.

Spielberg has spoken about wanting to direct a Western for years, and the opportunity to collaborate with Anderson was a major draw. Speaking to Deadline, the director said the story quickly became close to both filmmakers' hearts, making it a project they wanted to tackle together.

The collaboration is particularly notable because Anderson is writing the screenplay without directing the film himself for the first time. The filmmaker recently won an Oscar for Best Adapted Screenplay for One Battle After Another, making him an especially intriguing choice to bring Barry's novel to the screen.

Spielberg also revealed that Buckley and Murphy were his first choices for the two central roles, saying they were the only actors he could imagine playing the characters. The pair have never appeared in a film together before.

Production is expected to begin in the winter of 2027, according to Deadline. Spielberg also made clear that the Western isn't intended to be his final film, joking that if he wanted to ride off into the sunset, he would have made Indiana Jones and the Last Crusade his final movie.
