Forbs not feeling the weight of wearing Portugal’s number seven jersey after Ronaldo

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Carlos Forbs insists he has not felt the weight of the jersey after being the first player to don Portugal's number seven shirt after Cristiano Ronaldo.

Selecao legend Ronaldo retired from international football before the November break after a public falling out with head coach Jorge Jesus.

Ronaldo scored 140 goals and won 234 caps – both of which are world records – for his country, winning Euro 2016, and the Nations League in 2019 and 2025.

Club Brugge winger Forbs is still in the infancy of his international career, with all three of his appearances coming off the bench.

The most latest of those was when he scored the winner in a 3-2 Nations League victory over Wales in Cardiff on Tuesday, his first international goal.

Speaking after the game, Forbs said: "I know it's a number with special significance, but I didn't feel any extra weight wearing it.

"I have to keep working, not just here with the national team, but also with my club to show the coach he can keep calling me."

Portugal did things the hard way to finish top of their Nations League group, taking the lead through Bruno Fernandes but falling behind thanks to goals from David Brooks and Sorba Thomas.

Pedro Neto equalised before the break but was later sent off after Forbs scored the winner.

"It's a special feeling; I won't forget today," Forbs said of his first Portugal goal. "The most important thing is that we managed to win. I'm happy about that.

"We needed the goal to calm the game down, and it just so happened that I scored.

"We showed off our mentality, our character, and the win was. The aim today was to get the three points, finish first in the group, and that is what we did."

Wales coach Craig Bellamy thought the defeat summed up a campaign in which they competed well but placed bottom of the group with just one win and five defeats.

"It really summed up the group for us. There were really good moments for us. We make a mistake, concede, which at this level they are normal things that happen," he BBC Match of the Day Wales.

"That final moment, I have found these teams have that profile to hurt you. At both ends that last bit has just caught us in this group."
