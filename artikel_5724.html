Frontline Treatment in Multiple Myeloma Is Changing

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

— Experts discuss the broader use of four-drug regimens and whether treatment can be shortened

Ahead of the American Society of Hematology (ASH) annual meeting, frontline treatment for multiple myeloma continues to evolve, with quadruplet regimens increasingly becoming standard for both transplant-eligible and -ineligible patients.

MedPage Today brought together three expert leaders in the field: Moderator Joseph Mikhael, MD, is joined by Amrita Krishnan, MD, and Tulio Rodriguez, MD, all of the City of Hope, for a roundtable discussion. This first of five exclusive episodes focuses on the changing frontline treatment landscape, including the expanding role of quadruplet therapy and whether risk status and measurable residual disease (MRD) can help determine how long patients remain on maintenance therapy.

Following is a transcript of their remarks:

My name is Joe Mikhael. I'm a professor at the Translational Genomics Research Institute, which is part of the City of Hope family. And on the subject of the City of Hope family, I have two very dear friends and colleagues who are joining me today and I'd love to have them introduce themselves, Drs. Krishnan and Rodriguez.
