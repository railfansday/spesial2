Nutanix (NTNX -5.16%) reported first-quarter results that exceeded prior guidance while simultaneously announcing a leadership transition. Chief Executive Officer Rajiv Ramaswami plans to step down once a successor is appointed, marking the end of what he described as the company's second strategic chapter. Management indicated that the company is entering its third chapter, focused on establishing the Nutanix Cloud Platform as a foundation for enterprise artificial intelligence and cloud-native workloads. The company maintained its full-year revenue outlook but raised its operating margin and free cash flow targets for fiscal 2027, citing ongoing operational efficiencies and a healthy demand environment.
