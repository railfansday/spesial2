Joe Willock is Exposing Newcastle United Recruitment problem on a weekly basis

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Newcastle United still have issues to address in their attack, and it is being exposed by experienced winger Joe Willock, who remains a starter after all these years. Willock came into this season with uncertainty surrounding his future, entering the final year of his contract and with Sean Steur arriving.

Willock found his name in the transfer pages over the summer, but he stuck around, even after Eddie Howe departed, and it proved to be a good decision. The attacker has already racked up 14 appearances across all competitions and remains a starter under Matthias Jaissle.

Willock started in the attacking midfield role in the draw with Arsenal over the weekend, and he has played a key role in the Magpies' recent revival. Willock has two goals and an assist so far this season, and with eight starts in 11 Premier League appearances, he remains practically undroppable as far as Jaissle is concerned.

Still, the 27-year-old is out of contract in the summer, and while there were some reports of potential talks, it doesn't seem as though an agreement is forthcoming.

Some fans have criticised Willock, and there is a perception that the Magpies should be aiming for better, given their ambitions and the money the club has spent over the years. But the midfielder just keeps finding his way into the starting XI, and that says a lot about his character and work ethic. He has even managed to dispel talk that he was simply a Howe favourite, with Jaissle taking a liking to the former Arsenal man in a similar way.

"He is not normal. His energy and the quality he brings to our midfield is something special." - Matthias Jassile on Willock

But it may also say something about the quality of Newcastle's recruitment, and much has been made of the fact no new signings featured in the front four against Arsenal over the weekend. Newcastle are not hitting at the rate they should be with new recruits, and they can't seem to upgrade on Willock who, while very solid, does not have that something special that a club like Newcastle should have in the number 10 position.

The issue is more pressing, of course, because Willock's contract is up at the end of the season. Newcastle are likely to consider a new deal given how regularly he is playing, and Jaissle has said he 'definitely' wants to keep him, but either way, they need to get their act together with their midfield recruitment ahead of the summer. Willock's seemingly never-ending reign over the middle of the mark epitomises that, although that is not to take away from his efforts or talent level, which have been highlighted by more than one manager.
