Serie A Liveblog: Napoli-Lazio, Inter-Como, Genoa-Milan, Fiorentina-Juventus and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Join us for all the build-up and action as it happens from today’s five Serie A games, as Napoli in crisis host leaders Lazio, Milan visit Genoa, there’s Monza-Atalanta, a massive Inter vs. Como clash, then Juventus away to Fiorentina.

We begin at 11.30 GMT (12.30 CET) at the Stadio Maradona, where Napoli desperately need to turn things around, but are against a Lazio side flying high in top spot.

At 14.00 GMT (15.00 CET), Milan and Atalanta also try to shake off European setbacks with their trips to Genoa and Monza respectively.

All eyes on San Siro at 17.00 GMT (18.00 CET) for a huge Inter vs. Como Scudetto battle to fuel Cesc Fabregas ambition.

Finally, the bitter grudge match between Fiorentina and Juventus at 19.45 GMT (20.45 CET).

Taylor takes the free-kick but it deflects off a Napoli player and out for a corner kick.

It does not take long at all for Cancellieri to get involved, who wins the ball inside the box and fires a rather tame shot on goal. Napoli clear but Lazio win the ball back once again, with Isaksen brought down about 25 yards out from goal.

Cancellieri comes on for Frattesi, who is whistled and jeered by the home crowd as he exits the pitch.

Crunching tackle from Pedraza on Politano, who looks seriously hurt on the floor. Pedraza goes into the book for that one.

Mandas does well to smother a shot from Lucca from range. After a dull ten minutes, the game is starting to liven up a bit again. We’re in for an exciting end to this game, that’s for sure.

Monza: Tornqvist; Kouadio, Goglichidze, Lucchesi; Mout, Akinsanmiro, Touré, Mangas; Colombo, Robinson; Varela

Atalanta: Carnesecchi; Zappacosta, Scalvini, Kolasinac, Bernasconi; Ederson, Pasalic; Zalewski, De Ketelaere, Elmas; Krstovic

Vergara and Hojlund are replaced by Politano and Lucca.

Billy Gilmour makes way for Anguissa as Allegri looks to switch up his midfield.

Play stops after Taylor takes an accidental whack in the face from Beukema. Nothing malicious in it but the Dutchman was down on the floor and the referee stopped play, much to the chagrin of the stadium full of Napoli supporters.

Napoli win another corner and swing it in dangerously but Mandas does well to react to the resulting header.

Lazio launch a counter-attack and Vergara is booked for a cynical challenge to stop the Biancocelesti drive forward.

Pinamonti is replaced by Dele-Bashiru and Floriani Mussolini comes off for Lazzari.

De Bruyne goes one-on-one with Mandas, but has his shot saved, the ball still trickles its way towards the goal line with De Bruyne and Hojlund closing in before Leite gets back in the nick of time to clear it.

Milan bring back Diego Moreira, Christian Pulisic and Luka Modric and hope to revive their fortunes against Daniele De Rossi’s Genoa, who are coming off three straight wins.

Genoa: Bijlow; Marcandalli, Ostigard, Vasquez; Ehizibue, Traore, Wiafe, Ellertsson; Messias, Vitinha; Osmajic

Milan: Maignan; Gila, De Winter, Diawara, Musah; Moreira, Modric, Rabiot, Cisse; Pulisic; Ramos

Napoli playing with renewed urgency since their goal, with a bit of self belief in them now. Lazio look a little shell-shocked and the likes of Leite and Pedraza look far less comfortable on the ball than Sutalo or Tavares.

Oliveira misses a golden chance to give Napoli the lead after the ball lands at his feet in acres of space within the Lazio box, but he balloons it over the bar. Lucky break for Lazio.

Genoa: Bijlow; Marcandalli, Ostigard, Vasquez; Ehizibue, Traore, Wiafe, Ellertsson; Messias, Vitinha; Osmajic

Milan: Maignan; Gila, De Winter, Diawara, Musah; Moreira, Modric, Rabiot, Cisse; Pulisic; Ramos

Nightmare for Alfonso Pedraza, who turns the ball into his own net just minutes after coming on. Good play from McTominay to win the ball back, before the Partneopei fired a cross over from the left-hand side. Both Hojlund and McTominay went for the ball but it was the Spaniard who got the last touch to knock the ball past Mandas.

Tavares did make way at half-time, with Alfonso Pedraza on in his place.

Second half is underway in Naples as Lazio resume play.

The visitors lead heading into the break, thanks to Frattesi’s quality finish. A few injury troubles for Lazio, though, who lost Sutalo to an apparent hamstring in the first half, with Nuno Tavares also now struggling too.

Napoli have shown a couple of promising glimpses and if it was not for Mandas, could have equalised just before the end of the first 45.

One additional minute to be played at the end of the first half.

First yellow card of the game goes to Noa Lang after cutting down a promising counter-attack.

DAZN Italia claim Noa Lang gestured towards the fans telling them to make more noise. They did not appreciate that.

Lang picks the ball up and gives it to Oliveira who puts the ball into a dangerous space. McTominay and Hojlund seem to almost get in the way of one another and the Dane’s knock-on is saved by Mandas on the goal line.
