Sarri confirms ‘fragile’ Atalanta to enter training retreat after 3-0 home defeat to Parma

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Maurizio Sarri confirms Atalanta will go into a training retreat after suffering a 3-0 home defeat to Parma: ‘I didn’t expect this group to be so fragile.’

Atalanta lost 3-0 at home against Parma on Monday, extending their winless streak in Serie A to four matches.

La Dea has only earned 11 points in ten league games this season, winning just one of their last five Serie A matches.

“The first 40 minutes were high-level; we had the game in our hands. Then we conceded from a set piece, and we paid a high price. It’s hard to accept that second half,” Sarri said at full time, via Gazzetta.

“I apologise to the fans and the club; we can’t be so fragile in defence and in our heads. It’s better to go on a training retreat for some days.”

Atalanta players were loudly booed by their fans at full time. This coming Thursday, they’ll host Kairat in the Conference League.

“It wasn’t about Parma’s courage; it was about our fear,” Sarri said.

“We controlled the game, and we had time to turn things round. On the other hand, we got irritable, and that’s how it ended. I feel indebted to this club; I’m happy here, and I want to come out of this difficult moment. Honestly, I didn’t expect this fragility from the group, while other difficulties were predictable.”

Sporting director Cristiano Giuntoli also spoke to reporters at full time, confirming the decision to send the team into a training retreat.

“We apologise to our fans; the second half was unacceptable,” he said.

“Now we need to keep our heads down and keep working hard. Starting tomorrow, we’ll all head to training camp.”

Both Sarri and Giuntoli joined the club last summer, and when the director was asked whether the coach is under scrutiny, he replied honestly: “We are all under scrutiny these days.”

Atalanta currently sit 13th in the Serie A table with just 11 points in ten matches. They have scored 16 goals so far and conceded 20.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
