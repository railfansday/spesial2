Test your prompts like code: a Node.js prompt test sheet for AI students

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Most students learning generative AI write a prompt, try it twice and move on. Developers test. Here is a weekend project that turns prompt testing into code: a tiny Node.js script that runs one prompt over a list of test inputs and checks each answer against simple rules.

It doesn't depend on any one AI provider. You plug in whichever API you have access to.

The prompt's job here: summarise a college notice in one short sentence without losing dates and times. Write at least 10 cases, including awkward ones (two dates, Hindi-English mixed text, an empty notice).

Keep your API key in an environment variable, never in the file, and never commit it to GitHub.

Run it, look at the failures, change one thing in PROMPT, run again. Write down each change and the new score. After three or four rounds you will have a short, honest record like "v1 7/10, v2 9/10 (added 'keep dates exactly')". That record is a far better interview story than "I know ChatGPT".

I put this together for students at Dzimark Training in Mohali, where the AI programmes cover prompt engineering, AI agents and AI-powered full-stack work. If you are in the Tricity and want the structured version, the course outline is on the Dzimark Training AI programmes page.
