Why I Built 14 Privacy-First Browser Tools Using WebAssembly & WebGPU (No Uploads, Free Forever)

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Most online utility websites work the same way: you upload a confidential PDF, photo, or audio file to a random server, wait in a queue, dodge popups, and hit a paywall when you try to download the result.

I got tired of that pattern and built DodoDeck — a suite of 14 everyday tools that execute 100% locally inside your browser using WebAssembly and WebGPU. When you close the tab, your data is gone. Zero server uploads.

Would love feedback from fellow developers on the WebAssembly / WebGPU performance and security architecture!
