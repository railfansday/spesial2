Russell fastest as rain limits running in Las Vegas GP practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

George Russell topped second practice from Pierre Gasly after there was very little running in first practice on a wet first day of track action; watch the Las Vegas GP on Sky Sports F1, with qualifying at 4am on Saturday and the race at 4am on Sunday

Mercedes' George Russell topped Practice Two from Alpine's Pierre Gasly as rain limited running on the opening day of track action at the Las Vegas Grand Prix.

After no timed laps were completed in first practice due to heavy rain falling throughout, the weather relented to allow plenty of running during the second session, albeit on a damp, slowly drying circuit that prevented the use of slick tyres.

The best lap times came at the end of the session, which took place on Thursday night in Las Vegas, when Russell pipped Gasly by 0.071s, with Ferrari's Charles Leclerc three tenths off the pace in third.

"Realistically we were not the fastest today. McLaren looked exceptionally fast. Charles looked very fast at points, we just got the lap in when the track was at its driest and Norris didn't run," said Russell.

"We have got some things to understand. We were very competitive in the wet last week [in Brazil] and both Kimi and I were off the pace."

Leclerc and McLaren's Lando Norris, who won last time out in Brazil, had traded the fastest times for most of the session in the wetter conditions.

Norris ended up seventh, behind Red Bull's Isack Hadjar, his McLaren team-mate Oscar Piastri and Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton, but the times are likely to matter very little given qualifying and the race are expected to take place in dry conditions.

The schedule in Las Vegas runs a day earlier than a traditional F1 weekend, but the time difference means qualifying will take place at 4am on Saturday morning for UK viewers, with the race following at the same time on Sunday.

Remarkably, there were few incidents and the only red flag across the whole day was for a plastic bag blowing on to the circuit at the high-speed final corner in second practice.

New F1 world champion Kimi Antonelli was eighth fastest but will serve a five-place grid penalty after exceeding his internal combustion engine allocation for a second time this season.

Piastri, Williams' Carlos Sainz and Aston Martin's Lance Stroll have 10-place grid penalties for going over various power unit elements, while Haas' Oliver Bearman will start at the back.

Max Verstappen spent most of the day in the garage and completed only 10 laps in total as he finished second practice in 13th.

Verstappen, who is on a run of eight consecutive podiums, felt he had "no power" from the engine on the laps he completed and insisted "it was not normal" despite his engineer suggesting the issue was related to the power unit needing to warm up in the cold condtions.

McLaren and Mercedes have brought upgrades this weekend but the rain on Thursday meant it was virtually impossible to tell whether they made any impact. McLaren revealed they did not even run the new floor parts in the two practice sessions.

Final practice, which gets under way at 12.30am on Saturday for UK viewers, is set to be very busy as the drivers will attempt to get plenty of dry laps in before qualifying.
