When a Campaign Works, I Repeat the Elements Search Console Proved — Not the Whole Campaign

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

I decide element by element, from Search Console, not from how the campaign felt. My campaign was hand-checked local distribution for a one-person dentist site (dentalpicorivera.com, launched 23 October 2026): 147 live profiles and mini-sites, each checked logged-out to confirm the link actually points at the site, with the same name, city and website everywhere.

What I repeat is what the data shows working. Treatment-led pages surfaced real queries — "pico rivera orthodontist" (101 impressions), "pico rivera invisalign" (89), "gum disease treatment pico rivera" (81) — while the generic "dentist in Pico Rivera" framing did not. So I repeat the treatment-first framing and the consistent distribution, and I do not copy the whole campaign: the generic pages and the one-off profiles that brought nothing get left behind.

The honest part: this is an impressions story so far, not a results story. Between 24 October and 20 November impressions rose from 0 to 2,970, 152 pages are indexed, average position is 49.3 — page five, too deep for clicks — with one click in total and no leads yet. So "worth repeating" means "worth repeating while it is still only moving impressions", and the test for each element stays the same: does Search Console show it surfacing a real local query, or not?
