Wardrobe staples Taylor Swift apparently loves? T-shirts, cardigans, and… Uggs. In a recent viral Vanity Fair article (and if you’re not sure what that’s all about, you can read more here), it was revealed that Swift allegedly wore Uggs to middle school.

In the article, Swift’s former classmate Arielle Peppers stated, “She had the brown one, the black one, the blue one.” Peppers later told TMZ that her comment was meant to reference Swift’s financial standing.

The remark caused chaos online, with many now dubbing the conversation #UggGate2026.

While Uggs have always been a popular shoe, they reached their peak in the mid-2000s (around the time Swift was in the eighth grade). Pair them with skinny jeans or leggings, an Abercrombie fitted tee, and a puka shell necklace, and you were the envy of pre-teens everywhere. Recently, the boots have made a massive resurgence as consumers look to recreate that Y2K aesthetic. The catch? They’re notoriously expensive.

Sales on these styles are relatively rare, but during Black Friday and Cyber Monday, we’re seeing discounts pop up across a variety of retailers, from Nordstrom to Macy’s and Zappos. Some of the classics are up for grabs at up to 30% off, including the Mini boots, while more contemporary silhouettes have also had their prices slashed.
