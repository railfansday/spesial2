Derval Whelan Exits Searchlight As Head Of U.S. Distribution, Had 17-Year Run With Label

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

EXCLUSIVE: Derval Whelan is set to retire at the end of the month from her role as US Head of Distribution for Searchlight Pictures. The exec’s overall run with the 20th Century Fox originated arthouse label counts 17 years.

Whelan began at Searchlight in 2006 and had a brief break between 2021 and 2023 when she was the President of Distribution at Bleecker Street. Upon her return she took over for outgoing Searchlight Distribution President Frank Rodriguez. Working from the NYC office, she’s overseen strategizing and implementing release patterns for the Searchlight slate. Whelan’s replacement will be announced soon. Her run began with Little Miss Sunshine, and includes such award-winning titles as 12 Years a Slave, Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri, The Shape of Water, Birdman, 127 Hours, and Slumdog Millionaire. Searchlight notched 18 Best Picture Oscar nominations during Whelan’s tenure; in total, they received 161 Oscar nominations and 45 wins. Watch on Deadline

Whelan was planning to retire, we understand, and took the VERO which is being offered by Disney for long-running vets at the conglomerate.

Before Searchlight, Whelan held positions at Sony Pictures Classics, Miramax, MK2 USA, and Orion Classics.

Searchlight has the Marin McDonagh directed and written Wild Horse Nine in theatrical release which counts over $30M at the global box office. Wild Horse Nine is up at the Gothams for McDonagh’s original screenplay and John Malkovich in Outstanding Lead Performance. The specialty label has the Tony Gilroy directed Pedro Pascal classical musician feature Behemoth! coming out on Dec. 4.

Recently, longtime Searchlight marketing EVP and awards guru Michelle Hooper exited after a two decade run.
