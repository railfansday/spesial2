PlayStation Plus Officially Makes 10/10 2005 PS2 Masterpiece 100% Free

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Fans of classic shooters won't want to miss this new addition to the PlayStation Plus catalog. The late 90s and early 2000s gave us classics like the Nintendo 64's GoldenEye 007 and Perfect Dark. These have remained some of the best shooters (and games, in general) of all time, according to many fans and critics.

I'm talking about TimeSplitters, the time-traveling first-person shooter that served as a spiritual successor to Perfect Dark and GoldenEye 007. The first TimeSplitters title was developed in 2000 by Free Radical Design, a team which was made up of developers who'd worked on both the other two titles. The studio was founded in 1999 by former Rare devs who left the larger studio while work was ongoing for Perfect Dark.

Free Radical Design brought their experience and ideas to the creation of TimeSplitters, which can be felt in everything from the overall feel of the game to its combat and storytelling. The first game was released as an exclusive PlayStation 2 launch title.

It introduced the titular TImeSplitters, odd beings that travel through time and alter human history. The game is told through the point of view of multiple characters between 1935 and 2035, similarly to the cult classic Eternal Darkness, which was released two years later. Throughout the years, the characters you follow set out to complete their own agendas, but are foiled at every turn by the interfering TimeSplitters.

The second game, released two years later, continued the story and freed the PlayStation exclusive, expanding it to the Xbox and GameCube. It dove deeper into the lore and set up several characters and a story that led into the third and most recent addition to the PlayStation Plus Premium games catalog: TimeSplitters: Future Perfect.

The 2005 TimeSplitters: Future Perfect was the third and final game in the TimeSplitters saga. This was by far the most story-rich of the three games, exploring the titular time-leaping creatures and their origins. The title also lets players interact with their own past and future selves, taking the idea of time-travel a step further.

The game continues right after the end of TimeSplitters 2, with space marine Sergeant Cortez crash-landing on Earth in the future. Cortez sets out to stop the TimeSplitters from interfering with the world and dooming it. And to do so, he has to travel back in time in order to save the future.

TimeSplitters: Future Perfect completes the trilogy and resolves a lot of the questions left unanswered by the previous two games. You could technically play the third game without the previous two. But since all three are available for free, it's worth at least checking out TimeSplitters 2 before Future Perfect, since the third game is a direct sequel to the previous title.

The trilogy arrived on modern consoles—the PS4 and PS5—when it was re-released in 2024 as PS2 Classics. The games now include some quality-of-life upgrades like faster loading times, trophies, and a rewind feature. Future Perfect also originally included online co-op for long-distance gaming, but this is no longer supported.

All three games feature arcade-style first-person combat, a map creator, a multiplayer "Arcade" mode, and the ability to play through the game in local co-op. They offer fans of classic shooters a chance to revisit the origin of the modern shooter through these timeless classics.

A fourth game was in development more recently, but unfortunately, we'll never get to see it: development was canceled in 2023 when Free Radical, then owned by Deep Silver, shut its doors for good. Luckily, the three existing games are still available to experience, and now you can do so for free thanks to the PlayStation classics PS Plus catalog.

TimeSplitters, TimeSplitters 2, and TimeSplitters: Future Perfect are all now available to play for free with a PlayStation Plus Premium subscription.
