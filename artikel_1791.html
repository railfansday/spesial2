Angry Doncaster Rovers boss Grant McCann issues warning to players - with January transfer window on horizon

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Rovers find themselves in the League One relegation zone after a disappointing opening third of the campaign.

They failed to cash in on playing with an extra man for an hour in Thursday's 3-2 loss to Huddersfield Town, with McCann angered by his side's defensive deficiencies and lack of quality going forward.
