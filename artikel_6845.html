The Art of the Evening Sweater: 5 Silhouettes to Wear All Winter Long

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

These sumptuous knits are anything but frumpy.

While I love nothing more than burrowing into my most oversized, shapeless cashmere pullover during the colder months, I admit that it doesn’t feel like the most appropriate sartorial choice for a night out. When I want to feel sexy without spending the whole night shivering, I reach for the unsung hero of my winter wardrobe: the evening sweater.

This season, we’ve seen sexy knitwear in the form of cutout cable knits at Schiaparelli and see-through cardigans at Tom Ford—and the trend translates seamlessly off the runway, too. For the most wearable option, look for a knit with a single element that leans more sultry: a draped neckline that falls off the shoulder, a wrap silhouette that hugs the waist, or a deep plunge that shows off some décolletage. Ahead, see the evening sweater trends to shop for the colder days ahead.

With its ballerina-inspired silhouette, a wrap sweater offers timeless appeal. Alaïa’s and & Other Stories’s takes on the style offer an adjustable wrap design, while Naked Cashmere’s rendition adds a gold-toned pin closure for an elevated touch.

There’s something undeniably sensual about a top that exposes your collarbone, especially in the form of plush cashmere and merino wool knitwear. Whether you prefer Staud’s slouchy one-shoulder pick or Simkhai’s halter straps, these styles have a come-hither effect without trying too hard.

A daring neckline is a surefire way to heat up your cold-weather ensembles, and we saw down-to-there sweaters dominate the runways at Zimmermann and Prada. From Reformation’s chunky pullover to Same’s barrel silhouette, these knits pair nicely with your laciest bra.

Backless sweaters are an unexpected pick for date nights, especially when contrasted with a higher neckline. Standout picks from Lisa Yang, Aritzia, and Tove appear deceptively demure from the front for maximum impact.

For a less bundled-up option, reach for a sleeveless sweater. A halterneck silhouette offers an unexpected take on fall’s most ubiquitous layer while still fitting comfortably beneath a leather jacket, and Ossou’s slinky Halo Top is a perfect match for low-rise denim and a faux fur coat.
