Several unnamed German clubs courting Viktoria Köln coach Marian Wilhelm

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Sky Germany's latest transfer rumour update has highlighted that Marian Wilhelm is attracting attention elsewhere.

A handful of unnamed clubs from the Bundesliga and the 2.Bundesliga are keeping an eye on the Viktoria Köln head coach.

Wilhelm has been working for Viktoria Köln for a considerable spell, having worked his way up from U-19 assistant coach to head coach.

Currently, Wilhelm's side are top of the table in the third division with an impressive tally of 32 points from 14 matches so far.

Achieving promotion would be an incredible feat for the club who were playing in the fifth tier of German football a recently as 2012.
