Commanders may add help at running back after Jeremy McNichols's foot injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Commanders may need to add some help at running back this week.

Jeremy McNichols hurt his foot in Sunday night’s win over the Eagles and head coach Dan Quinn said at a Monday press conference that there is concern about the injury being a significant one.

“They’re gonna do some more scans as we get through tomorrow,” Quinn said, via the team’s website. “But it’s enough that we’re concerned today. I’ll give you an update as it goes to Wednesday, but it’s something that the team for sure is concerned with.”

Jacory Croskey-Merritt is on injured reserve and must miss at least two more games before he’ll be eligible to return in Week 11. Quinn said the two injuries make an addition “something that we’ll have to discuss” in the coming days.

Rachaad White and Austin Ekeler are the healthy backs on the 53-man roster. Craig Reynolds and Devin Neal are on the practice squad.
