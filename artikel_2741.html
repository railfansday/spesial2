Rams vs. Packers final score: L.A. wins their biggest game of the year

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Los Angeles Rams beat the Green Bay Packers by a final score of 26-13 in the first ever Thanksgiving Eve NFL Game. Blake Corum powered the Rams to this win with 184 yards on the ground with 32 carries. It was an incredible performance by the third-year player. L.A.'s defense also forced a fumble and two interceptions to support their offense, and notch this crucial win for the Rams and their playoff odds.

The Rams jumped out to a 6-0, and then 13-3 lead in the second quarter when Kyren Williams found the endzone for the first touchdown of the day, but the Packers scored 10 unanswered point of their own to tie the game at 13-13 going into halftime.

Christian Watson was on pace for a massive game with four receptions for 65 yards and score, but a hit by Kam Kinchens ended Watson's night with a concussion. Kinchens was ejected for the hit on Watson as both players saw their nights end early.

The Rams drove down the field to open the second half and when Blake Corum found the endzone for his first touchdown of the day, the Rams had a 20-13 lead after the extra point. L.A.'s defense forced a Green Bay punt which gave the Rams a chance to take a two-score lead, but Matthew Stafford threw an interception to give life back to the Packers.

Jordan Love then threw an interception of his own to give the Rams the ball back, but the Rams failed to add any points off the turnover, keeping the score 20-13 and a one possession game.

With about six minutes to go in the fourth quarter, L.A. tacked on their third field goal to take a 23-13 lead and a two-score lead. With under four minutes to go, and with the Pack driving, Love threw his second interception of the game setting up the Rams to chew clock and forcing the Packers to burn timeouts as L.A. tried to put the game away.

The Rams added a final field goal to take a 26-13 lead with 30-seconds remaining in the contest, and that was enough for the Rams. Sean McVay beat Matt LaFleur for the first time in his career. L.A. earned their most impressive win of the 2026 season, and now they get some time off before they get back to work and look to move their win streak to six games when they face the Kansas City Chiefs on Thursday Night football.
