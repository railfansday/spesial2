'The Pivot Sports with Ryan Clark' adds second weekly episode

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

When Ryan Clark left ESPN over the summer, he quickly launched The Pivot Sports, an offshoot of his popular interview podcast.

Clark's production company, Pivot25, announced the expansion this week. As part of the move, Pivot25 added a "multiyear" extension to its partnership with NFL Films, under which it will "continue to produce and create studio shows."

The Friday show will feature more in-studio interviews and new recurring co-hosts. Previously, executive producer Alicia Zubikowski told Awful Announcing that The Pivot Sports could eventually expand to cover sports beyond the NFL. The show has done segments on the WNBA and college football in its first handful of episodes.

Pivot25 recently received an investment from former Starbucks CEO Howard Schultz. The NFL Films partnership stemmed from Clark and Zubikowski's past work with the league-owned production house.

Pivot podcast co-hosts Fred Taylor and Channing Crowder contribute to the new sports talk show, but its title and format suggest Clark will continue to be the face of the show.

"I see building out The Pivot is to have it be a place where not only can it be the only place I work in the sense of only platform I need based on visibility and viewership, but also be the only platform you quote unquote need monetarily," Clark told Awful Announcing last month.

"But when you do these things, you want them to be profitable."
