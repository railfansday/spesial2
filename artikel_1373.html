Roy Keane Hits Out At Gary Neville After Man Utd Thrashed By Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Roy Keane could not believe what he was hearing from Gary Neville after Manchester United were thumped 4-0 by Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday afternoon.

The Red Devils got off to a shocking start against their old rivals, in what was Mauricio Pochettino's first game in charge of the club, with Leny Yoro sent off for the denial of a goalscoring opportunity just eight minutes in.

Things then went from bad to worse as Dominik Szoboszlai scored the resulting free-kick and a few minutes later it was effectively game over, when Florian Wirtz netted from a Rio Ngumoha cross.

United tightened up and restricted Liverpool somewhat for the rest of the first half, but the home side scored twice more after the break, through Ngumoha and Victor Munoz, to run out comfortable winners.

The four goals shipped means that United now have the worst record of any team in Europe's top five leagues and sit only off the bottom of the Premier League table by one point.

Liverpool could have had more than four goals on the day, with Bradley Barcola guilty of missing some good chances, and on commentary for Sky Sports, United legend Gary Neville intimated that it was not such a bad result for United, given the circumstances. He even made reference to the supporters that stayed behind to sing 'don't worry, about a thing, cos every little thing, is gonna be all right', saying that "things will be all right eventually under Pochettino".

After the match, in the Sky Sports studio, Nevilles former teammate Keane was not impressed.

"If I hear that one more time from the lads on commentary saying 4-0 is not too bad with ten men..." Keane said. "Has a team never got a result before with ten men? It can be done. I thought United were desperate, I thought they were so bad and everyone, Nev who's played for Man Utd hundreds of times saying he's ok with 4-0. Are you telling me they couldn't have done better? They lose 4-0 and everyone's saying that's not too bad. Has a team never got a result before with ten men, it can be done. At least show a bit more fight, a bit more quality, I can't believe I'm hearing from people who played for the club saying 4-0 is not too bad. Wow.

"And the fans applauding the players, applauding what? Applauding rubbish. This team two years ago finished 15th. The last year or two we're blaming the board, blaming ownership, blaming managers, when do the players take responsibility for some of the decision-making?

"Liverpool probably should have scored eight, probably eight or nine, unbelievable chances they had and everyone's coming off the pitch saying 4-0 is not too bad. I feel like swearing. I'll try not to.

"How can anyone say 4-0 is not too bad, just because we were down to ten men? If you go down to ten men is that it? You just down tools and throw the towel in?"

A nightmare start for the new United boss.
