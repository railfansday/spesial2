Skip the Revvi Card if you can spare a refundable deposit for a no-fee secured card. Its fees cost $145 to $220 in the first year, and its 1% cash back on payments won’t come close to covering them. Apply only if every secured card has turned you down.

MRV Banks offers the no-deposit Revvi Card to people with poor credit, and the Total Visa and First Access Visa charge the same fees and pay the same 1% back on payments. On a $300 credit line, charging $100 a month and paying each bill in full earns about $13 in cash back the first year, about $157 less than that year’s $170 in program and annual fees.

The Revvi Card makes sense only for applicants turned down for every secured card, because it reports to all three credit bureaus without a deposit. With a refundable deposit to spare, open a no-fee secured card instead, since its deposit comes back and these fees don’t.
