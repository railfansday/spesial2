California man pleads guilty to shipping fentanyl cross country to Buffalo following Homeland Security Task Force investigation

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

U.S. Attorney Michael DiGiacomo announced today that Fidel Beltran-Torres, 50, of Inglewood, CA, pleaded guilty before U.S. District Judge John L. Sinatra, Jr. to possession with intent to distribute 400 grams or more of fentanyl, which carries a mandatory minimum penalty of 10 years in prison, a maximum of life, and a fine of $10,000,000.
