Weight is the most important consideration when buying a weighted blanket. It should weigh roughly 10% of your overall body weight, to ensure you can comfortably move underneath it.

For children or people with limited mobility, a weighted blanket should weigh no more than 10% of overall body weight. If your child has a weighted blanket, they should be able to take it on and off easily, without any help.

It's also important to consider how the blanket is weighted. In most cases, small pockets are filled with glass beads to create weight. However, some blankets (such as the Bearaby and Cocoon) use heavy materials instead.

And be aware that weighted blankets can trap heat. You might find this adds to the coziness but for hot sleepers, we recommend looking for a weighted blanket with cooling covers.
