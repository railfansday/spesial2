Prediction: Fulham vs Bournemouth

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Turning their attention back to Premier League action on Sunday afternoon, Bournemouth will travel to the capital when they meet a resurgent Fulham.

With a stacked Sunday schedule from across England’s elite this weekend, there are plenty of eye-catching betting promotions available.

Ahead of a showdown from west London, you can find all of the key Fulham vs Bournemouth H2H stats here.

Starting with the hosts, while Fulham might have endured a nightmare start to proceedings when opening up the 2026/27 campaign, Alvaro Arbeloa’s camp have shown some major signs of improvement on either side of November’s international break. Marking their return from the fortnight interval with a well-deserved 1-1 stalemate away at Premier League giants Manchester City, the Cottagers have come skyrocketing out of the drop zone. Enjoying what has become a real resurge, Fulham are unbeaten in each of their previous five consecutive top-flight appearances (two wins, three draws). However, although the former Real Madrid boss has certainly eased some of the pressure on his shoulders, Sunday’s hosts are still searching for an essential clinical edge in the final third. In fact, Arbeloa’s side have failed to register more than a single strike in all of their last nine straight showdowns against Premier League opposition across all competitions.

Yet to feature since a three-minute cameo appearance against Ipswich on October 10th, former Arsenal starlet Emile Smith Rowe remains on the sidelines and won’t play any part this weekend. However, making an immediate return from a minor knock prior to November’s international break, club captain Tom Cairney could get some more minutes under his belt.

Finding the net against Manchester City last week and notching a second Premier League strike of the season, Brazilian marksman Rodrigo Muniz will spearhead Fulham’s attack. Completing a permanent switch to west London over the summer, Austrian international David Affengruber will pattern Calvin Bassey at the heart of Arbeloa’s defence.

As for the visitors, extending on their record-breaking run earlier in the week as they produced a sensational Europa League comeback on foreign soil, Bournemouth are in the midst of what has become an iconic purple patch. Eventually leaving Spain with a 2-1 victory against Celta Vigo and now the only Europa League outfit who can boast a 100% start to proceedings, Marco Rose’s men find the mood in their camp at a red-hot high. In fact, marking their return from November’s international break with a 2-0 win against Nottingham Forest last week, the former Borussia Dortmund boss has seen his side remarkably win all of their previous 10 consecutive appearances across all competitions. With that history-making flurry stemming all the way back to October 10th, the Cherries also open this weekend’s fixtures sat within touching distance of skyrocketing into the Champions League spots.

While former Lille star Bafode Diakite might have been shown a Europa League red card away in Spain on Thursday evening, the 25-year-old is free to feature in Rose’s squad here. However, yet to make a maiden appearance this season, Mexican international Julian Araujo remains on the sidelines with a long-term thigh injury.

Left as an unused substitute against Celta Vigo in midweek, red-hot leading marksman Evanilson will be handed an immediate full recall and spearhead Bournemouth’s attack. Likewise, former Chelsea goalkeeper Djordje Petkovic will return between the sticks on Sunday afternoon.

Sat at the top of the Europa League standings following a stunning comeback away at Celta Vigo on Thursday evening, Bournemouth must feel they are in the midst of a real fairytale. Embarking on what has become an astonishing purple patch on either side of November’s international break, Rose’s record-breaking squad have won all of their last 10 consecutive appearances across all competitions. Opening this weekend’s fixtures sat within touching distance of the Champions League spots, Bournemouth will simply be looking to extend on their iconic flurry in west London.
