AccessIT Group Appoints Josh Locker as First-Ever Chief Growth Officer

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Cybersecurity industry veteran tapped to accelerate AccessIT Group's next phase of growth.

KING OF PRUSSIA, Pa., Nov. 5, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- AccessIT Group, a 25-year cybersecurity solutions and services provider, announced today the appointment of Josh Locker as the company's first-ever Chief Growth Officer. In this newly created role, Locker will unify the company's go-to-market teams, including sales, marketing and partnerships, to build on its current momentum and capture emerging opportunities in the cybersecurity market.

"We created this role so every part of how we go to market works toward one goal: delivering security guidance built around each client's unique business needs," said Joe Luciano, founder and CEO of AccessIT Group. "Josh has spent his career keeping the client at the center of growth, and that makes him the right leader for this moment. Josh also shares the values that define us: we put our people and our clients first, and we believe great culture drives great results."

Locker brings 32 years of experience in the technology industry, including 16 years in cybersecurity leading sales and go-to-market organizations. Most recently, he served as chief revenue officer at Cyderes, where he oversaw sales, pre-sales, sales operations and channel alliances. Before that, he spent 12 years at Optiv Security, where he played a key role in scaling the business.

"AccessIT Group has built something rare in this industry, a company where people and client relationships are equally as important as the business outcomes they deliver. I'm excited to help bring that approach to more organizations facing an increasingly complex threat landscape," said Locker.

Cybersecurity isn't part of what we do, it's our sole mission.

For over two decades, we've helped organizations cut through the noise and build security programs that solve real business problems. We sit alongside your team, helping you navigate complexity, reduce risk, and make smarter security decisions with confidence.

As threats grow more complex, clients turn to AccessIT Group for our independent, trusted guidance.
