Just two more dominos to fall to finish the Colts tear down

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

What did I miss? I've always been told that the season never really started until Thanksgiving, so how's it looking?

I'll admit that I am shocked by the latest ousting by the Colts brass. I suppose that some day we might find out exactly what happened in the building concerning the release of Daniel Jones. I am left to wonder if it wasn't imbedded in the contract somewhere that he would be granted his release, if he was not the starting QB. I have no evidence of it, but the other reasons do not make much sense to me, so this theory is my leader in the clubhouse.

Another thought might be that Carlie has already chosen (or at least confided in) a GM, and that person wanted no part of Jones. This is pretty unlikely as well. I actually thought that our decisions made to this point, were to give the next GM the say on who would stay and who would go. That is why we only traded players in the last year of their contract and kept players like JT for the next management group to decide on. Consider me confused as to why the next group will not get any say on the most important position on the field.

Now we are just left to wonder how Steichen and Anarumo are still allowed to roam the sidelines. I can understand Carlie's resistance to changing head coaches during the season. She was fully immersed at the facility when Riech was relieved of his duties, so she has seen how inept that can look. Possibly the biggest flaw in that transition, was the fact that the Colts had to dig deep to find someone who could even call offensive plays. It is one of the problems associated with having your HC also being your OC. Jim Bob is the OC in name only.

If we are truly tearing things down, why do we have to continue to look at and listen to Steichen, as both are equally as infuriating. It wouldn't be a great position to put anyone else in, but if we are going to continue to use the word "message" as we escort someone else out the building, how is he remaining the face? I wouldn't expect a suddenly effective offense by allowing Jim Bob to call plays on offense or a respectable defense by allowing Bettcher to call the defense, but maybe we'd get away from some of the things that make us mad.

I've suggested this before, but let Reggie be the head coach as a resume builder. As a player, he may have more in game awareness of what is happening on the field and on the clock. Maybe Jim Bob has a preference about what his banged up OL should look like and how to get AR into some space. Win or lose, the product might look better and more attractive to the new regime. On D, it would be pretty easy to tell Bettcher to simply blitz more often.

Seeing different coaches on the sidelines would not transform the team into a winner and maybe not even into a competitive team, but it would make me not want to chuck a rock at my screen each time I see Shane and Lou. Some feel that CIG is doing the right things to get the Colts back on course and in so mending some of the fan driven angst towards the team. I know that with only six games to go, that making a coaching change would not have any real bearing on the 2026 season, but some of us could start washing that bad taste out of our mouth a bit quicker.

I know that I am not nearly alone on this thought, as I have seen many comments calling for ousters as well. I'm not breaking any new ground. Just like Jones, I wouldn't want to give the next GM an option of keeping either of these guys around.
