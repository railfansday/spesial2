Packers’ Christian Watson, Matthew Golden Pull Off Receiving Feat Not Seen Since World War II

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Green Bay Packers' passing attack absolutely unleashed on Christian Gonzalez and the New England Patriots on Sunday at Gillette Stadium in Foxborough.

The Patriots probably had no idea about the historic performance the wide receiver duo of Matthew Golden and Christian Watson was about to pull off on the field at New England's expense.

With Jordan Love running the show from under center for Green Bay's offense and Golden and Love downfield, the Packers had their way all game long offensively.

Watson led all players in the contest with 171 receiving yards and a touchdown on seven receptions and eight targets. But Golden wasn't too far behind, with the former first-round pick wideout from the Texas Longhorns football program going off for 164 receiving yards and a score on 10 catches and 10 targets.

Oct 29, 2026; Green Bay, Wisconsin, USA; Golden (0) celebrates a touchdown reception against the Carolina Panthers during their football game at Lambeau Field. Mandatory Credit: Wm. Glasheen USA Today Network via IMAGN Images

According to NFL Media researcher Tony Holzman-Escareno, what Watson and Golden did together was something not seen since before the end of the Second World War.

"Christian Watson and Matthew Golden are the first Packers teammates with 150+ receiving yards and 1+ receiving TD since Don Hutson and Andy Uram in 1942," Holzman-Escareno shared via a social media post on X, formerly Twitter.

Love, meanwhile, was simply too good for New England's stop unit to shut down. He went 23/29 for 372 passing yards and three touchdowns against zero interceptions, as Green Bay compiled a total of 478 yards, while also forcing New England to commit four turnovers.

Green Bay will play its next game at home, as the team is scheduled to host the Minnesota Vikings in Week 10 at Lambeau Field.
