Warriors vs. Pelicans injury report: Steph Curry and Trey Murphy III are both out

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Golden State Warriors and New Orleans Pelicans play each other tonight, just one week after the Dubs trounced the Pelicans by 51 points. In that game, Golden State's biggest star, Steph Curry, put on a show, while NOLA's biggest star, Zion Williamson, sat. Tonight, the roles are reversed. Here's the injury report for both teams.

Curry will miss a good chunk of games as he rehabs an abdominal injury. He'll be re-evaluated next week, but could be out for a bit longer than that.

Out — Brandon Williams (left hamstring strain)

Without Curry and Williams, the Dubs are seriously lacking in perimeter scoring, and that puts a lot of pressure on Brandin Podziemski and De'Anthony Melton. Hopefully Williams is able to come back soon.

Out — Jimmy Butler III (right ACL repair surgery)Out — Moses Moody (left patellar tendon repair surgery)Out — Kristaps Porziņģis (not with team)

No new news with either of these three. Same old, same old.

Questionable — Gui Santos (right ankle inflammation)

In good news, Santos is no longer on the injury report with knee issues. In bad news, he's on it for ankle issues. Hopefully he can play, given the talent the Dubs are lacking tonight.

Out — Trey Murphy III (bilateral patellar tendinopathy)

Murphy, who has long been linked to the Warriors in trades, will once again miss playing the Dubs. This is the fifth consecutive absence for the 26-year old, who was sidelined in last week's matchup.

Out — Dejounte Murray (left ankle sprain)

Murray's days with the Pelicans appear numbered, as he recently requested a trade from the organization. The 2022 All-Star is having his worst season in a while, but is still a quality starter, and New Orleans is worse without him.

Out — Trendon Watford (right ankle sprain)

Watford has been solid in his first season with the Pelicans, but injuries have limited him to just six games.

Out — Bennedict Mathurin (left plantar fasciitis)

Mathurin had a brutal game when these teams played last Monday, as he shot just 1-for-9 from the field. Despite averaging 16.2 points per game over his first four seasons, Mathurin is averaging just 5.5 points in his first year with the organization.

Out — Bryce McGowens (left elbow infection)

McGowens isn't playing a huge role for the Pelicans, but has been a contributor off the bench, averaging just over 10 minutes per game.

Enjoy the game, everyone! It tips off at 5:00 p.m. PT.
