Bills injuries could put young defenders in spotlight vs. Dolphins

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

ORCHARD PARK - This is going to be an interesting countdown to game day for the Buffalo Bills' secondary. There was injury damage incurred in the victory over the New York Jets as two starting defensive backs left that game and didn't finishing.

Top cornerback Christian Benford, who was already dealing with a wrist injury, suffered a groin injury and that would obviously be a blow to the defense if it's serious enough to where he misses time. Not so much Sunday against hapless Miami, but certainly Thanksgiving night against Patrick Mahomes and Kansas City, and that alone might be a good reason to just sit him out.

And slot corner Dee Alford twice suffered head injuries in New York and the second time he banged his head on the turf and was down several minutes. One can assume he's in concussion protocol but as usual, Joe Brady did not provide any updates Wednesday. If that's the case, the protocol is different for every player so it's quite possible he could miss the game.

"There's always concern whenever guys are nicked up or aren't able to finish a game," Brady said before the Bills conducted a walkthrough which, given its nature, did not shed any light on Benford's condition, mainly because the media isn't allowed to watch the practice.

"I think it's a natural tendency, just with our team right now," Brady continued, "is us being able to get our bodies back. So I'll have a better answer for you guys with all those situations coming down this week, but you guys saw with Spencer Brown last week."

Yes, Brown was listed as doubtful on the final injury report last Friday which almost certainly meant he wasn't playing. Doubtful players rarely end up playing, but Brown felt good progress on his sore ankle Saturday and then into Sunday morning, so he gave it a go and wound up playing the entire game, and playing very well.

"If they have an opportunity to go play in the game, we're going to play them, and Christian is really no different," Brady said. "We'll progress it day by day, but I know it's very easy to think long term (and hold him out against Miami to get him ready for Kansas City Thanksgiving night). Right now, we're just thinking about how do we win this football game and we'll go from there. Christian's always going to do everything he can to get ready to go, and if he can't, feel confident with the guys on the depth chart to be able to step in like they have."

If Benford can't play against the Dolphins, it means rookie Davison Igbinosun, who has settled into a starting role, will be joined by 2025 first-round pick Max Hairston, and it may also mean that veteran Tre'Davious White gets elevated from the practice squad to back up the two youngsters he has been asked to mentor ever since he re-signed with the Bills a few weeks ago.

"He didn't have a training camp, he didn't have an offseason, so any time you onboard some veteran players, as the season goes on, that's part of the process is how many plays do you think you could play right now?" defensive coordinator Jim Leonhard said of White. "I like where he's at. I think he's really close to being able to help us. We had some injuries here this last game and if need be, I think he's ready to step up and play for us."

If Alford can't play, the Bills will turn to Jordan Hancock who just returned after missing six games.

On the first play after Alford left the game the first time, Geno Smith immediately tested Hancock. Garrett Wilson matched up with Hancock in the slot and caught a pass down the seam for 33 yards. But that was the only catch the 2025 fifth-round pick allowed on three targets including one other to Wilson.

"Needed to get settled in, and I'm happy they tried me first play," Hancock said. "Competed to the end, he caught the ball, you got to live with that. You're gonna have to do it again so just putting the ball down and competing and try to do my job and be ready when my number was called."

Leonhard liked what he saw from Hancock after that play as he was targeted two other times, one while covering Wilson, and he did not allow a completion.

"I thought he played a really calm game," Leonhard said. "He went in there and the ball found him. It was an unbelievable throw that Geno had. Sometimes you get hit and you get gun shy or a little bit cautious, but I didn't think he did that. I thought he played confident, he communicated with confidence. After that, the ball they didn't necessarily come his way very often. I thought he played a clean game and he helped settle things down after Dee went out."

Another player to monitor is linebacker Dorian Williams, who suffered a calf injury. He said he was OK after the game, but all players say that. If he can't play, it would mean rookie Kaleb Elarms-Orr, who has been getting more and more playing time, would get his first NFL start.

Williams and Bernard have started every game, and in the first eight games the only time Elarms-Orr reached 20 defensive snaps was against the Raiders. Against the Jets he played a season-high 25 snaps and was in on two tackles and recovered a fourth-quarter fumble.

"When you start talking about young guys … the fans want it all to happen right now. Just put them out there and let's grow," Leonhard said. "Sometimes there's a progression to it. You're just trying to put them in situations where they can use their skillset the best, where they can play fast and free. That's not always every play. (Igbinosun, T.J. Parker), Kaleb, all these young guys that are getting on the field that you're starting to see them just settle in and be more comfortable and just show more consistency week in and week out."
