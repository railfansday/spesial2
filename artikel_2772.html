Chiefs vs. Falcons: RB Emmett Johnson scores first TD in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Kansas City Chiefs got off to a fast start in their Week 10 matchup against the Atlanta Falcons at Mercedes-Benz Stadium.

The Chiefs made quick work of the Falcons' defense as they marched down the field with ease on a 10-play, 80-yard drive that ended with a touchdown by running back Emmett Johnson.

Quarterback Patrick Mahomes found Johnson in the red zone, where the former fifth-round pick danced around several defenders before finding the end zone.

Take a look at this video of Johnson's receiving touchdown, which was posted to Twitter by the Chiefs' official account.

The former Nebraska Cornhusker came into Sunday's tilt with big shoes to fill after running back Kenneth Walker was ruled out with a groin injury.

Johnson has already made a name for himself in Kansas City and is well on his way to helping the Chiefs notch their seventh win of the season.
