Vic Fangio delivers good news on Quinyon Mitchell for Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Vic Fangio delivers good news on Quinyon Mitchell for Thanksgiving originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

Eagles defensive coordinator Vic Fangio delivered some good news about Quinyon Mitchell's status for the Cowboys matchup on Thanksgiving.

"Yeah, I think he'll be fine," Fangio said on Tuesday afternoon.

Mitchell suffered a hamstring injury in the second half against the Steelers on Sunday and had to leave early. And he was listed as a non-participant in the Eagles' first estimated injury report of the week on Monday.

But Fangio thinks Mitchell will be able to play against the Cowboys, which is very good news. Because this is a high-powered Cowboys offense that has put up 91 points over the last two weeks.

"They've just got a great offense," Fangio said. "Their talent level is really, really good at all positions. This is Dak's, I believe, seventh year in this offense. Because it's the same offense they ran when Mike McCarthy was there. (Brian) Schottenheimer was an assistant on that staff. He's kept the offense. I'm sure he's put a new spin on it here and there.

"When you get a quarterback as good as he is in the same offense for seven straight years or whatever it is, and he's a really smart player to begin with, your efficiency goes way up."

The Cowboys have two excellent receivers in CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens as their 1-2 punch. Fangio agreed this is the best duo of receivers the Eagles have faced this season.

Lamb in 2026 has 88 catches for 1,108 yards and 9 touchdowns. While Pickens has 57 catches for 780 yards and 6 touchdowns. And even their third receiver, Ryan Flournoy, has 477receiving yards.

While the Eagles hope to have Mitchell, it doesn't sound like they'll have Jonathan Jones (ankle), who got some playing time over a healthy Riq Woolen on Sunday before suffering an injury. It looks like it'll be Mitchell, Woolen and Cooper DeJean against the Cowboys on Thanksgiving.

Woolen after the game admitted he was "annoyed" by being pulled from the game but was able to fight through that to finish off the game. When asked how Woolen has handled all that this week, Fangio said, "Good."
