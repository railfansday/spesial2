Final FSU vs. NC State injury report

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Florida State and the NC State Wolfpack are set to play this afternoon. Before they hit the field, both teams released their final injury reports. Everything remains the same for both teams from the Friday's report.

Defensive back Quindarrius Jones, Ja'Bril Rawls and Karson Hobbs, wide receiver Duce Robinson, running backs Ousmane Kromah, Sam Singleton Jr., Amari Thomas and Quintrevion Wisner, linebackers Izayia Williams, Daylen Green, and Noah LaVallee, defensive linemen Rylan Kennedy and Kevin Wynn, and offensive lineman Bradyn Welch-Joiner are out for FSU.

Quarterback Will Wilson, safeties Ronnie Royal III and Tristan Teasdell, tight ends Vander Ploog, Hunter Provience, and Ian Flynt, nickel cornerback Isaiah Crowell, wide receiver Keenan Jackson, linebacker AJ Richardson, offensive linemen Rico Jackson, Yousef Mugharbil, and Spike Sowells Jr., defensive end Chase Bond, and defensive lineman AJ Prim won't play for NC State.

The kickoff for Florida State and NC State will be at 2:30 p.m. ET on the ACC Network. The Seminoles are favored by 10.5 points.
