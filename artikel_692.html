NBC Shakes Up Thanksgiving Broadcast With 9 Broadway Shows Performing a Medley

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

'Wicked,' 'Chicago,' 'Hamilton,' 'Schmigadoon!' and 'The Lost Boys' are among the shows participating.

NBC is making a big change for Broadway performers in this year’s Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade.

Rather than featuring individual performances, the telecast has gathered nine Broadway shows to sing a 10-minute medley at the top of the show created for this year’s parade, which is Macy’s 100th. The shows participating in the medley include several long-runners, in the case of Wicked, Chicago, Hamilton, The Book of Mormon and Aladdin, as well as Schmigadoon! and The Lost Boys, new musicals that both opened last season. The current revival of The Rocky Horror Show will perform, as well as former cast members from Rent, which recently held a 30th anniversary reunion concert.

The medley, which will be introduced by Julianne Hough (who is starring in the upcoming Broadway revival of Damn Yankees), is described as taking “viewers on a musical journey through Broadway past and present, filled with beloved songs and Easter eggs for theater fans to discover along the way.”

Tom Kitt, the Tony Award-winning composer of Next to Normal, and creative producer Sarah O’Gleby, choreographer on Shucked, arranged the medley. The performances air on Peacock and NBC at 8:30 a.m. in all time zones.

The change opens up performing on the telecast to more shows than usual, with last year’s parade only featuring three Broadway shows. However, it remains unclear how much time each show will get in the spotlight and if they’ll perform numbers from their own show. It also shines a light on the long-runners, which is atypical as the performance is typically used to highlight shows that opened within the prior season.

Performing on the telecast is coveted, as it gives Broadway shows rare exposure on national television, with more than 34 million viewers tuning into last year’s parade on NBC. It’s seen as a major means to boost ticket sales as the shows enter into their lucrative holiday weeks, and now opens up that option for some long-runners that have fallen from former box office highs, such as Wicked and Aladdin. But it remains to be seen how this will translate for these shows that have shorter performance windows than the typical full numbers seen on the telecast.

In addition to Broadway performers, the parade will feature performances from Megan Moroney, Teddy Swims and the Radio City Rockettes. Paddington The Musical, set to open on Broadway in the spring, is also scheduled for a special appearance.

Natasha Bedingfield, Baha Men, Blues Traveler, DEVO, Jimmy Fallon & The Roots, Feid, EJAE, Mckenna Grace, Indigo Girls, Icona Pop, JoJo, Wyclef Jean, Queen Latifah, Il Volo, Natalie Merchant, Jamie Miller, Ryan Peter Murphy, Leslie Odom Jr., Keke Palmer, Anthony Ramos, RIIZE and Smash Mouth will also perform.

Jack McBrayer, Janet McTeer, John Stamos, Melissa Joan Hart, Megan Hilty, Holly Robinson Peete and Rodney Peete are also scheduled to appear.
