6 New Holiday Pop Songs From Hilary Duff, Olivia Rodrigo, Elton John & More for Your Christmas Party Playlist

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

On the new Pop Shop Podcast, Katie & Keith listen to a half-dozen new holiday songs to see if we should add them into the reindeer rotation.

Christmas music is already adorning the Billboard charts, and this year, there are some new holiday hits to add to the Christmas classics we know and love.

On the new Billboard Pop Shop Podcast, Katie & Keith are taking a listen to a half-dozen new holiday songs to see if we should add them into the reindeer rotation. The six songs: Olivia Rodrigo’s “Take Me Home (4 Christmas),” which has already peaked at No. 42 on the Billboard Hot 100; Hilary Duff’s “All You Want for Christmas Is Me,” from her five-track Holiday Things EP; Elton John & CMAT’s “Step Into Christmas,” a new duet version of his 1973 classic; Hozier & Jessie Buckley’s cover of The Pogues’ “Fairytale of New York”; EJAE’s “Christmas In New York,” which she’ll perform at Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade on Thursday; and Alanis Morissette’s “Beautiful Star of Bethlehem,” featuring Brandi Carlile & Steve Martin, from Morissette’s Good Weather for Immaculate Conception holiday album.

Also on the show, we’ve got chart news on how ATEEZ gets their fourth No. 1 album on the Billboard 200, U2 collects its 14th top 10 album, and holiday songs surge into the top 10 on the Hot 100 — but when might we get our first holiday Hot 100 No. 1 this year?

The Billboard Pop Shop Podcast is your one-stop shop for all things pop on Billboard‘s weekly charts. You can always count on a lively discussion about the latest pop news, fun chart stats and stories, new music, and guest interviews with music stars and folks from the world of pop. Casual pop fans and chart junkies can hear Billboard‘s executive digital director, West Coast, Katie Atkinson and Billboard’s managing director, charts and data operations, Keith Caulfield every week on the podcast, which can be streamed on Billboard.com or downloaded in Apple Podcasts or your favorite podcast provider.
