Disney+ and Hulu’s Black Friday Deal Is Here: Get Both Streamers for Just $4.99/Month for an Entire Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Variety may receive an affiliate commission.

Two of Hollywood’s biggest streaming services are available for less than $5 a month thanks to Disney’s annual Black Friday deal, which officially went live on Tuesday.

This year, Disney is also offering a Black Friday discount on its premium bundle, which brings together the largely ad-free versions of Disney+ and Hulu for $14.99/month for an entire year.

Disney+, meanwhile, houses the entirety of the Star Wars and Marvel franchises, alongside Pixar films, National Geographic documentaries and decades’ worth of Disney animated classics. The service is also home to original series such as “Percy Jackson and the Olympians,” “Andor” and “Daredevil: Born Again.”

Those who took advantage of last year’s Black Friday promotion may also qualify for this year’s deal, provided their previous subscription expired before Nov. 24 and was fully canceled.

Sign up for the Disney+ and Hulu Black Friday bundle before the promotion ends on Nov. 30.
