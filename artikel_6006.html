Booking Holdings’ AI Agent Vayo Can Now Reserve Flights and Hotels

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Photo Credit: A month after its debut, Booking Holdings' Vayo now introduces trip-booking capabilities, the company announced Tuesday.  Courtesy of Vayo

Booking Holdings’ newest AI agent can now transact to book hotels, flights, and other trip arrangements. But Vayo's ability to complete reservations depends on supplier sites.

Booking Holdings' month-old AI startup Vayo is moving from planning trips to booking them. That includes reservations with travel companies outside its parent's portfolio.

Vayo said Tuesday it can book flights, hotels, and activities through direct supplier connections or by navigating brands' websites. But according to a spokesperson, where suppliers don't permit agent bookings, Vayo hands the checkout back for the traveler to finish.

For hotels, airlines, and rival booking platforms, that could mean travelers purchasing their products through a Booking-owned agent, without necessarily visiting their websites.
