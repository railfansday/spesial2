England handed suspended away ticket ban for fans' chants in Spain

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

UEFA hand England suspended away ticket ban for "racist and/or discriminatory behaviour" during the 3-2 Nations League win over Spain in Madrid on Sunday; FA have also been fined £12,600

News Editor @AnthonyRJoseph

England have been given a suspended away ticket ban by UEFA for "racist and/or discriminatory behaviour" by their fans during the 3-2 win over Spain in Madrid.

The punishment is a one-match ban and is suspended for a "probationary period" of two years.

Sky Sports News understands the punishment is for anti-Catholic and anti-Irish chanting during the Nations League match.

A spokesperson for The FA told Sky Sports News: " We will not tolerate this unacceptable and deeply offensive chanting, and will work with the police who attend all England home and away matches to identify the perpetrators and take the necessary action."

Sky Sports News has contacted the UK Football Policing Unit for comment.
