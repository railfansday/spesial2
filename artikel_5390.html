Ending the "Continuity Crisis": How Patented MGCOne Architecture Restores Digital Sovereignty and AI-Driven Governance to Condos and HOAs

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Synthesizing U.S. Patent No. 12,443,952 with dedicated AI agents and on-chain financial readiness, MGCOne establishes the permanent "System of Record" for community associations.

HIGHTSTOWN, N.J., Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- My Green Condo Inc. (MGCOne) today highlighted how its patented, association-centric operating framework is solving the long-standing "Continuity Crisis" facing Condo, HOA, and Co-op boards across North America and globally.

For over 100 years, residential community associations have been the ultimate paying customers in property management. Yet legacy software has forced them to surrender custody of their financial ledgers, governance policies, and maintenance histories to third-party intermediaries. Backed by U.S. Patent No. 12,443,952, MGCOne establishes "Digital Sovereignty" for communities. The association owns its core infrastructure and grants or revokes service-provider access with a single click, a model the company likens to the "Amazon of Community Management."
