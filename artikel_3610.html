'Hunting Wives' Star Breaks Silence on [Spoiler]'s Gruesome, Shocking Death

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Hunting Wives surprised viewers by introducing a new character who quickly became beloved — shortly before being killed off.

During season 2, which started streaming on Thursday, November 26, Kim Matula made her debut as TV producer Nadia. The newcomer initially appeared to be filming a reality series centered on Callie (Jaime Ray Newman), but it turned out Nadia was really digging into the death of Abby (Madison Wolfe).

“I did always know that she was not who she says she is. I always knew what she was actually digging for,” Matula, 38, exclusively told Us. “But what I didn’t know is the lengths that she would go to get it. I didn’t know where her moral compass lied.”

The actress, who pulled inspiration from Shiri Appleby‘s role in UnReal, was excited to explore Nadia’s role in season 2, adding, “She has a firmer moral compass than any of these other women do. But then, as we were coming out, I was like, ‘Well, maybe she doesn’t.’ The hardest part for me of this whole shoot was getting those scripts week by week and being like, ‘OK, so I had set something in my head about who I thought she was, but now this script came out, and it’s actually turning that around for me. So now I have to make different choices.'”

Matula continued: “And as someone who is a classic overthinker like I am, it totally rocked my world a little bit. Luckily, I’m able to call [creator] Rebecca Cutter and talk things out with her. So that was really helpful. But yeah, getting the scripts week to week is hard. I like to have it all laid out in front of me.”

The twists kept coming when Nadia’s investigation ultimately led to her death.

“I had a feeling it was going to be one and done. I really went into it going like, ‘She knows too much. She’s digging too much.’ So I was not surprised,” Matula said about Nadia’s gruesome death in a car accident. “I kept being a little antsy for it.”

Matula recalled the death scene being quite memorable, as Nadia bled out because Margo (Malin Åkerman) and Sophie (Brittany Snow) didn’t help her — and ultimately ended her life.

“I read the script, and then I went, ‘Please tell me they don’t have sex next to my dead body,'” Matula joked. “‘Please don’t.'”

Nadia’s loss also upset fans, who found themselves rooting for her love story with Callie.

“It’s tough because you know they’re starting this relationship out on a lie and a secret. It does make it hard for her to connect fully at times, but also I think she just at some point doesn’t care,” she noted. “It’s nice for Callie to have this other romance and to have something so deep and genuine — and then to lose it. I think that’s going to be really nice for Jamie to play.”

Looking ahead, Matula weighed in on the chances of her returning — even just in flashbacks.

“That would be diabolical and really cool, actually. That would be really really fun. Let’s put that out into the universe,” she told Us. “Nadia has a twin sister, and she’s gonna come back for vengeance. I love that. Anything could happen.”

Matula isn’t ruling out anything, adding, “I feel really, really lucky that I’ve gotten to do all kinds of genres. Comedy has my heart. That’s really where everything lies. Every script I read, even if it’s a drama, my brain turns it into a comedy. That is really where I’m happiest is being silly. But I want to do everything. I don’t need any more deaths where I’m really cold and covered in blood. I’ve done it; I don’t need to do it again. I will, but let me do some more comedy again. I think that would be really fun [like something on Widow’s Bay].”

The Hunting Wives is streaming on Netflix.
