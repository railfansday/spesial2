The Stars Nearly Bared It All With Dramatic Leg Slits at 2026 LACMA Gala

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The 2026 LACMA Art + Film Gala red carpet was clearly all about the leg.

Numerous stars — including Candice Swanepoel, Alix Earle and Lindsey Vonn — brought plenty of fashion drama to the Saturday, November 7, donning designer gowns with sky-high leg slits.

Swanepoel, 38, and Earle, 25, each sizzled in silver Gucci numbers covered in fringe, while Vonn, 42, opted for a plum-colored gown for the occasion.

“It’s all Gucci baby,” Vonn wrote via her Instagram Stories of her ensemble.
