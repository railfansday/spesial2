Brandon Cisse named defensive rookie of the month for October

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Packers cornerback Brandon Cisse has been named defensive rookie of the month, the NFL announced on Thursday.

Cisse had 21 total tackles, seven passes defensed, two tackles for loss, an interception, and a sack in October.

He was the only rookie defensive back to have at least 20 tackles with an interception last month. He also led all rookies with seven passes defensed last month.

Cisse is the third Packers corner to win defensive rookie of the month, and the first since 2015.

In eight games, he's tallied 34 total tackles with two for loss, 1.5 sacks, eight passes defensed, and an interception.
