How Will Butler used Mr. Football snub to lead Green Hill past Blackman

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

MT. JULIET – Although Will Butler was left off the list of 2026 Class 6A Tennessee Titans Mr. Football semifinalists announced Nov. 5, he proved to be Mr. Everything for Green Hill a night later, leading the Hawks to a TSSAA football playoffs Class 6A first-round victory over Blackman.

Butler, a Swiss Army Knife for Green Hill, scored the Hawks' first and last touchdowns in a 21-14 win. Butler, who was a Class 6A Mr. Football nominee, played wide receiver, quarterback, safety, linebacker, punter and other special teams.

The versatile Marshall commit said he played with a chip on his shoulder after not being named a Mr. Football semifinalist.

"There was definitely a little grudge for me being told all week I got snubbed of it," Butler said. "At the end of the day, that's just a piece of hardware I won't get, but there will be others."

Green Hill (9-2) advanced to play Cleveland (10-1) at home in the second round of the playoffs on Nov. 13. Blackman, which scored with 2:26 remaining to make it a one-score game, finished with a 6-5 record.

Green Hill coach Josh Crouch was naturally pleased with the victory, but having Butler play such a pivotal role made it even sweeter.

"The kid is Mr. Football," Crouch said. "We don't need votes. We don't need people telling us who are the finalists, it's No. 17 for Green Hill High School. And what a supporting cast he has, too. His offensive line played great tonight, and we were able to close out that game."

Butler moved from receiver to quarterback with 4:59 left in the first quarter and ran off right tackle for a nine-yard touchdown through a swarm of Blackman defenders for the game's first score.

With Green Hill leading 14-7, the Hawks came up with a turnover on the Blaze 17-yard line with 8:33 left to play in the game. Butler moved back to quarterback and ran the ball four straight times before sweeping around right end for a 4-yard touchdown that put the Hawks up 21-7.

Blackman's Chase Bell threw his second touchdown pass to Max Wilcox from seven yards with two minutes left, trimming Green Hill's lead to 21-14. Then Butler, still at quarterback, carried around right end again for a 20-yard gain and then three more times to run out the clock.

Blackman coach Will Hester said he knew the Blaze had its work cut out trying to contain Butler.

"You've just got to try to tackle him and get him on the ground; he's a good football player," Hester said.

Butler said he is confident Green Hill can continue to rely heavily on him throughout the playoffs.

"We've done it all year through 11 games," he said. "I mean, we're still producing the same as we were at the beginning of the year."
