Aday Mara's breakout performance draws big-time praise from coach Mark Daigneault

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Ever since he made his debut, Aday Mara has been an impressive rookie for the Oklahoma City Thunder. As a 7'3" big man, Mara is one of the best pieces they have for the long-term future.

Mara has all the physical tools to become a dominant center for the Thunder, which is especially important because they have to play against the San Antonio Spurs that is led by Victor Wembanyama. In the most recent game against the New Orleans Pelicans, he had the best game of his career.

The Thunder are still one of the best teams in the league, but they are now lacking the depth that made them so good in recent years. Of course, this is due to the injuries that have happened recently, but they are thankful that Sam Presti has worked on the roster beyond the usual names.

They have also drafted well, which includes the likes of Mara and Bennett Stirtz, who have become valuable contributors as of late. Mara was superb against the Pelicans on Friday, getting a massive double-double of 22 points and 16 rebounds.

Since the Thunder need as much talent as they can get this season, Mara's breakout is a welcome sight. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander continues to be excellent, but having players like Mara showcase themselves like this is a big deal for the Thunder and Coach Daigneault.

"I think that's the thing that can be a little invisible, number one, is his intelligence. He really has a great sense for the game, even on the other end of the floor," Daigneault said about Mara and his impact for OKC.

"Number two, he's a big-time competitor. And I think sometimes the personality, which I said can be friendly and kind, can be misinterpreted. But that guy competes."

Mara will continue to be a fantastic player for the Thunder, and he will keep developing into a bigger name. At the moment, he is a rookie, but he is already showcasing potential to be a long-term piece.
