Rajan Warns AI Boom Faces a Reckoning on Returns

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

University of Chicago Booth School of Business Finance Professor Raghuram Rajan says the key question hanging over the artificial intelligence (AI) boom is whether massive investment in compute can generate returns large enough to justify the capital being deployed. He adds that as supply expands, weaker-than-expected demand could create oversupply and put pressure on valuations across the AI ecosystem. Rajan, who is also US Fed Balance Sheet Task Force Co-Chair and Former RBI Governor, speaks with Haslinda Amin on "Insight with Haslinda Amin." (Source: Bloomberg)
