John Harbaugh hints at Giants NFL trade deadline plans

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

New York Giants head coach John Harbaugh is signaling that the team could prioritize improving its roster ahead of Tuesday's NFL trade deadline, even if it means placing greater emphasis on the current season than future plans.

With the Giants sitting atop the NFC East at 5-2-1, Harbaugh believes the opportunity to strengthen the team now deserves serious consideration, though he made clear that New York will not sacrifice its long-term interests for just any deal.

Speaking about the team's trade deadline approach, Harbaugh explained that the Giants want to do everything possible to make the roster stronger for the current season.

"You want to do everything you can do to make your team as strong as you can right now," Harbaugh said. "I would say that the now weighs more than the future, if you're going to put it on a scale."

The comments suggest New York is willing to be aggressive as it looks to capitalize on its position in the NFC East. The Giants have exceeded expectations in Harbaugh's first season and currently hold a 5-2-1 record, giving the organization a legitimate opportunity to compete for a division title and a playoff spot.

However, Harbaugh also emphasized that the team must evaluate every potential move individually. The cost, benefit, and long-term impact of any acquisition will all factor into the decision.

New York has already been connected to several significant trade targets ahead of the deadline. The Giants reportedly pursued Tennessee defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons before he was ultimately dealt to the Chicago Bears.

They also explored a potential deal for Indianapolis Colts running back Jonathan Taylor. However, the Giants were unwilling to meet Indianapolis' asking price and reportedly pulled their offer.

Harbaugh has made it clear that the organization is not interested in acquiring a player simply to make a move. Any trade must make sense now and in the future.

The Giants also have limited draft capital available, making the price of a potential acquisition especially important. Still, Harbaugh's latest comments suggest New York isn't approaching the deadline with a strictly long-term mindset.

With the Giants currently leading the NFC East, the organization appears willing to consider opportunities that could help immediately. Whether that leads to another significant trade before Tuesday's 4 p.m. ET deadline remains to be seen, but Harbaugh's comments suggest the Giants are taking the possibility seriously.
