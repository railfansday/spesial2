Scoot Henderson returning to the bench in favor of Sidy Cissoko

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Scoot Henderson returning to the bench in favor of Sidy Cissoko appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Portland Trail Blazers' experiment to start Scoot Henderson amid the absence of injured Ja Morant appears to be over. Morant is still unavailable as he deals with a hamstring injury, but Henderson is moving back to the bench in favor of guard Sidy Cissoko.

The decision to get Henderson back on the bench has been made prior to Tuesday's game between the Trail Blazers and the Oklahoma City Thunder at Moda Center in Portland.

"After starting the last 5 games, the Portland Trail Blazers are moving Scoot Henderson back to a reserve role and Sidy Cissoko will get the start tonight against the Oklahoma City Thunder, sources tell me," NBA insider Chris Haynes of NBA on Prime shared via a social media post on X, formerly Twitter.

Over the last five Trail Blazers games, Henderson averaged just 9.2 points and he didn't shoot efficiently either. He shot just 31.6 percent from the field and was a 21.1 percent shooter from behind the arc on 3.8 attempts per game during that stretch. In addition, his 97.4 offensive rating in that span was second-worst overall on the team, and his minus-11.3 net rating was the worst overall.

The Trail Blazers won the first three games with Henderson in the starting unit but lost the next two, which perhaps contributed to the team's decision to start Cissoko over the former No. 3 pick overall. Portland is entering the showdown against the Thunder on a two-game losing skid and with a 6-3 record.

The Blazers guard Henderson, who is on an expiring contract, has yet to live up to the hype after being selected third overall in the 2023 NBA Draft, behind Victor Wembanyama and Brandon Miller. His recent subpar form and being benched for a second-rounder from the same draft class just aren't encouraging signs for Henderson.
