Bears call Colston Loveland week-to-week with a knee injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Bears tight end Colston Loveland went for tests on his injured knee after leaving Thursday's game in the first quarter and the team updated his condition on Friday.

The Bears say that Loveland is considered week-to-week as the team heads toward a Week 13 game against the Jaguars. They did not comment on the specific nature of Loveland's injury.

There was also no mention of injured reserve, which is a positive given reports earlier in the day that indicated Loveland was going to go on the list.

Loveland has 41 catches for 397 yards and three touchdowns. With Cole Kmet on injured reserve, the healthy Bears tight ends are Sam Roush and Stephen Carlson.
