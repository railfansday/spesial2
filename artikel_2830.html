NFL fans outraged after Rashee Rice catch gets controversially overturned in Chiefs-Bills

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Kansas City Chiefs and Buffalo Bills have contested some special matchups over the years, but unfortunately, controversy always seems to loom above the scoreboard. KC was on the wrong side of the latest scrutinized officiating decision.

Facing third-and-10 on the Chiefs' own 9-yard line, Patrick Mahomes threw what was originally called a completed 11-yard pass to Rashee Rice. The fourth-year wide receiver absorbed a big hit from trade deadline acquisition Amani Hooker and was credited with a catch. However, after reviewing the play, the referees overturned the call and halted the drive with 1:47 left on the game clock.

The officials determined that the ball had touched the ground and Rice did not maintain possession. Many fans around the NFL are up in arms, arguing that KC's playmaker met the requirements of a catch. The Bills ultimately secured a 13-10 victory in soaked Orchard Park, courtesy of a Tyler Bass 57-yard field goal.

While one play does not define the outcome, that type of conversion could have ignited Kansas City's offense. Mahomes and company still had an opportunity to win the game on their final drive, but the Bills forced another three-and-out. Nevertheless, tempers are high on social media. Fans frustration is not solely tied to this AFC collision.

People are once again lamenting the current state of officiating.

"Beyond ridiculous what this ref system does. Ludicrous," @HTTRQGC commented on X. "We went from the Tuck Rule to now not even knowing what a simple catch is. The league is BROKEN," @ShadyGrady345 opined.

"Do we think the nfl flips a coin on controversial catch/non catches? Feels like there's no consistency," @CaesarSZN remarked. "Indisputable evidence.Isn't that what is needed in order to have calls overturned? No way in Hades is this a clear and obvious incompletion that should have been changed. Unreal," @chief_il declared."

In a game where both teams combined for 471 yards and 11 first downs, one debatable call will obviously dominate headlines. The Chiefs' chances of securing the No. 1 seed for the playoffs suffer a massive blow. Moreover, they are still searching for their first signature win since demolishing the Denver Broncos in the season opener.

KC (8-3) can change that in upcoming road matchups versus the Los Angeles Rams (7-4) and Cincinnati Bengals (7-3). This squad has done an excellent job of drawing motivation from every source possible, and the Rashee Rice play might have just provided a little more.
