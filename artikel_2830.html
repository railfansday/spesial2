Como boss Cesc Fabregas admits he was 'angry' to concede despite breaking Italian away scoring record in 9-1 Champions League win at Lens

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Como head coach Cesc Fabregas expressed frustration over failing to keep a clean sheet even after watching his side dismantle Lens 9-1 on Wednesday night. No Italian club had previously managed seven away goals in European Cup or Champions League history before the Serie A visitors ran riot in France.

Fabregas saw his maiden Champions League away win slightly soured by a second-half consolation strike as Como recorded their sixth consecutive victory across all competitions. Having beaten RB Leipzig and Manchester United 4-1 at home either side of a 3-2 loss at Feyenoord, the visitors climbed to fourth in the standings despite missing Moise Kean, Trevoh Chalobah, Assane Diao, Jesus Rodriguez and Jayden Addai.

Tournament top scorer Tasos Douvikas netted a treble to score in all four European outings, while Nico Paz, Lucas Da Cunha, Marc-Oliver Kempf, Mattia Liberali, Martin Baturina and 18-year-old debutant Matteo Papaccioli also struck.

Speaking to Sky Sport Italia about sitting fourth midway through the league phase, Fabregas smiled: "It is also our fourth ever Champions League game, so four is the key number here.

"I'm happy, this is football and everything went our way. The lads did well, now we have to prepare for a very important match on Sunday, the ninth in a run between breaks for international duty."

Urging his young squad to stay grounded after their historic display, the Spaniard added: "It is a wonderful night for Como, for all our fans and many lads here who are at the start of their careers, but they need to keep their feet on the ground, be humble, keep learning and not think everything will go well from here on.

"They must keep working to continue these performances."

Despite almost every goal coming from slick passing moves out from the back and an early penalty against Maxence Caqueret being overturned by VAR, Como are still waiting for their first European shutout after conceding a 65th-minute corner volley.

Reflecting on that solitary blemish, Fabregas confessed: "I am angry over the goal we conceded, as we changed something in our defending with the three substitutions.

"Plus it was scored by a former teammate of mine, I played with Ruben Aguilar at Monaco, and I told him I'd never seen you score a goal like that before! It was honestly a great goal."

Como must now shift their focus back to domestic duties ahead of a challenging Serie A Matchday 11 trip to reigning Scudetto holders Inter on Sunday, November 8, prior to the international break. On the continental front, the Lariani will resume their league phase campaign by hosting AEK Athens and visiting Real Betis. Fabregas' men will then wrap up their schedule against Paris Saint-Germain before travelling to Barcelona on January 27.
