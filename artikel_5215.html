A software problem at JPMorgan Chase disrupted banking services across the US on Monday, delaying payments for customers at several other major banks. Chase said the issue came from a bug in an on-site hardware system and was not linked to a cyber attack. Because Chase is the largest US bank and handles a huge share of transfers between institutions, the slowdown spread to anyone sending money to or from Chase accounts. Downdetector reports peaked around 2 pm Eastern time before easing later in the day. Chase spokesperson Joe Evangelisti said the problem was not systemic, and other banks were only affected when dealing with Chase. Transactions were posting normally again by about 6:30 pm Eastern. Customers were advised to check recent payments and contact billers if any due payments were delayed.
