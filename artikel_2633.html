Tracking Drake Maye Injury Updates: What We Know So Far About Patriots' QB Ankle Injury Timeline

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Tracking Drake Maye Injury Updates: What We Know So Far About Patriots' QB Ankle Injury Timeline originally appeared on NESN. Add NESN as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Patriots are holding their breath after Drake Maye sustained what appears to be a significant leg injury on the first drive Sunday against the Lions. As the Patriots' offense completely sputtered without him, Maye was evaluated in the locker room to see the full extent of the injury.

As the Patriots head to their bye week in Week 11, New England could have to face the fact that Maye misses a chunk of time that would alter the team's season.

New England is one of several teams in the mix for the final AFC wild card spot, but Maye's injury could completely change the complexion of that race.

Here's more on Maye's injury and his expected recovery timeline.

Maye suffered a serious right leg injury that will force him to miss significant time, perhaps ending his season. It's unclear the full extent of the injury, but Maye will be further evaluated and expected to miss most of the rest of the season.

As Maye got injured when Aidan Hutchinson rolled on his ankle, the initial concern was that Maye suffered a sprained ankle. However, Adam Schefter reports the Patriots fear that Maye has a fractured ankle, which could require surgery.

On Monday, head coach Mike Vrabel declined to give a firm timetable for Maye's return.

"We'll have to see what comes back and what he looks like when he's back here and make sure we're doing what's best for him and his health," Vrabel said.

Speaking with the media on Monday afternoon, Vrabel wouldn't commit to a full timeline, just saying that he doesn't expect Maye to start in Week 12 after the bye week.

On Wednesday, ESPN's Adam Schefter gave a little more information regarding Maye, but the prognosis remained the same.

"I think that he's got some type of fracture in his ankle, I think that he's going to be out indefinitely, if not for the season," Schefter said. "It's a devastating injury for them, a brutal blow for him, the team."

With the Patriots on a bye week, Mike Vrabel and the rest of the team haven't been in the building to answer questions from the media. However, Vrabel did address Maye's health on a Patriots.com video, where the head coach acknowledged the quarterback will be out for a bit.

"I think he's going to be unavailable for the short time and we'll see how that goes down the road," Vrabel said. "But we'll have to prepare to win without Drake in the short term."

On Friday, Schefter finally reported that Maye suffered a fractured tibia that will sideline him for most of, if not the entire rest of the regular season.

Maye's recovery timeline will depend on his full diagnosis and the rehab process that follows. If Maye did fracture his ankle, he will be out for most, if not all, of the regular season, as he needs to wait for the fracture to heal before returning to the field.

However, if Maye needs surgery, then the quarterback will surely miss the rest of the season and shift his focus to the offseason. There are essentially two months left in the NFL regular season, which is probably only enough time for Maye to return if he avoids surgery.
