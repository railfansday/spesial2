Thioune concedes Bremen fortunate to draw Gladbach as West Ham loanee Füllkrug discusses potential red card incident

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

With 13 points from their first five Weserstadion encounters, sixth-placed Werder Bremen were the overwhelming favorites against cellar-dwellers Borussia Mönchengladbach on Friday. However, it took two goals after the 85th-minute mark to maintain their unbeaten home record.

"Today, we got some entertaining football," Daniel Thioune started his post-match interview with Sky Sports on a positive note. "In the end, you have to be happy if you equalize. Gladbach could have wrapped up the game earlier.

"We even had a chance to make it 4-3 at the end, that's crazy. That would have been too much. If you get a point based on your mentality, I'm satisfied." Thioune added regarding Cedric Itten's missed chance after Niclas Füllkrug's injury-time equalizer.

The Germany international extended his lead at the top of the scoring chart with his 11th strike since coming back to Bremen on an initial loan deal. The 33-year-old has 14 goals in all competitions, including scoring in every game at Weserstadion.

"I don't think Gladbach quite deserves their position in the table," Füllkrug praised the opposing team after the match. "The last few games have always been close. They played well against Dortmund, and they played well again today, giving us some problems."

Despite the late turnaround, the ex-BVB man wasn't satisfied with their own performance. "When you play the way we have been, expectations rise for yourself. Of course, I'm disappointed. We were far too sloppy, and lost possession far too often. We left room for improvement there today. We have to be honest about that."

Füllkrug was fortunate to repeat his Dortmund heroics because Felix Zwayer could've easily sent him off for a second booking at the hour mark. For the West Ham loanee, it was best to leave that for the professionals.

"I wanted to play the ball with the toe, I don't know if that was a yellow. I have no idea about that," Füllkrug said about his challenge on Philipp Sander inside Gladbach's penalty box. "The referee has to decide that. A lot of little things were called against us. We take it as it comes. And now it turned out that I was allowed to play the whole game – great!"

Werder Bremen's managing director Clemens Fritz also echoed Thioune and Füllkrug's dissatisfaction with the performance. "We didn't play well today. We struggled to get into the game, were too passive and made a lot of mistakes. We were nowhere near our best today, but we still managed to fight back. All things considered, we have to be satisfied with a point because our performance wasn't good enough to beat Gladbach." the former defender said after the game.
