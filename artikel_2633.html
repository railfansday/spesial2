Chargers' nightmare temporarily ends on MNF, but does it change anything?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Quarterback Justin Herbert and the Los Angeles Chargers found a bright spot in what has been a nightmare start to the season with a victory on "Monday Night Football" on Nov. 16.

The Chargers earned their first victory of the season with a 23-20 win over the Baltimore Ravens at M&T Bank Stadium.

The victory doesn't dig the Chargers out of the hole created by losing their first eight games of the season, but it provides some hope for further success this season.

The Chargers' win could be the start of some much-needed momentum on a short week, but also pushes them back in the race for the first overall pick in the 2027 draft.

Harbaugh and the players have remained committed to trying to win and can display that again at SoFi Stadium on Sunday against the 2-8 New York Jets.

"There's a lot of fight in this team," Harbaugh said during his postgame press conference. "Guys just battled, fought and made plays in all three phases (of the game). Thrilled. Let's do it every week. Good to see the smiles back on the faces."

On the field, the Chargers defense could have opportunities in front of them on Sunday to attack Jets quarterback Geno Smith, who is listed as questionable for the game due to an ankle injury.

The Chargers' offensive line helped make their own quarterback situation look better by keeping Herbert upright and providing him with a clean pocket on Sunday night.

He was brought down eight times the week prior by Houston Texans defenders as the Chargers' front line worked together through some growing pains, fielding a line of protection that consisted of multiple rookies playing together.

Herbert and the Chargers snapped a streak last week as well, scoring a touchdown for the first time after going 37 consecutive offensive possessions without scoring one.

This week, touchdowns came easier for Los Angeles as Omarion Hampton and Herbert each scored a rushing touchdown against Jesse Minter's defense in Baltimore.

Both contributed to the Chargers' rushing attack that finished with a season-high 160 yards.

Herbert also managed to have his first game this season without an interception. His 11 interceptions this season tied him with New England Patriots quarterback Drake Maye for most in the league.

Even with success on offense, the game nearly slipped away.

The Chargers led by as many as 10 points twice in the first half but were still met with adversity, holding onto a three-point lead late in the fourth quarter as the Ravens were unable to complete a Hail Mary as time expired.

Los Angeles' fortune had the team on the right side of a turnover as Chargers cornerback Tarheeb Still stripped receiver Zay Flowers of the ball when Baltimore was 10 yards away from field goal range and a chance to tie the game.

"That was just us being relentless and not giving up," Still told Sports Central LA after the game. "It just feels good to win."
