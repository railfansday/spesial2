Pochettino backs returning Shaw to make a difference for Man Utd

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Mauricio Pochettino says Luke Shaw must rediscover his best form after returning to the Manchester United squad.

Shaw is back in training following his recent injury and could be involved in United's game against Sporting on Wednesday, with Pochettino yet to decide whether the defender is ready to start or feature from the bench.

"All the players need to feel that there can be an impact for the team, and they can perform," Pochettino said.

"I think it's up to us after to give the opportunity to every single player in the moment that we believe that they deserve to be involved in the game."

Pochettino previously worked with Shaw during their time together at Southampton more than a decade ago, but the United boss stressed both have changed considerably since then.

"Of course, I know Luke from the past in our time in Southampton, but we all evolve," he said.

"We are not the same people now than more than 10 years ago.

"But always it's good to see him and I think he came from a small issue, but now he needs to recover his best form."

Pochettino believes Shaw's experience can still be valuable to the squad as he works his way back to full fitness.

"It's good to have him because his experience and everything with the squad," he added.

"But we'll see if he can be involved tomorrow or not, from the beginning or during the game."
