Who has made Troy's Premier League team of the week?

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

After every round of Premier League matches this season, BBC football pundit Troy Deeney will give you his team and manager of the week.

Here are this week's choices. Do you agree? Give us your thoughts using the comments form at the bottom of this page.

Konstantinos Tzolakis (Hull City): If you watched the Brighton game and wondered how on earth Hull won it was because Konstantinos Tzolakis was in goal. An unbelievable performance with five or six top-class saves. Big moments, big personality, he has stepped up every time. He's been one of the players of the season - I know it's a bit too early to crown people but if it stopped now he has to be goalkeeper of the season.

James Hill (Bournemouth): He backed up getting into the England squad. Sometimes when people get called up they start to get above their stations but he didn't do that at all. He was in a really good state of form. A cheeky assist as well but defensively solid. He has been in my team a few times now - a centre-half who does his job without complaining.

Malick Thiaw (Newcastle): He divides opinion but he played really well and got his goal.

Keane Lewis-Potter (Brentford): Two goals against Everton and that was before the sending-off of James Tarkowski. Performed well again. Sneaky bargain buy of the century to be honest.

Noah Okafor (Leeds): Two goals again. Leeds battered Chelsea and made them look unbelievably average. Nobody has been talking about how sneaky good Leeds have been, some really big performances this season.

Jack Rudoni (Coventry): Two assists in a must-win game against Crystal Palace. He stepped up big, fair play to him.

Elliott Anderson (Manchester City): The only person who actually showed a pulse in this game. He got a goal and showed pace and showed he wanted to be there. He tried his best to win a game of football.

Luca Williams-Barnett (Tottenham): Young man, a with goal and an assist. He just gave Spurs a spark we haven't seen for probably six or seven years now. He gave them that spark instantly and fair play to De Zerbi for putting him in. All the money spent, who knew it would be a home-grown talent in their youth team who would step up and really get the Spurs fans excited.

Florian Wirtz (Liverpool): Unbelievable performance. He has had a bit of a purple patch since Jurgen Klopp has taken over Germany. Did really well in terms of finding the gaps, drifting between players and finding through balls and now adding goals. Now we are seeing the player we saw in Germany and I am buzzing for him.

Oli McBurnie (Hull City): The man of the moment. He works really hard, he does everything for the team to make sure they are there. He is solid defensively but then he gets two shots and two goals. Top performance from him.

Rio Ngumoha (Liverpool): He made Diogo Dalot look like he'd never played a game before, not the fact he has played over 250 games for Manchester United and has gone to World Cups with Portugal and made him look childish, Ran him every time and twisted him inside out and got his goal.

Frank Lampard (Coventry): In the biggest game of the season so far for him he made sure he got the job done. Big win. No new-manager bounce for Palace as Lampard gets the job done and he made some big calls.
