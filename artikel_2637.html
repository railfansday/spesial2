Yellow Jackets receives first-round bye in D-III football playoffs

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

After capping off the regular season at 10-0, the Baldwin Wallace College football team will now turn its attention to the posteason.

The Yellow Jackets, who are making their first Division III postseason for the first time since 2003, are scheduled to open against the Susquehanna-Eastern in a second-round contest at noon Nov. 28 at BW.

"The team is definitely very excited about it," BW junior tight end and Lakeside graduate Jimmy Timonere said. "It's great for us that we got a bye week, since Week 1 was ours, so we've played 10 straight."

Susquehanna, which ended the regular season 9-1 overall, will host Eastern, 8-2, in a first-round game at noon Nov. 21.

The River Hawks, from Central Pennsylvania, secured the Landmark Conference Championship with a 6-0 and claimed the program's fourth consecutive LMC football title and fifth straight NCAA appearance.

Eastern, which is located in the Philadelphia area, secured an NCAA Tournament bid and its second Middle Atlantic Conference (MAC) Championship with a big win Saturday.

BW closed the regular season with a 78-21 win over Muskingum Saturday.

The Yellow Jackets finished 8-0 in the Ohio Athletic Conference, capturing their first OAC title since 1994 and first one outright since 1991.
