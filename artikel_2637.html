Kaleb Johnson bottled up by Rams

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Green Bay Packers RB Kaleb Johnson rushed eight times for 13 yards and caught one pass for 19 yards against the Los Angeles Rams in Week 12.

Why it Matters: The good news is that Johnson was the only Green Bay running back to carry the ball more than once. The bad news is that he found absolutely no running room. Johnson looks like he will be the lead back until Josh Jacobs (finger) returns, but even in a big role, Johnson looks like a matchup-based flex option. On the plus side, he has a decent matchup on tap in Week 13 against the Saints.
