Raiders vs. Browns Week 12 picks: Cleveland’s chances still being viewed as a toss-up

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Cleveland Browns take on the Las Vegas Raiders this Sunday. The Browns are a perfect 4-0 at home and coming off of a bye week. The Raiders have lost both starts with new quarterback Fernando Mendoza, but that also doesn't mean they're a pushover.

Here is what the national media is saying about the Week 12 game between the Browns and Raiders:

Why Gennaro picked the Raiders: Will the third start be the charm for Fernando Mendoza? In his debut as Las Vegas' QB1, the No. 1 overall pick sprinted out of the gates with 10 points in the first two drives vs. Seattle … but he appeared to run out of steam when the Raiders ran out of scripted plays. In his second go-around, Mendoza fell behind 17-0 in Denver before admirably rallying Vegas back to a seven-point, fourth-quarter deficit … but his late pick-six ultimately sealed the Raiders' fate. So, we've seen an inspiring beginning and a resilient middle, but how about a triumphant finish? I think it's coming on Sunday night. While the Browns have been one of the league's most pleasant surprises at 6-4, they're not exactly beating teams into submission. In fact, according to NFL Research, Cleveland is the only team besides 1-9 Tennessee to have been tied or trailing in the fourth quarter of every game this season. The Browns have relied on the late-game heroics of Deshaun Watson, whose six game-winning drives have him tied with Bo Nix for the league lead. In this contest, though, I see the rookie quarterback seizing the spotlight at winning time. Showcasing the clutch gene that he put on display time and again during Indiana's 16-0 national championship campaign, Mendoza forges a game-winning drive to log his first NFL victory.

This has been flexed to prime time because the Browns would be a playoff team right now. Plus, the Raiders have rookie Fernando Mendoza making his third start. The Raiders are playing consecutive road games, and Cleveland is off a bye. Big edge to the Browns as they win it behind their defense.

Fernando Mendoza survived the Seahawks' defense and then did all he could do to keep his team in the game in his first road start at the Broncos, save for one bad decision on a pick six. That should set him up well, along with the running game, to perform well in prime time. The Browns' offensive line will fold against the Raiders' pressure on Deshaun Watson, led by Maxx Crosby.

What an opportunity for Cleveland to continue its run in the AFC North. It marks the prime-time debut for Raiders rookie Fernando Mendoza. The rookie QB will face a Cleveland defense that ranks sixth in the NFL in pass defense (201.1 ypg.) The Browns are 4-0 S/U at home – all three-point victories. Tight end Brock Bowers (ankle) would help Mendoza here.
