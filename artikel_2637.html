‘Mike Vrabel Is No Longer Good for You’ — Analyst Rips Patriots HC’s Questionable Decision ‘The Night Before’ Lions Matchup in Germany

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Mike Vrabel's decision to attend a beer hall event in Munich, Germany, the night before the New England Patriots' game against the Detroit Lions has come under scrutiny after the team's disappointing 35-10 loss.

The event was meant to be a lighthearted moment with fans, but analyst Craig Carton questioned Vrabel's judgment and whether he is still the right person to lead the Patriots.

The controversy traces back to Saturday, Nov. 14, when videos of Vrabel at a beer garden event in Munich surfaced on social media. The Patriots coach was seen thanking fans and holding a large beer stein. The team's official social media accounts also shared footage of him tapping a keg.

The event was part of the team's international trip, but its timing did not sit well with everyone. New England entered the game coming off a 45-27 loss to the Green Bay Packers and needed a strong response against Detroit.

Speaking on "The Craig Carton Show," Carton questioned Vrabel's decision to attend the event the night before such an important game.

"Mike Vrabel's addicted to Mike Vrabel because the night before your international game, if you're getting drunk with Patriot fans or other NFL personnel and it's on camera, you don't think the players are going to see that?" Carton asked.

"You don't think the adult grown men making millions of dollars are going to not get together at some point and say, 'Why is it okay for coach to be out there drinking the night before a game? But I have a 10 o'clock bed check.'"

Carton believes Vrabel's actions could send the wrong message to the locker room, especially if players are expected to follow rules that their head coach does not appear to follow himself.

"With Mike Vrabel, it's never about what's good for the goose is good for the gander. With Mike Vrabel, it's only about what's good for me. And if you're a diehard Patriots fan, I think you've come to the realization now that Mike Vrabel is no longer good for you," Carton continued.

The criticism comes despite Vrabel's impressive first season in New England, when he guided the Patriots to Super Bowl LX and earned NFL Coach of the Year honors for the second time in his career.

"And, man, they've (fans) turned on him fast, and I don't blame them," Carton added.

The beer hall controversy would likely have blown over had the Patriots delivered against the Lions. Instead, New England unraveled in all three phases.

Quarterback Drake Maye, who ranks No. 4 in the league with a PFN QB Impact Score of 84.3, suffered a lower-leg injury after being sacked by Aidan Hutchinson on the opening drive and had to leave the game. Backup Tommy DeVito struggled to get the offense going, finishing with just 81 passing yards, one touchdown, and an interception.

The defense had an equally rough afternoon, allowing 494 total yards, 303 through the air and 191 on the ground. New England also missed 12 tackles as Detroit repeatedly found ways to move the ball.

Lions running back Jahmyr Gibbs was especially impressive, finishing with 252 total yards and two touchdowns. He alone produced more yards than the entire Patriots offense. Yikes.

The loss dropped New England to 5-5 and left the team with a lot of questions heading into its Week 11 bye. The Patriots now begin a challenging seven-game stretch, although the first matchup in that slate is a road game against the Los Angeles Chargers, who just picked up their first win of the season in Week 10 on Monday Night Football.
