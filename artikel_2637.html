Monday Night Football: Commanders rally for 22-20 win over Bengals

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Commanders have wilted in the final moments of games at points this season, but they didn't do so on Monday night.

After a 56-yard field goal by Evan McPherson put the Bengals up 20-19, the Commanders took possession on their own 28-yard line with 46 seconds left to play. Jayden Daniels hit rookie wideout Antonio Williams with a pair of passes that covered 64 yards and exorcised the demons from their tie with the Giants by spiking the ball with time left for Drew Stevens to try a 26-yard field goal.

Stevens knocked it through the uprights and the Commanders were 22-20 winners on their home field. It's the second straight win for the Commanders and the end of a five-game winning streak for the Bengals.

Daniels was 23-of-35 for 289 yards and the Commanders scored on six straight drives after opening the game with four punts on four possessions. The final five of those drives ended with Stevens field goals, but the Commanders dominated time of possession in the final 30 minutes.

Their run game had a big hand in that. Jacory Croskey-Merritt had 13 carries for 54 yards and a touchdown and Daniels had a 47-yard run to set up one of Stevens's kicks. Williams led all receivers with nine catches for 122 yards, but the Commanders remained in position to pull out the win thanks to their defense.

The Bengals were limited to 124 yards in the second half and they punted twice after taking a 17-10 lead in the third quarter. Joe Burrow finished with 300 passing yards, but the big plays that were the hallmark of their winning streak didn't develop against a Commanders defense that limited Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins to seven catches for 74 yards.

Washington improved to 4-5-1 with the win and they have to feel good about their chances of sticking around the NFC East race with games against the Cardinals and Titans on tap in the next two weeks. The Giants finished Week 11 at 6-3-1 and the Cowboys are 5-6.

The Bengals are now 7-3, which leaves them a game clear of both the Browns and Steelers in the AFC North. They split their season series with the Steelers and still have both games against the Browns on their upcoming schedule, but their immediate focus will be on next Sunday's game against the Saints.
