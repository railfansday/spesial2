In pictures: Thousands attend annual racing event

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Thousands of spectators have gathered for an action-packed annual equestrian event.

The November Meeting, which began at Cheltenham Racecourse in Gloucestershire on Friday, has also attracted the stars, including Olympic equestrian and royal Zara Tindall and actor Stanley Tucci.

A memorial bench to the late author Dame Jilly Cooper was also unveiled at the site, which was her favourite racecourse. Some of her family and members of the cast of TV drama Rivals cast attended the ceremony on Friday.

The November Meeting will continue for a third day with Carnival Sunday.

Braving the weather: Racegoers were not put off by the cold as they dressed up for the event.

Soaring: Harry Cobden rode horse Jonbon in the Shloer Chase on Friday.

Good spirits: Away from the races, spectators were pictured socialising and enjoying a drink outside at the event

Celebrating success: Richie McLernon waved to the crowd after he was victorious with horse Passing Diamond in the Paddy Power Arkle Challenge Trophy Trial Novices' Chase

Fun for all the family: As well as the races, the event offered circus shows, arts and crafts activities and food stalls

Iconic: The racecourse is known around the world for its impressive hilly backdrop.
