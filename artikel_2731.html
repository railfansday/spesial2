AFC playoff standings: Browns move up early in Week 10, down now only option this week

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Cleveland Browns Week 10 game versus the Houston Texans has huge implications for the AFC playoff picture. Heading into this week, the Browns were tied with the New England Patriots for the final playoff seed in the conference, losing out on the spot due to a tiebreaker, despite losing two straight games.

While we await kickoff in Cleveland's game versus Houston, the Patriots were playing the Detroit Lions over in Germany. Early in the game, both teams lost key players when DE Aidan Hutchinson injured QB Drake Maye during a sack, then injured himself while celebrating that sack.

While the Hutchinson injury is a big deal long term, New England's loss of Maye to an ankle injury all but finished off any hopes they had of securing a victory this week. The Lions ended up controlling the rest of the game, leading to the lopsided 35-10 final score.

With the loss, the Patriots drop to 5-5 and are a half game behind the Browns, who move up to the seventh seed. No matter what happens the rest of Week 10, Cleveland cannot move up any higher in the AFC playoff standings, with the rest of the conference's elites having two or fewer losses on the season.

If the Browns beat the Texans, moving to 6-4, they will be a game up on New England. If Cleveland loses, the Las Vegas Raiders and Baltimore Ravens have a chance to move into a four-way tie for the seventh seed, along with the Browns and Patriots. If that happens, we will have the official tiebreaker information after the Ravens game on Monday Night Football.

A Houston victory will help them climb just a game behind the final playoff spot.

A lot of "if" outcomes in Week 10. For now, the Cleveland Browns technically have the seventh seed in the AFC, with a chance to solidify that spot with a victory over the Houston Texans in Week 10.
