NHL rumors: Senators looking to trade veteran winger

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

NHL rumors: Senators looking to trade veteran winger appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Ottawa Senators are off to a strong start in 2026-27, sitting 13-6-2 and in third place in a competitive Atlantic Division through 21 games.

Not contributing much to that success is veteran winger Andre Burakovsky. The 31-year-old has gone pointless in eight games with Ottawa and hasn't played since leaving a 6-5 overtime loss to the Los Angeles Kings with an injury Oct. 29.

As Sportsnet hockey insider Elliotte Friedman reported on Saturday Headlines, the Senators are looking to move on from Burakovsky. Ottawa acquired him from the Chicago Blackhawks at the 2026 NHL Draft for a 2027 sixth-round pick.

Friedman added on Monday's 32 Thoughts podcast that Ottawa could include Burakovsky in a larger trade. The Senators are looking to add to a core that has played well despite injuries to Jake Sanderson and William Eklund, among others.

"I can't say if this is going to be part of something to add, or it's just going to be to move him and then create more opportunity to add," Friedman said.

Burakovsky has struggled since signing a five-year, $27.5 million contract with the Seattle Kraken ahead of the 2022-23 season. He helped the Colorado Avalanche win the Stanley Cup in 2022 before signing the deal.

The winger put up 39 points in just 49 games in his first season with Seattle, but his production dipped from there. He managed 16 points in 49 games in 2023-24, 37 in 79 games in 2024-25 and 33 in 75 games with the Blackhawks last season.

Now averaging just 11 minutes of ice time per game, Burakovsky has yet to carve out a role in Ottawa. His cap hit, declining production and lingering lower-body injury could make a standalone trade difficult.

One league executive told the Ottawa Citizen's Bruce Garrioch that Burakovsky is a "high-end talent," while another said "he's not that good a player."

The question now is whether the Austria-born forward plays another game for Ottawa as the Senators explore their options.

The Senators have just over $3.2 million in salary cap space, per PuckPedia. General manager Steve Staios has reportedly been looking for a top-six forward since mid-October.

Ottawa is back in action against the Winnipeg Jets at Canada Life Centre on Tuesday night.
