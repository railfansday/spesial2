Jim Curtis Shares Rare Post About ‘Love’ for His Girlfriend Jennifer Aniston

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Jennifer Aniston’s boyfriend, Jim Curtis, is expressing his affection for the Friends star in a rare public message.

Taking to his Instagram Stories on Wednesday, November 25, Curtis, 50, shared a picture of a cafe with the punny message written on its windows. The window text read, “I love you so matcha.”

While sharing the snap to his social media, Curtis left no doubt who was on his mind while reading those words, with the hypnotherapist tagging Aniston, 57, directly underneath.

The Instagram post comes several months after Curtis admitted that he was forced to adjust to a new level of fame after striking up a romance with the Horrible Bosses star.

“To be quite honest, that was something to get used to because I’ve been pretty public for a long time but never this level or this, kind of, segment,” Curtis said on the September 8 episode of “The Jamie Kern Lima Show” about making headlines with his and Aniston’s romance.

He continued, “I’ve never been in the Hollywood kind of trades, the press. No one has really cared who I’ve dated in the past. So that was an adjustment for me, but it was a real learning experience, too.”

Curtis noted that he learned to block out the chatter about his personal life, adding that he wants to focus on his work and helping people. According to Curtis, this is what “matters most.”

“Don’t Google yourself again, Jim, to see what the news is saying today. And that took like, you know, three or four months. And now, the kind of thing if a tree falls in the woods and no one is there to hear it, doesn’t make a noise,” he said. “I told my family, like, ‘You don’t need to send me the latest shot of me on a boat with my stomach hanging out’ type of thing. These are just private moments any couple have and [fame] just kind of highlighted.”

The couple have been linked since 2025 after meeting through mutual friends before their romance grew organically.

“We found out that we had mutual friends, and we started to just chat,” Curtis said on a January episode of the Today show while speaking about how the relationship began. “And it took a long time. We chatted for a long time, and we became close.”

Aniston was previously married to Brad Pitt from 2000 to 2005 and Justin Theroux from 2015 to 2018. For his part, Curtis wed Rachel Napolitano in 2003 before later going their separate ways. The pair welcomed son Aidan during their marriage.
