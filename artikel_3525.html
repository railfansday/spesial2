Denzel Washington's Déjà Vu Is Now Streaming For Free On Tubi

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Denzel Washington is a master in practically every genre. Whether he's taking out bad guys in action movies like The Equalizer, delivering Shakespeare in dramas like The Tragedy of Macbeth, or playing devilishly charming antagonists in films like Gladiator II, he can do it convincingly.

That has allowed the two-time Oscar winner to prove time and again the breadth of his abilities, with thrillers becoming a genre that he goes back to all the time. He's done some incredible action thrillers, like Crimson Tide and Man on Fire, as well as those of the crime variety with Training Day and Inside Man; it's a realm he's poised to return to with his upcoming movie with Christopher McQuarrie.

But a genre Washington hasn't explored as much is sci-fi. He took a big swing with Virtuosity opposite Russell Crowe in 1995, while he later went post-apocalyptic to make The Book of Eli in 2010. Between those two films, Washington made Déjà Vu, a sci-fi thriller that is currently free to stream.

Déjà Vu is a 2006 sci-fi action thriller by director Tony Scott, in which Washington plays the lead role of ATF agent Doug Carlin. The movie follows his character as he investigates the fallout of a domestic terrorist attack, only to learn of a top-secret time-traveling device he can use to possibly prevent it from ever happening.

Running 2 hours and 6 minutes in length, Déjà Vu is now streaming for free on Tubi. The ad-supported service makes the movie accessible to anyone without requiring a streaming subscription. The film came to Tubi on November 1, 2026, and will stay on the platform for the entire month.

Washington is certainly the headliner of Déjà Vu's cast, but it has a stacked ensemble around him. Paula Patton is the victim he's trying to save, while Jim Caviezel is the wanted criminal. Val Kilmer and Bruce Greenwood join the investigation as FBI agents, while Adam Goldberg, Matt Craven, Elden Henson, and Erika Alexander are among the other cast members.

Déjà Vu was the third of five Denzel Washington and Tony Scott movies, but it is easily the most underappreciated. Crimson Tide and Unstoppable are highly regarded, while Man on Fire is frequently reclaimed as an excellent action thriller. Déjà Vu deserves the same treatment after critics were initially mixed on the sci-fi thriller, which is why it has a 55% on Rotten Tomatoes.

However, Denzel Washington and Tony Scott's collaborations are always entertaining, and Déjà Vu is no different. It's a thrilling sci-fi movie with a strong ensemble cast, solid directing, strong visuals, and more. Denzel's underrated movie works a lot better than its Rotten Tomatoes score suggests, explaining why audiences responded so well that it made $180 million 20 years ago.

Unsurprisingly, Denzel's lead performance is a big reason why. He is as electric as expected, making Doug's desperation to save the day, pining after Patton's character, and intellect come across without sacrificing his charisma. He has a great back-and-forth with Kilmer, while his standoff with Caviezel is captivating.

Scott's direction keeps the film moving and packed with good action set pieces. The film easily could've become confusing due to how it explores time, but the director navigates the past and present seamlessly. And while the time-travel logic was a point of confusion during Déjà Vu's release, the rules established are not that different from time travel movies that came after and were well-received.

Now that Déjà Vu is on streaming, its a great choice for anyone to watch who is a fan of Denzel, Tony Scott, or sci-fi thrillers. Whether you want to watch it again or for the first time, just don't wait too long and miss the film before it is no longer streaming for free on Tubi.
