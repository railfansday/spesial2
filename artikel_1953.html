Arsenal plan to accept Viktor Gyokeres offer when replacement is in place - Transfer Expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Arsenal will only accept offers for Viktor Gyokeres if they believe they can find a replacement who will do a better job.

The 28-year-old has struggled for regular game time so far this season, and is no longer regarded as the first-choice striker in Mikel Arteta's side, raising new concerns.

So far this season, he has started just seven games across all competitions and scored just three goals, raising questions about his long-term future at the Emirates.

However, former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Gyokeres still has a role to play for Arsenal and will not be forced out unless a replacement emerges.

Arteta is happy with the Sweden international's contributions, and was full of praise following an impressive goalscoring display against Borussia Dortmund midweek.

Gyokeres, meanwhile, is working to regain his place in the Arsenal starting XI and is not planning to push for a move any time soon as he remains a strong option for Arteta.

Football Insider revealed last month that Arsenal's admiration of Nicolo Tresoldi could force them into a decision over Gyokeres' future, as the deal hinges on his potential exit.

However, they may opt against signing Tresoldi as they are content with the Sweden striker's performances when called upon, despite speculation about his future.

Should Arsenal locate a striker who they feel would be an upgrade on Gyokeres, they could allow him to move on, but that is not the case just yet.

"Viktor Gyokeres is going to be one to watch, I'm sure we'll keep on speaking about him," Brown told Football Insider.

"But from what I've heard, they're happy with how he's contributed this season and they're in no rush to force him out, they feel he can still play a big role in their side.

"Even if he's not the first choice any more, he's still a good striker, and Arsenal aren't exactly blessed for goalscorers in that position.

"The only way I can see him leaving is if they identify somebody who would come in and do a better job, but as far as I can see, there isn't anybody who fits that mould.

"So unless that happens, they're going to be happy to keep Gyokeres, and they're happy with how he's been playing when called upon this season."

Much of whether Gyokeres is allowed to leave the Emirates this summer will depend on the player's stance, and if he remains comfortable playing a lesser role under Arteta.

Having been pushed down the pecking order by the performances of Kai Havertz, there have been doubts over the striker's future, as he is keen to play more regularly.

Brown told Football Insider last month that Gyokeres could push to leave Arsenal if he is unhappy with his playing time, but for now, is working to get back into the side.

An impressive performance against Borussia Dortmund will have done his chances no harm, and a repeat of that display could ensure he remains at Arsenal long-term.

However, rival Premier League clubs are believed to be keeping tabs on Gyokeres' situation and could move to lure him away if they feel he could become available.
