‘Gilded Age’ Scores Early Season 5 Renewal at HBO

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

“The Gilded Age” has been renewed for Season 5 at HBO, Variety has learned.

The news comes after the period drama has aired just three of its eight episodes for Season 4. This marks the earliest renewal announcement for the show to date, which has continued to grow its audience season to season. Per HBO, Season 4 is averaging 5.9 million U.S. multiplatform viewers per episode to date, with episode three hitting 4.3 million viewers in three days time. The episodic average is up nearly 20% over Season 3, though there are still five more episodes to air. “Julian Fellowes and the cast of ‘The Gilded Age’ have once again captured the zeitgeist this fall, delivering another captivating, unmissable season, and we love how our growing audience has continued to embrace the series year after year,” said Francesca Orsi, president of HBO Drama Series and Films. “We can’t wait to return to 61st Street, as the Russells, van Rhijns and Kirklands continue to wrestle with the cost of ascent, while we expand the canvas and deepen the operatic stakes for these beloved characters.”

The show’s expansive ensemble cast includes: Carrie Coon, Christine Baranski, Cynthia Nixon, Morgan Spector, Louisa Jacobson, Denée Benton, Taissa Farmiga, Harry Richardson, Blake Ritson, Ben Ahlers, Ashlie Atkinson, Dylan Baker, Kelley Curran, Jordan Donica, Jessica Frances Dukes, Amy Forsyth, Jack Gilpin, Simon Jones, Celia Keenan-Bolger, Ben Lamb, Audra McDonald, Brian Stokes Mitchell, Debra Monk, Hattie Morahan, Donna Murphy, Kristine Nielsen, Kelli O’Hara, Taylor Richardson, Douglas Sills, Erin Wilhelmi, John Douglas Thompson, Taylor Trensch, James Scully, Dennis Haysbert, Maggie Kuntz, Neal Huff, Bonnie Milligan, Jim Gaffigan, Dallas Roberts, Andrew Burnap, Elizabeth Marvel, Adrienne Warren, with Bill Camp and Phylicia Rashad.

“It’s very exciting and deeply satisfying to see audiences fall more in love with ‘The Gilded Age’ every season,” said Erin Underhill, president of series producer Universal Television. “Our remarkable cast, writers – led by Julian Fellowes, and the entire creative team continue to make this world feel both timeless and resonant while keeping us on the edge of our seats season after season. We can’t wait for audiences to see what’s in store for Season 5.”

The official description of Season 4 states, “Bertha Russell (Coon) changed Society at a cost. Now, her family must reckon with the consequences as Agnes van Rhijn (Baranski) seizes an opportunity to regain her position. Meanwhile, Marian (Jacobson) forges a new path for herself, and Peggy (Benton) works to be accepted by her future in-laws. In this new era, you must be careful what you wish for.”

Julian Fellowes is the creator, writer, and executive producer of “The Gilded Age,” with Sonja Warfield also writing and executive producing. Salli Richardson-Whitfield serves as director and executive producer. Additional executive producers are Gareth Neame, David Crockett, Robert Greenblatt, Kate Churchill, Davita Scarlett, and Elaine Aronson. Erica Armstrong Dunbar and Holly Rymon are co-executive producers. The series is co-produced by HBO and Universal Television.
