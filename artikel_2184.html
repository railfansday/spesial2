Wayne Rooney leaves Jamie Carragher surprised after naming EFL club with 'horrible fans'

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Wayne Rooney once described Blackpool as having ‘horrible fans’ as he reflected on his experiences at Bloomfield Road.

The England legend is currently in Australia as part of the I’m A Celebrity Get Me Out Of Here as he continues his career after hanging up his boots.

However, Rooney is known to most people for what he did on the pitch, as he was arguably one of the best players the country had produced in decades.

During his glittering career, Rooney broke the goalscoring record for England and Man Utd, and he won every major honour that was up for grabs with the Red Devils.

With over 850 appearances in the game, after famously starting out as a 16-year-old, it’s fair to say that Rooney saw a lot over the years.

In that period, he would’ve played in some of the biggest and most intimidating venues on the planet, and on the biggest stage.

Yet, when he was previously asked on The Overlap about which club had the most ‘horrible’ fans, the 41-year-old surprisingly namechecked the Seasiders.

“As a player going… (a club) that had horrible fans was Blackpool. Honestly, we played Blackpool away a few times, and, like God, they were horrible.”

This was a surprising answer, as, given the rivalries that Man Utd and Everton have, you would have expected Rooney to name clubs that he came up against regularly.

FLW have ranked the 10 best EFL League One stadiums so far in the 2026/27 season, including Bradford City and Sheffield Wednesday.

But, Blackpool clearly left an impression on Rooney, although, unfortunately for the Seasiders, they don’t exactly have the best record against the former Birmingham City chief.

As a player, Rooney only played against Blackpool twice, which came during their year in the Premier League under Ian Holloway, with United winning both games.

Despite his team picking up three points, the game at Bloomfield Road wasn’t great for Rooney, as he was replaced after 66 minutes with Man Utd trailing 2-0 at the time, but they would turn things around to win 3-2.

So, you can only imagine the reception that Rooney received from the home supporters when he was subbed at a time when the team were getting beat.

United would then beat Blackpool on the final day of the campaign at Old Trafford, with that result relegating the Seasiders.

Rooney did also do the double over Blackpool as a manager, with his Derby County side picking up six points during the 2021/22 season.

Blackpool fans will look back fondly on their time in the Premier League, as it was a remarkable journey to get to the top flight against all odds.

Then, despite a lack of resources compared to most of their rivals, they put up a great fight, and Bloomfield Road was a great place to watch football that year.

Sadly, with the side now battling at the bottom of League One, those days seem a long way away, and it could be a long time before Blackpool welcome Man Utd and players like Rooney to their ground again.
