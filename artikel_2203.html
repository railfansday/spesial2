Hearts manager provides Calvin Miller injury update as casualty list climbs to 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Wouter Vrancken tonight attributed Hearts’ painful loss to Hibernian in the Premier Sports Cup semi-final at Hampden to the first half injuries that Tomas Magnusson and Calvin Miller suffered as his casualty list climbed to 10.

The Tynecastle club took an early lead against their Edinburgh rivals this afternoon when Claudio Braga netted at a corner – but Magnusson and Miller both had to be replaced before half-time.

Their Easter Road rivals fought back and triumphed thanks to Martin Boyle and Jamie McGrath goals and they will now take on Rangers in the competition final in Mount Florida on Sunday, December 13.

Vrancken, who was already missing Eduardo Ageu, Craig Halkett, Oisin McEntee, Harry Milne, Tom Renaud, Blair Spittal, Christian Borchgrevink, Rogers Mato, felt the enforced changes ultimately cost Hearts the victory.

“I’m really sore and disappointed of course,” he said. “Especially when you see how the game was going. It didn’t have to go that way because we had control with how we started.

“But it changed the moment Tomas was injured, that’s clear. And then afterwards with Cal. I’m not pointing out the guys who came on, but those changes took us out of our rhythm.

“We lost some connections and weren’t top in the duels. At first, we were picking up second balls and finding the right spaces. But then we lost those profiles and we were searching again. There were hesitations and that gave Hibs too much space.

“You lose the game in that 20 minutes. At half-time, we tried to make adjustments, be clear and try to take those hesitations out. It was much better and we dominated the game again.

“Unfortunately, we didn’t get rewarded. The players have taken a lot of knocks mentally and emotionally so you just hope they get rewarded for fighting hard. It’s a pity they didn’t especially in such a game."

Vrancken added, “We have 10 out. When you have those 10, you have a really good squad in the Premiership.

“I’ve never known anything like this. Nothing even close. I’m used to 98 per cent availability last year and that was with Europe. The year before, it was 97 per cent with Europe.

“Over the whole season, I don’t think we can have had 70 per cent at any point already. It’s really crazy. But, okay, we have to deal with it. Unfortunately, in an all or nothing game like today, we couldn’t make up for it.”

Miller was seen on crutches following the game, but his Belgian manager is unsure how long he is going to be sidelined for.

“I haven’t seen Tomas because he’s at hospital,” he said. “With Tomas, the medical staff did a concussion test on the pitch at that moment. He passed it, but when he came to me, he was going from left to right and couldn’t stand on his legs any more.

“When I saw that, there was no doubt in my head about letting him back on the pitch. He said, ‘I can’t do it’. Calvin is in a brace, but it’s difficult to say. We have to wait for a scan tomorrow.

“I don’t know how I make changes. We’ll see how it goes and who’s fit. Players will have to play out of position so we’ll see who has the quality to play in certain ones. We have to make the puzzle on Monday or Tuesday.

“We have to keep pushing the guys because when you go on the pitch, there can be no excuses, you have to take responsibility, go for it and try to win the game.  Like they did today in the second half. It’s just a pity the last 20 minutes of the first half which killed the game.”
