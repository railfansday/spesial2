John Cornyn blocks Trump judicial nominee after Senate shouting match with Ted Cruz

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Outgoing senator withheld ‘blue slip’ from T Elliot Gaiser, blocking his appointment to northern Texas district court

An outgoing Republican senator blocked one of Donald Trump’s judicial nominations on Wednesday, throwing a wrench in the party’s plans to approve the US president’s picks before Democrats take control of Congress.

John Cornyn of Texas, who is leaving the US Senate after nearly 24 years following his primary loss to a Trump-backed challenger, told the Associated Press he would not support T Elliot Gaiser, a 37-year-old official in the justice department, to serve on a district court in northern Texas.

“He’s not from Texas. He hasn’t been vetted. And this is a lifetime job, and there’s better-qualified people to hold that job,” Cornyn said on Wednesday.

Cornyn’s refusal to support Gaiser came a day after he got into a heated verbal argument with a fellow Texas Republican senator, Ted Cruz, on the Senate floor over Gaiser’s nomination, according to the Washington Sun.

The two men’s yelling match was reportedly so loud that those in the press gallery could hear Cornyn tell Cruz: “I don’t find you credible. I’m mad at you.”

At one point, Cornyn told Cruz he was upset at someone “for ending my career”, though it is unclear whether Cornyn was referring to Trump or Cruz.

In a statement obtained by Reuters, Cruz said: “If the ⁠Senate Judiciary Committee does not move Gaiser forward, the people of Texas will pay the price because it will effectively give the judicial seat to the Democrats, who will keep it vacant in perpetuity.”

Cruz said the White House indicated it would be “impossible” to get a new nominee confirmed because the FBI’s background check would take too long.

Republicans are trying to approve as many of Trump’s judicial nominations before Congress recesses for the rest of the year and Democrats take over in January.

But Cornyn withheld his “blue slip”, derailing that plan.

As part of a longstanding tradition, the Senate judiciary committee chair will only support a president’s judicial nominee when the senators of the state in which the nomination is occurring approve. That approval is known as a “blue slip”.

While Cornyn justified his decision by pointing to the fact that Gaiser is not from Texas, there has been tension between Cornyn and the White House after Trump refused to endorse Cornyn for re-election and instead backed Ken Paxton in the Senate race. Ultimately, Paxton lost to Democrat James Talarico.

Cornyn told reporters he recommended Steve Fahey, a federal prosecutor in Texas, for the empty seat on the northern district of Texas court

Gaiser ultimately did not appear before the Senate judiciary committee on Wednesday.

The justice department said in a statement obtained by the AP that it was “deeply disappointing that Senator Cornyn would choose political obstruction over advancing a highly qualified nominee”.

Kevin Roberts, the president of the Heritage Foundation – the conservative thinktank behind Project 2025 – called out Cornyn on social media for refusing to support Gaiser.

“John Cornyn is going out as the Republican Texas Senator who helped get radical leftist James Talarico’s agenda started early. What a legacy,” Roberts wrote on X.

Cornyn responded by calling Roberts “irrelevant” and claiming his “sycophancy has destroyed the credibility of a once great institution”.
