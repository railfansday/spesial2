Cardinals fire defensive coordinator Nick Rallis

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Arizona is making a significant change to its coaching staff.

The Cardinals have fired defensive coordinator Nick Rallis, according to NFL Network’s Ian Rapoport.

Rallis, 33, was retained under first-year head coach Mike LaFleur after previously serving in the same role under former head coach Jonathan Gannon. Rallis came with Gannon from Philadelphia, where Gannon had served as defensive coordinator and Rallis was linebackers coach.

The Cardinals have struggled mightily on defense this season, with the unit currently ranked No. 26 in yards allowed and No. 31 in points allowed. The team had offered little resistance in losses to the Rams and Chiefs in consecutive weeks, with both teams scoring 34 points.

Senior defensive assistant Teryl Austin will serve as interim defensive coordinator. He was Pittsburgh’s DC under Mike Tomlin from 2022 to 2025. He previously served as defensive coordinator for the Lions and Bengals.
