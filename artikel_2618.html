2026 CIAC girls volleyball tournament semifinal previews, predictions, tickets

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The CIAC Girls Volleyball tournaments have reached the semifinals.

All four of last year's champions are still alive in Greenwich (LL), East Lyme (L), RHAM (M) and Coventry (S) with chances to go back-to-back.

WHEN: Tuesday, 5 p.m.WHERE: Law High SchoolTICKET INFO: Tickets at GoFan $10 for adults (plus service fees); $5 for students, senior citizens (65 and older), military members with ID; free for children 5 and under.

HOW THEY GOT HEREGreenwich: Bye; def. Hamden 3-0; def. Warde 3-0Southington: Def. New Britain 3-0; def. Newington 3-0; def. Bristol Central 3-0

OUTLOOKGreenwich is the defending champion, unbeaten and has only dropped four sets all year, three of them to Trumbull and one to Ludlowe. Greenwich is deep beyond the senior trio of Harvard-bound Kayah Armstrong, Rider commit Heidi Zieme and Victoria Sullivan, who is headed to Franklin & Marshall. Junior libero Bailee Dayon has been excellent getting down for digs and fellow junior Sammie Tommasino earned MVP honors in the FCIAC final. Southington is led by senior outside hitter Lily Eidukaityte, middle hitter Meredith Barry and libero Grace Zehnder. Southington has reached four CIAC finals, winning its lone title in 2009.ERICSON'S PICK: Greenwich

WHEN: Tuesday, 5:30 p.m.WHERE: North Haven High SchoolTICKET INFO: Tickets at GoFan $10 for adults (plus service fees); $5 for students, senior citizens (65 and older), military members with ID; free for children 5 and under.

HOW THEY GOT HERETrumbull: Bye; def. Cheshire 3-0; def. Staples 3-0Maloney: Bye; def. Simsbury 3-0; Def. Ludlowe 3-0

OUTLOOKTrumbull has made two finals appearances and won a title in 2019. Maloney has a deep history in postseason, reaching 18 finals and winning 13 titles, but has not reached a championship game since 2002 when it won Class L. Greta Koscuk reached 100 kills this fall and along with Jenna Feitel and Tessa Holmes, leads the way for the Falcons. Trumbull has a dominating force at the net in Mckenzyi Bell, who is joined by fellow seniors Lauren Yakowicz and Adriana Castillo in the starting lineup.ERICSON'S PICK: Trumbull

WHEN: Tuesday, 5:30 p.m.WHERE: Middletown High SchoolTICKET INFO: Tickets at GoFan $10 for adults (plus service fees); $5 for students, senior citizens (65 and older), military members with ID; free for children 5 and under.

HOW THEY GOT HERERockville: Def. New London 3-0; def. North Haven 3-1; def. Branford 3-0East Lyme: Bye; def. Platt 3-0; def. Bristol Eastern 3-1

OUTLOOKEast Lyme returned six seniors from last year's Class L championship team, including Natalie Knowlton and Bella Albert as well as talented junior libero Julia Diep. The Vikings have won six-straight CIAC playoff games going back to last season, dropping just two sets in that run. East Lyme has played in five CIAC finals since 2006, winning three titles. Rockville is paced by senior Annelle Agyeman and sophomore Maya Smith. Rockville has never played in a CIAC championship game.ERICSON'S PICK: East Lyme

WHEN: Tuesday, 6 p.m.WHERE: Amity High SchoolTICKET INFO: Tickets at GoFan $10 for adults (plus service fees); $5 for students, senior citizens (65 and older), military members with ID; free for children 5 and under.

HOW THEY GOT HEREMasuk: Bye; def. Brookfield 3-0; def. Berlin 3-0Naugatuck: Bye; def. Fitch 3-0; def. Guilford 3-0

OUTLOOKMasuk has never won a CIAC title and has only made two appearances in finals in 2004 and 2005. The Panthers are led by Mia Tyler and Addison Scott, both of whom are performing at an all-state level. Across the net, Naugatuck only has one senior, but is led by juniors Maya Chatfield and Carly Luchina. The Greyhounds have one of the CIAC's longest championship droughts, last winning a state title in 1978 and last making a championship game appearance in 1981.ERICSON'S PICK: Masuk

WHEN: Wednesday, 4:30 p.m.WHERE: Plainville High SchoolTICKET INFO: Tickets at GoFan $10 for adults (plus service fees); $5 for students, senior citizens (65 and older), military members with ID; free for children 5 and under.

HOW THEY GOT HERESuffield: Def. O'Brien Tech 3-1; def. Northwestern 3-2; def. Watertown 3-1Woodland: Bye; def. Foran 3-0; def. Weston 3-1

OUTLOOKMany expected Woodland to be in this spot. Few had Suffield penciled in as a semifinalist following an up-and-down regular season, though it did reach the NCCC tournament championship game. Meanwhile, Woodland is unbeaten, won the NVL and only dropped five sets all season, four of which came against Naugatuck which is a Class L semifinalist. Ayla DeCrescenzo and Nora Nemec lead the way for Woodland which has reached three CIAC finals, winning Class M in 2013 and last making an appearance in a 2022 loss to RHAM.ERICSON'S PICK: Woodland

WHEN: Wednesday, 6:30 p.m.WHERE: Plainville High SchoolTICKET INFO: Tickets at GoFan $10 for adults (plus service fees); $5 for students, senior citizens (65 and older), military members with ID; free for children 5 and under.

HOW THEY GOT HERERHAM: Def. Watertown 3-0; def. East Hampton 3-0; def. Barlow 3-0SHA: Def. Goodwin Tech 3-0; def. Griswold 3-0; def. Nonnewaug 3-0

OUTLOOKThis is a contrast in CIAC postseason championship experience. From 2013 to 2021, RHAM played in six Class L finals, winning four titles. Then the Raptors moved down to Class M. Since moving to Class M in 2022, RHAM has won three championships in four seasons, including last year. Rian Calhoun has been stellar for the Raptors with 102 assists already in this tournament. On the other side, SHA has never reached a CIAC championship game, but the Sharks did just recently win their first SCC title. Bella Spinato and JJ Kirck lead the Sharks as they attempt to go where no team from their school has gone before.ERICSON'S PICK: SHA

WHEN: Tuesday, 6 p.m.WHERE: Plainville High SchoolTICKET INFO: Tickets at GoFan $10 for adults (plus service fees); $5 for students, senior citizens (65 and older), military members with ID; free for children 5 and under.

HOW THEY GOT HEREHoly Cross: Def. Classical 3-0; def. Morgan 3-0; def. Grasso Tech 3-0Coventry: Bye; def. Valley 3-0; def. SMSA 3-0

OUTLOOKCoventry is the defending Class S champion and have reached seven Class S finals since 2015, including the last three. The Patriots are led by junior Hailey Mayo and seniors Teia Ransford and Tessa Young. Holy Cross is paced by Amelia Mowad, Rylee Gray and Ava DePaulo. The Crusaders Have reached one final, losing to Coventry in the 2012 Class S championship. Coventry has won six-straight CIAC postseason matches, only dropping one set in that run going back to last season. ERICSON'S PICK: Coventry

WHEN: Tuesday, 5:30 p.m.WHERE: Bacon AcademyTICKET INFO: Tickets at GoFan $10 for adults (plus service fees); $5 for students, senior citizens (65 and older), military members with ID; free for children 5 and under.

HOW THEY GOT HERELyme-Old Lyme: Def. Innovation 3-0; def. Ellis Tech 3-0; def. Derby 3-1Hale Ray: Bye; def. Lyman Memorial 3-0; def. North Branford 3-0

OUTLOOKNo. 22 Lyme-Old Lyme is the lowest seeded team remaining in any of the CIAC semifinals for volleyball. The Wildcats were 8-11 this season and have just one senior on the roster. Hale-Ray twice beat LOL this season, both times by scores of 3-0. The Noises, which only have one loss to Class LL NFA, have not won a CIAC title since 1982 but are led by talented players like Reagan Bean, Katy Hagerty and Fenway Jankowski. Hale-Ray lost in the semifinals last season and has reached three finals, most recently losing in five sets to East Hampton in 2022.ERICSON'S PICK: Hale-Ray
