Commanders vs. Bengals odds: Opening lines for Week 11 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Washington Commanders are 3-5-1 after a Week 10 Thursday Night Football win over the New York Giants. The Dallas Cowboys also lost on Sunday which is good news for the Commanders as their stock in the NFC East continues to rise. They are now a half game behind the Cowboys and two games back from the division-leading Giants. Winning the division is the only realistic path for Washington to make the playoffs this season, and every game is important towards that goal over the final 8 games.

Washington is back home this week, and they're playing in primetime again. The Cincinnati Bengals are coming to Northwest Stadium for another Jayden Daniels vs Joe Burrow Monday Night Football showdown. The two former LSU QBs famously squared off against each other during Daniels' rookie season in 2024, with Jayden leading the Commanders to a win during a breakout performance. The Bengals are also coming off a big primetime division game that saw them beat the Steelers 38-18 to take control of the AFC North at 7-2.
