Steelers vs Eagles halftime takeaways

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The first half of the Pittsburgh Steelers and Philadelphia Eagles looks a lot like last week's game against the Cincinnati Bengals. The Steelers look flat, listless and uninspired and the Eagles are playing hard for a team with only two wins. Here are our big takeaways as the Eagles lead the Steelers 13-3 at halftime. Here are our halftime takeaways.

It's no coincidence that the Steelers offense started looking much worse when the play of the offensive line took a nosedive. And this was prior to the injury to Mason McCormick. This offense is exactly as good as the offensive line is and right now, this group is playing awful as the Eagles defensive front is in full control.

The Bengals last week and the Eagles this week are proving that when you are a one-dimensional offense, stopping them isn't overly complicated. The lack of threat in the passing game is allowing the Eagles to focus on the running backs, so until the Steelers can find a way to manufacture a passing attack, things aren't going to get better.

Defensively, the Steelers have looked better than against the Bengals but continue to be hamstrung by poor tackling. The offense is leaving them on the field and this puts even more pressure on them to play strong fundamental football.
