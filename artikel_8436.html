Tanggal: 2026-11-22

FM used a professional moving company over 20 years ago to move filing cabinets, etc., from one office suite to another. This time, FM is not relocating to another suite. Do landlords give the option of removing filing cabinets etc., for a fee payable by tenant? This would be more convenient for a tenant, but I do not know how inconvenient it would be for landlord! This building is operated by a property management company that has decades of experience is the industry and manages many commercial office buildings in the geographic area of FM. If landlord removes office furniture, FM would not have to worry about a junk removal company or charity damaging wall, door or common area.
Sounds like a call to the landlord would have great value at this time before doing anything else.
