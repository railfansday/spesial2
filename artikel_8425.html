My father is 82 and hitting that point - less good judgment especially on busy roads. And getting very nervous on highways.

True FSD would be a blessing for him. He is in good health and mentally sharp. He's very social. True FSD would maximize his lifestyle.

The Tesla UI is confusing to him, which I get. That needs to be solved. Examples - an app easy to use like apple maps, voice direction, or allow someone else to give the car directions.
Voice commands can do almost everything. The problem is there are 2 voice assistants. The original voice assistant and Grok which was added later. The Grok integration with the car was low until fairly recently. If Grok can't do something directly it often will put you on the screen where that option/feature is and highlight it.

The trajectory is Grok will take over as the voice/AI interface. An example now is if I ask Grok to open the trunk it says it cannot and will show me on the screen how to. If I use the Tesla voice assistant it can open the trunk. Grok can probably do 80% of car control now and I expect very soon it will be close to 100%.

With Grok you can give multiple commands in one statement and they can be pretty sophisticated. Examples: "Hey Grok, aim the driver's air at my face, spread it wide, and make the passenger cooler" or "Hey Grok, take me to Whole Foods and then to Walgreens and at Walgreens remind me to pick up some mouthwash and then take me back home."
