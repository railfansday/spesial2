Ryan Gravenberch is Liverpool's big loser so far, here's why he faces an uphill battle

Ryan Gravenberch faces a battle for his position and maybe even his future at Liverpool in the coming months.

The midfielder was dropped from the starting line-up and did not even make it off the bench as the Reds lost 1-0 to Arsenal on Sunday.

Andoni Iraola backed 19-year-old Trey Nyoni from the start instead, and he was rewarded with a strong performance in such a big game.

In fact, Liverpool appeared to lose their momentum and their attacking shape when the teenager was substituted for the returning Dominik Szoboszlai around the hour mark.

Gravenberch only signed a new contract in March, committing his future at Anfield until 2032 after two superb individual seasons under his countryman Arne Slot.

But the midfielder has been poor this season, and Iraola's preference for Nyoni could spell big trouble for the 24-year-old.

At times over the past two seasons, Gravenberch has seemed to be single-handedly holding the Liverpool midfield together.

The Dutchman failed to appear in just three Premier League games in that period, starting 71 of them.

Most suspected that it would be a similar story this season, despite the departure of Arne Slot, the manager who transformed Gravenberch's career after pushing him back in midfield to the number six role.

Yet Gravenberch has found it harder to break into the partnership of Alexis Mac Allister and Dominik Szoboszlai, and now has Nyoni to contend with as well.

The 19-year-old has become an instantly key player for Iraola, and may well keep his place in the Premier League.

Gravenberch can have no complaints - when he has featured, he has been inconsistent and mistake-ridden.

The 24-year-old was heavily at fault for defeat against Manchester City last month when he was sent off, and was fortunate that his side progressed in the Carabao Cup last week after his foolish tackle in the box gave away a penalty.

The Netherlands international now faces a battle for his future at Liverpool, with Nyoni not going anywhere.

In case things could not get any worse for Gravenberch, his social media activity on Sunday frustrated fans.

The Dutchman liked Jurrien Timber's post on Instagram, which included a picture of the right-back squaring off against Alexis Mac Allister.

The Arsenal man is an international teammate, but some would argue that Gravenberch could have read the room.

With the January transfer window approaching, the midfielder may already be mulling over his options.
