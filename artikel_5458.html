10 easy exercises that may help ease knee pain, from half squats to leg raises

10 gentle stretches and strengthening moves that may ease knee pain, widen range of motion, and take stress off the joint.

A sore knee has a way of shrinking a life. The competitive athlete sits out games, the weekend player skips the pickup match, and the daily walker starts finding reasons to stay home. The culprits vary. Overuse can wear a knee down. Osteoarthritis, tendinitis, and bursitis all make the list, and so do meniscus tears and sprained ligaments. Any of these conditions can turn a favorite pastime into something to dread.

Keeping a hurting knee still can seem like the sensible response. Movement can feel like the wrong call for a joint that throbs after an injury or aches from arthritis. Rest carries its own price. A motionless knee can stiffen, and the stiffness tends to deepen the pain. Gentle stretching and strengthening offer a different path. Such work may ease pain tied to an injury, surgery, or arthritis while improving flexibility and widening range of motion. The muscles surrounding the joint share the burden. Firmer hamstrings, quadriceps, glutes, and hip muscles can reduce the stress the knee absorbs. Anyone with knee pain should talk with a healthcare professional or physical therapist before starting a routine. Severe pain or other unusual symptoms signal a time to stop.
