The ranking of European top scorers

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

This Monday is the eighth report in the ranking of European top scorers for the 2026/2027 season. We thus find the scorers who shook the nets in Germany, England, Belgium, Spain, France, Italy, Netherlands, Portugal and Türkiye. Once again, it was Raphinha who finished first. Even if he did not score against Atlético de Madrid yesterday (2-1), the Brazilian has 15 goals in 11 matches.

For his part, Kylian Mbappé keeps the pace in second position. The French striker once again got the ball rolling against Valencia (3-0) with a new pawn. He is now credited with a nice record of 13 goals in 12 matches. The podium is completed by Vangelis Pavlidis. The Greek striker is now back with 11 goals in 11 matches. Yesterday against Estrela Amadora (3-2), he scored a new goal in Benfica’s victory.

Gift Orban falls short of the podium with a new goal against Galatasaray despite Amedspor’s 4-3 defeat. The Nigerian now has 10 goals in 11 matches. Fifth, Lamine Yamal follows with 10 goals in 12 matches. The Blaugrana winger did not score in FC Barcelona’s 2-1 victory against Atlético Madrid. Victor Osimhen is sixth with 9 goals in 8 matches. Injured, the Nigerian did not play against Amedspor (4-3) with Galatasaray.

Dušan Vlahović comes in seventh place with 9 goals in 9 matches. The Serb remains on a double with Beşiktaş against Gençlerbirliği (3-0). Just behind, Niclas Füllkrug has similar statistics. The Werder Bremen striker failed to score in a 1-0 loss to VfB Stuttgart on Saturday. Ninth, Sven Mijnans scored a resounding hat-trick yesterday against Cambuur (5-2) and has 9 goals in 10 matches. Tenth, Gustavo Varela marks time following Monza’s 1-0 defeat against Atalanta.

Erling Haaland (Manchester City), Lassine Sinayoko (Paris FC), Søren Tengstedt (Go Ahead Eagles), Brynjólfur Willumsson (Groningen), Wout Weghorst (Twente), Mohamed Salah (Trabzonspor), Luis Suárez (Sporting CP), Yassir Zabiri (Racing Santander) fail to reach the top 10 with also 9 goals. Nicolò Tresoldi (FC Brugge), Norman Bassette (Westerlo), Eldor Shomurodov (Başakşehir), Antonio Raimondo (Frosinone), Patrik Schick (Bayer Leverkusen), Ante Budimir (Osasuna), Donyell Malen (AS Roma) and Thom van Bergen (Groningen) follow with 8 goals.
