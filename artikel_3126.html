Apple Working on Own AI Agent to Rival OpenAI and Anthropic

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Anurag Rana, Senior Tech Analyst for Bloomberg Intelligence, discussed the latest from Apple with Bloomberg's Paul Sweeney. Apple Inc. is developing its own autonomous artificial intelligence agent that can get things done on behalf of users, looking to enter a fast-growing market already being pursued by OpenAI, Anthropic PBC, Meta Platforms Inc. and others. The company outlined the plans in job listings added to its website this week. In one, Apple said it’s seeking a senior engineering manager for a proactive, agentic AI assistant. The system will write code, browse the web, control apps and navigate devices on a user’s behalf, according to the post. (Source: Bloomberg)
