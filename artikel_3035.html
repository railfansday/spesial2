Medtronic: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Hugo's FDA clearance for general surgery opens multi-quadrant and hernia procedures, a second growth lever beyond the diabetes exit.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 24, 2026 at 11:20PM EST

On its second-quarter fiscal 2027 earnings call, held on Nov. 24, 2026, Medtronic (MDT -4.56%) reported organic revenue growth of 6.5% as operational and strategic adjustments yielded consistent results. Shedding noncore divisions while expanding high-margin therapeutic platforms allows the medical technology leader to concentrate capital where it earns the highest returns. Three key takeaways from management highlight where the business is headed.

Medtronic completed the separation of its MiniMed diabetes unit to eliminate lower-margin profit drag and reallocate capital toward high-return core franchises.

Electrophysiology Therapies (EPT), which treats cardiac rhythm disorders, delivered 19% revenue growth, driven by a 58% surge in cardiac ablation solutions, which pulled through high-margin catheter sales across an expanding installed equipment base.

Clearance from the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for the Hugo robotic-assisted surgery system for general surgical procedures enables higher-volume, multi-quadrant operations that drive hospital utilization.

Organic revenue growth is set at 7.25% to 7.75% for fiscal 2027, with operating margins expanding up to 90 basis points despite tougher second-half comparisons.

Expanding the installed equipment base drives steady, high-margin consumable sales, while disciplined spending protects underlying cash flow.
