Tanggal: 2026-11-18

U.S. sales surged 83% while operating losses widened for the period.

IceCure (ICCM +25.29%) reported an 83% increase in U.S. sales for the first nine months of 2026 as it expanded its commercial reach to 21 states. Management stated that total revenue for the period grew 26%, supported by increased physician adoption of the ProSense cryoablation system. The company advanced its ChoICE post-marketing study with initial patient treatments and new site activations while securing regulatory approval in China and a distribution agreement in the Netherlands. Financial results were characterized by an increase in operating loss to $12.9 million, driven by investments in the U.S. sales organization and ongoing clinical development activities. The company maintains $8.2 million in cash to support its growth strategy and clinical evidence generation.

Since initiating the study, we have now established three clinical sites, including West Cancer Center in Germantown, Tennessee, Adventist Health Glendale in Glendale, California, and Legacy Health in Portland, Oregon, and have begun enrolling and treating patients at participating U.S. centers.

We are also engaging with additional leading medical centers regarding potential participation in the study. We view ChoICE as closely connected to our broader commercial strategy. As additional leading institutions and physicians gain clinical experience with ProSense, the study can generate real-world evidence, support physician confidence, and contribute to a broader adoption over time. Beyond our commercial and clinical progress in the United States, we continue to expand the regulatory and commercial footprint of ProSense internationally.

At the same time, we continue to build out the scientific foundation supporting cryoablation.
