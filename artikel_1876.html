Arsenal monitor Antonio Nusa and Kevin Schade

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

According to Sky Sports, Arsenal are continuing to monitor both Antonio Nusa and Kevin Schade as they assess attacking reinforcements ahead of future transfer windows. The Gunners are still looking to strengthen their forward line after failing to secure several high-profile attacking targets during the summer, such as Vinicius Junior, Bradley Barcola, Morgan Rogers, and Julian Alvarez.

Arsenal are said to be particularly keen to progress their interest in Nusa, who has enjoyed an impressive season with RB Leipzig, contributing 14 goals and assists in just 15 appearances across all competitions. However, Leipzig are not interested in selling the winger during the January transfer window, making a move next summer more likely.

Schade also remains firmly on Arsenal’s radar. The Brentford winger has been tracked for some time, with Arsenal continuing to monitor developments while the Germany international discusses a new contract with his current club. On that front, however, the Gunners could face strong competition from Liverpool, with the Reds also aiming to add to their wide attacking options.
