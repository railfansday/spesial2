Laporta talks Lamine, Pedri, Flick, Rodri, Balde, Gabriel Jesus, Farinas: &#8216;He is a genius&#8217;

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

In a detailed interview with TUDN in New York (h/t SPORT), Barcelona president Joan Laporta has spoken glowingly about the club young superstars, singling out Lamine Yamal for particular praise.

The Barça chief also discussed Hansi Flick's influence, the team's ambitions for the season and the importance of maintaining a close relationship with the players.

During the interview, Laporta expressed his enormous admiration for Lamine, but also recognised that Barcelona have a responsibility to help him navigate the demands that come with being one of the most prominent young footballers in the world.

"There are a lot of home-grown players; a perfect symbiosis has developed between those who have come through La Masia and others who have arrived from different cultural backgrounds. That's the magic formula," Laporta noted.

"For example, Lamine is a 19-year-old lad, but at 16 he was very mature. He comes from the Rocafonda neighbourhood, which has sometimes been a tough area, but it must be wonderful because it produces talent."

Laporta in awe of Lamine Yamal. (Photo by David Ramos/Getty Images)

"There are lads from the neighbourhood who are very good, or from nearby, because they're at La Masia. They're 17-year-olds we need to help; they're mature but very young and full of life.

"It's an important exercise for us older guys – we have to understand how to build good relationships. I congratulate Flick and his staff. We have to put ourselves in their shoes," he added.

Laporta's admiration for Lamine goes beyond his personality. He recalled a moment at the training ground when the forward scored a goal that left him particularly impressed.

"He's a genius. I tell him that every time I see him. He's very mature for his age and technically brilliant. The other day we were at training, and he scored a goal, so we asked him how he'd done it," he explained.

"He's very creative. He's in great harmony with his team-mates, and they know they have a standout figure from the world of football among them. As long as he trains well, he'll keep improving,"

The president also addressed several other members of the squad, including Alejandro Balde, who has fallen out of favour under Flick this season.

"We need to get Balde back on track this year. He needs to rediscover the enthusiasm he used to have," he said.

Talking about other young players like Gerard Martin, Laporta added: "Gerard Martin is one of those players who always delivers. He's top class."

Laporta backing Balde to return to his best. (Photo by Alex Caparros/Getty Images)

The Barcelona chief also spoke highly about the club's summer signings, Gabriel Jesus and Rodri, saying:

"As for Gabriel Jesus, he'll bring us a lot of joy, not just with his goals but also in creating space. We also have high hopes for Brian Fariñas; he can play in many positions and did very well at full-back.

"And Fermin… he's as strong as an ox. I'm absolutely smitten with Pedri, and as for Rodri, he's brought credibility and experience.

"Bernal will develop even further alongside Rodri. He has a unique opportunity, just as Deco, Xavi and Iniesta all improved because of him."

The Barcelona president also outlined the club's ambitions for the current campaign. Having won two consecutive league titles under Flick, Laporta wants the team to make history by securing a third in a row.

"Only one team can win, and our standards are extremely high. I want to highlight the work done by Koeman and Xavi, who managed to steer the club through the difficult period it was going through," he explained.

"With Hansi, we've won two consecutive La Liga titles, the Copa del Rey, the Supercopa de Espana and reached the Champions League semi-finals. We're already there, and we haven't yet achieved all our objectives.

"This year the expectations are at their highest: we want to win the Champions League, a third La Liga title and the Spanish Super Cup; we also want to be contenders for the Copa del Rey," noted Laporta.

The Barcelona chief also credited Flick with helping the squad understand the importance of collective involvement, even when players are not part of the starting XI.

"Flick's role was to make the players understand that, with four or five changes, there are 18 of us who always play. We approach everything with such intensity that this year we're very focused on our objectives," he said.

"So far, we're on the right track. A third consecutive league title would be historic. As for how to manage the squad, Flick has very clear ideas."
