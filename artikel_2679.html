How Jose Mourinho&#8217;s Midas touch has helped Real Madrid win games this season

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

It has been reported recently that Jose Mourinho is concerned by the lack of quality provided by his squad players, as evident in the recent win over PSV Eindhoven.

This is hardly surprising if we observe the kind of importance Mourinho has placed on his squad players throughout the majority of the campaign so far, often relying on substitutes to try and change the course of the game.

Indeed, Mourinho has developed a Midas touch of sorts when it comes to making substitutions and sealing Real Madrid comebacks.

Substitutions have changed games for Real Madrid this season. (Photo by Denis Doyle/Getty Images)

As Real Madrid enjoy a streak of 10 consecutive victories across all competitions, it is perhaps fair to say that coming from behind has become something of a trademark for the team.

In four of those 10 wins – against Roma, AEK Athens, Celta Vigo and PSV, Real Madrid had to come back from behind to secure the win. In every single case, Mourinho's in-game decisions proved decisive.

The coach has been quick to deflect the praise onto his players, especially after the PSV comeback. He also refuses to get carried away, but the Portuguese tactician certainly deserves the praise he has been getting.

A deeper look at Real Madrid's four comeback wins makes Mourinho's influence even clearer.

Against PSV, the coach made some bold calls, switching to a Guler-Bernardo double pivot while introducing Dean Huijsen and Ibrahima Konate at the back after a disastrous display from Antonio Rudiger and Eder Militao in the first half.

Mourinho's substitutions were crucial in the win over PSV. (Photo by Angel Martinez/Getty Images)

This allowed Real Madrid to push higher and put more pressure in the second half, before Jude Bellingham and Kylian Mbappe, among others, finished the job.

The other three comebacks were not so different. Trailing AS Roma 3-1, Mourinho's double wave of substitutions, including Carlos Espi, Endrick and Alvaro Carreras, sparked three goals in just seven minutes.

Against AEK, the introductions of Endrick, Federico Valverde, Brahim Diaz and Espi were decisive in helping Real Madrid overturn the deficit.

The win over Celta, too, followed a similar script, with Valverde, Marc Cucurella and Vinicius Jr. all introduced to help turn the tide.

This goes to show that Mourinho has developed a Midas touch, which could be even more important as Los Blancos brace themselves for a gruelling December period.
