Report: Mike LaFleur stuck with Nick Rallis as DC due to limited options in coaching cycle

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

When the Cardinals hired Mike LaFleur in February, he was the last head coach to start his job in this year’s NFL cycle.

That had consequences for his first group of assistants.

According to Josh Weinfuss of ESPN, LaFleur initially wanted to move on from defensive coordinator Nick Rallis. But being the last one hired, LaFleur had limited options for a new DC and elected to retain Rallis.

Weinfuss quoted a source as saying, “Nick was the [option] that made the most sense.”

But Rallis’ time in the role has come to an end, with the Cardinals firing him on Monday.

Arizona is currently No. 31 in points allowed and No. 26 in yards allowed this season.

Teryl Austin will take over as interim defensive coordinator. LaFleur noted he’s focused on the present when it comes to Austin becoming a candidate for the full-time role.
