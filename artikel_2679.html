Donovan Mitchell would prefer league cancel game against Mavs: 'Me, personally, yeah for sure'

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Despite the disrupted travel, with a now scheduled 8 a.m. Saturday morning bus ride to the airport and 9 a.m. flight out of Miami, a source told cleveland.com the plan is to play Saturday night's game as scheduled. The Cavs will host the Dallas Mavericks at Rocket Arena for a 7:30 p.m. tipoff — a chaotic schedule set-up that was not popular in Cleveland's locker room. "This is unprecedented," star guard Donovan Mitchell said following the 133-121 loss to the Heat. "Back-to-back. Three-hour flight. It was already going to be a tough one. But if they say we have to play then we have to play." So, would Mitchell prefer the league cancel Saturday's game against the Mavericks? "Me, personally, yeah for sure," Mitchell said.
