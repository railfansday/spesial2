Golden Knights stumble badly in 6-3 loss to lowly Canucks

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

LAS VEGAS — There are losses. Then there are bad losses.Then there are the atrocious losses. The ones that leave you shaking your head and asking "How is this possible?"The Vegas Golden Knights may have suffered one of the worst defeats in their 10-year existence Sunday night at T-Mobile Arena in dropping a 6-3 decision to the lowly Vancouver Canucks. You thought the Raiders' second half against Seattle earlier in the day was horrendous? Your should have witnessed the Golden Knights' performance against Vancouver. Pitiful would have been kind. But we'll save that for the garish green, red, black and gold Remix jerseys the team sported for the first time.When you sleepwalk through the first 20 minutes, spot the worst team in the league a 4-1 second-period lead, then expend a lot of energy to get back in it before you don't finish the job, you wind up with an embarrassing L on your ledger.Lucky threads? I think not.The Canucks have been so abysmal, having won just three games so far and have seven points total as the worst of the NHL's 32 teams. But for one night, they put it all together, withstanding an early third-period push by Vegas and leaving town with what was only Vancouver's fourth win of the season and first in regulation since Oct. 3."The game was decided when the puck was dropped," Knights coach Ryan Craig said. "We were chasing from early in the game, down 1-0."We did some good things at the start. Later we got it to 2-1 and 4-3 early ink the third. But chasing's hard. And in this league, anybody can beat anybody."The lone saving grace for the Knights? Mark Stone tied the franchise record for points when he scored on the power play in the third period which at the time cut Vancouver's lead to 4-3. But 24 seconds later it was back to a two-goal deficit when Drew O'Connor beat Carter Hart in close.Stone and Jonathan Marchessault each have 417 points with the Golden Knights. He'll get a chance to pass Marchessault Tuesday when the Knights host Dallas in the first of back-to-back home games with the Stars.But Stone's individual accomplishment was of little solace given two points were simply squandered by Vegas after failing to start the game on time and chasing it for pretty much the entire 60 minutes. The Knights were huge betting favorites at minus-475 at puck drop at William Hill, yet fell behind 1-0 just 5:20 in as Elias Pettersson scored the first of his two goals.Craig had decided to bring Ivan Barbashev back to the top line with Stone and Jack Eichel even though Barbashev, Tomas Hertl and Victor Olofsson had been clicking as the team's third line. He also sat Marc Gatcomb for Raphael Lavoie on the fourth line and stuck with his goaltender rotation of Hart playing instead of running Adin Hill back.Perhaps he would have been better served leaving well enough alone and maintaining the status quo. You want to rotate goalies? Fine. That's understandable. But to mess with what has been your most productive line of late?"Sometimes you look at matchups and Trevor had played well with Hertl and Olofsson and Barbie obviously has had time with Jack and Stoney," Craig said of his decision to shake things up.By the middle of the second period Barbashev was back with Hertl and Olofsson, the latter of who had scored to make it 2-1 in the first period on the power play. But the real backbreaker may have been Filip Chytil's shorthanded breakaway goal that gave Vancouver a 4-1 lead. The former New York Ranger broke into the clear, deked Hart and tucked the puck in the net for his second of the night and of the season.Yes, Vegas got within one early in the third, but O'Connor's goal was almost as damaging in terms of momentum. In the end, it doesn't matter. Your don't come out ready to play and not respect your opponent, no matter how hapless they seem to be, this is what you get."We had a great opportunity to get back in it and then we give up a goal right back," Craig said. "That makes it tough. We'll address it and get ready for Dallas."
