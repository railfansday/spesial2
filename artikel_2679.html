Mike LaFleur: I don't buy narrative that you can't evaluate Carson Beck in current offense

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Cardinals are set to start Carson Beck at quarterback for the rest of the season and the rookie’s performance over the remaining seven games will play a role in how the team approaches the offseason.

Cardinals head coach Mike LaFleur was asked if that evaluation will be compromised by the fact that injuries have taken players like wide receiver Michael Wilson, left tackle Paris Johnson, and running back Jeremiyah Love out of the lineup as Beck begins his time in the NFL. LaFleur said that he will be looking at what Beck does rather than at what’s missing.

“I don’t buy into the narrative that you can’t evaluate,” LaFleur said, via the team’s website. “You can absolutely evaluate an individual guy. You can’t always go off the production . . . but with his first start, he did everything we asked him to do.”

Beck was 11-of-18 for 108 yards and two interceptions, so a lack of focus on production would seem to be a good thing for him when it comes to the evaluation process. At some point, that will have to play a role in how the Cardinals consider their options at the position in 2027 but LaFleur’s eye is on other things for the time being.
