Seminoles Find Their Stride, But Tougher Tests Loom

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Florida State finally delivered the complete performance coach Luke Loucks has been demanding.

The Seminoles wrapped up their six-game homestand Tuesday night with a 97-61 win over UNC Asheville at the Donald L. Tucker Center, earning their fourth straight victory.

"That was probably our most complete game up to this point," Loucks said after the game. "I was really pleased to see a lot of what we've been working on these last few practices translate to the game."

Sophomore forward Shon Abaev left in the first half with an injury after landing awkwardly while attempting to block a shot and did not return.

"It looked like he just rolled his ankle," Loucks said after the game. "We're hopeful that it's nothing more than a sprain. No timeline right now until gets tested."

Loucks said Abaev will undergo testing, and the results should provide clarity on Wednesday.

Abaev scored seven points, grabbed three rebounds and added three assists in ten minutes.

Four days earlier, he scored a career-high 24 against Green Bay. He ranks second on the team at 13.1 points per game.

Without 6-foot-8 Abaev, the Seminoles (6-1) still won, led by 6-foot-11 junior Sebastian Rancik's 22 points on 7-of-10 shooting. The forward entered averaging an ACC-best 20.8 points per game.

After the game, Loucks praised Rancik's unique combination of size, skill, and athleticism.

"He's a special player. I think he's going to play in the NBA for a long time," Loucks said.

The Seminoles shot 55.4 percent from the field, out-rebounded UNC Asheville 43-26. They turned 17 offensive rebounds into 29 second chance points. Florida State scored 46 points in the paint and 22 on the fast break.

To Loucks' delight, the defense delivered. Florida State forced 16 turnovers, collected 12 steals, and blocked five shots.

The Bulldogs made just 35.5 percent of their field goals and went 9-for-29 from 3-point range. No UNC Asheville player scored in double figures.

The Seminoles made 27-of-32 from the free-throw line and, for once, their 3-pointers.

After entering the night last in the ACC in three-point percentage, Florida State went 8-for-19.

Coach Loucks expected the positive regression.

"I was never worried about our shooting to the level some were," Loucks said. "I don't think we got a lot of Reggie Millers running around, but we're certainly not going to be 364th in 3-point field goals made throughout the year. It will balance out and even out."

The bench scored 35 points. Freshmen guards Brandon Bass Jr. and Elisèe Assui added nine each, while Martay Barnes had five assists and four turnovers.

No UNC Asheville player reached double digits. The Bulldogs went 9-for-29 from 3-point range.

It was the game Loucks wanted a week ago, when he called an 85-71 win over Ball State "1,000% unacceptable."

"I thought tonight was a good step in the right direction," Loucks said.

As December draws closer, the opponents get tougher. On Dec. 1, the Seminoles visit No. 9 Tennessee in the ACC/SEC Challenge.

"It's important for us to build our habits in all these games to have a chance to go into Tennessee and compete…"

Dayton, Texas A&M, and No. 1 Houston all follow before Christmas.

Updates on Abaev may largely determine how the Seminoles perform over that stretch.
