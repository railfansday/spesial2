Eagles to start Joe Milton in Week 11 against the Steelers

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Philadelphia Eagles head coach Nick Sirianni confirmed that Joe Milton III will make his second start of the season on Sunday against the Pittsburgh Steelers.

Milton is the third starting QB that Philadelphia has used this season. Starting quarterback Jalen Hurts remains sidelined indefinitely with blood clots in his lungs. Tanner McKee stepped into the starting role, but after going 0-3, Sirianni pivoted to Milton, whom Philadelphia signed off the Dallas Cowboys' practice squad.

In his first career start against the Giants, Milton finished 18-of-34 passing for 169 yards. He also added 64 yards on nine carries, but his effort wasn't enough to keep the Eagles from losing 19-11. Milton also lost a fumble.

While Milton didn't play well, neither did anyone else on the Eagles. Saquon Barkley found the end zone for a touchdown, but finished with 22 yards on 13 carries. Philly didn't have DeVonta Smith in the passing game, and rookie Makai Lemon finished with 61 yards on six receptions.

Tight end Dallas Goedert had 50 yards on five receptions. No other Philadelphia player had more than 25 receiving yards or three receptions. The Eagles desperately need a more consistent offense, and Sirianni is giving Milton another chance this weekend to deliver.

Philadelphia started the season with close wins over the Washington Commanders and the Tennessee Titans. After that, though, the season has taken a sharp downward turn. Key injuries to the offense are easy to blame, but the Eagles head into this weekend's matchup against Pittsburgh on a seven-game losing streak.

The Eagles are in last place and desperately need a win. Pittsburgh comes into this game coming off a loss to the Cincinnati Bengals. The Steelers are 6-3 and in second place in the AFC North, trailing the Bengals by one game.
