Seahawks injury update: George Holani suffers MCL injury in win over Raiders

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Seattle Seahawks entered Sunday's game over the Las Vegas Raiders pretty banged up, and exited Las Vegas even more banged up after their 21-13 win. Their injury-filled afternoon started literally on the opening kickoff, when running back George Holani hurt his knee on Jadarian Price's kick return.

According to NFL insider Ian Rapoport, Holani suffered an MCL sprain. The injury doesn't appear to be long-term, but we might know more before Week 12 against the San Francisco 49ers.

Holani had injury issues at Boise State, including a torn left MCL. He was on practice squad IR for an ankle injury as a rookie in 2024, then injured reserve again for a hamstring injury in 2025.

Seattle noticeably made Emanuel Wilson a healthy scratch against the Raiders, with Holani expected to be the third-string running back (potentially in two-minute drills, as we saw against the Arizona Cardinals in Week 9) and a special teams contributor. Unfortunately, the Week 10 plan went up in flames quickly, leaving Zach Charbonnet and Jadarian Price as the only active backs on the roster.

Seattle has Velus Jones Jr available on the practice squad in case the Seahawks need to elevate him to effectively assume Holani's role.
