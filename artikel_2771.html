Jets Stick With Reich After 9 Quarters With No Touchdowns

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

New York Jets head coach Aaron Glenn decided earlier this week that Frank Reich will keep calling offensive plays for Week 11 against the Los Angeles Chargers. This choice comes after the offense went nine straight quarters without scoring a touchdown. Glenn expressed strong confidence in Reich, but the numbers show a unit that is struggling to move the ball. The Jets sit at 2-8 and need a quick fix before their bye week in Week 13.

The decision to keep Reich in charge ignores the clear failure of the current system. Aaron Glenn changed his defensive play-caller last week by handing the job to Brian Duker. The same shift did not happen on offense. ESPN's Rich Cimini reported that Glenn still has a lot of confidence in Reich. That confidence does not match the reality on the field. The offense has failed to score touchdowns for over two full games while the defense tries to cover for them.

The stats paint a grim picture for the New York offense. In recent weeks, the team averages the seventh-fewest points per game at 19.4. They also average 316.5 total yards per game, which places them in the bottom half of the league. The squad ranks 11th in offensive success rate and 19th in EPA per play. Perhaps most damning is the league-low 38 percent red-zone touchdown rate. These numbers prove the current play-calling is not working.

Frank Reich does not have a perfect roster to work with. Geno Smith, aged 35, directs the attack while top back Breece Hall lost significant time to a quad issue. The team needs to score touchdowns, not just get close. The current approach has failed to solve this problem for nine quarters.

Glenn might hope that sticking with the same plan will lead to a turnaround. Ahead of their Week 13 bye, the Jets could be ready to make more changes if the offense does not turn it around against the Chargers and division-rival Miami Dolphins in the next two games. If the unit continues to struggle, the pressure on Reich will become impossible to ignore. The team cannot afford to wait much longer for results.

Other teams in the league have already made changes when their offenses stalled. The Philadelphia Eagles confirmed that Sean Mannion is still their play-caller, but he faces similar pressure after a loss of production. Averaging 16.4 points per game, the Eagles post the second-lowest tally. They also have the fewest overall yards per game at 260.2. Both teams show that keeping a failing play-caller is a risky move that can cost a season.

The Jets must find a way to score points in the red zone or face a long winter. The current 2-8 record leaves little room for error. Aaron Glenn stated he has confidence in his coordinator, but the league does not care about feelings. These are the facts that will determine Reich's future in New York.
