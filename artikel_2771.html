Latest bowl game prediction for LSU football after Tennessee loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Sitting at 6-5, LSU won't be competing in the College Football Playoff like the Tigers had originally planned. However, LSU is bowl-eligible and will play in the postseason, even if the game doesn't carry the prestige of the CFP.

CBS Sports' Brad Crawford predicts LSU will face Baylor in the Liberty Bowl. This would mark LSU's second bowl meeting with Baylor in three years as the two met in the Texas Bowl to conclude the 2024 season.

LSU hasn't played in the Liberty Bowl since December 27, 1985. That game was also against Baylor and LSU fell 21-7.

A Liberty Bowl trip would be disappointing given the Tigers' preseason expectations in Lane Kiffin's first year. LSU had hoped to make the CFP after signing the top-ranked transfer portal class. But LSU has not been able to close games vs quality teams.

LSU's final regular-season game is a road trip to Arkansas on Nov. 28. LSU needs to win to finish the regular season above .500.
