Ohio State WR Jeremiah Smith breaks Big Ten all-time receiving yards record

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Ohio State wide receiver Jeremiah Smith broke the all-time Big Ten receiving yards record of 3,788 previously held by Purdue's John Standeford. Smith set the new mark after a catch against Northwestern on Nov. 14.

Smith entered the game needing 31 yards to pass Standeford.

Smith set the Big Ten touchdown record in the Buckeyes' loss to USC on Oct. 31, a record previously held by Michigan's Braylon Edwards

If he chooses to forgo his last year of college eligibility after the 2026 season, Smith is expected to be one of the top picks in the 2027 NFL Draft.

Jeremiah Smith, Ohio State - 3,798 and counting

Taylor Stubblefield, Purdue - 3,629 yards
