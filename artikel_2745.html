Max Hurleman signed to 53-man roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Pittsburgh Steelers practice squad WR Max Hurleman was signed to the 53-man roster Thursday, Nov. 26. Terms of the contract were not disclosed. The team also placed WR Ben Skowronek (calf) on the Reserve/Injured list.
