David Lesky has his free agent predictions.

You can see that I have the Royals signing Ian Happ. It's not what I'd do, but their outfield defense wasn't great last year, and Happ is a great defensive fit. I have concerns about his bat and whether he'll be enough to help the lineup, but he was really good against righties last year, hitting .241/.358/.458. It does not help with their issues against lefties. He hit .145/.217/.267 in 144 plate appearances against them last season. It wasn't quite so bad in 2025, so it could've been a fluke. He hit .305/.350/.438 against lefties in 2023, so he has the ability, but that was a long time ago. So no, I don't love the idea, but I get it.
