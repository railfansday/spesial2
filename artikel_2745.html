Pittsburgh Steelers (6-3) at Philadelphia Eagles (2-7) at 1:00 p.m. Sunday

The Steelers are coming into Philly looking for a bounce-back win after getting beat up by the Bengals 38–18 last week, and they have been installed as 4-point road favorites. The total for the game is set low at 37.5. The bad news for the extremely banged-up Eagles offense (66 Health Grade) is that they will be facing a Steelers defense ranked No. 7 in opponent points per game this season. If you're headed to the game, don't expect much scoring from Joe Milton and Philly's offense.

Bottom line: When it comes to betting this game, it is tempting to take the home dog at +4.5 points, but can Joe Milton really outperform Aaron Rodgers in a must-win game for Pittsburgh to keep pace in the AFC playoff hunt? In my opinion, the Eagles have quit on the season and are just going through the motions at this point. Vegas oddsmakers have given them the sixth-worst odds in the entire league to make the playoffs, their odds of winning the NFC East are now 80-1, and their adjusted regular-season win total is set at 4.5 games.

I think the Steelers come into Philly and roll. Take the Steelers on the money line, and a 7-point tease with Steelers +3 and under 44.5.
