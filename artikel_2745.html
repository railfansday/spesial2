Kirk Cousins trade: Justin Jefferson fantasy managers dancing after Vikings’ blockbuster with Raiders

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Justin Jefferson fantasy managers finally have a reason to feel optimistic again. The Minnesota Vikings made the biggest move of the NFL trade deadline on Tuesday, bringing Kirk Cousins back to town in a blockbuster deal with the Las Vegas Raiders. Minnesota sent a 2027 3rd-round pick back to Las Vegas to solidify the reunion.

Minnesota desperately needed help at quarterback, as seen on Monday Night Football in the loss to the Buffalo Bills. Cousins gives the Vikings someone who already understands Kevin O'Connell's offense. He spent six seasons with the franchise and knows what it takes to lead this team. With Minnesota trying to keep its playoff hopes alive, this reunion could be exactly what the offense needs.

However, no player stands to benefit more from this move than Jefferson.

The superstar wide receiver got off to a tremendous start this season, catching eight of nine targets for 92 yards and two touchdowns against the Green Bay Packers in Week 1. Unfortunately, his production has fallen off considerably since then. Jefferson has managed just 23 receptions for 301 yards and two touchdowns over the team's last seven games.

There is no question Jefferson remains one of the best wide receivers in the NFL. The problem has been getting him the football consistently. That has been incredibly frustrating for fantasy managers who expected another dominant season from the Vikings' top offensive weapon. Cousins should help change that.

The two already have plenty of history together in Minnesota, and Cousins knows how to get Jefferson involved. Their previous chemistry should make this transition much easier than bringing in a quarterback unfamiliar with the offense.

Fantasy managers should have every reason to expect Jefferson's production to improve. Getting back to six or seven receptions per game with the potential to approach 100 receiving yards is reasonable. And it won't just be Jefferson who should see improvement. Jordan Addison, Myles Price, and Jauan Jennings should see better numbers; we will see. Who knows, even Odell Beckham Jr. could make an impact.

Cousins could even be in line to start as soon as this weekend against the Packers. There is some irony in that possibility, considering Green Bay was the opponent Cousins faced when he suffered his torn Achilles during his final season in Minnesota.

The Vikings still have some questions to answer at quarterback. It remains unclear what the team's plans are for Carson Wentz, who could remain the backup until Kyler Murray returns from injury. Once Murray is healthy, Minnesota will have a decision to make about its quarterback depth chart. Keeping three experienced options on the roster would be an interesting situation, but that is a problem for another day.

For now, the focus should be on getting Cousins comfortable and finding a way to maximize the talent around him.

Minnesota has a challenging stretch ahead, with upcoming matchups against the Packers, San Francisco 49ers, Atlanta Falcons, and Carolina Panthers. If the Vikings want to remain in the NFC playoff picture and keep pace in the NFC North, winning at least three of those four games would go a long way.

Cousins' arrival does not guarantee a playoff run, but it could finally get Jefferson back to being the weekly fantasy difference-maker managers drafted him to be. After weeks of frustration, fantasy managers have every reason to be excited about what comes next.
