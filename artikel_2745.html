Bills can match their best record through 10 games, with a win over Dolphins

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The 8-1 Bills, barring a massive upset, will move to 9-1 today. It will match their best record ever through 10 games in a season.

Via NBC Sports research, the Bills have started 9-1 four prior times. Most recently, it happened in 1991. (They lost the Super Bowl that year.) It also occurred in 1990. (They lost the Super Bowl that year, too.)

It also happened in 1988, when they eventually lost in the AFC Championship to the Bengals. The first time the Bills started 9-1 was in 1964, when they won the AFL Championship.

Beyond the 16.5-point spread, the Dolphins are an ideal opponent. Buffalo has beaten Miami at home 10 times in a row. It's the Bills' longest winning streak at home against any team in franchise history.

The creampuff portion of the schedule ends today for the Bills. On Thursday night, they host the Chiefs. After what should be an easy win over the quarterback-less Patriots, the Bills visit the Packers, host the Bears, and visit the Broncos.
