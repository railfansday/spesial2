Cowboys vs 49ers: George Pickens proves he’s must-see TV

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

It was more of the same for the Dallas Cowboys on Sunday. As has become a constant throughout almost the entirety of this regular season, Dallas' offense had their moments while the defense got embarrassed time and time again.

The Cowboys scored 40 against a very impressive 49ers' defense that had not surrendered that amount of points in over a calendar year. Since the start of the 2023 season, Dallas became the fifth team to score 40 or more points in a regular-season game but lose the matchup.

While things were close to disastrous on the defensive side of the ball, the Cowboys offense was effective at times, but failed to capitalize totally during the middle part of the game. Several players had standout performances, but wide receiver George Pickens stood above the rest, earning him this week's game ball award.

After missing last week's game, Pickens returned with a vengeance on Sunday. Dallas finally got their star wideout involved early and often, and the results were stellar. Pickens finished the day with a career-high 11 receptions to go with a season-high 145 receiving yards. Pickens also had the first three-touchdown game of his NFL career, scoring more than half of Dallas' offensive touchdowns.

In the first half of the season, we rarely saw Dallas come into a matchup with an offensive game plan of feeding Pickens early to get him off to a good start. Finally, the Cowboys did just that on Sunday, and it paid dividends in a big way.

Moving forward, it's pretty clear the Cowboys are going to have to score 30 or more points in every game if they want to record any more victories the rest of the season. Following Sunday's game plan of getting Pickens involved early and often will be their best shot at keeping up the offensive success they had on Sunday afternoon.
