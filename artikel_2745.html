Justin Herbert runs for 22-yard TD, giving Chargers a 7-0 lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Justin Herbert's left hand seems just fine. So, too, do his legs.

The Chargers quarterback ran for a 22-yard touchdown, his first rushing touchdown since Week 9 of last season.

The Chargers have struggled to score this season, but had no problem moving the ball on their first drive of the night.

After the Ravens went three-and-out with new quarterback Jacoby Brissett, the Chargers drove 63 yards in only six plays. Omarion Hampton had runs of 11, 4 and 4 yards and recovered his own fumble on the second one.

Herbert was 1-of-2 for 22 yards, with Tre' Harris getting the only reception.

Herbert injured his left hand in last week's loss to the Texans, but was not on the injury report this week.
