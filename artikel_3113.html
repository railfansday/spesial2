BJ's (BJ -4.48%) reported third quarter results that surpassed internal expectations, primarily driven by a strategic decision to reinvest a onetime $240 million legal settlement into membership and stakeholder value. Management reported that the company utilized the settlement proceeds to fund its 42nd birthday event, which resulted in 6% traffic growth and the acquisition of 200,000 new members in October alone. The company stated that these investments were designed to accelerate the membership flywheel by driving shopping frequency and higher renewal rates. Furthermore, the company is executing a category management process to reduce SKU counts by 20% while reallocating space to high-growth areas such as snacks and protein. Management raised its full-year guidance for both comparable sales and adjusted earnings per share to reflect this operational momentum and the net benefit of the litigation settlement.
