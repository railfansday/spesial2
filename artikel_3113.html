Why Does Palantir Stock Keep Going Down?

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

This analyst just upgraded Palantir -- then Palantir stock went down. Wait. What?

Palantir Technologies (PLTR -4.52%) stock dropped another 3.7% through 2 p.m. ET Monday, its third straight trading day of losses. And why?

In case you haven't heard, multiple stories emerged alleging affairs by Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang last week, leading to the CEO's resignation -- and panicking AI investors. No one's entirely certain why, with theories ranging from potential tightening of U.S. regulation on semiconductor exports to worries Nvidia will have to stop financing its own customers' chip purchases to simple fear that "Jensen Huang is magic," and without his presence, this whole AI revolution thing will fall apart.

And maybe that's true; I just don't know that it has much to do with Palantir.

Is Palantir an AI company? Sure. But it doesn't do much business with China (close to 0%, according to data from S&P Global Market Intelligence). Nor have I heard anything about Palantir subsidizing purchases of its own products, as Nvidia does. To me, this seems more like a case of investors knee-jerk selling anything related to AI.

Meanwhile, Palantir just won a better price target from Phillip Securities analyst Alif Fahmi this morning. Fahmi points out that Palantir has grown its revenue 92% so far this year, and will hold onto most of that growth -- 91% -- through year-end. Rating the stock a "buy," the analyst is raising his price target to $248 per share.

Is Fahmi right? Could Palantir stock gain 54% in the next 12 months? I don't know about that. But at 99x free cash flow and a projected earnings growth rate of 62.5% over the next five years, Palantir is nonetheless approaching my own buy-it-now price.

A little lower, and I might just buy Palantir stock myself.
