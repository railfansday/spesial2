NFL On The Future Of Netflix’s Thanksgiving Eve Game & Starting A New Tradition: “We Are Committed”

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Netflix is launching a new annual NFL tradition: the first-ever Thanksgiving Eve game. The inaugural game will see the Los Angeles Rams and the Green Bay Packers face off a day before the Thanksgiving Day tripleheader, giving fans more football to enjoy with friends and family during the holiday season.

Grace Senko, NFL Vice President, Media Strategy and Business Development, recently told Deadline how this special game came to be.

“As part of our scheduling process every year, the team takes a look at the upcoming year and looks at potential windows and thinks about how we can continue to innovate the schedule, try new things that we’ve never done before,” Senko said. “[Last year] when we played Christmas on a Wednesday, that was the first time, and then now it’s become kind of a staple throughout the season.”

She continued, “When we think about Thanksgiving Eve, I don’t think we think about Thanksgiving as just being one day. At least I know it feels like everybody in New York has already left a little bit. But for a lot of people, it begins on that Wednesday, already gathering with friends and family, and that kind of extends through Sunday.” Watch on Deadline

Senko notes that the Thanksgiving Day games “continued to perform really strong and really well” last year, adding that they averaged 45M viewers “and was the fourth consecutive year of viewership growth.”

“When you see that, and then you pair that with the Black Friday game that Amazon began distributing in 2023, and the really strong growth over the last few years there, it really made us question: you know, should we be doing something on Thanksgiving Eve? Is there a special moment that we can create there as well, and really, you know, continue to kind of build and expand upon the temple that we’ve already established with Thanksgiving Day?” she added.

Senko says that the game is “an extension of the Thanksgiving Day holiday” and “a unique opportunity that we had in our schedule,” adding, “We are committed to play on Thanksgiving Eve through the duration of our deal with Netflix.”

The Thanksgiving Eve Halftime show will feature U2 amid their 50th anniversary celebration tour. Senko goes back to the Christmas Day game in 2024, which was dubbed Beyoncé Bowl as the singer performed during Halftime, adding, “I think Netflix, at kind of the core of entertainment and culture in our country, is focused on making it as big of a possible moment and event as they possibly can.

Netflix is set to bring the Thanksgiving Eve game back in 2027 as part of their deal with the NFL. This game could potentially be the start of a new tradition for many decades to come.

“We’ll look at this year, but then over time we’ll look at the audience growth,” Senko said about the future of the Thanksgiving Eve game. “We’ll look at feedback from our partners. We’ll get feedback from the clubs, feedback from the various constituents in the organization, and evaluate if it’s a day and an event that we want to continue building. So that’s definitely there. There’s a lot of conversations that will be had, but a lot of different things that we’ll look at, whether evaluating this game or any other window that we’re playing in. That’s kind of new and a non-traditional window, I would say.”

Netflix’s Thanksgiving Eve game streams on Wednesday, November 25, starting at 8 p.m. ET / 5 p.m. PT and will feature U2 during the halftime show.
