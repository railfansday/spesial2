Tyler Posey Joins Netflix’s ‘Myron Bolitar’ Series Adaptation As Recurring Guest

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: Tyler Posey has joined Netflix‘s Myron Bolitar in a recurring guest role.

The actor will appear in three episodes of the upcoming series adaptation, based on the bestselling novels by Harlan Coben, Deadline has learned.

Posey will play a confident, handsome and athletic young man from an extremely wealthy and privileged background. The son of a prominent politician, he is accustomed to getting his way and has a history of physically lashing out when angered.

He joins KJ Apa, Colin Woodell, and Diane Guerrero, who will lead the series. David E. Kelley (Margo’s Got Money Troubles, Big Little Lies) and Kyle Long (Suits, Unsolved: The Murders of Tupac and the Notorious B.I.G) will co-showrun, write and executive produce. In the series, after an injury ends his NBA dreams, Myron Bolitar (Woodell) reinvents himself as a sports agent — using charm, smarts and a ruthless partner to navigate the high-stakes and dirty world of sports, where saving his clients often means risking himself. Watch on Deadline

Best known for his leading role in the MTV series Teen Wolf, Posey also appeared opposite Josh Lucas and Walton Goggins in Ash Avildsen’s Queen of the Ring, based on the book by Pulitzer Prize-winning journalist Jeff Leen. His recent credits also include Death of a Brewer alongside Mena Suvari and The Real Bros of Simi Valley: The Reunion with Jimmy Tatro. He will next be seen in the independent film Desert Flowers, opposite Kyle Mooney and Leslie David Baker, which recently wrapped principal photography in Los Angeles.

Posey recently returned to television with a pivotal arc in NBC’s Line of Fire, from Josh Safran. His additional credits include Gregg Araki’s Now Apocalypse, Netflix’s The Last Summer and Alone opposite Donald Sutherland. He has also lent his voice to several animated series, including Fast & Furious: Spy Racers and Elena of Avalor.

In addition to acting, Posey has also directed episodes of Teen Wolf and runs his own production company. He is currently developing his own feature directorial debut, Sober House, as well as the horror-comedy Scareplane.

He is represented by IAG, Luber Roklin Entertainment and attorney Rick Genow.
