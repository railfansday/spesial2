Travis Kelce Couldn’t Join Taylor Swift at the 2026 CMA Awards, But Here’s Proof He Was There in Spirit

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The NFL star is gearing up to face the lowly Arizona Cardinals on Sunday (Nov. 22), but he made sure his wife knew he tuned in to Wednesday's (Nov. 18) show.

As we swing into holiday party season, it’s important to remember that your significant other can’t always make it to every event. Take Travis Kelce. The Kansas City Chiefs tight end who is nursing a high-ankle sprain and a recent calf injury as he preps for Sunday’s (Nov. 22) showdown with the lowly Arizona Cardinals was not able to get a hall pass to zip down to Nashville for Wednesday night’s (Nov. 18) 2026 CMA Awards. But just because he couldn’t be on hand in person to cheer on wife Taylor Swift as she took the stage for her first CMA performance in 13 years for a run through her Toy Story 5 hit “I Knew It, I Knew You,” doesn’t mean he wasn’t there in spirit and in the likes. Kelce showed up in the likes for a post from the Taylornation account featuring the star-studded intro to Swift’s set.

Not to worry, even though the newlyweds were apart, Swift had plenty of company as she made her way through the tunnels at Bridgestone Arena in Nashville, bumping into Brad Paisley, Little Big Town, Eric Church, Lainey Wilson, Brooks & Dunn and Bill Anderson as she sang the wistful tune. She finally made her way to the stage in her Jessie the cowgirl-inspired outfit, where she was joined by a banjo-strumming Keith Urban for the performance that also mixed in a bit of her No. 16 Billboard Hot 100 tune “Our Song” from her 2006 debut album.

While the power couple couldn’t be together on Wednesday, Swift walked the carpet with Brittany Mahomes, wife of Trav’s teammate, KC QB Patrick Mahomes. The couple were also on host Paisley’s mind when he joked about Swift and Kelce’s star-packed wedding in July at New York’s Madison Square Garden.

“We have to talk about the biggest story of the year. Somebody got married!,” said Paisley, as Swift laughed heartily from her seat and leaned into Mahomes as she mouthed the words “that’s good.” “Blonde singer, football player … it was such a big deal,” added the country icon who attended the MSG nuptials with wife Kimberly Williams-Paisley.

“Hi Taylor,” Paisley said from the stage to Swift. “Remember me? I ate all the shrimp.”
