Sources: Miguel Sierra 'most likely' to join Leeds next summer

Leeds United's attempt to potentially sign Sevilla winger Miguel Sierra is "most likely" to come next summer, sources have told Football Insider.

The Whites have been heavily linked with the wideman in recent weeks, but the possibility of a move in January is understood to be increasingly unlikely.

Sierra has a release clause set at over £16million in his contract, and Sevilla are not prepared to entertain a sale unless that is met in full.

However, sources say that Leeds are happy with the depth in wide areas right now, and are instead "monitoring" Sierra as more of a long-term target.

The 22-year-old is under contract with the La Liga outfit until the summer of 2029, and already has two goals from eight games in the league so far.

Leeds are clear admirers of the starlet's quality, but do not believe they could fit him into their squad in the middle of the season.

Leeds had enjoyed a largely positive start to the new campaign, but a winless run across their last five games has halted them in their tracks.

Daniel Farke's side have seen their progress stunted to some extent of late, but they still sit 12th at the moment, and four points clear of the relegation zone.

The West Yorkshire outfit have been linked with several potential targets ahead of the winter window, but they are likely to take a fairly measured approach in the market.

Leeds' team has been quite settled in the early stages of the campaign, and Farke is not keen to try and overhaul the group that he has relied upon so far.

As a result, fans can expect a window of relatively limited activity, with most targets like Sierra likely to be left until next summer, when there is more time to reshape the squad.

Football Insider recently revealed that Leeds' formation is proving to be a "problem" for winger Jean-Matteo Bahoya at the moment.

The forward joined from Eintracht Frankfurt on loan in the summer, and Leeds also have the option to buy him for a fee of around £19m.

Bahoya has played just 11 minutes of competitive football for the Whites so far, with Farke's 3-5-2 formation closing him off from opportunities.

The boss was asked about the 21-year-old's lack of football recently, and launched into a 10-minute rant, with the star's situation not expected to change any time soon.
