Unai Emery has 'made his mind up' on Alejandro Garnacho ahead of January talks - ex-Villa scout

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Alejandro Garnacho should have his loan move at Aston Villa terminated in the January transfer window.

That is the opinion of former senior Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 22-year-old arrived from Chelsea on loan with an obligation to buy in the summer transfer window, with the fee expected to be in the region of £41million if easily achievable conditions are met.

But Garnacho has featured just seven times across all competitions for Villa since signing, and has only made it into the squad for one of the last four Premier League games.

The situation seemed to improve after the last international break and the winger will hope it does again after this one, but the signs do not look good for him.

Harvey Elliott's season in the reserves proved that, once Unai Emery has made his mind up on a player, he is incredibly hard to sway.

Many were surprised when Villa signed Garnacho in the summer, considering his previous experiences.

The winger had been pushed out for reported attitude issues at Man United, and simply was not good enough to compete in the Chelsea team.

Emery was said to be extremely keen on Garnacho, but the months since have thrown that suggestion into serious doubt.

"Emery has made his mind up on Garnacho. He should have made his mind up in the summer," King told Football Insider.

"Don't bring him to the football club. I just could never see him being an Aston Villa footballer. I certainly don't like his attitude when he's on the pitch.

"He's another one who looks as though he doesn't enjoy playing football. Come January, I'd be saying, 'I think it's best you go back to Chelsea.'"

"I can't see him turning it around and being a regular at Aston Villa."

Emery has always insisted that Garnacho's struggle for minutes is merely a selection issue, rather than anything else.

When the winger came off the bench against Viking last month, his manager was full of praise.

"Definitely he is improving and getting better. The two weeks we worked here was very important," he said.

"He showed us in Chelsea in the first half of last season and at Man United his quality. He is still young and he is working well."

Garnacho has made just two appearances since, with Emery's words not backed up by his actions.
