Rams offensive line woes get even worse with latest injured reserve shakeup

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The tail end of the Rams' season is set to be a brutal stretch for an injured Los Angeles offensive line.

Both Rams starting and backup centers, Coleman Shelton and Dylan McMahon, left in the first half of Sunday's 34-6 victory against the Arizona Cardinals and will be absent for the near future.

Los Angeles Rams quarterback Matthew Stafford and Sean McVay complaining to an official during Sunday's game. Getty Images

On Monday, California Post senior sports reporter Michael Duarte reported that Shelton "suffered from a severe hamstring strain" and is expected to miss 4-6 weeks, while McMahon has been placed on the team's injured reserve due to a knee injury.

Due to the Rams holding one of the more crowded injured reserves in football, McMahon runs the risk of his season coming to an end.

The NFL allows teams to activate eight players from the IR during the season, and Los Angeles has already activated three players this season, while still waiting to activate Davante Adams, Puka Nacua, Jordan Whittington, and Terrance Ferguson — which means McMahon might be the odd man out.

Beaux Limmer was filling in as Los Angeles' backup left guard in place of Steve Avila. But once McMahon left, Limmer filled in at center.

Los Angeles Rams center Coleman Shelton on the sideline during an NFL game. AP Photo/Rick Scuteri

The Rams scored 17 points in the second half, and were led by their rushing attack.

Running back Blake Corum, who finished Los Angeles' first offensive possession of the second half with a one-yard rushing touchdown, finished the day with 17 carries for 100 yards.

Los Angeles Rams Dylan McMahon walking off the field during Sunday's game against the Cardinals. Getty Images

Ronnie Rivers provided the perfect punch to support Corum, finishing with 15 carries for 81 yards.

It remains unknown if the Rams will go back to Limmer under center or if they sign a free agent, but the Rams are about to endure a crucial part of their schedule.

Due to Los Angeles having to play on Thanksgiving Eve, the Rams are on a "shortened bye week" right now and will have to play a 6-4 Packers team that is expected to have Micah Parsons suited up.

After that, the Rams will play a Thursday night matchup against the Chiefs, then an 8-1 49ers powerhouse the following week.

Their schedule will end with them playing the Seahawks twice during their final three weeks.
