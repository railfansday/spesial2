Lions open game with missed field goal by Jake Bates

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

It’s a trap game for the Lions on Sunday against the 3-6 Bucs. And the home team missed a chance to claim an early lead.

After a three-and-out from Tampa (which included quarterback Baker Mayfield taking a massive hit on the first play from scrimmage), the Lions’ drive sputtered at the Buccaneers’ 35. Kicker Jake Bates missed the 53-yard field to the right.

Fox rules analyst Mike Pereira explained after the ensuing commercial break that the Bucs should have been flagged for leverage on the play, because one of the Tampa defenders put his hand on the back of the Detroit snapper to get elevation.

It wasn’t called, and now the Bucs are on the move.
