What happened to Jalen Hurts? Latest update on the Eagles QB

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Philadelphia Eagles' quarterback Jalen Hurts remains sidelined and will miss the rest of the season after being diagnosed with deep vein thrombosis and bilateral pulmonary emboli.

While in London prior to the Eagles' Week 5 matchup against the Jacksonville Jaguars at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, Hurts was first diagnosed with what appeared to be a superficial blood clot in his calf. Despite the initial findings, he was cleared to play and travel back to the United States. However, subsequent imaging and tests revealed the blood clot had developed into a deep vein thrombosis and had traveled to his lungs.

The NFL conducted an investigation into how the Eagles' team and medical staff managed Hurts' injury. Ultimately, the league determined that the situation was handled appropriately and that the Eagles complied with all injury report policies.

Despite that conclusion, the NFL Players Association (NFLPA) is still seeking a full investigation into the medical evaluation process that led to Hurts being allowed to play. In a statement released on Tuesday, Oct. 20, the union called for thorough scrutiny, and commissioner Roger Goodell stated the league welcomes such an investigation.

On Sunday, Oct. 25, the NFL and NFLPA issued a joint statement confirming they are "working together to review the matter," signaling a collaborative approach to ensure player safety protocols were properly followed.

With Hurts sidelined, quarterback Tanner McKee has stepped in as the Eagles' starter.
