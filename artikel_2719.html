Real Madrid star admits he asked Barcelona legend to use his goal celebration

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Real Madrid star Federico Valverde has this week admitted that he requested permission from compatriot Luis Suárez to make use of the iconic attacker's celebration.

Midfielder Valverde was for his part on target for the latest occasion earlier this week, keeping alive a fine stretch of form in the final third.

En route to Real Madrid's turnaround Champions League victory over PSV Eindhoven, the 28-year-old lashed home a trademark drive from just inside the penalty area.

From here, Valverde wheeled away towards the crowd, before unveiling a three-finger pose whilst celebrating.

And drawn on the gesture by the media in the game's aftermath, Real's captain confirmed that he was inspired by former Barcelona goalscorer and fellow Uruguayan Luis Suárez:

"My new celebration? I asked permission from Luis Suárez, and he authorised it!. I'll always do it with respect. Suárez is an idol for us Uruguayans.

"The goals and celebration are also for my daughter Bruna."
