'Long season ahead.' Joe Burrow reacts to MVP chants after Week 10 win

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Cincinnati Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow was asked about MVP chants he heard from the crowd at Paycor Stadium during a 38-18 rout of the Pittsburgh Steelers on NBC's Sunday Night Football.

"Long season ahead," Burrow told reporters.

Burrow was asked when he'll acknowledge that he's an MVP candidate.

Burrow was asked about his confidence level right now.

"Yeah, I'm seeing it," Burrow said. "I'm putting the ball where I want to for the most part and just got to keep getting better."

"Nobody's winning the division with seven wins," Burrow added. "Nobody's making the playoffs with seven wins, and we certainly aren't satisfied."

Burrow did acknowledge his outstanding night when asked what kind of QB he was against the Steelers.

Head coach Zac Taylor was asked after the game whether he heard the chants from the crowd.

"Well, I mean, (it would) be impossible to say anybody's playing better than him," Taylor told reporters after the game.

Burrow had 409 passing yards - including 302 in the first half - with four touchdowns before giving way to Joe Flacco in the fourth quarter. Receiver Ja'Marr Chase had the fourth 200+-yard game of his remarkable career.

Burrow's fourth touchdown pass gave him his fifth straight game with 4+ pass/rush TD, tying Peyton Manning in 2004 for the longest such streak in NFL history.
