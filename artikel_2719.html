Madrid suffer scare but thrash PSV, City snatch point against Napoli

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Real Madrid completed a sensational comeback to thrash PSV 5-1 in the Champions League on Tuesday, while Manchester City rescued a point in a 2-2 draw with Napoli.

Madrid were down 1-0 at half-time after Ivan Perišić scored from the spot to give PSV the lead in the 32nd minute.

But things changed in the second half and Los Blancos took control of the match. Federico Valverde levelled in the 50th and Madrid had the lead in the 60th thanks to a stunner from Jude Bellingham.

Kylian Mbappé then scored a brace, and Vinícius Júnior completed the turnaround in the 87th as Madrid now top the league phase standings ahead of Wednesday's matches.

Manchester City thought they could also claim a comeback win, but had a different fate.

Ayyoub Bouaddi scored in the 67th to cancel Rasmus Højlund's opener for Napoli.

Noa Lang gave the lead back to the Italians in 76th, but City snatched a point thanks to Rayan Cherki's goal in the 86th.

Last season's runners-up Arsenal were held to a 1-1 draw with Borussia Dortmund, while Antonio Nusa scored a brace in RB Leipzig's 3-1 win over Lens.

Elsewhere, Real Betis beat Slovan Bratislava 2-0, Porto claimed a 1-0 win away at Feyenoord, and surprise package Como moved up to third after a 1-0 triumph against AEK Athens.
