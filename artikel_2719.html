Confidence in the Chicago Bears Remains High

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Even though this week's survey results showed a drop in our fans' confidence in the direction our beloved Chicago Bears are heading, I'm gonna keep this one positive.

I've voted yes that I'm confident in the Bears' direction for the entire Ben Johnson era. Going back even further, I've been confident in the team since the day they fired Matt Eberflus, nearly two years ago, on November 29, 2024. It came on Black Friday, following the Debacle in Detroit, which was the last straw for chairman George McCaskey to give general manager Ryan Poles the okay to flush the 'Flus.

I'm thankful for Eberflus freezing in the waning seconds of that Thanksgiving game two years ago.

While I'm at it, I'm also thankful for the Fail Mary earlier that year in Washington, because that was the start of the losing streak and the first huge crack in the 'Flus facade.

I'm thankful Caleb Williams plays quarterback for my favorite team, and I'm thankful Ben Johnson is his head coach.

Johnson (+1000) currently has the fourth-best odds for the NFL Coach of the Year Award, according to our partners at FanDuel Sportsbook.

The three ahead of Johnson are the New York Giants' John Harbaugh (+150), the San Francisco 49ers' Kyle Shanahan (+200), and the Cleveland Browns' Todd Monken (+300).
