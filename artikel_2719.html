Broncos’ Sean Payton Apologizes to Reporter After Odd Interaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Denver Broncos head coach Sean Payton had a bit of an odd moment in front of the media following his team's win on Sunday against the Carolina Panthers.

During the postgame press conference, Payton called out a reporter for smoking.

"Can we not smoke in here, please?" Payton said.

However, the reporter wasn't smoking, and the cigarette Payton thought the reporter had in their mouth was just a pen.

"Oh, you're not smoking; you've got a pencil," the 62-year-old Payton later said.

"I'm sorry, I thought you were smoking. I owe ya. But I had a flashback and I was just thinking, 'Who's smoking a cigarette?'" added the former AP NFL Coach of the Year Award winner.

Denver is now on a five-game winning streak after beating Bryce Young and company, 26-21, at Bank of America Stadium in Charlotte.

The Broncos got off to a big start, scoring 10 unanswered points in the first quarter. Then, after getting outscored by Carolina by 11 points in the next two quarters, Denver popped off for 13 points in the final period, while allowing seven points to the home team.

The Denver got a major assist from its defense to close this game out, as Nik Bonitto recovered a ball after Young was strip-sacked with 1:24 left in regulation.

Denver quarterback Bo Nix had 237 passing yards and two touchdowns with an interception on 25/37 passing, while Jonah Coleman rushed for 57 yards and a score on 17 carries.

The Broncos lead the AFC West Division with a 7-2 record, as they go into a Week 10 bye.
