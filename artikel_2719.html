Yves Bissouma To Start: Ajax Predicted XI vs Feyenoord

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Ajax go into De Klassieker against arch-rivals Feyenoord in exceptional form, and confidence will be high in the Ajax squad.

The Amsterdammers are unbeaten in their last eight games and have won seven of those, with the most recent win coming against FC Thun on Thursday evening in the UEFA Conference League.

Across those eight games, Ajax have scored 25 goals and conceded only six. Ajax's performances have been inconsistent, but they have found ways to win even when they have not played particularly well.

While Ajax have been thriving, Feyenoord have been struggling. The Rotterdammers had a good start to the season under new head coach Giovanni van Bronckhorst, but are going through a difficult period, having not won a game in over a month. They are winless in their last seven games and have lost five of those. They now sit 5th in the Eredivisie table, three points behind Ajax.

Ajax are currently 2nd, and should PSV Eindhoven drop points against Go Ahead Eagles, a win against Feyenoord would take Michel Sanchez's team to the top of the table.

Jorthy Mokio has been a crucial player for Ajax this season, but fractured his fibula against ADO Den Haag and will be out for four months.

Simon Adingra and Viktor Tsygankov have both been dealing with injuries and trained with the Ajax squad on Wednesday before the game against FC Thun, but it is unclear whether they will be fit enough to be involved against Feyenoord.

Caio Henrique suffered a thigh injury in September against Excelsior. He was an unused substitute against FC Twente in Ajax's last game before the international break, but was not in the matchday squads against ADO Den Haag and FC Thun. He also did not train on Wednesday with the rest of the Ajax squad. It is unlikely he will be involved against Feyenoord.

Kasper Dolberg is also unavailable and will be out of action for a while after suffering a serious knee injury in September.

Marc ter Stegen will start in goal for Ajax. Lucas Rosa will come back into the team at right-back after being rested against FC Thun. In central defence, Aaron Bouwman will likely partner Youri Baas. Owen Wijndal will keep his place at left-back.

Sofyan Amrabat did not have his best game against FC Thun, but he has been quite solid this season and will likely keep his place as defensive midfielder. Yves Bissouma was very good against ADO Den Haag and will likely start as the No. 8, as Mokio is unavailable. Bissouma is not as good in the final third as Mokio, but they are both energetic, strong in duels and fantastic ball carriers. Julian Brandt will start in the No. 10 position.

Oscar Gloukh has been impressive in his last few games and will likely start on the left wing. Davy Klaassen could start on the right wing, with Rosa overlapping to allow the 34-year-old to move into more central areas when Ajax have the ball. Tolu Arokodare will come back into the team and start as the striker.

Ajax predicted starting line-up: Ter Stegen; Rosa, Bouwman, Baas, Wijndal; Amrabat, Bissouma, Brandt; Klaasen, Tolu, Gloukh.
