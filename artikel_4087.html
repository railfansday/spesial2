How To Get The Permanent Health Upgrade In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Here is how to unlock the permanent health upgrade and Hellspawn shotgun in GTA 6.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is filled to the brim with all sorts of strange and supernatural encounters you can stumble across. From easter eggs of classic horror games to elaborate puzzles that let you travel back to the original Vice City, there are a ton of things to discover for those willing to explore.

One of these is fighting literal demons that feels straight out of the DOOM franchise. Not only will completing this side mission reward you with the awesome Hellspawn legendary shotgun, but also a permanent health upgrade for Lucia and Jason. This makes it one of the best side quests to do and definitely worth your time.

To begin this side quest, you will need to first progress through the game until you're about halfway into Chapter 3. While it takes place in an area you can access right from the start, in all my testing I was unable to get the NPCs needed for this mission to spawn until I had progressed through Chapter 3 enough for the police to stop looking for Jason and Lucia in Port Gelhorn.

Once this happens, make your way back to the north portion of Port Gellhorn at night and look for an open garage with two metal heads sitting on a couch (shown above). The garage they hang out in isn't open until later at night, so I recommend stopping by around 9:00 P.M. This can also be done on either Jason or Lucia, so feel free to pick whoever you want for this side quest.

When you arrive at the garage, go inside to be introduced to Dave and Beelzebub. Talk to them to learn that they are about to leave for a ritual and wait until you've exhausted all their dialogue. Eventually, a van will pull up out front and Dave will offer for you to join them in the ritual. Get in the back of the van to be whisked away to the ritual site, which will trigger a brief cutscene.

Once you regain control, make your way to the fire everyone is around and stare into it. You'll be sucked into Hell, where you'll need to extinguish a pretender who sits on Satan's throne. There will be no way to leave, and you'll be in possession of a shotgun that never needs to reload.

Your goal is to wipe out all the demons standing in your way, by blasting them to pieces with your newly acquired shotgun. They will attack by running into you, so when you see them just blast away with your shotgun until they are reduced to ash. The bigger ones will take around 2–3 shots to kill, while the smaller, faster ones only need a single blast from your gun.

Always prioritize the quicker demons first, as they can quickly close the gap and deal a decent amount of damage if they hit you. You'll need to fight through several small arenas of them until you eventually reach the demon boss who is sitting on a throne. Approaching him will trigger a boss fight, but it's not as bad as it seems.

When the fight begins, the demon will be immune and launch waves of black goats at you. These can be shot or mostly ignored as their damage is negligible. Instead, look for the hulking, bloated demons among the goats as these should be your main targets. Killing them will both cause a small explosion killing any nearby goats, but dispatching all the demons will make the boss vulnerable.

Once you see the health bar at the bottom of the screen go red, start blasting the boss to damage him. Upon losing about a third of his health, he will become invincible once more and launch another wave of goats and demons at you. Repeat this process two more times by slaying the demons he summons, and then damaging him when his health bar turns red.

Upon killing the boss, you'll be sent back to the real world where you'll earn a new shirt, tattoo, the Hellspawn Comshot 12 legendary shotgun, and a permanent increase to your maximum health.

It can be tough to get started, but there are easy ways to pick yourself up.
