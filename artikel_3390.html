Markiplier’s Iron Lung Officially Returns In 3 Weeks With Exciting New Horror Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Markiplier's Iron Lung is about to terrorize viewers once again.

Based on David Szymanski's hit viral video game of the same name, the horror film centers on Simon, a convict living in a distant future in which all stars, inhabited planets and most of humanity have vanished, and is tasked with exploring a desolate moon with an ocean of blood in a small submarine. Written, directed by and starring Markiplier, Iron Lung left critics somewhat divided but was a thorough smash with audiences and at the box office.

Now, nearly a year after the film hit theaters, Markiplier has offered a new update on his Iron Lung adaptation, revealing that it is officially getting a DVD and Blu-ray release. The horror hit, which is currently available for pre-order in both of its versions, is slated to release in three weeks, with the filmmaker indicating it will arrive around December 13, when he will also host a special livestream offering a complete behind-the-scenes look at the creation of the film and its unanswered questions.

In addition to the film itself, the Iron Lung Blu-ray and DVDs, which cost $30 and $20 respectively, are said to come with over three hours of never-before-seen bonus content, including a behind-the-scenes documentary entitled One with the Blood running just over two hours long, as well as various other featurettes and yet-to-be-announced content.

The long wait for an Iron Lung physical release is sure to have been a surprising one compared to the general three-to-four-month turnaround for other films. However, as Markiplier explains in the video above, the reason for this stemmed from a desire to prevent losing creative control over his hit movie, and the desire to give an in-depth Blu-ray experience.

It certainly falls in line with the way the filmmaker treated Iron Lung throughout its initial development, which was in and of itself a slow-but-steady process. After being announced in Spring 2023, production was hit with a few notable setbacks, including the 2023 SAG-AFTRA strike, as well as dealing with minor injuries from the fake blood used on set, which set a new world record with over 80,000 gallons used. Moreover, the film took nearly two years from when he finished its post-production to ultimately giving it a grassroots-like independent theatrical release.

Ultimately, Markiplier's efforts have frequently proven worthwhile for Iron Lung. Despite coming out in January, an infamously lackluster window at the box office, the movie saw its opening weekend projections continually increase as anticipation grew around it, with it ultimately opening to $18.2 million, well surpassing its production budget, which the filmmaker has estimated being between $4–5 million. By the end of its run, it had grossed $51.2 million, somewhere between 10-13 times its budget.

While not as talked-about as its successors, Markiplier's Iron Lung notably kicked off 2026 as the year of YouTubers-turned-filmmakers taking over the box office. The combined might of Curry Barker's Obsession and Kane Parsons' Backrooms, which together grossed close to $925 million, has seen Hollywood now turning to other similar stories and projects, while also pushing forward with new titles from both Barker and Parsons.

Obsession has become the latest in a long line of horror films made by YouTube creators, and it's just one of six films that shows their creativity.

Now that Iron Lung is slated for a physical media release, it will be interesting to see how much its previous success carries over into a medium that many studios have started to phase out. Considering the passionate fanbase Markiplier and the film have, and it being a limited-run release, there's a very real chance it could enjoy a similar level of success and, depending on the speed of its inventory sales, may even get a restocking at some point in the future.

Iron Lung is a film set in a post-apocalyptic future where a convict is tasked with exploring a blood ocean on a desolate moon. Utilizing a small submarine called the Iron Lung, he navigates the aftermath of the Quiet Rapture, which caused the disappearance of stars and habitable planets.
