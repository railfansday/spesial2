Prime Video’s 16-Part Action Thriller Is So Good, It Officially Launched 2 Spinoffs

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Prime Video's 16-part action thriller hit has become a major franchise, with a second spinoff officially in development following its first prequel. The Chris Pratt-led series is expanding once again, firmly establishing it as one of Prime Video's most prized streaming shows despite receiving mixed reviews for its debut season in 2022. That said, the series is an undeniable streaming success, currently ranking #3 worldwide on Prime Video's viewership chart following the complete release of its 8-part second season nearly one month ago.

The Terminal List on Prime Video has been so good and popular since its recent season 2 premiere that it has officially launched not one, but two spinoff series. Based on the celebrated books by Jack Carr, The Terminal List spawned The Terminal List: Dark Wolf last year, a 7-part prequel that followed Taylor Kitsch's Ben Edwards, who starred alongside Pratt in The Terminal List season 1, as he transitioned from the Navy SEALs to the CIA. Now, the newly announced series Cry Havoc is a prequel following the father of Chris Pratt's Terminal List protagonist, James Reece, that is currently in development at Prime Video.

The recent triumphs of The Terminal List season 2 have not only led to the quick season 3 renewal of the series but have also paved the way for more adaptations of Carr's best-selling novels. Cry Havoc is the latest published book by Carr and was released in 2025, set well within The Terminal List universe and centering on a mysterious yet significant character in the franchise, Tom Reese. Just like The Terminal List season 1, Cry Havoc follows a Navy SEAL (Tom) whose military team is ambushed and heavily compromised, which leads Tom on an elaborate mission of revenge. The new series takes place in 1968 during the height of the Vietnam War.

Amazon has yet to announce any official casting news about Cry Havoc but, given the story's timeline, it's unlikely that Pratt will appear as James Reece in the series. However, since Pratt serves as an executive producer on The Terminal List and Dark Wolf, it's likely that he will do the same on Cry Havoc. This is promising news for the potential second season of The Terminal List: Dark Wolf, as Amazon's interest in growing the franchise has never been higher. What's more, Carr has written five books in James Reece's Terminal List saga that have yet to be adapted for the screen, with a sixth book, State of Exile, set for publication in October 2027, leaving plenty of source material.

The Terminal List is one of those series whose critical reception does not necessarily match its massive viewership, which had initially created some uncertainty about whether Prime Video would double down on making it a true franchise. Now, much has happened with The Terminal List that makes its franchise status undeniable, which is a major victory for Pratt, Carr, and co-creator David DiGilio. It's worth noting that the series earned a strong 92% Rotten Tomatoes audience score, which, along with its continued high viewership, is a clear indication that Prime is more interested in responding to fan-oriented feedback rather than being primarily concerned with critical opinion.

While viewers will learn a great deal about Tom Reece in Cry Havoc, the upcoming action thriller may also shed more light on Judy Reece, James' mother, who was included in the original novel but omitted from the Prime Video series. Although Tom has not yet met Judy during the events of Cry Havoc and instead has a different love interest named Ella, the series may alter this dynamic to focus more intently on the Reece family.

From what is already known about Tom from the novel and his on-screen introduction in The Terminal List season 2 through flashbacks as portrayed by David Keeley, he is deeply patriotic and loyal, a torch that James also carries. With so much more to discover about Tom, specifically his switch from being a soldier to a CIA agent, Cry Havoc will be a great expansion for The Terminal List.
