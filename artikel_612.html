‘GTA 6’ Music Breakdown: More Than 700 Songs and 90 Years of Music Across 20 Radio Stations

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

You may never run out of music to listen to while playing “Grand Theft Auto VI.”

The latest game in the “GTA” series, which officially released on Thursday and has already taken the world by storm, has a truly massive list of songs — nearly 750 individual tracks have been spotted online by players. You can check out the game’s full credits here, but it would take hours to read over every contributor, from directors to writers, animators to artists, and actors to tattoo artists. There are 20 in-game radio stations that players can listen to while they’re driving or even on foot with headphones or earbuds attached to Jason and Lucia’s phones. In total, there are songs from across nine decades of music featured in the game.
