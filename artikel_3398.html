The Walking Dead Franchise Officially Switches Genres In New Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Anyone who watched The Walking Dead back when the original show started will remember just how different it felt from any other zombie franchise. Our fearless leader Rick Grimes stepped out into a terrifying world with his son Carl in a unique take on the genre that would take the world by storm in its initial series run. The show became a cultural phenomenon that has since expanded well beyond its original 11-season run.

Since then, we've seen several spinoffs that have taken fans in all sorts of different directions, from Fear the Walking Dead and The Ones Who Live to Maggie and Negan's unlikely partnership in Dead City. As fans look forward to the fourth and final season of Daryl Dixon, arriving in February 2027, there's plenty to look forward to in the future as well.

However, one of the franchise's newest releases takes The Walking Dead in a completely different direction, officially switching to a genre fans might never have expected from Rick, Daryl, and the rest of the original rag-tag group of survivors.

The Walking Dead has entered a new genre with the release of Streets of Survival, a new arcade-style beat 'em up that puts a unique retro spin on the beloved zombie franchise. Developed by Odaclick Game Studio and published by Trailmark Games, Streets of Survival launched on September 18, 2026, for PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, Nintendo Switch, and Switch 2.

Unlike the narrative-driven adventures of Telltale's The Walking Dead games or the survival-focused gameplay of other adaptations, Streets of Survival takes inspiration from classic side-scrolling arcade brawlers. Think Streets of Rage or TMNT, but with iconic characters from AMC's original The Walking Dead series.

The game features a single-player campaign spanning 12 levels, with Rick Grimes, Daryl Dixon, and Michonne as playable characters. Players can also unlock Negan after completing their first playthrough, giving them an opportunity to wield his infamous baseball bat, Lucille.

The story actually revisits the "All Out War" storyline from Seasons 7 and 8 of the original series, pitting players against the Saviors and hordes of walkers. Along the way, you encounter familiar locations like Alexandria and the Sanctuary, while facing bosses including Negan, Simon, and of course, a series of the most infamous walkers.

Combat is obviously focused around building combos, doing special attacks, and using brutal finishers, although the developers have also incorporated scavenging, exploration, and character upgrades to retain some of the franchise's survival elements.

As for its reception, Streets of Survival has a relatively respectable user rating on Steam so far, hanging around the 7/10 territory pretty consistently and ranking Mostly Positive among players. Players who enjoy it have specifically praised the retro pixel-art vibes, the fact we get to see beloved characters again, and of course the nostalgic feel of the arcade gameplay. However, criticisms have noted repetitive combat, occasionally frustrating controls, and the relatively short campaign. The lack of co-op has also been a sticking point, especially considering the genre's history.

Still, for longtime Walking Dead fans looking for something completely different, Streets of Survival offers an entertaining new way to revisit some of the franchise's most memorable characters and conflicts.
