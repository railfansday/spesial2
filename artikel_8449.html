She really was an "A Lister" and showed signs of carrying that over the deadly barrier of c age 34, where Hollywood female actors basically run out of roles until they play "Grandma" 30 years later. You can name the exceptions (Sandra Bullock, Gwynneth Paltrow, Meryl Streep the canonical one) but you can most certainly name any number of talented young female actors who have just ... disappeared. In fact, there was a documentary "Whatever happened to Debra Winger" about precisely this phenomenon.
I've always had the sense Debra Winger was a "difficult" actor to work with. Meaning she was a perfectionist, and that can eventually wear an actor down. So not surprising she'd leave for a quieter life.

But man when she re-appeared, the first time I saw her was in "Kajillionaire". Totally unrecognizable. Part of my comments at the time:
Really just wanted to see what Winger was up to and had to look hard to see that she was the mother. It wasn't just that her looks, gait (she had a limp) and voice were all changed.
