Personal Investments • Re: Should I participate in my ESPP?

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

I think this is a very small percentage of your portfolio? I would participate in the ESPP to get the full match but wait the 2 years for a qualifying disposition. Yes, it's your employer stock and the argument is to sell to avoid concentration risk, but the cap on the match means there is very little concentration here. Keep the stock and wait two years for better tax treatment on the match.
Separate the match from the hold. You capture the full discount the day shares are purchased, so selling same-day keeps 100% of it. Holding two years doesn’t protect the match, it only shifts the tax treatment on that discount.

Run the numbers. The federal ESPP cap is $25K, so at a 15% discount that’s $3,750 of profit. For example, if your federal rate is 35%, selling right away taxes that as ordinary income, 35%. Hold 2+ years for a qualifying disposition and it’s long-term, 20% plus 3.8% NIIT, so 23.8%. That’s a difference of about $420 on the year.

And that saving is only on the $3,750 discount, not the $25K you put in. Your contributions are your own after-tax money and aren’t taxed again, so the entire tax question is about how that ~$3,750 gain is treated. Holding an individual stock two extra years to save roughly $420 on the gain is a lot of added risk for not much reward, especially when salary and RSUs already concentrate the household in the same company. Take the discount and sell right away imo.
There no discount in the OPs plan, so absolutely no reason to hold for 2 years. Discussion of whether the small tax benefit is worth it is all moot.
