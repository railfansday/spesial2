Personal Investments • Re: Asset ratio in retirement

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

If your annual expenses come to 3% or less of your portfolio size, per the celebrated 1998 Trinity study (pdf link) it is very likely that any asset allocation from 100/0 to 0/100 will last for another 20 years. So if that withdrawal rate matches your situation, you could let the market do its thing & your asset allocation drift without rebalancing.

I try to divide our RMDs - after gifting, QCD - into the 60/40 ratio, but it is not quite working. The dividends are not reinvested and go into the settlement account. I reinvest them back into VTSAX and the VFIAX - Total stock market
If your desired asset allocation is 60/40 and your actual allocation is closer to 70/30, you could compensate for that by reinvesting surplus cash to bonds only, with no new reinvestment to stock -- no further reinvestments to VTSAX and VFIAX until your asset allocation returns to 60/40.
