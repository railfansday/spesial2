'DWTS' Season 35 Grammy Night Songs and Dances Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Dancing With the Stars is weeks away from the finale — but first, the contestants have Grammy Night to look forward to.

Former contestant Andy Richter revealed details about the upcoming theme during the Wednesday, November 4, episode of the “Dancing With the Stars Official Podcast.”

After a season full of ups and downs, the remaining couples are inching ever closer to the semifinals and finale. The Grammy-inspired theme picks up right after Julia Stiles and Ezra Sosa were eliminated on Disney Night.

In addition to the individual dances, cohosts Alfonso Ribeiro and Julianne Hough stepped in on Monday, November 2, to take part in team dances. Hough’s Team Wildcats earned a perfect 40 out of 40 for their routine, beating Ribeiro’s Team Slaytopia, who earned 36 out of 40 points.

Keep scrolling for details on which style your favorite pair will be performing — and their song choices — for Grammy Night on DWTS:

The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air star is performing a tango to “Boom Boom Pow” by the Black Eyed Peas.

The Rookie star is using “All For You” by Janet Jackson for a jazz routine.

Beyoncé‘s “Crazy In Love (The Remix)” song is the soundtrack for the pair’s Argentine tango.

The Olympic gold medalist will perform a paso doble set to “Bring Me to Life” by Evanescence.

The reality star has an Argentine tango set to “Thong Song” by Sisqó.

The actor is showing off a salsa set to “Die With a Smile” by Lady Gaga and Bruno Mars.

The influencer and Arnold will perform an Argentine tango to “Sexyback” by Justin Timberlake.

Dancing With the Stars airs on ABC and Disney+ Tuesdays at 8 p.m. ET.
