Fans Have Strong Reactions to Lions Star Aidan Hutchinson’s Emotional Moment After Jack Campbell’s Injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Detroit Lions scored a 19-15 victory over the Minnesota Vikings at Ford Field in Detroit on Sunday, becoming just the first team in the 2026 NFL season to beat Kyler Murray and company. But linebacker Jack Campbell's scary injury has dampened Detroit's celebration.

The All-Pro linebacker was carted off the field and taken to the hospital after going down with a neck injury in the fourth quarter.

Campbell lay motionless on the field and showed no movement in his extremities before leaving the stadium.

The injury to Campbell left his teammates, including star defensive end Aidan Hutchinson, emotional.

The broadcast showed the former No. 2 overall pick Hutchinson, despite the emotions, rally his team and show what true leadership looks like.

Detroit Lions edge rusher Aidan Hutchinson (97) gets ready for the first half against Minnesota Vikings at Ford Field in Detroit on Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026.

Meanwhile, fans online praised Hutchinson for his actions amid a tough moment for this team.

"That's what a captain looks like. Hutchinson pulling the defense together right after Campbell went off, then they closed out the unbeaten Vikings. Praying for Jack," said a fan on X.

Another one said: "This is heartbreaking… he understands what this injury means

From a commenter: "I love that man's passion for his teammates and this game!

I know that had to be traumatizing for him as well as anyone else. What a way to lead!," a social media user posted.

Via a different commenter: And on top of this, balled out the very next play to force an errant Kyler throw. Big time playmaker and leader!

Said another: His parents raised him right! Compassion and empathy for JC!

The Lions provided an update on Campbell on Monday, saying that he "continues to rest and recover at a local hospital." The team also said it opted not to share more information about the player's status out of respect for him and his family.
