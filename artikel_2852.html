Celtics' Derrick White Named Trade Option, but Shouldn't be Moved

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Celtics' Derrick White Named Trade Option, but Shouldn't be Moved originally appeared on NESN. Add NESN as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Boston Celtics haven't played perfect basketball to start the season, and that was evident on Wednesday night when they struggled a bit against the 1-7 Milwaukee Bucks, winning just 109-102 and almost blowing a lead in the second half.

However, it's also fair to mention that the Celtics were without their best player in Jayson Tatum, who took a night off.

The Celtics should consider not playing Tatum every night after he suffered an Achilles injury and missed nearly all of last season because of it.

That injury also raised questions this offseason, as many worried Tatum wouldn't return to the player he once was. To say he's been great this season would be an understatement, as Tatum has done everything anyone can ask of him except hit three-point shots at the level we all know he can.

Tatum is averaging 27 points, six rebounds, and 7.9 assists. He is just shooting 22.9% from 3-point range, but this is the guy who should figure that out.

Simply put, the Celtics do look like a team that can win a championship again, but it'll come down to what buttons Brad Stevens can press at the right time and whether he's willing to move on from others.

Among the potential trade options Bleacher Report recently noted as the Celtics' best assets, it said the two first-rounders they got from the Sixers and their 2031 first-round pick are at the top. However, Bleacher Report also noted that Derrick White could be a guy who makes sense to move.

"That puts the 2031 first-rounder they got from the Sixers for Brown in play alongside their own 2031 first-rounder.

"Payton Pritchard's recent extension means he can't be traded for six months. Another team might have viewed him as a piece worth pursuing at an average annual value of $15 million per year through 2029-30, but buyers might instead have to aim for Derrick White, who has three years and $98 million left on his current contract," Grant Hughes of Bleacher Report wrote.

It doesn't feel like White has played as well as he had throughout the first three and a half seasons in Boston in comparison to what he's done over the past year and a half.

However, this is still a guy who is going to make big-time plays for the Celtics and lead to a lot of winning.

Unless the Celtics get an offer they can't refuse, it feels safe to keep White on the roster and continue to go to battle with him night in and night out.
