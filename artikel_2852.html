Midwestern State soccer, tennis shine in postseason competitions

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Midwestern State enjoyed another successful week across several sports, highlighted by the men's soccer team's advancement to the Lone Star Conference Tournament championship, individual conference honors and an upset victory at the ITA Division II Tennis Championships.

No. 4 Midwestern State men's soccer advanced to the Lone Star Conference Tournament championship Wednesday after defeating UT Dallas 4-3 in penalty kicks following a 1-1 draw at Stang Park.

Goalkeeper Jake Morris made three saves during the shootout, and Christian Castaneda converted the winning penalty kick in the sixth round.

The Mustangs (14-0-5) extended their unbeaten streak to 30 games and moved one victory away from their sixth consecutive LSC postseason tournament championship.

UT Dallas took the lead in the 53rd minute when Avery Brazzell scored off an assist from Evan Pustejovsky. Midwestern State answered just 31 seconds later as Angel Escobar scored his first goal of the season, assisted by Oscar Nunez and Juan David Valencia Vacca.

Neither team scored during the remaining regulation time or two 10-minute overtime periods, sending the match to a shootout.

Parker Plaxco, Liam Kervick and Anthony Barrera converted penalty kicks for MSU before Castaneda secured the victory in the sixth round.

The Mustangs will host third-seeded St. Mary's at 7 p.m. Saturday at Stang Park for the conference championship.

Midwestern State's men's and women's soccer programs earned several major Lone Star Conference awards this week.

Senior forward Parker Plaxco led the men's selections, earning LSC Player of the Year and Forward of the Year honors after leading the conference with 15 goals and 30 points during the regular season.

Plaxco also recorded seven game-winning goals, the most in NCAA Division II.

Junior Ethan Krchnak was named Defensive Player of the Year, while Morris earned Goalkeeper of the Year honors. Senior Alan Rivera was selected as Midfielder of the Year.

Morris also received the conference's Academic Player of the Year award, carrying a 4.0 grade-point average while pursuing a degree in business analytics and information systems.

All four individual award winners earned first-team All-LSC recognition, joining teammates Skyler Krchnak, Davis Mays and Cristopher Guevara.

On the women's side, sophomore goalkeeper Lauren Short was named LSC Academic Player of the Year, becoming the first Midwestern State women's soccer player to receive the honor.

Short, a pre-veterinary major with a 4.0 GPA, has recorded a program-record 12 shutouts this season. She also ranks among the national leaders in goals-against average.

Sophomore defender Bella Rose and senior midfielder Lexi Gonzales earned first-team All-LSC honors after helping the Mustangs finish the regular season 14-2-2.

Gonzales led MSU with six goals and seven assists during the regular season, while Rose helped anchor a defense that allowed just 10 goals in 18 games.

Sloane Sullivan, Sophie Ferro and Leeci Snyder earned second-team honors. Ferro was also selected to the All-Freshman Team.

Madison Brinkley received third-team recognition, while Rose joined Short on the conference's All-Academic Team.

Midwestern State senior Bruno Sanchez opened the ITA Division II Individual Championships with an upset victory Wednesday in Tuscaloosa, Alabama.

Sanchez defeated fourth-seeded Marc Mail of Embry-Riddle 6-4, 1-6, 10-5 in the opening round at the Tuscaloosa Tennis Center.

After winning the first set, Sanchez dropped the second before taking the deciding tiebreaker to advance. The senior from Sabadell, Spain, will face Yuto Miyagawa of Washburn in the second round.

Sanchez also advanced in doubles competition alongside teammate Filip Vacula. The top-seeded MSU pairing defeated Stanislas Noel and Finn Kirschner of Point Loma Nazarene 6-1, 6-3 in the opening round of the 32-team draw.

Sanchez and Vacula face Radu Florea and Jakub Hrasek of Lincoln Memorial in Thursday's second round.
