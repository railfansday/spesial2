Ohio high school football playoff schedule for Akron area in OHSAA Round 3

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Six Akron-area teams won Nov. 6 to remain alive heading into the regional semifinal round of the Ohio High School Athletic Association football playoffs.

That group includes Walsh Jesuit and Nordonia, who play each other in Division II, Region 5.

All regional semifinal games kick off at 7 p.m. Friday, Nov. 13. Here's a look at the playoff pairings for local teams.

Watch Ohio High School Football Live on the NFHS Network all season

No. 5 Hudson (9-3) vs No. 1 Lakewood St. Edward (10-1)

No. 1 Highland (10-1) vs. No. 4 Mayfield (11-0)

No. 2 Walsh Jesuit (8-2) vs. No. 3 Nordonia (10-1)

No. 1 Streetsboro (9-2) vs. No. 5 West Geauga (10-2)

No. 3 Mogadore (9-2) vs. No. 2 Cuyahoga Heights (10-1)
