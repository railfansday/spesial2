Patriots RB TreVeyon Henderson already ruled out for Lions clash in Germany

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

New England Patriots running back TreVeyon Henderson's availability remains a concern as the team prepares for Sunday's Week 10 matchup against the Detroit Lions in Munich, Germany.

The second-year back has been dealing with an illness that kept him out of New England's Week 9 game against the Green Bay Packers. Henderson's status adds to New England's injury concerns ahead of the overseas trip.

Rhamondre Stevenson also missed Wednesday's practice with a knee injury, adding uncertainty to the backfield as the 5-4 Patriots prepare to face the 6-2 Lions.

New England shared its official Wednesday injury report on X, formerly Twitter, providing a definitive update on Henderson's game status alongside practice participation details for Stevenson and the rest of the roster before Sunday's international contest at Allianz Arena.

Henderson recently disclosed that unexplained neurological symptoms required several days of hospitalization and extensive testing. The symptoms affected his strength, coordination and physical function.

Although the test results were described as reassuring, the cause of his condition remains unknown. Wednesday's report listed his absence under illness.

The Patriots also ruled out guard/center Ben Brown and cornerback Carlton Davis III, both with knee injuries. Stevenson did not practice Wednesday, but the Patriots had not assigned him a game designation.

Corey Kiner and Lan Larison are among the Patriots' remaining backfield options as head coach Mike Vrabel prepares for the Lions.

Elsewhere on the report, wide receiver AJ Brown was limited with ankle and knee injuries. Quarterback Drake Maye participated fully despite a right shoulder issue.

Sunday's game at Allianz Arena kicks off at 9:30 a.m. ET. Henderson will miss the international matchup, while Stevenson's status remains uncertain as the Patriots prepare with a depleted backfield.
