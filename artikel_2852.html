Victor Wembanyama gets real about his three-point shooting struggles this season

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Victor Wembanyama gets real about his three-point shooting struggles this season originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Victor Wembanyama has been the most dominant player in the NBA's 2026-27 season. He has been tremendous, with averages of 28.6 points, 11.9 rebounds, and 3.9 blocks per game.

He has done a lot of damage with his scoring inside the paint, which is why he is on track to shatter the record for most dunks in a season. However, Wembanyama is a versatile offensive player, but his three-point shooting numbers are the only blemish on his season so far.

The San Antonio Spurs mega star has been spectacular. Of course, not having the three-point shooting is a bit of a problem because that is still a big part of his game. He is still taking and making threes, but he might be a tad too inefficient as of late.

He acknowledges this, which is why he has been a dominant figure with his dunking. However, he also has to diversify his offense, so he can be harder to guard. That has not happened just yet because of his three-point struggles.

"So far in my career, I've really prioritized my body and working on my physical development. The truth is, my shooting repetitions come during the season. That's when I get most of that work in," Wembanyama said about his slow start with his jump shot.

Wembanyama has also said that he is confident that he will return to form soon. He is a slow starter, but he wants to catch fire with his three-point shooting sometime soon, but at the moment, he is doing fine.

Of course, he is dunking all the time, but he does admit that he has had to deal with a couple of blisters. That even led to him having yet another viral moment when he did a jump shot just above the rim, to reduce the impact of dunking on those blisters.

"I've had this blister forever, and it's killing me. And every time I dunk it, it kills me... If I can avoid it, I'll avoid it," Wembanyama said about that viral close jump shot.

Moving forward, Wembanyama will continue to be a scary matchup. If his three-point shooting follows suit, the Spurs will be a favorite to win the title just because of Wembanyama's potential impact on both ends of the floor.
