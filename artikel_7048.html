V4C.ai: Databricks QA Engineer

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

V4C is a pure-play Databricks partner, and in under two years we have earned 850+ Databricks certifications across 325+ customers and been named Databricks' 2026 Partner of the Year (C&SI Emerging).

As a Databricks QA Engineer on our data engineering team you will own the quality gate for enterprise data platforms, working directly with customers. You will work on live enterprise data platforms alongside one of the most heavily Databricks-certified delivery teams in the market.

This is a QA role for people who understand data engineering, not general software QA pointed at a data project. You will validate Lakehouse pipelines, which means reasoning about row counts, schema drift, late-arriving data, and silent transformation errors no UI will ever surface. Our approach is automation-first: suites in Python and PySpark, running in CI, blocking promotion when they fail. You own release sign-off, which makes you the last person between a customer and bad data in production.

Pay Rate: $70 – $80 per hour (1099 independent contractor).

This is a contract engagement rather than a salaried employee role. Where a rate lands within the range depends on depth of data-pipeline testing experience and automation capability. Open to contractors anywhere in the United States.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.
