Cardata: Customer Success Manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Cardata is where driven people make an outsized impact. We’re a small but mighty team transforming how businesses manage vehicle reimbursement — replacing outdated programs with smart, automated solutions.

Our vision is bold: to provide the most comprehensive, intelligent, and automated software and services for creating ideal vehicle programs. And we’re building toward that vision every single day.

Join us and you’ll find challenge, growth, and the chance to deliver real impact — helping hundreds of companies and thousands of drivers across North America focus on the work that matters most to them.

At Cardata, customers are at the centre of everything we do. Our Customer Success team protects retention by helping customers get greater value from their reimbursement programs and building strong, lasting relationships.

As a Customer Success Manager, you'll own a portfolio of customer relationships after implementation, helping customers navigate their programs, resolve challenges, and achieve meaningful business outcomes while contributing to Cardata's GRR and NRR goals.

This is a hands-on role that combines customer relationship management with problem-solving and day-to-day execution and management of customer expectations. You'll develop a strong understanding of our platform and reimbursement workflows to confidently investigate issues, troubleshoot challenges, and guide customers toward practical solutions.

You'll thrive here if you're naturally curious and enjoy learning, are resourceful, highly organized, and an excellent communicator. You enjoy digging into complex problems, finding answers, and balancing competing priorities while ensuring customers feel supported and nothing falls through the cracks.

This is a remote-first role with occasional travel, approximately 2–3 times per quarter.

The expected base compensation for this role is 68,000–76,000 CAD annually, dependent on experience and competency. This role is also eligible for target variable incentives of 5,000-10,000 CAD, with actual earnings dependent on experience, skills, and performance.

This is a vacant position, and we are seeking someone who will bring a new perspective, contribute meaningfully to the team, and grow with the organization.

We’ll be honest — Cardata isn’t for everyone. If you thrive in a fast-paced environment, take extreme ownership, and constantly push yourself (and your work) to be the best, you’ll fit right in.

You’ll be surrounded by driven teammates who genuinely care — about the work, about our customers, and about helping each other succeed. Together, we take on ambitious goals and celebrate the wins along the way.
