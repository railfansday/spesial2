Marvel’s Newest Anti-Hero To Join The X-Men, Avengers, And Fantastic Four Is the Last Person Fans Expect

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Marvel induces an unexpected character into the exclusive group of heroes who have joined the X-Men, Avengers, and Fantastic Four. Both the Avengers and the X-Men have opened their doors to hundreds of heroes throughout the years. Joining the Avengers typically requires proven heroism and a willingness to answer Earth’s call whenever global or cosmic crises strike, regardless of power. Everyone from street-level vigilantes like Luke Cage and Daredevil to reformed villains like Hawkeye, Scarlet Witch, and Rogue to planet-breaking powerhouses like the Hulk and Sentry find a home in the same team. Likewise, the X-Men are both a strike force and a protective sanctuary for all persecuted mutants.

The Fantastic Four is the name of the Richards family's superhero team, but it has also evolved to become a hub for similar heroes. Whenever sudden absences pull the main quartet away, iconic heroes like Spider-Man, She-Hulk, Wolverine, Ghost Rider, Hulk, Crystal, Medusa, Storm, Black Panther, and Ant-Man step in to act as the Fantastic Four's temporary replacements. The Fantastic Four's creation of the Future Foundation has also transformed the Baxter Building into an international think tank for young geniuses, and it has welcomed Moloids, Uatu’s offspring, and Dragon Man.

While the Avengers and the X-Men share dozens of heroes, the Fantastic Four's considerably smaller roster prevents most of Marvel's characters from being card-carrying members of all three teams.

Following months of teasing that Felicia Hardy would permanently join the mutants in Black Cat #17, Marvel reveals that Emma Frost recruits Black Cat to infiltrate Cyclops’s Alaskan X-Men base and steal an experimental Sentinel processor. Emma telepathically disguises Felicia’s appearance, giving her brown hair, a silver mask, and a custom X-uniform, and also shields her mind. Even though Marvel officially confirmed that Black Cat doesn't become a permanent card-carrying X-Men member as initially teased, Felicia Hardy's covert mission under a direct order from one of mutantkind’s main leaders technically makes her an honorary X-Man.

Black Cat's undercover X-Men assignment completes a rare superhero trifecta that places her in the exclusive company of icons like Wolverine and Storm. Years earlier, Felicia earned official clearance and worked alongside the Avengers during storylines like Beyond and MacKay’s Avengers tie-ins. More recently, Felicia became an honorary member of the Fantastic Four during the 2026 Queen in Black crossover event, where she played a vital role in Reed Richards’s improvised plan to stop Hela after the Goddess of Death seized Knull’s symbiote throne. Ultimately, Black Cat is only an honorary member of all three teams, but her record is still impressive.

Only Wolverine and Storm have genuinely held official membership across all three of Marvel’s biggest superhero teams. Logan and Ororo Munroe forged legendary careers with the X-Men since their debut in Giant-Size X-Men, later became key Avengers mainstays, and then joined the Fantastic Four to replace the original squad at different points. Spider-Man famously joined the New Avengers in 2004, served as Johnny Storm’s chosen replacement in the Future Foundation, and held an honorary teaching post at the Jean Grey School for Higher Learning, though Peter Parker himself has outright rejected offers to become an official X-Man.

Beast and Rogue have both held official spots on the Avengers and X-Men, with Hank McCoy frequently collaborating on Baxter Building experiments alongside Reed Richards without ever formally joining the Fantastic Four. She-Hulk, The Thing, and Crystal have been official members of both the Fantastic Four and the Avengers, and they maintain a close relationship with mutantkind during crises like Avengers vs X-Men and Krakoa, yet none of them are mutants. Iceman, Firestar, and Namor have served on both the X-Men and the Avengers, and Namor has shared decades of tangled history alongside the Fantastic Four as both ally and riva, but he has never officially joined the team.
