Avatar: Seven Havens introduced fans to not one but two new Avatars in a world where the four nations no longer exist and the tides of public opinion are against them.

Created by Avatar: The Last Airbender creators Michael Dante DiMartino and Bryan Konietzko, Seven Havens is the third series and second follow-up to the 2005 Nickelodeon classic. It features the voices of Saheli Khan, Aishwarya Devan, Darren Barnet, Major Curda, Sakina Jaffrey, Akshay Khanna, and Dee Bradley Baker. In addition to Konietzko and DiMartino, Seven Havens is executive produced by Ethan Spaulding and co-executive produced by Sehaj Sethi.

Set 225 years after The Legend of Korra, Seven Havens follows twin sisters Pavi and Nisha as they try to uncover the mysteries of their past and combat a mysterious, spiritual fear-based anomaly. Instead of the four nations, the world of Avatar is now divided into seven havens, with this first season set in the haven of Ellora. Despite the world being afraid of the Avatar due to a cataclysm caused by Avatar Korra, the sisters ally with the modern incarnation of the Order of the White Lotus, most notably Captain Karthik and Jae, both airbenders.

One of the key threads of the season is that Pavi's chakras are blocked, and the climactic finale sees her unlock her root chakra, which was blocked by fear. After doing so, the young Avatar enters a cosmic state where she sees Korra and the past Avatars in a constellation form. This emotional moment hits harder following the ending of The Legend of Korra season 2, which saw Korra sever her past lives. In theory, this would be Pavi and other future avatars would never be able to convene with Aang, Roku, Kyoshi or any of their past lives.

Pavi seeing Korra and the others sent a wave of speculation through the Avatar audience about whether she had reconnected the Avatar Cycle once more. Speaking with IGN, DiMartino said the scene symbolized "something else," adding that "she has not reconnected; It can't happen that easily." He also clarified that Pavi "does have a connection with Korra, but the other stuff's more of a vision."

In addition to DiMartino, Sethi addressed whether Pavi had repaired her connection to her past lives or if that was something the series could tackle in future seasons. According to the Seven Havens showrunner, they chose to depict the past Avatars in these cosmic forms to indicate a deeper disconnection; likewise, that is also why Korra did not speak to Pavi.

"That's something we really had to work [on showing] visually that the connection is not complete," Sethi said. "It was not just, 'oh, let's show Korra for a second or something.' It was like, 'hey, she's sort of faded, right? She's not completely there.'" Despite Pavi unblocking her root chakra, her journey to becoming the Avatar is not going to be that simple. Sethi concluded:

We wanted it to not just be so easy as, "yup, now Korra can talk to you." That's something that still needs to progress in the series.

Future seasons of Seven Havens will visit the other titular havens, with each presumably representing another chakra as Ellora represented the root chakra. Potentially, this sets up a storyline in which Pavi and Nisha unblocking their chakras restores their connection to past Avatars.

Season one of Avatar: Seven Havens is now streaming on Paramount+, while season two releases in spring of 2027.

Set in a world ravaged by catastrophe, Avatar: Seven Havens introduces a new Avatar, an Earthbender navigating a perilous journey with her twin. Hunted by adversaries from both humanity and the spirit realm, they must unravel their origins to save the waning Seven Havens.

The Legend of Korra follows Korra, the next Avatar after Aang, as she leaves her secluded upbringing to master the elements and embrace her role as the world's spiritual protector. Arriving in Republic City, Korra faces political unrest, powerful new enemies, and a rapidly changing world where the balance between tradition and progress is constantly being challenged.
