Cam Newton To Sit With Past, Present & Future QBs For Weekly Series ‘4th & 1 Presents: Quarterback Club’ On Amazon Prime

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

EXCLUSIVE: Former NFL MVP Cam Newton is set to host weekly series 4th & 1 Presents: Quarterback Club, in which he’ll talk to past, present and future QBs about one of the most scrutinized positions in sports.

Every Tuesday during the NFL season, Newton will brings together rising rookies, established players and veteran quarterbacks in a private setting. Shot at Iconic Saga Studios in Atlanta, Newton and guests will unpack the pressures, mental hurdles, and triumphs of playing the position against the backdrop of their own experiences and careers. Guests are said to include “retired legends”. The first episode of Quarterback Club will be available to stream on Prime Video in the U.S., beginning Tuesday, November 10th. Future eps will also show on Amazon Prime. Watch on Deadline

“Quarterbacks carry a city every Sunday, then get a few minutes at a podium to explain it,” said Newton, the longtime Caroline Panthers QB who was the Heisman winner, number one overall pick and league MVP. “Quarterback Club is the room where we can finally be honest, with each other and with the fans.”

Premiere dates, trailers and additional guest details are due to be announced every Thursday.

The series is produced in-house by Iconic Saga Productions, with Cam Newton, Derrick Brown, Travis Montaque, Rudy Vegliante, and RJ Mathis II serving as executive producers. Distribution is managed in association with Creator Sports Entertainment Network (CSEN).

“There are football shows, debate shows, and podcasts, but there has never been a room where quarterbacks can sit down peer-to-peer and candidly talk to each other about what it actually takes to play the game,” said Derrick Brown.
