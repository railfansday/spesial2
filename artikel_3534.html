December 15 Is Officially A Dark Day For PlayStation Plus

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A number of games are scheduled to leave the PlayStation Plus Extra and Premium Game Catalog next month, and the list includes a sizable action RPG alongside several smaller, more unusual adventures. You can still finish these games before December 15 arrives, but once it does, you’ll have to either stop playing or purchase these games to continue playing. So, this is your cue to run through them before the deadline.

The names appearing on the current PlayStation Store’s “Last Chance to Play” list are:

There is an important regional catch, though. The list was first spotted on Japanese and Asian storefronts, and not every title is marked for removal in every region. Dark Rose Valkyrie is flagged as absent from some regional lists, while Root Letter and Jake Hunter: Prism of Eyes are listed for select regions only. To confirm what’s leaving your store in particular, make sure to check the Last Chance to Play section on your console.

Coming to the games, the biggest loss for players who enjoy action RPGs is arguably Granblue Fantasy: Relink. Available on both the PS4 and PS5, Cygames’ creation sends players through the Sky Realm, where they build a party of four and fight using characters with different weapons, skills and combat styles. It can be played solo or with up to three other players online, and its quest list gives those who enjoy its combat plenty to work through. Somerville is another unique experience for the PS4 and PS5 that you don’t want to miss. The sci-fi adventure follows a family separated during an alien invasion, with players exploring the aftermath, solving environmental puzzles and trying to find their way through a strange, hostile landscape.

The rest of the games are PS4 releases, but can be played on the PS5 as well. Fade to Silence is a survival RPG set in a frozen, post-apocalyptic world. Gathering supplies, building a refuge and surviving blizzards are central to the experience, so it’s a more demanding pick than Granblue Fantasy: Relink or Somerville. Dark Rose Valkyrie is a Japanese RPG set in an alternate version of 1929, where a virus turns people into monsters called Chimeras. Root Letter is all about a mystery visual novel built around the disappearance of a former pen pal and the letters she left behind. And lastly, Jake Hunter: Prism of Eyes brings a detective adventure featuring 14 scenarios, including new cases and stories that previously appeared on mobile.

Game Catalog titles do not work like the monthly games that are claimed through PlayStation Plus. Downloading a game from Extra or Premium does not mean you keep access after it leaves the catalog. For the subscription, access ends when a title is removed from the collection, although purchasing it later can restore access to its save data, provided you don’t delete that data. There’s no need to panic-buy anything, but it’s worth checking your regional list for the last chance event and deciding what you actually want to finish. If you have any of these games in mind and own a valid Plus subscription, make sure you finish them before December 15.
