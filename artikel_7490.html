Jubilee Media is capping off its most active election year with a live midterm special

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

November 3 is Election Day in the United States, and Jubilee Media is keeping its eye on the night's returns. The Gen Z-focused media company will host its first-ever election special as it tracks the results in several key races across the country.

Jubilee has made a name for itself with political coverage that walks down the center of the aisle on platforms like YouTube and Instagram. Earlier this year, it doubled down on that approach by launching The Split, a semi-regular news program designed as an antidote to siloed feeds on social media. With both liberal and conservative hosts, The Split is a Gen Z answer to the bygone CNN program Crossfire.
