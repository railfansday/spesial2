CapsoVision, Inc. (CV +0.00%) reported third quarter financial results and provided updates on the U.S. commercial launch of its AI-assisted reading tool and the progress of its colon imaging platform. Management reported the FDA clearance of AI Highlights for CapsoCam Plus on Sept. 25, followed by its commercial launch on Nov. 2. The company completed clinical enrollment for the second-generation CapsoCam Colon study and confirmed plans for an FDA submission by year-end 2026. Management indicated that the $17.8 million net proceeds from a registered direct offering strengthened the balance sheet to support the AI launch and clinical pipeline. The company also expanded its clinical footprint for pancreatic cancer detection and planned a new esophageal varices trial for the second half of 2027.
