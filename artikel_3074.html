Google Plans to Spend $17 Billion on Wyoming Data Center

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Alphabet Inc.’s Google will spend $17 billion to build a data center in Wyoming, formally putting a price tag on the proposal two days after voters across the US delivered a broad rebuke to the development of such massive projects for artificial intelligence work.
