Jerash Holdings (US), Inc. (JRSH +14.92%) reported fiscal second quarter results that management attributed to increased shipment volumes to major U.S. customers and operational efficiency gains from automation. The company reported that its production capacity is fully committed through June 2027, prompting strategic initiatives to expand throughput via facility renovations and shift additions. Management stated that logistics challenges in the Middle East continue to elevate transportation costs for imported materials, which influenced the financial outlook for the upcoming period. Strategic partnerships with Korean apparel groups and a tax concession in Jordan provided additional support to profitability and volume growth during the quarter.
