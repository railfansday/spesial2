You haven't really specified what you want to accomplish with options.

With options:
- you can reduce risk, and reduce the expected returns
- you can increase risk, and increase the expected returns

And do this in many different complicated ways. But there is no free lunch, professional have squeezed out any arbitrage opportunities a long time ago.

It's far simpler and cheaper to simply adjust your stocks/bonds allocation to set the appropriate risk/return level.
