App Store and Google Play screenshot sizes in November 2026: what the spec pages actually say

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

This article was created with the help of AI. The sizes were checked against Apple's and Google's spec pages on November 6, 2026, and the check script was run on macOS 15.2 (Python 3.14, Pillow 13.0.0) with screenshots from an iOS 18.3 simulator in Xcode 16.2. The spec pages can change, so the upload page in App Store Connect or Play Console is the final word.

Apple reorganized its screenshot spec page, and the sizes a lot of people keep in their notes don't match it anymore. Below is where both stores stand right now, with links to the original pages.

If you still think in "6.9-inch" and "6.5-inch", those labels are gone from the page text. Apple's Screenshot specifications page now uses rows like "iPhone with Dynamic Island (large display)", and each row lists the devices it covers.

Landscape is the same pair, swapped. The older Home Button sizes are still listed.

At the top of the page, under "Required device sizes":

iPhone: At least one screenshot for iPhone with Dynamic Island (medium display).

But in the table, the Face ID (large display) row is marked "Required if app runs on iPhone and screenshots for iPhone with Dynamic Island (large display) aren't provided."

Then there's the scaling note. If your UI is the same across sizes, "you only need to provide screenshots for the highest required resolution. App Store Connect automatically scales them down for smaller device sizes."

From the page alone, you can't tell which slot App Store Connect will insist on for a particular app. One way to cover both readings: design at 1320 × 2868, open your app's version page in App Store Connect to see which screenshot slots are marked as required, and export a 1206 × 2622 set as well if it asks for the medium size. Apple doesn't spell this out anywhere, so treat it as a workaround.

The extra export costs almost nothing, because two pairs of sizes are nearly the same shape:

Scaling one into the other changes the proportions by less than 0.1%.

There's also a new "iPhone Duo" row, with an outer display (1398 × 2034) and an inner display (2007 × 2853). The page says: "Starting in April 2027, screenshots will be required for any app using the iOS 27.1 SDK or later." If you don't target it, there's nothing to do yet. It's worth a line on next spring's checklist, though.

What size should App Store screenshots be in 2026? For iPhone, the page names "iPhone with Dynamic Island (medium display)" (1206 × 2622 or 1179 × 2556) as the required size. It also marks Face ID (large display) (1284 × 2778 or 1242 × 2688) as required when you don't provide the large Dynamic Island size (up to 1320 × 2868). Your app's version page in App Store Connect settles it. For iPad, 2064 × 2752 or 2048 × 2732.

Do I need iPad screenshots? Only if your app runs on iPad, and then you need at least one 13-inch screenshot.

What are the Google Play screenshot requirements? JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha, each side between 320 and 3,840 px, and the long side no more than twice the short side. You need at least 2 to publish and can add up to 8 per device type. For the recommendation formats, upload at least 4 at 1080 × 1920 (9:16) or larger.

What tools can I use to create App Store screenshots online? Figma or another design tool, with frames at the exact sizes. fastlane's snapshot and frameit if you'd rather automate it from UI tests. There are also browser-based screenshot generators; compare their export sizes with the table above, and run the pre-upload check on whatever they give you.
