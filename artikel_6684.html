Bella Hadid Hard Launches a New Icy Blond Hair Color

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The supermodel brought a bit of extra frost to the stadium while attending an NHL game

Bella Hadid is no stranger to major hair changes—seamlessly going from chunky highlights to chestnut brown just this year—but her latest color debut may be her most shocking yet.

Hadid took to Instagram to share a series of photos from Saturday night’s Colorado Avalanche game, which honored Hockey Fights Cancer, a charitable organization through the National Hockey League, and among the ice and jerseys was the supermodel’s fresh icy-blond hair, which stood out in a sea of team colors.

Even though the model wore a Colorado Avalanche hat, her bright bleach-blond color and blunt shoulder-length bob peeked through. Hadid’s hair has skewed more blond lately, but this new color is icier and brighter, and it’s shorter, which suggests the debut look may be a wig disguise.

If Hadid did take the plunge and dye her hair, it’s likely by New York–based celebrity colorist Jenna Perry, who has colored Hadid’s hair in the past and is responsible for Hadid’s blond and brunette colors, plus other celebrity colors like Kendall Jenner’s red and several of Kaia Gerber’s blond and brunette variations.

Hadid isn’t the only celebrity to debut a new bright blond color this week. As a departure from his classic brunette hair, Jacob Elordi was recently spotted with fresh blond hair peeking out of a beanie hat while with girlfriend Kendall Jenner at a Broadway show, according to photos shared on the social media account Deux Moi.

Is bleach blond the new color for fall, or are Hadid and Elordi’s new hues just a phase?
