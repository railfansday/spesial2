Pokémon Go Wild Area Gigantamax Machamp Timed Research and rewards

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Get yourself a free Gigantamax Machamp ahead of the global Wild Area event

The 2026 Pokémon Go Wild Area in-person events are kicking off in Sendai, Japan and Mexico City, which means that even us global players not attending get a treat: a Gigantamax Machamp Timed Research set.

Notably, Duraludon, which will make its Gigantamax debut during the Wild Area event, is weak against fighting-type moves, so this is a pretty good opportunity if you don't already have a Gigantamax Machamp. Dialga, which will be a Dynamax target during the event, is also weak against fighting-type moves. (Palkia isn't, though. You win some, you lose some.)
