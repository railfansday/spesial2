Three Special Football Jerseys Go On Sale To Celebrate Xbox's 25th Anniversary

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

I've always been a big fan of wearing sports merch - you'll find me sporting a Vancouver Canucks hoodie or jacket most days of the week! However, when I was looking around for some kind of Xbox jersey in preparation for the Xbox FanFest event in London last month, I was disappointed to find basically nothing except old Seattle Sounders shirts.

That's all changed this week, as Xbox has teamed up with a company called Dyenamik to produce three Xbox "Jerseys" to celebrate the brand's 25th anniversary, all of which are now available to buy.

You can either purchase them individually (for £70 here in the UK with worldwide shipping available), or grab a special bundle of all three for £175 with a collector's box, although that one's limited to just 200 people.

So, let's go through them - here's a look at all three:

The black body carries a tonal X across the front and back, shaded in darker panels to echo the raised X on the original console's casing. The sleeve displays the original XBOX logo graphic while the retro wordmark takes pride of place on the jersey front. Neon green piping runs along the raglan seams, complimenting the vibrant accents in the collar and cuffs.

A pure white jersey with XBOX green panels sweeping down the body, finished with a banded silver pixel pattern. The green collar and double-striped cuffs are complimented with silver piping along the raglan seams. Across the chest is the XBOX 360 wordmark in its original green and grey. The XBOX sphere is embroidered in green on the left chest and the Dyenamik emblem sits on the right. On the back, just below the collar, is the comforting visual of a green Ring of Light.

Look into the green stripes and you'll find tonal hardware detailing: circuit-board traces, vent grilles, and port outlines, layered green on green. The pattern is inspired by the semi-transparent XBOX consoles that let you see the components inside the shell. Up front is the modern XBOX wordmark in white, with an embroidered XBOX sphere on the left chest and the Dyenamik emblem on the right. On the back, just below the collar, sits the A, B, X and Y button layout, an XBOX signature and a configuration that's felt like home for 25 years.

Twenty-five years of XBOX, in one drop. The Anniversary Collection brings together all three Dyenamik XBOX heritage jerseys, the X, the 360 and the ABXY, and crowns the set with the strictly limited XBOX Collector's Box. Three eras of the console you grew up with, made to wear and made to keep.

So, there we go! I'm personally a big fan of the mostly black Xbox design, but I bet many people will be drawn to the Xbox 360 version, or maybe the ultra-colourful third jersey.

£70 is a lot for a shirt, but any kind of sports merch seems to be competitively priced these days, and I don't believe there's anything else like these on the market. A fun little partnership for sure!
