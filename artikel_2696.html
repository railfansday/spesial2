CFP rankings reveal for Georgia football. See where the Bulldogs landed in second bracket

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

A 24-team playoff field is coming in the 2027 season, but Georgia football wants to make a run to win it all in the final 12-team playoff.

The second College Football Playoff rankings will be released Tuesday in a 9 p.m. show on ESPN.

Georgia, No. 1 in the coaches and AP poll, could find itself in that same spot in the selection committee's order after ranking No. 2 in the top 25 debut last week. The Bulldogs could move up after top-ranked Miami's 31-24 loss at Notre Dame.

Georgia last was No. 1 in the CFP on Nov. 28, 2023 when it was 12-0 before losing in the SEC Championship game to Alabama and falling to No. 6 in the final rankings.

"I don't feel like a ranking really should impress anybody or make anybody feel big," Georgia safety Rasean Dinkins said Tuesday. "I just feel we've got to go week by week, take it step by step."

Georgia dominated Ole Miss 42-7 Saturday night in Oxford.

The Bulldogs (8-1) return home to play Missouri Saturday at 3:30 p.m., and then close out SEC play at South Carolina on Nov. 21.

"Zero doubt that they're the benchmark for the league right now and the way Coach (Kirby) Smart runs his program, the philosophical identity he has in offense, defense and special teams," Missouri coach Eli Drinkwitz said on Tuesday. "The way his team plays consistently down in and down out. It's what everybody in our conference is chasing."

Here are the top 12 teams in the first reveal on Nov. 3

Georgia would be the No. 2 seed and play the winner of No. 7 seed Texas and No. 10 Ole Miss in a quarterfinal based on the first rankings. Check back later tonight for the updated projections.

The first-round matchups based on the first set of rankings would be: No. 12 Boise State at No. 5 Alabama, No. 11 Notre Dame at No. 6 Kentucky, No. 10 Ole Miss at No. 7 Texas and No. 9 BYU at No. 8 UCLA. The first-round byes are No. 1 Miami, No. 2 Georgia, No. 3 Utah A&M and No. 4 Indiana.

Teams on the outside of the bracket this week include No. 12 Ohio State, No. 13 Tennessee and No. 14 LSU.

Check back later tonight for the updated projections.

The playoff expanded from a four-team to 12-team field in 2024. This year, the four Power Conference champions are guaranteed a spot in the playoff. The four highest ranked teams get byes regardless of conference. This year, the top 3 seeds choose their bowl path in rank order. The CFP quarterfinal bowls are the Cotton, Fiesta, Peach and Rose.

The second CFP rankings will be revealed during the top 25 rankings show on Tuesday Nov. 10 at 9 p.m. on ESPN. The final rankings will be released Dec. 6.
