Packers QB Jordan Love reacts to MVP chants at place nearly 2,000 miles away from Lambeau

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

After stepping into the starting quarterback role following Aaron Rodgers' departure, Jordan Love has gradually established himself as the driving force behind the Green Bay Packers' offense. Love delivered another outstanding performance to help Green Bay secure its fifth win in the last six games against the New England Patriots on Sunday, earning MVP chants from the crowd at Foxborough in the process.

During his postgame press conference, Love offered his honest reaction to hearing MVP chants from fans at a stadium nearly 2,000 miles away from Lambeau Field.

"Man, it's awesome," Love said. "It's definitely a very cool feeling to be able to hear fans chanting that. But that's all I can say, man, it's a really cool feeling. And I appreciate all the fans, you know what I mean? We got the best fans being able to show up and pack the stadium out and be the last ones standing there.

"So it's definitely cool, but just gotta keep going and really just block everything else out and keep focusing on what I can do as an individual and to be able to help our team just keep getting better."

Love's continued development has become valuable to Green Bay as the franchise builds its future around him. His latest outing against the Patriots provided another example of his growing influence on the offense.

The Packers' star quarterback completed 23 of his 29 pass attempts for 372 yards and three touchdowns without throwing an interception in Green Bay's 45-27 win.

Through nine games of the 2026 season, Love has accumulated 2,618 passing yards, 19 touchdowns and six interceptions while completing 63% of his passes. His dominant play so far has already put him in the MVP conversation this season.

Meanwhile, Sunday's victory over New England improved Green Bay's record to 6-3.
