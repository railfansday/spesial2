Bengals designate LB Swayze Bozeman for return from IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Linebacker Swayze Bozeman is back at Bengals practice this week.

The Bengals announced that they have designated Bozeman for return from injured reserve on Thursday. He will be able to practice for the next 21 days and can return to the active roster as soon as Monday night's game against the Commanders.

Bozeman played in four games before landing on injured reserve with a forearm injury. He had four tackles in those appearances while playing on special teams.

The Bengals also have wide receiver Andrei Iosivas, cornerback Jalen Davis, and guard Brian Parker in the pipeline to return. They have until Monday afternoon to activate any of the four players in time to play against Washington.
