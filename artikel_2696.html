Rivalry Week Is Here: SEC Fan’s Guide to College Football’s Biggest Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

There is no week quite like rivalry week in college football. Every season, the final week of the regular season brings some of the sport's biggest rivalries, emotional moments and unforgettable matchups. For SEC fans, this week is especially important, with bragging rights, championship hopes and years of history all coming together.

The 2026 rivalry week schedule has plenty of games that fans should circle on their calendars. Whether it is the Iron Bowl, Egg Bowl or Palmetto Bowl, there is no shortage of reasons to spend the weekend watching college football.

There is no bigger rivalry in the state of Alabama than the Iron Bowl. Alabama and Auburn will meet Saturday night, with the Crimson Tide entering as one of the nation's top ranked teams.

For Alabama, the goal is to protect its position among the country's best teams and finish the regular season with another rivalry victory. Auburn, meanwhile, has an opportunity to change the narrative of its season by knocking off its biggest rival.

The Iron Bowl has produced some of the most memorable moments in college football history, and another chapter awaits under the lights.

Thanksgiving weekend also brings the annual Egg Bowl between Ole Miss and Mississippi State.

The rivalry is about much more than the final record. Both programs understand how important this game is to their fan bases, and neither wants to spend the offseason hearing about a loss to its biggest rival.

Ole Miss is currently ranked No. 25., while Mississippi State will be looking to play spoiler. The Egg Bowl is known for its unpredictable moments, making it one of the most entertaining games of rivalry week.

Another rivalry that deserves plenty of attention is the Palmetto Bowl between South Carolina and Clemson.

The two programs have a long history of competing for bragging rights across the state, and this year's matchup carries plenty of excitement.

South Carolina is currently ranked No. 21, while Clemson will have the opportunity to make a statement against its in state rival. For Gamecock fans, beating Clemson is always a major priority, regardless of what happened earlier in the season.

There are several other important matchups that SEC fans should keep an eye on.

Texas A&M and Texas will renew their rivalry Friday night in a matchup featuring two ranked programs. The return of this rivalry has quickly become one of the biggest events on the college football calendar.

Georgia will also face Georgia Tech in Clean, Old Fashioned Hate. With the Bulldogs ranked No. 1, the Yellow Jackets have a difficult challenge ahead of them.

Tennessee and Vanderbilt will close out their rivalry matchup Saturday night, while Kentucky and Louisville will meet in another important in-state showdown.

Rivalry week is about more than rankings and postseason opportunities. It is about traditions, family bragging rights and games that fans remember for years.

Some teams will finish the regular season celebrating, while others will head into the offseason with disappointment. That is what makes this week so special.

For SEC fans, there is no better way to close out the regular season than with a full weekend of rivalry football. The stakes are high, the emotions are even higher, and one thing is guaranteed: bragging rights are on the line.
