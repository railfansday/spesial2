Former teammate calls Aaron Rodgers' Mike McCarthy takedown 'despicable'

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Aaron Rodgers' scorched earth approach with Mike McCarthy was like the drop of a bomb. The fallout from its explosion has carried further than just the current Pittsburgh Steelers roster.

Former Green Bay Packers players from the Rodgers and McCarthy era – 2006 to 2018 – have started to weigh in on how the veteran quarterback handled his split from the Steelers. Greg Jennings, the wide receiver who spent seven years in Green Bay, and guard T.J. Lang, an eight-year Packer, both expressed their surprise at Rodgers' approach.

Neither player confirmed the quarterback's stories about McCarthy, and both had their criticisms for his explosive appearance on ESPN's "Pat McAfee Show" on Wednesday, Nov. 25.

AARON RODGERS: Veteran QB goes scorched earth on Mike McCarthy after Steelers benching

Rodgers' scathing comments about McCarthy came hours after the coach benched the quarterback in favor of Mason Rudolph late Tuesday, Nov. 24. In the aftermath of the "Pat McAfee Show" appearance, Pittsburgh waived the veteran quarterback ahead of Week 12.

Here's what Jennings and Lang said about Rodgers' comments:

The former Packers wide receiver said he could not corroborate any of Rodgers' stories about McCarthy during the time they shared in Green Bay. He also noted his surprise at the lack of questions for A.J. Hawk, a former linebacker for the Packers who spent the 2006 through 2014 seasons in Green Bay and a mainstay on the "Pat McAfee Show."

"A large part of me just wanted someone to ask A.J., who was sitting right there, 'A.J., do you recall any of this stuff?' Please, someone, ask A.J., 'Can you confirm, or can you second any of this?'" Jennings said on FS1.

The former receiver said he also reached out to some of his other previous teammates in Green Bay, who also said they could not confirm any of Rodgers' stories about McCarthy.

Lang, who spent the final two years of his career with the Lions, made an appearance on Detroit sports radio station 97.1 The Ticket on Wednesday, Nov. 25. He said Rodgers' decision to air out all of his grievances publicly was the exact kind of behavior the quarterback used to call out.

"I think it's despicable," Lang said. "I think that Aaron Rodgers just became everything that he has b----ed about and moaned about over the last 10 years with former coaches, former teammates. Airing grievances out, giving opinions out in the media.

"He has blacklisted I don't know how many former teammates from just simple comments telling the truth because he was sick of it."

The two-time Pro Bowler also cast doubt on some of Rodgers' comments, given that the Steelers chose to bench, then release the quarterback. Lang specifically called Rodgers out for saying he and Steelers general manager Omar Khan shared laughs about McCarthy's miscues as the head coach behind his back.

"Then why would Omar Khan side with Mike McCarthy? Why would Omar Khan allow Mike McCarthy to bench Aaron Rodgers and basically side with Mike McCarthy?" Lang said.
