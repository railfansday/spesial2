Eagles injury update: Some good news in latest report

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Eagles injury update: Some good news in latest report originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

Eagles cornerback Quinyon Mitchell was upgraded to a limited participant on the team's injury report on Tuesday after beginning the week listed as a non-participant.

Because the Eagles' held a walkthrough on Tuesday, their injury report was an estimation, just like Monday's. But this is definitely good news for Mitchell. Earlier in the day, defensive coordinator Vic Fangio said he thought Mitchell would be "fine" for Thanksgiving against Dallas.

It's also worth noting that Saquon Barkley (neck), Dallas Goedert (shin) and Tanner McKee (rib) were all upgraded to full participants after being listed as limited to start the week.

Barkley might not get back to 100% for the rest of the season, but he will be able to play on Thursday after leaving the Steelers game early with what looked like a stinger.

Here's the Eagles' full injury report from Tuesday:

DNP: OLB Arnold Ebiketie (oblique), CB Jonathan Jones (ankle)

Limited: CB Quinyon Mitchell (hamstring), LB Smael Mondon (ankle), RB Will Shipley (foot/illness)

Full: RB Saquon Barkley (neck/illness), DT Jalen Carter (quad), DB Michael Carter (abdomen), WR Britain Covey (knee), TE Dallas Goedert (shin), OLB Jonathan Greenard (ankle), QB Tanner McKee (rib), CB Kelee Ringo (neck), WR DeVonta Smith (hamstring)

In addition to Mitchell, linebacker Smael Mondon (ankle) was also upgraded from DNP to limited on Tuesday. That's good news because he would be the Eagles' top backup behind Jihaad Campbell and Jeremiah Trotter Jr., who are both starters with Zack Baun (back) on Injured Reserve.

With this quick turnaround, the Eagles are not planning on having a full practice at all this week. It'll be all walkthroughs to prepare for this NFC East battle on Thursday afternoon.
