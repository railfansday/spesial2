RSM Classic washout: Round 3 Sunday with a Monday finish

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

ST. SIMONS ISLAND, Ga. – For the first time in over a decade back-to-back PGA Tour winners will be decided in unscheduled Monday finishes.

Following five days of nearly nonstop rain, including more than 2 inches overnight, Tour officials were forced to cancel third-round play at the RSM Classic on Saturday, a move that will now necessitate a Monday finish.

Last week's Austin Championship was also forced into a Monday finish because of adverse weather.

"We played the first two days in some wind and rain and we already had very wet conditions, and then the storm that has been coming up the Northeast coast of Florida, it finally got here and just saturated the fairways," said Steve Rintoul, the Tour's vice president of rules and officiating. "It just got to the situation where the fairways were so saturated it would just jeopardize the integrity of the competition."

The third round will air on Golf Channel from 1 to 4 p.m. on Sunday.

The RSM marks the end to the Tour's fall season with the top 100 in FedExCup points earning full status next year.

Michael Thorbjornsen, Marco Penge and Rasmus Neergaard-Petersen co-lead at 9 under. Six players are tied for fourth, one shot back.
