NFL rumors: The absolutely garbage NFC South has league mulling bombshell playoff changes

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The NFC South has once again been the NFL's symbol of divisional ineptitude this year, with the Carolina Panthers currently in first place with a 4-5 record on the season. It appears that for a second straight year, the division will have its winner reach the playoffs with a losing record, which understandably frustrates the fans of other teams with better records who will inevitably miss out on a key spot in the postseason mix.

Although the NFL has long stuck by its guns amid fan complaints about other poor divisions through the years, the NFC South has been so bad recently that that may finally change.

"…there are rumblings from the league office about reviving the discussion from the 2025 offseason about whether teams that win their divisions with sub.-500 records should be guaranteed home playoff games and top-four seeds in their conference," reported NFL insider Dan Graziano of ESPN on X, formerly Twitter.

Graziano referenced a proposal from a couple of years ago put forth by the Detroit Lions that would keep the selection of playoff teams, including division winners, the same, but seed them in the postseason according to their record, and not give division winners an automatic home game.

"…its supporters in the league office continue to support it, and people around the league would not be surprised to see it resurface this offseason," reported Graziano.

Last year, the Panthers won the division and actually put up a strong fight against the Los Angeles Rams in the playoffs, and it's likely that whoever wins the division this year will once again be a heavy home underdog to their opponent, who will almost assuredly have a better record than them.
