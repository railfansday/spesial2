Legendary Barcelona manager Pep Guardiola bluntly rules out future Camp Nou return: &#8216;I won&#8217;t&#8217;

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Pep Guardiola has ruled out returning to Barcelona as manager, delivering a clear message to the fans who still continue to hope he might one day come back to the club where he established himself as one of football's most influential coaches.

The former Barça manager made the declaration during an interview with the official Bundesliga magazine (h/t Fabrizio Romano), where he was asked about the possibility of returning to Bayern Munich.

While Guardiola did not completely dismiss a future comeback to Germany, he was unequivocal about Barcelona.

Guardiola's refusal to return is particularly striking because of the extraordinary success he enjoyed at Barcelona between 2008 and 2012.

He won two Champions League titles, in 2009 and 2011, while establishing a team whose possession-based football influenced clubs across Europe.

Guardiola is one of Barcelona's most successful managers. (Photo by David Ramos/Getty Images)

The Catalan's connection with Barça remains strong among supporters, many of whom have continued to imagine him returning to the dugout at Spotify Camp Nou.

However, his latest comments clarify that the possibility should no longer be treated as a realistic managerial option.

When asked about a possible return to Germany, Guardiola did not completely close the door on Bayern Munich, but he explicitly ruled out returning to Barcelona as a manager.

"I don't know. I'm still in touch with Uli Hoeneß and Rummenigge, but should I return to a club I've already managed?

"For example, I won't return to Barcelona.

Guardiola confirms he will not return to Barcelona. (Photo by Alex Pantling/Getty Images)

" And Bayern Munich have a great coach like Kompany. After him, a new, young coach will come in," he said.

Guardiola left Manchester City at the end of the 2025/26 season after a decade in charge.

He won six Premier League titles and guided the club to its first Champions League crown in 2023, completing a historic treble that season. City confirmed he would continue working with the City Football Group as a global ambassador.

More recently, Guardiola has said his next coaching role will be with a national team, although he has not set a date for returning to management.
