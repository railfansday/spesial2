Nets’ Moritz Wagner to miss Raptors game with knee issue

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

TORONTO — The Nets frontcourt is going from thin to downright worrisome, with backup center Moritz Wagner out with a knee issue.

Wagner left Wednesday's win in Milwaukee in the first quarter and was ruled out with left knee soreness. Now the only pure center the Nets have besides Day'Ron Sharpe will miss Friday's game in Toronto with left knee injury management.

"I was about to sub him in, and I saw him limping, and I was like I got to save him from himself," coach Jordi Fernández said. "He was there to do whatever it took for his team and his teammates, and at the end I decided to not bring him back in.

"The good thing — the great thing — is he was cheering for everybody. He was cheering for Grant [Nelson] the whole time, and that means a lot for the group, and it speaks to the type of person he is."

Moritz Wagner (knee) will not play against the Raptors on Friday night in Toronto. Getty Images

It's the same knee in which Wagner suffered a torn left ACL on Dec. 21, 2024. That ended his season and sidelined him until January 2026.

Sharpe logged 31-plus minutes Wednesday and could be asked to shoulder another heavy load against the Raptors, who've lost seven of eight.

He had 11 points and a season-high 14 rebounds against the Bucks, his fifth double-double to match his total from all of last season. Sharpe also added five assists and four steals.

"My effort, my motor is always going to be me," said Sharpe. "This is part of my game. It's a part of who I am, the hard work I play with, the hard effort I play with."

Nelson had just one point against the Bucks, but six rebounds, including three on the offensive glass. He was a plus-6 in just 13:04.

"The offensive rebounds for us were huge, six rebounds. He was a plus on the court, a great 13 minutes. He definitely was a big part of this win," said Fernández.

"Grant obviously not necessarily was expecting to go there for as much as he did," added Egor Dëmin. "But with Moe being out of the game, he had to go out there and compete. I talked to him in halftime and he was extremely ready to go, and just do what's best for the team."

Noah Clowney broke out of his extended funk against Milwaukee.

The young forward had 17 points and finished plus-14, both season highs. He shot 7-for-13, grabbed five rebounds and scored nine points in the fourth quarter.

"It was great to see him be aggressive, seeing the matchup he has and being aggressive off the closeout and just letting the ball fly when he had opportunity," said Sharpe. "He helps us out a lot, especially with the defensive presence he brings, his versatility on the offensive end. We just need him to continue to be confident in who he is and bring it out every night."
