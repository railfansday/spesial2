The Killers’ Dave Keuning Says He Survived a Major Stroke: ‘By Some Miracle I Am Fine’

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The guitarist says doctors gave him a 40% chance of survival — and that the first thing he checked after surgery was whether he could still play.

The Killers guitarist Dave Keuning has revealed he suffered a major stroke two weeks ago and went straight into surgery.

Keuning, 50, shared the news in an Instagram post on Thursday (Nov. 26), writing that a blood clot had cut off the blood supply to the right side of his brain. “Two weeks ago, I had a major stroke, and thanks to my wife acting fast, I went straight to the hospital and into surgery,” he wrote. “The first hour after a stroke is critical when it comes to getting to the hospital.”

“By the time I arrived at the hospital, I was blind in one eye and had lost function on my left side,” Keuning continued, adding that even then he didn’t realize he was having a stroke. “And yes I can play guitar just as good as before,” he wrote. “It was the first thing I checked when I woke up from surgery.”

“I feel extremely fortunate that I am alive and well, and that I can use my hands and feet and talk just like before,” he added. “I am thankful for my wife, my son, my daughter, and all of my bandmates, who I feel closer to than ever.”

A founding member of the Las Vegas band, Keuning stepped back from touring with the Killers in 2017 and has stayed off the road since, though he returned to the studio for 2021’s Pressure Machine. He has also released two solo albums under the name Keuning: Prismism (2019) and A Mild Case of Everything (2021).

The Killers earned their first No. 1 on the Billboard 200 with 2017’s Wonderful Wonderful and have notched eight No. 1 albums on the Official U.K. Albums Chart, most recently with 2023’s Rebel Diamonds. Their 2004 debut, Hot Fuss, produced “Mr. Brightside,” which in 2021 became the first song to log 260 weeks on the U.K. singles chart.

Brandon Flowers released his third solo album, Thrasher, in August, and has said he expects the next Killers album to arrive in 2027.
