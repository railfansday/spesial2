Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Why is the 18-64 year old cohort treated worse than kids or elderly with respect to health insurance?
Part of this is the elderly have paid Medicare taxes all their life so the program can support them when they are too old to work. And there are still significant costs associated with Medicare.

Kids are vulnerable and unable to provide for themselves.

People in the 18-64 age range are expected to get a job that either has health insurance benefits or if the income is low, use subsidized Obamacare. If you are poor, there is Medicaid.
And yet, working 18-64 y/o's either cannot afford health insurance through their employer and/or are still going bankrupt due to medical bills even with it.
