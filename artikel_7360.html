Britt Nolan Makes the Jump From CCO to CEO at McCann NY

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

McCann has promoted Britt Nolan to CEO of its flagship New York office effective immediately, the agency shared with ADWEEK.

The former North America chief creative officer for McCann will lead the New York office’s business and strategy while overseeing all U.S. clients. He will report to Andrés Ordóñez, who was promoted to global chairman and global chief creative officer of McCann in October.

Over the past year, the two have worked closely together:

“I’ve seen firsthand what an extraordinary leader, creative mind, and human being he is,” Ordóñez wrote in a statement. “He understands our clients, our people, and our culture, and has been instrumental in building the momentum we’re seeing in New York.”
