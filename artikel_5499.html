Rocket Lab stock slips after revenue beat couldn't offset wider-than-expected loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Quarterly revenue rose 73% to $267.8 million, but net loss widened to $79.7 million from $18.3 million a year earlier

Rocket Lab reported third-quarter results on Monday, with revenue of $267.8 million, up 73% from a year earlier, and a net loss of 12 cents a share.

Rocket Lab stock fell 6.6% in after-hours trading Monday to $54.97. Revenue topped the $255.71 million analysts expected, according to Benzinga. Analysts had forecast a loss of 6 cents a share.

The net loss was $79.7 million, compared with $18.3 million in the third quarter of 2025. The operating loss was $79.2 million, up from $59.0 million. The quarter included a $27.3 million loss on extinguishment of debt and $21.3 million in transaction costs. The company recorded a $3.5 million income tax provision, versus a $41.1 million benefit a year earlier.

Adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed to $18.0 million from $26.3 million. Operating cash flow was positive $7.2 million on a GAAP basis.

Backlog reached a record $3.57 billion, up 52% from the end of the second quarter, the company said. It signed about $1 billion in new launch contracts across its Electron, HASTE and Neutron rockets, plus $470 million for space systems. That brought the total launch backlog to 117 launches.

Neutron contracts from constellation operators totaled more than $515 million in the quarter, lifting that rocket's backlog to 16 missions. The company said the base launch price rose to $70 million for several of those missions. It said the maiden flight is slated for 2027 at the earliest.

For the fourth quarter, Rocket Lab expects revenue of $270 million to $290 million and an adjusted EBITDA loss of $14 million to $20 million. It guided to GAAP gross margins of 34% to 36%.

Rocket Lab had moved the target for Neutron's first launch to 2027 in an October filing with the Securities and Exchange Commission. The company had planned for the reusable medium-lift rocket to debut in late 2025. In the filing, it said the shift came as the team continued development and added process improvements.

Rocket Lab also builds the Electron and HASTE small-lift rockets, which it describes as its current launch vehicles.
