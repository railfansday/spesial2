Real Madrid are still following Borussia Dortmund star ahead of summer transfer window &#8211; report

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Amid the sporting crisis that they were drowning in, Real Madrid found a way out by spending smartly on the transfer market over two windows.

While last year's signings were all let-downs, the recruitment this time around has been far better with Ibrahima Konate, Marc Cucurella and Bernardo Silva all finding a role in Jose Mourinho's plans.

There is still, however, some need for a new centre-back given Antonio Rudiger's age and Eder Militao's fitness, and the board is already working actively to identify targets.

As reported by Fabrizio Romano, Real Madrid are back monitoring the situation of Nico Schlotterbeck – a player they have held an interest in for a long time now.

Schlotterbeck has been a Real Madrid target for long. (Photo by Alexander Hassenstein/Getty Images)

Los Blancos are still following the player and see him as an apt centre-back signing given the club's situation, both in the short and the long run.

After all, the player is only 26 years of age and is just entering his prime. In that sense, he is at the right stage of his career to transition to a club like Real Madrid.

Schlotterbeck would appear to be the perfect signing for Real Madrid as a long-term replacement for Antonio Rudiger, not only for his quality but also his leadership.

The central defender has an imposing physical and aerial presence – something that is much valued at the Valdebebas.

Barcelona and Real Madrid are set to battle it out for the player. (Photo by Angel Martinez/Getty Images)

His addition to a department already boasting Dean Huijsen, Eder Militao and Ibrahima Konate would be the perfect development for Real Madrid.

Yet, Real Madrid are not the only party interested in signing him. Their direct arch-rivals, Barcelona, also see him as a top target and can possibly offer him a starting role with more conviction given their defensive frailties.

The price to sign the player is expected to be around the €60 million ballpark – a bargain given the current rates and state of inflation on the market.

Needless to say, the final decision will boil down to the player's preference, but Real Madrid will try their level best to convince him and lure him to the Santiago Bernabeu in the summer. They do, however, maintain alternative targets regardless.
