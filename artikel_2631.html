Indianapolis Colts Injury Report: Week 12 Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Indianapolis Colts today released their Wednesday injury report ahead of week 12 of the NFL season against the New York Giants.

Cornerback Sauce Gardner (calf) was a full participant at practice today. Fellow cornerback Charvarius Ward (knee) missed practice today. Gardner looks set to return this week whilst Ward is heading towards another game missed through injury.

Left tackle Bernhard Raimann (concussion) was a full participant at practice today m. Whilst fellow offensive lineman, right tackle Jalen Travis (knee) missed practice. Raimann looks set to pass through the concussion protocol this week in time to play while Travis looks set to miss this weeks game.

Cornerback Cam Taylor-Britt (groin) was a full participant at practice today. The team opened his 21 day practice window to return from injured reserve this week. Taylor-Brott will bring needed depth at corner soon.

Tight end Will Mallory (hamstring) and Drew Ogletree (knee) both missed practice today. Both look like they are not going to be available this week and the team may need to lean on Warren and Aloe-Cos again this week.

Running Back Seth McGowan (hamstring) missed practice today. McGowan looks set to miss multiple weeks while he recovers from a hamstring injury.
