Eagles News: “One of the darkest Thanksgiving Day games ever for the Dallas Cowboys”

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Welcome to The Linc, the daily Philadelphia Eagles news and links roundup at Bleeding Green Nation!

Man. What a great game. What an awesome Thanksgiving. This 2026 season has largely stunk. But that win is a moment in time we'll always have forever. And one that'll haunt the Cowboys and their fans. Thank you, Joe Milton, DeVonta Smith, Ty Robinson, Jake Elliott, and … the whole team. GBDH.

Let's get to the Philadelphia Eagles news and links …

Cowboys vs. Eagles recap: Dallas embarrassed by Philadelphia on Thanksgiving Day – Blogging The Boys

It will go down in history as one of the darkest Thanksgiving Day games ever for the Dallas Cowboys. Somehow, some way, the Cowboys threw away a 31-10 lead that they had in the third quarter. The Philadelphia Eagles won on what was basically a game-winning, 60-yard field goal. It is hard to fathom. On the other hand it is actually very easy to fathom given everything we have seen from the Cowboys this season. They are not a good football team. They are poorly coached. They are talent-deficient. They are all of the bad things. On a day where we are supposed to be thankful it is hard to find a reason to be for this specific part of our lives. The Dallas Cowboys are a weekly embarrassment and Thursday was the latest example of it. What even happens now?

10 Eagles things I think I think after incredible comeback vs. Cowboys – BGN

Incredible. There's no other way to describe the Philadelphia Eagles' comeback victory over the Dallas Cowboys — which was way more enjoyable than any meal you could consume on Thanksgiving. This is an Eagles game that's going to be remembered forever, just like the Christmas Day win over the Cowboys in 2006 and the Miracle at the Meadowlands comeback in 2010. The victory is that improbable, especially when the Eagles were down 31-10 with 3:35 left in the third quarter. They had no business being in this game, yet they found their way back to make it a game — and stole a victory away from the Cowboys on Thanksgiving. This was a game the Cowboys should have won handily. Instead they choked it away, thanks to the hands of a resilient Eagles team that didn't let their 3-7 record stand in the way of what seemed to be an insurmountable deficit. No matter how the rest of this season goes, that was one heck of a win. This victory will be talked about amongst generations of Eagles fans. A Thanksgiving miracle — that may have ended the Cowboys season. The Eagles are good at ruining seasons all of a sudden.

NFL Thanksgiving Winners & Losers – SB Nation

Winner: Joe Milton. I don't know what the long-term future is for the Eagles at quarterback, but Milton's performance on Thanksgiving is going to lead to some uncomfortable conversations. I don't think he's a perfect quarterback by any stretch of the imagination, but he's certainly close enough to Jalen Hurts that it's increasingly difficult to justify paying a QB $51M AAV, when there's someone playing equally well you could pay much, much less. Milton played solid football and didn't let the moment get too big for him. This is also a guy who is on a one year, $1 million contract — and now there are going to be teams putting him on their free agent list. There's a lot of football to go, but that was a statement game.

Eagles-Cowboys: MR. ROCKET Joe Milton With Eagles Thanksgiving MIRACLE Win, Brandon Graham Reacts – PHLY Sports

Joe Milton delivered one of the more memorable games by an Eagles QB in recent memory with two long bombs to DeVonta Smith, a rushing touchdown and a last-second drive following the biggest defensive stand of the game which set up an unreal 60-yard Jake Elliott game-winner. The shocking win gave the Eagles a shocking win to bring them to 4-7 and keep their slim playoff hopes alive. Brandon Graham joins Brandon Lee Gowton, Bo Wulf, Miles Johnson, Fran Duffy and EJ Smith to break it all down.

'It was just chaos:' Inside the Eagles locker room after improbable Thanksgiving comeback vs. Cowboys – PHLY Eagles

"There's no better day, Thanksgiving game against the Cowboys, against a division rival, against a great quarterback like Dak," Robinson said of notching his first career sack. "The adversity we overcame with the guys on the defensive side of the ball, there's no better feeling — I haven't had kids yet, so I don't know what that's like, but as of right know, that's probably No. 1 for me." Perhaps the most improbable contributor was Milton. The 26-year-old who started the season on the Cowboys practice squad going against Dallas defense as a scout-teamer supercharged the Eagles offense, one unbelievably rifled throw at a time. Milton went 21-for-31 for 311 yards and three passing touchdowns and achieved the rare feat of doing something on a football field that even a collection of professional football players couldn't believe was possible. He threw a ball 64.4 yards through the air, crossing from one sideline to the other and into the sure hands of DeVonta Smith for a 50-yard touchdown. "I don't know how far that first ball went to Smitty," Ertz said. "But it had to be one of the farthest throws I've ever seen. … It's insane. Rolling away, Smitty's going right, he's going left. Just a really impressive throw."

Thanksgiving NFL football had it all: Comebacks, walk-offs and plenty of points – ESPN

Of Milton's 311 passing yards, 276 (88.7%) came through air yards; only 35 came after the catch. Since 1992 — as far back as ESPN Research can take us — this is the only game of at least 300 passing yards with 40 or fewer yards after the catch. That should not be a surprise, because this is not how football has ever worked on the material plane. Milton threw so far downfield and so fearlessly into the sideline that receivers were either basically out of bounds or already in the end zone upon the ball's arrival. The middle of the field, where the entire modern game is played, is for mortal men. What is a checkdown to someone with this kind of arm? On the other side, these 2026 Cowboys are a particularly compelling team to watch lose given the spectacular scale of their collapses. The 5-7 Cowboys had a 99.4% win probability at the peak of this game, per ESPN's model. They let a listless Eagles team that had not scored more than 22 points in a game since Week 2 score 22 points in the fourth quarter alone. This was the second home game the Cowboys have lost this season in which they led by at least 14 points in the fourth quarter (the other being the Week 8 loss to Arizona). According to the Elias Sport Bureau, they are the only team to achieve that feat in a season in NFL history.

Handing out 10 awards from the Eagles-Cowboys Thanksgiving game – PhillyVoice

1) The 'Warrior' Award 🪖: DeVonta Smith. DeVonta Smith was a star in this game, catching 11 passes on 14 targets for 200 yards and 2 TDs. Postgame, Smith could barely walk, and even when he was able to make it to his locker stall, he could barely move. A team employee had to help him take his socks off. Given what he looked like after the game, it's remarkable that Smith was able to finish it. He was a warrior.

The Miracle At The Metroplex – Iggles Blitz

While it was to see the Eagles offense score 32 points, we do need to remember that they did it against the worst defense in the NFC. I'd love to tell you Milton will play like that again, but that could have been lightning in a bottle. We need to see him play several more games to have a real feel for who he is. Milton showed today that his special physical traits do translate to the field. He's not just made for the Scouting Combine. The Eagles may have found a young QB worth developing.

Thanksgiving tripleheader: What We Learned from Bears-Lions, Eagles-Cowboys, Chiefs-Bills – NFL.com

Bazooka Joe Milton III leads stunning Eagles comeback vs. Cowboys. The Eagles looked dead in the water, missing starters all over the field, playing with a backup QB, and were down 31-10 deep in the third quarter. Then the largest comeback in Thanksgiving history began with Joe Milton's big arm. The QB splashed fearless bombs over and over again to puncture a limp Cowboys defense. With Saquon Barkley out early with an injury, Milton diced up the Dallas defense, completing 21 of 31 passes for 331 yards, three TDs and an INT. Several of his heaves to DeVonta Smith (11 catches, 200 receiving yards, two TDs) were perfectly dropped in the bucket. Milton might not yet have consistent touch underneath or on intermediate throws, vacillating from laser to a ball four feet off the mark (see: fourth quarter INT). However, he throws a beautiful deep ball, particularly on the move. Case in point: 188 yards and all three TDs came on passes of 20-plus air yards (56.8%). Milton's rushing ability also provides pizzazz to an offense that couldn't get off the ground until Week 12. The Eagles switched to Bazooka Joe to inject explosives into a bland turkey of an offense. Boy, did that come to fruition on Thursday. He brings a cannon arm and athletic ability to overcome blocking deficiencies. He still has strides to make, and he will have to do it against a defense better than Dallas' to prove he can be a legit starter in 2027. But he jolted a sleepy game and turned it on its head. Milton makes the four-win Eagles immensely more watchable the rest of the way.

How Eagles QB Joe Milton rocketed into 'rock star' status on Thanksgiving vs. Cowboys – The Athletic

Perhaps the next start is better than this one, and the Eagles improbably turn around the season like they improbably turned this game around. In the locker room, they're not thinking that yet. Milton said he lived in the moment Thursday. The team is doing the same. But one can't help but wonder what this night means for his career. He's a restricted free agent at season's end. The Eagles weren't the only ones who saw it. The rest of the league is watching. It was one of the NFL's great quarterbacks on the broadcast. "The guy's a rock star, man," Mailata said. "Hats off to him, man. MVP." Milton was minutes past speaking with Brady — who said he was speechless — when Mailata made that comment. The hype was for Milton. He was the star on Thanksgiving.

Jake Elliott proves again he's one of the great long-distance kickers in history – NBCSP

However you measure it, Elliott reaffirmed his status Thursday as one of the greatest long-distance kickers in NFL history and one of the greatest clutch kickers ever. "I mean, yeah, I'm human," Elliott said after the game. "Definitely nervous, but you you just go back to your process. I mean, everything, every (kick) is the same. I treat it the same and just kind of have my process: What balls I'm hitting to the nets, at what times, and when I feel comfortable about about the timing of everything, everything usually goes well." It has lately. Elliott has now made three field goals of at least 55 yards in three attempts this year and his four career makes from 59 yards and out are tied for 4th-most in NFL history. This is a guy who was statistically the worst kicker in the NFL over the 2024 and 2025 seasons. Elliott is now 4-for-5 in his career from 59 yards, tied with Little for most accurate ever from that distance. Elliott thought he had a game-winning 48-yarder with eight seconds left against the Jaguars in London, only to watch Little turn the tables and make a 57-yarder as time expired. He finally got to experience a game-winner for the first time since October 2023, when he made a 54-yarder in overtime to beat the Commanders 34-31 at th Linc. "It's awesome," Elliott said. "We haven't had one for a little bit. We almost had one in London, but it's always good to be in those situations." It speaks volumes that for all the big throws Joe Milton made Thursday night he said his favorites were the two short passes that got the Eagles into position for a long-distane game winner.

Ty Robinson: From 'life support' to Eagles hero on Thanksgiving – PhiladelphiaEagles.com

In a game full of heroes for the Eagles, the comeback against the Cowboys would not have been possible without Ty Robinson. Not only did he sack Dak Prescott on fourth down to set up Jake Elliott's game-winning field goal, the second-year defensive tackle's punt block at the start of the fourth quarter couldn't have come at a better time. Down 31-10, quarterback Joe Milton was able to connect with wide receiver DeVonta Smith deep downfield for a 45-yard touchdown to make it a two-possession game. The Eagles' defense then made a quick stop in Cowboy's territory, forcing Cowboys to punt. Robinson and Chance Campbell lined up to the right side of the Cowboys' punt formation, with Michael Carter II and Kenny Moore II set to rush off opposite ends. At the snap, Campbell looped around Robinson's right on a stunt, freeing Robinson to shed a block while going left. "That was one of the rushes we had planned going into the short week," Robinson said of the punt rush. "Me and Chance (Campbell) were just pumping each other up out there, talking about, 'Let's go get one.' When I went to go pick the (blocker), he wrapped and the guard came off. I just felt a lot of open space. I just stuck my hand out and tried to get out of the way, so I didn't hit the punter. I didn't even see it hit my hand. I just felt it."

Like it or not, Nick Sirianni will sink or swim — like in thriller over Cowboys — doing it his way – Inquirer

The coach certainly is not without his flaws, as anyone who watched his postgame antics can attest — including judge, jury, and executioner Jeffrey Lurie. But the Eagles owner may look past a seven-game losing streak, an often-inefficient offense, and overall sloppy football and see this season's regression as a byproduct of misfortune more than incompetence. Certainly there is a case to be made that losing your starting quarterback, Hall of Fame-bound right tackle, and multiple skill-position players would be insurmountable for most. And yet, Sirianni's Eagles have kept one arm in front of the other through it all. "It's about fighting through adversity, a lot of adversity, throughout this season," Sirianni said of his Chadwick messaging. "The whole message is sometimes you can find yourself in situations that you weren't anticipating, and in those situations, can you keep swimming? "You never know how close you are to getting out of the situation. But you got to keep fighting through it all." With upcoming games against the lowly Cardinals and Colts, it isn't outlandish to believe that the 4-7 Eagles could get back to .500. But catching the 6-3-1 Giants probably is out of their grasp. A few more wins may only deprive them of higher draft position. Sirianni has built anything but a culture of losing. He's won as often as any coach over the last six years. While most of the credit he receives from outside the Eagles focuses on what he does off the field, he's not the bumbling caricature some have created when it comes to scheme.

Nick Sirianni is pulled away from fans after comeback win – PFT

Good or bad, right or wrong, Eagles coach Nick Sirianni has had from time to time the demeanor of a fan who won a contest to be the head coach for a day. On Thursday in Dallas, he displayed those qualities after a stunning come-from-way-behind win over the Cowboys. While celebrating the 39-38 win (they trailed 31-10, late in the third quarter), Sirianni approached fans in field-level suites. He eventually had to be pulled away by Dom DiSandro.

NFC playoff picture: NFC East looks like two-horse race with Giants and Commanders – Big Blue View

The Dallas Cowboys' stunning meltdown on Thursday night against the Philadelphia Eagles, blowing at 31-10 late-third quarter lead in a 39-38 defeat, changed the NFC East title race. The Washington Commanders are now the only team that appears to have a realistic chance of catching the first place New York Giants. Commanders vs. Cardinals Thursday injury report: Treylon Burks limited – Hogs Haven

Treylon Burks has been bothered with a foot injury for several weeks. The veteran Commanders receiver initially suffered the injury weeks ago and Washington has been trying their best to manage it while keeping him in the lineup. Commanders sign another former 49ers' draft pick to practice squad – CommandersWire

A key reserve for San Francisco's secondary, Ambry Thomas fractured his forearm during the 2024 preseason and was placed on injured reserve. The 49ers released him in December. The Indianapolis Colts quickly claimed him off waivers, but he failed his physical and was released the next day. Then, the Minnesota Vikings signed Thomas to their practice squad. He spent the offseason with the Vikings, but was released as part of their final roster cuts in 2025. He then signed with the Philadelphia Eagles and spent the entire season on their practice squad and was released in August.
