NFL writer calls 49ers 'bush-league' for waiting to cut Brandon Aiyuk until deadline

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

NFL reporter Mike Florio thinks the San Francisco 49ers are tempting fate with the way they are treating Brandon Aiyuk.

Once considered to be the team's receiver of the future, Aiyuk remains a Niner in name only. The league reinstated him earlier this month to mount a comeback to football after not playing a game for two years. But what preceded was a messy divorce where the Niners voided his guaranteed money for 2026 after Aiyuk missed offseason meetings and workouts last year. San Francisco has made it clear they don't want the receiver, and their actions have followed suit as they've kept him on a two-week roster exemption where they not only don't have to pay him, but also can delay his inevitable release. The exemption will expire on Monday, Nov. 23, one day after the 49ers face the Vikings in Mexico City.

"If the 49ers are simply squatting on Aiyuk out of spite, that's a bush-league move that creates bad karma," he wrote in a Pro Football Talk article published Wednesday. "Let the guy get back in the league. They apparently still have the ability to pursue upwards of $13 million in bonus money from him; use that process as the vehicle for getting a pound of flesh."

Of course, there is a bit more to it than spite at this stage. With Aiyuk seriously ramping up his efforts to make it back to the league, there's always a chance that he returns to form. The risk of him going somewhere else and possibly balling out is a fair reason for the Niners to stall his comeback, which Florio handwaves away as a "relatively minor strategic advantage."

That doesn't mean spite isn't a part of it. The knee injury he picked up in 2024 happened during an otherwise disappointing season that followed an extended holdout for a new contract. Drew Rosenhaus, Aiyuk's new agent, has certainly helped the receiver clean up his image, but it wasn't that long ago that the receiver's response to all of this was a combination of concerning behavior and obnoxious social media antics. It's hard to imagine the Niners are feeling bad that they're making this player's life a tad bit more difficult.

Regardless of everyone's feelings about this situation, it will all come to an end in one week, when the exemption ends and the Aiyuk era in San Francisco will mercifully come to an end. Whether the karmic retribution follows will have to wait until then.
