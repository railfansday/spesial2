NFL Week 11 Discussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

We're still roughly eight hours away from kickoff in Mexico City, but there's still a ton of other action around the league here in Week 11, and that can only mean one thing around here.

That's right, folks. . .we've got us a full-on RED ZONE SUNDAY here at The Daily Norseman! We can all just sit back, relax, and watch seven hours of commercial-free football before the main event of the day gets started.

If you are not fortunate enough to have the Red Zone Channel as a part of your cable, satellite, or streaming package, here are the maps from the folks at 506 Sports showing which games are being shown where this week. CBS has the doubleheader in Week 11.

Red: Arizona Cardinals at Kansas City Chiefs – Ian Eagle, Ross Tucker

Blue: Pittsburgh Steelers at Philadelphia Eagles – Kevin Harlan, Trent Green

Green: Miami Dolphins at Buffalo Bills – Spero Dedes, Adam Archuleta

Red: Jacksonville Jaguars at New York Giants – Jim Nantz, J.J. Watt

Blue: Las Vegas Raiders at Denver Broncos – Andrew Catalon, Logan Ryan

Red: Tennessee Titans at Dallas Cowboys – Joe Davis, Greg Olsen

Blue: New Orleans Saints at Chicago Bears – Kevin Burkhardt, Tom Brady

Orange: Baltimore Ravens at Carolina Panthers – Kenny Albert, Jonathan Vilma

Yellow: Tampa Bay Buccaneers at Detroit Lions – Kevin Kugler, Daryl Johnston

Green: New York Jets at Los Angeles Chargers (LATE) – Adam Amin, Drew Brees

Monday Night Football this week will feature the Cincinnati Bengals visiting the Washington Commanders. Six teams are on their bye this week: the Atlanta Falcons, Cleveland Browns, Green Bay Packers, Los Angeles Rams, New England Patriots, and Seattle Seahawks.

Enjoy the action today, ladies and gentlemen! We'll have our Open Thread for tonight's Sunday Night Football matchup dropping right around half an hour before kickoff at 6:45 PM Central Time, and we hope to see a bunch of you there!
