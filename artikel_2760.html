Thursday Night Football: Texans beat up on Colts, 26-3

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Texans did what they needed to do Thursday night to remain sort of, kind of, maybe in the playoff race.

Houston beat up on Indianapolis 26-3 to move to 4-6, while the Colts fell to 3-8.

The Texans outgained the Colts 355 to 167 as C.J. Stroud threw for 194 yards and three touchdowns, while completing 21 of 27 passes.

Stroud's touchdowns covered 42 yards to Nico Collins, 24 yards to Kayshon Boutte and 13 yards to Marlin Klein. It was Klein's first career touchdown as the rookie entered Thursday with only one catch.

Collins caught three passes for 58 yards.

Texans running back David Montgomery, who missed four games with a groin injury, had 26 carries for 107 yards in his return. The Texans played without running back Woody Marks, who has a concussion.

Will Anderson had a sack of Daniel Jones in the end zone for a safety, increasing his league-leading sack total to 11.5. Jadeveon Clowney also had a sack, and Kamari Lassiter had a pick of Daniel Jones.

Jones was 12-of-27 for 144 yards and a pick, and Jonathan Taylor rushed for only 21 yards on 11 carries. Anthony Gould made two catches for 48 yards.

The Colts scored their only points on Spencer Shrader's 34-yard field goal. But he missed from 56 and had a 62-yarder blocked by Logan Hall with 2:41 remaining in the first half.
