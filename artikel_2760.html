Weiss, Wimberley lead 10 Central Texas volleyball teams roll to 3rd round of UIL playoffs

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Ten Central Texas high school volleyball teams claimed area-round playoff wins this week, advancing them to third-round action that wraps up Friday night. Westlake, Round Rock, Lake Travis, Rouse, Georgetown, Liberty Hill, Anderson, Weiss, Glenn and Wimberley all won to reach UIL regional quarterfinals.

Deja Carroll slammed 15 kills and notched 13 digs as Weiss stormed past Rosenberg Lamar 25-9, 25-9, 25-13 in a Class 5A Division II match at La Grange High School. Ava Jackson served four aces and chalked up 34 assists in the Wolfpack's one-sided victory. Carrington Nichols had three aces, Annabelle Goldstein had seven kills and Brooklyn Hong added five kills and a block.

"We're playing good volleyball right now," Weiss coach Karen Huffman said. "I am super proud of the kids and their effort."

Weiss outside hitter Deja Carroll (22) digs the ball as Weiss and Liberty Hill face off at Weiss High School in Pflugerville on Tuesday, Aug. 25, 2026. The Panthers defeated the Wolves in three sets. (Mikala Compton/Austin American-Statesman)

A sweep for Westlake: Westlake swept San Antonio Clark as Kyrksen Cook served four aces and set up 22 assists while Ellie Ogden added 17 assists. The Chaps' offense was balanced and pounding; freshman Caroline Ehrlich led the team with 11 kills while Clayton Kelly and Heidi Brew had seven kills each and Geralynn Byers-Veal doubled up with Annabel McDuffee with six apiece. McDuffee also had four blocks while Sunny McWilliams finished with 15 digs.

Round Rock crushed Schertz Clemens 25-14, 25-9, 25-21 as the Dragons dominated the Buffalo front line and prevented Clemens from running its offense.

Avery Ralston slammed 18 kills to lead Rouse past Katy Seven Lakes 25-21, 19-25, 17-25, 26-24, 15-13. Alyssa Hall added 15 kills and 20 digs while Isla Ornelas had 36 assists, and Poppy Schirpik served three aces. Mila Ornelas had 32 digs and Danielle Shyllon put up five blocks.

Glenn makes history: Glenn beat highly regarded Fort Bend Kempner to win its first-ever area-round playoff 25-18, 19-25, 25-15, 25-14. The Cougars (36-3) didn't have an answer for Glenn's outside hitting tandem of Jennah Lee (19 kills, 11 digs) and Daisy Roberts (19 kills, 15 digs). Milan Benavides added 20 digs while Briley Camp had 29 assists and Brooklyn Upton chipped in 19. Aviana Mack had six kills and increased her single-season school record with 2½ more blocks.

Anderson rolls on: Red-hot Anderson swamped Richmond Randle 25-13, 25-14, 25-13 behind Piper Poage, who smashed 17 kills and hit .536 with seven digs. Chloe Frizzell had 12 kills while hitting .375 with 11 digs and two aces while Julyana Harris chipped in five kills and a pair of digs with two aces. Taylor Watts had 20 digs while Henley Magee finished with 33 assists and an ace and two kills.

Wimberley tops Lago Vista: Wimberley held off a hard-charging Lago Vista team that took the first set and led in the second before the top-ranked Texans aligned their passing game with their attacks for a 19-25, 25-23, 25-11, 25-20 win. Trinity Laney ripped 23 kills and Bianca Mohr had 26 assists. Mohr and Caroline Kieffer had 13 digs each while Laney and Skylar Mears both served three aces and Victoria Jones notched three blocks.
