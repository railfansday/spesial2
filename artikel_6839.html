Emma Chamberlain Is Officially Returning to Her YouTube Roots In 2027—But There’s a Catch

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Remember when Emma Chamberlain was constantly cranking out vlogs, driving around San Francisco and L.A. while listening to music, drinking copious amounts of coffee, and having photo shoots with her friends? Life was simpler back then, and we are so back because she’s officially returning to YouTube in 2027.

The internet star-turned-IRL celeb has been busy building her empire and being the best-dressed person at the Met Gala over the years, but she’s making a comeback to the video platform with an all-new series. According to The Hollywood Reporter, Emma is launching a docuseries called What Are You Waiting For?, in which she digs deep into the long lines taking over New York City.

“After deciding to take a hiatus from my podcast, I spent the next few months trying to figure out what I wanted to create next,” Emma told the outlet. “The answer wasn’t obvious at first, but I knew for sure that I wanted to make something for YouTube. From entertaining me as a child to allowing me to build a career as an adult, it was a no-brainer that whatever I make next had to go on YouTube.”

As for what the series will entail? She says she discovers “much more about humanity than I ever could have expected,” as she interviews people waiting in queues for different pop-up experiences around NYC.

“What Are You Waiting For? on the surface is about people who voluntarily wait in lines for stuff set in New York City, a phenomenon that I personally don’t understand but seek to understand, and along the way I discover much more about humanity than I ever could have expected,” she further explained, months after going viral for accidentally stumbling onto a line for her former “Sister Squad” member, James Charles’ pop-up.

“The experience of making this was unlike anything I have ever done and has profoundly changed me as a person, talking to strangers all day, working with a crew of unbelievably talented people, and making something bigger than I have ever made before, I feel the next chapter of my life coming to fruition, and I am deeply inspired and grateful,” she gushed.

Not counting her Anything Goes podcast, Emma’s last YouTube vlog on her main channel was posted in December 2025. So, it seems like our fave fashionista really heard us when we said, “Come home (…to YouTube), the kids miss you!”
