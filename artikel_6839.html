All the Celebrity Red-Carpet Looks at the 2026 WSJ. Innovator Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Including Dua Lipa, Rosalía, and Lupita Nyong’o

We’ve officially entered the season of the starry gala. From the Academy Museum Gala to the Golden Heart Awards and even the new Airbnb.org fête that was held this past Wednesday, these past few weeks have seen all the A-listers hitting red carpet after red-carpet dressed in their black-tie best.

On Thursday evening, that streak continued when the 2026 Innovator Awards were hosted by WSJ. Magazine at the Museum of Modern Art in New York City, to honor groundbreaking leaders in film, music, fashion, comedy, technology, literature, and more.

This year’s honorees included Rosalía, Marlon James, Matthieu Blazy, Amy Poehler, Martin McDonagh, Demis Hassabis, and the Savannah Bananas baseball team. All were recognized as trailblazing innovators in their respective fields.

For such a celebratory evening, it was no surprise that the attendees went all out with their looks. Many were dressed in designs courtesy of Blazy, who has made waves ever since he took over the helm at Chanel back in December 2024. Those dressed in his creations included Poehler, model Bhavitha Mandava, A$AP Rocky, and Dua Lipa, with the latter two presenting Blazy with his award during the ceremony.

Another striking moment came courtesy of Rosalía, who wore a black-and-white Schiaparelli set consisting of an embellished, skin-baring top paired with a silky maxi skirt. Then, there was also Cara Delevingne in elegant Erdem, Jordyn Woods-Towns in sculptural Christian Siriano, and Emmy Rossum in timeless Hermès.
