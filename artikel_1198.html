Premier League Issue Statement After James Tarkowski's Red Card in Brentford vs Everton

James Tarkowski was given a controversial straight red card in Everton's heavy defeat to Brentford.

Everton travelled to west London on Monday evening looking to pick up all three points and vault into the top five of the Premier League. However, they were second best throughout as they suffered a heavy defeat to the Bees.

Keane Lewis-Potter netted the opener for his side in the 23rd minute, and he notched his second three minutes later.

Everton's chances of pulling off a comeback took a huge blow when their captain, Tarkowski, was given a straight red card with five minutes remaining in the first half.

Brentford were able to make their numerical advantage count as they emerged 4-0 victors, with Vitali Janelt and Fabio Carvalho getting on the scoresheet in the second half.

Tarkowski, considered one of the best centre-backs in the Premier League, was initially given a yellow card after being penalised for a tackle on Yegor Yarmolyuk in the 40th minute. The Everton defender clearly won the ball, but his studs then caught the Ukrainian on the shin during his follow through.

VAR reviewed the incident and urged Craig Pawson to watch it again on the pitch side monitor. Pawson subsequently thought he had made the wrong decision and gave Tarkowski a straight red card. It was the first time the 34-year-old had been sent off in his Premeir League career.

The decision caused major controversy online. Many thought that Tarkowski should not have been sent off, including former Premier League striker Chris Sutton.

"Come on that's not a red… the game's gone," Sutton wrote on X, while Sky Sports commentator Alan Smith also thought the Everton defender was hard done by.

The Premier League felt the need to take to X to explain their decision. A statement posted on the Premier League Match Centre X account read:

"After VAR review, the referee overturned the original decision of yellow card for Tarkowski for the challenge on Yarmoliuk and issued a red card for serious foul play.

"VAR: 'Point of contact is on the shin. He's got speed and intensity. Studs are high'.

"REF: 'After review, Everton No.6 commits a challenge that endangers his opponent’s safety, making contact with the studs onto the shin. Cancelling the yellow card – and a red card'.

The BBC's rules expert, Dale Johnson, doesn't think Tarkowski should have been given a straight red card. He said:

"Really not sure about this as a VAR red card. Yes, Tarkowski absolutely ticks the boxes for speed and intensity and leading high with studs showing.

"But the question marks are that Tarkowski made only small contact and didn't have a straight leg when he did so.

"Maybe his reputation played a part? Remember, he wrongly escaped a VAR red card against Liverpool for a challenge, which was along the same lines. He won the ball and then followed through into Alexis Mac Allister. Thought that was a far stronger challenge."

David Moyes, meanwhile, understands why his captain was sent off. He said after the game:

"My first feeling at the time was I didn't think it was red because I thought James easily got to the ball before. He easily got to the ball before the Brentford player, so I would have said that the Brentford player was a bit late in getting there.

"But obviously after I've seen it again, I've seen Tarki's boots are a bit higher so I can understand why the position went against us."

Everton have dropped to ninth in the Premier League having amassed 16 points from their opening 11 matches. Brentford have moved up to sixth and are a point better off than the Toffees.
