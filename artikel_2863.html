Will Anderson Jr. gives heartfelt CJ Stroud endorsement after Texans crush Colts

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

CJ Stroud and the Houston Texans made light work of the Indianapolis Colts, 26-3, at Reliant Stadium on Thursday.

The Texans fired on all cylinders, limiting the Colts to a season-low 167 yards. It was a morale-boosting victory for Houston, which won for the first time at home and improved to 4-6.

Stroud was clinical, throwing 21-of-27 for 194 yards and three touchdowns. He also had six carries for 27 yards.

In the postgame interview, the 25-year-old quarterback received high praise from Will Anderson Jr.

"We're so similar. We share the same competitiveness. We wanna be great. We wanna lead this team to where it has never been before. I get a chance to see him work every day. I've seen him grow from his rookie year to now," said Anderson.

"When I'm down, he picks me up. When he's down, I try to pick him up. We've grown together to try to change this culture and make it for the better, and I couldn't ask for a better guy to lead us than CJ."

Anderson led the defense with five tackles, including two for loss, one sack, and four quarterback hits.

Stroud did not hide his frustration after Houston's anemic start, urging his teammates to "not get stuck."

"You gotta upgrade, whatever that looks like, from personnel to plays to execution, everything. You have to rise up," said the one-time Pro Bowler.

While they are still a middling unit, the explosive win against the Colts could jumpstart an inspired run.

The Texans will host the Baltimore Ravens in Week 12.
