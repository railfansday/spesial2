Our 3 takeaways after Kansas State football's pathetic showing vs Oklahoma State

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

MANHATTAN — Kansas State didn't even put up a fight as it was trounced by Oklahoma State, as the Wildcats' first season under Collin Klein continues to fall apart.

K-State lost 66-28 to the Cowboys on Saturday, Nov. 7, at Bill Snyder Family Stadium. It was the Wildcats' third consecutive loss. The 66 points allowed were the sixth most in Wildcats history.

The Wildcats (4-5, 1-5 Big 12) had no answers for Oklahoma State's offense when it was rolling. At the same time, the offense was ineffective and turned the ball over three times.

Here are our immediate takeaways from the Wildcats' latest loss.

The Wildcats knew they had a tough task going up against Oklahoma State's offense, led by quarterback Drew Mestemaker, who could be the No. 1 overall pick in the upcoming NFL Draft.

The Cowboys scored touchdowns on their first four drives. They had four touchdown drives that only took three plays, with three of them spanning 64 yards or more. Oklahoma State had never scored more than 45 points in Manhattan, and had 49 at halftime.

Mestemaker was great, finishing with 313 passing yards and four scores, along with one receiving and one rushing touchdown. Cowboys running back Caleb Hawkins rushed for 175 yards and three scores.

Barnett was presented with an opportunity against what's been a bad Oklahoma State defense. In a potential dress rehearsal to show that he can be a starting quarterback at the Power 4 level, he was benched at halftime.

Barnett was ineffective, completing 7 of 17 passes for 106 yards, a touchdown and one interception. His touchdown came on a 73-yard pass to a wide-open Derrick Salley, who didn't have anyone within 15 yards of him in apparent busted coverage. It's clearer than ever that the heir-apparent to Avery Johnson, whether that's next year or in 2028, is not on this roster.

Dillon Duff relieved him in the second half, after it was Barnett who relieved him the week before at Colorado. He looked more comfortable than he did against Colorado, but it was in garbage time and against backups.

K-State is trending toward an off-season where the phrase "roster overhaul" might not do it justice. Yes, Johnson's availability would have made a difference in the previous two games he missed, but the defense isn't close to competitive.

At this point, it would be surprising to see Kansas State win another game. TCU could lie down next week amid a disappointing season, but K-State could do the same. Arizona should be a big favorite in Manhattan on Nov. 21, while the Wildcats will likely be an underdog at Iowa State to close the year.

What a disappointing year Klein's first season looks like it will turn out to be. He'll have to consider many tough decisions after this season.
