Jude Bellingham reacts to stunning goal in Real Madrid’s sensational win over PSV: &#8216;I’ve no idea what I did&#8217;

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Jude Bellingham believes Real Madrid's second-half improvement was the decisive factor in their 5-1 Champions League victory over PSV Eindhoven at the Bernabeu.

The midfielder scored a stunning goal to put Real Madrid ahead and assisted Vinícius Jr. for the fifth, helping his side extend their winning run to ten consecutive matches across all competitions.

Speaking to Real Madrid TV after the match, Bellingham stressed that the result was important not only for the points but also for Real Madrid's ambition to avoid the Champions League play-offs.

Despite the emphatic comeback, the England international insisted the team still has work to do.

Notably, Real Madrid struggled during the first half against PSV's quality and intensity, but produced a much stronger performance after the break.

Bellingham said the improvement came from executing the fundamentals more effectively rather than relying solely on individual talent.

"It's an important victory because it's three more points. We don't want to go into the play-offs, so we need to win matches.

Jude Bellingham scored a sensational goal vs PSV. (Photo by Angel Martinez/Getty Images)

"The first half was tough and tricky. They're a side with a lot of quality, but in the second half our team's standard was much higher."

He expanded on the difference in Real Madrid's approach after the interval, explaining why the team's basic execution mattered so much.

"We played better in the second half because we got the simple things right.

"People know we've got lots of quality players who do marvellous things, but sometimes it's more important to get the simple things right. That's why we won."

The midfielder produced one of the night's standout moments with a superb strike to make it 2-1, completing Real Madrid's turnaround after Federico Valverde had equalised.

Asked about the goal, Bellingham admitted he could not fully explain how he had managed to score it.

"I've no idea what I did. But I got the ball in a good position, took a shot and it went in."

Bellingham admits Real Madrid were not at their best in the first half. (Photo by Angel Martinez/Getty Images)

Bellingham also discussed his side's repeated ability to recover from difficult situations in the Champions League.

He credited several factors, including the team's mentality and Mourinho's half-time intervention, but said winning convincingly should remain the objective.

"Comebacks are down to character, mentality, the manager's half-time team talk… It's a combination of many things, and that's why we're staging so many comebacks.

"But it's better for us to win convincingly, and that's why we still need to improve," he explained.

Lastly, the midfielder also praised the Bernabeu's backing, saying their positive energy can significantly influence the team's performance.

"The fans are always incredible. In the first half it's tough and it's very easy for the fans to be a bit more negative, but when they're positive the team plays a thousand times better.

"We need this in every match and for all the players. I'm convinced we're going to win matches," he concluded.
