Hamilton sends firm message over fears of fresh tensions with Leclerc

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Lewis Hamilton has opened up about his relationship with Charles Leclerc following the discussions held between the two Ferrari drivers in Brazil. The seven-time world champion insists that both now have a clear understanding of how they should approach their battles on track, dismissing concerns that further tensions could emerge between them.

Speaking to the media ahead of the Las Vegas Grand Prix, Hamilton was questioned about the meeting that took place in Sao Paulo, where he and Leclerc discussed their relationship as teammates and how to handle potentially difficult situations when racing each other.

The conversation followed tensions between the Ferrari pair, raising questions about whether their competitive relationship could become increasingly difficult to manage as both drivers continue to pursue their own results.

Leclerc already offered an insight into the discussions, revealing that the tensions were cleared up relatively quickly. According to the Monegasque, the initial disagreements were resolved within approximately five to ten minutes, despite the meeting lasting around an hour.

The remaining time was spent having a broader conversation, suggesting that the two drivers used the opportunity to discuss their working relationship rather than focusing exclusively on the incidents that had caused friction.

Hamilton was asked whether resolving disagreements away from the circuit would necessarily prevent similar situations from occurring again during a race.

The question also referenced his previous experience of intense teammate battles at Mercedes, where he became familiar with the challenges of maintaining a productive relationship while competing directly against the driver on the other side of the garage.

Although drivers can reach an understanding during private discussions, those agreements can be tested when they find themselves fighting for position on track, with little time to make decisions. Hamilton nevertheless expressed confidence that the conversation with Leclerc established a clear foundation for their future battles.

The Briton made it clear that the team's interests remain central to that understanding, even when the two drivers are fighting for the same position: "I think Charles is a great driver. The detail we went into about stuff like that, in terms of when we close the visor, it's very clear. We understand each other. We understand each other's boundaries and the ultimate goal, which is to get the best result for the team."

Despite the possibility of further wheel-to-wheel battles, Hamilton does not believe the previous tensions will create problems in the remaining races.

The seven-time world champion was particularly emphatic when asked whether he had any concerns about racing Leclerc following their discussions in Brazil: "I don't feel like that would be a problem moving forward. So I have no concerns or worries about racing him. I don't think he does for me either."
