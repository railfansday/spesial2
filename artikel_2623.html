‘There’s a Weird Vibe Coming From the Team’ — NFL Insider Wonders If Patriots May Fire Mike Vrabel After Dianna Russini Scandal, ‘Embarrassing Losses’

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Mike Vrabel was a breath of fresh air for the New England Patriots as soon as he was hired as the team's head coach. The Jerod Mayo experiment did not work out, and the franchise needed a new direction.

In Vrabel's first season at the helm, the Patriots posted a 14-win season that culminated in a Super Bowl appearance. However, midway through the 2026 NFL season, all of that positive momentum has evaporated. After a brutal start and an offseason full of drama, one NFL insider wonders if New England will fire Vrabel.

A 1-2 start raised some eyebrows for the Patriots, but it looked like they were getting their mojo back, as they strung together three wins in a row (including a victory over the Buffalo Bills). However, they've stumbled again recently, losing three out of their last four games to sit at 5-5.

New England has given up 30+ points in all three of those recent losses, including consecutive blowout defeats to the Detroit Lions and Green Bay Packers. Now, with Drake Maye sidelined indefinitely by a right ankle injury, the problems keep piling up for the Patriots.

Add in a few controversial comments from Vrabel about the state of the locker room, and there's not much optimism in New England. As a result, Mike Florio of NBC Sports isn't ruling out the possibility that Vrabel's time as head coach could be nearing an end.

"Now that the Patriots have suffered a pair of embarrassing losses, the possibility of Vrabel not being the head coach of the Patriots after the season is creeping up again. … There's a weird vibe coming from the team right now, as illustrated by some of the things Vrabel said both after Sunday's 35-10 loss to the Lions and on Monday," Florio wrote.

The comments in question saw him suggest that winning players only look for solutions rather than complaining. He tried to clarify that he didn't mean it as a shot at his players, but the pointed nature of his comments suggested otherwise.

Then, Vrabel followed that up by claiming he didn't know what a locker room problem was, and the bizarre statement certainly rang some alarm bells, which Florio isn't willing to completely dismiss.

"It's fair to ask whether Vrabel has lost his touch. It's fair to wonder whether his offseason entanglement has eroded some of his authority within the locker room," Florio wrote.

The "offseason entanglement" refers to the controversy surrounding published photos of Vrabel and NFL reporter Dianna Russini. Russini resigned from The Athletic in April, and the outlet later concluded that she violated its standards by not disclosing her relationship with Vrabel (without getting into the nature of their relationship).

In response to the scandal, Vrabel admitted to having tough conversations and missed Day 3 of the 2026 NFL Draft. It became a tabloid circus and major distraction, and Florio wonders what impact the scandal had on the team.

"Has a critical mass of players tuned him out? Are they not listening to him? Are they not responding to him? Ultimately, that's something for ownership to monitor and to diagnose. While the organizational play was to sharply turn the page and move on from the scandal, that only works if the team wins," Florio wrote.

At 5-5, the Patriots are fighting to stay in the playoff race in an extremely competitive AFC. If New England cannot reverse its slide over the remainder of the season, Florio believes Vrabel's future could come under increased scrutiny.
