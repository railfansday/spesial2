Robert Pattinson Almost Wasn’t Cast as James Bond for One Key Reason

It's official: Robert Pattinson is the next James Bond, making him the eighth actor to take on the role after Daniel Craig's 15-year tenure in the franchise. Before the announcement, many names were thrown as potential 007 candidates, including Idris Elba (Thor), Aaron Taylor-Johnson (Kraven the Hunter), and Tom Hardy (Venom). In October, Pattinson was shortlisted for the role, along with Nicholas Hoult (Superman), Jack Lowden (Dunkirk), Josh O'Connor (Disclosure Day), and Callum Turner (Fantastic Beasts: The Secrets of Dumbledore), and had a screen test in London. Now that the casting announcement is out, more details about the decision have been unveiled, especially by Amazon's leader.

The James Bond franchise focuses on the British MI6 agent of the same name, who was created in 1953 by Ian Fleming. Also known by his codename, 007, James Bond travels the world to fight threats such as criminals and terrorists. Over 20 films were made, with the first, Dr. No, released in 1962 and starring Sean Connery. Since then, James Bond has influenced pop and British culture, to the point that Craig's Bond appeared during the Opening Ceremony of the 2012 Olympics, escorting Queen Elizabeth II to the Olympic Stadium.

Traditionally, the role of James Bond went to a lesser-known actor, as it was believed the actor shouldn't outshine 007 and the role should help build one's career. But casting Pattinson broke the rule, and Amazon's CEO knows it. In a recent report, Jeffrey Bezos appeared at Semafor's Silicon Valley & The World event, where he discussed the latest 007 news and said he was "heavily involved" in the casting decision. Bezos admitted he was once against the decision because Pattinson was already famous, but when they narrowed down the candidates, it was obvious he was the "obvious" choice. This is what he said:

“We ran a really tight process and made the final decision on Friday. There was a big group initially, and we down-selected to five finalists. We did real, old-fashioned screen tests of those five finalists. It was interesting. Full costumes, real sets, and other actors with them. We did three different scenes with each of the five finalists, and then we got together on Friday to spend a couple of hours looking at all the tests and reviewing all that. By the time we got to this list of five, they were all amazing. But there was something special about Rob and his performance. It was immediately undebated. Denis [Villeneuve], the director … we all gravitated toward Rob. Initially, we were slightly biased against him just because he’s already so famous, like maybe we should have somebody more unknown, but in the screen tests, it was so obvious he is James Bond.”

As of writing, details of the next James Bond movie have yet to be revealed. No plot has been revealed, and the only names attached are Pattinson and Dune director Denis Villeneuve. One thing known about the next James Bond film is that it will reboot the franchise. In June 2026, Head of Prime Video and Amazon MGM Studios Mike Hopkins said this next James Bond installment would be a "new chapter" for the franchise and would aim to appeal to a new generation of audiences. Additionally, this would be the first James Bond film without Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson, as the duo handed creative control to Amazon MGM Studios.

Robert Pattinson's debut as James Bond is expected to be released in 2028. Follow Collider for more updates.
