Need For Speed Underground 2 Is Playable For Free In Your Browser Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

AI coding is a bit of a weird one because, while the technology is clearly going to be an overall net negative in many respects, it has enabled individuals to produce some fun projects that otherwise wouldn't have been possible. Most obviously, recent months have seen a ton of classic games brought back in a whole new format, which is actually quite a good thing for those titles that are hard to play in 2026.

Now, beloved racer Need for Speed Underground 2 is the next target in their sights, and a new release allows players to enjoy it from the comfort of their chosen browser. The fun doesn't stop there, though, so here's everything you need to know about why the 2004 release is retruning in a big way.

As first reported by Tech PowerUp, a new fan project called Underground 2 Revived lets you play Need for Speed: Underground 2 in a web browser. While it is obviously going to be vibe coded in many instances, the game seems to be running well for players at the time of writing, and its nice to see the classic release return to the fore once again.

Not only that, the developer, who goes by th0mk, has also included an online free roam, essentially turning the racing game into a pseudo-MMO for the first time. The project came about because the creator played the game with their brother about 20 years ago and they wanted to easily recapture that magic and play together again.

At the time of writing, it has been confirmed to be working in Chrome and Firefox using WebAssembly and WebGPU, though it will likely also work in other advanced browsers. There are also a shocking number of players online right now, with the project clearly striking a chord with those who grew up playing it.

It might also be the best way to play old Need For Speed games without digging out all the old hardware and booting up the CRT TV. EA is showing no signs of bringing back their older titles in the franchise with remasters and remakes, so the old visuals will have to do in this case.

At the same time, the sale of EA to an investment consortium based in Saudi Arabia has seemingly done nothing to brighten the company's future on the development front either.

We all heard the news that legendary developer Bioware was set to close, and the upcoming fifth game in the Mass Effect series cancelled. That came about after those in charge supposedly made the choice to pursue Battlefront 3 in earnest, with that presented as a straight choice between the two.

That likely means that EA has little intention of investing significant money elsewhere, particularly on franchises that don't have a track record over the last few years. Unfortunately, that will leave Need For Speed out in the cold for some time to come yet, if we ever get a new game in the series again.
