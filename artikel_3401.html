Where to find the Silent Hill hotel in GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Silent Hill hotel is one of the secrets you can stumble upon in Grand Theft Auto 6. Like the meeting with Dave and Beelzebub, you can miss the chance to enter this specific hotel and experience the creepy event that takes place inside. From the outside, it looks like a regular building, but after you visit this place, Leonida will literally change for a while. Besides, there's a safe waiting for you to open and a unique reward to collect.

If you're interested in checking out this mysterious place, here's where to find the Silent Hill hotel in GTA 6. Below, we also explain the Marbelle Hotel safe code location.

You start the Silent Hill event in GTA 6 by visiting the Marbelle Hotel in Crosstown, Vice City. The area is on the northern side of the city, southwest of Bayside. The Marbelle Hotel is an old building beside Pizza Pete. To enter the hotel, you must use the door on the east side.

In the initial room, you will come across a safe behind a desk, but nothing else of importance. Keep climbing the stairs until you reach the top floor, where the event will start.

Note that if you visit the hotel as a couple, only the character you're controlling will enter the hotel. After you leave it, you will receive a message from your partner asking where you have been. Also, visiting the hotel a second doesn't seem to trigger the event again. If you go too far from the hotel after the event starts, the fog will disperse, and the city will return to normal.

Even if you're not into supernatural experiences, you should visit the Marbelle Hotel. Inside, there's a safe containing $234 and the Crowex Ambassador 'Breakfast' luxury watch. To open it, you need a six-digit code, which you can find in two ways.

The first one is on the staircase where the event starts and red signs appear on the wall. If you look at the wall to your left while climbing the stairs, you will see the drawing of a hand with an eye in its palm. Above it, you will find six numbers.

If you got scared by the weird signs on the walls and were not paying attention to what was written on them, don't worry. You have another chance to find the code by heading to the roof and looking for the notes left on the ground on the northwestern side. Read them and you will find the same six digits.

At both locations, you will find the same code: 955214. Return to the safe, enter the code to open it and collect the rewards.
