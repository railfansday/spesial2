Ghosts Unveils The Most Dangerous Threat To Sam & Jay's Marriage

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Although Ghosts season 6’s Halloween special saw Jay bond with Woodstone Manor’s new arrival, this character could soon go on to unintentionally become his worst enemy. While Utkarsh Ambudkar’s Jay temporarily became a ghost himself during season 5’s festive special “A Very Arondekar Christmas,” he still can’t directly communicate with the spirits of Woodstone Manor. This makes Rose McIver’s Sam a pivotal character in Ghosts.

Her central role has only become more obvious with McIver’s Ghosts season 6 absence, as the sitcom recruited the existing supporting character, Kyle, to stand in for Sam while she was working on a movie in Australia off-screen. Played by Superstore’s Ben Feldman, Kyle was introduced back in season 4, episode 21, “Kyle,” where he revealed he could see, hear, and speak to the ghosts just like Sam.

Since Kyle’s first Ghosts episode ended with him trying to make out with Sam, it makes sense that the character didn’t return for a while and only became a series regular in season 6. However, although Kyle and Jay bonded during season 6’s standalone Halloween special "Halloween 6: The Mirror,” this could ironically prove to be bad news for Sam and Jay's marriage.

It seems fair to say that, up until now, Thor, Sass, Isaac, Hetty, Alberta, Flower, Pete, and Trevor represented the greatest existential threat to Sam and Jay’s marriage. Pete seemed to be gone for good when he vanished from Woodstone Manor, but he reappeared at the end of "Halloween 6: The Mirror,” while the disastrous return of Hetty’s sister proved the ghosts are still constantly causing havoc for Jay in Sam’s absence.

However, there is another threat to Sam and Jay’s Ghosts relationship that isn’t quite as obvious, and the longer he stays at Woodstone Manor, the more Kyle unknowingly embodies this threat. Kyle’s ability to see and hear the ghosts means that, thanks to his translation, Jay can now be in on what Sam and the ghosts are up to.

For the past few seasons of Ghosts, Sam has gotten very comfortable lying to Jay about the ghosts, for better and worse. Often times, this will be for the sake of saving her own time by choosing not to explain their ridiculous demands or complicated schemes, but sometimes, it is to save Jay’s feelings. On occasion, Sam even chooses to lie to Jay about the ghosts as part of her own scheming.

Kyle’s return and his promotion to a series regular role in Ghosts season 6 throws a proverbial spanner in the works for Sam, since he can inform Jay directly about what the ghosts are saying and doing. This could fly in the face of what Sam claimed, and Jay could be understandably horrified to find out she’s been lying for years.

There are numerous reasons that Kyle might do this, the most obvious of them being that he is jealous of Jay and still yearns for a romantic relationship with Sam. However, he could also just be bitter and frustrated by his inability to stop hearing the spirits, something Sam has struggled with, and this could lead him to derail their comparatively stable, happy marriage. Finally, he might genuinely not mean any malice, and could just end up contradicting Sam’s lies by blithely stating the truth.

If Kyle simply reports what the ghosts are actually doing, but Sam has been lying about their activities, he could end up outing Rose McIver’s Ghosts heroine to her husband without even meaning to do so. While this might cause serious problems for the couple, it also means that Ghosts season 6 now has a seriously interesting new character who can create organic, meaningful conflict that feels earned and rooted in the sitcom’s setup.
