How to watch Ohio State basketball vs. Vanderbilt today on streaming

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Ohio State men's basketball team will take a two-game winning streak into its latest high-major test of the nonconference schedule when No. 14 Vanderbilt comes to town.

This is the fifth home game for the Buckeyes and their seventh game of the season. Ohio State has wins in the Schottenstein Center against Youngstown State, Marshall, Prairie View A&M and UMBC and road losses to No. 25 BYU and No. 4 UConn.

The Commodores are 6-0 with all six games having been played inside the friendly confines of Memorial Gym. Three of those wins are against top-100 teams at KenPom.com. The most recent was an 82-74 win against Central Florida on Nov. 20.

The Buckeyes were picked to finish eighth in the preseason Big Ten media poll conducted by The Dispatch.

Here's how you can watch the latest Ohio State home game of the 2026-27 season:

You can watch the Buckeyes play the Commodores by subscribing to to NBC's streaming service Peacock. This is the first of three Ohio State games on Peacock this season.
