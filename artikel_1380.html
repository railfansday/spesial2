Paul Scholes Names 5 Transfers He'd Make to Save Man Utd

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

It has been a dreadful start to the 2026/27 season for Manchester United. Having played nine Premier League games, Michael Carrick has overseen just one win, with his team sitting 17th in the table.

Having helped the Red Devils finish third last term, there were high hopes that Carrick could finally bring some stability to the club. However, things have quickly fallen apart since the Englishman was made permanent manager in the summer, and a preferred candidate to replace Michael Carrick has already been named.

This is a familiar story in the post-Sir Alex Ferguson era, with club icon Ole Gunnar Solskjaer also struggling to get the best out of the side after a promising interim spell. A number of high-profile managers, like Jose Mourinho and Louis van Gaal, have also failed to impress at Old Trafford on a consistent basis.

With so many issues apparent, it's hard to know how the club will turn things around. With that question in mind, though, Paul Scholes has listed five dream transfers he would make to 'save' Man Utd from their woes.

While Gabriel might often rub rival fans the wrong way, he's the sort of player you'd love to have on your team. When it comes to defenders, there are few better in the game right now than the ferocious Arsenal star.

Too often, Man Utd looked weak at the back. Gabriel would quickly sort that out. Add to that his prowess when attacking set pieces, and he'd instantly boost the Red Devils' chances of winning tight games.

Scholes really is fantasising when it comes to picking Jude Bellingham. Every team in the world would love to have the Englishman, and it's extremely unlikely that he ends up at Old Trafford any time soon.

He's now scored 50 goals at Real Madrid, which is one more than the legendary Zinedine Zidane managed for Los Blancos. Having started out at Birmingham City and then moved to Borussia Dortmund, Bellingham is yet to play in the Premier League. Man Utd will have to improve vastly in the coming seasons if they want to have any chance of one day landing the midfielder.

Michael Olise's rise from Crystal Palace talent to Bayern Munich Ballon d'Or contender has been spectacular, to say the least. He's bagged goal after goal, and assists after assist, in Germany, proving to be one of the best wingers on the planet.

Olise would be an instant upgrade on any of the current wide men in Carrick's squad. If he does leave Bayern in the coming seasons, it's unlikely he would join a club struggling in the bottom half of the Premier League, though.

While Olise was attending the 2026 Ballon d’Or ceremony, his old teammate was in court.

Having finished second in the 2026 Ballon d'Or rankings, it's accurate to say there is only one player in the world better than Kylian Mbappe right now. The Frenchman continues to deliver electric performances for both club and country, having taken his career tally to a record 22 World Cup goals this summer.

Despite that, Mbappe is yet to win either La Liga or the Champions League with Real Madrid. It just goes to show that even the biggest individual talent doesn't guarantee team success. That said, he'd certainly improve this Man Utd squad.

Although he initially picked Ballon d'Or winner Harry Kane as his fifth signing, Scholes then tweaked his answer to choose Declan Rice instead. It just goes to show how good the Arsenal midfielder has been that he gets the nod above England's record goalscorer.

Rice certainly would bring some much-needed running power, on-ball quality and leadership to the middle of the park for the Red Devils. Having helped the Gunners win the Premier League last term, there's little the Englishman can't do, and he'd be a welcome addition to any team in the world.
