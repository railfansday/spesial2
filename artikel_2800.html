Tyler Booker set to miss multiple weeks

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Dallas Cowboys OG Tyler Booker (ankle) suffered a high ankle sprain during the Week 10 game, according to NFL Network's Ian Rapoport. The team hopes he will be able to return to the lineup within the next four weeks.
