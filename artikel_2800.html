Bengals rule out one player for Sunday's game in Madrid

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Cincinnati Bengals defensive back DJ Turner was again a full participant Friday in the team's first practice in Madrid, Spain, ahead of their NFL Week 9 game against the Atlanta Falcons.

Defensive back Jordan Battle (hamstring) also was a full participant, after he was limited Thursday.

The Bengals did rule out safety Kyle Dugger with a quad injury. Defensive end Cedric Johnson (ankle/foot) was a full participant Friday, but is listed as questionable for the game.

Dugger also was a non-participant Thursday.

Turner was injured during the fourth quarter of the team's NFL Week 8 overtime win against Tennessee at Paycor Stadium.

The Bengals also will be without Cashius Howell, a rookie defensive end who was placed on injured reserve earlier in the week.

Receiver Zachariah Branch (hamstring) was a non-participant again for the Falcons, and was ruled out. Running back Brian Robinson Jr., limited in practice, also was ruled out.

Falcons linebackers Devine Deablo (forearm) and Yasir Abdullah (hamstring) were limited, and are listed as questionable.

Also listed as questionable for the Falcons are wide receiver Beaux Collins, defensive end James Pearce Jr. and offensive tackle Cameron Williams.
