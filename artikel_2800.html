Raiders QB Fernando Mendoza thankful for Aaron Rodgers on Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Somewhere in the middle of airing his grievances with the Pittsburgh Steelers on Wednesday, Aaron Rodgers took a moment to say something nice about a fellow quarterback.

Imagine Fernando Mendoza's surprise to find out it was him.

Rodgers was on "The Pat McAfee Show" explaining how things had gone sideways with Steelers coach Mike McCarthy when he got around to the Denver film. Pittsburgh plays the Broncos on Friday. The Raiders had just been through Denver on Sunday, which is how a rookie making his second NFL start earned a cameo in somebody else's very public breakup.

Las Vegas Raiders quarterback Fernando Mendoza (15) throws against the Denver Broncos during an NFL football game in Denver, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. AP Photo/Jack Dempsey

"And I'll tell you: Hey, the kid Mendoza, man. He was running around, making some plays in the scramble."

Funny thing about that tape. If Rodgers goes back to the third quarter and watches what Mendoza did before throwing a 19-yard touchdown pass to tight end Ian Thomas, he might recognize the move the young quarterback busted out.

After all, Mendoza borrowed it from Rodgers.

The Raiders call it the guitar fake. Watch Mendoza's hands as running back Ashton Jeanty approached Mendoza as if he was going to be handed the ball. The empty hand carries out the handoff while the other keeps the football tucked against his body.

Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers gestures at a news conference after an NFL football game in Philadelphia, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. AP Photo/Chris Szagola

For a beat, the quarterback looks like a guy playing air guitar. By the time Mendoza brings the ball back into view, Thomas is wide open. The extra point tied a game the Raiders once trailed 17-0.

It makes sense that any young quarterback would pick out elements of a future Hall of Famer like Rodgers and make them their own. Even more so in the case of Mendoza, who played his first two seasons at Cal, Rodgers' alma mater.

"Yeah, Aaron Rodgers, he also went to Cal, so it's a little funny, but it was something that we've worked on a lot," Mendoza said.

Mendoza spent his last college season at Indiana working primarily from the shotgun, several yards behind the center, with the defense in front of him and the back alongside him.

Get under center in Klint Kubiak's offense, and the view changes in a hurry. Mendoza has to take the snap, turn his back on the defense, meet a runner coming downhill, then get his head around and find his receiver. His feet have to sell the handoff, too.

"So, if you don't have a lot of experience detailing under center handoffs, it's sometimes hard to then develop the fakes," Mendoza said.

In April, by Mendoza's own assessment, his guitar fake "looked horrendous."

Las Vegas Raiders quarterback Fernando Mendoza (15) receives instructions during the fourth quarter against the Denver Broncos in Denver, Colorado, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. Kate Benic/UPI/Shutterstock

"So then actually being a productive part of the offense this past Sunday was a cool moment to see the development," Mendoza said.

Imagine showing him those two clips back then. In one, Denver bites hard enough on his handoff for Thomas to get loose. In the other, the guy he borrowed it from interrupts a beef with his head coach to talk a little ball about the kid from Cal.
