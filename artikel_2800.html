Titans designate Fernando Carmona to return from IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Tennessee Titans have opened the practice window for rookie guard Fernando Carmona to return from injured reserve. Carmona was injured in the Titans' 12-7 loss to the New York Giants in Week 3. He started the first three games of the season at right guard. The Titans will now have 21 days to activate Carmona.

Carmona, a 5th-round pick out of Arkansas in the 2026 NFL Draft, was one of the biggest stories of training camp. He came in and won the starting job with a great camp.

Jackson Slater, Carmona's backup, was also injured in that game. The offensive line has done a remarkable job with both of those guys out of the lineup. A lot of credit goes to offensive line coach Carmen Bricillo. He has done a great job getting the next guy up ready to play each week.

It cannot be overstated how important having a good offensive line coach is. It will allow the Titans to not have to invest high picks in interior offensive linemen.
