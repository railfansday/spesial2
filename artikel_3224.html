Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Expro Ltd (XPRO -15.94%) reported sequential increases in revenue and adjusted EBITDA driven by a step-change in offshore activity and the integration of its Enhanced Drilling acquisition. Management raised full-year adjusted free cash flow guidance despite tempering fourth-quarter expectations by $20 million due to subsea activity delays in the U.S. Gulf and well intervention schedule changes in Colombia. The company implemented a strategic realignment by merging its Asia-Pacific and Middle East regions into a single AMENA segment to leverage economies of scale and better align with national oil company behaviors. Looking toward 2027, the company expects continued growth in profitability and cash flow, assuming a stable environment in the Middle East and the realization of deferred project revenue.
