Snubbed Bill Belichick a Pro Football Hall of Fame finalist again

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Bill Belichick and Robert Kraft, who won six Super Bowls together with the New England Patriots, have advanced a step closer to reuniting in Canton as members of the Pro Football Hall of Fame's Class of 2027.

Both were denied earlier this year for the Hall's most recent class, and in the case of Belichick – a first-ballot finalist in 2026 and second to Don Shula for most career coaching victories (333) – the omission ignited a furor that prompted the Hall of Fame to overhaul its selection process.

Now they are back in the mix for a final round, with Belichick advancing over four others – Tom Coughlin, Mike Holmgren, Dan Reeves and Mike Shanahan (in alphabetical order) – in voting by the coaches subcommittee.

Kraft – one of the NFL's most powerful and accomplished team owners, and a key figure in striking massive media rights deals for the league and in negotiating the collective bargaining agreement with players that ended a lockout in 2011 – was forwarded again as the candidate in the contributor category. He was chosen over four others – Ralph Hay, Virginia McCaskey, Doug Williams and John Wooten (in alphabetical order) – by a separate contributors subcommittee.

The Hall's full selection committee will consider Belichick and Kraft as part of a slate that will include 13 former players, during an in-person meeting in the weeks before Super Bowl 61. The 15 candidates will be reduced to 10, then 7. At that point, any candidate receiving 80% approval would be elected to the Class of 2027.

The new class will be unveiled during the NFL Honors show in Los Angeles in February.

At this point, balloting to reduce the pool of players under consideration is ongoing, with the full selection committee – reduced this year from 50 to 25 (plus 3 alternates) – engaging in multiple reduction votes in the coming weeks.

The Hall's bylaws stipulate that between three and seven new Hall of Famers will be elected.

Time (and voting) will tell whether that class includes Belichick and Kraft.
