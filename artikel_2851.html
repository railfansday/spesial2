Aaron Rodgers grades Mike Tomlin's performance as analyst on NBC

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers has made it explicitly clear that he wants nothing to do with the media or public life once he retires at the conclusion of this NFL season. But so long as microphones are still shoved in his face, Rodgers has no issue with expressing his opinions.

Rodgers and the Steelers are preparing to play the Cincinnati Bengals in a high-stakes AFC North battle on Sunday Night Football this week. Former Steelers head coach Mike Tomlin, who stepped down in January before joining NBC's Football Night In America this season, will be on site.

On Wednesday, ESPN's Brooke Pryor asked Rodgers about the Tomlin of it all.

"I still talk to him," Rodgers said. "I love Mike T, and I have so much appreciation for him bringing me here last year and the amount of fun that we had together. The closeness of the bond that we formed. So it'll be good to see him."

"I'm not surprised in the least," Rodgers said of Tomlin's post-NFL media stardom. "He is absolutely killing it on TV the few times I've seen him. Nobody can talk football better than Mike T, so it is an absolute match made in heaven. Him being on TV and doing what he does. And I don't know if he still wants to coach at some point, but he's doing a hell of a job."

Tomlin has been asked repeatedly about his desire to return to coaching in the NFL someday. On Wednesday, he was asked again during an NBC conference call.

"I'm having a lot of fun," Tomlin said, per Mark Maske of Front Office Sports. "Haven't given one iota of thought about coaching. I'm sure that some of that's going to come up in the not-too-distant future. But I'll cross that bridge when I come to it. Really haven't thought a lot about it. Really excited about my new role and working to get better each and every week."

Viewers overwhelmingly agree with Rodgers's review of Tomlin's broadcasting prowess. Tomlin's Steelers teams never finished with a losing record in his 19 years in Pittsburgh. Given the early returns on his first season on NBC, Tomlin is trending toward an indefinite winning streak as a media darling.
