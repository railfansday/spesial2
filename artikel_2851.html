3 quick takeaways from 49ers win over Vikings: Brock Purdy’s processing was off the charts

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The San Francisco 49ers beat up on the Minnesota Vikings 35-10 on Sunday Night Football in Mexico City, improving their record to 9-1 and creating a gap in the NFC.

With the win, the 49ers remained in sole possession of first place in the NFC, while the Vikings dropped to sixth place. Now, it's the Detroit Lions and Chicago Bears who are tied for second place with an 8-2 record.

San Francisco dominated from the jump in this one, and they held a three-score lead by halftime. They forced two turnovers, mitigated the run game, and got pressure on the quarterback on defense, while the offense flowed through the passing game.

Here are three quick takeaways from the 49ers win over the Vikings in Week 11.

Brock Purdy has had some phenomenal performances this season. He started off the season strong against the Los Angeles Rams after an early interception in that game.

The next week, he completed 20 of 22 passes for 287 yards and three total touchdowns against the Miami Dolphins in a 22-point win. He had four passing touchdowns against the Arizona Cardinals. And just last week, he threw for 389 yards and three scores in a 46-40 win over the Cowboys.

This one might've been his most impressive performance of them all. Purdy was just pristine with his timing in the passing game on Sunday. He was seeing things extremely well against the best defense in the league and letting it rip, with the ball placement being elite as well.

Credit goes to Kyle Shanahan as well for a phenomenal game plan and well-called game as well. But Purdy was poised, in rhythm, and on a heater on Sunday night.

When kept clean, he got the ball out quickly (2.48 second time-to-throw) and completed 15 of 18 passes for 170 yards and three touchdowns. On the nine dropbacks he was pressured on, though, Purdy still found a way to complete 6 of 8 passes for 85 yards.

Brian Flores looked to dial up the blitz at an extremely high rate, as always. But he did so even more on Sunday night, as Purdy was blitzed on nearly 89 percent of his dropbacks in the win. The result? He threw for 231 yards and two touchdowns while completing 19 of 23 passes against the blitz, completely dicing up Minnesota's defense.

We've seen Purdy make magic this season time and time again by extending plays with his feet. He did have a few of those plays again in this game, but the way he played with timing and saw the field against a challenging, blitz-heavy defense was huge for the 49ers on Sunday.

This was one of the best quarterback performances of the year, given the opponent. And Purdy did all that while taking only one sack, something much different than last week.

Coming into the game, it was going to be hard to get pressure on and sack Kirk Cousins. This season, Cousins has been very adept at getting the ball out quickly and avoiding sacks, taking only 14 total before Sunday night.

But the 49ers pass rush, which had admittedly been going through its ups and downs, delivered as the game went on Sunday night, coming up with 21 total pressures.

Cousins held the ball more than he usually did and the 49ers pressured him on 16 of his 40 dropbacks (40 percent). And while there was only one sack, Cousins averaged just 4.9 yards per attempt when pressured, while completing only 53 percent of his passes.

The Vikings quarterback was actually also quite good against the blitz, getting the ball out quickly and completing 10 of 13 passes for 132 yards. But when the 49ers brought only four, Cousins struggled, completing only 64 percent of his passes and averaging a measly 4.1 yards per attempt while having a passer rating of 55.9.

Nick Bosa had a strong game just days after coming out and saying he needed to be better. He had five pressures with a 23.3 percent pass-rush win rate, according to PFF, and he wasn't alone. Osa Odighizuwa had four pressures, half a sack, and a 21.7 percent pass-rush win rate. Romello Height had four pressures and a 21.7 percent pass rush win rate. CJ West and Keion White both had two pressures and good win rates.

It was just a better day for the defensive line, who won more with four rushing and still stopped the run, with Minnesota totaling just 69 yards and 3.3 yards per carry on the ground.

The 49ers suffered arguably their most brutal injury of the season so far, as right tackle Colton McKivitz is now out for the year with a fractured ankle, with Kyle Shanahan comparing it to Fred Warner's last year.

McKivitz, a fifth-round pick in the 2020 NFL Draft, had developed from a replacement-level play to one of the best tackles in all of football. Per SumerSports, McKivitz had the third-best pass block win rate of any tackle this season and the third-best positive run block rate. That's going to be incredibly difficult to replace.

San Francisco signed Vederian Lowe to be the team's swing tackle for this type of situation. He had a good game in relief of McKivitz, allowing zero pressures on 18 pass-blocking snaps. He's got experience, having started 25 games so far in his career with the New England Patriots.

But he's got tall shoes to fill, and the injury puts a damper on the biggest win San Francisco has had yet.
