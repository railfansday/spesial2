Bengals activate three players off the IR. Here's what it means

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Cincinnati Bengals had three familiar faces return to practice ahead of their primetime showdown with the Pittsburgh Steelers.

The Bengals on Nov. 12 activated three players − receiver Andrei Iosivas, cornerback Jalen Davis and guard Brian Parker II − from the reserve/injured list.

Iosivas injured his thumb, which later required surgery, during a play on special teams during Cincinnati's Week 2 victory against the Houston Texans.

Davis was placed on the IR Sept. 29 with a hamstring injury, and Parker returned after months away due to a knee injury that landed him on the IR Aug. 30.

The players' respective returns to practice commenced a 21-day period where their presence won't count against the team's 53-man roster.

The Bengals on Nov. 15 host the Steelers at Paycor Stadium on Sunday Night Football with a scheduled 8:20 p.m. kickoff.
