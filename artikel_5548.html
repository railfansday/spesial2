Syntax Recognized on the CRN® AI Innovators List For 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

MONTREAL, Nov. 3, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Syntax, a leading global technology solutions provider driving enterprise transformation in the cloud, announced today that it has been named to the 2026 AI Innovators list by CRNÂ®, a brand of The Channel Company.

CRN's inaugural AI Innovators list celebrates North American solution providers at the forefront of the AI revolution. The companies featured on this list have recognized the vast opportunities of AI and have integrated AI technologies into cutting-edge solutions for their customers.

AI has quickly evolved from an emerging technology to a strategic business imperative. Solution providers are increasingly deploying AI solutions to help businesses improve customer experiences, enhance employee productivity, strengthen decision-making, and uncover new opportunities for growth. As a result, the AI market has become a major catalyst for innovation across every sector of the IT channel. Each 2026 AI Innovator has shown dedication to building creative and unique AI solutions that deliver valuable business enhancements to customers.

Syntax puts AI to work across the mission-critical systems that organizations rely on, helping them move from experimentation to production-grade AI. The company has brought that vision to life with a portfolio of AI offerings, services, and industry expertise that help customers build a secure, scalable, and well-governed foundation for AI initiatives, including a new GenAI-powered retail and apparel offering and AI-First Application Managed Services for SAP solutions that deliver proven AI-driven efficiency from a reliable, secure, and fully integrated AI platform.

"We're honored to be recognized by CRN for the progress our teams and customers are making with AI," said Christian Primeau, Global CEO of Syntax. "Our focus is on turning the promise of AI into practical business value, helping organizations work smarter and improve how they operate. This recognition is a testament to the expertise and commitment of our people, as well as the close collaboration we share with our customers to apply AI to their business needs."

"Our 2026 AI Innovators honorees are helping shape the next generation of business transformation through the imaginative use of artificial intelligence," said Jennifer Follett, Vice President, U.S. Content and Executive Editor at CRN, The Channel Company. "By delivering forward-thinking solutions that create value for their customers and technology partners alike, they are pushing the industry forward and setting a high standard for innovation. We are honored to recognize their contributions."

Coverage of the 2026 AI Innovators list is available at crn.com/AIinnovators.
