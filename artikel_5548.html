Branch Ranked Number 146 Fastest-Growing Company in North America on the 2026 Deloitte Technology Fast 500™

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Embedded financial services provider named to the list for the sixth consecutive year

TAMPA, Fla., Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Branch today announced that it ranked number 146 on the Deloitte Technology Fast 500â¢, a ranking of the 500 fastest-growing tech companies in North America, now in its 32nd year.

Branch is the leading provider of embedded financial services for workforces. The company's award-winning infrastructure powers payments for W-2 and 1099 workforces at the nation's leading companies and platforms across hospitality, healthcare, logistics, marketplaces, vertical software, and financial institutions. Businesses use Branch for everything from earned wage access and cashless tips to contractor payouts, commissions and bonuses, and pay card and direct deposit. Branch powers payouts faster than traditional payroll for some of the nation's largest brands including Uber, Instacart, Jimmy John's, Dunkin, and Marco's Pizza franchise locations, and has delivered more than $68 billion in worker payments to date.

Branch's chief executive officer, Atif Siddiqi, credits the company's growth to businesses' increasing commitment to modernizing pay for their workers and Branch's continued expansion of their platform and industries they serve. He said, "We're building toward a future where every working American gets paid on their terms, not on a schedule designed decades ago. When people have more control over when and how they get paid, they can cover everyday expenses, plan ahead, and build real financial stability. Modernizing pay is how businesses make that future possible for their people, and we're proud to help them get there."

Companies are replacing outdated payment methods like paper checks and biweekly pay cadences with modern, flexible options that save time and money and help them attract and retain talent. Workers can choose how they receive their earnings, including the award-winning Branch App, a digital deposit account and debit card with fee-free options and financial wellness tools; Branch Direct, which sends earnings to a worker's existing bank account through a connected debit card; and Branch White Label, a custom-branded digital wallet that delivers a seamless payout experience within a company's own brand.

Branch's newest offering, Flex EWA, lets software platforms, HCM and payroll systems, and financial institutions embed earned wage access directly into their products and launch within weeks instead of building it from scratch.

Overall, the 2026 Technology Fast 500 companies achieved revenue growth ranging from 97% to 66,330% over the three-year time frame, with an average growth rate of 1,134%.
