‘I would be afraid not to take Arch Manning’: Skip Bayless issues a warning to NFL teams about passing up Arch Manning

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Texas Longhorns quarterback Arch Manning has had an up-and-down season, and many NFL scouts have soured on him as a 2027 draft prospect. But Manning still has at least one believer who would take him first overall.

Appearing on ESPN's First Take on Friday, Skip Bayless said he would be "afraid not to take" Manning if he had the No. 1 pick. Bayless admitted there are plenty of red flags in Manning's game, but said he couldn't pass on a quarterback with Manning's talent and family history.

Heading into the season, Manning was expected to be one of the top quarterbacks in the 2027 draft class. But his struggles with accuracy and turnovers have changed that, and in ESPN's midseason poll of NFL scouts and executives, Manning didn't receive a single vote to be the first quarterback drafted. Oklahoma State's Drew Mestemaker was the overwhelming favorite.

Manning has thrown for 2,198 yards with 19 touchdowns and 10 interceptions this season. Texas has gone 8-2, with back-to-back losses to Oklahoma and Florida in October.

Still, Manning has come through in some big moments. Last week, he threw three interceptions against LSU but hit Raleek Brown for the game-winning touchdown with three seconds left in a 22-21 win. He also led wins over Ohio State, Tennessee, and Missouri this season.

On Friday, First Take asked Bayless where he would draft Manning if he declared for the draft. Bayless said that Manning's comeback against LSU convinced him.

"Stephen A, you and I have gone back and forth about Arch Manning all season long, and I have watched every single game this young man has played. And after I watched the other night him come back to beat LSU despite throwing three interceptions, I concluded that if I had the first overall pick this year and I needed a quarterback, I would be afraid not to take Arch Manning," Bayless said, via ESPN.

"I would be afraid not to close one eye and just bet on him being a late bloomer. Bet on his bloodlines. Bet on the fact that I think he's got more talent than his grandfather or his two uncles had at 6'4, 225 pounds, nice arm, and can run," he continued.

Bayless then acknowledged the holes in his argument, and he didn't hold back on Manning's flaws.

"He's 54th in the country in QBR. He's 19 touchdowns to 10 interceptions, which ranks fourth most in the country. He has only one 300-yard passing game this year. I watched it. It was the opener against Texas State that Texas won 59-7. He gets over emotional. He can be thin skinned. He can be a bit of a spoiled brat. He loses his poise and his confidence too quickly. He loses sometimes even his accuracy, which scares me to death," Bayless said.

Still, Bayless said Manning's big moments outweigh all of that.

"But in the end, I saw him come back to beat Ohio State and I saw him beat Tennessee and I saw him come back at Missouri and then I saw him, what he did against LSU late in that game. I've told you all year, he'll make four or five throws every single game that makes you say, 'There, that's a Manning.' So to me, I'm closing my eyes, at least one of them, and taking him number one overall," Bayless said.

Stephen A. Smith said he couldn't go that far yet. He also argued that Bayless was only taking Manning because of his last name.

"You're saying you would because he's a Manning and you're scared to death what would happen if you passed up on a Manning. If his last name wasn't Manning and you just looked at his level of production on a college football field, I think it would not. It's because he's a Manning," Smith said.

Bayless also said he would love to see Manning land with the New York Jets, and he challenged Smith to compare Manning's tape to Mestemaker's.

"I'd love to see him with the Jets in New York. I think he would eventually grow up and turn around the Jets. He is that talented with that lineage," Bayless said.

Clearly, Bayless is all in on Manning. Whether Manning even declares for the draft is another question.
