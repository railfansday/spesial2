JPMorgan Chase said Monday’s banking disruption was caused by an internal software bug and was not cyber-related. The outage came days after Microsoft’s global Outlook and Teams failure and a nationwide FAA ground stop on Friday caused by faulty air traffic control equipment. There is no confirmed link between the incidents. Loyola University New Orleans business dean Bobby Savoie said the timing has fuelled speculation but called it an in-house slowdown at Chase. Experts have argued the public deserves a fuller explanation than software bugs and old equipment, and said the run of failures raises questions about the security of critical infrastructure.
