Confirming an MQL5 fill with OnTradeTransaction instead of guessing from OrderSend

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

OrderSend returning true, or CTrade returning a done retcode, means the trade server accepted the request. It does not always mean the position you wanted is open at the volume you wanted. Partial fills, closings that leave a remainder, and netting account quirks all show up after the request. The reliable place to learn what actually happened is OnTradeTransaction.

OnTradeTransaction receives three structures. The one that matters for fills is the transaction of type TRADE_TRANSACTION_DEAL_ADD. That fires when a deal is added to history. From there you can read the deal ticket, then the deal properties: symbol, volume, price, deal type, position identifier, magic and entry type.

Treat OrderSend as the request and OnTradeTransaction as the receipt. The receipt is what your risk logic should trust.
