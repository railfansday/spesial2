Ayden Heaven: 90% and 7.6 stats prove Man United could be about to make another transfer mistake

Ayden Heaven should not be let go by Man United in January despite reports claiming that he may seek an exit.

Mauricio Pochettino already has one eye on the January transfer window as he settles into life as Man United manager.

The new boss endured a tough first game in charge last weekend as his side's shortfalls became obvious in a 4-0 defeat at Liverpool.

Heaven, 20, sat on the bench for the clash and saw four centre-backs utilised ahead of him at Anfield following Leny Yoro's red card.

New reports have now claimed that he is open to leaving Old Trafford in the new year, with a loan exit preferred but a permanent departure yet to be ruled out.

It is pretty obvious that, given his desire for persistent game time, Pochettino should promote Heaven and not look to let him leave the club midway through the campaign.

BBC Sport correspondent Nizaar Kinsella has reported that Heaven believes United's ongoing struggles could limit his chances of more minutes as the season progresses.

He is keen to take the next step in his development and so could seek an exit in January, with the likes of Lisandro Martinez, Leny Yoro, Matthijs De Ligt and Harry Maguire all perceived to be ahead of him in the centre-back pecking order.

This simply shouldn't be the case, as despite still being raw and unpolished as a defender, Heaven has still impressed statistically and is not one of United's biggest problems at the back.

Heaven is among the top-performing Premier League centre-backs for numerous key metrics, after playing around 225 minutes in the top flight so far.

He ranks highly for headed shots (0.8, 92nd percentile), successful dribbles (0.4, 81st percentile) and duels won (7.6, 93rd percentile) per 90 minutes, as well as successful pass percentage (90, 80th percentile).

This comes alongside his strong stats for successful tackles (2.4, 89th percentile), ball recoveries (6, 95th percentile) and fouls won (2.4, 97th percentile) per 90 too.

Pochettino should be thinking of making Heaven a more permanent fixture in the Red Devils' eleven over their other struggling centre-backs, rather than potentially being open to letting him leave on loan or permanently.

Heaven may feel like he is down the pecking order right now, but that could all change in the coming months.

Martinez and Maguire are both approaching the end of their respective United contracts in June 2027, and neither seems likely to sign a new deal as it stands.

Sources have exclusively revealed to Football Insider this week that Pochettino may look to move De Ligt on in January if his poor form continues after his horrific return to the team against Liverpool.

Heaven should surely just stay put for now and bide his time to be handed regular chances in the Red Devils' team, as he clearly has the potential to be good enough and lots of time on his side.
