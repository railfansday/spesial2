Wonderkid Confirms Tottenham Deal As Spurs Beat Arsenal To Next Williams-Barnett

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Luca Williams-Barnett has finally made the breakthrough that many Tottenham Hotspur fans have been anticipating for years.

The 18-year-old scored and assisted on his full Premier League debut on Saturday, as Spurs beat Ipswich Town 2-0 to move into the top half of the table.

All eyes will now be on Roberto De Zerbi's team selection for the game with Sunderland on Sunday, to see if the teenage star is handed a second successive start in the top flight.

With Savio and Mathys Tel both out injured, the likelihood is that he will be thrown into the starting lineup once more, but De Zerbi may look to ease the pressure on him and take him out of the limelight, particularly away from home in a hostile Stadium of Light atmosphere.

It has taken a while for Spurs to see another young player come out of the academy and really follow in Harry Kane's footsteps and make the grade at the club, with Harry Winks and Oliver Skipp failing to come close to those heights.

In Williams-Barnett, Spurs supporters have someone to pin their hopes on.

Attention will soon turn to who could be next, with Tottenham working hard behind the scenes to ensure progression from the youth ranks is improved.

During last month’s Fan Advisory Board meeting, Spurs confirmed that they had been actively recruiting players under the age of 16, to showcase their commitment to the academy setup.

And they've brought in a player from London rivals West Ham United, with 15-year-old Isaac Allen making the move to the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.

Spurs' fierce north London rivals Arsenal were also keen on signing the defender, but Spurs have won the race for his signature - although he will not sign his first professional deal until he turns 17, such are the rules in England - and he will join up with their U16s side.

GIVEMESPORT reported Spurs were close to bringing the youngster in way back in July, but the player himself has only just confirmed the move, saying on Instagram: "Happy to be signed with Tottenham.”

Allen can play at centre-back or in a defensive midfield position and is regarded as one of the most promising young talents in England. He is the captain of the England U15s side.

Tottenham have lost a number of young players this year, with Luka Vuskovic joining Brighton in a £50million deal and Will Lankshear moving to Middlesbrough for £20million.

Alfie Devine has also completed a permanent move to Preston North End, while Tynan Thompson has joined Manchester United.
