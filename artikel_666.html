Jamie Dornan Hasn’t ‘Spoken Directly’ to Viggo Mortensen About Aragorn Recasting in ‘Lord of the Rings: Hunt for Gollum,’ But Says ‘His Approval’ is ‘All I Care About’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Jamie Dornan recently guested on “Watch What Happens Live With Andy Cohen” and revealed that he and fellow “Lord of the Rings” actor Viggo Mortensen haven’t yet spoken about his casting as Aragorn in “Lord of the Rings: The Hunt for Gollum.”

In the forthcoming film, Dornan takes over the role from Mortensen, who originated and then reprised it in the first three “Lord of the Rings” films, from 2001 to 2003. He continued: “But he gave the stamp of approval, and that’s all I care about. They released a thing that he sort of agreed to, to say that he was thrilled that I’d be taking over for him.” “I’m thrilled to see you,” added fellow “WWHL” gusset Anthony Mackie. (The two actors star on the recently released Apple TV series “12 12 12.”)

The stamp of approval Dornan is referring to came in April, when Andy Serkis appeared on the “Happy, Sad, Confused” podcast and told host Josh Horowitz that “we are thrilled that Jamie’s doing it. I mean, we’re, you know, we’re absolutely thrilled. And, by the way, so is Viggo.”

Previously, Dornan told Entertainment Tonight that he “cried” when he found out he got the part. “It’s like a dream,” he said. “It’s an absolute dream and an honor to be entering that world in any capacity, let alone playing a character of such significance and such admiration, and that we all love and love what Viggo did with that role in those three films.”

Serkis, who provided the voice and motion capture for Gollum in Peter Jackson’s original trilogy, directed the forthcoming film, which is set to open in theaters Dec. 17, 2027. The ensemble also sees the return of Ian McKellen (as Gandalf), Elijah Wood (Frodo) and Lee Pace (King Thranduil), in addition to new cast members Kate Winslet (Marigol), Leo Woodall (Halvard) and Anya Taylor-Joy as an elf.
