Scamacca ‘has everything’ but must start to show his best, says Sarri

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Gianluca Scamacca is responsible for his own performances and getting back to his best level, so says Atalanta boss Maurizio Sarri.

Scamacca has netted just one Serie A goal this season, with that effort coming against Cagliari in September.

The Italy international netted 10 goals in 24 league appearances last term.

And ahead of Sunday's clash with reigning Serie A champions Inter, Sarri has demanded more from Scamacca.

Sarri told reporters: "Scamacca is a top player; he has everything: technique and physicality.

"Someone like him should always be scoring 20 goals. Unfortunately, though, he looks inhibited.

"I have spoken to him a lot over the last few days. He needs to break through, mentally as well, but the solution to solving the problems is down to him.

"He has shown very little of his true potential; he is capable of 100%, but on the pitch he is only producing 20%. I can support him, but it depends on him."

Atalanta, who have been used to competing for Champions League qualification in recent seasons, are down in 12th, with 14 points from their 11 league matches so far.

And Ederson – the midfielder who seemed all set to join Manchester United this past summer – is another player Sarri wants to see more from.

"I expect more in terms of performance. He can improve in that regard: I think he is aware of that," Sarri said of Ederson.

Atalanta have gained seven points in Serie A this season thanks to goals scored by  Krstovic.

No other player has earned more points for their team with goals scored this term (Daniel Maldini and Davide Frattesi also have seven). The forward scored in his only previous Serie A match for Atalanta against Inter, in a 1-1 draw in March.

Martinez has been back in training after a spell out injured, in a huge boost for Inter boss Cristian Chivu.

The Argentine has scored in five of his last six matches against Atalanta in the competition; in total, he has netted eight goals against them in Serie A, only against Cagliari (12) and Salernitana (10) has he scored more (also eight against Lazio).

Inter have gained more points than any other side in Serie A this season with goals scored after the 75th minute (nine, more than twice as many as any other team), while Atalanta have dropped the most points with goals conceded in the same timeframe (five).

Atalanta have conceded 20 goals in Serie A this season, an average of 1.8 per game, while scoring 17; the only time they have conceded more goals in their first 12 seasonal games in the competition in the 2000s was in 2002-03 (23), while the last top-flight campaign in which they conceded more goals after 12 matchdays while scoring at least 15 was in 1955-56 (24 for, 22 against).

After a run of nine consecutive away wins, Inter have failed to win six of their last 11 Serie A away games (D4 L2); however, in general, the Nerazzurri have scored at least one goal in each of their last 24 top-flight matches played away from San Siro, their longest such streak in the competition's history.

Atalanta are unbeaten in three of their last four Serie A matches (W1 D2), after losing four of the previous five (W1); furthermore, their 1-0 win against Monza last time out was only their second clean sheet in the current campaign – the last time they kept two consecutive clean sheets in Serie A was in April.

Inter have won nine of their 11 Serie A games this season (D1 L1) and could reach double figures for wins in their first 12 matches for the fifth time in their history in the competition, after 1950-51, 1988-89, 2019-20 and 2023-24.

Atalanta have not won any of their last 15 Serie A matches against Internazionale (D6 L9).
