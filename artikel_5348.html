Former xAI founders are launching a predictive AI startup called Epochal

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Epochal says its time portal lets AI agents browse the internet as it existed on past dates, with trillions of pages over 30 years

Epochal, a new AI startup, announced a tool on Tuesday that gives AI agents a way to view the web as it looked on a past date.

The company was started by three of the people who founded Elon Musk's xAI and exited after he folded it into SpaceX in February, according to Bloomberg. They are Guodong Zhang, Zihang Dai and Manuel Kroiss. Former staffers from xAI, OpenAI, Anthropic and Google $GOOGL DeepMind have joined them.

Epochal calls the tool the Epochal Time Portal and said it holds trillions of pages spanning 30 years of history. The company said it believes the best measure of intelligence is the ability to predict the future, which requires learning from the past and working to be less wrong.

Epochal said current AI systems learn from the internet all at once, knowing how each story ends. It wants AI to live through thousands of yesterdays, one day at a time, the way people do.

Epochal's goal, according to Bloomberg, is a model meant to assist people when they have choices to make.

Zhang is putting $100 million into the company, according to Bloomberg, which cited an unnamed source. An Epochal spokesperson would not discuss funding, Bloomberg said.

Epochal joins a growing list of new companies founded by people who departed xAI this year, according to Bloomberg. xAI co-founder Igor Babuschkin started River AI, a personalized AI startup, alongside several ex-xAI and Tesla $TSLA employees. Former xAI staffers Yuchen He and Eric Zelikman were part of the founding team behind the AI lab Humans&.
