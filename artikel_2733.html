Announcers set for Raiders vs. 49ers Week 9 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The Las Vegas Raiders are back to square one after defeating the New York Jets in Week 8. But at 4-4, their road trip continues by visiting the San Francisco 49ers in Week 9.

The 49ers are coming off a shocking road loss to the Atlanta Falcons. That setback should motivate Kyle Shanahan's crew to do better this time. Along with trying to win again, the Niners are undefeated at home.

Therefore, the Raiders have the opportunity to dent that perfect record. As the Raiders compete for a victory, a crew from FOX Sports will call every snap from the broadcast booth.

The FOX trio of Kevin Kugler, Daryl Johnston, and Allison Williams will be the narrators for this cross-conference matchup.

Kugler has been a play-by-play announcer for FOX since 2020. He is also a play-by-play announcer for Westwood One's NFL coverage and previously covered games for the Big Ten Network. He is a University of Nebraska alum who has won the Nebraska Sportscaster of the Year award nine times.

Meanwhile, Johnston is a former NFL fullback who won three Super Bowl titles with the Dallas Cowboys. He has been an NFL analyst for FOX since 2001, and he formerly worked for CBS. Affectionately called "Moose," Johnston has also covered college football games, particularly the Orange Bowl and the Sugar Bowl.

Finally, Williams became a sideline reporter for FOX in 2022. She was previously a sideline reporter for the Miami Marlins and also covered the Florida Panthers. Williams also delivered insider stories during ESPN's coverage of college football and basketball games.
