Stampede Blue Reacts results (Week 12): Colts fan confidence falls to 12% as frustration reaches new low

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

According to this week's SB Nation Reacts survey, just 12 percent of Colts fans believe the team is headed in the right direction. That number marks another sharp drop in fan confidence and shows just how bleak things have become in Indianapolis. At this point, the overwhelming majority of the fan base appears to have little belief in the current direction of the franchise.

The continued decline in confidence is hardly surprising. The Colts have spent the past several weeks spiraling, and whatever optimism may have briefly surfaced after some of the front office changes has quickly disappeared. Poor offensive play, frustrating coaching decisions and the overall lack of progress from week to week have left fans with very little reason to feel encouraged. Rather than showing signs of a team building toward something, Indianapolis has looked like a franchise stuck in the middle of a messy transition.

The biggest recent development, of course, is that Daniel Jones is now gone, officially closing the book on a move that never gave the Colts the boost they were hoping for. Jones had a few respectable moments early on, but the offense became increasingly dysfunctional under his watch, and it eventually reached a point where a change simply had to be made. His departure does not solve all of Indianapolis' problems, but it does signal that the organization recognized the situation was no longer tenable.

Even so, the quarterback change alone is unlikely to restore confidence overnight. The issues with this team go far beyond one player. Shane Steichen continues to face questions about the offense, the roster still has major holes, and the Colts remain far away from looking like a legitimate contender. At the same time, fans are now left wondering whether this next phase will at least provide some clarity about the future, even if the short-term results remain ugly.

For now, this week's 12 percent confidence mark says it all. Colts fans are not just frustrated, they are increasingly unconvinced that this franchise has a real plan in place. If Indianapolis wants that number to rise in the coming weeks, it will need to show something far more convincing than it has lately.
