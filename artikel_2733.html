After Thursday's loss, will Colts give Anthony Richardson another real shot?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Down 26-3 to the Houston Texans Thursday night, the Colts made a quarterback change, benching Daniel Jones and bringing in Anthony Richardson for mop-up duty in the blowout loss.

The Colts took this approach late in the 42-7 loss to the Jaguars on Nov. 1, giving Richardson a few token snaps. It wasn't an evaluation; it was a cup of coffee. That was his last appearance prior to Thursday.

Richardson, 24, has been in limbo for a few years now, a gifted talent in a state of arrested development, playing for a team that doesn't appear too jazzed to get a longer re-examination of a player they drafted fourth overall just three years ago.

On Thursday, Richardson entered midway through the fourth quarter and missed his first two throws of the night. There was no second series; the Texans ran out the clock. And it almost feels like the Colts are trying to run out the clock on Richardson, Jones, Riley Leonard — all of it.

Head coach Shane Steichen this week said he takes "everything into account" when it comes to the quarterbacks. He added: "Obviously, the biggest thing is taking care of the football moving forward."

Asked about a potential QB change after Thursday's loss, Steichen said, "we'll evaluate everything."

Steichen previously has been reluctant to put all the blame on Jones, and it's fair to point out that the Colts offloaded two notable talents at the trade deadline in offensive guard Quenton Nelson and wide receiver Josh Downs.

But the problems have been chronic, even when those player were on the roster. Jones hasn't had more touchdowns than interceptions in any game this season. He's thrown picks in 10 of his 11 games. The contract extension he signed in the offseason is looking more painful, on the books for $46.8 million next season, although it's a deal the Colts can get out of as a post-June 1 cut.

Still, if we're already forecasting the potential end of Jones in Indianapolis, and likely another pivot at quarterback for the franchise, why is Richardson not even a provisional option at this point? Is he that far gone in their eyes?

The excuse for not making a change this week was the Thursday game and the shortened schedule. But it's not as if Richardson hasn't been here for a minute. It's hard to imagine Steichen sees a whole lot of upside in Richardson, or the hook would have come sooner for Jones. Heck, it wouldn't be stunning if Steichen gave Leonard a chance, even over Richardson, at some point.

The Colts declined Richardson's fifth-year option, so the writing has been on the wall for a bit. It's hard not to look back at that 2024 game when Richardson reportedly "needed a breather" as a turning point in Steichen's trust in the QB, even if he ended up bringing Richardson back a few weeks later. Whenever that turning point happened, the trust level just hasn't been there for some time.

Steichen has hinted that a QB change could come, even prior to Thursday night. So there could be an opportunity for someone. There are six games remaining, with the Week 13 bye upcoming.

But isn't Steichen worried about his job? The general manager is already gone, and the owner is watching the operation closely. Outside of a narrow home win over the Dolphins, it hasn't looked very pretty lately. Steichen is going to do what he feels he needs to do to keep his job.

Is there any upside to bringing Jones back next week against the Giants? The Colts might not believe in Richardson, but they could end up helping each other out. If the QB can string a few games together, it helps his chances elsewhere next season. And if the Colts can win a few games, maybe everyone won't lose their jobs.

The big picture isn't pretty, and the Colts still don't have a first-round pick next year, but it's hard to imagine they can't try to squeeze something out of a player who at least has made some energy-generating plays with his legs, even if Richardson's passing metrics haven't been terribly defensible.

Beggars can't be choosers. Jones is playing like a guy who's less than a year removed from an Achilles injury, and the Colts' roster has been gutted. Sure, Leonard is the QB who might have the best chance to return next season, but Richardson has the better talent.

Can he get one more shot to do something in Indy?
