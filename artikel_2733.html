David Pastrnak’s hilarious reaction to Elias Lindholm’s hat trick

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

David Pastrnak's hilarious reaction to Elias Lindholm's hat trick appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Boston Bruins have had some rough games as of late. But they are getting things back on track a little bit. David Pastrnak and his teammates defeated the Detroit Red Wings earlier in the week. And thanks to Elias Lindholm, they got another huge win over the Washington Capitals.

The Bruins won 7-5 over Washington. Lindholm was a major part of this win, scoring four goals and adding an assist. When he scored his third goal, fans didn't throw any hats onto the ice, as is tradition. It turns out fans weren't the only ones who weren't aware of Lindholm's feat at the time.

"Honestly, I also didn't know it was his third goal. I can't even blame the fans because I knew it was his goal, but somehow I forgot about the first-period goal," Pastrnak said, via Conor Ryan of Boston.com.

The Bruins went toe-to-toe with Alex Ovechkin and the Capitals on Saturday. Lindholm's first goal of the night tied the game in the first period, but the Capitals took the lead back before the first intermission.

The second period saw the two teams combine for six total goals. Lindholm completed his hat trick in the second period, giving the Bruins a 5-4 lead entering the third period.

Early in the third, the veteran Bruins forward scored his fourth of the night to make it 6-4. The Capitals added one to make it a one-goal game, but it wasn't enough.

The Bruins are fourth in the Atlantic Division, very much keeping pace in the playoff race early on. Boston will look for its third straight win on Wednesday when they take on the Winnipeg Jets before American Thanksgiving.
