‘He’s a coward. He can’t coach’: Dave Portnoy demands apology after being ‘right about Ryan Day from the jump’

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

It was a rough weekend for Michigan, as the Wolverines lost to rival Michigan State and fired their offensive coordinator. But Barstool Sports founder and devoted Michigan fan Dave Portnoy still found something to celebrate.

Ohio State suffered a blowout loss of its own, falling to Oregon for its second straight defeat. And Portnoy, who has been a longtime critic of Buckeyes head coach Ryan Day, said he's owed an apology from every Ohio State fan in Columbus.

Portnoy has been one of Day's harshest critics for years, and things have only gotten more personal this season. In August, he called Day a "scared little chicken-boy" after Ohio State moved its senior day from the Michigan game to Week 1.

Then in October, Barstool was removed from Fox's Big Noon Kickoff, and Portnoy blamed Day for the decision. "I always knew Ryan Day was soft but holy sh*t even I didnt realize he was this soft," Portnoy wrote on X at the time, calling the Buckeyes coach "Cryin Ryan." Awful Announcing noted that the breakup was always inevitable.

A couple of weeks later, Portnoy said that Day "would be unsuccessful at any program other than Ohio State." "Ryan Day was born on third base," Portnoy said. "Like, anybody here could coach that team to 10, 11 wins."

This weekend gave Portnoy plenty of new material. Michigan fell 15-6 to Michigan State, and the Wolverines fired offensive coordinator Jason Beck, whom Portnoy had called "horrific." But Ohio State blew a 14-0 lead in a 41-28 loss to Oregon, its second straight defeat, and Day said afterward that "there needs to be accountability" for the team's struggles.

On Monday's episode of Wake Up Barstool, Portnoy made it clear that he was enjoying Ohio State's struggles.

"I want an apology from every single person in Columbus. I want them to get in line and line up," Portnoy said, in a clip shared by the show.

"I want them all to come in a receiving line and say, 'Hey, Dave Portnoy, you've been right about Ryan Day from the jump. He's a coward. He can't coach. He was born on third base.' All of it. Get in line and say, 'I'm sorry, you should be back on Big Noon Kickoff. It was a joke what they did to you,'" he continued.

Portnoy admitted that Michigan has problems of its own, but he said that Ohio State's situation is even worse given the talent on the roster.

"Yes, we have problems in Ann Arbor, but that team's a joke with the talent they have. So yeah, real bad weekend for Michigan, no doubt," Portnoy said. "But let's not forget, as bad as a weekend Michigan had, Michigan State had a worse weekend, and Ohio State had the worst weekend of the bunch."

Needless to say, Portnoy is going to enjoy every minute of this before The Game.
