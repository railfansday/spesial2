LIVE: Winners and losers from NFL's Thanksgiving trifecta

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Ready for a Thanksgiving day of copious football portions? Then you're in the right place.

We know you're thankful for winners and losers any time there's NFL action, so we thought we'd provide them in real time as the trifecta of games on Thursday, Nov. 26, ramps up. From the early NFC North showdown between the Chicago Bears and Detroit Lions, into the midday game between the Philadelphia Eagles and Dallas Cowboys and through the nightcap in Buffalo, where the Bills will host the Kansas City Chiefs in what's become an annual rivalry of AFC powerhouses, we'll break it down over the course of the day.

Admit it, you thought the man coaching his 100th game for the Lions messed up by taking the safety with 73 seconds to go in the half when he could have declined a Bears batting penalty in their end zone and set up the Detroit offense inside the red zone. Instead, Campbell took the two points, and then the Lions added a touchdown with an eight-play, 71-yard drive that took 67 seconds after they accepted the the free kick − nine-point run to end the half. Aggressive coach listening to his gut and believing in his players, which has served him pretty well through the years in Motown.

You enjoyed the halftime show from Detroit, right? Good vibes. No U2 or anything but excellent performance, yeah?

Detroit's do-it-all − in spectacular fashion − tailback scored his 53rd career rushing TD on Thursday, the most ever by an NFL player yet to turn 25 years old. He broke a tie with Hall of Famer Barry Sanders, still the greatest Lions runner − and maybe the best in league history, depending on your perspective − who ever lived. By the fourth quarter, Gibbs had scored three times and had exceeded 200 yards from scrimmage as the Lions took a two-score lead.

Week 3: Indianapolis Colts kicker Spencer Shrader (3) celebrates with long snapper Luke Rhodes (46) after Shrader kicked a 58-yard field goal against the Houston Texans at Lucas Oil Stadium. The Colts won, 19-17, for their first win of the season.

Week 3: Buffalo Bills quarterback Josh Allen (17) hands off to running back James Cook III against the Los Angeles Chargers at New Highmark Stadium. The Bills won the game, 24-16.

Week 2: Los Angeles Rams quarterback Matthew Stafford (9) hands the ball to running back Kyren Williams (23) during a 28-6 win on "Monday Night Football" against the New York Giants at SoFi Stadium.
