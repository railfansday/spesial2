Why JetBlue Is Letting TrueBlue Members Earn & Spend Points On United Airlines

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

JetBlue Airways is giving members of its TrueBlue loyalty program more opportunities to earn and redeem points through its commercial partnership with United Airlines. Known as Blue Sky, the arrangement connects the loyalty programs of both carriers, giving customers access to a much larger combined network. For JetBlue customers, one of the biggest advantages is the ability to spend their points on United Airlines flights, including services to destinations outside JetBlue's own network.

The partnership was announced in May 2025, with reciprocal loyalty earning and redemption launching five months later. It has since expanded to include additional travel benefits for qualifying elite members. The arrangement gives JetBlue an opportunity to increase the value of TrueBlue while providing United Airlines with access to another established customer base. It also offers travelers greater flexibility when planning domestic and international journeys.

JetBlue and United Airlines announced their Blue Sky commercial partnership in May 2025, bringing together two carriers with different network strengths. JetBlue focuses heavily on domestic services, leisure destinations, and important East Coast markets, while United Airlines operates an extensive domestic and international network. Combining their loyalty programs gives customers more travel opportunities without requiring either airline to operate every route itself.

For JetBlue, the partnership addresses a limitation of its TrueBlue program: its comparatively restricted destination network. Although JetBlue serves several international markets, including transatlantic services to London Heathrow Airport (LHR) and Amsterdam Schiphol Airport (AMS), it cannot independently match the geographical coverage of a major global airline. United Airlines, meanwhile, serves destinations across six continents, giving TrueBlue members considerably more opportunities to redeem their points.

The arrangement also benefits United Airlines by making its network more attractive to JetBlue customers. Rather than building a separate MileagePlus balance, travelers can use their existing TrueBlue points to book eligible United-operated flights. This encourages customers to remain engaged with JetBlue's loyalty program, even when their preferred destination is outside the airline's route network.

Importantly, Blue Sky is a commercial collaboration rather than a merger. Both carriers retain their individual brands, aircraft, operating structures, and loyalty programs. Customers continue earning either TrueBlue points or MileagePlus miles, depending on their chosen program. The partnership simply creates additional opportunities to use those currencies across both networks.

The earning arrangement allows TrueBlue members to collect points when traveling on eligible United Airlines flights. Customers can include their TrueBlue loyalty number when booking qualifying services through participating booking channels. This is useful for passengers who want to maintain their loyalty with JetBlue despite choosing a United Airlines-operated flight for a particular journey.

TrueBlue members earn points on eligible spending, with the amount depending on applicable earning rules and their Mosaic membership level. Government-imposed taxes and surcharges are excluded from the calculation, while additional purchases may also be excluded. Members should check the current partnership terms to understand which parts of their ticket qualify.

Another important feature is that eligible United Airlines flights can contribute toward earning TrueBlue tiles, helping members progress toward Mosaic status. This means the partnership offers more than another way to collect points for future bookings. Customers can also use qualifying United Airlines travel to support their progress through JetBlue's elite membership program.

There is, however, an important distinction between earning points and transferring loyalty currencies. TrueBlue points cannot simply be transferred into a United MileagePlus account, and the two balances remain separate. Instead, members choose which program they want to earn with when making an eligible booking, preserving the independence of both loyalty schemes.

The ability to redeem TrueBlue points on United Airlines flights is arguably the most significant part of the Blue Sky arrangement. Previously, members were primarily restricted to using their accumulated points within JetBlue's network and its existing redemption partnerships. Adding United Airlines gives members access to considerably more destinations.

To find eligible flights, customers can visit JetBlue's website or mobile application and select the option to use TrueBlue points when searching. After entering their departure and arrival airports and preferred travel dates, they can view available United-operated services alongside applicable JetBlue options. Members must be signed in to their TrueBlue accounts before completing a booking.

The expanded network is particularly interesting for international travelers. United Airlines operates services across Europe, Asia, Africa, Oceania, and the Americas, giving TrueBlue members more opportunities to use their points. Destinations such as Rome (FCO), Barcelona (BCN), Cape Town (CPT), Sydney (SYD), and Auckland (AKL) illustrate the geographical reach available through the collaboration.

Nevertheless, not every United Airlines flight or fare is necessarily available for redemption through TrueBlue. Award availability and the number of points required can vary by itinerary and booking conditions. Taxes and fees may also apply, making it important for members to compare available options before confirming their reservations.

Although much of the attention surrounding Blue Sky has focused on JetBlue customers, the partnership is reciprocal. United Airlines MileagePlus members can earn and redeem miles on eligible JetBlue-operated flights, providing another way to access destinations popular with leisure travelers. This is particularly useful in markets where JetBlue has an established presence.

MileagePlus members can earn miles by including their United Airlines loyalty number when booking eligible JetBlue flights. They can also redeem accumulated miles for qualifying JetBlue services through United Airlines' booking channels. This provides additional flexibility without requiring United Airlines customers to open and maintain a separate TrueBlue account.

The arrangement is strategically useful for the Star Alliance carrier because JetBlue has a strong presence in important leisure markets, particularly across the Caribbean and other popular vacation destinations. Access to these flights gives MileagePlus members more choices when planning holidays, visiting family, or arranging connections. JetBlue's award-winning Mint cabin is also a unique offering for passengers.

The Blue Sky partnership expanded further in May 2026, when JetBlue announced reciprocal travel benefits for eligible TrueBlue and MileagePlus elite members. This development extended the collaboration beyond earning and redemption, allowing qualifying customers to receive additional airport and seating benefits when traveling on the other airline.

For JetBlue Mosaic members traveling on United Airlines, benefits include priority check-in, priority security, and priority baggage handling. Members also receive one complimentary checked bag, preferred seat selection, and access to extra-legroom seating at check-in. Mosaic 1 members receive Group 2 boarding, while Mosaic 2, Mosaic 3, and Mosaic 4 members receive Group 1 boarding.

United Airlines also provides additional recognition to eligible Premier members traveling on JetBlue. Premier Silver members receive Group 3 boarding, Premier Gold members receive Group 2 boarding, and Premier Platinum and Premier 1K members receive Group 1 boarding. Other benefits include priority check-in and security, one free checked bag, and priority baggage handling.

Extra-legroom seating is another useful addition, with eligible passengers receiving access to EvenMore seating on JetBlue and Economy Plus seating on United Airlines at check-in. The arrangement also includes same-day standby options, providing greater flexibility when travel plans change. These benefits make elite status with either airline more valuable across the combined network.

The booking process is relatively straightforward. TrueBlue members looking to earn points on United Airlines services should include their TrueBlue number when booking eligible flights. Those wishing to redeem points can visit JetBlue's website or mobile application, select the points option, and search for available United-operated services.

United Airlines MileagePlus members can similarly include their loyalty number when booking eligible JetBlue-operated services. For award travel, members should search through the appropriate airline's booking platform and select the option to pay using miles or points. Keeping the correct frequent flyer number attached to the reservation is also important for receiving reciprocal elite benefits.

The partnership does not combine loyalty balances or automatically provide every benefit associated with the operating airline's own elite program. Instead, customers receive the specific earnings, redemption, and reciprocal benefits outlined under Blue Sky. Availability and eligibility can differ between flights, and award bookings may involve additional taxes, fees, or restrictions.

For JetBlue, the collaboration represents an important step toward making TrueBlue more competitive with larger international loyalty programs. Members can earn points and work toward Mosaic status through eligible United Airlines flights, redeem their balances across a larger network, and enjoy additional travel benefits when they qualify. With reciprocal advantages also available to United Airlines customers, Blue Sky gives both carriers a stronger reason to encourage loyalty beyond their individual route networks.
