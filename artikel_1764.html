Prediction: Crystal Palace vs Hoffenheim

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Turning their attention back to Europa League action on Thursday evening, Crystal Palace will welcome Hoffenheim to Selhurst Park.

With Europe’s elite club competitions stealing all of the midweek attention, there are plenty of eye-catching betting promotions available.

With all eyes falling on south London, you can find all of the key Crystal Palace vs Hoffenheim H2H stats here.

Verdict: Crystal Palace win & Under 4.5 goals

Starting with the hosts, while Pierre Sage might have only arrived at Selhurst Park over the summer, the former Lens boss already finds the pressure on his shoulders at a boiling point. Although the Eagles might have extended on their EFL Cup adventure last week as they snatched a 1-0 victory away at Fulham, Crystal Palace came immediately crashing back down to earth. Last seen over the weekend getting hit with a 2-0 loss against Tottenham, Sage’s downbeat squad have dropped into the Premier League’s drop zone. Potentially facing a major battle in the race to keep hold of their top-flight status this year, Thursday’s hosts have mustered just a sole win from any of their previous six consecutive appearances across all competitions. With their worrying rut underlined by an infamous 5-0 drumming against arch-rivals Brighton on October 18th, Crystal Palace are desperately searching for an essential Europa League boost here.

While French international Jean-Philippe Mateta might have been shown an early Premier League red card away at Tottenham on Saturday, Palace’s leading marksman is free to feature here. However, sustaining a recent groin injury and once again missing out over the weekend, Sage’s side are once again expected to be without the services of Ismaila Sarr.

Making a timely return between the sticks over the past fortnight, club captain and England international Dean Henderson will start between the sticks. Despite coming under plenty of recent scrutiny, Chelsea loanee Axel Disasi should keep his spot at the heart of Palace’s defence.

As for the visitors, while Hoffenheim might have claimed their own respective essential boost last month, Christian Ilzer’s men have come crashing back down to earth. Last seen over the weekend putting in a toothless performance as they eventually fell to a 2-0 loss away at Werder Bremen, the Blues find themselves in a similar position to Thursday’s opponents. Despite shattering a string of records last season, Hoffenheim have drastically failed to replicate a similar level this time around. Crashing out of the DFP Pokal last week as they were hit with penalty shootout heartbreak against Kiel, Illzer’s side have also gained an unwanted reputation for their glaring struggles away from the SNP Arena. Getting their Europa League adventure underway with another 2-0 defeat against Greek outfit OFI Crete on September 17th, Hoffenheim have lost five of their previous six showdowns on the road.

Missing out against Werder Bremen over the weekend with a reported thigh issue, Hoffenheim are once again sweating over the fitness of former West Ham defender Vladimir Coufal. Likewise, all still recovering from respective injury blows, Ilzer’s side will travel to south London without the likes of Patrick Wimmer, Albian Hajdari and Nathan De Cat here.

Now in his fourth campaign at the SNP Arena, one-time Liverpool defender Ozan Kabak will start at the heart of Hoffenheim’s defence. Registering three direct goal contributions in as many Europa League appearances (one goal, two assists), veteran striker Andrej Kramaric will shoulder plenty of the Blues’ attacking threat.

With the pressure on Sage’s shoulders starting to reach a boiling point, Crystal Palace are desperately searching for a much-welcomed Europa League boost here. Currently sat in the drop zone among England’s elite, we could be set for a nail-biting showdown from Selhurst Park on Thursday night. Watching Hoffenheim lose five of their previous six showdowns away from the SNP Arena across all competitions, Palace will be eager to kickstart their campaign. Although we are backing a home victory in south London, it’s worth highlighting that the Eagles have registered just a sole strike in over 300 minutes of action.

Verdict: Crystal Palace win & Under 4.5 goals
