Prediction: Austria vs Republic of Ireland

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Austria can secure promotion to UEFA Nations League A when they welcome the Republic of Ireland to the Raiffeisen Arena on Saturday evening.

The hosts begin the penultimate round of fixtures at the top of League B Group 3 with eight points, while Ireland sit third on five points. Victory for the visitors would throw the group wide open heading into the final matchday.

Austria have placed themselves in a strong position to secure an immediate return to League A.

Ralf Rangnick’s side remain unbeaten in the group, collecting eight points from four matches. Their most recent outing produced a 1-1 draw away to Kosovo, a result that maintained their place at the summit and ensured they stayed in control of their promotion hopes.

While Austria have impressed going forward, defensive issues have occasionally threatened to undermine their progress. They have conceded five goals in four group matches and have now gone eight consecutive games without keeping a clean sheet.

However, their home record remains formidable. Austria are unbeaten in their last 14 matches when playing as hosts, winning 11 of those fixtures and making the Raiffeisen Arena one of the toughest venues in the group.

Felix Bacher and Kevin Danso are expected to anchor the defence in front of goalkeeper Alexander Schlager, while Nicolas Seiwald and Xaver Schlager should provide experience in midfield.

In attack, Raul Florucz could be handed an important role following Marco Grull’s withdrawal through injury.

Ireland remain firmly in contention, but they know defeat would effectively end any hopes of winning the group.

Heimir Hallgrimsson’s side currently have five points and remain within striking distance of both Austria and Kosovo. However, the margin for error is extremely small with only two matches remaining.

The visitors were frustrated in their last Nations League outing, drawing 1-1 with Israel in a match played at a neutral venue. Before that, they suffered a disappointing 1-0 defeat against Kosovo, a result that prevented them from building momentum in the group.

Despite that setback, Ireland have generally been difficult to beat under Hallgrimsson and have lost only one of their last eight internationals.

Another source of encouragement is their recent record against Austria. The Irish are unbeaten in the last three meetings between the nations and held Austria to an entertaining 2-2 draw earlier in the group.

Much will depend on goalkeeper Caoimhin Kelleher, who continues to be one of Ireland’s most reliable performers.

Up front, Troy Parrott is expected to lead the attack, while Finn Azaz and Conor Coventry should operate in midfield alongside captain John Egan’s leadership at the back.

Austria’s outstanding home record makes them favourites, and Rangnick’s side know that victory would remove any final-day uncertainty.

However, Ireland have consistently proven difficult opponents for Austria and have shown enough resilience throughout the campaign to suggest they can be competitive in this fixture. With Austria’s defensive vulnerabilities continuing to surface, the visitors should be capable of creating chances.

The hosts are unlikely to lose in front of their own supporters, but Ireland may have enough organisation and determination to earn a valuable point.
