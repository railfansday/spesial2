OnePlus rolling out ColorOS to OnePlus 13, others as Android 17 update expands

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

OnePlus 13 is the latest OnePlus device to ditch OxygenOS for ColorOS as the brand’s Android 17 update rolls out more broadly.

When OnePlus shut down in global markets earlier this year, it was announced that OxygenOS would go away in favor of ColorOS, with supported devices all moving over to Oppo’s Android skin going forward. The transition started last week, ahead of schedule, on the OnePlus 15, and is now expanding to more devices.

The most notable device in this latest batch is the OnePlus 13, the brand’s prior flagship and perhaps one of its most beloved devices in recent memory. Owners can expect their devices to be updated to ColorOS 17 starting in India, with other regions to follow in the coming weeks.
