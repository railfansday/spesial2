The Quentin Tarantino Western He Once Called “Traumatizing” Is Officially Free to Stream

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

2026 is shaping up to be an exciting year for Quentin Tarantino, who hasn’t directed a feature film since Once Upon a Time… in Hollywood hit the big screen back in 2019. Later this month, the two-time Oscar-winning director is returning to the screen for The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, which is a continuation of the 2019 film bringing Brad Pitt back to his Oscar-winning role. Leonardo DiCaprio is not expected back as Rick Dalton in the new movie, which is launching in IMAX on November 25 before releasing on Netflix in December. Tarantino did not direct the film, so fans can expect things to look a little different with David Fincher at the helm, but he did write the script for the new Cliff Booth movie, which should keep his signature dialogue style intact.

Quentin Tarantino has directed fewer movies than many of the iconic directors that belong to his same generation, but his success rate is as high as it gets. Back in 2012, Tarantino wrote and directed one of the greatest Western epics ever made, Django Unchained, which featured the second of two Oscar-winning performances from Christoph Waltz — the first came just three years prior in another Tarantino-written and directed feature, Inglourious Basterds.

The movie was a smash hit at the box office, grossing $426 million against a $100 million budget, and it has since found success streaming on various platforms in the years since its release. However, for now, Django Unchained is available to stream for free thanks to the platform Pluto TV, which only asks its user to watch a few ads strategically placed throughout each movie or episode of a TV show.

When Quentin Tarantino had finished shooting Django Unchained and was in the editing stage for the film, he had to cut out or tone down several scenes, including the Mandingo fight scene and the scene with the dogs. He feared leaving these scenes as he originally wrote them left the potential to traumatize audiences, which could lead to negative word of mouth reviews that would have hindered box office success and overall reception. Additional cast members for Django Unchained include Samuel L. Jackson, Jamie Foxx, and even Walton Goggins.

Check out Django Unchained streaming for free on Pluto TV and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage of Quentin Tarantino’s future projects.
