Marlon Jackson Reveals Stage 4 Prostate Cancer Diagnosis

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Marlon Jackson, the brother of the late Michael Jackson and member of the Jackson 5, has revealed that he has stage 4 prostate cancer.

Jackson revealed the diagnosis during an episode of the “Farah Shammas Podcast,” adding that he’s been battling prostate cancer for four years.

“I’ve had my prostate gland removed and I’ve had radiation treatment and everything is fine. Everything is great,” Jackson said.

According to Johns Hopkins, stage 4 prostate cancer can occur even when the prostate gland is removed, as the cancer has metastasized into nearby organs, bones, or lymph nodes.

Jackson, who continues to perform with the Jacksons despite the cancer diagnosis, did not elaborate on the details of his condition, but instead talked about the ways he’s keeping healthy, including his diet and exercise routine.

“I walk almost four miles four times a week. You know, and that’s great for me, and eating healthy food,” Jackson said. “But I’m great. I’m good. Bad grass don’t die. So, I’m going to be around for a long time.”
