Research Solutions Reports First Quarter Fiscal Year 2027 Results

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Reports 11.5% YoY B2B ARR Increase and ARR of $22.5 Million

HENDERSON, Nev., Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Research Solutions, Inc. (NASDAQ: RSSS), the leading AI-powered research workflow platform, reported financial results for its first quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Fiscal First Quarter 2027 Summary (compared to prior-year quarter)

"Our continued investment in innovation is expanding how researchers discover, access, and engage with scientific literature," said Roy W. Olivier, President and CEO of Research Solutions. "Recent launches of Article Galaxy and Scite integrations with ChatGPT, Claude, and Microsoft Copilot bring our capabilities directly into the AI-powered workflows researchers increasingly rely on, making it easier to find trusted evidence and access scholarly content. Platform revenue accounted for 45% of total revenue this quarter, up from 42% a year ago, reflecting our continued transition toward a higher-margin, recurring revenue model. This evolution is strengthening profitability and operating cash flow, enabling us to reinvest in sales capacity and product innovation to accelerate long-term growth."

Fiscal First Quarter 2027 ResultsTotal revenue was $11.9 million, compared to $12.3 million in the year-ago quarter, as increased platform revenue was more than offset by a decrease in transaction revenue.

Platform subscription revenue for the quarter was $5.3 million, compared to $5.1 million in the prior-year period. The increase of 11.5% in B2B platform ARR was due to a mix of new logo generation and upsell and cross-sell into existing customers, partially offset by a decline in B2C revenue. The quarter ended with annual recurring revenue of $22.5 million, up 5.7% year-over-year (see the Company's definition of annual recurring revenue below).

Transaction revenue was $6.6 million, compared to $7.2 million in the first quarter of fiscal 2026. The decrease was due to lower paid order volume. The transaction active customer count for the quarter was 1,360, compared to 1,326 customers in the prior-year quarter (see the Company's definition of active customer accounts and transactions below).

Total gross margin improved 300 basis points from the prior-year quarter to 53.6%. The increase was primarily driven by the continued revenue mix shift to the higher-margin platforms business and an improvement in transactions gross margin to 26.7% from 23.8%, partially offset by a lower Platforms gross margin on slightly higher hosting and technology costs.

Total operating expenses were $5.5 million, compared to $5.3 million in the first quarter of 2026. The increase was primarily related to higher general and administrative expenses, driven by legal and severance costs along with higher technology and product development expense, partially offset by lower sales and marketing expense and a non-cash recovery of stock-based compensation due to restricted stock forfeitures.

Other expense for the quarter was approximately $0.1 million compared to $0.2 million in the first quarter of fiscal 2026, primarily due to lower accreted interest expense on the Scite earn-out liability.

Net income in the first quarter was $765,000 or $0.02 per diluted share, compared to $749,000, or $0.02 per diluted share, in the prior-year quarter. Adjusted EBITDA was $1.0 million, compared to $1.5 million in the year-ago quarter (see definition and further discussion about the presentation of Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP term, below).

Active Customer Accounts, Transactions and Annual Recurring RevenueThe Company defines active customer accounts as the sum of the total quantity of customers per month for each month in the period divided by the respective number of months in the period. The quantity of customers per month is defined as customers with at least one transaction during the month.

A transaction is an order for a unit of copyrighted content fulfilled or managed in the Platform.

The Company defines annual recurring revenue ("ARR") as the value of contracted Platform subscription recurring revenue normalized to a one-year period. For B2C ARR, this includes the annualized value of monthly subscriptions, meaning their monthly value multiplied by twelve.
