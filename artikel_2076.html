West Brom and Derby County product Archie Brown is the real deal, he should get England call-up

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Thomas Tuchel should be preparing to call Archie Brown up to the senior England squad for the first time as he impresses for Fenerbahce in the Champions League.

Tuchel names his latest England squad on the morning of Friday, 6 November ahead of their upcoming Nations League fixtures.

The Three Lions are second in their Nations League A group and round off their campaign with key clashes against Croatia and Spain later this month.

He has already shown a willingness to blood new players into the senior set-up since taking charge in January 2025 and must be ready to spring more surprises this time around.

Twenty-four-year-old Brown is proving himself as one of the best left-backs in Europe at Fenerbahce this season.

He may be overlooked by national media because of the league he plays in, but has shown quality in the Champions League and looks set to emerge as a key player for England in the coming years.

Brown played youth football for West Brom and Derby County but has spent his entire senior career so far overseas.

Spells at Swiss side Lausanne and Belgian outfit Gent preceded a 2025 move to Fenerbahce, and he has continued to improve over the last 18 months.

The 24-year-old, who has two caps to his name for England under-20s, already has five goal contributions in 16 appearances from left-back for Fener so far this season.

He is in the 84th percentile (0.25) for expected assists, 81st percentile (1.25) for successful dribbles, 80th percentile (6) for duels won and 76th percentile (0.5) for big chances created per 90 against other full-backs in the Champions League in 2026-27.

Brown also ranks among the best full-backs in the Super Lig for several key metrics this term, while his goal and assist against Liverpool on Wednesday was further proof that he can mix it with some of the best players in the world.

None of Tuchel's other left-back options are performing to a particularly high individual standard right now, and Brown would be a shrewd inclusion to his latest squad.

His quality showings may well be rewarded on these shores soon, with Liverpool believed to be among the clubs keen on his services as they look to solve their left-back woes next year.

It seems like a matter of time before Brown makes his way into a Three Lions squad, even if it does not come this month.

The 24-year-old was asked by the press about his national team aims last month ahead of a Champions League clash against Aston Villa, saying: "The Champions League is the pinnacle of club football. If you can manage to perform at that level, then hopefully it shows national team coaches that, yeah, he’s ready to perform for his national team.

"I believe in myself and what I’m able to do. I just have to keep performing. No matter what you want out of football, if it’s to make a selection, whatever it is, the only answer is to play well. The pinnacle of football is representing your country. I’ve got a lot of hard work to do and, God willing, we’ll get there eventually."
