Star midfielder set to return for Birmingham City as Burnley clash awaits

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Birmingham City are looking to get back to winning ways against Burnley after a 2-1 defeat to Stoke City on Saturday. BCFC will be looking to close the gap on the top two again, after Middlesbrough and Wolves both won on Tuesday night.

In a boost for the Blues, Chris Davies told Birmingham Live that midfielder Jhon Solis, who missed the weekend’s loss, is fit to return for the trip to Lancashire.

“It was a knock that we weren’t thinking would be long-term. He’s recovered well so he’s available. Profile wise he is hard to replace. He has such presence on the pitch because of his size and physicality – six foot two, big, strong, moves well.”

Since making his move from Girona permanent this summer, Solis has been a key part of this Birmingham City side. In 15 games, the 22-year-old has locked down the holding midfield role, with many of the Blues’ attacks running from the defence through him.

The 22-year-old was missed during BCFC’s loss to Stoke City on Saturday, with Davies’ men failing to gain control of the midfield without his physical presence. Tomoki Iwata, who is often Solis’ midfield partner, was not able to make his strong runs forward without Solis taking control of defensive duties in his absence.

All round, the statistics back up just how important the Colombian has been to this team. Solis ranks third in the side for recoveries with 6.41 per 90, leading midfielders with 4.1 defensive contributions per 90 and fourth in line-breaking passes per 90, with 7.66 – the only midfielder averaging more is Iwata (all stats via FotMob).

After losing a game, it’s easy to let it spiral, especially with the pressure at Birmingham City. Welcoming back a calm yet strong influence like Solis can be exactly what the Blues need – not just to get back to winning, but to stop their momentum from dropping.

The 22-year-old is one of the biggest keys to connecting this team together. He not only allows Iwata to play his game, but the group of attackers to play their best football as well.

Prior to the Stoke City game, BCFC had scored eight goals in their last three games. With Solis returning, it gives them the defensive stability to allow star forwards Jay Stansfield, Liam Millar and August Priske to flourish and get Davies’ side back to winning ways.
