Middlesbrough man set for long-awaited return from injury in December

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Middlesbrough have been without the defender in their last 11 games with a calf injury and hasn’t featured since their 4-3 home win over Norwich City back in mid-September. They are currently positioned at the top of the Championship table after 17 fixtures ahead of Stoke City and Wolves.

Jones, 29, has made seven appearances for Boro in all competitions this season, five of which have come in the Championship, and he has scored one goal. He made the move to the Riverside Stadium in the summer of 2025 and is under contract until June 2029.

The centre-back isn’t too far away from being available for selection again which is a boost. In this latest update regarding his situation, his head coach Hellberg has said, as per a report by The Northern Echo: “Alfie is moving on – two or three weeks, something like that.”

Middlesbrough won 2-1 at home against Swansea City last time out after goals by Myles Peart-Harris and Will Lankshear. They are back in action on Friday night with a trip to St Mary’s to take on Southampton as they aim to keep their momentum going.
