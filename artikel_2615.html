How McNicholas volleyball fared in 2026 state semifinals

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The second day of the Ohio High School Athletic Association volleyball state tournament saw McNicholas and Kings take the court for their semifinals while Seton played St. Ursula and Fenwick faced Ottawa-Glandorf in state championship matches.

The Rockets are looking to hoist the trophy for the second straight year, even after moving up to Division II. The Knights are back in the state tournament for the first time since winning the D I title in 2023.

The Enquirer will update this story with each semifinal result and what happened.

After a Division IV state championship a year ago, the Rockets are one win away from a Division III title. McNicholas swept through Notre Dame-Cathedral Latin, 25-22, 25-11, 25-18 to win the Division III semifinal at Vandalia Butler.

McNicholas found itself in a back-and-forth battle to start, trading small runs with NDCL with six ties and five lead changes. The Lions nearly extended the first game with a late 5-2 run, but a service error gave the Rockets the win.

From there, McNicholas settled down and played its game much as it had in the regional finals. Starting with a pair of Maria Schulte aces, the Rockets went on a 10-1 run to gain control of the second set and never look back.

The offense started firing on all cylinders, led by Savanna Stacey and Lilly Tierney each with double-digit kills. Stacey would finish with 19 kills on a sky-high .514 kill percentage, while Tierney was right behind with 12 at .460 percentage.

Notre Dame-Cathedral Latin tried to make a late push in the third set, going on a run to cut McNicholas's lead to 19-16, but that was as close as the Lions could get. More service aces proved deadly as Ali Strange set up match point, letting Kennedy Haucke slam home the final kill.

McNicholas advances to the title game for the second year in a row. The Rockets, now 25-3, will await the winner of Parma Padua Franciscan (18-8) and Carroll Bloom-Carroll (22-4). The state title will be played at 5 p.m. tomorrow, Nov. 14, at Wright State's Nutter Center.
