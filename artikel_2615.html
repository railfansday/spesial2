ACC Football Week 13 Predictions: Picks for Every Saturday Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The ACC has an interesting mix of rivalries against the SEC, and conference games to finalize the pecking order in the standings. All eyes are on Wake Forest as it looks to make a College Football Playoff statement against Duke, but Virginia vs Virginia Tech and Louisville at Kentucky are some of the other fun Week 13 Rivalry Week showdowns.
