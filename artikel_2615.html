USC men's basketball cruises past Louisiana, improves to 7-0

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

On Sunday evening, the USC men's basketball team hosted Louisiana at Galen Center. It marked the final of three consecutive home games for the Trojans before they head to Palm Springs for Thanksgiving.

Well, it was three games and three decisive victories for USC. The Trojans cruised past Louisiana 82-56, improving to 7-0 on the season.

Rodney Rice and Alijah Arenas once again led USC in scoring with 18 points each. Rice also contributed six assists and three rebounds. The Ratliff twins both had strong games as well, with Darius chipping in 17 points, six rebounds, and three assists, while brother Adonis added 14 points and eight rebounds.

As previously mentioned, the Trojans will now head to Palm Springs for the Acrisure Series this week. USC will take on South Carolina on Wednesday and Utah State on Thursday. Both games will tip off at 1:30 p.m. Pacific time on CBS Sports Network.
