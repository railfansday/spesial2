360 Advanced Joins PACT AI to Scale Trust in The AI Economy

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Cross-sector coalition brings together enterprises, technical experts, and civil society to build the infrastructure to verify AI model and system behavior.

ST. PETERSBURG, Fla., Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Today, 360 Advanced, a cybersecurity and compliance firm, announced that it is joining the Partnership for Assurance, Credibility, and Trust on AI (PACT AI), a broad coalition of enterprises, technical experts, AI insurers, and civil society leaders working together to build the shared infrastructure that the AI economy needs to earn and sustain public trust.

360 Advanced clients have deployed AI in clinical workflows, customer support, underwriting, and the SaaS platforms their own customers rely on, and a growing number are asking how to demonstrate that those systems work as intended. 360 Advanced assesses AI systems and also uses AI-enabled processes in its own engagement delivery.

"Organizations are putting AI into the systems their customers depend on, and they need a credible way to show those systems can be trusted," said John Kadechka, Managing Director, 360 Advanced. "As a CPA firm, independent assurance is at the core of what we do, and we bring the same rigor to every system we evaluate. PACT AI is bringing that discipline to AI, and we're proud to help shape the standards that make trust in AI verifiable."

No single company or organization can do this alone, which is why 360 Advanced supports PACT AI's mission to start with foundational work required to build a trusted assurance ecosystem: building common-sense policies, professionalizing the AI assurance sector, and growing the market to help meet the demand for rigorous verification.

"For more than two decades, 360 Advanced has been leading the way in developing high-quality compliance programs that are informed by the latest technology and grounded in human judgement," said Bri Treece, Executive Director of PACT AI. "Their deep expertise in cybersecurity is a powerful addition to our coalition, as we help companies and consumers verify that the AI models and systems powering our economy are working as intended."

As a PACT AI member, 360 Advanced will participate in the coalition's work to establish independently validated and measurable standards and practices for trusted AI deployment, with the goal of ensuring that AI systems used across the economy are safe, secure, and reliable.

The Partnership for Assurance, Credibility, and Trust on AI (PACT AI) unites enterprises, independent technical experts, AI insurers, and civil society voices to build the infrastructure to verify that the innovative AI systems powering our economy are safe, secure, and working as intended. PACT AI's work centers on three pillars: policy development and advocacy, professionalizing the AI assurance sector, and growing the AI assurance market.

For more information, including a full list of members and how to join, visit www.pactai.org.
