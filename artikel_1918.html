Liverpool may finally be getting the Florian Wirtz they spent so much money on to bring to Anfield.

The German international has struggled to impress in the Premier League, with only rare moments of quality shining through.

There is no doubt the 23-year-old is talented, but adapting to English football has been harder than he may have expected.

When Jurgen Klopp was appointed as Germany's manager, Liverpool fans would have been hoping he could help his former employers get him back to his best.

Since playing under the ex-Reds boss, the playmaker has clearly stepped up.

His latest performance on the international stage is perhaps proving this season's transformation is here to stay.

Wirtz was not just pulling the strings for Germany against Serbia, but was showing he can consistently be effective in attack.

That is exactly the sort of attacking threat Liverpool have been waiting to see on a regular basis, and it suggests he may be returning to the level that brought him to Anfield in the first place.

As per FotMob, Wirtz was very active in the final third, with the Liverpool midfielder having the most touches in the opposition box (eight), while also taking the most shots (five).

His efforts at goal would be rewarded, with the German scoring in the 14th minute to put his national side ahead.

Across his whole display, German media outlet Sport1, described him as being "everywhere in attack" and said that his display made him "virtually unstoppable."

And his statistics perfectly paint that picture, with him having a 93 per cent pass accuracy, as well as creating two chances for his team.

Wirtz completed two out of four dribbles and accrued the highest combination of expected goals and expected assists (1.69).

Since the last international break, Wirtz' performances for Liverpool have been excellent and this latest Germany display proves its continuing.

He has averaged 0.77 goal contributions per 90 over the nine matches in all competitions between his last two Germany appearances.

His confidence has transformed him, and Andoni Iraola will be delighted to have a player of his level back in form.

While many may have written him off at Anfield, Wirtz is showing he may not yet be finished for the Merseyside club.
