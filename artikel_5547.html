GEYOTO Wraps Up RE+ 2026 Debut with Strong Visitor Engagement and Real-World Product Experiences

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

From creator coverage and product demonstrations to an artist-painted N1000, GEYOTO's Las Vegas debut connected energy innovation with everyday needs.

LAS VEGAS, Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- GEYOTO concluded its first appearance at RE+ 2026 with an active booth, interest among content creators and visitors, and product experiences that brought its "Go-to Energy" philosophy to life. During the Nov. 17â19 exhibition at the Las Vegas Convention Center, attendees stopped to photograph the booth, explore new products and speak with the team about energy needs at home and outdoors.

A First Appearance That Gave Visitors a Reason to Stop

At Booth C4753, sandstone textures and warm desert tones gave GEYOTO an American Southwest-inspired setting. The oversized "YO!" installation at the front added a playful welcome, expressing the brand's lively, approachable personality and giving visitors another reason to stop and take a photo.

Content creators, including @911Reviews and @Minute Man Solar, visited the booth to explore and record the new lineup. Alongside invited creators, other attendees passing through the exhibition also stopped to film and photograph the products, drawn to their distinctive designs. Attendees shared their own photos and posts on social media, carrying their impressions of GEYOTO beyond the exhibition floor.

This interest also led to conversations with distributors, retailers and solar industry professionals about product applications and potential North American partnerships, giving the team opportunities to continue discussions beyond the show.

Product Stories That Went Beyond the Specification Sheet

The N1500X demonstration setup was one of the booth's focal points, inviting visitors to look more closely at the model's outdoor-oriented protective design. With an IP65 rating for dust and water resistance, the portable power station gave the team a concrete starting point for discussing outdoor use, product protection and practical operating needs.

Another memorable exhibit was an N1000 previously used by participants at Burning Man and featuring original artwork painted by several artists. Brought from the desert to RE+, the unit paired a real-world use story with a distinctive visual identity, showing how a power station can support outdoor experiences while becoming part of the memories people bring home.

New Products Built Around Practical Details

GEYOTO's next-generation N300L, N1000L and N2000L portable power stations put a familiar travel inconvenience in focus: managing charging cables. Each model features an integrated retractable cable that delivers up to 140W of fast charging to compatible devices, bringing cable storage and device charging together in one design.

On the solar side, the S200 Rigid and S400 Rigid panels use heterojunction (HJT) cell technology and support bifacial generation, with a bifaciality rating of up to 95%. Reinforced aluminum frames are designed to support long-term outdoor use. The modular Y3000 and Y6000 balcony energy storage systems added another application to the display, with battery expansion options for different energy needs.

Small Gestures Made Energy Useful on the Show Floor

Beyond the displays, the team offered hats, charging adapters and cables, along with access to charging at the booth. For visitors who needed to recharge a device during the show, GEYOTO's products became part of their day rather than simply something to look at.

For some visitors, interest went beyond filming and conversation. Several visitors asked to buy the equipment directly and take it home, and nearly all of the spare units available at the booth were purchased on site. The response gave GEYOTO a tangible sign of product appeal during its first RE+ appearance.

As RE+ 2026 came to a close, GEYOTO left with new industry contacts, content from creators and attendees, and direct feedback from people who had seen and used its products. From a charging cable offered when a visitor needed one to a power station taken home after the show, the debut connected GEYOTO's products with real people and immediate needs, giving its "Go-to Energy" philosophy a practical expression.

GEYOTO develops portable power stations, solar panels and modular balcony energy storage systems, focusing on reliable energy solutions designed around everyday needs.
