Granite Ridge: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Its operator partnerships secure Permian drilling locations for under $2.5 million each, a cost edge that shapes how durable the inventory growth really is.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 6, 2026, Granite Ridge (GRNT +4.25%) reported that cash flow before working capital changes covered its drilling and completion capital for the quarter. Management expects adjusted free cash flow, defined as operating cash flow before working capital changes minus development capital expenditures, to fund future dividends and capital allocation.

Granite Ridge expands its inventory by partnering with private operators who source acreage through direct leasing rather than public auctions. This platform consistently secures Permian Basin drilling locations for less than $2.5 million per net location.

Natural gas pricing at the Waha hub, a West Texas regional market center, recovered from record low levels as new pipeline capacity opened. That recovery tripled realized natural gas prices sequentially and expanded total quarterly cash flow.

Granite Ridge established a clear framework for spending adjusted free cash flow as development capital spending catches up with cash generation. The Board authorized a $35 million share repurchase program while sustaining the company's quarterly dividend.

Production growth and higher oil weighting mark an operational turning point for the business. Granite Ridge maintains nonoperated positions across six basins with return targets above 25% at current commodity strip prices. Management is watching operational execution, cost inflation, and oil mix targets.

Management expects cash generation to strengthen further as production expands and legacy commodity hedges expire.
