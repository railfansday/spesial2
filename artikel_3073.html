Gibraltar Industries, Inc. (ROCK -25.72%) reported third quarter results that included a significant expansion of the Residential segment through the integration of OmniMax International, alongside a leadership transition and a strategic divestiture. Management reported that Chief Financial Officer Joe Lovechio will depart later this month, with Jeff Watorek appointed as Interim Chief Financial Officer. The company reached an agreement to sell its Infrastructure business for $208 million to reduce debt, resulting in the segment's reclassification as a discontinued operation. Financial performance was impacted by a sharp decline in the U.S. roofing market and commodity cost inflation, leading the company to lower its full-year 2026 guidance for revenue, adjusted EBITDA, and adjusted EPS.
