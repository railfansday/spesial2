Why Amentum Holdings Stock Surged This Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A strong financial report gave investors a lot to be thankful for.

By Scott Levine – Nov 27, 2026 at 12:30PM EST

A first-ever $500 million share buyback and a fiscal fourth-quarter earnings beat sent Amentum Holdings (AMTM -0.60%) soaring 22.2% this week, according to data provided by S&P Global Market Intelligence.

Amentum Holdings stock changed hands at $24.86 as of the close on Wednesday, Nov. 25, up 22.2% from Friday, Nov. 20's close through Wednesday, Nov. 25's close. Across those five sessions, the S&P 500 (^GSPC +1.60%) gained 3.9%, and the Nasdaq-100 was up 4.1%.

On Monday, Nov. 23, the government services provider reported fiscal fourth-quarter GAAP diluted earnings per share of $0.71, topping the analyst consensus of $0.66, while revenue of $3.62 billion fell short of the consensus estimate of $3.70 billion. Investors looked past the top-line shortfall to focus on cash flow. Full-year free cash flow reached $583 million, prompting the board to authorize a $500 million share repurchase program, equal to nearly 8% of the company's $6.1 billion market capitalization.

Amentum Holdings provides engineering, project management, and digital modernization services to government agencies and defense partners who rely on it for complex national security operations. The business depends on federal procurement cycles, having recently landed a $974 million 10-year contract with NASA Langley Research Center and a $375 million 6-year contract with the U.S. Army. These new awards expanded the total contract backlog to $48.3 billion, providing management with multi-year revenue visibility.

Amentum Holdings' trailing price-to-earnings (P/E) ratio of 24.9 sits in the middle of a wide peer group range, with Curtiss-Wright (CW +1.49%) at 30.9 and Huntington Ingalls Industries (HII +2.20%) at 15.4, so the valuation on its own does not say whether the stock is cheap or expensive. This week's buyback authorization and the expansion of the $48.3 billion backlog shift the investment thesis from top-line growth dependence to durable cash generation and margin execution.

Management set fiscal 2027 revenue guidance at $13.8 billion to $14.1 billion and adjusted diluted earnings per share at $2.50 to $2.70. Investors will watch the next earnings report in early February 2027 to verify whether new contract ramps can overcome historical program completion rates.

With Amentum reporting a strong end to fiscal 2026, it's unsurprising that investors are wondering if now's the time to load up on the defense stock. The company's $500 million stock buyback plan clearly demonstrates that management is keen on rewarding shareholders. Moreover, with the company projecting free cash flow of at least $600 million in fiscal 2027, it's possible that management will reward shareholders even further.

While Amentum stock seems reasonably valued in comparison to its peers, it's worth noting that shares are trading at only about 9.4 times forward earnings. At this point, Amentum stock is quite attractive, and investors would be well served to consider adding it to their buy lists.
