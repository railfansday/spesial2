Cerebras Soars on Sales; Cisco Down on Expectations Miss | Stock Movers

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

On this episode of Stock Movers: - Shares of Cerebras Systems (CBRS) soared ahead of the US market open after the chipmaker gave a sales guidance well above estimates, while unveiling new deals with Oracle and Microsoft. A key investor concern is a rollout delay in the firm’s contract with OpenAI. - Shares of Cisco Systems (CSCO) declined in the early session after the networking gear maker reported a 21% rise in product orders in fiscal 1Q, missing expectations and marking a slowdown from the previous period. - Shares of Applied Materials (AMAT) inched higher after the company reported fourth-quarter results that beat expectations and gave an outlook for adjusted first-quarter earnings that is stronger than expected. (Source: Bloomberg)
