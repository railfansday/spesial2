Ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse said cryptocurrencies such as XRP and Bitcoin could attract more interest in countries facing high inflation. His November 19 Bloomberg interview highlighted how falling currency values could push people toward alternative assets. Turkey recorded 29.77% annual inflation in October 2026, while XRP trading against the Turkish lira reached approximately $12 million across Binance, BtcTurk, and Paribu in 24 hours. However, XRP demand remains uneven. Argentina and Nigeria still favor Bitcoin and dollar-pegged stablecoins. Meanwhile, South Korea recorded $163 million in daily XRP trading against the won on Upbit.
