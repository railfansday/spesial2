UFC Fight Night 293 broadcast team set: Four UFC Hall of Famers at work

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The octagon returns to the Meta APEX in Las Vegas on Saturday with UFC Fight Night 293.

As always, some marquee on-air talent will be there in "Sin City" for the event to help guide viewers through the experience.

Details of who will be working as commentators and analysts for the show have been acquired by MMA Junkie through a person with knowledge of the plans – and you can see the scheduled broadcast team below.

The entire UFC Fight Night 293 card streams on Paramount+ in the U.S.

The main card lineup is scheduled to begin at 8 p.m. ET following prelims slated to start at 5 p.m. ET.

Watch UFC Fight Night 293 with Paramount+

XX serves as desk anchor and host at UFC Fight Night 293.

UFC Hall of Famers Chris Weidman and Rashad Evans, as well as "The Ultimate Fighter 15" winner Michael Chiesa fill the desk analyst role throughout the event and on the post-fight show.

UFC correspondent McKenzie Pavacich will conduct pre- and post-fight interviews backstage with some of the athletes on the UFC Fight Night 293 card, as well as report additional real-time updates for the event.

Legendary cage announcer Bruce Buffer will introduce the fighters and read official results at UFC Fight Night 293.

Brendan Fitzgerald will serve as the leading man on the mic from cageside at UFC Fight Night 293.

Fitzgerald will command play-by-play alongside two color commentators: UFC Hall of Famers Daniel Cormier and Dominick Cruz.
