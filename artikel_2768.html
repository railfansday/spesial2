Why UT and Baylor hope Sunday's game in San Antonio won't be the last one in the Alamo City

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Just a few months ago, Elijah Williams was another face in the crowd at a Spurs playoff game. The TMI senior was at Frost Bank Center with his family as the Spurs eventually worked their way into the NBA Finals.

Williams was back at the Spurs' home arena on Sunday afternoon, but this time he was there for his own game. The Baylor freshman earned the start in his hometown as the Bears took on the No. 7 Texas Longhorns, putting up six points in 16 minutes in a 75-72 loss that came down to the wire.

"He was really excited," Baylor coach Scott Drew said of Williams' energy heading back to San Antonio. "It was great seeing his excitement."

Williams, the son of former NBA Coach of the Year Monty Williams, played two years for his father at TMI and is a two-time Express-News Boys Basketball Player of the Year finalist.

"Each and every game, he gets more comfortable," Baylor coach Scott Drew said. "He's a really talented player, and these freshmen having opportunities to play games like this early on will really help them come conference time."

Despite the loss, Drew was clear he'd like Baylor to play more games in San Antonio to engage its fanbase in the city.

Sunday's game was Texas' first game in San Antonio since 2003, when the Longhorns won in the Elite Eight round of the NCAA Tournament at the Alamodome to advance into the Final Four. Baylor last played in San Antonio in 2014 in the first and second rounds of that year's NCAA Tournament.

"We have a lot of fans, both of us [Baylor and Texas] down here, and it's hard for them to make weekday games in Waco and Austin," Drew said.

Drew added new Baylor assistant coach and former player Jonathan Tchamwa Tchatchoua, who spent the last two years with the Spurs as a basketball operations video assistant, had to show the team how to get inside the gate Sunday. Drew also gave a shoutout to former Spurs player Jeremy Sochan, who spent one season with the Bears.

Texas coach Sean Miller hopes playing in San Antonio will strengthen the existing relationship between the Longhorns and the Spurs, who play regular season games at the Moody Center every year as part of their annual I-35 Series.

"My hope is that we can start to look at this as a fixture, and our fans in the San Antonio community can count on us coming here in the month of November and December and playing a marquee game," Miller said. "A game like tonight's against Baylor."

Miller also cited the short driving distance between Austin and San Antonio as a reason why the Longhorns should be playing at least one game in the city every year.

"There's nothing better than to be able to be home in an hour and a half," Miller said.

Texas sophomore guard Isaiah Johnson, who scored a game-high of 26 points, was thrilled about the opportunity to play in another NBA arena. The Longhorns played St. John's earlier this month in an exhibition game at Madison Square Garden.

"It's something you always dream about," Johnson said. "Being able to be in the Spurs arena is pretty dope, especially because they were just in the (NBA) Finals."

Young basketball fans hold signs as they watch the game in the first half as Texas takes on Baylor in the San Antonio Hoops Showdown at the Frost Bank Center in San Antonio, Nov. 15, 2026. (Sara Diggins/Austin American-Statesman)

Regardless of Sunday's result, Drew and Miller are both hoping the Alamo City game between the two former Big 12 rivals will not be a one-time thing and that they can continue to play quality games in San Antonio.

"It just makes a lot of sense," Miller said. "And there's a lot of teams in this country that would love to come and play in this arena in our state."
