Steve Sarkisian dials up game-winning TD pass to Raleek Brown

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

In high-profile calls on the goal line over the last several seasons, Steve Sarkisian has come up short in tenure-defining moments.

On Saturday in Baton Rouge, facing off against longtime friend and former colleague Lane Kiffin, Sarkisian faced a season-defining moment hoping to make a better call than he has in the past with six seconds remaining and Texas trailing 21-16 at the LSU 4-yard line.

With no timeouts remaining, the Longhorns had no more than two plays remaining.

After LSU took away some of the play calls that Sarkisian went back to over the previous two games, a condensed formation created leverage to the field with return motion from running back Raleek Brown creating a clear pre-snap picture for Arch Manning.

Trying to match up with Brown in coverage, LSU linebacker Davohn Keys was slow to respond to the return motion, had to take a wide angle around the field corner, and got torched, allowing Manning to hit a one-step drop and drop an easy pass into Brown's chest for the go-ahead, four-yard touchdown with four seconds remaining.

The Tigers were able to take away Brown in the passing game before the final offensive play for the Horns — Brown only had three catches for four yards on his first four targets, but pitting his speed to the pylon against a slower linebacker who had to navigate traffic ultimately represented one of Sarkisian's best goal-line play calls in his nearly six years leading the Longhorns.

Manning ran to embrace the head coach who trusted him in his lowest moment as an athlete, but it wasn't a moment to celebrate for the Texas head coach. Up 22-21, Sarkisian had to call a kneel down instead of attempting a point after the Tigers could return for a go-ahead score, and the Longhorns secured the victory on the ensuing kickoff.

Reflecting on the play, Brown knew it was won before the snap when a defender didn't follow him in motion.

"I was just real happy," Brown said. "Whatever it takes to win."

For Texas, that's required effective play calls from Sarkisian on the goal line in defining moments. On Saturday, he got it right with one of the single best decisions in his Longhorns tenure.
