Lewis Hamilton Vows to Make Formula 1 ‘Stronger, Fairer and More Open' in Powerful Message to Young Fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Lewis Hamilton has called on F1 to create more opportunities for young people from underrepresented backgrounds, insisting that F1 must turn its global influence into meaningful career pathways.

The seven-time F1 champion made the appeal on November 16, as his charity, Mission 44, marked its fifth anniversary. The occasion also saw the release of a landmark review titled "Igniting Inclusion", which outlines recommendations aimed at making the sport more accessible and inclusive for the next generation.

"To the young people reading this: the sport wants you," Hamilton said. "Not as a favour, but because it needs your talent to keep winning, and because you are the fans who will carry it forward."

"The work starts now, and I intend to be part of it until the sport is stronger, fairer and more open than we found it."

The Ferrari driver stressed that inspiring young fans is not enough if they are not given the opportunities to pursue careers within motorsport. He urged F1 to invest $66 million in new funding over the next five years to help young talents to enter the sport.

Addressing the next generation directly, Hamilton reaffirmed his commitment to pushing for meaningful change within the sport.

Lewis Hamilton believes F1's inspiration is nothing without the opportunity. (Source: MEGA)

"Formula 1 is inspiring millions of young people around the world. But inspiration means nothing without opportunity. We can't tell young people to dream big and then not open the doors when they try to make those dreams a reality," the Briton continued.

Hamilton also directed a message towards team principals, chief executives, and senior leaders at F1 and the FIA, urging them to turn the recommendations outlined in the "Igniting Inclusion" report into action.

"To every team principal, every chief executive and the leaders of Formula 1 and the FIA: let's make the three recommendations in this report happen… None of it is beyond a sport that already knows how to measure, improve and be ambitious," Hamilton said.

For Hamilton, the objective goes beyond encouraging more young people to follow F1. The 41-year-old wants them to see a future for themselves within the sport, whether as engineers, mechanics, strategists, designers, or in other professional roles.

Turning away from his social initiatives and looking at his Formula 1 season, the Ferrari driver is heading into the 2026 Las Vegas GP looking to end the final three rounds of the season on a high note. He has had a much better season in 2026 with Ferrari compared to last year, securing his first victory for the team at the Spanish Grand Prix and a record 105th pole in Mexico.

Lewis Hamilton will aim to secure his second win of the season in Las Vegas. (Source: MEGA)

Hamilton is going into the final triple-header sitting fourth in the drivers' standings. The Briton is one point behind George Russell in third and 18 behind Max Verstappen in second. He will hope to at least come out of the Las Vegas GP in third place.

However, the 44-year-old has a mixed but encouraging record in Las Vegas. He finished seventh in the inaugural 2023 race before producing an impressive recovery drive from 10th on the grid to second in 2024. Last year, despite qualifying 19th in a rain-affected session, he recovered to finish eighth.

It will be interesting to see how Hamilton fares in Las Vegas this weekend in his fight for second place in the championship. Watch the Briton in action on Apple TV (US) and Sky Sports F1 (UK).
