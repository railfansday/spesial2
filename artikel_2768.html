Bills vs Chiefs live updates, TV channel, start time, where to watch NFL Thanksgiving game

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Just when you thought Thanksgiving couldn't get any better, the NFL saved a terrific matchup to wrap up the holiday.

Forget dessert for a minute because the Buffalo Bills and Josh Allen are ready to serve up one more helping of football as they welcome Patrick Mahomes and the Kansas City Chiefs for a Thanksgiving night showdown.

With Kansas City at 8-2 and Buffalo at 9-1, they are set to be the main course on Thursday night, closing out what should be a fantastic day of holiday football with an AFC heavyweight clash.

By now, the history between Allen and Mahomes needs little introduction. This will be the 11th meeting between the two superstar quarterbacks, tying them for the third-most matchups between starting quarterbacks in non-divisional games since 1950.

Allen has largely had the upper hand in the regular season, with Buffalo winning five of the six meetings since 2020. However, Mahomes and the Chiefs have delivered the knockout punch when it matters most, winning all four postseason matchups during that same stretch. Needless to say, there's a little history here.

Of course, there's another opponent both teams will have to contend with: Mother Nature. Thanksgiving in Buffalo isn't exactly a tropical getaway, and conditions at the Bills' new stadium could play a significant role in how this one unfolds. Wind and potentially wet conditions could make life difficult for both offenses, adding another wrinkle to an already fascinating matchup.

With two of the NFL's best and plenty of postseason implications, this is about as good as Thanksgiving night gets. Buckle up.

The Week 12 Thanksgiving night matchup between the Bills and Chiefs will air on NBC at 8:20 p.m. ET.
