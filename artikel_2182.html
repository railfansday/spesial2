Jamie McGrath insists Hibs' win over Hearts 'reward' for fans after slow start

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Jamie McGrath believes Hibernian's win over Hearts in the semi-final of the Premier Sports Cup was "reward" for fans who have supported through thick and thin this season.

The Easter Road outfit saw off their Edinburgh rivals in the derby clash at Hampden on Sunday to seal their spot in the cup final against Rangers to be played on December 13.

Their form in the Scottish Premiership has been poor by comparison and Marink Reedijk's side are languishing at the foot of the Premiership table. But for McGrath that is somewhat of a false position.

Hibs have been unfortunate not to take more points this season and secure more victories, the midfielder says, as they have struggled to turn performances into results.

So he hopes the derby victory can catapult his side back to form and help push them up the division and give their fans more to shout about.

"Yeah it's been a frustrating start to the season obviously, we knew we had to just keep working," he said. "Today I think we got our reward.

"We've been unlucky in the past few weeks not to get a few wins, but better saving it for today, I suppose.

"We've had that belief (we could change the narrative) but for whatever reason the last few weeks we haven't got the results we wanted or that perhaps performances merited.

"I think first-half today we showed bits and pieces how good we can be on and off the ball so it's something we can work towards, keep building towards and working hard and now we can reward the travelling fans with a final."

Asked to talk reporter Connie McLaughlin through his winning goal after Martin Boyle had levelled in the aftermath of Claudio Braga's early Hearts opener, McGrath instead took time to applaud man of the match Joe Newell.

"Set-piece, Joe does brilliantly and I think he was outstanding today. He kept the ball alive and was unlucky not to score himself. I saw the ball coming to me so it was a nice one," he concluded.
