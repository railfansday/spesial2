West Brom begin talks over new contract for £1.5m star

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

West Brom named George Campbell as their new club captain in the summer following a stellar debut campaign from the American defender. Signed for £1.5m from CF Montreal in 2025, he has emerged as a key player for WBA.

Now, the Baggies are looking to reward Campbell’s success with a new contract. Reporter Mike McGrath says on X that the club are in talks over a fresh deal for the 25-year-old, despite the fact he is already tied down until 2029 as it stands.

Prior to the summer transfer window, reports stated that Campbell had interest from Scottish giants Celtic, amongst other clubs. A new contract would further secure the American international’s future at The Hawthorns in case admiration from elsewhere grows.
