UEFA Issue Statement Over Man Utd’s Disallowed Winning Goal vs Sporting

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

UEFA have explained why Manchester United were controversially denied a winning goal in their Champions League clash against Sporting CP on Wednesday evening.

The Red Devils were held by the Portuguese giants at the Jose Alvalade Stadium, meaning Mauricio Pochettino remains winless since succeeding Michael Carrick in the Old Trafford hot seat. In a game packed full of contentious incidents, Sporting took the lead after 31 minutes when Luis Suarez dispatched a penalty 76 seconds after failing to convert from 12 yards.

United hit back with two quick-fire second-half goals from Bruno Fernandes. First, the playmaker scored his own spot-kick before he netted a bizarre second, which was aided by the ball hitting a divot in the pitch.

Sporting responded with ten minutes left to play, once again through Suarez. The Colombian centre-forward pounced on a calamitous error from Senne Lammens to secure his side a point.

Pochettino's team's disallowed late winner condemned them to another disappointing result, leaving the Premier League outfit 19th in the Champions League table.

In the 82nd minute, with the game finally poised at 2-2, United thought they'd found a winner when Sporting keeper Kaique appeared to push the ball backwards into his own net from a Fernandes corner.

The United players had crowded Kaique in the process, and there was subsequently a lengthy VAR check. Referee Szymon Marciniak was sent to the monitor to make a decision, and the verdict he came to wasn't what United fans were hoping for.

Explaining the decision, per the Independent, UEFA said that Patrick Dorgu "committed a foul on the Sporting CP goalkeeper before the ball entered the goal."

Dorgu was the one who ultimately made contact with Kaique, although Lisandro Martinez and Leny Yoro were also in close proximity to the shot-stopper, and this group crowding almost certainly contributed to the goal being ruled out.

That said, the consensus seems to be that the decision was harsh, with many feeling that Dorgu's contact came after Kaique had pushed the ball toward his own goal.

Former Manchester City defender Nedum Onuoha told BBC Sport: "For the United goal that has just been ruled out, the keeper has pushed it into his own net before Dorgu has touched him."

This has to be one of the strangest Champions League goals ever.

Meanwhile, ex-United winger Ashley Young was perplexed by how the incident even went to VAR.

"The keeper comes out and flaps at it before he is even touched," Young said on BBC Radio 5 Live.

"How that has gone to VAR is beyond me. If a referee is on the pitch and being sent to the screen, if he's given the goal, then stand by your decision."

United will have the opportunity to avenge this result and finally secure a victory under their new manager when they entertain Brentford at Old Trafford on Saturday afternoon.
