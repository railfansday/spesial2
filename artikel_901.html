SZA's Vans Collab Has These Viral Styles Still in Stock

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The VANSZA collaboration offers unisex utilitarian footwear, apparel and accessories curated by the R&B artist. Inspired by the Gold Coast's natural beauty, shop the celeb drop worth bugging out over.

SZA has joined forces with storied skate brand Vans as its first-ever artistic director, and their first VANSZA collection is officially here.

The capsule collection marries the artist’s earthy personal style with Vans’ love for the great outdoors, bringing together limited-edition clothing, accessories, and two pairs of reimagined Vans classic sneakers that are surprisingly still in stock, for now: the Knu Skool Mule and the Authentic Kickdown, now SZA-fied.

The workwear-inspired capsule features nature-loving details—think camo prints, motifs of fish and insects—that fit right into SZA’s world, as well as Vans heritage. Standout ready-to-wear pieces include a crewneck fleece sweatshirt and sweatpants set innovatively printed to look like a worn-in work jacket and trouser—all the cozyness of a hoodie, but none of the labor.

This black suede mule reimagines the classic Knu Skool shape with a backless silhouette that's made to be easy for on-the-go moments. It features a sculpted heel and custom artwork on the footbed, making these a truly special limited-edition gift idea for yourself or the SZA-lover you know.

Footwear highlights include SZA’s fresh take on two classic Vans styles. The Knu Skool Mule is a slip-on style that reimagines Vans’ ’90s-inspired Knu Skool with an easy-on-and-off shape and sculpted heel designed to help keep the foot in place, so you won’t walk out of them. Second, the Authentic Kickdown takes a different route: distressed canvas is covered with fish-print artwork—by artist Sage Adams—and features a collapsible heel to easily shift into a mule-style when on-the-go.
