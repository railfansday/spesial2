Antonelli hit with grid penalty for Las Vegas GP

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

New Formula 1 world champion Kimi Antonelli will serve a five-place grid penalty for the Las Vegas Grand Prix; watch the Las Vegas GP on Sky Sports F1, with qualifying at 4am on Saturday and the race at 4am on Sunday

Kimi Antonelli has received a five-place grid penalty for the Las Vegas Grand Prix after further exceeding his engine allocation for the season.

New Formula 1 world champion Antonelli took his sixth internal combustion engine (ICE) of the season during Thursday's opening practice sessions in Las Vegas, breaching the limit of four across the year for the second time.

Given the penalty for a second breach is five rather than 10 grid places, the Mercedes driver will start no higher than sixth in the race, which begins on Sunday morning at 4am UK time (build-up from 2.30am), live on Sky Sports F1.

Antonelli started from the back of the grid at the Italian Grand Prix for exceeding the limit on several power unit elements, including his ICE, but remarkably won his home race in Monza.

Mercedes have also fitted Antonelli with an upgraded turbocharger that they have brought to the event, but the Italian is not incurring a penalty for that as it is within his allowance of four for the season.

In June, Mercedes were granted one chance to upgrade their power unit this season after being found to be between two and four per cent behind the best-performing internal combustion engine - which was unexpectedly judged to be Red Bull's - by the FIA in the year's first ADUO (Additional Development and Upgrade Opportunities) assessment window.

That ADUO upgrade, which Mercedes have focused on their power unit's turbo, is being used by seven of the eight Mercedes-powered cars on the grid, with Antonelli's team-mate George Russell the exception.

Russell has already used five turbos, one more than is permitted, due to unreliability earlier in the year, and a sixth would trigger a further five-place grid penalty.

The Briton, who dropped five places on the Sao Paulo Grand Prix grid due to an engine change, is in a five-way battle to finish runner-up to Antonelli in the standings with three races to go.

McLaren's Oscar Piastri will serve a 10-place grid penalty as he has exceeded his ICE limit for the first time this season.

Williams' Carlos Sainz also has a 10-place grid penalty for exceeding the turbocharger limit, and Aston Martin's Lance Stroll has the same penalty for a new energy store and control electronics.

Oliver Bearman, who says he is "disappointed" to not have a Haas contract signed for 2027, has changed several power unit elements and will start at the back of the grid.

The long straights in Las Vegas means overtaking is relatively easy, so, in theory, it is a good track to take a grid penalty.

Russell topped a rain-affected second practice on Thursday night in Las Vegas after the weather had prevented any lap times from being set in the opening session.

The opening day of track action provided few clues of how the remainder of the weekend is likely to play out, with both qualifying and the race expected to take place in dry conditions.
