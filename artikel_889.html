Eagles sink Cowboys with historic NFL Thanksgiving comeback

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Joe Milton throws three touchdown passes while DeVonta Smith records 200 receiving yards as the Philadelphia Eagles deliver the biggest comeback in NFL Thanksgiving Day history to shock the Dallas Cowboys in a 39-38 victory

Jake Elliott kicked a 59-yard field goal with two seconds left as the Philadelphia Eagles produced a staggering 21-point comeback to beat the Dallas Cowboys 39-38 in a Thanksgiving epic on Thursday.

"This is absurd!" said Tom Brady on commentary. Absurd it was.

Dallas had led by as many as 21 points in the third quarter only to see ever-reliable kicker Brandon Aubrey miss a 40-yard field goal before the Eagles pulled to within two, down 38-36 with two minutes remaining.

Dak Prescott was then sacked by Ty Johnson on fourth-and-one with 55 seconds left before Markquese Bell gifted the Eagles a first down with a pass interference penalty on third-and-one on the ensuing possession.

It would eventually tee up Elliott's crowning moment as he kicked the Eagles to the largest Thanksgiving Day comeback in NFL history.

Joe Milton dazzled at quarterback for the Eagles as he finished 21 of 31 for 331 yards and three touchdowns, with one interception, while rushing for a 15-yard score in what became a 29-point fourth-quarter.

His night featured a blockbuster 50-yard heave to underline his arm strength for a touchdown strike to DeVonta Smith, who led the Eagles with 200 receiving yards and two scores.

Prescott was 19 of 28 for 291 yards and two touchdowns alongside a valiant, diving rushing touchdown, while Javonte Williams delivered a hat-trick of scores that had inspired the Cowboys to a 28-10 first-half lead.

Victory follows up Philly's win last weekend over the Pittsburgh Steelers in lifting the Eagles to 4-7, while the Cowboys drop to 5-7 to lose ground on the division-leading New York Giants.

Milton's arm strength stole the show on one of the NFL calendar's most prized stages as he continued to fill in for the absent Jalen Hurts.

The Eagles' job was made harder by the loss of star running back Saquon Barkley to a stinger in the first quarter, during which the Cowboys had threatened to put the game out of reach.

Instead, Philadelphia would conjure a record Thanksgiving turnaround to prompt incredible scenes that included a fired-up head coach Nick Sirianni celebrating in front of Cowboys fans.

The Cowboys led by 14 points in the fourth quarter before Ty Robinson blocked a punt from Bryan Anger, with Kenny Moore II collecting the loose ball at the three-yard line and racing in for a touchdown.

Dallas hit back through Prescott's bravery as their quarterback powered over Moore on a bruising 14-yard house call to open up a two-touchdown lead once more. But it wasn't over.

Britain Covey came up with a 55-yard return on the ensuing kickoff to put the Eagles in strong field position, and it was Milton who this time capped the drive with a 15-yard run to the end zone.

Philly tried and failed a two-point attempt, before seeing Aubrey miss a field goal that would have given Dallas an 11-point cushion.

The Eagles rallied again as Milton connected with Makai Lemon for a 24-yard touchdown to reduce the deficit to 38-36, though again they were unable to convert the two points.

Cowboys head coach Brian Schottenheimer were seeking to close out the game when he elected to go for it on on fourth-and-one in the final minute as opposed to punting. Prescott was sacked for a loss of nine to give the Eagles a short field from their own 48.

From there Milton navigated his offense into field goal range for Elliott, helped by Bell's costly penalty, one of 11 Dallas were guilty of during the game.

Williams opened the scoring for the Cowboys with a one-yard touchdown on the opening drive of the game, before KaVontae Turpin raced away for a 31-yard score to make it 14-0 by the end of the first quarter.

Williams burst home for a 12-yard touchdown with five minutes left in the first half, before his 12-yard catch-and-run made it 28-10 at the break.

Having connected with Smith for 50-yards in the second quarter, Milton returned to his star receiver with a 45-yard touchdown strike to make it 31-17 at the end of the third quarter.
