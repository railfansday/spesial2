Arsenal booed after another home WSL draw as Man Utd beat Blues

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Arsenal drew their sixth successive home WSL game against West Ham; Man Utd left it late to beat Birmingham as their improvement continues; Charlton earned their first point of the season against Aston Villa; a horror own goal saw Brighton beat Everton; Man City also lost at home to Spurs

Arsenal were booed by their fans after they held to a sixth successive draw at home, drawing 0-0 with West Ham in the Women's Super League.

The Hammers avoided defeat to the Gunners for the first time to pile the pressure on Renee Slegers and her side, who now have just 15 points after 11 games and sit in sixth.

Arsenal had 22 shots in total, with 67 per cent possession, but did not create one big chance throughout.

The best chance of the game came through substitute Kyra Cooney-Cross when her shot struck the post following a big deflection.

But, overall, it was another tepid display in front of the home crowd, and those in red did not do enough to finally emerge with three points.

The well-deserved point for West Ham takes them above Liverpool into 12th.

Manchester United left it very late to claim a 2-1 victory over Birmingham.

Geum-Min Lee put struggling Birmingham in front in only the fourth minute and United struggled to create clear-cut chances, while Phallon Tullis-Joyce made a couple of crucial saves.

The visitors finally found the breakthrough 10 minutes from time through Elisabeth Terland, and Simi Awujo broke Birmingham hearts with a header four minutes into added time.
