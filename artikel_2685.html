Aaron Rodgers shows Fernando Mendoza love during scorched earth Steelers rant

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Aaron Rodgers just had one of the most memorable interviews in recent sports history on the "Pat McAfee Show" Wednesday.

One day after news broke that he would not be starting for the Pittsburgh Steelers on Friday, Rodgers went scorched earth on Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy, including (but not limited to) revealing that McCarthy was snoring during team meetings this past week.

Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers gestures at a news conference after an NFL football game against the Philadelphia Eagles in Philadelphia, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. AP Photo/Chris Szagola

The 42-year-old quarterback (who was released by the Steelers shortly after the interview concluded) did not mince words about his former head coach and franchise.

But the interview was not all negative. Rodgers took a moment to show Las Vegas Raiders rookie quarterback Fernando Mendoza some love.

Las Vegas Raiders quarterback Fernando Mendoza (15) speaks during a press conference after an NFL football game in Las Vegas, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. AP Photo/David Becker

Rodgers was talking about the play calling he has seen that he has liked around the league and said, "And I'll tell you: Hey, the kid Mendoza, man. He was running around, making some plays in the scramble."

Rodgers is one of the greatest quarterbacks in NFL history and knows what good play at the position looks like.

Therefore, his positive endorsement of Mendoza's performance speaks volumes, even if it has not amounted to a win for Las Vegas.

Las Vegas Raiders quarterback Fernando Mendoza (15) passes against the Seattle Seahawks during an NFL football game in Las Vegas, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. AP Photo/John Locher

Rodgers' future remains uncertain right now, and he might want to call it a career at this point.

If not, Mendoza and the Raiders could use a veteran quarterback to help mentor the rookie. Perhaps they will give Rodgers a ring.
