Colts sign CB to roster and former WR draft pick to practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Indianapolis Colts announced two more roster moves on Monday.

After signing TE Harrison Bryant to the practice squad earlier in the day, the Colts also announced that CB Johnathan Edwards was signed from the practice squad to the active roster. Free agent WR Samori Toure was also signed to the practice squad.

Edwards will provide needed cornerback depth with Sauce Gardner and Charvarius Ward navigating injuries on a short week. He'll also be able to contribute on special teams.

Edwards has appeared in two games this season as a practice squad elevation. A 2025 UDFA, Edwards played 313 defensive snaps as a rookie, allowing a completion rate of 63% and 14.8 yards per catch with two pass breakups.

Toure was a seventh-round pick by Green Bay in 2022. In his career, he's caught 14 of 30 targets for 163 yards and a score. A number of his targets have come downfield.
