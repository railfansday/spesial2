Tory Horton's heads up play earns him NFC special teams player of the week

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Late in Sunday’s victory over the Raiders, Seahawks receiver Tory Horton made a heads-up play on special teams that helped seal the win.

The league has recognized Horton for his efforts, naming him special teams player of the week.

There was 2:04 remaining in the fourth quarter after Las Vegas narrowed Seattle’s lead to one possession with a 43-yard field goal. The Raiders tried to send the kickoff out of the end zone to preserve the two-minute warning, having just two timeouts left. But Horton leapt up and tipped the ball into the end zone to prevent a touchback. He then signaled for Rashid Shaheed to pick up the football, as he’d stepped out of bounds, to prevent an illegal touch.

Shaheed returned the ball 22 yards, taking the clock down to 1:59.

While the play was flagged for an illegal bat, Las Vegas declined it. A re-kick would have taken more time off the clock.

Quarterback Drew Lock then sealed the victory with a 15-yard run.

This is Horton’s first career player of the week award.
