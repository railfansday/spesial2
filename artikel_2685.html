Chargers get their first win as the Ravens' disappointing season gets even worse

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

It was lonely at the bottom for the Los Angeles Chargers. Every other team had long ago collected its first win of this NFL season. The Chargers were the last winless team in the league.

There was too much talent on the Chargers' roster to go 0-17. There also should have been too much talent on the Baltimore Ravens to see their playoff hopes take a massive hit with a home loss to an 0-8 team.

The Chargers don't have to worry about the questions about being winless anymore. They played their best overall game of the season and held on to a 23-20 win over the Ravens on Monday night.

The Ravens were trailing by three points just before the two-minute warning when the Chargers made a huge play, as cornerback Tarheeb Still stripped Zay Flowers of the ball and Los Angeles recovered. The Chargers ran out most of the clock after that and had a long-awaited win.

The Ravens fell to 4-6 after their fifth straight defeat and the faint hope that they could stay alive with new quarterback Jacoby Brissett took a massive hit. Brissett, making his first start for Baltimore after a pre-deadline trade with the Arizona Cardinals, had 340 yards passing as Baltimore's run game stalled and the offense fell on his shoulders. He wasn't the biggest problem for the Ravens. Everything else was. The loss makes it even harder to envision a scenario in which Lamar Jackson can come back late in the season to a Ravens team still in the AFC wild-card race.

The Chargers don't care how it came or against who: They were just happy to get in the win column this season. Finally.

The worst thing the Ravens could have done was given the winless Chargers some hope right away. And that's what happened.

The Ravens started with a three-and-out. After the punt, the Chargers drove 63 yards, with Justin Herbert rushing 22 yards for a touchdown. That early 7-0 lead gave the Chargers some momentum that they have rarely experienced this season.

The defense came alive. In the first quarter the Ravens had three possessions and went three-and-out every time. It wasn't a great way for Brissett to begin his Ravens career. The Chargers' offense looked as good as it has all season and LA led 10-0 after the first quarter.

The Ravens started moving it in the second quarter and Derrick Henry got in for a 2-yard touchdown. That's all Henry got in the first half. He had -1 yard on five carries at halftime. The Chargers wanted to take Henry out of the game and did a good job of it.

The Chargers established early that they were set on putting forth a prideful performance on Monday night, and that carried into the second half.

The Ravens weren't playing well on either side of the ball. With Baltimore trailing and Henry being bottled up, Brissett had to throw it more often than the Ravens presumably wanted. That put a lot of pressure on a quarterback who had less than two weeks to learn the playbook.

The defense has been an underrated problem for the Ravens during their losing streak and it wasn't good Monday. The Chargers have been horrible most of the season on offense but suddenly could run it with Omarion Hampton. Herbert didn't do much but he didn't have to as the Chargers controlled the game on the ground.

Despite being outplayed most of the night, the Ravens were still in it when Henry scored his second touchdown to cut Los Angeles' lead to 23-20. Henry had 10 yards on 10 carries at that point.

Bad teams find ways to lose games, and the Chargers are a bad team. So naturally, their offense started stalling.

The Ravens eventually got in Chargers territory near the 2-minute mark and were closing in on field-goal range on a Flowers reception when Still reached in and ripped the ball out of his hands. The Chargers recovered. It was the biggest play of Los Angeles' season. The Ravens didn't get the ball back until 11 seconds were left and they couldn't pull off a miracle to win it.

The Chargers aren't going to turn around their season. It's too late for that. But getting that first win after eight straight losses had to feel good.
