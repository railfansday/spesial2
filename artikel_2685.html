Victor Wembanyama makes MVP statement in Spurs' comeback win over Lakers

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The roars of Frost Bank Center in the closing moments of the San Antonio Spurs' 99-96 win over the Los Angeles Lakers on Friday, Nov. 27 could've applied to two players on the floor at the time. After all, that's been the biggest debate of this young NBA season -- who between Victor Wembanyama and Luka Dončić is more of the favorite to be the league's Most Valuable Player five months from now?

But there was no mistaking who was the recipient of those chants on Friday. Spurs fans were going crazy for one of their own as Wembanyama capped off a 20-point comeback with a free throw to secure a 99-96 win over the Lakers, a spot in the NBA Cup quarterfinals and early bragging rights over Dončić.

It wasn't a bad night for the Lakers superstar; he finished the night with 31 points, six rebounds and eight assists. Nineteen of those points came in the first half as LA took a 16-point lead into the locker room. But once the teams came back out for the third quarter, it was all San Antonio.

The Lakers were outscored 61-42 by the Spurs over the last two quarters, powered by Wemby's 24 points, 16 rebounds and six stocks and 26 points from Stephon Castle.

"We just said we gotta find our energy, be on our front foot, which we weren't in the first half," Wembanyama said on NBA Nightcap postgame. "... New challenges every time, every season. We handled that one the right way."

The clash of MVP frontrunners lived up to its billing on Friday. So did the game's overall billing as the game of the year so far.

It was the two best teams in the Western Conference, with the two best players, with their supporting casts at full strength after Austin Reaves, Dylan Harper and De'Aaron Fox all returned from injuries. And for the Lakers, it answered a much-debated litmus test in regard to their strength of schedule. It's one thing to get to the top of the West by beating up on the Sacramento Kings and Utah Jazzes of the league, but the Lakers showed they can hang with – and at times look dominant against – the defending Western Conference champions.

This game was winnable for the Lakers, which makes the loss and the 17 turnovers all that much more frustrating. Things don't get any easier from here, however. They'll continue their road trip on Sunday against the No. 3-seeded Memphis Grizzlies before heading straight to Houston for the second night of a back-to-back against the fourth-place Rockets.

The Spurs will take on a slate of Eastern Conference opponents in the Boston Celtics, Milwaukee Brewers and Toronto Raptors before facing the Dallas Mavericks in the NBA Cup quarterfinals on Friday, Dec. 4.

The Lakers rallied at the end, but ran out of possessions as Mitch Johnson smartly had the Spurs foul down the stretch to prevent LA from having a chance to get the three they needed to force overtime in the closing seconds. The Spurs clinch their spot in the NBA Cup quarterfinals and supersede the Lakers for the top spot in the Western Conference.

The battle of MVP frontrunners was as advertised; Victor Wembanyama bounced back from a frustrating first half and finished the night with 25 points, 16 rebounds and six stocks while Luka Dončić scored 31 with six rebounds and eight assists.

The Lakers can't buy a bucket right now. The Spurs have open up an eight-point lead – their largest of the night – midway through the fourth after outscoring LA 13-5 since the end of the third. The Lakers have not made a field goal in almost two minutes; in that span, they've turned the ball over three times.

The Spurs have come alive in the second half. It was a chaotic third quarter with sloppy turnovers on both sides, but it's been San Antonio who had the upper hand, outscoring LA 30-14. They came out the gate on a 9-0 run that snatched the momentum in the early minutes to cut into the Lakers' 16-point halftime lead. Luka Dončić has 25 points but the Lakers as a whole missed 10 of their last 11 shots down the final stretch.

Victor Wembanyama jammed home the putback on a missed free throw from De'Aaron Fox to cut the Laker lead down to five at the 2:05 mark. Fox later hit a floater with 38 seconds left, and a three from Keldon Johnson knotted the game up at 68-all, erasing LA's lead heading into the fourth quarter.

Complete domination by LA in the first half. Luka Dončić has a game-high 19 points and is scoring almost at will to go along with five rebounds and three assists. And while Victor Wembanyama has been limited offensively due to head coach JJ Redick's clear gameplan of keying in on the 7'6 big man, but he still has five rebounds and four stocks (steals and blocks combined into one stat) with eight points.

In other Group C news, the Portland Trail Blazers lead the Golden State Warriors 67-44 at halftime. If both these scores hold, the Lakers will clinch the group and advance to the NBA Cup knockout rounds.

Reaves has struggled early in his first game back, missing each of his three attempts taken in the first quarter. So it's only fitting that his first points in the last five games came on a ridiculously tough shot.

Towards the end of the second quarter, Reaves dribbled out to the wing to avoid a one-on-one with Victor Wembanyama. When Devin Vassell switched onto him, Reaves immediately slashed to his left towards the paint and into the low post, stepped back and let it fly as he faded away with Wembanyama helping back late.

The ball swished through the net to give the Lakers a 19-point lead over the Spurs.

LA has gotten off to an early lead by forcing chaos in the paint, doubling Victor Wembanyama and forcing six Spurs turnovers. Luka Dončić is leading the way with 14 points for the Lakers on 6 of 13 shooting, though he's yet to hit a three in six attempts. Austin Reaves is still searching for his first points in his return to action.
