Lions dominate Patriots in Germany after knocking Drake Maye out of game

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Fans in Germany wanted to see Detroit Lions wideout Amon-Ra St. Brown have a big day. They got that and more during the Lions' 35-10 win over the New England Patriots in Week 10.

St. Brown wasn't the only member of the Lions' offense to show out, though. He was joined by running back Jahmyr Gibbs, who was even more impressive during the victory.

The pair combined for five total touchdowns, with St. Brown scoring twice and Gibbs scoring three times. St. Brown's first score came on a 56-yard reception that drew massive cheers from the crowd.

It was a meaningful score for St. Brown, whose mother was born in Germany. Because of that, the wideout speaks fluent German and holds dual citizenship in the country.

St. Brown finished with 98 receiving yards and two scores on the day.

Gibbs was arguably the bigger story, though. The running back dominated the Patriots. By the end of the first half, Gibbs had 88 rushing yards and 78 receiving yards. He finished the day with 153 rushing yards, 99 receiving yards and three total touchdowns.

Gibbs' third touchdown of the day was the final nail in the coffin for the struggling Patriots (5-5).

On paper, the game wasn't supposed to be this one-sided. But the tone was set early thanks to a pivotal play on the Patriots' first offensive drive. On a crucial third down, Patriots quarterback Drake Maye was sacked by Aidan Hutchinson. The Lions' superstar sustained a knee injury during his celebration and was unable to return to the contest.

While Hutchinson received attention in the moment thanks to the nature of his injury, Maye was also hurt on the play. When the Patriots came back out on offense for their second drive, Maye was replaced by backup Tommy DeVito.

Maye went to the locker room and was eventually ruled out due to the injury.

With DeVito in the game, the Patriots failed to get much going offensively. The team constantly went three-and-out, with just two of the team's next seven drives going more than three plays (excluding a one-play kneel down at halftime). One of those two "long" drives ended with a DeVito interception.

The Patriots' offense showed life just one time. At the end of the third quarter, the team was able to drive down the field thanks to wideout A.J. Brown's first couple catches of the game.

That drive continued in the fourth quarter, and resulted in the Patriots' only touchdown of the contest. It came at a cost, however, as Brown left the game with a hamstring injury. He did not return.

The loss leaves the Patriots with some tough questions to answer heading into their bye. The status of Maye and Brown will be a focus. The same thing can be said about running back Rhamondre Stevenson, who played through a knee injury Sunday. He didn't appear to re-injure himself, but his recovery from the issue will be worth watching.

Of that trio, the Patriots can least afford to lose Maye. Any missed time from the star passer would be devastating for a Patriots team fighting to make the playoffs.

For the Lions (7-2), the win is massive, especially considering the state of the NFC North. With the win, Detroit matches the Chicago Bears at the top of the division.

The victory, however, may have come at a cost. Hutchinson's status will need to be watched moving forward. While a win was necessary, a prolonged injury to Hutchinson could be the difference in the most difficult division in football.
