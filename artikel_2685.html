Dončić dazzles with 43 as Lakers hold off Suns for eighth straight win

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Luka Dončić needed little time to show the Lakers could keep scoring without Austin Reaves. Protecting the lead he helped build proved more difficult.

Dončić scored 43 points, including 25 in the first half, as the Lakers survived a late Phoenix rally for a 131-123 victory Sunday at Mortgage Matchup Center, extending their winning streak to eight games.

Los Angeles improved to 12-2 and completed a four-game road sweep as they improved to 7-1. The Lakers' second-leading scorer was sidelined with a left heel contusion/bone bruise suffered Wednesday against Milwaukee.

Without Reaves' scoring and playmaking, more of the responsibility fell to Dončić. He responded by immediately putting Phoenix's defense on its heels.

Dončić made four of his first five shots, including three 3-pointers, for 14 quick points as the Lakers raced ahead 23-5. Difficult jumpers looked routine, and possessions that Phoenix defended closely still ended with the ball dropping through the net.

The Lakers opened the game with 11 unanswered points. Dončić finished the first quarter with 20, giving L.A. a 33-24 lead and establishing the pace for another prolific afternoon.

His performance came in his first appearance since twisting his right ankle against the Bucks. There was little indication Sunday that the injury would interrupt his dominant start.

Dončić finished with nine assists and three rebounds, making 13 of 28 shots, five 3-pointers and 12 of 14 free throws. He committed just two turnovers while directing an offense missing its other primary creator.

It was his seventh 40-point performance in 12 appearances this season. His ability to combine that scoring volume with opportunities for teammates has continued to give the Lakers a foundation throughout their winning streak.

Sunday, Quentin Grimes provided the most important support.

Starting in Reaves' place, Grimes scored 22 points and made five of six attempts from beyond the arc. His shooting helped the Lakers build their advantage, and his final basket ensured they left Phoenix with another victory.

Walker Kessler added 14 points and 12 rebounds, while Collin Sexton scored 16 off the bench. Matisse Thybulle and Jake LaRavia each contributed 12 points.

The Lakers led 64-54 at halftime before taking control again in the third quarter. Dončić added 12 points in the period, and L.A. scored 40 as its advantage grew to as many as 31.

By the start of the fourth, the Lakers held a 104-80 lead. What followed prevented Dončić from getting the quiet finish he had anticipated.

Phoenix's reserves drove a comeback that exposed another uneven fourth quarter from Los Angeles. Collin Gillespie scored 12 of his 26 points in the final period, and his driving layup cut the Lakers' lead to 126-121 with 1:05 remaining.

Grimes then helped rescue the possession — and the afternoon.

After chasing down an offensive rebound, he relocated and banked in a 3-pointer with roughly 33 seconds left. Dončić closed the scoring with two free throws.

"We're gonna need our defense to win us games," coach JJ Redick said afterward. "This is a trend. Got to be better in the fourth quarter."

The Suns outscored the Lakers 43-27 in the fourth, with Devin Booker watching from the bench as Phoenix's reserves nearly erased the deficit.

That finish complicated an afternoon that otherwise illustrated why the Lakers keep winning. Even without Reaves, they started decisively, received contributions throughout the starting lineup and had Dončić producing another performance few defenses have been able to contain.

For Redick, those stretches are also evidence of the standard his team should maintain.

"I don't want these guys to put a ceiling on themselves," Redick said. "There's no reason to ever play less than that standard."

The Lakers return home to face Charlotte on Tuesday with eight consecutive victories and another reminder that their finishes still need to match their starts.
