Mike McCarthy backed Aaron Rodgers as Steelers QB. He benched him a day later.

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Pittsburgh Steelers coach Mike McCarthy looked almost confused the question had been asked.

A reporter at McCarthy's Monday, Nov. 23 news conference asked the first-year coach if he'd consider playing a quarterback other than Aaron Rodgers, the veteran in the final year of his career.

McCarthy, who also spent 13 seasons with Rodgers in Green Bay, left little doubt about his feelings while declaring Rodgers was the team's QB. Take a look:

Then, in a stunning development late Tuesday, Nov. 24, the Steelers announced quarterback Mason Rudolph will start in Week 12's Black Friday game against the Denver Broncos. Team PR spokesman Burt Lauten gave no reason for the change after McCarthy's backing but said that Rodgers, the team's starter since the start of last season, will be inactive for the matchup.

McCarthy was not scheduled to address reporters again before the Steelers face the Broncos at the time of the announcement about Rodgers.
