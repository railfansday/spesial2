Confirmed: Kylian Mbappe nominated for Champions League Player of the Week after brace vs PSV

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Real Madrid were able to continue their sensational run of form this midweek with a thumping 5-1 win over PSV Eindhoven.

The victory helped Los Blancos solidify their position within the top eight and extend their winning run of 10 matches across all competitions.

Needless to say, Kylian Mbappe was the star of the match, as he scored twice and played a key role in helping Real Madrid completely dominate the second half, after PSV Eindhoven had initially made a great start to the game.

In light of his electric performance against PSV, Mbappe has been nominated as one of the contenders for Champions League Player of the Week.

He is one of four contenders for the accolade, alongside Antonio Nusa of RB Leipzig, Anthony Gordon of FC Barcelona and Cucho Hernandez of Real Betis.

All the four players mentioned have scored a brace this week, with Nusa being a strong contender for leading RB Leipzig's exceptional victory over RC Lens.

Hernandez, too, scored a brace in Real Betis' win over Slovan Bratislava while Gordon scored a brace in Barcelona's thumping 4-1 win over Sabah.

This marks the first time Mbappe has been nominated for the Player of the Week in Champions League this season, even though the Frenchman has been scoring consistently in the tournament, notably racking up six goals so far this season.

Can Mbappe win the Ballon d'Or next year? (Photo by Florencia Tan Jun/Getty Images)

Thanks to his brace against PSV, Mbappe achieved another milestone by overtaking the scoring tally of Ronaldo Nazario in Real Madrid's all-time list.

The French international is already 20th in Real Madrid's list of all-time goalscorers. He is expected to surpass Gareth Bale to reach 18th by the end of the season and possibly even Juanito and Gonzalo Higuain.

In terms of his performances this season, Mbappe has scored 19 goals in 18 matches, which makes him a strong contender for the Ballon d'Or next year, provided Real Madrid can back him up by winning trophies this season.

One thing is for sure – Mbappe's fitness will be critical to Real Madrid's fate in all competitions.
