Houston Rockets vs. Miami Heat game preview

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

I was tempted to just write "This is a schedule loss" and move on. But you expect more out of me, so I'll clarify: the Houston Rockets played last night and then had to fly into Miami. The Miami Heat didn't play yesterday and have been in South Florida since last week and are in the middle of a five-game homestand.

The Heat started 0-4, which understandably led to a lot of hand-wringing. "Is Giannis Antetokounmpo washed?" and "Is Erik Spoelstra washed?" dominated the narrative, but the Heat are 7-2 since and lead the Southeast Division. That includes a perfect 3-0 at home.

I'm not sure how much the recent play has been Giannis and the Heat learning to play together or just a schedule thing. However, their most recent win came against the Detroit Pistons (sans Jalen Duren, but still). So, this is going to be a difficult game for the Rockets, even if the late-night arrival means that the Rockets probably won't be able to enjoy the Miami nightlife (in preparation for Grand Theft Auto VI, presumably).

Also, Houston should get Fred VanVleet back tonight; conversely, they will be without Steven Adams since this is a back-to-back.
