Luminous Holiday Eye Looks That Shine All Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Whether you go for gilded lids, jewel-toned liners, or a vibrant wash of color, you can find your next party-ready eye look at Ulta Beauty.

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

The holiday party circuit requires a shift in your beauty wardrobe—especially when it comes to eyes. Think softly smoked bronze lids, flashes of metallic gold shadow, and jewel-toned eyeliner that makes even your go-to little black dress feel entirely new. Festive gatherings are the perfect opportunity to try something outside your usual makeup routine, whether that means trading black eyeliner for emerald green or adding a touch of gold to an otherwise understated eye. And with the right products, you don't need a makeup artist's skills to pull it off.

From versatile shadow palettes to long-wearing liners, Ulta Beauty's prestige makeup selection offers endless ways to play with color and texture, whether you're dressing for an intimate dinner or going all out for New Year's Eve.

Below, 4 fail-safe strategies to sparkle on your next night out.

Unlike a traditional black smoky eye, which can sometimes feel a little severe, bronze brings warmth and definition while keeping the overall effect more understated. Charlotte Tilbury's Hollywood Instant Look in a Palette takes much of the guesswork out of choosing complementary colors, with three eyeshadows and a selection of complexion powders in one place. To create a soft, bronze smoky eye, sweep the lightest eyeshadow across your lids, blend the deeper shade into the crease, and tap a little shimmer onto the center to catch the light. Finish with the included bronzer and blush to bring some warmth to the rest of your face.

For an even simpler, more minimal approach, the Polite Society Dual Intentions Eyeshadow Duo pairs two complementary shades. Concentrate the darker color along your upper lash line before blending it outward for that slightly undone, smoky finish.

The most sophisticated way to incorporate jewel tones into your holiday eye makeup is to swap your usual black eyeliner or mascara for a rich, saturated color. Emerald green, sapphire blue, and amethyst purple can make even the simplest makeup feel a little more interesting, especially when paired with an otherwise neutral face. If you're hesitant to go too bright, deeper shades like midnight blue offer a less dramatic departure from black.

Whether you're drawing a thin line along your lashes or going for a bold, dramatic wing, Anastasia Beverly Hills DramaLine Longwear Waterproof Gel Eyeliner Pencil glides on as smoothly as a gel eyeliner, but is just as easy to use as a pencil. It comes in colors ranging from metallic purple to emerald green; use it to draw a sharp, colorful wing or keep the pigment close to your lashline for just a hint of something unexpected.

Mascara is another way to try out color without committing to a full eye look. Tarte Tartelette Tubing Mascara comes in statement-making hues that give lashes a little personality of their own. Try pairing a vibrant magenta with neutral eyeshadow to let your lashes take center stage, or match your mascara to your eyeliner for a coordinated, monochromatic effect. The tubing formula resists smudging and flaking throughout the evening, then comes off with warm water when it's time to wash your face.

For an effortless metallic gold eye, focus the color on the center of your lids or the inner corners, where it naturally catches the light. You don't need to cover your entire eyelid in glitter to make an impression; these carefully placed touches of gold can feel just as striking, particularly with an otherwise pared-back makeup look.

For a delicate, gilded finish, start with a gold or champagne shade from the Ulta Beauty Collection Eyeshadow Singles. Use a fluffy brush to diffuse the color across your lids, or press it on with your fingertip for a more intense, saturated effect. The single-pan format is convenient for slipping into your makeup bag should your after-work plans call for a quick change of pace.

To balance the warmth of gold eyeshadow, consider brown mascara instead of your usual black. The Hourglass Unlocked Instant Extensions Mascara Duo includes both Espresso and Black, giving you the option to keep your lashes understated or add a little more contrast. Espresso pairs well with champagne and bronze tones, defining lashes without overpowering the rest of your eye makeup. Whichever color you choose, the tubing formula creates length and separation while helping prevent smudges.

The best holiday eye makeup gift sets offer a mix of versatile neutrals, richer statement colors, and different finishes, so there's room to test out new styles without needing an entire makeup collection. Palettes that combine mattes and metallics are useful, since they can take someone from an understated daytime eye to a more dramatic look for evening.

For someone who enjoys changing up their makeup for different occasions, the Tarte You Know You Love Me Collector's Set offers plenty of options. It includes two mini Amazonian Clay eyeshadow palettes—one with warm golden shades for glamorous precious-metal-inspired eyes and another with cool rosy neutrals for a more subtle, romantic take—plus two mascaras and two lip products to top things off.

If a streamlined makeup routine is your holiday wish, Morphe Sky's the Limit Face & Eye Palette brings together coordinating rose-gold and mauve tones for eyes and cheeks. The mix of matte and shimmery eyeshadows makes it easy to adjust the intensity depending on the occasion, and the fact that the kit comes with blush, cheek balms, and setting powder means you won't have to reach for several other products to complete your makeup.
