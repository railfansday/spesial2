Bilt Rent Day Promotion November 2026: Up To $150 Off Hotel, Up To 100% Gift Card Bonus

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Bilt is the platform known for providing rewards for housing payments. Earlier this year we saw massive changes at Bilt, including an overhaul of the credit card portfolio, plus new ways that housing rewards are offered.

There are many innovative things about Bilt, including that there’s a Bilt Rent Day promotion on the first day of each month. With it now being a new month, I’d like to post a reminder of the November 2026 Bilt Rent Day offer, as it includes both a gift card redemption bonus and a hotel booking promo code. While there’s no transfer bonus to a travel program, I imagine this will still interest some.

There are a couple of aspects to the Bilt Rent Day promotion, including the ability to earn bonus points (which is the same every month), plus at least one additional promotion.

Note that Bilt Rent Day promotions are generally valid from 12:00AM ET until 11:59PM PT, so technically you have around 27 hours to take advantage of them.

Bilt has a portfolio of credit cards, including the no annual fee Bilt Blue Card (learn more), $95 annual fee Bilt Obsidian Card (learn more), and $495 annual fee Bilt Palladium Card (learn more) (Rates & Fees). Personally, I think the Bilt Palladium Card is by far the most lucrative.

On the first day of each month, you earn double those rewards on these cards, and earn up to 1,000 bonus points each month. It could be worth strategically making some purchases on the first day of each month to maximize this as much as possible.

For example, with double points, the Bilt Palladium Card offers 4x points on everyday spending on the first day of the month, so that’s pretty awesome (up to a cap of $500 worth of spending, since that would earn you 1,000 bonus points).

On November 1, 2026 only, Bilt is offering a bonus when members redeem points for gift cards. The bonus you’re eligible for depends on your Bilt elite status, as follows:

This same bonus is available for all 125+ gift cards that Bilt Points can be redeemed for. There’s no option to use Bilt Cash to increase your bonus here.

For what it’s worth, ordinarily Bilt points can be redeemed for 0.7 cents each toward gift card purchases. That means a Blue member would be getting 0.875 cents per point of value, while a Platinum member would be getting 1.4 cents per point of value. For me that’s a hard pass, though I suppose for some Platinum members, it could be an interesting way to “cash out” points, if that’s what you’re looking to do.

For the November 2026 Bilt Rent Day promotion, we’re actually seeing an additional offer as well. Bilt members can unlock up to $150 off a hotel stay booked in the Bilt Travel Portal during Rent Week, so it’s available for bookings between 9AM ET on October 28 and 3AM ET on November 2, 2026.

The catch here is that different members are eligible for different offers, so members have to log into their Bilt account, and they will see a targeted chart delineating different savings of up to $150 for hotel bookings. Here are the key details:

On the first day of each month, Bilt offers a Rent Day promotion. In addition to earning up to double points for purchases, for November 2026, there are two offers members can take advantage of.

One offer is for up to a 100% bonus on gift card redemptions, which can get you at most 1.4 cents per point of value. That’s not terribly exciting, in my opinion, but maybe others will appreciate it. The other offer is for up to $150 off a hotel stay booked through the Bilt Travel Portal. There are different offers there that members will be eligible for, so you’ll want to check in the Bilt app to see what you’re targeted for.
