How China’s Intelligence Operations Target Tech

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Former CIA counterintelligence analyst Peter Mattis tells Bloomberg This Weekend how China’s Ministry of State Security can use business and personal relationships to gather intelligence and potentially influence decision-making in strategically important industries. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Mattis explains why technology executives can become valuable targets even without access to classified information and how AI is expanding the capabilities of Chinese intelligence operations. (Source: Bloomberg)
