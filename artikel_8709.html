Corby Spirit and Wine Limited (CSW.A:CA) Q1 2027 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Corby Spirit and Wine Limited (CSW.A:CA) Q1 2027 Earnings Call November 13, 2026 9:00 AM EST

Florence Tresarrieu - President, CEO & DirectorJuan Alonso - VP, CFO & Director

Florence TresarrieuPresident, CEO & Director

Good morning everyone and thank you for joining us to review Corby Spirit and Wine's First Quarter Fiscal 2027 results. For those of you who may be joining us for the first time, my name is Florence Tresarrieu, President and CEO of Corby. Joining me today is our Chief Financial Officer, Juan Alonso. Corby delivered a solid start to fiscal 2027 with continued growth in RTDs and improved earnings despite a challenging market environment.

Organic revenue grew plus 1%, supported by our RTD portfolio and higher commission income, mitigating a soft domestic spirits market, while reported revenue declined minus 4%, reflecting the sale of the Lamb's Rum brand. Adjusted net earnings grew plus 18%, significantly ahead of revenue, reflecting gross margin improvements on RTD brands and disciplined investment behind our brands with a continued focus on cost.

Supported by a strong cash generation, our balance sheet remains strong, with net debt to adjusted EBITDA of 0.6x or 0.8x if we include the $18.7 million upfront fee payment for the Pernod Ricard representation agreement signed on October 1, 2026. The Board declared a quarterly dividend of $0.25 per share, up plus 9% compared with Q1 last year.

This performance very much reflects the resilience of our portfolio, the quality of our execution across multiple categories, and our continued focus on creating sustainable long-term value for our shareholders. While this is a steady start to the fiscal year, we remain prudent, given continued pressure on the spirits market, an evolving consumer environment, and macro and geopolitical uncertainties.
