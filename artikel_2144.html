Chris Wilder discusses potential permanent swoop for ‘refreshing’ Sheffield United loan star

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Sheffield United have impressed to start the season with Chris Wilder bringing some stability back to the club after the struggles of last term. SUFC currently sit 10th and just two points away from the Championship play-off places.

The Blades had another frustrating transfer window with some ownership issues holding them back, but one addition who has stood out is defender Liam Kitching. Wilder has revealed his desire to sign the defender permanently, speaking to The Star.

“His path was blocked there in the summer, and it was refreshing that Liam wanted to play football.” said Wilder. “Quite easily, he could sit there and be a Premier League footballer.

“Get on the pitch now and again and play the odd minutes. But he wants to play, he wants to compete and he wants to win. Those characteristics go hand-in-hand with what we’re all about.”

Kitching joined the club on loan from Coventry City after he was a key player in their promotion-winning campaign last term. SUFC have had encouragement from Kitching’s side over a permanent move already but the deal remains dependant on backing from the boardroom.

Wilder is keen to keep Kitching at Sheffield United permanently, with the defender’s performances and personality making him an ideal fit for the Blades.

Wilder is a clear fan of his and thinks he is ideal for SUFC highlighting his positive attitude, competitive nature and desire to win. His Yorkshire roots, having been born in Harrogate and previously played for Barnsley, also strengthen his connection to the club.

Kitching arrived with Championship pedigree following Coventry City’s title-winning campaign and has become a regular starter at Bramall Lane. According to FotMob, he has made 10 starts, playing every minute, recording 47 clearances, 16 tackles and 15 interceptions.

He has also won 20 aerial duels, ranking in the 84th percentile among Championship centre-backs. With Wilder valuing his reliability and mentality, United have clear reasons to pursue a permanent deal, although finances remain a consideration.

Sheffield United and Kitching look to be a match made in heaven in the early days of the season. The Blades have looked back to their solid best this term and the Coventry City loanee has been a big part of that.

After a frustrating transfer window in the summer, the Blades board need to back Wilder in the future. A move for Kitching next summer would be a good start.

With the defender already showing willingness to return permanently, it feels like a must-do deal for Sheffield United. A move in January might not be out of the question either.
