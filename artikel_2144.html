'I want beer, I want everything!' Marink Reedijk's Hibs dressing room demand

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Jubilant Hibs boss Marink Reedijk got the Hampden party started as he told his stars to get the beer flowing after downing Hearts.

The Dutchman had failed to win any of his first four games before arriving at Hampden, but tasted the sweetest triumph of his tenure as goals from Martin Boyle and Jamie McGrath fired the Hibees into the Premier Sports Cup Final.

Reedijk got it right on the pitch and then struck the perfect note in the dressing room afterwards. The Hibs boss began with a measured message before urging his players to let loose and celebrate their derby victory over the Jambos.

Read more: Reedijk on two Hibs difference makers who contributed to 'touching' Hampden day

In a clip posted on Hibs' social media channels, Reedijk said: "This is small. It's very small.

"Before the match, you said that you do it for yourselves. You remember the kids you were, and the kids out there. You gave them inspiration today.

"Not only in the first half, when we played them out and really dominated them, but especially in those difficult moments at the end when they put the ball in the box and we survived.

"We stuck together and we fought. Brilliant.

"Now I want music, I want beer, I want everything."
