Elle and Dakota Fanning Share Screen for First Time, Playing Sisters in ‘The Nightingale’ Trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Set during World War II, director Michael Morris' feature adaptation of the Kristin Hannah novel hits theaters next year from Sony.

Elle Fanning and Dakota Fanning play sisters on the big screen for the first time in the trailer for The Nightingale.

Sony Pictures is set to release director Michael Morris’ historical drama feature The Nightingale in theaters on March 19, 2027. Dana Stevens and Ann Peacock penned the script for the project that adapts author Kristin Hannah’s 2015 best-selling novel.

The Nightingale centers on estranged sisters Isabelle Rossignol (Elle Fanning) and Vianne Mauriac (Dakota Fanning) as they navigate different paths while attempting to survive and resist the German occupation of France during World War II. “I have one job: to keep my family safe,” Vianne tells Isabelle in the trailer. “And you’re part of my family, whether you like it or not.”

Later, Isabelle is ready to take a stand against the Nazi regime. “They’re at our doorstep, in our homes,” she explains. “We must show them that we will fight back.”

Hailing from TriStar Pictures and Hello Sunshine, The Nightingale counts Dakota Fanning, Elle Fanning, Brittany Kahan Ward, Reese Witherspoon, Lauren Neustadter and Elizabeth Cantillon as producers. Edmund Donovan, Albrecht Schuch, Shira Haas and Mark Rylance round out the cast.

This marks the first onscreen project to star the two real-life Fanning sisters. Elle Fanning, 28, can currently be seen in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping and earned an Emmy Award nomination for the series Margo’s Got Money Troubles. Dakota Fanning, 32, similarly landed an Emmy nomination at this year’s ceremony for her role in All Her Fault.
