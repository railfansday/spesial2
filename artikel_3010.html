MDxHealth (MDXH) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Sales turnover and a business wind-down weighed on results, and management expects a slow recovery.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

MDxHealth SA (MDXH -4.44%) reported third quarter results impacted by a significant restructuring of its commercial organization following the discontinuation of its Resolve UTI business. Management reported that the company eliminated the Chief Commercial Officer role and split sales and marketing into two distinct operating groups reporting directly to the Chief Executive Officer to increase accountability. The company completed the wind-down of its Plano, Texas, laboratory operations and is now focusing exclusively on its core prostate cancer diagnostic vertical. Management stated that it has made progress in backfilling its sales organization and expects to be fully staffed by the end of the fourth quarter. The company is preparing for several 2027 catalysts, including the rollout of strategic artificial intelligence initiatives and data from the PROTECT study conducted with the University of Oxford.
