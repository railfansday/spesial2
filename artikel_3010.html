More Volatility Forecast for AI as Nvidia Earnings Loom

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Xi Qiao, UBS financial advisor and managing director, said Nvidia faces a particularly difficult backdrop ahead of earnings, including tougher U.S. export restrictions and concerns surrounding Jensen Huang. Qiao expects near-term volatility in Nvidia and the broader AI trade, as markets brace for slower investment in the AI ecosystem and possible supply-chain disruptions. Qiao advised investors to stay invested while diversifying across sectors, regions and asset classes.
