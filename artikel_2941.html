Tyler Perkins believes win over Michigan is prime example of Villanova Wildcats’ resilience

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Tyler Perkins sent a huge message on Villanova's identity after helping the Wildcats pull off a 79-73 win over the No. 18 Michigan Wolverines on Saturday night.

Perkins is going through the senior campaign of his collegiate career, his third year with the Wildcats. He has been a full-time starter for throughout most of his career, being a productive player in Villanova's rotation.

Villanova had a solid matchup on the way, taking on the reigning national champions Michigan. They posed a solid threat, causing trouble throughout the course of the game. Despite this, Perkins and the Wildcats dug deep to pull away with the upset win over the Wolverines. Perkins reflected on the win after the game, expressing a resilient mentality about the squad.

"We're never gonna quit. And today was a prime example of that," Perkins said.

Tyler Perkins and Villanova got a valuable ranked win over Michigan. The Wildcats are going through their own ups and downs, but for this night, they managed to get an important win that will create momentum for them.

Free throws and turnovers made the difference in this matchup. Villanova prevailed in both categories by making 22 shots at the line while limiting their turnovers to just seven. It wasn't the same for Michigan, converting 12 free throws and committing 16 turnovers.

Four players scored in double-digits for Villanova in the win, including Perkins. He had a strong performance with 23 points, five rebounds, two assists, and two steals. He shot 6-of-19 from the field, including 1-of-6 from beyond the arc, and 10-of-13 from the free-throw line. Jake Fiegen came next with 15 points and two steals, Elijah Crawford had 13 points and five assists, while Devin Royal provided 11 points and seven rebounds.

The Wildcats will look forward to their next matchup, being at home. They host the Coppin State Eagles as tip-off commences on Nov. 24 at 7 p.m. ET.
