Five Games Are Confirmed For Xbox Game Pass In December 2026 So Far

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

We've made a habit of casting our gaze ahead and sharing with you an early look at what's in store for December 2026's Xbox Game Pass lineup. So far, we know of five games scheduled to arrive in a few weeks' time.

Our highlight of the bunch is probably A Game About Digging A Hole Together. On the surface it might not seem like the biggest game, but the more you dig down, the better. At least that was the case with the single-player version of the game. This one is co-op!

It's worth mentioning that Dumpster Gang is indeed coming to Xbox Series consoles in March, but will be launching onto PC Game Pass December 3. Then of course, expect to see plenty more games added to December's offering over the coming weeks.

So, without any further ado, here's what's confirmed for Xbox Game Pass in December 2026 so far:
