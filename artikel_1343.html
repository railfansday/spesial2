Premier League: What Mike Dean Said About Leeds’ Penalty vs Chelsea

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Morgan Rogers was left fuming after a controversial decision came in Chelsea's shock 4-1 defeat at home to Leeds United on Saturday and former Premier League referee Mike Dean has given his verdict.

The Blues have made a nightmare start to the winter period following the international break. After a 1-0 loss away to Sunderland, Xabi Alonso would have hoped his players' time away with their countries would have helped them refresh and bounce back.

That wasn't the case as Leeds claimed a famous Stamford Bridge victory, courtesy of goals from Noah Okafor (28' and 58'), a Dominic Calvert-Lewin penalty (53') and Brenden Aaronsen (90+2'). Emmanuel Emegha had given the hosts the lead (25') but soon after, Alonso's men capitulated.

Leeds were well worth their win, putting in an extremely impressive performance in west London but the turning point came in the 52nd minute. When Jayden Bogle struck at goal, the ball appeared to strike Rogers' arm and referee Oliver pointed to the spot and signaled he'd taken instruction from VAR.

The video assistant referee did their own check and stuck with the decision before Calvert-Lewin calmly sent Emiliano Martinez the wrong way from the spot. As soon as Rogers realised VAR's verdict to uphold Oliver's decision, he reacted in disbelief, "That's the worst decision I've ever seen."

Chelsea's defeat means they now sit eighth in the table, with five wins and as many defeats. Alonso's start hasn't lived up to expectations and the latest setback has raised doubts over his ability to turn the tide at Stamford Bridge.

Former Premier League referee Mike Dean felt Oliver got the decision spot on when speaking on Sky Sports' Soccer Saturday. He said he thought it was "the right call" because "the distance is quite far when Bogle strikes it to where Rogers is."

Dean added: "But I think he does direct it away from goal. It's probably inconclusive when you see the pictures, but it's not a wrong decision by the referee on the field. He's made the call. VAR will never overturn that kind of decision."

This is the second consecutive game a contentious decision has played a role in a Chelsea defeat. Huge debate ensued when Brian Brobbey went down after a challenge from Levi Colwill and won the winning penalty for Sunderland at the Stadium of Light.

The Blues continue to struggle for consistency this season and now sit eighth in the Premier League table.

Alonso opted not to blame the 1-0 defeat on that controversial call and instead pointed the finger at his team's defending. He may well take the same approach in response to yet another miserable defensive display from his side, who have conceded 19 goals in 11 games.
