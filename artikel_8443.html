So what was your purpose/goal in buying a TIPS ladder ETF in Taxable?

If it's for a bridge to some future income stream like SocSec or penalty-free asset access (Trad/Roth after age 59.5 and you're younger than that now), then you don't want to reinvest as your plan was to spend the dividends and returned principal. If not for a bridge, you'll have to tell us what the purpose of the bonds is (e.g., overall AA and out of space in Trad perhaps, but then tax-exempt bond funds/ETFs in Taxable likely would've made more sense).
My tax-deferred account is small compared to my taxable account, and the tax-deferred account is all bond funds. I need to maintain my desired AA, so I have to put some bonds in the taxable account. This is largely for a bridge from 57-ish to 70. I have plenty of individual treasuries in the taxable, but I wanted to diversify my fixed income sources a bit beyond the nominal return investments. I know muni bonds funds are an option, but at today's Treasury and TIPS rates, the muni's don't bring much to the table from a TEY standpoint, and I will not be in a high tax bracket when I/we retire in the coming year or so.
