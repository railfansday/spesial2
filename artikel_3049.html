Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Fewer Ford Amphitheater shows, yet revenue per show climbed sharply this year.

Venu Holding Corporation (VENU -2.65%) reported its third quarter results, highlighting the transition from a development-focused enterprise to an operating hospitality and entertainment company. Management reported that the company is currently finalizing construction on two major amphitheaters scheduled to open within the next two quarters. The company secured a $60 million sale-leaseback transaction to fund the completion of these projects while retaining ownership of the venue structures. Management stated that the financing strategy utilizes municipal contributions, fractional ownership sales, and permanent C-PACE financing to support its nationwide expansion of premium entertainment destinations.
