Wednesday Season 3 Is Finally Adapting The Real Addams Family

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Things are changing for Wednesday, as Jenna Ortega's hit Netflix show leaves Nevermore Academy behind in its upcoming third season. When it debuted in 2022, Wednesday was introduced as a coming-of-age whodunit that followed the titular heroine's supernatural investigation at Nevermore Academy and in the small town of Jericho. Season 1 saw Wednesday trying to adjust to her new school while investigating a string of murders caused by a Hyde monster. Meanwhile, in season 2, the teenage detective faced new threats involving a vision about Enid's death and a conspiracy about a psychiatric hospital.

Wednesday season 3 will be very different from previous installments, with Ortega's character embarking on new misadventures that will take her far beyond Nevermore Academy. Audiences last saw Wednesday tracking down clues that will lead her to Enid's whereabouts. Ahead of its expected 2027 premiere, Wednesday season 3 has already finished filming in Ireland and Paris. Ortega will reprise her leading role, along with Emma Myers as Enid Sinclair, Catherine Zeta-Jones as Morticia Addams, Luis Guzmán as Gomez Addams, Isaac Ordonez as Pugsley Addams, Fred Armisen as Uncle Fester, and Victor Dorobantu as Thing.

While fans will see Wednesday prioritize the search for Enid in the upcoming season, the Addams family will also drive one major storyline. Though Wednesday wasn't created to center the iconic family, Morticia, Gomez, Pugsley, Thing, and Uncle Fester still had key supporting roles throughout the show's first two seasons. Now, fans will get to see much more of them in the next installment, as the series explores buried family secrets that may put Wednesday in danger.

Wednesday season 2 ends with the shocking revelation that Morticia's missing sister, Ophelia, is actually alive and locked away in an underground basement cell at Grandmama Hester Frump's mansion. The cliffhanger also comes with an ominous warning, showing Ophelia writing "Wednesday must die" in blood on the wall of her cell. This effectively sets the stage for the Addams family to take on a much bigger role in season 3, as deeper family secrets finally unfold into the main narrative.

Before the season 2 finale reveal, Ophelia was introduced as Morticia's sister, who lost control of her extraordinary psychic abilities as a student at Nevermore. After her descent into madness, she supposedly went missing after being admitted to the Willow Hill Psychiatric Hospital. Throughout season 2, Ophelia served as a cautionary tale for Wednesday because of their similar psychic powers, with Morticia worrying that her daughter might suffer the same tragic fate because of Wednesday's tendency to overuse her gift for the sake of her investigations.

The character of Wednesday Addams has come a long way over the years, and each actor has brought something special to the iconic Addams Family role.

Fans will learn more about Aunt Ophelia in Wednesday season 3 with the arrival of Casino Royale star Eva Green, who has signed on to play the mysterious character. In addition to her casting, Joanna Lumley has been promoted to series regular in the next installment, following her commanding performance in season 2 as the fabulously wealthy and ruthless Hester Frump. Season 3 will likely see Wednesday trying to uncover why Ophelia was imprisoned. It remains to be seen how this revelation will affect Wednesday's relationship with her grandmother, especially since Hester has openly favored her.

As for Ophelia, it's unclear whether she'll be an enemy or an ally for Wednesday in the upcoming third season. Her chilling message in the season 2 finale could be an attempt to warn her niece about a much greater supernatural threat coming her way. However, if Green's character becomes the antagonist, an intense psychic clash will be inevitable.

As the extended Addams family becomes the central focus of the show's next mystery, Wednesday season 3 finally trades the high school whodunit formula for a multi-generational family thriller. This genre shift will bring the Netflix adaptation back to the franchise's roots. While Ortega's teen detective remains the lead, season 3 promises a full-blown gothic family saga in line with Charles Addams' original cartoon comics and 1960s sitcom, where the macabre clan is placed right at the heart of the story.

Even though the high school element gave the franchise a refreshing new look at the beloved character, Wednesday season 3's genre change will raise the stakes for the titular protagonist more than ever. Ophelia's impending arrival will throw the Addams Family into chaos, as loyalty gets tested and long-buried secrets threaten to further fracture relationships. This will be Wednesday's biggest challenge yet.

Wednesday season 3 is expected to premiere on Netflix in 2027.

Wednesday follows the journey of Wednesday Addams as she attends Nevermore Academy, a boarding school for outcasts. Tasked with mastering her psychic abilities, she confronts a series of town murders and a haunting family mystery from 25 years ago, all while managing new social dynamics.
