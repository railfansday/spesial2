Forget Pharaoh, 9-1-1: Nashville Season 2 Has A More Sinister Villain

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Mild SPOILERS ahead for 9-1-1: Nashville season 2, episode 6, "Tears of the Nile."9-1-1: Nashville season 2, episode 6, "Tears of the Nile," saw the reveal of the villainous Pharaoh, but not because Rogue, the newly introduced law enforcement character, finally caught them. Instead, Pharaoh captured both Rogue and Don, leaving the two in a precarious Saw-like situation. It might seem like a step back, but it’s really a step closer to taking down the season's Big Bad (Don and Rogue working together has proven quite effective so far). However, while Pharaoh might be on their way out when the show returns in January, a more sinister villain remains — and they need to be dealt with.

Cammie was again harassed by crank calls from her boss, Marcia, during the opening of 9-1-1: Nashville’s mid-season finale. Few clues have been offered about why Marcia might be so obsessed with bullying Cammie, but no clear explanation has been given. For now, Marcia’s villainy is more sinister than Pharaoh’s — Pharaoh might be murderous and chaotic, and their reasoning hasn't been revealed yet, but at least they have a plan. With no endgame to her harassment in sight, Marcia's next move is up in the air.

At first glance, Marcia seems like nothing more than a difficult boss. She’s somewhat manipulative. She's incredibly nice to everyone until she suddenly becomes overly critical, berating the 911 operators for being too emotional on calls and threatening to let AI take over their jobs. Then, she targets Cammie by playing the 911 call of her husband’s death in front of everyone. As harsh as that is, it's still "bad boss" territory. However, the revelation that she’s the one prank-calling Cammie turns it into a personal vendetta.

In the second episode she appeared in, Marcia told Cammie that she lost her son to a drunk driver when he was about 12 years old. Between that, the emphasis on not tying up the call center lines, and the harassment of Cammie, it makes one wonder if Cammie was the operator who answered the call about Marcia’s son, but got to it too late to help because the changes she was making weren't fully implemented, and the call was forced to hold. That would make Marcia’s efforts to tear Cammie down make sense, at least.

However, Marcia’s son had to have died at least a couple of years ago, so if she’s been plotting to find a way to become head of the call center solely to make crank calls, that’s wild (and honestly, not a great revenge plot). That leaves the question of where Marcia is headed with all of this, and how far 9-1-1: Nashville season 2 is willing to let her go. If crank calls are just the start, numerous possibilities could follow, from swatting to murder (depending on how dark the show wants to go this season).

The impact of Marcia’s villainy on Cammie, her family, her boyfriend, and the rest of the firefighting family depends on how her harassment evolves next. If she keeps up the crank calls, it will definitely affect Cammie’s mental health; that might even be the point, since, as Cammie’s boss, Marcia keeps saying she's responsible for the employees' mental health and well-being. Cammie’s mental health suffering gives Marcia the perfect opportunity to fire her from the call center, which might be all she’s aiming for.

ABC has confirmed its 2027 midseason premiere dates, confirming when viewers can expect new episodes of Will Trent, Shifting Gears, and many more.

It’s doubtful, though, that Cammie wouldn’t be back at her job in a few weeks (she’s the steadiest character on the show and 9-1-1: Nashville needs her), but it would create fantastic drama for a few episodes. Of course, if Marcia decides to take things further by putting Cammie actively in harm’s way, the consequences for everyone could be devastating. Doing that would put Marcia in harm’s way, too, though, because it’s safe to say the entire 113 (and Blythe) would come down in full force to protect Cammie.

That decision would likely end with Marcia behind bars. If 9-1-1: Nashville wants to keep her around long-term, though, she would need to actually have an honest conversation with Cammie. There would be no one better than Cammie to understand what it's like to have a loved one die because 911 didn't immediately answer a call. While that seems like the most reasonable option, Marcia has proven to be entirely unreasonable, so where her actions lead in the future remains to be seen.

9-1-1: Nashville returns Thursday, January 7, 2027, at 9 PM ET on ABC.
