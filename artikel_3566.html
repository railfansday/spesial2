Influencer Allison Kuch Defends Breastfeeding in the Middle of NYC Marathon

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Influencer and podcast host Allison Kuch found plenty to celebrate while running the 2026 New York City marathon, just months after giving birth to her daughter, Pepper.

Kuch, 31, stopped at Mile 17 of the marathon route earlier this month to breastfeed, which she documented on social media and hoped was “empowering for so many women to see.”

“It was also empowering for me to achieve something like running a marathon so soon postpartum,” Kuch exclusively told Us. “I have yet to have a full night of sleep to this point. I still trained, I was still able to dedicate time to myself. That was all made possible because of the support I have at home.”

Kuch and her husband, former NFL player Isaac Rochell, welcomed Pepper in April and also share daughter Scottie, born in December 2023. Together, the couple host the Netflix Original podcast “Couldn’t Agree Less,” with new episodes dropping every Thursday.

“Like, my husband being able to watch the kids while I go on runs, or meet me if the baby is hungry and she doesn’t want to take a bottle,” Kuch explained. “Getting to that moment [at the marathon], I was like, ‘Wow, this feels so good.’ It took so much work to get there. But I want it to be empowering to other women. You should take time for yourself because it’s important as a mom.”

Rochell, 31, told Us that welcoming baby Pepper into the fold has resulted in “no free time.”

“There is zero unscheduled free time,” he said. “We can force free time. We can find help. But you’re not just going to accidentally stumble upon 10 minutes during the day.”

Rochell continued, “It would have to be both kids napping at the exact same time for a super long time. I’ll be like, ‘I’ll take the kids, you take free time.’ But then that free time ends up being occupied with stuff that you can finally do.”

Kuch agreed, saying that any unexpected free time just becomes “catch-up time.”

“We have two very young kids, so I know that it won’t be like this forever,” she said. “But our life has completely changed. Of course, adding another child, things get harder.”

Along the way, the couple — who got married in April 2021 — have pushed back against the narrative that their relationship is “perfect.”

“Our relationship is very not perfect,” Rochell admitted. “No relationship is perfect. I think sometimes people probably look at our situation like, ‘Oh my God, they’re on Netflix. Everything is so awesome.’ I’m like, ‘Look, this podcast is the longest conversation we’re having every week.’”

Rochell added, “We’re working through things just like every other couple is. I would never want somebody to look at us and think we’re perfect, because it’s just not true.”

New episodes of “Couldn’t Agree Less” drop Thursdays exclusively on Netflix.
