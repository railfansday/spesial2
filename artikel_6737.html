Let Your Thanksgiving Tablescape Inspire Your Outfit

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

From cranberry velvet to bird motifs, holiday dressing starts at the table.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

It finally feels like we’re in the thick of fall. It’s just beginning to get cold enough to wear our favorite coats, all the leaves have turned graceful shades of red, yellow, and orange, and somehow, nothing feels as comforting as a hot cider cupped in the perfect pair of leather gloves. We’ve almost arrived at Thanksgiving, which officially marks the start of the holiday season.

When it comes to fashion, Thanksgiving is actually one of the best holidays for holiday dressing. There is ample inspiration to rework fall classics and experiment with new trends that have just recently been presented on the runways, like berry tones, antique Victorian-inspired style, or even statement coats. The weather (not too cold just yet, but cold enough to layer) also makes it a prime time to play with accessories, whether it be medallion belts or grandmacore-inspired tapestry bags.

So, this year, why not pull your Thanksgiving outfit inspiration directly from your tablescape? Aside from celebrating with friends and family, a decorative tablescape is one of the most fun parts of this holiday. Make a Pinterest board for both, and you’ll match: There’s nothing chicer.

Read on for some ideas for Thanksgiving tablescape-inspired outfits.

Look to Thanksgiving’s ultimate favorite hue and layer up juicy shades of it. Rich, stunning tones of saturated cranberry pop (and you’ll probably spot them on your plate, too). It gives you a dramatic look with low-key effort. Opt for mixed textures. Think: leather, velvet, silks, and satins all done in deep cranberry.

It’s completely okay to be a little bit literal. In fact, it can actually make your outfit more fun. Consider traditional tablecloth textiles—gingham, plaid, and tartan—and make them a part of your look. You can use them as an accent (a scarf or the lining of a coat) or make them the main focus.

By now, we all know the Thanksgiving mascot as the turkey. And many editorial tablescapes incorporate some kind of turkey or autumnal bird. Bird motifs are both a trend and extremely seasonally accurate (and inspiring) for a Thanksgiving outfit, too. Go conceptual: bird prints, peacock feather patterns, winged jewelry, and feather trims are all visually interesting.

The easiest Thanksgiving outfit (and tablescape) trick is to lean into the natural codes of Mother Nature. Accessorize your outfit and table with dark fall florals, leaf motifs, gourds and pumpkins, or atmospheric forest and woodland fall elements. There are so many different directions to go in. But when in doubt, think of berry tones and fun details like walnut-shaped clutches or mushroom motifs.

Choose a swanning maxi dress in shades of rich pumpkin orange or saffron yellow; even better if it has a fall-ready-feeling textile trend like fringe or tassels (which can also be found on tablescapes). Match it to your décor and the fall foliage: Layer up rich shades of red, rust, terracotta, chestnut brown, olive green, caramel, and sienna.
