Liverpool Songs & Chants (Complete Guide)

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Liverpool are one of the biggest clubs in world football and their success on the pitch is reflected by a long list of popular chants - both past and present - that reverberate around Anfield and away sections on matchday.

The passionate support of the Reds is regarded as one of the most impressive in the sport, with several players and managers giving glowing reviews of their visits to Liverpool’s iconic home ground. Whether it’s a dramatic European night under the lights or a high-stakes Premier League clash, Liverpool fans have a rich history of delivering an extensive soundtrack, full of amusing and catchy chants.

Many of those songs can transcend generations, with plenty of chants paying tribute to some of the club’s most cherished former players and managers. Here at GIVEMESPORT, we’ve narrowed it down and picked Liverpool’s best and most memorable chants over the years.

Luis Garcia stole the hearts of Liverpool fans when he arrived from Barcelona in 2004. The Spaniard was an instant hit and fans couldn't help but sing his name, referencing his time at Barcelona and his seeming love for sangria.

The song has since echoed around Anfield any time Garcia has returned, with fans particularly fond of his so-called 'ghost goal' against Chelsea on the way to Liverpool's stunning 2005 Champions League triumph.

Federico Chiesa is not the best player at Liverpool, but he has become one of the most loved. The Anfield faithful have admired his work rate from day one, even if things haven't quite turned out as planned, and sing his song most weeks.

When the Italian scored the winning goal in the dying embers on the opening day of the 2025/26 season against Bournemouth, Chiesa's song could be heard echoing around Merseyside.

Liverpool fans have endless eras of success to choose from and Bill Shankly always gets a nod as a result. The legendary manager is not forgotten on the Kop, with fans bellowing a chant all about his side's dominance in the 60s and 70s.

Liverpool fans just could not get enough of Luis Suarez, and who could blame them? The forward was untouchable during his time at Anfield. Largely forgiven for breaking the hearts of fans when moving to Chelsea in 2011, Fernando Torres can now show his face at Anfield and hear his song if he so pleases. The forward remains one of the best Liverpool players of the Premier League era, and even had a catchy chant to match his quality on the pitch.

He had to have a song, didn't he? And he absolutely loved it. Jurgen Klopp's chant was long overdue at Anfield and the lyrics still ring true to this day. Klopp promised to deliver a Premier League title in four years in 2015, and he did just that he 2019, turning Liverpool fans from doubters into believers.

Virgil van Dijk is comfortably one of, if not the best defender in the world. The Liverpool captain has been untouchable throughout his time at Anfield and it did not take long for fans to start singing his name. If the Dutchman could stick around forever to hear that chant every week, then he likely would.

Mohamed Salah was crowned Liverpool's very own Egyptian King during his debut season with the Reds, as he broke the then-record for the most goals scored in a single Premier League campaign. It was the start of something historic, with his song echoing around Anfield for nine, incredible years.

The 30 greatest players in Liverpool history have been named, including Steven Gerrard, Kenny Dalglish, Virgil van Dijk and Mohamed Salah.
