Airline responds as flight attendant took photo of TikToker sleeping & threatned to post it

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

An airline has responded after a TikToker said that a flight attendant snapped a quick photo of her while she slept on a trip to Mexico City for the Formula 1.

If you’re on a long flight, chances are you’re going to want to catch some shuteye in the process. You might miss out on food and drink service, but it can make the journey feel a whole lot quicker anyway.

In the case of TikToker Tasia Jewel, she claimed that a flight attendant on her flight from Austin, Texas, to Mexico City decided to take a photo of her while she slept and said that he was going to post it on Facebook.

Jewel told her followers that the flight attendant admitted to taking the photo, telling her: “Hey, yo friend, I took a picture of you while you were sleeping.”

She noted that some of the other cabin crew members joked about it as she was leaving the plane too. “I couldn’t believe that he said that,” the TikToker added.

Tasia called on Delta airline to “fire” the flight attendant who snapped the photo, noting that she “felt incredibly unsafe” by the situation.

After it had racked up more than 200,000 views, the TikToker updated viewers on October 31, revealing that she had since spoken to the airline.

“I spoke up about an inappropriate comment that a flight attendant made towards me and the internet tried to gaslight me and call me a liar but the airline is 100% on my side and everything is being handled as it should,” Jewel added. “The internet is so mean but I knew Delta would have my back.”
