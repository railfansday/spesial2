Leah Remini Reunites With Julie Chen Moonves Years After 'The Talk' Drama

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Leah Remini and Julie Chen Moonves’ reunion is certainly something to talk about.

“Getting Big Brother strategy tips from @juliechenmoonves on how to survive the rest of this season of The Real Housewives of Beverly Hills,” Remini, 56, wrote via Instagram on Tuesday, November 17, alongside a photo of her and Chen Moonves, also 56, sitting together in a restaurant booth.

While the moment may look like two old coworkers catching up over Mexican food and margaritas, fans may recall that Remini and Chen Moonves shared a complicated history while working together on The Talk.

The daytime talk show kicked off in 2010 with Chen Moonves, Remini, Holly Robinson Peete, Marissa Jaret Winokur, Sharon Osbourne and Sara Gilbert.

After one season, the show experienced a dramatic cast shakeup with Remini, Robinson-Peete, 62, and Jaret Winokur, 53, leaving with little to no explanation.

“A couple of my cohosts, while I was away, went to the network and said, ‘We can’t work with Julie anymore. She’s too uptight. She’s not one of us. If she stays, we go,’” Chen Moonves wrote in her 2023 book, But First, God. “And they ended up leaving and I ended up staying. But what was so hurtful and challenging about this time was that one of the people who left was someone who I became, like, overnight best friends with after working on The Talk. And that was Leah Remini. We were buddies. We were, like, going on vacation together, and when she went to the network with this request, I felt betrayed.”

The Big Brother host kept her distance from Remini for almost a decade until turning to Christianity and learning about the gift of forgiveness.

She later saw Remini’s then-husband, Angelo Pagan, at a party and asked for the King of Queens star’s phone number. The pair have since been able to move forward and reconcile.

Remini’s latest meeting with Chen Moonves comes as she continues filming her first season of The Real Housewives of Beverly Hills.

Robinson Peete previously shared her reaction to Remini joining the popular Bravo reality show.

“It’s hard when you watch your friends go on those shows because those shows can be brutal,” she exclusively told Us in September. “They can be brutal on friendships, on relationships. They talk about your kids sometimes. You really gotta have a thick skin.”

Robinson Peete added, “While I’m a little bit nervous about it, I don’t really worry about her because I know she can handle her own.”
