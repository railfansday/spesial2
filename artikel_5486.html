Wenge AI Adds Google and Apple Sign-In Options for ScienceOra International Users

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Update streamlines registration and sign-in for researchers using the international site

SHENZHEN, China, Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Wenge AI (1956.HK) today announced that ScienceOra, its AI-powered scientific research and decision-support platform, has enhanced registration and sign-in for its international site with Google and Apple sign-in options. The update is designed to give overseas researchers a faster, more convenient way to access the AI for Science platform.

Users can now authenticate with their existing Google or Apple accounts instead of completing a lengthy registration form. Based on the email address and account-linking status, ScienceOra identifies whether to create a new account or sign the user in to an existing account. Apple sign-in is currently available on the web version of the platform.

The update also improves the way third-party accounts are linked to ScienceOra. It supports linking for users with existing accounts and provides clearer validation and prompts where an email address is already in use, an account is already linked, or Apple's Hide My Email feature is used.

These refinements are intended to reduce duplicate registrations and help users resolve common sign-in situations with less friction, while maintaining a more consistent account experience. ScienceOra will continue to refine its product experience around the needs of international users, making access to research tools and scientific exploration more seamless.

Explore ScienceOra: https://www.scienceora.ai

ScienceOra is Wenge AI's AI-powered scientific research and decision-support platform. Powered by the ScienceOne Scientific Foundation Model, it brings together scientific literature, data, tools and specialized research agents to support researchers across scientific inquiry and research workflows.

Wenge AI (1956.HK) is an enterprise AI company developing proprietary foundation models and AI platforms to help organizations turn advanced AI capabilities into practical business applications. Founded by a team of AI scientists from the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Wenge AI combines deep expertise in foundation models, cognitive intelligence and complex-systems modelling. The company is committed to bringing advanced AI from research and technological innovation into real-world enterprise applications.
