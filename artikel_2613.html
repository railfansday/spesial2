Pacers get good news on Aaron Nesmith hand injury ahead of 76ers

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Pacers forward Aaron Nesmith is listed as questionable with a left hand sprain for Sunday's 1 p.m. game against the 76ers in Philadelphia.

Nesmith injured the hand while scrambling for the ball on a Pacers turnover in the final seconds of Friday's upset win over the Knicks. He appeared to be in significant pain and left the game immediately.

Nesmith scored 18 points on 7 of 10 shooting including 2 of 3 from 3-point range in the game. He's averaging 12.3 points per game this season.

All-Star point guard Tyrese Haliburton (left hamstring strain) and wings Johnny Furphy (ACL tear) and Ben Sheppard (left meniscus tear) are all still out. Veteran point guard T.J. McConnell, who has been increasing his minutes as he works back from a right hamstring strain, is still listed as probable.
