UConn women’s basketball drops four spots in latest USA Today poll

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

UConn continued to fall from the rankings after being ranked No. 2 in the preseason.

The Huskies fell to No. 8 in the latest USA Today Coaches Poll, which was released on Tuesday Nov. 17 — down four spots from last week. The fall comes after UConn was blown out on Sunday by Louisville in a 69-51 defeat.

UConn (3-2) has played in a tough noncon schedule to open the season. Two weeks ago, the Huskies were upset by Michigan on the road in their first ranked game of the year.

They followed that performance with back-to-back wins over No. 10 Iowa and No. 19 Ohio State before falling to Louisville on Sunday. Louisville moved up four sports to No. 3.

In the AP poll, UConn dropped from fourth to seventh.

The road doesn't get any easier for UConn. The Huskies will play their first Big East game on Friday, Nov. 20 against Seton Hall before a matchup with No. 1 South Carolina two days before Thanksgiving.
