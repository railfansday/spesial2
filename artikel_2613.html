Sean McVay slays biggest dragon snapping long-standing losing streakto Matt LaFleur

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Sean McVay finally slayed the dragon. In fact, he slayed a two-headed dragon.

For years, Green Bay Packers head coach Matt LaFleur has been McVay's kryptonite. No matter how talented the Los Angeles Rams were or how cleverly McVay constructed his game plan, his longtime friend always seemed to have the answers.

That all changed on Wednesday night at SoFi Stadium.

The Rams defeated the Packers 26-13 in a Thanksgiving Eve primetime showdown on Netflix, snapping McVay's five-game losing streak against LaFleur, including the postseason.

Even sweeter for McVay? He's now beaten both LaFleur brothers in consecutive games after previously going 0-6 against the family.

McVay earned his first victory over Arizona Cardinals head coach Mike LaFleur, his former offensive coordinator, in Week 10 after suffering a heartbreaking 18-16 loss to Arizona in Week 6. Eleven days later, he finally got the better of the older brother as well.

Los Angeles Rams head coach Sean McVay, left, and Arizona Cardinals head coach Mike LaFleur talk after a game in Glendale, Ariz., Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. Getty Images

The friendship between McVay and Matt LaFleur dates back to their days as assistants in Washington from 2010 to 2013. LaFleur later served as McVay's first offensive coordinator with the Rams in 2017 before becoming Green Bay's head coach in 2019.

"Before I met my wife and before they even had their kids, I'd go hang out with BreAnne [LaFleur] and Matt in Reston Town Center and I was like the third wheel of their relationship," McVay said before Wednesday's game. "I was like the little brother."

Of course, friendship only goes so far on a football field.

"I love Matt, love Bre, love their family," McVay added. "It'll be cool, but we're trying to kick each other's ass, I know that much."

McVay made good on that promise, as the Rams physically overwhelmed the Packers with 241 rushing yards to secure their fifth consecutive victory to improve to 7-4.

For once, it's Sean McVay who has bragging rights at the LaFleur family Thanksgiving table.
