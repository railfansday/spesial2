Spurs erase 20-point deficit to eliminate Lakers in NBA Cup action, advance to knockout round as wild card

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The most wide-open division of the 2026 NBA Cup entering Friday, the final day of group play, was West Group C, which had four different teams capable of clinching a spot in the knockout rounds. Two of those teams were the San Antonio Spurs and Los Angeles Lakers, and they delivered one of the night's wildest games.

The Spurs erased a 20-point deficit on their way to a 99-96 win over the Lakers, riding another huge game from Victor Wembanyama. The win officially put them in the knockout rounds as the West's wild card, thanks to a plus-57 point differential in four games.

Simultaneously, the Portland Trail Blazers won the group by defeating the Golden State Warriors and holding the tiebreaker over San Antonio. The Lakers and Warriors were eliminated with the losses.

Wembanyama finished with 25 points, 16 rebounds, 4 steals, 2 assists and 2 blocks, but his 3-for-8 night at the line also invited disaster toward the end of the game, when the Lakers were trying to inch their way back into it.

Los Angeles got the ball down three with a chance to tie the game twice in the final 10 seconds, but the Spurs fouled both times and managed to hold on for the win.

It was a brutal first half for the Spurs, who entered halftime down 54-38, but they proceeded to outscore the Lakers 30-14 in the third quarter and continued that momentum into the fourth quarter to take control.

It's a return to knockout-round action for the Spurs, who reached the NBA Cup championship game last year before losing to the New York Knicks (a result that was repeated in the NBA Finals). They will now face the Dallas Mavericks, the only team to go undefeated in group play, in the quarterfinals next week.
