AC Milan thump five past Frosinone in Serie A

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

AC Milan returned to the Serie A top four as Goncalo Ramos ended his goal drought in a 5-1 thrashing of Frosinone on Sunday, while Juventus hammered Venezia 4-0 to go sixth.

Ruben Amorim's side sealed back-to-back wins for the first time in over a month to go fourth, five points behind leaders Lazio, as club record signing Ramos broke a five-match goalless streak.

Frosinone have been one of the surprise packages of the season since winning promotion from Serie B but dropped to seventh behind Juventus after Luciano Spalletti's side extended their unbeaten run to four matches with a big win in Turin.

Milan got off to a flying start with a Gabriele Calvini own goal and a composed Ramos strike inside 19 minutes.

Amorim accused his players of "not helping Ramos" ahead of the game after the Portugal international scored just twice in his first 11 league games following a 74 million euro summer move from Paris Saint-Germain.

His third was met with a loud roar from the San Siro crowd after he was teed up by Adrien Rabiot, who became the first Milan midfielder since Kaka in 2004 to notch a goal or assist in seven consecutive league games.

The visitors soon hit back through Patrizio Masini, but Milan pulled clear in the second half with some lethal counter attacking as substitutes Ardon Jashari and Alphadjo Cisse struck before Christian Pulisic's dinked finish completed the biggest victory of the Amorim era.

Goals from Nick Woltemade, Teun Koopmeiners, Juan Cabal and Randal Kolo Muani moved Juve up to sixth place thanks to a second-half surge in Turin.

Juve coach Luciano Spalletti faced scrutiny after an underwhelming start to the season, with Antonio Conte linked with a return to his former club during the international break. But Spalletti celebrated becoming just the sixth coach to reach 600 Serie A games with a resounding win, despite his team being booed off at the break following a goalless first half.

"In the first half, we gave the ball away too much, nobody moved or followed up a pass. It was crucial the players recognised that something was missing," Spalletti told DAZN.

"In the second half, the players were decisive and more relaxed."

Woltemade's introduction after the break sparked the game into life as he pounced on a loose ball to smash home his second league goal in two games before turning provider for Koopmeiners' whipped finish. Cabal headed home Juve's third before Kolo Muani won and converted a penalty to complete the rout, with all four goals coming between the 53rd and 71st minute.

Elsewhere, Milutin Osmajic scored a double in a 2-0 win for Genoa at Sassuolo and Bologna were held to a 1-1 draw by Udinese.

Champions Inter visit Atalanta in the final game on Sunday.
