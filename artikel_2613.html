Kirby Smart’s viral halftime interview despite Georgia football crushing Missouri shows why he’s the best

On Saturday afternoon, the Georgia football program cruised to 9-1 on the 2026 season with a dominant home win over the Missouri Tigers. The Bulldogs did most of their damage in this one in the first half, racing out to a 31-7 lead as the teams headed to the locker room in Athens.

Still, despite that strong showing, Bulldogs head coach Kirby Smart wasn't exactly in a jovial mood as he walked off the field for his halftime interview with ESPN.

Just prior to the moment, the Bulldogs had suffered an empty offensive trip and then executed a poorly-placed punt to the Tigers, which had Smart visible berating his players on the sideline, as captured by ESPN cameras.

Still, Georgia ended up coasting in the second half to another easy victory over a ranked opponent.

Earlier in the season, the Bulldogs had gotten off to a similarly hot start to a game against the Auburn Tigers at home before taking their foot off the gas pedal and losing in shocking fashion, giving them their only defeat of the season to date. That debacle was clearly on Smart's mind as he looked to keep his team motivated throughout Saturday's beatdown of Missouri.

Georgia did more than enough in this one to hold onto its number one ranking in the country, especially considering that number two Utah narrowly escaped disaster in a win over the Arizona Wildcats.

The Bulldogs are next slated to hit the field for a road game against the South Carolina Gamecocks. Kickoff for that game is set for Saturday evening at 7:30 pm ET in Columbia.
