Historic first quarter powers No. 2 Michigan to blowout win over Toledo

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

If there is one thing the No. 2 Michigan women's basketball team will do, it's light up the scoreboard. No matter who the opponent is, the Wolverines' offense is a machine that never stops churning, and their matchup against Toledo in the Bahamas at the Baha Mar Pink Flamingo Championship was no exception.

Powered by its second-best quarter in program history, Michigan (7-0) put away the Rockets (4-3) with ease. The Wolverines scored at every level, draining threes and powering through contested layups against a potent Toledo defense. The result was five players ending up scoring in the double digits and a commanding 102-56 victory.

Michigan's record-breaking 36-point first quarter was characterized by its combination of aggression and pinpoint accuracy, shooting 70 percent and 71.4 percent from three. The Wolverines took control of the tempo of the game right away, speeding up the Rockets to give them no time to apply any semblance of defensive pressure.

Junior guard Kendall Dudley led the charge, shooting a perfect 5-for-5 in the quarter and tallying 11 points. She consistently used her size and swiftness to drive to the rim and tip in easy layups. With a point total of 10, junior guard Olivia Olson was closely behind Dudley in offensive contributions, making her own magic by grabbing boards, ripping steals and forcing a foul.

In the first quarter, Toledo certainly offered the path of least resistance for Michigan's assertive offense, giving the Wolverines wide-open looks and putting them up, 36-15. But the Rockets weren't going down without a fight.

Toledo looked like a different team entirely on the defensive end and went back to its bread and butter of active and communicative defense. The easy looks of the first half vanished as the Rockets' sticky man coverage stuck a wrench in Michigan's offensive game plan. On the offensive end, Toledo slowed down possessions, finding the perfect chances it needed to attempt to cut the deficit.

Still, the Wolverines didn't stay down long. Michigan consistently punished the Rockets for any misfires, bullying its way for rebounds and subsequently converting on the possessions. Junior guard Te'Yala Delfosse was the maestro behind the offensive fire the Wolverines did conjure up, consistently knocking down contested shots from all over the floor. Toledo just about outscored Michigan 19-18 in the quarter, but the Rockets' best wasn't nearly enough to change the tide of the game, with the Wolverines still up 54-34.

And once the second half began, it was clear that there was no more weathering the storm for Toledo. Michigan's defense upped the ante to begin the third quarter, giving the Rockets no easy shots and taking complete control of the glass to limit them to one-and-dones. The Wolverines went on a 16-2 run over the first four minutes, fueled by their dominance in the paint, ballooning their lead up to an untouchable 34.

By the fourth quarter, Michigan's 40+ point cushion allowed it to rotate its bench in at no cost. And they only packed on to the pummeling, limiting Toledo to just six points all quarter.

The Wolverines now leave Bahama still perfect, and they showed the Rockets exactly why their record is so unblemished. Their offense never took its foot off the gas for long, sealing the game in its usual assertive fashion.
