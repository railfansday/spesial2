Personal Consumer Issues • Re: Is anyone stocking up supplies?

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Genuinely curious what scenarios you're envisioning where two weeks of food and water will be insufficient. Unless you're already living off the grid or actually have the means to do so indefinitely, it's hard to imagine circumstances where evacuation or resupply isn't possible for two weeks yet preparedness short of a full-on (non-pretend) survivalist would be meaningful.
Very recent example is Hurricane Helene for those living in the mountains with blocked roads. Only helicopter drops in and out for quite a long time. Roads leading up to several communities were either completely washed out from the river and flooding or congested with dozens if not hundreds of downed trees and power lines. Multiple weeks without electricity, and many utilities like natural gas and water shut off. Even military and law enforcement radio communications were down for a bit as well, but the amateur radio repeater on that one Mountain was operational.

Hurricane Katrina 20+ years ago is a very similar example but with the added bonus of civil unrest and looting.

Point is, it's not hard to imagine a bad hurricane, earthquake, or ice storm/blizzard from being a multi-week SNAFU before even FEMA, American Red Cross, Samaritan's Purse, and other volunteers come in with food and water and other supplies. More than a minor inconvenience and quite life-threatening. Similarly, COVID-19 was a good reminder of what a pandemic can be as far as resources and lockdowns and various mandated closures. There is potential for worse diseases that could realistically shut down Rule of Law society for multiple weeks, and it would be prudent to have basic water, food, medicines, first aid, power, sanitation, and others ready to go.
