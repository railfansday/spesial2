SAG-AFTRA Reaches New Tentative Contract With Netflix

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

SAG-AFTRA has tentatively re-upped its solo contract with Netflix.

The guild revealed Tuesday that a tentative agreement had been reached on a new bargaining agreement, which is now subject to approval by the national board.

That vote is expected “in the near future,” per the union, and no details from the deal are expected in advance of that review.

SAG-AFTRA and Netflix Reach Tentative AgreementDeal Goes to SAG-AFTRA National Board for Review and Approval pic.twitter.com/RnRiwMPzh4

The performers’ union has had a standalone contract with Netflix Studios since 2019. Before that, the streaming giant dealt with the guild on a production-by-production basis. Guild officials said at the time that, with few exceptions, the contract’s terms followed the guild’s Codified Basic Agreement and Television Agreements.

The Netflix agreement applies to scripted, dramatic episodic and feature-length productions made by Netflix Studios, LLC. Watch on Deadline
