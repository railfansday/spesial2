Good Morning America's Gio Benitez Reveals 'Shocking' Health Diagnosis

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Good Morning America‘s Gio Benitez is speaking out after receiving a “shocking” diagnosis.

Benitez, 41, opened up about his health during the Tuesday, November 17, episode of Good Morning America, revealing he was diagnosed with coronary artery disease after previously hinting at facing issues via social media. He recalled doing a coronary artery calcium scan, which is a noninvasive CT test that detects calcified plaque in the heart’s arteries, and receiving a score of 137.

“It shocked me because I feel totally fine,” he said on Tuesday while reflecting on the “surprising” and “really scary” news, which he received one week earlier. “I eat right, I exercise, I do all the right things.”

Benitez has since found out that he also has “borderline” cholesterol numbers. With other family members having a history of heart attacks, Benitez quickly became worried about his next steps.

“I would lay down in bed and everything was silent … and I would just think, ‘Is this the night my heart stops?’” he recalled. “[My doctor] has assured me that is not the case. She has assured me we’re good, I’m going to live long.”

Benitez, who is married to podcast host Tommy DiDario, elaborated on the diagnosis in an interview with People, saying, “I actually had panic attacks every night for about a week after I heard. My husband would go to sleep or I’d been in my hotel room, because I was traveling for work, and that’s when I would literally just start thinking the worst.”

The journalist’s “heart health trauma” was addressed by his doctor, who helped put Benitez on a treatment plan. (The common treatment includes cholesterol-lowering medications and aspirin, in addition to exercise, a heart-healthy diet, sleep and stress management.)

“I’m feeling really, really good,” Benitez shared on Tuesday. “What she said would happen is happening. So that’s helped put my mind at ease.”

Benitez went on to reflect on how his health journey compared to those of his loved ones.

“I keep asking myself, ‘What would’ve happened if so many of my own family members had known about this?’” he added. “‘What would’ve happened if they were able to address these issues that they faced so much earlier, and this silent killer didn’t kill them?’”

Benitez concluded: “I know that I literally just found this out, but I kind of want to scream it from the rooftops. If there is any risk for you, I want you to do this; I want you to have this conversation with your doctor. Even if you feel fine, something could be lurking that isn’t fine.”
