Megan Moroney Walks 2026 CMA Awards Red Carpet Without Christian Pulisic

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Megan Moroney hit the 2026 CMA Awards red carpet solo amid her rumored romance with soccer pro Christian Pulisic.

The country singer looked like a fairy princess when she arrived at the Bridgestone Arena in Nashville on Wednesday, November 18, wearing a sparkling pink dress.

Moroney, 29, is up for two awards at the 2026 CMAs — Female Vocalist of the Year and Song of the Year for “Beautiful Things.” She’s also set to take the stage for a live performance.

Moroney and Pulisic, 28, sparked dating rumors in July when they were spotted spending time together at Dierks Bentley‘s Whiskey Row in Nashville. At the time, Pulisic also attended one of Moroney’s concerts in Florida.

The “Tennessee Orange” singer added fuel to the speculation in September when she posted photos from a trip to Milan, where Pulisic plays for AC Milan, and wore a jersey from his PUMA collection.

The following month, Moroney was seen leaving the Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show afterparty with her phone wallpaper set as a cozy photo of herself and Pulisic.

Moroney seemingly confirmed her romance with Pulisic at the Time100 Gala in New York City on October 29, where she was overheard chatting about boyfriends with Eiza González, People reported.

“We’re the new generation of WAGs,” González reportedly joked to Moroney. (The actress has been dating Bulgarian professional tennis player Grigor Dimitrov since April 2025.)

Moroney was previously romantically linked to country singer Morgan Wallen in 2022, clarifying on the “Call Her Daddy” podcast at the time that they were never in an exclusive relationship due to their busy schedules.

The “Cloud 9” singer dated what she described as a “normal guy” in 2024, although she never revealed his name.

Moroney also had a short-lived romance with country singer Riley Green, Us confirmed in January.

“Megan and Riley were seeing each other for about three months last summer,” an insider exclusively told Us at the time, adding that the fling “didn’t end on bad terms.”

“Neither of them wanted anything too serious,” the source added.

Green, 38, set the record straight on his relationship status in March 2025, noting on the “In the Blind” podcast that he is single.

Pulisic, meanwhile, was most recently linked to pro golfer Alexa Melton from 2024 to 2026.
