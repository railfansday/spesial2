Keeping One Character Consistent Across Short AI Video Clips

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

I've been making short AI video clips for a side project, and the problem that kept tripping me up was the character changing between shots. Hair colour drifts, the jacket changes, the face looks like a cousin rather than the same person.

What helped most was starting from a reference image rather than text alone. With a consistent character ai video workflow, I upload one or two clear pictures of the character and describe only the action and camera move in the prompt. The look stays anchored to the reference, so I can spend the prompt on motion instead of re-describing the outfit every time.

It's not perfect. Fast motion and hands still go wrong sometimes, and I usually throw away one clip in three. But for short story pieces it saved me a lot of retakes.
