My mom believes comfortable shoes should still look chic, and I couldn’t agree more. She gravitates toward neutral sneakers, classic silhouettes and cozy boots that work just as well for errands as they do for lunch dates. These picks have that understated, expensive-looking feel without sacrificing comfort.

One thing my mom never compromises on is a good pair of jeans. She prefers relaxed silhouettes that don’t feel restrictive but still look polished with a sweater, tucked-in tee or tailored coat. Barrel-leg, wide-leg and slouchy styles are especially good options for creating that effortless off-duty look.

If there’s one thing my mom knows how to do, it’s make a sweater look like a luxury purchase. She loves soft, slightly oversized silhouettes in classic neutrals that can be dressed up or down. I’d look for cashmere blends, simple mock necks and relaxed cardigans that feel timeless rather than overly trendy.

My mom also believes in accessories that make an outfit look more intentional. A structured tote, understated shoulder bag or beautiful cold-weather jacket can instantly elevate even the most basic jeans-and-sweater combination.
