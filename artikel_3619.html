Alix Earle Appears to Shade Tom Brady in New Dating Ad With Pete Davidson

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Alix Earle alluded to her brief romance with Tom Brady in a new ad for dating app Bumble.

The influencer and reality star, 25, teamed up with comedian Pete Davidson for the commercial, shared via Instagram earlier this week, in which she becomes upset when a date with the former Saturday Night Live star goes badly. The reason? He didn’t open a car door for her.

Cut to Earle lamenting on her love life with a group of girlfriends as she declares, “The dating thing just isn’t working. You can’t find a good guy anywhere these days.”

One of Earle’s friends suggests she “try something old,” to which she quips, “I did. He was almost my dad’s age.” (Brady is 49, while Earle’s dad, TJ, is 53.)

Earlier this year, the Earle Meets World star was linked with the NFL legend after they were spotted hanging out during a New Year’s Eve party in St. Bart’s.

“Alix and Tom were together the entire night on New Year’s Eve partying at a party hosted by Palm Tree Crew in St. Bart’s,” a source exclusively told Us in January. “They met through mutual friends who were also in St. Bart’s and had an instant connection. There was a lot of chemistry between them.”

In February, another insider told Us that the influencer and football star were keeping things casual.

“They are hooking up, but it’s not serious,” a source told Us at the time. “It’s been ongoing since they first hung out during New Year’s Eve, and they have really gotten to know each other more in the last month.”

“There’s chemistry and attraction, but neither wants anything serious,” the source continued. “It’s casual. They’re just having fun without overthinking it.”

“She is keeping him young and he is enjoying it. They both will text each other about upcoming plans and where they will be when there are big events happening,” the source added. “But [they] aren’t really hanging out or having much alone time otherwise.”

Earle previously dated New York Giants football player Braxton Berrios for two years before they split in late 2025.

In May, the TikTok star admitted she got emotional after seeing Berrios, 31, at a party six months after they parted ways.

“Obviously, I saw my ex tonight, but we’re on good terms, so it was normal and fine and we were joking around with each other,” the podcaster said in a TikTok video.

Earle continued, “It was funny and fun, and then it wasn’t. It kept turning into me looking around the place the whole night like, ‘Hmm, where is he?’ I think that really sucked, catching myself doing that. I think it’s hard when you see someone for a second and it’s like fun and normal, but that’s just a dose of it, and then that’s done. That started to hit me.”

“Sometimes you just can’t help it. Sometimes you’re just a girl and sometimes water just falls out of your eyes. Sometimes that happens and that’s OK,” she said. “I did the responsible thing and called myself a car home and cried to my dog.”
