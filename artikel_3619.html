GTA 6's Autosave Is Ruining The Game, But Not For The Reason You Think

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

GTA 6 fans say they're losing many hours of progress, but that's not the whole story

Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto 6 might be the best-rated game of all time, but that doesn't mean players love every single thing about the open-world shooter. Some drawbacks, like the game's wonky controls, can be adjusted. Others, players are simply stuck with. One of the biggest complaints is that GTA 6's autosave feature simply isn't good enough.

The issue began attracting attention after high-profile incidents involving Twitch streamers like JasonTheWeen, who lost 12 hours of progress after accidentally turning off his PlayStation 5. It's reasonable to expect that closing a game suddenly might result in some lost progress, but losing 12 hourswas so frustrating that the streamer burst into tears on camera.

Most people aren't losing half a day's worth of progress. Early reports suggest that streamers accessing the game early via the "New Zealand trick" are encountering unforeseen issues with the workaround. Still, many GTA 6 fans were incensed enough about lost playtime to complain about the autosave feature​​​​​​​ on social media.

"4 hours gone, no autosave, no save file..." one fan wrote on a gaming Facebook group. "What the actual! A billion dollar game and this has broken already!"

"I lost 10 hours to their​​​​​​​ dumbass save system," one Redditor alleged.

"What genius came up with this save feature," another complained.

The problem is partially due to a knowledge gap. GTA 6 does autosave​​​​​​​, typically at the start of a mission or at a checkpoint​​​​​​​. If a player elects to spend hours between missions shopping or cruising the streets, GTA 6 won't autosave any of that game time. Players should regularly pause the game and save manually.

The issue isn't just infrequent saving.​​​​​​​ Progress in GTA 6 is slow-going compared to previous games.​​​​​​​ It's harder to steal cars now, and the map is much bigger, so getting anywhere takes time. The roads are narrower, so it's easier to get into a car crash. Many players find themselves struggling with the game's idiosyncratic controls. It's easy to aggravate an NPC by pressing the wrong button, which can set off a chain of unwanted events. Loading an earlier save to undo the damage might also mean losing significant progress.

Many of GTA 6's systems mimic real life, and the autosave issue can make the quiet yet rewarding gameplay feel tedious. Even if a player loses just 20 minutes, during that time they could havewoken up​​​​​​​, worked out, drunk a protein shake, showered, chosena new outfit, and answered some texts​​​​​​​. They spoke to the neighbor, picked up a new quest, and biked to the grocery store. While buying bread,​​​​​​​ they accidentally pulled out a gun and scared the cashier. The worker called the police.

It wouldn't take that long to do it all over again from an earlier save file​​​​​​​, but repeating everything would be a major bummer. The player could try to outrun the cops — but then getting back to what they intended to do might take twice as long. That's assuming that nothing goes wrong during the chase, which is a tall order in the world of GTA.

That propensity for chaos might explain why Rockstar's current system works the way it does. If GTA 6 saved more often, players would be perpetually stuck with the haywire consequences of their actions. There must be a middle ground, though.​​​​​​​ The game could autosave more frequently, across multiple temporary save files. That tweak still wouldn't change GTA 6's focus on the monotony of day-to-day life, which is what fans are truly struggling with.

The likelihood of an update here seems slim since Rockstar has used a similar system for saves across many of its games​​​​​​​. Perhaps the best GTA 6 fans can do is save often​​​​​​​ — and learn to embrace the pandemonium.
