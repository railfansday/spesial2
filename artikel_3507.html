New Resident Evil Remake Collection Officially Announced For 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Having originally launched in March 1996, the Resident Evil franchise is celebrating its 30th anniversary this year, and with twelve mainline titles and over sixty total releases ranging from mobile, spin-offs, and remakes, it is safe to say that the Capcom horror franchise is one of the best.

Most recently, Resident Evil Requiem took 2026 by storm, and here at Screen Rant, our very own Chris Carter awarded the most recent mainline installment an almost perfect 9/10, with his review reading:

Still, playing as two characters that are this starkly different from each other is an experiment that paid off, and worked out far better than it did with Resident Evil 6. Capcom has thrills down to a science at this point, and Resident Evil Requiem delivers. Long-time fans are going to get more out of this entry than the previous two, and the development team seems to have mastered the first-person perspective. Here's hoping a VR mode comes eventually.

Now, nine months later and Resident Evil is back in the spotlight after some remakes made their way to the Nintendo Switch 2 back in October. Courtesy of Capcom, three modern Resident Evil remakes arrived digitally on 16 October with 55.9GB worth of gameplay to get stuck into. This includes: Resident Evil 2 Deluxe Edition, Resident Evil 3, and Resident Evil 4 Gold Edition.

Introducing a brand-new audience to the original titles, they are now available to purchase on the Nintendo eShop, but if you wish to get hold of them digitally, you will have to wait until January 2027. Even then, there has been no confirmation whether this will be a physical cartridge or a digital code. If recent releases *cough cough* Grand Theft Auto VI *cough cough* is anything to go by, then I would not get your hopes up.

The good news here is that the Switch 2 remakes do not just feature the base game, but also include all of the DLC for each game. For example, Resident Evil 4 Gold Edition contains both the base game and its much-loved Separate Ways DLC.

However, we are not just heading to the past when it comes to Resident Evil, as the horror franchise is expected to continue with the launch of the Resident Evil - Code: Veronica remake which is expected to arrive some time in 2027. Yes, it may be another remake, but it is a much-needed one, as the initial release was way back in 2000. For now, it is the only known Resident Evil entry to currently be in development, and will take players back to Rockfort Island and follows the story of siblings, Claire and Chris, after the former is captured by the Umbrella Corporation.

So, if you wish to head back to where it all began with some classic Resident Evil entries, you can now download them all on the Nintendo eShop. However, if you wish to get your hands on their physical counterparts, you will have to wait until January 2027, and hope that it will include a physical cartridge and not just a measly download code.
