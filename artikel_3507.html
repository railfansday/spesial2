Which Past Characters Would Lindsay Lohan Spend Holidays With?

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

If Lindsay Lohan could ring in the holiday season with one of her former beloved characters, she has one — or two — from an iconic film in mind.

“The best would be to have the best of both worlds, which would be Hallie and Annie, because then you’d be in London with all those Christmas lights, and then you’d be in Napa in this beautiful vineyard setting,” Lohan, 40, exclusively told Us of her Parent Trap characters while celebrating the launch of her Marshalls Unwrap the Internet partnership.

Lohan famously portrayed long-lost identical twin sisters in the 1998 remake, with Hallie and Annie growing up with their separated parents in Napa Valley, California, and London, respectively. The movie, directed by Nancy Meyers, followed the girls meeting at a summer sleepaway camp before switching places in a ruse to bring their parents (played by Dennis Quaid and the late Natasha Richardson) back together.

“I would definitely choose them because then you get two for one,” Lohan said to Us. “And that’s also like a Nancy Meyers Christmas. So you can’t really say no to that.”

While fans are still holding out hope that Lohan might reprise her Parent Trap roles in the future, they may be appeased by the actress reuniting with Mean Girls director Mark Waters for a new romantic comedy drama.

“I loved working with Mark again,” Lohan told Us of collaborating on the upcoming film Return to You. “You forget how lucky you are when you get to work with a director who knows you because we have done so many films together. And now doing it at this point in my life, at this stage — just our work ethic is so on point and on par and we get things done so quickly in such a great way.”

Lohan gushed that she is confident Return to You, which also stars Henry Golding, is going to be fantastic” because Waters is “such a great director.”

“It was a bit nostalgic because I was there alone,” she said of filming in Toronto. “So to go there with my family now is really exciting, and for Halloween it was really cute. My son really enjoyed it. We shot at the old Mean Girls house. We ate at some of the same restaurants that I had eaten with my Mean Girls cast at. So it was just a really nice nostalgic trip and everyone’s so lovely in Toronto.”

Offscreen, Lohan is celebrating the holidays with husband Bader Shammas and their 3-year-old son, Luai. This year, traditions in her household are officially kicking off now that Luai has a better understanding of the holiday spirit.

“Traditions are starting officially, kind of, now with our son because he’s now more aware of Santa and reindeer. A tradition that we have is we always watch Rudolph together — the original old one, the old cartoon one,” Lohan told Us, adding that there’s “nothing better” than seeing her little one experience the holiday magic.

“We read a lot of Christmas books. Wrapping the presents is going to be the fun part,” she continued. “And just seeing his face on Christmas morning.”

Before the holidays are officially underway, Lohan partnered with Marshalls and well-known influential personalities and creators to create gift guides for all the different types of people (think: wellness, reality TV, travel and more) in shoppers’ lives.

“I love giving gifts and I think any time that we can make gifting easier in one space in one store is a blessing,” Lindsay told Us. “I love a thoughtful holiday campaign where we can help people get gifts for the ones that they love and the right kind of gifts instead of kind of wondering, ‘Oh my God, what do I get?’”
