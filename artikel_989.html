Alcaraz shrugs off fitness issues as he beats Cobolli in 65 minutes

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Carlos Alcaraz will now take on Ben Shelton in the semi-finals of the ATP Finals; he topped the Ivan Lendl Group with a flawless 3-0 record after beating Flavio Cobolli on Friday; watch the ATP Finals on Sky Sports

Carlos Alcaraz didn't appear bothered by his various fitness issues as he extended his winning streak to 12 matches after beating debutant Flavio Cobolli 6-4 6-1 at the ATP Finals on Friday in just 65 minutes.

Alcaraz had already booked his spot in the semi-finals of the tournament following his victory against Daniil Medvedev, with the Spaniard now set to face Ben Shelton at the elite eight-man end-of-season event.

The seven-time Grand Slam champion broke the Italian's serve in the opening game of both sets and ended points quickly on his way to posting a flawless 3-0 record in the round-robin stage.

Alcaraz needed just 65 minutes to win his latest match and clocked up 29 winners in the process.

"I tried to play as aggressive as I can; hitting the ball as hard as I could, trying not to move that much," Alcaraz said.

Alcaraz had an elastic black band on his injured left thigh and a strip of tape on his right knee.

He appeared to sustain the injury during his match against Medvedev. It heightened concerns about his fitness, with Alcaraz having already missed four and a half months of the season due to a right wrist injury.

"The challenge now is not on the court, it's off the court," Alcaraz said. "To do whatever it takes to be in a good shape, to feel physically ready for tomorrow.

"It's going to be a really important half a day that I have ahead of me with my team, with my physio, with the doctor," Alcaraz added.

The semi-final will be a rematch of the US Open quarter-final between Shelton and Alcaraz. While Shelton won that five-set clash in New York, Alcaraz recently got revenge at the Paris Masters final.

In the other semi-final, top-ranked Alexander Zverev will face the winner of tonight's clash between Medvedev and Frances Tiafoe, which is live on Sky Sports Tennis,

Alcaraz is attempting to win the trophy at the ATP Finals for the first time. He lost to Jannik Sinner, who is currently out with a right knee injury, in the final a year ago.

"The way that I felt on the court today was incredible," Alcaraz said. "I'm getting more confident after every match."

"It was an incredible shot-making extravaganza from Alcaraz.

"He's got that in his locker; we knew he was into the semi-finals, but if he was to write the script for a match that he came into with a bit of an injury, I mean, that was absolutely perfect.

"It was an hour and five minutes; he didn't really have any extended rallies. I think there were only one or two that were over nine shots. His timing was exquisite, so job done today, that's for sure."

"Cobolli has had a tough few days here. Obviously, playing at home and the matches haven't gone his way.

"He just didn't have enough in his locker to have an impact on Alcaraz."
