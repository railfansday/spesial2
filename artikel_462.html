The Pokémon TCG Perfect Order Booster Bundle is back under $45 — save before Delta Reign drops

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Perfect Order Booster Bundle is on sale for $44.88 at Amazon, down from its $59.99 list price.

With the Pokémon TCG's Delta Reign expansion arriving on Nov. 6, there’s still time to catch up on older modern expansions like Perfect Order, with its six-pack Booster Bundle now pushed back under the $45 mark — making it one of the most affordable readily available options for trading card collectors right now.

As of Nov. 2, the Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Perfect Order Booster Bundle is priced at just $44.88 at Amazon. That's $15.11 less than its usual $59.99 list price, giving you 25% off and bringing the cost of each booster pack to $7.48.

That’s only 25 cents above the $44.63 offer we covered in October, and comes through third-party seller Bleacher Seats Sportscards & Collectibles, with free delivery currently set for Nov. 10 at the time of writing.

Walmart currently has the Perfect Order Booster Bundle for $41.99 with free shipping — while TCGplayer has listings charging as little as $39.99. The latter is only $3.92 above its estimated market price.

The bundle contains six boosters from the Pokémon Legends: Z-A-inspired Perfect Order expansion, each featuring 10 random cards, a Basic Energy, and a Pokémon TCG Live code card.

Among the expansion’s most valuable chase cards (still available to buy individually on TCGplayer) are the $85+ SIR Meowth ex, the $80+ MHR Mega Zygarde ex, and the $40+ SIR Mega Zygarde ex.
