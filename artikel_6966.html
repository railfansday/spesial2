The 9 Best Wine Advent Calendars of 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Every product was carefully curated by an Esquire editor. We may earn a commission from these links. Here’s how we test products and why you should trust us.

Turns out holiday cheer comes with tasting notes.

Being an adult means upgrading from Advent calendars filled with chocolates and candies to ones filled with wine and booze (though we thoroughly love a good chocolate Advent calendar—who doesn’t?). You get the same childhood thrill of opening a little door, only now there’s something on the other side that can really make your spirits bright.

And when it comes to wine Advent calendars, there are more options than you might think. Whether you’re loyal to reds, partial to whites, or just here for the bubbles, there’s a wine-filled calendar with your name on it—or someone else’s, if you can bring yourself to give it away as a gift. Some even include sommelier-led tasting videos, so you can raise your wine IQ.

So go ahead and snag a few of the best wine advent calendars below. Due to state laws—hello North Carolina—some items can’t be shipped to certain states, so make sure to double-check shipping restrictions before trying to place your order.

Don’t let the name fool you: Give Them Beer makes some of the best wine (as well as other spirit-filled) Advent calendars around. Unlike most other wine Advent calendars that only offer a single glass per night, this version contains a different half-size wine bottle behind every door. It’s a great option for couples—or really anyone who enjoys a heavy pour.

Shipping: Available for preorder now; shipping begins in November.

Give Them Beer's sparkling-wine Advent calendar is the perfect way for any wine lover to cheers to the season. The 12-night countdown contains mini bottles of Champagne, Prosecco, Brut, and other bubbles from popular European wineries like Chandon, La Marca, Freixenet, Mionetto and Ruffino.

Shipping: Available for preorder now; shipping begins in late October.

Interested in expanding your palate and learning more about wine from sommeliers? Vinebox’s wine Advent calendars let you do just that. Choose from the 12 Nights Velvet Collection (which features eight bold red wines and four whites) and the 12 Nights Vine Collection (which features whites, reds, and a rosé), or get the 24 Nights of Wine Advent calendar, which contains both collections. You’ll get access to sommelier-led wine tasting videos for each wine in the collection for an educational and delicious experience.

Shipping: Available for preorder now; shipping begins November 2.

What wine lover wouldn’t love counting down to Christmas with a different miniature bottle of wine each night? This 24-night calendar includes reds, whites, and rosés from around the globe—from a Brut Rosé to a Cabernet Sauvignon to a Pinot Grigio.

Shipping: Available to order now; shipping begins in late October.

Perfect for any red-wine devotee, this 24-night countdown contains a range of reds from France, Italy, Napa, and Sonoma, to name a few locations. Order during the presale to save $15 (which you can inevitably use to buy another bottle).

Shipping: Available to order now; shipping begins in late October.

If they’re keen on canned wine, snag this 12-day calendar from Just Enough Wines featuring eight traditional wines (with two of them doubled) and two non-alcoholic wines. Its sustainably crafted California Central Coast wine varietals include bubbles, whites, rosés, and reds, so there’s something for everyone in the box. We’re partial to the Brut Bubbles and Sauvignon Blanc.

If you’ve been seeing a bunch of mini wine bottles in these Advent calendars and thought, well that certainly won’t be enough wine for me, you’ll want to check out Harry & David’s Advent calendar filled with 12 full-size (750-ml) bottles of the brand’s beloved wine. It’s a great choice if you’re planning on sharing the love with someone else (or a group of people).

Looking to discover some cool under-the-radar wines? Maker’s Advent calendar includes various reds, whites, rosés, and sparkling wines from female and/or minority-led wineries—so you can use your dollars to support small businesses and most likely try something new. The 12 cans included equate to roughly four bottles of wine.

Shipping: Available for preorder now; shipping begins November 6.

If they (or, let’s be honest, you) prefer more variety, try this Advent calendar. It features 24 unique wines sourced from around the world, ranging from crisp whites to full-bodied reds. Priced at just $90, it’s one of the more affordable wine Advent calendars available, so grab one for yourself and one for a fellow wine lover.
