What Celtic are 'ruing' amid poor form and just three wins in last 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Celtic have been left "ruing" the hierachy's failure to equip Martin O'Neill with the transfers he sought early on in the summer.

That's the view of Alex Rae who believes the Hoops are struggling with poor results and performances as a result of a lack of planning and signings early doors.

Celts spent big on the likes of Camilo Duran, Kasper Hogh and Mika Baur before a late flurry in the window to bring in loan players while being unable to strengthen to replace Arne Engels with a defensive minded midfielder.

That has come back to bite the league leaders and they have won just three of their last 10 fixtures in all competitions.

Rae reckons, after their most recent 2-0 defeat to Falkirk, that the Glasgow giants might regret their business or lack thereof in the signing stakes. And the higher-ups will know it.

"I think John McGlynn's done an incredible job but I'd imagine a lot of the calls will be about Celtic and their current form," Rae said on Superscoreboard.

"It's been very indifferent to say the least and there's been people clamouring for the hierarchy to support the manager a lot quicker than the window just closing.

"It didn't quite happen and now they're ruing that because they're not getting the performances on the pitch."
