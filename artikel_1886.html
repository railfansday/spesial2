Arsenal have new Martin Odegaard 'concerns' as contract talks stall - expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Arsenal are having doubts about Martin Odegaard's long-term role in Mikel Arteta's side amid ongoing talks over a new contract.

The 27-year-old has made 18 apparances across all competitions this season, and has started all 11 Premier League games, with his place in the XI said to be secure.

However, his recent performances have not been up to his usual standard, and doubts are beginning to emerge about his role as an undroppable under Arteta's guidance.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Arsenal could be set for a midfield reshuffle in an attempt to get the best out of their options.

Bruno Guimaraes' arrival from Newcastle has kickstarted the concerns, while Declan Rice's new role has not yet yielded results, and Arteta may be forced into a change.

Meanwhile, talks over a new contract for Odegaard have dragged on for longer than many had expected, raising questions about what the future holds at the Emirates.

Brown told Football Insider in the latter stages of last season at Odegaard is facing pressure on his position, with Arteta eyeing long-term changes in the middle of the park.

Guimaraes' arrival has added to the hints that a reshuffle could be on the cards, which could see Odegaard drop out to accomodate a change in shape and style under Arteta.

A difficult performance against Newcastle United last time out has raised concerns once again about Odegaard's place, while contract talks stalling is not a good sign.

"It's a strange one with Martin Odegaard, this is something to keep an eye on," Brown told Football Insider.

"I've been hearing whispers for a while now that Arsenal could change up what they're doing with the midfield, and there have already been adjustments this season.

"Declan Rice has been playing a bit deeper to allow the other midfielders to operate further forward, but I'm not convinced that has paid off.

"There are some concerns, as well, about whether Odegaard can play in midfield with Bruno Guimaraes, because they play quite a similar role in Arteta's plans.

"So eventaully, he will have to make a decision about Odegaard's position and whether he will stay as one of the undroppable players in that side.

"At the same time, there have been delays in talks over a new contract which could be nothing, but it's never a good sign, because they'd want to get him tied down.

"So I can see Arteta switching up what Arsenal have been doing in midfield, maybe changing the starting XI a bit more, so let's see how that impacts Odegaard.

"I think the Newcastle game highlighted Odegaard's problems again, he doesn't show up when the going gets tough, and Arsenal can't afford to carry passengers."

Despite hints that Arteta could look to change his midfield plans at Arsenal, calling into question Odegaard's long-term role, contract talks remain a priority for the Gunners.

Having already tied Arteta, Bukayo Saka and Declan Rice to new deals in the last few months, they have now set their sights on agreeing further extensions.

Football Insider revealed last month that Arsenal are doing everything in their power to agree terms with Odegaard and are determined to keep him at the club long-term.

The Gunners are pushing to extend the midfielder's stay at the Emirates Stadium, with his current deal set to expire in the summer of 2028.

Despite recent delays meaning talks have taken longer than expected, there is still believed to be a willingness from all sides to reach an agreement.
