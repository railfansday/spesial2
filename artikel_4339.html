Where To Find Every Resource In Loftia

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Tracking down crafting materials can be a little tedious in Loftia, especially when you're not entirely sure where to start looking. The map is split into four distinct areas, each with its own collection of useful resources to help you with your crafting and building projects.

From Glass Coils and Scrap Wire at the Solar Farm to Portobello Mushrooms, knowing where to look will save you plenty of wandering around. Some materials are simply lying on the ground, while others require a little extra work, like breaking microwaves with a hammer or washing machines to get what you need. Here's where to find every resource and what you'll need to collect them!

Below, we detail each resource/material and where to find it within Loftia City!

Head to the Solar Farm in the northwest corner of the map. Break microwaves with a hammer to collect Glass Coils.

Visit the Solar Farm in the northwest corner of the map. Scrap Wire can be found sitting on tables and scattered across the ground. You can also occasionally get some by breaking microwaves with a hammer.

Head to the Solar Farm in the northwest corner of the map. Tires are found outside, often near trees.

Travel to the Hills in the northeast corner of the map, just east of Pebble. Portobello Mushrooms are scattered throughout the grassy area, and you'll need shears to collect them.

Visit the Hills in the northeast corner of the map. You can find Plant Fiber throughout the area, particularly in tall grass and grass bundles.

Head to the Hills in the northeast corner of the map. Search the bushes throughout the area to find Wild Berries.

Travel to the Construction Site in the southeast corner of the map. Look for the small wooden boxes scattered throughout the area and break them to collect Scrap Wood.

Visit the Construction Site in the southeast corner of the map. Clay can be found in several spots scattered across the ground throughout the area.

Head to the Scrap Quarter in the southwest corner of the map, near Milo. Screws can be found scattered throughout the area.

Visit the Scrap Quarter in the southwest corner of the map, near Milo. Look for piles of debris containing bottles to find Plastic Discards.

Travel to the Scrap Quarter in the southwest corner of the map, near Milo. Break the washing machines scattered throughout the area to collect Metal
