Starbucks is unveiling a holiday mystery-box Bearista glass cold cup collection

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Each $39.95 hand-blown cup comes with one of five bear characters, and buyers in coffeehouses can take up to two boxes

Starbucks $SBUX is launching a limited-edition Bearista Glass Cold Cup Surprise Box collection at select coffeehouses around the world on Tuesday, the company said. Each box holds one of five holiday-themed bear characters, and each cup costs $39.95 while supplies last.

The cups are made of hand-blown glass, Starbucks said. Customers are capped at two Surprise Boxes apiece when buying in coffeehouses.

The five characters are Reiny, Joy, Aspen, Bunny and Goldie. Every cup is packaged with a personality card, according to the company, that lays out the bear's mood, its favorite holiday ritual and the Starbucks drink it typically orders.

Starbucks described Aspen as a skiing bear fond of early-morning runs down the slopes and relaxing at the lodge afterward. Reiny is a reindeer who likes to watch classic holiday movies, and Joy is an ice-skating bear who collects snow globes. Bunny is a snow bunny that enjoys strolling through the snow, and its cup shifts hues when a cold drink is poured in. Goldie is a golden bear that the company called the rarest of the group.

Starbucks set aside a limited quantity for Starbucks Rewards Reserve members on the Starbucks Shop, starting Monday at 1 p.m. ET. Members are limited to one box each.

Starbucks Shop opens to every shopper on Tuesday, the company said, and will stock Bearista-themed holiday clothing such as hoodies, sweatshirts and T-shirts.

The Surprise Box Edition joins the Soccer Bearista, Pink Bearista and Snoopy glass cold cups, which Starbucks introduced this year. The collection is part of the company’s broader 2026 holiday merchandise lineup, according to Fox 5 New York.

The original Bearista Glass Cold Cup is due back on Dec. 1 at certain U.S. coffeehouses, though only a limited number will be available and only until stock runs out, according to Fox 5. That cup is modeled on a petite bear, and the upper portion of the glass is styled as a winter hat in Starbucks green, Fox 5 said.
