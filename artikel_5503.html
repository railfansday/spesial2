Lemonade Expands Renters Insurance to Minnesota, Completing Nationwide Availability

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Renters is the first Lemonade product available in all 50 states and Washington, D.C.

NEW YORK, Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Lemonade (NYSE: LMND) today announced the availability of its renters insurance in Minnesota. With this launch, Lemonade Renters is now available in all 50 states and Washington, D.C., making it the first Lemonade product offered nationwide.

Renters insurance remains central to the company's offering. Lemonade Renters is designed to make protecting personal belongings simple and accessible, pairing customizable coverage with a fully digital experience.

"From the start, we built Lemonade Renters to remove the complexity that makes traditional insurance frustrating, and to offer affordable coverage that renters can buy and manage on their own terms," said Dan Timsit, Head of Renters Insurance at Lemonade. "With Minnesota, that experience is now available to every renter in the country. Our team reached this milestone one state at a time, and we're proud to mark it today."

Lemonade Renters coverage starts at $5 per month. Through the Lemonade app or website, renters can get a quote, purchase a policy, adjust their coverage and file a claim in one place, without paperwork or a middleman. Renters may also lower their premium by bundling with other Lemonade products, adding qualifying home safety devices, or choosing annual billing. Coverage limits and deductibles may vary by state.

Minnesota renters can get a quote at lemonade.com or in the Lemonade app.
