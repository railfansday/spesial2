Jason Statham & Guy Ritchie Reunite For Netflix Feature ‘Dogfight’

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Jason Statham will star in Dogfight, a new Netflix feature written, directed, and produced by Guy Ritchie.

The streamer has said production is slated to begin in early 2027, with filming taking place in the UK. The production companies are Black Bear, Toff Guy Films, and Punch Palace. Producing alongside Ritchie are Statham, Ivan Atkinson, and John Friedberg.

The film is described as a “stylish revenge actioner set in the London criminal underworld.”

Dogfight will be the seventh film collaboration between Ritchie and Statham. Their film credits include Lock, Stock and Two Smoking Barrels, Snatch, and the upcoming film Viva La Madness. Viva La Madness is based on the novel of the same name by J.J. Connolly, who co-wrote the screenplay for the film adaptation with Ritchie. Watch on Deadline

Viva La Madness is a sequel to Connolly’s debut novel, Layer Cake, which Matthew Vaughn previously adapted for the screen. Starring in the film alongside Statham are Vinnie Jones (Snatch, Lock, Stock and Two Smoking Barrels), Jason Isaacs (White Lotus, Harry Potter), Babs Olusanmokun (Dune, The Ministry of Ungentlemanly Warfare), Camila Mendes (Riverdale, Palm Springs), Ben Foster (Motor City, Hell or High Water), Jonny Lee Miller (The Crown, Elementary), and Raúl Alejandro, making his feature film debut.

Viva La Madness was developed by Statham for Punch Palace Productions and Thomas Benski for Lumina Studios, who are producing alongside Ritchie and Ivan Atkinson for Toff Guy Films and John Friedberg for Black Bear. Black Bear will distribute the film domestically, while Amazon MGM Studios will distribute it in the majority of international territories.
