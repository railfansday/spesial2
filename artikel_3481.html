Sydney Sweeney’s R-Rated Sequel Officially Begins Its Next Chapter

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Sydney Sweeney has certainly been keeping herself busy, and one of her biggest recent hits is already getting a follow-up. The Euphoria star has spent the last few years moving between romantic comedies, horror movies, and thrillers, with plenty of attention following her wherever she goes. Her latest franchise has given her another chance to play a character who discovers that working for wealthy people can be a very dangerous business. And judging by the speed at which the sequel has moved forward, audiences won't be waiting around forever to see what happens next.

The original film stars Sweeney (Euphoria) as Millie Calloway, Amanda Seyfried (Mamma Mia!) as Nina Winchester, Brandon Sklenar (1923) as Andrew Winchester, Michele Morrone (365 Days) as Enzo, Elizabeth Perkins (Big) as Evelyn Winchester, Amanda Joy Erickson as Lexi, and Megan Ferguson (The Comedians) as Jillian.

It followed Millie Calloway, a young woman with a troubled past who accepts a job working as a live-in maid for a wealthy family. Unfortunately, what initially seems like an opportunity to rebuild her life quickly becomes a nightmare as she discovers that her new employers are hiding some deeply disturbing secrets.

The film's success inevitably resulted in a sequel, The Housemaid's Secret, which began production in late October. Sweeney will return as Millie alongside Morrone as Enzo, with Kirsten Dunst, Paul Anthony Kelly, Brittany Snow, and Stephen Kalyn joining the cast.

Sweeney has officially been spotted filming The Housemaid's Secret, the sequel to her 2025 thriller The Housemaid, in Hoboken, New Jersey. According to NJ.com, production took place around 10th Street and Park Avenue on October 14 and 15.

The sequel adapts Freida McFadden's 2023 novel of the same name, which follows Millie Calloway as she takes another housekeeping job. This time, however, her employer has a wife who remains hidden behind a locked door, and Millie soon discovers that something deeply disturbing is happening inside the house. The production has also been spotted at William Paterson University in Wayne, New Jersey, where Sweeney filmed scenes earlier in October. Local filming permits and temporary street closures were arranged in Hoboken to accommodate the shoot.

The sequel follows the success of the original movie, which adapted McFadden's bestselling 2022 novel. The book series also includes The Housemaid Is Watching, giving the film franchise more material to explore if the second installment proves successful.

The Housemaid's Secret is currently in production and does not yet have a confirmed release date. The original The Housemaid was released in theaters in December 2025.
