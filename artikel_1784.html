Wycombe v Bradford City: Scotland keeper Jon McCracken's double mission - in front of watching Sheffield Wednesday legend

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The priority is helping to ensure Bradford City remain in the top-six picture between now and the end of February.

He has certainly been doing his bit regarding that thus far and is currently some way ahead in the League One Golden Glove stakes with a mightily-impressive 10 clean sheets in 14 games - while also providing assists for goals in the recent home games against Barnsley and Doncaster Rovers.

The proud Scot is also conscious of country matters, having been called up for Scotland's past two camps.

The next international break is in March and he is aiming to impress under the watchful eye of Scotland goalkeeper coach - former Sheffield Wednesday favourite Chris Woods.

Glasgow-born McCracken said: "I feel like I've performed well in training when I'm there and I've a good relationship with all the players and staff.

"So hopefully, I can show signs that when I'm at Bradford, I'm worthy of staying in that squad and thankfully the manager has shown that faith in me.

"The goalie coach is someone I am in regular contact with and he's not far from this area and he will come to games and speak with Doyler (Colin Doyle) as well, so that's good. They are in contact and keeping tabs on me hopefully.

"They are in touch and it's one of those, you just focus on Bradford at the moment and then when March comes, I can worry about that."

Playing in marquee third-tier games for City has raised the 26-year-old's profile, and the club's healthy league position is something the ex-Dundee man hopes will work in his favour.

Speaking ahead of the trip to Wycombe, he added: "That can only be a positive thing when you are playing important games in this league, which we are every week.

"Then, you are in the hunt for something at the end of the season hopefully. Playing in these games and pressures is only a benefit to you really.

"For me, representing your country at any level is probably the pinnacle. It's the cherry on the cake. Hopefully I can remain in that conversation."
