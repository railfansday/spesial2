Alisson open to signing new Liverpool contract

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Alisson has declared he would rather stay at Liverpool than leave the club as a free agent at the end of the season.

The goalkeeper joined Liverpool from Roma in 2018 and has gone on to play a key role for the Reds during their successes under both Jurgen Klopp and Arne Slot.

Alisson has played 347 times for Liverpool, keeping 141 clean sheets and even scoring for the Reds. He netted a late winner against West Brom in the 2020-21 season.

However, the two-time Premier League champion is out of contract at the end of this season, and was of interest to Juventus this past summer.

But the Brazilian wants to stay at Liverpool.

"Yes, in my mind," Alisson said when asked if he wants to stay at the club.

"I'm focused on playing every game but in my mind, I'm happy to stay. You know, that depends on a lot of aspects and different situations.

"But my will is to stay. I'm happy here, my family is happy here. I'm still hungry for titles and to deliver good performances.

"I still want to play at the top level for as long as I can. So that's my thinking on that."

Alisson has featured 14 times for Liverpool this term, keeping four clean sheets and registering a 62.3% save ratio.

The 34-year-old made some impressive stops as Liverpool beat Crystal Palace 3-1 on Sunday to move fourth in the Premier League.

When asked about Liverpool's start under Andoni Iraola, Alisson added: "I think you have to manage all the situations.

"When you are doing well, you need to control the excitement, so you don't get overexcited. Our reality is that we're not doing as well as we can.

"It doesn't mean that we are not doing well – we are doing well in so many games – but the consistency is not there yet.

"The moment for us is to control the frustration that the defeats bring and stay with a clear mind, looking for the quality in our team.

"We have a top manager, top players. We are capable of great things, but as a team we need to get to a point where we feel: 'We are okay now. We have the confidence. We have everything that's needed to win.'

"We are building that. I hope we get there really quickly, but it takes time. We have to be patient but we players, we don't have time to waste.

"We want to win, we want to be Premier League champions, Champions League champions, EFL Cup champions, FA Cup champions.

"We want to win everything with this club because this is special, and it's what we're here for."
