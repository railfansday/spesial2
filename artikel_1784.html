Liverpool lead Lamine Camara race

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

According to Caught Offside, Liverpool have emerged as the frontrunners to sign Lamine Camara, with the Monaco midfielder increasingly viewed as a priority target ahead of the January transfer window.

Liverpool have apparently already held discussions with Camara’s representatives and that negotiations are progressing positively. The Reds have done significant groundwork on a potential deal and are close to reaching an agreement on personal terms with the Senegal international’s camp.

Camara was heavily linked with Chelsea during the summer, but a move failed to materialize. Suggestions that Liverpool had already agreed terms with the player at that stage are incorrect, with Monaco’s transfer plans elsewhere ultimately contributing to the collapse of the proposed Chelsea deal.

While Chelsea have not completely ruled out reviving their interest, Liverpool are currently regarded as the more likely destination. The Blues have reportedly shifted their focus towards other midfield targets, including Bournemouth’s Alex Scott, while Martin Zubimendi of Arsenal remains an alternative option.

Camara is also reportedly admired by Arsenal, Manchester United, Real Madrid and Bayern Munich. However, any club hoping to sign the 22-year-old will likely need to meet Monaco’s asking price of around €70 million.
