49ers Share Update After Colton McKivitz Suffers Grotesque Injury on ‘SNF'

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The San Francisco 49ers issued an update on the status of offensive lineman Colton McKivitz after he was carted off the field following a grotesque ankle injury on Sunday Night Football.

“We think it’s broken and dislocated,” head coach Kyle Shanahan said after the team’s 35-10 victory over the Minnesota Vikings in Mexico City on Sunday, November 22.

Due to the severity of the injury, McKivitz, 30, will almost assuredly miss the rest of the season.

“That was really crushing,” Shanahan, 46, added. “Colton has had an unbelievable year. … It was tough to see Colton go down like that.”

McKivitz suffered the injury during the game’s second quarter, when his leg and ankle got rolled up during a tackle on a run by teammate Christian McCaffrey. The lineman’s ankle bent at a horrifying angle, which was briefly visible on the NBC broadcast. No further replays were shown.

Players from both teams were noticeably upset, with 49ers left tackle Trent Williams slamming his helmet to the ground.

An air cast was placed on McKivitz’s leg and he was carted to the locker room. No official updates about his condition have been made available.

“It’s brutal,” McCaffrey, 30, said after the game. “He’s somebody that is one of the heart and souls of this team, a guy who shows up every day with the right attitude. He’s the standard, and we’re going to really miss him. We all love him. We’re praying for him.”

The running back added, “It’s one of the worst parts about this game is stuff like that, but we love him and we’re going to play for him from here on out.”

McKivitz was in the midst of his sixth season in the NFL and with the 49ers. He signed a three-year, $45 million contract extension with San Francisco in September 2025, which came after previously being cut by the team in August 2021.

“I don’t want to leave here,” McKivitz said after signing his extension. “Obviously being cut, I’ve got some scars there, but there was a reason why I was drafted here, why I was cut here, and why I still play here.”

He added, “There’s a sense of family. When you have a core group of players who understand a winning culture and understand the standard of what this franchise was built on … it’s hard for guys to turn that away.”

The 49ers return to action when they host the Seattle Seahawks on Sunday, November 29.
