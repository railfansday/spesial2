Hottest 2026 CMAs Couples: Lainey Wilson and Husband Duck and More Sizzle

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

In celebration of a musical genre dominated by make-you-cry-in-your-beer love songs, it’s only fitting that the 2026 CMA Awards were packed with the hottest couples in country music entertainment.

Lainey Wilson sizzled on the red carpet alongside husband Devlin “Duck” Hodges before heading inside Bridgestone Arena in Nashville to host the 60th annual ceremony. (This is the first CMAs the couple has attended as a married couple, following their May nuptials.)

Luke Combs and wife Nicole Combs were all loved up as they posed for photos on Wednesday, November 18.

Vanderpump Villa’s Marciano Brunette and Secret Lives of Mormon Wives’ Jessi Draper, meanwhile, decided to color coordinate for country music’s biggest night, hosted just off the iconic Broadway street in Music City.
