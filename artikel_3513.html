William Shatner Reacts to Director John Carpenter's 'Piece of S***' Jab

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

William Shatner has broken his silence after director John Carpenter called him a “piece of s***” following Shatner’s alleged slight during their first meeting.

“I live in infamy due to the fact that I didn’t look up when you said hello to me,” Shatner, 95, wrote via an Instagram statement on Friday, November 27. “I don’t know why I didn’t look up, but obviously I didn’t hear you.”

The Star Trek actor cheekily added, “I was probably reading a great review of one of your movies and it occupied my attention to the point where I didn’t look up.”

Shatner was referring to Carpenter’s recent claim that he ignored him completely when the Halloween director, 78, attempted to introduce himself at a convention in Orlando, Florida.

“This guy, he’s sitting in the greenroom, on his phone like this [scrolling]. And I just thought, ‘Well, I’m going to go in and introduce myself,’”, Carpenter recalled during a November 18 appearance on the “Dropping Names” podcast. “I said, ‘Hello, Mr. Shatner, I’m John Carpenter.’”

According to Carpenter, Shatner stared at his phone and simply said, “pleasure,” but didn’t “look up from the phone.”

“This piece of s*** did not look up from the phone … He’s an a**hole,” Carpenter declared following the alleged incident.

Shatner addressed the allegations in his statement, claiming, “I would have looked up because you are obviously one of the great directors in film and I admire you so much, and I would’ve relished the chance to talk to you.”

The Miss Congeniality actor insisted that the “next time you see me, please come over and introduce yourself, because I really don’t know what you look like and apparently you know what I look like.”

Shatner added, “But I would enjoy saying hello to you, and this piece of s*** would confide in you his admiration.”

He concluded by writing, “My Best, William Shatner.”

In the caption of the social media statement, Shatner tagged Carpenter and implored him to read the full message. “Love, Bill,” the TV star added.

Hours later, Carpenter responded to Shatner’s message by posting his own Instagram statement addressed to the Boston Legal alum, seemingly burying the hatchet.

“I met you in Orlando, or I should say, I said hello to you and introduced myself in Orlando. You would not look me in the eye. Maybe it’s a Canadian thing,” Carpenter wrote on Friday. “I’m willing to give you another chance. I’m nothing if not generous.”

The filmmaker continued, “Regardless, the next time I see you at a convention, I will happily introduce myself again, and we can actually start our relationship.”

Carpenter concluded with a nod to Shatner’s iconic Star Trek character, writing, “After all, even Captain Kirk deserves a second chance at first contact.”
