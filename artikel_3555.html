Man Killed Estranged Wife in Murder-Suicide, Prompting Lockdowns at Schools

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Authorities in Iowa said that a man fatally shot his estranged wife before he took his own life in an apparent murder-suicide, which prompted nearby schools to go into a precautionary lockdown.

Emmet County 911 Dispatch received a call on Friday, November 13, reporting that two gunshots were heard around 2:03 p.m. near the Good Samaritan Center in Estherville, Iowa, according to Northwest Iowa Now.

Dispatchers received another call around the same time reporting that an unresponsive woman had been found bleeding in a driveway.

Estherville Police Department officers and the Estherville Ambulance then responded to a nearby home and determined that the victim, Rhonda Oleson, had died from her injuries.

Following a brief investigation, officials determined that the victim, 57, had been shot by her estranged husband, Greg Oleson, and authorities set out on a search for the suspect.

The nearby Estherville Lincoln Central Schools and the Good Samaritan Center were placed on precautionary lockdowns to make sure that students, staff, residents and visitors remained safe as officers looked for Greg, 58, who fled to a rural area.

Authorities used investigative technology to locate Greg’s vehicle in Emmet County. As officers approached his vehicle, they found him deceased after he apparently died by suicide.

Officers eventually determined that there was no threat to the public, and the lockdowns were lifted.

It’s not currently clear what evidence authorities found to name Greg as the suspect in the case. Authorities have also not shared many details about the murder-suicide, including what events may have led up to the fatal shooting.

Additionally, it’s not known how long ago the couple got married, how long they had been separated for at the time of the killings or if they shared children together.

The Estherville Police Department and Emmet County Sheriff’s Office worked with the Iowa Division of Criminal Investigation, Iowa State Patrol, Estherville Ambulance, Emmet County Emergency Management, Estherville Fire Department, the Emmet County Medical Examiner’s Office, Emmet County Attorney’s Office and the Iowa State Medical Examiner’s Office amid the initial investigation.

Authorities also credited local citizens and neighbors for helping as officers worked to determine what had happened and to ensure the safety of the public.

The investigation into the case remains ongoing as authorities work to determine more details about the murder-suicide.
