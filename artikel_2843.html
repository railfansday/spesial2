New York Jets Urged to Make Blockbuster Trade in Latest NFL Mock Draft

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The New York Jets finally found a quarterback, but it still hasn't led to a winning effort. After tossing an NFL-high 17 interceptions last season, Geno Smith has thrown just three picks in 10 starts, but he's only gotten two wins and eight losses to show for it.

He's also 36 years old and in the final year of his contract. Smith could very well return for another season in 2027, but there's no guarantee he'd remain the starter all season long. Not to mention, since the Jets are armed with three first-round picks in the 2027 NFL Draft, chances are they'll be adding a potential franchise QB of the future. Doing so would surely place Smith's future in question, and that's if he re-signs for another season.

Yet, some feel like the Jets can't afford to sit around, they need to be proactive in securing their next signal caller, even if it means making a blockbuster trade to pull it off.

While the Jets won't be making the playoffs again, they will be in a strong position to drastically change their franchise's outlook throughout the 2027 NFL Draft. While they could stick and pick right where their draft choices fall, what if there's a prospect that general manager Darren Mougey just has to have? In that case, the Jets might just launch up the draft board, and doing so for the team's next franchise QB just might pay off.

Recently, ESPN's NFL Draft expert Jordan Reid suggested that the Jets move up a couple slots in the draft order to select Oklahoma State's standout QB Drew Mestemaker. In the proposed trade, the Jets would send the fourth overall pick, No. 13, and a third-round selection in 2028 to trade up to the No. 2 pick, exchanging with the Los Angeles Chargers in the process. Being that the Bolts have Justin Herbert, they may be a good trade candidate to negotiate with.

"Mestemaker has progressed from a walk-on who didn't start in high school to my top QB in this class. The Jets have used the past three drafts to establish an offensive base, selecting bookend tackles (Olu Fashanu and Armand Membou), wide receiver Omar Cooper Jr. and tight end Kenyon Sadiq in Round 1. Along with star wideout Garrett Wilson, they make New York an ideal landing spot for a rookie signal-caller. Mestemaker excels at pushing the ball down the field, with his 21 completions of 20 or more yards this season ranking eighth in the FBS. His decision-making is inconsistent at times, but Mestemaker is young (he's in his first season as a Power 4 starter) and has the tools to transform the Jets."

As Reid astutely pointed out, the Jets have spent the past few drafts solidifying their offensive core, to the point where the biggest piece left to address is at the quarterback position. With three first-round picks, chances are, the Jets will be drafting another quarterback in April, the question is who?

Right now, Mestemaker is viewed as one of the top quarterback prospects of the 2027 draft class. While his draft projection may change between now and April, he's surely someone the Jets' front office will be watching closely over the next several months.
