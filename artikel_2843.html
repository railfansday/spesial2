Skip Bayless gives Oklahoma football’s Jason Witten props for huge debut vs. Florida

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Oklahoma football pulled off an impressive 23-7 road win in their game against Florida, ending its losing streak in their Week 10 college football matchup on Saturday at Ben Hill Griffin Stadium. Interim offensive coordinator Jason Witten played a significant role in the Sooners' victory, earning recognition from popular sports analyst Skip Bayless.

Witten, who joined Oklahoma's coaching staff in this year as the tight ends coach, received his first opportunity to call plays at the college level against Florida. Following the Sooners' impressive performance, Bayless took to X to suggested that Oklahoma should consider making his position permanent.

"Jason Witten STRIKES AGAIN (AND AGAIN): OU, 20-play TD drive, Mateer to Sategna, 20-7. No more "interim." Give Witten a long-term deal," Bayless wrote.

The victory gives Oklahoma a much-needed lift following a challenging stretch of the season. The Sooners had struggled to find consistency, alternating between victories and defeats during their first eight games.

The coaching change followed Oklahoma's growing offensive concerns, particularly after its disappointing 42-25 loss to South Carolina. Head coach Brent Venables believed that bringing in a new offensive leader could help the Sooners make the most of their remaining opportunities this season.

Witten faced a challenging first assignment as Oklahoma traveled to Gainesville to take on a ranked Florida team. However, the Sooners came away with an impressive victory, providing their interim offensive play-caller with a promising start to his new role. Oklahoma's offense also showed noticeable improvement under Witten's leadership.

The Sooners also claimed their first win in Gainesville during the first on-campus meeting between the two programs. Their previous encounters took place in the 2009 BCS National Championship Game and the 2020 Cotton Bowl.
