Patriots reveal plan at QB after Drake Maye injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Patriots are sticking with Tommy Cutlets.

Patriots head coach Mike Vrabel indicated the team is not looking to add another quarterback when asked about the possibility of recently released Daniel Jones filling in the shoes of Drake Maye, who went down with an ankle injury during their Week 10 loss to the Lions in Munich.

"Tommy's our starter. Tommy's our starter," Vrabel told the media after the team's bye week on Monday. "We're excited about that. [He had a] good preseason. And Tommy's improved. I know that going into the last game, I think that being thrown in there, he had some good plays, had some things that he can improve on and little things. I think that was probably the biggest thing, just saying, 'Hey, getting back into game action, how do you work in the pocket?' These are things that he got so much better on when he got here.

"So, starting with those things and managing the offense and being able to get us in and out of the huddle and the right plays and just get into a flow and a tempo. And I think that some of the things that we saw in training camp, a lot of those guys that he'll be out there throwing to some of those guys that he worked with in training camp. So, I think that there's some carryover there."

New England Patriots quarterback Tommy DeVito (16) throws against Detroit Lions linebacker Derrick Barnes (55) on Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026, in Munich, Germany. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

DeVito's fellow former Giants quarterback, Jones, was released by the Colts on Friday after what has turned into a lost-cause season at 3-8. He was expected to draw interest from the Cardinals, Ravens and Patriots.

He seemingly cleared waivers and became a free agent on Monday, but is reportedly unlikely to sign immediately and will rather wait for the right opportunity.

In the wake of confusion surrounding Maye's injury, the 5-5 Patriots are turning to their backup, who endured a rough relief outing against the Lions.

DeVito completed 12 of 24 passes for 81 yards with one touchdown and one interception in the 35-10 loss.

"Not good enough are the words I would say," DeVito said of his efforts after the game. "Offensively we just never really got going and not really were able to get into a rhythm or get drives going. Too many three-and-outs, just kind of hurting ourselves. … We need to do better at sustaining drives."

New England Patriots quarterback Drake Maye (10) walks on the sideline after an injury in Munich, Germany, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. AP Photo/Doug Benc

New England also has rookie Behren Morton, a Texas Tech product and seventh-round pick.

"I think that this is also a great opportunity for Behren to improve in those reps that him and Tommy may have been splitting or Tommy taking the majority of those," Vrabel added. "I think that that's a great opportunity for Behren to improve in that regard of running the show team and doing those things.

"So, we will look at everything. But again, I don't think that that's something that's going to happen here in the next few days."

Maye exited the international clash early after taking a sack from Aidan Hutchinson, who suffered a worrisome knee injury celebrating the play.

The 24-year-old does not have a definitive timeline for his return nor has the team given information regarding the nature of the injury.

The Patriots travel to face the Chargers (1-9) on Sunday.
