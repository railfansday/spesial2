Injury update for Jets WR Garrett Wilson

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

New York Jets head coach Aaron Glenn updated the health status of wide receiver Garrett Wilson ahead of facing the Buffalo Bills in Week 10.

When the Bills (7-1) visit the Jets (2-7) at MetLife Stadium on Sunday, it sounds like Wilson will be on the field.

Wilson missed the Jets' game last Sunday against the Kansas City Chiefs, a 35-12 loss, because he was in the concussion protocol. Wilson was initially injured the week prior agains the Las Vegas Raiders toward the end of that contest.

"Garrett still has one more step to go through before he's cleared," Glenn said. "And I see that happening."

Wilson has a team-leading 43 catches for 527 yards and three touchdowns in 2026.
