Bears Discussion: Saints preview, bye week reflection, 2027 NFL Draft safety prospects

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Chicago Bears are coming out of their bye week, and with a 7-2 record, they have their eyes set on the Super Bowl. They make their return to the field on Sunday against the New Orleans Saints, a team with an inferior record at 3-6, but one that isn't out of the running for a playoff spot in the weak NFC South.

How will the Bears look in their return to the Bayou? What level of concern should we all have about the strength of the NFC North? Where can Chicago exploit New Orleans' weaknesses to pick up the victory? Jacob Infante and Jacob White talk about it all in the latest edition of Bears for Breakfast.

Plus, the guys share their thoughts on 2027 NFL Draft safety prospects, Jacob White's secret top 5 of the week, and more!

The video version will be live at 10 a.m. CT, so stop by to ask some questions and interact with the show!
