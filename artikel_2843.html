Limited practice for Dontayvion Wicks

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Philadelphia Eagles WR Dontayvion Wicks (ankle) was a limited participant in practice Thursday, Nov. 19.

Why it Matters: Wicks has been sidelined since Week 6, and he isn't a lock to return this weekend against the Steelers. Even if Wicks does return, it is unlikely he will be more than a WR4 or WR5 given Philadelphia's current quarterback situation.
