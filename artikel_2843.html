‘He Is Not Messing Around’ — Ex-Indiana Basketball Coach ‘Absolutely Loves’ Curt Cignetti Calling Out SEC Media’s ‘Fake News’ on Michigan Rumors

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Curt Cignetti's Indiana is coming off an incredible national championship season and has continued to establish itself as one of college football's most phenomenal teams. With a 9-1 record and a 6-1 record in Big Ten play, the Hoosiers remain in the race for another postseason run in 2026.

That success has naturally made Cignetti an attractive name whenever major coaching vacancies emerge. However, he has shown little patience for speculation about leaving Bloomington, and this week, he decided to put a stop to the rumors once and for all.

Cignetti signed a revised agreement with Indiana in February, with the updated terms becoming public in March. The deal runs through 2033 and retains a $15 million buyout if he leaves for another coaching job this winter.

This week, a joint report from John Talty, Richard Johnson, and Chris Hummer examined the possibility of Cignetti leaving Indiana for another Big Ten program. After Cignetti's X account dismissed the speculation, former Indiana basketball head coach Dan Dakich shared his reaction on Friday.

"Speaking of Indiana, there are rumors, and there are idiots in the media that are putting Curt Cignetti on the hot list for the Michigan football job," Dakich said. "What is wrong with these clowns? And what I love about Cignetti is he's not messing around. He is 1,000%, not even a little bit, messing around.

"Curt Cignetti put out 'Fake news created by three SEC media writers. Nice try, clown.' That's awesome. I don't know who's doing Cignetti's social media lately, but I'm digging all over it. I am. I absolutely love it."

The CBS Sports report explored whether Cignetti would consider leaving Indiana, which ranks No. 3 in PFN Defense Impact with a 96.1 grade, for Michigan if the Wolverines made a coaching change.

The report suggested that Cignetti could potentially be attracted by Michigan's financial muscle and ability to support a competitive roster. According to sources cited by the three journalists, Cignetti had expressed concerns about Indiana's funding and its ability to maintain its recent level of success.

The report also raised questions about Indiana's 2027 roster outlook, with several important players potentially departing after the 2026 season. One source also claimed that Cignetti had delivered a concerning message to his coaching staff regarding the program's future roster.

However, Cignetti was quick to respond after Talty shared the report on X.

"Fake news created by three SEC media writers. Nice try," Cignetti tweeted.

When Indiana announced another contract extension in October 2025, Cignetti expressed his intention to end his coaching career with the Hoosiers.

"I couldn't be more proud to be a Hoosier, and I plan on retiring as a Hoosier," Cignetti said. "The way that this state has embraced us and our success in football has meant more to me than anything else."

Cignetti's latest dismissal of the Michigan rumors also aligns with comments he made to Big Ten Network's Rick Pizzo in June. He discussed his relationship with Indiana president Pam Whitten and athletic director Scott Dolson, saying that their support had been an important factor in his decision to stay with the Hoosiers.
