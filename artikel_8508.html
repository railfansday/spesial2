Personal Consumer Issues • Package forwarding service from EU to US?

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Some years back, my wife and I were in Amsterdam, where we happened across a shop selling a local baked snack (kruidnoten) that we found delightful. When we returned to the US, we ordered a box full of these treats to pass out to family during the holidays, which the company shipped from the Netherlands to the US with only the usual customs/etc hurdles.

It's been a while since we did this. Yesterday, when I tried again, I ran into a snag in filling out the shipping address; the web site indicated that they could not ship our selections to our address. I messaged the company's support folks, and found that the company no longer ships to the US, only to a list of about six countries in the EU. (I'm not aware of any customs or other issues that might impede shipping from the Netherlands in general, nor did their response indicate that any such issues were in play.)

I happened across a web page for a company that claims they will forward packages from the EU to the US for a cost. This might be a solution to our problem, but I'm hesitant to go with an untried solution. Has anyone here had any experience with such a company, and did it go smoothly?
