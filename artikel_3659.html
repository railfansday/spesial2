When is the next full moon for Ursaluna in Pokémon Go?

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

You can only evolve this bear during a full moon, so when's the next one?

If you want Ursaluna in Pokémon Go, you'll need to evolve your Ursaring on specific evenings. Ursaring only evolves into its grizzly evolution, Ursaluna, when there's a full moon in real life! Full moons occur once a month, so if the full moon passes, you'll need to wait another month or so to evolve it, sadly.
