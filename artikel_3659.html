How to get and use Cargo in ROB RUN

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

There are plenty of things to sell in ROB RUN, and managing your cargo efficiently will make you richer in quick time. These items are harder to manage since you can’t fit them in your backpack.

However, knowing what to do with them will save you time and make you more money.

Unlike specific loot, Cargo is an umbrella term used for any item that you can’t fit inside the bag. Hence, the only option is to carry it in your arms. This creates an issue since you can’t equip any weapons when you’re carrying Cargo.

However, there’s a possible way to mitigate this problem with the use of vehicles. If you have free space in your vehicle, you can then shift the Cargo to that spot. This will now free up your hands without having to leave the valuable loot behind.

So, how do you get Cargo in the first place? There are several ways to obtain them.

Cargo has one, and only one, use in ROB RUN. You should sell them to smugglers for profit. When you find a Smuggler, approach their vehicle and place the cargo in the trunk.

To get your money, you have to confirm the sale. Make sure to check the price before you do so since the rate varies. Once you receive the payment, transport it back safely to your base. Otherwise, you run the risk of losing it to other players, or getting it confiscated by the police.
