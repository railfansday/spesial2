Michael Skubala sack twist at Bristol City emerges: 3 managers must be top of the list

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Bristol City haven't quite had the start to life they were hoping for under Michael Skubala after entering a new era, as doubts surrounding the stability of his role at Ashton Gate have emerged.

Bristol City head into the international break with one win in their last seven Championship games, with a 5-0 home defeat to Norwich City just before the interval raising legitimate questions regarding the future of Skubala.

The 44-year-old was poached by the Robins from Lincoln City in the summer, having guided the Imps to the League One title last campaign, and was backed heavily to reshape the Bristol City squad in his image.

However, despite the additions of the likes of Dom Ballard, Lorent Tolaj, Sam Tickle, Sam Greenwood, and Gibson Yah, to name a few, the Robins have performed well beneath expected standards, as now, reports have emerged that throw Skubala’s position into doubt.

According to Alan Nixon’s Patreon, Bristol City are making background checks on potential successors to Skubala, with the former Leeds United coach under scrutiny following a string of poor performances and results preceding the international break.

Nixon reports that the Robins have the money to end Skubala’s reign early, and are ready to pay the compensation required to bring another head coach to Ashton Gate if the right candidate is found.

As things stand, Skubala is set to undertake Bristol City’s away clash with Middlesbrough, though with Wrexham, West Bromwich Albion, and Swansea City shortly after, he will have to produce some magic to keep his job, as Football League World takes a look at three candidates that could realistically replace him in the foreseeable future.

Rob Edwards has been out of work since his surprise dismissal from Wolverhampton Wanderers in the summer, with everyone, including the Welshman himself, assuming he would guide the Old Gold into the Championship following their relegation from the Premier League.

Edwards has enjoyed spells in charge of the likes of Forest Green Rovers, Watford, Luton Town, and Middlesbrough before landing at Molineux in November 2025, jumping ship from Boro in the second tier with the club riding high.

He guided Luton to a historic promotion to the Premier League in 2023 via the play-offs, which remains the highlight of his managerial career at this stage, and though his time with the Hatters ended in disappointment, he remains a highly-rated head coach.

Edwards knocked back the opportunity to become head coach of Burnley in the summer, and whether he is interested in taking on another job in the Championship, at this stage, remains to be seen, though Bristol City should do everything they can to convince him to head to Ashton Gate.

Carlos Corberan is a name most Championship fans will know well from his time with Huddersfield Town and West Brom as these days, the Spaniard finds himself out of work.

Corberan was most recently employed by Spanish La Liga giants Valencia, but he and his coaching staff were dismissed back in September following a poor run of results.

The 43-year-old became Huddersfield's head coach in the summer of 2020, and turned the Terriers from relegation contenders to promotion hopefuls, having lost out to Nottingham Forest in the 2022 play-off final at Wembley.

He'd continue his excellent work in England with West Brom after a short stint in Greece with Olympiacos, and remains highly thought of among Baggies supporters.

Corberan has displayed an excellent ability to extract high levels of performance under tight budgets during his time as head coach, though with Bristol City, he would be afforded some more legroom, as the Spaniard simply must be considered.

The man who triumphed in the 2022 Championship play-off final at Corberan's expense, Steve Cooper has consistently proven he is an excellent manager in the second tier throughout his career.

The Welshman guided Swansea City to the play-offs twice, losing out in the semi-final the first time, and then the final the second, before finally winning it the third time with Forest, never finishing beneath 6th place in the division.

Spells with Leicester City and Brondby have since followed, with Cooper struggling to find familiar success outside of the Championship, as a return to England's second tier must be considered.

Out of work since April 2026 following his dismissal from Brondby, Cooper is awaiting his next role in the sport and could be tempted by the current project at Bristol City.

Cooper and the Robins could be the perfect fit, and if Skubala is to be dismissed, the Welshman would be an excellent alternative.
