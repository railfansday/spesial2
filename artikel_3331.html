Guy Ritchie’s 126-Minute Fantasy Epic Officially Earns 179 Million Views on Netflix

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Guy Ritchie is turning into Taylor Sheridan these days with the amount of television series he's got his hand in right now. He's enjoying plenty of success on streaming at the moment with the likes of MobLand, The Gentlemen, and Young Sherlock, but some of his older movies have also found an audience that wasn't necessarily there when they were released in theaters as a result of Ritchie's big wins on the small screen. A decade ago, Ritchie decided to try his hand at a $175 million fantasy epic that Warner Bros. had hoped would launch an entire franchise, but those plans were abandoned when the movie failed to make back its production budget at the box office. Nine years later, though, it's becoming one of those Netflix sleeper hits.

Ritchie's King Arthur: Legend of the Sword has officially recorded 179 million minutes viewed on Netflix, placing eighth on Nielsen's movie streaming chart in the United States for the week of November 2–8. Given the movie's 126-minute runtime, that's the equivalent of approximately 1.42 million complete viewings in a single week.

The film is an iconoclastic take on the classic Excalibur myth, tracing Arthur's journey from the streets to the throne. When the child Arthur's father is murdered, Vortigern, Arthur's uncle, seizes the crown. Robbed of his birthright and with no idea who he truly is, Arthur comes up the hard way in the back alleys of the city. But once he pulls the sword from the stone, his life is turned upside down and he is forced to acknowledge his true legacy — whether he likes it or not.

King Arthur: Legend of the Sword grossed just $149.2 million worldwide against its $175 million production budget, which obviously isn't the result anybody was hoping for. Critically, it didn't fare much better either, sitting on a 31% critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes. However, at least the audience kinda liked it, as it has a considerably healthier 69% audience score. Nice.

The cast includes Charlie Hunnam (Sons of Anarchy) as Arthur, Jude Law (The Holiday) as Vortigern, Àstrid Bergès-Frisbey (Pirates of the Caribbean: On Stranger Tides) as the Mage, Djimon Hounsou (Gladiator) as Bedivere, Eric Bana (Troy) as Uther Pendragon, Aidan Gillen (Game of Thrones) as Bill, Freddie Fox (House of the Dragon) as Rubio, and Annabelle Wallis (Peaky Blinders) as Maggie.
