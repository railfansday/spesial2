Mary Steenburgen, Kyra Sedgwick & Sophia Bush Set For Indie ‘Kindred Spirits’; Hilary Swank Among Producers

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

EXCLUSIVE: Academy Award winner Hilary Swank is among the producers of Kindred Spirits, an indie starring Mary Steenburgen (The Dink), Kyra Sedgwick (Family Movie) and Sophia Bush (Grey’s Anatomy) that has entered production in England.

Directed by Grant S. Johnson, known for his producing/exec producing of titles like May December and Magazine, the film follows a teenage girl who, after her parents die in a car crash, moves in with her estranged grandmother, only to discover she is in line to inherit a power that connects the living with their deceased loved ones. No word yet as to who is playing the teenager. The script is by Charlotte Hamblin, Daniel Corter, Johnson and Philip Schneider. Schneider, Swank, Johnson and Leonora Darby are producing. Johnson’s Project Infinity is the production company and sales rep. Watch on Deadline

An Oscar and Golden Globe winner, Steenburgen has most recently been seen starring opposite Jake Johnson in Apple’s pickleball comedy The Dink. She also recently appeared in Season 2 of Netflix’s A Man on the Inside, opposite husband Ted Danson, and can currently be seen in the comedy Let’s Have Kids! from Adam Sztykiel. She is repped by UTA, Entertainment 360 and Gang, Tyre, Ramer.

Sedgwick is an Emmy and Golden Globe winner who’s most recently been seen in films including Adam Rehmeier’s Carolina Caroline and Family Movie, where she stars opposite husband Kevin Bacon and kids Sosie Bacon and Travis Bacon. She is repped by CAA, Range Media Partners and Schreck Rose Dapello.

Bush has recently been seen on Grey’s Anatomy, and in films such as Broad Trip. Upcoming, she’ll be seen starring in Hulu’s mystery thriller series Count My Lies, opposite Lindsay Lohan, Shailene Woodley and Kit Harington, as well as Disney’s The Cheetah Girls: Next Gen. She is repped by CAA and Entertainment 360.

Kindred Spirits marks the fourth feature from the NY-based Johnson, who produced the Academy Award and Golden Globe-nominated May December and The Assessment, as well as Broadway’s The Band’s Visit, which was the recipient of 10 Tony Awards.

Recent acting credits for Swank, the Academy Award-winning star of Million Dollar Baby and Boys Don’t Cry, include Yellowjackets and Ordinary Angels. Alongside her onscreen work, she’s exec produced films and TV series like The Good Mother, Hulu’s Alaska Daily and the lauded indie drama What They Had, having starred in each of those three. She is repped by CAA and Entertainment 360.
