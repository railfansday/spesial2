Big Boro Blow Opens Door For West Ham Revival

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Stoke City have been handed an extra incentive ahead of todays’s Championship clash with West Ham United following Southampton’s 2-0 victory over Middlesbrough on Friday night. Mark Robins’ side will now go top of the Championship if they beat the Hammers at the London Stadium.

Former West Ham midfielders James Ward-Prowse and Flynn Downes both started for Southampton, who moved to within four points of league leaders Middlesbrough.

Victory for Stoke tomorrow would take the Potters to 38 points, a total neither Middlesbrough nor third-placed Wolverhampton Wanderers can match this weekend. Meanwhile, Scott Parker’s West Ham remain without a victory in six Championship games.

There is a certain irony in Ward-Prowse and Downes playing their part in denting Middlesbrough’s title challenge on the eve of West Ham’s game against Stoke.

Not that Southampton’s victory should come as a surprise to anyone. Were it not for their four-point deduction, the Saints would now be sitting at the top of the Championship, with a better goal difference than Middlesbrough.

Extra incentive for Stoke to beat West Ham at the London Stadium? You better believe it.

Having already won five consecutive Championship games, the Potters will arrive with their tails up.

Given the mounting pressure on Scott Parker, now would be a particularly good time to register his first victory as Hammers boss.
