Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Cote's cost decline and a royalty buyback reshape the cash flow outlook.

IAMGOLD Corporation (IAG +5.69%) reported a transition to a net cash position in the third quarter while achieving record throughput at its Cote Gold operation. Management stated that the company successfully discontinued external contractor crushing services at Cote, allowing processing costs to decline to targets during Sept. 2026. The company utilized its balance sheet to exercise a repurchase option on half of Franco-Nevada's gross margin royalty at Cote, a move intended to increase the company's share of cash flow from its largest asset. Management indicated that cash repatriated from Essakane continues to fund a multiyear share buyback program, while cash flows from Canadian assets are being reinvested into growth catalysts at Westwood and the Nelligan Mining Complex.
