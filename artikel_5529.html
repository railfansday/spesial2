OpenSSF Welcomes New Members and Expands AI Security Resources

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Broadened AI security guidance, relationships, and research to help close the gap between AI-driven development and software security

SALT LAKE CITY, Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The Open Source Security Foundation (OpenSSF), a cross-industry initiative of the Linux Foundation focused on sustainably securing open source software, today announced new membership growth, welcoming Beautiful Majestic Dolphin, DevGuard, and UElement Technologies to the foundation. The OpenSSF also notes new and expanded efforts to help organizations secure software in the age of AI, including membership with the Multi-Organization Secure AI Coordination (MOSAIC) for better AI security, a new guide to finding and fixing vulnerabilities using AI, and new research on AI's impact on software security.

AI is redefining the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC). It impacts both the rate at which code is produced and how quickly vulnerabilities are discovered and reported â for attackers and defenders alike. With bugs easier to find and reporting windows shrinking under regulations like the EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA), organizations need clear, practical guidance on using AI to secure software. OpenSSF continues to serve as a trusted, neutral hub for AI security education, collaboration, and open source software security.

"Security must keep pace with this new software development lifecycle."

"Security must keep pace with this new software development lifecycle," said Laura Guazzelli, Security Architect for AI/ML/LLM/Agentic Systems at OpenSSF. "This new SDLC has collapsed the window between bug discovery and automated attack, exposing defenses that are unprepared for AI-driven threats and offensive campaigns. This is why we are focused on equipping developers with practical resources, from guidance on finding and fixing vulnerabilities with AI to new data on how AI is shaping software development."

New OpenSSF members include Beautiful Majestic Dolphin, DevGuard, and UElement Technologies who join the Foundation as General Members. These organizations join a community of working groups, technical groups, and experts that are shaping the future of OpenSSF and software security. Their membership reinforces the long term sustainability of open source and the projects that are critical to modern infrastructure.

About the OpenSSFThe Open Source Security Foundation (OpenSSF) is a cross-industry organization at the Linux Foundation that brings together the industry's most important open source security initiatives and the individuals and companies that support them. The OpenSSF is committed to collaboration and working both upstream and with existing communities to advance open source security for all. For more information, please visit us at openssf.org.

About the Linux Foundation The Linux Foundation is the world's leading home for collaboration on open source software, hardware, standards, and data. Linux Foundation projects, including Linux, Kubernetes, Model Context Protocol (MCP), OpenChain, OpenSearch, OpenSSF, OpenStack, PyTorch, Ray, RISC-V, SPDX and Zephyr, provide the foundation for global infrastructure. The Linux Foundation is focused on leveraging best practices and addressing the needs of contributors, users, and solution providers to create sustainable models for open collaboration. For more information, please visit us at linuxfoundation.org.
