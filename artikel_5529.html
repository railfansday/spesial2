Kimberly-Clark beat earnings estimates as China furor weighs on sales

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Organic sales fell 1.3%, including a 270 basis point headwind from the China social media disruption; the company kept its 2026 outlook

Kimberly-Clark $KMB reported third-quarter adjusted earnings of $1.70 a share from continuing operations on Thursday, up 17.2% from a year earlier, as a social media disruption in China weighed on sales.

Adjusted earnings topped the $1.65 a share that analysts expected, according to The Wall Street Journal. Net sales fell 0.4% to $4.1 billion, and organic sales declined 1.3%, including a headwind of about 270 basis points from the China disruption, the company said.

Adjusted earnings per share attributable to Kimberly-Clark, which include discontinued operations, fell 6.6% to $1.70. Diluted earnings per share attributable to the company were $5.37, compared with $1.34 a year earlier. The latest quarter included a pre-tax gain of about $1.4 billion from the completion of its International Family Care and Professional business transaction with Suzano, which closed July 1.

In International Personal Care, organic sales fell 4.6%, including a headwind of about 770 basis points from China. Segment operating profit fell 32.7% to $144 million. North America organic sales rose 0.4%.

Adjusted operating profit was $677 million, down 0.9%, with the China disruption accounting for a headwind of about 530 basis points. Adjusted gross margin was 35.5%, down 130 basis points from a year earlier.

"Our third quarter results reflect a dynamic operating environment and several discrete impacts," Chairman and CEO Mike Hsu said in a statement.

In October, Chinese authorities said a multi-agency investigation found no formamide in the diapers it tested, including Kimberly-Clark’s, and no health hazards from the claimed chemical, according to Reuters.

The company kept its 2026 outlook. It expects organic sales growth about 100 basis points below the 1.7% weighted average growth of its categories and countries, reflecting about 100 basis points of impact from the China disruptions. It expects adjusted operating profit to grow in the mid-single digits on a constant-currency basis. It expects adjusted earnings per share from continuing operations to grow in the high single digits, and adjusted earnings per share attributable to Kimberly-Clark to decline in the low single digits, both on a constant-currency basis.

Kimberly-Clark acquired Kenvue, the maker of Tylenol, on Nov. 2. In October, the company had weighed a bond offering to help finance the Kenvue takeover, a deal valued at roughly $36 billion that included $6.7 billion in cash. JPMorgan $JPM Chase had supplied $7.7 billion of bridge financing for the transaction.
