Millennium Adds R&M Data Center Connectivity to Its Integrated Supply Chain Platform

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Partnership gives U.S. data center operators, contractors and integrators access to R&M's modular connectivity portfolio, backed by Millennium's engineering support and national footprint

DELAVAN, Wis., Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Millennium, an integrated supply chain solutions company for digital infrastructure, today announced a distribution partnership with R&M USA, the U.S. subsidiary of Swiss connectivity manufacturer R&M (Reichle & De-Massari AG). Under the agreement, Millennium will distribute selected R&M data center connectivity solutions to customers across the United States.

Data center builds are moving on compressed schedules, and the connectivity layer is where late or incomplete material most often costs a project days. The partnership pairs R&M's portfolio with Millennium's integrated platform, connecting engineering on the front end, with sourcing and value-added services such as kitting and staging, so customers can deploy faster and hold their build schedules.

"Our data center customers are building against dates that do not move, and connectivity is where small gaps turn into lost days," said Jim Anetsberger, Chief Revenue Officer at Millennium. "R&M gives those customers a proven, modular portfolio built for high-density environments. Our job is to make it deployable: engineered into the work plan, staged to the build sequence and on site when the crew needs it. That is how we help customers hold their schedules and lower their total cost to build."

R&M's data center portfolio supports computing rooms, meet-me rooms, building entrances, campuses and data center interconnect environments. Its modular approach brings together fiber and copper connectivity, distribution systems, racks, cable management and infrastructure-management capabilities.

"Data center customers need more than individual components. They need reliable access to solutions, technical competence and a supply chain that can respond to demanding project schedules," said Paulo Campos, President of R&M USA Inc. "Millennium's distribution reach and practical understanding of infrastructure projects make them a strong partner as we continue building the R&M brand in this important market."

The partnership takes effect November 5, 2026. Initial product availability, participating locations and ordering information will be announced through Millennium and R&M USA.

Millennium is a technology-enabled, integrated supply chain solutions company that helps customers deploy critical digital infrastructure faster and on time. Headquartered in Delavan, Wisconsin, Millennium brings engineering integration, materials, value-added supply chain services and capital solutions together in one platform, serving fiber broadband, data center and enterprise, public sector and utility customers through more than 20 locations across the United States. For more information, visit www.mymillennium.us.

R&M (Reichle & De-Massari AG) develops, produces and sells infrastructure solutions for communications and data networks worldwide. Based in Wetzikon, Switzerland, the company operates across the Local Area Networks, Public Networks and Data Center markets. R&M's expertise includes connectivity and distribution technology, cables, racks, enclosures and infrastructure-management software. For more information, visit www.rdm.com.
