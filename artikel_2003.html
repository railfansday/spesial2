Man City to accept Rayan Ait-Nouri offer ahead of January - transfer expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Manchester City are prepared to accept offers for Rayan Ait-Nouri ahead of the upcoming January transfer window.

The 25-year-old has struggled to nail down a place in Enzo Maresca's side so far this season, despite impressing when called upon, and is unlikely to be part of his long-term plans.

A number of Premier League clubs and others across Europe are believed to be keeping a close eye on his situation and would now be prepared to lure him away from the Etihad.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Maresca "doesn't rate" Ait-Nouri and will facilitate his exit if the right offer arrives in the mid-season window.

The Algeria international has yet to make a Premeir League appearance this season, while his minutes across other competitions have also been limited.

There is plenty of competition for places at left-back in this Man City side, with Josko Gvardiol and Nico O'Reilly in the way, opening the door for Ait-Nouri to move on.

Football Insider revealed in September that Maresca had plans to utlilise Ait-Nouri as part of his squad after his impressive performances in the Champions League this season.

However, his lack of playing time in the Premier League has raised new questions about his future, and there is a growing feeling he could be allowed to leave.

With a number of clubs keeping a close eye on his situation, Ait-Nouri is expected to seek a move away from Man City in January, with both the club and the player ready to move on.

"It doesn't seem like there is any place for Rayan Ait-Nouri in this Man City team," Brown told Football Insider.

"Look at their usual left-backs, you've got Gvardiol and O'Reilly, which one of those two would you leave out to put Ait-Nouri in? Realistically, that's not going to happen.

"He might get minutes here and there off the bench or in cup games, but it's become clear already this season that he isn't trusted to play in the Premier League.

"It seems to me that Enzo Maresca doesn't rate him, he doesn't see him as a big part of his squad, and he would be ready to let him go in January.

"From the player's point of view, I expect he'll be ready to move on as well, because if he's not playing regularly in this City team then he will want to go and play somewhere else.

"I can't see him staying at Man City for much longer, it's clear he doesn't have a real part to play, so it's probably best for everybody if he leaves."

Despite his lack of playing time at Man City in recent seasons, Ait-Nouri still remains very highly regarded by a number of Premier League clubs who could lure him away.

Everton and Tottenham have both been long-term admirers, while clubs outside of English football including the likes of AC Milan and Juventus are also very keen.

Meanwhile, Football Insider revealed in September that Man United saw a deadline-day move for Ait-Nouri blocked by Man City as they didn't want to see him join a rival club.

That move could be reignited in January, however, with United still among his high-profile admirers and Man City believed to be more open to his departure.

Michael Carrick is determined to add a new left-back to his squad, and while a deal for Ait-Nouri may only be a loan for now, he remains high on Man United's list.
