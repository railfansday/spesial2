Man United's new manager told by ex-employee what it will really be like when he takes over the dressing room

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Manchester United's issues run far deeper than Michael Carrick and changing their manager will not provide a quick fix.

The 45-year-old is expected to be sacked imminently after a dismal start to the season which has seen them fall into the Premier League relegation zone.

Work is already ongoing behind the scenes to identify his replacement, but regardless of who comes through the door at Old Trafford, they will be faced with an impossible job.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that dismissing Carrick from his role will not solve the problems that have placed the club in this situation.

Instead, a new manager will inherit the same issues that the likes of Erik ten Hag, Ruben Amorim and now Carrick have been forced to contend with during their spells.

Ongoing defensive issues, concerns about the standard of players available, below-par recruitment and distancing sections of the supporters, are among the major concerns at United.

Brown told Football Insider on 10 November that Carrick is set to be sacked by Man United amid genuine fears of a shock relegation from the Premier League.

With just one win from ten Premier League games so far, the Red Devils are in the bottom three, and it may be very difficult to turn things around from this position.

The appointment of a new manager will be designed to provide a boost to the squad, but given the deeper problems at play, there are doubts about the success of the decision.

"Man United have much bigger issues than Michael Carrick at the moment," Brown told Football Insider.

"If they decide to sack him and bring in somebody else, they're going to be faced with the same problems which have put the club in this position to begin with.

"What's happened between last season, when Carrick was so successsful, and now, when they've fallen into the relegation zone? Because it hasn't just happened overnight.

"Look at everything happening at the club, the players aren't good enough especially in defence, and they're not bringing in the right options to strengthen the squad.

"Then there's this disconnect with the fans, who have made their feelings very clear, and bringing in a new manager won't change their minds.

"So whoever steps into the role will have to deal with all of the same issues Carrick has been dealing with. It's not just a quick fix, bring someone else in and it'll all be alright."

While the new manager has been backed to face problems upon his arrival, a number of big names are believed to be in line to replace Carrick at Old Trafford.

Among those linked include the likes of Antonio Conte, Mauricio Pochettino and Didier Deschamps, with the board considering multiple high-profile candidates.

Meanwhile, Football Insider revealed on 7 November that Eddie Howe and Julian Nagelsmann remain the two front-runners to take over the role.

Both managers are currently out of work and would be available to step into the job immediately, and are believed to be keen on the chance to manage Man United.

While they are not the only managers being considered, they have emerged as the leading contenders, and both have serious backers in the Man United boardroom.
