Swiss ATP player reveals what Roger Federer was really like to practice with

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Dylan Dietrich had the opportunity to practice with Roger Federer when he was just 16 years old.

Dietrich is one of the next big hopefuls for Swiss men's tennis, as they search for their next superstar after the retirements of both Federer and Stan Wawrinka in recent years.

The 22-year-old has been based in Virginia as a college tennis player, where Dietrich has found a lot of success by winning NCAA titles in both doubles and the team competition. Dietrich has also transitioned this success to the professional game by winning two ATP Challenger titles in recent months and climbing from outside the top 1000 to his current career-high of world number 155, all in the space of this year.

Someone who Dietrich has taken inspiration from in his rise up the ranks is his compatriot and 20-time Grand Slam champion Federer, who he has twice had the opportunity to be on court with.

Following his recent successes, Dietrich appeared on the latest edition of the Nothing Major Podcast, where he was asked about his experiences with Federer.

Dietrich admitted that he was very nervous when he discovered that he would have the opportunity to practice with Federer for the first time at just 16 years old.

"I have been on court with Roger twice, which was super fun," said Dietrich. "I will never forget this. I was 16 years old, and obviously my coach called me up in the morning and was like you have a new practice partner today. He then told me and I was super nervous. I was an hour early and doing the best possible warm-up I have ever done in my life.

"He walks in, I could see him from far away walking towards the court, he comes up to me shakes my hand and goes 'my name is Roger'. I was thinking I know who you are! That was an experience, it was awesome and I will never forget."

Nothing Major Podcast co-host and former Wimbledon semi-finalist Sam Querrey then joined the conversation, as he suggested that Rafael Nadal is the most stressful of 'The Big Three' to practice with, before Dietrich followed up by explaining how good Federer made him feel about his game, despite struggling against his slice.

"He is just the best guy ever," Querrey added about Federer. "You practiced with Federer, Nadal, [Novak] Djokovic and [Andy] Murray. There is less stress practicing with Fed than there is with Novak or Rafa. Rafa is stressful as can be! When you were at the changeover you were probably chatting the whole time and he was asking questions."

Dietrich continued, "He made me feel so good about myself because I actually started playing well. At the start I was so nervous and he was making it fun. He was still hitting his slice and I could not hit it back much. I was like that's the toughest shot I have ever faced!

"But he made it so much fun I loosened up and started to play better, which made it probably a better practice for him as well. He tells you some jokes and makes you laugh. You always see him on TV and he is so serious, but he is such a great guy."

Despite the significant rise Dietrich has made over the past year, he has stated his intention to remain at the University of Virginia for one more year to complete his college degree. Dietrich is currently the Swiss number two on the ATP Tour, with only Jerome Kym, who is currently the world number 147, ranked above him.

Dietrich has only played one main ATP Tour match so far in his career, having lost to his compatriot Kym in a close-fought match at the Swiss Open in Gstaad earlier this year.
