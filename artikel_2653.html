Fantasy Football Waiver Wire: Justin Boone's top defenses to stream for Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

It's Week 11 of the 2026 fantasy football season and its the heaviest bye week of the year, six teams out of action. That list includes the Packers, Rams, Falcons, Seahawks, Browns and Patriots. With a few elite defenses off the slate, you may need a D/ST to stream for the week, or a longer-term option. Justin Boone offers his top defenses to stream.

Bengals at Commanders: Cincinnati's offense has been so dominant lately, it's helping the defense look better. In game scripts where the Bengals get out to a big lead, opponents will need to throw more. That means more sack and turnover opportunities. The Commanders rank 23rd in points scored this season.

Bears vs. Saints: Chicago comes off its bye with a solid matchup against New Orleans, which allows the ninth-most fantasy points to opposing D/ST. The Bears should be well-rested and the addition of DT Jeffery Simmons makes this pass rush more potent.

Lions vs. Bucs: There's a chance Tampa Bay doesn't have starting QB Baker Mayfield (foot) for this contest. The Lions have also won five straight games, holding opponents to 21 points or fewer in each contest. Detroit is a solid floor play at D/ST, and could have a high ceiling if Jalon Daniels starts.
