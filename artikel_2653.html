Daniel Dubois to defend heavyweight title against ex-Anthony Joshua rival in Manchester

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Daniel Dubois will put his WBO heavyweight title on the line against former champion Joseph Parker at Manchester's Co-op Live Arena on 30 January.

The pair were previously scheduled to fight for the IBF belt in 2025, but the contest was called off days before when Dubois fell ill.

Dubois claimed the WBO belt by beating domestic rival Fabio Wardley at the venue in May, before repeating the win inside two rounds in a London rematch in October.

Parker previously held the WBO crown between 2016 and 2018 before losing to Anthony Joshua in a unification fight, and he has not boxed since an interim title loss to Wardley in October 2025.

Dubois will face Joseph Parker in January (Steven Paston/PA Wire)

Promoter Frank Warren said: "Daniel is in his prime and when he is on his game it is hard to picture anybody beating him.

"But Joseph has long promised to become world champion again, and now he gets his chance."

Parker has not fought in 2026 after he tested positive for cocaine shortly before his defeat by Wardley, although the result of the test was only made public after the stoppage loss.

The New Zealander maintained his innocence following the positive test and was cleared to fight in August following an investigation.

"I'm pleased to confirm my provisional suspension is lifted, and I'll be back in the ring soon," Parker said in August, per the BBC.

"I've been advised to say no more for now while details are finalised. Thank you to all my supporters."

Parker has a professional record of 36-4 (24 KOs). His other defeats came against British duo Dillian Whyte and Joe Joyce. However, he does hold impressive wins over Deontay Wilder, Derek Chisora and Martin Bakole.

Wilder and Bakole are due to fight each other on the undercard of Joshua's highly-anticipated clash with Tyson Fury in Cardiff next month.
