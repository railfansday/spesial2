Bucks’ Brayden Burries gets thoroughly cooked by fans for what-if Heat game scenario

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Bucks' Brayden Burries gets thoroughly cooked by fans for what-if Heat game scenario appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Milwaukee Bucks continued their torrid run at the start of the season when they took on the Miami Heat at the Kaseya Center in what was a one-off GTA-themed event. Milwaukee lost 137-130 to drop to an NBA-worst 1-14 record as Giannis Antetokounmpo scored a game-high of 27 points in a double-double alongside 14 rebounds and nine assists in his first game against his former team since leaving.

However, rookie Brayden Burries had a grounded explanation behind why Milwaukee ended up losing against the Heat, per a post on X by Heat Central.

"It was a great game plan," he said. "They just made 3s tonight. If they don't make half their 3s then we probably win tonight"

Burries will undoubtedly be disappointed with how his life in the NBA have begun despite the lottery pick himself immediately becoming a regular contributor, averaging 10.1 points, 5.5 rebounds and 2.3 assists per game. However, the explanation was undoubtedly an "if my grandmother had wheels" moment as the Heat had effectively won the game in the first half after they returned with a 26-point advantage.

As a matter of fact, the Bucks were able to save themselves from a blowout due to a 44-25 fourth quarter, which came after Miami had already begun to bench starters. As one would expect, a range of fans immediately began to troll Burries as they begane to explain exactly how the Heat had dominated Milwaukee for much of the game.

"1-14. Down most of the game by 25. And this is what he says. Lol," one fan wrote.

Miami were leading by 30 points at one point and were fairly confident that they would get the win by the second half, as another fan pointed out.

"son, its a 30 points lead in the 4th before they give you some momentum to save face," they wrote.

Another user simply found humor in Burries' comment, writing the following.

"if they missed their shots tonight and we made ours we would've won," they said.

Still, it is fair to see exactly what Burries meant. The Heat's three-point shooting has been regularly questioned and they undoubtedly do not have a lot of shooters outside of Klay Thompson and Davion Mitchell, especially considering the interior threat of Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bam Adebayo.

Still, Miami managed to convert 22 of their 45 attempts against Milwaukee, going an impressive 45.8% from beyond the arc, which was one of the many reasons why they ended up with an easy win.
