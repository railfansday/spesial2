Lobos Look to Bounce Back From UNLV Loss With Mountain West Title Still in Reach

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

ALBUQUERQUE, N.M. (KRQE) – The New Mexico Lobos are turning the page after a difficult loss to UNLV, but their goals for the season remain firmly in sight.

The Rebels became the first opponent in more than a year to score more than 20 points against the Lobos, handing New Mexico a setback after a strong season. Now, head coach Jason Eck is challenging his team to stick together, learn from the loss and respond.

New Mexico struggled to stretch the field vertically against UNLV, limiting its opportunities to create explosive plays through the air.

One highlight came from a trick play, as freshman Bailen George connected with fellow freshman DJ Darling for a 65-yard gain — the Lobos' longest pass play of the game.

Despite the flashes of offensive potential, UNLV found ways to take advantage of New Mexico's struggles and pull away in the second half.

Eck emphasized the importance of accountability as the team looks to move forward.

"We got to improve in all facets, and again, I think we got to stick together. We can't be pointing fingers at others," Eck said. "Everybody has to look at what we can do better to improve on this journey. We got to see how we respond to adversity."

Despite the loss, the Lobos believe there's still plenty to play for this season.

Eck pointed to the team's remaining opportunities, including a chance to compete for a Mountain West championship and reach double-digit wins.

"We still can control a conference championship. We can control double-digit wins. So, I mean, those were really our true goals," Eck said.

Offensive lineman Malik Aliane also encouraged the team's younger players to embrace their opportunities and continue developing.

Defensive end Darren Agu echoed that confidence, emphasizing that New Mexico still has a chance to compete for the conference title.

"We bounce back. Obviously, we still got the Mountain West to play for," Agu said.

New Mexico won't have much time to dwell on the loss, with a quick turnaround ahead.

The Lobos hit the road Friday to face Wyoming in another important Mountain West matchup.

It's an opportunity for New Mexico to regroup, respond to adversity and keep its championship aspirations alive.
