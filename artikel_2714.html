This is where the Rams sit in the NFC playoff picture after their win on Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Los Angeles Rams won on Wednesday night to move to 7-4, and they are now officially in a playoff spot for the first time this season after five straight wins. They came into Week 12 tied with the Seattle Seahawks and the Green Bay Packers, but the previous tiebreakers favored Seattle so L.A. was on the outside losing in. The win over the Packers knocked Green Bay down and L.A. is a half-game ahead of Seattle — for now.

The Seahawks play the San Francisco 49ers on Sunday. If Seattle wins, they would move back ahead of the Rams based on their superior division record. The Minnesota Vikings at 7-3 are just a half-game in front of the Rams at this point, too. If they win, they stay ahead of L.A., as well.

If either Seattle or Minnesota lose, the Rams hold onto their playoff spot. The Seahawks would fall to 6-5 and a game behind the Rams. A Vikings loss would drop them to 7-4 in a tie with the Rams but also give them their third NFC loss, meaning the Rams would be ahead of them in the conference record tiebreaker. If both outcomes happen, the Rams would jump from ninth in Week 11 all the way to sixth after Week 12.
