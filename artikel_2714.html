Chargers vs. New York Jets: How to watch, start time, odds and prediction

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Chargers finally got their win. Now they're looking for more.

"There's a lot of fight in this team, and we all see it," said coach Jim Harbaugh, whose 1-8 team plays host to the New York Jets on Sunday.

The Chargers, who had a 23-20 victory at Baltimore on Monday night, are dealing with a short week against a Jets franchise that hasn't scored a touchdown in two weeks.

Read more: With newfound momentum, Chargers aim for second straight win against reeling Jets

The Chargers leaned into the run at Baltimore. Omarion Hampton ran for a season-high 105 yards, whereas Justin Herbert threw the ball just 18 times, completing 12 for 129 yards. The quarterback also had a 22-yard touchdown scramble.

The Chargers might see the return of receiver Ladd McConkey, who missed the past two games with back issues.

Jets: G Dylan Parham (knee, questionable); DT Harrison Phillips (ankle, questionable); LB Trevin Wallace (groin, questionable); WR Isaiah Williams (ribs, out); WR Omar Cooper Jr. (ankle/hip, out); DE Joseph Ossai (foot/ankle, questionable), DL David Onyemata (abdomen, out).

Chargers: CB Isas Waxter (ankle, questionable); CB Rodney Shelley (ankle, out); S Kendall Williamson (calf, out); CB Tarheeb Still (ankle, questionable); LB Daiyan Henley (knee, out); WR Brenen Thompson (hamstring, doubtful); TE Charlie Kolar (concussion, questionable); WR Ladd McConkey (back, questionable). OT Trey Pipkins (knee, questionable).

The Chargers will play the Jets on Sunday at 1:05 p.m. PST at SoFi Stadium. The game will be shown in Southern California on Fox. It will be available nationally via a subscription to NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube TV. In Southern California, fans can listen to the game on 640 AM and 103.9 FM (Español).

Sam Farmer's pick: The Chargers should be able to keep it rolling against a Jets team that struggles to score points and has allowed quarterback Geno Smith to be sacked 29 times this season. McConkey's status could be key here. Chargers 24, Jets 18
