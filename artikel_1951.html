Club adviser reveals how far Xabi Alonso is from Chelsea sack

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Chelsea will sack manager Xabi Alonso if the team endure an awful run of results between now and January.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who still believes the Spaniard has the "full backing" of the board at Stamford Bridge.

Alonso is under ever-increasing scrutiny in West London, with his team having now won just one of their last five games in all competitions.

However, Wyness is adamant that Chelsea still intend to give the 44-year-old more time, and feels that he can steady the ship.

The Blues endured a torrid time last weekend, as they collapsed in the second half to slip to a 4-1 defeat against Leeds United on home turf.

The home fans chanted against Alonso at times during the game, and are beginning to make their discontent towards his methods known.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy business advising elite clubs – believes that Alonso has become the victim of another change of direction from Chelsea's board over the summer.

The capital club have become accustomed to signing promising young talent in recent years, but pivoted this summer to add veterans Emiliano Martinez, Jordan Henderson and Danny Welbeck to their group.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained that it takes time to adapt to heavy turnover of players each summer.

"I think he's going to get the full backing at the moment, unless there was a really bad run of results through to January, which I don't anticipate," he said.

"I think they've got to admit that there is a change here. When they (Chelsea) used to have a squad full of 23-year-olds and then we had a bit of a change getting Jordan Henderson and a couple of others with a bit of experience in there, it takes time to adapt and to change.

"And with a new manager coming in, they've had too many changes at the managerial and playing levels.

"I think Alonso is a good manager, I rate him and I think he'll get them turned around and I think he will get the full support from those above. So I don't anticipate ructions and I think it will turn around on the pitch pretty soon as well."

Football Insider revealed earlier this week that Chelsea will sack Alonso if they fall out of contention for Champions League qualification.

The board outlined a top-four finish as the "minimum expectation" at the start of the season, and the boss is currently on course to fail that task.

Sources say that Chelsea are aware that they cannot continue sacking managers as frequently as they have in recent years, and are keen to give Alonso as much time as possible.

However, with his team now sitting down in eighth, the former Real Madrid boss is running out of time to save his future.
