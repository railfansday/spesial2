£2.5m man’s performance against Swansea City could be the breakout West Brom have been waiting for: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

West Brom extended their unbeaten Championship run to seven games on Saturday with a 2-2 draw away at Swansea City. WBA took the lead twice but were pegged back on both occasions, with their biggest frustration being a missed opportunity to move 3-1 ahead.

Kareem Tunde took a significant step towards establishing himself at West Brom with an impressive display in draw. The winger provided the assist for Isaac Price’s early opener, offering a glimpse of the quality that convinced Albion to bring him to The Hawthorns from Levante in the summer.

It was only Tunde’s second Championship start of the season, having previously been restricted to brief substitute appearances. However, James Morrison’s decision to reward his impressive cameo against Millwall proved inspired.

Given space on the right in the seventh minute, the 21-year-old produced a low, sweeping delivery across the box, allowing Price to arrive at the far post and finish. Tunde continued to cause problems before being substituted after 75 minutes, having reportedly struggled with cramp.

Tunde’s performance was reflected in a 7.7 FotMob rating, his highest of the campaign. However, even without that assist, the winger showed he had plenty to offer and a lot more to his game.

He also registered one assist, created a big chance and recorded an impressive 0.81 expected assists (xA), according to FotMob. Those numbers underline his creative influence, particularly considering he has played just 195 Championship minutes across eight appearances this season.

There is plenty of potential for West Brom to develop. Tunde has managed nine shots, created four chances and completed three crosses in the league, while his 11 line-breaking passes demonstrate an ability to move possession into dangerous areas.

His successful dribble percentage remains low at 20 per cent, and he is still learning when to make the right decisions in the final third. Nevertheless, those imperfections should not overshadow the encouraging signs.

Tunde arrived with an interesting pedigree, having spent time in Barcelona’s famed La Masia academy between 2017 and 2020 before progressing through Levante’s system. He also gained valuable La Liga experience, making more than 20 senior appearances before joining WBA on a four-year contract in August.

The Express & Star described him as raw but lively, highlighting his unpredictability, clever body movement and potential to trouble defenders. His combination play with right-back Brayann Pereira was another positive from the Swansea encounter.

West Brom ultimately dropped two points despite taking the lead twice, but Tunde’s contribution was an encouraging individual development. With the Baggies having several injury concerns at the moment, and competition for attacking places remaining fierce, the youngster has an opportunity to build on this performance.

Albion have been waiting to see what their summer investment can offer, and Saturday’s outing may finally have provided the breakthrough.
