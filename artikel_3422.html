Spider-Man Officially Teams Up With Another ‘Daredevil’ Favorite for New Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

After becoming the most successful Marvel movie of all time, Spider-Man: Brand New Day is now looking to add even more to its unprecedented total with a theatrical re-release. Similar to Avengers Endgame: Encore, which pushed the MCU finale film past James Cameron's Avatar and also added a plethora of new footage that directly ties into the upcoming Avengers: Doomsday, the upcoming re-release for Tom Holland's latest Spidey adventure will feature several minutes of extra footage. However, that's not all, as the new extended cut will also feature an appearance from an MCU favorite who hasn't been seen in the universe since Luke Cage Season 2 in 2018.

Ahsoka star Rosario Dawson, who played the character of Claire Temple (AKA Night Nurse) in Daredevil, Jessica Jones, Luke Cage, Iron Fist, and The Defenders, will officially reprise her fan-favorite role in a now-restored deleted scene as part of the Spider-Man: Brand New Day re-release. Dawson previously confirmed that she filmed a scene for the film that didn't make the final cut of the original theatrical release, but now that wrong is being righted. Claire Temple made her MCU debut in Daredevil after she had a chance encounter with a badly injured Matt Murdock (Charlie Cox), which then led to her having run-ins with several other New York street-level heroes after they took a thrashing from various foes. She even had a brief romance with Luke Cage (Mike Colter) before he restarted his relationship with Jessica Jones (Krysten Ritter) sometime before the events of Daredevil: Born Again.

Claire Temple's return is just the latest example of Marvel finally bringing the Netflix catalog of characters to the wider MCU following a planned reset of those characters with Daredevil: Born Again that was quickly reversed after massive fan outcry. Tom Holland's Spider-Man has already crossed paths with Matt Murdock in Spider-Man: No Way Home, but Jon Bernthal's The Punisher plays an even bigger role in Brand New Day, with the violent vigilante becoming one of Peter Parker's most trusted allies. If these recent crossovers mean anything, it hopefully implies that a more proper team-up with Spider-Man and Daredevil (one where perhaps they even go toe-to-toe with Vincent D'Onofrio's Kingpin) could be in the cards.

Tom Holland's Spider-Man will return, but the question of when he'll be back still remains. Holland isn't currently listed as a cast member for Avengers: Doomsday, but the new crossover event is all but confirmed to have a plethora of surprise cameos, with both Mark Ruffalo's Hulk and Elizabeth Olsen's Scarlet Witch expected to appear following the end-credits scenes of Avengers Endgame: Encore and VisionQuest. Meanwhile, the end-credits scene of Spider-Man: Brand New Day teased that Tom Holland's webslinger will return to outer space, perhaps hinting at his participation in Avengers: Secret Wars, which would make perfect sense since that's when Peter Parker gets the symbiote suit in the comics.

The extended cut of Spider-Man: Brand New Day officially premieres in theaters on December 4, 2026. Stay tuned to Collider for more updates.
