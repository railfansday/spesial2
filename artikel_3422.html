James Bond Franchise Star Officially Reacts to Robert Pattinson Casting

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Since joining the team in 2024, she has written more than 1,000 news stories, with her work also cited by outlets like AOL and IMDb.

We finally have our new James Bond, but fans still aren't satisfied. As announced yesterday, Robert Pattinson has officially taken up the mantle, becoming the seventh actor to lead the long-running 007 franchise, following Sean Connery, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton, Pierce Brosnan, and Daniel Craig. Pattinson's casting came as quite a surprise after October reports named Callum Turner, Josh O'Connor, and Jack Lowden among the final candidates under consideration.

Amazon MGM Studios is aiming to release the next James Bond film in theaters in 2028, with production expected to begin sometime in 2027. Dune director Denis Villeneuve is at the helm, and Steven Knight (Peaky Blinders) is writing the script. David Heyman and Amy Pascal are among the producers, while casting is being handled by Nina Gold, who previously said that whoever inherits the role would need to commit to potentially three or more films.

Despite the mixed reception to the James Bond news, Villeneuve, who recently worked with Pattinson on Dune: Part Three, welcomed the actor to the beloved franchise in a statement, saying the actor "has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new." And as opinions continue to trickle in, Pattinson's former co-star recently weighed in during an interview with Andy Cohen on SiriusXM Front Row.

Ralph Fiennes, who appeared in three previous James Bond films and co-starred with Pattinson in Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire, sadly isn't ready to let go of Craig's Bond just yet. "I'm sorry. I'm in love with Daniel Craig still," Fiennes admitted. "It's hard. I've got the wrench in my heart." The Conclave star then clarified that he believes Pattinson is up to the task of playing Bond. "No, he's a great actor," he said. "I'm sure he'll be great."

Fiennes joined the James Bond universe in 2012's Skyfall as Gareth Mallory, a high-ranking intelligence official. At the end of the film, Judi Dench's MI6 chief, M, dies, and Mallory takes over as the new M. Fiennes reprised the role in 2015's Spectre and 2021's No Time to Die. He isn't the only former 007 actor to have shared the screen with Pattinson. Brosnan, who played Bond in four films from 1995 to 2002, portrayed the Twilight star's on-screen father in 2010's Remember Me. Pattinson has also worked with Jeffrey Wright, who portrayed Bond ally Felix Leiter in three of Craig's films. He and Wright co-starred in The Batman, where Wright played Detective Jim Gordon opposite Pattinson's Caped Crusader.
