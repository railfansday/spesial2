Rangers will surely look to scrap Middlesbrough agreement ASAP after Bristol City flashpoint

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Middlesbrough flew to the top of the Championship with a 2-0 home victory over Bristol City at the weekend, but one key decision made by Kim Hellberg will have got supporters talking.

Middlesbrough have claimed their spot at the summit of the Championship once again after a 2-0 win against Michael Skubala's struggling Bristol City side at The Riverside.

Stoke City's 1-0 win over Birmingham City has allowed Boro to leapfrog Blues, whilst the Potters' inferior goal difference sees Hellberg's side claim top spot for now.

It was a tight win for Boro in the end, who didn't seem wholly convincing, as one substitution made by Hellberg must have had some supporters scratching their heads, as action must be taken in January.

With Boro racing into a 2-0 lead just ten minutes into the second half, the Robins tried to fight their way back into the game and had much the better of chances in the second half.

The away outfit would strike the same post three times, as on another day, Boro may not have gotten so lucky.

With around 15 minutes to go, Hellberg looked to freshen things up and see the game out, as Sam Byram, Will Lankshear, and Luke Ayling replaced Callum Brittain, David Strelec, and Sebastian Berhalter.

Ayling, a natural defender, was put into midfield alongside Aidan Morris, as on-loan Rangers midfielder Connor Barron was left on the bench, and must be wondering exactly what he has to do to get a game.

Barron, 24, joined Middlesbrough on loan from Rangers late in the summer window, and it would be fair to suggest that, at this point, the move has been a waste of time for everyone involved.

The Scottish midfielder has failed to play a single minute of football across any competition since his move to Middlesbrough, including in the Championship, and with the 35-year-old Ayling preferred in midfield over him, his future at the Riverside appears bleak.

Joining the likes of Will Lankshear, Amario Cozier-Duberry, Berhalter, Ashley Phillips, and Radek Vitek to make the move to Teesside in the summer, Barron has largely been forgotten, as surely Boro and Rangers will come to some sort of agreement in January.

According to FootballInsider, back in September, Rangers were considering recalling Barron from his loan spell in England early, and two months on, his situation hasn't improved, and their decision to bring him back to Glasgow has surely only been made easier.

For Boro, Barron won't be a big miss at all, having failed to make any sort of impact during his spell with the club, and, in truth, never really being allowed to do so for one reason or another.

Whether he will return to Ibrox and play any sort of notable role under Derek McInnes remains to be seen, though his situation at Boro will surely alert other clubs across the Championship, who may feel as though he could have an impact with them instead.
