Billy Corgan’s Supergroup Zwan to Reissue Debut Album With 19 Unreleased Tracks

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Zwan, the short-lived supergroup led by Smashing Pumpkins’ Billy Corgan and Jimmy Chamberlin, will reissue their first and only album, Mary Star of the Sea, with a bunch of new archival material.

The reissue will feature a newly mixed version of Mary Star of the Sea, overseen by Corgan and engineer Nikola Dokic. It’ll also boast 19 previously unreleased demos and outtakes, released under the name Rooms Full of Salt. The reissue will be available as a special four-LP vinyl set on standard black vinyl and a limited edition “Ocean Illusionist” color variant. And it’ll bring Mary Star of the Sea to streaming services, where it’s been unavailable for years.

To tease the project, Zwan released two songs, “Declarations of Faith,” a track off the original album, and one of the unreleased tracks, “The Empty Sea.”

Vinyl pre-orders for the Mary Star of the Sea will begin this Friday, Nov. 27, with orders expected to ship out in December. (Mary Star of the Sea will hit streaming services after the vinyl editions are released, Corgan’s reps said, though an exact date wasn’t given.)

Corgan and Chamberlin formed Zwan in 2001 after the Smashing Pumpkins split. They filled out their ranks with three other Nineties alt rock vets, guitarists Matt Sweeney (Chavez, Skunk) and David Pajo (Slint), and bassist Paz Lenchantin (A Perfect Circle). Mary Star of the Sea was released in January 2003 and reached Number Three on the Billboard 200 Albums chart. Lead single, “Honestly,” also became a Top 10 hit on rock and alternative radio. Zwan disbanded in 2003.

Over the summer, Corgan revealed he was digging around the Zwan archives during an interview with Stereogum. “There will be, like, maybe two more LPs of unreleased tracks,” he said, adding, “Because there’s so much Zwan stuff, I’m gonna put it out in different sets, otherwise the box set would be, like, 20 records long, and it’s just too much to ask fans to take on. [There are] 60-plus unreleased Zwan songs that have never been released. Not just versions, like other songs.
