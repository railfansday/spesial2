Baltimore Man Pleads Guilty to Firearms Charges Connected to Verbal Dispute at Baltimore Metro Station

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Baltimore, Maryland – A Baltimore man pled guilty to federal firearm charges in court, today, stemming from a verbal altercation at a metro station. Willie Ruff, 37, is charged with possessing firearms as a prohibited person after he threatened an individual with a weapon at a Northwest Baltimore metro station.
