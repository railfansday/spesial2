Tennessee's stat leaders versus Wofford basketball

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

No. 9 Tennessee (1-0) defeated Wofford (0-1), 90-59, Tuesday in a season opener for both schools at Food City Center.

Rankings reflect the USA TODAY Sports men's basketball coaches poll.

DeWayne Brown II and Terrence Hill Jr. led the Vols with 17 points each. Tyler Lundblade scored 12 points and Troy Henderson finished with 10 points.

Brown II recorded a double-double and was Tennessee's leading rebounder (17). Jalen Haralson totaled six rebounds, while Miles Rubin and Juke Harris recorded four rebounds each.

Brown II and Hill Jr. recorded three assists each, while Rubin, Lundblade, Haralson, Harris and Manny Green finished with two assists each.

Tennessee forced 12 Wofford turnovers. Rubin and Hill Jr. totaled two steals each, while Haralson, Braeden Lue and Marquis Clark recorded one steal each.

Brown II had two blocks, while Rubin finished with one block.

Tennessee will play Nov. 9 against Radford. Tipoff is slated for 7 p.m. EST at Food City Center (SEC Network+).
