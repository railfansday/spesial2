Brock Purdy has golden opportunity to shake up MVP race vs. Vikings

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The San Francisco 49ers are facing off against the Minnesota Vikings in an NFC heavyweight battle on Sunday night in Mexico, with the No. 1 seed in the NFC on the line.

The 49ers have shown several different versions of themselves this year. We've seen dominant offensive performances throughout the year, as well as some strong defensive performances, like against the Denver Broncos.

Last week, it was the offense that led the way, as Brock Purdy threw for 389 yards and three touchdowns en route to the 46-40 win over the Dallas Cowboys. Normally, those numbers could be game-changing in the MVP race, especially considering how consistent Purdy has been this year.

But Cincinnati Bengals star Joe Burrow has also played exceptionally well to date and is now the runaway favorite for MVP. He's thrown for 2,795 yards (second in NFL), 27 touchdowns (first in NFL), and six interceptions, while completing an astounding 74.7 percent of his passes.

With Cincinnati sitting at 7-2, it makes sense that Burrow is the favorite for MVP, but that doesn't mean other quarterbacks like Josh Allen and Purdy still don't have a chance at the award. We saw how close the race between Drake Maye and Matthew Stafford ended up last year, and there is still a ton of regular-season football to play.

So far, Purdy has thrown for 2,369 yards, 20 touchdowns, and five interceptions, while his QBR is second in the NFL to Burrow. The biggest advantage for Purdy is that he's been the primary reason for the success of an 8-1 team. And this weekend, he'll take on the league's toughest coordinator and defense with the Minnesota Vikings, who just stifled the Green Bay Packers 30-10 on the road.

If there's any game the rest of the way that could really boost Purdy's odds for MVP, it would be this one: a primetime showdown between two top NFC teams with a ton of eyeballs on the game.

The Vikings have been excellent at throwing quarterbacks off their game defensively this season, with Brian Flores using a blitz-heavy approach to thwart quarterbacks and keep them off their game. Purdy, however, has been excellent against the blitz this season, ranking first in the NFL in passer rating against the blitz.

On Wednesday, Kyle Shanahan attributed a big part of the 49ers success amid all the moving parts to Purdy's poise.

"I just feel Brock plays the right way. Like, he just truly plays the position," Shanahan said. "He's not ever like, 'Hey, this guy's better than that guy, so I'm just going to force the ball to that guy.' Or, 'That guy didn't make the play last time, so I'm going to ignore him and go somewhere else.' Brock keeps a clear mind and just plays each play one at a time and does what he's supposed to do. If nothing's there or he can't see something or something doesn't work, then he tries to compete and make a play on his own.

"So, I don't think much changes. It's not like, 'Hey, I know you've been going to this guy all day, but he's not there so you've got to change something on you.' Brock, to me, it's the same for him, his same plan every week, regardless of who's in there. It can make it harder when some people aren't in there, but the plan never changes for him."

General manager John Lynch shared a similar sentiment this week, while believing in Purdy's preparation for the tough matchup ahead.

"I just can't say enough about Brock. Brock can play on time and rhythm, and he can also create and make things happen, as we saw the other day. You saw all of it on full display," Lynch said. "He's playing at an incredibly high level, and it's just really what we've come to expect. He's having an unbelievable season, and he knows the challenge he'll have this week against Minnesota, but I know that he'll be up for it."

This is the perfect game for Purdy to jump back into consideration for the MVP race alongside Joe Burrow, and the 49ers offense could potentially get Mike Evans back, although resting him for Week 12 against the Seattle Seahawks makes sense.

A lot will be on Purdy's shoulders, as well as Kyle Shanahan's, to devise a game plan against a defense that not many have had answers for, and it could be instrumental for the 49ers the rest of the season with the NFC race.
