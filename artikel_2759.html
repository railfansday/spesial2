Philadelphia Eagles vs. Pittsburgh Steelers live updates | Eagles dominate first quarter

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

PHILADELPHIA — The Philadelphia Eagles are hosting the Pittsburgh Steelers for an in-state, Week 11 showdown on Sunday at Lincoln Financial Field.

The Steelers (6-3) are 3.5-point betting favorites against the Eagles (2-7), according to FanDuel Sportsbook.

The Steelers haven't beaten the Eagles in Philadelphia since 1965. But Nick Sirianni's team being a home underdog shouldn't surprise anyone given how this season has gone.

The Eagles looking to snap a seven-game losing streak coming out of their bye week. It's been a brutal stretch, highlighted by injuries on both sides of the ball.

Eagles happy to close the half up 10 with a passive final drive. Saquon Barkley has nine carries for 59 yards including the 38-yard TD. Joe Milton 5-for-7 for 91 yards.

Steelers' Alex Highsmith had two early sacks but left the game. Aaron Rodgers was 17-for-20 for 95 yards.

Steelers finally on the scoreboard with a 25-yard field goal from Chris Boswell that capped a 77-yard drive. It took all half but Pittsburgh put together solid drive, though it stalled with nothing meaningful inside the 10.

Steelers putting together a competent drive finally with Rico Dowdle, Germie Bernard and Jaylen Warren doing the lifting. Steelers down to PHI 8 after starting on own 37.

Back-to-back sacks for the Eagles. Aaron Rodgers just ran himself into the second one for a Greenard sack. Eagles take over at own 28 after punt.

The 2-7 Eagles owned that first quarter. Three possessions, three scores.

Saquon Barkley breaks off a 38-yard touchdown run, finds space on the left edge and breaks it off down the sideline for the game's first TD. Full-stretch catch from DeVonta Smith a play earlier set it up.

Eagles making the Steelers pay for poor play so far in this one.

29-yard field goal for Jake Elliott and the Eagles score in their first two possessions. A couple big gains on 29-yard strike to Darius Cooper and 38-yard gain to Dallas Goedert. Drive stalls on a pair of penalties as Birds settle for field goal.

Jake Elliott hits 41-yard field goal to give Eagles points from short field after Steelers failed to convert 4th-and-1. 10:01 into first quarter.

Wyomissing graduate makes his NFL debut returning the opening kickoff.

The Eagles will continue to be short-handed on Sunday. All-Pro linebacker Zack Baun and wide receiver Dontayvion Wicks were placed on the IR on Saturday, making the task that much harder to pull off a moderate upset.

DeVonta Smith is back from a hamstring injury that cost him three games, and Saquon Barkley should be healthier while managing his hamstring injury over the bye week.

But Joe Milton will be at quarterback, behind an offensive line that has disappointed, and Vic Fangio's defense will be tasked with stopping a dynamite rushing attack without Baun — and with a surprisingly hampered Jalen Carter (quadriceps).

After struggling in Cincinnati last week, the Steelers will look to bounce back from a 38-18 drubbing that puts them behind the Bengals in the standings. With a tougher schedule than the Bengals, they will have to be close to perfect to close the gap.

It could be tougher, as the Steelers will be short-handed at edge rusher, with Nick Herbig (groin) sidelined for the first time this season. Second-year man Jack Sawyer will take over his spot in the rotation. K.J. Henry, a veteran edge rusher, was promoted from the practice squad.

One focus for the Steelers will be slowing down the Eagles' passing attack after Ja'Marr Chase, Tee Higgins, and Joe Burrow went over 400 yards collectively last week. They will have safety DeShon Elliott back, who missed last week with a knee injury.

Pittsburgh will also welcome back wide receiver Roman Wilson, who missed four games with a hand injury. Michael Pittman Jr. (foot) is not far away, but Wilson's speed and explosiveness should add another element the Steelers have sorely missed in their offense.
