Sportsbooks favor the Rams to make the playoffs

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Los Angeles Rams are currently on the outside looking in when it comes to the NFL playoffs. In the NFC, the Rams are currently ninth while only the top seven seeds make the postseason.

But while some media outlets currently say Los Angeles is an underdog to reach the postseason, one notable source is giving them more than a puncher's chance: sportsbooks.

While the Rams are in the middle of their sort-of-not-really bye week, they'll be focused intently a couple of games that could shift the playoff odds around the NFC. Obviously, they need to just keep winning to further solidify their status.

More than anything, though, this is implying that gamblers are more confident in the Rams than some of their peer teams in the NFC playoff race, specifically thinking of the Green Bay Packers.

How convenient that the Rams play the Packers in Week 12.

According to FanDuel, the current price for a bet on the Rams to make the playoffs is -116. That means the implied odds of Los Angeles being postseason bound is 53.7%.

It's even more noticeable at DraftKings, where the Rams are currently sitting at -128 to make the playoffs, or a 56.1% implied probability.

If you enjoy gambling and prefer a different book, that's fine, because seemingly across the board, the gambling world thinks more highly of the Rams as a playoff team than their counterparts.

Compare that to some of the other predictive sources and you see a different story. According to NFL.com, L.A. currently only has a 47% chance to make the postseason (still ahead of Green Bay, for what it's worth). The Athletic and its playoff predictor give the Rams the same chance. The venerable PlayoffStatus.com is even more bearish on the Rams, setting their current playoff likelihood at 41%. You get the gist.

It's fairly easy to understand the disparity between the gambling houses and the current math. While both are trying to predict the future, only one can account for the return of Myles Garrett, an increasingly more comfortable Aaron Donald and a Matthew Stafford who is finally starting to push the ball more. The traditional models don't include the same kind of context.

It goes without saying that nothing can perfectly predict what will unfold for the Rams and their playoff chances down the home stretch of the season, and that the best recipe for playing deep into January (and maybe later) is to simply win more games now.

Still, the oddsmakers like the Rams. Or at least, they like the Rams a bit more than their closest competition.
