Luther Burden III on track to play in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Chicago Bears WR Luther Burden III (concussion) fully participated in practice Friday, Nov. 20, and is not on the injury report for Week 11.

Why It Matters: Burden III has passed his concussion tests and should be able to handle his normal workload in Week 11. He is one of the main options in the passing game for the Bears and can be a No. 2 receiver in all fantasy leagues.
