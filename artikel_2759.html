Sean McVay: Aaron Donald never ceases to amaze me, he's one-of-one

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

For the first time in 1,050 days, Rams defensive tackle Aaron Donald recorded a sack when he brought down rookie Carson Beck during the second quarter of Sunday’s victory over the Cardinals.

It was the first of two takedowns he was involved in on the day, as he was also credited for a half-sack later in the afternoon.

After the contest, head coach Sean McVay told reporters Donald “never ceases to amaze me.”

“I’m never surprised,” McVay said in his press conference. “I think the term ‘one-of-one’ is thrown around way too loosely in this day and age, but it’s not with him. He’s one-of-one. He can do things, he’s done things and his impact — whether he has to say it or just show it, he’s a total ‘G.’ I love everything he’s about, and he is one-of-one.

“He’s had a tremendous impact on this team and it certainly is not exclusive to just getting a sack today. He’s made a tremendous impact. He’s a guy that everybody looks at and they say, ‘Watching the way that he moves — if you just mimic and emulate that, we’ll be in good shape.’”

Donald has tallied 15 total tackles with four tackles for loss, four QB hits, and now 1.5 sacks since returning from his retirement at the start of the season.
