Treasury yields fall as investors await key inflation signal

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

U.S. Treasury yields edged lower Wednesday as investors looked ahead to fresh inflation and spending data in a holiday-shortened trading week.

The 10-year Treasury note yield — the main benchmark for mortgages, auto loans and credit card debt — was 3 basis points lower at 4.8699%.

The yield on the 2-year Treasury note, which typically moves in line with short-term Federal Reserve interest rate decisions, was down more than 4 basis points at 4.6291%.

The longer-dated 30-year Treasury yield, which tends to track geopolitical events, was 2 basis points lower at 5.2695%.

One basis point equals 0.01%, or 1/100th of 1%, and yields and prices move inversely to one another.

The dip comes after yields steadied on Tuesday, with traders digesting softer demand for a 5-year Treasury auction.

With trading shortened this week due to the Thanksgiving holiday, investors are awaiting more data releases for clearer insights into the U.S. economic outlook.

October's personal consumption expenditures price index — the Federal Reserve's preferred gauge of underlying inflation — is expected to remain unchanged month-over-month at 0.3%, according to economists polled by Dow Jones.

Meanwhile, orders for U.S.-made durable goods in October — a measure of manufacturing activity and business spending — are forecast to rise to 0.2%, down from 1.8% in September.

October's personal income and spending figures from the Bureau of Economic Analysis are also due.

Bond markets will be closed all day Thursday for Thanksgiving, and will close at 2 p.m. E.T. on Friday
