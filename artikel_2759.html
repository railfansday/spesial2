UFC 334 broadcast team set: Joe Rogan calls Josh Hokit's first title fight

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The penultimate numbered event of 2026 goes down Saturday with UFC 334 from Madison Square Garden in New York.

As always, some marquee on-air talent will be there in "The Big Apple" for the event to help guide viewers through the experience.

Details of who will be working as commentators and analysts for the show have been acquired by MMA Junkie through a person with knowledge of the plans – and you can see the scheduled broadcast team below.

The entire UFC 334 card streams on Paramount+ in the U.S.

The main card lineup is scheduled to begin at 9 p.m. ET following prelims slated to start at 5 p.m. ET.

Megan Olivi hosts the UFC 334 weigh-in show on Friday at 8:50 a.m. ET/5:50 a.m. PT.

UFC Hall of Famers Daniel Cormier and Dominick Cruz, as well as "The Ultimate Fighter 15" winner Michael Chiesa serve as panelists on the weigh-in show.

Brendan Fitzgerald serves as desk anchor and host at UFC 334.

UFC Hall of Famer Cruz and Chiesa, as well as former interim lightweight champion Dustin Poirier, fill the desk analyst roles throughout the event and on the post-fight show.

UFC correspondent Olivi will conduct pre- and post-fight interviews backstage with some of the athletes on the UFC 334 card, as well as report additional real-time updates for the event.

Legendary octagon announcer Bruce Buffer will introduce the fighters and read official results at UFC 334.

Jon Anik will serve as the leading man on the mic from cageside at UFC 334.

He will command play-by-play alongside two color commentators: UFC Hall of Famer Daniel Cormier and Joe Rogan.
