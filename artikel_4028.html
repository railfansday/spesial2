A new Batman 2 leak has hit the internet, and it might confirm some fascinating details about the upcoming DC movie.

The leak comes courtesy of – surprisingly – a behind the scenes look at Heidi Klum's latest Halloween costume. Mike Marino's ProRenFX worked their magic for Klum's gruesome, severed hand look, and Marino is also working on The Batman 2 (and worked on the first film).

In the background of the makeup team's studio, a few intriguing details can be spotted. One is a container labeled "Zsaz," another labeled "Owl 3," another labeled "Koch," and finally a face mold that looks a lot like Brian Tyree Henry.

Heidi Klum’s Halloween makeup team has seemingly accidentally spoiled some major character secrets for ‘THE BATMAN: PART 2’ 👀🦇 • Brian Tyree Henry as Two-Face 🎭 • Sebastian Stan as Victor Zsasz 👩‍🦲 • Court Of Owls Mask 🦉 • Sebastian Koch will also have makeup ❔ https://t.co/DmAk90COfyNovember 1, 2026

Now, persistent rumors have been circulating that Sebastian Stan is not playing Two-Face, as reported, but is instead playing Victor Zsasz – the terrifying, bald serial killer who keeps a tally of his victims on his body.

The facial mold that looks like Brian Tyree Henry, meanwhile, bears a strong resemblance to Two-Face. While the disfigurement doesn't look too extreme, his upper lip is seriously wounded in a pattern that mirrors the character's look in Batman: The Animated Series. This would suggest that it's Henry, not Stan, playing the iconic villain.

The Court of Owls – a sinister group of Gotham's elite who wear creepy owl masks and control the city from the shadows – has also been heavily speculated to be part of the film, and the "Owl" container seems to confirm they'll factor in somehow. Sebastian Koch, meanwhile, is part of the cast, and the container featuring his name suggests his mystery role will require prosthetics of some kind.

It's hard to know for sure without any further context, but it does seem a lot of interesting details have been exposed thanks to this new video.
