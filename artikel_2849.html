Ensminger scores 15 in Santa Clara’s 95-72 win over Milwaukee

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

SANTA CLARA, Calif. (AP) — Jake Ensminger had 15 points in Santa Clara's 95-72 win against Milwaukee on Wednesday.

Ensminger also contributed seven rebounds for the Broncos (2-0). David Castillo scored 13 points and added three steals. Brad Longcor III finished with 11 points.

Aaron Womack led the Panthers (0-2) with 14 points and three blocks. Dylan Omweno added 12 points for Milwaukee. Al Amadou finished with 11 points.

Santa Clara took the lead for good with 19:41 remaining in the first half. The score was 46-27 at halftime, with Ensminger racking up nine points. Castillo led the way in the second half with nine points.

Up next for Santa Clara is a Saturday matchup with Sacramento State at home, and Milwaukee hosts Ripon on Sunday.
