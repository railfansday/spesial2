4 reasons why Seattle Seahawks can beat 49ers in Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

4 reasons why Seattle Seahawks can beat 49ers in Week 12 originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Coming out of their Week 11 bye, the Seattle Seahawks get to go up against the red-hot San Francisco 49ers. It's not going to be an easy matchup by any means.

That will likely be the case with their defense, as the Kyle Shanahan-led 49ers just had a massive week against the Minnesota Vikings' elite defense in Week 11. So, it wouldn't be a surprise if this Seahawks defense struggled.

However, there are four reasons why the Seahawks can beat the 49ers this week. ESPN Research and reporters Brady Henderson and Nick Waonger revealed the four key reasons why the Seahawks can beat the 49ers this weekend.

"The Seahawks are 2-0 off a bye week under Mike Macdonald, allowing an average of 15.5 PPG," ESPN Research shared.

This key statistic for the Seahawks is one reason why they should have some confidence in their team's ability to beat the 49ers in Week 12.

Mike Macdonald's teams have a lot of success coming off the bye, and that streak continuing this weekend wouldn't be a surprise, especially after the 49ers' big Week 11 win.

But that's not the only reason why the Seahawks can beat the 49ers. ESPN's Brady Henderson highlighted how quarterback Sam Darnold is "on track to return" to his QB1 job this week after two weeks out due to a quad and rib injury.

While there are some question marks surrounding Darnold's play this season, he helped them win the Super Bowl last season and is someone the Seahawks should have confidence in.

49ers reporter Nick Wagoner highlighted the two biggest reasons why Seattle might beat San Francisco in Week 12. They revolve around a key injury to right tackle Colton McKivitz and how good this Seahawks defense is.

"Seattle's ferocious defense ranks fourth in pass rush win rate (55.6%) and seventh in pressure percentage (37.5%), and it sacked QB Brock Purdy three times in the first meeting," Wagoner writes.

With the 49ers down their starting right tackle, facing off against a strong pass-rushing team that has already found success against a fully healthy 49ers OL this year, Brock Purdy and the 49ers offense might be in a little bit of trouble this week.

Vederian Lowe will need to step up in a big way for the 49ers as the new right tackle. But, with how good the Seahawks' defense is, it would not be a surprise if he struggled.

Even though the 49ers beat the Seahawks earlier in the year, with Darnold back, their strong matchup with a backup right tackle on the other side, and Macdonald's history coming out of the bye on their side, Seattle might be able to pull off the upset this week.
