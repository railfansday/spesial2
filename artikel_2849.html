Chargers-Jets Game Preview: Geno Smith’s health could decide Week 11 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Chargers finally got their first win of the season by beating the Ravens on Monday Night Football who were forced to start quarterback Jacoby Brissett due to both Lamar Jackson and Tyler Huntley being hurt. In Week 11, the Bolts could once again face a team without their starting quarterback if Geno Smith, who did not finish their most recent loss to the Bills, remains sidelined.

If that is the case, it would be backup Cade Klubnik manning the offense. Klubnik's first ever NFL action came in Week 10, meaning the Chargers would have a much higher chance of winning their second consecutive game should he earn the start.

If Smith does end up pushing through and starting, then I'd start viewing this matchup as a toss-up. Smith has 12 touchdowns to just three picks this year as he's gotten back to playing fairly good ball in his first season back with the Jets.

Defensively, the Jets are middle-of-the-pack. They rank 16th in total defense (332.1) and 22nd in scoring defense (25.4). The Chargers offense was better — at least on the ground — against the Ravens but the passing attacked remained underwhelming. Justin Herbert threw for just 38 yards on three completions in all of the final 30 minutes with only three players seeing more than two receptions, and only one of them was a wide receiver (Tre' Harris).

With the Jets ranking fifth in pass defense this season, the Chargers' game plan may end up being much of the same this week.
