Cowboys QB Dak Prescott admits trying to trick his mind amid 49ers fan takeover

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Dallas Cowboys quarterback Dak Prescott admitted he tried to convince himself that the sea of red at AT&T Stadium represented his high school rather than visiting San Francisco 49ers supporters. The unusual admission followed Dallas' 46-40 defeat, which added to the Cowboys' struggles under Brian Schottenheimer.

Prescott anticipated a significant visiting crowd before Sunday's matchup. However, seeing so many opposing fans inside his home stadium still made an impression.

The Cowboys quarterback acknowledged the overwhelming 49ers presence during his postgame comments.

"Tricked myself, told myself the red was for my high school," Prescott said.

"Unfortunately, their fans were more present than ours."

Prescott explained that he expected San Francisco supporters to travel in large numbers. He also emphasized that the atmosphere remained enjoyable despite the unusual balance of support.

The quarterback confirmed Dallas never needed to use a silent count, even with the considerable noise generated by visiting fans.

On the field, Prescott delivered five touchdown passes, but his impressive offensive performance wasn't enough to secure a victory. San Francisco quarterback Brock Purdy countered with 389 passing yards and three touchdowns.

The defeat highlighted another frustrating afternoon for Dallas, which struggled to contain the 49ers' explosive offense. San Francisco also forced two turnovers, including a late fumble recovery that sealed the result.

Prescott has previously defended Schottenheimer amid criticism, but the latest loss adds pressure on the Cowboys to turn their season around.

For Dallas, the disappointing result came with an uncomfortable reminder of its home-field challenges. Prescott's lighthearted attempt to reinterpret the crowd colors couldn't hide how strongly San Francisco supporters represented their team.
