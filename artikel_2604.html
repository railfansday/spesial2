Bears vs. Lions: How to Watch and Listen, Odds, Previews, and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The second-place Chicago Bears (8-2) will battle with the first-place Detroit Lions (also 8-2) in the early Thanksgiving game. It's a huge game on so many levels, with plenty of storylines to follow. The game should be done before your feast begins, so let's hope nothing too wild happens that could affect your appetite!

Before we get into the game details, let's take a moment to be thankful for Ben Johnson and Caleb Williams.

The hype for this one has been cranked up to 11, and that's even with the short week to build the buzz.

Here's everything you need to know for the game!

The Bears and Lions kick off at 12:00 pm CT on Thursday, November 26, from Ford Field in Detroit, Michigan. It's a domed stadium, so the weather will not be a factor.

The game will be on CBS, with No. 1 team Jim Nantz and J.J. Watt on the call.

If you live anywhere on this map, you can see the game! Also, caramel apple pie with vanilla ice cream never lets me down on Thanksgiving.

ESPN Chicago (1000 AM, 100.3 FM HD2) with Jeff Joniak (play-by-play), Tom Thayer (analyst), and Jason McKie (sideline) on the call, and LATINO MIX 93.5 FM (Spanish) with Omar Ramos.

The Bears are 1.5-point favorites, with the game total over/under at 55.5.
