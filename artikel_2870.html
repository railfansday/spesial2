NFLPA names 49ers DL Osa Odighizuwa their Community MVP for Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The NFL Players Association announced on Friday that they have named 49ers defensive lineman Osa Odighizuwa as their Community MVP for Week 11.

Odighizuwa, who was also a Community MVP last season, recently held events in Dallas, San Jose and Portland. Odighizuwa’s foundation teamed with Progressive Insurance to provide a new car to a veteran in Dallas and Odighizuwa distributed 500 turkeys in the other two cities to local residents.

“I appreciate everything the NFLPA does for us as players, and I’m honored to be named the Week 11 NFLPA Community MVP,” Odighizuwa said. “Giving back to Portland, Dallas and the Bay Area means a lot to me because each of those communities has played a part in my journey. I hope this recognition encourages my fellow union members to use their platforms to lift others up in the communities they call home.”

The NFLPA will donate $10,000 to Odighizuwa’s foundation and he will join the other weekly MVPs in being eligible for the Alan Page Award at the end of the season.
