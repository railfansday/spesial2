Why United Natural Foods Rallied Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Even as the market fell, United Natural Foods rallied on the back of a Wall Street upgrade.

Shares of natural foods wholesaler United Natural Foods (UNFI +8.13%) rallied on Tuesday, up 7.6% as of 2:35 p.m. EDT, in defiance of the downturn in the broader indexes.

United Natural received an upgrade from a Wall Street analyst today, who is optimistic about the company's self-help improvements and the backdrop of more consumers likely moving toward food-at-home amid the recent bout of inflation.

Today, the sell-side analyst team at Goldman Sachs (GS -1.59%) increased its rating on United Natural to Buy from Neutral, while increasing its price target on the stock from $49 to $60, relative to the $52 price as of this writing.

Goldman's case consists of two parts: first, that the current inflation backdrop is favorable to more consumers opting to eat at home rather than eat out, and second, that United's operational improvements are playing out as management intended.

The fruits of those operational improvements were evident in the recently reported fiscal fourth quarter, which ended in August. Although United's revenue slightly decreased, this was mainly due to a strategic, intentional optimization of the business to focus on more profitable operations. This included closing and consolidating some distribution centers, while walking away from unprofitable supply contracts.

While revenue declined slightly, the optimization efforts significantly boosted profitability, with adjusted EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization) increasing by 48.3%. The company also guided to roughly high-single-digit growth in revenue and adjusted EBITDA for the year ahead at the midpoint of guidance.

United Natural has seen its stock surge nearly 55% this year, but it was also coming from a very low valuation before its turnaround took hold. Management just guided toward $300 million in free cash flow for the year ahead, against just a $3.1 billion market cap, or just over a 10x multiple.

United Natural has about $1.5 billion in net debt, bringing its enterprise value to about $4.5 billion, but that's still a reasonable 15x multiple of its free cash flow. All in all, United Natural still looks like a potential buy for those seeking greater exposure to consumer staples.
