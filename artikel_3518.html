Harry Shum Jr. Reacts to 'DWTS' Elimination, 'Bullying' Throughout Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Harry Shum Jr. is speaking out after being sent home from Dancing With the Stars just one week shy of the finale.

“I oddly feel at ease. I don’t feel sad — it’s really weird,” Shum, 44, told E! News after the season 35 semifinals on Tuesday, November 17. “I kind of felt like we accomplished everything we wanted to.”

Shum and pro Jenna Johnson earned back-to-back perfect scores on Tuesday night, receiving 10s across the board for their paso doble and quickstep. Their second dance was performed to “Valerie,” a subtle nod to Shum’s late Glee costar Naya Rivera.

“It felt like that song alone meant so much to so many people, and even to us,” he said after the show. “It was a moment where you kind of saw the underdog be able to have the spotlight, and I always remember that feeling.”

While Shum stayed optimistic post-elimination, Johnson, 32, admitted she was looking forward to their “really fun” finale plans.

“That’s the part that I’m most heartbroken about,” she told E! News. “We had concocted this incredible journey that the ballroom won’t see this time. But maybe we’ll put it out there somewhere.”

The pair also reflected on the “chaotic” conversations online this season, with Johnson joking that the cast is “a bit all trauma bonded.”

“There’s a lot of trauma bond just because this year has been a little crazy,” Shum added, noting that he hopes to “get to know” his competitors more outside of the ballroom. “Social media is a little nuts. I hope they address that at a certain point, because I think there’s a good amount of bullying that doesn’t feel really good, and I think the rest of the cast feels it.”

Shum previously spoke about the show’s passionate fandom in October, reminding viewers at the time that the experience should be “joyous” for everyone. (The message came amid drama surrounding Johnson’s husband, Val Chmerkovskiy, who was briefly suspended this season for controversial comments toward judge Carrie Ann Inaba.)

With Shum and Johnson out of the running, the remaining five couples — Amber Glenn and Pasha Pashkov, Connor Wood and Rylee Arnold, Ezra Frech and Daniella Karagach, Maura Higgins and Mark Ballas and Tatyana Ali and Jan Ravnik — will face off one more time in an epic three-hour finale on Tuesday, November 24.

Before bidding farewell to the ballroom, Shum offered some words of wisdom to the duos who are still standing.

“I would say you are good enough,” he shared. “You deserve to be where you’re at. Love this experience, and I think we’ve all been able to do something really incredible in our own way.”

Dancing With the Stars airs on ABC and Disney+ Tuesdays at 8 p.m. ET.
