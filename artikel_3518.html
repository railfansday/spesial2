Bella Hadid Details 'Intense' Desire to Have 'Lots of Children'

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Bella Hadid is opening up about her hopes to have children after making her romance with Nathan Lacy Instagram official.

“Success, for me, can be living in peace, in a small town, with a man that I love, with my child, having my animals,” Hadid, 30, told Harper’s Bazaar in an interview published on Monday, November 23.

The supermodel shared with the magazine that she also has goals that don’t involve walking the runway, including operating a local small business, learning hobbies and getting married and becoming a mother.

“I’ve always wanted to start a farm-to-table bakery, with flowers, and everything comes from the community. I want to do things that are fulfilling for me in my soul: having a greenhouse that I can garden in, having my glassblowing and ceramic studio, and having lots of children and being a mother,” she said. “It wasn’t a dream that I’ve always had, but it’s something that now is so intense for me. My intuition is screaming so loudly at me about it.”

Days prior, Hadid went Instagram official with Lacy by sharing a series of photos from their outing to a Colorado Avalanche game.

“Let’s go Avs!!!!!! 🏒 Saturday night was especially meaningful. 🫶Honoring Hockey Fights Cancer. Thank you @coloradoavalanche for using the power of sport for something so much bigger than the scoreboard,” Hadid wrote on November 7.

“To those who have fought, those still fighting, those we’ve lost, and the loved ones standing beside them every step of the way. This night was for you and im so glad I could have been there!! 💜” she continued. “I’ve always loved hockey living in NYC. And being a Colorado girl at heart, growing up here since i was young, I guess it was only a matter of time before I found my way to the @coloradoavalanche.”

Hadid and Lacy have been romantically linked since February, when they were spotted together during Milan Fashion Week. One month earlier, Us confirmed that Hadid had split from pro equestrian Adan Banuelos.

“It was after the holidays and is still very recent and fresh,” an insider told Us at the time, adding that Hadid had thrown herself “back into work” and had been “very focused” on her career.

The source shared that Hadid and Banuelos “both had busy schedules and were long distance” for a while, which “wasn’t working out.” Hadid had also been focused on her well-being amid Lyme disease complications, sharing that she “is feeling a little bit better and her health has improved.”
