Week 10 NFL scores, live updates: Travis Kelce questionable to return with ankle injury; Kirk Cousins returns to Minnesota

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Welcome to Week 10's Sunday game slate! We've got a jam-packed day to follow up on the morning's action, where the New England Patriots and Detroit Lions kicked off the Sunday slate from Munich, Germany.

Follow along with Yahoo Sports for live updates, highlights and more from Week 10.

The matchup between the Texans and Browns has been very one-sided early. Leading 10-0, CJ Stroud threw a pass back toward the center of the field after rolling right and found Xavier Hutchinson who took it for the 11-yard score.

The second quarter almost could not have begun worse for Green Bay.

The Packers started on the 13-yard line. Tight end Tucker Kraft was whistled for a false start, backing the offense up five yards.

Quarterback Jordan Love then threw an interception in the end zone and it was Harrison Smith who picked off his 41st pass of his illustrious career.

Green Bay is driving as the first quarter ends. When play resumes, the Packers will have it second and nine on the Minnesota 13-yard line, trailing 10-0.

Jonathan Taylor ended up not being moved at the trade deadline, but he is not the first Coots RB to score a TD in this week's game against the Dolphins.

That honor belongs to Seth McGowan, who scored from four yards out for the first points of the game.

The Titans-Jaguars game had two very quick turnovers.

First, Jacksonville quarterback Trevor Lawrence had a pass hit at the line that bounced in the air and right into the arms of a Tennessee defender.

A few plays later, Jaguars cornerback Travis Hunter was able to just get his hands in front of the intended receiver of a Cam Ward pass and came away with the diving interception.

The Bills had the ball at the goal line and quarterback Josh Allen bulldozes his way into the end zone for the score.

Kirk Cousins' first TD in his return to Minnesota came at an unexpected time. Cousins and the Vikings were facing 3rd and 23 after a facemask penalty.

Under pressure, Cousins got the ball away to the left. Myles Price happened to be turning when he caught it, and the Green Bay defender ended up not tackling him, but pushing him into space. He ran all the way to the end zone for his first career TD.

After the extra point, the Vikings lead 10-0.

The Packers were rolling, including a little trick play with a direct snap to Tucker Kraft, who flipped it to quarterback Jordan Love, who then threw it for a 15-yard completion to Christian Watson.

After that, though, Green Bay had back-to-back runs that combined for three yards, and then Vikings defender Dallas Turner logged his eighth sack of the season to force a field goal attempt.

Packers kicker Trey Smack lined up for the 53-yarder, but pushed it wide right.

New Orleans' first drive of the game ended abruptly, when a pass from Tyler Shough careened off his pass-catcher and into the air. It came down right into the waiting arms of Panthers linerbacker Devin Lloyd.

Carolina was unable to do anything with the turnover, as head coach Dave Canales rolled the dice and had his team go for it on fourth and short near the goal line, but they were stopped.

Kansas City scored a touchdown on its first drive, but did not come away from it unscathed.

Starting tight end Travis Kelce had a nice 23-yard reception, but he was taken down from behind and landed awkwardly.

Kelce was slow to get up, limped off the field and then walked with trainers to the locker room.

The Jaguars had little difficulty getting down the field and scoring on their first drive against the Titans.

Jacksonville ended the 10-play drive with a touchdown as quarterback Trevor Lawrence hit Parker Washington in the back of the end zone and the wideout was somehow able to keep his feet in bounds.

The Chiefs are without star running back Kenneth Walker III on Sunday who is out with an injury. This thrust rookie backup Emmett Johnson into the starting role against the Falcons, and he got involved early.

Patrick Mahomes swung a pass to him out left and Johnson took it 12 yards for the score.

The Vikings got on the board first with a field goal by Will Reichard. Minnesota took over with great field position after the Andrew Van Ginkel fumble recovery, but the offense couldn't make anything of it.

The short drive was highlighted by Aaron Jones picking up eight yards. Kirk Cousins has still not been able to pick up a first down, but the Vikings lead thanks to Reichard's 34-yard field goal.

The Texans get on the board first against the Browns. CJ Stroud zipped a quick pass to Nico Collins in the end zone. The ball was perfectly placed and the wide receiver held on for the score.

Minnesota got the ball back after the Packers ran just two plays. After a run for no gain, Green Bay quarterback Jordan Love completed a pass to Mark Redman for a gain of four.

Vikings LB Andrew Van Ginkel forced the fumble and Minnesota was able to recover and take over with great field position.

The Vikings were unable to capitalize on their first drive with Kirk Cousins back under center. Cousins began the drive with an incomplete pass to Justin Jefferson.

Running back Aaron Jones picked up three yards on first down, but then Cousins' third-down completion to Myles Price lost four yards with the Packers' defense all over it.

The Titans are at least looking good for Sunday's divisional game against the Jaguars. Tennessee is rocking its "Music City" jerseys.

The Jaguars ruled out DT Jordan Davis and S Jessie Bates III for Sunday's game against the Titans.

The Jaguars acquired both Davis and Bates ahead of the 2026 trade deadline.

The Jaguars acquired Davis from the Eagles for a 2027 first-round pick and a 2029 second-round pick. The Jaguars added Bates from the Falcons for a 2027 second-round pick and a 2028 fifth-round pick.
