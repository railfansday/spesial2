NFL flexes injury-riddled Patriots out of SNF in Week 12, swaps in Raiders and Fernando Mendoza

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The NFL announced Tuesday that it would switch to the Week 12 schedule. Originally, the New England Patriots were scheduled to face the Los Angeles Chargers on "Sunday Night Football."

Now, the league updated the schedule and moved three games around. The Patriots and Chargers will kick off at 4:05 p.m. ET, which bumps the Jacksonville Jaguars and Tennessee Titans' AFC South divisional battle to 1 p.m. ET.

The new "Sunday Night Football" matchup will see the Las Vegas Raiders go on the road to take on the Cleveland Browns.

No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza made his NFL debut last week against the Seattle Seahawks. Las Vegas lost and fell to 4-6. Mendoza finished with 143 passing yards passing, no turnovers and a rushing TD.

If he avoids injury against the Denver Broncos in Week 11, Mendoza would be in line for his first primetime start against the Browns.

Cleveland is 6-4 and third in the AFC North. Quarterback Deshaun Watson leads the league in fourth-quarter comeback wins. So while Browns games may not have been the most exciting early on, they tend to have fireworks late.

New England and Los Angeles looked like a blockbuster matchup when the schedule dropped this offseason. After 10 weeks, though, the Patriots are preparing to be without quarterback Drake Maye for the foreseeable future after he suffered an injury in last week's loss to the Detroit Lions.

The Chargers picked up a big win Monday night against the Baltimore Ravens, but it was their first win of the year, and they are now 1-8.

For now, the league puts the No. 1 pick in primetime as Las Vegas gears up to take on the Broncos this week. Cleveland is on bye and should be well rested when the Browns host the Raiders the following week.
