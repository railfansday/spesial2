NHL rumors: Wild linked to Penguins’ Evgeni Malkin after Dylan Larkin miss

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

NHL rumors: Wild linked to Penguins' Evgeni Malkin after Dylan Larkin miss appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Pittsburgh Penguins are struggling through a tough season. As a result, some players including veteran forward Evgeni Malkin are being discussed as trade candidates. At least one other NHL team is being linked with Malkin.

That club is the Minnesota Wild, per The Fourth Period.

"Malkin, 40, comes with a $5.5 million salary cap hit, but he can earn an additional $3.25 million this season if he achieves all of his bonuses," the outlet reported.

This season with the Penguins, Malkin has posted 18 points in 19 games. He is second on the team in points.

"Malkin has remade himself as a playmaking winger in the twilight of his NHL career and has quietly been effective in that role. There's bound to be some pull to remain a career Penguin, but a midseason trade to a contender can't be ruled out," The Athletic reported. "Malkin's contract calls for him to submit a three-team list of places he's willing to go in the new year, and there's a sense that he's at least intrigued by the possibility of trying to chase down another Stanley Cup. He remains a strong skater and big-game competitor."

Minnesota is looking to bolster the roster and return to the Stanley Cup Playoffs this season. The Wild have 41 points on the campaign. The club tried to reach a deal with the Detroit Red Wings for Dylan Larkin, but it didn't work.

Minnesota is pivoting after Larkin went to the Montreal Canadiens.

"Wild GM Bill Guerin has been exploring the market and evaluating his options, one of whom could be a player he once played with and won a Stanley Cup with," The Fourth Period reported.

Time will tell if Malkin is on the move. The NHL trade deadline this season is March 1, 2027.
