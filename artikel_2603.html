Ghilardi, Dybala and Malen return to Roma&#8217;s XI vs Monza

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Gian Piero Gasperini surprised everyone with the lineup he fielded at the Parc des Princes against PSG: Malen, Dybala, and Cristante were out, with Castro Pisilli and De Roon replacing them.

There were no surprises in defense or on the flanks with Mancini, Ndicka, Hermoso, Molina, and Wesley, but the starting eleven, which included Koné and Soulé, impressed the experts.

The Giallorossi coach is now considering the best options to win Sunday's match against Monza. Several changes are expected, starting in attack, with Malen ready to lead his teammates from the start.

Gasperini saw several players tired after PSG-Roma. The Giallorossi team ran hard, the tempo was extremely high from start to finish.

As reported by Tuttosport, the Rome native's coach is ready to make several changes to the lineup that started at the Parc des Princes. Svilar remains in goal.

A shakeup is expected in defense, with Ghilardi and Balerdi likely replacing Mancini and Ndicka, while Hermoso is ahead of Koulierakis.

Lulli and Wesley are warming up on the flanks, with Molina destined for the bench. In midfield, the Cristante and Koné pairing are expected to return, with Dybala and Soulé appearing behind Malen. Rodrigo Mora, who remained on the bench at the Parc des Princes, is eager to return. Gasperini has plenty of options.
