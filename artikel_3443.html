‘Game of Thrones’ Officially Brings Kit Harington’s Jon Snow Back in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Game of Thrones fans have had more content than usual to enjoy this year, and there are still plenty of new releases on the horizon to be excited about. Within the first few weeks of the year, HBO introduced the second original Game of Thrones spin-off, A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms, starring Peter Claffey and Dexter Sol Ansell. The show is a personal favorite of George R. R. Martin, who wrote the series of Dunk and Egg novellas that inspired it. A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms Season 2 is already confirmed to return in the first half of 2027, but the same can’t be said for the other spin-off, House of the Dragon. The fourth and final season of the Targaryen spin-off will not shoot until 2027, meaning it isn’t expected to air until 2028.

Earlier this year, Warner Bros. also announced it was working with HBO to develop a live-action Game of Thrones movie that will be released in theaters on June 1, 2029. A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms director Owen Harris has been tapped to direct the film with a script from Andor's Beau Willimon and Mattson Tomlin, who also penned The Batman Part 2.

Game of Thrones fans also have War for Westeros to be excited about, which is a new Game of Thrones video game set in Westeros that will give players the chance to rewrite history and live out some of the most famous events from the show. Kit Harington's Jon Snow is even returning in the game, and players will be able to use his character to take down The Night King, rewriting one of the most frustrating wrongs from the final season.

Kit Harington has all but confirmed that he's done with the Game of Thrones franchise when it comes to playing Jon Snow in live-action, citing a desire to move on to other projects after spending 10 years of his career focused so intensely on one role. Harington has also been cast in another massive fantasy franchise, HBO's Harry Potter series, where he will play Gilderoy Lockhart. He recently reunited with his long-time Game of Thrones co-star Sophie Turner for the bonkers horror thriller, The Dreadful, which is now streaming on Prime Video.
