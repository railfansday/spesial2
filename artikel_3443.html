OpenAI expanding ChatGPT dot access to iMessage and RCS

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Well this is pretty cool. OpenAI is expanding ChatGPT dot access to iMessage and RCS.

Before now, the proactive agent feature was limited to either Slack or access inside the ChatGPT experience on mobile, desktop, and the web. You needed the main ChatGPT app to interact with your dot.

In theory, this helps bring ChatGPT dot to the Apple Watch in an official capacity, though we’ll need to test this to be sure. It also opens up new ways to use voice potentially beyond the built-in “calling” simulated capability.
