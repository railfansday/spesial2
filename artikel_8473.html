Although I'm concerned about issues like identity theft generally (and have been experienced it myself), I see extracting data from old hard drives as being the least likely of potential exploits. If someone is concerned enough to destroy a hard drive, surely any sensitive data (probably all the data) on the drive has always been either encrypted or wiped (or both.) I just take my hard drives and other electronics to Best Buy or similar for recycling and don't feel I'm exposing myself to any risk at all when I do that. I hope that someone will be able to extract some value from recycling them, but don't actually know what happens to the various parts.
