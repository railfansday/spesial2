Do you have a reasoned plan for tax management that takes into account ACA subsidies / phaseouts, Roth conversion opportunities, and RMDs?
In short, yes. Good question, and sorry for the slow reply. Your question spurred me to revisit all my numbers, and I wanted to answer after that.

ACA is the main constraint before Medicare. I manage MAGI to stay under the 400% FPL cliff with a small margin. Taxable sales go out by tax lot, highest basis first, so realized gains stay small and mostly land in the 0% LTCG range. When spending runs past what the ACA limit allows, Roth basis covers the difference because it doesn’t touch MAGI. Crossing the cliff is a last resort.

I do Roth conversions late in the year, once I know that year’s MAGI, and fill whatever room is left under the ACA line and the lower brackets. Heavy spending years leave little room, so conversions shrink. The ladder would live in the IRAs, where its payouts get reinvested and the traditional balance keeps growing, which makes the conversions matter more.

RMDs are a long way off, so for now I treat them as a reason to fill the low brackets early. Once we’re within two years of Medicare I’ll watch IRMAA too. If you see something I’ve missed, I’d like to hear it.
