First-year attorneys average $131K at plaintiffs firms, new study finds

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

First-year attorneys receive an average of $131,313 per year at plaintiffs law firms and might receive a wide range of raises and bonuses, according to the National Plaintiffs’ Law Association’s inaugural salary report. (Photo by Andriy Onufriyenko/Getty Images)

First-year attorneys receive an average of $131,313 per year at plaintiffs law firms and might receive a wide range of raises and bonuses, according to the National Plaintiffs’ Law Association’s inaugural salary report.

The study, which surveyed 80 plaintiff-side attorneys in late 2024 and early 2025, found that the salary amount corresponded to the size of the firm. Plaintiffs firms with more than 100 attorneys averaged $160,857 per year, while those with fewer than 20 averaged $106,111 per year.

In addition, the study found that bonuses averaged $21,195, with the median for bonuses at $15,000.

While 21.5% of firms surveyed had no policy on structured or lockstep pay raises, about 49% did. Nearly 17% had a purely discretionary or merit-based raise structure.
