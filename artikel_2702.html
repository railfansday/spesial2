'Selectors are making a blunder': Krishnamachari Srikkanth questions India's handling of Vaibhav Sooryavanshi

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Former India captain and chief selector Krishnamachari Srikkanth has launched a strong criticism of the selectors and team management over their handling of teenage batting sensation Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, insisting that the youngster deserves a consistent run in the Indian side. Srikkanth believes the 15-year-old has the potential to become a match-winner across formats and even play a significant role in reviving Test cricket globally.Speaking at the sports event on Friday, Srikkanth said the selectors and team management were making a mistake by not providing Sooryavanshi with enough opportunities.

Srikkanth questioned the decision to pick Sooryavanshi for one match and then leave him out for several games, stressing that the youngster needed sustained opportunities to prove his abilities."What they (selectors and team management) are doing with him is not right. They are not encouraging him; they are discouraging him, which is not done. These guys are making a blunder. They play him for one match, they don't play him for five matches. That's not done. Sad," Srikkanth said at the event.

Going a step further, Srikkanth suggested that Sooryavanshi's success in the longest format could generate renewed interest in Test cricket, not just in India but across the cricketing world."If Sooryavanshi clicks in Test cricket, a Test cricket revival happens all over the world, not just for India, all over the world. Today in New Zealand, when he was playing the T20 matches, people came all the way -- a full crowd -- to watch Sooryavanshi bat. Wherever he goes, everybody comes to watch Sooryavanshi bat," he said.He called on the selectors to show faith in the youngster and give him a sustained run instead of making frequent changes to his selection."This boy has done so brilliantly for you -- let him play all the matches. Do you mean to say he is not good enough to play Test or One Day cricket? He is 100 per cent sure to play good One Day cricket. Give him 20 innings, tell him he won't get dropped -- you do whatever you want. That boy will win five to seven matches single-handedly for you."And if he scores in Test cricket, the whole world will start reviving Test cricket everywhere, in any part of the world," he added.
