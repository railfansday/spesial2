Frank Reich: I'm the Jets' offensive coordinator, our inability to score starts with me

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Jets haven’t scored a touchdown since the third quarter of their Week 8 loss to the Raiders. It’s now Week 11, and Jets offensive coordinator Frank Reich knows it’s past time for him to find a way to get his team into the end zone.

Reich said today that he believes the Jets have enough talent on offense to produce, and he and the coaching staff need to make it happen.

“I’m the offensive coordinator, it starts with me,” Reich said. “I, and we as a staff, have to put the players in position to do what they do. We’ve got a very talented group, so we need to do a better job coaching.”

Over the last nine quarters of play, the Jets have nine Jason Sanders field goals, and no other points. Reich is well aware that three points a quarter isn’t going to win many games.

“Obviously, players need to do their part and execute and make plays,” Reich said. “For all of us, it’s a collective effort. We know we need to put more points on the board, whether it’s in the red zone or whether it’s further out, we still want to be explosive. We need to get back on track there. It’s been nine quarters. Believe me, I feel every play of that nine quarters.”
