OHSAA soccer tournament scores tonight. Girls state semifinals

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

In two days, we'll know the matchups for every boys and girls soccer state final.

Girls soccer will play state semifinals on Nov. 10 and boys soccer will do the same on Nov. 11.

From the Akron area, Hudson will play in Division II semifinal on the girls side. The boys will have Revere vying for a spot in the Division III state final.
