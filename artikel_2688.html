Ayden Johnson scores 27 as Merrimack takes down Vermont 75-57

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

HEMPSTEAD, N.Y. (AP) — Ayden Johnson's 27 points helped Merrimack defeat Vermont 75-57 on Friday.

Johnson shot 9 of 14 from the field, including 5 for 8 from 3-point range, and went 4 for 4 from the free-throw line for the Warriors (2-4). Denver Anglin scored 15 points, going 5 of 15 (3 for 9 from 3-point range). AJ Lesburt Jr. shot 4 for 9 (2 for 6 from 3-point range) and 3 of 4 from the free-throw line to finish with 13 points, while adding six rebounds.

John Broom finished with 18 points and seven rebounds for the Catamounts (3-5). Vermont also got 15 points from Khalil Badru. Hayden Johns also had eight points and eight rebounds.
