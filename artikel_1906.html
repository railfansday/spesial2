Insider updates on Man United 'unsettling' Lewis Hall as he reveals 'what people are thinking'

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Manchester United have a limited chance of signing Newcastle United left-back Lewis Hall any time soon.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the England international remains "happy" on Tyneside.

The 22-year-old only signed a new contract with the Magpies back in September, but that has done little to quell the Red Devils' long-term interest in him.

However, Wyness believes that Man United simply no longer have the pulling power that they once had, given their struggles in recent years.

Mauricio Pochettino's side are in the market for a new left-back this winter, with Hall one of their top targets once again.

However, Wyness has no doubt that the Old Trafford outfit will have to look elsewhere, with Hall having no desire to move right now.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that Man United won't be able to "unsettle" Hall within the next 12 months,

Their issues have been clear to see in recent months, and Wyness doesn't see a way that Newcastle's star would be willing to join them right now.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained how Hall will have plenty of chances for a big move later down the line if he continues to improve at Newcastle.

"I think he's going to be quite happy staying at Newcastle, so I don't see him moving. I think his career is right to stay where he is right now. I think a few more years there won't do any harm," he said.

"There'll be plenty of time for moves further down the road for him. United can dream, and they can try and unsettle players, but a lot of people are thinking now United isn't what it used to be.

"Hall would be better off staying where he is in a more settled team and staying with the growth that Newcastle are trying to promote themselves right now."

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider on Saturday that Newcastle have now exhausted all their options to try and keep Hall long-term.

The ex-recruitment man outlined that Matthias Jaissle's side have "done all they can" to protect their asset, but the interest in the starlet still hasn't died down.

Brown suggested that the ball is almost entirely in Hall's court in terms of where he sees his own future, with Newcastle running out of options to tempt him to stay.

The left-back shone against Lamine Yamal in England's recent win over Spain, and that has only served to fuel further speculation over his future.
