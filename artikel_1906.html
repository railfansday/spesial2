Stefan Borson: Man United, Liverpool and Tottenham to 'split the pot' as 'joint claimants' against Man City

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Manchester United, Tottenham Hotspur and Liverpool could join forces in a compensation claim against Manchester City.

That is according to former Man City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that the Premier League trio could work in tandem in a more efficient attack.

The Citizens are still awaiting the result of their appeal after being found guilty of 114 finance-related charges dating back to between 2009 and 2018.

In the event that Man City's appeal fails, several clubs are queueing up to benefit, all with different arguments about how they've been impacted.

It has now been revealed by The Guardian that Man United, Spurs and Liverpool are all using the same law firm to prepare their legal battle.

It is believed that the trio could target hundreds of millions of pounds should City's 114 charges stand against the appeal.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson believes that three clubs could band together, rather than take individual approaches.

"So, I think the idea is that they would be joint claimants, but it's hard to know whether they'd all go in one group claim and then split the pot around their individual losses," he said.

"Or whether they're all separate cases. I would think that the use of one firm suggests that those three are going to try and push it as a group.

"And they could split the costs. I think it's likely to be a more efficient way of dealing with it.

"I would think, given the scope of the decision and the length of the time period, tens of millions of pounds are at stake for each club."

Instead of joining the trio, Arsenal are working with a different law firm, HFS Kramer, to prepare their own separate claim.

Borson told Football Insider that it will be "interesting" to se what the Gunners do and how they approach the situation.

Either way, the entire situation is a very worrying one for the supporters at the Etihad Stadium, who are seeing their club picked apart.

Some figures have even suggested that Man City owners will have to consider selling as a result of this legal battle.
