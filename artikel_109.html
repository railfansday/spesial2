Between holiday travel, gifts, and the rising cost of just about everything, expenses can quickly add up during this time of year. Thankfully, you don’t have to dip too far into your savings to take advantage of some of the better Black Friday deals we’ve seen.

Below, we’ve rounded up a range of great stuff currently on sale for $100 or less, from noise-canceling earbuds and portable Bluetooth speakers to pocket-sized e-readers, photo printers, games, and more. You’ll also find deals on Ember’s temperature-controlled mug, Kodak’s tiny keychain camera, and a variety of Lego sets, all of which would make great holiday gifts or simply a nice treat for yourself.

You can get an ad-supported Disney Plus and Hulu bundle for $4.99 a month ($8 off) for an entire year. Both streaming services feature a deep catalog of new and classic TV shows and movies, including The Simpsons, Andor, Toy Story 5, and The Fantastic Four: First Steps.

Although it’s not Amazon’s first bedside alarm clock, the Echo Time is focused on helping you sleep better. The camera-less Alexa speaker can detect your sleep stage via ultrasound and use smart alarms to ease you into the day, but only if you want it to.

Anker’s durable travel adapter delivers up to 100W of power and works in over 200 countries. It doesn’t support AC plugs, but it does offer a trio of USB-C ports and a nifty LCD display to keep tabs on the charge percentage.

An HBO Max Basic subscription provides access to shows like A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms, The Last of Us, and The Pitt, as well as films like Superman and Weapons. You can get a year of the monthly ad-supported subscription for $5.49 a month (about $6 off) until December 1st.

Noco’s Boost GB40 is a rechargeable battery with jumper cables to start six-liter gas or three-liter diesel engines. It also has USB ports so you can charge your phone, ensuring you always have a backup plan.

Apple’s latest AirTags feature an upgraded ultra-wideband chip for more precise location tracking, a noticeably louder speaker, and an expanded Bluetooth range. Read our review.

The Beats Studio Buds Plus sport an eye-catching translucent option and build upon the original with improved sound, better voice quality, and more effective ANC. They also include swappable silicone ear tips and support for native iOS and Android features. Read our review.

The Liene Pearl N200 Pro is a dye-sublimation photo printer that produces vivid, colorful prints for journals, scrapbooks, and more, with an easy-to-use companion app for customization. This particular bundle includes 50 sheets of photo paper and five ink cartridges, though refills are relatively affordable at around $25 for 50 sheets.

Peacock is a great way to catch up on a wealth of content from NBC’s various networks, whether you plan to rewatch The Office or catch up on all things Bravo. A Premium subscription also grants access to films from studios like Universal Pictures and DreamWorks, along with live sports and a host of Peacock Originals.

Do you ever wish you could turn on your coffee maker or hit the light switch without getting up? The SwitchBot Bot lets you do that. All you need to do is stick the inexpensive contraption on a standard button or rocker switch, which will allow you to control or schedule your pet feeder, garage door, and a wide range of other devices from your smartphone.

Ember’s latest smart mug is designed to keep your coffee at the ideal temperature for up to three hours, with five temp settings between 120 and 145 degrees Fahrenheit. The third-gen version is similar to prior models; however, you can now adjust the heat settings directly on the mug and throw it in the dishwasher, which wasn’t the case before.

Paramount Plus Premium provides access to live sports as well as movies and shows from both platforms, including Tales of the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, a host of Yellowstone spinoffs, Star Trek shows, Sonic the Hedgehog 3, Twin Peaks, Dexter, and more.

Apple’s entry-level stylus attaches magnetically to supported iPads, though it doesn’t support magnetic charging. Instead, you pair and charge it by connecting it to your device’s USB-C port. It includes tilt sensitivity and Apple Pencil Hover support, but it doesn’t offer double-tap gestures or pressure sensitivity.

The latest Beats Studio Pro feature improved noise cancellation, sound quality, and comfort. They also support lossless audio over USB-C and offer native support for a range of iOS and Android features like other Beats headphones. Read our review.

You likely won’t find wireless earbuds with a more striking appearance than Nothing’s Ear 3A. They also sound fantastic, boast active noise cancellation, and offer robust EQ settings, making them a great pick for the price. Read our review.

Rechargeable AA batteries are handy until it’s time to charge them. Most wall chargers let you refill four, maybe six, at once. Zepath’s clever solution is to ship them in an easy-to-use USB-C charging case that charges eight simultaneously. The batteries themselves are long-lasting, too.

The P9 is great if you need more storage for your Nintendo Switch 2. The microSD Express card offers sequential read speeds of up to 800MB/s, but only gadgets with a microSD Express card slot can take full advantage of the highest speeds. That includes certain cameras, tablets, and drones with a microSD USH-I slot.

With a 4.3-inch E Ink screen, the Xteink X4 is smaller and more pocketable than any Kindle and Kobo model currently available. It has some drawbacks — it’s limited to TXT and EPUB files — but it’s great for tinkering; it also has a magnetic mount so you can stick it to the back of MagSafe-ready phones.

Amazon’s last-gen Fire TV Stick 4K Plus streams in 4K and supports Dolby Atmos, Dolby Vision, and Alexa Plus. It also ships with the redesigned Fire OS and, unlike more recent models, still lets you sideload Android TV apps.

007 First Light, a third-person stealth action game from the studio behind Hitman, is one of the best action games we’ve played this year. It features a wide variety of missions that have you sneaking through enemy territory, engaging in hand-to-hand combat, and taking part in high-speed car chases — you know, classic Bond stuff. Read our review.

Fanttik’s rechargeable electric screwdriver features three adjustable torque settings, a built-in LED light, and 16 S2 hardened steel bits for tackling everything from furniture assembly to small electronics repairs. It also comes with a magnetic storage case and supports USB-C.

Outside of the Integrity, few spacecraft made as big a splash in 2026 as the one Ryland Grace piloted in Project Hail Mary. And now, thanks to Lego, you can construct a microscale replica of the titular Hail Mary, complete with a miniature Rocky figure.

Sony’s DualSense Wireless Controller for the PlayStation 5 features immersive haptic feedback and resistance triggers, which feel great to use. It offers a built-in mic, a 3.5mm headphone jack, and a mute button; it also works with PC, macOS, and mobile devices via Bluetooth or USB-C.

Apple TV offers ad-free streaming of original shows and movies, including shows like Severance, Silo, Slow Horses, and Pluribus. The service also features a rotating selection of films, along with live Formula 1 races and Major League Soccer matches.

Hoto’s rechargeable screwdriver is ideal for small repairs. It offers three torque modes, a rotational speed of up to 220RPM, and a helpful circular LED to illuminate dark spaces. The durable case also holds all 25 steel bits and an extension bar for getting into hard-to-reach places.

Moft’s Trackable Tripod Wallet is a MagSafe-compatible wallet that holds two cards and folds into an adjustable phone stand or small tripod. It also supports both Apple’s Find My network and Google’s Find Hub, offers Qi wireless charging, and includes a detachable shutter remote for taking photos.

Govee’s Table Lamp 2 is way more fun than your average table lamp. The color-changing device boasts built-in scheduling and more than 60 dynamic lighting effects, including preset scenes for various holidays. You can even create custom color combos for when “Dandelion” and other presets just aren’t cutting it.

The Icemag 3 can wirelessly deliver up to 25W of power to compatible smartphones and up to 35W wired charging via USB-C. The 10,000mAh Qi2 charger can also act as a foldable kickstand, comes with an integrated USB-C cable that serves as a lanyard, and sports a transparent design with colorful RGB lighting.

Saros is a third-person sci-fi shooter in which players fight alien enemies on a mysterious planet trapped in a time loop. The PS5 exclusive features fast-paced combat, a variety of powerful weapons, and challenging boss fights. Read our review.

TP-Link’s Wi-Fi smart plug lets you remotely control lamps, fans, and other appliances using your phone or voice commands. It supports Matter, so it works with all major smart home platforms to set schedules and timers to automatically turn devices on or off.

Google’s latest pair of wireless earbuds features crisp sound, a comfortable design, and solid active noise cancellation, which is great considering the budget-friendly price tag. They even offer built-in support for Gemini and, unlike the entry-level AirPods, a purple option! Read our review.

Sucker Punch’s follow-up to Ghost of Tsushima is everything you could want in a sequel. The open-world revenge epic brims with fluid combat and a host of welcome narrative twists, all set against the gorgeous backdrop of Japan’s Edo period. It’s precisely the type of game photo modes were made for, even if you never use them.

Donkey Kong Bananza is one of the Switch 2’s first exclusive must-have titles. Its destructible environment allows you to smash your way through each of its 17 levels, and the paths you make will remain intact unless you specifically choose to reset the world. Your companion Pauline’s singing allows you to transform into animals that have abilities that allow you to reach previously inaccessible parts of levels. Read our review.

Arguably the best thing to come out of The Book of Boba Fett was the Mandalorian’s N-1 Starfighter, which Lego has re-created here in miniature. It’s not quite as impressive as the 1,802-piece version, but the cheaper set still packs plenty of detail, including a spring-loaded shooter.

Nintendo’s latest Switch 2 exclusive is the best way to relive what is arguably the best Zelda game of all time. The revamped visuals look gorgeous, and while the game’s improved controls might annoy some purists who still cling to the heyday of ’98, we reckon most folks won’t bat an eye.

Twelve South’s entry-level Bluetooth transmitter delivers up to 25 hours of continuous use and lets you connect up to two pairs of headphones to an in-flight entertainment system, making it a great travel companion no matter where you’re going.

In Catan, up to four players strategically compete and trade for resources. The iconic board game ends when one player reaches 10 “victory points,” which is achieved by constructing a sprawling civilization of roads, settlements, and cities using sheep, ore, wheat, and wood.

Nothing’s Ear (a) earbuds have the same general design as the company’s previous earbuds — but you can get these in a snazzy yellow. They include ANC, more than decent sound quality, and other features like multipoint for well under $100. Read our impressions.

Control Resonant is all about manipulation — of gravity, not people. Remedy’s excellent sequel to Control is an exhilarating action game, with ever-changing combat, twisted level design, and the weirdest traversal mechanics we’ve seen in a long time.

The Chamera is not a good camera, but that’s not the point. Kodak’s charming, keychain-friendly tchotchke lets you snap lo-fi memories on a whim, with an ultra-small 1.6-megapixel sensor, a built-in LED flash, and the tiniest amount of storage.

Azul is a game in which you arrange patterned tiles in order to create the most beautiful tapestry possible. Arranging tiles in a strategic sequence will help you build your score while limiting your opponents’ ability to grow theirs.

The market is flooded with surprisingly cheap yet good over-ear headphones with active noise cancellation. The Headphone Pro by CMF are certainly some of the best-looking out there, and they come in multiple colors.

Magna-Tiles are a quintessential kids’ toy. The colorful, semi-translucent geometric shapes are a favorite here at The Verge, especially for those of us who prefer the simplicity of building with magnets rather than bricks.

Lego’s 709-piece kit lets you construct three brick-ified bonsai trees, including a black pine, a ginkgo with yellow leaves, and a wisteria with hanging lilac flowers. It also comes with three ornamental displays, just in case you want a raised stand on which to showcase them.

Anker’s 25,000mAh power bank sports two built-in USB-C cables (one of which is retractable), plus one USB-C and USB-A port. It tops out at 165W when charging two devices simultaneously, or 130W when charging three or four.

With 12.4mm drivers, active noise cancellation, and an IP54 rating, the CMF Buds 2A offer high-end features at an affordable price. You can also customize the sound, adjust ANC levels, and find the earbuds using the Nothing X app.

The Wuben G5 flashlight is as small as a Zippo lighter, yet far more versatile, with a bright, rotatable light that can shine in numerous colors. It features USB-C charging and has an adjustable clip.

No one should have to deal with a rat’s nest behind their desk when devices like Anker’s 2-in-1 USB-C cable exist. The handy USB-C cable splits at the end, giving you the ability to charge up to two devices from a single port. That means you can split power between a phone and tablet or juice a 16-inch MacBook Pro to 50 percent in just 20 minutes using a single port.

The Echo Dot Max features a flat design and a redesigned sound system, which delivers nearly three times the bass as its predecessor. It also offers improved conversation detection and better noise filtering, thanks to Amazon’s new AZ3 chip. Read our review.

Iniu’s P55-E2 Power Bank is an ultra-slim three-port USB charger that’s capable of fully recharging an iPhone 16 twice. It supports charging speeds of up to 45W via a USB-C port, which can deliver fast recharging for a range of gadgets.

Glocusent’s clip-in reading light features five brightness levels and multiple color temperature settings, ranging from a soft, warm glow that’s great for late-night reading to a brighter light that’s perfect for daytime use. The slim USB light easily slips into your pocket, too, so you can read anywhere, from a cozy bed to a dimly lit train.

Vantage isn’t your typical open-world exploration game. It’s a sci-fi roguelike that has you traversing an alien planet alongside up to five players, each of whom must solve puzzles and complete their mission before their time, morale, or health runs out. It’s not as charming as Stonemaier’s award-winning Wingspan, but it is far more interesting.

LIFX’s dimmable, color-changing bulbs offer Wi-Fi connectivity, simple setup, and Matter compatibility, so they work with any major smart home platform. The company is currently trialing a Thread beta that will bring the option to add Thread connectivity to all its Matter devices.

A great first portable music player/speaker for kids. It uses insertable NFC cards (sold separately) to play music and stories to keep kiddos occupied and entertained without zoning out in front of the TV. This model supports Bluetooth and 3.5mm for private listening, too.

A quiet, battery-powered fan that moves a lot of air despite its small size, the Switchbot Standing Circulator is a surprisingly versatile smart home addition.

The standard OpenRun are a lighter option than the newer OpenRun Pro 2, with a streamlined wraparound design, up to eight hours of battery life, and an IP67 rating for better water resistance. However, they don’t offer the same bass performance or USB-C charging.

The last-gen JBL Go 4 has been around for a bit, but the pocket-sized Bluetooth speaker still delivers with up to seven hours of runtime, dual-device connectivity, and an IP67 rating for water and dust resistance. You can also pair it with other Go 4 speakers for bigger stereo sound, if desired.

Nintendo’s latest Pokémon spinoff for the Switch 2 is part Pokémon, part Minecraft, and part Animal Crossing. Yet, despite borrowing gameplay mechanics from each, the cozy life sim is more than the sum of its expansive, building-centric parts. It’s breezy and warm-hearted, with enough secrets to keep you occupied for hours on end.

The proliferation of miniature screens continues with Ugreen’s latest Nexode USB-C Cable, which features a full-color LCD screen on one end that displays current power draw (up to 240W). It also supports 480Mbps data transfer speeds and, unlike similar power meter-equipped cables, lets you turn off the screen when needed.

The Kobo Remote lets you turn pages on a compatible Kobo e-reader with the click of a button, so you can keep reading without reaching for the screen. It connects directly over Bluetooth and features a curved, handheld design that doesn’t require clipping anything onto your e-reader.

The Buds Pro 2 offer a surprisingly robust set of features for the price, including ANC and a transparency mode that lets you tune into your surroundings. Their included charging case also features a smart dial that lets you adjust the volume, while multipoint Bluetooth support lets them connect to two devices simultaneously.

Fujifilm’s Instax Mini 13 is the best instant camera if you want to spend less than $100. While it lacks more advanced features, the simple instant camera takes good-quality shots quickly and easily and offers a built-in selfie mode along with a self-timer. It’s sold in a variety of fun colors that’ll especially appeal to kids and tweens.

The Tile Pro is the company’s loudest Bluetooth tracker, one that benefits from a 500-foot range. It also features a user-replaceable battery, unlike some other Tiles, and works with both iOS and Android devices.

Camp Snap’s latest digital point-and-shoot camera may still lack a screen and capture only 8-megapixel stills, but it offers a slimmer design, faster performance, and filters right out of the box. That, along with several other new features, makes it easier for kids to use.
