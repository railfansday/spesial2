Teyana Taylor Sells Female-Driven Comedy to Warner Bros. (Exclusive)

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Oscar-nominated actress will co-write and produce the project, with an eye to direct.

Teyana Taylor is plotting her next moves as a filmmaker. The Oscar-nominated actress has sold an original comedy pitch to Warner Bros. that she will co-write and produce via her The Aunties banner alongside producing partner Coco Gilbert.

Taylor would also likely direct the project, described as a female-driven, wish fulfillment comedy that explores the chaos of modern dating, and which draws upon stories Taylor has logged from friends, family, homegirl group chats, and her own experiences. Taylor earned an Oscar nomination earlier this year for One Battle After Another, which won multiple Oscars, including best picture as well as best director and best adapted screenplay for filmmaker Paul Thomas Anderson. The role of revolutionary Perfidia Beverly Hills also earned Taylor a Golden Globe and a BET Award. The Grammy nominee came up in the world of music, and had already amassed a number of acting credits, but the film and its accolades put her acting career on a whole new trajectory.

Since then, she’s been focused on her directorial debut, the Paramount feature Get Lite. The film is in post-production as it heads toward an Aug. 20 2027 release date. It stars Storm Reid and per the logline, centers on “a talented, but sheltered, dance student” who “finds inspiration, love, and family in the last place he expected: the subways of New York City.” It also stars NFL prospect Ceyair Wright.

Taylor is repped by WME, TaylorMade Management, and The Lede Company.
