Brooks Nader Joins ‘Playmates’ Movie at Searchlight (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Brooks Nader is the latest addition to the cast of the upcoming Searchlight movie “Playmates,” Variety has learned exclusively.

The film is inspired by writer and director Lorraine Nicholson’s childhood experiences at the Playboy Mansion. Nader will appear alongside previously announced cast members Lily-Rose Depp, Bill Pullman, Jessica Biel, Indiana Elle, and Anna Van Patten.

The official logline for “Playmates” states, “Elle (Elle), a thirteen-year-old know-it-all who considers the Playboy Mansion her second home, meets Anna (Depp), one of seven hopefuls vying to be Playmate of the Month. The two form an unlikely friendship and try to help each other get what the other wants most, a magazine cover and a boy.” Related Stories China Box Office: 'Resident Evil' Holds Top Spot for Fourth Straight Frame as 2014's 'John Wick' Debuts in Third

Pullman will star as Playboy founder Hugh Hefner, with Biel playing Hefner’s wife. Exact details on Nader’s character are being kept under wraps.

Nader will next be seen in Fox’s reboot of “Baywatch,” which will debut on the broadcast network on Jan. 6. She originally found success as a model, including appearing in the iconic Sports Illustrated Swimsuit Issue. She also currently stars in the Freeform reality series “Love Thy Nader,” which is prepping a second season, and also appeared in the 33rd season of “Dancing with the Stars.”

She is repped by WME, Dixon Talent, Inc. and by Leigh Fidler, Chief Brand Officer of Nader Sisters Inc.

Searchlight Pictures is the studio behind “Playmates.” As mentioned above, Nicholson will write and direct. Alex Orlovsky and Duncan Montgomery will produce via High Frequency Entertainment, with Depp producing in addition to starring. Searchlight vice president of production Richard Ruiz and creative executive Cornelia Burleigh will oversee the project for the studio. Ruiz and Burleigh report into the Heads of Production and Development, DanTram Nguyen and Katie Goodson-Thomas.
