NextPlat to Release Third Quarter 2026 Results on Thursday, November 12, 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Investor Conference Call Scheduled for Thursday, November 12, 2026 at 8:30 a.m. Eastern

HALLANDALE BEACH, Fla., Nov. 6, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- NextPlat Corp (NASDAQ: NXPL) ("NextPlat" or the "Company"), a global consumer products and services company providing healthcare and technology solutions through e-commerce and retail channels worldwide, today announced that it will release its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 on November 12, 2026.

NextPlat's CEO, President and CEO of Global Operations, David Phipps, its Chief Financial Officer, Amanda Ferrio and Vice President of Healthcare Operations, Birute Norkute will host a conference call on Thursday, November 12th at 8:30 a.m. Eastern to discuss the results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, as well as other recent developments. Investors are requested to submit their questions for the Q&A portion of the call prior to November 10, 2026, at investors@nextplat.com.

For more information about NextPlat, please visit www.NextPlat.com and connect with us on Facebook, LinkedIn and X.

About NextPlat CorpNextPlat is a global consumer products and services company providing healthcare and technology solutions through e-commerce and retail channels worldwide. Through acquisitions, joint ventures, and collaborations, the Company seeks to provide access to high-quality healthcare and pharmacy services supporting patients, providers, institutions, and public-sector organizations and creating online sales channels for the sales of products to domestic and international consumers. Through its subsidiaries, NextPlat provides pharmacy and healthcare data management services and prescription fulfillment services in the United States and operates an e-commerce division offering voice, data, tracking, and IoT products and services worldwide.
