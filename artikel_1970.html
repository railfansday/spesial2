Sources: Brentford 'doing everything' to block Man United signing Nathan Collins

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Transfer Correspondent AUTHORITY Former Sky Sports and ESPN FC correspondent who specialises in global recruitment, contract negotiations, and high-velocity transfer breaking news. FOCUS Exclusive reporting, domestic and international transfer markets, and elite-level insights. His “Deep-Lens” analysis connects the dots between boardroom strategy and pitch-level recruitment, providing a level of transfer clarity that goes beyond surface-level rumours.

Brentford are prepared to do everything possible to block Manchester United signing Nathan Collins, sources have told Football Insider.

The 25-year-old has emerged as a leading target for the Red Devils as they aim to bolster their defensive options under Mauricio Pochettino ahead of the January transfer window.

His performances at the Gtech Community Stadium have placed him firmly on their radar, with his leadership qualities and defensive attributes seen as valuable assets.

However, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Brentford are determined to keep hold of Collins and will not entertain offers mid-season.

The Republic of Ireland international has become a crucial part of Keith Andrews' side as they continue to put ambitious plans in place after a strong start to the season.

Despite the growing interest from Man United, Collins remains under contract at Brentford until 2029, placing the Bees in a strong position to dictate his future.

Football Insider revealed on 15 November that Man United have set their sights on Collins as they aim to back Pochettino ahead of the January transfer window.

The Brentford centre-back ticks a number of boxes at Old Trafford, with his commanding presence and physicality seen as assets they lack in their current options.

However, they may be forced to look elsewhere as Brentford are prepared to stand by their stance that Collins is not for sale, especially not in the January window.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Collins is captain of Brentford, captain of the Republic of Ireland as well so he's got leadership qualities in that respect.

"Obviously Brentford won't want to lose him. He's under contract until 2029. I think they do have an option to extend that by a further two years if they choose to.

"Nathan Collins would tick a lot of boxes for a lot of clubs in the Premier League, but as I said, Brentford won't want to lose him.

"He's an important part of the project they're putting together under Keith Andrews, so they'll be doing everything they can to keep hold of him, especially in the January window."

Collins' performances for Brentford and on the international stage have inspired interest not just from Man United, but from a host of top clubs in the Premier League.

His leadership qualities, defensive resilience, and physicality have made him a leading target for any club seeking a centre-back, and scouts are watching him closely.

This comes after Football Insider revealed last year that the likes of Liverpool and Tottenham have been keeping a close eye on Collins' performances.

That interest appeared to fade during the summer, when Brentford made clear they would not entertain offers, and that stance is not believed to have changed now.

Ahead of the January window, Brentford remain determiend to keep hold of their best players, and Collins is certainly among them.
