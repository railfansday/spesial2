Virgil van Dijk's most likely destination after Liverpool revealed by transfer expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Virgil van Dijk will leave the Premier League but will not retire when he does eventually leave Liverpool.

That is according to experienced former Tottenham, Everton and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 35-year-old's current terms are set to expire at the end of the season, with it still unclear if he will sign a new deal.

Van Dijk has enjoyed a superb season so far, and still looks just as excellent as he has in the eight years since he signed for Liverpool.

But the Netherlands international will eventually decline, and the Reds will not want to risk an uncomfortable final year that leaves a sour taste when he is bid farewell to.

Such a situation happened in the case of Mohamed Salah, who left Liverpool on the back of a final season that had seen him publicly fall out with then manager - Arne Slot.

Any team in world football would be lucky to have Van Dijk, but only certain clubs could currently pay for his wages.

There has always been a suspicion that it was never about the money for the Dutchman, however, and a pay cut cannot be ruled out.

Yet he could well disprove that theory and opt to play in the MLS or the Saudi Pro League for his final years, earning a boatload of money to end his career.

"He looks pretty fit. The only thing I'd question on him is his pace," King told Football Insider when asked about Van Dijk's future.

"I don't think he will retire. He'll either go back and become a player coach or something in the Netherlands, or he's going to end up in Saudi Arabia for a year or America.

"I don't think he'll play for another Premier League club, that's for sure. He's been certainly one of the top five players for Liverpool [this season]."

With Liverpool and Van Dijk sharing such a deep relationship, there is no need to panic on his contract.

"I want to keep playing and continue what I’m doing so let’s see what the season brings," he said before the international break.

"I’ve had an amazing time already so far. If that will be extended, that’s the case. If not, then I will go somewhere else. There’s nothing to report at the moment but that doesn’t mean that there’s any stress or anything.

"I feel good but it’s not about me at the moment. I’m not in any panic whatsoever.”
