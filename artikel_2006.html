Sources: Man City’s updated stance on Gianluigi Donnarumma revealed ahead of January

Man City remain committed to Gianluigi Donnarumma and have no plans to let him leave the club, sources have told Football Insider.

Donnarumma has come under fire this season for a lack of form that has seen many call for Enzo Maresca to drop him from Man City's starting eleven.

The 27-year-old Italian stopper has already made four errors leading to goals for club and country since the start of this campaign.

Some reports have claimed that City could look to move him on in 2027 as they look to reshape their goalkeeping department once again.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, the club are confident that Donnarumma can recapture his previous outstanding form as Maresca's number one.

The City boss has publicly backed his first-choice stopper ahead of their home clash against Fulham.

Maresca admitted on Friday that he had made Donnarumma "aware" of his mistakes, but confirmed that he would start against the Cottagers.

Geronimo Rulli is now the man waiting in the wings after James Trafford's exit, but the two-time Yashin Trophy winner is not going anywhere, anytime soon.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Donnarumma, although he’s had a few mistakes this season, he’s still a world-class goalkeeper, there’s no doubt about that.

"I don’t think there’ll be any regrets that Donnarumma is the number one, because he’s proven time and time again what a world-class goalkeeper he is and what he can be on his day."

Donnarumma's drop-off can be made clear when his Premier League stats from last season are compared to those from the opening 10 games of this campaign.

He boasted a save percentage of 72.9 in 2025-26, which has dropped to around 65.8 per cent in 2026-27.

His goals conceded (0.85 to 1.3), expected goals prevented (0.16 to 0.02) and clean sheets (0.44 to 0.30) per 90 minutes are all worse now than they were at the end of last season too.
