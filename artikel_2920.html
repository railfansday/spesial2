AI-Linked Stocks Take Off as Nvidia Brings Back Jensen Huang

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Stephanie Aliaga, Global Market Strategist at JPMorgan Asset Management says that market reaction to Nvidia shows the power of sentiment, and Jensen Huang's return as CEO "could power an upswing" if the fundamentals accompany it. Today, US stocks rallied, recovering from last week’s technology-led selloff, as Jensen Huang's return to lead Nvidia propelled Wall Street toward its best day since April 2025. The surge added more than $1 trillion to Nvidia's market capitalization, the largest such single-day increase for any company recorded by Bloomberg, after Nvidia's biggest weekly drop since 2008. (Source: Bloomberg)
