‘Novocaine’s Dan Berk & Robert Olsen To Helm Contained Action Thriller For Lionsgate

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

EXCLUSIVE: Dan Berk & Robert Olsen, the writer-directors of Paramount’s high-concept action comedy Novocaine, have sold an untitled pitch for a contained action thriller to Lionsgate, Deadline has learned.

The duo are writing the script and will also direct. Specifics as to the film’s plot are being kept under wraps.

Chris Bremner and Jeff Arkuss will produce via Unknown Quantity, with Chelsea Kujawa overseeing for Lionsgate. Robert Melnik negotiated the deals on behalf of the studio.

Opening at #1 at the box office last year, Berk & Olsen’s Novocaine stars Jack Quaid as a bank employee who cannot feel physical pain. Comedic chaos ensues when he’s pulled into the fight to rescue a coworker who is taken hostage during a bank heist. Berk & Olsen are also known for their Paramount+ genre-bender Significant Other and the SXSW horror comedy Villains, among other projects. Upcoming, they’ll direct the Ludacris actioner Limited Edition for Universal/87North — again, from their own script. They are represented by UTA, Kaplan/Perrone Entertainment and attorneys Andrew Hurwitz and Scott Goldman of Frankfurt Kurnit Klein & Selz. Bremner is the multi-hyphenate who signed his new Unknown Quantity banner to a writing and producing deal with Sony after co-writing their hit blockbusters Bad Boys for Life and Bad Boys: Ride or Die and exec producing the latter. Since then, he’s been on a tear, setting up one studio project after another. Most recently at Sony, he set up Castle, a medieval action film from writer Jill Blankenship, and White Knight, an R-rated, high-concept comedy to be directed for Screen Gems by Michael Lewen. As a producer at Sony, he also has Permission by Dan Roe and You Up by Gabe Delahaye.
