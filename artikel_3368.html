Stephen King’s Secret Pseudonym Novel Officially Arrives in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Stephen King is an author with a lot of lore surrounding his work. More than that, King himself has quite a bit of mystery surrounding him. Publishing his first book, Carrie, in 1974, by 1977 he was publishing novels under a pen-name to try and show audiences that he could write things outside of his typical horror stories. The first Richard Bachman book Rage was released in 1977 followed by The Long Walk and including novels like The Running Man. Now, his lore continues with a connected pen-name as The End Times: Volume One is set to release in 2027.

King's latest pen-name lore is in connection with Bachman through his wife, Claudia Inez Bachman. The fictional wife of King's pen-name has been included in King's The Dark Tower series and has been publishing The End Times through Bad Hand Books in a newspaper format with Benjamin Percy. The story, which Percy first began talking about on The Kingcast podcast, back in July and starting in November of 2025, issues of The End Times have been published monthly and are set to release until the end of 2026. The collection of these stories is set to release in 2027.

Set during the post-apocalyptic world of King's novel The Stand, the story follows a newspaper in the town of North End, Minnesota as the virus takes over. Fans of The Stand get to see how those survivors get to navigate a world destroyed by Captain Trips. Readers have been comparing the timelines between The End Times to that of The Stand since it began its publishing back in 2025 and with the complete volume coming out, it is only going to excite fans of his work further. This is also exciting for those who love the Bachman books and the lore there, bringing in Bachman's widow to write his stories.

In 2025, two important novels in the Bachman series were made into feature films. Edgar Wright's The Running Man was a more faithful adaptation to the novel, bringing in Glen Powell as Ben Richards in the run of his life. Then, the film The Long Walk from director Francis Lawrence put Mark Hamill in the villainous role of the Colonel, a man who is ruthless to the boys walking for the chance to change their lives. The addition of Bachman's widow to King's pen-name lore is exciting for fans and now, for those who love The Stand.
