Week 10 picks: Who the experts are taking in Texans vs. Browns

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

So, is this the week when the Houston Texans begin a new winning streak?

Currently sitting at 3-5, the Texans must get back to their winning ways long-term if they plan on making a postseason push in the AFC. Right now, no one should be talking about the playoffs, especially since Houston has blown three fourth-quarter leads and remains winless at home.

The good news? Sunday's matchup is on the road, a place where Houston is 3-1.

The Texans will travel to Cleveland for a date with Deshaun Watson and the Browns in Week 10. Cleveland, which could be gunning for a wild-card berth under first-year coach Todd Monken, fell short on Sunday in a battle down in the bayou amid an overtime loss to the New Orleans Saints.

Cleveland plans to start Watson, Houston's former franchise quarterback, but second-year passer Shedeur Sanders proved his potential by keeping the Browns alive in the Superdome. While throwing for nearly 200 yards and two touchdowns, Sanders was also sacked five times. Meanwhile, Cleveland's defense recorded five sacks, headlined by former Defensive Rookie of the Year Carson Schwesinger.

"They're really good players, they're athletic and they're disruptive," Texans offensive coordinator Nick Caley said. "They've got that speed off the ball. They use their hands and they play really hard continuously. They're a handful for sure."

As for Houston, quarterback C.J. Stroud threw for a season-high 384 yards and tied his career high in touchdown passes with five as Houston rolled past the Bolts in a 44-22 victory at SoFi Stadium. Despite a slow offensive start, Houston found a rhythm in the second quarter and never looked back.

The Texans could be dealing with a multitude of injuries following Friday's practice. For the third straight day, rookie offensive lineman Keylan Rutledge did not practice. Running back Woody Marks and defensive ends Will Anderson Jr. and Danielle Hunter also could be in trouble after missing practice on Thursday and Friday.
