Wolverines in the NFL: TE Marlin Klein catches first career TD

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A rookie tight end score his first career touchdown, while a punter made an incredible throw to keep a drive alive on fourth down. Here is this week's Wolverines in the NFL recap.

Wide receiver Nico Collins put Houston ahead for good Thursday night with a 42-yard touchdown catch late in the first quarter. That play in and of itself was worth celebrating, but so was a rookie catching his first career touchdown in the NFL.

Marlin Klein caught the touchdown that finished off Indianapolis. Klein's first NFL touchdown came from 13 yards out in the third quarter and capped Houston's 26-3 win. He caught all four of his targets for 24 yards.

Collins finished with three catches for 58 yards, with his touchdown accounting for the longest gain by either former Wolverine.

Buffalo faced a 4th-and-8 from its own 34 in the first quarter against Miami on Sunday. Buffalo punter Tommy Doman Jr. took the snap and lofted a pass to a wide-open target for 30 yards. A few plays later, Doman was back on the field to hold for the 54-yard field goal his throw helped set up. That put Buffalo ahead 6-0 on its way to a 26-9 win.

Elsewhere, Kenneth Grant started on Miami's defensive line and finished with two tackles. Josh Uche also made a solo tackle.

Dominic Zvada tied Jacksonville with a 53-yard field goal in the first quarter, then hit from a yard farther out with 11 seconds left before halftime. After missing wide left from 32, he connected from 43 in the fourth quarter. Add in two extra points, and Zvada accounted for 11 points in New York's 23-18 win.

With Jon Runyan Jr. starting at left guard, quarterback J.J. McCarthy went 18-of-38 for 173 yards, a touchdown and no interceptions. His eight-yard touchdown pass gave the Giants a 17-6 lead in the second quarter. McCarthy also ran seven times for 33 yards, including a 22-yard gain. Jacksonville cornerback Jourdan Lewis made two tackles.

Colston Loveland caught all three of his targets for 29 yards in Chicago's 21-13 win over New Orleans. His biggest grab came midway through the second quarter, when he hauled in a 15-yard touchdown — and hurdled a guy in the process — to put the Bears ahead 16-0.

Linebacker Devin Bush made eight tackles for Chicago, while Cesar Ruiz started at right guard for the Saints.

Tampa Bay had 4th-and-14 and one more chance to make it a game. With 3:23 left, edge rusher Derrick Moore got a sack on Baker Mayfield a nine-yard loss, giving Detroit the ball at the Bucs' 34. The Lions turned that field position into the touchdown that sealed a 31-21 win. Moore finished with two tackles and half of a sack. Meanwhile, Ben Bredeson started at left guard for Tampa Bay.

Eagles 19, Steelers 16: Joe Milton III started at quarterback for Philadelphia and went 11-of-21 for 141 yards without an interception. He added seven yards on five carries. Pittsburgh wide receiver Roman Wilson caught three passes for 43 yards, including a 33-yard gain in the third quarter.

Cowboys 51, Titans 30: Edge rusher Rashan Gary made two tackles for Dallas, while tight end Luke Schoonmaker caught his only target for six yards.

49ers 35, Vikings 10: Safety Josh Metellus assisted on five tackles for Minnesota in Mexico City. Max Bredeson played 11 offensive snaps at fullback.

Broncos 38, Raiders 24: Kwity Paye started at defensive end for Las Vegas and finished with four tackles.

Panthers 35, Ravens 33: Matt Hibner started at tight end for Baltimore and caught both targets for 12 yards.

Commanders 22, Bengals 20: Defensive backs Daxton Hill and DJ Turner, and defensive tackle Kris Jenkins Jr. combined for 10 total tackles for Cincinnati on Monday Night Football. Washington's Mike Sainristil chipped in three tackles.
