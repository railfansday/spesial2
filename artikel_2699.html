PSG manager Luis Enrique provides update on Désiré Doué injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Looking to control the ball, Désiré Doué (21) went down without making any contact with the incoming Sofiane Diop in the 40th minute. The reaction of those around him was indicative of the severity of the injury.

The Paris Saint-Germain forward, who just moments earlier had given his side the lead against OGC Nice, immediately clutched his right knee. The stretcher was called for seconds later, and the France international left the field in tears, with eight minutes added on to the end of the first half.

In a post-match press conference attended by Get French Football News, Luis Enrique provided an update on the Frenchman's health: "We will have to wait until tomorrow to know, but it is a serious injury. He is sad and worried. He will come back stronger than ever."

On the possibility of dipping into the market to sign a player, Luis Enrique added, "We are a real team. We are always open to the transfer window. It isn't because of two injured players that can't play for a certain number of matches… we have other players in our team. We will overcome it as a team. It isn't the first time."

A long-term absence for Doué would be a big blow for PSG and their chances of retaining their Champions League crown, especially given the loss of Khvicha Kvaratskhelia. The Georgian sustained an ACL injury last month and is in a race against time to feature again this season. In any case, Les Parisiens must do without Kvaratskhelia for much of the remainder of the season.

GFFN | Luke Entwistle – reporting from Nice
