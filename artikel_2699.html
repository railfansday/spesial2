Ravens' Devontez Walker returns kickoff for touchdown in wild game in Carolina

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

In the second quarter, the Ravens were about a yard away from taking a 19-7 lead. Moments later, they were losing 14-12. And moments after that, the Ravens were winning 19-14.

With the Ravens leading 12-7, running back Justice Hill broke into the open and appeared to be on the verge of a 37-yard touchdown run. Instead, after Hill had run 36 yards, the ball was punched out of his hand by Panthers cornerback Will Lee. The ball bounced into the end zone and out of bounds for a touchback.

From there the Panthers punted, but Ravens returner LaJohntay Wester muffed it, the Panthers recovered, and two plays later Chuba Hubbard scored to give the Panthers a 14-12 lead.

But Baltimore’s Devontez Walker returned the ensuing kickoff for an 86-yard touchdown and gave the Ravens a 19-14 lead.
