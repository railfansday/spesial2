No, the Chicago Bears are Not in Salary Cap Hell

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Yesterday, someone shared on social media (no, I won't elevate their account here) that the Chicago Bears had a dire salary cap situation looming in 2027. It's not the biggest account, and it's not even a sports-related account, but it picked up a bunch of traction as others shared it. Some believed the erroneous content and complained the Bears were screwed or wanted to lol at the stupid Bears, while others wanted to dunk on how wrong the take was.

A prominent gambling account (I won't elevate this account either) picked up on the narrative and, to gain clicks and engagement, shared their own version of the "oh no, the Bears are in cap Hell in 2027!" tweet to all their followers too.

For those not on social media, Bears fans are an easy mark. And that's not meant negatively; our fanbase is very, very active and passionate, and if you want to bait us or troll us, you're going to get a reaction.

ESPN's Field Yates, who knows a thing or two about the NFL and the salary cap, came across that above-referenced tweet and shared, "I don't know why this notion that the Bears are facing salary cap straits in 2027 has generated traction recently, but it's… not true. There are so many pathways for this team to easily clear cap room next year. And extend Caleb Williams."

Nick Korte, who is a contributor at Over the Cap and one of the best follows for salary cap and comp pick info, responded to Yates with this.

Simple restructures of the 5 players who the Bears have already devoted guaranteed salaries to in 2027 gets them in salary cap compliance without doing anything else. And the Bears have the 2nd highest availability in cap dollars in 2028 right now. Stay chill, Bears fans.

So even though Chicago's cap looks tight in 2027, and Caleb's extension will be a big one, there's nothing to worry about.
