Time, TV announced for Iowa football's matchup with Purdue

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

EVANSTON, Ill. — The kickoff time and TV designation for Iowa football's matchup against Purdue on Nov. 14 are official.

The game is scheduled to kick off at 6:30 p.m. CT and will air on NBC.

The Boilermakers enter the matchup 3-6 overall and 2-4 in the Big Ten.

The Boilermakers have losses to Wake Forest, UCLA, Notre Dame, Washington, Penn State and Maryland this season. Their wins came against Indiana State, Minnesota and Illinois.

Iowa is 7-2 overall and 4-2 in the Big Ten after a 24-10 victory over Northwestern on Nov. 7.

Iowa and Purdue have played two common opponents so far this season: Minnesota and Washington. Purdue blasted Minnesota 41-13 in West Lafayette, while Iowa fell at Minnesota 13-10. Washington won at Purdue 38-26, while Iowa won at Washington 41-24.

This is the first time that Iowa will play Purdue since Barry Odom took over as the program's head coach.
