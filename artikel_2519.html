T. J. Houshmandzadeh sides with Aaron Rodgers over Steelers HC Mike McCarthy

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

T. J. Houshmandzadeh sides with Aaron Rodgers over Steelers HC Mike McCarthy appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Pittsburgh Steelers fans are perhaps still trying to process everything that has unfolded within the organization. Within just one day, the team went from Aaron Rodgers being its starting quarterback to handing the reins to Mason Rudolph.

The strained relationship between Rodgers and Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy has emerged as a major factor behind the unexpected developments. However, former NFL player T.J. Houshmandzadeh has come to the defense of the former Steelers quarterback.

During an appearance on Speakeasy, Houshmandzadeh criticized the Steelers' coaching staff, including McCarthy, as well as general manager Omar Khan, for the way they handled Rodgers' situation.

"All the people, and I hope y'all know not all the people we gonna see that are not on Aaron Rodgers' side, they're biased. If you try to flame Aaron Rodgers for this, you are biased. The people on television, you're biased. You don't like them. You have an agenda because there's no way you're benching me for Mason Rudolph after Mason Rudolph was on the team when you brought me in. You brought me back this year."

The former NFL player then shifted his attention toward McCarthy, backing Rodgers' allegations against his longtime head coach during his appearance on The Pat McAfee Show.

"Mike McCarthy, you sleeping and beating, and you think I'm not gonna say that?" he added. "Mike McCarthy, you break us down 123 Steelers or Packers. You think I'm not gonna say that? Mike McCarthy, you ain't watching film leading up to the game; you ain't showing up for walkthrough. You think I'm not gonna say that?"

The Steelers' decision marks a disappointing ending to Rodgers' second season in Pittsburgh. The four-time NFL MVP returned for another year hoping to make a final push toward a Super Bowl, but everything ended the way no one wanted.

Rodgers had previously indicated that the 2026 season would be his final NFL season. Now, with his Steelers tenure officially over, questions surrounding his football future have become even more significant.

Meanwhile, the Steelers are preparing to face the Denver Broncos with Rudolph taking over as their starting quarterback.
