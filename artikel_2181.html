Julien Stephan confirms ‘long-term injury’ for QPR forward ahead of Burnley clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

QPR were beaten 4-1 by Southampton on Saturday, and the result wasn’t the only dampener on a tough day at the office for the Hoops. After being introduced as a second-half substitute, forward Alfie Lloyd hobbled off through injury.

Now, speaking to West London Sport ahead of Tuesday’s tie against Burnley, R’s boss Julien Stephan revealed the 23-year-old looks set for an extended spell on the sidelines. Lloyd is set to be out for two months, likely keeping him sidelined until 2027.

“It’s a long-term injury,” Stephan said. “It will probably be difficult for him to come back before the end of the year.”

Jake Clarke-Salter is also expected to miss the Burnley tie, though his issue is undisclosed at present. Nicolas Madsen, Rumarn Burrell and Tariq Lamptey are on course for returns right after the November international break, and Ilias Chair is set to be eased back in with more substitute appearances after his return on Saturday.

Stephan does not have the biggest of squads at QPR, but he has good depth in all positions. That has been put to the test in the first quarter of the campaign, especially in attack.

Lloyd offers his services across the front three, and his versatility has helped deal with a shortage of options. Up top, all of Richard Kone, Justin Obikwu, Burrell and now Lloyd have had time on the sidelines, while Rayan Kolli was omitted too.

On the wings, Karamoko Dembele is still working back to full fitness after his ACL injury. Chair and Koki Saito, have had their issues as well, so it has been a heavily impacted area.

Stephan’s options have replenished over recent weeks, but Lloyd now drops out to leave the boss without a flexible forward and a strong physical profile. It will be hoped he can recover swiftly to return as soon and as safely as possible.

Lloyd’s long-term absence serves as a blow, but QPR do have the players to deal with the blow.

Stephan could recall one of Leon Scarlett or Justin Obikwu to the bench to cover. Kone will be backed to hold onto his starting berth having netted again in the Southampton loss.

Burnley have conceded 25 times in 13 games this season, and that record is something the Hoops will be ready to target. They have scored nine goals in their last two matches at Loftus Road, and they have the attacking style and talent to find a similar level of success against the Clarets.
