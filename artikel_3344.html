All 13 Episodes Of This Masterpiece Detective Series Are Perfect From Start To Finish

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

One of the best detective series of all time has no bad episodes, with all of its seasons being perfect from start to finish, and it focuses on a character viewers know well. With the growth of streaming services, mystery shows and thrillers have become quite popular. After all, they pair off well with the concept of binge-watching, which is a streaming hit.

In 2026 alone, some of the best TV shows were mystery-filled series. On Prime Video, Young Sherlock and Sterling Point fit that bill, with the first being more fun, while the second was more dramatic. Netflix's His & Hers and I Will Find You were both crime thrillers with key mysteries at the center of their narratives, and they broke into the streamer's all-time TV show rankings.

As far as full-on detective TV shows go, there are also plenty of different concepts to choose from. The rise of murder mysteries since Rian Johnson's Knives Out conquered the box office has led to shows like Poker Face, DTF St. Louis, The Residence, Only Murders in the Building, and more. That said, we all know who is the most famous detective in fiction.

That would be Sherlock Holmes, an icon who has endured the test of time. There have been several Sherlock Holmes adaptations over the years, including movies, TV shows, video games, audio dramas, and more. That said, perhaps the best of them was BBC's Sherlock, starring Benedict Cumberbatch as the detective, with Martin Freeman co-starring as his loyal friend, John Watson. Sherlock has no bad episodes.

Sherlock debuted in 2010, with 13 episodes being released between then and 2017. It has been nearly a decade since the show last aired a new episode, which is disappointing, given how Cumberbatch and Freeman perfectly embodied their characters. As a modern Sherlock Holmes tale, the detective series made the lead character a quick-thinker who used all available technology to solve mysteries and worked as a detective consultant alongside Watson, DI Greg Lestrade, and more. Rather than being canceled, like most shows are in this streaming age, Sherlock stopped releasing new episodes due to its actors' busy schedules.

As it stands, Sherlock consists of four seasons with 12 episodes and a special. All of them clock in at around 90 minutes, feeling like movies in their own right, though with overall connective tissue including character arcs, dynamics, and so on. On IMDB, the lowest-rated episode of the detective series is season 4's "The Six Thatchers," which still comes in at a high 7.6 user score. On the other end, season 2's "The Reichenbach Fall" goes all the way up to masterpiece status with a series high 9.6 score. That episode culminated the story of Andrew Scott's James Moriarty.

While there are effectively no bad episodes of the BBC's Sherlock series, that is not to say that the show did not deal with its fair share of controversies over the years. How the show handled the end of Moriarty's storyline in the season 2 finale and the absence of the character who was the perfect foil to Cumberbatch's Sherlock did impact overall enjoyment of the series. That is also when the show's direction changed from more traditional clever mysteries to a psychological thriller.

Sherlock also poked fun at the fan theories surrounding the fallout of "The Reichenbach Fall," which was not seen as a positive. That said, perhaps the detective series' most controversial decision might have been its final, as the last episode of Sherlock was centered on Holmes' lost sister, Eurus, and made her nearly superhuman, as her high intellect allowed Eurus to essentially mind-control people just by talking to them, which is where many believe Sherlock jumped the shark. So, even though Sherlock has no bad episodes, its more controversial decisions have led to its overall reputation not being pristine.
