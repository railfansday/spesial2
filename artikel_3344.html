Taylor Sheridan Meets ‘Jurassic Park’ in the New Sci-Fi Western Officially Set for 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Modern Westerns are experiencing a major boom thanks in no small part to the brilliance of Taylor Sheridan. Dinosaur movies, on the other hand, not so much. The Jurassic Park franchise, the singular dominant force in the field, seems to have reached a point of diminishing returns, creating a golden opportunity for another major player to step in. Notable efforts made to launch its competition have included Adam Driver's 65, Anne Hathaway's The End of Oak Street, and Primitive War. Of the trio, Primitive War, directed by Luke Sparke, drew the best reception. Sparke is now officially set to return with another ambitious project that can truly rival Jurassic Park — a sci-fi Western mashup that is the stuff dreams are made of.

The news of Sparke's dino-Western project first broke in the summer. It was described as a sci-fi Western set in an alternate universe where dinosaurs and humans co-exist together in a symbiotic relationship. The bonkers idea immediately drew widespread appeal, leading to the release of a concept art and a teaser trailer, which went viral, generating over 5.5 million views. The project, titled Dinosaurs of the Wild West, however, had a financing problem. This led to the creation of a Kickstarter campaign, which has, fortunately, earned enough support to get things officially moving. An exclusive ScreenRant report reveals that Dinosaurs of the Wild West has begun filming as a TV series in New Zealand and Australia. An official synopsis also released reads as follows:

"Set in an alternate 1879, Dinosaurs of the Wild West imagines a frontier where humans and dinosaurs have built civilization together. Massive Brontosaurs haul entire towns across the plains. Raptors are ridden into battle. Pterosaurs rule the skies. Against this extraordinary backdrop, pioneers, outlaws and adventurers navigate a dangerous frontier where prehistoric creatures have reshaped the course of human history."

Dinosaurs of the Wild West has assembled a stacked cast. Leading the pack is Casper Van Dien (Starship Troopers), who will play Sheriff Virgil Ross. The main cast also includes Lincoln Lewis (Primitive War), Jesse Kove (Cobra Kai), Travis Jeffery (Before Dawn), Tony Bonner (The Last Frontier), Mark Ricci, and M.C. Huff, who will play a bounty hunter named Lily. Also featuring are veteran actor Martin Kove (The Karate Kid), media personality Rachel Pizzolato, and NFL star George Kittle.

Inspired by the work of illustrator Shaun Keenan, Dinosaurs of the Wild West plans to launch as a major cinematic universe that will include an ambitious tabletop RPG, collectibles, and plans for feature adaptations. Dinosaurs of the Wild West Season 1 will include 8 episodes, officially scheduled to premiere next year. A previous report confirmed the series will be released via streaming, but there's no official confirmation yet about which platform will handle distribution. As always, stay tuned for updates.
