Darkseid Unveils His Justice League, All Members And Powers

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Justice League are DC's greatest heroes - bastions of heroism and self-sacrifice - which makes it all the more disturbing that Darkseid just assembled his own version of the team. His roster combines some shock members with heavyweights designed to counter Batman, Superman and Wonder Woman.

Recent years have seen Darkseid take his rightful place as the overarching villain of all DC canon, as 2024's All In Special saw him leave behind his mortal limits and become a multiversal god, founding the Absolute Universe and spreading his influence out into the multiverse.

The recent DC All In Saga: Darkseid vs. the World #1 (Scott Snyder, Sanford Greene) confirmed that his plans remain on track, as well as revealing that the God of Evil has assembled his own personal Justice League. Here's every member of Darkseid's superhuman army (at least, so far.)

DC All In Saga: Darkseid vs. the World #1 sees Darkseid arrive on Earth-3, the home of the villainous Crime Syndicate. This group is DC's classic evil Justice League, mirroring the heroes of classic canon. Darkseid demands the Syndicate join him as his thralls, only for their leader Ultraman to launch an attack on Darkseid. Of course, he's quickly beaten to a pulp and drafted into the archvillain's service.

An incredibly powerful bully, Ultraman keeps Earth-3 under his thumb despite not being particularly intelligent or creative with his powers. He has the basic abilities of Superman, with the exception that Kryptonite only makes him stronger, leading him to seek out, crush and snort the radioactive rock wherever he finds it.

Ultraman once imprisoned Superman's son in his dimension, taunting and abusing him for years, leading the mainstream Clark Kent to develop a seething hatred of his opposite number. Recently, DC introduced Ultraman and Superwoman's son Ultraboy, though he isn't shown on Earth-3 when Darkseid attacks the Syndicate.

Having arranged the assassination of his mother and father as a child, Owlman used their fortune to take total control of Gotham city, turning it into his personal kingdom. A genius tactician and combatant, Owlman has an army of 'Talon' sidekicks who he considers totally disposable, sacrificing them on a whim.

Owlman chafes under Ultraman's absolute power, engaging in a stunted affair with Superwoman behind his back. Owlman is one of the few denizens of Earth-3 whose counterpart in DC's main continuity is also a villain - a rival to the Court of Owls formerly known as Lincoln March but claiming to be Batman's brother.

Earth-3's Superwoman is essentially a superpowered dominatrix. She's married to Ultraman but carries out a series of affairs to secretly influence the planet's politics, its supervillain rulers and even its heroes.

Despite being an Amazon, Superwoman possesses the power of the mainstream Superman, with the exception of wielding the Lasso of Submission - a length of barbed wire which forces whoever it binds to worship and obey its wielder.

Possessing the powers of the Flash, Johnny Quick is a superspeed serial killer who takes joy in running through normal humans, causing them to explode. Unlike the mainstream Flash, he's not particularly intelligent, and tends to follow the lead of Crime Syndicate member Atomica, who isn't shown as a member of the Syndicate in DC All In Saga: Darkseid vs. the World #1.

There have been multiple versions of the evil Green Lantern in DC lore, and it's unclear which one appears in DC All In Saga: Darkseid vs. the World #1. The most recent was John Stewart's Emerald Knight. A former police officer, Stewart was recruited by the Overlords of Oa as one of their personal enforcers.

As the Emerald Knight, John can summon powerful energy constructs, with his ring persuading him to kill at every opportunity. His vigilante murders estranged him from his daughter, leading to a further surrender of his humanity to the ring.

In contrast, the former Power Ring of Earth-3 wore a ring powered by the demonic Volthoom, which essentially enslaved the wearer and fed on their lifeforce, unleashing monstrous energy constructs when a fight was to its benefit.

The character shown in Darkseid's Justice League is John Stewart but is referred to as Power Ring, raising questions about which version is considered canon in this frequently rebooted reality.

A mysterious figure straight out of Greek mythology, Pandora is a skilled mystic with a range of magical abilities. Despite powerful magic, she prefers to fight by channeling her power through twin handguns, and is a master of modern and traditional weaponry.

Dying at the hands of Doctor Manhattan at the beginning of DC's Rebirth era, Pandora has been resurrected as Darkseid's "harbinger." He has tasked her with traveling across the multiverse, offering his most powerful enemies the chance to join him. Her first stop is the corpse of the beyond-godlike villain Imperiex, who it's suggested she intends to bring back to life.

Pandora believes that Darkseid intends to eventually consume her (and the villains she recruits) to obtain the 'Omega energy' that is powering his ultimate form, but is content to go along with his plans for the present.

The recent Superman Annual 2026: Year One Thousand revealed that Darkseid has embarked on an agreement with Brainiac, working together to limit Superman's power and influence across the multiverse. Brainiac has taken on the task of kidnapping every version of the Legion of Super-Heroes from across every reality.

Brainiac is at the height of his power, with a gigantic body and godlike technology that makes him a multiversal force. Indeed, he's so powerful that Cyborg Superman (aka Hank Henshaw) is acting as his enforcer, with Brainiac tasking him to patrol the future and ensure no new Legion of Super-Heroes ever forms.

Cyborg Superman has all Clark Kent's powers, plus the ability to take control of essentially all forms of machinery. It appears that the pair may soon split off on their own, with Brainiac scheming to use the combined power of the various Legions he's captured to topple both Darkseid AND Superman. For now, however, he remains in Darkseid's alliance.

The awesome conclusion of DC All In Saga: Darkseid vs. the World #1 reveals the full scale of Darkseid's Justice League. While he spends the issue pacifying and capturing the Crime Syndicate, he then reveals that "I have conquered nearly all variants of Earth" across the multiverse.

He ends the issue surrounded by versions of Superman he's captured and stored in tanks, presumably with the same offer he gives the Crime Syndicate - join him or be consumed as a power source.
