SXSW, Deadline & The Improv Launching New Comedy Competition Circuit Breakers For 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Next spring, SXSW, Deadline and The Improv will present the first edition of Circuit Breakers, a new take on the Comics to Watch list and new form of comedy competition, all in one.

With Circuit Breakers, we’re looking to help elevate a new generation of rising comedic talent — comics with unique voices and original, well-honed material, who are not yet overly well known to either the industry or the general public.

The idea with this program is to provide rising comics with more than just a showcase credit — the chance to turn one performance into multiple opportunities for visibility, audience growth, and career advancement. The program will kick off with the announcement of our list of 2027 Circuit Breakers, talent that the town needs to know. Selected comedians will then be invited to participate in a taped showcase at SXSW 2027, in front of both comedy fans and industry professionals, with top performers at the showcase being invited to join a limited-engagement tour at the Improv clubs. Sets filmed at the showcase will be distributed across our collective social platforms, with coverage of the event on the Deadline site and in our Comedy Means Business newsletter. Submissions to the inaugural Circuit Breakers program can be submitted here. Comics will submit a 6-8 minute set, recorded within the last year; they must be available to perform in Austin, Texas as part of SXSW between March 17 and March 20, 2027 to be considered. We’re only accepting comics who have not been named to other Comics to Watch/New Faces lists, and who have not yet been platformed by the likes of SNL, Funny AF and late-night talk shows.
