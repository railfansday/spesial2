Kate Middleton's Secret Documentary on Cancer Journey Revealed in New Trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Kate Middleton made a royally surprising reveal ... she made a whole documentary in secret about her cancer journey, with the trailer just dropping this morning.

In the preview for "Heal" ... Kate reflects on her cancer diagnosis and treatment as she tackles the National Three Peaks Challenge.

We reported earlier ... in climbing the three highest mountains in England, Scotland, and Wales, Kate raised money for The Royal Marsden Cancer Charity.

Kate's brother James Middleton also joins her on part of her trek, and together they discuss how she never rang the bell after finishing her treatment at the Royal Marsden hospital.
