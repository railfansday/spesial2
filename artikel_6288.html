United Airlines Becomes World’s Largest Carrier For 1 Day Only: Here's Why

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Thanksgiving is nearly here. On November 26, the US will have a total of 14,993 scheduled passenger services. Compared to the prior Thursday, OAG data shows that activity has fallen by a substantial 44%. 11,568 flights have been removed.

Hardly surprisingly, schedule analysis shows that the domestic market is particularly affected. Activity has dropped by 47%, against a much smaller reduction of 11% for the international arena. This is especially because foreign carriers have only cut flights modestly, if at all. Air Canada, which is the US's top foreign operator, is only down by 7%.

American Airlines is ordinarily the largest airline in the world in various ways, including for aircraft movements. Not so on November 26. As the oneworld member's activity on the festive holiday has fallen by 57% compared to the prior Thursday, well above the average, it'll now rank third globally. American plans 3,007 movements, involving 322 airports globally, down from 342. Most places will still see the carrier, just far less often.

In its place, for one day only, will be United Airlines. This is because the Star Alliance member's services have 'only' fallen by 44%. 3,102 movements will exist, involving 335 airports globally, down by six. Houston Intercontinental overtakes Chicago O'Hare and Denver to be United's most-served hub. Houston ranks third for the airline's punctuality out of all hubs in the Lower 48.

If the top 20 airlines serving the US are considered, Allegiant has cut most services. Only 154 movements will exist, down from 484 the week before — a drop of 68%. Its domestic-only operation might have disproportionately impacted it. However, movements have fallen by a quarter compared to Thanksgiving 2025. Allegiant likely saw last year's performance and adjusted its schedule downward accordingly.

The ultra-low-cost carrier will serve 56 airports on November 26, down from 113 on the previous week. Only 30 states will be served, down from 41. Florida will still be number one, with 67% less activity. Phoenix Mesa becomes the most-served airport, pushing Orlando Sanford down to the third spot.

Then there is Breeze, whose drop is the second-highest, at 59%. Only American came close (-57%). Breeze has 126 takeoffs/landings, down from 311. Its relatively small size, at least compared to obvious others, is partly why its reduction looks overly high on this basis. 40 airports will be served, down from 67. But Raleigh-Durham will remain number one.

While everyone knows that Atlanta is the world's busiest airport for passengers, it is not first for flights. Chicago O'Hare is, influenced by the considerable use of regional jets. O'Hare ranks first globally for RJs. The world order will, inevitably, be different for Thanksgiving Day.

On November 26, Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport will be the world's top airport, with 1,516 aircraft movements planned. Compared to the prior Thursday, it'll overtake O'Hare, Atlanta, Dallas/Fort Worth, Denver, and Charlotte. Interestingly, Atlanta ranked first for last year's Thanksgiving. O'Hare will return to the top spot on November 27.
