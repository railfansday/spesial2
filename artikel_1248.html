Amorim on Milan links to Inácio: ‘We have Pavlovic and Diawara’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Ruben Amorim has played down Milan’s links with Gonçalo Inácio, suggesting the Rossoneri need to strengthen in other areas in January.

With quotes via MilanPress, the Milan coach was speaking to Portuguese broadcaster Sport TV after Sunday’s 5-1 win over Frosinone, and was asked about reports linking his former Sporting CP captain with San Siro.

“Inácio? Gonçalo is a great player, and it was a real pleasure to work with him in the past,” Amorim said.

“In defence, we have Pavlović and Diawara. Diawara is obviously young, he came from the second division and cost us €3 million, but he’s responding well. So we have to look ahead and think about other players in other positions.”

Amorim’s comments come after a week of reports suggesting the Rossoneri were preparing a fresh move for the 25-year-old. According to Calciomercato.com, Inácio could leave Sporting in January, but only if the club receive a very substantial offer, with the Portuguese club willing to sell for around €40 million.

Milan had tried to sign Inácio in the summer, and Amorim reportedly sent him a text: “Gonçalo, come with me to Milan because we want to achieve great things.”

Instead, the club signed Sankhoun Diawara from Troyes. The 1.90m centre-back arrived for around €3 million, bonuses included, plus 10% of any future resale fee, as Strahinja Pavlović’s understudy in Amorim’s back three. The 20-year-old started on Sunday and has been given more opportunities in recent weeks.

Amorim’s words suggest the Rossoneri’s January plans could focus elsewhere. Gerry Cardinale has already said any winter signing will only come if it is “a move that counts”.
