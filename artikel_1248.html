Watch: Ronaldinho trains with Ravenna before first competitive game in over a decade

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Ronaldinho has trained with Ravenna ahead of what could be his first competitive appearance in over a decade.

The 46-year-old former Milan and Barcelona star signed for the Serie C club in August, and he’s expected to feature in Sunday’s derby against Forlì.

It would be his first competitive game since 2015, and tickets for the clash have already sold out.
