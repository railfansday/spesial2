Actress Robin Givens' former Hollywood Hills home is on the market for $3.5 million

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The 3,251-square-foot, four-bedroom house, which Givens bought for $520,000 in 1987, is located above Sunset Strip

Actress Robin Givens’ former Hollywood Hills home is on the market for $3.495 million, according to MarketWatch.

The "Head of the Class" star paid $520,000 for the property in 1987, when she and boxer Mike Tyson first became a couple, according to MarketWatch. The two married in February 1988, and their divorce was finalized in February 1989. Givens sold the home in April 1998 for $640,000.

The house has four bedrooms, five bathrooms and one half bathroom across 3,251 square feet, according to Cottages & Gardens. Its features include vaulted ceilings, interiors finished in warm wood and a fireplace wall faced with stone. Sliding glass doors give access to a terrace, which extends to a pool for private use, along with a raised spa and a pergola.

The kitchen has an island, cabinets made of wood and countertops in a dark finish. The primary suite has an accent wall paneled in wood and a walk-in closet, and a room on the lower level has been turned into a gym. Neyshia Go and Nichole Shanfeld of Carolwood Estates hold the listing.

The property sits above Sunset Strip, according to Canyon News. Real-estate records for the 1998 sale don't identify the buyers, according to Canyon News.

Givens now lives in Laguna Niguel, California, according to Canyon News.
