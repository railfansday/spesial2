Will Joao Pedro play against Leeds? Latest Chelsea team news after Xabi Alonso update

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Chelsea boss Xabi Alonso has delivered a new update on Joao Pedro’s injury ahead of the Premier League clash with Leeds on Saturday.

The Brazilian striker was a shock absentee from the team sheet as the Blues welcomed arch-rivals Tottenham to Stamford Bridge last month, with Alonso later revealing that he had suffered a thigh issue in training.

That problem saw him ruled out from the Tottenham match through to the November international break, after which it was hoped he would be fit to return.

But Alonso has now confirmed that there has been no such luck, and that Joao Pedro will play no part in the match against Leeds on Saturday.

Speaking at his pre-match press conference on Friday, the Spaniard confirmed that the player had yet to even resume training, suggesting he will not be returning to the pitch any time soon.

“Not yet,” the Spaniard replied when asked about his No9. “He’s not ready to train yet for the team. Not for Leeds, no. We need to see day by day how he is feeling.”

There are concerns behind the scenes at Chelsea that he could remain sidelined until the New Year.

That is, of course, bad news for the Blues, who have been generally off the boil without Joao Pedro as an attacking focal point. Of the four matches since his injury, the Blues have won only once - against crisis-stricken Manchester United.

That result came amid a run of three defeats by Tottenham, Sunderland, and Liverpool, who required a penalty shootout to knock Chelsea out of the Carabao Cup in the last 16.
