Sia Addresses Court Battle With Ex After Request to Be Declared Legally Single

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Sia has spoken out about her ongoing divorce battle with estranged husband Daniel Bernad shortly after she asked a judge to declare her legally single.

“I look forward to closing this chapter,” the “Chandelier” singer, 50, wrote via X on Thursday, November 24. “I believe in accountability, and I trust the legal process. My focus remains on being a loving parent and living a peaceful, meaningful, creative life.”

Sia’s post came approximately one hour after TMZ reported that her attorney, Laura Wasser — famous for representing high-profile clients including Kim Kardashian and Angelina Jolie in their divorces — had filed paperwork asking to bifurcate her marital status, meaning Sia would become legally single despite still battling Bernad over finances in court.

Sia and Bernad tied the knot in December 2022, followed by a wedding ceremony in May 2023. The pair welcomed son Somersault “Summi” Wonder Bernad in March 2024. (Sia also adopted two teen boys in 2019.)

The “Cheap Thrills” singer filed for divorce from her ex in March 2025, citing irreconcilable differences as the reason for the split.

In August 2025, the exes reached an agreement regarding legal and interim physical custody of their son with restricted/monitored visitation terms given to Bernad. A broader custody agreement was reached last month, with Sia remaining Summi’s primary custodial parent, according to TMZ. They reportedly worked out a schedule for holidays and vacations and agreed to work with a parenting plan coordinator.

In April, Sia agreed to pay Bernad $42,500 per month in child support, as well as the cost of private school tuition, agreed extracurricular activities, health insurance and uninsured healthcare costs and a $5 million life insurance policy or trust in their son’s favor.

“I’m a sober working mom trying to buy peace,” Sia wrote via X at the time. “I have primary custody of our son and since i am the only parent earning income i still have to pay California’s incredibly high child support.”

She continued, “This has been a horrific year, but it taught me how to navigate incredibly difficult situations, prioritize my family and not absorb other people’s negativity. ‘To err is human, to forgive is divine.’”

Despite reaching custody and child support agreements, Sia and Bernad remain locked in a battle over money, with the former claiming that her ex owes her more than $3 million in separate property funds, including investments tied to their business, Modern Medicine Services, PC.

Sia claimed in court docs that she invested $2.7 million to help Bernad, a former radiation oncologist, launch and operate the business between June 2021 and September 2025. However, Bernad allegedly did little to manage MMS. (Bernad, for his part, previously claimed in court docs that she stopped funding the business in March 2025 or April 2025, which he said left him unemployed.)

In her latest filing, Sia also claimed her ex owes her $675,000 in misappropriated funds after he allegedly abused a credit card she gave him under his name and connected to her bank account in March 2023.
