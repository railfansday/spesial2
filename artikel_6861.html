Two-Tone Heels Are the Playful Shoe Trend Your Fall Wardrobe Needs

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Add a bold, quirky finish to your monochrome outfits.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Whether your wardrobe is ready or not, one thing is clear: designers want us to wear more color. The message came through on several spring/summer 2027 runways, including Carolina Herrera, LII, and Miu Miu. Footwear is where the idea gets especially playful.

Two-tone shoes have been trending all year, largely thanks to Matthieu Blazy’s work at Chanel, where bicolor heels on the fall 2026 runway showed that fashion doesn’t have to take itself so seriously. A little quirkiness is the whole point. Bold yet unexpected, the two-tone shoe is a fun surprise against an otherwise tonal outfit.

The best part is how easy it is to try. You don’t need to rebuild your closet or commit to a head-to-toe color story. Start at your feet by pairing a split-color heel with a monochrome look, and let the shoes be the only loud note. If you want to go further, pick one shade from the shoe and echo it in a bag, a scarf, or a lip color. The contrast does the work, and the outfit looks intentional rather than overthought.
