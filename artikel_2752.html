Iowa football vs Northwestern, score, live updates, highlights

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Iowa football team takes a trip to Evanston to play No. 23 Northwestern on Saturday. Kickoff is scheduled for 6:35 p.m. CT.

The No. 20 Hawkeyes (6-2 overall, 3-2 Big Ten) beat Wisconsin 30-0 at home last weekend. Hank Brown completed 20 passes on 24 attempts for 222 yards and two touchdowns. Tony Diaz and DJ Vonnahme found the end zone in the air, and Vonnahme added a rushing score in the third quarter.

Iowa's defense kept Wisconsin off the board, recording two sacks, two fumble recoveries and an interception.

Northwestern (6-2, 3-2) is coming off a 34-26 loss at Oregon. Their quarterback, Aidan Chiles, will be out for the game today against the Hawkeyes with an upper-body injury.
