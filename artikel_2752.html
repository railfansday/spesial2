Rob Dillingham talks teaming up with ex-SEC rival Dalton Knecht in G League

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The New York Knicks signed Dalton Knecht to a two-way contract after being waived by the Los Angeles Lakers before the start of the season, recognizing that he still has the potential to be a key contributor.

Knecht has been sensational with the Westchester Knicks in the NBA G League. On Friday, he teamed up with Rob Dillingham to lead the Knicks past the Raptors 905, 130-117.

Knecht had a game-high 35 points on 9-of-18 shooting, including 6-of-13 from long distance. Dillingham added 26 points, four rebounds, seven assists, and two steals off the bench. It was their first time playing together. Westchester notched its first win after a 0-3 start.

After the game, Dillingham only had good things to say about Knecht, as shared by SNY's Ian Begley.

"That's my dog. We were from the same draft class. Anytime I see him, we say what's up. Yeah, that's my dog," said Dillingham.

"I've always played against him, and I respect him. He came from a JUCO to a small D-1 (Division I) school, then he goes to Tennessee, and he's a top draft pick, so a lot of respect for him. It's definitely a great opportunity to play with him."

Dillingham, who starred at Kentucky, recalled his scoring duel with Knecht in the SEC. He said he dropped 35 points against Tennessee, while Knecht responded with 40 points in their next meeting.

In his one-and-done stint with the Wildcats, Dillingham averaged 15.2 points and 3.9 assists, while Knecht tallied 21.7 points and 4.9 rebounds in his lone year with the Volunteers.

Knecht has played three games with the New York Knicks.
