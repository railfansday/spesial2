Pistons vs. Bucks Discussion: Game Time, TV, Odds, and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

It feels like the Detroit Pistons are playing with fire. They've been down Jalen Duren nearly all season and somehow have come out of it with a decent record. That is despite the big man's absence (along with the offseason trade of Isaiah Stewart) taking the Pistons from one of the best offensive teams in the NBA to one of the worst. Detroit has also survived without Duncan Robinson because they finally have some additional shooting they didn't have last season. Now they face a stretch without Cade Cunningham. All three of Duren, Robinson, and Cunningham are out tonight against the Milwaukee Bucks.

While the Pistons should be able to withstand a night without Cade when facing the league's worst team, there is no question Cunningham is the most important player on their roster, and the season is lost if Cade is lost. That is why I fully support keeping No. 2 out of the lineup for as long as is needed to get his knee issues sorted out. But a Cade-less Pistons team is obviously much worse. There is no Eastern Conference juggernaut so I am not afraid of the Pistons suddenly falling out of the playoff picture. But if Cunningham faces an extended absence to rest his knee, things could get ugly. Daniss Jenkins is smart, fearless, and can score, so that helps a ton. Ausar Thompson can't score, but he's stepping up as a distributor and playmaker. That helps too. Duren will get back, and so will Robinson. Yes, please. But I don't want to think about a big chunk of this season without Cade. Hopefully, he will come back soon.
