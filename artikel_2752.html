Steelers sign WR Max Hurleman to active roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Pittsburgh Steelers are signing wide receiver Max Hurleman to the active roster from their practice squad, per Adam Schefter.

Hurleman made his NFL debut in Week 11 against the Philadelphia Eagles, catching six passes for 61 yards and a touchdown, all for which led all Steelers pass-catchers.

The Steelers are looking to end a two-game losing streak that saw them go from 6-2 to 6-4 when they host the Denver Broncos on Friday. This will be their first home game since their 27-6 win over the Cleveland Browns in Week 8. The Steelers have yet to lose at home this season, posting a 4-0 record at Acrisure Stadium so far this season.
