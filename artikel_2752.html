Who loses last? UTEP, NIU generating buzz for ESPN's Bottom 10 showdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The UTEP football team's anti-title game against winless Northern Illinois has the program in the national spotlight, albeit not the kind it wants as it goes about a searching for its next coach.

The battle between statistically the two worst teams in America this season will be, in fact, 1 vs. 2 in ESPN's Bottom 10 and the hype for this game started a week ago, with some lobbying for ESPN Game Day to go to DeKalb, Illinois, for the showdown.

CFB Kings set it up like this Friday, Nov. 20:

"Last night's matchup between 1-9 Rice and 2-8 Temple was fun, but there's an ever bigger collision looming on the horizon…

"Next week, UTEP faces Northern Illinois. UTEP is currently 1-9 and travels to Air Force tonight where they are 21.5-point underdogs.

"Northern Illinois is currently 0-10 and travels to North Dakota State tomorrow, where they are 25.5-point underdogs.

That means we will very likely have a matchup between 1-10 UTEP and 0-11 NIU next week in a battle where the losing team will finish last in the FBS." (The message included emojis of a crying face and broken heart.)

CFB Kings then put together a tale of the tape graphic with the teams' (poor) national rankings in offense and defense and pointing out both teams have six losses by 20+ points.

Elitecollegefootball termed this "THE GAME OF THE CENTURY" while proclaiming it must-see TV.

A post reads: "Clear your schedules on Saturday because we have THE GAME OF THE CENTURY this weekend. 1-10 UTEP vs 0-11 Northern Illinois. MUST SEE TV WE (yes, you and me) will be watching this."

Not everyone agrees. A reply on UTEP's official X post that shared the result of the 52-3 loss to Air Force, from RolandV (Bear) proclaimed, "Why bother traveling to Northern Illinois next week? Just fake an illness outbreak and forfeit the game. Save on the travel expenses."

CollegeSportsOnly created a "Toilet Bowl" graphic and introduced it like this: "Your friend, a simpleton: 'I can't wait for Rivalry Saturday.' You, a CFB sicko: 'I can't wait for the Toilet Bowl between UTEP & NIU.'"

College Football Zone played off the immovable object meeting the irresistible force adage in a predictable way: "Northern Illinois has lost 13 straight games dating back over a year. UTEP has lost 15 straight games to FBS opponents dating back over a year. This Saturday we find out what happens when a stoppable force meets a movable object in must see TV!" This prompted a response, "Can we bring back ties for this 1?"

The samkingeryshow has devoted a podcast to breaking down this game and saying someone should make a T-shirt for it.

The Athletic's senior college football writer Chris Vannini introduces his full rankings of 138 teams like this: "One of the biggest games of the season is this Saturday: No. 137 UTEP vs No. 138 Northern Illinois to avoid the bottom spot. Full 138 rankings."

Google AI took the opportunity to make its own memes, including this one: "1–10 UTEP at 0–11 NIU this Saturday. Truly a battle of left cheek vs. right cheek. Someone has to leave DeKalb with a conference win (unless we get 12 overtimes and the stadium powers down)."

Meanwhile, UTEP and Northern Illinois are going about preparing for what looks like a rare winnable game for both teams, trying to tune out the outside noise. To that end, UTEP isn't allowing its players to speak to the media, same as last week when coach Scotty Walden was fired.

"I'm aware," interim coach Kyle Beyer said of the buzz surrounding the game. "I get a lot of text messages, people talk to me. We're aware of the situation and what the matchup is. But for me, I'm a very process-oriented guy. I try to take emotion out of everything."
