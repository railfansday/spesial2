Watch Jonaz Walton add a Notre Dame touchdown against Boston College

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

No. 3 Notre Dame has been running the ball with impunity in the first half against the Boston College Eagles.

This time it was running back Jonaz Walton's turn to score a touchdown. His 1-yard touchdown run capped off a 8-play, 69-yard drive that took 5:21 off the clock.

Walton had a 28-yard run on the drive, and wide receiver Jordan Faison had a 28-yard reverse, as well.
