Why Nvidia Stock Is Down Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Anthropic's record IPO was supposed to give the AI trade a lift, but Nvidia stock is down again on Friday and headed for its seventh straight losing day.

By Johnny Rice – Nov 20, 2026 at 12:35PM EST

Nvidia (NVDA -10.83%) stock is down 7.5% as of 12:28 p.m. ET on Friday, Nov. 20, after the Anthropic (ANT -9.54%) IPO failed to revive the AI trade.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC -2.77%) and the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -4.23%) are down 2.2% and 3.6%, respectively.

Stocks stumbled around the world after the record IPO of the AI company Anthropic failed to restore investors' faith in AI -- an industry vital to the overall market, with 49% of the S&P 500 directly linked.

AI stocks have had a tough go for some time now, with concerns over ballooning costs and limited value realized outside of specific applications -- namely, coding.

Many investors were looking to the Anthropic IPO as a sort of "shot-in-the-arm" for AI stocks. But after opening yesterday 34% over the offer price of $83, Anthropic shares have fallen 25% from their high, now hovering just above the IPO price.

Of course, Nvidia specifically has been dealing with fallout from the departure of its co-founder and 33-year CEO Jensen Huang, who resigned on Nov. 12 in the wake of allegations of extramarital affairs, one of which was with a possible Chinese intelligence asset.

Jensen Huang. Image source: Getty Images.

The stock is headed for its seventh straight losing day, down 26% since the reports first surfaced. That's despite reporting another round of incredible numbers for its latest quarter, with sales jumping 103% year over year.
