Lionsgate Lands Spec ‘Tell Me Everything’ With Dakota Johnson On Board To Star And Produce

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

EXCLUSIVE: Following a competitive auction, Lionsgate has landed the rights to the relationship-dramedy Tell Me Everything with Dakota Johnson attached to star and produce. The package had multiple offers with Lionsgate coming in with the winning bid last week.

In the film, when a celebrity couple’s therapist runs into marital troubles of her own, she encounters a cynical divorce attorney (Johnson) who no longer believes in love. Their unexpected bond causes both to question everything they know about love in this modern tale about marriage, commitment, and relationships.

Johnson, Ro Donnelly and Samantha Racanelli will produce for TeaTime Pictures. Gaelyn Golde penned the script and will executive produce. Chelsea Kujawa and Maria Ascanio will oversee for Lionsgate. The deals were negotiated by Dan Freedman on behalf of the studio. Watch on Deadline

Johnson recently starred in Amazon’s Verity, Neon’s Splitsville, which she also produced, and in Celine Song’s Materialists for A24. Other notable films include Universal’s billion-dollar Fifty Shades of Grey franchise, The Lost Daughter, Suspiria, Our Friend, The Social Network, Black Mass and The Peanut Butter Falcon among others. Through her production company, TeaTime Pictures, Johnson has produced and starred in Daddio, Cha Cha Real Smooth, Am I OK? And the upcoming The Three Incestuous Sisters.

Golde most recently wrote and executive produced Netflix’s upcoming Saturn Return, starring Rachel Brosnahan, Charles Melton, and Will Poulter. Academy Award nominee Greg Kwedar directed, with Plan B producing. She is also developing a feature at Netflix with Kelly Fremon Craig producing. On the television side, Golde is a writer and producer on both season 1 and 2 of Hulu’s Chad Powers, co-created by Michael Waldron and Glen Powell, who also stars.

TeaTime first gained recognition with two feature films at the 2022 Sundance Film Festival, Cha Cha Real Smooth and Am I OK?, the former winning the U.S. Dramatic Audience Award and acquired by Apple TV. TeaTime is currently in post on Alice Rohrawacher’s The Three Incestuous Sisters, starring Johnson, Saoirse Ronan, Jessie Buckley, and Josh O’Connor. Prior to that, the team released the chaotic comedy Splitsville with Neon, after premiering at Cannes.

Johnson is repped by CAA, Untitled, and Johnson Shapiro Slewett Kole. TeaTime Pictures is repped by CAA and Johnson Shapiro Slewett Kole. Golde is repped by CAA and Ginsburg Daniels Kallis.
