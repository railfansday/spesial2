Velocity Financial (VEL) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Record originations, and a Toorak deal reshaping the loan mix.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Velocity Financial, Inc. (VEL +2.81%) reported record quarterly loan originations and expansion of its total loan portfolio to $7.5 billion. Management reported that the company's specific market niche continues to see increased demand in rising rate environments as independent mortgage brokers transition away from consumer mortgage originations. The company expects the pending Toorak acquisition to close by the end of 2026, which is projected to diversify the product mix toward short-duration lending and provide an immediate presence in the U.K. market. Velocity maintained net interest margins above its 3.5% target while executing its largest-ever securitization in October.
