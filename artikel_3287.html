Bose’s Best Headphones and Earbuds Are Steeply Discounted for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Rolling Stone may receive an affiliate commission.

While Black Friday is in the home stretch, plenty of deals linger through the weekend and into Cyber Monday. Among the best deals we’ve spotted this year have been on tech, with headphones and earbuds getting some serious markdowns.

One of the biggest names in audio is also running some of the biggest discounts — right now, you can save up to $190 on noise-canceling headphones and earbuds from Bose.

The discounts apply to flagship models, as well as the brand’s lower-priced headphones. These are some of the best Bose deals you can get right now.

The second-generation QuietComfort headphones are a great pick for anyone looking for comfortable, easy-to-use headphones. They have physical buttons, rather than touch sensors. You can listen to them using a wired USB-C cable or wirelessly over Bluetooth, although they don’t have a port for a 3.5mm cable. The noise-canceling strength is customizable in the app, a feature found across Bose’s noise-canceling headphones.

Bose’s flagship QuietComfort Ultra headphones are also discounted, although the markdown is smaller. These feature a high-end build with metal and faux leather accents, as well as some premium features not found on the non-Ultra version. If you set the headphones down, they automatically disconnect from Bluetooth and enter a low-power mode. When you put them back on, they turn back on and reconnect. They have rich, full audio, and the noise-canceling can smooth out sudden spikes in background noise.

The older QuietComfort headphones are a great alternative to the newer model, offering many of the same features, including physical buttons and customizable noise-canceling modes. These include a wired 2.5 mm to 3.5 mm cable, which allows the headphones to be used passively (that is, you don’t have to turn them on to use them wired). The headphones are available for over half-off in select colors.

Of the models we tested, the QuietComfort Ultra earbuds have the best noise-canceling in a pair of in-ear buds. As with the headphones, you can adjust the noise-canceling in the Bose app, with the ability to create personalized noise-canceling modes. Swipe controls on the earbuds also allow you to adjust the volume and control playback. They’re also comfortable, with multiple eartips and stability bands for a secure fit.

The Ultra Open earbuds feature a clip-on design that hooks onto your outer ear, leaving the ear canal open for awareness while doing outdoor workouts or commuting. Despite the open-ear design, the sound is full, with surprisingly deep bass. The second-generation version, released this year, adds improved call quality and wireless charging. They’re $50 off during Black Friday.
