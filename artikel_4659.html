Dog bite survey identifies owner control as key to preventing attacks across breeds

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A new analysis focusing on dog attacks on humans in the U.K. suggests that most incidents are preventable and involve a wide variety of breeds rather than solely highly publicized "dangerous dogs"—and owners have a key part to play in prevention. The new report, entitled "Safety Around Dogs: A Process Analysis of Dog Bites," was carried out by Anglia Ruskin University (ARU) and examined court records, hospital admission, policy and survey data.
