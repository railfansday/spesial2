Juventus vs Omonia &#8211; Match preview and team news

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Juventus, sitting in fifth place in the UEFA Europa League group standings, will face Omonia from Cyprus on Thursday in their next match at the Allianz Stadium in Torino.

Manager Luciano Spalletti has confirmed several rotational changes for Juventus ahead of Sunday's Serie A clash against Como.

Goalkeeper Norberto Neto and young forward Jeff Ekhator will make their first starts of the season, while Andrea Cambiaso returns to the starting lineup on the left flank.

In positive fitness news, Francisco Conceição is available again after recovering from a 40-day shoulder layoff, and Kerim Alajbegović is reportedly in contention to return after picking up a minor knock against Venezia.

However, the Bianconeri still face a lengthy list of absences, with captain Manuel Locatelli, Kenan Yıldız, Khéphren Thuram, Kamil Grabara (ACL), Pierre Kalulu (hamstring), and Nico González all sidelined through injury.

Additionally, Daniele Rugani, Edon Zhegrova, and Arkadiusz Milik are unavailable for selection tonight because they were omitted from Juventus's official UEFA Europa League squad list.

Head coach Henning Berg brings an experienced Omonia Nicosia squad to Turin, though the Cypriot side must navigate a few fitness adjustments.

Midfielder Carel Eiting remains ruled out of action as he continues to recover from a long-term knee injury. On a more positive note, goalkeeper Thomas Kaminski is expected to return between the posts, while Hungary international Loïc Nego also recently returned from an injury layoff to bolster the defensive options.

Up front, forward Loïs Diony is expected to lead the attack after an impressive run of form that has seen him score in three consecutive matches.

At the back, former AC Milan and Genoa defender Stefan Simić provides valuable Serie A familiarity and experience as Omonia look to contain Juventus's rotated front line in front of a hostile crowd at the Allianz Stadium.

Juventus enter tonight's fixture in strong momentum, remaining unbeaten across their last four matches in all competitions (3W, 1D). Following a 2-0 Serie A victory over Napoli and a 2-1 Europa League triumph at AZ Alkmaar, the Bianconeri drew 2-2 against Fiorentina before routing Venezia 4-0 on Sunday. Luciano Spalletti's side is also unbeaten in their four Europa League outings this campaign, averaging 1.9 goals per game.

Omonia Nicosia arrive in Turin with mixed recent results, posting two wins, one draw, and two losses across their last five outings. Domestically, Henning Berg's side sits fifth in the Cypriot First Division after a 2-0 win over Anorthosis and a 1-1 derby draw against APOEL. However, their European form has struggled, having suffered consecutive Europa League defeats against Benfica (1-4) and NEC Nijmegen (1-3).

Juventus: Neto; Gatti, Bremer, Lucumi; Weston McKennie, Douglas Luiz, Pape Matar Sarr, Andrea Cambiaso; Francisco Conceição, Jeff Ekhator, Jérémie Boga

Omonia: Kaminski; Mayambela, Coulibaly, Satka, Duverne; Andreou, Maric; Ewandro, Tanase, Montnor; Diony
