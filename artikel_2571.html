Dolphins’ Struggles Continue as Defense Sizzles and Offense Fizzles

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Miami Dolphins fans endured another disappointing Sunday as the team fell 26-9 to the Buffalo Bills, marking a continued struggle for the offense. The final score may suggest a closer contest, but the reality was that Miami's attack faltered when it mattered most. Quarterback Kyle McCord, in just his second start, managed a mere 102 passing yards and threw one interception, resulting in the league's lowest passer rating at 32.6.

While the offense sputtered, the defense fought valiantly to keep the game competitive. Safety Zayne Anderson shone in a depleted secondary, earning a stellar 90.8 grade from Pro Football Focus for his performance. He recorded an interception, a sack, and three pass breakups, helping to limit Buffalo to field goals on three of five red-zone trips.

Defense Shines Amidst Offensive Struggles

Despite the offensive woes, Miami's defensive unit displayed tenacity and energy. Rookie linebacker Jacob Rodriguez led the team with 13 combined tackles and added an interception, placing him seventh in the NFL with 93 tackles this season. The defense also managed to pressure Bills quarterback Josh Allen, forcing him into two interceptions.

Head Coach Jeff Hafley praised the defensive strategy against Allen, stating, "We pressured him early and gave him a lot of different looks." He acknowledged that while the plan was effective, there remains room for improvement. "Rush plus coverage, I think that's a pretty good day against Josh. But we can do better," he added.

Unfortunately for Miami, the offense failed to capitalize on the defensive stops. The Dolphins converted just 2 of 14 third downs and were unable to reach the end zone. New kicker Daniel Carlson accounted for all nine of Miami's points, successfully making all three of his field goals, including two from impressive distances of 52 yards.

McCord recognized the struggles of the offense, stating, "It's tough to win games when you're kicking field goals all day." His receiving corps didn't help matters, with Chris Bell and Caleb Douglas combining for five dropped passes. Douglas, targeted ten times, only managed to catch two passes for 30 yards.

Hafley addressed the challenges faced by the rookie receivers, noting, "Young player. It was one of those days." He expressed disappointment that Douglas missed an opportunity for a breakout performance, saying, "It could have been another huge day for him. It wasn't."

With the loss, the Dolphins now sit at 1-9 for the season. While the team has shown resilience, it's clear that they need a more potent offensive output to secure victories. As the offseason approaches, the front office will face crucial decisions regarding potential reinforcements around the quarterback. Both Bell and Douglas have room for growth, but the need for additional receiving talent will become increasingly apparent.

Looking ahead, Miami will face the New York Jets on Sunday, November 29, at 1 p.m. ET at Hard Rock Stadium. The Dolphins will need to regroup and find a way to convert their defensive efforts into offensive production if they hope to turn their season around.
