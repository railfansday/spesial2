Magic: The Gathering Reality Fracture Play Booster Boxes are under $140 on Amazon — save almost $20

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

TL;DR: The Magic: The Gathering Reality Fracture Play Booster Box is on sale for $137.94 at Amazon, down from its $154.99 list price.

It hasn't even been two weeks since Magic: The Gathering's Reality Fracture expansion was released, but you can already score boxes of 30 packs for $4.59 each.

As of Nov. 2, the Magic: The Gathering Reality Fracture Play Booster Box can be bought for $137.94 at Amazon. That’s not only $17.05 less than the $154.99 preorder deal we covered back in July, but the lowest price we’ve seen since just before mid-September (confirmed with price tracker camelcamelcamel).

Keep in mind that, rather than Amazon directly, this deal is coming from third-party seller Undiscovered Realm Inc.. Delivery is currently set for Nov. 18. However, the slightly more expensive listing for $138.01, shipped and sold by Amazon, can get to you as soon as Saturday, Nov. 7 — or tomorrow if you’re a Prime member.

Walmart is selling the Reality Fracture Booster Box for $124.95 via third-party seller Cataclysm Games (but still delivered through Walmart). On the other hand, dedicated trading card marketplace TCGplayer has listings going for as low as $119.98 when factoring in shipping — which is scraping under its estimated $119.98 market price by 18 cents.

Reality Fracture's Play Booster Box contains 30 packs of 14 Magic cards each, including at least one Traditional Foil and between one and five Rare or higher cards per booster. Every pack also features a Legendary card paired with its alternate Echoverse version, giving you over 60 Legendary cards across the display.

The expansion follows Jace Beleren's attempt to reshape the Multiverse, introducing twisted counterparts of familiar characters such as Liliana, Chandra, Ajani, and Garruk, as well as the dystopian Hexhaven school.
