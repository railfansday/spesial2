How To Fly Drones In Grand Theft Auto 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

If you want to race without the consquences of a real crash, drones are for you.

Of all the ways to race in Grand Theft Auto 6, drones are some of the trickiest to get right. Even though they aren't as fast as some vehicles, the courses they have to fly can feel faster-paced than some car or bike races. They're tight and technical, but incredibly rewarding when you nail them.

These races aren't the same as others, either, because they're time trials rather than all-out races. This makes it easier to lock in and learn the courses a bit better, even though they aren't dedicated tracks. Just try not to hit any pedestrians, as it'll kill your race and still affect your reputation.

You can race drones all throughout Leonida, but you've got to buy into each one. It costs $500 to buy in, and it unlocks the first of three events in each location. There are city events, rural events, and even some beach events.

On the map, go to the Activities tab, and then scroll down to Drones. Any Top Fun Drones locations you've passed while playing will appear here, and you can set a waypoint to get to them.

Once you arrive at a destination, instead of speaking to an event organizer like at other races, approach the Top Fun van and scan the QR code when prompted. This will pay your admission fee and let you start the first event.

It can be tough to get started, but there are easy ways to pick yourself up.

Drone racing events are structured as time trials rather than races with multiple people. This means you don't have to worry about getting knocked around by other racers, but every move or mistake you make falls back on you and your skills.

When the event starts, you don't have to hold any button to accelerate; you can simply press forward on the left thumbstick to start to move. You can rotate the camera with the right thumbstick, and you control your elevation by using R2 and L2 on PS5 or the left and right triggers on Xbox.

You can also swap between first- and third-person cameras by pressing Triangle on PS5 or Y on Xbox. Which one you fly in is up to personal preference, but you can swap freely throughout a race if you find it helpful.

You can't boost or go any faster than pressing forward while flying drones.

Buying entry to the races will only give you access to the first event in the series, and you'll have to beat the par time to progress to the next event. The par times are fair but challenging, meaning you can make a mistake or two, but more than that means your time won't be enough to beat the goal.

Beating the first event in a series wins you $750, covering your entry fee and adding an extra $250. After that, the prizes double: the second event pays $1500, and the third wins you a cool $3000.

One of the best tips for doing well in drone racing is to practice and be patient. Each event gets more challenging than the last, with the last one requiring some truly technical flying. They have you pass through doorways or arches, fly alongside pedestrians, and even cut around cars in parking garages.

The drones are durable enough to take a few hits, but too much damage can destroy them. In first person, an on-screen alert shows when the drone reaches critical damage, but in third person you can't see it. Neither third nor first person gives you a proper health bar.

Unfortunately, you can't restart a race the same way you can for other race types, so if you make too many mistakes, you still have to see the event through to the end. This can be time-consuming, especially if you know you won't make it in time, but you can intentionally crash out of a race, which will automatically restart it without penalty.

If you're struggling with making up a few seconds, sometimes it feels faster to fly in first person. It can be harder to tell how close you are to checkpoints or obstacles, but once you get the hang of it, the drone feels much more nimble. Third person gives you a bit more spatial awareness, but it feels clunkier than FPV.

After you beat the last event in a series, you unlock the ability to fly the drone freely throughout the race area. You can only take it 2500 meters from the van, but that's still plenty to get a sweet view of the area.

Just note: if you harass people with the drone, you will get a wanted level that carries over to Jason or Lucia when you stop flying. The cops will shoot the drones out of the air, but they'll still have your details when you leave the van.
