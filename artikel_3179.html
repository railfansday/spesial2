Bioventus Inc. (BVS -1.31%) reported third quarter results characterized by modest top-line growth and expanded profitability margins as management continues to execute its strategy across four primary growth drivers. The company focused on accelerating revenue in peripheral nerve stimulation, platelet-rich plasma, ultrasonics, and international markets while maintaining operating discipline to offset increased strategic investments. Management noted that significant cash flow generation enabled the company to achieve a financial milestone by reducing net debt to below $200 million. During the call, the company confirmed that its ongoing review of strategic alternatives remains active following prior expressions of interest, though no specific timeline for a conclusion was provided.
