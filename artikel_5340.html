Coinbase plans to offer its own prediction market contracts in the first half of 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Coinbase offers event contracts through a Kalshi partnership; own listings would give it more control over contracts and revenue

Coinbase Global is aiming to launch its own U.S. event contracts within the first six months of 2027, according to Bloomberg. Bloomberg, citing a person familiar with the matter, reports that both retail and institutional customers would be able to trade the contracts.

Coinbase now provides access to prediction markets by working with Kalshi. In-house contracts would let Coinbase decide which markets appear on its platform and retain a bigger share of trading revenue, according to Bloomberg.

A Coinbase spokesperson referred to the company's plans from October, when it said it would broaden its prediction-market offering and strengthen that business's economics by building its own clearing and other infrastructure. "We have nothing new to announce today," the spokesperson said, according to Bloomberg. The statement did not say when an in-house launch might happen.

In October, Chief Financial Officer Alesia Haas indicated that Coinbase intended to list a number of event contracts of its own, though she offered no timeline, according to Bloomberg. In the third quarter, annualized revenue from the event contracts business ran above $200 million.

In December 2025, Coinbase announced its acquisition of The Clearing Company, a startup focused on prediction markets, according to Bloomberg. The Clearing Company runs a regulated derivatives exchange, and in September the Commodity Futures Trading Commission signed off on a clearinghouse of its own.

Coinbase's prediction-market business faces legal pressure from state authorities. New York state has sued Coinbase, Kalshi and Gemini, arguing the platforms are unlawful gambling businesses. The platforms say they are federally regulated financial exchanges not subject to state betting laws.

New York City Council Speaker Julie Menin announced an investigation in August into the marketing practices of Polymarket, Kalshi, Coinbase and Gemini Titan. The CFTC holds primary oversight of platforms such as Kalshi and the U.S. arm of Polymarket.
