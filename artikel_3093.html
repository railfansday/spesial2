Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Sequans is pivoting to 5G and defense after exiting its Bitcoin strategy.

Sequans Communications S.A. (SQNS +10.89%) reported a strategic pivot back to its core semiconductor operations following the full exit from its Bitcoin treasury strategy and the redemption of its convertible debt. Management reported that the company is now focused on scaling its 4G IoT product business and expanding into the defense and drone sectors via its RF transceiver platform. The company stated that growth is being driven by the conversion of its design-win pipeline into mass production revenue, particularly within the smart metering and telematics verticals. Efforts are also underway to transition the product roadmap from 4G to 5G technology, with development kit shipments and lab testing advancing for next-generation applications. Sequans is managing supply chain constraints through diversified sourcing while maintaining a simplified capital structure to support its path toward breaking even.
