Kevin Kline Joins Meryl Streep In Netflix’s ‘The Corrections’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Academy Award winner Kevin Kline is set to star opposite the previously announced Meryl Streep in The Corrections, Netflix‘s series adaptation of Jonathan Franzen’s National Book Award winner from Paramount Television Studios.

Kline plays Alfred Lambert, the husband of Streep’s Enid Lambert. A once-powerful patriarch battling the afflictions of aging, Alfred is a man of strict principles and old-fashioned decorum who must come to terms with his three very modern children — a daughter whose secrets he can no longer protect, an older son who has displaced him as the authority in the family, and a wayward son whose forgiveness he yearns for. The series brings Kline and Streep back together more than 40 years after they first collaborated on Sophie’s Choice, the Academy Award-winning classic which marked Kline’s feature film debut. Over the years, the duo have also worked together on the films A Prairie Home Companion and Ricki and the Flash, along with a handful of stage productions. Watch on Deadline

The Corrections is a comic portrait of a Midwestern family whose three unmoored adult siblings desperately resist their mother’s wish for one last Christmas together — each undone by the delusional ambitions that were supposed to save them from becoming their parents.

Franzen adapted his book for Netflix and exec produces the limited drama alongside American Fiction‘s Cord Jefferson, who also directs all episodes. Other exec producers include Mark Roybal (Task, Mare of Easttown) and Paul Lee (Task, The Summer I Turned Pretty) for wiip; Nicole Clemens (Sexy Beast, Shantaram); Amy Sayres and Streep.

An Oscar and three-time Tony Award winner, Kline is best known for turns in such classic films as A Fish Called Wanda, Sophie’s Choice and The Big Chill. Most recently, he’s been seen starring in MGM+’s American Classic, opposite Laura Linney, and Alfonso Cuarón’s Emmy-nominated Apple series Disclaimer, opposite Cate Blanchett and Sacha Baron Cohen. He is repped by UTA and Berwick & Kovacik.
