Lyon dropped by Australia for Test series vs New Zealand

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Nathan Lyon left out of Australia's 13-player squad for Test series at home to New Zealand, which starts in Perth on December 9; uncapped off-spinner Corey Rocchiccioli and batter Jack Clayton called up; Tests will also take place in Adelaide, Melbourne and Sydney

Nathan Lyon has been dropped by Australia for their four-Test series at home to New Zealand, with the veteran replaced by fellow off-spinner Corey Rocchiccioli.

Lyon, a mainstay for the Baggy Greens for 13 years, has not spoken publicly since Usman Khawaja's explosive Sandpapergate claims were published in his autobiography.

Australia selection chair George Bailey confirmed that the 39-year-old's omission for a series that starts in Perth on December 9 was not related to the ongoing saga but based on performances.

Pat Cummins (captain), Scott Boland, Alex Carey, Jack Clayton, Cameron Green, Josh Hazlewood, Travis Head, Josh Inglis, Michael Neser, Matt Renshaw, Corey Rocchiccioli, Steve Smith, Mitchell Starc.

The uncapped Rocchiccioli, who has taken 205 wickets in 59 first-class matches and last week scored his maiden century, could debut on his home ground at Optus Stadium in the series opener.

Other notable omissions include Marnus Labuschagne, Cooper Connolly and Beau Webster, with middle-order batter Jack Clayton drafted into the squad and in line for a first appearance.

Bailey said to reporters of Lyon: "Nathan has not quite had the impact we would like with the ball in recent matches.

"We also know he has an extraordinary record, ⁠and his experience, knowledge and skill can still be ⁠valuable to the program we have moving forward.

"[It was a] challenging conversation and the key to that conversation from the panel's point of view was we hope it's not the end for him."

Australia head to India for a five-Test tour following the series against New Zealand and Bailey indicated Lyon would be a likely inclusion for that trip if he wanted to extend his Australia career.

Bailey added: "I think he can still have enormous value when you project forward to India.

"Given his record there, his experience and his knowledge, and how challenging a place that is to tour, and the fact we've got five Tests there, no, I don't think [he would miss out].

"But understandably, it was a tough conversation for him. I think he's taking a little bit of time."

In recent years, Lyon has seen his role with the Test side diminished, with a right hamstring injury, sustained during day five of the third Ashes Test in 2025, ruling him out of Test selection for eight months.

Lyon, who ranks second as Australia's all-time leading wicket-taker behind Shane Warne with 577 wickets from 145 Tests, had previously been dropped for the second Ashes Test at The Gabba.

He spoke openly about the omission, saying he was "filthy" and "gutted" about being dropped.

Lyon had also been omitted from Australia's second Test against South Africa in October.

Prior to last year's Test at The Gabba, the off-spinner had remarkably not been left out of a Test squad since he made his debut in 2011.

Rocchiccioli will likely go head-to-head with seamer Scott Boland for a spot in the side to play New Zealand on the fiery Perth pitch in the first Test.

"Corey has been a strong performer in domestic cricket for the past few seasons and has traits that are well suited to Australian conditions," Bailey said.

Under-scrutiny skipper Pat Cummins will captain the side, while Josh Hazlewood, Mitchell Starc and Steve Smith, who have all found themselves at the centre of Australia's recent Sandpapergate saga, were also named in the group.

Smith had missed Australia's final Test against South Africa in Cape Town with a neck injury.

Australia will add one final player after the sixth round of Sheffield Shield matches.

First Test (December 9-13): Optus Stadium Perth

Second Test (December 17-21): Adelaide Oval

Third Test (December 26-30): MCG, Melbourne
