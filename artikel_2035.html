22-year-old yet to justify new Charlton Athletic contract amid challenging League One loan spell

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Charlton Athletic have some exciting prospects in the squad. Micah Mbick has remained for the 2026/27 season and has featured a few times for CAFC.

Four other young talents were loaned out for the campaign to further their development in the hope they can break into the first team in the next few years. Out of the quartet, Nathan Jones will be regularly monitoring Daniel Kanu.

The 22-year-old signed for Notts County for the third loan spell of his career. The striker had a first-team role whilst Charlton Athletic were in League One. However, the jury is still out whether his current contract should be extended and if he can make the jump to the Championship.

Notts County won the race to secure Kanu for the 2026/27 season. He was prolific in League Two last season for Walsall, scoring 18 goals and providing three assists in 44 games.

The 22-year-old has been unable to reach the same heights in League One, though, with the Magpies. In his 10 league appearances so far, the forward has started seven times and scored two goals.

His form at Meadow Lane is to be desired this term. The Charlton Athletic academy graduate has scored one goal from open play and has just five shots on target this season. Kanu has also won just 24.4% of his duels and has a successful dribble rate of 22.2%.

The 22-year-old still has time to rediscover his form from last year, though. His best display this campaign came in an impressive 2-0 victory over Sheffield Wednesday. The youngster had 10 touches in the opposition box and won 75% of his ground duels while scoring from the spot.

Kanu is currently under contract at The Valley until 2028. Realistically, though, his future at Charlton Athletic must be decided in the summer before entering the final year of his deal.

CAFC rejected an offer from Luton Town last summer, and if they are to sell him, they should do so at the end of the season. The Addicks’ goal-scoring problems have continued this term with just 14 goals in 16 games.

Given Kanu’s underwhelming stint at Notts County so far, it’s unlikely he will be able to make the step up to the Championship for next campaign. However, if Jones’ side are relegated, it could give the 22-year-old a chance to feature in the first team. If so, a new contract should be given to the striker.
