David Moyes' Everton side were put to the sword by Brentford at the Gtech Community Stadium on Monday night.

Keane Lewis-Potter fired two goals past Jordan Pickford before James Tarkowski was shown a straight red card in the 40th minute on 23 November.

That inevitably only piled more pressure on the Toffees, with Vitaly Janelt and Fabio Carvalho adding goals in the second half for a 4-0 win.

It was more than disappointing for Moyes' side, who have now dropped to ninth in the Premier League table, while the Bees have risen to sixth.

There was absolutely no way back after Tarkowski's red card, a controversial one at that, and the Toffees were eventually suffocated by their opponents.

In case you missed the match, Football Insider has taken a look at some moments that might have flown under the radar at the Gtech.

Of course, when Tarkowski flew into his challenge on Yehor Yarmoliuk, the veteran defender was pleading his innocence.

But every player now knows what a pitch-side monitor review means. So, before Craig Pawson had even returned to the pitch, he was down the tunnel.

Everton fans argued that Michael Kayode could have been sent off as well, but the commentators couldn't help but focus on something else.

The right-back's challenge on Jack Grealish was rather high, not enough for a red card, but it was a painful one.

Grealish may only have himself to blame, purely because of his tiny shin pads, ones that barely cover any of his leg at all.

It was a tough night for Pickford, conceding four goals for the first time since facing Brentford all the way back in January.
