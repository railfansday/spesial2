Ex-goalkeeper names England's 'most suited' number one out of Jordan Pickford and James Trafford

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Leeds United number one James Trafford should be Thomas Tuchel's first choice in goal for England ahead of Everton star Jordan Pickford.

That is according to former senior Everton scout and ex-Millwall and Coventry City goalkeeper, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The Leeds goalkeeper started on Sunday as England beat Spain 3-2 in Madrid, making several key saves in the second half as the 10-man visitors held on.

Trafford left Manchester City for the Whites in the summer transfer window, joining in a deal worth up to £45million after he had made just 17 appearances under Pep Guardiola.

Now the undisputed number one at Elland Road, the 24-year-old looks to be putting long-term England goalkeeper Pickford under serious pressure.

The 32-year-old has 94 England caps and has never let his country down, but may now face a battle for his position.

While it is debatable if Trafford is a better shot stopper than Pickford, there is no arguing about the difference in quality in build-up.

The 24-year-old is a natural with the ball at his feet - a quality that Tuchel may find hard to resist.

"I look at how many caps Pickford has won and I just shake my head. He's got 94 caps and that's basically because there's been no real opposition for him," King told Football Insider.

"[Dean] Henderson looked as though he could provide that opposition, but he's playing in a side where the team makes mistakes. That leads to poor goals going in.

"[Nick] Pope just disappeared off the face of the planet, you know, number three at Newcastle. [Aaron] Ramsdale looked as though he was going to be the business at Arsenal.

"Arsenal were too big for him. Emirates Stadium was too big for him. Went to Southampton, did okay, still there. He'll never been an England goalkeeper again.

"But then you start to think, who is there? You can't name today five top English goalkeepers. At the moment, Trafford is the most suited man for the job.

"He's a very down-to-earth boy, isn't he? I like him. I like his attitude. He's quiet. He gets on with his job. He's a Gordon Banks."

England had been cruising against the world champions until Elliot Anderson's red card in the 63rd minute.

Spain converted the penalty and scored 10 minutes later through Nico Williams, setting up a grandstand finish.

Trafford made six saves and prevented 2.57 goals in the closing stages, including a spectacular double save to deny Fermin Lopez and Aymeric Laporte.

Even if the Leeds man has not earned the shirt yet, he has almost certainly opened the conversation.
