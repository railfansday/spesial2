Zelenskyy Eyes New Congress If Trump Can’t Push Putin to Peace

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy will reach out to the new Democrat-controlled US Congress to intensify pressure on Russia if Donald Trump fails to convince Vladimir Putin to end the war by the end of the year. Ukraine will seek a robust package of US military aid and tougher sanctions on Russia if no peace deal acceptable to Kyiv is reached, Zelenskyy said in an interview with Bloomberg late Monday.  Angela Stent, Senior Fellow at the American Enterprise Institute, says despite recent negotiations, there's no sign Russia and Ukraine are any closer to peace. (Source: Bloomberg)
