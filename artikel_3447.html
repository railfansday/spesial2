Apple updates Design Resources portal with assets for the new touchscreen MacBook Pro

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

If you’re a designer who relies on Apple’s official design resources, the company has already added the new MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display to its collection of product bezels. Here are the details.

Apple’s Design Resources portal features a great collection of hardware and software assets that designers can use in their own work.

That includes design guidelines, Figma, Sketch, Keynote, Photoshop, PDF, PNG, and SVG assets for Apple platforms, products, and technologies, as well as fonts, SF Symbols, Apple’s Icon Composer, Parallax Previewer, and badges and logos for services such as Apple Health, Apple Wallet, and more.

Following today’s announcement of the long-rumored MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display and touchscreen support, Apple has updated its Design Resources portal with Photoshop and PNG assets for the new machine.

The downloadable .dmg file includes transparent PNG images of the new Mac in both colors and sizes, as well as PSD files with properly masked display areas that designers can use to insert their own layouts and interfaces.
