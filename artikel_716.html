Tatsuya Endo’s ‘SPY×FAMILY’ Vol. 18 Holds at No. 1 on Billboard Japan Book Hot 100

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Hiromu Arakawa's fantasy manga Yomi no Tsugai Vol. 14 follows, debuting at No. 2.

Tatsuya Endo’s SPY×FAMILY Vol. 18 logs its second week at No. 1 on the Billboard Japan Book Hot 100 chart after debuting at No. 1 last week.

On the list released Nov. 19, SPY×FAMILY Vol. 18 places No. 1 in brick-and-mortar and No. 2 in e-books, while also landing within the top 100 in e-commerce (EC) and social media. A fourth season of the TV anime adaptation has been announced for 2027. Hiromu Arakawa’s fantasy manga Yomi no Tsugai Vol. 14 follows, debuting at No. 2 and charting within the top 20 on three metrics: No. 2 in brick-and-mortar, No. 6 in EC and No. 14 in e-books.

Coming in at No. 3 is Uketsu no Ie, the latest work from Uketsu, whose Hen na (Strange) series has surpassed 10 million copies worldwide. Appearing at first glance to be a fan book looking back on Uketsu’s past 10 years of activity, it’s a “history novel” that draws readers into a new mystery. The title lands in the top 10 on four metrics this week — No. 3 in brick-and-mortar, No. 8 in EC and No. 9 in both e-books and social media — making it one to watch in the weeks ahead.
