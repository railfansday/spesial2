NBA’s recent Victor Wembanyama announcement has Lakers and Luka Doncic fans upset

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The NBA's Player of the Week announcement saw San Antonio Spurs' center Victor Wembanyama win the award over Los Angeles Lakers guard Luka Doncic.

The Lakers and Spurs both went perfect over the week of November 8 – 15, winning four games apiece while being led by their star players, Doncic and Wembanyama.

With only one of them being able to win player of the week, the NBA decided to award it to Wembanyama, presumably because of his incredible defensive output.

Wembanyama won player of the week in the West while Giannis Antetokounmpo was awarded in the East. Lakers fans are not happy about this, complaining that Doncic was robbed of the honour.

"Wemby, I'mma let you finish, but Luka had one of the greatest weeks of all time," said one fan, referencing Kanye West's interruption of Taylor Swift's VMA speech.

"NBA, stop with this Wemby favouritism! Luka is clearly the winner by a mile smh," said one angry fan.

"Did Luka die or something?" questioned one fan sarcastically after seeing the results.

"12-point game against Curryless Warriors and getting carried by your backcourt to an 8-point win against the 1-13 hospital Jazz gets you player of the week. Standards have never been lower for Chemby," said a fan critically.

"Is the NBA run by morons? Luka is destroying everyone, and the Lakers have the best record in the league, yet he can't sniff an award," said another frustrated fan.

Wembanyama averaged 27.5 points, 10.5 rebounds, 4.0 assists and 5.0 blocks over the week in question with four wins, while Doncic averaged 35.7 points, 8.0 assists, 5.3 rebounds and 2.7 steals in three wins.

The Spurs generally had bigger wins, and Wembanyama's value as a two-way star often makes people overcorrect takes in comparison to Doncic, who is an offensive juggernaut.

But ignoring a 40-point average in a week without a loss for Wembanyama's numbers is understandably frustrating for Lakers and Doncic fans.

However, this was two monster weeks facing up against each other, with voter preference seemingly falling Wemby's way this week after Doncic won the award for November 1 – 8 over Wembanyama.
