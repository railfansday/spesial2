Spanish journalist tears into two Real Madrid defenders &#8211; &#8220;Not to be starters&#8221;

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Real Madrid extended their brilliant run of form with an emphatic 5-1 win over PSV Eindhoven in the Champions League, thus stretching their winning streak to 10 games across all competitions.

Despite the solid attacking display, though, not everything was rosy for Jose Mourinho's side, which endured a difficult first half.

Real Madrid's sloppy start has drawn criticism, particularly regarding some of Mourinho's rotation options.

Militao didn't have the best of games against PSV. (Photo by Angel Martinez/Getty Images)

Alberto Pereiro, who covers Real Madrid for Onda Cero, singled out Antonio Rudiger and Eder Militao for their performances on the night against PSV.

The pair, which started ahead of Ibrahima Konate and Dean Huijsen, had a tough time defending against PSV's quick forward line and looked quite uncertain throughout.

It came to a point where Mourinho was forced to replace both at the start of the second half.

"After the changes of the centre-backs, both Huijsen and Konate have left Militao and Rudiger very much pointed, who are not right now to be starters in Madrid," Pereiro said on Radioestadio Night (h/t Defensa Central).

Pereiro believes Rudiger and Militao are among a group of squad players failing to take their chances when called up on.

Pereiro also criticised Tchouameni's performance. (Photo by Angel Martinez/Getty Images)

Rudiger and Militao were not the only ones to draw Pereiro's ire, with Aurelien Tchouameni facing the heat from the journalist.

The midfielder, who started after being rested against Celta, looked uncomfortable and conceded the penalty that led to PSV's goal.

"I have the feeling that the opportunities for those who do not perform can be ended, and Militao, Rudiger and Tchouameni have been very pointed out against the PSV," Pereiro added.

Performances from the trio were indeed far from perfect and something Mourinho needs to work on, as Real Madrid brace themselves for an important December, where they may have an opportunity to clinch the top spot in the league table.

In a congested schedule, it will be imperative that all Real Madrid players, including Militao and Rudiger, perform well whenever called upon, if Los Blancos are to indeed maintain their sensational winning run.
