We Finally Know the Severity of Drake Maye’s Injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

We Finally Know the Severity of Drake Maye's Injury originally appeared on NESN. Add NESN as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Drake Maye's injury — and the lack of information made available relating to the injury — has left Patriots fans in the mire in recent days.

The Patriots gunslinger, who was cut down in New England's loss to the Lions on Sunday, is expected to miss most of — it not all — of the rest of the season with his malady. It's unclear who will take his place as New England starter in his stead — Tommy DeVito seems the leading candidate, although things appear murky during the bye week.

Maye's status has been dictated by whispers and murmurs, at least thus far. On Friday, we finally got some tangible news on the true nature of Maye's ailment — as well as what onlookers can expect of his return.

Maye was diagnosed with a fractured right fibula, according to ESPN's Mike Reiss and Adam Schefter. There was no ligament damage, leaving open the possibility that Maye could potentially return at some point in the season, although he's expected to be sidelined for much — if not all — of the remainder of the year.

According to Reiss and Schefter, the nature of Maye's injury is such that he won't have to undergo surgery, In normal circumstances, he would be placed on injured reserve. New England's IR is incredibly bloated, however, so much so that the team has nearly reached its limit on number of players that can be designated to be placed on it.

The Patriots are currently slated to start Tommy DeVito in Maye's absence. The team could dip into the free agent pool ahead of Week 12; there are a host of sidewinders who could get some buzz, including Daniel Jones and Jake Browning.

New England (5-5) sits one game out a playoff berth. The Pats will take on the Bills, Vikings, Chiefs and Broncos as part of their end-of-season slate. No team has qualified as a wild-card team without winning at least nine games since the league extended the season to 17 games in 2021. New England will need some help as it attempts to vie for the final postseason slot.
