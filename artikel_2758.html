Barcelona star suffered an injury blow during Sabah clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Barcelona head coach Hansi Flick has confirmed that Alejandro Balde suffered a fitness setback during the club's meeting with Sabah.

Defender Balde was for his part a surprise inclusion in the Barcelona XI which took to the pitch in Baku on Wednesday night. Amid a challenging campaign to date, the 23-year-old was afforded his very first start at left-back.

Balde responded with an impressive individual performance, including an assist for his side's 3rd of the clash early in the 2nd-half.

After going to ground in evident discomfort a short time later, though, the Spaniard was hauled off for João Cancelo.

Speaking to the media in his post-match press conference, Hansi Flick was therefore asked for an update on the wellbeing of his out-of-favour full-back. And Barca's headmaster went on to explain:

"Balde suffered a minor sprain, which is why he had to leave the pitch. We'll have to wait until tomorrow to find out more."
