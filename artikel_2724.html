Abdul Carter returns to Giants practice on Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Giants welcomed linebacker Abdul Carter back to the practice field on Friday.

Carter was out of practice on Wednesday and Thursday due to illness and a calf injury, but reporters at the open portion of Friday’s workout noted that he was working with the rest of the team. The Giants will release injury designations later in the day, but head coach John Harbaugh downplayed concern about Carter’s availability earlier this week and Friday’s change in status suggests he’ll be ready to go against the Colts.

Carter had two sacks and five quarterback hits in last Sunday’s win over the Jaguars. He was named the NFC defensive player of the week in recognition of that effort.

Reporters also noted that cornerback Dru Phillips was practicing after missing the last two days with an illness. Defensive tackle Shelby Harris has also been sick and was not spotted during the open portion of practice.
