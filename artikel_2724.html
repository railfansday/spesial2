USA Soccer’s Balogun Returns To Action For Monaco

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Folarin Balogun finally broke his scoring drought for Monaco. The United States men's national team striker is back in the mix for the Conference League, according to Fox Sports. He earned his spot after Ansu Fati got hurt and had to leave the squad list. This move clears a huge path for Balogun to get more playing time. It also fixes a strange situation that kept him on the bench for weeks.

The change happened right before the next round of games. Balogun's name now appears on the official roster shown on Tuesday. He was missing from the list just a short time ago. The club decided to swap him in because Fati cannot play. The French daily L'Equipe reported that Fati is out for two months. He suffered the injury in a match against Lille on Oct. 25. That gap left Monaco needing a forward, and Balogun stepped up.

Getting to this point was not easy for the American striker. He only played as a substitute this season. He has featured in five games total. A 3-1 defeat at Paris FC on Sunday marked his first goal of the season. That goal matters more now because it ends a dry spell. It also proves he can score even when the team struggles.

The road to this moment was blocked by transfer drama. Balogun was close to joining Everton in late August. A deal for a reported £40 million (with add-ons) was on the table. He pulled out of the move at the last minute. Doubts from the English club regarding his health made him uneasy. He did not want to sign with a team that questioned his medical results. This decision kept him at Monaco, but it caused confusion.

The striker faced other hurdles before this season even started. A red card during the World Cup caused a major controversy. He got the card in a win over Bosnia and Herzegovina. The suspension was one game, but it was deferred by FIFA. U.S. President Donald Trump intervened to help. Gianni Infantino received a call to check if the red card review was possible. The request worked. Balogun was allowed to play in the round of 16. He had a forgettable performance as the U.S. lost to Belgium.

Now the focus shifts to the upcoming matches. The Conference League saw Monaco secure victories in its opening two matches. They beat CSKA Sofia and Freiburg. The team has tough matches coming up. They will play FC Nordsjælland on Thursday. They also face Hearts, Brighton, and Lincoln Red Imps later. Balogun needs to keep scoring if he wants to stay in the starting lineup. The clock is ticking for him to prove he is ready. The squad list is set, and he is now one of the forwards. Fans will watch closely to see if he can lead the attack.
