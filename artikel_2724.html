Monster performance for DeVonta Smith in Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Philadelphia Eagles WR DeVonta Smith (back) suffered a back injury against the Dallas Cowboys in Week 12, but he finished the game and caught 11 of his 14 targets for 200 yards and two touchdowns.

Why it Matters: Smith landed hard in the fourth quarter, but he managed to finish out the game. It remains to be seen if the injury will linger into Week 13, but if he is able to play, Smith will be a WR2 against Arizona.
