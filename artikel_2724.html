Patriots News: Turnovers fuel embarrassing 45-27 loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

To channel the infamous Grinch on the Patriots game yesterday afternoon:

The three words that best describe it are as follows, and I quote: "Stink, stank, stunk!"

Not sure I can sum it up any better than that. Any team can have a bad game. This one was straight out of a Black Mirror episode. Christian Gonzalez turned into 2005 Duane Starks, best known as a wide receiver escort to the end zone. CB Karen Prunty took up space as a lamppost. OL Walter Rouse became a subway turnstile. Drake Maye was either chucking balls to ghosts or into spaces tighter than my waistband after Thanksgiving.

All that's left is to swallow it, digest it and feel it sit in my gut for a week. Ugh…
