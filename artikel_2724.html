Sixers vs. Trail Blazers: Final playing status for Joel Embiid, others

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

PHILADELPHIA -- The Philadelphia 76ers will get back at it on Monday when they play host to the Portland Trail Blazers in an effort to keep their winning streak going in the right direction. The Sixers have won five in a row and will look to keep things moving forward.

With that being said, they will have to do so while missing star big man Joel Embiid, who was listed as questionable for this contest, but is now being ruled out due to an illness. Embiid is coming off a big game in a win over the Milwaukee Bucks on Saturday in which he had 26 points, 17 rebounds, and nine assists.

As for Jaylen Brown, Adem Bona, and Dean Wade, they have all been upgraded to available. They were all listed as probable for this matchup, but will now give it a go against the Blazers. Brown and Wade have missed the last two games while Bona hasn't played since Nov. 4.

The Sixers and the Trail Blazers will tip off at 7 p.m. EST on Monday night.
