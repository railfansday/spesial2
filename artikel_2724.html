Player Grades: Cavs vs Bulls – Donovan Mitchell scores 39 points

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Cleveland Cavaliers took care of business against the Chicago Bulls in a 126-115 victory.

Grades are based on our usual expectations for each player.

39 points, 4 rebounds, 4 assists, 3 steals

Mitchell proved to everyone that he's still spry, rising up for a crushing alley-oop dunk off of Proctor's pass.

The Cavs have been workshopping the correct approach for Mitchell this season. That's led to some games where he isn't as aggressive as we've come to expect. Tonight, Mitchell picked his spots and asserted himself consistently.

The second half was when Mitchell really had it rolling. He was getting downhill with ease and broke Chicago's defense with his attacks. Mitchell poured in 18 points on a perfect 8-of-8 shooting in the fourth quarter. A tough pull-up three-pointer was the cherry on top, and a final dash to the hoop was the cherry on top of a fantastic scoring night.

Cleveland might be testing their luck with all of this positive quarterback play recently, but Harden was launching Hail Marys all night to Peyton Watson, as it seems there was a clear effort to beat the Bulls in transition. Harden threw at least four full-court passes to Watson, who was leaking out after contesting three-point attempts.

Having Harden be the outlet passer is one way to play with pace without asking a 37-year-old to run a marathon each night.

That said, Harden shot 3-for-10 from the floor.

Allen had a strong game on the glass, racking up 3 offensive rebounds in the first quarter and finishing the game with 6 second-chance opportunities. It feels like the Cavs are at their best when Allen is crushing it on the boards, and tonight was no different.

The Cavs haven't unlocked anything new from Watson offensively yet, as they are still waiting for him to rediscover his three-point jumper. Another poor shooting night has put a limit on how impactful Watson can be — but he's overall been a positive, especially on defense.

Watson left the game midway through the fourth quarter after taking a nasty fall onto his back.

17 points, 3 rebounds, 2 assists, 2 steals, 1 block

Proctor remained in the starting lineup tonight after his triple-double performance this weekend. He looked comfortable on the floor again, playing off-ball next to Mitchell and Harden while making a positive impact on both ends of the court.

10 points, 6 rebounds, 3 assists, 2 steals

Thomas struggled with his outside shot tonight, shooting 1-for-7 from deep. Even so, being a threat to launch it from downtown is worth something. And Thomas managed to lean on his dribble to create good looks inside the paint and opportunities for his teammates.

Hezonja has been a bone-crushing presence for the Cavs. He's a monster on the glass and doesn't concede any ground defensively. Opponents can't move him on either end of the floor, and that makes him a valuable weapon to have.

Bryant has filled in nicely for both Mobley and Allen during this stretch where the bigs have been injured on and off. He gave more quality minutes tonight, providing a spark of energy and veteran leadership to the bench.

4 points, 6 rebounds, 2 assists, 1 block, 1 steal

Omier is earning every minute. He changed the dynamic of this game with his hustle in the second quarter, being always eager to attack the offensive glass or bury a defender with a deep seal in the post.
