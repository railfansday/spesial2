Chris Brown Brings Icy Usher Fan Onstage Months After Viral Snub Amid Beef

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Usher couldn't get a smile out of her, but Chris Brown is a different story ... 'cause the viral fan who gave Usher the icy treatment finally got her Breezy moment!

Gabrielle Cheyenne, who went viral back in July for her awkward encounter with Usher, finally got her wish Sunday night when Chris brought her onstage during the third night of their record five-show Atlanta run for "The R&B Tour."

As we previously reported ... Gabrielle became an internet sensation after looking less than thrilled when Usher brought her onstage during a July concert, barely reacting as the R&B superstar tried to charm her.

She later told TMZ she wasn't trying to disrespect Usher ... she was just hoping she'd been picked to meet Chris Brown instead.

Well, nearly four months later, Gabrielle finally got her wish ... and this time, she was onstage with the right R&B superstar.
