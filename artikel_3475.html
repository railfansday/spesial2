Pokémon Go’s Most Cursed Crossover Officially Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Ready to learn a new language with Pikachu?

Pokémon Go has had its share of crossovers with other brands over the past decade, including The North Face x Gucci, Major League Baseball, and Lego. The mobile gotta-catch-'em-all game's latest will bring a new critter to the game, one known to strike fear in the hearts of mobile users.

As the Fortnite-driven crossover era continues, Pikachu is making his way to the language-learning app Duolingo. The first trailer for the app's crossover with Pokémon Go shows Duo, the app’s owl mascot, finally deciphering what Pikachu is saying.

The crossover between Duolingo and Pokémon Go starts on Nov. 17. Those who join the limited-time Duolingo event can earn up to four Pokémon-themed outfits for their Duolingo profile. The outfits include a Pikachu onesie, a Chatot suit, a Poké Ball mascot getup, and a Pokémon trainer wardrobe.

Unlike Duolingo’s recent unlockable outfit events, which rely on some random chance, Duo’s Pokémon Party event lets players earn guaranteed costumes by simply logging in and completing a daily lesson. Users have until Dec. 29, 2026, at midnight local time to complete a lesson on nine different days, with the final reward being the Pikachu suit.

Beyond the costumes and other in-game rewards, like gems, Duolingo users can also earn a Timed Research offer code for Pokémon Go after completing seven lessons on separate days. The code lets Pokémon Go players participate in an event where they can “put their new language skills to the test.”

“In this Timed Research, Trainers can practice a new language alongside Professor Willow while completing tasks in Pokémon Go,” a press release reads. “Choose a language, and the research tasks will appear in that language. Plus, keep an eye out for Professor Willow’s hints if you get stuck on a task!” Completing these tasks earns XP, Stardust, and encounters with event-themed Pokémon — Pikachu, Meowth, Chatot, and Lucario — with a special background.

Additionally, the update will bring a new feature called Language Tags, which appear on the summary page of Pokémon from the Timed Research event, showing their names in different languages. While this Duolingo collab seems quite random, Pokémon Go has been running all sorts of crossovers lately, from more predictable ones, like a tie-in with the Pokémon anime, to unexpected team-ups, like with Major League Baseball.
