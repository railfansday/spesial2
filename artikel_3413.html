Travis Scott, Cardi B, and Justice Lead One-Off ‘Grand Theft Auto 6’ Music Festival

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

To ring in the long-awaited release of Grand Theft Auto VI, Rockstar Games and PlayStation have announced a one-day music festival celebrating the musical world of the Florida-inspired GTA installment. Taking place on Nov. 21 at Mana Wynwood in Miami, Travis Scott, Cardi B, Justice, and Metro Boomin top the festival’s stacked lineup.

Scott leads a Cactus Jack showing featuring Don Toliver, Kodak Black, and SoFaygo; similarly, Rauw Alejandro will also helm a guest-laden set (billed as “Rauw & Friends”). Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival also boasts performances from Latin artists Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso, Tainy, and Suero.

Justice, who are behind the video game’s score arriving Dec. 11, lead the charge on a series of DJ sets. South Florida’s Nick León and Queens’ DJ Python will be on the decks, as will England’s Prospa and Skream, and New York City’s The Dare and Fcukers, nodding to Grand Theft Auto VI‘s Florida-influenced setting of Leonida and Miami’s electronic scene.

To attend the festival, prospective attendees must enter a pre-sale lottery via DICE, which closes on Tuesday, Nov. 10 at 4:00 p.m. ET. If chosen, fans have the opportunity on Wednesday, Nov. 11 to buy up to two tickets. For additional information visit the festival’s official website. Grand Theft Auto VI is available to play on Nov. 19 via PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The game’s soundtrack arrives the same day, and features appearances from PinkPantheress, Future, Yung Lean, King Gizzard and the Lizard Wizard, Tame Impala, Fuerza Regida, and many more.

In October, Rockstar Games revealed Lana Del Rey, Bad Bunny, Robyn, and Burna Boy were among the biggest artists who will host the game’s in-universe radio stations.
