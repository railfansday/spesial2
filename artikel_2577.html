Blake Corum celebrates birthday by accomplishing feat not seen in over a decade

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Blake Corum spent his 26th birthday handing out punishment instead of opening presents.

The Los Angeles Rams running back bulldozed his way through the Green Bay Packers for a career-high 184 rushing yards on 32 carries in Wednesday night's 26-13 Thanksgiving Eve victory at SoFi Stadium.

Not only were Corum's 32 carries the most by any NFL running back this season, but they were also the most by a Rams running back in more than a decade.

Todd Gurley was the last Rams running back to eclipse the 30-carry threshold, rushing 30 times for 159 yards on Oct. 11, 2015, ironically against the same Packers franchise.

Aaron Rodgers was Green Bay's starting quarterback in that 24-10 Rams loss.

Blake Corum (24) of the Los Angeles Rams runs the ball against the Green Bay Packers during a game at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, Calif., Nov. 25, 2026. Getty Images

Former Washington Commanders running back, Jamie Morris, still holds the NFL's single-game record with 45 carries in a contest against the Cincinnati Bengals in 1988.

Perhaps the most remarkable part of Corum's accomplishment? He's not even the Rams' starting running back.

That distinction still belongs to Kyren Williams, although the workload suggests the lines between starter and backup are becoming increasingly blurred.

During the Rams' five-game winning streak, Corum has amassed 508 rushing yards on 88 carries compared to Williams' 71 attempts. He's also posted consecutive 100-yard rushing performances for the first time in his NFL career.

Los Angeles Rams running back Blake Corum (24) celebrates his touchdown with head coach Sean McVay during an NFL game in Inglewood, Calif., Nov. 25, 2026. AP Photo/Gregory Bull

"Coach's big thing this week was that the most physical team's going to win, and I think we were that. I know we were that," Corum said.

Behind a patchwork offensive line, Corum chipped away at Green Bay's defense like a lumberjack taking an axe to a tree. Eventually, the tree fell to the ground.

"I did know we were going to wear them out at some point," Corum said. "They were going to get tired, and that's when you started getting those chunk plays."

Los Angeles Rams running back Blake Corum (24) eludes Green Bay Packers cornerback Brandon Cisse (2) on Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026, at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, Calif. Jon SooHoo/UPI/Shutterstock

The Rams have gone from 2-4 to 7-4, rediscovering their offensive identity in the process.

And the guy listed second on the depth chart is increasingly becoming the engine driving it.
