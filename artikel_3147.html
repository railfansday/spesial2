Lauren Ash & Mae Martin Among Cast Of Shozer’s Adult Animation High-School Comedy ‘Dirts’

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

EXCLUSIVE: Lauren Ash (Superstore) and Mae Martin (Wayward) have been cast in Dirts, an ensemble adult animated high-school comedy series for New Metric Media‘s streaming service Shozer.

They will appear alongside the likes of Ash’s Superstore co-star Mark McKinney, Mark Little (Gary and His Demons) and Ryan Belleville (Workin’ Moms).

The show, set to premiere in winter 2027, follows Gus (Little) and Brus (Belleville), two deeply unpopular vegetable teenagers trying to survive life at Leguma Beach High, where high-society vine vegetables mingle with scrappy root transplants. The unlikely friends have to navigate the indignities of adolescence in a world where school politics can get particularly rotten. Ash plays Sproutabella, Martin is Trevin Stems and McKinney Principal Corny. Also in the voice cast are Kyle Dooley (Cupcake & Dino: General Services) as Eggmilio and Butternut; Evany Rosen (Total Dramarama) as Cheryl and Snap Pea; Kayla Lorette (Doomlands) as Pep and Ms. Jicama; Rodrigo Fernandez-Stoll (BlackBerry) as Janitor McKale and Theatre Squash; and Chelsea Clark (Ginny & Georgia) as Gabe.

Dane Clark, Viveno Caldinelli and Mark Montefiore co-created the show, with Caldinelli directing. New Metric Media is the producer in partnership with Little Black Stone Studios. Ariel Yang and Max Wolfond are the producers for New Metric, with Tysen Potter in the same role for Little Black Stone. Production will cater for vertical and horizontal viewing, and you can watch a first-look clip above.

Dirts toplines the latest slate for Shozer, the YouTube comedy channel brand that Shoresy and Letterkenny producer New Metric launched last year.

Also greenlit is The Historical History of Canada, a mockumentary series that takes a twisted and satirical look at the most infamous moments in Canadian history. These will include the mysterious fire that destroyed the Parliament building in 1916 and the Toronto Maple Leafs’ latest Stanley Cup win. New Metric’s Lana Maclin, Alona Metzer and Liv Berkowitz are producing.

Elevator Comedy is a 30-part series of 45-second episodes that will give emerging comedy talent a platform, with performers put in close quarters with the audience. As with Dirts, it’s being produced for vertical and horizontal viewing.

The Eh-List, New Metric’s stand-up comedy strand, is also getting specials from Just For Laughs regular Daniel Tirado, veteran comic Alex Wood and viral sensation Drew Behm.

Shozer debuted last year with a slate of shows and films including Lionsgate’s Running Wilde, Van Wilder: Party Liaison and Zack and Miri Make A Porno, along with Letterkenny‘s first YouTube outing, LetterkennyProblems.

“We’ve spent years building comedy franchises and seeing what happens when audiences connect with great characters and creators,” said Montefiore, CEO of New Metric. “With Shozer, we’re applying that experience to a D2C model where we can discover new talent, invest in original ideas and build audience communities.

“We want Shozer to become a trusted destination for comedy, where people come to discover their next favourite creator or series. And by developing and owning that IP from the outset, we have the flexibility to grow those properties across multiple platforms, formats and consumer experiences.”
