Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Sturm, Ruger & Company, Inc. (RGR -0.80%) reported substantial year-over-year increases in revenue and profitability for the third quarter, supported by a significant expansion in gross and adjusted EBITDA margins. Management attributed the improved financial performance to disciplined cost management, a more favorable product mix, and the successful execution of manufacturing efficiencies. The company is currently implementing its Ruger 2030 strategy, which focuses on modernizing manufacturing operations and leveraging a newly installed enterprise resource planning platform to improve business visibility. Additionally, the company initiated a portfolio rationalization process to reallocate capital and manufacturing resources toward products and markets with the highest demand and growth potential.
