JISOO Brightens the Day With Bubbly ‘Sun Showers’ Single From ‘The Angry Birds Movie 3’ Soundtrack: Stream It Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The animated film hits theaters in December.

JISOO returned on Friday (Nov. 20) to unleash her bubbly new single, “Sun Showers,” which is set to land on the soundtrack for The Angry Birds Movie 3.

The BLACKPINK singer keeps things light and fun for her feel-good pop addition to the Paramount Pictures comedy movie, which will hit theaters on Dec. 23.

“I’m so happy to be part of The Angry Birds Movie 3. I’ve always loved how the films bring together such colorful characters, humor and heart,” JISOO previously told The Hollywood Reporter. “With ‘Sun Showers,’ I wanted to capture the movie’s playful and bright energy through a fun, feel-good song that makes people want to smile and dance along. I really hope everyone will enjoy hearing it when they see the film over the holidays.”

Shuman, JISOO’s dustball-inspired character that she doodled back in middle school, even makes an appearance in the visualizer and is part of the carefree single’s cover art, fitting right in with other animated characters from the movie.

The 31-year-old is closing out 2026 on a high note, and she’ll look to make some major noise next year, as JISOO continues to gear up for the release of her anticipated solo debut album. The K-pop singer and actress is also headed to the desert, where she’s slated to hit the stage at Coachella 2027.

Earlier this year, JISOO released a pair of singles with “Click” and “Addicted,” the latter of which features an assist from Zayn and became JISOO’s first solo Billboard Hot 100 entry, peaking at No. 72 on the chart.
