Champions League quiz: Name all 136 Englishmen to score a goal

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Over 130 English players have scored in the Champions League – and we’re asking you to name them all. Gulp.

Arsenal, Aston Villa, Blackburn Rovers, Chelsea, Leeds United, Leicester City, Liverpool, Manchester City, Manchester United, Newcastle United and Tottenham Hotspur are all represented here.

But some English players have scored goals in the competition for foreign clubs, making this task even harder than it might first appear.

We’d like you to name every Englishman to score in the Champions League and you’ve got 20 minutes to do so. An asterisk denotes that the player was active in the 2026-27 edition of Europe’s premier club tournament.
