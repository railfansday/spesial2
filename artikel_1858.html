How to watch Nations League draw: TV channel and live stream for today

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

England are set to discover their route to the Nations League final this morning, as the quarter-final draw is made in Nyon, Switzerland.

The Three Lions finished atop their group to ensure their status as one of eight teams to have qualified for the knockout rounds of the competition, capping their group-stage campaign with a statement 3-2 defeat of Spain in Madrid.

They saw out that last win in dramatic fashion, clinging on with ten men after Elliot Anderson was sent for an early shower after a cynical foul on Lamine Yamal.

Thomas Tuchel’s men will be joined in the quarter-finals by the world champions, La Roja, as well as the likes of France, Germany, and the Netherlands.

Cristiano Ronaldo-less Portugal, Belgium, and Norway make up the numbers. The quarter-finals will be staged next March, with the final, which may yet be hosted at Wembley Stadium, pencilled in for the summer of 2027.

The Nations League draw for the 2026/27 quarter-finals will take place today, Friday, November 20, following the conclusion of the group phase fixtures.

Coverage of the draw is due to start at 11am GMT, with the draw itself expected to begin shortly afterwards.

TV channel: In the UK, the draw will be televised live UEFA TV.

Live stream: You can also stream the draw for free online via the UEFA YouTube page.

Live blog: You can follow the draw as it happens on the day through Standard Sport’s live blog.
