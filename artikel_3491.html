Disney Plus Reveals Official Cast for Zombie Apocalypse Reboot of Classic Comic

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Say goodbye to Riverdale and hello to Afterlife with Archie

It's been a little over three years since Riverdale, the CW's loose adaptation of the Archie Comics characters, aired its final episode. Now Disney is gearing up to bring audiences back to the town of Riverdale, but it will be in a wildly different way. As announced this July, Disney Plus is set to release an adaptation of the horror comic Afterlife with Archie next year. Now we know who will play Archie, Betty, Veronica, and more.

As reported by Deadline, executive producer Roberto Aguirre-Sacasa has begun casting the series. Aguirre-Sacasa is the chief creative officer of Archie Comics and wrote the original Afterlife with Archie comic, in addition to developing Riverdale. Vasco Faria will play Archie Andrews​​​​​​​, Aliyah Mastin (Paradise) will play Veronica Lodge, Sarah Abbott (The Body) will portray Betty Cooper, Angel Rivera will play Jughead Jones, and Sabrina Spellman will be played by Chloe Avakian.

While it didn't make sense for the cast of Riverdale to return, it will be hard to move on from​​​​​​​ that show's iconic and over-the-top takes on the classic characters. Afterlife with Archie will give the new cast plenty of opportunity to make the characters their own​​​​​​​, though, as the story sees the residents of Riverdale dealing with a zombie apocalypse​​​​​​​ caused by Sabrina casting a spell to revive Jughead's dog. The show is slated to premiere on Halloween 2027.

The original Afterlife with Archie​​​​​​​ comic was written by Aguirre-Sacasa with art by Francesco Francavilla. The comic first released in 2013 and ran for ten issues. The series, however, did not continue beyond those ten issues.​​​​​​​ According to Aguirre-Sacasa, that doesn't mean the comic has been canceled.

In fact,​​​​​​​ when Disney initially announced it had ordered an adaptation of the series, Aguirre-Sacasa revealed he had returned to work on the comic as well. He announced this about a year ago​​​​​​​ and there have been few updates since​​​​​​​. Reportedly,​​​​​​​ issues 11 and 12 have been written and issue 13 was being plotted​​​​​​​,with Francavilla resuming writing duties. Perhaps the comic will return​​​​​​​ ahead of the show's premiere next year.
