5 ‘proven’ strikers West Ham can sign as fresh Scott Parker revelation surfaces

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

There’s already been talk regarding West Ham’s transfer plans heading into January, with Scott Parker requiring reinforcements if he is to turn the tide at the London Stadium.

After taking over from Nuno Espirito Santos at the beginning of November, West Ham are yet to taste victory from Parker’s opening two league games, firstly fighting back from 3-0 down to draw 3-3 with Cardiff City.

And after such a spirited second half comeback, they fell to a 2-1 defeat away at Blackburn Rovers, leaving the East London outfit ninth in the Championship and six points behind league leaders Birmingham City heading into the latest international break.

When domestic action returns, the Hammers have a massive home game against bottom of the table Preston North End, with maximum points needing to be taken to help the club get back on track following such a stuttering run of form.

Of course, with so many games coming thick and fast in this relentless winter period, West Ham’s league position could look a lot different, and for the better it must be said, but it appears Parker is keen to get new players through the door at the turn of the new year regardless of where they are situated in the league standings.

A report by journalist Alan Nixon has detailed that the West Ham boss is on the lookout for an experienced and proven goalscorer who can make an immediate impact in the promotion race, while revealing the club has the money available to sign the right players.

So with this in mind, FLW has listed five forwards that would fit the bill for West Ham at the start of 2027.

The 29-year-old has spent just two seasons of his career in the second tier of English football, firstly in the 2016-17 campaign with Bristol City and then two years later when at Aston Villa, both times on loan from Chelsea.

Over the course of those two campaigns at Ashton Gate and Villa Park, Abraham scored a combined 48 goals, demonstrating his ability to be such a clinical and reliable frontman.

And it’s certainly possible that he could venture down to the Championship for a third term, with his game time at Aston Villa virtually non-existent at the moment.

Abraham has featured just four times in the Premier League so far this term and is clearly third-choice in the pecking order behind Nicolas Jackson and Brian Madjo.

Therefore, a loan move could be on the cards in January, and given Abraham’s excellent record of scoring goals in the Championship, he could be the missing piece to getting West Ham back in the promotion mix.

There will be some doubts whether he can replicate the levels given it’s been eight years on since his stint at Villa Park, but at 29, his career is yet to be over the hill and still has the promise to be a dangerous force at the top end of the pitch for a promotion-chasing side.

Next up is Wrexham striker Kieffer Moore, who has previously worked with Scott Parker at Bournemouth back in 2022.

The Welshman linked up on the South Coast in the January transfer window of that year, and despite missing most of the second half of that season after suffering a broken foot on his debut, scored four goals in as many games, with one of his strikes coming against Nottingham Forest that sealed promotion to the big time.

Moore has spent the majority of his career in the Championship, and has reached double figures for goals in three separate campaigns, with his best return coming in 2020-21 when he netted 20 times for Cardiff City.

The frontman knows the ins and outs of the division, as well as knowing where the back of the net is consistently, and the Hammers could well need a bullish forward to dominate opposition defences and profit from the service provided by the likes of Jarrod Bowen.

Whether Wrexham will be looking to sell Moore to a Championship rival remains to be seen, but Moore could well push for an exit to be involved in a promotion charge, with the Red Dragons struggling to rise up the table at this moment in time under Phil Parkinson.

A third striker that could well be on West Ham’s shortlist is Nottingham Forest frontman Chris Wood.

It’s been quite the fall from grace for the New Zealand international, who just two seasons ago netted 20 goals in a Premier League season to cement himself as one of the biggest surprise packages the division has ever seen.

But since then, injury issues and a lack of form have seen him demoted to being an impact player at the City Ground, with all of Wood’s eight appearances this term under Oliver Glasner so far coming from the substitutes bench.

It’s clear to see he’s not favoured by the Frenchman after Forest’s summer purchase of Liam Delap, and at 34, Wood will still want to play regularly in the latter stages of his career, so an exit in January could well happen.

While West Ham supporters are likely to turn their nose up at the prospect of signing a 34-year-old forward who’s on the decline, there’s every chance Wood can get back to his best when given regular opportunities, not to mention he’s also had a pedigree of scoring bundles of goals at Championship level, notably scoring 27 times for Leeds United in the 2016-17 season.

At 34, Wood may have to be rotated through a hectic Championship schedule, but there’s no doubt he has been a certified marksman in the top two levels at most clubs he’s been at.

And with Wood needing to turn the recent dip in his career around, a short-term move to the Hammers for the second half of the season is something that could benefit both parties.

The penultimate name for West Ham to look at signing is Chuba Akpom, who is also struggling for minutes in the Premier League at Ipswich Town.

The Tractor Boys were obligated to make Akpom’s loan move from Dutch giants Ajax a permanent one this summer, with the 30-year-old triggering an appearance-based clause after featuring 29 times in the Championship, scoring twice.

But after an originally underwhelming loan period anyway, Akpom was a signing that wasn’t welcomed by the majority of the Ipswich faithful, and that has been shown through his lack of game time in the top-flight.

Similarly to Abraham, all of Akpom’s appearances at Portman Road have been off the bench, with single-minute cameos made in each of his last three league games.

Once again, there’s a distinct possibility that he could be available to depart from Suffolk in January, and while he did have a poor last term in the Championship, his past displays at Middlesbrough could see him interest the likes of West Ham.

Back in the 2022-23 season on Teesside, Akpom claimed the Championship golden boot after scoring 28 times in 38 games, proving that in the right system and environment, he can be a deverstating presence when leading a line.

And while his form has dipped in recent times, he is another forward who, given the chances to shine, could begin to replicate those numbers and performances at the London Stadium.

A deal with Ipswich is definitely something to consider from a West Ham point of view, with Akpom showing he can be so sharp in front of goal when having the trust placed in his game.

This is definitely the most ambitious target of the lot when it comes to a January arrival, but there’s no reason as to why West Ham wouldn’t be able to make a statement signing of a player like Aleksandar Mitrovic.

Few strikers have ever owned the Championship quite like Mitrovic did in 2021-22, with Fulham, often defined as a cheat-code at this level.

The Serbian scored 43 league goals as Fulham stormed to the second tier crown, setting a record for a Championship season and leaving defences across the division wondering how to stop him.

If a chance came his way, it usually ended up in the back of the net, and that’s certainly a striker West Ham need to help get them back on track.

Ever since leaving Fulham, Mitrovic has spent the last three years of his career in Saudi Arabia and most recently Qatar, linking up with Al Rayyann in the summer of 2025.

But a move back to London with West Ham and a route into English football could be an appealing one, with Mitrovic having a great opportunity to get back featuring in the Premier League if he was able to wreak more havoc in the Championship.

When it comes to the financial pulling power, West Ham are one of very few clubs who could pull this signing off, and while a deal like this is sure to be laughed at by many avid Championship followers, it’s a target that shows genuine ambition that the club wants to get back to being a Premier League club as quickly as possible.

The Hammers must look into the prospect of making a deal for Mitrovic as soon as possible, with the possession of the most dangerous forward in Championship history boosting their promotion chances a considerable amount.
