‘He’s key’: 29-year-old star can fire QPR to promotion, says Andy Hinchcliffe

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

QPR won 1-0 away at Millwall last time out to boost their Championship promotion hopes. Nicolas Madsen got their winner against the Lions at The Den in the first-half as the London outfit secured a vital three points.

The R’s are in the top eight after their first 16 fixtures of the season and they are doing well under the guidance of head coach Julien Stephan. They are in the play-offs along with Wolves, Birmingham City, West Brom, Southampton and Swansea City, with the likes of Sheffield United and West Ham chasing them down.

Hinchcliffe, who used to play for Manchester City, Everton and Sheffield Wednesday, has delivered his verdict on Chair, as per Sky Sports: “Getting Ilias Chair back into the team regularly is important. He’s one of those players who can affect games in the Championship. This has to be a big season for Chair. He undoubtedly has the ability, but at times he’s coasted along and hasn’t become the dominant force Rangers need him to be.

“If they want to improve going forward and finish in the top eight, he’s key. He’s got the movement, intelligence and creative quality, and he can score goals. It’s not just about what he can do himself, but what he can do for the players around him. Looking at his performance against Millwall and what he could bring for the rest of the season, I think he’s key to improving their attack. It’s down to him to drive that improvement.”

As Hinchcliffe has alluded to, Chair can be a top player at second tier level on his day. At the age of 29, he is now experienced and has played over 300 games in his career to date.

The ex-Morocco international, who made 12 caps, has been a good servant to the Hoops. He was snapped up by them back in 2017 following spells in Belgium at Club Brugge and Lierse as a teenager.

Chair, who was born in Antwerp, can play down the middle behind the striker or off the wing if needed. This versatility comes in handy and keeps opponents guessing.

He has made 10 outings for the R’s in this campaign to date, nine of which have come in the league. He has chipped in with two goals and three assists and will be eager to boost his numbers further over the coming weeks and months.

QPR star Chair is always the subject of transfer speculation during windows. He was reportedly on the radar of Wrexham last summer but nothing materialised in the end.

The Hoops will be pleased that he stayed put beyond the last deadline in September. He is starting to stamp his mark for Stephan’s side now and looked good against Millwall last Saturday.

Next up for the R’s is a tricky away trip to Carrow Road on Tuesday evening to lock horns with Norwich City. Their upcoming opponents have been in decent form under Philippe Clement and they will be in confident mood so QPR will need to be at their best to get something from the game.
