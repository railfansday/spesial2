At Arcadia University in Pennsylvania, Undergraduate Education Programs Are Plentiful

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Arcadia's School of Education offers a variety of undergraduate education programs, including in-person, online, accelerated, and flexible pathways for students to get their degree and teaching certification.

GLENSIDE, Pa., Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Arcadia University, a top-ranked, private university in Greater Philadelphia with a long history of graduating teachers with high career outcomes, is proud to offer multiple pathways for individuals to earn a bachelor's degree in Education and become a teacher.

"The need for highly skilled, compassionate, and innovative educators has never been greater," said Dr. Peggy Hickman, chair of the School of Education. "As schools face a critical teacher shortage, Arcadia University is committed to preparing the next generation of teachers to meet this challenge and make a lasting impact on students, schools, and communities."

Roughly half of Pennsylvania's local education agencies reported vacancies in the 2024-25 school year totaling more than 3,300, according to the Pennsylvania Department of Education. Moreover, high attrition rates (up to 9% for teachers with five or fewer years of experience) further demonstrate the dire need for new teachers.

Arcadia's strong partnerships with local schools and organizations, including the Philadelphia School District, mean students have a variety of fieldwork and student teaching opportunities; these experiences often lead to full-time employment post-graduation. A full 100 percent of School of Education graduates from the undergraduate Class of 2025 were employed or continuing their education within nine months of graduating.1 Arcadia Education alumni in the Philadelphia School District and beyond are also recipients of several prestigious education awards, including the Lindback Award for Distinguished Teaching, the Lindback Award for Principal Leadership, and the Presidential Award for Excellence in Mathematics and Science Teaching.

"What sets Arcadia University apart is its commitment, in partnership with Lindley Academy Charter School, to preparing teachers who fully understand and embrace the realities and rewards of education in the city," said Dr. Margery Covello, CEO of Lindley Academy Charter School in Philadelphia, which employs Arcadia Education students and graduates. "We are grateful for this partnership and proud that it has brought exceptional Arcadia University graduates onto our faculty."

To learn more about Arcadia's School of Education programs for undergraduate as well as graduate studies, visit arcadia.edu/school-of-education.
