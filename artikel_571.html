Anurag Kashyap, Ranjan Singh Back Haryana-Rooted Short ‘The Melody of Destiny’ as Executive Producers (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Indian auteur Anurag Kashyap (“Gangs of Wasseypur”) and producer Ranjan Singh have boarded “The Melody of Destiny,” a short film rooted in the northern Indian state of Haryana, as presenters and executive producers, backing the film on the festival circuit.

The short is the first film from Chaitanya Chugh, a 24-year-old self-taught director from Pehowa, Haryana. He produced it with Diya Panjwani and Aabhar S Dev. Saurabh Bansal plays Rajesh, who lives in Delhi, far from his wife and daughter in Haryana, and sings on the city’s streets in secret. He is saving for the day his daughter Priya (Miraya Sehgal), 10, gets married. She lives at home with her mother, Savitri (Nagmeet Arora). One evening, a conversation over dinner turns into a fight, and the family’s strained relationships come out into the open.

Chugh shot the film guerrilla-style on Delhi’s streets. The idea came from two moments in his own life after the COVID-19 lockdown. In 2021, his mother told him she had given a saree from her wedding to a street performer who was collecting money for his daughter’s marriage. Months later, on the way to the mountains, Chugh saw a roadside banner offering 51 wedding items for INR 51,000 ($530), and the two memories came together.

“Initially drawn from the stark reality of the importance of marriages in India, which is maligned by the illegal practice of dowry, it grew into an exploration of gendered roles and power, and how they shape family dynamics, especially in traditional setups. By looking into a household whose relationships are governed by abuse rooted in the problems of class and patriarchy, the film tries to present a documentation of these gruesome truths,” Chugh said.

Most of the crew was female. The film also draws on Haryanvi folklore, which traditionally tells stories through metaphor in song.

“The film really punches you hard at one point,” Kashyap said. “Chaitanya really knows the world that he has captured intimately in the film. It’s powerful, it’s sensitive and carries some really authentic performances. I hope the film travels far and wide.”

Singh said it is rare to see a young filmmaker try to push boundaries with a first film, adding that Chugh “does that convincingly in ‘The Melody of Destiny.’ He definitely has a unique storytelling approach and an emotional outlook which is a must for this story. I saw the film through one of the producers Diya, and strongly felt it’s a film that should be taken to the world, and thus happy to onboard the film and try and do the same.”
