Russell posts statement to 'speak from the heart'

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Mercedes' George Russell took to social media on Monday after not speaking to the media after Sunday's Mexico City GP, where his team-mate Kimi Antonelli became world champion; watch Friday's practice sessions for the Sao Paulo GP at 3.30pm and 7pm, live on Sky Sports F1

George Russell has posted a statement on social media to "speak from the heart" after his Mercedes team-mate Kimi Antonelli secured the F1 Drivers' Championship on Sunday.

Mercedes boss Toto Wolff revealed Russell was "distraught" in Mexico and went home after congratulating Antonelli on becoming world champion.

The FIA gave Mercedes a €5,000 (£4,173) fine as Russell missed his mandatory media duties post-race, but the British driver has made his own first public comments on Monday.

"Now feels like a pretty suitable moment to speak from the heart," he wrote on Instagram.

"This season started off exactly how I had hoped but it very quickly unravelled with tough luck and poor performances.

"It's been the first proper season of my career where I've had more bad days than good. Has it been tough to deal with? Yes, very. Has it been tough to pick myself up week in, week out? Yes, very. Confusion at times? Often. All I know is that I've given it my all and 2026 was not meant to be my year."

Russell began the season as the clear title favourite but has been comprehensively outperformed by Antonelli, who became F1's youngest ever world champion at the age of 20, with four rounds remaining.

Antonelli has won nine races compared to Russell's three and the latter has dropped to fourth in the Drivers' Championship after a power unit issue left him with sub-optimal power on the opening lap of the Mexico City Grand Prix.

Russell continued: "I want to sincerely congratulate Kimi on what has been an absolutely incredible season. You truly deserve the Championship.

"Thank you to the team for producing the best car and power unit this season; it's been inspiring to see everyone coming together over four tough years to bring Mercedes back to the top.

"Now, all I can promise is that for the rest of this season, and into the next one, I will be giving my absolute all to rediscover the performance I have been capable of producing for the majority of my career and fingers crossed a little bit of luck returns.
