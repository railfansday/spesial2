Temwa Chawinga should be the NWSL Most Valuable Player.

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Malawian phenom didn’t play as much this year, but she was just as impactful as the best players who logged double her minutes total.

As The Athletic’s Tamerra Griffin recently noted, the field for the National Women’s Soccer League Most Valuable Player award is unusually wide.

The five finalists, announced on Monday, all have different levels of experience in this arena. Reigning MVP Temwa Chawinga could be the league’s first-ever three-time winner and three-peat MVP. Sophia Wilson is looking to be only the third player to nab the award twice. Golden Boot winner Ashley Sanchez, plus Trinity Rodman and Janine Sonis, are striving for their first-ever trophy.

Now, this largely comes down to the North Carolina Courage’s inability to make the playoffs. Had that happened, Sanchez would be a shoe-in for the honor. She tied Chawinga’s single-season scoring record, and was the engine that made the Courage’s attack run from wire to wire.

Much of the voting public seems to believe (mistakenly) that an MVP can only come from a playoff team, so that may count Sanchez out in their minds. Setting that aside, let us consider the finalists, and how much value they actually produced for their teams throughout the 2026 regular season.
