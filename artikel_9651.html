Two batches of 73 rows. Why did I get 133 candles?

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The 17:00 candle turned up twice. So did the one at 17:05. I was joining two six-hour slices of the same HYPEUSDT market-data file, and both slices included their endpoints.

The first slice starts at noon and ends at 18:00 Hong Kong time. The second starts at 17:00 and ends at 23:00. There are twelve five-minute steps from 17:00 to 18:00, but thirteen timestamps if you count both ends. That accounts for all 13 extra rows when you append the batches.
