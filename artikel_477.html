ETFs: High Conviction Through Novel Strategies

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Inkoo Kang, Executive Director of Tactical Products at ProShares, Ryan Lee, SVP of Product & Strategy at Direxion ETFs, and Corgi's Chief Investment Strategist Jeff Weniger join Bloomberg Intelligence's Athanasios Psarofagis at the Bloomberg ETFs in Depth conference in New York on November 19, 2026. The panel examines the rise of increasingly novel ETFs, from leveraged products to private-market and synthetic exposures, and where the limits of the ETF wrapper may lie. They also discuss investor sophistication, the challenge of turning new products into commercial successes and why education becomes increasingly important as strategies grow more complex. (Source: Bloomberg)
