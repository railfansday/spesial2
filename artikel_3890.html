ICYMI: Here's Another Look At The Zelda: Ocarina Of Time amiibo For Switch 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

In September, Nintendo revealed it would be releasing two new amiibo in 2027 based on characters from the Zelda: Ocarina of Time Switch 2 remake.

This includes Young Link (and Navi) and Young Zelda. Some photos of these amiibo at conventions are now appearing online. If you haven't already seen them, here's another look via the amiibo subreddit:

These same amiibo have also been spotted at other game events recently, with more photos of them surfacing on social media. GameSpot shared a closer look at these amiibo last month, alongside the limited-edition Switch 2 and Pro Controller.

The exact release date for these amiibo hasn't been confirmed just yet, so when we hear more, we'll let you know. As for the price, they'll set you back $24.99 USD (or your regional equivalent). You'll be able to unlock some special content with them in the remake. This includes masks based on Link and Zelda's original appearances in Ocarina of Time on the N64.

You can find out more about amiibo and what exactly they do in Ocarina of Time in our guides here on Nintendo Life. Nintendo also recently released the final Kirby Air Riders amiibo.
