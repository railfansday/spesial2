U.S. new home sales jumped to a 10-month high in October, beating forecasts

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Sales rose 14.2% to a 701,000 annual rate while the median price fell 3.2% from a year earlier and supply shrank to 8.0 months

The Census Bureau and the Department of Housing and Urban Development reported on Wednesday that sales of new single-family houses rose 14.2% in October to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 701,000. September's rate was 614,000.

Builders offered lower prices to entice shoppers, and October's pace was the strongest since December 2025, according to Reuters.

Economists surveyed by Reuters had expected sales to slow to a pace of 650,000 from September's initially reported 676,000. October sales were 7.5% above the 652,000 rate of October 2025.

The median sales price of new houses sold in October was $391,300, down 0.3% from $392,600 in September and down 3.2% from $404,200 a year earlier, the Census Bureau said. The average sales price was $459,900, down 12.1% from $523,400 in October 2025.

The estimated inventory of new houses for sale at the end of October was 470,000, down 1.5% from September, the Census Bureau said. That equals a supply of 8.0 months at the current sales rate, compared with 9.3 months in September.

A National Association of Home Builders survey found that 46% of builders lowered prices in November, versus roughly 43% in October, according to Reuters. The typical rate for a 30-year fixed home loan stood at 7.28% last week.

By region, sales in the South climbed by upwards of 36%, reaching a peak not seen in over five years, according to Advisor Perspectives. Sales in the Northeast and West slid to their weakest point in over 10 years, while Midwest sales also fell. One in four homes sold in October carried a price tag below $300,000, the largest proportion since 2021.

The Census Bureau said the 14.2% monthly change carries a margin of error of 22.6%.

The National Association of Realtors reported a 5.5% drop in existing-home sales for October on Nov. 12, bringing sales to a 3.75 million annual rate, the lowest level since August 2010. The group said the average 30-year fixed mortgage rate was 7.33% in October, up from 6.86% in September.
