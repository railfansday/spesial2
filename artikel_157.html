If our sub-$30 Black Friday roundup has you hungry for more great gadgets at low prices, you can currently grab the Nothing CMF Buds 2A in black starting at $17.89 on Amazon for Black Friday, which is about $11 less than the MSRP and the lowest price we’ve seen. The wireless earbuds are discounted in all three colors; however, the light gray and orange sets are slightly more expensive at $19.99 ($9 off).

The CMF Buds 2A deliver decent sound and noise cancellation. They also offer useful extras like customizable EQ settings, multipoint connectivity, and IP54 water and dust resistance.

While they may not have the same premium features or sound as flagship budget buds like the AirPods 5 and Google’s Pixel Buds 2A, it’s hard to argue with the value. For just $20, you get active noise cancellation, support for Google Fast Pair, and up to eight hours of battery life before you need to toss them into the included USB-C charging case. My colleague Brandon Widder keeps some in his backpack as a backup pair of earbuds, and their IP54 water and dust resistance rating makes a great choice for light exercise and yard work.
