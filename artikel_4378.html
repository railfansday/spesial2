Heroes of Crown tier list and reroll guide

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

If you're eager to check out all the best characters in Heroes of Crown, then look no further because our Heroes of Crown tier list will give you all the information you need to pick the best and form a powerful team to carry you through the game.

Heroes of Crown is a beautifully styled 3D idle RPG where you make a team before sending them out to farm and take down enemies, upgrade them, and eventually fight mighty opponents that drop amazing loot. It has lovely visuals that really add to the experience!

We are going to focus on giving you everything you need to know about the best heroes in Heroes of Crown, including which ones you should be adding to your team. We have ranked them starting with the S tier at the very top, which contains some of the best characters you could pick, all the way down to the D tier, which contains the weakest heroes, who probably want to avoid.

So without further ado, let's dive into our Heroes of Crown tier list! Also, feel free to use the links below to check out a specific tier!  Tiers SS & S| Tiers A,B & C | Reroll guide

As for the other heroes, it's pretty simple. They are all parts of either Nimbus or Heyday teams. Even if in isolation, some other heroes in lower tiers are better, it doesn't matter because that's not how this game works. It's all about having the best team. And currently, Nimbus is the one dominating the meta with Heyday being second, thanks to Heyday - Ancient Empress - Wu Zetian.

Some of them are better than others when it comes to Endless Abyss solo mode, but it's not a good enough reason to rank them higher or lower solely based on that alone.

As I've explained already, Heroes of Crown: Legends is all about the best teams. Heroes in A-tier can be part of your third team. It doesn't really matter which one you want to go for. God Heir, Apocalypse and Heaven are all good enough to help you progress past level 1701 in Dragonspine Tower. Usually, especially if you are a beginner or F2P, God Heir will be your third team because the beginner summons give you access to these heroes.

The Chaos heroes are good individually, but they have no team. Also, in no way, shape or form are they worth the investment needed. So, avoid them.

B-tier heroes in our Heroes of Crown: Legends tier list are of the core elements. These heroes will be usable in the very early stages of the game, and later on, they can be used for your Starburst Resonance. Obviously, you will use them in Elemental Tower as well. Also, if you don't have a solid third team, you can use them for that as well. Other than that, they have no real use.

C-tier heroes are food. That's their use. You can use some of them on your Starburst Resonance, also. That's all they are good for, really. Ah, and also for Elemental Tower.

If you're eager to reroll in Heroes of Crown, then all you need to do is follow the step-by-step process we've detailed below:

Make sure you log into the game using a Guest account. This will make rerolling a lot smoother since you won't have the account bound to your email address or social media accounts.

Play through the tutorial and do the free summons. Afterwards, claim all the free rewards and proceed to redeem all the codes for free Diamonds. The more Diamonds you get, the more times you'll be able to summon. You can summon with these, or you can keep playing with your current characters up to stage 2-2, where you will get an additional 10x summon tickets.

If the characters you received are not what you wanted, then all you need to do is, on your device, open Settings -> Apps -> find Heroes of Crown and Clear Data and Clear Cache. This will essentially reset the game, and you can start over!
