Falcons/Vikings Coverage Map: Wide Broadcast Area for Kirk Cousins Return

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

This Sunday's game at U.S. Bank Stadium between the Atlanta Falcons and the Minnesota Vikings will feature Kirk Cousins' return to the Twin Cities as the Vikings' quarterback to take on the team he left Minnesota for before the 2024 season. It's one of the more interesting storylines of Week 12 of this NFL season, and a pretty significant chunk of the country will be able to see it on over-the-air television.

The folks from 506 Sports have released their broadcast maps for this Week 12, and the FOX map for this week is below. The Falcons/Vikings game is represented by the red area.

As you can see, the portion of the country that will get Falcons/Vikings is pretty large, encompassing most of the western and northern United States, as well as a decent portion of the south. This will also be the game being shown to viewers in Alaska and Hawai'i. The other two games in the early window on FOX are the New York Giants at the Indianapolis Colts (represented by the blue area) and the Tennessee Titans at the Jacksonville Jaguars (represented by the green area).

Because of all the other games that have been interspersed throughout this Thanksgiving week, there will only be six games in the Sunday early window between FOX and CBS (and only three in the late window, so pray for Scott Hanson's sanity, Red Zone viewers). That could also play a role in why the viewing area for Falcons/Vikings is as wide as it is.

There's your broadcast coverage map for Week 12, folks. Hopefully there are quite a few of you who are in the red area on the map for this week's game.
