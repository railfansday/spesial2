Maple Leafs’ Auston Matthews shares how his health has improved in 2026-27 NHL season

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Toronto Maple Leafs captain Auston Matthews has said that he is playing at his best physical level in several years. The center discussed his improved health ahead of Saturday night's matchup against the Montreal Canadiens.

Matthews credited a productive NHL offseason and the extended recovery period following his March 2026 knee injury. He suffered a Grade 3 MCL tear that ended his previous season.

Asked whether this was his best physical condition in three or four years, Matthews agreed. He explained how the recovery period helped him address issues that had affected his performance.

"I mean, I think just a good summer. You know, some change, obviously, but a good summer," Matthews told reporters. "Obviously, a pretty long runway from the time I got hurt in March to September to put in a lot of good work and smooth out some stuff that I wanted to."

The Maple Leafs captain also acknowledged the physical demands of an NHL season. However, he thinks he has returned to feeling like himself.

"For myself, just wanting to come in and feel like myself again, and I feel like I've done that," Matthews said. "Just want to continue, yeah, keep that going."

The improvement has also affected Matthews mentally. He pointed to the atmosphere inside Toronto's dressing room as another important factor.

"Yeah, it does," Matthews said when asked about the mental impact of feeling healthier.

He added that the team's energy makes daily work enjoyable.

"I think you try to separate hockey from just your everyday life and kind of approach that the same every da," Matthews said. "But I just think the environment that we have here, the guys in the room, and just the energy that you feel in the locker room, in the rink every day, it's been a lot of fun.

"So, I mean, I think that's half the battle."

Matthews' comments offer encouragement for Toronto after his difficult previous campaign. So far this season, Matthews has 11 goals and 26 points in 17 games, and his health remains important to the team's offensive plans and playoff ambitions.

The Leafs need their captain available and productive throughout the season. Matthews now wants to maintain his physical condition as the schedule becomes more demanding.
