Arsenal star was subbed off as a precaution against Slavia

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Ezri Konsa has been included in the England squad for the November international break, easing concerns about his fitness after he appeared to struggle during Arsenal's match against Slavia Praha in midweek.

The defender seemed to limp off during the European fixture, raising questions about whether he would be available for the Gunners' upcoming matches. His fitness is particularly important to Arsenal as they continue their efforts to retain the Premier League title, with Konsa having become an important member of the team.

Konsa played 62 minutes against Slavia Praha before being substituted, but his inclusion in the latest England squad suggests that his condition is not as serious as some supporters feared. Mikel Arteta has also provided a reassuring update ahead of the match against Hull City.

The Arsenal manager is preparing to use the former Aston Villa defender against Hull, with the latest information suggesting that he is fit enough to return to action. Arteta explained that the decision to substitute him in midweek was precautionary rather than a response to a significant injury.

Speaking via Arsenal Media, the Spaniard confirmed that Konsa had felt better in the days following the European match and was making good progress.

"He's fine – he was feeling much better today. We took him out on Wednesday for a precaution, but yesterday and today he's been feeling good."

The update will be encouraging for Arsenal supporters, particularly with the international break approaching. England will also be pleased to have Konsa available for their upcoming fixtures, provided he continues to respond well.

With William Saliba back in contention, Arsenal have more defensive options available and should be better equipped to cope if any fitness concerns emerge. Nevertheless, Konsa remains an important player for Arteta, and the manager will want him available throughout the demanding period ahead.

His experience and performances have made him a valuable member of the squad, so avoiding further injury problems will be a priority. Arsenal will hope he can feature against Hull before joining up with England.

Saliba's return also raises the question of when he will next get an opportunity to play for the Gunners. Having more defensive options available should give Arteta greater flexibility as Arsenal continue their title challenge.
