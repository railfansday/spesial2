Please welcome Braden Mann to the wonderful world of Roob's Eagles Stats

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Please welcome Braden Mann to the wonderful world of Roob's Eagles Stats originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

We ran into Sean Landeta at Eagles training camp this summer and Landeta, who punted in the USFL for three years and the NFL for 21, couldn't stop raving about Braden Mann.

"I'm such a fan of punters and when he was at Texas A&M I would always check their box scores the day after they played and see these crazy punting numbers," said Landeta, who punted for the Eagles from 1999 through 2002 and again in 2005. "It's great to see how he's carried that over into the NFL. The ball just explodes off his foot and keeps going. He's phenomenal."

Let's welcome Mann to the world of Roob's Eagles Stats, and he makes his debut at the top of this week's stats column.

THE WORLD'S GREATEST BRADEN MANN STATS: Braden Mann averaged 59.3 yards on four punts vs. the Steelers, the highest single-game average ever by an Eagles punter on four or more attempts. The previous high was Mat McBriar's 56.6 on five punts in a game against the Lions at the Linc in 2012. Mann now holds four of the top-six punting games in Eagles history, with Briar and Cameron Johnston – who kicked for the Steelers Sunday – responsible for the other two. Mann has had 29 games since joining the Eagles in 2023 at 50 yards per punt or more, more than twice as many as any other Eagle. Johnston is second with 14. Mann is tied for most 50-yard games by any NFL kicker since 2023 with A.J. Cole of the Raiders, who also has 29. Mann's 49.2 average since joining the Eagles is highest in franchise history, smashing the previous record of 47.0 set by Johnston from 2018 through 2020. McBriar (46.5) and Donnie Jones (45.4) are also over 45.0. Mann also added two more punts inside the 20 Sunday with no touchbacks. So far this year he has 24 inside the 20 and two touchbacks. Mann had a 70-yard punt Sunday, a game after booting an 86-yarder against the Giants. According to a Stathead custom search, he's the first Eagles punter as far back as records go – which is 1977 – with a punt of at least 70 yards in consecutive games and the 11th NFL punter to do that since 1977. And since the Eagles didn't have a punt of 70 yards between 1963 and 1976, he's the first with a 70-yarder in back-to-back games in at least 64 years and most likely ever.

GOEDERT JOINS 5,000-YARD CLUB: He only had one catch, but it was for 38 yards and it increased Dallas Goedert's career total to 5,032, making him the 45th tight end in NFL history with 5,000 yards. He's actually 44th on that list, and the next three tight ends all played for the Eagles: Jimmie Giles (5,084), Mickey Shuler (5,100) and Charle Young (5,106). Goedert is only 435 yards out of the top 30 all-time. He also needs just 13 yards to pass A.J. Brown and move into the all-time Eagles top-10 in receiving yards. DeVonta Smith's 41 yards gave him 5,370 and moved him past Bobby Walston (5,363 yards) and into seventh place in Eagles history. He's only 30 yards behind Hall of Famer Pete Pihos (5,400) for sixth place.

FOUR IN A ROW FOR NOLAN: Nolan Smith now has a sack in four straight games, tied with George Karlaftis III of the Chiefs for the longest current streak in the NFL. It's the longest streak by an Eagle since Haason Reddick had a sack in five straight games in 2022. Smith has six sacks in his last eight games after recording just three in his previous 14 games.

OLDEST QB EVER: Aaron Rodgers, at 42 years, 355 days, is the oldest quarterback the Eagles have ever beaten. The previous oldest was Vinny Testaverde, who was 41 years, 36 days, when the Eagles beat Testaverde and the Cowboys 12-7 at the Linc in Week 15 of 2004. The Eagles beat Testaverde twice that year. The only other quarterback the Eagles have beaten after he turned 40 is Tom Brady in Super Bowl LII in Minneapolis.

AFTER JUST 45 GAMES, SAQUON CAUGHT SHADY: Saquon Barkley's 38-yard touchdown run was his 11th as an Eagle of at least 30 yards including postseason, tying LeSean McCoy for most in franchise history. Steve Van Buren (9), Wilbert Montgomery (7) and Brian Westbrook (7) are next on that list. Including seven TD runs of at least 30 yards with the Giants, Barkley now has 18 in his career, 4th-most in NFL history behind Barry Sanders (24), Adrian Peterson (23) and Derrick Henry (20).

LET'S GET BOBBY HOYING INVOLVED: With 169 passing yards, 52.9 completion percentage and no touchdown passes against the Giants and 141 yards, 52.4 percent and no TD passes against the Steelers, Joe Milton is the first Eagles quarterback with back-to-back starts under 170 yards and below 53 percent with no TD passes since Bobby Hoying vs. Washington (110, 48.4%) and the Giants (121, 50%) in November 1998.

TOUGH WAY TO WIN: The Eagles beat Washington on opening day despite just 10 first downs and they beat the Steelers despite just 11. This is the first time in 81 years they've won two games in a season in which they had 11 or fewer first downs. In 1945, they beat the Chicago Cardinals 21-6 at Shibe Park with nine first downs in Week 3 and the Cleveland Rams 28-14 also at Shibe Park with just six first downs in Week 6.

FIRST TIME SINCE 1936: The Eagles have just five takeaways, their fewest through 10 games in 90 years, since they only had four after 10 games in 1936 (and I question those 1936 stats because turnovers were so prevalent in the early years of the NFL. That's probably wrong). The Eagles are only the sixth team since 1940 with five or fewer takeaways through 10 games, along with the 2018 49ers, 2020 Texans, 2021 Jaguars and 2024 Raiders, who all also had five, and the legendary 2025 Jets, who had just one. The Eagles have now gone five straight games without an interception, the 3rd-longest streak in franchise history behind an eight-game streak in 2012 and a six-game streak in 1976.

BY LAND OR BY AIR: The Eagles don't have a 100-yard rusher or 100-yard receiver in their last eight games, the longest streak of games in 37 years without a 100-yard rusher or receiver. In 1989, Cris Carter had 113 receiving yards in a loss to the Bears in Chicago in Week 4 and Keith Byars had 109 yards in Week 15 at New Orleans, a 10-week span without a 100-yard receiver. Meanwhile, the Eagles didn't have a single 100-yard rusher from late in 1987 through early 1990. This year, DeVonta Smith had 117 yards in Week 2 at Tennessee and hasn't since. The Eagles' last 100-yard rusher was Saquon Barkley, who had 106 yards in the playoff loss to the 49ers.

WORST SINCE 1999: Over the last seven games, the Eagles have averaged 3.89 yards per play, their lowest in any seven-game stretch since the first seven games of 1999, when they averaged 3.65. This is the first time the Eagles have averaged below 3.9 yards per play and allowed over 5.2 yards per play in a seven-game stretch since October and November of 1976. The Eagles are last in the NFL in yards per play at 4.29.

46 POINTS AFTER HALFTIME: Despite exploding for two 2nd-half field goals Sunday, the Eagles have still scored an NFL-worst 46 points after halftime this year, their fewest after 10 games since 1972, when they scored just 26, and 2nd-fewest since 1941. It's also the fewest by any NFL team through 10 games since the 2019 Dolphins had just 37 and fewest in the NFL this year by 18 points. The Eagles have been outscored by 93 points in the second half this year, 139-46. That's their worst 2nd-half scoring margin through 10 games since 1972, when they were outscored by 97. The 92 points they've allowed in the fourth quarter are 2nd-most in Eagles history and most since 1973, when they allowed 94. And their 4th-quarter scoring margin of 62 points is their largest since 1941, when they were outscored by 73 points in the fourth quarter after 10 games. (And thanks to Stathead for finally adding quarter and half scoring margin functions to the Span Finder, making these stats possible!)

OUTGAINED YET AGAIN: The Eagles have been outgained by at least 20 yards in eight straight games, the 2nd-longest streak in franchise history and longest since a 10-game streak in 1971. They've also been outgained in passing yards in eight straight games, the 3rd-longest streak in franchise history and longest since 1947. They're averaging just 237 yards in their last eight games, their worst over any eight-game stretch in 27 years, since the 1999 team averaged 231 in an eight-game stretch over mid-September through early November. The Eagles have been outgained by 880 yards, the largest margin through 10 games since 1999 and 3rd-largest since 1937.
