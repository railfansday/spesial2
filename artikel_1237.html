Ben Jacobs: Man Utd Ready to Sell Marcus Rashford for 'Right Price'

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Manchester United are ready to back new manager Mauricio Pochettino in the January transfer window, but they are also open to selling some of their current players too, according to GIVEMESPORT senior correspondent Ben Jacobs.

Pochettino was given a rude awakening in his first match in charge of Man United as they were thrashed 4-0 by Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday, with the game turned on its head in the eighth minute when Leny Yoro was given a red card for bringing Alexander Isak down just outside the penalty area as he headed towards the goal.

However, it was confirmed on Tuesday that the dismissal has been overturned, and the young French defender will no longer serve a ban.

That doesn't help the Red Devils from what happened on the weekend, but it does at least ensure that Pochettino can select Yoro in the next game if he wants to.

Having now conceded more goals than any other team in the top five European leagues, it's clear where one of United's biggest issues is, and it was a nightmare first Premier League start in almost a year for Matthijs de Ligt.

Diogo Dalot and Lisandro Martinez also struggled on Merseyside, and GMS revealed previously that strengthening the defence in the New Year is one of the club's biggest priorities.

Club chiefs could look to sign a hybrid defender who can play as a centre-back and a left-back, with Fulham's Calvin Bassey a key target, while they could look at a right-back too, with Jacobs understanding that Chelsea's Malo Gusto could be 'one to watch'.

Their other transfer priority is a new striker in January, with the likes of Folarin Balogun and Ivan Toney mentioned alongside the Red Devils, with Pochettino knowing the former from his time managing the United States national team.

It could be one forward in and at least one forward out too, with a potential exit for Marcus Rashford still possible despite his reintegration into the squad at the start of this season.

Jacobs explained on the latest episode of GMS' Market Madness podcast:

Rashford as well I still think is available at the right price, even though Man United have pitched it as being a reintegration into the squad.

Given it was well-publicised that United were willing to sell the England international in the summer, it's no surprise that the club would still be ready to offload him and his big salary.

It was expected that the 29-year-old would go in the summer following his successful loan spell with Barcelona, but the LaLiga champions decided to sign Anthony Gordon from Newcastle United instead.

With no other attractive offers on the table, Michael Carrick decided to bring Rashford back into the fold, and he started four of United's opening five Premier League matches this term.

The forward was then benched in one of Carrick's final games at Leeds United, before suffering a hip injury that has kept him on the sidelines since.

Rashford failed to make much of an impact in the final third during his eight appearances, and United will want to see more from him if he is to come back into contention in the coming weeks.

It will be interesting to see what Pochettino does with the Englishman, given one source close to the Argentine manager is said to have claimed that he has 'always loved' the attacker.

It's quite well known that the 54-year-old tried to sign Rashford when he was in charge of Tottenham, and he clearly believes he is a player that suits his style of player and who he can get the best out of.

United chiefs could overrule him if the price is right, according to Jacobs, and it will be interesting to see if better offers arrive for the 29-year-old in the winter window.
