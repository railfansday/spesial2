Dubois fights Han in three-belt unification on February 20 live on Sky

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Caroline Dubois will take on Stephanie Han in Texas on February 20 live on Sky Sports in UK prime time; Dubois and Han will fight to unify the WBC, WBO and WBA lightweight world titles; "I'm coming to take her title and show everyone why I'm the best lightweight in the world," Dubois said

Caroline Dubois will box rival champion Stephanie Han on February 20 in Texas live on Sky Sports in UK prime time.

The unbeaten champions will fight at GECU Arena at the Don Haskins Center in El Paso with three world titles on the line as London's Dubois travels to fight Han in front of her hometown fans.

Most Valuable Promotion's unified WBC and WBO lightweight world champion, Dubois is one of the most accomplished young talents in women's boxing.

"I can't wait to get back in the ring and I can't wait to go into Stephanie Han's hometown and add another world title to my collection," Dubois said.

"I respect what she's accomplished, but I'm coming to El Paso to take her title and show everyone why I'm the best lightweight in the world.

"These are the kinds of fights I've been working toward my entire career. I want all the belts, I want the biggest fights, and I want to prove myself against the best. On Saturday, February 20, I'm coming to make a statement and take another step toward becoming undisputed.

"A big thank you to MVP and Sky for making this fight happen and continuing to give women's boxing the platform it deserves."

The southpaw known as "Sweet Caroline" enjoyed a decorated amateur career that included gold at the 2018 Youth Olympic Games, four European Youth Championships and representing Great Britain at the Tokyo Olympics.

Turning professional in 2022, Dubois quickly established herself as a dominant force in the lightweight division, capturing the WBC world title before unifying the WBC and WBO championships with a victory over Terri Harper at MVPW-01 in April.

She most recently defended her titles against Amelia Moore in August and now looks to add the WBA championship to her collection in the biggest unification fight of her professional career.

Han is an El Paso native, a devoted mother of two, veteran of the El Paso Police Department and former beauty pageant competitor whose remarkable journey has made her one of the most compelling figures in women's boxing.

Known as "The Enforcer," Han captured the vacant WBA lightweight world title with a first-round knockout of Hannah Terlep in 2025 and has since successfully defended her championship three times, including back-to-back victories over combat sports legend Holly Holm.

Most recently Han defeated Holm in front of her hometown fans in El Paso in May, further cementing her status as one of the division's elite champions.

She will now face the biggest challenge of her career at GECU Arena at the Don Haskins Center, with an opportunity to unify three major world titles.

Han said: "This fight is a dream come true! Finally, I have the opportunity to unify titles in my division and get one step closer to becoming the undisputed queen of the lightweight division.

"To be able to fight at home at GECU Arena at the Don Haskins Center is incredibly special, and I'm grateful to MVP for their support to make this happen. I invite all of El Paso to come out and support on Saturday, February 20 and witness this moment."

Nakisa Bidarian, CEO of Most Valuable Promotions, said: "Caroline Dubois and Stephanie Han are two undefeated world champions at the top of one of the deepest and most competitive divisions in boxing.

"Stephanie's story is remarkable. She's a police officer, a mother, a former beauty queen and a world champion who has become a source of pride for El Paso.

"Caroline has established herself as one of the most talented young champions in the sport, and bringing these two women together with three major world titles on the line is a huge moment for MVP and women's boxing.

"El Paso has shown up for its hometown hero time and time again, so we owed it to this city to bring one of the biggest fights in boxing to GECU Arena at the Don Haskins Center on Saturday, February 20, live in UK prime time on Sky Sports."
