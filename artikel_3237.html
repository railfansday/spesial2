Broadway’s Seaview Taps New York-Based Exec & Former Clinton Non-Profit Advisor Justin Klein As COO; Ups Anna Mack Pardee

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

EXCLUSIVE: Seaview, the Tony-, Olivier- and Peabody Award winning production company has tapped Justin Klein, a New York-based executive and former advisor to Hillary Rodham Clinton and her nonprofit organization Onward Together, as its new Chief Operating Officer, with senior Seaview producer Anna Mack Pardee named to the newly created role of Head of Theater.

The changes come as the company focuses on developing the infrastructure for its next phase of multimedia development. As Head of Theater, Pardee will lead the company’s largest department, with a company line-up this Broadway season to include Lost Lear starring Jessica Chastain, the current It’s a Wonderful Life: A Live Radio Play, and the Off Broadway revival of Spring Awakening at their Studio Seaview.

In a statement, Seaview’s co-founder and CEO Greg Nobile said, “We are so excited to welcome Justin to Seaview as our Chief Operating Officer. As we grow across theater, film and television, we’ve been intentional about building a team with the infrastructure to take on bigger and bolder work. Justin has spent his career turning big ideas into well-run organizations across media and politics, and he’ll be the one making sure our operations can keep up with our ambitions.” Watch on Deadline

Nobile continued, “I’m also thrilled to promote Anna to Head of Theater. She has been a driving force behind so much of what Seaview has put onstage, and she has more than earned the chance to lead our largest department. I couldn’t be prouder of our team, or more excited for all that’s ahead — on Broadway and beyond.”

Since its founding in 2012, Seaview’s credits have included Slave Play, Parade, Tony Award-winning Best Play Stereophonic, Illinoise, Every Brilliant Thing, and Good Night and Good Luck starring George Clooney, with film work including Reality starring Sydney Sweeney and Stress Positions.

Klein comes to Seaview from Fenway Strategies, a strategic communications firm, where he held the COO role, overseeing the firm’s business operations, financial management, and growth strategy. Previously, he was Vice President of Business Affairs at Recount Media, and earlier in his career, he held senior roles on political campaigns and at the Democratic National Committee, and served as a Senior Advisor to Hillary Rodham Clinton and her nonprofit organization, Onward Together.

“I’ve long admired Seaview for the ambition of its work and its role in shaping contemporary theater,” Klein said in a statement. “Bringing my experience building and scaling organizations to Seaview at this stage in its evolution is incredibly meaningful. I’m thrilled to join Greg Nobile and the team at such an exciting moment for the company.”

Pardee is Seaview’s most senior producer and has been with the company for three and a half years, rising from Associate Producer to Producer and now to the newly formed role. Prior to her time with Seaview, she spent eight years at National Artists Management Company (NAMCO) with Barry and Fran Weissler, rising from assistant to Barry Weissler to Associate Producer.

Since joining Seaview, she has led the company’s producing efforts on such Broadway and Off Broadway productions, as Sam Gold’s Enemy of the People, starring Jeremy Strong and Romeo + Juliet, starring Kit Connor and Rachel Zegler; Illinoise, the Sufjan Stevens musical directed by Justin Peck; Good Night, and Good Luck; All In: Comedy About Love and All Out: Comedy About Ambition written by Simon Rich and directed by Alex Timbers; The Queen of Versailles, starring Kristin Chenoweth and directed by Michael Arden; and The Fear of 13, starring Adrien Brody and directed by David Cromer, among others. She works across Seaview’s producing and development slate, partnering with artists and creative teams from early development through production.

In its announcement, Seaview noted that the additions to its leadership team “reflect the reality of Seaview’s operations, as it has grown from a theater producer into a multi-vertical entertainment company,” adding that Klein’s “breadth of experience building and scaling organizations across media, nonprofit, and political fields will help guide Seaview in its continues expansion across across all mediums.”

In a statement, Pardee said, “Since joining Seaview, I’ve had the great privilege of working alongside an incredibly talented and passionate team guided by Greg’s innovative vision. I’m honored to step into this new role and excited to lead the next chapter of our Theater Department, championing ambitious work and the extraordinary artists who bring it to life.”

Klein and Pardee will report directly to Nobile.
