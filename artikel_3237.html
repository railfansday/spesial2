Carlyle Secured (CGBD) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Joint venture yields rose as originations held steady in a slower market.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Carlyle Secured Lending, Inc. (CGBD +2.52%) reported third quarter results characterized by stable net asset value and the scaling of its joint venture platforms. Management reported that while market-wide direct lending volumes were lower due to macroeconomic uncertainty, the company utilized its proprietary sourcing network to maintain origination activity. The board declared a base dividend for the fourth quarter that is fully covered by net investment income, supported by floating-rate assets and increased yields from the investment funds. The company maintained its focus on senior secured lending and portfolio diversification while executing share repurchases at a discount to book value.
