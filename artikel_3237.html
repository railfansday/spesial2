Ariana Grande Says Reaction to ‘Petal’ Video Felt Like ‘Black Mirror’

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Ariana Grande opened up about how the wave of criticism following her “Petal” music video felt like an episode of Black Mirror.

In a Las Culturistas podcast released on Wednesday, Grande was asked about the irony of the public backlash, which zeroed in on Grande’s appearance in the video — which was about an aspiring actress named Pepper who is constantly scrutinized for her appearance by a panel of foul men.

“It’s Black Mirror,” remarked Grande, who added that she found the reaction “interesting” and “would put it out again today, exactly as is.” Grande elaborated, “I’ve never changed my musical style or whatever, based on what I think people want from me. I’ve always moved authentically in that way. That’s something I’m proud of.” She said, “Let the chips fall where they may. My people will be my people.”

“Petal” served as the title track of Grande’s bold eight album, which released in July.

Grande also shared insight into the making of the video. “I remember how it felt in the room too to be with all of those women dancers, and all of us discussing what it felt like and what it held for us,” she said. “We’ve all been told some kind of interesting thing about ourselves that has nothing to do with the art or our talent or our work or what we’ve given to our audition or whatever — that has nothing to do with anything. Grande’s interview arrives a day after she announced an upcoming two-part televised special event that will chronicle the work she put into preparing for her recent Eternal Sunshine tour. The documentary, The Eternal Sunshine Diaries: The Making of the Tour, will follow her during the 30 days that led up to the first show of the run, which marked her first tour since 2019. The film will highlight how Grande simultaneously prepped for the tour and finished Petal, which she rolled out while on the road.

The doc will debut on Peacock on Dec. 11, and be followed by the release of Grande’s concert film, The Eternal Sunshine Tour, on Dec. 12.
