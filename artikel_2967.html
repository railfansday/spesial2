Epsilon Energy (EPSN) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Powder River Basin and Permian wells drove a sharp production climb this quarter.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 24, 2026 at 3:23PM EST

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Epsilon Energy (EPSN +14.92%) reported a strategic shift toward accelerated development in the Powder River Basin and Permian Basin, focusing on high-return Parkman and Barnett targets. Management indicated that production growth was driven by new well completions and the outperformance of initial production rates relative to internal type curves. The company executed land transactions involving acreage trades and joint ventures to pull forward unconventional development through partnerships with larger-scale operators. Management stated that capital expenditures were focused on operated drilling programs and water infrastructure to support future cost efficiencies and recycling capabilities.

We traded acreage to join 2 EOG-operated units planned for 4-mile Niobrara laterals beginning in late 2027. In a separate transaction with a large private operator, we contributed acreage to a joint venture covering multiple drilling units in exchange for a carried interest and the opportunity to participate alongside the operator. These transactions high-grade our investment program.
