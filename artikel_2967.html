DBR Podcast #870: Duke Clobbers Coastal Carolina

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

That was a clobbering! Duke destroys Coastal Carolina 113-52, and we jump on quickly afterwards for Episode 870!

We quickly go through the headlines and then get into everything good in this game. Big Pat had a career high in points, Cayden Boozer had a career high in assists, and Sebastian Wilkins had a career high in rebounds as Duke gets its largest win under Jon Scheyer. We also discuss some things to clean up and give out our first Player of the Week honors for the regular season.
