UGI (UGI -8.12%) announced a plan to separate its Global LPG business into a standalone, publicly traded company. Management reported that the transaction is expected to be completed in the second half of calendar 2027 and is intended to be tax-free to the company and its shareholders. The separation will create two distinct entities: a natural gas infrastructure company focused on regulated utilities and midstream assets, and a liquid petroleum gas distribution company optimized for cash generation. The company stated that each independent business will pursue its own capital structure and dividend policy tailored to its specific operational and financial characteristics.
