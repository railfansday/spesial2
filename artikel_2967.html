Robotics spin-off shifts the company's future, with financing still a key question.

Faraday Future (FFAI -7.70%) is transitioning into a physical AI investment holding company and a robotaxi technology operator following the spin-off of its robotics unit into FF Robotics Ecosystem Inc. (FFR). Management reported that the company retains a 66% ownership stake in FFR after closing an all-stock acquisition valued at $200 million. The company reported a significant narrowing of its net loss to $9.8 million for the third quarter and indicated a shift toward a lighter-asset operating model focused on shared mobility services. Management stated the board of directors is also seeking stockholder approval for a reverse stock split at a ratio of up to 1-for-80 to maintain Nasdaq listing compliance.
