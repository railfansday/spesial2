Wraithwatch Achieves FedRAMP Class D Certification Through Partnership with Knox Systems

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Certification gives federal agencies an AI-native command and control platform to defend their networks at machine speed

AUSTIN, Texas and WASHINGTON, Nov. 3, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Wraithwatch, an AI cyber defense company founded by veterans of SpaceX, Anduril, Palantir, and Joint Special Operations Command, today announced it has achieved Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program (FedRAMP) Class D (High) Certification in partnership with Knox Systems (Knox), the largest, longest-running federal managed cloud. With this certification, federal agencies can now deploy Wraithwatch's AI-native platform to build a continuously updated digital twin of their environment, giving human and AI defenders a united data fabric for enterprise cyber defense teams to hunt threats, assess compliance, and predict adversary moves in minutes

Federal networks face cyberwarfare adversaries that are well trained and equipped, and increasingly using AI to find and exploit vulnerabilities at machine speed. Yet most security teams still work without a common operating picture of what exists in their environment, how it connects, or where an attacker could gain entry at any given time, and where they could move next. Manual analysis or annual red team exercises cannot keep pace with threats that move in seconds, and automated red team solutions do not scale safely into critical mission environments.

Wraithwatch uses swarms of AI workers to autonomously build a data fabric and ontology of what exists in the environment, possible attack paths into it, and predict what an adversary can do once inside. Its platform makes the entire digital twin queryable in natural language by users across disparate use cases. As a result, analyses, threat hunts, or compliance assessments that may have taken days or weeks to perform can be completed within minutes.

With Wraithwatch's FedRAMP Class D Certification, federal agencies gain unprecedented visibility into their own environments. Instead of piecing together findings from dozens of disconnected tools, GRC analysts, SOC analysts, threat hunters, and threat intelligence teams work from one live picture of every asset, connection, and human and non-human identity, along with the blast radius each one carries. Analysts can query that picture directly at the source, without a SIEM, data lake, or vendor-specific query language, turning three-day threat hunts into results in under 15 minutes and six-week ATO assessments into work completed in under 30 minutes, with real-time visibility into control drift. The same view lets teams map open-source threat reporting to their own environment and deploy the right prevention policies and detection logic across their existing security stack, autonomously or with a human in the loop.

"Cyber defense is moving away from human-led actions at human speed to machine-led actions at machine speed, informed by continuous predictive simulation of what adversaries will do to a network," said Nik Seetharaman, Founder and CEO of Wraithwatch. "Like a processor running speculative execution, security teams can now quickly orchestrate configuration changes, remediations, and patches before the next attack lands. That only works when all your data is tied together and turned into human decisions at speed. That's what Wraithwatch does, and our FedRAMP Class D Certification enables federal defenders to do."

FedRAMP sets the security standard federal agencies rely on before bringing any cloud technology into their environments, giving them a common, trusted baseline for evaluating new solutions. Through its partnership with Knox, Wraithwatch met that standard on Knox's managed federal cloud, so agencies can adopt its platform with confidence that it meets the government's most demanding security requirements.

"Adversaries are already using AI to probe federal networks at machine speed, and defenders can't answer that with tools and timelines built for human speed," said Irina Denisenko, CEO of Knox Systems. "Wraithwatch was built by operators who defended rockets, weapons systems, and national security networks firsthand. With FedRAMP Class D Certification through Knox, federal agencies can now put that experience to work, turning weeks of analysis into minutes and giving every defender the same clear picture of the fight."
