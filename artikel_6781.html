New York City’s Toughest Tables to Book This Holiday Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Scoring a difficult reservation feels that much sweeter this time of year.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

The holidays in New York are when the city feels most alive. Fifth Avenue’s shop windows glitter, hotel lobbies become elaborate seasonal spectacles, and the dining rooms that anchor the city’s social scene take on an added measure of importance. From Thanksgiving through New Year’s Eve, securing a table at the right restaurant can be as much a part of the holiday as seeing the tree at Rockefeller Center. The most coveted rooms are filled with out-of-towners, old friends back in the city, and New Yorkers determined to make the most of the season. Here are some of the toughest, most in-demand tables to book in New York this time of year—and the reservations worth pursuing for serious bragging rights to round out your 2026.

This reservation is coveted all year round, but the exclusivity of this fantastic restaurant takes on added coziness and flair come the holidays. Its wood-paneled dining room, equestrian memorabilia, and old-school American menu have made it a lasting favorite of the city’s well-heeled diners. You have to call the number on its website. May the odds be ever in your favor.

For a holiday evening that feels atmospheric and old-school, head to The Jazz Club at Aman New York. Tucked beneath the hotel, this intimate venue pairs live jazz with a sophisticated setting. Between the limited seating and the appeal of an evening at one of Manhattan’s most in-demand luxury hotels, advance planning is essential. New Year’s Eve will be especially enviable to book—there will be live music and a countdown.

Since opening in 2024, The Corner Store has been one of downtown’s most talked-about restaurants, in no small part due to a succession of visits from recurring customer Taylor Swift. A reservation here carries social currency, especially during the holidays—last year, during Hanukkah, The Corner Store offered a viral Matzo Ball Martini. Keep an eye out for more fun seasonal specials this year.

Watching the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade from four floors above Columbus Circle at an iconic, Michelin-starred restaurant? Say less. Per Se hosts a special Thanksgiving brunch with front-row views of the parade balloons, alongside chef-curated tasting stations featuring turkey, caviar, Island Creek Oysters, lobster rolls, and more. There’s also balloon twisting and face painting for the kids. There are a few reservations still available through Tock.

Thanksgiving at The Nines is a glamorous alternative to the typical family gathering. The restaurant serves a prix fixe holiday dinner featuring traditional dishes alongside its own signature offerings, with live piano music to boot. The 2026 seating is currently sold out, but Resy offers the option to receive an alert should a table become available.

A reservation at 4 Charles Prime Rib is difficult any time of year, but this West Village townhouse restaurant becomes particularly appealing this time of year. Known for its intimate, clubby atmosphere and indulgent steakhouse fare, 4 Charles releases its reservations 21 days in advance, and they always seem to disappear within seconds. If all else fails, it’s offering At Home Packages for Thanksgiving, so you can enjoy its all-American cuisine without having to jockey for a table.

L’Artusi has been a West Village fixture for two decades, and its popularity shows little sign of waning. During the winter months, the restaurant leans into the season with festive décor, winter truffle dishes, and rotating chef’s specials that celebrate the season. Its relatively small dining room means tables are always in demand. There is good news for devotees, however: A second, larger location is slated to open in Rockefeller Center this spring.

Few New York restaurants commit to the holiday bit like Rolf’s—a Gramercy institution that is known for its ceilings festooned with thousands of Christmas ornaments, twinkling lights, and garlands. The décor stays up all year round, but the holiday period is especially competitive to score reservations. You have to call to book. Good luck.

For those spending Christmas Eve in Manhattan, Chez Fifi offers a distinctly French alternative to your typical holiday dinner. The Upper East Side restaurant—known as one of the neighborhood’s hottest tables to book right now—offers French-Basque specialties, from steak au poivre to roasted chicken and baba au rhum, alongside foie gras terrine and seasonal specials. Pro tip for those having trouble scoring a reservation: the restaurant holds some upstairs seating for release the day before.

West Village hot spot Galo is a new addition to the restaurant scene but has become a quick favorite for in-the-know diners. Housed in a historic carriage house on West 10th Street, it’s about as cozy as you can get—and is one of the few popular restaurants of its ilk to be open on Christmas Day. An ode to the chef’s native Portugal, the menu spans large-format heritage dishes and shareable petiscos, like bacalhau, wine-poached pears, and a variety of special port wines.

Housed in a historic Rockefeller Center townhouse once frequented by Johnny Carson and generations of Saturday Night Live cast members, Pebble Bar oozes authentic New York history. Its intimate dining room is particularly appealing during the holidays, thanks to its proximity to the Rockefeller Center tree, Radio City Music Hall, and Fifth Avenue’s windows. With just nine dining tables, availability is limited—but the restaurant does reserve some space for walk-ins, so a spontaneous drop-in might work out in your favor.

Few dining rooms offer a better vantage point for the holiday season than L’Avenue at Saks, perched on the ninth floor of the iconic Fifth Avenue department store. Its seasonal Le Chalet experience adds live piano, festive cocktails—including a must-try French Onion Martini—and views of the Rockefeller Center tree to the equation. Reservations go quickly in the month of December, when the restaurant can serve 600 to 800 guests a day. Reservations are available through Resy.
