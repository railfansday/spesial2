IP54 vs IP67 on the Factory Floor: Which Ingress Rating Does Your Barcode Reader Actually Need?

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

A reader that survives one station can die in another. Two letters and two numbers decide a lot of that — but higher is not always better, and buying on the rating alone is a reliable way to overspend or still fail in the field.

An IP rating has two digits. The first is protection against solid particles (dust); the second is protection against water.

That's the whole difference. Neither number says anything about read performance, decode speed or drop durability — those are separate specifications.
