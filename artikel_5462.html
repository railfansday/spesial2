Pfizer beat earnings expectations and raised its full-year sales and profit outlook

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Pfizer raised full-year revenue guidance by $1.5 billion at the midpoint, to a range of $62.0 billion to $64.0 billion

Pfizer $PFE raised its full-year 2026 revenue and adjusted earnings guidance on Tuesday as it reported third-quarter adjusted earnings of 87 cents per share, flat from a year earlier.

The company expects full-year revenue of $62.0 billion to $64.0 billion, up from a prior range of $60.5 billion to $62.5 billion. It expects adjusted earnings of $2.95 to $3.05 per share, compared with a prior range of $2.80 to $3.00. The earnings range includes a one-time $650 million charge tied to an in-licensing and collaboration agreement with Innovent Biologics, which reduces earnings by about 10 cents per share.

Third-quarter revenue totaled $16.58 billion, down $71 million from a year earlier. Excluding Comirnaty and Paxlovid, revenue grew 7% on an operational basis, which removes currency effects. Revenue from products it has launched or bought rose 32%, measured the same way.

Analysts surveyed by LSEG expected adjusted earnings of 73 cents per share and revenue of $15.87 billion, according to CNBC. Analysts surveyed by Zacks Investment Research expected 72 cents and $16.13 billion, according to The Associated Press.

Eliquis revenue rose 25% operationally, and Padcev revenue rose 55%. Paxlovid revenue fell 63%, Comirnaty revenue fell 29% and the Prevnar family's revenue fell 10%, the company said. Pfizer attributed the Paxlovid decline mainly to lower COVID-19 infections in the U.S. and international markets.

Net income came to $3.24 billion, or 56 cents a share, versus $3.54 billion, or 62 cents a share, a year earlier.

Pfizer appointed Cecile Guegan as chief financial officer and executive vice president, effective Nov. 1. Guegan had filled the role on an interim basis beginning Aug. 16.

"Today, we are raising our full-year 2026 Revenue and Adjusted diluted EPS guidance, reflecting our strong year-to-date performance and confidence in our business," Guegan said in a statement.

Pfizer will host a conference call and webcast at 10 a.m. ET on Tuesday.
