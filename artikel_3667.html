Hayden Panettiere's Ex Brian Hickerson 'Distraught' After Her Death

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The brother of Hayden Panettiere’s on-off boyfriend, Brian Hickerson, is sharing an update on how he is doing following her tragic death in August.

“I spent almost eight to 10 weeks up in Greenville [South Carolina] around him,” Zach Hickerson said in an interview with TMZ published on Friday, November 27. “One word that comes to mind is just distraught, heartbroken, confused. I know that there’s still a certain level of shock and surrealness to him of that. There’s just no resolve to this. This isn’t, like, a legal thing. This isn’t a breakup thing. … It’s a definitive thing, and there’s no fixing [it]. There’s no resolve.”

“He’s, one, navigating the loss [and] two … trying to look ahead at the next chapter of his life or the rest of his life. Like, what now?” Zach, 39, continued. “Given all that’s happened, given the reputation, I just know that he’s carrying a lot. Rightfully so, to an extent.”

Panettiere was pronounced dead at the scene after authorities responded to a 911 call at an Airbnb in Greenville, South Carolina, on August 16. The Heroes actress was staying at the residence with Brian, 37. Zach was also present.

“I know that he’s heartbroken,” Zach shared on Friday. “I’ve tried to be there as much as I can for him. About three or four weeks ago, I realized that, man, I was probably trying to carry a little bit too much for everyone else and had not really dealt with the grief and loss myself. So I’ve taken the last two to three weeks to really kind of be on my own, so it’s hard for me to say exactly this moment in time how he’s doing. But what I witnessed was sheer distraught and despair.”

In another clip from the TMZ interview published on Wednesday, November 25, Zach described discovering the Nashville star unresponsive inside the Airbnb.

“I didn’t think it was abnormal that she was [sitting], kind of, Indian-style and laid over,” he recalled. “Her hair was longer, so it was covering. I just assumed that she would pop up and be like, ‘Zachary, what are we doing today?’”

“[Brian] took his hand and went, ‘Hayden,’ and when she didn’t immediately respond or make a noise or react, I stood up and moved to the side,” Zach continued. “Brian … pulled her onto his chest, and when he did, I had a clear view of her face, [which] was pale, lips were purple. I immediately knew something was incredibly wrong.”

“We did CPR for, gosh I don’t even know how long it was. I just did it until the EMS and the cops and the fire and whomever else came in,” he said. “I was like, ‘Oh my God, thank God, they’re here.’ Brian and I got up, got out of the way.”

Panettiere’s cause of death was the toxic effects of fentanyl, 4-ANPP, alprazolam, methocarbamol and quetiapine. It was ruled an accident.
