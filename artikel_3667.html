Golden Bachelor’s Peg Munson Hints at ‘Mean Girls’ in Cast After Mel Owens Split

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Peg Munson is revealing where she stands with her fellow season 2 contestants on The Golden Bachelor in the wake of her split from Mel Owens.

“I’m going to be honest with you Stefanie, those girls were mean girls. They really were,” Peg, 63, said on the Monday, November 16, episode of Stefanie Parker’s “She’s All Bach” podcast. “The only person I’ve heard from is Debbie [Siebers], of course Terri [Alani], my bestie from the show. I heard from Carol [Freeman-Branstine]. Carol reached out. She’s friends with the group but her and I, we do talk and we text. And Monica [Parham]. But I haven’t heard from anybody else. Nothing.”

She continued, “So what does that show you? That’s on them. They were very catty. They were not nice. They really were not nice, you know? Because if I was in their position, I want somebody to be happy. I can celebrate somebody’s success and I just don’t feel like those women — I haven’t heard from them, at all.”

When asked whether she knew that Mel, 67, had blocked the “majority” of the cast on social media, Peg confirmed that the pair had done so together.

“I don’t do negativity. I don’t do negative energy. And what’s really sad, Stefanie, is the women that were doing the negativity, they’re the Christians,” she claimed. “They’re the ones that were out there praying and reading the Bible in the morning. But here they are, throwing shade at me, right? I don’t know. It is what it is. I don’t do negativity. I have a door to my house and I only let a certain amount of people in and I have a door to my social media.”

Peg and Mel met on The Golden Bachelor season 2 in 2025, where they ended the show with a commitment to each other. Less than a year after the finale, Peg revealed that she decided to “step away” from her relationship with Mel.

“The parts that he showed me were good. You know, those good parts. But then there are the other parts that just didn’t work for me. And we were lucky. We were lucky we got to experience this together,” she said during Monday’s episode. “No harm, no foul … No bad blood whatsoever. I just need some things that he was just not either willing or capable of giving me. That’s all.”
