Commanders K Drew Stevens is the NFC special teams player of the week

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Commanders called on kicker Drew Stevens often in Week 11 and he came through each and every time.

Stevens was asked to attempt field goals at the end of all five of Washington’s possessions in the second half of their home date with the Bengals and Stevens made all five of those kicks. The last of them came on the final snap of the game and the 26-yard kick gave the Commanders a 22-20 win.

Stevens was named the NFC special teams player of the week on Wednesday.

The Commanders signed Stevens as an undrafted rookie this offseason and he is now 22-of-25 on field goals and 16-of-19 on extra points on the year.
