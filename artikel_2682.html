Dan Quinn: Commanders beat Bengals using this important trait Washington has been missing

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Commanders came through in the clutch and earned a 22-20 victory over the Cincinnati Bengals that was both high stakes and high drama. It was an outcome fans will always remember, with a late-game surge that will be seared into the mind for years to come.

Head coach Dan Quinn doesn't have tons of time to enjoy it, now working on a short week to prepare for Sunday's game against the Arizona Cardinals.

His virtual Tuesday press conference was the last time to look back on an excellent performance, which he believes was due to the complementary football Washington has been missing all season.

Quinn also explained why the defense continues to trend up, especially along a battered defensive line that continues to find ways to win. He also said that quarterback Jayden Daniels' non-throwing hand was a bit banged up but not injured. There was some different blocking strategies employed in the run game that increased efficiency as well. He also talked about kicker Drew Stevens' performance, while acknowledging that better third-down play will turn field-goal opportunities into touchdowns.
