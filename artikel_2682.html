Limited practice for Dallas Goedert

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Philadelphia Eagles TE Dallas Goedert (shin) was limited during practice Monday, Nov. 23. The Eagles did not hold a practice Monday, so this is an estimation.

Why It Matters: Goedert seems to have a decent chance to play this week, but fantasy players will know more over the next few days. He caught only one pass for 38 yards in Week 11 but should see more targets this week. He can be considered a low-end No. 1 tight end in most leagues.
