Chicago Bears Pass Protection Analysis on the Sacks Allowed vs Saints

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

It's a short week with the Chicago Bears playing on Thursday, and I have a lot to do before kickoff, so let's get right into the two sacks allowed against the New Orleans Saints.

Sack 21: 2nd Quarter: 1-10-NO 14 (12:40) (Shotgun) C.Williams sacked at NO 19 for -5 yards (C.Wooden)

The Saints dropped seven into coverage, and they had everything locked up. Caleb Williams worked left to right, and you could see him through his progressions. He got through at least 4 and had nowhere to go before he started to feel the pocket get tight. He tried to sneak up through the middle, but defensive lineman Colby Wooden got him.

There's less space to operate in the red zone, and things happen quicker, so he didn't have time to get to his fifth read and D'Andre Swift in the right flat. Even if he had, a defender was coming down there too to take that away.

Sometimes an offense has to tip their cap and acknowledge a good play by the defense, and sometimes sacks happen.

Sack 22: 3rd Quarter: 2-6-CHI 37 (11:35) (Shotgun) C.Williams sacked at CHI 26 for -11 yards (J.Sanker)

It's not often the network takes the time to break down a sack, but when they do, and it's a short week, I'll take the shortcut and steal their work!

As Tom Brady explained on the broadcast, the free blitzer is the quarterback's responsibility, so this one is on Caleb. He tried to spin out of there, but Jonas Sanker was controlled in his rush to get the sack.

Offensive coordinator Press Taylor talked about this play in his Tuesday presser.

"The one I can think of was an empty play where we were hot with the nickel coming from the field there. We had a hot answer that we felt like the ball could have come out to on time. That's just making sure we're dialed into it."
