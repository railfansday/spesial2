Newcastle 'concerned' about Harvey Barnes, he could cost Matthias Jaissle his job - expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Newcastle United are becoming concerned with Harvey Barnes' performances amid what has been a slow start to the season.

The 28-year-old has been a mainstay in Matthias Jaissle's side since the season began, and has scored four goals and provided two assists across 12 games in all competitions.

However, his inconsistency is starting to become a problem, as those goals and assists have come across just four of those 12 appearances as the Magpies form has dipped.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Barnes' performances are symptomatic of the issues Newcastle have faced so far this season.

The Magpies' inability to put a positive run of performances together has heaped pressure on Jaissle's position, with large sections of the fanbase now calling for his head.

There are also concerns about the fact that Newcastle have failed to adequately replaced their major summer departures, further adding to the manager's problems.

Football Insider reported on 30 October that Barnes is facing being axed by Newcastle after a string of indifferent performances which have not helped the team's form.

The Magpies were dumped out of the cup by Everton last week, before suffering a defeat to the same opponents in the Premier League, leading to concerns about the displays.

Barnes was one of a number of players to let the side down in those games, ramping up the pressure on Jaissle as he struggles to turn around the Magpies' form.

"Newcastle's biggest problem so far under this new manager has been about their consistency," Brown told Football Insider.

"Nobody sums that up more than Harvey Barnes, I think inconsistent has always been the word to describe him, and it doesn't seem like that's going to change at this stage.

"When he's on it, he can be one of the best wingers in the Premier League, he's electric, he can beat his man, he scores great goals, and he looks a real top-class player.

"But those performances might come once in every six or seven games, maybe even more than that, and when he's not on it you hardly notice he's even on the pitch.

"Newcastle will be concerned about that, because he's playing a big part in their starting XI this season but his performances haven't shown he deserves to be there.

"When you look at Anthony Gordon there compared to how Barnes has been this season, it's a major drop off, and it's no wonder their results haven't been great.

"It's not just Barnes and that position, they've downgraded in a number of important areas, in midfield as well, and so results take a dive and then the pressure comes on the manager.

"If Newcastle keep going with these inconsistent results, it will be the manager who takes the blame for it, but their issues are clear when you look at the players."

Jaissle is facing increasing question marks over his position as Newcastle manager, with inconsistent performances and below-par results sparking doubts over his future.

It has been a slow start to the manager's tenure with the Magpies, who have now one just one of their last five games and just three of nine in the league so far this season.

However, Brown told Football Insider on 3 November that Newcastle are keen to stick by Jaissle and hope he will inspire a resurgence despite a below-par start.

There is optimism that, despite a turbulent start, the boss can still turn things around and set them on track to reach their ambitious goals not just this season but long-term.

Newcastle are determined to ensure they got the decision right, and will give the boss time to prove himself rather than admitting their error in replacing Eddie Howe.
