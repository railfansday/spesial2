Expert reveals the real reason for Florian Wirtz revival amid Liverpool training ground claim

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Florian Wirtz's confidence and happiness in a better environment at Liverpool this season has seen him make serious strides forward.

That is according to experienced former Tottenham, Everton and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 23-year-old joined the Reds from Bayer Leverkusen in 2025 for up to £116million, and had been dubbed a flop by some after a difficult first season.

But Wirtz has started to turn things around at Liverpool under Andoni Iraola, and has began to add the goals and assists that had been missing from his game.

The midfielder has scored four times in his last three games for club and country, netting twice for Germany against the Netherlands in a 5-0 thumping on Monday.

Wirtz's first was a thing of beauty - a stunning strike from outside the box that clearly said everything about his current confidence.

When Wirtz arrived at Liverpool last season, there was an extremely positive vibe around the club.

The Reds had just won the Premier League and looked to be building on their success with an incredibly ambitious window that also saw the likes of Alexander Isak and Hugo Ekitike arrive.

But things at Anfield started to unravel quickly and the atmosphere became toxic, with Slot sacked at the end of the season.

For a player attempting to adapt to a new league like Wirtz, that environment cannot have been positive.

"The Germans ran riot against the Netherlands. Wirtz did well, scored two goals," King told Football Insider.

"He's worked hard and turned his season around where people were saying he was a waste of money. You don't spend £116m on somebody and say it's a waste of money, do you?

"You have to make sure when you're bringing players in from abroad that things are right around the football club.

"He's not going to be at the training ground for 24 hours a day. Players are funny animals. If they're happy off the pitch, they're going to be happy on it."

Jurgen Klopp's appointment as manager of the Germany team has been a positive for Wirtz, too.

The midfielder said as much at a recent event, revealing that the former Liverpool boss is in constant contact.

"We talk a lot. It's great to have a coach who is so genuinely interested in how things are going for us players at our clubs," he said.

"You spend more time at your club, so that's where you need to improve. The coach is very keen to help us with that."
