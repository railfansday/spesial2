PACKING PANIC, PLANE PET PEEVES AND TRAVEL "ICKS": NEW SURVEY REVEALS HOW AMERICANS TAKE TO THE SKIES

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

NEW RESEARCH UNCOVERS THE HABITS, PET PEEVES AND UNWRITTEN RULES OF MODERN TRAVEL

PALO ALTO, Calif., Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Whether you're an early arriver or a last-minute airport sprinter, a chatty seatmate or a headphones-on traveler, everyone has their own way of getting from point A to point B. A new survey of 2,000 Americans who travel for leisure or business, commissioned by Navan and conducted by Talker Research, takes a fun look at the habits, pet peeves and unwritten rules that shape the entire travel journey, from packing at home to stepping off the plane.
