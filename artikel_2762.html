Announcers revealed for Jaguars vs. Titans Week 10 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The announcers have been revealed for the Jacksonville Jaguars' Week 10 matchup with the Tennessee Titans.

This will be the Jaguars' first game since they made three big moves at the NFL trade deadline. Whether or not we see Jessie Bates, Davon Godchaux, or Jordan Davis on Sunday remains to be seen at this time.

Jacksonville enters this game at 6-2 with a commanding lead in the AFC South, and they are the three-seed overall in the AFC playoff picture. The Titans are 1-7, but have been playing better football lately than their record indicates.

On the call of Sunday's matchup will be Chris Myers, Mark Schlereth, and Jen Hale.

ESPN's Football Power Index model is giving the Jaguars an 83.9% chance of winning this week's game.
