USC set to end regular season on the road for first time since 2010

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

On Saturday, the USC football team will take on crosstown rival UCLA at the Rose Bowl. With both teams currently ranked in the top 15, the game will have major Big Ten and College Football Playoff implications.

The game will also be notable from a historical standpoint for the Trojans. For the first time since 2010 (with the exception of COVID-impacted seasons), USC's final game of the regular season will be played on the road.

2010 marked the final season that the Trojans played in a conference without a conference championship game. As a result, USC hosted Notre Dame at the Coliseum during college football's traditional rivalry weekend, before concluding the year on the road against UCLA the first weekend of December.

Traditionally, the Trojans have ended the regular season with a rivalry game at the Coliseum—against UCLA in odd years and Notre Dame in even years. With the USC-Notre Dame rivalry sadly not being played this year, however, the Trojans will instead wrap up the regular season in Pasadena.

In addition, when the USC-Notre Dame rivalry does return in 2030, it is set to be played in September. Because of this, the Trojans' great tradition of ending every year with a home rivalry game will sadly be no more.
