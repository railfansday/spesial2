How Timberwolves’ Rudy Gobert truly feels about playing with LaMelo Ball

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

How Timberwolves' Rudy Gobert truly feels about playing with LaMelo Ball appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Minnesota Timberwolves have been rolling of late, recently picking up a thrilling win over the Portland Trail Blazers at home thanks to a late comeback. The Timberwolves have been doing it all without Anthony Edwards in the lineup, forcing other players like Rudy Gobert to step into bigger roles.

Recently, Gobert got one hundred percent real on what it has been like playing alongside new point guard LaMelo Ball, who was acquired by the Timberwolves this summer.

"I like his joy, his energy, I think it's contagious, and I feel like every time their is frustration on the court… whatever's happening, whatever happened last game, whatever he is probably going through himself, he's always bringing a smile, joy, jokes, and I think we need that, it's great to have that balance," said Gobert, per Andrew Dukowitz of Sports Illustrated on X, formerly Twitter.

The early returns on the Timberwolves' trade with the Charlotte Hornets for Ball this past summer paint Minnesota as the clear winner of that transaction, with Charlotte floundering in the Eastern Conference standings and the Timberwolves holding steady near the top of the West.

Of course, the vision was to pair Ball with superstar Anthony Edwards in the Timberwolves' backcourt, and while the pair looked lethal together in their limited time, Minnesota has since seen Edwards go down with a significant knee injury that will keep him out for the foreseeable future.

Still, Ball is proving that he is more than capable of leading a team to wins as the primary offensive engine, just as he did for the Hornets down the stretch of last season before they inexplicably traded him.
