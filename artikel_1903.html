Alysson surely facing extended spell on Aston Villa sidelines after rotten 14% display

Aston Villa winger Alysson should not be in their starting eleven for the foreseeable future after his shoddy showing in their defeat to Galatasaray.

Villa suffered a second successive Champions League loss on Tuesday as Deniz Gul scored a late header to seal a 1-0 Galatasaray win.

Unai Emery's side were simply not at the races in front of a raucous RAMS Park as they failed to muster a shot on target throughout.

They now face a trip to Ipswich Town this weekend in the Premier League amid a winless run of six games across all competitions.

Alysson was among the most disappointing performers for the away side on Tuesday, as his own inconsistent recent form continued on the European stage.

The 20-year-old should now be dropped from the Villa team as others deserve to stake their claim for the right-wing spot going forward.

Alysson has been a key part of Emery's squad this season after making just three appearances in the second half of last term following a January move from Gremio.

He made his 18th outing of the campaign so far across all competitions in Istanbul, but massively flattered to deceive again following just one goal in his last nine appearances.

The Brazilian wide man took the fewest touches of any starter (18), won just 14 per cent of his ground duels (1/7) and completed just 25 per cent of his attempted dribbles (1/4).

He also failed to take a shot on goal or create a chance, producing the fewest accurate passes (6) of all 22 starters too.

It was a dismal showing from the right flank and one that surely means he is destined for the bench next time out and potentially beyond.

Ibrahim Mbaye came on for Alysson in the 66th minute of the Gala defeat and did little more than his teammate, with just nine touches of the ball and one pass into the final third.

He is the prime candidate to take his spot, however, with Emery surely keen to see more from his £47million man, who is yet to start a league game since joining from PSG but has four assists in 13 Villa games so far.

Captain John McGinn has also taken up the position on occasion this season, and Emery may push him back out there to accommodate another midfielder as Villa look to end their dismal recent run this weekend.
