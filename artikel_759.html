Noah Kahan Reacts to 2027 Grammy Nominations Snub: ‘I Was In My Feelings for a Sec’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

While Kahan did receive one folk nomination, the singer/songwriter was expected to show up across the Big Four categories.

Before the 2027 Grammy nominations were announced on Monday morning, Noah Kahan was expected to have a big presence across the Big Four categories with his latest album The Great Divide. But once all the nominees were revealed, Kahan only had a single nomination: best contemporary folk album.

The singer/songwriter became the poster child for countless “snubs & surprises” lists (Billboard‘s included), and the only thing fans heard from Kahan on Monday was this three-word tweet: “Life goes on.”

But he had more to say on Tuesday (Nov. 17), taking to X to thank fans and congratulate his fellow nominees.

“Needed a day to just recalibrate but I want to say THANK YOU so much for the support and love,” he wrote. “I also want to offer a huge congratulations to every nominee. They all deserve it and I am proud to have been considered in the same company!”

He ended the message on this upbeat note: “Onward.”

Needed a day to just recalibrate but I want to say THANK YOU so much for the support and love. I also want to offer a huge congratulations to every nominee. They all deserve it and I am proud to have been considered in the same company! Onward

Kahan also took the time to reply to some messages on X. One fan wrote to him, “Glad you took the time you needed, there is no alternate universe in which we could be prouder of you or more grateful for your music,” to which Kahan responded: “And that recognition is truly what’s most important to me. I was in my feelings for a sec but nobody is owed anything in this life, and I got a bunch of fans to play songs for! Love you guys so much.”

And that recognition is truly what’s most important to me. I was in my feelings for a sec but nobody is owed anything in this life, and I got a bunch of fans to play songs for! Love you guys so much.

He also heard from Vermont congresswoman Becca Balint, who posted a video on X in which she said: “Hey, Vermonters. We were robbed. Not a single Noah Kahan nomination in the Grammys.” (Balint corrected her error in the video text, adding “Couldn’t think straight. He did get one.”) “It’s just not right. Not right.” Kahan responded to the Democratic U.S. representative, “As long as you keep winning I’ll be happy! Thank you so much and thanks for what you do for our state.”

As long as you keep winning I’ll be happy! Thank you so much and thanks for what you do for our state

Before Monday, Kahan had two Grammy nominations under his belt, including for best new artist in 2024.
