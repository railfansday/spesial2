Smith: Paths align better for Stevenson unification than Azim fight

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Dalton Smith has had an outstanding 2026, winning and defending the WBC super-lightweight world title with wins over Subriel Matias and Alberto Puello; Smith believes he can headline at Hillsborough Stadium in his Sheffield hometown with American star Shakur Stevenson a potential opponent

Dalton Smith, the WBC super-lightweight champion, thinks he's more likely to fight Shakur Stevenson next than Adam Azim.

Sheffield's Smith has had an outstanding year, knocking out Subriel Matias in New York to win his world championship and defending the belt against Alberto Puello in his Sheffield hometown.

American star Shakur Stevenson secured the WBO's super-lightweight championship when he beat Teofimo Lopez in January.

Both Smith and Stevenson are looking for their next opponents and a unification between the pair would be an appealing match up.

"Boxing's business and if it makes good business on both sides it's definitely a fight that can be made. Obviously there's other champions, other belts. I just want to be part of the biggest fights," Smith told Sky Sports.

"It's a fight where the paths align a little bit better than me and Adam Azim right now. But like I said we'll see."

His advice to Azim is: "Be in world level fights.

"It's a fight what gets talked about a lot," Smith said of Azim. "I've got my own path I'm going on. I'm not just saying I'm doing. I think Adam needs to play his part as well.

"I'm not really concentrating on Adam. I'll let him do what he's doing.

"I'm concentrating on a big 2027 for me."

He would love to headline at Hillsborough Stadium in Sheffield next year.

"I'm in a position now to be part of some big, big fights," Smith said. "I'd love to have Hillsborough, I'd love to win more world titles.

"It's something I've dreamt of for years, I've always said it. People probably thought I was delusional at the time saying it, but I kept following the dream and I feel like it's very close. Possibly something we can get over the line next year. It'll just be big for Sheffield, big for the city and I'll be proud to say I've done it.

"I feel like I'm carrying that torch for the city now."
