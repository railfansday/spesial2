Hysa aims to win WBC title then take on 'favourite fighter' Fury

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Nelson Hysa challenges Agit Kabayel for the WBC title on Saturday in Dusseldorf; Hard-punching Hysa hopes to upset popular German Kabayel and then look to take on his 'favourite fighter' Tyson Fury; Hysa says Moses Itauma is still the best in the division - 'I don't want to fight him!'

Nelson Hysa has a vision. He wants to dethrone Agit Kabayel, win the WBC heavyweight world title and then take on his favourite fighter – Tyson Fury.

Hysa, unbeaten in 25 professional fights, challenges Kabayel on Saturday for the WBC heavyweight world championship.

The Albanian has taken 23 of those wins inside the distance and is looking to upset the popular German when they fight in Dusseldorf this weekend.

A power-puncher, Hysa told Sky Sports: "The knockout was with me from the first day that I started boxing.

"In this match [against Kabayel] I am preparing to do more good rounds because I know the opponent is a very good boxer, he's a tough boxer, a serious boxer.

"But we are planning, we are trying to do our best. We have our strategy to win the match and I think we will win the match earlier than the 12th round.

"I need to move up the ratio of knockouts!"

With Oleksandr Usyk vacating his championships, there are now new belt-holders in the division, but Hysa does not consider Kabayel the best of the current heavyweight titlists.

"For me No 1 now of the guys that have the belts, No 1 is Filip Hrgovic, [then Daniel] Dubois and for me the special one is Tyson Fury.

"Hrgovic will do very good because he's a good boxer," Hysa added. "Hrgovic beat the best guy in the heavyweight division. He beat Moses [Itauma]. I don't want to do a fight with Moses, he's a good guy! He will be the best heavyweight in the world. He is the best heavyweight in the world but he needs more time."

Fury will fight Anthony Joshua for the WBA 'super' heavyweight title when they meet on December 11. Hysa tips Fury to win and is eager to box him in a unification if he comes through his own fight.

"Who doesn't want to fight Tyson Fury? He's a legend," Hysa said. "The No 1 man at heavyweight.

"He's my No 1 favourite boxer, I love him, I love everything he does. His style, everything.
