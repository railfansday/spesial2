Kojima's Xbox-Published OD KNOCK Gets A New Game Poster

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Keeping up with Kojima and what he's working on is always a fun task, as we never really know what he's up to, how far along it is, when we'll hear from him again, and all that jazz.

Hideo Kojima's OD KNOCK was announced at The Game Awards in 2023, and yet we know hardly anything about the game. Last night, Kojima returned to provide us with another bump of his Xbox published OD, with a brand-new game poster.

It depicts Sophia Lillis' character looking up towards a windy red object. It kind of looks like a red broomstick, but honestly? Who knows what it is. Her character doesn't appear to be screamin' good and proper like in the previous poster, but they do share the same message: "For all players and screamers".

Sure, it's a pretty basic poster all things considered, but do you know what? It's something! Lord knows all we want is to know more about OD KNOCK and when we'll be able to understand what makes it so scary.

Earlier in the year, worries that Kojima's Xbox-published game was getting canned started to circulate, but those concerns have since been put to rest by Xbox boss Asha Sharma, who declared "to invention and adventure together with Xbox" when she visited Kojima Productions in September.

We have no idea when OD KNOCK will hit Xbox consoles, but what we do know is that when it does eventually release, we'll be here at Pure Xbox to bring you all the coverage.
