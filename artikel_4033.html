The Simpsons Hit And Run's Radical Games Cancels Multiple Projects

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Simpsons fans have been clamoring for Hit And Run to be remastered or remade for years, and it finally seemed like there was a studio actively working on the project. Now those hopes have been dashed once more as a game designer has revealed a game they were working on was one of many cancellations at the studio.

Hopes that Hit And Run might finally be playable in some form or fashion on modern consoles were given a shot in the arm back in February when Radical Entertainment, the studio behind the original, rebranded to New Radical Games. Now Darren Evenson, a senior game designer at the studio, has dashed those hopes.

“It seems that not even a fantastic project with an absolutely incredible team is immune from the current state of the industry right now, as what we were working on at New Radical Games (although mind-blowingly awesome) was part of a whole gaggle of cancellations,” Evenson writes on LinkedIn.

Evenson has seemingly been laid off, likely with several others at New Radical Games, as he ends the update by confirming he is available for work. The post also includes a paraphrased quote from Scarface, which has given Simpsons fans hope that the “awesome” game the designer is referencing was a Scarface one rather than one that will take us back to Springfield.

“Oh, you silly suits with spreadsheets... will you EVER learn? If you don't make the game, you can't make the money... and if you don't make the money, then you don't get the power... and... well, there's more to that quote, but I digress,” Evenson writes. That’s a tweaked quote from Scarface, and Scarface: The World Is Yours, is another game Radical worked on a few years after Hit And Run.

That quote, like so many other iconic lines from other shows and movies, was also paraphrased in The Simpsons in the mid ‘90s, and is likely what many people associate it with before Scarface.

The quote does make it seem more likely that some sort of Scarface remaster was the project Evenson was working on, but as he highlights, it was one of many to have been recently canceled at New Radical Games. If there really was a Simpsons Hit And Run remake or remaster in the works there, it now seems incredibly likely that that project has been put on ice.
