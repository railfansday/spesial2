Alabama Barker Gets Upset Over 'Violating' Photos of Her: 'I'm Freaking Out'

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Alabama Barker got visibly upset over photos of herself that went up online — and left her feeling violated.

During the Thursday, November 19, episode of The Kardashians, Alabama, 20, was filming a commercial with stepmom Kourtney Kardashian when she got an alert about her being photographed in a sheer ensemble. The photographers zoomed in on Alabama’s backside, which left her frustrated.

“Can they delete that? It is everywhere,” she said. “Now I’m freaking out.”

Kourtney, 47, and other members of their team attempted to reassure Alabama that the photos didn’t look bad.

“I think for Alabama, she’s grown up with her dad [Travis Barker] being in the spotlight,” Kourtney said in a confessional. “Now she has some attention just on her, I think there’s negatives that come with that.”

Alabama started “freaking out” over the “violating and annoying” moment. Kourtney, however, promised her that it looked “perfectly fine.”

“I know what it’s like, and I’ve been in that position,” the reality star continued. “So I can help give her perspective on how to deal with it and how to move through it. … I love that I have the experience and the wisdom to be able to guide her through these moments.”

Alabama has been featured more heavily in season 8 of The Kardashians. In an October episode, Kourtney spoke to Alabama about her and brother Landon Barker “still not communicating.”

“Yeah, we’re in an argument,” Alabama replied. “We just had a disagreement. And I found something out, and I told my father. And [Landon] didn’t like that. I haven’t talked to him. I’m going on four weeks.”

“It wasn’t that I was tattletaling. It was for his own good,” Alabama said. “Because I love him.”

She added: “Yeah, I agree. But me and him, we just don’t talk. And we’re fine. Like, we will walk past each other in the hallway. … Even in the kitchen, he’ll be making food, and I’ll just walk out.”
