BET Announces New Chaka Khan Humanitarian Prize At Friday’s Soul Train Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

BET on Friday announced the creation of the Chaka Khan Humanitarian Award to recognize “musicians and cultural figures who have made outstanding contributions to humanitarianism, community service and social justice.”

The award will be presented annually beginning in 2027 at the Soul Train Awards. The new honor was revealed at Friday’s Soul Train Awards on BET and CBS. The show, back after a three-year hiatus, was taped in Chicago earlier this month and hosted by Anthony Anderson.

Chaka Khan attended the show to receive the Classic Soul Honor, presented by Target.

“My music has given me a voice, but love has given me purpose,” said Khan in a statement about her eponymous honor. “This award isn’t about me; it’s about all of us who believe in lifting others, holding each other up, and building a world where compassion lives. I’m grateful to everyone who walks that path and future generations to carry this forward.” Watch on Deadline

During the ceremony that was light on awards, fans selected Tyla as the recipient of the R&B/Soul Song of the Year for her hit “Chanel” while PartyNextDoor was the inaugural winner of Spotify’s RNB X International Artist Award.

Elmiene received the New Soul Honor and Durand Bernarr took home the Soul Personified Honor.

The awards ceremony featured performances by Kirk Franklin, Lucky Dave, Muni Long and BET amplified artists Jaydon and Jai’Len Josey. The late Dolly Parton was honored by Mickey Guyton, Brittney Spencer and Robert Randolph, while El Debarge paid tribute to the late Freddie Jackson.

Connie Orlando served as executive producer of the Soul Train Awards. Jesse Collins Entertainment is the production company for the show, with Jesse Collins, Dionne Harmon and Jeannae Rouzan-Clay also serving as executive producers.
