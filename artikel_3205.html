Keke Palmer & Lena Dunham Circling Universal Rom-Com From Writers Tracy Oliver & Scott King

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

EXCLUSIVE: Universal Pictures is in early development on an untitled romantic comedy to team Keke Palmer and Lena Dunham, sources tell Deadline.

Reps for Universal declined to comment. Plot details are currently under wraps. The script is by Tracy Oliver, creator and executive producer of the Prime Video comedy series Harlem, and Scott King. Eyeing to star, Palmer is also in talks to produce, as are Lena Dunham and Michael Cohen through Good Thing Going, and The Bachelorette alum Rachel Lindsay.

Oliver’s series Harlem, exploring 30-something New York friendships, ran on Prime Video for three seasons from 2021-2025. Over its run, the show earned six NAACP Image Award nominations and two at the GLAAD Media Awards, among others. She’s otherwise perhaps best known for creating and writing the BET series First Wives Club and co-writing Girls Trip, with which she became the first African-American woman to write a film that grossed over US$100 million. Most recently, Oliver co-wrote the upcoming holiday comedy Deck the Y’Alls, starring The Help’s Octavia Spencer and Jessica Chastain, also contributing to the writing of Netflix’s Women Like Us. She is repped by CAA, Artists First and Myman Greenspan Fox. A three-time WGA Award nominee for his work on Mad TV, King worked with Oliver on Harlem and has most recently served as showrunner and executive producer of Prime Video’s Overcompensating. Also part of the writing and producing teams for Insatiable, SMILF, Difficult People and The Real O’Neals, he is repped by UTA, Entertainment 360 and Hansen, Jacobson, Teller. Watch on Deadline

A two-time Emmy winner, Palmer has most recently been seen starring in Peacock’s The ‘Burbs and Boots Riley’s I Love Boosters. Upcoming, she has Spaceballs: The New One and Ride or Die, a Lionsgate romantic buddy comedy where she stars opposite Marlon Wayans, among other projects. She is repped by 3 Arts and Hertz Lichtenstein Yong & Polk.

Dunham just announced a five-episode limited series revival of her lauded HBO series Girls. Prior to that, she released Too Much, her rom-com limited series created with Luis Felber for Netflix, which she and Cohen exec produced. Also upcoming for Dunham’s Good Thing Going banner is her latest directorial feature, the Netflix rom-com Good Sex, starring Natalie Portman and Mark Ruffalo. She is repped by CAA and Hansen, Jacobson, Teller.

Best known as a contestant on ABC’s The Bachelor turned lead on spin-off The Bachelorette — where she became the first Black lead in the history of the franchise — Lindsay has made appearances on a pair of HBO Max series: Hacks, where she played herself, and Made for Love. The Universal rom-com would mark her first feature producing credit. She is repped by Entertainment 360 and Myman Greenspan Fox.
