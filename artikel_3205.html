Why Micron Stock Crashed Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

TrendForce warns: NAND prices are suddenly falling.

By Rich Smith – Nov 20, 2026 at 11:34AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Micron Technology ›

Micron (MU -12.64%) stock tumbled 10.6% through 11:20 a.m. ET Friday morning, and I know what you're thinking:

Anthropic IPO'd yesterday at $83 a share, opened at $111.50 -- a 34% gain -- and then closed at $97.17 -- a 13% loss for anyone who bought in at the open. It's not quite a broken IPO, but for many investors it probably feels like one, and it may be making investors in other artificial intelligence-related stocks nervous. It doesn't help that Anthropic is down another 10.4% today.

But I think there's another reason why Micron stock is down today.

As StreetInsider.com reports today, memory-market research firm TrendForce just published a negative forecast for NAND flash memory demand in 2027.

Major Chinese cloud providers are likely to cut orders for enterprise SSDs next year, TrendForce warns. Indeed, they're already cutting orders, and NAND spot prices are already down 20% "as distributors clear inventory." This decrease in demand may eliminate pricing pressure on NAND chips, forcing NAND prices lower in Q1 2027, in Q2 2027, and "onward."

Now, the good news for Micron is that it doesn't just sell NAND. Actually, only about 26% of the company's revenue comes from NAND, with the balance coming from sales of DRAM. This means that Micron won't be as sensitive to NAND price declines as Sandisk (SNDK -15.65%), for example.

Investors also have to be alert to the risk that the same dynamics that depress demand for NAND may also affect DRAM demand. If we see demand and prices decline there as well, then Micron may fail to dodge this bullet.
