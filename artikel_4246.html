Guide: Upcoming Nintendo Switch 2 Games & Accessories For November & December 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

As the fog on the moors begins to clear after Halloween, we head into the month of November with the biggest game of the year on the horizon. Not that one. That one.

Yes, Nintendo gamers will have their eyes on the imminent return of a little green-garbed boy and his fairy this month, although it's worth pointing out some of the other big games coming to Switches 1 and 2 after Ocarina of Time drops on the 5th.

There's a Switch 2 Edition for Pikmin 4, for starters, but plus some big-name ports in Stellar Blade and Metaphor: ReFantazio, and heading into December, Capcom just can't stop releasing video games, it seems. Plenty to be getting on with when you're not saving Hyrule.

Below, we've highlighted the most exciting Switch 2 (and 1) games coming in the next couple of months. You'll find more great games beneath our top picks, as well as a bunch of accessories.
