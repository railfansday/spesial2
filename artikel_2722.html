James Cook joins O.J. Simpson in Bills history, makes NFL history with Josh Allen

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

James Cook joined O.J. Simpson in the Buffalo Bills record books Sunday.

Then he made NFL history with Josh Allen.

Cook became the second player in Bills history to reach 1,000 rushing yards in the first 10 games of a season during Buffalo's Week 11 game against the Miami Dolphins on Nov. 22.

The running back entered the game with 971 rushing yards and crossed the 1,000-yard mark on an 11-yard run in the second quarter.

The only other Bills player to accomplish the feat was Simpson, who did it in 1973 and 1975.

He capped the same 11-play, 57-yard drive with a 1-yard touchdown run, his 10th rushing score of the season.

That made Cook and Allen the first teammates in NFL history to each score at least 10 rushing touchdowns in three consecutive seasons.

Cook has reached double-digit rushing touchdowns in every season since 2024, when he scored a career-high 16. He followed that with 12 last season.

Cook has now rushed for at least 1,000 yards in four consecutive seasons.

Only two players in Bills history have recorded more 1,000-yard rushing seasons: Thurman Thomas with eight and Simpson with five.

Cook rushed for 1,122 yards in 2023, 1,009 in 2024 and a career-high 1,621 last season.

He entered Sunday's game averaging 107.9 rushing yards per game and reached 1,000 yards before Thanksgiving.

His touchdown gave Buffalo a 20-3 lead over Miami with 3:48 remaining in the second quarter.
