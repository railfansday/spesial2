Rashod Bateman expected to play in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Baltimore Ravens WR Rashod Bateman (calf) fully participated in practice Saturday, Nov. 14, and is not on the injury report for Week 10.

Why It Matters: Bateman was limited earlier in the week but has been a full participant in the last two practices. He has been held to under 35 yards in each of his last four games but should still see a handful of targets in Week 10. He can be considered a No. 5 receiver in fantasy leagues.
