The Toronto Raptors can’t lose this NBA Cup game

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Some games are nice to win, other games are acceptable to lose, but giving up a victory to the Brooklyn Nets at this point would bring abject humiliation onto the Toronto Raptors fanbase.

Last year, arguably the lowest point of the season was the Raptors' loss to an even worse version of the Brooklyn Nets team in December of 2025. That game saw Michael Porter Jr. give the Raptors a lovely Christmas present of 24 points and 11 boards, outscoring 3 of the Raptors' starters put together. The Nets won that game by 15 points.

That was a dark day. The sun set at 4:00, the TTC was slow, and Scotiabank Arena was a place of despair rather than joy. The Raps need to avoid a repeat of that happening.

The Nets are playing with both of their best players active, meaning that Toronto will be in for a challenge containing Porter Jr. and Julius Randle both. MPJ is a fantastic jump shooter whose height and accuracy makes him a deadly marksman that Allen Graves and Collin Murray-Boyles will be hard pressed to contain. Inversely, Julius Randle is one of the strongest, most physical players in the league, capable of bullying his way to easy points and rebounds. Scottie Barnes will likely take this defensive matchup, able to meet Randle in the paint more capably than perhaps anyone else in the league, short of Victor Wembanyama.

Graves has seemingly earned more opportunities to prove himself, and will be starting in tonight's matchup. The rookie has shown flashes of serious scoring and defensive ability, and on a night that would normally be a low-pressure matchup for the Raps, giving the rookie more opportunities and a greater defensive responsibility will go a long way to helping his development.

Indeed, not only is tonight marked by a chance to pick up a much-needed win during a rough part of the season, but it's an NBA Cup night, meaning that beyond the special courts, the Raptors have a chance to notch another win during the Group Stage of the former In-Season Tournament.

The NBA broke down what the Raptors would need to advance tonight. While a win tonight, coupled with the Detroit Pistons winning their matchup tonight, and then a Toronto tiebreaker win would clinch it for the Raptors, a few other scenarios involving a Toronto win, or a loss with a small point differential coupled with some good luck in other Cup games, could send the team forward.

Avoiding insult being added to injury would be the optimal result of tonight's game. Losing to the Nets last year was a blow to a team that were mere playoff hopefuls. This season, the Raptors have thus far failed to meet expectations, between some poor late-game performances and some serious starter injuries that have driven the Raptors to the point they find themselves at now.
