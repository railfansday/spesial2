Russell Wilson shares what he thinks is ‘crazy’ about the Cincinnati Bengals this season

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Russell Wilson believes the 6-2 Cincinnati Bengals have not yet 'hit their groove yet' despite proving to the NFL that they have one of the best offensive units in the league.

Joe Burrow has been playing brilliantly for the franchise, as have his wide receivers, Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins. They are scoring 30 points per game, which is second only to the Buffalo Bills this season.

Their defence has been a matter of concern, as they are still giving up massive leads. Despite this, the team has managed to string together a three-game winning streak.

A huge credit for this has to go to the offence, as they have put up some clutch performances, led by their superstar quarterback.

"I don't even think they've hit their groove yet. Which is crazy to say. I don't even think Joe Burrow has even hit his best moment yet. And so to me, when I watch Joe Burrow and the confidence he's playing with, the fun that he's having, right? The Neymar, the whole dance, something else- what I love about it is he's overcome this year. Because when I go back to last season, when I go back to the end of last season, when I go back to the beginning of this season, right? Before OTAs and all that, he was down and out mentally. He was frustrated. He was upset. He was trying to figure out, you know, his life and everything else," said Wilson on the Set Hut podcast.

Burrow came into the season stating that the team wants to go all the way and win the Super Bowl, which ordinarily would put pressure on the entire franchise, but his teammates have responded well. Even Ja'Marr Chase has been a little bit more expressive than his usual self, and all things point towards the Bengals having enough to win it all.

"He got back to his boys. He got back to the playing and he's done a tremendous job as a leader. And Joe, I'm proud of you as a leader and a great player that you are," Wilson added.

The Bengals' next game is against their division rivals, the Pittsburgh Steelers. A win against them will help the Bengals move to the top spot in the division. It will also help avenge the defeat they suffered earlier in the season against the Steelers.
