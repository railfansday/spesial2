Commanders release veteran running back

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

One day after the Washington Commanders surprised the NFL world by defeating the Cincinnati Bengals on Monday Night Football, they made a roster move. Washington released veteran running back Craig Reynolds from its practice squad. The Commanders didn't immediately add someone, leaving one spot open on the practice squad.

Reynolds, 30, initially entered the NFL as an undrafted free agent with Washington back in 2019 out of NCAA Division II Kutztown. He appeared in three games that season before being waived. Reynolds then signed with the Atlanta Falcons and spent parts of two seasons there. Next was a stint with the Jacksonville Jaguars.

However, in 2021, Reynolds signed with the Detroit Lions. He spent the next four seasons with Detroit, appearing in 55 games and making two starts. The Lions released him, and he signed with the New England Patriots' practice squad. In August, Reynolds signed with the Commanders. After being released during final cuts, Washington quickly re-signed Reynolds to its practice squad.

Reynolds was elevated to the 53-man roster for one game this season, against the Indianapolis Colts in London, carrying the ball twice for four yards.

The Commanders have struggled to keep running backs healthy all season, but Jacory Croskey-Merritt returned Monday, giving Washington four on the 53-man roster.
