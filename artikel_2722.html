Nunes calls for Harrison rematch at featherweight

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Amanda Nunes has called for a rematch against Kayla Harrison at featherweight after defeat by the UFC bantamweight champion on Saturday.

The Brazilian former double champion lost on her return to action after a three-year absence, submitted by American Harrison in the fourth round.

Nunes, 38, has a long history with Harrison, having been training partners, and posted on social media she is chasing a rematch at the weight class above.

"145 [pounds] rematch," Nunes wrote. "Accepted."

Nunes said before UFC 334 she intended to continue fighting even in defeat, while Harrison suggested it could be her last contest in MMA.

Harrison,36, had neck and spinal surgery just 10 months ago and has been open about the difficulties of making 135lb (9st 9lb).

She said after her win she was considering hanging up her gloves.

"I've got a lot of decisions to make when I get home," Harrison said.

"Every time I make weight, I say 'never again'. So we'll see."

Harrison is unbeaten in her four fights in the UFC and has just one loss on her record, a 2022 loss to Larissa Pacheco in the PFL.

Asked if there was any other fight other than the Nunes rematch that would interest her, Harrison said: "If Ronda [Rousey] wants to come get some. It'd be fun. Free money."
