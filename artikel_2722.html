UConn clinches bowl eligibility with win over ODU

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The UConn Huskies (6-5) celebrated Senior Day with a 29-21 victory over the Old Dominion Monarchs (3-8) to clinch the program's third consecutive year of bowl eligibility.

Quarterback Kalieb Osborne completed 12 of 22 (54.6%) passes for 239 yards, a touchdown and a pick. In the backfield, Cyncir Bowers led with 105 rushing yards for an end zone appearance, while Osborne added 94 yards. Through the air, six receivers racked up 20-plus yards, with Ryder Treadway leading the pack.

ODU's backup QB Jaxon Potter completed 23 of 29 (79.3%) throws for 240 yards and a touchdown. Starter Quinn Henicle also went nine for 15 (60%) for 99 yards and two touchdowns. Devin Roche logged seven receptions for 83 yards and a touchdown.

"It meant the world to me; I always play every game like it's my last for my seniors," Osborne said after the win. "It was definitely great to get that sixth win, just knowing they have another chance to get another game."

After trading punts, Osborne connected with Treadway for 36 yards, moving the chains to midfield. From there, Bowers nearly broke loose, but got tied up at the Monarchs' 36-yard line. With just over eight and a half minutes left in the opening quarter, Osborne scrambled the remaining 33 yards on two plays, giving Connecticut a 7-0 lead.

On the Huskies' next drive, an illegal block on the first play set up a first-and-22. However, Porter snagged a short pass and sprinted up the sideline for 41 yards. At ODU's 45-yard line, Bowers gained 17 rushing yards, resulting in JD Willoughby's 20-yard fingertip catch, advancing to the 7-yard line. With under four minutes left, Bowers swerved around the defense and crossed the goal line, cutting Old Dominion's deficit to 14.

In response, Josh Rodriguez reeled in a 37-yard catch. From the 41-yard line, the team inched into the red zone, and Henicle found wide-open Leon Haughton Jr. in the end zone. To begin the second frame, the Monarchs closed the gap to 14-7.

Following Christian Kenji's 15-yard rush to midfield, Osborne rolled out and targeted Matt Ragan for 29 yards. At the 10-minute mark, the defense jammed the offense on the 2-yard line and kicker Mike Baker buried a field goal, extending the margin to 17-7. ODU answered by converting back-to-back third downs in enemy territory, propelling Roche to bounce off defenders and rush in for a 33-yard touchdown.

With a little under five minutes before halftime, UConn marched to the 40-yard line before Cam Abshire made a diving sideline catch at the 13-yard line. Once again, Connecticut stalled in the red zone, and Baker buried a 36-yard field goal for a 20-14 lead.

Coming out of the break, Bowers carried the ball 37 yards to midfield, but the visitors forced a punt. After a quick three-and-out, Osborne fired to Ky Wilson, who brought it to the 33-yard line. Then, an 8-yard sack backed it up to midfield, but an unsportsmanlike penalty gifted Connecticut the first down. Safety Tavares Murray Jr. also committed pass interference, allowing Osborne to hit Treadway for a 4-yard touchdown. With just under four and a half minutes remaining, Old Dominion denied the two-point conversion, keeping it 26-14.

The Huskies had a chance to put the game away before an Osborne interception with five minutes left. At their own 27-yard line, Potter launched to Haughton Jr. for 17 yards. Rodriguez then weaved a short pass for 12 yards and Bedgood grabbed a 23-yard pass at the 13-yard line. Nonetheless, Melvin Hill III sacked Potter and turned the ball over on downs at the 20-yard line. Prior to the 2-minute timeout, UConn drove 79 yards for Baker to bank a 34-yard field goal, giving them a 29-14 lead. To cap off the night, Haughton Jr. scored a 6-yard touchdown as time expired.

Next up, the Huskies will finish the 2026 regular season against Wyoming on the road on Saturday, Nov. 28 at noon.
