Putin says EU is losing industry, falling behind competition ahead of meeting with Trump, Xi

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

SHENZHEN, China — Russian President Vladimir Putin said the European Union was falling behind competition in a speech Wednesday at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation CEO Summit.

"The EU is now losing its industrial capacity and it's losing in the competition," Putin said, according to an official English translation.

Putin used the speech to emphasize Russia's economic resilience despite U.S. sanctions, and played up opportunities for APEC members in the country, from tech to economic development.

He was the third APEC leader to speak at the summit, and followed remarks by Chinese President Xi Jinping and Malaysian Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim.

In his speech, Putin highlighted the country's ability to withstand a sharp drop in its U.S. dollar- and euro-denominated transactions. He also urged members to support Russia in hosting APEC in 2035.

The Russian leader is set to hold talks on Thursday with Xi and U.S. President Donald Trump in a historic meeting that could see a wide range of issues being discussed, including nuclear non-proliferation and also possibly Russia's war with Ukraine. He met with Xi on Wednesday, according to Chinese state media.

Russian presidential aide Yuri Ushakov said last week the meeting in Shenzhen would continue the efforts aimed at reaching a negotiated settlement to the Ukraine war, according to state-controlled local media Russia Today.

The summit will likely yield little concrete result, but presents an "almost 100% photo op" that lifts China's status and power at the top table, David Roche, strategist at Quantum Strategy, told CNBC's "Access Middle East" earlier this week.

— CNBC's Anniek Bao contributed to this report.
