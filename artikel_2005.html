Ref Watch: Three Peter Bankes howlers as clear penalty missed in Hull City win vs Brighton

Peter Bankes had a strong outing as Hull City secured a huge 2-0 win against Brighton on 22 November.

The 44-year-old took charge of the 2pm kick-off at the MKM Stadium, which was broadcast live on Sky Sports, in what was an impotant clash at both ends of the table.

The referee had one major decision to make and showed Joao Costinha a second yellow card in the 89th minute, but the decision was the correct one in an otherwise well-managed affair.

Fabian Hurzeler's side enjoyed a strong first half, in which they could have had three of four goals if not for an outstanding performance from goalkeeper Konstantinos Tzolakis.

However, Hull had chances of their own, mostly in the second half, before Ollie McBurnie scored the first goal in the 92nd minute to send the home crowd wild, before adding a second just minute later.

While Bankes did not make any massive errors, both sides will claim they should have had decisions go their way which could have swung the eventual result.

The game's biggest controversy came shortly before half-time, when Hull City thought they should have had a penalty when Mohamed-Ali Cho was pulled in the box.

After the ball broke loose from a corner, the forward turned to keep the move alive, and Brighton defender Joao Costinha had a handfull of his shirt.

Had Cho gone to ground, there is a real chance Hull could have been awarded a penalty which would have given them the opportunity to go 1-0 up at home.

However, VAR reviewed the situation, and despite replays shoing a clear pull from the Brighton man, they decided no further action was needed.

Just minutes following Hull's loud calls for a penalty, Brighton had claims of their own, after Pascal Gross went to ground on the edge of the six-yard box.

The midfielder would have had a tap-in to put his side ahead shortly before the break if he had not been tripped by the Hull City defender.

After Gross missed out, the ball fell to Diego Gomez at the back post, but his effort was pushed wide by the ankle of Tzolakis in what was another fine save from the keeper.

However, that was not enough to appease Gross, who was left raging by the decision not to award a penalty which, again, was checked and cleared by VAR.

Luka Vuskovic might consider himself lucky to have remained on the pitch for the full match after numerous encounters with Hull striker, and eventual match-winner, McBurnie.

The Croatia international was given a yellow card in the 34th minute after he petulantly kicked the ball away, and was walking a tightrope ever since.

However, he managed to get away with five fouls, and with his yellow card having come as a result of delaying the restart, he went without punishment for all five.

It may have been harsh to see Vuskovic sent off for any of the individual indiscretions, but as the fouls racked up, it was a surprise not to see him sanctioned.
