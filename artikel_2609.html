Brendan Schaub pushes Navajo Stirling towards title shot, says Ulberg won't pay his mortgage

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Brendan Schaub says Navajo Stirling would be making a costly mistake not fighting Carlos Ulberg.

Stirling (12-0 MMA, 7-0 UFC) knocked out his second-straight former champion in Jiri Prochazka (32-7-1 MMA, 6-4 UFC) this past Saturday at UFC Fight Night 294 at ABHA Arena in Doha, Qatar.

Although his resume easily makes him No. 1 contender, Stirling didn't callout Ulberg (14-1 MMA, 10-1 UFC), and said he needs to speak to his coaches first. Schaub tells Stirling that Ulberg isn't his brother despite training out of the same gym, City Kickboxing.

"Let's say you're retired after a 10--year long career. I'm being generous," Schaub said on his YouTube channel. "Let's say 10 years. Do you think when sh*t hits the fan, you can call Ulberg and ask to pay your mortgage? You think he's going to do that? You think your 'brother's' going to do that? No. He probably won't even pick up the phone. Take it from me, dude: I've lived a long, crazy life. The only reason you guys are so close is you're in the same lane; you're pursuing the same dream. That's why you guys are close, and you happen to train at the same gym and have the same coaches. ... All these guys who you think are your 'brothers,' you're never going to hear from them or they might check in on holidays or your birthday.

"Life gets busy. You're going to have family, kids, mortgage. You'll pay for the food, school's a motherf*cker. Maybe they have some issues, you get tutors, they start sports. That gets expensive. You have to build enough of a goose egg. You've got to build enough of a net to support those little dudes and your wife. And if you think anybody in that camp that you're training with in City Kickboxing is going to make sure those kids or your wife are good? Buddy, I'm telling you, you're heartbroken. You're on your own. They're not bad people. They're also going to be busy. Ulberg's also going to have a mortgage and a car payment, a family and kids to pay for. He has to look out for his. It's not that he's mean, and it's not mean if he doesn't call you on your birthday. He's busy trying to provide and live his life. I'm telling you Navajo, f*ck everybody."

Stirling instead diverted his attention to another top contender and former champion in Magomed Ankalaev, who accepted his callout. Ulberg is currently recovering from ACL surgery, an injury he suffered his knockout win over Prochazka at UFC 327 in April.
