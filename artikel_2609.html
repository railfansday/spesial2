Move over, Peyton Manning! Josh Allen now owns another NFL record

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Josh Allen no longer has to share the top spot with Peyton Manning.

The Buffalo Bills quarterback has officially moved ahead of the Pro Football Hall of Famer, becoming the NFL's all-time leader in combined touchdowns by a quarterback through his first 10 seasons. And Allen has done it before completing his ninth year.

Allen entered Sunday's game against the New York Jets tied with Manning at 322 career touchdowns. One more touchdown was all he needed to claim the record for himself.

On Buffalo's second possession of the day, Allen methodically moved the Bills down the field. After a defensive holding call gave the Bills a first and goal at the one-foot line, Allen called his own number and used the opportunity to pass Manning.

With that touchdown, Allen's career total climbed to 323, passing the mark Manning established during his first decade with the Indianapolis Colts.

But the difference between the two quarterbacks isn't just the number of touchdowns. It's how quickly Allen got there.

Manning needed 160 regular-season games to reach 322 touchdowns. Allen matched that total in just 136 games, throwing his record-tying touchdown against the Minnesota Vikings last Monday night. That's 24 fewer games, the equivalent of nearly a season and a half of football.

Allen has also taken a different route to the record. While Manning's touchdowns came almost exclusively through the air, Allen has been just as dangerous with his legs. His career total entering Sunday included 232 passing touchdowns, 88 rushing touchdowns, and two receiving touchdowns.

Patrick Mahomes is the only other active quarterback in the top 10 who can still add to his total. The Kansas City Chiefs quarterback entered Week 10 with 301 touchdowns and will spend the remainder of the season chasing Allen. But Mahomes is already in his 10th NFL season, meaning his final number will be set when the regular season ends in January. Allen, meanwhile, will have all of 2027 to keep adding to a record Mahomes will no longer be eligible to chase.
