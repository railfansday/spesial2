Rashee Rice takes part in practice Monday

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Kansas City Chiefs WR Rashee Rice (knee) fully participated in practice Monday, Nov. 23. The Chiefs only held a walkthrough Monday, so this is an estimation.

Why It Matters: Rice has recorded at least five receptions in each of the last four games but has only one touchdown during that span. He should see a good amount of targets again in Week 12 and can be considered at least a No. 2 receiver in fantasy leagues.
