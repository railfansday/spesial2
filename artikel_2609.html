Lakers crumble in second half, fall to Spurs in marquee showdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

After building a 20-point lead in the first half, the Lakers were walked down by the Spurs in the second half as they fell to San Antonio, 99-96.

The loss means they will not advance to the knockout rounds of the NBA Cup.

It was a tale of two halves. The Lakers shot 51.2% in the first half before shooting just 34.5% in the second half. LA was just 12-42 from three with Luka going 1-13.

The Lakers were active early, coming away with steals on the first two possessions. However, the Spurs jumped ahead through an alley-oop finish from Victor Wembanyama and a Stephon Castle fastbreak dunk.

LA responded with a fadeaway mid-range jumper from Luka Dončić and back-to-back threes from Jake LaRavia, putting the visitors up 10-6 and forcing an early timeout.

Out of the break, Dylan Harper got to the rim to stop the run as the two teams began trading buckets. Luka finished a floater and scored off the glass trying to draw a foul while Castle had another fastbreak dunk and found Luke Kornet for an oop.

Quentin Grimes knocked down a three before Harper and Luka traded baskets, keeping the Lakers ahead 21-14 with three minutes remaining. LA closed the quarter strong with four straight from Luka, giving him 14 in the quarter, and a Grimes lefty layup as LA led 27-14 after one.

San Antonio could get nothing going offensively as they were 0-8 from three. The Lakers, meanwhile, were cooking. Reaves found Kessler for a layup a handful of possessions before LaRavia drew an and-one on a foul-line jumper late in the shot clock.

After missing the freebie, LaRavia chased down his rebound and found Collin Sexton for a three, making it a 34-17 lead and forcing another Spurs timeout. Fox got a layup over Kessler to fall after the timeout but, again, the Lakers remained in full control.

Sandro Mamukelashvili was making plays on both ends, blocking a layup off the glass and even knocking down a three. Sandwiched between those plays was an and-one putback from Collin Sexton as the lead kept growing.

Sexton kept rolling, getting a floater to fall before hitting another layup at the rim and hitting the "too small" gesture.

Wemby stepped up to try to pull the Spurs back into the game, but Reaves and Luka answered every basket as both knocked down absurd shots. The Spurs never pieced together a run to close the half and the Lakers went into the half up 54-38.

The Spurs tried to come out with energy to cut into the lead again to start the third, but Luka and Austin again had replies. After Reaves hit his first three of the game, Luka converted on back-to-back baskets to make it a 61-45 lead.

Castle drew an and-one for the Spurs, made the free throw and then stole the ensuing in-bounds pass and knocked down a shot with his foot on the 3-point line, quickly cutting into the lead and forcing head coach JJ Redick to use a timeout.

A missed LaRavia three led to a Wemby oop to make it a single-digit game again. Wemby then banked in an off-balance shot, but the Lakers finally responded through a Kessler rebound putback and a Reaves three, prompting a timeout from San Antonio.

The Spurs regained momentum with a Harper putback of his own miss and a Champagnie three. Luka drew an and-one on a jumper to stem the tide. As well as Luka was playing, he was still struggling mightily from three as he was up to 1-11 shooting from range.

The Lakers were struggling for offense as Ziaire missed back-to-back looks from range and Luka airballed another. Fox drew a foul on a layup for an and-one. While he missed the free throw, Wemby grabbed the rebound for a layup. Another Wemby layup and Keldon Johnson three in the final seconds tied the game at 68 heading into the fourth.

Wemby opened the fourth with a pull-up jumper and an assist to put the hosts ahead. Sexton drew an and-one to temporarily stop the run, but Carter Bryant hit a three on the other end. Castle then connected on consecutive threes to make it an 81-73 lead and force an LA timeout.

The Lakers used a Grimes three and Luka off-balance shot to make it a one-possession game again. Wemby responded with an absurd driving reverse dunk before Vassell knocked down a three, extending it back to an eight-point lead at 86-78.

Reaves knocked down back-to-back threes of his own, pulling LA back within two with 3:16 remaining. Harper responded by converting a pair of freebies and hitting a runner. Luka hit his own runner before Austin finished on a fastbreak, keeping it a 90-88 Spurs lead with just under two minutes left.

Wemby converted on a layup after a series of misses, but Luka hit yet another off-balance shot on the next possession. Wemby was then fouled on a jumper and split the pair of free throws, but Fox chased down the rebound, leading to a dagger three from Castle with 43 seconds remaining.

Luka missed a three on the next possession, but just as it looked like all hope was lost, Matisse Thybulle forced a turnover and Reaves immediately hit a three, making it a one-possession game with 33.9 left.

Fox then turned the ball over on the next possession, giving LA a chance to tie the game. The Spurs, however, fouled LaRavia to deny the Lakers that attempt to tie the game. LaRavia made both with 7.7 seconds left, making it a 96-95 Spurs lead. Fox was fouled on the other end and made both with 6.6 seconds left.

Grimes was fouled at midcourt with 3.9 seconds left. He made the first and intentionally missed the second, but Wemby grabbed the rebound. He split the pair and Austin's heave at the buzzer was well short.

Luka had another superstar performance, pouring in 31 points with six rebounds and eight assists. In his return, Reaves tallied 21 points and grabbed eight rebounds.

Kessler finished with 10 points and 13 rebounds matched up against Wemby. Sexton provided a lift off the bench with 12 points.

LaRavia had 10 points. Grimes had nine points as he moved back to the bench. No other Laker had more than three points.

The Lakers will be back in action on Sunday when they play the Grizzlies in a matinee clash, slated to tip at 1 p.m. PT.
