The Miss Universe grand finale happens on Tuesday (Nov. 24) at José Miguel Agrelot Coliseum in San Juan, Puerto Rico, with a start time of 8 p.m. ET/5 p.m. PT (9 p.m. AST).

The pageant broadcasts live on REELZ in English and Telemundo in Spanish, while the Miss Universe is available to livestream on Peacock in Spanish.

For cord-cutters, there are a few ways to watch the Miss Universe if you don’t have cable, especially if you want to watch for free. DirecTV has a five-day free trial, while other streaming services offer free trials, so you can watch for free.

A subscription to DirecTV gets you access to live TV, local and cable channels, including REELZ and Telemundo, at $49.99 per month for the first two months of service ($94.99 per month afterward). The service even offers a five-day trial to watch for free, if you sign up now.

Fubo is an affordable option offering a five-day free trial for new users who sign up.

Fubo starts at $63.99 for the first month ($88.99 per month afterward) with more than 220 channels, including local and cable networks, like Telemundo, that are streamable on smart TVs, smartphones, tablets and web browsers. And with a five-day free trial, you can watch for free if you act fast and sign up now.

For Miss Universe, the cable network Telemundo is available to watch with Hulu + Live TV. Pricing for the cable alternative starts at $99.99 per month for more than 100 live channels, while each plan comes with Hulu, Disney+ and ESPN Select at no additional cost. Hulu + Live TV might be best for those who want all of these streaming services together in one package.

For Telemundo, there are two different plans offered on Peacock: The Premium Plan for $12.99 per month or the Premium Plus Plan for $19.99 per month. If you’re looking to save, you can get 17% off when you do the annual plan for $129.99 per year or $199.99 per year, respectively. With the Premium Plan, it’s ad-supported and you’ll receive access to a wide range of movies, TV shows, originals, live sports from NBC Sports and live news from NBC News.

Peacock’s Premium Plus plan comes with everything in the Premium plan, no ads, your local NBC channel live and the ability to download and stream offline.
