Rams snag 49ers tight end from practice squad amid injury crisis

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Injuries have not been kind to the Rams this season.

In the team's Thanksgiving Eve win over the Packers, Los Angeles lost tight ends Davis Allen and Tyler Higbee. Allen could miss several weeks with a high ankle sprain, and Higbee is week to week.

Jack Stoll #83 of the San Francisco 49ers during warm ups before the game against the Seattle Seahawks at Lumen Field. Getty Images

To combat the numerous injuries at tight end, the Rams signed Jack Stoll off the 49ers' practice squad. Coach Sean McVay enjoys using 13 personnel, but only Colby Parkinson and Max Klare are healthy.

The signing of Stoll from the NFC West rival will give quarterback Matthew Stafford another blocker and pass catcher. The veteran tight end played in one game for the 49ers this season.

Los Angeles Rams head coach Sean McVay walks across the field before an NFL football game against the Green Bay Packers in Inglewood, Calif., Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026. AP Photo/Gregory Bull

In 2021, Stoll joined the Eagles as an undrafted free agent. He's played in 77 NFL games throughout his career, including 32 starts.

Stoll has tallied 28 career catches for 239 yards and one touchdown.

Quarterback Matthew Stafford #9 of the Los Angeles Rams takes the field before a game against the Arizona Cardinals at State Farm Stadium on November 15, 2026 in Glendale, Arizona. Getty Images

While he hasn't seen much action this season, the tight end played 15 games (four starts) for the Saints last year.
