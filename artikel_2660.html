Dan Lanning and Oregon land commitment from 4-star wide receiver during late CFP push

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Dan Lanning and Oregon land commitment from 4-star wide receiver during late CFP push appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Oregon has clawed its way back into the conversation for the College Football Playoff. They have won three straight games. Oregon dominated Michigan this past weekend, winning 31-7.

Now, Dan Lanning and Oregon have just gotten another major win, this time on the college football recruiting trail, according to Hayes Fawcett of Rivals.

"Four-Star WR Relando Jefferson has Committed to Oregon, he tells me for Rivals. The 6'1 175 WR chose the Ducks over BYU, Arizona, Fresno State, and Oregon State/ He was one of the biggest risers in the 2027 Rivals300, going from NR to a 4-Star," Fawcett posted on X, formerly Twitter.

Jefferson originally was comitted to Fresno State, but reopened his recruitment last month. Then, Jefferson took a visit to Oregon when they faced Northwestern at the end of October. Now, he is the 26th member of the Ducks class that is currently ranked fourth in the nation. His trainer, Travon Jones, believes that the Ducks got a steal in Jefferson, according to Max Torres of ScoopDuck.

"Relando is a fast, explosive, twitchy receiver with great feet and an excellent catch radius," Jones said. "He can make plays downfield and catch the ball outside his frame. He finished the season with 54 receptions, 1,215 receiving yards, and 15 touchdowns, averaging 22.5 yards per catch."

While Jefferson has risen up the rankings, he is not the top receiver in the class of 2027 for Oregon. Dakota Guerrant, the number six receiver in the nation, is also comitted to Oregon. Xavier Sabb and Malachi Garlington are also comitted to Oregon at the position. Still, there is room for Jefferson to develop.

"His development has been amazing to watch, and I'm grateful to have been part of his growth," Jones added. "The progress he's made over the past two years has been astonishing. He's continuing to grow as a receiver and refine the details of his game as he prepares for the next level. He's a focused young man who is committed to his goals," he said. "He takes his future seriously and has put in the work to grow both on and off the field mentally and spiritually."

Oregon has the top-ranked class in the Big Ten for 2027, just ahead of Ohio State.
