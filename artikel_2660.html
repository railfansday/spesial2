Controversial TV personality drops eye-popping 4-word reaction in support of Aaron Rodgers

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Aaron Rodgers went on national television and bashed Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy.

Pittsburgh quickly responded to the viral Pat McAfee interview by releasing the veteran quarterback, who was previously benched only a day ago.

Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers #8 chats with head coach Mike McCarthy before a game against the Philadelphia Eagles on Nov. 22, 2026. Getty Images

While some of the stories Rodgers told about McCarthy have been confirmed, sports media personality Ryan Clark revealed what he's been hearing around the industry.

"Not 1 lie told," Clark wrote in a post on X.

Rodgers claimed McCarthy would snore in meetings and miss walk-throughs.

A story that has been confirmed by NFL insider Jordan Schultz centers around McCarthy's mishap last Sunday when he went to break down the team and instead of saying, "1-2-3 Steelers," he said, "1-2-3 Packers."

Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers gestures at a news conference after an NFL football game against the Philadelphia Eagles in Philadelphia, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. AP Photo/Chris Szagola

Pittsburgh Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy looks on during the first quarter against the Philadelphia Eagles in Philadelphia, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. Getty Images

McCarthy served as the head coach for Green Bay from 2006-18, before he was fired in the middle of the season. Rodgers alleges McCarthy still blames the quarterback for his departure.

Things have evidently reached a boiling point in Pittsburgh, but Rodgers won't go quietly.

Neither has Clark, who was pushed out of ESPN after a heated on-air confrontation.
