Commanders’ Jayden Daniels addresses past beef with Brandon Aiyuk

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Commanders' Jayden Daniels addresses past beef with Brandon Aiyuk appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

All is well that ends well for Jayden Daniels and Brandon Aiyuk, especially now that they have reunited on the Washington Commanders.

The Commanders signed Aiyuk to the practice squad on Wednesday after he was released by the San Francisco 49ers, ending a long dispute. The 28-year-old wide receiver pushed for his move to Washington.

In the offseason, Aiyuk repeatedly urged Daniels, his former teammate at Arizona State, on social media to recruit him, which the 25-year-old quarterback grew tired of. It also got personal, with Aiyuk talking about Daniels' mother, Regina Jackson. Daniels, meanwhile, posted and deleted a message telling Aiyuk to "enjoy retirement."

Following the Commanders' acquisition of Aiyuk, Daniels said everything is water under the bridge.

"Things happen. You don't know what he was going through. You don't know what I was going through at that time," said Daniels in the video posted by NBC4 Sports' JP Finlay.

"That's somebody who looked out for me when I was 18 years old, helped me out when I first got to ASU, stuff like that. I know the character and the person he is. He is man enough to settle our differences, and we will move forward from there."

Aiyuk, who has a mercurial personality, has not played since 2024, as he has been sidelined by a knee injury.

It remains unclear when the one-time Pro Bowler will return to action, but he will definitely be a welcome addition to the offense, giving Daniels another imposing target. Aiyuk, however, will have a lot of catching up to do with the Commanders.

For now, catching up with Daniels might be the most important.
