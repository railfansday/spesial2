‘As poor as it gets’ – Weather fears for Buffalo Bills and Kansas City Chiefs game on Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

A perfect night of viewing for NFL fans on Thanksgiving could only be ruined by the weather at Highmark Stadium.

The late Thanksgiving game between the Buffalo Bills and Kansas City Chiefs could be impacted by the weather, according to latest forecasts.

Local Buffalo reporter Jon Scott of the WGRZ station reports: "Just told by our @WGRZ weather folks that conditions for #Bills and Chiefs on Thanksgiving night are going to be about as poor as it gets.

"Steady rain and upper 30s before, during, and after the game."

The National Weather Service forecasts 100 percent chance of rain on Thanksgiving night, with a temperature of around 36 degrees.

A 10 mph wind speed is also being forecast, to mix in with the rainy and cold conditions.

The inclement weather will have an impact on the fans in attendance, in addition to the players – although the Highmark Stadium roof will offer some protection.

The curved canopy covers around 65 percent of the stadium, in contrast with the Bills' previous arena, where the entire stadium was open to the elements.

Unfortunately for the players and the turf, the open-air field will still bear the brunt of the downpour.

The Chiefs are 8-2 heading in to play the Bills, who are 9-1 and top of the AFC.

Fans in Buffalo are used to bad weather, with infamous snow games in the past. And while those games can be a lot of fun for viewers at home – they are tough for attending fans.

Rainy weather can be grim all around. The conditions rarely make for peak football conditions, and the game could really put the two teams' elite running games to the test.

James Cook has more than 1,000 scrimmage yards, as does Kenneth Walker for the Chiefs, if he is healthy enough to play. Emmett Johnson has filled in impressively in his absence.
