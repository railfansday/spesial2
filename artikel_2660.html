Steelers suffer blowout loss to Bengals, fall to 6-3

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

In what was a game that displayed a severe gap between playoff hopefuls and true Super Bowl contenders, the Cincinnati Bengals defeated the Pittsburgh Steelers 38-18. At 7-2, the Bengals now have possession of first place in the AFC North, while Pittsburgh falls to 6-3.

The Bengals started the game with the ball, marching 80 yards in nine plays before three straight incompletions forced them to settle for a field goal. The Steelers answered with a three-and-out, and the Bengals then put together a second consecutive 13-play drive that resulted in a Joe Burrow touchdown pass to Drew Sample, one of four on the night for Burrow. The Steelers then went three-and-out again, and were given a gift in the form of an Evan McPherson missed field goal to keep the game at 10-0, but the offense did nothing with the chance to remain in the game, going 11 yards in five plays before punting. The Bengals then stepped on the throat of Pittsburgh, as Burrow found Chase Brown for a two-yard score to put Cincinnati up 17-0. The Steelers then put together their first real drive of the game, driving 42 yards in 10 plays to set up a Chris Boswell field goal to cut the lead to 17-3. However, the 1:25 left on the clock was more than enough for the Bengals. Burrow led a 57-yard drive that saw Dohnte Meyers catch Burrow's third touchdown pass of the night to go up 24-3 at the half.

In the third quarter, the Steelers lost four yards on seven plays to open the second half before punting. The Bengals answered with a Joe Burrow touchdown pass to Chase Brown, his second on the night, to go up 31-3. Pittsburgh would finally put a touchdown on the board with Aaron Rodgers finding DK Metcalf for a 22-yard score. Metcalf also caught the two-point conversion to make it a 31-11 game. The Bengals erased that score in just over two minutes, driving 49 yards in four plays before Samaje Perine scored to make it 38-11. A Pat Freiermuth touchdown ended the scoring, giving Cincinnati a 38-18 win.

There were plenty of noteworthy numbers that best describe just how outmatched the Steelers were. At one point in the game, the Bengals had 356 net passing yards to the Steelers' 4. Of the game's first 28 plays, 25 of them were run by the Bengals' offense. Ja'Marr Chase had more receiving yards (231) than the Steelers had total yards (221). The Steelers averaged just 3.8 yards per play, a number that saw a bump in garbage time, compared to the Bengals' 7.4.

Pittsburgh will now take their 6-3 record to Philadelphia in Week 11, a place they haven't won in 61 years.
