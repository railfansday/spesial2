Chiefs rookie clears waivers, rejoins KC's practice squad in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Kansas City Chiefs reunited with rookie defensive lineman Jack Pyburn on November 11 after waiving him earlier in the day.

According to Greg Auman of FOX Sports, Pyburn cleared waivers and signed with Kansas City's practice squad mere hours after the Chiefs had initially parted ways with him.

Pyburn played for the Tampa Bay Buccaneers in the 2026 preseason and was claimed off waivers by Kansas City on August 31. Formerly a member of the Chiefs' 53-man roster, Pyburn has yet to make his debut for Kansas City in the regular season.
