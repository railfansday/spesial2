Carolina Hurricanes Game 25: Lines, Notes, Goalies, and How to Watch vs. Lightning

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Carolina Hurricanes have one more game before Thanksgiving, facing the Tampa Bay Lightning Wednesday in Benchmark International Arena.

In the Hurricanes' last game, they won against the New York Rangers 5-2 in a game filled with action. Alexander Nikishin played perhaps his best game of the season, Jesperi Kotkaniemi threw a few punches at Matt Rempe, and surprisingly – Jaccob Slavin went to the penalty box! Some other fun things happened, too. You can read all about it here!

Meanwhile, the Lightning had a wild time against the Buffalo Sabres in their last game. You know, the same team they faced last March in a back-and-forth physical match which saw 70 penalty minutes in just the first period. Anyway, while this game wasn't as crazy, it did finish with a 6-2 loss for the Bolts and a benching of their entire top line of forwards by head coach Jon Cooper in the third period.

During practice Tuesday, Pyotr Kochetkov was in the starter's crease – indicating he will likely be the man for the Hurricanes versus the Lightning.

This season, Kochetkov's goals against average is 1.80, with a save percentage of .911.

As for the Lightning, with Dennis Hildeby having played their last game, it is more than likely that Andrei Vasilevskiy will be their starting goalie.

This season, Vasilevskiy's goals against average is 2.59, with a save percentage of .895.
