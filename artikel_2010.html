One Chris Davies quality at Birmingham City is 'beyond anything' Tom Huddlestone has seen

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Birmingham City assistant coach and former Premier League midfielder, Tom Huddlestone, has issued a stunning claim on the coaching level of current St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park boss, Chris Davies.

Davies is now in his third season in charge of Blues, having taken over in June 2024 ahead of the club's solitary League One campaign this century.

His tenure hasn't been without mixed reviews, but right now the West Midlands outfit are fourth in the Championship, having lost for just a third time this campaign on Saturday against Stoke City in underwhelming fashion.

Davies' side will be hoping for a swift response when they face lowly Burnley on Wednesday night, with it representing another chance for the 41-year-old to show why he is so highly thought of.

Davies oversaw the most remarkable first season at St. Andrew's, with his side breaking the EFL's all-time points record en route back to the Championship, before finishing 10th last term.

There were some question marks over his future following a 6-1 EFL Cup loss to Brentford in August, but aside from three away losses to Norwich City, Cardiff City and Stoke, Birmingham have looked largely impressive across all departments.

Huddlestone, 39, became one of Davies' assistants in September 2024 after a brief spell at Wigan Athletic with former Hull City teammate, Shaun Maloney.

Asked how the game has changed from a coaching perspective by another ex-Hull colleague, Aaron McLean, and Adam Virgo, the former England international heaped praise on both head coaches.

"The detail that Shaun and Chris go into is beyond anything I've seen in terms of every little detail," Huddlestone revealed.

"Full clarity on how we press, how we play.

"A lot of stuff in my career, before Marco Silva (at Hull), was generally quite off-the-cuff, to be honest," he added.

"You'd have a system, know which three or four players you wanted to get the ball, but other than that, it was like 'go and do your thing'.

"The clarity is the biggest thing, and the details, even in throw-ins and every little detail in the game is nailed down and gone through."

There has certainly been shifts in how Birmingham have played in the early weeks of the current season compared to last, with an onus on moving the ball at speed and being direct when required.

Blues averaged 53.7% possession in last season's Championship, which was the seventh-highest total, but now have the ball just 51.6% of the time inside the first 16 matches.

Although there isn't much of a difference between such numbers, the way in which Birmingham have attacked has required far less time on the ball due to the speed of forwards such as Liam Millar and Patrick Roberts, as well as the intelligence of August Priske and Jay Stansfield.

Davies also admitted pre-season that more goals were required in order to sustain a promotion bid, and such feats have certainly been the case, whilst 15 concessions mean Blues have the joint-best defensive record along with Southampton.

Huddlestone played 242 times at Premier League level under some of the game's most experienced figures, such as Steve Bruce, Harry Redknapp and Glenn Hoddle.

The former technically gifted midfielder also knows what it takes to earn promotion from the Championship, having done so with Hull 10 years ago, and such knowledge will be pivotal for Davies and Birmingham's current squad to lean on.
