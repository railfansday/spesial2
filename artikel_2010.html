Birmingham City told to 'go and get' Ipswich Town star but he's not a guaranteed starter

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Birmingham City have, again, been urged to consider a potential January move for Ipswich Town's Anis Mehmeti, although there is a potential catch that has been noted.

Blues have put themselves in a strong position in the early weeks of the Championship season to make a sustained bid for promotion to the Premier League.

Chris Davies' side have impressed in the vast majority of their encounters, boasting a real threat in attack through the sheer pace, versatility and dynamism which the 41-year-old has at his disposal, as well as a rock-solid defence.

It remains to be seen how Davies looks to add further quality in the January window, with newly-appointed director of football, John Murtough, recently setting his stall out when it comes to what supporters might expect at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park.

Many of the Blues squad have now adapted to the pressures of the Championship after a 10th-placed finish last term, but they could have had further quality and experience amongst their ranks in the summer.

Mehmeti, 25, signed for the Tractor Boys in January in a cut-price deal with Bristol City, having continuously lit up the Championship stage at Ashton Gate.

The Albanian international was integral in the Robins' play-off finish of two seasons ago with 12 goals in 42 games, before helping Ipswich return to the Premier League in May under Kieran McKenna.

Despite never playing in the top-flight, Mehmeti was one player who generated exit talk, with all of Birmingham, Derby County and Wrexham interested in his services, before he snubbed the idea of a Championship return, although he hasn't made a single one of Gary O'Neil's Premier League matchday squads.

It was recently claimed by Football League World's Birmingham City fan pundit, Jason Moore, that Mehmeti, who netted for Ipswich in their EFL Cup loss to Arsenal in September, may regret the aforementioned decision.

The playmaker is still under contract with the Suffolk side until June 2029, so it remains to be seen if a temporary or permanent exit remains viable in January.

Moore has retained his stance that a deal could be on the table for Blues to discover, although Mehmeti may not receive the minutes he once could have at St. Andrew's.

"I still think Blues should get him, because there's clearly a player there, and at this level, he is a good player," he told FLW.

"But I don't think if we sign him in January, he's a guaranteed starter. I think if he signed in the summer, he probably would have warranted it just because of what he did last season.

"I don't think he's a guaranteed starter now, with how good Jay Stansfield's been, how vital he actually is to how we play and what he offers. And, when January comes, how much football would Mehmeti have played? Not a lot. It's going to take him a while to get up to speed," Moore added.

"I don't think he'd be a guaranteed starter. Most of the players, even on the left wing, where he could probably do a job, you've got Liam Millar and Demarai Gray doing a decent job there.

"I don't think he'd be a guaranteed starter, which isn't a bad thing because that means we've got more than one good player in most positions then.

"I'd still say, 'go and get him', because there's a player there who's a very good player at this level, and he offers us something different, but no, he won't be a guaranteed starter. He'd have to get up to scratch with what Chris Davis wants, how we play and probably match fitness at the same time.

Stansfield has performed to a much stronger level this term after previously adjusting to his role as a '10', although the club-record buy recently flourished against Millwall and Swansea City in the main centre-forward role due to August Priske's absence.
