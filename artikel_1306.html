Vitor Matos Set To Leave Swansea City for RC Strasbourg

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

GIVEMESPORT understands Vitor Matos is expected to leave Swansea City for RC Strasbourg, but the Welsh club are disappointed by the decision.

There has been growing speculation about the future of the Portuguese coach in recent times, with the Ligue 1 club wanting Matos to succeed Hugo Oliveira. Strasbourg saw Gary O’Neil leave in July, but his replacement has won just three of 10 league matches this season, leaving the French club just two points above the relegation zone.

Swansea, on the other hand, have picked up 26 points in the Championship, taking them up to sixth in the league, with eight wins from 15. They are only four points behind Birmingham City, at the top of the table.

GMS understands that the compensation package for Matos is £5m. Despite that influx of money on the horizon, the Swansea board are reluctantly accepting the 38-year-old's exit. The Welsh side are disappointed by Matos' impending exit, having given him a new contract as well as financial support in the transfer market during the summer.

The Portuguese manager was appointed in November 2025 on a deal until 2029, but signed a one-year extension in the summer. At the time, he spoke of building towards an exciting future, telling the club's website:

“This is something very special, it is a feeling myself and the players love, and the desire to keep building that and sharing great moments together is one of the key motivations in extending my contract with the club.

“We put good foundations in place last season, but now we have to look forward, we have to keep pushing and making progress."

Now, though, he is set to leave the Championship side and embark on a new adventure with Strasbourg, Chelsea's sister club.

Swansea will have to plan for a future without Matos, starting this weekend with a match at home to West Brom on Saturday. A trip to Middlesbrough follows, before a South Wales derby against Cardiff in the Welsh capital.

The Swans took a chance on a relatively inexperienced Matos, as his only previous senior management job came in charge of Maritimo in Liga Portugal 2. Prior to that, though, he had worked as part of Jurgen Klopp's staff at Liverpool. He later spent time with Pepijn Lijnders at Red Bull Salzburg in the Austrian Bundesliga.
