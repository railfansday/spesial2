Similarweb (SMWB) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

First positive GAAP profit since its IPO as AI demand reshapes the business.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Similarweb Ltd. (SMWB +13.17%) reported third quarter results that included the company's first positive GAAP net income since its initial public offering. Management attributed the performance to accelerating demand for real-time digital data to support artificial intelligence models and enterprise decision-making. The company reported a transition in leadership, with Michael Akkerman assuming the role of Chief Executive Officer as the company shifts its focus toward larger enterprise engagements and high-value data partnerships. CFO Ran Vered noted that the company is successfully implementing operational disciplines to offset foreign exchange headwinds while maintaining flat headcount to drive incremental margins.
