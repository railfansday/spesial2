IceCure: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The clinical timeline is slipping as partner site contracts move slowly, putting the 400-patient ChoICE target at risk for investors weighing growth against losses.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 18, 2026 at 3:13PM EST

On its third-quarter and nine-month 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 18, 2026, IceCure (ICCM +25.29%) announced that total revenue rose 26% to $2.6 million, driven by demand for ProSense, its system that freezes and destroys tumors. Long-term investors must weigh accelerating domestic sales momentum against an operating loss of $12.9 million and extended clinical study timelines. Three things management said stand out.

Domestic adoption of ProSense is gaining speed as regional sales infrastructure takes hold. U.S. revenue surged 83% year over year in the first nine months of 2026, leading sales leadership to project sequential quarterly gains.

Deploying dedicated sales and clinical support professionals in key regions like California and Florida allows the business to reach more medical institutions. This expanded commercial network provides the infrastructure required to train physicians and drive utilization.

Generating clinical evidence through the ChoICE study is essential to building physician confidence and securing long-term adoption. However, contract negotiations at partner sites are progressing slowly, threatening the target of treating 400 patients within three years.

Commercial adoption of ProSense is accelerating alongside regulatory approvals in China, though clinical and sales investments expanded net loss to $12.9 million. Management is prioritizing pricing discipline and physician engagement to sustain growth.

Expanding direct sales personnel and broadening international distributor partnerships position the business to turn early adoption into recurring revenue.
