Picture: Ronaldinho joins Ravenna teammates for dinner before first training

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Brazilian legend Ronaldinho has just completed his first training session with Ravenna, after being spotted last night at a restaurant with his teammates.

Former Barcelona and Milan star Ronaldinho will come back from retirement tomorrow, Sunday, November 15, more than ten years after his last competitive match.

Ronaldinho will start in a Serie C game against Forlì tomorrow, which Football Italia will cover on our website and YouTube channel.

Ronaldinho landed in Italy on Friday and went to dinner with his Ravenna teammates last night, as reported by La Casa di C.

Un post condiviso da LA CASA DI C (@lacasadic_)

The legendary 46-year-old attacker trained with his teammates for the first time at Ravenna’s stadium today, Saturday, November 14.

Ronaldinho played in Italy from 2008 to 2011, scoring 26 goals and providing 27 assists in 95 appearances across all competitions for Serie A giant Milan.
