‘Club Kid’ Star Reggie Absolom on His Friendship With Jordan Firstman and Missing Out on a ‘Harry Potter’ Role: ‘J.K. Rowling Is a No Anyway’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Walking the red carpet at the Cannes Film Festival and receiving a seven-minute standing ovation is an emotional event for anyone — but especially when you’re 14, and it’s your first time acting in a feature film. Such was the case for “Club Kid” star Reggie Absolom. But Absolom was determined to appear as cool, calm and collected as the blue waters of the Mediterranean Sea.

“Jordan Firstman was on my left, Cara Delevingne was on my right, and I was crying so much,” Absolom tells Variety about standing through the applause that followed the film’s premiere screening. “The first thing Cara says to me, she looks at me and goes, ‘I know you were crying,’ because I was trying to hold the tears because I knew cameras were on me.” Related Stories Elizabeth Holmes’ Partner Billy Evans Writes Her an Emotional Thanksgiving Love Letter: ‘Our Most Beautiful Days Are Still Ahead of Us’ Iranian Activist and 'It Was Just an Accident' Screenwriter Mehdi Mahmoudian Arrested in Tehran on Charges of 'Anti-Government Propaganda'

Despite the crowd’s cheers, it wasn’t until Absolom found out that A24 (his preference, “because they do pretty good things for indie movies”) had outmaneuvered the competition (with a whopping bid of $17 million) that he fully realized, “OK, people like this film.”

That’s a little bit of an understatement. “Club Kid” was nominated for best feature at the Gothams and has delivered impressive box office numbers since its limited release on Nov. 6; in its second week in theaters, Firstman’s film tallied an impressive $28,002 per-screen average. To date, “Club Kids” has grossed more than $5 million globally.

In the film, Absolom plays Arlo, the 10-year-old son of a washed-up party promoter named Peter (Firstman), whose excessive drug habits end his tenure running New York City’s queer nightlife scene. When Arlo shows up on his father’s doorstep, they are both forced to grow up and figure out how to navigate the rest of their lives.

The teenage British actor won the role after auditioning for HBO’s upcoming “Harry Potter” series. Absolom reached the final stage of auditions to join the wizarding world, but didn’t get the job. So, U.K. casting director Lucy Bevan mentioned him to her fellow casting director Carmen Cuba, who had been searching for the perfect youngster to play Arlo. Aside from the fact that both boys are British, Absolom and his character seem to have quite a bit in common.

“I love to get good at things,” Absolom says, listing the similarities between himself and Arlo, whose artistic aspirations are focused on DJing, instead of acting. “Obviously, getting there is a bit annoying, because I want to be good at everything the first time I try it. But even if I’m not, I will keep doing it until I am good at it.”

Read on to find out how a potential 10 years in Hogwarts turned into 26 days immersed in New York City’s indie sleaze scene, what Absolom learned from filming his first major movie and how he’s processing the Oscar buzz.

I don’t remember the exact words, but someone told me that having this performance as a kid shows that I will only get better. And someone told me that this is one of the performances of the year. That meant loads.

Josh Hutcherson. He was with Jordan in New York. He sat behind me in the screening, and he seemed like he was really enjoying it.

I mean, they’re pretty fun. I don’t stay for ages because I like to get myself a good night’s sleep, but I walk around, talk to people a bit, maybe have some Diet Coke or something like that. But they’re pretty fun. The music’s actually always pretty good.

I DJed in London, which was really fun. It’s a really cool club because you have to walk through this fully graffitied-up tunnel to get to it.

I learned during the movie, with Saturn Risin9. In the scene where she’s teaching me, she’s actually teaching me how to do things.

I mean, not like Peter or anything like that. I won’t be going weekly. Maybe once every month or two with a good group of friends could be fun.

The fact that he can let out so much emotion all at once. And then the next day, he wants to be such a good son, so he makes Jordan the playlist that they can add to together because he really wants to be a good kid so much, even though his parents before weren’t so good to him.

“Sex with Me” by Rihanna. That was on the top. I remember that. Basically, all the songs that are in the film. There were a few more songs that when I first listened to them, I was like, “Oh, I’m not sure.” Now I’m like, “Oh my God, some of these are my favorite songs.”

He seemed like a really kind guy, and the directions that he gave me during the auditions were really easy to understand and really easy to accomplish. So, I was really happy that I might be able to work with a director who can give me simple, easy-to-understand directions, instead of using big words in a direction that aren’t needed when you could just put it simpler. [It] will just make it harder for the actor to do it because they might be overthinking it. That’s what he never wanted anyone to do: overthink doing the lines. He just said, “Just do it how you would.”

We are super good friends after this whole press tour. This has only strengthened our bond, because we would have such random conversations and laugh about the stupidest things on set, like memes that we found on TikTok that we both know and we still do. There are so many things, so many references that we’ll say to each other that we just laugh about way too much before an interview. I bet there’s some interviews that are out there where we’re holding back laughter at the very beginning because we had just said something really stupid before the person walked in.

The first day is blocked out of my memory because all I remember is the second day, which is the throwing-up scene. Second day, and they make me throw up on camera. Not a good first impression.

The pizza that we ate was nice. It was pretty good, but then they had to blend it up with applesauce. It was cold but also warm; it was lumpy as hell, and it kind of stank too. There was a cup of it next to me because I had to drink it just before. Record, “Action!” And then they were like, “Wait, wait, wait! We need to move the slide.” I was like, “I have it in my mouth, guys. Hurry up!” And then I swear, I wish someone had taken a photo of me after every take to see how the color drained from my face because I was genuinely gonna be sick if I didn’t take a break. After the third take, I was like, “Guys, give me a break. I’m sitting outside. Just give me a second.”

I hadn’t been on set for that long — to the point where I’d been around adults for five weeks straight, not talking to kids. I had a PlayStation in my apartment so I would play with my friends. I would talk to them, and I could act like a kid because I wasn’t just around adults.

The music taste has to be one. But Arlo also seems like he wants to be, like, a really good DJ and he really wants to be good at skateboarding. And I love to be good. I love to get good at things. Obviously, getting there is a bit annoying because I want to be good at everything the first time I try it. But even if I’m not, I will keep doing it until I am good at it.

Because I used to play football, like what you call soccer [Absolom puts on an American accent], and on the way, because my dad was driving me, he played “Freak on a Leash” by Korn. I was like, “OK, this is making me feel something that I’ve never felt before.” It was like anger, but good anger. It was weird. But then I was like, “OK, I need to get more of this in my playlist” and I deleted all the ’90s and 2000s rap that I had in my playlist, swapped it out for metal and then recently swapped it out for Cocteau Twins.

Cocteau Twins. Part Time. You heard of them? No? No one has. It says they have 300,000 monthly listeners and I haven’t met a single one of them. Pinback. Earth, Wind and Fire. The song that’s like “the 21st night of September.”

OK! Yeah, Jordan and I have one. [The pair’s collaborative playlist was released on Spotify.]

Well, I feel like they go out somewhere, Oscar, Peter, Arlo, as a family, kind of. I don’t know where they would go. Where’s a place that they’d all like to go? I feel like Nicky (Eldar Isgandarov) and Evelyn (Colleen Camp) are there for some reason. I feel like they want to tag along because maybe they just have a day out. Maybe they go to Times Square or something for a day. And then I feel like just Arlo and Peter go to a record store again.

That no matter what’s happened to you, if you’ve had maybe a bad year, maybe you’ve had a bad few years, you’ve still got your whole life ahead of you. Don’t throw it away just because of the few bad years that you’ve had.

Oh my gosh! I’d be happy if we got nominated for one award, no matter how big it is. I’d be so happy if I got nominated for any award. Obviously, winning is a humongous bonus, but I’d just be happy to be there and experience it. Winning isn’t the most important thing, but it would be fun. I like to win.

Definitely bringing my mom, of course, because she’s like my [personal assistant] as well as my mom. Definitely bring my dad too. How many plus-ones do I get? I think my sister would want to come, maybe just to be in the place. Maybe not so much for me, but she would want to come for me and to see it. Probably just my real close family and one of my aunts because she is obsessed. She’s like, “Can I chaperone you? Can I come with you?” I feel like I’d bring her because she’s obsessed with New York, and I feel like she’d love L.A. as well.

I just want to do new stuff, like anything that comes to me, really. So, like, I’m not 100% sure what will come next.

“Harry Potter” is out the window. “Harry Potter” got me here, and I thank it for that. But I think we don’t worry about it anymore.

I’d rather do this. “Harry Potter” would have been a cool set to be on because it would have looked cool, but there would have been so many more people; I wouldn’t have gotten to bond with everyone. I loved everyone on this set. I had such a good time filming it, and I think J.K. Rowling’s a no anyway, but Jordan is a big yes.
