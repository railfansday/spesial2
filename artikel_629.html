‘GTA 6’: First Half-Hour Reveals Major Plot Twist Rockstar Has Managed to Keep Secret for Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

SPOILER ALERT: This article contains major spoilers for Rockstar Games’ “Grand Theft Auto 6” through the first 30 minutes of gameplay.

“Grand Theft Auto 6″ debuted Thursday at midnight local time around the world, and, as many expected, it’s already the main topic of conversation capturing the zeitgeist. While there are several early parts of the open-world title that are wowing gamers and internet onlookers alike, one key plot point revealed in the first 30 minutes of “GTA 6” gameplay is getting particular praise, as it shows the impressive lengths Rockstar Games went to keep the twist under wraps through years of intentional marketing and unofficial leaks.

Protagonists Jason Duval (Dylan Rourke) and Lucia Caminos (Manni L. Perez) have a son: Baby “Tommy” Tomas.

“GTA 6” opens with a flashback to a double-grand-theft-auto meet-cute between Jason and Lucia. He goes to steal a car that she’s already stolen, the cops come and love blooms. Through a montage of their relationship, we see their happy times and things fall apart as Lucia wants to get more involved in some of Jason’s criminal activities and he refuses to bring her along.

After the two seemingly split, we see Lucia sitting in a restaurant ordering only water when she decides to rob a payday loan shop across the street. She’s still living in their fictional state of Leonida (inspired by Florida), but she’s on her own and hurting for cash after being suspended from her rideshare driver job.

The crime is a bust, and we catch up to Lucia in present day, where she’s in prison for the attempted armed robbery. She’s now up for a house-arrest-type program, and we learn she wants to go live with Jason when she’s out. We also learn that he’s the “father” of her child.

Cut back to the day when Lucia tried to rob the loan shop, and she’s seen screaming about how she needs to go back to the restaurant. It’s then revealed to players that Lucia left her infant son, Tomas, behind in the restaurant across the street and he’s crying, surrounded by restaurant employees while waiting for her to return.

From here, it’s up to “GTA 6” players to decide how they want Lucia and Jason (who in present day knows he has a son but has still never met him) to live their lives when they reunite upon her release. Tommy is in the custody of Lucia’s aunt, but will he stay that way, depending on how you play?

The Tomas twist adds an emotional arc to the game that the franchise hasn’t seen before and comes as returning “GTA” players are in a different stage of their lives (many now likely parents) than they were when “GTA 5” came out 13 years ago. It heightens the stakes for a “GTA” player who now has to evaluate their choices beyond the fun of stealing cars and robbing banks.

Official “GTA 6” sales figures have yet to be revealed, though Take-Two Interactive CEO Strauss Zelnick has assured the Rockstar Games’ results are forthcoming, early data from market intelligence firm Sensor Tower estimates more than $2 billion in pre-order sales alone.

The game currently has a 99 score on Metacritic, tying it for the highest-rated video game of all time with 1998’s “The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.”
