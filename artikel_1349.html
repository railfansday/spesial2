Football Italia Show: Inter & Napoli’s obvious problems, will Roma humiliate Man Utd?

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Football Italia Show returns after a packed weekend of action: Lorenzo Bettoni and Richard Hall examine Milan’s 3-1 derby della Madonnina win over Inter, Juventus’ victory over Napoli, and more.

Another eventful weekend of football provides plenty to discuss, from Milan’s derby victory over Inter to Juventus’ impressive win against Napoli.

In this latest video, we look at Inter’s worrying weaknesses and whether Cristian Chivu has some serious problems to address.

We also examine Napoli’s struggles under Massimiliano Allegri after Kerim Alajbegovic and Jeremie Boga helped Juventus secure a 2-0 victory. Allegri is already under pressure, but should Napoli consider making a change?

Finally, we look ahead to Roma’s challenge against Manchester United. Can the Giallorossi exploit United’s weaknesses and deliver another embarrassing European night for the English giants?
