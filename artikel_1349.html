Unai Emery is edging closer to committing his long-term future to Aston Villa, with owners Nassef Sawiris and Wes Edens reportedly playing a major role in convincing the Spaniard that Villa Park remains the right place for him.

Emery has transformed Villa since arriving in 2022, taking the club from the lower half of the Premier League into regular European competition and delivering Europa League glory last season. Unsurprisingly, that success has attracted attention from some of Europe’s biggest clubs.

However, Villa are now moving to ensure their manager remains at the heart of their ambitious project.

According to Football Insider’s latest report on Emery’s new deal, former Villa chief executive Keith Wyness believes Emery’s relationship with Sawiris and Edens has been fundamental to his decision to stay.

The new agreement is expected to represent a significant financial commitment and could extend Emery’s stay beyond his existing 2029 contract.

Wyness pointed towards the club’s development on and off the pitch, including major improvements to Villa Park and their training facilities, as reasons why Emery has little incentive to walk away.

The Spaniard has been linked with several elite jobs, including Real Madrid and Manchester United, but appears comfortable with the control and backing he currently enjoys at Villa.

The most important aspect of this situation is not simply money.

Emery has considerable influence at Aston Villa and works closely with an ownership group that appears prepared to shape the sporting project around him. That level of trust can be difficult for even elite managers to find at bigger clubs.

A CaughtOffside report on Emery’s contract talks recently revealed that Villa were already closing in on an agreement amid Manchester United interest, with both parties keen to continue working together.

That makes staying an understandable decision.

Emery could potentially earn greater prestige by eventually taking another elite job, but Villa currently offer something just as valuable: stability, authority and owners who clearly trust his judgement.

There is always a chance an offer from Real Madrid or another European giant eventually changes the equation. A new contract cannot completely eliminate that possibility.

For now, though, Villa have done exactly what an ambitious club should do. Instead of waiting for someone else to tempt their manager away, they are giving Emery every reason to believe he can continue building something significant at Villa Park.
