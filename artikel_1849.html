Jarrad Branthwaite's price tag revealed by ex-Everton employee amid Man United 'interest'

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Manchester United would need to spend between £70-80million to sign Jarrad Branthwaite from Everton.

That is according to former senior Everton scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The Red Devils hold a historical interest in the 24-year-old, and had a £50m bid turned down in the summer of 2024.

Branthwaite has returned from injury this season and is in fine form, with Man United potentially set to test Everton's stance once more.

Mauricio Pochettino is expected to demand a new centre-back in the January transfer window, with his current options having made mistake after mistake this season so far.

Branthwaite signed a new five-year contract in 2025 and will certainly not come cheap, especially if a move is eyed for the January transfer window.

Branthwaite looked like being one of England's next best centre-backs when he broke through in the 2023-24 season.

And he still could be, if he can shake off the injury issues that plagued him throughout the last campaign for good.

"Listen, if the team's doing reasonably well and the player's doing well, then there's always going to be interest from the so-called bigger clubs," King told Football Insider.

"He's playing in front of 50,000 every week now. Everton are progressing as a team. They're getting results. The side's doing quite well.

"He's doing well. Again, he's the player that impressed me from day one. The only problem with him, of course, he's been a bit injury-prone, but that's no fault of his at times, but there will be interest.

"After watching Man United on Sunday, wow, they need a back four, not just a centre-half."

"Just remember, John Stones came to Everton for £1.8m and went to Manchester City for £50m," King added. "So I'd put a figure anywhere between £70-80m on Branthwaite if he's going to leave."

Though he never pushed for the move, Branthwaite did reportedly agree personal terms with Man United in 2024.

And it is hard to blame him - the move to Old Trafford would mean more money and more fame.

Even more so in the last two years, however, Man United has become a graveyard for careers, for both managers and players.

It remains unclear if Branthwaite would be willing to take up the offer again if it came in the coming months.
