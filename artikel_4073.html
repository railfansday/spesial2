Zelda Ocarina's ReDead Were Set To Be "Very, Very Scary" In The Remake, But Nintendo Decided To "Dial It Back"

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The team maybe went "a step too far", according to the producer

Ocarina of Time Remake isn't really a scary game, but there are still some enemies throughout this adventure that may give players a fright.

Perhaps the most notable are ReDead, the zombie-like creatures that are difficult to escape when they set their sights on Link. These return in the remake and apparently were even scarier at one point during development, until Nintendo decided to "dial it back".

Here's what producer Hidemaro Fujibayashi had to say about this during a recent interview with GameSpot:

"With this remake, the graphics are a lot improved, and so the ReDead–the sound, the graphics, and the movement–ended up being very, very scary. And the staff wanted to make this in service of making a good gameplay experience. But from our perspective, taking a step back, we felt like, let’s remember the experience that we don’t want to change, and taking that into consideration, maybe we’ve gone a step too far, so let’s dial it back."

So, the decision was made to ensure the experience was more in line with the original game. The final result is the ReDead are still somewhat scary, but not "very, very scary" to the point it changes the experience.
