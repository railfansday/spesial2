The Killers Guitarist Dave Keuning Survived ‘Major Stroke’ With ‘40% Chance of Survival’: ‘I Can Play Guitar Just as Good as Before’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Dave Keuning, guitarist for The Killers, said that he survived a “major stroke” two weeks ago that had a “40% chance of survival” but he’s still able to “play guitar just as good as before.” He revealed the news on Thanksgiving, along with announcing the birth of his daughter.

“Happy Thanksgiving, everyone! Three months ago, we had our baby girl, Odette,” Keuning wrote. “Two weeks ago, I had a major stroke, and thanks to my wife acting fast, I went straight to the hospital and into surgery. The first hour after a stroke is critical when it comes to getting to the hospital. I had a blood clot in my brain that cut off the blood supply to the entire right side of my brain. By the time I arrived at the hospital, I was blind in one eye and had lost function on my left side, but I still wasn’t able to realize I was having a stroke. Keuning thanked his Killers bandmates, including lead singer Brandon Flowers, drummer Ronnie Vannucci Jr. and bassist Mark Stoermer, for their support during his recovery.

“And yes I can play guitar just as good as before,” Keuning wrote. “It was the first thing I checked when I woke up from surgery. I feel extremely fortunate that I am alive and well, and that I can use my hands and feet and talk just like before. I am thankful for my wife, my son, my daughter, and all of my bandmates, who I feel closer to than ever.”
