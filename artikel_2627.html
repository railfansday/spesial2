Jerry Jones defends Mike McCarthy's Cowboys tenure after Aaron Rodgers claims

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Jerry Jones had plenty to reflect upon after the Dallas Cowboys lost to the Philadelphia Eagles 39-38 on Thanksgiving Day Thursday, Nov. 26.

Speaking to reporters after the loss, Jones said he wanted to keep former head coach Mike McCarthy an additional season after his five-year contract expired after 2024. McCarthy, now in his first season coaching the Pittsburgh Steelers, was replaced by Brian Schottenheimer.

"I offered him to stay at least another year when he left," Jones told reporters. "And so I thought he was outstanding as the head coach and had a great command of, frankly, (the) quarterback, (the) offense. (I) can't say enough good things about him. He's, frankly, one of the finest people around football that I've been around. And I can't say enough about him. I never had the type of thing that is being said about him. I never had that in consideration in any of my time with him, extending his contract. Any of that, that was never on the table. And we never had an issue within the organization."

A day earlier, now former Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers accused McCarthy of falling asleep during team meetings and skipping practices this season and in the past, dating back to the quarterback-head coach pairing's with the Green Bay Packers from 2008 to 2018. Rodgers said McCarthy recently broke down a halftime huddle with "3, 2, 1 ... Packers," which prompted the quarterback to go scorched earth during an interview with "The Pat McAfee Show." The Steelers released Rodgers later in the day and he cleared waivers Thursday. (McCarthy did not deny the incident.)

In five seasons with the Cowboys, McCarthy missed the playoffs in his first and final seasons but rattled off three consecutive 12-5 campaigns that resulted in playoff berths. However, his team was 1-3 in the postseason.

McCarthy and the Steelers are playing the "Black Friday" game Nov. 27 at 3 p.m. ET against the Denver Broncos.
