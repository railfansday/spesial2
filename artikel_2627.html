North Carolina basketball lands commitment from top 2027 prospect CJ Rosser

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The top boys basketball player in the 2027 cycle is officially off of the board and he's headed to Chapel Hill, North Carolina.

On Monday afternoon, Nov. 23, Southeastern Prep (Orlando, FL) five-star power forward CJ Rosser announced his commitment to North Carolina, choosing the Tar Heels over Arkansas, Kentucky, Louisville, NC State and a long list of other Power Four programs that were in pursuit.

North Carolina was the first of seven schools that received an official visit from Rosser during the fall. He was last in Chapel Hill on Sept. 11.

The 6-foot-10, 200-pounder is tied as the No. 1 overall prospect in the 2027 recruiting cycle, according to the latest USA TODAY Sports composite ranking. He's tied for first with his Southeastern Prep teammate Beckham Black.

Rosser, who was named to USA TODAY Sports preseason All-America team, is coming off of a massive junior season that saw him average 16.7 points and 6.7 rebounds per game. Southeastern Prep has already played 18 games this season and he's averaging a team-high 23.9 points and and 8.1 rebounds for the 16-2 Falcons.

North Carolina only has two commitments in its 2027 class but it holds pledges from two of the best prospects in the cycle.

Compass Prep (Chandler, AZ) five-star small forward DeMarcus Henry announced his commitment to North Carolina on Oct. 30. Henry is ranked as the No. 3 overall prospect in the composite ranking.

The recruiting wins have complimented the success that new head coach Michael Malone and the Tar Heels have seen so far on the court this season. North Carolina is 4-1 on the year at the time of Rosser's commitment.
