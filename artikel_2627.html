Orioles linked to blockbuster trade for former MVP candidate

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Baltimore Orioles enter the MLB offseason looking for ways to reshape their roster after missing the postseason for a second consecutive year.

With the organization exploring opportunities to add impact talent, a potential trade target has emerged who could make a significant difference in the lineup.

According to the report, the Orioles have held discussions with the Arizona Diamondbacks about a potential trade for second baseman Ketel Marte. No deal was imminent as of Saturday night, and neither team has publicly confirmed the conversations.

Marte would represent a major addition for Baltimore. The three-time All-Star has finished among the top five in National League MVP voting twice during his career and has established himself as one of the more productive offensive infielders in baseball.

The 33-year-old is coming off a 2026 season in which he appeared in 141 games, hitting .249 with 22 home runs, 76 RBIs and a .748 OPS. Those numbers were down from his previous production, but Marte would still give the Orioles an experienced bat with a proven track record.

Arizona has reportedly been open to considering trade offers for Marte, who has four guaranteed seasons remaining on his contract. However, any potential deal would come with an important complication: Marte has 10-and-5 rights, giving him the ability to veto a trade.

One of the biggest questions surrounding a possible trade is where Marte would fit on Baltimore's roster. The Orioles already have several infield options, including Jackson Holliday at second base, creating a potential logjam if Marte were added.

That situation has not necessarily stopped the organization from exploring the possibility. President of baseball operations Mike Elias acknowledged during the General Manager's Meetings that Baltimore has discussed having some infielders work in the outfield this offseason and during Spring Training.

The Orioles have also demonstrated a willingness to make significant financial commitments, signing Pete Alonso to a five-year, $155 million contract last offseason. The organization has additional flexibility through its collection of prospects and could consider moving players from its current roster to complete a major trade.

A deal for Marte would be another significant move as Baltimore attempts to return to postseason contention in 2027. Nothing is close at this stage, but the reported discussions suggest the Orioles are exploring opportunities to make a substantial addition this winter.
