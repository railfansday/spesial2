Sergey Bilostenniy Outpoints Vadim Nemkov to Become PFL Heavyweight Champion

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Sergey Bilostenniy was the superior fighter in the main event ofPFL Dubai at Coca-Cola Arena in Dubai, UAE, on Saturday, as hewrested the PFL heavyweight championship from reigning champ VadimNemkov.Bilostenniy (16-4) controlled the pace for much of the five roundsand kept Nemkov (20-3, 1 NC) at bay with timely head punches, mixedwith several leg kicks. Nemkov, a former Bellator light heavyweightchampion, tried to use his grappling prowess early and nearlylanded a belly-down armbar in the first, but Bilostenniy was wiseand kept the fight off the canvas the rest of the way.Bilostenniy busted up Nemkov's face with jabs and wore his fellowRussian countryman down, and when the final sounded, there was nodoubt as to who the victor was. In the end, two of the cagesidejudges favored Bilostenniy with scores of 48-47, while the thirdsaw it for him 49-46, ending Nemkov's 10-year winning streak.

Rising welterweight contender Patrick Habirora soared to 10-0 withan impressive first-round technical submission of Egypt's Omar ElDafrawy. The two fighters tore into each other early and often, butafter a wild scramble on the ground, "The Belgian Bomber" locked in a guillotine, stood up, squeezed harder, and put ElDafrawy (16-7) to sleep. The end came at 3:11 of the stanza, givingHabirora his biggest win thus far.Oleg Popov made it look easy against fellow heavyweight PouyaRahmani, dominating him for three rounds. Popov (24-2) landed a fewdecent strikes on the feet, but most of the fight took place on theground, where Rahmani (5-1) was overwhelmed. All three judgesfavored Popov with scores of 30-27, 30-26 and 30-25.Joji Goto scored arguably the biggest win of his MMA career bydominating longtime UFC veteran Sergio Pettis for three rounds atbantamweight, winning a lopsided unanimous decision. Goto (21-9)landed the far better strikes, controlled the pace, and dominatedPettis (25-9) on the ground. In the end, Goto won the contest viatallies of 30-27 across the board.Lewis McGrillen-Evans landed dozens of punches to the head and bodyand then landed a knee to Nawras Abzakh's face as the first roundended, and that was all the Englishman needed to soar to 14-2.Abzakh (15-7) stayed on his knees and bowed out after the round, givingMcGrillen-Evans the TKO win via retirement.Connor Hughes and Shaqueme Rock (11-4-1) had plenty of beef headinginto their lightweight grudge match, and it was Hughes whoprevailed. After a tactical opening round, Hughes (13-3) detonateda perfect left hook-right cross combo to Rock's head, knockinghim out cold. The end came just 64 ticks into the second.Saygid Izagakhmaev improved to 23-4 when he submitted DavidMartinez late in the third round of a closely contestedwelterweight skirmish. Izagakhmaev seized Martinez's back andlocked in a body triangle, then nabbed the rear-naked choke, forcing the tap at the 4:27mark. Martinez dipped to 17-7.In a spirited lightweight tilt, Russia's Akhmed Magomedov prevailedvia unanimous decision over previously undefeated Alexei Pergande.Each round was as close as they came, but it was the higher strikevolume and pressure from Magomedov (11-2) that impressed the judgesenough to award him the win via scores of 29-28 (twice) and 30-27.Pergande fell to 8-1.Adam Borcis scored a sizeable upset, submitting previously unbeatenKhasan Magomedsharipov in the first round. The featherweights sizedeach other up early, but when Magomedsharipov (11-1) scored atakedown, he became ensnared in a triangle during a scramble.Borics (21-4) kept switching between the triangle choke andtriangle armbar, landing elbows to the top of the Russian's headuntil Magomedsharipov tapped out. The end came at 4:17 of theround, giving Borics the win via triangle armbar.Denis Goltsov was too much for Maxwell Djantou Nana (9-3) to handleas he vanquished him in the first. Goltsov (38-9) peppered Nanawith jabs continually and picked him apart on the feet. Goltsovdropped Nana with a right cross to the head and then finished him off moments later with follow-up punches, endingthe heavyweight encounter at the 3:41 mark.Ernesto Rodriguez had a point deducted in the first for an illegalknee to a downed opponent, but he made sure that didn't factor intothe end result. Once the fight resumed after the penalty, Rodriguez(12-2) immediately dropped welterweight opponent Rhys McKee(15-8-1) with an overhand right and then finished him off with a flurry ofpunches. The end came at 2:28 of the stanza.Unbeaten light heavyweight contender Khabib Nabiev had his handsfull for two rounds before finally putting away Styve Ngono Essono.Though Essono (8-2) had a point deducted in the first for anaccidental eye poke, the Cameroonian dominated Nabiev on the feetand held his own on the ground. However, just moments into thethird, Nabiev (13-0) scored an easy takedown, quickly moved to top control and landed several elbows to acovered-up Essono, forcing a referee stoppage just 46 secondsinto the frame.Alexsandra Savicheva improved to 9-2 with a unanimous decision winover Jacinta Austin (9-3) at strawweight. Savicheva landed the farbetter strikes throughout the contest and won a unanimous decisionwith scores of 30-27 (twice) and 29-28.
