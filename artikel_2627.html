Houston Rockets vs. Philadelphia 76ers game preview

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Houston Rockets get an actual schedule advantage game tonight. After a few "Houston gets two days off and their opponents get one" matchups that technically favored the Rockets, the Philadelphia 76ers are coming in on the second night of a back-to-back after facing the Pacers in Indiana. Joel Embiid is questionable for that game and Jaylen Brown is out, which puts both on track to play tonight in Houston.

Philly only has one road loss as I write this, which is tied for the fewest in the NBA. The Rockets only have one home loss, which is also tied for the fewest in the NBA. So tonight is a game of strength. As the Rockets are in the middle of a five-game (and it could be six after Games 81 and 82 are announced) homestand, it continues to be important for Houston to win as many games as possible in the friendly confines of Toyota Center. As difficult as Houston's schedule has been so far, the Rockets have the tenth most difficult remaining schedule. The ten easiest remaining schedules all belong to teams in the Eastern Conference, even though I was told six ways to Sunday that the East was better than the West this season.

You already know the Sixers lineup, and Tyrese Maxey has seemingly taken the mantle as the go-to guy. He wrecked Houston last year in one of several "the Rockets led late but allowed their opponent to score every time down the floor when they only needed a single stop" contests. It was also one of Houston's many OT losses. That game was in Philadelphia, and Houston won the return matchup at home. Again, both teams look different now, but this is a game with a lot of starpower. It should be fun.
