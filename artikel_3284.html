Alix Earle Reality Series ‘Earle Meets World’ Renewed For Season 2 At Netflix

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

EXCLUSIVE: Alix Earle is staying in business with Netflix.

Netflix has renewed her reality series Earle Meets World for a second season. It comes after the show’s launch in September.

The second season will feature another eight episodes of Earle and her family including her sisters Ashtin, Izabel and Penelope and brother Thomas, dad TJ and his wife Ashley, mother Alisa and her husband Todd.

The first season of the show saw Earle deal with the fallout of her relationship with Call Her Daddy’s Alex Cooper, her breakup with NFL star Braxton Berrios and plenty of high jinx with her family and friends. “You’re all gonna see me cry, and I cry pretty much in every episode,” Earle said at the premiere.

Earle said since season one aired “some shit has hit the fan… but hey, that makes for a good season two”.

It’s not entirely clear what she’s referring to, but the internet did go wild when she admitted she had never read a book, only to make fun of that by reading Daddy Issues, the new book by Cooper’s former podcast co-host Sofia Franklyn. Her sister Ashtin has also started dating Landon Barker, the son of Blink 182 drummer and Kourtney Kardashian beau Travis Barker.

Season one premiered with 1.8M views in its first three days but grew by 28% in its second week to reach 2.3M. Netflix said that the show was its number one unscripted series on its social channels, generating 337.8M social impressions globally.

The series is produced by Fulwell Entertainment and David Grutman’s DGN Studios. It is exec produced by Ben Winston, Paul Loban and Charlie Van Vleet, Trish Gold, Glen Gottlieb, Ally Gasparian, David Grutman and Evan Rosenfeld.

Earle said, “I couldn’t be more excited to be back and get the cameras rolling. Lots to catch up on and share with our Netflix family.”

Brandon Riegg, Vice President, Nonfiction Series & Sports added, “Our goal with Earle Meets World was authenticity and going deeper than social media ever could. The response to the first season speaks to the incredible connection viewers have with Alix and her family, and we’re grateful to Alix for trusting us to tell her story. We’re excited to welcome her and her family back for season two.”
