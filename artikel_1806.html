Prediction: Venezia vs Udinese

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

In what is a real six-pointer at the foot of the Serie A standings, Venezia will welcome Udinese to the Stadio Pier Luigi Penzo on Friday night.

With a Friday schedule from the likes of Serie A, La Liga and the Premier League, there are plenty of attractive betting promotions available.

Ahead of Friday’s Serie A showdown, you can find all of the key Venezia vs Udinese H2H stats here.

Starting with the hosts, while Venezia might have only made their iconic Serie A return this season, the Winged Lions have an almighty job on their hands this time around. Sat rock-bottom of the table and drastically failing to find their feet among Italy’s elite, Davide Nicola’s men are almost universally regarded as the leading relegation candidate this year. Although the Veneto-based outfit might have claimed a famous Serie A scalp last month as they snatched a 1-0 victory against Napoli, it appears that was just a minor shock boost for Friday’s hosts. Last seen getting hit with a 1-0 defeat against Como, Venezia have lost nine of their first 10 top-flight battles. Signing off October with a 2-0 defeat at home against reigning Serie A champions Inter Milan, Nicola’s side have also shown some glaring issues in the final third.

Yet to feature over the past few weeks and still recovering from a thigh injury, Venezia are once again expected to be without Portuguese defender Thierry Correia here. Likewise, still waiting to make a maiden Serie A appearance this season, Croatian international Marin Sverko remains on the sidelines.

Settling for a spot on the bench against Como last week, one-time Wolfsburg youngster John Yeboah could be handed a full recall. Completing a permanent switch from Napoli over the summer, veteran defender Juan Jesus will start at the heart of Venezia’s defence this weekend.

As for the visitors, while Udinese might have sprung an upset among Italy’s elite last season as they booked a top-half finish, the Little Zebras suddenly find themselves at the heart of a public Serie A nightmare this time around. Rightfully coming under a huge amount of scrutiny since opening up the 2026/27 campaign, the pressure on Kosta Runjaic’s shoulders has now reached a boiling point. Last seen getting hit with a 5-2 drumming at home against Roma, Friday’s guests have remarkably put just a single point on the board from any of their previous eight consecutive Serie A appearances. Heading into September’s extended international break with a rollercoaster 5-3 loss at the hands of Inter Milan, Udinese have also gained a reputation for their glaring defensive struggles.

Suffering another injury setback last month, summer recruit and former Napoli defender Alessandro Zanoli remains on the sidelines with a thigh issue. Likewise, despite making a blockbuster switch to the Stadio Friuli, veteran superstar and ex-Real Madrid ace David Alaba remains a major doubt after sustaining a recent calf injury.

Notching a consolation strike against former club Roma last weekend, Italian international Nicolo Zaniolo will feature in the final third. Despite still waiting to open his Serie A account, one-time Aston Villa striker Keinan Davis will lead Udinese’s charge.

In what is a real six-pointer from the foot of the Serie A standings, we look set for what could already be a season-defining battle from the Stadio Pier Luigi Penzo. With both Venezia and Udinese showing a string of glaring issues since opening up the 2026/27 campaign, don’t be surprised to see an incredibly nervy battle this weekend. While Friday’s opponents might have a habit of producing fireworks when they meet, it should be noted that Venezia have drawn three consecutive Serie A blanks. Likewise, the Winged Lions have also notched just a sole top-flight strike in over 300 minutes of action.
