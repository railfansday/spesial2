Ballard Power Systems Inc. (BLDP -10.71%) reported third-quarter results that included the first month of financial contribution from its acquisition of GeoPura. Management stated that the company is now vertically integrated across the hydrogen value chain, providing production, distribution, and conversion through an Energy-as-a-Service model. The company reported improved gross margins and reduced operating expenses while targeting positive Adjusted EBITDA by the fourth quarter of 2027. Management indicated that its stationary power products address severe bottlenecks in regional utility grids where commercial customers face interconnection delays exceeding five years.
