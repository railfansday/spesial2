Worried About the Tech Sector? Buy This Vanguard ETF for Long-Term Safety and Dividends

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Investing in high-quality utility stocks can be a great move for risk-averse investors right now.

Now can be a risky time to invest in tech or even have exposure to it. Between rising interest rates and a Chinese spying scandal involving Nvidia, one of the most popular artificial intelligence companies in the world, there's plenty of bearishness in the markets these days.

Investors can seek safety, however, by pivoting to high-quality Vanguard funds, which can provide not only long-term stability and safety, but also a great deal of recurring income.

A top fund that may be ideal for risk-averse investors who don't want to worry about tech's troubles these days is the Vanguard Utilities ETF (VPU -1.25%).

Utility companies generate recurring, predictable earnings numbers. There may be volatility due to weather, but by and large, many of them are fairly safe and stable businesses to invest in. Given that they provide customers with essential services, they can be precisely the type of safe-haven assets worth investing in right now.

Through the Vanguard Utilities ETF, investors don't need to worry about picking the best ones, either. This can be ideal for investors who aren't familiar with utility stocks but who do want exposure to the sector.

And with 68 stocks in its portfolio, this Vanguard fund offers some excellent diversification. Its top three holdings include leading utility providers in the country -- NextEra Energy, Southern Company, and Duke Energy. Together, they make up 25% of the portfolio. The vast majority of the stocks in the fund, however, only make up 2%. Focusing on the big names in the sector is an excellent way to offer the best of both worlds: exposure to the leading companies while still offering a diversified mix of holdings.

This Vanguard ETF has generated modest returns of 2% this year, but when including its dividend, its total returns are up around 5%. With a yield of nearly 3%, the fund can generate substantially more income for investors than simply tracking the S&P 500 index, which currently averages just 1.1%.

With an extremely low expense ratio of 0.09%, an above-average payout, and top utility stocks in its portfolio, the Vanguard fund can be a solid investment for virtually any type of investor. And it can also be an ideal one to hang on to for the long haul.
