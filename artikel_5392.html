Johannesburg's Alchemi Pharmacy Credits Jocelyn Freimuth for Pharmacy Support

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Alchemi CEO Zvi Sacks calls Jocelyn Freimuth "an invaluable partner" to the South African pharmacy and recommends her "without hesitation."

NASHVILLE, Tenn., Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Zvi Sacks, CEO of Alchemi Compounding Pharmacy in Johannesburg, South Africa, has credited Jocelyn Freimuth, PharmD, MBA, BSN, BCSCP, CEO of Camino Compass, with supporting his team on formulation development and troubleshooting.

Medicines sold in South Africa generally must be registered with the South African Health Products Regulatory Authority (SAHPRA), which must first be satisfied that each is safe, effective, and of good quality. Section 14(4) of the Medicines and Related Substances Act exempts medicines compounded for an individual patient, as a July analysis by Werksmans Attorneys outlines, leaving quality largely in the compounding pharmacist's hands. Sterile preparations raise the stakes, since most are injected or infused and leave no room for contamination.

Formulation development in sterile compounding means choosing the ingredients, concentrations, and containers that keep a preparation stable and sterile through its shelf life. Troubleshooting starts when something fails, from early degradation to a failed sterility test, and traces the problem back to its source in the process.

"Jocelyn's scientific depth and experience have been especially valuable in addressing the critical aspects of safety and sterility, which matter enormously in a sterile compounding environment," said Sacks.

"Compounding rules differ from one country to the next, but sterility does not," said Freimuth. "A preparation has to be right before it reaches the patient, and that is the standard I work to with every pharmacy, in the United States or abroad."

Alchemi is among the compounding pharmacies Freimuth supports outside the United States. Her international work also includes specialty pharmacy lectures at universities in Vietnam and a research presentation at the 25th Asian Conference on Clinical Pharmacy in Bangkok in July 2026.

For Sacks, how she works matters just as much. "Jocelyn is always willing to help, and she does it in a friendly, kind and approachable way," he said. "Her guidance is practical and reliable, and she makes complex problems feel manageable. We really enjoy working with her and would recommend her without hesitation."

About Jocelyn Freimuth and Camino Compass

Jocelyn Freimuth, PharmD, MBA, BSN, BCSCP, is a board-certified sterile compounding pharmacist and the Co-Founder and CEO of Camino Compass, a Nashville-based healthcare consulting and management firm. Drawing on more than 15 years across nursing, pharmacy, regulatory compliance, education, and healthcare executive leadership, she advises pharmacies, clinics, telehealth organizations, and medical providers in the United States and internationally. The firm's services include operational pharmacy support, regulatory compliance, accreditation support, healthcare education, startup and build-out consulting and team training. For more information, visit caminocompass.com or connect with her on LinkedIn.

Alchemi Compounding Pharmacy is based in Midrand, Johannesburg, in South Africa's Gauteng province. Led by CEO Zvi Sacks, the pharmacy prepares customized medications for medical practitioners, allied healthcare professionals, and their patients. For more information, visit alchemicompoundingpharmacy.co.za.
