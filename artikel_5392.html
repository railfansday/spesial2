Binance Pay, DK Bank and Tether Sign MOU to Expand Stablecoin Payments to Merchants Across Bhutan, Starting with USD₮

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Signed at Binance Blockchain Week 2026, the initiative builds on Bhutan's national crypto payment network and plans incentives to help merchants adopt stablecoin payments

BANGKOK, Nov. 28, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Binance Pay, DK Bank, Gelephu Mindfulness City's official bank, and Tether today signed a memorandum of understanding (MOU) to bring stablecoin payments to merchants across Bhutan, starting with USDâ®. The MOU was signed at Binance Blockchain Week Bangkok 2026 by Thomas Gregory, Vice President of Payments & Fiat at Binance; Ugyen Tenzin, Chief Executive Officer of DK Bank; and Tether.

The MOU builds on the payment network Binance Pay and DK Bank launched in May 2025, when Bhutan became the first country to introduce a national-level crypto payment system for tourism. Subject to eligibility requirements, users can pay participating merchants with supported digital assets, and merchants receive Bhutanese ngultrum. More than 3,700 merchants now accept payments through the system, from hotels and airlines to roadside vendors.

Under the MOU, the parties will work to promote stablecoin payments for merchants on this network.

To encourage participation, eligible users can receive a 5% rebate on qualifying transactions, capped at US$5 per transaction. Eligibility criteria and final terms are subject to agreement.

"Bhutan has shown that crypto payments can work at a national level," said Thomas Gregory, VP of Payments & Fiat, Binance. "With Tether and DK Bank, we want to make stablecoins and crypto the easier way to pay in Bhutan, and make accepting it worth it for every merchant."

"Since 2025, Bhutanese merchants have shown growing interest in accepting digital payments," said Ugyen Tenzin, CEO of DK Bank. "This initiative further enhances their ability to accept payments from international customers, and merchants can benefit from the reach of digital payment networks while continuing to receive their payments entirely in local currency."

The MOU is non-binding. Implementation, including the launch date and rebate terms, remains subject to final agreement between the parties.
