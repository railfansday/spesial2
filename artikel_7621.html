New Orleans Man Guilty of Committing Bank Fraud with Stolen Checks

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

NEW ORLEANS, LOUISIANA – RAYNARD JAMES (“JAMES”), age 23, pleaded guilty on November 4, 2026 before U.S. District Judge Jane T. Milazzo to one count of conspiracy to commit bank fraud, and three counts of bank fraud, in violation of 18 U.S.C. §§ 1344 and 1349, announced U.S. Attorney David I. Courcelle.
