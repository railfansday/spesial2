Patriots Finally Get Answers on A.J. Brown’s Hamstring Injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Patriots Finally Get Answers on A.J. Brown's Hamstring Injury originally appeared on NESN. Add NESN as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Patriots hoped A.J. Brown would be an upgrade to the offense when traded from the Eagles in the offseason. Instead, Brown's first season with New England has been marred by injuries and inconsistent play.

Brown initially suffered an ankle sprain during the Patriots' first game of the season, against the Seahawks. He missed four games. The 29-year-old also missed two games due to a knee strain.

Things got worse against the Lions in Munich. In the third quarter of a 35-10 loss, Brown left the game due to a hamstring injury after a 21-yard catch-and-run play. It was reported he would be sidelined for some time, but it wasn't known how long.

When can the Patriots expect the former 2019 NFL Draft second-round pick to suit up again?

Per ESPN's Adam Schefter and Mike Reiss, Brown is expected to miss multiple weeks as a result of the hamstring injury.

He has not officially been placed on injured reserve.

The Patriots, currently 5-5 on the season, are on a bye this week. After that, they face the Chargers, Bills, Vikings, and Chiefs. If his last trip to IR is any indication, Brown could return during Week 16 against the Jets.
