Browns waive RB Audric Estime

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Browns waived running back Audric Estime on Monday, the team announced.

Estime was with the team for three games, playing in one. He totaled nine carries for 19 yards and a touchdown.

The Browns signed Estime off the Vikings’ practice squad on Oct. 13 after placing running back Raheim Sanders on injured reserve with a hip injury. Quinshon Judkins and Dylan Sampson were also battling injuries at the time.

Estime entered the NFL as a fifth-round selection of the Broncos in 2024. He appeared in 13 games as a rookie, with 76 carries for 310 yards and two touchdowns.

He also spent time with the Eagles and Saints, with whom he appeared in seven games last season and recorded 46 carries for 198 yards and a touchdown.
