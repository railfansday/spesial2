Alonso: Chelsea need to be tougher after Leeds run riot at the Bridge

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Xabi Alonso admitted his Chelsea side are in a "fragile moment" after they were swept aside 4-1 by Leeds at Stamford Bridge.

This was the visitors' first Premier League win on this ground since December 1999 and it saw Daniel Farke's organized, energetic side climb into the top six.

Chelsea by contrast are in chaos – only three teams in the league have now conceded more goals – and are plummeting into mid-stable after being badly bullied on their own pitch.

"We need to be tougher," said Alonso. "It's something mandatory in this competition.

"Today we were in a fragile moment mentally. Confidence that when will receive a punch we will not recover."

They fell apart so easily after going in front, Emmanuel Emegha on his first start rifling in a goal, a singular bright spot on an awful day.

Noah Okafor equalized almost immediately with three defenders standing watching as he finished into the corner, then Dominic Calvert-Lewin – who had already hit the bar – missed two wonderful chances to send Leeds in ahead at the break.

Chelsea were booed off at the end of the first half but the worst was yet to come. Morgan Rogers was perhaps unlucky to concede a penalty for handball shortly after the restart, but there could be no blaming a defeat this comprehensive on a tight refereeing call. Calvert-Lewin got his reward for a wonderful individual display by scoring from the spot.

Okafor was left in space to whack in a third on the half-volley as the ball sat up from a deflected cross, then Brenden Aaronson scored a fourth in stoppage-time, but by then the home side had long since given up.

Alonso pointed to the loss of Moises Caicedo, forced off injured midway through the first half, and Chelsea looked utterly lost without their most dominant midfielder.

"After we lost Moises we lost completely the momentum," he said. "Until then we were doing OK, but from that moment they started believing and we started not believing. After that, we never got the good feeling and were not able to come from the pressure. It completely changed.

"I'm not feeling (let down). We do it as a team, as collective. We need to support each other. We need to find solutions to do better because this will happen.

"There were moments that we were not competing at the level required. But the physicality, the duels, it's not the only explanation. The mentality part we can do better."

Leeds by contrast were excellent and even at 3-1 they never stopped looking to punish Chelsea.

"Nights like this, if you face a side that has world-class players, if you dominate the side from the first to the last second, of course it's always good for the mood," said Farke.

"It's important in this period of the season to collect points. It was give us more confidence but we travelled here with confidence and belief that it would be possible to give such a dominant performance.

"I can't wait to be on the sofa with coffee and cake and to watch this game back."
