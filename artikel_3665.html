Bruno Mars Announces Dad Peter's Death at 74: 'I Will Miss Him Everyday'

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Bruno Mars is mourning the death of his father, Peter Hernandez. He was 74.

“Last week I got to hug my father one last time,” Mars, 41, wrote via Instagram on Thursday, November 19, alongside a photo of the musician embracing Hernandez in a hospital bed. “If you’ve ever seen me talk about him I’ve always said that he’s the man that taught me everything I know, especially when it comes to music.”

Mars continued, “He raised my four beautiful sisters, my brother and me. In my eyes he was the greatest dad in the world, and I will miss him everyday until we meet again. I love you, Pops. Kiss mom up there for me.”

Peter shared Mars and his four other children with ex-wife Bernadette San Pedro Hernandez, who died in 2013 at age 55. The former couple had divorced when the Grammy winner was 12 years old.

“My dad was just the king of finding these little spots for us to stay that we should never have been staying at,” Mars recalled on a 2016 episode of 60 Minutes. “We’d all sleep in one bed.

We had each other and it never felt like it was the end of the world.”

Mars noted that his childhood leant itself to the determination and tenacity it took to pursue a music career in Hollywood.

“Maybe that’s why I have this mentality when it comes to the music,” he acknowledged. “Because I know I’m going to figure … it out, just give me some time.”

Mars’ passion for music was apparent to Peter, who also was a performer.

“[Bruno] was a ham,” Peter mused to Midweek in a 2014 profile. “As soon as he could walk and express himself, he, like, had to be in the middle of everything and wanted to be a part of whoever he’d seen on TV.”

He added at the time, “Bruno is the product of a lot of love from all his family. No woman could ever love Bruno as much as his late mother did, and no man could ever love Bruno as much as I love him.”

Even with all the success Mars has achieved, Peter still saw the “Uptown Funk” crooner as his “baby boy.”

“I’m the proudest man in the world of Bruno Mars and his brother and sisters, and all the people who helped him get to where he is now,” Peter noted to the outlet. “I’m excited for him, for his sisters, for his brother. I’m so excited that I hope to be blessed with as much time as possible to see how far they go into the universe.”
