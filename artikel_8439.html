Personal Consumer Issues • Re: Driveway basketball hoop

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

I installed a Goalrilla adjustable backboard and hoop. It lasted 14 years during our time st that house. It might still be there.

In-ground installation. 18 bags of Quickcrete. I was a bit zealous with the digging bar. I dug 42” down to get below the frost line. Hole diameter needed to be larger than required for the backboard, so soil could be removed from the hole.

I am sure less concrete would have been adequate.
