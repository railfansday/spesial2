Götze ahead of clash against brother: He managed to forge his own path

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Ahead of the emotional showdown between the Götze brothers in the Bundesliga, Mario expressed regret that Felix had long been seen as "the brother of the World Cup champion."

"It wasn't easy for my parents and my brothers because they were associated everywhere with something they couldn't control," the 2014 World Cup champion and winning-goal scorer Mario Götze told Wednesday's Sport Bild weekly.

"It's been 12 years now. He has managed, both physically and mentally, to forge his own path. And that's good," he said about his brother.

On Saturday, Felix's Paderborn will host Mario's Frankfurt. However, this isn't the first time they face each other, but the third. During the 2018–19 season, they also met when Mario was then playing at Borussia Dortmund and Felix at Augsburg.

Felix Götze said that "as the younger brother, I was teased every now and then. But you learn to stand up for yourself. That was good for me. Maybe that's why I've come this far."

Both had very different careers. Mario led Germany to the 2014 World Cup title with his goal, which gave the Germans a 1-0 victory over Argentina. With Borussia Dortmund and Bayern Munich, he won the Bundesliga five times and celebrated four German Cup victories.

Felix played for the youth teams of Borussia Dortmund and Bayern Munich. After stints in Augsburg, Kaiserslautern, and Rot-Weiss Essen, he ended up at Paderborn, who secured promotion to the Bundesliga last season.
