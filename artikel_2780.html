Eagles snap counts: Eli Stowers doesn't play on offense vs. Steelers

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Eagles snap counts: Eli Stowers doesn't play on offense vs. Steelers originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

The Eagles went into Sunday's 19-16 win over the Pittsburgh Steelers with five tight ends active.

Here's how much those tight ends played on offense Sunday:

Dallas Goedert: 47 snaps (89%)Johnny Mundt: 8 snaps (15%)Zach Ertz: 6 snaps (11%)Eli Stowers: 0 snaps Grant Calcaterra: 0 snaps

The thing that really stands out here is that Stowers, a second-round pick, didn't see a single snap on offense. While he did play a team-high 20 snaps on special teams, Stowers isn't going to develop offensively if he doesn't see the field. (Calcaterra also played 20 special teams snaps).

There's a fine line to walk for the rest of the 2026 season. The Eagles haven't been eliminated from playoff contention and the coaching staff has to try to win games. But the Eagles are also still just 3-7 and it would be nice to see them develop a talented but raw second-round pick.

When asked about Stowers' lack of playing time, head coach Nick Sirianni first pointed at the lack of plays the Eagles had on offense.

"We want to be able to mix him in there and develop him," Sirianni said. "When you're in the flow of a game, sometimes you do the things you think are right to win that individual game. Obviously, we wanted to be able to do that. Sometimes the personnel that you have tagged in and you have ready for a certain player, maybe [doesn't] get called because the way the flow of the game is going. But every intention is that we want to get him going and get him involved. We have a lot of confidence in that entire room of those guys. But some of the special teams plays that he gets will obviously be important for his development as well."

The Eagles ended up being in 11 personnel on around 83% of their snaps on Sunday so they didn't use their two-tight end package very much. And when tight ends were on the field, they were sometimes asked to stay in and help Markel Bell against T.J. Watt.

The only tight end with a catch on Sunday was Goedert, who had a 38 yarder down the left sideline after beating K.J. Henry in coverage. It was a nice touch pass from Joe Milton.

• Milton and his entire offensive line played all 53 snaps. It wasn't a perfect game but it was the best we have seen this line look in a while. The Eagles had just 53 offensive snaps, which is their second-lowest total of the season. They had 50 against the Bears in Week 3.

• Saquon Barkley played 34 snaps (64%) before leaving the game with an injury. He had a 38-yard touchdown run and finished with 17 carries for 75 yards — most of which came in the first half. Will Shipley played 19 snaps (36%) and had just 9 yards on 4 carries.

• DeVonta Smith returned from a hamstring injury that kept him out for three games before the bye. He played 43 of 53 snaps (81%), which was actually behind Darius Cooper – 49 snaps (92%) and Makai Lemon – 44 snaps (83%). Elijah Moore played 11 snaps and Britain Covey got 1. Cooper led the team in receiving with 4 catches for 59 yards. Smith was targeted a game-high 12 times and had 5 for 41. Milton just missed him on a deep ball.

• The Eagles ran one play from a jumbo package with John Ojukwu next to Jordan Mailata. But they tried a play-action and Ojukwu was unable to block Alex Highsmith, who sacked Milton for an 8-yard loss.

• Riq Woolen started this game but got benched in the second quarter in favor of Jonathan Jones. But then Jones and Quinyon Mitchell both got hurt so Woolen was back in the game and was joined by Kelee Ringo, who gave up the Steelers' only touchdown. Here's how the outside CB snaps shook out: Mitchell – 56 snaps (79%), Woolen – 44 naps (62%), Jones – 35 snaps (49%), Ringo – 7 snaps (10%)

• Cooper DeJean, Drew Mukuba and Jeremiah Trotter Jr. each played all 71 snaps in this game. Mukuba has missed just 2 snaps all season, while Mukuba has missed just 4.

• Jalen Carter played the most of the DTs despite being downgraded to questionable on Sunday morning with a quadriceps injury. Carter played 61 snaps (86%), followed by Moro Ojomo – 51 snaps (72%), Byron Young – 22 snaps (31%), Ty Robinson – 12 snaps (17%) and Gabe Hall – 8 snaps (11%).

• On the edge, Jalyx Hunt led the group with 43 snaps (61%), followed by Nolan Smith – 37 snaps (52%), Jonathan Greenard – 34 snaps (48%) and Arnold Ebiketie – 28 snaps (39%). The Eagles sacked Aaron Rodgers five times on Sunday.
