TCU QB Adam Schobel expected to start over doubtful Jaden Craig vs. Utah

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

TCU QB Adam Schobel expected to start over doubtful Jaden Craig vs. Utah appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The TCU Horned Frogs face a massive hurdle ahead of Saturday's showdown against the No. 2 Utah Utes. Starting quarterback Jaden Craig landed on Friday night's Big 12 availability report as doubtful, putting sophomore backup Adam Schobel in line to make his first career start, per ESPN's Pete Thamel.

Craig has battled an ankle injury since TCU's 29-28 loss to the Arizona Wildcats two weeks ago. He played through a noticeable limp during last week's 20-10 win over the Kansas State Wildcats and missed Tuesday's practice, though head coach Sonny Dykes initially expressed optimism that his starter would play.

TCU listed Craig as probable earlier in the week before downgrading him Friday night. Losing Craig deals a major blow to a 5-5 Horned Frogs offense chasing bowl eligibility against an undefeated 10-0 Utah squad. The Harvard transfer ranks third in the Big 12 with 266.4 passing yards per game, completing 223 of 344 throws for 2,664 yards, 13 touchdowns and seven interceptions while adding four rushing scores. Against Kansas State last Saturday, Craig went 20-for-34 for 225 yards and a touchdown without throwing a pick.

Now, the spotlight shifts to Schobel. The 6-foot-5 former four-star recruit from Columbus, Texas, has seen limited action across three appearances this season. He threw one incomplete pass against the Grambling Tigers in September and logged four carries for 11 yards, including three rushes for three yards against Kansas State. TCU's injury woes extend beyond the quarterback room.

The Horned Frogs also ruled out running back G'Ivori Graham and linebacker Keylan Abrams for Saturday's contest in Fort Worth, though defensive tackles Markis Deal and Ansel Din-Mbuh alongside edge rusher Cheta Ofili remain probable. Meanwhile, Utah ruled out safety Marcus Wimberly and listed long snapper Logan Castor as questionable. Throwing a first-time starter against the Big 12's top team is no easy task, but Schobel gets his shot to shock college football under the Fort Worth lights.
