Lakers’ Luka Doncic gets rare chant inside road arena

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

PHOENIX — It isn't often that a player is given their flowers when playing on the road.

But it also isn't often that a player has a start to a season that Luka Dončić has had to open 2026-27.

Luka Dončić (77) of the Los Angeles Lakers shoots against the Phoenix Suns in Phoenix, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

So when the Slovenian superstar stepped to the free throw line to seal the Lakers' win over the Suns on Sunday, he couldn't help but smile when he heard "M-V-P" chants filling up Mortgage Matchup Center for the second time during the game.

"It was very nice," Dončić said. "I think everybody can see my smile at the free throw line. That made me happy, honestly."

Luka Dončić (77) of the Los Angeles Lakers celebrates against the Phoenix Suns in Phoenix, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

It helped that the Suns' arena, which had its fair share of Lakers fans, is less than 400 miles away from the Lakers' arena.

Dončić's early MVP love from opposing fans is well-deserved.

He's averaging a league-best 39 points to go with 7.8 assists, 7.2 rebounds and 1.9 steals.

His 43-point performance against the Suns was his seventh 40-point game of the season.

Luka Dončić of the Los Angeles Lakers talks to the media after a game against the Phoenix Suns in Phoenix on Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

"He's been playing his…off," Collin Sexton said. "It's one of those things that we have to continue to ride the wave and continue to support him. I feel like everyone's coming to the game to watch him put on a show. He's doing that night in, night out and we're gonna continue to rally around him and continue to be a supporting cast. Whatever role we have to play.

"At the end of the day, we all wanna see him win MVP. I know I do."
