Notre Dame vs SMU Prediction: Can the Fighting Irish Leave No Doubt?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Notre Dame blew out Boston College 41-7 … and it dropped a spot in the College Football Playoff rankings to No. 6. It doesn't matter – as long as the Irish win, they'll end up in the tournament. But drop a game over the last two weeks, and all CFP bets are off.SMU had its chance last week to all but land a spot in the ACC Championship, but one 49-7 rocking from Wake Forest later, and now it's looking to play spoiler to Notre Dame's season.

SMU not only got blown out by Wake Forest, but that was the final home game. The Mustangs close out at Stanford.

Notre Dame still has to deal with a road game at Syracuse to close things out.

These two haven't played since 1989 – a 59-6 Notre Dame win. The Irish are 10-3 in the series going back to 1930.

SMU is still a dangerous team, even after the disaster against the Demon Deacons.The offense is terrific, with a great pace and balance that can be impossible to stop when it gets into a rhythm. Notre Dame hasn't faced too many sharp passing teams, but there was a problem against the stronger quarterbacks.BYU's Bear Bachmeier hit 65% of his passes in the 28-24 win, and Miami's Darian Mensah almost pulled the game out of the fire, throwing for 269 yards and three scores. One loss, one close call, and those were the two times the Irish allowed more than 300 total yards.SMU has one of the nation's most efficient passing games for an offense that averages 456 yards per game.

How did Wake Forest destroy SMU? It came out roaring, and it was over.The Demon Deacons cranked up the pass rush against a leaky SMU line, and the three takeaways made things ugly. Yes, SMU works with a pace, but when it doesn't work, everything fizzes.But it's about the takeaways. SMU is 2-3 when turning it over multiple times, with 14 turnovers packed into those five games, and with nine turnovers in the three road games. Notre Dame is sixth in the nation in takeaways with 20.

Yes, the Mustangs have a fun offense, and the quick-hitting style will hit the Irish defense for a few scores to throw a scare into the fan base for a half, but CJ Carr and the Notre Dame passing game are pretty good, too.Between the turnovers and lots of penalties, SMU isn't perfect. A few good drives will stall with giveaways, and even though the pass rush will get to Carr on a few third downs, the Irish will be locked in. They'll take over and pull away in the second half.
