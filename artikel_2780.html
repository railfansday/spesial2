Baker Mayfield says he's ready to go after return to practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Buccaneers quarterback Baker Mayfield was back on the practice field Thursday.

Mayfield did not practice on Wednesday as he recovers from a foot injury he suffered in the team's Week 9 loss to the Bears. Mayfield told reporters at a Thursday press conference he hurt the foot while scrambling ahead of a pass to running back Kenneth Gainwell and that he feels he progressed well during the team's bye week.

Mayfield also said that he feels like he has made enough progress to play against the Lions this weekend, but that decision will ultimately fall on head coach Todd Bowles.

Bowles said earlier this week that a decision would come on Friday, which leaves Mayfield with another day to show he's good to go on the practice field before any final call about his status is made.
