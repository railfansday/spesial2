Grealish can return to Everton squad, insists Moyes

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

David Moyes insisted Jack Grealish can work his way back into the Everton squad after a disciplinary issue.

Grealish was left out of the Everton squad for their EFL Cup win over Newcastle United last week after breaching the club's disciplinary procedures, with reports suggesting he missed a training session.

The 31-year-old also missed the Toffees' Premier League win over the Magpies on Monday, with both Moyes and the club stating it was due to a fitness issue.

This is Grealish's second loan spell with Everton, after also joining the Toffees on a season-long deal in 2025-26.

He scored two goals and provided six assists in 22 appearances last season before his campaign was cut short by a foot injury.

This term, Grealish has featured just six times and is yet to directly contribute to a goal.

"I've just said he wasn't available for tonight [Newcastle match]," Moyes said in his post-match press conference.

"I don't know yet [if he will be available for Friday's game against Coventry City]."

And when asked if there was a way back for Grealish, Moyes responded: "Of course there is."
