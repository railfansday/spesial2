Magnera (MAGN +4.37%) reported its fiscal fourth quarter and full year 2026 results, concluding the second year of its initial three-year merger integration plan. Management reported that the company achieved its synergy savings targets and successfully exited its transition services agreement with Amcor, a process that involved migrating 11 ERP systems across 10 manufacturing sites. The company reduced its total debt by $122 million during the fiscal year while generating $115 million in free cash flow. For fiscal 2027, management provided a constant currency EBITDA growth outlook of approximately 6%, supported by the continued rollout of the Project CORE business excellence platform and targeted investments in high-margin innovation categories.
