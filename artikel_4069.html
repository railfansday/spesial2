How To Fish In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

It's not for everyone, but anyone can try to catch a big one.

Of all the activities in Grand Theft Auto 6, fishing is one of the most relaxing. Pick a nice day to go out on the water, and it's great for boosting your focus or even strengthening your bond as a couple. You'll have to do it for the main story at least once, but that doesn't mean you can't work to get better and make it a habit.

While fishing is simple at its core, it can take work to get proficient. You've got to know where to go to catch certain fish, have the right tackle, and use the right strategy to catch some more difficult ones.

You can find different fish in different spots around the map. For example, to catch a parrotfish for a quest with Cal, head to the southern part of the map, where the waters are warmer and quieter; other fish prefer the swamps or the open ocean.

It takes some figuring out to see which fish you can find in different locations, especially since some territories overlap. You'll have to fish in a spot to see what fish are there, and you'll know once you pull them out of the water. Different lures also attract different fish in the same area.

Even when you reach the right spot for different fish, you still can't fish from anywhere. Click in both thumbsticks to enter focus mode, then look at the water's surface to find the best spots to catch something. The water will glow where you're more likely to catch fish.

Here is how to unlock the hurricane weather storm in GTA 6.

First, equip a fishing rod; some boats already have some on the deck. If they do, walk over to the rod and pick it up. If not, hold L1 on PS5 or Left Bumper on Xbox to open your inventory. In the weapons tab, navigate to your equipment, then cycle to the fishing rod.

Find the glowing spots, then cast your lure into one. Your control scheme may vary, but generally, you hold L2 on PS5 or Left Trigger on Xbox to grip the rod, then pull in R2 or Right Trigger to cast if you're not obstructed.

Keep holding your grip while you fish, or else you won't be able to hook a fish or reel it in. You may end up casting a bit too far, but you can practice how far you want to cast by holding down the trigger longer. Then you can reel the line back toward the boat to get it where you want it.

Once you're in the right spot and you've cast your hook, you have to actually catch a fish. This won't be the fastest process in the game, but you can speed it up a bit by rotating the right thumbstick as slowly as possible to lure them to your bobber.

Don't rotate the thumbstick too fast, or else you'll reel your line all the way in and have to cast it again.

Eventually, as long as you're near one of the glowing patches in the water, a fish will bite your lure. You'll know when this happens because the Hook Fish prompt in the bottom right of the UI will turn white, and the controller will start to vibrate.

Pull in the right trigger to hook the fish, and it will start to fight you. When it does, the controller will vibrate, and the water will splash around where your line is. Pull the left thumbstick left and right to tire the fish out.

Once the fish stops fighting, you can start to reel it in quickly. Simply rotate the right thumbstick as fast as you can to reel the fish toward you until it starts fighting again. Then, just rinse and repeat until the fish is close enough to pull up onto the boat.

Not all fish are attracted by the same things. If you want to use a different kind of lure, you'll need to stock up on supplies before you go out to catch fish. To get some, you'll have to make your way to specific stores.

You can go to Ammu-Nations around the map to pick up fishing supplies, but not every Ammu-Nations sells fishing equipment. When you view the stores on the map, it will show the Inventory and Features at each location. Look for the small fish symbol, as those stores have fishing supplies.

Once you're in the store, head to the section with fishing lures, usually at the side of the store. You can choose saltwater or freshwater lures, and lures for small, medium or large fish. Pick out what you need, then head back to a fishing spot.

Once you've acquired a new lure, equip your fishing rod again. While you're holding it, before you grip it, use left or right on the D-pad to choose which lure you want to use out of what you own. Jason or Lucia will equip the lure, and you can catch bigger fish.

You can't sell your fish for money, but it will buff your focus stat, so it's good practice to do it every once in a while. Just don't forget to go with your partner; she may say that she doesn't love fishing, but Lucia secretly enjoys it.

It can be tough to get started, but there are easy ways to pick yourself up.
