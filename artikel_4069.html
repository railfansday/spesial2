Rumour: Alleged Persona 1 Remake Gameplay Leaks Online

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Alleged footage of a Persona 1 Remake has emerged online, although in this age of AI generated content it’s incredibly difficult to ascertain whether this is legit or not.

My jerk reaction is that it’s probably real, as it appears to include reused animations from the unreleased Persona 4 Revival. A similar leak occurred prior to the official announcement of Persona 3 Reload.

But as always, you should take this with a pinch of salt.

The video shows combat featuring characters from Persona 1, alongside Persona 2’s protagonist Tatsuya Suou.

Series producer Kazuhisa Wada has spoken quite candidly about how Persona 1 and 2 remakes are something Atlus should “eventually do”, so this project has been an open secret for some time now.

It’s worth noting that Persona 4 Revival is due out on 17th February, so even if this footage is real, I wouldn’t expect two remakes in the same year. It’s probably still a little way away.
