Vancouver prepared for ‘extremely difficult’ playoff test, says Sorensen

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Vancouver Whitecaps boss Jesper Sorensen acknowledged an "extremely difficult" playoff match against Real Salt Lake awaits them on Friday.

The top seed from the Western Conference won Game 1 of the best-of-three series 2-0, with Edier Ocampo and Bruno Caicedo scoring either side of half-time.

The Whitecaps, who reached the MLS Cup showpiece last year, can secure progression to the next round of the 2026 postseason with victory in Game 2 at America First Field.

Vancouver defeated FC Dallas in this round of the MLS Cup playoffs last term in two games, but were taken to penalties in Game 2.

Sorensen believes RSL will pose a serious threat and has urged his side to remember their exploits against Dallas in 2025.

"Game 2 is a tricky one for those who won Game 1; it's really about seeing if you can close it out against a team that plays with desperation, and we've seen how many teams had to go to three games to advance," Sorensen told reporters.

"This is going to be extremely difficult, but we know that we have the experience from last year in Dallas.

"In this league, it can be very difficult playing away. We're going to play up with some altitude, and we're going to play probably in some cold weather.

"It's going to be a different scenario than we played under the other day in BC, and that's what makes this league beautiful as well.

"We're facing a team that has to win this game to stay alive in the season, and we know that it's going to be a bit difficult."

Sergi Solans has scored 11 goals at home in MLS matches this season (regular season and playoffs).

The only RSL players with more home goals in MLS play in a season (including playoffs) are Alvaro Saborio in 2012 (13) and Robbie Findley in 2009 (12).

Ocampo scored the Whitecaps' opening goal last week, scoring from a shot with an expected goals (xG) value of 0.034.

He has scored three goals in MLS play this season (regular season and playoffs), with two of those having an xG value of 0.035 or lower.

MATCH PREDICTION: VANCOUVER WHITECAPS WIN

Vancouver's 2-0 win over RSL in Game 1 was their fifth straight victory in the fixture. That is Vancouver's second-longest win streak in MLS play against any opponent, trailing only their current six-game run against Sporting Kansas City.

The Whitecaps have won three of their six MLS Cup playoff matches since the beginning of 2025 (D2 L1). That matches Vancouver's win total in their 14 MLS Cup playoff games prior to that (W3 D2 L9).

The Game 1 loss moved RSL's winless streak in the MLS Cup playoffs to 10 straight matches (D6 L4), as the club's last win was in 2021 against Sporting KC.

That is tied for the longest winless streak in MLS playoff history with Minnesota United's current 10-match run that started in 2020.
