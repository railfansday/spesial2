‘Read the Room,’ ‘No Accountability’ — NFL Fans Call Out Dianna Russini for Providing Update From Brandon Aiyuk’s Agent

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

It has been a long time since Brandon Aiyuk took the field. But after a prolonged controversy with the San Francisco 49ers, he has officially been waived and could soon be available to sign with teams across the NFL.

The latest update in his story came from Dianna Russini, who has recently revived her career in the sports reporting world. Fans, though, weren't too thrilled with her choice of words or the fact that she was the one delivering the news about the former first-round pick.

A serious knee injury in 2024 forced Aiyuk to miss the remainder of the season, with issues and disagreements with the 49ers keeping him away from football entirely in 2025.

He outright stopped reporting to the team's facility and communicating with coaches and staff. As a result, he was placed on the reserve/left squad list in December 2025, a distinction he did not contest at the time.

This year, he hired Drew Rosenhaus as his agent in a bid to make a return to the league.

Now, after being officially declared a free agent, Russini had a direct report from Rosenhaus about why teams should believe in Aiyuk, given how the last two seasons had played out in public.

"Why should a team give Aiyuk another chance, given the last two years? 'I think they are going to get the best version of Brandon Aiyuk moving forward.' –Drew Rosenhaus," she reporter, directly quoted the super-agent.

However, fans weren't on board with that line of questioning. Given her controversy with New England Patriots head coach Mike Vrabel that led to her stepping down from The Athletic, a number of fans weren't ready to embrace the legitimacy of her reporting.

"Why should anyone give you another chance?" one fan wrote, posing the question to Russini instead.

"Dianna, you're in no position to ask questions like this. Respectfully," another fan pointed out, as yet another fan added, "How ironic. Why should any media outlets give you a chance? No accountability whatsoever. Just like Aiyuk."

One fan argued, "Dianna…. Read the room. It's over. Release a book," while another fan snidely pointed out, "You should know a thing or two about second chances."

Even though she got the message across from Aiyuk's agent, it doesn't seem like fans are ready to accept her as the messenger just yet. It remains to be seen if that is something that changes over time.

But as things currently stand, she isn't a popular name for updates among the fan base.
