No. 3 Texas at Texas A&M final score: Longhorns defense holds late in 16-6 win

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

A berth in the SEC championship game and a first-round by in the College Football Playoff are on the line for the No. 3 Texas Longhorns in the Lone Star Showdown against the Texas A&M Aggies on Friday in College Station.

After Texas won the toss and deferred to the second half, Texas A&M picked up one first down before punting away to the Longhorns, who went three and out, but freshman wide receiver Kohen Brown recovered the muffed punt and senior kicker Gianni Spetic connected from 54 yards when the drive stalled.

Struggling to catch kicks, the Aggies had to start their second possession on their own 7-yard line because of another muff.

Overcoming a holding penalty on first down and a delay of game penalty on third down, Texas A&M quarterback Brady Hart completed a 10-yard pass against Texas senior cornerback Bo Mascoe to move the chains, doing so again on third down when Mascoe was flagged for pass interference. Into Longhorns territory, the Aggies had a decision to make on 4th and 3 from the 40-yard line, opting to take a delay of game and punt the ball away.

Good pass protection on 3rd and 3 allowed Texas senior quarterback Arch Manning to connect with sophomore tight end Emaree Winston for a 14-yard gain, then hit junior wide receiver Emmett Mosley V for 13 yards. Snapping the ball before the end of the first quarter, Manning improvised on a shovel pass to fifth-year running back Raleek Brown for eight yards on 2nd and 9.

In 21 personnel, sophomore running back Michael Terry III took a handoff, got vertical, and ran through a tackle attempt for an 18-yard gain. Looking to score for just outside the red zone, Texas head coach Steve Sarkisian called a double pass for junior wide receiver Ryan Wingo, who made a good decision not to throw it, instead settling for a two-yard gain.

Put behind the chains when a nine-yard reception by fifth-year tight end Michael Masunas was negated by offensive pass interference, the Longhorns got into a manageable 3rd and 3 thanks to an 18-yard reception by Wingo on an free-access throw by Manning. After a lengthy injury timeout for a Texas A&M defender, a designed quarterback run for Manning moved the chains with the help of some leg drive from the Texas quarterback.

In an empty formation, Manning connected with Masunas on a short pass that gained 13 yards.

On 1st and goal from the 3-yard line, Manning's pass was tipped at the line of scrimmage, but managed to flutter its way into the strong hands of junior wide receiver Cam Coleman, who had a two-way go and ran a good slant route to score the touchdown and go up 10-0.

With former starting quarterback Marcel Reed into the game, two carries by Reed went for four yards before his departure in favor of Hart. On 3rd and 6, an easy-access throw when senior linebacker Rasheem Biles got lost in coverage resulted in a first down.

Hart misfired on a slant on 2nd and 10, forcing a more difficult conversion. A comeback to Isaiah Horton went for eight yards, but A&M head coach Mike Elko made another conservative decision to punt on 4th and 2.

Terry flashed his athleticism with a hurdle on second down to pick up eight yards and a first down, carrying it two more times for three yards and eight yards. On a toss sweep to Terry, the play got blown up by a linebacker coming downhill for a six-yard loss. After a tunnel screen to Mosley, a deep over to the Stanford transfer was delivered well by Manning under pressure, but Mosley couldn't extend to make the grab, forcing a punt.

Trying to throw the ball on first down, Hart held it too long and Texas senior edge Brad Spence beat the running back in pass protection for a sack. After sophomore nickel back Graceson Littleton broke up a screen pass on second down, Hart had to throw the ball away on 3rd and 17.

A friendly roll on the 53-yard punt by Tyler White flipped the field. Scrambling for eight yards, Manning started the drive positively, and appeared to pick up a first down with a scramble on 3rd and 10 that was negated by a holding call against senior left tackle Melvin Siani.

After Texas punted, Texas A&M mounted an effective drive highlighted by a 19-yard reception by Aaron Gregory and a pass-interference penalty on senior safety Jelani McDonald on 3rd and 3 at the edge of field-goal range. David Olano ultimately finished the half with a 52-yard field goal to make it 10-3.

The Longhorns opened the second half with an incompletion targeting Coleman, who was held at the top of his route, and a short run by Brown to set up 3rd and 9. Forced to check down to Winston, the sophomore tight end was dropped near the line of scrimmage for a three and out.

Another easy-access completion to Mario Craver on a slant initially appeared to be a positive second-down play, but Craver just lost the football turning up the field and McDonald recovered. A lengthy review upheld the call on the field and Texas took over at A&M's 34-yard line.

With the Longhorns offensive line struggling to create any creases in the running game, Brown was dropped for no gain before freshman wide receiver Jermaine Bishop Jr. caught a short pass and gained six yards. Facing 3rd and 4, Coleman won over the middle for a first down.

Throwing a screen to Wingo from a bunch formation to the field, the blocking didn't hold up and Wingo was tackled for no gain. Looking for a touchdown, Sarkisian called a wheel route for Brown, who ran a good route against man coverage and won, but Manning couldn't deliver the ball on time because of pressure in his face and sailed it too far when he could try to make the throw. Needing to convert 3rd and 10, Manning connected with Terry for a catch spotted at nine yards. When Texas A&M called timeout before Texas went for it on 4th and 1, a sneak by Manning that created a lot of movement at the line of scrimmage.

Operating with a lack of urgency under center on 1st and 10 from the 11-yard line, Manning got a delay of game penalty, then missed Coleman on a glance run-pass option and Mosley on a back-shoulder fade. Unable to get a play called in a timely manner, Texas had to burn its first timeout of the second half to run a 3rd and 15 play, a double move to Coleman that never had a chance.

The Horns had to settle for a 33-yard field goal by Spetic and a 13-3 lead.

Looking for a big play on 2nd and 4, Gregory used a double move to beat Mascoe, who had to hold to negate the explosive play. A slot fade to Gregory on 3rd and 4 beat Littleton for a 30-yard gain, and A&M was on the edge of the red zone.

Two runs by Carsyn Baker netted zero yards and Hart came under quick pressure, forcing him to throw underneath to Gregory for six yards, resulting in a 35-yard field goal to make it 13-6.

A rare kickoff return for senior Ryan Niblett was returned for 32 yards to the 35-yard line, but Brown was cut down for a one-yard gain on first down. On 2nd and 9, a comeback route for Coleman resulted in a run after the catch and an important 19-yard gain.

The drive quickly lost momentum when Brown didn't run a route as Manning expected and the Texas quarterback nearly threw an interception trying to find Mosley over the middle. A pass to Coleman was completed just short of the first down and Sarkisian decided to go for it on 4th and 1, a handoff to Terry that was stuffed in the backfield.

After a four-yard completion on first down, McDonald came downhill with aggression against the run and inflicted a tackle for loss. On 3rd and 7, senior linebacker Justin Cryer wrapped his blitz around sophomore defensive end Lance Jackson and sacked Hart for a big loss that ended the third quarter.

After A&M's punt, Texas took over at its 38-yard line, where Manning scrambled for 13 yards by identifying man coverage and taking advantage of the space that afforded him. A personal foul for going low on a Longhorns offensive lineman resulted in 15 yards for Texas. On 2nd and 7 looking for a shot play, Manning took a five-yard sack with nowhere to throw the ball. A bubble screen to Wingo picked up 19 yards behind a good block from Mosley and an escort from the offensive line.

Mosley drew a holding penalty on a pass over the middle that would have otherwise gone for a touchdown, but another pass over the middle, this time to Wingo, went incomplete. On 2nd and goal, a designed run to the boundary for Manning was dropped for a loss. Trying to throw into a small window, Manning's pass was knocked into the air and intercepted in the end zone.

Beat in press man coverage, Mascoe gave up a 33-yard completion to Gregory before senior safety Derek Williams nearly came up with an interception on the next throw by Hart, also targeting Gregory. Going for it on 4th and 3, A&M called a timeout before spreading the field and targeting sophomore cornerback Kade Phillips, whose coverage on Horton forced a turnover on downs.

Brown found some running room on first down and the Aggies were called for a hands to the face penalty. Needing to break one open-field tackle for a big play, Brown was brought down instead, then tackled for a loss on second down. Facing 3rd and 7, Manning took the edge on a designed run, broke a tackle, and fell forward for a massive first down.

Looking for Wingo, the out route went for seven yards and kept the clock moving. A predictable run on 2nd and 3 was stopped for a three-yard loss and Manning had to throw the ball away on third down. Clutch as he has been all season, Spetic knocked the 42-yard field goal down the middle to make it a two-score game at 16-6 with 5:19 remaining.

In desperation mode, the Aggies went for a 4th and 1 deep in their own territory and converted it, then benefitted from a roughing the passer penalty on Jackson. Willing to trade short completions for time coming off the clock, the Texas defense forced A&M into another 4th and 1. Disorganized, the Aggies had to use their final timeout before Hart threw incomplete, setting the Horns up to ice the game.

Running hard up the middle, Terry broke a tackle for a first down, sending the game into the two-minute timeout and allowing the Horns to finish the game in victory formation to clinch a berth in the SEC championship game.
