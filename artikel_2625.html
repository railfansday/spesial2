Kahn praises Kimmich for defending Musiala: 'Important signal'

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Former Bayern Munich chief executive Oliver Kahn has renewed his criticism of the club's leadership over its public handling of Jamal Musiala's health problems, while praising Joshua Kimmich for defending his team-mate.

"Joshua Kimmich has always been someone who speaks his mind," the former Bayern goalkeeper told RTL and Sky's "Triple" football talk show.

Kahn said it was important for Kimmich, Germany's national team captain, to stand by Musiala and send a message of unity to the squad during a difficult period.

"That was an important signal from Joshua," he said.

Bayern honorary president Uli Hoeness and supervisory board member Karl-Heinz Rummenigge have raised the possibility of Musiala taking a pay cut amid concerns over his health. They have also publicly questioned whether Musiala, 23, can return to his previous level of performance.

"I still don't understand why those statements were made. What has been achieved?" Kahn said. "Jamal has been weakened, he has been damaged to some extent. In a way, financial damage has also been done."

Kahn, who had criticized Hoeness over his remarks about two weeks ago, said the public debate risked unsettling Bayern's squad.

He suggested the remarks may have been intended to put pressure on Musiala's representatives but said such matters should be handled privately.

Musiala has been struggling with health problems and trying to regain his previous form for around 18 months.

Kimmich criticized Bayern's handling of the situation following Wednesday's 2-1 Champions League defeat at Lille, publicly defending his team-mate against comments made by senior club officials.
