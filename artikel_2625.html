Rams All-Pro WR Puka Nacua reportedly suspended 3 games for violating league's personal conduct policy

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Los Angeles Rams wide receiver Puka Nacua has reportedly been suspended for three games for violating the league's personal conduct policy, ESPN's Adam Schefter reported on Thursday.

Nacua's suspension will go into effect immediately, and he will be eligible for reinstatement on Monday, Dec. 14, Schefter added. The All-Pro wideout is currently on injured reserve.
