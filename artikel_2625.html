Cam Ward posts another encouraging outing in Week 10 vs. Jacksonville

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Tennessee Titans QB Cam Ward completed 28 of 39 passes for 231 yards with two touchdowns and one interception in Week 10 against the Jacksonville Jaguars. He ran three times for 39 yards.

Why It Matters: Fantasy managers are trying to determine if Ward can be a viable asset, although he is starting to redeem himself after a rough start to the season. Ward threw for two touchdowns for the second consecutive game - accounting for as many passing scores as he had over the team's first seven contests combined. Ward, who threw for a career-high 304 yards in Week 8 against Cincinnati, is a low-end QB2 option at best most of the time, but he has a favorable matchup in Week 11 against a porous Dallas Cowboys pass defense.
