Matt Damon Narrates Ford Racing’s ‘Go Like Hell’ Thanksgiving Day Ad Directed by Zack Snyder

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Ford Racing tapped Matt Damon, star of “Ford v Ferrari,” to introduce the all-new Ford GT41 Hypercar in a new ad directed by filmmaker Zack Snyder.

The ad is slated to air during CBS’s broadcast of the Detroit Lions-Chicago Bears Thanksgiving Day NFL game, which starts at 1 p.m. ET. The 90-second spot centers on the GT41 Hypercar and the U.S. automaker’s return to the top class of the FIA World Endurance Championship and Le Mans in 2027 — and evoking Damon’s turn as a driver in “Ford v Ferrari.” The ad was produced by Artists Equity, the studio led by Damon and Ben Affleck. “What does it mean to be American?” Damon says in the ad. “Well, many things to many people. To us, it’s not just where we hang our hat. It’s downright relentless grit.” The spot ends with Damon praising Ford Racing fans: “More than part of the team, you’re our fuel. Why we persevere. Every mile feeds the next. Because that’s what it means to be American. That we always — without fail — go like hell.”

The spot chronicles the journey of Ford’s GT41 Hypercar from the U.S. to the legendary Circuit de la Sarthe in Le Mans, France, ahead of its competitive debut in 2027. The film was shot in and around Los Angeles, symbolizing “Ford Racing’s return to the scene of some of its greatest motorsport triumphs,” according to the car company. It also features connections to “Ford v Ferrari”: For example, the airplane hangar used to load the GT41’s shipping container is the same one used for the Ford GT40 race workshop in the movie.

Damon, who serves as chief content officer of Artists Equity, portrayed Ford driver Carroll Shelby in 2019’s “Ford v Ferrari.”

“Telling the story of Le Mans ‘66 [in ‘Ford v Ferrari’] was one of the highlights of my career, so getting to lend my voice to the next chapter felt very full circle,” Damon said in a statement. “Revisiting this one with Ford and Zack, as they take on the world’s toughest racing all over again, was a real privilege.”

Snyder, known for films including “300,” “Watchmen” and “Justice League,” commented: “The amazing part about getting a chance to work on this ‘Go Like Hell’ spot for Ford Racing was the chance to mix the nostalgia of the ’66 GT40 with this modern hypercar and its incredible engineering as well as this narrative that follows the transporting of the car across America.”

The ad coincides with Thursday’s official public unveiling of the Ford GT41 at Detroit’s Ford Field. The new vehicle takes its name from the Ford GT40, which secured four consecutive overall victories at Le Mans between 1966 and 1969. Like its predecessor, the GT41’s name reflects its dimensions, standing 41 inches from the ground to the air intake.

The Ford GT41 will make its competitive debut in the 2027 FIA World Endurance Championship, with the season opening in Qatar in March before the team heads to Le Mans in June.
