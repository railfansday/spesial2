Miranda Lambert Heats Up Halftime at Dallas Cowboys’ NFL Thanksgiving Game: Watch

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The three-time Grammy Award-winning star delivered a nearly seven-minute medley -- with support from the Dallas Cowboys Cheerleaders, a full band and a strings section.

Miranda Lambert gave Texans some homestyle country cooking Thursday night (Nov. 26), when she headlined the halftime spot in the NFL Thanksgiving contest between Dallas Cowboys and the visiting Philadelphia Eagles. Explore Miranda Lambert See latest videos, charts and news

Wearing a black cowboy hat and a little black dress emblazoned with stars and finished with long, tasseled sleeves, Lambert delivered a nearly seven-minute medley — with support from the Dallas Cowboys Cheerleaders, a full band and a strings section. The three-time Grammy Award-winning star kicked things off with “Snakeskin Boots,” and got cooking with “Kerosene,” “Gunpowder & Lead,” “Mama’s Broken Heart,” and a cover of Audra Mae and The Almighty Sound’s “Little Red Wagon.”

“Happy Thanksgiving y’all, we love you Texas,” she said at the close.

Lambert has been on a tear of late. Just last week, she was the big winner at the 60th annual Country Music Awards, where she collected three trophies. With that hattrick, she lifted her career total of wins to 17, extending her lead as the female artist with the most victories.

The Longview, TX native ushered in her new album, Crisco, with a set Oct. 1 at the newly-opened Nashville venue The Truth, then dropped her Songline album on Nov. 13, followed by the companion film Songline on Nov. 20, both via Amazon Music.

Also, Lambert co-wrote Ella Langley’s Billboard Hot 100 smash hit “Choosin’ Texas,” which is now the longest-charting No. 1 in Hot 100 history, with 29 weeks atop the leaderboard. In recent days, “Choosin’ Texas” logged a 50th week atop Billboard’s Hot Country Songs chart (dated Nov. 28), equaling the all-time mark set by Bebe Rexha and Florida Georgia Line’s “Meant To Be” in 2017-18.

Unfortunately for the home fans at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, TX, Lambert’s halftime spot was the only highlight. The Eagles defeated the Cowboys 39–38, after being down by as much as 21 points in the third-quarter. Eagles kicker Jake Elliott had the dagger, nailing a game-winning field goal with just two seconds on the clock.
