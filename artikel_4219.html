Round Up: Pikmin Day 2026 - Updates, Demo And New Animated Shorts

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Nintendo surprised us with a Pikmin-themed Direct this weekend, announcing Pikmin Day! If you haven't already watched the video, here's everything from the broadcast:

On this page: Round Up: Pikmin Day 2026 - Updates, Demo And New Animated Shorts

Joining MAR10 Day and other events such as Pokémon Day, is Pikmin Day! This will be an annual event every year on 1st November. Miyamoto explained how this came about:

Shigeru Miyamoto: "We're going to start calling November 1st, Pikmin Day. In the first Pikmin game, released on Nintendo GameCube in 2001, the red, blue and yellow Pikmin made their debut. If you imagine those three Pikmin as the number one, that makes November 1st, Pikmin Day!"

A free demo of Pikmin 4 - Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy is out 2nd November 2026. It will include the game's opening area Sun-speckled Terrace, along with two stages of Dandori Academy. Save data will also transfer over to the full game.

Nintendo also provided a quick rundown about the features coming in the Switch 2 Edition of Pikmin 4.

This includes voice commands for Oatchi with the built-in system mic. And then there's the new mode, Dandori Academy, where you'll take on challenges, collect the treasure, and take an exam. You'll receive medals based on your performance.

The mobile title Pikmin Bloom is adding a new update called 'Secret Caves'. It's scheduled to arrive in "Winter 2026". Players will collect treasure in the caves by defeating creatures.

Before you can venture in you'll need a Cave Map, attached to postcards. When you've got your map, you can then send out Pikmin to explore the cave and battle creatures. Win the battle and you'll get all sorts of treasures.

Additionally, there'll be in-app cave maps you can unlock by taking exams in Pikmin 4 Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy:

Following on from past animated Pikmin shorts, Nintendo Pictures and Miyamoto have unveiled the latest series, 'Hello! Pikmin'.

The first short aired during the Nintendo Direct and the rest will be released on the Nintendo Today! app and other Nintendo channels. Two more episodes are out this month, and from December onwards, one video will be released per month.

Nintendo has also announced it's hosting a Treehouse event today where it will showcase Pikmin 4 Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy. You'll be able to watch it on the official Nintendo YouTube channel.

A special broadcast timed in with Pikmin Day

As part of the Pikmin Day celebrations, the following icons are now available:

"Redeem your #MyNintendo Platinum Points to collect custom icons from Pikmin, available until Nov 28 at 5pm PT on Nintendo Switch 2 and Nintendo Switch. Icon elements will be refreshed each week."

And that's a wrap on Pikmin Day for 2026!

Once again, Pikmin 4 Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy arrives this month on 12th November 2026, and the demo for this version is out tomorrow.
