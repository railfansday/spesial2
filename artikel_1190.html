Celtic Must Bite Bullet And Sack O'Neill - 'Elite' Manager Is Right There

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Celtic need to bite the bullet and sack Martin O'Neill, despite their victory in the Europa League on Thursday night.

Celtic opened the scoring in the first half thanks to an own goal, but Portuguese side Torreense equalised just 10 minutes later.

Celtic were then held until the last five minutes of normal time, when Camilo Duran fired home to give Celtic the victory.

The Glasgow club have been picking up some strong results of late, but it's games that they are more than expected to win.

Their performances in the Europa League are where they will truly be judged, and in reality, it was another disappointing performance against Torreense.

They currently sit outside of the qualification section of the Europa League and look destined to be knocked out unless they beat Omonia Nicosia and other results go their way.

In the Scottish Premiership, Celtic are sitting top of the table by a single point, but given that's where they are expected to be, it's in Europe where they will be properly judged.

Celtic's performances in Europe this season simply haven't been good enough, despite two wins on the bounce in the competition.

Celtic made incredibly hard work of their win over Torreense, especially given the stature of the aforementioned club.

Torreense currently sit 13th out of 18 in the second division of Portugal. If Celtic are unable to get a convincing win against a side like that, it's worrying signs for O'Neill.

Given his age and his contract situation, everyone knows that O'Neill isn't going to be around Celtic for much longer, so why not bite the bullet and make a move?

They stand a serious chance of being knocked out of the Europa League, which would be a major disappointment considering the quality of some of the sides in there.

As mentioned, their Scottish Premiership form has been good of late, but it's still far from how O'Neill should be judged.

The Bhoys need to unlock their £6million signing

If Celtic are serious about trying to take the next step forward as a club, rather than having an ageing manager who is destined to leave, they need to go all out for former Ipswich Town manager Kieran McKenna.

He's currently out of work after leaving Ipswich, and although he may be waiting for better offers, it's certainly worth an ask.

McKenna, described as an 'elite' manager, isn't going to be a free agent for too much longer, so Celtic need to bite the bullet and make their move.

Whether they can attract him is another story, but there's no reason not to show some ambition if they want to take the next step.
