Hull 'has great laugh' on WTGL debut despite defeat to Woad's Boston

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Charley Hull off to Disney World after making first appearance at the SoFi Center; Englishwoman wins her opening singles match, against Solheim Cup team-mate Celine Boutier, but Atlanta Drive soundly beaten by Boston Common; watch every WTGL match live on Sky Sports

Charley Hull tasted defeat on WTGL debut as her Atlanta Drive side lost 8-3 to Lottie Woad's Boston Common at the SoFi Center in Florida.

English star Hull won her first singles match in the competition, beating Solheim Cup team-mate Celine Boutier as she holed a 15-foot return putt having shot an initial effort way past the hole.

But Atlanta Drive were still soundly defeated in their first game of the inaugural women's season as Boston Common bounced back from last week's loss to Motor City.

Still Hull, who smoked her opening tee shot 264 yards, thoroughly enjoyed the experience, saying: "Obviously it wasn't the result that we wanted, but we had a great laugh. I know we're not on the golf course, but you're in the golf environment."

Hull's first experience of the WTGL came after a whirlwind few days in which she finished tied 23rd at the LPGA's season-ending Tour Championship and then took part in a shoot with equipment manufacturer TaylorMade.

The 30-year-old said she was "massively flagging" but will now enjoy some downtime with her family at Disney World.

Woad, whose spectacular chip-in against Motor City had taken that clash into overtime, recorded a singles victory over Atlanta's Nataliya Guseva on Tuesday evening.

With Ina Yoon also thumping Andrea Lee, Boston picked up four points to Atlanta's one in the singles having opened up a 4-2 lead after the triples leg.

Yoon secured the win for Boston when she landed her tee shot at the par-three 14th to inside three feet, earning three points with two Hammers in play.

Earlier on, Boutier chipped in from just off the green to put Boston 4-0 up after eight holes of triples before Atlanta chalked up their first two points at the ninth.

The WTGL follows on from the success of the men’s Tomorrow's Golf League (TGL), founded by Tiger Woods and Rory McIlroy's TMRW Sports.

The women's edition of the tech-infused golf league sees LPGA Tour stars feature in a unique team format, with five teams of five-player rosters representing five cities.

The WTGL continues with a double header on Sunday, live on Sky Sports, as Woad's Boston play New York from 6pm UK before Hull's Atlanta Drive meet unbeaten Motor City from 8.30pm UK.
