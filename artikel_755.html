Zendaya Felt ‘Incredibly Nervous’ While Shooting ‘The Odyssey’ but Credits Tom Holland for Supporting Her: He Was ‘in the Corner Like, “You Got This”’

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Zendaya might be one of Gen Z’s definitive leading ladies, but even she was “incredibly nervous” while playing Athena in Christopher Nolan’s “The Odyssey.”

“I felt overwhelmed, to say the least,” said the star during a recent Q&A held at Los Angeles’s Academy Museum of Motion Pictures. “It was interesting because I was on a night shoot, and I remember I came in and I was like, ‘This is like, a pinch-me moment’…. I’m in front of these people who I’ve admired, but I’m just like, ‘I don’t want to mess this up.’” Related Stories 'Rockford Files' Reboot Sets NBC Release Date, Launches Jim Rockford Hotline Mariska Hargitay, Steve Carell and Jimmy Fallon Among Stars Set for NBC's 100th Anniversary Special

She continued: “I was incredibly nervous, which I guess is helpful for the scene, but there are so many other background actors as well who are performing their hearts out — so many — stunt performers who are performing and giving the most beautiful performances.”

She credits Tom Holland, her husband and co-star (he played Telemachus, the son of Odysseus and Penelope), for easing her on-set nerves: “It helped that Tom came to set that day, and he was, like, in the corner like, ‘You got this, you’re doing good.’”

All her worry was for naught, though, as Nolan told Fandango that the “Dune” actress was “always perfect.”

Zendaya and Holland tied the knot in early 2026, according to the actress’ long-time stylist Law Roach. On the red carpet at the Actor Awards, Law told Access Hollywood that “the wedding has already happened. You missed it.” Rumors of their engagement first circulated when Zendaya was spotted at the 2025 Golden Globes ceremony wearing what appeared to be an engagement ring. Holland confirmed they were engaged later that year.

But “The Odyssey” isn’t the first film set they’ve shared. The couple first met on the set of Marvel’s “Spider-Man: Homecoming,” and continued to co-star in “Spider-Man: Far From Home,” “Spider-Man: No Way Home” and “Spider-Man: Brand New Day.” In May, Zendaya told Elle that working on the films felt like a “family affair.” “I get to go to work every day with my best friend, the person that I love,” she said. “We bring our dogs to work; it’s like a family affair. We grew up on those movies! It’s like coming home.”
