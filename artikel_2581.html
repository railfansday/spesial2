Runners, walkers lace them up for 33rd Hornell YMCA Turkey Trot 5Ks

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Before placing that big bird in the oven on Nov. 26, put on your running shoes and head to the Maple City for the Hornell Area Family YMCA Turkey Trot 5K Run and Walk.

This Hornell Thanksgiving day tradition returns for its 33rd year in 2026, with a 5K run, a 5K walk and a Youth Fun Run providing opportunities for athletes at all levels of ability, from masters to beginners.

Dan Malatesta was the top finisher in the 2025 Hornell YMCA Turkey Trot 5K Run with a time of 17:03.3. Malatesta also finished first in the 2024 race.

Shelby Lamphier of Wellsville finished first in last year's 5K Walk with a time of 30:17.

All told, 343 athletes made it to the 5K Run finish line, while the 5K Walk saw 159 competitors complete the 2025 race.

Runners and walkers can reserve their spots in the event by registering online.

Registration cost for the 5K Run and 5K Walk are each $32.80, which includes all fees.

Registration for the Youth Fun Run is $6.30, fees included.

Registration for all events remains open until about 15 minutes before start time.

The first 300 athletes to register for the 5K run and walk will receive a long-sleeve shirt courtesy of the Hornell YMCA.

Packet pick up for athletes is at the YMCA building, 18 Center St., Hornell, from 4 to 7 p.m. on Wednesday, Nov. 25; and from 7 to 9 a.m., on race day, Thanksgiving.

The start time for the 5K Run and the 5k Walk is 9:30 a.m. The Youth Fun Run begins at 9 a.m.
