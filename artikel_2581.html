Titans week 12 game against Jaguars moved to noon Central kickoff

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

If you are like me, you had no idea that the Tennessee Titans Week 12 game against the Jacksonville Jaguars was supposed to be at 3:05 Central. I just assume that the Titans are going to play at noon every week. That has pretty much been the case for the past three seasons.

The NFL announced its flex changes this afternoon, and the Titans were one of the teams affected:

The game between the New England Patriots and Los Angeles Chargers was originally the Sunday Night Football game, but with Drake Maye being injured and the Chargers being bad, that game got moved to an earlier time slot. The game that got moved to SNF, the Las Vegas Raiders vs. the Cleveland Browns, doesn't hold that much intrigue for me, but I guess NBC wanted Fernando Mendoza vs. the surprising Browns.

The Titans will play a 3:05 game in Week 16 against the aforementioned Raiders.
