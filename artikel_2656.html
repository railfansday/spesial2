Rhamondre Stevenson sets record straight on speedy injury comeback

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Injuries have not been kind to the New England Patriots this season, and in the team's blowout loss to the Green Bay Packers, they lost one of their top offensive weapons.

Rhamondre Stevenson suffered a knee injury, with multiple reports implying that the running back would be sidelined for a few weeks. So, imagine the surprise when Stevenson not only was spotted with the team the next week in Germany, but also took part in practice as a limited participant.

And he didn't stop there, with Stevenson being listed as active for the game.

He went from a possible injured reserve candidate to not missing a single game. So, what went into that?

"A bunch of recovery," Stevenson told Patriots Wire in an exclusive interview. "(The trainers) did a good job of just getting me available again... Leg wasn't feeling 100 percent, but I was well enough to go out there and participate with the team and try to help them out a little bit."

However, he did admit that based on what he was initially told, his speedy return to the field came as a bit of a surprise.

"Yeah, honestly, yes," he explained. "It was great. If I could help my team in any way possible, I want to do that. So, I wasn't 100 percent, obviously, but if I could go out there and be healthy enough to participate, like I said, that's what I'm going to do every week."

But, while the media was saying a couple weeks, he didn't think it would take that long.

"First and foremost, probably the media and what they're saying isn't always true," Stevenson said. "But I think it just attests to how tough we are, how willing we're trying to be out there with our team, and just trying to be available for our teammates."

The running back was also asked if he felt 100 percent after the bye, but Stevenson chose to keep that information to himself. So, while he may not have been ready to reveal exactly where he stands physically, one thing is clear: He's willing to do whatever it takes to be on the field for his teammates.
