Cameron vs Mayer 2 Leads Boxing Schedule Additions

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

A flurry of fight matchups have been added to the boxing schedule through the end of 2026 and early in 2027.

Leading the list is the rematch between unified super welterweight champion Chantelle Cameron and pound-for-pound former champion Mikaela Mayer

Minimumweight world champion Oscar Collazo will fight in the co-main event of Amanda Serrano's farewell fight in Puerto Rico.

With just a few weeks to go until boxing media will put together their year-end awards for 2026, additional fight matchups are being announced and added to the calendar in 2026, along with cards to look forward to in early 2027.

Most Valuable Promotions (MVP) announced the rematch between number two Ring pound-for-pound boxer, two-division world champion and reigning unified WBO, WBA, and WBC Super Welterweight world champion Chantelle Cameron of England (23-1, 8 KOs) and fifth Ring pound-for-pound, three-division world champion, and reigning WBO World Welterweight champion Mikaela Mayer of Colorado Springs (22-3, 5 KOs).

Cameron vs Mayer 2 has been added as the co-main event of MVP's Jake Paul vs. Cédric Doumbé Christmas Eve card on December 24 at Coca-Cola Arena in Dubai, UAE.

If you don't think this one will be spicy, let us remind you how the first fight went down between Cameron and Mayer.

Cameron earned a unanimous decision over Mayer in August at MVPW06 in Birmingham, England, where Cameron claimed Mayer's WBA and WBC titles and unified three world championships. Mayer immediately called for a rematch, and now the showdown is booked.

Cameron enters determined to defend her unified crown and cement her place among boxing's pound-for-pound elite, while Mayer looks to avenge her defeat and reclaim the titles she lost just four months earlier.

Chantelle "Il Capo" Cameron is one of the most recognized names in women's boxing. Cameron won the vacant WBC super lightweight world title in October 2020 before winning the IBF and Ring Magazine titles, then the WBA title, to become undisputed champion. Cameron successfully defended her titles against Katie Taylor in May 2023, the only professional to beat Taylor in her career, before a November 2023 rematch resulted in an MD for Taylor.

Cameron won the WBC interim super lightweight title in her next fight, defended it twice successfully, then stepped up to win the vacant WBO World Super Welterweight title in April 2026 on MVPW01.

"I am genuinely excited to box in one of my favorite places on December 24 in Dubai," said Cameron. "I cannot thank Jake, Nakisa and Mike enough for this opportunity to box on a huge show. They promised to keep me active in 2026, and my third fight in nine months proves just that. We agreed to the rematch before the first fight, so we both kept our word, and now the rematch is on the biggest stage in boxing."

Three-division champion and reigning WBO World Welterweight champion Mikaela Mayer jumped out to a 17-0 record to begin her pro career while winning a pair of super featherweight world titles and becoming a pound-for-pound mainstay.

In January 2024, she contended for the IBF welterweight world title against Natasha Jonas, then seized her second opportunity to become a two-weight world champion in September 2024, defeating WBO World Welterweight Sandy Ryan in a Fight of the Year contender before besting her again in a rematch.

Mayer became a three-division champion with a defeat of Mary Spencer in October 2025 to win the WBA, WBC, and WBO super welterweight titles.

"Mayer vs. Cameron was a great fight that I truly hope everyone enjoyed. Although I don't feel I performed to my highest potential, I've been working hard to fix the mistakes I made and I will 100% come back stronger! I'm taking my belts back on December 24," said Mayer.

Oscar Collazo is among Puerto Rico's most popular young champions. Photo: Golden Boy Boxing

Most Valuable Promotions (MVP) confirmed an additional world championship matchup for The Last Walk: Amanda Serrano vs. Skye Falzon on Saturday, January 9 at Coliseo de Puerto Rico in San Juan.

Puerto Rico's undefeated pound-for-pound star and reigning WBA and Ring Magazine minimumweight world champion Oscar Collazo (15-0, 12 KOs) will defend his titles against top-ranked Filipino challenger Joey Canoy (26-5-2, 15 KOs) in the 12-round co-main event at 105lbs.

Also added is MVP's undefeated featherweight contender Jan Paul Rivera (17-0, 7 KOs) in a 10-round bout against a to-be-announced opponent.

Shurretta Metcalf and Krystal Rosado have a lot to gain with a win. Photo: Most Valuable Promotions

Former IBF world champion Shurretta Metcalf (15-6-1, 2 KOs) will also return in an eight-round bantamweight rematch against MVP's rising contender and Amanda Serrano protege Krystal Rosado (10-1, 2 KOs), who seeks redemption after Metcalf handed Rosado her first professional defeat in October 2025.

Nakisa Bidarian and Jake Paul, Founders of Most Valuable Promotions, said: "Amanda Serrano's final fight deserves a card worthy of her extraordinary legacy, and we're delivering back-to-back championship fights alongside some of boxing's most compelling rising talent."

Oscar Collazo first captured the WBO World Minimumweight title in 2023, becoming the fastest Puerto Rican fighter to win a world championship in just seven professional bouts. He unified titles by winning the WBA belt in November 2024 and the division's first-ever Ring Magazine title. After successfully defending titles, "El Pupilo" moved up two weight classes and earned a knockout victory to win the WBO International flyweight title.

His opponent, Joey "The Babyface" Canoy is an experienced southpaw from the Philippines. The former WBC International minimumweight champion, Canoy is currently ranked second by the WBC, sixth by the WBO, WBA and Ring Magazine, and thirteenth by the IBF. He will make his U.S. debut against Collazo.

"It is a privilege for me to be part of this very special event, where we will have the chance to support our champion, Amanda Serrano, on a historic night — her farewell to boxing — taking place in front of her own people at the Coliseo de Puerto Rico," said Collazo. It is an honor to share this moment with her and to be part of a fight card that is so significant for our sport and for Puerto Rico.

"On Saturday, January 9th, we are going to leave everything in the ring and give Puerto Rico an unforgettable night! Amanda, count on me to celebrate this very special night together! Puerto Rico, I'll see you at the 'Choli'!"

Light heavyweight contender Iman Khataev and his friends will return to the ring on January 28 in Montreal. Photo: Eye of the Tiger Management

Eye of the Tiger Management's 2027 season gets underway on January 28 at the Casino de Montréal with Olympic medalist and NABF light heavyweight titlist Imam Khataev (13-1, 12 KOs) appearing in his first main event after three co-main event appearances.

The Tokyo 2020 Games bronze medal winner Khataev picked up the NABF title in January 2024 by stopping Polish champion Michael Ludwiczak in the second round. He went on to put four wins on his record before taking on David Morrell Jr., scoring the first knockdown against the highly regarded Cuban before having to settle for a split-decision loss in a highly controversial verdict.

Khataev has since put another trio of wins on his resume. Khataev is ranked third by the IBF and WBC, and sixth by the WBO in a highly competitive division. Khataev hopes to earn a world championship opportunity with a win on January 28. His opponent will be named at a future date.

Also appearing on the card are super middleweight Moreno Fendero of France, light heavyweight Mehmet Unal, Canadian Olympic medalist Wyatt Sanford, and newly signed Keven Beausejour, all undefeated.
