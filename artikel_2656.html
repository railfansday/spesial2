The Bengals and Steelers final score has never happened before in NFL history

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Earlier on Sunday, the Dallas Cowboys and San Fransisco 49ers played a game that ended with the 1099th unique score in NFL history. On Sunday night, the Cincinnati Bengals and Pittsburgh Steelers finished with the 1100th unique score in NFL history.

It's the second time this season we've had two Scorigamis in one weekend.

It is the second Bengals Scorigami of the year following their 43-36 win two weeks ago.

When we get a new final score in the NFL, it's called Scorigami. Normally when we get a Scorigami it's because a team scores an absolute boatload of points and often something weird happens, usually with two-point conversions. We got both on Sunday with the Bengals and Steelers.

The Bengals just kept scoring, taking a 31-3 game early in the fourth quarter. It's a pretty typical 28-point, four-touchdown lead. Nothing weird. Then the Pittsburgh Steelers scored a touchdown and went for two. If turned a three-touchdown deficit into a… three-touchdown deficit. Really not sure when the Steelers chose to do it. That 31-11 score would have been a Scorigami.

The Bengals scored again very quickly and kicked an extra point to make it 38-11. That would not have been Scorigami but the Steelers scored one last time and kicked the extra point to make it 38-18 for the Scorigami.

The only 38-point winners that haven't hit now are 38-2 and 38-4.
