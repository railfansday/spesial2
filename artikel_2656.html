Parker Washington TD catch gives Jacksonville early lead in Tennessee

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Jaguars wide receiver Parker Washington is making plays, as usual.

On Jacksonville’s first drive of today’s game in Tennessee, Washington caught a third-and-goal pass in the end zone to give the Jaguars a 7-0 lead.

Trevor Lawrence also hit Josh Cameron for a 28-yard gain on the 10-play, 60-yard touchdown drive. It looked easy for the Jaguars against the overmatched Titans.

A sixth-round pick in 2023, Washington is in the fourth and final year of his rookie contract, and he’s made himself a lot of money this season. Halfway through the season he already has a career-high six touchdown catches, and he’s on pace to eclipse his career highs in catches and yards as well. He’s a big part of the reason that the Jaguars are one of the best teams in the NFL.
