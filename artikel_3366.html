Why There Are No New NCIS, New York, & Origins Tonight (Nov 24) & When They Return For Fresh Episodes

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Tuesday nights are one of the best nights for TV. It means the NCIS trio takes pride of place on the small screen, and there's certainly a lot of excitement as the shows get ready for their winter breaks. It was just last year when CBS finally put NCIS back in its rightful place, kicking off Tuesdays in style, and it was joined by two other shows.

While there has been a small change with NCIS: Sydney not returning in the fall, the new spinoff NCIS: New York has more than made up for it. LL Cool J and Scott Caan's refreshing change has brought an energy to the screen that hasn't been seen in a few years, and the rest of the team brings mystery, intrigue, and humor. Sure, there is some disappointment in that NCIS: Origins season 3 will only have 10 episodes, but each of those installments features Mark Harmon as Gibbs once more, bringing much-needed answers about Lala and a new mystery to unravel.

As for the flagship series, NCIS season 24 has gained one thing that was missing: Tony DiNozzo. Michael Weatherly's return has proven DiNozzo was the missing piece on NCIS for the longest time, while delivering a mature man worthy of the director's chair. With all this, there is the understandable question about why there are no new episodes of the NCIS universe on CBS tonight.

After just taking a week off due to the midterm elections, the NCIS franchise barely gives anyone time to recover as it takes another break. This time it's linked to Thanksgiving, as CBS has opted to give almost all shows on the schedule a break. Usually, Thursday and Friday shows are preempted because of Thanksgiving itself, and it's become expected since people want to gather with family or friends, and they're more likely to put the football on.

Taking a break earlier in the week hasn't been as common in the past. While the NCIS shows took time off in 2025, they didn't in 2024. However, as viewership has declined over the years, it makes sense to preempt most shows as people get ready to spend time with family, with some traveling during Thanksgiving Eve or earlier to beat the holiday rush.

Instead of airing new episodes, CBS will still have a night of NCIS, packing the November 24 schedule with reruns. It all starts with a rerun of the NCIS season 23 episode titled "Her," which saw the return of Ellie Bishop, finally offering closure for viewers and Torres. After that, the pilot episode of NCIS: New York will air, offering those who may have missed it a chance to see LL Cool J's newest series introduction.

While NCIS: Origins usually airs in the final slot of the night, that's not happening tonight. Unfortunately, CBS has opted for another NCIS rerun instead, airing another NCIS season 23 episode, titled "Fleeting." This brought far more trouble to Fleet Week than normal, with a sailor turning up dead in the trunk of a car.

While the break is disappointing, there is some good news. The break is not a long one, with all three NCIS shows set to return on December 1, 2026, with all-new episodes on CBS. Don't forget there's also a chance to watch whichever NCIS episode you want on Paramount+.

In even better news, these episodes won't be the fall finales. While NBC and ABC have opted to bring many of their shows to a close before Thanksgiving, CBS will continue to air new episodes later into December. NCIS, New York, and Origins will all air their fall finales on December 8, 2026, and even the winter hiatus isn't going to be that long.

When NCIS season 24 returns, it will be with an episode titled "Lucky Shot." MCRT is pulled into a case that involves a freelance nightcrawler videographer who turns up dead. It's soon clear that he was murdered, and the motive is linked to evidence he secretly recorded connected to a cover-up. Not only will the team need to figure out who the murderer is, but they'll also need to uncover a conspiracy that brings danger at every turn.

NCIS: New York will deliver its seventh hour of the season, titled "The River." Like other episodes in the new LL Cool J series, this one won't necessarily involve a murder. It could turn out that way, but the team is called in when a Marine sergeant's car is discovered after he fled Quantico. This Marine sergeant was supposed to be going on a high-stakes mission, and now the question is whether he left on his own accord or if something more serious is going on.

Although it was a gamble, the NCIS franchise taking a risk with its future is already paying off for CBS as its newest series continues to roll out.

In the final hour of the night, NCIS: Origins returns with en episode titled "Don't Let the Day Go By." This is a huge hour in the present day, as Gibbs turns to another former colleague and friend, Vera. Roma Maffia returns to the universe once more, reprising her role as Vera Strickland, to help Gibbs get to the bottom of those who are after him. Of course, he now needs to figure out what really happened to Lala, and why she wasn't on that plane.

In the past, the team would need to deal with the murder of a Marine. This is far more than a traditional murder, though, as the Marine is missing a tongue, and that usually means some sort of mob or cartel is involved. In this case, the team will come face to face with a Russian serial killer.

The fall finales of all three NCIS universe shows will air on December 8, 2026. It will then be time for the winter hiatus, but get ready for all three shows to be back before January comes to an end. CBS has set the date as January 12, 2027.
