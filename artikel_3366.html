‘The Hunting Wives’ Season 2 Officially Hits Netflix Tomorrow

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Netflix has enjoyed its fair share of success stories when it comes to novel adaptations, like His & Hers earlier this year or the ever-popular Bridgerton. However, there were few bigger breakout hits for the platform last year than Rebecca Cutter's take on May Cobb's steamy, dangerous mystery thriller The Hunting Wives. Acquired from Starz last summer, the Brittany Snow and Malin Åkerman-led drama became an instant streaming favorite, racking up an eye-opening 2 billion minutes viewed within its first two weeks. That was with only U.S. rights secured, too. The immediate reaction spurred Netflix to order a second and, eventually, a third season, betting big on how Cutter and company could build upon where Cobb's novel ends.

Now, fans are about to see how the story continues. Season 2 of The Hunting Wives is officially just around the corner, with eight new episodes debuting tomorrow on Thanksgiving. The release comes just in time to give fans a chance to dig into more East Texas drama throughout the holiday weekend. It'll take viewers beyond the bounds of Cobb's original novel, picking up from an explosive finale that sees Sophie (Snow) learning the truth behind Abby's murder before doing a bit of killing herself after hitting Kyle (Michael Aaron Milligan) with her car and dumping his body off a cliff. Everyone's a killer now, and that complicates things for both the Maple Brook newcomer and her socialite lover-turned-foe, Margo (Åkerman).

Cutter previously teased to Tudum that Season 2 would be "another sexy, twisted, batshit crazy ride through Maple Brook," escalating the consequences from an inaugural run full of body bags. Although Sophie and Margo are on the outs, they'll quickly be thrust back together thanks to new forces in town and old secrets requiring them to reluctantly bury the hatchet. However, their crimes are bound to catch up with them as they continue to play a dangerous game, putting them in a bind where they may not be the hunters after all. More drama, murder, manipulation, and seduction are expected to follow as the walls close in around them, and they stare down the barrel of a little "Texas justice."

A great detective is really just a personality with a method attached, which is why we keep remaking the same four of them. Here is yours, what it says about you, and the case for them being the best in the business.

You let people think you are not a threat and then you simply never go away. Columbo is the only major detective show that tells you who did it in the first ten minutes, because the pleasure was never the puzzle, it was watching a rich man slowly realise he is in trouble.

You are the most interesting person in the room and you know exactly how to use it. Rian Johnson built Blanc as a gentleman detective who is also an outsider everywhere he goes, which lets him be charming at people who would never otherwise talk to him.

Everybody tells you everything, and you have never once asked them to. Jessica Fletcher is a crime novelist from a small town in Maine who is so warm and so obviously trustworthy that suspects confess to her essentially as a social courtesy.

You expect to be lied to and you are almost never disappointed. Chandler wrote Marlowe as a man with a rigid private code in a city that has none, which is why he takes the case, solves it, gets nothing out of it and takes the next one.

Between its addictive, soapy depiction of the messy lives led by the titular Texas socialites and the murder mystery at its center, The Hunting Wives Season 1 became a critic darling with an 81% score on Rotten Tomatoes. For Season 2, the series is returning locked and loaded with a lot of new talent. Full House star John Stamos leads the latest residents of Maple Brook, alongside Emmy nominee Dale Dickey, Cam Gigandet, Kim Matula, and Alex FitzAlan. Snow and Åkerman will lead a returning group that includes Dermot Mulroney, Evan Jonigkeit, George Ferrier, Karen Rodriguez, Hunter Emery, and Branton Box.

The Hunting Wives Season 2 arrives on Netflix tomorrow, November 26. Stay tuned here at Collider for more on all the biggest titles coming to streaming throughout the year.
