Join us for all the build-up and action as it happens from today’s five Serie A games, Juventus-Venezia, Bologna-Udinese, Sassuolo-Genoa, Milan-Frosinone and Atalanta-Inter.

We begin at 11.30 GMT (12.30 CET) with Juventus in dire need of a victory as they host revitalised Venezia.

At 14.00 GMT (15.00 CET), new Udinese coach Paolo Zanetti visits on form Bologna, while Sassuolo welcome Roberto D’Aversa for his debut against Genoa.

Milan under pressure take on impressive high-flyers Frosinone at 17.00 GMT (18.00 CET), then Inter visit Maurizio Sarri’s Atalanta at 19.45 GMT (20.45 CET).
