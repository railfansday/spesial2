Xos Inc (XOS) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Power products now drive most of revenue as management revises full-year guidance.

Xos, Inc. (XOS -4.58%) reported a strategic expansion of its energy storage and power business, with the Xos Hub and powertrain segments accounting for 72% of revenue in the first nine months of 2026. Management reported sequential improvements in revenue and gross margin, supported by a shift toward higher-margin power products and continued cost discipline. The company received its first defense contract from the U.S. Air Force and continued expanding hub deployments with utilities and school districts. Management revised its full year 2026 guidance to reflect shifts in delivery timing while indicating that power products are expected to be the primary growth driver in 2027.
