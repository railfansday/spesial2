Revolut paid plans now include a free monthly Dott ride in 11 countries

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Premium, Metal, Ultra, and student customers get up to 30 free minutes a month on Dott e-scooters and e-bikes.

Eligible Revolut customers can activate a free monthly Dott ride inside the app

Revolut customers on paid plans are getting a new perk: one free Dott ride every month. The fintech and the scooter and e-bike company announced the deal, and it covers 11 European countries.

Premium customers get up to 15 free minutes a month, Metal customers up to 20, and Ultra up to 30, on either an e-scooter or an e-bike. The unlock fee is waived as well, as students get a 10-minute ride, available in the Netherlands, Italy, and Spain for now.

The offer is live in Austria, Belgium, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands, Poland, Spain, and Switzerland, the countries where both companies operate.

To claim it, riders open the Dott benefit inside the Revolut app, which issues a code straight away. Both offers may reach more countries during the partnership, the companies said.

The two companies already work together: Dott added Revolut Pay to its checkout last year, letting riders pay for trips straight from their Revolut account.

“After the integration of Revolut Pay into Dott’s payment checkout last year, partnering further with Revolut allows us to bring clean, convenient transport solutions directly to millions of active customers, in a very seamless way” said Matthieu Faure, VP Brand, Comms & Global Partnerships of Dott.

“By integrating Dott benefits straight into the Revolut app, we are making sustainable urban travel even more rewarding and accessible across key European cities.”

Dott became the largest operator of its kind in Europe through the TIER-Dott merger, completed in March 2024. It now runs 200,000 shared vehicles across 400 cities in Europe and the Middle East, and says its 12 million users have taken more than 500 million rides so far.

Putting its vehicles inside bigger consumer apps has become a habit for Dott. A similar deal in April put free monthly rides inside Wolt’s subscription scheme in eight countries, and Dott vehicles already appear in the FREE NOW and Citymapper apps.

The draw for Dott is Revolut’s reach: more than 80 million customers, who make over a billion transactions a month between them. The fintech also remains Europe’s most valuable private tech company.
