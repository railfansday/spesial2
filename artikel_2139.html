West Ham man picked out for high praise by Joe Cole amid ‘so much quality’ claim

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

West Ham managed to keep hold of the forward in the last transfer window following their relegation from the Premier League. WHUFC slipped out of the top flight after they finished in the bottom three along with Wolves and Burnley.

Bowen, 29, stayed put at the London Stadium in the summer and he remains under contract with the Hammers until June 2030. He has made 18 appearances in all competitions this season, 15 of which have come in the Championship, and he has chipped in with nine goals and eight assists.

Cole has been impressed and he has delivered his verdict, as per the official club website: “Some of the quality is fantastic. I watched the second half of the Cardiff game the other night and some of the football we play, Jarrod Bowen, the quality he brings, particularly at this level, he has so much quality.

“You’ve just got to shut the back door now. You know you’re going to score goals. Out of all the things you want to improve on at a club, as a coach, defending is the easiest one to do.”

Bowen showed a lot of loyalty by remaining with West Ham in the second tier. WHUFC could lose him next year though if they don’t go up.

The winger is far too good to be playing in the EFL. He is a cheat code at this level and can cause serious problems to opposition defensives.

The Hammers are the top scorers in the division having found the net on a whopping 37 occasions so far in this campaign. They need to tighten things up at the back though and have conceded too many goals to date.

Bowen linked up with West Ham back in January 2020 from Hull City and the ex-Hereford United man hasn’t looked back since. He has scored 94 goals in 296 matches for the London outfit to date.

West Ham have recently turned to Scott Parker as their new head coach. WHUFC have appointed the former Fulham, AFC Bournemouth, Club Brugge and Burnley man after their decision to part ways with Nuno Espirito Santo.

Their ex-midfielder drew his first game in charge away at Cardiff City. His side then lost 2-1 against Blackburn Rovers at Ewood Park last time out before the international break.

The Hammers are back in action this weekend with a home clash against Preston North End as they look to return to winning ways. Their upcoming opponents are in the drop zone along with Burnley and Derby County after a tough start to this term.

West Ham are sat in 9th place in the table after 15 fixtures. They are one point off the play-offs behind QPR and Southampton.
