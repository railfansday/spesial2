Rose Byrne To Head 2027 Tropfest Jury

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: Rose Byrne will head the jury at the 2027 Tropfest.

The long-running short film festival will take place on February 13, 2027, in The Domain in Sydney. Byrne previously served as the Tropfest jury head in 2017.

This year, the Tropfest films will stream globally on YouTube. The festival will feature as a week-long cultural event titled TropWeek. Programming across the week includes a three-day educational program at the National Institute of Dramatic Art (NIDA) exploring the craft and business of filmmaking, supported by Screen Australia and YouTube. The week culminates in a free day-to-night festival in The Domain on February 13, with live entertainment, followed by the evening screening of 16 world premiere shorts. Competition winners will be announced live on the night and via the YouTube livestream. Watch on Deadline

Tropfest film submissions open on Tuesday 1 December and close on Tuesday 5 January 2027. Filmmakers are required to create a short film of seven minutes or less featuring the 2027 Tropfest signature item: a necklace.

Prizes for winning filmmakers include a share of the CommBank Tropfest Emerging Filmmakers Fund’s $100,000. First, second, and third prizes will be $50,000, $30,000, and $20,000, respectively.

The Tropfest Foundation, a not-for-profit organisation launched in 2025, is led by a board comprising Chair Sarah Murdoch, Bryan Brown, Peter V’landys, Richard Weinberg and John Polson. Britt Wickes serves as Executive Director.

“I have such fond memories of watching great shorts at Tropfest with thousands of people under the stars in my hometown of Sydney,” Byrne said in a statement. “I’m thrilled to be back to lead the jury in 2027 and can’t wait to see what stories Australia’s next generation of filmmakers will tell.”
