Hull City man faces £0 exit: Lincoln City & Bradford must be on alert

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Hull City's return to the Premier League has led to many short-term changes at the MKM Stadium as Sergej Jakirovic looks to ensure a first-time survival bid is successful.

The Tigers, of course, achieved one of the most unexpected promotions in Championship history in May by defeating Middlesbrough in the play-off final at Wembley Stadium.

City had been operating under a strict transfer embargo for the entire campaign, but have since attracted a plethora of new additions and broke their transfer record when acquiring Nobel Mendy from Rayo Vallecano.

This, in turn, has led to question marks over the future of some that were a part of Jakirovic's pre-existing squad, with Kieran Dowell among those who were omitted from the club's 25-man Premier League rota.

Having not featured since the EFL Cup penalty shootout win against Stoke City in August, the midfielder potentially faces interest from suitors in January, 12 months after arriving from Rangers on an 18-month deal.

The 29-year-old has only made 16 appearances for Hull, scoring twice in the process, but does offer extensive EFL experience, particularly at Championship level.

With that in mind, Football League World have identified two clubs who should be ready to lodge January offers for the playmaker.

As previously mentioned, Dowell has featured for several Championship clubs since coming through the Everton academy ranks, with Hull the latest of those.

Having played a bit-part role in the Tigers' promotion, it was reported on Deadline Day that Sheffield United had rejected a potential reunion with their former loanee, but that shouldn't stop other second tier suitors from being on red alert, especially Lincoln City.

The Imps have several seasoned campaigners amongst their ranks as they look to consolidate in the division after winning League One, but it is fair to say that more creative quality could be needed during the gruelling second half of the season.

City have averaged one goal per game so far, which is solid if not spectacular, and the majority of those have been shared around the team. Ben House has currently provided the joint-highest number of assists for Lincoln so far this season with two in 12 appearances, and whilst it remains to be seen if the 27-year-old can be ousted from the starting lineup anytime soon, adding further depth with Dowell's addition would at least be a strong option to call upon off the bench.

Dowell has recorded 39 goal contributions at the level during his career, and whilst looking to get that back on track, his added quality could help create far more chances for Chris Cohen and Tom Shaw's side to edge narrow contests which will be crucial in aiding a survival bid, particularly if their poor recent run continues ahead of the January window.

Bradford City will be hoping to achieve promotion themselves this season, although the Michael Duff era at Valley Parade hasn't got off to the smoothest of starts in League One.

The Bantams have won just once in the league under their newly-appointed head coach, slipping outside the early-season play-off places as a result.

The West Yorkshire side do have plenty of quality and experience to call upon relative to the level, but have only scored 10 times so far this season, which is a concern given the club's overall ambition.

Only six assists have been registered among the squad, which includes one from experienced defender, Matthew Pennington, backing up the fact that further creativity is needed.

A short-term move for Dowell in January would be an extremely viable option, with the chance of helping a 'sleeping giant' achieve success also attractive for many players who have lacked recent gametime.

Dowell's versatility would allow him to feature in various positions across Duff's adopted 3-4-2-1 system too, which could also allow Kayden Jackson to move back into his natural centre-forward position and start firing once more after a 13-game goalless run in the league so far.

Duff would also be encouraged by how impactful the Ormskirk native's loan switch from Rangers to Birmingham City was two seasons ago, with five goals and assists in 23 appearances helping Chris Davies' side break the EFL's all-time points record.
