Wouter Vrancken reveals Hearts star in hospital after Hibs defeat

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Wouter Vrancken has revealed Tomas Magnusson was taken to hospital after being forced off for Heart of Midlothian in their Premier Sports Cup semi-final defeat to Hibernian.

The manager was left deflated at full-time as his side fell 2-1 to their rivals with a return to Hampden Park next month within touching distance.

The Jambos had led the action within the first four minutes when Claudio Braga found the net but their opponents turned the game on its head before half-time thanks to goals from Martin Boyle and Jamie McGrath.

Vrancken was concerned with more crocked stars with both Magnusson and Calvin Miller having to go off injured.

And the Belgian head coach has now explained the extent of those issues. "I didn't see Tomas because Tomas is to the hospital," said Vrancken. "Calvin was in a brace but it's always difficult to say directly after the game. So we have to wait for a scan tomorrow or the day after.

"The doctor and medical staff, they did their concussion test on the pitch, at that moment. (Magnusson) passed the concussion test and then he came to me to come in.

"Then he was going left and right, he couldn't stand on his legs anymore. When I saw it, there's no doubt in your head that you're going to let him on the pitch at that moment, because you cannot take the risk.

"Then he saw it himself also at the moment. He said, 'no, I can't do it. And then we had to change him, and now he's to the hospital, so I don't know."
