Tom Brady and Mahomes ‘warping our minds’ puts Caleb Williams in difficult position, says Nick Wright

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Tom Brady and Patrick Mahomes are the gold standards to judge NFL quarterbacks on, but have they made things difficult for those following them?

The current NFL is blessed with a rich crop of quarterbacks, most of whom promise to become the backbone of this league.

From upstarts like Fernando Mendoza, to growing vets like Caleb Williams, and the old guard like Patrick Mahomes, there's something for everybody.

However, Nick Wright says Tom Brady and Mahomes have "warped our minds" in such a way that it is making things impossibly difficult for Williams, in particular.

Mahomes and Brady have rewritten the record books in the NFL, with the latter, in particular, defying all expectations with his winning record.

Mahomes is as close to a serial winner as it gets to Brady, and together, they have set impossibly high expectations for those who follow them.

Chicago Bears' Caleb Williams is already on the path to greatness despite not being competitively quite there yet, and Wright believes that he will become the latest to fail on the Brady-Mahomes standard.

Speaking on The Herd with Colin Cowherd, he said: "I think what you're talking about, overall expectations, is a really useful exercise because Brady and Patrick have warped our minds on what is real for everyone else. Patrick's been to five of the last seven Super Bowls, won three of them. Brady went to 10 Super Bowls, won seven of them.

"For every other quarterback ever, making two and winning one is the best-case realistic scenario. For Caleb, I think he will win an MVP or two, and I think he will win a Super Bowl. I think that will make him, you know, right there, one of the 20 greatest quarterbacks ever."

Brady exists on an entirely different plane, and Mahomes is just beneath it, and those two, combined, exist many steps above the rest of the class.

Can anyone get close to them by having the talent, skill, determination, and longevity? It remains to be seen. But as it stands, they have rewritten what's possible for a quarterback in the NFL, and judging anyone by those standards is bound to leave them disappointed.
