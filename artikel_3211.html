Bob Greenblatt Opines Why Viewers Aren’t Sampling New Shows Today: “It’s Easier To Just Escape To Seinfeld or Downton Abbey”

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Veteran TV producer Bob Greenblatt understands why streaming old shows like Cheers or Mad Men is a far more appealing alternative to viewers these days than trying to vibe with a new series.

In an interview with the New York Times, Greenblatt admitted that it’s easier to be a producer on an old hit that’s enjoying a second life in streaming than it is to launch “a whole new show to the marketplace.”

“We’re still being pummeled by new shows on every streaming service and rather than try to figure out what’s worth committing to, it’s easier to just escape into Seinfeld or Downtown Abbey because you know exactly what you’re getting,” said Greenblatt, who’s currently an EP on The Gilded Age.

Greenblatt’s comments were included in a story about the growing popularity of old TV hits on platforms like Netflix and Amazon Prime. Luminate reports that viewership of old titles during this year’s third quarter increased more than 40 percent among the major streaming services, compared with the same period last year.
