‘Dancing With The Stars’ Finale Will Go Live Across The Country For The First Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: ABC is going all in for the season finale of Dancing with the Stars.

Deadline understands that the Disney-owned network is going to air the Season 35 finale live around the country for the first time in its 21-year history.

The move will see ABC air the finale on November 24 at 5 p.m. PT/8 p.m. ET. Normally, viewers on the West Coast are forced to wait until 8 PT to watch their favorite dancers. The three-hour finale also will re-air at 8 p.m. PT and stream via Hulu the next day.

The most significant impact of the move is that viewers on the West Coast for the first time will be able to vote live while watching the show.

This comes as the semi-final airs this evening with six celebrities left to waltz across screens: Tatyana Ali, who is dancing with Jan Ravnik; Ezra French, who is dancing with Daniella Karagach; Amber Glenn, who is dancing with Pasha Pashkov; Maura Higgins, who is dancing with Mark Ballas; Harry Shum Jr., who is dancing with Jenna Johnson; and Connor Wood, who is dancing with Rylee Arnold. Only one of these couples is expected to be eliminated this evening with the remaining five competing for the Len Goodman Mirrorball Trophy next week with a Judges’ Choice Dance, the return of the Instant Dance Challenge and a Freestyle.

The show, which also streams on Disney+, is hosted by Alfonso Ribeiro and Julianne Hough, with Carrie Ann Inaba, Bruno Tonioli and Derek Hough as judges.

It comes as Dancing with the Stars continues to defy the odds and grow its audience, 21 years after launching on ABC. On Super Bowl Night, the show scored a nine-year ratings high among adults 18-49. The episode drew 6.59 million live + same-day viewers, according to Nielsen’s Big Data + Panel, up 2% from the week prior.

Its opening episode, which saw only the male celebrities and their pro partners compete, also saw a 24% increase on the previous season.

The show also scored its first Emmy nomination in a decade in the Outstanding Reality Competition Program category.

Dancing with the Stars is produced by BBC Studios. Conrad Green serves as showrunner and exec produces alongside Ryan O’Dowd and Deena Katz.
