Starwood Property Trust, Inc. (STWD -3.73%) reported third quarter results impacted by significant non-cash credit loss provisions driven by rising interest rates and worsening macroeconomic models. Management stated that the company remains focused on resolving legacy assets and putting capital to work in new investments, deploying record capital during the quarter despite market volatility. The company confirmed it is on track to meet its annual resolution target for the current year. Management indicated that while the real estate market is healing due to falling supply, the elevated forward interest rate curve has increased borrowing costs and impacted borrower refinancing capacity.
