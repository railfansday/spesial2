Plaid Is the Ultimate Holiday Print—These Pieces Feel Chic Yet Festive

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

From tartan mini skirts to peplum hem tops, these plaid pieces make the perfect seasonal additions to your fall and winter wardrobe.

When it comes to seasonal fashion, certain styles just scream holiday dressing. Case in point, plaid.

Timeless with a preppy flair, plaid prints come around every fall and winter—and for a good reason. They feel festive yet chic. In other words, the pattern is on-season without making you feel like a walking decoration.

From tartan mini skirts to warm flannels and cozy loungewear, we found plaid pieces you'll rock all November and December long, from Thanksgiving dinners to holiday parties.

While plaid prints are already innately seasonal, certain color combinations feel extra festive. Think autumnal earthy browns and pops of orange or winter-ready reds like bright cranberry and deep burgundy.
