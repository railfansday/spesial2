Joaquin Phoenix Stars in Jonathan Glazer-Directed Film for Gentle Monster’s AI-Powered Glasses

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The buzzy debut for the brand's Intelligent Eyewear collection seems to be a big hit already: Pre-orders for the 16-piece collection, powered by Google and Samsung, are already sold-out.

Joaquin Phoenix has teamed with filmmaker Jonathan Glazer to star in a trippy new campaign for Gentle Monster’s AI-powered eyewear collection.

The actor, who famously starred in Spike Jonze’s 2013 film Her about a man’s relationship with an operating system, wears pieces from the brand’s Intelligent Eyewear line as he finds himself in a series of odd scenes. It finds Phoenix’s character, a salesman named Rogers, arriving to a hotel to check-in for two nights. After settling into his room and falling asleep, the man wakes up and lumbers down the hall only to find two costumed characters, one red and one yellow, having a fistfight in a light-drenched room. “Can you please keep it down? I’m trying to get some sleep,” he says to the fighters. “I don’t want to be that guy. But really.” After a knockout, one of the characters approaches Rogers and speaks in an unintelligible language, which he is able to translate using his glasses. After the climactic scene, Rogers checks out early and makes a brisk exit as he’s watched intently by multiple hotel staffers. “The result is a cinematic experience that prioritizes atmosphere, emotion, and imagination over conventional product storytelling,” reads the Gentle Monster press release.

The 16-piece collection is powered by Google and Samsung and comes in a variety of shapes, colors and lens options ranging in price from $449 to $489. According to Gentle Monster’s website, the bulk of pre-orders are already sold-out.

In an interview with GQ, Phoenix said he had been wanting to work with Glazer for quite some time. The filmmaker is known for a long list of credits that includes Under the Skin, Birth, Sexy Beast and The Zone of Interest. He’s also directed spots for Prada and Apple.

“I’ve known him for years, and when we all approached him, we came up with this really interesting and offbeat idea that just [had so] much mood. He just gets super excited and inspired,” Phoenix told the outlet. But even though viewers may think of his 2013 film in connection to his new AI-fueled campaign, Phoenix told GQ that it wasn’t really even on his mind at all. “It’s funny because I think that sometimes films have more of, maybe, an impact on the people that view them than for me, but I hadn’t thought about that. Somebody just made a joke about it actually, and I was like, ‘Oh yeah, you’re right.'”

As for Glazer, he told GQ that the concept came to life for him with the character at rest: “The first image I had was him asleep, maybe dreaming, with the glasses all wonky across his face. The rest kind of grew from there.”

Gentle Monster is shining a light on its Intelligent Eyewear collection at a New York public preview now open at 23 Wall Street through Nov. 23.
