Macy’s Thanksgiving Parade To Feature All-Star Broadway Medley With ‘The Lost Boys’, ‘The Rocky Horror Show’, ‘Schmigadoon!’, ‘Wicked’ & More

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Lost Boys, The Rocky Horror Show and Schmigadoon! are among the Broadway newcomers that will celebrate Thanksgiving as part of an all-star performance at this year’s Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade, with long-running stalwarts The Book of Mormon, Chicago, Disney’s Aladdin, Hamilton, Rent and Wicked also coming together for the 10-minute medley.

The “All-Star Broadway Thanksgiving Day Celebration,” created specifically for the parade, will be introduced by Julianne Hough, star (with Neil Patrick Harris) of the upcoming Damn Yankees.

The medley performance is being arranged by Tony-, Emmy-, Grammy- and Pulitzer Prize-winning composer Tom Kitt (Next to Normal) and creative producer Sarah O’Gleby (2026 Tony Awards, Broadway’s Shucked). Watch on Deadline

In its announcement today, NBC and Macy’s said the medley “will take viewers on a musical journey through Broadway past and present, filled with beloved songs and Easter eggs for theater fans to discover along the way.”

This year’s 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade airs Thursday, November 26, on NBC and Peacock at 8:30 a.m. in all time zones.

Broadway has been a beloved part of Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade tradition for years. Over the parade’s century-long history, more than 100 different shows have performed as part of the festivities.

In addition to the Broadway celebration, the parade will feature performances from multi-platinum country sensation Megan Moroney, Grammy-nominated artist Teddy Swims and the Radio City Rockettes.

The parade will also feature performances by Natasha Bedingfield, Baha Men, Blues Traveler, DEVO, Jimmy Fallon & The Roots, Feid, EJAE, Mckenna Grace, Indigo Girls, Icona Pop, JoJo, Wyclef Jean, Queen Latifah, Il Volo, Natalie Merchant, Jamie Miller, Ryan Peter Murphy, Leslie Odom Jr., Keke Palmer, Anthony Ramos, RIIZE and Smash Mouth; and special appearances by Jack McBrayer, Janet McTeer, John Stamos, Melissa Joan Hart, Megan Hilty, Holly Robinson Peete, Paddington the Musical and Rodney Peete.

The Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade broadcast is produced by Universal Television in conjunction with Done + Dusted Inc. Katy Mullan and Liz Kelly serve as executive producers, with RJ Durrell, Nick Flores, and Patrick Menton serving as co-executive producers.
