Marvel Officially Announces New Hulk Era, Age Of Monsters

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The "Age of Heroes" is over, and the "Age of Monsters" is here, as the Marvel Universe faces the wrath of the Infernal Hulk. The darkest, most dangerous incarnation of the Hulk ever has been on the rampage, trying to bring about a new global order ruled by darkness and chaos. It has been Infernal Hulk vs. everybody, from Wolverine, to Spider-Man, to the entire Avengers roster, but nothing has been able to slow him down. Now, his Infernal Rage is going to get even more intense and seemingly unstoppable.

That's the name of the newly announced five-issue limited series that furthers the ongoing Infernal Hulk storyline. According to Marvel, Hulk War: Infernal Rage is the "devastating conclusion" to the arc, after which the landscape of Marvel's Hulk lore should be expected to look very different.

The synopsis for the series teases "a new Gray Hulk," "a shocking face from Bruce Banner's past" making a comeback, and lots of things getting smashed, as New York becomes a supernatural war zone where the last stand against the Infernal Hulk is going to be made.

As part of his endgame plan, the Infernal Hulk is unveiling his own "Infernal Avengers" team, who will be in action throughout the climactic five issues of the Hulk War storyline.

Here's the full synopsis, just released by Marvel, for the February debut of the miniseries:

The final battle to stop the corruption of the Marvel Universe begins!

As Manhattan falls to an army of twisted monsters and the Baxter Building becomes the heart of a nightmare, the Infernal Hulk unveils his fallen Avengers and a plan that could transform all life on Earth. But a new Gray Hulk enters the war with no grand mission and no noble cause. He’s here for one thing: to smash the Infernal Hulk into the ground. And before the battle ends, a shocking face from Bruce Banner’s past returns.

Infernal Rage promises the culmination of a years-long arc, which kicked into full gear when Hulk's body was usurped by the villainous entity known as the Eldest. The villain's goal since day one has been to inaugurate the "Age of Monsters" on Earth, and it seems possible that it could be successful before Infernal Rage is over.

Whatever happens, it is going to mark the final stage of one Hulk era, and the genesis of another. Over the past several years, Hulk has had a creative renaissance; first as a body horror series, and then as a full-blown supernatural horror saga. When the dust settles after Infernal Rage, there's a good chance it will mean a major reset for the character.

The Infernal Hulk is, in a way, an evolution of what the character could have been if he hadn't join the Avengers way back when the team first formed. Hulk has been an antagonist before, but never a full-blown villain, at least not on the level of Infernal Hulk. Eventually, the character has to go back to the status quo of "Bruce Banner = Hulk," but while it has lasted, the "Infernal Hulk" arc has delivered something refreshing and revitalizing for the character.

In a change that will reshape the entire Marvel universe, Bruce Banner is no longer the Hulk, replaced by someone who is "ten times the monster."

That is, it will be a welcome return to form when Bruce Banner becomes the classic Hulk again. That may or may not come in Hulk War: Infernal Rage. It may or may not come in 2027. But with the franchise poised to start up a new era, anything is on the table. In the meantime, fans who haven't caught up on the "Infernal Hulk" era still have time to binge it before the final stretch kicks off early next year.

Hulk War: Infernal Rage #1 debuts February 3, 2027 from Marvel Comics.
