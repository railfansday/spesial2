Does every villain need a backstory reveal? No. But with Hollywood revisiting existing stories and old IP, it seems even the bad guys need a second chance. Recently, Netflix released a film based on a popular fairy tale, but from a different character's perspective, and it has become a streaming hit. The fairy tale in question is Cinderella, a story about a young girl forced into servitude by her evil stepmother and stepsisters; with the help of her Fairy Godmother, she goes to the ball and dances with the prince, but must leave before midnight. After leaving her glass slipper behind, the prince searches the kingdom for his true love and eventually reunites with Cinderella.

​​​​​​​This fairy tale has been adapted many times, including Disney, so much so that the character made her way to the Descendants franchise, revealing that Cinderella and the Prince have children. Recently, Netflix released an animated children's fantasy film called Steps, a retelling of Cinderella from the perspective of one of Cinderella's stepsisters, Lilith, voiced by Ali Wong. Steps was released on Netflix on November 20, 2026, and has since become a streaming hit, reaching #2 on Netflix's Top 10 Movie Charts Worldwide, between Guarding Stars and Heartland. Alongside its streaming success, Steps was well-received by fans and critics alike, earning a 78% critics score and a 71% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes.

Steps follows Lilith, one of Cinderella's stepsisters, whose family is treated as outsiders while Cinderella (Amanda Seyfried) earns the kingdom's sympathy following her father's death. The movie puts a twist on the classic fairy tale when Lilith steals the Fairy Godmother's (Bette Midler) wand and crashes the royal ball with her sister, Margot. However, things take another turn when Cinderella's rival, Priscilla (Nikki Glaser), steals the wand and uses its magic to make the prince fall in love with her and seize control of the kingdom. Now, Lilith and Cinderella must put aside their differences and work together to restore order.

Four Netflix characters, four completely different ways of showing up for somebody. This is the one who fits how you actually do friendship, and what having them in your life would be like.

You do not do friendship at half volume, and neither does he. What you want is somebody who says yes to the plan before you have finished describing the plan.

You are the friend who makes things happen, and so is she. Together you would be mildly exhausting to everyone around you and completely delighted with yourselves.

You have no patience for pleasant lies, and she has no ability to tell them. What looks like coldness from the outside is the most reliable friendship on this list.

You look after whoever is having the worst day. So does he. It is the least strategic way to move through the world and the best possible quality in a friend.

Steps isn't the only Cinderella retelling that puts the stepsisters in the spotlight. Back in 2007, Disney released Cinderella III: A Twist in Time. Like Steps, the movie features Cinderella's stepsister, Anastasia (Tress MacNeille), stealing the Fairy Godmother's (Russi Taylor) wand, and Lady Tremaine (Susanne Blakeslee), Cinderella's evil stepmother, using its magic to travel back in time and change the original movie's events, ensuring Anastasia marries the prince instead and leaving Cinderella (Jennifer Hale) to set things right.

While both movies feature a stepsister stealing a magic wand, Steps follows Lilith and Cinderella as they reconcile their differences and rebuild their relationship. Meanwhile, the Disney version has Anastasia learning that she wants to find her own true love rather than force someone to love her through magic. She eventually reconciles with Cinderella, while Drizella and Lady Tremaine face punishment for their actions.
