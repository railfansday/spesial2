Disney Officially Decides the Fate of James Cameron’s ‘Avatar 4’

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

It's a fairly ridiculous position for James Cameron to find himself in when making almost $1.5 billion at the worldwide box office is considered something of a disappointment, but that's the problem with having directed three of the highest-grossing movies in the history of cinema. Having spent much of the last two decades on Pandora, Cameron has been increasingly vocal about his desire to make other movies, with his planned adaptation of Joe Abercrombie's The Devils and his long-gestating Hiroshima project among those he's particularly keen to get behind the camera for. Whether Cameron gets to make those movies before heading back to Pandora, however, has been one of the bigger questions surrounding Disney's upcoming theatrical slate.

Disney has officially included Avatar 4 in its upcoming theatrical release slate, with the movie featured during the studio's Upfronts presentation in Buenos Aires, Argentina, where the fourth movie was listed alongside the likes of Black Panther 3 and Incredibles 3, which is particularly encouraging given Cameron's own uncertainty about making another trip to Pandora after Avatar: Fire and Ash.

Of course, some of Avatar 4 has already been shot, and not just recently. Ages ago in fact, Cameron filmed scenes involving the younger members of the Sully family alongside the previous two movies, given that the fourth installment will feature a time jump of around six years. Trinity Jo-Li Bliss, who plays Tuk, was just nine years old when she filmed her earlier scenes for the fourth movie. She is now 17. Yes, that's how long ago.

Despite Avatar: Fire and Ash making around $1.48 billion worldwide, that's still considerably less than the $2.32 billion made by Avatar: The Way of Water, and Cameron has spoken about wanting to bring down both the cost of making the movies and the amount of time he spends making them. Executive producer Rae Sanchini, however, told Inverse earlier this year that preparations for the next two movies were already underway, with the team working on budgets, scheduling, and technology.

The cast of Fire and Ash includes Sam Worthington (Horizon: An American Saga) as Jake Sully, Zoe Saldaña (Guardians of the Galaxy) as Neytiri, Sigourney Weaver (Alien) as Kiri, Stephen Lang (Don't Breathe) as Miles Quaritch, Oona Chaplin (Game of Thrones) as Varang, Britain Dalton as Lo'ak, Jack Champion (Scream VI) as Spider, and Bliss as Tuk.
