The Beatles Celebrate Liverpool in New Video for ‘In My Life’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Beatles celebrate their hometown of Liverpool in the new video for the Fab Four classic “In My Life.”

The visual, starring Adolescence’s Owen Cooper, follows both the arrival of the Rubber Soul reissue — where “In My Life” features — as well as the recent announcement that The Beatles Story immersive experience is moving into bigger digs in Liverpool, where the band was born.

The nostalgic video, co-directed by Jessica Swale and Irina Alexiu, follows three generations in a musical family living in Liverpool, and how the gift of music is passed down and history repeats itself in surprising ways, with the British city serving as the backdrop for all the action, playing up the “places I’ll remember.”

Eagle eyed Beatles fans might spot some Easter Eggs over the course of the “In My Life” video, like Cooper and friends reenacting the iconic Abbey Road walk in front of Liverpool’s Woolton Picture House, or a nod to George Harrison’s tumble in A Hard Day’s Night.

Earlier this month, Apple Corps. announced plans to relocate The Beatles Story from Albert Dock to the previous site of St Johns Market, located just across from the Liverpool Lime Street station. “I love Liverpool. It’s part of who I am, it’s part of who The Beatles are. If you’d told us this was going to happen when we were hanging out and getting started we’d never have believed it,” Paul McCartney said in a statement.

“The extraordinary plans the Apple Corps team have shown me for The Beatles Story take me right back to that journey, from Liverpool to the world and back home again. It couldn’t have happened without Liverpool and I can’t wait for people to be able to step into our story and share the experience.”
