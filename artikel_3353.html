Wheel Of Time: The Last Battle Officially Releasing June 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Robert Jordan's Wheel of Time culminates in one of the greatest showdowns between good and evil in all fantasy, but unfortunately, many people have never actually seen how The Last Battle plays out. Aside from there never being a proper live-action adaptation after the Amazon series' cancellation, there's actually a huge portion of readers that stall out due to fatigue in the middle of the series that's famously become known as "The Slog."

It's a shame, considering how spectacularly the series brings together its countless interconnected storylines, uniting an enormous cast of beloved characters for a truly unforgettable conclusion. The Last Battle, also known as Tarmon Gai'don, is the culmination of everything Jordan spent decades building toward, and it's an event that deserves to be experienced by fans beyond the original books.

Thankfully, that's finally about to become a reality, with an official new adaptation of The Last Battle set to arrive in 2027, offering fans an entirely new way to experience the legendary showdown.

War of the Dragon: The Wheel of Time is an upcoming grand strategy board game from Dire Wolf, the publisher behind Dune: Imperium and Clank!, that will finally give fans the story's finale in an entirely new way. It's also the first official board game adaptation of Robert Jordan's legendary fantasy series, and the project has attracted an enormous amount of attention after its wildly successful Kickstarter campaign.

Unlike traditional adaptations, War of the Dragon puts players directly in control of the conflict between the Light and the Shadow, allowing them to determine how the war unfolds. Players can take control of either side, assembling powerful allies, capturing territories, and preparing for the ultimate confrontation between Rand al'Thor's forces and the Dark One.

The game features two distinct modes, with Hero Mode offering a more accessible experience focused on building alliances and controlling the map. However, it's Epic Mode that fans are really stoked about as it will actually bring Jordan's sprawling fantasy saga to life, complete with massive armies and conflicts, character progression, and story-driven events.

Epic Mode begins with a prologue battle and spans four chapters before culminating in The Last Battle itself. Players commanding the Light can develop iconic characters like Rand, Mat, Perrin, Egwene, and Nynaeve, while those controlling the Shadow can unleash the Forsaken and strengthen the Dark One's forces.

Eventually, everything comes together in an enormous final confrontation, where players deploy the armies and characters they've assembled throughout the campaign. The decisions made along the way directly influence the outcome, so fans can actually experience an alternate version of The Last Battle where the Shadow emerges victorious.

War of the Dragon was massively successful with its crowdfunding campaign, raising over $1.16 million from more than 6,100 Kickstarter backers, absolutely demolishing its original $50,000 goal. The game is currently expected to begin reaching US Kickstarter supporters in the first half of 2027, with international fulfillment following afterward. A retail release is also planned for 2027.

While it certainly isn't a live-action adaptation, War of the Dragon offers something arguably just as exciting, which is the opportunity to rewrite the fate of the entire Wheel of Time universe themselves.
