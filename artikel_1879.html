Julian Alvarez has returned to form for Atletico Madrid following a summer saga that saw him want to leave and almost join Arsenal.

The 26-year-old was superb on Sunday as Diego Simeone's side beat Getafe 3-0 to keep on the tails of Barcelona and Real Madrid.

He had been left out of the squad at the start of the season following his demands to leave, with some suggesting that the fans would never take him back.

Alvarez had been desperate to join Barcelona, who had their interest in a potential move publicly denied and ruled out by Atleti.

Arsenal looked to capitalise on the situation and offer a route out for the former Manchester City forward, waiting until the final days of the window.

But the Argentine did not want the move, and Mikel Arteta was left with a summer that saw his squad bring in just one attacker after being depleted of three.

If not for the sudden improvement in Noni Madueke's form, Arsenal may have been in trouble going forward this season.

The Gunners have just one natural left-sided winger, are without Bukayo Saka through injury, and are yet to see either Kai Havertz or Viktor Gyokeres find their confidence this season.

Alvarez would have solved problems on the left of the attack and through the middle.

He was exceptional on Sunday, providing assists for both Alex Baena and Johnny Cardoso to make it three goals and three assists in his last four games for club and country.

And that was not all he did. Alvarez created four chances in total, more than anybody else on the pitch.

He took two shots on target and seven touches in the opposition box - the most. The forward also completed a successful dribble and made two clearances.

That will provide some encouragement to Arsenal, should they look to go back in for Alvarez next year.

Arsenal will be strengthened when Saka returns from injury, but they are still plenty lacking in attack.

Christos Tzolis desperately needs relief and the Gunners simply need more firepower going forward.

Links to Antonio Nusa have been strong, but it remains to be seen if a January move will be possible.

Arteta will hope to see at least something happen, with their hopes of a historic season in the balance.
