ANI Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (ANIP -2.01%) reported third quarter results that demonstrated 28% year-over-year revenue growth and announced a strategic offer to acquire Egetis Therapeutics for $560 million to expand its rare disease portfolio. Management lowered full year revenue guidance for Cortrophin Gel due to specialty pharmacy capacity constraints and slower-than-anticipated conversion of a doubling patient funnel in the gout segment. The company reported record quarterly operating cash flow of $83.8 million and raised its full year adjusted EBITDA and non-GAAP EPS guidance based on generic segment outperformance and improved margins. The Egetis acquisition brings Emcetate, the first approved treatment for MCT8 deficiency, which management expects will achieve peak annual sales exceeding $300 million.
