Hamilton denies lack of trust with Ferrari after strategy issues

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Lewis Hamilton ignored instructions from his Ferrari team to pit at recent races in Singapore and Brazil; the Brit insists there is no trust issue; watch the Las Vegas GP weekend on Sky Sports F1, with qualifying at 4am on Saturday and the race at 4am on Sunday

Lewis Hamilton has insisted there is no lack of trust between himself and his Ferrari team despite having overridden orders from the pit wall twice in recent races.

At the Singapore Grand Prix in October and the Sao Paulo Grand Prix earlier this month, Hamilton ignored instructions to pit, with his decision costing him places on both occasions.

The incidents followed several moments earlier in the season where Hamilton had been left frustrated by strategic decisions from Ferrari that failed to optimise his chances.

Asked ahead of this weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix whether taking matters into his own hands was indicative of a lack of trust, Hamilton insisted he was pleased with the work of his race engineer Carlo Santi.

The seven-time world champion said: "No, it's definitely not trusting. I think it's just we're constantly working on our communication. I think that it's been a lot better this year.

"Carlo has done an amazing job, and I think there's been a couple of times where it's just those particular two times was not, particularly the last time, was just I didn't have the information I needed, and I got the call at the last minute.

"And we'll just continue to work on the processes. We're super united and working, I think in the right direction."

Hamilton has also had to handle controversy with his team-mate Charles Leclerc, after a first-lap clash between the pair at the Mexico City Grand Prix resulted in something of a public fall out.

Hamilton was highly critical of Leclerc for aggressively attempting to challenge him for the lead on the first lap, with Red Bull's Max Verstappen taking advantage of the squabble to win a race that Ferrari likely would have claimed a one-two in had their drivers held position.

Both Hamilton and Leclerc said that a conversation in Brazil the week after the incident had resolved the situation, and the Brit on Wednesday reiterated his confidence in their working relationship moving forwards.

Please use Chrome browser for a more accessible video player

"I think Charles is a great driver," Hamilton said. "I think, as I said, the detail we went into about stuff like that in terms of when we close the visor, it's very clear we understand each other.

"We understand each other's boundaries and the ultimate goal, which is to get the best result for the team.

"So, I don't feel that that will be a problem moving forwards, and I have no concerns or worries about racing him. I don't think he does with me either."

Hamilton admitted he is unsure whether Ferrari will be able to challenge for victory in Las Vegas given the circuit is unlikely to suit their car.

The circuit's long straight could expose Ferrari's power unit weaknesses, while the cold temperatures could lead to issues heating up their tyres sufficiently.

Hamilton said: "We've had a car that's been quick through corners. Obviously, yeah, you have the long straight here. I don't know how that's going to be for us.

"We'll do the best job we can, but hopefully we can make up some of the time maybe in some of the corners. We'll have to wait and see."

Regardless of how competitive Ferrari are, Hamilton says the team will use this weekend, and the upcoming final two races of the season in Qatar and Abu Dhabi, to attempt to optimise their plans for the start of the 2027 season.

"This is a period of time… we don't have any new components to test, but this is definitely a really important data point for us to understand and make some changes setup wise, and try some things that we can potentially try to see if everything's working, if there's different elements of the car that we can remove or add, thinking about next year," he said.

"There's some big changes for next year. But otherwise, right now we have to focus on maintain second in the constructors' championship."
