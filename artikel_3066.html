Tanggal: 2026-11-16

CNSide moves from development to billing as payer coverage and trial milestones advance.

Cerenome, Inc. (CNSY -24.77%) reported its first quarter of commercial diagnostic revenue and the transition of its CNSide platform from a development project to a revenue-generating business. Management reported the expansion of its payer network to 190 million covered lives and the achievement of key enrollment milestones in its radiopharmaceutical pipeline. The company stated it has closed enrollment for its Phase II glioblastoma trial and presented interim findings for its multidose optimization study in leptomeningeal metastasis, which showed no dose-limiting toxicities. Management indicated that testing, billing, and collections are expected to accelerate in 2027 to materially offset the cash burn of its diagnostic segment.
