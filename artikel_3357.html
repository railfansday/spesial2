‘Peaky Blinders’ Creator’s Sci-Fi Series Officially Extends Its Impressive Streaming Run

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Apple TV's library has become a favorite for sci-fi lovers, offering a steady stream of high-budget, thought-provoking originals. Rather than relying on algorithms, the platform invests heavily in ambitious world-building and literary adaptations. Viewers can explore the alternate-history space race of For All Mankind, the unsettling corporate mysteries of Severance, and the sprawling galactic politics of Foundation. Hits like Silo, Dark Matter, and Pluribus further prove the streamer's commitment to smart storytelling. By giving creators the resources and multi-season security needed to tell complete stories, Apple TV has firmly established itself as the premier destination for modern science fiction.

The streamer's storytelling genius was visible from the word go. It launched with two sci-fi shows that have taken different paths. For All Mankind, the expansive space drama starring Joel Kinnaman, has aired for seven years, with the final season coming soon. Meanwhile, the other sci-fi series See concluded three years ago after three action-packed seasons. While For All Mankind was an immediate critical hit, See struggled to show its vision, especially in its first season. Professional reviewers didn't appreciate the violence that was part of the Jason Momoa-led thriller.

Viewers, on the other hand, loved the world-building, performances, and gore, giving the season an 83% score versus 44% for critics on Rotten Tomatoes. The series built a cult following that saw it through three seasons before it concluded in 2022. Now the series is once again proving itself a fan favorite after surging in streaming for the past several weeks. The surge has coincided with Apple TV's expansion into over fifty more countries, and based on where the show is trending, it has found an entirely new audience three years after the finale. It is ranked eighth globally by FlixPatrol at the time of writing.

Created by Peaky Blinders creator Steven Knight, See is a dystopian sci-fi thriller that leans into its thriller elements. It is set in a distant future where humanity has lost the sense of sight and has adapted to this world. Society has regressed, religiosity has risen, and sight is the enemy. The Payan Kingdom has outlawed sight, and any child who is born with it — or even rumored to have it — is supposed to be eliminated. But Momoa's Baba Voss is not about to let anyone kill his children, who were born with sight; he would rather kill them first. See tells a tale of a brutal world with immersive worldbuilding and thrilling action.

All seasons of See are available to stream on Apple TV. Stay tuned to Collider for more updates.
