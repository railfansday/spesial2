Star Trek Officially Returns To The Deep Space Nine Era Today

The Star Trek franchise has spent the better part of the past six decades exploring different corners of the galaxy, expanding with each release, but in today's newest release, the franchise is revisiting the Star Trek: Deep Space Nine era among others. Although the original series established the foundation for what would become a sci-fi phenomenon, the TV and film productions that have come after Star Trek: The Original Series have all been able to explore further reaches of the universe, creating an undeniably thorough franchise. Some eras have become important to building the legacy, while others have helped bridge the gaps in the larger story.

Regardless of how popular an era has been, Star Trek has been able to introduce some of the most recognizable characters in modern fiction throughout the franchise, changing the face of sci-fi in TV, film, and on the page. Star Trek's TV presence, specifically, has fundamentally changed what a sci-fi drama on the small screen could accomplish, and continues to push the boundaries. Star Trek: Deep Space Nine, specifically, was a strong era for the franchise. Beginning in 1993, the series abandoned the typical format viewers were used to and explored long-running conflicts using a familiar crew and a stationary space station.

Now, as Star Trek celebrates its 60th anniversary as a franchise, revisiting some of its strongest eras has never been easier than with Star Trek Timelines, a brand-new release that comes out today, Tuesday, November 24. The official visual history of the franchise, Star Trek Timelines brings each of the show's eras into focus for a deep exploration.

Today's release of Star Trek Timelines: A Visual Voyage Through Generations of History marks a whole new exploration of the franchise and celebrates its milestone 60th anniversary. One of the most important eras of the franchise's history began with the debut of Star Trek: Deep Space Nine in 1993. The series, which saw a major departure from the traditional way Star Trek stories were told, was able to reinvigorate the franchise for a whole new generation of viewers. With the series having gone against the established formula that worked for the Star Trek franchise, Deep Space Nine opened new doors.

The show's stationary setting allowed Deep Space Nine to develop its plots with more depth, reaching for larger thematic elements and baking them into each story with a different type of precision than other franchise series had allowed for. A highly regarded era of the franchise, the new book breaks down the era visually, expanding on how the show's rich style and different elements allowed for an entirely different experience than what Star Trek enthusiasts had experienced before. Challenging the franchise on nearly every front, Deep Space Nine is one of the most important pieces of the Star Trek journey, and is honored in Star Trek Timelines.

Star Trek Timelines: A Visual Voyage Through Generations of History is available for purchase through this link.

With Star Trek Timelines, written by Derek Tyler Attico, Michael Dismuke, Kelli Fitzpatrick, and Jim Johnson, readers will be able to explore the franchise from its early beginnings through its latest series, all with the expert knowledge that comes from its authors. Examining the timelines of each series thoroughly, the book looks at alternate timelines as well, including the Mirror universe, the Kelvin timeline, and the Year of Hell storyline from Star Trek: Voyager. With character-focused spreads that trace each major figure's development through the franchise and a ton of information that Star Trek fans will enjoy, the book is a wonderful resource.

While Star Trek Timelines isn't exclusively dedicated to an exploration of Star Trek: Deep Space Nine, the comprehensive approach of the book gives its readers the opportunity to explore major parts of the series and works through some of its most unique pieces. With over 300 pages of exploration and a foreword from Star Trek legend Kate Mulgrew, who played Captain Kathryn Janeway in Star Trek: Voyager, Star Trek Timelines provides its dedicated readers a chance to dig further into the franchise, looking at the Star Trek journey from a whole new vantage point.
