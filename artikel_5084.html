The U.S. House committee advanced a bill that could lock government-held Bitcoin for 20 years.

H.R. 8957 would ban reserve Bitcoin from being sold, swapped or auctioned for two decades.

Following this news, Bitcoin jumped more than 4% after the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve bill advanced.

The House Financial Services Committee has advanced a bill to make the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve, triggering a more than 4% jump in Bitcoin’s price. BTC climbed above $61,000 after falling close to $57,339.

While, hotter than expected U.S. PCE inflation has raised concerns about another Fed rate hike in December.

The House Financial Services Committee has advanced H.R. 8957, the American Reserve Modernization Act of 2026, introduced by Rep. Nicholas J. Begich. The bill aims to establish a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve using Bitcoin seized through criminal and civil asset forfeitures.

The bill passed the committee 28-21 on November 24 and moved to the Union Calendar for a possible House vote. The U.S. government currently holds around 306,800 to 328,000 BTC, mostly seized through asset forfeitures.

Under the bill, the government cannot sell, swap, auction, or use reserve Bitcoin as collateral for at least 20 years. It also rules out borrowing or using new taxes to buy more BTC.

An earlier plan to buy up to one million BTC was removed.

Instead, the Treasury and Commerce departments must study ways to acquire more Bitcoin without using new taxes or debt within 180 days.

The Strategic Bitcoin Reserve currently exists under President Donald Trump’s March 2025 executive order, which a future president could revoke. If passed by both chambers, the bill would establish the reserve under federal law and give it a more permanent legal status.

The bill is now on the Union Calendar, allowing House leaders to schedule a floor vote during the remaining lame-duck session. As of now, no vote date has been announced.

Even if the House approves it in December, the bill must still pass the Senate before Congress ends on January 3, 2027.

The Senate’s crowded year-end schedule, including spending and other must-pass bills, leaves limited time for a standalone crypto bill.

Meanwhile, Polymarket traders see less than a 4% chance of the U.S. making the Bitcoin reserve law before 2027.

Bitcoin price reacted quickly, rising more than 4% and reclaiming $61,000.

The rally came despite hotter U.S. PCE inflation data. November 25 PCE data showed headline inflation rising to 3.8% from 3.6%, while core PCE increased to 3.3% from 3.1%, above the 3.2% forecast.

The higher inflation has pushed expectations of a 25-basis-point Fed rate hike in December to 99%.
