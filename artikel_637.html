Meta Launches 150-Plus 3D Movies to Rent and Buy on Quest VR Headsets, With Thousands More Coming in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

If your idea of an awesome movie-watching experience involves strapping a VR headset to your head, Meta has got you.

On Thursday, Meta announced that it has launched over 150 3D movies available to rent or purchase in the TV app for Meta Quest 3 and 3S virtual-reality headsets. Initially, the feature is available only in the U.S.

Movies available for Meta Quest users include “Deadpool & Wolverine,” James Cameron’s “Avatar,” “Spider-Man: Far From Home,” “Jurassic World Rebirth,” “Wicked: For Good,” and Billie Eilish’s “Hit Me Hard and Soft: The Tour.” Co-directed by Eilish and Cameron, the concert film will be exclusively available to buy and rent in 3D through Dec. 31 on Meta Quest. Most of the titles cost $16.99 to buy and $6.99 to rent, although pricing may vary. The tech company has deals in the works for more movies: Meta said the TV app’s library will expand to “thousands of titles,” along international availability, with the launch of Meta VR Glasses in spring 2027, to cost $1,299 apiece.

Quest VR users have already been able to stream movies and TV shows using native apps for services including YouTube, Amazon Prime Video and Disney+ (which offers select movies in 3D) or access streaming services like Netflix or Apple TV using the built-in browser.

Meta continues to push the VR headset as a premium home-theater experience. With the latest Meta Quest software update, the devices’ “Theater” mode provides a big screen and dims viewers’ surroundings for a personal cinema experience.

Watching “Hit Me Hard and Soft: The Tour” in 3D on Meta Quest, “you can stand alongside Billie, look out at the crowd, and feel like part of the show,” according to Meta. “Getting those shots took more than pointing cameras at a stage. Billie performs on a 360-degree stage surrounded by the audience, so Cameron’s Lightstorm Vision team built a new system for filming 3D at long range, capturing the action up close without putting cameras between Billie and her fans. Another 3D camera on stage puts you right beside her.”
