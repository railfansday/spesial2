Supermarket fridges hacked again as cyberattack turns them up to 122 degrees

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

A number of stores across Europe have been hit with cyberattacks, with fridges having their temperatures increased to 122 degrees, forcing them to throw away food.

Back in early October, hackers took aim at a number of supermarkets and stores in France. Over the course of two days, they ended up hacking hundreds of fridges, increasing their internal temperatures to 122 degrees, thereby spoiling food and forcing stores to throw it away.

Now, as the calendar has turned over to November, similar attacks have taken place across Europe.

According to the BBC, numerous chains in Belgium, Ireland, Luxembourg, and Sweden have been affected by the new round of attacks, with their fridges are rising to boiling temperatures. Additionally, reports from HLN the Netherlands say that stores in the Netherlands, Finland, and Estonia are also affected.

As per the BBC’s report, 40 Delhaize supermarkets in Belgium and Luxembourg have been attacked, 13 Supervalu stores in Ireland have been affected, while ICA and Coop shops in Sweden have also been hit by the attack.

“Our refrigerators and freezers have been hacked overnight, and they have set the temperature to 50 degrees,” one ICA spokesperson said.

HLN also reported that IKEA in Belgium has been hit, but noted that the impact is “limited” for most customers.

Some of the other stores have blamed a “malfunction,” while others have cited an “IT incident” as the reason why they’ve had to dispose of food.
