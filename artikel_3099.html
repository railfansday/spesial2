Feeling the tension? Try this doctor-approved mobility workout to release aches and stiffness

If you regularly wake up feeling stiff, notice your hips tightening after sitting for too long, or find that everyday movements leave you feeling achy, it might be time to pay attention to your mobility. While some changes in flexibility and joint movement are a natural part of aging, aches, stiffness and tension don't have to become something you put up with.

One of the best ways to keep your body moving comfortably is to make mobility work a priority. Gentle, controlled movements take your joints through their range of motion, help maintain flexibility and strengthen the muscles that support everyday movement. You don't need to spend hours stretching to see the benefits — a short routine practised regularly can be a better way to build the habit.

With that in mind, I asked Dr. Gaby Van Sickle, physical therapist and co-founder of Functional Physical Therapy, to share a 10-minute mobility workout she recommends to her clients. The routine combines simple movements to keep your hips, knees and ankles moving, build lower-body strength and support the balance and coordination we rely on as we age.

Why: Standing marches gently move the hips and knees while encouraging you to shift your weight from one leg to the other. “It’s about keeping the lower body moving to ease feelings of stiffness after periods of sitting or inactivity,” Van Sickle advocates.

If aches, stiffness and tension are making everyday movement feel more difficult, a regular mobility routine can help. These exercises gently move your hips, knees and ankles through their range of motion while strengthening the muscles that support your joints, helping you maintain the mobility, balance and strength you need as you age.

The key is consistency, rather than pushing yourself to do more. Try setting aside 10 minutes each day to work through the routine, moving slowly and staying within a comfortable range of motion. Over time, a regular practice can help you feel less stiff after periods of inactivity and more confident with everyday movements.
