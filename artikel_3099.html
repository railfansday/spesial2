Delivery App Grab Sees Return on AI, Says CFO

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Grab CFO Peter Oey says the company is seeing returns from its use of AI tools in the form of higher spending by customers. The ride-hailing, delivery and financial services app raised its full-year revenue and profit forecasts. Oey joins Ed Ludlow on "Bloomberg Tech" to discuss Grab's earnings. (Source: Bloomberg)
