Mikel Arteta confirms Arsenal injury blow ahead of Newcastle United clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Arsenal head to Newcastle with one significant boost and two familiar concerns. William Saliba is back in the picture, Martin Zubimendi is not, and Bukayo Saka remains some way off.

That is the short version. The longer one matters because Arsenal have been juggling absences in key areas and the timing of each return shapes what comes next.

Saliba has yet to play for Arsenal's first team this season after a long-term back injury. He has, however, done the work required to get back into contention. First came 45 minutes for the Under-21s against Aston Villa, then a behind-closed-doors outing against Watford. That was enough to move him into the frame for the trip to St James' Park.

Arteta put it plainly: "He is ready, he's in a good condition. We want to ramp up his minutes.

"He played two games, one behind-closed-doors and one more official. He did really well and he feels in good condition."

This does not automatically mean a start. It means Arsenal now have the option of involving one of their most important defenders again, which is a different thing entirely. After a lay-off of this length, minutes are managed, not gifted.

If Saliba offers relief, Zubimendi brings another problem. The midfielder was forced off while on international duty with Spain against England, and the scan brought bad news. Not catastrophic, but enough to rule him out for Newcastle and beyond.

Arteta said: "[Zubimendi] is out. Unfortunately he has a slight problem in his hamstring.

"He wasn't too concerned after the game but after the MRI scan showed an injury there. he's going to be out for a few weeks I think."

Hamstring injuries rarely work to a neat schedule and Arsenal know that. The wording matters here. "A few weeks" is not an invitation to optimism, it is a warning that the player will miss a chunk of matches and that the club will need cover in midfield.

Saka is further down the road than Zubimendi in one sense, but he is still unavailable and there is no precise return date. He has missed Arsenal's last five games with a quad injury and is not expected back before Christmas.

Arteta said: "He had another test. He's evolving really well, but it looks like it's going to be an eight-week injury, more or less.

"I think it's four at the moment so still going to be a while until he's back with the team."

"In four weeks' time a lot of things can happen. Maybe it is three and then it is five. At the end, there are a few elements. One is the healing of that tissue – and we cannot predict exactly when that tissue is going to be in the best condition to push it.

"Then there is the rehab plan that we have now, what he needs to do in the gym, what he needs to do in terms of treatment and on the field.

"Everything can progress at the right speed to give him the best chance to come back."

For Arsenal, the picture is clear enough. Saliba is close, Zubimendi is out, Saka remains a waiting game.
