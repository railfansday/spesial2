College golf signing day: Top women's players, classes for Class of 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Class of 2027's early signing period began Wednesday, meaning hundreds of student-athletes from across the country signed grant-in-aid agreements with schools. Grant-in-aid agreements have replaced the national letter of intent moving forward.

Here's a look at the rankings for players and classes in the Class of 2027 on the women's side (subject to change).

Asterisk Talley, Stanford (not signing on signing day)

Texas (Adriana Garcia, Anna Ludvova, Alexis Lamadrid, Asia Young)

Florida (Alice Kong, Aroa Gonzalez Hernandez)

Kentucky (Madeleine Conser, Anna Christian Beeker)

North Carolina (Macie Rasmussen, Ava Osborne)

*Talley will not sign in the early signing period
