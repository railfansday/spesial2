5 potential landing spots for Brandon Aiyuk after 49ers release

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

5 potential landing spots for Brandon Aiyuk after 49ers release originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

The Brandon Aiyuk era is over in San Francisco.

The 49ers on Monday made the decision to release him two weeks after his NFL reinstatement. His roster exemption was set to expire, with San Francisco opting to move on.

Aiyuk last played a game in October 2024, when he tore his ACL against the Kansas City Chiefs. A long-term series of off-field issues followed, and now the former 2020 first-round pick is on the market.

At his best, Aiyuk was one of the league's premier route runners. He steadily improved his production each season, peaking in 2023 with 1,342 receiving yards and seven touchdowns.

It's unclear how fit the 28-year-old wideout is to immediately impact a team right now, and if any team is willing to take the gamble given how the last few years have unfolded. But if he does find a new home, these five teams may make sense:

The Commanders have been the most rumored destination for quite some time, though, as aforementioned, it's unclear if any team is seriously interested. Aiyuk has a long-standing rapport with QB1 Jayden Daniels, which is an instant plus in terms of chemistry.

Washington is also down its best wideout in Terry McLaurin, who tore his left patellar tendon earlier in the season against…San Francisco. The Commanders are hoping to stay in the playoff race, but they need more talent beyond Stefon Diggs and Treylon Burks. Aiyuk could help.

Beyond Washington, Pittsburgh is another team that had some previous interest in bringing in Aiyuk. The Steelers have fallen to 6-4 after two straight losses, as their lack of offensive talent is bringing them down to earth following a solid start. Aaron Rodgers is the veteran starter under center, but DK Metcalf doesn't work anymore as the WR1.

Youngster Germie Bernard still needs time to become a reliable target, which probably won't happen with Rodgers around. So, if the Steelers want to fix some issues and bring in help to fix their sinking season, Aiyuk may be worth a gamble to add some flair in a tight AFC North.

Another team that could work is the Rams. Matthew Stafford and Co. are still a hopeful contender despite early-season woes. Those woes worsened with Puka Nacua now out for the year after MCL surgery, while veteran Davante Adams is battling knocks and isn't an out-and-out WR1 anymore.

In this scenario, Aiyuk gets to stay within the state he was born in and, perhaps most importantly, in the same division as San Francisco. Sean McVay runs a similar system to Kyle Shanahan, and Aiyuk may be able to provide important information in the NFC race.

Staying in the NFC West, the reigning Super Bowl champions could work, too. The offensive system isn't the same as Los Angeles', as Sam Darnold is a few tiers below Stafford from a pure signal-calling standpoint. But the Seahawks need more offensive weapons.

Jaxon Smith-Njigba does the heavy lifting in the passing game, having already accumulated over 1,200 receiving yards. But that isn't sustainable, with the next highest receiver being Cooper Kupp. He hasn't eclipsed 300 receiving yards. A healthy Aiyuk would round out a decent trio for Darnold.

What about the team that started this domino effect? Patrick Mahomes and Co. are 8-2 and back among the true AFC contenders, despite having a relatively poor offense. Travis Kelce is leading in receiving yards at age 37. That shouldn't have to be the case.

Rashee Rice and Xavier Worthy haven't impressed as much, while Kansas City acquired Josh Downs from the Indianapolis Colts at the deadline. But if they want to try to improve things further, Aiyuk in a Chiefs uniform would be an intriguing sight.
