What's Coming To Disney+ & Hulu In December 2026: Full List Of TV Shows & Movies

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Disney+ and Hulu always offer plenty of options, adding new films and TV shows each month. While this year's December releases are a little lighter than most months — it's the end of the year, after all — the streaming services still have exciting new additions and returning shows. Hulu original Only Murders in the Building is back (except it’s called Only Murders in London this season), while Disney+ is premiering Disney Twisted-Wonderland: The Animation - Episode of Savanaclaw.

December 2026 also brings a slew of awards shows and holiday specials, such as The Game Awards and Disney Parks Magical Christmas Day Parade on Disney+. Additionally, fans of Japanese television will be thrilled on December 21st, as several Japanese TV series drop on both Hulu and Disney+.
