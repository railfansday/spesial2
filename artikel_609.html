Matthew Capper, Late Music Publishing Exec, Honored With New Award

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Matthew Capper Award recognizes "individuals whose dedication and actions strengthen the music publishing community for future generations."

SYDNEY, Australia — Matthew Capper is saluted with a new, annual award which bears his name.

Announced Wednesday, Nov. 25, the Matthew Capper Award recognizes integrity, generosity, professionalism, commitment and service to music publishing in Australia and New Zealand, and is presented by the Australasian Music Publishers’ Association Limited (AMPAL).

Capper, the long-time former Warner Chappell Australia managing director and AMPAL chair, died unexpectedly in January 2025 following a surgical procedure. “Matthew was widely respected for his integrity, professionalism, intelligence, humor, generosity and unwavering commitment to creators and the music publishing community,” reads a statement from AMPAL, announcing the new honor.

“A passionate advocate for music publishing, Matthew believed in the importance of doing things properly, ethically and in service of the broader industry. His approach to professional life reflected a belief that lasting contributions are measured not simply by individual achievements, but by the positive difference we make to those around us.”

In 2005, Capper became the youngest person ever appointed to the AMCOS board at the age of just 28. Two years later, in 2007, he became the youngest to join the APRA board, at 30.

He went on to serve as AMPAL chair for 11 years, as a non-executive director of APRA and AMCOS for more than 16 years, and as GM and managing director of Warner Chappell for over 20 years. On the international front, he served as a non-executive director and treasurer of the ICMP global board and as chair of ICMP’s Asia-Pacific Board.

The inaugural Matthew Capper Award, reads the AMPAL statement, honors his legacy by spotlighting “individuals whose dedication and actions strengthen the music publishing community for future generations.”

Meanwhile, AMPAL marked its 70th anniversary AGM with the election of six directors to its board: Craig Monagle (Warner Chappell Music), Linda Bosidis (Mushroom Music Publishing), Simon Moor (Kobalt Music), Art Phillips (101 Music), Theo Seffusatti (Heard & Seen Publishing/Gaga Music) and Philip Mortlock (ORiGiN Music).

Seffusatti is the sole newly-elected director and is believed to be the first New Zealand-based member elected to the trade body’s board, an appointment that coincides with AMPAL’s announcement of a new funding initiative supporting NZ music publishers, with further details to be announced in early 2027. At the same time, Ralph W. Peer (peermusic) has stepped down from the board.

APRA AMCOS, too, has announced its respective boards.

On the APRA board, writer directors Jonathan Zwartz and Mark Callaghan were re-elected, along with newly-appointed publisher directors Craig Monagle (Warner Chappell Music Publishing) and Maree Hamblion (Universal Music Publishing). Jenny Morris was returned as APRA chair and Damian Trotter as deputy chair.

Stepping down from the APRA board this year are outgoing publisher directors Jaime Gough and Andrew Jenkins.

On the AMCOS board, Trotter and Karen Hamilton were re-elected as directors along with newly appointed Karina Masters (Universal Music Publishing) and Theo Seffusatti. Jaime Gough was returned as AMCOS chair and Clive Hodson as deputy chair.
