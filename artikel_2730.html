James Cook makes NFL history on Thanksgiving night

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Buffalo Bills running back James Cook III has a share of another NFL record. Cook has now rushed for at least 75 yards and a touchdown in 10 consecutive games, tying a record Terry Allen set across the 1995 and 1996 seasons with Washington.

Cook found the end zone against the Kansas City Chiefs on Thanksgiving night during the second quarter to put the Bills up 7-0. He then eclipsed the 75-yard mark in the third quarter on a five-yard run that put him at 79 for the night.

It's been 30 years since Allen put together his streak of 10 games. Since then, we've watched some pretty impressive running backs come through the NFL. LaDainian Tomlinson, Adrian Peterson, and Derrick Henry have all had their share of record-setting performances, but none of them managed to do what Cook just did.

And unlike Allen, who needed parts of two seasons to put together his streak, Cook has done it all in the same season. In fact, the Bills' season opener was the only game this year in which Cook didn't meet both statistical requirements.

Cook entered Thursday night's game having rushed for 1,003 yards and 10 touchdowns over the course of his nine-game streak, averaging 111.4 yards per game and 5.1 yards per carry. He eclipsed the century mark in five of those nine games, including a season-high 157-yard performance against the New York Jets.

Remember when we spent so much of the offseason in 2025 talking about whether the Bills should pay Cook? Buffalo eventually gave its running back a four-year, $48 million contract extension, and Cook has spent the better part of two seasons giving us reasons to talk about what a good decision that was.

The Bills will head to Foxborough next week for their second meeting of the season with the New England Patriots. Cook rushed for 75 yards and a touchdown on 12 carries in their first matchup.

Another 75 yards and a trip to the end zone next week, and Cook will have an NFL record all to himself. Considering we've been watching him do just that every week since September, it might be time to start wondering just how big a number he's going to leave for the next running back to chase.
