Brian Mendoza Gets the Call to Replace Keith Thurman against Vergil Ortiz Jr.

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

While many replacement names were floated, former super welterweight champion Brian Mendoza will step in for Keith Thurman Jr. against Vergil Ortiz Jr. on December 5.

Thurman Jr. withdrew four days ago on November 21 due to injury, reportedly a damaged hand in sparring.

Ortiz Jr. vs Mendoza is the new co-main event on the Premier Boxing Champions card headlined by David Benavidez and Michal Cieślak at the Desert Diamond Arena in Glendale, Arizona.

Former world super welterweight champion Brian "La Bala" Mendoza will replace Keith Thurman Jr. as the opponent for undefeated Vergil Ortiz Jr. in a ten-round middleweight co-main event on the Benavidez vs. Cieslak card on Saturday, December 5, from the Desert Diamond Arena in Glendale, Arizona.

Ortiz Jr. had originally been scheduled to face former unified champion Thurman, who was forced to withdraw due to injury.

Rumors swirled, with some gaining traction, that Jermell Charlo would be the replacement opponent. Ortiz Jr.'s promoter Oscar De La Hoya of Golden Boy squelched the rumor last Saturday during an interview on the DAZN Boxing broadcast from Indio, California.

But De La Hoya confirmed Mendoza and undefeated prospect Eric Priest were on his short list. De La Hoya said he actually preferred Priest. Mendoza and Priest were originally scheduled to face each other on the early undercard.

As we correctly pointed out (and we're two for two this week correctly predicting replacement names), the Charlo matchup was a social media mirage.

Originally from Albuquerque, New Mexico, and now fighting out of Las Vegas, Mendoza (23-5, 17 KOs), age 32, earned the WBC Interim Super Welterweight title with a highlight-reel knockout upset win over world champion Sebastian Fundora in April 2023. It remains Fundora's only professional loss.

Mendoza followed up by traveling to Australia to challenge then-WBO World Super Welterweight champion Tim Tszyu, losing by decision. After the Tszyu fight, he took a short-notice fight against the tough Ukrainian Serhii Bohachuk in 2024 for a loss. Mendoza put a win on his record by stopping Jesus Antonio Rojas in July 2025.

Working with trainer Ismael Salas, Mendoza took another last-minute challenge against top contender Yoenis Tellez in March. This will be his third late replacement role in two years.

Mendoza has another tough assignment against Ortiz Jr. (24-0, 22 KOs), although he is catching him in a new weight division after 13 months out of the ring.

Trained by Robert Garcia, Ortiz Jr., age 28, was a quickly rising star in his early years, scoring impressive wins over Maurice Hooker, Egidijus "Mean Machine" Kavaliauskas, and Brad Solomon.

Ortiz Jr. then struggled with recurring health issues, including bouts with rhabdomyolysis, a muscle-wasting illness affecting kidney function that kept him out of the ring.

Vergil Ortiz Jr. has been out of the ring since a hard won victory in a sensational fight against Serhii Bohachuk. Photo: Cris Esqueda, Golden Boy Boxing

The native of Grand Prairie, Texas, moved up to super welterweight in 2024 and originally captured his WBC interim title with a 12-round decision over Serhii Bohachuk in a highly competitive, all-action fight. We covered the fight ringside and I named it my 2024 Fight of the Year.

Ortiz Jr. was finally on a roll with an impressive victory in Riyadh over Israil Madrimov in 2024. In his last fight, Ortiz Jr. stopped veteran Erickson Lubin in the second round of their November 2025 matchup.

But due to a contractual disagreement that has since been worked out between Ortiz Jr. and Golden Boy Promotions, Ortiz Jr. has once again been sitting out since the Madrimov fight.

Whoever stepped up, it is vital for Ortiz Jr.'s stalled career that he gets in the ring on December 5. Mendoza is a credible opponent, although he has struggled in recent bouts. It will be his first fight in the middleweight division.

The Benavidez vs Cieślak pay-per-view will feature three under-30 rising stars. Undefeated super lightweight Emiliano "El General" Vargas competes in a 10-round fight against contender Omar Juarez. Unbeaten Australian Nikita Tszyu, brother of Tim and son of Kostya, makes his American debut in a 10-round super welterweight fight against Roberto Valenzuela Jr.

Middleweight sensation Marco "El Tiburon" Romero of Olathe, Kansas, returns to action just a few weeks after a first-round knockout win on October 17. He will take on Cruse Stewart in the eight-round opening bout at 8 p.m. ET/5 p.m. PT.

The event is promoted by Sampson Boxing and TGB Promotions. Ortiz vs. Mendoza is co-promoted by Golden Boy Promotions. The Vargas vs. Juarez fight is co-promoted by Top Rank. The Tszyu vs. Valenzuela fight is co-promoted by No Limit Boxing and the Rose Brothers.
