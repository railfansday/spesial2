LSU’s Will Wade drops mic on SEC after playing RJ Luis Jr. vs. UL Monroe

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The game between LSU and UL Monroe at Pete Maravich Assembly Center garnered extra attention because of one person: RJ Luis Jr.

On Tuesday, the No. 25 Tigers added Luis to the roster following the court injunction that deemed him eligible to play despite playing for the Boston Celtics in the preseason. Although disappointed and alarmed, the SEC stated that it would respect the court ruling.

The 23-year-old Luis tallied 11 points, nine rebounds, one assist, and one block in 15 minutes off the bench, as LSU defeated UL Monroe, 79-67.

In the postgame conference, coach Will Wade refused to delve too much into the controversy surrounding the senior guard, though he issued a stern message.

"I'll be happy to answer any questions about how he played. But in terms of his eligibility and rostering, I will refer to our university statement yesterday," said Wade in the video posted by The Field of 68.

"But everybody we played tonight is eligible."

He added that LSU already "addressed the process" of adding Luis, who appeared in three games for the Celtics in October.

Luis went undrafted in June before signing a two-way contract with the Utah Jazz, who eventually traded him to Boston. After failing to land a roster spot, he entered the transfer portal and joined the Tigers after two seasons at St. John's.

"LSU signed RJ in May 2026. He is a full-time LSU student. He went to court when he believed the NCAA had violated his rights. After a full evidentiary hearing, the court ruled in his favor and enjoined the NCAA from declaring or treating him as ineligible. Neither the appellate court nor the Louisiana Supreme Court has disturbed that ruling," said LSU in a statement.

The Tigers improved to 5-0 with the win over the Warhawks.
