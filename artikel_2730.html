Steelers suddenly bench Aaron Rodgers amidst disappointing season

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Pittsburgh Steelers are in the midst of a disappointing stretch, and they've made a stunning change at quarterback in an attempt to get them out of it. With the team mired in a two-game losing streak, the Steelers announced that Aaron Rodgers is being benched and Mason Rudolph will start Friday's game against the Denver Broncos.

The four-time NFL MVP won't even be in uniform for the game, as the team said that he will be inactive. Rodgers, who turns 43 next week, came back to Pittsburgh for what was expected to be the final season of his career, and he may now have played his last snap.

Just a few weeks ago, the Steelers were 6-2 and sitting atop the AFC North. But Pittsburgh has dropped two straight games since, and the offense has gone cold in both of them.

First came a 38-18 blowout loss to the Cincinnati Bengals on Sunday Night Football, in which nearly all of Rodgers' production came in the fourth quarter with the game out of reach. Former NFL MVP Cam Newton said afterward that Rodgers "is not good enough to carry a team anymore."

Things didn't get much better on Sunday. Rodgers was sacked five times in a 19-16 loss to the Philadelphia Eagles, a game in which star receiver DK Metcalf was ejected in the third quarter after his second personal foul. Rodgers called the ejection "disappointing" after the game.

Now 6-4, the Steelers have failed to score 20 points in either loss, and they face an 8-2 Denver Broncos team on Black Friday.

With the season heading the wrong direction, the Steelers have officially made a change, announcing the move in a brief statement late Tuesday night.

"The Pittsburgh Steelers have named Mason Rudolph the starting quarterback for the game this Friday against the Denver Broncos. Aaron Rodgers will be inactive for the game on Friday," the team said.

ESPN's Tom Pelissero added that the call came from the coaching staff.

"Stunner: The Steelers are benching Aaron Rodgers and naming Mason Rudolph the starting quarterback for Friday's game against the Broncos. Sources say this was a coach's decision. But Rodgers has struggled and now Mike McCarthy is making a change," Pelissero wrote.

According to Ray Fittipaldo of the Pittsburgh Post-Gazette, the coaching staff believes Rudolph can give the offense a jolt.

"According to sources, the decision to bench Rodgers stemmed from the offense slumping badly the past two games after the bye week. The coaches are confident Mason Rudolph can provide the spark the offense needs against the Broncos," Fittipaldo wrote.

The move is especially surprising given Rodgers' long history with McCarthy, who coached him for 13 seasons in Green Bay. Rodgers has completed 60.9% of his passes for 2,241 yards, 16 touchdowns, and six interceptions this season, and his 86.6 passer rating is the lowest of his career/

Rudolph has started 19 games across seven seasons with the Steelers and the Tennessee Titans, going 9-9-1 with an 84.3 career passer rating.
