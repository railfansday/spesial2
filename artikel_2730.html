Decisive clash: Liverpool and Racing seek a place in the Uruguayan final

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Today brings a huge occasion in Uruguayan football: Liverpool and Racing battle for a place in the Uruguayan Championship decider, where Peñarol are already waiting for their opponents. The match takes place today at 20:30 at the Campus de Maldonado.

Both teams arrive as winners of the season's short tournaments: Racing as Apertura champions and Liverpool as Clausura champions. Both want to keep dreaming of the ultimate glory: lifting the trophy as 2026 Uruguayan champions.

Racing won the Apertura for the first time in their history with 31 points: 9 wins, 4 draws and 2 defeats. Chambián's side could not maintain their winning form for the rest of the year, having lost several key players after the Apertura. Agustín Álvarez Wallace, Felipe Cairus and Thiago Espinosa were among the stars who moved elsewhere. The Brewers are seeking victory today to continue writing the story of the best year in their history.

Liverpool, meanwhile, will contest the semi-final after being crowned Clausura champions. Under Fossati, who transformed the team, they earned 33 points and claimed their 11th official title since 2019. This is a team that has been in the mix for seven years, challenging for major honours almost every season. Today in Maldonado, they will look to overcome Chambián's side in their quest for a second Uruguayan Championship title.
