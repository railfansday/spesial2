NFL bumps struggling Patriots, Chargers out of Week 12 'SNF' slot

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The NFL has made the first major change to its prime-time schedule.

The league on Tuesday, Nov. 17, announced it was flexing the Week 12 "Sunday Night Football" matchup, replacing the New England Patriots' game at the Los Angeles Chargers with the Las Vegas Raiders at the Cleveland Browns.

The Patriots-Chargers game will move to 4:05 p.m. ET, while the Tennessee Titans at Jacksonville Jaguars showdown will shift to 1 p.m. ET.

The move marks the first significant shift the league has made to its schedule. Until Tuesday, the only tweaks to the slate had been reshuffling of afternoon games.

The new prime-time matchup will put the spotlight on No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza, who made his starting debut Sunday in the Las Vegas Raiders' loss to the Seattle Seahawks.

The Browns, meanwhile, will have just their second prime-time appearance of the season as they mount a potential playoff push.

Coming off their first consecutive losses of Mike Vrabel's tenure, the Patriots are poised to be without quarterback Drake Maye for at least the near term. In Sunday's loss to the Detroit Lions in Munich, Maye suffered what the team fears is a broken ankle, which could keep him out for the remainder of the season, according to multiple reports.

If Maye remains sidelined, backup Tommy DeVito would be poised to take the reins to the starting offense. DeVito threw for just 81 yards on 12-of-24 passing on Sunday.

The Chargers, meanwhile, were the NFL's last winless team prior to Monday night's victory over the Baltimore Ravens.
