Preview: Can Utah snap a 3 game losing streak against the Denver Nuggets?

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

It goes without saying that this Utah Jazz season has been a colossal disappointment for fans that were expecting a quick turnaround, after years of extraordinary tanking. At 3-16, the Utah Jazz have struggled out the gate, losing close game after close game and have been smacked straight in the mouth with injuries to key players Jaren Jackson Jr, Jusuf Nurkic, and Lauri Markkanen.

While at times tough to watch, there are also glimpses of what this team can become. The Jazz are rarely blownout of the gym and frequently keep games close. But just as often, the youth on this team (namely Keyonte George, Darryn Peterson, and Ace Bailey) find themselves unable to finish off wins or comeback and steal wins. Many games have given fans brief moments of levity and whiffs of a win, only for that hope to be snatched away with Utah's dumb turnovers, rookie mistakes, or just straight-up missed shots. There is also something to be said for the difficulty of Utah's schedule thus far.

This one will be a tough one. The Jazz lack size and will have to contend with former MVP Nikola Jokic. While a somewhat disappointing season relative to other years, the Denver Nuggets are still 10-6 and receiving All-Star caliber seasons from both Jamal Murray and Jokic. They struggle with their depth and have not received the best play from Christian Braun (on a crazy contract); Aaron Gordon as well looks to be a shell of himself, with years of injuries finally catching up to him.

There is hope on the horizon. Word on the street is that Jusuf Nurkic is getting close to suiting up. As of this morning, he was listed as questionable. If Nurkic plays, it could end up being the most important moment in the season for this struggling team.

When: Friday, November 27, 2026 | 7:30 PM MT
