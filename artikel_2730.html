‘Couldn’t help’- Cam Skattebo breaks backflip promise as Giants seal statement win over Jaguars

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Cam Skattebo brought back his trademark backflip celebration after the New York Giants secured a 23-18 victory over the Jacksonville Jaguars, despite recently promising to retire the move.

The running back had decided to stop performing backflips after watching Detroit Lions star Aidan Hutchinson suffer an ACL injury while celebrating a sack against the New England Patriots.

However, Skattebo could not resist bringing it back after producing a crucial late run to help the Giants seal an impressive victory.

Skattebo's celebration came after he picked up a five-yard first down on the Giants' final drive, allowing them to run down the clock and secure the win.

After getting up, the second-year running back screamed, flexed and performed the very move he had recently claimed was retired.

ESPN's Adam Schefter reacted to the moment on X: "Backflip has come out of its brief retirement."

Giants head coach John Harbaugh also noticed his running back's decision to break his promise.

"He couldn't help himself, could he?" Harbaugh said with a smirk. "He couldn't help himself."

Skattebo had previously told The New York Post's Ryan Dunleavy that he was finished with backflips after seeing Hutchinson's injury.

"I'm saying yeah (no more) backflips," Skattebo said. "Mentally, I'm like, 'I'm not doing that.' That ain't going to happen to me."

However, Dunleavy noted that Skattebo had left open the possibility of bringing the celebration back for a major occasion, such as the playoffs.

The victory was another important moment for a Giants team that improved to 6-3-1 and remained top of the NFC East.

New York built a 20-6 half-time lead before holding off Jacksonville's fourth-quarter comeback, with its defence recording three sacks and two interceptions.

Skattebo will now hope to keep contributing as the Giants travel to face the Indianapolis Colts in their next game, although Harbaugh may have something to say if another backflip appears.
