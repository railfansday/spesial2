Could the Seattle Seahawks (4-4) be playing for their season already? It's an arguable must-win situation against the Arizona Cardinals (3-5), who are 2-1 in their last three games while the Seahawks have dropped three out of four. The Seahawks have slumped from last year's Super Bowl win and they are in desperate need of a good next couple of weeks before their bye.

After the thrill of hammering the Kansas City Chiefs two Sundays ago, the Monday night blowout loss to the Chicago Bears was a massive reality check for Seattle. This team has not played to the level it played last season, and now the injuries are piling up. Drew Lock is in for Sam Darnold again, while the likes of Leonard Williams, Uchenna Nwosu, Charles Cross, Rashid Shaheed, and Quenton Nelson are all out. So… any thought that this will also end 31-7 like the first meeting did?
