VOTE for the HSS Volleyball Player of the Week for Nov. 2-8

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

After another exciting week of the high school volleyball season, it's time to look back on the best performances.

Read about the players who stood out last week and let us know who you think should be the HSS North Jersey Player of the Week in the poll at the bottom of this page.

The senior middle helped the Hudson County champs reach the North Group 1 semifinals, tallying 18 kills, 9 digs, 6 blocks and 4 aces in three sectional-tournament matches.

The senior surpassed 1,000 career assists during the Golden Knights' run to the North Group 3 semis as a No. 8 seed. Giordano put up 115 assists in three matches to end with 1,074 total.

The senior OH propelled the No. 13 seed Cardinals on a run to the North Group 2 semifinals with 42 kills, 54 digs, 4 blocks and 5 aces in three sectional-tournament matches.

The junior outside hitter helped the Hawks (21-4) return to the sectional finals, registering 53 kills, 26 digs and 4 aces in the first three rounds of the North Group 2 tournament.

The junior OH led the Eagles to state-tournament wins over Saddle River Day and St. Benedict's, recording 14 kills, 7 aces and a season-high 21 digs in the former match.

The senior OH powered the Bulldogs to their second straight North Group 4 final, serving four aces in each of the team's three sectional matches. Stipo totaled 38 kills and 24 digs.
