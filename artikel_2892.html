Stock Market Today, Nov. 24: Nvidia Gives Back $240 Billion in Market Value Despite Wall Street Upgrade Signaling 29% Upside

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

After staging its biggest rally in more than two decades, Nvidia is surrendering some of those gains as renewed selling pressure casts doubt on the sustainability of the AI recovery.

By RJ Fulton – Nov 24, 2026 at 4:29PM EST

Nvidia (NVDA -5.86%) fell 5.86% on Tuesday, November 24, closing at $155.38 as a historic semiconductor rally lost momentum. The decline followed Monday's extraordinary 31.48% surge, Nvidia's largest single-day percentage gain in more than two decades, as investors welcomed Jensen Huang's reinstatement as CEO.

Tuesday's reversal erased a portion of those gains as renewed selling pressure swept across the semiconductor industry. Trading volume reached 379.2 million shares, approximately 107% above its three-month daily average of 183 million, reflecting continued volatility and heightened investor activity.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC -0.50%) fell 0.50% to 7,201, and the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -1.48%) dropped 1.48% to 23,734. Semiconductor design and accelerated-computing peers also weakened, with Advanced Micro Devices (AMD -8.86%) closing at $413.27, down 8.86%, and Broadcom (AVGO -3.98%) ending at $301.08, down 3.98%.

Nvidia's sharp reversal following Monday's historic rally highlights the volatility surrounding AI stocks as investors reassess their valuations and long-term growth prospects. However, the recent price swings contrast with the company's underlying financial performance. Nvidia reported fiscal third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion, representing 103% year-over-year growth, and raised its fourth-quarter outlook, suggesting demand for AI infrastructure remains exceptionally strong.

Wall Street also remains relatively optimistic about Nvidia's prospects. Bernstein recently upgraded the stock to Outperform while maintaining a $200 price target, implying approximately 29% upside from Tuesday's closing price of $155.38. Although analyst targets are far from guarantees, the upgrade suggests some investors may view the recent sell-off as an attractive buying opportunity rather than evidence of deteriorating fundamentals.

Looking ahead, Nvidia's ability to sustain its growth will ultimately matter more than short-term market volatility. Investors should pay particular attention to whether major technology companies maintain their AI infrastructure spending, alongside developments involving U.S.-China semiconductor restrictions and regulatory scrutiny.
