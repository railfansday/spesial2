Eagles-Steelers matchup in Week 11 flexed to earlier time slot

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Eagles-Steelers matchup in Week 11 flexed to earlier time slot originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

The Eagles-Steelers game on Nov. 22 in Week 11 at the Linc has been flexed out of the 4:25 p.m. time slot to a 1 p.m. game at Lincoln Financial Field. The game is on CBS.

This probably won't be the last time the Eagles are flexed out of national games the rest of the season.

The Eagles (2-7) have lost seven straight games since winning their first two and have finally reached their bye week after a loss to the Giants. The Steelers (6-2) will face the Bengals in Week 10 coming off their bye week.

The Eagles-Steelers matchup was replaced by Jaguars (6-2) at Giants (5-2-1).
