Tigers Phenom Wins 2026 AL Rookie Of The Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The 2026 MLB Rookie of the Year awards were announced on Monday, and Detroit Tigers infielder Kevin McGonigle won the American League trophy in a landslide.

McGonigle captured 28 of 30 first-place votes and two second-place votes, beating out runner-up Parker Messick and third-place Payon Tolle.

McGonigle, 21, lived up to the hype in his first Major League season. A top prospect and first-round pick in the 2023 MLB Draft, he made the Tigers' Opening Day roster and hit the ground running.

While Detroit had a disappointing season and missed the playoffs, it wasn't McGonigle's fault. He made the AL All-Star team and finished with 7.1 WAR, 18 home runs, 12 steals, and a .278/.376/.430 batting line, becoming the sixth Tiger to win AL Rookie of the Year and the first since Lou Whitaker in 1978.

With his career off to a terrific start, McGonigle clearly has a bright future and will look to build on it in 2027 and beyond.
