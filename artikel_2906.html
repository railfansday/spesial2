Cowboys vs. Eagles recap: Dallas embarrassed by Philadelphia on Thanksgiving Day

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

It will go down in history as one of the darkest Thanksgiving Day games ever for the Dallas Cowboys.

Somehow, some way, the Cowboys threw away a 31-10 lead that they had in the third quarter.

The Philadelphia Eagles won on what was basically a game-winning, 60-yard field goal. It is hard to fathom.

On the other hand it is actually very easy to fathom given everything we have seen from the Cowboys this season. They are not a good football team. They are poorly coached. They are talent-deficient. They are all of the bad things.

On a day where we are supposed to be thankful it is hard to find a reason to be for this specific part of our lives. The Dallas Cowboys are a weekly embarrassment and Thursday was the latest example of it.

The Eagles won the toss and deferred which put the Cowboys offense to work first.

Dak Prescott and the group did not waste the opportunity. Javonte Williams was served seconds, thirds, and then some as the team marched down the field, partly thanks to a huge 3rd and 18 conversion from Dak to CeeDee Lamb, but it was Williams who capped it off with the touchdown to give the home team an early 7-0 lead.

Things were looking rather impressive for Joe Milton and the Eagles. I'd imagine that many Cowboys fans were starting to assume a score was happening any minute across the first drive.

Thankfully that was not the case. Right around field goal range the Eagles chose to roll the dice on fourth and short… and Quinnen Williams said no!

Weirdly a sequence occurred on the next drive that did the first.

Dak Prescott scrambled for a first on a third down, but the gain was negated by a penalty. This brought up third and long (16 here, 18 before) and Prescott connected with a star receiver (George Pickens here, CeeDee Lamb before) for the first down.

After that KaVontae Turpin had a stellar catch where he turned on the boosters and reached the endzone in a flash. Dallas was up 14-0 early.

The quarter ended with Philly holding the ball right after.

The Eagles punted shortly after teams flipped the field which put the Cowboys offense back up. Given that they were already leading 14-0 this was a chance to really blow things open. Unfortunately they could not and wound up punting the ball for the first time in a while.

A big 3rd and 12 conversion from Philadelphia put them within striking distance and while they were unable to fully get home they did give Jake Elliott a chance at a 48-yard field goal. It was good and the visitors had their first points on the day.

The Cowboys offense did not miss this time around. KaVontae Turpin was involved again, this time on a jet sweep, and there was a big conversion from Dak Prescott to CeeDee Lamb.

But it was Javonte Williams who cased in yet again. This marked his second score on the day and gave Dallas a 21-3 lead.

Joe Milton had been showing moments of promise to this point in the game and at long last he delivered. It was a classic Joe Milton throw… meaning it traveled quite a long ways.

The Cowboys received the ball with just over four minutes to go. It felt like an important possession as it would likely be their last one before the half and they received the ball to start the game. Points were essential.

In all honesty it was a pretty penalty-filled drive. Officials were calling them left and right, many of them helping the Cowboys, but Dallas was moved back after an apparent touchdown to Brevyn Spann-Ford.

On third and goal from the 12-yard line… Dak Prescott found Javonte Williams for a score to close the half. It also marked a hat trick of scores on the day for Williams specifically.

The Eagles took a knee and it was Miranda Lambert time.

Philadelphia went three and out to start the second half which is always great to see. The Cowboys took the field and once again with an opportunity to stretch their lead.

Dallas punted it back themselves. This game truly felt over, but obviously with a lot of time left that was not the case in any real way. Football had to be played.

Shemar James sacked Joe Milton which helped to ensure the punt streak continued. Philly booted it back and the Cowboys offense returned to the field.

It was a little bit of the same for the Cowboys offense in terms of not being able to fully convert. They did score, but only 3 points by way of Brandon Aubrey. This was notable though as it was a 59-yard field goal that he drilled with ease which was great to see given how this season has gone. The FOX broadcast also noted that this was the longest ever field goal made on Thanksgiving all-time. Pretty neat.

The third quarter ended right as Philadelphia crossed the 50-yard line… and then Joe Milton found DeVonta Smith again to get another touchdown on the board with 15 seconds left. It was suddenly 31-17, good guys.

Things did not go particularly well for the Cowboys offense to start the fourth quarter. They faced a fourth and short on their side of the field and left the offense on the field. It was bold, but nobody really thought they were going to run a play. Thankfully they just fished for offsides and punted.

Or so we thought. The Cowboys tried to punt… but it was blocked and returned for a Philly touchdown. Out of nowhere it was 31-24 with basically the entire fourth quarter left.

It goes without saying that the pressure was high. The Cowboys were on the verge of blowing this game. Something had to happen.

Dak Prescott and CeeDee Lamb specifically played like that was the case. They hooked up for a pair of big gains along the drive and then Dak put the exclamation point on things himself by running it on for a touchdown… thanks to some serious hops.

It seemed like this was going to essentially end the game. Have you seen the 2026 Dallas Cowboys, though

Philadelphia traveled 42 yards in 3 plays to match the touchdown. Joe Milton literally did by rushing it in himself.

The Eagles missed the two-point conversion so it was 38-30 Cowboys. In the blink of an eye it was 3rd and 14 right past the team's own 15-yard line.

It appeared as if the Cowboys had pulled off something truly incredible. Dak Prescott found George Pickens for a modest gain, but then he threw a lateral across the field to Hunter Luepke and the fullback took it all the way to the house. He even jumped into the Salvation Army kettle.

Unfortunately it was deemed to be a forward pass from Pickens and therefore a penalty which resulted in the loss of the down. Bryan Anger successfully got the punt off this time and the Eagles returned it past midfield. There was still half the fourth quarter remaining.

It was then that the Cowboys defense made a play. On third and forever they picked off Joe Milton. Cobie Durant was the hero in question.

You would think that this would have given the Cowboys the opportunity to ice the game. You would think that the offense that had been on fire for so long would have scored to fully put things away.

They wound up settling for a field goal attempt from 40 yards out. Brandon Aubrey missed. It was unbelievable.

Fine. Mistakes happen. Surely the Cowboys defense would lock in.

It took the Eagles just 4 plays to travel 70 yards and score. Seriously.

Given the failed two-point conversion earlier though the Eagles had to have one here. They failed again so Dallas retained a slim lead at 38-36.

The Cowboys got past midfield. In fact, they got all the way to the Philadelphia 39-yard line.

They were facing 4th and 1. Instead of letting Brandon Aubrey try a field goal, he had just missed obviously, the Cowboys chose to go for it. Dak Prescott was sacked. It felt grim.

You know what happened. The Eagles pulled off a 60-yard field goal to win 39-38.

The Cowboys' season is over. Unbelievable.
