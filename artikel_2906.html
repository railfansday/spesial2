Could Gennings Dunker’s return revive the Steelers’ jumbo package?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Despite being a third-round pick in the 2026 NFL Draft, Iowa tackle-turned-guard Gennings Dunker seemed destined for a redshirt rookie season with the Steelers.

Dunker was buried on the depth chart over the summer, missing some time with injury and then opening the regular season as a gameday inactive. Then, in late September, he landed on injured reserve with a reported MCL sprain.

Pittsburgh opened Dunker's 21-day return window from IR on Wednesday, the same day the team placed starting left guard Mason McCormick, who is expected to sit out until the playoffs, on the injured list. And although the Steelers' coaching staff has said the usual platitudes regarding Dunker's development so far this season, the rookie's lack of experience thus far makes it clear that backup lineman Spencer Anderson will be the new starting left guard for the immediate future.

Of course, there's a chance Anderson underperforms or Dunker impresses enough over the next few weeks for the rookie to overtake the veteran in the starting lineup. But whether or not that does happen, Dunker could still play a notable role for the Steelers as the sixth lineman in the offense's jumbo package.

The Steelers started using their jumbo package this season in Week 7, a 16-10 win over the Saints where Pittsburgh rushed for 148 yards. It appeared again in Week 8's victory over the Cleveland Browns, where the Steelers managed 200 yards on the ground.

Anderson, of course, has served as that sixth lineman in the jumbo package for the Steelers in recent seasons, with Pittsburgh making the package part of its offensive identity in 2025 under then-coordinator Arthur Smith. New head coach Mike McCarthy made the choice to emphasize it once again midway through 2026, and the results were still overwhelmingly positive.

According to Sumer Sports, Pittsburgh averages +0.25 EPA per rush when using its jumbo package in 11 personnel this season, well above the team's still-good +0.06 EPA per rush across all personnel. Per a detailed breakdown from BTSC's Ryan Parish following the Browns game, the Steelers earned a first down or touchdown on 37.5% of their runs out of a six-lineman package in Weeks 7-8.

Why did McCarthy take so long to come back to the jumbo package? A number of reasons could be in play, including game-by-game matchups and Pittsburgh's rash of wide receiver injuries in recent weeks. However, McCarthy also hinted after the Saints game that the team didn't use the package early as Anderson began the season in the starting lineup before suffering a reported bone chip injury in late September.

Once Brock Hoffman took over as a starting guard and Anderson was healthy again, the latter was available to play as the sixth lineman and the jumbo package suddenly reappeared.

With Anderson about to go back into the starting lineup, it might not be a great sign for the future of the jumbo package. But Dunker might be able to fill that role.

The Steelers seem to like Anderson as the sixth lineman because his athleticism (9.35 RAS as a guard) and versatility make him a strong candidate for the almost pseudo-tight end nature position.

The Steelers didn't seem to like Hoffman in that role while Anderson was the starter as the former scored just a 4.19 RAS at guard and has largely been an interior player over his career. Those same concerns shouldn't apply as much to Dunker, who has a 9.61 RAS as a guard after playing tackle during his Iowa days. And while Dunker is still learning as a pass blocker, his road-grading ability in the run game should translate to the pros more immediately.

Beyond that, Dunker's mean demeanor in the ground game fits perfectly with the bully-ball identity of the Steelers' jumbo package. Despite Pittsburgh moving Anderson back into the starting lineup following McCormick's injury, the pending activation of Dunker might not spell the end to one of the Steelers' most successful offensive packages this season.

And facing a bottom-10 rushing defense in the Philadelphia Eagles in Week 11, Sunday's game could be a good place to start.
