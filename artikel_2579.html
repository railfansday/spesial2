Vancouver Whitecaps ace first test on road to MLS Cup

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

SANDY, Utah – Vancouver Whitecaps FC are staying on track in the Audi 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs.

Emmanuel Sabbi's lone goal was the difference Friday night as the Western Conference's No. 1 seed topped Real Salt Lake, 1-0, to complete a Round One Best-of-3 Series sweep at America First Field.

Around two dozen Whitecaps supporters made the trip to Utah, many of whom also traveled to Florida last December to watch their club fall to Inter Miami CF in MLS Cup 2025 presented by Audi.

And on the same night the Herons suffered a shocking Round One exit to the Columbus Crew, those supporters got to celebrate another step in Vancouver's quest for redemption. The Whitecaps advanced to the Western Conference Semifinals with their second straight Round One sweep.

"We are focused on ourselves, obviously," head coach Jesper Sørensen told reporters. "Two years in a row sweeping the first round is not easy."

Since kicking off the 2026 regular season against Real Salt Lake in February with a similar 1-0 win, the Whitecaps have had a clear mission: finish what they couldn't in 2025.

That focus was on full display in the dying moments Friday night, when Mathías Laborda's spectacular diving header off the goal line denied Sergi Solans an equalizer and preserved the series sweep.

While other knockout competitions — the Concacaf Champions Cup, Leagues Cup and Canadian Championship — ended well before crowning moments, the focus on MLS Cup has been clear-cut.

"Everyone is really feeling it, and it's more like we are very focused, and we also gained more experience again through that," said German legend Thomas Müller. "Some players played this year for the first time with their countries, and that also develops the mindset and a little bit of everything around it."

Throughout 2026, Bruno Caicedo's slick skills have brought Whitecaps fans to their feet. However, the 21-year-old Ecuadorian has brought a new level of tenacity to the postseason.

In Game 1, he provided the insurance goal with a skillful finish to seal a 2-0 win before picking up the decisive assist Friday night. Caicedo is quickly emerging as a fan favorite in Vancouver — even if his coach sees plenty of room for growth.

"He has been doing a lot of things that have really created chances for us," Sørensen said.

"He was not perfect today, but he was a big threat, and his confidence is peaking in the playoffs, which is also very valuable."

Having dispatched Real Salt Lake, the Whitecaps now await their Western Conference Semifinal opponent, either the San Jose Earthquakes or Houston Dynamo, on home turf at BC Place next weekend.

While they won't get a rematch of MLS Cup 2025, the Whitecaps will have a vested interest in Saturday's Game 2 between Nashville SC and FC Cincinnati. Should the Supporters' Shield winners crash out in Round One, Vancouver's chances of hosting MLS Cup 2026 presented by Audi on Dec. 18 would receive a significant boost.

"We have now this big advantage to play in front of our fans for the enjoyment of it," Müller said. "I don't see a big advantage. It's more like enjoyment."
