&quot;Gadou is just a beast!&quot;: Beier and Schlotterbeck reflect on Dortmund's hard-earned draw at Arsenal

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Borussia Dortmund walked away from the Emirates Stadium with a hard-fought 1-1 draw against Arsenal in the UEFA Champions League on Tuesday evening, bouncing back from an early deficit through Maximilian Beier's 55th-minute equalizer.

Speaking after the match, Dortmund captain Nico Schlotterbeck admitted that his side made life difficult for themselves before the break before tightening up in the second half.

"I think in the first half we played too sloppily at times and gave away too many balls unnecessarily in attack," Schlotterbeck said. "We did that better in the second half."

Arsenal still carved out plenty of openings, though Schlotterbeck credited Dortmund's last-ditch defending - and voiced frustration over a second-half penalty appeal that went unpunished.

"Arsenal had a lot of chances, but we were also able to block many big opportunities," the center-back noted. "We could have had a penalty as well - I don't know why it wasn't given in the end. It looked clear to me."

Schlotterbeck also broke down Beier's equalizer, which arrived moments after the forward had passed up an earlier opening inside the box: "On his first chance, Maxi simply goes with the wrong foot - he knows that himself. He makes up for it the second time, using his left foot. Great setup from Joane [Gadou], and Maxi's touch afterward is top class, not easy to defend. We stepped up to Arsenal with our intensity tonight."

Beier himself was quick to single out the young French defender for the driving run and assist that unlocked Arsenal's back line.

"Yes, Joane is just a beast, the way he sets that thing up," Beier smiled. "And my finish wasn't too bad either."

For the Germany international, grinding out a point in north London sent a clear message about Dortmund's credentials against top-tier opposition.

"I'm very satisfied - we earned that point. You have to be able to hold onto a 1-1 like that," Beier added. "Today we showed that we can keep up with top teams like Arsenal and take points off them. We proved ourselves."
