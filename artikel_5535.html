Purity Scientific Expands Multi-Tier Service Programs for Analytical Instruments Across North America

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

ARCADE, N.Y., Nov. 10, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Purity Scientific, together with its subsidiaries GenTech Scientific and Conquer Scientific, announced the expansion of its multi-tier service programs for chromatography, spectrometry, and other analytical instruments across the United States and Canada. These programs give laboratories OEM-level coverage and response quality at a fraction of the cost of manufacturer service contracts.

The company's Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum tiers provide laboratories with scalable support options that align with instrument age, utilization, and uptime requirements. Every tier includes an annual preventive maintenance visit with all consumable parts, ensuring instrument reliability and compliance.

Customers can renew their programs annually to maintain continuous protection and avoid the price premiums of OEM contracts. Multi-year discounts are available for laboratories that commit to extended coverage terms. Details about each service tier are available at gentechscientific.com and conquerscientific.com

Purity Scientific is the only company in the analytical instrument secondary market with the scale, staffing, and technical expertise to deliver national service programs for analytical laboratories. "Laboratories should not have to choose between high-quality support and affordability," said Matthew Boucher, Chief Executive Officer of Purity Scientific. "Our tiered service programs give customers the flexibility to match coverage to their needs, knowing they receive expert support and genuine parts every time."

The company supports academic and commercial labs with consistent, high-quality service delivery with dedicated field teams across Boston, Philadelphia, San Diego, Puerto Rico, and Western New York.
