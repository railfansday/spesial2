Zifo Launches Service Accelerator to Reduce Validation Documentation Effort

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

AI-assisted validation document automation service accelerator connects requirements, risks, tests, and qualification records while keeping validation experts in control.

CAMBRIDGE, Mass. and CAMBRIDGE, England, Nov. 3, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Zifo, a leading global enabler of AI and data-driven enterprise informatics for science-driven organizations, today announced the launch of an AI-assisted service accelerator designed to reduce the documentation burden associated with computer system validation (CSV) and instrument and equipment qualification.

Validation teams in regulated environments must create and maintain requirements, risk assessments, test scenarios, test scripts, traceability matrices, and qualification documents. These deliverables are essential to demonstrating that systems and equipment are fit for their intended use, but preparing and updating them can require substantial specialist effort.

The service accelerator uses AI-assisted document analysis and generation to help validation teams create consistent, traceable deliverables while retaining structured human review and approval. The service accelerator is designed to support validation experts, not replace their scientific, quality, or compliance judgment.

"Validation experts should be focused on risk, system suitability, quality oversight, and inspection readiness, rather than spending most of their time manually creating and reconciling documents," said Loganathan Kumarasamy, Global Head of Compliance Services at Zifo. "Zifo's Validation service accelerator helps teams accelerate documentation while keeping validation SMEs firmly in control of review, approval, and compliance decisions."

From Source Documents to Connected Validation Evidence

Validation data is frequently distributed across user requirements, functional and design specifications, standard operating procedures, vendor manuals, configuration records, and existing qualification documents.

Zifo's service accelerator builds context from these materials before extracting and structuring requirements for risk-based analysis. The service accelerator can then assist with the generation of risk assessments, test scenarios, test scripts, and installation, operational, and performance qualification documentation.

Across the validation lifecycle, Zifo's service accelerator maintains connections among requirements, identified risks, test cases, protocols, and validation records. This traceability helps teams understand how a requirement is tested, which risks are being controlled, and which documents may be affected when the system changes.

Validation does not end when a system enters production. Configuration changes, application upgrades, vendor releases, platform migrations, instrument updates, integrations, and business-process changes can all affect previously approved validation evidence.

The service accelerator includes AI-driven change-impact assessment capabilities that analyze proposed changes, identify potentially affected requirements and risks, and highlight related protocols, test scripts, and qualification records. This allows teams to focus their review on the relevant validation artifacts rather than manually tracing every relationship across the document set.
