TCU coaching candidates: Lincoln Riley emerges as blockbuster name to watch

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

While Jeff Traylor has been at the center of Baylor's coaching search and has spoken with the Bears' search contingent, according to multiple individuals briefed on the matter, the UTSA coach also is expected to be in the mix at TCU.

The Horned Frogs fired Sonny Dykes on Sunday, four years after he led TCU to the College Football Playoff championship game.

Industry insiders stressed Sunday, Nov. 22, that the TCU job is viewed as more desirable than Baylor's among coaches.

And there could be a blockbuster candidate.

Three individuals told USA TODAY Sports, "Don't be surprised if they (TCU leaders) take a run at Lincoln Riley."

"I would expect he would listen," one industry insider added.

As USA TODAY Sports previously reported, Riley can leave USC for another job without owing a buyout. His contract stipulated that a change in athletics leadership would eliminate that obligation. USC was led by Mike Bohn when Riley was hired away from Oklahoma but has since transitioned to former Washington athletic director Jen Cohen.

Texas State coach G.J. Kinne and Florida Atlantic coach Zach Kittley also are expected to draw consideration because of their deep ties to Texas.

Kinne, a Mesquite, Texas, native, has guided Texas State to a share of the second-best record in the rebuilt Pac-12 at 5-2. He owns a 30-20 career record as a head coach, with all five seasons spent in Texas.

Before Texas State, Kinne coached Incarnate Word in 2022 and led the Cardinals to a 12-2 finish, the program's best season of the modern era.

Per terms of Kinne's contract reviewed by USA TODAY Sports, he would owe Texas State $2.4 million if he departed for another head coaching job after this season. However, that buyout allows the sum to be disbursed across three payments — the first of which is due within six months of Kinne's exit.

Kittley, an Abilene native, has less head-coaching experience but similarly deep Texas roots. He rose through the coaching ranks at Texas Tech, eventually serving as the Red Raiders' offensive coordinator from 2022-24.

Two industry representatives also told USA TODAY Sports that Louisiana Tech coach Sonny Cumbie is expected to be in the mix.

Cumbie is a former longtime TCU assistant who served as the Horned Frogs' offensive coordinator, either sharing the role or holding it outright, from 2014-20 under former coach Gary Patterson.

Another Abilene native, Cumbie had an interim stint as Texas Tech's head coach before taking over at Louisiana Tech. After a sluggish start to the rebuild, he has guided the Bulldogs to a 17-7 record since the start of the 2025 season.

Cumbie's contract specifies that he — or a third party — must pay Tech "75% of his remaining unpaid guaranteed compensation if he leaves Louisiana Tech for another job before his agreement expires on Jan. 31, 2028." Cumbie's currently agreed to salary for the 2027 season is $760,000, plus the remainder of his present-year compensation. He could exit Tech for approximately $679,000 as of late-November 2026.

One potential wild-card candidate is Army coach Jeff Monken, according to an FCS head coach and an industry veteran.

Monken has a natural connection to TCU athletic director Mike Buddie, who was his boss at Army from 2019-24. USA TODAY Sports also has learned that Monken already has spoken with a program that currently has a head-coaching vacancy.
