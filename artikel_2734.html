Jordan Davis surprises young Eagles fan upset over his trade to Jaguars

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

It's been a few weeks since the Philadelphia Eagles traded former University of Georgia star Jordan Davis to the Jacksonville Jaguars.

The news was particularly hard for one dedicated fan named Jack, who posted a tearful goodbye video for Davis.

[DOWNLOAD: Free WSB-TV News app for alerts as news breaks]

His video gained hundreds of thousands of views and got the attention of Fanatics. The sports apparel company sent Jack a new, customized Eagles-Jaguars Jordan Davis jersey. But that wasn't all.

Fanatics also invited Jack and his mother to watch Jacksonville play the New York Giants where Jack got to meet his hero.

Warning: Their reunion video may make you cry.

Jack and Davis spent a few minutes playing with Pokémon cards and Davis signed his customized jersey. And his new friend made sure he didn't leave empty handed: Jack gifted Davis a photo of him in his jersey for his locker.

"THANK YOU SOOO MUCH for the most incredible weekend of my entire life!!!!!!❤️❤️❤️" Jack posted.
