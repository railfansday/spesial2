Dan Quinn, Jayden Daniels talk Bengals, Bill Croskey-Merritt and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Commanders got back to work on Thursday after what was essentially a second bye. They had a Thursday night game followed by Monday night's affair creating an extended break from the football field. The Week 10 victory over the Giants gave players more time off, with them reporting back to work on Tuesday and hitting the field for the first time on Thursday.

That started this practice week and the standard press conference routine leading up to this clash with the Cincinnati Bengals. Head coach Dan Quinn and quarterback Jayden Daniels hit the podium to talk about a game with significant buzz, with the Bengals riding high and the Commanders suddenly back in the NFC East race.

Quinn provided some positive injury updates, with center Lucas Patrick out of the concussion protocol and running back Bill Croskey-Merritt possibly returning off injured reserve in time to play the Bengals. He also spoke about the difficulty facing Joe Burrow, Tee Higgins and Ja'Marr Chase.

Daniels spoke about…nothing to this point. This story will be updated after he meets with the media.
