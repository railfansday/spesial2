No. 8 Houston holds on to win Players Era 8 championship despite Tyran Stokes-led push from No. 11 Kansas

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Tyran Stokes, the Rivals industry ranking's No. 1 recruit in the 2026 class and a top NBA Draft prospect, took over in the second half Thursday night in the Players Era 8 championship at Las Vegas' Michelob ULTRA Arena.

Stokes fueled a head-turning comeback bid after No. 11 Kansas trailed No. 8 Houston by as many as 24 points in the first half.

The Jayhawks pulled within four points of the Cougars with a bit more than 2 ½ minutes remaining. Then Houston responded with an 8-0 run that effectively clinched what turned out to be a 92-83 victory.

Both teams were playing their third game in three days to cap the early-season tournament. The Cougars (6-0) came out on top and demonstrated that they're capable of winning a much more prestigious tourney in the spring. Kansas (5-1) will regroup and get another crack at the Cougars in Big 12 play on Feb. 1.

Earlier in the night, top-ranked Florida, which lost to Houston on Wednesday, won the Players Era 8 tournament's third-place game, defeating West Virginia 85-72.

While Stokes picked up a couple early personals and navigated foul trouble down the stretch, the freshman guard finished with 22 points and 11 rebounds, logging his third consecutive double-double. His intensity went a long way in igniting the Jayhawks' surge, as he accounted for 15 points and eight boards in the second half alone.

Kansas also got 18 points from senior guard Dennis Parker Jr. and 17 from sophomore guard Leroy Blyden Jr. They went a combined 22 of 23 from the charity stripe.

Houston was paced by junior guard Mercy Miller. He clocked out with 21 points, 8 rebounds and 4 assists, connecting on four 3-points in the process. Senior forward Delrecco Gillespie wasn't far behind with 19 points and seven boards. Dedan Thomas Jr. came through in the clutch, warding off Kansas late. The senior guard paired a layup and jumper to give Houston breathing room after the Jayhawks were on the brink of making it one-possession game.
