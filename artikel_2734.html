Michael Porter Jr. Invites Controversy With ‘Crazy’ Relationship Confession

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Michael Porter Jr. sparked controversy after revealing how he would handle a relationship if his girlfriend suffered a life-changing injury. The Brooklyn Nets player admitted that he would eventually consider leaving his partner if an accident left her paralyzed or severely injured.

The NBA star discussed the hypothetical situation with model Sommer Ray during an episode of his Curious Mike podcast. His comments drew criticism from social media users who questioned his approach to relationships.

During the podcast, Porter Jr. asked Ray whether she would continue dating someone who suffered a serious injury. He presented several scenarios, including a broken back, severe burns, and paralysis following an unexpected accident.

However, before Ray could respond, the basketball player shared his own perspective on the situation. Porter Jr. explained that he would initially support his girlfriend through the difficult circumstances. Nevertheless, he admitted that he would gradually distance himself from the relationship.

"I always say, if that happened to me, my girl, something crazy happens to her, I'm going to be by her side in the moment, but there's going to be a slow (exit)," he said.

The 28-year-old clarified that his relationship confession applied only to couples who were dating. He distinguished those relationships from marriages and engagements while explaining his position. Porter Jr. and Ray laughed during the conversation, but his remarks received a different reaction online.

According to TMZ, the remarks added to the attention Porter Jr. had previously attracted through his podcast discussions. Meanwhile, the Brooklyn Nets had struggled during the opening weeks of the 2026-27 NBA season. The team held a 3-10 record despite Porter Jr.'s strong individual performances. His latest podcast appearance, however, shifted attention from his basketball career to his views on romantic relationships.

His comments have since sparked debate about commitment and whether romantic relationships can withstand life-changing circumstances.
