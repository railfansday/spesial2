Eagles, ex-Cowboys QB Joe Milton III overcome 21-point deficit to stun Dallas: 'I know that defense like the back of my hand'

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Philadelphia Eagles stunned the Dallas Cowboys on Thanksgiving, kicking a 59-yard game-winning field goal with two seconds left to clinch a 39-38 victory for their second consecutive win in a crucial divisional matchup.

The 21-point comeback marked the largest Thanksgiving comeback in NFL history.

It looked like the Eagles were done for, unable to muster much offense and unable to stop the Cowboys' offense. Dallas led 31-10 with 15 seconds left in the third quarter, but Philadelphia stormed back thanks to quarterback Joe Milton III's arm.

The momentum swung in a big way when Milton launched a 45-yard touchdown pass near the goal line, finding DeVonta Smith for the score.

That was the duo's second long touchdown of the game; they connected earlier on a 50-yard score.

The Eagles claimed Milton after the Cowboys cut him from their practice squad on Oct. 16. Thursday was his third start for the Philadelphia, and he finished the great comeback 21-for-31 passing for 331 yards, 3 touchdowns and 1 interception.

"I know that defense like the back of my hand," Milton said, per the Forth Worth Star- Telegram's Nick Harris.

After the 45-yard Smith score, the Eagles' special teams unit blocked a punt and recovered it for a touchdown. In a minute and a half, Philadelphia went from trailing by 21 to seven.

Dak Prescott led a Cowboys touchdown drive, ending it with an impressive quarterback keeper as he lunged to the end zone, got hit, and still managed to carry the ball across the goal line while in bounds.

Milton answered, almost identically, with a rushing touchdown of his own on the following drive, though his didn't have to withstand a big hit from a defender.

After a Dallas punt, it looked like the Eagles had the chance they needed, but Milton made one of his few mistakes with an interception he threw into triple coverage.

The only harm came from time lost on the clock as Brandon Aubrey missed a 40-yard field goal.

Milton put the team on his back again and fired a laser to the end zone, finding first-round rookie wide receiver Makai Lemon leaping into the end zone.

The Eagles went for 2 and converted, but it was called back due to holding. Philadelphia's second attempt at a 2-point conversion fell to the ground.

Prescott and the Cowboys tried to run out the clock, but they faced fourth-and-1 on the Eagles' 39-yard line. Prescott was sacked for a 9-yard loss, giving the Eagles a chance to get into field-goal range and go for the win.

Milton got the Eagles to the Cowboys' 41-yard line, which was enough to put Elliott in range.

After the field goal, Prescott's Hail Mary attempt fell incomplete.

"Yeah, it's pretty stunning," Prescott said via the team's website. "I said that about the Cardinals game, but this one takes the cake."

Philadelphia won its first two games of the season, and then dropped seven in a row, leading to a Week 10 bye. Philadelphia beat the Pittsburgh Steelers on Sunday and has now notched its second consecutive victory, moving to 4-7.

Dallas (5-7) has now alternated wins and losses since Week 6.
