LIVE DISCUSSION: Brooklyn Nets vs. Philadelphia 76ers, 7:30 PM ET

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Brooklyn Nets are back home, but will they be home? The Philadelphia 76ers are 11-3, and their fans are known to travel to Brooklyn, particularly when their team is playing this well. We'll see how things look in the stands Monday night, but cut us some slack: It hasn't exactly been easy.

Brooklyn enters at 3-9 after dropping a 129-110 decision to the Memphis Grizzlies on Saturday. Head coach Jordi Fernandez told reporters the Grizzlies "played harder than us." Low effort is entirely unacceptable from everyone involved. It's Year three of the rebuild, not one. If players aren't bought in then it'll be really difficult to turn this thing around quickly and organically, exactly how they want to.

Let's see if the Nets first quarter woes can slow down — they trailed 38-23 in the opening quarter and spent most of the night trying to dig out of that hole. Meanwhile, Philadelphia is coming off a 131-124 win over Indiana on Sunday. Jaylen Brown led the way with 31 points, while Tyrese Maxey added 27.

LeBron James also had 15 points and 11 assists, but he'll be unavailable Monday due to low back/sciatica management. Joel Embiid is questionable with right foot plantar fascia injury management.
