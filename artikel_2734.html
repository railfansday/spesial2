Notre Dame making steady rising progress in SI’s mock CFP bracket

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

There have been weeks where US LBM Coaches Poll No. 3 Notre Dame has been at the top of Sports Illustrated's weekly College Football Playoff projections.

After being the No. 2 seed last week, the Fighting Irish are in the top spot of SI's latest mock CFP bracket.

SI has No. 1 Notre Dame facing No. 8 Texas in the Fiesta Bowl of the quarterfinal round, which comes after the Longhorns beat No. 9 Texas Tech in the opening round.

After the Irish knock off Texas, projections have them going up against No. 5 Georgia in the Orange Bowl of the CFP semifinals. According to SI, the Irish road ends there with the Bulldogs winning to punch their ticket to the national title game.

Following a 41-7 win over Boston College this past weekend, Notre Dame (9-1) will host SMU (7-3) on Saturday at 7:30 p.m. ET on NBC.
