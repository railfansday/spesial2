No. 17 Boise State suffers costly loss to San Diego State after late clock management snafu

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

San Diego State hit a field goal as time expired to beat No. 17 Boise State 22-20 in a stunning upset Saturday night on Boise's famed blue turf.

Cooper DiLeva's winning 42-yard kick deals a serious blow to Boise's College Football Playoff hopes, which it can earn by finishing the season as the highest ranked Group of Five team.

SDSU's victory was made possible by a bizarre sequence in the final minutes as the Broncos were trying to run out the clock with the ball.

On third-and-8 with 1:41 on the clock, BSU threw a pass that ended up with tight end Keawe Browne out of bounds, which stopped the clock at 1:36. The Aztecs had zero timeouts so that was a huge favor.

On the next play, fourth-and-3 at the SDSU 45-yard line, BSU tried for the first down rather than punting it away and pinning the Aztecs back. Quarterback Maddux Madsen was stuffed for a loss, giving SDSU then ball in prime field position with 1:31 left on the clock.

The Aztecs quickly drove into field goal range to set up the winning kick.
