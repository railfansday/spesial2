Giants CB Greg Newsome out 'multiple weeks' with 'serious' ankle sprain suffered in win over Jaguars

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

New York Giants cornerback Greg Newsome will be sidelined for "multiple weeks" with a high ankle sprain, head coach John Harbaugh confirmed on Monday.

It's still unclear if Newsome will need surgery or if he will land on injured reserve, which would knock him out for at least four games.

"It's a serious high ankle. It's gonna be one of those lengthy high ankles," Harbaugh said, via ESPN. "It's going to be multiple weeks."

Newsome went down on the opening drive of the Giants' 23-18 win over the Jacksonville Jaguars on Sunday. He was blocked on a run and went down awkwardly to the turf. Newsome ended up leaving the game and left MetLife Stadium in a walking boot.

Newsome is in his first season with the Giants this fall. He signed a one-year deal this past offseason and then reached a three-year, $46 million extension with the team earlier this month. Newsome has 35 total tackles and one interception so far this fall.

Paulson Adebo took Newsome's place on Sunday afternoon and ended up getting a game ball after the win. He had one tackle and a team-high four passes defended. Harbaugh called Adebo a "legitimate corner in this league," and said that "all he's done is just put his head down and get to work every single day and work his tail off."

While Adebo appears more than capable of holding things down, losing Newsome for the foreseeable future will undoubtedly hinder the Giants' defense. They sit at 6-3-1, which has them in first in the NFC East, entering a matchup with the Indianapolis Colts on Sunday. If they can hold on to win the division, it will mark their first NFC East title since 2011, the same season they won the Super Bowl.
