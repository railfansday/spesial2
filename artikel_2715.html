Vikings WR Jauan Jennings prepare to face his former team

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Sunday night’s game in Mexico City between the Vikings and 49ers has multiple interesting subplots. One relates to a player who had spent his entire career with the 49ers before signing with Minnesota.

Jauan Jennings is in his first season as a Viking. In six games with two starts, he has only six catches for 54 yards.

And there was a strong possibility of a trade back to the 49ers. He ultimately decided to stay where he was.

The Vikings will need him on Sunday night, especially with receiver Jordan Addison out due to a thumb injury.

“With us being down Jordan Addison, we’re gonna need him to make some plays in the pass game,” coach Kevin O’Connell said Tuesday on KFAN.

Jennings didn’t play in Week 10 at Green Bay due to a calf injury.

During five seasons with the 49ers, Jennings caught 210 passes for 2,581 yards and 22 touchdowns in 75 regular-season games. He also had 23 catches for 289 yards and a touchdown in 11 postseason games — including four catches for 42 yards and a touchdownand a touchdown pass in Super Bowl LIX.

Without Addison, Jennings could get more opportunities. And he will surely have a little extra incentive to show his former team that they should have kept him around.
