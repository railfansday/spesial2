Tjasa Turnsek leads Iowa State women's basketball past Eastern Illinois

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Tjasa Turnsek scored 20 points to lead Iowa State women's basketball past Eastern Illinois 77-61 on Nov. 8 at Hilton Coliseum in Ames.

Turnsek was 8-of-14 from the field and added four rebounds and three assists.

Mya Babbitt finished with 18 points for the Cyclones, who improved to 3-0. Xiao Zhang (14 points) and Gift Ezekiel (12) also scored in double figures.

Iowa State forced 18 turnovers in the victory and had 38-18 points advantage in the paint.

Sienna Harvey had nine assists for Iowa State, which plays host to Howard on Nov. 12.
