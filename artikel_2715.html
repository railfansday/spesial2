Injury Report: Washington Wizards vs. Orlando Magic

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Following the mini-Thanksgiving break, the Washington Wizards will return to action on Saturday afternoon when they take on the Orlando Magic for the second meeting of the week and already the third during the 2026-27 regular season.

With tipoff set for 5 PM, the Wizards will enter tipoff with its same inactive list as the last three weeks.

Guard Reece Beekman, forward Tristan Vukcevic, forward Justin Champagnie, rookie forward Felix Okpara and guard Jamir Watkins are all ruled out once again. While both Champagnie and Watkins are ruled out with long term injuries, head coach Brian Keefe noted that Vukcevic is "progressing well" as he still awaits the chance to make his season debut. Vukcevic was seen getting shots up and working through conditioning drills following Friday's practice, while he was ruled a partial participant during training camp after being diagnosed with a left hip impingement. Beekman, meanwhile, is "getting closer" after being diagnosed with a right AC joint injury.

While he has routinely carried the questionable designation ahead of tipoff during the regular season, Okpara was also officially ruled out just hours after he was ejected in the fourth quarter of the Capital City Go-Go's 21-point road loss. After being lightly shoved, Okpara swung on former first round pick Rob Dillingham, leading to an immediate stoppage with both Okpara and Dillingham ejected from the game.

Yet the Magic could enter tipoff with another notable absence in the rotation as point guard Anthony Black is ruled questionable due to back soreness. Black sits fourth on the team in scoring with 15.3 points per game, starting in all but one of his 14 appearances this season. Black's absence would sideline two of the Magics' top four scorers, giving the Wizards another opportunity to capitalize against a short-handed opponent.

The former first round pick out of Arkansas marks the lone question mark for the Magic ahead of tipoff. Orlando has already ruled out forward Paolo Banchero, forward Jonathan Isaac, forward Izaiyah Nelson, forward Noah Penda and guard Kevin Porter Jr. Two-way center Colin Castleton is also ruled out, though he has appeared in just two games in November.

While Washington looks for their fourth win over their last five games, Orlando enters Saturday sitting 7-10 overall after dropping three of their last four games. While all but one of Orlando's wins this season have come on the road, Washington will look to win the season series for the first time since 2021-22.
