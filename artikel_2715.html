Donovan Mitchell’s 39 points help push Cavaliers past Bulls 126-115

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Cleveland Cavaliers, coming off an exceptional win against the Dallas Mavericks with the reserves, had all fans wondering how the starters would respond. The win on Saturday was driven by intensity, effort, and a team that never let their foot off the gas.

The starters for Cleveland against the Bulls had a lesser opponent in front of them; however, the effort felt like an improvement from past games in the early season.

Right off the bat, the Cavaliers were honed in offensively by scoring through the paint; within the first few possessions, Jarrett Allen was rim running with little to no resistance.

Cleveland kept with this mindset and seemed to prioritize their effort on the glass and scoring inside. This proved to be the correct mindset. The Cavaliers scored scoring 68 of their 126 points around the cup. The Cavs were also outhustling the Bulls on the boards, 51-37.

Monday proved another great night for the young bucks in Tyrese Proctor and Meleek Thomas. Proctor, who was the star of Saturday night's game, was given the nod with the starters in the absence of Evan Mobley. Proctor fit in seamlessly with the starters, scoring 17 points and finding his spots without hijacking the offense.

Thomas (9 points and 6 rebounds) likewise was able to find his spots and continues to impress. His knack for scoring from almost anywhere on the court allows him to fit with any five-man lineup the Cavaliers throw out.

At the end of the day, this game was won by Donovan Mitchell, who scored the game-high 39 points on 16-25 shooting. Whenever it felt like the Bulls would attempt to mount a rally, it was Mitchell who would offer his own counterpunch to end the counter.

Mitchell's ability to sense when his buckets were needed was nice to see from the leader of the team. The Bulls, at the end of the day, do not have someone of Mitchell's caliber who can respond to the nuclear levels of scoring that Donovan can.

While not the dominant effort some were hoping for, it is nice that the Cavaliers can win games like this without having to sweat it out until the final buzzer. It isn't the 30-point blowouts we saw a few years ago, but this feels like a step in the right direction.

The Cavaliers look to take this momentum into their next matchup on Wednesday against the Memphis Grizzlies.
