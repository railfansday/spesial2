Raiders get encouraging sign on Brock Bowers’ ankle, but his status remains unclear

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Raiders got an encouraging sign Thursday regarding Brock Bowers' availability for Sunday's game against the Denver Broncos.

The star tight end was on the practice field during the portion of Thursday's workout open to the media, a potentially significant development after he sat out Wednesday's practice with an ankle injury.

Bowers' official practice designation won't be revealed until later Thursday afternoon, so it remains unclear how much work he was able to do or whether he will be available Sunday.

Brock Bowers of the Las Vegas Raiders leaves the field after an injury against the Seattle Seahawks in Las Vegas, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. Getty Images

Nevertheless, his presence on the field represented a step in the right direction for the Raiders and rookie quarterback Fernando Mendoza, who could certainly use his most dangerous offensive weapon against one of the NFL's premier defenses.

Bowers injured his ankle on the Raiders' opening possession against the Seattle Seahawks last Sunday. Although he initially attempted to return after being evaluated on the sideline, he quickly retreated to the bench and was eventually ruled out for the remainder of the game.

His absence was a significant blow to Mendoza, who was making his first NFL start against a Seattle defense that presented a series of difficult challenges.

Without Bowers, Mendoza was deprived of his most reliable receiving option and one of the NFL's most dynamic tight ends.

The Seahawks eventually overwhelmed the Raiders' offense, sacking Mendoza six times and making life increasingly difficult for the rookie as the game progressed.

Fernando Mendoza (15) of the Las Vegas Raiders warms up before a game at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. The Seahawks defeated the Raiders 21-13. Getty Images

The Broncos feature an aggressive defense coordinated by Vance Joseph, whose pressure packages and talented pass rush present another daunting assignment for Mendoza in his second career start.

Having Bowers available would provide Mendoza with a dependable target capable of creating separation, making contested catches and turning short completions into significant gains.

Las Vegas Raiders' Michael Mayer avoids a tackle from Seattle Seahawks' Josh Jobe during a game in Las Vegas, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. Getty Images

It would also give Raiders coach Klint Kubiak considerably more flexibility in designing an offensive game plan intended to better protect his young quarterback.

That is especially important given the Raiders' continued struggles running the football and protecting the passer.

For now, Thursday's development offers reason for optimism.

Whether that translates into Bowers being available Sunday remains to be seen, but his return to the practice field was an encouraging first step.

If he can't play, Mendoza will likely lean on Michael Mayer, who combines with Bowers to form one of the best tight end tandems in the NFL.
