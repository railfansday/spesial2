Texans QB CJ Stroud Gets 100% Real on His Flu Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Houston Texans quarterback CJ Stroud revealed that he battled significant illness throughout the week before leading the team to a dominant 44-22 victory over the Los Angeles Chargers. Despite barely practicing during Thursday's session, Stroud delivered a five-touchdown performance that helped Houston secure an important win.

Stroud explained that he had been feeling unwell throughout the week and struggled to participate in the team's preparation. The quarterback credited his teammates for helping him manage the difficult stretch, specifically acknowledging Cole Weeks for providing encouragement when he was feeling particularly sick. His limited practice time raised questions about his readiness heading into the matchup.

Jun 9, 2026; Houston, Texas, USA; Houston Texans quarterback C.J. Stroud arrives for the team's minicamp at Houston Methodist Training Center. Mandatory Credit: Maria Lysaker-Imagn Images

The Texans quarterback nevertheless managed to produce one of his strongest performances of the season against Los Angeles. Stroud threw five touchdown passes as Houston scored 44 points and controlled much of the contest. The offensive performance helped the Texans pull away from the Chargers and demonstrate their ability to execute despite the quarterback's physical condition.

Stroud also reflected on the challenges of overcoming difficult periods during an NFL season. He expressed gratitude for having the resilience to continue playing through adversity and recognized that difficult performances are part of a player's career. The quarterback's comments highlighted the importance of perseverance after experiencing both successful and challenging moments on the field.

Dec 21, 2024; Kansas City, Missouri, USA; Houston Texans quarterback C.J. Stroud (7) throws a touchdown pass during the second half against the Kansas City Chiefs at GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium. Mandatory Credit: Jay Biggerstaff-Imagn Images

Stroud shared his thoughts about how he played against the Chargers. This can be seen in a video shared by the league's official Instagram account.

"To be honest with you, I was very sick this whole week," Stroud said. "I don't know what I had, but I was not feeling good all week. I barely practiced on Thursday. My teammates really helped me. Cole Weeks came and encouraged me during the week because I was really feeling bad and just wasn't feeling great. I'm grateful that God has allowed me to have some tough skin and be able to push through some tough times. Days like this come from being on the other side when you're not playing so great, so I'm grateful that God has allowed me to have the opportunity to play today."

Houston's offensive production proved too much for the Chargers to handle, with Stroud leading a passing attack that consistently generated scoring opportunities. The Texans finished with 44 points, while the Los Angeles offense managed 22. Stroud's five touchdown passes became the defining feature of the game, particularly considering the illness that had affected his preparation during the preceding days.

The victory provided Houston with a significant boost, while Stroud demonstrated his ability to deliver under difficult circumstances. The quarterback will now look to recover fully and maintain his production as the Texans continue their regular-season campaign. His performance against the Chargers showed how Houston could remain productive even when its starting quarterback entered a game after a challenging week.
