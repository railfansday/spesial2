Right now, there’s an endless stream of articles telling you what and where you should be shopping for the holiday season. And I’m going to tell you right off the bat: Sure, this is another one of those. But this one is actually here to serve you.

I happen to be an expert gift-giver — just look up my LinkedIn if you don’t believe me — and I spend all day browsing the internet to find things people should actually want to unwrap from their loved ones. And if the people in your life happen to spend more time on the nice list than the naughty one (which is a good thing), your shopping tasks might take a little while.

A classic Carhartt logo sweatshirt so he can finally throw out the old, ratty one he’s been wearing since his college frat days. It’s a win for both of you, really.Want even more sweatshirt options? Right now, you can get limited edition hoodies for up to 40% off until 12/6.

My biggest hack? Instead of bouncing between multiple stores trying to find something for everyone, find one place where you can knock out as much of your “to-dos” as possible.

And no, I’m not talking about one of those massive retailers that might sell you something that looks slightly different from the pictures. I’m not trying to name names here. I’m talking about Carhartt.

The Michigan-founded brand has spent more than a century earning its reputation as an American institution, and, in my opinion, a gifting institution, too. Carhartt has something for men, women, kids, and even your furry best friends. Better yet, pretty much everything it sells is wildly wearable, reasonably priced, and surprisingly fashionable. If you’re already familiar with the brand’s staples, you know many of them look like much more expensive versions of styles you’d find elsewhere.

Below, I’ve rounded up some of my favorite finds from Carhartt’s website to gift this season. And because the holidays are closer than they appear, consider this your reminder to start shopping sooner rather than later. You’ll want to give yourself enough time for everything to arrive.

While we’re on the subject of upgrading his wardrobe: If your husband is perpetually indecisive about what to wear, just grab him a few of these Carhartt tees so the choice is basically made for him every morning. Bonus points for the fact that they’re ridiculously soft.

I love this jacket because it’s as fashionable or rugged as you want it to be. It all comes down to how he chooses to style it.

Remember how I mentioned earlier that their styles are incredibly well-priced? Take this piece, for example. It retails for less than $100, but if you asked my professional opinion — and you should — it looks like a much more expensive gift. She’ll be none the wiser.

Which is also exactly how I feel about these Chelsea boots. Typically, this type of silhouette can retail for well over $400, but Carhartt has them packaged up for less than $200. Essentially, they’re giving you an extra holiday present just by keeping the price down.

And because no holiday gift guide would be complete without at least one pair of socks: They might not sound like the most exciting Christmas gift, but they’re one of my personal favorite things to ask for. If you’re going to get someone socks, don’t get them just any pair, get them cute Carhartt ones they’ll actually be excited to pull out of the box.
