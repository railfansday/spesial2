‘Sunrise On The Reaping’ Firing Up Thanksgiving With $85M+ 5-Day; ‘Focker In-Law’ & ‘Hexed’ Fight For 2nd With $35M+ Apiece – Update

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

THANKSGIVING AM UPDATE: An alpha female property, Lionsgate‘s The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping with a continued 5-day forecast of $85M+, is stealing business from two other female-leaning movies at the Thanksgiving box office, Universal/Paramount’s Focker In-Law and Disney’s Hexed, both of which are coming in lighter than their forecasts with 5-days of $36M and $35M respectively. That’s essentially a fight for 2nd over the Wednesday-Sunday stretch at this point in time.

Now, that’s not to say that it was silly to date other female pics on top of another tentpole, however, the holiday has shown that two exceptionally pleasing events for the demo can coexist at the same time, read 2024’s Moana 2 and Wicked and even last year’s Wicked: For Good and Zootopia 2. Hexed and Focker In-Law aren’t exceptionally pleasing.

Most movies are expected to ease on average -23% today off their Wednesday daily B.O. as families ease back for festivities. Tomorrow, Black Friday, should see a daily surge between 50%-100% for most titles. Watch on Deadline

Sunrise on the Reaping‘s $15.1M Wednesday is the second best in the Suzanne Collins’ franchise after 2013’s Catching Fire which did $20.8M on the day before Thanksgiving. 3-day is still on track for $58M+, -51%.

Focker In-Law saw an uptick in its franchise CinemaScore to B+ from previous chapter, 2010’s Little Fockers which received a B-. The original movie 26 years ago has the best grade of A-. The pic is alright with a 64% definite recommend in Screen Engine/Rentrak PostTrak exits. Yesterday was $5.3M with the 3-day at $24.8M. Half of the audience was 18-34 yesterday, 46% over 35 and 64% female overall. Diversity demos were 37% Caucasian, 33% Latino and Hispanic, 13% Black, 10% Asian American and 7% Native American/other. PLFs rep 17% of the gross so far with the John Hamburg directed sequel (the first two pics were helmed by Jay Roach, the third by Paul Weitz) playing best in the West, South, and South Central. AMC Burbank is the top venue with $21k. Again, the Uni and Paramount co-production cost $90M net before global P&A.

Disney’s $200M Hexed is waiting for a box office spell tomorrow that could raise this original animated movie above the doldrums of the studio’s Thanksgiving 2023 dud Wish ($175M production cost, $8.1M Wednesday, $19.6M 3-day, $31.6M 5-day and final domestic of $63.9M). Hexed made $7.6M yesterday (including Tuesday previews) and is eyeing a 3-day of $20M. Hexed gets an A- just like Wish did. PostTrak shows 62% definite recommend for the general audience while kids under 12 gave it a 96% in the top 2 and a 51% must see right away. Kids under 12 rep 31% of the audience while 35-44 (parents) showed up at 31%. Diversity demos were 48% Caucasian, 26% Latino and Hispanic, 9% Black, 7% Asian American and 9% Native American/other.

Again, there are no original songs in this movie about a mother and daughter witch, the latter who realizes her powers as both are transported into another portal where they are trying to save their own kind from doom.

Like Focker-in-Law, Hexed is sharing PLFs with Sunrise on the Reaping which owns most of the holiday supply, those screens are counting 17% of the animated pic’s holiday B.O. so far. 3D is driving 12%. West, South, South Central, Midwest and Mountain are the best markets with the East soft performing 2% below the norm this week. AMC Dine-In Disney Springs Florida is the best location with $52k.

Paramount’s Ebenezer is looking at 4th place at 3,180 theaters with $1.6M yesterday, and still $6.8M-$7.3M third weekend, -6% to -13%, for a 5-day of $10M-$10.6M and running cume on the high end of $45.9M.

Fifth is Amazon MGM Studio’s I Play Rocky with $1.4M yesterday at 2,711 sites, $6.6M 3-day, -29%, 5-day of $9.6M and total of $22.6M by EOD Sunday.

Outside the top 5 is Netflix’s The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth at 252 locations, boosted by AMC Imax. Wednesday’s opening day estimate was $910K, 3-day is looking at $3.3M and a 5-day of $4.85M. At that level, the theater average over 5-days is $19,2K which is higher than what Netflix pulled did last Thanksgiving with Wake Up Dead Man: A Knives Out Mystery which had a per theater of $7K. Note that Rian Johnson directed thriller was booked in more theaters at 600 sites. Still a bit more robust result here on a limited basis than Wake Up Dead Man ($4.25M 5-day), but alas, Netflix, you’re leaving soooo much money on the table and there’s more than a enough room in this marketplace to have gone wide and minted at least $20M for this sequel to beloved fan Quentin Tarantino pic Once Upon a Time in Hollywood which at $142.8M is the Oscar winning filmmaker’s second highest grossing movie at the domestic B.O. Cliff Booth filmmaker David Fincher told Collider that the pic cost $120M-$123M, not $200M. We’ll leave it to Wall Street to figure out the downstream.

There’s a shot that Neon’s third weekend wide expansion of James Gray’s crime noir, Paper Tiger, based on the filmmaker’s life, comes in higher than Cliff Booth. Wednesday was an estimated $950K which translates into a $4.4M 3-day, 5-day of $5.1M at 1,322 (+1,294) theaters. Running total by Sunday is around $5.7M. Audiences weren’t wild about the Miles Teller, Adam Driver and Scarlett Johansson movie giving it a C+.

Angel Studio’s Hershey biopic about the chocolate maker with Alexandra Daddario and Finn Whitlock got a leg up with early shows yesterday with $1.3M including $534K estimated previews. No firm projection yet, but it was always expected to file in the single digits over its ‘4-day’ start. The pic doesn’t officially open until Thursday and all that preview cash will be rolled into day one. CinemaScore audiences love it with an A, and PostTrak isn’t shabby with 83% positive and a 69% definite recommend, but critics didn’t have a sweet tooth for it 17% Rotten. Male-to-female turnout was 50/50 with the Angel faithful attending at 37% over 55, and 51% over 45. Diversity demos are 61% Caucasian, 17% Black, 13% Latino and Hispanic, 6% Asian American, and 3% Native American/other.

WEDNESDAY AFTERNOON: If extreme weather doesn’t shutter theaters in the Northeast, then Lionsgate is looking forward to a lucrative Thanksgiving stretch with The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping potentially banking $85M+ over five-days.

Should the sixth title in the Suzanne Collins series file in that range, it will rep the second best Thanksgiving haul for a Hunger Games movie after Catching Fire‘s $109.9M Wednesday-Sunday turkey take 13 years ago. Sunrise on the Reaping‘s Wednesday is looking like $15.5M-$16.5M with a second 3-day weekend around $58M-$65M at 4,018 theaters, -46% to -51%. Five day forecasts are always a challenge for the industry to predict: Business dips on Thanksgiving then spikes on Black Friday, which is one of the most active moviegoing days of the year. By Sunday, we’re looking at a running cume for the Francis Lawrence directed movie of $233M domestic, which would be 40% higher than the lifetime cume on 2023’s Hunger Games: Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.

Second will either be Disney’s Hexed at 3,725 theaters or Universal/Paramount’s Focker-in-Law at 3,382 sites. Hexed is seeing around $7.8M today (including $2.1M previews), for a 3-day around $20M and 5-day between $35M-$40M. Let’s see if this picks up before passing complete judgement on this $200M original animation production, but it’s clear Hexed isn’t Coco or Frozen.

Focker In-Law is seeing a $5.2M Wednesday, 3-day of $24.8M and 5-day of $36.1M. No Rotten Tomatoes audience scores yet on either title.

Fourth goes to Paramount’s third frame of Johnny Depp Christmas movie, Ebenezer with $1.5M today at 3,180 sites and a 3-day between $6.8M-$7.3M, -6% to -13%, for a 5-day of $10M-$10.6M and running cume on the high end of $45.9M.

Fifth is Amazon MGM Studio’s fourth frame of I Play Rocky at 2,711 with $1.4M today, 3-day of $6.6M, and 5-day of $9.5M for a running cume of $22.5M.

Outside the top 5 is Netflix’s Once Upon a Time in Hollywood sequel, The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth at 251 locations with $1M today, $3M 3-day and 5-day of $5M.

WEDNESDAY AM: As we told you last night, Lionsgate scored its best Tuesday ever at the domestic box office with The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping and the best ever for the Suzanne Collins franchise as the day raked in $15.98M.

2013’s The Hunger Games: Catching Fire owned the previous best Tuesday for the franchise with $15.96M. Running cume for the fifth Francis Lawrence movie is $148.1M and by Thanksgiving, Sunrise on the Reaping will surpass its previous 2023 chapter’s lifetime domestic B.O. of $166.3M.

As we told you, Sunrise on the Reaping will dominate the Thanksgiving Wednesday through Sunday stretch as the franchise has historically done before since 2013, this time with a floor of $70M+. Previously, the pics over Thanksgiving cooked up the following: Catching Fire ($109.9M), Mockingjay Part 1 ($82.6M), Mockingjay Part 2 ($75.9M) with Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes way behind ($42.2M).

Lionsgate has had back-to-back global hit openings this year with Sunrise on the Reaping last weekend at $226.1M WW and Michael with $218.8M. This has all happened under the administration and watch of Motion Picture Chairman, Adam Fogelson. Still no word out there whether or not Lionsgate rejected or accepted Legendary’s bid.

Walt Disney Animation’s Hexed came in with $2.1M, $100K above the $2M we were spotting yesterday. That preview figure is higher than Encanto ($1.5M previews, 5-day of $40.5M) and Wish ($1.8M previews including $500K Saturday sneaks; 5-day of $31.6M). No RT audience score yet, but critics were meh at 67% fresh. There is a chance that the traditional Disney pic comes in lower than its 5-day forecast of $40M-$45M as original animated titles are very challenging to launch in the streaming era, but you have to start some place.

Universal and Paramount’s Focker In-Law, which didn’t have previews last night, rather began showtimes at midnight, has more potential to hit its 5-day forecast of $45M given the Ariana Grande factor and the fact that audiences love the franchise. The first Meet the Parents 26 years ago was the fave among critics at 85% fresh on Rotten Tomatoes. Focker-in-Law isn’t the worst at 26%, as that belongs to 2010’s Little Fockers. 2004’s Meet the Fockers stands at 39%.

Hexed, Focker-in-Law and Sunrise on the Reaping are sharing PLFs with the latter having Imax screens (except for AMC where they are being designated to Netflix’s The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth).

Thank God for Lionsgate Hunger Games at the box office: Sans a Wicked, Moana and a Zootopia, the Thanksgiving box office will certainly be down from last year’s five day robust $290.3M, which was the fifth best ever. Moana 2 and Wicked led the holiday to its best haul ever with $424.9M.
