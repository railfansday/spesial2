WWE Star Bronson Reed Dies at 38

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

WWE star Bronson Reed died Friday at the age of 38, the WWE confirmed in a statement.

The Australian-born wrestler, whose real name was Jermaine Haley, died “due to medical complications,” the league said.

“Jermaine was a larger-than-life figure in WWE, but more importantly, he was a phenomenal person, a devoted family man and a beloved colleague. Our thoughts and deepest condolences are with Jermaine’s entire family, including his pregnant wife, Paige, their daughter, as well as all his friends, co-workers and fans,” the WWE said. Haley was raised in Adelaide and got his start working “throughout Australia’s independent scene and wrestled for a number of local promotions, including International Wrestling Australia and Melbourne City Wrestling,” according to a report by Variety Australia.

Haley joined WWE in 2019 and made his first appearance on the league’s up-and-comers series “NXT.” He was released by WWE in 2021 but came back the following year and had remained active with the league until Friday.

Per Variety Australia, Haley “made his way into the main event scene in 2025 as part of The Vision faction, alongside Seth Rollins, fellow prospect Bron Breakker and arguably WWE’s greatest ever talker, Paul Heyman.”

In an interview with Rolling Stone AU/ZN last year, Haley said: “I think for me, a big change is when I got released from WWE and I came back, I sort of came back with a little bit of a chip on my shoulder, knowing that I need to be as confident as possible and show them I can do this. I’m supposed to be here,” according to Variety Australia.

He recently appeared at WWE’s Crown Jewel event in Saudi Arabia.
