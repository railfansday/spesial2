Meghan Markle was recently up for a big honor, thanks to one of her productions at Netflix. The Duchess of Sussex’s show, With Love, Meghan, was nominated for Outstanding Lifestyle Program at the 53rd Daytime Emmy Awards. The (non-televised) show took place this past Friday, October 30 in Los Angeles and, ultimately, Markle’s show didn’t win the award. Said honor was obtained by the dog-centric docuseries A Different Breed. Now, the Internet is sharing the same take on Markle’s series losing to one all about dogs.

With Love, Meghan – of which there are two seasons for Netflix subscription holders – sees Markle participation in beauty and home-based activities alongside special guests. Meanwhile, A Different Breed centers on dogs and their handlers as they compete in different athletic competitions. Upon learning that Markle’s show didn’t earn the gold trophy to Breed, a number of people took to X to share the same take about Markle losing the award to dogs. The loss itself isn’t the only topic of discussion that’s been circulating in regard to Markle’s nomination. Some commenters are also bringing up the Duchess’ absence from the awards show. As of this writing, an official reason for Markle – who recently moved back to the UK with her husband, Prince Harry, and their two kids – has not been given. Regardless, some users seem upset about Markle not showing up for the event.

Many have chastised the show for a few key details, including the presence of Le Creuset cookware, which was considered too fancy for the average kitchen.
