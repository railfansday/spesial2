Apple reveals pricing for Mac Studio with 512GB of RAM

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

When Apple announced the new M5 Max and M5 Ultra configurations of the Mac Studio in August, it said a configuration with 512GB of RAM would be available in October. Today, the company officially revealed the pricing of that option.

If you head to the Mac Studio configurator on Apple’s website, you’ll see the new 512GB upgrade option. As previously announced, the 512GB option is only available with the top-tier M5 Ultra chip that has a 36-core CPU and 80‑core GPU.

Who would have thought just a few years ago that upgrading from 96 GB of RAM to 512 GB of RAM would cost almost $16,000 US. Before LLMs really took off 512 GB of DDR5 ECC RAM could be had for about $4,000 US.

It’s a $15,500 upgrade over the base configuration of that processor, which has 96GB of unified memory.

To put it another way: you’ll pay $22,299 for the Mac Studio with the top-tier M5 Ultra, 512GB of unified memory, and 1TB of storage. You can spec it up all the way to 16TB of storage, which brings the total price to $29,799.00.

If you order a Mac Studio with 512GB of RAM, it’ll arrive in late November to early December.
