Confirmed 2. Bundesliga lineups: Hannover 96 vs. Dynamo Dresden

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Sandro Wagner makes three changes from the cup defeat to Kaiserslautern as he chases his first win. Leo Weinkauf, Stefan Teitur Thórdarson and Kolja Oudenne come in for Pascal Loretz, Franz Roggow and Maurice Neubauer.

Thomas Stamm has made three changes after the Pokal victory over Bundesliga side Schalke. Alexander Rossipal, Jakob Lemmer and Christoph Daferner replace Simon Straudi, Ben Bobzien and Vincent Vermeij.

Hannover 96 (3-4-3): Weinkauf; Ghiță, Tomiak, Hugonet; Sahiti, Hartel, Thórdarson, Oudenne; Matsuda, Källman, Chakroun

Dynamo Dresden (4-2-3-1): Grill; Sterner, Rapp, Müller, Rossipal; Raschl, Wagner; Lemmer, Hauptmann, Čović; Daferner
