3 Best Movies on Netflix This Week (Nov 16-20)

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Alessandro Carloni and Erica Rivinoja's new Cat in the Hat movie and writer/director Takashi Yamazaki's Godzilla Minus Zero have lost their spots at the top of the North American box office ranks, as an old face returned with his comeback to great success. Following his high-profile legal battle with his ex, Amber Heard, Johnny Depp stepped out of the Hollywood spotlight. Last weekend, he returned in Ti West’s Ebenezer, based on Charles Dickens' iconic 1843 novella. The film has shot to the top of the box office, earning $42 million in its opening weekend. With box office business taken care of, it's time to organize your streaming week. With that in mind, here's a list of three movies you should stream on Netflix.

For more recommendations, check out our list of the best shows and movies on Netflix.

Disclaimer: These titles are available on US Netflix.

One of the most exciting new arrivals on Netflix makes its splash this Tuesday, as Obsession bids to become the streamer's latest must-watch hit. Curry Barker's horror breakout stars Michael Johnston as Bear, who breaks the mysterious "One Wish Willow" and wishes for Nikki Freeman (Inde Navarrette) to fall madly in love with him. However, Bear's wish quickly backfires in catastrophic fashion.

A truly stunning, sub-$1 million budget horror film with its finger on the pulse of modern fears, Obsession also thrives as a dark comedy, something those familiar with director Barker won't be surprised about. Navarrette is the standout in this incredible debut, with a performance that was dubbed "one of the year's best" by Collider's Hannah Hunt in her review, and stands every chance of earning a Best Actress nomination for next year's Oscars.

Four franchises, four completely different ways of getting underneath your skin. This is the one built out of your particular fear, why it works so well on you, and what it would leave behind.

Your nightmare does not wait outside the door. It waits for you to close your eyes, which you are going to have to do eventually, because nobody has ever won that particular argument.

Your nightmare is not really the doll. It is the face of the person you tell, and the way they decide, very kindly, that you have imagined all of it.

Your nightmare has your number, knows your name, and has almost certainly been inside your house before. The worst part is that you already know them.

Your nightmare is not really about you. It is about the people asleep down the hall, and the feeling that whatever is here is not interested in leaving.

Samara Weaving has quietly had the best year of her career in 2026, with the Ready or Not sequel to the action comedy Over Your Dead Body standing as two of her most famous performances. But arguably her best came in Carolina Caroline, director Adam Rehmeier's crime flick about a young woman who goes on the run with a con man, as passion and chaos erupt in the search for her estranged mother.

Also starring Kyle Gallner and Kyra Sedgwick, this detailed, enticing crime tale will have you glued to the edge of your seat. The film was a hit with critics, earning a near-perfect 90% on Rotten Tomatoes, with Collider's Therese Lacson writing, "Rehmeier's take on an outlaw romance wrapped in a road movie is entirely too enjoyable to give too much hate to." One of the hidden gems on Netflix right now, don't miss out on Carolina Caroline.

If you're looking for something more family-friendly this week, then you might just find it In Your Dreams. The film follows siblings Stevie (Jolie Hoang-Rappaport) and Elliot (Elias Janssen), who dream of having the perfect family. They decide their only hope is to talk to the Sandman and begin a wild journey through the colorful wonder of their own dreams.

Called "one of the funniest and most heartwarming of the year" in Collider's official review, In Your Dreams is a delicate, animated gem that shouldn't pass you by. Imaginative and visually vibrant, this film has all the notes of an animated adventure of old, feeling both nostalgic yet full of fresh ideas. To keep every generation entertained this weekend, try In Your Dreams​​​​​​.
