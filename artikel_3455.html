‘New Girl’ Star’s New Detective Series Officially Sets Release Date With First Look

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

It's been more than eight years since New Girl ended its seven-season run on Fox. But now, one of its stars is returning to network TV for a new half-hour comedy that promises to be a classic mess-around. Sunset P.I. is set to premiere on NBC early in the New Year, and now we've got our first look at the detective comedy and its cast.

The series is set to premiere on February 8, 2027, at 8:30 p.m. ET on NBC; new episodes will then stream the next day on Peacock. It takes over the time slot from sophomore Tracy Morgan-Daniel Radcliffe comedy The Fall and Rise of Reggie Dinkins, which will conclude its second season at the midseason point. It's one of two detective series on NBC's midseason schedule; a reboot of the classic James Garner vehicle The Rockford Files will kick off on January 2, with David Boreanaz stepping into the shoes of Jim Rockford.

Sunset P.I. is a workplace comedy; it stars onetime Nick Miller Jake Johnson as Mickey Wilder, a lovelorn Los Angeles private dick who works for the detective agency of the title. It's run by Julius Royal (Keith David, The Lowdown), an old-school gumshoe who's somewhat stuck in the past. Wilder works alongside Royal's lovable son, Justin (Langston Kerman, The Boys); quick-witted investigator Faye (Jane Levy, Evil Dead), and deeply disinterested receptionist Raya (Mary Shalaby). As its logline states, the show is "about a proud tradition of Los Angeles private eyes that began with Philip Marlowe and will end with this show." The single-camera comedy series' first season is slated to run ten episodes.

A great detective is really just a personality with a method attached, which is why we keep remaking the same four of them. Here is yours, what it says about you, and the case for them being the best in the business.

You let people think you are not a threat and then you simply never go away. Columbo is the only major detective show that tells you who did it in the first ten minutes, because the pleasure was never the puzzle, it was watching a rich man slowly realise he is in trouble.

You are the most interesting person in the room and you know exactly how to use it. Rian Johnson built Blanc as a gentleman detective who is also an outsider everywhere he goes, which lets him be charming at people who would never otherwise talk to him.

Everybody tells you everything, and you have never once asked them to. Jessica Fletcher is a crime novelist from a small town in Maine who is so warm and so obviously trustworthy that suspects confess to her essentially as a social courtesy.

You expect to be lied to and you are almost never disappointed. Chandler wrote Marlowe as a man with a rigid private code in a city that has none, which is why he takes the case, solves it, gets nothing out of it and takes the next one.

Sunset P.I. is created, written, and executive produced by Dan Goor and Luke Del Tredici. Both are longtime sitcom veterans; Goor co-creating Brooklyn Nine-Nine, which Del Tredici served as a writer and producer on. Del Tredici is no stranger to detective comedy, either, having produced the HBO series Bored to Death. It is executive produced by Akiva Schaffer (The Naked Gun), who will also direct its pilot episode.
