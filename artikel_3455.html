Tom Hardy’s 120-Minute Sci-Fi Masterpiece Officially Resurfaces on Streaming

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Tom Hardy has given his fans a range of brilliant roles. He’s played iconic superhero characters like Eddie Brock in Venom, Bane in The Dark Knight trilogy, real-life characters like the Kray brothers in Legends, the titular notorious criminal in Bronson, but mostly he’s loved for his action, like Inception, Havoc, Dunkirk, Rock n Rolla, and more. Nonetheless, his most beloved character remains Max in Mad Max: Fury Road.

Directed by George Miller, the feature went through a lot of behind-the-scenes trouble but is often considered the best in the genre. Co-written with Brendan McCarthy and Nick Lathouris, the film had a distinct treatment from its predecessors. Miller’s directorial vision set a new standard for the action genre as Fury Road is often counted among the greatest sci-fi movies. We follow Furiosa (Charlize Theron) living in the wasteland where a tyrant rules over a stark desert, controlling every drop of water. Rebel Max (Hardy) teams up with Furiosa — one escaping grief and the other reaching out to her childhood — as the last hope for the ones longing for a new life. With its brilliant craftsmanship and widely relatable themes of oppression, isolation, and home, the movie proved a massive hit, earning over $380 million worldwide, gaining universal acclaim for its action, visuals, and technical proficiency.

Along with compelling characters, the movie is hailed for its technical proficiency, displayed in breathtaking stunt work, use of practical effects over CGI, background music, editing, and more; it garnered 10 Oscar nominations overall. The love for the movie can also be seen in its Rotten Tomatoes score: 97% from critics and 86% from fans. Despite the reported behind-the-scenes drama, both Theron and Hardy give impeccable performances with an equally impressive supporting cast, including Zoë Kravitz, Nicholas Hoult, and legend Hugh Keays-Byrne as the antagonist Immortan Joe.

Four worlds and four completely different ideas of what a good life looks like. This is where you belong, what it would ask of you, and what you would get back in return.

You belong somewhere old enough that the mountains have names and somebody can still tell you who gave them. Middle-earth is for people who think the right thing is worth doing even when it will not be finished in their lifetime.

You want the sea, a crew, and absolutely nobody telling you where you are allowed to go. The Grand Line is the world for people whose main ambition in life is finding out what is over there.

You would rather be feared and useful than liked and useless. The Continent is a world where the monsters are the straightforward part of the job and the people are where it all gets complicated.

You want the myths to be real and loose in the actual world, and you want to find out at about thirteen that you were never anywhere near as ordinary as everybody kept telling you.

Mad Max: Fury Road has fans' attention again, as it rises on Apple TV’s top 10 global list, as per FlixPatrol. It’s fans’ love for the feature that keeps pulling them back to the franchise. A second installment in the franchise, Mad Max: The Wasteland, is long due, which would take us back to a broken Max in the wasteland. In 2024, Miller returned to making a new movie in the franchise, Furiosa: A Mad Max Saga, starring Anya Taylor-Joy as a young Furiosa and Chris Hemsworth as her tormentor, Dementus. Despite a brilliant cast, Miller’s vision, and a return to its brand of practical action, the movie failed to impress audiences, putting an end to all the possible future installments, for the moment.
