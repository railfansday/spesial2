Danantara Reports $634 Billion in Assets in First Audited Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Indonesian state investment agency Danantara reported consolidated assets of 11,607 trillion rupiah ($634.2 billion) at the end of 2025 in its first audited financial statements, falling short of the roughly $1 trillion figure officials have often cited for the state-company group at the center of President Prabowo Subianto’s economic agenda.
