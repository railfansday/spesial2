Leclerc Denies Ferrari Contract Clause – Doesn’t Like People Questioning His Integrity

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Charles Leclerc has played down clauses in his F1 contract, amid reports of #1 status, as he agrees that on-track troubles with Lewis Hamilton need to stop.

Ever since the on-track clashes between Hamilton and Leclerc began, reports have claimed that the Monégasque has a special clause about being the #1 driver. Being a Ferrari junior, he has earned the privileges.

But Leclerc categorically denied having any clause on his F1 contract. "I normally never disclose what I have in my contract, but I think this one is an important one to speak about, and I absolutely have zero clause that says that in my contract," he said in Thursday's Qatar GP press conference.

"It's never something that I was interested in and never something that I personally wanted. So, no, I don't." Leclerc doesn't like people questioning his integrity and his love for Ferrari, considering he has been part of the family.

Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc at the Las Vegas GP. Image Credit: Scuderia Ferrari

There have been mistakes, but the questions raised about him hurt. "It's part of our job; I think the only thing that I don't like seeing is people questioning my integrity to the team, my love for the team," said Leclerc. "I've been in Ferrari since 2019.

"I think there's nothing clearer than the love I have for the team, and I will do absolutely everything in order to bring Ferrari back to the top. Then, of course, mistakes happen along the way and always will. We just try to minimize them, and particularly between teammates.

"But yeah, I think it's more the question mark of that I have seen going around that hurts me more than anything else. The rest is a lot of noise, and it's not the most enjoyable time, but it's part of the game and I have to accept that," summed up Leclerc.

Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc on stage in Las Vegas. Image Credit: Scuderia Ferrari

The Monégasque has a long-term contract until the 2028 F1 season, with Lewis Hamilton still to sign an extension. But Leclerc doesn't think that long-term or short-term contracts play any role. The only goal is to do well for Ferrari collectively.

He doesn't want things to be repeated, as they are working together to find solutions after another meeting in Qatar. "I mean, I don't think too much about it in a way, just because it's in both our interests," said Leclerc. "I mean, whatever, I think it's one year, at least one year more together.

"We've got to do the best for the team, so I think I've been very much thinking about trying to find a solution together with the team in order to make sure that we maximise the team points and that episodes like last week don't happen again. That is my main priority. I don't think that these other things, I mean, I didn't think about it too much, to be honest, and I don't think it's part of the thoughts of anybody that is trying to find the solution.

"So yes, I don't think it is of importance right now. I think we're just very much focused on trying to find a solution that works, and particularly because I really believe we are in a good momentum and if next year we are to be in a position of fighting for the championship, it's even more so important to find the right solution for both of us to bring Ferrari back to the top," summed up Leclerc.
