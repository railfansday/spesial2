Sunderland Are Showing Interest In This Juventus Defender: What Will He Add To Le Bris’ Side?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

In a recent report, Gazzetta dello Sport mentioned that Sunderland are showing interest in Juventus defender Lloyd Kelly. It has been claimed that the Black Cats are eyeing a move to bring the English talent to the Stadium of Light next year.

Kelly has struggled to impress after joining the Turin club earlier this summer. The 28-year-old has only featured in eight matches for Juve this season, but is still waiting to score or assist his first goal for them.

The English sensation has a wealth of experience in the Premier League from his time at Bournemouth and Newcastle United. Hence, it is no wonder that Sunderland are hoping to bring him back to England next year.

His current contract at the Allianz Stadium will run out in the summer of 2029, which could make it difficult for the Black Cats to land him on a cut-price deal this January or at the end of this campaign.

Kelly is a good tackler of the ball and can sense the danger well to earn possession back for his team inside his half. He usually puts his foot through the ball when required and is an accurate passer as well from the back.

The English talent can even use his strong 1.89m frame to win a few 50-50 aerial duels for his side. Kelly mainly functions as a centre-back but can also fill in as a left-back if told to do so. He is already quite familiar with the Premier League and won't take any time to adjust to life at Sunderland.

We can expect Kelly to add more bite and steel to Regis Le Bris' defence. He has what it takes to fight for a regular first-team spot at the Stadium of Light.

At 28, Kelly has his peak years ahead of him, which makes him a good option for the Black Cats to pursue this January or at the end of this campaign. He might even help the Wearisde club compete across all fronts over the next few years.
