LSU fans threw drinks at Arch Manning as Texas ended 75-year losing streak with comeback win

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Texas Longhorns QB Arch Manning did not get a nice reception from LSU Tigers fans after his first foray into their end zone during a historic 22-21 win.

The last time the Longhorns had beaten LSU on the road in Baton Rouge was in 1952, with Steve Sarkisian and company overturning 75 years of precedent by defeating the Tigers with an incredible second-half comeback on November 14, 2026.

The man to seal the victory for Texas was QB Arch Manning, who threw the game-winning TD with less than 4 seconds left on the clock to steal this win and end LSU's hopes for the college football playoffs.

During the first quarter of the game after the Longhorns took a 7-0 lead in the game, ESPN caught fans throwing their drinks at Manning on camera, although they seemed to miss the 22-year-old QB.

Tigers fans probably changed their opinion over the game multiple times, as they first saw Manning throw three TDs and somehow get outplayed by their own struggling QB, Sam Leavitt, until his game-winning throw firmly re-established LSU's dislike for him.

Manning ended the game with an unimpressive statline of 19/36 on pass attempts for 174 yards, one TD, and three INTs. However, his one TD was more impactful than anything else Leavitt or anyone else on the Tigers managed, as it was the game-defining moment.

The Longhorns' hopes of making the college football playoffs still remain high while the Tigers are virtually guaranteed to not earn a call-up to the dance after this marked their fourth loss of the season.

Manning's inconsistency has not allowed him to emerge as the bona fide No. 1 QB in his class or even a contender for the Heisman award, but his ability to stay resolute and force comebacks during bad performances highlight why opposing fans can grow to hate him.
