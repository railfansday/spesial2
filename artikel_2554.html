Confirmed 2. Bundesliga lineups: FC St. Pauli vs. 1. FC Nürnberg

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Marcel Rapp makes one change from the 0-0 draw with Darmstadt as Branimir Hrgota comes in for Youssoupha Niang.

Miroslav Klose has made three changes after Nürnberg’s 5-2 defeat away to Leverkusen in the cup during the week. Eric Porstner, Rayan Ghrieb and Piet Scobel replace Andrea Ceresoli, Sigurd Haugen and William Bøving.

FC St. Pauli (3-4-1-2): Voll; Ritzka, Smith, Nemeth; Pyrka, Rasmussen, Fujita, Oppie; Metcalfe; Hrgota, Ceesay

1. FC Nürnberg (4-2-3-1): Reichert; Janisch, Kalogeropolous, Otto, Porstner; Markhiyev, Fernandez; Ghrieb, Justvan, Lubach; Scobel
