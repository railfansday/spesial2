Half of Financial Institutions Say New Technology Rollouts Have Caused Problems for Clients, New Go-Live Faster Study Finds

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Research conducted by Datos Insights reveals two-thirds of financial institutions missed expected implementation deadlines and 46% went over budget, while just 3% have fully implemented AI in testing

WILMINGTON, Del., Nov. 3, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Go-Live Faster released the results of a new study examining how U.S. banks and credit unions execute and test commercial banking, payments and treasury management modernization projects. According to the findings, half (51%) of U.S. financial institutions surveyed say new implementations in the past 12 months have sometimes or often caused negative client-impacting issues after launch. Two-thirds (66%) missed expected deadlines on recent implementation projects, and 46% went over budget on their most recent implementation.

Datos Insights, on behalf of Go-Live Faster, surveyed 61 U.S. banks and credit unions with $5 billion to $100 billion in assets. All respondents had completed or planned a commercial digital banking platform or payment hub replacement or modernization within the past three to four years. The research found that while investing in the right technology is critical, a successful implementation and rollout is equally important, and that poorly executed implementations have negatively impacted client experience, with 18% of financial institutions experiencing above-normal client attrition because of their migration.

Additional key findings from the report include:

"Most financial institutions have only a few major implementations a year," said Mukesh Mulchandani, co-founder and CEO at Go-Live Faster. "Those projects are usually led by employees on top of their daily work, which is one reason timelines slip. External partners can relieve that pressure, and institutions can set themselves up for success by validating data early, automating testing and measuring results by how clients fare after launch."
