Jalen Green Criticizes Officiating And Rockets’ Flopping But Says Suns Needed The Reality Check

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Jalen Green received a warm welcome back to Houston, the city where he ended up getting drafted, as the Rockets faced the Suns tonight in the NBA Cup. The Rockets ended up defeating the Suns 116-104 on a historic night for Kevin Durant and Alperen Sengun as well.

The former Rockets player struggled to find his rhythm tonight and ended the game with 16 points, five assists, and four rebounds while shooting 8-21 from the field (38.1% FG) and missing all four of his three-point shot attempts.

While reflecting on the loss after the game, Jalen Green spoke to the Suns' media in the locker room scrum, where he called out the officials for seeming to fall for the Rockets' flopping consistently throughout the night.

"That was the one that we really needed overall. A top team in the West. We've got to be able to come out and play aggressive. Had some lapses on the offensive side," Jalen Green said.

"But overall, the refs were calling all these flops and things like that, kind of f–ked the game up… All that flopping needs to be called; it's what's been happening. But they didn't call it flopping even one time," Green added.

Even the Suns' player Oso Ighodaro, who fouled out of tonight's game late after chucking Alperen Sengun, also echoed the same sentiment as Jalen Green.

"That's how they called it from the jump," Ighodaro said about the physicality he expected from the Rockets. "Umm. Yeah. Refs were calling a lot of stuff, a lot of flopping. Just got to be smarter."

Hence, Jalen Green and the Suns players seem to commonly believe that officiating partly spoiled not just Green's homecoming game, but also their hopes of proceeding ahead in the NBA Cup. But Green did not hold the loss to his heart and greeted all his former teammates after the final buzzer.

"That's love. I was here for four years. The fans have always been great to me. Houston always got a special place in my heart," Green concluded with a message for his former team, showing him love with the introduction video tonight.

The Suns have fallen to 9-8 for the season and will now face the Mavericks on Friday, November 27. They have ended this five-game road trip with a 4-1 record before heading home now.

Meanwhile, the Rockets have improved to 14-5, and they now face the 76ers on Saturday, November 28. These two sides will face each other once again on February 5 in Phoenix.

One can certainly chalk it up as one of the most intense matchups to look forward to in the coming season. It will be interesting to see how Jalen Green hopes to get his revenge on his former team in that game for tonight's loss.
