Game Of Thrones: Devoted Light Officially Announced

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Although the Starks, Lannisters, and Targaryens tend to dominate the conversation surrounding Game of Thrones, House Hightower is arguably one of the most interesting families in George R.R. Martin's A Song of Ice and Fire universe. The Hightowers are among the oldest and wealthiest families in Westeros, having ruled over Oldtown for thousands of years and played an instrumental role in establishing the Citadel.

While the Hightowers had a relatively limited presence in HBO's original Game of Thrones series, with Ser Gerold Hightower briefly appearing in Season 6, the family finally got its time in the spotlight in House of the Dragon. Both Alicent and Otto Hightower have become synonymous with the prequel's political intrigue, with their family's ambitions sitting at the heart of the Dance of the Dragons.

For fans eager to see more of the Hightowers outside HBO's acclaimed fantasy series, one of the family's most iconic characters has officially returned to the franchise with Devoted Light.

Alicent Hightower has officially returned to the Game of Thrones franchise as a new Commander in Dragonfire, the free-to-play mobile strategy game inspired by HBO's House of the Dragon. Officially detailed on November 24, Alicent Hightower: Devoted Light depicts the character in her younger years, when her friendship with Rhaenyra had yet to give way to one of the most consequential rivalries in Westerosi history.

As the official Dragonfire character description reads:

Gentle in manner but keenly observant, Alicent understands how little freedom is granted to daughters of great houses. Her affection for Rhaenyra is true, yet it is tested by a father’s ambitions and a court where obedience may carry a heavier price than defiance.

The recently introduced Commander system is a game-changer for Dragonfire, giving players way more freedom when assembling their armies. With eight distinct Dragon Traits and Commanders who specialize in different combat strategies, dragons that players previously overlooked are suddenly indispensable.

Perhaps most importantly, Commanders and their upgrades carry over between reigns, giving players another meaningful avenue for permanent progression. With Rhaenyra, Daemon, Criston Cole, and Corlys Velaryon also among the initial wave of Commanders, the possibilities for future additions from the extended Game of Thrones universe are particularly exciting.
