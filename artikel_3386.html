All 7 Episodes Of HBO's Best Thriller Of The Decade Are Perfect From Start To Finish

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

One HBO miniseries with an A-list cast managed to blend character study with murder mystery plotting for a story that has not got a single bad episode. The longer a show runs for, the harder it is to maintain a perfect streak with no bad episodes. For a miniseries like the iconic historical drama I, Claudius or the more recent HBO masterpiece Chernobyl, this is sometimes achievable.

However, for a show that follows the murder mystery format, it is a lot harder to pull off this rare achievement. Traditional murder mystery shows like Murder She Wrote and Columbo introduce an entire new mystery in each outing, so some of these plots are inevitably duds. However, even modern, more mature and darker murder mysteries, like True Detective or Prime Video’s hit Bosch, struggle with nailing every single episode.

When a murder mystery is stretched out across a single season, this can theoretically give the show’s writers time to explore their main characters as well as simultaneously solve the mystery. However, this also often results in filler episodes as the show kills time before revealing its next twist. Fortunately, 2021’s HBO hit Mare of Easttown managed to make its character elements interesting enough to keep every episode engaging right through to its unpredictable finale.

Grim and brutal but suffused with tragic humanity, Mare of Easttown mixes the character-centric crime drama of shows like The Wire with the murder mystery storytelling of hits like The Killing. Mare of Easttown stars Kate Winslet as the eponymous “Mare,” Detective Sergeant Marianne Sheehan. Something of a hometown hero thanks to her high school basketball career, Mare has more recently been seen as a professional and personal failure.

Mare’s inability to solve the disappearance of a local girl has led some civilians to call her detective status into question, and her case is not helped by her messy personal life. Mare is ensnared in a nasty custody battle with the girlfriend of her late son, so she is failing to solve the murder of a local teen mother while trying to keep these struggles under wraps at work.

While the starry cast of Apple TV’s Colin Farrell detective drama Sugar proves A-list actors are no longer a rare sight on the small screen, Mare of Easttown still boasts a historically great lineup. As well as Winslet’s transformative central performance, the supporting cast includes Evan Peters, John Douglas Thompson, Angourie Rice, Jean Smart, Guy Pearce, Sosie Bacon, and Cailee Spaeny.

This astounding lineup helps make Mare of Easttown an all-time great psychological thriller, as the show always has an enthralling character to explore in every subplot. What might be filler episodes in a lesser show become vital clues to a larger puzzle as Mare of Easttown implicates the entire titular town in both Mare’s unsolved disappearance and the local mother’s murder.

While this focus on community might call to mind ABC’s seminal murder mystery Twin Peaks, Mare of Easttown has something much more grounded on its mind than creators Mark Frost and David Lynch’s infamously trippy whodunit. A deeply sympathetic and human piece of work, Mare of Easttown is more interested in the tragic human failings of its antiheroes than monstrous serial killers or existential battles between good and evil.

In the grey, dreary suburban setting of Mare of Easttown, everyone is struggling to get by and everyone runs the risk of falling between the cracks, most of all the show’s deeply flawed but human title character herself. While this makes for a tough watch, Mare of Easttown’s empathetic outlook also ensures the HBO show’s seven-episode story doesn’t have a dull moment from start to finish.
