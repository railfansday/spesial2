The Bills have a new path to the one seed after the Bengals’ loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Buffalo Bills took care of business against an underdog on Sunday, beating the Miami Dolphins. The Jacksonville Jaguars and Cincinnati Bengals both lost to underdogs from the NFC East on Sunday and Monday, which paves the way for the Bills to clinch the number one seed in a new scenario.

Yes, with seven games left it is premature to talk about clinching scenarios, but the Bills have a four-game lead in the AFC East plus two games left against the Jets and Dolphins. The division crown is heavily tilted toward the Bills now, and we are starting to look past that to the one seed. (The Bills are obvously focused one game at a time.)

With the Jaguars and Bengals losing this week, the Bills now can win the rest of their AFC games and secure the overall record and the AFC record they would need to clinch the one seed, a status that has eluded them throughout Josh Allen's entire career.

Buffalo has games against the Kansas City Chiefs, Denver Broncos, and New England Patriots plus the Dolphins and Jets. Winning those five AFC contests gets them to 14 wins and an 11-1 AFC record with head-to-head tiebreakers over the Broncos and Chiefs. Denver and KC would both have three losses on account of losing to Buffalo. Meanwhile the Jaguars and Bengals could top out at 14-3 but have two AFC losses apiece. Even in a three-way tie scenario, the Bills would come out on top.

In short, if the Bills win the rest of their AFC games, they can lose both games to the NFC North's Chicago Bears and Green Bay Packers and still be the one seed in the AFC. (Winning those is preferable, but irrelevant here.)

If the Bills stumble against an AFC opponent, obviously things will need to be recalibrated. Regardless, the Bengals' loss on Monday is a win for the Bills.
