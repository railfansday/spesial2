Aaron Rodgers’ Super Bowl-Winning Ex-Packers Teammate Drops 1-Word Message After Being Blindsided By Steelers’ Stunning Decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

In what might be the end of his legendary career, the Pittsburgh Steelers announced the shock benching of four-time NFL MVP Aaron Rodgers on Tuesday night ahead of their Week 12 clash against the Denver Broncos.

Mason Rudolph will take over the starting job for Friday's game against Denver, which feels like a stunning turn of events, especially with Mike McCarthy as the head coach.

For a team in the thick of the playoff race, it was a move that came out of nowhere, and it opens the door for a ton of speculation about the exact reason why. A former teammate of Rodgers, who won the Super Bowl with him, was among those who were left shocked by the news.

The Steelers announced the decision late Tuesday night, confirming that Rudolph would start against Denver while Rodgers would be inactive.

The timing made the move even more surprising. Pittsburgh had just lost consecutive games to the Cincinnati Bengals and Philadelphia Eagles, but McCarthy had publicly backed his former Packers quarterback as recently as Monday.

Even Greg Jennings, who shared a Super Bowl-winning locker room with Rodgers in Green Bay, seemed to have little to say for himself when the news broke. The former Packers wide receiver needed just one word to express his surprise.

Jennings, now an analyst on FS1's 'First Things First', reacted with "Wow." That was all the former wide receiver could muster.

The two-time Pro Bowler and Packers Hall of Fame inductee was an icon of the franchise and a key reason for their Super Bowl 45 victory. As Rodgers walked away with the Super Bowl MVP, Jennings' contribution of 64 yards and two touchdowns was invaluable on the night.

But over the years, the star wide receiver has become critical of some of Rodgers's commentary and antics, alongside the declining play on the field. The four-time MVP has been on a downward trend since his final year with the Packers.

Rodgers currently ranks 29th in PFN's Quarterback Impact Score with a career-worst grade of 67.1. In four of his last five games, the Steelers QB recorded an impact score in the 60s. All things ultimately ended with him being benched.

Rodgers' PFN QB Impact Score has fallen from 74.4 in his final Packers season in 2022 to 72.5 in his full season with the Jets in 2024, 71.9 with Pittsburgh last year, and a career-low 67.1 this season.

After previously announcing that this was going to be his final season in the NFL, it will be interesting to see if the benching makes him change course. But as things currently stand, there is a chance that Rodgers has played the final game of his career.
