Army OC gets first head coaching gig at alma mater

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Army OC gets first head coaching gig at alma mater appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The college football carousel is getting underway. At the FBS level, six coaches have already been fired, while multiple college head coaches are on the hot seat. Meanwhile, at the FCS level, hires are already being made. Wofford is set to hire former James Madison and East Carolina coach Mike Houston as their next head coach.

Now, Furman has made a move on their open coaching job, according to Pete Thamel of ESPN.

"Furman is targeting Army OC Cody Worley as the school's next head coach. A deal is expected to come together in the near future. Worley is a former quarterback at Furman and has worked at Army for seven years, three as the offensive coordinator," Thamel posted on X, formerly Twitter.

Cody Worley was the quarterback at Furman from 2007 through 2010. This is the second straight time Furman has hired a former player from a service academy to be the head coach.

Clay Hendrix is the current head coach at Furman. He played at Furman as an offensive guard from 1982 through 1985. He then was a graduate assistant at NC State for two seasons before returning to Furman. The coach was the offensive line coach at the school from 1988 through 2007. Hendrix then became the offensive coordinator at Air Force in 2007. He was also the assistant head coach and the offensive line coach, working at Air Force from 2007 through 2016.

Hendrix is 63-51 as the head coach at Furman, leading them to the playoffs four times and winning two conference titles. He announced at the start ofthe season that he would be retiring. Furman has one game left in the season, visiting VMI.

"Worley spent 6 seasons at Kennesaw prior going to Army. He was the OC on Army's 2024 AAC title team, which led the nation in rushing offense and was second in passing yards per completion (17.09). Army is No. 2 in rushing this year. Worley will coach through the Army-Navy game," Thamel added.

Worley also went straight into coaching after his playing career at Furman ended. He coached at The Citadel for three seasons before six years at Kennesaw State. He has spent the last six seasons coaching at Army.
