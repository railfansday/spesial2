OSSAA schools approve recruiting and summer participation rule changes

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Oklahoma Secondary School Activities Association member schools approved two rule changes that address summer participation and recruiting violations, the OSSAA announced Wednesday.

The votes included revisions to Rule 7 and Rule 9.

The change to Rule 7 will take effect at the end of the school year and has to do with summer participation.

It is essentially in place to prevent athletes from scouting a program. Now, participating in summer activities will play a role in determining whether an athlete is tied to that school. If they are, their ability to transfer will be limited.

Certain things — such as weightlifting, strength and conditioning, individual skill camps and spring football — will not count as participation.

The Rule 9 change will take effect on July 1 and is related to the recruitment of athletes.

A committee will now review complaints and hold hearings to determine if violations have occurred.

Coaches who are guilty of recruiting violations will be suspended for 365 days, and athletic programs will be given a one-year warning. Repeated violations for a program will result in more consequences.

"Our dedicated Rule Review Committee has expedited a lot of positive changes," OSSAA executive director David Jackson said in a release. "It's yet another avenue in which we're having in-depth conversations with school leaders to ensure OSSAA is serving students to the best of its ability and setting us up to be more adaptable for years to come."
