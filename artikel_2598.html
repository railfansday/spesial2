Magic Johnson wants to see a Spurs-Lakers rematch in Western Conference Finals

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Magic Johnson wants to see a Spurs-Lakers rematch in Western Conference Finals appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Los Angeles Lakers and San Antonio Spurs delivered one of the best games of the season thus far to this point. It looked like the Lakers were going to pull away in the first half and even pushed their lead up to 20 points at one point. But the Spurs aren't the reigning Western Conference champion for nothing. They stormed back with an excellent two-way performance in the third quarter, and they outlasted the Lakers in a heavyweight bout in the fourth, grabbing a 99-96 victory to advance to the knockout stage.

The Lakers' NBA Cup journey came to an end with this defeat; had they won, they would have advanced to the knockout stage (assuming the Portland Trail Blazers still defeat the Golden State Warriors the way they did on Friday) as the top team in Group C.

The Lakers and Spurs have been the two best teams in the NBA in the early goings of this campaign, and it won't be until February 6, 2027 when these two will square off against each other once again. But for Lakers legend Magic Johnson, he hopes to at least see these two advance deep enough in the playoffs to give fans a seven-game series.

"What a fantastic game between the two best teams in the Western Conference. I can see both of these teams in the Western Conference Finals this summer! It was truly a game of two halves: the Lakers dominated the first half, and the Spurs took over in the second," Johnson wrote on his official account on X.

The Spurs' offense picked up in the second half; they made just two of their 12 attempts from deep in the first half, and some positive regression brought them back to the game. But in the end, Victor Wembanyama and Stephon Castle were just too good for the Lakers to handle.

Wembanyama finished the game with 25 points and 16 boards, while Castle had 26 points on 10-of-15 shooting from the field while adding nine dimes for good measure.
