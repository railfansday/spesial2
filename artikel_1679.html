Chelsea boss Alonso reveals Fofana apology after penalty joke

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Xabi Alonso revealed Wesley Fofana has apologised to his Chelsea team-mates for giving an interview in which he appeared to joke about his penalty miss against Liverpool.

Fofana missed the decisive spot-kick as the Blues were beaten 6-5 on spot-kicks at Anfield in the round of 16 in the EFL Cup, at the end of October.

The centre-back blazed his effort high into the stands, and he subsequently told YouTube personality Zack Nani: "I knew I wasn't a good penalty taker to begin with!"

Those comments attracted fierce criticism from sections of Chelsea's fanbase, particularly with Alonso's team sitting eighth in the Premier League following back-to-back defeats against Sunderland and Leeds United.

But ahead of Friday's trip to Nottingham Forest, the Blues boss insisted the matter was now considered closed.

Asked if he had spoken to Fofana about the interview, Alonso said: "Absolutely. We have addressed that with him and, for sure, it was unfortunate.

"He has apologised. He knows it was not right in the moment, it was not right, the content.

"We are here to take things seriously and he has said it is not going to be repeated, and we know that it is something we don't want, these kinds of things.

"They are not going to be repeated, for sure."

Since the start of March, no side has lost more Premier League games than Chelsea (13), and among ever-present clubs, only Crystal Palace (17) have earned fewer points than the Blues in that same span (23).

This season, Chelsea's Premier League games have seen more goals than those containing any other side (40 – 20 for, 20 against).

This is the earliest point in a league campaign (11 games) where the Blues have both scored and conceded 20+ goals since 1960-61 (eight games).

Asked what he put Chelsea's struggles down to, Alonso said: "There are many reasons. Football reasons, things that we can't afford to do.

"The basics in football are so important to compete at this level. If you don't have those, you play as we did.

"The idea and the goal and the work and the talks have all been focused on that direction, to show a good game and to compete much better tomorrow."

Chelsea are unbeaten in their last five away league games against Forest (W4 D1), with their current run of three consecutive victories being their best ever at the City Ground.
