Dakota Johnson to Star and Produce Relationship Dramedy ‘Tell Me Everything’ for Lionsgate

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Lionsgate won rights to the project, penned by Gaelyn Golde, in a competitive spec sale

Dakota Johnson is set to star in and produce the relationship-dramedy “Tell Me Everything” for Lionsgate.

The studio landed rights to the hot title in a competitive spec sale with multiple preemptive offers. Gaelyn Golde (Netflix’s “Saturn Return,” Hulu’s “Chad Powers”) penned the script and will executive produce the film.

In “Tell Me Everything,” Johnson plays a cynical divorce attorney “who no longer believes in love,” who encounters a celebrity couples therapist “with marital troubles of her own.” As the logline explains, “Their unexpected bond causes both to question everything they know about love in this modern tale about marriage, commitment, and relationships.” Related Stories Two Directors, One Oscar: Can Lord and Miller, Los Javis, Fielder and Oppenheim or the Esiri Brothers Break Through? In addition to starring in the film, Johnson will also produce the project alongside Ro Donnelly and Samantha Racanelli will produce for TeaTime Pictures. Since launching TeaTime Pictures in 2019, Johnson has produced and starred in “Daddio,” Sundance audience award winner “Cha Cha Real Smooth,” “Am I OK?” and the upcoming “The Three Incestuous Sisters.” She most recently starred in Amazon’s “Verity”; Neon’s “Splitsville,” which she also produced; and Celine Song’s “Materialists” for A24. Johnson and Tea Time Pictures are repped by CAA and Johnson Shapiro Slewett Kole. Johnson is also represented by Untitled.

Golde most recently wrote and executive produced Netflix’s upcoming film “Saturn Return,” starring Rachel Brosnahan, Charles Melton and Will Poulter. The film is directed by Academy Award nominee Greg Kwedar, with Plan B producing. Golde is also developing a feature at Netflix with Kelly Fremon Craig producing. Her TV resume is highlighted by serving as a writer and producer on both seasons of Hulu’s “Chad Powers,” co-created by Michael Waldron and series star Glen Powell. Golde is repped by CAA and Ginsburg Daniels Kallis.

The deals were negotiated by Dan Freedman on behalf of the studio. Chelsea Kujawa and Maria Ascanio will oversee the project for Lionsgate.

Deadline first reported news of the upcoming project.
