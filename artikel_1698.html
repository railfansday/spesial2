Juventus youngster Ekhator to feature against Omonia

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Luciano Spalletti revealed youngster Jeff Ekhator will feature in Juventus' Europa League clash with Omonia Nicosia as they hope to have "an extra solution moving forward".

Ekhator joined Juve from Genoa in the summer, but missed the start of the season through injury.

The 20-year-old made his competitive debut off the bench in Juve's Europa League win over AZ, while he also made a substitute appearance in their Serie A draw with Fiorentina before the international break.

Ekhator, who scored for Italy's Under-21s during that break, was an unused substitute against Venezia last weekend, but will play on Thursday as Spalletti looks to add another attacking option alongside Randal Kolo Muani and Nick Woltemade.

"We have a lot of top-quality players, and they're all good. We also need to develop some young players," said Spalletti.

"We'll play Ekhator because we want an extra solution moving forwards. We will make rotations that we trust in. We're aware of what is at stake.

"I think he's a physical striker who attacks the space well. He's a player who makes bad balls playable.

"[Italy U21 boss Silvio] Baldini speaks very highly of him, and Baldini is one of my close friends within football. I'm getting to know him a bit better now, and I agree with Baldini.

"I expect him to be calm, but he's a player with good prospects, and all great players started out as a talent with good prospects before reaching that level."

Juve have won all four of their previous European meetings with Omonia, beating them twice in the 1977-78 European Cup and twice in the 1999-00 UEFA Cup. They also have a 100% win ratio against Cypriot sides, winning seven out of seven games.

The Bianconeri have never lost a home match in the Europa League (W6 D9, since 2009-10), with only Benfica playing more home games without losing (31).
