Falcons get high marks for Jessie Bates III trade with Jaguars

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The NFL trade deadline doesn't arrive until tomorrow afternoon, but the Atlanta Falcons have already struck a big deal to kick off the festivities. Today we learned that they have sent safety Jessie Bates III to the Jacksonville Jaguars, getting a second and a fifth-round pick in return.

While Atlanta's defense will take a significant hit without Bates, they are getting a damn-good return for half a season.

For their efforts, the Falcons front office has earned a B+ grade from USA TODAY for the trade. Meanwhile, the Jaguars only got a C+.

"Kudos to Matt Ryan and Ian Cunningham for not stubbornly barreling ahead with a roster that remains firmly in the mix for the NFC South crown... But Atlanta's new leadership clearly understands the scope of the reset it's staring down. Getting such a rich return for a player who likely wasn't in the franchise's long-term plans is nothing short of laudable." - Michael Middlehurst-Schwartz, USA TODAY

The second-round pick in 2027 is a surprisingly valuable gem and will help improve their draft return after a dismal haul in 2026 that did not include a first-rounder. The fifth in 2028 is just an added bonus.

It's always tough to swallow losing a playmaker of Bates' caliber, but it's probably the correct move to sell now and think about the future - which still looks pretty bright despite what we've seen the last two weeks on the field.

The Falcons have a strong young core - and with the right moves they could have a championship window opening up - provided that Michael Penix Jr. can continue improving his game in the coming years. Acquiring cap space and draft picks to build up the supporting cast is the right call.
