A Retractable Makeup Pen That Does More Than One Job

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

ELLE editors think Alleyoop’s innovative product is a game changer.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Growing up, I had exactly one prized possession: a gel retractable multi-pen. To my 10-year-old brain, it was a marvel. I could write one word in sparkly sky blue, then the next in lime green, and the following in hot pink. If I wanted to get crazy, I could change the color of each letter while I scrolled. Twenty years later, my taste in writing utensils has gotten much more practical (almost all of my pens are boring, one-color black ones). But beauty brand Alleyoop has managed to bring a retractable multi-pen back into my life in the most practical way: with a makeup touch-up version that has eyeliner, lip liner, highlighter, and a brow pencil.

The Alleyoop Pen Pal 4-in-1 Touch-Up Pen comes in three different variations (though they all have black eyeliner and champagne highlighter): In a Rouge, which is made for light skin tones and has taupe brow pencil and nude pink lip liner; Make a Mauve, meant for medium skin tones, which features dark brown brow pencil and mauve lip liner; and Berry Busy, a deeper option that has berry lip liner and dark brown brow pencil. The ELLE team put this innovative tool to the test. Read our unfiltered thoughts below.

“I can’t believe how much I like this pen. It’s so easy to click through the different options. Each tip is small and sharp, so you’re able to draw precise lines, and the formulas are creamy, glide easily, and last all day. Being able to basically do your entire face with one product is incredible. I want to keep one in my travel case, gym bag, and purse for easy touch-ups on the go.”

“This fun little pen totally took me by surprise! What I expected to be a bit gimmicky turned out to be a super convenient and well-formulated makeup product. To start, I lined my lips with the pink shade. The mauve color was beautiful and pigmented; it glided on smoothly, and actually lasted throughout the day. Next, I took the champagne shade and used it to highlight the tip of my nose, inner corners of my eyes, and my brow bones. The small tip was especially helpful for precise placement. After filming, I even tried using the black shade to create a little winged liner. It wasn’t super pigmented, but it definitely helped to lift and elongate my eye shape. I can definitely see myself throwing this tool in my bag for on-the-go touch-ups across my entire face.”

“My makeup bag is essentially a black hole, so you can imagine my delight at discovering that this clever little pen replaces four of the beauty essentials I find myself rummaging around for every morning: brow pencil, eyeliner, lip liner, and eye highlighter. The Make a Mauve shade is a perfect match for my everyday makeup routine, featuring a dark brown brow pencil, black eyeliner, champagne gold highlighter, and rosy pink lip liner. Each formula glides on effortlessly with great color payoff. The eyeliner is particularly easy to blend if you’re after a smoky, smudged-out look, but as someone who rarely leaves the house without a little wing, I’m pleased to find that I can still achieve a precise flick with the help of a straight-edged piece of card. The lip liner is another personal favorite, delivering smooth, even color without any dragging or skipping. And when one of the pencils starts to wear down, all it takes is a quick twist to reveal more product—no sharpener required. Suffice it to say, the next time I’m jet-setting somewhere, I’m leaving my assortment of pencils behind and making room for this multitasking Alleyoop pen instead.”

“The only way I can describe this pen is that it is an all-in-one makeup product designed for the girl on the go (a.k.a. me). Not only was I delighted by the novelty of clicking through the different products, but I find it incredibly useful for someone who needs to do a touch-up every now and then before a work event after a day at the office. And no, I'm not over the nostalgia for the pen, either.”

“I love the idea of having an all-in-one tool I can just throw in my purse or bag on days when I’m not sure if I want to wear makeup or not. In fact, I might get a second one of these pens to just leave at my desk.”
