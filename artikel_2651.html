Chargers Power Rankings Roundup: In the cellar no more!

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

After 10 weeks and nine games into the 2026 season, Jim Harbaugh and the Bolts finally got their first win of the season by beating the Ravens 23-20 on Monday Night Football. It was the team's most complete game of the season, which included zero turnovers from Justin Herbert and over 160 yards rushing.

The win put the Chargers tied with the Tennessee Titans and Miami Dolphins at 1-8. For the most parts, experts put either one of these teams at the very bottom, but the good news is that it is no longer a clean sweep by the Chargers!

That's something worth celebrating, right?

Anyhoo, here's where the Chargers landed in this week's power rankings after their first win!

"John Harbaugh says hello." – Mike Florio

"Non-QB MVP at midseason: Edge Tuli Tuipulotu"

"It's hard to find value in the Chargers' one-win debacle, but the defense has at least been passable. And there's no doubt that Tuipulotu is the best player on that unit. The 2023 second-round pick has a team-high 22 pressures and is tied for the team lead with three sacks. His average run tackle comes after a gain of just 2.2 yards." – Aaron Schatz

"They climbed out of the cellar by winning for the first time Monday night against the Ravens. Can you believe it?" – Pete Prisco

"The second-year wide receiver, a second-round pick out of Ole Miss, set a new career high in yards in a season Monday night after catching five passes for a career-high 86 yards. He now has 348 for the season. It took one sliding and reviewed catch to get there, but it helped the Chargers get their first win." – Josh Kendall and Chad Graff

"The Los Angeles Chargers finally have their first win. And while that one victory isn't enough to get them out of the basement of these Power Rankings, it's a start at least."

"After weeks of a backfield committee that was…let's go with confusing, the Chargers handed the keys in the backfield back to 2025 first-round pick Omarion Hampton. In doing so the Bolts may have unlocked something—Hampton racked up 105 yards and a touchdown on 22 carries."

"After a brutal stretch in the schedule, things let up some going forward—not one of the Chargers' next four opponents has a winning record."

"The 2026 season is lost—and has been for a while. But there's an opportunity for the Chargers to build some momentum over the next month, even if all it nets them is a lower pick in next year's draft. – Brad Davenport

''The Chargers got the win they needed to avoid a lot of annoying 0-17 questions. Let's not get too far ahead of ourselves; it was just one win, and on the whole, they've still been the NFL's worst team this season. But give the Chargers credit for a good effort. They figured out how to make the Ravens one-dimensional. Baltimore had 16 yards rushing on 15 carries. That forced Baltimore to lean too much on quarterback Jacoby Brissett, who was making his first Ravens start after being traded less than two weeks ago."

''It was also encouraging to see Omarion Hampton emerge with a 105-yard rushing day. Justin Herbert completed only 12 passes but he didn't need to do much more because the running game was effective. Maybe the Chargers can build on their first win and have a respectable second half of the season." – Frank Schwab
