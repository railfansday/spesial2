ABCmouse Launches New Mobile Learning Game, Expanding Access for Families Worldwide

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The standalone game brings ABCmouse's research-backed early learning content to children through the Netflix mobile experience

LOS ANGELES, Nov. 5, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Age of Learning, the company behind ABCmouse, the most downloaded kids' education app trusted by families and educators for more than 15 years, today announced the launch of a new standalone ABCmouse mobile game for Netflix members worldwide. The game brings a curated collection of ABCmouse's award-winning learning activities, animated lessons, and books to children ages 2â8, all fully playable offline after the initial download.

Age of Learning and Netflix share a commitment to giving families high-quality content that children enjoy and parents can feel good about, now available on a platform millions of them already use every day.

Built for children ages 2 to 8, the experience spans reading and writing, math, science and health, art and music, and social-emotional learning, with a primary focus on ages 4 to 6, a critical developmental window for early literacy and numeracy. A toddler track for ages 2 to 3 is anchored by fan-favorite character Todd Ler'Fox, while additional reading and math content supports early elementary learners ages 7 and 8. Parents remain in control, choosing when and how the game fits into their family's routine.
