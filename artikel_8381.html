It's just a reasonable guide. Nothing more. What else do you expect from them? Calling for some extreme result? They have to work the trends, history, valuation, etc. We all know it's a random walk. At some point, we'll have a downturn, and it will catch everyone by surprise by its severity. And then, if history is a guide (to some extent), things will recover and produce a reasonable 10-year return. Hard to say. I'd say what they have to offer is decent for planning purposes.

If I had to offer a prediction (2 cents worth), corporate earnings have been very strong recently driven by the AI surge. At some point, they will drift lower. I think valuations have largely taken that into account (based on P/E evolution), but there's always an overreaction to everything. All it takes is an event or a season of disappointments. But I'm confident in the longer term outlook for returns from the capital markets. It would be nice if bonds return to a more normal trajectory.
