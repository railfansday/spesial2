Hamilton and Antonelli linked by bizarre F1 championship coincidence

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Lewis Hamilton and Kimi Antonelli have more in common than their links to Mercedes, with the two drivers sharing a remarkable statistic after the Italian secured his maiden Formula 1 championship at the 2026 Mexico City Grand Prix.

Antonelli became the youngest world champion in F1 history on 1 November, finishing third behind Max Verstappen and Hamilton to secure the title with four races remaining.

The 20-year-old broke Sebastian Vettel's long-standing record, becoming the first Italian driver to win the championship since Alberto Ascari in 1953. However, another curious statistic has emerged from his achievement, linking Antonelli to the seven-time world champion he replaced at Mercedes ahead of the 2025 season.

Both drivers claimed their first Formula 1 world championships exactly 595 days after making their Grand Prix debuts, despite their careers beginning almost two decades apart. Hamilton made his debut with McLaren at the 2007 Australian Grand Prix on 18 March, immediately establishing himself as a championship contender alongside Fernando Alonso.

His maiden title arrived on 2 November 2008, when he famously overtook Timo Glock on the final lap of the Brazilian Grand Prix to beat Felipe Massa to the championship by a single point. Exactly 595 days separated his debut and first world title.

Antonelli's journey followed precisely the same timeline. The Italian made his Formula 1 debut with Mercedes at the 2025 Australian Grand Prix on 16 March, before securing the championship in Mexico City on 1 November 2026.

Although the two drivers needed exactly the same number of days to become world champions, their respective journeys unfolded differently.

Hamilton narrowly missed out on the title during his rookie season, losing the 2007 championship to Kimi Raikkonen by a single point before returning stronger the following year.

Antonelli, meanwhile, endured a more challenging debut campaign, finishing seventh in the 2025 standings before benefiting from Mercedes' resurgence under the new technical regulations in 2026.

The Italian claimed his maiden victory in China and followed it up with another triumph in Japan, becoming the youngest driver to lead the world championship standings. Unlike Hamilton, whose first championship was decided at the final corner of the final race, Antonelli secured his title with four Grands Prix still remaining.

The two also share the distinction of becoming world champions in their second Formula 1 seasons, although Antonelli achieved the feat at just 20 years and 68 days old, compared to Hamilton's 23 years and 300 days.

The coincidence is particularly striking given that Antonelli joined Mercedes as Hamilton's direct replacement, with both drivers now having reached their first world championship exactly 595 days into their Formula 1 careers.
