Travis Kelce injury: Chiefs TE hurts ankle vs. Falcons

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Patrick Mahomes looked to Travis Kelce early in Sunday's game against the Atlanta Falcons.

But that first-quarter connection could prove costly for the Kansas City Chiefs.

Mahomes connected with his tight end on Kansas City's opening drive for a 23-yard gain, but following the tackle from behind Kelce walked gingerly to the sideline, and eventually found his way to the locker room under the supervision of trainers.
