Alan Shearer has given this damning appraisal of Newcastle United.

This follows Monday night’s match at St James’ Park.

Newcastle United losing 1-0 at home to Everton in the Premier League.

This defeat coming only four days after the Toffees won 3-1 at the Hill Dickinson Stadium to knock United out of the Carabao Cup.

A depressing one-two that has knocked Newcastle United fans cold.

Alan Shearer watched on as the team he has always supported put in such a poor performance and the scouse mackems deservedly won.

Alan Shearer using his personal social media to issue this damning 16 word Newcastle United summary: “Well done Everton. Much better than Newcastle. Nothing at all from the Toon. Very very poor.”
