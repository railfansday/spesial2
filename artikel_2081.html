Man United defender Diogo Dalot has dedicated an emotional message to Cristiano Ronaldo after he retired from international football.

Ronaldo's iconic 23-year Portugal career came to an end with an official announcement on Thursday morning.

The 41-year-old left the last national team camp under a dark cloud in September after remaining an unused substitute in their 2-1 Nations League win over Norway.

His public fallout with manager Jorge Jesus led to widespread calls for Ronaldo to call time on a decorated A Selecao career.

Portugal will now begin life after the veteran icon following his new announcement on social media.

Ronaldo is an icon for many Portuguese players and former club and international teammate Dalot is clearly no different.

Ronaldo addressed his 680million Instagram followers on Thursday with a candid message to camera to confirm that he will no longer represent his national team.

The 41-year-old has retired with several records under his belt for the country and international football in general, including the most international goals (146) and most appearances in a national team (234) of all-time.

He also helped to deliver Portugal's maiden international trophy, as they won EURO 2016, while playing his part in their Nations League successes of 2019 and 2025.

Floods of stars from across the sporting world have sent messages to Ronaldo, but none more emotional and personal than United defender Dalot.

The 27-year-old took to Instagram following the announcement to dedicate a post to his ex-teammate, praising Ronaldo for helping the country "believe, dream and conquer."

Dalot has struggled for form at Old Trafford in recent times and is approaching the final 18 months of his contract, which expires in June 2028.

The 27-year-old recently surpassed 250 appearances for the club and remains a regular starter under Michael Carrick, despite fans slating his performances throughout the calendar year.

Sources have exclusively revealed to Football Insider that United are ready to let Dalot depart next year so they can sell him for a fee before he leaves for nothing in 2028.

They feel like the right-back position needs upgrading long term and he is coming to a natural end at Old Trafford.
