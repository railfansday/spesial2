Free agent training with Stoke City following Middlesbrough exit

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Stoke City invited the defender to train with them earlier this year. SCFC have had an eye-catching campaign so far under the guidance of head coach Mark Robins and they are positioned in 2nd place in the Championship table after 17 fixtures behind Middlesbrough.

Lenihan, 32, was released by Boro at the end of the last campaign after his contract at the Riverside Stadium expired. He has since been a free agent and he has been weighing up his next move in the game.

In this latest update regarding his situation, The Sentinel claim he has been keeping up his fitness levels with the Potters as he continues his search for a new club. The report adds that the Staffordshire outfit are unlikely to snap him up on a permanent basis at this time but he is said to have made a positive impression around the training ground.

Stoke City won 2-1 at home against Millwall on Wednesday night with striker George Hirst scoring both of their goals. They are back in action this weekend with an away trip to West Ham.

Stoke City are well stocked up in Lenihan’s position at the moment. SCFC have the likes of Ben Wilmost, Bosun Lawal, Luke Graham and Maksym Talovierov at their disposal.

The Potters also still have Ben Gibson in their squad. However, he has seen his game time dry up due to too much competition.

Therefore, they don’t really need to snap up Lenihan. He wouldn’t get any minutes so wouldn’t be worth paying a wage at this stage.

In addition, he has struggled with injury issues over the past few years and would take a while to get back fully up to match sharpness. He is vastly experienced in the EFL though and will be a useful character to have in and around the place right now.

Stoke City’s younger players will learn a lot from Lenihan being in the building. SCFC are on a roll and will be full of confidence at the moment.

Middlesbrough signed the ex-Republic of Ireland international back in 2022 and he spent four years there. Prior to his switch to Boro, the Belvedere academy graduate played for Blackburn Rovers and made 252 outings during his days at Ewood Park.
