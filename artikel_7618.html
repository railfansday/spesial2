Baltimore Man Indicted in Connection With Counterfeit Money Scheme

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Baltimore, Maryland – A Baltimore man is facing federal indictment, today, stemming from a counterfeit currency scheme. Clifton Monroe, Jr., 49, is charged with four counts of passing or possessing counterfeit obligations or securities, along with one count of possessing counterfeit obligations or securities and one count of counterfeiting and forging obligations or securities of the United States after he manufactured counterfeit money and used it in an attempt to pay for goods.
