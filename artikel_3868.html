I have a feeling Delivery Derpy Tiger is going to be popular

When you purchase through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission. Here’s how it works.

Sometimes you see a toy and go, "uh oh, that's gonna be popular," and I'm getting that feeling with a new KPop Demon Hunters plush.

This animatronic toy has 'easy Christmas gift' written all over it; besides being thoroughly adorable, KPop Demon Hunters Delivery Derpy Tiger is crammed with cool features (like sound and motion effects) at a pretty reasonable $44.99. It comes out at some point in November as well, so it's in prime position for that Holiday season rush. I've not seen it on shelves yet, but apparently it'll drop at major retailers this month. While it isn't available right now, the press release name-drops Amazon, Target, and Walmart. I've linked these with a quick search, so pop back every now and then to see if the toy has appeared.

In terms of what it can do, this toy comes with 40+ sounds and reactions that are activated by patting it on the back. It also sticks out its tongue thanks to a motorized jaw when you press its head. This is topped off with a holographic eye treatment alongside posable legs and head.

While it's one of the cooler pieces of KPop Demon Hunters merch I've seen, it's far from the only one. Hasbro has also produced Nerf gear based on the film, like this cool replica sword (which you can find via Amazon for just under $30), while Lego has also given its take on Derpy Tiger. This kit is very gift-worthy as well, and it's currently got a 20% discount at Amazon.

Want more cool collectibles? Lego Godzilla set seems to be appearing in stores early, but the jury's out on whether it'll become one of the best toys.
