Two Cool, Hidden Features GTA 6 Doesn’t Explain: Flipping And Sitting

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Grand Theft Auto 6 is out now after a long wait, and as players invade Vice City like a swarm of hungry rats, they’re likely to poke at all its missions, tutorials, and random events. And yet, most of them likely won’t realize that hidden in GTA 6 and its massive amount of controls are two very cool and much-appreciated features: the ability to flip cars over and to sit on the ground and chill.

In past GTA games, flipping a car onto its back was a death sentence for the vehicle. It would soon catch on fire and blow up, which could be very annoying. Then in GTA V, Rockstar added the ability to flip your car over by rocking the left thumbstick around. While this was handy, it always felt like an out-of-place mechanic that was too convenient. It almost felt like magic and trivialized rolling a car over in a crash. With GTA 6, Rockstar has implemented an option that lets you salvage a stolen car that gets flipped on its back, without the use of magic.

Simply walk up against the side of the flipped vehicle and click the left stick in as either Jason or Lucia. If done correctly, the character should move their arms into position and start pushing on the car. At that point, you can hold square or X to attempt to flip the car back onto its wheels. If the vehicle is too heavy or you run out of stamina, the flip won’t work. I’ve also had it fail on steep inclines.

You can also push cars down the road if they run out of gas or shove a boat or jet ski off the sand and into the water using this mechanic. For most people, this won’t be a super-useful feature, as you can just use the Scooter Bros app to repair your car or respawn it for a fee. But if you’re out and about in a stolen car and being chased by the cops, which blocks access to the app, being able to flip a speedy getaway car back over could prove handy.

I’ve played over 140 hours of Grand Theft Auto 6 at this point, and I don’t remember the game, at any point in those many, many hours, explaining that I could do this. I only discovered it by accident when, after flipping a car over, I got close enough to it for a small tutorial prompt to appear in the top left, hinting at the ability. Later, I tried pushing a small boat off a trailer into the water, and it worked. Neat!

Another feature the game never really explained to me (or I missed) is the ability to sit or rest anywhere in the game.

Simply hold circle or B while on foot and your character will attempt to rest. In some areas, like on grass or sand, this will let Jason and Lucia sit and chill. In other places, they’ll lean on one foot and relax. And if you do this near a wall, they’ll rest against it like a cool person.

Again, this is a feature that I don’t remember the game explicitly explaining to me while playing. I discovered this early on when I tried to see if I could go prone and held the crouch button. I didn’t go prone, but I did pop a squat on the dirt.

I really love that GTA 6 added the ability to just…sit and chill out almost anywhere. The series has rarely let players relax and take in the world in this way. Sometimes I’d find a busy section of the beach in Vice City and sit on the sand, just watching the many different NPCs mill about. Other times I’d find a campfire in the woods and sit next to it in the dark, listening to the world around me. I hope you enjoy sitting and flipping as much as I do in GTA 6. Take a break between the shooting and the robbing. Jason and Lucia deserve it.
