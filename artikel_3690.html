Cara Delevingne Is 'Lucky' to Have Girlfriend Minke After Recent Surgery

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Cara Delevingne opened up about her recent health struggles, revealing her partner, Minke, has been a godsend through it all.

Delevingne, 34, exclusively told Us on Wednesday, November 18, that Minke (real name Leah Mason), 27, has been her “biggest support system in this era of life,” explaining, “She sees me in and through everything.”

“I think I’ve never been able to be the most me until I’ve been with her,” the model shared while attending the L’Oréal Paris Women of Worth Celebration.

Delevingne called the musician her “biggest supporter, my biggest champion” and someone who has had to put up with a lot.

“Oh, I feel so bad for her sometimes, man, I don’t know. I really do feel so bad for her,” Delevingne said, noting that she had undergone an endometriosis procedure 10 days prior and Minke “had to look after me.”

Endometriosis is an “often-painful condition in which tissue that is similar to the inner lining of the uterus grows outside the uterus” and often affects the ovaries, fallopian tubes and the tissue lining the pelvis, according to Mayo Clinic.

Delevingne noted that the condition “sucks,” but she is feeling “OK” following the surgery.

“Being a woman is hard sometimes, man. But we’re here. We keep fighting,” she told Us. “We keep going, and we love it. Listen, I love being a woman. It’s incredible.”

While Delevingne is currently on the mend, she confessed that after the procedure, her “hormones have been everywhere,” and it was Minke who was able to comfort her.

“I’ve been working so much. I wasn’t recovering properly, well, because I had been working so much,” she recalled. “And she’s there, and she’s by my side, and she loves me, and I love her.”

Delevingne noted that Minke’s continued care and kindness during her health ups and downs has only strengthened their relationship.

“I can’t wait to have a family with her. I’m just, I’m very lucky that she’s in my life,” she gushed.

Delevingne and Minke have known each other since they were kids at boarding school. The pair reconnected more than a decade later at an Alanis Morissette concert and went public with their romance in 2022.

“Two magical years with you and so many pictures of us kissing in front of things ❤️,” Delevingne wrote via Instagram in June 2024. “These past two years have been A LOT of life. A lot of change, growth, pain and most importantly LOVE and I am so lucky to have found the perfect person to ride life’s ups and downs with.”

While Delevingne has found The One in Minke, she told Us on Wednesday that she has also learned to take L’Oréal Paris’ “You’re worth it” slogan to heart.

The company has been recognizing extraordinary women leading nonprofit organizations and “creating meaningful change” in their communities at its annual L’Oréal Paris Women of Worth for 21 years. This year’s honorees each received a $25,000 grant in partnership with Points of Light.

Delevingne said that believing in her own abilities and knowing your worth doesn’t have to be a big to-do.

“It’s got to come from within. If you’re not able to evoke that feeling, rely on your friendships. Remind yourself. Get into your body,” she said of her tips to feeling confident. “I always think if I don’t feel worth it, I’m in my head. So I’ve got to get out of my head and into my body.”

Delevingne pointed to dancing or “anything that makes you laugh” or cry or get angry as keys to getting out of your head.

“Something needs to move, and I think, yeah, self-worth comes through that. You know, it doesn’t always have to be pretty,” she added. “It needs to be real and ugly, and whatever it needs to be, it should.”
