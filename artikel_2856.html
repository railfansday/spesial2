Bears Exorcised Thanksgiving Day Demons from Detroit

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Chicago Bears exorcised some demons from the Detroit Lions locker room with their huge Thanksgiving comeback win. If you recall, the last time they played in Motown on Turkey Day, the visiting locker room saw a heated exchange after head coach Matt Eberflus' horrific late-game mismanagement cost the Bears a chance to win.

This time, the postgame locker room saw head coach Ben Johnson hoisted on the players' shoulders in celebration after the 39 to 35 win, Johnson's first over his former team.

This one meant a lot not only to Ben, but to his players who desperately wanted to do it for their coach.
