UCLA's defense leads the way in 76-44 victory over Arizona

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Even without their starting point guard, the No. 10 UCLA Bruins cruised to a 76-44 win over the Arizona Wildcats on Thursday with the defending national champions moving to 3-0 in the 2026-2027 season.

After grinding through the first half, UCLA broke away after halftime to stay perfect on the season. It wasn't an utter blowout that became a regular occurrence the past couple seasons but the new-look Bruins still managed to pick up a 32-point win over Arizona.

UCLA was without Elina Aarnisalo, who's missing this week's action as she represents her country, playing for the Finnish National Team. Aarnisalo returned for a second stint with the Bruins this season after a season away with North Carolina. Aarnisalo returned as UCLA's new starting point guard, with freshman Somto Okafor taking her spot in the starting lineup on Thursday.

Speaking of the 2024-2025 UCLA team, Timea Gardiner is back and is looking better than ever. Gardiner played her role to a tee on Thursday, scoring 16 points, rebounds, going 2-5 from three and even blocking a shot. Gardiner's career-high in scoring was 11.6 in 2023-2024 but she'll have a chance to surpass that total this season with the Bruins.

Addy Brown had her best game as a Bruin, scoring 16 points off the bench, including three made threes. Brown had an all-around game, grabbing six rebounds and dishing out four assists. If Brown can become an offensive hub off the bench for the Bruins, it'll add a much-needed extra element to the UCLA offense.

UCLA's defense stood out, with a physical defense likely being the team's path to serious contention this season. The Bruins held Arizona to 24% shooting from the field and forced 16 turnovers while blocking six shots.

The Bruins return to action on Sunday against Princeton where UCLA will have a chance at a 4-0 start to their season. Aarnisalo will miss Sunday's game but Cori Close's team should still have plenty of talent on-deck to remain unbeaten in the new season.
