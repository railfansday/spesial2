Jack Grealish Apologises to Everton Supporters, So What's Next?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Football fans are fickle. Not just Everton fans.

It's a natural thing among football fans. One day, you absolutely love your club, the manager, and the players, a few weeks later your opinion can change.

That's not a dig at supporters, by the way. As mentioned, it's completely natural and it's what makes following a football club so great.

No matter how bad things are, how poorly a player is performing, or how much you want a manager out, they are just 90 minutes away from making you the happiest you'll have been that week.

This leads us on to Jack Grealish. Many are disappointed, angry, and some have even washed their hands with Grealish. How long will that last, though? Who knows. Grealish might not be able to win back the love of some supporters, whereas others will forget anything ever happened, depending on the next few weeks.

As previously mentioned in the article about Grealish, he was always going to come back into the side, and that's something the fans have to accept. When you're paying a player so much money and have such a small squad, he wasn't going to rot away until his loan ended.

Everton have made the decision to reintegrate Grealish, with the experienced winger featuring in a behind-closed-doors friendly this week, but what happens next?

In reality, Grealish has to give fans a reason to forgive him. The Man City loanee isn't going to get much slack anymore, so he must ensure that he performs to a strong level when given an opportunity in the side.

Everton fans have been mostly pleased with the performance levels of the team when Grealish has been unavailable, so they know that they don't necessarily need him every week.

Grealish's performances when he's played this season haven't been at the top level we've seen from him previously. Tyrique George is a young asset for Everton that needs developing, and many now think that he should be a priority going forward.

As a result, Grealish needs to win fans back.

The apology isn't enough. It was a fairly weak apology, but probably enough for many to draw a line under it. The issue is now whether he doesn't perform.

A poor performance or too is going to be a real frustration among supporters, more so than most other players. Grealish owes David Moyes, the fans, and his team-mates so high-level performances over the coming weeks.

This goes back to the fickleness of fans. A strong performance against Brentford and many will love him again. Now just imagine he scores the winner in the derby?

As previously mentioned in the last article we did on Grealish, fans just need to accept that he's going to be involved. That will be a lot easier if he performs well, of course.

One thing that can't happen is that he's the first name on the team sheet and invincible. He's let the team down and everyone surrounding it, so we should see evidence that he's forced to work his way back into the side.

The apology has come, but fans will want more. After reading social media, it appears some supporters expected more than a small Instagram story when it comes to an apology from Grealish. Of course, there are probably details that should remain private, but the fans who have stuck with Grealish and supported him probably deserve more.

Either way, a line has been drawn under it now, but there's no doubt Grealish has to perform. He can't be brought straight into the starting XI, and he needs to work his way in, like Tyrique George, Tyler Dibling, and Hayden Hackney have to.

Grealish has been away training during the first few days of the international break, with the squad likely given some time off. The Everton winger has been away in Dubai and continuing his training, and hopefully behaving!
