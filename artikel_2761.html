Arkansas report card | Grading the Hogs after tight Auburn loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The wining streak inside Jordan-Hare Stadium is over.

Arkansas football could not hold on to an early two-score lead and watched Auburn rally for a 26-23 victory on Saturday, Nov. 7. Alex McPherson hit a game-winning field goal from 43 yards inside the final minute to break the Hogs' heart.

This was the Razorbacks' (3-6, 1-4 SEC) first loss in its last three road games against the Tigers (5-4, 3-4). It ended Ryan Silverfield's undefeated record against Auburn head coach Alex Golesh.

Here are our grades for the Razorbacks following the tight loss to the Tigers:

The running game had its moments, including explosive carries from Sutton Smith and Cam Settles, and the Razorbacks finished with 172 yards on 44 rushes. However, Arkansas wasn't efficient once it got inside Auburn territory and had to settle for too many field-goal attempts. It was also a rough day for quarterback KJ Jackson, who was 14-for-30 with 188 passing yards.

The Hogs held a running quarterback in check for three quarters, but Byrum Brown got loose in crunch time and finished the day with 23 carries for 120 yards. Arkansas gave up three points in the first half and 23 in the second. A strong start won't be remembered after the Razorbacks limped to the finish line.

Max Gilbert made a trio of kicks, but he missed two more fields goal of more than 50 yards. Those misses loomed large, and he's now 1-for-7 on the season from that distance. Arkansas must start to consider bailing on those long attempts. Add in a 29-yard punt from Jesse Ehrlich, and this unit remains a disappointment.

The defensive gameplan to limit Brown was effective overall, and Silverfield was solid managing the clock. The offensive play-calling was frustrating at times as Arkansas remained stubborn with its passing game despite a lack of success.

The sophomore running back finished with 100 yards on 16 carries and continues to reward his coaches for giving Settles the starting role over more experienced players like Smith and Braylen Russell.

This game was there for the taking, but Arkansas found a way to snatch defeat from the jaws of victory. This was a ninth consecutive loss in a one-score game, and Arkansas will reflect on this as a missed opportunity.
