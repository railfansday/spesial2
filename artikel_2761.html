Darius Acuff Jr.’s historic feat wasted in Kings’ 13th loss of the season

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Sacramento Kings may be a walking losing streak. But that is not due to any fault from rookie guard Darius Acuff Jr. Acuff has brought it for the Kings on every single night, and he had himself another brilliant outing despite their 128-107 loss to the Phoenix Suns on Wednesday.

Acuff has hit the ground running to begin his NBA career. He was the Kings' best player on the night yet again, finishing with 36 points on 11-of-22 shooting from the field to go along with four rebounds and six assists. He even added two blocks for good measure.

In fact, according to StatMuse, Acuff became just the third rookie since 2000 to record at least 36 points, six assists, and two blocks — joining Kevin Durant and Cooper Flagg in that exclusive club.

It's important to note that both Durant and Flagg won the Rookie of the Year award, and with the eye-popping stats Acuff puts up on a regular basis, it would be surprising if he doesn't bag home the award by the end of the season.

With this latest outing, Acuff is now averaging 26.2 points, 3.6 rebounds, and 7.0 assists per contest on the season — numbers that only true superstars in the NBA can put up.

Now, Acuff is not on that level quite yet. He's benefitted immensely from playing for a Kings team that is going nowhere fast and can afford to have him commandeer the offense despite his inexperience.

Moreover, the Kings are all-in on his development, and him learning to be this incredible scoring and playmaking threat at such a young age bodes well for his superstar trajectory moving forward.

Of course, the Kings are a terrible team all around, having won just one of their first 14 games this season. Be that as it may, they at least have a ray of hope to cling on to considering how well Acuff has been playing to start his NBA career.
