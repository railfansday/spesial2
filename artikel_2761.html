Caitlin Clark quizzed on when her Taylor Swift-inspired shoes are being released

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Caitlin Clark recently made an appearance on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon and was quizzed on the release of her Taylor Swift-inspired Nike shoes.

After becoming one of the biggest names in the WNBA, Clark has launched a shoe collection with Nike, which comes in a number of different colourways.

A few versions are already out, while there are some still unreleased, including the Friendship Bracelet Caitlin 1s.

The Indiana Fever star gifted a pair to Jimmy Fallon when she appeared on The Tonight Show this week and when doing so, revealed they are expected to be out in late February.

After being handed the shoes, Fallon asked: "Are these inspired by your buddy Taylor Swift?"

Clark said: "Obviously, I'm a big Taylor fan and, yeah, she's the best. You know, and I think a lot of my games, like, people always bring friendship bracelets. They want to trade, so I was like, 'let's put it on a shoe', how fun. People can make it their own."

The Tonight Show host then asked: "When is this shoe coming out?" to which Clark replied: "I think it's like in late February."

As well as the Swift version, Clark also has a Kansas City Chiefs-inspired colourway (Sea of Red) launching in the future and has already given a pair to Travis Kelce.
