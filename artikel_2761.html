Man United vs Brentford: How and when to watch the big game across the globe

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Manchester United take on Brentford at home tomorrow afternoon, looking for their first win in the league since August.

The Red Devils are 19th in the table and desperate for a win to climb out of the relegation zone.

This is when and where fans can watch the game across the globe.

Fans in the United Kingdom will be unable to watch the game live as it kicks off at 3pm on a Saturday.

Supporters in Ireland will be able to watch the game on Premier Sports 1, and Portuguese supporters will be able to tune in on DAZN at the same time.

Viewers based in Spain can also use DAZN, but at the local time of 16:00. French fans can also watch on Canal+ and Germans on Sky GO at the same 4pm kick-off local time.

Scandinavian fans in Denmark, Norway and Sweden can also watch the game at the same time but on Viaplay.

Fans based in the United States can catch the game on fuboTV at 10:00 Eastern Time. This means a kick-off at 09:00 Central Time, 08:00 Mountain Time and finally 07:00 Pacific Time.

Canadian fans can catch the match on DAZN at 10:00 Toronto time.

Moroccan supporters can watch the game on beIN Sports 1 at 15:00 local time, and Cameroonian and Nigerian fans can see the game on SuperSport at 16:00.

South African fans can catch the match on the same channel at 17:00 and Kenyan supporters at 18:00.

Viewers based in India can see the game at 20:30 local time on Star Sports Select, and Chinese supporters can follow the action on Migu at 23:00. Japanese supporters can catch the match on U-NEXT at midnight.

Finally, Australians can tune in on Stan Sport at 02:00 on Sunday morning.
