Huijsen and Courtois react to Real Madrid’s 5-1 win over PSV: &#8216;We’re aiming to win every trophy&#8217;

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Dean Huijsen and Thibaut Courtois have explained Real Madrid's response to their difficult first half against PSV Eindhoven, following a 5-1 Champions League victory that extended the team's winning run to ten matches across all competitions.

Despite the emphatic scoreline, Real Madrid were outplayed before the interval and went into the break trailing after Ivan Perisic's penalty.

Jose Mourinho made changes to his defence, introducing Huijsen and Ibrahima Konate, and the hosts produced a much-improved second-half display to complete the comeback.

Speaking after the match, Huijsen stressed that Madrid's focus is not on maintaining a perfect record but on achieving the club's ultimate objectives.

The Spanish centre-back explained the mentality within the dressing room.

"We don't talk about a perfect record. We talk about the fact that we have to win every match because we are Madrid. We're aiming to win every trophy," he said.

The defender was also asked about Mourinho's decision to replace Antonio Rudiger and Eder Militao at half-time, but said he was unaware of the manager's reasoning.

Dean Huijsen came on in the second half against PSV. (Photo by Angel Martinez/Getty Images)

"You'd have to ask him that. I don't know.

"Obviously, to be solid at the back. With the ball, to try to build from the back. Both Konate and I are in the team to contribute," he explained.

Huijsen also disclosed the message Mourinho delivered during the interval.

"That we weren't doing well in the match. Everyone could see that and we had to turn things around. And we did," he concluded.

Meanwhile, Courtois acknowledged that Real Madrid's performance before half-time was not good enough, particularly against PSV's aggressive pressing and man-to-man approach.

"They've squandered two or three good chances; they scored from the penalty spot, so it's about time I saved one.

"Sometimes I overthink it and try to read the player's mind; I dive the wrong way at the last moment and the ball goes the other way. It might be unstoppable, but that's just how it is," he began.

Courtois admits Real Madrid were substandard in the first half against PSV. (Photo by Florencia Tan Jun/Getty Images)

The goalkeeper also criticised Real Madrid's movement and collective effort in the first half, before describing the improvement after the break.

"I think once again the first half wasn't good. In modern football, if we don't all run, it's impossible – especially against a team like PSV, who go all out and press man-to-man. I

"If there's no movement or runs in behind, it's very difficult. We struggled a lot. In the second half we were much better; that's the Madrid we want to see – they didn't even get a shot on goal."

Courtois said the video analysis and Mourinho's instructions at half-time helped the players recognise their mistakes. Madrid scored soon after the restart and took control of the match.

"The manager, with the footage they showed us at half-time… You can see it clearly on the screen: we weren't doing things right and they were.

"We struggled in the first half. With that change in attitude, the second half was good; we scored in the 50th minute and everything became easier.

"The first goal came quickly, the second was a cracking goal from Jude, and on the counter-attack we finished them off," he concluded.
