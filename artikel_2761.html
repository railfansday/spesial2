With the Denver Broncos taking on the Carolina Panthers on Sunday, a moment occurred that has already had many in the sports world calling it one of the best interceptions of all time. As the Broncos try to go on a run to the Super Bowl, the team continues to make plays when it matters, even improbable ones.

The play in question happened in the third quarter with under two minutes left in the period when Bryce Young and Carolina were deep in the red zone, at the one-yard line, looking to take a lead against Denver. Looking to connect with Tommy Tremble, the pass got deflected as the tight end was being covered by Justin Strnad.

But the ball was barely tipped in the air after hitting Tremble's arm, enough for Devon Key to get the miraculous interception.

While the Broncos have been considered underwhelming this season, the team has been winning football games, especially close ones, as heading into Sunday's game against the Panthers, Denver had won 16 straight one-score games.
