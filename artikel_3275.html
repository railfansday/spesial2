Justin Long & Kate Bosworth To Headline Indie Comedy Series ‘The Renovation’

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

EXCLUSIVE: The resurgence of independent television series continues to grow with Justin Long (Barbarian, You Deserve Each Other) and Kate Bosworth (Still Alice, Along for the Ride) starring in The Renovation, a fully-financed indie comedy series. It hails from Lucas Carter (Summer of ’69), Nick Kreiss (Punk’d) who directs all episodes, Intrigue, Script2Screen and Hammerstone Studios.

Production has wrapped in Los Angeles, and EPs Intrigue, Script2Screen and Hammerstone will begin shopping the series to streaming platforms and television networks this month for distribution. Blending scripted comedy with hidden-camera elements, The Renovation centers on a real, unsuspecting married couple who believe they are participating in a home renovation television show. What they are unaware of is the contractors, fellow contestants, and everyone around them are actors, following a carefully constructed storyline designed to test the couple’s instincts, decision-making and sense of right and wrong. Watch on Deadline

Conceived by writer and producer Carter, The Renovation takes a fresh approach to the hidden-camera format, combining the storytelling and structure of a scripted comedy with the unpredictable reactions of real people.

The concept for The Renovation was originally developed by Carter, who brought the idea to Tariq Jalil’s Intrigue, Carter’s former employer, where the pair further developed the project. Recognizing the unique nature of the format and the creative freedom required to bring it to life, the team decided to independently finance the series. From there, Script2Screen (By Any Means, Mister) and Hammerstone Studios (Bill & Ted Face the Music, By Any Means) came on board to fully finance the production.

The series is executive produced by Jalil, Carter, Kreiss, Alex Lebovici, Ibrahim Mohammed, Yasmyne Mohammed, Chase Vergari, Michael Mazzarra, along with Long and Bosworth. Dan Swimer, Oscar-nominated screenwriter for Borat Subsequent Moviefilm, also serves as an executive producer.

Long and Bosworth are repped by Independent Artist Group and Yorn Levine. Kreiss is repped by Underground and Morris Yorn.
