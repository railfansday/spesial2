Texans run over ineffective Colts for easy 26-3 victory, winning first home game this season

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Houston Texans quarterback CJ. Stroud passed for 194 yards and three touchdowns, providing more than enough offense for a defense that shut down the Indianapolis Colts effectively in a 26-3 win on Thursday night.

The Texans won at home for the first time this season after losing their previous five at Reliant Stadium.

Stroud completed 21-of-27 passes with one interception. A second one appeared to be taken away in the second quarter before replay review ruled that Colts safety Cam Bynum didn't catch the ball cleanly.

Nico Collins caught three passes for 58 yards and a touchdown, while Kayshon Boutte followed with two catches for 40 yards and a score. Rookie tight end Marlin Klein caught Stroud's third TD pass.

The Texans' offense also benefited from the return of running back David Montgomery, back after a four-game absence due to a groin injury. The eight-year veteran rushed for 107 yards on 26 carries, keeping several drives moving on the ground. Emari Demercado also ran effectively, gaining 33 yards on five carries.

However, the sharpest thing seen on the field Thursday night may have been the Texans' "Rivalries" alternate uniforms that harkened back to the Oilers' classic look. With the victory, Houston improved to 4-6 and stayed on the fringes of the playoff race in the AFC. The Texans play host to the Baltimore Ravens in Week 12.

On the other side, the Colts could get nothing going with their rushing attack. Jonathan Taylor managed only 21 yards on 11 carries. That put more pressure on Daniel Jones to produce in the passing game, which did not happen.

Jones completed 12-of-27 passes for 144 yards and an interception. He did connect on four passes of 20 yards or more, but that simply wasn't enough to move Indianapolis toward the end zone. He was also tackled for a safety by Will Anderson Jr. in the third quarter after the Colts were pinned at their 5-yard line on a 62-yard punt from Houston's Kai Kroeger.

Anderson now has 11.5 sacks this season, tops in the NFL, after notching a career-high 12 last season.

With just under eight minutes left in the game and the Colts trailing by 23 points, Colts coach Shane Steichen replaced Jones with Anthony Richardson. But he threw two incompletions on his first series, the only action he saw.

Naturally, Steichen was asked if the Colts will make a quarterback change for their next game. "We'll evaluate everything," he said.

Altogether, the Colts generated only 167 yards of total offense. Anthony Gould tallied 48 yards receiving on two receptions, followed by 30 yards and two catches from Ashton Dulin. Taylor had four catches, but only for six yards.

Even kicker Spencer Shrader couldn't provide many points, missing two of his three field-goal attempts. One of those kicks was from 62 yards and was blocked.

Indianapolis has lost six of its past seven games, dropping to 3-8 for the season. Yet four teams have worse records in a subpar AFC. Up next for the Colts is a home matchup with the New York Giants (5-3-1).
