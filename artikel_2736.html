Luther Burden injury update as Bears WR left 'Sunday Night Football' with concussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The Chicago Bears lost multiple key players to injuries in Week 9. The most significant injury to affect their offense happened toward the end of the game.

Second-year wide receiver Luther Burden III exited the Bears' "Sunday Night Football" matchup with the Tampa Bay Buccaneers in the fourth quarter. Burden sustained an injury after taking a hard hit from Buccaneers rookie Rueben Bain Jr. near the sideline.

Bain's hit caused Burden to fumble the ball with fewer than four minutes left in the game. Tampa Bay recovered the ball and scored a touchdown and two-point conversion to tie the game a couple of plays later.

Prior to his injury, Burden was leading all receivers in the game with seven catches, 97 receiving yards and a touchdown.

The Bears did not provide any statement on the nature of Burden's injury or his status during the game.

Burden sustained a concussion in the final minutes of the "Sunday Night Football" clash on Nov. 8, Bears head coach Ben Johnson said after the game.

"We had two injuries. Tyrique (Stevenson) with the thumb, and Luther (Burden) with a concussion. We'll see where we're at there," Johnson said.

The second-year wideout will enter the league's concussion protocol as a result of his head injury. He will have to clear all five steps of the protocol before he returns to action.

The NFL breaks its concussion protocol down into five steps. The process is designed to break up the recovery process from a head injury and ensure a player's safety.
