Baker Mayfield is officially active against the Lions

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Buccaneers will have Baker Mayfield at quarterback for Sunday’s game in Detroit.

Mayfield missed practice on Wednesday with a foot injury, but returned on Thursday and head coach Todd Bowles signaled that Mayfield would play in his Friday press conference. Mayfield is reportedly dealing with plantar fasciitis and is at risk of aggravating the injury, so Jalon Daniels could wind up factoring into the game if things take a turn for the worse.

Mayfield and the Bucs come into the game with a 3-6 record, but they are very much alive in the NFC South race. The 7-2 Lions are trying to win the NFC North and will have a game against one of their rivals on that front when the Bears pay them a visit on Thanksgiving.
