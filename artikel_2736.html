‘Two years left’ reveal makes Mike Vrabel’s A.J. Brown trade decision even more puzzling

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

New details have come to light about the stunning A.J. Brown trade from Tennessee to Philadelphia in 2022, suggesting the wide receiver's football effectiveness is on borrowed time.

During the latest episode of "Stillman and Company," longtime Nashville radio host Jared Stillman revealed how Titans owner Amy Adams Strunk had no remorse for trading away Brown.

She reasoned that Brown's knees were a ticking time bomb, based on reports from Tennessee's medical staff.

"Amy was still OK with trading A.J. Brown because the trainers and the doctors had basically told the Titans' front office, including Amy, that AJ's knees had about two years left to go. This was 2022. And the trainers and the doctors say, 'We think the knees have about two years left,'" Stillman said.

The more shocking part was that then-Titans head coach Mike Vrabel had a say in the decision to move Brown.

Tennessee's front office was caught off guard by Vrabel's reaction to the blockbuster trade, as he appeared frustrated in the Titans' war room during the 2022 NFL Draft after the trade happened.

Tennessee Titans head coach Mike Vrabel greets A.J. Brown during a game against the Kansas City Chiefs in Nashville, Tenn., Oct. 24, 2021. Getty Images

Vrabel even said weeks earlier in a sit-down interview with Rich Eisen that he doesn't see a future with Brown leaving Tennessee as long as he is head coach.

"It was a group discussion, and that was what led to the A.J. trade. And then in front of the cameras, when the cameras turned on, Vrabel acted like a total piss pants, but he didn't get blindsided by that trade. He knew that was coming. He was part of those discussions," Stillman said.

New England Patriots head coach Mike Vrabel speaks to the media after an NFL game against the Detroit Lions in Munich, Germany, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. Getty Images

Not taking the risk on Brown unfortunately backfired on the Titans as the star wideout hit his stride in Philadelphia, recording three straight 1,000-yard seasons.

Brown also became a three-time All-Pro and won Super Bowl 59 with the Eagles.

Despite the prior warning, Vrabel still decided to reunite with Brown this season as New England sent a 2028 first-round pick and 2027 fifth-round pick to Philadelphia for the pass catcher.

New England Patriots wide receiver A.J. Brown (1) attempts a catch against Detroit Lions safety Chuck Clark (36) on Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026, in Munich, Germany. AP Photo/Lennart Preiss

The move required the Patriots to absorb Brown's three-year, $96 million extension, keeping him under contract through the 2030 season.

Brown has 11 catches for 128 yards and zero touchdowns through four games while battling injuries.
