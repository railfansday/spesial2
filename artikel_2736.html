Dan Quinn had an &quot;absolutely fantastic experience&quot; coaching with Mike McCarthy

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

With Wednesday's rant from former Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers painting a negative picture about Steelers coach Mike McCarthy that extended to his time with both the Packers and the Cowboys, those who have worked with him in those places will inevitably be asked about McCarthy.

Commanders coach Dan Quinn, who spent three years as McCarthy's defensive coordinator in Dallas, got the question on Friday.

"I had a like absolutely fantastic experience coaching with Mike," Quinn said, via Ben Standig. "He's part of the reason I wanted to go there. And over my three years with him, man, I learned a lot. So I can't speak anything from Pittsburgh but what I can say is like, you know, like I had an unbelievable coaching experience with him. Like, detailed about it. Got me better, challenged me in a lot of ways with how I'm thinking, what it can be, so that would go into it."

Some will say that Quinn is simply saying all the right things, and that he wouldn't want to make McCarthy look bad by telling tales of irregularities that may have transpired behind closed doors.
