2027 Browns mock draft: Selecting a potential turkey at quarterback

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Cleveland Browns have surprisingly played themselves into the AFC playoff race, sitting one game behind the Cincinnati Bengals for first in the AFC North.

Playing meaningful games rather than focusing on draft position is a refreshing change of pace for the franchise. But it does not erase the reality that the Browns will be rebuilding the quarterback room in 2027.

Just what that remodeling will look like is still in the "to be determined" file, as the Browns could:

Run it back with the current group (not at all likely).

Decide it is time to unleash Shedeur Sanders as the starter on an unsuspecting NFL (literally not happening).

Some mix of the always popular veteran "bridge quarterback" to pair with a rookie selected in the 2027 NFL Draft. (Quite possible, even if that bridge is current starter Deshaun Watson, which no one would have seen coming a few months ago).

That final bullet point brings us to a mock draft from Nate Tice at Yahoo Sports, which, unfortunately, has the Browns embracing the Thanksgiving spirit by selecting a potential turkey at the quarterback position.

Tice projects the Browns to be selecting at No. 17 (with the selection they received from the Los Angeles Rams as part of the Myles Garrett trade) and No. 20 (with their own pick).

With that first selection, Tice embraces the "Browns is the Browns" mantra by selecting South Carolina quarterback LaNorris Sellers:

The Browns throw another dart at the quarterback position. And boy, what a chance of hitting the bull's-eye this would be. Sellers is young (important to the Browns process) and has some of the loudest tools you'll ever see at the position. His play has felt calmer and more decisive over the past month, but he will need more time, especially in a more conducive situation than the one he's been in the past few years. Landing in Cleveland with the Browns' exciting young group of pass catchers isn't the worst place to settle, especially with head coach Todd Monken, who has gotten the most out of his quarterbacks at multiple stops.

Sellers is one of the most divisive college quarterbacks, which is saying a lot given the expected draft class. While athletic, he has only completed 63.1 percent of his career passes, while most top quarterbacks surpass 70 percent. He tosses interceptions (23), which are not really balanced out by just 50 touchdown passes.

Sellers is a quarterback who will have big games, like his four-touchdown performance this season against Florida – but offset that with clunkers, like completing 56 percent of his passes against Tennessee or throwing for just 87 yards against Georgia.

The Browns have searched so long for a quarterback, and made a mess of it far too often, that it is hard to see how selecting a player who is going to need a year (or likely more) to develop, with no guarantee of success.

Three picks later, Tice adds some depth to the defense with the selection of Notre Dame defensive end Boubacar Traore:

I peeked at another offensive lineman here, despite this year's Spencer Fano selection, and hit on the Austin Barber pick. Funnily enough, I have compared Traore to Jared Verse (but might be leaning more toward a DeMarcus Lawrence comparison). Either way, those are two strong defenders who can push the pocket, and that's exactly what Traore brings to the table. He would be another strong edge defender to add to the Browns' front.

The Browns can always use help on defense, and in 32 career games, Traore has 88 tackles, 15.5 sacks, and 26.5 tackles for loss. His draft stock has been rising this season, and if the Irish make a run through the college football playoffs, Traore will have plenty of opportunities to showcase his talent against tougher competition than the likes of Rice, Navy, and Purdue.

So there you have it. The Browns "throw another dart" at the quarterback position and add some help for the defensive line with a player who might be Jared Verse 2.0.
