Personal Consumer Issues • Re: Second credit card recommendations

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

We each only have one credit card. She has Chase Freedom and I have Chase Sapphire, but the free kind that isn't on offer any more.

I was thinking of getting a second card for each of us. I don't really believe in credit arbitrage or rewards programs.... So I'm really just looking for an occasional second card that has no annual fee and is from a different financial institution from Chase so that if there's issues with one, the other might be all right.
To start, I'd add the other person to each existing credit card so that you now each have a backup card without opening a new account. If you want a non-Chase account and don't care about rewards, then there are dozens of mostly equivalent no-fee options. Ours are from Citibank (one Costco, one non-branded), Capital One, Discover, and Synchrony. Most were once our primary card but later demoted when a better reward came along.
