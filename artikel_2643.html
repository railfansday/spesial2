Patrick Mahomes moves past Peyton Manning for most TDs in first 10 seasons

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Patrick Mahomes has accomplished another career milestone, giving the Chiefs an early lead in the process.

Mahomes hit Emmett Johnson for a 12-yard touchdown to put Kansas City ahead 7-0.

That play gave Mahomes 328 touchdown passes, including the playoffs, which pushes the quarterback past Hall of Famer Peyton Manning for the most all-time in a player's first 10 seasons.

It's worth noting that Mahomes played just one game in his rookie season.

Johnson is starting in place of Kenneth Walker, who suffered a groin injury late in the practice week. There wasn't much difference on the first possession, as Johnson rushed for 36 yards on five carries and had the 12-yard TD catch.
