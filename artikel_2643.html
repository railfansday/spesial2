Westbrook Christian football coach Steve Smith selected to AHSAA Hall of Fame

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

RAINBOW CITY — Current Westbrook Christian football coach, and former Piedmont coach, Steve Smith is heading to the AHSAA Hall of Fame.

The announcement came from the AHSAA on Friday, Nov. 20 ahead of the Warriors semifinal win over Tuscaloosa Academy. That win sends Smith and his Warriors to his eighth state championship game in search of state title number six for Smith and the first for Westbrook Christian football.

Smith won five state titles with the Bulldogs, over 17 years leading the program. The first came in 2009 with more following in 2015, 2016, 2019 and 2021. He won 198 games with Piedmont.

He moved to Westbrook ahead of the 2023 season, winning 34 games so far. He led the Warriors to their first region title since 2010 and has them in the state title game for the first time since 1997. His first stop was at Cedar Bluff. He has gone 317-92 in his career, spanning 32 seasons.

Smith reflected on the loyal coaching staffs that he's had between his three stops, and the support that his administrations have given him during an interview on Tuesday, Nov. 24 with The Gadsden Times.

"Tremendous communities that supported athletics and those schools. The most important factor is great players that were willing to let you coach them, push them and buy into the team first mentality. It sounds simple, but it's not that way everywhere, and I understand that. I'm just grateful that the good Lord has put me in position to work at places that those things were all in the mix," Smith said.

Smith is a Cherokee County alum, earning all-state honors as a senior. He earned valedictorian honors, the start of a legacy that has seen nine of his players take those same honors.

He went on to play at Jacksonville State, helping as a graduate assistant with the 1992 Division II national championship team. He has sent more than 50 players to the college level.
