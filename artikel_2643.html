West Ham interested in signing Arsenal youngster who is out on loan

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Ethan Nwaneri is facing an increasingly difficult situation at Borussia Dortmund, where he is spending the current season on loan. His future in Germany is now uncertain, with the possibility that his temporary spell could be brought to an end in January.

Should that happen, the young midfielder already has a potential destination, with another club reportedly prepared to bring him into their squad for the remainder of the campaign. A move could provide him with an opportunity to regain his confidence and secure more regular playing time.

Nwaneri is regarded as one of the finest young talents in the Arsenal squad and among the club's most promising academy graduates in recent years. However, he is currently struggling to establish himself, both at the Emirates and during his loan spells away from North London.

The youngster spent the second half of last season on loan at Olympique Marseille. His time in France initially showed considerable promise, but his situation deteriorated as the campaign progressed. A similar pattern appears to be developing during his current spell in Germany, raising questions about whether another change of environment would benefit his development.

Despite these difficulties, Nwaneri will be hoping to turn his situation around and demonstrate his considerable potential. Arsenal, however, want him to play regularly and continue developing, which could prompt the club to consider recalling him when the January transfer window opens.

West Ham have emerged as a possible destination should Arsenal decide to end his loan spell at Dortmund. According to Football365, the Hammers regard Nwaneri as a valuable addition who could strengthen their squad for the remainder of the season.

West Ham are keen to secure promotion back to the Premier League and believe that bringing in a player of Nwaneri's quality could improve their chances. They are reportedly interested in signing him on loan for the second half of the campaign.

Such a move could prove beneficial for the Arsenal youngster, particularly if it provides him with the regular opportunities he has struggled to secure in Germany. More consistent involvement would allow him to rebuild his confidence, gain valuable experience and demonstrate his abilities.

For Arsenal, finding the right environment for Nwaneri's development will remain important. A January move to West Ham could offer a practical solution for all parties.
