Who will Louisville, Kentucky soccer teams play in NCAA Tournament?

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Both Louisville soccer teams, along with Kentucky's women's soccer, have clinched a spot in their respective NCAA Tournaments.

Louisville men's soccer (12-3-3) earned an at-large bid and the No. 3 seed in the Indiana region when the bracket was revealed Nov. 16. The Cardinals will host the winner of Oregon State and CSU Bakersfield on Nov. 22. The opponent will be determined when those two squads play on Nov. 19.

Both UofL and UK will be in action when the NCAA Women's Soccer Tournament begins Friday, Nov. 20.

Louisville (10-4-3) secured a No. 8 seed in Vanderbilt's region and will host Wisconsin in the first round. If the Cards get by the Badgers, they'll face No. 1 Vanderbilt or UT Martin on Nov. 27.

Kentucky (9-5-4) brought home a No. 7 seed, also in Vanderbilt's region, and will see Virginia Tech in the first round. The winner of that match would play No. 2 Baylor or Lamar on Nov. 27.
