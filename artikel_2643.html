Saquon Barkley scores as Eagles dominate the first quarter against the Steelers

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Steelers are fighting for playoff position. The Eagles are going nowhere. And yet today in Philadelphia, the Steelers came out flat and the Eagles are showing a lot of fight.

It's 13-0 Eagles early in the second quarter after a Saquon Barkley 38-yard touchdown run followed two Jake Elliott field goals.

The Steelers' offense did nothing on its first three drives, first getting stuffed on fourth-and-1 at their own 40-yard line to start the game, and then going three-and-out twice.

It's not a great reflection on Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy that his team has come out so flat in a game that means a lot to the Steelers and nothing to the Eagles. Pittsburgh needs to get this turned around.
