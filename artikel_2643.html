6 takeaways from NFL Week 11: Giants demand respect, 49ers offense is unstoppable

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Nate Tice dives into his instant takeaways from Week 11 of NFL action.

Nate is solo for his Week 11 reactions this time around! He starts off with his takeaways from the San Francisco 49ers giving Brian Flores and the Minnesota Vikings defense their first stomping of the season. Next, 6 takeaways from Week 11, beginning with the New York Giants upsetting the juggernaut Jacksonville Jaguars, the Tampa Bay Buccaneers having no answers for the Detroit Lions, the Dallas Cowboys defeating the Tennessee Titans with six straight TDs to start the game, an unexpected shootout between the Carolina Panthers and Baltimore Ravens that may have spoiled Baltimore's playoff chances, the Chicago Bears winning their fifth in a row against the New Orleans Saints and an ugly win for the Philadelphia Eagles versus the Pittsburgh Steelers, where WR DK Metcalf got the boot.

Finally, for Extra Credit, Nate gives his thoughts on the speediest game the NFL has seen in years between the New York Jets and Los Angeles Chargers, a dominant Kansas City Chiefs win over the Arizona Cardinals and some exciting young players in the matchup between the Denver Broncos and Las Vegas Raiders.

(2:00) - 49ers crush Vikings in Mexico City
