How Tokenization Could Change the ETF Market

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Giang Bui, Head of Issuer Growth at Securitize, Jason Emery, Head of Product Management at DTCC Digital Assets, and Will Peck, Head of WisdomTree Onchain, join Bloomberg Intelligence's James Seyffart at the Bloomberg ETFs in Depth conference in New York on November 19, 2026. The panel examines how tokenization could expand the utility and distribution of ETFs, from collateral mobility and faster settlement to new ways of reaching global investors. They also discuss where the technology is already gaining traction and how traditional and onchain markets could converge over time. (Source: Bloomberg)
