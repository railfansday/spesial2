GET INVOLVED! Pick your FIFPRO World XI

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Fifpro's World XI shortlists have been revealed after more than 26,000 professional footballers casted votes for the best male and female players; who would make up your perfect starting side if you had a vote?

The Fifpro World XI shortlists have arrived... but who makes your best team?

Over 26,000 professional footballers have chosen their best men's and women's teams, with 26 finalists for both selections now revealed.

The 11 players who received the most votes in their position will make the final team.

Harry Kane, Jude Bellingham and Declan Rice are the English representatives on the men's shortlist.

The women's shortlist features seven English players: Hannah Hampton, Lucy Bronze, Alex Greenwood, Ella Toone, Aggie Beever-Jones, Chloe Kelly and Alessia Russo.

Unsurprisingly, Lionel Messi (18) and Cristiano Ronaldo (16) lead the way with most appearances for the men's XI.

England Lioness Bronze, meanwhile, has been voted into the women's team eight times - a remarkable achievement given the women's award was only launched in 2016.

Would veterans like Messi, Ronaldo and Bronze still make your XI? Who do you think should be in the final team?

Get involved and select your teams below. The male players have been nominated for their performances between August 4, 2025 and July 19, 2026, and the women's players for their displays between August 4, 2026 and August 16, 2026..
