‘I’m not going to’: Anthony Edwards says he’s changing his playing style when he returns from injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Anthony Edwards has been a big miss for the Minnesota Timberwolves.

The 25-year-old had to undergo knee surgery after his brilliant start to the season, and the diagnosis at the time was that he would miss around two months of action.

The Wolves have had their problems without their talisman, but for the most part, they have done really well thanks to LaMelo Ball, Ayo Dosunmu and the others.

That has convinced Edwards that he should change his game once he returns.

Many people expected the Minnesota Timberwolves to collapse when Edwards was ruled out for a couple of months.

The Wolves lost to the Golden State Warriors and the San Antonio Spurs in back-to-back regular season games after their star man's surgery, which was really worrying.

However, since then, Minnesota have won seven of their nine games and are looking good, despite the narrow defeat on Thursday to the New York Knicks.

Edwards can't wait to get back in action, but having watched the team from the sidelines, he feels he needs to tweak his game.

He said in a press conference: "I'm thirsty to come back, but not super fast because of the way they are playing. I don't want to take them out of rhythm.

"I told them, when I come back, I'm not going to try to be who I usually am, try to ball hog and take all the shots.

"I'm just going to fit in where I get in. Take a couple catch-and-shoots. If they need me, I'm there. But I ain't going to do too much."

Edwards definitely has a point there. The Timberwolves are working really well as a team and are getting the job done more often than not.

In an ideal world, nobody would want to disrupt that, but Edwards can take the team to a whole new level if he is his usual self.

His stats in the four games he played this season prove that.

133 points in 135 minutes, at an average of 33.3 points per game, is an outrageous return for any player.

That is why Edwards should be his usual self instead of changing. Scoring over 30 points in a game will make a much bigger difference than just being a team player for the sake of it.

It remains to be seen what he will do, but if it were up to us, we wouldn't change anything.
