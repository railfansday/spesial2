Big blow for Bangladesh! Star player ruled out of second Test against South Africa

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Bangladesh have suffered a major setback ahead of the second Test against South Africa, with experienced batter Mushfiqur Rahim ruled out of the series finale due to a groin injury.The second Test is scheduled to begin at Centurion on November 23, with Bangladesh needing a victory to level the two-match series after going 1-0 down in Johannesburg.Mushfiqur sustained the injury during the opening Test at the Wanderers, where he complained of acute pain in his left groin. A subsequent MRI scan confirmed inflammation and irritation around the bony pelvis, forcing the team management to rule him out of the upcoming contest.Bangladesh national team physiotherapist Bayjedul Islam Khan confirmed the development, explaining that the veteran batter needs immediate rest and a rehabilitation programme to prevent the injury from worsening."Mushfiqur complained of acute pain in the left groin region during the first Test match. Following the Test, he underwent an MRI scan and the results confirmed some inflammation and irritation around the bony pelvis," Khan said."He requires immediate rest and targeted rehabilitation to manage the stress response and prevent further aggravation and is unavailable for the second Test," he added.Mushfiqur's absence comes at a difficult time for Bangladesh, who will be keen to bounce back after suffering a nine-wicket defeat in the series opener. The veteran batter had scored 22 in the first innings and 22 in the second innings of the Johannesburg Test.Meanwhile, South Africa have also been dealt an injury setback, with captain Temba Bavuma ruled out of the second Test due to a calf strain. Bavuma is expected to remain with the squad, while Aiden Markram is likely to take over the captaincy. Tony de Zorzi is tipped to come into the playing XI as his replacement.

The opening Test at the Wanderers saw Bangladesh produce a spirited fightback after an early batting collapse. From 22/3 and later 121/5, they recovered through centuries from Towhid Hridoy (137) and Litton Das (120), who put together a record 206-run partnership for the sixth wicket to take the visitors to 400.South Africa responded with a formidable 557, powered by maiden Test centuries from Marques Ackerman (157) and Ryan Rickelton (102), while Zubayr Hamza contributed 83 and Kagiso Rabada struck a career-best 72.Bangladesh fought back with the ball, but South Africa secured a sizeable 157-run first-innings advantage.In their second innings, the visitors struggled against Rabada and Simon Harmer, eventually being bowled out for 168. Harmer was the chief destroyer with figures of 6/51, while Rabada claimed three wickets.South Africa were left with a modest target of 12 runs and completed the chase to register a nine-wicket victory, taking a 1-0 lead in the series.
