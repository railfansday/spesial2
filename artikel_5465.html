Nauticus Robotics, Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results and Advances Defense Strategy

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Revenue Growth, Expanded Defense Engagement, and UAE Manufacturing Progress Highlight Third Quarter

HOUSTON, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Nauticus Robotics, Inc. ("Nauticus" or "Company") (NASDAQ: KITT), a leading innovator in subsea robotics and software, today announced its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

John Gibson, Nauticus President and CEO, stated, "During the third quarter, we delivered improved financial performance while continuing to advance our commercial and technology initiatives. We are particularly encouraged by growing opportunities in defense, where our AquanautÂ® platform and Nauticus ToolKITTâ¢ software align with evolving mission requirements. With continued progress on our UAE manufacturing facility and expanding customer engagement, we remain focused on converting these opportunities into sustainable growth."

During the third quarter, Nauticus supported three offshore wind and subsea cable-laying projects, including two with existing customers and one with a new customer. The Company also completed a cable-laying project offshore California and continues pursuing additional West Coast and international opportunities.

Nauticus maintained its flexible operating model, aligning personnel and equipment with project requirements to manage costs while preserving the ability to respond to customer demand.

The Company remains focused on securing larger, longer-duration contracts to improve revenue visibility and reduce exposure to seasonal fluctuations in offshore activity.

Nauticus advanced its defense business development efforts through increased engagement with the U.S. Navy, allied naval organizations, and established defense contractors.

These discussions are progressing toward specific operational requirements, technical evaluations, and potential contract pathways, with particular emphasis on mine countermeasures, seabed security, and critical infrastructure protection.

The Company believes its AquanautÂ® autonomous underwater vehicle and Nauticus ToolKITTâ¢ software platform are well suited to support evolving defense requirements and provide opportunities for demonstrations, strategic partnerships, and future funded programs.

Nauticus made progress toward establishing its manufacturing and operations center in the United Arab Emirates. The Company has selected a facility and is finalizing the required regulatory approvals.

The planned facility is expected to support the manufacturing, integration, and deployment of Aquanaut vehicles for defense and commercial demonstrations while strengthening the Company's regional operating capabilities.

Nauticus continued demonstrating the capabilities of Nauticus ToolKITTâ¢ through its integration with the Comanche ROV system. Ongoing customer operations have provided additional field experience and demonstrated improvements in vehicle stability, operating efficiency, and data quality.

The Company also advanced AquanautÂ® mission capabilities, with increased emphasis on mine detection and identification applications for defense customers.

Development of Nauticus' next-generation fully electric manipulators continued through prototype testing, software enhancements, and design improvements in preparation for in-water testing and future integration with Aquanaut.

As Nauticus approaches 2027, the Company is focused on expanding its portfolio of longer-duration offshore contracts, advancing defense opportunities, and strengthening its international commercial presence.

Management believes growing interest in autonomous subsea defense capabilities, combined with continued technology development and the planned UAE manufacturing facility, creates additional opportunities to leverage its existing technology investments.

The Company remains focused on translating customer engagement, operational performance, and technology advancements into sustainable revenue opportunities while maintaining financial and operating discipline.

Revenue: Nauticus reported third-quarter revenue of $2.2 million, compared to $2.0 million for the prior-year period and $0.9 million for the prior quarter.

Operating Expenses: Total expenses during the second quarter were $6.5 million, a $1.4 million decrease from the prior-year period and a $0.4 million decrease from Q2 2026.

Adjusted Net Loss: Nauticus reported adjusted net loss of $5.3 million for the third quarter, compared to an adjusted net loss of $6.8 million for the same period in 2025 and an adjusted net loss of $6.9 million for Q2 2026. Adjusted net loss is a non-GAAP measure which excludes the impact of certain items, as shown in the non-GAAP reconciliation table below.

Net Loss: For the third quarter, Nauticus recorded a net loss of $4.9 million, or basic loss per share of $10.8. This compares with a net loss of $6.6 million from the same period in 2025, and a net loss of $11.1 million in the prior quarter.

G&A Cost: Nauticus reported G&A third-quarter costs of $2.7 million, which is a decrease of $0.3 million compared to the same period in 2025 and a $0.6 million decrease from the second quarter in 2026.

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash of $5.6 million, compared to $7.6 million as of December 31, 2025.

Nauticus Robotics, Inc. develops autonomous robots for the ocean industries. Autonomy requires the extensive use of sensors, artificial intelligence, and effective algorithms for perception and decision-making allowing the robot to adapt to changing environments. The company's business model includes using robotic systems for service, selling vehicles and components, and licensing of related software to both the commercial and defense business sectors. Nauticus has designed and is currently testing and certifying a new generation of vehicles to reduce operational cost and gather data to maintain and operate a wide variety of subsea infrastructure. Besides a standalone service offering and forward-facing products, Nauticus' approach to ocean robotics has also resulted in the development of a range of technology products for retrofit/upgrading traditional ROV operations and other third-party vehicle platforms. Nauticus' services provide customers with the necessary data collection, analytics, and subsea manipulation capabilities to support and maintain assets while reducing their operational footprint, operating cost, and greenhouse gas emissions, to improve offshore health, safety, and environmental exposure. www.nauticusrobotics.com
