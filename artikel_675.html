‘Presumed Innocent’ Season 2 Pairs Rachel Brosnahan With Matthew Rhys in First Look at Next Courtroom Face-Off

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Apple TV anthology series returns February 2027 with the Emmy winners at the helm.

Matthew Rhys and Rachel Brosnan are a married couple in court — but not for a divorce — in the first look teaser for Apple TV’s Presumed Innocent second season to premiere Feb. 19, 2027.

The Emmy award winners are legal eagles clashing over a murder case in the anthology series’ sophomore season. Brosnahan plays up-and-coming defense attorney Leila Reynolds facing off in court against her district attorney husband, played by Rhys, in a murder case that threatens to destroy both their careers and their marriage, according to a synopsis from the producers. The first season of Presumed Innocent was based on Scott Turow’s novel of the same name (previously adapted for a feature film starring Harrison Ford) and starred Jake Gyllenhaal, Ruth Negga and Peter Sarsgaard. In renewing the show, Apple TV+ and the show’s creative team, which includes executive producers David E. Kelley and J.J. Abrams, opted to make the series an anthology.

Apple TV is keeping plot details about season two mostly under wraps. Publisher Pan Macmillan’s description of Dissection of a Murder says the story will follow an attorney (Brosnahan) who is defending a man accused of murdering a judge.

Courtney B. Vance and Fiona Shaw also feature in the short teaser for the Emmy Award-nominated drama based on Dissection of a Murder legal thriller novel by Jo Murray. The second season ensemble cast includes Jack Reynor, Lesley-Ann Brandt and Judith Light as a guest star.

That star casting isn’t surprising as Presumed Innocent, from showrunner David E. Kelley and executive producer J.J. Abrams, during its first season earned Emmy nominations for Gyllenhaal, Bill Camp, Sarsgaard and Negga in varied acting categories.

Brosnahan is known for her Emmy-winning star turn on The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel, while Rhys stars in Apple’s popular Widow’s Bay comedy, about the residents of a seemingly cursed New England seaside town, and earned two lead acting awards at this year’s Emmy Awards.

Kelley, Abrams share executive producer credits on Presumed Innocent season two with Rachel Rusch Rich, Dee Johnson, Dustin Thomason, Brosnahan, Scott Turow and Gyllenhaal, after he starred in the first season. The opening two episodes will debut on Feb. 19, with weekly releases to follow until April 2.
