No, the Rams didn’t run at Micah Parsons

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

For the most part, the Green Bay Packers' defense has been solid this season, which is one reason why it was surprising that the Los Angeles Rams were able to run the ball at will against Green Bay on Thanksgiving Eve. This also happened to be the first game that edge defender Micah Parsons returned to the lineup for in 2026, so some people (incorrectly) are lining up dots and saying that the Rams ran at Parsons throughout the game.

I'm here to tell you that this narrative is simply not true.

The video above includes all of Parsons' snaps against the run on Wednesday (13 of them). Maybe twice he was run at in the entire game.

Was the Packers' run defense poor? Yes. Was it because of Parsons? No.

The bigger issue against the Rams was that the interior defensive line was getting pushed back consistently (even against a backup center and backup left guard), and the off-ball linebackers (both backups inserted into the lineup due to injury) had bad nights, too.
