Rookie Yaxel Lendeborg's maturation with Steph Curry out evaluated by Steve Kerr

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Rookie Yaxel Lendeborg's maturation with Steph Curry out evaluated by Steve Kerr originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area

The Warriors are in the midst of a rough stretch.

Golden State started the 2026-27 NBA season at 0-3 before going on an eight-game winning streak. But star guard Steph Curry suffered an injury in a Nov. 11 game against the Houston Rockets, and the team lost five straight before beating the Boston Celtics on Sunday at Chase Center.

The Warriors need to get by without Curry, forcing players like rookie Yaxel Lendeborg to step up. The former Michigan Wolverine star's debut NBA season has been a mixed bag, but he has shown promise.

Coach Steve Kerr joined 95.7 The Game's "Willard & Dibs" on Tuesday and evaluated Lendeborg's maturation throughout the season, especially in Curry's absence.

"It's a great learning process for him," Kerr told Mark Willard and Dan Dibley. "It's very different when Steph's not out there and he's going through it in terms of having the best defenders on him and having defenses prepared for him. So it's a different scenario."

Lendeborg, who has played in all 17 games so far this season, was added to the injury report with an illness ahead of Wednesday night's game against the Sacramento Kings. He missed Tuesday's practice and while Kerr is hopeful he recovers in time to contribute, the team is taking a cautious approach.

"We don't know," Kerr told Willard and Dibley. "He missed practice [Tuesday]; the flu bug is going around so we're not exactly sure what's going to happen but hopefully we have him. We're going to need him but we'll wait and see for tomorrow [Wednesday]."

Lendeborg, the No. 11 overall pick in the 2026 NBA Draft, is averaging 19.2 points, 7.4 rebounds and 3.6 assists per game and is climbing in the NBA Rookie of the Year odds. He was a focal point on Michigan's national championship team last season.

Lendeborg is 24 and on the older side for a rookie but he entered the league as a more polished prospect. He played six seasons of college basketball — three at Arizona Western College, two at UAB and one at Michigan.

The Warriors and Kings face off in the NBA Cup Group Stage at 7 p.m. PT at Chase Center on NBC Sports Bay Area. Golden State is 1-1 in the tournament, while Sacramento is 0-3.
