France has its sights set on reclaiming top spot in its UEFA Nations League group as it hosts Turkey on Sunday.

Zinedine Zidane is targeting a swift response to his first defeat as France manager when Les Bleus host Turkey in the UEFA Nations League on Sunday. The two-time world champions suffered a convincing 3-1 defeat at Italy's hands on Thursday, which means France must now better the Azzurri's result on the last matchday to stand any chance of finishing first in Group A1.

The pressure is on for Zidane after third-place Belgium came from behind to beat Turkey 2-1 on Matchday 5, leaving it just one point behind the top pair in the pool. That also means anything but a win over Turkey in Bordeaux leaves France open to slipping to third and out of the running for a spot in the quarterfinals.

The situation is considerably bleaker for Sergen Yalcin's men, who will finish last in the group regardless of Sunday's results. However, the Crescent Stars can still salvage their future in the top flight of the Nations League, where three points could mean the difference between automatic relegation and a spot in the playoffs.

Zidane may look to change up his options ahead of the Group A1 climax, with the likes of Eli Junior Kroupi, Ibrahima Konate and Warren Zaire-Emery in contention to start. Hoffenheim defender Ozan Kabak will miss the visit to France through suspension after he was booked in the loss to Belgium, while Hakan Calhanoglu and Zalih Ozcan could come into the XI after substitute cameos in Izmir.
