ESPN Pundit sets the record straight on his opinion of Oklahoma

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

SEC Network's Paul Finebaum is never shy about giving his opinion on teams, players, and coaches. This season, he's let his feelings known about the Oklahoma Sooners.

This offseason, Finebaum was asked what top-10 team he thinks will finish the season unranked. He picked the Sooners, and for much of the season, it looked like he would be right on that prediction. Then this past weekend, he got into a back-and-forth with Oklahoma athletic director Roger Denny over Denny's comments about the SEC moving its game against the Ole Miss Rebels to Sunday at noon instead of at 3:15 p.m. CT on Saturday due to travel issues.

However, Finebaum has since set the record straight on his opinion of Oklahoma.

"On your show, not long after you started, I came on and unloaded on Lincoln Riley for leaving OU, and that's why it came up again (Tuesday) on First Take about how badly you've done," Finebaum said on the 'Dari Nowkhah Show.' "So if somebody wants to, you know, say that I've got an axe to grind with OU or if I'm taking orders from somebody, that's fine. It doesn't really bother me, and I'm not being standoffish about it. But I think my record on OU is pretty clear."

Finebaum's history of commentary on Oklahoma has included plenty of criticism, but he believes his overall record shows that his opinions are not driven by any personal issues with the program. His praise following the win over Ole Miss also suggests he is willing to give the Sooners credit when they earn it. After predicting a difficult season for Oklahoma, he has acknowledged the team's recent turnaround and the optimism building around the program.

With two striaght wins over ranked opponents, Oklahoma has given its fans plenty of reasons to feel better about where the season is headed.
