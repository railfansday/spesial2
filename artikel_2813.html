NFC West Recap: 49ers stay atop NFC; Seahawks, Rams win ugly

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The San Francisco 49ers reached the halfway point with a 38-17 win over the Las Vegas Raiders, and for a hot second, it had the familiar 49ers play-with-their-food game feel. Then, thanks to a bad snap, Raiders quarterback Kirk Cousins opened the floodgates (and the second half) with this:

From there, the 49ers manhandled the Raiders. Yes, the Raiders scored another touchdown, but the 49ers were not blowing this lead. The win puts the 49ers at the No. 1 seed in the NFC, a half-game ahead of the Minnesota Vikings (6-1). They also lead the Seattle Seahawks and Los Angeles Rams by 2.5 games in the NFC West. Since both of those teams won, the lead stayed put, but the 49ers took care of business.

It came at a cost. Defensive tackle Gracen Halton broke his ankle and is out for the season. Cornerback Renardo Green also left with a back injury. Just when the pieces start coming back…

So that Minshew Magic we talked about last week? Well, it turns out that only lasts a single game.

Last Sunday, quarterback Gardner Minshew II came off the bench in Dallas with 1:35 left in the third quarter and the Cardinals down 17-3. He threw three fourth-quarter touchdown passes, won in overtime, and took the starting job. Like the Dallas game, Minshew found himself down 17-3, and unlike the Dallas game, we didn't get any fourth-quarter theatrics. We didn't even get a single touchdown. Instead, the Cardinals sat at three points.

That might be it for Minshew Mania, Magic, whatever we want to call it when he actually scores at least one touchdown.

Seattle had their own offensive issues. With Sam Darnold not suited for this game due to injury, Drew Lock helmed the offense despite an elbow injury that had kept him inactive the previous two weeks and a hip issue that popped up in the third quarter. An offense that got only 17 points. Right, the Cardinals, who have their own litany of issues, held the Seahawks to that. Drew Lock, or no Drew Lock, that seems a bit small, but the Seahawks found a way to win.

Minshew went 21-of-30 for 166 yards passing with no touchdowns and was sacked three times. Running back Tyler Allgeier ran for 147 yards in Dallas and managed 46 on 17 carries here. Meanwhile, Lock didn't turn the ball over and finished 19-of-28 for 175 yards and a touchdown.

The NFC West. Those games are slugfests. This one was a slugfest with 180 yards of penalties between the two teams. I guess it was entertaining?
