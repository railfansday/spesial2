Vincent Kompany sees opportunity during crisis at Bayern Munich

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Bayern Munich are hit by a storm of injuries and the going will get tough for the German giants. Manager Vincent Kompany is placing the focus on the opportunity arising out of this crisis to motivate his team to keep the show going.

After the injuries to Harry Kane and Michael Olise against 1. FC Köln, FC Bayern are missing six attackers! Olise is likely to return early but Kane is out for months. Considering Olise does return shortly, he will be aided by rookie Lennart Karl, left-back Nathaniel Brown and the magical Jamal Musiala. Karl has had only one season of senior team experience and Musiala's fitness is not a guarantee. That leaves Bayern with one true full time senior attacker in Michael Olise (if he can get fully healthy in time for the next game, which is expected). One!

Kompany has been stoic to this crisis and chooses to see the opportunity rally the youth players to rise to the occasion. Youngsters Tim Binder and Noah Codjo-Evora saw time against Cologne. Expect more of the same in the upcoming weeks as Bayern battles with its most intense injury periods in recent times.

"We have other players now who need to step up and carry the team. We need incredible performances from players who perhaps aren't yet used to having this kind of responsibility at Bayern. But that's the situation we're in. Together, we'll try to turn this situation into something positive for us. That's what FC Bayern has always done, and that's what we want to do again now," said Kompany (as captured by @iMiaSanMia).
