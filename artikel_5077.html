Shiba Inu's recovery is followed by a substantial 42% increase in transaction volumes. The on-chain data coincides with the formation of a U-shape bottom, which might become a springboard for the recovery.

The worst part about the current retrace are underlying market metrics like volume which has been showing a descending dynamic in the last 3 days. The descending volume is a sign of a fading momentum on the market.

The steady increase from approximately 4,200 transactions to more than 6,000 raises a few flags: users or investors are actively moving their funds, exchange operations are also on the rise. With the rise of exchange activity, volatility quite often follows.

WIth the increase in total exchange reserves to 85.27 trillion SHIB, the most likely answer is accumulation: transactional activity increase, exchange inflows and growing exchange supply are all reflecting an active funds concentration phase, rather than anything else.

The brief drop to $0.0000034 triggered a substantial reversal towards $0.00000372. As SHIB reached an extremely oversold state based on momentum indicators, the price successfully rebounded.

The price bounce is a reflection of a bullish presence around key levels here, despite the short-term picture being rather bearish.

Nearest moving averages will become main resistance points here and might create serious barriers for local recoveries.

As for now, 50 and 200 EMAs, which are considered long-term price levels, are unreachable for SHIB. It would require a 17% increase in price in the next 6 days in order to hit those moving averages and try to conquer higher levels.

Shiba Inu's price has been struggling a lot, the U-shaped recovery is somewhat a hope for investors, but it's certainly not a guarantee of a proper retrace, especially if we take into consideration factors like descending volume, exchange inflows and rich presence of resistance barriers.
