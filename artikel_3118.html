GS BDC (GSBD) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Leverage fell as nonaccrual loans rose and management defended selective lending.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Goldman Sachs BDC, Inc. (GSBD -0.91%) reported third quarter results characterized by a strategic reduction in leverage and the ongoing rotation of its investment portfolio. Management reported a decline in net asset value driven by company-specific challenges in legacy positions, even as the company successfully reduced its net debt-to-equity ratio to 1.21 times. The company prioritized capital recycling and share repurchases, executing $15 million in buybacks while maintaining a high concentration of senior secured debt. Executives stated that the current liability structure and liquidity levels provide the flexibility needed to remain selective in a competitive credit market while working to maximize recoveries on nonaccrual investments.

This broadly syndicated activity is healthy for the market overall, and we compete well when sponsors are seeking certainty of execution, the ability to underwrite a full capital structure, and a lender with deep industry expertise. A good example this quarter was a sponsor-backed acquisition of an industrial services business, where our platform served as administrative agent and the largest lender in the financing.

The opportunity came through collaboration with the broader GS ecosystem, and our ability to make a large early commitment gave the sponsor certainty of execution, which positioned us to lead the transaction. The company provides non-discretionary services that help protect and extend the life of critical industrial assets. During the quarter, we made new investment commitments of approximately $41 million across 14 portfolio companies, seven of which were new borrowers to GSBD.
