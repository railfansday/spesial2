Product(RED) HomePod mini 2 seemingly surfaces ahead of rumored announcement [U]

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Earlier this week, Bloomberg reported that Apple was gearing up to launch Product(RED) versions of select products, including the HomePod mini 2. However, a retail store may have inadvertently put the new model on display ahead of its official announcement. Here are the details.

On Monday, Bloomberg reported that Apple was planning to launch new Product(RED) versions of AirPods Max 2 and the HomePod mini 2.

Soon after, Gurman shared on X that Apple had “also developed a red AirPods Wrist Strap and red Apple Watch Sport Band.”

And while Gurman said Apple was planning to release the new products “on or around” December 1, which is World AIDS Day, it appears that at least one retail store may have jumped the gun.

In a seemingly now-deleted post that Gurman quoted on X, user @s_s_s_t_x appears to have published an image of the retail packaging for the red HomePod mini 2. Gurman then published what may be the same image, showing the box sitting on a shelf alongside another HomePod mini box.

https://t.co/V2C3Fgkh4u pic.twitter.com/0bgZQppOk6

In the image, the front of the box depicts a HomePod mini 2 with a red mesh exterior and a white touch surface, while the side features the product name in red, with what appears to be a small (RED) logo just below it.

There are no details as to where the picture was taken, though the X user’s profile lists “Aus10, Texas,” as their location. Apple currently operates two retail stores in Austin: Domain NORTHSIDE and Barton Creek, though it is entirely possible that the picture was taken at a third-party retailer.

As 9to5Mac reported earlier this week, Apple has also developed a Product(RED) version of the AirTag, though it remains to be seen whether the company will announce it alongside the new products seemingly coming next week.
