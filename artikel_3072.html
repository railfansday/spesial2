Data Storage Corp (DTST) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Revenue fell 10.3% as customers shifted to cheaper services and cash stayed strong.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Data Storage Corporation (DTST -7.96%) reported third-quarter results for 2026, focusing on the development of its new AI continuity platform and the ongoing operations of its Nexus subsidiary. Management reported that the company is leveraging its history in disaster recovery to build a platform designed to detect failures and orchestrate recovery for AI workloads in regulated industries. The company stated it maintains a debt-free balance sheet with $9.5 million in liquidity to support organic growth and potential cybersecurity acquisitions. CEO Chuck Piluso indicated that the company is being selective in its M&A strategy, seeking targets that require working capital while offering software solutions that complement the existing service architecture.

Our platform is being designed to detect failures, maintain secure replicas and orchestrate recovery using GPU resources activated on demand.
