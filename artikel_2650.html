Newcastle United Enter Race For 21-Year-Old Defender: Can They Beat Liverpool?

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Newcastle United have entered a crowded race for Lens centre-back Ismaelo Ganiou, according to an exclusive from Pete O'Rourke in Football Insider and its Transfer Insider podcast. The 21-year-old also attracts Liverpool, Chelsea, Manchester City, Tottenham, Paris Saint-Germain, Monaco and Marseille, and nobody leads clearly.

Ganiou rejected Crystal Palace, managed by his former Lens coach Pierre Sage, and signed a contract until 2030 in August. A January move looks unlikely because he is recovering from knee surgery until late November. Football Insider expects serious bidding next summer, when Newcastle hope to beat Liverpool by offering a bigger first-team role immediately.

Ganiou is 6ft 3in, is right-footed, can cover at right-back, and can be compared with Raphael Varane. FotMob's figures from five Ligue 1 starts support the praise: 88% pass accuracy, nine of 14 aerial duels won and 22 clearances in 433 minutes, plus one goal. FotMob values him at roughly £31m.

The concerns are availability and physicality. Five starts is a small sample; the knee operation interrupts his season, and Premier League strikers will test his physicality.

Jaissle's front-foot approach needs defenders who recover quickly, yet Newcastle have 12 points from 10 league games and have conceded 16 goals. Dan Burn and Fabian Schar are both 34, and Schar's contract expires in June 2027. Sven Botman signed until 2030 in January, but he had missed 58 games in his first three and a half years at the club.

They can, provided the starting place is genuine. Liverpool, Chelsea and PSG offer bigger stages, but none can promise a 21-year-old regular football the way Newcastle can with two 34-year-olds in the back line and a defence leaking 1.6 goals a game, including four at both Leeds and Coventry. Schar's deal ends next summer, so Ganiou would arrive just as a vacancy opens.

The doubts are medical and financial. Newcastle spent about £275m on eight signings this summer, including £51m on Matias Fernandez-Pardo, and L'Equipe reported that Lens wanted at least £43m (€50m) when Ganiou renewed. Paying that for a defender coming off knee surgery demands a full medical review and a careful plan for his minutes.

If the knee checks out in full, Ganiou solves a problem Jaissle has not yet fixed. A high line needs centre-backs who turn and recover, and neither Burn nor Schar will get quicker.
