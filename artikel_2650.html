Commanders QB Jayden Daniels hits a pair of big passes to set up game-winning field goal to beat Bengals

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

It looked like Joe Burrow might have added to his MVP résumé by leading a game-winning drive. But Jayden Daniels got the last word.

After the Cincinnati Bengals kicked a field goal with 46 seconds left to take a 20-19 lead, Daniels hit two huge passes downfield to get the Washington Commanders in range for a short field goal and the win. Drew Stevens made the kick and Washington escaped with a 22-20 win.

The Bengals had a shot to end the game but couldn't run more time off the clock after getting in field-goal range. That allowed Daniels a shot. He hit rookie Antonio Williams for 22 yards and then 42 yards to set up the win on Stevens' 26-yard field goal.

Burrow had taken over as the MVP favorite before Monday night's game. But Daniels has had moments as a special quarterback, too, and he and Williams had a magical couple of plays to pull out a dramatic win.
