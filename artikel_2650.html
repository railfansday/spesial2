Sule strikes late as Scotland U21s snatch draw against USA

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Eseosa Sule came off the bench to score a late penalty as Scotland Under-21s wrapped up their Spanish training camp with a 1-1 draw against the United States Under-23 side in Murcia.

St Mirren striker Sule slotted home with six minutes remaining after Sam Chambers was upended in the box by Andre Gitau.

That cancelled out Frankie Westfield's early opener, the Philadelphia Union full-back firing a smart finish across Rory Mahady to give the Americans a 14th minute lead.

The result at the Pinatar Arena capped an excellent week's work for Tony Docherty's young Scots, following their emphatic 4-0 win over Ukraine on Thursday.

Manchester United midfielder Tyler Fletcher captained a strong Scottish side that also featured Udinese's Lennon Miller, Kyle Ure of Crewe Alexandra, and Hearts' forward James Wilson.
