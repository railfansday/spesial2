Texas high school changes football workouts after players are hospitalized

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Several Texas high school football players were hospitalized after an offseason workout last week, with one reportedly requiring intensive care.

A Chron report said six players from Frenship Memorial High School in Lubbock were hospitalized with symptoms of rhabdomyolysis, a potentially life-threatening condition caused by the breakdown of muscle tissue. Amanda Blacklund, a physician at Lubbock Primary Care Center, told the outlet that six players were admitted for treatment, including one reportedly in intensive care.

In a statement included in the report, Frenship ISD confirmed that several players continue to receive treatment and said district officials are investigating what may have contributed to their conditions.

The district also reported the situation to Child Protective Services for investigation.

Frenship ISD has since reduced the intensity of its offseason football workouts and expanded education for coaches and players about hydration, nutrition, rest and recognizing symptoms of rhabdomyolysis.

The condition occurs when damaged muscle tissue releases substances into the bloodstream, potentially causing severe kidney damage or failure. It can be triggered by extreme physical exertion.

Frenship Memorial, which opened in 2025, is coached by Jason Richards. The Panthers finished their 2026 football season with a 2-8 record.
