Steelers playoff odds skyrocket after Ravens loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Pittsburgh Steelers may have lost in embarrassing fashion to the Cincinnati Bengals on Sunday night, but the Los Angeles Chargers did them a massive favor.

After the Chargers defeated the Baltimore Ravens, the Steelers now sit at a 76 percent chance to make the playoffs, per Derrick Bell of Steelers Now. The most likely outcome is that the Steelers are a wild card team, with there being an 11 percent chance they host that game as AFC North winners. Pittsburgh has just a 24 percent chance to miss the postseason.
