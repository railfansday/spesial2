Spectrum Brands is pushing to buy Helen of Troy for $37 a share

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Its Empower Brands unit, co-owned with Oaktree, made a non-binding offer that Helen of Troy's management called inadequate

Spectrum Brands is proposing to acquire Helen of Troy for $37 a share in cash through Empower Brands, its home and personal care venture with Oaktree Capital Management, the company said Wednesday.

Spectrum Brands said in a regulatory filing that Empower Brands delivered a non-binding proposal to Helen of Troy’s board to buy all of its outstanding common equity.

The price is a 28% premium to Helen of Troy's closing stock price on Oct. 30, Spectrum said. It is a 31% premium to the 30-day volume-weighted average price and a 16% premium to the 52-week high of $31.98. Helen of Troy stock closed Tuesday at $29.54.

Spectrum said the offer has no financing contingency and is backed by capital from Empower Brands, Spectrum Brands and Oaktree. A deal depends on a definitive agreement and customary closing conditions, including approval from Helen of Troy shareholders and regulators. Spectrum said it could finish due diligence and a merger agreement in about 45 days.

Spectrum said it made several attempts to engage privately with Helen of Troy's board. Empower Brands met with Helen of Troy management on July 27 and delivered an initial proposal of $34 a share on Aug. 19. Helen of Troy rejected that offer on Sept. 3, and Spectrum said it received no response to a Sept. 17 request for a discussion between financial advisers.

Empower Brands raised its offer to $37 on Oct. 12. On Oct. 20, Helen of Troy management called the increased proposal inadequate, Spectrum said. The next day, Helen of Troy's financial adviser, Bank of America $BAC, told Empower's adviser, UBS, that any information sharing would require a confidentiality agreement limiting Empower's ability to disclose its interest and proposal.

"Rather than substantively engaging with us about a transaction, Helen of Troy has attempted to halt any process by requiring us to enter into an overly restrictive agreement," Spectrum Brands CEO David Maura said, according to Bloomberg. "We remain willing to enter into a constructive dialogue that could potentially support an improved proposal and lead to consummating a friendly transaction," Maura said. A Helen of Troy spokesperson had not yet replied when asked for comment.

Spectrum asked Helen of Troy shareholders to contact the board and urge it to engage. The company said Helen of Troy stock has fallen 87% over five years as of Oct. 30 and that the company recorded impairment charges of $886 million in fiscal 2026. Spectrum said a combination would create a business with about $2.9 billion in revenue over the past 12 months.

The all-cash offer is valued at about $866 million, according to Bloomberg. Helen of Troy sells home, outdoor and beauty products under brands including OXO, Revlon, Hydro Flask and Osprey.
