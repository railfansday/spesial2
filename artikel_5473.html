In the news release, Umbra to Launch Next Phase of Constellation Expansion as Newest Spacecraft Deliver First Imagery, issued 16-Nov-2026 by Umbra over PR Newswire, we are advised by the company that changes have been made. The complete, corrected release follows, with additional details at the end:

Rapid delivery of first imagery, paired with secured launch capacity, strengthens Umbra's ability to accelerate mission demonstrations, deploy advanced capabilities, and respond to evolving U.S. defense and international customer needs.

SANTA BARBARA, Calif., Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Umbra, an American space technology company, today announced it will launch its next seven spacecraft starting in 2027, advancing its constellation expansion to support growing U.S. defense, national security, and intelligence needs, as well as critical missions worldwide.

The announcement follows a series of successful spacecraft deployments for Umbra's third-generation platform, beginning with Umbra-12 in early 2026 and continuing with Umbra-13 and Umbra-14, which launched aboard the Bandwagon-5 rideshare mission with SpaceX on October 20, 2026, and have already delivered their first high-resolution synthetic aperture radar (SAR) imagery. Umbra-14 delivered its first image on October 24, followed by Umbra-13 on October 28. These initial images demonstrate the capabilities of Umbra's newest spacecraft and reflect the company's continued investment in mission-driven space systems designed to deliver timely, actionable intelligence, greater persistence, and increased operational value for U.S. defense and intelligence customers.

Together, these milestones underscore Umbra's progress in expanding its on-orbit capabilities and delivering greater intelligence value per spacecraft.

"Every mission challenges us to improve on the last, from the spacecraft we build to how quickly we deliver the first image," said David Langan, Chief Executive Officer of Umbra. "The pace at which our team brought Umbra-13 and Umbra-14 through early operations and into imaging is a testament to the relentless improvement our people bring to their work every day. We design our spacecraft to be compatible with multiple launch vehicles, giving us the flexibility to secure access to space and respond to evolving mission requirements. With launch capacity secured for our next seven spacecraft, we're positioned to build on that momentum, expand our constellation, and deliver greater intelligence value to our customers."

Umbra-15, Umbra-16, Umbra-17, and Umbra-18 are scheduled to launch on the Bandwagon-6 rideshare mission with SpaceX no earlier than April 2027. Umbra-19, Umbra-20, and another spacecraft, supporting a private demonstration for a strategic industry partner, are scheduled to launch on the SEOPS Waymaker-1 dedicated rideshare mission no earlier than April 2028. All seven spacecraft will launch into mid-inclination orbits, aligning Umbra's future capacity with customer demand.

These missions with SpaceX and SEOPS will build upon Umbra's established relationship with the companies at a time of acute demand for access to space. With these commitments in place, Umbra can align spacecraft production and deployment around a defined launch schedule through 2028, supporting continued investment in the capabilities its customers need.

This long-term visibility supports Umbra's approach to redefining space, which centers on multi-mission capability and vertical integration to expand what each satellite can do. By connecting spacecraft development, production, and operations, the company applies lessons from each mission to the next, improving performance, strengthening resilience, and building systems that can adapt to evolving customer requirements.
