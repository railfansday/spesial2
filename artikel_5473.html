Thomson Reuters updated its full-year forecast to exclude print business as revenue climbed 10%

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The company's updated 2026 outlook excludes Global Print; adjusted earnings rose to 81 cents a share from 71 cents

Thomson Reuters reported third-quarter revenue growth of 10% on Tuesday and updated its 2026 outlook to exclude its Global Print business.

The updated outlook calls for total revenue growth of about 9.5%, organic revenue growth of 8.5% to 9.0% and an adjusted EBITDA margin of about 39%, the company said. The August outlook, which included Global Print, projected total and organic revenue growth of about 8.0%. Guidance for the "Big 3" segments is unchanged from August: total and organic revenue growth of 9.5% to 10.0% and a margin up 100 basis points from 2025.

The company said it expects fourth-quarter organic revenue growth of about 8% and an adjusted EBITDA margin of about 42%. It also said third-quarter revenue growth and margins came in ahead of its prior expectations.

Organic revenue rose 10%, as did organic revenue at the Big 3 segments: Legal Professionals, Corporates, and Tax, Audit & Accounting Professionals. Recurring revenue, which makes up 88% of the total, grew 9%, and transactions revenue grew 27%. Revenue at the news segment rose 12%, driven mainly by higher generative AI content licensing revenue.

Adjusted EBITDA increased 10%, while the margin slipped to 35.5% from 35.8% a year earlier. Adjusted earnings per share rose to 81 cents from 71 cents.

Net earnings declined to $339 million, equal to 78 cents a share, versus $423 million, or 94 cents a share, in the year-earlier period. Operating profit dropped 22%, mainly because the prior-year period included a gain from selling the company's remaining minority stake in the Elite business. Higher software amortization also weighed on operating profit. Free cash flow fell 23%.

Adjusted earnings of 81 cents a share fell short of the 83-cent consensus estimate in a FactSet survey of analysts, according to The Wall Street Journal. Revenue of $1.83 billion was a shade below the average analyst estimate of $1.84 billion.

Thomson Reuters closed the sale of a 51% stake in its Global Print business to capital accounts advised by KKR on Oct. 1 for gross proceeds of about $500 million. The company also issued notes for total proceeds of about $2.0 billion in September and completed a $600 million share repurchase program announced in February.
