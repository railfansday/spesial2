OpenAI is launching a payment wallet to let its AI agents shop for users

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

OpenAI is launching ChatGPT Wallet, a payment tool that lets its AI agents buy items for users with a card saved in the app.

Users add a card one time, then authorize every transaction by verifying their identity, for example with Face ID or a fingerprint scan, the company said. After approval, the payment network provides a token that stands in for the card details, so neither the business nor the agent receives the actual card number. The charge goes to the user's selected card, and users keep the card's rewards and protections for eligible disputes.

OpenAI said it expects users over time to be able to hand purchases fully to an agent by setting parameters such as spending limits, purchase categories or time periods.

Under the wallet terms, the merchant is the seller, and OpenAI is not a bank, card issuer, payment network, lender or merchant for wallet purchases. OpenAI does not hold purchase funds, and users must contact the merchant for refunds or order disputes.

The AI agents are called dots, according to CNBC. OpenAI is not collecting any share of transaction revenue at launch.

"It's going to take time for the ecosystem to build the right infrastructure to be able to make this experience really amazing and seamless across all of their sites," Chaya Nayak, OpenAI's product lead for monetization and payments, said in comments to CNBC.

Luke Gebb, executive vice president and global head of innovation at American Express, told CNBC the deliberately small launch is the correct way to confirm everything is functioning properly.

OpenAI said agentic commerce is in its early days and that some sites may block purchases made through the wallet. It published a Commerce Partner Playbook with guidance for businesses on making product information available, allowing agent access and working with payment and risk providers. Some companies are blocking agents. United Airlines said earlier this week it will not let agents book tickets, while Amazon $AMZN does not allow them to make purchases on its marketplace, according to CNBC.

OpenAI introduced Businesses in ChatGPT in beta on Tuesday, letting companies claim verified profiles and upload product feeds with pricing and availability. OpenAI retired an earlier ChatGPT shopping effort in March that used commission fees and included a checkout cart.

OpenAI said 1.2 billion people use ChatGPT each week.
