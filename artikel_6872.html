Dakota Johnson Wore Cherry Cola Nails, So Now I’m Wearing Cherry Cola Nails

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A syrupy upgrade to your typical burgundy mani.

One of my guilty pleasures is sipping on a large vanilla cherry Coke at the movie theater. I’m really not a big soda drinker, but there’s just something about the combination of a fizzy, cherry-flavored soda, a bucket of popcorn, and a good movie that does it for me. And luckily for me, I’ve recently discovered another way to indulge: cherry cola nails. Yep, my go-to concession stand order is officially making its way to my fingertips, and naturally, I’m eating (or should I say drinking?) it up.

Dakota Johnson was also spotted wearing cherry cola nails earlier this month at a screening of Flesh Impact in Los Angeles, which only further fueled my need to get the look. Similar to syrup nails, these have a glossy finish to them, but are dark, rich, and reddish-brown, just like the classic soda. It’s basically the perfect marriage of two of my favorite fall nail colors, burgundy and chocolate brown. Ahead, everything you need to know about the trend, plus plenty of inspiration for your next nail appointment. Movie theater soda optional... but highly encouraged.

Think of a Cherry Coke: It’s dark brown with touches of red when the light hits just right. And that’s exactly the goal of the manicure version. “Cherry cola nails are the perfect blend of deep burgundy, rich chocolate brown, and a hint of red,” says celebrity nail artist Jacqueline Pham. “Think of that beautiful, dark cherry soda color that looks almost brown in certain lighting but has a cherry red undertone when the light hits.”

They’re also a great way to dress up your nails for the holiday season: “It’s a little sultry, and a fun alternative to your classic burgundy red manicure,” adds Pham. “You can play around with different shades of brown and burgundy and a slight change in either color can create a completely different cherry cola effect.”

Need some ideas of how exactly to incorporate the color onto your fingertips? Firstly, Pham suggests wearing them as a single-color mani—glossy and straightforward with a classic, high shine finish. “It’s timeless, elegant, and looks beautiful on both short and long nails,” she says. She also likes adding a little shimmer too, which you can do with the help of Essie’s Gel Couture Liquid Diamonds in Size Me Up or Color Street’s Nail Strips in Velvet Dahlia. If you’re craving some nail art, you could also do a twist on a classic French manicure with cherry cola tips over a sheer nude base.

First things first, you’ll want to reach for more than one bottle of nail polish. “The secret to achieving the perfect cherry cola shade is layering your colors rather than using just one,” says Pham. “I personally recommend starting with a rich chocolate brown as your first coat, followed by a deep burgundy as your second coat.” This mimics a cherry soda too, with a brown base and a hint of cherry flavor and color. “The brown creates a warm base, while the burgundy adds cherry tint,” explains Pham. After applying a base coat, she says to do “one thin coat of chocolate brown and one thin coat of burgundy, allowing each layer to dry.” After those two coats, she says to “finish with a high gloss top coat to give it that glassy, soda-like shine.”
