Asian stock markets added nearly $1 trillion in value on Tuesday as Nvidia’s decision to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO sparked a strong rally in AI-linked stocks. Nvidia shares jumped as much as 40%, while major chipmakers in South Korea (KOSPI), Japan (Nikkei) and Taiwan (Taiex) gained 8% to 15%.

In a November 23 press release, Nvidia said its board unanimously voted to reinstate Huang after discussions convinced directors of his “candor and ability to lead.” The board also consulted senior US officials and found no national security risk.

“The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead NVIDIA,” Chairman Harvey C. Jones said.

Huang responded, “Thank you for the second chance. I’m going to be a better CEO the second time around.”

Meanwhile, President Donald Trump shared a meme of Huang alongside an image of his assassination attempt on Truth Social. Trump also said he had spoken with Nvidia’s board and helped convince directors to reinstate Huang.

Following the news, Nvidia shares jumped more than 40% during the rally, adding nearly $1 trillion to its market value. The stock later closed 31.5% higher, marking its biggest daily gain since May 2003.

The news revived investor confidence in the global AI chip trade. South Korea’s KOSPI jumped over 7%, forcing the Korea Exchange to trigger a five-minute trading halt known as a sidecar.

Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix led the move, gaining as much as 8% and 8.7%, respectively.

Japan also joined the rally, with the Nikkei 225 rising 4% and the broader Topix gaining 2.5%. The Nikkei’s technology sector jumped more than 5%.

Advantest climbed 15%, Tokyo Electron gained 10.5%, while Kioxia rose 8.2%. SoftBank Group also gained between 8% and 12%.

Taiwan saw an even stronger move, with the Taiex surging 9%. Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company led the gains, jumping nearly 9%, while Foxconn and MediaTek each rose around 11%.

The gains followed a strong Wall Street rebound. The S&P 500 rose 4.7%, while the Nasdaq Composite jumped 7%, marking its strongest single-day gain since April 2025. The Nasdaq 100 also climbed 6.9%.

The rally was not limited to tech stocks. The crypto market also recovered from last week’s fall, with its total value rising above $2.26 trillion. Bitcoin price jump over $63,562 but since then its earsied some gain now trading at $60,455.

However, the quick recovery caught many short traders off guard, with the market recording $645 million in liquidations over the past 24 hours.
