Fine and dandy that you have achieved a reasonably close goal.

However, it is categorically wrong to suggest one can choose which income to distribute or retain.

The way to do it, as you do, is to structure the holdings so the pro-rata distribution is friendly to the beneficiary.

Your initial post saying you can choose the tax character of the income you distribute is wrong though. That is a frequent and confusing misconception which is why I corrected it. You cannot ordinarily choose the tax character of distributions. And your trust certainly doesn't meet the requirements to do so under the exception.
