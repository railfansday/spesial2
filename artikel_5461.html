NEW YORK, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- DarioHealth Corp. (NASDAQ: DRIO) (the "Company", "DarioHealth" or "Dario"), a leading AI-powered healthcare technology company transforming the management of chronic conditions, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. This press release corrects certain information in the financial tables, including the reconciliations of GAAP to non-GAAP financial measures, contained in the Company's press release originally issued on November 12, 2026. The corrected financial tables are included below.

"In the second quarter of 2026, we described how Dario's business model is designed to support growth. We believe that our third quarter of 2026 shows that model is working, with multi-condition expansion within existing accounts and new health plan agreements through our channel partners," said Erez Raphael, Chief Executive Officer of DarioHealth. "At the same time, the market is moving toward the integrated platform we have spent more than a decade building. For years, the industry treated consumer health and payer-funded chronic care as two different businesses. We believe they are becoming one, and Dario sits exactly where they meet. Payers are moving from buying programs around a single therapy to buying a multi-condition integrated platform, as well as the therapy delivered inside a managed program, and we see that as a meaningful expansion of what Dario can do for payers and their members."

Today's consumer increasingly chooses the entry point into care, while the payer continues to fund the chronic condition behind it, and Dario believes the value in the market will move to whoever owns the path between the two. One of the largest national health insurers, an existing Dario customer, plans to implement a marketplace model that combines consumer choice with payer funding. This customer is working with Dario to extend the relationship from the digital program to delivery of the GLP-1 therapy itself.  Dario views the convergence of these markets around access to specific therapies, inside a managed and measured program, as a significant opportunity.

Dario believes this shift will move chronic care management from single-condition apps to an AI-led, clinically governed system built on one member record. We believe Dario is one of very few platforms with consumer engagement and payer-grade evidence on the same member record, built over more than a decade on both sides: consumer roots and FDA-cleared connected devices in the home, more than 13 billion proprietary data points, more than 100 published studies and payer savings analyses prepared by third party, all on one platform. As payers bring specific therapies into the benefit, starting with GLP-1, Dario aims to connect access to therapy with the chronic conditions it already manages, supported by its existing member engagement capabilities and clinical and economic evidence.

Added New Enterprise Accounts Through Channel Partners and Expanded Within Existing Customers

Validation of Dario Platform's Clinical and Economic Value

"We are adding large health plans through our channel partners and expanding inside the accounts we already serve," said Lara Dodo, Chief Operating Officer. "The rapid adoption of our new solutions like Dario Women reflects growing customer interest for more personalized, integrated care. We are meeting that demand with solutions that address interconnected chronic conditions and are demonstrated to deliver better health outcomes."

"We grew revenue 6% year-over-year while reducing our operating expenses by 10% and our operating loss by 15%. Our B2B2C gross margin has remained strong, and we are focused on scaling that model," said Chen Franco-Yehuda, Chief Financial Officer. "With $31.2 million in cash, cash equivalents and short-term deposits, we believe we have the resources to execute on our priorities."

Revenue grew to $5.3 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $5.0 million in the third quarter of 2025 and $5.2 million in the second quarter of 2026, representing increases of 6% and 3%, respectively. The year-over-year increase primarily reflects growth in direct-to-consumer sales.

Gross profit was $3.1 million, compared with $3.0 million in the third quarter of 2025 and $3.2 million in the second quarter of 2026. Gross margin was 59.2%, compared with 60.2% in the third quarter of 2025 and 61.7% in the second quarter of 2026, primarily reflecting changes in revenue mix, including increased direct-to-consumer sales, partially offset by lower hosting costs. Gross margin in the second quarter included an IEEPA tariff refund benefit of approximately $0.4 million, compared with approximately $0.1 million in the third quarter.

Operating expenses declined year-over-year to $11.2 million, compared with $12.5 million in the third quarter of 2025 and $9.7 million in the second quarter of 2026, reflecting lower research and development and general and administrative expenses, partially offset by increased sales and marketing expenses compared to the year-ago period. Operating loss improved to $8.0 million, compared with $9.5 million in the third quarter of 2025 and $6.5 million in the second quarter of 2026.

Net loss decreased year-over-year to $8.8 million, compared with $10.5 million in the third quarter of 2025, and $7.9 million in the second quarter of 2026.

Non-GAAP operating loss (excluding stock-based compensation, acquisition related expenses, depreciation and amortization expenses) for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $5.3 million compared to a non-GAAP operating loss of $6.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025, and non-GAAP operating loss of $5.3 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026.

A reconciliation of GAAP to non-GAAP measures has been provided in the financial statement tables included in this press release. An explanation of these measures is also included below under the heading "Non-GAAP Financial Measures."

Revenue for the first nine months of 2026 was $16.1 million, compared with $17.1 million for the first nine months of 2025. The decrease primarily reflected non-recurring revenue from a pharmaceutical partner recognized in the prior-year period, partially offset by growth in B2B2C revenues from channel partners and direct-to-consumer sales.

Gross profit was $9.5 million for the first nine months of 2026, compared with $9.9 million in the prior-year period. Gross margin improved to 59.4%, compared with 57.6% for the first nine months of 2025, primarily reflecting lower amortization of technology and hosting costs and IEEPA tariff refund benefits of approximately $0.4 million.

Operating expenses decreased by 17% to $31.4 million for the first nine months of 2026, compared with $38.0 million for the first nine months of 2025, reflecting lower research and development, sales and marketing, and general and administrative expenses. Operating loss improved by 22% to $21.8 million, compared with $28.1 million in the first nine months of 2025.

Net loss improved by 23% to $25.0 million for the first nine months of 2026, compared with $32.7 million for the first nine months of 2025.

Non-GAAP operating loss (excluding stock-based compensation, acquisition related expenses, depreciation and amortization expenses) for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $16.0 million compared to Non-GAAP operating loss of $18.2 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

DarioHealth (Nasdaq: DRIO) is an AI-powered healthcare technology company helping health plans, health systems and employers improve health outcomes while lowering the cost of care. The Company's integrated platform combines connected devices, personalized member engagement, AI-driven insights and provider-backed clinical care to support people living with conditions including diabetes, hypertension, weight management, musculoskeletal and behavioral health needs.

Powered by more than 13 billion proprietary longitudinal healthcare data points collected over more than a decade, Dario's AI platform personalizes care at the individual member level by analyzing biometric, clinical and behavioral data to deliver more timely and effective interventions. By combining engagement, clinical intelligence and care delivery within a single platform, Dario helps customers address multiple chronic conditions through one solution.
