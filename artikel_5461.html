Cleveland-Cliffs will recall 175 Stelco workers, resolving dispute with Canada

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The steelmaker will pay the 175 Hamilton, Ontario, employees back wages for the layoff period as customers commit to a restart

Cleveland-Cliffs will recall all 175 laid-off workers at its Stelco unit in Hamilton, Ontario, on Dec. 1, the company said on Friday. Stelco will make up the regular pay the workers missed while they were off the job.

The company said it has reached agreements with enough customers to restart its Hamilton finishing operations. The restart will take several weeks, and the company described the recall as a show of good faith to employees.

Cleveland-Cliffs said its customers pledged to back the Hamilton operations even though, in the company's view, Ottawa has not remedied gaps in the relief program meant to offset tariffs. The company also cited continued imports of galvanized products that it said have eroded the domestic market.

"I would like to thank our valued customers for the faith they have placed in us, and our workers for the patience and resilience they have continued to demonstrate during this challenging period," Stelco President Paul Simon said in a statement.

The recall resolves a dispute between Cleveland-Cliffs and the Canadian government, according to Bloomberg. Canadian Industry Minister Mélanie Joly set a 15-day deadline for the company in October to remedy alleged breaches of the job commitments it made when it acquired Stelco in 2024. The alternative was a possible court fight that could result in fines or an order to sell Stelco.

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney had threatened legal action against Cleveland-Cliffs in September after the company announced the Hamilton layoffs. Carney pointed to employment commitments the company took on as a condition of its C$3.4 billion ($2.4 billion) acquisition of Stelco in 2024. Those terms required the company to keep unionized headcount no lower than it was at acquisition and to retain most salaried staff for five years, the company said.

Stelco said at the time that it would idle cold-rolled and coated operations at the Hamilton plant, with up to 500 workers affected. The company said on Friday that the idling affected 175 employees.
