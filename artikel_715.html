Executive Turntable: Melissa Victor-Burkhardt Joins HYBE Global Label Services

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Plus, Kristina Heney and Greg Lombardo join Pophouse, Prescription Songs promotes Caitlin Lopilato and more music industry hires and promotions.

HYBE has appointed Melissa Victor-Burkhardt as executive vp of global artist services for HYBE Global Label Services. Within the newly created role, Victor-Burkhardt will work with the company’s worldwide teams to help with project management. She will also oversee a team of project managers for marketing, commerce, distribution, DSP and streaming.

Victor-Burkhardt previously served as senior vp, head of publicity and brand strategy at Epic Records, where she worked with clients including Future, Travis Scott, 21 Savage, Giveon, Mariah The Scientist, Madison Beer and Zara Larsson. She has also worked at labels including Interscope, J Records/Jive Records, Virgin, Island Def Jam and Bad Boy Records.

“Melissa is a deeply experienced executive in the music industry who has worked across countless campaigns for global artists and has an acute understanding of the scope and teamwork required across both internal and external partners for demonstrated success,” Juan S. Arenas, president for HGLS, said in a statement. “She will work closely with our worldwide teams to strengthen and build an ecosystem that encourages services to HYBE IP, a continued collaborative approach, and a focus on high impact. I’m grateful to have her on board to help execute the vision of a globally built platform powered by best-in-class services.”

Victor-Burkhardt added, “By helping artists grow from a first single to the global stage, my work has always been about strategically building careers that last. HYBE is redefining what a music company can be, and I’m excited to bring that experience to a global platform. Thank you to Isaac Lee [CEO, HYBE Americas] and Juan S. Arenas for this opportunity.”

See more music industry signings and promotions below.

Pophouse has appointed Kristina Heney and Greg Lombardo to the company’s leadership team. Heney has been named president, commercial and marketing, and is based in New York, while Lombardo is president, Pophouse Avatar Network, and is based in Los Angeles. Heney joins from Oak View Group, where she had served as executive vp, marketing, media and conferences. Lobardo had been vp and global head of experiences at Netflix.

“Pophouse was founded on the belief that the world’s most iconic artists can reach new generations through bold, imaginative experiences,” Jessica Koravos, CEO of Pophouse Entertainment, said in a statement. “Building on our partnerships with legendary artists and productions, including KISS, Iron Maiden, Tina Turner, Sia, Cyndi Lauper and Avicii, ABBA Voyage, Mamma Mia! The Party and our exhibitions, we aim to carry these legacies to audiences worldwide. Greg and Tina are two of the most accomplished leaders in live entertainment, and they bring exactly the combination of commercial discipline and creative ambition we need for the next chapter. I am delighted to welcome them both to our leadership team.”

Prescription Songs has promoted Caitlin LoPilato to director of A&R. She had previously held roles at Def Jam, Mogul Vision/Arista, 300 Entertainment and Platinum Grammar Publishing.

“Caitlin has been an incredible asset to the team at Prescription Songs, bringing a thoughtful creative perspective and a genuine passion for the artists, songwriters, and producers she works with,” Parsricha Kullas, president of A&R, said in a statement.

Louisa Shipnuck Jones has joined Iron Mountain as vp of media and architectural services.

“We are excited that Louisa will be leading our Media and Archival Services business. She brings a unique blend of experience, from owning the revenue of a premier television library to bringing media technology solutions to studios, broadcasters, news outlets and sports leagues,” Karen Feldman, senior vp and chief marketing officer at Iron Mountain, said in a statement. “Very few people understand our clients’ world the way Louisa does, and that perspective will help them unlock new growth opportunities and greater value from their media assets.”

AdTonos announced the company has rebranded to Turms and will be moving from programmatic audio to Movement Media, which is the company’s agentic media-buying technology.

“For ten years, we’ve shaped the evolution of programmatic audio advertising. But the way people experience media has changed dramatically, and the technology connecting brands with audiences needs to evolve alongside it,” Michael Marcinik, founder and CEO of Turms, said in a statement. “Turms represents our ambition to build on what we’ve established in audio and create something broader: an intelligent infrastructure that understands context as people move through their day. As early as 2019, our first attribution studies using Audiopixel highlighted audio’s ability to influence people’s decisions. But unlocking that potential requires a deliberate role in the media mix, with the right timing and coordination across channels, not simply as an add-on to extend reach.  We believe the future of advertising isn’t about reaching people more often. It’s about understanding when an interaction actually makes sense and this is exactly what Movement Media AI is for.”

David Wilson and Atika Lynch, who both previously served as directors of artist development at Sony Music’s AWAL, have launched cltr;shft, a firm that specializes in digital marketing and audience growth. Wilson serves as of-founder and fractional head of content and strategy at the company and is based in Los Angeles, while Lynch is co-founder and fractional head of audience growth and culture strategy and is based in New York.
