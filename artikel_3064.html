Why Urban Outfitters Stock Crashed on Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Anthropologie isn't turning around fast enough to please Urban Outfitters investors.

By Rich Smith – Nov 25, 2026 at 1:03PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Urban Outfitters ›

Urban Outfitters (URBN -10.33%) stock tumbled 12.1% through 12:40 p.m. ET Wednesday despite beating (barely) on Q3 earnings last night.

Heading into the report, analysts expected Urban Outfitters to earn $1.52 per share on $1.67 billion in quarterly sales. As it turned out, Urban Outfitters earned $1.54 per share, while sales met the consensus.

That hardly seems like the kind of results to spark a 12% sell-off! And yet it did. So what went wrong?

Brand by brand, growth was weakest at Anthropologie, responsible for 40% of sales -- but sales there inched up only 3.2%. The namesake Urban Outfitters brand grew its sales 4.6%. FP Group ("Free People") saw the briskest sales growth -- a whopping 14.6%, contributing most of the growth for the quarter.

Anthropologie's relatively weak performance appears to underlie today's falling share price. Commenting on the results last night, Morgan Stanley analyst Alex Straton complained that she had hoped to see recent "green shoots" at Anthropologie sprout into much bigger sales than actually materialized in Q3.

With "Anthro improvement increasingly necessary to unlock investor appreciation of the broader portfolio's value," says Straton -- and that improvement taking longer to arrive -- the worry now is that investors may have to buckle down and prepare to endure "Anthro's more prolonged deterioration."

With analysts now anticipating Urban Outfitters will grow earnings only about 5% annually over the next five years, even a relatively modest 13x price-to-earnings ratio may not be cheap enough to make Urban Outfitters stock a buy.
