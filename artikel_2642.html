How should Chelsea line up against Nottingham Forest?

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

We've got some action under the Friday night lights to look forward to this week as Chelsea travel to take on one of the worst teams in the Premier League, Nottingham Forest. Of course, one might say that on current evidence, Chelsea are also one of the worst teams in the league, so that may not be much advantage for us. And perhaps we're not really looking forward to it either.

And that will be especially true if we get no good news regarding injuries from Xabi Alonso in his upcoming pre-match press conference. Moisés Caicedo has not been seen in training since being forced off in the first half of last weekend's 4-1 defeat against Leeds United. If he cannot go, he will join João Pedro, Maxence Lacroix, Reece James, Jorrel Hato, and Jamie Gittens on the sidelines. That's not a long list, but it is important list.
