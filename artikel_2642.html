&#8216;We know him, we expected it&#8217; &#8211; Barcelona boss Flick accuses referee Hernández Hernández of bias

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Barcelona head coach Hansi Flick has on Sunday evening made crystal clear his feelings with referee Alejandro Hernández Hernández. This comes fresh off the Spaniard's latest outing overseeing the exploits of the Blaugrana.

Barca were of course back in action earlier today, marking their post-international break return to the pitch in a La Liga meeting with Villarreal.

When all was said and done, the Catalans recovered from a two-goal hole to ultimately emerge on the right side of a 3-2 result.

The Camp Nou clash was not without its controversies, with Villarreal convinced that Barcelona's opener came from an undeserved corner, and the Blaugrana adamant that their opponents should have seen a red card.

Speaking to the media in his post-match press conference, the aforementioned Hansi Flick was therefore asked for his take on the performance produced by referee Alejandro Hernández Hernández.

And Barca's headmaster did not at all hold back with his response:

"We knew who the referee was today, and we expected it. In general, I'm happy with them, but not with this one."
