Brock Purdy ruled out vs. Seahawks. Can Mac Jones carry 49ers offense?

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The San Francisco 49ers will be without their quarterback for the first of their two-game NFC West leg this week.

Brock Purdy has been ruled out for their Week 12 divisional matchup against the Seattle Seahawks, head coach Kyle Shanahan announced on Friday, Nov. 27. Mac Jones will get the start at home against the defending Super Bowl champions.

Purdy sprained his left ankle in the second quarter of the Mexico City game against the Minnesota Vikings on Nov. 22, though he kept playing and finished with 255 passing yards and three touchdowns in a 35-10 win to push the Niners' NFL-best record to 9-1. He did not practice all week while nursing his ankle and despite Shanahan's optimism that he wouldn't miss time, Purdy is now being shut down for the week after it was revealed he suffered a mid-ankle sprain, which typically is slower to heal.

Shanahan told reporters Friday that Purdy won't require surgery. Does he expect his franchise QB back next week against the Los Angeles Rams?

"It's too soon, but I mean I'm pretty optimistic about it," he said. "But I was (optimistic) about this week to start and then it changes, so hopefully it'll be all right."

Without Purdy, the Niners have been in good hands with Jones, who went 5-3 in eight games started a year ago when Purdy went down with a toe injury. In those eight games, Jones threw 13 touchdowns for 2,151 yards with six interceptions and a 97.4 passer rating.

The Seahawks, on the other hand, are no stranger to quarterback injuries this year. They'll have Sam Darnold back on Sunday from a quad injury that kept him out of their last two games.

Though the 49ers are tied with the Chicago Bears for most wins in the league with nine, these next two divisional games against the third-place Seahawks and second-place Rams will be crucial as they lead LA by 2 1/2 games in the ultracompetitive NFC West.
