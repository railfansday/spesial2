&#8220;Exceptional player, exceptional leader,&#8221; Maestro praises Calmac

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Callum McGregor of Celtic celebrates scoring Celtic v St Mirren, Scottish Premiership, Celtic Park, 21 November 2026. Photo Mark Runnacles Shutterstock IMAGO

The Maestro is back in Scotland at the moment for the launch of his go-it-alone autobiography The Maestro and is doing the media rounds as he works hard to make the project a success.

Paul McStay of course came through the ranks at Celtic and played his entire career at the club before having to hang up his boots due to an ankle injury in 1996.

Paul McStay and Roy Aitken celebrate Celtic winning the 1988 Scottish Cup Final on 14 May, against Dundee United at Hampden. Photo Imago/ Colorsport.

Aside from a short loan spell at Notts County at the start of his Celtic career over a decade ago, Calmac has also been a one club man after breaking through to the first team under Ronny Deila.

The current Celtic captain has won 26 major honours at Celtic, 11 Scottish Premiership titles, seven Scottish Cups, and eight Scottish League Cups and might be eyeing reaching 30 before he decides it's time to call it a day.

Callum McGregor of Celtic is seen at full time during the Scottish Premiership match between Celtic and Kilmarnock at Celtic Park on November 08, 2026. (Photo by Ian MacNicol/Getty Images)

That's a considerably larger haul than The Maestro himself who won eight major honours in a Celtic jersey – three league titles, four Scottish Cups and one League Cup.

Of course Paul broke into the Celtic team in the early 1980s when Dundee United and especially Aberdeen were very strong under Alex Ferguson, then played as the borrowed Murray millions poured into the old Ibrox club and Celtic toiled financially in the early years of the 1990s.

Paul McStay, Celtic FC. Photo The Celtic Wiki

In comparison Callum has had things relatively easy especially playing for Brendan Rodgers in his two spells and Ange Postecoglou in between. Even last season – which was turbulent to say the least, Calmac still managed to lift the Scottish Premiership trophy and the Scottish Cup under Martin O'Neill.

The Maestro outlined his thoughts on the current Celtic captain, describing Callum McGregor as an exceptional player but insisted that Celtic need to look at bringing in another quality midfielder in January to help the skipper.

Callum McGregor of Celtic looks on while lined up on the pitch before the UEFA Europa League match between Celtic FC and Besiktas JK at Celtic Park on November 05, 2026. (Photo by Zak Mauger/Getty Images)

As reported by Glasgow Times, Paul McStay said: "Exceptional player, exceptional leader. Great success for the club and you're talking about academy players coming through. Callum, Kieran Tierney and James Forrest are great examples of that.

"But just now, I always felt after last season, we needed somebody beside Callum who could help him. Baur's come in that sort of number ten. But I think Callum just needs that wee bit of help.

9th September 2026; McDiarmid Park, Scottish Premiership St Johnstone versus Celtic; Mika Baur of Celtic celebrates after he shoots and scores in the 52nd minute to make it 1-0 to Celtic. Photo Vagelis Georgariou

"I hope in January they can bring someone in."

"But he's still playing well and I think that last 30 minutes of the European game against Besiktas was outstanding by the team. So to me, probably in January, maybe some help."
