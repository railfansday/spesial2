Klint Kubiak calls Brock Bowers day-to-day with ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Raiders tight end Brock Bowers only played 11 snaps on Sunday because of an ankle injury, but there doesn't appear to be much concern about an extended absence from the lineup.

Head coach Klint Kubiak called Bowers "day-to-day" at the start of a Monday press conference. PFT reported earlier in the day that Bowers should be able to play in Week 11, but Kubiak didn't offer any predictions about his availability against the Broncos.

"It's not as bad as we thought, but it still is a significant injury so we'll keep monitoring him," Kubiak said.

Bowers leads the Raiders with 50 catches, 676 receiving yards and six receiving touchdowns this season, so life will be easier for quarterback Fernando Mendoza in his second NFL start if Bowers is able to go against Denver.
