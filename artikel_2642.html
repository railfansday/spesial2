Broncos vs. Steelers live updates, score: Pittsburgh starts Mason Rudolph following release of Aaron Rodgers, accusations against Mike McCarthy

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Denver Broncos and Pittsburgh Steelers will take the field this afternoon for a special post-Thanksgiving, Black Friday matchup, with kickoff set for 3 p.m. ET at Acrisure Stadium in Pittsburgh, airing on Amazon Prime Video. And this matchup is bound to deliver plenty of fireworks — even if not for the actual football being played on the field.

It was a week full of drama for the Steelers, as the team was sent reeling following a Week 11 upset by the Philadelphia Eagles, leading to the benching of future Hall of Fame quarterback Aaron Rodgers in favor of backup Mason Rudolph. The drama didn't end there, as Rodgers went scorched earth on Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy in an appearance on "The Pat McAfee Show" Wednesday after his benching, leveling a series of accusations toward his now-former coach, eventually leading to his release.

The Steelers sit at 6-4 and would be the No. 7 seed in the AFC playoffs if the season were to end today. So now, they must move forward with Rudolph as McCarthy attempts to wrangle in the chaos released in the locker room after this week's events in an attempt to maintain that playoff positioning.

The Broncos, meanwhile, sit at 8-2 in first place in the AFC West, all but locked into a playoff spot, and now with some cushion after the Kansas City Chiefs fell to the Buffalo Bills on Thanksgiving. After a slow start to the season, the offense has made some improvements since head coach Sean Payton resumed play calling, scoring 26-plus points in each of their past four matchups.

The Steelers are in desperation mode. They're currently in the seventh playoff slot and have a 57% chance to make the postseason, according to Next Gen Stats, as things stand right now. That might sound pretty good, and no team currently outside the playoff field is over 50%.

A loss would drop the Steelers' chances to 50%. A win would push them to 72%. Pittsburgh's next four games are against teams still fighting for the postseason.

It's not as if Denver has nothing to play for. It is currently the No. 2 seed in the playoff standings, just one game behind the Bills, whom the Broncos host in Week 16 on Christmas. The difference between the top seed (which comes with a bye) and the next spot down feels massive.

But the Broncos are near-certainties to make the playoffs, currently sitting at 99% chances to get in. A loss would drop them all the way down to 98%. Their schedule prior to and after the Bills game is soft. It would be tough to lose to a team making a QB change in a short week, but it wouldn't damage Denver's playoff hopes much at all.
