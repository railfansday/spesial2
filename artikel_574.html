Anya Taylor-Joy ‘Went Mute’ on ‘Lord of the Rings: The Hunt for Gollum’ Set When Ian McKellen Showed Up: ‘That’s F—ing Gandalf’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Anya Taylor-Joy, who is set to play Seren, an elf, in “The Lord of the Rings: The Hunt for Gollum,” says the last time she found herself starstruck was when she first came face-to-face with Ian McKellen as Gandalf on set.

“I wasn’t prepared,” she told Dazed. “I went mute. And he’s the loveliest, funniest, most wonderful man and actor. But that’s fucking Gandalf!”

While Taylor-Joy didn’t let much else slip about the highly anticipated film in her cover story, she told the outlet she had specifically hoped to land a role in a “Lord of the Rings” film. “I had called my agent earlier on ‘Dune,’ being like, ‘If they ever make ‘The Lord of the Rings,’ let them know that I’d be interested,’” she said. While McKellen originated the role of Gandalf in Peter Jackson’s “The Lord of the Rings” trilogy, a number of newcomers will make their franchise debut with “The Hunt for Gollum,” including Kate Winslet as Marigol and Leo Woodall as Halvard. Mainstays including Elijah Wood (Frodo) and Lee Pace (King Thranduil) are also set to reprise their roles. Andy Serkis pulls double duty, playing Gollum while also directing the film, while Jamie Dornan replaces Viggo Mortensen as Aragorn.

Back in July, Taylor-Joy admitted to Variety she hadn’t yet read the “Lord of the Rings” books despite landing the role in the forthcoming film. “I’m about to read the books now, actually,” she told Variety’s Marc Malkin. “So as a child, I had a misguided idea that you could only be a ‘Harry Potter’ fan or a ‘Lord of the Rings’ fan. Nobody told me that. I just came up with that on my own. Luckily, throughout COVID, I then watched all of ‘Lord of the Rings,’ and turns out you can love both, and you should.”

Malkin also asked the actress about her reaction to getting cast in “The Hunt for Gollum” at the time, to which she responded: “It’s so fun. I mean, if my tombstone can say ‘Lethal Elf,’ I feel like I did a good job on this planet. So yeah, I’m excited.” Dornan, her co-star, recently admitted he “cried” when he heard he’d gotten the job.

Nearly a year before “Lord of the Rings: The Hunt for Gollum” opens in theaters next Dec., Taylor-Joy will return to the big screen in Denis Villeneuve’s final “Dune” film, due out Dec. 18. In the film, she returns to play Alia Atreides, the younger sister of Paul.
