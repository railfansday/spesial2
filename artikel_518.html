BI Survey: Budgetary Constraints for 2027 Travel

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Jody Lurie, Bloomberg Intelligence Credit Analyst, discussed her recent Bloomberg Survey covering budgetary constraints for 2027 travel. Lurie spoke with Bloomberg's Paul Sweeney and Scarlet Fu.on her recent BI surveys. US vacationers appear poised to spend more in 2027, but inflation is reshaping rather than curbing demand as higher core trip costs squeeze spending on extras, based on Bloomberg Intelligence's seventh US travel survey conducted Oct. 26-Nov. 4. About 71% believe they'll spend more, but only 45% would absorb higher-than-expected costs vs. 50% two years ago, while 53% cite inflation as a reason for bigger budgets. This shift favors midtier hotels and pressures airline and cruise upselling, while generative AI is a nearer-term threat to deal-hunting than booking for Expedia, Booking Holdings and Airbnb. (Source: Bloomberg)
