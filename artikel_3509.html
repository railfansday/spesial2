Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie Reach Settlement in Years-Long Winery Battle

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie have settled a years-long legal dispute over the sale of their French winery, Château Miraval.

“We have settled out of court in the best interests of our family and will not be commenting further,” representatives of Jolie, 51, and Pitt, 62, said in a Wednesday, November 25, statement to Us. “Any comments in media stories attributed to ‘sources’ are in fact not coming from either of us, or anyone close to us.”

The couple first gained a controlling interest in the 1,200-acre winery in the South of France in 2008.

They began selling their own rosé in March 2013, before saying “I do” at the chateau in August 2014. Things got sour, however, after Jolie and Pitt filed for divorce in 2016.

According to a February 2022 lawsuit, Pitt alleged that Jolie illegally sold her shares of Miraval to Tenute del Mondo, the wine division of the Stoli group owned by Russian businessman Yuri Shefler.

Pitt claimed that both parties agreed not to sell their Miraval shares without the other’s permission. In his filing, Pitt argued that his ex-wife made the business decision without his knowledge or explicit consent.

“Through the purported sale, Jolie sought to inflict harm on Pitt,” the lawsuit alleged. “Jolie knew and intended that Shefler and his affiliates would try to control the business Pitt had built and to undermine Pitt’s investment in Miraval.”

Us has learned the litigation between Pitt and Stoli will continue despite the recent settlement involving Jolie.

Wednesday’s settlement ends a contentious case that put Pitt and Jolie’s divorce under the spotlight.  The exes — who share children Maddox, 25, Pax, 22, Zahara, 21, Shiloh, 20, and twins Vivienne and Knox, 18 — finalized their split in December 2024.

Before things were finalized, however, the pair went through a public legal battle with serious allegations spread.

Jolie responded to Pitt’s lawsuit via a countersuit, claiming her ex was “waging a vindictive war against” her while denying that she needed his approval to sell her stake.

In previous court filings, Jolie’s legal team alleged that Pitt refused to buy her out of the winery business after she declined to sign a non-disclosure agreement allegedly “designed to force her silence about his abuse and cover-up.”

Although authorities investigated an incident on a 2016 private jet flight where Pitt was allegedly verbally and physically abusive toward his family, Pitt was not charged with a crime and Jolie declined to press charges.

Since the divorce was finalized, several of Pitt and Jolie’s kids have distanced themselves from their father, with Shiloh, Maddox, Vivienne and Zahara taking steps to remove Pitt from their last name.
