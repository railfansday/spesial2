Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Every app that you can have on your phone

Every app that you can have on your phone

Phone apps in Grand Theft Auto 6 serve a purpose, but some are far more useful than others. Scooter Bros and RydeMe are among the best, whereas Snapmatic is mostly very disturbing. Nonetheless, you'd probably like to unlock them all.

Here's how to use your phone in GTA 6, including a list of phone apps.

To pull out your phone in GTA 6, press the up arrow on the D-pad. If you hold the arrow instead, you'll get a shortcut to the WAiNK vehicle scanner (handy when you're in a hurry and need a getaway car). Most phone apps are unlocked during the main story, so if you're wondering why you don't have the RydeMe app yet, you must simply keep playing.

Here's an overview of every GTA 6 app we've discovered so far, organized by how they appear on your phone. Additionally, we've listed each app's purpose and how to unlock them.
