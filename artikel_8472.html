Chiming in as there is so much misinformation in this thread; it's quite discouraging.

Ventless dryers available in the US are of two types - condensing only and heat pump (which are also condensing).
A heat pump dryer is far superior to a standard dryer in many ways
- It is NOT taking conditioned air in your house (that you either just paid to heat or cool) and pulling it through the dryer and shooting it out to the exterior vent. RATHER it runs the air in the dryer across a coil to remove the moisture, sends the moisture down the drain (you must hook up a drain hose to a heat pump dyer) then takes the now dry air and puts it back through the dryer for another round.
- Although the air in a HP dryer is warm, it is not hot, so not burning your clothes
- Your clothes last longer
- Your clothes come out MUCH softer
- You use FAR less electricity to dry your clothes

There are some cons:
- Cycle takes longer
- You must be diligent about cleaning out the lint traps
- It is difficult/ impossible to find a tech who can work on these

In 2022 (towards the end of Covid era shortages) I purchased the only full size heat pump dryer available in the US market at that time - a Whirlpool. It lasted just over 3 years, and there was no way to get it repaired. in 2025, I replaced it with an LG, who had introduced full size HP dryers to the US market. The LG Is a far superior product in every sense - better controls, light in the drum, and coil cleaning cycle to keep it operating efficienctly, but most important, the lint filter on the LG is far better - it actually keeps lint from the coil.

I would never have a regular dryer again, even if I had a vent (which I do, but I sealed it) - who wants a big hole in the wall and send conditioned air outside every time you run your dryer?? And the clothes are so much softer.
