Report: Giants DC Dennard Wilson had a virtual interview for Maryland head coaching job

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Giants defensive coordinator Dennard Wilson has helped the team to a 6-3-1 record so far this season and he’s landed on the interview list for a head coaching vacancy on the college level.

Mike Garafolo of NFL Media reports that Wilson recently had a virtual interview with the University of Maryland. Maryland fired Mike Locksley last month after a 2-5 start to the season.

Wilson grew up in Maryland and played defensive back for the Terrapins. He was a graduate assistant at the school in 2007 and 2008 before embarking on an NFL career that has also included stints with the Rams, Jets, Eagles, Ravens and Titans.

Wilson is in his first year with the Giants. They rank 12th in points allowed and seventh in yards allowed this season.
