PSG predicted XI v AS Roma: Ousmane Dembélé to start for first time since injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Ousmane Dembélé is set to start for the first time for Paris Saint-Germain since sustaining an injury back in October. He had come off the bench in the win over OGC Nice on Saturday.

Dembélé is expected to replace Senny Mayulu in leading the line for PSG against AS Roma this evening. It means that Senny Mayulu is expected to drop to the bench. There may also be no place for Maghnes Akliouche in the line-up. Ferran Torres is instead expected to start on the right and Mika Godts on the left. With Désiré Doué and Khvicha Kvaratskhelia both out with ACL injuries, Luis Enrique's options up front have now been limited.

The reigning European champions are set to start with their first-choice midfield and first-choice defence, meaning no place in the line-up for Warren Zaire-Emery.

Matvey Safonov; Nuno Mendes, Willian Pacho, Marquinhos, Achraf Hakimi; Fabián Ruiz, Vitinha, Joao Neves; Mika Godts, Ousmane Dembélé, Ferran Torres. (L'Éq)
