‘He Got Me Comfortable With Being Uncomfortable’ – 76ers Star LeBron James Reveals How Knicks Coach Helped Transform His Shot

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

In his 24th NBA season, LeBron James is continuing to perform at the top level. Even when he entered the league as an 18-year-old, James knew that his shooting lagged behind other components of his game.

The NBA icon has now opened up about how much he worked to improve his shooting and how one particular relationship helped him make substantial change.

James has enjoyed a glittering NBA career. At 41, an age at which many players have already retired, the forward continues to be one of the must-watch players in the NBA.

Yet, James isn't the same athlete he was when he entered the league in 2003. Early on, he appeared to depend on pure athleticism to succeed, a point he shared with Hall of Fame guard Steve Nash on the latest episode of the "Mind the Game" podcast.

"I would I would work on my shooting for sure, but I wasn't it wasn't like I was detailed," James stated.

In his rookie season, James connected on 41.7% of his field goals and 29% of his attempts from beyond the arc. Yet, an early point arrived for the legend that he felt he needed to alter his game. An interaction with a Cleveland assistant coach opened his eyes.

"Shout out Chris Jent… Won a ring with Hakeem (Olajuwon) and those guys his rookie year in Houston and he is now an assistant coach. Just won another ring with the Knicks… He joined Mike Brown when Mike Brown took over in Cleveland… I used to watch him shoot in practice," James said.

Jent played four seasons at Ohio State University. After bouncing around the CBA and internationally, he signed with the Houston Rockets and won an NBA championship in 1994. Jent was known as a shooter, including a 38.2% 3-point mark over four seasons at Ohio State. In six NBA regular-season games, he shot 42.9% from three.

The 56-year-old currently serves as the Knicks' associate head coach under Mike Brown, and helped them win their first NBA championship since 1973.

Under Jent's tutelage as his personal shooting coach, James won his only scoring title during the 2007-08 season. He credits Jent with one overarching aspect that fine-tuned his shooting.

"He got me comfortable with being uncomfortable… I was so dominant even with I was possessed with… I didn't want to have a flaw.. .I just I always wanted to be more than just my athletic ability," James said.

James and Jent crossed paths when the former was in the early stages of his career. Ultimately, through hard work and Jent's coaching, James improved his shooting from both inside and outside of the three-point line.

While many will always remember his dunks, blocks, and other athletic feats, his improved shooting has helped him enjoy a long, successful NBA career.
