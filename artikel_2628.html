Good news for 49ers WR Mike Evans despite not practicing

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

San Francisco 49ers wide receiver Mike Evans didn't suit up for the 49ers' first Week 11 practice.

Evans not returning to action might typically be bad news, but the Athletic's Matt Barrows posted a video from the 49ers' practice facility in Colorado Springs that provided some optimism Evans's return may be sooner rather than later.

Head coach Kyle Shanahan on Monday told reporters in a conference call that Evans would be meeting with the team in Colorado after not making the trip to Dallas. There was some optimism Evans might be able to practice during the week while recovering from Achilles tendinitis.

Barrows' video shows Evans in his jersey catching passes over his shoulder while lightly guarded by a coach.

It's probably a long shot to expect Evans back for Week 11. While the game is a big one in the race for the NFC's No. 1 seed, Shanahan has made it clear the 49ers are taking a long-term view with Evans's Achilles injury. It would make sense for them to use a couple of Week 11 practices to see how he responds to light football activity. After that they could try ramping him up for a Week 12 showdown with the Seattle Seahawks.

San Francisco has two more practices in Week 11 before facing off with the Minnesota Vikings on Sunday night in Mexico City. We'll keep a close eye on Evans's participation throughout the week.
