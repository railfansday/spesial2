Bill Belichick's Hall Of Fame Situation Takes Another Unexpected Turn

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Bill Belichick's Hall Of Fame Situation Takes Another Unexpected Turn originally appeared on NESN. Add NESN as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Bill Belichick and Robert Kraft are getting another opportunity to enter the Pro Football Hall of Fame together. Whether voters will finally agree on their places in football history is another matter entirely.

The former New England Patriots head coach and the franchise's longtime owner advanced Thursday in the selection process for the Class of 2027. Belichick was chosen from the coaching category, while Kraft emerged from the contributor category. Both reached a comparable stage last year before being denied induction.

For a franchise that spent nearly a quarter-century setting the standard in professional football, the possibility of another rejection would be extraordinary.

Belichick won six Super Bowls as New England's head coach and helped the Patriots reach nine championship games during his tenure from 2000 through 2023. His 333 combined regular-season and postseason victories rank second in NFL history behind Don Shula.

He also won two Super Bowl championships as defensive coordinator of the New York Giants, giving him eight championship rings from his NFL coaching career.

Those achievements have not eliminated debate over his candidacy. The Patriots faced league penalties for the 2007 videotaping scandal and the 2015 football-inflation controversy, matters that remain part of the broader discussion surrounding the dynasty.

Still, Belichick's influence on defensive strategy, roster construction and organizational culture extends far beyond New England.

Kraft has owned the Patriots since 1994 and helped oversee their transformation into one of the NFL's most successful franchises. His candidacy also considers contributions to league business, including media rights and labor negotiations.

The Hall revised its selection procedures after the previous election cycle. Belichick and Kraft now join a field that ultimately includes 13 player candidates, with further reductions before the final vote.

The new class will be announced in February 2027. For Patriots fans, the question is no longer whether Belichick and Kraft have the credentials to be considered. It is whether the revised voting process will produce a different result.
