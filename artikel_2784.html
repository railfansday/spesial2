Kings’ Ben Simmons returns to Sacramento facility after Achilles tear

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Kings' Ben Simmons returns to Sacramento facility after Achilles tear appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Ben Simmons is going through a lengthy rehabilitation process after suffering a torn Achilles tendon during a Sacramento Kings practice in October. However, the veteran guard has remained around the organization as he works toward another NBA comeback.

Simmons was recently spotted attending Kings practice and speaking with general manager Scott Perry, according to Brenden Nunes. Head coach Doug Christie had previously confirmed that Simmons had been spending time with the team during his recovery.

The 30-year-old signed a one-year contract with Sacramento in September 2026 after missing the entire previous season. He appeared to be building momentum during the preseason, including a strong performance against the San Antonio Spurs in which he recorded 14 points, eight rebounds, and seven assists.

But the Achilles injury ended his season before he could make his regular-season return. An MRI confirmed a rupture in his right Achilles tendon, and Simmons underwent surgery shortly afterward.

Despite the setback, Simmons has made it clear that he intends to return to the NBA next season.

"My mentality is that I'll be back," Simmons told Andscape's Marc J. Spears. "I'll do everything in my power to get back, get healthy and be better than I was before."

Simmons also explained that he had to make a decision immediately after suffering the injury about whether he would commit to another difficult recovery.

"For me, it was just choosing and making that decision to keep going and fighting. I felt there is no other option. For me, it was like, 'Let's go.' Surgery and start the rehab process," he said.

The former No. 1 overall pick has endured several injury setbacks throughout his career, but remains confident in his ability to contribute.
