Jayson Tatum reopens Steve Kerr’s Olympic controversy with ‘fishy s–t’ quote

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Jayson Tatum is well into the prime of his career and is considered one of the NBA's modern superstar players. But two years ago, Golden State Warriors' head coach Steve Kerr viewed him as more of an afterthought.

During the 2024 Paris Olympics, Tatum was benched in favor of Kevin Durant, who used to play under Steve Kerr, forcing Tatum to spend most of the Summer Olympics on the bench.

Tatum reflected on the benching during an interview with "Boardroom" and said Kerr told him he needed to be patient due to Kevin Durant returning to the lineup from a calf injury.

Steve Kerr reacting to a call during an NBA game. Getty Images

"He was like, 'You know, Kevin is back. We're gonna need to try and figure something out with the lineup. But just be patient.' The f— does that mean?" Tatum said after he was processing Kerr's words to him.

"Once I didn't get in the first quarter, I was like, 'Oh, some fishy s—t going on.' That's when it kind of hit me like, 'Oh, that's what he was trying to tell me.'"

That year, Tatum only appeared in three of Team USA's games, and he saw the second-least amount of action, behind Tyrese Haliburton, of the Indiana Pacers.

Tatum played a diminished role as Team USA won its fifth consecutive men's basketball gold medal, averaging 5.3 points, 5.3 rebounds, 1.5 assists and 1 steal in about 17.8 minutes per game.

Jayson Tatum says being benched in the Olympics for Kevin Durant was "some fishy s—t" NBAE via Getty Images

When Durant came back from his injury, he averaged 22.3 minutes, averaging 13.8 points, 6 rebounds, and 2.3 assists.

Two years before the Paris Olympics, Kerr and Tatum's paths crossed in the NBA Finals. Boston got off to a 2-1 series lead over Golden State, but the Warriors rallied and won three straight to give Kerr and Stephen Curry their fourth NBA title.

Steve Kerr responding during an NBA game. Getty Images

Tatum's time to superstardom came in 2024, when the Celtics dominated the Mavericks in the NBA Finals that year, winning Boston their first NBA title since 2008 by knocking Dallas out in five games.

Tatum was later crowned as the cover for the video game, "NBA 2K."

After the Olympics ended that summer, Tatum appeared in an interview with The Athletic and called his experience "humbling."

In November 2024, Kerr said he "felt like an idiot not playing" Tatum and was well aware that he played his Celtics teammates at the time, Derrick White and Jrue Holiday, extensively.
