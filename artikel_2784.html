Predicting SEC football scores in Week 12: Tennessee looks to fend off LSU

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Week 12 is here and we've been used to some big-time SEC games, but that won't be the case this weekend. We are going to see a few teams play FCS opponents ahead of rivalry week next Saturday. But that doesn't mean there aren't any good games.

We will see one ranked matchup this week when Georgia travels to South Carolina. But the Gamecocks were beaten by Arkansas last weekend, so not sure how much of a challenge South Carolina might be to the Bulldogs.

Tennessee will host LSU and that's arguably the biggest game of the weekend in the conference. Tennessee is ranked No. 9 and it can't afford a loss to the Tigers or the Vols' playoff spot could be in jeopardy.

Here's how every SEC game might play out this weekend.

Death Valley used to be a place where teams go to die, but LSU suffered two losses in a row at home. The Tigers had a real chance to assert themselves against Alabama and Texas, but LSU was destroyed by the Crimson Tide and lost a heartbreaker to the Longhorns.

Now, LSU travels to Tennessee to take on a Vols' team that continues to find ways to win. Tennessee doesn't light up the scoreboard, but the Volunteers play really good defense, and that isn't good for Sam Leavitt and Co.

The Tigers don't have issues moving the football, but turnovers are a real issue. Leavitt has thrown 12 interceptions in 10 games and the Vols find ways to force takeaways. Tennessee needs to win this game to stay relevant in the playoff race. While LSU can win this game, it's hard to predict it doing so.

The rest of the games will be quick-hitter style:

No. 1 Georgia 38, No. 21 South Carolina 14
