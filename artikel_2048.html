Newcastle: Expert 'had to laugh' after what he heard about Lewis Hall and Matthias Jaissle

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Matthias Jaissle has reportedly been told that Lewis Hall will not be sold in the January transfer window, but it should be his own position at Newcastle United that he is worried about.

That is the opinion of experienced former Tottenham, Everton and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 22-year-old has enjoyed an incredibly impressive start to the season and was superb against Lamine Yamal as England beat Spain 3-2 last week.

Hall attracted significant interest from Manchester United in the summer transfer window but eventually committed his future to Newcastle in September, putting pen to paper on terms until 2031.

The Red Devils remain interested and other clubs are likely to join them, despite the Magpies' determined stance on any potential sale.

The left-back spoke of belief in Jaissle's project when he signed, but the German now finds himself under real pressure after a disappointing start to the campaign.

Hall's loyalty to Newcastle is yet to waiver, but he will undoubtedly have aspirations that go beyond Champions League qualification.

It is up to the Magpies to match them because if they do not, another club certainly will.

"That was a wise move by Newcastle to get him tied up because he's going to cost a lot of money, that kid," King told Football Insider.

"The question will be, is he going to be loyal to Newcastle? But on the other end, are they going to win anything? I had to laugh when I read an article about Jaissle.

"They said that they told him that Lewis Hall would remain at the football club and he wouldn't be sold in January. Well, is that manager going to be there in January?

"They've let three of their best players go since they won the League Cup. And that's [Alexander] Isak, [Sandro] Tonali and [Bruno] Guimaraes.

"A lot of money, £300million there for three players. Now, that was decent recruitment."

Newcastle will hope to pick up after the international break despite winning just three of their opening 10 Premier League games.

It will not get much easier, however, with Arsenal, Brighton and Man United all to come in the next few weeks.

"It's been a weird season so far, I think we've shown in spells what a good team we can be," Hall said last week.

"We've got a young squad, which is only going to get better and better. I think it's difficult, the situation we're in at the minute, but we're working really hard in training and we're really looking forward to what's coming.”
