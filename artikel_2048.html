Leeds United must eye Sheffield Wednesday action after Dave Challinor decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Sheffield Wednesday’s unbeaten start in League One under Dave Challinor continued on Tuesday night with a 0-0 draw at Stevenage.

Whilst it wasn’t the best performance from the Owls, it was another clean sheet for the Yorkshire side, and the fans will see that progress has been made since Challinor’s appointment.

Yet, it’s also clear that improvements can be made, and the ex-Stockport chief is still working out his best XI as he finds out which players suit his style of play.

One player who has failed to make an impact for Wednesday this season is Harry Gray, with the striker having joined on loan from Leeds United this summer.

Even though Gray only scored three goals in 20 games on loan at Rotherham in the previous campaign, there was an expectation that he could flourish at Hillsborough.

Firstly, he would be joining a better team, and there was a feeling that Gray could’ve benefited from that experience with the Millers.

However, so far, it hasn’t really worked out. The 18-year-old has managed just one goal in 11 League One games, and he hasn’t impressed with his all-round game, even if there have been flashes of his undoubted talent.

Gray made two brief sub appearances during Challinor’s first two games in charge, but he was left out of the matchday squad for the draw at Stevenage.

That was a blow for the youngster, as Gray will be desperate to play football at this stage of his career, although he will understand that there is fierce competition for places with the Owls right now.

Speaking to the Sheffield Star, Challinor explained that there wasn’t a fitness issue with Gray, just that he had simply opted for others.

So, the challenge for the Leeds loanee is to force his way into Challinor’s plans by performing well in training, and then taking any opportunities that come his way.

If Gray is frustrated by his current plight, then it’s fair to say his parent club will be as well.

The England youth international is extremely highly-rated by the Whites, and there is a hope that, like his brother Archie did, that the forward can become a regular at Elland Road in the future.

Therefore, when Leeds decided to send Gray to the Owls, they would’ve done so with the expectation that he would get regular minutes.

Of course, they can’t dictate to the Wednesday manager what should happen, and it’s ultimately on Gray to prove he warrants a place in the XI.

But, with January approaching, it’s natural that Leeds will evaluate Gray’s situation.

Following on from that, Challinor will spend the next month assessing his players, and he will then hope he can get backing in January to strengthen the squad.

Things can change quickly in football, but it would be no surprise if the new boss prioritised a striker when the window opens.

So, if a new recruit does come in, players will have to move on, which could make a return to Elland Road in January the best outcome for Gray, Leeds and Sheffield Wednesday unless there is a remarkable turnaround in the next month.
