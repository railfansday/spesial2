Alphabet, Amazon, and Nvidia All Have a Stake in Anthropic. Here’s the One I’d Buy If I Could Only Pick 1.

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Many big tech companies will see a huge payoff with Anthropic's IPO.

Anthropic is planning to go public around Nov. 19. This will be a huge deal for the market, as its valuation will be north of $1 trillion. Many early investors will benefit from it going public, including companies like Amazon (AMZN -0.07%), Alphabet (GOOG -1.16%) (GOOGL -1.16%), and Nvidia (NVDA -1.57%). All three of these businesses invested in Anthropic when it was private and are in for a major windfall now that it's going public.

But of these three stocks, which one is the best pick? Or should you buy Anthropic itself? Let's take a look.

First, let's look at Anthropic itself. Some of the financials in its S-1 document are downright incredible. For the first six months of 2026, its revenue grew at a 1,305% year-over-year pace to $16.3 billion. That easily crushes the growth that Amazon, Alphabet, and Nvidia are putting up.

NVDA Revenue (Quarterly YoY Growth) data by YCharts

So, from a pure growth standpoint, Anthropic is the best buy. However, that's not all there is with a company. Anthropic is deeply unprofitable, although a large chunk of its $80 billion net loss over the first six months of the year is due to accounting artifacts.

However, it lost about $700 million from operations in the first six months of the year, which isn't a huge loss to overcome. So, Anthropic is very close to becoming a viable company on its own, which makes it a smart investment. But is it better than these other three that are established stalwarts in the artificial intelligence (AI) industry?

A month ago, if you asked me what the best buy between Nvidia, Amazon, and Alphabet would be, I wouldn't have hesitated to say Nvidia. However, with its CEO in the hot seat over two affairs, there is a lot less certainty.

Its business is still quite certain, as its graphics processing units (GPUs) are best in class and are utilized by companies like Anthropic to train and run AI models around Nvidia's leadership. Nvidia's stake in Anthropic is the smallest of the three, which also means it has the least to gain.

Alphabet may seem like an odd company to invest in Anthropic because it's openly competing against it with its Gemini family of AI models. However, Alphabet is an important Anthropic partner and hosts a lot of its workload on Google Cloud. This gives Alphabet multiple bets in the AI space, as it doesn't need its own AI platform to work out (although it would prefer that).

Next is Amazon, which is in a similar boat to Alphabet. Amazon isn't developing its own AI model. Instead, it's choosing to stay neutral and just host other companies' models. Amazon has been a longtime Anthropic investor and has hosted its workloads for years. Anthropic is also training some of its AI models using Amazon's proprietary chips. Amazon needs Anthropic to be successful for its AI bets to work out, so it has a lot riding on Anthropic's initial public offering (IPO).

So, of the three, which makes the best buy? I think it's Alphabet. Alphabet has a great relationship with Anthropic, although it doesn't need Anthropic to pan out due to its in-house AI model. Amazon needs Anthropic to be successful; otherwise, one of its largest clients is gone. While I usually prefer to invest in companies like Nvidia that are neutral hardware suppliers, the market's reaction to its CEO's resignation has me concerned, and I think it's best to stay patient with that stock right now.

We'll see how Anthropic's IPO goes shortly, and the reality is that all three stocks will benefit from it, but I think Alphabet has the edge right now.
