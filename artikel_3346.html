Taylor Sheridan’s New Yellowstone Spin-Off Is Officially Winning Back It’s Audience

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Taylor Sheridan is preparing a return to the big screen next year with his new Sicario-style action thriller, F.A.S.T., but that isn't stopping the Yellowstone writer from releasing new seasons of some of his most popular TV shows. Over the summer, it was the third season of Lioness that took the world by storm, and while the show is still in the top 10 in a handful of countries around the world, its fate beyond Season 3 has not yet been decided. Sheridan has expanded the Yellowstone universe with new shows like Dutton Ranch, the Cole Hauser and Kelly Reilly-led sequel series that aired its first season earlier this year. The show has been picked up for Season 2, but it's been confirmed that it will not make it back to streaming before the end of 2026.

However, Dutton Ranch isn't the only one of Taylor Sheridan's Yellowstone spin-offs to be thrust into the spotlight in 2026. During the first half of the year, CBS enlisted Luke Grimes to star in Marshals, the first ever network TV show set in the Yellowstone universe. It's safe to say that fans were not hot on the first season, as it registered a poor 28% on the audience-driven Popcornmeter on Rotten Tomatoes. However, Season 2, which is now in full swing and releasing new episodes every week on CBS, has begun to win fans back over. Marshals Season 2 holds a 52% on the Popcornmeter, which may not be what CBS executives are looking for, but it's still a step in the right direction after most fans agreed that the first season was one of the worst Yellowstone-adjacent projects.
