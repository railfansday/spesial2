This has been a terrific year for Brad Pitt, who successfully followed up the biggest hit of his career with two very different films. Last year, Pitt headlined the sports drama F1, which grossed more than $630 million worldwide and scored a Best Picture nomination at the Oscars. It was the biggest hit of his career as a leading man, but Pitt's immediate follow-up was a far riskier bet. The new movie, Heart of the Beast, defied expectations to emerge as a sleeper hit, grossing nearly $200 million worldwide against a reported budget of $82 million. This week, Pitt returned to his Oscar-winning role from Quentin Tarantino's Once Upon a Time in Hollywood in a new sequel that's getting an unusual release from Netflix.

At the same time, fans can also check out one of Pitt's more overlooked original movies, which is currently streaming on Paramount+. But first, a little about The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, directed by David Fincher and slated for a Netflix release on December 23. The streamer has given the movie a two-week IMAX-exclusive run that began on November 25 ahead of Thanksgiving weekend. The movie is projected to deliver a single-digit haul from 250 IMAX screens at the domestic box office.

The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth comes on the heels of fellow Netflix movies to get theatrical runs, such as the Spanish-language Oscar contender La Bola Negra and the World War II drama The Mosquito Bowl. Produced on a reported budget of $120 million, the Once Upon a Time in Hollywood sequel marks Fincher's fifth major project with Netflix, after the streaming series House of Cards and Mindhunter, and the movies Mank and The Killer. The new film has received positive early reviews, and is now sitting at a "Certified Fresh" 82% critics' score on the aggregator website Rotten Tomatoes.

Meanwhile, according to FlixPatrol, Heart of the Beast remains one of the most popular movies on the domestic PVOD market two weeks after its digital debut. The survival thriller, directed by David Ayer, holds a "Certified Fresh" 84% critics' score and a "Verified Hot" 94% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes.

Audiences can also check out Pitt's World War II espionage thriller Allied, which recently turned 10 years old. The movie was directed by Robert Zemeckis and written by Steven Knight, who is also penning the screenplay for the upcoming James Bond reboot starring Robert Pattinson. The movie underperformed at the box office, grossing around $120 million worldwide against a reported budget of more than $100 million, but audiences interested in seeing how Knight navigates an old-fashioned spy narrative can check it out on Paramount+.
