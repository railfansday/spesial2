Nvidia Faces First Earnings Test Since Huang’s Exit

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Nvidia reports earnings for the first time since Jensen Huang’s surprise departure, putting the chipmaker’s 70% growth outlook and the broader AI trade back in focus. JPMorgan Asset Management’s Stephanie Aliaga discusses why strong compute demand remains intact despite leadership uncertainty, what investors need to hear from Nvidia on growth and China, and how Anthropic’s IPO could give public-market investors a new window into the economics of frontier AI. She joins Ed Ludlow on "Bloomberg Tech." (Source: Bloomberg)
