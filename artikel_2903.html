NFL trade deadline tracker: Live updates on news, rumors for Jonathan Taylor, Derrick Henry and more at 2026 deadline

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The NFL trade deadline is quickly approaching, giving teams one last chance to make deals for the postseason stretch.

In recent years, the NFL trade deadline has increased in excitement as more teams have become willing to make moves during the season. That has continued this year, as there have already been major moves as contenders aim to strengthen their rosters for the second half and the playoffs.

Thus far, the biggest trade story has been the Indianapolis Colts, who have sold a few pieces amid a disappointing season. They still have one of the biggest names available, though, as Jonathan Taylor could be traded if Indianapolis finds a team to pay its asking price.

Taylor is not the only running back in rumors, though, with Derrick Henry also facing a trade possibility if the Baltimore Ravens want to sell the impending free agent. So far, the Ravens have been unwilling to trade the star as they hope to still compete down the stretch.

The trade deadline will come and pass on Tuesday afternoon, and now that Week 9 is wrapping up, teams can focus on making deals in the final few days before the deadline.

The Sporting News is tracking live updates on the latest NFL trade rumors ahead of the 2026 deadline. Follow along below with the news.

The Ravens' season is hanging by a thread with Lamar Jackson out, so teams have called about Henry, who is an impending free agent. For now, Baltimore has declined those offers and plan on keeping Henry for the rest of the season.
