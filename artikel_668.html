Taylor Swift, Jaafar Jackson, ‘The Odyssey’ Among Hollywood Music in Media Awards Winners

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Hollywood Music in Media Awards (HMMA) unveiled its 2026 winners, which includes a star-studded lineup of Taylor Swift, Jaafar Jackson and Diane Warren.

Celebrating the top film and television music moments of the year, the HMMAs honored Taylor Swift and Jack Antonoff for their animated song “I Knew It, I Knew You” from “Toy Story 5,” which Swift wrote and recorded in just eight hours. Ludwig Göransson took home his eighth HMMA for his score on “The Odyssey,” which also marked his second time winning in the score for a feature film category. Last year, the composer won two HMMAs for his work on “Sinners” and went on to earn an Oscar and Golden Globe for best original score. “La Bola Negra” composer Raül Refree also took home the score for an independent film award, as well as the song for an independent film with “La Nieve.”

Additional winners of the evening include Diane Warren for “One Right Here” in the song for a feature film category, marking her third HMMA, and Jaafar Jackson for “Bad” in the song for an on-screen performance in film category for his performance in “Michael.”
