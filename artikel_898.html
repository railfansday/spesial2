Price sets up Grand Slam final showdown with Van Veen

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Gerwyn Price beat Kevin Doets and Gian van Veen beat Michael van gerwen to make it into the Grand Slam of Darts final; watch the final of the Grand Slam of Darts from 7.30pm live on Sky Sports

Gerwyn Price will take on Gian van Veen in the Grand Slam of Darts final, live on Sky Sports from 7.30pm on Sunday.

Gerwyn Price came up with a 16-13 win over Kevin Doets in the semi-finals on Sunday afternoon, while Van Veen defeated Michael van Gerwen 16-11.

Price is targeting a fourth Grand Slam title, adding to his triumphs in 2018, 2019 and 2021.

There is also a big move in the rankings on the line for the Welshman as, if he wins, he will become world No 3, dropping Luke Humphries to world No 4.

Meanwhile, provisional world No 2 Van Veen is searching for his maiden Grand Slam title.

Price came through a real battle to make it into another final. He is aiming to maintain his perfect finals record in Wolverhampton and believes that over the majors in 2026, he has been the most consistent performer.

"I am into another final and I am not playing great," Price told Sky Sports. "I have played okay in patches this tournament. I am in another final and I am happy.

"I have probably been the most consistent player this season as far as majors are concerned.

"I obviously want to win this and there are some big ranking points at stake but I am just happy to get to another final.

"I care - there is £100,000 at stake and if I don't care about that there is something wrong - but if I lose it is another final.

"It is not about trophies, it is about earning a living for my family.

"I am just a rugby player pinching a living!"

It was a tight opening session between the pair from which Price took a 3-2 lead, increasing that to 4-2 with his fourth leg in a row.

Doets battled back but a brilliant 137 checkout and then break of throw from the Welshman put him 6-4 ahead.

From there, Price went on a rampage. He reeled off six legs in a row to get to double figures. Doets stopped the rot with a break of throw but the 10-4 scoreline showed how everything was going the Welshman's way.

Despite Price's momentum, Doets dug deep and won five on the next six legs to move the score to 11-9 and eventually 14-12.

However, the key moment came in the 27th leg when Doets missed two chances to break Price's throw and instead of bringing the score to 14-13, he went 15-12 behind.

That gave Price enough breathing space to wait for his throw, then take out a big 171 to leave 94 which he checked out on tops for a 12-darter.

"Price had control and did things at the right times.

"When he seems to have the most trouble in matches is when he has a big lead. Perhaps his focus gets away from a bit.

"Ultimately, the real clutch darts were Price's and now you have to say he is the favourite in the final."

It was expected to be a close contest between the two Dutch darting stars and early on it was just that with Van Gerwen and Van Veen matching each other in every department.

That saw the game levelled at 5-5 and it was clear someone was going to have to take control.

However, it was not necessarily that Van Veen took control. Some disastrous doubling for both players, but in particular Van Gerwen, proved to be the key factor.

As Van Veen improved, Van Gerwen continued to mess around on the outer ring which allowed his opponent to go from 7-6 down to 10-7 ahead.

Although Van Gerwen tried to get himself back into the contest, a burst of four legs in a row moved Van Veen one leg away from the Grand Slam final, a massive 124 checkout on the bullseye getting him there.

Van Gerwen did hold his throw but it was purely a consolation as the Dutch No 1 pinned 107 on D16 for his first Grand Slam final.
