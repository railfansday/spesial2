One LEGO Harry Potter Set's Upcoming Retirement Comes With A Major Twist

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

A popular LEGO Harry Potter set is leaving shelves for good, but it also comes with a major twist.

LEGO's Harry Potter collection has been a major part of the company since its debut in 2001, coinciding with the release of Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone. Over the past 25 years, the line has expanded a lot. The collection consists of hundreds of building sets inspired by the Wizarding World.

LEGO collectors have an opportunity to pick up a retiring Harry Potter set at a significantly reduced price ahead of the holidays. The LEGO Harry Potter Book Nook: Hogwarts Express, which was first released in 2025, is currently being offered for $50 on Amazon and at Walmart, lowering its original price of $99.99 by 50%. The discount comes as the popular set approaches the end of its run, making the deal more appealing and allowing fans to pick up the set before it is discontinued.

LEGO recently announced that the Book Nook: Hogwarts Express set is nearing the end of its run, listing it as "Retiring Soon" on its website. The set is also completely sold out on the company's site. However, it is still available on Amazon and at Walmart, for the time being. Considering how quickly the set sold out on its original platform, collectors shouldn't waste any time picking it up at other retailers.

The promotion arrives ahead of Black Friday this year and coincides with Walmart's first round of holiday deals. With $49.99 knocked off the price, the Book Nook: Hogwarts Express is likely one of the best LEGO Harry Potter discounts. However, it is important to keep in mind that prices and deals can change.

The LEGO Harry Potter Book Nook: Hogwarts Express recreates one of the film franchise's most iconic locations, Platform 9¾ at London's King's Cross station. The 832-piece set features the famous red train on the platform. It captures the scene when Harry begins his journey to Hogwarts with his owl, Hedwig. It also includes a mini figure of Ron Weasley and his rat Scabbers.

Unlike traditional LEGO train sets, this model was created specifically to be a bookshelf display. Its design allows collectors to arrange it in several different ways, including being able to close it like a book, which makes it easier to place in smaller spaces. When closed, it can actually be placed between books on a shelf, which is very fitting as the train station is beyond the barrier between two platforms.

With such a wide range of sets spanning from individual Hogwarts classrooms to major locations outside of the castle, LEGO Harry Potter continues to be an integral part of LEGO's brand. Its wide variety of products has helped the collection remain relevant and popular over two decades after its launch.
