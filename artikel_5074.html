Pi Network announced plans to launch Pi Launchpad, a decentralized exchange (DEX) and new tokens on its mainnet in the first quarter of 2027. The announcements came during the first day of the Pi Ecosystem Summit on November 17, which focused on app development, developer support and new uses for Pi.

Co-founder Chengdiao Fan shared updates on ecosystem growth, including more than 300 mainnet apps, 420,000 active nodes and 18.1 million KYC-verified users.

The Pi Core Team plans to bring Pi Launchpad, DEX and an automated market maker (AMM) to the mainnet in Q1 2027.

These features are currently available on Testnet. The team also plans to introduce the first mainnet tokens through Pi Launchpad. Era and Fly tokens, previously available on Testnet, were mentioned during the presentation.

The team has not announced an exact launch date. The planned rollout covers January to March 2027.

Chengdiao Fan announced a developer incentive initiative backed by a marketing budget of up to 20 million Pi. The funding aims to help Pi-based applications reach more users and support developers in growing their projects. The team also plans to provide additional resources for app developers.

Co-founder Dr. Nicolas Kokkalis demonstrated how developers can use AI assistants and MCP tools to build applications.

The team demonstrated a new on-chain subscription feature on Testnet.

It will allow developers to charge users in Pi for access to premium app features. For example, gaming and news applications could offer monthly or other time-based subscriptions.

Users would pay through Pi, and access to subscription-based features could end when their subscription expires.

This gives developers another way to earn from their applications while creating more opportunities to use Pi for payments.

The Pi Browser has also received an interface update. Users can now access third-party apps, App Studio and account management services through the updated interface.

The staking section also shows how much Pi users have committed to different applications. The presentation highlighted around 11.12 million Pi staked in Paid for Pi and 9.21 million Pi in CD Game.

The summit included a discussion with the developer of CD Game about gaming applications and future development opportunities. The team also explained how developers could use Pi’s tools to add notifications to their apps. Users may receive updates about applications and games they follow.

Pi Network’s next major development target is the planned Q1 2027 launch of Pi Launchpad, DEX, AMM and the first mainnet tokens.

The team is also working on AI-assisted app development, subscription payments and additional developer support. These updates aim to expand the number of applications that use Pi for payments and services.
