Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Management highlighted a clean safety record and cash runway through 2029.

Relmada Therapeutics, Inc. (RLMD -6.09%) reported significant clinical and operational progress focused on its lead oncology and central nervous system candidates, NDV-01 and sepranolone. Management highlighted the continued durability of response in the Phase 2 study for NDV-01, which has supported the design of the renamed Phase 3 SPECTRUM program. The company stated that its current capital position provides a multiyear runway to fund these registrational trials and secondary programs. Operational efforts are currently concentrated on finalizing manufacturing stability data to support two Investigational New Drug submissions before the end of 2026.

The data we are reporting today reinforce that view. Let me start with the initial 18-month Phase 2 results for NDV-01. In NMIBC, durability of response is a key question, and our long-term extension is where it gets answered. Twenty-three high-risk NMIBC patients who were in complete response, or CR, at 12 months continued into a long-term extension study.

Of the 14 evaluable patients at 18 months, 12 or 86%, remain in CR. In the BCG-unresponsive subgroup, 7 of 8 patients or 88%, remain in CR at 18 months. Two patients progressed to muscle-invasive disease at month 15, 1 BCG-naive T1 patient, and a second BCG-naive CIS with T1 patient. No patients in the study have undergone a radical cystectomy to-date. We also updated our 12-month results, which now include 51 high-risk NMIBC patients. As the dataset has grown, the results have remained consistent, with an anytime CR rate of 94% and a 12-month CR rate of 77%.

In the BCG-unresponsive subgroup, where the need for refractory bladder-sparing options is the greatest, the anytime CR rate was 96%, and the 12-month CR rate was 73%. Just as important, these efficacy results come with a consistently favorable safety and tolerability profile. In the Phase 2 study to-date, there have been no Grade 3 or higher treatment-related adverse events, no treatment-related serious adverse events, and no treatment-related discontinuations. Our market research shows that for a therapy designed for repeated in-office administration, including in the community setting where most patients are treated, such a clean safety and tolerability profile matters. Turning to our registrational Phase 3 program.

As Sergio noted, we have renamed it SPECTRUM to reflect the potential breadth of NDV-01 across the entire NMIBC disease spectrum. Importantly, the program itself remains unchanged, with two FDA-aligned independent registration pathways. The first is a single-arm registration study in second-line, high-risk BCG-unresponsive NMIBC patients with carcinoma in situ, or CIS, with or without papillary disease. In addition, we have a separate exploratory cohort in second-line, high-risk BCG-unresponsive papillary-only disease. The second is a randomized study of NDV-01 as adjuvant therapy following TURBT versus observation in low-grade intermediate-risk NMIBC, a setting with no approved therapy currently.

To sum up, we believe the larger and more mature Phase 2 data continue to support NDV-01's potential to uniquely combine high and durable responses, a favorable safety and tolerability profile, with a simple in-office administration of a ready-to-use sustained-release biodegradable product, the combination physicians tell us they need.
