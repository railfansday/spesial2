Report: Sauce Gardner is not expected to play vs. Texans due to calf injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Colts will be thin in the secondary when they play the Texans on Thursday night.

With cornerback Charvarius Ward already ruled out, NFL Network’s Jane Slater reports that Sauce Gardner is now not expected to play either.

Gardner is officially questionable for the contest with a calf injury. Indianapolis listed Gardner as a non-participant on all three injury report estimates this week.

Ward was also a DNP on all three injury reports, ruled out with a knee injury.

Gardner has recorded 31 total tackles with seven passes defensed in nine games for Indianapolis so far in 2026.

The official list of inactives for both teams will be released later on Thursday, 90 minutes before kickoff.
