‘Arrogant and selfish’ – Shannon Sharpe knows the person to blame for Aaron Rodgers saga

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Aaron Rodgers dropped a hammer on the usual NFL news cycle after he "exposed" the Pittsburgh Steelers in the wake of his benching, and Shannon Sharpe knows exactly who to blame.

It began with the Pittsburgh Steelers announcing that Aaron Rodgers will be benched for the upcoming game against the Denver Broncos.

The Steelers were set to start Mason Rudolph in a head-scratching move, so Rodgers finally made sense of that move with an astonishing rant about the organisation.

Shannon Sharpe has heard enough to know who's to blame with a sensational rant of his own.

Rodgers is one of the most accomplished players in NFL history, but he's certainly getting on in terms of age, so this is an ugly chapter in his career this late.

Rodgers didn't leave many unscathed with his rant, airing the team's dirty laundry out in public by breaking the sanctity of the locker room.

Expectedly, this was a sackable action, and the Steelers promptly released Rodgers shortly after his rant.

As the issue came to a head, Shannon Sharpe directed all the blame at only one party, deeming them "arrogant", "selfish", and "lacking leadership". That party, naturally, is Rodgers himself.

He said about Rodgers on Nightcap: "I said he was arrogant, he was aloof, he was flippant, he was condescending. And all you all did was complain to you-know-who. I said he lacked leadership. That explains why he's a four-time MVP and only went to one Super Bowl. Because he's not a leader of men. He's always been selfish."

Sharpe went on to question the hypocrisy of Rodgers, as he's been one of the flagbearers for keeping the sanctity of the locker room, only to go out and break it himself on this occasion.

He accused Rodgers of trying to go over and above the head coach by giving him ultimatums, ultimately undermining his authority, which is a big no-no in any team.

The tremors from this news are bound to keep hitting for a while yet, and Sharpe's rant about Rodgers is surely not going to be the final one on this matter.
