Former Detroit Lions owner Martha Firestone Ford dies at age 101

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Detroit Lions announced on Tuesday night that former team owner Martha Firestone Ford has passed away at age 101. According to the team, she passed away at her home surrounded by family.

Ford took over as principal owner of the franchise in 2014, following the death of her husband, William Clay Ford. She held onto the role until 2020, when she bequeathed primary ownership to her daughter and current Lions owner Sheila Hamp.

Since then, Ford has been serving as Owner/Chair Emeritus of the team. Here's how the team described her role over the past six years.

In her most recent role as Owner/Chair Emeritus, Mrs. Ford supported her four children – Principal Owner/Chair Sheila Ford Hamp, Owner/Vice Chair Martha Ford Morse, Owner/Vice Chair William Clay Ford Jr., and Owner/Vice Chair Elizabeth Ford Kontulis – as they assumed leadership responsibilities within the family franchise. Today the Ford family proudly carries forward the legacy established by Mr. and Mrs. Ford, preserving the values, love, and passion that have shaped a family tradition spanning multiple generations.

During Mrs. Ford's brief stint as primary owner, the Lions made the playoffs twice (2014, 2016) and produced three winning seasons.

The team offered this official statement on Ford's passing:

"It is with heavy hearts that we acknowledge the passing of Martha Firestone Ford. Mrs. Ford inspired us all as a pillar of the Detroit community and the passionate matriarch of the Detroit Lions football team.

"She will always be remembered for her fierce loyalty to her family, her community, and the city of Detroit.

"Her presence on our sideline, in our building, and in our community will be greatly missed. We wish the entire Ford and Firestone families peace in this time of mourning."

For more on Mrs. Ford's passing and her life accomplishments, check out the Detroit Lions' official story.
