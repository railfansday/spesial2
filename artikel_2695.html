Liverpool player ratings vs Man Utd: Dominik Szoboszlai and Florian Wirtz shine as Pochettino's debut ends in ruin

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Liverpool delivered a tactical masterclass at Anfield, ruthlessly exploiting an early Leny Yoro red card to secure a dominant 4-0 victory. Goals from Dominik Szoboszlai, Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha, and Víctor Muñoz ensured a miserable start for Mauricio Pochettino's tenure as Manchester United manager.

Liverpool reasserted their dominance at Anfield with a comprehensive 4-0 dismantling of a struggling Manchester United. The match was fundamentally altered as early as the 8th minute when Yoro was shown a straight red card for a professional foul on Alexander Isak. This systemic failure in the United defensive line allowed Szoboszlai to open the scoring from the resulting free-kick, followed quickly by a clever Wirtz finish. The visitors, reduced to a low block by necessity, offered little in transition, and Liverpool's 64% possession eventually told as the game wore on.

The second half saw the hosts maintain a high defensive line, suffocating any potential counter-attacks spearheaded by Bruno Fernandes. Ngumoha capped a fine individual display with a powerful strike in the 66th minute before substitute Muñoz added late gloss to the scoreline. Tactically, Andoni Iraola's side demonstrated superior spatial awareness, recording an expected goals (xG) value of 4.38 compared to United's 0.63. The result leaves Liverpool firmly in the top four hunt, while their rivals remain mired in a relegation battle, sitting 19th in the Premier League table.

Alisson enjoyed one of his quieter afternoons, though he remained alert to preserve his clean sheet. He made 4 saves in total, including a vital parry from a late Fernandes effort to ensure the visitors left without even a consolation goal. His distribution was typically efficient, facilitating Liverpool's high-tempo buildup.
