Bills secure a big conference win in chase for one seed in AFC

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Buffalo Bills fought the weather and the Kansas City Chiefs on Thursday, and came away with a hard-fought victory. The Bills are now the number one seed with a bullet.

Buffalo is up a game and a half on the Denver Broncos. The Broncos came into the week tied with the Chiefs atop the AFC West, but now the Bills have a win over those Chiefs. Buffalo plays the Broncos on Christmas in what will be another massive game in the conference.

The Cincinnati Bengals were right there until they lost on Monday night a few short days ago and the Jacksonville Jaguars were lurking, but they lost, as well.

If Buffalo wins the rest of their AFC games, they are the one seed. Thy took that first step on Thursday.
