Lions list DJ Wonnum, Chuck Clark as questionable for Thursday

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Lions issued a pair of questionable designations for Thursday's game against the Bears.

Defensive end DJ Wonnum and safety Chuck Clark received those tags on the team's final injury report ahead of their annual Thanksgiving home game. Wonnum missed Monday and Tuesday with a back injury, but returned as a limited participant for Wednesday's practice. Clark was limited the last two days with a hamstring injury.

Cornerback Ennis Rakestraw (shoulder) and defensive end Skylar Gill-Howard (knee) were ruled out. Head coach Dan Campbell said earlier this week that Gill-Howard will miss the rest of the season.

Tight end Sam LaPorta (ankle) and cornerback D.J. Reed (hip) did not receive injury designations. Reed is set to return after missing the last three games.
