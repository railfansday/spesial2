Seahawks sign offensive tackle off Bills’ practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A day after veteran tackle Bobby Hart was signed to the 53-man roster, the Seattle Seahawks have added another offensive lineman to the roster.

The team signed tackle Chase Lundt off the Buffalo Bills' practice squad, The Ringer's Tom Pelissero reported on Tuesday. In a corresponding move, cornerback Avery Smith was waived by the Seahawks.

The 26-year-old Lundt was a sixth-round pick of the Bills in the 2025 NFL Draft, and played two games in a rookie season spent on their active roster. After he was waived during roster cuts ahead of the 2026 season, he was signed to their practice squad where he had remained until Tuesday.

A four-year starter at UConn, Lundt was a collegiate teammate of former Seattle guard Christian Haynes, now of the Miami Dolphins.

Lundt and Hart both joining the roster appear to be insurance for right tackle Abe Lucas, who is dealing with an ankle injury and is a question mark for Week 12 against the San Francisco 49ers.

Smith's departure from the Seahawks comes a day after veteran Trevon Diggs, like Hart, was signed to the active roster off the practice squad. The 22-year-old appeared in six games with Seattle after the team acquired him from the L.A. Chargers for a sixth-round pick ahead of the regular season.

After moving a 2027 day-three selection in order to acquire Smith just a few months ago, the Seahawks will presumably be interested in signing him to their practice squad, should he clear waivers.
