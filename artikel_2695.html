Eagles-Cowboys Thanksgiving inactives: Grant Calcaterra is a healthy scratch

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Philadelphia Eagles officially announced the inactives list for their Week 12 Thanksgiving game against the Dallas Cowboys.

The following Eagles players WILL NOT PLAY: Jonathan Jones, Arnold Ebiketie, A.J. Epenesa, Grant Calcaterra, Ajani Cornelius, and Cole Payton.

Jones and Ebiketie were ruled out on Wednesday's final injury report.

Jones might've been starting if he wasn't hurt since the Eagles benched Riq Woolen during their Week 11 game. But now it's back to Woolen.

Ebiketie's injury absence and Epenesa being a healthy scratch (that's a little surprising, especially with Ebiketie out) means the Eagles have four active edge rushers: Jalyx Hunt, Nolan Smith, Jonathan Greenard, and Mason Reiger (who was temporarily elevated from the practice squad).

Calcaterra, who is inexplicably on the roster, is a healthy scratch behind four tight ends: Dallas Goedert, Johnny Mundt, Zach Ertz, and Eli Stowers.

Cornelius, a former Cowboys draft pick, is a healthy scratch behind eight active offensive linemen.

Payton is the emergency third quarterback. The rookie can only enter the game if Joe Milton and Tanner McKee become unavailable.

OT Ajani Cornelius – Ninth offensive lineman.

QB Cole Payton* – Emergency third quarterback.
