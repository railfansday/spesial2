Travis Kelce Looks Back on Taylor Swift’s First Appearance at Arrowhead

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Travis Kelce fondly recalled the day Taylor Swift first came to watch him play at Arrowhead Stadium in September 2023. Looking back, he feels that the experience has evolved into something beyond his expectations.

According to PEOPLE, Kelce recalled Swift's first time watching him play during a Chiefs game. His comments came during an ESPN interview reflecting on his 14-year career in Kansas City. The singer attended the game against the Chicago Bears on September 24, 2023. She watched from a suite alongside Kelce's family and friends.

When ESPN asked Kelce about Swift's first game and the friends and family who now gather in his suite for home games, he said, "I'm a lucky man." "The fans in general make it so hospitable for everybody, not even just my family. Once they figured out that that was my suite, it's a game-day experience that has gone far beyond my imagination," he added.

Kelce also described Arrowhead Stadium as "home." "It's a part of my heart, playing in that stadium," he said.

The Chiefs won the game 41-10, with Kelce scoring a touchdown. He and Swift left the stadium in his Chevelle convertible. Their relationship quickly became a much-talked-about romance that brought the entertainment and sports worlds together. Swift also became a regular attendee at Chiefs home games.

Kelce bought the suite as a gathering place for his family and friends. Following Swift's first game-day appearance, it became a popular game-day destination. Celebrities such as Tom Cruise, Selena Gomez, and WNBA star Caitlin Clark have visited the 20-person suite. Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes also praised Kelce in the ESPN profile, describing him as the "true Chiefs' Chief."
