Rhamondre Stevenson suffers knee injury in Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

New England Patriots RB Rhamondre Stevenson (knee) had six carries for 32 yards and caught three passes for 46 yards and a touchdown against the Green Bay Packers in Week 9 before exiting with a knee injury.

Why it Matters: Stevenson was off to a solid start before the knee injury brought an early end to his day. The severity of the injury remains to be seen, and with TreVeyon Henderson's (illness) availability also in question, Corey Kiner is worth a waiver add.
