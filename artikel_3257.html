Ariana Grande Doc to Show the Process Behind ‘Eternal Sunshine’ and ‘Petal’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

An upcoming two-part televised special event will show the work Ariana Grande put into preparing for her recent Eternal Sunshine tour. The documentary, The Eternal Sunshine Diaries: The Making of the Tour, premieres on Peacock on Dec. 11 and chronicles the 30 days that led up to the first show of the trek, which marked her first tour since 2019. Then a concert film, The Eternal Sunshine Tour, premieres on Dec. 12.

The doc shows how Grande was simultaneously prepping for the tour and wrapping up Petal, her eighth album, which she released while on the road. She also had to get ready for an intense live production that split the main part of the concert into five acts before an encore. The Eternal Sunshine Tour is a concert film, which Peacock promises is “a cinematic celebration of healing, connection, and the music that has defined [Grande’s] life.” The set list at the tour’s June Oakland kickoff included 11 songs from Eternal Sunshine, as well as selections from Positions, Dangerous Woman, Thank U, Next and others, as well as Petal’s “Hate That I Made You Love Me.” By the end of the tour, which Peacock describes as a journey “through the collapse of her reality, a cosmic rebirth, a reconnection with her inner child, and the memories that shaped her,” she’d worked in several other songs off of Petal. She has not yet named which songs appear in the picture.

Grande helped oversee the making of both films and gets an executive producer credit on each.
