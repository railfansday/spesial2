Random: Did Dave The Diver Just Tease A GTA 6 Collab?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

If you didn't already know, Dave The Diver actually has something in common with Rockstar's new open-world title, Grand Theft Auto 6. In both games, you can go diving!

Now, can you imagine a collab? Well, before we get too carried away, it might already be on the mind of the Dave the Diver developer Mintrocket, as the game's official social media account has shared a rather interesting image today. Here it is:

If you've been playing GTA 6 over the past week, this character should look rather familiar, as it appears to be Mintrocket's take on an individual known as Brian (not to be mistaken with the Dave the Diver character, Cobra). The male protagonist in GTA 6 (Jason) works for Brian.

The whole look, including the shirt, seems very similar to Rockstar's character. And then there's the Vice City-like backdrop. Just about every reply to this post so far seems to be convinced it's Brian. However, as one fan notes, it might be hard to get him out of retirement.

While nothing has been mentioned about an official collab, it's worth noting Dave the Diver has already done some pretty crazy crossovers in the past with titles such as Yakuza: Like A Dragon, and it's teaming up with Subnautica early next year. Dave has also appeared in games such as Fortnite.

And as part of the GTA 6 marketing campaign, Rockstar did a collab with NBA 2K27, another title under parent company Take-Two.
