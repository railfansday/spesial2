Estonia closes border to Russia after suspected drone factory arson

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Tallinn has accused Russia of conducting hybrid warfare after a drone factory was set alight in Viimsi. Now it will close its border crossings at the start of December.

Estonia foiled an attempted Russian arson attack on a drone fractory, authorities said on Sunday, prompting the country to close all border crossings with its larger neighbor.

Prime Minister Karsten Michal said that Russia's GRU intelligence service had attempted to sabotage the Estonian defence company Threod Systems in Viimsi on Saturday night.

He said "extensive damage" had been averted after a response by law enforcement, who detained the suspected attackers.

"This is part of Russia's broader hybrid war against Europe, with the aim of decreasing support to Ukraine and pressure on Russia," Michal said.

Hours after the announcement, Estonia's Foreign Minister Margus Tsahkna said the country would close all border crossings with Russia by December 1.

"Russia must understand that hostile actions against Estonia come with consequences. Attempts to intimidate, destabilise, or undermine our security will not go unanswered," he said on social media.

"Closing the border crossings will reduce Russia's ability to conduct hostile activities in Estonia and strengthen our efforts to prevent sanctions circumvention."

Tsahkna said he informed his Finnish, Latvian, and Lithuanian counterparts of the decision.

Moscow said it would verify whether Estonia was planning to close all border crossings.

"If these are Estonia's actual plans, they will be seen as yet another bout of aggressive and unbridled Russophobia," Russian Foreign Ministry spokeswoman Maria Zakharova told the TASS news agency.

Security forces arrested eight people living in Latvia, Chief Prosecutor Astrid Asi told journalists.

Threod Systems manufactures reconnaissance and attack drones. In August, Milrem, a Tallinn-based defence subcontractor, was targeted in an attempted arson attack. Latvian nationals were arrested in connection with the incident.

Estonia, a member of the European Union and NATO, has three border crossing points with Russia: Narva, Luhamaa and Koidula.

Russia suspended vehicle traffic through Narva in February 2024, although pedestrians were still allowed to cross.

The crossings are among the few remaining operational overland links between Russia and the European Union since Moscow launched its full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022.
