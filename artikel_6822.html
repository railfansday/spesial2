All the Stars at the 2026 CFDA Fashion Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

From Hailey Bieber to Elle Fanning to Pamela Anderson

Every fall, the Council of Fashion Designers of America (CFDA) brings together designers, editors, and the biggest names in entertainment to celebrate another year of American fashion. At a dinner held at New York City’s storied Natural History Museum, underneath a 94-foot-long model of a blue whale, new faces and tenured creatives alike are honored for their contributions to the industry—with awards such as the Emerging Designer of the Year and the American Accessory Designer of the Year handed out throughout the evening.

This year, the event was hosted by Keke Palmer, with presenters including the likes of Demi Moore, Naomi Osaka, Lupita Nyong’o, Matt Damon, and Cole Escola. Ralph Lauren took home the American Menswear Designer of the Year award (for the third time!), Rachel Scott of Diotima was awarded American Womenswear Designer of the Year (for the second time), and Ana Khouri was recognized with the aforementioned accessory designer accolade. Colleen Allen won the coveted Emerging Designer trophy, in a stacked lineup of nominees that included Lii’s Zane Li and Emily Dawn Long, among others.

In addition to the winners announced last night, several powerhouses were also spotlighted. Anthony Vaccarello, who recently announced his departure from Saint Laurent after a decade as creative director, was recognized as the International Designer of the Year, joined by YSL muses Hailey Bieber, Zoë Kravitz, Rosé, and Anja Rubik. Tory Burch received the Positive Change Award, Linda Fargo accepted the Founder’s Award in honor of Eleanor Lambert, and Gap Inc., under the direction of Zac Posen, took home the Innovation Award.

The Geoffrey Beene Lifetime Achievement Award went to photographers Inez van Lamsweerde and Vinoodh Matadin, Eva Chen accepted the Media Award in honor of Eugenia Sheppard, and the Isabel Toledo Board of Directors’ Tribute was awarded to Susan Cianciolo. Lastly, the Fashion Icon Award—previously presented to everyone from A$AP Rocky to Zendaya to Naomi Campbell—recognized architect Peter Marino.
