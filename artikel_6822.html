Aneha Claims Fans Didn’t See an Accurate Edit of Her Wedding to Cam on ‘Love Is Blind’

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

“You wanna get the raw footage let's get the raw footage.”

Aneha and Cam were one of the few couples to make it to the altar on Love Is Blind season 11, and Aneha ended up saying... “I don’t!” But then, somewhat awkwardly, she walked back down the aisle and made a joke about still being Cam’s fiancée, while the cameras cut to Cam looking sad post-rejection. However! Turns out there’s more to this edit than meets the eye.

Aneha hopped on Instagram to defend herself, explaining that both she and Cam agreed it would "be a no for both of us" at the wedding, but wanted to go to the altar anyway just in case. "No one got blindsided, I want to make that very very clear,” she explained, before adding that they’d also agreed to stay together, which is why she joked about still being his fiancé.

"Everyone laughed, you wanna get the raw footage let's get the raw footage,” she said. “It wasn't sad, solemn, sorrowful, blah blah blah that you see on screen. ‘Cause how weird would that be.”

Aneha also pushed back on comments that she's not socially aware with "I have more social awareness in my pinkie than most of these crazy mean bullies in my comments," and called out the show for cutting to “Cam’s sad face” after her joke. As she put it: “They cut out the part where Cam responds and says ‘you have the ring, don’t you!’”

Aneha ended her video asking fans to “Please stop with this narrative that I broke Poor Cam's heart. Okay? We all were in the know, clearly you guys weren’t in the know, you’re in the know now. And if you still refuse to believe what I'm telling you...I tried. I tried to spread the truth.”
