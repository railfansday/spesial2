Carrick previews Roma showdown: “The 7-1 match was a special night for us.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

It’s the eve of the Champions League for Roma, who will play tomorrow at Old Trafford against Manchester United. Gasperini’s press conference is scheduled for this evening (around 6:00 PM), while Carrick, the hosts’ unsettled manager, has already spoken.

“The negative results haven’t changed my attitude. The atmosphere in the dressing room is good. Everyone’s commitment to the club, and vice versa, has never been in doubt.”

One of the most criticized at the moment is Lisandro: “I have no concerns about commitment. These are things that happen in football. Sometimes you do things, make decisions, and sometimes it goes well, other times a little less so. Commitment is certainly not a problem.”

“I don’t think that will be the case,” Carrick replied when asked if the next two games could be his last in charge of Manchester United.

The manager spoke about the 7-1 victory a few years ago: “A magical night, proof that sometimes things can change so quickly. One of the greatest nights.” The midfielder scored a brace on that occasion. Carrick also confirmed Dorgu’s absence for tomorrow’s match.
