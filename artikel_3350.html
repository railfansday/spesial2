Netflix Officially Brings C.S. Lewis’ First Narnia Story to Life in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Hannah covers horror, sci-fi, and games at Collider, where she writes news and features, reviews new releases, and interviews the people who make them. She’s helping build out Collider’s gaming coverage, with Pokémon as one of her specialties. If she isn’t writing about a monster, she’s probably watching one on a tape from her VHS collection.

Lucy Pevensie finds a whole world waiting on the other side of a wardrobe. There are people to meet, rules to learn, and a witch who’s already made everybody miserable by the time she arrives. The Magician’s Nephew goes back far enough that two children get to watch that world come into existence. It’s a pretty extraordinary way to enter Narnia, especially for anyone who’s spent years wondering how all of it ended up behind some coats.

C.S. Lewis published the story in 1955, five years after The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe, but placed its events at the beginning of Narnia’s history. Greta Gerwig is starting her adaptation there, too. The filmmaker behind Barbie and Little Women has described falling in love with the idea of a lion singing a world into being when she read the book as a child. Now she gets to decide what that sounds like in a theater.

Gerwig’s Narnia: The Magician’s Nephew arrives on Netflix on April 2, 2027, following a theatrical release on February 12 and IMAX previews beginning February 10. Netflix reaffirmed the movie's place on its 2027 slate during its Texas Book Festival appearance. David McKenna and Beatrice Campbell lead the adventure as Digory Kirke and Polly Plummer, two friends whose encounter with magical rings takes them far beyond London.

Four worlds and four completely different ideas of what a good life looks like. This is where you belong, what it would ask of you, and what you would get back in return.

You belong somewhere old enough that the mountains have names and somebody can still tell you who gave them. Middle-earth is for people who think the right thing is worth doing even when it will not be finished in their lifetime.

You want the sea, a crew, and absolutely nobody telling you where you are allowed to go. The Grand Line is the world for people whose main ambition in life is finding out what is over there.

You would rather be feared and useful than liked and useless. The Continent is a world where the monsters are the straightforward part of the job and the people are where it all gets complicated.

You want the myths to be real and loose in the actual world, and you want to find out at about thirteen that you were never anywhere near as ordinary as everybody kept telling you.

For viewers who know Narnia through the Pevensies, Digory and Polly give Gerwig a different way into familiar territory, because they’re discovering how travel between worlds works before anyone has a convenient wardrobe to walk through. Digory’s Uncle Andrew has been experimenting with magic, and the children end up having to deal with consequences he’s quite happy to leave to somebody else. The adventure takes them through other worlds before they reach Narnia at its beginning.

That makes the creation itself something the audience gets to experience alongside them. The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe asks you to believe a place like Narnia could exist just out of sight, but The Magician’s Nephew lets you be there while it happens. There’s room for Gerwig to make that first encounter strange and overwhelming, even for people who already know Aslan’s name and exactly how much trouble a certain witch will cause.

Emma Mackey plays the White Witch, with Carey Mulligan, Daniel Craig, Meryl Streep, and Ciarán Hinds also among the cast. Mark Ronson and Andrew Wyatt, who previously worked with Gerwig on Barbie, are composing the score. For a story in which music helps bring a world to life, they have quite an opening number to work toward.

Narnia: The Magician’s Nephew opens in theaters February 12, 2027, and streams on Netflix April 2.
