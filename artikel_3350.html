‘The Gilded Age’ Renewed For Season 5 At HBO

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

HBO has renewed The Gilded Age, the company’s hit period drama from creator Julian Fellowes, for a fifth season. Season 4 is currently airing new episodes on Sunday nights on HBO and streaming via HBO Max.

The news arrives following last week’s episode (403) titled “Ain’t That Good News,” which welcomed viewers to the wedding of Peggy Scott (Denée Benton) and Dr. William Kirkland (Jordan Donica), the grand celebration of love that took place in 1886 New York. And while the nuptials are a highlight of Season 4, the bumpy road to the altar has also been electrifying to watch. Fans may never tire of seeing Dorothy Scott (Audra McDonald) going toe-to-toe with Peggy’s now mother-in-law, Elizabeth Kirkland (Phylicia Rashad), which has no reason to end just yet, just because their children have made things official.

The season’s other high points include watching George (Morgan Spector) battle addiction to pain medicine, which has helped him grow a bit warmer to his wife Bertha (Carrie Coon), the birth of their first grandchild, Oscar (Blake Ritson), and Enid’s (Kelley Curran) lavish wedding of their own, and their battle over becoming one to produce an heir, Marion’s (Louisa Jacobson) maybe baby, and her frustrating back-and-forth with Larry (Harry Richardson), and Ada’s (Cynthia Nixon) forbidden love. The full cast list includes Coon, Christine Baranski, Nixon, Spector, Jacobson, Benton, Taissa Farmiga, Richardson, Ritson, Ben Ahlers, Ashlie Atkinson, Dylan Baker, Curran, Donica, Jessica Frances Dukes, Amy Forsyth, Jack Gilpin, Simon Jones, Celia Keenan-Bolger, Ben Lamb, Audra McDonald, Brian Stokes Mitchell, Debra Monk, Hattie Morahan, Donna Murphy, Kristine Nielsen, Kelli O’Hara, Taylor Richardson, Douglas Sills, Erin Wilhelmi, John Douglas Thompson, Taylor Trensch, James Scully, Dennis Haysbert, Maggie Kuntz, Neal Huff, Bonnie Milligan, Jim Gaffigan, Dallas Roberts, Andrew Burnap, Elizabeth Marvel, Adrienne Warren, with Bill Camp and Phylicia Rashad.

Viewership for Season 4 has grown week over week, with Episode 3, Peggy’s wedding episode, hitting a season high of 4.3 million U.S. cross-platform viewers (L+3). Season 4 episodes are averaging 5.9 million U.S. cross-platform viewers and growing, outpacing last season by nearly 20 percent and tracking as the show’s best season.

“Julian Fellowes and the cast of The Gilded Age have once again captured the zeitgeist this fall, delivering another captivating, unmissable season, and we love how our growing audience has continued to embrace the series year after year. We can’t wait to return to 61st Street, as the Russells, van Rhijns and Kirklands continue to wrestle with the cost of ascent, while we expand the canvas and deepen the operatic stakes for these beloved characters,” said Francesca Orsi, President, HBO Drama Series and Films.

Adds Erin Underhill, President, Universal Television, “It’s very exciting and deeply satisfying to see audiences fall more in love with The Gilded Age every season. Our remarkable cast, writers – led by Julian Fellowes, and the entire creative team continue to make this world feel both timeless and resonant while keeping us on the edge of our seats season after season. We can’t wait for audiences to see what’s in store for Season 5.”

Fellowes serves as the show’s creator/writer/executive producer. Sonja Warfield is The Gilded Age‘s writer and executive producer. Salli Richardson-Whitfield is director and executive producer alongside Gareth Neame, David Crockett, Robert Greenblatt, Kate Churchill, Davita Scarlett and Elaine Aronson; co-executive producers Erica Armstrong Dunbar and Holly Rymon. The drama is a co-production between HBO and Universal Television, a division of Universal Studio Group.
