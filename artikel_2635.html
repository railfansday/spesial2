Brock Purdy is dealing with an ankle injury, status uncertain for Week 12 vs. Seahawks

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

San Francisco 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy is dealing with an ankle injury, head coach Kyle Shanahan told reporters on Wednesday.

Shanahan specified that Purdy does not have a high-ankle injury, but the quarterback may not be able to play Sunday against the Seattle Seahawks.

If Purdy can't start the NFC West divisional game, backup quarterback Mac Jones is expected to take over.

San Francisco is 9-1 and leads both the division and the NFC. The 49ers had their bye week in Week 8 and have won three consecutive games.

Purdy is having one of the best seasons of his career. In just 10 games, he has already thrown more touchdowns (23) than in each of his last two seasons (20). Purdy has thrown for 2,624 yards and five interceptions, and has added 222 additional yards and two more scores on the ground.

With Purdy playing so well and remaining healthy, Jones hasn't seen much playing time, largely being used for kneel-downs.

Sunday's game between the Niners and the Seahawks will be the second time the divisional rivals have played this season. They met in Week 5, and San Francisco won 32-29.

Purdy finished the win 19 of 29, throwing for 189 yards, two touchdowns and an interception.

Seattle started the game with quarterback Sam Darnold in the first matchup against San Francisco. The veteran quarterback finished with 195 yards, 1 touchdown and 2 interceptions.

Darnold has missed the team's last two games, with Drew Lock leading the Seahawks to wins over the Arizona Cardinals and the Las Vegas Raiders. Last week's bye came at a good time for Seattle, as Darnold is expected to return for this game.

As the week progresses, the question seems to be whether Darnold and Co. will face off against Purdy or Jones.
