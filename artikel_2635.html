Sixers Bell Ringer: LeBron records fifth straight 20-point game in blowout vs. Pacers

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Sixers Bell Ringer Season Standings:VJ Edgecombe – 4Jaylen Brown – 3.5Tyrese Maxey – 3.5Joel Embiid – 3LeBron James – 3Anfernee Simons – 1Dean Wade – 1

The Philadelphia 76ers will be in the knockout stage of the NBA Cup.

By beating the Indiana Pacers 111-87, the Sixers clinch Group B in the East, finishing pool play 3-1.

It was a rough night from Tyrese Maxey, shooting 3-13 for only 14 points, but LeBron James, Joel Embiid and company picked up the star's off night.

The Sixers jumped out to a couple early leads, and withstood counter-runs from the Pacers by exploding in the third quarter. They ballooned the lead from 11 at halftime to 27 by the end of the frame.

It will be a red-eye flight to Houston for the Sixers as they are slated to play the Rockets tomorrow.

LeBron James: 21 points, 8-of-13 FG, 3-of-6 3PT, 7 rebounds, 6 assists, 1 block

James set the tone early, carrying over his scorching hot three-point shot from the past two games. He hit his first three attempts from deep, and helped the Sixers establish their first lead of the game singlehandedly.

He continued to display his all-universe passing ability, finding cutting Sixers all over the floor, especially Edgecombe and Embiid in the pocket.

He got comfortable in the midrange in the first half, and was attacking the rim through tough defense, drawing a couple fouls in the process.

It was his fifth straight game scoring 20 or more, securing it with a strong finish through Jarace Walker in the fourth.

Joel Embiid: 14 points, 5-of-14 FG, 1-of-3 3PT, 11 rebounds, 3 assists, 3 steals

It was not Embiid's most prolific outing yet it was great to see him get to his spots. He hit open shots off the catch and was persistently attacking even with a defender like Zubac in front of him. He had a beautiful post spin at the free throw line in the second quarter.

His consistent play was crucial with Maxey being aggressively defended by the Pacers. He picked up three steals, and despite letting a couple defensive rebounds turn into offensive rebounds, maintained a defensive prescence inside.

Anfernee Simons: 19 points, 7-of-14 FG, 5-of-12 3PT, 3 assists, 2 rebounds, 1 steal

Simons hot shooting continued against the Pacers. He hit a three in the first half and then four in the third quarter to help put the game way out of reach by the final frame. He kept it up to begin the fourth with a nice running layup over Siakam.

Simons has been a weapon off the bench, and he survived defensively tonight against the struggling Pacers.
