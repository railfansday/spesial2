Seahawks vs. Raiders: NFL Week 10 TV coverage map and CBS commentators

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Seattle Seahawks have one more game before their bye week, traveling to Nevada to play the Las Vegas Raiders. It's the first time the Seahawks have played a regular season game in Las Vegas, and all eyes will be on No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza and how he fares against the vaunted Seattle defense. Klint Kubiak will also be tasked with solving his former team's defense.

Just like in the late 1990s through 2001, the Seahawks and Raiders will square off on CBS. There's only one game being shown locally on CBS this weekend, with most of the country seeing the Kansas City Chiefs take on the Atlanta Falcons. It looks as if a good chunk of America will still be seeing Seahawks-Raiders, particularly markets like Minneapolis, Green Bay/Milwaukee, Cleveland, and other cities in which the hometown team is playing at 10 am PT on FOX and therefore don't want a CBS game in the corresponding time slot.

If you're in the blue areas of this 506 Sports map, you'll get Seahawks vs. Raiders at 1:05 pm PT. A note for folks in Alaska that Anchorage will be getting Chiefs-Falcons, whereas Fairbanks gets the Seahawks. Hawaiian Hawk fans, I have some bad news…

Ian Eagle and Ross Tucker, the B-team at CBS behind Jim Nantz and J.J. Watt, will be on the call from Las Vegas, with Evan Washburn as the sideline reporter. Tucker was promoted to B-team status after Watt replaced Tony Romo on lead broadcasts. Eagle called the Seahawks' win over the Carolina Panthers last season.
