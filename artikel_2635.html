Aryna Sabalenka must end a troubling trend in her matches

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

WTA World No. 2 Aryna Sabalenka was unstoppable this spring winning the Sunshine Double. Many thought the reigning Indian Wells champion would be the odds-on favorite to win her first WTA Finals title because she plays some of her best tennis on the Indian Wells hardcourts.

Mirra Andreeva, the 2025 Indian Wells champion, played the best match of her season against Sabalenka in the second round of the 2026 WTA Finals round robin action. Down 1-4, Andreeva won 11 straight games to win the match 6-4, 6-0.

A startling statistic from that match has plagued Sabalenka too often this year.

It is shocking that a player with Sabalenka's talent, power, and serve got bageled for the third time in 2026.

Sabalenka was bageled in back-to-back losses in June at Roland Garros against Diana Shnaider and at Berlin against Jessica Pegula.

Sabalenka added some finesse shots to her game. She is trying to keep her opponents off balance and unsure what is coming at them.

However, players are adjusting to her power game which frustrates her. Sabalenka spent the last couple of years earning lopsided wins. She is not accustomed to being on the losing end in a similar fashion.

Sabalenka is a great champion, and we cannot wait to see how she evolves her game to overcome this in 2027.
