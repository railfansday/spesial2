What is Christian Parker’s future after comments from Jerry Jones

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Things are looking bleak for the Dallas Cowboys defense, and for the man who runs it. Defensive coordinator Christian Parker was once thought to be the savior of the defense, now his job situation seems tenuous at best.

Earlier on Tuesday, Jerry Jones was on a radio show and was asked about Parker and the defense. His words were no vote of confidence for Parker. In fact, he seemed to say that all remedies are on the table.

That followed on the heels of head coach Brian Schottenheimer also not giving a confidence vote to Parker in his post-game press conference.

So over to you BTB. Is there anyway Parker survives this season to stay with the Cowboys as defensive coordinator in 2027? Or is it over and he's basically a dead man walking?
