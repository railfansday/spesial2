Tigers make late-night splash in Vegas to bolster bullpen: report

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

LAS VEGAS — The Tigers made the first significant move of Major League Baseball's general managers meetings.

Hours after Tigers president Scott Harris told reporters that his top priority was pitching, specifically swing-and-miss stuff for the back end of the bullpen, Detroit possibly found its next closer. The Tigers signed veteran right-handed reliever Emilio Pagan to a one-year contract, national baseball writer Jon Heyman of the New York Post reported.

Heyman reported late Tuesday, Nov. 10, that the deal is worth $10 million. The Tigers didn't immediately confirm the move, though Harris seemed to foreshadow something might be brewing earlier in the day Tuesday.

"We want to add more swing-and-miss in the back end of our pen. We want to add like a stable veteran presence who can get righties out and get lefties out and add a little more leadership to our clubhouse," Harris told Detroit reporters earlier Tuesday. "If we can find that, that'd be great."

Pagan, 35, is a 10-year major-league veteran who pitched the last three seasons for the Cincinnati Reds. He turned down a $10-million player option to stay in Cincinnati for 2027.

He also has pitched for the Seattle Mariners, A's, Tampa Bay Rays, San Diego Padres and Minnesota Twins. He has a 3.64 ERA for his career, and has struck out 626 over 549.1 innings. His walk rake is solid, 2.6 for his career and 2.3 during his last season with the Reds.

Pagan also has closer experience the last two years with the Reds, and earlier in his career with the Rays. The last two years with the Reds, he's saved 57 games.

Earlier this offseason, the Tigers declined the $12-million option for 2027 on veteran closer Kenley Jansen, who had an up-and-down season in his one year in Detroit. Jansen had 22 saves in 2026; no returning Tiger had more than two in 2026. Will Vest saved 23 games in 2025 but just two in 2026, a year mostly lost to elbow and hip surgeries. But he is trending toward being ready for the start of spring training, Harris said, and he also figures to be in the mix to close some games for manager AJ Hinch, who doesn't much care for locked-in defined labels for relievers.

The Tigers as a team blew 32 of 64 save opportunities in 2026, a big reason, but not the only reason, why they finished 76-86 and missed the playoffs for the first time since 2023.

Pagan had a 6.43 ERA in early May 2026, before going on the injured list with a hamstring injury. When he returned in early July, he dominated. From his return to the end of the season, he had a 1.88 ERA, 34 strikeouts and five walks in 28.2 innings. He throws a four-seam fastball in the mid-90s, as well as a cutter and a sinker, according to FanGraphs.

The MLB general managers meetings run through Thursday, and there's expected to be a flurry of moves in the coming days ahead of an almost-certain lockout when the Collective Bargaining Agreement expires at 11:59 p.m. Dec. 1.
