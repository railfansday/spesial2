Photo: Barcelona&#8217;s new-look 3rd kit for 2027/28

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The first image of the alternative kit set to be made use of by La Liga giants Barcelona for the 2027/28 campaign has come to the fore online.

And it features an altogether head-turning pattern.

The info comes courtesy of 'memorabilia1899' on 'X', considered highly reliable when it comes to all things Barcelona.

The kit is expected to feature a black base, across the shirt and shorts alike. The former, though, will be highlighted by a striking 'fire' pattern, with Nike's famous 'tick' and the Blaugrana's crest also set to be orange.
