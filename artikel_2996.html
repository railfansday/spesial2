Bristol-Myers Squibb: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A $1.5 billion to $2 billion revenue cliff looms in 2027, yet management's pipeline momentum and cost discipline may suggest the company can absorb the hit.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 18, 2026 at 3:00PM EST

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 29, 2026, Bristol-Myers Squibb (BMY -2.54%) announced an increase to its full-year earnings guidance as expanding sales across new medicines offset generic competition on older treatments. The drugmaker is building a broader commercial base to absorb upcoming loss of exclusivity (LOE), or patent expiration, on major therapies while advancing its drug development pipeline.

Management offers three takeaways on how the strategy is unfolding.

The company expanded revenue in its growth portfolio by 12% year over year to $7.7 billion during the quarter. Diversifying revenue across multiple blockbuster drugs helps replace sales lost as older therapies face generic competition.

Management advanced four registrational late-stage clinical trials over two months across oncology and rare lung diseases. The accelerated Food and Drug Administration (FDA) clearance of Symvexis, an oral therapy for multiple myeloma, establishes a new revenue driver in blood cancer care.

A strategic productivity initiative is driving $2 billion in cost savings to protect operating margins as legacy drug sales decline. Management generated $4.2 billion in operating cash flow during the quarter while raising full-year adjusted earnings per share (EPS) guidance.

Commercial execution is accelerating across new therapeutic launches, and management isn't hiding its satisfaction with the momentum. The company is leaning on proprietary manufacturing sites, pipeline execution, and cost discipline to secure future cash flows.

The business faces an estimated $1.5 billion to $2 billion revenue step-down in 2027 from European patent expirations on blood thinner Eliquis. Even so, internal manufacturing investments and active drug development create a resilient platform for long-term shareholder value.
