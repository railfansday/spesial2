Summit Midstream (SMC) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Volume gains in two basins lifted the outlook, while a major pipeline expansion moved forward.

Summit Midstream Corporation (SMC +8.37%) reported third quarter results driven by volume growth in the Rockies and Permian segments, prompting management to raise its full-year 2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance. The company reached a final investment decision for the Double E mainline compression expansion, which is projected to increase delivery capacity by 50% by late 2028. Management confirmed that the company is on track to reach its long-term leverage target of 3.6x by year-end, which may facilitate the reinstatement of a common dividend in 2027. Operations are supported by a backlog of 140 drilled-but-uncompleted wells and current drilling activity involving seven rigs across key basins.
