APEC's Pedrosa on Shenzhen Summit Expectations

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Eduardo Pedrosa, Executive Director for the APEC Secretariat, discusses his expectations for a highly-anticipated APEC Summit in Shenzhen, where leaders of the APEC economies, including Chinese President Xi Jinping and US President Donald Trump, will meet to discuss economic, technology and trade issues. He speaks with Stephen Engle on "Bloomberg: The Asia Trade". (Source: Bloomberg)
