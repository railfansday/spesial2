Vince Lombardi Biopic In Works From Producers James Manera And Massimiliano Musina

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: With the Green Bay Packers a week away from traveling to play the Los Angeles Rams on Thanksgiving Eve, another legendary Packer is looking to head to Hollywood. Sources tell Deadline that the family of the NFL franchise’s Vince Lombardi has granted an exclusive life-rights option to Eighteen Worldwide filmmakers James Manera and Massimiliano “Max” Musina for a feature film about the man who is considered the best football coach of all time.

“Vince Lombardi demanded excellence, and he gave it back in equal measure to the men who played for him,” said Manera, who wrote the script that won over the Lombardi family. “Writing this film meant living with that standard every day. To have the Lombardi family’s trust, and the voices of the players who were there, is one of the greatest responsibilities I have ever carried.”

Manera and Musina will produce the film through their Eighteen Worldwide LLC. Watch on Deadline

Insiders add that a scripted TV series and documentary projects are also a possibility through the rights deal. The film currently has no distributor and is expected to be independently financed. The agreement was made with Lombardi’s son, Vince Lombardi Jr., on behalf of the family, through its representative Luminary Group.

Former Lombardi players Dave Robinson, Boyd Dowler, Marv Fleming and Chuck Mercein are also expected to be part of the film. The story begins with Lombardi’s arrival in Green Bay in 1959, when the franchise’s very existence in the NFL was in jeopardy.

“Coach Lombardi built a culture of winning that still shapes how people compete and lead, long after they leave the locker room,” said Musina. “His philosophy was simple and almost impossible to live up to: prepare harder than anyone and take care of the man next to you. That’s how we’re approaching this film. James has written a script worthy of him, and we’re grateful to Vince Lombardi Jr., the Lombardi family, and Luminary Group for placing his story in our hands.”

Lombardi Jr will serve as an executive producer, as will Pete Enfield and Jonathan Faber of Luminary Group LLC. Co-executive producing will be Robert Schmidt, head of the Vincent T. Lombardi Foundation, who also served as founding executive director of the Pro Football Retired Players Association.

“Coach Lombardi’s lessons outlived every game he coached,” said Schmidt. “This film will carry them to a new generation, on and off the gridiron.”

When Lombardi took hold of the Green Bay Packers in 1959, the team was coming off a 1-10-1 season. He turned around the franchise’s fortunes immediately; over nine seasons, he coached the team to five NFL championships including winning the first two Super Bowls.

Lombardi applied his personal philosophy of chasing excellence, reaching one’s mental and physical best individually while giving all to the team, and pushing through adversity to achieve your goals to the game of football. In doing so, he gained undying loyalty.

“More than fifty years after his passing, Coach Lombardi’s name still stands for excellence, and protecting his legacy is a responsibility the family takes seriously,” said Enfield, president of Luminary Group. “James Manera’s screenplay and Eighteen Worldwide’s vision for a project of this magnitude earned the family’s trust, and Luminary Group is proud to be part of bringing it to audiences around the world.”

Harris Tulchin of Harris Tulchin & Associates will serve as co-executive producer and provide entertainment legal services for the project.
