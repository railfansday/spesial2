Nvidia shares surged 31.5% after Jensen Huang returned as CEO on November 23, following an 11-day absence. The stock added nearly $1 trillion in market value, setting a new single-day record. Before his return, Nvidia lost more than $1 trillion in value over seven trading days. President Donald Trump said he spoke with the board and helped secure Huang’s reinstatement. However, regulatory scrutiny continues over suspected chip shipments to China. Huang is scheduled to face three House committees on December 1 and the Senate Banking Committee on December 3.
