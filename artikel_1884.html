Ref Watch: Erling Haaland controversy among three Istvan Kovacs clear errors in Man City v Napoli

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Correspondent AUTHORITY Football journalist with experience at Reach PLC and PA Media; University of Central Lancashire graduate. FOCUS Fan-engagement content and match analysis across the Premier League and Championship. THE INSIGHT Oliver utilises a network of club and industry contacts to deliver verified reporting and fan-first content. He provides the human stories behind the results to ensure fans get the full picture as it happens.

Man City were held to a 2-2 draw by Napoli at the Etihad Stadium in their latest disappointing performance under Enzo Maresca.

City were forced to come from behind twice against Serie A opposition as they slipped to sixth in the Champions League standings.

Former Man United man Rasmus Hojlund put the visitors ahead shortly before the hour mark as he slipped the ball under Gianluigi Donnarumma from close range.

Ayyoub Bouaddi levelled with his first City goal in the 67th minute before Noa Lang put the visitors ahead.

Rayan Cherki's late strike rescued a point for City as referee Istvan Kovacs made fans fume with his performance in the middle.

Football Insider have picked out three decisions that Kovacs should have done better with during the Champions League clash.

Cherki has dazzled those across Europe with his trickery and immense attacking prowess in recent years, but he was fortunate not to be booked on Tuesday night.

The French playmaker made a clear attempt to control the ball with his arm in the 17th minute as he brought down a long pass, but the infringement was not spotted by any of the officials.

Referee Kovacs was unmoved as Cherki ultimately lost the ball and the game carried on, but it should clearly have been a yellow card to the City man and a free-kick to Napoli.

Haaland unsurprisingly made himself a nuisance for the visiting defenders throughout the game, and should have won a spot-kick early on.

He tried to meet a cross in the 19th minute, but was unable to jump, as both Matias Olivera and Sam Beukema effectively wrestled him to the floor inside the six-yard box.

Kovacs somehow failed to sanction the offence, and VAR also failed to get involved, to the detriment of City.

McTominay came in for some stick from City fans for his United allegiances, and he repaid their boos and jeers with a good performance and some robust challenges on their players.

The most notable of those came as he hacked Cherki down just after the interval, slightly outside the right edge of the Napoli area.

It was definitely a tackle worth a booking, considering his failure to get close to the ball and the proximity of the challenge to the penalty box.
