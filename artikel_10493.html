Student pilot panics after nationwide FAA ground stop hits during first solo cross-country flight

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

A student pilot flying his first solo cross-country trip was left asking air traffic control what to do after being told to land in the middle of the FAA’s nationwide ground stop.

The exchange happened on November 13, when the FAA halted flights at every US airport following what it described as “communication issues impacting multiple Air Route Traffic Control Centers.”

Audio of the call, which has since gone viral, captures a controller telling the pilot there is “a ground stop nationwide” and advising him to land his aircraft, before asking where he would like to do so.

“Ma’am, I’m a student pilot. Honestly, this is my first time doing solo cross-country, and I don’t know what to do,” he replied.

Student pilot gets caught in the air mid-outage 😬😬 pic.twitter.com/teLabecQ3h

After learning he had taken off from Hicks Airfield in Fort Worth, Texas, the controller advised him to head back there, warning him about two helicopters in the area.

When he asked if he was cleared to land, she explained she couldn’t give clearance for that airport and that he would need to switch to its advisory frequency. The pilot then checked the problem wasn’t with the airfield itself.

“Affirmative, nothing wrong with Hicks Airport itself,” the controller said. “Just suggest you land the airplane.”

“Will do, thank you so much, I’ll switch to Hicks now,” he replied,.

More than 3,500 aircraft were in the air when the ground stop was issued, and flights were halted for more than four hours before the FAA lifted it at around 8:29pm that evening.

The FAA said its technicians were investigating the issue, with preliminary reports suggesting it wasn’t caused by a cyberattack.
