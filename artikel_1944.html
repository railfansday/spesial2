Expert: British Government to intervene in Man City vs Premier League 114 charges battle

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Manchester City could avoid severe sanctions due to the owners' ties across Great Britain and the British government.

That is according to experienced former Tottenham, Aston Villa and Everton scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

A verdict on Man City's appeal against their 114 charges is expected to be revealed in January 2027, with their argument being heard in December.

An independent panel must then deliver its verdict within 30 days of the appeal being heard, and fans up and down the Premier League are waiting patiently.

Possible punishments have ranged from points deductions to the stripping of titles, even to expulsion all the way to the National League.

King is already concerned that the Citizens' punishment could be reduced, and he's now outlined why that might be based on the long wait.

When discussing Man City's legal battle, King told Football Insider: "There's not a full guilty or not guilty decision made on it. And that's what worries me.

"This has not only become a football thing; this has become a political thing, I'm pretty sure of it. Think how much money the guy who owns Manchester City has invested in the Premier League and the British government.

"No other club, apart from maybe Newcastle, have got the kind of money to invest in the Premier League or a Premier League club like the owners of Newcastle and Manchester City have got.

"None of the American owners... They haven't got this kind of money. You're talking banks and banks here. And that Middle East bank has got a lot of money.

"And I believe that the investment that these banks and these people, who own the football clubs, have made in Great Britain is probably far greater than the money they put in their Premier League football clubs.

"So, what would happen if they withdrew their funds from the British government? It would hurt the British government more than it would hurt Manchester City and Newcastle."

With everything going on behind the scenes, performances on the pitch appear to have taken a heavy hit.

Man City have won just one of their last seven matches across all competitions, most recently drawing 2-2 with Napoli at the Etihad.

Mick Brown told Football Insider that alarm bells are already ringing over Enzo Maresca's future, just 19 games into his tenure.

The likes of Nico O'Reilly, Marc Guehi, Phil Foden and even Erling Haaland appear to be suffering on the pitch, and that could be to do with the distractions off it.
