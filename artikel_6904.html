Storyblok: Senior Global Partner Specialist - USA

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Storyblok is a headless CMS that enables marketers and developers to create with joy and publish with confidence. It empowers you to deliver structured and consistent content everywhere: websites, apps, AI search, and beyond.

Legendary brands like Virgin Media O2, Oatly, and TomTom use Storyblok to make a bigger, faster market impact.

You will be joining a growing company where you can contribute to many “firsts”. Plus these benefits:

The Senior Global Partner Specialist owns the growth and commercial performance of Storyblok's global Longtail partner ecosystem, including New, Nurturing, and Emerging partners of all sizes.

This role develops and delivers scalable programs that enable partners, increase partner-sourced and partner-influenced revenue, and move partners through the lifecycle from onboarding to sustained commercial activity. Operating primarily through one-to-many engagement, automation, self-service, and data-driven segmentation, the role ensures Storyblok can support and grow a broad partner base efficiently. The role also identifies practical opportunities to use AI and emerging technologies to improve partner experience, efficiency, and scale.

The Senior Global Partner Specialist identifies high-potential Longtail partners that are ready to progress into the Focused segment and works cross-functionally to continuously improve Storyblok's global partner growth model.

U.S. Base Salary Range: $97,000 - $132,000

At Storyblok, we are committed to equitable compensation and pay transparency. The range displayed represents the target US base compensation range within which we are hiring for this role. For some roles Storyblok also offers a competitive commission structure.
