23 of Heidi Klum’s Most Iconic Halloween Costumes Through the Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Her severed hand costume is just the latest jaw-dropping one in her history.

No one does Halloween quite like Heidi Klum. Since 2000, the supermodel has been throwing yearly Halloween parties where celebs gather to mingle and have fun. Just as exciting as her annual bashes, however, are the truly over-the-top costumes she dreams up. Each year, Klum arrives in a costume more elaborate and incredible than the last—it’s truly a sight to see.

Ahead of Halloween, we took a walk down memory lane and rounded up some of the legend’s most memorable costume ensembles throughout the years. From adorable recreations of cartoon characters to fantastical superhero outfits, here are some of our favorites.

For her first-ever Halloween party, Klum wore a striking leather outfit that included a black wig, spiked collar, and knee-high boots. Her red lips and voluminous lashes were also nice touches.

The supermodel pulled a Bianca Jagger and arrived at the function in 2001 dressed up as the 13th-century horse-riding legend Lady Godiva. Her all-white ensemble featured a silver sequined bodysuit paired with a long bleached blonde wig.

The stunning look was created by designer Marc Bouwer. Here, Klum smiles during a fitting for the creation.

In 2002, the star decided on an adorable Betty Boop costume with a strappy red dress, red stilettos, elbow-length gloves, and suitably extravagant makeup. Her Halloween party that year was held at Capitale restaurant in New York City.

She’s pictured here posing with her fellow Victoria’s Secret Angel, Karolina Kurkova, while at the festivities.

For her 2003 holiday fête, the supermodel chose a gold alienesque look which showcased a metallic headpiece and gold metal teeth.

Klum showed up to her bash dressed in a witchy red look that consisted of a waist-length red wig, voluminous pointed hat, bone accessories, and plenty of red eye makeup. The party was held at Marquee in New York City that year.

She wore another spooky look for her 2005 Halloween get-together held in Happy Valley, New York. It’s hard to recognize her between the gigantic black wig and the pointy fangs.

The model attended her 7th annual Halloween party dressed as the forbidden apple (snake headdress and apple dress included) from the biblical story of Adam and Eve. Her then-husband, Seal, showed up as Eve.

In 2007, she stole attention at her get-together dressed in a head-to-toe cat ensemble which consisted of a gray leotard, bedazzled collar, and lifelike whiskers. The party kicked off in Los Angeles that year.

She’s pictured here testing the look a few days before the festivities.

A few years later, Klum was photographed on the red carpet walking into her event dressed as a superhero. The super original look showcased towering platform shoes, an elbow-length red wig, and all-over purple sparkles.

Klum shocked everyone when she opted to wear a skintight costume that transformed her into a skinless body. To make the look even more convincing, she was wheeled onto the red carpet on a stretcher with a “surgeon” by her side.

She flashed a smile at the camera while getting fitted a month or so before the event.

For her 13th Halloween party, Klum had a team of designers custom make her Cleopatra costume. She offered fans a sneak peek ahead of the finished product.

Klum decided to travel into the future and show up to her 2013 Halloween celebration dressed up as a much older version of herself. She donned a gray wig, a walking cane, and a strand of pearls for the occasion.

Klum wowed partygoers when she chose an intricate and hyper-realistic butterfly costume for her 2014 event.

Here’s a closer look at her awesome face paint that year.

The supermodel pulled out all the stops when she arrived at her 2015 party wearing a Jessica Rabbit costume. Between the lacy red dress, purple gloves, and makeup, Klum looks just like the iconic character.

Klum worked with talented makeup artists to bring her look to the next level.
