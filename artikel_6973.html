True Talent Solutions Partners: Founding Enterprise Account Executive

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Join an early enterprise sales team at a B2B SaaS company serving go-to-market teams. You will close complex enterprise deals while helping establish the sales playbook and shape how the business serves larger customers.

Manage full-cycle enterprise opportunities at organizations with 1,000 or more employees, including new business, expansions, and renewals.

Navigate multi-threaded sales cycles and build relationships with director, VP, and C-suite buyers across RevOps, Sales, and Marketing.

Use structured qualification and messaging approaches to progress complex deals, typically valued at $200,000 to $400,000.

Turn lessons from customer conversations, wins, and losses into repeatable enterprise sales processes.

Partner with Solutions Consulting, BDR, Customer Success, and Product teams to build pipeline, support adoption, and grow accounts.

Contribute to territory and account planning as the enterprise team and its processes take shape.

At least five years of quota-carrying Account Executive experience at go-to-market SaaS companies.

Experience selling sales, marketing, or RevOps technology to go-to-market buyers.

A track record closing enterprise deals with companies of 1,000 or more employees, including deals with $100,000 or more in ACV.

A clear, repeatable approach to complex sales, including executive engagement and multi-threading.

Fluency with MEDDPICC, Command of the Message, or an equivalent sales methodology, and a consistent record of exceeding quota.

Experience owning new logo, expansion, and renewal targets, ideally on an early-stage or growing sales team.

Familiarity with go-to-market data or sales engagement platforms and comfort building processes as the team grows.

Base salary: $125,000 to $140,000 USD annually. Visa sponsorship is available.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.
