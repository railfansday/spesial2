Andrew Garfield's Apple TV Series Wild Things Gets Release Date & First Look

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Credit: Manuele Mangiarotti/IPA/INSTARimages

Jude Law and Andrew Garfield will star as Siegfried Fischbacher and Roy Horn in the upcoming series Wild Things, and Apple TV just announced the premiere date and shared a teaser video.

Created by John Hoffman, Wild Things tells the true story of the iconic Las Vegas magician act Siegfried and Roy, who regularly incorporated tigers and lions into their performances. Back in 2003, a tiger attacked Horn on stage, bringing the duo's long-running stage show to a close.

The Wild Things teaser begins with a shot of the Las Vegas Sphere. Images of Law and Garfield as Siegfried and Roy appear on the exterior's LED display, along with a clip of the duo walking on either side of a white tiger. The video ends with the tiger growling and the April 16 premiere date reveal. Throughout the teaser, Siegfried says in a voiceover, "We will give you the most spectacular event anyone has ever seen." Roy then adds, "Siegfried and Roy, masters of the impossible."

Aside from creating the series, Hoffman is also showrunner, executive producer, director and writer. Garfield, Law, Leckart, Brian Grazer, Tony Leondis, Kathy Ciric, Will Malnati and director Matt Shakman serve as executive producers as well. Garfield and Law's co-stars in Wild Things are Justin Theroux, Jessica Madsen, Brett Gelman, Cameron Britton, Darius Fraser, Justin Bartha, Bill Heck, Jason Butler Harner and Wanda Opalinska.
