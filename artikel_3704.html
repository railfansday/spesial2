Jennifer Lawrence Ranks Her Onscreen Kisses: Who Was the Worst?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Jennifer Lawrence just ranked her on-camera kisses from best to worst — and it’s bad news for Chris Pratt.

The Oscar winner, 36, was asked to evaluate her screen smooches over the years during an appearance on the Thursday, November 19, episode of Watch What Happens Live With Andy Cohen. She placed her Hunger Games costar Josh Hutcherson — who also appeared as a guest on Thursday’s show — in first place.

“They were all so amazing,” Lawrence playfully said before naming Hutcherson, 34, as her top onscreen kiss.

Next came Hunger Games costar Liam Hemsworth.

In third place was Silver Linings Playbook costar Bradley Cooper and in fourth was Timothée Chalamet, with whom she shared a steamy scene in Don’t Look Up.

Lawrence’s Passengers costar Pratt, 47, ranked last among the five.

In 2015, the actress talked about sharing intimate scenes with Pratt — who was married to Anna Faris at the time — in a roundtable interview with The Hollywood Reporter.

“I had my first real sex scene a couple weeks ago, and it was really bizarre. It was really weird,” she said. “It was weird. And everything was done right; nobody did anything wrong. It’s just a bizarre experience.”

“I got really, really drunk,” she continued. “But then that led to more anxiety when I got home because I was like, ‘What have I done? I don’t know.’ And he was married. And it was going to be my first time kissing a married man, and guilt is the worst feeling in your stomach. And I knew it was my job, but I couldn’t tell my stomach that. So I called my mom, and I was like, ‘Will you just tell me it’s OK?’ It was just very vulnerable. And you don’t know what’s too much. You want to do it real, you want everything to be real, but then … That was the most vulnerable I’ve ever been.”

As for Hemsworth, 36, Lawrence previously admitted to kissing her hunky Australian costar offscreen during a 2015 appearance on WWHL.

“Liam and I grew up together,” she told host Andy Cohen. “Liam’s real hot. … What would you have done?”

For his part, Hemsworth described their awkward kissing scenes in the Hunger Games movies during a 2014 appearance on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon.

“When you look at it on the outside, it looks like a great picture,” Hemsworth said. “She’s one of my best friends, I love her, but if we had a kissing scene, she would make a point of eating garlic or tuna fish or something that was disgusting. Right before the scene she’d be like, ‘Yeah, I ate tuna’ or ‘I had garlic, and I didn’t brush my teeth.’ And I’d be like, ‘Fantastic, I can’t wait to get in there and taste it!’”
