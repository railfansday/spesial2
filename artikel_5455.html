GreenCore Solutions Rebrands as WeFetch.ai for the AI Shopping Market: gtinfetch.ai lead product

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

AI sector in 2026: 3 billion AI users, half of consumers AI shopping, 8 in 10 buyers in leading sectors on AI agents, $2.7 trillion in AI spending

VANCOUVER, BC, Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- GreenCore Solutions Corp. (GSC) today announced it is rebranding as WeFetch.ai, a maker of artificial intelligence (AI) software for AI shopping, with 84 million barcodes across 70 countries live for AI agent shopping by consumers, retailers and brands.

The turn came in November 2025, when Anthropic released Claude Opus 4.5. GSC's management team recognized a confluence of events:

AI commerce has arrived, and it relies on gtinfetch.ai or similar AI software products to execute reliable transactions over x402, the new financial system's payment protocol.

One record for every product an AI agent might buy, keyed on the barcode, the Global Trade Item Number (GTIN). It answers only when the sources agree, and says "unknown" rather than guess. Around that record, a door for each party in AI shopping:

It offers a promotional and paid per answer model over x402.

WeFetch.ai, the barcode fetch system for AI agents. It answers for 84 million products in 70 countries, with 3.5 million points of sale on its own record, and includes:

It is AI native, and works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Mistral, Perplexity and Muse. Every answer is signed.

"I am pleased to announce to shareholders that we moved early into the AI agent market," said Matthew Keddy, Chief Executive Officer. "In the 11 months of 2026, AI reached 3 billion users, and 8 in 10 buyers in leading sectors went to AI agents. Those companies broke from the trendline. The 2 in 10 manufacturers still starting out are 4 times behind, 2 to 5 years back, and facing a specialist gap. In this market, behind stays behind: customers have moved to trading through AI agents, and the laggards lose value and velocity."

"We read the same signals everyone else had, and we acted. We treated the arrival of AI agents as a fiduciary matter for our shareholders; doing nothing would have been the mistake. We called it with the 8 in 10. The 2 in 10 stood still and watched. Today, we lead. With WeFetch, millions of AI agents can work with our barcode (GTIN) algorithm in real time, every transaction checked twice and recorded in our Record of Cleared Orders (RCO). Structural change this deep rarely happens in under 12 months. We saw it coming, we built for it, and we got it right."

The market: Gartner forecasts $2.7 trillion in AI spending in 2026. McKinsey projects that AI agents will orchestrate $3 trillion to $5 trillion in global commerce by 2030, up to $1 trillion of it in US retail. Every one of those AI agent purchases needs the right barcode.

WeFetch.ai is AI software for AI shopping, with products for shopping, retail and brands. Powered by gtinfetch.ai, WeFetch.ai is headquartered in Vancouver, Canada.
