JetBlue Launches 8 New Routes From New No.1 Airport: See Full List Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

JetBlue Airways quickly expanded its presence in markets affected by Spirit Airlines' collapse earlier this year. One airport where it has significantly increased operations is South Florida's Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport (FLL). According to schedule data from aviation analytics provider Cirium, it is now JetBlue's busiest airport by scheduled departures, overtaking John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK) and Boston Logan International Airport (BOS).

During peak periods this winter, the airline expects to offer more than 150 daily departures, marking its largest schedule ever at the airport. The carrier has already added several destinations to its network from Fort Lauderdale in recent months, and further additions are planned for next year.

JetBlue recently announced plans to introduce two new daily nonstop international routes from Fort Lauderdale, serving Comayagua, Honduras, and George Town, Cayman Islands. The Honduras service will operate to Palmerola International Airport (XPL), which serves the capital, Tegucigalpa, while flights to Grand Cayman will operate to Owen Roberts International Airport (GCM).

Comayagua will be an entirely new destination for the airline and its second in Honduras. The two additions will bring the airline's international network from Fort Lauderdale to 20 destinations. In addition, JetBlue will operate daily nonstop flights to Port of Spain, Trinidad and Tobago, for a limited period.

It is very evident that JetBlue has been expanding into Spirit's former international network from Fort Lauderdale. Both of its newly announced routes to Honduras and the Cayman Islands were previously served by the ultra-low-cost carrier. The same applies to the new Colombian routes that it launched in October.

In fact, before ceasing operations in May, Spirit was the largest US airline operating in Colombia, serving seven cities from Fort Lauderdale. JetBlue quickly moved into these markets. Just two days after the airline's shutdown, it announced plans to introduce daily flights to Barranquilla's Ernesto Cortissoz International Airport (BAQ) and Cali's Alfonso Bonilla Aragón International Airport (CLO), complementing its existing services to Medellín and Cartagena.

Notably, JetBlue's expansion into these former markets is not limited to international routes. The airline also introduced nonstop services from Fort Lauderdale to John Glenn Columbus International Airport (CMH), Indianapolis International Airport (IND) and San Diego International Airport (SAN) this month. All three routes were previously served by Spirit.

JetBlue is doubling down at FLL just as Spirit Airlines is forced to pull back.

These are just the latest additions to JetBlue's network from the South Florida airport. The airline has also introduced several new routes in previous months. In July alone, it launched eight routes, including daily services to Baltimore/Washington International Thurgood Marshall Airport (BWI), Charlotte Douglas International Airport (CLT), Chicago O'Hare International Airport (ORD), George Bush Intercontinental Airport (IAH) in Houston, Nashville International Airport (BNA), and Mercedita International Airport (PSE) in Ponce, Puerto Rico.

JetBlue is now the largest airline at Fort Lauderdale in terms of scheduled departures, and its growth is reflected in the latest schedule figures. According to Cirium data, the carrier has scheduled more than 3,900 flights from the airport this month, up about 57% from November last year.

The expansion is even more apparent in its overall winter schedule. JetBlue has scheduled approximately 21,500 departures from Fort Lauderdale for the winter 2026/27 season, compared to nearly 13,700 during the previous winter. Its nonstop network has also grown from 47 destinations in winter 2025/26 to 62 this season.
