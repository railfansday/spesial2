ADT Inc. (ADT) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Merger terms, free cash flow and customer attrition all shaped the quarter's results.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

ADT Inc. (ADT +0.20%) entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Thrive Holdings in an all-cash transaction expected to close in the first quarter of 2027. Management reported that the company is transitioning toward an intelligent, connected platform through investments in artificial intelligence and next-generation sensing technologies. The company also announced a new service partnership to support retail customers and expanded its digital distribution footprint. Management stated that the organization remains focused on its core safety differentiators and customer experience while operating as an independent company until the merger concludes.

Operator: Hello, everyone. Thank you for joining us, and welcome to the ADT third-quarter 2026 earnings conference call. I will now hand the conference over to Elizabeth Landers, Vice President of Investor Relations. Please go ahead.

Elizabeth Landers: Good morning, and thank you for joining us today. Speaking on today's call are Jim DeVries, ADT's Chairman, President, and CEO, and Jeff Likosar, our Chief Financial Officer. As you know, on October 29th, we announced that ADT has entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Thrive Holdings. In consideration of the pending transaction, we will not be taking questions on today's call. Additionally, we will not be providing financial guidance and are withdrawing previously issued guidance. This morning, we issued a press release and presentation summarizing our third quarter results. Both are available at investor.adt.com. During today's call, we'll reference certain non-GAAP financial measures.

Reconciliations to the most comparable GAAP measures are included in the earnings presentation on our website. Unless noted otherwise, all financials and metrics discussed reflect continuing operations. Investors and stockholders are urged to read the proxy statement when it becomes available. Now, I'm happy to turn the call over to Jim.

Jim DeVries: Thanks, Elizabeth, and good morning, everyone. I'll keep my remarks brief this morning, focusing on the transaction we announced last Thursday, and Jeff will walk through our third-quarter results. On Thursday, October 29th, we announced that we have entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Thrive Holdings in an all-cash transaction. Under the terms of the agreement, which our board of directors unanimously approved, ADT stockholders will receive $10 per share in cash upon completion. $10 represents a premium of 46% to ADT's unaffected closing share price on October 27th and 52% to our 30-day volume-weighted average share price ending on that date.

The agreement values ADT at approximately $7.6 billion in equity value and $15.3 billion in enterprise value. We believe this transaction represents an important next chapter for ADT, bringing together our more than 150 years of security experience, nationwide service network, and trusted customer relationships with Thrive Holdings' long-term capital and engineering capabilities. I am pleased with this outcome, which recognizes the value built by our more than 12,000 employees and dealer partners, our longstanding commitment to providing peace of mind, and our continued advancement of ADT's capabilities and technologies. Our progress and this transaction deliver an attractive cash premium to our stockholders.

As is customary, we do not intend to disclose developments with respect to that process unless and until we determine disclosure is appropriate or otherwise required. Until the transaction closes, ADT will continue to operate independently as a public company. Importantly, with this transaction, ADT's focus has not changed. We remain committed to serving our customers, supporting our dealers and partners, and executing our key initiatives. During the quarter, we continued to make progress across our three core differentiators: unrivalled safety, premium customer experience, and innovative offerings.

We introduced Unusual Activity Alerts in ADT+, advanced pilots of our Origin AI presence sensing technology, expanded ADT Blu to walmart.com, and launched ADT Partner Services, through which we will install and service select products for Apple Retail and its customers beginning later this month. Before I turn the call over to Jeff, I want to thank our employees, our dealers and partners, and the first responders we work alongside every day. Your commitment is what has earned ADT the trust of millions of customers for more than 150 years. I'll now turn the call over to Jeff.

Jeff Likosar: Thanks, Jim, and good morning, everyone. I'll take the next few minutes to describe our third quarter with all comparisons year-over-year unless I note otherwise. In summary, I'm very pleased to report that ADT again delivered strong results with continued revenue growth, solid earnings, and robust cash generation, all in line with our expectations. Our consistent performance, while we continue to invest in our business, underscores the strength of our cash generation capabilities, which remain a highlight of our results. Adjusted Free Cash Flow, including interest rate swaps, was $217 million, up 4% in the quarter.

On a year-to-date basis, we're up 46%, having generated more than $1 billion through the first three quarters, in excess of last year's full-year level. Key drivers include lower cash taxes and interest, along with working capital management and some timing favorability. On the top line, total revenue was $1.35 billion, up 4%. Installation, product, and other revenue was $265 million, up 32%, driven by a higher mix of professionally installed systems under our outright sales model. Monitoring and related services revenue was $1.08 billion, down 1%, which includes the revenue loss from the October 2025 divestiture of our Multifamily business.

Ending recurring monthly revenue was $360 million compared to $362 million a year ago, with that decline also primarily reflecting the Multifamily Divestiture. Adjusted EBITDA was $665 million, down 2%. This reflects higher general and administrative costs, including technology investments, partially offset by the margin on our higher installation revenue. Adjusted EPS was $0.24, up 4%, and on a year-to-date basis, we have generated $0.71 of Adjusted EPS, up 6%, benefiting from a lower diluted share count along with lower interest expense. Turning to operational metrics, gross customer revenue attrition was 13.1% compared with 13% a year ago, with elevated non-pay cancellations partially offset by fewer voluntary disconnects. Trailing 12-month revenue payback was 2.3 years, consistent with the prior year.

Gross RMR additions were $12.1 million on 195,000 gross unit additions. Direct professional install unit and RMR additions continue to perform strongly, improving versus the prior year, offset by lower volume from dealers and bulk account purchases. During the quarter, we completed a bulk account purchase of approximately 8,000 customer accounts for $9 million in cash. In August, we secured $100 million of incremental borrowings under our First Lien Term Loan A due 2030. We designated the proceeds for general corporate purposes, which may include repayment of a portion of our 2027 notes. Including these proceeds, we ended the quarter with $228 million of cash and cash equivalents, with no borrowings under our revolving credit facility.

Net leverage was 2.7x Adjusted EBITDA compared with 2.8x a year ago. During the quarter, we returned $64 million to shareholders, including $23 million in share repurchases and $41 million in dividends. Year to date, we have returned $748 million to shareholders through dividends and the repurchase of 89 million shares. To conclude, we delivered solid overall third-quarter results with revenue growth, strong year-to-date cash generation, and stable core operating metrics, all while continuing to invest in our business for the long term. As Elizabeth mentioned, due to the pending transaction, we won't be taking questions today. Thank you to our employees for their continued dedication, and thank you to our customers, dealers, partners, and our investors.

Thank you all for joining us this morning. Operator, that concludes today's call.

Operator: This concludes today's call. Thank you for attending. You may now disconnect.
