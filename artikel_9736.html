To understand why a reranker is necessary, we must examine the difference between Bi-Encoders and Cross-Encoders:

All vector embedding models (Amazon Titan v2, OpenAI text-embedding-3, Cohere) are Bi-Encoders:

The Fundamental Flaw: Query tokens and document tokens never interact in the neural network's attention layers. The model compresses an entire 512-token document into a single fixed vector. Nuanced relationships (e.g., subtle conditionality, negation, or multi-step logic) are lost during compression.

A Cross-Encoder (such as BAAI/bge-reranker-base) does not produce isolated vector coordinates. Instead, it passes both the Query and the Candidate Chunk together as a single input pair:

Inside the Cross-Encoder's transformer layers:

If Cross-Encoders are vastly superior, why not use them for the entire search pipeline?

Compute Complexity: Running full cross-attention across 100,000 documents for a single user query would take dozens of seconds and require tens of thousands of dollars in GPU compute.

The industry gold standard is the Two-Stage Retrieval Pattern:

Total retrieval time: under 60ms with zero loss in precision!
