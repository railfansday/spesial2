This Walk-In Closet Blends Form and Function

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Rimadesio’s new wardrobe system brings endless possibilities.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE Decor editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

No longer purely utilitarian, the closet is evolving into a room in its own right. “I wanted to design the walk-in as a full-fledged architectural environment,” says Giuseppe Bavuso, art director of Rimadesio, an Italian brand that makes modular systems for defining and subdividing rooms. Hedon, their new bespoke wardrobe system, has cabinets, shelves, and racks in more than 300 finishes—from raffia to lacquered glass—that allow endless adaptation. “It gives value to an everyday ritual and creates a space where function and personal expression co-exist.”

Geoffrey Montes is an associate editor at ELLE Decor with a serious love for all things real estate and design. Before that, he worked at Architectural Digest, Galerie, and Preservation magazines, covering everything from jaw-dropping listings to world-famous architects and design events like Salone del Mobile and Homo Faber.
