Eltek (ELTK) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Production gaps and plating delays weigh on margins despite firm demand.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Eltek Ltd. (ELTK -0.85%) reported a net loss for the third quarter of 2026 as management focused on stabilizing manufacturing operations and improving delivery performance. The company reported a year-over-year decline in revenue and gross profit, which management attributed to production inefficiencies and the depreciation of the U.S. dollar against the New Israeli Shekel. Although customer demand remains strong, management stated that current production capacity is insufficient to meet all orders. Strategic initiatives include the qualification of a new plating line in early 2027 and the integration of foreign workers to enhance production throughput.

Our primary management focus is currently on two closely related areas: stabilizing our manufacturing operations and enhancing our production planning and management capabilities. Our objective is to better align production capacity with incoming orders and most importantly, improve our ability to meet customer delivery. We recognize that reliable delivery performance is essential to maintaining customer confidence and supporting our long-term business relationship. Accordingly, we are committed to maintaining transparent and open communication with our customers while working together to improve delivery performance and better manage expectations.

In parallel, we are establishing a more structured management approach with clearly defined operational priorities and ongoing performance monitoring. This enables us to identify gaps, measure progress and take corrective action where necessary. Let me elaborate on the progress we are making in these areas. One of our key priorities is to enhance the engineering support provided to our manufacturing operations. As you know, PCB manufacturing involves complex and processes that are influenced by various operating and environmental conditions. Since a significant part of our production still relies on our existing plating line, maintaining close engineering control and continuously optimizing these processes are essential to ensuring stable and consistent production.

To address this, we are expanding and reinforcing our process engineering team with the goal of providing broader engineering coverage throughout production operating hours. This will enable us to respond more quickly and effectively to production challenges, minimize disruption and improve overall process. In parallel, we are reinforcing our maintenance capabilities by transitioning to a more structured preventive maintenance strategy. Our maintenance team actively supports our core manufacturing operation as well as the advanced equipment installed across our facility in recent years. Enhancing process engineering alongside preventive maintenance is key to maximizing the availability, ensuring consistent operational quality and driving overall manufacturing efficiency. Another important development is the progress of our new plating line.

During the third quarter, we successfully completed the installation of the first plating line. We are currently completing the required testing and fine-tuning activities to establish the operating parameter necessary for consistent ongoing production. The results achieved so far indicate a significant improvement in manufacturing quality. We plan to begin the customer qualification process with our key customers in early 2027. Alongside improving the stability of our manufacturing operation, we are implementing additional management routines and processes designed to improve production planning and execution. These measures are intended to help us better align our production schedule with customer requirements and delivery commitments while making more effective use of our available manufacturing capacity.

Importantly, these practices are being implemented not only during production, but also at the order acceptance stage. This allows us to evaluate our available capacity and better broaden the delivery expectation with customers before committing to new orders. We are also continuously monitoring production bottlenecks and managing them more closely with the objective of maximizing throughput and improving the utilization of our existing production resources. As part of this process, we are introducing a more systematic approach to measuring operational performance. We are establishing key performance indicators, monitoring them on an ongoing basis and reviewing actual performance against our objectives.

This management process is designed to provide better visibility into production performance, identify areas requiring attention and ensure that corrective actions are implemented [ for this year ]. Turning to the commercial side of our business. We continue to experience strong demand for our products. Our main challenge at this stage is not a lack of customer demand, but rather our ability to fully meet customer requirements with our current production capacity. We are working intensively to address this gap. We expect the qualification and subsequent ramp-up of the new plating line will contribute to increased capacity and improve manufacturing efficiency.

In addition, our ongoing investment in production stabilization, engineering support and maintenance are expected also to support higher throughput and enable us to gradually increase sales. Regarding raw materials, the price increases of the previous quarter has moderated somewhat. Nevertheless, pressures in the raw materials market continue with an increasing allocation bias towards the AI sector for key raw materials. Market conditions remain highly dynamic with frequent changes and securing the required raw material is becoming increasingly challenging. Evolving environment may require significant adjustment to our procurement, production planning and supply chain strategy. As part of our efforts to enhance manufacturing capabilities, we continue to integrate foreign workers into our production operations.

To date, approximately 23 employees have joined various production departments. This is an additional step in reinforcing our workforce and supporting our ability to increase production capacity. To conclude, we recognize that there is still a significant work ahead of us. Our priorities are clear: stabilizing manufacturing operation, enhancing our engineering and maintenance capabilities, improving production planning and increasing our ability to meet. We believe that our disciplined management approach together with completion and qualification of our new line will provide a stronger operational foundation for Eltek and support improved business performance going forward. We remain fully focused on executing our operational road map and maintaining open transparent partnership with our customers.

We have strong market demand, a clear operational strategy and confidence that our investment in equipment, technology and talent will translate into sustainable profitable growth. I will now discuss our financial results. I will mention key financial highlights for the third quarter. All figures are presented in U.S. dollars. In my review, we will refer to EBITDA, a non-GAAP measure of financial performance. Please refer to our earnings release for the definition of EBITDA and [indiscernible]. Revenue for the third quarter of 2026 were $12 million compared to $13.3 million in the third quarter in 2025. Gross profit was $0.2 million compared to gross profit of $1.6 million in the prior period.

The year-over-year decline in gross profitability was driven by lower revenue volume, production inefficiency and the depreciation of the U.S. dollar relative to the New Israeli Shekel. Operating loss was $1.6 million compared to operating profit of $0.05 million in the third quarter of 2025. Financial income was $0.3 million compared to financial expenses of $0.3 million in the prior year period. The financial income in the current quarter primarily reflected the depreciation of the New Israeli Shekel against the US dollar. Net loss for the quarter was $1.2 million or $0.17 per share compared to net loss of $0.2 million or $0.03 per share in the third quarter 2025.

EBITDA loss was $1 million compared to EBITDA of $0.6 million in the prior year period. As of September 30, 2026, we have $10.4 million in cash, cash equivalents and short-term bank deposits, and no outstanding debt, providing us with a strong balance sheet. I'm now ready to answer your questions.
