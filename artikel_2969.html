NBA suspends Rockets' Marcus Smart three games after punching Charles Bassey

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

NBA suspends Rockets' Marcus Smart three games after punching Charles Bassey originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area

Houston Rockets guard Marcus Smart is being suspended three games without pay by the NBA after punching Warriors center Charles Bassey during Golden State's loss on Wednesday night, the league announced Thursday.

Smart received a Flagrant 2 foul and was ejected from the game after officials reviewed the altercation and determined he struck Bassey in the face.

The incident occurred late in the third quarter, when Smart and Bassey got tangled up under the Warriors' basket while fighting for a rebound. Then, as Bassey ran down the floor to get back on defense, Smart chased after him before throwing the punch.

The players quickly were separated, and Smart immediately left the floor, perhaps already knowing his night was over. Bassey, who did not retaliate, stayed in the game.

After the game, Bassey called out Smart's "fake toughness" while speaking to reporters.

"He's been playing dirty the whole game, man," Bassey said. "I remember in the first half, I was trying to set a screen. Bro, he grabbed my knees. He was playing dirty.

"I think I was trying to get a rebound. He was grabbing my hand, and I was trying to run back on defense. And then he got up on me and just swung, and obviously that was weak as …"

Bassey added that he thought Smart "was trying to get himself out of the game."

Smart will begin serving his suspension on Friday night, when the Utah Jazz visit the Rockets at Toyota Center.
