Nikki Glaser Signs Deal With Newly Created Netflix Is a Joke Studios

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The in-house unit at the streamer will be an incubator for projects that go beyond stand-up comedy.

Netflix is expanding its comedy ecosystem. The streamer has launched an in-house studio called Netflix Is a Joke Studios and signed Nikki Glaser to the new unit’s first talent deal.

The new studio will “take a holistic approach to comedy talent across multiple formats,” Netflix said in its announcement. Netflix is a Joke Studios, named after its stand-up comedy brand, will work with comedians to develop scripted series and original formats in addition to continuing the streamer’s commitment to stand-up showcases.

Under her overall deal, Glaser will develop and star in a scripted series — with Will Ferrell and Jessica Elbaum’s Gloria Sanchez Productions (Dead to Me, Netflix’s upcoming Prep series) executive producing — and headline and executive produce two new stand-up specials for Netflix. “Netflix gave me some of my earliest and biggest opportunities in comedy, so I couldn’t be more excited to begin this next phase of my career with them,” Glaser said in a statement.

Glaser’s first solo stand-up special, 2019’s Bangin’, debuted on Netflix. She also appeared on the streamer’s 2017 series The Stand-Ups. Her two most recent specials, 2024’s Someday You’ll Die (on HBO) and this year’s Good Girl (on Hulu), were nominated for Emmys, as was her writing for Good Girl.

“Great comedy starts with an unmistakable voice, and few comedians have one as fully formed as Nikki Glaser’s,” said Tracey Pakosta, vp comedy series at Netflix. “She’s fearless, funny and disarmingly real, which makes her the ideal first partner for the NIAJ Studios vision. We can’t wait to see the world she’ll build.”

Added Robbie Praw, Netflix vp stand-up and comedy formats: “I first met Nikki Glaser in a small comedy club 20 years ago and have had a front-row seat to her rise ever since. When Netflix’s The Roast of Tom Brady sent her career into overdrive in 2024, we watched audiences embrace what many of us have seen for years. She’s one of the sharpest comedic minds working today, with instincts to build something lasting well beyond stand-up. Netflix Is a Joke has always celebrated comedy in all its forms, and NIAJ Studios is the next step in our commitment to this community. We’re honored to be in Nikki’s corner as she builds what’s next.”

Glaser is repped by WME, Brillstein Entertainment Partners and Ginsburg Daniels.
