‘Good to get punched in the face’- Fernando Mendoza embraces harsh lesson from first NFL start

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Fernando Mendoza has taken a positive lesson from a difficult first NFL start after the Las Vegas Raiders suffered a 21-13 defeat to the Seattle Seahawks.

The rookie quarterback completed 17 of 29 passes for 143 yards as the Raiders struggled to find their rhythm against Seattle's defence at Allegiant Stadium.

Mendoza had been waiting for his opportunity to start after weeks of calls from Raiders fans for him to replace Kirk Cousins. However, his first outing also showed how much work lies ahead.

Rather than dwell on the disappointing result, Mendoza praised the Seahawks' defensive performance and insisted the experience could help him improve.

Speaking after the game, he said: "A lot of credit to them and coach Macdonald…It's good to get punched in the face because now, it's a great opportunity to grow and learn and to really unveil all the deficiencies that I have in my game and as a future leader, future hopeful leader of this offense."

It was an honest assessment from the rookie, who will now have plenty to review before the Raiders' next game against the Denver Broncos.

Mendoza's afternoon was made more difficult by the loss of star tight end Brock Bowers to an injury early in the contest. His availability could become an important factor as Las Vegas looks for more production from its passing game.

The numbers also showed the difference between the two offences. Seattle finished with 382 total yards compared with just 163 for the Raiders, while Drew Lock completed 14 of 17 passes for 240 yards and two touchdowns.

Jaxon Smith-Njigba was particularly difficult to contain, finishing with eight receptions for 171 yards and a touchdown.

Mendoza did manage a 10-yard rushing touchdown in the first quarter, but Las Vegas could not keep pace after Seattle scored twice in the third.

The Raiders are now 4-6 and face another difficult test against Denver. For Mendoza, the priority will be turning the lessons from his first start into progress as he continues his development in the NFL.
