Did Texas break LSU’s Lane Kiffin? Finebaum says, ‘This is a man who wants to be loved’

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Lane Kiffin admitted he is having nightmares.

Days after his LSU Tigers fell to 22-21 to Texas, thanks to Arch Manning found Raleek Brown for a 4-yard touchdown pass along the left sideline with 3 seconds left, the LSU coach admitted he hasn't slept.

"I wake up literally every hour the last two nights, and I have this nightmare of the last play of the game." Kiffin during a recent press conference. "I think it's a nightmare when I wake up that we didn't cover somebody to lose the game and that we lost to Texas. And then I wake up, and it's real."

It was College Football Playoff or bust for the LSU program, and that's not happening.

"Lane Kiffin looked like a sad sight on Monday," ESPN's Paul Finebaum said. "It was a sad sight to see. He sounded incoherent, even for him. While it is not unusual for coaches to curse, for Kiffin to drop two f-bombs was troubling.

"This is a man who wants to be loved, and now, his own fan base, which so fervently stood behind him during the long buildup for this season, seems to have, at least, momentarily, deserted him."

Later in the day, Finebaum discussed the topic on "First Take."
