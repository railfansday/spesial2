Bruce Springsteen’s Historic 1988 East Berlin Concert Chronicled in New Documentary

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

On July 19th, 1988, Bruce Springsteen and the E Street Band performed to an estimated crowd of 300,000 fans in East Berlin, just a few miles from the Berlin Wall that had divided the city in half for decades. “I want to tell you that I’m not here for or against any government,” he said to the crowd in prepared remarks that he delivered in German. “I have come to play rock & roll for the East-Berliners in the hope that one day all barriers will be torn down.” Just over a year later, the Berlin Wall fell.

The story of this incredible concert will be told in the upcoming documentary When The Walls Came Down, directed by Nicholas de Taranto and Danielle Peck and featuring new interviews with Springsteen, Jon Landau, Roy Bittan, Patti Scialfa, Stevie Van Zandt, and Max Weinberg. There are also conversations with former East Germans who attended the show, unseen archival footage, and unsealed Stasi files about the band.

“When Bruce and I discussed playing in Communist East Berlin in 1988, we knew it would have impact,” Landau said in a statement. “But it was only when we arrived at Radrennbahn Weissensee, saw the 300,000 fans thirsting for music, connection, and most of all, freedom, that we realized this was going to be one concert that would stay in our hearts — and the hearts of every single person who was there — forever. When the Walls Came Down tells the beautiful and emotional story of that day with complete integrity and accuracy.”

Springsteen himself feels the same way. “In East Berlin, Bruce and The E Street Band put on a blistering, unforgettable show,” he says in his own statement. “But the story of how this unlikely concert came together is gripping in its own right. The concert — and this film — show how music, dancing, and freedom go hand in hand. “In East Berlin, Bruce and The E Street Band put on a blistering, unforgettable show,” When The Walls Came Down producer James Marsh says in a statement. “But the story of how this unlikely concert came together is gripping in its own right.
