What GQ Editors Impulse-Bought in October

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Every day, your faithful, over-caffeinated GQ Recommends editors cover everything from wardrobe essentials to step by step guides on how to find your office fragrance so that you know exactly what to purchase when you're shopping for your everyday items and big-ticket splurges. But, in-between bouts of menswear proselytizing and shilling for Big Gloop we do a lot of shopping for ourselves. And—while we like to think of ourselves as thoughtful consumers—even we smash that add to cart button on a whim a few times a month. That's why we put together this non-exhaustive list of what GQ Recommends editors impulse-bought this month. Here's everything we tossed in our virtual bags against our better judgement.

As a wise man once said, it's turtles all the way down. —Louis Cheslaw

The wired headphones community, which I consider myself a member of, finally has a good pair of noise-cancelling earbuds on deck. —Jordan Bowman

I see your overdone PSL as your signal of autumnal times and raise you a mulled-wine deep burgundy of a shoulder bag. —Mari Herrema

I had to retire my dusty old dog walking shoes and I was immediately drawn to these classic mocs from the folks at L.L.Bean. —Jordan Bowman

The second-best men's fashion magazine on the planet (forgive my bias) dropped its seasonal guide to fall-winter style, and it's packed with cozy-weather inspiration. —Avidan Grossman

Of the two best-loved 1947 special editions, the heads seem to slightly prefer Sugar Cane's slightly-darker Okinawa model. But I couldn't resist that turtle on the patch. —Louis Cheslaw

Arts and crafts hour, boys! —Michael Nolledo

You better believe these butter yellow Quince socks are going to be all over my feet this winter. —Jordan Bowman

The weather's cooling down which is the excuse I gave myself to buy this Portal windbreaker. —Gerald Ortiz

Tarvas' turbo hiking boots are exclusive to our pals at Haven for the moment, but our source in Finland told me to expect a wider release come November. —Avidan Grossman

The eau de parfume was not enough … everything must smell like Eau Splendide. —Mari Herrema

On a recent trip to Los Angeles, I made a pilgrimage to the temple of tees, the Lady White Co. flagship store and walked away with, among other impulse purchases, my new favorite T-shirt. —Gerald Ortiz

PSA for culinary heathens: you must press your tofu to maximize flavor. This lil gadget makes it soooo much easier. —Mari Herrema

I had no intention of buying another overcoat this season. Then I saw this shade of green and that was that. —Michael Nolledo
