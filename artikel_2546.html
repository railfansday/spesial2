Week 11 NFL Power Rankings: Top 5 remains steady

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

We're now officially past the midway mark of the NFL season for several teams, and with another week of action in the books, it's a bit clearer who the top teams in the league are – with most of our top 10 teams in our previous power rankings picking up wins.

But that also means there wasn't much movement at the top of the rankings; we avoided chaos. All four top teams from last week's rankings remained, though a new team entered the top five. Let's take a look at this week's NFL Power Rankings.

The Bills hold onto their top spot this week with a dominant win over the New York Jets in AFC East play. They are now on a five-game winning streak and hold a four-game lead in their division.

The 49ers showed why they might have one of the league's most explosive offenses, securing a 46-40 shootout win over the Dallas Cowboys. They now lead the NFC North by three games and will stay in the No. 2 spot this week.

The Broncos were off this week, so there is no real data point for them. But with nine of the top ten teams in these rankings either winning or off in Week 10, and the Broncos still holding a narrow lead in the AFC West, they remain No. 3.

The Bears also didn't play this week, like the Broncos. Unlike Denver, however, their bye week dropped them from first place to third place in the competitive NFC North. But we still feel the Bears are the cream of the crop, and they maintained their spot this week.

The Chiefs earned a dominant win this week, beating the Falcons by three scores. It didn't feel right to move them above the Broncos, but they remain a top-five team in this week's rankings.
