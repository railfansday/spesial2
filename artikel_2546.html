Takeaways as Iowa women's basketball downs Utah

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Iowa women's basketball team added to its impressive early-season resume on Monday afternoon.

The Hawkeyes took down the Utah Utes 75-66 in the Baha Mar Pink Flamingo Championship, held in the Bahamas. It was a back and forth game, with both teams making big second-half runs. But it was Jan Jensen's team that used a late 8-2 run to close out the Utes.

There was a lot to like from this game as Iowa improved to 6-1 on the young season. Here are the biggest takeaways from Iowa's 75-66 win over Utah.

Carnegie and Heiden did it all do the Hawkeyes on Monday. The duo combined for 53 of Iowa's 75 points, shooting a combined 22-33 from the field. Carnegie led the way with a team-high 27 points. She got to her spots with ease and efficiently poured in buckets. She also added a pair of steals to her ledger as well. The Georgia transfer has been excellent this season.

Heiden had 26 points, nine rebounds, and four assists in the win. She went 12-of-16 from the field, which is great sign that Iowa's offense is at its best. The Sherwood, Oregon, native is one of the best post players in the entire country. Pairing her with a guard that can score at will, like Carnegie, makes Iowa a very dangerous team.

Both teams pride themselves on rebounding. They each had averaged over 41 boards a game entering this contest. On Monday, it was Iowa which won the battle on the glass. The Hawkeyes outrebounded Utah 32-26, which included nine offensive rebounds. Those allowed for second-chance opportunities that Iowa cashed in on.

While Heiden did a lot of work on the glass, first-year forward McKenna Woliczko deserves some credit too. She grabbed six boards in the win, to go along with eight points and four assists. The five-star from San Bruno, California, has done a little bit of everything this season to help the Hawkeyes win games.

Iowa has played a tough non-conference slate and is being rewarded with a good resume. The Hawkeyes already have wins over a top-ten Vanderbilt squad and an underrated Utah team. A 12-point loss on the road to UCONN is nothing to scoff at either. And they're going to have a chance to add another marquee win before conference play starts.

The annual Cy-Hawk game is just a few weeks away. The Hawkeyes will be out for revenge after dropping last year's game in Ames. Iowa State has raced out to a 7-0 start, albeit against a weak schedule. They'll be tested against USC on Sunday. Jensen's team has a nice opportunity to build upon an already strong resume before Big Ten play begins.
