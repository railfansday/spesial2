Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Re: Employer-Sponsored Whole Life with LTC Benefits — Anyone Have Experience?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

I’ve generally avoided whole life insurance and don’t see much value in it as an investment vehicle, particularly given our existing term coverage.
You’ve been wise to avoid whole life in the past. Kudos to you.

In my view, there’s no reason to change your stance on whole life because of this particular offering. Permanent insurance offered through employers is generally of low value, relative to other options for life insurance. And, in all candor, I think you’re a little young to be thinking too hard about LTC coverage.

I’d suggest that you restart thinking about how you’d pay for long term care maybe 10-15 years from now.

And sock aside the money you were considering spending for this whole life policy into maxing out your workplace savings, or your taxable account if your workplace is maxed out.

I’m glad that you posted here before you threw away your money on low-value employer-sponsored permanent life insurance
