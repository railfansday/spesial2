Bitcoin fell to $65,420.59, down 4.8% in 24 hours and 13.6% over the past week, according to CoinGecko data. Ether and XRP fell further. The levels traders had marked for a rebound gave way one after another.

Ether dropped 5.7% to $1,826 and is down 18.2% for the week. XRP fell 6.1% to $0.9573, putting the token back below $1. Solana took the biggest hit among the top coins, sliding 8.6% to $72.66.

Bitcoin Slices Through the $67,000 Target Zone

Gareth Soloway, chief market strategist at Verified Investing, had warned that a break below $75,500 would open a path to $66,000 to $67,000. Bitcoin first stalled near $67,500. It has since broken through the whole zone and now trades about $1,500 below its lower end.

Soloway said he started buying small amounts of Bitcoin near $67,000, along with some Solana. He argued that a recent higher high on the chart pointed to a short-term bottom. On that view, the drop would be a pullback within a larger recovery rather than a new downtrend.

That view has a clear line in the sand. If Bitcoin sets a lower low below its previous cycle low, the higher-low structure fails, and that would signal the cycle bottom has not formed. With the price now below the target zone, the market is getting closer to testing that line.

Ether has slipped below the trend line it broke above earlier. It now trades under the area where buyers had been expected to step in.

Soloway said he went long Ether at an average price of $1,952, roughly a quarter of a full position, and planned to add near $1,850. At $1,826, the price is now below both levels. Ether’s market value has fallen to about $223 billion.

XRP first slipped below $1 earlier in the week, then bounced to $1.02. That rebound has now faded. At $0.9573, the token sits below its August low of about 98.5 cents and is down 16.9% for the week.

On the four-hour chart, the relative strength index (RSI) had been rising from its lows even as the price fell. That gap can point to fading selling pressure, but it has not yet produced a lasting bounce.
