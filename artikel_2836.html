Aaron Rodgers waived by Steelers after benching, comments on 'Pat McAfee Show'

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Aaron Rodgers' time with the Pittsburgh Steelers is about to officially come to an end following his benching and comments on Wednesday's episode of "The Pat McAfee Show."

According to NFL insider Jordan Schultz and Pittsburgh Post-Gazette's Gerry Dulac, the team will release the soon-to-be 43-year-old quarterback once his contract situation is finalized. Rodgers signed a one-year, $22.5 million deal in May.

The situation went from the Steelers announcing late Tuesday evening that Rodgers would be benched to him airing his feelings about head coach Mike McCarthy less than 24 hours later.

On "McAfee," Rodgers claimed that McCarthy fell asleep and snored during a quarterbacks meeting after arriving late. He also said the Steelers head coach broke down the team during halftime of Sunday's loss to the Philadelphia Eagles by saying, "Alright, 1-2-3-Packers!"

The four-time NFL MVP added that he found out he was being benched in favor of Mason Rudolph from Steelers tight end Pat Freiermuth.

"I was talking to Muth and a couple of the guys, and he said, 'I can't believe they're doing this.' And I said, 'Doing what?' I didn't know," Rodgers said. "And he told me, and obviously, there had been a lot of things in motion at that point. I didn't hear from Mike [McCarthy]."

"Mike and I talked for a long time the last few days, and that word [benching] never came up."

What comes next for Rodgers? That's to be determined. He said on "McAfee" that the "ship has sailed" in regards to a return to the Steelers. Now, he will be free agent once he clears waivers.

Rodgers announced in May that the 2026 season would be his last in the NFL.

In 10 games this season, Rodgers has thrown for 2,241 yards, 16 touchdowns and 6 interceptions.
