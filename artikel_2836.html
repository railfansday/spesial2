2026 Week 10 Preview: Tulsa Golden Hurricane @ Tulane Green Wave

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Time and date: Saturday, November 7 at 12:00 p.m. ET

Location: Yulman Stadium — New Orleans, LA

Last meeting: Tulane 31, Tulsa 14 — September 27, 2025

Saturday afternoon's result could be the difference maker between bowl eligibility and spending December at home for two American Conference programs. Tulsa and Tulane collide in New Orleans sporting identical 4-4 records, looking for some November momentum.

Tulsa is progressing toward its first bowl berth since 2021, while Tulane is looking for a fifth-straight appearance. When the dust settles in the Big Easy on Saturday, one will be on the brink of the postseason, while the other will marinate in sub-.500 territory.

Tulsa (4-4, 1-3 American) was one of the stories of September, as the Golden Hurricane constructed a 3-0 record, fueled by a signature upset win over Oklahoma State. However, Tulsa has dropped four of its last five since and many of those losses transpired in heartbreaking fashion.

The Golden Hurricane lost to North Texas in overtime, squandered a 12-point third quarter lead to Navy, and fell to Army on a buzzer-beating field goal, making them 2-10 in conference games in the past two seasons. Even in the face of defeat, Tulsa has shown promise on offense, averaging 35.8 points per game in American play after averaging just 22.4 points per game in conference matchups in 2025.

Quarterback Baylor Hayes has progressed in his second year as the starter. He is one of five American quarterbacks averaging over 220 passing yards per game, and he has drastically improved his ball security, delivering 10 touchdowns against two interceptions. Another reason for Hayes' upward development is the presence of Oran Singleton Jr. The 5'8", 165-pound receiver has been pivotal to the offense's success, leading the unit with 43 receptions, 535 receiving yards, and four touchdowns.

The Golden Hurricane attack the ground through the thunder and lightning tandem of Trequan Jones and Damari Alston. Jones is the bigger, short-yardage specialist, while the speedy Jones can explode for significant gains. They combine for 10 rushing touchdowns in a run game that ranks 67th nationally in production.

Tulsa's offense has struggled mightily on third downs, ranking 119th nationally with a 33.3 conversion percentage. But the team has made up for those struggles with the fifth-most efficient fourth down offense in the country. Tre Lamb has rolled the dice often, converting 18-of-23 attempts this season. Only Air Force, Florida Atlantic, and Virignia have cashed in on more fourth downs.

While Tulsa's defense allowed just 9.0 points per game across its 3-0 start, the unit has become far less stingy since late September. The Golden Hurricane surrendered 33.6 points per game in their last five outings, allowing at least 34 points in four of those games.

Each of Tulsa's last three opponents were option-based teams. That skews the Golden Hurricane's run/pass defense numbers as they've faced the second-most rushing attempts in the country. Inside linebacker Chris Thompson Jr. has excelled in this facet, totaling 81 tackles and 3.5 tackles for loss, and he enters this Tulane game fresh off a season-high of 15 tackles.

On the back end, Tyson Williams has been the team's top playmaker. The cornerback leads the team in interceptions (2) and pass breakups (5), while ranking third with 47 tackles. Tulsa intercepts passes at a high rate, and it ranks third in the conference with seven picks despite facing three different option offenses.

Tulane (4-4, 2-2 American) qualified for the American Conference Championship Game in each of the past four seasons. That streak is in serious jeopardy as the Green Wave have multiple conference losses for the first time since 2021. Tulane narrowly avoided a third defeat last Friday night, overcoming a 10-point fourth quarter deficit at Charlotte to eke out a 38-31 overtime victory.

The Green Wave serve as the host for this matchup, having won 10 of its last 11 at Yulman Stadium, including an Oct. 16 upset over Memphis. However, Tulane lost its latest game in New Orleans, falling 42-19 to UTSA in its largest conference defeat since 2021.

Tulane trots out true freshman Trace Johnson at quarterback. Johnson offers a 2-1 record as a starter and is coming off the team's most prolific passing performance of the season, registering 284 yards on a 77.8 completion rate against Charlotte. So far, the Laguna Niguel, CA native has been more of a pocket passer, but the Charlotte game provided some glimpses of his mobility. For packages that require quarterback mobility, Tulane may insert Dagan Bruno, who has 26 rushing yards and a touchdown on six attempts this season.

The run game is the defining feature of Tulane's offense. Two different running backs — Jaylin Lucas and Maurice Turner — have attained 200 yards in a game this year. The Green Wave also regularly utilize Johnnie Daniels, who has led the team in rushing in four-straight games, producing 203 yards across his last two.

Through the air, Destyn Hill has served as the primary target, hauling in 32 receptions for 390 yards and two touchdowns. The aerial attack has not been effective in the red zone, as only seven teams have fewer passing touchdowns than Tulane's six. Speaking of red zone, Tulane has scored on an efficient 18-of-20 opportunities this year. However, the Green Wave need to turn three into seven more often, managing 11 red zone touchdowns compared to seven field goals.

The kicking game has been one of the most consistent features in Will Hall's first year at the helm. Freshman Cooper Helmke is 10-of-12 on field goals this year, including 3-of-3 from 50+ with a long of 58.

Tulane ranks 125th in total defense, surrendering 421 yards per game. Defending the run has been the Green Wave's kryptonite. They are 132nd nationally against the run, allowing a 100-yard rusher in six of eight games. Inside linebacker Makai Williams and defensive tackle Ed Smith IV have been the best counters though, combining for 74 tackles and 12.5 tackles for loss.

Tulane generated six takeaways in its upset win over Memphis, but this has not been a recurring feature of the defense. The Green Wave have forced just four turnovers across their other seven matchups, sitting at a minus-3 in turnover margin this season.

This one has the makings of a close finish. Both teams have competed in four one-score games this season, regularly playing to the level of competition.

Tulane has won three of four home games this year, but Tulsa offers the more dynamic offense in this matchup. The Green Wave should be able to move the ball on the ground on the Golden Hurricane's 130th-ranked run defense, but they'll need a greater aerial presence to keep pace with Baylor Hayes and Oran Singleton Jr.

Tulsa emerges with a late touchdown to improve to 5-4 and snap a three-game losing streak against Tulane.
