Packers Injury Report: CB Brandon Cisse is a full participant on Monday

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Green Bay Packers have had three practices where their entire active roster has been healthy enough to at least be a limited participant in practice this week — a huge boost for the team coming out of their mini-bye. On Monday, we got two changes to the teams' injury report: Cornerback Brandon Cisse (rib) went from a limited participant on Sunday to a full participant on Monday, and offensive lineman Travis Glover (shoulder) was added to the injury report. Glover wasn't given a status on Sunday, so it appears that he suffered some sort of setback in practice today.

Beyond stating that the team was just giving running back Josh Jacobs some individual drill reps to keep him in the groove of the season, head coach Matt LaFleur has been pretty non-committal about who will or won't play against the Los Angeles Rams on Wednesday. With that being said, players who participate in all three practices in a week usually are able to play on game days. Officially, we'll get the players' game statuses (questionable, doubtful, in or out) on Tuesday, the Packers' travel day this week.
