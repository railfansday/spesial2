Latest injury update on Bears CB Kyler Gordon vs. Saints

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Chicago Bears are gearing up for a Week 11 game against the New Orleans Saints, where they're looking to improve to 8-2 coming off their bye week. But there's a question about cornerback Kyler Gordon's availability, especially as he's dealt with injury struggles in his career.

Gordon missed the first five games of the season due to a lingering calf injury that held him out for the entire summer. But, when he returned, he showed his importance on defense, particularly as a blitzed, with a three-sack, two-fumble performance. Unfortunately, Gordon suffered a shoulder injury back on Nov. 8 in a win over the Tampa Bay Buccaneers.

So how does that impact his availability? Here are the latest injury updates on Kyler Gordon ahead of Week 11's game against the Saints:

Kyler Gordon suffered a shoulder injury during a Week 9 win over the Buccaneers, and there was optimism the bye week would provide an extra week of rest and wouldn't require him to miss time. Unfortunately, Gordon did not practice all week ahead of Sunday's game against the Saints, and he's officially been ruled out. This will be the sixth game Gordon has missed this season.

Bears head coach Ben Johnson was asked about Kyler Gordon's availability for Sunday's game, and what followed was an interesting exchange putting Gordon's immediate future in question.

-Will Kyler Gordon play on Sunday vs. the Saints?

-Was the decision made ahead of a short week against the Lions on Thanksgiving?

*Johnson said: "I haven't even thought about it."

Kyler Gordon will miss Sunday's game against the Saints, but his status is in question for the remainder of the season. Gordon will likely miss next Thursday's Thanksgiving game against the Detroit Lions, but could he miss extended time? According to 104.3 The Score's Herb Howard, he expects Gordon to miss at least the next two games. He added that he doesn't "think he's going to be back on the football field soon."
