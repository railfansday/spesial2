Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Re: I Have a Spending Problem!

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

I plan to retire soon at age 50 with a comfy nest egg and an estimated conservative 2.0% withdrawal rate. After a lifetime of earning/saving/investing and developing outdoor hobbies that don’t cost much, I realize that: (a) I need to start spending a lot more (or my nest egg will get bigger and then I’ll have regret that I didn’t retire sooner), and (b) that I am horrible about spending. As in, it’s hard for me. I doubt I’m the only Boglehead who has found themself in this position.

What to do to “break out of it” and become a better spender? Are there any strategies that helped others?
I reject your premise. Who ways you “need” to spend more or are “horrible” for not spending more? (Except you, lol.) Who the heck cares? The point of life is enjoying your time here. If beating yourself up at spending 2% and going crazy trying to find a way to spend 4% is the reality, then maybe you shouldn’t try. Maybe just focus on enjoying your time on earth and living the life you want to live, even if you do only find enough stuff to buy at 2%. Forcing yourself to do something, even if it is spending more money, is no way to live life.

If this really bothers you then donate half of it to charity to reduce your nest egg so that your current spend of 2% becomes 4%. Magic!
