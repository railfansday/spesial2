Osman stars as Brighton bounce back with Conference League win

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Brighton and Hove Albion vs CS Universitatea Craiova. UEFA Conference League Round 1.

American Express StadiumAttendance: Attendance26,796.

Match report as Brighton beat 10-man Universitatea Craiova 4-0 in the Conference League; Ibrahim Osman scored his first Brighton goals in the win; Maxim De Cuyper superbly curled home his fifth goal of the campaign; Osman also set up Charalampos Kostoulas for a third

Ibrahim Osman scored his first Brighton goals as his side returned to winning ways by cruising to a 4-0 success over 10-man Universitatea Craiova in the Conference League.

Talk of the high-flying Seagulls potentially being shock Premier League title contenders this season was dampened slightly by Sunday's surprise 2-0 defeat at Hull.

Winger Osman finished a flowing move to set Albion on course to avoid a further unexpected defeat before Maxim De Cuyper superbly curled home his fifth goal of the campaign to double the lead.

Romanian champions Craiova were left with little hope of a comeback at the Amex Stadium when defender Vladimir Screciu was sent off in the 39th minute for stamping on the ankle of Matt O'Riley.

Osman set up Charalampos Kostoulas for the third goal before quickly claiming his second with a fine individual effort as Brighton made it 10 points from four games in the competition on a bitterly cold evening in Sussex.

3: GOAL! The move started with Kadioglu down the right and he then picks out Kostoulas in the box, who in turn feeds Hinshelwood in space and his low driven ball to the back post is tapped home by the unmarked Osman.

33: GOAL! Brighton double their lead as the visitors play a risky crossfield pass that is intercepted by De Cuyper, who then beats Popescu with a delightful right-footed curler from distance that leaves the 'keeper rooted to his line as the ball goes in off the post.

39: RED! The referee did not need long to look at that, with Screciu shown a straight red for that dangerous-looking tackle on O'Riley.

46: SAVE! Azeez, with his first touch since coming on, whips a lovely ball in from the right and Hinshelwood must have thought he had scored with a bullet header - but Popescu responds with a brilliant save.

61: GOAL! Osman feeds Kostoulas in the area and from a tight angle, the Greek's low drive beats Popescu at his near post.

78: CLOSE! Azeez twists and turns on the right edge of the box, but his curler is inches away from finding the top corner with the 'keeper motionless.

"He [Osman] showed a really good performance today," said Brighton head coach Fabian Hurzeler.

"We know that his biggest quality is one against one, especially against teams who are sitting in a low block.

"This is very helpful to have these one-against-one players. He showed a very impressive style today."

Asked about the decision not to loan out Osman again, Hurzeler said: "It's not the Conference League (campaign) that was the key.

"The key was his performance and his effort and his development he showed through the loans. But also his effort and work ethic and his quality he showed in pre-season.

"That was the key for us to make this decision. Therefore he is a big part of our team. We need a good depth in the squad and we are very happy that he is here now."
