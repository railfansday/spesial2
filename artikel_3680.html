Country Singers Ella Langley and Tucker Wetmore's Relationship Timeline

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Ella Langley and Tucker Wetmore have spent most of their rumored romance dodging direct questions so far, letting side-by-side vacations, tour bus cameos and flirty Instagram comments do the talking instead.

Us confirmed in September 2026 that the Nashville-based country singers had been “quietly dating for more than six months but less than a year,” though further details remain unknown.

Scroll below for Langley and Wetmore’s low-key relationship timeline:

Langley and Wetmore were first linked after being spotted vacationing together in the British Virgin Islands. The sighting kicked off dating rumors that neither artist immediately publicly addressed.

Dating speculation gained steam when a TikTok user posted footage of Langley boarding Wetmore’s tour bus after the two performed together at a charity event in Chattanooga, Tennessee. The clip surfaced just days after Wetmore appeared on Kristin Cavallari‘s “Let’s Be Honest” podcast and claimed he had “sworn off blondes.”

Later in the month, the pair traded flirty comments on Instagram. Langley wrote, “Cute fish” beneath a shirtless photo of Wetmore fishing in Australia, and he replied, “This is good” on a video of her performing an acoustic version of “Loving Life Again.”

A source confirmed to Us that Langley and Wetmore had been “quietly dating for more than six months but less than a year,” explaining that the two met organically in Nashville.

“They run in the same circles, so they met naturally through mutual connections,” the insider said. “It started casually, so they kept it on the DL at first, but they’re more serious now. They’ve met some of each other’s family and friends. It’s going really well.”

The source added that Langley “has been grateful for Tucker’s support and guidance” as she navigated a career-defining year.

Later that month, the couple were photographed leaving the 2026 Nashville Songwriter Awards together, hours after Langley’s “Choosin’ Texas” logged its historic 23rd week at the top of the Hot 100.

Before the month came to an end, Langley brushed off a viral date request from influencer @TikiBeen1, who asked, “Ella, how many likes to take you on a date?”

“Not enough in the world,” she shot back, without mentioning that she was off the market.

Wetmore has stayed similarly tight-lipped. When a concertgoer asked about the relationship during a Q&A session, he responded, “Next question.”

Langley and Wetmore walked the red carpet separately at the 60th annual CMA Awards at Bridgestone Arena in Nashville. Langley wore a beaded, plunging Elie Saab dress, while Wetmore opted for a striped, double-breasted Amiri suit paired with a black cowboy hat.

Langley arrived as the night’s most-nominated artist with nine nods, while Wetmore was up for New Artist of the Year. While neither star publicly confirmed their relationship on the red carpet, audiences got a glimpse of Langley rocking out in the crowd to her man’s live performance.
