Dan Campbell: Tay Martin's special teams tape was so good, I was shocked we got him

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Lions wide receiver Tay Martin has been in the NFL since 2022, and he has only four career catches. So he’s not exactly a household name. But Lions coach Dan Campbell has always been well aware of Martin, and was delighted when he arrived in Detroit this year.

That’s because Martin isn’t only a wide receiver, but also a special teams player. And according to Campbell, he’s one of the best special teams players in football, so good a player that Campbell was surprised to learn Martin was available for the Lions to sign in June.

“Tay’s just a heck of a football player,” Campbell said. “I still can’t believe that we got him. I’m just shocked, I couldn’t believe, seriously I couldn’t believe it. His tape last year, his special teams tape. We’re fortunate to have him. He’s a football player.”

The 28-year-old Martin spent three years in college at Washington State, then two more at Oklahoma State, before signing with the 49ers as an undrafted free agent. He has also spent time with the Titans and Commanders, and he was the leading receiver for the UFL’s Columbus Aviators in the spring.

Campbell said Martin is perfectly capable of playing wide receiver in the NFL, and while the Lions haven’t used him there much, knowing that he can catch passes when he needs to while also tackling as well as a defensive player in special teams coverage is a huge boost to the Lions.

“He’s grown as a receiver,” Campbell said. “But the special teams production is outstanding. What he’s able to bring to us from the receiver position — it’s one thing when you get a DB, a safety, a corner, this guy serves that role on special teams, so it kind of buys you a spot, in a good way. He’s a stud. Thank goodness we got him.”
