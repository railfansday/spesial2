Browns becoming Bengals’ biggest threat to win AFC North

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The AFC North has been a bit of a whirlwind this season. Every team started off the year fairly hot. It took until Week 7 for any of them to lose three games, and that was aided a bit by Lamar Jackson hurting his ankle.

Well, poop has hit the fan for two of the more historically recognized teams by the national media. The Baltimore Ravens have collapsed several times even when Jackson was healthy, but it looks like he will miss a majority, but more realistically the whole, season.

The Pittsburgh Steelers are two weeks removed from coming off a win against the Cleveland Browns to take control of the division at 6-2 to facing a Cincinnati Bengals team fresh off a trip to Madrid to keep that top spot. They got throttled by the Bengals, and then the struggling Philadelphia Eagles helped spark the team to bench quarterback Aaron Rodgers. That led to an absolute airing out of complaints on national television, and they seem to be free-falling.

That leaves one other 6-4 team that the Bengals play twice still this season, the Cleveland Browns. The Browns now are clearly the biggest threat to the top of the AFC North. The Browns sit only one game back. Their first meeting comes in Week 13, and if the Bengals can't create some cushion, there is a Week 18 matchup that could end up determining who wins the division and who ends up as a Wild Card team.

One of the big reasons why the Bengals in the Zac Taylor and Joe Burrow era have felt different is simple. They have one of the best kickers in the league with Evan McPherson. Before the defense allowed Jayden McDaniels to go the entire length of the field in two plays, he hit one of the more difficult kicks during his career to potentially win the game.

Stewart and Murphy make the Bengals' biggest offseason decision easier

It is clear that the Bengals will need to improve at the EDGE position. Myles Murphy has shown promise, and maybe he would return on a prove-it type deal. Shemar Stewart, however, hasn't even been able to touch the field. Part of that is due to injury, but part of that is the coaches simply not rotating him in there. Even when given the choice of putting him out there more after Cashius Howell's injury, they opted to try and trade for a player before backing out. They need a long-term solution at the position.

Why Joe Burrow staying late after Bengals practice matters

There is no doubt Burrow shoulders some of the blame for the Monday Night Football loss. This also just supports that idea that he is looking to gain any possible edge he can on a short week. He knows he will be right back in a pressure storm against the New Orleans Saints Sunday.

Aaron Rodgers clears waivers, now a free agent after stunning release from Steelers

It will be interesting to see if Rodgers is going to end up anywhere. Maybe a team like the Patriots who are still looking for a possible replacement for Drake Maye, but you have to find it hard to believe a lot of teams are going to go out of their way to bring in a guy who just went scorched Earth after being benched.

'Rock star' Joe Milton III leads Eagles to stunning rally over Cowboys, his former team

Milton truly put on a stunning display to erase a 21-point Dallas Cowboys lead. He has now won games over the Pittsburgh Steelers and Cowboys. The Eagles at 4-7 are not quite as dead as they should be in the NFC East. Milton also could become an interesting quarterback option for a team come this offseason.

Looking at this from the perspective of the MVP race, which got a bit more heated after the Bengals lost on Monday Night Football, Josh Allen and Patrick Mahomes did themselves no favors. Allen was more poised to take a lead, but instead he ended the game with two interceptions in the red zone (you could say wasn't his fault), but more importantly, with two chances to get in range for a field goal to take the lead he took sacks on third down. It made Burrows third down sack last week not look nearly as bad.
