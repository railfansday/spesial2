Do the Patriots play today? Schedule, time, channel for next New England NFL game

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Patriots will enjoy a needed bye week after losing to the Lions on November 15 in Germany.

Drake Maye was injured in the game, and he may miss the rest of the season. New England, with a .500 record, now attempts to make the playoffs with a few weeks left in the season.

Mike Vrabel's squad will have an extra week to regroup before returning to action against the Chargers, a team that won its first game of the season in Week 10.

Here is everything you need to know about the Patriots' next game, including the date, kickoff time and TV information.

The Patriots will face the Chargers at 4:05 p.m. ET on Sunday, Nov. 29. The game will take place at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California.

Patriots vs. Chargers will air on CBS. The game will also be available to stream on Fubo, DIRECTV and Paramount+.

Bye weeks: Falcons, Browns, Packers, Rams, Patriots, Seahawks
