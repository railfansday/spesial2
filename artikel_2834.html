Report: Kendrick Bourne has a fractured tibia, will miss the rest of the season

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Cardinals head coach Mike LaFleur said at his Monday press conference that wide receiver Kendrick Bourne could miss the rest of the season with a leg injury he suffered in Sunday’s overtime win over the Cowboys and a report on Monday evening says that will be the case.

Adam Schefter of ESPN reports that Bourne suffered a fractured tibia. He does not need surgery, but does need three months to recover and will not be able to return to action this season as a result.

Bourne had one catch on Sunday and 26 catches for 263 yards while appearing in all eight Cardinals games this fall.

Michael Wilson, Simi Fehoko, Devin Duvernay, and Reggie Virgil are the other wide receivers on the active roster in Arizona.
