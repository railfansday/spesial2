The Bachelor’s Lauren Burnham Reveals Where She Stands With Alex Cooper

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Bachelor’s Lauren Burnham admits she still can’t forget some of Alex Cooper’s previously controversial podcast segments.

“It was, like, seven years ago. It was something that was really difficult for me to see because that was a recurring series on the ‘Call Her Daddy’ podcast where they would be talking about baby Alessi,” Burnham, 35, recalled on the Tuesday, November 24, episode of iHeartRadio’s “Bachelor Happy Hour” podcast. “They were mainly making fun of [my husband] Arie [Luyendyk] and I, which is totally fine. But I feel like they took it a bit too far.”

According to Burnham, Cooper, 32, once featured a recurring segment on her podcast to make fun of her then-unborn daughter Alessi, now 7. Some of the clips resurfaced online when Cooper found herself in a public feud with Alix Earle.

“If anyone’s interested, they can go find it,” Burnham told podcast hosts Charity Lawson and Rachel Recchia of the previous segments. “But I just didn’t really like her name being included in conversations about adult topics as well. And that’s what made me really uncomfortable.”

Us has reached out to Cooper’s rep for comment regarding Burnham’s claims.

In their latest podcast interview, both Luyendyk, 45, and Burnham confirmed they have yet to receive apologies from Cooper.

“I mean, just the irony of her becoming a mom too. That timing of that, you know,” Luyendyk pointed out. “I’m just like, well, would you want somebody saying that about your child when she’s first born?”

Burnham added, “I think maybe her ‘mom journey’ will teach her another perspective on the whole thing.”

In October, Cooper confirmed she had welcomed her first baby with husband Matt Kaplan after sharing the first glimpse of her son via Instagram. She captioned the post with a simple blue heart emoji.

Speaking of babies, both Luyendyk and Burnham are confident in saying their family is complete after welcoming four children.

Although Burnham said their youngest, baby Livvy, is “the best,” both parents believe the door is closed when it comes to expanding the family. (Luyendyk’s vasectomy also clearly makes things a bit more final.)

“We’re done having kids, I guess. That’s final,” she proclaimed. “Livvy’s like the best baby in the world, so it’s hard to be like, ‘OK, this is the last time.’ But it’s for sure.”
