How to Style Flannel Shirts Like the Cool Girls

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Muted plaids, sleek layers, and smart contrasts make flannel feel instantly elevated.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Once associated solely with the lumberjack aesthetic, flannel shirts have become a wardrobe staple. Flannel is, at its core, a remarkably versatile fabric—soft, structured, and warm without bulk, with a texture that looks expensive when styled with intention. It’s timeless, perfect for in-between weather, and, you have to admit, effortlessly cool.

The trick to elevating it lies in contrast. Pair a brushed-cotton shirt with sharp tailoring and it instantly looks considered. These days, flannel is being worn with everything from denim to satin skirts, and each pairing adds up to an elevated outfit formula.

Beyond the pairings, the details do the heavy lifting. Choose muted, earthy plaids over loud ones, and let the shirt’s softness play against something sleek, like pleated trousers or an oversized fit thrown over a slip dress. Layer it under a cozy coat or knot it at the waist, and flannel stops feeling like a seasonal afterthought. It becomes the piece the whole outfit is built around.

Ahead, we’ve rounded up elevated flannel outfit ideas to inspire you, built around fall’s most essential piece.

The unexpected pairing of black cargo shorts, a blue check flannel, and black loafers works surprisingly well. It reads as casual with a side of cool-girl attitude.

Pairing a practical flannel shirt with a romantic pink satin skirt and black flip-flops brings a fresh approach to fall styling. Trade the flip-flops for ankle boots and add a cropped jacket when temperatures drop.

We love how this outfit pairs an everyday plaid top with wrap-skirt trousers and statement yellow sneakers. The pop of color keeps the look playful, while the easy plaid grounds it in classic fall style.

Styled with wide-leg jeans, this look reads casual, but it’s the luxe accessories that instantly elevate it. A quilted Chanel bag, sleek loafers, and oversized sunglasses add polish without losing the easy feel of the denim.

Tied around the waist, this flannel shirt pairs seamlessly with a checked skirt, even with their different prints. Worn with a chunky knit and shearling suede jacket, every layer and detail feels intentional.
