Nvidia’s RTX Spark mini PCs go on sale for local AI, from $4,399

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Windows desktops from ASUS, Dell and MSI are built to keep AI agents running around the clock.

RTX Spark mini PCs from ASUS, Dell and MSI are now on sale, from $4,399.

Compact desktops using Nvidia’s Arm-based RTX Spark chip are now on sale from ASUS, Dell and MSI, with Acer, GIGABYTE, HP and Microsoft to follow. They run AI models and agents locally with up to 128GB of memory, and early prices start at $4,399, well above RTX Spark laptops.

Small desktop PCs built on Nvidia’s RTX Spark chip are now on sale from ASUS, Dell and MSI, Nvidia announced on Wednesday. Acer, GIGABYTE and HP will follow, along with Microsoft’s Surface RTX Spark Dev Box.

The Windows machines pair a 20-core Nvidia Grace processor with a Blackwell graphics chip and up to 128GB of shared memory. Nvidia says that is enough to run AI models with 120 billion parameters on the device, with no cloud bills for those tasks. It pitches the desktops as always-on boxes for AI agents, the role that drove record Mac mini demand this year.

They are not cheap. MSI’s EdgeMesa N AI+ costs $4,399 with 64GB of memory, and $7,499 with 128GB, Notebookcheck reported. The ASUS ProArt GR1X starts at $4,499.

That is about $700 more than RTX Spark laptops, Windows Central said in its ASUS review. It found graphics performance up to 22% higher than the laptop chip, thanks to more power and better cooling, but only about 5% more processor speed.

Nvidia also lined up software. Adobe’s Illustrator and InDesign now run natively on RTX Spark’s Arm chip, and Blender 5.3 adds Nvidia’s DLSS ray-tracing tech to its preview window.

RTX Spark is Nvidia’s push into Windows PCs, unveiled at Computex in June. The first RTX Spark laptops went on sale in October from $2,599, including Microsoft’s Surface Laptop Ultra.
