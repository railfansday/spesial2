Diane Keaton’s ‘Memory Is Honored in Everything‘ in ‘Family Stone’ Sequel, Says Star Dermot Mulroney

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Stone family is reuniting more than two decades later to honor Diane Keaton in the sequel to “The Family Stone.”

Dermot Mulroney, who starred as Everett Stone in the 2005 holiday film, returns alongside original stars Sarah Jessica Parker and Rachel McAdams. Despite their increasingly busy schedules, Mulroney tells Variety that the actors agreed to step back into their characters’ shoes to honor the memory of Keaton, who starred as the family matriarch Sybil. “Diane’s memory is honored in everything,” Mulroney said at the premiere of “The Hunting Wives” Season 2. “I would suggest that it has a different quality than just your garden variety sequel. Maybe it’s just that it’s a family returning together that gives it resonance. But there’s something magical about this movie.”

Titled “The Families Stones,” the sequel follows the Stone family 20 years after their mother’s passing. As they reunite for Christmas, they’re met with an unexpected surprise when their father brings home the woman he’s since fallen in love with.

Photos of Mulroney on set with Parker and McAdams went viral in October and generated buzz among fans of the original film, which Mulroney said “amazed” him and his co-stars.

“It really affirmed for us how beloved that film is, as if we needed to be told again,” he said. “It ripened on the vine and grew over these 21 years, and we are, right now, in the process of a very magical experience. We’re riding high, just combining all of these loved ones playing loved ones. It’s remarkable.”

Jean Smart joins the sequel alongside returning cast Claire Danes as Julie Morton, Craig T. Nelson as Kelly Stone, Luke Wilson as Ben Stone, Tyrone Giordano as Thad Stone, Elizabeth Reaser as Susannah Stone, Paul Schneider as Brad Stevenson and Brian White as Patrick Thomas.

Mulroney was promoting the sophomore season of “The Hunting Wives,” where he stars as politician Jed Banks. Although the show’s first season was based on May Cobb’s novel of the same name, Season 2 had no source material and is the result of the imaginations of creator, writer and showrunner Rebecca Perry Cutter and Cobb.

“They took Season 1 and went further somehow,” Mulroney said. “We jumped off from that book that May Cobb wrote and made up a bunch of shit you won’t believe, to be quite honest.”

Recalling reading the Season 2 script for the first time, Mulroney said, “Everything I think might happen doesn’t and whatever they come up with is 10 times better than anything I thought.”
