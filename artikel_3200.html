Pan American Silver Corp. (PAAS -2.99%) reported third quarter results characterized by significant attributable free cash flow and enhanced shareholder returns, despite downward revisions to annual gold production targets. Management reported that the silver segment continues to perform better than expected on a cost basis, while the gold segment faces operational headwinds at Jacobina, El Penon, and Shahuindo. The company stated it is advancing long-term growth projects at La Colorada Skarn and the Timmins camp while maintaining a strong liquidity position of $3.4 billion. Capital allocation for the quarter focused on returning $300 million to shareholders through a combination of increased dividends and substantial share repurchases under its 2026 framework.
