Alex Eala reacts to potentially breaking Roger Federer and Rafael Nadal’s exhibition attendance record

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

In 2020, Rafael Nadal and Roger Federer took part in the Match for Africa, an exhibition match held in Cape Town, South Africa.

It was played in front of 52,000 people and, to this day, it remains the biggest official crowd in the history of the sport.

However, next month, that record could finally be broken by none other than Alexandra Eala.

In just a couple of weeks' time, this 21-year-old superstar will face Janice Tjen in her home country of the Philippines. It may only be an exhibition match, but the fact that they selected a venue holding more than 55,000 fans to host this showdown highlights the scale of their expectations.

By now, the world is fully aware of Eala's popularity.

And now, speaking at her latest homecoming, she has discussed the prospect of potentially breaking the attendance record previously set by Roger Federer and Rafael Nadal.

Although it remains uncertain and unconfirmed whether she will break the record, it definitely feels likely.

After all, she has sold out stadiums all around the world over the past year, with her legions of Filipino fans more than happy to follow her from tournament to tournament.

In theory, a match played in her home country should make it even easier to fill seats, with that 55,000 attendance target being more than manageable.

Discussing the prospect of reaching such a number at a press conference, she admitted: "It's incredibly mind-blowing, and one of the things why events like this specifically are so special.

"And one of the things that makes my whole journey, especially this year, so emotional is the moments when I look back that make now so emotional, because then you realise how far you've come, and every time I come back home, the experience changes, and I'm able to experience my home in a different light.

"So, to see the changes in the places of my life kind of makes it a very personal thing.

"To know that that's even a possibility, the whole December 8th event, you know, to fill up an arena here in the Philippines for tennis. That was, you know, that was unheard of when I was growing up here. So to see just the growth of my sport and the growth of how much people are interested in it and how much people have grown this passion for tennis. It's one of the things that makes it sentimental."

This should come as no surprise, though. As recently as September, Eala was backed to break this Federer and Nadal record. Now, as it draws closer, it finally seems tangible.

These two legends of our sport raised $3.5m from their Match for Africa, having initially hoped to top $1 million.
