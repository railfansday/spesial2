Nothing derails a gameweek plan quite like a injury to a key forward. Joao Pedro (£7.8m) is out for a few weeks, so it is time to find a new route into the front line. Here is how I would handle it.

The latest on Joao Pedro is that he missed the weekend game against Spurs, and Xabi Alonso did not offer much hope afterwards. “He has an injury on his thigh," explained the Chelsea manager, "and he is going to be a few weeks out. Let’s see how many. We don’t want to predict on that, but not ready for the next games.”

That is about as clear as it gets without a firm return date. Two weeks looks like the minimum, and nobody at Chelsea is willing to put a number on it.

For most managers, this is a sell. Two weeks out is the minimum, and when he does come back, Chelsea will be playing midweek Premier League games, which makes his minutes hard to trust.

With strong alternatives sitting at lower prices, there is little reason to wait. The one case for keeping him is if you only have a single free transfer and more urgent problems elsewhere in the squad, but that is rare. Here are my recommendations for his replacements...

He is my number one, and I am buying him myself this week. Only Haaland has registered more expected goals (xG) and big chances in the league this season, and at £5.6m he frees up money for other areas of the team while being easy to rotate as an eighth attacker. People say he cannot finish, yet he scored eight goals from roughly 8.5 expected goals last season, which is hardly poor. Penalties could come his way, though the price is the real selling point. Newcastle and Coventry are next, and he scored and assisted against Newcastle in the EFL Cup last night. With no European football to worry about, his minutes should stay steady.

If you already have Barry, Calvert-Lewin is the next best move. He takes penalties, rarely gets taken off early and has no doubts over his place. Bournemouth, Spurs, Coventry and Ipswich make up four of Leeds’ next six, and he is a possible captain option in Gameweek 12. No European games, again, is a big plus.
