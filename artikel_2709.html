'He will win games single-handedly': Ex-cricketer backs Vaibhav Sooryavanshi for 2027 ODI World Cup

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Former India captain and chief selector Kris Srikkanth has thrown his weight behind teenage batting sensation Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, backing him for opportunities across formats and urging the Indian team management to consider him for the 2027 ODI World Cup.Sooryavanshi has taken the cricketing world by storm with his extraordinary batting performances, showcasing remarkable power, timing and confidence at such a young age.Impressed by the youngster's fearless approach, Srikkanth believes Sooryavanshi has the potential to become an all-format cricketer and should not be restricted to a particular format. Speaking on his YouTube channel, the former India opener also suggested that the teenager should be considered for the upcoming ODI series against Sri Lanka.

Srikkanth believes Sooryavanshi deserves to be included in India's 15-member squad for the 2027 ODI World Cup, even if the team management is not yet convinced about his readiness for Test cricket. The former India selector stressed that the youngster should be given opportunities in the 50-over format to help him develop and gain experience at the international level."He is an all-format player. You don't want to play him in Tests, okay, but at least keep him in the 15. There is no questioning now. Sri Lanka will be coming for ODIs, give him a chance there. Put him in and let him play ODIs against them. If you really want to develop Suryavanshi, take him to the 2027 World Cup. Let him be in the 15. One day he will win you games single-handedly," Srikkanth said on his Youtube Channel.

Drawing comparisons with two of India's greatest batting icons, Sachin Tendulkar and Virat Kohli, Srikkanth went a step further by describing Sooryavanshi as the "son of the god of cricket"."The way he is batting, power alone is not enough. You need timing as well. It is not just about power, it is also about his confidence. He looked so still and calm. He does not look ruffled. The greatest thing is that he knows exactly what he is doing. This is genius. First came the god of cricket, Sachin Tendulkar, then the King Virat Kohli, and now he is the son of the god of cricket," he said."If you do not encourage this guy and keep saying that he has to be groomed, it is unfair. You are going to spoil a cricketer this way. Team management and selectors, please wake up," he added.
