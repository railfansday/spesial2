49ers’ Kyle Shanahan faces his 0-5 kryptonite in Mexico City

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

History is not on Kyle Shanahan's side when the 49ers take on the Vikings in Mexico City on Sunday.

Minnesota's defensive coordinator, Brian Flores, has never lost when facing Shanahan as a head coach or offensive assistant. Shanahan is 0-5 against Flores.

San Francisco 49ers head coach Kyle Shanahan looks on during the second quarter against the Seattle Seahawks at Lumen Field in Seattle, Oct. 11, 2026. Getty Images

The most memorable Shanahan-Flores matchup was in Super Bowl LI, where the 49ers head coach was Falcons offensive coordinator and Flores was linebackers coach for the Patriots. The Falcons famously blew a 28-3 lead with New England taking the game in overtime.

As things stand, Flores leading a defense with Shanahan leading an offense has never resulted in a win for the latter.

Brian Flores, defensive coordinator, of the Minnesota Vikings celebrates after the game against the Chicago Bears at U.S. Bank Stadium on Dec. 16, 2024, in Minneapolis, Minnesota. Getty Images

Shanahan said NFL offenses usually feel the most pressure on third down, when a defense can throw just about anything at them. Against Flores, that's what even first down feels like.

"They put so much pressure on the quarterback, the play caller, the whole line, the receivers, on everybody," Shanahan said. "When you play against Brian, it's 70 straight plays of that."

Flores has had Shanahan's number, but the 8-1 49ers have been upending expectations since their season opener in Australia. The Rams entered as Super Bowl favorites, with some even talking about an undefeated season.

The 49ers ended that talk with a Week 1 rout.

Brock Purdy (13) of the San Francisco 49ers looks to pass against the Dallas Cowboys on Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026, at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas. Getty Images

Quarterback Brock Purdy has also defied history with an MVP-worthy performance in 2026. As the last pick in the 2022 NFL Draft, Purdy was never supposed to be a star, let alone a franchise quarterback.

Injuries have plagued the 49ers this season, as they have in recent years, often derailing their Super Bowl hopes.

But Shanahan has still guided the offense to the top of the NFL in several statistical categories while keeping the team at the top of the standings.

Overall, the 49ers can afford to lose a game with an 8-1 record. But it would be hasty to immediately rule the squad out of a win in Mexico City.
