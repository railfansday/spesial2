Steph Curry ruled out for next three games

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Golden State Warriors have ruled Stephen Curry out for at least the next three games as he continues rehabilitating a torn oblique.

While the organization confirmed Friday that Curry is making encouraging progress and advancing to individual on-court work in San Francisco with lead medical decision-maker Rick Celebrini, his next official reevaluation is scheduled for Wednesday — exactly three weeks after he sustained the injury against Houston on Nov. 11.

The extended timeline keeps Curry sidelined for Friday's road matchup in Portland, Sunday's home clash against the LA Clippers, and Tuesday's tilt against the Utah Jazz. Golden State has gone 2-4 in his absence, stumbling through an 0-5 road trip before stabilizing with back-to-back home victories over Boston and Sacramento. With Curry's 27.8 points per game still off the floor, Brandin Podziemski, De'Anthony Melton, Brandon Williams, and LJ Cryer will continue carrying heavy backcourt responsibilities, while rookie Yaxel Lendeborg will continue to be at the top of the priority list on opponents' scouting reports.
