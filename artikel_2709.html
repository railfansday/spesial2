Yes, the "Sunday Night Football" matchup was horrible — Philadelphia Eagles versus Washington Commanders. But it could have been the 1972 Dolphins versus 2007 Patriots, and I wouldn't have watched. The Chiefs left too bad a taste in my mouth.

Hopefully the Chiefs have moved on, but I'm still working through my anger over that game.
