Week 11 NFL TV Map for Cleveland Browns fans, and playoff implications

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Cleveland Browns are on the bye this week, but take a look below at the games that you'll be able to watch if you're in Northeast Ohio.

WEEK 11 NFL GAMES AIRING NATIONALLY OR IN CLEVELAND

Thursday – 8:15 PM ET: Indianapolis Colts vs. Houston Texans (Prime)

Sunday – 9:30 AM ET: New England Patriots vs. Detroit Lions (FOX)

Sunday – 1:00 PM ET: Pittsburgh Steelers vs. Philadelphia Eagles (CBS)

Sunday – 1:00 PM ET: New Orleans Saints vs. Chicago Bears (FOX)

Sunday – 4:25 PM ET: Jacksonville Jaguars vs. New York Giants (CBS)

Sunday – 8:20 PM ET: Minnesota Vikings vs. San Francisco 49ers (NBC/Peacock)

Monday – 8:15 PM ET: Cincinnati Bengals vs. Washington Commanders (ESPN)

Doing a little extra thinking this week, what games are important for Browns fans to watch or root for with respect to playoff implications? Obviously, we want the Steelers and Bengals to lose, although both teams should be favorites in their respective games. Since they're both on the road, though, maybe we'll see an upset?

The more the Patriots can lose, the better. And even though the Browns now own a tiebreaker over the Texans, we still don't want them to get hot.

As for other games of interest that aren't being aired locally, the Raiders face the Broncos, and the Ravens take on the Panthers. We can't hope for everyone ahead or behind us to lose, but the more losses that pile up for them, the greater margin for error it buys Cleveland.
