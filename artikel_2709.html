Full injury report for Joel Embiid, Sixers vs. Heat in the NBA Cup

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Philadelphia 76ers will take on the Miami Heat in the NBA Cup on Wednesday as they look to earn a big win in Group B play with the hopes of being able to advance to the knockout rounds. The Heat are 2-0 in group play and a loss to Miami would just about do it for the Sixers with one final game to play in group play on Friday against the Indiana Pacers.

As far as the injury report is concerned, the Sixers have listed Joel Embiid (illness) as doubtful. If he can't give it a go, the Sixers will continue to rely on a smaller center rotation of Adem Bona, Jabari Walker, and Dominick Barlow to take on Miami's large frontline of Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bam Adebayo.

Ariel Hukporti (right Achilles tear) is listed as out for the contest.

Tip off from the Xfinity Mobile Arena is set for 5 p.m. EST on Thanksgiving Eve.
