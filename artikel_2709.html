Lonzo Ball says which NBA team has the ugliest fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Lonzo Ball has just damaged his chances of ever signing with one NBA team.

Lonzo Ball is in a pretty interesting spot in his career right now. Sitting on the open market as a free agent, the former number two overall pick is still looking for a new NBA team to give him a shot and let him prove he can stay healthy on the floor.

While he waits for the right deal to come along, Ball has been staying busy off the court by leaning into his podcast and media work. However, all that extra time behind a microphone has definitely kept him in the headlines, even if roasting Memphis fans wasn't on anyone's bingo card.

Lonzo Ball clearly isn't worried about making friends in Tennessee anytime soon. Speaking on NBAT2 around seven months ago, he bluntly named Memphis as the city with the ugliest fans in the league.

He said: "I don't want to say Memphis because I f*** with people from Memphis, but that was the first city that popped into my mind. If you're going to go somewhere, I wouldn't suggest you go to Memphis first."

Ball looked like he was finally putting everything together in Chicago, thriving as a two-way playmaker alongside Zach LaVine and DeMar DeRozan.

However, things fell apart in January 2022, though, when a torn meniscus turned into a brutal string of knee surgeries, including a rare cartilage transplant, that sidelined him for more than two full seasons.

Those constant setbacks wiped out his momentum and led to him struggling with the Cleveland Cavaliers before being waived.
