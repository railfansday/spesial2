Elizabeth Debicki Looks Angelic in All White at ELLE’s 2026 Women in Hollywood Event

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Elizabeth Debicki knows a thing or two about red carpet elegance. For ELLE’s 2026 Women in Hollywood event in Beverly Hills tonight, the actress arrived wearing a sleeveless, silky white gown. The dress featured a minimalist silhouette, but she added a playful touch to the look with a feathered oversize clutch.

Earlier in the night, she stepped out at the Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth premiere at TCL Chinese Theatre, just a short drive away. For that event, she opted for a plunging black halter gown.

Debicki is among this year’s Women in Hollywood honorees. In her recent ELLE cover story, she reflected on the challenge of finding her next project after her buzzed-about performance as Princess Diana on The Crown.

“I was really looking for a while, after I did The Crown, for something I wanted to do,” she said. “It took a little while for me to find those jobs.”

She ultimately found two projects that excited her: Wicker, a fairy-tale comedy starring Olivia Colman as a woman who marries a man made of wicker (played by Alexander Skarsgård), and David Fincher’s The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, in which she stars opposite Brad Pitt.

Debicki also opened up about the lessons she’s learned throughout her career, including the importance of maintaining a life outside of work.

“The thing that’s taken me the longest to learn is what to do with my real life,” she told ELLE. “When acting is the thing that you obsess over, that interests you most, that you’re the most driven to continue to do, it’s a bit of a compulsion, and your real life can get waylaid.”
