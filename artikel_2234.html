Portsmouth must show patience with £2m man amid 25-year-old’s surprising breakthrough: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Portsmouth have come leaps and bounds since last season and look far from relegation danger. Pompey currently sit 11th in the Championship, and their transfer business in the summer has been a crucial factor in their solid opening quarter of the campaign.

John Mousinho’s side made 12 new additions in the last window and spent a total of £11m. The South Coast outfit have seen a few of the signings hit the ground running. However, they are yet to see Rocco Shein justify his £2m fee.

Portsmouth signed the 23-year-old from Norwegian side Fredrikstad after the midfielder impressed in Europe. However, with the breakthrough of fellow summer arrival, Iyad Mohamed, Pompey haven’t had the chance to witness his ability as much as they would have liked.

Shein was also linked with Cardiff City and Millwall in the summer, and his performances in Europe warranted interest from the EFL. Before sealing his move to Fratton Park, the 23-year-old averaged a rating of 7.3 for Fredrikstad, according to FotMob.

The Estonian is known for his defensive attributes in midfield. He won 22 aerial duels and won possession in the final third 12 times in just 11 games during the 2026 Eliteserien season.

The Portsmouth summer signing can also play in a more advanced role in midfield and has been used there on one occasion this term. He put in a solid display against Wolves, where he had a pass accuracy of 84%.

Shein’s struggle to adapt to the Championship isn’t a surprise. It’s a main reason why he hasn’t featured as much as he would’ve liked. The 23-year-old has won just 34.7% of his duels and created just four chances.

Another reason as to why the Estonian has found game time hard to come by is due to Mohamed‘s displays. Pompey signed the 25-year-old from Portuguese outfit Casa Pia AC. He has been a welcome surprise at Fratton Park.

The Comoros midfielder has started Portsmouth’s last five games and has been a revelation in what would be Shein’s natural position. In Mousinho’s side’s latest outing, Mohamed won 80% of his ground duels and made seven defensive contributions.

In those last five games too, the South Coast outfit are unbeaten, and it isn’t a coincidence for it to happen when the 25-year-old is starting. He has been dribbled past just twice this term and Pompey have conceded only four times. They are a better defensive unit when he is on the pitch. Shein faces a tough task to get back into contention.
