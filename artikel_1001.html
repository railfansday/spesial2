Rams star Nacua set to miss rest of NFL season after knee surgery

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Los Angeles Rams star Puka Nacua injured ligaments in his knee during their win over the Washington Commanders; Rams coach Sean McVay was unable to confirm whether Nacua would be fit to return for the postseason; watch the NFL throughout the season live on Sky Sports

Puka Nacua is likely to miss the rest of the NFL season after undergoing knee surgery, Los Angeles Rams coach Sean McVay admitted on Friday.

Nacua injured ligaments in his knee while carrying the ball on the final offensive snap of the Rams' win over the Washington Commanders on November 8.

McVay had previously expressed optimism that Nacua could return to the field this season, with a possible late-December date mentioned, but said his star wide receiver decided this week that he should have knee surgery.

Nacua received a three-game suspension from the NFL on Thursday for violating its personal conduct policy, although he is currently on the injured reserve because of injuries to his knee and toe.

The earliest Nacua was eligible to return was when the Rams host Dallas Cowboys on December 20, although the MCL surgery on his knee - performed Tuesday - will leave him facing a longer wait for a competitive return.

"That'll be a couple months [out] for him," McVay said on Friday. "We knew that when the initial injury occurred that this was more likely the direction that this would go, so this isn't anything necessarily new on our end.

"There's a lot of thought and conversations that go into whether you're going to do that [surgery] or not, and what's the best plan of action for return to performance for him - not exclusive to this year, but for his career moving forward.

"He was very thorough and deliberate about that, but I did think that this [surgery] was a possibility. I knew that that was a serious injury that he sustained, whether he got it fixed or not. In my mind, this was always a direction that we had to plan upon anyways."

Nacua has been one of the most productive receivers in NFL history during his first four seasons, with the 25-year-old having 70 catches for 715 yards and four touchdowns while playing in just seven games this year.

McVay didn't rule out a return by Nacua during the playoffs, which begin in mid-January, but wouldn't speculate that far ahead.

The Rams (6-4) are currently outside of the playoff picture and will be without their top two pass-catchers when they host the Green Bay Packers on Wednesday night, as Davante Adams also isn't ready to return from a hamstring injury.
