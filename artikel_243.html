I test tablets, and the Amazon Alexa Tablets are my pick to shop during Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

When I'm not in the thick of testing e-readers, I'm working with tablets. The tablet that took me by surprise this year was Amazon's Alexa Tablet 12 Pro. Let's just say I was less than impressed by Amazon's Fire Tablets, and luckily the new Alexa tablets are nothing like them. No longer are you tied to Amazon's ecosystem, as the Alexa Tablets now run on Android OS, meaning you finally have access to the Google Play Store.

But an even better surprise was how discounted the Alexa Tablets are this Black Friday. They were just released last month, but already they're on sale. The Amazon Alexa Tablet 12 Pro is stacked with a nano-matte display and 128GB of storage, and you can get it all for under $400.

I think these are the best tablet deals to shop for during Black Friday, even better than iPads.

I couldn't wait to test the Amazon Alexa Tablet 12 Pro. A new Android operating system, access to the Google Play Store, and a nano-matte screen? Amazon seriously stepped up its game, making a tablet that could actually compete with the iPad. It does, in fact, live up to my expectations. The screen is vibrant, the speakers play clear, crisp audio, and it's quick to process, too. It's so fast that it even beats iPads in computing power. The Amazon Alexa Tablet 12 Pro has a Geekbench multi-core score of 6,498, beating the basic iPad's score of 6,233.

And right now, you can get this high-performing tablet for 27% off, saving $150. The Amazon Alexa Tablet 12 Pro is just $399.99, a stellar deal and a huge discount not to be missed.

But if you want something more affordable with a little less power, you can shop the Alexa Tablet 11 and 8 on sale too.

If you don't mind a little less power, check out the deals on the Amazon Alexa Tablet 11 and 8. Both are under $200 but still come with the new Android operating system. The Alexa Tablet 11 comes with 8GB of RAM, the same memory as many laptops, just at a much more affordable price. Meanwhile, the Amazon Alexa Tablet 8 is only $129.99 and is an affordable alternative to the iPad Mini.
