Should You Avoid MercadoLibre (MELI) Stock, Even Now That It's Near a 52-Week Low?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The stock has fallen far, but the company retains solid growth prospects.

By Selena Maranjian – Nov 19, 2026 at 8:05PM EST

10 stocks we like better than MercadoLibre ›

Shares of MercadoLibre (MELI -4.75%) were recently trading for about $1,427 apiece -- very close to their 52-week low of $1,418. The shares have dropped in value by about 30% over the past year (as of Nov. 17) -- though they've averaged annual gains of 20.6% over the past 15 years.

Many investors, seeing such a big drop in a stock's price, will steer clear, assuming there's trouble afoot. Others will take a closer look to see if the lower price is actually an enticing opportunity.

MercadoLibre is home to both a dominant online marketplace (Mercado Libre) and a major fintech business (Mercado Pago), both focused on the Latin American market. It's active in 18 countries and was recently delivering 3.2 billion items annually. It sports 136 million unique buyers and 93 million monthly active users (both numbers as of the third quarter). And it's growing briskly, with net revenue recently up about tenfold since 2020.
