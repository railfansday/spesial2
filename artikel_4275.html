Petit Planet unveils release date for MiHoYo's debut cosy life sim

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

MiHoYo are stepping out of their comfort zone a bit with the upcoming Petit Planet, a cosy life sim where you take care of your own planetoid. And soon, you'll be able to get your hands on it and give it a go. Petit Planet has been confirmed to launch for iOS and Android on December 4th!

Petit Planet sees you take on the role of a Tender, growing and expanding your own mini planetoid. You'll slowly gather a resource called Luca, and when you've created enough, you'll be able to add new life to your planet. Between building, planting, decorating and just enjoying life on your planet, you can explore the vast Starsea and go on adventures to other areas.

At the same time, this is a very different game for MiHoYo to tackle, given we best know them for exciting, action-packed ARPGs that are all about kicking ass and taking names. Petit Planet is far more laid-back, with a cast of talking animals and calming, soothing adventures across a colourful world. But perhaps that's exactly what people are looking for?

We'll just have to see when Petit Planet drops December 4th for iOS and Android.
