'Scream 8' Officially Confirmed by Original Star

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Since joining the team in 2024, she has written more than 1,000 news stories, with her work also cited by outlets like AOL and IMDb.

One of this century's biggest horror franchises has just confirmed that a veteran star will be back for its planned sequel. As reported earlier in March, Scream is set to return for its eighth installment, following the commercial success of Scream 7, despite its critical misfire. Released in theaters on February 27, 2026, Scream 7 debuted with a 43% critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes, which has since fallen to 29% from 228 reviews. Still, it became the franchise's highest-grossing film, earning $214 million worldwide against a $45 million production budget.

An official release date for Scream 8 has yet to be announced, but the film appears to be in active development, with no director on board yet. Meanwhile, writing duo Lilla and Nora Zuckerman have been tapped to write the screenplay. The sisters most recently served as showrunners for the hit Peacock series Poker Face and have also worked as writers and co-executive producers on popular shows including Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D., Suits, Fringe, and Prodigal Son.

As the horror sequel moves forward, Neve Campbell, who has been part of the franchise since its inception in 1996, has officially announced her return, per Deadline. While promoting her role in the Netflix series Black Doves, Campbell confirmed her involvement in Scream 8, naming it one of her two upcoming projects in an interview with the Associated Press. "Scream 8. That's in the pipeline as well," she said. However, when pressed for further details about the upcoming film, she said, "You know I can't tell you anything about Scream 8," before adding, "I'll be in it."

Campbell has starred in six of the seven Scream films so far, taking a break from the franchise for 2023's Scream VI after a contract dispute. She returned for Scream 7 after reportedly receiving a $7 million salary to play Sidney Prescott (now Evans) again, which supposedly helped the movie achieve its biggest box office success, with $63.6 million in its opening weekend alone.

In Scream 7, Sidney has built a new life: she's married to police chief Mark Evans, runs a coffeehouse, and raises her children, including her teenage daughter Tatum, named after Sidney's late best friend, Tatum Riley. Soon, her life is turned upside down when a new Ghostface killer emerges and targets her daughter. Franchise actors Courteney Cox and Matthew Lillard also returned in Scream 7.
