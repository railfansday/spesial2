Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Re: Looks like EFTPS won't be available for much longer for individuals

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The axe has fallen. I logged in to my EFTPS account and can confirm that tax payments can no longer be initiated.

Notice to Individual Taxpayers:
As of November 13th, 2026, Individuals can no longer make or modify payments via EFTPS.gov. If you are an individual taxpayer and are NOT enrolled in EFTPS.gov, you'll need to create an IRS Online Account for Individuals or use the IRS Direct Pay guest path. For existing enrolled individuals, the website will remain active to allow Individual taxpayers to login and see their Payment History and, they will be able to CANCEL a Scheduled payment.
