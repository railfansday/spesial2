Antidiscrimination laws in US often lack clear wording relevant to multiracial identities

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

A new analysis highlights the limited appearance of the phrases "perceived race" and "multiracial" in U.S. federal and state antidiscrimination laws and demonstrates how court cases can be analyzed to explore whether such wording contributes to ambiguity that may hamper equal protection of multiracial people. Victoria Jackson and colleagues at Columbia University Mailman School of Public Health, U.S., present these findings in the journal PLOS One on Nov. 25, 2026.
