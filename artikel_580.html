Music Industry Moves: Gram Parsons Estate Signs With Peermusic; Roger Miller Posthumously Inducted Into Songwriters Hall of Fame

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Peermusic has signed a publishing deal for the song catalog of Gram Parsons and will assume worldwide administration, it was announced last week by Peermusic CEO Mary Megan Peer and U.S./ Canada president Jamie Cerreta. Perhaps more than any other artist, Parsons — who was inducted posthumously into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame on November 14 — was responsible for the country-rock fusion that has spawned everything from the music of the Eagles to the entire Americana genre. His influence was obvious in a showcase held earlier this month at his old haunt, the Troubador in Los Angeles, featuring his musical collaborator Emmylou Harris, as well as Elvis Costello, the Black Crowes, and Margo Price. Polly Parsons said: “My father’s music has always had a life of its own, reaching across generations and continuing to inspire artists and listeners in ways I don’t think even he could have imagined. It means so much to me to help preserve that legacy while also finding new ways for people to discover his songs. His music was never bound by genre or convention, and I think that’s why it still resonates so deeply today. My hope is that this new partnership will not only honor the incredible body of work he left behind but also introduce his music to a whole new generation of fans. Keeping his spirit and his songs alive is something I hold very close to my heart.”

Jamie Cerreta commented: “Gram’s music is an enduring testament to how great songs remain in our hearts. All of us at Peermusic are so honored to represent his songs and to see his work celebrated through his recent induction into the Rock n Roll Hall of Fame. I am personally so happy to be working with Polly and Vector Management to help another generation discover the greatness of Gram Parsons.”

+ The Songwriters Hall of Fame has announced the posthumous induction of legendary singer-songwriter Roger Miller, known for such hits at “King of the Road” and “Dang Me.” Miller, who died in 1992 at age 56, was honored during an intimate ceremony held November 17, 2026, at BMI headquarters on Music Row in Nashville.

Miller’s widow Mary said: “We were ecstatic to learn that Roger was being inducted into the Songwriters Hall of Fame. Of all his many talents, he was most proud of his songwriting. This lifetime achievement would have meant a great deal to him, and we are honored to celebrate the recognition of his extraordinary body of work.”

+ Ignacio Meyer has been named president of Universal Music Latin America & Iberian Peninsula, effective immediately. He will report to Felipe Holguin, who was named CEO of the division last week. Meyer has held leadership roles at TelevisaUnivision since 2011. Most recently he was president of its U.S. Networks & Local Media; previously, he founded and served as managing partner of advisory firm Konexxion Enterprises, worked as an artist manager and served on the management team of IFPI Latin America.

Holguin said: “Artists are at the heart of how we’re building this next chapter. Nacho combines creative sensibility and strategic vision with a deep understanding of how to nurture each artist’s individuality, grow their audience, and heighten their impact across platforms. He’ll add a powerful dimension to our leadership team as we invest in extraordinary artists and bring bold ideas to life. We know from experience how well we work together, and I’m delighted to welcome him to UMG.”

+ Americana award-winning artist Nathaniel Rateliff has signed with Red Light Management’s Will Botwin and Coran Capshaw for representation. Rateliff recently marked his 10th straight year performing at Farm Aid after being unanimously appointed to the Farm Aid board of directors; performed at Tom Morello’s inaugural Power to the People festival alongside Bruce Springsteen, Dave Matthews and Morello; and he closed this year’s Newport Folk Festival alongside Matthews, Joan Baez and more with his Nathaniel Rateliff & Friends set.

+ Warner Records has signed 21-year-old Brazilian-Mexican-American singer Lambb and released her second EP, “There’s Someone in the Yard,” last week. The singer, born Julie Gaeta, combines indie singer-songwriter approach with house and techno, and last summer performed at the Reading Festival and Leeds Festival in England as part of her first headlining tour.

+ Position Music has signed Grammy-nominated artist and songwriter Masego to a worldwide publishing deal. Masego recently released his third album, “Fix Your Face,” and was signed to Position Music by Delmar Powell, VP of A&R for the company.

“Masego is a generational creative talent and a true artist in every sense of the word. His ability to seamlessly blend jazz, R&B, hip-hop, and global influences has created a sound that is uniquely his own, while continuing to push the boundaries of Black music. Beyond his incredible gifts as a songwriter, producer, and musician, I genuinely believe there are few artists who can match what he brings to the stage,” said Delmar Powell, VP of A&R.

+ Sweden-based music investment firm Pophouse Entertainment has made two senior appointments to its leadership team: Kristina Heney joins as President, Commercial & Marketing, based in New York, and Greg Lombardo joins as President, Pophouse Avatar Network, based in Los Angeles. According to the announcement, the pair will play central roles in the continued growth of Pophouse’s brands, live experiences and productions, which include artists such as Kiss, Iron Maiden, Tina Turner, Sia, Cyndi Lauper and Avicii. Heney joins from Oak View Group, where she most recently served as EVP, Marketing, Media and Conferences. She was previously Chief Marketing & Experience Officer of Cirque du Soleil Entertainment Group, along with roles at Madison Square Garden Entertainment and the NBA’s Global Merchandising Group. Lombardo joins from Netflix, where he was VP and Global Head of Experiences. He was previously at 20th Century Fox and BRC Imagination Arts.

+ Litmus Music, a music rights investment and management business, has announced approximately $475 million in new capital commitments from global investment firm Carlyle and investment funds managed by KKR, a leading global investment firm, to support its continued growth and strategic acquisition opportunities. Carlyle Global Credit, which partnered with Litmus’s management team to establish the business in 2022, will retain a majority stake in the Company following the transaction. The platform’s portfolio includes over 100,000 copyrights and recordings, including music from globally recognized artists and songwriters including Katy Perry, benny blanco, Randy Newman, and Keith Urban.

“Together with Carlyle and our team, we’ve built Litmus into a scalable music rights management business rooted in trust, creativity, analysis and, above all, our passion for music,” said Hank Forsyth, Co-Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Litmus Music. “This investment marks an exciting next chapter for our company, providing meaningful additional capital to pursue new deals and accelerate growth. We’re grateful for Carlyle’s continued partnership and look forward to working with our new strategic partner, KKR.”

+ David Wilson and Atika Lynch, both former Directors of Artist Development at Sony Music’s AWAL, have announced the launch of Cltr;shft, a fractional executive firm specializing in digital marketing, content, audience growth, and culture strategy. Wilson joins as Co-Founder and Fractional Head of Content & Culture Strategy, based in Los Angeles, and Lynch as Co-Founder and Fractional Head of Audience Growth & Culture Strategy, based in New York City.

The announcement states, “Through an approach the founders call culture architecture, the firm identifies what makes a client culturally meaningful, builds the narrative around it, and develops the content systems and audience-growth strategies that create the conditions for scale. As fractional executives, Wilson and Lynch work with clients on a project, fractional, or advisory basis, providing senior-level leadership without requiring them to build an entire department.” Wilson was previously with Nettwerk Music Group, while Lynch held roles at RCA Records, and Tommy Hilfiger.
