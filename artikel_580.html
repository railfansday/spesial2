‘You Can See Everything’ Now Streaming: Where to Watch Nathan Fielder’s Elizabeth Holmes Doc Online

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Fielder and Lance Oppenheim brought viewers into Holmes' home. Now she's coming into yours.

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, The Hollywood Reporter may receive an affiliate commission.

Barely six weeks after You Can See Everything first conquered the cultural conversation, Fielder and Oppenheim‘s genre-bending Elizabeth Holmes film has hit digital.

The duo’s record-breaking A24 documentary, capturing the disgraced Theranos founder in the days leading up to her prison sentence, is available on Prime Video and Apple TV as a premium video-on-demand (PVOD) for digital purchase or rental starting Tuesday, Nov. 24 at 12 a.m. ET (Monday, Nov. 23 at 9 p.m. PT). At a Glance: How to Watch You Can See Everything at Home

As a premium video-on-demand, the nearly three-hour documentary (2 hours and 54 minutes, to be exact) is available for digital purchase or rental starting Tuesday, Nov. 24 at 12 a.m. ET (Monday, Nov. 23 at 9 p.m. PT) via both Amazon Prime Video and Apple TV — $24.99 to buy or $19.99 to rent.

Entering an Elizabeth Holmes hole? Here’s your reading and watch list.

Catch Nathan for You on Paramount+ or HBO Max, The Rehearsal on HBO Max and Oppenheim’s Robert Pattinson-fronted Primetime for digital rental or purchase on either Prime Video or Apple TV.

The A24 doc is expected to hit HBO Max around four months after its theatrical release (give or take), which puts the anticipated release date sometime in February of 2027. This is a rough estimate and The Hollywood Reporter will update this story once said date has been confirmed.
