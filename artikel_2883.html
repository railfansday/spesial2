SoftBank plunges over 16% as AI sell-off hits Asian tech stocks following Anthropic debut

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Shares of Japan's SoftBank Group plunged 16.16% on Friday, leading a broader sell-off in Asian technology stocks following the highly anticipated market debut of artificial intelligence startup Anthropic.

SoftBank is one of the biggest backers of OpenAI, Anthropic's rival, having invested a total of $64.6 billion in the ChatGPT maker.

The sharp decline underscores growing investor concerns over SoftBank's aggressive bets on artificial intelligence, particularly its exposure to OpenAI, as markets question whether massive investments in AI infrastructure will generate sufficient returns.

SoftBank's heavy exposure to OpenAI has made its shares particularly sensitive to shifts in sentiment toward the AI sector. The stock lost 30% in October and is down 31% so far this month.

If Friday's losses hold, it would be SoftBank's worst day in more than two years.

Japanese semiconductor stocks also tumbled, with Tokyo Electron falling 7.19%, Lasertec declining 6.75% and Renesas Electronics dropping 6.71%.

The sell-off extended to South Korea, where Nvidia suppliers SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics dropped 6% and 4.7%, respectively, pressuring the benchmark Kospi, which fell 4.6%.

Elsewhere in the region, Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co, or TSMC, fell 4.76% in Taiwan.

Chinese tech giant Tencent declined 1.89%, while memory chipmaker CXMT fell 4.35% to a fresh record low, while Baidu dropped 4.42%. AI chipmaker Enflame, dubbed as one of China's "four little dragons" fell 3.93%.

The declines in Asia come after the tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite fell more than 2% overnight following Anthropic's highly anticipated Wall Street debut.
