New Wolves transfer update may spark interest at Birmingham City and Norwich City

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The January transfer window is on the horizon, and clubs in the Championship will already be looking at ways to strengthen their squads.

It’s a notoriously difficult month to do business, as clubs are reluctant to lose their key men midway through the campaign, which also means deals can come at a premium price.

However, there will be potential bargains out there, and one player who could fall into that category is ex-Celtic winger Liel Abada, who is currently playing with Charlotte FC in America.

The Israeli international is currently playing for Dean Smith’s side as they aim to win MLS Cup, and a 2-1 win over Chicago Fire last time out, where Abada scored, means they are in a good position to progress to the semi-final of the Eastern Conference.

Yet, it seems Abada’s future beyond this season is away from Charlotte, as Sky Sports reporter Lyall Thomas revealed that he is poised to leave when his contract expires next month.

That will end a three-season stay for Abada with the MLS outfit, and it’s fair to say he has had a mixed time of things.

Abada hasn’t always hit the heights expected of a designated player, but he has enjoyed his most productive campaign this time around, scoring 11 goals and registering five assists in 28 games.

Interestingly, the update over Abada suggested that Wolves are among the clubs tracking the winger, although those reports have been played down elsewhere.

So, it will be intriguing to see how it develops, but Wolves are likely to be in the market for a new winger in January.

Cesar Peixoto has a good squad at his disposal, although Wanderers are lacking consistent end product from the likes of Mateus Mane and Rodrigo Gomes.

That means it’s an area they could look to address, and the prospect of signing Abada on a free transfer is something that may appeal, even if it has been said that work permit issues could complicate a move for the 25-year-old, who may need to be loaned out initially.

Given Abada’s contract situation, it would be no surprise if a host of clubs were keeping tabs on the player.

He has done reasonably well for Charlotte over the years, and he hit double figures in consecutive years in Scotland, where he helped Celtic dominate domestically.

It has been claimed previously that Birmingham and Norwich were tracking the winger, so the opportunity to pick him up on a free transfer is likely to appeal.

With Abada capable of playing down both flanks, you could argue he would provide good depth for both clubs, who will fancy their chances of winning promotion this season.

When you consider Abada’s age, combined with the fact he will be available on a free, he is going to be a potential bargain for any club heading into January.

He does have flaws to his game, but Abada has proven regularly over the years that he can be a very good attacker.
