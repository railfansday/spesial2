Derby County dealt another injury blow with summer signing ruled out until January

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Derby County’s summer signing sustained the blow last time out away at Southampton. DCFC lost 2-0 to the Saints at St Mary’s and they are back in Championship action on Tuesday night with an away trip to Molineux to face Wolves.

Meister, 23, joined the Rams on loan from Pisa in the summer. He has made 10 appearances in all competitions since his switch to Pride Park but he hasn’t scored or picked up an assist yet.

In this latest update regarding his condition, his head coach Eustace has said, as per a report by DerbyshireLive: “Henrik has, I think, strained a ligament in his shoulder so I think he is going to be out for roughly around six weeks, I think.”

Derby County remain in deep trouble and they are in the drop zone with Burnley and Preston North End after their first 16 fixtures of the season. They are five points from safety behind Lincoln City and Watford as they aim to claw themselves to safety and away from the treat of relegation down to League One.

Derby County signed Meister in the last transfer window to bolster their attacking department. When fit, he gives DCFC more competition and depth going forward.

However, his switch to the Rams hasn’t worked out yet. He has struggled for fitness and his numbers haven’t been great.

Meister, who is an ex-Denmark youth international, was given the green light to leave Pisa on a temporary basis to get some game time under his belt at Pride Park. He hasn’t been able to make the impact he would have hoped to and now faces another frustrating spell on the sidelines.

The Copenhagen-born man has played for a variety of different teams across Europe so far in his career. Prior to his move to Italy with Pisa, he featured for Sarpsborg 08 and Rennes in Norway and France respectively.

Derby County have an option to buy Meister permanently from Pisa. It seems unlikely that DCFC will utilise this though as things stand.

The Rams’ chances of a full-time swoop will depend on a few factors. Firstly, if he continues to struggle with injury niggles then they won’t snap him up for good.

In addition, if he doesn’t start scoring goals soon they will likely look for other options. If Eustace’s side go down, they will need to cut their cloth accordingly and slash the wage bill.
