Hidden leaf dynamics could improve Amazon rainforest carbon uptake estimates

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

From space, the Amazon rainforest looks like an unbroken expanse of green. But a new study conducted by researchers from the School of Biological Sciences at The University of Hong Kong (HKU), together with international collaborators, reveals seasonal leaf changes hidden within this seemingly constant canopy. Largely undetected by conventional satellite observations, these changes have significant implications for estimating the rainforest's carbon uptake.
