Stock Market Midday, Nov. 27: Tech Stocks Recover and Amazon Posts Black Friday Gains

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Investors are weighing strong Black Friday data and AI optimism as stocks trade higher, today, Nov. 27, 2026.

By Emma Newbery – Nov 27, 2026 at 12:11PM EST

As of 11:36 AM ET, the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC +2.31%) is up 1.86% to 24,016, the S&P 500 (^GSPC +1.60%) has gained 1.38% to 7,282, and the Dow Jones Industrial Average (^DJI +1.18%) is trading 1.15% higher to 50,760 as technology stocks rebound from recent correction territory.

Gold prices have jumped 1.66% to $3,626.60, while the 10-Year Treasury yield is at 4.96% (-0.07%). All sectors gained, with energy and consumer cyclical stocks outpacing healthcare and consumer defensive sectors.

Sandisk (SNDK +15.96%) and Dell Technologies (DELL +6.70%) jumped 12.74% and 5.96%, respectively, as investors returned to artificial intelligence (AI) stocks. Nvidia (NVDA +0.80%) slipped on reports that it would halt chip orders to certain countries in Southeast Asia and that its Blackwell semiconductors had been installed in a Shanghai data center.

Amazon.com (AMZN +3.96%) has climbed 3.81% on strong Black Friday demand. Walmart (WMT +0.90%) also edged higher as investors digested early Black Friday data, which could indicate the health of consumer spending as we head into the end of the year. Vertex Pharmaceuticals (VRTX -5.24%) fell after the FDA delayed a decision on a key kidney drug.

Markets snapped back in today's shortened trading session, as tech stocks bounced and Black Friday spending boosted retailers. Revised data released today showed that Q3 GDP increased by 4.3%, underscoring economic resilience. This, along with initial positive insights into holiday outlays, seems to show spending remains relatively robust.

Major indexes look set to finish the week in the green, and investors are watching to see if this recovery will hold. For investors looking for stock bargains, the important thing is to focus on quality companies with solid fundamentals that you plan to hold for the long term. Given that key questions about the AI trade remain unanswered, more volatility is likely.
