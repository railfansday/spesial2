CoreWeave and Nebius: 2 AI Neocloud Stocks Due for a Rebound

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Neocloud stocks have sold off heavily in the wake of the Nvidia transition.

By Keithen Drury – Nov 18, 2026 at 2:15PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Nebius Group ›

The neocloud industry has gotten some bad headlines recently. While not as damaging as some of the headlines surrounding former Nvidia (NVDA -2.79%) CEO Jensen Huang, the secondary effects are harming neocloud stocks like CoreWeave (CRWV -4.30%) and Nebius (NBIS +2.22%). Both of these companies operate in this segment and may have seen the ground shift from underneath them.

But does that warrant the huge sell-off they've experienced? Let's take a look at these two, and see if now is the perfect buying opportunity.

The neocloud business is a fairly new one, and it was brought about by the massive investments in AI infrastructure. Essentially, the biggest AI hyperscalers couldn't get enough computing capacity online fast enough, so other companies decided to form and start building their own AI data centers, then rent those out to the AI hyperscalers. This has been a fast-growing business, as the hyperscalers are basically willing to pay a huge sum of money for access to more computing power.

The biggest problem with this is who is funding these data centers. These upstarts don't generate ample cash flows to build data centers, like some of the other major cloud computing providers. Instead, they must seek outside funding.

This can come from multiple sources, including issuing debt and selling shares in the company. One of the biggest backers of these two was Nvidia, as it saw CoreWeave and Nebius as two of the best operators in this space. Nvidia became a major shareholder of both companies as a result, but that relationship may be coming to an end.

With Huang out as CEO and Colette Kress, who comes from the CFO role, assuming the interim CEO role, these deals may be on hold. CoreWeave and Nebius could lose one of their major financiers and have to turn to the debt markets to raise funds.

That could be an issue, as CoreWeave recently saw some of its debt get priced at "distressed" levels, which means there's a low likelihood of it repaying its commitments. If CoreWeave were to default on its debt, that would leave the neocloud industry with a black eye. Funding would likely dry up for every company involved in this space, as lenders worry about the next firm to go belly-up.

There is huge risk in this industry right now, and the stocks are being priced accordingly. But with high risk comes high potential reward, and these stocks could rebound with a vengeance if the market changes its perception.

Both CoreWeave and Nebius are growing rapidly, but the market really only cares whether these two can pay off their debts and grow sustainably. Because neither of these companies is profitable, judging them on projected sales is the best measure.

CRWV PS Ratio (Forward 1y) data by YCharts

CoreWeave trades for a forward price-to-sales ratio of just 1, while Nebius is a bit more expensive at around 3. The price-to-sales ratio isn't as commonly used as the price-to-earnings ratio because it doesn't account for profit margins, but with these two producing no profits, it's the best tool.

To put it in perspective how cheap these two stocks are, if they could turn a 20% profit margin (which they're a long way from doing), that would price CoreWeave at 5 times forward earnings and Nebius at about 14. Those are cheap price tags for the growth these two are putting up.

But once again, the problem isn't growth; it's whether they can reach profitability. If they can, there's huge upside here. If they can't, then investors should stay away.

I think the market has gotten too bearish on these companies, pricing in virtually no chance of success. If these two can continue with the status quo, they could be due for a huge rebound.

I'm a cautious buyer here, and investors need to keep sizing to a minimum, as the risk is high.
