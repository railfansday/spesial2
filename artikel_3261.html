Screen Gems Sees Layoffs Under Newly Appointed Fede Goldenberg

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Screen Gems has made significant cuts: SVP Nathan Samdahl, VP Ellie Walker and Creative Executive Peyton Collins are all out, Deadline has confirmed.

This comes a month after a shift in leadership at the Sony genre division: Fede Goldenberg, formerly of YouTube, took the reins as head of Screen Gems in October, taking over from Ashley Brucks, who segued to a producing role after three years at the top.

Reporting to Sanford Panitch, President of Sony Pictures‘ Motion Picture Group, Goldenberg was hired to help guide Screen Gems into a new era — helping it set up projects with “the next generation of digital-native storytellers” — i.e., your Curry Barker or Kane Parsons — alongside more “traditional genre films.”

Screen Gems is coming off the release of Insidious: Out of the Further, which grossed over $171 million worldwide. THR was first to the news of the cuts. Watch on Deadline
