Let Bella Hadid’s Cozy Snow-Day Looks Inspire Your Winter Wardrobe

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

It’s hard to forget the joy-filled photo of Bella Hadid catching snowflakes with her tongue while in a fur coat and hair rollers. The sweet, candid moment happened in January, when the snow hit the ground—and luckily for the supermodel, it’s around that time again.

As the holiday season nears, Hadid is bringing out her cozy winter wardrobe, and from the look of it, she’s leaning into warm browns, bright reds, easy knits, plaid and paisley patterns, and some Western staples.

In a recent Instagram post, Hadid wears a zip-up candy apple-red ski jacket from Miu Miu with a high neck, paired with black ski pants, snow boots, and fluffy gloves. The snow-day look was accessorized with some very Hadid pieces: oversize black sunglasses, big gold hoops, and a red hair clip.

In another post, Hadid gave us glimpses at several of her recent winter fits.

One happy video saw her standing under a rain of snowflakes (throwback!) while in a pretty cream cardigan with scallop detailing. She layered the knit over a classic white T-shirt and paired it with low-rise blue jeans, a brown leather cowgirl belt with a silver buckle, and a stretchy brown headband. A third layer, in the form of a fluffy flannel jacket, kept her warm.

Other outfits included a black turtleneck sweater styled with skinny glasses and big gold hoops; a tan jacket with a black fur collar worn with a medallion necklace; a red bandana look topped with a trucker hat and her go-to hoops; and a caramel suede jacket paired with chocolate-brown leather pants and a matching scarf and hat.

If ever you find yourself in doubt about what to wear this winter, just look to the queen of the snowflakes for inspiration.
