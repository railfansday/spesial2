Aaron Rodgers claims Mike McCarthy slept through, missed meetings in Green Bay

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

I did not have former Green Bay Packers head coach Mike McCarthy's sleep patterns on my bingo card for "Thanksgiving Eve," but that's where Aaron Rodgers' appearance on The Pat McAfee Show took us on Wednesday. We're going to focus on the Packers-related news that Rodgers, who was benched and then later waived by the Pittsburgh Steelers this week, dropped today.

According to Rodgers, a tipping point in his relationship with McCarthy, now the head coach of the Steelers, was McCarthy breaking the locker room with a "1-2-3-Packers" chant against the Philadelphia Eagles last week, when Pittsburgh dropped the game 19-16 to fall to 6-4 on the season. That moment, per Rodgers, was an encapsulation of McCarthy's lack of preparedness — something that apparently stretches back to his days in Green Bay.

Rodgers claims that McCarthy has had issues with sleeping in meetings or missing meetings for well over a decade now:

Nobody knew it was going on for ten years in Green Bay. Even when I brought that up yesterday, his response was, "I fucking hate those coaches; that's why I didn't hire any of them in Pittsburgh." And I'm like, that's the worst response you could give. That's not even addressing the content of what I'm saying. I'm saying that you had unhealthy habits back then. We worked around it. I looked into what was going on in Dallas. Same exact stuff.

When discussing why he believed in McCarthy turning a corner in Pittsburgh, Rodgers stated that he didn't believe that McCarthy, a Yinzer, was "going to sleep through his dream job." He also noted that Packers head coach Matt LaFleur's schedule during the week is much different than McCarthy's approach.

In terms of blow-ups, Rodgers could only remember two periods in Green Bay that were touchy with McCarthy:

The only time we had it out was back in 2014, like behind closed doors, where it got really contentious. 2014 after the NFC Championship Game and I just asked him about his habits that week and he had missed multiple walkthroughs and meetings and post-practice stuff. Man, it got heated. It got heated in 2018 as well, after the Buffalo game. Same thing.

Worth mentioning: A former Packers teammate of Rodgers, linebacker A.J. Hawk, did not speak up to push back on the quarterbacks' stories from his time in Green Bay. You'd think that if Hawk didn't see these patterns of absence from McCarthy, he probably would have said something on Wednesday.

Since Rodgers' appearance, there have been a lot of reports that have supported what he said today.

Kalyn Kahler, currently of ESPN, referenced her 2018 piece for Sports Illustrated, where she mentioned that McCarthy would miss meeting in Green Bay. Nearly a decade later, she is adding that Rodgers brought up McCarthy sleeping through or missing meetings from a new source, a former Packer, who mentioned Rodgers bringing up these issues in both 2017 and 2018.

ESPN's Brooke Pryor, who covers the Steelers, claims multiple sources can vouch for Rodgers' "1-2-3-Packers" story.

Former Packers offensive lineman TJ Lang isn't exactly pushing back on Rodgers' stories, but is saying that a Rodgers of the past would have been against using the media in this way.

NFL reporter Jordan Schultz says at least one Steelers player is "happy it's out there," and added that McCarthy has an ice cream sandwich assistant.
