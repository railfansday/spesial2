The Nvidia Affair

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

An illicit relationship between the C.E.O. and an alleged Chinese spy is putting the spotlight on a murky ecosystem of companies smuggling A.I. chips into China.

The news this weekend could have been ripped from a Hollywood thriller: The founder and chief executive of Nvidia, one of the world’s most valuable companies, admitted to an affair with a woman who, according to U.S. intelligence, is a spy for China. The scandal could have enormous implications for a company that is crucial to America’s strategic advantage on artificial intelligence.

It’s also shining a spotlight on a group of companies, like the one where the woman was an executive, that seem to exist primarily for one purpose — to help Chinese companies gain access to Nvidia’s chips, the crucial commodity needed for A.I. data centers. Today, my colleague Alicia Wittmeyer writes about that ecosystem.

The Singapore-based firm that employed Alice Huang, the woman who had an affair with Jensen Huang, bought roughly $2 billion of Nvidia products over the past few years.
