Serie A | Como 2-0 Cagliari: Comfortable Lariani return to victory

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Como got back on track by disposing of Cagliari 2-0 thanks to Jacobo Ramon and Lucas Da Cunha headers, both assisted by Yan Couto.

The Lariani saw their run of four straight wins ended by defeat to Inter going into the break for international duty, with Moise Kean, Assane Diao, Jesus Rodriguez and Jayden Addai still long-term injuries, but Trevoh Chalobah returned to the bench and Nico Paz was rested after bagging a brace for Argentina.

The Sardinians had the opposite, ending four defeats in a row with victory over Frosinone. Yerry Mina, Harry Winks, Joseph Liteta and Mattia Felici missed out.

Bruno Kaiki sent three defenders to ground with a dummy, but then curled just wide of the far top corner, while Daniel Maldini burst down the left and Yan Couto did just enough to stop him getting the angled drive on target.

Como took the lead when Couto got down the right and pumped in a cross for his fellow defender Jacobo Ramon to leap up for a header from six yards, as a makeshift striker.

Ramon was also decisive as a defender, because he made a desperate goal-saving interception when Daniel Maldini rolled it across for a Paul Mendy tap-in at the back post.

Maldini went clear again after a give and go with Jacopo Fazzini, but at full stretch under pressure he turned the finish wide, and Ramon threw himself in the path of an Alessandro Romano volley.

Michel Adopo limped off with a suspected calf injury, and Como doubled their lead with another assist from Yan Couto, this time for the crouching header from Lucas Da Cunha.

That really did knock the wind out of Cagliari’s sails and the hosts were in complete control, with Lahdo’s snapshot palmed round the far post by Elia Caprile.

Lahdo tested Caprile again, but in stoppages the goalkeeper kept the scoreline down by pushing the ferocious Tasos Douvikas strike onto the woodwork, then defenders scrambled to prevent Nico Paz turning in the rebound.
