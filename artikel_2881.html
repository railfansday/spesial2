Republic Power receives Nasdaq delisting notice over delayed annual filing

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Republic Power Group (RPGL) received a Nasdaq delinquency notice dated November 19 for failing to timely file its FY2026 Form 20-F annual report.

The company has until January 19, 2027, to submit a compliance plan to Nasdaq.

If Nasdaq accepts the plan, the company may receive an extension until May 17, 2027, to regain compliance.

RPGL intends to submit its compliance plan within the prescribed 60-day period.
