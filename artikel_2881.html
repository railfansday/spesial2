Auburn basketball battles Appalachian State: Live updates, highlights

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Auburn Tigers are back on the floor to face the Appalachian State Mountaineers in a key nonconference showdown.

The Tigers will face stiff competition including No. 11 Arizona and West Virginia over the next two weeks, and Monday's game against Appalachian State will help them prepare for the gauntlet.
