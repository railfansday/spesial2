Celestica: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Custom AI chip platforms are becoming a major revenue driver for Celestica as hyperscalers shift toward proprietary processors.

In its third-quarter 2026 financial results and investor day call, held on Oct. 27, 2026, Celestica (CLS +0.43%) announced that full-year 2027 revenue is projected to reach $40 billion behind accelerating demand for artificial intelligence infrastructure. The enterprise hardware manufacturer is rapidly expanding production capacity and working capital investments to meet multiyear commitments from major technology customers. Three key takeaways from the presentation outline how the company is positioning itself for sustained growth.

Demand for high-speed network switches in data centers is shifting rapidly toward faster transmission speeds. Management projects 1.6T (terabit per second) networking equipment will generate more revenue than 800G units in 2027 as hyperscale cloud providers upgrade their infrastructure.

Tech leaders are moving beyond off-the-shelf graphics processors toward application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs), custom microchips engineered for specialized artificial intelligence workloads. Celestica is building hardware for major custom chip programs, including Google's tensor processing unit (TPU) platform and OpenAI's Jalapeño architecture.

To support $20 billion of expected top-line growth in 2027, the company plans capital expenditures (spending on physical assets and manufacturing facilities) of $1.5 billion to $2 billion. Management is deploying working capital and expanding facilities in Texas, Thailand, and Mexico to handle massive product rollouts.

Data center capacity constraints are extending customer commitments further into the future, creating multiyear visibility for hardware providers. Customers are engaging in long-term capacity planning to secure necessary manufacturing space and power availability.

Structural advantages in joint design, automated assembly, and liquid cooling position the business to capture long-term market share. Management is watching working capital efficiency, supply chain lead times, and factory utilization as capacity expands globally.
