FluentVoice Pro 1.6.7: limits that protect you from accidents, and a window that stays tidy

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

FluentVoice Pro is my free, open-source text-to-speech tray app for Windows 11 and 10: select or copy text anywhere, press a hotkey, and it reads it aloud with natural Microsoft online voices, offline HD voices (Piper and Kokoro) or the built-in Windows voices. Version 1.6.7 is out, and it's about what happens at the edges: very long text, very fast copying, and very wide screens.

Here's the two-minute tour (the real app, real voices; sound on):

A tray app that reads your clipboard has to cope with whatever lands there. Four small limits came out of that:

A switch under Auto-Read on Copy (off by default) shows the copied text in the Direct Text Reader while Settings is open. The rules matter more than the feature:

Maximized on a wide monitor, everything used to stretch from one edge of the screen to the other. Now the header, all five tabs and the footer keep a comfortable width (about 1,100 points), centred, and the sliders and main buttons keep a fixed size. At the normal window size nothing changes.
