Colts rule out 5 players ahead of primetime matchup with Texans

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Colts have ruled out five players ahead of Thursday night's primetime Week 11 road game against the Texans, including three starters.

The Colts have been hit hardest on its offensive line, which will be without starting tackles Bernhard Raimann and Jalen Travis on Thursday against NFL sack leader Will Anderson Jr., who managed 2.5 sacks during the teams' Week 3 matchup when Indianapolis' offensive line was healthy. Raimann suffered a concussion early in the Colts' Week 10 win over the Dolphins, and with a short week, progressing through the NFL's concussion protocol was an unlikely scenario.

Travis, missing his second straight game due to a knee injury, was banged up entering the Colts' Week 9 home game but played. He missed all three of the team's practices leading up to Week 10 and was ruled out prior to the game. Travis was estimated to have missed all three of the team's practices -- had they been held this week -- as Colts head coach Shane Steichen opted for walkthrus.

Without Travis in Week 10, right guard Matt Goncalves had shifted to right tackle, with Dalton Tucker filling in at right guard. Now also without Raimann, Luke Tenuta will make his first NFL start at left tackle, next to rookie left guard Jalen Farmer, who will be making his fourth career start.

The Colts will also be without starting cornerback Charvarius Ward, who left Sunday's game with what was initially described as a shin injury. Ward has since been an estimated DNP throughout the team's three walkthrus this week with a knee injury. Despite several injuries cropping up for Ward since the summer, this will be his first missed game of the year. Defensive tackle Jerry Tillery (calf) and tight end Will Mallory (hamstring) will both miss their second consecutive game for the Colts.

The Colts have also listed four players, including starting cornerback Sauce Gardner, questionable. Gardner left Sunday's game with an injury to the same left calf that held him out of Week 7 earlier this year and lost him four full games and parts of two more in the back-half of 2025. He was estimated to have missed all three practices this week. Starting long snapper Luke Rhodes, who missed just his second game in nearly a decade in Indianapolis, is also questionable with a hamstring injury he suffered in Week 9 that cost him all three practices last week and ruled him out for Week 10. Rhodes was an estimated non-participant Monday but was limited Tuesday and Wednesday this week, a sign he's moving in the right direction.

Starting slot receiver and return man Anthony Gould (toe) is questionable, though he's also trending in the right direction. Gould was limited in the team's first two estimated practice reports this week, but he was listed as a full participant Wednesday.

Offensive tackle Charlie Heck will not travel with the team to Houston on Wednesday due to personal reasons, and he's also listed as questionable.

Earlier this week, the Colts also placed linebacker Jaylon Carlies (neck) on injured reserve, joining a slew of Indianapolis players added to the list who have not yet been activated off it including tight end Mo Alie-Cox (groin), Akeem Davis-Gaither (knee), defensive lineman Micheal Clemons (toe), wide receiver Alec Pierce (heel) and Cam Taylor-Britt (groin). Pierce and Clemons have both missed eight games, while Taylor-Britt has missed three and Alie-Cox and Davis-Gaither each missed their first on Sunday.
