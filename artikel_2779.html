Chicago Bears Injury Update: Colston Loveland is out

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Chicago Bears have ruled tight end Colston Loveland out of the remainder of today's game against the Detroit Lions with a knee injury. He was rolled up on, hobbled to the sideline, went into the medical tent, and was ultimately carted to the locker room.

The sideline report on CBS didn't sound promising, saying Loveland was in tears and frustrated before he left the field.

With Loveland out, the Bears are left with rookie Sam Roush and Stephen Carlson at tight end.

We'll have more about Loveland when it's reported.
