UCLA vs Michigan Preview: Win Clinches Big Ten Title Spot

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Support Local Media - Click to Add LA Sports Report as a Preferred Source on Google

No. 4 UCLA (10-0, 7-0 Big Ten) visits Michigan (5-5, 2-5) at Michigan Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 21, at 12:30 p.m. PT on CBS. A win would send the Bruins to the Big Ten Championship Game and give them a program-record 11th victory.

Nov 7, 2026; Minneapolis, Minnesota, USA; UCLA Bruins head coach Bob Chesney walks the sideline against the Minnesota Golden Gophers during the second half at Huntington Bank Stadium. Mandatory Credit: Nick Wosika-Imagn Images

The stakes in Ann Arbor are clear. Beat Michigan and UCLA locks up a place in the 2026 Big Ten Championship Game, set for Dec. 5 at Lucas Oil Stadium in Indianapolis.

A victory would also give the Bruins 11 wins, the most in a single season in program history. It would tie the school record of eight conference wins in a season, too.

For head coach Bob Chesney, it would be a 21st straight regular-season win.

Chesney has already rewritten the record book. His seven-win improvement is the biggest one-year turnaround in UCLA history, topping the six-win jump Pepper Rodgers produced in his second season in 1972.

This is UCLA's 10th 10-win season and its first since 2014. It's also only the third time the Bruins have won 10 regular-season games, after 1946 and 1998.

Voters have noticed. Over the last eight weeks UCLA has climbed from No. 23 to No. 4 in the AP poll, its highest ranking since Week 8 of the 2001 season.

The Bruins take the ball away better than anyone in the country. They lead the nation in total takeaways, interceptions and fumble recoveries, and they're second in turnover margin.

The run defense is just as stingy. UCLA ranks seventh in FBS against the run and has given up only three runs of 20 yards or more all season, the longest a 26-yarder against Wisconsin on Oct. 17. Seven of 10 opponents have been held under 100 rushing yards.

UCLA has also allowed seven points or fewer in 12 of its 20 halves this season, including six halves without a point allowed.

On offense, UCLA runs the ball better than anyone in the conference. The Bruins lead the Big Ten with 202.7 rushing yards per game and 26 rushing touchdowns.

They spread it around, too. In three different games this season, three different UCLA running backs have scored rushing touchdowns.

Kickoff at Michigan Stadium is 12:30 p.m. PT (3:30 p.m. ET) Saturday, and CBS has the TV broadcast. Radio listeners in Los Angeles can tune to 790 AM KABC, and the game is also on SiriusXM channel 85.

Before the trip, defensive lineman Tyson Ford and wide receiver Semaj Morgan spoke to reporters, along with offensive coordinator Dean Kennedy, defensive coordinator Colin Hitschler and Chesney. Chesney and selected players will talk again after the game.
