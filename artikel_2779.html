Cowboys injury: Jalen Thompson questionable vs. Eagles

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

According to reports, not everyone is in the holiday spirit around the Dallas Cowboys. It came out in the media today that changes to the defensive playcaller were not well received by everyone in the building. As for the head coach Brian Schottenheimer, he is in for a critical stretch to end the regular season. As the rumor mill tells us Schottenheimer must guide this team to the playoffs or be at risk at losing his job. It's hard to imagine this season being anything but a disappointment if they can't do that much. The team has invested a lot into the roster and expectations are high. That also means that a visiting Philadelphia Eagles team missing several key pieces from a postseason run last year shouldn't give the Cowboys too much trouble Thursday. After looking at the injury report for both teams the Eagles are looking healthier but, Dallas should take care of business to draw back to .500. Here's what we know after the final injury report of the week.

Tyler Booker (Ankle), Jake Ferguson (Knee/Ankle) , Jonathan Mingo (Ankle) have all been ruled out. Jalen Thompson (Hamstring) has been listed as questionable and hoping to come back from injured reserve. Jaishawn Barham (Hip/Groin) and Dee Winters (Knee) were upgraded to full participants and will play against the Eagles. Cobie Durant (Neck), Donovan Ezeiruaku (Ankle), Joey Porter Jr. (Foot), and Dak Prescott (Left Hand) carry no injury designation entering the game.

Arnold Ebiketie (Oblique) and Jonathan Jones (Ankle) have been ruled out. Quinyon Mitchell (Hamstring) and Saquon Barkley (Neck/Illness) practiced fully today and will play tomorrow. Also practicing fully today for Philadelphia are Jalen Carter (Quadriceps), Dallas Goedert (Shin), Jonathan Greenard (Ankle), and DeVonta Smith (Hamstring) and will take the field against Dallas.
