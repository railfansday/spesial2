Abilene Wylie falls to Montgomery Lake Creek in 5A DII UIL Volleyball State Championship

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Abilene Wylie Lady Bulldogs made their return to the UIL Volleyball State Championships on Saturday morning, Nov. 21, at the Curtis Calwell Center in Garland, Texas.

The Lady Bulldogs – who were making their first appearance in a UIL Volleyball State Championship game since beating La Grange for the 3A state title in 2012 – found themselves locked in a fiercely competitive match against the Montgomery Lake Creek Lady Lions.

What began as a back-and-forth battle, though, gradually slipped away from Wylie, which fell in straight sets to Lake Creek 3-0 (25-19, 28-26, 25-14) in the Class 5A Division II UIL Volleyball State Championship match.

The loss ends a remarkable postseason run by the Lady Bulldogs that included straight-set victories over West Texas powers Wolfforth Frenship and El Paso Del Valle, as well as several signature wins against DFW-area powerhouse programs Eagle Mountain, Argyle and Colleyville Heritage in the later rounds of the UIL Playoffs.

Senior outside hitter Haddie Mock – a Stephen F. Austin commit – and senior middle blocker Jenna Stricklin set the tone for the Wylie defensively and led the Lady Bulldogs offensively along the net.

Mock tallied seven kills, six blocks and one dig, while Stricklin recorded seven kills, five digs and two blocks.

Junior outside hitter Kiki Bolden and junior defensive specialist Morgan Bearden and Annalise Loudermilk anchored Abilene Wylie's back-line defense and were instrumental in extending several key rallies.

Bolden – Wylie's team captain – logged 17 digs, five kills, three blocks and one assist. Loudermilk posted a team-leading 19 assists with two digs, two kills and two blocks, while Bearden finished with a team-high 18 digs and three assists.

Lake Creek junior Blaire Sexton was named MVP of the 5A DII UIL Volleyball State Championship match after racking up 15 kills, 10 doubles and four aces.

Montgomery Lake Creek (44-10) secured its first UIL Volleyball State Championship in school history during the Lady Lions' first trip to the state tournament since 2022.

Abilene Wylie, meanwhile, closes out its best season in program history at 39-5 overall with the Lady Bulldogs finishing their season in the state tournament for just the fourth time in school history and the first time in 14 years.
