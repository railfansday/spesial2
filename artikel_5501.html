PangeAI Partners with Sky Blue Impact to Accelerate Data Center Site Analysis with Agentic Geospatial AI

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Sky Blue Impact uses PangeAI's plain-English geospatial platform to assess grid, climate and hazard factors across potential data center locations, without lengthy consultant-led studies.

PALO ALTO, Calif., Nov. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- PangeAI, the agentic geospatial AI platform, announced that Sky Blue Impact has become a client, using PangeAI to support site analysis for data center development. The work focuses on helping Sky Blue's team evaluate and compare potential locations earlier and with greater confidence.

As demand for data centers continues to grow, choosing the right location has become one of the most consequential decisions developers make. Beyond land and cost, every candidate site must be evaluated for:

Agentic geospatial AI screens data center sites for grid access, water and climate risk in minutes, not weeks.

Getting any one of these wrong can delay a project by years or add significant cost over the life of the facility. This analysis has traditionally meant lengthy studies from external GIS and engineering consultants, slowing down early-stage decisions and limiting how many options a team can realistically consider.

With PangeAI, Sky Blue Impact's team can ask site questions in plain English and receive decision-ready answers drawn from Earth-observation, climate and infrastructure data. PangeAI's autonomous geospatial analyst does the work a GIS specialist would normally do, gathering the relevant data, running the analysis and presenting the results in a form the team can act on. Questions that would typically take a consultant days or weeks to answer can be answered in minutes.

Because every site is assessed against the same criteria, the team gets a consistent view of risk and opportunity across all the locations it is considering. Answers come back with the underlying data and maps, so findings can be checked and shared with partners, investors or planning authorities.

"Data center siting is a high-stakes decision, and the teams making it shouldn't have to wait weeks for answers," said Johanna von der Leyen, CEO and co-founder of PangeAI. "We built PangeAI so that anyone on a development team can ask a question about a site and get a clear, evidence-based answer. Working with Sky Blue Impact shows how quickly that can change the way location decisions get made."

The collaboration reflects PangeAI's growing focus on data centers and energy infrastructure, sectors where location decisions carry long-term cost and resilience consequences.

Organizations evaluating sites for data centers or energy projects can learn more and see how PangeAI works at pangeai.com.
