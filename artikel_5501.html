SuperX (NASDAQ: SUPX) to Make U.S. Exhibition Debut at SC26, Showcasing Full-Stack AI Infrastructure Solutions

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Company to highlight its NVIDIA ecosystem-aligned AI infrastructure capabilities, AI Token Platform and Asia-Pacific delivery expertise.

SINGAPORE, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- SuperX AI Technology Limited (NASDAQ: SUPX) ("SuperX" or the "Company"), a full-stack AI infrastructure solutions provider, today announced its participation in SC26, the International Conference for High Performance Computing, Networking, Storage, and Analysis, taking place November 17â19, 2026, in Chicago. Making its U.S. exhibition debut, SuperX will showcase its full-stack AI infrastructure solutions built around the NVIDIA accelerated computing ecosystem, alongside its AI Token Platform and regional deployment expertise.

As inference-intensive and agentic AI applications grow alongside model training, infrastructure requirements are becoming increasingly diverse. Bringing AI computing capacity into production involves more than GPU performance alone. It requires the integration of computing, networking, power, cooling and operational support to enable reliable, scalable AI environments.

As an AI infrastructure solutions provider, SuperX designs workload-driven solutions around NVIDIA accelerated computing technologies and ecosystem architectures. Combining system integration expertise with modular AIDC solutions and lifecycle services, the Company helps customers turn infrastructure requirements into deployable AI environments.

With an established supply chain and Global Services network across Asia-Pacific, SuperX has built a growing track record in regional AI infrastructure delivery, including completed AI server deliveries and ongoing customer projects across Asia Pacific regions including Japan and Australia.

At SC26, SuperX will demonstrate its AI Token Platform. The platform simplifies access to third-party AI model services through unified APIs, intelligent routing and centralized management. The showcase also highlights SuperX's broader capabilities across AI computing infrastructure and model services, reflecting its expanding role in supporting AI inference workloads.

"As AI moves into production at scale, the competitive advantage will increasingly depend on how effectively advanced computing technologies can be translated into reliable, operational infrastructure. This requires more than individual products. It takes the right architecture, regional execution capabilities and sustained support. At SC26, we look forward to engaging with U.S. customers and partners on how SuperX can support their AI infrastructure ambitions across Asia-Pacific and beyond," said Kenny Sng, CTO of SuperX.

SuperX invites U.S. enterprises, AI cloud providers, infrastructure operators and technology partners to visit Booth 4550 at McCormick Place, Chicago, November 17â19, to discuss their AI workload requirements, regional deployment plans and potential collaboration opportunities.

To arrange a meeting with the SuperX team, visit https://www.superx.sg/events/2026-sc26.

SuperX AI Technology Limited (NASDAQ: SUPX) is a full-stack AI infrastructure solutions provider headquartered in Singapore. Through its subsidiaries, the Company provides AI infrastructure solutions, including AI servers, related IT equipment and services, and AI cloud computing and AI model API services.
