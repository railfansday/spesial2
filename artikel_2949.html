Why Skip Bayless would still take Texas football’s Arch Manning No. 1 overall in 2027 NFL Draft amid rocky season

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

After the No. 6 Texas football team got the 22-21 win over the No. 21 LSU Tigers last Saturday, there was some praise for quarterback Arch Manning after leading the comeback despite a rough start. With the Texas football team looking to build off the ugly win, Manning's NFL future is a completely different conversation, as Skip Bayless has a bold declaration on his draft stock.

There's no denying Manning is in the midst of a disappointing season compared to pre-season expectations, as he has thrown for 2,198 yards, 19 touchdowns, and 10 interceptions. However, Bayless would say on ESPN's "First Take" that if he had the No. 1 overall pick in the 2027 NFL Draft, he "would be afraid not to take" Manning due to the possibility of him being a "late bloomer."

"After I watched the other night, him come back to beat LSU, despite throwing three interceptions, I concluded that if I had the first overall pick this year and I needed a quarterback, I would be afraid not to take Arch Manning," Bayless said on Friday. "I would be afraid not to close one eye and just bet on him being a late bloomer, bet on his bloodlines, bet on the fact that I think he's got more talent than his grandfather or his two uncles had, at six-four, 225 pounds, nice arm, and can run.

Bayless admits that when it comes to his argument for the Texas football star going No.1, one "can shoot all kinds of holes" in it, as he's 54th in the nation in quarterback rating and only has one game of 300 passing yards more. That performance came in the opening 59-7 win over Texas State.

While Bayless said Manning can be "thin-skinned" and "can be a bit of a spoiled brat," the analyst mentions the many comeback performances the quarterback had this season that live up to his namesake. At any rate, it remains to be seen what Manning's outlook is like for the NFL Draft, as the quarterback prepares for the No. 5 Longhorns' next game against Arkansas on Saturday afternoon.
