Prince Harry and Prince William Pen Rare Letters About Their Mother to the Winners of the Diana Award

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The prize recognizes “young people who work to improve the lives of others”

One area in which Prince Harry and Prince William will always share common ground is in their respect for their late mother, Princess Diana.

Two years after Diana’s untimely passing in 1997, the Diana Award was established by former U.K. Prime Minister Gordon Brown, in an effort to recognize young people who “work to improve the lives of others.” Now, the 2026 recipients have received a special treat in the form of personalized letters from Harry and William, who expressed their congratulations, while recognizing that the winners are helping keep Diana’s legacy alive.

“You should be immensely proud of what you have achieved and the difference you are making in your community and beyond,” Harry wrote in his message. “This award is deeply personal to me, not only because it carries my mother’s name, but because it champions her belief that young people have the power to change the world. You are putting that belief into action and inspiring others in the process.”

Meanwhile, William noted: “The world we live in today can feel challenging and uncertain, and creating change is not easy. Yet, in the face of these challenges, you have shown extraordinary resilience, courage, and a belief in the power of kindness to make things better.”

The Prince of Wales also added: “My mother, in whose memory you receive the Diana Award today, would be so proud of your achievements and the deep care you show for others. Thank you for your compassionate leadership, which is helping to create a more hopeful and positive world.”

Over the past few years, the royal brothers have continued their tradition of honoring Diana Award recipients. Back in 2023, the Duke of Sussex even made an in-person visit to see some of the honorees at a Conversations for Change seminar in Los Angeles.

However, most of their efforts have been done separately, as the two continue to deal with strains in their relationship.

One of the last times the princes were seen together was back in 2021, when they attended the unveiling of a statue dedicated to their mother at the Sunken Garden in Kensington Palace. The ceremony was held on what would have been Diana’s 60th birthday.
