Clearwave Joins the MEDITECH Alliance as an Accelerator Partner

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Hospitals and health systems on MEDITECH Expanse can deploy Clearwave's AI-powered check-in, including kiosks, pre-registration, eligibility verification, payments and phone handling - with data written back into Expanse.

ATLANTA, Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Clearwave, the AI Patient Engagement Platform, today announced it has joined the MEDITECH Alliance as an Accelerator Partner, formalizing a long-standing integration with MEDITECH. For MEDITECH hospitals, the partnership means a vetted path to capturing more revenue before care, lighter front desk workloads and faster, simpler patient experiences, backed by Clearwave's continued investment in MEDITECH Expanse.

The MEDITECH Alliance is MEDITECH's partner program for organizations with proven, successful and interoperable solutions and services. As an Accelerator Partner, Clearwave amplifies MEDITECH's capabilities with an agentic workforce that automates the healthcare industry's most manual work â from phone calls to health insurance checks, patient check-in and collections.

How MEDITECH Hospitals Use Clearwave for Pre-Registration and Check-In

Busy organizations already running Clearwave and MEDITECH together show the impact of this integration in action.

Holyoke Medical Center, an independent community hospital serving 17,000 patients a month, moved patient check-in, eligibility and communications onto Clearwave, with patient-entered data flowing directly into MEDITECH Expanse. The organization now sees 94% patient adoption of self-registration, average check-in times of about two minutes and a 56% co-pay collection rate, up from next to zero previously.

"A big value-driver is the data collection. It's a strength Clearwave has that's different than what other vendors can do with MEDITECH. The state is very pleased with what we're able to produce, it's better, more accurate data, flowing where we need it and it's helped us get that much closer to being paperless," said Chris Giroux, Director of IT Application Services at Holyoke Medical Center.

What Clearwave Automates for MEDITECH Organizations

Clearwave replaces patient engagement vendor sprawl with one AI-powered platform. Its AI agents work alongside staff across the patient journey, and every agent writes back to MEDITECH Expanse to reduce manual data entry and reconciliation.

"Hospitals can't hire their way out of rising patient volume, and they shouldn't have to. Our agents take on the high-volume, repetitive work across the patient journey, so MEDITECH organizations capture more revenue before care and grow access without growing administrative headcount. Joining the Alliance gives every Expanse customer a proven path to that model," said Mike Coffey, Chief Executive Officer at Clearwave.

"We are excited to welcome Clearwave as an Accelerator in the MEDITECH Alliance," said Brian Schuller, Director of Business Development at MEDITECH. "Collaborating through the Alliance will certainly drive enhanced operational efficiency and deliver better results for our customers."
