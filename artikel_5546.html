2026 AI Innovation Awards Program Announced: Recognizing AI Technologies, Products, and Agents Transforming Business

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

CHICAGO, Nov. 5, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The AI Leaders Council announces the launch of the AI Innovationâ¢ Awards, a recognition program honoring companies with proven artificial intelligence technologies, products, platforms, and solutions that are transforming business and industry.

The AI Innovation Awards recognizes outstanding technologies and products across major business functions and applications including operations, information technology, finance, cybersecurity, sales and marketing, human resources, and other areas. The program also recognizes the AI Agent of the Year, and the Emerging AI Innovation of the Year. Of these winners, judges will select the programs highest honor, the AI Innovation of the Year.

Nominations are now open and there is no fee for participating in the AI Innovation Awards. Participating companies are invited to complete an application form including a brief product description, key capabilities, demonstrated business results, and a short video demonstration of the technology. Nominations will close December 31, 2026, judging complete by January 15, and winners announced by January 29, 2027.

"Artificial intelligence is advancing at an extraordinary pace, with innovative new technologies and solutions emerging across virtually every business function," states Neil Brown, Executive Director and Board Chair of the AI Leaders Council. "The AI Innovation Awards program will recognize the companies and technologies leading this transformation, while helping business and technology executives discover innovative AI solutions that deliver meaningful results."

The awards program is open to AI technology providers of all sizes, from emerging startups to established enterprise software providers. Submissions will be evaluated based on innovation, functionality, practical applications, and demonstrated business value. Companies interested in participating can learn more about award categories, eligibility, and submission requirements at: https://aileaderscouncil.org/ai-innovation-awards/.
