Chivu ’embarrassed’ by comparisons to Inter ‘legend’ Mourinho

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Cristian Chivu pushed back the comparisons between himself and Jose Mourinho, saying he is "embarrassed" to be put in a conversation with the former Inter boss.

Chivu's comments came after he was awarded the Panchina d'Oro on Monday, an accolade presented to the best Serie A coach of the year.

He led Inter to the Scudetto in 2025-26 along with the Coppa Italia, the first time they had achieved the feat since 2009-10 when Mourinho was at the helm.

Inter also won the Champions League that season, with Mourinho even comparing his treble-winning side, one that Chivu was a part of, to the current Nerazzurri team.

However, Chivu dismissed that comparison and further distanced himself from being likened to Mourinho, having become only the third non-Italian to win the Serie A Coach of the Year award, and the first since the now-Real Madrid boss 16 years ago.

"Being compared to Mourinho is too much, you're making me embarrassed! He's a legend and I have only just begun," Chivu said.

"This is only my second year in the professional ranks. If I could achieve even a fraction of what he has won, I would be a very happy man, even happier than I am."

Inter have made a strong start to their Serie A title defence, sitting second and just one point behind Lazio, with Roma and rivals AC Milan also in the mix.

Chivu's side signed off for the final international break of 2026 with a 2-0 win over Como, bouncing back from their defeat to Milan in the Derby della Madonnina.

"I'm not a fan of labels like that. It's far too easy to talk about Inter as the clear favourite that 'must' win every game," Chivu said when asked about Inter's title chances.

"We simply need to continue what we have been doing over the last few years. Working hard, delivering results and maintaining consistency.

"It's a long road, the margin for error is razor-thin. We all know how it goes. A single defeat can feel like a tragedy, but a week with two wins can spark immediate euphoria. We need balance from everyone."

Inter return to league action on Sunday against Atalanta at New Balance Arena.

"I expect us to pick up where we left off. Keeping the right balance, securing results and keeping clean sheets," Chivu added.

"We also want to show a certain level of aggression and quality on the pitch, making the right decisions in the right situations.

"I expect to see a team that will do everything in its power to get through the demanding run of fixtures between this weekend and December 22."
