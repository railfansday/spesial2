No One Hurt At Xbox Studio InXile Entertainment After A Gunman Allegedly Opened Fire On The Building

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

InXile Entertainment is the Xbox studio working on the console exclusive Clockwork Revolution, set to release in 2027. The office building that houses the team in Tustin, California was reportedly struck by bullets in an alleged drive-by incident earlier this week. Fortunately, no one was injured.

Local news reported a shooting near the office on November 11 around 1:10 p.m. in which “several bullets struck at least one building in the area” and five to eight shell casings were left behind. Kotaku understands that the bullets struck the bottom floor of the building InXile is housed in, and police at one point were exploring the possibility of whether the game studio was being targeted.
