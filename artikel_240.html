US releases files on suspected Bigfoot sightings in Montana and Oregon

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Forest Service releases files months after residents in rural Ontario reported encounters with massive figures

Well, not Sasquatch directly, but new files about the cryptid.

In a public archive, the US Forest Service recently added reports concerning suspected encounters with Bigfoot – or its remnants in Montana and Oregon.

A newly released 1982 report showed how a wildlife biologist investigated peculiar tracks in north-east Oregon.

The additional documents also included a 2003 Montana newspaper story about a reported sighting of Bigfoot tracks and a testimonial about the history of a creek area in Montana where people spotted what they thought was “a very large bear, except bears don’t walk on their hind legs only for short distances”.

The recent release of the files by the US Forest Service comes months after residents in rural Ontario, Canada, reported encounters with massive figures that matched the description of Bigfoot.

Collectively, they add to a long history of suspected sightings of Bigfoot or markings from the bipedal ape whose existence has not been confirmed by science. The Bigfoot Mapping Project contains more than 16,000 reports on the cryptid, a term referring to an animal or creature whose existence has not been proven.

Carleton University philosophy professor Josh Redstone previously told the Guardian that Bigfoot is a prime example of how fascinating the unverified can be for humans.

“For people who believe in Sasquatch, there’s an excitement around the possibility of discovering something new,” Redstone said.

In the 1982 report, the biologist, Rodney Johnson, investigated tracks at three locations in the Umatilla National Forest in north-east Oregon. To determine their origin, Johnson – or a fellow investigator – made plaster casts of two of the tracks.

Johnson gave eight reasons why he did not think the tracks belonged to Sasquatch, including:

“The stride of the tracks did not change with slope.”

“The tracks at both sites were not to the ‘bottom’ of the mud.

“The small toes lack a definite ‘pad’ at the tips as would be expected.”

Still, Johnson did not entirely dismiss the Bigfoot theory.

“If the tracks were faked, someone went to a great deal of trouble and detail,” Johnson wrote. “The ‘print’ marks on the foot would have to be very hard to duplicate. They appear very natural. I would almost suspect a cast made of an actual foot would have had to be used to make the ‘fake’ feet. If this were the case though, the other creases in the foot should have shown.”

In a subsequent report from the forest service, the investigator stated that the tracks “were a very clever attempt to perpetrate a hoax”.

“Trusting that this memo will suffice to close this ‘Bigfoot’ case,” the report stated.

That case might have been closed – but there were others.

In the 1980 testimonial, which reads like a novel, Leo Kinney in Montana wrote: “Now to tell some of the things that made history.”

He and three others entered the woods to retrieve an elk carcass, he wrote. But he said they instead spotted “another type creature that walked on its hind legs that altho [sic] there wasn’t actual sightings, tracks, a strong odor and a gruntling sound were observed”.

After determining that the creature had supposedly picked up the elk and dragged it, “we held a conference and decided that we were not particularly anxious to catch up to whatever it was and tho [sic] we didn’t confess it at this time, in later conversations we found out that we all had a very uneasy feeling and could feel chills running up and down our backs.”

The journalist who produced the 2003 Clark Fork Valley Press story wrote that a group in Montana found hundreds of “strange footprints”.

“I didn’t believe it until I saw the tracks,” one woman said.

Residents in rural Ontario in June reported seeing two creatures scavenging through garbage.

“One big, one not so big,” an account stated. “The cinnamon was prominent on the smaller one.”

All who believe they have spotted a trace of Bigfoot submit a report to the Bigfoot Mapping Project.
