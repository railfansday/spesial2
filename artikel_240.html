Save $100 on the Apple Watch Series 12 for Black Friday 2026, a record-low price

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

SAVE $100: As of Nov. 27, the new Apple Watch Series 12 is on sale for just $299 during Amazon and Walmart's Black Friday sales. That's 25% off its $399 list price, a record low. Last year's model only got a $60 discount for the holidays.

The new Apple Watch Series 12 is an impressive smartwatch. Take it from me: I just spent the past few weeks testing it for Mashable. It's only fair that it's getting an equally impressive Black Friday discount.

Released just last month, the Series 12's centerpiece is a revamped Health Sensing System that can take heart rate readings in near-real time. Whereas last year's Series 11 captured heart rate data every five minutes, and only continuously during workouts, the Series 12 checks it every five seconds all the time. To quote my review, "These changes make the Series 12's heart rate data much more precise and detailed than before, and more useful for non-athletes."

As of Nov. 27, the day before Black Friday, Amazon and Walmart have the Series 12 on sale for only $299. That's $100 off its usual sticker price of $399, and a 25% savings — its biggest discount yet. Frankly, I'm surprised it's so cheap: On Black Friday 2025, the Series 11 was only $60 off.

While other Apple wearables are also on sale right now, this is easily the best Black Friday Apple Watch deal I've spotted so far. Note that Amazon's $299 deal only applies to the Series 12 variant with a light gold aluminum case and a sand sport band. As of this writing, it's up for grabs at Walmart in that colorway, plus dark bronze/olive and space gray/navy combinations.

The Series 12 can produce a new "Readiness" recovery score that adjusts throughout the day based on your activity (or lack thereof). It also brings support for a step count widget, or "complication," which Apple Watch users had to jerry-rig on older models.

The Series 12 charges faster than the Series 11 and lasts a little longer, too. In my testing, its battery reached the two-day mark without Low Power Mode enabled.

Apple has equipped the Series 12 with some new AI-powered Audio Intelligence features, including a note-taking tool called Siri Recap, but I'm less enthused about them. Buy it for the health tracking improvements above all else.
