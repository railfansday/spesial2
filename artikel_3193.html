ProMIS Neurosciences, Inc. (PMN -5.75%) reported progress on its lead Alzheimer’s candidate, PMN310, as the company nears the completion of its Phase 1b PRECISE AD clinical trial. Management stated that patient visits are expected to conclude in the fourth quarter of 2026, with unblinded 12-month topline results scheduled for the first quarter of 2027. The company's financial position was strengthened by a $70.1 million private placement earlier in the year, providing a cash runway through 2027. ProMIS remains focused on demonstrating the safety and efficacy advantages of its oligomer-selective approach compared to currently approved therapies that show higher rates of brain swelling and require intensive monitoring. The clinical strategy includes the parallel development of a subcutaneous formulation to improve patient compliance and reduce the need for recurring imaging surveillance.
