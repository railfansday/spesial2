I was at the Bogleheads 2026 Conference but couldn't find you and your spouse. I so much wanted to meet you both.

The only other person I met who posts consistently other than Boglehead celebrities was bsteiner.
I thought going into it that maybe we would run into you, but that was a rather large crowd between the 700 in attendance, plus all of those staying at the resort, visiting the casino, and in the restaurants. All we had to go on was your mention of your hairstyle, but there were at least a couple hundred, if not more, in attendance sporting the same style.

The first cocktail hour the lines were so long, the portion of the line we were in ended up in a smaller side room where the staff brought in a third bartender and the 20 or so of us stood around away from the foyer where everyone else was. The second cocktail hour after the induction on Saturday, again had long lines. So we just decided to skip it so we could go up to our room and freshen up as we were going out to eat with a group. We did miss not having the sit down dinner for the induction that was held at the Chicago Conference in 2022. It was a good way to socialize for several hours and took care of having to get dinner on your own for at least one of the nights.

We ran into a few regulars. At least from my standpoint, the username font appeared too small on the name tags for my eyesight, so I really wasn't able to see it. The one name tag I did want to read leads a local chapter in Florida, and was sitting at one of our lunch tables contributing to some nice conversation, but he had either covered his username with white out or some white tape, or scratched it off. We enjoyed meeting a lot of new folks this time around from places like Ohio, South Carolina, Florida, Texas, Norway, Minnesota, Nevada, New York, California, Arizona. Iowa, and Illinois.

Sorry to hear of everyone's flight woes.

We are not home yet as we made a full trip out of attending the conference, so we drove across prime outdoor recreation states of Colorado, Utah, Arizona, and Nevada with my mountain bike on the back rack. We did 7 days of hiking in all the National Parks. Today's mountain bike ride outside of Moab in Dead Horse Point State Park was the final ride of the trip, so another day or two and we should make it home.
