Aston Villa 'certain' to agree John McGinn deal - ex-Villa employee

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Aston Villa will likely soon be looking to extend John McGinn's contract by another year.

That is according to former senior Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 32-year-old signed a new deal last year, extending his terms until the end of the 2027-28 campaign.

But with McGinn set to have just one year remaining on his contract next summer and seemingly still at the height of his powers, Villa will be keen to secure his future.

The Scotland international scored twice against North Macedonia last week, and has provided six direct goal contributions already in the Premier League this season so far.

The Villa captain set his career-best tally when he netted 10 goals and provided seven assists across all competitions last season, but he already looks to be well on his way to beating both of those records.

Super John McGinn. pic.twitter.com/K91RjzjZhq

Everton attempted to sign McGinn in the summer of 2025, and saw an £18million bid rejected for the Villa captain.

He would subsequently sign a new deal, and the Villans cannot rule out the chance that interest could re-appear as his contract ticks down again.

"Villa will certainly be looking to offer him a year extension," King told Football Insider. "But they've got next season anyway. They've got plenty of time to think about it.

"He'll be on a good contract. He's done marvellously for the club over the last two or three years. He's looked up there with [Morgan] Rogers and [Ollie] Watkins and these players.

"He's captain of the football club, which is a prestigious position at any football club to be captain of the team."

In an incredibly busy summer transfer window, Villa lost six key players who started the Europa League final.

Rogers, Watkins, Youri Tielemans, Lucas Digne, Emi Martinez and Ezri Konsa all left and have been difficult to replace, with the latter name likely proving the biggest loss so far.

But replacing McGinn when he eventually decides to move on may be the most difficult task of all for Unai Emery.

While the Scot's qualities can ultimately be sought elsewhere, his presence and personality simply cannot.
