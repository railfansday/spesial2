Ex-scout shares instant reaction to West Ham appointing Scott Parker

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Scott Parker is a sensible appointment for West Ham United after they sacked Nuno Espirito Santo on Tuesday.

That is the opinion of experienced former Tottenham, Everton and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

Parker has reportedly already agreed to return to the Hammers, where he four years during his playing days.

In a statement on Tuesday, West Ham announced that Nuno has been relieved of his duties after a poor start to the Championship season.

The Irons were beaten 3-1 by West Brom on Saturday to mark a second consecutive league defeat at the London Stadium, leaving them eighth after 13 games played.

It is hoped that Parker will already be in place for Wednesday's trip to Cardiff City, where West Ham will need to bounce back.

Despite getting relegated, West Ham managed to keep hold of many of their key players.

It is suspected that the likes of Jarrod Bowen are still earning sizable wages, with the hopes set on an immediate return to the Premier League.

But it will clearly not be a given, and in Parker, West Ham have hired a promotion specialist.

The Englishman has guided Fulham, Bournemouth and Burnley to the top flight, although he has a history of failing once he reaches the Premier League.

"Bowen, he's playing in the Championship on Premier League wages, as are quite a few of them. I honestly thought the West Ham would come down and go straight back up," King told Football Insider.

"On paper, they look as though they've got the strongest side anyway. Who takes over? Are they looking for an Englishman?

"West Ham can't afford to be in the championship for another year. Parker would be very sensible. That's a good shout. He's out of work. He did a good job at Fulham.

"He did a good job at Burnley. He played for West Ham. Alan Curbishley hasn't been working for many years. I don't know whether he'd fancy going back, but you know, they're the kind of names that could certainly lift the fans in the football club."

Though he ultimately left for fierce rivals Tottenham, Parker has always retained love at the London Stadium.

The midfielder is a three-time Hammer of the Year, and will be welcomed back with open arms.

"I’ve got amazing memories. It’s a fantastic football club where I had four unbelievable seasons," Parker said in 2020.

"It ended in relegation but that disappointment will not dampen the memories. The fanbase is incredible, Upton Park – where I was playing – was a special place, so I’ve got many fond memories.”
