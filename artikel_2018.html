The 4 players set to miss Southampton vs Middlesbrough ft £4.3m ace

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Spygate derby is upon us, as Southampton welcome Middlesbrough in what is set to be a fiery affair under the lights at St Mary's on Friday night.

It's arguably the one game fans circled on fixture release day, and the one that everyone has been looking forward to ever since the final whistle blew in the play-off final at Wembley, when it was confirmed that both Southampton and Boro would be spending the season in the Championship.

Back in May, the two sides were gearing up for a play-off semi-final clash, but days before, Middlesbrough caught a member of the Saints staff spying on their training session. The semi-finals went ahead, and Southampton won 2-1 after extra time.

However, it would be Boro who would play in the play-off final, after Southampton were expelled due to their breaches of EFL regulations, and two other incidents of spying brought about a four-point deduction to begin this season.

Both Kim Hellberg and Tonda Eckert have since buried the hatchet, but it's brewed a new rivalry in the Championship, and both sets of fans won't want to lose this one. These four players won't be available to play their part, though.

Southampton go into Friday night nearly as healthy as they could be. Both Leo Scienza and Casper Jander, who have missed the two games after the international break, could return, which will give them a big boost both in the middle and going forward.

One player who is confirmed to be out, though, is Keven Schlotterbeck. The German centre-back has missed the last two games after dislocating his shoulder 11 minutes into their defeat at Preston North End in the final outing before the international break.

The 29-year-old will be targetting a return nearer the end of the calendar year, so will remain out for this one.

Rokas Pukstas has had a terrible start to life at Middlesbrough after signing from Croatian side Hadjuk Split late on in the summer transfer window in a £4.3 million deal.

The 21-year-old played just 12 minutes in all competitions for Boro in his first month at the club before going down with a hamstring injury over the three-week international break while doing some private training.
