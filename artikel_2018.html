Blackburn Rovers can deal bitter transfer blow to Burnley for in-demand Huddersfield Town star

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Blackburn Rovers have defied many people's expectations in the early stages of this campaign to be within touching distance of the Championship play-off places, but as the January window approaches, the club must brace itself for a plethora of bids for one player in particular.

Under Tony Mowbray, despite a lack of funding in the transfer market, Blackburn have emerged as an outside shout of breaking into the top eight this campaign.

Mowbray, 62, has had to take a step back from management for health reasons in recent weeks, leaving first-team duties to assistant managers Damien Johnson and Mike Dodds.

The pair will be hoping to continue Mowbray's good work at Ewood Park in the 62-year-old's absence, and as the January window approaches, must be braced for interest in one of their star assets.

Andri Gudjohnsen has been by far and away Blackburn's star man this season, firing in 11 goals and one assist in 15 Championship games thus far.

According to FotMob, from 24 shots, the Icelandic international has accumulated an expected goal return of just 4.35, displaying an incredibly clinical nature in front of goal at Ewood Park.

Trailing only Middlesbrough's Will Lankshear in the golden boot race as things stand, Gudjohnsen's displays in the Championship have no doubt seen him drawn up on the shortlist of numerous clubs across Europe, as bids will almost certainly test their resolve this winter.

If the 24-year-old is to leave in January, naturally, Blackburn will need a replacement, as the former Premier League champions should be drawing up their own shortlist of targets just in case.

Blackburn must be prepared in the event of Gudjohnsen's departure, and though they will certainly be hoping to keep him around at Ewood Park for the foreseeable future, a bid too good to turn down may well arrive.

In that instance, a list of replacements must be lined up, and on that list should be Huddersfield Town's Joe Taylor.

Taylor, 24, first established himself in the EFL with Peterborough United before thrusting himself into the spotlight in spells with Colchester United and Lincoln City, scoring prolifically in League Two and League One.

Having failed to force his way into the side at Luton Town, the Welshman joined Huddersfield Town in the summer of 2025 for around £3 million, and having initially struggled in West Yorkshire, heading out on loan to Wigan Athletic for six months, he has forced his way back into Martin Drury's plans at the ACCU Stadium.

With 12 goals and three assists in 18 appearances across all competitions this season, Taylor trails only MK Dons' Aaron Collins in the third-tier golden boot race, as interest from the Championship has emerged.

Revealed exclusively by Football League World earlier this week, Championship duo Burnley and Swansea City are interested in Huddersfield's Taylor this January.

Burnley find themselves at the foot of the division after an incredibly poor start to the campaign, as up top, Fabio Celestini has the likes of Andreas Houtondji, Jamie Vardy, Zeki Amdouni, and Armando Broja to pick from.

However, none of those has been able to nail down a place in the side or fire in the goals required to drag the Clarets up the league, with the club now turning their attention to one of League One's best.

Swansea can boast each of Zan Vipotnik, Adam Idah, and Ross Stewart as their centre-forward options, but despite this, they are seemingly interested in adding Taylor to the fold.

Under contract at Huddersfield until 2029, Taylor won't come cheap, but if Gudjohnsen does go, Blackburn will have the money to spend and must beat Burnley to his signature.
