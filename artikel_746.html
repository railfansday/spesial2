Wondermind Investor Lawsuit Against Selena Gomez and Co-Founders Dismissed

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A federal lawsuit against Selena Gomez and fellow Wondermind co-founders Mandy Teefey and Daniella Pierson has been dismissed, the star’s litigator Mathew S. Rosengart confirmed Monday.

“Selena is pleased by the dismissal of the case in its entirety and looks forward to moving forward,” Rosengart said in a statement to Variety. The plaintiffs “voluntarily” dismissed the suit “in its entirety, with prejudice,” according to documents. In the lawsuit, filed in August by two companies, Wondermind SRS 44 LLC and Bespoke Wondermind SPV I LLC, Gomez, Teefy (Gomez’s mother) and Pierson were accused of defrauding investors in the mental health startup, Wondermind. Gomez, Teefey and Pierson founded Wondermind in 2021 to make mental health resources more accessible.

The lawsuit claimed the Wondermind team falsely represented the company’s trajectory and leadership structure, with investors alleging Gomez and her mother solicited about $1.2 million from them, among other claims. It also alleged that the company collapsed around them, while “not one of its founders, officers, or directors said a word to the investors,” according to The New York Times. Investors claimed they were made aware of the company’s “utter financial and operational disarray” from a story published by New York Magazine’s The Cut in 2025.

“The allegations that Selena Gomez engaged in any way whatsoever in any purported ‘fraud’ or other wrongdoing are completely meritless, both factually and legally,” Rosengart told People at the time. “We will vigorously defend these false allegations and indeed are filing a motion to dismiss the baseless claims against her.”
