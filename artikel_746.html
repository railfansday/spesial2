Netflix Launches First AI-Branded Content Recommendations Feature on TVs

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Initial test of feature will be available on select smart televisions in the U.S.

Netflix, which has seen viewer engagement slide in recent quarters, is turning to AI for a new feature aimed at spurring users to find something new to watch.

The streamer will begin rolling out AI Discover in the U.S. to customers who use Netflix in English on supported smart TVs. If you have access to it, you will see a “Discover” tab with a red gem-shaped icon and a “New” label in the top navigation bar on your TV. The company plans to expand the feature to more regions and customers in the coming weeks.

The AI content recommendation feature is interactive, but it’s not a chatbot-like experience. AI Discover will not be available on kids’ profiles.

Netflix is positioning the AI-powered recommendation feature as a work in progress — and it says it wants customers to provide input about how useful they find it. “Think of AI Discover as our test kitchen: we’re trying out new recipes for discovery, and your use and feedback help us decide which ones to keep, improve or serve up next,” Netflix says in a blog post about the new feature. “Some may even make their way into the main Netflix experience.”

AI Discover is powered by a “new intelligence layer we’ve been building that responds to what members tell us in real time and keeps learning,” according to Netflix.

“What we’re most excited about is how it feels to use,” the company says. “Tell us what you’re after, and the experience adjusts. Tell us a recommendation isn’t for you, and we’ll remember it in AI Discover, so it won’t keep turning up.”

Netflix has also adopted AI in other areas of its business. About 300 titles across the streamer’s library have used generative AI across their production process, the company announced in July. This spring, Netflix paid $587 million to buy Ben Affleck-founded company InterPositive, which it says gives filmmakers AI tools to use throughout film and TV productions.
