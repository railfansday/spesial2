The IKEA shopping experience is about to look very different

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

IKEA stores are known for being so massive they make furniture shopping feel like a bit of a journey. Albeit, one with meatballs. But the brand is expanding on a whole new, and noticeably sleeker, shopping experience for its customers.

Ingka, the largest IKEA retail operator in the world, says that while the company’s “big blue box” stores aren’t going anywhere, smaller stores, which debuted in Los Angeles in 2025, will be popping up in more cities. The new locations will include smaller versions of IKEA stores, as well as more plan and order points, where customers seeking design and planning advice can go. 

“The big blue box will remain the backbone,” Ingka CEO Juvencio Maeztu, who came on board last year, said per Reuters. The CEO added, “We are adding smaller formats for convenience, either because you don’t have an ​IKEA in your city, or you have one but you want a bit more convenience on a ​weekly and monthly basis.” 

The announcement comes amid struggling sales for the brand. On Nov. 6, IKEA reported its third straight year of dwindling sales, which were down about 2% across 64 countries compared to last year as consumers are budgeting out larger purchases and non-essential items more often. 

“We are determined to make IKEA even more affordable and accessible for the many people. Our business model is built on a simple idea: high volumes and low costs enable us to offer low prices,” Jakub Jankowski, CEO of Inter IKEA, which owns ​the IKEA brand, said in the report.

Jankowski added, “Sales have remained stable in recent years, despite challenging market conditions. But to reach more people with affordable home furnishing, we need to return to growth. In FY26, we took important steps to strengthen the business, create the conditions for growth and enable us to lower prices even further.”

In a previous announcement, IKEA was already laying out plans for how to accomplish its goals. The brand explained that it has been amplifying efforts to meet the needs of the modern furniture shopper, as big blue box shopping has dwindled. It said it would add nine small format stores, which carry IKEA’s full range of products. It also said it would add 10 Plan and order points with pick-up which give customers a more personalized experience, and 10 new meeting points inside Best Buy locations.

In September, the brand also announced it would invest $1.39 billion (€1.2 billion) in price cuts for European customers amid declining sales. 

The iconic brand has already opened a number of new US stores this year, including locations in Chantilly, VA; Huntsville, AL; Phoenix, AZ; Newnan, GA; Escondido/San Diego, CA; and Tulsa, OK. It opened its final big blue box store for the year in Sarasota, FL on Nov. 18.

IKEA also recently opened three new small-format stores in Texas (Dallas/University Park, Rockwall, and Webster). It plans to open at least six new smaller-format stores next year. The planned locations include: Fort Collins, CO; Gurnee, IL; Naperville, IL; Buffalo, Westlake, OH; New York City; and Madison, WI.
