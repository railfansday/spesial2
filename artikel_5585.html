Data Quality Software Champions Recognized in Infotech's 2026 Emotional Footprint Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Drawing on verified end-user reviews from SoftwareReviews, Infotech's peer-review platform, the 2026 Data Quality Emotional Footprint Report recognizes Champions for customer experience and vendor relationships. The findings help organizations assess how providers support their customers alongside the capabilities needed to maintain accurate, consistent, and reliable data.

ARLINGTON, Va., Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Infotech has named four Champions in its 2026 Data Quality Emotional Footprint Report, recognizing four solutions that stand out for user satisfaction, trust, and overall customer experience based on verified feedback collected through the firm's SoftwareReviews platform.

Inaccurate, incomplete, and inconsistent data can undermine business decisions and create costly inefficiencies. Data quality solutions help organizations maintain accurate, complete, and consistent data across applications, databases, and cloud environments. Capabilities such as data profiling, cleansing, validation, standardization, duplicate detection, and ongoing quality monitoring also help IT and data teams identify and resolve errors, establish quality standards, and support governance requirements. These capabilities provide a more reliable data foundation for analytics, AI initiatives, and everyday business operations.

"Data quality is an ongoing business discipline, essential to trusted analytics, reliable AI outcomes, and confident decision-making," says Varun Palengetira, principal advisory director at Infotech. "Organizations should look for solutions that measure data reliability, connect data quality issues to business impact, and enable coordinated action across business and IT teams. It should enable teams to detect issues early, establish repeatable practices, prioritize remediation, and demonstrate measurable quality improvements. The greatest value comes from ensuring that the data supporting operations, analytics, automation, and AI is reliable enough to deliver the intended business outcomes."

Infotech's Emotional Footprint measures high-level user sentiment by aggregating responses across 25 proactive questions, creating a powerful indicator of overall user feeling toward the vendor and product. The result is the Net Emotional Footprint (NEF), a composite score that reflects the overall emotional tone of user feedback.
