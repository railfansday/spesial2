BHHS Fox & Roach to Host RISE Event Featuring Renowned Industry Speakers

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Group of business people silhouettes listening speech at the conference hall, blurred male speaker wearing suit, sky background

Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices Fox & Roach, REALTORS® has announced its RISE event will take place on Nov. 4 at Live! Casino & Hotel in Philadelphia.

A release notes that the full-day, educational and inspirational event will gather upwards of 1,000 agents and employees for presentations by nationally renowned real estate industry leaders. The list of leaders includes acclaimed real estate coach Brian Buffini, CEO and founder of Buffini & Company; Chris Kelly, CEO of HomeServices of America; and Jimmy Burgess, HomeServices of America’s chief coaching officer and president of Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices Beach Properties of Florida.

The event will also feature a sneak peek at Maestro, Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices Fox & Roach’s new all-in-one technology platform agents will get access to in December. Combining client relationship management, marketing, contract management and financial analysis tools, Maestro is designed to streamline agents’ workflow, enhance efficiency and boost productivity, the release states.

The company says RISE comes at a pivotal time for Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices Fox & Roach as the organization continues to grow and invest in innovative resources for agent support. In 2026, the brokerage onboarded more than 300 new and experienced agents, opened a new office, planned its upcoming move to new Main Line Headquarters and prepared to launch the Maestro platform for its nearly 4,000 agents.

“RISE is about bringing our people together and celebrating the momentum we’re building as an organization,” says Larry Flick, CEO, Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices Fox & Roach. “As we continue to grow and innovate by introducing new resources like Maestro, it’s important that we create opportunities for agents to learn from industry leaders, strengthen connections, and share ideas. We want everyone who attends RISE to leave feeling inspired, energized, and equipped with new perspectives to take their business and careers to the next level.”

For more information visit www.foxroach.com.
