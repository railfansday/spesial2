Frankie Muniz Says He's 'Grateful to Be Walking' After Race Car Crash

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Frankie Muniz shared an update on his health after he was involved in a race car crash and hospitalized.

The Malcolm in the Middle alum, 40, was injured during a practice lap prior to the Proud to Honor Veterans Day Classic race at the Daytona International Speedway in Florida on Wednesday, November 11.

“I’m sure a lot of you saw the news that I was involved in a pretty bad wreck yesterday at Daytona International Speedway for Ford’s Proud to Honor event that they were hosting,” Muniz said in an Instagram video on Thursday, November 12.

He continued, “I, first of all, want to say I’m doing OK. I’m extremely sore, but fortunately, no major injuries. I’m not 100 percent sure how that’s the case, but that’s just a testament to how well built the Ford Mustang Dark Horse R cars are.”

Muniz shared more details on the crash, revealing that he had to be cut out of his race car.

“I can honestly say that I’m grateful to be walking right now,” he said in the video. “I’m heading home back to Phoenix. I’m forever grateful for all you reaching out and to the safety team. It was tough getting me out of the car. We had to cut the roof off, but [they] got me out safely, got me to the hospital, and all is well.”

He added, “So, recovering now, and I’ll be back on the track soon. Can’t wait. But thank you to everybody for reaching out and being concerned. I’m feeling good. See you at the racetrack soon.”

“Yesterday at Daytona was one of those moments that puts everything into perspective,” the actor and racing driver captioned Thursday’s upload. “First and foremost, thank you to everyone who’s reached out with messages, calls, and prayers. The support has been overwhelming in the best way, and it means more than I can put into words.”

“Still processing it all, but I’m okay and already looking forward to getting back on the track soon. Love you all,” he wrote.

Ford Racing said Wednesday night that Muniz had been released from the hospital after spending several hours under observation.

“I am disappointed I won’t be able to take part in tonight’s Ford’s Proud to Honor event tonight,” Muniz said in an accompanying statement. “I was looking forward to honoring the service of our veterans and racing against these other amazing drivers.”

“I am a little sore, but my Mustang did its job in protecting me,” he added. “I want to thank everyone at Ford Racing, the Daytona International Speedway and the staff here at the hospital.”
