The Legend Of Zelda: Master Collection Officially Releasing For Series' 40th Anniversary

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

As The Legend of Zelda turns 40, fans are gearing up for even more exciting releases. Of course, Nintendo dropped what many are already calling the Game of the Year with the critically acclaimed Ocarina of Time remake, and an upcoming Zelda remaster aims to further fill the gaps between releases. There has also been a wealth of new merchandise released as part of the ongoing celebrations, much of which has offered fans new and occasionally exciting ways to engage with their favorite franchise.

However, evidently, both for fans and Nintendo, this isn't enough. With concerns around the future of the Zelda franchise becoming ever more prevalent, the studio is aiming to do as much as it can to both distract fans until the next mainline game drops and appease those hungry for more. One way it is doing this is with the upcoming Master Collection, a brand-new product that aims to give collectors yet another way to support the Zelda IP.

The Legend of Zelda: Master Collection is a brand-new product created by Italian company Panini in collaboration with Nintendo. Fans of Panini's trading cards may already be aware of the collaboration, but for those who're just getting started with the world of trading cards, Panini routinely makes various sets of collectible cards for IP, including, of course, Nintendo. The Master Collection brings the expansive roster of Zelda video games to the world of trading cards, offering fans a fun new way to collect art from their favorite titles in the series.

The entire collection spans the series' 40 years of history, covering every single mainline game through assorted character portraits, concept art, locations, and more. 21 different games will be getting dedicated cards within the set, including, but not limited to, Wind Waker, Twilight Princess, Ocarina of Time, Breath of the Wild, and Tears of the Kingdom. You can expect to see familiar faces pop up across these cards in various forms, giving fans the opportunity to have collectibles of all their favorite characters. Nintendo and Panini have yet to confirm a price for the individual booster packs for the Master Collection set, or indeed for any product within the range. However, what we do know is that fans will get six different cards per booster pack they buy. With most Panini sets typically including between 150 and 300 cards, you'll likely need to buy a lot of packs in order to collect them all. Of course, as with all trading cards, there's no guarantee of which ones you'll get per pack.

What we do know is that, along with the aforementioned booster packs (and boxes), fans can also pick up an official binder to store their Legend of Zelda: Master Collection cards in. Additionally, Panini booster packs traditionally cost around $2, so fans can also likely expect the price point of the Master Collection packs to be around that. Nintendo will likely announce more details about the Legend of Zelda: Master Collection closer to its release in 2027.
