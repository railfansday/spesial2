Netflix’s New Psychological Thriller Is Officially Streaming’s Latest Breakout Hit

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Some of the best movies and shows prove controversial. Doctor Who showrunner Russell T. Davies' groundbreaking drama Queer as Folk, Ava DuVernay's four-part Netflix drama When They See Us, and even the now-iconic sketch series Key & Peele have all drawn backlash from certain groups. The latest to be added to this list is Netflix's The Body, a superb blend of Jennifer's Body, Mean Girls, and Yellowjackets that has the whole of social media talking.

But this new Netflix hit has sparked huge backlash from conservative groups. A petition for the show's cancellation has now reached over 100,000 signatures, with the campaign aimed at Netflix’s Chief Content Officer, Peter Rice, accusing the series of "defiling the faith of millions of Christian families." "Netflix has just sent something into Christian homes that no parent would ever invite in... sign today and tell Peter Rice, Netflix's Chief Content Officer, to cancel The Body before it reaches more Christian homes," the petition reads.

But, as a huge hit with audiences, a counter petition has begun online to renew the series for a second season. This petition, made on Change.org, has now reached over 30,000 signatures and is backed by the undeniable evidence that The Body ​​​​​​is Netflix's biggest breakout of the month. At the time of writing, The Body is the most-watched show on Netflix worldwide. The series has reached 5.1 million views in its first five days, with Quinn Shephard's twisting, horror coming-of-age tale the series of choice right now.

Inspired by Stephen King's Carrie, Shephard's "raunchy teen psychodrama with a dash of Catholic horror" isn't just a big hit with audiences; it has earned widespread acclaim from critics too. Currently boasting an 87% critics' score and a near-perfect 96% from audiences on review aggregator Rotten Tomatoes, reviewers have praised the show's unapologetic storytelling and success in subverting expectations. "The Body is like nothing else on streaming right now," wrote Gabrielle Moss in Collider's review, adding that "its riskiness and rawness will appeal to anyone who's sick of formulaic TV thrillers — or anyone who just remembers the glory and horror of being a teenage girl."

The Body is the most-watched show on Netflix worldwide. Make sure to stay tuned to Collider for all the latest streaming stories.
