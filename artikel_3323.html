Apple TV’s Masterpiece Spy Thriller Series With 4 Seasons On The Way Delivers A Masterclass In Tension

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Apple TV is the home of many high-profile shows, including a spy thriller series that defies streaming rules in multiple ways, such as by having four confirmed seasons on the way, and it is a show that delivers a masterclass in tension for a few reasons. Apple TV has become the go-to for those searching for good production values and consistency in the age of streaming.

Apple TV tends to give shows the necessary time to develop, find their footing, and conclude their stories. In addition to that, it is the perfect streaming service for viewers who are looking for engaging, high-stakes, intense series. Multi-season projects that fit that bill include Severance, Silo, For All Mankind, and more, with not all of them being part of Apple TV's excellent sci-fi catalog.

Slow Horses, a spy thriller series, is certainly part of that group, and Apple TV recently confirmed how it might trust the gritty series more than any other project on its library of shows. After all, while Slow Horses season 6 recently concluded, the show is confirmed to return for at least four more seasons, up to season 10, and its delivery of a masterclass in tension is key to that.

Slow Horses has released six seasons so far on Apple TV, all of them consisting of just six episodes. This plays into how the show is so intense, as the spy thriller series is all plot, no filler, yet it packs quite a bit of much-needed character development without impacting the show's pacing. Another factor that ups the tension in this espionage story is the fact that nobody is safe, as both major characters and long-running supporting players have died over the course of Slow Horses' six seasons. Fans are left worrying about the fates of their favorites the whole time.

Another added layer of tension for the series comes from the fact that the agents of Slough House, while capable, are not near-perfect spies like James Bond. These are fallible characters who are known to make mistakes and misjudge situations, having to find ways to overcome the many challenges that arise along the way as they stumble upon major conspiracies. This both makes the show an exciting watch, but also a nerve-wracking espionage thriller. Finally, there is also the fact that Gary Oldman, one of the best actors alive, lights up the screen when Jackson Lamb drops the slacker act and means business.

Slow Horses has another four seasons on the way, but fans of the spy thriller series only have a good clue of what might happen in seasons 7 and 8. After all, the show is based on Mick Herron's series of novels, and there are currently only two more books left to be adapted, Bad Actors and Clown Town. A clear plot point set to be picked up and elevate the tension next season is the reveal of what will happen to Jack Lowden's River Cartwright, who collapsed at the end of season 6 and looked like he was about to die.

Season 7 should feature the team at Slough House hunting a mole inside the British government, a trope known to always add tension related to the character(s) being investigated and even within those leading the investigation. When it comes to season 8, a clash between Jackson Lamb and Diana Taverner should shake things up in a major way, continuing to make the thriller a show you never know exactly what to expect, and that is why Slow Horses delivers a masterclass in tension.
