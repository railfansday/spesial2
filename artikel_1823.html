‘The past is not going to help’ – Pochettino urges Man Utd players to look ahead

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Mauricio Pochettino has called on his Manchester United to start "building our own way" as "the past is not going to help" them.

The former Tottenham, Paris Saint-Germain and Chelsea boss was appointed as the Red Devils' new head coach after the dismissal of Michael Carrick.

Ahead of Pochettino's first match in charge, which is a trip to Anfield on Sunday, United sit in the Premier League relegation zone after 10 games.

United are winless in eight Premier League matches (D5 L3), the joint-longest ongoing run of any side this season, along with Hull City. The Red Devils last had a longer winless league run between November 1989 and February 1990 (11 in a row).

This is the latest into a league season (MD11) that Liverpool and United have faced with one of the sides in the relegation zone since 1972-73, when bottom side United beat league-leaders Liverpool 2-0 at Old Trafford in November.

Pochettino has urged his United players to forget about their poor form as they look to make a winning start under the Argentinian this weekend.

"I don't think too much about the past. It is about building our own way here. Of course, we feel the support from the club and we start to work," said Pochettino.

"The past is not going to help, to think about yesterday. We have been speaking to the players and the most important thing is to think about the next action.

"We feel the support and we are going to work really hard to help the players to perform. That is the priority."

Pochettino will become the first-ever United boss to see his first league game with the club come against rivals Liverpool.

Just one of the Red Devils' last nine managers (including caretakers) has lost his first Premier League game of a spell in charge of the club (W4 D4), with Erik ten Hag losing 1-2 to Brighton in August 2022.

"When we arrive, I think we found a group of players that we propose to do the football that we feel," added Pochettino.

"To be brave and be Manchester United. It is about proposing that idea to be brave and take it to the field.

"We are here not to denounce things. We are here to build something with our players.

"I think it is a race to arrive and be competitive against Liverpool. That is where our focus is 100%."
