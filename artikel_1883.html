'I'll tell you what...' - Ex-scout reveals what makes Newcastle's Lewis Hall special

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Newcastle United left-back Lewis Hall has earned comparisons to Arsenal and England legend Kenny Sansom.

That is according to experienced former Everton, Tottenham and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 22-year-old earned his ninth England cap on Sunday as Thomas Tuchel's side beat world champions Spain 3-2 in Madrid.

Hall now looks to be the first choice for the Three Lions on the left of defence and rightly so, considering the superb start to the season he has enjoyed for Newcastle.

The former Chelsea man attracted interest from Manchester United in the summer but turned it down, signing a new contract until 2031.

It remains to be seen just how long the Magpies can keep hold of him, however, with interest only set to grow as long as he keeps developing.

Sansom is recognised among England's best ever left-backs, winning 86 caps between 1979 and 1988.

He also made 394 appearances for Arsenal, joining in 1980 and winning the FA Cup in 1987.

In fact, the defender would eventually join Newcastle from the Gunners, signing in 1988 but making just 24 appearances before he left in the following year.

"I'll tell you what, at times he reminds me of Kenny Sansom," King told Football Insider. "Kenny went on to win 86 caps for England and was probably one of the best left backs since Ray Wilson from the team that won the World Cup.

"Football can have a bright future. He's signed a new contract and that was a wise move by Newcastle to get him tied up because he's going to cost a lot of money, that kid.

"The question will be, is he going to be loyal to Newcastle? But on the other end, are they going to win anything?"

On the surface, Hall has never given any hint away of wanting to leave Newcastle anytime soon.

The left-back signed his new deal in September and spoke of his confidence in the project at St James' Park.

"We've got ambitions and as long as we achieve these ambitions and achieve what we want to achieve, I'm sure I'll be here for a long time," he said.

"But I'm happy to do it as long as it benefits the team. He's been a positive for everyone who's come in so far," Hall added, when asked about Matthias Jaissle.
