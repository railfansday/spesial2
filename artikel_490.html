US Strikes Iranian Missile Site after Bahrain Attacks

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Bloomberg’s Jon Herskovitz discusses the latest exchange of strikes between the US and Iran after American warplanes hit an Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps missile launch site in Iran. Iran previously targeted an air base in Bahrain in the first direct strike on the Gulf state in weeks, even as it reiterated that a path to ending the conflict with the US remains open. (Source: Bloomberg)
