Nations League Liveblog: Belgium vs. Italy

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Join us for all the build-up and action as it happens from the final Nations League group game, as Italy visit Belgium for quarter-final qualification and potentially top spot.

It kicks off at the King Baudouin Stadium in Brussels at 19.45 GMT (20.45 CET).

The Azzurri go into this last game as leaders on 10 points, the same as France but with a superior head-to-head record, while Belgium follow on nine.

That means a draw would suffice for Italy to reach the quarter-finals, but a victory here is required to win the group overall.

It is a remarkable situation considering Roberto Mancini opened his tenure with a 2-0 home defeat to Belgium only seven weeks ago.

“The lads are accustomed to a certain style of game, they need to not think about getting two results out of three, but only on the one,” Maccarone told RAI Sport.

“Pio Esposito is very humble and eager to learn, he always asks for notes on how his games went. I try to give advice, he accepts it eagerly, and when you see a player act like that, it is a sign of great intelligence.”

Dimarco and Vergara were tipped to start in the trident attack, instead Koleosho is promoted from the Under-21 side, but Pisilli wasn’t even on the bench against France.

Pisilli is more versatile, he can unite the midfield with the attack.

Arguably, Dimarco was considered too defensive an idea, and Mancini wants Italy to go for the win.

Italy bring Luca Koleosho and Niccolò Pisilli into a trident with Pio Esposito, as they battle for the Nations League quarter-final spot against Kevin De Bruyne and Charles De Ketelaere’s Belgium.

Belgium: Lammens; Castagne, Ngoy, Mechele, De Cuyper; Lavia, Tielemans, De Bruyne; Moreira, De Ketelaere, Doku

Belgium bench: Vandervoordt, Penders, Sabbe, Theate, Lukaku, Trossard, Lukebakio, Cuypers, Seys, Vanaken, Godts, Raskin

Italy: Donnarumma, Kayode, Scalvini, Bastoni, Calafiori; Barella, Tonali, Fagioli; Pisilli, Pio Esposito, Koleosho

Italy bench: Carnesecchi, Vicario, Ahanor, Cisse, Dimarco, Palestra, Doumbia, Romano, Liberali, Scamacca, Vergara, Ghilardi

Roberto Mancini gave a brief interview to Rai ahead of Italy’s Nations League clash against Belgium on Sunday, paying his respects to the victims of the Heysel disaster and explaining why he decided to leave Sebastiano Esposito out of his matchday squad.

Italy have a strong record on Belgian soil, winning six of their nine visits, including a 1-0 in the Nations League in November 2024.

They are unbeaten here since a 3-1 friendly loss in November 2015.
