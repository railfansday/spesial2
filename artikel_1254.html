Van Bommel: ‘Italy didn’t surprise me, maybe France underestimated them’

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Belgium coach Mark van Bommel says Italy’s 3-1 win over France didn’t surprise him, and he suggested Les Bleus may have underestimated Roberto Mancini’s side.

With quotes via the Corriere dello Sport, the former Milan midfielder spoke at his pre-match press conference ahead of Sunday’s Nations League showdown with Italy in Brussels, which kicks off at 20:45 CET [19:45 GMT] at the King Baudouin Stadium.

Belgium must win to reach the quarter-finals. They’re third in Group A1 with nine points, one behind Italy and France. Italy will go through with a draw, and they’ll top the group if they win.

“It’s an important game that would allow us to continue or not,” Van Bommel said. “It’s a test comparable to other cups, an interesting game to play under pressure. We always prepare the same way, whether it’s a final or not.

“Tomorrow only victory counts, but it’s not a final that lets you win a cup.”

Italy go into the game full of confidence after beating France at San Siro on Thursday. Pio Esposito scored twice, and Zinedine Zidane suffered his first defeat as France coach.

“Italy didn’t surprise me, and Turkey also deserved more against France,” Van Bommel continued. “Italy are a very good team. Maybe France took them lightly, but it doesn’t surprise me.”

Belgium kept their hopes alive with a 2-1 comeback win in Turkey on Thursday, with Kevin De Bruyne and Charles De Ketelaere scoring.

Van Bommel also confirmed Romelu Lukaku will have a role to play, whether from the start or off the bench.

“He’s always important, whether he starts or comes on as a substitute. He’s a player with a lot of experience, and it’s a good thing to have him in a full stadium.”
