Two car household. Including the two I currently have, I have owned 5 different cars in my life. I've a kept my cars 10-12 years before replacement. One was replaced at 12 years old after developing rust which I was concerned would eventually become unsafe so I sold it. The other two were replaced after accidents (neither my fault). One was totaled at 10 years old. Another was in an accident at 10 years and I was concerned the car wouldn't drive the same even after repair so I just sold it damaged and replaced it.

With the exception of my first ever car, I have always bought new though. Currently have a 2017 model and a 2024 model in my fleet.
