Man Arrested for Allegedly Stabbing Double Amputee Girlfriend in Brutal Attack

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

A Florida man has been accused of stabbing his double amputee girlfriend – who uses a wheelchair – in a brutal attack, leaving her to suffer several severe injuries.

The woman was stabbed around 11 p.m. on Tuesday, November 24, in her home in Miami, Florida, according to a Miami-Dade county criminal complaint obtained by Us.

Before she was intubated, the woman identified her boyfriend, Marcos Enrique Sanchezpaz, as the person who attacked her. Sanchezpaz, 42, fled the scene, but he was later arrested when he returned to the residence.

An investigation was launched into the matter, and authorities learned that the incident escalated after the couple got into an argument. Sanchezpaz allegedly grabbed a knife and stabbed the woman several times.

The woman fell out of her wheelchair and managed to FaceTime her son while lying on the ground. “My boyfriend stabbed me,” she reportedly told her son, per police.

The victim then made the same claim to officers when they reported to the scene of the crime. Before she was transported to the hospital, she reportedly said, “My boyfriend stabbed me.”

The woman sustained multiple lacerations to her torso, her spleen and her chest area during the attack. Additionally, she suffered broken ribs and experienced bleeding in her lungs. Authorities said that was intubated as she was being taken to the hospital.

The victim’s current condition is not known as of time of publication.

Authorities said that Sanchezpaz fled the scene after he stabbed the woman, though he later returned. Police noted that he seemingly had blood on his shoe.

Police said Sanchezpaz was incoherent and he was taken to a hospital for evaluation. Once he was checked out, he was transferred to a jail.

Sanchezpaz has since been charged with one count of attempted murder, according to his online court records. The defendant has pleaded not guilty to the charge, and it’s not currently clear if he has retained legal counsel following his arrest.

Authorities confirmed that the couple had been in a relationship for about two years. However, they did not reveal if the couple has a history of domestic violence.

The Miami-Dade Sheriff’s Office had no additional information to share about the case when contacted by Us about updates.
