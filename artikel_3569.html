Couple Arrested After 2 Children Were Discovered Living Inside Drug-Filled Van

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

A Florida couple was arrested after they were found passed out in a U-Haul van alongside two children and a stockpile of drugs.

Vilmarie Acosta Diaz and Arian Hernandez were arrested on Saturday, November 7, after police discovered they were living in the van with the two children. Acosta Diaz, 46, was charged with two counts of child abuse and other drug-related charges, while Hernandez, 44, was arrested for allegedly driving under the influence.

The Hillsborough County Sheriff’s Office said in a Sunday, November 8, press release that deputies responded to a gas station on Saturday around 7:30 a.m. after authorities received a call regarding two unresponsive people in a U-Haul van.

Deputies found both adults unresponsive, eventually ordering Hernandez, Acosta Diaz and her kids to exit the van. Authorities claim both Acosta Diaz and Hernandez appeared to be “dazed, confused and disoriented” when they woke up, according to a probable cause arrest affidavit viewed by Law & Crime.

When Acosta Diaz exited the van, officers allegedly found a baggie that contained a “white crystalline substance.” The substance later tested positive for methamphetamine. Authorities also reported that Acosta Diaz was allegedly in possession of Xanax pills that were not legally prescribed to her.

Officers noted in the affidavit that Hernandez had red, glossy eyes and appeared to be unsteady on his feet.

Authorities also said that there were two children in the back of the van, who didn’t have secure seats to safely sit in. The back of the van was seemingly set up to be a living space, per the affidavit.

As authorities searched the van, they allegedly recovered muscle relaxers that were easily accessible to the children.

Deputies ultimately arrested Acosta Diaz and Hernandez at the scene. They are both currently in custody at the Hillsborough County Jail. Acosta Diaz is being held on a $15,800 bond, while Hernandez has a $500 bond.

“There is no excuse for this,” Sheriff Chad Chronister said in a statement. “Two children, no car seats, drugs within arm’s reach, and an impaired driver behind the wheel. These kids deserved better.”

It’s not currently clear if Acosta Diaz or Hernandez have entered pleas following their arrests. The investigation into the case remains ongoing.

While announcing the arrests, the Hillsborough County Sheriff’s Office shared body camera footage of officers approaching the vehicle and talking to the defendants.

Several people took to the comments section to react to the crime, with several people praising the officers for bringing the children to safety.

“Thank you for keeping those babies safe! And thank you for the sacrifice you make every time you walk out the door to go to work,” one person wrote, and another added, “The cops saved those poor kids. Thank you!”
