Pokémon Winds & Waves Starter Pokémon Plush Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Pokémon Winds & Waves will introduce a new generation of Pokémon, and as usual, you can expect all sorts of merchandise to follow.

With this in mind, The Pokémon Company has this week announced plush toys based on the three new starters - Browt (Grass), Pombon (Fire) and Gecqua (Water).

They'll be made available at Pokémon Centers in Japan on 5th December 2026 for ¥2,200 each (about $13.80 USD), but there's no update about a local release just yet. The game will follow with a worldwide simultaneous release at some point in 2027.

The Pokémon Company has also unveiled its new Pokémon "Timeless Adventure" merch. It includes bags, pouches, and other items, and features the new starters on the artwork, alongside starter Pokémon from other generations. This merch will also be available at Pokémon Centers in Japan next month.
