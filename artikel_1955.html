Sources: Thomas Frank to Crystal Palace update

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Crystal Palace are pushing forward in talks for Thomas Frank to become their next manager, sources have told Football Insider.

The 53-year-old has emerged as the leading candidate to replace Pierre Sage, who was dismissed earlier this week with the Eagles sitting bottom of the Premier League table.

Talks have been ongoing in recent days, with growing optimism that a deal will be agreed before too long, after the Crystal Palace squad returned to training on Friday.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Crystal Palace are making progress in talks with Frank and believe a deal will be reached soon.

A number of other candidates have also been considered in case talks break down with the former Tottenham manager, but that is considered unlikely as things stand.

Frank has a number of backers on the Palace board, who have been impressed by his work and believe he is the right man to turn their nightmare season around.

Football Insider revealed on 9 November that Sage would be sacked by Crystal Palace, with work ongoing to land his replacement after Frank emerged as the top target.

New talks were held with Frank following the 1-1 draw against Hoffenheim last Thursday, and there is now a belief he is in pole position to land the job in the near future.

Those discussions have continued into this week, and following Sage's dismissal, there is growing optimism that a deal will be reached before too long.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "It's an ongoing situation, discussions are happening between Crystal Palace and Thomas Frank.

"They have made him first number one target to replace Pierre Sage, so negotiations are ongoing and they'll be getting closer to an agreement.

"I'm sure Palace will be hoping that he could repeat his success that he had at Brentford at Crystal Palace. The squad is good at Palace as well, I think it's just about getting the best out of them.

"They have to sort out themselves defensively because again they've conceded too many goals under Pierre Sage and I'm sure that Palace feel that Thomas Frank is the right man to turn things around."

There had initially been optimism that Crystal Palace would bring their next manager in quickly, with the international break seen as the ideal opportunity to do so.

However, almost one week after their 3-1 defeat against Liverpool that put the nail in the coffin for Sage, they remain in negotiations as they work to bring in Frank.

Football Insider revealed on 12 November that Crystal Palace appointment of Frank has been delayed as they negotiate over his backroom staff.

Crystal Palace are keen to provide Frank with their backing by bringing in a number of his most trusted allies, as they did with Sage following his arrival from Lens.

While the deal is not expected to face any major issues as a result, the ongoing negotiations explain the reason for the delays surrounding Frank's official appointment.
