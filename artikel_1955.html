Rangers have 'serious concerns' about Connor Barron as Middlesbrough move stalls

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Rangers are having serious concerns about Connor Barron's lack of playing time since his loan move to Middlesbrough.

The 24-year-old left Ibrox on a season-loan loan deal during the summer after it became clear that he would not play a part in Derek McInnes' plans at Ibrox.

The door was left open to a potential return to Ibrox, while a permanent move to Boro remains on the cards, but his lack of minutes could scupper both of those possibilities.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Rangers hoped Barron would play regularly, either so he could improve their side or command a high fee.

Instead, the midfielder has failed to make a single appearances for Boro, even off the bench, with his only minutes this season coming during Rangers' Conference League qualifyers.

That has been flagged as a serious concern at Ibrox, where they are considering their next steps as they remain keen for Barron to play regular football this season.

Football Insider revealed back in September that Rangers are planning to evaluate Barron's loan spell in January, when he will have had time to settle in the Championship.

As the mid-season transfer window nears, his lack of involvement remains a problem despite Kim Hellberg's positive words, and a return to Ibrox is now firmly on the cards.

Any recall from his loan spell would be unlikely to see Barron thrust into McInnes' plans, but his lack of minutes for Boro could open the door to another loan spell.

"Rangers will have serious concerns if Connor Barron is not playing for Middlesbrough," Brown told Football Insider.

"It was clear he wasn't going to be part of their plans, but they sent him on loan to see if he could impress elsewhere, and then they could take their next steps afterwards.

"Whether that would see him come back and have another go at Rangers, or it would lead to a permanent move for a big fee, that will have been in the club's thinking.

"But if he's gone away and can't get a game, you have to ask serious questions about his ability and whether he is good enough.

"That's a problem for Rangers, because it raises questions about their recruitment, but it also means it might be difficult for them to deal with his return.

"Maybe it's something that could happen in January and he could move elsewhere so they don't have to tear up their plans, but it's not a situation they wanted to be in."

After it became clear that Barron was not wanted as part of McInnes' plans for the season, preparations were put in place to move the midfielder on, before he joined Middlesbrough.

Despite eventually agreeing to let him leave on loan, Rangers had been keen to do a permanent deal in a bid to raise funds from the midfielder's sale.

Football Insider revealed during the summer that Rangers would listen to "big offers" for Barron, with a fee of around £8million likely to be required to lure him away.

Despite attracting no suitors at that lofty price tag, there was later optimism at Ibrox that an impressive loan spell could inspire clubs to spend big on a deal.

Instead, he has struggled at Boro, and Rangers could be forced to reevaluate their position and Barron's asking price if he returns in the near future.
