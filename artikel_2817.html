Steelers WR Michael Pittman Jr. expected to play vs. Broncos

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

After getting Roman Wilson back in the lineup versus the Eagles, Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy told the media Monday that the Steelers should get another top wide receiver back ahead of a Black Friday matchup against the Denver Broncos.

"I'm very confident that Michael Pittman (Jr.) should be out there this week," McCarthy told the media in his Monday press conference. "We're getting healthy, and that always helps."

Pittman, who went on injured reserve on Oct. 10 with a left foot injury, entered his 21-day return window last week. The veteran receiver logged two full practices and was listed as questionable ahead of the Steelers' matchup against the Eagles, but was ultimately ruled out.

With DK Metcalf avoiding a suspension after an altercation against Philadelphia, Pittman's return could get the Steelers' wide receiver room back to full strength against the Broncos.

Despite a dismal showing in the passing game against the Eagles on Sunday, where Aaron Rodgers averaged just 5.2 yards per attempt and Metcalf was ejected after recording one catch for two yards, the Steelers receiver room showed some signs of life with Roman Wilson and practice squad elevation Max Hurleman returning from injured reserve.

Wilson logged a 33-yard gain while Hurleman unexpectedly led the team with six grabs for 61 yards and a score. Rookie Germie Bernard also contributed with six catches for 40 yards.

"Roman and Max gave us a good shot in the arm," McCarthy said.

According to Steelers.com's Brian Batko, Pittsburgh offensive coordinator Brian Angelichio noted Monday that Pittman's return can help the Steelers control the middle of the field.

Pittman has recorded seven catches for 89 yards in three games with the Steelers in 2026.
