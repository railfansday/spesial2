Commanders CB Amik Robertson to miss second straight game

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Commanders got a win over the Bengals without cornerback Amik Robertson and they'll try to do it again when they face the Cardinals on Sunday.

Robertson remained out of practice this week with an ankle injury and head coach Dan Quinn told reporters on Friday that he will not travel with the team to Arizona. Quinn said the team does not have any long-term concerns about Robertson's injury at this point.

Robertson has 53 tackles, a forced fumble and 10 passes defensed in nine games this season.

The Commanders will release a full injury report later in the day, but Robertson is the only player who has missed practice this week.
