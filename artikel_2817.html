Rams safety ejected after huge hit on Packers’ Christian Watson

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Rams, who have had issues at defensive back, will have bigger worries in the second half of Wednesday night's game against the Packers.

Rams safety Kam Kinchens was ejected for an illegal hit on Packers wide receiver Christian Watson, leaving him lying on the ground for an extended time.

Rams safety Kam Kinchens ejected for illegal hit. AP Photo/Gregory Bull

The play happened near the 1:54 mark of the second quarter, when Watson ran a corner route near the Rams' sideline.

Packers quarterback Jordan Love threw a floater to Watson but led him too far outside.

Watson was attempting to haul in the reception, but Kinchens led with his helmet, injuring the Packers receiver.

While Watson was unable to get back to his feet, Kinchens was seen celebrating the play.

Watson eventually got up and walked away under his own power.

The Packers announced during the second half that Watson was ruled out with a concussion.

Watson was off to a hot start for Green Bay as the team's leading receiver, having four receptions for 65 yards and a touchdown.

The Rams went on to pick up their fifth consecutive victory and beat the Packers, 26-13.
