Goring CC FC 10 v Wittering United Reserves FC 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Goring started well and on 15 minutes skipper Todor Bankov won a header on the half way line from which Connor Pomeroy's clever flick set Luke Tate on his way to open the scoring. This was quickly followed by a goal from Pomeroy who latched onto a fine pass from Tate and smashed home from the edge of the box.  Pomeroy added a third on 24 minutes before Wittering responded with a goal from Hashur Hussain. Pomeroy completed his hat trick from the penalty spot after 36 minutes for a 4-1 lead but Wittering again fought back and a second goal took the score to 4-2 at half time.

Goring made several changes at half time and wasted no time in reasserting their authority when Pomeroy fed Ben Rubano and his left foot drive made the score 5-2 just five minutes into the half. Goring were now rampant and when Rubano was brought down In the box on 58 minutes up stepped Bankov to fire home for a 6-2 lead. A clever pass from Willcox set up Pomeroy for his fourth goal and Goring's seventh in the 74th minute.
