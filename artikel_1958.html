Stefan Borson reveals Chelsea's 'problem' with new 'profitable' VVIP experience

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Chelsea will face trouble trying to scale up their new VVIP packages at Stamford Bridge in a new fan experience.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that the introduction of these experiences are worth it, but they won't move the dial.

Ahead of facing Leeds United on Saturday, 21 November, the Blues have sold out on the VVIP dressing room experience, with prices starting at £750.

It appears to just be a trial, with the tickets not yet available for the Crystal Palace clash after that, but it seems to have gone down well with the fans.

Chelsea and Leeds will likely put on quite a show after the international break, especially if recent meetings are anything to go by in all competitions.

Supporters are already excited for the game to kick off at Stamford Bridge, but they're being warned not to get too carried away by the idea of the new VVIP experience.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson is a fan of the new experience, but did warn that it'll have little impact behind the scenes.

"They're obviously very profitable because there's no cost to having somebody walking around the stadium," he explained.

"And it does give that added value that allows people to justify spending it. There aren't many teams that can charge £750 to £2000, £3000 for these sorts of things.

"Chelsea have a particularly convenient location to be able to charge these premium prices. But they're not very scalable. That's the problem with them.

"You can have 10, 15 people a game doing it, but that's probably your limit. So, it's worth doing, definitely. I don't think it matters what the number is."

Only once everyone returns for the resumption of the Premier League will Xabi Alonso be able to reveal which players are available.

However, the current situation in West London has been pretty dire, with Jorrel Hato, Maxence Lacroix, Joao Pedro, Jamie Gittens and Reece James all missing the trip to Sunderland.

Josh Acheampong is the latest injury concern, having pulled out of international duty for England Under-21s.

It is understood Emi Martinez could return after breaking his jaw, though the same cannot be said for Pedro, who has been a big miss so far.
