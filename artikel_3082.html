Inuvo (INUV) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Legacy search now runs for cash as capital shifts to AI audience modeling.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Inuvo, Inc. (INUV -2.43%) reported a shift in operational focus from legacy search toward its AI-driven IntentKey platform. Management indicated that the legacy search business is now being managed for cash flow rather than growth, while capital and personnel are being redirected to audience modeling. The company reported the implementation of a new enterprise sales team and a white-label partner program to accelerate market adoption of its identity-free targeting technology. Management stated that recent updates to mobile operating systems that restrict third-party tracking have served as a catalyst for interest in IntentKey.
