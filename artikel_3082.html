Inspire Medical Systems, Inc. (INSP -1.31%) reported a decrease in revenue during the third quarter primarily due to domestic reimbursement and coding challenges. Management implemented a strategic realignment through Project Horizon to redirect annualized cost savings toward high-impact growth initiatives and field personnel expansion. The company secured a Category I CPT code for its Inspire V system, effective Jan. 1, 2028, which management stated will simplify long-term coding and expand patient access. For the full year 2026, the company narrowed its revenue guidance and forecast a return to low single-digit growth in 2027 based on a more stable coding environment and increasing battery replacement revenue.
