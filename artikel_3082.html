Apple Taps Bond Market for $7B as Tech Sells Off

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Apple is looking to raise about $7 billion in its first investment-grade bond sale in more than a year, with proceeds helping to fund share buybacks. Bloomberg Intelligence’s Robert Schiffman says the move looks opportunistic rather than driven by a need for cash. He also explains why Apple is standing out in the broader tech selloff as investors reassess the debt and spending behind the AI infrastructure boom. He joins Tim Stenovec on “Bloomberg Tech.” (Source: Bloomberg)
