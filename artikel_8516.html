Personal Investments • Windfall Advice for Partner

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Hi All -

My partner recently received her share of an inheritance amounting to around $350k - I've read advice on here and elsewhere to largely just sit on the money for some time before deciding what to do, but looking for some advice for her particular situation. For context:

Age 42 |Current income ~$100k annually | Current retirement accounts <$100k spread between a Roth IRA and a few different workplace retirement accounts | She will receive a small pension through her current employer - which could grow depending on if she stays with the employer or not.

She owns a condo valued at approximately $250k, but she doesn't have much equity in at as she only purchased it in 2022 without much of a down payment and a rate ~5%. She currently rents it out to a friend at sub-market rates, and lives with me rent free. She could conceivably rent the condo out for ~80%+ of her monthly mortgage cost.

She has suffered from various health problems in her adult life, which have impacted her professional career and was very recently considering leaving her job, selling her condo and focusing on health and finding a less demanding job (likely as significantly less pay). She has never thought too much on retirement and has always assumed she would just work as long as she could, though I suspect her health issues will cause her to need to retire much earlier than she expects.

My current advice is -
- Immediately max out her Roth IRA for 2026 and set aside another ~7500+ to do the same for 2027 on ~Jan 1.
- Contribute as much as possible to her 401k with the few remaining paychecks she'll receive this year
- Do a deep dive on expenses, determine a realistic 1-year emergency fund and set that much aside in a HYSA
- Put the rest into another safe investment (CD/HYSA/?) and let it sit for a year or more before making any further decisions.

Would it make sense for her to pay off (or pay down and refinance) her mortgage to eliminate some monthly expenses in the event she does need to leave her job? Should she consider starting to DCA some of the funds into a investment account? Anything else she should be considering?

Thanks all!
