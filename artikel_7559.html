Chinese Telecommunications Conglomerate Huawei and Subsidiaries Convicted of Sanctions Violations, Bank and Wire Fraud, Conspiracy to Steal Trade Secrets, and Money Laundering

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

BROOKLYN, NY – Today, a federal jury in Brooklyn returned guilty verdicts against Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd. (Huawei) and three of its subsidiaries, Chinese subsidiary Huawei Device Co. Ltd. (Huawei Device) and U.S. subsidiaries Futurewei Technologies Inc. (Futurewei) and Huawei Device USA Inc. (Huawei Device USA).  One or more of the entities was convicted of each of the following crimes:  conspiracy to steal trade secrets; wire fraud conspiracy; wire fraud; bank fraud conspiracy; bank fraud; conspiracy to defraud the United States; conspiracy to violate the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA); IEEPA violations; and money laundering conspiracy. The verdict followed a 12-week trial before United States District Judge Ann M. Donnelly.  When sentenced, the defendants face financial penalties including criminal fines, forfeiture, and restitution.
