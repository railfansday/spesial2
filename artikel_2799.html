FPL GW11 Captain Picks: Erling Haaland vs Alex Isak – Who Should You Captain?

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

As we return from the November international break for Gameweek 11 of the 2026/27 Fantasy Premier League season, locking in the right armband choice is crucial for propelling your team up the rankings.

With prime home matchups, international goal-scoring momentum, and vulnerable defenses across the schedule, fantasy managers have several exciting routes to explore.

Here is our breakdown of the best captain picks for game week 11 to help you make the ultimate decision before the deadline.

Despite blanking in his last two Premier League appearances, Erling Haaland still averages an impressive 7 points per game.

Fresh off scoring three goals in two matches for Norway during the international break, his shooting boots are clearly polished and ready for action.

Manchester City hosts Fulham at the Etihad Stadium in Saturday's early kickoff, offering an ideal bounce-back spot against a 15th-placed side struggling for defensive consistency.

Backed by guaranteed minutes and penalty duties, Haaland remains the premier choice among GW11 FPL captaincy pick options.

For managers daring enough to hand the armband to a defender, Jan Paul van Hecke offers massive upside.

Tottenham Hotspur welcomes Ipswich Town to London, facing a newly promoted side that has conceded the second-highest total of goals in the league at 20.

Van Hecke brings clean-sheet security, reliable defensive baseline contributions, and towering aerial threat on set pieces, making him an intriguing differential among game week 11 FPL captain picks.

Brighton travels to face Hull City, presenting a prime opportunity to back Pascal Groß. After starting the season strongly, Hull has slipped below the top ten and enters the weekend winless in five matches, whereas Brighton has claimed four wins in their last five outings.

Operating as Brighton's set-piece specialist with guaranteed 90-minute appearances and steady attacking form, Groß stands out as a clever differential for captain picks for game week 11 of the 26/27 season.

Alexander Isak enters Gameweek 11 in sharp scoring form, having found the net in two of his last three league outings. Liverpool hosts a Manchester United side at Anfield that currently possesses one of the leakiest defenses in the division, having shipped nine goals across their last three matches.

While United recently appointed Mauricio Pochettino as head coach, limited training sessions during the international break suggest their backline will remain unsettled, establishing Isak as one of the top captain picks for GW11 of the 26/27 season.

Whether you play it safe with Erling Haaland at the Etihad, trust Alexander Isak against a vulnerable Manchester United defense, or take a bold differential punt on Pascal Groß or Jan Paul van Hecke, aligning your armband with high-upside matchups is key to a green arrow.
