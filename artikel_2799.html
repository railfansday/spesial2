Whitney Point field hockey and Vestal boys' soccer fall at states

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Whitney Point girls' field hockey and Vestal boys' soccer were named state finalists in the NYSPHSAA Class C and Class AA tournaments, respectively.

The Whitney Point Eagles entered the tournament as the defending state champions for 2024 and 2025. The Vestal Golden Bears boys' soccer team entered the tournament seeking the program's first state title since 2017.

The Whitney Point Eagles (18-2-1) fell to Section 9's Rondout Valley (20-0) in the NY state field hockey tournament at Arlington High School on Sunday, Nov. 15.

Rondout Valley held a 1-0 lead after scoring in the first two minutes. Junior Ella Morse hit the long shot that put the Ganders ahead. Whitney Point's defensive line kept Rondout Valley scoreless for the rest of the game.

The Eagles made a stride with 0:15 left in the fourth after getting a short-corner shot at goal, but Rondout Valley won as the clock expired.

What was at stake: Whitney Point had faced Rondout Valley in the state final two prior years (2024, 2025) and were looking to add to the Eagles' two-time state title streak against the Ganders. The Eagles lead Section 4 with the most state titles (10) since 1984.

Section 4 Class C Final | Defeated Spencer-van Etten 8-1

Section 2's Bethlehem (20-0-2) defeated the Vestal Golden Bears (9-2) 5-0 in the NY state boys' soccer tournament at Middletown High School on Sunday, November 15, capturing the program's first state title.

Bethlehem took an early 2-0 lead and extended it to 5-0 in the 76th minute when senior forward Luke Tatro scored from outside the box. Senior Ishan Chatur sparked the Eagles' 3-0 second-half stretch after scoring a volley from the top of the box six minutes into the half.

What was at stake: Bethlehem was seeking a program-first Class AA state title, and Vestal was looking for its first AA title since 2007. The Golden Bears lead the section with the most state titles since 1978 (3).
