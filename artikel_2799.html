Cowboys fans grade Mike Pettine’s first game calling the defense

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Last week there was a big shakeup among the Dallas Cowboys coaching staff. Defensive coordinator Christian Parker wasn't stripped of his title or fired, but he was stripped of his play-calling duties for the defense. Senior advisor Mike Pettine took over the play-calling, even though he's only been with the team for a month or so.

The Cowboys won their game 51-30 over the Tennessee Titans. We wanted to know how Pettine did in his first go-around calling the Cowboys defense. The fact that Tennessee scored 30 points had some wondering if the change made any difference. Others saw improvement in communication and other areas, even if it didn't show up as much in the final score.

We asked you to grade his performance, and 50% of respondents gave him a middle of the road 'C'. The next biggest group of votes was 29% for a 'D' grade. Suffice it to say, most Cowboys fans came away pretty unimpressed, although a 'C' grade is probably as good of a grade as any defensive effort this season, so maybe there was improvement.

On Thanksgiving, Pettine will get another shot against the Philadelphia Eagles. The Cowboys are 8.5-point favorites according to FanDuel.

So what's you take BTB? Was a 'C' grade meant as an improvement? How do you think he will do against the Eagles?
