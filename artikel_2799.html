Chiefs-Cardinals: Game Thread and Live Updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The (7-2) Kansas City Chiefs will host the (3-7) Arizona Cardinals in Week 11 of the NFL regular season. The game is kicking off at noon at Arrowhead Stadium on a sunny, cool day in the heartland. The interconference battle is being broadcast on CBS (KCTV-5 locally).

The Chiefs are aiming for a third consecutive win since the two-game skid that followed a 5-0 start to the campaign. Kansas City is looking to be 8-2 heading into a pivotal AFC battle with the Buffalo Bills on Thanksgiving night.

To accomplish that goal, the Chiefs will have to go through a fourth starting quarterback for the Cardinals in as many weeks: Josh Johnson. After trading away veteran Jacoby Brissett earlier this year and watching Gardner Minshew fall to injury, rookie quarterback Carson Beck is dealing with back spasms that will keep him out today.

Emmett Johnson opened with a 16-yard run and gained 36 yards on seven carries during Kansas City's 12-play, 72-yard drive. Patrick Mahomes found Rashee Rice for 15 yards on third-and-5 to keep the possession moving, then hit Travis Kelce over the middle for an eight-yard touchdown on third-and-6. Harrison Butker's extra point gave the Chiefs a 7-0 lead with 9:50 left in the first quarter.

Kansas City's defense forced a three-and-out on Arizona's opening possession. Tyler Allgeier gained three yards on two carries before Josh Johnson's third-and-7 pass intended for Harrison Wallace III fell incomplete, with Nick Bolton credited with the breakup. Blake Gillikin's 55-yard punt went into the end zone for a touchback, giving the Chiefs the ball at their own 20.

Kansas City marched 80 yards in 16 plays, taking more than seven minutes off the clock. Patrick Mahomes opened with an 11-yard completion to Rashee Rice, who later converted two third downs with catches of 10 and nine yards. Emmett Johnson kept the drive alive with a fourth-and-1 conversion and an eight-yard run on third-and-3 before finishing with a three-yard touchdown. Harrison Butker's extra point extended the Chiefs' lead to 14-0 with 51 seconds left in the first quarter.
