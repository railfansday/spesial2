‘The Flop King,’ ‘Absolute Joke’ — NBA Fans Slam Spurs Star Victor Wembanyama Over ‘Absurd’ Flop Against Pistons

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The San Antonio Spurs' 12-game winning streak ended Tuesday with a 105-93 home loss to the Detroit Pistons. A whistle in the final seconds of the first quarter drew accusations from fans that Victor Wembanyama had flopped.

Wembanyama went to the floor with 1.2 seconds left in the period, and Pistons guard Chaz Lanier was called for a foul that sent the Spurs star to the free-throw line. Fans who watched the replays called it a flop, but the foul on Lanier stood.

The sequence came right after Cade Cunningham's driving dunk with 1.7 seconds left had cut San Antonio's lead to 31-26.

On the ensuing possession, Wembanyama tumbled to the floor, and Lanier was whistled for a personal foul.

After Lanier was called for the foul, officials went to replay and the ruling stood. Wembanyama then made both free throws to give San Antonio a 33-26 lead at the end of the quarter.

Clips of the sequence were posted while the game was still going. The basketball page Brick Center shared one and wrote, "Wemby with a CRAZY flop."

Some of the anger was aimed at the whistle itself, with one fan writing, "They really called this a trip … Wemby is genuinely the flop king."

One fan described it: "They went to the monitor and looked at it for 5 minutes to see what time was on the clock and every replay showed absolutely no contact besides Wemby falling over his own feet."

Another fan also pointed to the review before turning the criticism squarely on Wembanyama: "Wemby's flop there against the Pistons was absurd, and they reviewed it and upheld the foul called against the Pistons. Absolute joke. Wemby is an absolute joke."

Some of the reaction moved past the single call to what the league should do about plays like it, with another fan writing, "At this point, the only way the NBA can fix this is by suspending players."

One more fan questioned Wembanyama's standing among the league's stars, asking, "This your MVP?"

Tuesday's play also revived a line Wembanyama delivered after the Spurs beat the defending champion Oklahoma City Thunder 111-109 in the NBA Cup semifinals at T-Mobile Arena in December 2025.

That win snapped a 16-game winning streak for the Thunder, and Wembanyama scored 22 points in 21 minutes off the bench in his return from a 12-game absence.

"I'm just glad to be a part of something that's growing to be so beautiful. Pure and ethical basketball," he said that night.

Two days later, he expanded on the remark at a press conference, contrasting the Spurs with what he sees in modern basketball: "Lots of isolation ball and, sometimes, kind of forced basketball. We try to propose a brand of basketball that can be described as more old school sometimes; the Spurs way as well. So it's tactically more correct basketball, in my opinion."

One fan threw the original line back at him Tuesday night, writing, "Pure and ethical basketball, you know."

San Antonio stretched its lead to 40-26 early in the second quarter, its largest of the night, before Detroit outscored the Spurs 79-53 the rest of the way. The Pistons took a 55-54 lead into halftime and went ahead for good on Cunningham's pull-up jumper with 4:56 left in the third quarter.

Cunningham finished with 27 points, Daniss Jenkins scored 25, and Ausar Thompson added 14 points, 12 assists, and five steals. Detroit held a 60-44 edge in the paint, while the Spurs shot 9 of 40 from the 3-point range. The Pistons won without Jalen Duren, Isaiah Joe, Ron Holland II, and Duncan Robinson.

Wembanyama finished with 25 points on 11-of-23 shooting, 18 rebounds and four assists in 34 minutes. He said after the game: "I think it's more on us. We didn't do the right things. I think we should have been smarter."

The loss was San Antonio's first since a 0-2 start and left the Spurs at 12-3, second in the Western Conference. They host the LA Clippers on Thursday, while Detroit (9-4), third in the Eastern Conference, plays at the New Orleans Pelicans on Wednesday night.
