West Ham monitor Manchester United starlet ahead of potential January swoop

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

West Ham have not had the start to the Championship season that they’d have liked. However, with WHUFC just a few points away from the Championship play-off spots, a strong January window could easily propel them back into contention.

They are already eyeing up one particularly ambitious swoop as, according to Sky Sports’ Danyal Khan, Scott Parker’s side are monitoring the situation of Manchester United’s Ayden Heaven. However, they are going to face a lot of interest from Premier League sides.

It has been suggested that Heaven could leave the club on loan in the January window. With Manchester United likely to strengthen at centre-back in January, he is worried about his gametime.

A permanent move cannot be ruled out for Heaven, as reported by the BBC. They have stated that Heaven feels that new Manchester United boss Mauricio Pochettino may favour his more experienced options due to the clubs league position.

Heaven could prove to be a smart, low-risk developmental addition for West Ham. The 20-year-old left-footed centre-back came through the Hammers’ academy before spells with Arsenal and Manchester United, where he has demonstrated composure in possession, impressive physical attributes and considerable potential.

With United struggling in the relegation zone and Pochettino favouring more experienced options, Heaven could be tempted by the prospect of regular first-team football. His athleticism and versatility to cover left-back or midfield make him an attractive option.

His impressive duel and recovery statistics also suggest he has the foundations to develop into a dependable squad member or eventual first-choice defender with consistent game time.

A move to WHUFC may be more likely on loan with Premier League clubs keen. However, in the short-term he would be a very useful pickup and could be a good option at left-back too.

Most Premier League outfits will no doubt be keeping tabs on Heaven. He is one of England’s best young defenders, having already racked up 32 appearances for Manchester United.

Despite his sides struggles this season he is still a very highly-regarded player with lots of potential. With that in mind, West Ham have to be looking at other targets.

Permanent moves from Premier League sides could scupper any plans that WHUFC have had for a potential loan deal. Additionally, the fee he could possibly cost, likely would not be worth it too West Ham right now, considering their need for a natural left-back and a new striker.
