Former West Ham Star ‘Wants’ Manager’s Job as Pressure Mounts on Nuno

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

After the third defeat of the season at the London stadium, there has been inevitable speculation that Nuno’s job is under threat. Supporters favourite would be Eddie Howe, yet it’s unlikely that the former Newcastle United manager would be interested in the job.

However there is a a former West Ham player reportedly interested in becoming the club’s next head coach should the Hammers decide to part ways with Nuno Espírito Santo in the near future. And it’s not Scott Parker!

Increasing pressure after three home defeats – and six goals conceded in eight days

Current Wales manager Craig Bellamy has privately indicated that he would welcome the opportunity to manage West Ham if the position became available. Bellamy’s former team-mate James Collins recently endorsed the idea during an appearance on TalkSport.

Bellamy has implemented an attractive brand of football with Wales, built around possession, high intensity and an aggressive pressing style. Yet, since taking on the job in 2024, results have been mixed with a 37.5% win ratio during that time. This has not stopped speculation over whether he could one day return to east London in a managerial capacity.

Following Saturday’s 3-1 defeat to West Bromwich Albion, Nuno Espírito Santo acknowledged the scrutiny surrounding his position.

“I am always under pressure, every day,” he said.

Supporters at the London Stadium made their feelings known after the final whistle, with chants of “You’re getting sacked in the morning” directed at the Portuguese Head Coach.

Yet despite the clubs current position in eighth place, Nuno Espírito Santo is expected to be given more time. Daniel Kretinsky continues to support the Portuguese coach, although both parties understand the pressure that comes with football management.

For now, Bellamy’s interest appears to be one for the future rather than the present. Nuno still retains the backing of the club’s hierarchy, but football can change quickly and results will ultimately determine his fate.

Should a managerial vacancy arise down the line, Bellamy’s connection to West Ham and his exciting coaching philosophy could make him a name worth watching.
