Broncos sign CB Riley Moss to multiyear extension. See the details

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Denver Broncos have established slightly more stability to the future of their secondary.

Cornerback Riley Moss is signing a four-year, $67 million extension with the team that drafted him, according to multiple reports. Moss was one of six defensive backs – and one of three members of the secondary at the top of the depth chart – poised to hit free agency after the 2026 season. Now, he's set to stay in Denver through 2030.

The Iowa product was a third-round pick by the Broncos in the 2023 NFL Draft. The 2026 season, Moss's fourth, was the final year of his rookie contract.

Through eight games this year, Moss is on pace for a second solid season in a row. Last year, he tied for the NFL lead with 19 passes defended, per Pro Football Reference. Moss also recorded his second career interception last year and his first career sack. In 2026, Moss already has an interception, along with eight passes defended in eight games.

Moss has missed six games due to injury in his career, including Week 5 this year with a rib injury. He also suffered a concussion in Week 9 that kept him out of the second half. He's not expected to miss any additional time after Denver's Week 10 bye.

Here are the terms of the fourth-year cornerback's contract extension, according to reports.

Average annual value (AAV): $16.75 million

Moss is getting a significant pay bump from the four-year, $5.44 million deal he signed before his rookie year. The AAV of his extension with the Broncos is bigger than the total value of his initial contract.

According to Over The Cap, the Moss extension will rank 16th among NFL cornerbacks in total value, above the Browns' Denzel Ward but below Bills CB Christian Benford. Moss's deal also comes out to 23rd among NFL cornerbacks in AAV, between Rams' Jaylen Watson ($17 million/year) and Buccaneers' Zyon McCollum ($16 million/year).

Moss has missed the second half of Weeks 4 and 9 with rib and head injuries, respectively. He also missed Week 5 while still dealing with his rib ailment.
