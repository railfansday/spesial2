Oklahoma City takes home win streak into matchup with Atlanta

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Atlanta Hawks (8-6, fifth in the Eastern Conference) vs. Oklahoma City Thunder (9-5, seventh in the Western Conference)

BOTTOM LINE: Oklahoma City will try to keep its five-game home win streak alive when the Thunder play Atlanta.

The Thunder have gone 5-0 at home. Oklahoma City is fourth in the Western Conference with 34.6 defensive rebounds per game led by Isaiah Hartenstein averaging 7.7.

The Hawks are 5-3 in road games. Atlanta ranks fifth in the Eastern Conference with 44.9 rebounds per game led by Jalen Johnson averaging 9.0.

The Thunder average 10.9 made 3-pointers per game this season, 1.7 fewer makes per game than the Hawks allow (12.6). The Hawks are shooting 45.1% from the field, 3.2% higher than the 41.9% the Thunder's opponents have shot this season.

TOP PERFORMERS: Hartenstein is shooting 54.3% and averaging 8.6 points for the Thunder. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander is averaging 32.4 points over the last 10 games.
