DAZN Bet Alberta offers many ways to wager on your favorite sports. These include the most common methods like moneyline, point spread, totals, parlays, props, and futures, but there are also system bets (round robin) and teasers.

Moneyline: Pick a winner. Those with "-" odds are the favorite; "+" odds are the underdog.

Point spread: This is a number designed to make a game as close as possible. If a team is -3.5, they need to win outright by 4 points or more; if a team is +3.5, they can cover by winning the game or losing by 3 points or fewer. The odds are almost always -110.

Totals: The odds on these markets are also almost always -110, and you'll wager on whether a point total will fall short or exceed a number.

Parlays: Combine multiple events together. For a parlay to succeed, all legs must win.

Futures: Wager on events that'll resolve later, such as the Super Bowl winner.

Props: Wager on player performance, such as quarterback passing yards, hits in a baseball game, or rebounds in basketball.

Same Game Parlays: Same as a standard parlay, only instead it's focused on one game.

System:  Another term for Round Robin, this will allow you to wager on multiple iterations of a parlay, such as a 4-leg parlay having 6 2-leg parlay options.

Teasers: Altering the point spread or totals in your favor while sacrificing some odds value. For example, if you had a 2-leg parlay with two NFL teams with a point spread of -7 and you did a 6-point teaser, their new spread is now just -1.
