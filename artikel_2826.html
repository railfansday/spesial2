Steelers vs. Eagles: Week 11 game picks

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Pittsburgh Steelers and Philadelphia Eagles will face off on Sunday at 1:00 EST. The Steelers are hoping to avoid losing consecutive games for the first time this season, while the Eagles are looking to end a seven-game losing streak. Who will come out on top? Our staff weighs in.

Ryland Bickley: I've bet against history once this year, unwisely picking the Steelers to beat the Browns in Cleveland on Thursday Night Football in Week 4. And you know what? I haven't learned my lesson. Yes, the Steelers haven't won in Philadelphia since the '60s, but this Eagles team is a mess. After floundering against Joe Burrow and the Bengals last week, the Steelers defense should be able to find its footing against Joe Milton and a struggling Philly O-line. On the other side of the ball, I'm not too confident in Aaron Rodgers and co. to get things going on the road, but getting Roman Wilson back certainly helps. I think Pittsburgh does just enough to rebound from last week and win an ugly game on the road. Steelers 20, Eagles 17.

Mike Nicastro: Mike Nicaatro: The Steelers haven't won in Philadelphia since 1965, but they'll enter their house of horrors against a much different Eagles team. The Eagles have a depleted offensive line, an underwhelming run game, and they are on their third quarterback in Joe Milton. Unfortunately for the Steelers, their offense also leaves much to be desired. Father time might have officially hit Aaron Rodgers at the worst time, and DK Metcalf can't seem to catch a cold in the winter. However, the black and gold are getting healthier at wideout, as Michael Pittman and Roman Wilson appear to be on track to get a helmet. That will help mitigate the challenges, and in an absolute rock fight, the Steelers offense will make a few more plays than the Eagles. Steelers 16, Eagles 10.

Austin Bechtold: Lyndon B. Johnson was president, the Steelers played in Oakland at Pitt Stadium, and the Penguins weren't even a team yet. These are all true events from the last time the Steelers beat the Eagles in Philadelphia in 1965. This game SHOULD be a different story. The Steelers need a significant bounce-back week after being embarrassed in primetime against the Cincinnati Bengals. They only had 40 yards of total offense at halftime and the defense couldn't slow down a red hot Bengals offense. This is a turning point in the season and will go one of two ways: the Steelers dominate Philadelphia and attempt to show the Bengals game was a fluke, the Steelers and Eagles play a close game down to the end and whoever has the ball last will walk away with a victory. Joe Milton starts for the Eagles and the team hasn't scored more than 21 points since Week 2 against the Titans. The Steelers get Roman Wilson back and should help the passing game, but losing Mason McCormick is likely a big loss up front. The Eagles are also getting healthier off their bye week. I don't feel great about this game, but I think the Steelers will do enough for an ugly win. It would be great to see them force three turnovers and put together over 350 yards of offense to get back into form. The Steelers win for the first time in 61 years, but it's not pretty! Steelers 20, Eagles 13.

Joey Bray: Joey Bray: We are all exhausted by the insult stat reminding us the Steelers haven't won a game in Philadelphia since 1965. Still, this might be the Steelers' best shot at winning in Philly in years. The Eagles are losers of seven straight, and for good reason. Their season has been defined by injuries and controversy. With Joe Milton starting at quarterback, his inexperience makes the chances of him being able to pick apart the Steelers' lackluster secondary quite low. The Steelers offense looks to be getting some receiver help back, but how much will that actually help what has been a bad passing attack over the course of the last month? With Mason McCormick out indefinitely, I'm interested to see how the run game fares with Spencer Anderson back in the starting lineup. Ultimately, the Steelers pass rush needs a huge bounce back game. While the Steelers should still be able to rely on their run game against and Eagles defensive line that ranks 25th in the league. I'll take the Steelers to get back on track and break the Philly curse in a close one. Steelers 23, Eagles 20.

Jarrett Bailey: The Steelers are the better team facing a third-string quarterback. The Eagles' defense has also struggled this season, currently sitting at 27th in EPA per play. However, they still have several players on that side of the ball capable of slowing down a Steelers offense that doesn't have a passing attack. The Steelers win, but it's low-scoring and closer than anyone would like it to be. Steelers 17, Steelers 13.
