Everything We Know About Spider-Man & Wolverine Being In Avengers: Doomsday

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

It's almost time for Avengers: Doomsday, and rumors swirl that Spider-Man and Wolverine will turn up. Marvel is determined to one-up even Avengers: Endgame, with this movie bringing back heroes from the last 26 years of Marvel blockbusters. The final Doomsday trailer divides its time between Steve Rogers and the X-Men, and even marked a franchise first: we finally got James Marsden's Cyclops saying an iconic line, "To me, my X-Men."

The X-Men, the Fantastic Four, and several different teams of Avengers are all going up against Robert Downey, Jr.'s Doctor Doom. But with the entire multiverse expected to end ahead of next year's Avengers: Secret Wars, we're all pretty certain there are going to be a lot more surprises. There have been particularly exciting rumors about both Spider-Man and Wolverine, but how likely are they to be true?

There's currently no evidence Tom Holland's Spider-Man is in Avengers: Doomsday. The core issue lies in the real-world, with the fact there are only small gaps in Holland's schedule where he could have been available for filming; he was occupied on The Odyssey through most of Doomsday's production, before moving on to Spider-Man: Brand New Day. If he does make an appearance at all, it would have to be a very small one indeed.

That said, there's intense debate about whether the end of Spider-Man: Brand New Day is actually Doomsday setup. It shows Ned Leeds' "Spider-Tracker" app losing Spider-Man completely, only to spot him in space. This is most likely a multiversal collision known as an incursion, where two different Earths collide. It's possible Holland's Spider-Man has been transported to another Earth, one that's about to impact Earth-616.

Tobey Maguire and Andrew Garfield both reprised their roles in Spider-Man: No Way Home, meaning they've already been part of the Multiverse Saga. It wouldn't be at all surprising to see them turn up; in fact, there have been rumors Doomsday kicks off with Maguire's Spider-Man killed in battle with the X-Men, who are fighting to save their universe. We'll have to wait and see whether that actually happens.

One thing's for sure: If Maguire or Garfield do return as Spider-Man in Avengers: Doomsday, it's likely to only be for a small role. The film principally involves three different Earths: Earth-616, the Fantastic Four's world, and the X-Men's. That already means Doomsday has a lot of cast to juggle (even more, given Doctor Doom may have been collecting people from other timelines as well). Many characters are likely to be relegated to cameos, just to make the story navigable.

Marvel is keeping very quiet about whether Hugh Jackman's Wolverine is in Doomsday. That said, the House of Ideas do seem intent on keeping Jackman on board till he's 90 (at least), and Deadpool and Wolverine brought the character into the Multiverse Saga. In this case, it feels likely Wolverine is being kept back as a surprise. But nobody will be shocked should Logan pop his claws and fight alongside the rest of the X-Men. Perhaps he's even one of the heroes who responds to Cyclops' call to arms in the trailer.

Speaking as a lifelong X-Men fan, I'm delighted to see other members of the team get the spotlight in Doomsday's marketing. Fox depended far too much on Wolverine's popularity, and it's been so good to see Marvel course-correct a little, choosing to emphasize the likes of Professor X and Cyclops. I'm sure there are going to be more X-Men surprises, too.

Oddly enough, though, there are actually quite a few heroes couldn't missing from Avengers: Doomsday's roll call. Sticking with the X-Men, two characters stand out: Ryan Reynold's Deadpool and Famke Janssen's Jean Grey. The former has already appeared in the Multiverse Saga, and even has connections to the Time Variance Authority. The latter is an absolute powerhouse (literally the Phoenix), so there's likely a story reason here. Perhaps Jean has already been killed (or recruited) by Doctor Doom.

Far too many heroes from Earth-616 are still absent. Brie Larson's Captain Marvel is surprising, again because she's one of the most powerful heroes in play. A multiversal event really should feature the "boss of space," so we can only hope she's going to turn up. Teyonah Parris' Spectrum was stranded in the X-Men timeline at the end of The Marvels, which should surely mean she's unwittingly triggering an incursion between Earth-616 and the X-Men universe. It would actually be rather odd for Marvel to ignore her in Doomsday.

The problem, of course, is that the MCU expanded a little too quickly during the Multiverse Saga. We were introduced to so many different superheroes; some are street level (such as Alaqua Cox's Echo), while others didn't prove especially popular (the less said about Secret Invasion's new Super-Skrulls, the better). Avengers: Doomsday already faces a huge challenge if it's to give due time to multiple Avengers teams as well as the X-Men, so some Earth-616 heroes are going to be neglected. Don't expect to see the Eternals anytime soon.

That said, Marvel did recently confirm two new heroes for Doomsday. Hailee Steinfeld is returning as Kate Bishop, meaning at least one of Marvel's two Hawkeyes (and, in this case, a Young Avenger in the comics) is due to appear. Meanwhile, VisionQuest's post-credits scene confirmed Doctor Doom has resurrected Elizabeth Olsen's Scarlet Witch, presumably confirming rumors Wanda Maximoff is one of the Latverian Witches we see in the recent trailer. If these two heroes are also back, then it's possible Marvel has quite a few surprises in the works. We'll find out more when Avengers: Doomsday finally releases in December.
