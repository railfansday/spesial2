Star Trek Officially Brings Back Captain Janeway For 60th Anniversary Celebration

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It was in May 1964 that Star Trek burst onto the scene and sent the sci-fi genre into a tail spin thanks to a universe which introduced diverse characters, stories, and settings. Created by Gene Roddenberry and first premiering on television in 1966, Star Trek has now cemented itself as one of the most influential shows of all time, going on to inspire multiple spin-offs, movies, video games, novels, and more.

Now, over six decades on from its inception, Star Trek has returned an iconic character as part of its 60th anniversary celebrations, and as of 24 November, you can hear all about Captain Janeway's perilous journey home in one brand-new release which will be a must-buy for the Star Trek fans in your life.

Despite the Star Wars universe being home to many iconic characters, there is no denying that Captain Kathryn Janeway is definitely one of them. Serving as the main character in the television series Star Trek: Voyager, she was the captain of the Starfleet starship USS Voyager and had to navigate the trials and tribulations of being lost in the Delta Quadrant on the other side of the galaxy. Portrayed by Kate Mulgrew, the actor has returned to bring life to the role once more in Star Trek Timelines, a new hardcover book from DK that features 336-pages of the entire fictional universe we have fallen in love with over the past 60 years.

Not only that, but Mulgrew has provided a foreword detailing how the franchise, and more specifically, Star Trek: Voyager, had an impact on her life, and it is lovely to see such an iconic actor and character being showcased during this celebratory time.

Star Trek Timelines retails at $60, so it is quite the investment, but readers will discover a recap of Star Trek's universe, including the various television shows which have aired over the years, as well as all the other media it has popped up in. Not only that, but the book aims to share information on the Star Trek universe as a whole; all the way from the Big Bang that created the universe to the many significant moments that have made the fictional universe as successful as it is. As such, it will feature some of the most beloved entries, such as The Original Series, The Next Generation, Deep Space Nine, Voyager, and Enterprise, as well as more recent television shows like Discovery, Strange New Worlds, and Starfleet Academy.

With the Star Trek universe being set, well, across the universe, Star Trek Timelines has a lot to cover, but you will be pleased to hear that it will also delve into various alternate timelines, such as the iconic Mirror Universe, and even Voyager's unforgettable "Year of Hell" storyline that I am sure none of us have forgotten. With this being one of Captain Janeway's most memorable storylines, it was obvious that it needed to be included, especially with Mulgrew endorsing the book herself.

Star Trek Timelines: A Visual Voyage Through Generations of History is now available to purchase, and just in time for Christmas. So, if you have a Star Trek lover in your life, you may have just found the perfect gift to surprise them with.
