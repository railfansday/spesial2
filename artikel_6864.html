Prince William and Kate Middleton’s House Was Broken Into on Monday Night

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Prince William and Kate Middleton are facing a major security breach: the couple’s Sandringham estate, Anmer Hall, was broken into on Monday night. And while they do typically spend the holiday season there, the family fortunately was not home at the time.

According to The Sun—which broke the news—Prince William, Kate Middleton, and their kids (Prince George, Princess Charlotte, and Prince Louis) were at their main residence, Forest Lodge in Windsor, at the time of the break-in. There’s no reporting on who broke into the estate or if anything was taken, but this news comes about two years after two people broke into a home on the Windsor estate in 2024—when William and Kate were at home in nearby Adelaide Cottage.

The couple has 24/7 security at Forest Lodge, but it’s unclear how protected Anmer Hall is when they aren’t at home. The Georgian estate was built in the 18th century and was gifted by the Queen to Wills and Kate when they got married. The royal pair dished out over £1.5 million for a renovation, and spent most of the COVID-19 pandemic there. Their other homes include the aforementioned Forest Lodge, Apartment 1A at Kensington Palace (their London residence), and Tam-Na-Ghar (their home on the Balmoral estate where the royals spend their summers).
