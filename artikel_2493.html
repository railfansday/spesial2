Wilmington football wins emotionally-charged playoff thriller

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

FRANKLIN – Chase Mitcheltree's shoulders dropped as he ran into the endzone.

He was finally able to take a breath after helping carry the weight of the Wilmington community on his shoulder the past four days.

The game and another District 10 championship were likely in hand and his mission was accomplished.

Mitcheltree even took time to pick up a Mercyhurst Prep linemen knowing how devastated the Lakers were in the loss.

"The past week has been a lot, but we knew we had to come together for our community," said Mitcheltree. "We wanted to bring a championship back to Wilmington."

Mitcheltree ran for 255 yards and four touchdowns, including the game-winning score with 11 seconds left, as Wilmington outlasted Mercyhurst Prep 37-30 for the District 10 Class 1A football championship on Monday, Nov. 23, at Franklin High School.

Wilmington (10-2) moves on to the PIAA Class 1A quarterfinals on Saturday against Port Allegany at 1 p.m. at Clarion University.

The New Wilmington community was in shock and mourning on Thursday, Nov. 19, when senior cheerleader and softball star Skylar Leishman died in a tragic car accident on her way to school. District 10 officials moved the Wilmington-Mercyhurst Prep playoff game to Monday because of the situation.

"Skylar was a student that meant so much to our community and to the boys in the locker room," said an emotional Wilmington coach Brandon Phillian. "They wanted to play with her initials on their helmets and to play in her honor."

With emotions running high, Wilmington took the field against a Mercyhurst Prep team that hasn't won a D-10 title since 2007.

It turned into an all-time playoff game in D-10 history with a special battle between the two all-time rushers in their school histories.

Mercyhurst Prep (9-3) star Vinnie Feliciano ran for 252 yards and three touchdowns to go with a 64-yard touchdown pass to lead the Lakers. He became the third rusher in District 10 history to rush for 3,000 yards in one season, joining Christian Cole (3,252) and Ethen Knox (3,705 in 2022) of Oil City. Feliciano finished with 3,021 yards as a senior. Feliciano also ran for 7,667 yards in his career and finishes second in D-10 history behind Knox (7,870 yards).

Mitcheltree is up to 2,082 yardds and 30 touchdowns this season and is closing in on 5,000 yards in his career. His 78 career touchdowns are in the top in Mercer County history.

"I couldn't be more grateful for a person like Chase on and off the field," Phillian said, "He couldn't be more respected in that locker room and he couldn't be more admired by the coaches and the players."

Mitcheltree powered in with 1 minute, 59 seconds to go to give the 'Hounds a 29-22 lead. Mercyhurst Prep countered with a play it has been sitting on for weeks. The Lakers started their 66-21 win over Conneaut in Week 10 with Feliciano taking a pitch and throwing a 75-yard touchdown pass.

Mercyhurst lined up for a game-tying extra point, but holder JT Jones took off with the snap and beat Mitcheltree to the edge for a two-point conversion to make it 30-29 Lakers.

Wilmington went back to work with Mitcheltree as he had all six rushes in the next drive for 37 yards. The drive also included a pass interference call as the Mercyhurst Prep defender ran through Mitcheltree on an under thrown pass.

Mitcheltree ran in the game-winning touchdown and a two-point conversion to make it 37-30 with 11 seconds left in the game.

"When you have a player like Chase you put the ball in his hands and don't overthink things," Phillian said. "In our biggest games he has his biggest moments and he saved his very best for the D-10 championship."
