Brooklyn building collapse kills at least one, traps others in wreckage

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Three-story building was at the edge of a construction site where another edifice had been demolished

Firefighters dug through the rubble of a three-story building in Brooklyn on Wednesday after it collapsed at the edge of a construction site, killing at least one person and trapping others beneath the wreckage.

Firefighters were called to the scene in the borough’s Flatbush section at about 4pm. News helicopter footage showed dozens of firefighters scouring the debris, with a group homing in on one area and appearing to use a saw to cut through the wreckage.

One person died, and four others were rushed to a hospital in critical condition, according to the New York City fire department. Six additional people were taken to hospitals with minor injuries.

The aerial videos showed firefighters digging at least one person out of the rubble and removing them on a stretcher.

The building that collapsed had been perched at the edge of a lot where another one-story building had been torn down for a construction project. The site was being excavated, and the building that fell down filled the construction pit with bricks and wood.

Another building that shared a wall with the collapsed structure remained standing, but its wall was shorn off, exposing the rooms inside, including a bedroom and closets filled with clothing.

A drone hovered over the site as firefighters dug by hand, tossing chunks of rubble aside. Rescuers also used rescue dogs to search for survivors. As darkness fell, firefighters used flashlights to illuminate the area where they were working.

Phone and email messages seeking comment were left with the architect, property owner and general contractor listed in city records as being associated with the construction project.
