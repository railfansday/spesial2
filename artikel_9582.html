Reconcile Thai Distributor Deductions Against Your Own Records with a Small Script

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Brands selling into 7-Eleven (7-11) through a Thai distributor rarely receive the full invoice value. Rebates, promotion support, markdowns, returns and penalties come off the payment. Each line may be perfectly legitimate. The trouble is that many brands check only the total, months later, when the evidence for any single line is hard to find.

This post sets out a small, repeatable method: every deduction line gets matched to something you can point to, and anything unmatched lands on a short list for the monthly review. Background on what to ask a distributor for each month is here: https://www.flowupth.com/en/insights/distributor-monthly-review?utm_source=devto&utm_campaign=P503

Example only, with made-up figures: if a month shows ten deduction lines and two are unmatched, the conversation is about those two lines and their references, which takes minutes rather than an afternoon arguing about a total.

In our work at Flowup Thailand this sits next to the monthly stock and sell-out review: one tells you whether product is moving, the other whether the money from it is arriving as agreed.

Deduction types, bases and settlement timing differ by agreement and category, and nothing here describes any retailer's internal process. Treat the script as a skeleton to adapt, and check accounting treatment with your own finance team.
