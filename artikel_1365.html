Forget Yoro And De Ligt: Pochettino Must Instantly Axe Dalot at Man Utd

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Mauricio Pochettino's first game in charge of Manchester United ended in a disappointing 4-0 defeat at Liverpool.

Looking to get off to the best possible start, Pochettino's United tenure couldn't have begun much worse. Indeed, the Reds were reduced to ten men in the eighth minute after Leny Yoro was sent off for denying a goalscoring opportunity.

A long ball over the top of the United back-line caused havoc, and after Matthias de Ligt misread the flight of the ball, Alexander Isak ran through on goal, only for Yoro to bring him down outside the box. As Isak was about to shoot and have a chance at scoring, Yoro hauled him to the floor, and referee Sam Barrott had no choice but to brandish the red card.

From the resulting free-kick, Liverpool went 1-0 up. Dominik Szoboszlai fired the ball through the crowd and into the bottom corner, giving Senne Lammens no chance and sending the Anfield crowd into delirium.

Three minutes later, Liverpool doubled their lead. Rio Ngumoha dribbled past Diogo Dalot with ease before squaring the ball across the face for Florian Wirtz to volley home from close-range.

Liverpool created a number of big chances to extend their lead further, but had to wait until the 66th minute to make it 3-0. Blistering pace from Bradley Barcola saw him steam clear of Lisandro Martinez before squaring for Ngumoha to tap home.

It proved to be another frustrating and difficult evening at the office for Dalot, who let Pochettino down with a dismal performance against Liverpool. Ngumoha - and then Victor Munoz - had so much joy against the Portuguese on Liverpool's left.

Dalot should have done so much better with his defending in the lead-up to Liverpool's second goal. He allowed Ngumoha to have too much space to glide past him on the outside and therefore couldn't get back to block the cross, which led to Wirtz scoring.

Throughout, Dalot couldn't handle Ngumoha. He wasn't able to deal with his pace and threat both on the inside and outside, and even when United defended in a compact shape, he was caught out of position time and time again.

When United pushed to pull one back in the second half, Dalot got caught high up the pitch leading to Liverpool's third goal. His man, Ngumoha, was free in aces of space at the far post and had the simplest of tasks to slot home.

Even after Ngumoha was taken off in the 78th minute, Dalot's evening didn't get much easier. Munoz moved out to the left and was effective against the out-of-form United defender, playing a one-two with Wirtz inside the channel before scoring.

Overall, Dalot was given a dire 5.3 rating by Flashscore, the lowest out of any United player on the pitch. He won just TWO out of his 11 duels, won just two tackles, committed two fouls and was dribbled past twice.

Yoro and De Ligt weren't good, but Dalot was even worse, and he should be taken out of the firing line by Pochettino going into Wednesday's Champions League trip to Sporting CP. Noussair Mazraoui should instead start at right-back.
