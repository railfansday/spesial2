Clarivate Develops IPOne in Collaboration with Leading Global Organizations

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Fortune Global 500 companies and top law firms including Mercari, Brndpwr and Sterne Kessler join Clarivate in shaping the future of AI-powered IP intelligence

LONDON, Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Clarivate Plc (NYSE: CLVT), a leading global provider of transformative intelligence, today announced that leading global corporations and IP law firms, including Mercari, Brndpwr and Sterne Kessler, are development partners for IPOne, an AI-native platform which connects across patents, trademarks and litigation intelligence.

IPOne is being developed to bring together Clarivate trusted, curated IP data with specialized AI capabilities to power IP research and workflows. Customers will be able to use IPOne directly, or integrate Clarivate intelligence into enterprise AI environments, including Anthropic's Claude, Microsoft Copilot and OpenAI's ChatGPT. It is being developed in close collaboration with corporate IP teams and leading law firms to ensure its capabilities across research, analysis, monitoring and decision support are shaped around the real-world workflows, challenges and decisions of IP professionals.

Simon Webster, President, Intellectual Property, Clarivate, said: "The future of IP intelligence will be shaped by bringing trusted data, advanced technology and human expertise together in the environments where professionals work. By developing IPOne alongside leading corporate IP teams and law firms, we are grounding our innovation in the real decisions and workflows our customers navigate every day.

"Our partners' expertise helps us build technology that is practical, responsible and designed to deliver meaningful value across the IP lifecycle."

Alongside Mercari, Brndpwr and Sterne Kessler, IPOne's development partners include some of the world's largest energy, beauty, beverage and consumer goods companies, from the U.S. and Europe to the Middle East, as well as an Am Law 100 law firm.

Hidekazu KAMINO, Intellectual Property Patent Attorney, Mercari, said: "As AI becomes increasingly embedded in intellectual property workflows, it is important that new technology is shaped by the professionals who will use it. Working with Clarivate on IPOne has shown us how trusted IP intelligence and AI can help improve the detection of potential trademark infringement and enable teams to respond more quickly and effectively."

Job van Mierlo, Founder, Brndpwr, said: "The value of AI in intellectual property depends on the quality of the intelligence behind it and how effectively it supports day-to-day decision-making. Working with Clarivate through the IPOne development partner program gives us the opportunity to help shape solutions that bring trusted data and AI together around the real challenges facing IP teams."

Robert Burger, Chief Operating Officer and Executive Director, Sterne Kessler, said: "As a development partner for IPOne, we are bringing the perspective of practicing attorneys to the creation of an intelligence platform designed specifically for IP. We believe this collaboration will help build a stronger foundation for how professionals access, interpret and apply IP intelligence, supporting more informed decisions throughout the innovation lifecycle."

IPOne is designed to extend trusted Clarivate intelligence into the tools and environments where IP professionals increasingly work; this includes Derwent patent data, Darts-ip litigation intelligence and CompuMark trademark and industrial design data. Whether used directly or accessed through the enterprise AI tools customers already use, secure integrations including MCP and API access are designed to enable organizations to embed IP intelligence into their AI environments and workflows while maintaining transparency and control.
