Triple Crossing Specialty Launches with Inland Marine Products

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The new MISSION-backed program, led by industry veteran Havis Wright, delivers tailored coverage for transportation, construction, and mobile equipment segments across the continental United States

ALPHARETTA, Ga., Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Triple Crossing Specialty, an underwriting program offering a suite of inland marine insurance products, today announced its launch on the MISSION platform. Founded by CEO Havis Wright, an underwriter with more than 20 years of experience in inland marine and property underwriting, Triple Crossing Specialty offers coverage in the continental United States.

Triple Crossing Specialty serves wholesale and retail brokers specializing in inland marine risks. The program was built for ease and speed of doing business, clear communication, and robust technical solutions. Working collaboratively with brokers to understand the nuances of each risk and develop tailored coverage strategies, Triple Crossing specializes in exposures that fall outside standard market categories, such as non-fixed mobile equipment. Through Accelerant's Risk Exchange platform, the program provides up to $25 million in capacity for admitted risks with Accelerant National Insurance Company and for non-admitted risks with Accelerant Specialty insurance company, both rated A- (Excellent) by AM Best.

"Brokers and agents are facing a variety of challenges when obtaining capacity for inland marine risks including ease of access, reliable counterparties, adequate capacity, and alignment of appetite," said Havis Wright, Founder and CEO of Triple Crossing Specialty. "Triple Crossing Specialty was created to address those challenges and offer clear communication and collaboration grounded in years of technical expertise."

"Havis brings a strong vision for what is possible and how to best serve agents and brokers seeking coverage for complex inland marine risks. We are thrilled to officially welcome his program to the MISSION portfolio," said MISSION Underwriters CEO Jim Dwane.

To learn more about Triple Crossing Specialty, visit TripleCrossingSpecialty.com.
