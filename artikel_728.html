Rihanna and Baby Rocki Snuggle Up For Adorable Lady Dior 2027 Campaign

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The singer and her 14-month-old daughter strip down to nearly nothing in the ad for the luxury brand.

Rihanna is turning her longtime gig as a brand ambassador for luxury fashion house Dior into a family affair. The “Where Have You Been” singer and her 14-month-old daughter with partner A$AP Rocky, Rocki Irish Mayers, appear together in a new 2027 ad campaign for the Lady Dior handbag. Explore Rihanna See latest videos, charts and news

In a moody monochrome video posted on the Dior site, Rihanna wears an oversized black leather jacket over a slip dress, with Rocki wearing the same, only with a crisp white diaper on her bottom while mother and daughter stare into the lens as the camera slowly pans out. In another image, Rocki leans her head on mom’s chest, thumb firmly stuck in her mouth, as the two sit next to a pink quilted $8,700 Dior handbag from the collection. An Instagram post from the campaign also features an image of Rihanna planting a kiss on Rocki’s forehead, and in a video cued to Afrobeat legend Fela Kuti’s 1972 song “Lady,” a seemingly nude Rihanna cradles a diaper-wearing Rocki to her chest while prominently displaying a black version of the Dior bag.

“A lady is a prism. In a way that light shines through a prism and becomes multidimensional, and every color performs at a different angle, there’s no one way to truly define us,” Rihanna said in a statement about the campaign.

In April, Rihanna, 38, appeared on the cover of W magazine with her daughter, crowing in a behind-the-scenes video, “The latest fashion killa just joined the set,” in reference to the title of one of partner A$AP Rocky’s hits. It was just the latest example of the Fenty Beauty boss incorporating one of her three children with Rocky into her fashion and beauty empire.

The couple’s three-year-old son, Riot Rose, made his magazine cover debut last week in the new issue of Arena HOMME+. Riot was photographed for six different spreads, showing off a series of stylish looks including a skunk-patterned fur hat, a beaded necklace and a denim-on-demin outfit accented by a wallet chain and shiny boots. Rocki was also featured in an October Savage X Fenty holiday campaign alongside brothers RZA and Riot dressed in their finest red and white Christmas pajamas.
