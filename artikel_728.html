Maluma Takes Off for Loco X Volver World Tour 2027 With Surprise Airport Show: See Where He’s Headed

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The global trek will visit more than 60 cities beginning in February.

Maluma announced his Loco X Volver World Tour 2027 with a surprise performance pop-up at Miami International Airport on Friday (Nov. 6). He also premiered the tour’s official video the same day. Explore Maluma See latest videos, charts and news

For lucky travelers waiting to board flights to Colombia, the Medellín superstar took over part of the American Airlines terminal with his band, performing “Según Quién,” “Felices Los 4,” “Hawái” and “Loco X Volver.” The airport setting echoed the themes of Loco X Volver, his May 2026 album about returning to one’s roots and reconnecting with the people and locations that helped make them who they are. “There are places that shaped my story and people who made every one of my dreams possible,” Maluma said in a press release. “Loco X Volver was born from a deep desire to go back to where it all began, to reunite with those who have grown up alongside me, and to reach new corners of the world so we can keep writing our story together. Because Maluma wouldn’t exist without my fans, without their love and without every voice that has sung my songs.”

Continued Maluma: “This tour is my way of thanking [my fans] and reminding them that no matter how far we go, wherever my people are, I’ll always be home.”

Produced by Fenix Entertainment and Cárdenas Marketing Network, the tour kicks off Feb. 5 in Bogotá and will visit more than 60 cities across Latin America, North America, Europe and Africa. Stops include Buenos Aires, Mexico City, Santiago, Lima, Miami, Los Angeles, Toronto, Madrid, Paris and Berlin. Visa will serve as lead sponsor and official payment partner for the Latin American dates.

The announcement follows a two-week campaign that left clues in Madrid, Bogotá, Buenos Aires, New York and Mexico City, including street installations inspired by Colombian vendor stands.
