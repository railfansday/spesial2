Something potentially explosive could be building for Pi Network (PI) by January 1, 2027. Microsoft Copilot AI predicts that a specific event could send PI surging to new heights in Q4.

A full, unrestricted listing of PI on Binance, including major trading pairs, deep liquidity support, and prominent placement across the exchange’s products.

In this framework, the massive existing Pi community would gain seamless access to the world’s largest crypto exchange, triggering a sharp influx of new capital, improved price discovery, and a powerful wave of speculative and utility-driven demand.

On higher timeframes, PI has been consolidating in the $0.075–$0.080 region after a prolonged sideways-to-down move from its earlier peaks. Price continues to hold above recent lows while facing overhead resistance around the $0.085–$0.10 zone.

In a standard bull market, a sustained breakout above $0.12–$0.15 would confirm intermediate strength and open the path toward higher recovery targets.

Under the extreme full Binance listing scenario outlined above, clearing significant resistance levels would likely trigger powerful measured-move extensions and multi-cycle Fibonacci projections from the longer-term base, amplified by the sudden expansion in accessibility and liquidity.

Momentum indicators sit in neutral territory, with substantial room to run into the extreme overbought readings typical of high-profile, listing-driven rallies. Key nearer-term supports sit near $0.072–$0.075 and $0.065–$0.070; holding those levels would keep the broader structure intact.
