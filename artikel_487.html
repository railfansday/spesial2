Will the Nvidia Saga Stunt Chinese AI?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Jensen Huang's shocking return to Nvidia has brought some relief to the AI trade, but the political fallout is already in motion and is set to tighten Chinese firms' access to advanced chips. Bloomberg Tech: Asia examines what it means for the US-China tech rivalry, China's AI ambitions, and the Southeast Asian economies tied to the supply chain. Annabelle Droulers spoke with Epoch AI Senior Supply Chain Researcher Isabel Juniewicz about US-China AI chip flows, and Counterpoint Research's Wei Sun about the future of Chinese internet companies and AI labs. (Source: Bloomberg)
