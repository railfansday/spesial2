Activation of the POA is an instantaneous transition, but the decline is not. Someone may approve and receive care because a specialist says that is the best he or she has to offer when the future POA can see that it has virtually no benefit, but is causing substantial distress. It may be difficult to envision if one has not been in the situation.
Unfortunately, at that stage, that is still the patient's choice to make. The person who will become POA can discuss options and try to convince them to take a different course of treatment (or lack of treatment), of course.
That basically would be telling a loved one to turn down care that a specialist is saying is all he or she has to offer (though it did not seem to be helping). The PCP eventually suggested a less aggressive approach, but the specialist was considered to be among the top docs in the state in the given specialty, and held sway.
