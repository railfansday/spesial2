Trent McDuffie proved worth in win over Packers

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The defense led the Los Angeles Rams to a victory over the Green Bay Packers on Wednesday night. Chris Shula's unit forced three takeaways and allowed just 13 points to an opposing offense that can put up points in a hurry.

One of the stronger individual performers? Trent McDuffie, one of LA's prized offseason acquisitions.

SumerSports charted 26 passing plays where McDuffie was matched up directly with a Packers receiver. You can't realistically expect nor ask for better results.

11 plays vs Matthew Golden: 2 targets, 0 catches allowed

6 plays vs Christian Watson: 0 targets, 0 catches allowed

4 plays vs Tucker Kraft: 1 target, 0 catches allowed

3 plays vs Savion Williams: 1 target, 0 catches allowed

2 plays vs Bo Melton: 1 target, 1 catch for 12 yards

While the Rams overall had trouble slowing down Watson, who accumulated four first-half receptions for 65 yards and a score before missing the second half due to injury, McDuffie was one of the defenders able to slow down his momentum. Kraft is one of the NFL's top tight ends and the Rams held him to just three catches for 24 yards. Nate Landman punched out a fumble shortly after one of those receptions for a key defensive takeaway.

All-in-all, Jordan Love finished with just 17 completions on 29 passes (59%) for 198 yards, one touchdown, two interceptions, and a passer rating of 62.1.

This was an impressive outing for the Los Angeles defense. They limited Love and company even without the team's other high-profile offseason addition at cornerback, Jaylen Watson. Emmanuel Forbes and Josh Wallace were also stingy in coverage, and that is a positive sign for the remainder of the season. Watson will not return in 2026.

Even more positive is the fact the Packers had very little production on the ground. Savion Williams was the most efficient with two carries for 18 yards. Kaleb Johnson was held to 13 yards on eight carries (1.6 average). Byron Young and Myles Garrett each made notable tackles for loss that put Green Bay behind the chains.

The Rams have now won five-straight games. Their previous four wins were against weaker competition. The defense is demonstrating they can get the job done even when facing superior quarterback talents and overall stronger opponents.

Whether that is true for the offense remains to be seen. What is obvious is that the Rams are a defensive-led team in their current state.

On Wednesday, Trent McDuffie proved his worth to a defense that is performing at a high level.
