Marc Guehi should not be in Man City's starting eleven as they aim to avoid another Premier League defeat at Nottingham Forest this weekend.

Enzo Maresca's side have lost each of their last two league outings to Aston Villa and Brighton respectively.

They returned to winning ways in the Champions League in midweek by defeating RB Leipzig 3-2.

Guehi returned to the starting eleven in Germany next to Abdukodir Khusanov after being benched following two poor showings against Villa and in their Carabao Cup penalty win against Brighton.

The 26-year-old centre-back is still yet to show his best form at the Etihad Stadium following a January switch from Crystal Palace.

He clearly does not belong in their best starting eleven right now and his performance against Leipzig should be all the proof Maresca needs to relegate Guehi back to the bench at Forest.

Guehi was a mainstay in City's backline for the first eight Premier League games of the campaign before being dropped for the first time last weekend against Brighton.

He had been hooked off at half-time in their defeat at Villa and was outperformed by Vitor Reis against Brighton in the prior cup clash.

The Citizens lost to Brighton in the league in his absence, but Khusanov impressed with the joint-most passes (85) and the second-most defensive contributions (11), while Ruben Dias had the most touches (105) and covered more ground than any of his teammates (12.2km).

Guehi came in against Leipzig alongside Khusanov, but struggled once more, with just three defensive contributions, two accurate long balls of six attempted (33 per cent) and only one aerial duel won.

The England centre-back is clearly not at his best right now, with Khusanov a definite starter and Dias possessing better leadership qualities and form than him too.

Maresca will be well aware of the outside noise around Guehi's recent performances from City fans and neutrals.

He made the decision to take him out of the firing line after a shocking first-half showing against Villa last month, and needs to continue to do so for the time being.

The Italian leapt to Guehi's defence after that Villa Park clash, but did address his shaky form, saying: "I am completely happy with Marc because the way he reacts is fantastic. He is a top guy, he is one of the captains, he is a leader and I love Marc, to be honest.

“Same as Gigio [Donnarumma], when they make some mistakes they recognise it. He has played almost all the minutes since the start of the season and so he is completely OK.”
