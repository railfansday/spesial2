Edson Alvarez & ex-West Ham teammates react as Nuno is sacked

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Edson Alvarez has liked a post confirming that Nuno Espírito Santo had been sacked as West Ham United manager.

The Hammers were widely expected to push for the Championship title this season, but, even though they are only six points away from top spot, they have had an inconsistent start to the campaign.

A disappointing 3-1 reverse at home to West Brom on Saturday meant the Irons had suffered back-to-back home defeats, and it left the Londoners sitting 8th in the Championship.

Therefore, the pressure was building on the manager, and it was announced on Tuesday morning that the former Wolves chief had been dismissed.

Interestingly, the post shared by West Ham on Instagram also prompted responses by both current and former players.

Alvarez, who has been frozen out by Nuno this season, with the Mexican international yet to play a minute for the club in the league, was seen to be liking the update.

Elsewhere, George Earthy and Callum Wilson were two former players to like the message from the club confirming Nuno’s exit.

Wilson was released on a free this summer after a difficult relationship with Nuno last season, with the boss having been ready to sanction his exit ahead of the January window before then calling on the experienced striker.

West Ham United have parted company with Portuguese head coach Nuno Espirito Santo and set their sights on Scott Parker

Meanwhile, Earthy may have hoped for a chance with his boyhood club this season following relegation, but he was sold to Reading on a permanent basis in the summer.

Of course, we don’t know the exact details of what has gone on between Alvarez and Nuno, but there’s no denying that midfield is a major issue for the team.

They are lacking a physical, defensive midfielder that would provide balance to the XI, and it’s a key reason as to why they have shipped far too many goals.

So, fans were naturally wondering why Alvarez, and Soungoutou Magassa, were not playing under Nuno, and it's something that Scott Parker, who is poised to take over, will surely look to rectify immediately.

Even if the duo wanted to leave in the summer, which seems likely, the reality is that once the window had closed, it would make sense for all parties to come together to ensure they could contribute - until January at least.

This gives an insight into the division that may have existed at West Ham under Nuno’s guidance, and it did show at times on the pitch.

Obviously, last season was very tough, but, to their credit, the Hammers did put up a fight to stay in the league after a disappointing start to the campaign.

But, this season was always going to be different. West Ham are the big fish in the Championship, and the resources they boast means anything other than promotion is a failure.

As well as that, the performances have been alarming, and it will now be down to Parker to try and unite the dressing room to help West Ham go on a run to close that gap to the top two.
