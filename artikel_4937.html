CertiK Finds 38 Hidden AI Bugs That Could Cost the Industry Millions

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Researchers at CertiK and Columbia University have discovered 38 bugs in software that performs some of the most fundamental calculations behind modern AI.  Among them are two bugs in DeepSeek’s DeepGEMM library and 23 in FlashAttention, a widely used technology that makes AI models run faster. One of these bugs went undetected through 17,760 runs
