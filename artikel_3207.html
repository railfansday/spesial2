Courtney Love Praises Nirvana’s ‘Unsung Hero’ Tracy Marander Amid Cancer Battle

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Courtney Love has praised Tracy Marander – Kurt Cobain‘s one-time girlfriend and photographer behind Bleach‘s album cover – as the “unsung hero” of Nirvana following announcements she is entering hospice and suspending treatment for her cancer. Marander’s family launched a GoFundMe last week to finance her medical bills at her request. As of Monday afternoon, the GoFundMe has raised over $80,000.

“In the early years, [Tracy] gave Kurt a home and all the space in the world to make music. She believed in him before he believed in himself,” Love wrote on Instagram. “The medical bills have piled up. The American medical system is broken. Give what you can.”

Marander’s husband, Matt Estrada, defended Love from comments under the post saying she should donate a large sum to the fundraiser. “It’s not her responsibility … Technically, it’s ours,” he wrote. “[Tracy] didn’t want to saddle us with these bills after she’s gone. So, if people want to chip in that’s fine. It’s nobody’s responsibility; not Courtney’s, not Dave [Grohl]’s, not Krist [Novoselic]’s, certainly not Frances [Bean Cobain]’s (as I have unbelievably seen as well), and not even yours. According to the GoFundMe, Neko Case, Girl Trouble, record executive Todd Moscowitz, and more are among the reported donors, though a series of anonymous donations ranging from $1,000 to $6,500 top the web fundraiser.

“[Tracy] is an INCREDIBLE person and a wonderful, real friend to people,” Case wrote on her Substack. “Her family is going to be mired with massive medical bills after she passes and it would be such a kindness to take such worry of her plate in her final days.
