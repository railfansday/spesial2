Sources: Celtic to push for Simon Ebonog deal to solve midfield problem

Celtic believe that Le Havre star Simon Ebonog can solve the lack of dynamism in their midfield, sources have told Football Insider.

The Bhoys are desperate to bring in a new centre-midfielder this winter, and have the 22-year-old among a long list of potential targets.

However, the Glasgow giants are now understood to have “singled out” Ebonog due to his tireless energy in the middle of the park, something Martin O’Neill believes his team are lacking right now.

Sources say that Celtic also want to bring in a young player as a long-term project, and Ebonog is viewed as a player with a “big ceiling” in the game.

The starlet spent time in both PSG’s youth setup and the famous Clairefontaine academy during his youth days, and is now excelling in Ligue 1 for a struggling Le Havre side.

His team are sitting bottom of the table after the opening 10 league games, but Ebonog has been an ever-present in their starting lineup.

Ebonog is under contract with Le Havre until the summer of 2029, but with the prospect of relegation from the top flight staring them in the face, they may be tempted to sell this winter.

Football Insider revealed on Wednesday that Celtic won’t have to break the bank to land the promising youngster, who is likely to be available for a fee of less than £5million.

O’Neill’s side are perfectly capable of affording a fee in that ball park, and still have money remaining after several lucrative summer sales.

The veteran boss feels that his side are lacking depth in the middle of the park at the moment, and are continuing to rely heavily on veteran Callum McGregor as a result.

Ebonog is being targeted to try and get more youthful energy into Celtic’s squad and improve the engine in the midfield.

Football Insider recently revealed that Celtic have no intention of cashing in on new midfielder Mika Baur any time soon.

The 22-year-old only joined from Paderborn in a £4.5m deal in the summer, but is already attracting interest from some top European clubs.

However, sources say that Celtic remain relaxed about Baur’s growing reputation, knowing that they hold the cards in terms of his long-term future.

The starlet was recently called up to Germany’s senior side for the first time, only for injury to force him to withdraw from the camp.
