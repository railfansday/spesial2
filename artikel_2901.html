Could Alabama basketball face Providence in 2027? Bryan Hodgson reveals talks with Nate Oats

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Another Alabama basketball reunion could be in the works for head coach Nate Oats and a former assistant.

Former Crimson Tide assistant Bryan Hodgson, now the head coach at Providence, confirmed during an interview published Friday, Nov. 6, with CBS Sports' Jon Rothstein that he and Oats have discussed a potential home-and-home series.

"Absolutely," Hodgson said when Rothstein asked about the possibility. "Nate does not duck any smoke when it comes to scheduling basketball games every year."

"They're right there in the mix for toughest nonconference schedules in the country," Hodgson continued.

While Hodgson expressed interest in hosting Alabama at Amica Mutual Pavilion in Rhode Island, he indicated that the matchup would have to wait until at least the 2027-28 season.

"I've had that conversation with Nate," Hodgson said. "Hopefully we can get something on the books."

Alabama is scheduled to visit South Florida on Dec. 16 as part of a two-for-one series originally arranged when Hodgson was coaching the Bulls, who fell 104-93 in Tuscaloosa last season. No agreement between Alabama and Providence has been announced at this time.

"I believe his (Nate's) response to me this year was, 'I've still got to go return a game to South Florida thanks to you.' So it's not happening this year, but let's talk about it for next year," Hodgson laughed.

Hodgson spent four seasons as an Alabama assistant under Oats from 2019-23 before taking head coaching positions at Arkansas State and South Florida. He was named Providence's head coach in March of this year, leaving an opening with the Red Wolves for fellow former Alabama assistant Ryan Pannone, who returns to Coleman Coliseum for a game against the Crimson Tide on Friday, Nov. 6.

"Hopefully we can get something going home-and-home with the Crimson Tide here soon," Hodgson said.
