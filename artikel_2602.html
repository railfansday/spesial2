Tony Pollard amasses 99 total yards, TD in Week 10 against Jaguars

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Tennessee Titans RB Tony Pollard carried nine times for a season-low 33 yards but added eight catches for 66 yards and a touchdown in Week 10 against the Jacksonville Jaguars.

Why It Matters: Pollard ran for 246 yards and five TDs the previous two weeks after scoring one TD in the first seven games. He was held in check on the ground against Jacksonville's elite run defense but posted season highs in receptions and receiving yards to go with his first pass TD catch of the season. Pollard will have a chance for another big game in Week 11 when he plays against his former team, the Dallas Cowboys, who gave up a franchise-record 584 yards and 46 points to the San Francisco 49ers this week.
