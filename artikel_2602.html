The telling Jaxon-Smith Njigba stat that makes 49ers-Seahawks showdown even more interesting

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The 49ers and Seahawks square off for the second time this season on Sunday.

While San Francisco beat their division rivals 32-29 in Week 5 at Lumen Field, the 49ers have their work cut out for them because of a telling Jaxon Smith-Njigba statistic.

The Seahawks wide receiver has 33 catches for 439 yards and two touchdowns in his last five games against the 49ers (including the playoffs).

Seattle wide receiver Jaxon Smith-Njigba celebrates after scoring a touchdown in the Seahawks' win over the Raiders on Nov. 15, 2026, in Las Vegas. AP Photo/John Locher

Smith-Njigba has three games with 100-plus yards against San Francisco.

The only players with more since Kyle Shanahan became 49ers head coach are Davante Adams (5) and Cooper Kupp (4).

San Francisco linebacker Fred Warner (54) leaves the field at halftime of the 49ers' blowout win over the Vikings on Nov. 22, 2026, in Mexico City. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

Evidently, San Francisco's defense hasn't been able to shut down Smith-Njigba, but the unit is coming off a strong performance against the Vikings where they limited the offense to 10 points in Mexico City.

Beyond the Smith-Njigba stat, the 49ers also will be without MVP candidate Brock Purdy, who suffered a high ankle sprain last week.

San Francisco defensive coordinator Raheem Morris reacts after a play during the second quarter of the 49ers' loss to the Falcons on Oct. 25, 2026, in Atlanta. Getty Images

San Francisco will turn to backup quarterback Mac Jones to lead the offense in the highly touted divisional matchup.
