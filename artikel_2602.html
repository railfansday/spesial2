Florida State announces special plans for Homecoming weekend

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Florida State is giving Seminole fans another reason to celebrate Homecoming weekend, with a special basketball event scheduled to kick off Friday's festivities in Tallahassee.

The Seminoles will host Green Bay on Friday, Nov. 20, at noon ET inside the Donald L. Tucker Center as part of the university's "Tip-Off to Homecoming" celebration, featuring free admission for all fans, exclusive promotions and opportunities to meet the team.

Head coach Luke Loucks and the Seminoles (4-1) enter the matchup riding a two-game winning streak following Tuesday's 85-71 victory over Ball State.

Friday's contest, originally scheduled for 8 p.m., was moved to noon to accommodate Florida State's Homecoming festivities. All seating will be general admission, and fans will not need a ticket to enter. Beyond the basketball action, Florida State has organized several promotions designed to bring the Seminole community together.

Fans can purchase a $9 Nole Lunch Deal, which includes a hot dog, chips and a drink. An in-game raffle will also feature several prizes, including a basketball jersey signed by Loucks, an autographed basketball and a Seminoles gear pack.

Following the game, fans will have the opportunity to meet Florida State players during a special postgame meet-and-greet and autograph session on the plaza outside the Tucker Center. The event will run until 3 p.m.

Friday's matchup will also serve as the men's basketball program's Seminole Heritage Game, recognizing Florida State University's longstanding relationship with the Seminole Tribe of Florida.

The basketball celebration leads into the university's annual Homecoming Parade, scheduled for 3:30 p.m., before the festivities continue Saturday when FSU football hosts NC State at Doak Campbell Stadium.

Fans attending Friday's game should plan accordingly, as parking around the Tucker Center will be significantly limited due to parade preparations. The university recommends considering alternative transportation or parking farther away, including Kleman Plaza.

For Loucks' squad, Friday offers an opportunity to extend its winning streak while helping tip off one of Florida State's biggest weekends of the year.
