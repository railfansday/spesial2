Champions League: Man United vs. Roma – probable line-ups, where to watch on TV

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Donyell Malen will lead Roma‘s attack in an away Champions League match against Manchester United.

It kicks off at 21:00 CET [20:00 GMT] at Old Trafford.

The Giallorossi have travelled to Manchester without the injured Mile Svilar, Paulo Dybala, Lorenzo Pellegrini, and Manu Koné.

Pierluigi Gollini will replace Svilar between the sticks, while Gianluca Mancini is expected to return to the XI after a 5-2 win away at Udinese on Saturday, according to Sky and other sources.

Rodrigo Mora will likely start on the bench, making room for Nicolò Pisilli, Marten de Roon and Bryan Cristante in central midfield. Emanuele Lulli or Nahuel Molina will play as right midfielder.

Gian Piero Gasperini’s side has lost only once in all competitions this season, a 4-3 Champions League defeat at home to Real Madrid on October 14.

Man United, on the other hand, last won on September 10 against Sabah FC, also in the Champions League, and lost four of the last five games across all competitions.

Amazon Prime Video will broadcast the match live in the UK and Ireland, and Paramount+ in the USA. Football Italia will provide live updates via a live blog.

Man United (4-2-3-1): Lammens; Yoro, Maguire, Lisandro Martinez, Dalot; Mainoo, Santos; Mbeumo, Tielemans, Bruno Fernandes; Cunha.

Roma (3-4-2-1): Gollini; Mancini, Ndicka, Hermoso; Lulli, De Roon, Pisilli, Wesley; Soulé, Cristante; Malen.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
