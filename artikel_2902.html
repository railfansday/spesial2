Dycom (DY -18.02%) reported third quarter results that exceeded the high end of its guidance across all primary financial metrics, including revenue, adjusted EBITDA, and adjusted EPS. Management raised the company's full-year revenue outlook to a range of $7.66 billion to $7.73 billion, supported by a record total backlog of $12.9 billion. Additionally, the company announced a new $200 million share repurchase authorization following the completion of $100.5 million in buybacks during the third quarter.
