Jordan Pickford told not to quit Everton after being axed - ex-employee

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Jordan Pickford should not be thinking about a move away from Everton with his England place under threat.

That is according to former senior Everton scout and Millwall goalkeeper, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 32-year-old has been England's number one for countless years, earning 94 caps, but his shirt now looks to be under threat.

James Trafford started against Spain on Sunday and was superb, with some now suggesting that he has usurped Pickford in the England goal.

The Leeds United keeper offers far more with his feet and gave an great example of his shot-stopping ability on Sunday, too, making two excellent double saves.

It remains to be seen whether Thomas Tuchel does now see Pickford as his number two, with the England boss playing down such talk after the game.

For years, there have been suggestions that Pickford could leave Everton for a bigger club.

But the 32-year-old has never had a reason to, with his position in the England team never in question.

Now, for the first time in a long time, it is, and the thought that Pickford could push to join a bigger club to win it back has entered some minds.

"Why would he push for a move? He's playing first team football, where he is," King told Football Insider when asked about the idea.

"If he's going to get his England place back, he's got to be playing games. His Everton place is not under threat. He's got to play first team football to keep in the frame for an England place.

"There's a shortage of England goalkeepers, let's be fair. There is a shortage. When you've got one who was, who was number two at one time, Nick Pope.

"All of a sudden, he's number three at Newcastle. Then you've got [Dean] Henderson at Palace. You've got Trafford. You've got Pickford. There isn't much that springs to mind as top English goalkeepers."

Speaking after the clash against Spain, Tuchel was keen to play down the idea of an established number one.

"There is no need for a new number one. It’s a good double save. That’s what we wanted," the German said.

"He [Trafford] had a brilliant match. It only works if you’re top from number one to 11. [He was] very good. That’s his job, to close the gap.

"We want competition in all positions. We need to see Trafford in high-pressure moments. The obvious save, where he deserves full credit, is the double save at the very end. Absolutely brilliant."
