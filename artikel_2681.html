Aaron Rodgers’ Friend Pat McAfee Drops 3-Word Message as Steelers Make Shock Decision to Bench 4-Time MVP

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Late Tuesday night, the Pittsburgh Steelers dropped a bombshell, announcing they were benching Aaron Rodgers, the four-time NFL MVP, ahead of their Week 12 matchup against the Denver Broncos. Pittsburgh holds a 6-4 record and is firmly in the AFC playoff picture, but reporting indicates it was a coaching decision to turn to Mason Rudolph.

In an even bigger surprise, Rodgers will not dress as the backup quarterback either, and has been listed as a healthy inactive for Friday's game. The decision has raised questions about the veteran quarterback's future in Pittsburgh, with his longtime friend Pat McAfee reacting to the news.

McAfee and Rodgers have shared a close friendship over the years. From playing golf together to Rodgers making regular appearances on "The Pat McAfee Show," the two have maintained a strong relationship.

Following the news of Rodgers' benching on Tuesday night, McAfee took to X and shared a three-word reaction: "This feels large."

Although the former NFL punter did not offer any further explanation, his message reflected the significance of Pittsburgh's decision and how it blindsided most of the NFL world on a Tuesday night, days before Thanksgiving.

Although Pittsburgh's 6-4 record suggests the team is still in a decent position, its recent performances have raised concerns. The Steelers are coming off back-to-back defeats, including a disappointing 38-18 loss to the Cincinnati Bengals and a 19-16 defeat to the Jalen Hurts-less Philadelphia Eagles.

Rodgers has completed 60.9% of his passes for 2,241 yards, 16 touchdowns, and six interceptions in 10 starts this season. However, he has struggled to get the offense going consistently.

His PFN QB Impact Rating of 67.1 also highlights his struggles, ranking him 29th among qualifying quarterbacks. In fact, Rodgers graded in the 60s in four of his last five games, highlighting a clear problem as the season approaches crunch time.

The veteran quarterback has particularly struggled to move the ball downfield. Over his last two games, he has averaged fewer than 5.5 yards per pass attempt. Against Philadelphia, he recorded just 0.5 air yards per attempt in the first half and missed a wide-open Jaylen Warren on a potential 30-yard touchdown.

His struggles under pressure have also become a concern. Rodgers has taken 31 sacks this season, already surpassing his total from last year (29). He has lost 257 yards on those sacks, the most in the NFL.

The Steelers have not provided a specific reason for the decision, but Rodgers' recent performances may have played a major role. McCarthy will now turn to Rudolph in the hope of giving Pittsburgh a much-needed spark.

Rudolph is familiar with Pittsburgh's offense, having gone 3-0 in three starts late in the 2023 season to help the team reach the playoffs. Friday's matchup against Denver will mark his 20th career start, though it comes against the sixth-ranked defense in terms of PFN Defense Impact.

Rodgers, 42, had already announced that 2026 would be his final NFL season. Now, with the Steelers choosing not to even keep him available as a backup in Week 12, questions remain about whether this is the final blow for a future Hall of Famer.
