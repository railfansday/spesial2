The Hoddle of Coffee: Tottenham Hotspur News and Links for Thursday, November 26

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

good morning everyone and happy thanksgiving to all those who celebrate! It's a wonderful day for feasting and watching football (the american kind – except in those odd years where harry winks scores that worldie from the halfway mark in the europa league).

For today's hoddle, we're revisiting something we did back in November 2023: ranking pies.

Back then I listed as many pies as I could and asked you, the hoddle, to vote your favourites. Some were understandably poorly rated (like fish and eel pies). Others soared to the top of the rankings.

It appears as if that poll has been lost, but we have some of the results.

First place: Shepherd's / Peasants pie (20%, 69 votes)

It was a savoury pie that won the competition, and by a good margin too. Also surprised by the kind of meat pie that won, thinking you'd prefer a steak and ale or a steak and mushroom pie. Voters did not differentiate if they wanted ground lamb or ground beef.

The bulk of the results suggest the hoddle prefers a sweet pie. That might explain why a shepherd's pie, which had less competition among its savoury counterparts, emerged atop this list. But of course the apple pie rules the sweet pie kingdom. Your hoddler-in-chief currently has 25% of one in his refrigerator. Unclear if it will still be there by Thursday evening.

Your hoddler-in-chief's favourite pie. I love the texture of the pecans and the rich gooey filling, offset by that flaky and slightly salty crust. I think it's perfect either refigerated or heated up. It's a sensation for the ages.

Key lime and strawberry rhubarb also featured near the top of the rankings, perhaps stealing some votes from the more traditional sweet Thanksgiving pies.

One person voted eel pie as their favourite, while two people voted for fish pie. It has been three years, and the voters have yet to come forward. So I demand again: Unmask yourselves!

Fitzie's track of the day: Forever, by Haim
