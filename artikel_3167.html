Why USA Rare Earth Stock Dropped Again Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Oops. USA Rare Earth just lost its buy rating.

USA Rare Earth (USAR -12.62%) stock crashed after earnings last week... and never really recovered.

Shares of the rare-earth metals-miner and rare-earth magnets-maker closed Friday trading nearly 7% below where it began last Monday. This Monday, USA Rare Earth is diving once again, down 10.5% through 12:55 p.m. ET.

And you can blame William Blair for that.

Blair analyst Neal Dingmann downgraded USA Rare Earth stock to market perform this morning, as StreetInsider.com reports, citing "a whole list of near-term challenges" including "recent feedstock shortages" that are slowing rare-earth magnet production.

Mind you, Dingmann still sees USA Rare Earth stock as undervalued, and he thinks it's worth at least $9 a share, or 23% more than it costs today -- that's the good news.

The bad news is that Dingmann also reminded investors that USA Rare Earth doesn't have a long track record as a publicly traded company. (It was only founded in 2019, and didn't go public until just last year.) The analyst cautions investors against investing in a company that hasn't yet demonstrated its ability to ramp production to fulfill its potential.

Meanwhile, USA Rare Earth's other issues continue to dog it. Most analysts agree profits won't arrive before 2028. (Dingmann now says "well into" 2028.) Free cash flow is deeply negative as the company continues to burn cash -- and no one on Wall Street seems sure when that might change.

Despite having a $3 billion market capitalization, USA Rare Earth remains a speculative stock at best. Caveat investor.
