Why Take-Two Stock's Winning the Game Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Early reviews suggest the video game specialist is rolling out its biggest hit in years.

By Danny Vena, CPA – Nov 18, 2026 at 12:48PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Take-Two Interactive Software ›

Shares of Take-Two Interactive (TTWO +5.98%) charged sharply higher Wednesday morning, gaining as much as 8.8%. As of 12:39 p.m. ET, the stock was still up 7.4%.

The catalyst that sent the video game specialist higher was the release of its latest video game, which has all the makings of a blockbuster hit.

By all accounts, the release of Grand Theft Auto (GTA) VI was always going to be an event. Fans have been waiting for more than 13 years for the next installment of what many view as the most popular and enduring video game franchise in history. And so far, the launch is living up to the hype.

Research firm Sensor Tower reports that GTA VI has already sold 17.07 million units, worth $1.65 billion, more than double the $800 million GTA V made in the first 24 hours after its release -- and the debut has only just begun. The game is being rolled out in a phased release, dropping first in New Zealand and Australia at midnight local time and becoming available in new geographies as the clock strikes 12:00.

It isn't just the pre-sales that have fans and investors excited. Early reviews of the game are wildly positive. Metacritic currently reports a near-perfect average score of 99 out of 100 (as of this writing), currently the highest score ever on the site.

A review by Luke Reilly of IGN called it, "Unprecedented. Unbelievable. Unmissable." The reviewer went on to say it was the "single most impressive video game I have ever played." GameSpot reviewer Tamoor Hussain went even further, saying GTA VI sets the bar "impossibly high," adding that it "pushes the genre to heights that, in all likelihood, no other developer will be able to reach."

Some industry sources are calling GTA VI the "biggest video game launch of all time," and possibly "the biggest entertainment launch of all time."

If the game is as big as many are predicting, the coming year could be a good one for shareholders. At less than 32 times forward earnings, Take-Two is well below its three-year average multiple of 56. While it's worth remembering the cyclicality of the video game industry, GTA VI might well be a game changer.
