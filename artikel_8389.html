SHT Day 11: A Lightbulb Moment

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Wow, was it cold last night! My little thermometer said 35 degrees at 8 am inside my tent, which means it was definitely colder earlier and outside the tent. I stay warm as long as I don’t let any gaps open in my quilt. I definitely had the straps and neck collar closed tight overnight. Because of the cold, I had a very late start. I wasn’t on trail until about 9:20.

All bundled up! The eyemask serves a dual purpose-keeping out light, and keeping the rest of my face warm.

As I lay in my tent last night looking at the next day’s plan and the map, I had an idea that I believe I could make work today. It definitely wasn’t in my original itinerary, but I think I could squeeze in a town stop. Two Harbors is nearby, and if I could get a ride into town and a room, I would only be losing four miles off my mileage for the day. Four miles that could be made up some other day.

I swear this mushroom formation was bigger than my head.

This little fella posed like this for several minutes for his photo shoot.

The trail this morning brought me up to a high point on a ridge. I checked my phone. 4 bars! Yup, let’s make this happen. I quickly did my Wordle (priorities!), and then called the Viking Motel in Two Harbors to see about a room. Whatya know, one was available! And, after inquiring about the best way to get into town, the woman who answered the phone said she could pick me up at the trailhead, which is amazing because the trailhead at Reeves Road is about six miles out of town. This is all working out!

Many footsteps have worn the rock face smooth of vegetation.

Of course, now that I know town luxuries are in my near future, I’m jolly AF. That, combined with the pleasant forest walk, temperature, and no wind, made for a very enjoyable day, indeed. I did run into a handful of SOBO thru’s, and of course we gabbed for a bit. They had just come from Two Harbors and said good things.

As I neared Reeves Road, the trail became a bit challenging before cutting out to a gravel road reroute around some logging activity. I was able to pick up my pace in order to meet my ride at our pre-arranged time of 4:00. The sweet woman who picked me up was the owner of the motel. The drive into Two Harbors was quick, and she got me checked in and oriented. I also met and loved up her cat, Cheeto, an adorable tabby orange hotel greeter.

The owner went out of her way to supply me with some quarters to get laundry started, as I only had a $20 bill (I would rather not carry the remaining weight in quarters!), and she even agreed to take me back to the trailhead in the morning. I’m blown away by her kindness. I thank her profusely and promise to pay her back for the quarters once I can break my $20 at a gas station.

I had plenty of time left in the day for the typical town chores-shower first, then a very short walk down the same side of the street was the gas station, Subway, and laundromat. I had been eyeing the Culvers at first, but it was further away and on the other side of busy Highway 61. I got my clothes started in the wash, then grabbed a sandwich from Subway, and some drinks, snacks, and cheddar jalapeno Cheetos (wonder where I got that idea) from the gas station. I returned to the laundromat and ate my dinner there while my clothes cooked.

Back at the motel, I took care of my blisters, and organized my stuff. I heard a noise that sounded like water being sprayed and thought at first that maybe someone was watering the flowers outside. I peeked out my door and it was pouring! I sighed with a deep feeling of relief. What a great idea to come into town, clean up, and get out of the elements for a bit. I sure am going to enjoy this warm, comfy bed tonight.
