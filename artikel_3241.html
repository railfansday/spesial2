Sotera Health Company (SHC -9.64%) reported third-quarter results marked by steady top-line growth across its Sterigenics and Nelson Labs segments, even as the Nordion business faced expected headwinds from cobalt-60 harvest timing. Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 constant currency revenue and Adjusted EBITDA guidance despite identifying recent softness in the European medical device market that is expected to persist into the fourth quarter. The company continued its deleveraging efforts, reaching a net leverage ratio of 2.9x, which contributed to a recent credit rating upgrade. Operational focus remains on completing ethylene oxide facility upgrades and expanding capacity at the North Carolina X-ray facility to meet long-term demand.
