The Hunger Games Movies Are Officially Being Eliminated From Free Streaming

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Hunger Games' sixth installment, Sunrise on the Reaping, isout, and it’s already a certified hit. The Francis Lawrence-directed dystopian drama takes fans back to the 50th Hunger Games, where sixteen-year-old Haymitch Abernathy (Joseph Zada) is taking part in the games. The 67% Rotten Tomatoes-rated feature is being hailed for its performances and visual direction, while fans rated it even higher at 94%. It has earned $253.2 million worldwide on a $150 million budget, making it a commercial and critical success. After The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes, a prequel following a young Coriolanus Snow, the latest feature has brought the franchise back to its box office dominance era.

The Hunger Games franchise started back in the 2000s, capitalizing on the viewers’ interest in the YA fantasy genre, which also includes the likes of Harry Potter, The Twilight Saga, and The Chronicles of Narnia. What distinguishes the Hunger Games franchise from its peers is the world-building where class divide, disparity, and an authoritarian system reign supreme. The themes of media manipulation, propaganda, violence as spectacle, and the cost of war, among others, resonated with fans worldwide. Resulting in the four movies collectively grossing approximately $2.97 billion worldwide. Fans who’d love to revisit the future, buckle up as the Hunger Games franchise, including The Ballad of Songbird and Snakes, is soon leaving free platform Tubi at the end of November.

Adapted from Suzanne Collins' novel trilogy of the same name, in The Hunger Games, we follow District 12 teenager Katniss Everdeen (Jennifer Lawrence), who volunteers to take her sister’s place in the upcoming games, surviving alongside fellow tribute Peeta Mellark (Josh Hutcherson). With her win, she sparks nationwide defiance against the Capitol. In Catching Fire, President Snow (Donald Sutherland) forces past victors back into a deadly 75th Quarter Quell arena, where underground rebels rescue Katniss.

Like Harry Potter, the final book was divided into two movies. Mockingjay – Part 1 sees Katniss become the propaganda symbol of the revolution while rescuing a brainwashed Peeta from the Capitol. Finally, in Mockingjay – Part 2, full-scale war engulfs the Capitol. Katniss assassinates both Snow's regime and manipulative rebel leader Alma Coin (Julianne Moore), dismantling tyranny before returning home to build a peaceful life. The franchise has an illustrious cast including Liam Hemsworth, Woody Harrelson, Elizabeth Banks, Stanley Tucci, and more.
