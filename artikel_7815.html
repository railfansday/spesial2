Homeland Security Task Force Investigation Leads to Arrest of Chinese National, 2 Others Charged with Multimillion-Dollar Counterfeit Goods Smuggling from China to L.A., Long Beach Ports

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Three defendants were arrested today on a nine-count federal indictment charging them with coordinating the shipment of tens of millions of dollars’ worth of counterfeit and other contraband from China to the Ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach to unauthorized locations where the smuggled cargo containers were unloaded and stuffed with filler cargo that was then presented to United States customs officials for inspection.
