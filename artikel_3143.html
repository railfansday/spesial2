Allison Transmission (ALSN) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Legacy transmission business hit record quarterly revenue amid Off-Highway integration.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Allison Transmission Holdings, Inc. (ALSN +4.67%) reported record quarterly revenue for its legacy transmission business while integrating its newly acquired Off-Highway Drive & Motion Systems unit. Management reported that the company is transitioning from a component manufacturer to an integrated propulsion systems provider, which allows for increased content per vehicle across conventional, hybrid, and electric platforms. The company increased its full-year 2026 guidance for the second consecutive quarter, citing accelerated demand for North American vocational trucks and robust activity in global mining markets. Despite inflationary pressures in raw materials such as steel and aluminum, the company maintained positive price-cost realization and focused on debt reduction following the significant acquisition at the beginning of the year.
