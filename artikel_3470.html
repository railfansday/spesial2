Netflix Is Officially Removing Hulu’s Best 2-Part Stephen King Thriller

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Stephen King's writing has inspired countless movies and television shows, and that trend shows no signs of slowing down. It: Welcome to Derry provides some surprising backstory for one of King's biggest novels, particularly the fact that the titular town of Derry's been terrorized for years by the malevolent Pennywise. Mike Flanagan has built his career on adapting King's work, including his adaptation of Carrie and a planned television series for The Dark Tower.Last year,The Long Walk andThe Running Man hit theaters. However,one of King's most underrated adaptations is set to leave Netflix in less than a month.

That series is Castle Rock. Originally airing on Hulu, Castle Rock is unique because it isn't based on a single King book or short story. Instead, it's set in the fictional town of Castle Rock, where most of King's stories take place. Despite its seemingly original story, Castle Rock pulls characters from King's work into a swirling web of murder, mystery, and magic. Horror fans will have to move quickly, as Castle Rock ​​​​​​departs Netflix on December 14. They won't want to miss it, because this series does something new with King's work rather than being a straight adaptation.

From the jump, Castle Rock isn't shy about showcasing characters and locations from Stephen King's work. Season 1's central mystery lies within Shawshank State Penitentiary, the titular prison in The Shawshank Redemption. Aspiring writer Jackie Torrance

(Jane Levy) is the niece of The Shining's protagonist, Jack Torrance, and Season 2 takes things a step further by introducing Lizzy Caplan as Annie Wilkes, who becomes the main villain in Misery. Co-showrunner Sam Shaw would later explain that he and Dustin Thomason wanted each season of Castle Rock to feel like its own separate story.

"The plan for the show, in the beginning, was to tell a different kind of original Stephen King story every season. This season is a ten hour story with a beginning, a middle, and an end, and we hope we will have a satisfying conclusion to all the trajectories that get introduced."

Indeed, both seasons of Castle Rock feel like two distinct stories. Season 1 focuses on Henry Deaver (André Holland), who's drawn back to Castle Rock after a mysterious Shawshank prisoner referred to as "The Kid" (Bill Skarsgard) requests his presence. As Henry uncovers the Kid's origins, he deals with the shadows of his past, including the death of his adoptive father. Season 2 deals with Annie's descent into mass murder while incorporating elements from Salem's Lot. Though Castle Rock lasted only two seasons, it captured the feeling of a good Stephen King novel.

Fans who enjoy Castle Rock or are sad that it's leaving Netflix are in luck, as the streamer has another great King adaptation in the form of 11.22.63. Like Castle Rock, 11.22.63 originally debuted on Hulu and was produced under J.J. Abrams' Bad Robot banner. But while Castle Rock is merely inspired by Stephen King's work, 11.22.63 is a straight adaptation of one of his novels, in which teacher Jake Epping (James Franco) discovers a portalto 1963 in his friend's diner. Jake is tasked with stopping the assassination of John F. Kennedy, entering a literal race against time and a thriller that'll have viewers on the edge of their seats.

One of Castle Rock's stars would reveal the plot for Season 3 last year, which would have hewed closer to one of King's novels than previous seasons. At Emerald City Comic Con in 2025, Jane Levy said plans were in place for Castle Rock to center on Jackie Torrance, especially since Season 1 ends with her on a journey to learn more about her family. Castle Rock proves that Stephen King's work can inspire unique stories beyond adaptations, as its approach showed a deep appreciation for allhis books.
