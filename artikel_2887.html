Nvidia Has Incredible News for Artificial Intelligence (AI) Investors. Here Is My Top Stock to Buy Before It Is Too Late

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The massive cloud backlog that Nvidia noted on its latest earnings call bodes well for this incredibly cheap AI infrastructure stock.

By Harsh Chauhan – Nov 24, 2026 at 2:07AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Dell Technologies ›

Artificial intelligence (AI) bellwether Nvidia (NVDA +31.48%) released results for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 on Nov. 17, and the company's numbers and guidance clearly suggest that the AI build-out will continue at an impressive pace.

Nvidia's revenue increased by 103% year over year to $115.8 billion, driven primarily by a 110% increase in data center revenue to $107.6 billion. Even better, Nvidia increased its fiscal 2028 revenue growth estimate from 70% to 75%. The company noted that demand for its chips is outstripping supply and said the trend could continue due to a huge backlog cloud computing providers are sitting on.

Specifically, Nvidia says that the cloud industry's backlog increased by $500 billion sequentially in the previous quarter to a whopping $2.5 trillion. As a result, Nvidia sees a "significant multiyear opportunity" for itself and its partners. That's why we will take a closer look at the prospects of Dell Technologies (DELL +20.46%), an Nvidia partner that could win big from the $2.5 trillion cloud backlog.

Dell Technologies' infrastructure solutions group (ISG) has been growing at an incredible pace, driven by phenomenal demand for the company's AI-optimized servers. Dell's ISG revenue increased by an impressive 89% year over year in the second quarter of fiscal 2027 to $31.8 billion. Its revenue from AI servers doubled year over year to $16.4 billion. What's more, traditional servers and networking revenue increased 122% from the year-ago period to $10.5 billion.

Dell's servers are used to mount chips produced by Nvidia. In fact, Dell claims that it was the first company to ship Nvidia's Vera Rubin chip systems. So, the company is a direct beneficiary of Nvidia's impressive growth. Also, Dell's server rack solutions are the backbone of AI data centers, helping hyperscalers and AI companies integrate a range of chips and cooling systems to run AI workloads seamlessly.

This puts Dell in a solid position to capitalize on the $2.5 trillion backlog mentioned earlier. Not surprisingly, the AI server market that Dell serves is poised for a big boom next year. Gartner estimates that AI server spending could surge by 81% in 2027 to $957 billion. Importantly, Dell already has a strong enough backlog that should help it capitalize on this massive opportunity.

The company's AI server backlog was $95 billion at the end of fiscal Q2. That should be enough for Dell to sustain its stunning AI revenue growth next year. Dell anticipates a 3x increase in AI server revenue to $74 billion in fiscal 2027. Its backlog already points to a nice improvement next year, though don't be surprised to see Dell's AI server revenue multiplying once again.

That's because Dell management says that its order pipeline is in "multiples of our backlog." As such, I won't be surprised to see Dell's revenue growth outpace analysts' expectations in fiscal 2028 (ending in January 2028). Specifically, analysts expect Dell's revenue to increase by 71% in fiscal 2027 to $194.3 billion, followed by a 20% increase in fiscal 2028 to $232.6 billion.

However, as the chart below shows, Dell's revenue estimate for fiscal 2028 has been rising, and I won't be surprised to see the trend continue.

DELL Revenue Estimates for Current Fiscal Year data by YCharts

Dell stock can be bought at an incredibly cheap valuation right now. The stock is trading at just 19 times earnings, which is incredibly cheap considering that its earnings per share are predicted to increase by 151% in the current fiscal year. What's more, the stock has a price-to-sales ratio of just 1.5, well below the tech-focused Nasdaq Composite index's price-to-sales ratio of 4.5.

These multiples clearly indicate that Dell is undervalued, and that makes this AI stock a screaming buy. For instance, if Dell's revenue increases to $233 billion in fiscal 2028 and it trades at even 2 times sales, its market cap will reach $466 billion. That's 119% higher than Dell's current market cap, suggesting that investors have the opportunity to buy a potential multibagger at dirt cheap levels.
