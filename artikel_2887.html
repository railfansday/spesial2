Brock Purdy: Practicing at altitude was the right move, for sure

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

When the 49ers play the Vikings this weekend, they’ll do so at the altitude of Mexico City at Estadio Banorte.

That’s why the team is in Colorado Springs this week to practice at the Air Force Academy to get acclimated. The altitude at Colorado Springs is comparable to that of Mexico City, at over 7,000 feet above sea level.

Quarterback Brock Purdy told reporters on Thursday that he appreciates being at altitude this week to prepare.

“It’s a real thing. I think it’s been nice to be here, practice and get some workouts in,” Purdy said, via transcript from the team. “Even just obviously doing meetings and just living here for a week. It’s been a real thing that we’ve had to adjust to, and I’m glad that we’re here. I think it’s the right move, for sure. Obviously, Mexico City is going to be in a higher elevation than this, but overall, it’s going to be tough for everybody. We’re not living in it year-round or anything like that, so I don’t know. It’s going to be a part of the game for sure.”

Purdy was on the team the last time the 49ers played in Mexico City back in 2022, so he has some understanding of what awaits him. But that was before Jimmy Garoppolo’s injury put Purdy in line to start.

“Obviously, I didn’t play, but just warming up and being there in the game, seeing guys come off the field and seeing them pretty exhausted. It’s a real thing and I think we’re all ready for it,” Purdy said. “For me, I remember those kinds of things, so I’ve just got to be on top of it and prepare for it.”

Kickoff between the Vikings and 49ers is set for 8:20 p.m. ET on Sunday night.
