2 Chainz Shares Health Update After Cybertruck Crash, Reveals He Suffered Broken Ribs

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

"I wasn't drinking, smoking, sleeping or on auto-pilot," the Atlanta rapper claimed alongside footage from the accident.

2 Chainz was involved in a frightening car accident, but assured fans he’s resting and doing OK while recovering. The Atlanta native posted footage of the gruesome scene that totaled his orange Tesla Cybertruck on Tuesday (Nov. 24), while also revealing he suffered broken ribs from the crash.

Chainz claimed that there was no wrongdoing on his part and another driver swerved across the double-yellow line to avoid an opossum, which led to a head-on collision.

“A lot of calls and concern on my well being , i appreciate everyone who reaching out , im good , just resting with broke ribs,” he wrote on IG. “No I wasn’t drinking,smoking ,sleeping or on auto pilot , the other driver dodged a possum and hit me head on , smh , hope that answers all of your questions , Blessed.”

Plenty of 2 Chainz’s hip-hop peers reached out to send their well-wishes following the accident. “Dam bro🙏🏽🙏🏽🙏🏽 Glad u was in the tank💪🏽 God Bless u,” DJ Paul wrote. “That other driver should’ve been on FSD, it saved my life a few times.”

DJ Premier added: “May Strength and Power be returned to your physical being. Glad it’s not worse!”

Busta Rhymes said: “Blessings King. Grateful that you are good family. Prayers up, sending light and wishing you a speedy recovery family. We need you King.”

Mannie Fresh quipped: “Hope u didn’t mess up them polo pants lil brother!”

The “No Lie” rapper posted on his Instagram Story hours later on Tuesday night, showing off the hefty amount of medication and painkilling pills he was prescribed, but had a wider message for fans about how easy it is for some to get hooked on pharmaceutical drugs like opioids.

“This what’s wrong with America right here,” he stated while taking a video of the pill bottle. “I had a little accident and they gave me all these. I guess they want me to become addicted to big pharma. You know the smaller they are, the more geeked you gon’ be. What I need all this s–t for?”

The 46-year-old was involved in a scary car crash in Miami at Art Basel in December 2023, which resulted in him being hospitalized for his injuries.

On the music front, Chainz is gearing up for the release of his Playaz Only Live Once album, which is fully produced by Statik Selektah and arrives on Dec. 4. He’s released a trio of singles: “Reign,” “A Week in Paris” and “Situation” featuring Big Sean, Erykah Badu and Aasher Saintt.

The rest of the project is littered with star-studded appearances from the likes of Mary J. Blige, T.I., Leon Thomas, Cash Cobain, Young Nudy, Vory, Macy Gray, Westside Gunn, Muni Long, CeeLo Green, The-Dream, 6LACK and Dolla Boy.

2 Chainz’s last solo album arrived with Dope Don’t Sell Itself in February 2022, which reached No. 23 on the Billboard 200. He teamed up with The Alchemist and Larry June for Life Is Beautiful in February 2025.

Billboard has reached out to 2 Chainz’s reps for further comment.
