The one key question on which nearly all West Ham fans are in agreement

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

It is not often that West Ham fans appear unified over anything, after recent seasons of strife, division, disagreement and dispute with the Sullivan and Brady leadership, it feels as if ‘disharmony’ has been the default setting.

Not, it seems, about everything. There is one hot topic on which a huge majority of West Ham fans are in almost total agreement.

According to the NY Times’ fan survey, the biggest consensus was not for the approval of the new manager – although there is a pretty strong 77% majority of fans who responded who give the appointment of Scott Parker the thumbs up.

The biggest single factor on which most West Ham supporters agree is the choice of goalkeeper. According to the survey:

“Of the fans surveyed, an overwhelming 85.4 per cent believe Alphonse Areola should reclaim the No 1 jersey from Mads Hermansen.

Likeable yes, dependable no: 85% of West Ham fans want Hermansen out of the team

Interestingly, though, a sizeable 62 per cent of supporters believe the travelling contingent at Ewood Park were not right to boo Hermansen.”

Eighty five percent of fans have had enough of the Dane’s inept performances in between the sticks, with one fan driven to comment that West Ham’s goal would be better defended simply by hanging a green jersey on the cross bar rather than continuing to select the out of form Hermansen.

Of course the fly in the ointment is that Areola has been reported as ‘unfit’ – whether that means third choice Finlay Herrick comes into consideration – who can say- but it’d be a brave selection for Parker to leave his number one on the bench if Areola isn’t ready for the Preston North End fixture next weekend, however much it seems supporters want to see Hermansen out of the team.
