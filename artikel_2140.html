Celtic star Luke McCowan 'will end up agreeing January move' - expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Celtic will give serious consideration towards sanctioning a loan move away for midfielder Luke McCowan in January.

That is according to former Aberdeen chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the 28-year-old still has plenty to give to another Scottish Premiership club.

McCowan was heavily criticised for Celtic's exit in Champions League qualifying back in August, and has since played just 87 minutes of football.

Wyness believes that the Bhoys will loan the former Dundee man out this winter, in the hope that they will be able to sell him next summer.

Martin O'Neill recently suggested that McCowan still has a future at Parkhead, but he has not received the level of football to back that up of late.

The veteran made 42 appearances for Celtic in all competitions last season, but is now viewed as little more than a bit-part player.

Aberdeen's former chief Wyness - who served as CEO at Pittodrie between 2000 and 2004 and now runs a consultancy business advising elite clubs - believes that Celtic's treatment of their squad players has been poor of late.

Anthony Ralston is another loyal servant to the club who has also been forced onto the fringes of the group.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained how McCowan's future at Celtic is likely to look.

"The way I'm looking at that is that your top stars, those situations tend to fix themselves in some way, you know, either the agents are pressing, or the club does something and that gets fixed," he said.

"It's the players who are the 15th to 20th players in the squad that you've got to look at and how the club treats them. And it's the values of the club that dictate how those players get treated when there isn't pressing demand from fans.

"McCowan fits into that category as far as I'm concerned. They've got to either show that they're going to back him, and then he's going to feel that they can go forward.

"But, I think it will end up being a January loan to try and get some sort of valuation going forward. Loans can work well in Scotland. And I'm sure there is still a talented player in there that needs a chance to go and prove it.

"But again, it speaks to what Celtic are doing in terms of values around those squad players that made some big mistakes. With a few of them recently, they're not treating a lot of the squad well."

Football Insider also revealed on Friday that O'Neill could be sacked by Celtic if they lose their position at the top of the Scottish Premiership.

The board view the recent defeat to Falkirk as a huge missed opportunity, given that victory could potentially have sent them seven points clear at the top.

Sources say that the lack of consistency is concerning the Celtic hierarchy, and O'Neill could ultimately pay the price by losing his job.

Rangers are just four points behind Celtic in the table with a game in hand at the moment, and if they are leapfrogged by their bitter rivals, then it could provide the trigger for the boss' exit.
