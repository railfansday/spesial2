‘Trinity’ Sets Release Date At Netflix; First Look & Trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Netflix has slotted Tuesday, December 1 for the premiere of Trinity, its upcoming eight-episode drama series, starring Gugu Mbatha-Raw and Richard Madden. The streamer also released several first-look images (see below) and the first trailer, which you can see above.

Writter by Jed Mercurio, Trinity revolves around a heroic female naval officer (Mbatha-Raw) becomes involved with the outwardly charismatic secretary of defense (Madden), only to discover he may be at the heart of a dangerous conspiracy.

Cast also includes Marcia Gay Harden, JD Pardo, Bruce Greenwood, Robert Wisdom, Kirk Acevedo, Ben Cotton, James Remar, Jason Ralph and Mark O’Brien.
