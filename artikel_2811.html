Dowman's late winner as England Under-21s beat France

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Mikey Moore scored in Tottenham's EFL Cup win against Charlton earlier this season - and he got England's youngsters off to a flyer against France [Getty Images]

Max Dowman's late strike gave England Under-21s a dramatic friendly win over France - after the Young Lions let a 2-0 lead slip away.

The 16-year-old Arsenal prospect, making his first start for England at this level, tapped in to spare the blushes of hosts who had allowed their visitors to get back on terms late on.

First-half goals from Mikey Moore and Divin Mubama gave European champions England a comfortable lead, a positive sign as they look for a third successive continental title in Serbia and Albania next year.

But keeper Tommy Setford's own goal handed France a late lifeline, and Giovani Versini then equalised with five minutes left, before Dowman struck.

The Young Lions will discover their Euro 2027 opponents on 26 November, with the draw held in Belgrade, yet boss Lee Carsley will not work with his squad again until March, leaving few chances to impress the head coach.

After being frustrated in Friday's 1-0 defeat by Portugal, the hosts produced a composed and dominant display in Bristol as Moore scored his first England Under-21 goal after six minutes.

The Tottenham forward has yet to score on loan at Cologne, who are fighting a relegation battle in the German Bundesliga, but here he calmly rolled past Mathieu Patouillet to cap a flowing move.

Manchester City forward Mubama, on loan at Southampton this season, then added a second England goal after 29 minutes when he nodded in Ethan Nwaneri's deep cross.

Nwaneri was twice denied in the second half as England chased a third goal, with Jovan Makama also testing Patouillet.

England looked comfortable, but Setford's error, when he dropped Versini's looping effort, allowed France a way back.

Versini's excellent 25-yard strike levelled the game, only for Dowman to have the final say when he scored from close range with a minute left.
