Aidan Hutchinson injury: Lions star hurts knee during costly celebration

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Update: The Detroit Lions have officially ruled Aidan Hutchinson out with a knee injury.

Aidan Hutchinson dragged down quarterback Drake Maye to cut short the New England Patriots' opening possession Sunday in. Germany. The sack forced Mike Vrabel to send his field goal team into the game, ending what was a promising drive for the Patriots to start the game.

But the post-sack celebration might prove to be costly for the Detroit Lions star.
