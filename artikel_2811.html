How to watch Nebraska volleyball vs. Indiana: Time, TV channel

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Nebraska Cornhuskers will face the Indiana Hoosiers in the Big Ten Volleyball Tournament on Sunday. The Hoosiers defeated the UCLA Bruins in five sets (25-22, 22-25, 13-25, 25-21, 15-9) to advance.

Nebraska enters the tournament as the No. 1 seed and clinched the outright Big Ten regular-season title with a sweep against Illinois. It was the program's 38th conference title and seventh Big Ten crown.

The Huskers ranked No. 1 in the latest AVCA Coaches Poll, released Nov. 16. It marks Nebraska's 136th week at No. 1 in the AVCA Coaches Poll, the most of any program in the nation.

This is the 28th meeting between these two programs, with Nebraska leading the series 26-1. Since joining the Big Ten in 2011, Nebraska is 25-0 against Indiana.

These two played on October 8, with Nebraska sweeping Indiana (25-15, 25-19, 25-19) in Bloomington.
