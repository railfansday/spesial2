Miami vs Notre Dame live updates: Time, TV, predictions for Week 10 game

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The date has been circled since before the schedules even came out. Tonight, the undefeated No. 1 Miami Hurricanes are out to prove last year was no fluke against a No. 6 Notre Dame team that feels perpetually wronged.

Last year's epic showdown to kick off the calendar saw Miami win 27-24 in their home stadium, where they would end the season five months later in the national championship game. Notre Dame remains bitter over the Canes leapfrogging them for the final College Football Playoff spot at the last minute, and now that they're 11th in the initial CFP rankings despite a 7-1 start, even Touchdown Jesus might not be able to contain the fans' bloodlust.

A showdown expected to feature three Heisman contenders will have to settle for two after Malachi Toney's ankle injury during practice. It's the Darian Mensah show now for Miami's top-ranked offense, while CJ Carr is ready to show how much he's grown since his three-touchdown day in last year's meeting.
