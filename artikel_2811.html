Boys basketball: Chargers upend Pirates in season opener

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

After trailing by one point to Greensburg at the end of the first quarter, North Decatur responded and led at the end of each quarter, including the final one as the Chargers knocked off the Pirates 63-57 in the season opener for both squads.

Greensburg opened the scoring with a bucket by Jacoby Miller. North answered with seven straight points. A jumper by Owen Rennekamp, left-wing 3-pointer from Wyatt Reisman and drive by Rennekamp had North up 7-2.

Greensburg's Max Buening stopped the run with a 3-pointer from the top of the key, but Reisman answered with a left-corner 3-pointer. After Greensburg's Jacoby Miller and North's Rennekamp traded 3-pointers, Logan Simpson scored for the Pirates to cut the deficit to three points.

A drive to the bucket by Jacoby Miller and 3-pointer from Buening gave Greensburg the lead 15-13. Troy Brown's bucket for North tied the game. Buening's free throw closed the first quarter with Greensburg leading 16-5.

A rebound bucket by Brown and two free throws by Reisman had the Chargers back in front 19-16 to start the second quarter. Two Jacoby Miller free throws and a traditional three-point play by Simpson gave the lead back to the Pirates.

North answered with a right-wing 3-pointer by Henry Kinnker and 3-pointer from Rennekamp to grab a 25-21 lead. After a Jacoby Miller 3-pointer, North's Reisman closed the first half with a free throw to give the Chargers a 26-24 lead.

Early in the third quarter, Greensburg's Buening had a steal and finished with a dunk to cut the deficit to 28-27. North went on a 12-0 run. A Kinker 3-pointer started the spurt and Rennekamp's steal and layup capped it with North on top 40-27.

Buckets by Buening and Bryson Chilton cut the deficit to 40-31. The Chargers ended the third quarter on a 6-2 run. A buzzer beater by North's Davis Scripture put the Chargers on top 46-33 heading to the fourth quarter.

A traditional three-point play by North's Brown opened the final eight minutes. Greensburg answered with a 3-pointer from the top of the key by Simpson. A Reisman 3-pointer and inside bucket by Brown extended the lead to 18 points for the Chargers.

A 9-2 run by the Pirates cut the deficit to 56-45. Buening's 3-pointer started the run and Jacoby Miller ended it with a layup. Back-to-back buckets in the paint by Brown extended the lead to 15 points for North.

Greensburg put together another run. This one was 7-1 with Charlie Parmer's free throw the lone point for North. A pair of Buening free throws with :29.9 left in the game cut the deficit to 61-52. Rennekamp hit a pair of free throws to put North back up by double figures with :23.8 to play.

An Adam Powers 3-pointer and layup by Jacoby Miller closed out the scoring with North coming out on top 63-57.

For North, Brown led the Chargers with 21 points. Reisman was next with 17 points and Rennekamp finished with 16 points. Kinker added six points. Scripture scored two points and Parmer added a free throw.

For the Pirates, Buening led in scoring with 21 points. Jacoby Miller finished with 16 points and Simpson had 12 points. Powers added five points followed by Chilton two and Levi Muckerheide one.

First-year head coach Brandon Tucker and the Rushville Lions opened the boys' basketball season hosting Shelbyville.

The Golden Bears led by five points at the half but pulled away in the fourth quarter to post the 59-45 victory. Shelbyville held a 16-9 lead after the first quarter and 23-18 advantage at the half. The Golden Bears stretched the lead to 10 points 41-31 heading to the fourth quarter.

For the Lions, Zy Adams led in scoring with 16 points. Owen Zachery was also in double figures with 10 points.

Emmett Knecht added seven points. Luke Jackman had six points. Ryder Flannery and Braydin Pease both chipped in with two points.

The Lions host Connersville (1-0) Friday.

The Rushville freshman team battled through a tough shooting night to hold off Shelbyville 30-24. Brinton Himes led the Lions' scoring attack with 13 points. Joey Aldworth finished with a double-double: 10 points, 12 rebounds, and five steals. Kellen Rankin added four points. Drake Viera scored three points.

The Lions freshman team travel at 6 p.m. Monday to Greensburg for an EIAC matchup with the Pirates.
