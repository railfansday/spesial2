Draymond Green offers Yaxel Lendeborg reality check after Warriors' latest loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Draymond Green offers Yaxel Lendeborg reality check after Warriors' latest loss originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area

After the Warriors' 115-94 loss to the New Orleans Pelicans on Monday, veteran forward Green suggested that perhaps the team and its fans are expecting too much out of rookie Yaxel Lendeborg.

"Right now, we're putting too much pressure on him. It's tough to deal with," Green told ESPN's Anthony Slater. "Depend on him, but lower the expectation."

The last two Warriors losses have been their first two games without Steph Curry since he exited Wednesday's matchup with the Houston Rockets with an oblique/abdominal injury, a game Golden State would go on to lose 115-108.

A season that started with such promise and optimism for the Warriors very quickly turned into one of concern and anxiety with the absence of Curry and Golden State's lackluster performances in the two-plus games without him, and many fans are looking to Lendeborg to step up from his already Rookie of the Year-caliber season to carry the load left behind by Curry.

But Green said it is unrealistic to expect Lendeborg to shoulder the burden Curry often does on the court.

"It's unfair to him to try to make him [Lendeborg] step into Steph's role 13 games into his career," Green said. "So depend on him like we need to, but lower what we expect out of him."

Instead, Green wants to see the Warriors focus on the little things.

"Just gotta play," Green said. "We can control what we can control. We can't control the world."

Tuesday's loss in New Orleans was Golden State's second blowout loss in a row after the Spurs demolished the Warriors 123-85 on Friday in San Antonio. Lendeborg scored just three points on 1-of-13 shooting, and spoke very candidly about how hard he took the loss.

"I'm not going to move past this," Lendeborg said. "I don't know about the rest of us. Everybody is saying throw it out the window, I can't throw a damn thing out the window. We just lost by like a hundred. I'm f–ked up mentally right now. I don't have nothing to learn. We got the s–t beat out of us… I was flustered the entire game. Couldn't make a damn layup."

The Warriors return to action on Wednesday against the Dallas Mavericks at American Airlines Center, where they seek their first win since losing Curry to injury.
