Celtic defender Kieran Tierney was superb as Scotland beat North Macedonia 2-1 in the Nations League on Friday.

John McGinn's double saw Sebastien Pocognoli's side over the line, with the Belgian earning the second win of his tenure.

The home side should have made it far more comfortable, missing several big chances, but will be happy to have got over the line.

Tierney was deployed at left centre-back in a back three, and delivered a solid performance in the same way he has all season for Celtic.

The 29-year-old has been a constant source of reliability for Martin O'Neill this season, despite a inconsistent start to the campaign.

The former Arsenal man has been excellent since returning to Celtic in 2025, and continues to be a main man for both club and country.

Tierney has been playing for the senior Scotland side for over 10 years now, and has remained a key player for much of it.

Friday's match saw him produce yet another superb display as the Tartan Army got over the line against North Macedonia.

The full-back completed 91 per cent of his passes and created two chances, as well as taking a shot.

More impressively, Tierney completed an extraordinary 17 passes into the final third - at least eight more than any other player.

He was excellent defensively too, making two tackles, five clearances, three interceptions, two recoveries and winning five duels.

The Scotsman rated the defender a seven out of 10, and wrote: "The Celtic wing-back played on the left of a three and showed good composure, covering diligently for [Andy] Robertson. He headed over on 22 minutes. A good display."

Edinburgh News came to a similar conclusion, and added that he was "positionally sound throughout and his range of passing was good."

The Herald gave Tierney a six, and added: "The Celtic defender was kept busy in the latter stages of the match but managed to keep [Darko] Churlinov quiet. He supplied the skipper with some excellent balls."

During his time at Arsenal, Tierney was constantly plagued by persistent and nagging injuries.

The defender undoubtedly had the talent to perform for the Premier League giants, but managed to make more than 30 appearances in a season just twice in six years with the Gunners.

Yet since returning to Celtic, Tierney appears to have been given a new lease of life and looks to be in his prime.

The defender made 53 appearances last season and is on course to do similar over the next year, with his career revitalised.
