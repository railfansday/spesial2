Jenna Ushkowitz Says ‘Glee’s Cory Monteith Tribute “Wasn’t The Right Thing”: “It Was Too Soon For That”

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Jenna Ushkowitz doesn’t want to remember when Glee did a tribute to Cory Monteith after his death.

In a new interview, the star of the musical comedy explains why it was difficult to film the episode and why she felt they shouldn’t have done it.

“To shoot ‘The Quarterback’ [tribute episode]… I think it wasn’t the right thing,” Ushkowitz said on the We Need to Talk podcast. “And I think everybody collectively agrees that it’s maybe not the thing that we should have done, because we were grieving both privately and publicly.” Related Stories News Jane Lynch On 'Glee' Reboot & If She'd Return: "I Would Love To Do Sue Sylvester For The Rest Of My Life" News 'Glee' Stars Stage Broadway Mini-Reunion As Hulu Reboot Gears Up

Monteith played Finn Hudson in the Ryan Murphy series until his death in July 2013. The show continued and paid tribute with an episode dedicated to the late actor, which also acknowledged that the quarterback from the fictional William McKinley High School had died. Watch on Deadline

“I think the fans definitely needed something, because they loved Cory so dearly like we did, and [loved] Finn so dearly,” Ushkowitz said. “But it’s a really hard thing to be grieving your friend and be a character grieving their friend at the same time.”

She continued, “It was like, too soon for that. [It] was not fun, [I] don’t want to remember that time. The show itself was never the same. He was the heart of the show, our friend, most importantly, and our leader. He really was the quarterback for us. He held everybody together. He hosted parties for the crew. He was the kindest. He knew everybody’s names. He just was such a good person, a kind person.”

Ushkowitz mentioned that at the time “even our creators, they were dealing with grief too” and “nobody had the tools” to deal with Monteith’s death.
