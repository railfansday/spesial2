Ray Tintori Dies: Filmmaker Who Worked On ‘Beasts Of The Southern Wild’ Was 43

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Ray Tintori, a director and screenwriter of shorts and music videos and a founding member of the Court 13 filmmaking collective with whom he created of Beasts of the Southern Wild, has died. Authorities confirmed to Deadline that his death is “under investigation by the New York City Office of Chief Medical Examiner, with an official cause and manner of death pending further study.” Tintori was 43.

The Oscar-nominated Beasts of the Southern Wild was directed by Benh Zeitlin with the help of Court 13, an arts collective that works to grow and sustain New Orleans’ cinema culture. Tintori served as Aurochs and Special Effects Unit Director on the project, which was released in 2012 and later nominated for Best Picture, Best Director, Best Adapted Screenplay and Best Actress at the 2013 Oscars. He worked in various other capacities on Court 13 projects, including production designer and story co-writer on Glory at Sea.

In 2017, Tintori and co-director Gabe Spitzer won a Sports Emmy for their work on Follow My Lead: The Story of the 2016 NBA Finals. The Occulus-enabled documentary sought to provide a 360-degree view of the finale to the 2015-2016 season. Watch on Deadline

Tintori’s directing credits include the shorts Jettison Your Loved Ones and Death to the Tinman, which screened at Slamdance and Sundance, respectively. His Untitled Cabal Project was accepted into the Sundance Institute’s 2013 June Screenwriters Lab.

He also directed the music videos off MGMT’s first record as well as those for The Killers’ “Spaceman” and Arcade Fire’s “Chemistry.”

Tintori dated former Saturday Night Live castmember Heidi Gardner in 2025.
