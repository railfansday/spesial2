Mariah Carey Hopes Her New Christmas Barbie Doll Brings ‘Joy, Glamour, and Holiday Cheer’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Rolling Stone may receive an affiliate commission.

Mariah Carey isn’t just celebrating the holidays with music this year, the “All I Want For Christmas” singer is also ringing in the season with a new gift for her fans.

Carey has teamed up with Mattel for a brand new Barbie doll, inspired by the singer’s iconic look from her original 1994 Christmas album cover.

The Barbie Signature Mariah Carey Doll features a likeness of Carey with long blond hair and dressed in a santa suit.

The red velvet suit features a fluffy collar and a black belt and it’s paired with black, high-heel boots. A silver necklace, Carey’s signature butterfly ring, and a glittery mic help to accessorize the look. The doll comes in a specially-designed display box with a snowy backdrop and the singer’s “MC” logo on the front.

Carey says she was excited to collaborate with Mattel on another festive release for her fans, especially one that paid homage to her landmark album, Merry Christmas, which has now been certified diamond.

“Christmas has always been such a magical time for me, so having Barbie celebrate the season with me again is incredibly special,” she says. “Seeing this festive look reimagined as a Barbie doll is such a fun full-circle moment. I hope she brings lots of joy, glamour, and holiday cheer to everyone’s celebrations this year!”

This is the second Christmas Barbie release for Carey, who first teamed up with Mattel on a holiday doll in 2023. While that version sold out in 24 hours, per Mattel, the doll has been restocked and is once again available online.

Amazon has the 2023 Mariah Carey Barbie doll on sale for 47% off right now (as of this writing). Both holiday Barbie dolls are expected to be hot sellers this year, both for fans and collectors.

Carey’s latest Christmas Barbie doll arrived as the singer kicked off her 2026 Christmas tour in São Paulo, Brazil on Wednesday night, playing before a captivated crowd at Nubank Parque, the 40,000-seat stadium that’s home to the Palmeiras soccer team in Brasileiro Série A.

The singer largely stuck to the same setlist as her previous Christmas concerts, though she performed a number of non-holiday songs as well, including her cover of Foreigner’s “I Want to Know What Love Is,” first recorded for her 2009 album, Memoirs of an Imperfect Angel. The song was a massive hit in Brazil, topping the charts for 27 straight weeks.

Carey’s São Paulo show was sponsored by Bauducco, the Brazilian food brand best known for their wafers, biscuits, and panettone, the latter being an annual bestseller during the holidays.

Ambassadors from Bauducco were seen handing out mini samples of the brand’s panettone inside the stadium but you can pick up a full-sized boxed loaf for yourself on Amazon, with delivery across the U.S.

It’s been a busy holiday season already for Carey, who is no doubt hoping to return to number-one on the charts with “All I Want For Christmas Is You” — the singer recently appeared in Gucci’s new Christmas campaign and debuted a new capsule collection with the Italian fashion brand.
