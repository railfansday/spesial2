3 Elite Prime Video Series to Binge This Week (Nov 16-20)

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

After multiple weeks at the top of Prime Video’s TV rankings, the Chris Pratt action thriller series The Terminal List has finally been displaced by The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power, which premiered its third season last Wednesday. Inspired by J.R.R. Tolkien’s Middle-earth canon, the show is one of the most epic works of high fantasy television ever attempted, with the latest season earning high praise from critics. But The Rings of Power is hardly the only great TV series you could watch on the streaming service. Here’s a look at three great shows that we think you should binge this week on Prime Video.

For more recommendations, check out our list of the best shows and movies on Prime Video.

David Angell, Peter Casey, and David Lee’s spin-off of their hit sitcom Cheers, Frasier is centered on Kelsey Grammer’s titular psychiatrist, who leaves Boston and returns to his hometown in Seattle. The show focuses on Frasier’s new life as a radio show host helping troubled people while reconnecting with his father and brother and navigating all their collective quirks. Jane Leeves, David Hyde Pierce, Peri Gilpin, John Mahoney, and Dan Butler star as other main characters, with Bebe Neuwirth reprising her role from Cheers as Frasier’s ex-wife.

One of the most popular and critically acclaimed sitcoms of all time, Frasier is as much of a classic as Cheers and became a major TV landmark of its own. The series is highly regarded as one of the most successful spin-off shows in American television history and has at times surpassed the popularity of its parent show, with a record 37 Emmys to its name. The series has earned acclaim for its perfect balance of heart and humor that ended with one of the most perfect series finales seen on television.

Developed by Rob Thomas and Diane Ruggiero-Wright, iZombie is a supernatural drama procedural series adapted from the DC comic book created by Chris Roberson and Michael Allred. The series follows the adventures of Olivia “Liv” Moore, a doctor-turned-zombie, who serves as the medical examiner for Seattle Police and solves murders by consuming the victim’s brains and absorbing their memories and personalities. Rose McIver plays Liv, with Malcolm Goodwin, Rahul Kohli, Robert Buckley, and David Anders as other main characters.

iZombie puts a creative spin on the zombie trope with quirky characters and wild storylines, and it was particularly popular with young adult audiences of its time. During its run, the CW show was often compared to its contemporary Veronica Mars for its wit and humor and also shares some tonal and stylistic DNA with Buffy the Vampire Slayer. Though it was canceled after four seasons, iZombie was highly praised during its run for its charming take on the zombie genre.

A series adaptation of Robert Jordan's fantasy book series of the same name, The Wheel of Time tells the story of Moiraine Damodred, a member of the Aes Sedai, a powerful organization of women who harness​ powerful magic called the One Power. To fulfill a prophecy, Moiraine seeks five young people and leads them on a search for the Dragon Reborn, who can either save the world from darkness or destroy everything. Rosamund Pike heads the ensemble cast as Moiraine, alongside Daniel Henney, Zoë Robins, Marcus Rutherford, Josha Stradowski, and more as main characters.

Created by Rafe Judkins, The Wheel of Time is an epic saga of complex magic set in a fantastical world that comes to life through a great production and a star-studded cast. When it first premiered, the show was a massive success and earned acclaim for its storytelling and world-building, even though critics remain divided on the show’s faithfulness to the books. Unfortunately, despite its phenomenal run, The Wheel of Time was canceled after three seasons, cutting short its epic fantasy story.
