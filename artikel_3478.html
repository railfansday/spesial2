HBO Max's 10-Episode Sci-Fi Series Could Be the Perfect 'Lanterns' Replacement

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Lanterns is the kind of success no one expected. The more grounded take on Green Lantern lore, combined with excellent character work from leads Kyle Chandler as Hal Jordan and Aaron Pierre as John Stewart, created a winning formula and made Lanterns one of the most-watched shows on HBO Max. While a second season of Lanterns remains in development, that doesn't mean DC Studios is done with Green Lantern. Pierre is set to return as John Stewart in Man of Tomorrow next year, while Nathan Fillion is reprising his role as Guy Gardner for Mister Miracle. Another series looks to embrace all the cosmic elements of Green Lantern while building out the world of another fan-favorite DC series.

Appropriately enough, this new series is titled My Adventures with Green Lantern. Spinning out of the events of My Adventures with Superman, My Adventures with Green Lantern will see Jessica Cruz (Auli'i Cravalho) joining the Green Lantern Corps. There's just one catch: while the Guardians of the Universe usually choose Green Lanterns, Jessica becomes a Green Lantern when a power ring literally lands in her lap. She's then pulled into the fight of her life "when more debris from the Lanterns’ ancient space war arrives – along with their alien foes," according to the official logline.

One of the major debates surrounding Lanterns is that its narrative felt better suited to a terrestrial DC hero like Batman or the Question than to two men charged with defending the universe from interstellar threats. While the show mentions other Green Lanterns like Ch'p the squirrel and Mogo the living planet, some viewers felt there could have been more Green Lantern elements in a show called Lanterns. My Adventures with Green Lantern is about to fulfill their wish, as the series is set to bring in plenty of characters from the comics. A preview at Annecy Festival reveals that Jessica attends Hal Jordan Memorial High, with her classmates, including future Green Lantern Kyle Rayner.

Jessica will also have to deal with other Green Lanterns, most notably the Corps' drill sergeant, Kilowog. She will also be fighting Yellow and Red Lanterns in the series, hinting at the presence of two iconic Green Lantern villains. The Yellow Lanterns are usually part of the Sinestro Corps, led by the titular Sinestro — Hal Jordan's former mentor and Green Lantern who now wields the yellow power of fear. The Red Lanterns, on the other hand, draw on the red power of rage thanks to their leader, Atrocitus. The presence of Yellow and Red Lanterns hints that My Adventures with Green Lantern will explore the emotional spectrum established in Geoff Johns' Green Lantern run, and that it'll be a more cosmic affair than Lanterns was. Producer Jake Wyatt also said that he wanted the villains to stand out at Annecy:

“We wanted to get weird with the bad guys. We needed hot evil people to scare the hell out of Jess and her buddies.”

While My Adventures with Green Lantern is spinning out of My Adventures with Superman, it's inspired by another iconic series. My Adventures with Superman draws heavily on shonen anime like Dragon Ball Z, while My Adventures with Green Lantern draws heavily on Sailor Moon. A clip shown at Annecy features Jessica singing the iconic Green Lantern oath and transforming into a costume that largely resembles Sailor Moon's, putting another unique stamp on the Green Lantern mythos.

Lanterns was only the beginning of DC Studios fully embracing the Green Lantern comics, as My Adventures with Green Lantern looks poised to revamp the mythos for a new generation. It's a far cry from the days of Ryan Reynolds' box-office bomb, and the "brightest day" that these characters deserve.
