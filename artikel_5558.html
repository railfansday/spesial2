F/A-18 Blue Angels Super Hornet Flown in TOPGUN: MAVERICK Retires at Palm Springs Air Museum

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

WHERE : Palm Springs Air Museum, 745 N. Gene Autry Trail, Palm Springs, CA 92262

WHY: Celebrate an historic milestone as the Palm Springs Air Museum commemorates its 30th Anniversary with the water salute and dedication of an extraordinary new addition to its permanent collection the iconic Blue Angels F/A-18 Super Hornet featured in Top Gun: Maverick as one of Captain Pete"Maverick" Mitchell's on-camera aircraft.

This remarkable aircraft becomes the first F/A-18 Super Hornet ever placed on permanent public display outside a military installation, offering visitors an unprecedented opportunity to experience one of America's most advanced frontline fighters up close.
