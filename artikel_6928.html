Obsession: The Comforter That Keeps Me in Bed

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Parachute’s Comforter adds the right amount of weight and warmth when the temperature drops—and it’s on sale right now.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE Decor editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

When the radiators in my apartment start creaking and squeaking, I know it’s time to add another layer to the bed. I run incredibly cold, so winter usually means some combination of blankets, socks, and anything else that might help keep the heat in. Lately, the final layer has been Parachute’s Relaxed Cotton Comforter. It has just the right amount of weight to make the bed feel cozy without becoming heavy or stuffy, and somehow manages to be warm and breathable at the same time. Once it’s on, I’m pretty much sealed in for the night.

The cotton is soft to the touch, with a relaxed texture that makes it forgiving when the bed isn’t perfectly made. I don’t need to smooth it out every morning or spend much time fluffing it into shape; it settles naturally and holds its form well. There’s enough texture that a few wrinkles actually look right, which is helpful for someone who likes a bed to look good without spending ten minutes making it look perfect.

It’s also machine washable, which makes a big difference. There’s no separate duvet cover to wrestle with and no complicated care routine. When it needs a wash, I can simply put the entire comforter in the machine.

As December comes near, it has become the layer I notice most when I get into bed. The weight is reassuring; the cotton keeps things breathable, and the relaxed texture looks effortless. When the radiators are coming alive, this is what keeps me cozy and, more importantly, keeps me from waking up cold at three in the morning.
