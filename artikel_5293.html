Workday Introduces Workday GO for Financials, Giving Growing Companies Financial Software They Won't Outgrow

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Workday GO for Financials Includes Accounting Agent to Help Teams Reduce Manual Audit Prep While Keeping Company Controls in Place

New Offering Combines Simpler Setup with Specialized Packages for Financial Services, Professional and Business Services, and Technology and Media Companies

BARCELONA, Spain, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Workday Rising EMEA -- Workday, Inc. (NASDAQ: WDAY), the trusted agentic platform for HR, finance, and IT, today introduced Workday GO for Financials, which gives growing companies a faster, simpler way to buy, set up, and use Workday Financial Management. Customers can get up and running with clearly defined packages and transparent pricing, then add HR or industry-specific tools as they grow without replacing their system.

As businesses grow, their finances get more complicated, but their teams and budgets don't always keep pace. Workday GO for Financials gives companies access to an agentic accounting and operational finance environment that scales with them at any level. Customers can start with what they need today, giving finance teams more time to focus on work that needs their attention.

Workday GO for Financials serves companies across industries, with specialized add-on packages for financial services, professional and business services, and technology and media companies.

"Every growing business reaches a point where spreadsheets and patchwork tools stop working," said Max Wessel, senior vice president of product, Workday. "We created Workday GO for Financials so finance teams can spend their time using the software, not waiting for it to be deployed."

Financial Tools Built Around How Businesses Work

Workday GO for Financials is built for businesses across many industries. Its specialized add-on packages are designed around the way different businesses work. For example:

Accounting Agent Reduces Manual Audit Prep for Finance Teams

Workday GO for Financials includes Workday Accounting Agent, which helps finance teams gather records needed for an audit and prepare reports, reducing manual work and giving employees more time for tasks that need their judgment.

For example, when an auditor asks to see the invoices behind a sample of payments, Accounting Agent can help pull together the supporting documents, so the finance team doesn't spend hours tracking them down one by one.

Accounting Agent follows each company's existing rules and processes, including who can access financial information and what requires approval, so finance teams can reduce audit prep while keeping their controls in place.

Deployment Agent and Workday GO Partner Network Simplify Setup

Workday GO for Financials also includes Workday Deployment Agent to guide customers through setup and simplify ongoing maintenance. For example, when a finance team needs to set up an approval process, the agent can use the customer's existing Workday setup to recommend next steps and flag potential issues. It can also help teams prepare for updates and adopt new capabilities as their needs change.

Alongside Deployment Agent, customers can work with trusted partners through the Workday GO Partner Network to deploy Workday GO for Financials. Partners include Albida, Everforth TopBloc, HR Path, Kainos, and Three Link Solutions.

"No two growing companies have the same finance needs, but they all want a setup process that is clear and predictable," said Padraig Callaghan, CEO, Kainos Services. "With Workday GO for Financials, we can apply our industry experience from day one, helping customers shape a finance foundation that fits how their business actually runs."

Expanding Workday GO for Growing Global Businesses

As businesses grow, managing their people and finances gets more complicated, but adding more disconnected systems only makes things harder. Workday GO gives customers a path to start with the capabilities they need today, including HR, Financials, or both, and add more as their business grows.

Alongside the new financial offering, Workday is expanding Workday GO for HR to new countries, including Australia, Belgium, the Netherlands, the Philippines, Spain, Vietnam, and more. These additions bring Workday GO for HR to 27 countries, giving more growing businesses access to Workday GO's simplified purchasing and setup approach.

"Through our work with Workday GO for HR, we've seen firsthand the difference a simpler approach can make for growing businesses," said George Gabriel, partner at HR Path. "With Workday GO for Financials, we can bring that experience together with our finance and industry expertise, helping customers build a strong foundation today that can evolve as their business grows."

AvailabilityWorkday GO for Financials is currently available in Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom, and Ireland, with additional markets expected to follow. Accounting Agent and Deployment Agent are generally available.
