IHSA postseason: Peoria Notre Dame sophomore wins state swim title

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Peoria Notre Dame sophomore won the 100-yard butterfly at the 2026 Illinois High School Association girls swimming and diving state finals on Saturday, Nov. 14 at the FMC Natatorium in Westmont.

Gardiner won in 54.01 seconds, topping runner-up and Aurora Rosary senior Annabelle Nawrocki (54.84) by 0.83 seconds. This state championship gives the PND program their fifth all-time and first since Rebecca Stoughton won two crowns at the 2010 state finals.

In making the 100-yard butterfly finals, Gardiner improves her third-place finish from last season. She entered as the top seed after swimming 54.22 seconds in the Friday, Nov. 13 prelims.

It was the second medal on the day for Gardiner, who took fifth at 2 minutes, 2.98 seconds in the 200-yard individual medley. She was third in this event during her freshman campaign.

Samantha Nauman of the LaSalle-Peru co-op finished second in the 100-yard backstroke, touching the wall in 53.94 seconds. The Henry senior, who posted the second-fastest, 100-back time in the prelims, was eighth in the 200-yard IM (2:05.24), giving her four state medals — the only ones in the history of the L-P program.

Maya Happel from the Washington co-op took 12th in the 100-yard freestyle. The Morton senior clocked in at 52.22 seconds, improving her prelim time by 0.21 seconds.

Washington co-op senior Ava Gensler also swam in three events for athletes with disabilities — the 200-yard freestyle (4:27.78, fifth), the 50-yard freestyle (53.36 seconds, sixth) and the 100-yard freestyle (2:06.41, sixth).
