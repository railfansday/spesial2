Lakers Player Ratings: Doncic Has Inefficient Game As Winning Streak Gets Snapped By Jazz

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Los Angeles Lakers' nine-game winning streak ended with a frustrating 109-97 loss to the Utah Jazz. Luka Doncic struggled to find his shooting rhythm, finishing with 29 points but needing 28 attempts to get there.

His nine turnovers were particularly costly, as Utah kept turning defensive pressure into fast-break opportunities.

The Lakers never found consistent offensive production outside of Doncic and Quentin Grimes, who finished with 20 points, seven rebounds, and four assists. Utah, meanwhile, received a balanced offensive performance led by Keyonte George's 30 points and 10 assists. Ace Bailey added 19 points, while Josh Green was perfect from the field, scoring 11 points on 4-4 shooting.

The difference was efficiency. Utah shot 48.1% from the field and 37.8% from three-point range, compared to just 38.2% and 27.3%, respectively, for Los Angeles. The Jazz also committed 21 turnovers, but the Lakers couldn't capitalize enough to overcome their shooting struggles.

Here are the Lakers' player ratings from the loss.

Game Stats: 20 PTS, 7 REB, 4 AST, 1 TOV, 8-20 FG, 4-10 3-PT FG, 35 MIN

Grimes was the Lakers' second-leading scorer and one of the few players who consistently provided offensive production. He knocked down four three-pointers and contributed seven rebounds and four assists. However, his 8-20 shooting wasn't particularly efficient, and he couldn't do enough to compensate for Doncic's struggles. Still, he was one of the more dependable Lakers on a difficult night.

The nine turnovers were arguably even more damaging, repeatedly giving Utah opportunities to control the game. His seven assists and 7-7 free-throw shooting kept his offensive production respectable, but the inefficiency and ball security issues were too much to overlook.
