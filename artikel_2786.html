‘Don’t want to invite problems’ – Bayern not interested in 26y/o former Man Utd attacker

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Bayern Munich are not planning to sign Jadon Sancho.

The England winger is currently without a club after his deal expired in the summer.

Recently, Getafe have made an offer to sign the 26-year-old winger, who has been exploring a number of options.

Bayern have been going through injury crisis of late, and many suggested that whether they could move for Sancho.

Christian Falk has told CfBayern Insider that Bayern are have not talked about the possibility of signing the former Manchester United winger.

Sancho joined Man Utd from Borussia Dortmund in 2021 for a fee of £73m, but failed to prove his quality during his five-year stay.

He also had loan spells with Dortmund and Chelsea during his spell, and spent last season on loan at Aston Villa, making 39 appearances.

The former Dortmund winger is on high wages, and he would demand regular game time, which Bayern may not able to offer him.

Falk said: "It is NOT TRUE: Bayern Munich have talked about the possibility of bringing Jadon Sancho in. He's been without a club since the summer and he's not a player Bayern are looking at right now. It would cause some trouble bringing such a player in, as he'd want a big salary and regular minutes. It's a difficult situation and Bayern don't want to invite problems. So they just don't need Sancho at the moment."

Sancho has Bundesliga experience and could be a fine addition for any side on a free transfer, but he needs to lower his wages and expectations.

While Bayern desperately need a new left-winger, they are unlikely to go for Sancho, even if he wants to reduce his wage demands.

Bayern are interested in young attackers like Rio Ngumoha and Antonio Nusa, while Porto's Oskar Pietuszewski is also on their radar.
