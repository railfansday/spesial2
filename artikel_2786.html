Mexico opens U17 World Cup with a victory over Romania

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Mexico started their U17 World Cup campaign by defeating Romania with a 1-0 score. In a very even match, Mexico was the slightly better team and defended well over a Romanian side that was physically imposing but failed to create any shots on goal. The three points are a huge gain for Mexico ,who now has a great chance of making it to the Round of 32. Mexico will now play against Cameroon on Tuesday while Romania will face off against group leader, Venezuela.

The first half started with Mexico coming out with a strong lineup including Da'vian Kimbrough and Matthew Arana. Mexico would win the ball seconds into the match and have a counter that ended when Adan Sanchez got into the area but Romania's defense was able to clear the ball. After the early domination play by Mexico, the match became more even and Romania started to gain possession. Jonathan De la Fuente got into the area and scored but the goal was correctly called off for a foul by De la Fuente. Romania subbed out the injured Cosmin Matei for Erik Manaila. A cross into the area was misjudged by Romania's goalkeeper, Tudor Cosa, and Andre Godinez had a chance to head the ball into the empty net but it went wide in a wasted opportunity. Alexandru Goncear got the ball in the area and got off a left footed shot that went just wide. Off a corner kick, the ball was crossed into the area where Robert Corduneanu got off a volley that went well wide. Off a counter, Goncear got into the area where he got off a left footed shot that went wide. Although Mexico had started as the better team, it was Romania who now clearly looked the better side. A free kick ended with a cross into the area that went past Cosa but no Mexican player could get to the ball and Romania's defense was able to clear the ball. The first half ended in a match that was so even that neither goalkeeper had yet to make a save.

The 2nd half started with neither team making a change during the halftime break. For a second time in the match, Mexico would score after Alan Hernandez got a pass thru Cosa and Sanchez scored a tap in goal into the empty net but it would be called off for offside as Hernandez was clearly offside in the play. From just outside the area, Angel Reyes got off a right footed shot that went wide. Mexico would win the ball just outside the area and Adan Sanchez got off a great left footed shot that went past Cosa to score and give Mexico the 1-0 lead. Mexico subbed out Adan Sanchez for Juan Carlos Martinez Jr. Matthew Arana would make a great play to get into the area and past a defender but his cross was deflected wide. Romania subbed out Eduardo Bodnar and Darius Tripon for Darius Bota and Andrei Niculcea. Mexico subbed out Gerson Gutierrez and Matthew Arana for Jesus Orduño and Miguel Rivera. Romania made their last move when they subbed out Alexandru Goncar for Denis Anevoaei. A good chance was wasted when Martinez Jr got the ball in the area but took too much time to get off his shot and it was blocked. Mexico subbed out Alan Hernandez and Da'vian Kimbrough for Edy Lugo and Alberto Cisneros. Off the free kick, Lugo got off a left footed shot that went just wide. Eduardo Corlat got off a left footed shot from outside the area that went wide. Manaila got off a right footed shot from way outside the area that unsurprisingly went well wide. Off a counter, Mexico made a number of good touches but the play ended with Lugo getting off a shot that went well wide. Although Romania tried to get the tying goal, they never were able to creat a clear chance and Mexico got the victory to make a great start to their U17 World Cup.

Mexico started the U17 World Cup with the right foot forward after getting a 1-0 victory over a tough Romanian side. Mexico traded possession in a very even match in which there were few chances for both sides. Mexico's goalkeeper, Jose Corona, barely moved and didn't have to make a single save. Still Mexico suffered with the imposing physicality of Romania, which will be a problem in their next match against Cameroon. Still the three points are golden for Mexico, as in the 2025 edition, they single victory in the group stage they got was enough to make them move to the Round of 32. Mexico will need to improve on the offense especially in the velocity of getting off shots before the other team is able to clear them. Mexico will face off on Tuesday against Cameroon, who will go all out for the win after losing their opener against Venezuela by a 2-1 score. Romania will face off against Venezuela, who leads the group because of their two goals scored so far.
