Florida football going back to Aaron Philo as starter at QB for Kentucky

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Florida football is going back to Aaron Philo as its starting quarterback for its next game Saturday, November 14 at Kentucky (ESPN, 6:15 p.m.), sources confirmed to The Sun.

Philo is 4-3 as a starter for the Florida Gators. He sat out UF's 27-24 win over Texas with a shoulder injury and was benched after throwing two first-half interceptions in Florida's 27-8 loss to Georgia in Atlanta.

A Georgia Tech transfer, the 6-foot-1, 207-pound Philo has thrown for 1,367 yards with 9 TDs to 5 interceptions. A Georgia Tech transfer, Philo brings familiarity with the offense under first-year offensive coordinator Buster Faulkner, who also played at Georgia Tech.

Florida is in search for any kind of spark for a struggling that is averaging just 15.8 points over its last five games and 7.5 points over its last two games. Before that, Florida had scored 40 or more ponts in each of its first four games to start the 2026 season.

Faulkner offered a vote of confidence to both Philo and Tramell Jones Jr. earlier this week, saying he believes in both quarterbacks. Faulkner said Philo and Jones have three games left to show whether they can be in UF's plans for 2027, of if the Gators need to look elsewhere at the quarterback position in the transfer portal.
