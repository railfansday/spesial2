Black Friday football: Broncos vs. Steelers discussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

After a trio of games on Thanksgiving, we're getting a Friday afternoon matchup in Week 12, featuring the Denver Broncos (8-2) on the road against the Pittsburgh Steelers (6-4).

The Broncos lead the all-time series between these teams, 15-11-1, but the Steelers have won their three most-recent meetings. They last faced off back in 2024, when the Steelers won on the road in Denver, 13-6.

This will be an interesting evening for Steelers fans, who have had a whirlwind week with Aaron Rodgers being benched and then the QB going on the Pat McAfee show to expose head coach Mike McCarthy's incompetence. The locker room is dealing with a dumpster fire — as revealed by Mike Tomlin on Sunday night — and that drama could end up affecting how they play on Friday.

Here's everything you need to know about today's game.

Location: Acrisure Stadium | Pittsburgh, PA

Announcers: Al Michaels, Kirk Herbstreit, Kaylee Hartung (field reporter)
