Art Rooney II backs Mike McCarthy, rips Aaron Rodgers' ultimatum

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Art Rooney II heard Aaron Rodger's complaints and doesn't care. The Pittsburgh Steelers owner and president backed Mike McCarthy Thursday, Nov. 26, saying he will be the team's coach "this season, next season and beyond."

A day after the team waived Rodgers, Rooney came out and made it clear he backed the coach. The 42-year old quarterback had spent about 30 minutes on "The Pat McAfee Show" going after McCarthy, hours after he had been benched for Mason Rudolph.

"The big thing, what happened this week, is insubordination can't be tolerated in any organization," Rooney told the Pittsburgh Post-Gazette. "When you start getting ultimatums, you can't live with that. "

Rooney also had no time for Rodgers' suggestion that McCarthy is unwell.

"Mike's as healthy as the rest of us," Rooney said. "Health was not an issue. I'm not sure where that part comes from."

In a statement to ESPN, Rooney said he was "disappointed in where this went with Aaron Rodgers," but has "100% confidence in Mike McCarthy."

Rooney had an issue with Rodgers' airing his complaints where and when he did.

"There's maybe a time and place after the season, if he wanted to kind of unload," Rooney said. "He talks about wanting to be a good teammate, but you don't do that to your teammates a couple of days before the game, that's for sure.

"In general, I think, historically, what's in the locker room stays in the locker room," he said.

Rooney also said McCarthy spent the season covering for his quarterback.

"The sad thing is Mike took some of the bullets for Aaron in terms of some of the plays that were called, particularly in the red-zone troubles we had," Rooney said. "He tried to protect him, and it just wasn't working."

Rodgers said he went to McCarthy on Nov. 23 and told him to hand play-calling to another coach or release him. The Post-Gazette, citing sources, reported a sharper version from a meeting the next night. "Either let me call all the plays or just release me." McCarthy then met with Rooney and general manager Omar Kahn. Later that night, the team announced Rudolph would start when the Steelers (6-4) play the Denver Broncos on Black Friday.

Rodgers, who played for McCarthy in Green Bay from 2006-2018, said he found out from tight end Pat Freiermuth, not McCarthy.
