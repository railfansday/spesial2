A toothless attack is beginning to become a constant theme for Manchester City

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Manchester City are winless in their past four Premier League matches. Saturday's 1-1 draw with Fulham continued City's winless run in the league. As of right now, Enzo Maresca's side looks to be a disjointed side finding its way. In a sense that is to be expected given the size of the overhaul of Manchester City's squad with a new manager. Across the past four Premier League games that Manchester City have played, they have scored four goals. A lack of a consistent goalscorer outside of Erling Haaland is hurting Manchester City and is a problem when you look deeper into Manchester City's attacking options.

In their defeats to Brighton and Aston Villa, Manchester City created more than enough chances to win both games. However, they squandered too many chances in those games. Against Nottingham Forest, City left themselves a mountain to climb after falling 2-0 down inside 18 minutes. In Saturday's 1-1 draw with Fulham, Erling Haaland could have scored a brace before halftime. But Haaland was below his best and in a way, Haaland having an off day illustrates an issue with this City squad.

Manchester City have been without Antoine Semenyo since their 3-2 win over Ipswich Town in the middle of October. The Ghana international is expected to return in the next few weeks from a knee injury. Semenyo's loss has hurt Enzo Maresca's side. He is City's biggest goal threat outside of Erling Haaland. Without him, City's attacking options are depleted.

Jeremy Doku and Iliman Ndiaye have never been consistent goalscorers. They are creative outlets out wide, but not goalscorers. The same can be said for Allan as his career record for Palmeiras illustrates. Phil Foden and Rayan Cherki offer a threat when playing as number tens. Enzo Fernandez and Elliot Anderson could provide goals from midfield. But they aren't yet consistent in this aspect of the game. City can score goals, they have on occasions this season. But too often if Haaland is having a off day, they struggle to have a consistent goal threat as their past four Premier League games have shown.

Enzo Maresca's system is heavily reliant on creativity from out wide. In essence, City's attacking play sees the ball worked out wide to Jeremy Doku and Iliman Ndiaye with the aim of them creating an opening from the wings. In theory, it is a sound tactic. However, Manchester City's opponents have become aware of this. Often, Doku is double marked on the left. On the right, Iliman Ndiaye offers a similar type of threat to Doku. The issue is that teams are defending both players well or the pair take their time before playing a ball. Doku and Ndiaye are exceptional dribblers but they'd be impossible to read for Erling Haaland or Manchester City's midfield for when the ball will be played.

You do wonder how Enzo Maresca can alter his system to add more goals, or a more consistent goal threat. The attacking options that Manchester City have are set. Perhaps playing to their strengths may alleviate the issue. For example, playing quicker balls into the box can help Haaland and allow Cherki, Foden, Enzo Fernandez or Elliot Anderson to time their runs into the box better. Or, City need to vary their attacking play to make it less predictable in the final third. Perhaps a combination of the above is needed for Enzo Maresca's side.

Manchester City have struggled to hit the back of the net in their past four Premier League games. It does feel like it is Erling Haaland or bust at times for City. This isn't a sustainable system so Enzo Maresca needs to find a system that will break City's drought of sorts in the Premier League in front of goal.
