Michigan State, Michigan both have miserable offenses. College football world was shocked

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Michigan State football entered Week 10 with the worst offense in Power Four this season, in terms of average yards per game. It was Michigan, though, that was by far the worst offense on the field on Saturday, Nov. 7.

The Spartans and Wolverines combined for zero touchdowns, but Michigan State was able to claim their first conference win of the season with a 15-6 victory over its in-state rival on the road.

Michigan has now lost four of its last six games under first-year coach Kyle Whittingham, with its offense scoring 21 points or fewer in six of its last seven games. The Wolverines have scored more than 21 points only twice this season, once against UTEP and another against Rutgers, one of the worst teams in Power Four.

Offensive coordinator Jason Beck, who followed Whittingham from Utah to Michigan, saw plenty of success with the Utes. But that hasn't translated in Ann Arbor. The highest-paid offensive coordinator in the country, Beck typically calls plays from the press box, but came down to the sideline at halftime against the Spartans.

Whittingham said he's not considering making a change at offensive coordinator right now, but said he'll re-evaluate everything after the season.

"Not right now as I'm sitting here at this podium," Whittingham said after the game. "At some point, you've got to have results. It's a result-driven business, a bottom-line business. We'll re-evaluate everything after the season and determine what's the best course of action."

A loss to Michigan State has not only left fans speechless, but also Whittingham. The 66-year-old coach said after the game he can't explain what he watched on Nov. 7.

"As disappointed as I've ever been as a head coach and as frustrated as I've ever been as a head coach," he said after the game.
