QPR man ruled out of Sheffield United clash due to injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

QPR’s defender has an ankle injury and has missed their last two matches against Millwall and Norwich City respectively. The Hoops drew 1-1 away against the latter at Carrow Road last time out with attacker Harvey Vale getting their goal.

Dunne, 29, is a key player for the R’s and has enjoyed plenty of game time this season for the London outfit. He has made 15 appearances for Julien Stephan‘s side in all competitions, 14 of which have come in the Championship, and he has chipped in with three goals and one assist.

In this latest update regarding his condition, West London Sport report he won’t be fit enough to return against Sheffield United which is a blow. Elsewhere, winger Paul Smyth is a doubt and will need to be assessed, whilst midfielder Nicolas Madsen is back available.

QPR are positioned in 7th place in the table after 17 fixtures following their draw at Norwich City on Tuesday evening in East Anglia. They are in the play-offs along with Wolves, Birmingham City, West Brom, Southampton and Swansea City with the likes of Sheffield United and Portsmouth chasing them down.

QPR are well stocked up with options at the back in Dunne’s absence. They have Ronnie Edwards, Jake Clarke-Salter and Liam Morrison to pick from.

The Hoops will be hoping their skipper can return as soon as possible. He is a valuable member of Stephan’s starting XI.

Dunne, who has made four caps for the Republic of Ireland national team, has been a great servant to the R’s. He joined them back in 2021 and hasn’t looked back since.

The Dundalk-born man has now played over 200 games for them altogether to date. He will be eager to guide QPR into the top eight in this campaign if possible.
