Not Just Frank: Crystal Palace Hold Fresh Talks to Appoint New Manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Crystal Palace have held talks with a number of candidates for the manager's job as they look to replace Pierre Sage.

The Eagles made the decision to part ways with the French manager after just 16 games during the week, with the team sitting rock bottom in the Premier League table, having won just two of their opening ten games, while they have also won just one of their four Europa League games in this campaign.

The poor form saw Sage dismissed as manager, with GIVEMESPORT sources understanding that Thomas Frank is the leading candidate for the role to step in and take over.

But while the former Brentford and Tottenham manager is still among the top contenders, he is not the only manager being considered according to a new report.

Sky Sports News journalist Christopher Reidy has reported on his personal X account that the club have held fresh talks with the Danish boss as they look to finalise a deal to appoint a new manager before the international break is over.

However, other candidates are not considered to be out of the question and have also been spoken to. While no names are mentioned, GIVEMESPORT understands that Sean Dyche and Vitor Pereira have also been considered, while Tony Popovic has previously held talks about the role.

Frank remains the frontrunner to become the next manager, having been out of a job since being sacked by Tottenham Hotspur earlier this year after less than a full season in charge in north London.

Prior to that, Frank spent seven years at Brentford and helped them earn promotion to the Premier League, before helping them to establish themselves as a top-flight team. He is now ready to return to management after a break, and Selhurst Road looks to be the most likely destination.

Crystal Palace are coming off the back of replacing their best-ever manager in Oliver Glasner, having won three trophies during his time in charge. Sage had almost the impossible job of coming in straight after, and the summer transfer business won't have helped him too much.

But Frank can come in now and implement his more sturdy approach and flexible style that would suit the squad better, and Palace will feel that they are upgrading massively in the dugout as a result.

The manager is keen to join, the board are clearly interested in bringing him in, and the players will benefit from having him on the training pitch sooner rather than later. This is a scenario that will perfectly suit everyone involved, and it would be a real surprise if Frank isn't the man to come in next week.

But it's clear that the club are keeping their options open as a result of negotiations and talks, and until it's a done deal anything can happen.
