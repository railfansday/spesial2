Richard Sperber Teams Up with American Operator to Preserve Independent Landscape Businesses

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Industry veteran aims to protect local business legacies and create new opportunities for the next generation of landscape professionals

LOS ANGELES, Nov. 6, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Landscape industry veteran Richard Sperber has partnered with American Operator to help preserve independent landscape businesses and make business ownership more accessible to the next generation of entrepreneurs.

Drawing on decades of experience building and leading some of the nation's largest landscape companies, Sperber will advise on buying and selling landscape businesses, identify acquisition opportunities and connect interested parties with American Operator.

As consolidation reshapes the landscape industry, the partnership offers business owners another option for succession while helping experienced professionals take the next step toward owning a company.

"Small, local businesses are often the toughest competitors in any market," said Sperber. "They're the heart of their communities, but too many are disappearing. This partnership is about creating opportunities for people who have spent their careers building successful businesses but haven't had the opportunity to own one, while preserving the businesses and legacies that make our industry special."

American Operator buys established businesses while keeping their names, employees and local identities intact. Through its Operate-to-Own model, the company acquires small businesses alongside experienced industry professionals, providing the operator the opportunity to run these businesses as CEO, earn an ownership stake and eventually become the majority owners. With this approach, every business stays locally owned and operated, creating opportunities for local leaders to become owners, preserving jobs for employees, and strengthening the communities they serve.

"Richard brings extraordinary industry expertise and a trusted network of relationships built over decades," said Dan Long, Chief Investment Officer at American Operator. "Together, we can help retiring owners preserve what they've built and give the next generation of landscape leaders the opportunity to become business owners."

Richard Sperber is a landscape industry entrepreneur who helped grow his family's business, ValleyCrest, founded by his father, Burt Sperber, into one of the nation's largest landscape services companies. Today, he is focused on preserving independent businesses and creating new opportunities for the next generation of industry leaders.

Based in Austin, Texas, American Operator acquires established small businesses and partners with experienced industry professionals to lead them through its Operate-to-Own model. The company focuses on preserving local business identities, supporting employees and creating pathways to majority ownership for qualified operators.
