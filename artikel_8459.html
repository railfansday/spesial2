Personal Investments • Re: Late thirties, with most of my financial assets tied to my employer—how should I diversify?

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The wise thing to do going forward is to always sell your RSUs as they vest. You're paying taxes on that day, you might as well diversify.

As for selling existing shares, I would probably be a little tax-sensitive as I sold, but realize that its better to pay the taxes on the gain than have the value drop as I wait.

As reference, I have worked for a number of big tech companies but none of them have been "darlings". Since 2005, I have never held any shares other than un-vested RSUs.....but as I've diversified into VTI, I've held all of the darlings!
