Jaguars vs Titans: Injury Report Ahead of Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Jaguars (6-2) head north to Nashville this weekend looking to keep their foot on the gas in the race for the AFC's No. 1 seed. Across the sideline sits a struggling Titans squad (1-7) eager to play spoiler against a division rival. Both teams enter the matchup with notable names on the report, though Tennessee is navigating a litany of injuries—with multiple starters already trending toward or ruled out—while Jacksonville appears largely intact despite a crowded list of limited participants.

Here's where things stand after Thursday's practice:

DE B.J. Green II – Forearm – Full Participant

Jacksonville's Thursday report carries a lengthy designation list, but the trend lines are mostly encouraging as the team prepares for their division foe. Travon Walker and Jakobi Meyers both bounced back to log limited sessions after sitting out Wednesday as DNPs. Meanwhile, running back Bhayshul Tuten was the lone non-participant on the field. However, he essentially put any lingering fears to bed himself this week by confirming on Kay Adams' podcast that he'll be ready to roll on Sunday. Trade acquisitions Jessie Bates III and Jordan Davis also sat out for rest and have already been declared out this week. At the same time, B.J. Green II made his welcome return to the practice field as a full participant coming off injured reserve.

DL John Franklin-Myers – Back – Full / Full

RB Tony Pollard – Finger / NIR-Rest – Full / DNP

G Peter Skoronski – Hamstring – DNP / DNP

P Tommy Townsend – Left Knee / Right Achilles – Full / Full

S Kevin Winston Jr. – Shoulder – Full / Limited

Tennessee's injury report paints a grim picture as they prepare to host the Jaguars, with several key contributors already ruled out. The Titans are dealing with heavy losses, particularly upfront and on the defensive side, where starting guard Peter Skoronski, linebacker Cody Barton, linebacker Anthony Hill Jr., and wide receiver Carnell Tate have already been ruled out. Adding to their special teams and field-position woes, punter Tommy Townsend is also unable to go, leaving a struggling Tennessee squad heavily depleted across multiple units against a rolling Jacksonville team. But hey, at least they'll look good!

With both rosters sorting through their final adjustments before kickoff, expect the chess matches to play out early in Nashville.
