Adepoju Adebawore addresses his future at Oklahoma beyond this season

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The NCAA's move to the five-for-five rule has changed college football forever. The new rule gives players five years to play five years. No more redshirts, whether medical or for developmental purposes. This has changed how teams like the Oklahoma Sooners build their roster.

Adepoju Adebawore is a perfect example. Heading into this season, this looked like it would be his last in Norman. He had played in more than four games in his first three seasons, meaning he couldn't use a redshirt year, and he has been a key piece for Oklahoma this season. But the rule change now lets Adebawore return for one final season if he wants to.

Adebawore was asked if he has put much thought into returning for his final season next year, but he said that's something he'll think more about after the final game against the Missouri Tigers.

"I really haven't," Adebawore said via SoonerScoop. "I'm more so trying to maximize the rest of the season and just keep getting better. I'll think more about that toward the actual end of it."

Adebawore's decision will be one of the offseason storylines to watch for Oklahoma, especially after the impact he has made along the defensive front this season. An additional year of experience and production could be valuable for a Sooners defense looking to maintain its strength up front. However, Adebawore appears determined to keep his attention on the task in front of him rather than get caught up in offseason decisions.

The matchup against Missouri will give him one more opportunity to make an impact before the regular season comes to a close. Once the game is finished, Adebawore can turn his attention toward what comes next and evaluate the opportunity created by the five-for-five rule. For now, Oklahoma will be focused on finishing the season with another win, while the future of one of its key defensive pieces remains undecided.
