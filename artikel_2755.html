Colts CB Justin Walley earns AFC Defensive Player of the Week honors after momentous strip-sack

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

On the opening snap of the second half against the Miami Dolphins, Indianapolis Colts cornerback Justin Walley disguised a nickel blitz to come screaming off the edge against an empty pocket. Dolphins quarterback Kyle McCord tried to escape, but Walley closed in to lay a hit before McCord had enough time to react. Walley punched the football out of his grasp for a strip-sack and sent the leather tumbling into the end zone.

Defensive tackle Adetomiwa Adebawore hustled to recover the fumble for a touchdown, giving Indianapolis a defensive score that ultimately proved to be the winning play in its 24-22 victory over Miami inside Lucas Oil Stadium. Walley admitted as soon as defensive coordinator Lou Anarumo dialed up the playcall, he knew it was his moment to wreak havoc in the backfield and terminate Miami's drive just seconds after it got started.

The momentum-shifting play earned Walley AFC Defensive Player of the Week honors, the first league-wide recognition of his NFL career. Walley finished with five tackles, including two tackles for loss, two pass deflections and a pivotal strip-sack. His standout performance makes him the first defensive back to reach that statline in a single NFL game since at least 1993.

Indianapolis managed to score just three points from its offense in the second half, letting a comfortable two-possession advantage shrink to two points as Miami mounted a rally. Walley's strip-sack gave the Colts enough cushion to withstand the Dolphins' late push. The 24-year-old posted a 91.2 Pro Football Focus defensive grade in Week 10, which was the highest among all cornerbacks and third-best among all NFL players. He made life difficult for McCord in his first NFL start, giving up just three catches on six targets for a measly 13 yards.

Through 10 games, Walley has collected 40 tackles, including three tackles for loss, two sacks, three forced fumbles, and five pass deflections. His 22 defensive stops rank second among all NFL cornerbacks this season.

The Colts selected Walley in the third round with the 80th overall pick in the 2025 NFL Draft out of Minnesota, with intentions to add a young, versatile nickel to their secondary. His NFL introduction didn't go according to plan, because Walley missed his entire rookie season after suffering a torn ACL during a joint-practice with the Baltimore Ravens in training camp, delaying an early opportunity to establish himself on the field.

For a player who missed his rookie campaign, the recognition represents the journey it took to return to the gridiron. Walley is the fourth Colts cornerback to earn AFC Defensive Player of the Week honors over the past decade, joining Kenny Moore II (twice), Jaylon Jones and Xavier Rhodes.

The Colts (3-7) needed someone on the defensive side of the ball to make a play and halt the Dolphins' first drive of the second half. Adebawore deserves credit for recovering the football, but Walley's fingerprints knocked the football loose into the blue turf for the fumble recovery touchdown.
