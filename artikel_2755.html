Titans changed their offensive philosophy after Colts game

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Robert Saleh said at his Monday press conference this morning that the Tennessee Titans changed their offensive philosophy after the game against the Indianapolis Colts. You can see the whole answer here:

The changes they have made have certainly paid off. The Titans are averaging almost 28 points per game in that span. Cam Ward has looked much more comfortable. He wasn't that good in the loss to the Cleveland Browns, which was the first game they made the switch, but he has been really good since.

There are probably a couple of reasons they made the switch. The first is that running the offense up-tempo benefits Ward because it fits his style of play. He is better out of structure than in structure. You can't always play out of structure as an NFL quarterback, but it makes sense to create more of those opportunities.

The other reason is that the defense, which was already bad, lost its two best players over that span. If the Titans are going to have any chance to win games, they are going to have to score a bunch of points. It is going to take another 30-point effort for them to have a chance against the Dallas Cowboys on Sunday.

I wrote early on this season that Brian Daboll needed to open things up for Ward. The conservative offense just wasn't working. It is good to see that they made the decision in-house to do that. Hopefully, it will eventually lead to some wins.
