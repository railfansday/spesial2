James Cook goes over 1,000 rushing yards, puts Bills up 20-3

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Bills were heavy favorites coming into Sunday's game against the Dolphins and they have played like it in the first half.

Running back James Cook scored from a yard out to extend Buffalo's lead to 20-3 with 3:48 left in the first half. It was the 10th rushing touchdown of the season for Cook, which makes him and quarterback Josh Allen the first teammates to each post 10-plus rushing touchdowns in three straight seasons.

Cook is up to 36 yards on 13 carries and he crossed the 1,000-yard milestone on the scoring drive.

It's the fourth time he has run for 1,000 yards in a season, which puts him behind Thurman Thomas and O.J. Simpson for the most in franchise history. He and Simpson are the only Bills to run for 1,000 yards in the first 10 games of a season.
