Nissan Is Bringing a 300-HP Sentra SE-R Tribute With a Stick Shift to SEMA

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The built-out Sentra uses aftermarket parts—and, sadly, does not seem to be previewing a new Nissan sport compact.

Nissan still builds a sports car—the Z, in case you forgot—but the brand’s Sentra sedan no longer fights the Hyundai Elantra N or higher-end Civic variants of the world with a hot sport compact. The closest thing the brand sells to an SE-R these days is the SR, a high-spec compact that is not exactly optimized for your local autocross. While that may not be changing any time soon, Nissan is at least using the 2026 SEMA Show as an opportunity to show off the performance potential of the current Sentra platform.

The build is called the Sentra Spec-V, after another speed-oriented variant of the compact the brand formerly offered, and Nissan says its turbocharged MR20DD engine makes 300 horsepower. That routes to the front wheels through a modified six-speed manual from the short-lived Juke Nismo RS, complete with a helical limited-slip differential.

Nissan notes that the car rides on a “customer track version” of the coilovers from the late Nismo Sentra, paired with new front shocks that have external reservoirs. Damping profiles and spring rates are also changed, while the chassis gets some extra rigidity from the sway bar set that would have been installed on that same Nismo Sentra.

Exterior changes are a good deal more extreme than what someone would expect from a production SE-R, headlined by the rear spoiler from an R35-generation Nissan GT-R Nismo. A widebody from Bomex and a custom hood with an intake inlet give the car a heavy aftermarket look, one completed by an aggressive wrap and what can only be described as one of the strangest applications of a side-exit exhaust ever imagined. (A full list of mods is provided here, in case you’re curious.)

The Sentra Spec-V is set to be on display at the SEMA show next week. Nissan seems unlikely to produce a road car like this any time soon, but the fact that the company is showing off this build, at least, offers the slightest spark of hope.
