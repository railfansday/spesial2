Bears' Colston Loveland Breaks His Silence After Tearful Injury Against Lions

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Chicago Bears tight end Colston Loveland shared a hopeful message after he suffered an emotional knee injury against the Detroit Lions on Thanksgiving.

Loveland, 22, posted a Bible verse via his Instagram Story on Friday, November 27, reading, “You will keep in perfect peace all who trust in you, all whose thoughts are fixed on you!”

Hours before, Loveland was carted off the field during the first quarter of the Bears’ 39-35 win over the Lions on Thursday, November 26.

“He was in tears, he was frustrated, and he is officially out with a knee injury,” CBS sideline reporter Tracy Wolfson said on the broadcast.

Loveland will undergo tests on his left knee on Friday in an attempt to assess the severity of the injury, according to ESPN.

Bears head coach Ben Johnson did not have an update about Loveland’s condition after the game.

“Nope, don’t know yet, no,” Johnson, 40, told reporters. “We’ll find out.”

Loveland, the 10th overall pick by the Bears in the 2025 NFL Draft, had started in all 11 of the Bears’ games this season. The University of Michigan grad has 41 receptions for 397 yards and three touchdowns.

The Bears return to action when they host the Jacksonville Jaguars on Sunday, December 6.

Chicago’s other two active tight ends — Sam Roush and Stephen Carlson — saw increased action in Loveland’s absence. The Bears were already without tight end Cole Kmet, who suffered a season-ending right knee injury against the New England Patriots on October 22.

Roush, 23, caught a career-high five passes for 31 yards as the entire Bears offense was forced to adjust.

“They did a phenomenal job,” Johnson said of his tight ends. “Seeing them just continue to get better and better and better, [Roush] had a little hiccups there earlier in the season, and now he’s really smoothed out and he’s only going to continue to ascend. I mean, I’ve got a lot of confidence in him as a blocker. We saw him — we threw to him, I think he had five catches this game, and so he’s become the security blanket there for our quarterback, as well.”

He added, “We’ll see if he needs to have a bigger role here going forward.”

Johnson recalled tight ends coach Jimmy Dray gushing about Carlson, 29, when Johnson took over head coaching duties in Chicago in January 2025.

“When I first came to the building a year and a half ago, Jimmy Dray, he just pounded the table like, ‘You’re going to love this guy. He’s dependable, he does everything right, and we’re going to use him at some point,’” Johnson said on Thursday. “And here we go, in one of the biggest games that we’ve had in a year and a half, and he doesn’t miss a beat. So, it’s who he is, and we’re very fortunate to have him.”
