Twitch kicks security guard out of TwitchCon after he told streamer to pour alcohol on her feet

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Twitch has removed a third-party security guard from TwitchCon San Diego after streamer Tuppy said she was sexually harassed by him at the event’s partner party.

The Twitch partner first shared what happened on X, saying the incident “ruined my first partner party ever” and that the man turned out to be a security manager. TwitchCon San Diego runs from November 13 to 15.

Really glad that twitchcon stepped up their security so that they could sexually harass me at the partner party.Tell me alcohol isn't allowed and that I should pour it over my feet.I told 3 security guards only to find out he's a manager because he is in a suit.Ruined my first…

Speaking on stream the following day, Tuppy said she was sitting alone at the back of the party when a security guard in a suit crouched down next to her and told her alcohol wasn’t allowed, despite the party serving free drinks.

“He goes, ‘I know that’s not juice, you’re gonna have to empty that out. You can pour it on your feet,’” she said, adding that he looked at her feet as he said it and told her “don’t leave” when she got up to walk away.

Tuppy said she reported it to two security guards at the entrance, who told her the man was their manager and said they would talk to him, but didn’t move from their spot. She claimed a third guard outside the party laughed when she explained what had happened.

“I talked to four security guards and no one did anything until I tweeted about it,” she said.

After her posts gained attention, Twitch arranged to meet Tuppy at the convention, and later confirmed it had identified the guard through video footage.

Thank you Twitch for taking action!! I'm glad the individual has been removed. They reviewed security footage and found him! pic.twitter.com/XXcWwoq2dt

“This investigation determined that this individual’s behavior violated our Events Code of Conduct,” Twitch wrote in a message shared by Tuppy. “As a result, we removed the third-party security personnel from the venue earlier today, and have deactivated their credentials to ensure they cannot return.”

Tuppy later told viewers the two guards she first reported it to had also been removed, after footage showed them staying in place once she’d spoken to them. Twitch’s message only refers to one individual.

“Let this be a lesson. Always stand up for yourself, chat,” she said.

It comes a year after Twitch promised to overhaul security at its events, having admitted it “failed” Emiru when she was assaulted during a meet and greet at TwitchCon 2025.
