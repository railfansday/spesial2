Newsmax Files Antitrust Lawsuit Against Fox Corp. Over Roku Acquisition

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Newsmax has filed an antitrust lawsuit challenging Fox Corp.‘s $22 billion acquisition of Roku, a transaction that was closed earlier this month.

In the lawsuit, filed in federal court in San Jose, claims that the Justice Department “allowed this merger to an effective monopoly to close unchallenged,” dismissing a set of commitments that Fox made as “ill-defined” and with “no evident enforcement mechanism.”

Newsmax contends that Roku’s Roku Channel with Fox’s Tubi have a combined 70% share in the market for free ad supported television streaming channels.

“DOJ virtually never clears a transaction combining the two leading competitors in a concentrated market; it typically demands divestiture of one or the other to protect competition,” Newsmax’s legal team wrote in their lawsuit. Watch on Deadline

A Fox Corp. spokesperson said in a statement, “Newsmax’s complaint, which comes after a rigorous review process by regulatory agencies, is wholly without merit. Upon closing, we commenced actively integrating this approved transaction for the benefit of consumers, partners and other stakeholders.”
