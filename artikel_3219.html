Management stated that the company completed a $19.2 million acquisition of vascular grafts and surgical tools from B. Braun to strengthen its market share outside of North America. Strategic focus remains on expanding the international sales force and consolidating manufacturing operations in Burlington to support future growth and improve operational efficiencies.
