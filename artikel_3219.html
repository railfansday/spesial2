Jermaine Haley Dies; WWE Star Known To Fans As Bronson Reed was 38

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The World Wrestling Federation announced the passing of Jermaine Haley, aka Bronson Reed. The 38-year-old died Nov. 26 of medical complications.

“Jermaine was a larger-than-life figure in WWE,” the organization posted via Instagram. “More importantly, he was a phenomenal person, a devoted family man, and a beloved colleague. Our t thoughts and deepest condolences are with Jermaine’s entire family, including his pregnant wife Paige, their daughter, as well as all his friends, co-workers and fans.”

Multiple reports say Haley was injured during WWE Crown Jewel in Saudi Arabia and suffered multiple broken ribs and a lacerated kidney during the show. He reportedly returned to the U.S. on November 18.

Sports Illustrated reports that Haley’s last on-screen appearance in WWE featured a match between him and against Oba Femi. Watch on Deadline

Haley launched his wrestling career in 2007 in his home country of Australia. He signed with WWE in 2019.
