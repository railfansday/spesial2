‘Dancing With The Stars: The Next Pro’ Renewed For Season 2 At ABC & Prepares To Move Filming To LA

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

EXCLUSIVE: Adele Zaikman, the Israeli-Ukrainian Latin ballroom dancer and choreographer, landed a spot as a professional on the current season of Dancing with the Stars after winning spinoff The Next Pro earlier this year.

Expect more dancers like Zaikman after ABC renewed Dancing with the Stars: The Next Pro for a second season.

The show is also bucking the trend of big-ticket competition shows moving out of Los Angeles by moving its second season to California, having shot the first season in Australia.

Deadline understands that the BBC Studios-produced show has been successful in its bid to receive the California tax credit. This would make it only the second unscripted show to receive the credit after Netflix’s upcoming series Schooled!, which comes from YouTuber Mark Rober and Jimmy Kimmel’s production company Kimmelot. It would also become the first show to successfully score the tax credit in order to relocate to California, which was one of the core missions of the California Film & Television Jobs Act. It is a massive boon for the business in California, given that the pendulum has been swinging the other way recently with shows such as American Idol moving to Georgia and The Masked Singer moving to New Jersey.

The first season of The Next Pro employed around 110 local cast and crew and contributed an estimated A$7.2M ($5M) to the local Australian economy, per Screen Queensland.

The law, otherwise known as California Assembly Bill 1138, was signed in to law in 2025 by Gov. Gavin Newsom, expanding the tax credits system to include unscripted shows for the first time. The act now covers “large-scale competition shows” with around 15% of its budget allocated to relocating television series.

The Next Pro will return next year for its second season. It will feature eight aspiring make dancers competing for the chance to become the next male pro on Dancing with the Stars, which is currently airing its finale episode.

The spinoff will join its mothership in California; Dancing with the Stars, which premiered in 2005, has filmed all 35 seasons of the show in Los Angeles.

Dancing with the Stars superfans will also have the chance to partner with hopeful contestants and help determine if they have what it takes to make it to the ballroom.

Robert Irwin returns as host, although, at the moment, it’s unclear if three-time Mirrorball champion Mark Ballas and his mother Shirley Ballas, who is the head judge on British OG format Strictly Come Dancing, will return as judges.

Dancing with the Stars: The Next Pro is produced by BBC Studios. Conrad Green serves as showrunner and executive produces alongside Deena Katz, Ryan O’Dowd, Krystal Whitney and Alex Cross are executive producers. Quinn Lipton is co-executive producer.
