Chainlink price is drawing fresh attention as a large whale continues accumulating LINK despite the token’s recent weakness. The wallet has withdrawn more than $1.8 million worth of Chainlink (LINK) from Binance over four days, bringing its combined holdings to approximately $2.16 million. The accumulation comes as LINK struggles to recover after breaking below a key technical support zone.

This divergence between whale buying and weakening price momentum raises a key question: Is whale accumulation signaling a potential LINK price rebound, or does the broader downtrend still have room to extend?

According to on-chain tracking data, the wallet accumulated 202,820 LINK, valued at approximately $1.81 million, over the past four days. Its combined holdings across its main wallet and Safe wallet now stand at 241,630 LINK, worth approximately $2.16 million.

The repeated withdrawals have attracted attention as LINK faces selling pressure in the broader market. Moving tokens away from centralized exchanges can potentially reduce immediately available selling supply, although withdrawals alone do not confirm that the investor intends to hold the assets long term. The accumulation pattern nevertheless suggests that the whale is increasing its exposure to LINK despite the recent decline.

Chainlink’s price chart shows a weakening technical structure following its earlier rally. LINK experienced a strong advance during the late summer and early autumn before reaching a peak in October. Since then, the token has entered a sustained correction, with lower highs and lower lows signaling weakening momentum.

The decline intensified after LINK lost a major horizontal support zone that previously acted as a base for several price movements. The breakdown has turned that region into a potential resistance area, complicating the recovery attempt.

LINK is also trading below several key moving averages, reflecting the broader bearish trend. The latest price action shows a sharp decline followed by a modest attempt to stabilise, but buyers have yet to demonstrate enough strength to reverse the trend. A sustained recovery above the broken support zone would improve the technical outlook. Until then, the token continues to favour caution.

The latest accumulation offers a potentially bullish signal for Chainlink, particularly as the whale continues withdrawing tokens while prices weaken. Large investors accumulating during a correction can sometimes indicate confidence in an asset’s longer-term prospects. Exchange withdrawals may also reduce the amount of LINK immediately available for trading if those tokens remain in private wallets.

LINK faces a critical test as on-chain accumulation contrasts with its bearish technical structure. If the token stabilises around its lower support zone and begins recovering toward the previously broken resistance, traders could view the move as an early sign of a potential trend reversal. A sustained breakout above that zone would strengthen the bullish case.

However, continued selling pressure could push LINK lower despite the whale’s accumulation, particularly if broader crypto-market weakness persists. For now, the whale’s $2.16 million LINK position is an encouraging on-chain development, but the price chart has yet to confirm a recovery. Traders will be watching whether accumulation translates into stronger buying momentum or whether LINK’s downtrend continues.
