Crystal Palace owners have lost their minds as Thomas Frank terms reported

Crystal Palace have made the cut-throat decision to sack Pierre Sage and hire Thomas Frank before the season's halfway point.

Many will feel that the Eagles were left with no other choice, given that they're currently sitting bottom of the Premier League table.

Palace are just one win away from leapfrogging their way out of the relegation zone, but that is not a risk that the owners wanted to take.

Mick Brown told Football Insider that those behind the scenes were "surprised" by Frank's demands, but eventually, terms were agreed.

The former Tottenham Hotspur manager will be expected to make an immediate impact, with a clash against Coventry City his first challenge.

Fans were amazed to learn just how much Sage was earning at Selhurst Park before his inevitable demise. The latest boss is on even more.

There is no doubt over Frank's experience and capabilities, even if his time at Brentford was far more successful than his Tottenham spell.

When he made the switch from the Bees to North London, his annual salary jumped from £3.9million to £5.5m, albeit that didn't last long.

To put that into context, it is believed that Sage was earning £4.74m a year, which also means that Palace may have to pay out a lot after sacking him.

Rather than learning from their mistakes, reports from Denmark state that Frank is just below the wage he was on during his time at Tottenham.

It's a huge gamble to take on a manager who seriously struggled at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, and one who hasn't been in work since.

It is also understood that should he meet certain requirements, Frank will earn bonuses that take him towards his previous Spurs wage.

He won't want another failure on his managerial CV, but the potential payout is unlikely to be seen as a negative in his eyes.

According to Mick Brown, the new manager has been "set up to fail" after delays to the agreement stalled his preparation time at Selhurst Park.

In the current situation, Palace need as much time as possible to work on new tactics, but precious time has been taken away from the new boss.
