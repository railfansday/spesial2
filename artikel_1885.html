Viktor Gyokeres still an unsolvable enigma, Arsenal should accept January offer

Viktor Gyokeres continues to baffle Arsenal fans with his performances, and not in a good way.

The 28-year-old scored the only goal in the Gunners' 1-1 draw with Borussia Dortmund on Tuesday, 24 November.

He lashed home from outside the box, getting the crowd of its feet as his dart arrowed past a helpless Gregor Kobel.

Minutes have been hard to come by for Gyokeres this season, despite netting 21 times in 55 appearances in his first campaign in England.

But with fans being left frustrated by just one point at the Emirates Stadium, it is clear to see why Gyokeres is often the fall guy.

He may have scored one of the most beautiful goals in the competition this term, but he continues to be a difficult puzzle to solve in North London.

His outrageous long-distance strike was not his only shot on target of the net, also letting fly from even further out just six minutes earlier.

But his 33 per cent shot accuracy would come back to haunt him in the 75th minute [FotMob], as he sent a diving header wide of the far post.

Normally, a missed chance would be normal for any big-name striker, but this moment cost the Gunners all three points.

And frankly, it was easier to score than it was to miss. That sums up his game, really, especially when he's taking the ball off Piero Hincapie's toe.

The Sweden international made some good runs, battled well, completed dribbles, but it was all undone by one moment of ineptitude in front of goal.

Fans will point to Erling Haaland and his number of missed chances for Manchester City. The difference is that he has 14 goals this season.

Football Insider understands that Nicolo Tresoldi of Club Brugge could be his natural replacement, but a deal only goes through if Gyokeres is sold.

Of course, Julian Alvarez would be the ideal signing for the Gunners, but that ship appears to have sailed with the Atletico Madrid striker.

It's frustrating because Gyokeres keeps showing what he's capable of, and then minutes later, he ruins it and sparks calls for his sale.

A decent January offer for the Swede should be enough to get rid, and recruit a more effective replacement.
