Binance resets Anthropic futures, wiping out stop-loss orders

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Binance's Anthropic futures reset changes position sizes, cancels stop-loss orders and raises liquidation risks.

Binance’s Nov. 17 rules for resetting its Anthropic pre-IPO perpetual contract call for canceling existing protective orders and closing bot positions without restoring them.

Under the exchange's reset rules, ordinary positions are reinstated with revised sizing, leaving holders with different consequences depending on how they traded.

The reset changes the contract’s estimated share-count basis from 1 billion to 13,116,075,288, with Binance specifying an adjustment factor of 13.116. These perpetual contracts provide exposure to Anthropic’s price without ownership of the underlying shares.

The exchange’s early-completion notice set the adjustment and rename from ANTHROPICUSDT to ANTUSDT for 10:25 UTC, with continuous trading at 10:30 UTC after a cancel-only phase.

Under Binance’s adjustment procedure, an increase in the share-count basis raises position quantity. Because the procedure preserves position notional value, it implies a proportionally lower mark price, subject to minor rounding as prices and quantities align with permitted increments.

The process also closes ordinary positions, settles unrealized profit or loss into the Cross Margin Futures Account, and resets the reinstated position’s average entry price to the adjusted mark. Profit or loss previously displayed as unrealized becomes settled account balance.

All open orders are canceled, including reduce-only take-profit and stop-loss orders, so restoring a position does not restore its old protective instructions. Holders who want equivalent protection need to place new orders suited to the adjusted position.

Binance said Grid, DCA, and Snowball bots would terminate at 08:15 UTC on Nov. 17, closing their positions regardless of the close-on-stop setting.

The rules move realized profit or loss for isolated positions and the entire isolated margin balance back to Cross Margin. The system then funds the reinstated isolated position according to the initial-margin requirement for the leverage selected before the adjustment.

If the available Cross Margin balance falls below the required initial margin, Binance says the system transfers only the available balance.

The exchange urged holders to reassess collateral and positions in both Cross Margin and Isolated Margin accounts after the adjustment, warning that insufficient maintenance margin or price fluctuations could trigger liquidation.
