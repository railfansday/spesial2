Back to Black Friday: 'We are now bombarded with offers throughout the year'

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Black Friday was once a single day of holiday sales, held the day after Thanksgiving. Then retailers began kicking things off a day early, on Thanksgiving itself. “We are now bombarded with offers throughout the year,” says Hilary Ingham, Professor of Economics and Department Head at Lancaster University, who has no plans to shop this Black Friday. Ingham acknowledges a growing sense of “retail fatigue” but still sees the potential for a peak in sales. Meanwhile, an investigation by The Telegraph suggests that six in ten of the most popular products were actually more expensive than earlier in the year, underscoring the need for consumers to remain vigilant and track prices over time.
