Kingdom Hearts 4 Snubbed For Most Anticipated At The Game Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Kingdom Hearts 4 has been noticeably left out of The Game Awards 2026’s Most Anticipated award, despite having a “100 percent” set-in-stone release next year.

Yesterday, The Game Awards finally shared its nominations for its upcoming end-of-year show, including a bunch of potential awards for GTA 6 before most have had a chance to play (which was controversial until the glowing reviews). Most of the categories and nominations weren’t all that surprising and about what you’d expect, including the divisive Most Anticipated.

This year around we’ve got Fable, Final Fantasy 7 Revelation, God of War Laufey, Resident Evil Veronica, and, despite it not releasing until 2028, The Witcher 4. A very strong lineup to be sure, but there’s one game that’s sorely missing from the category despite being one of the year’s most anticipated releases - Kingdom Hearts 4.

As pointed out by Redditor greatestlantern on the Kingdom Hearts subreddit, Kingdom Hearts 4 is noticeably missing from the Most Anticipated nominees. While I’ll be the first to admit that there is some bias on every fan’s part, Kingdom Hearts 4 is confidently aiming for a late 2027 release and is hugely anticipated.

The latest trailer for Kingdom Hearts 4 might be short, but it’s absolutely packed with easy to miss details.

Considering how much Geoff Keighley’s show has mentioned Kingdom Hearts and the massive hype for the next game in the past, it’s a little surprising to see it being missed out there. Sure, there are plenty of big games coming in 2027, but it’s a weird choice considering The Witcher 4 has already been confirmed for 2028.

I know Most Anticipated doesn’t just mean next year, but it generally tends to. Otherwise we’d have games like Intergalactic up there.

In the Reddit post about the snub, some fans have suggested that it’s because Final Fantasy 7 Revelation is already representing Square Enix and is unarguably the bigger game out of the two. That’s a fair point, but it still doesn’t stop Kingdom Hearts 4 from being one of the bigger snubs in the category.

Others have pointed out that Kingdom Hearts has had a bit of a rocky track record in the past, with Kingdom Hearts 3 not exactly lighting the world on fire. Again, fair point, but I think the insurmountable hype for KH4 does give it some leeway for an award that’s literally for the Most Anticipated game. Not that I think it’d win over the others, but it should have been highlighted.
