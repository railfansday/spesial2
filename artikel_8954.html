carpet diem and 1000 vases merge textile, ceramic, and glass art at espace commines in paris

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

curator francesco pirrello pairs carpet diem with 1000 vases, using a vertical scenography by hervé sauvage and jean-christophe vaillant to exhibit 32 textile works alongside 100 glass and ceramic objects.

G delaBaume edited by christina vergopoulou November 2, 2026 3 min

Curated by Francesco Pirrello, two international design exhibitions, Carpet Diem and 1000 VASES, converge at Espace Commines in Paris from October 23rd to 25th, 2026. The joint event pairs 32 textile pieces with 100 ceramic and glass objects, creating a unified material dialogue within the historic hall. Through a distinctive vertical scenography, the exhibitions explore contemporary interpretations of craft, form, and material boundaries.

Conceived by Hervé Sauvage and Jean-Christophe Vaillant, the exhibition scenography utilizes a pronounced vertical arrangement to integrate the two disciplines within a singular volume. Rather than isolating objects into segregated typological zones, the layout elevates hanging woven surfaces, embroidered textiles, and sculptural fabrics directly above and adjacent to ceramic and glass vessels displayed on tiered plinths. Carpet Diem showcases diverse fiber manipulations, ranging from structural weaving to experimental surface finishes, while 1000 VASES presents sculptural explorations of the functional vase typology, using glassblowing, slip-casting, and ceramic glazing to bridge functional design and spatial sculpture.
