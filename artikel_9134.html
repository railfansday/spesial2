Introducing the PetaPixel Print Shop: Limited-Edition Fine Art Prints, Made for the Wall

PetaPixel is proud to partner with WhiteWall for the launch of the PetaPixel Print Shop, a series of 10 limited-edition print drops featuring photos from the PetaPixel team and partner artists.

The first print drop features photos from Chris Niccolls, Jaron Schneider, Jeremy Gray, David Imel, Sarah Teng, and Matt Growcoot. Each artist selected the paper, frame, matting, and size options to match their specific vision for the artwork—so when you see these prints in person, you know this is exactly how each artist intended them to appear.

WhiteWall makes hand-crafted, premium photo prints and is the best photo lab worldwide (proven by its eight TIPA’s Best Photo Lab award wins). Prints are available in a variety of sizes depending on the artists’ choices and are printed and shipped directly from WhiteWall to ensure the highest quality: fine-art pigment prints on museum-grade stock, crafted to hold color and detail in real light.

Collect archival prints of photographs made by the people who cover photography every day. Each edition is produced for the wall, not the feed.

WhiteWall’s support also means that the prices you see were set directly by the artists. The price includes production costs and all net sales support the artist directly. Neither WhiteWall nor PetaPixel stands to gain financially from any print sales.

“Here at PetaPixel, we have always loved WhiteWall’s print quality, and many of us have been personally recommending that artists use them for years. When the opportunity arose to work with them directly for this project, everyone was extremely happy that WhiteWall wanted to support us because we knew the quality would be unmatched,” says Jaron Schneider, Editor-in-Chief at PetaPixel.

Working with WhiteWall also means that nearly anyone who wants a print can get one, as WhiteWall ships worldwide. Each print is carefully packed with protective corner and surface guards to guarantee it arrives in pristine condition.

This first edition of the PetaPixel Print Shop drop will be open from November 2, 2026 through January 31, 2027. Once this limited-edition drop is completed, these photos in these exact formats, papers, colors, and frames will never be offered again. Browse the drop today and collect your print!
