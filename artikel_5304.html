HSK Chain and Base Collaborate to Connect Asia's Premier Onchain Asset with Global Liquidity

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

HONG KONG, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- HSK Chain, the institutional-grade blockchain powered by HashKey Group, is collaborating with Base, the blockchain for global finance built by Coinbase. The collaboration pairs HSK Chain's strengths in asset origination and institutional-grade finance with Base's global ecosystem, aiming to connect Asia's premier onchain assets with global liquidity.

HashKey On-Chain will adopt Base as its core onchain network, while HSK Chain will continue to operate independently. Both teams will also partner with Morpho to offer permissioned CeDeFi lending to eligible clients, alongside a full Base integration for HashKey Wallet. Additionally, Base and HSK Chain will serve as the default networks for HashKey's tokenized assets and explore technical collaborations around real-world assets (RWAs) and stablecoins. These initiatives mark a deeper strategic alignment between both ecosystems, with specific details subject to formal documentation to be officially published.

This collaboration marks a significant step toward linking Asia's premier onchain assets with global onchain liquidity. Drawing on their respective strengths in tokenized assets and global ecosystems, both teams will advance the landscape together.

HSK Chain is an institutional-grade blockchain under HashKey (stock code: 3887.HK), dedicated to providing the underlying infrastructure for global on-chain Web3 technology applications, and a secure, compatible and auditable technical operating framework for stablecoin technology, RWA data mapping and institutional DeFi development. As an Ethereum Layer 2 blockchain, HSK Chain aims to connect traditional finance with on-chain activities.

Base is a blockchain built by Coinbase to power the global onchain economy. Designed for trading, payments, and agents, Base delivers sub-second settlement and sub-cent transaction costs at scale. It is the #1 onchain venue for BTC and ETH spot trading, the leading chain for stablecoin volume, and the dominant rail for agentic payments. Open, permissionless, and EVM-compatible, Base is trusted by leading institutions and used by millions of people worldwide.

HSK Chain is a blockchain infrastructure network.
