Quick facts, numbers from Commanders' Week 10 win over Giants

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Washington Commanders looked to have lost another game late, but scored with 57 seconds remaining to defeat the New York Giants 24-21 at MetLife Stadium.

Here are some quick facts from the Commanders' third victory of the 2026 season.

J.J. McCarthy entered the game having won his last six starts for the largest active streak in the NFL (4 with Vikings, 2 with Giants.

But after this Commanders win, Jayden Daniels has still not lost to the New York Giants. He is 4-0-1 against the Giants in his NFL career.

Jayden Daniels had not thrown an interception through eight games. Thursday he threw two... in the fourth quarter! Both came with Washington trailing 21-17. Daniels then threw a 32-yard touchdown to Stefon Diggs with 57 seconds remaining.

Interestingly, both Jayden Daniels and J.J. McCarthy finished the game with 23 completions.

TE Ben Sinnott had a career-high four receptions for 42 yards, including a 24-yarder.

Dan Quinn challenged a Giants first down late, and it was the right call: the play was overturned, and the Giants had to punt.

One Giants' drive was killed in Washington territory when Frankie Luvu forced a Malik Nabers fumble, recovered by Jeremy Reaves. It was his first recovered fumble in his NFL career.

The Commanders converted 7 of 14 third downs; the Giants only 4 of 12.

The Commanders were not killed by penalties, only having four for -17 yards.

The Commanders surrendered a 66-yd TD pass from McCarthy to Isaiah Likely. It was the longest pass of McCarthy's career and the longest reception of Likely's career.

Jayden Daniels finished 23 for 33 for 249 passing yards, three touchdowns and two interceptions.

Sonny Styles led Washington with 13 tackles (5 solo), one for a loss.

Commanders entered the game with 26 presnap penalties, the third most in the NFL.
