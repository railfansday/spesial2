Notre Dame’s Hannah Hidalgo ties program’s career points record

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The feats continue to roll in for Notre Dame senior star guard Hannah Hidalgo this season.

The senior captain tied former Fighting Irish guard Arike Ogunbowale for the most career points in program history (2,626 points), when Hidalgo scored 22 points in the 64-52 Battle 4 Atlantis win over Dayton on Tuesday.

Ogunbowale, who is currently a WNBA all-star for the Dallas Wing, played for Notre Dame in 2015-19 and was an All-American. She was also part of the Irish's national title season in 2017-18.

The recent accomplishment for Hidalgo came after she became the first college basketball player to reach 2,500 points, 500 rebounds, 500 assists and 500 steals in her career.

Hidalgo will have an opportunity to break the career points record on Thursday when USA TODAY Sports Coaches Poll No. 12 Notre Dame (6-0) faces No. 22 West Virginia in the Battle 4 Atlantis.

Through six games, Hidalgo is averaging 27.7 points and is shooting 53.4% from the field and 92.5% from the free-throw line.
