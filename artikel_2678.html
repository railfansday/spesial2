Browns vs. Texans injuries: HOU adds 2 on offense to DNP list, 2 added limited on defense

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

In a game that has huge AFC playoff implications, the Cleveland Browns and Houston Texans started Week 10 with a ton of players not on the field Wednesday. The home team saw two changes to the injury report on Thursday, while the visitors from Houston have a few more concerns than they did just a day ago.

All changes are in bold below for the second injury report of Week 10:

OL Elgton Jenkins – Knee – DNP to Limited

S Donovan McMilon – Hip – Added as Limited

LB Carson Schwesinger – Wrist, Ankle – Limited

LB Azeez Al-Shaair – Groin, Not Injury Related – Personal – DNP

RB David Montgomery – Groin – DNP to Limited

OL Keylan Rutledge – Neck, Shoulder – DNP

DE Will Anderson Jr. – Ankle – Added as Limited

DE Danielle Hunter – Hamstring – Added as Limited

S Reed Blankenship – Concussion, Hand – Limited to Full

WR Tank Dell – Hand, Shoulder – Limited to Full

WR Marvin Harrison Jr. – Hamstring – Limited to DNP

LB Jacob Hummel – Groin – Limited to Full

OL Wyatt Teller – Illness – Limited to Full

Adding Anderson and Hunter to the list as limited at the same time as Harrison and Marks go from limited to DNP is big news for Week 10 and something to monitor on Friday.
