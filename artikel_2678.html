TSG Hoffenheim vs. Sturm Graz preview: Team news and predicted lineups

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

TSG Hoffenheim and Sturm Graz will hope to make huge strides in the Europa League when they meet at SNP Arena on Thursday. Both teams only have four points each after the first four rounds of actions in the competition.

Christian Ilzer's men recorded their points in the last two matchdays as their stunning 3-0 victory over Lyon followed by conceding a late equalizer to Conference League champions Crystal Palace in London. Sturm Graz lost to Celje at home on matchday four.

The visitors also had a tough start to the Austrian Bundesliga season as they trail surprise league leaders SK Rapid by a staggering 15 points after only 13 rounds. Sturm Graz won the title in back-to-back seasons before LASK Linz earned the crown last term in one of the most competitive leagues in Europe after the end of RB Salzburg's decade-long dominance.

It'll be a special game for Illzer as he guided Sturm Graz to their first league triumph in over a decade before taking the helm at Hoffenheim. After a difficult first campaign, the Austrian coach was able to even compete for a Champions League spot last season with the Sinsheim club.

Hoffenheim have been inconsistent in Bundesliga this term as they stay in the bottom half of the table after a narrow defeat at Eintracht Frankfurt on Saturday in a game both sides missed first-half penalty-kicks. However, they won each of their last three games at SNP Arena in all competitions.

This will be the first occasion Hoffenheim clash a club from Austria in a competitive fixture, while Sturm Graz will face a German Bundesliga opponent for the first time since their games against RB Leipzig (1-0 win) and Borussia Dortmund (1-0 loss) in the league phase of the 2024/25 Champions League.

It'll be the first time two Austrian coaches lock horns in the Europa League, although Fabio Ingolitsch is under pressure after his side's winless run extended to six games with Sunday's defeat to Altach.

Nathan De Cat, Valentin Gendrey, Albian Hajdari and Robin Hranac remain on sidelines for the hosts. Similar to his coach, central midfielder Alexander Prass is preparing to face the club he won three trophies with during his time in Graz.

Germany U21 international Joshua Quarshie is a regular fixture in Sturm Graz's defense. Leon Grgic, Daniil Khudyakov, Otar Kiteishvili and Filip Rozga will not be in the traveling squad due to long-serving injuries.
