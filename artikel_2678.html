Jake Ferguson set to miss some time

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Dallas Cowboys TE Jake Ferguson (knee) is set to miss multiple weeks following a Week 9 injury against the Indianapolis Colts.

Why It Matters: Brevyn Spann-Ford is set to be the Cowboys' top tight end in Ferguson's absence. He could be worth an addition in deeper leagues or tight end premium formats.
