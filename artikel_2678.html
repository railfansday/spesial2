MSSU men lose in overtime to SBU, 84-78

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Missouri Southern State University men's basketball team lost 84-78 in overtime to Southwest Baptist University Saturday at home.

SBU won the tip but MSSU struck first as Isaiah Atwater hit a 3-pointer after a pass from Gavyn Elkamil.

Southern held the lead for most of the game.

The teams traded points to make it 10-7 for MSSU before both teams went scoreless over the next few possessions.

MSSU's Jackson Shorter broke the stalemate with a close range jump shot.

SBU (2-2) has been a top-tier rebounding team all year, averaging 40 rebounds a game (57th in the nation), and showed it early in the first half during a possession that started with a defensive rebound and continued after an offensive rebound before Ben Nooman scored to bring the game within 2 points.

Several missed shots and turnovers kept the score 19-17 in favor of the Lions before a personal foul brought Shorter to the line, where he scored twice.

With under 7 minutes left, Isaiah Atwater hit a shot from the top of the lane. The Bearcats then got a steal and a score to keep the game close.

Tyrique Brooks widened the gap with a 3-pointer to give MSSU a 7-point lead.

In the final minutes of the first half, SBU got a layup but Southern responded with Atwater finding Hunter Fitch for an alley-oop that shook the Leggett and Platt Athletic Center. Fitch followed that highlight with another as he would posterize a defender for a dunk.

SBU and MSSU traded blows but the half ended in Southern's favor 38-31.

Starting out the second half, Fitch continued the highlight reel shots with another alley-oop from an Elkamil pass.

SBU answered with a 3-point bucket and then a layup.

The game remained closed but MSSU slowly extended its lead to the largest gap it would get all game, 52-40 with 11 minutes left in the second half.

After that, the Bearcats fought back starting with a quick layup. A steal after would give them another 3 points.

The Lions (3-1) scored a free throw but SBU would get another 2, making the score 53-47. A steal on the next possession gave the Bearcats a breakaway layup. Then SBU got one off a pair of free throws.

Elkamil gave the Lions some breathing room with a 3 but Taevean Famutimi-Brown helped close it up with a layup for SBU.

Both teams traded points until, with 1 minute left on the clock, SBU's Evens Appolon hit a free throw to set the score 64-63, MSSU.

Atwater hit a jumpshot to put MSSU up 3 but SBU soon tied it up. MSSU was unable to score at the end of regulation after an Appolon block.

With just 5 minutes left to complete the comeback, SBU started overtime with a quick layup. Fouls would trouble the Lions as Shorter fouled out and SBU scored off a free throw.

Eventually, SBU pulled away with a 10-6 run to win the game 84-78.

MSSU's men's team plays Rockhurst University at 7 p.m. Tuesday in Kansas City.
