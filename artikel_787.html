Led By Ella Langley, Women Artists Shine in Grammys Country Categories

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Women artists land all five spots in best contemporary country album category, and dominate other country categories.

Ella Langley’s starry 2026 just keeps getting brighter.When the final nominees were revealed for the 2027 Grammy Awards, Langley was among the artists leading the way, with seven total nominations, including nominations in each of the Grammys’ Big Four categories: album of the year (for Dandelion), record of the year and song of the year (“Choosin’ Texas”).In doing so, Langley joined just 16 other artists ever to earn nominations in each of the Big Four categories in a single year, including country artist Bobbie Gentry, who was the first artist to achieve the feat, in 1968. That year, Gentry won the best new artist category, while her album Ode to Billie Joe was nominated for album of the year, and its title track was nominated for song and record of the year. Kacey Musgraves and Beyoncé were the most recent artists to have country projects take home a Grammy for album of the year, with Golden Hour in 2019 and Cowboy Carter in 2025, respectively.The success of “Choosin’ Texas” stretches beyond Langley, as “Choosin’ Texas” and Dandelion garnered nominations for many of the song’s collaborators. Beyond nods in both Big 4 and country categories, “Choosin’ Texas” co-writer Joybeth Taylor also picks up her first nomination in the songwriter of the year, non-classical, while Miranda Lambert earns her first producer of the year, non-classical nomination.Langley, Taylor and Lambert are among many women spanning musical genres who dominated this year’s Grammy nominations, including women earning seven of eight nominations for album of the year.As has been the case for several years, women artists continue to largely struggle to receive airplay on country radio, outside of a handful of women earning significant amounts of airplay at any one time. According to Jada Watson’s Song Data analysis, the years-long decline of women being played on country radio continued in 2025, to a low of 8.08% of country radio airplay.This year, Langley’s “Choosin’ Texas” spent three weeks at the top of Billboard’s Country Airplay chart, while also earning No. 1 Country Airplay hits with “Be Her,” “Loving Life Again,” and the Morgan Wallen collab “I Can’t Love You Anymore.” Beyond Langley, this year’s women chart-toppers on the radio-driven Country Airplay chart have been Stella Lefty with “Boston,” and Lauren Alaina with the Chase Matthew collab “All My Exes.”Zeroing in on this year’s country Grammys nominations highlights the power of women’s songs, stories and perspectives. With the exception of the best traditional country album category, women artists hold the lion’s share of nominations in each of the country categories, which is especially remarkable given that many of the nominated artists haven’t received equal airplay as their male counterparts.Langley’s “Choosin’ Texas,” Megan Moroney’s “Beautiful Things,” Kacey Musgraves’ “Dry Spell,” the Lambert- Stephen Wilson Jr. collab “Hardwired” and Lainey Wilson’s John Mayer collab “Phone, Keys, Wallet” are all nominated in the best country song category. In the best country solo performance category, three of the nominations go to women artists: Langley’s “Choosin’ Texas,” Musgraves’ “Dry Spell,” and Wilson’s “Can’t Sit Still.” In the country duo/group category, Parton’s Zac Brown Band collab “Butterfly,” along with the Musgraves/Lambert collaboration “Horses & Divorces,” Wilson’s John Mayer collab “Phone, Keys, Wallet” and Parton’s all-women collaboration of “Light of a Clear Blue Morning,” featuring Lainey Wilson, Reba McEntire, Queen Latifah and Miley Cyrus, are nominated.Women also hold every nomination in the best contemporary country album category: Kelsea Ballerini’s Mount Pleasant, Carter Faith’s Cherry Valley, Langley’s Dandelion, Moroney’s Cloud 9 (Langley’s and Moroney’s projects both debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 this year) and Musgraves’ Middle of Nowhere.This isn’t the first time women artists have dominated in the country album category. In 2021, prior to the Grammys splitting the country album category into separate traditional and contemporary categories, women artists or groups featuring women vocalists filled the entire best country album category, with nominations going to Lambert’s Wildcard (which took home the win), Little Big Town’s Nightfall, Ashley McBryde’s Never Will, Ingrid Andress’s Lady Like and Brandy Clark’s Your Life Is A Record. In 1999 and 2006, women earned four out of the five nominations in the category. In an interesting split, women fill all five spots in the best contemporary country album category, while men do the same in the best traditional country album category. The best traditional country album category consists of Charley Crockett’s Clovis, Vince Gill’s 50 Years From Home: Down at the Borderline, Cody Johnson’s Banks of the Trinity, Willie Nelson’s Workin’ Man: Willie Sings Merle, and Worsham’s Once Upon a Second Time Around.Some of the projects, especially Musgraves’ country soaked and Tejano/Norteno-laced Middle of Nowhere and Faith’s Cherry Valley, could sonically feel at home in either the contemporary or traditional album category, as could Charlie Worsham’s eclectic Once Upon a Second Time Around.May’s ACM Awards winners could be seen as a harbinger for the traditional country-leaning sounds that dominate the newly-announced Grammy nominees. In May, Johnson took home the ACM’s entertainer of the year; he’s represented in the Grammys best traditional country album category for Banks of the Trinity. Langley took home seven wins at the ACMs, and is among the most-nominated artists heading into the upcoming Grammy awards. ACM winner Lambert scored multiple Grammy nominations for her work on “Choosin’ Texas” and Langley’s Dandelion, as well as for“Hardwired,” a collab with Stephen Wilson Jr. The ACMs also ushered in striking performances and deserved wins for rock-leaning singer-songwriter Wilson Jr., who in addition to “Hardwired,” earned a Grammy nomination for his song “Gary.”Notably, CMA musician of the year winner Worsham earns a win for indie artists. He previously released multiple albums for Warner, but he earned his first-ever Grammy nomination for his new independently-released album Once Upon a Second Time Around. This year’s country Grammys categories also continued honoring long-established Grammy winners. Gill, who has 22 Grammy wins to his credit, continues to be honored for releasing thoughtful music, this year honored for his album 50 Years From Home: Down at the Borderline, and best country solo performance (“The Whole World”). This also marks his 50th Grammy nomination. Meanwhile, Nelson is also nominated in the best traditional country album category.Most prominent would be the late country music icon Dolly Parton, who died in August. Parton earns a trio of nominations: best country duo/group performance category with her Zac Brown Band collaboration “Butterfly,” a collaborative version of “Light of a Clear Blue Morning,” and best audio book, narration, and storytelling recording, for the audio version of her book Star of the Show: My Life on Stage, written with Billboard contributor Tom Roland.Langley’s music received support from both a radio and label perspective, and it has created a history-making success with “Choosin’ Texas” now spending 28 weeks at the top of the Billboard Hot 100. When women artists are given ample support, and their music amplified, it can often lead to cross-over success and enduring careers, such as those of Parton, Shania Twain, and Reba McEntire. Langley is another example, setting the stage for a long, impactful career.Women are making some of country music’s most fearless, impactful, unique music at the moment, and regardless of the particular category they are nominated in, they deserve to be recognized.
