BAFTA North America Breakthrough US Cohort Includes Connor Storrie, Inde Navarrette And Kane Parsons

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

BAFTA North America on Tuesday revealed 14 rising film, TV and games creatives as the organization’s 2026 BAFTA Breakthrough US cohort, a list thank includes Heated Rivalry star Connor Storrie, Obsession star Inde Navarrette and Backrooms director-composer Kane Parsons.

The program, in its seventh year, is designed to support creatives at pivotal points in their careers, opening access to professional development and industry connections. Recipients undertake a yearlong program that includes one-on-one meetings and career guidance, full BAFTA voting membership, access to BAFTA events and screenings, and networking opportunities worldwide.

This year’s list, selected by a jury of industry peers, spans the crafts of costume design, production, casting, editing, games design, performance, directing, composing and cinematography. The names include The ‘Burbs creator Celeste Hughey, 1923 costume designer Gaby Acosta, Nuisance Bear director Gabriela Osio Vanden, Night Nurse writer-director Georgia Bernstein and Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 lead writer Jennifer Svedberg-Yen. “The BAFTA Breakthrough US program is an investment in the extraordinary voices shaping what comes next in film, television and games,” said Courtney LaBarge Bell, executive director of BAFTA North America. “This year’s cohort reflects the imagination, ambition and distinct perspectives that continue to push our industries forward. At a time when emerging creatives face increasing challenges in finding opportunity, mentorship and a path forward, BAFTA is proud to create a space where talent can be recognized, supported and connected to a broader creative community.”
