Brad Pitt Says ‘Ocean’s 14’ Not Shooting Until ‘Late’ 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Brad Pitt is giving an update on the shooting schedule of the much-anticipated “Ocean’s 14.”

“We’re talking about sometime late next year,” he told me Monday at the premiere of “The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth” in Hollywood.

The fifth installment of the “Ocean’s” franchise was supposed to start shooting next month after being postponed from June. Clooney, Pitt, Julia Roberts, Matt Damon and Don Cheadle are set to reprise their roles. David Leitch is set to direct. “I tell you, Grant and George, they can write a good script,” Pitt said.

Clooney first teased there was a “great” script for “Ocean’s 14” in 2023. Roberts said at the Golden Globes in January, “Yes, I have seen a script. And I was kind of surprised, because I was like, ‘Oh, what is the story going to be?’ And it’s good. I mean, we wouldn’t do it if it’s not good.”

“The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth” is a sequel to Quentin Tarantino’s “Once Upon a Time in Hollywood.” Quentin Tarantino wrote the script for the follow-up but handed the directing reins to David Fincher.

“The thing was dead on the shelf for like nine months,” Pitt said. “It goes that way a lot. Then one moment I had this idea … I gave it to [Fincher] when we got on a plane, we landed, and he said, ‘Yeah, I can do that.’ I called Quentin. He was into it, and here we are.”

Pitt won an Oscar for his work in the first movie: “I did have a little moment going, ‘Shit, what did I do in the first one?’ But you get right back into it.”

In the sequel, set a few years after the first in 1970s Los Angeles, Cliff has left Hollywood stunt work to become a projectionist at a movie theater in West Hollywood. He’s also an occasional hit man. Elizabeth Debicki plays his love interest, the talent agent of a television star (Scott Caan) who enlists Cliff’s help when he runs into trouble in the Philippines.

Pitt and Fincher first worked together 32 years ago making “Seven” in 1994. “It’s embarrassing how fortunate we are,” Pitt said. “It’s gross. Those two together are like hydrogen fusion.”

Pitt also said he is eager to do a sequel to his hit racing film “F1: The Movie.”
