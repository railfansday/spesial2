Disney Launching ‘Only Murders in the Building’-Themed Escape Rooms in Cities Across the U.S. (Exclusive)

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The experience is inspired by the sixth season of the series, which will be set in London.

Disney Entertainment on Thursday announced a new escape room experience themed around Only Murders in the Building.

Disney’s Hulu and The Escape Game are introducing “Only Murders in London,” an all-new adventure inspired by the upcoming sixth season of the Emmy-winning comedy series. Starting Dec. 9, the escape experience will debut in Midtown Manhattan before it heads to Los Angeles; Lynnfield, Massachusetts; and Washington, D.C,

“We’re excited to team up with Disney Entertainment and bring a new chapter of Hulu’s Only Murders in the Building to The Escape Game,” said Daniel Daher, senior director of Marketing at the Escape Game. “With the sixth season heading to London, we had the perfect opportunity to reimagine the experience with fresh twists and surprises. We can’t wait for fans to step into the story and test their detective skills.”

The revamped experience coincides with the debut of the show’s sixth season, which sees Charles (Steve Martin), Oliver (Martin Short) and Mabel (Selena Gomez) heading overseas for the first time, trading New York City for London and a new international investigation.

The official description of the experience: “As Charles, Oliver and Mabel take their crime-solving talents across the pond, Escape Game guests will work together to uncover clues and test their detective instincts against the clock. Designed for both longtime fans and newcomers alike, the interactive adventure will bring the humor, intrigue and unexpected twists of Only Murders in the Building into the real world. Expect hidden passageways, familiar props and clever nods to the show throughout.” For more details, visit theescapegame.com.

The logline for season six: “After successfully bringing the guilty to justice once again, Charles, Oliver and Mabel embark on their hardest case yet when the next victim — Cinda Canning — arrives at their doorstep, summoning them to London to catch her killer. Our beloved trio of true crime-solving podcasters must now navigate their way through the home of the cozy murder mystery, replete with a whole new collection of suspicious and particularly English-eccentric characters. But can they follow in the footsteps of the woman whose podcast started it all for them … without having their story end the same as hers?”

Special guest stars will include Olivia Colman, Meryl Streep, David Tennant, Nicola Coughlan and Martin Freeman. The show hails from co-creators and writers Steve Martin and John Hoffman and is produced by 20th Television.

The Escape Game is an experiential entertainment company with 65 retail locations and a team of over 1,500 employees, who have served over 13 million guests at TEG locations nationwide.
