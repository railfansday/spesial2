Jeff Goldblum, Marion Cotillard and John Turturro Dine With Morocco’s HRH Prince Moulay Rachid at Starry Royal Gala During Marrakech Film Festival

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Jeff Goldblum, Marion Cotillard, John Turturro and more stars got the royal treatment on Saturday night at a glitzy dinner hosted by Morocco’s His Royal Highness Prince Moulay Rachid and Her Royal Highness Princess Lalla Oum Keltoum during the Marrakech Film Festival.

Held at the 16th-century El Badi Palace — known for its stunning architecture and dramatic ruins — guests were greeted with fresh passion fruit and strawberry juice (and by the palace’s resident orange tabby cat) before being ushered into the glass-walled hall for dinner. Raised above dozens of meticulously-set tables was a large head table, decorated with a smattering of colorful flowers, where the guests of honor took their seats. The entire room rose to its feet as HRH Prince Moulay Rachid and HRH Princess Lalla Oum Keltoum arrived, joining Marrakech Film Festival jury president Cotillard as well as Goldblum and Turturro, who are both receiving career tributes. The festival was created by HRH Prince Moulay Rachid’s brother Mohammed VI, the King of Morocco, 23 years ago, with the entire royal family still heavily involved in the black-tie affair.

Other guests seated at the head table included Cotillard’s fellow jury members Emma Mackey, Vicky Krieps, Claes Bang, Gilles Lellouche, Kaouther Ben Hania, Andrew Haigh, Ramata-Toulaye Sy and Saïd Hamich Benlarbi, as well as festival director Melita Toscan du Plantier. There were plenty of bold-faced industry names seated throughout the room, including Francesca Scorsese, daughter of director Martin Scorsese, who is a friend of the festival, and Mubi CEO Efe Cakarel.

While a live orchestra played famed scores from throughout cinema history, attendees were served a traditional Moroccan feast including lamb tagine, a mound of couscous and pastillas, which was, of course, accompanied by plenty of mint tea.

As the dinner wound down, guests got up out of their seats to mingle and migrate to a more intimate room. Red velvet couches invited guests to settle in, while photos of famous faces who had graced the festival throughout years past dotted the walls. Goldblum and his wife, Emilie Goldblum, chatted animatedly to Mackey while Krieps — who was sporting a chic headscarf printed with red roses — caught up with Cakarel.

The Marrakech Film Festival runs through Nov. 28, when Cotillard and her jury will award one of the 14 competition films — consisting of first and second features — the prestigious Étoile d’Or.
