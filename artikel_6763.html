Noah Cyrus Delivers a Daring Take on Sister-of-the-Bride Attire

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

She chose a goth naked dress for Miley’s intimate nuptials this weekend

This past weekend, Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando tied the knot with an intimate ceremony in Palm Springs, California, where both the bride and groom wore Saint Laurent by Anthony Vaccarello. For the occasion, Cyrus chose a silk boatneck maxi dress with an open back, while Morando wore a classic black tuxedo. And based on early photos from the ceremony, it appears that all of their guests were asked to stick to a black-and-white color scheme, too.

This was further evidenced late last night, when Cyrus’s younger sister, Noah, shared a series of Instagram photos showing off her gothic sister-of-the-bride ensemble.

For the wedding weekend, Noah chose a plunging scoop-neck maxi dress in black, made of a semi-sheer fabric that was decorated with jacquard florals. The body-hugging design featured cap sleeves and slight ruffles at the bottom of the skirt, and Noah paired it with a set of matching peep-toe heels, while layering a black blazer on top and matching panties underneath.

Later, based on photos she shared to her Instagram Story, it appears Noah switched into another black dress—a high-neck bodycon piece with a large silk sash tied into a bow along her waist.

Other members of Cyrus’s family who were in attendance included her mom, Tish, and her mother’s husband, Dominic Purcell, as well as Cyrus’s dad, Billy Ray Cyrus, and his girlfriend, Elizabeth Hurley. Miley’s older half-sister, Brandi, was also seen leaving the venue with her fiancé, Matt Southcombe. It is unclear whether Miley’s younger brother, Braison, or her half-brothers, Trace and Christopher, were present.
