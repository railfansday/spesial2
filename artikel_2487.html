Mike LaFleur sends clear message after Cardinals fire DC following Chiefs blowout

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Mike LaFleur has explained his decision to dismiss defensive coordinator Nick Rallis after the Arizona Cardinals suffered a heavy 34-7 defeat against the Kansas City Chiefs.

Arizona have struggled defensively throughout the 2026 season, allowing 29.4 points per game, the second-worst record in the NFL. They have also conceded 355.2 total yards per game.

The defeat against Kansas City proved to be the breaking point for LaFleur, who has appointed senior defensive assistant Teryl Austin as interim defensive coordinator.

Speaking about his decision, LaFleur explained that he felt a change was necessary to address the team's defensive problems.

He said: "I just felt in my heart this morning we needed a change, a fresh voice."

LaFleur also took responsibility for Arizona's wider struggles, making it clear that the problems extend beyond one side of the ball.

"It has not been good enough collectively," he said. "All phases. As a team. It's my job to find solutions, and this is one we are trying to find."

The Cardinals have now allowed at least 30 points in four of their last five games and eight times overall this season. Their 3-8 record reflects a difficult campaign, with the defence repeatedly struggling to contain opposing offences.

Against the Chiefs, Patrick Mahomes threw for 324 yards and three touchdowns while Travis Kelce caught six passes for 74 yards and two scores. Kansas City finished with 435 total yards compared to Arizona's 205.

Austin now faces the challenge of improving a defence that has struggled to find consistency. He has previously served as defensive coordinator for the Detroit Lions, Cincinnati Bengals and Pittsburgh Steelers.

LaFleur believes Austin's experience makes him the right person to take charge for the remainder of the campaign.

"He's been around this a long time," the Cardinals head coach said. "He's been around successful defenses. I just thought it was the right time. He'll put his own spin on this."

However, the defensive changes are only part of Arizona's problems. The Cardinals have scored fewer than eight points in four of their last 11 games and have used three different starting quarterbacks across their last three fixtures.

Austin's first major test will come against the Washington Commanders, with LaFleur needing to see an immediate improvement from a team that has struggled on both sides of the ball.
