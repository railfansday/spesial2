UCSB opens the season with an 85-55 win over San Francisco State

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

SANTA BARBARA, Calif. (AP) — Antonio Chol scored 13 points as UCSB began the season Monday with an 85-55 win over San Francisco State.

Chol shot 3 for 8 from the field, including 2 for 5 from 3-point range, and 5 for 7 from the free-throw line for the Gauchos. Eric Dibami scored 12 points, going 4 for 5 from the field and 4 for 6 from the free-throw line. Marvin McGhee had nine points and shot 3 for 4 from the field, including 2 for 3 from beyond the arc.

Achilles Woodson led the Gators with 14 points. San Francisco State also got 11 points from Kiahn Nice. Dontrell Johnson finished with nine points and five steals.
