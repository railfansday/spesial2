Koepka’s Untucked Shirt Sparks Heated Golf Debate

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Brooks Koepka sparked a fresh debate over golf etiquette after appearing with an untucked Nike polo shirt during Friday's Skins Game. The five-time major champion played at Atlantic Fields Club in Hobe Sound, Florida, breaking a long-standing unwritten rule about professional presentation. While the PGA Tour strictly enforces tucked-in shirts at standard events and majors, this Black Friday exhibition operates outside those official regulations. The casual look immediately split the audience watching the Prime Video broadcast, proving that even entertainment formats have their own line in the sand.

The Farmers Insurance Skins Game featured a $4 million purse and included Justin Thomas, Wyndham Clark, and rising star Jackson Koivun. Even though the occasion was laid-back, social media traditionalists immediately called the clothing sloppy and disrespectful to the game's customs. Numerous people claimed that leading professionals ought to uphold standard presentation no matter the format. One X user opined, "Love Brooks but gotta tuck the shirt in," while another wrote, "Brooks playing in the Skins Game with his shirt untucked is making my eye twitch." A third viewer added that "Brooks is really not helping the apparel traditionalist with his designed untucked golf shirt," according to GolfMagic.

Some supporters of the 36-year-old noted that the Skins Game aims to be a relaxed, entertainment-first spectacle instead of a formal championship. This marks another instance where Koepka's wardrobe selections attracted notice. At the Masters, Koepka has also donned collarless mock-neck shirts. Friday's round marked Koepka's debut in this specific exhibition format, yet his fashion choices continue to generate more conversation than his swing mechanics in some circles.

The reaction highlights a growing tension between golf's rigid history and the modern player's desire for individual expression. Golf has always been a sport where the clothes matter almost as much as the score, yet the lines are blurring. Koepka's decision to wear a shirt designed to be untucked challenges the notion that a pro must look the same as a club amateur. The criticism from fans suggests that for many, the visual cue of a tucked shirt signals respect for the competition and the game itself. When that signal is missing, even in a fun event, it feels wrong to those who grew up watching the strict dress codes of the Tour.

However, the Skins Game has always been an outlier in the golf calendar. It was created to be a high-stakes, low-pressure show where the rules are looser to encourage aggressive play. If the format allows for a relaxed vibe, why should the attire be different? Koepka, known for his aggressive play style, seems to be applying the same logic to his fashion. He isn't trying to break the rules of the PGA Tour here; he is simply embracing the specific culture of the exhibition.

Fans who feel the "eye twitch" reaction are defending a standard that has existed for decades. The untucked look might feel like a step too far for purists who believe that professionalism requires a uniform look.

Koepka's season has been a mix of highs and lows, with the player himself calling his performance "pretty pathetic" in August when he missed the playoffs. He stated, "To be in the prime of your career and be as good as I think I am, I think that's very pathetic, disappointing," according to GolfMagic. That self-criticism shows a player who is constantly re-evaluating his game, and perhaps his image is part of that process. The untucked shirt might be a small rebellion against the pressure of the season, a way to separate the serious business of winning majors from the fun of the Skins Game.
