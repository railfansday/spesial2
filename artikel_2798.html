Jesse Minter Reveals Concerning Update on Ravens Kicker Situation

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Baltimore Ravens are facing a setback at kicker as Tyler Loop deals with a right groin injury that could force him to miss several weeks. His condition has raised questions about his availability and the team's plans for the position.

With the Ravens considering placing Loop on Injured Reserve, Jake Moody is set to handle kicking duties. The change comes as Baltimore prepares for its Thursday night matchup with the Jacksonville Jaguars.

The Baltimore Ravens are dealing with injuries at multiple positions ahead of Thursday's game against the Jacksonville Jaguars. Their kicking situation remains uncertain, while injuries in the quarterback room have added to the team's injury concerns.

Head coach Jesse Minter said the Ravens are considering placing their kicker on Injured Reserve because of a right groin injury. He has been dealing with the issue for some time. Minter addressed the possibility, saying: "Tyler is has been dealing with something um potentially may go on IR dealing with a little bit of an injury uh so we'll see how that shakes out."

An Injured Reserve placement would sideline him for at least four weeks. However, the Ravens have not made a final decision.

Against the Buffalo Bills, he missed a 34-yard field goal and sent a kickoff out of bounds. The play allowed Buffalo to start a possession at its own 40-yard line before scoring a touchdown. He has made 13 of 17 field goal attempts this season after converting 30 of 34 during his rookie campaign.

Jake Moody will take over as the Ravens' kicker after the team signed him to its practice squad as a competitor for Tyler Loop. Baltimore brought him in on October 20 to compete for the kicking position. The San Francisco 49ers selected Moody in the third round of the 2023 NFL Draft.

Moody has made 64 of 82 field goal attempts and 107 of 110 extra-point attempts during his NFL career. Ravens long snapper Nick Moore also discussed Moody's work during practice.

The Ravens also have concerns at quarterback after Lamar Jackson and Tyler Huntley suffered injuries against Buffalo. Jackson injured his right thumb, while Huntley sustained a toe injury. Their availability for Thursday's game remains uncertain.

Joe Fagnano entered the game after both quarterbacks exited against the Bills. He completed 10 of 21 passes for 91 yards, with one interception and one lost fumble. However, Fagnano is also dealing with an ankle injury.

Minter said Fagnano was working through multiple physical issues but expected him to have a chance to play against Jacksonville. The Ravens continue evaluating their quarterback options ahead of Thursday's game.

Baltimore also signed quarterback Austin Reed to its practice squad to add depth at the position while Jackson and Huntley deal with their injuries.

The Baltimore Ravens enter Thursday's matchup with Jake Moody handling kicking duties while Joe Fagnano's status remains uncertain. The team has not yet decided whether to place Tyler Loop on Injured Reserve.
