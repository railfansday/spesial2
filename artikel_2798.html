Kelce Knows Arrowhead Magic While Chiefs Face Injury Risk

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Travis Kelce knows exactly what makes Arrowhead Stadium special. The veteran tight end said the real magic happens when players step out of the tunnel. He told ESPN that those moments are something he will cherish forever, according to a report. Kelce loves making big plays for the fans late in the game. He believes the crowd pours so much energy into the stadium that it forces him to play harder. This bond between player and fan is why the 37-year-old refuses to take the stadium for granted, even as the franchise plans a new home for 2031.

That love for the game was tested on Sunday. Kelce tweaked his ankle in the first quarter against the Atlanta Falcons. He left the field briefly but returned to finish the matchup. He recorded two receptions for 30 yards in the 35-13 win. Head coach Andy Reid addressed the media on Wednesday with a mixed message. The coach noted that the star player was not happy with the tackle that caused the injury. Reid said, "He was very mad, wanted to play." The veteran has dealt with this specific issue before and usually recovers fast.

Despite the pain, Kelce is insisting he will play this weekend. He addressed the incident on his "New Heights" podcast and clarified the play. He does not believe the Falcons defender acted with malicious intent. Kelce admitted the pain made him think the tackle was intentional at first. After reviewing the play, he changed his mind and blamed the physical nature of the sport. The team needs him healthy for the upcoming stretch, but the injury remains a concern for the offense.

The hype around the stadium is growing beyond just football. Caitlin Clark appeared on the podcast on Wednesday to discuss her new shoe line. She gifted Kelce and his brother a pair of her signature sneakers. Kelce loved the all-black colorway and called them fire. She said her friends are planning a blackout theme for the suite. They will all wear the Caitlin 1 "Triple Black" shoes during the event.

Kelce responded to her plan by saying the team is standing on business. The Chiefs currently sit at 7-2 and lead the AFC West. The offense has scored 35 or more points in consecutive games. Patrick Mahomes threw for 348 yards and five touchdowns in the last win. The team faces the Arizona Cardinals next on Sunday at 1 p.m.
