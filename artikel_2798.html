Super Bowl LXI is heading to IMAX cinemas. How to get tickets

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Super Bowl is coming to IMAX cinemas near you for the first time in history.

The National Football League (NFL), ESPN, Walt Disney Company and IMAX are partnering to bring the Super Bowl to IMAX theaters for the first time in ESPN's 47-year history, "giving fans a new, larger-than-life way to experience America's biggest sporting event."

ESPN's telecast of Super Bowl LXI will be shown at hundreds of IMAX locations across the U.S. and in select international markets, including Australia, New Zealand and Brazil, a Disney news release said, adding the screening will mark "a milestone in cinema" by being the "largest live event in IMAX history."

This year's broadcast will be ABC's first Super Bowl telecast since 2006, when the network aired Super Bowl 40.

"The Super Bowl is a one-of-a-kind global cultural event imbued with spectacle, sound, drama, and heroes — all the makings of a great IMAX experience," IMAX CEO Rich Gelfond said in a statement. "This is a milestone for IMAX that further elevates our standing as a premier global platform for entertainment and events, and we're excited to work in partnership with Disney, ESPN and the NFL to give football fans an unforgettable night."

ESPN's Monday Night Football broadcast team of Joe Buck, Troy Aikman, Lisa Salters and Laura Rutledge will call the game.

Super Bowl LXI is scheduled for Sunday, Feb. 14, 2027, Valentine's Day, with kickoff at 6 p.m. ET. The big game will be at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles for a second time.

Disney said the event's expansion into IMAX "extends the reach of the Super Bowl across more platforms than ever," and creates "an entirely new opportunity for fans to come together and experience the NFL's premier game in a communal setting."

IMAX theater tickets for Super Bowl LXI are expected to go on sale in January 2027, according to the news release. A date has not yet been specified.

Disney said additional details, including a list of participating theater locations and ticketing information, will be released in the coming weeks. For more information, fans can visit IMAX.com or local theaters that sell IMAX tickets.

Super Bowl 61 will air on ESPN and stream on ESPN+.
