NFL predictions: Our picks for Giants-Jaguars, the full Week 11 slate

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The New York Giants face major test this week when they face the Jacksonville Jaguars. It is also a game that will provide a huge swing in the Giants' expected chances to make the playoffs. Will the Giants upset Jacksonville? See what our Big Blue View staff members think about that game and the rest of the Week 11 action in this week's staff picks.

I think there's a path to a win for the Giants, if they can find it. The Giants are one of the very best teams in the NFL at forcing drives to end in punts. At the same time, the Jaguars will be without their starting center and the Giants are one of the best teams in the League at creating quick pressure. That's important because Trevor Lawrence has been one of the quarterbacks most affected by pressure in the NFL.

At the same time, the Giants have rediscovered the explosive pass with J.J. McCarthy, which has been the weakness of the Jags' defense.

If the Giants can score touchdowns while disrupting Lawrence, they've got the chance to come away with the upset. We've seen the Giants rebound after bad games, as well as play much better than anticipated when they're underdogs. Maybe both will happen here.

The flip side of the coin is simpler and scarier to me: The Giants have one of the worst red zone defenses in the NFL, and the Jaguars have one of the best red zone offenses in the league. If the Giants can't stop them between the 20s, this game could get ugly fast.

Jacksonville is one of the hottest teams in the league with a young and explosive offense that can run the football. Their defense is aggressive but doesn't NEED to bring the blitz — they blitz at about a league-average rate, but Anthony Campanile does an excellent job utilizing simulated pressure to harass opposing quarterbacks. Jacksonville is third in offensive EPA, 4th in defensive EPA allowed, and 1st in Net EPA — they're a juggernaut based on their performance and the advanced analytics.

Still, that doesn't mean the Giants don't have a chance — they just must play efficient football and avoid the catastrophic mistakes that have robbed them of wins. It's possible, but against the most efficient team in the NFL, it will be difficult. John Harbaugh will have the Giants ready and prepared for the game, and I believe it will be more exciting and competitive. Jacksonville is concluding their third straight road game, which could give the Giants a slight advantage at home, but I ultimately believe the better team finds a way to win.

It took 11 weeks, but for the first time this season, the Giants will face a team with a winning record when the 7-2 Jaguars come to town on Sunday. Not only is Jacksonville running away with the AFC South (they lead the division by four games), but they're steamrolling the competition. They've won their last three games by a combined 75 points. This is easily Big Blue's toughest test of the season, so far.

Jacksonville is very good on both sides of the ball. They're fourth in offensive DVOA, third in defensive DVOA, and third in overall DVOA. They also have an excellent field goal kicker with crazy range. If you look at both rosters, it's hard to find clear advantages for the Giants at any position group outside of tight end, and even there, Jacksonville has good players.

Can the Giants win this game? Yes. They're at home, they're getting an extra three days of rest, and as we saw when they played Houston, they aren't scared and can surprise as a big underdog. They'll need to dial it up on defense and pressure Trevor Lawrence into the kinds of mistakes he used to make before Liam Coen got there. They also might have a little extra motivation after letting last week's game against the Commanders slip away at the end.

I won't be surprised if John Harbaugh's team hangs in there and keeps it as a one-score game most of the way. But in the end, until the Giants show me that they can beat a really good team, I can't pick them to do so.

You can talk yourself into believing that the Giants can beat Jacksonville this Sunday. They needed a miraculous last-second 57-yard field goal to beat the Eagles team (with Jalen Hurts) that the Giants defeated (without Hurts). They lost to the Texans team that the Giants beat the following week. The woeful Titans were within a TD against them last week with 5 minutes left. So indeed, it could be a competitive game. Unfortunately, the Jags have also won games by 24, 29, 35, and 23 points this season. They have scored 31 or more in each of their past four games, and over 40 in two of those. The Giants have only scored more than 30 points once, and only then because of a 99-yard pick-6 on the last play of the game.

Trevor Lawrence isn't necessarily playing any better this year than in the past. He's a QB that simultaneously makes great passes and puts the ball up for grabs, yet unlike in years past, he's cut down on the INTs while getting the TDs. Part of that may be due to 5'10" wide receiver Parker Washington (a 2023 6th-round pick), who had a breakout 2025 and is on pace for 1,000 yards. Running back Bhayshul Tuten, a 4th round 2025 pick, isn't quite in the Jahmyr Gibbs – Kenneth Walker class but he's not far below and is a missed tackle machine. A lot of the credit has to go to head coach Liam Coen, who also calls the plays and turned this team around as soon as he was hired. Lawrence, like most QBs, has trouble with pressure, but he is not too fazed by the blitz. Finding a way to disrupt his rhythm is a key to any chance the Giants may have.

On defense, the Jaguars are strong at linebacker with fourth-round 2023 pick Ventrell Miller, one of the league's best (note how many mid-round picks the Jags have hit on), along with Foyesade Oluokun. They also put on a decent pass rush with Josh Hines-Allen, Travon Walker, and Dennis Gardeck. In the secondary, Travis Hunter is playing more like a No. 2 pick, having mostly given up his evening gig as a receiver.

Are the Giants a serious playoff contender? This is the type of game they can use to make a statement against a team that is in the Super Bowl conversation. I think they'll put up a fight. I also don't think they're there yet.

I want to convince myself to pick the Giants to defeat the Jaguars. I really do. I just can't. The Jaguars are a team with justifiable Super Bowl ambitions. They are a well-rounded team with a high-powered offense, a very good defense, and one of the best field-goal kickers in the business in Cam Little.

The Giants are a good team. They are resilient. They will give the Jaguars their best shot. Maybe, just maybe, it will be good enough to pull off an upset that would go a long way toward ensuring that the Giants make the playoffs.
