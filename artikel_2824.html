Patrick Mahomes’ Trainer Fires Off 7-Word Message on Officiating Controversy in Chiefs-Bills Thanksgiving Thriller

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Kansas City Chiefs thought they had one last chance to steal a Thanksgiving victory from the Buffalo Bills, but a controversial replay decision changed everything.

A stunning one-handed grab by Rashee Rice was overturned in the final minutes, leaving Patrick Mahomes and the Chiefs furious after their 13-10 defeat. Now, Mahomes' longtime trainer, Bobby Stroupe, has joined the criticism, firing off a pointed seven-word message about the officials' decision.

With the score tied 10-10 inside the two-minute warning, Kansas City faced a difficult third-and-10 from its own 9-yard line. The Chiefs needed a big play to keep their potential game-winning drive alive.

Mahomes, who ranks sixth among quarterbacks with an 83.9 PFN QB Impact Grade, found wide receiver Rashee Rice over the short middle in the tight coverage. Rice appeared to make an impressive diving, one-handed catch that would have given Kansas City a much-needed first down.

While the officials on the field initially ruled the play a completed pass, the booth initiated an automatic official review given the timing in the final two minutes. After looking at the replay, officials overturned the decision, ruling that Rice did not maintain control of the football through his contact with the ground.

Although Kansas City subsequently forced Buffalo to punt, the Chiefs went three-and-out on their final possession. Joe Andreessen then partially tipped Matt Araiza's punt, with Greg Dortch's 17-yard return setting up Bass' game-winning 57-yard field goal.

Stroupe, who has trained Mahomes since he was nine years old, was clearly unhappy with the decision. Taking to X after the game, he wrote, "The ground didn't help secure the catch."

He was not the only person close to Mahomes who expressed frustration with the call. The quarterback's wife, Brittany Mahomes, also took to Instagram Stories to share her reaction.

"HAHAHAHAHA WHAT A JOKE," she wrote. "Guess we can't even play football without getting it called against us… not sure what an actual catch is after that, I guess? I don't wanna hear [expletive] anymore from anyone."

In his postgame press conference, Mahomes took a measured approach. "I thought from my angle when I threw the ball, I thought his hand was under the ball, but I haven't seen the replay, so I can't put too much into it," the Chiefs superstar said.

The loss dropped Kansas City to 8-3, while Buffalo improved to 10-1 and secured a crucial head-to-head tiebreaker. That puts the Chiefs in a tougher position as they look to secure the AFC's No. 1 seed.
