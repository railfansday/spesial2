Why Carlos Alcaraz now deserves even more credit for splitting with Juan Carlos Ferrero after winning the ATP Finals

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Around this time last year, it was announced that Carlos Alcaraz and Juan Carlos Ferrero would be ending their partnership together.

It marked the conclusion of a hugely successful professional relationship, with the latter having helped guide his young compatriot to six Grand Slam titles and world number one, among many other accolades.

Many were left perplexed by this decision, especially considering it was coming off the back of a year where he had added another two majors to his trophy cabinet.

It's fair to say that now, roughly 12 months on from that bombshell decision, Carlos Alcaraz can feel confident that he made the right move.

However, when he announced that he would be splitting with Ferrero, Alcaraz came under some rather intense scrutiny.

In fact, even Ferrero was said to have criticised Alcaraz, though he later came out to deny it.

Guy Forget, who enjoyed a long and successful career on the ATP Tour, was one particularly vocal critic. He spoke after rumours emerged that it was finances which had torn the two apart, claiming: "I would find it a little strange, because today the sums generated by Carlos Alcaraz or Jannik Sinner are so disproportionate that… I mean, a coach like Juan Carlos Ferrero today, I can't imagine that he couldn't be paid at his fair value.

"So if this is really the case, it's frankly ridiculous, because a player who has to generate, I don't know, 30 million euros a year, who pays his coach 800,000 or 1,500,000… at the end, it brings him so much more than he can "pay", in any case."

Alcaraz even admitted that criticism impacted him during his Australian Open run, despite going on to win the title.

Well, though five of the last 12 months have been spent dealing with a long-term wrist injury, it's fair to say that the 23-year-old's decision has already been vindicated.

Admittedly, it could be argued that by winning the Australian Open, he immediately justified his choice.

However, with what he has gone on to achieve since returning from injury, his stance has only strengthened.

After all, the 23-year-old has managed to claim titles in places he had previously failed with under Ferrero, like the Paris Masters and ATP Finals. The Davis Cup could be the next domino to fall.

For all the ridicule Alcaraz received for trusting in Samu Lopez and his brother, Alvaro, it's fair to say he has silenced the doubters. He is now poised to enjoy even greater success in 2027, provided he can keep fit.
