NFL playoff picture Week 12: Rams smack Packers out of projected field

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Every week for the duration of the 2026 regular season, USA TODAY Sports will provide timely updates to the NFL's ever-evolving playoff picture − typically starting Thursday night and then moving forward for the remainder of the week (through Sunday's and Monday's games ... plus Saturday's, if applicable. And, when the holidays roll around, we'll be watching then, too).

What just happened? What does it mean? What are the pertinent factors (and, perhaps, tiebreakers) prominently in play as each conference's seven-team bracket begins to crystallize? All will be explained and analyzed up to the point when the postseason field is finalized on Sunday, Jan. 10.

Here's where things stand with Week 12 underway:

1. Buffalo Bills (9-1), AFC East leaders: Big game on Thanksgiving night as they try to take another step toward the home-field advantage they've never had during the Josh Allen era − but could secure in the first year they're in their glorious new stadium. The offense isn't firing on all cylinders right now, but the defense has been sublime lately ... albeit against mostly second-string QBs, who haven't engineered a TD drive against Buffalo since Nov. 1. Remaining schedule: vs. Chiefs, at Patriots, at Packers, vs. Bears, at Broncos, at Dolphins, vs. Jets

2. Denver Broncos (8-2), AFC West leaders: Seems like a lot is going their way, from the AFC's easiest remaining schedule to drawing the assignment against new Pittsburgh QB1 Mason Rudolph on Friday. A second straight No. 1 seed seems quite viable depending on how things unfold. They moved back atop the division Sunday thanks to their advantage in the common-games tiebreaker with the Chiefs − which pretty much boils down to Denver's OT defeat of Seattle. Remaining schedule: at Steelers, vs. Dolphins, at Jets, at Raiders, vs. Bills, at Patriots, vs. Chargers

3. Jacksonville Jaguars (7-3), AFC South leaders: A roster reloaded ahead of the trade deadline and a weak division should keep the Jags on the fast track to a second straight divisional crown. But Sunday's loss to the Giants could sting as it pertains to a pursuit of the top seed. At least they regained a spot with Cincinnati's loss Monday night. Remaining schedule: vs. Titans, at Bears, vs. Steelers, at Texans, at Cowboys, vs. Commanders, at Colts

4. Cincinnati Bengals (7-3), AFC North leaders: Their divisional advantage dwindled to one game with Monday night's last-second loss and could be a major blow to any bid for the No. 1 seed. Still, Aaron Rodgers' departure in Pittsburgh further pronounces the gulf between QB Joe Burrow and his divisional counterparts. Remaining schedule: vs. Saints, at Browns, vs. Chiefs, at Panthers, at Colts, vs. Ravens, vs. Browns

5. Kansas City Chiefs (8-2), wild card No. 1: Doesn't help having the AFC's toughest schedule the rest of the way. And despite cruising past Arizona on Sunday, K.C. also lost its tiebreaker advantage over Denver for the time being. Remaining schedule: at Bills, at Rams, at Bengals, vs. Patriots, vs. 49ers, at Chargers, vs. Raiders

6. Cleveland Browns (6-4), wild card No. 2: Fruitful bye week as they moved up one spot in the seeding thanks to Pittsburgh's loss on the field ... to say nothing of the Steelers' disarray behind center. The Browns' superior AFC North record provides them an advantage over the Steelers, with whom they split their season series. Remaining schedule: vs. Raiders, vs. Bengals, vs. Falcons, at Giants, at Ravens, vs. Colts, vs. Bengals

7. Pittsburgh Steelers (6-4), wild card No. 3: As if the Week 13 game against Houston didn't loom as potentially crucial for both squads, Friday should foretell how viable the Steelers are the rest of the way after the abrupt pivot at quarterback. Remaining schedule: vs. Broncos, vs. Texans, at Jaguars, vs. Ravens, vs. Panthers, at Titans, at Ravens

8. New England Patriots (5-5), in the hunt: They're without QB Drake Maye and running out of time to make a run, especially with the opponents they're about to face. Remaining schedule: at Chargers, vs. Bills, vs. Vikings, at Chiefs, at Jets, vs. Broncos, vs. Dolphins

9. Houston Texans (4-6), in the hunt: They moved up from 11th place after Week 11's rollover of the Colts. On the outer rim of the playoff fringe for weeks, the schedule should provide a pathway to relevance ... and maybe more. Remaining schedule: vs. Ravens, at Steelers, at Commanders, vs. Jaguars, at Eagles, at Packers, vs. Titans

10. Las Vegas Raiders (4-7), in the hunt: A superior conference record keeps them ahead of Baltimore. Remaining schedule: at Browns, BYE, vs. Chargers, vs. Broncos, vs. Titans, at Cardinals, at Chiefs

11. Baltimore Ravens (4-7), in the hunt: The chance they give QB Lamar Jackson a reason to return this season looks increasingly remote. Remaining schedule: at Texans, BYE, vs. Buccaneers, at Steelers, vs. Browns, at Bengals, vs. Steelers

1. San Francisco 49ers (9-1), NFC West leaders: They turned a highly anticipated matchup against Minnesota's vaunted defense into a rout − and hung on to the top seed last weekend, when the Niners were in jeopardy of losing it. Remaining schedule: vs. Seahawks, at Giants, vs. Rams, at Chargers, at Chiefs, vs. Eagles, at Cardinals

2. Detroit Lions (8-2), NFC North leaders: Their first head-to-head meeting with the Bears on Thanksgiving will determine who's atop the division. Remaining schedule: vs. Bears, at Falcons, vs. Titans, at Vikings, vs. Giants, at Bears, at Packers

3. New York Giants (6-3-1), NFC East leaders: They have the most difficult remaining schedule in their division. But they knocked off Jacksonville on Sunday, and the Cowboys have yet to prove they can take advantage of their opportunities to gain ground this season. Remaining schedule: at Colts, vs. 49ers, at Seahawks, vs. Browns, at Lions, at Cowboys, vs. Eagles

4. Carolina Panthers (5-5), NFC South leaders: By squeaking past the Ravens on Sunday, they opened up a two-game lead on the rest of the division. But getting swept by Tampa Bay would cost them ground and a massive tiebreaker. Remaining schedule: at Buccaneers, at Vikings, vs. Saints, vs. Bengals, at Steelers, vs. Seahawks, at Falcons

5. Chicago Bears (8-2), wild card No. 1: They picked up a spot in the seeding thanks to Minnesota's loss Sunday. Next up, first place in the NFC North? Remaining schedule: at Lions, vs. Jaguars, at Dolphins, at Bills, vs. Packers, vs. Lions, at Vikings

6. Minnesota Vikings (7-3), wild card No. 2: Kirk Cousins' return was a smashing success ... after one game. Sunday's loss to the Niners south of the border was a systemic disaster that could call the Vikes' Super Bowl bona fides into question. Remaining schedule: vs. Falcons, vs. Panthers, at Patriots, vs. Lions, vs. Commanders, at Jets, vs. Bears

7. Los Angeles Rams (7-4), wild card No. 3: Look who's won five in a row and worked their way back into the projected field. The Rams got behind the eight ball quickly this season thanks to poor play and injuries, but they're adapting − and were clearly the better team Wednesday night, when they thumped the Pack right out of this slot. Still, LA has a tough schedule to navigate into January while harboring hopes that at least WR Davante Adams can return to the passing attack. Remaining schedule: vs. Chiefs, at 49ers, vs. Cowboys, at Seahawks, at Buccaneers, vs. Seahawks

8. Seattle Seahawks (6-4), in the hunt: The defending champs have been beset by various issues − a new offensive coordinator, a series of QB injuries, having to use their entire running back depth chart. If most of those resolve, maybe they can ultimately break through the NFC North logjam ahead of them. More immediately, they need to stave off a season sweep by the Niners on Sunday afternoon − and will overtake the Rams if they do so. Remaining schedule: at 49ers, vs. Cowboys, vs. Giants, at Eagles, vs. Rams, at Panthers, at Rams

9. Green Bay Packers (6-5), in the hunt: Their usual home as the NFC's seventh seed suddenly looks pretty good. But the Rams drummed them out of the spot, and the Pack − even with OLB Micah Parsons back − have a lot of work to do to get back into postseason position. Remaining schedule: at Saints, vs. Bills, vs. Dolphins, at Bears, vs. Texans, vs. Lions

10. Dallas Cowboys (5-6), in the hunt: Convincing victory Sunday against Tennessee. (Yawn.) But winning the NFC East is probably Dallas' only hope of making the field. Remaining schedule: vs. Eagles, at Seahawks, BYE, at Rams, vs. Jaguars, vs. Giants, at Commanders

11. Washington Commanders (4-5-1), in the hunt: They're stacking wins and wading into increasingly advantageous circumstances that might just pave the road to the top of the NFC East. Remaining schedule: at Cardinals, at Titans, vs. Texans, vs. Falcons, at Vikings, at Jaguars, vs. Cowboys
