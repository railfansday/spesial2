NFL Week 10 expert picks, predictions: Moneyline, against the spread

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

We're somehow already in Week 10 of the NFL season, and it kicked off with a surprisingly thrilling game between the Washington Commanders and New York Giants on Thursday night. But there are plenty more fireworks ahead in a pretty good slate of games this week.

Of course, things kick off in Germany with a huge showdown between the Detroit Lions and New England Patriots. But don't sleep on some big contests in each time window for Sunday. Lions fans will be particularly interested in the 1 p.m. ET showdown between the Minnesota Vikings and Green Bay Packers, but Kansas City Chiefs vs. Atlanta Falcons could be a low-key banger. The late afternoon window features all four NFC West teams—all relevant to the Lions' playoff chase: Seahawks vs. Raiders, Rams vs. Cardinals, and 49ers vs. Cowboys.

The night closes out with a somewhat interesting Steelers vs. Bengals game, if you can stomach all the Aaron Rodgers talk. Unfortunately, the week closes out with a Monday night matchup between two hugely disappointing teams: the 0-8 Chargers and the 4-5 Ravens.
