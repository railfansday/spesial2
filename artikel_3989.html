Tomodachi Life: Living The Dream Soundtrack Added To Nintendo Music, Here's Every Song Included

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Nintendo has this week updated its Nintendo Music app with the Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream soundtrack.

It adds 141 tracks and has a run time of 3 hours and 39 minutes. Toru Minegishi was the sound director on this project (known for his previous work on Zelda, Splatoon, Pokémon and the Mario series).
