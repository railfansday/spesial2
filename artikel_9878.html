Virtual Art Museum built with Three js

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

home blogblogrollcool linksbooks read languaging usesnow

I made a web site and a virtual museum for my wife's artworks after winning three grand prizes(one of them being City Beauty Exhibition Grand Prize) single-handedly with two of her works at an exhibition competition in which she participated in Japan. She always believed she wouldn't be able to generate any income or achieve success through art, so I created this website and the virtual museum to serve as a source of motivation for her.

Virtual museum is still in progress but it works! There is also a guest notebook that you can leave notes. It is updated one time everyday. So you might need to wait one day until it gets published.

It works best at PCs, laptops and tablets. Phones are also good but try to lower the quality in the settings if you feel low fps on mobile or on other devices. Try the high quality if your device is ok tho!

Also try to tinker other settings to see different effects in which you can set quality, time of the day, the weather etc! You can use mouse scroll to zoom in-out on PC and walk with W A S D or arrow keys.

Photography hall will have more photos soon and sculpture hall will be open soon as well!

If you like cats, turn the rain mode on :)

If you happen to find any bug please feel free to contact me so that I can fix it.

I opened the sculpture hall and added one of her sculptures. Since we only have photos of it, I 3D scanned it roughly and edited rest of it make it resemble the original. It took few days to finish it. It is not as good as the original but this is all I can do for now. I decided not to do it via the photos anymore lol. I will only do it when I have original one in my hands.
