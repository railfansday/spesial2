Former Akron Zips standout Shammah Scott signs with Cavs G-League team

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Shammah Scott has proven you can go home again.

The former University of Akron standout guard is returning to his home roots in Cleveland by joining the Charge, the Cavaliers' G-League team.

Scott signed with the Charge on Nov. 18, a team spokesperson said. He will be active for the team's game Nov. 20 at the Grand Rapids Gold.

The announcement was posted to Instagram on Nov. 19 by the team's offical account.

A 2020 Shaker Heights graduate, Scott started his collegiate career at Northwest Florida State before transferring to Wichita State, where he was a teammate of Craig Porter Jr. for the 2023 season. Porter signed with the Cavs as an undrafted free agent that summer and has been a rotational player for the team ever since.

Porter said he views Scott as a younger brother, and Scott said he views Porter as an older brother.

"When he was a younger guy on my Wichita team, he was just always in the gym constantly 24-7," Porter told the Beacon Journal in December 2025. "That was just one of the things that stood out because he never knew when he was going to play, and it was just a big thing that he made sure he was always ready."

The two guards have remained friends since then. They keep in touch and follow each other's careers.

When Scott transferred to the University of Akron, he became a key member of the Zips basketball team for three years. The Zips won three Mid-American Tournament championships and made three NCAA Tournament appearances with Scott, a two-time MAC Sixth Man of the Year, on the roster.

Scott played a pivotal role in leading the Zips to the 2026 MAC Tournament title game. He made a crucial 3-pointer with 1:50 to play in the Zips' 75-68 semifinal win over rival Kent State, and he drained a game-winning 3-pointer with six seconds left to give Akron a 79-76 win over Toledo in the championship game.

Scott received recognition from Cavs All-Star guard Donovan Mitchell on X after his game-winning shot.

Scott went 9-0 in MAC Tournament games with the Zips. In the 2026 tournament, he scored 12 points in a 73-70 quarterfinal win over Buffalo, 18 points against Kent State and 12 points against Toledo.

He appeared in all 34 games for Akron, averaging 12.7 points on 44.9% shooting from the field (42.2% on 3-pointers), 2.6 rebounds, 2.9 assists and 26.6 minutes.
