Monday Night Football: Zach Charbonnet, Jadarian Price are active for Seahawks vs. Bears

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Seahawks will officially have running backs Zach Charbonnet and Jadarian Price for Monday Night Football.

Charbonnet will make his 2026 debut after returning from a torn ACL that ended his season in the divisional round of the playoffs in January. He set career highs in 2025 with 730 rushing yards and a team-high 12 touchdowns.

The Seahawks activated Price from injured reserve on Monday, and he is active for the first time since Week 3, when he injured his chest. He has 32 touches for 140 yards and two fumbles.

Offensive guard Quenton Nelson will make his Seattle debut after a trade from Indianapolis this week. He will play left guard with Grey Zabel moving to right guard.

The Seahawks won’t have outside linebacker Uchenna Nwosu, who was doubtful with an ankle injury, or back up quarterback Drew Lock, who was questionable with a right elbow injury. Jalen Milroe will backup Sam Darnold, with Lock designated as the emergency third quarterback.

Wide receiver Rashid Shaheed (knee), offensive tackle Charles Cross (ankle), running back Velus Jones Jr. (hamstring), nose tackle Brandon Pili (back) and offensive guard Beau Stephens are the team’s other inactives.

The Bears won’t have cornerback Malik Muhammad (toe). He was limited in practice and given a questionable injury designation.

The Bears’ other inactives are running back Kyle Monangai (thumb/toe), quarterback Case Keenum, defensive tackle Jayden Loving, offensive lineman Jordan McFadden and defensive tackle Jordan van den Berg.
