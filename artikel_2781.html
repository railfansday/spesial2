Broncos' Sean Payton pushes back on Jim Harbaugh after failed trade

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The failed trade of linebacker Daiyan Henley left one NFL coach seething. Another is now shrugging it off.

Reports emerged last Tuesday that the Los Angeles Chargers had agreed to send Henley to the Denver Broncos in exchange for a 2027 fourth-round pick and 2028 fifth-round pick. Last Wednesday night, however, Denver announced the trade would "not be finalized."

According to multiple reports, the Broncos flagged a knee issue in Henley's physical. Chargers coach Jim Harbaugh then said the Broncos attempted to renegotiate terms of the deal, which then fell apart when the two sides couldn't find common ground. Afterward, Harbaugh aired out his frustration with the team not merely issuing a passing or failing grade, which he called "a loophole" that "sets a bad precedent."

"There's no loophole," Payton told reporters on Monday, Nov. 16. "It just is part of the trade process every step of the way."

Payton refused to address the matter beyond that.

"I'm not going to get into specifics," Payton said. "He's a prospect we thought – and still do – very highly of. It just didn't work out. It's not uncommon with trades. That's kind of where it went. I'll leave it at that."

Henley, 26, is in the final year of his contract. He leads the Chargers with 51 tackles this season.
