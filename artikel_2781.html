NBC removed polarizing Deshaun Watson from promo hyping Browns-Raiders ‘SNF’ clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

On second thought, NBC doesn't really feel the need to use Deshaun Watson for promotional ads for this week's "Sunday Night Football" clash.

After previously using the polarizing Browns quarterback with a checkered past to promote this Sunday's Raiders-Browns game in Cleveland, NBC removed Watson from its video package promoting the game and instead went with linebacker Carson Schwesinger, as noted by Ari Meirov.

He tweeted a collage showing last Sunday's promo featuring Watson alongside Raiders rookie quarterback Fernando Mendoza, compared to Thursday's version absent Watson.

It's certainly a notable omission due to Watson's transgressions.

Watson faced 27 civil lawsuits alleging misconduct, including allegations of him exposing himself, touching them with his privates and kissing them without consent.

The NFL suspended him 11 games in 2022 and fined him $5 million over the allegations ahead of his first season with the Browns.

How the NFL media has covered Watson has since become a hot topic, with some getting called out for glossing over the topic.

Prime Video's Al Michaels drew criticism earlier this year for not discussing the topic during a Steelers-Browns "Thursday Night Football clash."

"If you follow football, you know the story — we don't have to go through the whole Deshaun Watson thing," Michaels said.

Cleveland Browns quarterback Deshaun Watson (4) celebrates after an NFL football game against the Houston Texans in Cleveland, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. AP Photo/Sue Ogrocki

While replacing a quarterback for another star player in a promo graphic is not unheard of, it stands out in this game since Schwesinger is not a household name.

The second-year linebacker is having a strong season for the upstart Browns, yet he's not a well-known star like Aaron Donald or Micah Parsons.

NBC is airing this game since the original Sunday night game featuring the Patriots and Chargers holds the entertainment value of watching grass grow.

Houston Texans wide receiver Nico Collins (12) is tackled by Cleveland Browns linebacker Carson Schwesinger (49) on Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026, in Cleveland. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

The Patriots' season may be over with Drake Maye possibly sidelined for the year and the Chargers are 1-9 and may be without quarterback Justin Herbert.

The league then instead flexed to this matchup featuring a pleasant surprise in Cleveland, which currently owns a playoff spot, against the 2026 No. 1 pick in Mendoza.

Even if NBC doesn't use Watson in the promotional material, his troubling past is a storyline that Mike Tirico and Cris Collinsworth will decide how to address.
