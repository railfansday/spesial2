Barcelona vs Aston Villa: Match Preview, Latest Team News and Score Prediction

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Aston Villa have made a fast start in the Champions League. Three games, three wins, nine points. That is the clean summary. The harder part comes now, away at Barcelona, where good form gets tested properly and where European campaigns tend to reveal what a side really is.

Villa head to Camp Nou sitting second in the table after beating Club Brugge 3-2, Fenerbahce 6-0 and Viking 3-1. Those are strong results and, more importantly, they show variety. Villa have won a tight game, they have blown one open, and they have handled expectation at home. That matters. So does context. Barcelona away is a different level of problem.

Barcelona have taken six points from their first three matches. They opened with a 5-1 win against Feyenoord, followed it with a 4-1 victory at Galatasaray, then lost 4-2 at Paris Saint-Germain. So there is no mystery here. They are dangerous, they score goals, and they still leave openings.

Kick-off is at 8pm GMT on Tuesday, November 3. The setting is Camp Nou, and the numbers attached to Barcelona at home are serious. They have won 12 of their 14 matches in all competitions this season, and across seven home games they have scored 29 times. That works out at 4.14 goals per match. You do not need much interpretation beyond that.

Unai Emery knows this stadium well enough, and not in a sentimental way. His record there is poor, one win in 15 visits, and there is history attached to that. Managers can say each game stands alone, and sometimes that is true. Sometimes it is also convenient. Camp Nou can turn one bad spell into a long night very quickly.

Villa will still travel with some belief. They are among the stronger attacking sides in this season's Champions League so far, and they have shown enough edge in the final third to think they can hurt Barcelona. If Villa score first, this gets interesting. If they defend passively, it probably gets ugly.

Barcelona's biggest concern is in defence. João Cancelo, Gerard Martín, Pau Cubarsí and Alejandro Balde are currently the only first-team defenders available for selection. That is thin by any standard, and it has already forced changes. In the 6-1 win over Deportivo Alavés at the weekend, Hansi Flick used Frenkie de Jong at centre-back and gave Brian Farinas a first-team debut at right-back.

Xavi Espart and Jules Koundé are out with ankle injuries. Eric García and Andreas Christensen are still recovering from thigh problems and are not ready to return. In midfield, Dani Olmo has a left adductor injury picked up in the defeat to Paris Saint-Germain, so Fermín López is expected to continue in his place. Roony Bardghji remains out long term after suffering a torn ACL in his right knee.

This is the contradiction in Barcelona right now. They can overwhelm teams going forward, but they are patched up defensively and they rarely look fully secure. In fact, they have not kept a clean sheet in the Champions League since beating Borussia Dortmund 4-0 in April 2025. That run now stretches to 18 matches in the competition. They have also managed just one clean sheet in their last 10 games in all competitions. That is not a minor detail, it is a pattern.

Villa have their own changes to consider. João Gomes and Emi Buendía could come into the starting line-up, with Leon Goretzka and Alysson the likely players to drop out. Aaron Wan-Bissaka and Matteo Ruggeri remain unavailable with hamstring injuries and are not expected back until after the November international break.

Amadou Onana is a major absence after rupturing his ACL while on international duty with Belgium at the 2026 World Cup. Brian Madjo and Modou Keba Cisse are not eligible for this phase of the competition after being omitted from Villa's Champions League squad. Madjo's absence should bring Tammy Abraham back into the matchday group.

The interesting attacking note is Nicolas Jackson. He scored in the 1-1 draw with Fulham and now has seven goals in his last seven appearances in all competitions. His pace is a real issue for any side that defends high, and Barcelona do exactly that. If Villa can break pressure and release him early, there will be space to attack.
