Micah Parsons to practice in pads on Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Packers plan to have defensive end Micah Parsons on the field against the Rams on Wednesday night and Parsons will check off one more box toward that return to action at practice on Sunday.

Head coach Matt LaFleur told reporters at a press conference that Parsons will practice in pads for the first time since he got back on the practice field earlier this month. LaFleur called it "the next marker" for Parsons to clear before he gets the green light to face the Rams.

LaFleur said the team will have a plan in place for how many snaps Parsons would play in his first game since tearing his ACL last December.

"He is an elite player, he's an elite athlete. I do think you see with many of these guys — I never want to put limitations on anybody, but he hasn't played football in a long time. You gotta be conscious of that."

The Packers will release their first injury report for Week 12 later on Sunday and injury designations for the NFL's first Thanksgiving Eve game will be released on Tuesday.
