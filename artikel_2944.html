Cowboys’ DeMarvion Overshown apologizes after his sideline blunder vs. Colts

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Dallas Cowboys linebacker DeMarvion Overshown has apologized after receiving an unusual 15-yard penalty during Sunday's win over the Indianapolis Colts.

Overshown was inactive for the NFL Week 9 matchup but ran onto the field to celebrate after Shemar James recovered a fourth-quarter fumble. Officials flagged Dallas for "unsportsmanlike conduct" because Overshown was not in uniform.

Speaking to reporters, Overshown explained that excitement led to his mistake. He also confirmed that he had apologized to head coach Brian Schottenheimer and his teammates.

"It was all excitement. At the end of the day, I talked to Schotty about it. I apologized about it," Overshown said. "It was definitely in the moment."

He explained that he had encouraged the defense to celebrate more after creating turnovers. However, he recognized the timing made his actions costly.

"You can laugh at it now because we won, but in the moment, fourth quarter, tied ballgame or close ballgame, you don't want a 15-yard penalty from a guy, especially that's not even playing," Overshown said. "That's a lesson learned."

Dallas, TX, USA; Dallas Cowboys linebacker DeMarvion Overshown (0) goes through a drill in Frisco, Texas. Mandatory Credit: Chris Jones-Imagn Images Credit: IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect (Reuters)

Overshown admitted he initially did not realize that entering the field in street clothes would result in a penalty. He said teammate Dee Winters alerted him that he could not be on the field. By then, the official had already noticed his celebration.

"That's when I looked back and I had already seen the ref, he's pointing at me," Overshown recalled.

The linebacker accepted responsibility and promised to avoid another incident. "But I learned. It won't happen again," he said.

Overshown also made clear that the incident would not change his enthusiasm for teammates making big plays.

"It's not going to change how passionate I am about my teammates when they make plays," he explained.

He even joked about how he would handle future celebrations. "In the future, I'll have my helmet and my uniform (on), so it won't be a flag."

Dallas eventually defeated Indianapolis 27-23, but the penalty exposed another discipline issue. The Cowboys committed 14 penalties for 153 yards during the victory.

At 4-5, Dallas needs better discipline as it prepares to face the 7-1 San Francisco 49ers on Sunday. Overshown's apology addresses his mistake, but the team's penalty problems remain a concern.
