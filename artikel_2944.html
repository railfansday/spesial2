Dodgers’ Shohei Ohtani joins exclusive elite athletes club that incudes Michael Jordan, LeBron James after New Balance deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Los Angeles Dodgers superstar Shohei Ohtani has secured another milestone off the field, agreeing to a career-long partnership with New Balance. The agreement places the four-time MVP among a select group of global sports icons with lifetime endorsement deals, further strengthening his influence beyond baseball.

According to The Athletic, Ohtani joins Michael Jordan, LeBron James, Kevin Durant, Cristiano Ronaldo, and Damian Lillard as the only professional athletes with lifetime endorsement agreements. The 32-year-old also expressed his excitement about extending the partnership. "There is a lot of trust in our relationship and I've always felt like a true partner whether I'm testing products, sharing feedback or helping shape new ideas," Ohtani said in New Balance's announcement.

Ohtani first joined the Boston-based brand in 2023, following an already historic start to his MLB career. Since making his debut in 2018, he has won four MVP awards while establishing himself as one of baseball's most accomplished two-way players. His new agreement covers the remainder of his playing career, making him the first MLB player to secure an endorsement deal of this kind, according to The Athletic.

New Balance is also recognizing Ohtani's contributions to product development. The company will rename its Sports Research Lab the Shohei Ohtani Sports Research Lab, highlighting his involvement in testing and improving its products.

In a recent GQ interview, Ohtani further explained why he had no reservations about extending the partnership. He pointed to the friendships they had built and the company's willingness to accommodate his requests.

The agreement marks another major milestone in Ohtani's career beyond baseball. His growing partnership with New Balance also gives him a more prominent role in the company's product innovation efforts.
