Target Enters Holiday Season With Pop Culture Momentum

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Nearly 27%: Growth in Target’s Fun 101 category, which includes products such as toys, electronics, sports, and games—especially driven by trading card sales.

1,600: Number of stores where Target reinvented its Fan Shops for sports merchandise.

$27.1 billion: Target’s third-quarter net sales, up 7.1%—its strongest growth in five years, according to the Associated Press and CNBC.

2,026: Number of Target stores, up 31 from a year ago. The company said it opened the most stores this year since 2009.

~20%: Year-over-year growth in non-merchandise sales, including Roundel ad revenue, credit card profit sharing revenue, membership revenue, and marketplace.
