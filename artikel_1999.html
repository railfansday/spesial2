Florian Wirtz dazzles, 5/10 is a let-down: Liverpool player ratings vs Man United

Liverpool will be delighted with the majority of their star players following a dominant 3-0 win against Manchester United.

The Reds eased to a comfortable victory at Anfield, and ruined Mauricio Pochettino's first game as Man United manager despite their pre-game optimism.

After Leny Yoro was sent off just eight minutes into the clash, and Dominik Szoboszlai put his side ahead from the resulting free-kick, before Florian Wirtz made it 2-0 minutes later.

That strong start gave them a comfortable platform to build on before goals from Rio Ngunoha and Victor Munoz, and while the 4-0 final score was not the 7-0 demolition we saw in 2023, it was arguably just as comfortable for them.

Liverpool's defensive players and goalkeeper had very little to do throughout, but handled their responsibilities well when asked.

The midfield and attack, however, were allowed to shine and display their talents in what was finally a statement win for Andoni Iraola at Anfield, where they have otherwise struggled.

Wirtz has been heavily criticised during his time at Liverpool, with a lofty price tag and a lack of consistency leading to constant jibes about his performances.

However, he has shown a remarkable turn of form of late, and thrived under Liverpool legend Jurgen Klopp in the most recent international break with Germany.

He seems to have returned to Anfield a new man, as he scored the second goal of the game and set up the fourth, while he completed ten key passes, created four big chances and completed 100 per cent of his dribbles.

Outside of the statistics, however, his constant energy and running in behiind posed a threat, while his movement and quick-thinking caused Man United no end of trouble.

While there have been questions asked of his continued involvement since his move in 2025, the midfielder showed today he can be worth every penny of that £116million.

Bradley Barcola looked electric on the wing whenever he got the ball, got into some great positions and was a constant threat for the new-look Man United back line.

His pace and close control made him a dangerous asset for the Reds, but ultimately his end product let him down, as has been the story of his Liverpool career so far.

Speaking live on Sky Sports, Jamie Carragher insisted Barcola "lets himself down" with his end product after consistently failing to provide goals and assists. He later labeled him "unconvincing".

Barcola was later booked for a late challenge on the edge of the Man United area, capping off what was a disappointing display for the £123million man.

Despite eventually picking up an assist when he set up Rio Ngumoha's finish, the France international could and should have left Anfield with four or five contributions.
