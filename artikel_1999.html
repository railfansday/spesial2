Newcastle make 'firm' Matthias Jaissle sack call after fans rage - expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Newcastle United are planning to stick by Matthias Jaissle despite growing unhappiness with his position as manager.

Following back-to-back defeats against Everton, first away from home in the Carabao Cup and then in the Premier League at St James' Park, frustrations have grown again.

It has been a slow start to the manager's tenure with the Magpies, who have now one just one of their last five games and just three of nine in the league so far this season.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Newcastle's board are keen to stick by the decision they made to bring Jaissle to the club in the summer.

There is optimism that, despite a turbulent start, the boss can still turn things around and set them on track to reach their ambitious goals not just this season but long-term.

Eddie Howe's exit during the summer, alongside a number of major player sales, were always bound to make Jaissle's job difficult, and the board are understanding of that.

Football Insider revealed on 23 October that Newcastle have no plans to sack Jaissle in the near future and are willing to stick by him despite their difficult run of form.

Despite growing frustrations from the crowd at St James' Park, there is still a belief that the manager retains the club's backing and will be supported in his efforts to turn things around.

Newcastle are determined to ensure they got the decision right, and will give the boss time to prove himself rather than admitting their error in replacing Howe.

"Newcastle have been pretty firm with their decision to stick by the manager," Brown told Football Insider.

"There's obviously been a lot of talk about him and a lot of anger from the fans, because they want to be winning games and fighting higher up the table.

"But Newcastle aren't worried at the moment, they want things to improve but they will feel this manager is the right man to turn things around.

"When he was brought in, it was with the idea that he would be around for a long time and get them back to competing at the top level, so they're still hopeful he can do that.

"Part of that decision is that they won't want to admit they got it wrong, because he was they one they immediate wanted when Howe decided to leave in the summer.

"To make a change just a couple of months later is just admitting they got it wrong, and they're going to be reluctant to do that.

"So for now, I think Jaissle still has the full backing of the board at Newcastle and they hope he will turn things around, even if recent results haven't been good enough."

Should Newcastle decide to part ways with Jaissle in the near future, they will be forced to consider the alternatives, with one eye likely to be on the current manager market.

The board remain in support of their manager as things stand, and have no plans to sack him, but contingency plans are in place in case they are forced to pull the trigger.

However, Football Insider revealed on 25 October that Newcastle are unimpressed with the current crop of available managers, which could impact their decision.

Their efforts to identify a replacement have hit a roadblock as a result, with fellow Premier League clubs also facing the same concerns about a lack of clear options.

They could be tempted to lure a manager out of their current job should they be forced to make a change, although it would likely cost a significant compensation fee.
