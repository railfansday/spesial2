Two actors who worked with Zach Cregger on the Oscar-winning horror epic Weapons last year reunited to play father and son in a new survival thriller released a month ago. The new movie was unable to replicate the success of Weapons, which grossed $270 million at the worldwide box office in 2025. One of the stars, Austin Abrams, was coming off the record-breaking success of the video game adaptation Resident Evil, also directed by Cregger. Resident Evil has so far generated more than $415 million at the worldwide box office. His co-star, Josh Brolin, recently suffered a major box-office setback.

Brolin appeared alongside Jacob Elordi in Ridley Scott's post-apocalyptic sci-fi movie The Dog Stars, which emerged as a major flop with just $40 million in the bank at the worldwide box office against a reported budget of $100 million. Brolin and Abrams' new film, which generated significant pre-release buzz thanks to its unique premise — a diver gets sucked into the belly of a whale — failed to take off commercially.

The movie did, however, receive mostly positive reviews. It's now sitting at a 75% 'Certified Fresh' score on the aggregator website Rotten Tomatoes, where the consensus describes it as 'unforgettably visceral.' The movie's audience score is perched at an even higher 81%, which bodes well for its post-theatrical release on the PVOD market.
