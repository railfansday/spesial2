The Israeli market is particularly fascinating. Despite its small size and geopolitical tensions, it is highly competitive. Most of the world’s leading technology companies operate development centers there, including Microsoft, Google, AWS, and NVIDIA, along with many others. Israeli high-tech companies raised approximately $14.6 billion in 2025, placing Israel fourth in the world in terms of capital raised by startups. According to the Momentum Cyber report, in 2025 Israeli cybersecurity companies attracted approximately 24% of the total capital invested globally in minority financing deals in the cybersecurity sector. The WIPO Global Innovation Index for 2026 ranks Israel first in the world in the number of venture capital deals relative to the size of its economy.

In the wake of the AI revolution, companies have begun working vigorously to achieve massive efficiency gains, and in Israel, the situation is even more extreme. This is a small and competitive market, where thousands of companies operate alongside tech and consulting giants. Within this market, new companies have begun to emerge that have made it their primary mission to bring AI to businesses. One such company is KO AI, which was the first in Israel to offer FDE (Forward Deployed Engineering) services in an AI vector. Today FDE is an approach to integrating artificial intelligence into organizations through professionals who specialize in AI technologies and business process engineering. Its goal is to effectively integrate the technology into organizational processes. KO AI has entered this field in a big way.

KO AI was founded in early 2024 in Israel, and its core service is business and operational optimization for large and medium-sized companies in Israel. “In large companies, there are hundreds or even thousands of processes that can be optimized immediately. Some can be eliminated, some can be streamlined, and new processes can be created that are more efficient and flexible through the integration of AI and ML technologies,” says Kuzmanko.

Today, the company offers advanced FDE services, AI and ML software development services, implementation of enterprise AI tools, and strategic, business, and technical consulting services. All these services share a single goal: improving organizational performance. This is achieved through operational efficiency and cost reduction and by creating a competitive advantage, including through the integration of AI and ML solutions. “We are a company that optimizes organizations, and AI is the tool we understand well and can use to make businesses much more efficient,” adds Kuzmanko.

One of the company's key advantages is its proprietary technology platform, which, according to KO AI, has been under development for nearly three years. The platform enables the company to accelerate AI implementation across organizations while maintaining a high level of efficiency and effectiveness. Although KO AI has disclosed few technical details about the platform, it appears to help the company leverage its expertise, standardize complex implementation processes, and deliver AI solutions at a significantly greater scale.

The company works with a wide range of organizations in various fields, including private and government entities, on projects costing hundreds of thousands of dollars or more. “The cost of the projects is directly derived from the savings and growth we generate for these organizations and represents a small percentage of those savings and growth,” concludes Kuzmanko.
