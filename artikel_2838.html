Rams hope to have Kobie Turner back for Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Rams head coach Sean McVay shared a negative update on wide receiver Puka Nacua’s health on Friday, but he had better news regarding defensive tackle Kobie Turner.

McVay said that the team is planning to activate defensive tackle Kobie Turner from injured reserve ahead of next Wednesday’s game against the Packers. Turner has missed the last four games with a groin injury.

Turner had 22 tackles in six games before his injury.

Getting Turner back would give the Rams the full defensive front they envisioned when they traded for Myles Garrett and coaxed Aaron Donald out of retirement this offseason. With Nacua expected to miss the rest of the season and Davante Adams still out, they’ll be banking on that defensive front being a major asset to their playoff push.
