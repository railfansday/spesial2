Winners, losers as Chargers earn first win vs. Ravens on Monday Night Football

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Winners, losers as Chargers earn first win vs. Ravens on Monday Night Football originally appeared on NBC Sports Boston

The last winless NFL team is finally on the board.

The Los Angeles Chargers secured their first win of the season with a 23-20 victory over the Baltimore Ravens on Monday Night Football.

Baltimore's offense just never looked smooth in Jacoby Brissett's debut under center, while the running game had one of the worst showings of the season.

Justin Herbert wasn't anything special through the air, but Los Angeles' running attack was the difference in a tight finish. The Chargers are now 1-8, while the Ravens fell to 4-6.

Let's analyze the Week 10 concluder further with winners and losers:

How must the Chargers' locker room feel now? After eight straight losses to start the season, despite having some quality players, the Chargers can finally hold their heads high going forward. That won't automatically equate to a winning streak of some sort, but the morale needed this.

Up next is a home game against the New York Jets. That's the type of game this Los Angeles side under Jim Harbaugh should be winning with the remaining schedule featuring tougher teams.

Baltimore's top rusher on the night had 10 carries for 10 yards and two touchdowns. Yes, Derrick Henry was playing. Yes, that was Henry's stat line. The Ravens only mustered 16 total rushing yards on the night, with Justice Hill collecting four on three carries. That will never cut it in the pros.

Henry did have two goal-line scores, but the game plan relied way too much on Brissett's arms rather than going at Los Angeles' defensive line with more intensity. Brissett threw 49 passes on the night, completing 33 for 340 yards and a pick.

On the opposite side, Los Angeles' running game enjoyed a solid night. Omarion Hampton led the way with 105 yards and a score on 22 carries, giving the Chargers one of their better individual ground performances of the season.

Herbert, as aforementioned, didn't make much of an impact through the air. But he did run in the opening touchdown from 22 yards out, finishing with seven attempts for 51 yards. It wasn't a memorable win, but the Chargers will take it anyway they can get it.

After starting the season 4-1, the Ravens have now lost five straight. They still have four divisional games left in a strong AFC North. Lamar Jackson is on injured reserve, and if this current form is anything to go by, there's no point rushing him.

Baltimore does not look like the same team it did to start the season. And by the time Jackson is ready, it'll likely be on the outside of the playoff picture. Brissett doesn't move the needle enough despite being better than Baltimore's other QB options, while Henry can't carry offenses anymore. It's another frustrating year for the Jackson-Baltimore duo.

Despite having an agreement with the Denver Broncos on trade deadline day, the Chargers ended up welcoming back linebacker Daiyan Henley not long after. And the franchise's 2023 third-round pick made an immediate impact.

Henley recorded a game-high 11 tackles, with five solo. He recorded a tackle for loss early on and a pass defended. He would've helped provide the 7-2 Broncos with better depth amid their playoff pursuit, but, for now, he'll be a key piece to Los Angeles' defense.
