Texans DE Chris Braswell seriously injures leg on opening kickoff

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Texans defensive end Chris Braswell was seriously injured on the opening kickoff.

Braswell, who was elevated from the practice squad for the game, injured his right leg on what appeared to be a blindside block by Caden Curry. No penalty was called on Seth McGown's 40-yard return.

The team's medical personnel placed his right leg in a vacuum splint before he was carted to the training room.

Braswell's teammates wished him well before he departed on a cart.

It was the first play of the first game Braswell has played this season. A second-round pick of the Bucs in 2024, he had played all 34 games in his first two seasons.
