49ers’ Mike Evans makes confident Brock Purdy claim after Vikings win

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

49ers' Mike Evans makes confident Brock Purdy claim after Vikings win appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The San Francisco 49ers dominated the Minnesota Vikings on Sunday Night Football. San Francisco got a huge performance from Mike Evans in his return to the field on Sunday night. But the veteran pass catcher didn't want to take full credit for his team's win.

Evans met with the media at the Estadio Azteca in Mexico City, Mexico on Sunday night. He was asked about his quarterback, Brock Purdy, and the effect he has on this offense. Evans' response was quite straightforward.

"Mike Evans on Brock Purdy: Best player in the game right now," SI Now reporter Gilberto Manzano reported on Sunday night.

Evans, as mentioned, was a star for the 49ers on Sunday. He racked up 109 yards in his return to the field on Sunday night. He also scored a touchdown to aid in the offensive onslaught for San Francisco.

Purdy continued making his case for MVP on Sunday as well. He threw just five incompletions against a tough Vikings defense. The 49ers star was exceptional against Minnesota's vaunted blitz, too. Purdy tossed three touchdowns in this win on Sunday night.

The 49ers sit alone at the top of the NFC with a 9-1 record. San Francisco has some teams breathing down its neck in the race for the No. 1 seed, to be fair. But they certainly control their destiny at this point in time.

San Francisco is going to be a tough out once the playoffs roll around. However, they still need to take care of business in the regular season. The 49ers will look for their 10th win of the year when they take on the Seattle Seahawks in Week 12.
