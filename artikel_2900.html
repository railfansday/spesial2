Open Thread: Gregg Popovich and his wife treat H-E-B patrons to groceries

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

In a recent online post, Gregg Popovich and his wife Mary were seen purchasing groceries at the H-E-B on New New Braunfels for shoppers. The President of the Spurs did not want the moment to be about himself, but he also did not turn down requests for photos during the excursion (although his expression reveals he would rather have not).

"Was blessed to meet coach Pop and his wife today at my store where they surprised not only us, but our shoppers, and paid for all of their groceries. P.S. We got to get a photo with Pop," shared Michael Wiggins on social media.

El Jefe does not make personal appearance often, the sighting caused many double takes. Having groceries covered was the icing on the cake.

It's great to see the legendary Spurs coach brightening the day for fans and blessing some families with food right before the holidays.
