Berrada Is Wrong: Man Utd Must Go All Out to Sign £80m Star for Pochettino

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Manchester United's defensive problems have been well documented in recent months as they sit in the relegation zone of the Premier League.

The opening few minutes against Liverpool on Sunday afternoon summed up their issues at the back, with Matthijs De Ligt losing his runner, Alexander Isak, before Leny Yoro chopped him down and was sent off.

Liverpool scored from the resulting free kick through Dominic Szoboszlai, giving them a mountain to climb from the early stages in Mauricio Pochettino's opening match.

Michael Carrick must have been sitting, watching, thinking that these are all problems he's been forced to deal with of late, and that was never going to change for Pochettino.

The new United manager hasn't had much time to implement his ideas, but he's also stuck with the same players who have let United down.

United went on to lose 4-0 in humiliating fashion, and in reality, it could have been a lot more.

The first-half performance points back to recent comments from Chief Executive Omar Berrada. He claimed that United had nine defenders and therefore there was little need to strengthen their options at the back.

What that doesn't show, though, is that the options United have in defence simply aren't good enough.

Yoro is still a young defender who makes plenty of mistakes, and the same can be said for Ayden Heaven. De Ligt was clearly out of his depth for Anfield after a year out, while Harry Maguire is reaching the latter stages of his career.

Lisandro Martinez is also making regular mistakes at the moment and letting his team down through ill-discipline.

Moving on to the full-back options, they aren't good enough either, so Berrada's comments make him look completely out of touch with the situation.

United heavily focused their summer transfer window recruitment on bringing in new midfielders. In fact, they failed to bring in a single defender or attacker during the previous window, instead only adding bodies in the middle of the park.

Former Man United defender Gary Neville hasn't held back.

According to reports, Manchester United have made fresh contact with the representatives of Everton defender Jarrad Branthwaite ahead of a possible move.

The Red Devils have previously shown an interest in signing the England international, seeing multiple bids rejected back in 2024.

United could already regret not getting a deal over the line, considering the state of their defence, but there's still time for them to prise him away from Everton.

The Manchester club should be going all out for Branthwaite in 2027. It won't be easy to get him out of Everton in the middle of the season, but there's no reason why United shouldn't be looking to do everything they can to secure a signature.

He's the ideal mix of a player who's capable of slotting in straight away while also being young enough to have plenty of potential.

The signing of Branthwaite would transform United's defence, offering them balance as a left-footed centre-back, pace to cover in behind, and much, much more.
