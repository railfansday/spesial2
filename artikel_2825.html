‘No (more) Love’ — Former UFC champ announces ‘farewell fight’ for Feb. 13 in Cleveland

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The former bantamweight champion plans to move on following the final fight of his UFC contract, which is expected to be satisfied on Sat., Feb. 13, 2026 at Rocket Arena in Cleveland, Ohio. That would put Garbrandt on the undercard of the "Fight Night" lineup topped by Joe Pyfer vs. Bo Nickal.

"I've got one fight left on the contract, and [UFC is] going to let me fight it out and be done with it," Garbrandt (15-8) told MMA Junkie. "We're looking for February in Cleveland. No opponent yet, but that looks to be the date and my farewell from the UFC. It's bittersweet. At the end of the day, I competed at the highest level, became a world champion and I wasn't supposed to make it this far in life and where I'm at. I'm okay with it. I'm forever grateful for the opportunity that I was blessed with and took advantage of. You can't fight forever. I had a good run of 12 years in the sport, world champion. A lot of people spend their whole career chasing what I was able to accomplish in under two years in the UFC."

Garbrandt was once ranked among the top pound-for-pound fighters in the sport.

It's been nearly 10 years since Garbrandt, now 35, was sitting atop the 135-pound mountain, embroiled in feuds with the likes of Dominick Cruz and TJ Dillashaw. Losing his bantamweight crown to Dillashaw sent "No Love" spiraling to a 4-8 record with five of those losses ending by way of knockout.
