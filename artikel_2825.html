Hamilton routs O’Connor, will face rival Chandler in Open semifinals

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

After dropping two of its final three games in the regular season, there might have been some questions on how the No. 3-seed Chandler Hamilton would look against the No. 11-seed Phoenix Sandra Day O'Connor in the Open Division Quarterfinals on Friday, Nov. 20.

Any questions were quickly answered as Hamilton (9-2) came out looking like a squad who had two weeks to prepare for the contest. The Huskies jumped out to an early lead, and then used that wave of momentum to shut out O'Connor (7-5), 41-0, and earn a berth in the Open Division semifinals.

The Huskies will have a rematch with their rival, Chandler, on Nov. 27, to see who advances to the Open Division championship game on Nov. 5. The Wolves defeated Higley, 46-25, on Friday in another quarterfinal game. Chandler defeated Hamilton, 31-24, on Nov. 5, in the regular season finale.

With Hamilton leading 14-0 and the Huskies driving down the field on their first possession of the second quarter, it appeared O'Connor's defense was gaining some momentum as Hamilton took a holding call on a second and goal situation. Then the O'Connor defense stopped Hamilton on a third down.

But on fourth down, with Hamilton's field goal unit on the field, senior Billy Davis elected to run left instead of attempting the kick, and the fake worked as Davis found the end zone from six yards out, putting the Huskies ahead 21-0 with 8:24 left in the first half. The successful fake was a backbreaking move that snapped any momentum the Eagles had built up to that point.

Junior quarterback Jax Sculley was on point for Hamilton, passing for three touchdowns on the night. His first of the contest came with 8:37 left in the opening quarter when he found senior Christian Riley on a 38-yard strike to give their team an early 7-0 advantage. He followed that up later in the quarter with a an 11-yard touchdown reception to sophomore receiver Roman Oliver to extend the lead to two scores.

Sculley's final touchdown pass came with 9:19 left in the third quarter when he again connected with Riley, this time for 31 yards. Sculley got the rest of the night off after that drive.

Hamilton senior defensive end and Air Force commit Derrick Leblanc Jr. etched his name in the Huskies' record book early in the fourth quarter with a sack that made him Hamilton's all-time sack leader.

"We used the extra week to get healthy. Everybody was back this week, so that was good. We had that mentality that we were going to come out here and take care of business." - Hamilton junior quarterback Jax Sculley on how his team's bye last week benefitted them against O'Connor

"I think the best thing we're seeing is his confidence growing over the course of the season. You look at it as a guy came back from surgery, post-surgery he's recovered faster than anyone I've ever seen with an ACL, to see him playing with the confidence that he had tonight is awesome as we get ready to go into the semis, and our guys are playing confident right now." - Hamilton head coach Travis Dixon on Jax Sculley

No. 3 Hamilton advances to the semifinals against rival No. 2 Chandler on Nov. 27, with the winner advancing to the Open Division State Championship game.
