Southern Utah beats West Coast Baptist 134-69 behind JaJuan Nicholls’ 14 points

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

CEDAR CITY, Utah (AP) — JaJuan Nicholls' 14 points helped Southern Utah roll past West Coast Baptist 134-69 on Monday in the Thunderbirds' season opener.

Nicholls added five rebounds for the Thunderbirds. Mikhail Protsenko scored 14 points while going 7 for 7 from the field. DJ Smith went 6 for 9 from the field to finish with 13 points.

Isaiah Dolloso led West Coast Baptist with 17 points and two steals. West Coast Baptist also got 14 points and two steals from Cayden Penha. AmenEl Tadesse added 13 points.

Southern Utah hosts Grace U on Thursday, and West Coast Baptist visits Boise State on Wednesday.
