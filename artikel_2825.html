Brazil U17s work on attacking moves at Granja Comary

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Training at Granja Comary Credits: Nelson Terme /CBF

The Brazil Men's U-17 National Team continued preparations for the 2026 World Cup in Qatar on Tuesday (10). At Granja Comary in Teresópolis, Rio de Janeiro, head coach Carlos Eduardo Patetuci led a session focused on developing attacking movement and creating scoring opportunities.

The session began with coordination and technical drills. The squad then worked on refining their technique through attacking movements to build plays, deliver crosses and shoot on goal against two central defenders.

The session is part of the team's preparations for the World Cup, which will be held in Qatar. Brazil are in Group I alongside Ireland, Tanzania and Costa Rica, and will open their campaign against Ireland on November 21.

The team will remain in Teresópolis until November 14, when they depart for Qatar.
