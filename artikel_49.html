India news: US prepares new tariffs after trade probe

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The United States is reportedly readying fresh tariffs on India and 15 other economies under a trade probe. Also, a woman was allegedly gangraped by her colleagues in the national capital. Follow live.

Here are the biggest headlines from India on Wednesday, November 25:

India's foreign Ministry on Wednesday "strongly condemned" the Houthi attack on one of the towers of the financial district in Riyadh, which injured at least 11 people including two Indians.

"These attacks seriously endanger civilian lives, violate the sovereignty of Saudi Arabia, and undermine regional security and stability. We call for immediate cessation of such attacks," the ministry said in a statement.

"We also reiterate our firm solidarity with the people and government of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and reaffirm our unwavering support to the Kingdom in safeguarding its sovereignty, territorial integrity, and the security and safety of its citizens and residents," the statement said, expressing hope that "peace and normalcy" return to the region at the earliest.

On Tuesday, India's mission in Saudi Arabia had acknowledged that two of the 11 people injured in the attack were Indians and that all assistance would be provided to them and their families.

Riyadh's tallest skyscraper was truck for a second time in days on Tuesday, in what the Saudi coalition fighting Yemen's Houthis called was a "terrorist attack."

The building was reportedly the 80-storey Public Investment Fund Tower which houses the kingdom's sovereign wealth fund and dominates the city's financial district.

On Wednesday, a day after the attack, a Houthi military spokesperson claimed the attack on the building.

Three people have been arrested after a 22-year-old woman said she was gang-raped by two men, with assistance from others, at a cafe in east Delhi, local media said citing police sources.

Police were currently making efforts to trace three others who remained in hiding.

News agency Press Trust of India reported that the incident took place when the victim gathered at the cafe with six colleagues on Monday night for a party. The cafe had shut for the day before midnight on Monday but the group entered after hours as they had the keys to the establishment. News channel NDTV reported that the victim and her colleagues worked at the cafe.

Delhi Police has launched a formal investigation against four men and two women under India's criminal law. Three suspects, including at least one woman, have been arrested so far.

This comes just less than two months after a woman was gang-raped in a public park in Delhi, triggering public anger and protests. Women's safety remains a contentious issue in India with frequent headlines of violence against women in the news.

India could be one of the 16 countries facing fresh and imminent US tariffs with the Trump administration set to release the findings of a probe aimed at countering alleged excess industrial capacities, Reuters reported citing three people with knowledge of the matter.

The results of the investigation are likely to be released over the course of the day, followed by new import duties on a wide range of goods if the probe find India guilty of excess capacity.

It remains unclear what tariff rates would be proposed but one of the sources told the news agency that they would be specifically tailored to each country.

That said, the US is likely to honor capped tariff rates agreed on in finalized US trade deals with partner countries.

A report by the Wall Street Journal said that India could face higher US tariffs than economies like Japan or the European Union.

The US introduced 25% reciprocal tariffs in 2025 on India, under US President Donald Trump's second term. The rate was later lowered to 18% with Washington and New Delhi agreeing to an interim trade framework while a more comprehensive trade agreement would be worked on. Leaders from both nations have since said the first tranche of a trade agreement was "close," but a deal remains elusive.

Meanwhile in July 2026, the United States Trade Representative (USTR), which carries out trade-related investigations, imposed an additional 10% tariffs on India and other countries after a probe on traded goods made using forced labor.
