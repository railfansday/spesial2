Raye, Rosalía and Dolly Parton recognised in Grammy nominations

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Raye's latest record This Album May Contain Hope also won the Mercury Prize last month

British singer Raye leads the charge at the forthcoming Grammy Awards with 11 nominations, while Dolly Parton has been recognised with three posthumous nods.

Raye is nominated for her latest Mercury-winning record This Album May Contain Hope and its lead single Where Is My Husband!, which is up for both song and record of the year.

Parton's two nominations, both in the best country duo/group performance category, come three months after the country music star died aged 80.

Parton is recognised for Butterfly, her collaboration with the Zac Brown Band, and Light of a Clear Blue Morning, alongside an all-star line-up including her goddaughter Miley Cyrus.

The late country star also received a nomination for best audio book, for her narration of Star of the Show: My Life on Stage.

The Grammy Awards 2027 will take place on Sunday 7 February in Los Angeles.

They have been added as part of the Academy's attempts to make the event "more inclusive" and represent a wider range of music genres.
