College golf signing day: Top men's players, classes for Class of 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Class of 2027's early signing period began Wednesday, meaning hundreds of student-athletes from across the country signed grant-in-aid agreements with schools. Grant-in-aid agreements have replaced the national letter of intent moving forward.

Here's a look at the rankings for players and classes in the Class of 2027 on the men's side (subject to change).

Arizona State (Vaclav Svub, Brayden Jones, Ethan Butters)
