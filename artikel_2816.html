'That horse's name was Rodeo': Pat McAfee rides horseback into 'College GameDay'

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

ESPN's traveling road show College GameDay was in Boise, Idaho on Saturday for the first time in 16 years ahead of No. 24 Boise State's showdown with Oregon State. With a win, the Broncos can secure their spot in the Pac-12 title game and continue their march towards a potential spot in the College Football Playoff.

It was a packed house for GameDay, with Boise State fans showing up in droves despite the early call-time — it was still dark out when the show first went on the air. Notably, Pat McAfee was absent from the GameDay desk at the very start of the show, but only because he was waiting to make a grand entrance.

"As you might have heard, people have had some travel difficulties," host Rece Davis teased, referencing the nationwide flight delays that made travel impossible for much of Friday. "That includes Pat McAfee. Pat is trying to find his way here. We're certainly hopeful that we find him soon, and that McAfee can make it to the set."

At that point, McAfee called out, "Rece! Rece Davis can you hear me?" Cameras cut to McAfee riding on horseback alongside Boise State handlers and representatives carrying a school flag.

"Don't you worry about an airplane. Don't you worry about an automobile. I'm riding on Rodeo. A horse, that was of course, built to be amongst these Broncos fans," McAfee said, throwing in some "Whoaaaaaaas" when necessary as his horse got a little excited.

"Thank you Kathy Joe, you're the best," McAfee said as he dismounted. He then thanked the rest of the crew that helped him in and began running towards the desk.

"This particular Saturday is going to be a magical one," McAfee said. "There's going to be chaos, implications across the country as people will be punching their ticket to the College Football Playoff."

"That horse's name was Rodeo, and I will say to Rodeo, thank you for not bucking my ass off. A magical morning here at Boise State. I've never ridden a horse before. I certainly lied to them to let that happen."

McAfee's presence on GameDay has come under more scrutiny this year, but this felt like a return to the type of bits that found him success early on in his run with the show.

While there's always going to be a segment of fans that have simply had enough of McAfee due to his overexposure, his best moments on GameDay are ones like this that come from embracing the school he's at, rather than pushing up against it.
