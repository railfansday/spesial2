Week 12 NFL Survivor strategy and picks

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Happy Thanksgiving! Even if you don't celebrate the holiday, you know that it means a Thursday tripleheader of NFL action. The NFL has added a Black Friday game in recent years and now we also get a Wednesday game because the NFL clearly needs more revenue.

Last week was a relatively quiet one, with few competitive matchups and the best game of the week, Vikings vs. 49ers in Mexico City, ending up a lopsided Niners win. The hope is this week brings something a little better, and the matchups on tap suggest it should.

The week opens on Wednesday with two 6-4 squads as the Rams host the Packers. Thanksgiving brings us a huge NFC matchup between the Bears and Lions and a huge AFC matchup between the Chiefs and Bills. Black Friday features Broncos-Steelers with AFC seeding implications. Sunday brings Seahawks-49ers, and we close things out with the NFC South on Monday featuring Panthers vs. Buccaneers in Tampa.

I'm back with my Week 12 Survivor picks on Wednesday ahead of Packers-Rams. Survivor involves picking one team each week to win. If they win you advance, if they lose you're out in most cases. I'm in one pool with four entries, but it's also a double-elimination pool. I'm down to one entry that has one strike against it, but I'm hanging around for now.

Below I've got a look at the safest choices, best value picks, and then trap picks. I'll post my own picks at the end. I'll update on Sunday once I lock in my three picks for the weekend.

I've included odds for the favorite in each game, courtesy of FanDuel.

Jaguars (-14.5) over TitansBengals (-9.5) over Saints

The Jaguars are the biggest favorite this week, and it's not close. The Bengals are the second biggest favorites and with the Saints struggling, they should roll.

Cowboys (-8.5) over EaglesJaguars (-14.5) over TitansCommanders (-7) over Cardinals

Cowboys over Eagles is very much a trap game (see below), but at this stage of the season, there's value in them if you wanna get a little nuts. The only game you might use them is Week 17 at home against the Giants, but that would be arguably a wilder pick given the Giants performances this season. Avoid this pick if you can, but there's some value if you are in a pickle.

The Jaguars offer some value late in the season, traveling to face a bad Colts team in Week 18, but that's a long ways off. First, you might not even make it to Week 18. Second, who knows what the Jaguars will or won't have to play for and if they'll even be playing starters. If you have them available, now is the week.

UPDATE: I forgot about the Commanders game in Arizona. You're probably not using Washington again this season and this is a good week if you want to save the Jaguars.

While there is some value, this is a tricky one given the divisional nature, the Eagles coming off a spicy win over the Steelers, and it being Thanksgiving. And the Cowboys remain a questionable option most weeks.

UPDATE: Scratch that, I'm going with the Commanders. I want to save the Jaguars for December, so we'll roll the dice with Washington. It's a road game, but Arizona stinks.
