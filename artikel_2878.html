Drake women's basketball falls at Kansas

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Drake women's basketball lost at Kansas 74-49 on Nov. 6, falling to 1-1 on the season.

Brooklin Dailey led the Bulldogs with 12 points on 5-of-7 shooting. Quinn Vice added eight.

Kansas was led by Jaliya Davis' 20-point game. She had nine rebounds to lead the Jayhawks.

Drake was held to 27% shooting (18-for-67), including 6-for-30 from 3-point range (20%). The Bulldogs were outrebounded 53-38.

The Bulldogs play host to Cleveland State on Nov. 10 at 11 a.m.
