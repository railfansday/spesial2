Blake Corum receives a heartwarming birthday surprise from his family moments before Thanksgiving Eve game

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Los Angeles Rams and Green Bay Packers are about to play in the first-ever Thanksgiving Eve game, and the calendar just happened to have it land on Blake Corum's birthday.

While Corum took the field at SoFi Stadium for pregame warmups, the Rams running back had a moment he'll never forget.

Blake Corum running past a Giants defender in an NFL game. Charles Wenzelberg/New York Post

Corum was seen walking toward the end zone and was surprised by members of his family who showed up wearing matching sweaters that included Corum's face while he was wearing a birthday party hat on his head.

Corum's family members were also holding individual signs that matched the face on their sweaters to show their support for the Rams running back.

Before the Rams went on the bye week last week, Corum had his best performance of the season, when he was the Rams' leading rusher in their 34-6 win against the Cardinals.

Blake Corum celebrating a play during an NFL game. AP Photo/Stephanie Scarbrough

Los Angeles ran the ball for 202 yards, and Corum was their lead running back, carrying the ball 17 times for 100 yards and a touchdown.

Corum is in his third season in the NFL and has shared lead bell-cow duties with Kyren Williams. The duo has nearly matched each other's production entirely this year, as they've combined for 1,163 yards and 11 touchdowns.

Blake Corum running past a Washington Commanders defender during an NFL game. Getty Images

Corum comes into Wednesday's game, scoring a touchdown in four consecutive games.

The two will have a tough load to face against Green Bay, as they will be without starting and backup centers, Coleman Shelton and Dylan McMahon, who are both on the injured reserve.

The woes of the Rams' offensive line also came when the Packers announced that three-time edge rusher Micah Parsons would be activated from the team's injured reserve.

The Rams and Packers will kick off at 5:15 p.m. and will be streaming live on Netflix.
