Ken Urker Investigation Ongoing After Cause of Death Revealed, Sheriff Says

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The investigation into Gypsy Rose Blanchard’s ex Ken Urker’s death “remains ongoing” after a coroner confirmed he died by accidental overdose Us can confirm.

“The Lafourche Parish Sheriff’s Office continues to investigate the circumstances surrounding the death of Kenan Urker,” the sheriff’s office told Us in a Monday, November 15, statement. “While we understand the Lafourche Parish Coroner’s Office has released updated information regarding the cause of death, our investigation is separate and remains ongoing.”

Authorities added, “At this time, we have no additional information to release. We will provide an update when we reach a point in the investigation where additional information can be shared.”

The sheriff’s statement comes shortly after the Lafourche Parish Coroner’s Office confirmed on Monday that Urker’s death last month was the result of an accidental drug overdose.

“The final cause of death has been determined to be accidental poly substance drug toxicity,” a statement read. “The case is now complete, and no further testing is pending.”

News broke in October that Blanchard’s ex-fiancé was found dead inside a Louisiana home on his 34th birthday. At the time, foul play was not suspected.

Urker — who is survived by his and Blanchard’s 22-month-old daughter, Aurora — met Blanchard, 35, in 2017 as prison pen pals. At the time, Blanchard was incarcerated for her role in the 2015 murder of her mother, Clauddine “Dee Dee” Blanchard.

Gypsy was briefly engaged to Urker in 2018, but they parted ways that same year.

Gypsy married Ryan Scott Anderson in July 2022 while in prison, getting out of jail the following December. The couple’s marriage didn’t last and they split in spring of 2024.

Following her breakup with Anderson, Gypsy reconnected with Urker in 2024 and they welcomed their daughter that December. (That same month her divorce from Anderson was finalized.)

Gypsy broke her silence over Urker’s death in early October, telling People, “I’m in disbelief. I can’t think. I’ve screamed until my throat burns.”

She called Urker “an amazing father and a man who was deeply loved,” adding, “He will forever be my soulmate and my red string, a connection that time, distance and even death can never erase.”

Earlier this month, Gypsy responded to rumors that she was somehow involved in Urker’s death, telling 60 Minutes Australia on Sunday, November 15, “[It was] disgusting to hear because that was the love of my life, that’s the father of my child.”

She explained, “I’ve known Ken for almost nine years; no other man, no other person had my heart like he did.”
