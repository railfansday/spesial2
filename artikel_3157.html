Costco Just Announced a Special Dividend for Shareholders as of Nov. 24. Should You Buy Shares Before Then?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Costco stock soared after the announcement, and it's back to beating the market.

By Jennifer Saibil – Nov 20, 2026 at 12:23PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Costco Wholesale ›

Costco Wholesale (COST +1.45%) is usually a market-beating stock, but it's spent most of this year trailing the S&P 500 (^GSPC -2.77%). However, Costco stock soared last week after the company announced that it will pay a $30 per-share special dividend to shareholders as of Nov. 24. Should you buy Costco stock now?

Costco has paid a special dividend to investors four times since 2015, and it keeps getting larger. The last one was in 2023 for $15. Costco's regular dividend yields a paltry 0.53%, and this is a fantastic addition. The latest special dividend will be paid on Dec. 4 to shareholders as of Nov. 24.

Management is always talking about the possibility of issuing the special dividend as its cash pile increases. The company has been reporting outstanding performance, with accelerating revenue growth, including a 13% year-over-year increase in both September and October.

The only issue with Costco stock is its valuation. Even before the recent rise, Costco stock traded at more than 40 times trailing-12-month earnings, which is pricey for a retail stock. As I write this, its P/E ratio is 53.

If you're planning to hold for many years, it could make sense to buy Costco stock ahead of the special dividend payment. But I'm waiting for a better entry price.
