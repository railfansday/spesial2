Juventus vs Omonia &#8211; Predicted lineup and team news

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Juventus, currently fifth in the UEFA Europa League group table, take on Cypriot side Omonia Nicosia on Thursday at the Allianz Stadium in Turin.

The Bianconeri come into tonight's game in excellent form, having avoided defeat in their previous four matches across all competitions, with three wins and one draw. After beating Napoli 2-0 in Serie A and winning 2-1 away at AZ Alkmaar in the Europa League, Juventus drew 2-2 with Fiorentina before crushing Venezia 4-0 on Sunday. Under Luciano Spalletti, the Italian club have also gone unbeaten in their four Europa League fixtures this season, scoring an average of 1.9 goals per game.

Spalletti has confirmed that he will rotate his squad ahead of Sunday's Serie A meeting with Como. Goalkeeper Norberto Neto and young attacker Jeff Ekhator are both set to start for the first time this season, while Andrea Cambiaso comes back into the side on the left.

Francisco Conceição is fit again after spending 40 days out with a shoulder problem, and Kerim Alajbegović may also return following a small injury picked up against Venezia.

However, Juventus remain without a number of important players. Club captain Manuel Locatelli, Kenan Yıldız, Khéphren Thuram, Kamil Grabara, who has an ACL injury, Pierre Kalulu, who has a hamstring issue, and Nico González are all unavailable.

Meanwhile, Daniele Rugani, Edon Zhegrova, and Arkadiusz Milik cannot feature tonight because they were left out of Juventus's official UEFA Europa League squad.

Possible Juventus starting XI: Neto; Gatti, Bremer, Lucumi; Weston McKennie, Douglas Luiz, Pape Matar Sarr, Andrea Cambiaso; Francisco Conceição, Jeff Ekhator, Jérémie Boga

The match will kick off at 8pm BST on Thursday, 26th November.

In the United Kingdom, the match between Juventus and Omonia Nicosia will be broadcast live on TNT Sports 4.
