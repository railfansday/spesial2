Aspen Dental Opens in Willis With Comprehensive Care, Emergency Appointments and App-Based Scheduling

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The new office on Interstate 45 is led by Dr. Anthony Spates, who completed a fellowship in oral and maxillofacial surgery focused on implants and extractions

WILLIS, Texas, Nov. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Patients in Willis, Panorama Village, northern Conroe and the Lake Conroe area can now get comprehensive dental care at a new Aspen DentalÂ® office at 12250 Interstate 45 N. The practice offers exams and cleanings, fillings, root canals, extractions, wisdom tooth removal, dental implants, dentures, crowns and bridges, along with same-day emergency appointments for patients with a toothache or a broken tooth, subject to availability. Patients receive a written cost estimate before treatment begins, and third-party financing is available for larger treatment plans.

The office is independently owned and operated by Anthony Spates, DMD, who also owns and operates Aspen Dental practices in Conroe and Houston. Dr. Spates completed a yearlong fellowship in oral and maxillofacial surgery at the University of Missouri-Kansas City, where he focused on implants and extractions, training that supports the office's tooth replacement options, including dental implants, dentures, crowns and bridges. Amy John, DDS, a graduate of the University of Texas School of Dentistry, serves as managing clinical director.

"Opening in Willis feels like a natural next step for our team, and we are looking forward to meeting our new neighbors," said Dr. Spates. "We want this to be the office people think of first, whether they need a cleaning, help with a sudden toothache or a plan for replacing missing teeth."
