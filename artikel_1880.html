Matthias Jaissle defied the odds at St James' Park as Newcastle United avoided defeat against Arsenal this weekend.

The Magpies even took the lead via Malick Thiaw in the 62nd minute, only for Noni Madueke to hit back 16 minutes later.

Even with 10 minutes of added time, Mikel Arteta's side could not find a winner away from home. Yet, questions remain over Jaissle's future.

Newcastle sit 13th in the Premier League table after their 2-2 draw on Saturday, 21 November, eight points off the Champions League spots.

The 2026-27 campaign was always going to be a difficult one, with a change of manager and transfer tactics in the space of one summer.

However, expectation remains amongst the fanbase, and it has led to some toxic atmospheres so far this season.

Jaissle is just 14 games into his tenure on Tyneside, but with so much recent success at Newcastle, he was always in for a rough ride.

Now, via iNews (22 November), sources have played down any tension between manager and members of the hierarchy.

It may not be a good sign in itself that this has to be clarified, but it is hardly a surprise when Newcastle have gone from winning titles to these struggles.

Transfers, or lack thereof, has left the manager with limited options on the pitch, and he is already making requests ahead of the January window.

Jaissle believes that his team needs more experience and more goals to meet his playing style, and in a "perfect world," he would sign Ivan Toney.

Signing Toney would make a lot of sense, and given his connections to the club, it wouldn't be a surprise if he wanted a return to prove himself.

Having worked under Jaissle at Al Ahli as well, a move for the in-form centre-forward would be a no-brainer for the Magpies this season.

Keith Wyness has already warned Newcastle that signing the 30-year-old would be a big gamble, but it might be one worth taking.

Newcastle will not be the only team interested, with Football Insider understanding that Manchester United are also keeping an eye.
