Trafford’s England heroics come as no surprise to Leeds boss Farke

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Daniel Farke admitted he was surprised that everyone was so shocked by James Trafford's display during England's thrilling Nations League win over Spain.

Trafford was selected to start ahead of the Three Lions' final Group A3 fixture against the reigning world champions, and he more than played his part in their 3-2 victory.

England were three goals ahead thanks to strikes from Harry Kane, Ezri Konsa and Ben White before Elliot Anderson was sent off for a last-man foul on Lamine Yamal.

Mikel Oyarzabal and Nico Williams were able to find a way past the Leeds goalkeeper, but in the latter stages at the Metropolitano, Trafford produced a string of important stops, including a superb double save to deny Fermin Lopez and Aymeric Laporte.

But Trafford's display did not come as a shock to Farke, whose Leeds side have conceded just eight Premier League goals so far this season.

Only Arsenal (five) and Brighton (seven) have let in fewer, while Trafford has made the second-most saves (37) and has the best save ratio (81.4%) in the competition in 2026-27.

"I'm a bit bored because in my last 237 press conferences I've been praising him and right now the whole country is praising him," Farke said of Trafford.

"I'm surprised that everyone is so surprised that our goalkeeper is capable of delivering a top performance. That is why he wears the gloves.

"I also expect him to catch some balls and make some saves. I'm not surprised he's had a good performance."

Trafford will expect to busy again this weekend when Leeds make the trip to Stamford Bridge to face a Chelsea side who signed off for the international break with a defeat.

Enzo Le Fee's first-half penalty was enough to earn Sunderland a 1-0 win, a result that saw Xabi Alonso's side drop out of the top four at the expense of Liverpool.

Valentin Barco was an unused substitute at the Stadium of Light and was unable to see any minutes for Argentina in their defeat to Uruguay and 7-0 thrashing of Costa Rica.

Barco has only made four Premier League appearances for Chelsea this season, totalling 119 minutes, but he believes he will eventually come good at Stamford Bridge.

"As a player, you always want to play so you are not happy when you don't, but I understand this is the best national team in the world," Barco told ESPN.

"But I'm happy to come to the national team, to continue to represent my country. I will now try to play at Chelsea and contribute."

"I think I can give a lot more. I also said the same thing when I was at Strasbourg; even when I was playing well, I knew I could give more because I know what I can give.

"I have a lot of confidence in myself, so I have to keep working to continue growing."

Rogers also boasts a good record against Leeds in the top-flight. Only against Manchester United and Bournemouth (both three) has he netted more times against than Leeds (two, level with four other teams).

Chelsea have won eight of their last 10 home games against Leeds in all competitions (D2) since a 2-0 loss in December 1999. They have already beaten the Whites 6-3 at Stamford Bridge in the EFL Cup this season.

The Blues have also won 10 points from five top-flight home games this season (W3 D1 L1), their most after five home matches since 2022-23 under Thomas Tuchel/Graham Potter (11), and their most at the start of a campaign under a new manager since 2018-19 under Maurizio Sarri (11).

But this could be an intriguing battle at both ends of the pitch. Leeds have generated the most expected goals (xG) from set-pieces (excl. penalties) in the Premier League this season (6.1), with their only two goals across their last four league games coming from a throw-in and a corner.

Chelsea, meanwhile, have conceded the second-most goals from set-pieces (eight) in the big five European leagues in 2026-27 (Monza – nine, excluding penalties).

Leeds avoided defeat in both Premier League meetings with Chelsea last season (3-1 home, 2-2 away), last going unbeaten in three in a row against the Blues between November 2000 and October 2001.

However, Leeds have won just one of their last 25 matches in London in all competitions (D6 L18), a 3-0 win at Millwall in September 2023. Four of their last five defeats have come in the capital, including two against Chelsea: 1-0 at Wembley in the FA Cup in April and 6-3 at Stamford Bridge in September.
