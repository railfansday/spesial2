Ye Previews New Music During Yeezy 01 Fashion Show: Kid Cudi, 2 Chainz, Big Sean & More Attend

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

It's the first Yeezy runway show since 2022.

Yeezy season has returned. Ye (formerly Kanye West) had the runway back on for the Yeezy Season 01 fashion show on Wednesday (Nov. 4) in Los Angeles, which served as his first Yeezy show since 2022’s controversial Paris Fashion Week exhibit, at which he wore a “White Live Matter” shirt.

Plenty of star power was on hand to support West — who continues to face backlash for his years of antisemitic hate speech, though he took out an ad in the Wall Street Journal to apologize in January — including Big Sean, Kid Cudi, 2 Chainz, Natalie Nunn, Sab Zada, designer Jerry Lorenzo, model India Love, his wife Bianca Censori, along with Ye’s four children with ex-wife Kim Kardashian: North, Saint, Psalm and Chicago.

Attendees formed a circle around the white dome structure as models donned black military-style garb, including combat boots, trench coats and leather jackets. There were also tan boots, along with red pants, sweaters, T-shirts and bomber jackets, with other minimalistic looks resembling earlier Yeezy aesthetic.

As he’s done in the past, Ye previewed unreleased music, providing the soundtrack to the 17-minute runway show, which featured rising rap star Nettspend as one of the models. “Must be out my mind/ Haven’t felt this way in a long time,” Ye sings over a high-pitched sample and drums.

A second song featured a blaring siren on the production side. “I went through your phone/ Who the hell is Tone?/ I went through your phone,” Ye repeats on the haunting track.

West posed for photos in the rare public appearance with all four of his children. North sported her signature neon blue hair, while Saint rocked a gray hoodie and Psalm and Chicago opted for more earthy tones.

On the music front, Ye released his Bully album in March, which debuted at No. 2 on the Billboard 200. He’s been back out touring across the globe for much of 2026, performing in stadiums with spot U.S. dates.

The 49-year-old has a pair of shows left this year, as he’s set to take over AT&T Stadium in Dallas on Saturday (Nov. 7) before heading to Glendale, Ariz., on Nov. 21.
