CBS Inks New Deal to Air Kennedy Center Honors

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The awards gala, taking place this year at the Capital One Arena instead of its namesake venue, had been without a TV or streaming home.

The Kennedy Center Honors will air on CBS once again this year, despite the longtime network home’s pact to air the awards ceremony expiring after last year’s broadcast aired.

CBS has inked a new deal to televise the event, which will also stream live on Paramount+ (Premium), on Dec. 27 after the awards themselves take place on Dec. 7.

The 2026 Kennedy Center Honors will take place at Capital One Arena in Washington D.C., not the Kennedy Center itself. The new digs for the Kennedy Center Honors follows a shut down of the Kennedy Center and efforts by President Donald Trump to rename the arts institution. There’s no word on whether Trump will again host the 2026 Kennedy Center Honors event.

Previously announced honorees include Paul Anka, David Foster, Andy Garcia, David Mamet and Barbara Mandrell. CBS taking on broadcast duties for the 2026 Kennedy Center Honors follows last year’s event, hosted by Trump, being the lowest-rated Kennedy Center Honors telecast. Still, the Kennedy Center defended the show’s popularity, noting that it aired two days before Christmas and didn’t have the Sunday spot, with an NFL lead-in, that the 2024 broadcast had enjoyed.

CBS has televised every Kennedy Center Honors since 1978, but the last deal ended with the 2026 broadcast. And CBS’ exclusive negotiating window lapsed in 2025, opening up the potential pool of bidders.

The low ratings, however, wasn’t initially hurting interest in TV or streaming rights for the ceremony, The Hollywood Reporter revealed in January.

“Several outlets want to get the next contract — and CBS says they won’t lose it,” a source told THR at the time.

The Kennedy Center itself has been at the center of a legal battle as Trump slammed a court decision to block his name, twice, from being added to the building’s exterior. The board at the arts institution also chose to shut down the Kennedy Center, with Trump in social media posts suggesting the closure would allow future renovations.

Trump’s close interest in and involvement with Kennedy Center events, since he first added his name to the exterior of the building in a controversial move at the end of 2025, was a wild card in dealmaking.

The 49th Kennedy Center Honors will be produced by Den of Thieves, with Jesse Ignjatovic and Evan Prager as executive producers, and Barb Bialkowski as executive producer and showrunner.

Directing the special will be Micah Bickham, while Cheche Alara will serve as music producer and music director.
