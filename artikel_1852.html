Ex-refs chief hits out at Dermot Gallagher's 'incorrect' Sky TV verdict as Man United decision overturned

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The FA have rightly overturned Leny Yoro's red card after he was given his marching orders in Man United's defeat at Liverpool.

That is according to former PGMOL chief and FIFA referee, Keith Hackett, who believes that the incident should force Howard Webb to advise the officials involved about DOGSO rules.

Yoro saw red inside the first eight minutes as Mauricio Pochettino's first game in charge of the Red Devils ended in a 4-0 loss at Anfield on Saturday.

The French centre-back pulled Alexander Isak down just outside of the penalty area and was adjudged to have denied the Reds striker a goalscoring opportunity.

Referee Sam Barrott produced the red card without hesitation, while VAR officials John Brooks and Paul Howard were content to see the decision stand.

The FA have now gone against their verdict and overturned the call, with Yoro now clear to face Brentford in Premier League action next weekend.

There was a clear element of debate around Yoro's red card, given that Isak did not look to be in control of the ball when he was fouled following a heavy touch.

Pro Ref chief Webb backed his officials by insisting that it was the "correct" decision to send the United defender off on Sky Sports' Mic'd Up show.

The FA have taken a different view and removed the sanction from his record, which Hackett believes is the right outcome.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, he said: "The [DOGSO] criteria was not fulfilled, i.e. likelihood of keeping or gaining control of the ball. Having watched Dermot Gallagher on Ref Watch stating that the decision by the referee was correct, it was evident that he had failed to refer to law.

"I advised that Manchester Utd should appeal. The appeals panel have come to the correct decision by overturning the red card. I suggest that Howard Webb and his group of match officials need to deliver some operational advice so that, in future, the law is applied correctly."

Yoro made a mistake against Liverpool that ultimately contributed to United's heavy loss, but Pochettino must keep him in the side for the time being.

He has been in and out of the team so far this term, but ranks highly among several key metrics among top-flight centre-backs up to now.

Yoro has a 90 per cent successful pass rate (93rd percentile) from 41.7 successful passes (77th percentile) per 90 minutes, as well as 15 line-breaking passes (90th percentile) per 90.

He also has 10.6 defensive contributions (96th percentile) per 90, with 3.01 tackles (88th percentile), 3.21 interceptions (97th percentile) and 4.21 clearances (91st percentile) per 90.
