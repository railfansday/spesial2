INVE Tech (INVE) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

INVE exits IoT and pivots to acquiring recurring-revenue software.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

INVE Technologies, Inc. (INVE -0.19%) completed the sale of its IoT business to Trackonomy and transitioned its strategy toward becoming a physical AI enterprise software operating company. Management reported that the company currently has no commercial operating revenue following the Sept. 15 divestiture and is focusing on acquiring SaaS businesses with durable recurring revenue. The company established a strategic partnership with Trackonomy to integrate the Sentient platform into future software acquisitions, aiming to provide verified, real-time data for critical operational workflows. A $20 million Dutch auction tender offer is currently underway as part of a broader $40 million capital return program, supported by a debt-free balance sheet and $87.1 million in cash and equivalents.

James Greenwell: Thanks, Ed. I would like to spend the remainder of my remarks discussing the path forward for INVE Technologies, covering our market opportunity, go-forward strategy, and our planned operating model. INVE Technologies is moving in a focused, opportunistic direction. Our mission is to deliver real-time, verified intelligence for the most critical operational workflows. The thesis of our model begins with the core operational reality: physical assets need a voice. Today, that voice is created by ground truth IoT sensor data, measuring variables like temperature, humidity, moisture, G-force, and assembly line delay times. When physical world data feeds directly into intelligent software workflows, enterprise applications move beyond manual tracking into automated, transparent operating systems. Generative AI is changing enterprise workflow software.

Traditional forms, checklists, and standalone SOP platforms are facing margin pressure because AI tools can automate routine administrative workflows very quickly. However, software alone cannot independently verify ground truth data in the physical world. Many systems still rely on manual data entry, self-reporting checkboxes, and retrospective audit trails. In critical, highly regulated industries, manual attestation creates operational and regulatory liability. Regulators, insurers, and enterprise customers increasingly require continuous, tamper-evident physical proof. This is the purpose of physical AI and intelligent enterprise software. Software backed by verified, real-time physical world data creates workflow transparency, retains pricing power, and drives long-term customer retention. Our strategic partnership with Trackonomy provides the foundation for our new physical AI strategy.

Trackonomy orchestrates more than 20 million shipments daily for global enterprises across logistics, aviation, healthcare, defense, and retail in over 55 countries. Under our definitive strategic agreement, Trackonomy will serve as our advanced physical AI technology partner, providing the physical data engine, site infrastructure, and hardware deployment. This will allow our acquired software businesses to integrate Trackonomy's Sentient platform, smart BLE labels, and sensors directly into their application under our strategic terms. This integration will create a structured data layer using verified real-world operational inputs, enabling our acquired SaaS businesses to launch premium physical AI offerings designed to expand existing customer values and improve margins.

To execute this vision, we are actively pursuing acquisitions of established enterprise software companies to build an integrated operating platform. We are targeting businesses with durable recurring revenue, healthy gross margin, strong customer retention, and an underlying reliance on physical world verification. Our pipeline includes compliance and regulated sectors like worker safety, food safety, pharmaceutical logistics, and environmental monitoring, while expanding into broader enterprise software where physical custody and operating conditions dictate business outcomes. Acquired companies are expected to retain their brand identities, product roadmaps, and customer relationships. At the same time, INVE anticipates centralizing corporate G&A, managing finance, legal, human resources, IT, and public company reporting at the corporate level.

This is designed to remove administrative friction for our acquired companies and drive operating leverage. Our corporate development team is actively assessing target opportunities. We have established clear screening criteria and are evaluating small to mid-sized businesses across our priority vertical markets. We expect to fund acquisitions through a disciplined mix of cash and equity while maintaining flexibility to form strategic relationships when appropriate. Completing our first transaction remains a top priority. In closing, as we look ahead to 2027, INVE Technologies maintains strong fundamentals to execute our physical AI strategy for success. We have a debt-free balance sheet with cash for strategic acquisitions, an active capital return program underway, valuable equity ownership, and a strategic commercial partnership with Trackonomy.

This focused operating strategy can scale our physical AI enterprise software platform. I want to thank our employees, our partners at Trackonomy, and our shareholders for their continued support as we build INVE 2.0. With that, I'd like to open the call for questions.
