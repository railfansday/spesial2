ServiceNow vs. Palantir Technologies: Comparing Steady Sales Scale Against Rapid Revenue Acceleration

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Palantir's quarterly revenue has more than quadrupled in two years, narrowing the gap with ServiceNow's far larger base at a pace the rival cannot match.

ServiceNow (NOW -2.82%) primarily generates revenue by providing global enterprise clients with specialized cloud-based software platforms that are designed to automate critical business workflows, streamline customer service operations, and optimize complex information technology infrastructure across various industries.

It recently expanded strategic technical integrations with Alphabet-owned Google Cloud and Nvidia. It also launched distinct artificial intelligence governance applications, while concurrently navigating multiple legal investigations initiated by external law firms during a period of stock price volatility.

Palantir Technologies (PLTR -4.52%) primarily generates revenue by engineering highly sophisticated software systems that facilitate complex data integration, secure data management, and advanced analytics for national intelligence agencies, defense organizations, and large commercial enterprises globally, helping them manage unstructured data.

It secured a major production agreement from the U.S. Army to manufacture advanced intelligence targeting ground stations, and it simultaneously established fresh collaborations with Nebius Group and Nvidia to enhance the commercial delivery of its software models.

Revenue serves as a primary financial baseline for individual investors to evaluate because it establishes the total amount of incoming money a business generates from its daily operations before accounting for payroll, taxes, or general operating expenses. Tracking this metric reveals whether an organization is successfully attracting customers and growing its overall business volume over time.

Looking at the revenue trends between ServiceNow and Palantir reveal the massive growth momentum the latter is experiencing. Palantir's sales expansion is thanks to its commercial business.

Palantir's roots are in providing data analytics to government agencies, and so, its revenue was heavily dependent on federal spending. That changed with the arrival of its artificial intelligence platform (AIP) in 2023.

AIP began attracting commercial customers, and in the third quarter of 2026, the company's U.S. commercial revenue surpassed U.S. government revenue for the first time. This helped Palantir grow total Q3 revenue by 96% year over year to $2.3 billion, the first time its sales exceeded the $2 billion mark.

ServiceNow introduced AI into its platform, and the company is demonstrating solid sales growth, but not at the impressive rate Palantir is seeing. In Q3, ServiceNow's revenue of $4.1 billion represented a 20.5% year-over-year increase. This rate of growth is consistent with what it's delivered over the past eight quarters.

Despite ServiceNow's consistency in the top-line, that growth hasn't trickled down to its bottom line as costs rose. Q3 net income fell year over year to $277 million compared to $502 million in 2025. Meanwhile, Palantir's Q3 net income soared 49% year over year to $1.1 billion, demonstrating why its stock’s valuation is so much higher than ServiceNow's.
