Fidus Investment Corporation (FDUS +1.13%) reported a period of stable portfolio value as originations of $51.8 million nearly offset repayments of $52.4 million in the third quarter. Management indicated that deal flow began to accelerate midyear following lower activity in the first half of 2026, primarily driven by a resurgence in the mergers and acquisitions market. The company maintained its focus on the lower middle market, specifically targeting niche market leaders in business services and industrial sectors while maintaining a conservative debt structure with 88% first-lien exposure. Management stated it intended to use its at-the-market program to keep leverage within a target range of 0.9x to 1.1x to protect its investment-grade rating and provide flexibility for new originations.
