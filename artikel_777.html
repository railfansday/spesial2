Offset Says He Has ‘No Hard Feelings’ for Stefon Diggs After Athlete’s Cardi B Romance

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Diggs and Cardi appeared to split earlier this year.

Offset’s coparenting relationship with his estranged wife, Cardi B, with whom he has three children, is in a much better place these days. The Migos rapper joined Tony Yayo and Uncle Murda’s The Real Report on Tuesday (Nov. 17), during which Set showed love for Cardi and said he has no issues with her NFL star ex, Stefon Diggs.

“No issues, grown adults,” he said of Cardi. “We handle our kids. I give our kids no issues. Everything’s sweet spot. I respect where she at and salute and big up everything she doing. That’s the mother of my kids, so it’s all love.”

Being his unfiltered self, Tony Yayo tried to spice up the conversation, asking Offset if he ever wanted to fight Stefon Diggs, whom he mistakenly calls his brother, Trevon.

“Ain’t no hard feelings,” Offset contested.

Yayo continued to needle Set, alleging that the Atlanta rapper would’ve crashed out if he ever saw Diggs in person when he was with Cardi. “Nah, I’ma look like a shrimp doing some s–t like that,” Offset replied.

He continued: “I’m having bankroll and I’m having stuff. At the end of the day, I feel like that’d been the worst thing ever … if I did some s–t like that. That’s lame as hell.”

Cardi and Offset welcomed three kids together: 8-year-old daughter Kulture, 5-year-old son Wave and 2-year-old girl Blossom.

The former couple tied the knot in 2017 and Cardi filed for divorce for the second time in July 2024. The divorce has yet to be finalized.

Diggs, who currently plays for the Washington Commanders, began dating Cardi B last year; they went public with their relationship in May 2025 while attending a New York Knicks playoff game.

Cardi and Diggs welcomed a baby boy, Grey Marsean Diggs, in November 2025. They seemingly split months later, around the time Cardi hit the road for her Little Miss Drama Tour in February 2026, shortly after Diggs suited up in the Super Bowl for the New England Patriots.

On the music front, Offset returned to release his GMFU album Nov. 13. The project features appearances from Gunna, Quavo and more.
