Grey Dawning oozes class in Betfair Chase repeat

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Dan Skelton-trained Grey Dawning showed his quality when defending his Betfair Chase crown at the expense of Jagwar in Haydock's National Hunt feature; the winner is now a 10-1 chance for the Ryanair Chase at the Cheltenham Festival

Grey Dawning joined an illustrious list when following up his success of 12 months ago with a smooth performance in the Betfair Chase at Haydock.

Having seen off Royale Pagaille last year, this time Dan Skelton's grey found Jagwar a tougher adversary.

Jagwar jumped with aplomb throughout for Harry Cobden, but made his first mistake at the third last which handed the initiative to Grey Dawning and Harry Skelton.

However, Jagwar came back for more and it was not until Grey Dawning showed his superior speed after the final fence the contest was over, as the 11-10 favourite went on to win by three and three-quarter lengths.

Grey Dawning joins Kauto Star, Cue Card, Bristol De Mai and Royale Pagaille to have won the Grade One back-to-back.

After returning to the winner's enclosure serenaded by chants of "walking in a Skelton wonderland" and "there's only one Harry Skelton" the jubilant brothers admitted this was a special victory.

"This is very special. Cheltenham is obviously very vast and it is hard to soak up the atmosphere but do you know what, one day when I am not riding I will always remember this place. And those are the memories you do it for," said the winning rider.

"Ups and downs you have to push through but what a special day and thank you to everyone here for making that happen. He has been a fantastic horse. He was fantastic out there today and never put a foot wrong."

The winning trainer said: "It is great to be involved in such iconic horses and it is just an amazing achievement from the horse and a fabulous ride from Harry. It feels like a real team atmosphere and it's great.

"Harry is riding at the top of his game and he does more for me than you will ever appreciate and it is a fantastic team effort. Me and Harry are the strikers if you like but it is our job to help everybody along."

Grey Dawning finished a highly creditable fourth to Gaelic Warrior in the Gold Cup last season before landing the Melling Chase on his final start at Aintree and the champion trainer is inclined to leave the blue riband to others this time round.

"Of course the Gold Cup is our ultimate test in the sport but we tried that. I thought we had him in Gold Cup form and so it turned out. If he hadn't been in great form he wouldn't have been able to do what he did at Aintree," said Dan Skelton.

"The truth is we tried for a Gold Cup and we weren't quite good enough. The Ryanair is a Grade One, it is the marquee race on the Thursday and this horse is definitely better in a Ryanair than a Gold Cup and that's where he will go."

Skelton went on to say he would enter the horse in the King George "in case it is abandoned and we got it left-handed somewhere" as well as Leopardstown at Christmas.
