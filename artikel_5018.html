US banks want access to Fed funding on holidays as Wall Street prepares for longer settlement hours

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Fed’s planned payment expansion could allow Bitcoin futures margin to settle on Sundays and weekday holidays.

Thirty-five of 56 domestic banks in a Federal Reserve survey said borrowing from the Fed’s discount window on Sundays and weekday holidays was important, and this has a direct impact on Bitcoin.

The results, released Nov. 20, come as the Fed prepares to add those days to its wholesale payments calendar.

CME’s Thanksgiving advisory shows settlement banks will be closed on Nov. 26, so cash transfers for margin will wait even as CME recalculates its requirements.

The Fed collected responses in September from 91 banks, including 35 foreign banking organizations. In the survey, 24 domestic respondents said matching weekday discount-window hours mattered, and 11 chose access for some hours.

Across all 91 respondents, 41 selected one of those two answers.

The biggest group was unsure whether it would set aside extra cash. In the full sample, 22 expected to temporarily raise reserves ahead of weekends and holidays once the payments schedule expands. Thirty-one did not expect to increase reserves, and 38 were unsure, according to the survey.

In October 2025, the Fed announced it would add Sundays and weekday holidays to Fedwire Funds, its wholesale wire service, and the National Settlement Service. The rollout is expected in 2028 or 2029.

Both services would keep their existing daily hours, and Saturdays would remain outside the business-day schedule.

The Fed’s operating notice treats holiday discount-window loans as a separate decision. The Fed intends to offer eligible banks intraday credit on the added operating days under existing terms.

That credit can bridge a gap between incoming and outgoing payments during the day. Requests for discount-window loans through Discount Window Direct currently follow each local Reserve Bank’s business hours.

CME will recalculate initial margin, the collateral it requires to cover potential losses, at the end of the holiday. It will defer cash margin and collateral transactions, along with settlement variation, the payments that settle gains and losses on open positions.

Under CME’s clearing timetable, trades and transactions submitted for clearing on Thanksgiving will clear Friday. Settlement banks are due to confirm the prior business day’s settlement variation and margin requirements by 7:30 a.m. Central time Friday.

In a 2024 comment letter on the earlier Fedwire proposal, CME said longer wire access could let clearinghouses move dollars for initial margin and settlement variation outside normal banking days. CME called for coordination with settlement banks and other market participants.

For CME Bitcoin futures, an added Fedwire day could let settlement banks that choose to participate move margin dollars on a holiday.

CME would still have to run the applicable clearing and settlement cycle for the margin to settle that day. Banks would have to line up the dollars, and access to holiday discount-window loans depends on the Fed’s lending decision.
