Michael Carrick issues Manchester United injury latest in four-man update

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Michael Carrick has delivered an update on Manchester United’s injury list ahead of his side’s clash with Roma.

The Red Devils return to European action on Tuesday as the Serie A side visit Old Trafford.

United are on a run of nine games without a victory in all competitions, which stretches back to the end of August, where they beat Ipswich Town 5-2.

That result is their only win in the Premier League to date, with their only other victory coming against minnows Sabah in the Champions League on matchday one.

The heat is cranking up on Carrick amid United’s poor run of form, which has seen three straight defeats, including heavy 4-1 and 3-0 results against Como and Bournemouth respectively.

Last time out, Carrick’s ten men - after Lisandro Martinez’s sending off - were beaten 2-0 by Chelsea at Stamford Bridge.

United have been dealing with persistent injuries so far this term, as they look to shake off their worst start to a season in 40 years.

They welcomed Kobbie Mainoo back off the bench at the weekend, while Patrick Dorgu returned to team training after a lay-off with a hamstring issue.

Luke Shaw and Marcus Rashford have been sidelined with muscle and hips problems.

“It’s great to have him back," Carrick said of the returning Dorgu. "He's worked ever so hard to be back with the group, but he won’t be involved tomorrow.

"We’ll see how he progresses through the week.”

There was a concern as eagle-eyed fans spotted Leny Yoro sporting strapping on his knee in open training on Monday morning.

Carrick said of the Frenchman: "Leny has had a bit of a tendon [issue], it's one of them that comes and goes. But he's been fine and I've been delighted with him.”

As for Shaw and Rashford, neither of the England internationals will feature against Roma, with neither out training with their team-mates.
