Ivan Toney move a 'big gamble' for Newcastle as 2028 issue looming - Keith Wyness

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Newcastle United should rethink their pursuit of Al-Ahli striker Ivan Toney ahead of the January transfer window.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that there are concerns regarding the 30-year-old's attitude.

Toney has been heavily linked with a return to the Premier League in recent years, and Newcastle are the latest side to take an interest in the free-scoring ace.

However, Wyness does not believe that the Magpies should be committing a significant chunk of their budget to Toney due to his volatile character.

The frontman is set to go on trial in 2028 for an alleged assault in a London nightclub back in December 2025.

Wyness believes that there are plenty of negatives away from the pitch with Toney, which he feels arguably outweigh his goalscoring prowess.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that Newcastle need to think very carefully about whether Toney would be the right fit for their project moving forward.

The England international has already netted 12 goals from just nine games in the Saudi Pro League this season, but would come with plenty of baggage attached.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness warned that he is not really what Newcastle need right now.

"Toney, again, is he the right gamble for the manager coming in? He's 30 years old. So again, I'm stuck with this one," he said.

"This isn't really the move that I'd be making in terms of the manager. There's a bit of mixed feeling about Toney in terms of his mental attitude. He's got this big court case still hanging over him as well.

"There are a lot of negatives, and it's a big gamble. And if that's where the bulk of the budget is going to go, you've got to wonder if that's the right choice to do it.

"On the positive side, Toney has scored goals in the Premier League and has got that experience. But it would be a big move for Newcastle to put all their eggs in the Ivan Toney basket. So I'd be asking them to think carefully about that one."

Wyness previously told Football Insider earlier this week that the "key" to Newcastle unlocking Matias Fernandez-Pardo will be finding him a "settled role" in the team.

The 21-year-old joined in a £51million deal from Lille in the summer, but is still waiting for his first goal involvement for his new club.

Wyness believes that Matthias Jaissle's regular team changes haven't helped the starlet so far, as he has already been used as a central striker, an attacking midfielder and a right winger.

The boardroom expert feels that Fernandez-Pardo will flourish for Newcastle if he is afforded time, but believes that they must first nail down a specific role for him.
