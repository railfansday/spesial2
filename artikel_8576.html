Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Why would you want to keep this house? Is the neighborhood so important? Are there resources in your area for your child that are indispensable?

Why not buy a lovely $750k-$1 million house in a much lower cost of living area and live a very comfortable life where you don’t even need part time work?

Personally, I would strongly consider moving unless you have enough money saved up elsewhere so that you can live in this house indefinitely. I assume that’s not the case since you are discussing part time work and renters. Why do that if all you need to do is simply move?
Lately I am tempted to buy a little $750,000 condo or a little house in a less desirable town.
I would still have some pretty significant living expenses, between taxes, car payment, essentials. The actual Mortgage portion of my house is only $2500 a month. The rest is insurance and taxes so let’s say I get my essentials down to $7500/mo instead of $10,000 a month it still puts me at a deficit of 3000 a month compared to my soc sec of 3200/month…And no room in a small house to rent out to close that deficit it almost feels like a zero sum game?
I guess by selling I would have an extra $1 million invested even in treasuries it could throw off an extra 40 K a year which would cover the deficit. Still Doesn’t feel like a great lifestyle. Maybe my income goes in that case to 40k social security, 40k from 401k and 40k from treasuries from sale of house in that case… so essentially a $40,000 bump and a reduction in housing costs of $2500 a month or 30k a year.

And the loss of rental potential income from room or summer rental of between ~15k-30k gross in good years.
