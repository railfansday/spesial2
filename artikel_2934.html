Brandon Smith’s big game helps lead Oregon past Michigan State

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Brandon Smith's big game helps lead Oregon past Michigan State appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Oregon football head coach Dan Lanning believes his team has what it takes to compete for a College Football Playoff spot. However, it was up to his players to back up that confidence on the field, and they certainly delivered in Friday's matchup against Michigan State. Behind Brandon Smith's outstanding performance, Oregon cruised to a 45-7 win over the Spartans at Spartan Stadium.

With injuries creating concerns in Oregon's backfield, Smith stepped up and delivered a career-defining performance to help the Ducks secure another important win. He finished the game with 14 carries for 121 rushing yards and two touchdowns, while also contributing 14 receiving yards.

The Ducks encountered an unexpected setback on their opening drive when Dierre Hill Jr. limped off the field and headed to the locker room. With Jordon Davison already unavailable, Hill's injury raised further concerns about Oregon's running back depth. Nevertheless, Smith once again proved that he was more than capable of taking on an expanded role in the offense.

Meanwhile, several other Oregon players also stepped up as the Ducks maintained control of the game. Quarterback Dylan Raiola completed the game with 197 passing yards and a touchdown.

For the first time in program history, Oregon has defeated Michigan State on the road. The Ducks had previously gone 0-3 in their trips to East Lansing. Oregon exploded offensively in the final 30 minutes and seven seconds to beat Michigan State, scoring 35 points and recording 256 yards during that stretch.

The victory extended Oregon's winning streak to four games and improved its overall record to 8-3, including a 6-2 mark in Big Ten play. More importantly, Smith's stellar showing provided another encouraging sign for the Ducks as they continue their pursuit of a College Football Playoff berth.
