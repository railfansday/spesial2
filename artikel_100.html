If you’ve been holding off on buying a new MacBook Air since Apple raised prices by $200 in June, you’re in luck. The 13-inch M5 MacBook Air is on sale at Amazon and Best Buy with 16GB of RAM and 512GB of storage starting at $999 ($300 off) for Black Friday, which is the lowest price we’ve seen since the hike. If you want a larger display, Amazon and Best Buy are also taking $300 off the otherwise identical 15-inch model with 16GB of RAM and 512GB of storage, dropping it to $1,199.

The M5-equipped MacBook Air is a good middle ground if you need more power than the MacBook Neo — which, thanks to a $100 discount, is $599 — but don’t want to splurge on a MacBook Pro. Its faster chip, 16GB of RAM, and 512GB of storage make it better suited for multitasking and creative work in apps like Photoshop and Premiere. You also get a higher-resolution display, better speakers, a backlit keyboard that makes typing in the dark easier, and faster ports. Plus, its sharper webcam supports Apple’s Center Stage feature, which automatically adjusts the framing to keep you in view during video calls.

Of course, the Air can’t match the performance or higher-end displays you can get with a MacBook Pro, whether you opt for an M5 model, the newer M5 Pro model with a PROLED XDR Display, or one of the M6-powered machines. A Pro may be worth the extra money if you regularly edit video or work on more demanding creative projects. But if you mostly need a laptop for work, browsing, and occasional photo or video editing, the M5 Air is more than capable.
