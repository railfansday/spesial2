Italy star Tonali out for Tottenham Hotspur, but Udogie back to start

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Sandro Tonali is not even on the Tottenham Hotspur bench against Ipswich Town, but fellow Italy international Destiny Udogie returns from injury for a rare start.

The Premier League returns today after the break for international duty, and there are several Italy players involved in the tournament.

Roberto De Zerbi’s Spurs host Ipswich Town at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium at 15.00 GMT (16.00 CET).

The coach spoke in his press conference yesterday and made no mention of a potential physical issue for Tonali, so it was entirely unexpected when the midfielder’s name did not appear on the squad list this afternoon.

If it had been a simple issue of rest after international duty, playing in Italy’s Nations League victory over France and defeat to Belgium he would’ve at least been on the bench.

De Zerbi confirms that the Italian has been “sick for the last couple of days,” so is allowed to take time out and rest for this weekend.

If Tonali missed out, then his fellow Italy international Udogie stepped into the starting XI, returning after a series of injury problems in recent months.

The left-back has made just seven competitive appearances so far this season for Spurs, then after returning from a knee injury and playing 13 minutes against Crystal Palace, he was left out of the squad entirely for the next match with Leeds United, despite stating on Snapchat that he was “not injured.”
