Call Of Duty: Black Ops 1 Zombies Playable Completely Free In Browser Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Call of Duty: Black Ops is widely regarded as being one of the best entries in the series and for very good reason. Its campaign is undeniably one of the greatest in the history of the genre, let alone Call of Duty, but, more crucially, its Zombies mode proved to be one of the most captivating multiplayer modes of all time. Indeed, while not solely responsible, Black Ops' Zombies mode helped propel it to become one of the best-selling Call of Duty games of all time, and certainly further cemented the series' place in the annals of gaming history.

While the series' subsequent entries have seen Call of Duty change for the worse (and later for the better), Black Ops will forever be fondly remembered by fans and likely seen as the pinnacle of the series, or at least one of its best entries. As a consequence of being one of the most beloved COD games, it has been ported ad nauseam and made all the more accessible to newcomers. However, it has never been more accessible than now, as Black Ops 1's Zombies mode is completely playable in your browser for the very first time.

As spotted on Reddit, Black Ops 1's Zombies mode is completely playable in your browser for free. The entire mode, including all maps, the ability to play multiplayer, and even the option to choose between classic BOPs guns or Counter-Strike 2's guns, is available and completely functional. The browser version even allows you to choose between High or Low graphics, ensuring that it is capable of being run across all hardware, regardless of specs.

All you need to do to access it is follow the link to the browser version, select your choice from the aforementioned options, pick your map, and then wait for it to load. Once in the game, you'll play it like normal, using mouse and keyboard controls to navigate around the maps, blasting zombies in the head, and attempting to complete the assorted objectives. There are even additional graphical, game, and sound options that can be toggled or tweaked once you've loaded up the map.

It is fairly remarkable that such an iconic game mode is available in your browser and playable with friends. However, it is more or less becoming the norm thanks to the ridiculous capabilities of AI. This browser port and many like it for other games are possible because AI can simply decompile the game, add WASM support and WGPU, and then recompile it. We've seen this happen with GTA, Halo, Half-Life, and many more, and even more games are almost certainly on their way to getting their own dedicated browser ports.

Of course, while this is great for fans, it has seen pushback from the original developers and publishers. Links to browser versions of games, including Call of Duty: Black Ops Zombies, have been taken down and made unavailable. It's only a matter of time before restrictions on this grow even tighter. However, for now, if you're interested in playing Black Ops in your browser, you absolutely can, and it works surprisingly well.
