Director Peter Berg Isn’t Giving Up on the Unreleased Rihanna Documentary: ‘Keep Hope Alive’

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The director said in 2023 that he spent six and a half years filming the project.

Remember the Peter Berg-directed Rihanna documentary that was announced way back in 2016, and was reportedly sold to Amazon for $25 million in 2019?

Well, Berg is choosing to remain optimistic.

During a conversation with Complex published on Wednesday (Nov. 25), Berg — who also directed the singer and business mogul in 2012’s Battleship — discussed the doc’s current status. “There’s always hope. Keep hope alive,” he said of the unreleased documentary. “If and when it does [come out], it’s going to be a banger.”

He was talked about why he thinks the nine-time Grammy winner hasn’t done more movies, and he commended the “We Found Love” singer’s ability to always make the right decision. “Rihanna does what she wants to do. She follows her heart. She follows her creative instincts,” Berg told the publication. “When she did Battleship, she was really interested in seeing what being in a big, wild Hollywood action movie was like. And then she went and made a bunch of great albums and now she’s raising a beautiful family. I guarantee you she’s going to be back in music and it’s going to be a banger.”

Berg and his team spent years recording and piecing things together for the documentary about the star, but it’s ultimately up to Rihanna whether she wants to release it eventually. He said as much in 2023 when talking with The Hollywood Reporter. “It’s done and sold, and Amazon’s ready,” the director told the outlet at the time. “She’s a perfectionist, so we keep adding. It’s been six and a half years of filming, so, yeah, it’s ready to come out. We’re just waiting on her to say, ‘Yeah, let’s do it.’ I don’t know. Maybe it’ll be a 10-year project.”

Rihanna most recently starred in 2025’s animated film Smurfs, lending her voice to Smurfette. The last live action movie she starred in was Amazon Prime’s Guava Island opposite Donald Glover, though she makes a cameo in Ali G: Who Iz I?, which arrived in October.
