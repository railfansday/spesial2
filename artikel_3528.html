Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey Gives Percy Jackson a Massive Streaming Boost on Disney Plus

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Percy Jackson gained a lot of fans thanks to Nolan's epic adaptation

Everybody and their mother became obsessed with Greek mythology this summer thanks to Christopher Nolan's latest box-office hit, The Odyssey. It turns out people were so hungry for more that they went out searching for it in other movies, books, and TV shows. One beneficiary of that was Disney Plus' Percy Jackson and the Olympians, which got a much-needed boost prior to the premiere of its third season.

The Rick Riordan adaptation's co-showrunner, Dan Shotz told IGN that the Percy Jackson team had "heard that there was a huge boost in viewers during that period because everybody obviously was searching for The Odyssey." That came at the perfect time for the series, as its second season had concluded in July, ahead of the third season's premiere on Nov. 20. "Anybody who didn't know about Sea of Monsters found it," said Shotz. He's referencing the second book in the original series of novels, which was adapted in the show's second season and largely plays with the narrative structure and characters of The Odyssey.

Shotz added that it was surreal to see Nolan's vision of the story versus the show's. While Percy Jackson is obviously based on a story inspired by The Odyssey, the two pieces still share a lot of the same themes and plot beats because of the shared source material of Homer's original epic. His favorite part of the Odysseus-versus-Percy discussion was the memes joking about how Percy did the same journey in seven days and was far more efficient. The Odyssey isn't the only thing that has boosted interest in the excellent Disney adaptation. Recently, the first two seasons were added to Netflix, and it quickly became one of the streaming services' top performers.

If The Odyssey got you into Percy Jackson, then the imminent release of season 3 is sure to be exciting. The new season will adapt the third book, The Titan's Curse, which is something of a tonal shift for the series into darker territory. With five books in total, it also means the show is over the hump. Unfortunately, Disney has yet to announce a fourth season, which is starting to worry fans.

Season 3 will see the return of Percy, Annabeth, and Grover as the trio embark on a new quest that puts them in the middle of a battle between the gods and titans. They'll also meet new faces along the way, such as the Di Angelo siblings, the goddess Artemis, and her hunters. Walker Scobell, Aryan Simhadri, and Leah Sava Jeffries will reprise their roles as Percy, Grover, and Annabeth respectively. Newcomers to the cast include Levi Chrisopulos as Nico di Angelo, Dafne Keen as Artemis, Kate McKinnon as Aphrodite, Ming-Na Wen as Hera, and Holt McCallany as Atlas.
