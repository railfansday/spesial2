Man Remembered as 'Loving Father' Before He Killed Wife, Kids in Murder-Suicide

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Just days after a father killed his wife and their three young kids in a murder-suicide, community members of Plant City, Florida, expressed their shock over the tragedy after believing the patriarch to be a “loving father.”

As Us previously reported, the Hillsborough County Sheriff’s Office confirmed authorities found five family members deceased after reporting to the family’s home on Thursday, November 19.. Authorities ultimately determined that Issac Luechtefeld killed his wife, Raquel Luechtefeld, and their kids Enoch, 5, Hannah, 3, and Job, 1, before he died by suicide.

Many community members are in shock in the wake of the killings, including Enoch’s martial arts teacher.

“They were an amazing family, always smiling, laughing, and having a good time. This is a shock to us all. I never would have imagined something so horrific could happen,” Samuel Kimmel, owner of Carlson Gracie Plant City, told Tampa Bay 28.

Kimmel also recalled his last moment with Issac, 34, and Enoch, which he said was just days before their deaths.

“He always seemed like such a loving father. Just last week, when they came into the gym, he was leaving and telling Enoch that he loved him,” Kimmel said of Issac. “That moment and that conversation keep echoing in my head. It’s something I can’t seem to get past.”

After admitting he was having a hard time wrapping his head around the tragedy, Kimmel said that he and his coworkers “are forever grateful for the time we got to spend with Enoch at the gym.”

“He was an amazing little boy who brought so much energy and happiness with him every time he walked through our doors,” Kimmel said. “Our hearts are broken. Our thoughts and prayers are with the entire family during this unimaginable tragedy.”

Redeemer Church of Lakeland, which the family attended for several years, also shared a statement about the murder-suicides.

“Redeemer Church is heartbroken over the loss of the Luechtefeld family. They have been a vital part of our church community for several years,” the church said, per the outlet. “Please join our congregation in praying for their loved ones and the many people in our community who are grieving this loss.”

While police have determined that Issac was behind the killings, they have not yet been able to determine a motive. The investigation into the case remains ongoing.
