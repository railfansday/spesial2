Michelle Randolph Makes Rare Comment About Sister Cassie's 'Bachelor' Journey

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Michelle Randolph made a rare comment about sister Cassie Randolph‘s tumultuous time on The Bachelor.

“We jokingly told her, ‘You’re single, you’re young, go on the show.’ Not thinking twice about it,” Michelle, 29, recalled during an interview with The Hollywood Reporter, which was published on Tuesday, November 24.

Cassie, 31, appeared on season 23 of The Bachelor in 2019 amid Colton Underwood‘s search for love. Underwood, 34, infamously hopped a fence away from the cameras after Cassie chose to leave the season early instead of getting engaged.

The pair got back together off screen and dated for a year. Us confirmed in September 2020 that Cassie was granted a restraining order against Underwood after she claimed he had been stalking and harassing her. The case was dropped in November 2020, and Underwood came out as gay the following year.

“It was a crazy time,” Michelle told THR. “It was pretty overwhelming to be thrown into a world like that — the social media of it all.”

The Landman actress struggled with the online commentary, adding, “People online were so beyond cruel. Sometimes I just want to black it out. Bachelor Nation is … well, it’s a lot.”

Before Michelle’s profile came out, Underwood revealed whether he’s on speaking terms with Cassie.

“I have no contact. If she ever did reach out or want to reach out, I would be open to answering any questions that her or her family would have for me,” he told Vanity Fair in February. “That is something I’ve always offered. I’m in no position to really say much more than that or reach out. There’s been no part of me that would. I would always be up for it. It’s all on her terms.”

Underwood, who married husband Jordan C. Brown in May 2023 before welcoming a son together, continued: “I will always own the mistakes that I made. I will always feel guilt and feel this extreme sadness for how I showed up for certain friends, for people in my life. Also fans — letting people down. At that point, people had been sort of invested in me and in my story. So I did feel like not only I let myself down, I let a lot of people down there.”

Cassie, meanwhile, recently spoke to Us about life out of the spotlight after finding love with husband Brighton Reinhardt, whom she wed in December 2025.

“[The] best part [of being married] is we just have so much fun together,” she gushed earlier this month. “We always want to hang out and talk and chat and spill the tea.”
