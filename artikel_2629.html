‘He just…’ – Luka Doncic addresses throwing wristband at Spurs fan before post-game altercation

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Los Angeles Lakers star Luka Doncic found himself in some post-game controversy after the team's 99-96 loss to the San Antonio Spurs in the 2026 NBA Cup.

Doncic had a mixed night in which he stuffed the box score with 31 points, six rebounds, eight assists and three steals. However, he also took 31 shots, shot 1-13 from three-point range, and left with a high-profile loss to their rivals in San Antonio.

As Doncic left the court, he appeared to throw his wristband at some Spurs fans courtside, who did not appreciate the gesture. As Doncic went to pick the wristband back up, a fan shoved him back, leading to a highly contentious postgame moment.

Doncic spoke to the media after the game and explained what went down, revealing that this is something he does every game, and he got shoved by the fan when he heard them say they didn't want it and went to retrieve it.

"I just threw my wristband like I do almost every game, and then I heard she didn't want it, so I went back to grab it, and he just pushed me, and then I gave it to somebody else."

Anyone who has seen Doncic play in Dallas or Los Angeles knows he does throw his wristbands at fans after the game, usually because they want to take it home with them as memorabilia.

Mavericks fan and the lead producer for LeBron James' 'Mind the Game' podcast, Jason Gallagher, revealed that Doncic has done the same towards his child, but in a way that was always considered Doncic being charitable with fans.

"He nonchalantly throws wristbands/headbands at fans all the time. My kid has one! Tons of players do that. It sure looks like he didn't like what this woman said, so he went back and got it from a fan who didn't want it. Then the dork shoved him lol."

Doncic seemed to aggressively throw the wristband, which went low and hit the woman that the Spurs fan presumably came to the game with, leading to the fan's aggressive response when Doncic approached them to get the wristband back.

The heightened tensions of this early-season marquee clash between Doncic and Victor Wembanyama's Spurs seemed to have fans on edge, especially as this was a couple in Spurs jerseys who probably did not want Doncic's wristband anyway, and disagreed with him throwing it towards them.
