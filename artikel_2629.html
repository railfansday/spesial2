Cause of death revealed for ex-NFLer Alphonso Smith Jr. as heartbreaking new details emerge

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Former NFL cornerback Alphonso Smith Jr. was crying and praying during an incident where he fatally shot himself in front of two women at a home in Pahokee, Florida, on Sept. 2, according to a police report by the Palm Beach County Sheriff's Office obtained by TMZ.

One of the women was inside the home and heard Smith crying, praying aloud and speaking to several people on a conference call, per the report.

He asked her to go upstairs when she tried to comfort him — and then the mother of Smith's children arrived at the home moments later.

Alphonso Smith #33 of the Denver Broncos runs up the field during the game against the Seattle Seahawks on Aug. 22, 2009 at Qwest Field in Seattle, Washington. Getty Images

Both women, who were not named, witnessed Smith shoot himself while sitting on a living room couch.

The 40-year-old Smith was rushed to a nearby hospital before being airlifted to St. Mary's Medical Center, where he was pronounced dead at 3:19 p.m., the report stated.

Toxicology results also revealed THC, or marijuana, was present in his system.

TMZ previously reported that Smith died by suicide at a Florida housing community on Sept. 2.

Hours before his death, Smith shared a troubling Facebook post, in which he called himself a "coward" and said "this is a demon in me."

Ted Ginn Jr. #19 of the San Francisco 49ers runs for a long gain during a second quarter kick return as Alphonso Smith #27 of the Detroit Lions attempts to make the stop during the NFL game at Ford Field on Oct. 16, 2011 in Detroit, Michigan. Getty Images

Smith played four seasons in the NFL with the Broncos and Lions after Denver selected him in the second round of the 2009 draft.

He recorded eight interceptions, two defensive touchdowns, 88 tackles, two fumble recoveries/forced and 19 pass deflections.

A standout at Wake Forest, Smith earned All-American honors and he set an ACC record with 21 career interceptions, which still stands today.

Smith was inducted into the Wake Forest Sports Hall of Fame in 2022.
