Why Ravens’ Marlon Humphrey still has hope after MNF loss to Chargers

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Why Ravens' Marlon Humphrey still has hope after MNF loss to Chargers appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Baltimore Ravens' postseason hopes just became dimmer after losing to the Los Angeles Chargers, 23-20, at M&T Bank Stadium on Monday. The Ravens dropped their fifth straight game, falling to 4-6, including 1-4 at home.

Jacoby Brissett made his debut for the Ravens after being acquired in a trade with the Arizona Cardinals. Brissett went 33-of-49 for 340 yards, zero touchdowns, and one interception.

Baltimore was badly outrushed by Los Angeles, 160-16. Despite the losing streak, Marlon Humphrey remained defiant, stressing that the Ravens are not yet out.

"We're gonna get a day off, and after that, we really got to come out strong. We gotta be ready to work at practice. Everybody's in different situations, but this is definitely not the time to give up on anything. It's time to push forward," said Humphrey in the video posted by Baltimore Banner Sports.

"There's still hope for us. I think that's gotta be the key. Hopefully, everybody on the team understands that. Obviously, this one sucks. But with the quick turnaround, there's still life for us, so we gotta seize the opportunity."

Baltimore lost Nnamdi Madubuike due to a hamstring injury in the third quarter, leaving a hole on the defense, which was almost non-existent against the Chargers.

"It was a group effort, kind of just not being there fast enough, being where we needed to be, and it kind of got some leaky yardage. It's been similar to the story of this season. We've been having good weeks of preparation, and it just has not fully clicked on the field just yet," added Humphrey.

The Ravens' upcoming games against the Carolina Panthers and the Houston Texans are practically must-wins to stay alive and avoid a second straight season of missing the playoffs.
