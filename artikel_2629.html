Chiefs QB Patrick Mahomes’ deep ball is back, and this stat from Falcons drubbing proves it

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Patrick Mahomes achieved an amazing career feat following his dominant performance throwing the ball in the air throughout the Kansas City Chiefs' 35-13 win over the Atlanta Falcons on Sunday afternoon.

Mahomes stands out as one of the best quarterbacks in the league. He has Kansas City back in playoff contention after missing the postseason last year due to Mahomes' season-ending ACL injury. With him back in full force, the Chiefs are making their case as serious contenders.

Mahomes dominated Atlanta's defense throughout the day. He completed 22 out of 35 passes for 348 yards and five touchdowns. He also was active with throwing the long ball, achieving a feat he hasn't pulled off since 2021, per ESPN Insights.

"Patrick Mahomes had 5 TD passes today, including 3 on passes of 15+ air yards. He hadn't thrown a TD pass of 15+ yards downfield since Week 4 of last season, and hadn't had a game with multiple such TD since Week 10 of 2021," the post read.

Patrick Mahomes balled out on the road, helping the Chiefs offense be red-hot all game in their 35-13 win over the Falcons.

Kansas City controlled the game from start to finish, scoring in all four quarters. Atlanta tried to rally in the fourth quarter, but they made too many errors to execute it down the stretch.

Emmett Johnson excelled in his start with Kenneth Walker III out due to injury. He racked up 22 carries for 101 yards while catching six passes for 70 yards and two touchdowns.

Rashee Rice led receivers with 82 yards on five catches, his longest being a 50-yard reception. Josh Downs impressed in his Chiefs debut by catching four passes for 53 yards and a touchdown. Meanwhile, Cyrus Allen had two receptions for 44 yards and a touchdown, and Andrew Armstrong caught a 43-yard touchdown pass.

The Chiefs will look forward to their next matchup, being at home. They host the Arizona Cardinals in Week 11 as kickoff commences on Nov. 21 at 1 p.m. ET.
