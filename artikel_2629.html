‘The NFL is rigged’: Former NFL player says game results ‘already predetermined’

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Breiden Fehoko played in 19 games over his NFL career before announcing his retirement in November of 2025. Since leaving the game, Fehoko has become known for giving his unfiltered opinions on all things sports on his X social media account.

Fehoko did just that after Wednesday night's Green Bay Packers-Los Angeles Rams game, a 26-13 Rams win, asserting the results of NFL games are "rigged" and calling NFL commissioner Roger Goodell a "spineless coward."

"All right, I'm not trying to beat on a dead horse, but the officiating needs to be held accountable in the NFL. Roger Goodell is a spineless coward, and I'm going to make it my sole goal to bring this thing down, to tear it down," Fehoko said in a video posted to his account.

Fehoko took exception to the way Packers quarterback Jordan Love was officiated.

"As somebody who's played in the NFL, as somebody who was a part of games where— I won't get into that right now. But when you watch this Rams-Packers game tonight, when you watch Jordan Love get hit the way he gets hit, doesn't get one single call. When you watch Matthew Stafford cry, he doesn't even get hit to the ground and the ref throws a flag not even looking at him… I just kinda question the integrity.

"I don't question it; I know the integrity is messed up because the higher-ups, the commissioner, the NFL is all about storylines," Fehoko continued. "You gotta realize that. Myles Garrett gets traded to the Rams. Matthew Stafford's old; this is probably his last year in the NFL. Aaron Donald comes back from retirement.

"You gotta realize the NFL ain't gonna let the Rams finish less than .500. So it's just piss-poor to see. And I'm gonna make it an effort, a full effort, to keep tearing down this, this billion-dollar organization. Roger Goodell is spineless, and the way he does things, it's just gut-wrenching that players work their ass off Monday through Saturday and then have to go out there and games are already predetermined. I've said it once, and I've said again, the NFL is rigged.

"Stop putting your money into it. Stop buying into this product, because that's the only way they're going to start keeping them accountable is when fans, when people that love the sport, stop giving their financial commitment to the sport."

Fehoko's accusations are as damning as you'll find from any former player, but there's no denying that the NFL's officiating has been remarkably bad this season. ESPN's Scott Van Pelt, one of the most respected figures in sports media, did not hold back in his assessment of the league's officiating this season.

"The problem is the amount of conversation about how games are adjudicated and the amount of times that you're throwing flags … I think it's bullsh*t," Van Pelt said on SVPod. "I think the officiating, it's inconsistent. The only consistent thing seems to be third-down flags. That just can't be what they want."

Fehoko's comments feel like a major reach, but there's no denying that fans will only continue to lose faith in the integrity of the sport, especially in the era of legalized sports gambling, if the league doesn't clean up its officiating.
