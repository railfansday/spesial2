New York Jets vs. Los Angeles Chargers odds: Opening lines for Week 11 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The New York Jets lost their fifth straight game and eighth of their last nine on Sunday. The Jets are now 2-8 on the season. In one and a half seasons under Aaron Glenn the Jets have a 5-22 record and a 0-9 record in the AFC East. This is not good.

This week the Jets were home to take on the Buffalo Bills. They kept it competitive for a little more than a half. At halftime the Jets trailed 10 – 6. The Jets got the ball to start the third quarter. Cade Klubnik took over for an injured Geno Smith and made a few nice plays with his leg and his arm to get the Jets in scoring position. They settled for a field goal to get within a point of the Bills at 10 – 9 early in the third quarter. That was as close as the Jets would get in the game. The Bills outscored the Jets 21 – 3 the rest of the way as Klubnik and the Jets offense were no match for Josh Allen and the Bills offense.

The drubbing at the hands of the Bills left the Jets with a 2-8 record to start the season, in 3rd place in the AFC East. Though mathematical elimination is still probably a month or so away, it has become obvious the Jets playoff drought will extend to 16 years and counting by season's end. The Jets are also just one loss away from their 11th straight losing season. The last time the Jets won more than seven games in a season Barack Obama was still President.

Now the Jets go on the road to face the only winless team left in the NFL, the Los Angeles Chargers. The Chargers play the Baltimore Ravens on Monday Night Football, so it's possible they will no longer be winless by the time they face the Jets. The Chargers rank dead last in the NFL in points scored, with Justin Herbert somehow crashing to by far the lowest point in his career. After spending his entire career in the vicinity of a top 10 quarterback in the NFL, Herbert has arguably been the worst starting quarterback in the league this year. New Chargers offensive coordinator Mike McDaniels' system appears to be a poor match for Herbert's skill set, and the result has been an offensive train wreck. The Chargers have not scored more than 23 points in any game this season, and they have not allowed less than 22 points in any game this season. It's tough to win games like that, but the Jets may prove to be an elixir for what ails the Chargers.

Let's take a look at the current odds for this game according to FanDuel Sportsbook. The oddsmakers think the Chargers will beat the Jets next Sunday. It's come to this: the Jets aren't even favored over a winless team. The current line has the Jets road underdogs by 3.5 points. The game has an over/under of 40.5 points. On the moneyline the Jets are +156 while the Chargers are -186.

A loss next Sunday against the Chargers would be just another humiliation for the Jets. Perhaps they can find a way to avoid that fate after being beaten by a winless Dolphins team earlier this season. Perhaps. Perhaps not. Does it really matter in another lost Jets season?
