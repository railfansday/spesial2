The Jaguars gained ground in the AFC playoff standings after the Bengals loss on Monday

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Jacksonville Jaguars lost on Sunday to fall from third to fourth in the AFC playoff picture behind the Cincinnati Bengals, leaders of the AFC North. Well on Monday night, the Bengals also stumbled against and NFC East opponent and Jacksonville has once again moved back up into third place in the conference.

The Jaguars' win over the Bengals is the tiebreaker at 7-3. Both teams are one game behind the AFC West leader, be it the Chiefs or the Broncos, those Denver currently owns that tiebreaker. The Buffalo Bills are in first place at 9-1. The Bills face both the Broncos and Chiefs down the stretch, so the one seed is still very much in play.

2. Denver Broncos (8-2, 4-0 vs KC common opponents)

3. Jacksonville Jaguars (7-3, win over CIN)

5. Kansas City Chiefs (8-2, 3-1 vs DEN common opponents)

In the AFC West, the tiebreaker is the record vs common opponents now that they have the same division record. The Broncos are 4-0 against the Raiders, Chargers, Seahawks, and Cardinals. The Chiefs are 3-1 against the same opponents.
