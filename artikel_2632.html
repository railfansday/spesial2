Cincinnati Reds hire Anthony Iapoce as assistant hitting coach

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

LAS VEGAS – The Cincinnati Reds rounded out their coaching staff this week with the hiring of veteran hitting coach Anthony Iapoce as the new assistant hitting coach.The move creates a reunion of Iapoce and Reds head hitting coach Chris Valaika.Valaika was an assistant to Iapoce in their only previous year coaching together, 2021 with the Chicago Cubs.Iapoce most recently worked as the Astros' assistant hitting coach last season after two seasons as the Tigers' first base coach.

The 1994 draft pick of the Milwaukee Brewers spent nince seasons as a minor-league outfielder, reaching Triple-A in both the Brewers and Marlins system, and producing a career .345 on-base percentage with 168 stolen bases.The move comes less than a week after the Reds hired Cleveland Guardians assistant pitching coach Joe Torres as their new head pitching coach.

Team president Nick Krall said the team is still working through specific roles for some of staff, including the two other pitching coaches under Torres: Matt Tracy and Oscar Marin.
