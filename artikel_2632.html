Colts sign CB Johnathan Edwards to the active roster from the practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Colts signed cornerback Johnathan Edwards to the 53-player roster from the practice squad on Monday, the team announced.

They also announced wide receiver Samori Toure's signing to the practice squad.

Edwards has spent time on the team's active roster and practice squad this season. He was elevated for the Week 8 game against Jacksonville and the Week 9 game against Dallas.

Edwards has totaled one special teams tackle.

As a rookie in 2025, Edwards appeared in 14 games with five starts and registered 24 tackles, three passes defensed and two special teams stops.

He originally signed with the Colts as an undrafted free agent on May 9, 2025, out of Tulane.

Toure has played 23 career games with two starts in his time with the Eagles (2026), Saints (2025), Broncos (2025), Bears (2024-25) and Packers (2022-24). He has 14 receptions for 163 yards and one touchdown.
