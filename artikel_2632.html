Carson Beck remains out of Cardinals practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It looks like the Cardinals are on track to be without Carson Beck against the Commanders this week.

The quarterback missed practice for the second straight day due to the back injury that kept him from playing in last Sunday's loss to the Chiefs. Beck made his first NFL start in Week 10, but the lack of practice work points to veteran Josh Johnson making another start for a team he joined in the last month.

Guard Jon Gaines (ankle) and linebacker Josh Sweat (rest, shoulder) were also out of practice on Thursday.

Tight end Hunter Long (ankle), linebacker Karson Sharar (groin), and linebacker Cody Simon (calf) were limited participants. Safety Budda Baker (rest, elbow), center Hjalte Froholdt (rest), guard Isaac Seumalo (rest), linebacker Mack Wilson (rest), and defensive lineman Zach Carter (knee) were all full participants.
