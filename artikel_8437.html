Congrats! You’ll get a mix of very conservative and moderately conservative opinions here. Mine is the latter.

IMO, work until the point both of you can retire. Then you’ll have full optionality and a level of buffer.

25X is the minimum (not including rental) so: $130*25 - $1M = 3.25M - 1M (rental) = $2.25M liquid target.

But, your not close to 60 so let’s change the equation to 33X

$130k * 33 = $4,290 K
Take away the $1M rental = roughly $3.3M liquid.

You have $2.1M liquid.
Work until you have another ~$1M liquid & then really do what you want. Even if your wife loses her job you’ll be ok. Plus it’ll give some time for kids expenses to settle down.

How long will it take to save another $1M liquid? I’d say 5-7 years max.
