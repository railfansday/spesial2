US Blacklists Megaspeed, Targeting a Key Player in Nvidia Crisis

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Trump administration put Megaspeed International on a US trade blacklist barring it from buying advanced artificial intelligence chips, targeting a Singapore-based firm at the heart of the scandal that briefly cost Jensen Huang his job as Nvidia chief executive officer. In a filing Monday, the Commerce Department formally added Megaspeed to its so-called entity list, a designation that bars the company from buying AI chips and other advanced technology without a US government license. Maggie Eastland, Bloomberg News Tech and Industrial Policy Reporter, and Michelle Jamrisko, Bloomberg News White House & National Security Editor discuss what today's White House moves mean for the relationship with China. (Source: Bloomberg)
