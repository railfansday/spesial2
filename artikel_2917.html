‘They Could’ve Just Labeled It Grand Theft’ — NFL Fans Take Notice as Amazon Uses GTA 6 Graphic to Explain Sauce Gardner Trade on TNF

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Sauce Gardner spent Thursday night on the Indianapolis Colts' inactive list, but the trade that brought him to Indianapolis still found its way onto the national broadcast.

Amazon Prime Video's Thursday Night Football coverage revisited the Colts' deal for the cornerback with a graphic styled after Grand Theft Auto VI during the Houston Texans' 26-3 win. Notably, the segment drew jokes about the deal itself, along with amusement at an NFL broadcast borrowing from a video game.

Gardner was inactive with a calf injury after being listed as questionable on Wednesday's final injury report in a loss that dropped Indianapolis to 3-8.

The broadcast paired the graphic with sound effects imitating the game, putting the deal back in front of a national audience more than a year after the Colts made it.

NFL reporter Ari Meirov shared the clip, writing, "Amazon really used a GTA 6 graphic to explain the Colts-Jets Sauce Gardner trade."

One fan decided to take a cheeky little dig, zeroing in on the first two words of the game's title: "Grand theft for sure."

Meanwhile, Amazon also got an editing note from FanDuel's Zach Cohen: "They could've just labeled it 'Grand Theft,' and it would've gotten the point across still."

Gardner had seven passes defended through Week 10 of his first full season in Indianapolis. Pro Football Network's NFL CB Impact Grade has him at 70.8 through Week 11, 78th of 108 graded cornerbacks, after a 76.8 for the full 2025 season.

This fan took Amazon's approach in stride: "Adapting with the times."

Another fan looked at it from the gaming side, pointing to the exposure the game got from an NFL broadcast. "Game gets marketed without even trying."

The New York Jets had drafted Gardner fourth overall in 2022, and he was the NFL's Defensive Rookie of the Year that season and a first-team All-Pro in both 2022 and 2023.

Indianapolis was 7-2 when it struck at the 2025 trade deadline, sending its 2026 and 2027 first-round picks and wide receiver Adonai Mitchell, its second-round pick in 2024, to the Jets on Nov. 4, 2025.

He brought 46 passes defended, three interceptions and 201 tackles in 55 career games to Indianapolis, while Mitchell had 32 catches for 464 yards and no touchdowns in 25 games as a Colt. The 2027 first-round pick Indianapolis sent to New York will be slotted by how the 3-8 Colts finish the season.

With Gardner sidelined and the Colts sitting third in the AFC South, the GTA 6-inspired graphic gave fans another reason to revisit a blockbuster trade that has yet to deliver the results Indianapolis hoped for.
