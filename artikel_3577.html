'DWTS' Runner-Up Amber Glenn Breaks Silence After Emotional Finale

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Dancing With the Stars season 35 cast left it all on the ballroom floor one last time — including runners-up Amber Glenn and Pasha Pashkov.

“It really was, like, a very triumphant feeling for me,” Glenn, 27, told Extra after the finale on Tuesday, November 24, reflecting on coming in second place to Ezra Frech and Daniella Karagach. “I couldn’t ask for a better result.”

Glenn and Pashkov, 40, performed a judges’ choice dance, an instant dance and the fan-favorite freestyle, earning perfect 30s from judges Carrie Ann Inaba, Bruno Tonioli and Derek Hough for all three performances. At the end of the night, the pair were tied on the leaderboard with Frech, 21, and Karagach, 33, as well as fellow finalists Maura Higgins and Mark Ballas.

Though she didn’t take home the mirrorball trophy herself, Glenn was proud that the victory stayed in the family for Pashkov and Karagach.

“From day one, we all met up, had a little dinner over at their house and we said, ‘We wanna go 1-2,'” she said. “And we didn’t specify, like, whatever happens, happens, but the fact that we came in here and we both got those perfect scores. We both did everything we could and it was truly in the hands of the people at home. I’m really proud of what we did and I’m really, really happy that Team USA took it home.”

In a separate interview with Variety, Glenn opened up about the biggest lessons she’s learned from her DWTS experience.

“Being able to work so closely with someone else, being able to trust. We have tour, I’m excited to keep dancing and keep learning. This is a part of my life and really a highlight of my life,” she gushed.

While the intense — and at times, scandalous — season has come to a close, several of the celebrity contestants have spoken about maintaining their close bonds outside of the show.

“I’ll miss getting to see everyone at the rehearsal studio. I’ll miss getting lunch with Dani after practice,” Frech told Cosmopolitan in an interview published on Monday, November 23. “I’ll miss getting the opportunity to commit myself to something like this so entirely. And I’m gonna miss the show days.”

He continued, “You become so close to everybody, and you spend so much time around these people, and then all of a sudden, it’s going to end. But the good thing is that these people are still going to be in my life. I’m still going to hang out with Dani, and I’m still going to see Connor [Wood] whenever I can, and we’ll get lunch with Guillermo [Rodriguez]. These are people I’ll be with forever.”

Wood, for his part, reflected in the same interview about how the reality TV competition experience has felt “like a study abroad trip.”

“Like, I am fully immersed in a culture that is so far outside of my comfort zone and so far away from what I know,” he said. “Learning something new at my age is so rare, let alone at this level on TV with a world-class athlete teaching me. And if it does happen, it probably happens to billionaires’ kids who are, like, paying Serena Williams to do private lessons for two years. I think I’m gonna wake up on Wednesday feeling like I walked out of a loud party and shut the door with my ears still ringing. Like, I’m alone. Party is over. It’s time to go home.”

Dancing With the Stars airs on ABC and Disney+ Tuesdays at 8 p.m. ET.
