2026 U17 Super Cup confirmed: Corinthians v Athletico-PR

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

2026 U-17 Super Cup matchup confirmed: Corinthians vs. Athletico-PRCredits: Rafael Ribeiro/CBF and Luis Miguel Ferreira/athletico.com,br

Corinthians won the Brazilian U-17 Championship on Tuesday (3) and qualified for the 2026 U-17 Super Cup. In the competition bringing together this year's two national champions in the age group, Timão will face Athletico-PR, winners of the U-17 Copa do Brasil.

Both teams will compete for this title for the first time, having secured their places with historic first-time triumphs.

On Tuesday night, Corinthians lost 3–1 at Arena Independência in Belo Horizonte but secured the title with a 4–3 aggregate victory, having won the first leg 3–0.

The U-17 Super Cup trophy; the competition returned to Brazil's men's youth football calendar in 2026Credits: Lucas Figueiredo/CBF

Furacão, meanwhile, lifted the U-17 Copa do Brasil trophy in May. In the final, they beat Atlético-MG 4–3 on penalties at Arena MRV, also in Belo Horizonte, following a 2–2 draw in regulation time.

The U-17 Super Cup will be decided in a single match. Like the U-20 Super Cup, it had not been held for five years. Its three editions took place between 2019 and 2021. In the first, Copa do Brasil winners Palmeiras defeated Brazilian Championship winners Flamengo 4–3 on aggregate.

In 2020, the clash between the two tricolor clubs, São Paulo and Fluminense, ended in victory for the São Paulo side. The teams drew 1–1 after 90 minutes, but São Paulo, the Copa do Brasil champions, won 4–2 on penalties. Finally, in 2021, Flamengo were automatically crowned champions after winning both the Brazilian Championship and the Copa do Brasil in the age group.
