Open Thread: Harrison Barnes creates a memeworthy moment

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Late in last night's Spurs game, frustrations began to get the better of the Utah Jazz. Lauri Markkanen was the team's only player in double figures. Everyone else seemed to be struggling through the drubbing the Spurs handed them on the way to a 119-97 victory.

With just over nine minutes left in regulation, Utah's Brice Sensabaugh and Harrison Barnes got tangled. When they broke free, Barnes found himself sliding across the floor. In what quickly went viral, Barnes stared down Sensabaugh. The moment was played back in slow motion while the play was reviewed for a Flagrant 1.

There were already over as hundred and forty thousand views in the little over an hour since the incident took place.

Sensabaugh was eventually assessed the Flagrant 1.
