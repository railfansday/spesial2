With no Rams game, who are you rooting for?

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

While it has been another gripping week for Los Angeles Rams fans, the Week 11 bye offers both the team and its fans a brief respite until Thanksgiving Eve potboiler. That's when the Green Bay Packers take to steed from the frozen tundra of the North, marauding west into the City of Angels. How L.A. handles this battle will be the harbinger for a stretch of games, with all games bigger than the previous.

There could be nothing better than the Rams gaining ground in the playoff race while on a day off. There are three games this Sunday that have some meaning to L.A. fans. Get down on the rail and root them home.

Here's how to follow these three important games.

Location: Soldier Field in Chicago, Illinois

Sideline reporters: Erin Andrews and Tom Rinaldi

To keep it simple, Rams fans can clearly root against the NFC North every week over the final eight weeks. The Bears, Lions, Packers, and Vikings are all within reach and surmountable. This one's a long shot. The weather will be rather mild for this time of year, 58 degrees, with morning rain that may linger after kickoff.

A wet track favors Chicago, they lead the NFL in run attempts and rushing yards per game, while New Orleans languishes in the Bottom 10 defending the ground attack. Although two the Saints three wins have come on the road, their offense is plodding, particularly the run game. It won't help that three starting offensive linemen are out.

Location: Ford Field in Detroit, Michigan

The Lions are 5-0 at thus far and have already banked victories over division rivals Minnesota and Detroit. They could get caught looking past the Bucs, with their eyes on a big Week 12 home meeting with the Chicago Bears. All six of Tampa Bay's losses have come by seven points or less.

The Buccaneers have one the top rushing offenses in the league, but rank at the bottom in passing. They like to to make full use of the clock, most seconds per play (2nd), time of possession (3rd), and time of possession percentage (3rd). Detroit has fairly balanced offense (55% pass), but likes the chunk plays, they land fourth in explosive plays.

Location: Estadio Banorte in Mexico City, Mexico

No matter how this game falls, the Rams gain ground on a squad ahead of them. I get it, rooting for the 'Niners is like a snake eating a porcupine, only once a lifetime and you have to be in dire straits. West coast rivalry aside, the Vikes have a fairly soft schedule after Sunday. It seems prudent to root for them to beat up on San Francisco and then later on their division rivals, Detroit and Chicago.

San Francisco has been an offensive machine so far and trending up, 400 yards or better and 33 points per game over three straight outings. The defense is salty against the run, but is below the midline stopping the pass. Minnesota's blitzing defense had carried the team and its lack of competent quarterback play. Now that Kirk Cousins is at the throttle, the offense might be able to pull its own weight.
