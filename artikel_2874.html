Tom Brady admits Kirk Cousins pulled off something he never did in NFL career

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Las Vegas Raiders quarterback Kirk Cousins' strange elbow-centric celebration after scoring a rushing touchdown against the New York Jets got a lot of attention.

Cousins crouched and did an elbow flap that looked like he was trying to do a dance move while pretending his arm was a chicken wing.

The celebration made a lot more sense when he explained it after the Raiders' win.

Las Vegas Raiders quarterback Kirk Cousins throws against the New York Jets during an NFL football game in East Rutherford, N.J., Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026. AP Photo/Frank Franklin II

"I call that my wellness celebration. You know, we do a lot of tissue work here. That's one of the main techniques they do," Cousins said of his celebration.

"So I said, 'If I run a touchdown in, I'm gonna give a shoutout to all the staff that work so hard to help us feel good,'" he added before admitting that Brock Bowers had to remind him to do the celebration because he was about to forget.

The NFL's Instagram account posted a clip of Cousins saying this along with him doing the celebration on Sunday.
