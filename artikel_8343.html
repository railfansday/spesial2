Tanggal: 2026-11-25

I am so sorry to hear of your situation; it's a lot.

I've had a friend pass (for whom I was her Executor) and I managed a lot of the details when my FIL passed in 20224. These are the things to take care of now:

- Make sure his life insurance clearly notes you as his beneficiary.

- Make sure that all bank accounts have joint ownership and clear beneficiary designations.

- Make sure his IRAs and 401(k)s have clear beneficiary designations. I've seen prior designations lapse when the HR systems are updated at work, so it's worth checking.

- Make sure you have at least 2 credit cards in your name only.

- Make sure you have his password manager info, and a list of passwords for important accounts: social media, memberships, etc.

- Make sure that you are listed on all utility accounts.

- Make sure you have a will, health care proxy, and HIPAA release for him in your records. Also - whatever his Do Not Resuscitate desires are, as well as medical donor information on file.

- Make sure any vehicles are titled as His OR Her Name on the title. AAA will help you with title changes as a membership benefit. Totally worth doing, as any physical property not in joint accounts could trigger probate.

- Make sure you have copies of all life insurance policies. My FILs was found in a box of old work papers. If we hadn't sorted all of his paper carefully, MIL would have missed out on $60k.

- Make sure you have a copy of his birth certificate, and your marriage certificate. You'll need this for any Social Security claims for your son.

Sending strength and courage - this is hard work, but you have an opportunity to handle business with some runway, which is a blessing.
