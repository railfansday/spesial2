Savvas Learning Company Named to the 2027 GSV 150

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

For the fifth year in a row, Savvas is recognized among the world's most transformational growth companies in learning and workforce skills

PARAMUS, N.J., Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Savvas Learning Company, a next-generation K-12 learning solutions leader, is proud to announce that it has been named to the 2027 GSV 150, earning recognition as one of the world's most transformational growth companies in learning and workforce skills. This is the fifth year in a row that Savvas has been named to the GSV 150.

At Savvas, our instructional philosophy is built upon delivering flexible educational resources from pre-K-12 to workforce learning.

"At Savvas, our instructional philosophy is built upon delivering flexible educational resources, from pre-K-12 to workforce learning, that empower teachers and improve student outcomes," said Bethlam Forsa, CEO of Savvas Learning Company. "Savvas is honored to be recognized on the GSV 150 as it demonstrates our commitment to using technology to advance personalized learning and data-driven instruction."

GSV is a global community and investment platform focused on elevating the scope and scale of innovation in the education and workforce skills sector. The GSV 150 recognizes category leaders building at scale, expanding access, and creating new possibilities for how people learn, grow, and thrive.

Savvas is a longtime leader in providing the highest quality print and digital instructional materials that meet the needs of today's pre-K-12 classrooms. Its portfolio of engaging, rigorous, standards-aligned learning solutions span all grade levels and disciplines, including award-winning math, literacy, science, and social studies programs, all accessible on the Realize digital platform. The company takes an intentional and innovative approach towards utilizing advanced technologies and developing AI-powered tools that support educators and promote student learning in a protected, safe environment.

Savvas is also helping to prepare students for both post-secondary education and career success with Savvas PathMaker, an all-in-one digital college and career readiness solution. Designed to offer flexibility for educators and choice for students, Savvas PathMaker delivers a comprehensive catalog of Outlier by Savvas dual enrollment and Savvas CTE career-focused courses so high school students can earn college credit, job-ready skills, and industry-recognized certifications before they graduate.

"At a time when the possibilities for learning and work are expanding rapidly, we're seeing companies break down traditional boundaries, bring together complementary capabilities, and find new ways to create meaningful opportunities," said Lucy Baldwin, vice president, product, GSV Summit. "As we consider what the world needs now, this year's GSV 150 demonstrates the value of bringing different ideas, expertise, and capabilities together to address the evolving needs of learners, educators, and employers."

Visit GSV 150 for the full list of 2027 winners.
