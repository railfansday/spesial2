Transfer Capital One Miles To Air France-KLM Flying Blue With 20% Bonus: Worth It?

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Capital One has just introduced its latest points transfer promotion, whereby members can receive a 20% bonus when moving rewards to Air France-KLM Flying Blue. This has the potential to be an excellent deal.

Capital One is offering a bonus when you transfer points to Air France-KLM Flying Blue. Between 12AM ET on November 1, 2026, and 11:59PM ET on November 30, 2026, members can receive a 20% bonus when moving points.

The transfer ratio is usually 1:1, so through this promotion you can transfer miles at a 1,000:1,200 ratio. The 20% bonus should post at the same time as the standard rewards. There’s no registration required, and no limit to how many bonus points you can earn. Transfers from Capital One to Flying Blue are generally instant.

We see bonuses from various transferable points currencies to Flying Blue every so often, in the range of 20-30%. So if you’re in the market for earning some Flying Blue miles, this is a good opportunity.

Air France-KLM Flying Blue is a valuable SkyTeam frequent flyer program. The program has attractive award pricing on Air France and KLM, offering many of the best ways to cross the Atlantic. Admittedly saver award availability can be tough to come by, though if you have Flying Blue Platinum status, you get access to more award space, which is an awesome perk.

On top of that, Air France and KLM make more award seats available to members of the Flying Blue program than to members of partner programs. So even when Flying Blue’s pricing isn’t the best, there are situations where it could make sense to book through Flying Blue (though with all of the Delta SkyMiles devaluations we’ve seen, typically you will find the best pricing through Flying Blue).

I should mention that in September 2026 we saw a Flying Blue devaluation, whereby awards now followed a tiered pricing structure. This means that the cheapest awards are now “Light” fares, and if you want the same inclusions as before, you need to buy up to a “Standard” fare, which costs more miles.

There’s always value in having a stash of Flying Blue miles if you think you’ll be traveling transatlantic in the coming months. I redeem through Flying Blue every year (usually multiple times per year), and consistently get value. I find it to be one of the most useful frequent flyer programs for redeeming points.

The only other consideration here is that all major transferable points currencies partner with Air France-KLM Flying Blue, and we see transfer bonuses with some frequency. So I’d be strategic with which program you transfer points from, especially based on how you’d otherwise redeem them.

Through November 30, 2026, you can receive a 20% bonus when you transfer miles from Capital One to Air France-KLM Flying Blue. This bonus is being offered on Capital One’s end, so you should see the bonus right away, and can take advantage of this as often as you’d like.

This has the potential to be a good deal for redemptions on Air France-KLM as well as SkyTeam and other airline partners. If I didn’t already have a stash of Flying Blue miles, I’d take advantage of this.
