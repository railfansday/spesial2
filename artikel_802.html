Penn & Teller Join Short Film ‘The Inspector’ as “Executive Magic Producers” (Exclusive)

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Spanish short is written and directed by Jandro, one of Spain's biggest magician acts.

Penn & Teller have pulled off their newest trick, coming on board Spanish short film The Inspector as its “executive magic producers.”

The move puts the pair in league with Spanish magician, filmmaker and television personality Jandro, who wrote, directed, and produced the dark comedy thriller, which is seeking to become an Oscar contender this awards season. The parties already worked together when Janto appeared on pair’s magic competition series Fool Us.

Inspector, set in Spain, tells the story of a fraudulent gas inspector who targets vulnerable elderly people, exploiting their trust to gain access to their homes and steal their valuables. His next victim is Carmen, a sweet grandmother with a collection of valuable antiques. He soon gets more than he bargained for. The 12-minute, genre-bending short film combines thriller, mystery and dark comedy.

“Jandro, like Hitchcock, understands cinematic sleight of hand. The Inspector is my kind of movie,” said Teller in a statement. “If you like Penn & Teller, you’ll love The Inspector. It’s tricky.”

Jandro is one of Spain’s most well-known magicians and one of the most recognizable faces in Spanish television, having spent 15 years as a writer and performer on the popular Spanish late-night show El Hormiguero. He earned fame in magic circles by hoodwinking Penn & teller as part of their Fool Us competition series. He won that contest six times. He also won the award for Spanish Champion of Comedy Magic in his home country.

Inspector has already been making its own magic on the festival circuit. The short has played in roughly 200 festivals, winning 50 awards across 22 countries. The film has played at four Oscar qualifying festivals including Sitges in Spain, ShortShorts in Japan, Ireland’s Foyle, and Edmonton in Canada. It is now vying for an Oscar nom.
