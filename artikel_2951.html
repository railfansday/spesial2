LeBron James And Joel Embiid Reflect On 76ers’ Struggles After Blowout Loss In NBA Cup vs. Cavaliers

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Philadelphia 76ers were blown out 95-119 by the Cleveland Cavaliers tonight after an abysmal team performance that saw two of their star players have historically bad games.

LeBron James recorded only his ninth career game with eight points or fewer, finishing with eight points, seven rebounds, three assists, and two steals while shooting 3-12 from the field (25.0% FG) and missing all four of his three-point shots.

Following the game, LeBron James spoke to the media and reflected on a rare off night for the 76ers' veteran superstar.

"I definitely did not feel in a good rhythm offensively; we didn't look like much offensively, and I think we just had some spurts where we were able to cut it to three in the third quarter. But too many turnovers on my behalf. And I didn't feel like I was in a good rhythm offensively at all."

LeBron James had six of the 76ers' 15 turnovers tonight. Subsequently, James was asked whether he believed the turnovers were due to communication issues or whether he takes accountability for the mental errors on his own part.

"A bit of both; a couple of them were just careless, and a couple were just me trying to make the right play and not being successful at it," LeBron James responded.

"Every game has its own challenges. Offensively, as a group, our offense was not good at all, and I know I damn sure wasn't good at all. I'm looking at the film and trying to get better from there," James concluded.

This game ended a 396-game streak in Cleveland in which LeBron James scored at least 10 points. Meanwhile, tonight, Joel Embiid also had only the second game of his career in which he did not make a single field goal.

He finished with six points, seven rebounds, one assist, and two blocks, scoring all his points from the free-throw line and missing all nine field-goal attempts. Even Joel Embiid candidly reflected on his struggles in the postgame media scrum in the locker room.

"I believe in myself, but when you're in these moments, and nothing is going in, whether it's the three, the midrange, the layups, everything, it's hard not to look at yourself and be down, but we've gotta keep going and still stay up to keep working," Joel Embiid said.

The 76ers fell to 8-2 tonight, suffering only their second defeat of the season. It is easy to hang your head after a loss like that, but the right thing to do, as Embiid correctly said, is to pick yourself up, dust yourself off, and keep going.

While this loss may be a major setback in the NBA Cup for the 76ers, it is just a regular-season loss if winning the NBA Cup is not the 76ers' priority. But it should open their eyes to the fact that not everyone can perform at their best every night, and the team's aging veterans might need more leeway than their younger stars.

Ultimately, the 76ers must treat this defeat as a learning experience, not a reason to panic. With an 8-2 record, they have plenty of time to regroup, address their offensive struggles, and ensure their veteran stars get the support they need to bounce back.

They now face the Bulls on Sunday night, hoping this isn't the start of an extended slump for LeBron James and Joel Embiid.
