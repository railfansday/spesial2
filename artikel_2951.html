Apple Has a Software Problem + Nvidia’s CEO Crisis with Joanna Stern

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

In this episode of Power On, Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman is joined by technology journalist and reviewer Joanna Stern of The New Things and NBC News for a critical look at Apple’s software leadership and its struggles to compete in artificial intelligence. They discuss whether software chief Craig Federighi’s management style and Apple’s rigid development processes are holding the company back, why Siri continues to fail at basic tasks and whether Apple needs to start over with a new AI assistant. They also examine how Apple’s AI shortcomings have delayed major hardware products. Plus, their experiences with the iPhone Duo, its new face-unlock feature, Joanna’s HomeView review and whether Apple’s Johny Srouji could be a candidate to lead Nvidia following its leadership crisis.
