'Law & Order' Alum E.J. Carroll's Cause of Death Revealed After Collapse

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Law & Order alum E.J. Carroll’s cause of death has been revealed after he collapsed in the streets of New York City on his way to a voiceover job.

Carroll died of a pulmonary embolism, his manager, Jon Mason, confirmed to Us. He was 66.

Us understands that Carroll recently underwent major surgery to correct a hernia issue. He had taken great lengths to improve his health before his death, including weight loss throughout his career.

Carroll was recovering from his surgery while on his way to a voiceover gig to add dialogue to a commercial he recently filmed with New York Knicks player Jalen Brunson and actress Quinta Brunson. He collapsed on the street en route to the job on Thursday, November 19.

A memorial service will be held in NYC on December 5.

Mason confirmed Carroll’s death in a statement shared on Saturday, November 21.

“When E.J. Carroll performed, it was impossible to take your eyes off him,” he said. “But his lasting legacy will be the kindness and generosity he showed his friends, fellow performers and even strangers.”

Mason added that Carroll was “as good a human being as it gets.”

Carroll’s close friend Jayce Bartok remembered the actor in a statement to Us after working together on the upcoming film A Delicate Arrangement.

“E.J. was my closest friend for the past 20 years. I met him on the set of a film I wrote, The Cake Eaters starring Kristen Stewart and Bruce Dern. I was preparing for a very dramatic scene, and E.J. and I shared a van. He was so sweet and genuine, he became connected to my family and professional life,” Bartok, 51, recalled.

The filmmaker continued, “We ended up working on numerous films together. He was an essential part of getting my film A Delicate Arrangement made, which is about a love affair between Vivien Leigh (played by Carla Gugino) and a local florist (Matthew Modine). He worked with Carla Gugino and David Hyde Pierce on the film, who both adored him, as anyone who seemed to work with him did.”

Bartok concluded, “His belief in the material allowed us to raise our post-production funding, and he refused to take any credit for this. He was a man of deep faith and charity, and spent every Thanksgiving serving the homeless. Ultimately, his passing on the way to an acting job personifies his commitment to his craft. It was his happiest place working, working with other actors, and supporting stories he wanted to tell.”

In her own statement, Gugino, 55, shared, “I had the great fortune of working with E.J. last winter in Philadelphia. He was a source of talent and light. My deepest condolences to his family and loved ones. RIP E.J. 🤍 🕊️.”

Carroll was known for his roles in Law & Order, Boardwalk Empire and The Deuce.
