Personal Investments • Re: 'Hands-On' Hourly Advisors

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Does anyone know an hourly (or subscription-based) advisor who will not only give financial advice, but also provide plenty of "hands-on" help with carrying those recommendations out? I ask because an elderly friend of mine has a three-fund indexing portfolio, but would like to find an affordable planner to check in with on a recurring basis. Since he's a senior, however, he feels a need for someone who can be very proactive in terms of helping guide him through making the actual transactions at Schwab for carrying out any recommendations. Those might include reminders to take his annual RMDs and/or reviewing his annual rebalancing, if necessary.
First, I want to congratulate your friend on realizing he's at a stage where he may need some extra help. I'm not sure how "elderly" he is, but I have several friends now who are in their early 80s and struggling with things they used to find easy, but are stubborn about hiring help. It is frustrating.

Along those lines, I wonder if your friend might further consider going one step further at this point, and just move his 3-fund portfolio into an all-in-one balanced fund? An elderly relative of mine has made this transition, and once she got used to it, has been very happy with the hands-off approach. That would cut way down on the number of things that would require consultation with -- and an hourly fee charged by -- a financial advisor.

The firm that I work with and highly recommend, Valor Wealth Partners, does have two subscription models available as part of their ongoing planning services: https://www.valorwealthpartners.com/ongoing-planning If your friend is willing to simplify his portfolio in the manner I suggested above, then the "membership" level at $500 per year would probably suit him well. That includes an annual review meeting and access to planning software, with any additional services priced on an hourly basis. While ongoing planning services usually require one-time purchase of an initial financial plan, per their FAQ section they are sometimes able to waive that requirement: https://www.valorwealthpartners.com/cop ... iduciaries. They also have a more expensive "partnership" subscription that includes unlimited meetings, but I suspect that would be more than your friend strictly needs, unless what he is really looking for is not so much discrete assistance but rather the interaction that comes with ongoing consultations. AUM is not required for either subscription, but if elected, the fee for ongoing planning clients is a very low .5%.

VWP specializes in federal and military employees but has non-federal clients as well, including a significant number of seniors. (I know because I've met them at client functions). The staff is comprised of warm, conscientious individuals that go the extra mile for their clients, and might be a good fit for your friend. They are based in Boston and Washington, DC but work with remote clients across the country.
