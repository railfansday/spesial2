Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Re: Telling our children about your net worth - pros and cons?

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

What is the real purpose of telling your kids how much you are worth ?

Is it to impress on them that good saving habits and investing will lead to a good outcome?

Is it to alleviate some sort of fear they might have , to know that they will get a big pot of gold when you move on?

Is it for some sort of arrangement to transfer assets to them, to keep your assets away from the government taxation ?

I never knew how much my parents had. I always assumed they were poor by the way they spent money. But little did I know they were sitting on a gold mine. I dont think I would have done anything different if I knew this along the way. Similarly, I am not sure what advantage or disadvantage to having this discussion with my kids. My kids are in their late 20's - early 30s.
- teach them as many financial lessons as possible
- help them coordinate their best investment plan
- longer term perparation for a transition plan
- let them take larger risks then they may have otherwise
