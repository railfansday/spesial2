EU leaders seek agreement on long-term budget and Ukraine at summit

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Leaders of the EU's 27 member states are expected to thrash out the EU's budget for 2028 to 2034. Ukraine and European Central Bank leadership are also up for discussion. DW has more.

You can follow developments from the EU summit taking place on Thursday 26th and Friday 27th November below:

The Euro Summit will also deal with the process of appointing a new president and two executive board members of the European Central Bank (ECB).

Christine Lagarde has been president since November 2019 but has announced her intention to step down on February 7, 2027.

Eurozone leaders are expected to back Spanish candidate Pablo Hernandez de Cos who is the current head of the Bank for International Settlements.

He's up against Dutch central bank chief Klaas Knot for the presidency, while France's Laurence Boone and Germany's Markus Brunnermeier have been nominated for the ECB's two other top jobs.

European leaders are meeting for the next two days to thrash out a nearly €1.6 trillion ($1.76 trillion) budget proposal for 2028 to 2034.

The 27 member states are hoping to find common ground for a new budget proposal in the coming weeks and for members to agree on it at a summit on December 17 and 18.

"We, collectively, have the responsibility to find a balanced agreement that will allow the European Union to take on the challenges ahead: boosting our competitiveness, strengthening our security and defence and projecting Europe in the world," said President of the European Council Antonio Costa ahead of the talks.

Revenue would come from digital services, non-recycled e-waste, tobacco related products, the carbon border adjustment mechanism and the emissions trading system, according to a statement from the European Council.

Ukraine's financing needs for 2027 and beyond will also be dealt with during talks. Currently, €45 billion has been earmarked in additional financial support for Ukraine in 2027, building on the EU's €90 billion Ukraine Support Loan for 2026 and 2027.

It's not expected to be a smooth process with Reuters quoting a diplomat as expecting "leaders to be yelling, complaining, showing they are willing to fight. They need that ​first. They need a breakdown, blood on the walls, before they can get to an agreement."

Welcome to DW's coverage of the two-day Special European Council meeting in Brussels, which is expected to focus on the EU’s next long-term budget, support for Ukraine and then the leadership of the European Central Bank (ECB).

Leaders of the bloc's 27 members states will be looking to find common ground over the budget proposal for 2028 to 2034 ahead of  an expected deal in December.

Continued support for Ukraine in 2027 and beyond is up for discussion as Russia's invasion drags on towards a fifth year.

The question of who replaces European Central Bank chief Christine Lagarde is also on the agenda.
