Voyager 1 spacecraft reaches one light-day milestone

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

NASA's Voyager 1 probe, which has been traveling through space for nearly 50 years, hit a milestone distance of one light-day away from Earth on Wednesday. Already the most distant man-made object in the universe, the spacecraft is now about 26 billion kilometers (16 billion miles) away, according to the US space agency. That is the distance a particle of light can travel in 24 hours. FRANCE 24's Emerald Maxwell reports.
