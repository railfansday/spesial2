Bears vs. Lions Injury Report: Chicago has 3 DNP, and Detroit has 4

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

With the game on Thanksgiving, the Chicago Bears and Detroit Lions issued their first injury reports of the week on Monday, but since both teams played on Sunday, the reports were estimates after each team only held a walk-through.

The Bears listed three players as DNP (did not practice): starting left tackle Theo Benedet (quadricep), nickleback Kyler Gordon (shoulder), and running back Roschon Johnson (ankle)

Benedet left Sunday's win over the New Orleans Saints, but returned after missing just one snap. Johnson was carried off with his injury and did not return.

On Monday, the Chicago Tribune's Brad Biggs wrote that Gordon may be out a little while longer with a separated shoulder, but it shouldn't be long-term. After the Lions game, the Bears host the Jacksonville Jaguars, then are at the Miami Dolphins.

The only other Bears player on the injury report is linebacker D'Marco Jackson (elbow), but he was listed as a full participant.

The Lions had 4 DNPs: safety Chuck Clark (hamstring), defensive tackle Skyler Gill-Howard (knee), corner Ennis Rakestraw Jr. (shoulder), and defensive end DJ Wonnum (back). Clark and Wonnum are starters on Detroit's defense. Corners Khalil Dorsey (wrist) and D.J. Reed (hip) were both listed as full, and tight end Sam LaPorta (ankle) was limited.
