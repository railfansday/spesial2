Lucky break? Bruno Fernandes scores bizarre Man United goal thanks to divot

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Bruno Fernandes scored a bizarre goal for Manchester United against former club Sporting as his long-range shot took a fortuitous bobble off the Estádio José Alvalade pitch.

Moments after the United captain levelled against Sporting from the penalty spot, Fernandes saw his low strike beat goalkeeper Kaique Pereira after what appeared to be a deflection.

But the assist went to a divot in the pitch, as the shot from Fernandes struck the turf and diverted away from the goalkeeper Pereira to find the net.

It was perhaps the lucky break new United manager Mauricio Pochettino needed after a chaotic first half where several decisions went against his team.

United conceded two penalties and had an equaliser from Amad Diallo controversially disallowed by the referee Szymon Marciniak.

It also came after Leny Yoro was ruled to have been wrongly sent off in the eighth minute of United's 4-0 defeat at Liverpool on Sunday in Pochettino's first match.
