4 Dates That Could Move the Stock Market in Epic Fashion This Week. Here's What Investors Need to Know.

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Several market-moving events are converging on Wall Street this week.

Wall Street is heading into one of the most consequential trading weeks of 2026, with several major events capable of influencing investor sentiment converging at once. Between Nov. 17 and Nov. 20, investors will receive Nvidia's (NVDA -1.57%) fiscal third-quarter earnings report, the Federal Reserve's October meeting minutes, and the initial public offering (IPO) of artificial intelligence (AI) developer Anthropic. Meanwhile, memory manufacturer Sandisk (SNDK -1.88%) is preparing to execute an 8-for-1 stock split that could attract attention from retail investors.

These events arrive during a particularly uncertain period for the stock market. Concerns around AI valuations, interest rate policy, political uncertainty, and corporate leadership shakeups have introduced unusual levels of volatility into an otherwise multiyear bull market.

Let's take a look at five important events taking place this week and what investors should be watching.

Nvidia is scheduled to report earnings after the market closes on Tuesday, Nov. 17. The company guided for revenue of $108 billion, representing roughly 89% growth year over year.

Ordinarily, investors would be focusing on whether Nvidia exceeds this forecast and provides encouraging guidance for the upcoming quarter. However, this earnings announcement comes under much different circumstances following Jensen Huang's unexpected resignation as CEO. As a result, another quarter of robust financial growth will likely take a back seat to questions surrounding Nvidia's leadership transition.

Huang played an instrumental role in transforming Nvidia into the dominant supplier of AI accelerators, making his departure a meaningful source of uncertainty for investors. During the earnings call, my hunch is that analysts will be trying to get some clarity around the company's succession plans and its ability to maintain competitive advantages without its former leader.

Anthropic is expected to finalize the pricing of its IPO on Nov. 18. The start-up behind the Claude chatbot is indicating an offering price in the range of $70 to $78, potentially raising around $64 billion at the upper end. Such a debut would rank among the largest IPOs in stock market history, making it an important test of demand for AI investments.

Anthropic is subsequently expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq Thursday, Nov. 19. The offering could generate widespread enthusiasm throughout the AI ecosystem, particularly among companies with financial and commercial relationships with the developer. Amazon, Alphabet, and Space Exploration Technologies are among the companies investors could scrutinize as Anthropic's valuation and infrastructure spending commitments receive greater attention.

Meanwhile, the Federal Reserve is scheduled to release minutes from its October monetary policy meeting at 2 p.m. ET on Nov. 18. Smart investors will look for clues about whether the Fed is considering another interest rate hike before the end of the year. Any indication that interest rates could remain elevated might pressure growth stocks, whose valuations are highly sensitive to changes in borrowing costs.

The week's final major event involves Sandisk, which is preparing to execute an 8-for-1 stock split. The transaction follows a period of extreme volatility for the NAND flash specialist, whose stock has attracted meaningful attention amid surging demand for memory and storage solutions.

Although stock splits do not change a company's underlying valuation, they can still influence investor psychology and trading activity. By reducing the price of individual shares in terms of absolute dollars, Sandisk is making shares more accessible to investors who prefer purchasing whole shares rather than fractional shares.

Ultimately, the combination of Nvidia's earnings, Anthropic's IPO, the Federal Reserve's meeting minutes, and Sandisk's stock split makes this an unusually eventful week for Wall Street. Investors should pay attention to any comments around Nvidia's leadership outlook, Anthropic's reception among institutional investors, and any changes in the Fed's assessment of inflation and interest rates. While these developments will likely fuel short-term volatility, their longer-term implications will depend on how they affect earnings and confidence in the broader economy.
