Colts offensive lineman questionable to return with injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Indianapolis Colts' left tackle Bernhard Raimann is being evaluated for a concussion.

He is questionable to return against the Miami Dolphins.

In his place at left tackle is Luke Tenuta. The Colts are already without Jalen Travis, who is inactive with a knee injury.

Matt Goncalves is currently starting at right tackle, with Dalton Tucker at right guard.

This season, Raimann has allowed three sacks and 20 QB pressures over 356 pass-blocking snaps. He has a run-blocking grade of 69.5.

We will update this article as new reports come in.
