Big Ten now one-bid league after latest CFP rankings as UCLA, Indiana plummet

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Big Ten would be a one-bid league if the College Football Playoff opened this week, the result of upset losses by UCLA and Indiana that sent them tumbling down the rankings released Tuesday night.

The Bruins fell five spots to No. 9 and the defending NCAA champion Hoosiers dropped six spots to No. 14 — behind conference foes No. 12 Oregon and No. 13 Iowa, who would also be on the outside looking in.

It means the league that has produced the past three national champions would be relegated to a single spot in the 12-team playoff if it began this week.

It also puts more focus on who will play in the conference title game in two weeks, where the Big Ten's automatic — and maybe only — spot in the playoff will be won.

By those standards, the Southeastern Conference's troubles looked quaint.

Yes, Alabama fell three spots to No. 6 — based on a hit it took in strength of schedule after beating Chattanooga of the FCS, along with some other results that dampened its resume.

Curt Cignetti's Indiana team would not make the College Football Playoffs if the season ended today, according to the latest rankings. Getty Images

But that just reshuffled things — allowing Tennessee, with an identical 9-2 record, to finally move into the same four-team "pod" with Alabama, which, under the committee's "principles," allows it to place the teams side by side and consider Tennessee's 26-10 win over the Crimson Tide five weeks ago.

With that in the mix, the Volunteers jumped four spots to No. 5.

Georgia and Utah stayed steady in the top two spots, followed by Texas, which moved up two notches and would give the SEC four teams — two with byes — in the projected 12-team bracket.

The Big 12 would have three: BYU stayed steady at No. 10 and Texas Tech moved off the bubble into the No. 11 spot, with the 12th spot going to unranked Boise State — deemed the best in the Group of Six.

No. 4 Notre Dame and No. 7 Miami and No. 8 Wake Forest of the Atlantic Coast Conference rounded out the top 12.
