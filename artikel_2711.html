Eagles reportedly bring Jeff Stoutland assistant back to Philly

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Philadelphia Eagles are not bringing Jeff Stoutland back on their coaching staff.

But they are bringing back one of his old assistants!

The Eagles are re-hiring two-time Super Bowl champion T.J. Paganetti, their former run game specialist/assistant offensive line coach, in a consultant role for the rest of the 2026 season, according to a report from NFL insider Adam Schefter. (The news was first teased by T.J.'s brother and fellow former Eagles assistant, Ryan, on social media.)

T.J. Paganetti left the Eagles after the 2024 season to follow Kellen Moore to the New Orleans Saints as their run game coordinator. Here's what we wrote back at the time:

After beginning his coaching career at Oregon, Paganetti joined the Eagles as part of Chip Kelly's original staff in 2013. A snapshot of his coaching experience since then:

2013-2014 — Eagles analyst (offense and quarterbacks)2015-2016 — Oregon Graduate assistant/linebackers coach2017-2018 — Eagles offensive quality control/assistant offensive line coach2019-2020 — Eagles assistant running backs coach2021-2022 — Eagles offensive quality control coach2023 — Eagles run game specialist/assistant tight ends coach2024 — Eagles run game specialist/assistant offensive line coach

Paganetti, a two-time Super Bowl champion, clearly has experience working with legendary Eagles offensive line coach Jeff Stoutland. With the Saints unable to hire Stout, they went for one of his proteges instead.

Now it's the Eagles going for a Stoutland protege instead of Stoutland himself.

Paganetti was fired by the Saints on November 4 due to their run game struggles. It's not like dismissing him has fixed their problems:

How much of an impact will Paganetti have in his return to Philly? That much remains unclear. Chris Kuper is seemingly still in charge of the Eagles' offensive line room, though it seems like Paganetti is now being granted a voice there. I wonder if they might eventually fire Kuper and promote Eagles assistant offensive line coach Greg Austin (another former Stoutland assistant) … and then backfill Austin's role with Paganetti.

Bringing Paganetti back doesn't magically make the Eagles' offensive line significantly better, nor does it make an injured Saquon Barkley healthier. But, hey, no real reason not to give it a shot.

Beyond the scope of this season, which is likely already lost, it's fair to wonder if the Eagles bringing Paganetti back is an olive branch to Stoutland in an effort to make him their offensive line coach again in 2027. That is, assuming he even still has interest in the role after being cast aside by the team this year.

Nevertheless, welcome back, T.J. Paganetti.
