‘It’s Crazy, But Not Impossible,’ ‘Keep Dreaming’ — NFL Fans Have Mixed Reactions to Eagles’ Potential Path to Playoffs After Win vs. Cowboys

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Dallas Cowboys' Thanksgiving loss to the Philadelphia Eagles has given their NFC East rivals a potential route back into the playoff race. Philadelphia erased a 14-point fourth-quarter deficit to secure a 39-38 victory, improving to 4-7 and keeping its postseason hopes alive.

However, the Eagles still need several results to go their way over the remaining weeks of action. The NFC East's playoff picture is certainly an intriguing one, with some fans believing a late-season turnaround is possible for Philadelphia, while others have chosen to dismiss the idea outright.

Philadelphia entered Thanksgiving with a 3-7 record and needed a strong finish to remain in contention. The victory over Dallas gave the Eagles their fourth win of the season, while the Cowboys fell to 5-7 after letting a two-touchdown lead slip away in the final quarter.

The result also changed the conversation around the NFC East race when Associated Press reporter Rob Maaddi outlined one scenario in which Philadelphia could turn its Week 18 visit to the Giants into a winner-take-all divisional showdown.

Maaddi spelled out that the Eagles would need to win four of their final five games. The Giants, who currently sit at 6-3-1, would have to lose four of their remaining six. Washington would also need to finish no better than 3-3, while Dallas could win no more than two of its final four games in this scenario.

The Eagles still have games against the Arizona Cardinals, Indianapolis Colts, Seattle Seahawks, Houston Texans, and San Francisco 49ers. Their final matchup against the Giants could then determine the NFC East winner if the other results fall into place.

The possibility of a late Philadelphia turnaround prompted some fans to look at the remaining schedule with optimism. Although winning four of the final five games would be a difficult task, the Eagles' comeback against Dallas gave supporters another reason to believe the team could make a push.

"It's crazy but not impossible," one user wrote.

Another fan went further, suggesting that an Eagles run could also change the conversation surrounding head coach Nick Sirianni.

"So you're saying there's a chance? Also, how funny would this be if it happens? Sirianni would be deserving of the Coach of the Year award, which would be hilarious given all the grief he's received this year," the fan commented.

Others focused on the importance of Philadelphia winning its upcoming games against strong opponents, particularly Seattle and San Francisco. "Dude, there's actually a chance. Have to beat one of SEA or SF," another user wrote.

One supporter was also ready to embrace the possibility of a late-season run, regardless of how unlikely it appeared at this stage.

"[Expletive] it, let's run the tables and get delusional," the fan added.

Not everyone was convinced that Philadelphia could turn its season around. Some fans felt the team's record and remaining schedule made the proposed playoff scenario too difficult to achieve, despite the comeback victory over Dallas. One user wrote: "Lol won't happen."

Another fan shared a similarly brief reaction,writing, "Keep dreaming." A third user also questioned the likelihood of the scenario becoming reality. "That's not happening, pal," the fan commented.

Others were more concerned about Philadelphia's overall performance throughout the season. One supporter argued that the win against Dallas should not change expectations for the remainder of the campaign, pointing out that the Eagles still have considerable work to do.

"Stop it. We are finishing 2-3 at best. This is what I mean. The Cowboys suck. Now fans think the season ain't over. It's over! It was a great win, but it's over," the fan wrote.

Philadelphia will now look to build on its Thanksgiving victory when it visits the Arizona Cardinals. According to PFN's Impact Metric, the Eagles' offense ranks 32nd in the league in 2026 with a grade of 64.3, while the defense ranks 21st with a 72.1 grade.

The defense has been middling overall, allowing 25.0 points per game (20th) and 5.3 yards per play (18th), but its 0.7% takeaway rate ranks last and its 43.5% score rate allowed ranks 26th. It has been better at getting off the field, with a 22.6% three-and-out rate that ranks ninth, and it has done so against the fourth-toughest schedule in the league.
