Brandon Aiyuk has his way with Christian McCaffrey’s brother in deal after 49ers exit

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Even though he's no longer on the San Francisco 49ers, Brandon Aiyuk still has a McCaffrey as a teammate.

That would be Luke McCaffrey, the Washington Commanders wide receiver who is also 49ers star running back Christian's younger brother.

Washington Commanders wide receiver Luke McCaffrey warms up before an NFL football game against the New York Giants in East Rutherford, N.J., Thursday, Nov. 12th, 2026. AP Photo/Frank Franklin II

Aiyuk wore No. 11 throughout his tenure in San Francisco and clearly wanted to keep that going when he arrived in Washington. But Luke McCaffrey had been wearing that number for the Commanders this season.

The two have solved this matter, as The Athletic's Nicki Jhabvala revealed that Aiyuk and McCaffrey worked out a deal so that Aiyuk could have No. 11.

Brandon Aiyuk of the San Francisco 49ers stands on the field during a game against the Minnesota Vikings at U.S. Bank Stadium in Minneapolis, Sept. 15, 2024. Getty Images

San Francisco 49ers running back Christian McCaffrey runs off the field after an NFL football game against the Dallas Cowboys in Arlington, Texas, Nov. 15, 2026. AP Photo/Jeffrey McWhorter

It was also reported that Aiyuk will not be elevated from the Commanders' practice squad for when they play the Arizona Cardinals on Sunday.

Not that this matters for Christian McCaffrey and everybody else associated with the 49ers. The franchise must be relieved that Aiyuk is now with another franchise, which means they don't have to worry about his actions.

The 49ers have their hands full with their own game on Sunday, as they face the defending Super Bowl champion Seattle Seahawks.
