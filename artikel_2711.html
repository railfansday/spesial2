Coach Zac Taylor's Cincinnati Bengals are 7-2 on the season after their 38-18 home domination of Mike McCarthy's Pittsburgh Steelers that featured a celebratory mood set off by rampaging defensive tackle Dexter Lawrence. Joe Burrow reached a record-setting level alongside NFL legend Peyton Manning that bodes well for his MVP chances.

The Bengals' stirring win caused many media analysts to step up and share their bullish takes on Taylor's team, including ESPN's Sam Block.

"The Cincinnati Bengals are good enough to win the Super Bowl this season," Block said.

Burrow's Bengals have never won the Super Bowl but have played in the big game three times in franchise history, with the last one coming in 2022, when he was the quarterback, a 23-20 loss to the Los Angeles Rams. This season could mark a return trip if Burrow and his team continue to play the way they did on November 15 against Aaron Rodgers and the Pittsburgh Steelers, who fell to 6-3 after their loss.

The Bengals are now a full game ahead of the Steelers with eight games left on the schedule for both teams. The Browns and Ravens are lurking in the background, but Taylor's team looks like the one to beat after they showed flashes of the 2022 team that many thought should have won the whole thing.

Burrow also joined Tom Brady with a record-setting feat before showing out on Sunday Night Football. With the Commanders, Saints, and Browns on deck, fans should expect more high-level passing performances that could place him in the same pantheon of league legends by the time his season is over.
