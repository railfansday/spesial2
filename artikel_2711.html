C.J. Stroud throws for three TDs in easy win

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Houston Texans QB C.J. Stroud connected on 21 of 27 pass attempts for 194 yards, three touchdowns, and an interception in Week 11 against the Indianapolis Colts. Stroud also ran for 27 yards in the blowout win.

Why It Matters: Stroud shook off a slow start to carve up the Colts, though with the game well in hand he wound up handing the ball to David Montgomery a lot on Thursday night. The result was a solid but unspectacular statistical performance in a high-upside matchup. The former No. 2 overall pick is enjoying easily his best season since his rookie year, which includes 16 touchdown passes over the last five games. Stroud has moved into low-end or even midrange QB1 territory with his play this year, and with some attractive matchups remaining, the former Buckeye should be a viable starter most weeks.
