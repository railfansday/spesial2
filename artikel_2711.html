Barcelona ready to listen to offers for defensive duo in January &#8211; report

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Barcelona could sanction the departures of Jules Kounde and Alejandro Balde during the January transfer window as the club looks to generate funds for defensive reinforcements.

According to journalist Matteo Moretto on DAZN, the Catalan club is prepared to consider suitable proposals for both defenders, although their futures will also depend on the decisions they make about their playing time.

"One or both of them could leave. We'll see what offers come in and what the players decide," Moretto said.

The possibility of losing two established full-backs would be a significant development for Hansi Flick, particularly with Barcelona also exploring ways to strengthen their defensive options.

The club's financial requirements could therefore force difficult decisions about players who were once important parts of the starting XI.

Kounde's situation has attracted attention from Chelsea, who are still monitoring his availability ahead of January.

Chelsea continue to value Kounde's versatility. (Photo by Fran Santiago/Getty Images)

The Frenchman has struggled to maintain his previous position in Flick's defensive hierarchy, with Eric Garcia moving ahead of him at right-back.

His physical problems have further complicated his efforts to reclaim a regular starting place.

It must be remembered that Xabi Alonso's side attempted to sign Kounde from Sevilla in 2022, but the defender ultimately chose Barcelona.

However, with Malo Gusto's future at Stamford Bridge uncertain, Alonso views Kounde's ability to operate at both right-back and centre-back as an attractive solution.

Balde faces a different situation, with competition for the left-back position increasing following Joao Cancelo's arrival and Xavi Espart's emergence.

Alejandro Balde has suitors in Italy. (Photo by Pedro Salado/Getty Images)

Previous reports have linked him with interest from Serie A, particularly Roma and Juventus, but the fact that Barcelona value the 23-year-old at approximately €40 million and would prefer a permanent transfer is complicating any potential departure.

At present, a loan arrangement that guarantees future income is also being considered, but no agreement is finalised with any club at the moment.

It is known that Barcelona wants to strengthen its defence, but generating sufficient transfer income through departures could require sacrificing players who still offer valuable positional depth, creating a severe dilemma for Flick and the sporting department.

On that note, Barcelona will also need to consider that Kounde's versatility and Balde's experience would not be easy to replace, particularly during a January window when quality defensive alternatives are difficult to secure.

As a result, Barcelona need to analyse all possible pros and cons before making any decision.
