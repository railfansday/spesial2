North Central Michigan women's cross country makes program history

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

PETOSKEY — The North Central Michigan College athletic program is still young.

So, history-making moments are more than likely still going to come each season.

But, the NCMC women's cross country team just made the kind that stands out in any program and is sure to leave a lasting mark.

The Timberwolves' women's cross country team became the first NCMC athletic team to make a national championship when they recently qualified for the NJCAA DII National Championships.

After finishing runner-up in the Michigan Community College Athletic Association championships, the Timberwolves turned in a fifth place finish out of 12 teams in the MCCAA/Region XII Championships at Goodells County Park in St. Clair.

The finish comes a year after the NCMC women's team placed 11th in the region championship race, highlighting their leap in one season for a completely new team made up of all freshmen.

The NJCAA DII National Championships will take place on Saturday, Nov. 14 in Huntsville, Ala.

Muskegon Community College earned the regional title behind a 63-point finish, followed closely by Grand Rapids Community College with 69 points. Macomb then placed third with 93, St. Clair County was fourth with 128, followed then by North Central's 154.

On top of the team finish for NCMC, Esther Hornibrook set a new program record for a 5K race. Hornibrook, who grew up home schooled in Petoskey, ran an eighth place time of 19 minutes, 45.5 seconds.

North Central's Sahara Davis (Reed City) then followed in 27th (21:12.8), Teryn Firack (Pickford) placed 30th (21:22.2) and Makayla Watkins (Reed City) took 44th (22:09.6).

The Timberwolves also had Lindsey Masterson (Rudyard) run 22:26.2, Cambrynne Giem (Boyne City) ran 22:35.7 and Ellie Emrich (Rudyard) ran 24:52.9.

Ivy Forrest of Mott claimed the individual title in 19:02.3, with Grand Rapids' Mackenzie Baldrica placing second in 19:14.5 and Adrienne Fluette of Muskegon taking third in 19:16.6.

The North Central men's program placed eighth in the 8K race of the NJCAA Region XII Championships, led by Dakota Taylor (Engadine) in a 41st place finish in 28:37.1, a season-best time.

The North Central men's basketball team got it's 2026-27 season started in the Lake Michigan College Classic over the weekend and wasted no time putting a win on the schedule.

The Timberwolves opened the season with a 66-64 victory over Glen Oaks Community College. The Timberwolves then lost to Lake Michigan College 79-74 the following day.

Ty'Reese Burnett (Indianapolis, Ind.) led in the opener with 21 points and six assists, then came within a rebound of a triple-double in game two, finishing with 17 points, 10 assists and nine rebounds.

Temi Jegele (Brampton, Ont.) also had 14 points, six assists and five rebounds in the opener for NCMC, then scored 12 and added six more assists in Saturday's game. Gavin Goetz (Cadillac) also had 10 points and eight rebounds against Lake Michigan.

The NJCAA Region XII Great Lakes Championship tournament awaits the women's volleyball team at North Central after finishing the regular season against Muskegon and Montcalm last week.

Abby Hicks (Leland) finished the regular season finale with a career-high 38 assists, which helped set up a career-best hitting night from Lilah DuBose (Haslett), who finished with nine kills.

Hicks, a sophomore, finished her second regular season with a team-high 595 assists, along with an impressive 91 aces. The aces mark set a new program record by a wide margin and she'll be able to add to both numbers ahead in the Great Lakes Championship tournament.

The regular season aces total for Hicks also ranks her top five in all of the NJCAA Division II this season and top 10 across all three divisions of the NJCAA.
