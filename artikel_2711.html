Road America's Vintage Weekend wins Motorsport Event of the Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

ELKHART LAKE – Road America's WeatherTech Vintage Weekend with Brian Redman, which brings historic race cars to Sheboygan County and downtown Elkhart Lake, has been named Motorsport Event of the Year at the 2026 International Historic Motoring Awards.

The honor gives the Sheboygan County track and the village of Elkhart Lake international recognition as the event prepares to return July 15-18, 2027, with Porsche as its featured marque. Friday and Saturday evening concours gatherings extend the celebration into downtown Elkhart Lake.

The award was announced Nov. 13 in London. It is the event's first win in its first year as a finalist.

Other finalists were the Grand Prix de Monaco Historique, Rolex Monterey Motorsports Reunion and Pittsburgh Vintage Grand Prix. The Revs Institute accepted the award on Road America's behalf.

"This recognition has been years in the making," said Mike Kertscher, president and general manager of Road America. "Our sponsors, participants, staff, volunteers, fans and neighbors in the Village of Elkhart Lake have all helped build this event. We're proud to share this award with them."

Porsche will be recognized through its early creations, road cars and racing machines, with attention to its long history at Road America. Featured cars, special guests and activities will be announced as plans are finalized.

Bill Wuesthoff and Augie Pabst won the 1963 Road America 500 in a 2-liter Elva-Porsche, the only car of 2 liters or less to win the race.

George Follmer won in a Porsche 917/10 in 1972, followed by Mark Donohue in a 917/30 in 1973. Porsche 935 and 962 race cars combined for seven Road America 500 victories from 1980 to 1993. Porsche 963s won in 2023 with Matt Campbell and Felipe Nasr and in 2024 with Nick Tandy and Mathieu Jaminet, part of a Porsche Penske Motorsport one-two finish.

Kellymoss, which has deep roots in Wisconsin and longstanding success in Porsche motorsports, is helping shape the 2027 celebration.

The 2026 event welcomed more than 450 historic race cars. Highlights included 45 All American Racers Eagles honoring Dan Gurney and 57 Trans-Am cars celebrating the championship's 60th anniversary.

Road America credited the Dresang family, the Gurney family and the Eagle reunion organizers for assembling the collection.

Road America thanked title sponsor WeatherTech, along with Bring a Trailer, National Exchange Bank & Trust and Lake Forest Motor Sports.

It also credited corner marshals, safety and rescue crews, medical personnel, officials, staff and volunteers, along with fans who return year after year.

The WeatherTech Vintage Weekend with Brian Redman returns to Road America July 15-18, 2027. Tickets and event information are available at RoadAmerica.com.
