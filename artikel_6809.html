Hailey Bieber Wears 2 Very Different Takes on ’90s Fashion in 1 Day

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Hailey Bieber’s latest New York looks have one thing in common: the ’90s. First up was a nod to Carolyn Bessette Kennedy, whose wardrobe has become a regular source of inspiration for the model.

On Wednesday, November 18, the Rhode founder attended the New York Times’s DealBook Summit in New York City wearing a black Yohji Yamamoto jacket from the designer’s spring 1998 collection.

The archival piece featured a high neckline, a fitted waist, and a row of buttons down the front. She paired it with a cream-colored midi skirt, black pointed-toe pumps, and a black headband reminiscent of CBK’s signature accessory.

Bessette Kennedy wore the same jacket design with a white skirt to the Robin Hood Foundation benefit in June 1998, which she attended alongside her husband, John F. Kennedy Jr.

Bieber’s jacket was not Bessette Kennedy’s personal piece, but her rep confirmed to Vogue that the two share the same design.

Later that evening, Bieber changed into an entirely different outfit for dinner with Justin Bieber at Emilio’s Ballato. She wore a black, plunging top with a bow at the center, paired with a black slip skirt.

She finished the look with pointed-toe heels, narrow sunglasses, and a pastel yellow shoulder bag.

Justin, meanwhile, wore a black leather jacket over a light green shirt, loose gray trousers, and sunglasses.

Wednesday’s outfit is just the latest instance of Hailey channeling Bessette Kennedy’s fashion. In September, she attended a dinner at Harry’s Bar in London wearing a champagne-colored Cerruti by Narciso Rodriguez slip dress from the spring 1997 collection.

The dress was the same design Bessette Kennedy wore to her wedding rehearsal dinner in 1996.

Hailey’s appearances come just days before her 30th birthday on November 22. She has already begun celebrating the occasion, wearing a vintage red-and-black Dolce & Gabbana corset for an early birthday gathering with friends and family on November 13.
