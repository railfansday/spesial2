Cashmere Perfumes Are the Coziest Way to Wear Fragrance

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

These soft, musky scents capture the plush warmth of your favorite sweater.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Few things are as cozy as cashmere—whether it’s a sweater, scarf, or throw blanket. One unexpected way to wrap yourself in the fabric’s luxury is with a cashmere perfume. These scents capture the best parts of the warm, fuzzy material through an actual cashmere note called cashmeran. The synthetic note, used in perfume since it was developed in the 1960s, adds warmth, woodiness, musk, and spice to a perfume’s layers, giving the overall composition a soft, velvety texture. Grounded in the warm woods and sweet gourmands of the season, or anchored alongside a fresh floral, these cashmere perfumes are the next best thing to a premium garment.

The richness of sandalwood and the creaminess of a milk accord layer seamlessly at the bottom and top of a cashmeran center.

Key notes: milky accord, nutmeg, Iris, rose, cashmeran, sandalwood, Madagascar vanilla bourbon

Customer review: “Santal Calling is divine; on my skin it is magic. Pure comfort in a bottle—creamy sandalwood wrapped in soft iris, warm milk, and the smoothest touch of vanilla. It feels like cashmere against the skin: elegant, intimate, and so addictive that I can't stop spraying myself throughout the day.” - Madrone

Many lavender perfumes capture the essence of the flower itself, but Matiere Premiere took a different approach, encapsulating the atmospheric notes from a lavender distillation factory in Provence. The result is a hybrid of fresh lavender blending with crisper hints of metal that are then softened by musk and cashmeran.

Customer review: “For me, lavender is a tricky scent. It depends how it is paired in a perfume composition. This one opens with a very "fresh" lavender, which will go quiet in the background, and it will reappear in the dry down. Lovely perfume, easy to wear and very good longevity (6+ hours on my skin).” - Ramona D.

One of Maison Margiela’s most popular scents blends the smokiness of burning wood with notes of gaiac wood, cashmeran, and clove oil to bottle the experience of sitting by a crackling fireplace.

Key notes: clove oil, chestnut accord, vanilla accord

Customer review: “My all-time favorite perfume. It smells like sitting by the fireplace wrapped in your comfy blanket, sipping your favorite drink. The scent is perfect for winter!” - MelB

This Mugler classic intensifies as it warms up with your body heat, bringing out the long-lasting notes of white amber, cashmeran wood, and jasmine. It’s equal parts floral and woody, with just the right warmth and sweetness from its amber base.

Key notes: jasmine sambac, cashmeran wood, white amber

Customer review: “This perfume has so many layers and is so sophisticated and wonderful. I really like the notes and how they mature over time. Great longevity as well.” - prespri_7178

Cashmere is the star here, anchoring the scent’s base and owning the dry-down, as toasted coconut, passionflower, and vanilla milk musk notes layer at its heart and on top.

Key notes: vanilla milk musk, toasted coconut, passionflower, plumeria, cashmeran wood

Customer review: “Soft, cozy, sweet, and luxurious. It’s giving cashmere sweater + clean skin! It’s an easy reach that I can’t stop wearing.” - Regina

This intensified version keeps the crisp, linen-like freshness of Byredo’s original Blanche eau de perfume. It takes a warmer turn as black pepper and neroli make way for a floral center, before settling into a base of white musk, cashmere wood, and ambroxan. It wears like a skin scent, although it’s in the floral family.

Key notes: aldehyde, black pepper, neroli, rose absolute, violet, jasmine, cashmeran, musks, ambroxan, cashmere woods

Customer review: “I absolutely love Blanche Absolu, intense aldehydes, clean laundry done to perfection with good longevity on skin and fabric! 10/10” - layla88

Carmina combines sweet and spicy top notes of pink pepper, black cherry, and saffron with a hearty floral center of rose, violet, and peony. To round out the profile, cashmere wood, amber, and musk peak through on the dry down.

Key notes: pink pepper, black cherry, saffron, rose de mai, violet, peony

Customer review: “It's giving 'date night,' to me. It's soft, feminine, and sensual all at the same time. As far as longevity, I sprayed it around 5pm, and I could still smell it on my wrist the next morning.”

Although it’s not quite a gourmand, Esplendor has a sweetness that’s not cloying. Bottom notes of amber and vanilla sit alongside cashmeran. Adding to the warmth at the center is benzoin, a creamy note with a balsamic-like aroma. Classic florals like rose and lily of the valley are stacked at the top to open the scent.

Key notes: lily, freesia, peony, rose, muguet, benzoin, vanilla, amber, cashmeran

Customer review: “Grandano Esplendor drapes the skin in quiet luxury. It is a cashmere sweater, washed in lilies and creamy vanilla, then folded into warm benzoin resin. The sweetness is gentle rather than sugary, and the amber gives it a comforting warmth. It doesn't overly project and lasts 8 hours, at least, on skin. It feels like velvet brushed in slow motion.” - AlchemyAge

Gap’s Om features cashmeran, musk, and amber woods—the trifecta that gives cashmere perfumes their warmth. As the scent develops, hints of spice shine through from coriander flower at the center and ginger to open the blend. Heavier notes of frankincense are balanced with light florals like water lotus.

Key notes: ginger, olibanum essence, water lotus, lilyflor, sandalwood, coriander flower, amber woods, cashmeran, cedar, balsamic musks

Customer review: “If you loved Heaven in the 90s, try the new 2026 "Om". It's smell reminds me of the old Heaven and I LOVE it. It smells like fresh laundry, clean baby clothes, spring flowers, all the calming things. I think I found a new signature scent!” - Samantha H.

Chloé’s Parfum Intense takes you on a journey that hits all the high points of a versatile fragrance. It opens with the sweetness of juicy raspberry and rose, which then shifts into a honeyed element at the center before settling into its intense ending with rich musks and woods.

Key notes: raspberry, rose, ambrox, cashmeran, cedar

Customer review: “Chloé Eau de Parfum is a wardrobe essential for anyone who treasures floral elegance that never feels fussy. It’s instantly recognizable yet versatile — perfect for spring and summer, but cozy enough for year-round wear.” -Beautybae01

Unlike many fragrances on this list that place cashmeran at the base of their composition, this one nestles it right at its heart. The scent experience begins with a kick thanks to a sweet and spicy mix of blood orange, Sichuan pepper, and ginger. You’re then brought right into a cozy heart of cashmeran and a brand-exclusive floral accord before rounding out the journey with a blend of woods, musk, and vetiver.

Key notes: ambrette, baies rose, blood orange, bergamot, Sichuan pepper, fresh ginger, fleuriste fusion, magnolia aroma space, Egyptian jasmine, cashmeran, guaiacwood, cedarwood, blond wood, vetiver, moss, ambroxan, musks

Customer review: “It’s a clean fragrance. Quiet luxury, skin scent that still has a silage that draws attention. Easy wear if you like musk. It’s all you really get. But it’s what I wanted.” - Edward B.

Donna Karan’s Cashmere Mist is quite possibly the perfume that introduced plush, cozy, cashmere-like notes to fragrance. The iconic eau de parfum, which launched in the mid-'90s, features rich florals and sandalwood—and manages to encapsulate all the soft attributes of cashmere without including cashmere wood in its composition.

Key notes: lily of the valley, Moroccan jasmine, sandalwood

Customer review: “It is a soft, sensual scent that has musky overtones. It stays with you all day long, whereas most scents disappear shortly after applying. This is one of the most pleasing scents that works with all occasions and all the different lotions I like to use. I believe I have found a scent that I will purchase over and over again without it getting old or boring. I have had numerous compliments when I wear this scent, which always makes a girl happy.” - Sasicat
