David Boreanaz’s ‘Rockford Files’ Reboot Officially Sets Release Date

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Jim Rockford is back. The iconic TV detective from the classic crime caper series The Rockford Files will return in the New Year, thanks to NBC and star David Boreanaz. Now, we finally know when the new series will premiere—and how you can get inside information on the series by calling up Jim Rockford himself.

A reboot of the classic detective series, The Rockford Files will star Boreanaz (Bones) as Rockford, stepping into the shoes of the late James Garner. He's a private eye who was framed for a crime he didn't commit; now, he's back on the street and ready to make a name for himself—even if that means stepping on the toes of both cops and criminals. The series also stars Michaela McManus (Redux Redux), Ilfenesh Hadera (Godfather of Harlem), and Jacki Weaver (Silver Linings Playbook); it is showrun by Mike Daniels (Sons of Anarchy), who also executive produces with Carl Beverly, Sarah Timberman, and David Hudgins.

Now, we finally know when Rockford will be cracking his first case. The Rockford Files is set to premiere on Saturday, January 2, 2027, immediately after NBC's NFL broadcast at approximately 8 p.m. ET as a special preview; it will then debut at its normal day and time on Thursday, January 7, at 8 p.m. ET. All episodes will also stream the next day on Peacock. Want to know more? A great detective is really just a personality with a method attached, which is why we keep remaking the same four of them. Here is yours, what it says about you, and the case for them being the best in the business.

You let people think you are not a threat and then you simply never go away. Columbo is the only major detective show that tells you who did it in the first ten minutes, because the pleasure was never the puzzle, it was watching a rich man slowly realise he is in trouble.

You are the most interesting person in the room and you know exactly how to use it. Rian Johnson built Blanc as a gentleman detective who is also an outsider everywhere he goes, which lets him be charming at people who would never otherwise talk to him.

Everybody tells you everything, and you have never once asked them to. Jessica Fletcher is a crime novelist from a small town in Maine who is so warm and so obviously trustworthy that suspects confess to her essentially as a social courtesy.

You expect to be lied to and you are almost never disappointed. Chandler wrote Marlowe as a man with a rigid private code in a city that has none, which is why he takes the case, solves it, gets nothing out of it and takes the next one.

Jim Rockford's answering machine was an important part of the original series, which ran from 1974 to 1980. Answering machines weren't yet common in American homes; in fact, Rockford rented his. It was another way that Rockford, with his stylish suits, sports car, and fake business cards, distinguished himself from the detectives of the past. They were also a trademark of the series; before the opening credits, every episode opened with a new, humorous message on Rockford's machine, akin to the "couch gags" on The Simpsons; they were typically unrelated to the content of the episode, and eventually the writers had to take suggestions from the cast and crew to keep coming up with new ones.
