Ziemba: Oil Supply Risks Outweigh OPEC+ Moves

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Ziemba Insights founder Rachel Ziemba says physical supply risks are a bigger challenge for OPEC+ than managing the oil market as attacks on tankers and energy infrastructure raise concerns about further disruptions. She also discusses risks to diesel supplies, the outlook for Iraq and the Saudi-Russia relationship, and how higher crude prices could feed through to consumers with Abeer Abu Omar on Bloomberg’s “Horizons Middle East and Africa.” (Source: Bloomberg)
