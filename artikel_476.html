B3 to Add Brazil Stocks Listed Abroad to Aid Ailing Local Market

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Brazil’s exchange operator B3 SA will incorporate shares of Brazilian companies listed abroad to the nation’s benchmark equity index starting next year in a push to boost liquidity after years of de-listings in the local market.
