A career change into medical might be possible. I see quite a few ads for nurses and positions like ultrasound or CT techs. Also see quite a few ads for truck drivers.
Since you probably have no background in those fields, plan on 4-6 years of schooling just to become eligible to be a tech. Nursing probably requires as much or more depending on which kind.

If you have the passion for it, go for it.

To get a CDL is considerably quicker. Some do it without lessons, other people take a course for 6-9 weeks but sometimes over months if they can't dedicate full time to it. I know someone who did it for fun.

There was a time I thought driving was fun, but I don't think I ever thought driving a truck all day would be fun. Perhaps you could get a job delivering Amazon packages. No idea how well it pays or what hours you have to work. I am glad there are people who want to do it, since it improves my life and I appreciate them.
