Game Of Thrones: The Sea Snake Officially Unveiled

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

House of the Dragon has been a relatively controversial series among Game of Thrones fans over its three-season run, though it's likely viewers who have made it this far are bound to tune in. Season 4 will see the epic conclusion to The Dance of the Dragons, bringing the civil war between House Targaryen's rival factions to an end when the series returns in 2028.

While Rhaenyra, Alicent, and Daemon have dominated much of the story so far, the series has also introduced a sprawling cast of incredible characters from George R.R. Martin's Fire & Blood. Among the most influential is House Velaryon, whose wealth and naval strength have played a crucial role in the conflict.

At the center of the family is none other than Lord Corlys Velaryon, Lord of the Tides, Master of Driftmark, and better known as the Sea Snake, whose legendary voyages established his family's influence. Now, ahead of House of the Dragon's final season, the Sea Snake is officially back in the spotlight with a brand-new release that will be exciting for fans of House Velaryon.

Lord Corlys Velaryon, better known as the Sea Snake, is officially joining Game of Thrones: Dragonfire as one of the game's new Commanders, bringing the legend to the battlefield. First detailed in a November 12 announcement, Corlys is available to unlock through Daily Login Events during the Heirs of the Dragon campaign, with the Alliance Market being a secondary way to get him after you've completed your first reign.

Much like his character in House of the Dragon, Corlys specializes in calculated strategy and endurance. He's effective when paired with dragons who have tnhe Cunning or Relentless traits, and can disrupt enemy attacks while buffing his own army.

The Sea Snake can apply Vulnerable stacks to three enemy dragons at the beginning of the first round, with a Relentless ally making the attack stronger. Blockade is an ability that reduces incoming Physical and Tactical damage for allied dragons, while Driftmark's Snare targets a single enemy dragon, weakening its attacks when it deals damage.

Recommended dragons include Crimson, Feskar, Zivern, and Arulix for Cunning, alongside Meleys, Kalspire, Starshower, Arrax, and Solstryker for Relentless.

Corlys is also part of a brand-new Commander system introduced to Dragonfire, which introduces iconic Westerosi characters from House of the Dragon. Commanders occupy a dedicated seat within an army and have unique Talents that strengthen allies or weaken opponents. They can also be upgraded using special resources, with progression carrying over between reigns.

The first announced wave includes Rhaenyra Targaryen, Alicent Hightower, Daemon Targaryen, Criston Cole, and Corlys Velaryon, each with different strategies.

With House of the Dragon preparing to conclude its story in Season 4, currently scheduled for 2028, this new gameplay update gives fans a great opportunity to experience the Sea Snake's considerable influence on Westeros. And while the series has plenty of unfinished business involving House Velaryon, Dragonfire offers fans a chance to see Corlys's expertise firsthand.
