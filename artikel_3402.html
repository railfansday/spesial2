Paramount+'s Best True Crime Series Returns Next Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

True crime is one of the most popular genres on streaming. Now, Paramount+'s long-running true-crime series is back for another season to take a look behind the scenes of one of the largest law enforcement agencies on the planet. Collider is proud to exclusively present the first trailer for season 9 of FBI True, which will premiere next month on Paramount+.

Debuting in 2023, FBI True interviews former and current Federal Bureau of Investigation agents who take a deeper look into the inner workings of the agency. The series has taken a look at some of the most notorious cases the FBI's ever worked, including the murder of Gianni Versace, the 1996 Olympic bombings, and the hunt for FBI mole Robert Hanssen. It also puts the spotlight on lesser-known cases, like the neo-Nazi paramilitary organization The Base, the 1980 bombing of Harvey's Resort Hotel in Nevada, and the search for the Yosemite Killer. The series has been very prolific, releasing multiple seasons a year since its debut three years ago; Season 8 premiered earlier this year in March.

Now, in the new trailer for its upcoming ninth season, FBI True spotlights three cases that will be examined this year. The first is the disappearance of teenager Dylan Rounds, which sparked a massive manhunt in rural Utah; it will highlight the forensic examination of Rounds' cellphone, which was reconstructed and repaired despite being disposed of in a pond. Next up is a 2018 shootout in Brooklyn in which an FBI agent on a surveillance stakeout was shot by gang members, for reasons unrelated to the surveillance, while on the phone with his wife; FBI True talks to the agent in question, getting a first-hand account of the incident. This season will also cover the abduction and murder of 7-year-old Georgia girl Somer Thompson, and the hunt for her killer. Season 9 will premiere exclusively on Paramount+ on December 1.

FBI True is executive produced by Shawn Efran for Bright North Studios, Craig Turk for Thinking Hat Productions, and Anne Beagan for Anne Beagan Productions. The series is showrun by Solly Granatstein, who also executive produces alongside Morgan Hertzan and Azadeh De Leon. Executive producers for See It Now Studios are Susan Zirinsky and Terence Wrong, with Aysu Saliba and Cara Tortora serving as supervising producers.

Season 9 of FBI True will premiere on Paramount+ on December 1. Stay tuned to Collider for future updates.
