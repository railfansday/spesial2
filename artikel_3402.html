3 Elite Netflix Series to Binge This Week (Nov 16-20)

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Netflix has been taken over by the perfect follow-up to Prime Video's recent adaptation of Stephen King's Carrie. The Body, a brilliant, twisting thriller starring Kristina Bogic and Sara Boustany, has taken the #1 spot on the Netflix charts in the U.S. "The Body is like nothing else on streaming right now," wrote Gabrielle Moss in Collider's review, with this unique appeal helping to catapult Netflix's latest hit to the streaming summit. But it isn't the only show that should be on your radar at the moment. With that in mind, here's a look at three shows you should binge-watch on Netflix this week.

For more recommendations, check out our list of the best shows and movies on Netflix.

Disclaimer: These titles are available on US Netflix.

After arriving on Netflix last month, one of the best horror shows of all time is now also one of the best shows on the world's biggest streamer. The first three seasons of From, courtesy of a limited-time licensing agreement with Amazon MGM Studios, are available to binge right now, ahead of the show's fifth season, which has been confirmed as the final installment.

Harold Perrineau stars in From as Sheriff Boyd Stevens, a retired U.S. Army veteran who appoints himself in charge of a community plagued by terrifying creatures. Blending sci-fi and horror to near-perfect effect, the series has shocked and delighted audiences in equal measure for four years, picking up several impressive award nominations along the way, including a nod for Best Horror Series at the Critics' Choice Super Awards.

A great detective is really just a personality with a method attached, which is why we keep remaking the same four of them. Here is yours, what it says about you, and the case for them being the best in the business.

You let people think you are not a threat and then you simply never go away. Columbo is the only major detective show that tells you who did it in the first ten minutes, because the pleasure was never the puzzle, it was watching a rich man slowly realise he is in trouble.

You are the most interesting person in the room and you know exactly how to use it. Rian Johnson built Blanc as a gentleman detective who is also an outsider everywhere he goes, which lets him be charming at people who would never otherwise talk to him.

Everybody tells you everything, and you have never once asked them to. Jessica Fletcher is a crime novelist from a small town in Maine who is so warm and so obviously trustworthy that suspects confess to her essentially as a social courtesy.

You expect to be lied to and you are almost never disappointed. Chandler wrote Marlowe as a man with a rigid private code in a city that has none, which is why he takes the case, solves it, gets nothing out of it and takes the next one.

If you're looking to start a binge-watch this week that you can continue for the rest of the year and even into 2027, then why not make it one of the most important police procedurals ever? Dick Wolf's Law & Order has been a mainstay on network television for 36 years, and a healthy portion of its 26 seasons are currently available on Netflix following the addition of Season 25 in late September.

As Season 26 continues on NBC, catch up on seasons 21 through 25 right now on Netflix, with each pulse-racing beat ready and waiting to be indulged in. A winner of six Primetime Emmys and countless other awards, this hugely important procedural has inspired many others like it and still stands today as one of the best network shows.

Every year, higher-ups at Netflix have to make the tough decision about which shows to keep and which to give the axe. In 2025, they made the controversial call to cancel a brand-new fan favorite and a series that had earned plenty of praise from both audiences and critics. The show in question is The Residence, with fans even starting a petition online for the show to be renewed.

The series follows detective Cordelia Cupp (Uzo Aduba) and FBI special agent Edwin Park (Randall Park) as a puzzling mystery unfolds inside the White House. Giancarlo Esposito and Ken Marino also feature in Paul William Davies' politically charged gem, which is packed with twists and turns and a cast who are clearly loving every second of this must-watch whodunnit. Despite being canceled, the show was nominated for four Primetime Emmys, including Outstanding Lead Actress in a Comedy Series for Aduba.
