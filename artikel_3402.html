Marvel Officially Announces Captain America’s New Codename For Steve Rogers’ Next Era

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Steve Rogers loses his Captain America title and replaces it with an ominous three-word moniker. Unlike Tony Stark, who has retained the Iron Man mantle almost permanently throughout his whole superhero career, Steve Rogers' historical legacy as Captain America has spawned dozens of official and unofficial replacements. Sam Wilson and Bucky Barnes are Steve Rogers' direct successors, and Sam himself has cemented himself as a definitive Captain America on the same level as Steve.

In the comics, following Steve’s apparent death in 1945, the US government quietly recruited William Nasland and later Jeffrey Mace to wear the uniform and preserve public morale without revealing the original Captain America’s loss, while Isaiah Bradley endured secret super-soldier experimentation as a Captain America tragically forgotten by history. Later decades introduced impostors like William Burnside, the obsessed 1950s Cold War fanatic who surgically altered his face and took a flawed serum that made him violent, alongside Roscoe Simons, a brave young Cap admirer briefly given the shield by Falcon before the Red Skull murdered him.

Steve Rogers has even been replaced by himself, back when Captain America's infamous Captain Hydra clone supplanted him in Secret Empire, and now by his own monstrous evolution.

Six months after Marvel first unveiled the Infernal Hulk's army for the upcoming Hulk War, the monster's nightmarish Avengers finally get their official names. Iron Man becomes "The Iron Thing" and Spider-Man becomes "The Arthropod," while Captain Marvel transforms into "The Ebon Sphinx" and Jubilee into "The Angler," with Vision evolving into the mind-bending "Diaboli Ex Machina." The darkest redesign in the Infernal Hulk's Avengers is probably Captain America, who's crucified and carried by four zombie soldiers, bearing the name "The War Dead."

The first image featuring the Infernal Hulk's Infernal Avengers revealed a crucified Captain America standing above the already large Iron Thing, Diaboli Ex MAchina, and Infernal Hulk, only surpassed in height by Spider-Man's colossal Arthropod transformation. It also included a couple of undead soldiers mysteriously clad in all red. Now, the Infernal Avengers character sheet designed by Nic Klein provides a closer look at "The War Dead," revealing that the four soldiers carrying Steve Rogers' body are actually bathed in his blood.

The Infernal Hulk has already proved nearly unbeatable with nothing but the Eldest-possessed body of the Hulk. Now aided by even bigger creatures like the muscular Iron Thing and the grotesquely large Arthropod, the Infernal Hulk seems truly unstoppable. The flying Ebon Sphinx also seems particularly dangerous in battle, and the Angler's energy-based body makes her the most unpredictable monster. Likewise, Diaboli Ex Machina may retain all of the Vision's overpowered abilities and none of his restraints.

Captain America may be single, but he sure has a lot of kids in the multiverse.

Captain America's body doesn't appear to undergo a transformation of any kind. Instead, Steve Rogers is pierced in the chest and the back by two spears, with an additional spear going through each hand decorated with skulls. Cap seems to be unconscious or dead, while the undead soldiers carry the whole weight of the platform supporting him on their shoulders. There's very little The War Dead may be able to do during battle, but his design is without a doubt the most gruesome of the Infernal Avengers.
