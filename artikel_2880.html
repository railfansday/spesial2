Houston Texans vs. Indianapolis Colts ‘TNF’ Week 11 live game chat

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Look what we have here, Rumblers: It's a primetime matchup between a pair of AFC teams. Though we've had a couple this season and more frequently of late, AFC vs. AFC is about as rare as a Barbary lion these days. The trouble here is that this is a terrible matchup, featuring teams that are decidedly anything other than apex hunters.

That the NFL can still sell this product to anyone is an incredible feat. Yet football fans continue lining up to see awful teams take on each other. It's not that this one is without some interest, which is due to a few star players among both Houston and Indianapolis.

The issue is that as Buffalo Bills fans, you're settling in to watch the 3-6 Texans take on the 3-7 Colts. Those two teams are a combined 6-13 — which, if you've done the math, is less total wins than the 8-1 Bills have on the season.

Neither of these teams was supposed to flop this season, though the writing was on the wall for Indianapolis. But Houston? It's been an epic collapse for the Texans in 2026. A once-mighty defense has proven feckless far too often this season, which has some saying that head coach DeMeco Ryans could be warming the very seat he sits on in Houston. Fair or not, the NFL is a production-based industry.

If you're like me, the game will be on simply because it helps fill the room a bit this evening. You may also share my passion for the Thursday Night Football crew, and their pre-/postgame coverage. If nothing else, there's also the chances to see the Texans in their Nike Rivals uniforms — and yes, they sure did again steal the Oilers' vibe.

Are you looking for a reason to root on one team over the other here? What you favor depends on who to cheer on to victory. A Houston win helps Buffalo's strength of schedule/strength of victory, which matters most for NFL teams with postseason hopes on life support. Instead, you may prefer seeing the Colts win simply so it "harms" the slotting of 2027 first-round pick they sent to the New York Jets.

With that, here's your open thread for Week 11 of Thursday Night Football. Maybe this one also turns into a decent game, like a few suspect snooze-fests in primetime before it. As always, be kind to one another — and in all things… Go Bills!
