IDK Brings Together Eminem and J Dilla for First Time on New Song ‘Welcome’

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

IDK brings together a pair of Detroit legends — Eminem and the late J Dilla — for the first time on the rapper’s new single “Welcome.”

Slim Shady contributes the chorus over a hard-edged, choppy Dilla beat while IDK shows off his dark lyricism on the all-Motor City affair; in the music video, IDK is driven around by Nissan, the son of D12 rapper Proof, who collaborated with both Eminem and J Dilla.

However, despite the Detroit ties, “Welcome” marks the first time that Eminem has appeared on a Dilla production; the producer born James Yancey died in 2006 from complications from lupus.

“When I first started working on this music, I remember looking up at my ceiling and saying ‘thank you’. I was speaking to Dilla because it felt like everything that could have been, in a way, started to clear itself organically. I knew this was an important opportunity and a statement for hip hop itself,” IDK said of the track in a statement. He recorded it shortly after completing his debut mixtape, Even the Devil Smiles released in January. “I had received [the beat] from some people from the Dilla estate. The prospect of doing some music came up in a conversation with Dilla’s brother and a close friend.

“The first rap album I’ve ever owned was 50 Cent’s Get Rich or Die Tryin’. A Shady Records release,” he added. “The first raps I made it a point to memorize were Eminem raps. I’ve been at it for some time now, so to be at the point in my journey seems surreal.
