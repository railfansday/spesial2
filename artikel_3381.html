New Fallout Game Leaks Ahead Of Fallout 5

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Fallout has revealed two major games on the horizon, but those apparently aren't the only new adventures coming to the Wasteland. Plans for Fallout 5 have been established for a while now, and Microsoft announced this year that Fallout: New Vegas developer Obsidian Entertainment would also develop a new title in the universe. Remasters for Fallout 3 and Fallout: New Vegas have also been announced, further filling out an apparently crowded slate.

The latest news comes on top of all of that, and it's a lot more out of left field than any of the other games on the horizon. Made in partnership with Bethesda, A Wasteland Full of Cats looks like it may be the next return to the Fallout series.

The existence of A Wasteland Full of Cats was revealed in an Australian Classification rating, which was filed on October 22, 2026 and surfaced in the news cycle today. The board rates the game as PG for "Mild themes and violence" — a much more family-friendly classification than the typical Fallout offering — but the real details come from the description provided on the website.

Like a number of other titles from developer Devcats, A Wasteland Full of Cats is a hidden object game built around finding, you guessed it, cats. This time, those cats have escaped from a Vault into the Wasteland, forcing the protagonist to seek them out and bring them back to safety. A Wasteland Full of Cats consists of 14 locations and 33 rooms in total, along with some hidden levels that can be optionally uncovered through "light puzzle mechanics."

A Wasteland Full of Cats sounds like a surprising turn for both Fallout and the Full of Cats series, neither of which have taken a turn in such an odd direction before. Previous Fallout spin-offs have generally been more grounded in the typical style and lore of the series, and the Full of Cats games haven't previously attempted a game built around a franchise collaboration.

Nonetheless, there's no reason why this shouldn't be a good game in its own right. The Full of Cats games range between Very Positive and Overwhelmingly Positive review aggregates on Steam, and Fallout's vivid worldbuilding and sense of humor lend themselves to more lighthearted takes without much trouble. The greater question, really, is how this collaboration came about to begin with, a question that will hopefully get answered whenever the game is officially announced.

We likely won't have to wait long to learn more about A Wasteland Full of Cats, as the Australian rating suggests that an announcement could be just around the corner. One obvious window for a reveal would be The Game Awards 2026, with the ceremony coming up on December 10. Otherwise, Bethesda and Devcats could simply choose to drop the Fallout news in a dedicated Xbox showcase or on any random day of the week, since revealing the game on the next Fallout Day would mean waiting until October 2026.
