Game Of Thrones Officially Brings Back Tywin Lannister In The Lion's Reign

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Game of Thrones might have jumped the shark somewhat during its final season, but there's still plenty of love for the fantasy franchise, if you are able to quash those memories and leave them in the past. The best evidence for that is just how many other adaptations there are out there right now, giving fans plenty of options, regardless of what medium they tend to inhabit.

Game of Thrones: War for Westeros is a great example, launching in 2027 and giving players the chance to lead any of the show's major factions in their ultimate quest for the Iron Throne. There are also ongoing adaptations that continue to release regular updates, including one which is about to thrust one of the book's most calculated characters back to the fore.

Game of Thrones is full of all kinds of scheming characters, from those who are loyal to nobody, like Littlefinger, to those who are simply trying to make it through life in as good a shape as possible (a la Tyrion). Arguably the figure who is most successful in their machinations, though, is the Lannister patriarch Tywin, who leads arguably the most powerful family in Westeros at the time of the books.

He's about to serve as a central figure in the latest set of events for Game of Thrones: Legends, the mobile puzzle role-playing game by Zynga that is available for both iOS and Android. The game essentially tasks players with color-matching gems in order to defeat opponents.

The latest event is Kingsroad: The Lion’s Reign, which is set to run from November 9, at 3:00 a.m. ET to December 7, at 2:00 a.m. ET. Set around the very important Kingsroad and Goldroad, there are a bunch of Daily and Weekly Quests to complete for rewards that you would otherwise struggle to accrue in the usual gameplay loop.

In breaking down both the events, the developer shared the following insight:

"Kingsroad: Complete Daily and Weekly Goals to earn Kingsroad points, and progress along the Kingsroad for rewards! Earn 300 points on each of your five Daily Goals, and 800 points for each Weekly goal to reach new Tiers every day. This season’s Kingsroad rewards include Common Champion Shards for the Lannister Spearman, Summoning Tokens, and more! Beyond Tier 50, you can claim a bonus chest of rewards for every 700 points!

"Goldroad: This season’s Goldroad rewards include 15 Legendary Champion Shards for Tywin Lannister (The Hand). Upgrade to Elite Goldroad Pass to earn Exalted Tokens, Legendary Gear, Splendid, Grand, and Forge Summon Tokens, Valyrian Essence, Skill Books, and other bonuses on top of the Kingsroad rewards. Progress through the Tiers to earn more rewards for unlocking and powering up Champions!"

It's worth noting that the Elite Goldroad Pass will cost real world money, though it absolutely isn't necessary to play the game in its current state.

Outside of that, fans of George RR Martin's universe can look forward to the final season of House of the Dragon in 2028, as well as the second season of the excellent Knight of the Seven Kingdoms next year. Don't count on that Game of Thrones Season 8 reshoot any time soon though.
