Aidan Hutchinson makes promise in first comments since crushing Lions injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

After suffering a season-ending injury, Lions edge rusher Aidan Hutchinson promised to keep his celebrations grounded from here on out.

"That's the last time I go airborne," Hutchinson wrote in an Instagram post Thursday.

The post was the first time the star defensive end publicly shared his thoughts after tearing his ACL in Germany against the Patriots.

Detroit Lions defensive end Aidan Hutchinson (97) sits on the field after an injury against the Patriots in Munich. AP Photo/Matthias Schrader

In the first quarter, after sacking quarterback Drake Maye — also injured on the play — Hutchinson jumped into the air in celebration and landed awkwardly on his knee right after.

The sequence still puzzles Hutchinson, who dealt with a gruesome leg injury in 2024 that cost him his season.

"I honestly can't believe it. I've jumped hundreds of times on a football field, and never in a million years did I think this would happen to me," Hutchinson wrote.

"But even something like this won't hold me back."

Lions head coach Dan Campbell isn't letting the injury faze the team, as he didn't set any restrictions on celebrations and understands the injury was no more than a freak accident.

The timing comes as a crucial blow for the Lions, as star linebacker Jack Campbell is also on injury reserve after a scary neck injury against the Vikings.

Even without their two defensive cornerstones, Detroit (7-2) impressively handled business with a 35-10 win over New England (5-5).

Lions receiver Amon-Ra St. Brown took over the game with 98 receiving yards and two touchdowns, while running back Jahmyr Gibbs had 252 all-purpose yards and two rushing scores.

Detroit Lions edge rusher Aidan Hutchinson (97) celebrates a sack of Patriots quarterback Drake Maye (10). USA TODAY Network via Reuters Connect

The Lions' defense will have to prove it can withstand injuries again this week in a matchup against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers (3-6).
