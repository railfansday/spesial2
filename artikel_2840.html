Rams captain has blunt message for LA’s defense before Packers showdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Los Angeles Rams spent millions of dollars assembling a loaded defense in the offseason.

They signed an All-Pro cornerback in Trent McDuffie, acquired the reigning Defensive Player of the Year winner in Myles Garrett, and lured future Hall of Famer Aaron Donald out of retirement.

Now, as they head into their bye week, just one week away from a primetime Thanksgiving Eve showdown with the Green Bay Packers, it would be a good time for that investment to start paying dividends.

Trent McDuffie of the Los Angeles Rams stands on the field before an NFL football game against the Los Angeles Chargers in Inglewood, Calif., Nov. 1, 2026. Getty Images

Against Green Bay, and with their playoff hopes potentially hanging in the balance, the Rams can't afford to just stop Jordan Love and the Packers' offense, they need to create turnovers and McDuffie knows it.

"Our defense is sitting somewhere in the 20s for turnover ratio," McDuffie told The California Post in an exclusive interview. "So it's a big mindset this week: turnovers, turnovers."

He's close. The Rams are currently tied for 16th in opponents' turnovers in the NFL, and that's before the rest of the league plays their Week 11 slate of games.

But that message of needing to force turnovers carries even more weight heading into a nationally televised Thanksgiving Eve showdown between two teams tied for the NFC's seventh and final playoff spot.

The winner would seize control of the head-to-head tiebreaker. The loser could find itself chasing not only victories but favors from other teams over the final stretch.

The Rams' current injury crisis puts even more pressure on their defense heading into this crucial matchup.

Trent McDuffie #22 of the Los Angeles Rams stands on the sidelines prior to an NFL game against the Los Angeles Chargers in Inglewood, Calif., Nov. 1, 2026. Getty Images

The Rams will take the field against Green Bay without star receivers Puka Nacua and Davante Adams, along with starting center Coleman Shelton. Left guard Steve Avila and tight end Terrance Ferguson are also dealing with hamstring injuries, leaving their availability uncertain.

The Rams' offense has continued to find creative ways to replace those weapons, but if McDuffie, Garrett and Donald can help that offense with stops, sacks, turnovers, and field position, it could be the difference between making the playoffs and watching them from home come January.

Quentin Lake #37 of the Los Angeles Rams celebrates with teammates after an interception against the Los Angeles Chargers, Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, in Inglewood, Calif. Getty Images

"W​​hether it's picks, whether it's forced fumbles, whether it's turnovers on fourth down, whatever we can do to get the ball back to the offense and, like I said, produce this win," McDuffie said.

McDuffie, whose 98.9 impact grade leads all NFL cornerbacks according to Pro Football Network, understands the difference between a good defense and one capable of changing a season.

"As a defense, we're coming with the right mindset and attitude and understanding of what this game is," said McDuffie. "We have a really good opportunity. Everybody's kind of in this mode where it's 'fight for a win.' And you've got to love being in those battles."

The Rams' defense is coming off a 34-6 victory over the Arizona Cardinals in which they held them to no touchdowns and just two field goals. They will need that performance to carry over against the Packers if they want to keep their playoff hopes alive.

"This is a huge one for everybody," McDuffie said.
