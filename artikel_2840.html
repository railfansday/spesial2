Miami Dolphins overreaction Monday: Listen to all trade deadline calls

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

MIAMI GARDENS ― The Miami Dolphins lost to the Detroit Lions 19-9 to fall to 1-7.

Chris Johnson had his first career interception. Jacob Rodriguez had his first career sack.

The Dolphins defense allowed only one touchdown and has shown impressive progress.

It's never a bad time for an Overreaction Monday, so ... here goes.

Overreaction? No. As of Sunday night, Willis' NFL ranks were: passer rating (26th), passing yards (25th), passing TDs (24th) and yard per attempt (20th). He also is tied for the 8th-most interceptions in the league. That's not good enough and also below preseason expectations. There are reasons. There always are. Pressure. Young receivers. A few injuries that have hampered his running in recent games. But Willis has up to nine more games to show Miami they don't need to draft a first-round quarterback on April 29.

Overreaction? No. Linebacker Jacob Rodriguez, linebacker Jordyn Brooks, cornerback Chris Johnson and defensive tackle Jordan Phillips are four defensive players good enough for any team to win with. We will see how things play out for Kenneth Grant, Trey Moore, Michael Taaffe, Kyle Louis, Dante Trader and others. Chop Robinson is a unique situation only because of five career concussions, which complicates matters of the future. But Miami's defense was strong against the Lions and there are some foundational pieces.

Overreaction? No. Miami must listen closely to any and all offers for players like Austin Jackson, Zach Sieler, Josh Uche, Jaylen Wright, JuJu Brents, etc. The Dolphins have no choice but to try to add a pick or two to help foster the dramatic rebuild. The deadline is Tuesday, Nov. 10 at 4 p.m. Eastern.

Overreaction? No. The Dolphins missed a field goal, had a botched kickoff and allowed a fake punt completion to the Lions. We can feel a little bit of empathy for veteran special teams coach Chris Tabor, who is working with a room of kindergartners. But it has to be better. After the last game, coach Jeff Hafley said Zane Gonzalez cannot miss a short kick before halftime that would have tied the score at 6. This has been hard to watch. Really hard.

Overreaction? No. The Dolphins have upcoming games against the bad Colts, bad Jets and bad Chargers. Is a 4-13 finish possible? Sure. But a 3-14 finish is more probable. That would put Miami in the top two positions of the next NFL draft, which is helpful, considering Ohio State wide receiver Jeremiah Smith and Oklahoma State quarterback Drew Mestemaker may be the top two picks. There are also pass rushers from Texas and South Carolina who will be in that mix. Top 4 is OK. Top 3 is better. Top 2 is ideal.
