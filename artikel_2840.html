WATCH: Mason Rudolph connects with Metcalf for 22-yard TD vs Broncos

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

After being forced to punt on their first two drives, Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback Mason Rudolph led a four-play, 85-yard drive that ended in a 22-yard touchdown pass to wide receiver DK Metcalf to extend Pittsburgh's lead to 14-0 against the Denver Broncos.

Here is a look at Metcalf's touchdown, courtesy of the NFL's official social media:

Rudolph had multiple downfield passes on this drive, something that was lacking in the Steelers' offense with Aaron Rodgers at quarterback. So far, the offense is looking far better than they have the past few weeks, especially in their loss to the Philadelphia Eagles, where they only had one touchdown.

This game has major playoff implications for both teams. For Denver, they can stay in control of their playoff destiny and the No. 1 overall seed in the AFC with a win. For Pittsburgh, they stay in the wild card race and move into the No. 6 overall seed with a win. They can also put some pressure on the Cincinnati Bengals at the top of the division as the Bengals are currently 7-3 and the Steelers would be 7-4 with a win.
