Seahawks re-sign Deven Eastern to practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A member of the Seattle Seahawks' 2026 draft class is back, again.

After being waived to begin the week, the Seahawks signed seventh-round pick Deven Eastern to their practice squad on Tuesday, the team announced.

Eastern, selected with the team's penultimate selection in this year's draft, 242nd overall, has spent most of the season on Seattle's practice squad after he was waived during the cut down from 90 to 53.

Promoted to the active roster ahead of the Week 9 win over the Arizona Cardinals, as the Seahawks entered the game without Leonard Williams or Brandon Pili, Eastern was waived from the 53-man roster on Monday.

Despite spending two weeks on the active roster, Eastern did not get on the field on defense in Week 9 and was inactive for Seattle's Week 10 win in Las Vegas.

In a corresponding move, the Seahawks released quarterback and former UFL MVP Adrian Martinez from the practice squad, in the latest sign they expect Sam Darnold to be back in the fold as they emerge from their bye week.
