Rams S Kam Kinchens ejected; Packers WR Christian Watson evaluated for concussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

In the second quarter of the Green Bay Packers-Los Angeles Rams game, Rams safety Kam Kinchens was ejected for leaving his feet and leading with his helmet on a tackle attempt on Packers receiver Christian Watson. You can find video of the play below:

Initially, a cart came out for Watson, but the receiver was able to leave the field under his own power. The Packers are currently listing Watson as questionable to return and say that he's being evaluated for a concussion. To be honest, it looks like the biggest hit he ended up taking was when his head hit the turf on the way down, not necessarily Kinchens' blow to Watson's facemask.

Green Bay was not awarded a first down on this play due to offsetting penalties. Packers receiver Matthew Golden was called for offensive pass interference (you can only set a pick within a yard of the line of scrimmage), so they simply replayed the fourth-and-one play. After not getting a man coverage look with their running back's motion, like they had on the play that Watson was injured on, head coach Matt LaFleur called a timeout and elected to kick the football, setting up a 17-17 tie going into halftime.

We'll keep you updated when we hear more news on the Packers' injury situation.

Update: The Packers have now officially ruled Watson out with a concussion.
