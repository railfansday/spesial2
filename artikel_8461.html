Investing - Theory, News &amp; General • Re: AI Discussion and bubble

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

This discussion is not new. Folks have been debating and predicting an AI bubble for the last few years.

There have already been several Bogleheads threads on essentially the same concerns:

  • Evidence of AI Bubble? — started in mid-2024, discussing Nvidia and whether its rise was evidence of an AI bubble.
  • Mulling the AI "Bubble" — 2025, discussing whether investors should change their portfolios because of concerns about an AI bubble.
  • Howard Marks - The Memo - Is It a Bubble? — 2025.
  • Tech Stocks: This Time It Is Different (Or Not) — 2025, including concerns about AI capex, Nvidia, and whether the investments will ultimately generate sufficient returns.
So these concerns are not new information. The interesting question is whether the people making these predictions will ultimately be proven right, and if so, when.
