Sources: Newcastle and Sunderland in 'open race' for Sydie Peck

Newcastle United and Sunderland are in an "open race" to sign Sheffield United midfielder Sydie Peck, sources have told Football Insider.

The pair of bitter North East rivals are both keeping a close eye on the 22-year-old at the moment, but neither side are currently closing in on a deal.

Both clubs view Peck as a player with a "big ceiling" and feel that he could become a top-level talent in future.

Sources say that Newcastle and Sunderland are both in the market for a young midfielder to act as a long-term project, and Peck "fits the profile" as someone with a huge future.

The England U21 international has been a staple in Sheffield United's team in recent years, and played 40 games in the Championship last term.

Peck has already chipped in with four goals and two assists this season, as his reputation continues to grow with each passing week.

Both Newcastle and Sunderland are keen to get hold of Peck long-term, but they will have to be patient if they want to get their hands on him.

Football Insider revealed on Tuesday that Sheffield United have no intention of selling him in January, as they see him as a central part of their play-off push.

However, it could potentially be a different story next summer, with the youngster heading towards the end of his contract at Bramall Lane in 2028.

The Blades are "aware" of the growing interest in their star man, and are expecting even more Premier League clubs to move for him next summer.

Newcastle and Sunderland are both expected to make attempts to sign Peck, given their long-standing interest in the starlet.

Former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness told Football Insider last week that Newcastle and Leeds are the favourites to sign Coventry midfielder Jack Rudoni next summer.

The duo are both interested in the 25-year-old alongside the Toffees, after some impressive performances from him so far this season.

However, Wyness simply doesn't see Rudoni being a genuine target for his former club, given their existing depth in midfield.

The boardroom expert views Newcastle and Leeds as far more likely options for Rudoni, who is likely to remain in the Premier League next term, regardless of Coventry's eventual fate.
