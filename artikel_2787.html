Valkyries' Kiah Stokes wears T-shirt mocking ESPN at victory parade

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Few things drive athletes to greatness as much as a chip on their shoulder, and Golden State Valkyries center Kiah Stokes showed that off Wednesday at the team's WNBA championship parade.

Stokes arrived at the Golden State parade down San Francisco's Embarcadero sporting a T-shirt with ESPN's WNBA BPI, or basketball power index. The shirt refers to ESPN Analytics' July 26 ranking of which teams had the best chances to win the 2026 WNBA title. The Indiana Fever ranked No. 1, with a 24% chance, followed by the Las Vegas Aces, Minnesota Lynx, New York Liberty and Atlanta Dream. Conspicuously absent, of course, were the Valkyries.

The Valkyries clinched their first WNBA title on Saturday with a Game 7 win over the Dream. Stokes came ready to celebrate on Wednesday, spraying sparkling wine over the fans who lined the Embarcadero to cheer on the team. Stokes is no stranger to success; this is her fourth WNBA championship. She won her first three with the Aces in 2022, 2023 and 2025.

ESPN Insights, the home of ESPN's analytics division, posted a mea culpa on X on Wednesday. "Golden State was 6th on the leaderboard when we posted it on July 26, but went 13-4 from there!" the post read. "ESPN Analytics had the Valkyries as title favorites by August 10. Sorry we were a little late to the party."

If you're hoping to grab the shirt, it does appear to be a custom-made design. But perhaps Stokes could stand up a merchandise business, as Valkyries fans undoubtedly wouldn't mind the chance to buy their own.
