Cowboys have the perfect chance to bounce back against Tennessee

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Cowboys dropped to 4-6 after a wild 46-40 loss to San Francisco, but Sunday brings a much more favorable matchup. Tennessee comes to Dallas at 1-8, having lost its last three games and allowed 40-plus points in consecutive weeks. The defense struggled badly against San Francisco, forcing a desperate comeback that fell short.

Dak Prescott now has 2,724 yards and 21 touchdown passes this season. Tennessee has allowed 43 points to Cincinnati and 40 to Jacksonville in its last two games. This looks like another opportunity for Prescott and the offense to score heavily, especially after what George Pickens did against the 49ers.

Pickens returned against San Francisco and immediately produced his best game of the season. He caught three touchdowns and finished with 145 receiving yards. Now Tennessee has to deal with Pickens alongside CeeDee Lamb, who already has 976 receiving yards and eight touchdowns. The Titans simply don't have one receiver they can concentrate on stopping.

The numbers between these two team's offenses aren't close. Dallas averages 28 points per game, fifth in the NFL. Tennessee averages only 18, ranking 28th. The Cowboys also average 368 yards per game compared with Tennessee's 278. If Dallas gets into the high 20s or 30s again, Tennessee may struggle to keep pace. This is where having both Lamb and Pickens, along with players like Ryan Flournoy will come into play.

Perhaps the most interesting statistic is time of possession. The Titans average only 25:12 with the football, worst in the NFL. That gives Dallas exactly what it needs after watching San Francisco control huge parts of Sunday's game. Get Tennessee off the field and give Prescott, Lamb and Pickens as many possessions as possible.

Tennessee has some serious injury issues and placed starting guard Peter Skoronski on injured reserve because of a hamstring injury. Linebackers Anthony Hill Jr. and Cody Barton are dealing with injury, and wide receiver Carnell Tate has a big question mark on whether he'll suit up this Sunday. Wan'Dale Robinson is going on injured reserve.

The offensive line injuries should interest a Dallas pass rush that finally produced some numbers against San Francisco. They recorded five sacks against Brock Purdy, including four in the first half. Cam Ward is a talented young quarterback, but Dallas needs to make him uncomfortable behind a weakened offensive line. Tennessee's rookie quarterback can be pressured into mistakes Ward has shown improvement, but he is still learning.

Through nine games he has thrown eight touchdowns and six interceptions with an 80.7 passer rating. He threw another interception against Jacksonville on Sunday.

Then there's the past between these two teams. The Cowboys won the last time these teams met when they travelled to Tennessee in December 2022, coming away with a 27-13 victory. Now the Titans have to travel to AT&T Stadium while carrying one of the worst records in football and knowing they've lost four out of the last six meetings between these two teams.
