Russell hits out at Verstappen after Vegas collision: 'I won't accept it'

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

George Russell and Max Verstappen made contact during the final free practice session of the Las Vegas Grand Prix earlier today. The Briton explained that he would not accept the Dutchman's behavior.

Afterwards, the stewards confirmed that the Red Bull Racing driver was at fault for the incident and decided to hand him a warning.

After qualifying, Russell was clear in his verdict about what the Dutchman did earlier today when speaking to Sky Sports. The Mercedes driver commented: "Somebody's got to stand up to that guy!

"There is nothing in the rules to say you cannot overtake someone who is starting a lap, but when you do this, you are screwing this person over and he does this week in, week out with many drivers and everybody accepts it.

"I'm not just going to accept somebody to screw me over like this, and walk around like they are bigger and better than everybody else."

Verstappen and Russell finished second and third respectively in qualifying, as Kimi Antonelli set the fastest time in Q3. However, the Italian driver has a five-place grid penalty for Saturday's race in Las Vegas, which means that the old rivals will once again lock out the front row tomorrow.

During the FIA press conference after qualifying, there was also some tension when the two drivers were asked about the incident. "No confusion. I mean, it was pretty clear I think," Russell said. Verstappen then responded by saying "Yeah."
