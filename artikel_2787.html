Mikel Brown Jr.’s shot eludes him late another rough Nets loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Mikel Brown Jr. missed out on the Nets' first win of the season Sunday in Indiana, sitting as part of the team's plan to manage the back injury that forced him to miss 14 games during his lone season at Louisville.

His return Monday — and his Madison Square Garden debut — didn't go as he envisioned.

Brooklyn Nets guard Mikel Brown Jr. (0) reacts during the fourth quarter against the New York Knicks at Madison Square Garden on Monday, Nov. 2, 2026. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

After contributing 13 points on 5-for-9 shooting to give the Nets the lead entering the fourth quarter, the No. 6 overall pick fell apart in the final quarter of the 103-90 loss, missing each of the four 3-pointers he took while failing to attack the rim.

"I think Mikel's a rock star," head coach Jordi Fernández said. "He's gonna keep taking steps. And right now, we always want him to be aggressive, but he has the responsibility to run the team, and that's a big deal. And you have to communicate. … Every shot that is good, every shot that is not good, it falls a little bit on you, right?"

Brooklyn Nets guard Mikel Brown Jr. (0) brings the ball up court against New York Knicks guard Josh Hart (3) on Monday, Nov. 2, 2026, in New York. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

Brown, who finished 1-for-8 on 3-pointers, is now shooting 31.6 percent from the field through his first five games with the Nets.

"I'm still growing and developing and learning," Brown said. "I'm doing what I love every single night, and to be able to play in this type of environment, I'm just extremely blessed."
