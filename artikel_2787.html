D'Andre Swift's TD run gives Bears a 20-9 lead on Seahawks

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

They put together a 10-play, 69-yard touchdown drive after both teams opened the second half by going three-and-out. D'Andre Swift's 1-yard touchdown run, and Cairo Santos' PAT have the Bears leading 20-9 with 5:38 remaining in the third quarter.

Rome Odunze had catches of 23 and 22 yards to get the drive going.

Quarterback Caleb Williams is now 22-of-31 for 227 yards and a touchdown. Odunze has four catches for 87 yards, and Luther Burden has seven catches for 56 yards.

Swift has 16 carries for 39 yards and a touchdown.

Seahawks defensive lineman Leonard Williams was injured on a tackle of running back Roschon Johnson for no gain with 7:37 left in the third quarter. He is in the sideline medical tent, but the Seahawks have not updated his injury.
