‘Total Fail,’ ‘Needed to Be Live’ — NFL World Reacts to NBC’s ‘Poor Execution’ of Mike Tomlin’s Breakdown of Aaron Rodgers-Mike McCarthy Controversy

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Pittsburgh Steelers' decision to release Aaron Rodgers on Wednesday sparked widespread debate, with fans and analysts weighing in after the veteran quarterback went scorched earth on coach Mike McCarthy in an interview on "The Pat McAfee Show."

On Thursday, former Steelers head coach Mike Tomlin, who coached Rodgers last season, addressed the situation in an NBC sit-down with Mike Tirico during the halftime of the Buffalo Bills vs. Kansas City Chiefs Thanksgiving game. The Bills won the battle 13-10. Notably, the NFL world quickly reacted to Tomlin's comments after the halftime.

Former radio host John Ziegler gave a flattering review of Tomlin's interview.

"Mike Tomlin and Mike Tirico are two of the last real pros in their respective elements of the NFL business… This was well done, and far more fair to both sides of the Rogers/McCarthy saga than the vast majority of sports media have been," Ziegler wrote.

Meanwhile, Awful Announcing writer Drew Lerner ripped the "execution" of Tomlin's pre-recorded interview.

"Good idea, poor execution. This needed to be live. Came off as safe, and the abbreviated version on the telecast was choppy. Tomlin, as he does, still delivered a money quote calling the situation a dumpster fire," Lerner remarked.

Radio host Bob Fescoe also slammed NBC for "fumbling" the opportunity to interview Tomlin live.

"NBC had an opportunity that no one else had with Tomlin, and they fumbled big time. The interview should have been live and Tomlin should have been allowed to give his opinions in full. Not edited. Total fail by NBC," Fescoe criticized.

Notably, ESPN analyst and former NFL LB Will Compton did not mince his words about Tomlin's interview.

"Hate how produced and edited that piece was with Coach Tomlin," Compton complained.

Meanwhile, Barstool Sports Eric Nathan was unimpressed by NBC bumping the Goo Goo Dolls' halftime show in favor of the Tomlin interview. "NBC had a Johnny Rzeznik in the rain in Buffalo performance in the palm of their hand and threw it away for a crying Mike Tomlin," Nathan wrote.

Analyst Joshua Hoerner was fuming that fans missed out on the halftime show for a sanitized interview.

"We really only saw a minute of Iris for this???" Hoerner questioned.

Tomlin has a more balanced outlook on the Rodgers situation in Pittsburgh after coaching the four-time NFL MVP last year before stepping down in January. Rodgers went 10-6 in 16 starts last season as Pittsburgh finished 10-7 and won the AFC North.

Under Tomlin's tutelage, Rodgers amassed 3,322 passing yards on 65.7% completion, 24 touchdowns and 7 interceptions, earning a PFN QB Impact score of 71.4, 30th among 38 qualified quarterbacks.
