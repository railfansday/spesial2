Drake Maye reportedly suffered fractured fibula against Lions

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Five days after he departed the New England Patriots' game against the Detroit Lions with an injury, additional details about quarterback Drake Maye's status have emerged.

According to a report by Mike Reiss and Adam Schefter of ESPN, the 24-year-old suffered a fractured fibula without any additional ligament damage. The injury won't require surgery but is nonetheless expected to keep Maye on the sidelines "deep into the regular season," if he is able to return at all.

Maye exited the Patriots' game against the Lions after the opening drive. He was sacked on a third down by edge rusher Aidan Hutchinson, who subsequently tore his ACL while celebrating the takedown. Maye hobbled to the sidelines where he received additional treatment before exiting for the locker room. He was ruled out with an ankle injury shortly thereafter.

Due to the injury, Maye was unable to join his teammates on the flight back from Munich. The third-year QB eventually flew back to the United States after undergoing additional preparations.

"I wouldn't imagine him being available when we come out of the bye," head coach Mike Vrabel said earlier this week.

With Maye sidelined, the Patriots turned to Tommy DeVito at quarterback. DeVito completed 12 of 24 passes for 81 yards with a touchdown and an interception as New England ended up losing 35-10 to drop to 5-5 on the year.

The Patriots are currently on their bye and will visit the Los Angeles Chargers in Week 12.
