Mauricio Pochettino has six Man United problem positions to fix, one is on '£300,000-a-week' - ex-scout

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Mauricio Pochettino will hope a busy January transfer window can help him solve extensive Manchester United issues.

That is according to experienced former Tottenham, Everton and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The Argentine will arrive in Manchester on Wednesday after overseeing his final game in charge of the United States on Tuesday.

Pochettino has signed a contract until 2029 and will replace Michael Carrick, who was officially dismissed last week.

The former Tottenham boss' first immediate priority will be to stabilise the Red Devils amid what is one of the worst starts to a season in the club's history.

He will be well aware of the importance of the January transfer window, however, with his squad littered with holes.

Pochettino will face a baptism of fire on Sunday, with Man United set to take on Liverpool at Anfield.

And the difficult games do not stop there, with Brentford, Newcastle United and Arsenal to come in the weeks ahead.

"Now, the major problem for me with that side is that they're not good enough," King told Football Insider.

"You've got a captain there who never seems to enjoy playing football, and he's earning a fortune. When you're on £300k a week, as I know is the case, I don't think you should go out on a football field or meet anybody around that football club without a smile on your face, to be honest.

"He's got to sort it out. I don't think the goalkeeper's [Senne Lammens] good enough. The back four's all over the place, and they definitely need a centre forward."

"They've got enough players there to warrant that they should be at least mid-table," King added.

"I just think [it's wrong] what they've done and the way they're handling it, by spending all this money on removing one coaching team and a manager and bringing in another one who's done nothing.

"In England, he's done very little. His CV in England is no better than what Michael Carrick's is. Michael Carrick hasn't won anything, nor has Pochettino in England."

Much of the focus is on the players Pochettino's team may centre around, but he is not looking too far in the future for now.

"We are excited. I think the new challenge is a big challenge, but at the same time it’s adrenaline you want to feel," he said after his final game with the United States on Tuesday.

"We are going to have a different job, but I think it’s important that we have this experience [today with USMNT]. We need to bring our common sense for the way that we are going to approach things.

"We know the situation is not a situation that a club like Manchester United deserves to be. And for the fanbase, everything is amazing after we signed. In America, every 10 people that I cross in the street is a Manchester United fan. It’s amazing.”
