‘Dancing With the Stars’ Couples Perform Double Dances as Another Top-Scoring Duo Exits Before Season 35 Finale: See the Scores, Who Went Home

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Contestants performed a ballroom dance, and a second performance in the Latin style in Tuesday's semi-finals.

[This story contains spoilers for episode 11 of Dancing With the Stars season 35.]

Couples are competing for a chance to appear in the Dancing With the Stars season 35 finale next week, but first they have to impress in the semi-final round.

Contestants competed in two dances in the semi-finals: one in a ballroom dance style, and a second performance in the Latin style. With six couples left in the competition, that meant there would be a five-couple finale.

Harry Shum Jr. and Jenna Johnson were eliminated at the end of DWTS‘ semi-final episode. Another stunning elimination two weeks in a row, Harry and Jenna earned two perfect scores during Tuesday night’s episode, which left them tied at the top of the leaderboard. Ahead of next week’s episode, Alfonso Ribeiro announced that the finale will air live on both coasts; the show traditionally airs at 8 p.m. PT/ET, leaving West Coast viewers unable to tune in “live.” However, the DWTS season 35 finale will air at 5 p.m. PT/8 p.m. ET, also allowing West coast fans to vote while they tune in (voting typically only takes place during the East Coast airing of the show).

Celebrities who will compete for the season 35 Len Goodman mirrorball trophy include Tatyana Ali, Ezra Frech, Amber Glenn, Maura Higgins, and Connor Wood.

The semi-finals were jam-packed with perfect scores, as Harry and Jenna; Maura and Mark Ballas; and Amber and Pasha Pashkov all earned a respective 30/30 in the first round of dancing.

Connor and Rylee Arnold earned all eights during the first round, making for a score of 24/30, while Ezra and Daniella Karagach received nines across the board, bringing their total to 27/30. Tatyana and Jan Ravnik also matched that score, earning a 27/30 for their Waltz.

During the second round of performances, Connor and Rylee matched their first-round score of 24/30. Maura and Mark trailed their first score with a 29/30 for their Viennese waltz, while Ezra and Daniella earned their first perfect score for their Jive to “Footloose.”

Amber and Harry were the two celebrities to earn perfect scores for both of their performances of the semi-finals, making them tied for the top of the leaderboard. Tatyana and Jan also earned their first 10 from Carrie Ann Inaba for their Cha cha, bringing their second dance mark to 28/30.

Harry’s final dance was a Quickstep to “Valerie,” an homage to his time on Glee. He and Jenna specifically danced to the version of the track performed by Naya Rivera, whom Harry shouted out after the performance.

“I feel like Dancing With the Stars is synonymous with Glee, and to be able to dance to Naya’s version, it means so much to so many people, including myself,” Harry said. “We miss you, baby girl.”

Jenna Dewan was shockingly eliminated from DWTS last week during the Grammy Night episode, which also scored the best ratings of the season to date. The episode averaged 7.39 million viewers on ABC and Disney+ in Nielsen’s final same-day ratings.

The couple with the lowest combined total of judges’ scores and viewer votes is eliminated at the end of each DWTS episode. Each viewer is allowed up to 10 votes per couple, per voting method (via ABC’s website and by text). Live voting takes place during the East Coast airing of each show and ends shortly after the final performance; that means if you’re a West Coast viewer and want to vote, you must do so between 5 p.m. PT and roughly 6:45 p.m. PT, as voting ends after the final performance.
