Musiala sidelined ‘until further notice’ with hamstring injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Jamal Musiala will be "out of action until further notice" after suffering a hamstring injury, Bayern Munich have confirmed.

Musiala has been restricted to just six appearances in all competitions this season, only two of which have been starts, due to a variety of issues.

In August, he revealed he had been diagnosed with "easily treatable absence seizures" after collapsing twice on the pitch, during friendlies against Heidenheim and RB Leipzig.

Musiala did not return to action until September, but he subsequently suffered a torn muscle fibre in his right thigh while on international duty with Germany and returned to his club early.

The attacking midfielder has made back-to-back substitute appearances in Bayern's last two games – a 1-1 draw with Koln and a 2-1 defeat to Lille – but he now faces another stint on the sidelines.

A statement issued by the club on social media said: "FC Bayern's medical team have confirmed on Friday that Jamal Musiala has sustained a muscular injury in his right hamstring and will therefore be out of action until further notice.

Musiala also saw his 2025-26 campaign blighted by injury, making just 12 starts in all competitions after breaking his leg during Bayern's quarter-final defeat to Paris Saint-Germain at the 2025 Club World Cup.

Bayern were already without Harry Kane, Ismael Saibari, Michael Olise, Serge Gnabry and Alphonso Davies due to injury, though the latter trio are not viewed as long-term issues, with Gnabry and Davies returning to training on Friday.
