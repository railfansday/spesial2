'What this shows...' - Keith Wyness shares verdict on Man City's new discounted Etihad deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Manchester City are being unfairly criticised for their new discounted lease deal for the Etihad Stadium.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who has an element of “sympathy” for the Citizens in this case.

Recent reports have suggested that City’s lease on their council-owned stadium will increase by just £33,000 each year, despite recent upgrades taking its capacity to over the 60,000 mark.

Wyness believes that City have earned the discounted rate from the local council as a result of the development in the local areas that they have contributed towards in recent years.

Reports suggest that City’s stadium expansion will earn them an extra £11million a year in terms of ticket revenue, but that is not reflected by their latest lease agreement.

The situation is facing plenty of scrutiny from fans across the Premier League right now, but Wyness believes that the club do not deserve to be criticised.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that City’s ownership group’s investment in the local area has helped them secure favourable terms on their lease.

Sheikh Mansour has been heavily involved in the funding of several development and building projects across Manchester over the last few years.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider’s Inside Track podcast, Wyness outlined how City’s controversial lease deal is merely a return on that investment.

“I actually have some sympathy here because if you look at what the owners have invested in the whole area, if you look at the Co-op Live, you're looking at the whole campus they've built, there's a lot of redevelopment that has gone into that area,” he said.

“And that's obviously been reflected in terms of the lease terms that they did. To me, what this shows is that there are lessons for the public authorities to learn from when they're negotiating these contracts.

“Now of course, as I say, you can't look at a good lease deal without looking at what the promises were to reinvest in the whole region, in the whole area. So the two things can't be decoupled.

“I think you're going to look at the overall redevelopment in the whole and that's been a big part of Manchester's development as say.”

Former Manchester United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that City goalkeeper Gianluigi Donnarumma has set “alarm bells” ringing behind the scenes with recent poor form.

Mistakes have crept into the Italian’s game so far this season, and he is not the calming presence that he once was for City right now.

Brown believes that Donnarumma is becoming a “serious problem” for Enzo Maresca and Co., given the lofty wage that he occupies at the Etihad.

The 27-year-old is widely regarded as one of the best goalkeepers in the world, but he has been a far cry from that level of late.
