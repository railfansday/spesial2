Stefan Borson shares Liverpool January transfer verdict as Jeff Bezos and co. spot 'opportunity'

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Liverpool's new investors led by Jeff Bezos will have to be "opportunistic" in the January transfer window to make improvements to the current squad.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that Andoni Iraola's squad is still "a little bit short" this season.

After 10 games, the Reds are sitting fourth in the Premier League table, nine points behind leaguer leaders Arsenal, while also sitting eighth in the Champions League.

Iraola has Jurgen Klopp's incredible legacy to live up to, but with just two wins in Liverpool's last five games, it has not been the perfect start to life at Anfield.

Supporters are already suggesting areas of the pitch that need improving on in January, but some might be disappointed by the end result.

Mick Brown told Football Insider that Bradley Barcola's early struggles have set off some alarm bells, forcing the owners to retreat on the idea of spending big again.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson has revealed that he also finds it hard to believe that the Reds would spend big in the upcoming transfer window but does not rule out a major signing.

"Well, first off, it's really hard to spend in January, we know that. You've got to be pretty opportunistic," he said.

"They're probably a little bit short in terms of what they've got in that squad, and I think maybe the spending will depend on the involvement of the new ownership and how aggressive they want to be.

"It may be that they say, 'We need to go for it a bit'. And it could be that they're in the running. Obviously, at the moment, they don't look like they're going to be close to Arsenal.

"But you never know. And if let's say, in mid-January, it looks like they're in the mix or if they're doing particularly well in the Champions League...

"It's not impossible that they go, 'Well, let's see what happens if we bring in an opportunity'."

Just because Liverpool have been tipped not to spend big doesn't mean that Iraola won't be able to welcome some new faces into the squad.

Football Insider understands that Brighton's Bart Verbruggen is already being targeted, but that seems like a move that can wait until the summer.

Lamine Camara remains a talking point at Anfield, and Football Insider's latest understanding is that Monaco are ready to accept an offer.

He would undoubtedly be the dream signing, given Ryan Gravenberch's struggles so far this season, but he wouldn't be a cheap one, that's for sure.
