Wizards at Magic final score: Washington wins 103-94 to go above .500

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Tbe Washington Wizards defeated the Orlando Magic on Monday night, 103-94. The win pushes Washington to a 4-3 record and past .500.

Anthony Davis led the Wizards with 27 points, while Kyshawn George added 22 points and AJ Dybantsa scored 21. It's a career high for the rookie. Of all the baskets, I think this one was the best exclamation point for the night

Washington's third quarter was also an X-Factor. They held Orlando to just 9 point and a sub 20 percent shooting performance ater the second half began. After the game, Davis also broke down why that third quarter was a major turning point.

For the Magic, Franz Wagner led with 24 points. Orlando fell to a 2-5 record after the contest.
