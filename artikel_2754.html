Patriots’ closest playoff rival descends into chaos amid Aaron Rodgers drama

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

In less than 24 hours, the New England Patriots' closest playoff rivals went from benching their starting quarterback to full-on implosion.

After the team announced it had benched Aaron Rodgers favor of backup Mason Rudolph, the veteran quarterback stayed away from the team facility and later went on ESPN's Pat McAfee Show to air his grievances. And there were quite a few, mostly aimed at head coach Mike McCarthy. Pittsburgh is now expected to cut Rodgers.

As a reminder, the 6-4 Steelers are currently the No. 7 playoff seed in the AFC — one game ahead of the eighth-seeded 5-5 Patriots.

Losers of back-to-back games, the Pittsburgh Steelers have decided on a drastic move. Starting quarterback Aaron Rodgers will not be under center for the upcoming game against the Denver Broncos, with backup Mason Rudolph elevated to the top spot on the depth chart.

Rodgers, meanwhile, will be inactive. The organization announced the benching of the future Hall of Famer on Tuesday night.

Rodgers, 42, is in his second season in Pittsburgh and has led the team to a 6-4 record and the current seventh seed in the AFC playoff picture. The Steelers are one game ahead of the closest non-postseason team at the moment, the eighth-seeded New England Patriots.

In his 10 starts this year, Rodgers completed 60.9% of his passes for 2,241 yards, 16 touchdowns and six interceptions. He is ranked 29th among qualifying quarterbacks with -0.022 expected points added per play.

Head coach Mike McCarthy's decision comes after Pittsburgh's offense has scored just three total touchdowns in its last two contest, defeats to the Browns and Eagles. On the year, the unit is ranked 22nd in the league with a scoring average of 20.7 points per game.

The Steelers' AFC rivals will naturally keep a close eye on the situation. The team entered Wednesday with a 73% chance of making the postseason, according to The Athletic's playoff simulator, but Rodgers' benching might end. up impacting the team's outlook. If so, the Patriots — whose own postseason chance is a mere 29% — would be among the first candidates to pounce.
