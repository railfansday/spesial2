Scott Gardner proud of the performance despite Fleetwood defeat

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Leeds United Under-21s saw their Jersey Mike's Trophy campaign end in the group stages, after a defeat away at Fleetwood Town on Wednesday evening.

The Sky Bet League Two side secured the three points they needed to progress into the knock-out stages, in a game that was all to play for. Scott Gardner's young side had put themselves in with a chance of qualification, but needed a result on the night at the Highbury Stadium, which ultimately they did not get.

Speaking after the game, Gardner reflected on a spirited performance from his team.

He said: "I thought we were very good and I thought we deserved something from the game.

"We looked much more like ourselves tonight compared to the Oldham game and we had enough moments to win it.

"But, you know, those moments are key, so if you don't take your chances, senior players find a way. They got two goals in the last 10 minutes, it's a shame for the players, but there's loads we can take from it.

"We knew that there was a respect from them [Fleetwood] and obviously a desire to go through. But our players showed they're more than capable across the board, and hopefully people can see that and the effort they put in.

"I'm really proud of this group and how they work every day and wanted to get through, but we have to go again. I am disappointed for them they haven't got the reward of going through, but, you know, credit for how they performed."
