49ers backup gives real reason for hope after stalwart’s season-ending injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Right tackle Colton McKivitz might be one of the 49ers' most underrated players, making his ugly season-ending ankle injury difficult to overcome the rest of the season.

But San Francisco should be encouraged about what they saw from McKivitz's backup in his first action Sunday night.

San Francisco 49ers' Christian McCaffrey #23 is congratulated by Vederian Lowe #76 after scoring a touchdown Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026, in Mexico City, Mexico. Getty Images

Veteran Vederian Lowe was thrust into the lineup against the Minnesota Vikings and held his own against coordinator Brian Flores' vaunted front seven.

Lowe allowed zero pressures in 18 pass-protection snaps, according to Pro Football Focus, who gave him a positive 72.3 grade in 19 run-blocking opportunities.

San Francisco 49ers offensive tackle Colton McKivitz (68) goes down with an injury during the first half of an NFL football game in Mexico City, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. (AP Photo/Eduardo Verdugo) AP Photo/Eduardo Verdugo

The 49ers signed Lowe in March to a two-year, $12-million contract, investing in him to become their swing tackle behind McKivitz and star left tackle Trent Williams.

He joined San Francisco after three years with the New England Patriots, where he started 25 career games, including four during last season's run to the Super Bowl.

San Francisco 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy (13) hands off to running back Christian McCaffrey (23) against the Minnesota Vikings in Mexico City, Nov. 22, 2026. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

He was originally a sixth-round draft pick of the Vikings in 2022 and was traded to New England for a sixth-round pick in 2024.

The offensive line was hardly the Patriots' strength during Lowe's time there, and the team used the No. 4 overall pick in 2025 on LSU standout Will Campbell to play left tackle after Lowe played 803 snaps over 14 games the year prior.

McKivitz was an under-the-radar success story for San Francisco and he was playing arguably the best football of his career before the injury. His PFF grade ranked No. 7 among offensive tackles for the season while he allowed no sacks and just 12 pressures on 307 pass blocking snaps.

Lowe has big shoes to fill and has another difficult matchup on Sunday against the Seattle Seahawks, who are coming off a bye and would love revenge after losing to the 49ers on their home field Oct. 11.
