The Daily: Red Wings Skid Hits Four; Michkov Leak by Weekes

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Detroit Red Wings lost their fourth game in a row 3-2 last night to the St. Louis Blues despite one of John Gibson's best performances of the year in his return from injury. Mo Seider brought the Red Wings within one goal with just over a minute to spare in the game, but Detroit was ultimately unable to generate the offensive push to send the game to overtime in Justin Faulk's first return to Enterprise Center.

Last night also saw the return of Marco Kasper and Mason Appleton to the lineup, though neither were able to provide the Red Wings with the energy needed to win the game. Kasper started on the third line, though he did see some time on the top line with Emmitt Finnie and Alex DeBrincat. If the change remains for the Red Wings Thursday night matchup is yet to be seen, however.
