Broncos-Steelers thread discussion: How many references to Aaron Rodgers?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Black Friday football is back with the Broncos and Steelers facing off on Amazon Prime. The game kicks off at 12 p.m. PT and both teams are competing for playoff positioning.

Of course, it's safe to say a lot of people will be talking about something else with this game. The Steelers released Aaron Rodgers after he went scorched earth on head coach Mike McCarthy. It's unclear what is in Rodgers' future, but it won't be with the Steelers. Mason Rudolph is starting in his place.

The Broncos can take a one-game lead on the Chiefs in the AFC West with a win. The Steelers can move into sixth place in the AFC standings with a win. But we'll see what the announcers are talking about for much of this game.
