Updated Missouri football bowl game projections after Texas loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The College Football Playoff is now almost certainly off the table for the Tigers.

While a three-loss team could very well make it into the 12-team field next month, it's unlikely to be Missouri football after its 34-20 loss to Texas on Saturday in Columbia. The Tigers were already at the bottom of a large two-loss bunch out of the SEC in the CFP's initial top 25 rankings, and another defeat is not going to help the Tigers' cause.

The Tigers next head to face newly minted No. 1 Georgia on Nov. 14 in Athens, Georgia, and then Eli Drinkwitz's team closes the regular season out on home turf with back-to-back games in Columbia against Kentucky and Oklahoma.

With three games left to play in the regular season, here is where various national media outlets have Missouri projected to play in the postseason in their updated bowl projections:

Mark Schlabach of ESPN has the Tigers heading to Las Vegas for a bowl game against Arizona, while his colleague, Kyle Bonagura, has Mizzou going to Houston for a matchup with Baylor in the Kinder's Texas Bowl.

Mizzou has never played a bowl game in Las Vegas, and the Tigers haven't played Arizona in any game since 1967. The Las Vegas Bowl is scheduled for Dec. 31 at Allegiant Stadium.

Mizzou's last trip to the Texas Bowl was for a 2017 loss to — shield your eyes now, MU Fans — Texas. This year's Texas Bowl will be played Dec. 31 at Reliant Stadium.

Brad Crawford at CBS Sports has Missouri slotted in the Texas Bowl this week, setting up a game against old Big 12 rival Oklahoma State.

The Tigers have faced OSU — now coached by Eric Morris — in two bowl games since joining the SEC: the 2014 Cotton Bowl and the 2018 Liberty Bowl.

Bryan Fischer at Sports Illustrated has the Tigers traveling for a Jan. 2 game in Memphis, home of the Liberty Bowl, for a matchup with UCF.

UCF, which just beat Kansas 33-32 on a last-second touchdown pass, still needs one more win to become bowl eligible this year. Scott Frost is the Knights' head coach.

For the second straight week, Athlon Sports' Steven Lassan has Mizzou headed to Houston for a game with Oklahoma State in the Texas Bowl. The Cowboys are 6-3 this season.

Scott Dochterman at the The Athletic has Mizzou in Memphis for a game against Rich Rodriguez's West Virginia team in the Liberty Bowl.

Missouri beat the Mountaineers in the 1998 Insight Bowl, and the teams played a home-and-home series with games in 2016 and 2019.
