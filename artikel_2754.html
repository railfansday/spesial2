Tyler Shough is the Saints' leading rusher, with 187 yards this season

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

As Saints quarterback Tyler Shough struggles through his second season, a good running attack could help take some pressure off him. Unfortunately, the Saints’ leading rusher is Shough himself.

That might be OK if running the ball were Shough’s strong suit, but he has just 187 yards rushing this season. Shough is the leading rusher in New Orleans because the Saints haven’t found a running back who can establish himself as a consistent threat running the ball, not because he’s doing anything special himself.

The Saints have given five different running backs a significant number of carries this season, but none has emerged as the lead back: Alvin Kamara has 49 carries for 161 yards. Kendre Miller has 57 carries for 159 yards. Travis Etienne has 38 carries for 142 yards. CJ Donaldson has 17 carries for 81 yards. Audric Estime has 22 carries for 78 yards.

Every other team in the NFL has at least one running back with more rushing yards than Kamara, the Saints’ leader. Most teams have two or more. For the Saints, relying on Shough to be their leading rusher is not a successful way to run an offense.
