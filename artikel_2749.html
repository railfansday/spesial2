Wyoming coach Jay Sawvel will serve 1-game suspension for UConn game for NCAA tampering violations

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

LARAMIE, Wyoming (AP) — Wyoming coach Jay Sawvel will serve a one-game suspension for tampering and will miss Saturday's visit from UConn.

Defensive ends coach Brian Hendricks will lead the Cowboys (7-4) against the Huskies.

The school said it imposed the suspension after collaborating with NCAA enforcement staffers. The violations are tied to improper communication with seven athletes and their representatives in December 2025, coming after those athletes had publicly announced via social media that they intended to transfer but had not yet entered the transfer portal.

In its announcement Tuesday, the school said it self-reported the violations. The case was resolved through the NCAA's negotiated resolution process, which is used when parties agree on facts, level of violations and penalties as a way to streamline infractions cases.

The school said athletic director Tom Burman would comment further on the case once the NCAA's infractions committee issues a final report.
