UFC 334 faceoff: Ciryl Gane, Josh Hokit exchange words at Times Square

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

NEW YORK – The big stars of UFC 334 came face-to-face in one of the world's most famous tourist destinations.

With fight week activities now underway, the main and co-main event title fight competitors faced off at Times Square on Tuesday evening. UFC 334 headliners Ciryl Gane and Josh Hokit got a good early look at each other as they squared off at one of New York City's most popular attractions.

Both were dressed warmly with glasses on. Gane (14-2 MMA, 11-2 UFC) wore a leather jacket with a hoodie underneath, and Hokit (10-0 MMA, 4-0 UFC) wore a New York Yankees jersey, also with a hoodie underneath. As they got face to face, the glasses were removed, and words were exchanged as fans gathered around cheered for the intense faceoff moment.
