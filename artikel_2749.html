Tennessee versus Kentucky football game officials

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

No. 16 Tennessee (6-2, 3-2 SEC) will host No. 7 Kentucky (7-1, 5-1 SEC) on Saturday in Week 10. Rankings reflect the US LBM Coaches Poll.

"Coach (Will) Stein, their staff, they've done a really good job," Tennessee head coach Josh Heupel said of facing Kentucky. "Some personnel that's been there, they've added some personnel, but at the end of the day, I think the major thing is that they're playing smart football.

"They're playing all three phases of the game. Quarterback that doesn't turn it over. They've created turnovers. That turnover margin is one of the best in the country."
