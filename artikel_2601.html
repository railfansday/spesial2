From fastest double century to most sixes: List of records Vaibhav Sooryavanshi shattered with his 204 against UAE

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi produced an innings for the ages as he rewrote the record books with a breathtaking 204 off just 53 balls against the UAE in the ACC Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026 on Tuesday. The 15-year-old India A opener smashed 28 sixes and seven fours, registering one of the most extraordinary batting performances in professional cricket.Sooryavanshi's incredible knock helped India A chase down a target of 250 in just 14.2 overs, securing a seven-wicket victory. In the process, the youngster broke multiple records across List A and professional cricket, setting new benchmarks for batting strike rates, fastest centuries, double centuries and six-hitting.Here is a look at the records Sooryavanshi created with his sensational innings.

Sooryavanshi registered a staggering strike rate of 384.90, the highest ever in a List A innings featuring a century. He surpassed former South Africa captain AB de Villiers, who had held the record for over a decade after scoring 149 off 44 balls at a strike rate of 338.63 against the West Indies in Johannesburg in 2015.Australia's Jake Fraser-McGurk occupies the third position with his 125 off 38 balls at a strike rate of 328.94 for South Australia against Tasmania in 2023.

The India A opener smashed the fastest double century in List A cricket, reaching the 200-run mark in just 52 balls. He comfortably surpassed the previous record held by Chad Bowes, who had taken 103 deliveries to score a double century for Canterbury against Otago in Christchurch in 2024.Tamil Nadu's N Jagadeesan and England's Harry Brook are next on the list, having reached their respective double centuries in 114 balls.

Sooryavanshi smashed an astonishing 28 sixes, setting a new record for the most maximums hit in a single innings across professional cricket. He went past Tanmay Agarwal's previous record of 26 sixes, scored for Hyderabad against Arunachal Pradesh in a Ranji Trophy match in 2024.Colin Munro and D'Arcy Short had previously struck 23 sixes each in their respective innings.

The teenager also set a new record for the most sixes in a single List A innings with his 28 maximums against the UAE. Australia's D'Arcy Short previously held the record with 23 sixes for Western Australia against Queensland in 2018.Bevon Jacobs is third on the list with 21 sixes for Auckland against Otago in 2026.

Sooryavanshi reached his 150-run mark in just 40 balls, registering the fastest 150 in List A cricket. Interestingly, he also occupies the second position on the list, having scored 150 in 59 balls for Bihar against Arunachal Pradesh in 2025.De Villiers had previously held the record with his 64-ball 150 against the West Indies in Johannesburg in 2015, while Jos Buttler reached the milestone in 65 balls against the Netherlands in 2022.

Sooryavanshi brought up his century in just 28 balls, registering the fastest hundred in List A cricket. He broke Jake Fraser-McGurk's record of 29 balls, set for South Australia against Tasmania in 2023.De Villiers had previously held the third spot with his 31-ball century against the West Indies in 2015, while Bihar's Sakibul Gani and Jharkhand's Ishan Kishan had also registered centuries in 32 and 33 balls, respectively.

Sooryavanshi's 28-ball hundred against the UAE also became the fastest century of his professional career. His previous best was a 32-ball hundred for India A against the UAE in a T20 match in 2025.He has also scored centuries off 35 balls for Rajasthan Royals against Gujarat Titans in 2025 and 36 balls for Bihar against Arunachal Pradesh in List A cricket in 2025, as well as for Rajasthan Royals against Sunrisers Hyderabad in 2026.
