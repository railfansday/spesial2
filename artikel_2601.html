Who is performing the halftime show for Eagles-Cowboys on Thanksgiving?

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It's Thanksgiving Day, meaning food, family and football. There are back-to-back-to-back NFL games on Thursday, starting with an NFC North battle between the Bears and Lions, both holding 8-2 records atop the division. The second game of the day takes us to Dallas, with the Cowboys hosting the 3-7 Philadelphia Eagles.

Wednesday night's game had U2 as the halftime show, and the Wu-Tang Clan is performing for the fans in Detroit from the early game.

So who is entertaining the crowd at AT&T Stadium in Arlington on Thursday?

That would be none other than country star Miranda Lambert, who will be singing a medley of her hits for The Salvation Army's Red Kettle Kickoff.

Lambert is a three-time Grammy Award winner, including two Best Country Album wins. She's also nominated for seven Grammys in February's upcoming awards show.

The NFC East rivalry game starts at 4:30 p.m. ET and will be aired on NBC.
