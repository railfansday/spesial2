Duratec Limited (DURCF) Shareholder/Analyst Call November 25, 2026 10:00 PM EST

Martin BrydonDennis Wilkins - Company SecretaryAshley Muirhead - Chief Financial OfficerChristopher Oates - MD & Executive Director

Good morning, everyone. My name is Martin Brydon. I am the Non-Executive Chair of your company and the Chair of this meeting. It is 11:00 a.m. Australian Western Standard Time, and I now call this Annual General Meeting of Duratec Limited to order. Today's meeting is being held in a virtual format, allowing all shareholders to participate via the Computershare online meeting platform. On behalf of the Board, I would like to welcome all participants to this Annual General Meeting of Duratec Limited, its sixth since -- as an ASX-listed company.

I would like to begin today by acknowledging the Noongar people, the traditional custodians of the land on which we gather and pay our respects to elders past, present and emerging.

Joining me here today are my fellow directors: Chris Oates, Managing Director and Non-Executive Directors Gavin Miller, Phil Harcourt, Emma Scotney and Jamie Cullen. We also have Dennis Wilkins, our Company Secretary; and Ashley Muirhead, the company's Chief Financial Officer, in the room with us.

I would also like to welcome Tutu Phong and Karien Erasmus from RSM Australia Partners, the company's auditor, who are joining us online this morning.

Before we commence today's formal meeting proceedings, I would like to provide a brief recap of the business performance for FY '26 and discuss the success of Duratec's strategic business model. Following my address, we will attend to all items outlined in the Notice of Meeting. Following the meeting's formal proceedings, our Managing Director will provide a more detailed overview of the company's performance in FY '26 and the commencement of FY '27.
