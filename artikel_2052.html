The best combined Southampton & Middlesbrough XI right now - Jack Stephens axed

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Middlesbrough travel to St Mary's to take on automatic promotion rivals Southampton on Friday night, with the two clubs meeting for the first time since the now-infamous 'spygate' scandal, in what is shaping up to be a feisty affair.

As things stand, Middlesbrough sit top of the Championship table, level on points with Stoke City but with a superior goal difference, as Kim Hellberg's side look to extend their lead at the top of the division when they travel to Southampton on Friday night.

This will be the first time the two clubs have met since the 'spygate' scandal took place, with the Saints being expelled from the play-offs despite beating Boro over two legs.

Although that occurred some six months ago, it will still be fresh in the memories of not only both sets of supporters, but of players and coaches alike, and with both teams targeting automatic promotion this year, tensions will be high.

Both sides can boast some exceptional talent across the board, as Football League World takes a closer look at the two teams and attempts to create an overall best XI from these two Championship behemoths.

For the purpose of this XI, a 4-2-3-1 shape will be used, with both sides having often deployed that formation this campaign.

In between the sticks, our pick is Southampton's Daniel Peretz, just about pipping Radek Vitek to a place in the side.

Peretz joined the Saints in January 2026, initially on loan from Bayern Munich, as Eckert looked to replace Gavin Bazunu in goal with a safe pair of hands, and with the 26-year-old, he certainly succeeded.

According to FotMob, the Israeli international prevented 6.54 goals during the second half of last season, among the very best records in the Championship despite a lack of minutes in comparison to the other keepers.

Unsurprisingly, Southampton would make the move permanent in the summer for just £6.05 million, and he has continued to impress on the South Coast with his displays, taking his place as our number one.

Beginning our back four at right-back is Middlesbrough's Callum Brittain, with the 28-year-old our pick as the first defender just ahead of James Bree.

Bree was recalled from his loan at Charlton Athletic by Southampton in January 2026 as one of Eckert's first big decisions at the helm, and since then, he has been an almost ever-present in the side.

However, Brittain gets the nod in this XI, being equally as important for Hellberg on Teesside ever since his move from Blackburn Rovers in the summer of 2025, barely missing a game and captaining the side on occasion.

In his prime years, Brittain is among the best and most underrated full-backs the Championship has to offer, and whilst a solid argument for Bree's inclusion over him could be made, it's the Boro man who gets the nod for us.

Adilson Malanda agreed to join Middlesbrough in the summer of 2025 from MLS side Charlotte, having emerged as one of the best defenders in North America, but wouldn't officially make the move to Teesside until the following January.

The Frenchman, since his arrival, has been a constant in Hellberg's side, with his calm nature and technical ability a standout feature of his game, allowing him to move into midfield if required.

An excellent one-on-one defender as well, the 25-year-old has proven to be an excellent pickup from Boro, and whilst Ashley Phillips and Luke Ayling will put forward arguments to be included in this side, Malanda's inclusion was never really up for debate.

German defender Keven Schlotterbeck joined Southampton in the summer, essentially replacing the outgoing Taylor Harwood-Bellis, who joined Aston Villa for around £30 million.

Signed from Augsburg for around a tenth (£3 million) of what they sold Harwood-Bellis for, Southampton's defence hasn't gotten any worse since the introduction of the 29-year-old, and some may argue it has actually improved.

The brother of Borussia Dortmund and German international Nico Schlotterbeck, Keven has forged an excellent career for himself, with over 150 Bundesliga appearances, and now has taken his talents to St Mary's, where he has emerged as one of the best centre-backs in the division this season.

Jack Stephens, Nathan Wood, and Zach Abbott may feel as though they have arguments to be involved, though our pick for this spot is Schlotterbeck.

Completing the back line is another Southampton player in the form of Ryan Manning, with the Irishman having established himself as one of the best full-backs in the division over the past few years.

The 30-year-old first made the move to England with Queens Park Rangers in January 2015, and across spells with the R's, Rotherham United, Swansea City, and now Southampton, is approaching 300 Championship appearances; he could make his 300th appearance in the division against Middlesbrough.

Like Bree, Manning has been a constant in the Saints side since the German's appointment in late 2025, and has proven to be excellent both going forwards and in defence, take his place in this side.

Neto Borges and Max Arfsten can make claims, though our pick to complete the back four is the Irishman.

Beginning our midfield pairing is USMNT international Aidan Morris, who, over the past few years, has quietly become one of, if not the best midfielders in the entire Championship.

Joining Middlesbrough from MLS side Columbus Crew in the summer of 2024 for around $4 million (£3.14 million), the 25-year-old was often out of the limelight at the expense of midfield partner Hayden Hackney, but following Hackney's departure to Everton in the recent window, he has taken the mantle.

When fit, Morris is one of the first names on the Middlesbrough team sheet, with his presence in the middle of the park almost irreplaceable for the Swedish head coach, capable of breaking up play, dictating tempo, and, more recently, becoming more of a goal threat.

Bundesliga side Stuttgart have reportedly registered an interest in recent weeks, though Boro will have no intention of parting ways with Morris anytime soon, especially with Premier League football on the line.

Joining Morris in the midfield is current teammate and compatriot Sebastian Berhalter, who, having made the move to England in the summer, has adapted seamlessly to the Championship and looks a cut above.

Capped 24 times by the USA men's national team, appearing at the World Cup and scoring, Berhalter has replaced Hackney in Middlesbrough's midfield with ease, and if Boro are to achieve any sort of success this year, he will almost certainly be pivotal to those efforts.

A midfield duo of Morris and Berhalter will be argued by Saints supporters, who will believe the likes of Caspar Jander, Flynn Downes, and James Ward-Prowse deserve a spot, but we believe the Boro duo just about edge it here.

A player both sides will know well, Irish attacking midfielder Finn Azaz began his career with West Bromwich Albion, but having failed to make a single senior appearance for the Baggies, he made a name for himself through spells with Cheltenham Town, Newport County, Plymouth Argyle, Middlesbrough, and now Southampton.

With Boro, Azaz scored 17 and assisted 17 in 68 games across all competitions for the club, averaging nearly a direct goal contribution every other game before joining Southampton in the summer of 2025 for around £14 million.

Azaz has continued his consistently excellent output at St Mary's, with 14 goals and 16 assists in 66 games across all competitions for Southampton so far, and when his old side travel to the South Coast on Friday, he will be eager to get one over his former employers.

Amario Cozier-Duberry was heavily courted by almost every Championship club over the summer, but it would be Middlesbrough who would sign him on loan from Brighton and Hove Albion in the end.

The 21-year-old came through the youth ranks of Arsenal before joining Brighton in the summer of 2024, and after a quiet loan spell with Blackburn Rovers in 2024/25, shone on loan with Bolton Wanderers in League One last time around.

With the Trotters, Cozier-Duberry scored eight and assisted 12 in 34 third-tier appearances, helping to fire the club back to the Championship, and in 12 league games so far this season, has three goals and six assists.

Best deployed off the right, neither Tom Fellows nor Kuryu Matsuki can make a solid argument for getting ahead of the England youth international in this side.

Brazilian winger Leo Scienza hasn't quite had the best start to this campaign, but his performances from last time around are still fresh in the memory, and one would be brave to deny his undoubted ability.

The 28-year-old joined the Saints in the summer of 2025 from German outfit Heidenheim, having worked his way up to the professional game across spells in the lower reaches of Brazil, Uruguay, Sweden, and Germany.

With seven goals and ten assists in 37 Championship appearances last season, Scienza was pivotal to the Saints' push towards the play-offs, and whilst he hasn't quite yet hit those similar heights this year, he remains a key figure in Eckert's side.

Neither Jeremy Sarmiento nor Tommy Conway get in ahead of the 28-year-old, who takes his place in the final third behind one other player.
