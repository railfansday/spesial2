Arsenal are more likely to "seriously consider" a move to sign RB Leipzig winger Antonio Nusa next summer than they are in January, sources have told Football Insider.

The Gunners are long-term admirers of the Norway international, but are expected to find Leipzig difficult to negotiate with this winter.

The German side are determined not to lose Nusa mid-season, especially given they are still competing in the Champions League.

Sources say that their star's £66million price tag will also be prohibitive for Arsenal given the hectic nature of the January window.

However, a summer move to the Premier League is seen as being much "more likely" for the 21-year-old, with Arsenal set to continue tracking him.

Mikel Arteta is desperate to add another left-sided forward to his attack, and Nusa is seen as the perfect fit for the role when he becomes available.

Nusa has enjoyed a remarkable rise since he first joined Leipzig back in 2024, and he has taken his game to new heights already this term.

The wideman has firmly established himself as Leipzig's prized asset, and already has four goals and seven assists from just nine Bundesliga games in 2026-27.

Nusa is even closely matching Ballon D'Or winger Harry Kane, who is just one goal involvement ahead of him at the top of the Bundesliga charts.

The starlet has been continually linked with a move to the Premier League throughout the last 12 months, but Leipzig remain determined to sell him on their terms.

He is under contract with them until the summer of 2029, and that is allowing them to hold out for a lofty fee for him.

Arsenal will have to splash the cash to sign Nusa, but that may be something they are prepared to do next summer.

Football Insider revealed earlier this week that Arsenal are keen to make a move to sign QPR's teenage sensation Leon Scarlett.

The 17-year-old has caught the attention of several clubs of late, after breaking into the Hoops' first-team this season.

Brighton and Rangers are also closely monitoring Scarlett's progress, and the British trio feel that theey could steal a march on their rivals.

Scarlett's age will prevent him from moving to a European club until next summer, giving Arsenal and Co. an advance window to strike a deal.
