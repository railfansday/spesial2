Ian Holloway prediction made after Swindon Town sack decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Ian Holloway has left Swindon Town, with the former Queens Park Rangers and Blackpool manager expected to be finished as a football manager — according to EFL pundit Callum Fowler.

Ian Holloway has managed Bristol Rovers, Queens Park Rangers (twice), Plymouth Argyle, Leicester City, Blackpool, Crystal Palace, Millwall, Grimsby Town, and Swindon Town.

It's fair to say he has many managerial highlights, which include taking QPR into the Championship in 2004 and guiding both Blackpool and Crystal Palace into the Premier League via the play-offs in 2010 and 2013 respectively. However, his Swindon appointment in October 2024 marked a return after almost four years away from management.

He had resigned from Grimsby Town in December 2020 and spent nearly four years out of the dugout before deciding that the opportunity at the County Ground was right for him.

The 63-year-old leaves with Swindon 17th in League Two. The likes of Callum Fowler are now predicting him to be finished as a manager, speaking via The Pyramid Podcast on YouTube. He said: "He's got to be finished in football now. Surely his pension is big enough?"

The 25 top scorers in the Championship since the second tier was rebranded back in 2004/05, including Billy Sharp and Aleksandar Mitrovic.

Fowler concluded: "He's calling players out. He's just got a problem with everybody. Nothing was ever his fault. Nothing has ever been Ian Holloway's fault, has it?"

Jimmy said: "He's obviously gone well past his prime. And it's a shame to see one of the characters go out of the game, but yeah, he's not a football league manager anymore."

Holloway has achieved three promotions during his managerial career. His first came with QPR in 2003/04, when he guided them back into the second tier after finishing runners-up in Division Two.

However, his greatest achievements came at Blackpool, taking the Tangerines into the Premier League through the play-offs in 2009/10 despite operating with one of the division's smallest budgets.

He then took them back to the play-off final in 2012, losing to West Ham. Holloway subsequently repeated his Premier League promotion success with Crystal Palace in 2012/13, winning another play-off final.

That said, having now been dismissed by Swindon after a disappointing run of results, Holloway may have reached the end of the road as an EFL manager. At 63, another lengthy managerial rebuild is far from guaranteed.

Similarly, George Elek of Not The Top 20 said: "I don't want to sound like a broken record, but this is just what always happens with Ollie. Like it's the cycle. I'm sure he will now take some time out of the game.

"He's someone who's incredibly emotional, who obviously loves football. You know, me and you know him and have worked with him. His adoration of the game and the fan aspect of it is very evident when you talk to him about it.

"But he is so emotional that it feels like he comes in, he's positive, he's rested, it's great. He breathes that energy into his team. And then the cynicism part comes out, and the difficult side comes out, and the emotion that he feels.

"We saw it at the back end of last season with that remarkable interview around what he felt was a, you know, an unfair um administration error that cost him his captain. And we laughed at it then because they were doing okay, but then that horrendous end to the season followed by the complete falling apart of his relationship with the fans.

"And this is what happens then, because he's so emotional, and because he says things in a way that when you're doing okay, is okay, when things turn, I think his character is basically the worst possible person to try and arrest the slide and to try and kind of get rid of the toxicity.

"Because the guy who initially comes in to clear the toxicity, he's the one that becomes toxic."

On his tenure with Swindon, Ali Maxwell added: "I'm quite pleased it's over because it's been like watching a really bad relationship. Like where it seems like no one's happy or in love or anything."
