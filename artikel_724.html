Latin Grammys 2026 Preview: Top Nominees, Performers & More | Billboard News

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The 2026 Latin Grammys are almost here! Billboard News previews this year’s ceremony, with Edgar Barrera leading the nominations, plus Karol G, Ca7riel and Rosalía among the artists earning seven nods each. Anitta and Carín León are set to perform, and Daddy Yankee will be honored as Person of the Year.

The 2026 Latin Grammys are just around the corner, and the list of nominees and performers couldn’t be more stacked. Billboard News is breaking down exactly what to expect at this year’s ceremony, presented by Capital One. Edgar Barrera leads all artists with 10 nominations, with artists like Karol G, Ca7riel, Rosalía, and more tied with seven nominations each. But it’s gonna be all about who takes the stage, with artists like Anitta, who is nominated for Album of the Year, Carín León, and Daddy Yankee, who will be honored with Person of the Year. All this is going down Thursday, November 12th at the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas. We’ll be there on the carpet. See you then.
