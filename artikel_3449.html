Dropout’s Most Unhinged Show Officially Returns with New Trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Very Important People season 4 begins next month

Fresh off their hilarious role in Star Wars Zero Company, Vic Michaelis returns to Dropout with a new season of Very Important People. Season 4 premieres next month, and Dropout released a new trailer on Wednesday that showcases just how chaotic it will get.

Very Important People stars Michaelis as a fictionalized version of themself. They play a talk show host who interviews wacky characters that comedians improvise on the spot, like Rekha Shankar's Mother Hot Dog from season 3. As showcased in the trailer, season 4 will feature Brennan Lee Mulligan as a fluffy unicorn, Oscar Montoya as some sort of Willy-Wonka-but-made-of-ice-cream character, and plenty more.

Joining Mulligan and Montoya as guests are plenty of returning faces. Jeremy Culhane, Lisa Gilroy, Angela Giarratana, Bobby Moynihan, Shankar, and more will return with all-new chaotic characters. Season 4 will feature new faces (under heavy amounts of makeup, presumably) too with comedians like Ben Schwartz and Bob The Drag Queen making their Very Important People debuts.

Just like previous seasons, Last Looks episodes will premiere on Fridays after the main Very Important People episodes. The behind-the-scenes episodes will offer fans deeper insight into the transformation process for each character.

Very Important People season 4 will debut on Thursday, Dec. 10 on Dropout.tv. The 15-episode season will stream new episodes every other week until June 24, 2027.
