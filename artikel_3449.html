Watch Karol G Perform Unreleased Song During Mexico City Celebration

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Is there more Karol G music on the way? It seems like it after the Colombian superstar performed an unreleased track during her TikTok Live celebrating new album No Me Arrepiento de Sentir Tanto.

Karol amped up the crowd in Mexico City’s Quarry Studios with the old-school reggaeton track alongside La Obsesión and DJ Maracuya, as clips taken from the Live show. Earlier this year, a song titled “Altafarra” was posted on SoundCloud by the username “caroliNA Xxxtra SparKLYyy” and featured La Obsesión and DJ Maracuya, along with a vocalist that sounds a lot like Karol G. (The track also seems to sample Daft Punk’s “Veridis Quo.”) Now, the Colombian star has confirmed the song is, in fact, hers. The singer was full of surprises on Wednesday night as she performed No Me Arrepiento de Sentir Tanto during her TikTok Live, which generated 87 million impressions worldwide and set an all-time record for an artist in simultaneous viewers on TikTok LIVE Premiere. In addition to the unreleased song, Karol also invited Chicago DJ John Summit to perform her massive hit “Bby Wow.” The pair closed out the show with Summit adding his own touches to the sad perreo, making some fans think an official remix is on the way. “Big love Karol for having me out,” Summit wrote on his Instagram after the performance.

Karol’s TikTok Live performance streamed from Mexico City, Mexico. On Friday, the singer she is set to kick off the Latin American leg of her Viajando Por El Mundo Tropitour in Monterrey before circling back to the capital for three nights at GNP Seguros Stadium. Last month, Karol wrapped the U.S. leg of her tour, which was filled with big moments and special guests, including the official premiere of the “Bby Wow” music video.
