Andrew Garfield Officially Transforms Into Sam Altman in New ‘Artificial’ Trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

By now, many people know Sam Altman because he has become almost synonymous with ChatGPT and OpenAI. Since ChatGPT burst onto the scene several years ago, it has become one of the most valuable tech companies in the world after pioneering AI as we know it. But while many might have known about the app and its parent company, OpenAI, for a few years, Altman spent decades steering it as it sought to make one of the biggest technological advancements in history. Like many companies, OpenAI has faced a lot of problems, chief among them leadership wrangling.

When OpenAI was launched, its goal was to become a venture geared towards the flourishing of humanity. It was a non-profit organization that relied on funding from the ultra-wealthy to keep its research and breakthroughs going. But at some point, its funders had a different vision, leading to internal struggles that saw Altman removed as CEO before returning. OpenAI is now a public benefit company that does business with other companies and the US government. Despite some problems with ChatGPT, Altman and his team have adopted the same mission as other AI companies: growth at all costs. They think AI will, at the very least, replace human labor, and if they really succeed, annihilate humanity.

Enter Artificial, a drama film directed by Luca Guadagnino that turns back the clock and explores how we got here. The film explores the two-decade AI race that has upended the world as we know it, for good or for worse. When a computer scientist and AI pioneer, Professor Geoffrey Hinton, makes a breakthrough they are certain will alter humanity forever, Sam Altman, Elon Musk, and the most prominent figures in Silicon Valley race to become part of their vision. As the technology becomes more powerful and more valuable than anyone ever imagined, a battle wages over who should control the tech that could change the fate of humanity," the film's official description explains.

The film was written by Simon Rich, with celebrated director Guadagnino behind the camera. Andrew Garfield is having his second The Social Network moment, leading the cast as Altman. Other cast members include Yura Borisov (Anora), Monica Barbaro (One Night Only), Cooper Hoffman (The Long Walk), Jason Schwartzman (Asteroid City), Ike Barinholtz (The Studio), and Mark Rylance (Wolf Hall). Artificial is being distributed by Neon after Amazon MGM dropped it following the signing of a $50 billion deal with OpenAI. Neon is bringing the film to select theaters on December 25 before it streams on January 8. The trailer embedded in the YouTube video above previews the film's events and characters.

Catch Artificial in theaters on Christmas Day and stay tuned to Collider for more updates.
