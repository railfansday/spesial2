New Dungeon Crawler Carl Book Releases Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Dungeon Crawler Carl Book 9 may not be here for a while yet, but the franchise is officially returning today with a brand-new release that fans of the franchise will not want to skip. Matt Dinniman is working diligently on the final chapters of the epic LitRPG series, and while there are a ton of loose ends to tie up for Carl, Princess Donut, and the rest of their comrades in the World Dungeon, it seems like the author is making steady progress.

Recently, Dinniman released several teaser chapters for Book 9 that are currently available to read on his Patreon account, while the recent launch of the Dungeon Crawler Carl RPG and Unstoppable board game will give fans something to do while we wait for more details on The Beautiful Place's release.

The newest release for the series officially drops today, however, bringing back the story that started it all in a brand-new Deluxe Edition with some stunning new artwork and brand-new content as well.

The Dungeon Crawler Carl: Deluxe Edition is finally available starting today, November 17, and it's easily one of the most impressive physical releases the franchise has seen yet. Published by Ace, the 464-page collector's edition revisits the very first novel, complete with gorgeous new artwork by acclaimed illustrator Victo Ngai, plenty of premium design features, and an exclusive interview with Matt Dinniman "conducted by GC, BWR, NW Princess Donut the Queen Anne Chonk herself."

Priced at $65, the Deluxe Edition includes a stunning new cover by Ngai, featuring Carl, Donut, and a whole load of unique shouts for longtime readers to enjoy. The book also features decorated endpapers, stenciled page edges, illustrations on the inside dust jacket, and a beautifully illustrated hardcover case featuring an actual solvable maze. There's even a full-color ribbon bookmark depicting Carl and Donut on the run.

Of course, the biggest selling point for longtime fans might be the exclusive interview with Matt Dinniman, conducted by none other than the Queen Anne Chonk, Princess Donut.

As you might expect, Donut has plenty to say, and the interview is filled with hilarious little "Donut-isms" that perfectly capture her larger-than-life personality. However, there's also some genuinely interesting information about Dinniman himself, including a few pieces of trivia that even the most dedicated Dungeon Crawler Carl fans probably aren't privy to.

Among other things, the interview explores Dinniman's favorite video games, which character from the Dungeon Crawler Carl universe he'd most like to sit down and have a drink with, and plenty more. It's a fun opportunity to learn a little more about the mind behind the series, with Donut naturally making sure she's the center of attention throughout.

You can purchase the Dungeon Crawler Carl Deluxe Edition from Amazon here.

Dungeon Crawler Carl: Deluxe Edition is available now through the Amazon link above, as well as other major booksellers like Barnes & Noble, Books-A-Million, etc. While longtime fans likely already own at least one copy of the first book, the gorgeous design and exclusive bonus content make this a too-tempting addition to any collection. It should also make a fantastic gift for anyone who's fallen in love with Carl and Donut's chaotic adventures or wants to get their friends/partners/family hooked on a new obsession and geek out together over the holidays.
