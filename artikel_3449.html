Jaime King Grabs Coffee With Director George Moïse Month After Divorce

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Jaime King has some company on her coffee run ... stepping out with director George Moïse a month after finalizing her divorce from Austin Sosa.

In photos, obtained by TMZ, the actress and Moïse are seen grabbing coffee in Los Angeles ... where the duo also shared a pastry during their outing.

No word on whether there’s anything romantic brewing between them, but they clearly had no problem splitting something sweet and enjoying L.A.'s fall weather.

The coffee date comes as Jaime starts a new chapter following her split from Sosa, with their divorce finalized just last month.

For now ... coffee and a little company are on the menu for these two.
