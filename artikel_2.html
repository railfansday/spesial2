Ukraine and Russia trade strikes on key bridges

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Northern Bridge (pictured in October) is closed after being targeted again by Russia

Russia and Ukraine traded attacks on key bridges overnight, with reports of significant damage to a symbolic bridge linking occupied Crimea and Russia.

A former adviser to Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky shared images purporting to show that a section of the Crimea bridge - also known as the Kerch Bridge - had collapsed into the water.

Meanwhile, Kyiv Mayor Vitali Klitschko said the Northern and Southern bridges, important routes over the Dnipro River, were closed after Russian ballistic missile strikes.

Those attacks came a day after Ukraine began its most recent attempt to disable the Crimea bridge, a prized Kremlin asset which has been seen as a symbol of Russia's occupation of the peninsula.

Two people were killed in that strike overnight on Sunday, the Russian-installed governor of Crimea said.

Former Ukrainian adviser Anton Gerashchenko shared images and video footage appearing to show extensive damage to the bridge from the latest strike, with a support arch and road section seen collapsed in the water. BBC News has not independently verified those images.

The bridge, which has come under repeated attack by Ukrainian forces, was built by Russia after it invaded and annexed the peninsula in 2014.

Russian President Vladimir Putin personally opened the bridge in 2018.

The severity of the latest strike on the route, which is protected with multiple layers of aerial and maritime defences, is still not fully clear.

Crimea's Moscow-backed governor Sergei Aksyonov said on Telegram that traffic over the bridge had been suspended.

The Crimea bridge (pictured in 2023) has been repeatedly targeted by Ukraine

Zelensky had warned of major attacks in the days leading up to a meeting between the Chinese, US and Russian leaders in China's Shenzhen on Thursday.

Loud explosions were heard in Kyiv just before midnight on Tuesday, with Klitschko saying shortly after that the city had suffered "damage to critical infrastructure systems".

"Some areas are experiencing disruptions to water and heating supplies", he added.

Some of Kyiv's above-ground subway services were damaged in the overnight attacks, officials said, after civilians were told to go to civil defence shelters.

Residents of Kyiv typically shelter in the city's underground stations during air raids.

Eight major bridges span the Dnipro River connecting the western and eastern parts of Kyiv. An estimated three million residents rely on the bridges to travel across the city.

Several of the capital's major bridges were also targeted by Russian drone strikes in October.

Elsewhere, three people have been injured in a Russian missile attack in the Cherkast region, according to local officials.

Russian military intercepted and destroyed 2,049 drones and four Flamingo long-distance missiles over 24 hours, officials said. Around 600 flew towards the capital alone, Moscow Mayor Sergey Sobyanin said.

The latest round of tit-for-tat strikes came after US, Ukrainian and European officials held talks on ways to facilitate "direct engagement" between Russia and Europe to end the war.

Following Monday's meeting, US envoy Jared Kushner, President Trump's son-in-law, said the US was committed to providing Ukraine with a "strong, credible security guarantees", as well as a path towards EU membership.

He added that Nato "will remain a critical pillar of European security", and that preparations are underway for a further multilateral meeting "in the near future".

Zelensky said that the meeting was "difficult", adding that more needed to be done to support Ukraine's defence against Russian ballistic missile strikes.

The Ukrainian president has long called for European nations to be included in any peace talks, which he argues is necessary because of Kyiv's longer-term ambition to join the EU.

He also used his address to say Moscow is receiving "dozens of missiles per month" from North Korea.

Meanwhile, Ukrainian officials said that the final death toll from a Russian attack on the southern Ukrainian village of Kamyshuvakha earlier this month had reached 72.

Four people are still considered missing after the bombing, which Ukrainian officials say is one of the deadliest individual attacks since the start of the war in 2022.

Footage, from a camera on one of the killed police officers, showed residents going to an underground shelter after the initial attack on 2 November. Soon after a blast is heard and flames are seen as the shelter is hit.

Ukrainian police said of those killed were police officers, "staff of the gymnasium, utility workers and medical personnel".

Russia's launched its full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022. Approximately one-fifth of Ukraine is now under Russian occupation and Kyiv has refused Putin's demand to give up areas of Ukraine's eastern Donbas that remain under its control.
