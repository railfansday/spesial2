The Best Early Black Friday Jewelry Deals of 2026 to Gift (or Keep for Yourself)

From tennis bracelets to drop earrings, these dazzling deals are too good to miss.

Black Friday is fast approaching, and one category I’ve been keeping a close eye on is jewelry. Much to my delight, some seriously good early deals are already up for grabs, from fine diamonds and dainty everyday essentials to statement pieces that elevate your next night-out look or wedding guest ensemble. To save you the scrolling (and help you score a little sparkle for less), I’ve rounded up the chicest jewelry finds worth shopping ahead of the holiday rush.

With cult-favorite jewelry labels like Catbird, Blue Nile, and Haverhill among those offering impressive discounts, consider this your sign to treat yourself or surprise someone on your list with something special. Among the can’t-miss finds are diamond tennis bracelets, unique rings to add to your everyday stack, and gemstone studs that instantly glam up any look. And with holiday gifting season upon us, why not check a few names off your list while you’re at it? Some brands are even offering personalized pieces that can be engraved with a loved one’s name or initials for an extra meaningful touch.

Ahead, discover the best ELLE-approved jewelry deals to add to your cart while they’re still in stock.

Pandora: 30 percent off storewide from November 12 to December 1

Give the fashion girl on your list 12 days of surprises with Pandora’s advent calendar, now 45 percent off. Once she’s unwrapped the last gift, she can keep her new finds in the jewelry box they came in.

Catbird: Sitewide deals starting at 20 percent off from November 16 to November 30

I love a hoop that clicks into place without an earring back to fuss with. This Catbird huggie duo, now 15 percent off, includes a smaller, chunkier pair and a larger, diamond-studded style. Keep both for your own rotation, or gift one and save the other for yourself.

With Clarity: Up to 30 percent off sitewide from November 23 to December 3

Diamond studs can dress up even the most minimalist outfit. This sparkling four-prong pair is one of my favorite finds from With Clarity’s early Black Friday sale, and you can choose both the metal and carat weight.

Blue Nile: Up to 40 percent off select jewelry

A 14K white gold tennis bracelet at 40 percent off? I don’t need much convincing. This classic Blue Nile design features a single row of round brilliant-cut lab-grown diamonds, each held in a four-prong setting, and fastens with a secure box clasp.

En Route: 35 percent off sitewide from November 13 to December 2

Add a pop of color to any ensemble with these precious peridot drop earrings, now 35 percent off during En Route’s early Black Friday sale. Pair them with the equally chic coordinating necklace, also marked down.

Rogers & Hollands: 20 to 50 percent off sitewide and up to 60 percent off select sale items from November 6 to November 29

Lab-grown diamond accents give this slender, 14K gold-plated cuff just enough sparkle to wear on its own, though it layers just as easily with slim bangles or a favorite watch. Right now, it’s over 30 percent off during Rogers & Hollands’ early Black Friday sale.

Gilded New York: Up to 35 percent off sitewide from November 23 to November 30, plus free 1/4-carat diamond studs on orders of $500 or more

An oval cut never goes out of style, and these four-prong lab-grown diamond studs from Gilded New York are guaranteed to draw compliments. Choose your carat weight and a setting in 10K or 14K yellow or white gold—all combinations are 20 percent off.

Haverhill: 25 percent off sitewide from November 13 to November 30

Haverhill is treating us to 25 percent off sitewide, including this elegant gemstone pendant that makes for an extravagant gift for a loved one. Personalize it with your choice of stone and metal color, whether it’s their birthstone or favorite gem—my pick is a rich red ruby set in gold.

Astrid & Miyu: 25 percent off everything from November 12 to December 1 with code DREAM25

Add an unexpected twist to your ring stack with Astrid & Miyu’s sculptural wave ring, available in both gold and silver. It’s currently 25 percent off as part of the brand’s sitewide sale.

Jared: 20 percent off select items from November 13 to November 30

Whether you’re considering an engagement ring upgrade or shopping for a statement-making cocktail ring, Jared’s nearly sitewide sale is worth a peek. Among the discounted designs is this stunning lab-grown diamond ring, in your choice of precious metal and carat weight.
