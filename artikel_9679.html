FaceStealer Deepfake Video Calls: How to Check Your Wallet

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Posted on Nov 14 • Originally published at botlab.org.uk

You usually cannot tell a good deepfake by looking at it, so the most reliable check is to confirm the call through a separate channel you already trust. Treat any "technical fix" link or download offered during a call as the main warning sign. There is also no way to scan a wallet address for malware, because malware lives on your device and not on the blockchain. What you can do is check your wallet on a public block explorer for transactions you did not make, and treat the device as compromised if you installed anything during a suspicious call.

This guide was written on 2026-11-14. The situation may change, so check official channels.

The DOJ announced a separate case at the same time. It obtained court authorization to seize or restrain more than $1.2 billion of the crypto stolen from Binance in the Oct. 29 hack. That sum is part of more than $2.7 billion frozen so far.

The two sources give slightly different totals for the theft. Cointelegraph says $6.3 billion and Decrypt says "roughly $6.4 billion". The Block reports the same headline seizure.

The FBI attributed that hack to the group TraderTraitor, and North Korea has denied involvement. Cointelegraph reports that Binance said on X that it covered the affected assets with its own funds. The FaceStealer campaign is a different case from the Binance hack.

This is the wider effect. A compromised developer could expose many software users, not only that developer. Ordinary holders who never take work video calls appear to be less directly targeted, based on these reports. The sources do not say they are unaffected, though.

No visual trick reliably detects a well-made deepfake. Prerecorded real footage of a real contact may look completely normal. Pay attention to the process instead of the face.

You cannot scan a wallet address for malware. You can check whether anything has moved, and whether your device may be at risk.
