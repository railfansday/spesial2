Save what you decide with one AI to a note, and another AI can read it

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

You work out the structure of next week's presentation with one AI. The next day you ask a different AI to build the slides, and it has no idea what structure you decided on. Pasting the conversation in works. But you can only paste what you remember at that moment, and the decision stays split across each AI's chat history.

I wanted what you decide to stay in a note on your side. Graphium notes live in your own folder, so I made the outside AI come to those notes. The connection uses MCP, a common standard for connecting AI apps to outside tools. In the desktop app, open Settings → AI → Use your notes from other AI assistants and press Register on the row for the AI you use. Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex and the ChatGPT desktop app can all be registered from this screen. Some AI apps need to be quit or restarted around registration. If the Claude Code command is not found, the screen shows a command to run once in your terminal.

After that, you ask in your usual conversation. Tell one AI, "Save this presentation structure to Graphium," and a note appears. Ask another, "Search my Graphium notes for the structure of next week's presentation," and it can find and read that note. You can also ask it which note the answer came from.

I also wanted to be able to tell later how a note got there. A note saved by an AI keeps, in its provenance (the record of where it came from), the fact that it was written through an AI and the name of the app used. Since every AI reads and writes the Graphium notes themselves, what you kept stays in your folder even if you change AIs.

In an AI app that can draw screens inside the conversation, such as Claude Desktop, you can also talk while watching a graph of which pages the AI read, as above.

There are limits. Only what you saved to a note reaches the other AI. Conversations do not turn into notes on their own, and they are kept only when you ask. The other AI will not necessarily look through your notes by itself, so you ask it to search your Graphium notes. Only AI apps running on the same computer as your notes can connect. In ChatGPT, it works in Work mode conversations in the desktop app, and not from Chat mode, the web version or the phone apps.

The manual's page "Connecting from an AI assistant (MCP)" covers the registration steps and what you can ask for. Using your notes from outside AIs arrived in v0.46.0 (2026-08-28), registering from the app in v0.115.0 (2026-11-04), and registering with the ChatGPT desktop app in v0.136.0 (2026-11-15).
