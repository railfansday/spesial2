5 Years Later, Netflix’s Brilliant 4-Part Crime Thriller Only Gets Better With Every Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

That series is Lupin. First premiering in 2021, Lupin follows Assane Diop (Omar Sy), a chauffeur who seeks to avenge his father's death. To achieve his goals, he takes on the mantle of Arsène Lupin, the iconic gentleman thief, and launches a campaign of theft and deception to take down the insanely wealthy Hubert Pellegrini (Hervé Pierre), who bankrupted and imprisoned his father. Each season of Lupin's gathered critical acclaim, with Seasons 3 and 4 earning a perfect score on Rotten Tomatoes. It's well-earned, as Lupin has plenty of style and story to keep viewers hooked from beginning to end.

While Lupin starts as a simple revenge/theft story, it slowly builds a narrative that raises the stakes for Assane in every way. Though he manages to take down Pellegrini, his actions make him the most wanted man in France, straining his relationship with his wife, Claire (Ludivine Sagnier), and son, Raoul. Things get worse when Raoul is kidnapped, sending Assane on a rescue mission. Season 4 raises the stakes even higher, as a cybercriminal frames him for a series of thefts while he's in prison, forcing him to escape and clear his name. You never know what you'll get with this show, and that's what makes it all the more exciting.

The series also has a meta-narrative, as Lupin was originally meant to adapt the Arsène Lupin stories before making a major shift. Instead of playing Lupin, Assane adopts his methods, and detectiveYoussef Guedira (Soufiane Guerrab) can pursue Assane thanks to his own knowledge of the Lupin stories. Series co-creator George Kay said that he took this approach because he wanted to create an adaptation that felt in step with modern times:

"There's certain things that you may want to remain faithful to if you are to update that [original] character - but what I wanted to do was create this modern man and force him through the filter of Lupin, and running alongside that, in the earlier stages of development, I was really into the idea of the intellectual property sitting as a character within the show."

The gamble paid off, thanks to Sy's suave performance and Kay leading a skilled writing staff. But while Lupin hooked plenty of viewers during its run, it would actually increase in popularity thanks to a major event that dovetails with the series' narrative.
