Fox Midseason 2027 TV Schedule Reveals Surprise Reduced Episode Order

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Fox has released its midseason 2027 TV schedule with details on new additions and returning shows — and some major programming changes.

The network confirmed that existing shows would be back after the holidays to their usual time slots. Best Medicine, however, will have a reduced 14-episode order this season, which will pave the way for The Interrogator to take over in February.

There will also be new shows — including the highly anticipated Baywatch revival — to look forward to.

Baywatch, which ran from 1989 to 2001, followed lifeguards who worked on beaches in California (and Hawaii) while dealing with a variety of unexpected situations, from earthquakes and shark attacks to serial killers. At one point during its run in the ’90s, Baywatch made history as the most-watched television series in the world.

The series had dozens of cast shakeups over the years but ultimately turned into a cultural phenomenon. Some of the show’s memorable cast members include Nicole Eggert, Carmen Electra, David Hasselhoff, Billy Warlock, Alexandra Paul, Jeremy Jackson, Traci Bingham and Erika Eleniak.

In September 2025, Fox announced that a “reimagined” version of the iconic series is coming to the network, which will officially premiere next year. It will star Stephen Amell, Jessica Belkin, Hassie Harrison, Shay Mitchell, Thaddeus LaGrone, Noah Beck and Brooks Nader with cameos from OG Baywatch stars as well.

Keep scrolling for a full schedule for Fox shows airing in midseason:

Tuesday, January 5 Best Medicine 8 p.m. Doc 9 p.m.

Wednesday, January 6 The Masked Singer 8 p.m. Baywatch 9 p.m.

Monday, February 1 Murder in a Small Town 8 p.m. Memory of a Killer 9 p.m.

Tuesday, February 9 The Interrogator 8 p.m.

Thursday, February 18 Next Level Chef 8 p.m.
