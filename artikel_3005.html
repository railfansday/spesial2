Is Coinbase Stock a Buy, Sell, or Hold 64% Below Its 52-Week High?

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The cryptocurrency exchange still looks like a shaky investment.

Coinbase's (COIN -6.34%) stock has declined about 64% from its 52-week high of $284.74 per share, reached on Dec. 9, 2025. It's also trading 76% below its all-time high of $419.78 per share, which it set on July 18, 2025. Should investors buy, sell, or hold this unloved cryptocurrency stock as the crypto winter drags on?

Coinbase generates roughly half of its revenue from crypto transaction fees, with the rest mainly coming from its add-on subscriptions and services. Over the past few years, it's increased its exposure to stablecoins to reduce its dependence on more volatile tokens, and expanded its Coinbase One subscription platform to lock in its customers and widen its moat.

Coinbase ended its latest quarter with 7.4 million monthly transacting users (MTUs), compared to 9.3 million MTUs a year earlier and a peak of 11.4 million MTUs in the fourth quarter of 2021. That decline was caused by rising interest rates, which drove investors toward more conservative investments, competition from other crypto trading platforms, security concerns, and unpredictable regulatory challenges for the broader crypto market.

That's why its revenue fell 29% year over year in the first nine months of 2026, as its adjusted EBITDA plunged 62%. Coinbase's stock might seem cheap at nine times next year's adjusted EBITDA, but I wouldn't touch it unless the Fed finally cuts rates and the crypto market stabilizes.
