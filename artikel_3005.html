Doc Rivers sounds like a Lakers and Luka Dončić truther: ‘Has to be more than any of us know’

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Los Angeles Lakers pulled off one of the most surprising trades in NBA history when they acquired Luka Dončić, and Doc Rivers insists there has to be more to the story.

Rivers appeared on The Bill Simmons Podcast this week, which he will do somewhat regularly throughout the NBA season. And during the podcast, Simmons asked the former Milwaukee Bucks head coach where he was when he learned the Lakers were acquiring Luka Dončić from the Dallas Mavericks.

Rivers recalled receiving a text from a Milwaukee Bucks PR person about the trade. And Rivers' response was, "That would be crazy if that happened for real," only to learn it was real.

"I was like, 'No way!' It was a no way! I think there's gonna be a book in 10 years [that's going to explain it]. It has to be more than any of us know."

Simmons noted it has since worked out for both teams, citing the fact that it made the Mavericks bad enough to acquire the top pick in the 2025 NBA Draft, selecting future superstar Cooper Flagg. But Rivers clarified, "No, it worked out great for the Lakers."

It was viewed as one of the great heists in American sports history when the Lakers acquired the 25-year-old superstar from the Dallas Mavericks in exchange for a package centered around Anthony Davis. Some analysts attempted to defend the trade, believing Davis and Kyrie Irving could lead Dallas to a championship. But after sharing the court for just 25 minutes, Davis was eventually traded to the Washington Wizards, and the Mavericks began a total rebuild around Cooper Flagg.

The media, however, is always hot and cold on Luka. After initially touting the trade as a franchise-altering move for the Lakers, they quickly turned on Dončić last season. Colin Cowherd claimed Dončić was aging and regressing too quickly while he was still averaging 32, 8, and 8. Max Kellerman even suggested the Lakers should consider trading Dončić. They moved on from LeBron James instead, and all of a sudden Luka Dončić and his 38 points per game to start the season are back in the media's good graces.

But Doc Rivers seems steadfast in believing the Lakers got a steal. More than that, Rivers seems to believe something went down behind the scenes that landed Luka in Los Angeles for Anthony Davis. Something that might be worthy of a book 10 years from now.
