Tanggal: 2026-11-12

BK Technologies Corporation (BKTI -2.79%) reported record third-quarter revenue and gross margins exceeding 50%, driven primarily by strong federal demand and the adoption of specialized radio units by the U.S. Forest Service. Management reported that a partial roof collapse at its West Melbourne headquarters on Oct. 8 caused a temporary disruption in BKR 9000 production, leading to a downward revision of full-year revenue guidance to $85 million. Despite this event, the company stated that affected orders have been postponed to early 2027 rather than being canceled. The company also highlighted its strategic transition toward becoming a public safety technology provider through a $3 million investment in Tango Tango and the expansion of its distribution footprint to Texas. BK Technologies maintained a debt-free balance sheet with $29.2 million in cash and increased its share repurchase authorization to $10 million.
