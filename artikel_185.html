What can you get for $30 these days? When it comes to tech, you might be surprised. Even as gadgets get more expensive, $30 can still go a long way, especially on Black Friday. For just $8, for instance, TP-Link’s Tapo smart plug will let you control the Christmas tree lights from your phone. You can also grab a decent pair of ANC-equipped earbuds for under $20, or spend even less on a clever set of rechargeable AA batteries. And it’s not just gadgets that are on sale: you can even sign up for a year of Disney Plus and Hulu for $4.99 ($8 off) a month.

Below, we’ve rounded up the best Black Friday deals for $30 or less, from charging accessories and smart home tech to earbuds, streaming subscriptions, games, and more. Whether you’re looking for practical stocking stuffers or want to treat yourself without spending too much, these are the deals that won’t put a (huge) dent in your wallet.

Apple’s latest AirTags feature an upgraded ultra-wideband chip for more precise location tracking, a noticeably louder speaker, and an expanded Bluetooth range. Read our review.

You can get an ad-supported Disney Plus and Hulu bundle for $4.99 a month ($8 off) for an entire year. Both streaming services feature a deep catalog of new and classic TV shows and movies, including The Simpsons, Andor, Toy Story 5, and The Fantastic Four: First Steps.

Do you ever wish you could turn on your coffee maker or hit the light switch without getting up? The SwitchBot Bot lets you do that. All you need to do is stick the inexpensive contraption on a standard button or rocker switch, which will allow you to control or schedule your pet feeder, garage door, and a wide range of other devices from your smartphone.

Who needs magnets when you have suction cups? Octobuddy’s cheap silicone mount works with most phones to provide hands-free flexibility whenever necessary. It features 24 single-sided cups, which allow you to stick your phone to a mirror, window, or any other surface you think will provide that perfect TikTok angle.

The Chamera is not a good camera, but that’s not the point. Kodak’s charming, keychain-friendly tchotchke lets you snap lo-fi memories on a whim, with an ultra-small 1.6-megapixel sensor, a built-in LED flash, and the tiniest amount of storage.

Hoto’s rechargeable screwdriver is ideal for small repairs. It offers three torque modes, a rotational speed of up to 220RPM, and a helpful circular LED to illuminate dark spaces. The durable case also holds all 25 steel bits and an extension bar for getting into hard-to-reach places.

Rechargeable AA batteries are handy until it’s time to charge them. Most wall chargers let you refill four, maybe six, at once. Zepath’s clever solution is to ship them in an easy-to-use USB-C charging case that charges eight simultaneously. The batteries themselves are long-lasting, too.

An HBO Max Basic subscription provides access to shows like A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms, The Last of Us, and The Pitt, as well as films like Superman and Weapons. You can get a year of the monthly ad-supported subscription for $5.49 a month (about $6 off) until December 1st.

The Wuben G5 flashlight is as small as a Zippo lighter, yet far more versatile, with a bright, rotatable light that can shine in numerous colors. It features USB-C charging and has an adjustable clip.

The original LifeStraw was designed for those heading into the backcountry. The new LifeStraw Sip? Not so much. The reusable steel straw is geared more toward travelers than outdoor enthusiasts, though fortunately it still manages to remove about 99.99 percent of microplastics, bacteria, and parasites from your drinking water.

Peacock is a great way to catch up on a wealth of content from NBC’s various networks, whether you plan to rewatch The Office or catch up on all things Bravo. A Premium subscription also grants access to films from studios like Universal Pictures and Dreamworks, along with live sports and a host of Peacock Originals.

Anker’s 621 Magnetic Battery is a slim, 5,000mAh charger that can attach magnetically to the back of any MagSafe-ready iPhone.

TP-Link’s Wi-Fi smart plug lets you remotely control lamps, fans, and other appliances using your phone or voice commands. It supports Matter, so it works with all major smart home platforms to set schedules and timers to automatically turn devices on or off.

Paramount Plus Premium provides access to live sports as well as movies and shows from both platforms, including Tales of the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, a host of Yellowstone spinoffs, Star Trek shows, Sonic the Hedgehog 3, Twin Peaks, Dexter, and more.

Amazon’s last-gen Fire TV Stick 4K Plus streams in 4K and supports Dolby Atmos, Dolby Vision, and Alexa Plus. It also ships with the redesigned Fire OS and, unlike more recent models, still lets you sideload Android TV apps.

With 12.4mm drivers, active noise cancellation, and an IP54 rating, the CMF Buds 2A offer high-end features at an affordable price. You can also customize the sound, adjust ANC levels, and find the earbuds using the Nothing X app.

Twelve South’s entry-level Bluetooth transmitter delivers up to 25 hours of continuous use and lets you connect up to two pairs of headphones to an in-flight entertainment system, making it a great travel companion no matter where you’re going.

In Catan, up to four players strategically compete and trade for resources. The iconic board game ends when one player reaches 10 “victory points,” which is achieved by constructing a sprawling civilization of roads, settlements, and cities using sheep, ore, wheat, and wood.

Azul is a game in which you arrange patterned tiles in order to create the most beautiful tapestry possible. Arranging tiles in a strategic sequence will help you build your score while limiting your opponents’ ability to grow theirs.

Halo: Campaign Evolved lets you revisit the original Halo: Combat Evolved campaign with better graphics, updated controls, and improved sound. The well-crafted remake also adds three new missions starring Master Chief and Sgt. Johnson, which take place before the main campaign. Read our review.

No one should have to deal with a rat’s nest behind their desk when devices like Anker’s 2-in-1 USB-C cable exist. The handy USB-C cable splits at the end, giving you the ability to charge up to two devices from a single port. That means you can split power between a phone and tablet or juice a 16-inch MacBook Pro to 50 percent in just 20 minutes using a single port.

The Witcher 3 lets you explore the war-torn Northern Kingdoms as Geralt of Rivia while preparing to face the mysterious Wild Hunt. The recent remaster adds improved visuals, smoother combat, and better movement, cementing the game’s status as one of the best RPGs of all time.

Iniu’s P55-E2 Power Bank is an ultra-slim three-port USB charger that’s capable of fully recharging an iPhone 16 twice. It supports charging speeds of up to 45W via a USB-C port, which can deliver fast recharging for a range of gadgets.

Glocusent’s clip-in reading light features five brightness levels and multiple color temperature settings, ranging from a soft, warm glow that’s great for late-night reading to a brighter light that’s perfect for daytime use. The slim USB light easily slips into your pocket, too, so you can read anywhere, from a cozy bed to a dimly lit train.

LIFX’s dimmable, color-changing bulbs offer Wi-Fi connectivity, simple setup, and Matter compatibility, so they work with any major smart home platform. The company is currently trialing a Thread beta that will bring the option to add Thread connectivity to all its Matter devices.

The ChomChom is a reusable roller that works great for removing unwanted pet hair from furniture, allowing you to skip lint rollers, vacuuming, and other cleaning tools in a pinch.

Apple TV offers ad-free streaming of original shows and movies, including shows like Severance, Silo, Slow Horses, and Pluribus. The service also features a rotating selection of films, along with live Formula 1 races and Major League Soccer matches.

Anker’s Qi2 charging pads support wireless charging speeds of up to 15W, and their 5-foot cable shouldn’t feel constricting if you need to move the charger around.

Blink’s Mini 2K Plus is a small plug-in security camera that lets you monitor your home or pets with 2K video, motion detection, and color night vision. It also offers two-way audio, a built-in siren, and Alexa support; however, some features, including person and vehicle detection, require a Blink subscription.

Attending a live concert? You can protect your hearing with Loop’s Quiet 2 earplugs. They pack up to 24dB (SNR) of noise reduction and come with four eartip sizes, allowing them to accommodate a range of ear shapes.

Crunchyroll is an anime-centric streaming service that offers ad-free access to shows like One Piece, Jujutsu Kaisen, The Apothecary Diaries, and Black Clover, with new episodes available shortly after they air in Japan.

Logitech’s Pop is a cute, simple wireless mouse with an ambidextrous design and the ability to switch between up to three devices. It supports customization through the Logi Options Plus app and can last for several months on a single charge.

Logitech’s G502 Hero has most of the same features as one of our top choices among gaming mice, the G502 Lightspeed, except it’s wired. If you’re okay with that, this is an excellent mouse that you can usually find for $50 or less.

The proliferation of miniature screens continues with Ugreen’s latest Nexode USB-C Cable, which features a full-color LCD screen on one end that displays current power draw (up to 240W). It also supports 480Mbps data transfer speeds and, unlike similar power meter-equipped cables, lets you turn off the screen when needed.

The Tile Pro is the company’s loudest Bluetooth tracker, one that benefits from a 500-foot range. It also features a user-replaceable battery, unlike some other Tiles, and works with both iOS and Android devices.

Anker’s jack-of-all-trades travel adapter packs enough ports to refill your devices during some downtime. It features one AC outlet, two USB-C ports (one tops out at 20W), and two USB-A ports. It’s not the fastest wall charger, but it’s a solid choice when you can find it at a discount.

The Kobo Remote lets you turn pages on a compatible Kobo e-reader with the click of a button, so you can keep reading without reaching for the screen. It connects directly over Bluetooth and features a curved, handheld design that doesn’t require clipping anything onto your e-reader.

The second-gen Ring Indoor Cam lets you keep an eye on your place in 1080p, with support for motion detection alerts, a live feed, and color night vision.

Workpro’s EDC is a lightweight folding utility knife with a side-mounted button that lets you flick it open and closed with one hand. It uses standard utility blades, which you can easily replace using a spring-loaded plate, making it handy for breaking down cardboard boxes and other tasks.

Lamicall’s cord organizers use magnetic clasps to keep cables in place, preventing them from slipping behind desks, shelves, and other furniture when unplugged.

Nalgene’s lightweight, 16-ounce water bottle features a narrow opening for sipping, a leakproof lid, and a slim design that fits in cup holders. It’s made from durable, BPA- and BPS-free plastic, with measurement markings along the side and dishwasher-safe construction.

This tiny cordless driver comes with five double-ended bits for most basic around-the-house projects and Ikea-level builds.
