Fox completed its $22 billion acquisition of Roku, creating a television giant

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Fox expects about $400 million in cost savings within two years of closing as Roku stock stops trading on the Nasdaq

Fox completed its acquisition of Roku $ROKU on Friday, the company said, adding a connected TV platform that reaches more than 100 million households to its sports, news and local programming businesses.

The $22 billion deal creates a television giant, according to Bloomberg. The cash-and-stock transaction was announced in June, according to Reuters.

Under the merger agreement, Roku stockholders are entitled to receive $95.30 in cash and 0.9799 shares of Fox Class A common stock for each Roku Class A and Class B share they held before closing. Fox stockholders own about 74% of the outstanding Fox common stock, and former Roku stockholders own about 26%. Roku stock stops trading on the Nasdaq $NDAQ on Friday.

Fox expects to reach about $400 million of run-rate cost synergies within two years of closing, with additional potential revenue upside. It anticipates the deal will add to free cash flow per share by the second full year after closing.

Roku founder Anthony Wood will join the Fox board of directors. He will also hold the co-chair post at Fox Digital Platforms, a recently created unit that comprises Roku, Tubi, Fox One and Red Seat Ventures. Paul Cheesbrough will serve as co-chair and CEO of the division.

“Beginning today, our top priority for the combined company is to continue to relentlessly serve audiences and partners, while reinforcing the openness and choice that is the essence of the Roku platform,” Executive Chair and CEO Lachlan Murdoch said in a statement, according to Bloomberg.

Fox said Roku will remain an open platform for the streaming industry and will continue to serve more than 100 million households worldwide, with access to thousands of apps and channels.

"We started Roku more than two decades ago to give people a better way to watch TV, and that mission doesn't change today. What does change is our reach and resources," Wood said in a statement.

The acquisition follows other recent expansion by Fox. The company debuted Fox One last year, and it also bought Red Seat Ventures that year, according to Bloomberg.
