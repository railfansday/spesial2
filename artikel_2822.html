Germany prepares emotional Berlin farewell for Antonio Rüdiger

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Antonio Rüdiger will receive an official farewell from the German national team on Monday in his hometown of Berlin ahead of Germany's match against the Netherlands, according to a report captured by @iMiaSanMia.

Rüdiger announced his retirement from international football following the World Cup, bringing an end to an impressive chapter with Die Mannschaft. The center-back built an outstanding career at both the international and club levels, establishing himself as one of the most recognizable and respected defenders of his generation during his time with Real Madrid.

For Germany, Rüdiger brought far more than his defensive ability. His physical presence, competitive mentality, intensity, and willingness to lead made him an important figure in the locker room. He was never a player who shied away from responsibility, and his personality was just as noticeable as his performances on the pitch.

That is what German supporters will miss most. Replacing a center-back's individual qualities is one challenge, but replacing the leadership, experience, and personality that Rüdiger brought to the group is another entirely.

Monday's farewell in Berlin will give German football one more opportunity to recognize everything Rüdiger contributed to the national team. His international career may be over, but the impact he made on Die Mannschaft and the memories he leaves behind will not disappear anytime soon.
