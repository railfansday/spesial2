📺 Zappacosta handball, 🟥 or not? Replays spark debate: all the views

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

In the crazy Atalanta-Inter match that ended 4-4, one incident that sparked plenty of debate was the penalty scored by Petar Sucic to put the visitors 4-2 up.

Referee Maresca decided not to send off Davide Zappacosta for the incident, despite his handball almost on the goal line.

Here is how the newspapers analysed the incident, offering slightly different interpretations.

"In the 75th minute, it is a clear penalty. Zappacosta, on the ground, makes a second movement with his left arm that looks deliberate (and therefore worthy of a red card), without knowing whether the ball is heading into the goal. He appears to make that extra movement with his elbow to push the ball away: Maresca sticks with a yellow, but the question of a straight red remains."

"Zappacosta touches the ball with his heel and, as he falls, touches it twice with his left arm. The first contact can be considered a supporting-arm contact, and is therefore not punishable. The second, however, is a touch intended to push the ball away from the line and is therefore punishable. At that point, though, it is no longer preventing a goal from being scored. Nowadays, however, even that does not automatically result in a sending-off: a red card is only shown for an unnatural, deliberate and unsporting action."
