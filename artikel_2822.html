How to Watch Missouri Football vs. Oklahoma: Kickoff Time, TV Channel, Radio and Weather

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

COLUMBIA, Mo. — Missouri football returns to Memorial Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 28, for its regular-season finale against Oklahoma.

The Tigers will welcome the Sooners to Columbia for another meeting between former Big 12 rivals, now competing in the Southeastern Conference. Both programs will look to strengthen their postseason standing with a victory.

Matchup: Oklahoma Sooners at Missouri Tigers

Location: Memorial Stadium at Faurot Field, Columbia, Missouri

SEC Network will broadcast the matchup nationally as Missouri closes its regular-season schedule.

Additionally, fans can stream the game through the ESPN app with an eligible television subscription.

The Tiger Radio Network will carry the Missouri broadcast, with longtime play-by-play announcer Mike Kelly leading the coverage.

Howard Richards will provide analysis, while Chris Gervino reports from the sideline.

Fans can listen through the Tiger Radio Network's statewide affiliates or stream the broadcast through the Varsity Network app.

SiriusXM also carries SEC football, although the specific satellite channel for Saturday's Missouri-Oklahoma matchup should be confirmed through the game-day listing.

Saturday's weather in Columbia should remain relatively mild for late November.

As of Wednesday, Nov. 25, the forecast calls for a high near 54 degrees, with increasing cloud cover during the afternoon.

Temperatures should hover around 53 degrees at kickoff before falling into the upper 40s during the evening.

Rain appears unlikely during the game, although fans should prepare for cooler conditions after sunset.

Missouri and Oklahoma will renew a familiar rivalry as SEC opponents.

The two programs spent decades competing in the Big Eight and Big 12 conferences before eventually joining the SEC.

Saturday's matchup provides Missouri an opportunity to close its regular season with a meaningful home victory.

Meanwhile, Oklahoma enters the contest ranked among the nation's top 25 teams, giving the Tigers another opportunity to make a statement.

Missouri has faced a challenging SEC schedule this season, including matchups against Georgia, Texas and Texas A&M.

However, the Tigers will have the benefit of playing their final regular-season game at Memorial Stadium.

For Missouri fans, the contest offers one last opportunity to support the Tigers at home before bowl season begins.
