The team is in safe hands, we want to delight Chivahermanos - Omar Govea

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Finishing the Apertura 2026 regular season with a win alongside the Red-and-White Nation is the goal for Guadalajara as they prepare to face Cruz Azul at Estadio AKRON. There is plenty at stake on Matchday 17 of the top flight, as the Guadalajara side and the Cement Makers will decide who finishes top of the table.

The squad and coaching staff are united in their confidence in the work and style of play the team has developed since Gabriel Milito took charge, as Omar Govea reaffirmed at a press conference this afternoon at Verde Valle. The red-and-white midfielder highlighted the strength of Chiverío, while offering a preview of what we can expect at the Home of All Mexicans against La Máquina.

Omar Govea's thoughts ahead of Matchday 17 of AP26

"It's an important match for both us and Cruz Azul. Our games against them have been very tight, and they're an opponent who will show us where we stand."

"It helps us focus on what's ahead. It will be a good spectacle for the fans, and we'll be looking to win. We want AKRON to be our fortress in the playoffs."

"There are little things we've lacked in past tournaments to take that next step, but we believe beating this opponent would give us a confidence boost going into the playoffs."

"The squad we've put together is capable of challenging for the title. We've had games where we've been solid in every aspect. Seeing this team compete fills me with pride."

"Seeing my teammates run, battle, play, and be smart about keeping possession and knowing when to attack. We're a great team, but this is where we have to prove it."

"Before the season started, we set ourselves the goal of finishing first. While finishing top is important, so is playing the second leg of our playoff ties at home. We'll go out this weekend looking for that win against Cruz Azul. We know how they play, but what matters to us is playing the return legs at home in the final phase."

"I focus on what my team can do and where we can improve. I think we're a very well-rounded team. Defensively, we're very solid, and offensively, we can be more clinical."

"Teams that take a big mental blow in one match and then stand up for the club and institution they represent in the next are the ones that can become champions. I think our team is like that. I genuinely feel we can compete for everything."

"We know the work we put in, we're aware of what we've failed to do, and we understand that to become champions, you have to be close to perfect. The group has a very healthy atmosphere, and the team is very solid in the way it plays."

"We're absolutely committed to becoming champions. Doing it at a club as big as Chivas stays with you forever. We don't let it overwhelm us; instead, we focus on what we have to do. We have a wonderful opportunity ahead of us, and it's time for this team and this squad to take on that responsibility."

"Putting in a good performance against Cruz Azul will give us a lot heading into the playoffs."

"I feel I'm at a wonderful stage in my life, and I'm really enjoying it. Chivas has made me feel like a footballer again, and that has helped me appreciate where I am. The only way I can repay the club is with my commitment."

"I want to tell the fans that we're in very good hands. The team plays very good football. Let's appreciate what we've built, because now we have the foundations and the tools to bring the fans that joy once again."

"It feels different when we have the full squad available. This isn't about one player. Whoever plays, the team performs at a high level."
