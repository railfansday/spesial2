Stephen A Smith says the Minnesota Vikings should be ‘ashamed’ of themselves

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Minnesota Vikings attempted to solve their issues at quarterback on Tuesday by trading for Kirk Cousins from the Las Vegas Raiders.

It is a return to Minnesota for Cousins after the quarterback departed the organisation back in 2023 to join the Atlanta Falcons. It is a trade that Minnesota have made in an attempt to try and play complementary football, given that their defense has considerably outplayed their offense.

How big a difference Cousins will make remains to be seen, considering he failed to beat any of the five contenders this season with the Raiders. Although the Vikings are pushing to go all the way this season, Stephen A. Smith has heavily criticised the organisation for how they've handled the QB position over recent years.

The Vikings clearly have a defensive unit capable of making it to the Super Bowl, but there has been a lot left to desire on offense. It has been a merry-go-round in the quarterback department over recent months in Minnesota and Smith has been left far from impressed.

"I went on the air this morning [Tuesday] and said Justin Jefferson should demand to be traded, and I meant it. And I hope that had an impact, with the Minnesota Vikings going out and getting Kirk Cousins. They should be ashamed of themselves that they allowed it to get to this point," Smith said on his YouTube channel.

"I didn't even bring up Addison and Hockenson and how bad those brothers are when they've got a quarterback. You don't have cats like that on your squad and not have a quarterback to throw them the football. Shameful, shameful. So maybe they're making amends by getting Kirk Cousins. We shall see."

Ever since the Vikings allowed Sam Darnold to leave the building for the Seattle Seahawks, the organisation has struggled to nail down their next quarterback. J.J. McCarthy has since been moved on, Kyler Murray has failed to impress, and Carson Wentz is what he is.

For a team that has a blue-chip receiver on their roster like Justin Jefferson, it is clear that Smith feels the Vikings are fortunate the WR has provided the organisation with so much patience, given his own stats have taken a hit as a result of the quarterback situation.

Cousins is now 38 years of age, so he is unlikely to be the long-term solution in Minnesota. What lies ahead for the organisation over the long term at quarterback remains a complete mystery.
