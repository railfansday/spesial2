Girls tennis: Conrad Weiser’s Juliet Perkins named BCIAA player of the year

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Conrad Weiser freshman Juliet Perkins, who won championships in singles and doubles this fall, has been named the BCIAA girls tennis player of the year by the conference's coaches.

Perkins won the BCIAA singles title and then combined with fellow freshman Natalie Perkins to win the county doubles title.

The pair then won the District 3 Class 2A doubles title and ended the season finshing second in the state tournament.

In the BCIAA singles tourney, Perkins won her first two matches 6-0, 6-0 before beating Wilson's Madison Uzar 6-1, 6-2 in the championship match.

Wilson's Todd Gensemer was named the coach of the year. The Bulldogs won the county title, beating Wyomissing 3-2 in the championship match.

The individual honors and team selections were done by a voting of the conference's coaches.
