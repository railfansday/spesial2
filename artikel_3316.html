2 Chainz Shares Video of Shocking Car Crash Scenes, Says He Broke His Ribs

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

2 Chainz is lucky to be alive after a recent highway crash ... because the video he posted shows extensive damage to his vehicle.

The rapper took to Instagram on Tuesday to share more of the scene from his accident early Monday morning ... and the video he posted shows both vehicles damaged beyond repair.

The video starts with a gray car spread across both lanes of the lonely road ... the front of the car completely smashed in.
