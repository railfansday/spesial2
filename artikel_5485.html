Continental is raising its full-year profit outlook after a strong quarter in its tire business

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The tire maker expects an adjusted EBIT margin of around 13.4% to 14.0% this year, up from a prior range of 12.0% to 13.5%

Continental raised its full-year outlook for adjusted EBIT margin and adjusted free cash flow on Wednesday after its tire business posted third-quarter results, the company said.

For continuing operations, Continental expects an adjusted EBIT margin of around 13.4% to 14.0%, compared with 12.0% to 13.5% previously. It projects adjusted free cash flow of around €1.0 billion to €1.3 billion, up from €0.7 billion to €1.1 billion. The company narrowed its sales forecast to around €13.5 billion to €13.9 billion from €13.2 billion to €14.2 billion.

Continental stock gained roughly 6% on Wednesday during early trading in Europe.

Third-quarter adjusted EBIT rose 6.7% to €514 million from €482 million a year earlier. The adjusted EBIT margin increased to 14.9% from 13.9%. Sales from continuing operations fell to €3.5 billion from €3.9 billion, due to the sale of the Original Equipment Solutions business area at the beginning of February. Organic sales declined 1.5%.

Net income for continuing and discontinued operations was €349 million, compared with a loss of €756 million a year earlier. The prior-year quarter carried special effects from the Aumovio spin-off and preparations for the Original Equipment Solutions sale. Adjusted free cash flow for continuing operations fell 69.0% to €31 million from €99 million.

Adjusted EBIT topped the €478.4 million that analysts expected, based on a company-compiled consensus, according to The Wall Street Journal. A U.S. tariff refund in the double-digit millions of euros and inventory accounting effects lifted the figure, Bernstein analyst Harry Martin said in a note.

Continental credited the results to earnings in Europe and Asia. Higher sales of ultra-high-performance tires, which measure 18 inches and above, positive exchange-rate effects, lower tariff impacts and commercial measures taken in response to higher raw-material prices contributed. Weak replacement markets hurt earnings in North and South America, and weak automotive markets weighed on original-equipment business.

The adjusted EBIT margin in the EMEA region rose to 18.7% from 17.7%, and the Americas margin rose to 8.7% from 8.5%. The APAC margin slipped to 18.6% from 19.0%.

"We delivered another very good quarter in the tire business. We have raised our outlook in line with these results and head into the challenging final stretch of the year in a strong position," CEO Christian Kötz said in a statement.

CFO Roland Welzbacher said higher raw-material prices "are increasingly affecting our earnings and will impact us even more in the fourth quarter."

Continental has excluded its ContiTech group sector from the outlook for continuing operations since Aug. 4 because of its planned sale. For ContiTech, the company expects 2026 sales of around €4.2 billion to €4.4 billion and an adjusted EBIT margin of around 6.5% to 7.5%. The prior ranges were €4.2 billion to €4.8 billion and 7.0% to 8.5%.
