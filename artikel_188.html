Tesla planning €3BN investment in German factory to produce Cybercabs

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Elon Musk-owned company is looking to further expand its only European production line. Brandenburg's state leader has welcomed the decision.

The electric car manufacturer Tesla said on Monday it was planning to invest €3 billion ($3.3 billion) in its German factory near Berlin so that it can start producing Cybercabs.

The plan to start production on the driverless taxis in Tesla's only European factory comes after billionaire owner Elon Musk announced last week that the company was shutting down Cybertruck production.

Construction work at the Grünheide complex is set to begin in 2027, with the first vehicles coming off the production line in 2029, according to company plans.

Capacity is set to reach 1 million units, according to the company, although the timeframe for the goal was unclear.

Employment at the so-called "gigafactory" in the state of Brandenburg had dipped to around 11,000 before rising to 12,600 in October. The new expansion was expected to lead to the hiring of several hundred additional staff.

"With this, we are laying the foundations for the world's most technologically advanced Tesla factory here in Grünheide," Tesla said.

The company said it would submit an application for the new building by the end of the year.

Brandenburg's State Premier Dietmar Woidke welcomed the decision to expand the factory in his state.

"The €3 billion investment, and the prospect of producing up to a million Cybercabs in Grünheide in the future, is an enormously important economic decision," Woidke said in a statement.

"Especially in economically challenging times, this sends a strong message: Germany, even today, remains an attractive location for major industrial investments, and Brandenburg is showing how it's done," he added.

After a slump in sales put the expansion plans on hold, an uptick in demand he seen Tesla's fortunes back on the up.

It recently said it aims to increase weekly vehicle production to 7,500 units, equivalent to 375,000 a year. It also wants to expand its battery cell production on site.
