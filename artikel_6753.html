These Beauty Advent Calendars Under $100 Are Such a Good Deal, It’s Almost Suspicious

They won’t break the bank, and you’ll actually want the products inside. It’s a win-win.

As we all know, the holidays can get prettyyyy pricey. My bank account starts begging for a break the minute I finish putting together the list of friends and family I need to buy for. But as a beauty editor and someone who tries her very best to be a good gift giver, I’ll be the first to admit that makeup, skincare, and fragrance are pretty dang hard to say no to. In my experience, the best beauty advent calendars, whether it’s a gift for my best friend or for my teenage cousins, never fail. They basically come wrapped, plus they give your giftee the opportunity to try a bunch of new products (and maybe even discover a few new favorites) without committing to a full-size version of everything.

Plus, good news for your wallet: You don’t have to spend hundreds of dollars to get in on the fun. There are plenty of beauty advent calendars under $100 that are packed with everything from cult-favorite makeup to essentials for your skincare routine, all for a price that won’t make you cringe at checkout. I’ve rounded up the calendars that are actually worth the money—and that I’d be genuinely excited to unwrap myself. Check ‘em out below.
