Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Fortnite Treasure Hunters is now live, and the mini-season brings plenty of One Piece-themed content to enjoy. The mini-season is highlighted by multiple NPCs you can find all around the map. To make matters more interesting, you can hire all of them for free.

In this guide, I will show you their locations so you can find them on the map.

There are eight NPCs to find, and five of them are in the southern part of the map. You’ll find another one to the far left of the map, another on the far right, and the remaining one is up north.
