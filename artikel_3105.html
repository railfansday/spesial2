Hims & Hers Stock Just Plummeted 17%. Is the Stock a Buy Ahead of a Peptide Push?

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Hims & Hers stock crashed after issuing weak guidance.

A difficult year for Hims & Hers Health (HIMS +0.41%)got worse after the stock price plummeted 17% following its Q3 earnings report. While the company's quarterly results were essentially in line with expectations, its Q4 guidance fell short. The stock is now down more than 40% on the year.

However, with a setup for a potential peptide push, now could be the time to buy this growth stock. Let's take a closer look at the telehealth stock's most recent results and prospects.

With the U.S. government allowing compounding pharmacies to begin selling peptide compounds, Hims & Hers will launch its new Peptides+ category at the end of the month. The company has been preparing for this by investing in its supply chain and manufacturing facilities and plans to start by offering sermorelin, glutathione, and NAD+ at launch. BPC-157 and TB-500, which have shown strong promise in healing soft-tissue injuries, will be added later "when the regulatory environment permits."

However, the company said its peptide launch won't materially impact its Q4 results, and its quarterly forecast fell short of analyst expectations. The company projected Q4 revenue to rise between 42% and 45% to a range of $875 million and $895 million, while adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA) are forecast to be between $70 million and $90 million. Analysts were looking for revenue of $955.1 million.

For Q3, Hims & Hers reported sales that climbed 49% year over year to $890.4 million. That was around the midpoint of its $880 million to $900 million guidance. U.S. revenue rose 21% to $657.1 million, while international revenue skyrocketed 321% to $233.3 million.

The company continued to face gross margin pressure in the quarter, with it falling 1,700 basis points year over year to 57%. This was largely due to increased sales of lower-margin branded weight-loss drugs and the scaling of its international business, which carries lower margins.

However, Hims & Hers has been able to leverage its marketing spending, which fell to 28% of revenue from 39% a year ago.

Adjusted EBITDA increased 5% from $78.4 million to $82.4 million. Adjusted earnings per share (EPS) came in at $0.03, down from $0.08 a year ago.

Data source: Hims & Hers Health. YOY = year over year.

Hims & Hers is not without its warts as it navigates increasing sales of lower-margin branded weight-loss drugs while expanding internationally, where margins are also lower. However, the company is still growing its gross profit dollars and leveraging its marketing spend, which should eventually lead to renewed profitability growth.

At the same time, I think the company's move into peptides could be a huge boost. Peptides show great medical promise, but there isn't much human testing for some of them, and they have not been approved by the FDA, despite a robust grey market.

From a valuation standpoint, the stock trades at a forward price-to-earnings (P/E ) ratio below 26 based on the analyst consensus for 2027. The company's expenses are outpacing its peptide opportunity, so I think it should see some nice operating leverage as future sales begin to flow in.

Overall, Hims and Hers remains a high-risk, high-reward stock, but I think this dip could be a nice buying opportunity.
