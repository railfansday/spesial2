New system enables highly sensitive detection of trace hydrogen

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A research team led by professor Fang Yonghua at the Hefei Institutes of Physical Science of the Chinese Academy of Sciences has developed a new system for detecting trace amounts of hydrogen that improves detection sensitivity by reducing background interference, enabling it to directly detect hydrogen at a concentration of one part per million (ppm).
