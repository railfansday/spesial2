Comedian Morgan Jay Launches ‘Music Therapy’ Show With Guests Kam Patterson, Josh Johnson and More (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Morgan Jay, the stand-up comedian best known for his viral auto-tuned crowd-work songs, has launched a new show on YouTube called “Music Therapy.”

The interview series, which debuted on Wednesday with first guest Kam Patterson (“Saturday Night Live”), is part talk show, part improvised jam session. In each episode, Jay inhabits the role of a musical therapist, blending unscripted, intimate conversation with games and live songwriting. At the end, each guest will create a song or jingle with Jay. Following Patterson, the season lineup includes comedian Ashby (Nov. 25), rapper Yung Gravy (Dec 2.), “Dimension 20” game master Brennan Lee Mulligan, “Love on the Spectrum” star Abbey Romeo, actor and author Mara Wilson, comedian Mark Smalls, Los Angeles Chargers safety Tony Jefferson, impressionist Matt Friend and comedians Josh Johnson, Ron Funches and Mandal.

“It’s cool to finally get to a point in my career where I can make cool things with my friends that show who I am. And part of who I am is who I surround myself with,” Jay said in a statement. “I couldn’t be happier to work with Hello Party on this. We have such a great collaborative team, and I know we will make something new and unique in the space.”

“Music Therapy With Morgan Jay” is developed and produced by Austin-based creative agency Hello Party and financed by Live Nation Studios, which also provides access to its talent roster for guest appearances.

“The industry is changing, and agencies that want to stay relevant in the creator economy are going to have to pivot toward actually being creators,” said Ty Stafford, Hello Party’s founder and CEO. “‘Music Therapy’ is proof that branded entertainment can start with a great idea and, with the right partners in place, become something audiences actually want to watch.”

“At Live Nation Studios, we’re always looking for new ways to bring music and comedy together, and ‘Music Therapy’ is exactly the kind of original, creator-driven content we’re excited to branch into,” added Ryan Kroft, head of film and television at Live Nation Studios. “Morgan is a one-of-a-kind storyteller and partnering with him and Hello Party gives us an opportunity to bring that creativity to an even bigger audience as a digital series.”

“Music Therapy With Morgan Jay” was created by Jay and Stafford and is directed by Stafford. Brooke Schley serves as showrunner and co-writes the series with Jay. Executive producers are Stafford, Graham Sicalowski, Robin Rapino, Stella Bulochnikov, Brooke Schley and Matt Sadeghian.

Jay was named one of Variety‘s 10 Comics to Watch in 2025. He has become one of the fastest-growing comedians in recent years, amassing more than 20 million followers across social platforms. That virality has translated to hundreds of thousands of tickets sold and a recent comedy special, “Live at the Village,” which is streaming on Hulu.

As an actor, Jay’s credits include “Love Language,” “Stick,” “St. Denis Medical” and “Wonder Man.” He’ll next appear opposite Pete Holmes in the indie feature “The Big Kill.”
