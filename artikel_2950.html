Victor Wembanyama: Spurs’ Game 4 loss to Knicks ‘the most intense heartbreak moment in my career’

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Spurs star Victor Wembanyama didn't sugarcoat his feelings about San Antonio's loss to the Knicks in the 2026 NBA Finals.

The 7-foot-4 center recalled Game 4 specifically — in which his side blew a 29-point lead in a 107-106 loss at Madison Square Garden on June 10 — during an interview with "60 Minutes" on Sunday.

"I think there's two. I think… there's three," Wembanyama said. "I would say Game 1 [of the Western Conference Finals] in OKC, Game 7 [WCF] in OKC, and Game 4 in New York."

When asked about Game 4, Wembanyama said, "Because it's the most intense heartbreak moment in my career."

Despite being favored in the Finals, the Spurs lost in five games to the Knicks, culminating in a 94-90 Game 5 loss in San Antonio in which the Knicks secured their first championship in 53 years.

San Antonio Spurs forward Victor Wembanyama reacts to a call during Game 5 of the NBA Finals at Frost Bank Center, Saturday, June 13, 2026. Jason Szenes for NY Post

Wembanyama explained that it didn't bother him that he was portrayed as a villain during the Finals — specifically after his viral shove of Knicks guard Jalen Brunson in Game 3.

"Because I'm not paying attention to this during the Finals. And I don't think any player on either team really is," he said.

"It showed my limits more than anything else going out there, you need to play basketball. You don't need to be beating the s–t out of people, if you want to win at least."

New York Knicks guard Jalen Brunson (11) drives to the basket as San Antonio Spurs forward Victor Wembanyama (1) defends in Game 2 of the NBA Finals on Friday, June 5, 2026. Jason Szenes for the New York Post

Wembanyama was candid on a number of emotional topics, including his 2025 blood clot diagnosis in his shoulder, which shut down his second NBA season.

"It had a huge impact on this," Wembanyama said regarding him being less afraid to say what he thinks. "After that, life is so short, I don't even want to waste it being somebody else. Simple as that."

Wembanyama added that he should win the NBA MVP this year.

In six games for the Spurs this season, he is averaging 28.0 points, 10.7 rebounds, 3.0 assists, 4.2 blocks, and 1.3 steals.
