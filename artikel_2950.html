Kandi (KNDI) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Battery swapping orders and new product launches shape the outlook.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 19, 2026 at 2:53PM EST

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Kandi Technologies (KNDI +0.59%) reported revenue growth and a return to operating profitability for the first nine months of 2026, supported by its core off-road electric vehicle business and the integration of the Rawrr brand. Management stated that the company is executing a dual-engine strategy focused on North American market expansion and the commercialization of battery swapping equipment for heavy-duty trucks. The company reported a strong cash position of $291.3 million, providing a foundation for upcoming product launches and manufacturing capacity expansions. Strategic developments during the period included a new retail partnership with a North American home improvement retailer and expanded cooperation with CATL.

Our businesses continued to grow, and our strategic initiatives increasingly translated into tangible operating results. Meanwhile, we continued to upgrade our products and optimize our sales channels in our core off-road electric vehicle business, strengthening the foundation for further growth.

Mr: Turning to our financial performance, for the first nine months of 2026, total revenue reached $82.1 million, up 38.5% year-over-year. Revenue from our core off-road electric vehicle business grew 33.5% to $74 million, including $18 million contributed by Rawrr. Alongside revenue growth, we continue to improve operating efficiency. We were profitable at the operating level for the first nine months, turning around from an operating loss in the same period last year and making a meaningful improvement in overall operating performance. As of September 30, our cash and cash equivalent, restricted cash and certificates of deposit totaled $291.3 million, providing a solid financial foundation to support our next phase of development.

Mr: Our business and financial results for the first nine months further demonstrate our progress in translating strategic initiatives into tangible operating results. We will continue to strengthen our off-road electric vehicle business, advance the commercialization of battery swapping equipment, and pursue market opportunities in backup power and energy storage for AI data centers, building momentum for sustainable growth.

Mr: Next, I would like to share more details on our progress in the third quarter and recent developments, as well as our priorities going forward.

Mr: Let me begin with our new business initiatives. Our battery swapping equipment business continued to make steady progress, and we further deepened our strategic cooperation with CATL. Supported by reliable product quality and service capabilities, our subsidiary, China Battery Exchange, secured a total of four orders from CATL subsidiary, QIJI Energy, in the third quarter, covering 33 heavy-duty truck battery swapping stations. These orders provide additional support for the business further development.

Mr: At the same time, we made encouraging progress in strengthening our production and delivery capabilities for battery swapping equipment.

Mr: During the third quarter, we organized production of heavy-duty truck battery swapping equipment in four batches, with a total of 32 units entering production. Of these, manufacturing was completed for 17 units, and a cumulative 11 units were installed and delivered on site. This reflects our steady progress in converting orders into production output and completed projects, as well as our growing capabilities in commercial execution.

Mr: On capacity expansion, China Battery Exchange's new manufacturing facility is progressing as planned through a phased renovation and production ramp-up approach. The first renovated area commenced operations on September 5, while renovation of remaining main production area is expected to be completed in the next couple weeks. As the new facility gradually becomes fully operational, we expect to further strengthen our manufacturing capacity and delivery capabilities at scale. With a target production capacity of 36 units per month in 2027. This will provide a solid foundation for fulfilling further orders and expanding the business.

Mr: Looking ahead, we will continue to align production with customer orders and actual delivery requirements, optimize our manufacturing operations, improve product quality and delivery efficiency, and pursue additional commercial opportunities. Through consistent and reliable project execution, we aim to translate our order book and capacity investments into a more stable revenue stream over time.

Mr: Turning to our core business, North America remains our key market for off-road electric vehicles. Throughout this year, we have continued to expand our dealer network, optimize sales channels, and upgrade our products to strengthen the competitiveness of our core business.

Mr: In the third quarter, we took an important step in expanding our sales channels by establishing a partnership with a leading home improvement retailer in North America. Kandi UTV products have begun entering its sales channel. By leveraging the retailer's extensive retail network and customer base, we see opportunities to broaden our market reach, increase brand visibility, and generate additional sales. Meanwhile, we continue to optimize our dealer network and develop a more diversified channel mix to enhance market coverage and sales efficiency in North America.

Mr: On the product side, we continue to optimize inventory and upgrade our product lineup. Clearance of slow-selling models and non-new vehicles is progressing according to plan, and we continue to target completion in the fourth quarter of this year. At the same time, to strengthen product competitiveness and operating efficiency, we are refining our golf cart and UTV product portfolio and transitioning the Kandi brand to a refreshed product offering. We expect to complete the related upgrades in the fourth quarter of this year, with new products scheduled to arrive in phases in the first quarter of 2027.

We will closely coordinate new product introductions with channel expansion to better meet North American market demand and support the long-term development of our core business.

Mr: As of Rawrr, we continue to leverage Kandi's R&D, manufacturing, supply chain, and distribution resources to support its premier electric off-road motorcycle business. In the third quarter, we steadily advanced new product development to enhance competitiveness and strengthen Rawrr's differentiated brand positioning. Development is progressing well, with new products expected to launch progressively beginning in the first quarter of 2027. Looking ahead, we will further align product development with our sales channels to support new launches and market expansion, creating additional momentum for Rawrr's long-term development.

Mr: Looking ahead, we will remain committed to our dual-engine strategy, maintaining a long-term perspective while continuously improving operational quality and our ability to generate returns on capital.

Mr: First, we will continue executing battery swapping equipment orders and scaling up deliveries, converting commercial opportunities into firm orders. Second, we will sustain momentum in our North American off-road electric vehicle business through product portfolio upgrades and channel optimization, improving product competitiveness and sales efficiency. Third, we will deepen Rawrr's integration, strengthening product development, supply chain, and distribution capabilities, and prepare for new product launches and future growth in 2027.

Mr: We believe we have entered a new phase where our strategic initiatives are beginning to deliver tangible operating results. Looking ahead, continued product upgrades, the scaling of our emerging businesses, and further expansion into overseas markets will help us build a stronger, more resilient foundation for sustainable growth. We will remain disciplined in our capital allocation, aligning resources with actual business needs and continuously improving capital efficiency. Through focused execution, we aim to translate our strategic strengths into sustained operating performance and create long-term sustainable value for our shareholders.

Mr: Now, let me turn the call over to our CFO, Alan Lim, who will provide details on our financial performance in the first nine months of 2026. Thank you.

Alan Lim: Thank you, Mr. Chen and Kewa. Thank you everyone for joining us today. I will go over our financial results for the third quarter and first nine months of 2026. The net revenues were $82.1 million, up 38.5% from $59.3 million for the same period of 2025, mainly due to an increase in sales of off-road vehicles, as well as the revenue contributed by Rawrr, which the company acquired in February 2026. The cost of goods sold was $50.4 million, up 56% from $32.3 million for the same period of 2025, mainly due to the corresponding increase in sales. The gross profit was $31.7 million, up 17.6% from $26.9 million for the same period of 2025.

The gross margin was 38.6%, compared with 45.5% for the same period of 2025, mainly due to the changes in product mix and the relatively lower gross margin on sales of Rawrr products. The total operating expenses were $30.9 million, down 5.7% from $32.8 million for the same period of 2025. The R&D expenses were $4 million, up 4.2% from $3.9 million for the same period of 2025. The selling and marketing expenses were $7.2 million, down 34.3% from $11 million for the same period of 2025, mainly due to the savings in advertising expenses resulting from the optimizations to the company's approach and distribution channels.

The G&A expenses were $19.7 million, up 9.5% from $18 million for the same period of 2025. Overall, the income from operations was $0.8 million compared with a loss from operations of $5.8 million for the same period of 2025. The net income was $5.6 million compared with a net loss of $6.3 million for the same period of 2025, primarily driven by the higher net revenues and gross profit. A year-over-year decrease in operating expenses and a reduction net from the $8.3 million in the same period for 2025 to $1 million in the current period.

The basic and diluted net income attributable to the company shareholders were $0.58 per share, compared with a basic and diluted net loss of $0.55 per share for the same period of 2025. The per-share amounts for the all periods reflect the 10 for one share consolidation effective in October 16, 2026. Turning to our balance sheet. Our financial position remains strong. As of September 30, 2026, the company had cash and cash equivalents, restricted cash, and certificates of deposit totaling $291.3 million, compared with $211.9 million as of December 31, 2025.
