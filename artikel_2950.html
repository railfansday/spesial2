Nique Clifford Adjusting to Life With the Thunder, Embracing New Opportunity in Oklahoma City

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Joining a new team in the middle of a season is never an easy adjustment, especially when that team is dealing with injuries, limited practice time and a schedule that doesn't leave much room to get comfortable.

For Nique Clifford, that's exactly the situation he walked into when he arrived in Oklahoma City.

But a few games into his Thunder tenure, Clifford is beginning to settle in, learn his new teammates and take advantage of an opportunity he didn't necessarily see coming.

"It's been amazing, just getting on the floor, being able to be on the floor, be able to learn and grow through experience. That's a blessing in and of itself," Clifford said.

"And then to do it with an organization like this, it's just been super fun to just learn from some of the best, from coaching staff to players."

The transition hasn't been without its challenges. Clifford joined a team that was already in the middle of its season, with injuries forcing players into different roles and responsibilities. There wasn't much time to ease into things, either.

For a young player trying to establish himself, learning a new system while also figuring out where he fits can be a difficult balancing act.

"I mean, it's definitely tough, but it's part of the profession. First time I went through it, so it was a little bit of an adjustment for me," Clifford explained.

Still, he believes he landed in the right situation. The opportunity to play, develop and earn the trust of the people around him has made the adjustment a little easier.

"But like I said, I ended up in a great situation where I'm able to grow and be myself, and they believe and trust in me," Clifford said. "So it's been pretty easy to transition into it, but it's always tough going into a new environment where you don't know nobody. They don't know you, and then just trying to earn that trust and that respect from your teammates and from the coaching staff."

There is also an interesting connection between Clifford and Oklahoma City that dates back to the NBA Draft. The Thunder originally selected him before trading his draft rights to Sacramento. He had already worked out in Oklahoma City and spent time getting familiar with people inside the organization before eventually finding his way back.

"Yeah, I worked out here, met with everybody. So I kinda had some familiarity, knowing certain people around the building," Clifford said. "They ended up trading that pick, obviously, to Sacramento. But they were technically the ones who did draft me, which is crazy. So ending up back here is pretty wild."

Beyond basketball, Clifford has also enjoyed getting acquainted with Oklahoma City. The reception from the community has made an impression, particularly the way fans have welcomed him without making everyday activities uncomfortable.

"I think just the love, the fan base since I've been here," Clifford said. "Even just walking around, going furniture shopping or trying to get groceries and stuff like that, people have just shown love."

He appreciates that Oklahoma City fans have made an effort to welcome him while still allowing him to enjoy some normalcy away from the court.

"They're super cool, they don't come up trying to go crazy on you. They just welcome to the city and just keep it pushing," Clifford said. "You're just a regular dude, so I've appreciated that."

Of course, the atmosphere inside Paycom Center has been another part of the experience.

"At the games, the fan base is high level. It's electric in the building, they really support the team. So that's been awesome," he said.

Clifford also credited the personalities inside the Thunder locker room for helping him feel comfortable. Being surrounded by teammates who bring energy and positivity every day has made a difference as he continues learning his new surroundings.

"And then just my team, being around all the guys, their energy and their positivity each and every day, I feel like is rubbed off on me," Clifford said. "And it's fun to be around every day."

For Clifford, the focus now is continuing to build on the opportunity in front of him. He arrived under difficult circumstances, but the early experience has given him a chance to learn, contribute and become more comfortable in Oklahoma City.

The Thunder may have added him to help address their immediate roster needs, but Clifford is also looking at this as an opportunity to grow with an organization that has already made him feel welcome.
