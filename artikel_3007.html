BingEx (FLX) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Revenue slipped as delivery rivals intensified, but AI now handles 90% of service cases.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 24, 2026 at 2:57PM EST

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

BingEx (FLX -1.99%) reported a year-over-year decline in total revenue as competition in the Chinese on-demand delivery market intensified. Management maintained a stable gross margin through optimized merchant acquisition and disciplined spending while significantly expanding the role of artificial intelligence across research and operational functions. The company launched a new service category for everyday assistance and deepened integrations with major platform ecosystems to diversify user acquisition channels. Strategic focus remained on high-value delivery segments and improving the fulfillment capacity of the courier network while executing a multi-million dollar share repurchase program.

As a reminder, this conference is being recorded.

In addition, a webcast replay of this conference call will be available on the BingEx company's IR website at ir.ishansong.com. Furthermore, throughout the call, we will constantly use the company brand name FlashEx to refer to its publicly listed entity, BingEx Limited. Joining us today from FlashEx senior management are Mr. Adam Xue, Founder, Chairman of the Board and Chief Executive Officer; Mr. Hongjian Yu, Co-Founder, Director and Executive President; and Mr. Luke Tang, Chief Financial Officer. I will now turn the call over to Mr. Adam Xue.

Peng (Adam) Xue: Thank you, Helen. Hello, everyone, and welcome to FlashEx's Third Quarter 2026 Earnings Call. Competition in the on-demand delivery industry remained intense in the third quarter, responded by sharpening our execution and strengthening our differentiated on-demand dedicated courier model. We improved user acquisition efficiency, our merchant mix and fulfillment experience while also expanding into new scenarios and new cities at a measured pace. Doing this work well is what positions FlashEx for long-term growth. Let me start with our overall operations. We fulfilled 62.8 million orders in the third quarter, largely in line with the second quarter and average delivery time held steady at around 25.3 minutes.

As of the end of the third quarter, our registered users increased to 128 million, registered Flash Riders rose to 3.33 million and our service network expanded to 301 cities. As we keep growing our user base and delivery capacity, we are focusing more on turning these resources into reliable service capabilities while steadily improving operating efficiency. Turning to our financial performance. Total revenues for the third quarter were RMB 929.4 million with a gross margin of 11.2%. Non-GAAP income from operations was RMB 16.1 million. As of the end of the third quarter, our cash and cash equivalents, restricted cash and short-term investments totaled RMB 816.4 million, reflecting a healthy financial position.

We also remained committed to enhancing shareholder returns through share repurchases. As of November 23, we had repurchased a total of approximately 4.9 million ADS for an aggregate consideration of approximately USD 13.3 million. Now let me walk through our business in more detail. On the individual user side, we prioritize acquisition efficiency and the conversion of new users. We concentrated our marketing spend on higher converting channels in the third quarter, lowering new user acquisition cost by more than 30% quarter-over-quarter. First order conversion and order volume from new users both increased. Beyond our own channels, we are also reaching users by integrating our services into the ecosystem of major platforms.

Orders through our Quick Apps in the Huawei HarmonyOS ecosystem grew 27% quarter-over-quarter. Our partnership with Alipay expanded from assisted purchasing to its lifestyle services channel and users have begun placing orders through the Zhixiaobao AI agent. In terms of use cases, both our core categories and our diverse everyday lifestyle scenarios continued to grow. In cakes, a long-standing core category for us, order volume increased 19% year-over-year in the third quarter. This growth was driven by stronger gifting demand and stable partnerships with leading cake brands, reflecting the trust FlashEx has built with high-quality merchants over the years.

On the everyday side, total order volume across our 5 lifestyle scenarios grew 3.5% quarter-over-quarter, led by food pickup and parcel pickup. These everyday needs also point us in a clear direction for expanding our lifestyle services. With this in mind, we launched our new service, Flash Helper or Flash Assist in the third quarter. It brings the standardized processes and attentive one-on-one service to everyday needs such as waiting in line at restaurants, handling small tasks and moving or carrying items for users. Demand for this kind of assistance has long existed but remains highly fragmented.

Early results show that new users account for high share of Flash Helper orders, making the service an additional channel for expanding our user base and extending user lifetime. We will keep refining the service and use our existing Flash Rider network to explore more services that fit users' daily needs. For users with higher service expectations, we continue to refine our premium delivery service in the third quarter. Premium delivery order volume grew significantly quarter-over-quarter, and the number of new users increased by approximately 37%. This service further diversifies our product tiers, so we can better meet users' varying needs for speed and attention to detail.

On the merchant side, we focused on improving both the quality of our merchant base and our service capabilities while deepening our relationships with existing merchants. We concentrated on raising the quality of newly acquired merchants. To that end, we ran a pilot in 6 cities in northern China, where we optimized resource allocation based on merchants' operating profiles and flexibly adjusted pricing strategies to market conditions in each channel and city, balancing merchant acquisition with ongoing operations. These adjustments are already paying off with gross profit improving in the pilot cities. For our KA business, we moved into a tiered operating model, managing national and regional accounts separately, leveraging the stable high-density order flow from our enterprise clients channels.

We have built a strong delivery competitive base in core business districts, which in turn is driving new merchant acquisition and revenue growth in these areas. In the third quarter, average daily order volume from KA merchants grew quarter-over-quarter, and their share of GMV rose to 17.6%. As the quality of merchant business continues to improve, our business has become even more resilient. For entering new cities and new districts, we adopted a more return-driven expansion model. Our existing teams and our partners run this market directly, drawing on the people and experience we already have to get started quickly with lower upfront investment.

In the third quarter, average daily order volume in newly launched cities and districts rose sharply from the second quarter with some areas turning profitable within a short period. These results provide a proven template for future expansion. Going forward, we will replicate the model progressively, tailoring our approach to local market conditions as we enter new cities. Whether we are serving existing users or developing new services, reliable fulfillment always comes first. In the third quarter, we strengthened our delivery capacity by starting with the Flash Rider experience. We refined our tiered growth framework for Flash Riders with development paths for those riding regularly, those riding flexibly and those building advanced skills.

We also introduced an owner certification that recognizes Flash Riders for strong service and encourage them to keep improving. As Flash Rider income rose steadily, we saw a higher share of regular active Flash Riders and better fulfillment quality with the perfect order rate climbing further and the complaints continue to fall. Order completion rates also improved year-over-year during peak demand periods and in high-density areas such as holidays and core business districts. A stable, well-supported Flash Rider team ultimately translates into a more reliable experience for our users. Yesterday, we celebrated our ninth annual Flash Rider Day together with Flash Riders across the country.

Since we established the day in 2018, we have marked every November 3 by recognizing those who stand out for their professional service and good deeds in the community and by thanking all of our Flash Riders through online and offline events and benefits. Going forward, we will keep investing in safety, skills training, career development and everyday support, creating a better working environment and giving our Flash Riders more room to grow. Our Flash Riders' experience on the road are also helping more users understand the value of FlashEx.

In the third quarter, we built content around real-life Flash Rider stories and expanded our network of around 690 Flash Rider KOLs beyond Beijing to more cities using AI tools to produce and review content more efficiently. For the first time, we also combined a celebrity partnership, holiday timing and a new service in a single campaign, promoting Flash Helper's everyday assistance services during the Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day holidays. Our brand communications focus on showing users what FlashEx can do for them in specific real-life situations so that every service experience gradually adds up to trust in our brand.

On the technology front, we continued to put AI into practice to improve efficiency across R&D, customer service and operations. Efficiency gains in these areas increased from 30% in the second quarter to 46% in the third quarter. In R&D, the share of AI-assisted coding exceeded 98%. At the end of September, Super Agent, our self-developed internal development agent, completed its first development tasks end-to-end on its own. Next, we will extend AI further into requirement analysis, testing and release with the goal of shortening development cycles. In customer service, AI has moved beyond standardized Q&A into more open-ended inquiries, now handling close to 90% of cases in core scenarios allow our human agents to concentrate on more complex requests.

In operations, AI tools are increasingly used by our frontline teams. They are gradually moving into a more efficient digital way of working at every level from headquarters to the frontline. Our low-altitude delivery business moved into routine operations in the third quarter. By the end of the quarter, we have built 15 takeoff and landing sites and 24 2-way delivery routes in Hangzhou, covering the city's main districts. Order volume grew 13% quarter-over-quarter. Next, we will focus on 3 areas to further extend the service coverage and capabilities, building a denser network coordinating across channels and partnering with government and other enterprises. Looking ahead, we will focus on improving new users conversion and optimizing our merchant mix.

Building on our existing capabilities, we will also steadily expand our service scenarios and city network. We will also continue to invest in enhancing the experience of both our Flash Riders and our users and deepen the application of AI across our business to deliver better service with greater efficiency. For 12 years, FlashEx has built its business around on-demand dedicated courier services. Our focus has always been the same, providing reliable service the user needed at urgent and important moments. Going forward, with every high-quality delivery and service, we will keep building the trust that the users and merchants place in FlashEx and make that trust the foundation of a long-term sustainable growth. That concludes my remarks.

Now I will turn the call over to our CFO, Luke Tang. Thank you.

Le (Luke) Tang: Thank you, Adam. Hello, everyone. This is Luke. I would like now to walk you through our third quarter 2026 financial results. During the third quarter, our unique on-demand dedicated courier model remained resilient in a competitive market with order volume holding steady quarter-over-quarter. Backed by a disciplined approach to spending and a healthy cash position, we have the flexibility to keep investing in fulfillment quality while exploring new services and use cases. We also remain committed to enhancing shareholder returns through our repurchase program. Before I begin, please note that all numbers are in renminbi and all percentage changes are on year-over-year basis, unless otherwise noted.

Our revenues for the third quarter were RMB 929.4 million compared with RMB 1,005.4 million in the same period of 2025. The decrease was primarily driven by intensifying market competition. Our cost of revenues for the third quarter was RMB 824.8 million compared with RMB 893.6 million in the same period of 2025. The decrease was in line with the decline in revenues. Our gross profit was RMB 104.5 million for the third quarter compared with RMB 111.8 million in the same period of 2025, representing a gross profit margin of 11.2% compared with 11.1% in the same period of 2025. Turning to operating expenses.

Our total operating expenses for the third quarter were RMB 93.5 million, representing a decrease of 4.3% from RMB 97.7 million in the same period of 2025. They consisted of RMB 40.3 million in selling and marketing expenses, RMB 36.6 million in general and administrative expenses and RMB 16.6 million in research and development expenses. The decrease in operating expenses was mainly attributable to the reduction in advertising expenses and share-based compensation expenses. Our income from operations was RMB 11.1 million compared with RMB 14.1 million in the same period of 2025. Excluding share-based compensation expenses, our non-GAAP income from operations was RMB 16.1 million for the third quarter compared with RMB 23.7 million in the same period of 2025.

Our net loss was RMB 6.8 million compared with net income of RMB 43.7 million in the same period of 2025. The change was mainly due to an investment loss of RMB 19.7 million in the third quarter compared with investment income of RMB 33.2 million in the same period of 2025. Excluding changes in fair value of long-term investments and share-based compensation expenses, our non-GAAP net loss was RMB 1 million compared with non-GAAP net income of RMB 62.6 million in the same period of 2025. Our cash position remained healthy with cash and cash equivalents, restricted cash and short-term investments totaling RMB 816.4 million as of the third quarter end.

We also continue to carry out share repurchases under our existing buyback program. As of November 23, we had repurchased a total of approximately 4.9 million ADS in the open market for an aggregate consideration of approximately USD 13.3 million. This underscores our confidence in the company's long-term value. As we close out 2026, we remain committed to disciplined execution and to the high-quality service that differentiates us with a distinctive business model and an improving merchant mix and the deeper use of AI across our operations. We are confident in our ability to create sustainable long-term value for our shareholders. That concludes our prepared remarks. We would like to open the floor to your questions.
