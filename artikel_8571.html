Get the Discover it® Student Cash Back if you’re a student who’ll activate its 5% categories every quarter and pay your balance in full. You can apply with no credit score, and the card matches your first year’s cash back dollar for dollar. If you won’t track categories, pick a flat-rate student card.

Discover is now part of Capital One, so you apply through Capital One and manage the card in its app, though it still runs on the Discover network. Its non-student version, the Discover it Cash Back, earns the same way and is the one to apply for after graduation.

It suits students who activate the categories every quarter and pay in full, and after the first year it still beats a flat 1.5% card once at least an eighth of their spending falls in the 5% categories. Students who’d rather not activate anything should pick the Capital One Quicksilver Student for 1.5% on every purchase or the Discover it Student Chrome for 2% back on up to $1,000 of gas and restaurant spending a quarter.
