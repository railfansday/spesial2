Bitcoin's latest decline toward $62,000 mirrors its sharp correction before the SpaceX IPO earlier this year.

BTC rebounded toward $64,000 as Anthropic's record IPO and improving risk appetite lifted markets.

The SpaceX pattern suggests a possible relief rally, but Bitcoin's previous recovery was followed by another downside move.

Bitcoin’s latest price decline is drawing comparisons with its pre-SpaceX IPO correction, raising speculation that history could repeat as Anthropic makes its public-market debut. BTC has dropped toward $62,000 after a sharp sell-off, closely mirroring the weakness seen before SpaceX’s IPO earlier this year.

With Anthropic’s debut adding a fresh market catalyst, traders are now watching whether Bitcoin can stage another relief rally, or if the current sell-off has further room to run.

Bitcoin has declined nearly 28% from its September peak near $87,400, with selling pressure intensifying ahead of Anthropic’s November 19 debut. The cryptocurrency briefly dropped toward $62,000 before recovering above $63,000.

Bitcoin is repeating the exact dip it made before the biggest IPO in history.If history repeats, we could see another leg up after a few weeks of sideways price action. pic.twitter.com/vQpjUAxhyn

The decline closely resembles Bitcoin’s movement before the SpaceX IPO in June. During that period, BTC dropped from around $83,000 toward $59,000, marking a sharp correction ahead of the company’s public listing.

The similarities have fueled speculation that investors may be reducing exposure to liquid assets ahead of major IPOs, potentially creating temporary selling pressure across crypto markets. However, the broader decline also reflects weakening risk appetite, ETF outflows, leveraged liquidations and macroeconomic uncertainty.

Bitcoin’s recovery following the SpaceX IPO initially strengthened the historical comparison. After SpaceX went public in June, BTC staged a short-term rebound, climbing from around $63,000 toward $66,000 over the following days. However, the recovery did not mark the market bottom. Bitcoin subsequently fell again, reaching a lower level later in June before beginning a broader recovery that eventually carried it toward its September high.

This suggests the IPO-related pattern may involve a temporary relief rally rather than an immediate trend reversal. If the same sequence repeats, Bitcoin could recover in the short term before facing renewed selling pressure.

Anthropic’s IPO has become a major market event, with the company pricing shares at $83, raising approximately $81.3 billion through its public offering. The broader transaction is expected to exceed SpaceX’s record fundraising when additional private placements are included.

The listing has also coincided with a rebound in technology stocks and Nasdaq futures, improving risk appetite across financial markets. Bitcoin recovered toward $64,000 as equity futures strengthened, suggesting that sentiment surrounding the IPO could influence short-term crypto price action.

However, the relationship remains uncertain. Bitcoin’s recent decline has also been driven by broader market deleveraging, institutional flows and macroeconomic concerns, meaning the IPO alone may not determine its direction.

Bitcoin’s immediate recovery remains fragile after the sharp decline toward $62,000. The $62,000 zone is the critical support level. Holding above this area could allow BTC to stabilize and attempt a recovery toward $65,000–$67,500. A sustained move above $67,500 would strengthen the short-term bullish outlook and potentially open the path toward $70,000.

However, a breakdown below $62,000 could expose Bitcoin to further downside toward $59,000, followed by the $57,800 region, which represents a key previous low.

Bitcoin’s pre-IPO decline has created a compelling comparison with the SpaceX listing, and Anthropic’s debut could provide a short-term catalyst for a recovery.

Yet the previous cycle also showed that an initial rally can fade before the market establishes a durable bottom. Traders will therefore watch whether BTC can hold $62,000 and regain $67,500 in the coming sessions. If history repeats, Bitcoin could see a relief rally, but the next move may not be as straightforward as bulls expect.
