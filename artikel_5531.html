For accelerated computing, the BG18-A10710 is a 1U AI server with an integrated 2P DLC solution to support CPUs up to 600W TDP and two single-width 600W GPUs in a compact chassis for high-density compute. The BG28-A10710 is a 2U AI server with four double-width FHFL PCIe 6.0 x16 slots for GPUs up to 600W, while the BG48-A20813 supports eight double-width FHFL GPUs in a 4U chassis for demanding AI workloads and next-generation model training.

To address versatile enterprise needs, the BS28-A10710 features 16 E3.S drive bays for high-speed storage and AI data pipelines. The BX28-A10710 supports up to 12 U.2 NVMe SSDs, while the BX28-A10711 accommodates 24 2.5" SATA drives, providing flexible storage configurations for HPC and enterprise workloads. These platforms extend AEWIN's portfolio to support broader capabilities across compute, storage, and data-intensive applications.

Beyond individual server platforms, AEWIN leverages its subsidiary Arivor's 2P DLC solutions to build energy-efficient, rack-scale AI infrastructure. The system utilizes a phase-change coolant for rapid thermal dissipation, paired with an intelligent rack-scale CDU for dynamic system management. This cutting-edge cooling solution sustains up to 200kW per rack for higher-density AI deployments with minimized PUE and optimized sustainability.
