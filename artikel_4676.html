Engineered nanosyringes open a new route into plant cells

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Understanding and improving plants often requires changing what happens inside their cells. Proteins control how cells grow, respond to stress, and carry out countless other tasks. Delivering these molecular tools directly into plant cells could give researchers new ways to study those processes and engineer useful traits. But getting active proteins past the cell wall and membrane that protect each plant cell remains a major obstacle.
