Week 9 Fantasy Football Quarterback Rankings

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

We're into Week 9 of the 2026 fantasy football season and that's officially the mid-way point of the campaign. We have fewer byes in Week 9 with the Steelers and Titans the only teams not playing.

That gives us a full QB pool, though we won't have Ravens signal-caller Lamar Jackson, who left Sunday against the Bills with a thumb injury and did not return. He's headed for injured reserve, while Tyler Huntley could also be out long-term for the Ravens. So Baltimore will turn to third-string QB Joe Fagnano on TNF in Week 9 against the Jaguars.
