Here is where the Cowboys fit in the NFC East division race after Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Dallas Cowboys are a broken football team. They had a win firmly in their hands and just handed back to the Philadelphia Eagles. Brian Schottenheimer fell apart in the fourth quarter with several awful decisions. Once again they played for a field goal late in the game instead of being aggressive and playing for the touchdown to win the game. Then they made a baffling call on fourth down – instead of kicking a field goal, or running the ball for one yard, they called a pass. Disaster when Dak Prescott was sacked leading to a long field goal from the Eagles to win 39-38.

The Cowboys are now done for the year and are just going to play out the string. It was an embarrassing loss, one they need to accept as an impetus for change. They can't continue running this back in the same form any longer.
