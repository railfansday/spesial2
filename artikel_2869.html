The NFL is back and so are the Yahoo Fantasy x Arena Club Slab Packs!

Loaded with top performers and fantasy stars, the Yahoo Fantasy x Arena Club Slab packs will be available each week during the regular season for collectors and fans to digitally rip.

For those new to Arena Club, it is a new way of collecting. As the premier tech-powered marketplace and grading platform, Arena Club brings the physical experience of card collecting into the digital age. Users can purchase and open virtual packs of real, pre-graded cards ("slabs"), buy and sell on the marketplace and manage their card showrooms all in one place. Each Arena Club Slab Pack displays the full lineup of potential cards and the likelihood of ripping each.

Yahoo Fantasy Slab Packs contain graded NFL trading cards curated by Arena Club. Fans can purchase a slab pack, each containing a graded card of an NFL player, via the Arena Club platform. This week, we're upping the ante with grails of some of the most collectible players — and top fantasy performers — being available as chases in both tiers of packs. For the Silver ($70) tier, each grail is worth roughly 35-60x of the pack price. In the Gold ($200) tier, each grail has an estimated value of 25-40x the pack price.

The Yahoo Fantasy Slab Packs will go on sale on at kickoff of the Colts-Texans game (8:15 p.m. ET, Thursday) and be available until the conclusion of the Monday night game (or until they sell out) every week through the end of the season.

You can head to ArenaClub.com or the Arena Club app and use the promo code YAHOO20 at checkout for 20% off your first slab pack or card purchase.

Joe Burrow, Bengals – Burrow threw for 409 yards and four touchdowns in a 38-18 thrashing of the Steelers on Sunday night. That statline is remarkable on its own, but what makes it even more stunning is those aren't the biggest single-game numbers from Cincinnati's star QB this month! Burrow is firmly in the conversation for NFL MVP and has the Bengals tied for the longest current win streak in the NFL (5 games).

Jahmyr Gibbs, Lions – We'd say Burrow is in the driver's seat to win NFL MVP, but Jahmyr Gibbs is making a strong case to be the first non-QB to win the award since Adrian Peterson in 2012. Gibbs' trip to Germany was jaw-dropping, as he compiled 252 scrimmage yards (153 rushing, 99 receiving) and found the end zone twice. Gibbs now leads the NFL in touches (240), rushing yards (1,000), scrimmage yards (1,384) and total touchdowns (13).

Patrick Mahomes, Chiefs – Now here's a guy who looked like his old self in Week 10. All kidding aside, Mahomes was in rare form against the Falcons, throwing for five touchdowns for the first time since Week 1 of 2022. It was the seventh time in his career Mahomes has thrown for five or more TDs (for a fun trivia tidbit, three of those times have come agains bird-themed teams). After hitting a bit of a lull in the middle of October, Mahomes has 10 touchdowns and zero interceptions over the past three games.
