The Montreal Canadiens are establishing themselves as a playoff contender this season. They are tied on points for first in the Atlantic Division at this time. And they have looked the part of a true force out of the Eastern Conference.

However, things aren't always perfect. The Canadiens lost on Monday night against the New York Rangers. Jacob Fowler made 23 saves, but it wasn't enough. Captain Nick Suzuki did not mince his words following the contest.

"We let them hang around. I don't think we played very well," Suzuki said, via NHL.com.

The Habs opened the scoring thanks to Phillip Danault in the second period. However, Montreal allowed two goals in the middle frame to trail entering the third period. The Rangers added another in the final stanza to come away with a 3-1 win.

"The execution seemed off right from the start, honestly," defenseman Lane Hutson said in alignment with Suzuki's thoughts.

A major pain point for the Canadiens was the power play. Montreal had the man advantage four times in this contest. They managed just four shots on goal. Furthermore, Will Cuylle's eventual game-winning goal came during a Montreal power play.

Though the Canadiens have played well overall this year, they are in a slump. The Habs have now lost three of their last four games. With the Ottawa Senators and Tampa Bay Lightning neck-and-neck with them atop the division, they will need to find a way out of this rut soon.

Montreal will look to put this one behind them and move forward. The Habs have a chance to bounce back in their next contest against the New Jersey Devils on Wednesday.
