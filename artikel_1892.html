'Absolute rubbish' - Ex-Tottenham employee 'can't understand' one thing about Mauricio Pochettino to Man United

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

It is baffling that Manchester United have allowed Mauricio Pochettino to see out two more games with the United States before taking over.

That is the opinion of experienced former Tottenham, Everton and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The Red Devils confirmed on Friday that the 54-year-old has been appointed to replace Michael Carrick, who had guided his team to one of the worst starts in the club's history.

Pochettino will arrive with Man United in the Premier League's relegation zone, with his first game set to be a trip to take on Liverpool at Anfield.

But despite such a difficult start, the Argentine will have just a few days to prepare when he arrives in Manchester later this week.

The United States beat Haiti 6-0 on Saturday, and will play them again on Wednesday as part of the CONCACAF Nations League.

Mauricio Pochettino has agreed to become the Manchester United men’s head coach.

It had been clear for some time that Carrick would be sacked and once it emerged that Pochettino was the first choice, an agreement was quickly sewn up.

The former Tottenham and Chelsea manager will hope to be the first in a long line of managers to ultimately succeed in turning the ship at Old Trafford.

"He ends up managing the USA and now he's coming back after being away from the Premier League for two years - he had the Chelsea debacle," King told Football Insider.

"What a great season they had, finished sixth, and now he's sitting wiping the tears away in Cincinnati. I cannot understand why he has been kept on and he's not left already.

"As soon as that decision was made, that he's going to be the manager, he should have been in Manchester. He'll step off the plane this week. He should have stepped off the plane last week when Michael Carrick got the news he was sacked.

"And if they lose to Liverpool, [people will say] 'well, he hasn't really had time. He hasn't had time enough to work with the team because of the international break, and he had to honour the end of his contract with the USA.'

"Absolute rubbish. Haiti. 6-0, the first game. The whole thing is a circus."

Pochettino was emotional in his first press conference after news emerged of his move, and explained why he is staying on for two final games.

"I am a professional person and the emotional link between us we have built in this two years is to show the commitment and respect and care and love," he said.

"Even if we take a decision which is not the best for one part, we've always been open. We have the respect of the players and staff, they understand the situation.

"Of course, if Manchester United and the federation agreed different terms, I would have respected between both. But this happened and of course, all the games are important."
