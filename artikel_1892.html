'There's only one person who won't like it' - De Zerbi's Tottenham team selection plan assessed

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Roberto De Zerbi is keen to encourage competition throughout his Tottenham Hotspur squad.

That is according to former senior Tottenham scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

Club captain Micky van de Ven was the latest to drop to the bench, with the Italian opting to give Marcos Senesi just his fourth appearance of the season in Saturday's 2-0 win over Ipswich Town.

The Argentine performed well and will hope to keep his place against Sunderland on Sunday, with Jan Paul van Hecke potentially to have his spot under threat instead.

A busy summer saw Tottenham pack the squad with quality, with very few positions in De Zerbi's starting XI considered guaranteed.

It is hoped that competition will raise the levels at Tottenham even more, as they attempt to build off the back of four wins from five in the Premier League.

Senesi arrived in the summer transfer window for free, after he had left Bournemouth at the end of his contract.

The 29-year-old is a superb option for De Zerbi's squad, and proved as much when given the opportunity on Saturday.

"I think it's a rotation thing. Again, Senesi did well, but Van de Ven is the club captain, isn't he," King told Football Insider.

"I think it's good to have a player who's from the same neck of the woods as [Cristian] Romero. Another Argentinian.

"It's nice that against these kind of sides, and I would imagine he will do, that he will be using Senesi and probably swapping Van de Ven or Van Hecke, as he's done with [Pedro] Porro and [Destiny] Udogie.

"There's nothing better than competition for the manager, for the club. The only person who won't really like it will be the player in question.

"I think it was a chance after the international break to maybe give the other player a go, and I just think it's a rotational thing with these clubs at the moment."

Senesi can play on either side of the defence and will hope to have impressed De Zerbi on Saturday.

The Argentina international had more touches than anyone, and made a remarkable 22 line-breaking passes.

He was fouled twice and record a number of defensive contributions, including two tackles, eight clearances, three recoveries and three blocks - more than anybody else.

There is ittle more Senesi could have done to prove himself, and it is now up to De Zerbi to reward him.
