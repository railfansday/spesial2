Chelsea's dressing room 'know the writing is on the wall' for Xabi Alonso - expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Chelsea are playing like a team who know that Xabi Alonso will soon be dismissed.

That is according to ex-manager and experienced former Tottenham, Aston Villa and Everton scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The Blues were destroyed 4-1 by Leeds United at Stamford Bridge on Saturday, marking a first win for the away team in that fixture since 1999.

Alonso's team have won just one of their last five games across all competitions, and now find themselves 12 points off the top of the Premier League table.

The 44-year-old signed a four-year contract when he was appointed in the summer transfer window, but Chelsea have shown no hesitancy in making changes in the dugout before.

And with much of Stamford Bridge having voiced their disapproval on Saturday, the former Liverpool midfielder will now be under significant pressure to turn the situation around.

Chelsea seemed willing to try a new approach with the appointment of Alonso, signing more established, experienced players.

The likes of Jordan Henderson, Pep Chavarria and Danny Welbeck looked to be incomings directly approved by the Spaniard.

But it remains to be seen whether Chelsea are willing to take a different approach with how much patience they have for their manager, too.

Speaking to Football Insider, King said: "Alonso had a four-year deal at Real Madrid. How long did that last?

"I'm afraid Chelsea have shown in the past what they'll do. Look at the list of managers they've had. They will not hang about.

"And I don't think they show any patience at all. It's not fast food they're looking for, they're looking for fast results.

"One minute it looks as though he might be cracking it. The next minute [he doesn't]. It's what's on the pitch. It's what you put on the pitch.

"And for me, these players don't look as though they'll run through brick walls for him. It looks as though they know the writing is on the wall."

The home fans would have expected Chelsea to push forward when they went 1-0 up at Stamford Bridge on Saturday.

But their lead lasted for just three minutes and they were ultimately lucky to only concede four. Alonso spoke of a lack of belief after the game, in hugely concerning quotes.

"After conceding we had to substitute [Moises] Caicedo and never recovered. Leeds believed more than us.

"We didn't show a good enough reaction. We need to find solutions together."
