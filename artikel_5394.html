Tesla tuner Unplugged Performance is offering to take over the Cybertruck line

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The proposal would give Tesla a license fee and FSD revenue on each truck as it winds down production around mid-2027

David Paul Morris/Bloomberg via Getty Images

Unplugged Performance, an aftermarket tuner for Tesla $TSLA vehicles, offered on Tuesday to take over Cybertruck production from Tesla, according to Electrek. The company said a consortium of investors would fund the effort.

Unplugged Performance put its proposal to Tesla in a public post on X $TWTR, framing it as a standing invitation, according to Electrek. It said it would run Cybertruck production "at no cost to Tesla's floor space, headcount, or focus." Under the proposal, Tesla would receive a licensing fee on each vehicle built, as well as revenue from the FSD and Robotaxi networks.

The tuner said it backs making Tesla's Cybercab the top priority and would let that vehicle have priority access to battery cells. It said its lineup would consist of the Cybertruck, a CyberSUV with three rows of seats and a driverless CyberCargo hauler.

Unplugged Performance founder Ben Schaffer is comfortable with Tesla's pivot to self-driving technology but does not want the Cybertruck program to end, according to Electrek.

Investor Ross Gerber, co-founder of Gerber Kawasaki Wealth Management, quoted the post on X. "Free the tesla cybertruck," Gerber said, according to Benzinga.

Neither Elon Musk nor Tesla had responded to the offer when Electrek published its story on Tuesday.

The offer follows Musk's statement last week that Tesla would begin phasing out the Cybertruck at its Giga Texas plant around mid-2027, according to Electrek. Musk said Tesla needs to "make room for increased Cybercab production," according to Electrek.

Electrek reported that Cybertruck sales are at about 12% of Tesla's original target of 250,000 annual vehicles. Benzinga also reported that sales have fallen well below that target.

Unplugged Performance has a stake in the truck. It sells a range of Cybertruck products, among them lift and armor kits, and it has won multiple contracts to rebuild Cybertrucks as police cars for law enforcement, according to Electrek.
