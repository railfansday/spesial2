Former Netflix Exec Grant Hamilton Joins Weinstein Senior LLP as an Associate (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Former Netflix exec Grant Hamilton has joined law firm Weinstein Senior LLP as an associate. It’s a homecoming for Hamilton, who had previously worked with Weinstein Senior founding partners Todd Weinstein and Tara Senior at Del Shaw Moonves.

Hamilton will help expand Weinstein Senior’s unscripted content practice, including creators and production companies across development, production and legal affairs. “Grant’s return to Weinstein Senior is a testament to the relationships we’ve built,” said Weinstein Serior founding partner Todd Weinstein. “We hired and trained him at Del Shaw, and when he moved to Netflix, Tara and I remained closely connected, mentoring him throughout his journey. We’re thrilled to reunite with him during this exciting time at the firm as we scale our unscripted practice.”

At Netflix, Hamilton worked on physical production team for scripted film, managing production across departments including directors of photography, crew, locations and vendor relationships through to service launch.

Before Netflix, Hamilton was an associate at Del Shaw Moonves, working alongside Weinstein Senior founders Todd Weinstein and Tara Senior.

“I’m thrilled to join Weinstein Senior and work again with Todd, Tara, and Mitch, people I deeply respect and have admired,” Hamilton said in a statement. “It really feels like coming home. I’m excited to bring my production experience to the firm and help support the incredible work they’re doing with creators and production companies.”

Weinstein Senior was founded by Todd Weinstein and Tara Senior in 2022. The company’s clients include content creators, production companies, writers, directors, on-camera talent and brands.
