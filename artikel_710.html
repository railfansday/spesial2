Emma Chamberlain Returning to YouTube With Original Series ‘What Are You Waiting For?’ (Exclusive)

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The veteran creator is ditching the vlog format for a docuseries concept.

One of YouTube‘s original superstars is planning a major comeback to the platform.

Emma Chamberlain, whose vlogs on the platform propelled her to stardom (she has more than 12 million followers there), is launching a new show on YouTube next year, The Hollywood Reporter has learned.

Chamberlain will launch the docuseries What Are You Waiting For? in which she explores some of New York’s most popular lines, and the people waiting in them.

“What Are You Waiting For? on the surface is about people who voluntarily wait in lines for stuff set in New York City, a phenomenon that I personally don’t understand but seek to understand, and along the way I discover much more about humanity than I ever could have expected,” Chamberlain says. “The experience of making this was unlike anything I have ever done and has profoundly changed me as a person, talking to strangers all day, working with a crew of unbelievably talented people, and making something bigger than I have ever made before, I feel the next chapter of my life coming to fruition, and I am deeply inspired and grateful.”

The show is being produced in partnership with Amplify Pictures, underscoring how content on YouTube is professionalizing and becoming more ambitious. In fact, Chamberlain is debuting the new concept at a YouTube Creator Premieres event in Los Angeles Tuesday evening, with Wesley Wang and Aryna Sabalenka also making appearances.

It’s also a coup for the platform, which has been trying to entice top creators to develop shows and programming primarily for itself at a moment when there are a number of competitors pursuing deals with top talent.

“After deciding to take a hiatus from my podcast, I spent the next few months trying to figure out what I wanted to create next,” Chamberlain says. “The answer wasn’t obvious at first, but I knew for sure that I wanted to make something for YouTube. From entertaining me as a child to allowing me to build a career as an adult, it was a no-brainer that whatever I make next had to go on YouTube.”

“Working with a TV studio to create YouTube content is a full 180 from what I have done in the past, producing everything myself with very little help, but I knew this was crucial,” she added. “I met with Joe Lewis and Amplify Pictures and explained a little idea I had for a docuseries. To my surprise, they loved it and wanted to make it with me.”
