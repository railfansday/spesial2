‘Dancing With the Stars’ Producers Allegedly Want to Bring Back This Controversial Judge

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Dancing With the Stars producers are already making plans for Season 36, including bringing controversial Strictly Come Dancing judge Shirley Ballas back for a guest-judging stint. Shirley (who’s pro Mark Ballas’ mom) is known for being a very tough judge—delivering 3s and 4s while the rest of the panel is out here giving 9s and 10s. So she definitely keeps things...interesting!

“Shirley came in at Halloween, and really shook up the show with fresh energy and tough marking,” a source tells The Sun. “Shirley was also very well liked on The Next Pro, which prompted excitement from the top, too. Shirley is going to appear a few more times next season if everything goes to plan. The Next Pro is also ready for Season 2. So it is all there to play for Shirley.”

There’s speculation that Shirley could become a full-time judge on Dancing With the Stars, but again—she’s currently a judge on Strictly Come Dancing, which airs its season in the UK at the same time as DWTS in the U.S.. Plus, Shirley said she has no plans to leave England, telling Chris Evans on the Breakfast Show, “I am not moving to the United States, although my son and my grandson are there… He has made it through to the final, I was up all night so if I look a little dark under the eyes.”

By the way, Shirley also implied that she prefers Strictly’s formatting, saying, “I feel like our show is very organized and actually for a Saturday night it's quite calm. For me everybody seems to know what they're doing and over there it was a bit manic for me, maybe because I didn't know the system.”
