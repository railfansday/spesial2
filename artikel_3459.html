PlayStation Announces New Black Friday Deals On Hardware

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The PlayStation VR2 is discounted, but the PlayStation 5 is not

Black Friday is fast approaching, so companies like Sony are already teasing the deals people can look forward to later this month. While PlayStation previously highlighted some of the games that will be discounted, it has now shared its official Black Friday hardware deals for the DualSense controller, PlayStation VR2, and more.

On the PlayStation Blog, Sony Interactive Entertainment highlighted the upcoming hardware deals. The PlayStation 5 console itself isn't getting an official discount from Sony, which is disappointing given its recent price hikes. Still, the following peripherals, listed in alphabetical order, will see deep discounts come Black Friday:

If you plan on buying directly from PlayStation, this Black Friday seems like a good time to stock up on additional controllers if you currently only have one DualSense. If you're still intrigued by the PlayStation VR2 despite its lack of first-party support, that deal isn't too shabby either.

First-party games like Marvel's Wolverine, Saros, Ghost of Yotei Complete Edition, and Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls will also get discounted at the same time as those peripherals, which will be at 12 a.m. in your local time on Nov. 16. The deals are then expected to be live through Nov. 30 or as long as the items are in stock. It's also worth keeping an eye on other retailers like Walmart, as they may have some additional Black Friday deals on top of the ones Sony is setting for PlayStation.
