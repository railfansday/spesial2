5 Horror Movies With Bad Rotten Tomatoes Scores That Made Money In 2026, Including Other Mommy

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The new golden age of horror continued in 2026, with Other Mommy being yet another worthy addition to the genre's stellar lineup of films for this year. Films like Obsession and Backrooms became both critical and commercial successes that demonstrated boundary-pushing, experimentally engineered stories could upend the mainstream.

Filmmakers Curry Barker and Kane Parsons, applauded by none other than Steven Spielberg, destroyed every other horror film released this year with heated online conversations and undeniably monstrous box office receipts. But numerous others did prove rotten critical reviews way wrong with unbelievable theatrical success that made them impossible to ignore, including Other Mommy. Audiences were hungry for horror, and they shelled out the big bucks to see their chosen favorite score at the cinema.

2026 is littered with additional critical/commercial darlings, including Nia DaCosta's 28 Years Later: The Bone Temple, Zach Cregger's Resident Evil, Sam Raimi's Send Help, and James Watkin's DCU-horror hybrid and box-office buster Clayface. The list just scrapes the surface of horror's remarkable success and indicates that fans will always show up when it comes to terrifying, macabre tales about the undead. For every critical hit that also smashes the box office, there's always a critical failure that cleans up with its impressive commercial viability and buzzy online discourse. Countless factors come into play, and sometimes, the stars magically align on their own.

Box Office Mojo reports that Ghostface's return witnessed a $63.6 million opening weekend, ultimately grossing nearly $122 million domestically. Scream 7 became the highest grossing in the franchise's 30-year history. Everyone's favorite cheeky, meta-slasher shows no signs of slowing down the money train. Its 29% rotten score on Rotten Tomatoes bolsters the critical consensus of a "dull script" with "a disappointing creative regression" compared to previous entries. Kevin Williamson, who penned the first two Scream films, returned to wield his pen, but he also took his first spin in the director's chair for the franchise.

Scream 7 also saw the return of its Final Girl, Sidney Prescott (Neve Campbell). After a pay dispute surrounding Radio Silence's Scream VI, Campbell sat the film out, and the story focused more intensely on Melissa Barrera's Sam Carpenter. Spyglass fired Barrera in 2023 and then re-enlisted Campbell for Sidney's big comeback. Campbell and Williamson joining forces again felt like getting the band back together. Franchise originator Wes Craven passed away in 2015, and Williamson's long-waited return to the Scream playground marked a high for fans.

Lee Cronin's The Mummy reinvented the 1932 Universal Monster classic, The Mummy, a tale about the fear of death and resurrection. Through a modern lens, the story finds itself deconstructed for something socially potent and frightening. With a 45% Rotten Tomatoes score, you'd never expect the remake to draw such a massive audience. Following a $13.5 million domestic opening, the film grossed $29 million domestically and $90.5 million globally, according to Box Office Mojo. That's not too shabby for a nearly-100-year-old IP that bore witness to countless interpretations over the decades, including 1999's remake starring Brendan Fraser and Rachel Weisz.

Lee Cronin marked his directorial debut with 2019's The Hole in the Ground, but it wasn't until Evil Dead Rise that he was able to truly showcase his unique modern style. Numerous viewers have stated the story's eerie resemblance to an Evil Dead movie: a young girl is possessed by a demonic entity and performs deadly supernatural acts.

As a result, long-standing Evil Dead fans came out in full force to support Cronin's endeavor. Evil Dead Rise opened with $24.5 million domestically and grossed $67 million ($147 million globally), according to Box Office Mojo. The built-in audience proved undeniable.

The barely-fresh Iron Lung (now 63% after riding in the rotten on Rotten Tomatoes) took a big, bold swing with its one-location sci-fi/horror story. The critical consensus notes that the ambitious film is "more interesting for its production than its story," but the 86% audience popcorn-meter rating falls more in line with the box office haul. Box Office Mojo reports that Markiplier's feature film debut revved with a $17.8 million opening and went on to gross $40.8 million domestically and $50 million globally. Numbers don't lie when it comes to where audiences choose to put their hard-earned dollars.

One-location horror is a tough sell. However, Markiplier (real name: Mark Fischbach) has a built-in audience from his successful YouTube channel. Fischbach follows Parsons and Barker using online fame as a launching pad to a theatrical tent release with an equally viral appeal. Iron Lung carries the feeling of being low-budget at high-budget stakes. Fischbach also takes the reins as the protagonist trapped inside an underwater vessel in a post-apocalyptic hellscape. The draw lies solely with Markiplier and his potential to execute a gripping horror narrative worthy of major change. And audiences seem to agree that it does.

After a $27.8 million opening, Other Mommy with Jessica Chastain grossed $59 million domestically and $116 million globally, Box Office Mojo reports. The trailer suggests one kind of film, and the story takes a hard left turn fairly early on, owing to scattered expectations from critics. A paltry 19% Rotten Tomatoes score doesn't stand a chance against the box office numbers. Not only does Jessica Chastain continue to be a big draw, but the word of mouth on such a polarizing film drove it to become a surprise hit. Against all odds, Other Mommy never slowed down.

The October 9, 2026, release date was a smart move. During Halloween season, casual horror fans seek out scary movies to celebrate the occasion when they usually don't watch horror any other time of the year. Other Mommy tapped into unshakable fear at just the right moment while keeping much of its actual story close to the vest. The creative decision not to reveal much of the real story in promotional material can never be understated. The studio tricked audiences and allowed their experiences to fuel it to a box office success.

Scary Movie pulled a Scream for its reboot. Marlon and Shawn Wayans return to the franchise, as well as Anna Faris and Regina Hall, for a film that serves as a sequel to the first two films. Nostalgia for the series' roots led to a stunning $108 million domestic and $231.8 million global gross, according to Box Office Mojo. You can never underestimate the power of getting the band back together, particularly when it comes to beloved horror/comedy IP. Scary Movie fans are loyal, almost to a fault, and once again prove that there's still juice in the franchise.

With a 23% Rotten Tomatoes score, critics were hard to please. Even the original film only has 52% on Rotten Tomatoes. Scary Movie was already fighting an uphill battle, and it comes as no shock it wasn't well received. Coincidentally, Scary Movie, which began as a satire of Scream, dropped the same year as Scream 7. Both horror films bank hard on their fanbases. In 2026, fans haven't gone anywhere and their obsession has only grown in recent years. Whether it's Neve Campbell or Anna Faris and Regina Hall, Scream and Scary Movie remain forces at the box office.
