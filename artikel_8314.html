Count me as skeptical of that. I personally know 5 people who received hospital care for cancer or acute, life-threatening conditions with Kaiser insurance or Kaiser Medicare Advantage. None had connections to Kaiser physicians to advocate on their behalf. All received excellent care.
This has been my experience as well. The common complaint with an HMO is that you need a referral to see a specialist. In my experience, this has never been an issue and often when I ask a question with an online message to a doctor, the response includes a referral or an order for a test I didn't even ask for.
Referrals from a PCP who is a community partner (not a Kaiser physician, but in-network) seem like they may draw more scrutiny, and seen like they may take a little longer than a referral from a Kaiser doc. I don’t know if that is true generally.
