Jazz Pharmaceuticals plc (JAZZ -0.71%) reported record quarterly revenue driven by growth in the oncology portfolio and sustained demand for the sleep medicine franchise. Management raised full-year revenue guidance to reflect commercial execution across multiple products in the U.S. and Europe. The acquisition of Actio Biosciences expanded the rare epilepsy pipeline with a precision therapy candidate, ABS-1230. The company utilized its cash flow to exhaust a share repurchase program and authorized a new repurchase plan to continue its capital allocation strategy. President and Chief Executive Officer Renée Galá stated that the results demonstrate momentum in advancing practice-changing medicines while maintaining a disciplined approach to capital allocation.
