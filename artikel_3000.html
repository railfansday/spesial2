JBS N.V. (JBS -5.49%) reported third-quarter performance affected by challenging market conditions across multiple business units, including global pork oversupply and margin pressure in the poultry segment. Management stated that the company achieved a return to positive profitability in the North American beef business, attributing the turnaround to structural changes that integrated fed, regional, and case-ready operations. The company emphasized its multiprotein diversification strategy as a core mechanism for navigating protein-specific industrial cycles and stabilizing long-term earnings. Management reported the acquisition of personal care brand Flora as a strategic move to verticalize byproducts and access new sales channels such as drugstores in Brazil. The company indicated that cash generation and deleveraging are primary objectives, with planned reductions in capital expenditures and anticipated asset sale proceeds expected to improve the net leverage ratio toward a target of below 3x by the end of 2027.
