Pokemon World Champion selling 2022 championship trophy for $400,000

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

A Pokemon world champion has advertised the trophy they won at the 2022 World Championships for sale, seeking $400,000 for the gong.

Junglebook, who won the inaugural Pokemon Unite World Championships as part of North American team BLVKHVND, has put the trophy up for sale four years after claiming the title in London.

The six-figure asking price isn’t set in stone, and Junglebook says they’re open to fielding non-cash trades, including for “high-end Pokemon slabs”.

In a post on X, Junglebook shared photos of the trophy, which features Pikachu holding a smaller trophy while standing on top of a Poke Ball.

“For sale is the official World Champion Trophy from the 2022 Pokémon World Championships in London,” he wrote.

“I earned this trophy myself by taking 1st Place in Pokemon Unite the very first year the game was included in the World Championships.”

Junglebook described the trophy as being in pristine condition, claiming it had only been held by him during the 2022 awards ceremony.

Before listing the trophy, a post on the PokeInvesting subreddit featuring the same images asked community members for advice on how much the award would be worth and how to sell it. It’s not known if Junglebook created the Reddit thread.

Pokemon World Championship trophies have been sold before, albeit for far less than Junglebook’s asking price.

In May 2025, a trophy awarded at the 2024 Pokemon World Championships in Honolulu sold through auction house Goldin for $12,810, including fees, after attracting 22 bids.

Junglebook’s $400,000 asking price is more than 30 times that amount, although there is no guarantee his trophy will sell for the listed figure.

The listing comes after the announcement that Pokemon Unite will end service on March 23, 2027.
