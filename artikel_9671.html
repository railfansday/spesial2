VITI Engineering Guide: The "Machine Reality" Matrix (Or How to Replace Your ITIL Priests with 5 Lines of Code)

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Another dispatch from the VITI Magazine Editorial Board and VITI Labs.

If you have survived reading our previous VITI Memos, you already know the truth: Enterprise IT is drowning in process theater. CAB meetings, 300-page TOGAF blueprints, and Word-based security policies are simply illusions of control.

In the Business Impact Governance Framework (BIGF), we replace the "ITIL Priesthood" with Machine Reality. We move from Policy-as-Document to Policy-as-Code.

To demonstrate how little code it takes to replace a corporate bureaucracy, VITI Labs has compiled the definitive Machine Reality Matrix. We have taken the most common ISO 27001, DORA, and NIS2 requirements and translated them from manual liturgy into deterministic runtime logic.

When it is this blatantly obvious how easily these controls can be automated and strictly enforced, why does the ITIL Priesthood still fiercely defend their Tuesday CAB meetings?

Is it simply a lust for bureaucratic power, or do they genuinely lack the technical competence to trust the code?

VITI Labs suspects it is the former. When the machine takes over the governance, the priests lose their pulpit.
