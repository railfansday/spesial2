Pregnant Savannah Chrisley & Husband Jason Galanis Reveal Sex of Baby

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Shortly after announcing their pregnancy and marriage, Savannah Chrisley and husband Jason Galanis shared in a sweet sex reveal video that they're expecting a baby boy.

Savannah Chrisley is unlocking a new chapter.

The Chrisley Knows Best alum is going to be a boy mom, as she and husband Jason Galanis shared they are expecting a son.

"My sweet miracle boy… you are everything we prayed for and more," Chrisley wrote on Instagram Nov. 2 alongside a video of her slicing into a cake with blue icing inside. "The past few days have reminded me just how cruel this world can be. But no amount of hate could ever overshadow the joy of loving you. You are a MIRACLE."

"My precious baby boy, your daddy and I will spend the rest of our lives protecting you, praying over you, and loving you beyond measure," she vowed in her caption. "We may not be able to shield you from every bit of darkness in this world, but we promise to raise you to be a LIGHT in it."

The 29-year-old added, "You are already our greatest blessing, our answered prayer, and the sweetest chapter of our lives."

Chrisley first revealed she was pregnant in an Oct. 27 Instagram post, praising Galanis, who she hadn’t named at the time, for being “my protector. My fiercest fighter. My most loyal person. My lover. My safe place.”

“For the first time in a long time, I feel safe,” she said. “I feel fulfilled. I can finally exhale. I found my forever in you. And just when I thought my heart couldn’t hold any more love, God gave us our sweetest blessing yet… we’re having a BABY!”

And even before the pair welcomed their little one, the Unlocked podcast host was able to get a preview of the kind of dad Galanis would be.

“J, watching the way you already love and protect our baby before he/she has even entered this world has made me fall in love with you in an entirely new way,” she explained. “Not a single doctor’s appointment missed. Constantly caring for me, talking about our baby, and reminding me how beautiful my growing bump is.”

The reality star added, “I thank God every day that this baby gets to call you Daddy.”

Though Chrisley didn’t confirm her baby daddy’s identity, multiple outlets reported that “J” was Galanis shortly after her pregnancy announcement.

Days later, Chrisley confirmed she and Galanis, 56, were married, privately tying the knot in the Cayman Islands earlier this year.

"We ended up getting married in the Cayman Islands in July," Savannah said on the premiere episode of Golden Tate's The Golden Ticket YouTube series released Nov. 1. "We did everything there, but we haven't filed it here—or else then it would be public record."

And while they still plan on having a larger wedding celebration in May, she shared that the couple’s priority was welcoming their family’s new addition, which Chrisley called “such a gift.”

"Like, I can say, 'Oh, being pregnant sucks,' or, 'It's not fun,'" she said, "but when you feel your baby kick and you literally see your pants move because you kicked so hard, you're like, 'That's what it's all for.'"

Plus, she shared that stepping into motherhood presents the perfect opportunity to “raise a kid up right, teach them the right ways, make a difference, be different from other people.”

For Chrisley—whose parents Todd Chrisley and Julie Chrisley were released from prison last year—she can’t imagine anything better.

"It's the biggest blessing, alright?” she added. “It's nothing. I can be large and in charge for a few months. I'll be fine. I might not be happy about it, but I'm good."
