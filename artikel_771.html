Kelly Clarkson Calls ‘The Voice’ Contestant Kyle Davis ‘a Born Storyteller’ After Keane Cover

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

"I actually feel sorry for everyone else in this competition," Clarkson told him.

Kelly Clarkson delivered one of the biggest compliments of the season to her own team member during Monday night’s (Nov. 16) live episode of The Voice, telling 61-year-old Kyle Davis that his competition was in trouble. Explore Kelly Clarkson See latest videos, charts and news

Davis took on Keane’s 2004 single “Somewhere Only We Know” for the second half of season 30’s Y2K-themed Top 13 showcase, and Clarkson couldn’t contain herself when it was time for feedback. “I actually feel sorry for everyone else in this competition,” she told him. “Your tone is so incredible! Like, you’re a born storyteller. I know you’ve been doing it for a minute, but there’s something so easy and effortless. I’m so proud I’m your coach. Well done.”

The moment was a full-circle one for Davis, who wasn’t among Clarkson’s picks for the live shows. Each of the four coaches — Clarkson, Riley Green, Queen Latifah and Adam Levine — chose three of their artists to advance, with viewers handed one pick to round out the Top 13. America chose Davis, the season’s oldest contestant and a decades-long music industry veteran.

“I got a call that said I’d won America’s vote,” Davis said in a pre-performance package. “Thank you for voting for me. I couldn’t even process it. Totally shocked. I mean, it’s really an honor.” He also gifted Clarkson a mood ring during rehearsals.

With Team Queen and Team Adam having performed the previous week, Monday’s episode belonged to Team Kelly and Team Riley. The setlist pulled from Christina Aguilera’s “Reflection” and The Chicks’ “Cowboy Take Me Away,” among others, and the packed broadcast left the coaches little time for commentary.

Green did make room to praise Joe Henley, who covered Lee Ann Womack’s “I Hope You Dance.” “Joe, you crushed it, man,” the country star said. “You’re one of the most naturally talented people on this show, and how much better you continue to get and how much you continue to step up when you have an opportunity like you did tonight, I’m so impressed with it. I’m proud of you.”

By the end of the night, two artists were sent home: Team Kelly’s Anna McElroy, who sang Faith Hill’s “Breathe,” and Team Riley’s Cade Mower, who performed the Goo Goo Dolls’ “Iris.” They join Team Queen’s Lillie Kolich, who was eliminated last week after reworking Britney Spears’ “(You Drive Me) Crazy.”

Season 30’s Top 10 is made up of Brianna Cameron, Kyle Davis and Koumba Diallo (Team Kelly); Ferryn and Brinkley and Joe Henley (Team Riley); Mia Knight and BigCountry (Team Queen); and Connor Closs, Lexi Stephens and Dakota Cohen (Team Adam).

The Voice airs Mondays at 9 p.m. ET on NBC and streams on Peacock.
