Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (ARWR -4.48%) management indicated that the company is transitioning toward a commercial-stage organization as it builds a global cardiometabolic franchise. Management stated that the company is preparing for a potential U.S. launch of plozasiran in severe hypertriglyceridemia (sHTG) during the second quarter of 2027, following a recent supplemental application submission. Strategic priorities include advancing a diverse pipeline of RNAi therapeutics targeting the liver, lungs, muscle, and central nervous system. Management indicated that commercial progress in the initial rare disease market is critical for establishing the infrastructure needed to address larger patient populations.
