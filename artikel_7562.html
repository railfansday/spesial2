North Carolina man pleads guilty to defrauding the government out of hundreds of thousands of dollars

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

U.S. Attorney Michael DiGiacomo announced today that Kareem Highsmith, 51, of Charlotte, NC, pleaded guilty before U.S. Magistrate Judge Michael J. Roemer to making false, fictitious, or fraudulent claims against a government agency, which carry a maximum penalty of five years in prison and a fine of $250,000. This case is part of the Trump Administration’s Task Force to Eliminate Fraud.
