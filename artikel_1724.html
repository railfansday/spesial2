Lewis-Potter commits future to Brentford with new long-term contract

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Brentford have announced that Keane Lewis-Potter has put pen to paper on a new five-and-a-half-year deal with the club.

Lewis-Potter, who scored twice in Brentford's 4-0 win over Everton on Monday, has agreed fresh terms that will keep him at the club until the summer of 2032.

The 25-year-old joined Brentford from Hull City in July 2022 and has gone on to make 144 appearances in all competitions, scoring 15 goals and providing 10 assists.

In 11 matches this season, Lewis-Potter (four) has already beaten his best-ever scoring season in the Premier League (three in 36 apps in 2025-26 and three in 30 in 2023-24).

Lewis-Potter also tops Brentford's ranks for chances created (14) in the Premier League in 2026-27.

"It's the way the club works. We don't just think about tomorrow; we think about next week, next year and the years after," Brentford boss Keith Andrews said.

"We're trying to make the club really secure, really solid and forward-thinking. Keane is another very good player that's just signed up for the long term, so that is really pleasing."

Brentford enjoyed their joint-biggest home win in the Premier League against Everton, equalling their 4-0 thrashing of Manchester United in August 2022.

Lewis-Potter netted his second Premier League brace, with his first coming against Wolves in December 2025, with Vitaly Janelt and Fabio Carvalho also on target.

There were just 178 seconds between Lewis-Potter's two goals, making it the second-quickest Premier League brace for a Brentford player after Yoanne Wissa against Ipswich Town in November 2024 (137 seconds).

Since Andrews took charge of Brentford ahead of the 2025-26 season, of London clubs, only Arsenal (113) have won more Premier League points than the Bees (70).

Brentford and Lewis-Potter will be hoping to build on their win against Everton this Saturday, with a trip to Old Trafford to face Manchester United up next.
