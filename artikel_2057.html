Unai Emery and Aston Villa appear to be dealt a new blow every time the international window rolls around.

Amadou Onana's season-ending injury was the biggest kicker after the 2026 World Cup, and he's not the only player on the sidelines.

Matteo Ruggeri has been out since October, while Aaron Wan-Bissaka could make a return, and question marks remain over Johan Manzambi.

Up next is Sunderland on Saturday, 21 November, a team that only just lifted off the bottom of the Premier League table before the break.

All signs point towards the Villans getting their first win in five matches, but he won't have Matty Cash at right-back for the game, that's for sure.

Wan-Bissaka's fitness latest might just about save Emery from a major headache, but the manager will still be furious about what's happened.

By now, all Villa fans must have seen the incident that caused Cash to pick up a pretty nasty concussion during Poland's 2-1 loss to Romania.

Daniel Birligea elbowed Villa's No.2 directly in the face, forcing the player off quite soon after. The opposition faced no consequences at all.

Due to the incident, Cash will be unavailable to face the Black Cats, and he may not even make the Champions League game against Galatasaray.

Cash, himself, even posted footage of the incident on social media, simply captioned with multiple thumbs up emojis. It was clear for all to see.

Now, the Villans face the consequences, potentially missing him for two important games in the Premier League and Champions League.

The natural replacement for Cash would be Wan-Bissaka, but the West Ham United loanee hasn't been since the win over Newcastle United.

He has recently returned to first-team training, ready to make a comeback from a hamstring injury at Villa Park.

Of course, it could be far too soon to throw him back into the lineup, but what other choice does Emery have against Sunderland?

Taylor Harwood-Bellis could feature out of position again, or Lamare Bogarde could make a rare start, but both seem unlikely.
