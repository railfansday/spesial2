Nuno Espirito Santo could make a dramatic return to Nottingham Forest in a stunning twist of events.

The 52-year-old bowed out of his job at the City Ground in rather explosive circumstances, coming to blows with Evangelos Marinakis.

Nuno was clearly too honest about things going on behind the scenes, expressing his disappointment at the club's lack of transfers.

Marinakis doesn't have a calm head at the best of times, but the former manager's sacking was hardly a surprise, despite his good form.

Since then, Forest have been through Ange Postecoglou, Sean Dyche and Vitor Pereira, before finally landing on Oliver Glasner for this season.

The 2026-27 campaign has been a huge struggle for the new boss, however, and the expectation is that he'll be sacked soon as well.

The main question would be, who replaces the former Crystal Palace manager? But that might have just been answered by a new development.

Nuno has just been sacked by West Ham United, just 13 games into the new Championship season, which has left them eighth in the table.

Performances have been incredibly patchy under his leadership, but nobody can deny that achieved a lot of positive things at the City Ground.
