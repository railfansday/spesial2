Netflix's 8-Part Hard Sci-Fi Series Is Already Renewed For 2 More Seasons

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

While there are a lot of great original sci-fi shows to come out of Netflix over the years, only one has preemptively avoided a major industry issue with its two-season renewal. It’s a tale as old as time. Viewers are only just getting into a new series, often an ambitious genre show with sci-fi, fantasy, or horror elements, when it is abruptly canceled despite critical acclaim.

From HBO’s one-season wonder Lovecraft Country to the iconic space western Firefly, there is no shortage of promising shows that were unexpectedly ended early by shock cancellations. Often, this leaves their storylines frustratingly unfinished, and fans end up feeling short-changed no matter how much money might have been spent on the budget of these shows during their runs. One need only look at Prime Video’s fantasy series The Wheel of Time for another example.

While the Robert Jordan adaptation was consistently critically adored throughout its three-season run, the show’s abrupt ending arrived long before the series had adapted the entire literary saga to the small screen. As such, what had felt like three increasingly promising seasons of fantasy TV ended up seeming instead like a lot of buildups to nothing. This is why it is so exciting to hear that Netflix’s 3 Body Problem has already avoided this issue with a preemptive two-season renewal.

Based on Chinese novelist Liu Cixin’s trilogy of books Remembrance of Earth's Past, 3 Body Problem’s title came from a real-life Newtonian physics problem. Fittingly enough, 3 Body Problem’s plot focused on a group of scientific researchers from different disciplines, dubbed the “Oxford Five,” coming together to investigate the mysterious deaths of their fellow academics. Eventually, the group unmask a secret conspiracy involving a cult and alien fleets that are preparing to invade earth.

Only, that’s not quite an accurate summary, since 3 Body Problem’s depiction of first contact with extraterrestrial life isn’t as simple as invasions or attacks. Unlike the earlier underrated TNT sci-fi series Falling Skies, 3 Body Problem’s extraterrestrials aren’t straightforward sentient, corporeal creatures, and their plans for earth aren’t as obvious as world domination or the intentional, malicious destruction of human civilization.

Instead, 3 Body Problem’s story merges the past, the present, virtual realities, and layered versions of its story told from different perspectives to create a rich, complex character-driven saga. 3 Body Problem is undoubtedly an odyssey, mixing the ambitious scope of earlier genre shows like The Walking Dead, The Expanse, or Game of Thrones with the narrative complexity of Westworld or Lost.

Despite this ambition, 3 Body Problem doesn’t end up overextending itself in its first outing, and the show is now guaranteed to make it to the end of its source story without needing to rush through the plot at a rate of knots. Where earlier Netflix sci-fi shows like Stranger Things expanded their once-modest stories to become ever more commodious and elaborate, 3 Body Problem was always a complicated show.

Luckily, however, Netflix renewed 3 Body Problem for seasons 2 and 3, ensuring the show has sufficient screen time to adapt the remainder of the source novel series. This is particularly reassuring for fans of Remembrance of Earth's Past since, while the novel was celebrated at the time of its release, Liu Cixin’s books were also thought to be unfilmable due to their unique structure.

3 Body Problem needed to reorder events and rejig characters, while still maintaining the same central plot, and pulled this off with aplomb in season 1. However, it is only since Netflix’s renewal that viewers can be reassured 3 Body Problem will be able to stick the show's landing.
