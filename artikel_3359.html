Susan Sarandon & Aubrey Plaza Drama ‘The Accompanist’ Gets North American Deal With Watermelon Pictures

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Watermelon Pictures has picked up North American distribution rights to The Accompanist, co-written and directed by actor, writer and comedian Zach Woods, starring Oscar winner Susan Sarandon alongside Aubrey Plaza and Everly Carganilla.

The Tribeca world premiere, which also played at festivals including Rome and Deauville, is due for release in the U.S. in 2027.

The distribution deal was initiated by Audrey Delaney, Marketing Executive at Watermelon Pictures and was negotiated by Munir Atalla, EVP of Production and Acquisitions for Watermelon Pictures, and Jessica Lacy of Gersh on behalf of the filmmakers. Written by Zach Woods and Brandon Gardner, the film “centers on Emily (Carganilla), a nine-year-old girl living with her loving grandfather in New Jersey as his advancing dementia makes it increasingly difficult for him to care for her. After a young child-welfare agent (Plaza) intervenes, Emily is placed in the care of Sylvia (Sarandon), an eccentric, mischievous 70-year-old foster parent whose unconventional world initially leaves Emily uncertain of what to expect. As Emily and Sylvia form an unexpected bond, the boundaries between memory, imagination and reality begin to blur. Haunted by experiences from their pasts and visions they cannot easily explain, the two find themselves confronting old wounds and mysterious visitations as they discover the possibilities — and risks — of creating a family in an unlikely place.” Watch on Deadline

Watermelon specialises in releasing marginalized narratives and politically sensitive films and has a track record of acquiring movies about Palestine and the Israel-Gaza conflict. The Accompanist is not in its traditional wheelhouse but Susan Sarandon has been a strong advocate for Palestine and has spoken publicly about feeling marginalized within Hollywood for her political views.
