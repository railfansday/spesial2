Legend Of Zelda: Oracle Of Ages & Seasons Officially Returning In New Collection

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Legend of Zelda has returned to impressive prominence over the last year thanks to its 40th anniversary celebrations, after a quiet few years only punctuated by Echoes of Wisdom in 2024. The release of Ocarina of Time only bolstered that good feeling around the franchise, and it was so brilliant it feels like the good times could keep rolling for a while.

The movie is set to debut next year, marking the first time the franchise has ever appeared on the big screen in any meaningful way. That's also set to give us an entirely original story separate from the main narrative we follow in the games, which could be a fascinating way for them to tell stories outside the bounds they have set for themselves normally.

While we wait for all of those interesting questions to be answered, there's still plenty going on around the franchise that's worth tuning into if you're a fan. That includes one release in particular that's an absolute collector's dream.

As first reported by GameRant, Nintendo has officially revealed new box art for The Legend of Zelda: Master Collection, a new trading card collection being produced in conjunction with Italian company Panini, who made their name making football stickers.

The set was revealed as part of a relatively recent Nintendo Direct, and it's set to feature artwork from across the course of the series including all of the titles above. These will be distributed at random, so any pack could contain characters from any era and in any style, which should make the whole thing a little more fun to collect.

It's worth noting that this isn't a trading card game, and it doesn't come with any kind of dedicated mechanics that will allow people to play with one another. Instead, it's purely being released with the goal of allowing the most dedicated fans to fill up their binder, and they're even doing their own, custom binder to make that even easier.

Details about the set remain relatively thin on the ground, and other than announcing its existence we really don't know anything about it just yet. We haven't even been given the number of cards or a potential release date beyond Spring 2027, so it could be a little while before we hear much else.

Other than that, it's going to be a case of sitting tight and waiting to find out what's to come next for the mainline Legend of Zelda series. The rumors remain that it won't be as much of a departure from the Breath of the Wild formula as many are expecting, with an announcement predicted in the first half of next year.

The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages is a puzzle-focused action-adventure game that is one of two games that tell a complete story, Oracle of Seasons being the other title. In the game, Link is sent to Labrynna, where he must battle across the land to save the goddess of ages, Nayru, from the dark sorceress Veran.
