Man United 3-2 Roma: United end nine-game winless streak in remarkable fashion by battling back from 2-0 down to snatch priceless victory

Michael Carrick saw his job saved in the most remarkable way as Manchester United fought back from 2-0 down against Roma in the Champions League to snatch the unlikeliest of 3-2 wins at Old Trafford.

Carrick, who had seen United go winless in nine games for the first time in 40 years, was desperate for a change of fortunes to save his skin but his time at Old Trafford appeared to be coming to an end as the hopeless Red Devils were 2-0 down inside 20 minutes as part of a diabolical first-half display.

But they were a different team in the second half and were given a lifeline when Bruno Fernandes scored a re-taken penalty – with a Roma player encroaching into the area during the initial miss – after Matheus Cunha was fouled.

Cunha then became the hero when he capitalised on a Roma error seven minutes from time to equalise before firing a winning goal in style shortly after, although he was booked for his celebration and then sent off injury time after an clumsy lunging tackle earned a deserved second yellow.

There was huge relief for Carrick who will head into a crunch clash with Aston Villa on Sunday with at least a little bit of confidence restored as he looks to come back from the brink. Follow all the reaction from Man United’s remarkable comeback win over Roma in our live blog below:

Speaking to Uefa on what the next few weeks might hold for his future, Michael Carrick said: "I don't have a crystal ball to know what happens next. We move on to the weekend, we've got a really important game here against Aston Villa, which we need to prepare for right."

On the game, he said: "It was a challenging game. I think we started OK in the game, we knew it was going to be tough - they've only had one defeat all season. They're a very, very good team.

"So we talked about what we wanted to do in the game, we started OK and then the goal came from nowhere. We caused ourselves a few issues. In some ways understandable with the run we've been on, a little bit anxious and a few nerves at times.

"Then I thought we grew into the game a bit at the end of the first half and I spoke at half-time about being positive. Anything can happen here, I've seen it many times over the years. You just need something to go our way and the boys were fantastic second half.

"They kept going, didn't get disappointed and got exactly what they deserved in the end."

"There has been a lot of pressure on him and that's how we repay him, with a comeback like this, big character, big fight,” Youri Tielemans told Amazon Prime Video when asked about Michael Carrick.

On whether this result has saved the manager’s job: "That's a different question. The only thing we can do is stick behind him, fight for him and fight for the club.

"At the end of the day we are on the pitch and we need to show what we are capable of and tonight, especially in the second half, we showed what we can do and we turned up the gears.

"Let's hope this is a good turnaround for us and let's hope we can build on this."

Michael Carrick may still need a greater comeback than this if he is to keep his job. But even if it does prove his last Champions League night at Old Trafford, at least he was given a glimpse of the Manchester United he remembers and which, increasingly, had seemed consigned to the past.

There was an extraordinary fightback, a victory plucked from the jaws of defeat. The crowd who had reacted to the award of a penalty by calling for the Glazer family to go ended up galvanised and roused, cheering a team who had been an absolute embarrassment for 45 minutes to a victory few saw coming,

Carrick had said he knew it sounded crazy when he said he was looking forward to this game. By half-time, his optimism appeared delusional. But now and again, even when they are at their lowest ebb for decades, United can justify the faith.

Manchester United looked hopeless after that first half - but Michael Carrick somehow managed to drag his players out of the fire.

“Great credit to Michael Carrick because they were down and out at half-time,” said Alan Shearer on Amazon Prime. “They were everywhere in that first-half, and he had to change and tweak things.

“I said what can he do, I didn't know where he would start, and he must have started in the dressing room, and pointing at a few people telling them to be brave and courageous and move the ball quicker, and run in behind and they did all those things in the second half and got their reward by getting their win.”

Bruno Fernandes just passionately addressed the speculation surrounding him and Michael Carrick in recent weeks.

“The players needed that win,” he said on Amazon Prime Video. “It's something that we needed as a group of players together with the staff. We've been working so hard to get out of this situation all together.

“It's been tough. It's been a lot of speculation in the media, a lot of lies around many things that have happened in this club between me and the manager, like that we had cleared the air then after when nothing of that happened.

“The manager took a decision of making myself be on the bench because I wasn't performing, and that's fair. Everyone felt that was a punishment for the words that I had said. And if it was, it was right. Manager is the one that makes the decisions. I have to accept them. I have to go forward with that.

“It doesn't change the belief I have on the manager and on the staff and and every player that is in this club, and it doesn't change my aim to win every f***ing game that I have ahead of me.”

But after tonight’s turnaround, United are back in the win column.

Manchester United pull off a remarkable turnaround to surely save Michael Carrick’s job for another day. It’s a huge lifeline for the Red Devils boss, there’s no other way of putting it.

Roma played an abysmal United off the park in the first half, with goals from Matias Soula and Donyell Malen putting Roma 2-0 up at the break. United were booed off at half-time, and it didn’t look like this team were capable of a recovery.

But they produced a much improved performance after the break and after getting on the board through Bruno Fernandes’ re-taken penalty, Matheus Cunha’s late double flipped the result on its head and delivered United’s first win since they beat minnows Sabah in September.
