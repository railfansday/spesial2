Liverpool latest: Andoni Iraola explains ‘messy’ Champions League collapse against Fenerbahce

Fenerbahce 3-2 Liverpool: The visitors twice led in Istanbul but Reds icon Dirk Kuyt oversaw a stunning turnaround to inflict back-to-back defeats for Andoni Iraola

Liverpool dropped their first points of the Champions League as Dirk Kuyt’s Fenerbahce scored two late goals to stun Andoni Iraola’s side in an incredible comeback in Istanbul.

Alexander Isak had looked to make an instant impact for Liverpool as he met fellow substitute Cody Gakpo’s cross to head the Reds into the lead in the 69th minute, after Trey Nyoni’s first senior goal was cancelled out by Mason Greenwood’s deflected free-kick in an entertaining first half in Turkey.

The chances continued to flow but it was Fenerbahce, previously without a win in the Champions League this season, who capitalised. Liverpool’s lead was once again short lived as Archie Brown arrived at the back post to convert a wayward Gakpo header, before a towering header from Vedat Muriqi completed the comeback.

Liverpool, who had won their first three Champions League matches of the league phase, have now suffered back-to-back defeats following Sunday’s 1-0 loss against Premier League champions Arsenal. They will return to action when they travel to Premier League strugglers Crystal Palace on Sunday.

Follow all the reaction from Fenerbahce v Liverpool with our live blog below:

Andoni Iraola will be hoping to send his players off to the international break with a win when the Reds face Crystal Palace in the Premier League this weekend.

Another defeat would raise the pressure on Liverpool and make their first match after the break, versus Manchester United at Anfield, more important that it already is.

In the Champions League, the Reds will face Club Brugge at the end of November before fixtures against Porto, Inter Milan and Lens round out the league phase.

Fenerbahce’s interim manager, and former Liverpool midfielder, Dirk Kuyt gave his reaction to the result.

Tonight’s match was his second and final fixture as interim boss and Kuyt described the win as ‘a miracle’.

“A very special night for everyone who loves Fenerbahce,” he said.

“We told each other to stick together and be united. That’s something I learned from Liverpool.

“We made a miracle tonight, not many expected us to win.”

Andoni Iraola made five changes to the Liverpool starting XI for this evening’s game but admitted that he does not think switching out the personnel was to blame for the defeat.

“We have done this not just today, we have done it all season. All the games midweek we have been changing four or five as it’s impossible to retain the starting XI,” Iraola added.

“I trust the squad to sustain the level and I don’t think it is the main reason why we lost.

“We have to look more at how we conceded the goals as the reason we didn’t win.”

“We conceded from a free-kick, a corner, a long throw and that has hurt us,” Iraola continued.

“We have gone in front twice, we conceded in situations we should have definitely done better (with).

“It is frustrating because it is a difficult place to play and for the most part of the game we were thinking we could win this one.

“The first one the wall we didn’t set properly, the last one when he jumps he wins the ball against three defenders.

“I think before scoring the goals we had very good moments on the ball, very good offensive situations. The thing is we conceded almost straight away.

“We have been dealing well with these situations up to today. It has cost us. We have to do better.”

More reaction from Liverpool’s manager who is not happen with the manner in which his team conceded.

He called Fenerbahce’s goals ‘cheap and messy’ amid the atmosphere inside the stadium.

“The free-kick is quite far out, through the wall. The last goal was a long throw, the second one Cody (Gakpo) touched the ball just in front of Ali (goalkeeper Alisson).

“In the stadium it was almost impossible to communicate, it was difficult for me to give instructions and the communication was difficult between Cody and Ali.”

The Fenerbahce striker added: "Just that we were all going to suffer together because we know Liverpool are one of the best teams in Europe. They've got great players at the top of the pitch.

"We knew if we stuck together in the difficult moments, in the low block, we'd be able to create chances, catch them on the break like we did in the first half.

“Then we knew we'd be very good in the air, would be able to create some chances, we managed to take them and get the win."

Fenerbahce’s first goalscorer, Mason Greenwood, said: "It was a great performance from the team, we dug in until the end and always thought we'd have a chance to win the game, even though we went 1-0 down.

"I want to congratulate all the coaching staff, the players and especially the fans for getting behind us. We're very happy to get this win tonight."

That’s the belief of striker Alexander Isak who came off the bench to score and put the Reds 2-1 ahead in the second half.

He told the club’s website: "It's never easy here but today I think we could and should get more out of the game.

“It's a bit sloppy that we had the lead twice and for us to then lose the game is not good enough.

“Obviously we're going to be disappointed from that."

The Liverpool boss added: "It's frustration because it's a difficult place to come here and play. In most parts of the game we were thinking we could win this one but obviously it hasn't happened.

"Especially the first goal and the third goal - the second one is a rebound - but the first one, I don't think we set the wall properly and the last one, Muriqi wins the ball against three defenders, it's definitely something we need to defend better."

Liverpool manager Andoni Iraola voiced his thoughts on the match at his post game press conference.

He spoke first about the result and where Liverpool lost the game.

"The main reason that I can find for why we lost this game is we conceded from three set pieces,” Iraola explained.

“It's something that we were doing very well in the league, we were doing quite well until today.

"We knew it was one of the strengths of Fenerbahce. We concede from a free-kick, a corner and even from the long throw at the end.

"They're goals have hurt us because we've gone twice in front after having good moments, controlling the game, arriving, we've found the goals but after we've conceded in situations we should definitely defend better.”
