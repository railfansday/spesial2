Germany face Netherlands as the rivals vie for top spot in their Nations League group.

Jurgen Klopp’s side earned a 1-1 draw against the Netherlands in Amsterdam in his first game in charge of Germany last month.

But it is Xavi Hernandez’s Dutch side who are in control of Group A2, sitting on 11 points after thrashing Greece 7-0 last time out.

Germany are three points behind the Netherlands in third place, which means they need to win to overtake the Dutch into top spot.

A Greece victory over pointless Serbia combined with a Germany victory, however, would mean all three sides finish the group on 11 points.

With the Netherlands needing to avoid defeat to guarantee top, and Germany at risk of missing on the Finals altogether, here’s everything you need to know.

The Nations League clash is available to watch on Amazon Prime pay-per-view, at a cost of £2.49. Coverage begins at 7:35pm GMT, with kick-off at 7:45pm.

The draw will take place at 11am GMT on Friday 20 November.

England will feature in the last eight of the Nations League 2026/27, with a two-legged tie to decide who will play in the semi-finals and final, both to be played across a ‘finals’ week.

As well as the Nations League quarter-finals, the League A/B and League B/C promotion/relegation play-offs will be drawn, set to be held in March 2027. The final tournament is scheduled to take place across 9–13 June 2027.

England's potential opponents in the last eight of the Uefa Nations League as things stand are Belgium, Greece or Denmark.

Beating Spain ensured the Three Lions will face a second-placed team in their quarter-final; Belgium have finished second in their group, while Greece could be pipped by Germany and Denmark could still be replaced by either Norway or Portugal. England topping the group also means they will play the second leg of their quarter-final at home.
