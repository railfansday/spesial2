Allied Gold (AAUC) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Kurmuk output ramps as the company turns to free cash flow and shareholder returns.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Allied Gold Corporation (AAUC +4.73%) reported its third quarter results highlighted by the commencement of production at the Kurmuk mine in Ethiopia and operational improvements at the Sadiola mine following the termination of the Zijin transaction. Management reported that the company is transitioning into a period of increased free cash flow generation as major development capital expenditures at Kurmuk decline and production ramps toward 300,000 ounces annually. The company stated that its capital allocation framework now prioritizes shareholder returns, including a 10% share buyback program and the introduction of a base dividend in 2027. Management indicated that operational initiatives deferred during the corporate transaction process, such as equipment contract terminations and fleet reliability commitments, are now being implemented to lower unit costs and improve production grades across the West African operations.
