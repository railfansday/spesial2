Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Separating business and personal expenses

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

How do folks separate out personal and business expenses for home businesses for joint expense like utilities and insurance? I have been reading about the need to keep accounts separate to keep the LLC liability shield in place. It seems painful to separately pay the business portion (e.g. 10%) of each utility bill with a separate check each month.

Likewise, do I need to make bank transfers each time I consume something from the business? As simpler example than my actual business, if I have lemonade stand, do I need to put cash in the register each time I drink a glass of lemonade? Or can I just track how much lemonade I consume for the year and include that in the final profit/loss accounting?

Regarding deductible meals: Do you pay those on a personal credit card and then have the business transfer the deductible portion? Or pay with the business credit card and reimburse the business for the personal part?
