Yellowstone star Josh Holloway's upcoming sci-fi thriller has officially secured a release date.

Roadside Attractions has acquired U.S. distribution rights to One Second After, setting a nationwide theatrical release for April 30, 2027. Directed by Scott Rogers, the thriller also stars Hannah John-Kamen, Steven Ogg, Famke Janssen, and Mary McDonnell. One Second After marks Rogers' feature directorial debut.
