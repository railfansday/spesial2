‘Dancing With the Stars’ Crowns Season 35 Champion

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Ezra Frech and Daniella Karagach shimmied off with the Len Goodman Mirrorball trophy.

Ezra Frech and Daniella Karagach shimmied off with the Len Goodman Mirrorball trophy after winning season 35 of ABC’s Dancing With The Stars.

In doing so, Frech, the two-time Paralympic gold medalist for Team U.S.A. in track and field, becomes the first amputee winner of the celebrity dance-off.

The finale episode on Tuesday night (Nov. 24) saw contestants compete in three rounds. First-up, the judges’ choice round, where each judge threw down the gauntlet by choosing the dance style for the final competitors. Next was the instant dance challenge, which meant the contestants needed to be fast learners, and quick on their feet. And finally, each couple contested the freestyle round — the make-or-break business end of the popular show.

The winning pair hit a freestyle soundtracked by Linkin Park’s “Numb.” Ezra began the number without his prosthetic on, and had a problem with it slipping during the routine. They recovered, and the judges loved it. Carrie Ann Inaba, Derek Hough and Bruno Tonioli awarded 30/30.

They also nailed perfect scores for the instant dance challenge and judges’ choice, for an unbeatable night.

In an earlier interview published in The Hollywood Reporter, Frech said it would be “very, very special” to become the first amputee winner of DWTS. “Of course, getting the opportunity to make the finale, to win the show, to get to represent the disabled community in that way and become the first amputee to win Dancing With the Stars would be very, very special, and would mean a lot to myself and hopefully to all people with disabilities and the community as a whole,” he remarked.

After the final votes were counted, Amber Glenn and Pasha Pashkov came in second, with Connor Wood and Rylee Arnold completing the podium.

Also during the final, season 34 winner Robert Irwin, son of the late Australian environmentalist Steve “Crocodile Hunter,” joined the broadcast to announce the return of Dancing With the Stars: The Next Pro, from which contestants will get a chance to join the season 36 cast.
