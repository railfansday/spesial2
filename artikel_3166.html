Reliant Talent Adds Jason Zenowich, Kyle Levinson, Rick Ferrell & Michelle Romeo As Agents

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

EXCLUSIVE: Reliant Talent Agency has added four new agents across their talent, touring and corporate departments.

On the talent theatrical side, the company has hired veteran talent agent Jason Zenowich who has worked at ICM, A3 and most recently the Kohner Agency. Zenowich will be located in Los Angeles and will bring a majority of his clients from the former.

In their touring division, Reliant has hired Rick Ferrell and Kyle Levinson to the division. Ferrell has 30 years experience with tenures at ICM, APA and Gersh. Levinson comes to the company following stints at UTA and IAG. On the corporate and private events side, the company brought on veteran talent buyer Michelle Romeo. Formerly a co-owner at Romeo Entertainment Group, Romeo counts more than 20 years of industry expertise across talent buying and live events. Watch on Deadline

With its headquarters in Nashville, TN, Reliant was formed six years ago when the Nashville offices of APA/IAG left with their roster to form their own music touring agency. The firm has agents located in Nashville, Los Angeles and New York City. Reliant added a TV/Film/Theatrical department and a comedy touring division over two years ago securing senior talent agents Scott Simpson and Jonathan Perry whom they worked with closely at APA/IAG. That department has now grown to five talent agents and three comedy touring agents.

“We’re pleased to announce these additions to our company as we continue our upward scale,” said agency President, Steve Lassiter. “These agents bring decades of experience, integrity and passion that align with our client facing, 360 approach to our business.”
