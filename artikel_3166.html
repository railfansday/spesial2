Bending Spoons (BSP +0.84%) reported financial results for the third quarter of 2026, highlighting the integration of large-scale enterprise software assets and the expansion of its global acquisition pipeline. Management reported significant growth in total revenue and adjusted operating income driven by the consolidation of recent acquisitions including Airtable and Vimeo. The company stated it has secured $8.35 billion in financing since the start of the quarter to support its M&A strategy, including a debut bond issuance and private placements from investors such as Berkshire Hathaway. Management indicated that it is focusing on transforming acquired businesses by rewriting codebases and implementing proprietary AI-driven operational tools to improve productivity and monetization.
