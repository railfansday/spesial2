Khloe Kardashian Feels 'Played' by Lamar Odom After Netflix Documentary Drama

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Khloé Kardashian aired out her frustrations about ex-husband Lamar Odom – and revealed the message he tried to pass along to her after his documentary drama.

During the Thursday, November 19, episode of The Kardashians, Khloé, 42, got annoyed when Kim Kardashian showed her some of Odom’s interviews about her, saying, “The fact that he’s doing interviews being so angry toward me, are you well? I know you’re not. Anyone that was around saw what was going on. And then, you played me.”

“Everyone can f*** off. I honestly hate everyone and everyone’s a pathological liar and they all are sociopathic. You can’t trust any of them,” she continued. “All those years that I thought we were in love, I was used.”

The reality star admitted her marriage to Odom, 47, was “real” for her, adding, “That’s what hurts my heart so badly. Seeing how bamboozled I’ve been, do you think I care to trust any male ever in my life again? No.”

In a confessional, Khloé made it clear she had no regrets. “When I look back at my relationship with Lamar, I am proud of myself for how I’ve conducted myself from everything that I chose to do for him without him even asking,” she said. “Because I do believe it’s the right thing to do.”

She continued: “I am pissed. Regardless of how Lamar ended up treating me, it doesn’t matter. I don’t believe an eye for an eye. I just want to do the right thing. I want to have a good moral compass. I want to feel good about myself. People will have to answer to their own moral compass at some point in life.”

Khloé went on to reveal what Odom texted her friend after his Netflix documentary, Untold: The Death & Life of Lamar Odom, was released.

“Three days ago [he texted], ‘We’re always gonna be family. Tell Khloé that out of my mouth,'” she claimed.

The former couple dated for one month before getting engaged in 2009 and exchanging vows that same year in a televised ceremony. Khloé filed for divorce four years later but later postponed her petition after Odom was found unconscious in a Las Vegas brothel. (The former athlete was in a coma for three days and later required months of rehabilitation.)

They officially got divorced in 2016, with their relationship being revisited in Netflix’s Untold documentary earlier this year. The project made headlines, however, when Odom walked back the suggestion that Khloé saved his life after his near-fatal 2015 overdose.

“You guys are the biggest blood-sucking users. Get the away from me. When I come to my senses and think about humans, I’m never gonna date again,” Khloé continued on The Kardashians. “I hate everyone. I don’t trust a soul. You guys are all full of s***.”

Khloé hinted she changed her opinion on dating as a result of the drama, adding, “Just when I think I’m going to try something and this could be fun. No, it can’t. Wake up. It’s all bulls***. … Everyone just leave me alone. I’m just happier.”
