You can finally add me to your meetings now, Built a conference website on top of convex

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Back in 2024 I started a project something around the lines of "code study" where you will be able to join meetings with a bunch of random people who have the same perspective as you do.. and Just sit down and do something while just being muted the whole time (Kinda like a public library), and back then the realtime kit was in beta, so we kinda just shook it off.

Today, or for a few weeks I've been iterating over the idea again, and decided to just do it, so I did! I'm still making it feasible and better as I go through the permissions side of things, but general scope, of the things I used: *Convex, Convex auth V2, Aggregate component, cloudflare Realtime kit, Convex's AI gateway, convex's Rate-limit component, My cloudflare email component and a bunch of other things. *

You can schedule meetings, schedule public rooms, and add custom permissions and so much more.

Schedule meetings across time zones, call in the browser and keep a recap of every call. A working, open-source app built with Convex.
