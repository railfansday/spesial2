14-year-old Adrian Peterson Jr. receives offer from Oklahoma

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

14-year-old Adrian Peterson Jr. receives offer from Oklahoma appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Oklahoma has extended a scholarship offer to Adrian Peterson Jr., giving the son of former Sooners standout and NFL running back Adrian Peterson an opportunity to follow in his father's footsteps in Norman.

The 14-year-old announced Saturday on social media that he received an offer from the Sooners. A member of the 2030 recruiting class, Peterson Jr. is currently a freshman at Fort Bend Ridge Point High School in Missouri City, Texas. He is listed at 6-foot-1 and 175 pounds and has experience playing multiple positions, including running back, wide receiver and defensive back.

Peterson Jr. has already attracted considerable attention from college programs. His scholarship offers include Miami, Florida State, Texas Tech, Texas A&M, Baylor, Minnesota, Syracuse, TCU, SMU, UTSA, Missouri State and Texas State, along with Oklahoma, according to 247Sports.

The young athlete has begun making an impact at the high school level. Through six games, he has recorded seven receptions for 95 yards and two touchdowns, according to MaxPreps.

His father became one of the Sooners' most accomplished players between 2004 and 2006. Adrian Peterson Sr. racked up 4,041 rushing yards and 41 touchdowns across three seasons with the Sooners, averaging 5.4 yards per carry. He was a unanimous All-American in 2004, finished as the Heisman Trophy runner-up and earned first-team All-Big 12 recognition in each of his three seasons.

Peterson Sr. eventually carried that success into the NFL, finishing his 15-year professional career with 14,918 rushing yards, the fifth-highest total among running backs in league history.

With Oklahoma now among the programs pursuing his son, the Sooners have an opportunity to add another member of the Peterson family to their football program when the 2030 recruiting cycle arrives.
