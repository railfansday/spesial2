Aidan Hutchinson’s injury was a freak accident – and totally avoidable

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Aidan Hutchinson's sack-celebration injury is not the first time the Detroit Lions have seen a defender's season end thanks to having too much fun after putting the opposing quarterback on the ground.

Hutchinson's reported torn ACL, which will end his 2026 season pending a second opinion according to ESPN, occurred early in the first quarter of the Lions' 35-10 victory over the New England Patriots in Munich, Germany. But 12 years earlier, former Lions defensive lineman Stephen Tulloch mimicked Aaron Rodgers' "discount double check" State Farm spot only to land awkwardly and end his season with a torn ACL.

Players have all kinds of ways of expressing their wins play to play. New York Giants running back Cam Skattebo backflips after he scores (or appears at Fanatics Fest with an injured leg). Hutchinson's injury was a freak accident – and totally avoidable.

Speaking on "Sunday Night Football," NBC's Cris Collinsworth said that he would ban players jumping in jubilation if he were an NFL head coach.

"If you're celebrating a touchdown, a sack, or whatever, it is against the law to leave your feet. No jumping," he said. "How many more players are we gonna have to see ... have a major injury, celebrating by jumping and landing the wrong way? It made me sick today watching Aidan Hutchinson hurt himself after a great play."

Hutchinson's injury wasn't the lone ailment suffered on the play; Patriots quarterback Drake Maye exited with an ankle injury that could also keep him out for an extended period. It was Hutchinson's sixth sack of the year.

A sack ended Hutchinson's 2024 season when he suffered a broken tibia against the Dallas Cowboys. But that occurred during the play.

Hutchinson is certainly not the first NFL player to suffer a serious injury while celebrating. One famous example – certainly more synonymous with the celebration injuries compared to Tulloch – is Washington quarterback Gus Frerotte giving himself a concussion and sprained neck by head-butting a padded concrete wall after scoring a touchdown in a tie against the New York Giants in 1997. As his Chicago Bears were trailing by 25 points against the Patriots in 2014, Lamarr Houston suffered a torn ACL while celebrating – a few weeks after Tulloch went down.

Hutchinson's celebration, which he's done for years, is based on soccer star Cristiano Ronaldo's patented goal celebration ‒ in which the player sprints towards the corner flag, leaps high, twirls in mid-air, then lands with his feet planted wide and his arms extended. Baltimore Ravens kicker Tyler Loop (and essentially the entire team) did the celebration after Loop's walk-off field goal in Brazil in September.

Arizona Cardinals kicker Bill Gramatica also tore his ACL in 2002 by jumping and landing awkwardly after a successful 42-yard field goal.

"For some reason, people tend to remember me every time something like that happens," Gramatica told ESPN. "This is the most high-profile one I think we've seen. It's going to sting for a while, but it won't define him. He's such an incredible athlete and he's going to come back. He's already playing with a chip on his shoulder, it feels like. So this is just going to add to that.

"I think mine will still be the most famous just because they can pick on me. They made fun of a kicker. I guarantee you no one's going to be making fun of him. ... You want to compare me to him? I'll take it."
