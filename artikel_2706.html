Panthers Burn IR Slot Despite Christensen Being Healthy

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

They let the 21-day practice window for offensive lineman Brady Christensen expire on Tuesday. This decision ended his 2026 season early even though he is physically ready to play. The team chose to keep him off the active roster, a choice that burns a valuable resource for the front office.

Head coach Dave Canales did not hide the fact that Christensen is fit to play. When the team opened the window in November, Canales stated, "He's healthy, he's strong, he's fit, he's ready to go." The offensive lineman has practiced in full for weeks after suffering a hamstring injury back in August. Despite this readiness, the Panthers did not activate him to the 53-man roster before the deadline. Instead, he reverts to injured reserve and his season is officially over.

The coach admitted the team wanted to see him play football to evaluate his fit. "But we've got to get some football under his belt here, so that'll be a good addition to the group to evaluate where you can help us potentially." Waiting for a player to get game reps is a valid strategy. The team now has a player who cannot help them in the immediate future.

The real cost of this decision goes beyond just losing a player for a few weeks. Designating Christensen as a return candidate means the Panthers still count that move against their injured reserve return limit. The team wasted one of its IR return slots even though he never got activated.

According to ProFootballTalk, Christensen stays on injured reserve and cannot rejoin the 2026 team roster. He is a third-round pick from 2021 with 59 career games and 34 starts to his name. A torn Achilles during the 2025 season and a hamstring problem in August have affected him. They also know he is healthy right now.

Canales called the choice difficult during a press conference on Friday. "It's a difficult decision. That's something Dan and I have been talking about and working through, especially with what Brady has done for us. The Panthers think current roster options surpass Christensen. That is the reason given for the move. The team plays the Tampa Bay Buccaneers on Monday, Nov. 30, at 8:15 p.m. ET at Raymond James Stadium.
