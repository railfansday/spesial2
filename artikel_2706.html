Colts sign Shane Buechele and Wanya Morris in flurry of roster moves, place Caden Curry on IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Indianapolis Colts made a series of roster moves Tuesday, signing quarterback Shane Buechele from the Buffalo Bills' practice squad, promoting defensive end Carl Lawson to the 53-man roster and placing rookie defensive end Caden Curry on injured reserve. The organization also waived offensive tackle Charlie Heck while adding tight end Sean McKeon and defensive end Cam Sample to the practice squad, according to the team's official announcement.

Indianapolis wasn't finished there, as ESPN's Adam Schefter also reported that the Colts are signing offensive tackle Wanya Morris from the Dallas Cowboys' practice squad to their active roster, providing additional help to an offensive line that has been severely affected by injuries throughout the season.

The addition of Buechele comes just days after Indianapolis released Daniel Jones and announced that Anthony Richardson would return as the starting quarterback for the remainder of the 2026 season. With Richardson now receiving another opportunity to prove himself and Riley Leonard also on the roster, Buechele provides additional depth and experience in a quarterback room that has undergone considerable changes.

Buechele entered the NFL as an undrafted free agent with the Kansas City Chiefs in 2021 after playing collegiately at Texas and SMU. He spent several seasons developing behind Patrick Mahomes before joining Buffalo in 2023, where he spent time on both the active roster and practice squad. His only regular-season appearance came with Kansas City in 2025, when he completed seven of 14 passes for 88 yards. While he is unlikely to challenge Richardson for the starting job, his familiarity with NFL systems gives Indianapolis another option as it navigates the final six games of the season.

Morris, meanwhile, represents an interesting addition given the current state of the Colts' offensive line. The 26-year-old was selected in the third round of the 2023 NFL Draft by Kansas City and appeared in 43 games, making 16 starts during his three seasons with the Chiefs. He was traded to Atlanta during the offseason before being released in August, eventually joining Dallas' practice squad.

Indianapolis has been forced to make significant adjustments at offensive tackle following injuries to Bernhard Raimann and Jalen Travis. With Raimann dealing with a concussion and Travis sidelined by a knee injury, the Colts started Luke Tenuta at left tackle and moved Matt Goncalves to right tackle against Houston. Morris provides another experienced option at a position where the team has struggled to maintain continuity, while Heck's departure ends a brief stint in Indianapolis after signing with the organization on October 29.

On the defensive side, the promotion of Lawson comes at the expense of losing another promising young contributor.

Curry, a sixth-round selection in the 2026 NFL Draft, has appeared in all 11 games this season, recording 14 tackles, one sack, two fumble recoveries and 12 special teams stops. The former Ohio State defensive end has provided useful depth along the defensive line while establishing himself as a dependable contributor on special teams. His placement on injured reserve means he will be required to miss at least four games, a disappointing development for a rookie who had begun carving out a role within the organization.

Lawson should help compensate for that absence, providing Indianapolis with an experienced pass rusher who has recorded 32 sacks across his NFL career. The former Cincinnati Bengals fourth-round pick joined the Colts' practice squad in October and was previously elevated for the team's matchup against Jacksonville, recording one tackle. Although he is no longer the player who consistently generated pressure earlier in his career, he offers additional experience to a defensive front that has already undergone considerable changes following DeForest Buckner's departure.

The Colts also added two familiar NFL veterans to their practice squad in McKeon and Sample.

McKeon returns to Indianapolis after previously spending time with the organization during the 2024 and 2025 seasons, as well as participating in this year's offseason program and training camp. His addition provides additional tight end depth following Mo Alie-Cox's placement on injured reserve earlier this month.

Sample, meanwhile, was originally selected by Cincinnati in the fourth round of the 2021 NFL Draft and has appeared in 61 career games, recording seven sacks. The veteran defensive end has spent time with the Bengals, 49ers and Falcons, and could provide another option for Indianapolis should additional injuries occur along the defensive line.

With the Colts sitting at 3-8 and preparing to face the New York Giants on Sunday, the latest moves reflect an organization attempting to manage mounting injuries while continuing to evaluate its roster for the future. Richardson's return has already brought significant changes to the offense, and the additions of Buechele and Morris provide some much-needed depth at two important positions.

While none of these transactions is likely to dramatically change the team's outlook over the final six games, they should help Indianapolis navigate the remainder of an increasingly difficult season while giving several players an opportunity to demonstrate whether they deserve a role moving forward.
