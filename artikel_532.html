Disney Profit Beats With Marvel Films Powering Streaming

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Walt Disney reported sales and earnings that exceeded analysts’ expectations, with a particularly strong result in its streaming TV unit. Operating income in business that include the Disney+ and Hulu streaming services doubled to $824 million in the fiscal fourth quarter, due to higher subscription and ad revenue. Viewership of Marvel superhero-related programming more than doubled as fans caught up on the company’s catalog of Spider-Man and Avengers films, tied in part to theatrical releases from both this year, Disney said in a statement Tuesday. Geetha Ranganathan, Bloomberg Intelligence US Media Analyst said Disney earnings mean show a strong outlook for 2027. (Source: Bloomberg)
