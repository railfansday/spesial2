Larry Poindexter, ‘S.W.A.T.’ and ‘Days Of Our Lives’ Actor, Dies at 66

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Larry Poindexter, an actor who appeared in more than 175 film and TV shows including “The Lincoln Lawyer,” “S.W.A.T” and more, died Wednesday, his rep confirmed. He was 66.

Poindexter had been hospitalized nearly five days prior after he was hit by a car while crossing the street in Studio City on Friday. He died suddenly Wednesday morning ahead of a scheduled surgery to repair his broken ribs. Over the course of four decades, Poindexter made appearances on TV shows like “The Facts of Life,” “Growing Pains,” “The Twilight Zone,” “Duet,” “Bones,” “Quantum Leap,” “Murphy Brown,” “Melrose Place,” “Friends,” “Sabrina the Teenage Witch,” “Criminal Minds,” “Weeds” and more. He also had prominent roles on “Santa Barbara,” “General Hospital,” “The Hard Times of RJ Berger” and “Days of Our Lives.” In recent years, Poindexter appeared on “The Lincoln Lawyer,” “9-1-1,” “The Rookie,” “Station 19,” and “NCIS,” among other shows.

On the silver screen, Poindexter is perhaps best known for his role in 2003’s “S.W.A.T.,” though he also appeared in films including “17 Again.”

Born and raised in Texas, Pointfexter’s parents are Tony Award-winning lighting and set designer H.R. Poindexter and opera singer Sue Ann Poindexter. He began acting in college before appearing in summer stock at Dallas Summer Musicals, the St. Louis MUNY, Atlanta’s Theatre Under the Stars and The Kenley Players in Ohio. He began his career in Los Angeles as an actor and production co-coordinator with Franklin R. Levy and Catalina Production Group.

In addition to acting, he was also the lead singer and songwriter for the band “The High Lonesome” in the early ’90’s. He served as an executive producer on Broadway’s “The Cher Show” and the upcoming “Saved By The Bell, The Musical.”

Poindexter is survived by his wife, casting director Carol Kritzer, and their two sons.
