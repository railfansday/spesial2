‘GTA VI’ Generates $3.79 Billion in “Day One” Sales

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Grand Theft Auto VI is out here breaking every record: the game generated $3.79 billion in “day one retail sales,” Rockstar Games said on Friday. That’s a whole lot of love — it’s like Jason- and Lucia-levels of love, actually (if you play the game correctly).

Rockstar is using its own internal estimate through the end of Thursday. The eye-popping number includes pre-sales and makes GTA VI the “highest-grossing entertainment property” of all time, the Take-Two label announced.

It’s a “new record for entertainment across any medium” and “the biggest entertainment launch in history,” per Rockstar. GTA VI sold 32.5 million units pre-release.

“It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI,” said Sam Houser, president and founder of Rockstar. “We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.”

For the 10 of you who have not yet bought it, Grand Theft Auto VI is available at the PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store, Rockstar Games Store, and global retailers and storefronts. A PC launch will come at some point, as will GTA VI Online.
