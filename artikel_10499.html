GTA 6 album leak reveals 34-song tracklist featuring Travis Scott, Dua Lipa, Morgan Wallen and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The full tracklist for Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album has allegedly leaked online, with Dua Lipa, Lana Del Rey, and Playboi Carti among the artists reportedly contributing.

Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album was confirmed back in September, with some musical artists sharing posts to their social media accounts all but confirming their involvement in the soundtrack.

Since then, Rockstar has released a number of singles, including Travis Scott’s RHYNO, Yung Lean’s That’s It, and Justice’s Flower in a Hurricane, which features Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. Now, the full track list has seemingly leaked online.

While Rockstar Games has yet to officially confirm the tracklist, the leaked running order matches the positioning of songs already released by the developer, lending credibility to the claims.

The purported 34-song album is separated into four sides, each featuring an eclectic selection of established artists, spanning genres such as hip-hop, pop, electronic, Latin and R&B.

BREAKING: The full GTA 6 album tracklist has leaked online. 34 songs from artists including Travis Scott, Playboi Carti, Lana Del Rey, Dua Lipa, JENNIE, SZA, Cardi B, Future, Camila Cabello, Zara Larsson, Tame Impala, Young Thug and more. https://t.co/MJMTq0u8lw

Rockstar have confirmed that the companion album is set to drop on November 19, the same day that the game releases. It will available digitally on streaming apps such as Spotify as well as physically. Elsewhere on the internet, leaks of the physical vinyl sleeves have also been shared.

GTA 6: The Album’s tracklist has been leaked. pic.twitter.com/DJQLMimHsW

The album is separate from the game’s in-game radio soundtrack and original score. Composed by Justice, the original score will receiving its own 30-track album release on December 11.
