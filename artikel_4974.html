CoinGecko secures Solana Foundation backing and eyes crypto trading

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Solana Foundation backing puts CoinGecko's data independence under scrutiny as the platform explores trading services.

CoinGecko announced Solana Foundation backing on Nov. 17, saying its website, app, and data API will continue operating as usual while the crypto data provider explores trading capabilities.

The company's growth announcement described the relationship as backing without setting out its value, board arrangements, or transaction-specific governance rights. It assures users and businesses that it will keep serving the broader crypto ecosystem as its products evolve.

CoinGecko plans to strengthen its data and API infrastructure and expand coverage of on-chain assets, real-world assets and derivatives. Through that expansion, it aims to serve developers, businesses and financial institutions.

It also plans broader market intelligence and simpler discovery tools, with trading as the third priority. CoinGecko said it is exploring capabilities that connect research and discovery with market participation.

Alongside those plans, CoinGecko pledges to maintain data integrity and keep its methodologies transparent and publicly accessible. Its existing documentation gives data users a concrete reference for how the company says it calculates prices and assesses exchanges.

CoinGecko outlines three areas of development with Solana Foundation backing, alongside a continuity pledge for its existing data platform.

Trading remains exploratory; the growth announcement gives no launch date, execution model, fees or eligibility.

The published price methodology notes that it collects ticker data across integrated exchanges, excludes anomalous prices, and takes a volume-weighted average of the remaining tickers.

A volume-weighted price gives more weight to higher-volume trades. The resulting figure combines the retained tickers, making the treatment of anomalous prices part of how the displayed aggregate is formed.

The methodology also says changes to price aggregation require prior discussion and approval by a multi-departmental Governance Forum.

CoinGecko's growth announcement does not explain whether the backing gives the Foundation any rights over that process, so continued access to methodology documents is a transparency commitment.

CoinGecko's Trust Score documentation separately points out that exchange-quality assessments are based on liquidity, cybersecurity, regulation, past incidents, and proof of reserves, with scores recalculated weekly.

The measure considers more than an exchange's reported trading volume, with these criteria making exchange ranking a separate assessment from the coin-price calculation.

Users consulting prices and exchange scores are looking at two documented processes with different inputs and purposes.

The documentation also limits how much users can inspect, with CoinGecko saying it will reflect methodology changes in published documentation, while withholding specific thresholds and metric breakdowns to reduce manipulation.
