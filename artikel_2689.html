Christian Watson blows up for 171 yards, TD in Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Green Bay Packers wide receiver Christian Watson posted seven receptions for 171 yards and a touchdown on eight targets in Week 9 against the New England Patriots.

Why it Matters: Watson has managed to stay healthy this season, and that's finally helped him realize his immense talent. We've seen glimpses of brilliance before, but hamstring injuries kept him off the field. He is rolling as a WR1 in all fantasy leagues.
