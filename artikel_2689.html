Thunder vs. Pacers: Live updates, highlights for Monday's game

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Oklahoma City Thunder travel on the road to face the Indiana Pacers on Monday. This will be the first matchup between the two squads in the 2026-27 regular season.

The Thunder (10-7) are on the second night of a road back-to-back. They punted in a 123-113 loss to the Miami Heat on Sunday. Jared McCain had 20 points and three assists. Bennett Stirtz scored a career-best 21 points and five assists.

Meanwhile, the Pacers (5-12) are also on the second night of a back-to-back. They fell to the New York Knicks in a 113-106 loss on Sunday. Pascal Siakam had 25 points and six rebounds in the losing effort. Indiana has dropped four in a row.
