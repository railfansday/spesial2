Eagles sign LB Chance Campbell, waive C Jake Majors

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Eagles made a couple of changes to their roster ahead of Thursday's game against the Cowboys.

The team announced that they have signed linebacker Chance Campbell off of their practice squad. They waived center Jake Majors in a corresponding move.

Campbell has appeared in one game this season. He played 11 special teams snaps and made one tackle in Week 3. Campbell also played in four games for the Titans during the 2023 season.

Majors started two games earlier this season while the Eagles were dealing with a number of injuries on the offensive line. Drew Kendall is now handling center duties for the Eagles.
