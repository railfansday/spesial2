Ja'Kobi Lane targeted 11 times in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Baltimore Ravens WR Ja'Kobi Lane had three receptions for 61 yards against the Los Angeles Chargers in Week 10. He was targeted a game-high 11 times in the loss.

Why It Matters: Lane played just one game with Lamar Jackson before suffering a fractured wrist. When he returned, it was Tyler Huntley, and then Joe Fagnano. Jacoby Brissett was already the fourth starting QB that the rookie has played with, but this might have been his most encouraging performance as he set career highs in both targets (11) and receiving yards (61). Lane is worth adding in deeper leagues and could even some value in standard formats if he continues this level of involvement.
