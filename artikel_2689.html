Jordan Addison is out for Vikings in Green Bay

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Kirk Cousins will be short on targets in his return to the Vikings lineup.

Wide receiver Jordan Addison is inactive for Sunday’s game in Green Bay. Addison was added to the injury report on Friday with a thumb injury and he was listed as questionable to play against the Packers.

Tight end T.J. Hockenson, who leads the team with 35 catches this season, was ruled out on Friday with wrist and chest injuries. Addison is third with 18 catches, which leaves wide receiver Justin Jefferson and running back Aaron Jones as the only active players with more that 10 catches so far this season.

Jauan Jennings was also ruled out, so Jefferson is joined by Tai Felton, Myles Price, and Jeshaun Jones at wideout. Ben Yurosek, Gavin Bartholomew, and Devin Culp are the team’s tight ends.
