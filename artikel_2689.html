Here is where the Packers fit in the NFC playoff picture after Wednesday night

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

After a pitiful second half against the Los Angeles Rams, the Green Bay Packers lost grip on a 13-13 tie with the help of a struggling offense and a defense that couldn't stop the run. Ultimately, the team fell 26-13 to Los Angeles, which has now jumped past the Packers in the wildcard race.

In the second half, Green Bay averaged just 3.1 plays per drive, compared to Los Angeles' 9 plays per drive. If you can believe it, the Packers' defense, which was seemingly on the field for the entire second half, ended up wearing down.

The Packers can't hold onto the ball, in part because they can't run the ball. Since the merger in 1970, only two teams have had worse rushing yard outputs than Green Bay through 11 games: the 2019 Miami Dolphins (which went 2-9) and the 1991 Indianapolis Colts (which went 1-10).

With the loss behind us now, let's look to the future. Currently, here's how the NFC playoff race is playing out.

12. Tampa Bay Buccaneers (3-7, 1-0 vs ATL+NO)

14. New Orleans Saints (3-7, 0-1 vs TB+ATL, 1-5 NFC)

Yes, we're two spots behind the last playoff spot. Hopefully, the Seattle Seahawks lose to the San Francisco 49ers this weekend, so we at least can have a tied record with them. The Rams being a full game ahead of the Packers and having the head-to-head tie-breaker over Green Bay is going to be a major factor in the playoff race moving forward.

According to The Athletic's playoff simulator, the Packers only have a 25 percent chance to make the playoffs right now, and their opportunity to win the division is essentially zero.
