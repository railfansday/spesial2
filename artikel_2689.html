Colts coaches weigh in on Anthony Richardson’s abrupt return to the starting lineup

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

After the surprising release of starting quarterback Daniel Jones, Indianapolis Colts quarterback Anthony Richardson Sr. now receives one last opportunity to prove he's a viable QB1 for the franchise going forward.

As long as he can stay healthy, he projects to start these dismantled 3-8 Colts' final six games down the stretch of the 2026 campaign.

The former 4th overall pick of the 2023 NFL Draft has only started 15 games since being selected by Indianapolis, and his last start for the Colts occurred on December 22, 2024, against the Tennessee Titans. The reason being because of both lengthy injury recovery timelines and a demotion in favor of Jones, until the latter's surprising recent release.

When asked about his rumored "rocky" relationship with Richardson Sr., and his thoughts on Richardson's long awaited return to the starting lineup, Steichen downplayed any lingering issues and told the local media on Monday (via The Athletic's James Boyd):

"I think we're in a really good place," Steichen said recently. "I know there is a lot of stuff out there, but me and him, we still talk regularly, and I'm excited for his opportunity. He's matured over these years. He puts in good work, and now he gets another opportunity, and I'm really looking forward to seeing him go out and compete."

That being said, Richardson Sr. may have to rely on his still elite athleticism, dual-threat mobility, and improvisation more than ever because he'll be returning to the first-team offense in less than an ideal situation.

He won't exactly be placed in a position to succeed, as the Colts have been stripped to the studs following early November's trade deadline.

Along the offensive line, the Colts traded All-Pro left guard Quenton Nelson and now have a rookie starting in his place, with both starting left tackles out because of injuries during last Thursday night's blowout loss in Houston.

Meanwhile, this isn't Hall of Famers Marvin Harrison and Reggie Wayne that he'll be throwing to, as Indianapolis' starting receiving corps more closely resembles a patchwork positional group you'd find in preseason. The group has recently struggled to separate, get open, and create any sort of big plays downfield.

Perhaps, Richardson with his ability to run, create plays outside the pocket, and willingness to throw downfield can provide a spark for this stagnant Colts offense, which mustered just 3 points last Thursday night.

If nothing else, he should be more exciting on offense, as the prior disastrous Daniel Jones led offense in 2026 had clearly run its course this season, and a change at the position was long overdue.
