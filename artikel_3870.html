Despite No Disc, GTA 6 Recorded the UK's Biggest Physical Launch Since 2019

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

GTA 6 has been breaking records all over the place, so it doesn't come as much of a surprise to learn that its performance at physical retail has been mighty impressive.

As reported by The Game Business, based on data from UK tracker Nielsen IQ, GTA 6 has recorded the country's largest physical launch since 2019's FIFA 20. Turns out only the nation's love of football can compete.

GTA 6's physical sales generated £32 million in revenue in its first three days. Apparently, it's the 17th biggest physical launch in the UK ever.

That might not sound particularly grand, but if you consider how much the physical market has shrunk over the last few years, it's actually pretty remarkable.

All this has transpired despite the game's physical copies coming with a download code rather than a disc, something that caused some anguish among collectors and enthusiasts.

I think it maybe proves that people will still show up for a physical product, even in a world where discs are on their way out.

PlayStation plans to discontinue making discs for its platform starting in 2028, but it's possible we'll still see physical launches for major games — just with a code in the box instead of a Blu-ray.
