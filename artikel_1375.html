Liverpool 4-0 Man Utd: Fans Tear Into ‘Bang Average’ Youri Tielemans

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Mauricio Pochettino quickly learned about the massive issues he has to address as Manchester United head coach following a 4-0 loss to Liverpool and he may well need to consider wielding the axe on one of this season's biggest disappointments.

It took just eight minutes for a United mishap to kick Pochettino's tenure off to a disastrous start when Matthijs de Ligt got caught in no man's land. Alexander Isak raced through on goal and Leny Yoro barged into the Swede, sending him to the turf and receiving a red card.

From the resulting free-kick, Dominik Szoboszlai gave Senne Lammens no chance with a sensational low-driven strike. The hosts doubled their advantage three minutes later when Florian Wirtz adjusted his body to find the back of the net.

Rio Ngumoha grabbed himself a deserved goal in the 66th minute before Victor Munoz netted in the 82nd to hand Pochettino the worst start possible. His side's defeat means they remain 19th in the league heading into December, with just one win and five defeats in 11 games. Grim.

After Pochettino's first press conference on Friday, United CEO Omar Berrada came out of hiding and defended the co-owners' recruitment over the summer, claiming that all four new signings had improved the squad. Those comments didn't sit well with fans because of the glaring need for a new left-back, central defender and even a new striker due to Benjamin Sesko's recurring injury.

Three central midfielders arrived and the biggest name among them, Youri Tielemans, looked to be a bargain at just £35 million. There were even early suggestions that United had pulled off this season's best signing.

Tielemans has instead been one of the poorest summer acquisitions made by a Premier League club, a completely different player to the one that spent years controlling the tempo at Aston Villa. At Anfield today, the Belgian constantly wasted possession, and struggled with the pace and movement of the opposition.

United needed to replace Casemiro with an experienced midfielder who could have a similar influence on his team but Tielemans hasn't come anywhere close to living up to such billing. And with Pochettino now fully aware of the problems he needs to address, he'll surely have a decision to make over whether the 29-year-old will continue in his starting lineup.

Tielemans' wastefulness in possession along with his regression since joining United haven't gone unnoticed. Pochettino waited until the 65th minute to bring the former Villa captain off for Mason Mount and that change perfectly sums up why Berrada's comments were tone deaf.

Fans were calling out Pochettino's players as soon as Yoro saw red and among those who frustrated them the most was Tielemans. One fan wrote on X: "I swear Tielemans loses the ball every time he gets it. Utter liability who thinks he's got time on the ball. Poor."

Another supporter took issue with his lack of physicality: "Youri Tielemans is this poor in the midfield and it's his first year on a five year contract btw. He's getting bullied off the ball by wirtz!!!!"

Peter Hall of the i Paper believes the Belgian has been struck with the United curse: "Tielemans has been properly Manchester United-ed already. Another top talent a shadow of his former self."

One fan questioned his effort: "Tielemans shouldn’t be in this team if we are been honest that lad is very very lazy and he’s stressing Mainoo."

Another added: "Tielemans. Convince me he's a good signing. Bang average wherever he's played and now he's even worse."

The games come thick and fast for Pochettino, who swapped a relaxing spell as the USA manager with quite possibly the worst period in United's modern day history. And performances like the one Tielemans put in at Anfield make clear that the longer the mess continues, relegation becomes a genuine possibility.

Former Man United defender Gary Neville hasn't held back.

How the Argentine reacts to the defeat to Liverpool could go a long way in determining if he can turn things around. Next up is a trip to Portugal to face Sporting CP in the Champions League on Wednesday, giving the beleagured Red Devils three days to bounce back.
