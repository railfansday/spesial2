Niemann Eyes DP World Tour Return As LIV Struggles

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Joaquin Niemann stands as the all-time wins leader in LIV Golf history, yet his future now points toward a return to the European circuit. The 28-year-old Chilean star, who has secured nine victories on the Saudi-backed league, appears ready to sign a new agreement for the 2027 season. This potential move comes as the LIV Golf league faces significant financial headwinds that have unsettled the competitive landscape. Reporting from Heavy.com indicates the deal is nearly finalized, marking a major shift for a player who dominated the rival tour.

The arrangement would see Niemann rejoin the DP World Tour, a circuit that has seen its share of turmoil in recent years. Heavy.com reports that official confirmation of the move is anticipated after the current season ends. Golf Channel Latin America has characterized the agreement as essentially a formality, suggesting that the negotiations have reached a point of no return. The terms give the golfer access to 15 to 20 tournaments via sponsor invitations, according to NUCLR Golf., which noted that Niemann had already agreed to these specific conditions. The DP World Tour has not yet publicly confirmed the agreement or the exact number of invitations, leaving the official announcement pending. Heavy.com reports that the league has remained silent on the specific details of the contract, though the sources suggest the deal is solid.

Niemann's nine wins on LIV Golf established him as a cornerstone of the league's early success. His dominance included multiple victories that helped define the tour's initial identity. However, the financial stability of LIV Golf has come under scrutiny recently. Heavy.com notes that the league is experiencing ongoing financial troubles, which may be the primary driver behind this potential departure. A player of Niemann's caliber commands significant value, and the ability to play 15 to 20 events on the DP World Tour offers a stable alternative. The lack of a public confirmation from the DP World Tour adds a layer of uncertainty, but the reporting from multiple outlets suggests the deal is close to completion. Golf Channel Latin America stated that the return is expected to happen once the current season wraps up, implying that the transition will be smooth if the formalities are handled correctly. A return to the DP World Tour by the all-time LIV wins leader signals a change in the balance of power between the two entities. Niemann's nine wins proved his ability to win consistently on the LIV stage, but the financial realities of the league may force a strategic pivot. The agreement, if confirmed, would allow him to compete in a mix of events that leverage his status as a top-tier player.
