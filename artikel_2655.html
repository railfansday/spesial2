Iraola says Liverpool should have scored more after thrashing 10-Man Manchester United

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Andoni Iraola believes Liverpool should have 'scored more' during the club's 4-0 win against Manchester United.

Liverpool secured a stunning win against Manchester United at Anfield, as goals from Dominik Szoboszlai, Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz sealed the points.

Leny Yoro's early red card for Manchester United changed the complexion of the game, and Liverpool took advantage to run riot.

The win was only Liverpool's second on home soil in thePremier League this season, and Iraola was delighted with the result and performance. Speaking to Sky Sports, the Spaniard said Liverpool should have scored more.

"I enjoyed it. Honestly, I'd like to do it a bit more. I wanted to celebrated in that moment and I think it's nice always to enjoy it with supporters.

"We have been very good away this season but at home we haven't been good enough and still haven't been good enough. We needed a big game, and Manchester United is always a big game, to finish like this.

"It's true the game's condition changes because they play with 10 most of the game, but still we've done our job, we controlled the game and I think it's always nice when you can win like this in these kind of games."

"Today we created a lot of clear chances, we missed against the keeper three or five times. I think we probably should've scored more than four. There is a part where we need to be more ruthless but for us today it was not needed and I think we finished the game well."

The win leaves Liverpool fourth in thePremier League table, seven points behind leaders Arsenal. Iraola said it's still early days in the campaign but stopped short of suggesting Liverpool were in the title race.

"It's still early days. I think we are missing the two big games we lost here at home between Arsenal and [Manchester City] they are making the difference in the standings. This is still so early. This doesn't stop because we have a very difficult game against Borussia Dortmund and then we have the big derby."
