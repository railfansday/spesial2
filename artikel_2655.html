Sean Payton pays tribute to late Tom Jackson with heartwarming gesture after Broncos win

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Broncos head coach Sean Payton made a heartwarming gesture toward the family of the late Denver great Tom Jackson following the Broncos' 38-24 win over the Raiders on Sunday.

Payton told reporters after the game that he had presented Jackson's family with the game ball.

Jackson died on Friday with his death being reported publicly on Sunday by ESPN's Adam Schefter.

"I would be remiss if we didn't start with just honoring Tom Jackson tonight," Payton said. "As a coach, you're always teaching and a lot of these players certainly won't remember him as a player, maybe not as an ESPN media member. But [his family] got a game ball tonight. He's part of the rich history. Tough loss for everyone."

Jackson's family notified ESPN of his passing on Friday and no cause of death was revealed.

Jackson, a fourth-round pick by the Broncos back in 1973, played 14 seasons in Denver and appeared in 191 regular-season games and 10 playoff contests.

He then went on to have an impressive 29-year run at ESPN as a staple alongside Chris Berman.

"I knew him more as an ESPN member with Chris Berman, like everyone grew up with that," Payton said. "I felt like I got to know him. Before even coming here [to Denver], and that was in his ESPN role. But when you get here and you learn more about the history of who was coordinating the defense, what position guys were playing, that kind of thing, it's interesting."

The Broncos released a statement earlier on Sunday, mourning the loss of Jackson when the news was announced.

Denver Broncos linebacker Tom Jackson looks on during a game against the Buffalo Bills at Rich Stadium in Orchard Park, N.Y., Oct. 21, 1984. Getty Images

"The Denver Broncos are heartbroken by the passing of Ring of Fame linebacker Tom Jackson, a cornerstone of the legendary Orange Crush defense and one of the most beloved players in franchise history," the organization said in the statement. "An inspirational leader and fierce competitor, TJ was a proud member of the Broncos' first Super Bowl team that helped establish a winning tradition in Denver. Off the field, Tom carried himself with class, warmth and humility, forging deep connections and lifelong friendships with teammates and throughout Broncos country.

"TJ's dynamic personality, quick wit and football knowledge made him a natural for television and paved the way for a Hall of Fame broadcasting career. For generations of fans, Tom was an iconic fixture on ESPN's Sunday NFL Countdown and NFL Primetime, sharing his thoughtful insight with an unmistakable sense of humor that defined an era of sports broadcasting.

"Above all, TJ loved the Broncos and was a cherished member of our family for more than 50 years. We extend our deepest sympathies to Tom's wife, Jennifer, his daughters, Taylor and Morgan, and all of his family, friends and teammates."
