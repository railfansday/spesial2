Khamzat Chimaev to defend RAF title against UFC legend Rashad Evans

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

KhamzatChimaev will defend his Real American Freestyle lightheavyweight crossover title against former Ultimate Fighting Championship light heavyweight championRashadEvans at RAF 16 on Dec. 19.RAF announced the matchup Tuesday. The event will take place atNassau Veterans Memorial Coliseum in Uniondale, New York.

Chimaev most recently defeated former UFC middleweight championChrisWeidman by technical fall at RAF Abu Dhabi on Nov. 14. He hasalso recorded RAF wins over DillonDanis, TyronWoodley, GilbertBurns and RobbieLawler.Evans, 47, is a UFC Hall of Famer and former Michigan Statewrestler. He last competed in MMA in January 2022, defeatingGabrielChecco by unanimous decision at Eagle FC 44.Evans will make his RAF debut, while Chimaev looks to remainunbeaten in the promotion.
