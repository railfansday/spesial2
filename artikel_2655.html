Steelers credit T.J. Watt, Cam Heyward for leadership amidst adverse week

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Pittsburgh Steelers responded to a very adverse week by dominating the Denver Broncos 27-5 to get to 7-4 and remain unbeaten at home in 2026.

It was a trying week for the Steelers, who released quarterback Aaron Rodgers after he demanded to take over play-calling duties. Rodgers then went on The Pat McAfee Show and put head coach Mike McCarthy on blast for falling asleepp in meetings, missing walk throughs, and not getting to know defensive players, among other things.

In trying times like this, teams lean on their leaders, and several players credited T.J. Watt and Cam Heyward for the work they did with the team this week.

"I thought TJ and Cam did a really good job before the game," Pat Freiermuth said via Brooke Pryor. "Really talking about it's bigger than one person, it's bigger than two people… It's about the Rooney family and the Steelers and the fans, and they deserve our best effort today."

Safety Jaquan Brisker echoed those sentiments.

"We had a very important leadership council meeting this week, and we all were in there and talked about keeping this team together and turning the page," Brisker said via Nick Farabaugh.

Quarterback Mason Rudolph, who threw a touchdown pass in the win, pointed out how lucky the Steelers are to have superstars with high leadership abilities.

"Your average team doesn't have a Cam Heyward or a T.J. Watt or a Jalen Ramsey," Rudolph said. "Those guys did an awesome job at just pointing the trajectory of where the team needed to go.

Pittsburgh took a bunch of cannonball shots this week, but it's clear their foundation is strong due to the leaders they have inside their locker room. They will now get ready for a Week 13 matchup with the Houston Texans next week on Sunday Night Football.
