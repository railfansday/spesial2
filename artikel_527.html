T-Mobile Kicks Off Holiday Deals With Up to $1,200 Off Apple’s New iPhone Duo and iPhone 18 Pro

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Variety may receive an affiliate commission.

T-Mobile is getting an early start on the holiday shopping season, rolling out a slate of new deals on some of the year’s biggest smartphone releases, including Apple‘s new iPhone 18 Pro and its first-ever foldable phone, the iPhone Duo.

The wireless carrier is offering up to $1,200 off both devices with qualifying trade-ins on select plans, along with the option for eligible customers to walk away with a new phone for $0 upfront. The offers arrive just weeks after Apple introduced its latest iPhone lineup in September, with the iPhone Duo representing the company’s most significant design departure in years.

Released Oct. 23, the iPhone Duo marks Apple’s long-awaited entry into the foldable smartphone market, joining competitors including Samsung and Google. The device features a 5.4-inch exterior screen that unfolds into a 7.6-inch display — the largest ever on an iPhone — designed for watching videos, gaming and multitasking across apps. The foldable design also makes it the thinnest iPhone Apple has released when opened. It’s currently available in Star White and Night Sky

Meanwhile, the iPhone 18 Pro, which arrived Sept. 18, introduces Apple’s first variable-aperture camera, allowing users to adjust depth of field and lighting directly in the Camera app. The device also features a new 48-megapixel Fusion Main camera, Apple’s A20 Pro chip, an upgraded cooling system and enhanced AI capabilities through iOS 27. Available in Black, Silver, Glacier and Burgundy, the Pro starts at $1,199.

Beyond Apple’s newest releases, T-Mobile’s early holiday promotions include $830 off the iPhone 17, offers on the iPhone 16 and 17e that can cover the full device cost, and discounts on Android devices including the Samsung Galaxy S26 series, Google Pixel 11 lineup and Motorola Razr. You can also get four lines right now for just $25/line and get four iPhone 17e devices “on us” when you switch.
