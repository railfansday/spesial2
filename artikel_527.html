The price of the black colorway is fluctuating right now, but if you are flexible on the color you get, you may be able to snag a better deal. I've seen various colors go for between $199 and $219.

In my review of these headphones, one of my major gripes was price, as they float in a strange middle ground between midrange and premium. That concern flies out the window with this discount.

These are the best, most comfortable over-ear headphones for noise cancellation.
