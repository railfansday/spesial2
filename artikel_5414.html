Covius rolls out capital markets dashboard for bulk title reviews

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Covius expanded high-volume batch title processing and launched Loanscape CM for capital markets. The dashboard pairs bulk title searches with rule validation across 100-plus attributes to flag loans that may need additional review.  AI Summary

Covius announced on Thursday that it has expanded its high-volume title capabilities and launched a new dashboard designed to help capital markets clients review property information and identify potential risks in large loan portfolios.

The company said its Covius Settlement Services (CSS) business unit has enhanced its batch title processing capabilities and introduced Loanscape CM (Capital Markets), a dashboard that provides greater visibility into title-related risks and helps streamline portfolio reviews.

Capital markets investors and due-diligence providers rely on large volumes of property reports for portfolio acquisitions, risk monitoring and loan reviews. Following its integration of Title365 operations earlier this year, Covius said it has increased its capacity to process and analyze property information while reducing turnaround times.

The new dashboard combines high-volume title searches with automated business rule validation to identify loans that may require additional review.

The company’s automated workflow evaluates more than 100 attributes in a standard title report to identify potential risks or conditions that warrant further investigation, said Joe Chappell, president of Covius Settlement Services.

“On bulk orders of any size, our new dashboard is able to isolate the loans that require further internal review prior to delivery, as well as identify the risk elements necessary for our clients’ review, creating significant efficiencies for large scale transactions,” he added.

Loanscape CM is the latest addition to the company’s Loanscape Decisioning Framework, a suite of title decisioning tools. The suite also includes Loanscape HE (Home Equity), which provides underwriter-approved title clearance for home equity originations, and Loanscape LM (Loss Mitigation), which identifies title-related information and potential loss-mitigation options in a single view.

The Loanscape framework integrates with major point-of-sale, origination, servicing and default systems, as well as multiple order management platforms, to automate processes and streamline operations.

Covius acquired Title365’s operations earlier this year and integrated them with its existing title business. The combined operations now operate under the Covius Settlement Services name.
