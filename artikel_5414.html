Circuit Appoints Transportation Leader Gabe Klein to Board of Directors

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Former federal transportation and energy official and leader of Chicago and Washington, D.C. transportation departments brings decades of experience advancing sustainable transportation, public-private partnerships, and urban innovation.

NEW YORK, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Circuit, a leading provider of all-electric, tech-enabled microtransit services, today announced the appointment of Gabe Klein to its Board of Directors. A nationally recognized transportation leader, Klein brings decades of experience across the public and private sectors, including leadership roles in federal transportation and energy policy, municipal transportation systems, and emerging transportation technology companies.

Klein most recently served as the founding Executive Director of the U.S. Joint Office of Energy and Transportation, where he helped lead the federal government's efforts to accelerate electric vehicle charging infrastructure and support the transition to zero-emission transportation. Previously, he served as Commissioner of the Chicago Department of Transportation and Director of the District Department of Transportation in Washington, D.C., where he championed innovative transportation programs, infrastructure improvements, and public-private partnerships.

"Gabe has spent his career helping cities rethink how transportation can better serve their communities," said Alex Esposito, Co-Founder and CEO of Circuit. "He understands the challenges municipalities face and what it takes to turn innovative ideas into impactful, scalable solutions. His experience navigating both the public and private sectors will be incredibly valuable as we continue expanding Circuit's partnerships and helping more cities deliver accessible, efficient, and sustainable transportation."

Klein's appointment comes as Circuit continues to expand its footprint nationwide, partnering with municipalities, transit agencies, and private organizations to address first- and last-mile gaps, improve access to local destinations, and reduce dependence on personal vehicles. Since its founding in 2011, Circuit has delivered more than 12 million passenger trips through its all-electric transportation services.

Beyond his government leadership, Klein brings extensive entrepreneurial and investment experience. He previously served as Vice President at Zipcar, co-founded the urban innovation advisory firm Cityfi, and is a Venture Partner at Fontinalis Partners, an investment firm focused on emerging technologies and industries, including transportation.

"We've spent decades designing transportation around cars, and then wondering why so many people are left without good options. That has to change," said Klein. "What drew me to Circuit is that they're not just talking about solutions. They're putting shared, electric transportation on the streets and adapting it to what communities need. That combination of urgency, flexibility, and execution is exactly what we need more of, and it's why I'm joining the board."

As a member of Circuit's Board of Directors, Klein will help guide the company's long-term strategy, drawing on his experience in transportation policy, government partnerships, electrification, and emerging technologies. His appointment strengthens Circuit's ability to work alongside cities and transit agencies seeking flexible, cost-effective transportation solutions that complement existing public transit networks.

About Gabe KleinGabe Klein is an entrepreneur, investor, author, and former government transportation executive whose career has focused on creating more sustainable, connected, and people-centered cities. He served as the founding Executive Director of the U.S. Joint Office of Energy and Transportation from 2022 to 2025 and previously led the transportation departments of Chicago and Washington, D.C., where he helped launch the Divvy and Capital Bikeshare programs. Klein is a founding partner of Cityfi, a Venture Partner at Fontinalis Partners, and the author of Start-Up City: Inspiring Private and Public Entrepreneurship, Getting Projects Done, and Having Fun.

About CircuitFounded in 2011, Circuit provides all-electric, tech-enabled microtransit solutions that help cities, transit agencies, and private partners connect their communities. Through its turnkey approach combining technology, vehicles, and daily operations, Circuit delivers affordable and accessible transportation that fills first- and last-mile gaps, supports local economies, and reduces congestion. With more than 12 million rides delivered across 40+ markets, Circuit is helping communities build smarter, more sustainable transportation networks. Learn more at www.ridecircuit.com.
