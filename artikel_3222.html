Adeia Inc. (ADEA -3.59%) reported third quarter results and revised its full-year financial outlook to reflect the shifting execution timelines of several significant license agreements. Management appointed Dipti Vachani as the new chief executive officer and emphasized the strategic importance of AI-driven media discovery and semiconductor infrastructure. The company highlighted multiyear renewals with key partners in the pay TV and consumer electronics sectors while expanding its footprint in e-commerce and social media markets. Management reported that while certain deal closings have been delayed, the long-term annual revenue objective remains unchanged as the industry transitions toward advanced technologies like hybrid bonding and thermal management.
