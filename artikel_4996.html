7 Best Glassware Sets to Elevate Your Tablescape (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

You don’t need a perfectly mixed cocktail or an expensive bottle of wine to justify drinking out of the good glasses. Sometimes, a cold glass of water is occasion enough. And if you’re going to reach for the same one several times a day, it might as well be one you enjoy looking at. Picture it now: curved and patterned drinking glasses that can make an everyday meal feel a bit more festive.

Choosing the right glassware is a surprisingly personal affair, though. Some people swear by a barely-there rim; others prefer the reassuring heft of a substantial tumbler. Then there’s the way a glass sits in your hand. These details might seem inconsequential until you find a glass you love drinking from—and suddenly every other one in your arsenal becomes a little less appealing. To make your sips more style-forward, we’ve rounded up a variety of designer water glasses, special-occasion stems, and other favorites you'll want to clear a little cabinet space for.

The generously sized bowls of these LSA International wine glasses leave plenty of room for a proper pour (and an enthusiastic swirl without worrying about spilling over the rim). They’re also made from dishwasher-safe crystalline glass, so you can skip the handwashing once the party wraps up.

If you want to introduce color to your glassware collection but can’t settle on just one shade, this set makes it easy to have a little bit of everything. Estelle Colored Glass’s six Champagne coupes come in an assortment of vibrant hues, so no one has to lose track of their drink at your next dinner party.

Perfectly imperfect is part of the vintage charm of these handblown glasses from il Buco Vita. Made from recycled glass without the use of molds, each one has subtle irregularities in shape that make it entirely its own. Even the color is left to chance, with undertones that shift between green, blue, and gray.

These featherlight Glasvin wine glasses live up to their name. Their tulip-shaped bowls help aerate the wine and bring out its aromas, and reviewers praise their surprisingly sturdy construction despite their delicate appearance. They’re dishwasher-safe, too, making them an easy addition to your regular rotation.

The Elisa tumblers prove you can bring in pattern without competing with the rest of the tablescape. The delicate, almost translucent dots and stripes add just enough flourish to dress up any occasion, whether you’re hosting brunch or just setting the table for a weeknight meal.

Swap your everyday glasses for something that feels like a little work of art. Inspired by the organic shapes of wildflowers, Table Mates alum Sophie Lou Jacobsen gives the ubiquitous water glass a romantic flair with her Verre design, with gently flared, fanning rims resembling a flower in bloom. Made from durable borosilicate glass, they’re stackable and dishwasher-safe, so they’re just as practical as they are whimsical.

The martini glass has an unmistakable silhouette, but Noon and Moon gives it a playful update. The sculptural, spiral stem adds an unexpected detail to the otherwise simple design, making it a statement piece on your bar cart.
