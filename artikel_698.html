Ron Howard Says a Jim Carrey ‘Grinch’ Sequel Won’t Get Made Unless ‘It Has a Chance to Be Great’: We’re Moving ‘Cautiously’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Months after a “How the Grinch Stole Christmas” sequel was announced, director Ron Howard is setting the record straight on what it would take for the follow-up film to actually come to fruition.

“I just have to caution everybody that Jim [Carrey] and I aren’t gonna go forward unless we think we have a great script, and we don’t even have a script right now,” Howard told People. “We have really good writers and an idea that I’m excited about that I think is very much in keeping with the spirit of Dr. Seuss, his blend of comedy and fantasy. So I’m very pleased with what we have to work with, but I don’t wanna make any promises that it’s happening because Jim and I are aligned on one thing — we’re not gonna go forward unless we believe it has a chance to be great.” Related Stories 'Russell Brand: Open Secret' Director Says Finding Contributors Wasn't Easy: 'There's Been a Landscape of Fear' Russell Brand's Wife Calls Him an 'Amazing Husband' at Rape Trial: 'I Feel Very Seen by Him'

Howard continued, noting that the duo feels so “proud of the original movie.” “We feel so fortunate, because it takes a little bit of luck for a movie to come together and become an evergreen the way that film has. And the following, the fact that it’s become a tradition in a lot of households is something that means a great deal to all of us involved, Jim and I in particular.”

But Howard isn’t ruling out a sequel in its eternity. “We’re gonna move cautiously but hopefully toward another movie because we both believe in the premise,” he said. “With a little luck, we’ll get to revisit that world.”

The original Christmas classic first premiered back in 2000 and was a box office hit, earning more than $350 million. Carrey, who starred as the Grinch and would reprise his role for the sequel, has long been outspoken about the toll the specific effects makeup needed to transform into the green creature took on him.

Last year, Howard told Vulture that the actor “started having panic attacks” during the early days of filming because of how uncomfortable the transformation was on his body, adding: “I would see him lying down on the floor in between setups with a brown paper bag. Literally on the floor. He was miserable.” Howard also revealed that Carrey was “ready to give his” $20 million salary back.
