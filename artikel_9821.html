Canonical's CVE Response Times

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

CVE-2026-98432 was first published on October 15th, thanks to Linux's cautious CVE policy. On October 24th, after a blog post by Anthropic, it had a CVSS score of 9.8 assigned.

Since this was a critical vulnerability, patches and advisories followed quickly:

At time of writing (3rd November), Canonical does not have a fix in their main repositories. This morning, they released a fix to the proposed pocket, for some of their distributions, for "users who run their own kernel acceptance tests".

Their blog post also takes the time to remind the reader that if you paid for Ubuntu Pro, you can get security updates for their older LTS branches for longer. This is a strange thing to point out when all of the versions that you can pay them for don't actually have this security patch yet (not even in a proposed pocket).

For now, I guess RedHat's nftables mitigation will have to do. For the future, the reasons to choose Ubuntu over Debian are only growing weaker (at least on the server side). At least, if you're not paying for pro.
