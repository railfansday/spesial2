Jhené Aiko Is Bringing ‘Whimsy’ on the Road With H.E.R., Swae Lee & Karri: Here Are the Tour Dates

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Ark to Agartha - A Westside Whimsy Experience kicks off in March

Jhené Aiko is heading out on a 37-city North American tour to support her first No. 1 album, Westside Whimsy.

Jhené Aiko Presents: Ark to Agartha – A Westside Whimsy Experience begins on Wednesday, March 10, in St. Louis at the Enterprise Center, and will end in her hometown of Los Angeles on Friday, May 21, at Crypto.com Arena. She’ll be supported by Swae Lee and Kari for every single tour date, while H.E.R. will be a special guest starting on Friday, March 26, at Barclays Center in Brooklyn, N.Y., until the final tour stop in L.A. Aiko’s last tour, 2024’s Magic Hour Tour, during which she performed songs from her album Chilombo, which debuted at No. 2 on the Billboard 200. She was supported by Coi Leray, Tink, Umi and Kiana Ledé on that trek.

Westside Whimsy debuted at No. 1 after arriving in September, and features fellow California natives Ab-Soul, Tyga, Larry June, Kendrick Lamar and OHMA

Tickets for Ark to Agartha – A Westside Whimsy Experience will be available starting Wed., Nov. 18, at 2 p.m. local time with artist presale; sign up for access via her website.

General onsale starts Friday, Nov. 20, at 10 a.m. local time via Live Nation. Verizon customers who are part of the carrier’s loyalty program will have access to an exclusive presale period for select shows on Wednesday, Nov. 18, at 10 a.m. local time to Thursday, Nov. 19, at 10 p.m. local time.

There, of course, will also be VIP packages available ranging from premium tickets, exclusive access to the Westside Whimsy Soundbath Experience with Jhené Aiko, invitation to the pre-show VIP Lounge, specially designed VIP merchandise item and more.
