Whoopi Goldberg Details Bond With "Irreplaceable" Patrick Stewart

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Whoopi Goldberg honored Sir Patrick Stewart after sharing decades of friendship with her Star Trek: The Next Generation costar before his Oct. 28 death at 86 years old.

Whoopi Goldberg's friendship with Patrick Stewart meant the world—and beyond—to her.

In remembering the Star Trek: The Next Generation alum after his Oct. 28 death following a brief battle with Alzheimer's disease and Lewy body dementia, Goldberg expressed how precious her decades-long friendship with the late 86-year-old was.

"We lost a wonderful actor and a great human being this weekend. My friend, Sir Patrick Stewart, he passed away." Goldberg—who played Guinan to his Captain Jean-Luc Picard—remarked on The View Nov. 2. "He is, in a word, irreplaceable, really."

And their bond—which began in the '80s when they costarred in the sci-fi series—was one that withstood the test of time. As the 70-year-old explained, "We went way back, and I adored him."

In addition to their personal relationship, the Ghost actress recognized Stewart's incomparable legacy in the public eye as photos from his iconic career flashed on screen, including a shot of his character Professor Charles Xavier in X-Men.

"As a performer, he could do everything," Goldberg continued. "He could do drama, science fiction, comedy, and of course Shakespeare."

As such, she treasured getting to work alongside the famed thespian again in 2022 after Stewart invited her to reprise her role in Star Trek: Picard during a visit to the morning show. Goldberg said the "amazing" memory makes her "just giggle all over the place."

"Star Trek remains one of the great events in my life," she explained of her time playing his onscreen confidante. "I'll miss him dearly, but he's up with a lot of great people, so I know he's running around having fun."

The comedian first celebrated her late friend's life by sharing a photo of their Star Trek characters as action figures, captioned, "See you on the next holodeck @sirpatstew RIP."

And Goldberg is in good company when it comes to appreciating Stewart's one-of-a-kind humor and kindness. Boldly go and read more moving remembrances…

"We first met as ambitious young actors with the Royal Shakespeare Company half a century ago but we weren’t in the same productions. My first strong memory was a few years later, meeting him in the street in central London when he told me about an offer to take over a leading part in Star Trek. 'But Patrick think of your stage career in the UK. Do you really want to risk—getting lost in Hollywood for years and years?' Well, he took the risk and thank goodness for us all that he did for not just his devoted Trekkies but film and theatre audiences, who marvelled at his using international celebrity to star in a string of cinema successes (X-Men of course) and in great classical plays by Shakespeare, Ibsen, Arthur Miller, Peter Shaffer, Pinter and Beckett at Stratford-upon-Avon, in London’s West End and on Broadway."

"We lost one of the greats of stage and screen today. And, in my book, a tremendous friend and man. I learned so much from him. I will miss you, Patrick Stewart, all of my days."

"Patrick was one of those icons who we somehow all took for granted would always be with us. I grew up as a fan, and was later gifted the privilege of becoming a colleague and friend, working with him for many years on American Dad!, Family Guy, and Ted. He was that rare actor who could take any bit of dialogue and elevate it, making a good writer sound great, and a great writer sound masterful. He was also as humble as they come. I remember exchanging emails with him a number of years ago when he was beginning day 1 of a new film. He commented to me that he always walked onto a new set feeling like the biggest novice there. My stunned reaction was the same as anyone’s would be. I said, 'Patrick, if you feel that way, the rest of us are screwed.' We have lost a legendary actor today, and for many of us, a good friend. He will be deeply missed by the entire planet."

"What a heartbreaking week it has been. Sir Patrick Stewart was a friend, a colleague, and a magnificent actor. From the stage to the bridge of the Enterprise, he brought intelligence, grace, and a deep sense of humanity to everything he did. Captain Picard stood for the best of us, and so did the man who played him. I was filled with affection and admiration for him and his work."

"I feel so grateful to have known him for the last 25 years and to have been in his orbit for the last 25 years, both as a fellow 'mutant' and as a fellow member of Starfleet. He was positively delicious, hilarious, naughty, incredible sense of humor, and that booming voice of his that always spoke to the very back of the room—as every classically trained Shakespearean great needs to master—has always been an Inspiration. I'm gonna send my condolences to his family, and I'll miss my friend dearly. Rest in peace, Patrick."

"My friend, we shared some memorable times…from the deserts of Arrakis to the mysterious jungles of Thailand and onto the New York stage. I already miss you. You will always be my Gurney Halleck and I, your young pup."

"Patrick Stewart is a legend. His acting talent was not only innate but built from his professionalism, his dedication to craft, his enthusiasm, his generosity, his gentlemanly way of being. He was mischievous and game, always made me laugh and always made a day better. He leaves a treasure trove of work behind and I’m so grateful he came to play w me on Charlie’s Angels. Rest peacefully Patrick. Sending love."

"Patrick Stewart was one of the greats, not just because of his extraordinary talent, but because of the man he was. It was a privilege to work alongside him and an even greater pleasure to know him personally. He was loved by all and will be deeply missed. My heart goes out to his family and loved ones."

I am, and always will be, grateful for this amazing experience and adventure we shared. I love you and will miss you, my dear sweet friend. The world lost a great one today... Rest now, Captain, my captain."

"Ouch. This one hurts. Rest well, Patrick."

"He was such a kind, genuine, funny, unique, caring and charming man. I remember his endless jokes, nurturing nature and colourful reading glasses. Every single day spent working by his side was an honour and a privilege I can’t quite believe I had. He was a walking master of his craft, and of life. One of the greatest of our time, on stage and on screen. I have always looked up to him, and always will. As an actor and as a man. He had a wonderful way of wrapping every ancient lesson he imparted in wonder, fun and excitement. I will always remember his warmth and the respect he showed to everyone around him. I will forever hold those months we spent together close to my heart, and will always think of him as a pivotal part of how I view the world around me. So rest in peace, you giant, you legend amongst men. The beautiful work, humanity and kindness you leave behind will live on forever."

"Fans across the world mourn the passing of a true and irreplaceable legend. As Captain Jean-Luc Picard, Patrick Stewart took the starship Enterprise to places it had never gone before, and he inspired a whole next generation of star-gazers who looked longingly ahead to a brighter future for all humanity. Our hearts are heavy, indeed. Rest in the heavens, Captain."
