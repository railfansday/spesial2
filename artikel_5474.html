LuminoCity's 2026 Holiday Lights Festival Now Open at Eisenhower Park

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Returning for its fifth year, the festival welcomes families with four immersive worlds, a new holiday train ride, and festive theme nights through January 3, 2027

EAST MEADOW, N.Y., Nov. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- LuminoCity Holiday Lights Festival officially opened to the public at Eisenhower Park on November 11, following an opening ceremony on November 10 that brought together young artists, community partners, and local officials. Returning for its fifth year, the 14-acre festival runs on select evenings through January 3, 2027, with four immersive worlds, the new Lumi Express holiday train ride, and December theme nights.

"We're grateful to celebrate another season with the Nassau County community here at Eisenhower Park. We're proud to bring joy to Long Island families and continue a holiday tradition they can look forward to year after year," said Xiaoyi Chen, founder and CEO of LuminoCity.

Nassau County Executive Bruce Blakeman presented Chen with a county citation recognizing LuminoCity's return to Eisenhower Park. "I'd like to present a citation on behalf of the County of Nassau, the 1.5 million residents, in appreciation for picking Nassau County for this beautiful show," said Blakeman.

Other speakers included Nassau County Comptroller Elaine Phillips, Nassau County Legislature Deputy Presiding Officer Thomas McKevitt, North Hempstead Town Supervisor Jennifer DeSena, and Long Island Children's Museum President Erika Floreska.

"You don't need to leave Nassau County for a fabulous holiday night, so come bring your families and make memories here," said DeSena.

The ceremony also celebrated the young winners of Lumi's Utopia 2026, LuminoCity's children's art initiative with the Long Island Children's Museum. Their "Dino Friends" designs were transformed into handcrafted light sculptures on display at the festival.

"Everyone who comes to LuminoCity, make sure you stop and see the children's arts. Activating 2D art into 3D is fabulous. We love this tradition. We're so grateful to Nassau County, and we hope everyone comes to visit," said Erika Floreska, president of the Long Island Children's Museum.

Four Immersive Worlds and New Holiday Experiences

Guests can journey through Magical Wonderwood, Dino Safari, Holidayville, and Tundra Twilight, featuring more than 100 illuminated and animatronic creatures, larger-than-life installations, and a 40-foot Christmas tree. Themed photo opportunities invite families and friends to step into holiday scenes together.

For the first time, LuminoCity's festival at Eisenhower Park features a holiday train ride: the Lumi Express, which takes visitors through the illuminated landscape. Families can bounce inside Happy Holidays Hop, a 20-foot-tall Santa inflatable, dance on the LED dance floor, and enjoy illuminated seesaws. Santa pop-up appearances on select weekends and limited-edition holiday merchandise add to the festivities.

December theme nights include Kisses Under the Mistletoe on December 5, Wizards & Witches Night on December 12, Winter Story Night with Westbury Memorial Public Library on December 18, and Jingle Bell Jazz on December 19.

LuminoCity Holiday Lights Festival is located at Eisenhower Park, 1899 Hempstead Turnpike, East Meadow, NY 11554. The festival opens at 4:30 p.m. on operating days, with free onsite parking.
