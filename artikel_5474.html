New Elsevier report suggests 63% of R&D organizations have made important decisions with flawed data

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Global Corporate R&D of the Future report reveals a paradox at the heart of R&D decisions: confidence in data is high, but gaps in quality and connection persist

NEW YORK, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- R&D teams are confident in their evidence, yet report making decisions based on flawed or incomplete data, according to Elsevier's Corporate R&D of the Future report, a global survey of more than 1,000 R&D leaders, managers and researchers at R&D-led corporations.

More than three quarters (76%) of respondents are confident in the quality of their data. Yet 63% of those who answered say their organization has at some point made an important R&D decision using data or evidence later found to be inaccurate, incomplete or outdated. About half (51%) say their organization has had to make an important R&D decision before all the evidence was in.

The survey findings show AI is moving into R&D decision-making, but expectations are running ahead of use: 81% expect AI to fundamentally transform how R&D decisions are made over the next five years, yet under half (47%) say it is used regularly in their organization's R&D function today.

Stuart Whayman, President, Corporate Markets at Elsevier, commented, "Confidence is not the same as accuracy. R&D teams are under pressure to decide faster, and often before all the evidence is in. Confidence keeps them moving, but it can't make up for evidence that is incomplete, outdated or unreliable. AI will raise the stakes, because it will amplify whatever evidence it is given, good or bad. The advantage will go to the organizations that can bring their evidence together and trace and verify what sits behind their decisions."

The report looks at how R&D teams use data and evidence to make decisions, where that evidence comes from, how well it is connected, and how AI is being applied and trusted.

The full Corporate R&D of the Future report, which also compares views by region, organization size and role and sets out recommendations for R&D organizations, is available at www.elsevier.com/insights/corporate-r-and-d-of-the-future.

Corporate R&D of the Future is based on an online survey, conducted by email invitation between August and October 2026, of 1,038 R&D leaders, managers and researchers at R&D-led corporations in 55 countries across North America, South America, Europe, the Middle East and Africa, and Asia Pacific. Responses are weighted. Figures may not sum up because of rounding. Full methodology is in the report appendix. Figures on flawed decisions exclude respondents who answered don't know or preferred not to say.
