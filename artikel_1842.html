Prediction: PSG vs Roma

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Turning their attention back to Champions League action on Wednesday night, reigning European kings PSG will welcome Roma to Paris.

With the European action rolling onto Wednesday, there are plenty of eye-catching Champions League betting promotions available.

With all eyes falling on the French capital, you can find all of the key PSG vs Roma H2H stats here.

Verdict: Fabian Ruiz over 0.5 shots on target

Starting with the hosts, while PSG might have endured a stuttering start to proceedings when opening up the 2026/27 campaign, Luis Enrique’s record-breaking side have come roaring back in a dominant fashion. Making real history back in May as they successfully defended their European crown, the former Barcelona boss will be calling on his superstar squad to lay down another Champions League marker in midweek. Last seen over the weekend marking their return from November’s international break with a rollercoaster 3-2 victory away at Nice, PSG have now won all of their previous 12 consecutive appearances across all competitions. Getting November underway with a sensational 6-0 European romp away at Spanish outfit Villarreal, Enrique’s men have also gained a fearsome reputation for their free-scoring performances in the final third.

Stretchered off in the first-half away at Nice on Saturday with what looks set to be a long-term knee injury, PSG will be without attacking starlet Desire Doue here. Likewise, sustaining his own respective serious knee injury against Barcelona last month, Georgian skipper Khvicha Kvaratskhelia faces a lengthy stint on the sidelines.

Making a timely return over the weekend, former Ballon d’Or winner Ousmane Dembele could make a first start since PSG’s 4-2 victory away at Manchester City on October 14th. Bagging a fifth Ligue 1 strike of the season on Saturday, big-money summer recruit Ferran Torres should be handed a chance to lead the line in Paris.

As for the visitors, while Roma might have suffered an infamous Champions League collapse against Manchester United earlier in the month, it appears that was just a minor blip for Gian Piero Gasperini’s camp. Marking their respective return from November’s fortnight interval with a dominant 5-1 victory against Parma, the Wolves have rightfully received a string of public praise from across the continent. Remarkably sat in silver medal position among Italy’s elite, Roma have won four of their previous five appearances across all competitions. Signing off October with a thrilling 5-2 win away at Udinese, the former Atalanta boss has also seen his high-flying squad continue to shine with their clinical displays in the final third. Along with already notching 36 goals in their first 12 Serie A showdowns, Gasperini’s men have bagged at least a pair of goals in all of their previous nine straight appearances across all competitions.

Yet to feature over the past month and still sidelined with a thigh injury, Roma will travel to Paris on Wednesday night without Italian international Lorenzo Pellegrini. Left as an unused substitute a few days ago, former Atalanta midfielder Marten de Roon could be handed a full recall in the middle of the park.

Astonishingly notching a quartet of Serie A strikes against Parma on Saturday and only further shattering a string of records since making his move to Rome a permanent one over the summer, the red-hot Donyell Malen will spearhead Gasperini’s attack. Making a first appearance since October 14th, Argentinian superstar Paulo Dybala will be hoping to keep his spot from the start.

Two camps who have continued to shine with their free-scoring performances in the final third, neutrals will be hoping for another Champions League thriller on Wednesday night. While Roma might travel to the reigning European kings more than aware that they face a monumental challenge, Gasperini’s side have gained a reputation for their lively outcomes. In fact, the Italian heavyweights have seen their last four matchups away from the Stadio Olimpico produce a staggering 24 goals at both ends of the pitch.
