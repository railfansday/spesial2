All Devil Fruits in Fortnite and how to get them

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Treasure Hunters mini-season in Fortnite brings the One Piece collaboration and popular characters to the game. That’s not all, however, since we also have the infamous Devil Fruits to find. There are two of them, and you can find them in every round.

Let’s find out how you can collect these items and how they work in the game.

There are two Devil Fruits to find on your run: Gum-Gum Fruit and Flame-Flame Fruit.

Thankfully, Devil Fruits are easy to find since their locations will be displayed on the HUD. If you observe the image above, you can always find a spawn spot where both types can be found together.

Every round, the fruits will spawn at multiple locations, but they are first-come, first-serve. Once you find a Devil Fruit, make sure to add it to your inventory to complete the transformation and activate its effects.
