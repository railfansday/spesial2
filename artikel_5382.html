ByteDance is planning an AI agent similar to Meta's Muse within weeks

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Co-founder Zhang Yiming told AI staff that competing with Anthropic and OpenAI at the cutting edge will take at least until 2029 or 2030

ByteDance, the Chinese parent of TikTok, is preparing to launch an AI agent comparable to Meta $META Platforms' Muse within the next few weeks, according to Bloomberg. The company hopes the launch will build on its advantage in AI video and help close the gap with leading American labs in as little as three years, Bloomberg said.

Co-founder Zhang Yiming described the plan to the company's AI team at an all-hands meeting, according to Bloomberg, which cited unnamed people familiar with the matter. Zhang said ByteDance is capable of challenging Anthropic and OpenAI at the frontier, but he warned that getting there would mean waiting until 2029 or 2030 at the earliest.

Meta introduced Muse in September. It talks with users by voice, appears on screen as an animated character, and assists them in completing a range of chores, while Meta is rolling it out on its smart glasses and on a forthcoming Muse Charm device, according to Bloomberg. Bloomberg said it remains unknown if ByteDance intends to pair its agent with a physical device.

Zhang, 43, named data as a top concern. He said the training datasets behind ByteDance's Seed large language models are orders of magnitude smaller than what frontier rivals use, according to Bloomberg.

Zhang again argued that ByteDance should steer clear of distillation, which means teaching a compact model with what a bigger one produces, and keep its attention on long-range goals, according to Bloomberg. White House officials and Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei have faulted Chinese rivals including Alibaba, Moonshot AI and Z.ai for building on top American models through distillation.

Seed stands alone among China's top AI players in having neither produced a language model that ranks among the world's best on benchmarks nor made its weights accessible to outside developers, according to Bloomberg. The Seed 2.1 update in June was ByteDance's last major language model release.

Video is the area where ByteDance holds a meaningful lead, according to Bloomberg. Its Seedance models stepped in after OpenAI discontinued Sora, and the company intends to commit up to $70 billion to turn Seedance into the leading video AI platform in China.

ByteDance secured a $29.6 billion loan in September from close to three dozen banks, with proceeds earmarked for AI initiatives outside China, including data centers under development in Southeast Asia. The three-year facility had no collateral.

The Seed team employs around 1,000 people in research and engineering roles and is headed by Wu Yonghui, whom Zhang recruited away from Google $GOOGL DeepMind in 2025, according to Bloomberg. In October, Seed became the first of China's big AI labs to launch its own separate unit for safety and alignment, Bloomberg said.

A ByteDance spokesperson did not respond to Bloomberg's request for comment.
