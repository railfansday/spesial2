UConn men's basketball forward Nik Khamenia out for remainder of game vs Wagner

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

STORRS — UConn starting small forward Nik Khamenia has been ruled out for the rest of Monday's game against Wagner, UConn told the TruTV broadcast.

Khamenia appeared to injure his foot early in the first half, limping to the locker room around the 10-minute mark. Before his injury, Khamenia had his best start of the season, scoring 11 points on 4-of-5 shooting from the field and 3-of-3 shooting from beyond the arc.

The Duke transfer has been vital to UConn on both ends of the floor as a perimeter defender and as a scorer.

UConn coach Dan Hurley has used a mixture of Colben Landrew and Jayden Ross to replace Khamenia's minutes.

Khamenia is averaging 10.5 points and four rebounds through two games this season.
