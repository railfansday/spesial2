Mauricio Pochettino reacts to 'painful' Manchester United defeat and questions red card decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Mauricio Pochettino has called Manchester United's 4-0 defeat at Liverpool 'so painful' but questioned the decision which changed the game.

Pochettino's first fixture in charge ended in defeat at Anfield, as 10-man Manchester United were beaten badly.

The red card incident arrived just eight minutes into the contest as Leny Yoro was sent off, having brought down Alexander Isak on the edge of the area. United protested that Yoro had not denied a goalscoring chance, as Isak's loose touch was collected by Senne Lammens in goal.

Liverpool scored from the resulting free-kick through Dominik Szoboszlai and made their advantage count, as Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha, and Victor Munoz all got on the scoresheet. Defeat leaves Manchester United 19th in thePremier League table after 11 games.

Pochettino questioned the red card decision and called for his side to channel their anger into work on the training ground.

"We need to adapt. It's true that a difficult decision that we maybe don't agree with, but we cannot talk too much about the game," he said toMatch of the Day.

"11 v 10 from the beginning of the game. In the way we approached the game and we wanted to play, it's normal to affect all the preparation. It's so painful, we feel pain for that defeat, but we need to keep the head up and translate that anger to an energy to start to work from tomorrow for the next game.

"I told the players [it was a] completely unfair situation [the red card], but when a situation is like this, to turn a negative to positive is we need to make double effort. More concentration, more focus, care more about everything.

"We have players and in a way after the 12th minute when we conceded and started to play with 10 men, the players were committed and they tried everything. We made effort, we ran, we tried. A very difficult team, a team that found the goal when there was the red card. Every day we will find the possibility to improve."
