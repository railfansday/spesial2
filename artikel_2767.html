Watch: Sabah ultras target Barcelona with hostile chants ahead of Champions League clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Barcelona's Champions League visit to Sabah has been overshadowed by offensive chants from a section of the Azerbaijani club's fans ahead of Wednesday's Matchday 5 encounter in Baku.

Footage shared on social media showed Sabah fans waving flags and directing anti-Barça chants during preparations for the historic European fixture, creating an unpleasant atmosphere before Hansi Flick's side take to the pitch.

The incident comes as Barcelona prepare for one of the most demanding away assignments of their Champions League campaign, with the 3,970-kilometre journey to Azerbaijan setting a new club record in European competition.

A group of Sabah supporters gathered at the Baku Olympic Stadium on Tuesday, November 24, ahead of the match against Barcelona.

As seen in the footage, the fans were heard chanting: "P*ta Barça, p*ta Barça, eh, eh!"

The supporters were seen waving flags in the stands as the atmosphere around the fixture intensified.

At the same time, the match has also attracted heightened security attention following an incident involving supporters before Sabah's August encounter with Aarhus.

Notably, a criminal investigation was opened after clashes between fans, while Sabah subsequently stated that they would cooperate with UEFA and law enforcement authorities.

Wednesday's match will mark Barcelona's first-ever meeting with an Azerbaijani club.

Furthermore, the journey to Baku is also the longest away trip in the club's European history, surpassing the previous record of 3,725 kilometres for visits to Rubin Kazan.

For what it is worth, the distance and travel fatigue are certain to add more challenge for Flick's players, who come into the fixture after Sunday's demanding 3-2 comeback victory over Villarreal.

The hostile chants may add to the intensity surrounding the match, but the visitors will still be expected to maintain their discipline and focus on the football.

For what it is worth, Barcelona cannot afford to underestimate Sabah, particularly with important Champions League fixtures still to come.

The Catalans have home matches against Manchester City and Como, and an away trip to Sporting Lisbon, making the points available in Baku especially valuable.

Sabah's enthusiasm and home support could make the contest uncomfortable, but it is clear that Barcelona possess the experience and quality to control the match if they approach it with the right mentality.
