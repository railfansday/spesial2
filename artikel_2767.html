Hawks need Flemings, Ejiofor to step up amid injuries

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Atlanta Hawks' depth is facing another significant test, placing greater responsibility on Zuby Ejiofor and, once healthy, Kingston Flemings.

CJ McCollum will be re-evaluated in approximately four weeks after suffering a left calf strain. Asa Newell underwent surgery after additional imaging revealed a plantar plate tear accompanying his left great toe hyperextension. He will be re-evaluated in approximately four months. Those timelines mark medical checkpoints rather than guaranteed return dates.

The situation becomes more complicated because Flemings remains sidelined by a lumbar strain, while Jock Landale is out with right hip inflammation. Atlanta therefore has immediate needs in both its backcourt and frontcourt as it attempts to remain competitive in the Eastern Conference.

Flemings practicing Tuesday was an encouraging development ahead of the Oklahoma City matchup, although participation alone does not establish his readiness to return. His recovery remains the first priority before the Hawks can lean more heavily on him.

When cleared, the rookie will return to a role that had already expanded. Flemings started five consecutive games before his back injury, with McCollum providing an experienced option off the bench. Losing that veteran support increases the importance of the rookie's decision-making and consistency.

His speed offers Atlanta something valuable. Flemings can push the pace, attack gaps and pressure the rim before opposing defenses establish their positioning. Those drives can create scoring chances for him while drawing help away from teammates.

The challenge will be balancing that aggression with control. Atlanta needs him to create advantages, recognize when to move the ball and avoid possessions that end with rushed decisions. Returning to his previous rhythm would help the Hawks maintain offensive flow without asking other creators to carry every possession.

Ejiofor's opportunity is more immediate. With Newell and Landale unavailable, his energy and physicality could become increasingly important behind Onyeka Okongwu.

The rookie can contribute through purposeful screens, strong finishes and effort on the glass. Those actions do not require a featured offensive role, but they can help Atlanta sustain productive possessions and compete physically when its rotation gets thin.

Defensive discipline will matter just as much. Ejiofor must learn when to help, how to recover and how to challenge shots without accumulating unnecessary fouls. Consistent execution would give Quin Snyder greater confidence in using him for meaningful stretches.

Neither rookie should be expected to replace everything the injured players provide. The task is to handle larger responsibilities while continuing to develop.

For Atlanta, that development now carries immediate competitive stakes. Ejiofor has a chance to establish himself, while Flemings' eventual return could restore much-needed pace and ball handling. Their progress will help determine how effectively the Hawks navigate this difficult stretch.
