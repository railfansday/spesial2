Bears tie Lions 10-10 on Kyle Monangai TD

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Bears spotted the Lions a 10-point lead in the second quarter, but they've wiped it out with four minutes to go in the first half.

Running back Kyle Monangai scored from a yard out to make it 10-10 in Detroit. The touchdown capped an eight-play, 80-yard drive that saw Monangai's backfield mate D'Andre Swift account for the longest gain.

Swift caught a short pass from Caleb Williams and followed downfield blocks for a 33-yard gain. Williams also completed a pair of passes to tight end Stephen Carlson for 30 yards on the way to the end zone.

Those were Carlson's first two catches of the season and the Bears may continue to look his way now that they've lost Colston Loveland to a knee injury.
