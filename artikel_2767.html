Declan Rice to sign Arsenal contract extension

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Declan Rice will sign his new, five-year Arsenal contract soon, reports David Ornstein. The deal will keep him at the club through summer 2031, taking him past his 32nd birthday. Rice's new contract follows on the heels of Jurrien Timber's extension reported earlier today as well as Mikel Arteta putting pen to paper on an extension last month.

For my money, Declan Rice is the best midfielder in the world. He does it all and that's what makes him the best. There are other players in the world who can do specific things better but his combination of athleticism, game-reading, defensive application, attacking contribution, and set piece service is unique.

If there was to be any criticism of his game coming into this season, it was his progressive passing. He generally fared better with an exceptional passer alongside him. In typical Declan Rice fashion, he's patched that hole. He's among the Premier League leaders in progressive passes and through balls this season and leads the competition with five assists.

We can worry about Declan Rice's ability and contribution levels towards the end of his new contract when we get there. You should always be a little leery of deals that take players beyond 30, particularly ones whose athleticism is a big part of what makes them great, but if any player is going to buck the age curve, it's Declan Rice. The guy is a physical freak (in the best way). Given his high football I.Q., you'd also expect him to compensate for any decrease in speed, strength, whatever (if he experiences that at all) with veteran wiles.

Arsenal are in win-now mode. They have a wide open window to be the top dog in the Premier League for multiple seasons running. It's clear they've recognized that and are aggressively looking to maximize the opportunity. Extending Declan Rice is a big part of that.

Also, it's awesome because he's a magnificent player, precisely the kind of guy you want at your club, and keeping your stars is so much more fun than sweating their contracts and potentially seeing them move elsewhere.
