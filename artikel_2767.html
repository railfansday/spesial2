USC Football Falters As Playoff Hopes Vanish In Bloomington Loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The USC Trojans lost their chance at the College Football Playoff after a 27-13 defeat to Indiana on Saturday. The team made too many errors in a game they needed to win to stay in the hunt. Quarterback Jayden Maiava threw a late interception that sealed the loss and ended the season's big dreams.

Coach Lincoln Riley now faces questions about his job after the team failed to deliver when it mattered most. The Trojans dropped to 7-3 overall and 4-3 in the Big Ten conference. This loss to the No. 9 Hoosiers means USC must fix its mistakes fast or risk losing its coach too.

USC had a real shot to tie the game late in the fourth quarter. Jayden Maiava looked toward the end zone with the ball in his hands. The drive was moving forward well until the very end. Isaiah Jones, a defender for Indiana, read the play perfectly and intercepted the pass meant for Kayden Dixon-Wyatt.

Jones caught the ball at the 1-yard line with 6 minutes and 35 seconds left on the clock. This single play stopped USC from scoring and gave Indiana the ball back. The Trojans never got another chance to score after that moment. The game had swung back and forth, but this one mistake ended the hope for a comeback.

Indiana then put the game away for good. With 3 minutes and 30 seconds remaining, Grant Wilson delivered a 42-yard touchdown pass to Charlie Becker. This score made the final margin 27-13. The Hoosiers finished the game with a 9-1 record and a 6-1 conference mark. USC could not stop them when the game was on the line.

The Trojans were full of errors throughout the entire match. They dropped multiple chances to intercept the ball. They missed two field goals during the game. They also committed several penalties on special teams that hurt their own position. These mistakes were even more frustrating because the team had just come off a bye week.

Lincoln Riley had promised a better effort after the team took their first extended break. The previous bye week led to a bad loss against Wisconsin on the road. The team wanted to bounce back but instead made things worse. USC converted only 2 of 11 third downs during the game. This poor conversion rate killed their offense and kept them off the scoreboard.

The errors came at the worst possible time. The team had built momentum two weeks ago with a big win over then-No. 2 Ohio State. That victory had made people believe in USC again. Now that belief is gone. Under Riley, the Trojans dropped to a 1-8 record against ranked opponents on the road. This record shows a clear pattern of failing in big games away from home.

The loss brings the issue of Lincoln Riley's job back into focus. Early in the fourth quarter, the coach let his shoulders sag on the sideline. He watched kicker Caden Chittenden push a 27-yard field goal wide right. The missed attempt ended the promising drive without a result. The Trojans had reached the 8-yard line before a pass to Mark Bowman was tipped and incomplete.

The New York Post reported that failing to win this game means USC athletics director Jen Cohen must seriously consider Riley's buyout. The coach had put himself on the clock with "The Time Is Now" billboards around Los Angeles. Those signs promised a championship push. The loss to Indiana means that promise was broken.

Riley's record in big games away from home is now very poor. He has lost all the momentum from the Ohio State win. The team made the least of a chance to prove they belong in the top tier. Now the focus shifts to the front office and what they will do next. The season is not over, but the playoff path is closed.

The game was not just about the players on the field. A fan came onto the field during a timeout and made a field goal. This strange event happened while the teams were trying to handle the pressure. It added to the confusion and distraction for the USC team. The Trojans were already struggling to handle the moment.

Such distractions can hurt a team that is already making mistakes. The Trojans could not use the bye week to fix their issues. They arrived in Bloomington with the same problems they had before. The result was a devastating setback for the program.

The next step for USC is to decide what to do with their head coach. The New York Post reported that the loss likely ended their CFP hopes.
