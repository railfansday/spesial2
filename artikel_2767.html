Tom Brady’s call of the Eagles’ Thanksgiving game-winning FG might be the greatest ever

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Tom Brady is arguably the greatest quarterback in NFL history. He's also a minority owner of the Las Vegas Raiders, and the holder of the largest broadcasting contract ever to call games for FOX Sports. Brady was working on Thanksgiving to call the Philadelphia Eagles vs. Dallas Cowboys matchup, which was clearly the worst game of the Turkey Day slate. The Eagles have been a legitimate trainwreck this year, while the Cowboys just can't stop shooting themselves in the foot.

Fans who thought they could take a nap during Cowboys-Eagles made a huge mistake. This somehow turned into one of the most exciting games of the NFL season, and Brady made a call on the game-winning play that might just eventually come to define is broadcasting career.

The Eagles beat the Cowboys, 39-38, to erase a three-touchdown deficit on the final play of the game. Jake Elliott kicked a game-winning 59-yard field goal just before time expired in the fourth quarter, and Brady was ready for the moment. Please listen to the call from Kevin Burkhardt and Brady on Elliott's kick.

Yes, that's Tom Brady moaning as the kick sails through the uprights. I can't stop watching this video, and I can't stop laughing.

After he composed himself, Brady said Elliott "absolutely nuts one right down the pipe." That's what it sounded like, Tom.

Brady was in awe of Eagles QB Joe Milton the entire game. Milton — who was filling in for the injured Jalen Hurts — is known for his ridiculous throwing power, and Brady was truly losing his mind watching Milton lead the comeback. The Cowboys had better than a 97% chance to win this game with under five minutes left. Somehow, they blew it again, and the Eagles came away with a win they will never forget.

Brady's call made the kick even better. The GOAT has gotten progressively better in the booth now midway through his third ceiling, and calling Elliott's kick might be his best moment yet.
