Daniella Karagach Wants Daughter to be Flower Girl at Ezra Frech's Wedding

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Daniella Karagach has secured a plum job for her 3-year-old daughter, Nikita, when her Dancing With the Stars partner Ezra Frech ties the knot someday.

“My daughter is going to be the flower girl at his wedding,” Karagach, 33, announced during a Wednesday, November 25, appearance on Hulu’s “Get Real” podcast, before joking, “She’ll be like 12 when he gets married.”

The Paralympian, 21, is currently single but he confirmed that Karagach has indeed locked up a ceremonial role for little Nikita whenever he does walk down the aisle. (Karagach and her husband, fellow DWTS pro Pasha Pashkov, welcomed their daughter in May 2023.)

Fresh off their mirrorball trophy win on Tuesday, November 24, “Get Real” host Amanda Hirsch expressed hope that Karagach and Frech will remain close now that season 35 is over.

“One thousand percent [we will],” Frech assured. “We’re going to be friends or family for life. We’ll dance together again.”

Karagach got emotional as she discussed the possibility of pairing up with another celebrity in 2027 given her immense success with Frech. (The DWTS pro previously won the mirrorball trophy in 2021 with celebrity partner Iman Shumpert.)

“How am I going to move on from him as a partner? What am I going to do?” Karagach wondered during the podcast interview.

The pair made history in Tuesday’s DWTS season 35 finale when their three dances received a combined score of 90 out of 90 from judges Carrie Ann Inaba, Bruno Tonioli and Derek Hough, landing them in a three-way tie with Amber Glenn and Pashkov as well as Maura Higgins and Mark Ballas. Viewers’ votes ultimately delivered Frech and Karagach the mirrorball when factored into the final scores.

Earlier that night, Frech and Karagach triumphed with their freestyle even though the Paralympian struggled to get his prosthetic onto his leg.

“Last night was crazy. … I can’t believe this happened,” Frech recalled on Good Morning America on Wednesday morning. “I can’t believe we got that opportunity to represent [the disabled community].”

Frech shared how his professional partner proved to be crucial in helping him regain his focus following the mishap.

“We started our freestyle without the prosthetic; that was a creative choice, and then things went a little off script,” the athlete explained. “I wasn’t able to suction my leg on and I knew it wasn’t on. Sometimes when that happens, it’ll re-suction, but it didn’t. It came off. She told me to put it on mid-dance, I kinda blacked out.”

He added, “When it came off, I was like, ‘I’m doing the rest of the dance without my leg on.’ And I heard [Daniella] go, ‘Put your leg on!’”

“I saw his face, I was like, ‘If I don’t tell him to put his leg on, he’s gonna do the whole dance without it,” Karagach concurred.

Dancing With the Stars is expected to return to ABC and Disney+ in 2027.
