PlayStation Store Black Friday 2026 Sale Is Live With Huge Deals

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Deals on Marvel's Wolverine, NBA 2K27, and many more await

Sony's PlayStation Store Black Friday sale is now officially live, with thousands of games, game bundles, and add-on packs discounted for the holiday shopping season. The list includes PlayStation 5 and PlayStation 4 hits and recent releases such as Marvel's Wolverine, Control Resonant, and Silent Hill: Townfall, plus deep discounts of up to 85% off a long list of titles.

If you aren't saving space for Grand Theft Auto 6 on your PS5 SSD, there's lots of good stuff on the PlayStation Store to fill up your console's storage.

The PlayStation Store's Black Friday Sale runs until Nov. 30. The sale's offerings will be refreshed on Nov. 16 with certain discounts ending on Nov. 20. Head over to the PlayStation Store for the full list, but here are some highlights for PlayStation's Black Friday 2026 sale.

Sony Interactive Entertainment hasn't announced any deals yet on PlayStation 5 consoles or DualSense controllers, but it's still early.

Last year's Black Friday sale included the announcement of a new PS5 bundle and several discounts on PlayStation hardware. That included a $100 discount on consoles, which followed a PS5 price hike a month earlier. Gamers hoping to take advantage of similar discounts this year may be disappointed.
