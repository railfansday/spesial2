Rams send playoff message with win vs. Packers: They are not dead yet

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Coach Sean McVay and the Los Angeles Rams have managed to overcome a slow start to the season and are very much in the playoff picture.

The Rams have put together five consecutive wins and improved to 7-4 with a 26-13 win Wednesday against the Green Bay Packers. Four of their wins during the streak have come by 13 or more points.

If the season ended today, the Rams would claim a wild card spot.

The Rams' latest stretch of adversity featured a remake of their identity on offense, as they have to move forward without Puka Nacua, who is out for the season due to an injury. Fellow star receiver Davante Adams continues to work his way back.

McVay put a stronger emphasis on the running game, led by Blake Corum.

Corum celebrated his 26th birthday Wednesday with a career night, finishing with 184 yards and a touchdown on 32 carries. His rushing total was the most by a Rams player since Todd Gurley's 208-yard performance in 2018. Corum's carries were also the most by any player this season, and the most by a Rams back since Zac Stacy's 33 in 2013.

While the run game was effective against the Packers, the Rams will need to get back to the passing game. Quarterback Matthew Stafford managed just 140 yards Wednesday and threw a pick.

Other receivers will need to step up if Adams is not back soon.

Konata Mumpfield had four receptions for 69 yards on five targets. He did flash some big-play ability with a 24-yard gain on one reception. Corum and Kyren Williams also contributed in the passing game with six total catches for 40 yards.

The Kansas City Chiefs and San Francisco 49ers make up the next two games for the Rams. Those two opponents entered the week with a combined record of 17-3.

Quarterback Patrick Mahomes and the Chiefs will provide another test for a defense that's managed to get healthy with Myles Garrett and Aaron Donald available.

The Rams will not only have another important NFC West game against the 49ers approaching but are also in search of revenge after struggling in the season opener in Australia. Garrett and Donald were not totally healthy or present, respectively, for the opener. With them available, the rematch could have a playoff feel.
