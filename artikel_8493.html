Investing - Theory, News &amp; General • TIPS are they really worth holding?

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Since TIPS have adjustment based on CPI vs actual inflation, do they really provide enough to make them worth holding? Is it better to just hold short duration or floating rate as they will adjust quickly to interest rate changes. As was evident in 2022, TIPS are slow to adjust and very sensitive to interest rate. And if your government is trying to underplay inflation, they might not do a great job of keeping up with inflation adjustments.
