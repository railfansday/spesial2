The Game Awards 2026 Nominees to Be Revealed on 17th November

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Geoff Keighley has announced the nominees for all of the categories at The Game Awards will be confirmed next week, on 17th November 2026.

A livestream will confirm the game's nominated, with the show itself airing on 10th December 2026. You can tune in for the nominee livestream at 9am PT / 12pm ET / 5pm GMT.

Obviously, the question now becomes: what games will be in contention for Game of the Year?

It's confirmed that GTA 6 will be eligible for all the awards, despite the fact the game won't launch until two days after it could be nominated for the top accolade. I personally believe, with complete confidence, it will be nominated for Game of the Year, but we'll have to see what happens during the livestream next week.

Six games compete for Game of the Year, so what could join it? The likes of Resident Evil Requiem, 007 First Light, and Big Walk all seem like obvious contenders. Other potential inclusions could be Control Resonant, Phantom Blade Zero, or The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time — depending on how the jury feels about including remakes.

All will be revealed on Tuesday, 17th November 2026. What games do you want to see nominated? Post your pick in the comments below.
