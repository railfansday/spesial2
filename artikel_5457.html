Buster + Punch Expands CAST Hardware Collection with Signature Smoked Finish

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The new addition brings Cast to four finishes across door and cabinet hardware, and bath accessories.

DALLAS, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Buster + Punch is expanding its Cast hardware and bath collection with the introduction of its Smoked finish. The addition brings Cast in line with the brand's broader hardware finish palette, offering four finish options across door and cabinet hardware and bath accessories.

Known for its distinctive combination of rough-cast metal and smooth, hand-finished surfaces, Cast brings a tactile, industrial-inspired aesthetic to everyday interior details. Smooth, precisely machined solid-metal bars meet textured cast-metal knuckles and grips, creating a distinctive contrast that plays with light, shadow and touch.

Taking inspiration from the brand's roots in custom motorcycles, industrial architecture and traditional metalworking, Cast celebrates the beauty of the manufacturing process itself. Each rough-cast element is individually formed and refined by hand, giving every piece its own unique character. The addition of Smoked offers another way to bring this interplay of textures and materials throughout the home.

"Cast has always been about the contrast between the raw beauty of cast metal and the precision of highly polished solid metal. With the introduction of Smoked, we're bringing another new expression to the collection and giving designers more ways to carry that distinctive combination of materials and finishes throughout the home," says Massimo Buster Minale, Founder and Creative Director of Buster + Punch.

"With Smoked joining the Cast finish range, the collection offers another way to coordinate hardware and accessories throughout the home. From interior doors to bathroom cabinetry and bath accessories, the finish helps create a cohesive approach to material and detail across different spaces," says David Schlocker, President of Buster + Punch.

The Smoked finish is being introduced across select Cast hardware, including door hardware, bathroom cabinet hardware and bath accessories. Cast is available in four finishes: Steel, Brass, Welders Black and the new Smoked.

For more information, visit Buster + Punch's Cast collection.

ABOUT BUSTER + PUNCHBuster + Punch is a disruptive home fashion brand redefining interior design through precision-crafted solid-metal detailing across lighting, hardware, switches, plumbing fixtures, and furniture. Founded in London in 2013 by architect Massimo Buster Minale, the brand draws from custom motorcycle culture and contemporary subculture design.

Today, Buster + Punch operates globally with a multidisciplinary team spanning product development, engineering, and design innovation. Notable collaborations include GROHE SPA, Travis Barker, Jon Olsson, Tom Sellers, Zwilling-Staub Cookware, Tonester Paints and The Oppenheim Group from Selling Sunset. The brand is a supporting member of Be Original Americas, promoting authentic design and craftsmanship.
