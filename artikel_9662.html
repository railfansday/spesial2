AI Jobs Beyond Engineering: Where Remote AI and Automation Roles Are Hiring Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Posted on Nov 3 • Originally published at nodjobs.com • AI-assisted

Originally published on NodJobs, where we track live remote jobs open to US applicants.

Search for "AI jobs" and you mostly get machine learning engineers. So we read every live remote job title on NodJobs this week and counted the ones that mention AI, machine learning, LLMs, generative or agentic AI, MLOps or applied science. The result looks less like an engineering trend and more like a change in how whole companies hire.

Sales is the biggest group. Samsara lists Specialist Seller roles for its AI products at $350,000 a year, and Datadog is hiring sales engineers for AI accounts at $149,000–$200,000.

Inside AI & machine learning engineering, yes: the median posted salary is $272,675. Outside it, not clearly. AI-titled product and software roles pay about the same as their peers, and AI-titled sales roles pay a little less. It is an early read we will keep tracking every week.

AI roles often come with a second word: automation. Titles like Legal Operations Specialist (AI & Automation), Digital Customer Success Manager, AI & Automation and Finance Systems Analyst, Integrations and Automation sit in operations teams, with a median posted salary around $116,000. If you already build workflows, integrations or internal tooling, these titles describe work you are doing.

In February 2024, Klarna announced that its new AI assistant was handling the workload of about 700 support staff. About a year later, its CEO told Bloomberg the company was hiring human support staff again, fully remote, because quality had suffered. The jobs above follow the same pattern: companies are not only automating work, they are hiring people to run, sell, secure and improve that automation.

The full report, with every table and the method, is on NodJobs. You can also browse live remote AI & machine learning jobs.
