ITRI Wins Three R&D 100 Awards for Innovation in Emissions Reduction, Wireless Sensing, and Semiconductor Processing

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

HSINCHU, Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI) received three 2026 R&D 100 Awards in Scottsdale, Arizona, on November 19. The recognition marks ITRI's 19th consecutive year of winning R&D 100 Awards, with 72 awards in total. More than 90% of its award-winning technologies have been applied in industry.

"One of the biggest trends this year was a strong focus on process development and prototyping, with roughly one in five entries focused on AI and software. This highlights the potential to speed up core processes, as demonstrated by ITRI's IonTwin, which reduces simulation time from hours to less than 10 seconds," said R&D World Editor-in-Chief Brian Buntz. "I think ITRI's success comes down to execution, simplicity, and excellence. Its strength lies in manufacturing, collaboration with global partners, and consistent execution. Taiwan is also uniquely positioned to turn applied research into real-world solutions," he added.

In response to global trends in AI, semiconductors, and the transition to net zero, ITRI focuses on industry needs and draws on expertise across disciplines to translate advanced research into commercially valuable solutions. This year's award-winning technologies deliver advances in greenhouse gas emissions reduction, wireless sensing, and semiconductor process efficiency, with proven industrial applications.

Ubiquitous Network-Native Radar (UNR) adds sensing and positioning capabilities to existing communications equipment with modular software upgrades. By analyzing changes in wireless signals in the environment, it turns communications networks into sensing systems. This "network-as-a-sensor" approach brings integrated sensing and communications (ISAC), a key technology envisioned for 6G, to conventional wireless infrastructure. UNR achieves centimeter-level positioning accuracy indoors and positioning accuracy within three meters outdoors. ITRI has completed field validation with base station companies in Taiwan, the UK, Spain, and the Netherlands, paving the way for broader ISAC adoption.

The Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI) is a world-leading R&D organization dedicated to innovating a better future. Founded in 1973, ITRI has played a vital role in transforming Taiwan's industries from labor-intensive into innovation-driven. Over the years, ITRI has incubated hundreds of startups and spinoffs, including well-known companies such as UMC and TSMC.
