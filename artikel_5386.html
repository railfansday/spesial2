Xi calls for inclusive regional cooperation based on solidarity, openness, innovation

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

BEIJING, Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The following article was published today by China Daily.

President Xi Jinping outlined economy and trade, connectivity, innovation and development as four pillars for building an Asia-Pacific community and opening up new prospects for regional cooperation, as he chaired the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting in Shenzhen, Guangdong province, on Thursday.

The gathering marked the third time that China has hosted the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation leaders' meeting, following those held in Shanghai in 2001 and Beijing in 2014.

"Today, momentous changes not seen in a century are accelerating across the globe," Xi told the leaders and representatives of APEC economies and heads of international organizations attending the meeting.

APEC's 21 member economies are home to more than one-third of the world's population and account for over 60 percent of global GDP.

Xi called for efforts to build an Asia-Pacific community characterized by solidarity and mutual assistance, openness and integration, enterprise and innovation, as well as inclusive development.

Members of APEC are like passengers riding the same ship, he noted, adding that "to navigate the waves of the Pacific, we must unite as one and look out for each other".

"We must expand our shared interests, meet the difficulties and challenges together, and steadily build a shared sense of community," he said.

He called for upholding openness and integration, advancing economic integration and championing free and open trade and investment. Development should benefit everyone, he said, while taking into account the different needs of Asia-Pacific economies.

Xi said that China, an advocate, promoter and forerunner of Asia-Pacific economic cooperation, is ready to work with all parties to strengthen the pillar of economy and trade, upgrade the pillar of connectivity, foster the pillar of innovation and enhance the pillar of development.

On trade, Xi said the Free Trade Area of the Asia-Pacific is a shared vision of APEC members, and he called for steady progress in realizing this vision and setting a higher standard for openness.

He urged efforts to align regional economic and trade agreements, boost trade and investment facilitation and digitalization, reduce barriers to trade and investment, and help economies build the capacity to participate in high-standard economic and trade arrangements.

Regarding connectivity, he called for stronger infrastructure networks, closer coordination of policies and regulations, and easier travel for business and tourism. Cultural and people-to-people exchanges should also be promoted to foster mutual understanding, he said.

On innovation, Xi described the digital economy and artificial intelligence as engines of innovation-driven growth.

"We should build an open ecosystem for innovation, encourage the broad application of AI, and promote the innovation-driven transformation and upgrading of industries," he said.

On development, he emphasized the need to promote regional cooperation on energy and food security, deepen anti-corruption governance across borders, and expand green and low-carbon cooperation to achieve sustainable economic growth.

He also called for improving livelihoods and investing in people to tap into the full potential of talent across the Asia-Pacific.

"China is rooted in the Asia-Pacific and will continue to support Asia-Pacific cooperation," Xi said.

He announced that China will provide $20 million to support APEC's practical cooperation, and will establish a Center for Asia-Pacific Community Studies in Shenzhen to conduct policy research and exchanges.

China will also set up cooperation institutions in areas including digital technology, cross-border data flows, clean energy and the development of small and medium-sized enterprises, he said.

Leaders and representatives of member economies delivered remarks in turn. All parties spoke highly of the fruitful outcomes of the APEC China Year, thanked China for its excellent work, and made positive responses to the major initiatives and measures put forward by Xi.

Kristalina Georgieva, managing director of the International Monetary Fund, who was invited to brief the meeting on the state of the global economy, said that AI has had a material impact on global growth this year, helping counterbalance the economic effects of an energy supply shock.

Describing AI as a significant factor in medium-term competitiveness, productivity and growth, she called on economies to develop digital infrastructure, prepare workers for the technology, and address risks to financial stability and cybersecurity.

She also underscored the importance of dialogue and cooperation through forums such as APEC, saying that working together can help advance shared prosperity and peace.

After listening to the other participants' remarks, Xi called for turning the consensus on building an Asia-Pacific community into action, maintaining a clear direction for cooperation and delivering solid results to continuously achieve tangible and accessible outcomes.

At the conclusion of the meeting, leaders adopted the 2026 APEC Leaders' Declaration, which puts forward the Shenzhen Outlook on building an Asia-Pacific community, as well as three outcome documents covering trade, connectivity and innovation.

Vietnamese President To Lam, whose country will host APEC in 2027, welcomed leaders and representatives to attend next year's meeting.

With China's leadership and the positive contributions of its members, APEC 2026 has achieved important accomplishments, adding momentum to economic integration and development in the Asia-Pacific, he said.
