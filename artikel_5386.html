Fed minutes show officials want more rate hikes, flag AI buildout as inflation risk

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Two Fed members voted against holding rates at 3.75% to 4% in October, preferring a quarter-point increase

Renovation work continues on the Marriner S. Eccles Federal Reserve Board Building, the main offices of the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System on December 9, 2025 in Washington, DC. (Andrew Harnik/Getty Images)

Federal Reserve officials concluded in October that additional rate hikes would be warranted to bring inflation back to 2%, according to minutes the central bank released Wednesday.

Nearly all participants at the Oct. 27-28 meeting concluded that, based on their baseline forecasts for prices and employment, lifting the federal funds target range "would likely be appropriate." A couple of participants said an increase of more than 25 basis points could warrant consideration at the next meeting if incoming inflation data were unfavorable.

The Federal Open Market Committee voted 10-2 to hold the benchmark rate at 3.75% to 4%. Fed Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack dissented, preferring a quarter-point increase. The next meeting is Dec. 8-9.

Many participants viewed the AI buildout as "a material source of inflation risk." Some said the investment surge could raise aggregate demand relative to supply if capacity expansion proved slower than expected. Many said demand for AI-related products was straining supply and raising prices of technology-related consumer goods.

The minutes also show concern about AI financing. Fed staff highlighted rapid growth in AI-related debt and said that leasing obligations kept off balance sheets, along with circular funding deals among AI-linked companies, could magnify losses if AI investments failed to deliver the expected returns.

Most participants noted a rapid rise in AI-related borrowing, much of it arranged through intricate, hard-to-see financing structures. A few said risk tied to AI was increasingly clustered in a handful of companies. Almost all participants commented on the risks of a scenario in which AI returns disappointed investors.

Staff estimated that total inflation, measured by the 12-month change in the personal consumption expenditures price index, rose to 3.6% in September. Core inflation edged up to 3.1%.

Fed officials have voiced differing views on how fast to raise interest rates. Fed Governor Christopher Waller told CNBC on Wednesday that the Fed should consider a half-point increase in December and may have to "shock" people.

New York Fed President John Williams said Tuesday, “I don’t think the situation calls for dramatic action,” according to CNBC. St. Louis Fed President Alberto Musalem said Tuesday the economy “will likely require several interest rate increases before the spring of 2027.”
