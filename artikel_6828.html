Watch the Stunning U.S. Nationals Short Program That Inspired Amber Glenn’s Freestyle on the ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Finale

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The moment you’ve been waiting for on Dancing With the Stars is incoming: the remaining five couples get to perform freestyle dances of their choice during the finale—and Amber Glenn and Pasha Pashkov went with “Like a Prayer” and “Music” by Madonna.

The song choice is seemingly inspired by Amber’s incredible short program at the 2026 U.S. Nationals, which set a record and won her first place.

Meanwhile, here’s a sneak peek from finale rehearsals on Dancing With the Stars, where Pasha says the freestyle is “very special and very personal.”

It’s been a pretty chaotic season so far on DWTS, but Amber told E! News last month that she’s been leaning on Pasha, tuning everything out, and enjoying the ride.

“I’m no stranger to being in a competition where there’s chaos happening,” she said. “Everyone’s gonna have an opinion on everything, and we’re allowed to. But what really matters is how we interact with each other, and that’s to lead with kindness and love and support. That’s something that I feel like Pasha and I have really done this season is try to really embrace everyone, because this is such a special experience, and it’s been incredible seeing these pros—how much work they put in.”
