How Clearing Space for Barkov and Marchand Reshaped the Panthers’ Forward Depth

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The returns of Aleksander Barkov and Brad Marchand in Wednesday's 5–3 victory over the Ottawa Senators not only upgraded the top tier of the Florida Panthers' lineup but also triggered a ripple effect throughout the entire depth chart.

As the two veterans combined for four points after returning ahead of schedule from left knee injuries, Florida's front office and coaching staff had to navigate the complexities of roster management, waiver losses, and depth chart adjustments that will influence the team heading into a demanding weekend.

Reintegrating high-impact veterans from injured reserve rarely comes without its challenges. To make room on the active roster for Barkov and Marchand, Florida lost forward Cole Reinhardt to the Vancouver Canucks via NHL waivers. This situation serves as a stark reminder of how tight NHL roster margins can become when key players are ready to return. With top-six and middle-six roles reclaiming their minutes, fringe forwards and depth pieces find themselves under immediate pressure as available lineup spots dwindle.

Depth Chart Movement: Vilmanis and Devine

Despite the tightening roster, Florida's younger organizational options have played a crucial role in recent transitions:

Sandis Vilmanis: The young forward has received consistent praise for his contributions as a regular, demonstrating that he can compete effectively at the NHL level while providing the Panthers with energy and secondary scoring.

Jack Devine: Having experienced recent recall and loan movements with the American Hockey League, Devine's situation underscores how Florida is balancing developmental opportunities with the need for NHL readiness in case of emergencies.

These moves exemplify a classic contender dynamic: relying on proven stars to deliver immediate results while simultaneously managing younger depth pieces to ensure organizational flexibility over the course of a long season.

A Weekend Test for the Reconfigured Lineup

Any roster shuffle faces scrutiny when back-to-back games loom on the horizon. The Panthers will travel to face the Washington Capitals on Friday, November 27, 2026, before returning to Sunrise on Saturday, November 28, 2026, to host the Tampa Bay Lightning.

With Barkov and Marchand back in action and the depth chart newly adjusted, the next two days will provide a critical test of how smoothly this reconfigured Panthers group can operate under real-game pressure. As they look to build on their recent success, the Panthers will need to ensure that their depth can rise to the occasion alongside their star players.
