Brian Schottenheimer’s take on DC’s future with Cowboys will intrigue fans

The Dallas Cowboys are currently getting ready for their upcoming home game vs the Tennessee Titans. The Cowboys recently made a big change by taking away the play-calling duties of defensive coordinator Christian Parker, although he remains in that position on the coaching staff.

Now, head coach Brian Schottenheimer has spoken on Parker's status moving forward.

"…bright future here as defensive coordinator," he said of Parker, per Calvin Watkins of the Dallas Morning News on X, formerly Twitter.

"Different perspective was good for us," added Schottenheimer on the decision to take away Parker's playcalling duties, noting that Mike Pettine will now be calling the defensive plays for the Cowboys.

The Cowboys' front office spent all offseason touting the team's supposedly revamped defense, which had been heavily criticized throughout last season, consistently costing the team wins despite its historic offensive output in 2025.

This year, the team's defense has been arguably just as bad despite some of the changes made, which included spending both of their first-round draft picks last year on that side of the ball. Many fans were irritated when Jerry Jones and company opted not to make any deals at the recent trade deadline, although the team had traded for cornerback Joey Porter Jr. of the Pittsburgh Steelers earlier in the year.

It remains to be seen whether or not the move of Parker away from playcalling duties will pay dividends for the Cowboys' defense, but clearly, the team is desperate enough to throw anything at the wall to see what sticks at this point.

Kickoff between the Cowboys and Titans is set for Sunday afternoon at 1:00 pm ET from Arlington.
