Patrick Mahomes on Harrison Butker: I try to have confidence in everybody, I know he's working at it

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Harrison Butker missed yet another field goal on Thursday night, which was a key factor in the Chiefs eventually losing to the Bills, 13-10.

With a whopping eight missed field goals in 11 games so far this season — plus one missed extra point — it’s fair to question how much longer the Chiefs can stick with their longtime kicker.

After the Thanksgiving loss, quarterback Patrick Mahomes offered some public support for the embattled kicker.

“I try to have confidence in everybody on the team. That’s part of playing this sport, is believing in the guys around you,” Mahomes said in his postgame press conference. “And obviously, Harrison hasn’t had the season that he’s wanted this year. But he’s also been one of the best kickers in the game for a long time.

“I know he’s working at it. I can tell you that. And he’s doing his best to be the best teammate he can be, and go out there and execute in those moments. Unfortunately, tonight it didn’t go his way. But, I promise you, he’ll come back and work at it again. And hopefully, the next time, he’ll be able to go out there and make it happen.”

The Chiefs signed Justin Tucker to their practice squad with the hope of pushing Butker to improve. But at this point, if Tucker is going to be around, it might be time to try him in a game.
