Ponce retains WBC Collegiate title, UCLA’s Chen stops USC’s Matias at Rivals in the Ring

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

LOS ANGELES — Gabriel "El Tigre" Ponce is still the WBC Collegiate champion.

Ponce, a USC sophomore who fights out of St. Louis, Missouri, retained his title Saturday night with a unanimous decision over Air Force's Russell Klug in the headliner of the Los Angeles Athletic Club's Rivals in the Ring collegiate boxing invitational. The three-round, two-minutes-per-round fight was back-and-forth from the opening bell to the final one.

Klug, a southpaw, applied forward pressure and tried to work from the center of the ring. Ponce countered that with timing and distance, going to the body to set up hooks to the head and slipping out of exchanges quickly before lining up his next attack. It was a sharp gameplan that won over the judges and the crowd.

The card marked the first Rivals in the Ring at the LAAC since 2019, bringing live boxing back to the club whose boxing tradition dates to 1880. The bouts were held on the John R. Wooden Award basketball court in downtown Los Angeles.

In the co-main event, UCLA's Nick Chen stopped USC Trojan Boxing Club's Nico Matias by TKO in the "Battle of Los Angeles." Chen kept Matias at the end of his hard jab as the USC fighter kept advancing, and he forced two standing eight counts before the stoppage. Matias stayed busy with his ones and twos, but Chen was the one finding the target.

UCLA's Nick Chen celebrates his TKO win over USC's Nico Matias.

Beyond the two Los Angeles schools, Air Force, Crandall University of Canada and UC Riverside were the night's other big winners. Air Force and Crandall flew in Friday, narrowly beating the nationwide FAA ground stoppage, and both left with multiple victories. Air Force's Mikayla Gangadeen, Aweh Ayodele and Sierra Dace all won by decision, as did Crandall's Ivan Cardenas and Luke Whitton.

UC Riverside went a perfect 2-0. Jaskirat Shoker opened the show with a unanimous decision over Crandall's Eduardo Ortiz in a closely contested, action-packed fight. Shoker, who is close to launching his professional career, was joined in the win column by teammate Augusten Wieners.

With the 2026 event in the books, the LAAC now looks ahead to the next annual collegiate boxing invitational, expected in November 2027.
