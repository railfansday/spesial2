Vote for the Knoxville TVA Employees Credit Union boys athlete of the week for Nov. 16-20

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Here are the candidates for the Knoxville TVA Employees Credit Union boys athlete of the week. Voters have until noon on Nov. 25 to submit their responses.

Micah Jones, Alcoa: Jones had 14 carries for 66 yards and a touchdown. On defense, he had an interception, a forced fumble and a fumble recovery that led to a defensive touchdown in Alcoa's 38-7 win over Greeneville.

Will Croley, Anderson County: Croley nearly reached a triple-double with 10 rebounds, nine points and seven assists in a 59-56 win over Heritage.

Hunter Bennett, CAK: Bennett had eight catches for 120 yards and a touchdown in CAK's 42-7 loss to Lausanne.

TSSAA FOOTBALL; CAK's Maddie Braden opens TSSAA basketball season with 42 points

Dontay Kibaiyu, Central: Kibaiyu logged four total tackles, four quarterback pressures and two tackles for loss in Central's 28-21 win over Rhea County.

Hadyn Hedrick, Coalfield: Hedrick led the offense with 172 rushing yards and three touchdowns on 16 carries in Coalfield's 42-7 win over Whitwell.

Cris Fudger, Gatlinburg-Pittman: Fudger scored 24 points and had two rebounds, an assist, a steal and a block in a 74-57 loss to Anderson County. He also had 38 points and nine rebounds in a 72-55 loss to Austin-East.

Alex Owens, Gibbs: Owens won the Region 1 bowling qualifying tournament with a total score of 1,302.

Taren Claridy, Greeneville: Claridy caught all 11 of his targets for 119 yards in a 38-7 loss to Alcoa.

Dax Cole, Maryville: Cole rushed for three touchdowns and 66 yards on six carries in Maryville's 38-14 win over Smyrna.

Landon Washam, Scott: Washam had 18 points, eight rebounds, seven steals and four assists in Scott's 65-49 win against Claiborne.

Talon Pederson, Seymour: Pederson led with 15 points, six rebounds, three assists and two steals in a close 67-66 win over Jefferson County.
