Davis Allen won't return against Packers

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Los Angeles Rams TE Davis Allen (ankle) exited the Week 12 game against the Green Bay Packers and will not return after suffering an ankle injury. Prior to exiting, Allen had two receptions for 14 yards.

Why it Matters: Injuries continue to pile up to members of the Rams' passing attack, and with Allen sidelined, Tyler Higbee and Colby Parkinson should see a bulk of the work at the tight end spot.
