How Tiger Woods performed on his last competitive appearance as his return to golf is confirmed

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Tiger Woods will make his first appearance in a competitive tournament in more than two years next month, with the 15-time major champion announced for the Hero World Challenge.

Footage on social media recently showed Woods confirm that he planned to be in the Bahamas for the unofficial event that he hosts. However, many would have assumed that the 50-year-old was simply expecting to watch the action rather than tee it up.

It turns out that Woods is indeed set to play at Albany. He has been named as one of the final three participants, with Rickie Fowler and Gary Woodland also involved.

It will be Tiger Woods' first appearance in an event with world ranking points since The Open Championship in 2024.

Given everything that has happened in Woods' life over the last two years, it is almost surprising to think that he managed to play in all four majors in 2024. Unfortunately, he only managed to make the cut at The Masters that year.

Woods had to answer questions about his future leading into the final major of the year at Royal Troon, with Colin Montgomerie suggesting that the time had come for the American to retire after he missed the cut at the US Open the previous month.

Woods fired back at Montgomerie, noting that he was able to play in The Open until he was 60 as a past champion.

Sadly, he was unable to send the Scot a message with his performance on the course. Woods missed the cut after finishing the second round at 14 over par.

He would go on to play in the PNC Championship later that year, losing out in a playoff alongside his son, Charlie, to Bernhard Langer and his son. That week will largely be remembered for the hole in one Charlie Woods made.

Woods has an outstanding record in the Hero World Challenge with five wins under his belt. In fact, he finished outside the top two on just one occasion during his first 10 appearances at the event.

His best result since the Hero moved to the Bahamas came in 2019, as he finished in fourth place, four shots adrift of winner Henrik Stenson.

That was also the year of his most recent victory on the PGA Tour. The question now is whether this appearance will lead to one final run for arguably the greatest golfer in the game's history.
