Sixers vs. Heat: Final playing status for Joel Embiid in NBA Cup

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

PHILADELPHIA -- The Philadelphia 76ers will take on the Miami Heat in the NBA Cup on Wednesday night as they look to bounce back following a loss on Monday to the Portland Trail Blazers. The Heat will offer up a challenge when considering they have won seven in a row, but the Sixers have also been a resilient bunch and will look to get back on track.

They will have to do so without the services of Joel Embiid. The big fella is set to miss his second straight game with an illness after initially being listed as doubtful for the contest.

One has to wonder what coach Nick Nurse will do in terms of his center rotation with Embiid out. The Heat have a big frontline and the Sixers will have to find a way to combat that.

"I think that it could probably be a little different tonight, right?" Nurse said pregame. "But I do think that, again, we look at the reasons why it needs to be different. Some based on the other night, some based on who we're playing. But again, I think a lot of it plays itself out, too, and what's going on out there."

The Heat will have some real hitters up front in the form of Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bam Adebayo. Therefore, the Sixers will have to try and figure out how to contain them and keep them off the glass without the services of Embiid.

"I mean, I think we have a really good idea of what we're going to do early tonight with that," Nurse finished. "But again, that kind of depends on how the game starts out too a little bit. It's not giving you much. It's kind of it's a little bit jumbled up there and what we're doing and who's healthy enough and all that kind of stuff. So it's a little bit jumbled up to try to figure it out. So it's not it took a lot of discussion over the last 24 hours. Let's put it that way."

Tip off from the Xfinity Mobile Arena is set for 5 p.m. EST on Wednesday.
