Fantasy Basketball: Landry Shamet, AJ Dybantsa are among players on hot streaks — but what do the stats tell us?

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Fantasy basketball managers should keep an eye on these hot streaks. Landry Shamet is shooting 51.5% from 3 over his last five games, Donovan Clingan is racking up double-doubles and blocks, and Jalen Smith is in the middle of a career year for the Bulls. Corey Kispert and Aaron Wiggins are sleepers who are fueling the Hawks' 11-6 start with sharpshooting, while rookie AJ Dybantsa is heating up in Washington. Whether you want a long-term hold or a short-term streamer for roto, category or points leagues, here is what each trending player's recent stats mean for your lineup.

Shamet has been on fire lately, averaging 16.4 points on 55.8% shooting, including 51.5% from deep over the last five games, helping the Knicks go undefeated over that stretch. He reached the 20-point mark twice in the five games and has hit it three times on the season, which is remarkable considering he only topped 20 points twice through all of last season.

He is averaging 26.9 minutes per game through 17 appearances this year, which is up from 23.0 minutes last season and just 15.2 per game in his first campaign with the Knicks. In his extended playing time, he is averaging a career-high 2.5 made 3-pointers per game, while shooting a strong 44.2% from beyond the arc this season. He has also been very good from the foul line, knocking down 81% on 1.2 attempts per game, helping fuel a career-best 66.4% true shooting percentage. With a championship under his belt, the 29-year-old, ninth-year guard may have found a new level of confidence that has him on track as one of the most valuable shooters in the league.

Clingan is emerging as one of the league's most complete young big men, averaging 11.6 points, 11.3 rebounds and a career-high 2.1 blocks in 26.1 minutes per game. His scoring and rebounding are down marginally from last season, but that's a product of reduced minutes rather than any decline in play, as the Trail Blazers added offseason talent that reshaped the rotation.

The third-year center ranks eighth in the league with eight double-doubles and has already clocked in impressive season highs of 24 points, 18 rebounds and five blocks (twice). His 11.3 rebounds per game also ranks fifth in the league. In addition to his counting stats, he provides efficiency, shooting 59.4% from the field, while averaging 1.0 made threes on 2.4 attempts per game, which amounts to elite 41.5% shooting from long range. He is also extremely reliable when fed in close, as he is averaging 73.5% shooting from within five feet of the basket. Clingan can be expected to continue to excel in his role without needing to be the center of attention.

Kispert is off to an anomalous start to the season, managing to provide a notable impact for his squad in only 12.4 minutes per game through 10 appearances. The sixth-year marksman is averaging 9.3 points on 55.9% shooting, including a blistering 48.8% from deep, with 2.0 made 3s per game.

Wiggins is doing similarly impressive work by averaging 13.9 points on 45.5% shooting, including 40.5% from deep, with a career-best 2.8 made 3s in 25.8 minutes per game. On the other hand, he is hitting a career-low 61.5% from the foul line on a total of 13 attempts through 17 games this season.

Both Kispert and Wiggins' ability to provide such a valuable contribution in limited time has been a big help in the Hawks getting out to an 11-6 start to the season. The sharpshooting is also a defining aspect of the team's identity, as they have been heavily reliant on the long ball, ranking sixth in the league with 14.8 made 3s per game.

Smith is working on a career season, averaging highs across the board, with 14.1 points, 7.5 rebounds, 2.2 assists, 0.7 steals and 1.0 blocks in 25.3 minutes per game. He is also shooting an efficient 54.7% from the field, which is all the more impressive considering he is also averaging a career-high 2.1 made 3-pointers on 5.1 attempts per game, for a 40.3% shooting mark from long range. Additionally, it can not be overlooked that he is hitting 74.2% from within five feet of the basket, which is helping make up for the increase in 3-pointers and boosting his impressive overall shooting percentage.

Smith got a boost from the fact that Nic Claxton was sidelined for the first few weeks of the season, and his strong play has allowed him to keep the starting role. The seventh-year big man has been showing flashes that he had more to offer for a while now, and it finally looks like the tide has turned in his favor. He should continue to build on the momentum of his strong start, which has included four double-doubles and season highs of 26 points, 13 rebounds, three steals and four blocks.

Dybantsa has hit a hot streak, averaging 19.2 points, 8.4 rebounds, 3.6 assists and 1.6 steals over the last five games, reaching the 20-point mark three times and securing two double-doubles in that stretch. He is shooting 44.3% from the field but just 23.8% from deep over the five games, which, although unremarkable, is still better than his season average of 42.3% from the field and 23.5% from deep.

The rookie has a lot of competition for the spotlight within the Wizards' lineup, as Trae Young, Anthony Davis, and Kyshawn George are leading the team in scoring. However, Dybantsa is still averaging the second-most shots per game on the team, with 14.4 attempts per outing. Moreover, he is doing well getting to the line, with 4.9 attempts per game, but he could still work on his efficiency in that area, as he is hitting 72.1%, for an average of 3.5 makes per outing. He is also doing a decent job of taking care of the ball, with an average of 2.3 turnovers in 34.0 minutes of action per outing. He should continue to develop more flow to his game and is already showing a positive trajectory towards being able to lead his team offensively.
