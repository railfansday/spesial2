Braden Mann’s 86-Yard Punt Rewrites Eagles Special Teams History

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Braden Mann kicked an 86-yard punt against the New York Giants on Sunday, marking the second-longest punt in the last 25 years. This single play put the Giants on their own six-yard line and shifted the momentum of the game. The punt occurred during Week 9 of the NFL season when the Philadelphia Eagles faced off in a defensive struggle. Fox Sports reported that this boot was the longest punt seen since 2001, ending a long dry spell for such massive kicks in the league.

The score at halftime was a tight 8-6 battle, showing just how hard both defenses fought before Mann struck. Head coach Nick Sirianni sent the punt team out for one of the final plays of the first half, and Mann delivered a perfect strike from his own end zone. After rolling out of bounds at the six-yard line, the ball first touched down near the 17-yard line with only seconds remaining in the second quarter. This specific distance ranks as the sixth-longest punt in the entire league since 1975, proving that Mann has a rare gift for distance.

Historical context makes this kick even more impressive when looking at the all-time records. The longest punt in history still belongs to Steve O'Neal, who kicked a 98-yarder with the New York Jets back in 1969. Just above Mann's recent effort was a punt by the New England Patriots' Luke Prestidge, who went 89 yards in 1984. Mann's 86-yard boot falls right between these two giants of the sport. Fox Sports noted that Mann's career-high before this game was a 72-yarder he punted in 2022 while playing for the New York Jets. This new distance exceeds his previous best by over 10 yards, a significant jump for any professional punter.

Mann is now 5-foot-11 and playing in his seventh season in the league, yet he just notched a career best that fans will talk about for years. However, this kick does not hold the record for the longest punt in Eagles history. Legendary quarterback Randall Cunningham earned that distinction with a 91-yard punt in 1989. Cunningham's record still stands, but Mann's performance on Sunday brings the team back into the conversation for the biggest kicks in franchise history. The sheer distance of the punt forced the Giants to start their next drive deep in their own territory, a nightmare scenario for any offense.

Special teams are often overlooked until a moment like this shifts the flow of a game. Since the Eagles and Giants engaged in a fierce defensive battle marked by low scoring, every yard gained on special teams became vital. Mann's ability to flip the field so drastically means the Giants' offense will have to work twice as hard to score points. This kick demonstrates that special teams can be a weapon just as potent as the offense or defense. Fox Sports highlighted that this was a wild stat for Week 9, proving that even in a league of thousands of players, one boot can define a game.

Since 1975, the 86-yard punt stands as the sixth-longest in the league.

The Eagles' big special-teams moment did not change the outcome. New York rallied after halftime behind backup quarterback Jameis Winston (who entered after J.J. McCarthy left with a hip injury) and won 19-11 at Lincoln Financial Field. Winston's 5-yard touchdown pass to Isaiah Likely in the third quarter, plus four field goals from Dominic Zvada, proved decisive. Philadelphia managed only a Jake Elliott field goal after the break and fell to 2-7, extending its losing streak to seven games. The Giants improved to 5-2-1 and snapped a long home drought against the Eagles in Philadelphia.

Mann's 86-yard punt remains the clearest highlight of a low-scoring, defensive struggle. It flipped the field late in the first half and forced New York to start deep in its own territory, but the Eagles' offense never built on the advantage. For a team searching for positives in a rough season, the career-long boot from Mann stands out as a rare bright spot on an otherwise quiet afternoon at the Linc.
