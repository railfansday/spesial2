McBurnie's late double stuns 10-man Brighton after Tzolakis heroics

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Hull City vs Brighton and Hove Albion. Premier League.

Report and free match highlights as Oli McBurnie's late double earns Hull a shock 2-0 Premier League victory over dominant Brighton following Costinha red card; Tigers goalkeeper Konstantinos Tzolakis produces string of fine saves to deny visitors before added-time goals

A Konstantinos Tzolakis goalkeeping masterclass teed up Oli McBurnie to score two late goals as Hull stunned dominant Brighton in a smash-and-grab 2-0 win at the MKM Stadium.

Like the legendary Greek mythological king, Tzolakis had the Midas touch throughout for the Tigers, making five first-half saves - the most by any goalkeeper all season - before McBurnie netted twice in added time to snatch a victory that had looked beyond Hull for so long.

High-flying Brighton had racked up an xG of 2.24 and five presentable chances but were denied by a combination of the goalkeeper's fine form and some wasteful finishing, and ultimately missed the chance to move three points off leaders Arsenal.

Tzolakis' highlight was a stunning one-handed stop to keep out Diego Gomez's 15th-minute effort, which set the tone for a perfect performance to frustrate the visitors.

He was also at his best to deny Georginio Rutter, after Yasin Ayari's shot had rebounded off the crossbar, before a superb stop with his trailing leg frustrated Gomez again moments before the interval.

"We can be critical of some of the misses but the goalkeeper has been fantastic," said Sky Sports' Roy Keane at the break. "He's man of the match already for me."

With the one-way traffic unabated after the restart, Tzolakis owed some credit to midfielder Regan Slater for keeping his clean sheet intact, nodding off the line when substitute Charalampos Kostoulas lifted the ball over the goalkeeper after he had given the ball away cheaply himself.

The hosts did threaten with Mohamed Belloumi twice cutting in off the right before wasting good positions, and Sorba Thomas almost netting a fortuitous goal when his cross bounced back off the crossbar.

But, after Brighton's Costinha was shown a second yellow card for a clumsy challenge late on, Hull saw their moment to strike, with McBurnie scrambling in an opener following a counter-attack before tapping home a second with the Seagulls pushing to equalise.

Having been dropped by Greece during the international break following a 7-0 hammering by Netherlands, Tzolakis provided the perfect response with an unstoppable performance between the sticks.

The 24-year-old has been a hit on Humberside since his summer move from Olympiakos but was at his very best to earn them a first home win since August.

It was his second entry into the top 10 of xG prevented in individual matches this season, the only goalkeeper to manage such a feat.

Statistically, he performed better across 90 minutes in Hull's 1-0 win away at Coventry early in the campaign - another where their opponents had dominated - but his individual saves against the high-flying Seagulls were even more impressive.

They lifted him into fifth place in the season rankings of over-performing goalkeepers, ahead of Premier League greats Alisson and David Raya.

Though the Greek stopper would never claim to be on their level - yet - Hull increasingly look like they have a gem on their hands nonetheless.

Hull City's double goalscorer Oli McBurnie to Sky Sports:

"He [Tzolakis] was unbelievable, the cat today. He has been all season for us to be fair to him. We give him a bit of stick about his gear and that, but he's been good in net.

"He was fantastic, kept us in the game and that's how we want to play. We know if we are always in games we fancy ourselves to get chances and make it as horrible as possible and that's what we did."

Hull goalkeeper Konstantinos Tzolakis to Sky Sports:

"So many saves but two goals from Oli. It was a team effort and everyone helped each other.

"We are a unit here like a team. We play for each other. We saw a very good team effort, and we deserved to take the three points.

"In the first half we suffered a lot but we managed the second half and scored in transition two goals from Oli and that's it."
