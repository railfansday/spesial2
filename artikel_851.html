Arsenal host Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League as Mikel Arteta’s side look to continue their unbeaten start, but the Gunners will do so with injured goalkeeper David Raya.

The Spaniard was forced off while holding his shoulder during Arsenal’s draw at Newcastle on Saturday but Arteta confirmed he does not need surgery in what is a huge boost to last season’s Champions League finalists.

One of Kepa Arrizabalaga and Illan Meslier will deputise as Arsenal enter a challenging stretch of fixtures that includes a meeting with Premier League rivals Manchester City on Sunday, followed by Brentford, Tottenham and Real Madrid.

Arsenal enter the week sitting third in the Champions League league phase, with three wins and a draw from their first four. Dortmund lie 12th, with three wins and one defeat, but the German side are top of the Bundesliga - two points ahead of Bayern Munich.

Arsenal host Dortmund at the Emirates Stadium, London on Tuesday 24 November, with kick-off at 8pm.

Arsenal return to Champions League action against Borussia Dortmund hoping to extend their unbeaten start to Europe’s premier competition this season.

The Gunners return to the Emirates after dropping points in the Premier League title race thanks to a Newcastle comeback at the weekend - although rivals Manchester City and Brighton both failed to capitalise and close the gap to the league leaders.

Dortmund are just one point behind them in the Champions League group stage rankings but nine places below, having got past Real Betis 2-1 in their last encounter, while the Gunners beat Slavia Prague by the same scoreline.

Arsenal and Dortmund have not faced off since 2014 - when Mikel Arteta wore the captain’s armband in a 2-0 Arsenal victory.
