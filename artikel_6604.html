The Halloween Candy That Matches Your Zodiac Sign, According To An Astrologer

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Whether you fully commit to spooky season or prefer to keep things low-key, you can’t truly celebrate Halloween without enjoying some candy. Different sweets have cultivated fierce brand loyalties. But the key to finding your favorite Halloween candy may be hiding among the stars.

We consulted astrologer and author Lisa Stardust to find which candy best reflects each zodiac sign's personality. From old-school chocolates to wild, wacky treats, her choices span the full sweet spectrum.

Aries is defined by its bold and ambitious approach to life. Stardust describes the cardinal fire sign as “passionate, electrifying, and intense.” But Aries can also be a bit impulsive. “You never know what you’re going to get next based on their emotions,” she adds.

In honor of Aries’ fiery spirit, Stardust chooses Hot Tamales as the candy that best reflects the sign. With its vibrant red hue, intense spice, and unapologetic cinnamon-forward flavor, there’s no better sweet to symbolize the sign.

Compared to Aries' bold energy, Taurus is decidedly more practical. The earth sign is known for being stable, dependable, and guided by logic over emotion. While Taurus may resist change, the sign also has good taste and knows its way around the finer things in life.

And what candy represents that tried-and-true energy better than a classic bar of Hershey’s milk chocolate? “They’re both iconic and never go out of style,” Stardust explains. “Their tastes might be simple, but they’re always decadent and indulgent.”

Gemini is the astrological embodiment of street smarts. The air sign is very astute, with the uncanny ability to read a room and adapt accordingly. This perceptiveness translates to strong social skills and the ability to elevate the mood of any room.

The candy that best reflects this energy is, unsurprisingly, Smarties. “Geminis are intellectual, and they have a little bit of zest to them,” explains Stardust. “They’re sugary and sweet but also a little messy.”

The cardinal water sign is not shy about expressing emotions. Cancers are best described as the teddy bears of the zodiac: sensitive, empathetic, and the embodiment of comfort. Those with Cancer in their chart are prone to shielding others from their complex emotions, but make no mistake—there are a lot of feelings.

A candy that matches Cancer’s mushy inner self is Reese’s Peanut Butter Cups. “They are very protective on the outside, but they're tender on the inside,” Stardust says.

Leo can’t help but be the life of the party. The fire sign is very extroverted, with a personality so big it verges on theatrical. Stardust describes Leos as “fun and flamboyant,” even if they sometimes get on other people’s nerves.

In her opinion, the sweets that represent Leo best are Twizzlers. Stardust notes that you “can have a lot of fun eating them,” whether it’s peeling them apart or biting off the ends to use as a straw. If it involves playing with your food, it’s perfect for Leo.

Virgos are Type A to their cores. The earth sign has a precise attention to detail and is always looking to solve problems. But the constant focus on logic and perfection often obscures a deep well of emotions.

“There’s more to them than you may think, and there’s definitely another flavor to them on the inside, because they’re ruled by Mercury,” Stardust explains. The candy that best mirrors this energy is a York Peppermint Patty. The seemingly simple chocolate exterior hides a sweet, sharp minty center (if that’s not Virgo, I don’t know what is).

Libra is represented by scales, which reflects the air sign’s desire for fairness and justice. The empathetic and compassionate Libra tends to consider all points of view to make the most measured decisions that’ll make everyone happy. (But sometimes, that may mean not making a decision at all.)

Libra’s balanced approach to life is why Stardust chooses Twix as their ideal candy. Left Twix or right Twix? Thankfully, Libra doesn’t have to choose. “They’re a package deal,” Stardust says. So whether they want to enjoy both or share with someone else, Libras are Twix’s target audience.

Scorpios embody the phrase, “looks like they could kill you, is a cinnamon roll.” The water sign is often misunderstood. While undoubtedly intense, Scorpio’s dark, almost brooding exterior hides a lot of emotional complexity.

The sign shares a lot of characteristics with one candy in particular: Sour Patch Kids. “They have a really deep personality to them; they’re sweet with a sharp, tangy bite.” And, like the multicolored gummy treats, Scorpio is the true manifestation of “sour, sweet, gone.”

Sagittarius is seemingly always on a journey—whether it be physical, emotional, or spiritual. The fire sign is known as an explorer, with lofty aspirations and an aversion to playing it safe.

This energy is exactly why Stardust chooses 3 Musketeers as the quintessential Sagittarius sweet. “The namesake is adventurous, and it speaks for itself,” she explains.

When you need someone reliable, responsible, and grounded, you can’t go wrong with a Capricorn. This pragmatic, logical earth sign is incredibly goal-oriented and has the Type A personality to make it happen.

How many licks does it take to get to the center of a Tootsie Pop? Capricorn may be the only sign in the zodiac that’s disciplined enough to find out, which is why Stardust chooses the lollipop as their ideal candy. “It’s a classic, just like Capricorn,” she says. “They’re traditional, steady, strong, and you have to have patience to eat them.”

Aquarius marches to the beat of its own drummer. The air sign loves to be a contrarian and will unapologetically take the road less traveled. Nothing is too unique or too strange for Aquarius.

And what candy matches that energy better than a bag of Pop Rocks? “They’re unexpected and veer from the norm of traditional candy,” Stardust explains. They’re shocking, surprising, and delightful in their own special way—just like Aquarius loves to be.

Pisces always seems to have its head in the clouds. The water sign has a dreamy, romantic approach to life and has an extremely vivid imagination. “They feel all the feels,” Stardust explains.

Pisces needs a candy that can keep up mentally and emotionally, which is why she chooses Skittles as their ideal sweet. “It’s all about the rainbow and the imagination,” Stardust says. “They get to taste the rainbow and all of the sensations in every bite.”
