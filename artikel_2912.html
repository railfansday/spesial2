Heat's Klay Thompson hopes to ‘beat up on old friends' Thursday vs. Warriors

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Heat's Klay Thompson hopes to 'beat up on old friends' Thursday vs. Warriors originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area

Every player loves to show out when they return to their old home.

Miami Heat guard Klay Thompson is no exception, and after dropping a season-high 30 points Wednesday night against the Los Angeles Clippers, he looks to keep it rolling on back-to-back nights against his former team at Chase Center.

"Hopefully I can continue to stay in flow because I made a lot of buckets in that building," Thompson said Wednesday in a postgame interview with WPLG 10's Kacy Hintz. "It's fun to beat up on old friends so that would be awesome to come away with a back-to-back win."

Thompson was the hero in the Heat's overtime win over the Clippers, draining a 3-pointer from the corner with one second remaining while Miami trailed by two points.

It was his eighth 3-pointer of the night, marking his 25th career game with eight or more 3-pointers made, the third-most in NBA history. It was the first time he's reached the mark in a game since leaving Golden State.

Thompson is on a heater, scoring at least 19 points in each of his last three games.

In seven career games against the Warriors, Thompson has averaged 15.3 points per game on 42.7 percent shooting from the field. He looks to improve that average Thursday night at Chase Center.

"I know the Warriors are playing real well, so it's going to be a really good challenge tomorrow night," Thompson told reporters Wednesday night in the Heat locker room. "Always going to be awesome seeing all the guys I grew up with, and it's just going to be a special night. I only get to go there once this season, so I'm going to soak it all up."

Thompson isn't the only former Warrior who will play Thursday for the Heat at Chase Center. Andrew Wiggins will play his fourth game against Golden State since the team traded him away in the 2024-25 season.

Warriors coach Steve Kerr is looking forward to greeting both back to the Bay.

It will be great to see both guys, Klay and Wiggs…" Kerr told reporters during a media availability Wednesday after practice. "Two just incredible contributors to the Warriors' championships, to the culture, to just the vibe of the team. Both Klay and Wiggs are wonderful human beings and great teammates."

Unlike Thompson, Wiggins is struggling to score early in the season for the Heat. His 9.4 points per game is a career low through the first eight games of a season.

The Warriors hope that the once-friendly confines for Thompson and Wiggins will not give the two a spark as Golden State looks for its sixth consecutive victory.
