Rams vs Cardinals: How to watch on TV, stream, or listen on radio

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Week 10 has the 5-4 Los Angeles Rams taking the short hop over the state line for a Sunday November 15 meeting with the 3-6 Arizona Cardinals. Kickoff from State Farm Stadium in Glendale, Arizona is set for 1:05 p.m. PST. L.A. looks to avenge a humiliating Week 6 18-16 defeat to the Cards. In that tilt, Arizona used a balanced run/pass attack to control the game flow, to the tune of a 35-25 minute advantage in time of possession.

Injuries have decimated the Rams wide receiver corps, Puka Nacua (WR#1) and Davante Adams (WR#2) are on the Injured Reserve List, as is backup (120 offensive reps) and kickoff returner Jordan Whittington. The Rams will fill this void with Tutu Atwell, Konata Mumpfield, rookie CJ Daniels, Xavier Smith, and Brennan Presley, who have a combined total of 18 catches on 36 targets for 261 yards and one score in 2026.

With the state of the receiver room in flux, the Rams should look to run early and often in Arizona. Kyren Williams and Blake Corum have given L.A. a solid inside/outside rush attack. Williams, working primarily between the tackles, has shown an innate ability to make the first tackler miss and create yards after contact. Corum effectuates his yards off tackle, bursting through the slightest of openings and showing the acceleration to break runs outside and scoot in open field. The Rams offensive line is #1 in ESPN's run block win rate. Right tackle Warren McClendon (#2) and center Coleman Shelton (#5) are amongst the NFL Top 5 in this category.

Location: State Farm Stadium in Glendale, Arizona

In past seasons, a live stream was available on the following platforms if the game is available on TV in the local market. Unsure if this is available in 2026, for preseason or regular.

For the entire season in general, Rams fans can also stream, but are not limited to: NFL+, NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube, Peacock, Paramount+, and many others.
