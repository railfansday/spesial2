Rivals' Nafessa Williams Breaks Silence on Decreased Season 2 Screen Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Rivals star Nafessa Williams addressed the offscreen demand for her to have more screen time after her character, Cameron, appeared to have a decreased role in season 2.

“Oh, it feels good [to hear about the support]. I haven’t seen much. I have seen some, like, ‘Where is Cameron? We want to see more of Cameron,'” Williams, 40, exclusively told Us about the public outcry over her character appearing in fewer scenes. “As an artist, you are being really sensitive about your stuff so to see it be loved and appreciated [is meaningful].”

Williams reflected on how she has found herself connecting with Cameron.

“We work really hard as artists — and for me in particular — this isn’t the easiest job for me. Being away from home, there’s so much that I can relate to with Cameron, and I understand her on so many levels,” she explained. “Being a foreigner, being an outsider and being the only Black cast member. These are real life things that I actually can genuinely say that I embody. I understand it’s my reality as much as it is hers.”

Based on Jilly Cooper‘s novel of the same name, Rivals follows a feud between two men fighting for control of a television station in 1980s Britain. Cameron, who was introduced in season 1, quickly gets involved through her past affair with Tony (David Tennant), her eventual romance with Rupert (Alex Hassell) and her professional drive, which initially put her at odds with Declan (Aidan Turner).

Cameron was front and center when the series originally premiered in 2025. By the time season 2 returned earlier this year, eagle-eyed audience members noticed that having such a large ensemble cast led to Cameron being featured — to their frustration.

“It’s just really nice to see the love and to feel it. Thank you for sharing that with me,” Williams added. “That’s just for Disney and Hulu and everybody to hear. I don’t know how that’ll go possibly in season 3, I think Cameron speaks not just for Black women though but for all women. There’s so many women who are so inspired by her.”

As a result, Williams is hoping Cameron’s story will keep getting explored, adding, “It’s kind of interesting and cool to see how she’s affecting so many different women. She transcends not just color but age too. There’s teenagers who come up to me and they talk about how empowering and how inspiring she is for them. That feels pretty damn good because as an artist, you want to pick roles that matter, that means something, and that move people where people can see themselves.”

She continued: “If you can transcend all of those different variables then that’s the kind of role that I want to be attached to. And Cameron is that right? So who knows? Maybe more Cameron in season 3.”
