Hello, UCLA: Bruins make statement in upset of Arizona to start season

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

If last season taught us anything, the best way to kick start a run to the Final Four is to make an opening-month statement in Las Vegas.

UCLA would like to make the claim this time around.

The Bruins started the college basketball season with the most impressive outing of opening night, notching the first top five upset of the season in a 84-75 win over No. 4 Arizona in the Sin City.

An old school Pac-12 battle, this was framed to be as another big out for Arizona, playing in the pseudo-McKale Center North to take over T-Mobile Arena and begin another run to the Final Four. UCLA was an unknown, with no knowledge of how it would attack in what could be a slugfest or high-speed affair.

It ended up being both − and UCLA punched the Wildcats right in the mouth.

The versatility of the Bruins shined in every way, showcasing a range of depth Mick Cronin has spent his entire tenure searching for. The new leader, Trent Perry, was a true floor general commanding the offense and taking matters into his own hands when needed. Serbian newcomer Nikola Kusturica wasn't stellar, but showcased a range of skills that will only get better. Texas Tech transfer Jaylen Petty was the sharpshooter.

In total, UCLA had nine guys score, getting contributions from every one that came in.

Defensively, UCLA had no answer for Ivan Kharchenkov, Arizona's Energizer bunny who had a career-high 38 points and propelled a near second half comeback.

However, that was the only thing going for the Wildcats.

UCLA made every other Arizona player work hard, seemingly shocking a team made with a great blend of experience and freshman star power. Big 12 preseason player of the year Motiejus Krivas was collecting rebounds but had limited opportunities at scoring, held to just eight points with the Bruins' hard-nosed defense focused on limiting his impact near the basket. Freshman Caleb Holt had seven points and four missed 3-pointers.

There was nothing flukey about it. UCLA controlled this one, leading for the final 33 minutes of the game.

Cronin tasted the Final Four with an unlikely run as a No. 11 seed in 2021, and has been chasing that high ever since, ending in extreme disappointment every year since then. It's taken longer than desired, but this is a squad having its coach, known for his hot-head, all smiles.

"We're more athletic than we've been," Cronin said postgame on the TNT broadcast. "I've not had a bad day coaching this team."

It's coincidental UCLA took down Arizona considering one year ago the Wildcats did the same thing when they beat defending champion Florida in the same arena to open the season. A few weeks later, Michigan won the Player's Era Festival in dominant fashion in Las Vegas.

Those two ended up in the Final Four, with the Wolverines winning it all.
