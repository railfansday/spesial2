Cowboys, Titans trade early punches in 6-3 game

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The game between the Titans and Cowboys figures to include plenty of points. So far, the teams have scored nine.

Dallas scored a touchdown on a one-yard run from tailback Javonte Williams to cap a 14-play, 73-yard drive. The Cowboys went for two and failed.

Tennessee answered with a nine-play, 45-yard drive that stalled out deep in Dallas territory. Joey Slye's 38-yard field goal cut the margin to 6-3.

The Cowboys are driving again. And it could be like that all day, with both teams in the 30s (or maybe the 40s — again) by the time it's over.

Unless, of course, new defensive play-caller Mike Pettine can do better than the guy he replaced.
