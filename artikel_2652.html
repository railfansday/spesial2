Steelers bench Aaron Rodgers, name Mason Rudolph starting QB vs. Broncos

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Pittsburgh Steelers have named Mason Rudolph the starting quarterback vs. the Denver Broncos in their Week 12 game on Black Friday, the team announced in a statement Tuesday night.

The Steelers' passing game took a massive step backward over the last two weeks, with Aaron Rodgers' play actively hurting the offense. In that span, Rodgers ranks 26th in success rate and the Steelers have failed to score 20 points.

On the season, Rodgers ranks 35th in EPA per play (min. 25 dropbacks). He has thrown 16 touchdowns to six interceptions.

Rudolph's last start came in 2025 against the Chicago Bears in relief of an injured Rodgers. Rudolph went 24-of-31 for 171 yards, a touchdown, and an interception in a 31-28 loss. He finished the game the previous week when Rodgers broke his wrist, throwing a touchdown in a 34-12 win over the Bengals.
