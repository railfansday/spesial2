‘My stance is...’: Patrick Queen shares what his relationship with Aaron Rodgers is like now

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Aaron Rodgers situation has made this a very difficult week for the Pittsburgh Steelers.

The quarterback fell out with coach Mike McCarthy, then went on the Pat McAfee Show and had a go at him before being released by the organisation.

Rodgers was a very popular teammate inside the locker room, so there have been theories on how the players would react to his behaviour following his exit.

For Patrick Queen, however, there is no difference.

When there is a big split like in the case of Rodgers and McCarthy in the locker room, people would usually take sides.

However, it looks like the Steelers have handled things brilliantly.

On Friday, Pittsburgh produced one of their best performances of the season to beat the Denver Broncos 27-5. They were absolutely amazing.

However, while speaking to the media after the game, several players showed their support for both parties. Queen is one such player, and he has spoken about his relationship with Rodgers now.

He told Jenna Harner: "My stance is not going to change against Aaron. I love Aaron to death. Probably one of the coolest teammates I've ever had, just from the few conversations that we had.

"My stance is not going to change on that. I don't think anybody's stance has changed on that."

That is the perfect way to handle the situation. Nobody should hate Rodgers because of the way he left, and at the same time, nobody should turn their back on coach McCarthy either.

The Steelers showed on the field against the Broncos that they are fully behind their coach, and their relationship with Rodgers remains unaffected as well.

The saga is well and truly over now, and it's time for everyone to move on.
