Round Up: The Reviews Are In For Pikmin 4 - Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Following the anticipated return of Zelda: Ocarina of Time last week, Nintendo has now released Pikmin 4 - Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy. The critic reviews have now gone live. If you haven't already seen ours, we gave it seven stars, calling it a pleasant but perplexing piecemeal.

"Overall, £8/$10 is a small price to pay for some minor new features and a fun little mode that'll keep me entertained over the coming year, but it's more of a budding shoot right now than the full-bloom upgrade."

Mini Review Pikmin 4 - Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy (Switch 2) - Pleasant But Perplexingly Piecemeal

And here's what other critics had to say:

"For late-bloomers who have yet to experience the magic that is Pikmin 4, the Nintendo Switch 2 Edition is the best place to start. Not only is native 4K resolution a significant improvement that allows the colourful world of Pikmin to shine brighter than ever before, Dandori Academy brings some worthwhile new content, held back only by Nintendo’s puzzling monthly stage distribution method. While not quite as extravagant as some of the other Nintendo Switch 2 Editions we’ve seen in recent memory, the lower-end $10 for the Upgrade Pack is an appropriate price point for what Pikmin 4 has to offer on Nintendo Switch 2; with the base game being in a class of its own."

"The main takeaway is that Pikmin 4 is still excellent, and I was grateful to transfer my save file from the Switch version (and I was tempted to start a new game + save), but Dandori Academy is not a strong incentive to return if you already played the game. It is a nice bonus and an interesting concept that reimagines the familiar time trial mode featured in plenty of video games, but the original campaign is still the main attraction."

"Pikmin 4 still remains one of my favourite titles released on the original Nintendo Switch, but unfortunately this update has left me feeling more than a little underwhelmed. While the game has seen a visual upgrade and the Oatchi commands are certainly a novel addition, the drip-feeding of its major new Dandori Academy content proves more frustrating than anything. More Pikmin is never a bad thing, but having to wait months to see it all, well… that’s a drag."

"Pikmin 4 is already a great time; Pikmin 4 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy is just the 4K cherry on top. If you don’t own the original Switch version, then this is an easy recommendation; if you’re here for the upgrade/additions, then the visual upgrade will help. Dandori Academy won’t be for everyone, but over the next year it will continue to add extra challenges. The cheaper upgrade price point helps make it an easier recommendation."
