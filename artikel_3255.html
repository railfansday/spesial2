Management lowered full-year guidance for adjusted EBITDA and RevPAR to reflect the occupancy carryover and the impact of a fire at a Mississippi community. The company is executing a significant portfolio transformation, shifting toward an 80% ownership model to reduce lease expense and capture future economic upside. Management stated that the organizational realignment and new leadership in sales and operations are nearly complete, allowing the company to focus exclusively on occupancy growth as its primary operational priority for 2027.
