Obscured Releasing Picks Up North American Distribution for Finland Oscar Entry ‘The Squirrel’ (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Obscured Releasing has picked up North American distribution rights to “The Squirrel,” written and directed by Markus Lehmusruusu.The film reps Finland in the international feature Oscar race.

In the futuristic drama, Pasi, a designer of artificial animals, encounters a living squirrel, and the unexpected discovery begins to disrupt his carefully ordered world. Blending dark humor, science fiction and an unexpectedly tender perspective, the film explores humanity’s longing for nature, freedom and genuine connection. The film premiered internationally at Fantastic Fest in Austin and went on to receive the Grand Jury Prize in the International Feature Film Competition at Utopiales, the international science-fiction festival in Nantes, France.

“Markus Lehmusruusu transports us to a future world in ‘The Squirrel’ which, on the surface, is as cold and sterile as those in the most significant sci-fi films such as ‘2001: A Space Odyssey’ and ‘Gattaca,’ ” said Obscured founders RJ Millard and Bill Guentzler in a statement. “In his future, he re-introduces a sneaky, furry burst of warmth in a long forgotten animal which has the impact of reconnecting humanity to nature and can unleash the animal instincts inside all of us.”

Produced by BUFO, the film is a co-production between Finland, Germany, Netherlands, and Sweden. It stars Miro Lopperi, Mimosa Willamo, Hannu-Pekka Björkman, Roderick Kabanga, Pirjo Heikkilä, Eero Ritala, Ella Mettänen and Pirjo Lonka.

The deal was negotiated by Peter Ahlén of REinvent Yellow Sales on behalf of the filmmakers and Bill Guentzler and RJ Millard of Obscured Releasing.
