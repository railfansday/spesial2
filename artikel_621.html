Brad Pitt, Angelina Jolie Call It Quits on Years-long Legal Saga, Settle Case That Led to Abuse Claims

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

After the 'Cliff Booth' star sued in 2022 to stop Jolie from selling her stake in winery Château Miraval, the actress revealed accusations of abuse during an altercation on a private plane.

Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie have settled a long-running dispute over the sale of their French winery, a lawsuit that opened the door for the actress to accuse her ex-husband of abusing her and their children.

Both sides on Wednesday moved to dismiss claims against each other. Terms of the settlement weren’t disclosed.

“We have settled out of court in the best interests of our family and will not be commenting further,” said representatives for Jolie and Pitt in a statement. “Any comments in media stories attributed to ‘sources’ are in fact not coming from either of us, or anyone close to us.”

The couple purchased the 1,200-acre winery in the South of France where they married, Château Miraval, in 2008. Jolie planned to sell her half to Pitt in the wake of their divorce, court filings alleged, until negotiations broke down over his insistence that she sign a nondisclosure agreement barring her from publicizing allegations of abuse leading up to their split.

Jolie balked at the ask and instead struck a $64 million deal with billionaire alcohol magnate Yuri Shefler for her half of the winery. Pitt in 2022 filed a lawsuit, claiming that the actress reneged on an unwritten agreement that they wouldn’t sell their stakes in the business without the other’s consent. That led to a countersuit from Jolie publicizing for the first time abuse allegations against Pitt in a 2016 altercation on a private plane in which she claimed that the actor “grabbed her by the head and shook her,” “choked one of the children” and “struck another in the face.”

At the time, a representative for Pitt denied the allegations. Jolie “continues to rehash, revise and reimagine her description of an event that happened 6 years ago by adding completely untrue information each time she fails to get what she wants,” the spokesperson said. “Her story is constantly evolving.”

Wednesday’s settlement ends a contentious case that peeled back the origins of Pitt and Jolie’s divorce and hostility between the former couple. Pitt continues to pursue claims against Shefler, who allegedly orchestrated the acquisition in secret alongside Jolie, and Shefler’s company Tenute del Mondo, the wine arm of the Stoli Group. The actor seeks a court order allowing him to purchase Jolie’s shares in Miraval at the same valuation Shefler acquired them, plus unspecified damages.

A state appeals court earlier this year found that Shefler played a vital role in structuring and finalizing the deal to purchase Miraval. It pointed to him guaranteeing $39 million of the purchase using his own funds, a provision he wouldn’t agree to if he wasn’t involved in the agreement. Shefler sent three letters to Jolie over the course of the transaction, according to court filings. One thanked her for accepting his offer and assured her that a deal would be made “despite personal complications.” Another asked Jolie to postpone paying an installment on the purchase due to complications with Pitt.

Since the allegations became public in 2022, Pitt has continued starring in major studio productions, like F1 and Heart of the Beast, maintaining his dual-standing as one of Hollywood’s most bankable leading men and a prolific producer of prestige film through Plan B Entertainment. Jolie, meanwhile, momentarily took a step back from acting before earning critical acclaim for her portrayal as Maria Callas in Maria.

In her cross complaint, Jolie detailed a physical altercation with Pitt, with him on one side and her and their children on the other.

“Pitt lunged at his own child and Jolie grabbed him from behind to stop him,” the complaint stated. “To get Jolie off his back, Pitt threw himself backwards into the airplane’s seats injuring Jolie’s back and elbow. The children rushed in and all bravely tried to protect each other.”

At one point, Pitt poured beer on Jolie; at another, he poured beer and wine on the children, the lawsuit alleged. Once the flight landed, Jolie claimed Pitt pushed her when she told him that she and the children were going to a hotel. “He also grabbed and shook Jolie by the head and shoulders, causing one of the children to beg, ‘Don’t hurt her,’” the complaint stated. “He let Jolie go, but then called her a ‘bitch,’ before adding, ‘Fuck you, fuck you all.’” Jolie filed for divorce five days later.

The FBI investigated the 2016 altercation but declined to press charges. A redacted report on the case stated that the investigating agent found there was probable cause to pursue a criminal case but that charges would not be filed due to “several factors,” according to The New York Times. It described Jolie as being “conflicted on whether or not to be supportive of charges.”

Pitt is represented by John Berlinski and Julia Cherlow, partners at Bird Marella, while Jolie was represented by Paul Murphy and Daniel Csillag. Alejandro Brito, who handles media lawsuit for President Trump, represents Shefler.
