Billy Ray Cyrus on Miley’s Wedding: ‘I’m the Proudest Dad Alive’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The singer posted his own images from the Palm Springs ceremony, where his daughter married Maxx Morando on Saturday.

Billy Ray Cyrus has shared a series of photos from daughter Miley Cyrus’ wedding to Maxx Morando, with the “Achy Breaky Heart” singer posting publicly about the ceremony for the first time. Explore Billy Ray Cyrus Miley Cyrus See latest videos, charts and news

“My little girl married the love of her life! I can’t describe how beautiful it felt to have all my family together in one room, united by love,” he wrote on Instagram. “Words can’t begin to express how proud you make me every day. Addressing Morando directly, he added: “I’m so honoured to welcome you to the family. The two of you kids are a match made in heaven.”

The post confirmed that his fiancée, Elizabeth Hurley, attended along with her son Damian Hurley, who also shared images of the lavish nuptials.

Miley married Morando on Saturday (Nov. 21) at the Frank Sinatra House in Palm Springs, Calif., in a poolside ceremony officiated by Wayne Coyne of the Flaming Lips, who collaborated with her on 2015’s Miley Cyrus & Her Dead Petz.

She shared her own images on Monday, her 34th birthday, including one of her mother, Tish Cyrus-Purcell, walking her down the aisle and another of her dancing with her father at the reception. “Waking up on my 34th birthday feeling so grateful,” she wrote. “Thank you for all the wishes and to everyone who made this weekend a memory we’ll cherish for a lifetime and beyond.”

Father and daughter share a long musical history. Billy Ray broke through in 1992 with “Achy Breaky Heart,” which topped Billboard’s Hot Country Songs chart and reached No. 4 on the Billboard Hot 100, and returned to the top of the Hot 100 in 2019 alongside Lil Nas X on the “Old Town Road” remix, which spent a record 19 weeks at No. 1. He played Miley’s on-screen dad on Disney Channel’s Hannah Montana, and the pair dueted on 2007’s “Ready, Set, Don’t Go.”

Miley has since scored two Billboard Hot 100 No. 1s of her own, with “Wrecking Ball” and “Flowers,” the latter earning her first Grammy wins, for record of the year and best pop solo performance. Her latest era has extended that chart run: “Bass Persuades” debuted at No. 35 on the Hot 100, becoming her 24th top 40 hit, while the album of the same name opened at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 with 61,000 equivalent album units — her sixth chart-topper and first since Bangerz in 2013. The set followed 2025’s Something Beautiful, which debuted and peaked at No. 4.

The post marks a shift for a family whose tensions have played out publicly. Miley and Billy Ray were estranged for years following his 2022 divorce from Tish Cyrus, and he did not attend her Hollywood Walk of Fame ceremony in May, though she referenced him in her speech. Miley confirmed last year that they were no longer estranged, saying she had learned to respect her parents as individuals, and Billy Ray publicly celebrated her engagement in December 2025.

Morando, 28, is the drummer for Los Angeles band Liily. He and Miley began dating in 2021 and became engaged in December 2025. It marks the singer’s second marriage; she was previously married to Liam Hemsworth from December 2018 until their separation the following August.
