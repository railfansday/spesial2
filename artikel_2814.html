Joe Burrow named AFC offensive player of the week for second time this season

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow is the betting favorite to be the NFL MVP this season and games like the one he had last Sunday are the reason why he's in that spot.

Burrow lit up the Steelers during a 38-18 win that extended Cincinnati's winning streak to five games. Burrow was 35-of-44 for 409 yards and four touchdowns in the victory.

The four touchdowns moved him up to 20 over the course of the winning streak and he leads the league with 27 touchdown passes over the entire season.

The NFL announced on Wednesday that Burrow has been named the AFC offensive player of the week. It's the second time he's won that prize this season and the 10th time in his career.
