Remembering Denver Broncos legend Tom Jackson

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Broncos Country woke to the news of the legendary Tom Jackson's passing. He was 75.

For those of us old enough, he is remembered as one of the stalwart members of the Orange Crush defense of the late 70s. He played for the Denver Broncos from 1973 to 1986.

The issued the following statement this morning:

"The Denver Broncos are heartbroken by the passing of Ring of Fame linebacker Tom Jackson, a cornerstone of the legendary Orange Crush defense and one of the most beloved players in franchise history.

"An inspirational leader and fierce competitor, TJ was a proud member of the Broncos' first Super Bowl team that helped establish a winning tradition in Denver. Off the field, Tom carried himself with class, warmth and humility, forging deep connections and lifelong friendships with teammates and throughout Broncos Country.

"TJ's dynamic personality, quick wit and football knowledge made him a natural for television and paved the way for a Hall of Fame broadcasting career. For generations of fans, Tom was an iconic fixture on ESPN's Sunday NFL Countdown and NFL Primetime, sharing his thoughtful insight with an unmistakable sense of humor that defined an era of sports broadcasting.

"Above all, TJ loved the Broncos and was a cherished member of our family for more than 50 years. We extend our deepest sympathies to Tom's wife, Jennifer, his daughters, Taylor and Morgan, and all of his family, friends and teammates."

He played in 191 career games, which still ranks fourth in team history. He was a two-time All-Pro and a three-time Pro Bowler. He finished his career with 1,531 total tackles, 44 sacks, 20 interceptions, eight fumble recoveries, and three touchdowns. He was a sole addition to the Broncos' Ring of Fame in 1992 — his first year of eligibility for the honor.

For those not quite old enough for the playing days, Tom Jackson had a second career that may have been more impactful on the youth in America. In 1987, he teamed up with Chris Berman on Sunday NFL Countdown and NFL Primetime that went on to win seven Sports Emmy awards over the decades.

As kids, many of us would find the only way to catch the weekend's action was through their highlight show. Many a fond childhood memory exists there, I am sure.

The team has announced they will be holding a pregame moment of silence and a video tribute to honor Tom Jackson before today's game against the Las Vegas Raiders. Quite the fitting moment for a man who once taunted John Madden after a 30-7 beatdown in Oakland in 1977 by yelling, "It's all over fat man!" from across the field.

Tom Jackson, 1951-2026. Gone, but forever remembered.
