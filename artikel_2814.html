Inside the looming chess match between Kyle Shanahan and Brian Flores this weekend

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The San Francisco 49ers are back in primetime on Sunday night, as they're taking on the Minnesota Vikings in a battle between Top NFC contenders.

San Francisco, sitting at 8-1, is coming off a 46-40 win over the Dallas Cowboys, where its offense was unstoppable, while the defense had a ton of holes. Minnesota, on the other hand, just traded for Kirk Cousins at the trade deadline and blew out the Green Bay Packers in his first game back.

While Minnesota's offense has raised questions this year, it's the defense that has remained elite under coordinator Brian Flores, who has continued to find different ways for his defense to excel. This year, it's with a blitz-heavy approach.

Flores has been able to neutralize teams all year long. No team has scored more than 25 points against the Vikings, and that lofty defense was what propelled Minnesota while its offense was struggling.

Flores has had head coach Kyle Shanahan's number in the chess match between play-callers over the past few years. Regardless of the circumstances of the previous matchups, this is a perfect opportunity for Shanahan and the 49ers to establish themselves in many ways. It would continue to provide a boost in the NFC race, while also giving Shanahan another game to bolster his Coach of the Year case.

But Shanahan acknowledged the difficulty that Flores and his game plan will provide on Sunday.

"He just, he does it. He's more unique than all the other 31 guys," Shanahan said of Flores. "He's the one guy who commits to this and it's not just a fad for these nine games or whatever. He's been doing it his whole time in Minnesota. He did the same thing in Miami. He's as good as it gets at that stuff. Anybody who lives in that world as much as he does, it's usually a matter of time.

"I think offensive coaches used to like to say that early on, but it's too many years of it, and it's too many games in this year that they're sound. They put so much pressure on the quarterback, the play caller, the O-Line, the receivers, on everybody. A lot of times, when you go into an NFL game, the pressure's really on people on third down and stuff like that because those are things where everything can come and when you play against Brian, it's 70-straight plays of that."

One position that Flores's defenses have done really well to neutralize is the tight end position because of how much the Vikings blitz, forcing another blocker to stay in-line. That's critical with Mike Evans currently not trending to play, leaving George Kittle as the team's top weapon.

But overall, this game will be a chess match between the two brilliant minds who have arguably been the best play callers in the NFL this season. And there's a lot on the line on both sides with the NFC race heating up.
