49ers game grades: Put it on the fridge, Niners ace test, ice the Vikings

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The San Francisco 49ers came into their high-altitude confrontation against Minnesota in Mexico City with questions about whether their offense had been clicking against sub-par defenses, and whether their defense could stop a Vikings offense rejuvenated by the addition of QB Kirk Cousins. Their 35-10 takedown of Minnesota should put both questions to rest.

The Vikings came into the game vaunted as one of the best defensive teams in the league; they left it as one of the best teams in football at stopping any NFL opponent but the 49ers. At the half, Brock Purdy was 12-for-15 with three touchdown passes (two to George Kittle) and 176 yards. Mike Evans looked anything but rusty in his return to action, snagging six catches on seven targets and a touchdown. Christian McCaffrey's cutback dance into the endzone at the start of the fourth quarter completed a spirit-killing 98-yard drive that chewed up more than 7 minutes, a nice echo of Kyle Shanahan's past successes. That McCaffrey score also made the 49ers 4-for-4 on their red-zone opportunities on the night, continuing their stretch as the NFL's best team when it comes to scoring once they get inside the 20.

Initial suspicions that Cousins might tear up the 49ers with the advantage of seeing them a second time in three weeks, surrounded by a better team, ended up being unfounded. Despite being on the field for 65 plays and the absence of Dre Greenlaw, the 49ers' defenders returned to their earlier bend-not-break ways while delivering big plays. The highlight was Ji'Ayir Brown's second-quarter pick-six that turned the game from a contest to a romp, followed by Deommodore Lenoir's stripping Justin Jefferson for a fumble recovery on the Vikings' first possession in the second half. Before coming out early with the game in hand, Fred Warner delivered a big performance that had some commentators noting he'd delivered a highlight reel for his case for NFL Defensive Player of the Year.

It would be trite to say they did no harm, but punter Corliss Waitman helped the 49ers win the field-position game, Eddy Piñeiro connected on all five of his extra-point opportunities, and the coverage teams kept dangerous Vikings return man Myles Price from making a difference.

The challenge for Shanahan and his staff of going up against an elite defense coached by Vikings defensive coordinator Brian Flores – who had never lost to a Shanahan-coached team – was answered handily, as Purdy & Co. repeatedly created and exploited openings. Raheem Morris' defensive unit was aggressive in attacking and anticipating, shutting down an opponent that had the weapons to score, while creating pressure despite the Vikings' elite offensive line.

The season started with a big test against the Rams; the 49ers won that game handily. In Week 5, it was the Seahawks; the 49ers eked out a road win. This was the biggest test yet, against a team vying for the top spot in the NFC, and Shanahan's team delivered what might have been its most satisfying one-sided romp of the season. The only sour note was Colton McKivitz's devastating injury in the first half, a reminder that the only thing that might trip this team up is injuries.
