Allegany girls seek first region title in four years

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

CUMBERLAND — Allegany has fielded some quality teams over the past few seasons, but it's been four years since the Campers have won a region title.

The Campers have a chance to break the dry spell when they host Northern Garrett for the Class 1A West Region I final Wednesday at 5 p.m. at Greenway Avenue Stadium.

The Campers (12-2-1) are searching for their first region title since 2022 when they were eliminated in the state semifinals after a 2-1 overtime loss to South Carroll.

Since then, Allegany has lost in the region semifinals twice (2023 and 2025) and fell to Northern in the 2024 final.

"I just need to drill into these girls' heads that the next game you lose, you're done," Allegany coach Andy Farrell said after his team beat Southern Friday. "So we just need to come out and play 80 minutes at all times, and then we'll be OK."

The No. 2 seed Campers are coming off a come-from-behind 4-3 win over No. 3 Southern in the region semifinals, avenging last season's loss in the same stage.

The Rams went up 2-0 early before the Campers tied it in the first few minutes of the second half.

Southern scored less than a minute later to make it 3-2 before Madalyn Bucy tied it again and Ally Ricker scored the game-winner with 22:35 left in the game.

"We've been stressing to all of our defensive midfielders, our outside backs, shoot the ball," Farrell said.

"We're a very possession-style team, and when you do that, you start missing your opportunities from 20 or 30 yards out.

"So we stressed all week, put the ball in the air, put it up high, and that's exactly what she did. I think the placement was about as perfect as you could have. I don't know if you could write that script any better."

"The first half showed us that we gotta come out stronger," Ricker said. "I feel like when we came out in the second half, we looked a lot better and we connected a lot better."

Allegany swept Northern in the regular season, but both wins came in dramatic fashion.

In the teams' first meeting Sept. 21, Northern (8-5-1) took a 2-1 lead a few minutes into the second half before Kyla Farris tied it up.

Desirae Hilton's goal with about 12 minutes left gave the Campers a 3-2 win.

Bucy scored with 38 seconds left in double overtime to give Allegany a 1-0 win in the teams' second matchup Oct. 7.

Bucy is the Campers' leading scorer with 37 goals and her 13 assists are second on the team.

Ricker has contributed seven goals, while Harper Farrell has scored six times and leads the team with 20 assists.

Goalie Brilynn Beatty has four shutouts and 100 saves on the season.

Ainsley McHenry-Sorber has more than half of Northern's total goals and assists with 28 goals and 13 assists.

Freshman Dorthy Hertel has nine goals, while sophomore Jenna Bowser has five goals and six assists.

Huskies goalie Veda Rebuck has made 110 saves.

Northern is coming off a 2-1 upset over top seed Mountain Ridge in the semifinals.

The winner of Wednesday's game will play in the state quarterfinals Friday or Saturday.
