Kyle Shanahan: Brock Purdy's ankle sprain is &quot;more mid than low&quot;

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

49ers quarterback Brock Purdy emerged from Sunday's win over the Vikings with an ankle injury. He'll miss Sunday's game against the Seahawks. Whether he'll miss any games beyond that remains to be seen.

"It's too soon, but I'm pretty optimistic about it," coach Kyle Shanahan told reporters on Friday. "But, I was about this week to start and then it changes, so hopefully it'll be all right."

Next Sunday, the 49ers visit the Giants. The following weekend, the Rams visit.

Shanahan also revealed some details about the specific nature of the ankle sprain.

"Anytime it's not a high ankle sprain, you get pretty relieved, but it was more mid than low, which is a little bit worse," Shanahan said. "Kind of had a feeling just the way it was by the time Wednesday came around. Didn't heal up as fast as we wanted, so had to shut him down."

Shanahan added that surgery isn't on the table for Purdy.

Purdy's performance in 2026 has made him one of the favorites to win the NFL MVP award.
