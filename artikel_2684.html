MSSU women's basketball takes town Northwestern Oklahoma 72-58

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Missouri Southern State University's women's basketball team notched a comfortable 72-58 win over Northwestern Oklahoma State University Saturday at home.

The Rangers (2-2) scored first and each team put up 5 points quickly before both defenses settled in to keep things quiet.

About halfway through the first quarter, Southern's Jayla Yonkers found herself hitting a 3 before getting another 2 points.

A pair of free throws for Northwestern and a layup for MSSU kept the gap the same at 12-7 in Southern's favor. That gap would close to 4 points by the end of the first quarter as both teams traded scores, with Southern remaining up, 15-11.

Grace Frazier scored first in the second quarter with a 3-point shot. The Lions rode that momentum through and finished with a 9-3 run to go into half up 35-24.

The opening of the third quarter saw Ashlyn Alloway get open to score 2. MSSU followed that with a 10-2 run.

A Hattie Pyle steal set Camyrn Smith up for a breakaway MSSU basket. Northwestern replied with a score of its own, but Pyle and Smith linked up again for another MSSU basket. Then Nariah Clay would get a steal and a bucket for Southern. Clay would end the third quarter with another bucket as the score climbed to 59-38, MSSU.

The fourth quarter started with a bucket from the visitors before Southern matched it.

Northwestern went on a run but the closest it would come was still 12 points short. The clock expired with the score 72-58, MSSU.

The Lions (4-2) travel for their next game against Missouri S&T on Tuesday.
