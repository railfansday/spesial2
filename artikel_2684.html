2027 NFL Draft order: Titans take over No. 1 spot after Chargers grab first win of season in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

It's never too early to start looking ahead to the 2027 NFL Draft, right?

Week 10 of the current NFL season wrapped up on Monday night, and the order for the draft in a few months is already starting to take shape.

And, at least for now, the Tennessee Titans have taken over control of the No. 1 pick. That's all thanks to the Los Angeles Chargers, who finally picked up their first win of the season on Monday night in Baltimore.

The Chargers, who entered Week 10 as the last winless team in the league, beat the Ravens 23-20 at M&T Bank Stadium. That marked the fifth straight loss for the Ravens, who now sit at 4-6 on the season.

The Chargers have now joined both the Tennessee Titans and Miami Dolphins with 1-8 records. The Titans fell to the Jacksonville Jaguars in a 40-23 blowout on Sunday afternoon, which marked their third straight loss and moved them to 0-5 at Nissan Stadium this season. The Dolphins barely lost to the Indianapolis Colts 24-22 on Sunday. The win snapped the Colts' five-game losing skid, yet the victory moved them to only 3-7 on the year. The Dolphins have lost three straight heading into a road game against the Buffalo Bills next week.

Though all three teams share the same record, the Titans jumped the Chargers into the top spot through the tiebreaker. The Chargers sit at No. 2, and the Dolphins are at No. 3. The New York Jets and Philadelphia Eagles round out the top five.

Even though things are undoubtedly bad, the Chargers have finally gotten their first win. That felt inevitable, especially since the NFL hasn't seen a team go a full season without winning a game since the 2017 Cleveland Browns.

With several weeks to go in the regular season, any of those three teams can still absolutely claim the No. 1 overall pick down the stretch. The Jets and Eagles, both with just two wins this season, aren't out of the question yet, either.

For now, though, the Titans are in control of that top spot.
