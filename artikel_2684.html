What draft capital do the Buccaneers have in 2027?

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Tampa Bay Buccaneers enter the second half of the 2026 season with all seven of their original 2027 NFL Draft selections, giving general manager Jason Licht a full set of picks to work with next April.

Tampa Bay made no moves before the NFL trade deadline, leaving its 2027 draft capital unchanged. The Buccaneers currently hold one selection in each round, with no additional picks acquired from other teams.

With a 3-6 record entering their Week 10 bye, the Buccaneers are currently projected to select eighth overall in the first round. However, that position could change significantly over the final eight games.

Tampa Bay remains within reach of the NFC South lead, and winning the division would guarantee a playoff berth and move its first-round selection outside the top 18.

The Buccaneers could also receive additional selections through the NFL's compensatory pick process, although those awards have not been finalized or analyzed in this article.

For now, Licht retains all seven original 2027 draft picks, providing flexibility to address roster needs or make trades when the 2027 NFL Draft arrives.
