I haven't really had time yet to address the serious leg injury suffered by Thunder All-Star forward Jalen Williams in Sunday's game against the Clippers. It looked quite bad, and the 25-year-old had to be stretchered off the court with what turned out to be a fractured fibula and dislocated left ankle, according to general manager Sam Presti. The team hasn't given a definitive timeline, but we are talking months and maybe the season.

Such a bummer for Williams, who has been plagued by injuries since his breakout 2024-25 season as he was limited to just 33 regular-season games last year for a variety of issues. Williams, probably the club's second-most important player after two-time MVP Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, also missed 10 of Oklahoma City's 15 playoff games, exiting three other games early. He played through a wrist injury in helping OKC to the 2025 title. I believe a full-strength Thunder would have gotten past the inexperienced Spurs in the 2026 West Finals.

The Williams injury did shift some Thunder futures odds from a general ballpark of +160 to win the West to +200 (behind San Antonio at +165), +300 for the NBA title to +360, and a win total of 61.5 to 58.5. And none of that movement really included the serious shoulder injury also suffered by Cason Wallace, who recently signed a five-year, $139 million extension.
