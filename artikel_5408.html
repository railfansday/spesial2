Phoenix Contact Training Team Sets New Guinness World Record in Wiring

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

BLOMBERG, Germany, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Ten first-year dual-track students at Phoenix Contact set a new Guinness World Record on November 4, 2026. As the sponsor day at HSG Blomberg-Lippe, the team used Push-X 500 terminal block connection technology to wire 500 terminal blocks in 2 minutes and 20 seconds, setting a new official record in the category 'Fastest time to connect 500 terminal blocks'.

With great dedication and enthusiasm for the shared task, the apprentice team took the stage in front of 1,200 spectators in the sold-out Synflex Arena. Wiring the 1,000 conductors required precise hand movements and seamless teamwork. The Push-X connection technology made the task easier for them. Guinness World Records documented the time achieved and confirmed the new record during the official verification.
