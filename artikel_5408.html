Yazan Al Homsi-Backed Aduro and Rocket Doctor AI Announce Pilot, Network and Study Milestones

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Aduro targets a third-quarter 2027 finish for its life cycle study, and the pharmacy pilot is set for a 90-day evaluation

VANCOUVER, BC, Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Aduro Clean Technologies said in November that it had engaged Delphi to advance a comparative life cycle assessment of its Hydrochemolytic Technology. Vancouver-based Rocket Doctor AI Inc. said its physicians joined the Altais network in California and that it launched a 90-day pharmacy pilot in Canada. Investor Yazan Al Homsi holds a position in both companies.

On Nov. 17, Aduro, based in London, Ontario, said it had engaged Delphi, a Canadian sustainability consultancy, for the next phase of its life cycle assessment program. The study will use operating data from Aduro's Next Generation Process Pilot Plant and follow ISO 14040 and ISO 14044 standards. Three independent experts will review it. It will compare Hydrochemolytic Technology, a water-based process for recycling waste plastics and upgrading heavy oils, with pyrolysis. Aduro is targeting completion in the third quarter of 2027.

On Nov. 11, Rocket Doctor AI said that Rocket Doctor Practice Group, P.C., an affiliated professional corporation, had joined the Altais network. The move adds primary care physicians and specialists for eligible patients in California. Altais is a clinical services company that supports physicians and care teams.

On Nov. 18, Rocket Doctor AI announced that its subsidiary, Rocket Doctor Inc., had begun a 90-day pilot with a Canadian pharmacy chain. The pilot offers physician-led virtual care at 25 pharmacy locations in Alberta, British Columbia and Ontario. The pilot launched in October. The chain, which has not been named, will review each location against utilization, engagement, commercial results and other agreed criteria before deciding whether to expand.

Al Homsi invests in clean technology and AI-enabled healthcare.

Statements about the Aduro study's completion, the pharmacy pilot's evaluation and any expansion are forward-looking and depend on the companies' own timelines and decisions.

Aduro Clean Technologies, based in London, Ontario, develops patented water-based technologies that chemically recycle waste plastics, upgrade heavy crude and bitumen into lighter oil, and convert renewable oils into higher-value fuels or renewable chemicals. The company says its Hydrochemolytic Technology uses water as the reactive agent and runs at relatively low temperatures and cost.

Rocket Doctor AI Inc., based in Vancouver, develops physician-built, AI-powered healthcare tools. Its subsidiary, Rocket Doctor Inc., operates a digital health platform and marketplace that lets physicians launch and manage their own virtual or hybrid practices. The company says more than 350 physicians have provided care through its platform across more than 800,000 patient visits.

Yazan Al Homsi is a UAE-based investor and managing partner of Catalyst Communications DMCC, which he founded in Dubai in 2018. He was previously based in Vancouver, where he founded Founders Round Capital in 2017 and built a track record of investments in early-stage and small-cap companies. He earned a finance degree from McGill University in 2004 and holds the Chartered Financial Analyst designation. Before turning to investing, he spent more than a decade with PricewaterhouseCoopers in the Middle East, rising to director and focusing on transaction due diligence and valuation.
