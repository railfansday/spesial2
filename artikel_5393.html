Dubai International Chamber opens representative office in Miami to strengthen trade and investment ties between Dubai and the United States

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

DUBAI, UAE, Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Dubai International Chamber, one of the three chambers operating under the umbrella of Dubai Chambers, has opened a new representative office in Miami. The office aims to strengthen business ties between Dubai and the United States and expand trade and investment cooperation between companies in both markets.

The Miami office is Dubai International Chamber's second representative office in the United States, following the opening of its New York office during the Dubai Business Forum â USA, organised by Dubai Chambers in November 2025.

Salem AlShamsi, Executive Vice President of International Relations at Dubai Chambers, stated: "The opening of our new office in Miami marks an important step towards expanding trade and investment partnerships between Dubai and the United States and creating new growth opportunities for the business communities in both markets".

The new office comes amid growing trade between Dubai and the United States. Bilateral non-oil trade reached AED 121.8 billion in 2025, representing an increase of 4.6% compared to the previous year.

The number of active American companies registered with Dubai Chamber of Commerce reached 3,718 by the end of December 2025, recording year-on-year growth of 18.1%. A further 439 American companies joined the chamber during the first nine months of 2026, reflecting continued interest among American businesses in Dubai as a destination for business, expansion, and investment.

The Miami office will strengthen engagement with key public and private sector stakeholders, deepen cooperation with the American business community, and highlight the competitive advantages that make Dubai a leading global business destination. It will also provide information and market intelligence to help American companies enter the Dubai market and expand their operations from the city into international markets.

In addition, the office will support Dubai-based companies seeking to expand into the United States by providing specialised market insights, helping them identify promising trade and investment opportunities, and connecting them with trusted local partners.

Dubai International Chamber, one of the three chambers operating under the umbrella of Dubai Chambers, was established to promote Dubai as a global business hub, attract multinational companies and expand the emirate's trade ties with promising markets.
