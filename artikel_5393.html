Arm's CEO is being courted as a candidate to replace Jensen Huang at Nvidia

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Recruiter Heidrick & Struggles has begun reaching out to possible candidates after Jensen Huang left Nvidia last week

Arm Holdings CEO Rene Haas has been contacted by Heidrick & Struggles, the search firm Nvidia $NVDA's board retained to find Jensen Huang's replacement, and asked about taking the top job at the chipmaker, according to Bloomberg.

Heidrick has begun sounding out potential candidates for the position in the days since Huang's departure last week, according to Bloomberg. Nvidia representatives could not be reached for comment.

Nvidia Board Chair Harvey Jones said on the company's earnings call on Tuesday that the board has brought on Heidrick & Struggles to run the search for its next CEO. Jones said directors will consider both insiders and outsiders and will carry out a "thorough search." He also said the board "does not intend to reinstate" Huang.

Jeff Sanders, co-managing partner of Heidrick’s global CEO and board directors practice, is leading the search, according to The Wall Street Journal. Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress is serving as interim CEO while keeping her CFO role.

Huang resigned last week after admitting to an affair with a former executive at Megaspeed, a Singapore-based Nvidia customer. On Tuesday, in his first public remarks since stepping down, he said he would be open to returning if the directors asked.

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent pushed the board over the weekend to hire someone from outside the company. “They need a dramatic shakeup,” Bessent said on Fox News.
