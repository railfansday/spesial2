XTI Aerospace Eliminates Class B Units and Enters into Warrant Exchange Agreement, Simplifying Capital Structure

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

FORT LAUDERDALE, Fla., Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- XTI Aerospace, Inc. (Nasdaq: XTIA) ("XTI Aerospace," "XTI," or the "Company"), parent company of Drone Nerds, LLC, ("Drone Nerds"), a leading drone solutions platform serving commercial, enterprise and government customers, today announced that the Company has eliminated all outstanding Class B Units held by the sellers of Drone Nerds. In addition, the Company has entered into warrant exchange agreements, subject to customary closing conditions, with holders representing approximately 40% of its outstanding warrants, simplifying the Company's capital structure.

In a transaction dated November 18, 2026, all Class B Units of XTI Drones Holdings, LLC, a subsidiary formed in connection with XTI Aerospace's November 2025 acquisitions of Drone Nerds and Anzu Robotics, were converted into 6,524,576 shares of XTI Aerospace common stock. They were exchanged on a one-to-one basis. These securities are held directly by The Origin Group DN, Inc. and The Origin Group AZ, Inc. Following the exchange, no Class B Units remain outstanding.

The Company also announced today that it has entered into Securities Exchange Agreements with certain holders of outstanding warrants to purchase shares of the Company's common stock, par value $0.001 per share (the "Common Stock"). Pursuant to the Exchange Agreements, certain warrant holders agreed to exchange warrants exercisable for an aggregate of 8,522,052 shares of Common Stock for an aggregate of 2,840,690 newly issued shares of Common Stock. Following the Exchange, warrants to purchase approximately 12,670,129 shares of Common Stock remain outstanding.

The warrants were originally issued pursuant to the Underwriting Agreement dated June 24, 2025 between the Company and ThinkEquity LLC as representative of the several underwriters named therein, and the Placement Agency Agreement dated September 12, 2025 between the Company and ThinkEquity, and certain warrants were issued to ThinkEquity and its designees in connection with certain financings.

As a result of these transactions, XTI Aerospace now has approximately 47.8 million shares of common stock as of November 25, 2026.

"The elimination of certain legacy acquisition and financing securities marks an important step in simplifying our capital structure," said Jeremy Schneiderman, Interim CEO of XTI Aerospace. "With the removal of the Class B Units we believe we are better positioned with a solid foundation to execute on our strategic growth initiatives while enhancing shareholder value."

ThinkEquity served as financial advisor, and Blank Rome LLP and Taft Law served as legal counsel in connection with the transactions.
