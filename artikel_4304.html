Damaged rocks may help explain why small earthquakes outnumber large ones

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

University of Michigan researchers have developed a better way to understand why earthquakes occur and what size they might be. Currently, models that predict earthquakes only account for slipping that occurs along a main fault, activity that usually generates large earthquakes. Instead, the U-M researchers have built a model that considers not only the main fault action but also the activity of rocks that lie adjacent to the fault and become damaged during earthquake events.
