Bucks vs. Hawks Player Grades: Jakucionis notches career high, Trent proves he’s worth every penny

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Despite leading by as much as 22 in the second quarter, the Milwaukee Bucks just couldn't go the distance with the Atlanta Hawks, losing 113-106. As I get into later, the "up by 22 points" thing was mostly the result of unsustainable shooting, so I didn't see this game as some choke, but it's fair to wonder how that big a lead could have dissipated so quickly. On balance, if you remove their horrid second quarter—which they lost 37-16—this was probably one of Milwaukee's best games of the year. They showed real resolve, made multiple comebacks, and gave themselves multiple chances to tie the game at the end. Unfortunately, the Bucks simply could not make the crucial play. Read our full summary of the game here and catch a six-minute audio recap on the Bucks+ podcast, Bucks In Six Minutes, below.

26 minutes, 25 points, 5 rebounds, 5 assists, 3 steals, 2 turnovers, 9/16 FG, 4/10 3P, +7

The Hawks ran a switch-everything defence, and the Bucks had basically no answer for it until Jakucionis came in. His speed and burst were the perfect antidote to that defence, unlike other players—coughTyler Herrocough—who operate with more finesse. I loved basically everything about Jakucionis' game, who dropped a career-high 25 points. He was decisive with the ball, knowing when to pass, dribble, or shoot. He also made a major impact on defence, picking up full court and making his man work; he even had that possession in the fourth where he forced a turnover in the backcourt. Also, a hat tip to KJ for the passion he played with; this Bucks team can sometimes be a bit lifeless, and it felt like he came in with an agenda to bring energy no matter what.

20 minutes, 8 points, 5 rebounds, 3 assists, 1 steal, 5 turnovers, 2/13 FG, 0/5 3P, 1/1 FT, -12

Herro was awful. As I just alluded to, he does not have a lot of burst in his game, which is fine when he can pick on one weak defender, whether that be a guard or a big, but the Hawks just had nobody to attack. Herro had nothing, so he resorted to BS foul-drawing and hoping for calls. Had some bad off-ball defensive possessions too. Easily his worst game of the year.

30 minutes, 6 points, 5 rebounds, 4 assists, 2 steals, 5 turnovers, 2/7 FG, 2/4 3P, 1/3 FT, +3

Jaquez ended his streak of elite performances with this stinker. He made some solid plays here and there, but man, some of those turnovers were backbreaking. felt like the Bucks went on multiple runs that ended with deflating Jaquez giveaways.

22 minutes, 14 points, 6 rebounds, 2 assists, 2 turnovers, 5/11 FG, 3/6 3P, 0

Almost identical to the Celtics game, Kuzma started hot on 4/4 shooting, then made just one shot the rest of the way. Took two shots in the second half and had two turnovers before Jenkins took him out midway through the third and never put him back in.

23 minutes, 8 points, 7 rebounds, 1 block, 0 turnovers, 5 PF, 3/6 FG, 1/3 3P, -12

As I mentioned, the Hawks were switching everything with a bunch of like-sized players, and Turner has always struggled to play against switching defences because he can't punish mismatches. This basically rendered him obsolete. Suffice to say, a guy who is paid as much as Turner shouldn't be this easy to shut down.

24 minutes, 8 points, 11 rebounds, 2 assists, 3 blocks, 2/7 FG, 1/3 3P, 3/4 FT, +5

Even though he didn't score much, I liked Ware's motor, as shown by his block number and plus/minus. He continues to look more engaged on the defensive end and, at times, looked far and away the better option compared to Turner.

29 minutes, 13 points, 8 rebounds, 4 assists, 2 steals, 2 turnovers, 3/9 FG, 2/5 3P, 5/6 FT, -13

Burries found it tough to finish around the tall timber, but he was back to his do-everything ways and really made an impact on this game. Like Jakucionis, Burries' speed and physicality were needed against the up-and-in Hawks defence, and he rightly played a whole lot late because of that.

17 minutes, 5 points, 2 rebounds, 1 assist, 1 turnover, 2/5 FG, 1/3 3P, -9

30 minutes, 18 points, 2 rebounds, 3 steals, 7/16 FG, 4/12 3P, -3

We cool, Mr Silver? In all seriousness, Trent did provide a boost as a someone who was willing to get shots up at real volume once he got hot. But even more importantly, I just loved the energy he played with and how infectious he was out there. Anyone who has followed my writing knows how I feel about Trent's gambling defence (not a fan), but I guess when it comes off, he looks good.

11 minutes, 4 points, 2 rebounds, 2/3 FG, -4

Sims played only the opening five or so minutes of each half, but he was fine in those two spurts and did make an impact on the team's ability to compete for rebounds (even if I didn't love the combinations he was paired with). Made a nice floater off the reverse pivot in the first half.

Another surprise starter with Sims, and I can't say I loved how that unit played. In 10:50, the starting five had a -14.3 net rating. On the positive side, Jenkins clearly has a way of getting his groups to play with elite energy early in games, even if some of that has been driven by unsustainable shooting. Jenkins' MO to crash the glass was also a major reason they were hot early, outrebounding Atlanta 15-8 in the first quarter. On the note of unsustainable shooting, I also don't buy that it was Jenkins' fault that the team led by 22 and then was down two at the half; the shots the Bucks were making—and the Hawks were missing—just weren't going to continue. Finally, I liked how he kept cycling through units until he found something, even if that meant not playing Herro and Kuzma for the final quarter and a half.

Inactive: Kam Jones, Cormac Ryan, Pete Nance, Ousmane Dieng, Ryan Rollins, Bogoljub Markovic

If you play with energy and heart, Jenkins will reward you.

The Bucks shot a ridiculous 58% from the field and 55% from three in the first quarter. They actually did OK in some key metrics in the second quarter—even winning the possession battle 24-22—but their main issue was keeping the Hawks off the line; Atlanta shot 10 free throws to Milwaukee's one. The Bucks also turned it over eight times to the Hawks' three.

The Bucks got a season-high 62 points from their bench, outscoring the Hawks' bench by a whopping 28 points.

Gary Trent Jr. played his first first-quarter minutes all season, going 3/3 in the period.

Kyle Kuzma and Tyler Herro were subbed out in the middle of the third quarter and didn't return.

The Bucks have a few days to prepare for the big one against Giannis and the Miami Heat on Wednesday night in South Beach. Catch the Game on ESPN—yes, ESPN—at 6:00 p.m. CST.
