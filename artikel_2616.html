NFL injury tracker Week 12: Latest news, fantasy implications as Kenneth Walker III (groin) ruled out; Seahawks 'optimistic' about Sam Darnold (quad)

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Week 12 is about to get underway, and we're tracking all of the injuries before game time.

Kenneth Walker III will miss a third straight game with a groin issue.

The running back returned to practice on Tuesday, but is not ready to return and will miss the Chiefs' Thanksgiving night game against the Buffalo Bills. Rookie Emmett Johnson, who has rushed for 206 yards and recorded 108 receiving yards while scoring three times over the past few weeks, will once again be called upon to handle RB1 duties.

Sam Darnold has a chance to return in Week 12, but how he performs in practice on Wednesday will determine which way head coach Mike Macdonald will go. The Seattle Seahawks quarterback has missed the past two weeks with a quad injury with Drew Lock filling in and leading the team to a pair of victories.

"He was able to train a little bit here, do some rehab here, he also was able to get away," Macdonald said this week of Darnold. "He's optimistic, but I think with [Monday's] practice cadence and speed of today, it's probably wise to wait to make any declarations until we get through Wednesday's practice."

Los Angeles Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert remains in concussion protocol as they begin preparations for the New England Patriots on Sunday. Herbert has played better over the past two weeks for the 1-9 Chargers, but his availability for Week 12 remains unclear as he did not practice on Wednesday. If he's unable to clear protocol in time, rookie DJ Uiagalelei will get the start.

Brock Purdy sat out Wednesday's practice with a low ankle injury. According to San Francisco 49ers head coach Kyle Shanahan, the quarterback was hurt in the second quarter of Sunday night's win over the Minnesota Vikings. He is considered "day-to-day."

One running back who could be questionable for the weekend is Bhayshul Tuten of the Jacksonville Jaguars. He played hurt during last week's loss to the New York Giants and did not take part in Wednesday's practice as he nurses foot and ankle injuries. Chris Rodriguez Jr. would get the RB1 responsibilities if Tuten cannot go against the Tennessee Titans.

Kirk Cousins likely won't get one of his weapons back in Week 12 as wide receiver Jordan Addison remains sidelined with a thumb injury. Minnesota Vikings head coach Kevin O'Connell said Addison is "progressing well," but it does not look like he will be back on the field in Week 12.

Here's the rest of the Week 12 injury report:

TE Dalton Kincaid (thumb/ankle): questionable

CB Christian Benford (groin): questionable

DB Tyrique Stevenson (groin): questionable

TE Terrance Ferguson (ankle/hamstring): OUT
