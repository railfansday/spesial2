Raiders vs. Broncos live updates, inactives, where to watch: 'Not a lot of optimism' that Brock Bowers plays in Week 11 as Fernando Mendoza makes second start

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Las Vegas Raiders and Denver Broncos will face off for the first time this season in Week 11's afternoon window, with kickoff set for 4:25 p.m. ET at Empower Field at Mile High in Denver.

After starting the season 3-0, the Raiders have been on a slide, going 1-6 over their past seven games. No. 1 overall pick rookie Fernando Mendoza is set to make his second career start following the trade of Kirk Cousins ahead of the deadline this week, up against another challenging defense after facing the Seattle Seahawks in Week 11. All-Pro tight end Brock Bowers is questionable for this matchup, which could leave Mendoza without his top receiving option.

The Broncos entered the week tied with the Kansas City Chiefs for the best record in the AFC West at 7-2. They're 10.5-point home favorites in this contest and are on a five-game winning streak coming off the Week 10 bye. That win streak began as head coach Sean Payton resumed offensive play-calling from OC Davis Webb after a slow start on offense.

Follow along with Yahoo Sports for live updates, highlights and more as the Raiders face the Broncos in Week 11, airing on CBS.

There is "not a lot of optimism" that Raiders star tight end Brock Bowers will play in Week 11, per ESPN's Adam Schefter. Bowers is dealing with ankle and quad injuries. Bowers logged limited practices on Thursday and Friday and is still "pushing to play" even though his status is looking grim.

Bowers exited with an ankle injury in Week 10's loss to the Seattle Seahawks, though head coach Klint Kubiak labeled him "day-to-day" and said the injury wasn't as bad as initially feared.

It's a tough blow, particularly for rookie quarterback Fernando Mendoza, who is making his second career start in Week 11 against the Denver Broncos. Mendoza completed 17 of 29 pass attempts for 143 yards and no touchdowns or interceptions in his debut as a starter last week.
