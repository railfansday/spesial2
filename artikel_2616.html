Cherki calls for more following Napoli draw

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Rayan Cherki has urged City to continue working hard after coming from behind twice to earn a 2-2 UEFA Champions League draw against Napoli.

Rasmus Højlund grabbed the opener for the visitors after 55 minutes, latching onto Billy Gilmour's pass before drilling into the net.

Enzo Maresca's men reacted well to the setback and levelled 12 minutes later thanks to a thunderous left-footed strike from midfield maestro Ayyoub Bouaddi, who claimed his first-ever goal in senior football.

With City in the ascendancy, the visitors broke on a quick counter and restored their lead through Noa Lang's controlled volley.

But Maresca's men showed grit and determination to fight back for a second time, with UEFA's Player of the Match Cherki precisely finding the net with four minutes of normal time left at the Etihad to earn a point.

Despite another sparkling individual performance, the Frenchman made it clear that the three points are always the utmost priority.

"I don't care about scoring my goal today. I want to win, just that," said Cherki in an honest interview after the game. Today we played good but we didn't put a lot of energy I think.

"We need to do more. We need to win this game, today I don't care about scoring my goal, we need to win the game. We try to put all we have on the pitch. Sometimes the game is not like how you want.

"Today it's like the last two or three games. We need to work more and come back for a win. Just that."

The draw means we have three wins, one draw and one defeat in the competition this term, with three fixtures left of the League Phase.

Attention now turns to Sunday's huge Premier League clash away to league-leaders Arsenal and Cherki explained that City only have one intention heading into London.

"We are in a difficult position, that is true because we didn't win the game," he continued.
