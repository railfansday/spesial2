Taylor & Lewis lose all five pairs matches on return

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Phil Taylor and his England team-mate Adrian Lewis lost all five matches as the pair returned to darts action together at the MODUS Super Series International Pairs tournament.

Taylor, a 16-time world champion, retired from senior-level darts in 2018, while Lewis has not competed on the PDC tour since 2023.

They signed for the tournament in Portsmouth after Taylor was tempted back by the prospect of playing alongside his long-term friend.

The 66-year-old landed his first dart at a double as he checked out on 84 but the duo went on to lose their opening match 4-3 against Scotland's John Henderson and Robert Thornton.

They subsequently suffered successive defeats to pairs from Sweden, Switzerland, Germany and Denmark.

The pair finished the night bottom of Group C, but will return to action on Friday for a second round-robin phase.

Taylor had not played competitively since the 2024 edition of World Seniors Darts Championship and was adamant the south-coast competition will be his last appearance at the tournament.

Speaking prior to his first match, he told Talksport: "This is the final time. It's got to be now. I've got all the things in my life now that I'm enjoying.

"I understand what Adrian's saying about travelling and being away. He's got a young family still, where my family have grown up, but I've still got grandkids.

"One stops and then another one begins again, life carries on. I enjoy being at home, that's the reason why I wouldn't be on the road again."

Taylor and two-time world champion Lewis, 41, won the World Cup of Darts four times together for England between 2012 and 2016.

Asked how his preparation for the tournament had gone, Taylor added: "For me, not very well, to be quite honest with you. I've lost it a little bit.

"Aidy, I know has been playing really well, so I'm going to be on his shoulders today. He's going to have to carry me a little bit."
