Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Management reported that Ethos Technologies Inc. (LIFE -1.77%) achieved record revenue performance driven by triple-digit growth in both its direct and third-party distribution channels. The company stated that its vertically integrated platform, which handles marketing, underwriting, and policy administration, allowed for significant market share gains and operational resiliency during a partner system outage. Management indicated that the business is benefiting from a virtuous data cycle where increased scale leads to improved conversion rates and more efficient unit economics. The company raised its full-year guidance for both revenue and adjusted EBITDA, reflecting sustained consumer demand and the rapid scaling of its agency partnerships.
