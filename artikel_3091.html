TransAct Technologies (TACT) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Software sales surged while casino demand softened and guidance was trimmed.

TransAct Technologies Incorporated (TACT -1.01%) reported third quarter revenue growth of 10% year over year, led by a 33% increase in the food service technology segment. Management highlighted a 61% surge in software revenue as the company transitions toward a recurring revenue model by monetizing its growing installed base of 22,600 online terminals. However, overall performance was affected by cyclical softening in the Casino and Gaming market and higher operating expenses related to the migration of the BOHA! platform to Microsoft Azure. The company revised its full-year guidance downward at the top end for both net sales and adjusted EBITDA to reflect expected continued weakness in gaming demand through the end of the fiscal year. Additionally, the Board of Directors is actively conducting a strategic review of the Casino and Gaming business and considering potential alternatives for the FST segment.
