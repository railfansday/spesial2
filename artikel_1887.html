Huddersfield Town team news for game where mentality matters even more than most

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Both teams have won their last four matches and the top-of-the-table Terriers are unbeaten in seven gams in all competitions.

But backing up Saturday victories on a Tuesday is extremely difficult in League One, particularly when Jack Wilshere's Hatters play a very different style to the direct Bradford City team Huddersfield bat 3-0 at the wknd.

"The game will come down to mentality after a tough game on Saturday," said Huddersfield coach Drury. "They had a tough game on Saturday.

"We played Peterborough recently and we knew how chaotic that game was and how much it takes out of you physically and mentally.

"We had a tough derby game on Saturday, so I often feel Tuesdays, as much as there's some tactical elements to it, a lot of it will come down to mentality.

"I think what both teams do without the ball will be key. As much as both teams have got many good footballers and can do things with the ball, it'll come down to the other side of the game in my own instinct.

"It's a different challenge, a completely different style of football.

"They can play in a variety of ways. They're not just set in a certain way.

"They've got a really, really good side and it's been a really good moment in terms of form. I think Jack's done a brilliant job there.

"I think they look better than they looked last season in terms of being able to adapt to certain scenarios in games."

"We won't expect anybody back that wasn't involved on Saturday in terms of injuries," said Drury. "No forced changes, but obviously we'll assess what type of challenge the game will pose to us and what players will feel the best suited to."
