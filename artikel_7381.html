RaceTrac Starts an Ad Business to Leverage Loyalty Base

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

RaceTrac, a chain of gas stations and convenience stores largely located in the Southeast, has launched a retail media network.

Jamie Miller, vp of marketing for convenience at RaceTrac, told ADWEEK that its Trac Media Group will leverage its loyalty program of 6 million members. Miller said the company has invested in its loyalty program over the past two years, making now the time to invest in advertising.

“We had a little bit of work to do to get up to speed on that in order to lay the foundation for us to be able to move forward with a retail media network,” Miller said.

Mitchell Parton is ADWEEK's senior commerce reporter, covering retail media as well as the rise of agentic and social commerce. Before joining ADWEEK in 2026, he was a reporter at Digiday Media's Modern Retail and before that wrote for several regional publications in Texas.
