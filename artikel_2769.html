Motorcycle rider collides with horse in lead-up to Baja 1000

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The SCORE Baja 1000 began Friday in the Mexican state of Baja California Sur and mishaps were already being reported, including one during the qualifying period that involved a "presumed" competitor and a free-roaming horse.

The accompanying footage, captured by a fellow motorcycle rider and provided to BCS Noticias, shows the collision from a distance as several horses attempted to cross in front of the rider.

BCS Noticias states in its translated description: "Ahead of the official start of the #Baja1000 competition in Baja California Sur, a presumed competitor hit a horse that got stuck in the path."

The footage shows what appears to be a violent collision, but the unidentified rider stands and claims only to have injured his hand. "I don't think it's broken, but it's going to affect my racing," he says.

He informs the cameraman that he fell briefly onto the horse and thought he might be kicked by the animal.

The incident occurred on a portion of a 965-mile route for a competition that features several types of off-road vehicles.

Qualifying for the SCORE Baja 1000 began Nov. 10. The competition route starts and finishes in San Jose del Cabo and passes through several Baja California Sur cities, including La Paz, Cabo San Lucas, Loreto, and Todos Santos.
