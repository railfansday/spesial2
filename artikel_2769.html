A.J. Brown is expected to miss multiple weeks with hamstring injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A season marred by injuries continues for receiver A.J. Brown and the Patriots.

According to a Friday report from ESPN, Brown is expected to miss multiple weeks with a hamstring injury.

This will be the third separate injury that sidelines Brown this season. He suffered a high-ankle sprain early in the Week 1 loss to the Seahawks. Upon his return, he suffered a knee injury that kept him out for two games.

He returned in Week 10 only to be saddled with a hamstring issue.

Brown has played just 160 offensive snaps in four games this season, catching 11 passes for 128 yards.

The Patriots will have to evaluate who is placed on injured reserve going forward, as the team has been dealing with a wave of injuries and can have just eight players return from the list.

Quarterback Drake Maye seems likely to go on IR with his fractured fibula, as he's unlikely to return until the end of the regular season — if he comes back at all in 2026.
