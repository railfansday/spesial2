Ex-Mets Pitcher Eyeing Return From Injury; Nearly 24 Teams Reportedly Attend Throwing Session

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Frankie Montas has pitched in 169 MLB games, but he underwent Tommy John surgery in 2025 and missed all of 2026.

Montas had a 6.28 ERA in nine games for the New York Mets in 2025, and has not pitched in the big leagues since August 2025. However, according to The Athletic's Will Sammon and Dennis Lin, he looked good during his throwing session on Tuesday.

"A whopping two dozen or so teams attended Montas' throwing session Tuesday in Arizona, and many left the showcase buzzing about the right-handed pitcher's ability to land a major-league deal, said league sources who spoke to The Athletic on condition of anonymity because they were not authorized to speak publicly about the free agent," wrote The Athletic.

Montas had a 3.37 ERA in 2021 for the Athletics, but he has not been able to replicate the same success ever since. He has had a 4.65 ERA since the start of the 2022 season, pitching for the Cincinnati Reds, Milwaukee Brewers, Mets, New York Yankees and Athletics.

"Multiple scouts said Montas, whose fastball sat at 95 mph and was up to 98 in front of an audience featuring roughly 35 scouting personnel, appeared to be in great shape and displayed sharp mechanics," wrote The Athletic. "Evaluators said that based on Tuesday's workout, they would recommend their clubs sign him to a major-league contract. The sources pointed to the lack of intriguing options in this year's free-agent class as another reason to like Montas."

Tarik Skubal is the best pitcher on the market. After him, there are Michael King and Freddy Peralta, and then there is a drop-off. Montas could be a low-risk, high-reward addition for a team in need of a starting pitcher.
