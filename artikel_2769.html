76ers HC Nick Nurse reveals why Jaylen Brown had ‘no chance to get going’ vs. Celtics

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Jayson Tatum came out on top against Jaylen Brown in their first matchup since the shocking trade that sent Brown to Philadelphia, as the Boston Celtics cruised to a 123-101 win over the 76ers on Tuesday. Brown endured an unusually difficult outing against his former team, and head coach Nick Nurse had a clear explanation for his struggles.

When asked about Brown's performance, Nurse pointed to the forward's early foul trouble, explaining how it prevented him from finding his rhythm.

"Really tough for him in the beginning by getting in foul trouble, and right from the start, and then he gets another early one in the second half, so he really had no chance to get going."

Brown finished the night with 13 points on 4-of-9 shooting, along with four turnovers.

Fans expected Brown to deliver a big performance and make a statement against his former team, especially as the matchup marked the first time he and Tatum faced each other as opponents after spending nine seasons together in Boston.

However, their first meeting on opposite sides was not as warm as many had anticipated. Brown barely acknowledged Tatum's pregame greeting, and his performance on the court also fell short of expectations.

Philadelphia struggled to find consistent scoring support despite Tyrese Maxey's impressive effort. The guard led the 76ers with 24 points, while Joel Embiid contributed 16 points and LeBron James added 11.

The matchup also marked Paul George's first game against his former team. However, George's outstanding performance against Philadelphia ultimately took the spotlight away from the much-anticipated reunion between the two former Celtics teammates.

The loss moved Philadelphia to 9-3 on the season, while Boston improved to 9-2, moving ahead of the 76ers in the Eastern Conference standings.
