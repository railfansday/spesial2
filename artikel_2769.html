Oklahoma State had a dreadful season in 2025. They went 1-11, and it led to the termination of longtime coach Mike Gundy. The Cowboys turned to North Texas head coach Eric Morris to lead the program. He was coming off an 11-2 season with the Mean Green and brought multiple players with him to Oklahoma State. One of those players was quarterback Drew Mestemaker. The quarterback has been impressive for the Cowboys, leading to Mestemaker being seen as a potential number one pick in the 2027 NFL Draft.

Now, the star quarterback is getting compared to a current NFL star, Josh Allen of the Buffalo Bills, according to Gilberto Manzano of Sports Illustrated.

"Mestemaker is the clear No. 1 quarterback in this class. He showed flashes of being the next Josh Allen in performances against Oregon, Houston and Texas Tech. Oklahoma State won only two of those games, but it had a chance in every one because of Mestemaker's gunslinging and knack for getting hot in a hurry," Manzano wrote. "Mestemaker can be a bit reckless with the football at times, but ball security was also an issue for Allen early in his career. Mestemaker will need time to develop, but the flashes have said plenty about his upside."

The star quarterback is a redshirt sophomore. In high school, Mestemaker was not recruited, with one of the reasons being that he never started a varsity game. He decided to walk on at North Texas, and in 2024, began the season as the fifth-string quarterback. He would ultimately play in five games that year, including starting in the bowl game.

In 2025, he broke out. He started all 14 games for the team, passing for 4,379 yards and 34 touchdowns. He followed Morris to Oklahoma State, where he has thrown for 3,239 yards and 30 more touchdowns. Mestemaker has a choice now. He can return to school or head to the NFL.

"There's always the possibility of the redshirt sophomore returning to college, but it'll be hard for him to pass up a potential top-three pick. After what has occurred with Manning and Moore, Mestemaker might need to strike while the iron is hot," Manzano added.

Dante Moore was seen as the potential second overall pick in the 2026 NFL Draft, but decided to return to school. He has since been injured and seen his draft stock take a hit.

As for Mestemaker, his focus right now is getting healthy and finishing the season strong. Mestemaker's status this week against Arizona State is currently in doubt, as he was listed as questionable in the initial injury report for the game.
