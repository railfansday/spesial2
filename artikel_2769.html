PLAYER RATINGS | Lens 1-2 Marseille: Pressure remains on Lens after Champions League disaster

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Ligue 1 McDonald's, Matchday 10, 08/11/2026

Olympique de Marseille kept the pressure on RC Lens with a 2-1 win over their hosts.

Lens and Marseille experienced contrasting fortunes in their midweek European adventures. Les Lensois suffered resolute humiliation at the hands of Como 1906, losing 9-1 in a historic Champions League defeat at the Stade Bollaert-Delelis. Marseille, on the other hand, would pick up their first points in the Europa League with a 2-0 win over Bayer Leverkusen for their third win in a row across all competitions.

Lens likely dreaded their home reception, and for good reason, as the supporters made their feelings clear about a horror week from the start of the game, with a banner in front of the ultras reading, 'You are a disgrace to 120 years of history.' The scars from the defeat were not just present in the stands, but reflected on the pitch, with Lens gifting Marseille a goal in the 2nd minute when Jonathan Gradit's backpass was picked up by Amine Gouiri for a simple finish.

Heads began to lift after the early shock, and even the supporters found their voice, as they temporarily put aside their frustrations to cheer on a team that desperately needed to put the 9-1 defeat out of their minds. Lens showed some threat getting into promising positions in the final third; their endeavour would be rewarded when Florian Sotoca dug out a cross for Florian Thauvin to head home.

Gouiri would threaten again when he slipped between the defenders to poke a shot onto the crossbar, while Amine Harit would test Robin Risser's reflexes, forcing a save off Lens's France international. Down the other end, Igor Paixão would give away the ball close to goal, allowing Yacine Titraoui a free hit. However, the midfielder shot straight at Jeffrey De Lange.

The intensity that had defined the first half waned after the break. It would take until the 76th minute for a bit of life to be put back into the game when Thauvin dropped to the floor in the box. There were big appeals from Lens, but in truth the Lens captain had fouled Tochukwu Nnadi, with his studs scraping down the leg of the Marseille midfielder.

It felt as if the game would end in a stalemate, but with eight minutes left, Marseille took the lead through the substitute Keyliane Abdallah. A scuffed shot from Gouiri, following a good combination down Marseille's left, would travel on to find Abdallah. The winger fired home to give Marseille the late win and send the club into fourth ahead of the international break.

His game was later raised, and he made some good interventions, but ultimately it felt like he still looked a little shell-shocked from the European shellacking.
