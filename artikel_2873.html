Thomas Müller ‘delighted’ for Harry Kane winning the Ballon d’Or

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

When Harry Kane won the 2026 Ballon d'Or, messages of congratulations stormed in from past and present players and notable figures from other sports. Thomas Müller, who played for Bayern for a quarter of a century, is undeniably on cloud nine after the 33-year-old won the award.

"I'm absolutely delighted for him," Müller began (as captured by X account @iMiaSanMia). "73 goals in 65 competitive appearances. This award is the perfect crowning achievement for both his individual performances and the team's success at FC Bayern. I think everyone is more than happy that Harry has received this award.

"Harry hasn't just delivered the numbers; he's also a brilliant footballer," Müller commented. "His shooting technique, his ability to hold up the ball, his commitment in challenges. And he's never afraid to do the dirty work, despite knowing that he's the team's top striker. Other players might think: Why should I run all the way back to our own penalty box and put in a sliding tackle to win the ball? Harry just gets on with that He also plays passes from positions nobody would expect.

"No other striker does that, simply because no other striker is capable of doing it as well as he is. As a leader and as a person, he's also a role model and a worthy ambassador for the game. Well done, mate," the 37-year-old concluded.

Müller's heartfelt endorsement underscores just how vital Kane has been not only as a record-breaking goalscorer, but as the ultimate team player in Munich. As Kane continues to cement his legendary status, tributes like this prove that his impact on the pitch goes far beyond the stat sheet.
