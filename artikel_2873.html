76ers star LeBron James declares win over Magic ‘the best win of the season so far’

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Philadelphia 76ers had to dig deep to escape the Orlando Magic on Wednesday night, and LeBron James believes the 121-116 road victory might be the team's most impressive one yet.

Orlando came out firing and built a 40-27 lead after the first quarter, eventually stretching its advantage to as many as 17 points. The Magic finished the night 19-of-37 from beyond the arc, but the Sixers clamped down when it mattered most, outscoring Orlando 29-19 in the fourth quarter to pull off the comeback.

After the game, James praised his team's resilience and didn't hesitate to put the win near the top of Philadelphia's list.

"Yeah, we just weathered the storm and obviously they kept hitting us with haymakers, you know, and then I mean they shot the piss out the ball tonight from the three-point line, but in that fourth quarter we just buckled down," the four-time NBA MVP said, via Kris Rhim. "And some of the shots that they made throughout those first three quarters, they didn't make in the fourth, and we were able to get rebounds and execute on the other end."

James added that it was "one of our better wins, if not the best win of the season so far."

The 41-year-old forward finished with 22 points and six assists on 8-of-16 shooting. Joel Embiid led the way with 26 points, seven assists and three blocks, going 12-of-14 from the free-throw line. VJ Edgecombe chipped in 24 points, eight rebounds and six assists while knocking down four triples, and Anfernee Simons added 18 points on an efficient 7-of-12 shooting.

Franz Wagner did everything he could for Orlando, posting 27 points, 11 assists and eight rebounds. Jalen Suggs scored 18 points and hit five 3-pointers, while Desmond Bane added 15 points on five makes from deep.

The victory extended Philadelphia's winning streak to four games and improved its record to 13-3, the best mark in the Eastern Conference.

The Sixers will look to keep rolling when they host the Milwaukee Bucks on Saturday.
