NFL Predictions: Bears will get their first win in Detroit since 2021

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Thursday is the biggest regular-season game between the Chicago Bears and the Detroit Lions in a really long time. The Athletic's Kevin Fishbain dug up that the only two years both the Bears and Lions made the playoffs were 1994 and 1991. With each team currently holding a 90% chance to make the postseason, the odds are pretty good that it happens again.

In 1994, both teams snuck into the playoffs at 9-7, but in 1991 the Lions won the NFC Central at 12-4, with the Bears right behind at 11-5. They split the series in '91, with the Bears winning at Soldier Field and the Lions winning ugly at the Pontiac Silverdome on Thanksgiving, 16-6.

The winner of this Thanksgiving Day game will move to 9-2, be the two-seed in the NFC, and claim the top spot in the division with just six games to go.

Our partners at FanDuel Sportsbook have the Bears as a 1.5-point favorite (it opened at 2.5, so money has been coming in on the Lions) with the over/under at 55.5, which is the highest point total this week. I went back and forth on my prediction all week, and I can see it going either way, but give me the Bears to win, 28 to 27.

So that's the Bears and the moneyline (-122), the Lions and the 1.5 points (-112), and the under (-112).

Not only is Ben Johnson looking for his first win over the Lions, but Caleb Williams is too. The last time the Bears beat Detroit, Justin Fields had 289 combined yards, Chicago sacked Jared Goff 4 times, and he had 3 turnovers.

The last time the Bears won in Detroit, Andy Dalton threw for 317 yards, with Darnell Mooney catching 5 for 123.

Did you realize the Lions are just 1-8 on Thanksgiving since 2017? The Bears are 3-1 against them on Turkey Day in that stretch, with the only loss coming in 2024 when Matt Eberflus was a deer in headlights in the waning seconds, forgetting to call a timeout, and was fired the next day.
