GTA 6: How To Wear A Helmet When Riding

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

GTA 6 has so many surprises; you likely won't see them all the first time through. While we won't spoil the big ones here, suffice it to say you'll find a new concept to acclimate to every few hours. That includes minigames, TV shows to watch, video games to play on your couch, and tons of vehicle-related nuances.

The game is called Grand Theft "Auto," after all, so there are plenty of things to keep in mind when flying a plane, driving a car, riding a bike, or hoofing it on foot. Each mode of transportation is controlled differently, and there are some secrets you'll need to pick up along the way. Here's how to equip a helmet and save yourself some anguish.

Note that if you're using a helmet in GTA 6, you'll take less damage when you wipe out. This is pretty huge, as the physics system in GTA 6 is so complex that I've seen some incredibly gnarly wipeouts. Jason and Lucia can go flying under the right circumstances, especially if there's enough momentum in the crash.

Also keep in mind that helmets can help through rough terrain, especially if you happen to fall down some stairs during a heated chase. Since you can take scooters pretty much anywhere in Vice City, you're probably going to be using them a lot.

In GTA 6, you can "equip" a helmet from the L1 radial menu. It defaults to weapons, so press R1 to switch to "items," then select the helmet option in the top-right corner of the screen. Weirdly, when you're not on a bike, scooter, or any vehicle that can support helmets, you won't see the option to swap to a helmet. So keep this in mind if you're searching for it.

If you're looking for an area that is perfectly suited for helmets, you'll come across a racing squad in Chapter 2, while playing as Jason and Lucia.

The main thing you may want to use a helmet for is scooters, which are found all over Vice City and its surrounding area. You can rent scooters instantly. However, they're also incredibly finicky, and it's easy to fall off. Just brushing against an NPC, vehicle, or a wall can fling Jason and Lucia off a scooter.

Another great part about scooters and bikes is that they're often stacked together, letting Jason and Lucia explore the open world together. When playing as either character, and they're hanging out next to each other, jump on top of a bike or scooter, and your computer-controlled partner will follow suit.

This can be really useful for just exploring and finding new collectibles, as well as roaming around looking for trouble. Since your partner is on a scooter and can easily jump off, any altercation can be dealt with swiftly.

If you're using bikes or scooters a lot in GTA 6, consider wearing a helmet to avoid nasty wipeouts. It would be a shame to pull off a huge bank score and then smash into a wall, Looney Tunes style.
