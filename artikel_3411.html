Marvel’s Webtoon Era Is Officially Here, And It Will Change Comics Forever

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

With access to 35,000 comics encompassing the Marvel brand as well as previously included properties like Star Wars and 20th Century Studios properties like Alien, the Marvel Comics app is a subtle yet noticeable technical overhaul to find what fans want to read next. Thanks to features like personalized, character/series-based options and an intuitive Pull List feature, as well as other algorithmic suggestions based on preferences and reading history, it's easy for comic book novices, casual readers following their favorite series, and seasoned fans to find and stay up to date through this new service, available today, November 16, 2026.

Marvel's had multiple rebrands for its digital comic services since the mid-1990s, such as CyberComics, DotComics, Marvel Digital Comics, and many more. But by partnering with WEBTOON, much like previously with comiXology, Marvel is joining forces with the biggest name in modern digital comics publishing, licensed and operated by the vertical scrolling webcomics giant this time around. It continues to integrate Marvel's pre-existing Infinity lineup of vertical scroll comics with a massive back catalog of beloved comic books digitized from 1963's first issue of The Amazing Spider-Man through the most recent issues of Midnight Spider-Man.

As Marvel Unlimited is on the way out, Marvel fans have already been encouraged to create an account with WEBTOON so they can transfer their accounts and collections on the new platform. The feature is built into the Account tab for the new Marvel Comics app, where one can select "Import from Marvel Unlimited" to get started.

As a reminder, fans have until December 7, 2026, per Marvel's official confirmation, to successfully migrate their content over.

The service is similarly structured to Marvel Unlimited, down to a Base tier of $9.99/month or $69.99/year for full access plus the standard ability to read new issues three months after they hit store shelves.

But the Ultimate tier, for $12.49/month or $99.99/year (tax included for all prices shown) goes that extra step further by giving fans access to the newest comics 30 days after their print release, prompting earlier access rather than previous merch-based incentives through the old "Plus" tier. ScreenRant was given Ultimate-level access, and was able to blitz through Tim Seeley and Michael Sta. Maria's Inglorious X-Force up to the penultimate issue before Fugitive X-Force was set up, which in and of itself was a delight.

For those who enjoy reading digital comics, if simply for accessibility and organizational benefits, Marvel Comics' new app has plenty of great features. The Pull List is convenient with how quickly it'll suggest related material to dive into further, as are the tailored reading lists and moments in Marvel history where fans can read classics dating back to the Golden Age. Comics can be downloaded for offline reading, saved to users' libraries, and marked as read. The app will intuitively provide guides and character close-ups, too, giving recommended issues to read next whether to indulge curiosity or prep for the newest events going on for Marvel today.

Smart Panel features are present on this app much like elsewhere, but unlike competitors like GlobalComix, there aren't quite as many custom viewer settings. Fans will still see two-page spreads run uninterrupted on this new platform, which is a delight for folding smartphone owners, but there's not a dedicated feature on each comic to always have two pages visible, at least not yet.

Skip the decades of confusing continuity and jump straight into the best modern resets and standalone storylines.

That being said, WEBTOON has built its brand on mobile optimization, and reading Infinity spins on Jeff the Land Shark or comics like Miles Morales: Spider-Man – Brooklyn's Finest Infinity Comic is beautiful and seamless either on regular or folded screens. Smart Panel translates especially well on wider screen formats, while most mobile users will hardly complain at the crisp presentation of each individual page.

The only worrisome aspect left is arguably for print comic store proprietors seeing the Ultimate tier's option to read new comics 30 days after launch. But it's important to remember that collectors are still eagerly buying up new releases, particularly hard-to-find variants causing huge reseller price increases, like for Absolute Batman — a situation perhaps more nuanced than can be summarized here, but still a strong sign of life for print sales. This Ultimate option is priced higher because it's intended for hardcore fans looking to support the industry and its creators, while discovering new series. Brick-and-mortar stores aren't going anywhere, at least not for now.

The new Marvel Comics app will expand on what readers have come to appreciate about their digital comics offerings, with even more content out of Disney's library to come next. It's available now, so be sure to check it out!

A mobile-first digital comics hub that helped popularize the vertical scroll webtoon format. Webtoon mixes professionally-produced Originals with an open self-publishing space where creators can upload episodes, build communities, and earn through programs like ad revenue sharing. It first launched in South Korea under Naver in 2004 and later expanded worldwide, turning serialized comics into an always on pipeline for new stories and breakout creators.
