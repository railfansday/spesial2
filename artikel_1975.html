Newcastle and Leeds the two favourites to sign Jack Rudoni - Club adviser

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Newcastle United and Leeds United are the most "likely" contenders to secure the signature of Coventry City midfielder Jack Rudoni.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who does not believe that the 25-year-old will be a top priority for his former club.

All three sides have been linked with a move for Rudoni in recent days, after he has impressed for Coventry upon their return to the Premier League.

However, Wyness has no doubt that Everton are the firm "outsiders" in the race for his signature at the moment.

Rudoni was in League One with AFC Wimbledon as recently as 2022, and has since enjoyed a rapid rise to the top flight.

He notched 13 goal involvements in Coventry's promotion season last term, and has already netted three times so far this season.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that Newcastle and Leeds are best-placed to swoop in for Rudoni in the next 12 months.

The Scottish businessman simply cannot see Everton moving for the Englishman, given their current abundance of options in the centre of midfield.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained how he sees Newcastle and Leeds in the box seat.

"I think Newcastle and Leeds would be the ones I think are more likely to go for it and agree a deal. Everton would probably be outsiders in terms of that group," he said.

"You've got to look at Everton's midfield, which is moving ahead very well right now and really not an area where they're going to need strengthening as such.

"To be honest, it's probably packed with talent at the moment. So I don't know if Rudoni is going to be the right one to go box to box when you've got a (Kiernan) Dewsbury Hall, you've got a (James) Garner, you've got a Harrison Armstrong.

"So I don't really see Everton chasing him. There is no doubt, though, that if it were an audition for Everton, he played very well in the game at Hill Dickinson recently."

Football Insider revealed earlier this week that Coventry are "confident" of keeping hold of Rudoni long-term, if they can survive relegation this season.

The Sky Blues are understood to be optimistic that their star may commit to a new contract if they can secure top-flight football for another year.

Nevertheless, Newcastle are sure to be playing close attention to that situation as it unfolds, with Rudoni's current deal set to expire in 2028.

The Magpies began a revamp of their midfield in the summer, and view Rudoni as another option to refresh their group moving forward.
