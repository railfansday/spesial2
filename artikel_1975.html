Irish media are wrong about Celtic star Liam Scales, the stats tell the real story

Irish media have wrongly praised Celtic's Liam Scales after he was hooked off early in the Republic of Ireland's win over Kosovo.

Scales has divided opinion at Celtic for several seasons since becoming a first-team regular.

He has retained a regular starting spot in the Hoops' backline under Martin O'Neill, making 18 appearances so far this term.

The 28-year-old earned his 21st Republic of Ireland cap as they beat Kosovo 3-0 in the Nations League on home turf on Tuesday night.

National media have let him off the hook with several strong reviews of his performance even though he was taken off at half-time after an early booking.

He will now return to Parkhead aiming to stay in Celtic's starting eleven as they host St Mirren on Saturday afternoon.

Scales started on the left of Ireland's back three as they hosted Kosovo on their final Nations League group fixture of the 2026-27 competition.

He was shown a booking just four minutes in, which was unfortunate as he caught Vedat Muriqi with an arm on his neck rather than his face, but also gave the referee a decision to make and left him walking a tightrope for the rest of a key game.

Scales went on to make just three defensive contributions and won just two of his five attempted aerial duels in a pretty poor showing for 45 minutes.

He was taken off at the break, likely due to that yellow, yet some Irish media outlets saw fit to praise his contribution to the eventual 3-0 win.

and RTE praised his "valuable contribution"

While he was not particularly impressive for Ireland, Scales will hope to continue his fine Celtic form this weekend against St Mirren.

He has netted twice in his last three Premiership games for the Hoops, including a winner against Kilmarnock, and is one of the statistically best centre-backs in the league so far.

Scales ranks highly among his peers for successful pass percentage (89.5), line-breaking passes (18.8) per 90 and touches (110) per 90.

He is also a leading defender for recoveries (3.92) per 90, successful dribbles (0.27) per 90 and duels won (5.66) per 90.
