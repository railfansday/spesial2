HeartBeam, Inc. (BEAT -1.78%) reported that heart attack detection is its primary strategic priority, supported by the confirmation of a 510(k) regulatory pathway with the FDA. The company stated that its Breakthrough Device Designation and enrollment in the TAP program have facilitated frequent communication with regulators and payers regarding pivotal study design and reimbursement pathways. Financial results showed a reduction in operating expenses following the completion of primary product development for the HeartBeam cardiac platform and the ALIGN-ACS pilot study. Management indicated that clinical data from the ALIGN-ACS study demonstrated that the handheld 3D ECG device has diagnostic accuracy comparable to a standard 12-lead ECG. The company is currently finalizing its U.S. pivotal study design and expects to commence patient enrollment by the first quarter of 2027.
