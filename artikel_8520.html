I would think there’d be 2 modes, accumulation and spend.

1) In accumulation the nearest rung matures and the proceeds are used to fund the furthest rung.

2) In spend each year you spend the nearest rung until all the rungs are gone. After the last rung the fund (your investment) goes to zero.

So in 1) it looks like a bond fund.

2) is where you see the benefit, because each rung matures AND you’re not adding a rung on the far end. This is where you pick up the benefit of the decreasing duration, lower volatility as you approach the time you want the money.

So you could start a 5-year ladder and let it grow over 37 years. But in year 38 you spend the nearest rung, the ladder then has 4 rungs…and so on til there are no more rungs.
