Personal Consumer Issues • Re: When is it “too late” to come back to a second career?

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

I think it depends on your skill set, attitude, network, and the need / demand for you in your industry. I returned to my 7 year second career in Defense Bus Development after 2 and 1/2 years away at age 53 then worked until I wanted to re-re retire at age 60. I did some part-time work in the defense industry during my time away.

I was a retired military flyer with a good understanding of the use of surveillance radars and had learned in industry how the defense acquisition process worked (an in demand skill set). I worked hard, left my job on good terms with my co-workers, stayed in touch with my network, and was humble when I interviewed w/ prev coworkers and when I asked my boss for my old job back.

When re entering the work force I told them the truth, I wanted to stay busier and felt I still had something to contribute to help winning new business. And we did win some new ground based radar business.

Of course there was some luck involved. And I doubled my investments working / investing the additional income from age 53 to 60 (2014-2020).
That is a great story and sounds like it was fulfilling for all. Thank you for your service.
