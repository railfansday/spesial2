Momentous 61st Venice Biennale Ends With 2nd-Highest Visitor Turnout

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Political disputes, a historic strike, and the curator's sudden passing did not keep visitors away.

The 61st Venice Biennale, curated by the late Koyo Kouoh, concluded yesterday, November 22, reporting 714,015 visitors during its six-month run. Nearly 30,000 visitors attended the pre-opening of In Minor Keys, which included a record-high 100 participating countries.

Despite running three weeks shorter than previous editions to allow for the installation of the renovated Central Pavilion at the Giardini, In Minor Keys is the second highest-attended exhibition in the event's history. (The 2022 edition holds the record, counting more than 800,000 visitors.) The news follows the highest-ever visitor numbers at the most recent editions of the Venice Architecture Biennale and Venice International Film Festival, two other landmark events organized under the La Biennale di Venezia.

Following Kouoh's unprecedented death in May 2025 at the age of 57, her team of collaborators — Rasha Salti, Marie Hélène Pereira, Gabe Beckhurst Feijoo, Siddhartha Mitter, and Rory Tsapayi — carried out the exhibition of 109 international artists, many of whom hailed from Africa and the Caribbean diasporas.
