New Mexico vs Wyoming Prediction: Is This the Mountain West Championship?

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

There's still an outside shot that the Mountain West champion could be in the College Football Playoff mix, but for now, just getting to the conference title would be enough.It's a loaded conference with teams trying to make it into the Mountain West Championship, with five teams having two league losses or fewer. Wyoming and New Mexico each have one conference loss, setting up a whopper of a showdown on Friday night.

Wyoming lost to North Dakota State, but if it wins this, it's in the conference title game with UConn to close out the regular season.

New Mexico lost to UNLV last week, but if it wins, it's in the conference title game if UNLV loses to San Jose State. Otherwise, if the Lobos win this and beat Air Force, they're in.

It's the 77th all-time showdown between these two. New Mexico won 12-2 in 1931, and Wyoming won the last meeting last season 49-45.

New Mexico can take away Wyoming's ability to control the tempo and the game.It's among the best teams in the nation in the time of possession battle, the pass rush and ability to get into the backfield are dominant, and no one is rushing on this bunch. The Lobos are third in the nation against the run, allowing just 76 yards per game, and they haven't allowed more than 170 yards on the ground.Wyoming doesn't move the chains, there's not much of a passing offense, and it turns the ball over too much. Nevada is the only team that didn't turn it over multiple times against the New Mexico defense.

So how, exactly, did UNLV beat Wyoming? The Lobos had it in the bag up 17-7 with 12 minutes to play, but two big plays from the Rebel offense pulled it off. That's what the Cowboys have to do.Wyoming has to pick its spots. It won't be able to pull off too many long drives, so it'll have to own the field position battle, and in this low scoring game, when there's a chance to put up points, it has to come through. But here's Wyoming's issue …

This isn't the old Wyoming – it likes to open it up a little bit and get into offensive fun shows. That's not New Mexico.The Cowboys got the offense going against UNLV, and the Rebels played along. On the year, they're 7-0 when scoring more than 13 points in regulation. New Mexico has allowed 16 points or fewer in every game but two.Wyoming's defense will be great, New Mexico's defense will be great, and it'll be a tight fight that comes down to field goals. Wyoming's Erik Sandvik is one of the best kickers in the nation, but his huge day won't be quite enough.
