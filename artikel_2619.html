Commanders vs. Cardinals Thursday injury report: Treylon Burks limited

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Treylon Burks has been bothered with a foot injury for several weeks. The veteran Commanders receiver initially suffered the injury weeks ago and Washington has been trying their best to manage it while keeping him in the lineup.

He missed the Rams game but has found a way to play in every other game, and the strategy of limiting his practice work has been effective in getting him ready for game day.

It's clearly limiting his production, but his presence is key to keeping this offense moving.

Cornerback Amik Robertson missed a second straight day with an ankle injury that kept him out of the Bengals game. If he can't go on Sunday against the Arizona Cardinals, we'll see a combination of Rasul Douglas on the outside and Mike Sainristil and possible Quan Martin and Fabian Moreau on the inside.

Defensive lineman Charles Omenihu (rib) was limited for a second straight day. Everyone else on the active roster was a full-go.

Here's the Comanders participation report:

CB Amik Robertson – Dealing with an ankle issue from a Week 11 practice

WR Treylon Burks – Still dealing with a foot issue

TE John Bates – Hurt his shoulder vs. Eagles

S Quan Martin – Still dealing with a shoulder problem

QB Jayden Daniels – Still listed with Week 2 elbow dislocation

TE Chig Okonkwo – Dealing with a back injury from Giants game

TE Colson Yankoff – A knee injury suffered in Week 8

LB Sonny Styles – Has a new ankle problem
