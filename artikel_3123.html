Tanggal: 2026-11-13

That same engineering depth is also producing protected intellectual property. On Tuesday, we announced that the U.S.

Patent and Trademark Office has granted Nortech 2 patents covering our active optical cable capabilities. These patents solve 2 fundamental engineering challenges for our customers. The first is bringing high-speed fiber optics into strong magnetic fields. Our patented connector is built so that every key component is nonmagnetic. That gives customers the speed, lightweight and EMI immunity of fiber in environments such as MRI systems, where components with magnetic parts ideally should not go. The second is monitoring cable health without adding noise to sensitive systems. Our patented design builds diagnostics directly into the cable to track conditions such as optical power, voltage and temperature in real time.

Because diagnostic electronics can introduce electrical noise, the design also allows the whole system to pause those diagnostics on command during noise-sensitive operations, such as an imaging scan. Our customers no longer have to choose between monitoring their cables and protecting signal quality. Innovation is a core value here at Nortech and remains an important element of our long-term strategy. Our engineering and R&D work is focused on helping customers solve complex connectivity challenges with technologies that are ruggedized, lighter, faster, more sustainable and more affordable. And because every application we deliver is custom engineered, protected IP like this strengthens our position in the high-reliability programs where our customers need us most.

These advantages are particularly valuable in medical devices, imaging systems, aerospace, defense, drone and satellite applications where reliability, weight reduction and EMI immunity are critical.
