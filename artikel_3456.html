Apple just announced the MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display, the company’s first touchscreen Mac.

It features a tandem OLED display, M5 Pro and M5 Max chip options, up to 24 hours of battery life, a Dynamic Island for notifications, alerts, and activities, and more. It also marks the long-requested return of the glowing Apple logo on the lid.

It starts at $2,899 for the 14-inch model and $3,399 for the 16-inch model, and preorders start today, with deliveries beginning November 6.
