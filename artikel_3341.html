8 Must-Have PlayStation Accessories To Add to Your Black Friday Wishlist

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

A PlayStation setup rarely stays the same as it was on the day you brought it home. The console might still be running perfectly well, but there’s always something a gamer could upgrade or replace, whether it’s an aging controller, a better headset, or an accessory that makes the overall setup more convenient.

Black Friday, being one of the biggest sale events of the year for tech and games, is a good time to finally make those upgrades without spending quite as much. To make it easier for you, I’ve handpicked 8 PlayStation accessories worth checking out this Black Friday, covering everything from everyday essentials to more specialized additions, including controllers, audio gear, console covers, and accessibility equipment. Just make sure you have your cards ready to swipe before those sale timers run out.

Where to buy: Amazon, Best Buy, GameStop, PlayStation Direct, and select colors at Target and Walmart

A spare controller is one of those purchases that doesn’t seem particularly exciting until you actually need one. Whether your existing DualSense has developed stick drift, its battery doesn’t last as long as it used to, or you simply want another controller for convenience, Black Friday is a good time to pick one up.

The standard DualSense Wireless Controller is currently available for $54, down from its regular $74 price. That’s a $20 saving on one of the most frequently used accessories in a PS5 setup. Several colors are included in the deal, such as Midnight Black, White, Galactic Purple, Starlight Blue, Cosmic Red, and Gray Camouflage—depending on the retailer you choose. The DualSense remains a big part of what makes playing on PS5 enjoyable. Its haptic feedback gives you a better sense of different in-game surfaces and actions, while the adaptive triggers can change their resistance depending on what’s happening on screen.

A second controller is also handy if you have friends or family over for local multiplayer. You can keep one charging while using the other, so you don’t have to pause a session when the battery runs low.

If you’re looking to upgrade your headphones, the Nacon RIG 900 MAX HS is the one I’d recommend this Black Friday. Unlike many third-party gaming headsets, this one’s officially licensed for PlayStation 5, so you can enjoy wireless gaming audio without worrying about compatibility. It’s currently available for $149.99 on PlayStation Direct, down from its regular $249.90 price—a saving of approximately $100, or 40% off.

The RIG 900 MAX HS combines low-latency 2.4GHz wireless connectivity with Bluetooth 5.2, allowing you to connect to your PS5 while also having the option to use compatible Bluetooth devices. It features 40mm high-sensitivity drivers designed to deliver louder audio with less distortion, making it suitable for everything from competitive multiplayer matches to story-driven games with detailed soundtracks.

Nacon advertises over 50 hours of use on its 2.4GHz wireless connection and up to 60 hours over Bluetooth. You also get a dedicated charging base, so you can simply place the headset on its dock between gaming sessions instead of constantly reaching for a charging cable.

The headset has a self-adjusting headband, breathable fabric earcups, and a flip-to-mute microphone for multiplayer communication. You can also customize equalizer settings and microphone sensitivity through Nacon’s companion app.

Where to buy: PlayStation Direct and Best Buy.

If you don’t have a dedicated gaming monitor to use with your PlayStation console or simply looking to upgrade to a beast, this popular 27-inch Sony model is currently available for $299 on PlayStation Direct and around $299.99 at Best Buy, down from its comparable regular price of $349.99.

The monitor features a QHD IPS panel with a 2560 x 1440 resolution, HDR support, and variable refresh rate. It can reach up to 120Hz when connected to a PS5. However, the advertised 240Hz maximum applies to compatible PCs and Macs, so don’t mistake that figure for the console’s refresh-rate ceiling. It also comes with two HDMI 2.1 inputs, a DisplayPort connection, USB ports, built-in stereo speakers, and a headphone jack. This gives you enough connectivity to use it with your PS5 and another device without constantly swapping cables.

The little detail that makes this monitor particularly interesting for PlayStation players is its DualSense charging hook. You can hang your controller there when you’re done playing to conveniently charge it up when idle.

Running out of storage on your PS5 is annoying, especially when you have to keep deleting games to make room for new releases. Installing an additional SSD is a much better solution, but there’s one problem: SSDs aren’t exactly cheap these days, particularly those high-capacity drives designed for gaming consoles. With storage and hardware prices remaining on the expensive side, Black Friday sales could be the perfect opportunity to grab some of those increasingly rare discounts on premium SSDs.

The SanDisk 2TB Optimus GX PRO 850P is one such deal worth checking out. It’s currently available for $569.99 at Best Buy, down from its regular $669.99 price. That’s a $100 saving on a drive officially licensed for PS5 and PS5 Pro consoles, and if you ask me, it’s one of the best futureproof offers you could grab right now.

With 2TB of additional storage, you can keep more games installed without constantly managing your console's internal storage. The drive uses a PCIe Gen 4 NVMe interface and advertises sequential read speeds of up to 7,300MB/s. It also comes with a dedicated heatsink designed for the PS5’s M.2 expansion slot, so you don't have to purchase one separately.

Even at $100 off, it’s still a fairly expensive accessory—and the most expensive suggestion on this list—so make sure you actually need the extra 2TB and that you actually expand your library regularly with demanding games before spending that $570.

Fighting game fans may prefer an arcade stick to a standard controller, and the HORI Alpha Fighting Stick is a perfect pick for them. This is a more specialized PS5 accessory, but it gives games built around precise directional inputs and button combinations a more realistic feel.

It’s currently listed at $159.99 on PlayStation Direct, compared with its $219.99 recommended retail price. If you’ve been wanting to shift from the traditional controller for your Tekken or Street Fighter sessions, the $60 discount might be just the motivation you needed to finally grab the fighting stick.

HORI has equipped the stick with its HAYABUSA joystick and high-speed buttons, so this is one of the best fighting sticks you can get at this price. The top panel can be opened for easier customization and maintenance, and the artwork can be replaced if you want to change its appearance. It also has a tournament lock to prevent accidental button presses during competitive play. The controller is compatible with PS5, PS4, and Windows PC. You also get a 10-foot cable, a touchpad, and a 3.5mm audio jack for connecting a headset. The companion app lets you create up to four custom profiles and switch between them using the function button.

Note that this isn’t a general-purpose controller like your DualSense, and it may not feel worthwhile if fighting games are only an occasional diversion for you. However, if you are a regular to the genre of punches and kicks, an arcade stick can feel more natural and interactive than a traditional gamepad.

Not every PlayStation accessory needs to improve performance. Sometimes, you just want your console to look a little different and, of course, not accumulate dust or endure accidental scratches. Console covers for the PS5 are one of those often-overlooked accessories, but they’re worth considering if you want to customize your device with some colors, refresh worn panels, or simply protect your setup.

Sony has discounted its console covers across the PS5 lineup this Black Friday, with options available for the original PS5 Disc Edition, original Digital Edition, and both Slim models. Prices start at just $29 and go up to $59, depending on the console model and finish. Some options are discounted by as much as $26, making this a good opportunity to pick up an official set without paying full retail price.

There are plenty of colors and finishes to choose from, including Midnight Black, Cobalt Blue, Volcanic Red, Sterling Silver, and some of Sony’s more distinctive Chroma designs. Whether you want a color that matches the rest of your gaming setup or simply prefer something different from the standard white panels, there's likely an option worth checking out.

Console covers might not be the first accessory that comes to mind when upgrading a PS5, but they’re an economical way to give your console a fresh look. And since covers for all the major PS5 models are on sale right now, gamers can take advantage of these discounts rather than putting off the purchase for another day. Make sure you select the covers designed for your particular PS5 model, as the original and Slim editions aren’t interchangeable.

Where to buy: PlayStation Direct and Walmart

For gamers who love to travel, you don’t have to leave your precious console behind anymore. Carrying a PS5 with all its accessories around can be a lot of trouble: you’d need something that keeps the device protected while giving you enough room to pack your controllers, cables, and other essentials. Thankfully, Nacon’s Backpack for the PS5 is designed to make a traveling gamer’s life a little easier, and it’s currently available for $79.99 instead of its regular $99.99 price.

The backpack has a dedicated compartment for your console, along with a second main section for smaller accessories. There are two storage pockets for DualSense controllers, an additional zipped pocket, and two external bottle pockets. It also has another zipped pocket on the outside for smaller items you might need to access quickly.

A carrying handle and adjustable shoulder straps with a chest strap give you a couple of ways to carry it. The interior is designed to keep your equipment organized instead of leaving your controller, cables, and other accessories rattling around in the same compartment.

Needless to say, it’s an excellent accessory for players who move their PS5 regularly or for anyone who wants to easily carry their console around when required. Whether you want to take your console to a friend’s place, bring it with you on your vacation, or simply want to pack up your console when it isn’t in use, Nacon’s Backpack has your back.

If you’re a fan of racing games, a standard DualSense controller can only take you so far. Titles like Gran Turismo and Forza Horizon offer a much more involved driving experience when paired with a dedicated racing wheel, and the Logitech G29 is a great entry point for anyone looking to build their first sim racing setup. The G29 is currently available for $199.99 at Target and Best Buy, down from its regular $299.99 price. That’s a substantial $100 saving, making this Black Friday deal particularly tempting for racing sim enthusiasts.

The wheel features dual-motor force feedback, allowing you to feel resistance and movement as you navigate corners, accelerate, and brake. It offers up to 900 degrees of rotation, giving you more natural steering control than you'd get with a conventional controller. You also get a set of three floor pedals for acceleration, braking, and clutch input. Logitech has included a leather-wrapped steering wheel, stainless steel paddle shifters, and a range of PlayStation controls built into the wheel itself. The G29 is compatible with PS5, PS4, and PC, so you can use it across multiple platforms.

Of course, this is a more specialized purchase than a regular controller or headset. But if Gran Turismo 7 and other racing titles are a regular part of your gaming routine, the G29 is a worthwhile upgrade. Saving $100 makes it an even better opportunity to finally get into sim racing without paying full price.

You’d likely not be able to relate to all 8 of these accessories, so your Black Friday purchases should depend on what your setup is missing. If your controller needs replacing, the $54 DualSense is an easy purchase to justify. Those looking for better audio can consider the officially licensed Nacon RIG 900 MAX HS, while players who want to move beyond a regular gamepad have the HORI Alpha Fighting Stick and Logitech G29 racing wheel to choose from.

I’d also pay attention to the storage and monitor deals, since these purchases will go the longest way. With SSDs becoming increasingly expensive, a $100 saving on the SanDisk 2TB drive is worth considering if you’re running out of space. Sony’s 27-inch gaming monitor is another substantial upgrade for anyone planning a dedicated gaming desk setup. The console covers and Nacon backpack are slightly niche purchases, but if you’ve had them in your mind, this is the best time to grab them.

Ultimately, prioritize accessories you’ll actually use rather than get carried away by the discounts. Black Friday is a good opportunity to pick up the PlayStation upgrades you’ve been postponing, so compare prices across retailers, check availability, and make the most of those deals before they disappear.
