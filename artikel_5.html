ICG: "It's hard to imagine" Tripoli would risk its ties with Russia by approving a Ukrainian attack

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Angela Diffley is pleased to welcome Claudia Gazzini, International Crisis Group's Senior Analyst for Libya. The Ukrainian attack on a Russian warship off the coast of eastern Libya has raised questions about Russian and Ukrainian military activity in the country. Gazzini discusses Russia's established military presence in eastern Libya and allegations that Ukrainian forces are operating in the west. She notes that while Russia is militarily present in eastern Libya, it also maintains "good commercial ties and political ties with western authorities as well." For Gazzini, the suggestion that Tripoli would approve such an operation "just sounds a bit too hazardous."
