Malaysia's Pension Fund Favors Infrastructure Assets

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Malaysia's Employees Provident Fund CEO Ahmad Zulqarnain Onn says the EPF favors infrastructure in 2027. It's one of the world's oldest and largest pension funds, with total investment assets of over $350 billion. He speaks with Haslinda Amin on "Insight with Haslinda Amin." (Source: Bloomberg)
