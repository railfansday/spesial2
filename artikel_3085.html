Gilead Sciences Has Raised Its Dividend for 11 Straight Years. Here’s Why the Streak Looks Safe.

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Gilead's consistent cash flows from HIV therapies help sustain its dividend.

By James Halley – Nov 22, 2026 at 9:53AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Gilead Sciences ›

Gilead Sciences (GILD -0.06%) increased its dividend by 3.8% this year, the 11th consecutive year of dividend increases. Its dividend yield is above average, at 1.94%, based on the stock's current price.

The company holds a dominant position in HIV therapies, and these drugs provide dependable cash flow for the near future. Gilead is also seeing expansions into other areas, including oncology, liver, and inflammation therapies.

There are three reasons why Gilead's dividend is a safe bet to continue to grow.

Over nine months, Gilead produced $10.6 billion in free cash flow, up 68% year over year, leaving its cash dividend payout ratio at just below 30%. Spending less than a third of its cash flow on its dividend leaves this pharmaceutical company with plenty of funds to buy back stock, fund research and development, and increase its dividend.

The company's lead drug, Biktarvy, brought in $4.1 billion in the third quarter, up 10% year over year. It commands a 53% U.S. market share and holds patent exclusivity through 2036.

In the meantime, its newer HIV therapies, such as Yeztugo and Descovy, are rapidly improving sales. Descovy revenue rose 49% year over year to $1 billion in the third quarter, and Yeztugo's sales jumped 682% from the same period a year ago to $305 million.

While HIV therapies remain its primary revenue source, the company has worked hard to broaden its portfolio.

Breast cancer drug Trodelvy has become a blockbuster product, one of seven Gilead medications that bring in more than $1 billion a year. Trodelvy recently obtained European approval in combination with pembro to treat first-line PD-L1-positive metastatic triple-negative breast cancer. The therapy brought in $506 million in the third quarter, up 42% over the same period a year ago.

Gilead has seven clinical-stage antibody-drug conjugate oncology programs in the pipeline.

The company reported revenue of $203 million in the quarter for liver disease treatment Livdelzi, up 93% year over year. Gilead's pipeline includes 49 clinical programs, including more than a dozen inflammation and immunology candidates such as emvistegrast, which showed strong phase-2 response rates in ulcerative colitis.
