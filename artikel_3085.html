Malibu Boats, Inc. (MBUU -17.05%) reported quarterly growth driven by the acquisition of Saxdor despite a softening retail environment in the ski-wake category. Management noted that higher interest rates and cautious consumer behavior continue to impact payment buyers, particularly in the towboat segment. In response, the company is managing wholesale shipments with discipline to protect dealer health while leveraging vertical integration and sourcing scale. The company transitioned its fiscal year end to December 31, with production now underway for Saxdor models at its Fort Pierce facility to better serve North American markets.
