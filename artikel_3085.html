Workday (WDAY +1.79%) reported third-quarter results highlighted by an acceleration in AI-driven bookings and an upward revision to its full-year profitability and revenue guidance. Management reported that the company is evolving its business model through the expansion of flex credits and the general availability of its agentic platform, which allows customers to deploy autonomous agents for HR, finance, and IT workflows. The company recently completed its acquisition of C1.ai to integrate identity governance for both human and AI workers, positioning itself as a central security layer for the hybrid enterprise. Management stated that the innovation cycle has reached its highest velocity since the company's inception, with new products like Workday Data Cloud and Sana meeting robust customer interest across global markets.
