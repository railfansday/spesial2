Geospace: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

On its fourth-quarter fiscal year 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 20, 2026, Geospace (GEOS -31.42%) announced a net loss of $44.3 million on $80.7 million in full-year revenue. Long-term investors may weigh whether cost reductions and a substantial backlog can restore profitability amid sharp demand declines in key segments. Three things management said stand out.

Geospace suffered a $44.3 million net loss in fiscal year 2026 as total revenue fell to $80.7 million. A contract backlog crossing $100 million across all three business segments could support management's goal of returning to profitability in fiscal year 2027.

Design changes by Petrobras previously delayed the Permanent Reservoir Monitoring, or PRM, project. Production is now underway, with project completions expected to generate revenue across the first three quarters of fiscal year 2027, and a cash infusion expected in early fiscal Q2.

Trade policy shifts, Middle East conflicts, and higher borrowing costs reduced order volumes across core markets. Management responded by cutting staff and trimming overhead expenses to align operating costs with lower sales volumes.

Working capital stands at $43.6 million, and a credit agreement with Amerisource Funding provides $13 million in available borrowing. Management relies on disciplined spending and milestone payments from the Petrobras project to maintain liquidity through early fiscal year 2027.

Cost cuts give the company some flexibility, and its long-term results depend on converting existing orders into sustained cash flow.
