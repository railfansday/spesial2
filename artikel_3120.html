Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Liquidity hit $2.3 billion as Golar pushes Argentina and FLNG expansion.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 18, 2026 at 1:46PM EST

Golar LNG (GLNG -4.48%) reported a transition in its operational fleet during the third quarter as it completed a long-term contract in Cameroon and repositioned assets for a multi-decade project in Argentina. Management reported a significant expansion of liquidity to $2.3 billion through multiple financing initiatives, intended to fund the conversion of the Esperanza and its fourth FLNG unit. The company stated that all regulatory milestones and infrastructure development for its Argentinian operations remain on schedule for start-up between 2027 and 2028. Management indicated that it is advancing commercial discussions for the long-term employment of its newest vessels while maintaining a disciplined capital allocation strategy that includes quarterly dividends and a share buyback program.
