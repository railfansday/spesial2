Why Marvell Stock Is Falling Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Marvell is getting hit twice: investors are fleeing AI data center stocks after the midterms, and its all-stock Lumentum deal could mean significant dilution.

Marvell Technology, Inc. (MRVL -13.60%) stock is down 13.4% as of 2:42 p.m. ET on Thursday, Nov. 5, after Democrats' midterm victory set off a sell-off in chip and AI infrastructure stocks.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC -3.95%) and the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -5.06%) are down 3.9% and 5.0%, respectively.

Investors are fleeing anything tied to AI data centers two days after Democrats flipped both houses of the U.S. Congress and won several key governorships.

Many Democrats ran on a platform that included increased oversight of the AI industry, with the party's new governors in Texas, Ohio, and Georgia -- major AI data center states -- promising construction moratoriums.

Marvell sells the networking chips that move data around inside those data centers -- it sits right in the blast zone.

But this comes just days after an all-stock deal was announced Monday to buy Lumentum (LITE -15.79%) -- an AI photonics maker -- worth $115 billion. That could lead to significant dilution for Marvell stock. But it also means that as Marvell stock drops, the value of the shares it is offering Lumentum falls as well. There's a risk the deal falls apart or the terms get renegotiated, and Marvell shareholders see even more dilution.
