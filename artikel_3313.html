‘Can’t Hardly Wait’ Stars Campaign For Sequel To 1998 Teen Movie: “Would Be So Fun”

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

After nearly 30 years, the cast of Can’t Hardly Wait is ready for a Huntington Hillside High reunion.

Having reprised their roles from the cult classic 1998 teen film for Halloween, Peter Facinelli and Joel Michaely noted that fans “have been clamoring” for them to make a sequel for years, suggesting they make their interest known to Sony Pictures.

“I brought Mike Dexter back for Halloween, and weeks later the internet is still talking about it,” Facinelli told TMZ of his bully jock character.

“A Can’t Hardly Wait sequel is the one high school reunion everyone actually wants to go to. Would be so fun to get the whole gang back together and see where they ended up,” he added. “If we’re lucky, LoveBurger might finally play a song — and maybe, just maybe, if I play my cards right I can finally get Amanda Beckett [Jennifer Love Hewitt] back.” Watch on Deadline

Joel Michaely, who played one of the “X-Phile” nerds, noted, “People have been clamoring for us to do a reunion movie for years. Now is the time for our 30th anniversary in 2028. While we all still look hot.”

“We need Sony to make it or partner with a streaming service like Netflix or Amazon, so tell them you want to see it,” he added.

In Can’t Hardly Wait, six high school seniors attend their grad night house party, where Preston Meyers (Ethan Embry) plans to give his long time crush Amanda (Hewitt) a love letter after their chance encounter four years before.

The star-studded ensemble also included Seth Green, Lauren Ambrose, Charlie Korsmo, Selma Blair, Jason Segel, Melissa Joan Hart, Jerry O’Connell, Donald Faison, Jaime Pressly, Clea DuVall, Leslie Grossman, Breckin Meyer, Sara Rue, Sean Patrick Thomas, Amber Benson, Marisol Nichols, Jennifer Elise Cox and more.
