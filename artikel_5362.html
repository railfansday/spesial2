Mindgruve Appoints Former WPP Executive Steve Terjeson as Vice President of Technology

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Former Choreograph technology leader to accelerate AI innovation, advance Mindgruve's proprietary Sightline platform, and scale enterprise technology capabilities.

SAN DIEGO, Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Mindgruve, a modern media, commerce, and data technology company, today announced the appointment of Steve Terjeson as Vice President of Technology. Terjeson will lead Mindgruve's software development and data engineering teams, advancing the agency's established technology capabilities while accelerating innovation, scalability, and enterprise integration. A key priority will be driving the next generation of Sightline, Mindgruve's proprietary predictive marketing and business intelligence platform.

"Technology and data intelligence have long been central to how Mindgruve delivers measurable business growth for our clients," said Chad Robley, CEO of Mindgruve. "Sightline gives us a differentiated advantage that even the largest holding companies are working to replicate. Steve brings the enterprise technology experience to accelerate that advantage, scale our innovation, and deliver the sophistication of a global holding company with the agility and speed of an independent agency."

Terjeson joins Mindgruve from WPP's Choreograph, where he served as Vice President and acting CTO of data and technology for North America. He led enterprise data and technology delivery for global brands, managed a globally distributed engineering team, and developed sophisticated cloud data systems connecting brands' data to leading platforms, including Google and Adobe. He previously held senior roles at Wunderman Thompson (WPP), The Richards Group, and Costco Travel, with client experience spanning IBM, Uber, The Home Depot, and NBCUniversal.

"I've worked with marketing technology at nearly every scale throughout my career, and what Mindgruve has built stands alongside the very best," said Steve Terjeson, Vice President of Technology at Mindgruve. "The opportunity to build on an already sophisticated technology ecosystem, accelerate Sightline's evolution, and bring even more powerful AI and data-driven solutions to global brands is incredibly compelling. Mindgruve has the technology, talent, and entrepreneurial culture to move faster and deliver greater impact for clients."

In his new role, Terjeson will oversee Mindgruve's technology strategy and roadmap, with a focus on expanding the agency's AI, software engineering, cloud infrastructure, and data integration capabilities. He will also help accelerate the evolution of Sightline, strengthening its predictive intelligence, enterprise scalability, and ability to connect marketing investments directly to business outcomes. His leadership will further enhance the agency's commitment to delivering secure, flexible, and enterprise-grade technology solutions that enable smarter decisions, stronger marketing performance, and more profitable growth.
