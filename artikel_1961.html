Leeds United may have to part with a "huge" fee to land Dinamo Zagreb star Dion Beljo, sources have told Football Insider.

The 24-year-old is high on the list of targets at Elland Road, with scouts keeping a close eye on his performances as a result of his outstanding goalscoring record this season.

Daniel Farke putting plans in place to strengthen his attacking options ahead of the January transfer window, as he aims to land competiton for Dominic Calvert-Lewin.

However, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Dinamo Zagreb are unlikely to allow Beljo to leave in January and could delay Leeds' plans.

The Whites want to sign a new striker in the mid-season window to aid them in their ambitious plans for the season, so waiting until the summer to do a deal may not be an option.

However, should they be prepared to delay their move, there is a growing chance Beljo could be made available for a big fee come the end of the season if his form continues.

Football Insider revealed on 15 November that Leeds are plotting a move to sign Beljo after being impressed by his performance against Sunderland in the Europa League.

The 24-year-old is enjoying an outstanding campaign both domestically and in Europe, where he has scored 19 goals and provided nine assists in 20 games across all competitions.

Leeds are extremely high on the striker, but fear they could miss out in January, despite suggestions a significant offer could tempt his club to sell.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Half the Premier League are probably looking at Beljo right now as well.

"He's got 19 goals in 20 games in all competitions. Obviously, he enhanced his reputation by scoring against Sunderland in the Europa League earlier this month as well.

"Beljo is a player that Leeds are monitoring, but I don't expect any move in January for him because I think Dinamo Zagreb will be desperate to keep hold of him.

"He's under contract until 2030 and they've put a huge price tag on his head as well trying to ward off that interest.

"I think if he continues his rich vein of goalscoring for him I think you'll have a lot of clubs across Europe ready to try and sign the Croatian international next summer, but not in January."

Beljo's goalscoring exploits across Europe this season have placed a number of top clubs on high alert, not just in the Premier League, but elswhere on the continent.

Leeds are among his English admirers, but are certainly not alone, with every club in need of a new striker keeping a close eye on his performances.

Football Insider revealed last month that Everton have been keeping tabs on Beljo's performances, and have also targeted him as a potential solution to their striker woes.

David Moyes' side are seriously short of recognised strikers, with Beto's exit in the summer leaving Thierno Barry as their only senior centre-forward.

However, much like Leeds, they could be left disappointed if Zagreb stick to their stance of not selling Beljo in the January transfer window.
