Tovah Feldshuh Reveals If She'll Return to 'Nobody Wants This' After Drama

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Tovah Feldshuh revealed whether she will appear on Nobody Wants This season 4 after offscreen tension over Adam Brody‘s public support for Palestine.

“Do I hope for [my character] Bina to be written in? Heavily so,” Feldshuh, 77, exclusively told Us ahead of PaleyImpact’s “One-on-One with Tovah Feldshuh” panel at the Paley Museum in New York City on Monday, November 23.

Feldshuh weighed in on whether the hit Netflix series would have larger conversations about the ongoing conflict between Israel and Palestine, adding, “I imagine there’ll be no politics whatsoever about Zionism. But nonetheless, Bina is the sturdy anchor to old world [Jewish] values — some of which are cliche because they work and they’ve worked for centuries.”

The actress ensured she’s fully prepared to continue playing Brody’s onscreen mother.

“I hope Bina lives for at least five seasons,” she continued. “And I love working on Nobody Wants This and with everybody in the cast.”

Nobody Wants This, which premiered in 2024, follows the love story between agnostic Joanne (Kristen Bell) and unconventional rabbi Noah (Brody). While Nobody Wants This has resonated with viewers throughout the years, the show recently made headlines when Brody, 46, shared his personal political views.

“I have spoken a lot about [the Israel and Palestine conflict] privately. I haven’t spoken much about it publicly,” he told GQ in October. “Put me down as thinking it’s a genocide. Put me down as wanting a free Palestine.”

Erin Foster, who cocreated the show, later spoke out on the “complicated” situation regarding her and Brody’s different perspectives.

“I think our work environment, our set is very similar to dinner tables around the world where people are having debate and discussion and disagreeing,” she said on the “Not Skinny But Not Fat” podcast as Brody’s comments went viral. “While I love Adam, I also disagree with him. And that’s OK.”

Feldshuh also disagreed with Brody’s point of view, sharing more insight into their dynamic while speaking with Us on Monday.

“After he said his comments, he asked to see me. So we’ve talked privately about it,” she said before clarifying there was “no animosity” between her and Brody. “But [there is] profound disagreement. We’ll see hopefully if he will — based on fact and history — change his point of view.”

Feldshuh stood by her past statements of support for Israel.

“I’ve never directly addressed Adam in public, nor will I. We are colleagues so we can disagree without being disagreeable,” she explained. “However, I’m sure everybody is very clear about how I stand and this isn’t a political statement. This is a statement based on whether or not I have a right to live.”

Feldshuh concluded: “I’m not Israeli and neither is Erin Foster. But fortunately for me, she agrees with me and that’s lucky. I will not cross swords with Adam. He plays my son, and you know what else? He’s been an incredible friend to me. When they showed my documentary at the temple that hosted us, who came out of all the cast? Adam Brody. He sat next to me.”

Looking ahead, Feldshuh shared what she would like to explore on Nobody Wants This.

“I would love a flashback,” she said, also suggesting a possible cameo from her former Crazy Ex-Girlfriend costars. “Rachel [Bloom], of course needs to come on. Also Santino Fontana — I thought he was great. I thought Donna Lynne Champlin was fabulous.”

Nobody Wants This is streaming on Netflix.
