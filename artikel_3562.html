Baldur's Gate 3: Shadowheart Officially Confirmed For 2027 Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The arrival of Grand Theft Auto 6 has taken over most gaming discussion, but it wasn't too long ago that Baldur's Gate 3 was at the top of the pile. While it's no longer the center of every conversation, the game has proven to have legs, and its story is now set to be expanded next year.

Those waiting for a game expansion or Baldur's Gate 4 aren't likely to be in luck any time soon, as developer Larian Studios has moved on to a new project in the Divinity series instead. If you're open to returning to the world of the Forgotten Realms in a different medium, though, Shadowheart is getting the spotlight in a 2027 book.

Today, the official Titan Books X account announced Baldur's Gate 3: Shadowheart, a prequel novel set to release in May 2027. Rather than picking up where her story left off in the game (which could vary wildly depending on the playthrough), Baldur's Gate 3: Shadowheart dives into the cleric's past. If you've played through much of the game, you won't be surprised to learn that it focuses on her time as a devoted priestess of Shar, setting up a "brutal murder" for her to investigate.

The description also refers to the book as a "darkly sexy fantasy," so it sounds like it may be leaning more into the romantasy genre. On the other hand, the sexy part could simply refer to the allure of Shadowheart's personality. The book comes courtesy of Tasha Suri, the author of the Burning Kingdoms trilogy, which is more of a fantasy story that features romance than a full-on romantasy series.

Some Baldur's Gate 3 fans might receive the news of another book with a bit of trepidation, as the track record behind the idea is a bit controversial. Baldur's Gate 3: Astarion, which released recently, didn't go over particularly well with much of the community. Astarion's backstory touches on plenty of heavy topics, and the character's fans had plenty to say about how the book handled them.

Baldur's Gate 3: Shadowheart may also have to handle some loaded material, as Shadowheart has a dark backstory herself, although not in quite the same way as Astarion. With a different subject and a different author at the helm, though, it should still be on the radar of Baldur's Gate 3 fans. There's always a chance that it could deliver a meaningful narrative expansion to the game, although satisfying every Shadowheart lover would be a tall order.

Elsewhere in the realm of Baldur's Gate 3 tie-ins, a new comic series will be kicking off in February 2027, starting with an arc focused on Astarion that will hopefully go over more smoothly than the book did. And, of course, it's always possible for Dungeons & Dragons players to tell new Baldur's Gate 3 stories at the game table, giving the beloved game a chance to live forever in the hands of all of its fans.
