Stop Losing Background Jobs: Meet Masterly.NightShift for .NET

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Ever found yourself debugging a background task that mysteriously disappeared after an application restart? Or trying to coordinate multiple dependent jobs while dealing with retries, duplicate execution, and concurrency issues?

Masterly.NightShift is an open-source background job processing engine for .NET 8+, built on a modernized fork of Hangfire 1.8.25.

It brings durable execution, workflow orchestration, resilience, and observability together under a familiar API.

NightShift persists the job, processes it through a background worker, and manages its execution state.

The job remains available through application restarts, and failures can be handled through retry policies.

No separate Windows Service or mandatory message broker required.

Run jobs immediately, after a delay, on a recurring schedule, or after another job completes.

No complicated scheduling infrastructure for common background operations.

When an order is created, you need to process it, generate an invoice, notify the customer, and synchronize with an ERP.

Instead of embedding everything inside the HTTP request, define the operations as background jobs.

Your API responds immediately, while the worker processes the operation in the background.

For more complex processes, use job chains to express execution dependencies.
