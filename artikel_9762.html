Threshold: a camera-only check-in before a big morning

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Presage SmartSpectra SDK Challenge Submission

My entry for the Presage SmartSpectra SDK Challenge. #presagechallenge

Picture a student getting ready for a JAMB or WAEC exam, or someone waiting for a first job interview. They have a phone and a laptop, and may have no wearable. I built Threshold for that moment. It gives them a minute to sit still and see the pulse and breathing readings their camera can support, with an optional breathing guide afterward.

Threshold is a Windows desktop app. It asks for consent, helps the user frame their face and upper chest, and lets them choose whether to start a 60-second check. A completed result shows the pulse and breathing summaries available from that session. Vs your usual appears for a measure only after three eligible prior live days in the seven-day window. Readiness and confidence remain unverified, and HRV stays baseline pending.

The recording shows the live chest-breathing trace, but this check stopped early because of a low frame rate. It does not show a completed 60-second check. Three earlier product checks completed 60 seconds under different settings; their limitations are documented below.

Evidence so far: Three Camo/Infinix product runs completed 60 seconds, each with 640 × 480 coded derived frames. Local SDK callback cadence was 27.47, 27.80, and 28.55 FPS, calculated from timestamps on callbacks marked sent. Low-FPS warnings appeared near the end of the first and third checks and during part of the second. No SDK error was recorded in any of the three. I disabled Camo effects and automatic framing after the second check. The third was the first under the new quality rules and reported 6 eligible pulse estimates and 9 eligible breathing estimates. Accuracy and repeatability remain unverified. Earlier stopped attempts are excluded.

On Windows, start from the project root with Node.js 20 or later:

Choose I’m a judge or press J on the consent screen to open the walkthrough. To try the flow without a camera, SDK session, key, or credits, agree to consent, choose Continue with replay, and select Start replay.

Live testing requires your own Presage developer key configured locally using the repository’s setup instructions. After framing, choose Use live camera and confirm the credit cost for that run. Presage developer registration

Threshold uses SmartSpectra Node SDK 3.5.0 in Electron. It opens the selected camera at exact 640 × 480 with a 30 FPS target and creates a 640 × 480 live derived stream that preserves aspect ratio. Each output frame comes from a fresh camera frame. The SDK receives that stream and streams back pulse and breathing readings, an upper chest waveform, and validation states. Threshold updates the measurement tiles and trace as readings become available and translates SDK validation states into short coaching messages.

The resize step came from a capture problem. On Camo, a track reported 640 × 480 while its coded frames were 1280 × 720. I compared the direct path and the smaller stream in a standalone diagnostic that discarded its pixels. That test measured the local path up to a mock sink. The three completed Camo/Infinix checks above used the product with the native SDK.

After a completed check, the optional 4-7-8 guide follows a timer with the camera off. The user can stop it at any time.

Best Thoughtful Real-World Use. I wanted this to be usable on a consequential morning by someone who may have only a phone and laptop. The camera choice, framing tips, camera-free walkthrough, and one-click deletion make the check easier to understand and leave the user in control.

I used Codex to help implement and test the app. Transcript sharing is optional and no session is linked in this draft.
