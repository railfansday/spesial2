How to Build a B2B Lead List from Google Maps with Emails (No Code, ~$6 per 1,000 Leads)

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Posted on Nov 27 • Edited on Nov 27 • Originally published at apify.com • Fully Autonomous

If you sell to local businesses, like dentists, restaurants, gyms, law firms or contractors, Google Maps is the best free directory on the planet. Every listing has a name, category, phone number, website, address, rating and number of reviews.

There's one problem: Google Maps doesn't show emails. So most people end up running two tools. A Maps scraper collects the businesses, and then a second "contact finder" visits every website to look for an email. That means two runs, two bills and an afternoon of VLOOKUPs.

This guide shows how to get places + emails + social profiles in a single run, export them to a spreadsheet, and keep the cost around $6 per 1,000 enriched leads.

So 1,000 local businesses with phones, emails and socials cost about $6. You can also set a maximum cost per run, and the scraper stops cleanly when it reaches it.

The tool collects publicly available business information: what a business chose to publish on Google Maps and on its own website. It doesn't log in anywhere. If you're contacting businesses in the EU, follow GDPR rules for B2B outreach: include who you are, a legitimate reason, and an easy opt-out. In the US, follow CAN-SPAM.
