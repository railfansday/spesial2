M-tron Industries (MPTI) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Defense demand lifts backlog as new missile contracts loom ahead.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

M-tron Industries, Inc. (MPTI +0.98%) reported a profitable third quarter driven by sustained demand in the defense and aerospace markets, particularly for radar and electronic warfare systems. Management reported that the company is transitioning toward more program-based business, which now constitutes the majority of its aerospace and defense revenue. To address this demand, the company has increased its capital expenditures and initiated an active hiring program. Management stated that while fiscal 2027 will be a building year focused on scaling production, they anticipate revenue growth will accelerate into 2028 as new multiyear missile production contracts begin to impact the top line.

As a reminder, M-tron designs and manufactures highly engineered RF solutions, including electronic components and subassemblies used to control the frequency and timing of signals and electronic circuits. We're a global company with 3 manufacturing sites, 2 of them in the United States and 1 in India.

The company's primary markets include the defense and aerospace markets, commercial avionics, industrials and space. So we're pleased to report that the company continues to perform well with continued strength in our 2026 Q3 sales and backlog. Our revenues continue to be driven by defense-related orders. With consistent operating performance, we've been able to continue to make strategic investments in research and development, and our manufacturing capabilities and capacity, continue to improve the market profile of the company and prime the pump for future growth. This morning, we reported the following Q3 fiscal year '26 results.

Total revenues for the third quarter were $15.7 million, an 11.1% increase over the $14.2 million of revenue we reported in the third quarter of '25. The increase was primarily due to strong growth in aerospace and defense, avionics, and space shipments. Gross margin was 44.5% for the third quarter of '26 compared to 44.3% for the 3 months ended September 30, 2025. The slight increase was primarily due to product mix. Our gross profit in the period grew to $7 million, an 11.5% increase over the prior year's Q3 performance of $6.28 million.

Net income was $3 million or $0.70 per diluted share for the 3 months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $1.8 million or $0.60 per diluted share for the 3 months ended September 30, 2025. The increase was primarily due to the increase in revenue and the associated gross profit discussed above, and the higher interest income earned on investments and money market mutual funds, driven by higher balance -- cash balances invested. Adjusted EBITDA was $3.6 million for the 3 months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $3.2 million for the 3 months ended September 30, 2025. The slight increase was primarily due to higher revenues and gross profit, partially offset by higher general and administrative expenses.

We continue to execute on our strategy of continually moving into more program business, which now makes up the vast majority of our aerospace and defense revenue. The key growth areas in that sector for us this year has been radar and electronic warfare systems, with both areas growing at well over 100%. We are also a key supplier to numerous precision-guided munition programs, including many of the programs that were included in the 7-year framework agreements signed between the Department of War and Raytheon and Lockheed Martin in the early spring of 2026.

Despite the FY '27 federal defense budget not being signed, we have seen contract awards totaling more than $127 billion with Raytheon and Lockheed in multiyear contracts for several missile systems, including PAC-3, Tomahawk, SM-3 IB Interceptors, Standard Missile-6 and AMRAAM. The majority of these contracts have been 5-year production agreements with substantial increases in production volume. With this prioritization of missile replenishment, we expect to see significant increases in order quantities for 2028 and beyond for the programs that we support. We continue to innovate as evidenced by the high rate of revenue from newly designed products. Oven-controlled oscillators are growing quite strongly, finding demand for new radar systems from traditional vendors and Up-and-Comers alike.

We are also seeing strong interest for these products from our electronic warfare systems customers. There are many counter-UAS projects currently underway, and we are also competing for slots in a number of large radar programs. And many of you have seen the recent Boeing announcement about their 5% reduction in force in order to realign the size of their workforce with the current ramp of airframe deliveries. We are not alarmed by this as some near-term deliveries are being pushed further out and their near-term CapEx for that division is greater than the anticipated free cash flows.

We don't believe that this will impact our '26 results or 2027 growth, as both Boeing and Airbus order through their subcontractors and orders were expected to be slower in 2027. They are currently working through a prior 3-year order. Backlog remained strong through the quarter with a book-to-bill ratio slightly above 1. As we have said in the past, our bookings are very lumpy, and timing is subject to a lot of factors, including the current political environment in Washington.

We anticipate several large aerospace and defense orders originally anticipated for Q3 or Q4 of 2026, which we now believe will take place early in the new year, which means that our backlog will likely decrease some in Q4 and then strengthen quite a bit in Q1 2027. We continue to look at acquisition and strategic investment opportunities and execute on our 3-pronged inorganic growth strategy, which includes M-tron acting as a holding company, M-tron serving as a vehicle for larger corporate carve-outs or reverse mergers, and M-tron as an investment platform. We're currently executing on a transaction that would fit into the first category and have made corporate development hire to help accelerate the breadth of our efforts.

I'd like to thank our loyal employees for supporting the company in its mission of serving the nation and our dedicated customers for their continued business and partnership. M-tron plays a critical role in the defense of our nation by providing U.S.-sourced highly engineered components for many U.S. and allied military programs. Having a U.S.-based advanced manufacturing capability remains important, and we are looking to expand that capacity to support our national interest.
