Douglas Emmett, Inc. (DEI -2.22%) reported a recovery in net operating income to 2019 levels despite office occupancy remaining approximately 10 percentage points below historical highs. Management stated that the primary obstacle to FFO growth is the increased cost of debt, which has created a $135 million annual interest expense headwind compared to 2019 figures. The company reported its fourth consecutive quarter of positive office absorption and a significant quarterly increase in occupancy, supported by a lease-to-occupied spread that is nearly double the historical average. Strategic focus remains on completing high-yield residential redevelopments and pursuing acquisitions of top-tier office properties available at cyclical discounts.
