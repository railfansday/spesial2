Brock Purdy’s MVP chances could disappear sooner than you think

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

49ers quarterback Brock Purdy has made a strong case for winning the NFL's Most Valuable Player award.

But Purdy's chances of staying in the conversation hinge on how well he performs against the Vikings' defense, led by coordinator Brian Flores, on Sunday in Mexico City. At least that's what analyst Nick Wright believes.

Brock Purdy #13 of the San Francisco 49ers looks to pass against the Dallas Cowboys in Arlington, Texas, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. Getty Images

"I think he could get almost eliminated this weekend," Wright said on "First Things First."

"I don't even know that I think it's fair, but I wanna explain why I think there's real evidence to that. It shouldn't matter when your bad game happens, but it always does."

Purdy is coming off a fantastic performance against the Cowboys, but the 49ers quarterback will face his biggest challenge yet. History isn't exactly on San Francisco's side, either, as coach Kyle Shanahan is 0-5 against Flores as a defensive coordinator or head coach.

The 49ers spent the week before the NFC showdown in Colorado Springs, Colo., to acclimate to the high altitude the teams will face at Estadio Azteca.

San Francisco 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy (13) looks on during the fourth quarter against the Washington Commanders on Monday, Oct. 19, 2026, in Santa Clara, Calif. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

Minnesota Vikings defensive coordinator Brian Flores looks on during a game against the Buffalo Bills in Minneapolis, Monday, Nov. 9, 2026. Getty Images

While Purdy has done a superb job in leading the 49ers' offense this season, Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow and Bills quarterback Josh Allen are being heavily considered for MVP.

The final pick in the 2022 NFL Draft has always been underestimated, but a shaky performance against the Vikings could put an end to Purdy's MVP hopes in 2026.
