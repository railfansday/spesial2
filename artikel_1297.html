Arsenal are reportedly among the clubs interested in signing Matias Soule, with the Roma winger attracting significant attention from several European sides ahead of the upcoming transfer windows.

The Argentine has impressed in Italy this season, and his growing reputation could put him in contention for a move to the Premier League.

According to a report from Fussball News, Arsenal are monitoring Soule alongside Newcastle United, Aston Villa, Bournemouth and Sunderland.

The 23-year-old has registered four goals and five assists in 16 appearances across all competitions this season, establishing himself as an important figure in Gian Piero Gasperini’s attacking setup.

The Gunners have been watching attacking reinforcements, and Soule’s ability to operate on the right flank while cutting inside could make him an attractive option for Mikel Arteta.

The Argentina international has impressive technical qualities, creativity and the ability to contribute in front of goal, potentially adding another dimension to Arsenal’s final third.

Arsenal are likely to face considerable competition for his signature, with Borussia Dortmund, Stuttgart, Atletico Madrid, AC Milan and Napoli also reportedly interested.

However, the Italian club is in a strong negotiating position, with Soule under contract until June 2029. Roma are reportedly reluctant to lose the attacker, although financial considerations could influence their stance.

The Serie A side are believed to value him between €55 million and €60 million, significantly higher than his estimated market value of around €35 million.

While no concrete offers have reportedly been submitted, interested clubs could explore different transfer structures, including an initial loan with an obligation to buy or performance-related bonuses.

A January move remains possible, but next summer could provide a more realistic opportunity for Arsenal to pursue the Argentine if his impressive performances continue.

Soule’s versatility and attacking quality make him an intriguing target, although Arsenal would need to consider the financial implications before committing to such a significant transfer.
