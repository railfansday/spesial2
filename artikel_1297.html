Romano: Man Utd Keen to Sign Calvin Bassey for Mauricio Pochettino

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Manchester United are expected to make new signings for new manager Mauricio Pochettino in the January transfer window, and they have already identified a potential defensive addition from the Premier League in the New Year, according to a new update from Fabrizio Romano.

No sooner had Pochettino been confirmed as Michael Carrick's successor at Old Trafford on Friday, then players began to be linked with a switch to the Theatre of Dreams in 2027.

The Red Devils are already being linked with a number of USA players following the Argentine's successful spell in charge of the nation, including Fulham left-back Antonee Robinson.

Romano has confirmed that while left-back is a position that United are looking at, having explored moves for Newcastle United's Lewis Hall and Barcelona's Alejandro Balde in the summer window, their priorities in January are the centre-back and striker positions, as backed up by GIVEMESPORT sources.

United have been linked with American striker Folarin Balogun at Monaco, who came close to joining Everton in the summer, while GMS sources revealed previously about their interest in Leeds United's Dominic Calvert-Lewin.

The former Everton centre-forward is set to extend his contract at Elland Road, which means the Red Devils will have to look elsewhere in their quest to sign new competition for the injured Benjamin Sesko.

The 2026-27 campaign has also exposed the club's defensive issues, with an expectation that there will be an overhaul of the area next summer, as they have done with the midfield and attack in the previous two summers.

When Man United Signed First-Choice Defence

United could bring that plan forward to January though, given no club in the top five European leagues has conceded more than they have (30) in all competitions this term.

United were linked with a move for Fulham centre-back Calvin Bassey over the weekend, and Romano has now confirmed that the 26-year-old is a target.

Speaking on one of his latest YouTube videos, the journalist said:

"Many questions on Calvin Bassey, centre-back from Fulham. It's true that there are people at Manchester United appreciating Calvin Bassey, and it's a name that Man United had under consideration for a few months.

Man United are following his performances. Man United like his physicality, Man United like the fact that he could be a left-footed centre-back to add to the squad. So there are some points in his favour.

"He's not the only name in the list, but he's one of the players being monitored, as the Telegraph reported, and so a name to watch for sure for Manchester United. Then we have to see what Fulham want to do, and we also have to see the alternatives."

United clearly need upgrades in their defence if they are to push on in the next seasons, but there surely have to be question marks whether Bassey is going to be that player.

One of the reasons the Red Devils will be attracted to him is the fact he is left-footed and is able to play as a left-sided centre-back and a left-back, but does he have the quality to be a starter for the club in the coming years?

That is up for debate, but it would be hard to categorically say yes he is, even if he has captained the Cottagers this term.

The 26-year-old is known for his strength, as has been mentioned by Manchester City star Erling Haaland, but there has also been little interest in him from bigger European clubs even though he is racking up appearances for Fulham.

United star Bryan Mbeumo has been impressed with what he has seen from Bassey, saying:

Yeah, Calvin Bassey is a beast, the guy is a beast. He’s too strong, it’s crazy, and on top of that he’s fast too. It’s not cool to play against him.

The Nigeria international's contract at Craven Cottage expires at the end of the season so this could be another market opportunity United have identified, although Fulham do have the option to extend his deal by a further 12 months.

It will be interesting to see whether United do step up their interest in Bassey, if Pochettino decides he is the right defender to make a difference to the club mid-season.
