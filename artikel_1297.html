Best Signing Since Bruno: Man Utd Ready to Bid 'Top Money' for Paz - Romano

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Manchester United look set for a big 2027 in terms of transfer business, with the potential of many new arrivals at Old Trafford for Mauricio Pochettino.

It was already expected that Man United would complete a major overhaul of their defence next summer having done the same with the midfield and attack in the previous two summer transfer windows, but they are set to accelerate some of their business into the New Year too.

With a new manager who can revolutionise Man Utd and clear flaws in areas of the squad following a poor start to the season, new additions are needed in January in order to turn the Red Devils' season around.

According to Fabrizio Romano, despite links with new left-backs, as there was during the summer transfer window as the club looked into deals for Newcastle United's Lewis Hall and Barcelona's Alejandro Balde, it isn't said to be a main priority position this winter, with a new centre-back higher up the list.

That is slightly surprising given Luke Shaw has, once again, had his injury issues this term, but Pochettino does have Patrick Dorgu, Diogo Dalot and even Lisandro Martinez who can play in the position.

United are being linked with a move for Fulham defender Calvin Bassey, which could allow them to kill two birds with one stone given the Nigeria international is able to play at left-back as well as centre-back, where he usually features for the Cottagers.

The other priority position according to Romano, as GIVEMESPORT also revealed, is to bring in a new striker, with Benjamin Sesko spending the majority of the campaign out through injury.

While it may not be a priority in January, United are also needing to consider options for the No.10 position next summer, with the possibility that captain Bruno Fernandes leaves the club.

Journalist Samuel Luckhurst first reported that the club are watching Como playmaker Nico Paz, which was backed up by Romano last week.

The Italian journalist said: "The fact that Manchester United can appreciate Nico Paz, it's something absolutely normal. This is one of the best players in the world, and it's not only Manchester United, believe me.

In case Nico Paz is not going back to Real Madrid, there are a few top, top clubs ready to put top money on the table. But at the moment, all of this is just appreciation, because the reality is that it's Real Madrid in total control, and it's way too early to decide. So that's the story. That's the situation.

United chiefs are fully aware of how good Paz is, having seen it first hand when he destroyed them in the 4-1 Champions League humiliation in Como last month.

The 22-year-old was unplayable next to the famous Italian lake, scoring twice and assisting another, in one of the matches that ultimately led to the dismissal of Michael Carrick.

The bad news for United is that, even if they are willing to make a big bid for Paz, they face a huge battle to win the race for his signature.

Firstly, Real Madrid are ultimately in control of the situation as they have a buy-back clause worth £67m, and if he does leave Como and the LaLiga giants want him, then he will end up at the Bernabeu.

Jude Bellingham's presence in a white shirt could make things interesting in that regard, but even if it does turn out that Paz is going elsewhere, United would likely face huge competition from other top European clubs.

Paz has been described as a 'superstar' by TNT Sports commentator Adam Summerton, and he looks to have a huge future ahead of him in the European game.

What is clear is that if Fernandes is to leave next summer, then Paz is one of the outstanding players on the market who could effectively replace the Portugal international in the long-term.

Fernandes has become renowned for contributing in terms of both goals and assists during his time at Old Trafford, and Paz is showing he can do the same after 25 goals and 25 assists in 89 outings for Como.

If United were able to sign Paz in 2027, it would have to be seen as a huge transfer coup and given how good and exciting he is, he would be their best addition since Fernandes.
