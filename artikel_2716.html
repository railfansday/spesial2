Deshaun Watson on future with Browns: That's up to my agent, I don't worry about that

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

After several years marred by poor play and injuries, Deshaun Watson has provided some return on the investment the Browns made with the infamous 2022 trade to acquire him.

Watson has engineered a league-high six game-winning drives for the Browns, who stand at 6-4 and the AFC’s current No. 7 seed.

The five-year commitment the franchise made to him is just about at its end, with 2026 representing the final season of the original deal.

With the team on its bye in Week 11, has Watson thought about an appropriate time to speak with the organization about his future?

“That’s up to my agent. So, that’s all David Mulugheta,” Watson said, via transcript from the team. “I don’t even worry about that, even all the way up to the last game. My future and what that holds is all in God’s hands, and whoever contacts David. Right now, again, it’s just playing good football, winning games, enjoying this moment, because it can all be taken away regardless. Right now, I’m just focusing on today, this bye week, and then getting back up here and finishing out the season strong for us.”

Asked as a follow-up if he’s opened the door for his agent to speak with the Browns about the future, Watson again sidestepped.

“Yeah, that’s a David question,” Watson said. “That’s my agent, I’m going to leave that up to my agent for sure.”

While the Browns have gone 6-3 with Watson as a starter so far in 2026, the quarterback’s numbers are not overwhelming. He’s completed 65.1 percent of his passes for 2,118 yards with seven touchdowns and four interceptions — good for an 89.1 passer rating. He’s also taken a league-high 37 sacks.
