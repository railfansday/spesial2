‘It makes zero sense’: NFL world stunned as Steelers bench Aaron Rodgers

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Pittsburgh Steelers stunned the NFL world late Tuesday night by benching Aaron Rodgers and turning to Mason Rudolph, who will now start at quarterback when the Steelers host the Denver Broncos on Black Friday.

Rodgers, a four-time MVP who turns 43 next week, will be inactive for the game. The timing of the move left plenty of people scratching their heads, as Rodgers had been practicing and talking to reporters as the starter just hours earlier.

The Steelers were 6-2 and leading the AFC North just a few weeks ago, but they've lost two straight since. They were blown out 38-18 by the Cincinnati Bengals on Sunday Night Football, with most of Rodgers' numbers coming in garbage time, prompting Cam Newton to say that Rodgers "is not good enough to carry a team anymore."

Pittsburgh then fell 19-16 to the Philadelphia Eagles on Sunday, with Rodgers getting sacked five times and DK Metcalf getting ejected in the third quarter. The Steelers didn't reach 20 points in either loss. On the season, Rodgers has thrown for 2,241 yards, 16 touchdowns, and six interceptions, and his 86.6 passer rating is the worst of his career.

With the season slipping away, the Steelers officially made the quarterback change, announcing the move in a short statement.

"The Pittsburgh Steelers have named Mason Rudolph the starting quarterback for the game this Friday against the Denver Broncos. Aaron Rodgers will be inactive for the game on Friday," the team said.

ESPN's Tom Pelissero reported that it was "a coach's decision," and Ray Fittipaldo of the Pittsburgh Post-Gazette reported that the coaches are "confident Mason Rudolph can provide the spark the offense needs against the Broncos."

The move was especially jarring given that head coach Mike McCarthy coached Rodgers for 13 seasons in Green Bay. Needless to say, plenty of people around the league had thoughts.

"To recap: Mike McCarthy said Aaron Rodgers is the starter on Monday. Aaron Rodgers practiced as the starter on Tuesday. Aaron Rodgers spoke to reporters as QB1 on Tuesday afternoon. Aaron Rodgers was benched on Tuesday night and will be completely inactive on Friday," wrote Ari Meirov, NFL insider for The 33rd Team.

"Steelers QB Aaron Rodgers got benched in the (almost) middle of the night. It makes zero sense when you follow the path over the past 48 hours," wrote Mark Kaboly, Steelers correspondent for The Pat McAfee Show.

"Something weird happened with Rodgers today. McCarthy was asked yesterday about going to a backup and he laughed it off. Now, benched and inactive? Story's gonna be juicy," wrote Jason McIntyre, host of the Straight Fire podcast.

"There are like four QBs in NFL HISTORY who you could bench Aaron Rodgers for. Tom Brady. Peyton Manning. Joe Montana. Patrick Mahomes. Steelers benched him for Mason Rudolph," wrote Sam Block of ESPN.

"Not following Pittsburgh closely at all, but seeing a clip like this makes it apparent Rodgers is in an ego war with people in the front office and tonight's benching is a retaliatory move from them," wrote David Lombardi, who covers the 49ers for The San Francisco Standard.

"Aaron Rodgers is a future Hall of Famer, a Super Bowl MVP, 4x NFL MVP & 10x Pro Bowler. Meanwhile, the truth is the time had come for his benching, and that's ok," wrote Josina Anderson, NFL insider.

"To be clear: The #Steelers did tell Aaron Rodgers of the news he's being benched. The organization is unsure what's coming next and what he does," wrote Jordan Schultz, NFL insider.

Clearly, the Steelers' decision has raised more questions than answers.
