Mike Evans not expected back this week

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

San Francisco 49ers WR Mike Evans (Achilles) is not expected back for Week 10, according to head coach Kyle Shanahan.

Why It Matters: Evans being out could mean a big target week for Christian McCaffrey and George Kittle. No other wide receiver has seen much work, so fantasy players should see an uptick from those two the most.
