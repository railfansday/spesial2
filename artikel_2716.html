Joe Milton shines in Week 12 win

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Philadelphia Eagles QB Joe Milton completed 21 of his 31 passes for 331 yards, three touchdowns and an interception and rushed three times for 15 yards and a score against the Dallas Cowboys in Week 12.

Why it Matters: After forgettable outings against the Giants and Steelers, Milton shredded the Cowboys on his way to a career-best performance. Granted, the Cowboys have been a mess on defense all year, but Milton at least showed the potential to put up strong numbers in the right matchup. With the Cardinals up next in Week 13, Milton could have some QB2 potential.
