Will Colts bench Daniel Jones? Shane Steichen to 'evaluate everything'

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Indianapolis Colts coach Shane Steichen wouldn't commit to Daniel Jones remaining the team's starting quarterback after the team's 26-3 loss to the Houston Texans.

Steichen was asked during a postgame news conference whether Jones would remain Indianapolis' starter after the team was throttled by Houston on "Thursday Night Football" to kickstart NFL Week 11.

"We'll evaluate everything," Steichen replied.

Steichen's non-commitment comes after a rocky outing from Jones against the Texans. He led the Colts to a field goal on their first drive but struggled after that, completing just 12 of 27 passes (44.4%) for 144 yards and an interception.

Jones struggled to connect with his receivers throughout the contest, routinely overthrowing players and tossing a few passes out of bounds. He didn't look comfortable in the pocket, suffering two sacks, which Steichen noted in assessing the quarterback's play.

"I thought he could do some things better," Steichen told reporters when asked about Jones' performance. "But obviously, there's pass rush coming at him for sure. But we'll go back and evaluate the tape."

Jones' issues weren't limited to Thursday's game. He has struggled badly with turnovers in 2026, throwing a league-leading 13 interceptions, including at least one in each of his last six starts. And the Colts have gone just 3-8 under his leadership, which has led many to wonder when Indianapolis might make a change at quarterback.

The Colts did bench Jones for the final offensive drive of their loss to the Texans. Fourth-year quarterback Anthony Richardson took the field in his place, failing to complete his only two passes while running for two yards on one carry.

Richardson, 24, hasn't started a game since the 2024 NFL season. The No. 4 overall pick from the 2023 NFL Draft has a career record of 8-7 as a starter but entered Thursday's game having completed just 50.9% of his career passes for 2,408 yards, 11 touchdowns and 13 interceptions.

Richardson does have elite mobility, as evidenced by his 638 career rushing yards and 10 touchdowns on 117 carries, and also possesses a cannon arm, so the Colts may want to evaluate his potential as he prepares to be an unrestricted free agent during the offseason.

The Colts also have second-year quarterback Riley Leonard as their third-string quarterback. It isn't clear whether he would be elevated to the backup role if Jones is benched.

Either way, Steichen will have time to make his decision. The Colts don't play again until Sunday, Nov. 29, when they face Jones' former team, the New York Giants.

Indianapolis also has a bye in Week 13, so Steichen could opt to hold off on any choice about Jones' future until that time as well.
