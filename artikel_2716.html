Real Madrid superstar achieves remarkable 13-match winning streak in just 50 days

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Real Madrid have enjoyed an extraordinary turnaround under Jose Mourinho in recent weeks, winning eight consecutive matches ahead of the November international break.

The Portuguese manager has made some important tactical tweaks that have made such a streak possible.

One player in the squad has taken the winning streak to a whole different level, emerging victorious in all 13 matches he has played in the last 50 days.

According to MARCA (h/t Madrid Xtra), Jude Bellingham has not only enjoyed an unbeaten run with his club but has also maintained a perfect record with England.

The 23-year-old's extraordinary streak began in September. Bellingham has started all eight of Madrid's games during this period and four of his five games with England.

Bellingham has also managed three goals and six assists during this 13-match sequence, continuing his great form in 2026.

His latest outing came against Spain on Sunday, when England secured a dramatic 3-2 victory at the Metropolitano.

Bellingham delivered another outstanding performance on the night, providing an assist and earning the Man of the Match award.

It was a record-breaking assist for the Los Blancos superstar, who reached seven assists for England in 2026, setting a national team record.

Bellingham's resurgence is particularly impressive considering the physical difficulties he experienced last season.

He missed 111 days through injury, with persistent shoulder problems preventing him from performing at the highest level.

How long can Bellingham's run continue for? (Photo by Angel Martinez/Getty Images)

He showed signs of returning to his best towards the end of last season before enjoying an outstanding World Cup with England.

He now appears to have carried that energy and confidence into Mourinho's first season back in the Spanish capital.

Having Bellingham back to his best is massive news for both the Portuguese manager and Thomas Tuchel.

It remains to be seen how long Bellingham's winning streak can continue and how his form pans out over the course of the season.
