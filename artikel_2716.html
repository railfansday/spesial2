Packers’ Matt LaFleur talks injuries, Micah Parsons’ return

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

When asked about the status of edge defender Micah Parsons, Green Bay Packers head coach Matt LaFleur stated, "That's my plan," when the topic of Parsons playing against the Los Angeles Rams on Thanksgiving Eve was brought up. "I think my plan's been different than everybody else's this whole time," LaFleur joked on Monday about Parsons' ACL and meniscus recovery.

When he snapped back into serious answers, LaFleur added, "There's still a couple more hurdles for him to clear before we can definitively say that he's a go." So if you're tracking at home, the answer is that Parsons will probably make his 2026 debut against the Rams in two weeks, but some progress needs to be made before then.

Beyond Parsons, LaFleur also touched on a couple of other injury situations that popped up last week for the Packers.

On the offensive line, LaFleur stated that the team was "expecting" Kevin Zeitler to make the start at right guard last week, but he had a "freak thing" happen to his triceps during Thursday's practice. On whether or not Zeitler will play against the Rams, LaFleur said, "We'll see where he's at."

Instead of Zeitler, the starting guards against the Minnesota Vikings were Laken Tomlinson on the left and Travis Glover on the right, with Aaron Banks sidelined with an injury of his own but still healthy enough to be active on game day. When pressed on whether Banks is still in the mix as a starter, LaFleur replied, "He certainly is."

Here's the short: The Packers' next game is in late November, and we have no idea which two of the six players that have played guard for Green Bay this year are going to be their long-term options. Expect what amounts to be a position battle to continue when the team returns to practice.

There is some good news on the injury front, though. LaFleur stated, "No," when asked if cornerback Brandon Cisse's rib injury is going to lead to him missing significant time. He, along with two receivers, dropped out against the Vikings.

Receiver Bo Melton will be "alright," according to LaFleur, while fellow receiver Skyy Moore, who just returned from the injured reserve, "will be closer to the end of the week before we can have any determination about that." Melton injured his thigh, while Moore injured his ankle. For what it's worth, Moore's injury that led to him going on the injured reserve previously was also an ankle issue.
