Avalanche Game 22 at Utah Mammoth: Lines, Notes & How To Watch

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Colorado Avalanche (12-6-3) are back on the road and open a back-to-back against the Utah Mammoth (10-9-3). Puck drop is scheduled for 8:18 p.m. MT and will be broadcast nationally on TNT.

Read Next10 Takeaways: Avalanche Get Contributions From Up and Down the Lineup in Montreal

The Avalanche will play their second consecutive game without both Nathan MacKinnon and Cale Makar. They picked up defenseman Mike Reilly off waivers from the Carolina Hurricanes over the weekend but he'll sit out for this one. Reilly appeared in eight games this season with the Canes and won the Stanley Cup last year.

Their lineup will remain entirely the same as the one they used Saturday in Montreal. That means Colorado is rolling with Mackenzie Blackwood in goal, which likely sets up a rare home game for Scott Wedgewood on Thursday night at Ball Arena. The forward lines are also going to look the same, keeping Nic Roy at center and Brock Nelson on the top line in the absence of MacKinnon. They used the same lines at practice on Tuesday as they had against the Habs.

The Mammoth are dealing with their own issues right now. Just a few weeks ago, they were challenging Colorado and even Minnesota atop the division. Now, they're sitting in the final playoff spot and are at risk of being passed up by the red-hot Winnipeg Jets and the St. Louis Blues. Utah has just two wins in its last 10 games after suffering a really bad regulation loss to the last-place Vancouver Canucks on Monday.

Goaltending has been one of the biggest concerns for the Mammoth. Both Karel Vejmelka (.872) and Sebastian Cossa (.863) are among the worst goalies in the NHL in save percentage. Vejmelka is expected to get the nod tonight. He is 0-4-2 in his last six starts, which includes a 1-0 shootout loss to the Blues, where he recorded his second shutout of the season.
