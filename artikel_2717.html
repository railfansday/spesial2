Jayson Tatum opens up about famous benching during 2024 Olympics

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Let's go back in time. The year is 2024, and Team USA Men's Basketball is in search of their fifth straight gold medal in the Paris Olympics. Although not quite the biggest name on the roster, Duke alum Jayson Tatum enters the tournament as a recent NBA champion, having helped lead the Boston Celtics to the 2023-24 title against the Dallas Mavericks. Not only that, Tatum was one of the most integral returning players for Team USA, having led the team in scoring during the group stage in the 2020 Tokyo Olympics.

However, Tatum was famously benched in the tournament, as head coach Steve Kerr leaned heavily on Stephen Curry and Kevin Durant, two players he had plenty of experience working with. Still, Tatum, a sixth-place finisher in MVP that season, didn't play a single minute in two games against Serbia.

Recently, the former Blue Devil opened up about the experience and the decision, saying, "He came to me before we got on the bus to go to the game, and I remember Steve was like, 'Kevin (Durant's) back (from injury), so we're going to have to try to figure something out with the lineup,'" said Tatum.

"I think it was like once I didn't get in in the first quarter, I was like, 'Oh, some fishy s— going on.' That's when it kind of hit me like, "Oh, that's what he was trying to tell me without trying to tell me."

Despite finishing the tournament with the second-fewest minutes on the team, Tatum and the United States were still able to earn gold medals. Now, Tatum has two Olympic gold medals and will be 30 years old during the 2028 Olympics, which are set to take place in Los Angeles. Kerr will not be there, having stepped down from coaching duties following the conclusion of the Paris games.
