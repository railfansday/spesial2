Patrick, Brittany Mahomes expecting their fourth child: ‘Another blessing’

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Patrick and Brittany Mahomes' home team is going to get a little bit bigger.

Brittany revealed in a post on Instagram that the couple is expecting their fourth child.

The last photo in the series showed Patrick and his three children — daughters Sterling, 5, and Golden, 1, and son Bronze, 3 — touching Brittany's belly.

The family did not announce an expected due date, name or the sex of the new addition.

The Mahomes family welcomed Golden to the family in early 2025, just weeks before Patrick and the Chiefs took on the Eagles at the Super Bowl in New Orleans.

Patrick commented, in fitting fashion, six heart emojis on the Instagram post.

The revelation comes two days after the Chiefs crushed the Jets 35-12 at Arrowhead Stadium in Kansas City to move to 6-2 on the season.

Kansas City Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes speaks during a news conference after an NFL football game against the New York Jets, Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026. AP Photo/Charlie Riedel

Patrick led the way with 242 yards passing with two touchdowns in the rout. His three children were on hand, as Brittany shared photos of the family on the sideline for the big afternoon. "Making memories they will never forget," she wrote in the caption.

The three-time Super Bowl-winning quarterback did have an injury scare after Jets star rookie edge rusher David Bailey nailed Patrick in the elbow.

Head coach Andy Reid tried to assuage fears when he spoke to reporters Monday.

"It was more positive news than negative news. It looks like it's just a matter of rehabbing and getting rid of some of the soreness," the coach added Monday, according to the Kansas City Star.

The Chiefs next play Sunday on the road against the Falcons.
