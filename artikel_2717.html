Seahawks add Chase Lundt off Bills practice squad, waive CB Avery Smith

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Seahawks are making a pair of roster moves on Tuesday.

Per Tom Pelissero of Netflix, Seattle is signing offensive tackle Chase Lundt off of Buffalo's practice squad.

As a corresponding roster move, the team is waiving cornerback Avery Smith.

Lundt, 26, was a sixth-round pick in 2025. He's appeared in two career games, playing three offensive snaps and five special teams snaps.

Smith appeared in six games for Seattle this season, playing exclusively on special teams.
