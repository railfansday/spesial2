Harry Kane forced off with ankle injury - Köln lead in Munich

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Bayern Munich have been dealt a potential injury blow after Harry Kane was forced off with an ankle issue.

The England international was fouled by Köln defender Joel Schmid and seemingly hurt his ankle. Kane was unable to continue and was replaced by Tom Bischof.

Further details regarding the severity of Kane's injury are yet to emerge.

The injury came just a few minutes after Köln took the lead in Munich via a brilliant goal from Mikey Moore.
