Oklahoma football lands 4-star Weiss LB: Tylan Henderson commitment post thanks 'village'

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

One of Central Texas' top football prospects made his college decision over the weekend.

Weiss junior linebacker Tylan Henderson, a four-star recruit according to the 247Sports composite rankings, committed to the Oklahoma football, he announced in a social media post Saturday night. The American-Statesman rated Henderson the second-best football recruit in the Austin area regardless of class before the season.

Henderson's offer list included other prestigious programs like Ohio State, Miami, USC and Notre Dame. He took visits to both SMU and the University of Washington in October, according to social media, before deciding on the Sooners.

Weiss football linebacker Tylan Henderson during the national anthem ahead of a game. (Weiss Football)

In a post on X, Henderson mentioned multiple members of the Oklahoma staff, including head coach Brent Venables, defensive coordinator Todd Bates and inside linebackers coach Nate Dreiling.

"The entire OU staff and front office has welcomed me and my family with open arms," he said. "They have continued to support, encourage and believe in me and my abilities from day one. I believe in what Oklahoma is building and can't wait to be a part of it."

Henderson is the No. 224 player in the country and the 19th-best linebacker in the country, according to the 247Sports composite. He is the 29th-ranked player in Texas, according to the composite.

Weiss linebacker Tylan Henderson makes a tackle during a Nov. 13, 2026, bi-district playoff game against Iowa Colony. (Weiss football)

The linebacker, who lists himself as 6-foot-1, 220-pounds on X (formerly Twitter), missed much of the season as he recovered from an injury. But he's shown why he's rated highly since returning to the field. Henderson has recorded 37 tackles, six tackles for loss and 2½ sacks in four games. He also caused a fumble in Friday's 56-7 bi-district round win over Iowa Colony.

In his commitment announcement, Henderson thanked Weiss coach Malcolm Hill, defensive coordinator Cam Devereaux, his teammates and the "entire Weiss family."

Weiss is 9-2 this season and Henderson helped the team win the District 12-5A Division I title. The Wolves will take on Port Arthur Memorial on Friday in the area round of the Class 5A Division I playoffs.

"Coach Hill & Coach Dev, thank you for pushing, challenging, believing and continuing to develop me into the player I am today on and off the field," Henderson said. "I would not be in this position without you."

Henderson also showed gratitude to the numerous coaches, trainers and friends that have been there for him. "I feel like I have the greatest village of all time," he wrote.

Weiss linebacker Tylan Henderson breaks through the line during a Nov. 13, 2026, bi-district playoff game against Iowa Colony. (Weiss football)

Oklahoma is 6-4 this season and 4-3 in SEC play. The Sooners will host Texas A&M on Saturday. Oklahoma has seven players committed in the Class of 2028, according to 247Sports, which ranks the crop as the best in the nation as of Monday afternoon.

Henderson will be far from the first Austin-area star to make his way to Norman. Vandegrift alum Jacob Henry is a junior on the Sooners roster and Hutto senior and two-way standout Jaiden Fields is committed to the program as well.
