US midterms: Michigan candidate El-Sayed pledges to tackle the affordability crisis

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The cost of living is one of the main concerns for many US voters as the midterm elections get underway. In Michigan, Democratic Senate candidate Abdul El-Sayed has pledged to "take the money out of politics and put it in your pocket", as FRANCE 24 correspondent Jessica Le Masurier reports.
