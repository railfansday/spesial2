EnerSys (ENS +0.10%) reported second quarter net sales of $949.9 million, as growth in defense and data center markets balanced continued softness in the material handling sector. Management indicated that the company's diversified portfolio helped maintain stable revenue while achieving significant margin expansion and earnings growth. During the quarter, the company announced a definitive agreement to acquire Ultralife for $320 million to expand its mission-critical defense power portfolio, particularly in drone and autonomous systems. Management provided third quarter guidance that anticipates sequential improvement in certain sectors while acknowledging potential headwinds from freight and commodity inflation.
