John Brown is wrong about Olwethu Makhanya, this is the reality of his Rangers future

John Brown's outlook on Olwethu Makhanya's Rangers future is unfair as the commanding centre-back shines in the Premiership.

Makhanya, 22, arrived at Rangers from MLS club Philadelphia Union for around £3million in the summer window.

The South African international centre-back is proving to be one of their biggest bargains in recent years as he stars in their long ongoing unbeaten run.

He is already being widely discussed as someone who the Gers could sell on for big money as clubs across Europe take notice of his talents.

They may ultimately be forced into a sale midway through this season, such is the nature of his commanding performances, although Rangers hero Brown believes he should not even consider a January exit.

Makhanya is believed to be content at Ibrox despite transfer interest arising, but it would be completely remiss of him to ignore clubs across Europe just because he is contracted to Rangers.

Makhanya has arguably been Rangers' Player of the Season so far, with the club losing just two of his 17 appearances across all competitions.

Recent data from the CIES Football Observatory claims that his price tag has already shot up to around £12m as it stands, so the club are in a strong position to turn a giant profit on him already.

Club legend Brown has told the Scottish Sun that he thinks Makhanya should stay at Ibrox until he has properly established himself and won silverware in Scotland.

He said: "Whatever amounts are thrown to try to entice him, the biggest thing is winning the title and getting the chance to play in the Champions League. You can do things down the line, but I know Derek McInnes wouldn’t want to lose him.

“If it’s a chance to really be a force and win a league championship, then the board has got to look at that too. Unless you’re getting a silly amount then you want him to be here.”

Brown's understandable bias has likely played a part in his verdict, and it is very possible that Rangers go on to win the Premiership this season with Makhanya playing a key role.

The 22-year-old should not be dismissing outside interest purely because of his loyalty to the Gers, though, as he already looks good enough to make the step up to a top five European league.

When compared to other Premiership centre-backs in 2026-27, he is in the 91st percentile (0.55) for successful dribbles per 90, 87th percentile (4.27) for accurate long balls per 90, 83rd percentile (0.64) for chances created per 90 and 74th percentile (53) for successful passes per 90, so is clearly supremely confident with the ball at his feet.

Makhanya is also in the 100th percentile (6.82) for ball recoveries per 90, 81st percentile (1.55) for successful tackles per 90 and 96th percentile (0.73) for goals conceded while on the pitch per 90, meaning he is also not afraid to do the dirty work needed at the back.

Sources have exclusively revealed to Football Insider that Rangers are worried about Makhanya's head being turned after he was scouted by Brentford and Sunderland.

Such interest from Premier League clubs is surely hinting at a possible move for him in January, and he would have every right to pursue a huge step like that if it materialises into a genuine approach.
