Sources: Liverpool provided ‘huge boost’ after Conor Bradley injury update

Liverpool insiders are excited to see Conor Bradley back training but he will not be rushed back into match action anytime soon, sources have told Football Insider.

Bradley has been sidelined since he suffered a serious knee injury in Liverpool's goalless draw at Arsenal in January.

The 23-year-old right-back underwent surgery to repair both bone and ligament damage and has been working away in the background ever since.

Liverpool have struggled to solve their problems in his position ever since, with loanee Ronald Araujo now Andoni Iraola's first-choice on the right of the back four.

Bradley was pictured back on the grass earlier this week, but as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, he is not close to a full return to action just yet.

Iraola is content with his right-back options as it stands, and so there is little rush to get the Northern Irish international back playing before schedule.

Bradley had just won his spot back in the Liverpool team under Arne Slot when disaster struck at the Emirates shortly after the turn of the year.

He remains widely tipped to make the position his own in the years to come, provided he can avoid more long-term injury problems that have plagued his short Reds career so far.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "There’s no time frame as yet. Obviously it’s a huge boost to see him back on the training pitch and with some of his teammates. He’s not back in full team training just yet as he continues that recovery process.

"It's a huge boost for Bradley, first and foremost, that he’s back on the training pitch and also good news for Iraola. I think Liverpool will be careful with Bradley. They don’t want to take any risks with him. Obviously it will take him a number of weeks to regain full match fitness and sharpness, but it's good that he is back out there amongst his teammates."

Araujo has made himself an Anfield favourite with his performances on loan from Barcelona at right-back in recent months.

Sources have exclusively revealed to Football Insider that Liverpool would like to sign Araujo permanently, as they have a £47million option to do so at the end of the campaign.

Barca sporting director, Deco, opened the door for him to join the Reds on a permanent basis with his comments this week, saying: "The most important thing for us now is seeing Ronald recovered, happy, enjoying himself and in a positive place.

"If he decides to stay, Liverpool have an option to make the deal permanent. They would also have to negotiate with him -- it's not just about paying the clause - but we would be happy for several reasons [if the deal goes through].

"Firstly, because the move has worked out and because Ronald is happy and is able to continue his career successfully. We want players to succeed. Also, the money would not hurt, either."
