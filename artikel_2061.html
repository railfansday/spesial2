Scott Parker must take Andri Gudjohnsen transfer action in brutal West Ham blow

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

West Ham United are struggling in the Championship right now, and whilst Scott Parker will be aiming to turn things around quickly in West London, the former Burnley boss may have one eye on the January window already.

It hasn't been the start to life Parker hoped for at West Ham when he succeeded Nuno Espírito Santo in early November, failing to win any of his first four games in charge against Cardiff City, Blackburn Rovers, Preston North End, and Portsmouth.

With three promotions from the Championship as a manager on his CV, the former England and Hammers midfielder will certainly be backing himself to turn these fortunes around sooner rather than later, but red flags are everywhere around the London Stadium right now.

Parker will be looking to stamp his authority on the West Ham squad in the January window, but first, he must make sure he is still in charge by then, as his intentions for where he wants to improve have been revealed.

As revealed by Alan Nixon via Patreon in mid-November, Parker is looking to target key positions in his West Ham squad ahead of the January window - namely centre-forward and left-back.

Neither Taty Castellanos nor Pablo Felipe have adapted particularly well to the Championship since relegation, leaving just Joel Piroe as his main focal point up top as things stand.

Meanwhile, Ezra Mayers has emerged as his preferred option at left-back right now over the likes of Ollie Scarles, Adama Boiro, and Morato, with the 19-year-old being thrown straight into the deep end by Parker.

With the number of goals the Hammers are scoring having dipped in recent weeks, a new centre-forward being on the cards isn't a surprise, as one standout candidate ought to be on the cards.

Blackburn Rovers striker Andri Gudjohnsen will be a name that begins to dominate headlines as we approach the January transfer window, with the Icelandic striker having caught fire at Ewood Park this campaign.

With 11 goals and one assist in 16 Championship games this season, the 24-year-old has exploded into life in Lancashire, and alongside Will Lankshear, has emerged as the standout centre-forward in the division.

Gudjohnsen, according to FotMob, has an expected goal return of just 4.43 this campaign from 25 shots, scoring with nearly every other shot he takes and overperforming wildly in front of goal, displaying elite finishing ability.

The centre-forward will be on the list of numerous clubs across the United Kingdom and the rest of Europe heading into winter, but aside from being a deadly finisher, Parker has one more clear reason to win the race for his signature.

Parker has established himself as one of the most successful managers in Championship history over the years, having earned promotion with each of Fulham, Bournemouth, and Burnley.

With the Clarets, Parker reached a century of points during the 2024/25 campaign, only being pipped to the league title by Leeds United on goal difference, conceding a record-low of 16 goals across the entire season.

Although he was unable to keep Burnley in the top flight last year, he remains a well-regarded boss at Turf Moor, and his connection to the club would certainly annoy Blackburn supporters if he were to sign Gudjohnsen.

Blackburn will be expecting big offers for their star man this winter, as supporters will be hoping that he doesn't head to the Hammers amid this obvious connection.
