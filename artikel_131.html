Strike on Gaza Hospital Kills at Least 6 and Injures 20, Rescuers Say

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Israeli authorities said that Hamas had established a command center at the hospital complex. Hamas said the attack was “a heinous crime targeting a civilian medical facility.”

The Israeli military struck a hospital in the southern Gaza Strip on Thursday, killing at least six people and injuring 28 more, according to local health officials.

Dr. Ahmed al-Farra, general director of Nasser Hospital, said in a news conference on Thursday that two missiles had hit the facility and that among the dead was Rezeq Abu Shaira, 52, a doctor.

Health officials reached in Gaza by phone said that a man called Khaled Abu Anza had also been killed, though his affiliation was not immediately clear.

The Israeli security agency and military said in a joint statement on Thursday that they had targeted Muhammad Abu Ismail, whom they identified as a Hamas commander “responsible for holding numerous hostages captive,” during the Gaza war.

The Israeli authorities said that Hamas had established a command center at the hospital complex to stage operations against Israeli forces and was storing weapons there. They added that steps had been taken to mitigate harm to civilians.

Hamas said the Israeli claim that the hospital was used for military activity was a “blatant attempt to justify a heinous crime targeting a civilian medical facility.”

In a statement, Gaza’s health ministry condemned the attack, calling it a “flagrant violation of international laws and conventions.” It did not respond to the Israeli claims.

The attack comes more than a year into a cease-fire between Israel and Hamas that has not brought an end to Israeli military strikes in Gaza and that has done little to alleviate the dire and deadly living conditions for the enclave’s two million residents.

Shortly after midnight, at least two missiles hit the roof of a four-story building in the hospital complex, penetrating the third floor and exploding on the second floor in the surgery department, Naseem Hassan, a paramedic and ambulance driver who works at Nasser Hospital, said by telephone.

Israel has continued to carry out frequent strikes in Gaza despite a U.S.-brokered cease-fire that Israel and Hamas agreed to in October 2025. More than 1,600 people have been killed by Israeli fire during the cease-fire, according to Gaza’s health ministry, which does not distinguish between civilians and combatants in its death tolls.

Nasser Hospital has been struck by the Israeli military multiple times since the Hamas-led attack on Israel on Oct. 7, 2023, prompted a devastating Israeli military response in Gaza. More than 74,000 people have been killed in the war, according to the health ministry.

In August last year, 20 people — including medical staff, rescue workers, patients and journalists — were killed in a strike on the hospital that drew a rare admission of error from Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu of Israel, who called it a “tragic mishap.”

An earlier version of this article, using information from an emergency worker, said that Khaled Abu Anza had been killed and that he was the governor of Khan Younis. Mr. Abu Anza was among the dead, but he was not the governor. The emergency worker also said that guards and a disabled patient had been killed in the attack; it is unclear whether they had been killed.
