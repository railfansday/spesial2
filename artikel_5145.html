KAVA price crashes 44%: The token plunges to approximately $0.038, marking a new all-time low.

$3.2 million wallet transfer raises concerns: Around 48.3 million KAVA moves to BNB Chain and is reportedly swapped for BNB.

Exchange warnings add pressure: Upbit, Binance and Coinone issue caution notices as investors seek clarity over the sudden sell-off.

KAVA price has suffered one of its sharpest declines, plunging 44% in just 24 hours and hitting a new all-time low near $0.038. The crash followed an unusual $3.2 million token transfer to BNB Chain, raising questions about a possible security incident or large-holder exit. With major exchanges issuing warnings, investors are now watching for an official explanation and signs of price stabilization.

KAVA’s sudden price collapse has caught investors off guard. After trading within a relatively narrow range, the token experienced a sharp sell-off on November 14, losing nearly half its value within a day. The decline pushed KAVA toward $0.038, its lowest recorded price, while selling activity accelerated across the market.

$KAVA dumps almost 50% after 48M tokens were reportedly moved to BNB Chain and mostly sold. pic.twitter.com/us7yaNvfG3

A major concern emerged from an unusual wallet transaction. Approximately 48.3 million KAVA, worth nearly $3.2 million, was reportedly transferred to BNB Chain and swapped for BNB through a decentralized exchange. The same wallet reportedly continues to hold around 47.6 million KAVA.

Investors are questioning whether the movement was connected to a large-holder exit, a security issue or another operational problem. The project’s official explanation will be important in determining whether the incident has broader implications for KAVA holders.

The sudden KAVA price crash has also drawn attention from major cryptocurrency exchanges. Upbit, Binance and Coinone issued caution warnings related to KAVA, adding to uncertainty among traders. Binance also restricted deposits and withdrawals, increasing concerns about access to the token.

Exchange warnings can affect market confidence, particularly when investors are already dealing with a sharp price decline. Restrictions on deposits and withdrawals may also complicate trading and transfers between platforms. However, these warnings should not be treated as confirmation of a hack or security breach.

KAVA’s daily chart shows a dramatic breakdown, with the latest selling candle pushing the token sharply below its previous trading range.

The token is now hovering near $0.038, making this the immediate level to monitor. A recovery above $0.040 could provide the first indication that selling pressure is easing. Beyond that, the $0.045â$0.050 region represents an important resistance area.

However, the technical structure remains strongly bearish. KAVA has fallen below its previous trading range and moving averages, while the sudden decline has weakened its recovery setup. If buyers fail to defend the current price region, further losses remain possible. A sustained recovery will require stronger buying volume and, more importantly, clarity surrounding the reported wallet activity.

KAVA faces two immediate challenges: restoring market confidence and stabilizing its price after the 44% collapse. The reported $3.2 million token transfer and exchange warnings have increased uncertainty, but the exact reason behind the crash remains unconfirmed.

Investors should watch for an official project update, changes in exchange restrictions and signs of stronger buying activity. Until the reason behind the KAVA price crash becomes clear, attempts at recovery may remain vulnerable to further selling pressure.
