Joe Biden criticizes Zelenskyy as ‘moody’ and ‘not as grateful’ in new memoir

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Promise Me, America was published just before the former US president’s 84th birthday as he battles advanced cancer

In a new memoir, Joe Biden offers harsh criticism of Volodymyr Zelenskyy, who has led Ukraine through four and a half years of war against Russia. According to Biden, the Ukrainian president was “difficult to work with”, “moody and stubborn in our private conversations”, and “not as grateful as I expected” for US diplomatic support and military aid.

Biden’s memoir, Promise Me, America, was published on Tuesday, shortly before his 84th birthday and as he battles advanced prostate cancer. Largely a trumpeting of achievements in office, with scant admission of failings ultimately crowned by his own party deeming him unfit for office and forcing his withdrawal from the 2024 campaign against Donald Trump, the book has not been well reviewed.

The war in Ukraine takes up much of the middle of the book. Detailing frustrating talks with Vladimir Putin, the Russian president, Biden describes the buildup to war and US-Ukraine cooperation from the invasion of February 2022 until Biden left office in January 2025.

His portrait of Zelenskyy is not entirely negative.

“I had worried that President Zelenskyy, who had come from such an unlikely background [as a comedian and actor] might not have the right stuff for a crisis like this one,” Biden writes. “This was no TV comedy.” But, the former president adds, he found himself impressed by Zelenskyy’s fortitude, and “delighted that my worries proved to be unfounded”.

Biden also praises Zelenskyy’s communication skills and describes his own secret visit to Kyiv in February 2023, when Zelenskyy’s then 10-year-old son handed Biden a drawing of a US-supplied air defense system and the message, “Thank you for helping my father to save us all.”

But such praise is soured by Biden’s verdict on Zelenskyy as the recipient of US support.

Biden writes: “We were proud, of course, of our Ukrainian allies. But I must admit that it could be difficult to work with Zelenskyy.

“As brave as he and the Ukrainians were, they tested our patience on many occasions, demanding more armaments and money. The United States has tremendous resources, but they are not limitless. The American people should know: we got a good return on our investment; almost all the money was spent in the United States on our weapons systems, an expenditure leading to more jobs at home.

“Zelenskyy could be moody and stubborn in our private conversations, and not as grateful as I would have expected. That could be frustrating, and I let him know that in no uncertain terms. Still, I refused to cut and run on the Ukrainians, who possessed a tremendous fighting spirit. And so did Zelenskyy.”

Biden’s verdict carries unsavory echoes of the behavior of Trump and JD Vance, who insulted and belittled Zelenskyy at the White House in February 2025, for supposedly being ungrateful and “disrespectful”. Senior Trump aides have also insulted Zelenskyy. The treasury secretary, Scott Bessent, reportedly called him a “little fucker”, a “special-needs child” and “Mr Bean on crack”.

In response to Trump’s attacks, Zelenskyy told Fox News: “I’m thankful to President Trump and to Congress, bipartisan support, and I was always very thankful from all our people.”

A year and a half later, still leading Ukraine’s fight against Russia, Zelenskyy is also trying to negotiate peace while keeping Trump from making too many concessions to Russia. Zelenskyy did not immediately comment on Biden’s verdict.
