Saint James' Natalie Barton signs with UAH after years of hard work

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Katie Barton paused for a moment at the pulpit and looked towards the ceiling for a brief moment as she gathered herself. Through tears and emotion, she talked about the sacrifices that her daughter, Natalie Barton endured being a coaches daughter.

From missing time with her friends, missing home-cooked meals and meeting high expectations that Katie set for Natalie — she detailed why her daughter was sitting at table decorated with University of Alabama in Huntsville blue and a piece of paper and pen in front of her.

Natalie reciprocated the tears and emotion when she thanked many for the support over the course of her girls basketball career at Saint James. And of course when she thanked her coach and mom, Katie. But today was Natalie's day. She officially inked her intentions to play for UAH women's basketball on Friday, Nov. 13.

It was a day that personified the work Natalie's put in over the course of a six year varsity career for Saint James.

"It's fun, I've enjoyed every moment of it all," Natalie said. "All my past teammates, all my sisters and my teammates this year. I love them so much. It's been a pleasure. I'm really excited for my senior year with this group of girls, and it can't get any better."

While at Saint James, Natalie has set multiple school records that include varsity games started and career 3-pointers made. She's also been named to the Alabama Sports Writer Association's all-state team three times. But the shining moment came when Natalie led Saint James to its first state championship in program history last season. A game that she scored her 2,000th-career point in.

Natalie joins a surging UAH women's basketball program that's coming off a season in which it went 32-6 and made it to the NCAA Division II Tournament, making it as far as the semifinal round in Pittsburgh.

"It's not too far from home and the coaching staff is great," Natalie said about choosing UAH. "The coaches made a relationship with me, and I love it up there. The girls are the best, and I'm just really excited."

Natalie promised that UAH was getting a player that's committed to the program. A player that will contribute in any possible way that she can. And of course, one that's competitive.

Saint James returns the majority of its roster from its state championship team, and has an opportunity to repeat this season. Natalie said her main goal for her senior season is to live in the moment and enjoy the final ride in a Trojan jersey.
