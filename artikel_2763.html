Hawks’ Jalen Johnson ‘not worried’ about his shot after solid showing vs. Hornets

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

ATLANTA – The Atlanta Hawks were coming off a big win against the Oklahoma City Thunder on the road and came back home two days later to defeat the Charlotte Hornets 111-97. Jalen Johnson was big in both of those games, finishing with a triple-double against the Thunder and scoring 26 points against the Hornets. Both games looked different for him, and there was one thing that changed everything for him in the latter.

Johnson has been trying to find a rhythm with his jumper early in the season. His typical mid-range shots haven't falling consistently, and even his 3-point numbers are tremendously down. That all changed against the Hornets, where he knocked down three of his four 3-pointers.

"Haven't shot the ball the way I wanted to, but I put in the work," Johnson said after the game. "I'm not worried about a percentage or whatnot. I know my shots are going to fall. It was good to see a couple go in, for sure. But I'm going to keep shooting the shots that I work on and just let them fall."

As much as the jump shots were falling, it was everything else he did that helped the Hawks win the game. He finished the game 10-of-15 from the field, and that was mostly because of him getting out in transition for easy shots. He even created those opportunities on his own, hence the four steals he collected.

"I thought the thing that he did tonight where he was really impactful was he played without the ball," head coach Quin Snyder said. "That's important for him and for us. Particularly when people are game planning, and there's a matchup on the ball and his ability to do both. To get out in transition, to get to the foul line, to offensive rebound, all those things add up."

It was a fast start for the Hawks in this one, but the Hornets found a way to get comfortable, building a 14-point lead in the first half. The Hawks took back control late in the second quarter and into the third quarter, and that's when the game opened up for them.

"Towards the end of the first quarter and the start of the second, we really just kind of took our foot off the gas and gave them too many good looks, and we didn't push the pace enough," Dyson Daniels said. "So I think going into halftime, the last couple of minutes we got some momentum and we knew we just had to stick with it."

It was another solid showing for the bench, as guys such as Zuby Ejiofor, RayJ Dennis and Corey Kispert are getting key minutes with guys out due to injuries. They've been able to keep the team afloat while they're on the floor, while also bringing much-needed energy at times.

"I think everyone stepped up," Daniels said. "We don't have a Kingston [Flemings] or CJ [McCollum], like a true point guard out there. But we've just been pushing the pace. Everyone's getting touches. We've got guys that can play with the ball, guys that can play off the ball. I think everyone's filling those voids. RayJ's been really good coming in as well, playing some good minutes for us. Corey's come in as well and played some good minutes."

"It sucks having those guys out right now, but we'll eventually get them back," Johnson said. "I think just continue to stick to who we are, I think it's going to help us continue to give ourselves a good chance to win these games and continue to play the way we want to play."
