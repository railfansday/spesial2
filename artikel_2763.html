USA TODAY ALL-USA guard Adan Diggs opens season with triple-double

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

After a dominant sophomore campaign, five-star guard Adan Diggs reclassified to the Class of 2027. He was ready to make a college decision and compete for a spot as the best player in Arizona.

Two days after announcing his commitment to the University of Arizona, he opened his senior campaign with a bang, recording a triple-double of 30 points, 13 rebounds, and 10 assists to lead Millennium (Goodyear, AZ) to a 104-74 win over Hamilton (Chandler, AZ).

See highlights here from Ballislife of Diggs finishing down low, fighting through contact, and pressing on defense early against Hamilton. He turned it on in the second quarter, hitting a trio of 3-pointers and getting out in transition to help Millennium pull away in the first half.

Ranked No. 6 in the USA TODAY Sports Recruiting Rankings Composite, Diggs was named to the boys basketball preseason All-America Second Team last week. He's ranked the second-best player in Arizona and the third-best guard in the country.

Last year, he averaged 22.5 points, 8.0 rebounds, four assists, and three steals per game, helping Millennium reach the semifinals. In that playoff loss, he scored 33 points.

He committed to Arizona in the days leading up to the start of his senior season, choosing the Wildcats over Arkansas and USC, as well as other programs including Kentucky, North Carolina, Duke, and Houston.

Diggs had a productive summer, averaging 19.6 points, six rebounds, 3.4 assists, and 1.7 steals per game on the Nike EYBL Circuit and playing on the 2026 FIBA U18 AmeriCup team, where he averaged 10.4 points and 6.6 boards per game.

Entering the season, Millennium is ranked No. 19 in the Super 25 national boys basketball rankings. The Tigers' schedule will ramp up heading into December, competing in HoopHall West against programs like Sierra Canyon (Chatsworth, CA) and McEachern (Powder Springs, GA) from Dec. 10-12.
