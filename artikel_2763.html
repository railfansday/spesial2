MAC admits error in overturned Kent State touchdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Mid-American Conference acknowledged Wednesday that officials incorrectly overturned a Kent State touchdown in the closing seconds of Tuesday's 17-12 loss to Eastern Michigan.

The conference said a postgame review determined there was insufficient video evidence to overturn the original ruling on the field, which should have stood.

With six seconds remaining, Kent State faced fourth-and-9 at the Eastern Michigan 14-yard line. Quarterback Ethan Thanthanavong connected with wide receiver Wayne Harris, who was initially ruled to have scored a touchdown.

After a replay review, officials overturned the call, ruling that Harris did not establish firm control of the football until his left foot was off the ground and that his right foot subsequently landed out of bounds. The play was ruled an incomplete pass.

However, the MAC said its review found that the video showed only slight movement of the football and did not clearly establish that Harris lacked or lost control.

"The video evidence did not meet the clear and obvious standard required to overturn the ruling on the field," the conference said in a statement. "The decision to overturn the touchdown was incorrect, and the original ruling should have been upheld."

The conference said it regretted the error and its impact on the outcome of the game, adding that it remained committed to maintaining the highest standards of officiating and competitive integrity.

The game was Kent State's season finale. The Golden Flashes finished the year 3-9 and 2-6 in conference play.
