Mikal Bridges' buzzer-beater against the Trail Blazers is a cathartic moment for him

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Mikal Bridges has been a stellar player for the New York Knicks for the past couple of seasons. However, he has been struggling to start the 2026-27 campaign. While he has had some good defensive performances, he still needs a jolt of confidence.

That came during Saturday night's game against the visiting Portland Trail Blazers. After taking a big 26-point lead against the Blazers, Damian Lillard's team came back and took a two-point lead with just a few seconds to go. Thankfully for the Knicks, Bridges was once again ready to sink the Blazers with a buzzer-beating game-winner.

Bridges has always been one of the most solid players throughout his career. He has never missed a game, and he shows up on both ends of the floor when needed. Despite that, there is a problem of inconsistency when it comes to the start of a season.

In the past two seasons, Bridges has started the regular season slowly. That is not a good look for him because he is a starter for the defending champions. However, he manages to pick up when he is needed, just like this game-winner against Portland.

The Blazers clawed their way back into the game, and when Andre Drummond committed a silly foul against Deni Avdija with a few seconds to go, it seemed the Knicks were in danger of blowing what was once a 26-point lead.

Thankfully for New York, they had Bridges, who already had a history of beating the Blazers with a game-winner at Moda Center. He did it all over again, cementing his impact for the Knicks after a slow start to the year.

"Try to get JB open, and then, just be ready, you know... They played hard in that second half, and that's on us, but we just finished the game. You know, it was 48 minutes, and we just kept persevering through the time, and ended up winning the game," Bridges said after the massive victory.

Moving forward, this could be just what Bridges needs to keep working hard. He is one of the Knicks' most important players, and having this kind of moment is a big confidence boost.
