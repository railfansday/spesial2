Eagles issue estimated injury report ahead of Cowboys Thanksgiving game

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Philadelphia Eagles issued their first official injury report of the week on Monday in advance of their Week 12 game against the Dallas Cowboys on Thanksgiving.

Note that this report is an estimation because the team held a walkthrough in lieu of a normal practice.

The Eagles listed four players under DID NOT PARTICIPATE: Quinyon Mitchell, Jonathan Jones, Arnold Ebiketie, and Smael Mondon.

Mitchell suffered a hamstring injury during the fourth quarter of the Eagles' Week 11 win and did not return to the game. He told Ashlyn Sullivan that he was feeling OK while he was riding an exercise bike in the postgame locker room. So, he might be able to play? But maybe not since it's a hammy on a short week … plus travel. We'll see how he's listed over the next two days.

Jones was seen in a walking boot after Sunday's game. Seems like he's in jeopardy of not playing, which is relevant since he replaced Riq Woolen after the former was benched against the Pittsburgh Steelers. If neither Mitchell nor Jones can play, the Eagles will be starting Woolen and … Kelee Ringo? … against the Cowboys. Yikes. They might have to move Cooper DeJean to the outside or give Mac McWilliams a chance because Ringo is most definitely not a viable option. Being short-handed at corner against CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens, who helped the Cowboys reach 51 points scored in Week 11 … far from ideal.

Ebiketie's inclusion on the report is a surprise; he's dealing with an oblique injury. If he can't play, A.J. Epenesa could work his way back into the edge rusher rotation after being limited to special teams duty over the past three games.

Mondon is on track to miss his second straight game. The Eagles currently only have two healthy off-ball linebackers on the roster: Jihaad Campbell and Jeremiah Trotter Jr. The team will likely look to temporarily elevate Anfernee Orji again for more LB depth. They also have Chance Campbell on the practice squad.

The Eagles listed four players under LIMITED PARTICIPATION: Saquon Barkley, Will Shipley, Dallas Goedert, and Tanner McKee.

Barkley is listed with "neck/illness" issues after getting banged up on Sunday. He'll probably play against the Cowboys, albeit at less than 100%.

Shipley will also probably suit up, albeit at less than 100%.

Goedert is newly dealing with a shin injury. Dallas will probably play in Dallas.

McKee has been dealing with a rib injury. Currently no indication it'll cause him to miss Thursday's game.

The Eagles listed five players under FULL PARTICIPATION: DeVonta Smith, Jalen Carter, Jonathan Greenard, Michael Carter, and Britain Covey.

They should all be able to play against the Cowboys.
