New Year’s Pork Roast with Sauerkraut

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Recipe by Bernadette Laganella, Voorhees, New Jersey

In our home, we eat pork with sauerkraut for forward progress in the new year, along with 12 grapes for good luck each month ahead. Many years ago, our friend Doug was facing financial difficulties, so we brought this New Year’s dinner to his family. His fortunes turned for the better, and he became such a believer that when we later faced a heartbreaking time, he returned the gesture and brought the same meal to us. I can’t guarantee this pork and sauerkraut will bring good luck, but I can promise a delicious, comforting New Year’s Day dinner. —Bernadette Laganella, Voorhees, New Jersey
