Summer Intern – Practice Guidelines – Remote

The American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) is currently hiring a remote Summer Intern for our Practice Guidelines department. This part-time, seasonal position will offer challenging work/project(s) within an exciting industry. The Summer Intern will have the opportunity to collaborate with experienced Association professionals in an innovative, mission-driven, and member-centric environment.

The intern may lead a primary implementation science project tied to an ASA clinical practice guideline such as applying a framework to identify adoption barriers, mapping them to strategies, and developing an implementation and evaluation plan. Possible products include a toolkit, evaluation framework, presentation, abstract, or manuscript.

The American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) team provides education, resources, and advocacy to help our members improve the experience and safety of patients in their care. You can be a part of this important work—join more than 160 change-makers, collaborators, and advocates as we continually work towards excellence.
