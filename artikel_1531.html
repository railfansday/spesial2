Report: Liverpool go big to replace Alisson Becker, incredible award-winning star wanted

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Liverpool are ready to go big on their replacement for Alisson Becker. An award-winning star is wanted at Anfield.

Alisson Becker’s contract will expire at the end of the season. So as things stand, he’ll leave Liverpool after nine incredible years and with a serious case for being the Reds’ greatest-ever goalkeeper.

There’s no question that he’s the best since the early 1980s and of the Premier League era, after all. Alisson has been sensational - and we’re not quite ready for it to end.

After all, the Brazilian remains an elite-level goalkeeper. Exactly how long he’s going to remain that way, however, isn’t entire clear and especially with his injury record. Liverpool may simply prefer to move on, rather than risk giving him a big-money contract.

But that would mean finding a replacement as the jury remains out on Giorgi Mamardashvili. Even if the Georgian is ultimately the pick to be Liverpool’s no.1, the Reds still have to find someone new just in case he isn’t.

L'Equipe reports that Liverpool are in the running to sign RC Lens goalkeeper Robin Risser. The 21-year-old is one of the outstanding young goalkeeper in football right now and was named the best in Ligue 1 last season.

That naturally makes him a wanted man. Paris Saint-Germain, Bayern Munich, Inter and Manchester City are all eyeing Risser with a view to signing him.

It makes things complicated. Risser is only going to join the one team, after all, so convincing him to join you means going all-out with promises. He'll have to be the no.1 goalkeeper, for staters.

Thus Liverpool can't sign Risser if they renew Alisson. Similarly, Bayern need to get rid of Manuel Neuer if they want him and City would need to give up on Gianluigi Donnarumma.

This is one to watch, then. Risser is an elite option and anyone who does commit to him stands a good chance of picking him up while the others debate internally whether to go for it. Liverpool may well jump to the front of the queue, then.
