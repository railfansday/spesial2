Survey results – Giants fans still think the Cowboys are the biggest threat

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Few predicted that New York Giants would be the first place team in the NFC East as we get ready to sit down for Thanksgiving.

Yet here we are, with the 5-3-1 Giants leading the division and in position to win it for the first time since 2011. That said, their perch atop the division was made significantly more precarious by their loss to the Washington Commanders in Week 10. That loss opened the door for both the Dallas Cowboys and the Commanders to overtake them for the division lead and knock the Giants out of the playoffs.

This week we asked Giants fans which of the two teams is the bigger threat to the Giants' control of the division, the 4-6 Dallas Cowboys or the 3-5-1 Washington Commanders.

And while the poll was surprisingly close, Giants fans fear the Cowboys more than the Commanders.

There's a good argument that either division rival is a significant threat to the Giants' playoff hopes.

On one hand, Dallas has one of the most potent offenses in the NFL, which gives them a chance to win any game in which they play as long as they can turn it into a shootout. The Cowboys also only trail the Giants by 1.5 games (as of this writing), and the two teams still have a game against each other in Week 17.

On the other hand, the Giants' loss to Washington in Week 10 gives the Commanders the head-to-head tiebreaker over the Giants. So if both teams somehow wind up with the same record, Washington will win by virtue of their 1-0-1 record against the Giants. And while the Giants are entering the toughest stretch of their schedule, the Commanders have a much easier road, with games against the Cardinals, Titans, Texans and Falcons from November 29th to December 20th. It's possible we could see Washington go on a four-game run after this week's game against the Cincinnati Bengals.

The good news is that the Giants are still in control of their own destiny and are still the favorites to win the NFC East. As of this writing the Giants are -146 to make the playoffs this year, per FanDuel, and lead the NFC East in playoff odds. That said, they're facing a tough stretch with games. They have matchups against the Jacksonville Jaguars, San Francisco 49ers, Seattle Seahawks, and Detroit Lions looming — as well as the Cleveland Browns and Cowboys. The Giants likely won't be favored in any of those games, but they are opportunities to show that they deserve to be considered playoff contenders if they can come up with some wins as underdogs.

Giants fans still believe in their team despite the Week 10 loss.

The loss dented Giants' fan confidence a bit, dropping it from the 97% of fans who were confident that the team was heading in the right direction a week ago. However, 90% of fans still being confident in the direction of the team is impressive considering how few fans were confident in the team a year ago.

It will be interesting to see how the outcome of this week's game against the Jaguars impacts fan confidence going forward.
