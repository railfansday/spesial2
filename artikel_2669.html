'Dolly Hated Kiffin' banner flys over Neyland Stadium for Tennessee vs. LSU

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Tennessee football fans started their trash talk against LSU coach Lane Kiffin hours before the Vols kick off against the Tigers.

A plane carrying a banner saying "Dolly Hated Kiffin," referencing the late country music icon and Tennessee native Dolly Parton, flew over Neyland Stadium and University of Tennessee campus around 12:30 p.m. ET ahead of the No. 9 Vols' (8-2, 5-2 SEC) game vs. No. 23 LSU (6-4, 3-4) on Nov. 20 (3:30 p.m. ET, ABC).

At the end of the banner was "265hurt.com," the website for Chattanooga, Tennessee-based personal injury law firm Warren & Griffin.

Kiffin can expect a hostile crowd to welcome he and the Tigers to Neyland Stadium. He became an infamous figure in Tennessee football history when he abandoned the Vols coaching job for USC in 2009 after just one season. It sparked a protest on campus that turned into a wild scene, including a mattress burned outside the football facility.

Kiffin returned to Neyland Stadium in 2021 as the coach of Ole Miss and, when some controversial calls went against the Vols in the fourth quarter, fans threw mustard bottles, golf balls and other debris onto the field.

He gave Vols fans an inadvertent challenge going into the big game during the SEC coaches teleconference on Nov. 18. He said that he hasn't thought about returning to Neyland Stadium because they're already been through "some really toxic environments" this year.

"I'm not challenging Tennessee," Kiffin said. "But I just don't think you can top that (Ole Miss atmosphere) – just the feeling of what that was like and the anger and hatred because it was so recent. Again, I'm not challenging Tennessee. It sounds like I am," Kiffin said. "But I think once we've gone through (the Ole Miss game), I don't think anything is going to (match that)."
