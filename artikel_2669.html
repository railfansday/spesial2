Eagles sign practice squad linebacker to roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Philadelphia Eagles officially announced two roster moves on Tuesday afternoon.

LB Chance Campbell was signed from the practice squad to the active roster.

Let's sort through this news on a player-by-player basis.

Campbell was once believed to be pushing for a roster spot. Back in training camp, Vic Fangio told reporters to put him on their 53-man projections.

Campbell began the season on the practice squad, though, before being temporarily elevated for the Eagles' Week 3 game against the Chicago Bears. Campbell was then placed on the practice squad injured list in early October before the team cut him

Campbell returned to the practice squad on November 16. In need off off-ball linebacker depth with Zack Baun going on injured reserve, the Eagles decided to temporarily elevate Anfernee Orji instead of Campbell for their Week 11 game against the Pittsburgh Steelers.

But now Campbell is on the roster while Orji remains on the practice squad. It's possible they'll temporarily elevate the latter to give the team four linebackers for their Week 12 game against the Dallas Cowboys. Chance Campbell and Orji would be the backups behind Jihaad Campbell and Jeremiah Trotter Jr.

Or perhaps they'll just roll with three again, like they did in Week 11. That situation almost got dire with Jihaad Campbell suffering a finger injury that caused Orji to see three defensive snaps before the former player quickly returned.

Chance Campbell seems like a solid depth player in addition to being able to help out on special teams. He wears No. 59.

With the Eagles trading Cam Jurgens and Drew Kendall getting hurt while Landon Dickerson was on injured reserve, Majors started at center for the eagles in Week 6 and Week 7. His bad snap in the Carolina Panthers game was a big momentum killer that prevented any hopes of a comeback in that game.

Majors hasn't played since Week 7 and has been a healthy scratch in recent weeks. He was understandably deemed expendable.

It's possible the Eagles will bring him back on the practice squad (swapping places with Campbell, then) if/when he clears waivers. Or maybe not. We'll see.
