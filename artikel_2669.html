The Steelers and Bengals final score has never happened before in NFL history

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

On Sunday, we saw two scores that have never happened in the history of the NFL. On Sunday afternoon, the Dallas Cowboys and San Francisco 49ers were the 1099th and on Sunday night, the Cincinnati Bengals and Pittsburgh Steelers played to the 1100th.

It's the second time this season we've had two Scorigamis in one weekend.

When we get a new final score in the NFL, it's called Scorigami. Normally when we get a Scorigami it's because a team scores an absolute boatload of points and often something weird happens, usually with two-point conversions. We got both on Sunday with the Bengals and Steelers.

The Bengals took a big lead on Pittsburgh in the fourth quarter, but it was a pretty typical 28-point, four-touchdown lead. Nothing weird. Then the Pittsburgh Steelers scored a touchdown and went for two. If turned a three-touchdown deficit into a… three-touchdown deficit. Really not sure when the Steelers chose to do it. That 31-11 score would have been a Scorigami, too.

After the Bengals scored to make it 38-11, that would not have been a Scorigami but the Steelers scored one more time to make it 38-18. That threaded the needle. The only 38-point winners that haven't hit now are 38-2 and 38-4.
