How to watch Liverpool vs Manchester United: TV channel and live stream for Mauricio Pochettino debut today

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Manchester United will have a fresh face in the dugout as they visit Liverpool in the Premier League this afternoon.

Mauricio Pochettino was poached from the United States national team during the international break and touched down in the north-west on Thursday.

Three short days later, he is already facing the first match of his tenure, which will begin with a daunting trip to Anfield.

United remain mired in the relegation zone, having won just once in the Premier League this season - at home to Ipswich Town on matchday two.

Liverpool, meanwhile, are in fine form and seem to have clicked into gear under the leadership of Andoni Iraola.

They are, however, winless at home to United in their last three meetings, their most recent triumph over their great rivals at Anfield coming in the form of a famous 7-0 win back in 2023.

Pochettino, though, will hope to perpetuate that run and make a statement start to his United reign here, though he has his work cut out for him.

TV channel: In the UK, the game will be televised live on Sky Sports Main Event and Premier League, with coverage starting at 4pm GMT on both channels, ahead of a 4.30pm kick-off.
