Fernando Mendoza named team captain for his third NFL start

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Las Vegas Raiders quarterback Fernando Mendoza is still seeking his first victory as a starter. While win No. 1 in his promising career remains elusive, the first overall pick in the 2026 NFL Draft is already earning respect from his teammates.

The team has also recognized his leadership traits by making him one of three Raiders captains for their Week 12 "Sunday Night Football" game against the Cleveland Browns.

Mendoza will be the captain of the offense in only his third start. Maxx Crosby will lead the defense, while return specialist Dylan Laube will head the Raiders' special teams. Crosby has 35 tackles and five sacks coming into Week 12. Laube, the first player this season to return a kickoff for a touchdown, averages 29.9 yards per kickoff return.

As for the quarterback from Indiana, Mendoza showed significant improvement in his first two starts. He followed up a 143-yard performance in Week 10 against the Seattle Seahawks with 267 yards and three touchdown passes against the Denver Broncos. The Raiders still lost to their division rivals in Week 11, but the 2025 Heisman Trophy winner had an encouraging performance against one of the NFL's top defenses.

Raiders fans hope that being named captain will inspire Mendoza to perform even better. In doing so, he could help stop their three-game losing streak and keep their playoff hopes alive.
