Who is singing the national anthem for Eagles-Cowboys on Thanksgiving?

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It's Thanksgiving Day, which can only mean food and football. There are three NFL games on this afternoon, starting with Bears-Lions at 1 p.m. on CBS, followed by Eagles-Cowboys at 4:30 p.m. on Fox and ending with Chiefs-Bills at 8 p.m. on NBC. The Packers and Rams kicked off the action on Wednesday night, with U2 performing the halftime show.

Thursday's games also feature some top-notch talent, with Off Campus' Antonio Cipriano singing the national anthem before the Bears and Lions face off and the Wu-Tang Clan handling halftime duties.

The Cowboys are bringing in some stars of their own, with country icon Miranda Lambert performing at the halftime break. But who will sing the national anthem at AT&T Stadium?

That would be two-time Grammy winner Lauren Daigle!

Daigle is a contemporary Christian artist known for hits like "You Say" and "Rescue."

You can catch Daigle before the 4:30 p.m. kick off on Fox.
