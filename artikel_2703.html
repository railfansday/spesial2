Stephen A. Smith says what the Vikings have done to one of their players is a ‘disgrace’

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Minnesota Vikings made their move before the trade deadline to bring in Kirk Cousins as their new quarterback.

Kevin O'Connell decided that heading into the remainder of the season with Kyler Murray and Carson Wentz as his options at QB was not going to help their hopes of making it to the Super Bowl. Although a move for Cousins was widely viewed as a positive, it also highlighted the mess created by the Vikings over recent years.

Stephen A. Smith is one of those people who have come down heavily on the Vikings for not looking after the quarterback position in recent years. The constant changes at the position have been criticised by Smith, who admits he feels sorry for Justin Jefferson.

Ever since allowing Sam Darnold to depart the building, Jefferson's production has declined, with only two touchdowns to his name throughout the entire 2025 season. Jefferson has already surpassed that with four touchdowns this season, but his 393 receiving yards is nothing in comparison to league leader Jaxon Smith-Njigba (1,036).

"Well, we have every reason to believe that this week he's going to start looking like the receiver we know him to be, at least to some degree. It was an utter disgrace what the Minnesota Vikings have done to him. I called out Kevin O'Connell yesterday, and I meant every word of it. It's a shame what they've done to Justin Jefferson over the last year and a half," Smith said on First Take.

"You don't have an all-world receiver like that. He hasn't looked like that over the last year and a half because he hasn't had a quarterback in the history of the National Football League. I am guaranteeing that everybody is going to agree with the statement that I'm about to make.

"There is no wide receiver that has ever been successful throwing the football to himself. It has never happened. You can't throw the football to yourself."

The Vikings' defense has been operating at an extremely high level this season, but the offense has been unable to hold its end of the bargain. Being unable to extract the best out of Jefferson has been a major failure for the organization, as they have messed around at the quarterback position.

There will now be hope that bringing Cousins back to Minnesota will bring some consistency back to the position and help Jefferson get back to doing what he does best.
