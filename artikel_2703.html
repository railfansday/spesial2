DJ Moore goes viral again after Sea Lion celly in Bills-Dolphins game

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Buffalo Bills wide receiver DJ Moore has built a reputation this season for pairing big catches with equally memorable celebrations, and Saturday's matchup against the Miami Dolphins gave him another chance to show it off.

Moore first debuted his now-signature "sea lion" celebration last week against the New York Jets, following a 45-yard touchdown catch from quarterback Josh Allen. He later explained to NFL Network's Nate Burleson that the move actually came together during a competitive period at Friday practice, though he initially wasn't sure he'd actually use it in a game.

"He did it again," the Bills posted on X, sharing video of Moore performing the sea lion celebration after a touchdown against Miami.

The celebration, which resembles the viral "hit the sea lion" TikTok trend of lying on the ground and flailing like the animal, quickly made the rounds on social media for a second straight week.

Moore entered the game with three touchdowns on the season, scoring in two of his previous three appearances.

Moore has found the end zone in multiple games down the stretch, making him a reliable red-zone target for Allen all season. His combination of production and personality has made him one of the most popular figures in the Bills' locker room this year.

Off the field, Moore has also built goodwill with Buffalo's fan base, including a gesture earlier this season where he covered the tab for an entire restaurant after a fan anonymously paid for his own meal.
