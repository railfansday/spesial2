Russell Wilson on KC Chiefs: 'I think they end up the No. 1 seed'

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

In a recent episode of 'Set Hut' with his co-host Kyle Long, former Seattle Seahawks quarterback Russell Wilson issued a bold prediction about the Kansas City Chiefs, saying that he believes the team will finish its 2026 regular-season campaign as the No. 1 seed in the AFC playoffs.

At 7-2, Kansas City is currently in a four-way tie with the Denver Broncos, Jacksonville Jaguars, and Cincinnati Bengals for second place in the conference standings behind the 8-1 Buffalo Bills.

While it is too soon to tell if Kansas City will be able to maintain its momentum in the second half of its 2026 campaign, the Chiefs will get an excellent opportunity to earn their eighth win of the season against the Arizona Cardinals in Week 11 before facing Buffalo on Thanksgiving Day in Week 12 with a chance to take a lead in the AFC.
