'Goosebumps!' - Super-sub Carlos Espi salutes Bernabeu roar after bailing out Real Madrid against Celta

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Carlos Espi admitted the Santiago Bernabeu atmosphere gave him "goosebumps" after his 85th-minute rebound secured Real Madrid's 2-1 comeback win over Celta Vigo. The young striker stressed squad unity as Los Blancos extended their winning streak to nine matches ahead of Tuesday's Champions League battle.

Carlos Espi struck in the 85th minute to seal a 2-1 comeback victory for Real Madrid against Celta Vigo and extend their winning run to nine consecutive matches. After Pablo Duran gave Celta a sixth-minute lead and Kylian Mbappe blazed a first-half penalty over the crossbar, Jude Bellingham levelled on 66 minutes before Espi pounced on a rebound from Vinicius Junior's cross.

The decisive finish marked the Valencian forward's fourth goal of the campaign and preserved Real Madrid's perfect home record this season. Speaking after the final whistle, Espi admitted the backing from the stands left a powerful impression on him.

Espi explained that every substitute entered the pitch determined to turn the contest around, crediting the collective spirit inside the 23-to-24-man squad alongside the vocal home crowd.

"I get goosebumps," Espi said. "It's indescribable how much support I feel from everyone. I will give my all for the madridistas and for this club. I try to do what the coach asks of me, and every minute I get, I will make the most of it. If I can help the team, even better. As I said the other day, the goal is to stay united. We're 23-24 players, and we all need to move in the same direction. Those of us who came on did so fully focused on securing the victory, and that's what we achieved."

While Real Madrid endured a frustrating opening half, Espi insisted dropping points at the Santiago Bernabeu was never an option once the crowd lifted the team in the second period.

"Honestly, the fans are incredible; they support us from the very first minute," he added. "Today, they carried us forward. We can't afford to drop points here. Honestly, it's a real energy boost. It was a tough match that we managed to win, and now we have to keep moving forward because this doesn't stop—there's another battle in the Champions League."

With only 48 hours to recover from a demanding domestic test, Espi stressed that Los Blancos must immediately channel their momentum into European action.

"Now, the most important thing is Tuesday because this doesn't stop," he concluded. "We have two days to prepare for the match, and with all our enthusiasm, we're going for it."
