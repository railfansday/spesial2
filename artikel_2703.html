Patrick Mahomes shakes off injury scare, throws for two TDs in Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Kansas City Chiefs QB Patrick Mahomes completed 23 of 31 passes for 242 yards with two touchdowns in Week 9 against the New York Jets. He carried two times for eight yards but also fumbled twice – losing one.

Why It Matters: Mahomes, who shook off pain in his throwing elbow after taking a hit from Jets rookie David Bailey near the end of the first half, posted his sixth multi-touchdown pass game of the season. He has five TD throws and 563 pass yards in the last two weeks, which coincides with the return of Rashee RIce. Mahomes remains a consistent fantasy QB1 option in all formats, including in Week 10 against the Atlanta Falcons.
