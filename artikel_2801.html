Rafael Cabral plays hero as Real Salt Lake shock Seattle Sounders

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

SEATTLE – When Real Salt Lake needed a hero to keep their Audi 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs hopes alive, Rafael Cabral delivered.

The Brazilian goalkeeper produced a heroic performance in Thursday night's Western Conference Wild Card clash at Seattle Sounders FC, saving three penalty kicks across regulation and the decisive shootout to help RSL advance, 4-2, following a 1-1 draw at Lumen Field.

Cabral first denied Albert Rusnák from the spot in the 31st minute, then came up with two more saves against Dejan Joveljić and Rusnák in the shootout.

With RSL converting all four of their attempts, the visitors punched their ticket to Round One of the playoffs. They'll face No. 1-seeded Vancouver Whitecaps FC in a Best of-3 Series that begins Sunday at BC Place (8:30 pm ET | Apple TV).

"We studied a lot. I have an amazing goalkeeper coach, Mirza [Harambasic]," Cabral told Apple TV post-match. "He gave me tips, he gave me cues, and thank God I could execute and we could win this game."

Just five minutes after Cabral's first penalty save, Rusnák made amends by finishing off a slick team move involving Sebastián Gómez and Jesús Ferreira to give Seattle a 36th-minute lead.

But RSL battled back, as Morgan Guilavogui capitalized on a costly Seattle giveaway to find a second-half equalizer before Cabral's shootout heroics completed the upset.

It was a remarkable result for an RSL side that entered the match as clear underdogs, having lost their two most dangerous players - Diego Luna and Zavier Gozo - during the regular season.

For Cabral, the result was also a testament to the resilience of a group that endured plenty of adversity this year.

"I think we suffered too much this season. It wasn't an easy season," Cabral said. "We have an amazing group, an amazing young group, and I think we deserve this."

Now, RSL face an even bigger challenge against a Vancouver side looking for MLS Cup presented by Audi glory following a runner-up finish in 2025.

Drawing on his experience winning titles in his native Brazil with the likes of Santos and Cruzeiro, Cabral believes RSL have what it takes to continue defying expectations.

"As soon as you make the playoffs, anything can happen," Cabral said. "I won titles in Brazil, and we were not the best team. We didn't qualify first, but we still won.

"We have a hard game against Vancouver, but as I said, we believe in ourselves, and we know we can compete and beat any team."
