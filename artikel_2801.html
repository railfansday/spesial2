Nebraska volleyball vs. Iowa live updates, stats and highlights

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

No. 1 Nebraska volleyball remains at home for a Big Ten showdown with Iowa Hawkeyes for Senior Night. This will be the 42nd match between these two schools, with Nebraska leading the series 41-0.

The Huskers were ranked No. 1 in the latest AVCA Coaches Poll, released Nov. 9. It's Nebraska's 135th week at No. 1 in the AVCA Coaches Poll, the most of any program in the nation.

Senior Bergen Reilly was named Big Ten Setter of the Week for the second consecutive week. Reilly set the Huskers to an NCAA-leading .448 hitting percentage last week. The senior was fourth nationally with 13.33 assists per set across the two matches.

Nebraska is on a 57-match regular-season winning streak that dates back to the 2024 season. It's the longest regular-season winning streak in program history in the NCAA era (since 1981).
