Georgia defensive coordinator Glenn Schumann set to become new Florida State head coach

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Georgia football defensive coordinator Glenn Schumann is expected to leave the Bulldogs to become head coach at Florida State, a source confirmed to the Athens Banner Herald Monday night.

Schumann is the only assistant who has worked under coach Kirby Smart for all 11 of his Georgia seasons.

ESPN's Pete Thamel reported that Schumann agreed to a five-year deal and is expected to stay with Georgia through the postseason.

The 36-year old interviewed in the past for the Philadelphia Eagles defensive coordinator job and his name was connected to the North Carolina head coach opening before Bill Belichick was hired.

He's in his fourth season as defensive coordinator after being promoted from co-defensive coordinator with Will Muschamp after the 2022 season.

Georgia is sixth in the nation in both total defense (267.0 yards per game) and scoring defense (13.9). The Bulldogs are allowing the fewest points per game in the SEC.

Schumann was making $2.2 million this year in deal that runs through the end of the 2028 season, but would not owe Georgia any liquidated damages for taking a Power Conference head coaching job like Florida State.

It remains to be seen if Schumann will coach with Georgia in the SEC Championship game on Dec. 5 or in the College Football Playoff that runs through Jan. 25.

Schumann, who was born in Valdosta, has ties to Florida State, His grandfather Jack Haskin coached on the first Florida State football team at and is a member of the FSU Hall of Fame.

Schumann will have a coaching staff to assemble and a roster to build through the high school ranks and transfer portal.

Schumann has recruited and developed three Butkus Award winners that he coached as inside linebackers coach: Roquan Smith, Nakobe Dean and Jalon Walker

He worked with Smart and coach Nick Sabana at Alabama from 208-15 as an undergraduate analyst, graduate assistant and director of player development.

"I just think I've had the privilege of being around some of the best in the game, not just from a coaching standpoint, but from a leadership standpoint, from how people run a meeting room, how they handle tough situations, how they handle the staff," Schumann said in August. "There's attention to detail every single day, whether it's here or whether it was there, it sets the tone for everybody in the organization. There's a level of urgency to that approach, and your personality has to be your personality."

Schumann becomes the seventh former assistant under Smart to become a head coach including four currently: Oregon's Dan Lanning, Syracuse's Fran Brown, South Carolina's Shane Beamer and Georgia State's Dell McGee. Former head coaches hired from Georgia include Sam Pittman at Arkansas and Mel Tucker at Colorado and Michigan State.

"He is extremely bright, extremely passionate," Smart said in 2023. "I rely heavily on Glenn for input on practice schedules, defensive design, thinking outside the box. There's not a time when I think of who can I ask on my staff that understands what we want and what we need, Glenn Schumann is that guy. He's very talented."
