Reign end four-game win streak in 6-4 loss to Coachella Valley Firebirds

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

ONTARIO, CA. -- In Erik Portillo's 100th AHL game, the Ontario Reign fail to extend their winning streak to five games or take home the victory to make the night even more special for Portillo.

Victor Östman had 30 saves as Coachella Valley squeaks by with a 6-4 win in Ontario.

The Reign are now 2-2-0 against the Firebirds this season and although it seemed like Coachella Valley ran away with the game in the second period - scoring three goals - it didn't end up that way.

"The second was OK to start," Ontario coach Andrew Lord said in postgame interviews. "And then it really got away from us there. Unfortunately after we tied it up, our next couple of shifts weren't great. Give them the credit, they really had some bounce back shifts there, took the momentum back. We lost our tracking for sure, we're getting beat up the ice and a couple odd-man (rushes) there. And they'll (Coachella Valley) make you pay with how much firepower they have."

Although Ontario had a four-game win streak, Coachella Valley was 3-1-0 in their last four.

And the Firebirds hold a better record and standing in the AHL Pacific Division (9-6-3-1; 22 points; 4th place) in comparison to Ontario (9-8-1-0; 19 points; 8th place).

Ian McKinnon scored an unassisted goal as his puck ricocheted off Portillo's leg pad, sneaking to the back of the net and giving the Firebirds the early 1-0 lead.

After tonight, the Firebirds improve to 5-1-1 when scoring first.

Lane Pederson scored a goal in his fourth straight game for the game-tying shot 2:49 into the second period, now leading the team in goals with eight this season.

Michael Mastrodomenico and Kirill Kirsanov earned assists on Pederson's shot.

That tie would be short-lived - less than two minutes actually - as Jagger Firkus and Logan Morrison help Ty Nelson find the back of the net to quickly give the lead back to the Firebirds.

Firkus would end with three points as he earned another assist on Logan Morrison's goal and scored an unassisted goal of his own six minutes later.

J.R. Avon would also help on Morrison's insurance goal.

The Firebirds would head into the final period up ahead 4-1, but there was still 20 minutes left of hockey.

And the Reign hadn't given up, they came out swinging.

When asked about the message during intermission before period three, Lord said:

"We needed to take care of their transition game. They were really good; out of their own zone, encountering us, and making some of those fast break plays. So trying to eliminate that, obviously the power-play goals were big … you can just see the guys didn't stop believing, they stayed positive. They kept playing all the way through and when you do that sometimes big things can happen."

And big things happened, but closing out the game did not.

With assists from Taylor Ward and Scott Perunovich, Jämsen scored a power-play goal 43 seconds into the third period.

Kenny Connors would put them in a 4-3 deficit with his second goal this season, with help from Francesco Pinelli.

And just like that - the Reign went from down three goals to down one - in less than five minutes.

John Hayden and Eduard Šalé passed to Mitchell Stephens who would get his puck past Portillo, giving Coachella Valley its fifth goal of the night.

A goal that would seal the fate of the game's outcome.

Kirsanov's power-play goal would bring hope to the crowd with eight minutes remaining in the matchup, but the comeback couldn't quite be finished.

Pederson's second point of the game and Kenta Isogai's first point came from assisting Kirsanov's goal.

Ryden Evers would score an empty-net, power-play goal with 11 seconds left as Reign fans headed towards the exit.

Portillo finished with 30 saves but fell to 4-3-0.

The Reign went 2-for-4 on the power play and 2-for-3 on the penalty kill.

Ontario will have its final November home game Sunday at 3 p.m. against the Henderson Silver Knights (11-5-0-0; 22 points; 3rd place).
