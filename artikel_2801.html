Eagles' Nick Sirianni pulled away from fans after wild Thanksgiving win over Cowboys

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Philadelphia Eagles head coach Nick Sirianni has been known to lose his cool on sidelines before.

A dramatic 39-38 come-from-behind Thanksgiving Day victory over the rival Dallas Cowboys, punctuated by a 60-yard field goal from Jake Elliott with 2 seconds remaining, was certainly cause for celebration.

Sirianni needed to meet Cowboys head coach Brian Schottenheimer for the customary postgame handshake between the two head coaches, but instead ran toward a smattering of fans in the first-row luxury seating at AT&T Stadium, including several Cowboys fans, to celebrate. Sirianni was pulled away by Eagles' security chief Dom DiSandro, with senior vice president for communications Bob Lange in tow.

Sirianni said after the game that he was excited to celebrate.

"There was a lot of Eagles fans back there," Sirianni said with a smile. "I was excited to celebrate with those Eagles' fans and our team and everything like that."

The Eagles trailed 31-10 late in the third quarter before erasing the deficit, with former Cowboys backup quarterback Joe Milton (eventually named the game's MVP) leading the way.

Sirianni's postgame meeting with Schottenheimer was brief. Walking in the tunnel afterward, Sirianni let out a triumphant yell.

"That's how you fight," he said. "We know how to fight."

This is not the first time Sirianni has acted oddly with regard to fans. Earlier this month, he had to deny flipping off home fans as the team dropped to 2-7. He had to apologize in 2024 for engaging with fans negatively despite a win at home.

Earlier in the game, running back Will Shipley and Sirianni got into it on the sidelines.

Other antics of Sirianni's have included staring down the camera and running toward the end zone to celebrate with his players after a touchdown. He led the Eagles to the Super Bowl 59 title and the team has made the playoffs every season he has been the head coach.
