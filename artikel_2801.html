Lions star Aidan Hutchinson injures knee while celebrating sack of Drake Maye

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Detroit Lions star Aidan Hutchinson made his presence known early against the New England Patriots. On the Patriots' first offensive drive, Hutchinson broke through the line on third down and sacked Drake Maye.

Following the play, Hutchinson got up to celebrate the sack. He jumped into the air and immediately fell to the ground after landing. Hutchinson appeared to be holding his right knee.

Hutchinson walked off the field, but headed straight to the medical tent on the sideline. He was later spotted on the broadcast heading to the locker room.

He was deemed questionable to return with a knee injury, but late in the third quarter, he was ruled out for the remainder of the game.

Hutchinson wasn't the only player injured on the play. Maye also left the game. He was deemed questionable to return due to an ankle injury.

Detroit eventually cruised to a 35-10 victory.

"We'll know more when we get back in the States," head coach Dan Campbell said, according to the Detroit News' Nolan Bianchi. "Anytime you lose a player of his caliber, the type of teammate he is, he's a captain of ours, that doesn't make things easy."

Even before that play, Hutchinson made a big impact in the game. On the play before his injury, he smartly rushed across the line of scrimmage once he noticed the play clock ran out. His quick thinking resulted in the Patriots being charged with a delay of game, setting up a third-and-long.

Losing Hutchinson for any significant period of time would be a devastating result for the Lions. Coming into the game, Hutchinson was Pro Football Focus' top-graded edge rusher on the year.

While NFL players rarely get injured while celebrating plays, it has happened before. Back in 2001, Arizona Cardinals kicker Bill Gramatica tore his ACL after celebrating a first quarter field goal in a game against the New York Giants.
