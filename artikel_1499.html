Roma among teams pursuing Alejandro Balde

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Barcelona has decided to sell Alejandro Balde, who is finding himself increasingly less available in La Liga.

According to ElNacional.cat, in addition to Roma, Manchester United are also in the running for the Spanish winger.

There’s talk of interest, not a formal negotiation.

However, Flick, among other things, has reportedly been open to leaving the defender, who hasn’t played since the start.

The left-back has also been linked with Roma, even in recent weeks. He would be the alternative to Oosterwolde to further boost Roma’s wing options.
