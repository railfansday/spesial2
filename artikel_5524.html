Canada is giving Ford $324 million to retool its Oakville plant for Super Duty trucks

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The C$464.5 million from the Strategic Response Fund also supports a new stamping plant, and the government says it will protect more than 2,000 jobs

Cole Burston / Bloomberg via Getty Images

Canada's federal government announced C$464.5 million in funding on Friday to retool the Oakville Assembly Complex in Ontario, owned by Ford $F Motor Co.'s Canadian unit, for production of the F-Series Super Duty truck. The amount equals $324 million, according to Bloomberg.

The money comes through the Strategic Response Fund and supports Ford Motor Company of Canada's project of nearly C$5 billion, the government said. Industry Minister Mélanie Joly and Foreign Affairs Minister Anita Anand, the member of Parliament for Oakville East, made the announcement in Oakville.

The funding goes toward enlarging and re-equipping the Oakville plant, and toward building a new stamping facility that will let Canada make more big auto components at home. Ford has announced it will introduce multi-energy variants of the Super Duty trucks. Trucks built in Oakville will supply the North American market and be exported to other countries, the government said.

The government said the investment will protect more than 2,000 jobs at the complex and support production of up to 100,000 vehicles a year. The Ontario government is contributing C$294.6 million to the project.

"Canada is an auto nation, now and into the future," Joly said in a statement. "In a more complex global economy, that means protecting well-paying jobs and securing our world-class auto sector."

Bev Goodman, president and CEO of Ford Motor Company of Canada, said in a statement that the investment reflects the company's confidence in Canadian manufacturing. "Oakville gives us the capacity to build more of the trucks our customers count on and makes Ford stronger on both sides of the border," Goodman said.

Ontario Economic Development, Job Creation and Trade Minister Vic Fedeli said in a statement that tariffs and trade complexities continue to pressure the auto industry. He said the retooling and expansion of the Oakville facility shows the strength of Ontario's business environment and workforce.

Ford Motor Company of Canada is a wholly owned subsidiary of Ford Motor Co. and employs more than 7,000 people in Canada, according to the government.
