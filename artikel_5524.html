Yamaha Introduces Next-Generation YDS-152 and YDS-122 Digital Saxophones

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Enhanced playability, expanded instrument voices and upgraded connectivity bring new possibilities for musical expression to players of all experience levels

BUENA PARK, Calif., Nov. 3, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Yamaha today announces the U.S. availability of the YDS-152 and YDS-122 Digital Saxophones, the second generation of its innovative digital wind instrument series. Building on the success of the original YDS-150 and YDS-120, the new models combine the expressive, breath-controlled experience of a traditional saxophone with the flexibility and convenience of digital technology, making music creation more accessible and enjoyable for beginners and experienced musicians alike.

First introduced in 2020, the Yamaha Digital Saxophone series was designed to bring the joy of playing a wind instrument to more people, regardless of experience, environment, or musical background. Offering the familiar key layout and expressive capabilities of an acoustic saxophone alongside adjustable volume and headphone connectivity, the series provides an approachable way to play, practice and explore music anytime, anywhere.

The new YDS-152 and YDS-122 build on this foundation with 89 instrument voices, including 16 newly added acoustic instrument sounds, USB-C connectivity, improved key durability and action, and an updated analog controller for more expressive pitch manipulation. Together, these enhancements expand the creative possibilities of the instruments while preserving the intuitive, engaging playing experience that defines the series.

"The new YDS-122 and YDS-152 represent the next evolution of the Yamaha Digital Saxophone. We've enhanced the features players value most while making the experience even more versatile, accessible, and enjoyable," said Sarah Hollandsworth, product marketing manager for winds & strings at Yamaha. "Whether you're an experienced musician looking for new creative possibilities or someone discovering the joy of making music for the first time, these instruments are designed to inspire expression, exploration, and connection through music."

Product InformationThe Yamaha YDS-152 and YDS-122 Digital Saxophones are available now for purchase.

For more information, visit: yamaha.io/YDS2026.
