Three Rivers Report: A Week 11 win nearly guarantees playoff spot for Steelers

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Pittsburgh Steelers sit in second place in the AFC North and face a critical week for their AFC playoff chances against the Philadelphia Eagles and Joe Milton. No team should be taken lightly, but the Steelers need to take advantage of the very banged-up Eagles team starting their third quarterback of the season. No disrespect to Milton, but this is a perfect bounce-back spot for the Steelers and their defense.

This week is very important for Pittsburgh. With a loss, the Steelers' playoff chances drop from 75% to 64%. A win over the Eagles increases the playoff chances to 81%. The Steelers follow up their matchup with the Eagles with a Black Friday matchup with the Broncos, which is a far tougher test. The Steelers need this one badly.
