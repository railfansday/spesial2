The Link Farm: 11.13.26

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Welcome to The Link Farm. This is your update on all things Texas A&M (that we didn't already talk about in another article). And if there's something we missed, be sure to talk about it in the comments.

Volleyball looks to halt skid against LSU. Coming off three straight road losses, Texas A&M returns home to College Station looking to get back in the win column against LSU. The Aggies and Tigers get started at 6:30 p.m. CT on SECN+ this evening.

Men's Hoops continue winning ways. Bucky Ball is in full swing as the Aggies notched an 88-69 victory over Oklahoma State last night. The win was the fourth straight for Texas A&M to start the season. The Aggies will look to keep the momentum up when Southeastern Louisiana comes to town on Monday.

Women's Hoops set to face Lamar on Monday. Before the men's squad takes the court at Reed Arena on Monday, Texas A&M's women's basketball team will host the Lamar Cardinals. Aggie fans can tune in at 4:00 p.m. CT on Monday on SECN+.

Trio of Aggies headed for NCAA Nationals. The Men's and Women's Cross Country teams competed in the South Central Regional, finishing 4th (M) and 5th (W). Three Aggies advanced to the NCAA Cross Country Championships: Michael Moiben and Jack Johnston of the Men's team and Debora Cheromo of the Women's team.
