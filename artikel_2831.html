Eagles injury update: Quinyon Mitchell a DNP to start Thanksgiving week

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Eagles injury update: Quinyon Mitchell a DNP to start Thanksgiving week originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

Eagles cornerback Quinyon Mitchell (hamstring) was listed as a non-participant to kick off a short week of prep before the Eagles play the Cowboys on Thanksgiving in Dallas.

The Eagles didn't actually practice on Monday, so their injury report was an estimation.

Not only was Mitchell (hamstring) listed as a non-participant, but so was fellow cornerback Jonathan Jones (ankle). Both players got hurt in the win over the Steelers on Sunday. Mitchell was seen getting on a stationary bike in the post-game locker room, while Jones was in a walking boot on his right foot.

If neither are available, the Eagles would potentially start Riq Woolen and Kelee Ringo against a high-powered Cowboys offense in Dallas. Although, the Eagles could also move Cooper DeJean to outside corner and replace him with either Michael Carter or Kenny Moore.

Mitchell was playing very well on Sunday against DK Metcalf before Metcalf was tossed and Mitchell left with his injury. Woolen was actually benched in the second quarter but had to re-enter the game after injuries.

Here's the Eagles' first injury report of the week:

DNP: CB Jonathan Jones (ankle), CB Quinyon Mitchell (hamstring), OLB Arnold Ebiketie (oblique), LB Smael Mondon (ankle)

Limited: RB Saquon Barkley (neck/illness), TE Dallas Goedert (shin), QB Tanner McKee (rib), RB Will Shipley (foot/illness)

Full: DT Jalen Carter (quad), DB Michael Carter (abdomen), WR Britain Covey (knee), OLB Jonathan Greenard (ankle), WR DeVonta Smith (hamstring)

It's good news that Barkley was listed as a limited participant to start the week after leaving the Steelers game early with what appeared to be a stinger. Barkley came off the field holding his left arm limp at one point. He had a stinger earlier in the season too.

We'll also monitor Dallas Goedert's injury the rest of this week, but the fact that he was limited on Monday seems like a good sign for his availability on Thursday.
