Ka'imi Fairbairn is the AFC special teams player of the month

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

It has not been a successful season for the Texans, but kicker Ka’imi Fairbairn is coming off of a strong October.

Fairbairn went 11-of-11 on field goals last month with five of those kicks coming from at least 50 yards. He also hit a 49-yard kick on the final play of the game to lift the Texans to a win over the Jaguars in Week 6.

Fairbairn was also 10-of-10 on extra points over the course of the month, which helped make him the choice as the AFC special teams player of the month. He also took those honors in December 2018.

Fairbairn is 15-of-16 on field goals and 16-of-16 on extra points for the season.
