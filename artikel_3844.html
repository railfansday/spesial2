The answer is Grand Theft Auto 6, of course. Rockstar has once again created a masterclass open world game, arguably the greatest yet (thanks to its massive budget).

There is a singular word that defines their games: moments. Rockstar knows how to create interesting moments throughout the entire game to not only feel unique, but personal, as if it was put into place at a specific time just for you to encounter and maybe nobody else.

Interestingly, I love the game but I don’t spend much time playing. It’s such a dense game that I want to take my sweet time with it, and experience what each town and county has to offer. I don’t care if it will take months to beat the game—because the entire game is another Rockstar “moment”.

One of these moments occurred when I was dirt biking up in the hills. I heard commotion off the beaten path so I go explore the noises. There were two friends hunting. One guy is dressed up in a deer outfit, and his buddy “accidentally” shot him. They ask me to grab their truck and take them the hospital. On the way the two start arguing in the backseat, with the guy who got shot saying it was done on purpose because he slept with his wife. All hell broke loose, and they were fighting over the shotgun. The guy who originally got shot ended up blowing the head off his friend during the struggle, and pushed him out of the car. I finish the drive to the hospital and the man thanks me, giving me his shotgun as a thank you gift.

In another incident, I’m cruising the backroads with Lucia when she notices three people on a bridge. Two of the guys are tempting the other to jump off into the water. Lucia tells me to slow down and check it out. All of the guys are getting drunk and acting stupid, and they dare us two to jump. We agree, hold hands, and jump into the water. The other two guys start laughing saying, “Thanks for your ride, idiots!” and stole my car.

This game is so full of these random moments. GTA VI is simply a generational piece of fiction. To its characters, the writing, mechanics, exploration, and other details, it’s a new bench mark for gaming.

Keep playing the Phantom Liberty DLC for Cyberpunk.
