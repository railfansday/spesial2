Man City are in talks with Matheus Nunes' representatives over extending his contract beyond 2028, sources have told Football Insider.

Nunes has become a fan favourite at Man City after becoming a regular in their starting eleven over the last few seasons.

The 28-year-old arrived at the Etihad Stadium from Wolves as a midfielder in 2023, but became a right-back under Pep Guardiola and has since made the position his own.

Enzo Maresca has relied on the Portuguese international this season, and he is believed to be his first choice in the position when fully fit.

City are keen to agree a new contract with Nunes in the coming months, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast.

They want him to extend his stay at the club beyond 2028 despite the threat of sanctions from the Premier League looming large.

Nunes registered nine goal contributions in 53 appearances for the Citizens last term, making 32 starts in the Premier League.

He has been in and out of the side under Maresca due to fitness issues, but looks set to play a key role going forward, even as they eye more additions at the back.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "They [Man City] will be pushing to reach an agreement. It’s a no-brainer for City, really, to try and sort out Nunes’ future. He’s under contract until 2028, and I’m sure they’ll be looking to secure his long-term future and end any doubts that maybe he could move away from Manchester City.

"He has made that right-back spot his own since he was converted by Pep Guardiola. I think talks are ongoing between all parties."

Nunes' emergence as City's main right-back has seen academy graduate Lewis struggle for regular minutes in recent seasons.

He only made four league starts last term and has featured in six games so far in this campaign, with just 102 top-flight minutes to his name.

Sources have exclusively revealed to Football Insider that Lewis is open to leaving Man City in the coming months as he looks to progress in his career.

The 21-year-old was the subject of interest from Nottingham Forest in the summer, and is bound to be on the radar of other Premier League clubs in January.
