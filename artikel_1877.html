Man Utd player ratings vs Chelsea as two stars humiliate Michael Carrick

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Manchester United were taken apart once again against Chelsea in what could very well prove to be Michael Carrick’s final game in charge.

United turned in a rather promising display in the opening half at Stamford Bridge on Saturday afternoon, finding some cohesion and fluidity in attack to trouble the hosts.

Their lack of conviction in front of goal came back to haunt them however in a disaster of a second-half. United imploded again after Morgan Rogers sliced through to open the scoring, losing their heads with Lisandro Martinez sent off in ridiculous fashion.

Cole Palmer scored from the penalty spot with Estevao having an effort chalked off for offside. It could have been plenty more for Chelsea with the visitors surrendering completely as the contest wore on.
