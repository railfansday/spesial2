Julianne Moore Wears a Sleek White Coat With Her Black Evening Dress at ELLE’s 2026 Women in Hollywood Event

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Weeks before the release of her next film, The Debut, Julianne Moore is making a splash on the red carpet for ELLE’s Women in Hollywood event.

Moore wore a Ralph Lauren ensemble to the celebration, which took place at The Four Seasons Beverly Hills in Los Angeles on November 16. Her look featured a long white coat over a black dress, finished with a thick silver choker. Her bright red lip popped against the black-and-white color scheme.

The actress opened up about working on The Debut in her recent ELLE cover story and revealed that singing in the movie—in which she stars as Mona Friedman, a New Jersey housewife who tries out for a local musical—was a challenge.

“The unsteadiness was easy for me, because I’m not a natural singer. I haven’t been trained. So that was easy and kind of fun in a way—because it’s not like she can’t carry a tune; she sang in church—but she doesn’t have any power, and she has no emotion behind it,” she explained. “I worked with a wonderful vocal coach and with all of our [supporting cast]; they’re all from musical theater. But the day that I had to sing that song [Mona’s big solo moment] live in the room was very early on in our shoot. A lot of people were there—they were in the other room around the monitor, and all of those people I’m singing to are real [Broadway] singers. There was a lot of support for me, and I think a lot of concern. I was scared.”

Moore also praised the movie’s director, Jesse Eisenberg, for offering her kindness while working with her during one of the movie’s most important scenes. “[Mona’s pivotal scene] is, like, eight pages. It’s a lot. And he said, ‘Listen, I don’t want you to worry about memorizing it. On the day, we’ll have a teleprompter. You don’t have to worry about it. Because I know if it were me, all I would be thinking about is remembering those lines,’” she recalled.

Moore added, “I was like, ‘Phew, what a relief.’ But of course, because he said that, by the time we shot it, I had memorized it. He took the burden away from me emotionally and allowed me to be able to learn it.”

Following the release of The Debut in December, Moore will next star in the film adaptation of Alex Comfort’s The Joy of Sex and in Tom Ford’s Cry to Heaven. In addition to her work onscreen, Moore will make her return to the stage with a starring role in Sarah Ruhl’s Faydra at the Manhattan Theatre Club in February.
