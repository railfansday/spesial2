Docebo Inc. (DCBO +0.69%) reported third quarter results featuring 14% total revenue growth and the declaration of a $60 million one-time special dividend. Management reported that the company is unifying skills intelligence, learning, and knowledge into a single workforce readiness platform to support performance in the flow of work. The company reported that average contract value increased 24% year over year to $77,900, driven by the expansion of multi-product enterprise agreements and a move into regulated verticals. Management raised full-year 2026 total revenue guidance to $276.7 million, representing approximately 14% growth compared to an initial target of 10.6%.
