We're in Hawaii, and DH got an escort to breakfast the other morning by a very friendly bird that has since been identified as a Cattle Egret.

It came up to him, and walked with him down the path and across two foot bridges, before it got scared away by oncoming children.
Perhaps it was substituting your DH for cattle. They get their name for hanging around cows/horses as they graze and eating the bugs etc. that are flushed by the grazing animals.
