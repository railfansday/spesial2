Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Wind resources and manufacturing sales drove 28% EBITDA growth.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

ReNew Energy Global Plc (RNW +0.58%) reported growth in operational capacity and financial performance for the second quarter of fiscal 2027, leading to an increase in full-year Adjusted EBITDA guidance. Management noted that higher wind plant load factors and profitable external sales from the manufacturing segment contributed to the 28% year-over-year increase in Adjusted EBITDA. The company continued its capital recycling strategy with the announced sale of assets expected to generate $300 million in total cash flow. Management also highlighted an improving credit profile, with rating outlook revisions to positive by Moody's and CARE, and a reduction in net debt leverage for operational projects to 5.3x.
