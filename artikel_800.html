Florence Pugh Celebrates Robert Pattinson’s Bond Casting: “He’s Been the No. 1 Vote in My Household”

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The star, who worked with Pattinson on 'Dune: Part Three,' also revealed she has pitched Denis Villeneuve on her own Bond role.

By Kirsten Chuba, Lilly Workneh November 17, 2026 11:41am

Florence Pugh is fully on board for Robert Pattinson‘s James Bond era.

The star appears alongside Pattinson in the upcoming Dune: Part Three, which is also from Bond director Denis Villeneuve. His official casting was announced on Monday morning, and that night Pugh was honored at Elle’s Women in Hollywood celebration, where she weighed in on the news.

“I’m so happy because he’s been the no. 1 vote in my household for like quite a long time,” she told The Hollywood Reporter. “He’s so unbelievably talented. He makes moves that you’re just not guessing, and it’s just so wonderful to see a career just float and be this unbelievably versatile thing. I’m so excited that he got that role. So good, and with Denis.” Related Stories Movies Robert Pattinson's Bondmaxxing: How 007 Still Has Pop Culture in a Vice Grip Movies We Now Know What Robert Pattinson Was Doing When the James Bond Casting News Broke

Villeneuve also made an appearance at the Los Angeles event to honor Pugh, telling the room at the Four Seasons Beverly Hills that she “belongs to the stratosphere of major movie stars” and has “a rare ability to convey so much inner stillness. She listens with intent and purpose, projecting a deep inner strain, a brilliant intelligence.”

During her speech, Pugh reflected on her career and paid tribute to the many who have helped her along the way, saying, “My existence in this job is down to thousands of people.” She also tossed it back to Villeneuve by revealing, “I sent Denis a message this morning, and I said, ‘Congratulations on finding your Bond. Nice catch. Any villains with the size of a five-foot-four female?'”

Pattinson’s casting — up against actors including Callum Turner, Jack Lowden, Josh O’Connor and Nicholas Hoult — caps years of speculation in Hollywood for who would take over from Daniel Craig as the next 007.

“James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honor of a lifetime,” said Pattinson in a statement. “I’m deeply grateful to Denis, Amy, David, Tanya and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me. To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.”
