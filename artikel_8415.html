None - what do you need to insure for those 1-2 weeks? (For health coverage does the health insurance you have not already provide adequate coverage?)

Seriously, 1-2 weeks in a developed country, no need for travel insurance at all.
I think that is where I come out as well. We have been taking a couple of trips per year with no travel insurance. I lost some money when our London/Normandy trip got cancelled when Covid hit, but I think that I am ahead, financially, for having skipped travel insurance all of these years. I also worry about the terms and conditions. Knowing insurance companies, they will try to find a way to deny a claim.
I use the Forbes travel insurance page to find a deal that fits my needs: https://www.forbes.com/advisor/travel-i ... insurance/

I skipped travel insurance for years as everybody was healthy. I had a relative that needed an expensive air ambulance; that opened my eyes.
Now, I always buy some form of travel insurance.

I'm still relatively healthy...but of Medicare age, so on my last trip overseas, I only covered medical and evacuation. I didn't insure the cost of the trip.

On big ticket family trips, I insure the cost of the trip and medical evacuation as a minimum. In the US, I pass on medical coverage.
On weekend type trips, I pass on travel insurance.

During Covid days, we missed a trip because one of us flunked the Covid test. We got a doctor's note and the insurance company paid as promised.
