Vusion Unveils Its Strategic Ambitions for 2030

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

PARIS, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Vusion (Euronext Paris: VU), the global leader in digitalization solutions for physical commerce, today presents its new Vusion 2030 strategic plan at its Capital Markets Day.

Thierry Gadou, Chairman and CEO of Vusion, said:

"Vusion's management team is proud to present its new strategic plan for 2030. It is built on a conviction increasingly shared by leading retailers: physical stores will be at the heart of tomorrow's omnichannel and agentic commerce, and their digital transformation is more than ever a strategic priority.

Vusion has demonstrated that technology can turn physical stores into ultra-efficient environments and strategic assets in the battle for e-commerce. Artificial intelligence will further accelerate the need to digitalize physical stores.

We are ready for this next chapter. Since launching our previous strategic plan, Vusion'27, in 2022, we have significantly grown and transformed our company: over the past 4 years, our revenue has increased more than 2.5-fold. We have built the world's leading and most advanced technology platform. EdgeSense and VusionOX have redefined the standard of performance and functionality that physical stores can achieve. We have built strong positions in AI-powered computer vision, Retail Intelligence and Retail Media, and our VAS revenue has more than tripled, with approximately â¬300 million expected this year. Finally, we are on track to achieve, or come close to achieving, in 2027 the ambition we set ourselves four years ago, and we have built the financial strength required to support our future development.

Vusion is now a leading player in a broader, rapidly expanding market. Store digitalization, within the scope of Vusion's solutions, is expected to represent a market of approximately â¬12 billion by 2030. Our ambition is to reach approximately â¬3 billion in revenue by 2030, driven by substantial growth in our installed base and even stronger growth in our VAS revenue.

To deliver on this plan, Vusion has several decisive competitive advantages: a strong presence in the most attractive markets through 2030 (the Americas and EMEA); a technology platform whose unrivaled performance and breadth of capabilities deliver substantial value to our customers, continuously enhanced through innovation; significant embedded growth potential within our existing customer base and an all-time-high pipeline of new retail customers; a technological lead that we intend to extend further through major product launches as soon as 2027; and, finally, the financial strength to support our ambitions.

Our business model is becoming increasingly predictable, recurring and profitable as we deliver ever greater value to our customers.

Vusion has been in business for over 30 years and has always embraced a long-term view. That vision extends well beyond 2030. By then, the digital transformation of physical retail will still be in its early stages, with many drivers of growth ahead, from greater adoption of our solutions to geographic expansion, particularly in Asia.

Vusion will continue its remarkable journey of pioneering innovation and sustained growth."

Vusion is building a portfolio of solutions that continuously deepens store digitalization, delivers more of its benefits to every retailer, and provides the essential capabilities needed to deploy artificial intelligence in stores.
