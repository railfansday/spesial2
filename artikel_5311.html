AppWork Partners with Make Ready Central to Cover the Cost of AppWork for Operators

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Make Ready Central will cover the AppWork subscription for operators that work with both companies, giving mutual clients a complete maintenance platform at no added cost.

RAMSEY, N.J., Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- AppWork, the maintenance operations platform built for multifamily property operators, today announced a partnership with Make Ready Central (MRC), a tech-enabled make-ready partner that manages unit turns from inspection to rent-ready. Through the partnership, MRC will cover 100% of AppWork platform fees for all mutual clients, with the rollout beginning in Texas.

MRC covers the full cost of the AppWork subscription for mutual clients that complete at least 80% of their Make Readies through MRC and pay MRC invoices within 60 days.

Mutual clients receive the same AppWork experience as every other client. Site teams can assign turn tasks to MRC directly from the Make Ready Board and follow each unit from notice to move-in ready in one place, while technicians continue to manage work orders and inspections in the AppWork Tech App.

Unit turns carry real cost, and every day a unit sits vacant is lost revenue. This partnership pairs a dedicated turn partner with the platform that tracks the work, so operators gain clear visibility into every Make Ready and faster turn time without adding a new line item to their budget.

"Slow turns are one of the most expensive problems an operator faces, and most of that delay comes from juggling and managing vendors. We built Make Ready Central to take that coordination off site teams' plates and deliver consistent turns in five business days. Partnering with AppWork gives our clients full visibility into every turn, and covering the cost of the platform makes that an easy decision." - Nick Schanbaum, Founder & CEO, Make Ready Central

"Make Readies are where a property gains or loses ground every month. We built AppWork so teams can see every turn clearly and keep units moving toward move-in. Partnering with Make Ready Central puts that visibility in the hands of every client we share, with the cost of the platform fully covered." - Sean Landsberg, CEO, AppWork

The rollout begins in Texas across Dallas, Houston, Austin, and San Antonio, with plans to expand into Georgia and Tennessee next. Property teams interested in learning more can contact Make Ready Central at [email protected] or visit appworkco.com.

AppWork is the multifamily maintenance platform built to simplify and elevate maintenance management. AppWork gives maintenance teams the tools to manage work orders, Make Readies, and inspections, recognizes their efforts, and gives operators real-time visibility into performance across their portfolios. Learn more at appworkco.com.

Make Ready Central is a tech-enabled make-ready partner that helps property operators turn units faster and more consistently. MRC coordinates every part of the turn through a single team of vetted subcontractors and tradespeople, including inspections,  scoping, painting, flooring, cleaning, resurfacing, and punch-list repairs. The company works with multifamily, student housing, manufactured housing, build-to-rent, and single-family rental operators and completes standard turns in about five business days. Learn more at makereadycentral.com.
