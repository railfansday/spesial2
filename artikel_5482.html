APPOINTMENT OF CHAIRMAN OF THE AUDIT COMMITTEE

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

JOHANNESBURG, Nov. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- In compliance with para 6.71 of the JSE Listings Requirements and para 6.42 of the JSE Debt and Specialist Securities Listings Requirements, Sasol is pleased to announce the appointment of Mr Stanley Subramoney as the Chairman of the Audit Committee and Sasol's financial expert with effect from 14 November 2026.

Mr Subramoney has been an independent non-executive director of Sasol Limited since 2021 and is a member of the Audit and Remuneration Committees. He is a chartered accountant with extensive experience in accounting, auditing and international business. During a 27-year career with PwC, he served as an audit and advisory partner and Deputy Chief Executive Officer of PwC Southern Africa. He gained significant international exposure through complex assignments and global organisational roles. He is currently Chief Executive Officer of Menston Holdings, a diversified investment company. He serves as an independent non-executive director of Nedbank Group, where he formerly chaired the Group Audit and Credit Committees.

The Chairman of the Sasol Board, Ms Muriel Dube, said: "Stanley has been a valued member of the Sasol Board and Audit Committee since 2021. His extensive accounting and audit expertise, together with his deep knowledge of Sasol gained during his tenure on the Board, position him well to lead the Audit Committee. The Board is confident that he will provide strong leadership to the Committee and continue to make a valuable contribution to Sasol."
