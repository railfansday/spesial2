Soluna Reports Q3'26 Results; Begins Construction Work at Kati 2 and Dorothy 3

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Revenue up 80% year over year; Signed a new non-binding LOI for 118 MW at Kati; Partnership with Applied Digital at Dorothy 3; Kati 1 delivered (83 MW); Generate Capital debt fully repaid

ALBANY, N.Y., Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Soluna Holdings, Inc. ("Soluna" or the "Company") (NASDAQ: SLNH), a developer of green data centers for intensive computing applications, including artificial intelligence and high performance computing, announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

"Our focus is converting the AI projects in our pipeline to signed leases," said John Belizaire, CEO of Soluna Holdings. "In recent months, we advanced development and initial construction at Kati 2 and Dorothy 3. We received ERCOT Base Load classifications for 266 megawatts across our two Texas campuses, and closed on the Dorothy 3 land. In October, we signed a subscription agreement with Applied Digital for a planned 50-megawatt AI data center at Dorothy 3. In November, we signed a non-binding letter of intent with a new prospective tenant for 118 MW at Kati 2. Our priorities now are securing definitive leases, arranging project financing and advancing both projects toward delivery. We are committed to developing these projects responsibly and working with local communities as they progress."

"Our third-quarter results reflect continued revenue growth, despite $3.0 million in unplanned turbine repair expenses and related downtime at the Briscoe Wind Farm. Completing the wind farm integration and associated turbine upgrades positions the asset for improved operational performance and supports our AI strategy at Dorothy 3," Belizaire continued. "We exited the quarter with $109 million in cash and, subsequent to quarter-end, fully repaid our Generate Capital debt, strengthening our balance sheet and enhancing financial flexibility as we advance our AI infrastructure development pipeline."
