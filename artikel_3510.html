Welcome to Plathville’s Ken Palmer Confirms He and Kim Plath Have Split

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Welcome to Plathville’s Ken Palmer has confirmed that he and Kim Plath are no longer an item.

Palmer posted a carousel of outdoorsy photos via his Instagram on Wednesday, November 25, announcing, “I’m officially a Mississippian again.”

The post prompted his followers to question whether the move meant that he and Plath had gone their separate ways.

Palmer confirmed the split in the comments section. When one commenter suggested that Ken walked away due to Kim’s weight — which he had previously described on the show as a “dealbreaker” if she didn’t shed pounds — he claimed that it wasn’t the only reason for the breakup.

“Weight, laziness, and hygiene issues all go hand in hand. Don’t judge me. She put on 130 pounds in 4 years,” he wrote. “I’ve forgiven her twice before for hooking up with other men too.”

After Ken posted a photo of himself with his cat via Instagram the following day, he also confirmed there was no argument before they called time on their relationship.

“So did you and Kim have a fight? You seemed so in love,” asked a follower. Ken replied, “Irreconcilable differences. No fight.”

For her part, Kim has not yet commented publicly on the split.

Kim had been dating Ken since 2022, following her split from ex-husband Barry Plath. The exes share nine children: Ethan, Hosanna, Micah, Moriah, Lydia, Isaac, Amber, Cassia and Mercy. Their 10th child, son Joshua, died in 2008 when he was 17 months old following a farm accident.

Kim and Ken have broken up and reconciled several times in the past.

In May 2026, their relationship issues were shown on an episode of Welcome to Plathville when Ken called out Kim for not putting in enough effort to lose weight.

“I want her to be around for a long time for me and for the kids,” Ken told the cameras in the May 19 episode of the TLC series. “But I feel like she just kind of put the getting healthy [plan] on the back burner.”

He added, “I’m not seeing any changes and it’s starting to get frustrating.”

In the episode, Kim opened up to Ken about the issues she’s having when it comes to her weight loss journey.

“It’s been a little bit daunting, you know, being in my 50s and trying to lose weight. I mean, it’s not as easy as it was in my 20s or even in my 30s,” Kim told Ken as they sat on a swing on their farm. “I’m trying to work out more and I don’t know. There’s days where it’s just really hard to work in something, you know?”

Ken was seemingly unconvinced that Kim was trying hard enough to take care of herself and admitted it was a sticking point for him.

“I really love Kim and I want to see her get healthier, but I think it could potentially be a deal breaker if she is not going to commit to her health,” Ken said in a confessional. “It makes me wonder, is she committed to me?”

Ken’s comments left Kim concerned. In a confessional, Kim said she felt pressured to lose weight or lose the relationship.

“I don’t really like that it’s a deal breaker for him. I mean, I feel like he’s giving me an ultimatum,” she said at the time. “I do worry about the future of our relationship when I hear him say things like that.”
