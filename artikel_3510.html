Candace Cameron Bure Addresses Distance From Mary-Kate and Ashley Olsen

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Candace Cameron Bure made a rare comment about the distance between the Full House cast and Mary-Kate and Ashley Olsen.

During the Tuesday, November 24, episode of “The McBride Rewind” podcast, Cameron Bure, 50, was asked about attempts to bring the Olsen twins, 40, back for Netflix’s Fuller House revival in 2016.

“There were many calls,” she recalled. “No, [I didn’t call] because it wasn’t my show to pitch. I think that was between [creator] Jeff [Franklin] and John [Stamos] because it was really their show.”

Cameron Bure referred to Mary-Kate and Ashley’s decision not to make an appearance as “a bummer,” adding, “Because it would have been fun to have everybody back. But at the same time, it wasn’t surprising just because none of us really have stayed close with the girls.”

She continued: “Not because anything went wrong or there was any bad blood. I just think they were kids. They were [filming from] 9 months old until they were 8 years old. They have very different memories than we do. Even me being 10 years older than them, I have significant, great, wonderful memories.”

Cameron Bure didn’t fault the former actresses for setting boundaries.

“They were babies. They probably don’t even remember half the stuff [we do]. They didn’t have the attachment to the crew and the people like we did being older,” she explained. “And they had such a successful fashion business. They haven’t been actors for a long time. They just haven’t been in that part of entertainment.”

Cameron Bure was still grateful with the final product, which aired until 2020, saying, “It would have been cool if they were there, but it wasn’t surprising, and everyone was kind of like, ‘We can still do the show without them. We’ll just have to figure out how to how to say something about why they’re not here, and we’ll figure it out and just move on.'”

Mary-Kate and Ashley became household names while sharing the role of Michelle Tanner on Full House from 1987 to 1995. After the ABC sitcom came to an end, the twins branched out with their own empire of movies, TV shows and brand deals.

As they got older, both Ashley and Mary-Kate chose to take a step back from the public eye. They stopped acting in the mid-2000s after starting their fashion label, The Row.

“We were raised to be discreet people,” Mary-Kate told i-D magazine in a 2021 profile, with Ashley noting that they “have each other to lean on.”

“We definitely go by intuition and instinct and it can either confirm that feeling, or if we’re both not feeling right about something, for some reason, we just don’t do it,” Ashley explained at the time. “Our instincts are kind of the same. But I think what’s great is that we have each other to lean on.”

In the past, the sisters denied having any plans to return to acting.

“We’ve been there, we’ve done that, we started out that way,” Mary-Kate told Vogue in 2019 about her and Ashley returning to their roots. “This is the way we chose to move forward in our lives: to not be in the spotlight, to really have something that speaks for itself.”

Ashley agreed, adding, “We didn’t want to be in front of it, we didn’t necessarily even want to let people know it was us. It was really about the product, to the point where we were like: Who could we get to front this so that we don’t have to?”

She continued: “We like working together and we like having that dialogue. I think it helps harden your ideas to be able to hear them out loud, to speak something through.”
