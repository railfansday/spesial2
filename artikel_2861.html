Steelers feel ‘unprepared’ and locker room reportedly in disarray ahead of matchup with Broncos

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Earlier in the week, the NFL landscape was shaken up when the Pittsburgh Steelers acknowledged late at night that quarterback Aaron Rodgers would be benched for today's matchup versus the Denver Broncos in favor of Mason Rudolph.

The next day, Rodgers went on the Pat McAfee show to give his side of the story. The future Hall of Fame quarterback shared various details that painted Head Coach Mike McCarthy in an unfavorable light. After that, the Steelers would release Rodgers.

Yesterday, former Steelers Head Coach Mike Tomlin called the team and situation a 'dumpster fire' during the Kansas City Chiefs and Buffalo Bills matchup on Thanksgiving night. Earlier this morning, ESPN Steelers team reporter Brooke Pryor gave more insight on the dilemma and it doesn't sound good.

According to Pryor, Pittsburgh's locker room is shell-shocked from the fallout. Via the report, players have dished out that they feel underprepared for this afternoon's matchup versus the Broncos. In just a few hours, we will get to see first-hand whether or not they are ready to play.

If not, the game could get quite ugly in a hurry. Nevertheless, the Broncos have to focus on themselves and take care of business on the road and not underestimate their opponent. Of note, Steelers are undefeated at home this season. Will that streak end today? We are fixing to find out.
