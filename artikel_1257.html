What Mancini told Seba Esposito after missed penalty against France and Pio’s goal

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

La Gazzetta dello Sport reports that Roberto Mancini didn’t hide his frustration at Sebastiano Esposito’s missed penalty against France and then joked with the Sassuolo striker after Pio’s goal: ‘Did you want to take this one too?’

Safe to say Italy coach Mancini was not happy to see Sebastiano Esposito’s penalty kick miss in a 3-1 win over France on Thursday.

The Azzurri CT already said in his post-match interviews that Sebastiano’s brother, Francesco Pio, was supposed to take the spot-kick.

Sebastiano was subbed off immediately after his error on the spot, and just a few seconds later, Italy took the lead on Davide Frattesi’s strike.

Sebastiano Esposito arrived on the bench right after Frattesi’s goal, but according to Gazzetta, Mancini was still upset: “Why did you take it? Pio was the penalty taker,” Mancini reportedly told Sebastiano Esposito.

The report claims the striker also spoke to Mancini’s assistants, Leonardo Bonucci and Gabriele Oriali, on the touchline, which helped de-escalate tensions.

A few minutes later, Antonio Vergara, who had replaced Seba Esposito, earned the Azzurri’s second penalty kick of the night, which Pio Esposito converted.

By then, Mancini looked more relaxed and turned to Sebastiano, sitting on the bench, and sarcastically asked: “Did you want to take this one too?”

Football Italia was among the accredited media at San Siro on Thursday and reported on what happened before Sebastiano Esposito’s penalty kick in our latest YouTube show, which you can watch below.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.

If Sebastiano broke rank and disobeyed orders from Mancini, then he should be reprimanded.

However, if Pio was petrified to take the spot-kick, that’s a different matter altogether. But that was not the case here, seeing as how Pio eventually scored from the spot and sealed the win.
