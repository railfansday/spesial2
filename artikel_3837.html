Fortnite lets you plunder free cosmetics from the item shop in new One Piece season, but only if you hit gold

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The real treasure were the friends we made along the way, but I only say this because there's no chance I'll find one of those.

Fortnite‘s new Treasure Hunters season brings One Piece‘s pirate themes to the game, and starting today, the luckiest players can find the shooter’s biggest treasure: two weeks of free cosmetics from the item shop. Just make sure you’re digging in the right spot.

The Treasure Hunters season launched on Nov. 1, with Epic calling it “The Greatest Adventure of All Time” (I’m guessing the devs forgot about Outer Wilds). As part of the festivities, battle royale players can find special treasure chests loaded with loot.

One of the possible rewards in the chest is a Pirate Shop Token, which grants you and your team two weeks of free cosmetics in the item shop. This offer applies to most items puchased with V-Bucks, but transactions with real money are off the table. These can include the One Piece skins that arrived in the game with the collab on Nov. 1, plus whatever’s on tap during that time period.

You can reportedly receive two different types of Pirate Shop Tokens: one lets you time your two weeks between November and Dec. 15 this year, while you can activate the other from Jan. 1 until the end of 2027.

Of course, this generous prize seems to have drastically low drop rates. You must first find the special treasure hunt chests, then luck out in getting the Pirate Shop Tokens from them. The announcement mentions “Mastery Mondays and Treasure Hours Sundays” throughout the season, which will include “boosted drops.” The post doesn’t officially specify if this affects Pirate Shop Tokens, however.

Fortnite is bringing the big guns this month, including a cosmetic-filled collab with One Piece. The items are spread out around the shop: Luffy, Nami, and Usopp anchored in the store alongside the season release, while Zoro, Sanji, and the Chopper Sidekick will arrive on Nov. 7.

With this being Fortnite, of course, there’s also a plethora of other skins up for grabs throughout the month—including A$AP Rocky, Lil Uzi Vert, Kasane Teto, and Cap’n Hatsune Miku. I’m not quite sure what the connecting thread is, but at least the One Piece skins seem cool.
