Earn Cashback on PS5 Games in Sony's Black Friday Sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Sony’s giving cashback if you purchase games included in its Black Friday PS Store promo this weekend.

The offer – available in the UK, although we’re still working to get confirmation in the US – will award you with £5 in PS Store credit if you purchase £50 of games from its latest sale.

The offer ends on 1st December, and the cashback will be credited to your account thereafter.

The cashback only applies to titles included in its 20 Years of PS Store Black Friday promotion, although there’s over 5,000 to choose from, so it shouldn’t be too difficult to find a few you like.

However, it means you can’t just buy GTA 6 and get the cashback – it has to be something in the aforementioned sale.
