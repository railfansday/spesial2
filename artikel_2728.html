C.J. Stroud: "There's still a lot of ball to be played"

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

With Sunday’s loss to the Browns, the Texans have fallen to 3-6 on the season.

At this point, Houston will have to rip off several wins in a row to have a chance at the postseason.

Quarterback C.J. Stroud is remaining optimistic.

“There’s still a lot of ball to be played, if I can remember I think we’ll be fine,” Stroud said on Tuesday, via transcript from the team. “It’s not perfection that is what’s needed, but it’s a chase of excellence from everybody. Me and [ Will Anderson Jr.], we try to set the standard every day for everybody in the building to look at us and be like, ‘Hey, those are two guys that I want to follow after,’ and it’s up to us to keep that standard going.

“It doesn’t mean because you lose a couple games that everything got to switch or change. There’s a lot of ball to be played left, so we’re still excited about what’s coming next.”

So Stroud remains motivated and wants that attitude to permeate throughout the team.

“What else are we going to do? Cry about it and hold our heads down? Woe is us, woe is this team? I don’t believe in that,” Stroud said. “It sucks, yeah, I’m not the happiest camper, but I think the motivated me is one of the best me’s that I’ve seen.

“I think even as this team, the motivated Texans is a scary team. It’s up to us. We don’t need anybody else. That’s my message to the guys. We don’t need anybody else to have faith in us or to think that we can do this or can’t do this. It doesn’t really matter. We’re the ones on the field making plays and we have the opportunity. Everybody can have an opinion, but when you have the opportunity, you’ve got to go and do it and I think it just starts one thing at a time.”
