Hudson vs. St. Ursula high school girls soccer state semifinal score, live updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The chase to win two more games begins Nov. 11 for the girls soccer teams of Hudson (20-0) and Cincinnati St. Ursula Academy (13-5-3) in a Division II state semifinal.

The winner plays Bishop Watterson (17-1-2) or Avon (15-3-2) at 7 p.m. Nov. 14 in Crew Stadium for a state championship.

High School Sports Live on NFHS Network all year

The game will be streamed on the NFHS Network.

This is the 10th state semifinal appearance for Hudson and the second time in a row it has been in the final four. The Explorers won a state title in 2000 and were runners-up in 2025, 2001, 1999 and 1995.

This is the 10th state semifinal appearance for St. Ursula and the first since 2008. The Bulldogs won state titles in 2008, 2007, 1993 and 1991. They were second in 2005 and 2002.

The Explorers beat Painesville Riverside 14-0 and Hoover 8-0 to win the Northeast 7 district and then topped North Ridgeville 3-0 and Highland 3-0 to win Region 7. Hudson is one of only three teams (Seton and West Branch) that is unbeaten and untied this season.

The Bulldogs beat Riverside Stebbins 10-0 and Loveland 3-0 to win the Southwest 2 district and then topped Harrison 3-2 and Cincinnati Anderson 2-1 to win Region 6.

Montana commit Alyssa Mainar (30 goals, five assists) and Hannah Meese (22 goals, 19 assists) lead Hudson this season with Elliana Durachinsky (11 goals, eight assists) adding valuable support. Addie Frank, along with Hannah Gorman, have helped anchor a Hudson defense that hasn't conceded a goal in seven straight games (Oct. 5, Hudson 3, Walsh Jesuit 2). The duo has recorded 14 shutouts this season while allowing just 0.5 goals per game.

Eva Lackmeyer, Holly Stagnaro, Maggie Ruehl, Kate Sullivan, Matilda Miller and Gigi Vollmer are the players to watch for St. Ursula. The Bulldogs are on a five-game winning streak and have won seven of their last eight.
