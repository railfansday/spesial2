Denver plays Toronto, aims for 5th straight road win

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Denver Nuggets (11-6, seventh in the Western Conference) vs. Toronto Raptors (7-11, ninth in the Eastern Conference)

BOTTOM LINE: Denver will try to keep its four-game road win streak intact when the Nuggets take on Toronto.

The Raptors have gone 3-7 in home games. Toronto is 3-8 against opponents with a winning record.

The Nuggets are 7-2 on the road. Denver averages 116.2 points and has outscored opponents by 1.2 points per game.

The Raptors average 112.2 points per game, 2.8 fewer points than the 115.0 the Nuggets allow. The Nuggets average 11.0 made 3-pointers per game this season, 1.6 fewer made shots on average than the 12.6 per game the Raptors allow.

The teams meet for the second time this season. The Nuggets won 128-109 in the last meeting on Nov. 5. Nikola Jokic led the Nuggets with 32 points, and RJ Barrett led the Raptors with 22 points.

TOP PERFORMERS: Scottie Barnes is averaging 25.5 points, 6.5 rebounds, five assists and 1.7 steals for the Raptors. Immanuel Quickley is averaging 14.7 points and 5.8 assists over the last 10 games.

Christian Braun is shooting 50.8% and averaging 14.2 points for the Nuggets. Jamal Murray is averaging 2.6 made 3-pointers over the last 10 games.

Nuggets: 7-3, averaging 116.8 points, 42.1 rebounds, 27.6 assists, 7.2 steals and 5.0 blocks per game while shooting 48.0% from the field. Their opponents have averaged 115.2 points.
