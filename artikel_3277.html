‘Grendel’ Animated Series Based On Dark Horse Comic Greenlighted At Netflix

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: Matt Wagner‘s signature comic book character Hunter Rose is coming to the screen, this time for real. Netflix has ordered Grendel, an adult animated action series based on Wagner’s long-running comic book series published by Dark Horse.

Bobak Esfarjani (Widow’s Bay, WandaVision, Alien: Earth) is writer, executive producer and showrunner on the TV adaptation. In it, after the woman he loves is shockingly murdered, Hunter Rose devotes himself to one thing: vengeance. But as he tears through street gangs, the elite, and New York City’s supernatural underworld in pursuit of her killer, a chance at a real life forces him to choose: walk away from ultimate power, or become the very monster he hunts. Esfarjani executive produces with Chris Tongue, Tim Wiesch and Jay Komas for Dark Horse Entertainment (The Umbrella Academy), Lydia Antonini (Locke and Key, Halo: Forward Unto Dawn); and Wagner (Dead Boy Detectives). Bobbypills (Creature Commandos, Captain Laserhawk) is the studio. Watch on Deadline

“It’s incredibly exciting to see my creation finally make this leap to the animated screen! Netflix’s dynamic new series retains the source material’s dark adult themes and also brings a fresh and thrilling approach to the narrative,” Wagner said. “This is Grendel as I’ve always wanted to see the character portrayed — a hyper-kinetic level of action, bracing explosions of violence and a poignant engaging storyline.”

Netflix previously ordered a live-action Grendel series in 2021 as part of the streamer’s first-look deal with Dark Horse Entertainment, with Andrew Dabb as writer/executive producer, Abubakr Ali in the titular role and Jaime Ray Newman and Erik Palladino among the rest of the cast. A year later, Netflix opted not to move forward with the series.

The streamer remained high on the IP, and by early 2026, it had put in development an animated series adaptation, also under the first-look deal with Dark Horse, commissioning a writers room several months later.

“Grendel has been at the forefront of independent comics for over forty years, and we’re thrilled to bring Matt Wagner’s creation to Netflix,” said John Derderian, Netflix’s VP, Animation Series + Kids & Family TV. “Like the comic, this animated series will break the traditional superhero mold just as Matt’s time-honored comic book has always done. Bobak, Dark Horse Entertainment and Bobbypills are bold storytellers who love this world as much as its fans do, and we’re so excited to partner with them to bring this story to screen.”

Originally published in 1982 by Comico, Grendel was moved to Dark Horse following the indie label’s bankruptcy by Wagner who owned the character. Wagner is also known for creating the Mage comic book series and his work for DC Comics.

Esfarjani most recently served as a consulting producer on Season 1 of Apple TV’s Widow’s Bay, sharing in its Emmy win for Outstanding Comedy Series.

“Being able to bring a character as iconic as Grendel to life has been a true thrill,” he said. “Having the support of Matt Wagner, my great EPs, and the awesome team of Netflix, has made it all the more exciting, and partnering with Bobbypills Studio has made it all a true joy.”

Grendel joins Netflix’s slate of current adult animated series, which includes Blue Eye Samurai, Long Story Short, Haunted Hotel, Devil May Cry and Mating Season.
