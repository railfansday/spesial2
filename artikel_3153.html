Newsmax (NMAX) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Affiliate growth and AI licensing deals fueled results, with a one-time fee adding a notable lift.

Newsmax Inc. (NMAX -5.81%) reported record quarterly revenue driven by structural growth in affiliate fees and international licensing, alongside a $14.0 million nonrecurring fee from an advertising agreement amendment. Management stated that the company is shifting its focus toward high-margin digital streams and AI content partnerships while reducing investment in legacy print and newsletter products. The company reported that the midterm election cycle serves as a precursor to the 2028 presidential cycle, which is expected to drive higher national advertising and audience engagement beginning in 2027.
