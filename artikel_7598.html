District Election Officers Support Election Integrity and Voter Protection On Election Day

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Baltimore, Maryland – Kelly O. Hayes, U.S. Attorney for the District of Maryland, announced, today, that Assistant U.S. Attorneys Philip Motsay and Joseph Wenner are leading the District’s efforts to implement the Department of Justice’s nationwide Election Day Program for the upcoming general election.
