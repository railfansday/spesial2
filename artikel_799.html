Busta Rhymes Toasts to His Pair of 2027 Grammy Award Nominations: ‘I’m in My Crib Running Around Right Now, Drinking Grey Goose’

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Dillagence II noms are Busta's first since 2012 for his legendary assist on Chris Brown's "Look at Me Now."

Busta Rhymes earned his first Grammy nomination in 1997 — and 30 years later, the legendary New York rapper will be back at the award show in a full-circle moment for Bus-a-Bus.

Busta picked up a pair of 2027 Grammy nominations (now with 14 noms in all) for his polished DILLAGENCE II album with the late superproducer J Dilla, which marks his first since earning a 2012 nod for his iconic assist on Chris Brown and Lil Wayne’s “Look at Me Now” — and you’d have to stretch back to 2007 for Busta’s last nomination that came on one of his own projects, with the top 20 Billboard Hot 100 hit “Touch It.”

“I’m on some f–king wild-out s–t right now. I’m in my crib running around right now, drinking Grey Goose in the morning,” Busta tells Billboard on a Monday morning (Nov. 16) call, as he hasn’t gone to sleep yet from the night before. “This is a toast to me, my family, my queen, my kids. I ain’t even gonna lie, like I’m so happy. I’m so grateful. I can’t even explain it, bro.”

The 54-year-old released his DILLAGENCE II album, a sequel to the 2007 mixtape, in August alongside Dilla, with the cinematic project made up of the 300 some odd beats Busta got from the Detroit producer prior to his 2006 passing due to a cardiac arrest, which was tied to complications from lupus and thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura (TTP).

“It’s a whole other feeling. It’s a whole other experience,” he adds. “It’s a whole other level of celebration. Whole other level of appreciation. And I’m just thankful, man. God is the greatest, bro. What matters to me so much is that the Academy acknowledged the fact that this body of work was super-special, and it warranted the acknowledgement that was way deeper than just music.”

With DILLAGENCE II, Busta is represented in the best rap album category for the first time since 2000, when he was nominated for E.L.E. (Extinction Level Event – The Final World Front) and is seeking the first overall victory at the Grammys.

Dilla also posthumously picked up the first nomination of his career, while the 2027 ceremony fittingly will air on what would have been his 53rd birthday (Feb. 7), which Busta calls “a divine alignment.”

A second nomination came from DILLAGENCE II standout “This Woman,” a woozy slow jam featuring an appearance from late R&B dignitary D’Angelo, who passed in October 2025 — and who, like Dilla, has history with Busta dating back to the ’90s. The track picked up a nomination in the best melodic rap performance category.

Busta Rhymes praised the Dilla and D’Angelo estates for trusting him to tastefully deliver on his vision and honor the legacy of both music luminaries. “It’s nothing sweeter as the joy to be able to get my first nomination for my own work in 20-plus years than to have it with them,” he states.

The innovative rapper makes it a point before we hang up to list every person who played a role in DILLAGENCE II, from the collaborators on the music side to his management, record label executives, lawyers, along with his family and more.

“You cannot script this. You can’t make this up. No man can make this calendar work like this,” Bus concludes. “This is not normal.”
