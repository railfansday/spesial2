8 real estate brokerage moves and tech launches to know this week

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

A weekly roundup covering brokerage expansion, affiliation, and agent team moves, plus proptech and embedded finance product launches. Items include Realty of America’s San Diego entry, eXp’s team addition and new AI, compliance and CRM tools.  AI Summary

Realty of America (ROA), a RealTrends Verified top brokerage, is expanding into San Diego with Zandra Ulloa and Team Z Realty, marking the company’s entry into one of Southern California’s key residential markets. Led by Ulloa, Team Z brings more than a decade of experience and a strong track record of performance in the region as Realty of America continues to expand its national footprint.

Ulloa joins the brokerage alongside 12 Team Z agent partners, bringing Realty of America’s agent-owned model to the San Diego market. Since its founding in September 2024, Realty of America has grown to more than 3,700 agents across 26 markets, establishing a national platform designed to give agents the tools, resources and ownership opportunities to grow their businesses.

Baird & Warner, a RealTrends Verified brokerage, is expanding its Oak Park, Ill. presence with the addition of Beyond Properties, a boutique brokerage whose dozen agents will join the firm as Beyond Properties Group. Led by owner and designated managing broker Erica Cuneen, the group will join Baird & Warner’s Oak Park office while retaining its existing physical location on Marion Street. Beyond Properties Group represents buyers and sellers in Oak Park, the surrounding suburbs and in the city of Chicago.

RealTrends Verified brokerage eXp Realty, the core subsidiary of AGNT, Inc., announced that Jon Bowling and RealTrends Verified Jon Bowling’s Team have joined eXp Realty after 20 years with REMAX.

Based in Mason, Ohio, the team includes 11 licensed agents and six support staff. It serves buyers and sellers across greater Cincinnati and closed approximately $90 million in sales volume in 2025.

Recruiting Insight launches HiringCenter, with done-for-you recruiting campaigns, and an upgraded Core Capacities Index that gives brokers a 90-day coaching plan for every new agent. For two years, Recruiting Insight’s quarterly Agent Migration reports have shown brokers where agents move, why they leave and who is likely to go next. Today the company is launching two tools that turn that research into action: HiringCenter, a recruiting CRM built exclusively for real estate brokerages, and an upgraded Core Capacities Index™ (CCI), an agent assessment that shows brokers how a candidate likes to work and how to support them once they join.

Paradigm Realty, a family-owned, boutique brokerage headquartered in Fenton, Mo., is the latest company to affiliate with the Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate brand.

Broker/owner David Fendler began his real estate career at the brokerage in 2003, where he developed an extensive background in new construction and worked as one of the business’s top producers. In 2008, he purchased the company and rebranded it, and it now does business as Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate Paradigm Living.

Shortly after purchasing the company, David was joined by his wife and current co-owner, Elizabeth “Liz” Fendler. Liz is an MBA recipient and home staging specialist for the firm’s largest real estate team, where she has built a reputation for her strong mentorship and client service skills over the past 18 years.

Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices EWM Realty recently unveiled its newly redesigned website in partnership with RELIANCEai, a real estate technology and agentic AI provider.

A member of the RealTrends Verified HomeServices of America family since 2003 and rebranded as Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices EWM Realty in 2019, the company has successfully facilitated thousands of multi-million-dollar transactions and earned recognition as a four-time “Best Brokerage” award winner from South Florida’s real estate industry leaders.

RealTrends Verified brokerage Equity Union Real Estate announced the launch of a new healthcare program designed to expand health insurance options for eligible affiliated real estate agents and their families. Offered through a partnership with Trident BPO, the initiative gives participating agents access to healthcare plans featuring a nationwide PPO network and coverage options for themselves, their spouses and their dependents.

Rechat, the operating system and super app for real estate brokers, teams and agents, today announced Rechat Text, which syncs an agent’s texts and calls to their CRM, placing every client conversation on the contact’s profile. Rechat Text is available starting today in Beta for Rechat customers.

Built for client communication, not mass marketing, Rechat Text integrates phone call history and text messaging directly into Rechat, allowing brokers and agents to text clients right from a contact’s profile. One-on-one text threads and call activity, complete with timestamps and call duration, sync automatically to the contact’s timeline alongside existing deals, email and marketing history.
