Leading Sage Intacct Consulting Practice to Combine with EisnerAmper

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

NEW YORK, Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Global business advisory firm EisnerAmper announces that the professionals from the Sage Intacct consulting practice at RSM US LLP are joining EisnerAmper in a carve-out combination expected to close in early 2027.

This award-winning consulting team helps growing and mid-sized organizations implement and optimize Sage Intacct, an artificial intelligence-powered, cloud-based ERP platform focused on financial management and accounting solutions. Its services include implementation, ongoing optimization, customized workflows, and interactive reporting. The team serves nonprofit, real estate, healthcare, financial services, professional services, and other sectors.

The team's 35-plus professionals will join EisnerAmper's growing Technology Solutions practice.

"This expanded partnership with EisnerAmper brings together deep industry knowledge and experience to help organizations solve real challenges and grow," said Senior Vice President of Partner Sales, US, Sage, Nancy Sperry. "Bringing this talented team into EisnerAmper creates even more opportunity to support our mutual customers and help them obtain greater value from Sage Intacct."

"Joining together with EisnerAmper's Sage Intacct practice will enable us to continue to grow, scale, and deliver exceptional client experiences," said Principal and leader of the Sage Intacct practice at RSM, John Szuberla. "The synergy between our two consulting practices will yield tremendous benefits for our clients."

EisnerAmper, one of the largest business consulting firms in the world, is comprised of EisnerAmper LLP, a licensed independent CPA firm that provides client attest services; and Eisner Advisory Group LLC, an alternative practice structure that provides business advisory and non-attest services in accordance with all applicable laws, regulations, standards, and codes of conduct. Clients span all sectors and leverage a complete menu of services. Our combined entities include approximately 475 partners and 5,000 employees.
