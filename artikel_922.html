Brown reveals Djokovic advice that will help McLaren after 'underperformance'

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Listen to Sky Sports' full length interview with McLaren chief Zak Brown on the latest episode of The F1 Show; watch the Las Vegas GP this weekend on Sky Sports F1, with qualifying at 4am on Saturday and the race at 4am on Sunday

McLaren chief executive Zak Brown says he is using advice from Novak Djokovic to aid his team's attempt to bounce back after losing both of their Formula 1 titles to Mercedes this season.

Lando Norris was replaced as world champion by Kimi Antonelli at the Mexico City Grand Prix earlier this month before Mercedes sealed a dominant Constructors' Championship triumph a week later in Brazil.

McLaren won their first constructors' title in 26 years in 2024 before repeating the feat the following year, but a major change in the sport's regulations reset the pecking order ahead of the 2026 season.

Asked during a special episode of Sky Sports' The F1 Show about watching Mercedes celebrate on the podium after Norris had claimed something of a consolation victory for McLaren at the Sao Paulo Grand Prix, Brown jokingly replied: "I just want to push them off and get our team back up there!

"I mean, first of all, congratulations to Mercedes and Kimi. Now I have an appreciation for how hard this is.

"And the sport is so competitive and the top four teams, I mean, to beat a Red Bull, beat a Mercedes, beat a Ferrari, you've got to be damn good.

"It just makes me want it more. We won 14 races last year. I don't think anyone's ever won 14, two in a row.

"We've won three this year. Madrid, coulda, shoulda, woulda. So it's been a year that I feel like we've underperformed on the whole, but that's a learning. It makes us stronger.

"I think Brazil was awesome. That was McLaren at its best. The upgrades were great, the strategy, the drivers.

Brown then explained how tennis icon Djokovic, who has won a record 24 men's Grand Slam singles titles, previously gave him some advice that he and McLaren team principal Andrea Stella have taken on board as they prepare for the 2027 season.

"It's sport, and the big thing is, Novak Djokovic is a good friend of ours, and he's quite inspiring," Brown said. "When people say things to me, they stick with me, certain things.

"And he said, 'you can't control your emotions, how you feel is how you feel, but you can control how you react to them.'

"So, the emotions of not winning, as Andrea says, no poison biscuits, but you kind of sit back, take a deep breath, and you go, you know what, 'that's what happened, now let's go push forward and win.' And it makes winning feel so good."

McLaren's relative struggles this season, during which Norris has still managed three wins with two races to go, were contributed to by the team pushing until the end of 2025 while others switched more focus to working on their 2026 cars.

There ended up being fundamental issues with the MCL40, such as a shorter wheelbase than some of their rivals, that made it difficult for them to consistently compete for wins.

Asked whether this was something that will be addressed on next year's challenger, Brown said: "Yeah, always addressing the race car.

"Weight was a big issue this year for a lot of cars, obviously a shorter wheelbase (results in a) lighter car.

"So yeah, I think there's things that, slow speed corners weren't great for us.

"We've learned a lot about the power unit this year, and deployment, and all the stuff that, we're all still learning.

"So yeah, I think this season, there's a lot of takeaways and changes we're going to make going into 2027."

Norris' superb victory at Interlagos saw the Brit continue his dominance over his team-mate Oscar Piastri this season.

After the pair were extremely closely matched last season as Norris edged out Red Bull's Max Verstappen and Piastri for the title, there has been a clear gap between them in 2026.

Norris is 94 points ahead of Piastri in the drivers' standings, has all three of McLaren's grand prix victories this season, and six podiums to Piastri's two.

There have been similarly surprising shifts in performance from last year to this year at other teams, with Antonelli outpacing George Russell at Mercedes and Lewis Hamilton proving far more competitive against his Ferrari team-mate Charles Leclerc in the new cars.

Brown reflected: "I think we've seen it mix up a little bit of the grid, right? Charles last year got the better of Lewis. Lewis is getting the better of him. George got the better of Kimi, so I think it's mixed up the grid a little bit.

"I think if you look at Oscar's strengths, there were kind of big, brave corners. Now all of a sudden these new big, brave corners, it's like, 'hey lift, conserve, preserve.' So, I think there's a small element of him having to adapt to a new car.

"And then let's not take any credit away from Lando. I've never seen him drive better.

"So, I think you have a combination of Lando who's driving the best I've ever seen him drive. Oscar who's just not yet clicked with it. There's your gap, but it's not a big gap."
