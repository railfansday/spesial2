New Bachelor Sam Hartman Admits He's Never Watched the Show

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

NFL quarterback Sam Hartman has a lot to learn before starting his Bachelor journey.

“I haven’t seen an episode,” the 27-year-old athlete admitted to reporters on Thursday, November 19. “So if you guys have any tips, I would love to hear your thoughts on the dos and do nots.”

Hartman added, “I’m gonna probably do some tape watching after the season’s over and study up.”

The Washington Commanders player was announced as the season 30 Bachelor earlier this month and has maintained that he’ll be able to balance his football responsibilities with filming reality TV.

“[It’s an] exciting opportunity,” he said on Thursday, “but with all that being said, [I’m] super focused on football, super excited to still be doing the thing I love and also have the opportunity at the end of the season to try and find the person I love.”

When asked about his outlook for finding The One, Hartman hinted he’s optimistic. “To go on there with any other reason wouldn’t be fair to the TV show, the girls … so super excited, but again, it’s football until the season ends and then I can focus on my offseason,” he said.

Hartman further joked that he’s getting some “really bad advice” from his teammates, but overall, they’ve been supportive — and they even kept his casting secret before the official reveal. He also suggested the offer came “out of the blue.”

“I talked to everybody [within the team] that needed to be talked to and they were very supportive, and that was much appreciated by me,” Hartman said, confirming he’ll kick off his reality TV journey once the NFL season ends.

While he’s looking forward to finding his new “teammate,” Hartman declared football will always be his “first love.”

Hartman previously opened up about how he plans to navigate his new schedule, noting in a TikTok video shared Tuesday, November 17, that he was “really excited to get back to work.”

“I gotta get back to the day job running around throwing the football. All the focus is there,” he said. “But once the season wraps, can’t wait to join. Can’t wait to meet all the beautiful women. Can’t wait to go on this journey with you guys. I’m super excited.”

He added, “Bachelor Nation, I just wanted to give a huge shout-out to you guys. I appreciate all the support. Appreciate all the love, and especially the jokes. … Thank you for all the support and kind words.”

The Bachelor season 30 premieres in March 2027.
