Thomas Markle Competent to Stand Trial in Child Porn Case After Evaluation

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Meghan Markle’s estranged father, Thomas Markle Sr., was found competent to stand trial amid his ongoing child pornography case.

Page Six reported on Thursday, November 19, that an independent competency evaluation by a psychiatrist determined that Thomas Sr., 82, has “sufficient ability to understand the nature and consequences of the charges against him” with a reasonable degree of medical certainty.

Federal Judge Lindsay Griffin reportedly said the update was being accepted into evidence, noting that it was uncontested. Thomas Sr.’s preliminary hearing in Florida is set for December 1.

Meghan and her father have been estranged since 2018, the same year she married Prince Harry. Meghan’s half-brother, Thomas Markle Jr., and Thomas Sr. relocated to Cebu Island in 2025 before moving to the U.S. earlier this year. Prior to returning in May, Thomas Sr. had his left leg amputated due to a blood clot. Thomas Jr. was reportedly his father’s primary caregiver since he suffered a stroke in 2022. Thomas Sr. is now reportedly living in a nursing home in Florida.

News broke in October that Thomas Jr. had found storage boxes containing suspected child sexual abuse material (CSAM) at Thomas Sr.’s home in the Philippines. At the time, Thomas Jr. told The Sun that he turned over the material to local authorities.

Thomas Sr. denied the allegations. He was arrested in Florida earlier this month on a federal charge of possession of child pornography. Thomas Sr. was released on bond the same day.

Investigators allegedly found material depicting child sexual abuse in two storage units registered to Thomas Sr. in Los Angeles, according to the Central District of California U.S. Attorney’s Office. The material reportedly contained photographs of a man believed to be Thomas Sr. with a young nude girl.

“The FBI and our partners have arrested Thomas Markle Sr. after an FBI investigation revealed he was in possession of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM),” FBI Director Kash Patel shared in a statement.

Patel, 46, continued, “Mr. Markle allegedly targeted innocent children and sought to steal their innocence through his criminal actions. Working with our Justice Department partners, our agents moved quickly, identified and located the explicit material, and arrested Mr. Markle in Florida. This FBI will use every single resource at our disposal to protect innocent kids — any predator who targets children will have zero safe haven in this country or anywhere they try to hide.”

Thomas Sr. was arrested earlier this month after he cut off his ankle monitor. He is also now facing charges in the Philippines with two counts of offering, distributing, selling, exporting or importing any form of child sexual abuse or exploitation material and one count of possessing such material, according to ITV.

Meghan shared in a statement last month that she learned about the allegations against Thomas Sr. in 2018.

“I hope it can now be understood that, despite certain media publications’ distortion over the years, any distance from my father and my choice to not allow him to have contact with our children was for one reason: to protect them,” Meghan said in a statement, in part. “I trusted that the authorities were doing the same for other families.”
