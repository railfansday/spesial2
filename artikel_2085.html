Daniel Farke at loggerheads with Leeds board over Jean-Matteo Bahoya - Transfer expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Daniel Farke is facing a disagreement with the Leeds United board over the summer signing of Jean-Matteo Bahoya.

The 21-year-old arrived on loan from Eintracht Frankfurt in a deal which included a conditional obligation which could see him make the permanent switch for £25.7million.

However, he has played just 11 minutes of football across all competitions since making the move to Elland Road, and has yet to find his place in Farke's first-team plans.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Farke and the Leeds board "clearly" have a "disconnect" and are at odds over Bahoya's future.

This comes after Farke's press conference outburst in which he questioned the "hype" surrounding the winger and insisted he has not done enough to be involved in the starting XI.

That has led to questions about Bahoya's long-term position at Leeds and whether the deal could be made permanent, with the boss still yet to be won over by his displays.

Brown warned Football Insider back in September that Farke has major doubts about Bahoya and has not seen enough in training to convince him he deserves to play.

Meanwhile, the winger's lack of minutes since joining on loan have now led to suggestions of a disconnect between Farke and the recruitment team at Elland Road.

Bahoya's move to Leeds, so far at least, has been a disappointment and questions are now being asked about who is truly to blame for his lack of involvement this season.

"It was strange for Daniel Farke to come out with everything he said about Jean-Matteo Bahoya," Brown told Football Insider.

"If he's got a player there and he can't answer questions about why he's not playing, why is he there? If he's not going to be playing and the manager doesn't rate him, why bring him in?

"You assume Leeds have brought him in because they've seen some potential in him, but if he's playing three minutes here and there, he's never going to show that.

"It seems to me there is some sort of disconnect from the manager and the board, they obviously disagree, and that's never a good thing when it comes to signing players.

"Ultimately, it's the recruitment team's job to sign players who will help the manager, but now you've got disagreements between them

"The board think this is a player who can make a difference, he has potential and he can be a good asset, but the manager doesn't think he's good enough to play.

"Is everybody pulling in the same direction there? I wouldn't be sure any more because these are basic things, there is clearly some sort of disconnect or misunderstanding."

Doubts about Bahoya's involvement in the side under Farke have also led to questions about his long-term role in the side, and the potential of a permanent move.

The obligation to buy in his loan deal is believed to be appearance-based, and given his lack of minutes so far, it is far from a certainty that he will trigger that clause.

However, Football Insider revealed on 2 November that Bahoya is still part of Farke's plans for the immediate future and must work his way into the starting XI.

After the manager threw down the gauntlet, it will be up to the winger to earn his place, although there are doubts given the competition for places in his position.

There are also concerns that Bahoya is not a natural fit for any clear role in the side as he is not trusted at wing-back and is also not suited to playing an inverted attacking role.
