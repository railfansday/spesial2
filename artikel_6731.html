Merit Makes Some of the Best Beauty Products Out There—Cosmo Editors Ranked All 19 of Them in Honor of Their Black Friday Sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

It’s the brand’s only sale of the year, and they’re offering up to 44% off everything.

Every time Merit Beauty has a new launch, the Cosmo beauty team silently fights over who gets to go to the event and hear about it first. It’s one of the only brands out there that we all equally agree is one of the best. It launched in 2021 (yup, mid-pandemic) with the goal of simplifying your makeup and skincare routines, all while offering up high-performing, clean formulas.

The brand has won multiple Cosmo beauty awards, from its hero perfume being crowned best fresh scent in our most recent Holy Grail Beauty Awards to a cleanser that earned a top spot in our 2025 Acne Awards. And if you didn’t know what to try first (or next, if you’re already a brand fan), we ranked and reviewed every single one of their products.

FYI: Merit is currently running its biggest sale of this year, offering up to 44% off every single product and gift set on their site. Their best-selling cream blush, the brand-new perfume going viral, or a hidden gem (ahem, the Solo Shadow)—all of it’s on sale right now. If you wanted to try something out or stock up on your favorites, now is the time. We won’t see deals like this again likely until next year, since Black Friday is the only sale Merit ever runs. Shop now through Monday, November 30.

OUR REVIEW: "You know when you finally find the perfect (but slightly expensive) white T-shirt, and you’re like: “holy shit this is worth every dollar—I’ll wear this all the time and a million different ways?” That’s Retrospect. It’s indulgent, but good for everyday, and the clean, skin-like scent easy to wear, whether you’re a perfume novice or full-on nose."—LAUREN BALSAMO, BEAUTY DIRECTOR

ANOTHER REVIEW: “I love how sophisticated I smell when I spritz this on. It’s playful, but still grown-up. When I don’t know what perfume to wear—which happens often—I just spray this on, and I never regret my decision.”—BETH GILLETTE, BEAUTY EDITOR

OUR REVIEW: "I'm a blush girl through and through, and Merit's Flush Balm is my desert island makeup product. It feels super lightweight and blends out so nicely, leaving my cheeks with a glowy finish that still looks natural on my skin."—JASMINE HYMAN, ASSISTANT BEAUTY EDITOR

OUR REVIEW: “Clean Volume is the perfect name for this surprisingly un-clumpy volumizing mascara. The formula coats every lash, making my eyelashes look thicker and more fanned out, but it doesn't give them a spidery or clumped-together look at all. The wand really helps separate every lash, and it even helps hold a curl for quite a few hours.”—BETH GILLETTE, BEAUTY EDITOR

OUR REVIEW: “Merit’s lipsticks remind me of the formulas my mom used when I was a kid. It’s got a satin finish that’s not glossy but not matte either. It’s soft and creamy, but not too buttery or balmy. And the shade range is reminiscent of the ‘90s, with a mix of neutrals and browns and rusty reds that look good on anyone.”—BG

OUR REVIEW: "I have super sensitive eyes, and this gentle-yet-lengthening mascara is one of the few that never irritates them. The brush itself is so soft on my lashes, and the formula stays put for hours, coming off easily with some micellar water at the end of the day."—JASMINE HYMAN, ASSISTANT BEAUTY EDITOR

OUR REVIEW: “This foolproof shadow elevates any eye look. It comes in a bunch of neutral shades that have a subtle sheen. And it couldn’t be easier to use—I just swipe the cream formula on with my fingers and then quickly blend.” —JH

OUR REVIEW: “Most nights I’m too lazy to do a full double cleanse, which is why I love this face wash so much. It thoroughly removes makeup, cleanses skin, and gently exfoliates in one step. The light, foamy texture feels delicious on skin, and it rinses totally clean, too.”—LB

OUR REVIEW: "This tinted sunscreen has replaced all of my skin tints and tinted moisturizers. It’s the perfect amount of soft, sheer coverage (I just pair it with some concealer to hide zits or acne scars) with a slight dewy finish."—BG

OUR REVIEW: "I have about 50 lip liners at home, but these never make it into my pile since they live in my bag. I love how easy I can apply this sheer lip liner. I don't even really need a mirror, TBH. They glide on and smudge for a soft look that still adds dimension. They also come in a mix of the chicest browns, which totally makes sense for Merit."—BG

OUR REVIEW: “I’ve been using this since Merit first launched as a brand, and it’s one of my go-to makeup products, period. It’s not a foundation, not a concealer, but a secret, magical third thing. I can use it all over my face and blend with a brush, or just dab it on a few spots as a concealer. The formula is creamy and blendable, but provides a pretty great medium-level coverage. It’s so easy to travel with that I keep a tube in my travel bag at all times.”—BG

OUR REVIEW: “A trendier altnerative to the standard lipstick formula from Merit, the Lip Blush imparts a little less color but with a velvety, smooth finish. You can build it up for a little bit more pigment, but it’s best when dabbed on and blended out with hazy edges.”—BG

OUR REVIEW: “If you’re looking for a full glam contour stick, this is not the one. However, it’s perfect if you want to look like you’ve been sunnin’ it up on the beaches of Ibiza for two weeks (a girl can dream). It provides a natural expensive-looking glow to cheeks—as if you really have been kissed by the sun. As it’s not incredibly pigmented, I would worry it might be too subtle to bronze up deeper skin tones, but I do love the healthy finish it leaves on the skin and how quick and easy it is to blend out.”—CLARE STEPHENSON, SENIOR FASHION AND BEAUTY E-COMMERCE WRITER, COSMOPOLITAN UK

OUR REVIEW: "I love layering this over a neutral lipstick for a hydrating finish and a lil extra pop of color. It's really thin compared to other lip oils, which is nice if you want a more subtle glossing effect.”—LB
