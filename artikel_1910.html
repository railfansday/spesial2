O'Rourke reveals chances of Joel Piroe returning to Leeds

Joel Piroe could return to Leeds United at the end of the season as his West Ham struggles continue, sources have told Football Insider.

The 27-year-old was deemed surplus to requitements under Daniel Farke and was allowed to leave on loan during the summer, but he may miss out on a permanent move.

Despite having agreed an obligation to buy, that will only be triggered if West Ham earn promotion from the Championship, and their recent form has dented their ambitions.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Leeds are keeping a close eye on Piroe's situation and remain hopeful of a permanent exit.

Any return to Elland Road would likely see the striker cast aside again and told to look for a new club, as he is now highly unlikely to return to Farke's plans at any stage.

West Ham, meanwhile, are still hopeful of winning promotion back to the top flight which would see Piroe make the permanent switch to the London Stadium for £8million.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider during the summer that Piroe would not be part of Leeds fans, with Farke keen to move the striker on.

Despite Leeds' own issues in attacking areas, particularly surrounding a lack of depth, a return for the Suriname international is not being considered at Elland Road.

Instead, they are optimistic he will join West Ham permanently, and hope to see the Hammers' form improve so the deal goes through without any issues.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "There's no guarantee that Joel Piroe will join West Ham permanently next summer.

"Obviously there was an obligation in there to make it permanent, but I think that was on the condition that West Ham won promotion back to the Premier League.

"Then the loan move would become permanent for around £8million, but as things stand right now, that's looking doubtful.

"It's pretty much up in the air right now as well if West Ham were to go on and win promotion, whether that's automatic or via the playoffs, then the deal would become permanent.

"But I would imagine he doesn't have a long term future at Leeds whatever happens at West Ham."

Doubts about Piroe's long-term future at West Ham have emerged as a result of the Hammers' poor form of late, as they have now gone four games without a win.

Scott Parker's side have now slipped to 13th in the Championship table, and are three points shy of the playoff places, with automatic promotion already looking difficult.

However, Piroe has played an increased role in recent games having started each of the last three, despite starting just one of the five games before Parker's arrival.

It has become clear that the boss trusts the Leeds loanee to lead the line and he could play a key role in the Hammers earning promotion, and therefore signing him permanently.
