Norway are aiming to salvage their Group A4 campaign after taking just three points from their opening four matches, which included a shock 2-1 defeat against Wales in Cardiff.

That victory is all Bellamy's men have to show for their efforts so far, but both teams remain just three points behind Denmark and still have a chance of reaching the quarter-finals.

After their run to the World Cup quarter-finals this summer, Norway would have started this Nations League campaign believing they could finish top of Group A4.

However, a 3-2 win over Denmark in their opening game was followed by three successive defeats — including the shock loss to Wales in Cardiff — and they now need at least four points from their last two matches to snatch second place, and with it a quarter-final spot.

Injured Crystal Palace striker Jorgen Strand Larsen is the only notable absentee for Stale Solbakken's side, with the likes of Erling Haaland and Martin Odegaard expected to start as usual.

Wales' victory in the reverse fixture represented the biggest scalp of Craig Bellamy's reign, but they still have work to do to avoid automatic relegation to the second tier.

The trip to Oslo is followed by a home game against group leaders Portugal on Tuesday and the Dragons almost certainly need to win one of those two matches to finish third or higher.

Bellamy is able to call upon several players who were unavailable in the previous international window due to injury, including goalkeeper Karl Darlow, key centre back Joe Rodon and influential playmaker Harry Wilson.

Solbakken's side will be favourites and need a good result to keep their quarter-final hopes alive, so we are backing them to claim an important three points.
