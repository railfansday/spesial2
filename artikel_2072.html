‘My man of the match’: Sheffield United player hailed by reporter after Southampton draw

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Sheffield United drew 1-1 with the Saints at Bramall Lane in their latest Championship outing. SUFC equalised late on through midfielder Sydie Peck to earn a useful point.

Ayew, 35, was snapped up by the Blades on a free transfer back in September on a deal until the end of the season. He has made 10 appearances for the South Yorkshire outfit in all competitions to date, nine of which have come in the league, and he has chipped in with two goals and one assist.

Journalist Hall was impressed with his display on Tuesday night and has delivered his verdict, as per a report by The Star: “My man of the match. Some great moments set off the Blades fans with a bit of skill or a movement of his body and one of the best came early in the second half when he held off three players on the halfway line before beautifully nutmegging one and then being fouled by (Nathan) Wood.

“Tested (Daniel) Peretz’s handling with a forceful header after good work from (Romelle) Donovan down the left before some excellent composure on the byline saw him ghost into space before picking out Peck to finish. Made way to a standing ovation.”

Sheffield United’s decision to land Ayew has turned out be a shrewd decision. He has been an effective weapon for SUFC so far.

He provides the Blades with more competition and depth in their attacking department. The veteran battles it out with the likes of Tom Cannon, Patrick Bamford and Tyrese Campbell for game time in Chris Wilder’s starting XI.

Ayew, who is a Ghana international with 126 caps to his name, is a vastly experienced player and has played over 600 games in his career to date. He will be a good character to have in and around the dressing room and is someone who the younger players can learn a lot from playing with.

Prior to his switch to Sheffield United, he cut ties with Leicester City at the end of the last campaign after his contract at the King Power Stadium expired. He has also had spells in the past at Marseille, FC Lorient, Aston Villa, Swansea City and Crystal Palace and he was playing in the Premier League as recently as 2025.

Ayew has proved for Sheffield United that he still has fuel left in the tank. He is showing no signs of slowing down for SUFC and has be a decent addition for them this term.

The Blades are eyeing a place in the play-offs under the guidance of head coach Wilder and are in a strong position. They are 9th and are only behind Swansea City in 8th by a point after the Swans lost at Middlesbrough. Next up for Wilder’s men is an away trip down to London this weekend to take on QPR.
