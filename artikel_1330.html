‘I Made Millions Alongside Lionel Messi - But I Was Happier Earning €113 in Prison’

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

It's a dream of any footballer to be able to make millions and play alongside Lionel Messi but one of his former teammates preferred prison and earning far less.

Footballers don't usually swap winning trophies at the most famous stadiums around the world for incarceration as the fame that comes with the profession should prevent them from criminality. Some, unfortunately, can't help themselves and not even their riches or celebrity status are enough.

There are many high-profile football names who have landed in prison after their playing careers, including the disgraced Robinho and Joey Barton. Those two remain behind bars, unlike Dani Alves who, after spending 839 days in prison, was acquitted on appeal and had his charges overturned.

In January 2023, former Barcelona full-back Alves was arrested and accused of sexually assaulting a young woman in a Barcelona nightclub in December 2022. Handed a four and a half year prison sentence in February 2024, his provisional release came in March of that year after he paid €1 million in bail.

Alves became a free man in March 2025 when the top court in the Spanish region of Catalonia overturned his conviction. It ruled that the earlier verdict contained a number of inconsistencies and that the evidence presented was insufficient to overcome the presumption of innocence.

The 43-year-old's lengthy stay in prison came after a successful football career, becoming one of the most decorated players in history. A former 126-cap Brazil international, he won titles with club and country, including three Champions League trophies with Messi and Luis Suarez at Barcelona.

That period of Alves' career should be the best days of his life, but instead he values his time in incarceration more. That's because the Brazilian turned to faith while serving time.

Alves has now become an evangelical preacher and holds religious talks, of which, during his latest he reflected on his time in Brians 2 prison. He explained why he preferred the small amount of money gained from working while in prison to that of the millions that came while winning at Camp Nou:

"I was earning millions of euros, thank God, but in prison, earning 113 euros, I was happier than I was when I was earning millions."

Alves spoke of his relationship with God and how he asks a lot of questions, describing himself as a 'child before Him'. This spiritual transformation he has gone through has given him a new outlook on life and that money isn't as important as he once felt during his playing career.

The 29-year-old represented Manchester City at youth level, as well as Wolves and Nottingham Forest at senior level.

Away from religious speaking, Alves does remain tied to the sport, currently holding a co-ownership stake in Portuguese club Sao Jao de Ver. He retired from the game after arrest in January 2023, having won 46 trophies.
