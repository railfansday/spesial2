Tether has sought reissuance fees of up to 20% from some crypto hack victims seeking to recover frozen USDT, according to a Bloomberg investigation. Singapore-based crypto exchange Bittok, which lost $50 million in a 2020 hack, has yet to recover its frozen USDT more than five years later. Tether CEO Paolo Ardoino said the company reissues funds in stages in civil cases to manage legal risks due to unclear legal guidance. A spokesperson said Tether has reissued $1.2 billion in USDT so far, with fees covering reissuance and legal costs. The company is also establishing direct emergency communication channels with major crypto exchanges.
