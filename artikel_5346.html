Nearly 640,000 Medicare beneficiaries will have no Medicare Advantage drug plan in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The count is up from 399,000 in 2026, and 193 counties will lack a Medicare Advantage drug plan as major insurers exit

KFF found that 638,000 Medicare beneficiaries, 1% of the total, will have no Medicare Advantage prescription drug plan available for general enrollment in 2027. They live in 193 counties, up from 123 counties in 2026.

That compares with 399,000 beneficiaries in 2026, an increase of 239,000 people. The counties are in 16 states and in U.S. territories other than Puerto Rico. New Hampshire, North Dakota and Oklahoma are new to the list since 2026.

No county in Alaska has a general-enrollment plan, which affects about 110,000 beneficiaries. Six counties in Nebraska and South Dakota have a Medicare Advantage plan without drug coverage. The other 187 counties have no Medicare Advantage plans of either type for general enrollment. Some of these counties have cost plans or special needs plans.

Choices are narrowing in other areas. The average beneficiary can choose from 28 Medicare Advantage drug plans in 2027, offered by seven firms, one fewer firm than in 2026. The share of beneficiaries with more than 40 options fell to 20% from 27%, or nearly 13 million people from 16.6 million. Nearly 7 million people have 1 to 10 options, up from 4.8 million. Only one firm offers a drug plan in 259 counties, up from 180.

The two largest insurers are cutting their county footprints. UnitedHealth $UNH Group is leaving 228 counties and entering 9, while Humana $HUM is leaving 62 and entering 4. Health Care Service Corporation is exiting 498 counties and entering none. Centene $CNC is exiting 359 and entering 15. Molina Healthcare and Group 1001 are leaving the drug plan market. Ten firms are exiting the Medicare Advantage market in 2027, and four are entering it.

The cuts follow insurer announcements of plan discontinuations ahead of open enrollment. In October, Humana said its cuts would affect roughly 600,000 members, and UnitedHealth said approximately 390,000 members are in plans being discontinued. UnitedHealthcare President Bobby Hunter attributed the changes to "funding pressures, rising medical costs, rising drug costs, and increased utilization."

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services said more than 99% of beneficiaries will have access to at least one Medicare Advantage plan in 2027.

Open enrollment runs through Dec. 7, and 2027 coverage begins Jan. 1. Beneficiaries can compare options on the Medicare Plan Finder at Medicare.gov or by calling 1-800-MEDICARE.
