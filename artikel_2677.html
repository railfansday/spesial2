Bills list Greg Rousseau, Dalton Kincaid as limited on Monday's practice report

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Bills did not hold a practice on Monday, but they did issue an estimated injury report ahead of Thursday's game against the Chiefs.

Linebacker Greg Rousseau (back) is on the report as a limited participant. Rousseau had a pair of sacks in Sunday's win over the Dolphins and now has 10 sacks on the season.

Tight end Dalton Kincaid (thumb, ankle) did not play on Sunday and is also listed as a limited participant. Right tackle Spencer Brown (back), safety Damar Hamlin (toe, neck), defensive lineman T.J. Sanders (shoulder), right guard O'Cyrus Torrance (ankle), and linebacker Dorian Williams (calf) rounded out that category.

Cornerback Christian Benford (groin) was listed as out of practice after missing Sunday's win. Safety Cole Bishop (shoulder), tight end Jackson Hawes (shoulder), safety Amani Hooker (knee), running back Ty Johnson (knee), center Connor McGovern (neck), and defensive lineman Ed Oliver (oblique) were listed as full participants.
