Previewing the biggest Bears- Lions game of the Super Bowl Era

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Chicago Bears face the Detroit Lions on Thanksgiving to determine who is going to take over sole possession of first place in the NFC North. The last time the Bears and Lions faced each other with both teams 6 or more games over .500 was 1956!

To discuss both teams, we sat down with VSIN's Patrick Meagher. Patrick is a huge Lions fan and knows the league as well as anybody. We discussed the Lions' outlook without Aidan Hutchinson and Jack Campbell, and we discussed the Bears' defense with Jeffery Simmons and the current look of the Bears' offense. We discussed Jahmyr Gibbs, Dan Campbell, Ben Johnson, and the NFC Playoff picture. There's so much packed into this one, you won't want to miss it.
