Who’s got Cup fever?! Sixers dominate Pacers, win Group B, advance to knockout stage

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Cup continues to bring the best out of the game's greatest.

The Sixers handled the Indiana Pacers 111-87 Friday night. They advance to the Knockout Stage of the NBA Cup for the first time as a franchise, winning Eastern Conference Group B. They're next Cup game will be either Dec. 4 or 5.

LeBron James kept his hot hand, finishing with 21 points, seven rebounds and six assists shooting 8-of-13 from the floor. VJ Edgecombe went for nine points on 4-of-6 shooting along with five rebounds and seven assists.

Tyrese Maxey struggled from the field, shooting 3-of-13 for 13 points, but did also have six assists. Returning from a two-game absence due to illness, Joel Embiid had some rust to shake off, going 5-of-14 from the floor to finish with 14 points along with 11 boards.

Jaylen Brown was once again out for the Sixers with hamstring injury management, as was Dean Wade. Tyrese Haliburton and T.J. McConnell were out for Indiana.

This game started as ideally as it could for the Sixers. The fifth starter for the night was Dominick Barlow, who opened the scoring by cutting to the basket for an easy layup. LeBron buried a couple of threes, one just beat the shotclock with a hand in his face, before Barlow put back a Maxey miss to start the game on a 10-2 Sixers run. LeBron drilled his third attempt following a Pacer timeout but missed his heat check on the following possession.

There was a lot of violence at the rim early as Embiid tried to dunk on Kelly Oubre Jr. as he got open on a roll, but Oubre stuffed the attempt and gave a hearty celebration after. Trying to get down the floor quickly, the Pacers turned it over with a sloppy pass, as Maxey was able to get the ball out to Edgecombe who threw it down hard.

The center substitutions were very frequent early. Embiid's first shift was under five minutes. Adem Bona played for just 2:41 and made an impact in that time, pinning an Aaron Nesmith attempt against the backboard. Indiana opened the game 3-of-12 from the floor as a team.

It was good to see Labaron Philon Jr. back in the mix, but the Embiid-plus-bench lineup did not get off to a good start. Embiid allowed Jay Huff to swoop into the lane for an offensive rebound, turning that into a corner three. The Sixers called timeout, but were unable to stop the run as Jabari Walker got called for an offensive foul coming out of it. Nesmith got to the line to cap off a 9-0 Pacers push. Embiid knocked down a three and Simons put up a quick five to keep the Sixers up by eight after the first.

The Sixers' offense slowed down, taking nearly three minutes to score their first points of the second. It was another impressive LeBron bucket as he banked home a layup despite still trying to get his hands on Kentavious Caldwell-Pope's pass, but Indy kept creeping back on the other end.

Edgecombe hit a big three off an offensive rebound which forced a Pacer timeout, but the offense was still struggling to get back on track. Maxey didn't score his first points until halfway through the quarter, starting 0-of-3 with three turnovers.

It does seem like luck snow piles when your shot isn't falling. The offense had broken down into a lot of isolations, but Maxey also had a very open floater in the paint rim halfway down and out. On the next possession he took a deep, early three in the shotclock which also bounced around before falling out.

Nick Nurse called a timeout after the Pacers had cut the lead to one and was able to draw up something to get them out of the funk. A few screens got Walker an open corner three that he nailed. Embiid and Maxey went to their two-man game on the next two possessions, getting an elbow jumper for the former and a layup for the latter.

The Sixers had plenty of time to get their act together thanks to the Pacers shooting 2-of-17 from behind the arc in the first half. They got to the free throw line way more than the Sixers, but they would have been able to take a lead had they hit anything from outside. Ironically, it looked like their third of the night was falling right before the half, but Ivica Zubac got called for basket interference. That gave Maxey time for one more drive, getting to the line to give the Sixers an 11-point lead at the half.

Barlow's impact was felt inside when he was on the floor. He picked up his second block early in the third. A couple possessions after he did that, he grabbed a missed Embiid jumper and dunked it back over the head of a defender.

Offensively the Sixers got some easy stuff to start the half. Maxey and LeBron each got to the line and LeBron bully balled his way for an easy post-up. They got a little jumper-happy again once they got the lead up to 18 and went cold on their pull-ups. A nice cut from Barlow was rewarded by James to break that up and prompt another Pacers timeout.

Bona struggled a bit more in his quick third quarter shift, picking up his third and fourth fouls in about a 30-second span. The Sixers seemed to have even more leeway to mess around though. Just like how they shot from the first half from three, the Pacers opened the third quarter 2-of-17 from the field.

In 59 seconds of game time Simons hit as many threes as the Pacers had all night. He started it with a stepback off the dribble over his defender. After a timeout, he came off a screen for his second and hit another pull-up for his third. Maxey hit him with a crosscourt pass a few possessions later for another before he missed his heatcheck several feet behind the arc as the Sixers coasted to a 27-point lead.

Despite some other games making it not really matter, the Sixers were fighting for that point differential to start the third. KCP got on the board with a couple of baskets, Barlow picked up a couple more cutting layups, and Simons hit a running layup.

Philon might have checked back into the game a little later in the second half than some may have wanted, but it was nice to see him drill a three as well. The end of the bench did allow a quick 9-0 run that prompted an angry timeout from Nurse after a Philon turnover.

The Sixers' scoring drought that lasted over three and a half minutes was ended by Tacko Fall, who got a big round of cheers from the fans left in the arena upon doing so. Fall was able to front Huff and slam home a big dunk on the next possession to give the people what they wanted to see.
