The Detroit Lions are 8-2 on the NFL season after a 31-21 win over the Tampa Bay Buccaneers at Ford Field that included a return from former linebacker Alex Anzalone, who reacted sharply to Ford Field boo birds. The Lions' defense put in work against Baker Mayfield and the Buccaneers without star defensive end Aidan Hutchinson, while receiver Jameson Williams scared fans with a risky end zone celebration.

The Lions piled up four sacks against Mayfield and the Buccaneers in a winning effort, accomplishing something not seen in over 50 years.

"The 36 sacks recorded by the Lions D this season are the most they've registered through the first 10 games of a season since 1969," the Lions' X account wrote.

The sacks came by committee in Hutchinson's absence. Defensive end DJ Wonnum got one of them, and a game ball from coach Dan Campbell after the game. Linebacker Malcolm Rodriguez and rookie defensive end Derrick Moore combined for another sack, while Trevor Nowaske had a key sack of Mayfield late in the game and safety Chuck Clark added another one.

Kelvin Sheppard's defense has improved greatly, and now will be charged with carrying the torch forward without arguably its best player, Hutchinson. Safety Kerby Joseph got a big ovation from the Ford Field crowd as he returned with a two-tackle effort, while Jared Goff starred for the Lions' offense with 279 yards and two touchdowns.

The week also brought more tough injury news as Detroit lost a starting center to the IR. The Lions are again facing injury problems down the stretch of an otherwise successful season, giving Campbell and Sheppard's players a chance to stare down and conquer their biggest demons. The pass rush is just one part of a defense that looks like it's taking well to Sheppard's teachings, a huge plus with the Bears on tap for Thanksgiving in Week 12.
