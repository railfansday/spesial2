Yahoo Fantasy is teaming up with BreakingT, home of the fastest-licensed fan gear in the world, to launch the Yahoo Fantasy Shirt of the Week, celebrating the one play or player that defined the fantasy week. The names on your roster are about to be the names on your chest.

BreakingT is a newsroom that happens to make shirts. Using its proprietary CrowdBreak platform, BreakingT tracks what fans are reacting to across social media in real time — surfacing the moments, memes and micro-stories that matter most to each fanbase — and its design team turns those moments into officially licensed gear in hours, not months.

Every week of the NFL season, once the Monday night game goes final, the Yahoo Fantasy team will crown the play or player that defined the fantasy week.

Some weeks it's a single snap — the 75-yard catch-and-run that decides thousands of matchups, or the goal-line plunge that flipped your week with 30 seconds left. Other weeks, it's a full-blown eruption — the four-touchdown, 200-yard game that carried a lineup on its own. Whatever it is, BreakingT turns it into a shirt, and that shirt drops.

Missed out on a Yahoo Fantasy x BreakingT drop? You can still purchase any of the weekly shirts here!

Week 11's shirt: Jonah Coleman, Mile-High Salute

Jonah Coleman went from just another piece in Denver's backfield to looking like a fantasy football league winner in Week 11. The Broncos rookie put together the best game of his career against the Raiders, rushing 24 times for 111 yards and three touchdowns. For those of you in a PPR league, Coleman added four catches as well. Coleman has scored in Denver's past three games and the fourth-round pick looks to have a firm grasp on a role in the Broncos' offense.
