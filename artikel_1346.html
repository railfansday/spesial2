Chelsea Fans Claim 2 Players Must be Sold After Loss vs Leeds

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Chelsea fans have torn into two players who 'must be sold' following 'chaotic' performances in a 4-1 home loss to Leeds United on Saturday afternoon.

The Blues are living up to their nickname in recent times under Xabi Alonso, having now sunk into eighth place in the Premier League table after having failed to hold on to the early lead Emmanuel Emegha's first goal for the club offered them.

Leeds United, in the ascendancy, restored parity just three minutes later through Noah Okafor, who would score another on either side of Dominic Calvert-Lewin's penalty goal before Brenden Aaronsen added the cherry on top to give Daniel Farke's side their first win at Stamford Bridge since 1999.

There can be very few players to leave west London with their heads held high this weekend, but the focus has mostly landed at the feet of the defenders, who were passive in trying to stop their visitors from running away with the result.

In particular, the Stamford Bridge faithful have torn into Wesley Fofana and Levi Colwill, with one X user remarking: "I’d pack Wesley Fofana and Levi Colwill’s bags for them in January," as a second added:

"LEVI COLWILL AND FOFANA. It's time to leave Chelsea for good. Such a chaotic and miserable duo. Inconsistent and not good enough to play for this club. Overhyped."

Another supporter labelled them 'absolutely useless', as a fourth asked: "Why is Fofana and Colwill playing against us?" and a fifth wrote: "We can't start with Colwill and Fofana and expect to win."

Elsewhere, a sixth disgruntled user remarked: "Fofana and Colwill are both proper clowns," while a seventh concluded: "Fofana and Colwill are a disastrous combo in defence, Palmer was just not ready for football. Emegha and Estevao had no service. Alonso ball is not balling..."

Chelsea have now conceded 20 goals in 11 league fixtures this season. That's 15 more than leaders Arsenal, while only cellar dwellers Crystal Palace, and newly promoted Ipswich Town and Coventry City have shipped more than them.

At first, it seemed the majority of their issues lied with an eccentric goalkeeper in Robert Sanchez, who was error-prone and replaced by Emi Martinez after just two games this season.

But as the problems pile up, it's now clear the entire backline needs a rethink, while perhaps Alonso's tactics aren't helping them out either, given that Real Madrid also faced similar issues under his guidance last term.
