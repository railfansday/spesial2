Callum Hudson-Odoi's career has stagnated at Nottingham Forest thanks to various injuries since the 2025-26 campaign.

So far in 2026-27, he has yet to make a single start in the Premier League or Carabao Cup, coming off the bench for brief cameos.

Oliver Glasner clearly isn't a fan of the former Chelsea academy graduate, with other players fitting the new manager's playing style much better.

Fans at the City Ground are baffled by the current situation, to say the least, and there doesn't seem to be an easy solution.

The tricky winger missed a total of 11 games via thigh and leg issues last term, according to Transfermarkt, but a reason for his lack of minutes now is unknown.

Forest may have a way out in the January transfer window, though, as unexpected new emerges weeks before deals can begin to be done.

Given the current situation, it is only natural for the player to be linked with a move away halfway through a frustrating season.

And according to Corriere dello Sport, AS Roma are hoping to reproduce the success from signing Donyell Malen from Aston Villa.

Hudson-Odoi and Jamie Gittens of Chelsea are both targets ahead of January, with the report noting the former's "particularly complicated" situation.

It is believed that Gian Piero Gasperini would be the perfect manager to bring him back to life should he move away from the City Ground.

And importantly, it is understood that Hudson-Odoi would look "favourably" on a move to Serie A. Roma are now waiting for the right moment to strike.

In the summer of 2025, Hudson-Odoi signed new terms to extend his stay with Forest until 2028, and that will be upon us in the blink of an eye.

There are no signs that the Reds want to give the winger another new contract, with his injuries clearly a frustrating factor.

It has been reported that Hudson-Odoi earns £6.2million a year on his latest deal, and that is money that Forest could definitely use elsewhere.

Sadly, he just doesn't suit the new system. Over the last three seasons, he chipped in with noticeable contributions, but that looks to be over.
