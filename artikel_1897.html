Ex-PGMOL chief slams VAR as Arsenal vs Dortmund penalty footage re-analysed

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

"Viktor Gyokeres should have conceded a penalty in Arsenal's 1-1 Champions League draw with Borussia Dortmund on Tuesday.

That is according to former PGMOL boss and FIFA referee, Keith Hackett, who has told Football Insider that VAR needed to intervene after the Arsenal striker's second-half handball.

Gyokeres' first-half strike opened the scoring at the Emirates Stadium and ultimately earned his side a point after Maxi Beier's 55th-minute leveller.

The visitors had felt hard done by shortly prior as the Swede seemed to use his arm in his own penalty area but none of the officials spotted an offence.

VAR did check the handball, yet referee Maurizio Mariani was not sent to review the call at the pitchside monitor.

Former refereeing chief Hackett has insisted that Arsenal were fortunate not to concede a spot-kick in a game-defining mistake by the officials.

Daniel Svensson's 51st-minute corner did not get into the six-yard box as Gyokeres flung himself at the ball, with several visiting players then appealing loudly for a penalty.

VAR quickly checked but saw nothing wrong with the incident, even though replays showed that the Arsenal striker had moved his arm away from his body somewhat before the ball struck it while he jumped.

Hackett has insisted that Arsenal were lucky not to concede a spot-kick for the offence.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, he said: "Borussia Dortmund will be disappointed that a penalty kick was not awarded for this handball offence.

"After the referee made his decision, the VAR should have intervened and sent the referee to the pitchside monitor, it made a clear error by not doing so. Gyokeres moved his arm towards the ball. It's a penalty."

Gyokeres is doing all he can to prove that he deserves to start more Premier League games than the single one he has so far in this campaign.

The 28-year-old put in a strong performance against Dortmund, despite his lucky moment, after opening the scoring with a stunning long-range strike that caught Gregor Kobel off-guard.

He took the most shots (7) of any player and the second-most touches (11) in the opposition box, created one chance and won six duels.

He now has four goal contributions in five Champions League matches this season and will feel like he is getting better as the campaign progresses.
