Keith Wyness explains unexpected Man United 49 per cent boardroom increase

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Manchester United fans shouldn't read too much into the fact that the value of shares in their club has climbed during 2026.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that United's ownership are not primarily focused on financial gain.

This comes after Kieran Maguire revealed that the price of United's shares has grown by 49 per cent since the start of 2026, despite the team's fortunes on the pitch.

However, Wyness believes that the increase may merely be down to less people selling their shares in the club, creating scarcity in the market.

United are currently languishing inside the relegation zone, and are arguably at one of the lowest ebbs in their lengthy history.

Expectations would suggest that should coincide with the club's valuation on the stock market dropping, but Wyness outlined why that is not always the case.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that the change in United's shares may be down to a natural "float."

He explained that floats are events that can dramatically change the price of shares, far more than usually expected.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained why fans shouldn't draw conclusions from the notable increase.

"You would think so from the outside looking at that, but the reality is there's a very small, what they call a float for these shares," he said.

"So events can make dramatic increases or decreases more than a normal share might do. So I wouldn't read too much into it.

"It's just the fact that very few shares being traded can lead to different price increases. I don't see the share price being a great indicator of United's position right now."

Wyness previously told Football Insider last week that the latest "noises" from Old Trafford suggest that Bruno Fernandes will still sign a new contract.

The club captain recently rejected a second offer of a new deal from the Red Devils, as his long-term future remains hugely uncertain.

However, Wyness believes that new manager Mauricio Pochettino will be capable of repairing the relationship with Fernandes in the coming months.

The Portuguese is out of contract at the end of the season, although United have the option of an additional one-year extension.
