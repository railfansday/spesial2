West Brom £2.5m summer signing has gone under the radar as a bargain addition: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

West Brom made a lot of new signings over the summer window with newly appointed permanent boss James Morrison looking to bring a more youthful approach to the squad. While the likes of Jimmy-Jay Morgan and Rabby Nzingoula have earned the headlines for WBA, one of their more experienced additions is starting to shine through.

The reported £2.5 million signing of Felix Horn Myhre from Brann is looking like an excellent piece of business. The 27-year-old arrived at WBA following a Player of the Season campaign in Norway, bringing international experience and a proven record of goals and assists from midfield.

Four months into the Championship season, Morrison’s side appear to have secured a player capable of making a significant contribution to their promotion ambitions without breaking the bank.

Horn Myhre had plenty to live up to after five productive seasons at Brann, where he registered 32 goals in 176 appearances across all competitions, helped the club win the Norwegian Cup and second-tier title, and earned the vice-captaincy.

His transition to English football with WBA has not been entirely straightforward. The Championship’s physicality and direct approach have required an adjustment, with the midfielder acknowledging the need to take greater responsibility in the final third and look to make things happen rather than simply retain possession.

His recent performances have been particularly encouraging. Horn Myhre scored in victories over Blackburn Rovers and Millwall before producing a goal and assist in the 3-1 win at West Ham, earning an impressive 8.6 FotMob rating.

His versatility is another major asset. Morrison has deployed him in central and attacking midfield, as well as further forward and out wide, providing valuable tactical flexibility.

West Brom needed additional goal contributions from midfield, and Horn Myhre is demonstrating that he can provide exactly that. His three goals are closely aligned with his 3.57 expected goals, while his 12 chances created and 87% passing accuracy underline his ability to influence attacking moves.

There is also scope for further improvement. At 27, Horn Myhre should be entering his peak years, while his performances in England could help him re-establish himself in the Norway national team after missing out on their World Cup squad.

Alongside fellow Brann arrival Aune Heggebo, he represents an increasingly useful Norwegian influence at The Hawthorns. Consistency remains the next challenge, particularly across the demanding Championship schedule.

Nevertheless, West Brom have acquired an experienced international with European pedigree and proven attacking quality for a relatively modest fee. If Horn Myhre maintains his recent trajectory, the £2.5 million investment could become one of the smartest pieces of recruitment in the Championship this season.
