Whyte offers to fight Wilder - 'They know where I am'

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Dillian Whyte has made himself available to step in for Jai Opetaia and box Deontay Wilder on the undercard of the December 11 fight between Tyson Fury and Anthony Joshua at the Principality Stadium in Cardiff; "If they want it and they want to have a good fight, then I'm down," he said

Dillian Whyte is ready to rekindle his rivalry with Deontay Wilder.

At one point Whyte was the WBC's No 1 contender when Wilder held that title. He never got to box Wilder, though did eventually challenge Tyson Fury after he'd beaten the American for the belt.

Wilder is currently looking for a new opponent after Jai Opetaia withdrew from a heavyweight bout that was scheduled for the undercard of Fury's December 11 showdown with Anthony Joshua.

Whyte has made himself available as a replacement. "If they want it then they know where I am," the Brixton heavyweight told Sky Sports News.

"If they want it and they want to have a good fight, then I'm down. You know, I always come to fight. I give it my best and I give my all.

"I've already waited 100 years for him, so if he wants it in December, then let's go."

He added: "I've been trying to fight him for 100 years. I've always believed that I could have beaten him.

"He's a big puncher, he's wiry, and he's fast and all that. I just believe that I have the mentality and the style to beat him and cause him all sorts of problems. And I still believe that."

Whyte insists he could be ready for the bill at the Principality Stadium. "I'm training to fight a week after them. So I'm in a gym, I'm looking to come back December 18. So if they want it, I'm here.
