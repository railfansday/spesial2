Andie MacDowell Praises Daughters Margaret, Rainey for Finding Their Paths

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Andie MacDowell is relishing how daughters Margaret Qualley and Rainey Qualley have emerged as two of Hollywood’s most promising young stars.

“I love watching my kids and seeing that they’re not mini-mes,” the Four Weddings and a Funeral actress, 68, exclusively told Us at the Wednesday, November 18, L’Oréal Paris Women of Worth Celebration event recognizing extraordinary women leading grassroots nonprofit organizations and creating meaningful change in communities across the country. “They have defined themselves, and they are who they are, and they have strength and power in that. So I love that.”

MacDowell memorably worked with Margaret, 32, on the hit Netflix series Maid, with her daughter going on to land a Best Supporting Actress Golden Globe nomination for her command performance in body horror hit The Substance.

Rainey, 37, led the 2022 thriller movie Shut In and has since embarked on a flourishing music career. (MacDowell and ex-husband Paul Qualley share a son, Justin Qualley, who is not involved in the entertainment industry. She is also a grandmother of three.)

Asked how she encourages her children, MacDowell shared some gems of wisdom with Us, “I think finding who you are, being your authentic self, not curating who you are to please other people, but really being truthful and honest with who you are.”

The Golden Globe winner credited both of her daughters as having “done well” in Hollywood by following their own instincts.

“I love watching their creativity and the choices that they make, and just their creative spirits. They’re just highly creative individuals, and I love watching them grow,” she added.

While chatting with Us, MacDowell reflected on the “innate bond” she felt instantly with her three granddaughters.

“It happens automatically, which is really beautiful. They know you. They know you. It’s like they can sense it when you look in their eyes,” she said. “They know you’re part of them, you know. And I don’t know, it’s really special just having the opportunity to love your baby’s baby.”

With the holidays just around the corner, MacDowell revealed that her family have decided “not to have a lot of gifts” this year.

“We just want to have connection, bonding,” she explained. “But I do want to learn how to make marshmallows. … So that’s my goal.”

Next up for MacDowell on the career front is Beach Read, writer-director Yulin Kuang‘s 2027 comedy based on author Emily Henry‘s romance novel of the same name. MacDowell is part of an impressive ensemble that also includes Kevin Bacon, Bridgerton alum Phoebe Dynevor, The White Lotus‘ Patrick Schwarzenegger, Kristin Davis and Tig Notaro. (Beach Read will be released in theaters on May 7, 2027.)
