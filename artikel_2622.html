Pittsburgh benching Aaron Rodgers

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Pittsburgh Steelers QB Aaron Rodgers will be inactive for Week 12 against the Denver Broncos, according to a team statement on Tuesday, Nov. 24. Mason Rudolph will draw the start in his place.

Why It Matters: Well, there's a bit to unpack here. First of all, the team waiting until after 11 PM ET on a Tuesday night to announce a quarterback change is highly unusual. The fact that it is happening to someone of Rodgers' ilk - regardless of the perception of how he is playing right now - is even more hard to believe. Then there is the dynamic of Rodgers' long-standing relationship with head coach Mike McCarthy. While no reason was given why the change was made in the statement, it seems obvious that Rodgers either did something that could be perceived as "conduct detrimental to the team" over the last 24-48 hours or McCarthy (or the front office) determined that his play has fallen off to the point that he is worthy of being benched. If it is the latter (which it likely is), it should not come as much of a surprise if Rodgers' release is coming shortly. Rudolph will make his 20th career start (in his seventh NFL season) and has typically performed reasonably well when called upon, but make no mistake about it: he is not a quality NFL starter. Rudolph's "promotion" will most likely benefit managers who use the Denver D/ST this week more than anyone else.
