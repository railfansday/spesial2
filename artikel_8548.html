Tanggal: 2026-11-16

DW got a statement from her provider for about 4 months for various visits including a minor surgery. Total billed was $67,500, Medicare approved $4,755, Medicare paid 80% of the approved amount, about $3,800. That leaves about $1,000 to be paid by us (if no supplement policy), she has a supplement so they paid the $1,000.

The point I am trying to make is that Medicare only allows the providers to charge a fraction of what they bill, you will not be on the hook for 20% of the provider's bill, it will be a fraction of that.
