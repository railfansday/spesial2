NexPoint Residential (NXRT) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Leverage, occupancy pressure and a looming dividend decision shape the outlook.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

NexPoint Residential Trust, Inc. (NXRT -8.65%) reported third-quarter results that missed analyst expectations, prompting management to lower its full-year guidance for Core FFO and same-store NOI. The company stated that elevated repairs, maintenance, and marketing expenses, combined with occupancy pressure in a high-supply environment, negatively impacted the bottom line. Management reported that while renewal growth remains positive, new lease pricing turned sharply negative late in the quarter as the company shifted strategy to prioritize physical occupancy through the winter. The company is actively pursuing a deleveraging plan involving asset dispositions to reduce exposure to floating-rate debt as interest rate hedges continue to expire.
