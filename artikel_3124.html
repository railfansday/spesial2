Calumet, Inc. (CLMT -8.37%) reported a period of significant debt reduction and strategic restructuring of its Montana Renewables business. Management accelerated its deleveraging strategy by retiring debt and reducing annual interest obligations while executing a major turnaround at the Shreveport facility. Operational focus remains on the MaxSAF expansion project to significantly increase sustainable aviation fuel production capacity through 2028.
