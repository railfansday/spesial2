Tilt: Head of Growth, Tilt Mexico

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

At Tilt, we see a side of people that traditional lenders miss. Our mobile-first products and machine learning-powered credit models look beyond credit scores, using over 250 real-time financial signals to recognize real potential. With millions of customers worldwide, we're not just changing how people access financial products — we're creating a new credit system that backs the working, whatever they're working toward.

Mexico was Tilt's first market outside the US. Since 2022, our mobile-first personal loan has helped over 1 million customers access short-term credit on transparent, affordable terms. We now offer a 3-month installment loan and are rolling out a credit card in beta. As Head of Growth, you'll own how Tilt Mexico finds, activates and keeps customers across all three products, with real budget accountability and the mandate to drive profitable growth.

Acquisition works today but is concentrated in a few paid channels. Lifecycle and cross-sell are early, and the brand is under-measured. This is a chance to build an AI-native growth function in one of Latin America's largest and fastest-moving consumer credit markets.

This is a remote role based in Mexico. Travel for company onsites is expected at least four times a year.

Own the full growth funnel: Lead acquisition, activation, retention and cross-sell as one strategy, measured on profitable customers rather than installs or approvals. Paid acquisition stays the engine; you'll bring lifecycle and brand up to parity over time.

Drive performance marketing sophistication: Allocate the acquisition budget across channels with rigour: proper attribution, event-based optimisation, and payback and CAC (customer acquisition cost) discipline tied to credit outcomes. Push beyond the current channel mix into new paid, partnership, referral and app store channels, backed by steady creative testing.

Make growth and credit work together: Use the unit economics model with Credit and Data Science to decide which customers to acquire, at what cost and through which channels. Make sure growth targets account for approval rates and losses, not just volume.

Build the brand and its measurement: Define how Tilt shows up in Mexico, in voice, visual identity and awareness strategy, and put in place the measurement it lacks today: brand tracking, organic search and app store visibility, and social listening.

Grow customer value through lifecycle and cross-sell: Build triggered campaigns across push, SMS, WhatsApp, email and in-app that move customers from first loan to repeat loans, installment loans and the credit card. Partner with Product and Engineering to define what the lifecycle stack needs, and own the strategy that runs on it.

Run a small, leveraged team: Lead a tight, high-output team that uses AI for creative production, budget allocation and analytics rather than defaulting to headcount growth. Share what works with the growth teams in our other markets: the US, Brazil, India and the Philippines.

8+ years in growth or marketing, with hands-on performance marketing experience earlier in your career. You've personally run campaigns, not just overseen them.

You've had real budget accountability: your name on spend decisions, not just advisory input.

Consumer fintech or digital lending experience in Mexico or Latin America. You understand how acquisition cost, approval rates and credit losses shape what growth is worth.

Strong quantitative fluency. CAC, LTV (lifetime value), payback, attribution models and A/B test design (sample size, statistical significance) are second nature.

Familiarity with CRM and lifecycle tooling (Iterable or equivalent). You know what good looks like, even if you weren't the one building it.

You hold both halves of growth at once, analytical rigour and creative judgment, and you're honest about where your edge sits.

An AI-native mindset. You use agentic AI tools to raise your team's speed and quality, and you see AI as a force multiplier, not a threat to headcount.

Native or fluent Spanish and professional fluency in English.

Bonus: Hands-on experience with AppsFlyer (or a comparable mobile attribution platform) for install and event measurement.

Initial Recruiter Call: A conversation to learn about your experience and what you're looking for in your next role.

Hiring Manager Interview: A deeper discussion about your experience.

Skills Panel: Meet with Tilt team members to discuss your expertise and problem-solving approach.

Leadership Conversation: A final conversation with a leader to discuss how you'll contribute to Tilt's mission, and how we can help you achieve your career goals along the way.

Don’t meet every qualification? We care about potential over your past. If you're bringing ambition and drive to what we're building, we want to hear from you.

Virtual-first teamwork: The Tilt team is collaborating across 14 countries, 12 time zones, and counting. You’ll get started with a WFH office reimbursement.

Competitive pay: We're big on potential, and it's reflected in our competitive compensation packages and generous equity.

Complete support: Find flexible health plans at every premium level, and substantial subsidies that stand up to global standards.

Visibility is yours: You can count on direct exposure to our leadership team — we’re a team where good ideas travel quickly.

Paid global onsites: Magic happens IRL: we gather twice yearly to reconnect over shared meals or kayaking adventures. (We’ve visited Vail, San Diego, and Mexico City, to name a few.)

Impact is recognized: Growth opportunities follow your contributions, not rigid promotion timelines.

We're looking for people who chase excellence and impact. Those who stand behind their work, celebrating the wins and learning from the missteps equally. We foster an environment where every voice is valued and mutual respect is non-negotiable — brilliant jerks need not apply. We're in this together, working to expand access to fair credit and prove that people are incredible. When you join us, it's not just another day at the [virtual] office, you're helping millions of hardworking people reach better financial futures.

You’re pushing ahead in your career? We can get behind that. Join us in building the credit system that people deserve.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.
