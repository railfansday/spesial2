Paramount+’s Hit 39-Episode Survival Thriller Officially Returns This Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Warning! Spoilers ahead for seasons 1-3 of Yellowjackets!One of the many new streaming TV shows coming to Paramount+ in November includes the long-awaited final season of Yellowjackets. Since its premiere in 2021, Yellowjackets has been shocking audiences, paving the way for the highly anticipated finale that will mark the end of the characters' struggle for survival.

It's been over a year since the season 3 finale in April of 2025, and there are many stories Yellowjackets needs to wrap up before the survival thriller ends for good. The returning members of the all-star cast include Melanie Lynskey, Christina Ricci​​​​​​, Tawny Cypress​​​​​​, Sophie Nélisse, Jasmin Savoy Brown​​​​​​, and Sophie Thatcher.

Following the dual timelines of the Wiskayok High School girls' soccer team stranded in the Canadian wilderness for 19 months in the 1990s and the stories of the survivors in the present, Yellowjackets season 4 promises to deliver the jaw-dropping horror and intensity that viewers expect from the show.

It's finally time to bid farewell to the cannibalistic survivors of the plane crash in Yellowjackets season 4, which premieres on Paramount+ on November 20th. After finally revealing the identity of the pit girl as Mari, who had been teased since season 1, and Natalie's success in getting a signal on the satellite phone, there's a lot to be reminded of ahead of season 4. While the series' creators, Ashley Lyle and Bart Nickerson, originally planned for a fifth season, Yellowjackets will be tying up its loose ends in the coming months.

Though there will be no Yellowjackets season 5, the series has already taken the original narrative as far as it can go, leaving the girls stranded in the woods until now. The season 4 teaser hints at the girls' rocky return home to Wiskayok, where old friends and family are waiting. Not only will the girls have to deal with the fallout of their actions before the plane crash, but they'll have to do so as the very different people they're coming home as.

Yellowjackets' co-creator Bart Nickerson sheds new light regarding the hit series' infamous missing episode that never made it to the screen.

In the present, Shauna, Taissa, Misty, and most recently Melissa, continue to grapple with the secrets they've tried to keep buried since they were rescued. After several violent seasons of murders and cover-ups, and the tragic death of Van at the end of season 3, the adults are heading into more unhinged territory than ever, and it might finally be time for them to pay the price for what they did in the wilderness all those years ago.

It will be sad to say goodbye to the bloody, high-drama world of Paramount+'s Yellowjackets, but there's good reason to believe that the final installment of the hit series will deliver the twists we're hoping for. Season 4 is only a few days away, but there's still time to catch up on all the deaths, hunts, and betrayals in seasons 1-3 before November 20th.

Yellowjackets season 4 premieres on Paramount+ Premium Plan on November 20th.
