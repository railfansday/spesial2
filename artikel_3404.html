UTA Hires Sam Yabrow As Talent Agent

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

EXCLUSIVE: UTA has hired Sam Yabrow as a talent agent based in its Los Angeles office.

Yabrow joins from Buchwald, bringing nearly a decade of entertainment industry experience spanning casting and talent representation.

Clients expected to join him at UTA include Will Price (The Only Living Pickpocket in New York, The Chair Company), Igby Rigney (The Shards), Everett Blunck (The Plague, The Audacity) and Jaren Lewison (Eternally Yours).

Yabrow began his career in casting in New York before moving into talent representation at Abrams Artists Agency, later known as A3 Artists Agency. Following A3’s closure in 2024, he joined Buchwald, where he spent the past two and a half years. He relocated to Los Angeles earlier this year.

Originally from Georgia, Yabrow is a graduate of the University of Michigan. Watch on Deadline
