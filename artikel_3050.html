These Seniors Will Get Their First Social Security Check With the 2027 COLA in December, But Most Others Will Have to Wait

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Only a few more weeks are standing between you and higher Social Security benefits.

By Kailey Hagen, CFP® – Nov 23, 2026 at 3:15PM EST

If you've been struggling with your retirement expenses, the 2027 Social Security cost-of-living adjustment (COLA) probably can't come soon enough. This will boost everyone's benefit by 3.6%, adding roughly $75 per month to the average check.

Though the COLA will be applied to the December 2026 payment, benefits usually go out in the month after they're due, so most won't see an increase to their checks until January. However, a small group of seniors will receive their benefit increase in December.

The Social Security Administration follows a strict payment schedule in which most people receive their checks on the second, third, or fourth Wednesday of the month, depending on their birthday. The later your birthday falls in the month, the later your checks arrive.

But there are a couple of groups who don't follow this rule. Seniors who began claiming Social Security prior to May 1997 and those on Supplemental Security Income (SSI) usually receive their Social Security checks on the third of the month, regardless of their birthday.

In most months, this isn't an issue, but sometimes the third falls on a weekend. In that case, payments go out on the last business day before the third. And that's what's happening with the first checks that include the 2027 Social Security COLA.

Jan. 3, 2027, is a Sunday, so it's not an eligible payment day. The weekday before that is Jan. 1, 2027, which is a federal holiday, so payments won't go out then either. Instead, older beneficiaries and those on SSI will get their first Social Security checks with the COLA on Dec. 31, 2026.

It's not much earlier, but it could be a big help to some seniors, especially those struggling with holiday costs. Just bear in mind that, if you fall into this group, you won't get another Social Security check until Feb. 3, 2027, so make sure you're budgeting carefully to stretch your check until then.
