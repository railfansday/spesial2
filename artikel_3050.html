HCI Group (HCI) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Operating leverage and a bigger investment portfolio drove a sharp profit rise.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

HCI Group, Inc. (HCI -4.74%) reported an increase in pretax income driven by operational leverage and expansion in its investment portfolio. Management stated the company is transitioning from a period of aggressive top-line expansion to a focus on maintaining business stability and increasing earnings per share through share count reduction. The company reported a combined ratio below its historical target range and completed an $80 million share repurchase program during the year. Management indicated that its catastrophe strategy facilitated a faster recovery of book value following recent storm activity compared to historical performance.
