Lemonade, Inc. (LMND -9.63%) reported accelerating top-line growth in the third quarter, with management confirming a trajectory toward positive adjusted EBITDA in the fourth quarter. The company reached nationwide availability for its renters product and expanded its car insurance offerings to 14 states, now covering more than half of the U.S. population. Management highlighted the role of its proprietary AI platform, Lemonade OS, in driving operational leverage and enabling a sevenfold increase in the pace of product launches compared to previous years. The company indicated that its focus remains on high return-on-investment growth, particularly in the car and autonomous vehicle segments, while managing inflationary pressures in its pet insurance book.
