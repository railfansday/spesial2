Agility Robotics Makes Public Debut as Humanoids Scale

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Agility Robotics is making its public market debut, becoming the first dedicated US humanoid robotics company to go public. CEO Peggy Johnson discusses growing deployments of its Digit robot, $300 million in pending orders and why the company chose to go public now despite market volatility. She joins Tim Stenovec on “Bloomberg Tech.” (Source: Bloomberg)
