Insane Bo Nix Stat Emerges After Week 9 Broncos-Panthers Tilt

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Denver Broncos just keep on finding ways to win. On Sunday, Bo Nix and company stretched their winning streak to five games after taking down the Carolina Panthers in another thriller, 26-21.

Denver scored the first 10 points of the game with a Wil Lutz field goal and a Courtland Sutton touchdown catch in the first quarter.

The Panthers got on the board with a Tommy Tremble score late in the second period before Lutz scored another field goal with under four seconds left in the first half.

Darren Waller scored the only points in the third quarter with a 15-yard touchdown catch. In the fourth frame, Jonah Coleman rushed for a nine-yard score before Pat Bryant caught a 26-yard pass to put Denver in front, 26-14. Carolina closed the gap with a Tetairoa McMillan touchdown with 3:05 to go in regulation, but those were the final points scored in the game, as Nix and the Broncos added another win to their record.

Jan 17, 2026; Denver, CO, USA; Denver Broncos quarterback Bo Nix (10) celebrates after winning an AFC Divisional Round playoff game against the Buffalo Bills at Empower Field at Mile High. Mandatory Credit: Ron Chenoy-Imagn Images

According to Doug Clawson of CBS Sports, the win also improved a particular Nix record when down by up to eight points in the fourth quarter.

"Bo Nix is 11-1 in the past 2 seasons when down 1-8 points in the 4th quarter, including playoffs. The league avg win pct in those spots is .296," Clawson shared on X (formerly Twitter).

Nix has developed a reputation as a clutch performer, and that record and the win over the Panthers further prove it.

"I think we're just comfortable in that moment — when things get close at the end of the game," Nix said after the game, via the Associated Press (h/t ESPN). "… I'm just glad we're on this side of winning them and not in a streak of losing them."

Denver has the best record in the AFC West with a 7-2 slate.
