Jayson Tatum takes blame for controversial foul in Nuggets loss: ‘That’s on me’

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Celtics looked like they were going to get one last chance to tie the score against the Nuggets. Even though Jamal Murray hit a midrange jumper with 6.4 seconds left, the C's were still only down three points. So, they took a timeout to reassess and advance the ball.

The problem was that Jayson Tatum picked up a controversial technical foul. Instead of giving the ball to a nearby referee, he instead passed it down the court. As a result, he picked up the tech. After Murray hit the free throw to give the Nuggets a four-point lead, that essentially ended the game with such little time left. Tatum took blame for his mistake after the Celtics lost 105-101 to the Nuggets on Wednesday in Denver.

"I should've probably just gave the ball to the ref closest to me," Tatum said, via CLNS Media. "Whatever I thought that John (Goble) was looking at our bench, I should've just found the guy or the ref closest to me and just gave them the ball. So, that's on me."

It was a struggle of a night for the Celtics and Tatum in the second half. It was all C's early, building a 17-point lead in the first half. Tatum scored 20 points in the opening two quarters. But after halftime, the Celtics were called for several fouls while they watched their lead evaporate into a tight game. The C's shot 10-for-12 on free throws compared to a 24-for-29 showing for the Nuggets as part of a frustrating night.

Tatum finished with just four points in the second half as the Nuggets made it a point to swarm him. He shot 10-for-23 from the floor, including 3-for-9 on 3-pointers. Tatum also picked up just three assists as he wasn't quite as effective on the court like he normally has been this season. The technical foul put a damper on the night, as well.

"I told Eric (Dalen), I was like I wasn't mad," Tatum said. "It wasn't a foul on me. We just called a timeout. John Goble called timeout and pointed at our bench. Thought he was looking at this way. But yeah, they called a tech. For me, just find (Chelisa Painter) that was there and whoever was probably the closest, and just gave them the ball."

Regardless, the Celtics will look to bounce back on this four-game road trip. They're on a losing streak for the first time this season, dropping their last two games. But Boston is still 11-4 and has one of the best net ratings in the league. The C's get the Kings next in Sacramento for a chance to earn a bounce-back opportunity.

"We can't put ourselves in that position to even give the refs an opportunity to interpret it a certain type of way," C's coach Joe Mazzulla said. "We just gotta be better there."
