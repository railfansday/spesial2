‘Football’s dead in Ann Arbor’: Dave Portnoy tears into Michigan

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Michigan Wolverines are not having the sort of season most expected when they brought in Kyle Whittingham, one of the most well-respected coaches in the country, to replace the outgoing Sherrone Moore.

The Wolverines entered Saturday's game against the UCLA Bruins at 5-5, and have flip-flopped between Bryce Underwood and Tommy Carr at quarterback (Underwood is starting against the Bruins).

Bartstool Sports founder Dave Portnoy, a Michigan alum and superfan, has not been shy about offering his feelings toward Whittingham.

"This guy is asleep at the wheel. He's watching Golden Girls," Portnoy said on Wake Up Barstool after Michigan lost to its in-state rival, the Michigan State Spartans. "This guy may have had a great career at Utah, but this man is asleep. He shows no reaction. It's like he's sedated on the sideline."

"Now, did he have a good run at Utah? Yeah. Is this killing his legacy? Well, go look what Utah's doing. Utah's what, two, three in the country, having the best season they've had in 20 years. All they had to do is get rid of this guy," Portnoy continued. "So yes, Jason Beck, see ya later, but we bring in a guy who's not a fit on any level, on anything, and we can play the clips. He's done it all year, but he's living in a dream world, this guy."

On Saturday, Portnoy was in Knoxville for the Bartsool College Football Show and was met with boos after he mentioned his Wolverines. However, rather than defend his alma mater, he let Whittingham, who turned 67 on Saturday, hear it again.

"You want me to boo Michigan, I'll boo Michigan," he said. "I want to fire the coach. I hate the season. Football's dead in Ann Arbor right now, so you're not gonna get under my skin booing Michigan. I'm mad at them too."

Portnoy went on to say that he doesn't see any way his team can beat the Bruins.

He later added, "I don't see any way Michigan wins this game. It's one bad headline after another. I want him fired. I want the season to start over. Everything's bad in Ann Arbor. I'm taking UCLA today. It's not good times for the Michigan Wolverines."
