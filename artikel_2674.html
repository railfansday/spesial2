How to watch Texas vs Missouri football Saturday

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Texas football got back on track last week against Mississippi State.

The Longhorns notched a 31-3 win against the Bulldogs thanks to three touchdowns from Arch Manning and a scoop-and-score from defensive lineman Maraad Watson. With this being Texas' second straight win after a pair of losses, the Longhorns (6-2, 3-2 SEC) rose one spot to No. 10 in the AP Poll.

Now, Texas is getting ready for another ranked SEC matchup against No. 19 Missouri. The Tigers (6-2, 3-2) cruised to a 34-10 victory against Arkansas last weekend; their only losses are to Mississippi State and Ole Miss.
