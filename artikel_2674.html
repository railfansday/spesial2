John Harbaugh’s wife perfectly sums up NFL Thanksgiving Day drama

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

John Harbaugh could run for mayor of what his wife calls "Crazy Town."

Harbaugh will coach for his 200th career victory (regular season plus playoffs) Sunday against the Colts in Indianapolis. He ranks third among active coaches behind mentor Andy Reid (315) and Sean Payton (202), though only the last six wins are with the Giants.

"The wins are really hard to get," Harbaugh said. "They're really valuable. … There's no greater football joy than that winning locker room. I would just say every one of those wins is the same. It's like my dad says, 'You've never had a better win than this one.' Every [win] feels that way. The next one is really hard to get."

Ingrid Harbaugh started referring to the NFL as "Crazy Town" this year because of all the games decided by twists and turns in the final minutes.

The three Thanksgiving Day games all featured game-winning scores in the final three seconds. The Eagles' 21-point comeback against the Cowboys had the biggest impact on the NFC East-leading Giants — and serves as a great example of "Crazy Town."

New York Giants head coach John Harbaugh speaks at a press conference when the New York Giants practiced Wednesday, November 25, 2026 at Quest Diagnostics Training Center in East Rutherford, NJ. Robert Sabo for NY Post

"Oh, my gosh, was it ever," Harbaugh said. "It just goes to show you that these games are going to come down to the fourth quarter. You give the Eagles a lot of credit for the game they played. All six of those teams are fighting in the fourth quarter for the win.

"Kind of goes to the other point … how hard it is to win these games. And, if you can, become a team that finds ways to win games and can keep their poise and make plays. Hopefully, you scheme some good plays up, too, to win games at the end. That's the way they go. You have to be expecting that, I think — not be surprised by it."

Head coach John Harbaugh of the New York Giants is all smiles during the second half at MetLife Stadium, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026, in Brooklyn, NY. Corey Sipkin for NY Post

DT Shelby Harris (illness) will not play after not practicing for the third straight day.

The Giants will ask more of Darius Alexander, C.J. Okoye and Bobby Jamison-Travis next to D.J. Reader.

CB Dru Phillips (illness) and OLB Abdul Carter (calf/illness) were limited participants but are expected to play.

Could Carter's workload be limited after his breakout 12-pressure game?

"He seemed OK today," Harbaugh said. "I'm sure we'll be monitoring that, but he seemed good today. He seemed strong."

The Jameis Winston quarterback sneak package isn't going anywhere.

When the Giants benched Winston (after four starts) to go to J.J. McCarthy, they kept Winston involved by having him run the Tush Push and other versions of sneaks.

"It was something he had been repping already, and so it was one less thing on J.J.'s plate," Harbaugh said. "Just being honest, I'd probably say that was kind of where we started with it. We kind of got to the point where, if it ain't broke, don't fix it. It's been good for us. It ends up being a whole package for us."
