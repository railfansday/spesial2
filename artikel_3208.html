Management reported that momentum in the Power Solutions segment and the integration of Koch Filter supported the year-over-year growth. The company indicated that its debt reduction efforts reached a target level of 1.7 times net leverage, allowing for an increased focus on share repurchases and potential industrial acquisitions. Management stated that the business transition following its separation from Cummins is complete, resulting in improved free cash flow generation without previous one-time separation costs.
