Air Travel Chaos Puts FAA Systems in Focus

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Bloomberg Intelligence senior airlines analyst George Ferguson tells Bloomberg This Weekend that the nationwide ground stop underscores the difficulty of modernizing an aging US air traffic control system while keeping flights moving safely. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Ferguson explains why upgrading legacy technology is so complicated and how airlines face the challenge of restoring schedules after thousands of cancellations and delays.
