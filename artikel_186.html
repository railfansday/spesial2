Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns over scandal and is replaced by CFO Colette Kress on interim basis – follow live

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Nvidia shares plunge 10% in pre-market trading after board ousts longtime boss Huang over two affairs, including with a suspected Chinese spy, in big blow to AI boom

Nvidia’s long-time boss Jensen Huang has stepped down over the scandal, and the company has appointed finance chief Colette Kress as interim chief executive.

The company issued this statement just now, after its board met last night:

double quotation markThe Board of Directors announced today that Jensen Huang has resigned as President, Chief Executive Officer, and a member of the Board, effective immediately.

Colette Kress, current Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer of NVIDIA, has agreed to serve as Interim Chief Executive Officer. Kress will continue to serve as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer while serving as Interim Chief Executive Officer until a permanent successor has been named. The Board is initiating a search for a permanent Chief Executive Officer and will consider internal and external candidates.

double quotation markColette Kress is well respected by investors and should be able to guide Nvidia through this challenging period. The task is daunting, as Nvidia has been founder-led since inception and Mr Huang concentrated much of the decision making.

Ben Barringer, head of technology research at Quilter Cheviot, said:

double quotation markJensen Huang clearly saw the writing was on the wall and has made the decision to resign as chief executive of Nvidia. This is an important moment in broader financial markets due to the clout that Huang carried, and the fact he had developed Nvidia into the most valuable and influential company in the world.

It remains to be seen if Huang will eventually stick around in some sort of ceremonial or non-executive capacity. It would be difficult to see him not retaining some sort of influence over Nvidia, but we do not yet know the full details of the allegations or the outcome of the investigation, and much of what happens next still depends on that.

For now, CFO Colette Kress has taken the role on an interim position. While there is no natural successor in place, she is the most obvious internal candidate and will now likely use this time to prove she deserves it full time. She certainly knows the company inside out and has helped develop what is a hugely successful strategy.

This doesn’t necessarily harm Nvidia in the short-term. Orders remain strong and the company has a robust earnings outlook. What this news threatens to do is harm the wider AI ecosystem in the longer-term. Huang is hugely influential in the debates around AI regulation and has developed Nvidia into the leader in AI investment and financing. With his voice now removed from the table, and the potential for Nvidia to pivot on its strategy over time, the company may not carry the sway it once did.

Turning to the wider outlook for technology firms, he said:

double quotation markThis news ultimately pours petrol onto what is already a volatile picture for tech companies following the midterm elections. Nvidia and its neocloud partners are likely to bear the initial brunt of this news, while competitors like AMD and Broadcom benefit. That said, this news has the potential to be game changing over the long-term with a key figure out of the picture.

Lindsey Stewart, director of institutional investor content at Morningstar, says governance issues and succession planning, long neglected at Nvidia, will become important for other founder-led companies.

double quotation markThe sudden departure of one of the world’s most influential CEOs illustrates just how quickly an unconventional governance structure can become a source of risk, and often real losses, for investors. While Nvidia’s share price rocketed over the last few years, very few people were asking deep questions about CEO succession planning, board composition and challenge, or oversight of geopolitically sensitive aspects of company strategy. Now, all those issues will be front and centre, perhaps not just for Nvidia, but also for other founder-led companies like Anthropic, Meta Platforms and SpaceX.
