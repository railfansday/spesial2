Zac Affleck Breaks Silence on Jen Affleck Divorce Amid Her Pregnancy

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

After Secret Lives of Mormon Wives' Jen Affleck filed for divorce from Zac Affleck, he addressed the dissolution of their marriage following seven years and amid her fourth pregnancy.

Jen Affleck and Zac Affleck's new reality is focusing on their kids.

After the Secret Lives of Mormon Wives star filed to end her marriage of seven years Nov. 3 per court documents obtained by E! News, he spoke out on where they stand, telling People Nov. 5 that he'll “always be Jen’s supporter.”

Zac added, “Hope she is happy in this next phase of life."

He also addressed how they’ll remain dedicated parents to their three children—Nora, 5, Luca, 3, and Penelope, 16 months—in addition to a fourth on the way, saying that he's “just focusing on my kids and figuring out what’s next for me.”

And while the split follows eight years together and an announcement of baby No. 4 in June, Jen had previously shared they were navigating difficulties "behind closed doors" while entering the new era as a family.

"It’s been a lot the past couple of months, I’ll be honest," she told People in August after their move to Orange County, Calif. "But I do feel like I’m seeing the light at the end of the tunnel and just trying to count my blessings every single day."

Around the same time, the 27-year-old also shared on Instagram that she was experiencing prenatal depression. Amid the health challenges—which also included dental surgery—she praised her husband for getting her through the first trimester, admitting to the outlet, "I could not be there for my kids, and so Zac really had to show up," though she added, "he didn’t really have a choice."

Beyond recent obstacles, Jen and Zac's marital ups and downs were a core part of their storyline on the Hulu hit. Following the show's debut season in 2024, they separated after arguing about Jen's role as the breadwinner and influencer friend group, in addition to Zac gambling in Las Vegas on the show.

However, during season two, the Dancing With the Stars alum and the medical student rebuilt their relationship while championing therapy, and soon announced their pregnancy with Penelope in February 2025.

Now, with the divorce update, viewers will see Jen's perspective leading up to the split on The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County, though according to TMZ, Zac ultimately did not film any scenes for the spinoff.

But where the rest of the SLOMW: OC relationships stand is no secret. Read on...

Following her divorce from Cory Althoff, podcaster Bobbi Althoff is dating Tyler Hawkins, who plays a special role in Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County.

"He styles me, that’s how he supports me,” she told E! News in May, adding that the basketball player, "picks out my outfits every day. He styles me every day for the show. It’s great."

Aspyn Ovard, who filed for divorce from Parker Ferris in 2024 after eight years of marriage, is currently in a throuple relationship with married couple Billy Davis and Bri Davis.

After many ups and downs, Jen Affleck—who is pregnant with her and Zac Affleck's fourth baby—officially filed for divorce from her husband of seven years, E! News confirmed Nov. 4.

Despite ongoing relationship speculation, Salomé Andrea and Aaron Brimhall are still going strong as they prepare to welcome their third baby together. The duo, who married in 2012, also share 8-year-old twins Luca and Rue.

McCall DaPron—the sister of Mormon Wives' Mayci Neeley—shares four kids with husband Kirk DaPron, who she wed in 2011.

Ashleigh Pease and Nate Pease are rock solid after over a decade of marriage. "This 13th year together is one that has felt full circle!" she wrote on Instagram in September. "A season of fruits. Comfort. The happiest milestones."

Madison Bontempo and Kyler Steven Fisher, who wed in 2013, have been open about the highs and lows of their relationship. "Kyler and I aren’t perfect," she wrote on Instagram in October. "We’ve had arguments. We’ve had hard seasons. We’ve had moments we wish we could take back. And we’re not afraid to admit that."

But, she added, "Those moments aren’t our whole story. There’s also laughter, friendship, forgiveness, growth, five beautiful children, a million memories, and a love that has continued to evolve through every season of our lives."

Avery Woods and David Woods, parents to Ziggy and Stevie, are still feeling the love after a decade of marriage. As she simply put it, "my forever + always."
