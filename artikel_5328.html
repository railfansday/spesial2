Garrett KochOwner, Koch & Associates, Inc.Prepare Your Soil Before PlantingProper soil preparation can make a big difference in the long-term health of new plants. For example, removing the native soil and replacing it with nutrient-rich compost creates a more fertile environment for roots to expand. Slow-release fertilizer tablets can also provide consistent nutrients during the early growth stage.

Juan OsorioOwner, J.O Roofing Inc.Health Benefits of Roof ReplacementAn old roof can negatively impact your indoor air quality by allowing dust and pollutants to circulate inside your home. These contaminants can lead to health concerns that are often mistaken for weather-related sickness, so it's worthwhile to check the condition of your roof and replace it if necessary.
