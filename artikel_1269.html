He Wants to Join: Man Utd 'Hold Talks' With Man City to Sign Ait-Nouri

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Manchester United could be ready to raid rivals Manchester City as they look to boost Mauricio Pochettino's squad in 2027, according to a report.

Despite signing three new midfielders at a cost of more than £150m in the summer, following on from a rebuild of their attack the previous summer, the current Man United squad looks nowhere near good enough.

11 matches into the Premier League season and they have one win and lie 19th in the standings, which shows how poor they have been.

Pochettino has been tasked with turning their campaign around and lifting them up the table, but his reign got off to the worst possible start when they were reduced to 10 men eight minutes in away at rivals Liverpool on Sunday, going on to lose 4-0 on Merseyside.

Aside from Casemiro, the current Red Devils' squad is the same as the one that went on a stunning run of form in the first half of 2026 to finish third behind Arsenal and Manchester City in the top flight, qualifying for the Champions League as a result.

Many of those players look a shadow of the ones that achieved that placing, and it has shown with their performances and results so far this term.

The lack of a recognised number nine in the absence of the injured Benjamin Sesko is something they will look to put right in January, with the Times claiming that it is their top priority for the winter window.

Defence is clearly an issue too though, with only Lens conceding more goals than United's 34 this term from teams who make up the top five leagues in Europe.

The defensive rebuild was on the agenda for the summer but it appears as though it will be brought forward, as club chiefs look at signing a centre-back and left-back hybrid, or potentially even one for each position.

GIVEMESPORT senior correspondent Ben Jacobs reported on deadline day in the summer that United had made a late enquiry for Man City left-back Rayan Ait-Nouri, although no move materialised.

Now, TEAMtalk claim that the Red Devils have once again reached out to their neighbours about the Algeria international, with a switch to Old Trafford said to hold high appeal for the former Wolves star.

According to the report, Ait-Nouri has been informed that he can leave the Etihad in January if the deal is right, but Jacobs revealed in September that United were unwilling to agree to City's demands for a loan move with an obligation to buy.

It will be interesting to see if they have changed their stance and if they would be open to a straight loan with an option, because that is something that would surely appeal to the Red Devils given there are suggestions that their budget could be limited mid-season.

Ait-Nouri has struggled to force his way into the City XI ever since joining in a deal worth up to £36m from Wolves, but he does have extensive Premier League experience, he has played in the Champions League, and he has the full-back traits that Pochettino likes.

The 25-year-old may count himself unlucky at how limited his minutes have been at the Etihad, especially this season, but he established himself as one of the best in his position during his time at Molineux previously.

While United are being linked with the likes of Antonee Robinson of Fulham and Barcelona's Alejandro Balde, with Ben Jacobs naming a £75m star as another 'one to watch', Ait-Nouri is a strong option to finally provide some strong competition for Luke Shaw.

Shaw has once again shown this term that the club cannot afford to rely on his fitness, even if he could be back on the pitch soon after an eight-match absence.
