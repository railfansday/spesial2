Rashford Ready for New Man Utd Role, Pochettino is Obsessed With Him

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Manchester United are set to embark on a new era under Mauricio Pochettino, after the Argentine coach was announced as the new manager of the football club.

Pochettino has signed a two-and-a-half-year deal at Old Trafford to replace Michael Carrick, who was officially sacked on Thursday morning following a run of just one win in 10 Premier League games so far this season, which has left the team sitting 18th in the table.

The 54-year-old will be returning to the dugout of a club side for the first time since leaving Chelsea in the summer of 2024, and his first game in charge of the Red Devils will see him take his side to Anfield against fierce rivals Liverpool.

One of the biggest tasks Pochettino will have will be to sort out the defence, which has conceded 30 goals in all competitions this season, more than any other side in Europe's top five leagues. But he must also sort out the lack of goals in attack, with the current forward options struggling for consistency under the previous regime.

Benjamin Sesko is considered to be the first-choice striker at Old Trafford, but he has been ruled out with a recurring shin injury until the new year at the earliest, while Amad hasn't scored a goal for the club in almost a full calendar year, and Joshua Zirkzee's future at the club is up in the air after scoring just 11 goals in 85 appearances since joining.

But it has now emerged that Pochettino has already been looking closely at the squad, and Marcus Rashford is a player that he is looking forward to working with more than any other.

According to The Telegraph, Pochettino is expected to put his arm around the England winger and help him to get back to his best form, having seen his quality at Barcelona last season, showing that he is far from being finished at the top level.

Rashford has missed the last month of action with a hip and groin problem, but there is hope that he will be able to get back to fitness and return for the clash at Anfield for Pochettino's first game in charge. But according to Fabrizio Romano, speaking in his latest YouTube video, there's a chance that the academy graduate could find himself in a new role as the number nine.

"I want to mention specifically the situation of one player as well: Marcus Rashford," he said.

"Marcus Rashford, for Pochettino, is not a normal player. Mauricio Pochettino, when he was Paris Saint-Germain manager, was trying in every way to sign Marcus Rashford. Pochettino was obsessed with Rashford. Can play as a nine, can play as a winger. He loves Rashford.

"Obviously, Marcos had a difficult start of the season physically, in the sense that he had to deal with some struggles, and so he was not in the best physical condition possible. But my understanding is that Mauricio Pochettino is a big, big fan of Marcos Rashford. So this could be a surprise, Rashford under Pochettino, because this start of the season was difficult.

"But I'm sure, I'm sure Pochettino will try his best to get the best out of Marcus Rashford, because he's a player he already wanted at PSG, a player he always wanted with him.

United's attack has got plenty of quality, but one thing it lacks is genuine pace. Matheus Cunha is not the quickest off the ball, while Mbeumo is fine running without the ball but is a bit slower with it, which often means he can't beat his opponents one-on-one.

Rashford is a gamechanger in that sense. He has the most explosive acceleration of any of the forwards in the squad, and his ball striking is second to none too. If given the chances, he can find the net, and Pochettino will be desperate to get him into those positions to be able to do just that.

Prior to his injury this season, Rashford looked sharp and was robbed of assists by his teammates missing absolutely guilt-edged opportunities. If he is put in the position to finish those chances, United would be better off. Pochettino knows what he wants to do, and believes Rashford can be a huge help to achieving his goals.
