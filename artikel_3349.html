Ancestry Launches Black Friday Deal — Save Up to 70%

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Rolling Stone may receive an affiliate commission.

Genetic testing kits are popular for a number of reasons — some people are looking to screen for health conditions, while others want to learn more about their family. Ancestry has become one of the most popular options in the latter category, allowing users to connect with family members and build their own family trees.

Ancestry can also make a surprisingly thoughtful gift. Right now, Ancestry is offering its gift memberships for 50% off ahead of the holiday season. There are three tiers to choose from, all of which are discounted right now. Here’s what to know about Ancestry’s deals.

Ancestry’s DNA gift subscriptions are broken into U.S. Discovery, World Explorer, and All Access tiers, each of which is available for 50% off for a one-year plan. U.S. Discovery includes access to records from across all 50 states, while World Explorer includes access to U.S. and international records from over 80 countries. All Access, meanwhile, adds access to military records on Fold3 and articles on Newspapers.com.

The memberships include access to family records, newspaper clippings, and more, but they do not include the genetic testing kit itself. The kits are discounted during Black Friday as well, with discounts of up to 70% off. The test gets you a detailed origins report and connections to genetic relatives, including a pie chart breaking down your ancestral origins.

The results can also break down family origins into specific regions, even down to cities in some cases. Results can potentially include a timeline of familial migration, sometimes down to when and even why your family moved.

If you’re looking for a unique gift, Ancestry is worth considering. But these deals are limited; Ancestry’s gift membership promo ends on Dec. 1, while the 70% off deal on the Ancestry kit ends on Dec. 31.
