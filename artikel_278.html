21 classic Black Friday deals that are live now

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Having covered many a Black Friday, I'm very familiar with all the classic deals. Whenever the sale kicks off, I know that boring essentials like Crest Whitestrips will be majorly marked down, and so will the flashier items like Sony headphones.

This Black Friday, our favorite deals are back. In spotting the very best sales on offer, we've rounded up classic deals that we're always happy to see discounted. And this list may as well be a gift guide of its own, as stocking stuffers like LifeStraws are now on sale along with bigger-ticket items like MacBooks, KitchenAid mixers, and Kindle Paperwhites.

So if you're looking for what to shop during Black Friday, don't skip the classics. These are some of our favorite Black Friday deals that are marked down now and worth your attention.

Black Friday isn't just about the big flashy tech; it's about stocking up on the unsexy essentials too. Crest 3D Whitestrips are the prime example of that. We use them to get our teeth looking camera-ready, but they're expensive, except during Black Friday when they reliably go on sale. Catch them on sale and save $16 on a pack of 22 treatments.

We always count on seeing substantial discounts on Echo devices during Black Friday, but this year the deals are out of this world. Not only are the previous generation of Echos on sale, but the 2026 models are too. Save 50% on the 2022 and 2026 Echo Dot. The previous gen is down to $39.99, while the 2026 Echo Dot is $49.99.

There's a world of earbuds out there, but AirPods remain a Black Friday classic. For iOS users, they connect to Apple devices right away, and in the case of the Apple AirPods Pro 3, they bring active noise cancellation, hearing aid features, and live translation. Plus, AirPods reliably go on sale for new lows during Black Friday. This year we've spotted the AirPods Pro 3 for just $149, which is a total steal.

Nintendo Switch bundles feel like the epitome of a Black Friday deal — one that you might have busted down the doors of Best Buy for. This Nintendo Switch 2 and Mario Kart World bundle gives us that feeling. During Black Friday, this bundle is now on sale for $499, saving you $50.99, a quintessential Black Friday deal.

The flashy beauty tech from Shark is a splurge most of the year, but during Black Friday it's a wise purchase with its steep discounts. The FlexStyle is a classic Black Friday deal that some of us Mashable reporters have even taken advantage of ourselves (guilty). But this year's most sought-after deal is on the CryoGlow Face Mask, a red light LED mask with cooling capabilities.

If you've been wanting to try out a streaming service, Black Friday is the time to do it, as there are a plethora of deals to shop. HBO Max always offers a steep discount, and Black Friday 2026 is no different. Shop the HBO Max basic plan with ads for just $5.49 per month this Black Friday, saving you 50% on the streamer for a whole year.
