Two PGA TOUR Spots Loom For Walker Cup Teammates

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Amateur golf rarely offers a prize this sweet, yet the ITG Tournament of Champions is about to hand out two full PGA TOUR starts. Between December 14 and December 16, 2026, the Ocean Club Golf Course on Paradise Island will host an event that offers rewards exceeding a gold medal. Grayson Wood already secured one of these golden tickets by winning the inaugural event last December, but the second spot remains wide open for the current field.

Tyler Watts, Miles Russell, and Kihei Akina are the names to watch as they return to the Bahamas to chase that elusive second exemption. The tournament structure creates a unique pressure cooker where the runner-up finish carries real weight. According to AmateurGolf.com, the Myrtle Beach Classic and the Puerto Rico Open are the specific events on offer for the champion and the runner-up. This setup forces elite amateurs to treat every shot with the intensity of a major championship final round.

History shows just how much the second spot matters when the leaderboard tightens. Last year, Grayson Wood shot an 8-under 208 to claim his prize, leaving a group of four players tied at 7-under 209. That tie triggered a four-for-one playoff for the remaining exemption, turning a standard amateur tournament into a do-or-die battle. North Carolina's Carson Bertagnole was one of those players who had to fight for his spot. He made two crucial pars to stay alive alongside Tyler Kowack, eventually securing the opportunity.

When tournament media asked if he knew about the event offering two PGA TOUR spots, he gave a short, grounded answer. "No. But I'm not going to complain," Bertagnole told tournament media. That line sums up the mindset required here. The players know the odds are stacked against them, yet the reward is too big to ignore.

The tournament organizers have designed a format that guarantees tension right until the final putt drops. Beginning with a 36-hole competition day and concluding with an 18-hole final round, the tournament comprises 54 holes of stroke play. Ocean Club, a Tom Weiskopf design, plays as a par 72 stretching 7,159 yards. The champion and the runner-up will receive the two sponsor exemptions, making every hole on the final day a direct path to the professional ranks. According to AmateurGolf.com, the White Sands Bahamas Men's Invitational first appeared in 2025 utilizing its individual amateur format.

In 2025, the first individual edition drew 19 players from the global top 100 and secured the ninth spot among men's amateur tournaments via WAGR rating. Those figures are the organizer's reported assessment, but the results speak for themselves.

Walker Cup teammates head back to the ITG Tournament of Champions from December 14–16, 2026, where two PGA TOUR starts are on offer.
