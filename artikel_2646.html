Chiefs vs. Cardinals: Mahomes finds Kelce for second TD in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Kansas City Chiefs are off to an impressive start in their Week 11 matchup against the Arizona Cardinals at Arrowhead Stadium.

The Chiefs' offense has kept its foot on the pedal in Sunday's interconference showdown and shows no signs of letting up.

Legendary tight end Travis Kelce shook off some of the early-season rust as he notched his second touchdown of the day on a 22-yard pitch-and-catch from quarterback Patrick Mahomes to increase Kansas City's lead to 17.

Take a look at this video of Kelce's second touchdown snag, which was posted to Twitter by the Chiefs' official account:

The 13-year veteran has had a slow start to the season through the first 10 weeks, but has seemingly hit his stride against the Cardinals.

Kelce will look to ride his hot hand to a win over Arizona before facing the Buffalo Bills in an exciting matchup on Thanksgiving Day.
