Seahawks QB Drew Lock leads NFL in key stat for Week 10 prior to MNF

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Seattle Seahawks quarterback Drew Lock leads the NFL in a very telling stat for Week 10 thus far.

According to Pro Football Focus, Lock leads NFL in passer rating through the Sunday slate of games with a 158 passer rating.

San Francisco 49ers QB Brock Purdy ranks No. 2 for the week at 154.5, followed by Kansas City Chiefs superstar QB Patrick Mahomes (135.5), Los Angeles Rams star and reigning AP MVP Matthew Stafford (122.1), and Jacksonville Jaguars star QB Trevor Lawrence (120.2).

Lock delivered yet another efficient performance in the Seahawks' 21-13 victory over the Las Vegas Raiders, as he completed 14 of 17 passes for 240 yards and two touchdowns without throwing an interception. Lock came into the matchup fresh off of a win over the Arizona Cardinals in Week 9 and maintained his ball security and efficiency against the Raiders while strengthening his credentials as a dependable replacement for injured starter Sam Darnold.

The veteran Lock had already led the league in passer rating earlier this season, making Sunday's performance another indication of his effectiveness when called upon. He has shown that he is capable of leading Seattle to wins behind his efficient play. Seahawks coach Mike Macdonald stated after the loss to the Lions that Darnold is still the team's starting quarterback amid a wave of support for Lock given his winning play, but Lock's play still makes him a great option for the team to turn to when needed.

For a Seahawks team navigating Darnold's absence, that reliability provides meaningful insurance for them as their Super Bowl hopes remain alive.
