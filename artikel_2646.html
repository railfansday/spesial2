Sterling Shepard makes most of Jets chance with breakout performance after ‘roller-coaster’ season

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

LOS ANGELES — Good things happen for those who wait patiently.

Jets receiver Sterling Shepard has patiently waited his turn, mostly on the practice squad this season.

And there he was Sunday, catching two touchdown passes from quarterback Geno Smith in the Jets' 27-24 win over the Chargers at SoFi Stadium.

For the 33-year-old Shepard, on his third team of an 11-year career, Sunday marked the first time in his 120-game career that he scored two TDs in one game.

Shepard, who played his first eight seasons with the Giants, had not had more than one TD reception in an entire season since 2020.

"The Energizer Bunny, that's who he is,'' Jets head coach Aaron Glenn said. "He has so much juice, and he loves playing the game. I'm glad we have someone like that who practices his ass off every week.

"And the thing about him is, he's been inactive all these weeks, and he's the same guy week in and week out. So I'm happy he had a chance to come in and make the plays he did.''

Shepard's first TD catch gave the Jets a 17-3 lead in the second quarter. He celebrated madly in the end zone — as he should have given his resilience.

"I don't even have to tell you,'' Shepard said when asked how good it felt. "Y'all already know how it felt, man. It's been a roller coaster for me — going to the Texans [earlier this season], getting cut by them after six days, coming over here, being on the practice squad, getting activated for two games.

"It's a true testament to the Lord and to being patient, bro.''

Shepard joked that, on the first TD, "I knew it was going to be a touchdown; I didn't know it was going to go to me. I'm like fourth in the progression. Nine times out of 10, that ball is going to G-5 [Garrett Wilson] on the other side.

Sterling Shepard reacts after a win against the Los Angeles Chargers at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, Calif., Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. Getty Images

"Then I looked up and thought, 'Oh wait, Geno's coming over here.' I won my one-on-one and was able to find a little pocket. It was fun.''

Wilson said, "I just love Shep. I love his energy. I haven't known him for a long time, but I feel like he's a good friend of mine. Man, he can ball. To see him do what he did today, he's just a grinder, and he deserves it. He's got great hands — special hands.''

It'll be interesting to see what Glenn does with Shepard from here. He was playing because Omar Cooper Jr. and Isaiah Williams were out with injuries.

Shepard, who hadn't played since Week 4 and had just one catch for 3 yards in Week 3, is out of designations from the practice squad to the regular roster. Teams get only three per player, which means Shepard should stay on the regular roster.

New York Jets wide receiver Sterling Shepard (87) tosses the ball after scoring a touchdown against the Los Angeles Chargers in Inglewood, Calif., Nov. 22, 2026. AP Photo/Gregory Bull

"This game will humble you,'' Shepard said of his time on the practice squad. "I go out there every week and try to give our defense the best look possible [on the scout team]. They get tired of me at times.

"But I go out there, try to do my job and wait for my opportunity. When my opportunity came, I knew I had to be ready for it. And the only way to be ready is to take care of business every single day, whether you're on the practice squad or wherever you are on the depth chart.''
