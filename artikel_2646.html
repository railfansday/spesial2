MN Vikings Hopeful on Injury Front

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Christian Darrisaw left Sunday night's game in Mexico City twice. The Minnesota Vikings' left tackle went to the sideline with a shoulder injury in the first half, came back after halftime and was ruled out in the fourth quarter of their 35-10 loss to the San Francisco 49ers.

Now, after dropping three of their last four, the Vikings are 7-3 and looking to turn the momentum back in their favor Sunday against the Atlanta Falcons (3-7), who have lost three straight and are coming off their bye week.

Wednesday's practice was just a walkthrough, but Kevin O'Connell said Darrisaw, Harrison Smith, Jordan Addison, Isaiah Rodgers and Jalen Redmond were all estimated non-participants.

The Vikings will hold a walk-through instead of a practice today, but Christian Darrisaw, Harrison Smith, Jordan Addison, Isaiah Rodgers and Jalen Redmond will be estimated as non-participants, Kevin O'Connell said.

— Ben Goessling (@BenGoessling) November 25, 2026

KOC said the Vikings will have full practices Thursday and Friday, and seemed hopeful that most of the DNP's from Wednesday will be ready to roll by Sunday: "Guys are kind of working through things with the intent and the hope of maybe seeing where they can get to by the end of the week."

This isn't the first shoulder issue Darrisaw has dealt with this month. He hurt his other shoulder in the Nov. 1 loss at Detroit and was limited in practice that week, but still played against the Buffalo Bills that Monday.

On Wednesday, O'Connell was whether he was confident that Darrisaw will play Sunday against Atlanta. If nothing else, it appears the injury is not longterm.

"Yeah, I think it's early. It's early to tell right now one way or the other, but I would say we definitely don't have long-term fears of this. And it's something that we're kind of dealing with day to day."

Darrisaw has played in nine of Minnesota's 10 games this season. The only one he missed was the Oct. 11 game at New Orleans, where Ryan Van Demark played all 71 offensive snaps at left tackle in a 23-13 win.

Against the 49ers, Darrisaw had an 89.8 pass-blocking grade from PFF and didn't allow a pressure on 28 pass-blocking snaps. Van Demark, who replaced him, had a 21.2 pass-blocking grade and gave up three pressures on 19 pass-blocking snaps.

Nobody reported an in-game injury for Smith against San Francisco. When a reporter asked whether the veteran safety had an injury or a vet rest day, O'Connell said:

"He had a little soreness coming out of the game, but yeah … I think if you asked him, he probably does most, most days, so he'll work through it and we'll see where he's at, but very hopeful on Harry."

Smith has played in all 10 games this season, with 21 tackles, half a sack, two interceptions and five passes defensed. Redmond is dealing with an ankle injury, Kevin Seifert (ESPN) reported.

The defensive lineman has also played in all 10 games, with 32 tackles, two sacks, 6.5 tackles for loss, a forced fumble and four passes defensed. He had four tackles, two for loss, against San Francisco.

Isaiah Rodgers has a quad injury. The cornerback was added to the injury report on Nov. 19, was limited the last two days at practice last week, then was made inactive against the 49ers. In nine games, Rodgers has 29 tackles, an interception, a fumble recovery and five passes defensed.

Addison has missed the last two games with a thumb injury he suffered in practice on Nov. 13. He had surgery three days later, and O'Connell said Nov. 18 that the receiver will not go on short-term injured reserve for now.

On Monday, O'Connell said he doesn't "necessarily expect to have him this week," but that Addison is "progressing really well for having just gotten that thing fixed." Addison has 18 catches for 277 yards and a touchdown in seven games this season.
