DJ Uiagalelei to start in Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Los Angeles Chargers QB DJ Uiagalelei will start against the New England Patriots in Week 12 after QB Justin Herbert (concussion) was ruled out.

Why it Matters: Uiagalelei will be making his first NFL start, and while the Patriots haven't exactly been a defensive juggernaut, it is tough to be optimistic. The Chargers have been struggling on offensive all season, and that is unlikely to change with Uiagalelei under center.
