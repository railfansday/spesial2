Patriots vs. Lions recap: Drake Maye injury overshadows 35-10 loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

It was a disaster in Germany for the New England Patriots.

While the Patriots dropped their Week 10 contest 35-10 to the Detroit Lions, the biggest hit again came in the injury department as quarterback Drake Maye was ruled out with an ankle injury he suffered on the first drive of the game.

Without Maye, the Tommy DeVito-led Patriots offense struggled to move the football as they put together just one scoring drive compared to seven three-and-outs. Even on their lone scoring drive, wide receiver A.J. Brown departed the game with a hamstring injury.

Meanwhile, the Patriots defense continued their recent struggles as they allowed nearly 500 yards of total offense behind Jahmyr Gibbs rumbling for 252 total yards and a pair of scores.

A tough season continues to for the Patriots, who now head towards a much needed bye week at 5-5.

Sunday, Nov. 15, 9:30 a.m. ET | Allianz Arena (Munich, Germany) | Broadcast information | Game day roster | Inactives | Weather forecast

The Patriots lost the coin toss which led to Drake Maye and the offense starting things off in Germany. New England's offense moved the ball well to quickly work into the red zone behind a 26-yard flea flicker to Mack Hollins. Two pre-snap penalties and a third-sack in the red zone by Aidan Hutchinson working against Will Campbell, however, limited the group to an opening drive field goal. [Patriots 3 : 0 Lions]

After Detroit picked up an opening first-down, Jared Goff hit Amon-Ra St. Brown short of the sticks on third-down before the receiver made Christian Gonzalez miss to take it 56 yards to the house. [Patriots 3 : 7 Lions]

Tommy DeVito took over at quarterback on the Patriots following drive as Drake Maye made his way to the locker room with an ankle injury. A defensive pass interference converted one third down before DeVito was sacked on the following third down.

Starting with their backs in their own end zone, Detroit got their run game rolling on their following possession as Jahmyr Gibbs logged an explosive stretch run before Sione Vaki carried the group across midfield. With the backs brining them into the red zone, Goff found Isaac TeSlaa for a 12-yard touchdown. [Patriots 3 : 14 Lions]

The Patriots offense got nothing going on their next drive as it started with a five-yard tackle for loss and then a drop by A.J. Brown. They punted after a short scramble from DeVito.

New England's defense finally got off the field as a third down sack by Milton Williams forced a punt.

With DeVito still in the game, the Patriots offense went backwards on their first two offensive plays which led to another three-and-out.

Gibbs moved the chains on the ground before the Patriots set Detroit up in a 3rd-and-9. Goff's pass was then intercepted by Christian Gonzalez.

The Patriots took over at their own 39-yard line following the interception. With Maye (ankle) ruled out for the remainder of the game, DeVito still could not get anything going as his third-down pass to Brown was broken up. New England punted again.

Jahmyr Gibbs' impact shifted to the pass game on Detroit's next drive as he converted a pair of third downs with receptions of 22 and 50 yards to set the Lions up at the five-yard line. One play later Gibbs then found the end zone with his legs. [Patriots 3 : 21 Lions]

Taking over with 2:06 left in the half, the Patriots converted their first first down with DeVito under center. Disaster then continued as DeVito was sacked before throwing an interception over the middle of the field.

In New England territory with 38 seconds remaining, the Lions needed just four plays to quickly work inside the five-yard line. Kevin Byard then stopped Detroit from adding onto their lead as he broke up a touchdown in the end zone before intercepting Goff. New England took a knee to end the half.

The Lions started the second half with the football while leading 21-3. New England's defense got a much needed three-and-out as Goff's third down pass fell incomplete.

New England started their first drive of the second half with two straight run plays for minimal yardage. After Brown was called for a false start DeVito's third down pass to Kyle Williams fell to the turf as the receiver was led to far inside and took a big hit.

Gibbs continued to carve up the Patriots defense as he moved the Lions offense into New England territory. Facing a 3rd-and-9 from the 28-yard line, a screen to St. Brown resulted in the receiver finding the end zone for the second time today. [Patriots 3 : 28 Lions]

The Patriots went three-and-out yet again as another hospital ball from DeVito to Efton Chism III fell incomplete on third down.

After picking up an initial first down with Sam Laporta picking up 16 yards against Darius Muasau, who was in for Robert Spillane, a third down holding penalty then put the Lions behind the sticks and forced a punt.

A.J. Brown recorded his first catch of the game — a six-yard slant — on the following drive after recovering a first down fumble by Reggie Gilliam. After a strong 21-yard gain on the ground by Kyle Williams was wiped out due to penalty, Brown converted the third down on another slant which he took for 21 yards. The receiver then went into the blue medical tent with a hamstring injury. New England then slowly worked their way to the red zone before Williams drew a DPI to set the offense up at the 15-yard line. From there, DeVito found Lan Larison for his first career touchdown. [Patriots 10 : 28 Lions]

An illegal kick started the Lions next drive at their own 40-yard line. Detroit marched right down the field from there before Gibbs ended things with a 28-yard touchdown. [Patriots 10 : 35 Lions]

The Patriots went three-and-out as DeVito did not see an open Kyle Williams downfield on third down and instead saw a check down fall incomplete.

Joshua Dobbs took over for Jared Goff with seven minutes left in the fourth quarter. A first down offensive penalty set them behind the sticks which led to a punt.

New England went three-and-out again as DeVito was sacked on third-down.

The Lions killed the remainder of the clock.
