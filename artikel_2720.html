Chargers’ Daiyan Henley excited for 1st win after nearly being traded to Broncos

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Los Angeles Chargers became the second team since the 2013 Tampa Bay Buccaneers to enter a Monday Night Football matchup with a 0-8 record or worse and come away with a victory. Despite their difficult start to the season, the Chargers finally got things going, defeating the Baltimore Ravens 23-20 on Monday. The much-needed first win clearly meant a lot to linebacker Daiyan Henley, who had nearly been traded to the Denver Broncos.

Following the game, Henley expressed his excitement about finally getting the victory.

"It's definitely exciting," Henley said. "I think you know, everybody was feeding for one, but I think we also know that this feeling is something that we want to keep going, man, so one can lead to two, and we got a short week, so that's the best part of all of this."

The win carried extra significance for a Chargers team that had struggled to deliver consistent performances on both sides of the ball.

Henley also opened up about his commitment to his teammates, saying that his motivation throughout the difficult stretch has been to give everything he has for the people around him.

"I think I made it really clear when I got back in the building that I'm gonna do it for my teammates," he added. "I love them, they love me. And that's what tonight was for, man."

On Monday, Los Angeles capitalized on Baltimore's defensive difficulties to maintain its advantage and secure the three-point victory. The Chargers' defense was particularly dominant, holding the Ravens to zero rushing yards in the first half, the fewest by Baltimore in a first half in franchise history.

The victory brought an end to the Chargers' eight-game losing streak and finally put their first win of the season on the board. Though one result cannot erase the struggles of their opening eight games, it gives Los Angeles a much-needed starting point to build momentum for the remainder of the season.
