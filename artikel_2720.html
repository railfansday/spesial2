Liverpool star admits Iraola giving him real confidence

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

There was something particularly compelling about watching Florian Wirtz dismantle Manchester United on Sunday. Liverpool's £116million investment was beginning to make sense in the most emphatic fashion, his intelligence and imagination providing the creative foundation for a comprehensive 4-0 victory.

For Wirtz, the process of adaptation has required patience. Andoni Iraola has helped accelerate that development, while Jurgen Klopp, somewhat fittingly, continues to exert an influence on Liverpool's fortunes from afar.

Speaking ahead of Liverpool's Champions League encounter with Club Brugge, the German star said:

"I am really enjoying playing under him," Wirtz told reporters.

"He is a really good coach. He has shown that his whole career as a manager. He is bringing me back into the place where I was maybe two years ago where I played really good football.

"I can feel that he gives me confidence and I am really happy about that. I am just trying to give him back what he gives me. In the last few games I got a lot of goal contributions and I think you could say that I am in the best form since I arrived."

Iraola appears to have established an environment in which Wirtz feels sufficiently comfortable to express himself. His 29 chances created from open play, more than any other Premier League player this season, illustrate the extent of his influence.

English football places considerable physical demands on creative players. Wirtz has gradually developed an understanding of how to navigate those challenges.

"I cannot really say that I have changed much," he added. "I think that I have become a bit better on a physical level. I also started to know better the English league and I am feeling very good.

"I know that offensive players get judged by numbers but for me it's just about the success of the team. If I see a team-mate in a better position I give them the ball and I hope they also do that with me.

"I always believed in myself so I never got too annoyed (with criticism). I knew my qualities and I know them now."

Klopp's relationship with Wirtz through the German national team offers Liverpool an intriguing indirect benefit. The midfielder scored three goals during the recent international break.

"Jurgen and I always speak about Liverpool when we have the time to do so," he said. "He had a great time here. He stayed here the most in his career and he tells me all the time that he loved it. The people love him too.

"He tries to give me some advice to be successful because he knows how it's done. It is really good for me that he knows a lot about this club. He gives me good advice. Small things, private things. He has experienced it."
