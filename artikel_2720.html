PFT's Week 11 2026 NFL picks

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

After going 27-2 between Week 8 and Week 9, I regressed to the mean with a 9-5 week. It was inevitable.

This week, with six teams on byes, there are only 13 games. Which means that, at most, I’ll get 13 of them wrong.

For the year, the record is now 96-53-1. That’s a success rate of 64 percent.

At 3-6, time is running out for the Texans. If they can’t beat the Colts at home, the Texans have no shot at pulling themselves back into the race for a wild-card berth.

Buffalo has a rough stretch coming. This is the worst time to step into a trap. Fortunately, the Dolphins may not be talented enough to take advantage of the opportunity to catch the Bills napping.

It’s desperation time for Baltimore. If they can’t win this one, they have little or no chance of closing the gap with the Browns for the No. 7 seed.

It’s a big week for the Bears, who can’t afford to look past the Saints as Chicago prepares for a Thanksgiving showdown in Detroit.

Tony Pollard’s return to Dallas will give the Tennessee offense a lift, and the Titans generally have played better than the Cowboys in recent weeks.

It’s a trap for the Lions, and they know it’s a trap. While the game could leave them a little bruised and battered for the Thanksgiving visit from the Bears, the Lions will do whatever they have to do to get to 8-2.

They’re getting the Cardinals at the perfect time. Then again, pretty much any time anyone gets the Cardinals is the perfect time.

Pittsburgh has no chance at holding its ticket to another quick exit from the playoffs if it can’t win the games it’s supposed to win.

The Chargers have a chance to get on a little bit of a winning streak against a team that can’t figure out how to get off a losing streak.

Fernando Mendoza’s three-week “Welcome to the NFL” continues.

Another week, another opportunity for Jacksonville to convince more people that they are elite.

Vikings vs. 49ers (Sunday night; Mexico City)

Brian Flores will hold the San Francisco offense sufficiently in check for Kirk Cousins to outscore the San Francisco defense.

Joe Burrow keeps thriving in standalone games.
