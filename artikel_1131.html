Bournemouth win their TENTH game in a row after coming from behind - as Brighton thrash Romanian minnows to remain unbeaten in Europe

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

By LUKE POWER, SPORTS REPORTER and BEN WINSTANLEY

Published: 15:09 EST, 26 November 2026 | Updated: 15:15 EST, 26 November 2026

Celta Vigo 1-2 Bournemouth, by Luke Power

Can anybody stop Bournemouth? For long stretches here it looked as if their relentless march would finally be stopped - but the 10-man Cherries found another gear to continue their rampage.

Marco Rose’s men have won 10 games in a row now, extending a club record. For two months, they have steamrolled their opponents with dauntless football and racked up an aggregate score of 25-3. They sit top of the Europa League with a perfect record.

Underneath the red and black armour, fans are starting to wonder if these players are actually of our species. Is Justin Kluivert a man or a cyborg? If you prick James Hill, does he bleed?

With each victory, the cry of the football world grows louder: somebody, please, stop this Bournemouth juggernaut, for the sake of everybody else’s trophy prospects.

Celta Vigo stepped up as the next challengers and thought they would be the heroes. They found Bournemouth’s net four times in all, but were denied by the offside flag for three of them. For long stretches, they were in command.

Bournemouth have now won 10 games in a row after coming from behind to beat Celta Vigo 2-1

After a disappointing first half, Alvaro Rodriguez got them level early in the second

Rayan scored the winner against the run of play as Bournemouth continued their perfect run in the Europa League

In the first half, Bournemouth looked average. They were gifting away the ball in their own half, Celta found ways to sneak through their pincer-movement press, and the Spaniards had the better chances.

The first red flag came 11 minutes in, when Michele Di Gregorio spilled a save into the path of Pablo Duran, who dinked over the goalkeeper but saw his effort ruled out for offside.

Such warning bells were unheeded. Not long later, former Liverpool man Iago Aspas forced a save with a spectacular overhead kick at the age of 39.

Bournemouth failed to clear their lines, and Aspas punished the visitors, ghosting past Alex Scott on his return from injury before blasting beyond Di Gregorio with his left foot.

Things went from bad to worse minutes later when Bafode Diakite was sent off for a stamp on Couhaib Driouech, his yellow card upgraded to a red after a review by referee Harm Osmers.

Celta almost doubled their lead when Duran beat Di Gregorio again, but he had strayed offside.

The cameras panned to Rose, who stood looking bewildered on the sidelines, hands on hips. It took them 37 minutes for Kluivert to register their first shot. Bournemouth had been played off the park. This was a case of clinging on.

But the side that emerged in the second half were unrecognisable. If Bournemouth were a man down, they played with all the swagger of having an extra body.

They were level within two minutes of the restart, Alvaro Rodriguez glancing in from Amine Adli’s header.

Suddenly Bournemouth were their efflorescent selves again. Antonio Silva, Adli, and Rodriguez combined for a beautiful passage of play but were spurned by the offside flag. Hill tested Ionut Radu from range.

They finally got their rewards on the counter-attack, when Marcus Tavernier teed up his fellow substitute Rayan to slot past Radu and stun their hosts. Another comeback job complete.

And so the Bournemouth army marches on unfettered. In the next nine days, they face Fulham, Brighton, and Hull in the Premier League. By the time they visit Arsenal in mid-December, they may be breathing down their necks.

Earlier this week, Brighton CEO Paul Barber revealed the lengths that his club go to before agreeing to sign a player.

'We try and talk to people that know them, people that have coached them, people that played with them, sponsors that have worked with them, media that have interviewed them, maybe even neighbours that have lived next door to them,' he told Men in Blazers media network. 'You know, what sort of people are we talking about? Are we talking about players that are entirely focused on their work?'

There can be little doubt that the rigorous selection process is working, and two of their budget buys were on the scoresheet in the first half as they made light work of the Romanian champions Universitatea Craiova - and continued to make a mockery of the supposed challenge of balancing domestic and European assaults.

In the third minute Ibrahim Osman - £16.6m from Nordsjaelland in 2024 – finished off a sharp team move from close range after a pinpoint Jack Hinshelwood cross. Then shortly after the half-hour mark full back Maxime de Cuyper, £17m from Club Brugge in 2025, seized on a slack defensive ball into midfield, took two touches in advancing forward and - still well outside the box – wrongfooted the goalkeeper as he stroked the ball into the far corner. He made the finish look much easier than it was.

Brighton thrashed Romanian champions Universitatea Craiova and look capable of competing on multiple fronts

If the result was still in any doubt then, it wasn’t by the 39th minute when Vladimir Screciu was sent off for a clumsy challenge on Matt O’Riley. Referee Matthias Jollenbeck showed a yellow card but a replay showed a hefty tread on the ankle and - after being sent to his monitor - upgraded it to red.

The second half, then, gave the Seagulls a chance to remind everyone of their ruthless side. Already the highest scorers in the Premier League - averaging 2.4 per game this season – they wasted little time adding to their tally.

First, 19-year-old Charalampos Kostoulas made it three with his sixth of the season thanks to a cool finish on the hour mark, two minutes before Osman – like Hinshelwood a constant menace - picked the ball up on the left and cut inside to roll home his second.

They ended 90 minutes of domination having had 27 shots – one of which saw Femi Azeez missing the chance to make it five on debut by inexplicably blazing over from five yards.

Brighton have now scored four or more in five of their eight home games this season, and ended last night’s rout top of the Conference League having yet to concede a goal. The competition does not look big enough for them.
