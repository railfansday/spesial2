Disney and Adobe are building custom generative AI models for parks and ESPN

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Adobe will give Disney early access to Firefly, Premiere and Photoshop under a multi-year alliance announced Monday

Walt Disney $DIS and Adobe $ADBE are building custom generative AI models for Disney’s theme parks, studios and ESPN under a multi-year alliance the companies announced on Monday at Adobe MAX in Miami Beach, Florida.

As an Official AI Innovation Partner of Disney, Adobe will provide early access to its AI, creative and media technologies, the company said. Those include Adobe Firefly, Adobe Firefly Foundry, Adobe Premiere and Adobe Photoshop, along with dedicated engineering and research resources for Disney's production pipeline.

Through Firefly Foundry, Disney will collaborate with Adobe on proprietary generative AI systems built around its own franchises and properties, Adobe said. The models learn from character attributes, environments and visual styles, which lets them produce output that is safe for commercial use and ready for production.

Walt Disney Imagineering Research & Development has used Firefly Foundry in theme park development, Adobe said. Its tools include a sketch-to-image model that takes artists' hand-drawn ideas and renders them as 2D concept art, plus a custom image model that produces assets faithful to the franchises.

The work will begin by shortening project schedules in Disney's Experiences division and supporting early concept work at StudioLAB, Walt Disney Studios' internal innovation arm, according to Reuters.

"We talk about the time frames in our projects in years — it's years' worth of development. And what Adobe Foundry has allowed us to do is compress the creative process that would have taken months to now being able to do that in hours and days," Kyle Laughlin, Disney's senior vice president of R&D technology and engineering, told Reuters.

Laughlin said the tools will help the team avoid expensive modifications on site after construction of park experiences gets under way. He said they are "in no way meant to replace" roles at Imagineering, according to Reuters.

Adobe joins StudioLAB as an Innovation Partner to explore internal creative workflows with Disney creatives and technologists, according to Adobe. At ESPN Edge, the sports network's innovation center, ESPN and Adobe will identify ways for AI to increase engagement with sports fans and to bring ESPN's archive to life.

The alliance also covers marketing activations, industry events and customer storytelling tied to Walt Disney World Resort, Disneyland Resort, Disney Cruise Line, ESPN Edge and StudioLAB. Adobe will increase its presence across Disney's media platforms. Co-innovation projects with StudioLAB and ESPN Edge are under way, and more activations will roll out over the alliance's term, Adobe said.

"Today's announcement marks the next chapter of the relationship," Hannah Elsakr, Adobe's vice president of GenAI New Business Ventures, said in a statement.

Becca Vodnoy, Disney's senior vice president of Corporate Alliances, said in a statement that the alliance gives Disney's creative teams "new ways to explore and develop their ideas."
