GovCIO Announces Partnership with the Washington Commanders to Honor Those Who Serve

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

WASHINGTON, Nov. 3, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- GovCIO, a leader in federal IT modernization, is proud to announce a new partnership with the Washington Commanders for the 2026/27 season.

Supporting those who serve sits at the center of GovCIO's mission. GovCIO works alongside federal agencies to strengthen the technology and services that support service members, Veterans, and public safety communities. Through its partnership with the Washington Commanders, GovCIO will bring that commitment beyond the workplace, creating meaningful opportunities to recognize service, strengthen connection, and celebrate the people who make a difference.

The Washington region is home to one of the nation's largest military and Veteran populations, making it a community deeply connected to public service. GovCIO is proud to stand alongside the Commanders in recognizing the sacrifices of service members, Veterans, and military families.

"We are honored to partner with the Washington Commanders to celebrate the people who serve our country," said Jim Brabston, CEO of GovCIO. "Supporting service members, Veterans and their families is at the heart of who we are. This partnership gives us an opportunity to recognize their contributions, share their stories, and bring our community together in appreciation of their service."

GovCIO is proud to support the Washington Commanders' annual Salute to Service game against the Los Angeles Rams on Nov. 8, 2026. The event honors and celebrates the service and sacrifice of military members, Veterans, and their families. As part of this effort, GovCIO will help create meaningful opportunities to recognize those who serve, connect military families with the broader community, and celebrate their contributions through a memorable game-day experience.

This partnership will continue throughout the season with support of the Commanders' Monday Night Football matchup against the Cincinnati Bengals on Nov. 23. The game will provide a powerful platform to engage the community and spotlight initiatives that improve the lives of Veterans and their families.

At GovCIO, service extends beyond technology. Whether supporting federal agencies, honoring military communities, or investing in meaningful local partnerships, GovCIO is committed to making a positive impact where it matters most. Through this partnership with the Commanders, GovCIO looks forward to celebrating those who serve and strengthening the communities they call home.
