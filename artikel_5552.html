Medical Solutions names Adrian Bartella Chief Financial Officer

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

OMAHA, Neb., Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Medical Solutions, one of the nation's largest healthcare workforce solutions partners, has named Adrian Bartella its new Chief Financial Officer. Bartella will lead the company's finance function, including financial planning and analysis, accounting, treasury and other financial operations.

Bartella brings more than 20 years of finance leadership experience across public and private companies, including serving as Chief Financial Officer of Upstream Rehabilitation, The Fresh Market and Hudson Group. His background spans financial strategy, mergers and acquisitions, capital management and business transformation.

"Adrian brings the financial expertise and collaborative approach we need as we build for the future," said Rebecca Rogers Tijerino, CEO of Medical Solutions. "I'm looking forward to working with him to pursue the opportunities that will make the greatest difference for our business and the healthcare facilities, clinicians and communities we serve."

"Having worked on the provider side of healthcare, I've seen how much the right clinical team matters to patients and to the organizations that care for them," Bartella said. "Medical Solutions helps facilities build those teams every day. I'm excited to get to know the people across the business and to work with Rebecca and the leadership team to shape a financial strategy that supports the facilities and clinicians who rely on us."

Throughout his career, Bartella has successfully helped organizations strengthen financial performance, navigate change and complete complex transactions. At Medical Solutions, he will work alongside the executive leadership team to guide financial decisions and align resources with the company's strategic priorities.

Bartella earned graduate degrees in corporate finance and commerce from European University Viadrina in Germany and Lincoln University in New Zealand.

Medical Solutions helps healthcare organizations build and manage their workforces through travel nursing, allied health staffing and other workforce solutions. The company works with healthcare facilities to address staffing challenges, support care teams and connect clinicians with meaningful career opportunities.
