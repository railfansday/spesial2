Nope... Very few company's match after-tax - aka Mega Backdoor Roth - contributions. (Same for any 50+ "catch-up" contributions.) If your employer does, you have an exceptional plan!

Other than that, yes - it does not matter if my "regular" contributions are pre-tax or Roth, my employer matches them the same.

But to my prior point, getting > $1 for every $1 in "regular" contributions is better than getting no match on MBR. So I frontload the contributions that get the match, then move to MBR after the match is maxed.
