Use MoneyLion Instacash to cover a short gap before payday if you can wait up to five business days for its free delivery, which goes only into a MoneyLion Spend account. Instant money costs 7.5% to 9% of each advance in most states. Move your paycheck to MoneyLion Spend only if you’d use a limit above $500.

Instacash advances part of your next paycheck with no interest, credit check, or subscription, and MoneyLion collects it automatically, usually on the day that paycheck arrives. An outside checking account needs to be open 60 days and show three recurring direct deposits from one employer or benefits payer, while direct deposits into MoneyLion Spend, MoneyLion’s own checking account, qualify you sooner.

Instacash costs nothing for a worker with a steady paycheck who takes advances into MoneyLion Spend and waits a few days, and moving the paycheck there pays off only for someone who’d use more than $500. EarnIn is the better pick for anyone who won’t open a MoneyLion Spend account, since it sends standard transfers to the bank they already use at no cost.
