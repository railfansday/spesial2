WWE Star Bronson Reed Dead at 38

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

WWE announced that wrestler Bronson Reed died on Nov. 27 at the age of 38 from medical complications: "He was a phenomenal person, a devoted family man, and a beloved colleague.”

The wrestling company announced that wrestler Bronson Reed (real name Jermaine Haley) died on Nov. 27 at the age of 38, citing medical complications.

“Jermaine was a larger-than-life figure in WWE,” the company wrote in an Instagram tribute, “but more importantly, he was a phenomenal person, a devoted family man, and a beloved colleague.”

WWE also noted that Reed and his wife, Paige Haley, were expecting their second child together, adding, “Our thoughts and deepest condolences are with Jermaine's entire family, including his pregnant wife, Paige, their daughter, as well as all his friends, co-workers and fans.”

No additional details about the medical complications that led to Reed’s death were shared.

The wrestler’s final WWE appearance came at Crown Jewel on Nov. 7, when he was thrown off the stage and through tables by Oba Femi during a tag team title match, according to Sports Illustrated. Reed reportedly suffered multiple broken ribs and a lacerated kidney. He remained in a hospital in Saudi Arabia for treatment before returning home.

Born in Adelaide, Australia, Reed recalled that he’d always been interested in wrestling.

“I was always a fan of pro wrestling, since I can remember,” he shared with Sporting News in 2021. “I had my uncle who lived with us at the time, he was a huge wrestling fan, so as a little kid at 2, 3 years old, he was watching wrestling on TV and I guess I was sitting there watching as well. From there, I just became a fan."

"As a little kid, I was always like, ‘One day I’m going to be a wrestler,’ but you don’t know if that is going to happen,” he added. “As a teenager, I saw some local wrestling and that’s when I was like, maybe I can do this, join a school and learn how to do it locally."

And that certainly paid off as he won a number of awards during his tenure, as he was the NXT North American Champion in 2021, as well as the winner of the 2024 André the Giant Memorial Battle Royal.

Of course, he knew that his career was only possible thanks to his high school sweetheart Paige, whom he wed in May 2021.

“This is as much her journey as it is mine,” he shared with Sports Illustrated in 2021. “I’ve been with my wife since I was a teenager. She’s actually seen me since before I was a wrestler, when I was this huge wrestling fan and dreamed of one day getting to WWE.”

“That seems like a big dream, especially when you’re from a really small town in Australia,” he admitted. “She supported me the whole way, and it meant a lot to have her there.”
