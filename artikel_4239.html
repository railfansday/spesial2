How Ultimate Edition And Pre-Order Exclusives Work In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

GTA 6's Ultimate Edition comes with a bunch of goodies, but you won't have them from the get-go. Here's what you need to know.

For some time, everyone feared that Grand Theft Auto 6 would cost $100 and raise the bar for triple-A prices going forward. As we know now, that's mostly not the case.

GTA 6 launches with two editions, including the $100 Ultimate Edition that has a range of exclusive goodies, including shops, clothes, vehicles, and more. The way they work is a little strange, though.

As Rockstar detailed back when the Ultimate Edition was first revealed, the deluxe version's unique items aren't all available from the very start of the game. Although a few, like the Electric Fang Tattoo Parlour and Goodtime Gear, can be accessed from the start of the game, the more interesting bonuses are spaced out a bit.

For example, the Hawk & Little Morgan Revolvers are only available once you reach Chapter 2, while the stunning ’95 Grotti Cheetah isn't available until all the way in Chapter 5. The shops offering exclusive cosmetics like tattoos and hairstyles can be accessed from the start, but some don't offer their unique styles until Chapter 2.

To make things a little easier, GTA 6 does at least tell you when you've gained access to some of the Ultimate Edition's items, either by adding a collection mission to the menu or showing a pop-up during gameplay. For almost all of them, though, they become available at the start of a chapter - it just changes for each item. Here's when and where to expect them.

Jason's safehouse in the Keys (only for Chapter 1).

Look in the weapon locker of any Ammu-Nation

Wonder Whale, Key Lento, found on mannequins

Visit South Vice-Dale County first, robberies then appear in Southside

Chapter 1, but Signature styles unlock from Chapter 2

New sidequest is started once you collect the '67 Dominator Buggy.

Look in the weapon locker of any Ammu-Nation

Head to the Ocean View Hotel in Vice Beach to unlock the new clothes, garage, and hairstyles.

Collected from Washington Beach, Vice Beach

Added to various stores a few missions into the chapter.

I wasn't able to test this myself, but various Grand Theft Auto 6 players have shown what happens to the various shops and locations if you don't have the Ultimate Edition. They don't disappear from the map like some expected, but Jason and Lucia simply won't be able to access them since the doors will be locked.

If that bothers you, there's an Ultimate Edition upgrade that can be purchased at any time. I personally think the extra $20 is worth it, but it really depends on how much you're going to be customising Jason and Lucia, since it's mostly just cosmetic options.

If you want to get around Leonida and clear GTA 6's map with ease, Auto-Drive is one of your best friends.
