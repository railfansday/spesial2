Sioux Falls Christian wins 11A state title again

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Vermillion, S.D. (KELO) — The Sioux Falls Christian Chargers defense posted its sixth shutout in 12 games this season to claim the 11A state championship over Lennox, 41-0.

This is the second straight season the Chargers have defeated the Orioles in the state championship game after losing to them back in 2024.

The Chargers held the Orioles to just 166 yards of offense, forced three turnovers and kept Drake Mikkelsen in check, as the University of Minnesota commit posted four catches for 51 yards in his final high school game.

The Sioux Falls Christian offense was led by Trey Meehan who scored three rushing touchdowns, however it was his teammate Jude Prins taking home the Joe Robbie MVP.

Prins had a 52-yard touchdown on the ground in his only carry of the game and added a 31-yard receiving TD as well on his only catch.

Meehan scored three of the games first four touchdowns and Cooper Strande hit Deair Smith for the fifth touchdown of the half to force a running clock in the second half with the Chargers up 35-0.

Sioux Falls Christian took its foot off the gas in the second half scoring just one touchdown in the third before a scoreless fourth wrapped up the game at 41-0.

The Chargers finish the season at 12-0 and the win marks the fourth state championship in school history.

"We talk about family, effort, and win the moment. We call ourselves the few. We started off my first year with 34 guys. So when they don't care about stats and plays, I know that they're living out our core values," Chargers Head Coach Jared Smith said of his team post game.

Sioux Falls Christian joins Elk Point-Jefferson, Parkston, Freeman and Wall as undefeated South Dakota state football champions in 2026.
