Colts rejected a trade offer from the Ravens for Daniel Jones

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Ten days after the November 10 trade deadline, the Colts cut quarterback Daniel Jones. They could have gotten something for him. Now? Nothing.

Per ESPNFL, the Ravens would have sent a fourth-round pick to the Colts for Jones and a fifth-round pick. The Colts would have gotten their fifth-round pick back if the Ravens had made it to the playoffs.

The Vikings, per the report, were talking to the Colts about Jones but never made an offer. (That doesn’t mean, obviously, that two teams didn’t speak in terms about what it would take to get Jones. If anything, the possibility of getting Jones for less than a third-round pick may have helped the Vikings get Kirk Cousins from the Raiders for only a third-round pick.)

Obviously, the Colts should have taken the offer. They cut him after only two more games — a win over the Dolphins and a loss to the Texans. (Compensatory draft picks aren’t available when a player is released.)

Some will say that the Colts shouldn’t have dumped Jones for whatever they could get because he was still the starter. But the Colts knew (or should have known) what was coming. Eventually, Jones was going to be benched in order to ensure that he won’t suffer an injury that locked in his $10.51 million guarantee for 2027. (It was mentioned before the trade deadline.)

So the choices were obvious. Trade him and get something or keep him and get nothing.

Given the various ill-advised decisions the Colts have made since trading for cornerback Sauce Gardner last November, this one isn’t as glaring. But it’s still another unforced error by the Colts, fueled by a lack of vision and strategic planning.
