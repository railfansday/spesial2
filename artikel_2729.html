JJ Redick praises Adou Thiero's 'awesome' play versus Trail Blazers

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Adou Thiero finally made his 2026-27 NBA regular-season debut on Friday when the Los Angeles Lakers hosted the Portland Trail Blazers. He had missed their first nine games due to a knee issue, and while he only played seven minutes, he made a decent impact on the game.

He scored two baskets, one on an alley-oop dunk and another on a putback slam, and he also contributed four rebounds and two assists. The Lakers ended up winning 124-118, and coach JJ Redick sounded content with how the second-year forward performed, based on his postgame comments.

"Yeah, I thought his first two defensive possessions, he made mistakes, and then after that, he was awesome," Redick said. "He literally did everything we asked him to do. He was great on the offensive glass. Even sometimes when we didn't necessarily get the possession, he was tapping the ball and crashing. I thought the read he made out of the short roll, I think they blitzed three times in the first half, and two of those were against him. He makes the short roll, and he gets the lobs. So, I thought he played a great game. Defensively, [he] was really good in the second half."

At this point in his career, Thiero can be an energy and hustle player in spot duty for Los Angeles. At 6-foot-7 and 220 pounds, he's arguably the most athletically gifted player on the team, and he also has the ability to be pesky and disruptive on the defensive end.

What was interesting during Friday's game was that Thiero got minutes but forward Jarred Vanderbilt, who had played meaningful minutes in each of the previous five games, never got off the bench.

Thiero missed stretches of action last season due to knee problems. The hope is that his knees will hold up throughout this season so that he can develop into whatever the best version of himself will be.
