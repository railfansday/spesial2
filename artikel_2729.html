Will the Buffalo Bills defeat the Kansas City Chiefs on Thanksgiving?

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Welcome to SB Nation Reacts, a survey of fans across the NFL. Throughout the year we ask questions of the most plugged-in Bills fans and fans across the country.

The Buffalo Bills remain in control of their playoff fate thanks to an AFC-best 9-1 record. Buffalo has been able to meet almost every challenge thrown its way during the team's current six-game win streak.

An early concern, the Bills' defense has demanded respect around the league in recent weeks thanks to allowing zero touchdowns in three consecutive games while surrendering just nine field goals. Since their bye in Week 7, Buffalo has given up just one touchdown (to running back Derrick Henry of the Baltimore Ravens).

There has been plenty of discussion about the quality of quarterbacks the Bills have faced in the last month, but it's impossible to ignore the defensive turnaround post bye. In their first six games, Buffalo's defense allowed 171 points, giving up an average of 28.5 for last in the NFL in scoring defense. In the four games since the bye, that figure has plummeted to 37 points — an average of 9.3 points per game.

For all those saying the Bills have had an easy road, the same can be said of the Chiefs who haven't defeated a team over .500 since Week 1. One thing's clear: It gets much tougher for both teams on the road ahead, starting against one another in Week 12.

Quarterbacks Josh Allen and Patrick Mahomes bring out the best in each other, and they get to renew their friendship during primetime in front of a holiday audience. This time, however, both teams will have a potent rushing attack to lean on. That means we could see running backs James Cook III and either Kenneth Walker III or rookie Emmett Johnson take featured roles in the game's outcome.

Bills Mafia knows how great Allen and Cook are, and that when both are producing it's almost impossible to stop Buffalo's offense. That's especially true with running back Ty Johnson in the fold, and one of the team's most consistently productive receivers.

The same is true of Mahomes and Walker, but Walker has been sidelined with a groin injury. He may return on Thursday, but he won't have to handle the load himself. Buffalo's defense is facing a unique challenge against Kansas City, though similar in ways to the Detroit Lions.

Is another all-time classic shootout in the works for Thanksgiving? The weather may play a role, with sustained soaking rain expected amid temps in the mid-low 30s and winds gusting near 25 mph. Does that play better to either team's strengths?

The Bills and Chiefs both play outdoors and are used to inclement weather. Ball security will be of enhanced concern given the conditions, but it's something that you can be sure both teams have placed as the number-one focus regardless of weather.

Allen and Mahomes are both playing MVP-caliber football this season, and they're likely to have featured roles in deciding which team gets to play in Super Bowl LXI. But first, they have to get through Week 12.

Who wins in Bills vs. Chiefs to score a major coup in the AFC playoff standings on Thanksgiving night? Will the Bills win, and are you confident the team is headed in the right direction?
