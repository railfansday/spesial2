49ers WR Christian Kirk had meniscus surgery

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

49ers wide receiver Christian Kirk went on injured reserve because of a knee injury a couple of weeks ago and that injury has also led him to the operating room.

Kirk shared a picture of his knee post-surgery on Instagram on Wednesday night. The picture was part of a post commemorating Kirk’s 30th birthday and he called this season, which began with another stint on injured reserve due to a calf injury, “the hardest of my career.”

Ian Rapoport of NFL Media reports that Kirk had surgery on his meniscus. Neither he nor Kirk outlined any recovery timeline, but it seems like a long shot that he’ll be returning to action this season.

Kirk only played in one game for the 49ers between the two injuries. He signed a one-year deal with the team this offseason, so he’ll be a free agent come March if he does not re-sign with the team ahead of the 2027 league year.
