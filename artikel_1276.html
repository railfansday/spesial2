Rangers Considering Signing 'Outstanding' Ex-McInnes Favourite Armstrong

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Rangers are now being linked with a move for Dinamo Bucaresti winger Danny Armstrong as Derek McInnes lines up a possible reunion with his former Kilmarnock attacker. It's going to be interesting to see what Rangers do in the winter transfer window, given the committed a fair amount of money to transfers during the summer.

The Gers loosened the purse strings to allow McInnes to put his own stamp on his new squad, estimated to have splashed out more than £24million. That was a strong statement for Rangers, who are looking to bridge the gap to Celtic, who have dominated the Premiership over recent years, albeit they scraped it on the final day of last season.

Going into the international break, Rangers are four points behind Celtic with a game in hand, and they have also booked a place in the Premier Sports Cup final. After a slow start that included Light Blues missing out on Europe, McInnes has turned things around, and their campaign is very much alive at this still relatively early stage.

The question now is whether Rangers double down and make more signings in the winter window, and much may come down to how the team is performing. According to Gazeta Sporturilor, Rangers are showing interest in Bucaresti winger Armstrong.

Armstrong has been in Romania since his 2025 move from Kilmarnock on a free transfer, and he is out of contract next summer, although there is an option to extend the deal. That's an option Bucaresti will likely be keen to trigger, given Armstrong has scored six and assisted 10 in just 16 league appearances so far this season. In fact, there is an automatic trigger to extend the deal should he play more than 60% of the game time this season, and he has played in virtually every game.

The report claims Rangers have Armstrong on their radar, although they are yet to make their interest formal.

"What a difference-maker he's been for us recently and long may that continue.” - McInnes on Armstrong in 2022.

Interestingly, McInnes worked with Armstrong at Kilmarnock previously, and that time included three goals and eight assists in 33 league games in his final season at the club. He scored six and assisted 10 in the previous campaign. McInnes described the winger as 'outstanding' in December of 2024.

Clearly, he is a big fan, and the feeling is mutual, with Armstrong having said: “Derek brought the best out of me in that side of things as well and we were quite successful.”

Rangers could get a cheaper deal given Armstrong isn't under a long-term contract, and that isn't going to change even with the one-year extension trigger likely to come into play. It could be an affordable way for Rangers to add another attacking option, and a player who has experience working with McInnes and playing in the Premiership.
