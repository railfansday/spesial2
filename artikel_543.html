Laxman Utekar, Rajkumar Periasamy and Vishnuvardhan Set New Films With Bhushan Kumar’s T-Series (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Directors Laxman Utekar, Rajkumar Periasamy and Vishnuvardhan are set to collaborate with Indian studio T-Series chair and managing director Bhushan Kumar on three new films.

Title, story, cast and production details have not been disclosed yet.

Utekar directed the 2025 hit Hindi-language historical drama “Chhaava.” Periasamy won the best director prize at India’s National Film Awards for Tamil-language military biopic “Amaran,” while Vishnuvardhan’s Hindi-language war film “Shershaah” took the event’s Special Jury Award. The three collaborations add to a busy T-Series pipeline that runs across Bollywood and South Indian cinema, with leading stars from both industries attached. Several of those titles are produced by T-Series, while others are presented by the company.

Under its own production banner, T-Series is backing Sandeep Reddy Vanga’s “Spirit,” which stars Prabhas, and an untitled Anurag Basu film with Kartik Aaryan. Also on the roster are Ram Gopal Varma’s “Police Company” and an untitled Milap Zaveri project pairing Aditya Roy Kapur and Tara Sutaria.

Kumar is producing “The Pride of Bharat – Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj,” a historical drama in two parts from director Sandeep Singh, with “Kantara” star Rishab Shetty in the lead. Aaryan has a second film on the slate, Shimit Amin’s “Captain India.” “Khosla Ka Ghosla 2,” “Karnika” and “Jaani Dushman” round out the list.

In the south, T-Series is presenting Hanu Raghavapudi’s “Fauzi,” which gives Prabhas a second title on the slate, and Prasanth Varma’s mythological feature “Jai Hanuman.” The company is also presenting Prashanth Neel’s “Dragon,” led by Jr NTR, and Sukumar’s next film with Ram Charan, which reunites director and star.

In all, the slate takes in action, romance, period drama and mythology, and spans both the Bollywood and South Indian industries.
