Roberto De Zerbi has new plan for Dejan Kulusevski at Tottenham

Roberto De Zerbi wants Dejan Kulusevski to focus on returning to full fitness rather than on his Tottenham contract situation, sources have told Football Insider.

Kulusevski's long road to recovery is now at an end as he begins to feature regularly for Tottenham once again.

He suffered a serious knee injury in Spurs' May 2025 defeat to Crystal Palace and spent over 500 days out of action as a result.

The 26-year-old made his long-awaited return to the pitch against Coventry City last month and has since made three more appearances from the bench.

Despite uncertainty over his future, the club are not looking to extend his contract right now, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast.

His deal is set to expire in 2028, but De Zerbi would rather the Swede put his full focus into getting fully fit and back to the player he was before his injury.

Kulusevski could be in line to make his first Spurs start since early May 2025 this weekend as they travel to face struggling Sunderland.

His return to the fold has coincided with some strong recent form from Tottenham, but he has not yet done enough to show that they should open talks over fresh terms.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I don’t think the contract situation will be uppermost in the thoughts of Kulusevski or Tottenham right now. It’s just so good to see him back playing football after such a hellish injury journey, getting some minutes under his belt off the bench.

"Tottenham are being very careful with him. They’re not going to put any extra pressure on him or anything like that because of the lengthy spell that he had on the sidelines. They’re just hoping that he can continue to build up his sharpness and match fitness."

Tottenham could pick up a fifth win in six league games at the Stadium of Light on Sunday afternoon.

Kulusevski has played for 42 minutes since returning but could increase his tally at Sunderland with a long-awaited start, which De Zerbi has hinted at this week.

Speaking in his pre-match press conference, he said: "Listen, I am thinking to start [him], maybe not now, but I think we are very close to start the game. He is working well. I watched his games in the national team just to understand the physical condition.

"“He played the first game as a winger and the second game as a No.10 and especially in the second game he played very well, but we have to be good to understand the right moment to put him on the pitch. I think medical staff, physical staff, they are working very well with him and we can’t lose any time, but we can’t take any risk because he is a special player for us."
