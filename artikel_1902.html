Dan Burn's 'strange' comments sum up everything going wrong with Newcastle United - ex-coach

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Newcastle United are struggling due to a lack of confidence and belief in the plans they are putting in place at St James' Park.

The Magpies have endured a nightmare start under Matthias Jaissle, with just three wins from their first ten Premier League games, while they have been knocked out of the League Cup.

Pressure has mounted on the manager's position as a result, while fans have openly criticised the players, and they look some way off of reaching their ambitious targets.

Former Man United chief scout and assistant manager Mick Brown told Football Insider that Newcastle's star players do not believe they can be successful after their slow start to the campaign.

This comes after Dan Burn admitted while away with England that Newcastle expected this season to be inconsistent, leading to questions about the feeling behind the scenes.

There appears to be a lack of confidence in the Newcastle dressing room, and whether Burn's comments were harsh or realistic, it reflects the issues they have faced on the pitch.

Football Insider revealed on 9 November that Newcaslte are confident of putting together a strong season, and were prepared for inconsistency after a difficult summer.

A number of influencial players were allowed to leave St James' Park, while many of their new additions are still young players who have not yet adapted to the league.

Burn's comments reflect that attitude, but as one of the leading influences in the dressing room, there are suggestions he should be inspiring confidence rather than question marks.

"I'm not sure whether to think Burn's comments were a bit strange, or just realistic," Brown told Football Insider.

"Ultimately, that is the situation Newcastle are in, they've been inconsistent this season and there are plenty of reasons for that, it's something they have to sort out.

"But having influencial players come out and say they expected things to be difficult, that raises questions for me.

"It's never a good sign when you're not confident going into a new season, you should be ambitious and looking to have a strong start, not worrying that things might not go well.

"I think that shows what the problem has been at Newcastle this season, they don't seem to have any confidence in what they're doing."

Burn's comments have also raised questions about the players' commitment to Jaissle's plans, amid reports that they have been left confused by the boss' tactics.

Things have not gone right for the new boss since his took charge in the summer, with fans already expressing their frustrations about their results and style of play.

Meanwhile, Brown told Football Insider that Newcastle have been concerned by leaks coming from the dressing room amid suggestions of growing unhappiness.

After Burn's comments, it seems there is a lack of confience behind the scenes which may have been sparked by the manager, if reports elsewhere are to be believed.
