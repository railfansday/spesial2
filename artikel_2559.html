2026 Corpus Christi high school football area round playoff schedule

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

After a first round with upsets, impressive showings and historic outcomes, 14 Corpus Christi-area teams remain alive in the Texas high school football playoffs.

Tuloso-Midway, fresh off the program's first playoff win, treks to New Braunfels for an area round matchup with La Vernia, which upset Kerrville Tivy.

Carroll and Calallen have each secured home games this week, as the Tigers clash with Medina Valley on Thursday at Cabaniss and Calallen brings Floresville to Phil Danaher Stadium.

Here is a look at the area round playoff schedule for Corpus Christi and the Coastal Bend:

Veterans Memorial vs. Austin Anderson, 7:30 p.m. Friday, Nov. 20, San Antonio's Dub Farris Athletic Complex

Medina Valley at Carroll, 7 p.m. Thursday, Nov. 19, Cabaniss Multipurpose Stadium
