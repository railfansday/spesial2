Tanggal: 2026-11-12

My wife needs medically necessary procedure (vein a lation to prevent recurring clots). It turns out my current plan (continuation of Cigna employer plan under COBRA) specifically excludes Vienna ablation from coverage. We appealed since our doctor says it is medically necessary, and waiting on the decision.
I believe you will need approval. This shows the criteria for WA (following national policy) which may or may not apply to your location. https://wa-provider.kaiserpermanente.or ... /veins.pdf
In the meantime I'm looking at ACA plans for 2027. I called Kaiser permanente enrollment dept with the medical codes to be covered and they told me to get the gold plan. When I asked for something in writing that the plan covered those code they told me they couldn't look them up and they didn't know if it would be covered.
Is there a way to make progress or is it just a gamble?
First, whether or not it is covered does not depend on the metal level. The metal level does affect your costs so perhaps they were suggesting it for that reason, but you should probably look into which metal plan is best depending on your overall care usage.

Second, I've found the only way to get an answer in general is to go through the clinic. Tell them you have to pick a plan and want to know about coverage under ACA. They should have access to the Kaiser database and could tell you the requirements (and maybe even compare it to your wife's clinical notes). I base this on calling my insurance company whose reps in several departments couldn't answer cost and coverage questions on a specialty medicine, but the clinic could.
