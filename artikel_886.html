Price hopes undisputed history could lead her to Shields showdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Olympic gold medallist Lauren Price wants to test herself against Claressa Shields, Chantelle Cameron and Mikaela Mayer; Price will become Wales' first undisputed world champion in the four-belt era if she beats Michaela Kotaskova and hopes victory can lead to American star Shields

Sports Journalist @johndennen

Lauren Price believes becoming an undisputed champion could lead her to a showdown with American star Claressa Shields.

Price faced off with Shields after her last fight but no agreement has been reached for them to box.

Shields has been an undisputed champion in multiple divisions and has returned to middleweight after a period as the undisputed heavyweight champion.

Price, like Shields, won the Olympic gold medal at middleweight. She's unbeaten as a professional too. On Friday Price looks to add the now vacant WBO welterweight title to the WBA, WBC and IBF belts she already holds when she fights Michaela Kotaskova.

If she wins she will become Wales' first undisputed champion in the four-belt era.

"It hasn't sunk in yet," Price told Sky Sports. "When I turned over, that's the dream to become undisputed. I can honestly say if I achieve that on Friday then I've ticked every accolade in boxing.

"Regardless of the belts on the line, you step through the ropes and it's another fight. I just let the boxing do the talking and make sure I get that win and I win in style."

If Price does go undisputed at welterweight, moving up through divisions will be the natural next step. Shields remains a target though that fight is yet to come to fruition.

"It has to make sense on both ends," Price said. "I'm giving away weight, fine. But it was in her back garden, I wanted to come back to Wales as well.

On December 24 Chantelle Cameron and Mikaela Mayer, who vacated the WBO welterweight title, will rematch for the WBA, WBC and WBO super-welter championships. Price has an eye on fighting the winner.

"They're both great. But I believe Chantelle will win that fight again and that's a great fight for British boxing, I'm 100 percent up for it," Price said.

"I think right now obviously myself, Cameron, Mayer's in there [the pound-for-pound list] and obviously Shields. For me we're the big names and that's what I want, I want the big fights."
