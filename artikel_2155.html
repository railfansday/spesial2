Harry Kane's Tottenham return 'not a big surprise' after confirmed news

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Harry Kane returning to Tottenham Hotspur one day cannot be ruled out despite his new big-money contract at Bayern Munich.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that a move back to North London wouldn't be a surprise in the slightest.

The 33-year-old made the switch to the Bundesliga in August 2023. Since then, he has notched 156 goals and 40 assists in 161 games.

He is also England's all-time leading goalscorer, with 91 goals in 125 appearances, many of which came during his time at Tottenham.

During his time at the Allianza Arena, Kane has been constantly linked with a Premier League return, with both Tottenham and Manchester United interested.

With his latest contract making him one of the highest-paid players in Europe, Spurs may have to wait a few years to re-sign the 33-year-old.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson has predicted Kane to come back to Spurs, but only at the end of his career.

"He could easily end up coming back to Spurs at the end of his career. That wouldn't be a big surprise. But it won't be on this kind of money," he said.

"It's just wild if his wage is anywhere near the record. Because the record... Once you've got Vinicius Junior, [Kylian] Mbappe, [Erling] Haaland, those are the three highest, I would think.

"You're probably looking at those players being on overall, gross, £1million a week. I can't believe he's on more than that.

"Kane's an amazing player, brilliant attitude. Now when he's the Ballon d'Or winner, everything's perfect, but he is 33. And it's just so much money.

"We will see. But if he comes back to England for the end of his career, he won't be anywhere near that."

Not everyone has been so joyous about Kane's new contract, with Dietmar Hamann raising some alarm bells over the 33-year-old's situation.

Mainly due to the player's age, Hamann has suggested that Bayern are taking a "significant risk" in extending Kane's contract this season.

However, the England international already has 10 goals and nine assists in 14 games this season, and remains a key figure in the squad.

Hamann will undoubtedly be forced to eat his own words by the end of the campaign, especially if Kane can bring a Champions League title to Bayern.
