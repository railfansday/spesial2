Loma Negra (LOMA) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Operating cash flow more than doubled despite segment pressure.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Loma Negra Compañía Industrial Argentina Sociedad Anónima (LOMA -0.91%) reported a decline in consolidated revenue attributed to subdued economic activity and adverse weather conditions. The company saw improved profitability in its core cement segment and strengthened its financial position by reducing net debt and successfully issuing a local corporate bond after the quarter end. Management reported that while volume remained soft in July and August, a strong rebound in September suggests underlying demand reacts quickly to supportive weather conditions. The company is actively participating in multiple tenders under the RIGI regime to secure long-term volumes through energy, mining, and infrastructure projects.
