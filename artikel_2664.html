Trent McDuffie reveals how Myles Garrett, Aaron Donald could help him haunt Packers

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Trent McDuffie was standing on the sideline of Northwest Stadium in Landover, Maryland, when he first saw Myles Garrett and Aaron Donald play alongside each other.

The duo, which has five NFL Defensive Player of the Year awards between them, wreaked havoc on the Commanders' offensive line, with Garrett sacking quarterback Jayden Daniels three times in his return from knee surgery.

McDuffie couldn't help but smile as he watched.

Los Angeles Rams defensive tackle Aaron Donald (99) greets defensive end Myles Garrett (95) during an NFL game in Landover, Md., Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026. AP Photo/Stephanie Scarbrough

It wasn't because the Rams cornerback was enjoying watching the two defensive stars from the sideline but because he was imagining what it would be like to play behind them.

"I couldn't stop smiling on the sideline. Just seeing how aggressive and violent the D-line was attacking," McDuffie told the California Post in an exclusive interview. "As a DB, knowing that I have him up front, it just tells me I have more plays and more opportunities to go make game-changing plays."

McDuffie's first experience playing behind Garrett and Donald in the same game came in LA's 34-6 victory over the Cardinals. The Rams' defense recorded five sacks, two turnovers, and held its NFC West rivals to six points.

Now they'll need to replicate that performance when they face a much different challenge in the Packers on Thanksgiving Eve with the stakes considerably higher.

Los Angeles Rams cornerback Trent McDuffie (22) sits on the NFL Network set during a joint practice against the Dallas Cowboys at the Rams Practice Facility in Woodland Hills, Calif., Tuesday, Aug. 11, 2026. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

Both teams enter at 6-4, with the winner of Wednesday's matchup gaining a crucial head-to-head tiebreaker in the NFC wild-card race.

The Rams have won four consecutive games, and despite being without defensive tackle Poona Ford and cornerback Jaylen Watson for the remainder of the season, they are finally beginning to resemble the formidable unit they envisioned when they acquired Garrett and McDuffie and lured Donald out of retirement.

"There hasn't been a game yet where really everybody was out there playing together," McDuffie said.

Los Angeles Rams Kobie Turner (91) celebrates a sack against the Detroit Lions during the third quarter of an NFL football game at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, Calif., Sunday, Dec. 14, 2025. Getty Images

Kobie Turner was activated off injured reserve ahead of the Thanksgiving Eve matchup with the Packers, and the world will finally have an opportunity to see what the Rams' defense looks like when McDuffie's coverage ability meets the Rams' pass rush at full strength, featuring two of the NFL's most feared defensive players.

"It should be really exciting," McDuffie said. "I think, as a defense, we have a really good opportunity. Just getting another game that we can all play together."

McDuffie's presence could be especially important against Packers quarterback Jordan Love, who will have considerably less time to find open receivers if Garrett and Donald are collapsing the pocket.

And with the Rams missing Puka Nacua, Davante Adams and center Coleman Shelton, the defense may have to do more than keep Green Bay's offense in check.

It may have to carry Los Angeles to victory.
