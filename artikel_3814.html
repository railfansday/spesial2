GTA 6 Is 'Probably the Greatest Artistic Achievement in History', Says Cyberpunk 2 Director

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Just about every veteran developer in the industry has been heaping praise on GTA 6 since its release last week, but I'm not sure any of that rampant positivity matches the thoughts of Igor Sarzynski.

Sarzynski is currently the creative director on Cyberpunk 2 (as it's officially known right now), having previously worked on both The Witcher 3 and Cyberpunk 2077 — two games that are widely considered to be amongst the best that the open world genre has to offer.

In other words, Sarzynski knows what he's talking about when he says GTA 6 is "probably the single greatest artistic achievement in history". Now that's praise.

"GTA 6-induced-open-world-game-dev-depression is real," he noted on social media.

"Probably the single greatest artistic achievement in history, I mean it. Scale, density, coherence, thoughtfulness of it all, just a technological and creative miracle."

"Cyberpunk 2 is cancelled," Sarzynski jokes, suggesting that no one's going to match Rockstar's effort for quite some time. "I'mma go sweep autumn leaves in my backyard now."

If you've played GTA 6, you can surely understand why every non-Rockstar developer is looking at it and being left in a state of near disbelief. We're not getting another release like it for years and years to come — and just like Red Dead Redemption 2 before it, it's going to become the benchmark for open world games.

Still, there'll come a time when Cyberpunk 2 takes all the headlines — it's just that right now, it's hard to see beyond GTA 6 because it's absolutely bloody everywhere (and with good reason).

"Seriously though I think we're doing something of our own, with a different unique flavour," Sarzynski continues in a follow-up post.

"GTA is impressive in a way all great art is, it expands what is possible, a captivating vision flawlessly executed," he concludes — and that's pretty much a perfect way to put it.
