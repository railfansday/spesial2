9 players set to miss Bolton Wanderers v West Brom

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Bolton Wanderers grabbed a much-needed point at home on Saturday against Portsmouth, but face a tough test to try and maintain that momentum at the Toughsheet Stadium, as they welcome West Brom.

The Baggies have been brilliant this season, with the decision to give James Morrison the permanent role after a strong interim spell to end last year proving more and more of a masterstroke as the campaign goes on.

They were made to rue a missed penalty at 2-1 up at Swansea City on Saturday, but even then, they head to Bolton on a seven-game unbeaten run and three points off top spot. If results go their way, they could end the gameweek in the top two.

Wanderers, meanwhile, know that their home form needs improving. They're averaging less than a point per game at the Toughsheet, and have won just one of their last seven there.

It'll be a difficult task for Steven Schumacher's side, but they'll be hoping that a Baggies outfit that were playing in South Wales just four days earlier can be the right matchup to grab a big three points.

These nine players, however, won't be available for the game.

Bolton boss Steven Schumacher revealed over the international break that captain Eoin Toal will be out until around Christmas with an ankle injury.

The Northern Irishman has battled injury throughout the four-and-a-half years he's spent at the club, but Schumacher insisted that he's not too concerned about his long-term availability, with his contract up at the end of the season.

John McAtee hasn't played in over two months now, and hasn't featured in a Championship squad since the extended international break back in October. Initially, he was playing with the Under-21s, but his recent absence has been due to a knee injury.

Of the five players sidelined currently, the 27-year-old is the closest to a return, with Schumacher revealing that he was set to rejoin training late last week.

Midfielder Ethan Erhahon has missed the last four games for Bolton after suffering a thigh injury.

The 25-year-old has had his injury demons in the past, playing just 31 league games in 2024/25 in his final year at Lincoln City and then just 30 in his debut campaign at the Toughsheet Stadium.

His return shouldn't be too far away, either, with Schumacher stating he'll be back in training this week.

Canadian international Jayden Nelson hasn't been able to get going in a Bolton shirt thus far, after arriving in the summer from MLS side Austin FC.

He made just six appearances before going down with a groin injury, and won't be back until around mid-December.

Ryan Hardie has had a nightmare-ish 18 months or so, since moving from Plymouth Argyle to Wrexham ahead of last season.

The Scotsman struggled to stay fit during a loan spell with Huddersfield Town in the second half of last term, and just as he was on the cusp of making his first Bolton start this season, he went down with a hamstring injury.

Hardie reunited with ex-Argyle boss Schumacher this summer, but hasn't found his goalscoring form he exhibited under him in Devon just yet.

West Brom were handed the news that no side wants to hear during the international break, as midfielder Ousmane Diakite picked up a calf injury while on duty for Mali and will miss the next two months.

The 26-year-old has been very impressive for James Morrison's side this season, finally starting to realise the promise surrounding him in his third campaign at the Hawthorns. This setback is a real hammer blow for the Baggies.

Diakite's injury highlights the need for Toby Collyer to make a swift return soon, which he should be on track to do. After picking up a hamstring injury in late October, it was said that he'd be making a return in early December.

The 22-year-old battled injury both at West Brom and at Hull City last year while on loan from Manchester United, and it'll be hoped that he can finally get an extended run out upon his return.

Mikey Johnston has, effectively, missed the last nine months of action now after initially fracturing his ankle against Oxford United back in February.

The Irishman did make his return in pre-season and came off the bench in their EFL Cup opener against Rotherham United, but fatigue from the fracture sidelined him once again.

There wasn't a set date for his return, but footage last week did show him back in training, so Baggies fans may not have to wait much longer to see him back in action.

Right-back Nolan Galves will miss the rest of the season, though, after he suffered a knee injury in their win over Derby County back in early September.
