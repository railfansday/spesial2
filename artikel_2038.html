Sheffield United flop yet to make league debut for new club after summer escape from Bramall Lane

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Sheffield United had a challenging summer transfer window, as wrangles between the past and present ownership disrupted business. Despite this, SUFC were still able to offload some fringe players to free up space in the squad.

Among those to move on from Bramall Lane was goalkeeper Ivo Grbic. The Croatian goalkeeper was unable to impress in his time with the Blades, and after returning from a second loan to Turkey, he saw a permanent exit sanctioned ahead of the 2026/27 campaign.

Grbic moved to Bulgaria with Ludogorets, and given his experience in top-flight football in France, Turkey and England, he would have backed himself to find a starting role. Unfortunately, that has not been the case.

Grbic joined Sheffield United in the winter of 2024. The Blades were in the Premier League at the time and needed a ‘keeper to help them tighten up at the back, but the former Atletico Madrid man struggled to impress as he conceded a hefty 32 goals in 11 games for the club.

The Blades responded by signing Michael Cooper ahead of the 2024/25 campaign, and Grbic was loaned out. He played regularly for Caykur Rizespor and returned to the Turkish Super Lig for the 2025/26 season, joining Fatih Karamguruk.

Despite his starting roles in Turkey, Grbic has hardly had a look-in with Ludogorets. He has been unused on the bench for all of their league matches, only playing once in a 3-0 Bulgarian Cup win over Beroe.

32-year-old German Hendrik Bonmann is the starter for Ludogorets, keeping five clean sheets and conceding only 11 goals in 13 league games. It seems that only an injury will bring former SUFC man Grbic into the side.

Grbic fared better in Turkey while on loan away from Sheffield United. That and his experience at a higher level means he would be backed to deliver for Ludogorets if they called him into a starting spot.

However, it seems he has an uphill battle on his hands to land a place.

Meanwhile, back at Bramall Lane, Chris Wilder has a dependable goalkeeper in Cooper. He came in and locked down the starting berth after Grbic and Wes Foderingham’s spells as the number one.

At 27, Cooper is at a great age to hold the starting berth at SUFC for a while yet. He could feasibly earn a step up to the Premier League, but with 38 clean sheets in 98 games for the Blades, the club will hope to keep him for a while longer.
