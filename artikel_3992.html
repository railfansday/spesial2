God of War's Dave Bautista Beefs Up 9kg for Kratos TV Show Role

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Dave Bautista has been busy in the gym after being cast as Kratos in Amazon Prime’s upcoming live action God of War adaptation.

Showing off in a branded shirt on social media, the former wrestler confirmed he’d put on 20lbs in muscle to play the role. That’s about 9kgs for those of you outside of the US.

The 57-year-old now weighs 250lbs (113kg), so is an absolute beefcake considering his age. He’s also 6’6” so certainly has the imposing presence required to play the protagonist.

Bautista is taking over the role from Ryan Hurst, who got injured during filming earlier in the year.

While I obviously sympathise with the original actor, I think Bautista is an upgrade – and he’s clearly put in the work to build the physique required to represent the character.

I’m excited to see the first photos of him in full makeup, with Kratos’ iconic red “lightning strike” tattoo.
