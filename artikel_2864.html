Matchday Musings: Sunderland Rediscover Their Identity At Villa Park

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Midway through the first half Saturday, this popped up in my feed…

…and I reacted the only way that felt right.

This is the Régis Le Bris Sunderland we've come to know and love over the past two years: desperate not to concede defensively, offensively bereft and a little lucky.

There was a cry for evolution at the end of last year and the beginning of this season and it led to more chances, more xG, but only eight goals in seven matches, with fourteen conceded and four points. In the last four matches, there have been fewer chances, a virtually non-existent xG only two goals, but three clean sheets and five points.

Our season started difficult because we had the ball with the ability to create chances to play good football at times, but we were often too exposed," said Le Bris afterwards.

"So it was really important to adjust this part of the game because if you are always chasing it's hard, even if you have a talented team. It was important to reduce the opponent's threats. I think that was the case today."

The Black Cats started (and almost finished) with Robin Roefs in goal, Reinildo, captain Luke O'Nien, Nordi Mukiele and Thomas Meunier in the back.

Jules Ahoka and Noah Sadiki sat in front of the backline with Chemsdine Talbi, Enzo Le Fée ahead on the left and centre, and Wilson Isidor alone up top. Trai Hume played nominally as a right winger, but even more than usual he often slotted into right back in a back five, as Meunier played tighter to Mukiele.

Villa enjoyed 73 percent of possession and outshot Sunderland 19-7, but none of the seven on target particularly troubled Roefs. For all their possession, Villa created only one big chance and less than a goal of xG.

"It's a bit boring to play that kind of game," said Meunier.

"Of course you'd like to have ball possession and play nice football and create many chances, but it's not always possible and we have to be honest as well."

"Aston Villa is a better team, but i think that our collective is really really wonderful and we've shown it against Chelsea and the game today."

Mukiele and the midfield three combined for 47 defensive contributions, led by Ahoka's sixteen in his twelfth senior match and first Premier League start, leaving Meunier suitably impressed.

"Jules was six months ago playing in Belgium for Antwerp and now he's like fucking Busquets," the Belgian said.

The solidity of the back line and midfield led to what seemed like a number of impatient and frustrated shot attempts by Villa. Nine of the total shots and six of the seven on goal came from outside the box, which included Ross Barkley's final attempt from thirty yards that was nowhere near the goal.

Barkley did test Roefs from a similar distance in the 83rd minute, but the Dutchman pushed his shot over the bar.

Roefs' best save of the match, however, came just a couple of minutes later as Villa captain Boubacar Kamara nodded a John McGinn free kick into the goal box toward teammate Brian Madjo. Mukiele won the header, but sent it goalward where Roefs had to parry it over.

Villa worked a carbon copy free kick in the first half, also onto Kamara's head. Leon Goretzka shanked the second ball, but Kamara hustled to the bylibe and headed again to Nicolas Jackson standing alone on the six-yard line. O'Nien, though, lunged back and kicked the pass away at the last second.

"The team spirit, the way we defended, our goal high up the pitch, the mid-press and lower on the pitch, the lads did really well to reduce their threat," Le Bris added.

Sunderland's counter-press did cause Villa problems when it had the opportunity, but the overall attacking threat was minimal.

Le Fée retained possession when he could and Talbi provided the primary counterattacking outlet. Aaron Wan-Bissaka had gotten the better of Talbi a couple of times early in the game, but in the 91st minute, Talbi absolutely dusted the DR Congo defender at midfield, cut in on his left in the box and just missed the far corner.

It was one of only seven Sunderland shots, and Le Fée's free kick straight at Zion Suzuki in the 72nd minute was the only one on target.

"If we were a bit smarter in some moments, like keeping the ball, making the right move, but it's not always easy to have this freshness," added Meunier.

"Because at the end, you struggle the whole game trying to keep the clean sheet and when you get the ball it's not always easy to sprint sixty meters or seventy metres to finish one on one against the goalkeeper."

The Black Cats are back at the Stadium of Light on Thursday for a Europa League matchup with Polish side Jagiellonia Bialystok.
