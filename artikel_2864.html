Greg Newsome injury: Giants CB out multiple weeks with ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

New York Giants starting cornerback Greg Newsome will be out multiple weeks with a high ankle sprain, head coach John Harbaugh said on Monday.

Newsome left Sunday's 23-18 victory over the Jacksonville Jaguars after playing only four snaps. He was replaced by Paulson Adebo, who ended up with an interception and four passes defensed.

"It's a serious high ankle," Harbaugh said. "It's going to be one of those lengthy high ankles. Gonna be multiple weeks."

Harbaugh did not say whether Newsome would undergo surgery or be placed on injured reserve.

A 2021 first-round pick by the Cleveland Browns, Newsome signed a one-year contract with the Giants in free agency this past offseason after a couple of sub-par years with the Browns and Jaguars.

The 26-year-old has had a resurgent season with the Giants. His passer rating against of 67.3 is the best of his six-year NFL career. His completion percentage against of 57.9 is just a percentage point off his career-best.

Newsome was rewarded with a three-year, $45.75 million contract extension recently. That deal runs through the 2029 season.
