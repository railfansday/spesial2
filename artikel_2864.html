Adetomiwa Adebawore gets an extension from the Colts

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Indianapolis Colts DL Adetomiwa Adebawore agreed to terms on a two-year, $35 million extension that includes a $13 million signing bonus and $22.5 million in new money through 2027 on Tuesday, Nov. 3.
