PL: Roberto De Zerbi elected coach of the month

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Roberto De Zerbi is rewarded for his excellent month of October! The Italian coach of Tottenham was voted best manager of the month in the Premier League, after completely relaunching a Spurs team which had previously struggled to obtain results. In a complicated sporting context, the former coach of Olympique de Marseille was able to restore confidence in his players and find the right formula. After a draw against Manchester United, Tottenham had three consecutive victories against Coventry, Chelsea and Crystal Palace. An almost perfect record which allows the Italian technician to win this individual distinction and to confirm the progress made by his team in recent weeks.

This good series above all allowed Tottenham to distance themselves from the relegation zone. With ten points collected out of twelve possible in October, Spurs now occupy eleventh place in the standings with twelve points. A significant comeback for a team which encountered great difficulties at the start of the season and seemed incapable of stringing together good performances. Under the orders of Roberto De Zerbi, the Londoners have regained positive momentum, notably thanks to their successes against Chelsea and Crystal Palace. Now installed in the middle of the table, the Spurs will try to continue this momentum to gradually get closer to European places.
