Collapse of COP31: 'This deal was a bad deal and better no deal than a bad deal'

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

François Picard is joined by Ireland's former Environment Minister Eamon Ryan following the collapse of COP31 in Antalya, Turkey. “Political science trumps climate science still,” Ryan said the talks failed after the European Union and up to 100 countries refused to accept a final text that abandoned earlier commitments to transition away from fossil fuels.
