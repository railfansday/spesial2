The Brooks Running Shoes to Power Your Soccer Training

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Find the right pair for sprint work, endurance runs, and active recovery days.

Anyone who’s ever huffed and puffed their way through a pickup soccer game after not playing for a while knows how quickly the sport can expose your conditioning. A match requires repeated bursts of speed with few opportunities to catch your breath, making stamina essential from the first minute through the 90th or beyond.

The average elite soccer player covers about 6.4 miles per game. But even if you’re just a weekend rec-leaguer, cross-training can help you cover more ground without fading late in the match. Running—including sprints, endurance work, and easy jogs—should be a regular part of your routine, and the right shoes can help you get more out of every workout.

Not sure which pair you need? Brooks—a leader in performance running shoes—offers an option for every type of run. Whether you’re doing sprint repeats, heading out for a 10K, or keeping things easy on an off day, here’s how to choose the right model for your workout.

You know what you don’t want to think about when you’re doing sprint drills? Your feet—or at least how your feet feel in your shoes. During workouts built around acceleration and all-out speed, you need a shoe that delivers support, flexibility, and comfort. For the Glycerin Flex, that’s check, check, and check.

Support comes in the form of Brooks’ proprietary nitrogen-infused DNA TUNED midsole foam, which features large cells in the heel to cushion your landings and smaller cells in the forefoot for responsive toe-offs. The combination helps you land comfortably and push off with power as you pick up the pace.

For sprint work, you also need a shoe that moves with you. The Glycerin Flex’s FlexZone technology combines a midfoot flex groove with a podular forefoot design to provide flexibility and support where you need it. A thin layer of sticky rubber on the outsole adds traction as you work on your speed.

You cover plenty of ground every time you take the field for a practice or match, making endurance work essential if you want to keep going through the final whistle. Longer runs can have a place in your conditioning on the days you aren’t wearing your cleats, as long as you build gradually and don’t overdo the mileage.

That’s where the Brooks Ghost Amp comes in. This new take on the brand’s popular Ghost model combines comfort with an energized ride for days when you want to take on a few more miles. Key among its features is SpringRamp forefoot technology, which helps you push off the balls of your feet with a little extra oomph.

Also new is the shoe’s nitrogen-infused DNA HYPE cushioning, which combines two types of foam: lightweight PEBA, which provides high energy return and a springy feel, and EVA, which offers cushioning and stability. Together, they deliver a balance of softness and responsiveness.

Up top, the lightweight warp-knit upper provides breathability and comfort, with enough structure to help secure your foot when you pick up the pace. The eye-catching color options add a little more personality to your training miles.

Recovery days are an essential part of any athlete’s training, especially soccer players. An easy walk or jog can help you stay loose, ease stiffness, and give your mind and body a reset.

But slowing the pace doesn’t mean forgetting about your footwear. On active recovery days, you still want a shoe that provides support and keeps you comfortable. The Glycerin Max 2 does both. Though it’s also built for longer runs, this plush shoe is a natural fit for easier days.

The defining feature of the Glycerin Max 2 is its dramatic GlideRoll Rocker technology. Its sculpted bevel and toe spring help your foot move smoothly from heel to toe. The shoe also features a high stack of DNA TUNED foam, which provides generous cushioning underfoot to keep your legs feeling fresh and protect your body against impact.

Active recovery may not directly put the ball in the net, but the work you do between matches can help you feel ready for the next one. And the right shoe can make that work more comfortable.
