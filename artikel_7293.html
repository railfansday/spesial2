Smartsheet: Software Engineer II, AI Experience (Remote Eligible)

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

For over 20 years, Smartsheet has empowered teams to manage work seamlessly and scale solutions smarter. Now, in our most ambitious chapter yet, we are uniting human teams with AI agents. By orchestrating the work agents do best, automating manual tasks and uncovering insights at scale, we create the space for people to focus on what truly matters: judgment, creativity, and big thinking. That is magic at work, and it’s what we show up for every day.

Smart Assist is Smartsheet's AI agent for work management, used by enterprise customers to analyze, create, and act on their work. AI Experiences builds the orchestration for Smart Assist and the user-facing implementations that expose Smartsheet's Intelligent Work capabilities to any AI surface, so users can reach the right intelligence at the right time through a coherent, reliable experience.

AI Experiences is a pillar within Applied AI, and this is a full-stack team. The work spans these areas: the Smart Assist frontend (UI components, interaction patterns, and rendering for AI experiences across Smartsheet); the Smart Assist backend and orchestrator; UX for Smart Assist tools and agents, from response formatting and onboarding to error states. On the backend, the orchestrator routes each request to the right sub-agent based on user intent, keeps conversation state across multi-turn interactions, handles fallbacks when sub-agents are unavailable, and controls feature-flagged rollout of new capabilities. We partner closely with Agent Engineering on much of this logic.

The role. You will own features and components of Smart Assist from prototype to production, across the front end, the orchestrator and back-end services. You will turn ambiguous product asks into reliable, well-measured behavior customers can trust, and work with product, design, and AI Experience to ship.

You would help define the trust model for that experience from real customer learnings and feedback.
