AI promised 10x. Most companies got 1.2x. Wrike founder Andrew Filev launches Hops to close the gap

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

AI made people faster. Hops is built to make teams faster.

SAN FRANCISCO, Nov. 10, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The Hopsâ¢ Collaborative AI workspace opens to everyone today, after more than 2,500 teams used it in early access. Until now, AI at work has mostly meant individuals working out of their own private chat windows. Built by Wrike founder Andrew Filev, Hops brings AI to teamwork: people and AI work together in everything from chats and docs to slides and custom apps, and what one person teaches Hops, the whole team can use.

AI can now do in minutes what used to take an afternoon. But businesses don't run on one-off deliverables. They run on projects and programs that whole teams move forward together. Most AI tools are still built for one person in a private chat with an assistant such as ChatGPTâ¢. Teammates copy and paste results, repeat context and coordinate next steps by hand, and what each person learns stays buried in their own chats. Individuals got faster. Their companies barely did.

"I spent 20 years building software to help teams work together. Then AI arrived, and the industry started building tools for people to work alone," said Filev, founder and CEO of Hops. "When I ask founders and executives of established companies how much faster their company moves than it did a year ago, the answer is usually 10 to 20 percent. That's well short of 2x, and of what the individual gains would suggest. The PC era didn't pay off when PCs got faster. It paid off when they were networked. I believe AI will follow the same path."

Adding an AI bot to an existing collaboration tool only takes a team so far. That's why the Hops team built the whole workspace from the ground up, so AI works inside everything a team uses, down to its own custom apps.

On the Hops team, every ad, email and landing page used to wait in line for the head of marketing's review. Then she taught Hops the marketing voice: which message leads, what each audience cares about and which examples to use. Now a salesperson can ask Hops for a customer email in the team chat and get one in the company's voice right away, written straight into the shared doc. The head of marketing spends her time on strategy instead of line edits. The team's designer did the same with the company mascot, so anyone he gives access to can add it to any graphic or video, even a teammate's birthday card, and it comes out on-brand.Four things make that possible:

"I wanted our sales and marketing teams working together in Hops, reviewing the pipeline, drafting follow-ups for people we meet at events and prospects who visit our website, and building campaigns. Hops replaced major tools like Slack and Jira for us," said Sai Venkataraman, CEO of Discern Security.

A team of 30 engineers and a fleet of AI agents rebuilt the collaboration stack in a year. The Hops team now runs its own CRM, support desk, IT requests and project tracking in Hops. Employees even ask Hops about leave policy and get an answer with a link to the handbook.

"This is a David and Goliath moment, and I wouldn't have it any other way. Some of the world's biggest companies are spending billions on personal AI assistants. We're betting on something different: people do their best work together, and AI should make the whole team better," Filev said.

Hops is available now at hops.ai, and signing up is free. There are no per-seat fees, and because a team's docs, slides, dashboards and apps all live in one workspace, teams can cut down on separate subscriptions. Hops also connects to more than 2,000 apps, from CRMs to cloud drives, so teams can keep using the tools they still need. Customers buy AI credits, which Hops uses to perform tasks such as drafting documents or building presentations. Hops never uses customer data to train models.
