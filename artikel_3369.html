'The Last of Us' Meets 'Silo' in Hulu’s 4-Part Sci-Fi Thriller

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Post-apocalyptic sci-fi thrillers have become some of TV's best shows. With examples like The Last of Us or Silo, these stories have grown incredibly popular for their spectacle and epic tales. The genre is more than gruesome stories about the world ending, as it offers a deep exploration of universal human struggles. While these recent examples have earned considerable attention, and rightfully so, they are not the first. In fact, Snowpiercer took a similar approach in 2020. The series creates an immersive world full of danger and division that all fans of the genre should watch.

Snowpiercer delivers a unique premise and incredible worldbuilding that sets it apart. Inspired by the 2013 film of the same name, the story isn't totally original, but the series builds on the concept that always deserved more than two hours. And Snowpiercer certainly expands the idea with four unforgettable seasons. Though it may not have gained the popularity that other comparable series have, it still deserves attention. Six years later, Snowpiercer is the perfect binge watch for anyone who loves sci-fi thrillers, dystopian worlds, or complex moral questions.

Snowpiercer features a world like no other, where a massive luxury train is the only place people can live. Eerily set in 2026, the story takes place seven years after an environmental disaster created a new ice age, killing much of human civilization. However, some survive on a constantly moving train designed by eccentric billionaire Mr. Joseph Wilford (Sean Bean). While the movement keeps them alive, the survivors continue to struggle. The system is strictly divided into distinct social classes. The elite live in the front, third-class laborers keep the machine running, and the stowaways, known as the Tailies, are just trying to survive. However, the dynamic alters when a series of murders threatens the train. Head Engineer Melanie Cavill (Jennifer Connelly) pulls Chicago police officer Andre Layton (Daveed Diggs) up from the Tail to uncover the truth.

Layton has his own motivations. He dreams of revolution and believes this investigation can take him one step closer, allowing him to map out the train. However, the fallout spirals as the train is full of secrets. Most prominently, the passengers begin to realize that inventor Joseph Wilford isn't running the train because Melanie abandoned him during boarding, leaving him for dead. Although this society is confined to a train, Snowpiercer covers a lot of ground as it explores the brutal realities of civilization, oppression, and unavoidable moral questions of survival. This is the crux of the series, giving it a dark and fascinating tone.

What stands out most about Snowpiercer is its worldbuilding. A train is an uncharacteristic setting, but it comes with plenty to explore as the series delves into how different biomes separate the classes, how food is grown, and even how the passengers deal with their dead. These may seem like small details, but they reveal a lot about the world and the desperate characters who inhabit it. Through these elements, the series brings this society to life and creates something new. As the story goes on, Snowpiercer even explores beyond the train, showing the devastation of the icy world and further immersing the audience in the dystopian setting.

Idris Elba has fans on the edges of their train seats.

Though this concept is rare, the story is inspired by the movie, but the two are far from the same. Of course, there are similarities, but Snowpiercer isn't even strictly a remake. The series takes the concept, but uses different characters and circumstances to tell its own story. With significantly more time, the series sets a slower pace, building tension and introducing the world a little at a time, which is a luxury the film doesn't have. Ultimately, that dedication to the story is what makes Snowpiercer work. The series stands out, even as the dystopian genre grows, and though it's a few years old now, Snowpiercer is an unmatched sci-fi watch that deserves more attention.

Snowpiercer is streaming on Hulu in the U.S.
