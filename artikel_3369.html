November 18 Is Officially A Huge Day For Xbox Game Pass

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Xbox Game Pass is having a huge day today, November 18, as several new titles are being added to the subscription service’s catalog, granting access to even more players than before. The latest updates to Microsoft’s subscription service have been announced. As part of the Wave 2 updates in November, the company also announced the first games joining Xbox Game Pass in December 2026, for example. Of course, throughout the second half of November, there’s still a lot going on, and November 18 may represent the most important day of this period.

Microsoft made an official announcement regarding the Wave 2 updates for Xbox Game Pass in November via a post on the Xbox Wire website. There, it details everything that is happening with the service during the second half of November, and there are a total of seven new game updates scheduled for the period between November 18 and November 27, excluding the titles from December and the ones that are leaving on November 30. It just so happens, however, that out of these seven updates, three of them happen today, November 18.

As is seen in the official announcement, the first update on November 18 is for Call of the Elder Gods. The sequel to Call of the Sea joined Xbox Game Pass in May 2026 as a day-one release and, as such, access to it was limited to the Ultimate and PC Game Pass tiers. Now, the Lovecraftian puzzle-adventure game is also becoming available for members of the Game Pass Premium tier. The same update is also happening for High on Life 2 and Roadside Research, as they join the Premium tier catalog on November 18.

These are still considered day-one releases on Xbox Game Pass, though they are now open to a much larger audience, since Ultimate members are the ones who get access to day-one titles on launch. The three titles are very different from each other. As was mentioned, Call of the Elder Gods is a horror-themed puzzle-adventure game, while High on Life 2 is a fast-paced first-person sci-fi shooter. Meanwhile, Roadside Research is a co-op gas station simulator in which you control aliens working undercover, disguised as humans, to learn more about them and their habits.

With all three games getting these updates at once today, Premium members are going to have a blast. Of course, there are more new additions in store for the rest of the month. Black Jacket and Motorslice will also join the Premium tier catalog soon, while Sniper Ghost Warrior Contracts 2 and Arkheron are added to the service in late November. This is disregarding all the previously added titles on Xbox Game Pass in November 2026, like Gravhounds and Thronefall.

A lot has already been confirmed for November, and while only two titles have officially been announced for December, the next big period is already looking promising, especially if it follows the quality of the games offered before it. We’ll soon get a better sense of how Xbox Game Pass will wrap up 2026, but meanwhile, be sure to check out the updates that just dropped.
