All 8 Episodes Of HBO's 'Modern Sopranos' Masterpiece Are Perfect From Start To Finish

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

HBO Max has a modern Sopranos-like series that has eight episodes that are perfect from start to finish. HBO’s best shows tend to be in the drama genre, with The Sopranos being one of them. Similarly, the iconic show has aged well on HBO, overcoming the possible revisionism that tends to happen with early 2000s products. Several modern projects will likely do the same as time goes on, though revisionism eventually comes for all things.

That said, The Sopranos provides more evidence of aging well than anything else, given that the show is almost 30 years old and still counted among the best TV shows of all time. Whether these aforementioned modern productions will eclipse this reputation remains to be seen, but there are several that emulate The Sopranos in several ways. One such show is a drama that actually exists within another genre first, despite being more like The Sopranos than anything else.

That show is The Penguin, the spin-off to 2022’s The Batman. The show was released in 2024 and focuses on the titular Batman villain, otherwise known as Oz Cobb. Across eight episodes, Oz navigates the criminal underworld of Gotham in the aftermath of The Batman, rising to power as a ruthless crime lord. With such a premise, The Penguin has many more similarities to The Sopranos than any other comic book project. For one, Oz Cobb and Tony Soprano are both physically imposing main characters who have to manage ruthless crime families.

In fact, Colin Farrell’s prosthetics even make him look physically like Tony Soprano, and his complex relationship with his mother adds a more nuanced similarity. Beyond that, both The Penguin and The Sopranos feature crime families at their center. This means that both deal with well-written relationships and bonds that make viewers unsure of whom to root for, or who will betray whom. Adding in the visual language of a mobster story, The Penguin is more like a modern Sopranos than a Batman spin-off and, as its excellent reception proved, was a true masterpiece.

Although The Penguin's Sopranos comparisons could be used against it as a comic book story, they actually have the opposite effect. In the 2020s, superhero TV shows have become more abundant than ever, with constantly increasing scale, budgets, and connectivity to wider franchises. As a result, this type of superhero show has become somewhat stale, which is what makes The Penguin so special. By embodying a true crime drama like The Sopranos, The Penguin established itself as a standout in the superhero realm.

Beyond a single shot of the Bat-signal in the final minutes of The Penguin's ending, the show has no overt connections to a world filled with superheroes and villains. Yes, there are Easter eggs and references that Batman fans will recognize, but they are presented in a grounded way. Ultimately, The Penguin becomes a crime drama through and through, shaking off any superhero trappings that may have plagued TV shows of the genre in other franchises of late.

All of this makes The Penguin a true masterpiece from beginning to end, but it makes the unclear future of the franchise much harder to take. In 2026, it was confirmed that The Batman - Part II had hit more production hurdles, with Matt Reeves stepping away from the production due to personal issues. Steven Soderbergh was hired to complete production, but, added to the already lengthy delay between the first movie and its sequel, this caused more worries that The Batman - Part III may never happen.

Also in 2026, it was announced that James Gunn and Peter Safran were stepping down as the co-heads of DC Studios, further throwing the future of the franchise into doubt. After all, Gunn was a big advocate of Reeves and his vision for The Batman universe. Now, with Gunn no longer in charge and Reeves continually struggling to balance his personal struggles and the commitment that The Batman requires, the future of this sect of the DC Universe, including The Penguin and its characters, hangs in the balance.

DCU fans are still reeling from the surprise ousting of James Gunn and Peter Safran as the heads of DC Studios, creating more than a few big questions

The final nail in this possible proverbial coffin could be Robert Pattinson's recent casting as James Bond. This could pull the actor away from playing Batman for an extended period of time, further casting a shadow over it. Of course, all could be well for the future of The Batman's Epic Crime Saga, which fans of The Penguin would certainly hope for. Unfortunately, though, the continuation of HBO's modern Sopranos masterpiece is only speculative as of 2026's conclusion.
