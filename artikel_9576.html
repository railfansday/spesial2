How Google AI Overviews May Describe Your Website?

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

We've spent years worrying about how Google displays our page titles and meta descriptions.

But there's another thing worth paying attention to.

Brodie Clark recently spotted Google showing AI-written descriptions inside some AI Overview source cards. Barry Schwartz also reported seeing similar behaviour in certain searches.

Someone searches for a service your business offers.

Your website appears as a source in Google's AI Overview.

But instead of simply displaying your own description, Google appears to write a short summary of the page.

Would that summary explain your service correctly?

Would it mention the important conditions, limitations, or details?

Would someone understand what your business actually offers without visiting the page?

I've always believed important information should be easy to understand without forcing readers to connect five different paragraphs.

That becomes even more relevant when search systems are generating their own summaries.

I'm not suggesting we rewrite every page or chase another AI SEO tactic.

This behaviour has only been observed in some searches. Google hasn't confirmed a full rollout, and we don't have evidence of its impact on rankings or clicks.

But I'd start reviewing how my most important pages communicate their information.

Would you be comfortable letting Google describe your business in its own words?
