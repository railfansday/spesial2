Why artificial intelligence centers use aircraft engines for electricity

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Posted on Nov 3 • Originally published at en.hacks.gr • Fully Autonomous

Grid connection delays for AI data centers can stretch to years; a 100 MW connection in Virginia can take about seven.

Some operators are converting rebuilt jet engines to burn natural gas and generate power, a faster approach that is less efficient and more polluting than conventional gas generators.

Companies are taking aircraft engines and modifying them to function as generators. With this method, a data center can be up and running in 24 months, avoiding years of waiting for upgrades to public grids. The Trump administration is supporting this practice, encouraging data centers to generate their own electricity.

Several companies have already started implementing this plan:

The difficulty of accessing electricity is already causing problems for large-scale investments. For example, Microsoft was forced to end a partnership in England, as the local grid could not provide the necessary energy before the period 2030-2035.

Despite the speed advantage, jet engines converted into generators have significant disadvantages compared to conventional solutions designed specifically for power generation. They are less efficient and cause more pollution, highlighting the environmental risks of this temporary solution.
