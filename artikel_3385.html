’Yellowstone’s Biggest Star Is Officially Adapting a Bestselling FBI Memoir

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Taylor Sheridan's Yellowstone is one of the most popular TV shows of the last 10 years, and while Kevin Costner's role in the show certainly helped draw viewers, the series helped turn some of its regulars into huge stars. Luke Grimes, for example, had featured in other projects before Yellowstone like American Sniper, but it wasn't until Yellowstone that he blossomed into one of the world's biggest action/western stars. Grimes can be seen starring in the hit Yellowstone spin-off, Marshals, and he also recently featured in Ari Aster's A24 Western, Eddington, along with Joaquin Phoenix and Pedro Pascal. Marshals is one of two Yellowstone spin-offs to take over streaming on Paramount Plus streaming charts, with the other being Dutton Ranch. The series returns tenured Yellowstone veterans Cole Hauser and Kelly Reilly to play Rip Wheeler and Beth Dutton.

Long before he ever starred in a single episode of Yellowstone, Cole Hauser developed a name for himself, featuring in other projects such as Good Will Hunting and Olympus Has Fallen. While he is set to star in the second season of Dutton Ranch on Paramount Plus, that also isn't stopping him from finding new projects through his American Outlaw Entertainment banner. Last week, news broke that Hauser's AOE is partnering with Constantin Film to adapt the bestselling true-crime memoir, Code Name: Pale Horse, by Scott Payne and Michelle Shephard. Payne is at the center of the story as he infiltrated the neo-Nazi white supremacist group The Base in Georgia. He also went on a two-year assignment known as Operation Roadkill, where he successfully infiltrated Outlaws Motorcycle Club in Massachusetts.

At the time of writing, it's not officially confirmed or reported that Cole Hauser is going to star in Pale Horse, but he is interested in headlining the film. Right now, his role is limited to producing through the aforementioned American Outlaw Entertainment, but only time will tell if he also inks a deal to star in the project. Hauser's producing partner at AOE, Randall Batinkoff, is adapting the screenplay for Pale Horse, and Robert Kulzer will also produce with Payne and Oliver Berben. Whenever it hits theaters or streaming, the true-crime story is going to be must-watch entertainment.
