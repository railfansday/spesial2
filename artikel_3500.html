Unlike serums and creams that rely on active ingredients, Frownies take a more mechanical approach to skincare. The patches are made from paper with a water-activated adhesive that holds the skin in place, temporarily limiting the facial movements that contribute to expression lines. The idea is to help soften the appearance of existing wrinkles with consistent use, although results vary from one person to the next.

Applying them is super simple. Just moisten the adhesive side of each patch with water, allow it to become slightly tacky, and apply it to clean, dry skin while gently smoothing the area underneath. The brand recommends leaving the patches on for at least three hours or overnight, then thoroughly wetting them before removal. And, because they’re available in different shapes, you can customize their placement around the outer corners of your eyes and mouth.

One five-star reviewer, who called the patches “botox in a box,” wrote, “They do work and help a lot with headaches. You get so many of these per box that, depending on how much you use per night, you might get a few months’ worth of frownies treatments, which is worth the price of a box.”

“With consistent use, they really do help train the facial muscles and soften expression lines,” shared another five-star reviewer. “It’s amazing to see the difference they make. I’ve always used warm water to activate the adhesive, and it works perfectly. In the past, I wore them overnight, although sometimes they’d become uncomfortable as they dried and I’d end up removing them during the night. These days, I wear them for about three hours, and that works great for me.”

For anyone curious about adding a non-invasive step to their anti-aging routine, Frownies offer an affordable place to start. At $26, the patches are a relatively small investment compared with many high-end skincare treatments, and Close’s enthusiasm makes them all the more intriguing. Just remember that consistency is key.
