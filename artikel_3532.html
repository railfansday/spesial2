3 Best Movies Streaming For Free This Weekend (November 21-22)

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Tubi has an intriguing selection of movies to watch this weekend, leaning into sci-fi, but with each of them providing a different kind of story, so viewers can choose what suits their taste the best. On last weekend's Tubi movie recommendations, I went over why users of the free streamer should watch Priscilla, The Night Before, and The Hunger Games: The Ballad Of Songbirds & Snakes.

None of those movies are trending on Tubi right now, but I believe it is important to keep the latter in mind. After all, Mckenna Grace's The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is now playing in theaters, marking the second prequel in the hit dystopian sci-fi franchise. With that in mind, The Ballad Of Songbirds & Snakes remains worth watching on Tubi.

For our new weekend recommendations, I felt leaning into sci-fi this week made sense due to the major new theatrical release, and I also chose to include another The Hunger Games movie that pairs off well with Sunrise on the Reaping. As for the rest of our Tubi movie recommendations, viewers can get two different types of sci-fi stories.

While The Hunger Games franchise is all about dystopian stories, the other genre entries on our list include one of the most beloved time loop stories of all time, as well as an action-packed movie with major stars that is all about introducing a world where giant beasts and monsters exist. These are the best movies to watch on Tubi for free this weekend:

First up is Groundhog Day, the only non-franchise release on our Tubi list and also the biggest classic among the options. The film follows Bill Murray as Phil Connors, a weatherman who is very much unhappy about having to cover a small town's annual Groundhog Day event. However, when he finds himself stuck in a time loop, forced to relive the exact same day over and over, Phil is pushed to change.

Directed by Murray's Ghostbusters co-star Harold Ramis, Groundhog Day is an unforgettable movie, filled with sharp comedic moments, such as the "I'm a god" scene at the diner, where Murray's character showcases the time he has spent in the time loop by telling Andie MacDowell's Rita Hanson about the lives and secrets of patrons and workers. Groundhog Day is rewatchable, funny, heartwarming, and a great story about personal growth.

On to the franchise movies on Tubi this weekend, Kong: Skull Island has just become available on the free streaming service this Friday, November 20. It is the second movie in the live-action Monsterverse, setting the stage for future Kong appearances, as well as Skull Island as a major location for the franchise, including the Apple TV kaiju series Monarch: Legacy of Monsters. Kong: Skull Island presents a straightforward story.

A group of scientists and soldiers venture onto Skull Island on a research expedition led by Monarch head Bill Randa. There, they are faced with growing threats, making this an action-packed sci-fi movie where the death toll is quite high. Kong: Skull Island is worth watching for how it reintroduces King Kong in live-action, sets up so much Monsterverse lore, and features talented stars, top-notch CGI, and shocking action sequences. Marvel stars Tom Hiddleston, Brie Larson, and Samuel L. Jackson are part of the talented ensemble.

Finally, after going over a couple of franchise movies over the past few weeks to prepare viewers for Sunrise on the Reaping's theatrical release, now is the time to watch The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2 for free on Tubi. The Haymitch prequel perfectly ties into the themes and events of the final film in the core The Hunger Games franchise, so watching it after finishing Sunrise on the Reaping is a perfect combo.

The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2 also stands on its own as an emotional and high-stakes sci-fi story about defiance and the toll war takes on someone. It is dark, gripping, and topical, with powerful performances from Jennifer Lawrence, Josh Hutcherson, Philip Seymour Hoffman, and more. It ends our Tubi list with a bang.
