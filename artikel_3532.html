Ian Somerhalder’s Shirtless Photo Grabs the Attention of Costar Paul Wesley

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Ian Somerhalder sure knows how to grab the attention of the Internet over a relaxing Thanksgiving break.

On Thursday, November 27, The Vampire Diaries actor got fans talking when he posted a shirtless photo online.

“✅Grateful for YOU ALL,” Somerhalder, 47, wrote via Instagram while posing shirtless outside the room of a home gym. “TBT and ready to get back to this in 2027! Using Absorb Co products!”

The Absorption Company is a supplement brand cofounded by Somerhalder and his wife, Nikki Reed, which focuses on increasing nutrient bioavailability.

In his social media post, the actor and businessman also said he was grateful for his Brother’s Bond Bourbon and his Groundswell film, which was released earlier this year.

“I’m so grateful for the love and support for these massive tentpole things in my life that I am building! What are you grateful for?” he asked. “Getting back to this body at 48 is going to take dedication, discipline and absorption!”

Instead of sharing what they were thankful for, many followers were quick to praise Somerhalder — who shares two children with Reed, 38 — for his physique.

“I’m grateful for the internet, which gave me the opportunity to see this picture on Thanksgiving🥵,” one fan wrote via Instagram. Another user added, “Not Ian breaking the Thanksgiving internet! ❤️❤️❤️.”

The photo even grabbed the attention of Somerhalder’s TVD costar Paul Wesley, who reportedly left a funny comment that read, “Blink twice if u need help.”

Somerhalder and Reed — who have been married since 2015 — quietly left Los Angeles to raise their kids on a California farm.

“I start my days at 5 [a.m.] and I usually finish them around midnight,” Reed once exclusively shared with Us in May 2024. “It is really full on and full time.”

Although acting roles don’t appear to be the priority for both Reed and Somerhalder at this chapter in their lives, they are always grateful for their careers in Hollywood, which have given them the opportunity to focus on causes near and dear to their hearts.

“The entertainment world really gave us these amazing platforms,” Somerhalder once told Us. “We’re super grateful for it.”
