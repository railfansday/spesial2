E. Lockhart, Gotham Group Tackling Fantasy Novel ‘The Curse Workers’ for 20th Television (Exclusive)

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The trilogy hails from fan favorite author Holly Black, known for 'The Spiderwick Chronicles.'

20th Television is in development on The Curse Workers, the best-selling fantasy books from Holly Black, with a plan to adapt it for a TV series to stream on Disney+.

E. Lockhart, the author turned showrunner behind Prime Video’s hit We Were Liars, will adapt the trilogy into a series, serving as writer and executive producer.

Also exec producing are Ellen Goldsmith-Vein and Jeremy Bell for The Gotham Group. The project marks a reunion for Gotham Group and Black; the company produced the 2023 series adaptation of The Spiderwick Chronicles, based on the children’s series that Black co-wrote with Tony DiTerlizzi. Curse Workers is set in a world where magic is illegal and controlled by the Mob. The story centers on Cassel Sharpe, the youngest son of a criminal family and the only one without magic. When his mother returns from prison, his brothers start acting strange, and his own memories are no longer making sense, forcing him to rely on his knowledge of con artistry to uncover the truth.

Lockhart is the New York Times bestselling author who wrote the book sensation We Were Liars, and then was intimately involved in the book’s translation to mystery thriller series, which reached No. 1 on Prime Video and was quickly renewed for a second season, now in production. Lockhart wrote the season one finale, participated in casting and location scouting, and continues as an executive producer on season two, which incorporates the story of her prequel novel, Family of Liars.

In addition to Liars, Lockhart is adapting her own novel Again Again for Wayfarer Entertainment while novel Genuine Fraud is in development as a series at Prime Video, with Rain Spencer attached to star and Sinead Daly writing the pilot.

One of the biggest names in fantasy, Black is known for her New York Times best-sellers including Novels of Elfhame, The Coldest Girl in Coldtown and The Charlatan Duology, as well as an Arthurian picture book called Sir Morien. She has been a finalist for an Eisner Award and a Lodestar Award, and the recipient of a Mythopoeic Award, a Nebula Award and a Newbery Honor. Her books have sold over 40 million copies and been translated into 32 languages worldwide.

Working in both film and television, Gotham is one of the entities behind Disney+ hit Percy Jackson and the Olympians and exec produced the Apple TV+ and Skydance Animation series The Search for WondLa as well as the Hulu limited series Washington Black. It’s most recent movie was the 20th Century Studios release Springsteen: Deliver Me from Nowhere, written and directed by Scott Cooper.

Lockhart is repped by Verve, Anonymous Content, and Elizabeth Kaplan Literary Agency. Black is repped by CAA and New Leaf Literary & Media.
