Josh Brolin's latest survival thriller has suffered a major box-office setback.

Directed by Brian Duffield, Whalefall stars Austin Abrams as Jay Gardiner, a scuba diver who is swallowed by a massive sperm whale while searching for his late father's remains. Brolin plays Jay's father, Mitt, alongside Elisabeth Shue and Jane Levy.

According to Box Office Mojo, the movie lost 1,625 theaters during its fourth weekend, dropping from 2,000 locations to just 375. The significant theater reduction comes less than a month after the movie's October 16 release.

Whalefall is currently playing in select theaters.
