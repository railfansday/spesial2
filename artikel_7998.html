“In recent years, we’ve seen retail drift into a sort of sameness,” write Nancy and Kimberly Wu. “Building Block Etc. is our way back to meaning—a space for connection, where relationships happen face-to-face, and where you might hold something in your hands before choosing to make it part of your life.” The sisters are both industrial designers who studied at the ArtCenter College of Design in Pasadena and had impressive corporate careers before teaming up. Kim was a car concept designer for Honda in Tokyo and Nancy worked on performance footwear for Nike in Portland. They’re first generation Taiwanese-Americans, raised in Southern California—their father is an engineer—and after moonlighting long distance on their own designs, they returned home to their parents’ house in Orange County to launch Building Block.

That was back in 2011—the sisters were 25 and 27—and the brand started as a collection of five geometric handbags that attracted a cult following. It led to a successful shop and workshop in LA’s Chinatown, where the line expanded to include housewares. In 2023, Kimberly and Nancy transitioned their business into a creative consultancy, Studio Building Block, offering industrial design solutions. And more recently, they opened Building Block Etc., a shop showcasing a tightly edited array of their own designs and work by fellow maker friends. Explains Nancy, “we’re drawn to pieces that have clarity in material and form, a sense of utility, and the ability to reveal more over time.”

Photography courtesy of Building Block Etc. (@buildingblock).

Building Block Etc. is at 3021 Rowena Ave. in Silver Lake; it’s open Wed.–Sun., 11-5.
