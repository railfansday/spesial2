Something Strange at Maggie Ellis’s Thanksgiving Table

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

In Ellis's world, you can never be quite sure whether you've stumbled into a dream, a nightmare, or someone else's very strange family gathering.

It’s fitting that Maggie Ellis’s exhibition opened the day before Halloween. Her canvases teem with creatures, graveyard scenes, and all manner of ghoulish debauchery, making for a spooky and thoroughly entertaining show.

There is, yes, another iteration of Ophelia from Hamlet, which feels a bit overdone, but I’ll forgive it for the rest of the wacky scenes she has going on.

There’s a forest fire rendered in bright red, complete with snakes, a lizard the size of a human wearing a dress, a severed hand, and a woman leaning against a tree doomscrolling on her phone.
