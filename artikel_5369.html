Goodfirms Survey Finds 66.3% of Marketers Reported Organic Traffic Growth Even as AI Search Gains Ground

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

LAS VEGAS, Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- In a survey of 192 content marketing professionals, 60.9% also reported growth in content-driven leads or conversions.

Goodfirms' 2026 Content Engagement Metrics report found that 66.3% of surveyed content marketers reported organic website traffic growth over the previous 12 months. During the same period, 63% reported stronger content engagement, and 60.9% reported growth in content-driven leads or conversions.

The findings come as AI summaries change how people interact with Google results. Pew Research Center found that users clicked a traditional search result on 8% of visits with an AI summary, compared with 15% of visits without one.

In the Goodfirms survey, 65.2% of respondents said AI search or answer engines had become more important to content distribution. Organic search and SEO gained importance for 56.5%, while 53.3% said the same of LinkedIn. Businesses can compare generative engine optimization (GEO) agencies for help improving visibility in AI-generated answers, SEO agencies for organic search, and social media marketing agencies for distribution on platforms such as LinkedIn.

Content practices associated with stronger results

The figures reflect respondents' own assessments. Businesses evaluating digital marketing agencies can use the findings to ask how providers measure traffic, engagement and leads.
