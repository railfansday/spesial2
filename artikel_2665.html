Todd Bowles: Baker Mayfield's made progress, decision on playing to come on Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Buccaneers quarterback Baker Mayfield missed practice on Wednesday with a foot injury, but the team hasn't made a decision on his availability for Sunday's game against the Lions.

Mayfield suffered the injury during Tampa's Week 9 loss to the Bears and he recuperated during the bye week. Head coach Todd Bowles said that has allowed him to improve enough for the team to leave the door open for him to play this weekend.

"He's made a lot of progress," Bowles said, via the team's website. "We want to see him do some things later on today and tomorrow. We'll kind of make that determination on Friday. But he's a lot better than he was a week ago. I don't know if that's ready or not. I want to see it for myself and we'll see what he can do tomorrow."

Rookie Jalon Daniels worked with the first team on Wednesday. Easton Stick was signed to the active roster and the Bucs also added Cooper Rush to the practice squad this week.
