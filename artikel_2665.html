Kylian Mbappé surpasses Ronaldo&#8217;s Real Madrid goal tally

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Real Madrid star Kylian Mbappé on Tuesday overtook a club legend in Los Blancos' goalscoring charts. This came courtesy of his exploits against PSV Eindhoven.

José Mourinho's troops marked their return to action on the continent this evening, welcoming PSV to Spain's capital for a Champions League clash.

When all was said and done, Real emerged dominant victors, recovering from a goal down at the interval to ultimately put five past their hapless Dutch opponents.

The aforementioned Mbappé played a starring role in the rout, notching a fine brace to keep alive a superb campaign to date in front of goal.

And as alluded to above, in the process, the 27-year-old made some individual history. As revealed by Spanish statistician MisterChip, Mbappé has now scored more goals in the colours of Real Madrid than the legendary Ronaldo Nazário.

The latter managed 104 in 177 Blancos appearances, whilst Mbappé has racked up 105 in 121 outings for the La Liga heavyweights.
