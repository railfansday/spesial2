AstraZeneca has launched a three-year cost-cutting program that aims to slice off $1.5 billion a year, with the savings reinvested in R&D, Pharmaphorum writes. There’s no word yet on the job implications from the initiative, which the company estimates will carry a one-time charge of around $2 billion and is expected to be completed by the end of 2029. The drug maker is already facing headwinds from the loss of patent protection for some products, including diabetes blockbuster Farxiga, with a string of other big brands due to lose exclusivity between now and the mid-2030’s, including three cancer drugs.

Indian regulators ordered Sun Pharmaceutical to stop manufacturing, selling and distributing ophthalmic products made at its Assam facility after finding failure in ​its quality management system, Reuters reported. The Central Drugs Standard Control Organization and the office of Drugs Controller Assam ordered the shutdown on ‌Aug. 20 after a joint federal-state inspection identified lapses in the system meant to ensure product quality and safety, following Sun Pharma’s move in July to voluntarily recall about 11 eye-care brands. Sun Pharma had previously said the site was expected to become one of its largest manufacturing ⁠hubs for sterile products in a business identified as a key global growth driver.
