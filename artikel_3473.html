Spoiler alert for the post-credits scene of VisionQuest

Fan reactions are officially in for Marvel's post-credit scenes of VisionQuest.

The Disney+ series concludes the WandaVision trilogy, following White Vision's journey to reconnect with his humanity. However, the finale saved its biggest surprise for last. MCU post-credits scenes have long been a fan-favorite tradition, giving audiences one last surprise after a movie or show's ending. VisionQuest is no exception, with its second post-credits scene delivering a major reveal.

Fans have taken to social media to celebrate the return of one of the MCU's most beloved characters. The scene reveals Doctor Doom resurrecting Wanda Maximoff, potentially setting up Elizabeth Olsen's Scarlet Witch for a return in Avengers: Doomsday.

Fans are not holding back their excitement. Taking to X, @POPin4k exclaimed:

"WANDA IS ALIVE OMG WE ARE SO BACK... MOTHER RETURNS TO THE MCU"

Similarly, @siilllver celebrated the reveal, writing:

"LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THE QUEEN HAS RETURNED"

The excitement continued, with @syriangeekvibes declaring, "WE ARE SO BEYOND BACK," while @oyemnassxo celebrated Wanda's return following Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness.

Meanwhile, @bekhayalime expressed their loyalty to the character, writing:

"SCARLETT WITCH YOU HAVE MY LOYALTY FOREVER"

The reveal has also fueled excitement for Avengers: Doomsday. @AvengersUpdated hinted at a potential connection, writing:

"The ‘AVENGERS: DOOMSDAY’ clock is now glowing red following the Scarlet Witch’s return."

The excitement has also extended beyond Wanda's resurrection. Marvel fan account @AvengersUpdated pointed to another potential development, writing, "The ‘AVENGERS: DOOMSDAY’ clock is now glowing red following the Scarlet Witch’s return."

The reactions reflect just how anticipated Wanda's return has been since Marvel confirmed Scarlet Witch's death following Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness. With Vision, Billy, and Tommy reunited during the finale, the Scarlet Witch's resurrection also opens the door to a long-awaited Maximoff family reunion.

The reveal is especially surprising considering Olsen previously claimed she had no knowledge of her MCU future. During a March 2026 appearance at Chicago Comic & Entertainment Expo, the actress said she didn't know about her return in Avengers: Doomsday or Avengers: Secret Wars, stating, "I don’t know nothing about any of it." However, Olsen has also expressed interest in returning as Scarlet Witch, making her unexpected comeback a major moment for longtime fans.

Wanda's return also confirms a major fan theory that had been circulating ahead of the finale. With the Maximoff family slowly reuniting throughout VisionQuest, fans had theorized that Doctor Doom could be responsible for the Scarlet Witch's resurrection, particularly following the reveal of mysterious Latverian witches in Avengers: Doomsday. Now, the post-credits scene has seemingly brought that theory to life, raising new questions about Doom's plans for Wanda.

This would explain the buzzing excitement from fans, including Kate, who wrote, "How am I supposed to sleep after this? I feel like I can run through a wall." Her reaction falls in line with just countless posts shared within the first hour of the season finale's release.

The reactions reflect just how anticipated Wanda's return has been since her apparent death in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness. With Vision, Billy, and Tommy reunited during the finale, the Scarlet Witch's resurrection also opens the door to a long-awaited Maximoff family reunion.

However, Doctor Doom's involvement could make a Maximoff family reunion more complicated. The villain's decision to resurrect Wanda raises questions about his intentions, especially with Avengers: Doomsday approaching. Whether Scarlet Witch will reunite with her family or become caught up in Doom's plans remains to be seen. Now, all that's left is the official green light from Marvel confirming that "The Scarlet Witch Will Return" in Avengers: Doomsday.
