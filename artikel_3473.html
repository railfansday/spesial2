Elden Ring: St. Trina Officially Announced For December 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The recent release of Elden Ring: Tarnished Edition has been a massive success so far, bringing in a wave of new players and seeing the return of veteran Tarnished as well, thanks to the accompanying DLC. Even if the new content isn't nearly as massive as something like the Shadow of the Erdtree, it's given players something new to look forward to in their next playthrough.

While we don't have any news on an Elden Ring 2, the future of the franchise is looking bright thanks to Alex Garland's film, which apparently has an astronomical budget to play with. While we wait for more updates on the film, however, Tarnished can now look forward to the upcoming release of Elden Ring: St. Trina.

As part of the Tarnished Edition's release, Bandai Namco has been running a series of unique community-driven quests over the past few months, each with a set of distinct rewards and goals based on different "paths" through the Lands Between. One of the exciting new quests is called Path of Shadows, which will actually feature a brand-new St. Trina reward based on the beloved and critically acclaimed Shadow of the Erdtree expansion.

The St. Trina reward is a special Twitch chat badge designed by artist CarsonDrewit, giving fans a way to show off their own flair based on one of Shadow of the Erdtree's most mysterious characters. In Elden Ring universe lore, St. Trina is closely associated with sleep and dreams, and is eventually revealed to be an aspect of Miquella that he abandoned during his pursuit of godhood. Players encounter her during Thiollier's questline in Shadow of the Erdtree, where drinking her nectar is an integral, albeit deadly, part of uncovering her story.

The Path of Shadows event runs from December 1 through December 28, with players collectively working toward several ambitious goals. These include collecting one million Scadutree Fragments, reaching the Abyssal Woods 15,000 times, drinking St. Trina's Nectar 10,000 times, obtaining 70,000 Larval Tears, and collecting 500,000 Revered Spirit Ashes.

In addition to the exclusive St. Trina Twitch badge, participants can look forward to brand-new commemorative artwork by Crisppyboat.
