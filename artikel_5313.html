Binance Collaborates with Cboe to Bring Trusted Real-Time Market Benchmark Data to Users

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

ABU DHABI, UAE, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Binance today announced a collaboration with Cboe Global Markets to integrate the Cboe Global Indices Feed into the Binance platform and app, giving users access to trusted real-time market benchmark data.

The initial rollout will include the S&P 500 Index (SPXÂ®), the Dow Jones Industrial Average (DJXÂ®), and the Nasdaq-100 (NDXÂ®), three of the world's most widely followed benchmarks for U.S. equity market performances. Binance plans to progressively add more index data through the Cboe Global Indices Feed over time, expanding the market context available to users across its platform.

The collaboration follows the recent launch of Binance AI, a new suite of AI-powered features that bring adaptive, personalized financial experiences to Binance's global platform. Through the Cboe Global Indices Feed, Binance is giving users access to trusted traditional market benchmarks directly within its platform, app, and Binance AI, helping bring fragmented market information together in one place.

As Binance expands its multi-asset offering across crypto and traditional market-linked products, data from the Cboe Global Indices Feed may give users trusted TradFi benchmarks to compare against digital asset market activity. Binance AI will also use this data to explain cross-market movements and highlight market context personalized to each user's interests and activity. Behind the interface, Binance's agentic framework orchestrates the functions that pull market data, generate recommendations, and shape what each user sees.
