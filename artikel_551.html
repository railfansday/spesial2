Golden Globes Predictions: 170 Films and 475 Actors Vie for Nominations as Voting Gets Underway. Who Will Make the Cut?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Variety Awards Circuit section is the home for all awards news and related content throughout the year, featuring the following: the official predictions for the upcoming Oscars, Emmys, Grammys and Tony Awards ceremonies, curated by Variety chief awards editor Clayton Davis. The prediction pages reflect the current standings in the race and do not reflect personal preferences for any individual contender. As other formal (and informal) polls suggest, competitions are fluid and subject to change in response to buzz and events. Predictions are updated every Thursday.

Golden Globes Predictions Commentary (Updated: Nov. 24, 2026): Golden Globe voting is underway! Here’s the breakdown of all the submissions.

Eighty-eight films were submitted for best motion picture (drama), and 43 are competing for best motion picture (musical or comedy). Among the drama submissions are Christopher Nolan’s “The Odyssey” and Denis Villeneuve’s “Dune: Part Three.” The musical or comedy field also includes Destin Daniel Cretton’s “Spider-Man: Brand New Day” and David Fincher’s “The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth.”

In the lead acting races, the drama category for best actor has 69 submissions representing 64 performers with Brad Pitt in contention for “Heart of the Beast,” alongside Robert Pattinson for “Primetime.” The musical or comedy category features 32 submissions from 31 performers, including Tom Holland for “Spider-Man: Brand New Day” and Ryan Gosling for “Project Hail Mary.”

The best actress (drama) race has 39 submissions from 39 performers, with Zendaya (“Dune: Part Three”) and Anne Hathaway (“Verity”) among the contenders. The musical or comedy field has 23 submissions from 22 performers. Meryl Streep returns as Miranda Priestly in “The Devil Wears Prada 2,” while Olivia Wilde earns two entries, for “I Want Your Sex” and “The Invite.”

The acting races are led by the supporting actor category, which boasts 221 submissions from 198 performers, making it the most crowded of the six acting fields. Mark Ruffalo has three entries in the supporting category alone: “Being Heumann,” “Crime 101,” and “Spider-Man: Brand New Day.” The supporting actress category has 158 submissions from 146 performers. Florence Pugh appears twice, for “Dune: Part Three” and “Spider-Man: Brand New Day,” while Zendaya is also a double entrant, with submissions for “The Odyssey” and “Spider-Man: Brand New Day” that go along with her two leading bids.

Behind the camera, 131 filmmakers are attached to 123 films in the best director race, including Steven Spielberg for “Disclosure Day” and Nolan for “The Odyssey.” The screenplay category features 121 submissions, among them Quentin Tarantino’s script for “The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth” and Drew Goddard’s adaptation of “Project Hail Mary.”

The music categories include 100 submissions for best original score, with John Williams (“Disclosure Day”) and Hans Zimmer (“Dune: Part Three”) among the composers in contention. Best original song has 51 entries, including Taylor Swift and Jack Antonoff’s “I Knew It, I Knew You” from “Toy Story 5,” as well as two submissions from Diane Warren.

The animated feature category has 24 eligible films, including Andrew Stanton’s “Toy Story 5” and Brad Bird’s “Ray Gunn.” Meanwhile, 42 films are submitted for best motion picture (non-English language), including Ryusuke Hamaguchi’s “All of a Sudden,” representing Japan, and Lee Chang-dong’s “Possible Love,” representing South Korea.

The cinematic and box office achievement category features 20 submissions, among them “The Devil Wears Prada 2,” starring Streep, and surprisingly, “The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth.”Across all categories, 170 distinct films have been submitted, while the six acting categories account for 475 different performers.
