Why Beta Technologies Stock Lost Altitude Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

One firm is anything but charged up about the prospects of this eVTOL stock

By Scott Levine – Nov 18, 2026 at 3:22PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Beta Technologies ›

A firm's newly released critical report on Beta Technologies (BETA -2.21%) stock is sending shares of the electric vertical take-off and landing (eVTOL) stock lower today.

As of 3:12 p.m. ET, Beta Technologies shares are down 2.1%, recovering from an earlier 11.4% decline.

Characterizing Beta Technologies as a stock that's "heading for a meltdown," Gotham City Research released a critical report on the company today.

The firm identified numerous concerns regarding the upstart eVTOL company, including its consistent failure to meet deadlines. Gotham City points out that the consensus among analysts is that Beta Technologies will soar from $55 million in 2026 to $276 million in 2027, powered by the delivery of an estimated 48 aircraft. But because the company has consistently failed to adhere to the timeline it set for achieving regulatory approvals, this revenue forecast is highly unlikely to be met.

Gotham City Research contends that Beta Technologies stock, which currently trades at around $20, is worth, at most, $10.

Beta Technologies is developing eVTOL aircraft for several applications such as cargo, medical passenger, and military aviation.

While the critical report from Gotham City Research is certainly concerning, it's important to remember that the firm maintains a short position in Beta Technologies stock, meaning it stands to profit from the stock's decline. That being said, it's also possible that Gotham City Research is right, and the bull case for Beta Technologies stock is overblown.

In light of the report and the uncertainty surrounding the company, potential investors would be smart to watch this eVTOL stock from the tarmac for now and wait for the company to provide greater insights into its operations when it reports fourth-quarter 2026 financial results in the coming months.
