Naruto Movie Officially Casts Fan-favorite Actor for Key Role

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Director Destin Daniel Cretton has found his Hokage

Spider-Man: Brand New Day director Destin Daniel Cretton is gearing up for his next live-action adaptation of an iconic franchise. He's writing and directing a live-action Naruto movie, and is in the process of casting an important role.

On Monday, Deadline reported Hiroyuki Sanada is in "final negotiations" with Lionsgate to play the Third Hokage of the Hidden Leaf Village, Hiruzen Sarutobi. He's a compassionate leader who tries to look out for Naruto, and in his younger days he led a team of Jiraiya, Orochimaru, and Tsunade. Early in the series, he gets to show off his power as a shinobi in a battle against Orochimaru during the invasion of Konoha.

Hiroyuki Sanada is a renowned Japanese actor who gained prominence in Hollywood in the 2000s with roles in the Tom Cruise epic The Last Samurai and Rush Hour 3. He's joined more nerdy franchises than you can count, starring in Speed Racer, The Wolverine, Avengers: Endgame, John Wick: Chapter 4, and the 2020s Mortal Kombat movies as Scorpion. On TV, you've seen him in Westworld and Shōgun. He's a trained martial artist and demonstrates that skill every time he's on screen.

Sanada will join Charles Melton in the Naruto movie. Reports surfaced a few months ago that Cretton was eyeing Melton to play Naruto's teacher, Kakashi Hatake. The latest Deadline report notes that Melton is still in talks to star in the film.

Cretton announced a casting search for the Team 7 leads, Naruto, Sasuke, and Sakura, in July. His picture doesn't have a release date yet, but production will start in spring of next year.
