Netflix’s 10/10 ‘Slow Horses’ Replacement Officially Reclaims Streaming Crown

It's always tricky when a show takes longer than usual between seasons, especially in the streaming era. Audiences are accustomed to their favorite shows returning like clockwork every year, and that's one of the reasons why Prime Video's Reacher has been such a massive hit. The action series aired its fourth season this year and delivered record numbers. On the other hand, Netflix's The Gentlemen witnessed a 45% drop in opening-week viewership, presumably because of the two-year gap between seasons. Prime Video's The Terminal List, which spent four years off the air, failed to crack the standard 1 billion minutes watched milestone upon its return.

The jury's still out on Netflix's acclaimed spy thriller series, which premiered to 10.8 million views in 2024 and reclaimed the streamer's viewership crown upon its recent return. According to Netflix, the show clocked 3.8 million views in the first three days of release, but its actual opening-week performance will only become clear when the streamer shares its latest viewership report in the coming days. The show has the potential to become Netflix's answer to Slow Horses, which has emerged as a platform-defining hit for Apple TV over the past four years.

A key to Slow Horses' success is the strict production schedules that Apple TV has enforced. Two seasons are produced simultaneously, which is how the show has aired six seasons since 2022. Like Slow Horses, the Netflix series also consists of six episodes per season. However, it took two years for the show to return. The positive reviews that the show has received will only go so far in attracting audiences, but catching up on six episodes isn't a huge time commitment for new viewers.

We're talking, of course, about Black Doves. Starring Keira Knightley, Ben Whishaw, and Sarah Lancashire, the show reclaimed the number-one spot on Netflix worldwide upon its return on November 5. According to FlixPatrol, it's currently trailing the breakout hit The Body and the true-crime title Taken: Natalee Holloway.

Also starring Sam Riley, Ambika Mod, and Babou Ceesay, the new season of Black Doves has received critical acclaim. It's now sitting at a "Certified Fresh" 96% score on Rotten Tomatoes, up from Season 1's 92%. The aggregator website's consensus reads, "Black Doves takes flight once more in this fun-fueled second season where Keira Knightley and Ben Whishaw reign in the proceedings with verifiably pristine performances." Black Doves is a drama series that centers on the complex interplay of espionage and personal relationships. Focused on Helen Webb, a seasoned spy striving to balance her covert missions with a turbulent family life, the series delves into themes of loyalty, betrayal, and sacrifice. Starring Keira Knightley, it examines the moral ambiguities faced by those who live double lives in service to their countries.
