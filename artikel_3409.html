Jude Law and Andrew Garfield Officially Become Siegfried & Roy in First Look at ‘Wild Things’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Best known for playing Spider-Man in the 2010s and later in Spider-Man: No Way Home, Andrew Garfield has broken out of the Marvel mold and is known as one of the best actors of his generation. Garfield is a two-time Oscar nominee for his work in Hackwsaw Ridge and tick, tick... Boom, and while he hasn't taken home an award yet, it wouldn't be surprising if he does by the time he decides to retire. Garfield has never had a problem taking on daring roles, and that's exactly what he's done with his new movie, Artificial, where he plays OpenAI CEO and founder Sam Altman. The movie is scheduled to be released in theaters on January 8, 2027. Only three months after the debut of Artificial, Garfield also has a new project coming to streaming that you won't want to miss.

We're talking, of course, about Wild Things, the new Apple TV miniseries starring Garfield opposite Jude Law that's set to be released on April 16. Hailing from Emmy-nominated creator John Hoffman, the show tells the story of legendary Las Vegas performers Siegfried and Roy. In typical Apple TV fashion, the streamer made a splash by showcasing the first look at the series at the Sphere in Las Vegas this weekend while the world watched the Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix this weekend. Matt Shakman, who directed all episodes of WandaVision and also recently helmed The Fantastic Four: First Steps, is also involved in the project as an EP. Additional cast members include Jessica Madsen, Brett Gelman, Justin Theroux, Cameron Britton, Darius Fraser, Bill Heck, Justin Bartha, Jason Butler Harner and Wanda Opalinska.

The most-watched show in the world on Apple TV right now, paving the way for the arrival of Wild Things in just a few months, is 12 12 12, the new heist thriller starring Anthony Mackie and Jamie Dornan. To take the top spot, 12 12 12 had to pass Stick, the Ted Lasso-style golf comedy starring Owen Wilson and Rhea Seehorn. The most popular movie in the world on Apple TV right now is Mayday, the Cold War thriller starring Ryan Reynolds. Brad Pitt's F1 is also still among the most-watched titles on the platform, now almost a year removed from its streaming debut.

Check out the new look at Wild Things from the Las Vegas Sphere and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage of Andrew Garfield's future projects.
