After 53 Years, Charlie Brown’s OG Thanksgiving Special Actor Finally Gets Crucial Payments

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The actor behind one of the Peanuts franchise's most beloved holiday specials finally gets the recognition he deserves decades later.

A Charlie Brown Thanksgiving is still considered a holiday classic over fifty years after its initial debut. The animated special originally premiered on CBS in November 1973, just two days before Thanksgiving. And, it remains one of the most recognizable installments of the franchise's massive collection of holiday specials.

More than fifty years after A Charlie Brown Thanksgiving made its TV debut, one of the original voice actors behind the titular character has revealed a shocking behind-the-scenes detail about the iconic holiday special. Todd Barbee, who voiced Charlie Brown in several Peanuts projects throughout the 1970s, went decades without receiving residual payments for his work on the massive franchise.

During an interview on Nostalgia Tonight with Joe Sibilia, Barbee discussed his experiences working on the hit animated property and revealed that he only started receiving residual payments around six years ago. Despite the continued popularity of the specials, the former child actor had not received any compensation beyond what he was paid to play the role in these productions.

However, Barbee made it clear that he and his co-stars were very well paid for their original recording work. He explained that the amount he earned from just the Peanuts specials alone was enough that he was able to help his mother buy a house, which she still lives in to this very day.

Although Barbee's residual payments arrived decades after his time with Charlie Brown and his friends ended, the actor said that he appreciates the additional income. While the payments are not very large, they are still helpful, and he looks forward to receiving them every year.

Barbee's involvement with the Peanuts franchise extended beyond the Thanksgiving special. Between 1972 and 1974, he portrayed Charlie Brown in four other projects, You're Not Elected, Charlie Brown, There's No Time for Love, Charlie Brown, It's the Easter Beagle, Charlie Brown!, and It's a Mystery, Charlie Brown.

Barbee's history with Peanuts began several years before he landed the role. He previously auditioned for It's the Great Pumpkin, Charlie Brown, which premiered in 1966. His father also worked for TV producer Lee Mendelson, who was handling the auditions. However, Barbee was ultimately not cast in that specific special.

A Charlie Brown Thanksgiving and all other Peanuts specials can currently be streamed on Apple TV.
