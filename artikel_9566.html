"Same as last week, but…": a standing report spec for recurring analysis requests

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

"Same as last week, but split by region this time." That's the whole request. If you're the person who builds the weekly sales numbers at a small online shop, you know the sentence is short because the asker assumes you remember everything that made last week's numbers right.

I build Maildoer, an email assistant, and requests like this one shaped a lot of my thinking. This post isn't about the tool, though. It's about a small habit that makes "same as last week" safe: a standing report spec.

Say you're the only analyst at a shop with a few thousand orders a month. Over three emails the founder has told you:

Week three is only correct if weeks one and two are still applied. Miss the test-order rule and the new regional view quietly disagrees with every report before it.

Keep one short file per recurring report. Five sections:

The changelog is the part people skip, and the part that saves you. When someone asks why the March total dropped, the answer is one line: test orders were excluded from week two.

Before you send the new numbers, reply with what "same" means:

Running it as before: Monday–Sunday, net of refunds, staff test orders excluded. New this week: split by region. One question: should region follow the shipping address or the billing address?

That's two sentences of restatement and one question. It catches drift before it reaches a chart.

The habit above is the main point. Where Maildoer comes in: when the same person emails again, the new message is read together with their earlier ones, not on its own. So "same as last week, but by region" can be checked against the week-one and week-two instructions instead of starting cold. They can also reply to a result to adjust it without restating everything.
