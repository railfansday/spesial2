Flyte Aviation Reports Record Third Quarter 2026 Aviation Revenue Increasing 145% Sequentially

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Flight Hours Surge 129% Sequentially; Flyte Fleet Fulfills More Than 99% of Customer Flights

Three Additional Jets Expected to Begin Revenue Service in Q4, Expanding Fleet Capacity to Meet Growing Demand

AI-Powered Operations, Digital Booking and International Expansion Position Flyte for Continued Growth

FORT MILL, S.C., Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Flyte Aviation, Inc. (VJET: NYSE/American) (the Company), one of the nation's largest Vision Jet operators, today announced record quarterly revenue of $1,838,000 for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, a 145% sequential increase. The presentation of financial results is affected by the Company's classification of its legacy MedTech segment assets as Held for Sale on the balance sheet and their associated financial performance as Discontinued Operations on the Income Statement. MedTech revenue increased 4% sequentially and 24% year-over-year to $280,000 during the Third Quarter, 2026.

Aircraft Utilization and Operating MomentumMore than 99% of customer flights were fulfilled on our aircraft within our own fleet rather than more expensive third-party charters. Total Flight Hours increased 129% sequentially resulting from increased demand for our flights and the addition of two aircraft to our fleet. Third Quarter Time Flown per Leg averaged 52 minutes which, when combined with the increased demand experienced during the quarter, we regard as affirmation by the market of our business strategy of providing the convenience, comfort, time efficiency and reliability of short-hop private aviation at a highly competitive price.

Fleet Expansion and OutlookThree aircraft are currently in process of being added to our fleet. All three have successfully completed the pre-purchase inspections of our lessor and are anticipated to join our fleet and begin flying revenue generating flights during the current December quarter. Concurrently with the process of adding these aircraft to our fleet we have been hiring and qualifying additional pilots to fly our new jets as they enter the fleet. We anticipate adding additional airplanes to our fleet during the first half of 2027 and beyond.

CEO CommentaryCommenting on the third quarter results, David Jenkins, Flyte Aviation CEO, said, "I am delighted with the rapid ramp up of Flyte's revenue, aircraft utilization and more. Our financial and operating results combined with increasing customer demand further validates our business strategy of providing the convenience, comfort, time efficiency and reliability of short-hop private aviation at a highly competitive price. In October we received our Foreign Air Operator Certificate and Transport License, along with the filing and acceptance of our Canadian tariff and we flew our first Canadian flights. With the addition of Canada to the growing number of markets that we serve in North America, including the Bahamas and Caribbean, our capability to provide private aviation flights and increase the strength of our brand continues to broaden across the continent and attract increasing interest from new partners."

MedTech (Catheter Precision) Financial Segment Summary ReportMedTech's revenue increased by 4% sequentially and 24% year-over-year to $280,000.  In September 2026, the Company classified its MedTech segment as held for sale on its balance sheet and its results as discontinued operations in its statement of operations. On November 1, 2026, the Company entered into a non-binding letter of intent ("LOI") for the sale of the MedTech segment. The MedTech business continues to operate in the ordinary course pending any sale. The LOI is non-binding, and completion of the transaction remains subject to, among other things, satisfactory due diligence, negotiation and execution of a definitive agreement, board and any required stockholder or other approvals, receipt of third-party consents, and satisfaction of other closing conditions. There can be no assurance that the LOI will lead to a definitive agreement or that the transaction will be completed on the anticipated timeline or at all.

For the third quarter of 2026, the Company reported a net loss of $15.1 million, which includes a $13.3 million non-cash impairment charge related to the MedTech assets held for sale, and a loss from continuing operations of $1.9 million. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had cash and cash equivalents of $2.4 million.

The Company's detailed report on the third quarter financial results has been filed with the SEC on Form 10-Q and is available at www.SEC.gov.

About Flyte AviationFlyte Aviation, Inc. (NYSE American: VJET) is a technology-enabled private aviation company focused on modernizing how consumers access private air travel. Through its Flyte brand, the Company operates a growing fleet of Cirrus Vision Jets and is building a scalable aviation platform designed to connect customers, aircraft, operators, and aviation services.

Through its direct-to-consumer booking platform, standardized pricing on select routes, strategic partnerships, and FAA-certified Part 135 operating subsidiary, Ponderosa Air, LLC, Flyte is focused on delivering what it believes is a faster, safer and more convenient private aviation experience while expanding its technology, customer network, and geographic footprint.

For more information, visit www.flyflyte.com.
