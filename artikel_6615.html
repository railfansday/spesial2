Jenna Dewan Says “Online Noise” Was the Reason for Her Shocking ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Elimination

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

In one of the more gasp-inducing moments from the season, Jenna Dewan and Val Chmerkovskiy were voted off Dancing With the Stars during Grammy night—despite an incredible jazz performance to “All for You” by Janet Jackson.

The competition was incredibly stiff last night (everyone was incredible!), but Jenna thinks her surprising elimination was the result of “online noise.”

When asked by Extra why she thought she was going home despite dancing her best night of the show, Jenna said, “I don’t honestly know the answer to that. I know it’s hard to know how this all plays into it. I know this was a particularly tough week for a lot of the online noise and all stuff that comes with the show. I don’t know, I’ll never know how, or if, any of that plays into this. But I know that we went out there and had the best time of our life.”

When it comes to who she’s rooting for, Jenna said, “Clearly as you see you can’t count anyone out in this competition. I have the best hopes and wishes for all of them, I hope they have a beautiful rest of their journey and I’ll be cheering them all on.”

As for Val, he told Extra, “Every week’s a shock. It’s very competitive. Lot of great couples out there, wishing everybody the best, gonna be rooting from home. Definitely a sad result, but no regrets, we had a great season.”
