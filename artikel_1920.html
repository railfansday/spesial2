Keith Wyness: Aston Villa could accept '£80m' Boubacar Kamara offer

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Aston Villa could potentially be tempted to sell midfielder Boubacar Kamara to Chelsea if an offer in the region of £80million was put on the table.

That is according to former Villa chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that a move could potentially happen next summer.

The two clubs exchanged several deals in the summer, and now Kamara is the latest player to emerge on Chelsea's radar.

However, Wyness believes that his former club will try to price the Blues out of a move, and would only accept "silly money" for the 26-year-old.

Kamara has become a key figure for Unai Emery's side in recent years, and has started all 10 Premier League games so far this season.

He is currently making more defensive contributions than any other Villa player so far this season, averaging 10.6 per 90 in the Premier League.

Villa's former chief Wyness - who served as CEO at Villa Park between 2016 and 2018 and now runs a consultancy business advising elite clubs - believes that an £80m offer for Kamara would really make his former club think about his future.

Emery's side lost several of their key stars in the summer, and would be reluctant to see the Frenchman depart, especially to a rival for the European places.

However, speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained that there could be a point where a sale would become worthwhile for Villa.

"Well yes I do (think Villa will price Chelsea out). Look, we know that Chelsea and Villa have managed to do some trading recently and have got to know each other quite well," he said.

"Villa also know that Chelsea can pay silly money if they have to, and you know, if there was an £80m offer for him coming in, then who knows.

"Villa wouldn't have to take it, so they'd only take silly money for it just now. But they've also proven they can replace players as well.

"I think that if anything were to happen, it probably would be the summer. But I don't see it happening at all yet. I think Villa would want a little bit more stability."

Former Villa scout Mick Brown recently told Football Insider that Chelsea loanee Alejandro Garnacho may already be "finished" at Villa.

The Argentine has joined Emery's group on a season-long loan deal, but was left out of their matchday squad altogether last weekend.

Brown believes that the writing is already on the wall for Garnacho's time with the club, and believes that Villa cannot "rely" on him to make a positive impact.

They have an obligation to buy the 22-year-old once he makes a certain number of appearances, but that prospect feels distant at the moment.
