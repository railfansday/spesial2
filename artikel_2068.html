'They're talking' - Man United set sights on Cole Palmer and three other stars in audacious '£500m' January spree, says transfer expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Manchester United are eyeing an audacious January transfer window to back new manager Mauricio Pochettino.

That is according to experienced former Tottenham, Everton and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The former Tottenham and Chelsea boss was appointed last week to replace Michael Carrick, and will take charge for the first time against Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday.

Pochettino will hope to turn Man United's season around, and he will expected significant support in the January transfer window.

The Red Devils left Carrick short in several areas in the summer, and the Argentine will be keen to fill those gaps as soon as possible.

Cole Palmer is one player that has been mentioned, though it would likely take an extraordinary amount of money to convince Chelsea to sell him.

Man United have been credited with interest in Palmer before, though have never firmed that up with a bid.

The 24-year-old is on a staggering eight-year contract with Chelsea, who would likely need the biggest fee in football history to part ways with him.

It is unclear if Palmer would even accept the move, given his former allegiances to Manchester City.

"They're talking about Man United and Pochettino to go for [Cole] Palmer and Lewis Hall and maybe two others in January," King told Football Insider.

"They've got somewhere like £400-500million to spend. That's the sort of money they're going to have to spend to get Manchester United back to any kind of winning team again.

"Or even a team that's going to win the championship, because they certainly won't win it this year. Manchester United is not the name that Alex Ferguson made Manchester United into.

"It was a great era. Arsenal and Liverpool were the big teams to challenge them. And it was exciting times, wonderful to watch. But Manchester United today are not the same team."

Pochettino was in charge when Palmer arrived at Chelsea and oversaw his first and best season so far, in which the midfielder scored 25 goals and provided 15 assists across all competitions.

While he will be under no illusion about the difficulty in any deal that reunite the pair again, he will likely dream of it.

"One of the best skills is his mentality and his capacity to deal with the pressure." Pochettino said in 2024 after Palmer had scored a hat-trick against Man United.

"He's young and I think it's his first season playing very consistently. I think it's amazing how he is dealing with the pressure.

"That is all that can be a very good player. With the performance today it's impossible to say that he's not a top player."
