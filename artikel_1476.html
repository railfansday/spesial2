Cardinale clarifies his ‘Berlusconi 2.0’ statement: “Lead the club into a new era”

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Gerry Cardinale has clarified his ‘Berlusconi 2.0’ statement from about three years ago, stating that investments and resources are needed to get AC Milan back to the top.

Back in September 2023, a little over a year after he completed the purchase of Milan, Cardinale sat down with Corriere della Sera to explain his project for the club. “In football I want to be a Berlusconi 2.0, to have the same impact as him in a completely changed context,” he said, among other things.

It’s a quote that didn’t land brilliantly with some of the Milan fans, who felt that Cardinale grossly underestimated how much work Berlusconi put in with the throwaway comment. It was more than just a throwaway comment, though.

Speaking to Gazzetta dello Sport this morning, following the launch of Milan’s new holding company MSE, Cardinale clarified what he meant by the quote. It was not meant as an arrogant comparison, but rather as praise for Berlusconi and what he can learn from the legendary Milan owner.

The connection with Milan deserves a separate mention: RedBird and the city are now even more connected…

“This is the legacy we will leave in Milan. The stadium, obviously, represents a huge legacy that will transform our financial profile, but then there’s also the San Donato development project and the rest.

“If we want AC Milan to become great again, we need to close the revenue gap with the Premier League clubs. When I bought the club four years ago, I used the expression ‘Berlusconi 2.0’ and no one understood what I meant. Now perhaps we are starting to see it.”

“Everyone thought I was making an arrogant, self-centred comparison between me and Berlusconi, but that wasn’t my point. What I was trying to explain was that, just as Berlusconi transformed AC Milan and Serie A, we want to be able, through our ownership, to lead the club into a new era.

“We need to do it in the same way Berlusconi did, albeit in a modern way. I’m used to working a certain way because that’s how I do things, at the highest levels worldwide and in America.”
