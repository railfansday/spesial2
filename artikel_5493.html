Crocs™ Achieves Cradle to Cradle Certified® Circularity Gold for the Classic Clog, Marking a New Milestone in Circular Design and Recycled Material Innovation

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

This certification recognizes the brand designs three popular styles for circularity: the Classic Clog, Bae Clog and Keep It Going Classic Clog

BROOMFIELD, Colo., Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Crocs, Inc. (NADSAQ: CROX), a world leader in innovative casual footwear for all, today announced the Crocs brand has achieved Cradle to Cradle CertifiedÂ® Circularity Gold Version 5.0 for its iconic Classic Clog. This certification verifies that the Classic Clog has been assessed against circularity criteria for design, material use, takeback and recovery, and contains an average of 29% recycled material1 while delivering the same comfort people know and love. Also now certified are the trendy Bae Clog, made with an average of 36% recycled material2, and the limited edition Keep It Going Classic Clog, made with an average of 42% recycled material3. All previously stated percentages of recycled material were calculated using a mass balance approach. Together, approximately 31% of Crocs shoes globally are now Cradle to Cradle CertifiedÂ® Circularity Gold.

The Cradle to Cradle CertifiedÂ® Circularity certification is awarded by the Cradle to Cradle Products Innovation Institute, an international nonprofit and one of the world's leading organizations advancing a circular economy where products are designed for their next life rather than disposal. Through its science-based certification program used globally across industries, the Cradle to Cradle Products Innovation Institute independently assesses products against rigorous sustainability performance. Within circularity, each of these Crocs products passed requirements on circular sourcing, circular design and circular system.

For consumers, this certification serves as a credible, third-party signal that a product has been designed with circularity in mind and evaluated against one of the industry's leading standards. It helps shoppers make more informed purchasing decisions by providing independent validation of how materials are sourced, used and ultimately recovered.

"Innovation has always been at the heart of the Crocs brand, and this achievement highlights how innovation can extend beyond comfort and style and into circular systems for positive impact," said Deanna Bratter, Chief Sustainability & Compliance Officer, Crocs, Inc. "We're proud of this milestone and remain focused on identifying new opportunities to advance circularity and sustainability across the Crocs brand."

With this certification, the Cradle to Cradle Products Innovation Institute recognizes the circular design characteristics of Crocs' iconic molded footwear and proprietary Crosliteâ¢ material. "We're proud to recognize Crocs for achieving Cradle to Cradle CertifiedÂ® Circularity Gold based on Version 5.0, and to support a brand that is advancing circular design at scale," said Nienke Steen, Director of Consumer Products & Textiles at the Cradle to Cradle Products Innovation Institute. "As one of the world's most recognizable footwear brands, Crocs demonstrates how circularity can be integrated into products reaching millions of consumers. This achievement represents meaningful progress for the industry and a credible signal to consumers that a product was designed for circularity."

At Crocs, circularity means designing products with their next life in mind and creating pathways to keep materials in use for as long as possible. Through thoughtful material choices, innovative product design, and Old Crocs. New Life., the brand helps enable worn shoes to be reused, recycled into Crocs Shred and transformed into new products and applications. Crocs' vision to advance a more circular future and reduce its waste combines product innovation, industry collaboration, and consumer participation.
