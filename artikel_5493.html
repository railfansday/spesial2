Genpact Launches Genpact Onyx, the Agentic Operations Platform for Building, Running, and Governing AI Agents in Enterprise Operations

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Powered by Genpact's Process Intelligence Core, Genpact Onyx grounds agents in how enterprise work gets done and enables earned autonomy over time

NEW YORK, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Genpact (NYSE: G), the Agentic Operations company, today announced the availability of Genpact Onyx, the Agentic Operations platform for building, running, and governing AI agents in enterprise operations. Genpact Onyx is powered by the Process Intelligence Core, the operating knowledge Genpact has codified from decades of running mission-critical work for global enterprises, so agents start with context on how the work gets done rather than learning it from scratch. The platform was unveiled at Genpact's Agentic Operations Day in New York on November 12.

Genpact Onyx is the foundation for Genpact's proprietary agentic solutions, starting with the Genpact Accounts Payable Suite, and for custom solutions Genpact builds for clients. It embeds into a client's existing technology stack, connects to the client's data and AI foundations, and supports a choice of leading AI models across Genpact's partner ecosystem. With Genpact Onyx, enterprises can activate agents in live operations that handle the volume of execution with context on how work gets done while providing governance over every action and accountability for the outcomes.

"Genpact runs mission-critical operations for many of the world's largest enterprises, and that is where the return on AI is decided," said Balkrishan "BK" Kalra, President and Chief Executive Officer, Genpact. "Genpact Onyx codifies the process intelligence of three decades and makes it the foundation for Agentic Operations. Agents run the work. Our experts own the outcome. And the outcomes matter to our clients: grow faster, operate leaner, and convert more to cash."

Key capabilities of the Genpact Onyx platform:

"Enterprises get value from AI when they turn operating knowledge into trusted execution, not simply by adding more agents," said Priya Vijayarajendran, Chief Product and Platform Officer, Genpact. "Genpact Onyx is built for that. It grounds agents in how the work actually gets done, builds governance into every step, orchestrates across agents and human expertise, and lets autonomy be earned one decision at a time."

Unveiled at Genpact Agentic Operations Day

Genpact launched Genpact Onyx at Agentic Operations Day, its third annual flagship event, held on November 12 at Spring Studios in New York. Hundreds of clients, partners, and industry analysts attended a program of Genpact announcements, product demonstrations, and client presentations. Leaders from Advantage Solutions, Wesco, USA Today, and Ryan Specialty shared how they are putting Genpact's agentic solutions to work.
