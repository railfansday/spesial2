Where to find the bowling alley safe code in GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

How to open the Kingpin Bowling Alley safe

How to open the Kingpin Bowling Alley safe

The Kingpin Bowling Alley safe code in Grand Theft Auto 6 is, frustratingly, not actually in the bowling alley itself. No, to get your hands on it legitimately, you need to ransack a different Kolt & Associates illegal operation, which essentially acts as the gang's headquarters, and has multiple safe codes available at once. But that doesn't help when you're deep in the back offices of the bowling alley, and you're being shot at by dozens of angry gang members.

So, if you want to get the Kingpin Bowling Alley safe code legitimately in GTA 6, we can help tell you where to go. Alternatively, if you just want to know what the code is so you can open the safe without doing the legwork, we've got you covered there too.

The Kingpin Bowling Alley safe code can be found at the Brooks Boatworks illegal operation in northwestern Port Gellhorn. This place is full to the brim with Kolt & Associates gang members, so unless you're incredibly stealthy, be prepared for a shootout.

From the main entrance, if you follow the dirt track to the right instead, then jump over the rusted chain gate, there is a hole in the fence you can sneak through. In front of you is a boat underneath a tarp, and to the right, a smaller grey building that isn't attached to the main warehouse, next to a blue shipping container.

Go inside this grey building and on the wall, above a calendar with a sunglasses-wearing cat, will be a map of Port Gellhorn. Inspect it with L2/LT, and you'll get three safe codes added to your notes. One is for the safe at Brooks Boatworks, aka where you are currently, another is for La Cascara Motel, which is further down the west coast of Port Gellhorn, and the third is for the Kingpin Bowling Alley.

With the bowling alley safe code in hand, you just need to head back there to use it, as the game will remind you of what the code is when you're by the safe. However, before you do, I'd recommend ransacking Brooks Boatworks and looting the safe there first, as you can come out with around $5,000.

Just want to open the safe without having to visit Brooks Boatworks first? The code for the safe at the Kingpin Bowling alley in GTA 6 is 0300. This will net you a bunch of cash, as long as you manage to get away safely.

After you open the safe, you'll find just cash, but it'll be upwards of a couple grand.
