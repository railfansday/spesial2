Martha Stewart has officially convinced me I don’t need another cardigan — but I do need to add a chunky turtleneck to my cold-weather wardrobe. Don’t get me wrong, I love a good button-up knit, but something about a cream or beige turtleneck feels instantly polished and incredibly easy to style. Tossing one on feels like a cozy, quiet luxury weekend in the Hamptons, even if I’m just running errands or grabbing coffee. And hey — if Stewart can make an everyday sweater silhouette look this elegant on the red carpet, I’m taking notes!

I’ve rounded up five sweaters that capture the same timeless, luxe-looking appeal without the designer price tag. These cozy turtlenecks are so flattering and elevated, they’ll make even the simplest jeans-and-boots combos feel intentional. With prices starting at just $15, I’m ready to give my cardigan collection a little competition.

1. Cable-Knit Classic: This turtleneck in classic cream has all the makings of a cold-weather staple, with timeless cable-knit details and polished ribbed trim on the sleeve cuffs and hem. The stretchy fabric holds its shape, too, so I can count on it to look just as good at the end of a long day. I’d wear it with straight-leg jeans and tall boots for an outfit even Martha would approve of!

2. Everyday Elegance: Sometimes, the simplest sweaters make the biggest style statement. This creamy beige turtleneck has a clean, elegant silhouette that I can easily picture wearing to the office, out to lunch or just about anywhere on my weekend calendar. I’m especially drawn to the cozy brushed fabric — what a dream for a cold day!

3. Oh-So-Cozy: There’s nothing more comfortable than an oversized sweater, but this one also manages to be incredibly chic. The chunky cream pullover has a textured waffle-knit finish, a relaxed cropped silhouette and a dramatic oversized turtleneck that practically begs to be worn on a chilly day. The ribbed trim adds a nice finishing touch, too. I’d style it with high-waisted jeans and sleek ankle boots to balance out the slouchy fit.

4. Rolled-Edge Charm: I’m always a fan of little details that make a basic sweater feel more special, and this soft cotton-acrylic mock-neck pullover has plenty. The rolled edges along the neckline, cuffs and hem give it a relaxed, lived-in look that feels a little more elevated than an ordinary crewneck — honestly, it looks like a J.Crew sweater! I also love the drapey fit and drop-shoulder sleeves, which make it perfect for layering over a fitted tee.

5. Effortlessly Oversized: This oversized turtleneck looks so soft and cozy with a relaxed fit and comfy drop shoulders, it makes me want to cancel my plans and spend the day cozying up at home. Dramatic batwing sleeves give it an effortlessly chic shape, though, so I can dress it up for dinner, too. The drapey silhouette makes it an easy match for leggings or skinny jeans, and I love that the cozy cowl-style neckline adds a little extra polish.
