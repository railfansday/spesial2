DWTS’ Gleb Savchenko Confirms Breakup From OnlyFans Model Kaitlin Trujillo

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Dancing With the Stars pro Gleb Savchenko is back on the market after splitting from OnlyFans model Kaitlin Trujillo.

“I’m single,” Savchenko, 43, exclusively shared with Us at Press Pass LA’s 15-year anniversary event on Tuesday, November 10. “We’re good friends. We just decided to go our separate ways, and I’m taking my own time.”

Savchenko and Trujillo, 29, were first linked in January when the pair were spotted displaying PDA at the Studio City Farmers Market in California. At the end of September, Trujillo was seen supporting Savchenko as he walked in the Blue Jacket Fashion Show presented by Johnson & Johnson in Hollywood.

Us has reached out to Trujillo for comment.

As cuffing season approaches, Savchenko said he isn’t focused on finding someone new before the end of the year. Instead, he’s decided to spend more time at his house in the California desert.

“I was selling it before. Now, I took it off the market. I was like, ‘You know what? I’m gonna actually put more money into it. Build a gym. Build this. I’m gonna get a dirt bike and the whole thing,’” the professional dancer told Us at the White Rabbit Sky Lounge in West Hollywood. “I’m really focusing on me, on my inner self. I’m meditating. I’m back to meditation. Some people don’t know this, but since I was 15, 16 years old, I’ve been boxing for a few years, and now I’ve gotten back into it. I’m just focusing on me.”

He’s also looking forward to a positive 2027.

After taking a “hiatus” from Dancing With the Stars for season 35, Savchenko teased an upcoming project that will feature a few familiar faces.

“I have a project that’s coming out at the beginning of January on OnlyFans TV,” he previewed. “I don’t want to talk about it too much, but it’s a dance show which I’m hosting, I’m producing, I created it, and I’m very, very proud of it. … It’s going to be awesome for the audience to check out and watch.”

While celebrating his title as a Press Pass LA Winter 2027 honoree Tuesday, Savchenko assured Us he’s still watching Dancing With the Stars. And yes, he has a prediction for who will take home the mirrorball trophy by the end of the month.

“Love Daniella [Karagach] and Ezra [Frech],” he previously told Us. “I think, in my opinion, what I’ve seen is like, ‘Wow!’”
