Oklahoma Basketball Left Searching for Answers After Another Heartbreaking Loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Pittsburgh handed Oklahoma its second loss of the season Tuesday, using a dominant first half performance and a strong showing from A'lahn Sumler to secure a 79-68 victory in the Fort Myers Tip-Off.

The Panthers improved to 7-0, continuing their unbeaten start to the season, while Oklahoma fell to 4-2. Despite a much better offensive effort after halftime, the Sooners spent too much of the afternoon trying to recover from the early hole Pittsburgh created.

Oklahoma opened the game with five straight points, looking ready to challenge the unbeaten Panthers. However, Pittsburgh quickly answered with a 13-3 run that completely changed the momentum.

Sumler helped spark the response with an early three-pointer, and Pittsburgh continued finding ways to get open looks from the perimeter. Another 10-0 run, highlighted by three-pointers from Sumler and Chase Foster, pushed the Panthers' advantage to 30-13.

Oklahoma managed to slow the momentum late in the half. Consecutive three-pointers helped the Sooners cut the deficit to single digits, but Pittsburgh responded well enough to take a 36-24 lead into halftime.

The Panthers' early shooting and defensive pressure put Oklahoma in a difficult position heading into the second half.

Sumler was one of the biggest reasons Pittsburgh remained in control. He finished with 21 points and knocked down six three-pointers, giving the Panthers an important scoring presence throughout the afternoon.

Baye Ndongo also delivered a strong performance, recording 16 points and 10 rebounds for a double double. Naithan George and Foster added 14 points apiece, while Jonathan Powell contributed 10.

Pittsburgh continued building its advantage after halftime. A Ndongo jumper pushed the Panthers ahead 61-39, giving them a commanding 22 point lead and putting Oklahoma in serious danger of falling out of the game.

Oklahoma found more offensive rhythm in the second half, led by Xzayvier Brown. Brown finished with a game-high 23 points, including 18 after halftime, as the Sooners attempted to make the matchup competitive again.

The Panthers went through a lengthy scoring drought, going more than seven minutes without a field goal. That stretch gave Oklahoma an opportunity to climb back into the contest.

Still, Pittsburgh managed to keep the Sooners from getting within striking distance. The Panthers made enough free throws during the drought to protect their advantage and prevent a complete momentum swing.

Oklahoma finished the game shooting 50% from the field in the second half, but the early deficit proved too difficult to overcome. B.J. Edwards and Khani Rooths also finished with 10 points each for the Sooners.

The victory gives Pittsburgh a 7-0 record and another important result as the Panthers continue building momentum early in the season. Their balanced scoring, outside shooting and ability to respond when Oklahoma made a run were key factors in the win.

Oklahoma, meanwhile, will have to regroup quickly after missing an opportunity to earn a quality victory. The Sooners showed improvement after halftime, but their slow response to Pittsburgh's first half runs ultimately cost them.

Both teams will return to action Thursday in Fort Myers. Pittsburgh will face DePaul, while Oklahoma gets another opportunity against Purdue.

For the Panthers, the goal is to keep their unbeaten season alive. For the Sooners, Thursday presents a chance to respond and show that Tuesday's loss will not define their early season progress.
