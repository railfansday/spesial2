Punishing start for Pochettino as size of Man Utd mess made clear

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Mauricio Pochettino’s first game in charge of Manchester United turned into an Anfield nightmare as his side were beaten 4-0 by Liverpool. Adam Bate was there to see it unravel as Pochettino was confronted with the reality of the mess that is United...

Football Features Writer @ghostgoal

Well, they ran. They had to because there were only 10 of them for all but the first seven minutes. If running were the only problem then this mess might soon be solvable. But Manchester United's 4-0 defeat to Liverpool at Anfield highlighted more flaws than that.

Dismal defending, confused combinations, conceding in clusters, confidence long departed. And that all evident in the first quarter of an hour of what was never a contest. This is the only team in the Premier League without a win since August for a reason.

Welcome to Manchester United, Mauricio Pochettino. The new boss might be only days into a contract that runs until 2029, but it has not taken him long to see the mess he has inherited. Liverpool had this match won in 13 minutes. Unlucky for Pochettino.

And yet, even at this early stage of his reign, the Argentine cannot escape blame entirely. The decision to give Matthijs de Ligt his first Premier League start in almost a year was exposed as an error immediately. He was partially culpable for Leny Yoro's red card.

De Ligt's inability to judge the flight of the ball and then appear to give up the chase entirely was not the sort of support his young centre-back needed. Whatever the verdict on the decisions of Yoro and the referee, the Dutchman was given the runaround.

Man Utd head coach Mauricio Pochettino speaking to Sky Sports: “The ball was for the goalkeeper. In that situation, if you review the VAR you can see for sure, he never had the intention to connect with Isak. When the control was long with the striker, the ball was going to the goalkeeper, he runs and goes down. “In Leny's case, it wasn't intentional. It was an unusual situation that is normal, everyone who plays football can feel it. “I have to defend my club. And tell what I feel. That was an action that everyone who plays football, it was clear that it was a situation that was not a red card.”

His only previous start in any competition this season came in Como, where United were dismantled, and it all looked too quick for him. One wonders what Pochettino saw in training. Thrusting him into a game of this intensity was a massive mistake, regardless.

Maybe the subsequent substitutions will raise a few eyebrows too. The only way back into the game was on the counter-attack but the removal of Bryan Mbeumo and Matheus Cunha robbed United of any pace on the breakaway. That was a strange call too.

Kobbie Mainoo and Youri Tielemans paired together in midfield? That one might already have been discredited. And somehow the struggling De Ligt, desperately short of match fitness, was asked to labour on for the full 90 minutes of what was a chastening defeat.

The change of role for Diogo Dalot was a smarter switch - and an easy win for the coach - given how the Portuguese player had been caught out repeatedly in that left-back position under Michael Carrick. But the truth is that there were no real positives here.

The bar was so low as well. The seven previous away days had yielded no wins but a number of horrible moments. From the humiliation at Hull to the late equaliser conceded at Everton. From the crushing in Como to Carrick's final away defeat to Chelsea.

But in its own way, this was just as devastating to morale.

The dice have been rolled with this appointment, fragile hopes raised. When the fans in the Kop were mockingly chanting 'Poch is at the wheel' with 15 minutes still to go, and the fourth Liverpool goal yet to go in, it was a clue that the day had unravelled spectacularly.

Pochettino is not getting 'sacked in the morning' as the Kop repeatedly suggested, but the nature of this defeat means that his immediate challenge is to rebuild shattered confidence. What Ralf Rangnick once famously called open-heart surgery must wait.

How do you press high with defenders who do not feel comfortable following? How do you keep a compact midfield in the gap that this creates when mobility is a major weakness? Issues for another day. Ones that will take work and windows to fix, not mere weeks.

And yet, Pochettino will have to think short term as well as long. The Premier League table demands that on the day Hull City went six points clear of them. United are only one from safety, but can there be any confidence of them easing away from danger?

Welcome to Manchester United, Mauricio Pochettino.

Since the start of the 2021-22 season, Liverpool's four wins by four goals or more against Manchester United are the most any Premier League team has achieved against another across all competitions. Accounting for three points for a win, Manchester United’s eight points this season are their fewest after 11 games of a league campaign since the 1972-73 season. That is also the previous season in which the Red Devils only managed one win through their first 11 league matches. Mauricio Pochettino suffered the joint-heaviest defeat by a Manchester United manager in his first game, along with a defeat by the same scoreline for Lal Hilditch in October 1926. This was the quickest Liverpool had gone 2-0 up in a Premier League match against Manchester United, going two ahead after just 12 minutes and 40 seconds. Manchester United boss Mauricio Pochettino was the first manager in Premier League history to see his team concede twice and receive a red card in the first half of his first match in charge of a club. Manchester United will end the day in 19th position in the Premier League, with this the first time since November 1986 that they have been within the bottom two places of the league table having played at least 10 matches. Leny Yoro’s eighth-minute red card was the earliest shown to a Manchester United player in a Premier League match.
