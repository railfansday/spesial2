What North West Really Thinks of Kim Kardashian's All's Fair Fashion

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Kim Kardashian exclusively revealed to E! News whether daughter North West, 13, approves of her risqué red carpet looks during the season two press two for All's Fair, premiering Nov. 4 on Hulu.

In Kim Kardashian's closet, North West is the judge and the jury.

Which is why the opinionated 13-year-old is never afraid to tell her mom what she really thinks of her risqué red carpet looks. So, did Kim and Kanye "Ye" West's eldest child officially give her style stamp of approval on the 46-year-old's All's Fair season two press tour wardrobe?

"She would love all of our looks," Kim told E! News in an exclusive interview with costars Sarah Paulson and Niecy Nash-Betts at the Hulu legal drama's New York City premiere Nov. 2. "She's been really supportive—lately."

But even when North isn't so generous with her fashion police rulings, Kim appreciates the constructive criticism.

"That's what we love about them," The Kardashians star—also mom to Saint, 10, Chicago, 8, and Psalm, 7—continued. "They're so honest and I love getting fashion advice from North. Honestly, she's never wrong."

But the SKIMS founder isn't the only cast member getting fashion advice from her family.

Niecy revealed her three kids Dominic, 34, Donielle, 31, and Dia, 27—whom she shares with ex Don Nash—have become extra critical of her wardrobe since her 2020 wedding to wife Jessica Betts.

"My kids actually said to me after I got with Jessica, 'You have to dress nice, even now,'" the Reno 911 alum recounted to E!. "And I said, 'Why?' She said, 'Because the gays are vicious.' I said, 'Oh, I didn't know.'"

Speaking of viciousness, fans better buckle up for a wild ride as the lawyers of Grant, Ronson & Greene return for even more jaw-dropping drama during season two of All's Fair.

But while the show's fictional attorneys are more razor-tongued than ever, Kim and co. are quite the opposite behind the camera.

Just ask self-professed trouble-maker Sarah.

"I get the giggles pretty easily," the American Horror Story star admitted. "But also some of the things we're doing are so outrageous that it does strike me as really funny."

As she further explained, "Also what I find really funny is when a person, a.k.a. Kim, is trying really hard not to laugh and you can see in the face that something's happening. And then it's just like I'm in seventh grade and the teacher's like, 'Be quiet,' and I just can't."

And the banter doesn't stop when Ryan Murphy yells "cut."

"We were doing an interview the other day," Sarah continued, "and I just started mimicking what she was saying. She's like, 'How old are you?' And I'm like, 'I know, I just turned into a 12-year-old.'"
