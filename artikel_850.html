Mauricio Pochettino sends coaches to Manchester to help prepare for United job

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Assistant Jesús Pérez and two others depart US early

Pochettino to begin as United head coach on Wednesday

Mauricio Pochettino has sent key members of his coaching staff to England in an early move to prepare for his role as Manchester United’s new head coach while he gets set to lead the US men’s national team for a final time.

Pochettino’s assistant Jesús Pérez, goalkeeping coach Toni Jiménez and athletic coach Sebastiano Pochettino travelled to Manchester soon after the US’s 6-0 victory over Haiti in Miami and in the run-up to United’s Premier League visit to Liverpool on Sunday, when Pochettino will take charge of a fourth English team for a first time. Before then, he will take charge of the US in the second leg of their Concacaf Nations League quarter-final with Haiti in Cincinnati on Tuesday.

Pochettino will have little time before his first game with United, with whom he has a signed a two-and-a-half year contract. He officially joins United on Wednesday.

“For me, it’s not difficult. I think we are well prepared to switch quick - ‌like a player, from the national team to the clubs,” Pochettino said on Monday. “From one game that you don’t play well to another that you need to be ready again. From one action to the next action.

“The business today is to be focusing here in ‌Cincinnati for the game against Haiti tomorrow. But for sure, in the moment that [we] finish that game, we are going to start ​to be 100% focused on our next project.”
