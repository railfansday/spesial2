Wasteful Arsenal held by Borussia Dortmund

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Arsenal vs Borussia Dortmund. UEFA Champions League Round 1.

Match report as Arsenal are denied victory by Borussia Dortmund in a Champions League encounter at the Emirates Stadium; Viktor Gyokeres scored a spectacular opener but Maximilian Beier equalised as the Gunners wasted a string of chances

Football Features Writer @nicholaspwright

Arsenal were punished for failing to take their chances for the second game in a row as Maximilian Beier clinched a 1-1 draw for Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League.

Viktor Gyokeres gave Arsenal an early lead with a stunning strike from distance but the game followed a similar pattern to Saturday's 2-2 draw with Newcastle as they were unable to capitalise on a one-sided first half before conceding after the break.

Dortmund's leveller came when Beier slotted a clinical finish past Kepa Arrizabalaga, standing in for the injured David Raya, following a rampaging run from defender Joane Gadou.

Arsenal racked up 30 shots and had a string of chances to win it in the closing stages, hitting the woodwork through Jurrien Timber's deflected cut-back and again through Declan Rice's low shot.

Gyokeres also spurned opportunities, firing over from close range from Martin Odegaard's pass shortly before half-time, then heading awkwardly wide from a few yards out when it looked easier to score from another Odegaard ball.

Arsenal remain unbeaten in the Champions League but the draw causes them to drop two places in the league phase table to fifth before they resume their Premier League campaign at home to Manchester City, live on Sky Sports on Sunday.
