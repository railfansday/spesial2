Gen.G recover from opening loss to beat LYON 2-1 and reach Worlds 2026 quarter-finals

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Gen.G completed a 3-0 Swiss Stage run at Worlds 2026 on Sunday by coming from a game down to beat LYON 2-1. The LCK champions are through to the quarter-finals, while LYON must look to Round 4 to join them.

The best-of-three series began at 20:55 on Sunday 25 October 2026.

Earlier in the evening, on the third day of the Swiss Stage, Dplus KIA swept Movistar KOI 2-0. Smash scored the first pentakill of the tournament in game one of that series.

The Swiss Stage runs over five rounds. Teams reaching three match wins advance to the Knockout Stage. Teams reaching three defeats are eliminated. Gen.G’s run began with 1-0 wins over Invictus Gaming and Hanwha Life Esports.

LYON controlled game one. They took Baron as soon as it spawned at 20 minutes without losing a player. A second Baron followed after a five-versus-five fight, and LYON pushed on to the Nexus. Dot Esports picked out jungler Inspired and bot laner Berserker, on Kalista, as LYON’s key performers. On the other side, Canyon’s Qiyana kept missing his ultimates.

Gen.G responded quickly. A convincing top-lane teamfight at 23 minutes gave them Baron and the Dragon Soul, and the series was level. In the decider, Gen.G lost Galio early in a teamfight. Kiin then joined on Camille via Teleport. Gen.G went on to break the mid inhibitor, collect three dragons and win the final fight.

On paper, the gap between the sides was wide. Gen.G sit fourth in the Elo power rankings with a rating of 1868 and a 118-30 record. LYON are 28th with a rating of 1720 and a 34-28 record.

The defeat was LYON’s first at the event. They had opened with 1-0 wins over Team Secret Whales and Karmine Corp. They go into Round 4 with a 2-1 record. One more match win would send them to the Knockout Stage.

LYON’s season already includes a significant result against G2 Esports. LYON knocked G2 out of MSI 2026 with a 3-0 win on 10 July 2026, and Inspired was named MVP of the series. In Allen, the LEC’s representative were beaten 1-0 by Top Esports in Round 3.

Elsewhere in Round 3, Invictus Gaming beat Hanwha Life Esports 1-0. Both sides had lost to Gen.G earlier in the stage.

At the Credit Union of Texas Event Center in Allen, Texas, Swiss Round 4 is scheduled for 28 to 30 October, with Round 5 to follow on 31 October. The quarter-finals are scheduled for 3 to 6 November at the same venue.
