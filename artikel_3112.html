Arista Revenue Up 58% as Q4 Outlook Slows: Rapid Earnings Reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Arista's own forecast for next quarter calls for far smaller gains than this one delivered. We look at which parts of the profit picture hold up.

Arista Networks (ANET +3.52%) reported third-quarter revenue of $3.66 billion, up 58.5% from a year ago and above its own $3.3 billion guide. Shares rose about 8% in after-hours trading. Q3 grew 20.5% from Q2, but the Q4 guide of $3.9 billion to $4.0 billion implies only 7% to 9% more. Growth is set to slow sharply, and gross margin is already slipping.

The guide matters more than the quarter. Arista sells to a few very large customers, and the release's risk list flags that concentration and volatile growth rates. Big customers order in lumps, so growth this fast is hard to hold, and the guide already points to a slowdown.

Gross margin fell because mix shifted toward product sales, which earn less than services. Product was 87% of revenue. Operating margin still rose because operating expenses grew much more slowly than sales. The release gives no gross margin outlook.

GAAP profit includes a $134.5 million gain on strategic investments, which the company removes from non-GAAP earnings. That is why GAAP earnings per share top the non-GAAP figure, and why operating profit is the cleaner read.

Watch whether Q4 revenue lands at or above the top of the $3.9 billion to $4.0 billion guide. Also track whether gross margin stops sliding as product sales keep growing.
