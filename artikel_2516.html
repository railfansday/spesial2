Where to watch Players Era 16: TV schedule, channels, live streams, bracket for 2026 college basketball tournament

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Where to watch Players Era 16: TV schedule, channels, live streams, bracket for 2026 college basketball tournament originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Thanksgiving week brings some of college basketball's best to Las Vegas for the Players Era 16.

Seven Top 25 teams are represented, led by No. 5 Texas Tech, No. 9 Tennessee, No. 13 St. John's and No. 15 Alabama.

Schools are split into two eight-team brackets, with the winner of each set to face off in Saturday's championship game. Each participating team receives at least $1 million in NIL money, with additional bonuses to the winner and runner-up.

Eight games a day are on tap in Vegas over the holiday week. Here's all you need to know to watch every one.

Players Era 16 games will air on ESPN, ESPN2 and ESPNU. See the full schedule below. Fans can stream every game live with Fubo.

Additional streaming options include DIRECTV, which also offers a free trial, and the ESPN app.

Here are the initial matchups in each bracket. The Bracket 1 and Bracket 2 winners meet in the championship game.

Bracket 1 (B1) games will be played at T-Mobile Arena and Bracket 2 (B2) games at Michelob Ultra Arena, which will also host the championship game.
