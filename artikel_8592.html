MLG Oz Limited (MLGZF) Shareholder/Analyst Call Prepared Remarks Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

MLG Oz Limited (MLGZF) Shareholder/Analyst Call November 25, 2026 9:00 PM EST

Murray Leahy - Founder & Executive ChairAnna NeulingJoan Dabon - Company SecretaryMark Hatfield - Chief Executive Officer

Good morning, everybody. As it is now 10 a.m. and I'm advised that we have a quorum in present, I officially declare the meeting open. My name is Murray Leahy. I'm the Executive Chair of the company and will chair this meeting today.

On behalf of the Board, it is my pleasure to welcome shareholders attending online to the Annual General Meeting of MLG Oz Limited. I'd like to introduce my fellow directors, Anna Neuling, Garret Dixon and Simon Price. As with me in the room today is our CEO, Mark Hatfield; our Chief Development Officer; Tom Gregorczyk; along with our Company Secretary, Joan Dabon. I'd also like to welcome Norman Neill from HLB Mann Judd, the company's auditor to answer questions on the conduct of the FY '26 audit.

Before we move into the formalities of today's meeting, I, as Chair, would like to make a few remarks on the year. FY '26 was another year of improved financial and operational performance for MLG. On a pro forma basis, we achieved a 3.9% uplift in revenue to $561.4 million for the year, delivering an EBIT of $29.4 million, up 25.7% and NPAT of $16.3 million, a 34.7% improvement on FY '25. Our CEO, Mark Hatfield, will talk in more detail on the year's performance a little later. But on behalf of the Board, I would like to congratulate and thank the team for their efforts across FY '26.

This year's result is an outcome of cost control, productivity, better asset utilization and stronger demand for our integrated services.
