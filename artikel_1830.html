PSG reportedly agrees to new deals with Dembélé, Doué, Kvaratskhelia and Safonov

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Paris Saint-Germain has agreed to keep Ousmane Dembélé through 2030, according to a Canal+ report carried by Le Figaro. His fellow forwards Désiré Doué and Khvicha Kvaratskhelia would stay through 2032, as would Matvey Safonov, Luis Enrique’s first-choice goalkeeper.

Doué signed through 2029 when he arrived from Rennes in 2024. His talks with PSG had gathered pace by late October, and the reported agreement would add three years to that deal.

Keeping all three forwards would preserve the attack that delivered PSG’s first European Cup. In the 2025 Champions League final against Inter, Dembélé set up a goal for each of his two attacking partners. Doué scored twice and Kvaratskhelia added the fourth in a 5-0 win.

PSG is also in talks with captain Marquinhos, who joined from Roma in 2013. Foot Mercato, citing Canal+, says the Brazilian is discussing a one-year extension.
