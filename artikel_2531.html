Duke Basketball beats out UNC for international prospect

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The North Carolina Tar Heels suffered their first loss of the 2026-27 season last week, falling to Georgia at home on Friday night. But now, they took a tough loss on the recruiting trail as well.

On Tuesday, international wing Matija Lukic pledged his commitment to Duke, giving the Blue Devils another commitment in the 2027 class. The commitment comes just a few weeks after the Serbian wing visited the United States and took trips to different campuses, including UNC, Duke, NC State and UConn.

After the visits, there was belief that the Tar Heels and Huskies were the top two teams involved. But over the past weekend, the momentum shifted to Duke's favor. Now, the Blue Devils have another commitment and a recruiting win over the Tar Heels.

The 6-foot-9 international prospect is among the top players overseas in the 2027 class. He joins fellow international prospect Luke Paul as well as Kager Knueppel and Asa Montgomery for Duke's 2027 class.

For Malone and his team, they go back to the drawing board in terms of recruits who they are targeting.
