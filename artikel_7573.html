Spokane, Washington, woman who smuggled drugs into Federal Detention Center pleads guilty

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Seattle – A 49-year-old Spokane, Washington, resident pleaded guilty today in U.S. District Court in Seattle to conspiracy to distribute controlled substances, providing contraband in prison, and distribution of controlled substances, following the death of a Federal Detention Center (FDC) inmate in January 2025, announced Acting U.S. Attorney Charles Neil Floyd.
