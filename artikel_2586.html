📊 Ireland's reliance on Troy Parrott summed up by remarkable stat

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

If anyone knows a thing or two about carrying the weight of a nation, it's Republic of Ireland striker Troy Parrott.

The 24-year-old Real Betis star has continued his remarkable goalscoring form for his country in tonight's Nations League clash against Kosovo.

Not long before the half-hour mark, the visitors were reduced to 10 men as Kreshnik Hajrizi was sent off and Parrott was there to convert from the penalty spot.

There is no denying that the striker has become Ireland's go-to in front of goal and one quite extraordinary statistic highlights just how reliant they are on him.

According to Opta, excluding own goals, Parrott has scored 11 of their last 14 goals in competitive fixtures, with Adam Idah and Jack Moylan the only other players to find the net during that run.

Just how far can Parrott carry this Ireland side?
