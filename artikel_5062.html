Investor Steve Eisman has questioned whether Anthropic’s upcoming IPO will meaningfully restore confidence in the artificial intelligence sector, arguing that investors are more concerned about data centre expansion and changes in the AI financing model. Speaking on CNBC’s Squawk Box, Eisman said the listing has become less important as market attention shifts towards potential restrictions on data centre construction by local and state governments. He warned that a complete construction moratorium could severely disrupt economic activity, while a 10% to 30% slowdown could make AI infrastructure spending more sustainable by forcing hyperscalers to rely more on cash flow rather than debt. Eisman also raised concerns about the unwinding of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s interconnected investment and financing structure, suggesting that neocloud companies could face significant pressure. He added that tighter US restrictions on computing services linked to China could further affect the sector.
