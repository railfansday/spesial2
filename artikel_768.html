David Fincher’s ‘The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth’: First Reactions

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The standalone sequel to Quentin Tarantino's Oscar-winner 'Once Upon a Time in Hollywood' sees the return of Brad Pitt's movie stuntman, and also stars Elizabeth Debicki, Scott Caan, Yahya Abdul-Mateen II, Carla Gugino and Peter Weller.

The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, the buzzy, David Fincher-directed standalone sequel to Quentin Tarantino‘s Once Upon a Time in Hollywood, held its premiere in Los Angeles on Monday evening and the first reactions have hit social media.

The embargo for full critics reviews for Cliff Booth is Nov. 19, but Netflix allowed some brief social media reaction to the film after its L.A. premiere. Cliff Booth is set to have a short, and very controversial, theatrical run, with the film being released exclusively in Imax cinemas on Nov. 25, before its Netflix streaming debut on Dec. 23. Cliff Booth sees Brad Pitt return as the titular character, the effortlessly cool veteran Hollywood stuntman we saw in Once Upon a Time. In the sequel, we see Pitt’s character as now more a part-time stuntman who seems to be doing odd, and sometimes dangerous, jobs in 1977 Los Angeles.

The ensemble cast includes Elizabeth Debicki, Scott Caan, Yahya Abdul-Mateen II, Carla Gugino, Peter Weller, Matt Groove, JB Tadena, Corey Fogelmanis and Karren Karagulian.

Timothy Olyphant is the only other star from Once Upon a Time reprising his role, returning as Western actor James Stacy. At Monday evening’s premiere, Pitt explained why Once Upon a Time star Leonardo DiCaprio’s cameo as Rick Dalton in the sequel fell apart.

Released by Sony Pictures in 2019, and written and directed by Tarantino, Once Upon a Time was a huge commercial and critical success. The film made $410 million at the box office and was nominated for 10 Oscars, winning two — Pitt for best supporting actor and Barbara Ling and Nancy Haigh for production design.

Netflix purchased the script for Once Upon a Time‘s sequel from Tarantino in 2025 and engaged Fincher to direct. Fincher previously revealed that Cliff Booth was almost developed as a Netflix series instead of a movie, as Tarantino’s original script was 230 pages long. Fincher ended up cutting 60 pages.

Despite handing over the directing duties to his friend Fincher, Tarantino has been involved in the promo for Cliff Booth. The Pulp Fiction filmmaker presented 17-minutes of the finished Cliff Booth at AFI Fest in late October and also explained his reasons for reviving Pitt’s character. Tarantino will also pen and release the novel, The Adventures of Cliff Booth, for Harper this December.
