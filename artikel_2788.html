Knicks address Taylor Swift “fake fan” label in new statement

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The New York Knicks have responded to the recent criticism surrounding Taylor Swift's support for the team. The pop superstar has faced questions about her connection to the franchise, with some fans questioning whether she is a genuine Knicks supporter.

The discussion has now prompted a response from the organization, which has publicly backed Swift and highlighted her long-standing connection to the team and Madison Square Garden.

A New York Knicks spokesperson expressed appreciation for Swift's support and pushed back against suggestions that she is a newcomer to the fanbase.

"Taylor Swift has been a Knicks fan for many years, has a long history with Madison Square Garden, and we are incredibly appreciative of her support of the organization."

The statement makes the team's position clear. The Knicks recognize Swift as a longtime supporter and value her connection to the organization.

Swift's association with Madison Square Garden also extends beyond basketball. The iconic New York venue has hosted several of her performances, giving her a long-standing relationship with the building.

The Knicks' response comes amid the attention surrounding her support for the team.

The statement also reinforces the relationship between the Knicks and one of the world's biggest music stars, whose support brings additional attention to the franchise.
