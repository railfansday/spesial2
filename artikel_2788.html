Browns players bonded over reading, so they started a book club

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

From bonding over high school football games to starting their own book club, Cleveland Browns players are continuing to show their "brotherhood" off the field.

"The Browns book club, it has a nice ring to it. It's funny guys come together, you get assigned like a character that people say you remind them of," said Browns safety Grant Delpit in an interview Good Morning America posted on Instagram Nov. 18.

The six-book "Red Rising" saga, written by No. 1 New York Times best-selling author Pierce Brown, caught the attention of the Browns locker room and staff.

"The series is kind of an interplanetary dystopian space, novel, sci-fi, with a whole lot of war in it, and fighting, but some really cool characters. It's entertaining and a nice escape for us to get outside of the football room," said linebacker Carson Schwesinger.

Given the Browns' strong start into the season, fighting and war go hand-in-hand for them. Schwesigner even has a celebration dedicated to the book where he will lock arms with his teammate after a big play during games.

The book club doesn't just included players. Ron Brewer, director of player engagement for the Browns, along with other staff members are also a part of it.

They extended an invite to Brown to join them for their game against the Houston Texans this past Sunday and a book club panel Q&A with that author that was hosted the following day.

Brown saw the team's love for reading and couldn't say no to the invite.

"Being on the field, and meeting some of the players, members of the staff, and seeing that everyone had read 'Red Rising' or was familiar with it, was utterly humbling," Brown said. "It makes me extremely proud that busy NFL players are carving times out of their schedule to read 'Red Rising' because it is really showing how books are almost a meditation.  It's an escape."

Brown was able to meet Schwesinger, Delpit and other players and staff members during the game against the Texans game. It was a good time for Brown to visit since he watched a crazy fourth quarter comeback win for the Browns that Sunday.

"I was like, I'm going to go say hi, and I think that might have been the best moment of my life," said Schwesigner.

Brown is currently working on a seventh novel for the "Red Rising" saga and his fans will be patiently waiting for it to come out. The series has sold 14 million copies worldwide.

The Browns are on a bye week; they host the Las Vegas Raiders Nov. 29 for Sunday Night Football.
