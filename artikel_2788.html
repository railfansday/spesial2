Georgia football hit a new SEC record by landing back in the conference title game. Coach Kirby Smart and defensive coordinator Glenn Schumann helped create this Bulldogs run. But now Florida State could siphon the latter.

Smart, though, sounded off on the growing Schumann to the Seminoles possibility Monday with effusive praise.

"Schumann has done a fabulous job here," Smart began. "If anything has come with his success, he's earned every bit of it."

However, he added where Schumann and Georgia's real focus is on.

"The University of Georgia is focused on Georgia Tech," Smart said.

Still, Smart knows this situation quite well. Georgia plucked the ex-Nick Saban assistant out of Alabama toward the end of the 2016 season. Plus during a time Smart coordinated the defense. Smart's 10-year Athens run now includes back-to-back national titles in 2021 and 2022.

Georgia also can seal a spot in the College Football Playoff with two more wins, starting with the rival Yellow Jackets. But Georgia could position itself for the No. 1 seed by winning the SEC title down in Atlanta on Dec. 5. That can come while Schumann still runs the defense.

Schumann still rises as one of the trendiest names available for this college football hiring cycle. Perhaps FSU wants to recreate national championship success with an assistant from the Saban/Smart coaching tree.

Florida State fired head coach Mike Norvell during the season following some mediocre results on the field. Norvell went an underwhelming 41-39 overall that featured no national title appearances. FSU is seeking its fourth head coach since the late legend Bobby Bowden retired.
