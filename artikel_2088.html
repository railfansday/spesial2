Sources: Rangers to revisit Danny Armstrong deal

Rangers will reassess their planned move for Dinamo Bucharest winger Danny Armstrong next summer, sources have told Football Insider.

The 29-year-old has emerged as a target for the Gers ahead of January, but a deal is more likely to take place next summer.

The Scot is out of contract in Bucharest next summer, but recent reports have suggested that Dinamo have an automatic extension clause that will be triggered if Armstrong plays 60 per cent of their minutes this season.

Sources say that Rangers expect that clause to be triggered, which will move the goalposts in terms of Armstrong's future.

The Ibrox outfit will still explore a move for Armstrong if his deal is extended by a further year, but it would mean a transfer fee would be needed.

The wideman has been in electric form in Romania so far this season, and already has 16 goal involvements from his opening 15 league games.

Armstrong is no stranger to Rangers boss Derek McInnes, having previously played under him during a hugely successful stint at Kilmarnock

The pair are both keen to strike up that relationship once again at Ibrox, and next summer may provide the ideal opportunity.

Football Insider revealed earlier this week that Armstrong is keen to return to Scotland and join Rangers, having been away from the Premiership since 2025.

McInnes is understood to be hopeful of reuniting with his former star within the next 12 months.

Sources say that Armstrong would find it "very hard" to turn down the chance to move to Rangers, given how much he enjoyed his previous time with McInnes.

Football Insider revealed on Friday that Besiktas are open to accepting an offer for Rangers target Vaclav Cerny in January.

The Czech winger enjoyed a hugely successful loan spell at Ibrox back in 2024-25, and the Glasgow giants have been eyeing a reunion ever since.

Rangers were priced out of a move for Cerny in the summer, but it could be a different story for them this winter.

Besiktas are open to selling the former Wolfsburg ace, and could accept an offer if one comes their way from Ibrox.
