Paul McCartney, Chrissie Hynde, Peter Hook Lead Ukraine Benefit Concert

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Songs of Freedom, a star-studded benefit raising money for women and children in Ukraine this winter, will feature performances by Chrissie Hynde, Nick Lowe, the Doobie Brothers, Bob Geldof, Peter Hook and the Light, and many others. The concert’s headlining artist has not yet been announced.

Paul McCartney, Barbra Streisand, Sean Penn, and others will also make appearances via video. The concert will take place in Manchester, England, at Co-Op Live on Feb. 3. Tickets are available now.

Some of the event’s other performers include Within Temptation, Alabama 3, Jonny and the Hardwickes, the Lathums, Inspiral Carpets, Midge Ure, and Graham Parker, as well as the Songs of Ukraine Choir and the London Metropolitan Orchestra. Huey Lewis and the News, Ron Sexsmith, Okean Elzy, and Flyte are also scheduled to join via video. The event’s organizers, which include Live Aid producer Harvey Goldsmith, intend to announce more performers in the weeks leading up to the event.

“I am really pleased to have the opportunity to play my part in helping the Children and Communities in Ukraine,” Goldsmith said in a statement. “This war has gone on far too long, and more and more children are suffering as a result. The concert at Co-Op in Manchester will highlight their plight. Proceeds from ticket sales for the concert include the organizations United24, Voices of Children, People of the Future, and Masha Foundation Unbreakable Mom. United24’s goal is “to provide humanitarian relief, education, healthcare, and psychological support to women and children affected by the war.” In addition to a ticketing link, the event’s website includes one for donations to these organizations.

The idea for the concert came from Hardwicke Circus, fronted by Jonny Foster, which last year was the first U.K. band to tour Ukraine since Russia’s invasion. “We wanted to bring some light relief to people who have been through so much,” Foster said. “We are proud to see this simple idea grow into an international night of solidarity.”
