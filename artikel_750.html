Toukie Smith, Top Runway Model and ‘227’ Actress, Dies at 74

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Her late brother was fashion designer Willi Smith, and she had twins with Robert De Niro.

Toukie Smith, a top model in the 1970s and an actress who appeared as Eva Rawley on the Marla Gibbs-starring NBC sitcom 227, has died. She was 74.

Smith died Wednesday in a New York hospital after a “long, courageous battle” with multiple sclerosis, according to an announcement from her twins, Airyn and Julian, whom she shared with former partner Robert De Niro.

Her brother, fashion designer Willi Smith, died in 1987 at age 39 of complications from AIDS.

Smith modeled for her brother as well as for fashion designers Hubert de Givenchy, Thierry Mugler, Issey Miyake and Patrick Kelly, illustrators Alvaro and Antonio Lopez and photographers Jean-Paul Goude and Guy Bourdin, among others. In 1989, she joined 227 as Eva, an artist who moves into the Lexington Place building whose landlord is Gibbs’ Mary Jenkins, and she recurred on the comedy during its final two seasons through May 1990. (Her character manages the building for a time.)

Doris Smith was born in Philadelphia on Sept. 25, 1952. She said her grandmother called her Toukie after listening to “a children’s record about a little red fire engine with lots of energy. I’ve always had lots of energy,” she told the Los Angeles Times in 1990.

She attended the Fashion Institute of Technology and began her modeling career in 1972, eventually appearing in a photo campaign for Yves St. Laurent and on the pages of such magazines as Vogue, Ebony, Cosmopolitan, Redbook and Seventeen. In 1978, Bloomingdale’s named her its model of the year.

Smith left modeling in the early ’80s and in 1987-88 appeared on three episodes of NBC’s Miami Vice as Angie, the assistant to the pop singer Caitlin Davies (Sheena Easton) — and wife of Don Johnson’s Sonny Crockett — on the show’s fourth season.

Her acting résumé also included roles in the films Talkin’ Dirty After Dark (1991), I Like It Like That (1994) and Joe’s Apartment and The Preacher’s Wife, both released in 1996.

In the ’90s, she had an eponymous restaurant on West Houston Street in New York.

She and De Niro were partners from 1988-96, and their twins were conceived by in vitro fertilization and delivered by a surrogate mother. The actor has five other children with three other women.
