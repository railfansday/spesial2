Amazon Prime Video Black Friday Cyber Monday Deals: Save Big on Streaming Add-Ons, Movie Purchases & More

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Running Nov. 19-30, Prime Video's Black Friday Cyber Monday deals bring big savings on streaming add-ons for watching live sports, holiday movies and more.

All products and services featured are independently chosen by editors. However, Billboard may receive a commission on orders placed through its retail links, and the retailer may receive certain auditable data for accounting purposes.

At a Glance: Amazon Prime Video‘s Black Friday Cyber Monday Deals

Prime Video’s Black Friday Cyber Monday streaming deals kick off Thursday, Nov. 19, and run through Monday, Nov. 30, giving shoppers less than two weeks to score discounts throughout the Thanksgiving holiday period. More add-on subscription promotions are expected throughout November and December, so keep an eye out for additional opportunities to save on your favorite streaming services.

You will need an active Amazon Prime or eligible Prime Video membership to access the Black Friday add-on subscription deals. However, these promotions aren’t exclusive to new customers. Existing Prime members can also take advantage of the discounts by adding eligible streaming channels to their accounts.

Prime members can also pick up select holiday movie classics at reduced prices, including The Grinch, The Polar Express and A Christmas Story, with prices starting at $7.99. These are purchases rather than rentals, and the promotion ends Nov. 26 at 9 p.m. PT.

A quick reminder: Promotional subscription prices only last for the stated periods before regular pricing applies. Free trials are also unavailable for channels with active Black Friday and Cyber Monday promotions, so check the renewal terms before signing up.
