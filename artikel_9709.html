I Built an Interactive 3D Camera You Can Take Apart in Your Browser 📷

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

I've been exploring Three.js and WebGL lately, experimenting with different ways to make websites more interactive.

One thing I really enjoy about 3D on the web is that you're not limited to displaying objects. You can actually let people interact with them, explore them, and understand how things work.

So I decided to build something around that idea.

Meet Camera Lab — an interactive 3D experience where you can explore a DSLR camera, take it apart, and see what's happening inside.

Instead of simply displaying a 3D camera that rotates, I wanted people to be able to explore the engineering behind it.

One of my favourite parts is the exploded view. Seeing the different components separate and come back together makes the whole experience much more interesting.

A website doesn't always have to be a collection of pages, buttons, and forms.

And honestly, there's still a lot I want to learn.

I'd really appreciate some feedback from fellow developers, especially anyone working with Three.js, WebGL, or creative frontend development.
