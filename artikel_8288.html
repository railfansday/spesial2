TA Tales – Phase 2/3: The Pivot and Rebirth

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

This post is dedicated to Rose. The gal with answers to all your flora and fauna questions, holder of “Arthur’s fist,” and a spirit of endless energy for adventure.

Maya and I had countless conversations, before starting Te Araroa, about all the worst case scenarios that come with thru hike attempts. We were prepared for injuries, but what surprised us was how quickly they can happen. We felt sad, irritated, and burdensome. However, a change of perspective kept our hopes alive, and with help from Rose, we hit the road in our little white sedan.

The roadwork signs are delightfully polite

A section of TA here, and surf lesson there. More TA sections happened, followed by a dip in a natural thermal pool, and a walk through an old gold mine. During this time, our visit to Cathedral Cove proved very exciting. The archway was in the film The Chronicles of Narnia: Prince Caspian, so a childhood dream was satisfied. We walked, drove, experienced, camped, ate, and were merry. Just a beautiful way to see Aotearoa, and allow all of us to heal from our 90 mile beach ordeal.

We parted ways with Rose on October 31st, but in true Halloween fashion, adopted a travel size skeleton, so she can still accompany us on our adventure south.

Heading to the cave concert after dropping Rose at the airport

Mermaid skeleton Rose is clipped in and ready for some walking

Maya and I had one more adventure with Sina (our car, in case you forgot) before we dropped her off. A cave concert at Waipu Caves Farm Park on Halloween night. We listened to live music in the darkness of the cave with thousands of glow worms thriving above us. Just cool, and all the artists were amazingly talented.

We are currently in Auckland completing the Coast to Coast Walkway section of TA. Keeping the days short with hopes by next week Maya will be good as new. So far, our walks have been very successful, and she is feeling little to no discomfort. A huge blessing and such a relief to know our month of galavanting has paid off.

Having an area specific sign is the secret ingredient to a successful hitch

To everyone who kept us in your prayers, thank you so so much. We are not out of the woods yet, but the future is looking awesome. Our walk continues, woohooo!
