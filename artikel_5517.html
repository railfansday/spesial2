Treasury secretary said an Anthropic founder will donate to Trump Accounts

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Scott Bessent did not name the founder, who is set to make the gift over the next few weeks, he told CNBC

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said one of Anthropic's founders plans to make a large gift to Trump Accounts in the coming weeks.

"One of the Anthropic founders is going to make a large gift to Trump accounts over the next few weeks," Bessent told CNBC on Friday. He did not name the founder. "You'll hear from their family soon," he said.

Tom Brown, an Anthropic co-founder and its chief compute officer, intends to disclose a gift to Trump Accounts, according to Bloomberg, which cited a source familiar with the situation. Brown is worth $23.8 billion, according to the Bloomberg Billionaires Index.

Bessent said Trump Accounts are drawing a growing number of gifts, pointing to donations in Montana, Oklahoma and Connecticut and from Susan Dell $DELL and Michael Dell. He said Elon Musk plans to give one share of SpaceX to every Trump account in January. With 70 million accounts, Bessent put that gift at about $10 billion.

"This is the perfect vehicle to donate to the children of America, and they will receive 100 percent of the proceeds," Bessent said.

Anthropic went public on Thursday. Bessent called it the most valuable IPO in history. Anthropic stock fell on Friday. Every one of Anthropic's seven co-founders is now worth upward of $10 billion, according to Bloomberg.

Anthropic's founders had planned to sell stock in the offering. Each of the seven co-founders intended to sell about $250 million of stock in the IPO, according to a revised prospectus. That would leave each with a stake worth about $18 billion.

The co-founders are Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, Brown, Jared Kaplan, Sam McCandlish, Jack Clark and Christopher Olah.
