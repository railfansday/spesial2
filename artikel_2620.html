Urban Meyer says Jeremiah Smith may be best college football player ever

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

It's no surprise that former Ohio State coach Urban Meyer picked Ohio State to beat Michigan in the upcoming rivalry game on Nov. 28. While Meyer picked the Michigan defense as the best unit on the field, he had glowing words to say about Ohio State wide receiver Jeremiah Smith.

"On offense, we've got the best player in the country, maybe the best player ever to play college football. That's how good Jeremiah [Smith] is," Meyer said to 10TV. "But we also have a backup quarterback. And the offensive line's not playing great."

Meyer predicted a low-scoring "rock fight" between the two likely non-playoff teams because of the strengths in Michigan's defense and Ohio State's offense.

"The roster's better at Ohio State ... the best unit performing right now is the Wolverine defense, and that concerns me a little bit. They really hammered UCLA," Meyer said. "You've got to think: the other side of the ball for the Wolverines is terrible ... they kept UCLA to three points until right near the end of the game, so that's a legit defense."

Michigan upset UCLA with a 18-16 triple overtime victory on Nov. 21.

Michigan's offense has been stagnant for most of the season, ranking 132nd in the FBS in yards per game and 129th in points per game. Bryce Underwood, who was benched earlier in the season, is expected to start against Ohio State. He went 8 for 18 for 63 yards and an interception against Ohio State in 2025.

Ohio State will be entering the rivalry game with a loss as its latest result. The Buckeyes fell 31-28 to Nebraska and struggled defensively, allowing 30-plus points for the third time in the last four games.

Smith had 165 receiving yards and two touchdowns against Nebraska. He leads the FBS in receiving yards and touchdowns.
