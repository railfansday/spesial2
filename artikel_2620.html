Scherhant and Wirtz star as Germany reach quarters with beaten Dutch

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Derry Scherhant scored his first Germany goal and Florian Wirtz netted twice to send Jürgen Klopp's side into the Nations League quarter-finals after a 5-0 thrashing of the Netherlands, who also go through.

The Freiburg winger has been one of the finds of Klopp's early reign and he shone on Monday in the group A2 finale at Berlin's packed Olympic Stadium.

Wirtz doubled Germany's lead with an equally stunning strike and Nico Schlotterbeck headed in the third from a free-kick. Wirtz made it 4-0 on 71 minutes and Brajan Gruda capped a strong display with his second international goal.

Germany reached March's last eight as group winners ahead of the Dutch, with third-placed Greece empty-handed despite a 1-0 win over bottom side Serbia giving them the same points as the big duo.

Scherhant was born in the German capital and it was a homecoming to remember. His grandfather, who used to drive him to youth games in Berlin as a child, was in the stands to watch his breakthrough moment in just his fourth international appearance.

Klopp made a slow start to his stewardship after replacing Julian Nagelsmann following another World Cup flop.

Germany lost at home to Greece in September, but three handsome wins since put them through. A complicated three-team mini group meant Germany finished top on head-to-heads after winning their 50th meeting with old rivals the Netherlands.
