Atlético beat Viking 2-0 as both strikers score, but Sørloth injury hits Barcelona trip

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Alexander Sørloth and Julián Álvarez both ended goal droughts as Atlético Madrid beat Viking FK 2-0 to reach seven points in the Champions League. Sørloth's muscle injury now adds to an injury list that already includes Lookman, just as Atlético face Barcelona and PSV.

Atlético Madrid's win over Viking FK revived their hopes of a direct top-eight finish. Three league-phase games remain, and one of them is away to PSV. Both of Simeone's leading strikers found the net. Yet the night also added a new name to the injury list.

Sørloth broke the deadlock by firing in a pass from Alejandro Grimaldo. Five minutes later, Álvarez's free-kick crept in at the near post. It was his 27th goal in the Champions League. It was also his first Champions League goal at the Metropolitano in 210 days. The strike was his third of the 2026-27 season: one in LaLiga and two in Europe.

Grimaldo was named Player of the Match. Viking defender Henrik Falchener was sent off late in the game.

The result follows an uneven league phase. Atlético opened with a 2-1 defeat at Liverpool. They then beat Manchester United 2-0 at home. A 1-1 draw at Stuttgart followed. Bayern München then won 0-2 at the Metropolitano on 3 November.

That home defeat was unusual. Before kick-off against Viking, Atlético had won 11 of their previous 14 home matches in the group stage or league phase. They lost the other three. The victory over the Norwegian side returned them to winning ways in front of their own supporters.

The mood inside the stadium was not entirely positive, however. Some home fans whistled Álvarez when he was substituted, even though he had scored.

Sørloth went off limping after 71 minutes. He had taken a heavy knock against the post and left the field with an overload, the club said on 26 November. No date has been set for his return. He will do physiotherapy and gym rehabilitation.

The expected absence is significant. ESPN Deportes expects him to miss at least the games against Elche, Barcelona, Real Betis, Valencia and PSV. That would rule him out of the visit to Eindhoven.

His injury adds to a list that already includes Lookman. The forward has a high-grade knee sprain. Simeone is therefore short of attacking options for a demanding run of fixtures.

The first major test comes at the Spotify Camp Nou on Wednesday 2 December, with kick-off at 20:30 GMT (21:30 in Spain). Viking host Feyenoord on 8 December. Atlético's next Champions League game follows a day later, away to PSV Eindhoven on 9 December.
