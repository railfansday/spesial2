Sophie Cunningham’s Fever concern echoed behind the scenes amid Stephanie White talk

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

As questions grow around Stephanie White's future in Indiana, Robin Lundberg has had his say.

Right now, Stephanie White's future is still up in the air after Kelly Krauskopf and Amber Cox both left the front office last month.

Whoever steps into those open roles has a busy offseason waiting, as free agency is upon us and big names like Kelsey Mitchell and Sophie Cunningham are hitting the market, meaning Indiana has plenty of big decisions to make around Caitlin Clark and Aliyah Boston before next season.

Back in September, Sophie Cunningham raised eyebrows when she questioned how long Indiana practiced late in the season under Stephanie White.

And it seems as if that's an issue that is shared with the Fever, as Robin Lundberg has claimed that that has been echoed behind the scenes.

He said: "I mean, look, Sophie Cunningham talked about them practicing too hard and it being a real grind. And that is something I heard behind the scenes as well—that that was an issue, them being overworked or feeling that way."

That wasn't the only topic, as he also voiced his opinion on Stephanie White's future.

"I just don't see how Stephanie White can return. I really don't. And I don't say that to be mean. I gave Stephanie White the benefit of the doubt for a lot of last season, really a lot of her Indiana Fever tenure.

You know, you can go back and see the article I wrote about what I saw to be a philosophical divide between Caitlin Clark and Stephanie White in 2025—the year that Clark missed much of the season due to injury—because I noticed at one game I was at, it was a Liberty game in Indianapolis, where they had sort of different responses to the same question in regards to what they were going to go out and do.

And then there was this year. And of course, you know, the bits of conflict that did happen that we saw on the sideline, even into the playoff series—some of the frustration—and Stephanie White was addressing it."

Any final call on White's future probably won't happen until Indiana finds a replacement for former general manager Amber Cox. With the front office still in flux, the team will likely wait for its new decision-maker to get in the building and assess the roster before deciding if White stays.
