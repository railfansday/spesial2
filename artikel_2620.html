Leaders BVB host in-form Leverkusen, Wounded HSV and Bayern clash, Englischen Wochen planning: Five things to look out for on Bundesliga Matchday 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Before the Round of 16 DFB-Pokal midweek action, matchday 11 of the Bundesliga promised some intriguing fixtures. Here are the five talking points before Werder Bremen host bottom-placed Borussia Mönchengladbach in the opening fixture of the weekend's actions.

Days after returning to the Bundesliga summit, Borussia Dortmund became the first men's team to return home with a point from a visit to Emirates Stadium this season. Niko Kovač's men will have four more tricky fixtures in the space of 11 days.

In the middle of home games against Bayer Leverkusen and Inter, the Black and Yellows travel to Eintracht Frankfurt and fierce rivals Schalke. On Sunday night, Carles Martinez's men will hope to record their fourth successive win in all competitions.

In addition, it'll be their fourth win on the trot at Westfalenstadion (Signal Iduna Park) if they manage to end Dortmund's unbeaten start to the league campaign. Goals from Ibrahim Maza and Robert Andrich helped Die Werkself to upset BVB both in the DFB-Pokal and Bundesliga last term.

Staying with the topic of unbeaten runs, Bayern's streak in all competitions came to an end on Wednesday as Vincent Kompany's depleted side lost at LOSC Lille. Uncharacteristically, the Rekordmeister have only managed two wins from their last five games.

Meanwhile, Merlin Polzin's side lost four in five, including a 4-0 defeat to Nordderby rivals Werder Bremen last week. Miro Muheim suffering yet another serious injury compounded HSV's misery as they showed plenty of cracks in a shocking second-half performance at Weserstadion.

However, facing a Bayern side with plenty of their own issues present a perfect opportunity to end a 21-game winless run in this fixture. Since they beat Bayern twice in 2009 with Mladen Petric's goals, it has been a one-sided affair between the 1980s rivals. Last season's 2-2 draw at Volksparkstadion could also be used as a source of motivation for the hosts.

Freiburg extended their winning streak to six games with an injury-time triumph over Twente on Thursday. Julian Schuster's side are targeting an automatic qualification to the Round of 16 in the Conference League, while they face 2. Bundesliga side Kaiserslautern in the DFB-Pokal next Wednesday.

In the Bundesliga, they encounter a top-four clash on Sunday with RB Leipzig next to visit Europa-Park Stadion. Martin Demichelis' men scored five goals each in back-to-back league games before climbing to the top 24 in the Champions League with a 3-1 win over RC Lens. Brajan Gruda and Antonio Nusa will head to Freiburg with impressive form in front of goal.

In a matchday packed with high-profile games, the Topspiel will be contested between two historic clubs in Stuttgart. Sebastian Hoeneß has a stacked squad in the final third, but they have only scored a single goal in their last four games in all competitions.

Meanwhile, Eintracht Frankfurt are unbeaten in their last four matches as they climbed over their Saturday hosts with a narrow win in Hoffenheim last week. Nine goals were scored in the head-to-head games between the sides last term as Nikolas Nartey scored a late winner in a five-goal thriller in Stuttgart.

Both Ermedin Demirović and Deniz Undav were on target back then, and they will hope to end their respective goal droughts on Saturday. Adi Hütter's first win back as a Frankfurt coach came at Mainz, but that remains their only victory on the road in Bundesliga.

For the sides in Europe, the last international break of the year followed with six games in the space of three weeks. The DFB-Pokal Round of 16 sandwiches the last two European matchdays of 2026 as the fixtures come thick and fast before the winterpause.

Usually, it's not something that worries FC Bayern, but the whole atmosphere at the club and injuries to key players made this the perfect time to face the juggernauts. Kompany's men will host 2. Bundesliga leaders Hertha after their tricky trip to Hamburg.

Stuttgart and Freiburg will also clash 2. Bundesliga teams, while Leverkusen and Frankfurt host Leipzig and Dortmund, respectively, in the standout fixtures of the round. Köln, Augsburg and Borussia Mönchengladbach need to find the right balance between a deep cup run and a relegation fight.
