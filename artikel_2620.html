Jaguars vs. Giants live updates, inactives, where to watch: Jacksonville brings elite defense to MetLife Stadium as Giants seek bounce-back win

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The 5-3-1 New York Giants are in the pole position to win the NFC East. They're hoping to maintain their first-place cushion in the division on Sunday, searching for a bounce-back victory after falling to the Washington Commanders on "Thursday Night Football" last week.

Returning to the win column this time out means beating an AFC title contender. The Giants are hosting the 7-2 Jacksonville Jaguars, who enter MetLife Stadium having won six of their past seven games.

Jacksonville is firmly in command of the AFC South. Liam Coen's team is among the most complete in the NFL this season. After taking down the Tennessee Titans last week, the Jaguars were the only team in the league that ranked inside the top 10 in EPA in all three phases of the game, according to ESPN Insights, which also noted that the Jags' plus-125 point differential was the best by any team through its first nine games since the 2021 Buffalo Bills (plus-145).

Jacksonville's defense is imposing. The Jaguars came into the weekend allowing only 16 points per game, the second fewest of any team this time around. Trevor Lawrence is posting a 21:5 touchdown-to-interception ratio. Giants quarterback J.J. McCarthy has his work cut out for him to keep pace and navigate everything Jacksonville throws his way.

Fortunately for McCarthy, he's buoyed by a Giants defense that's giving up the third-fewest passing yards per game (184.1) this season.

Follow along with Yahoo Sports for live updates, highlights and more as the Jaguars face the Giants on Sunday afternoon, airing on CBS.

Despite dealing with knee and ankle injuries, the Jaguars are expected to give running back Bhayshul Tuten the "bulk of carries" in Week 11, per CBS reporter Tracy Wolfson.

Tuten's practice reps have been limited as he works through the injuries, logging limited sessions on Wednesday and Friday while sitting out of Thursday's practice. He was left without any official designation on the final injury report.

Tuten is the team's leading rusher with 609 yards and five touchdowns this season, adding another 206 yards and three scores through the air. Chris Rodriguez is his backup.
