Working Hours: Following AU business hours

Work set-up: 100% Remote work under Independent Contractor Agreement

You will also use an established ERP system to manage estimating information and coordinate with internal teams to support smooth project execution. This is a great opportunity for someone with commercial joinery estimating experience who is detail-oriented, organised, and comfortable working within a structured remote team.
