Malik Nabers named NFC offensive player of the month

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Giants receiver Malik Nabers had a strong October and now has been recognized for it.

The NFL named Nabers NFC offensive player of the month.

He had 36 catches for a league-high 509 yards with two touchdowns in October.

As noted by the league, Nabers was No. 2 with 36 receptions last month and was the only player to record at least 100 receiving yards in four games.

This is Nabers’ first career player of the month award.

In seven games this year, Nabers has caught 48 passes for 605 yards with two touchdowns.
