Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Re: Is withdrawal rate modeling too simplistic to be useful to most people?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

If you’ll need a new roof in 10 years that will cost $20,000, then you set aside $2,000/year.

That $2,000 is part of your annual withdrawal rate, even if stays invested until needed.
I guess that makes sense as an approximation. Certainly better than not factoring in lumpy expenses at all. I don't have any sense for how much error could be introduced by trying to spread all future lumpy expenses across all planned years of life to arrive at a smoothed annual number and then comparing that against a SWR. I guess that would be pretty conservative since in modeling, that money would be leaving the portfolio sooner than it would IRL.
