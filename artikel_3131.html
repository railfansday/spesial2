CleanSpark (CLSK -7.51%) reported a total net loss of $987.1 million for fiscal 2026 as mark-to-market adjustments on bitcoin holdings impacted the bottom line. Management stated that the company is executing a strategic shift toward artificial intelligence (AI) and high-performance computing (HPC) infrastructure, highlighted by a $6.6 billion long-term lease at its Sandersville facility. The company reported ending the fiscal year with $1.3 billion in liquidity and 13,530 bitcoin. Management emphasized that its bitcoin mining operations continue to fund daily operations, reporting a gross margin of 34% in the fourth quarter. The company announced the hiring of Ginna Raahauge as SVP of AI Data Centers to oversee the commercialization of its campus portfolio.
