Is Walmart Stock Too Expensive to Buy Now?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The dominant retailer's share price is up a remarkable 162% in five years.

By Neil Patel – Nov 20, 2026 at 1:00PM EST

Walmart (WMT +2.38%) is no longer your grandfather's supermarket chain. The company has evolved. Its financials now include contributions from online sales, advertising, and subscriptions. Shares have soared 162% in the past five years (as of Nov. 16).

The business is valued at $1 trillion. Is this top retail stock too expensive to buy right now?

Walmart's size hasn't gotten in the way of its strong fundamental performance. Net income went from $13.7 billion in fiscal 2021 to $22.3 billion in fiscal 2026, growing 63% over that five-year stretch. This financial metric has been an important tailwind propelling the stock to market-thumping returns.

The valuation, however, is not appealing to new investors. The company can be purchased today at a price-to-earnings ratio of 46.5. You can buy exposure to the S&P 500 index at a 23.3 multiple. And leading artificial intelligence stocks trade at much cheaper valuations.

According to the sell-side analyst community, Walmart's earnings per share will increase at a compound annual rate of 11.1% from fiscal 2026 to fiscal 2029. That kind of growth doesn't justify the valuation, even though this is a competitively advantaged business that has long dominated its industry.

In the coming five or 10 years, I think there's a much higher likelihood that the P/E ratio compresses than expands. This introduces greater downside risk, even if Walmart keeps performing well from a fundamental perspective.
