Sylvamo Corporation (SLVM +19.08%) reported third quarter results featuring a strategic move to divest its European operations to focus on high-return opportunities in North America and Latin America. Management indicated that the proposed sale will enable the company to invest more heavily in core business performance and pursue operational excellence in its most attractive markets. The company is advancing significant capital projects at its Eastover mill and Sumter facility to increase internal converting capacity and reduce reliance on expensive outsourced services. Financial results for the period were supported by successfully implemented price increases and improved mill performance, although management noted that global supply chain pressures and rising input costs continue to impact the industry.
