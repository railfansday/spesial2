‘Grand Theft Auto VI’ Revs Its Engines & Leads 2026 Game Awards Nominations: Full List

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Fans may cast their votes now through Dec. 9.

Grand Theft Auto VI is one of the year’s most-anticipated video games, even ahead of its official release on Thursday (Nov. 19). Now, it’s the leading contender for the 2026 Game Awards, which honor the video game industry’s most innovative games, studios and creatives.

The Rockstar Games title received eight nominations, including game of the year, best score & music, best action/adventure and best narrative.

This year’s list of nominees includes 112 different games, individuals and teams across a variety of gaming genres and platforms.

This year’s other game of the year nominees are 007 First Light from IO Interactive; Control Resonant from Remedy and Annapurna; Phantom Blade Zero from S-GAME; Resident Evil Requiem from Capcom; and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time from Nintendo.

The other nominees for best score & music, presented in association with Spotify, are Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve (Bandai Namco Aces/Bandai Namco), Mixtape (Beethoven & Dinosaur/Annapurna), Phantom Blade Zero (S-GAME) and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Nintendo).

Nintendo is the most nominated publisher with 12 nods across its combined portfolio, followed by Capcom with 11 nods, and Annapurna Interactive and PlayStation with nine nods each.

Nominees are selected by a jury of over 160 media publications and creator outlets from around the world. From now through Dec. 9 at 6 p.m. PT, fans will be able to help decide the winners in all categories via online voting on TheGameAwards.com. In China, fans can vote for their favorites on a variety of platforms including Bilibili.

The Game Awards will air live from the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles on Thursday, Dec. 10. Lorne Balfe will conduct The Game Awards Orchestra. Balfe, a top scorer for film and TV, has also scored such video games as Assassin’s Creed Revelations, Assassin’s Creed III, Crysis 2, Skylanders and Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2. Since the 2018 Game Awards, he has conducted a medley of the themes of the six nominees for game of the year to accompany the presentation of the award at the end of the show.

This year’s show will stream for free across more than 40 digital video platforms including Disney+, Live TV on PS5, Prime Video, Roblox, Steam, TikTok Live, Twitch, X and YouTube. The Game Awards 2025 broke viewership records with an estimated 171 million global livestreams, up 11% over 2024’s showcase.

The Game Awards is once again partnering with Twitch for “Twitch Predicts The Game Awards,” an extension where participants can assemble a bracket of the titles they anticipate will win big at The Game Awards.

The Game Awards is executive produced by Geoff Keighley and Kimmie Kim. Richard Preuss is the director, LeRoy Bennett is the creative director, and Lara Karchmer is co-executive producer.
