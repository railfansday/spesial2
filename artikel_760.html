Louis Carr Exits as BET President

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Carr was named president in December 2025.

Louis Carr has exited BET less than one year after he was named president. Carr was with the brand for 40 years.

Cheeks says Skydance “believe(s) deeply” in BET and “are committed” to its future.

Carr’s tenure will officially end when 2026 officially ends. It was Carr’s decision to exit, Cheeks says. Skydance acquired BET with its Paramount merger, which closed last summer. Carr was named BET president in December 2025.

“We will have more to share soon on BET leadership and structure,” Cheeks wrote on Monday.

Carr’s farewell email, like his tenure, was lengthy. Read his message in full below.

Dear Team,For 40 years, I have had one of the best jobs in the media and entertainment industry. Having a position that gives one the opportunity to carry out a purpose that is bigger than oneself is a privilege. Working for a brand whose mission from day one was to uplift and empower the Black community is an honor I’ll never forget.

That said, even as I write this email, I wrestle with my decision that the time has come to make a transition. Based on recent events within our community and our country, I’ve decided to chart a new path and my last day will be December 31.

BET has never been just a job to me. It has been a community, a culture and a place to serve. When I reflect on this journey, I am incredibly proud of what we have accomplished together. Over the years, we helped transform the multicultural advertising landscape, built meaningful relationships with some of the world’s most recognized brands and served as the launching pad for today’s top Black creators. We demonstrated the undeniable value, influence, and purchasing power of Black consumers while helping companies better understand and invest in our communities.

I am particularly proud of the momentum we have created and the work we have undertaken to strengthen the brand, expand our business and reaffirm BET’s place at the center of Black culture. Stepping into the role of President gave me the opportunity to see just how much the BET brand is loved by our talent, our consumers and the next generation.

We established our brand positioning as “Something You Can Believe In,” reinforcing the trust, purpose, and cultural connection that have always defined BET. We established a trusted Board of Advisors, one that would be envied by any Fortune 500 company, to help inform our direction and ensure that the voices and perspectives of our community remain part of the conversation.

And that was just the beginning. In 2026 alone, we:

The work of this incredible BET team has afforded me the opportunity to earn an abundance of professional accolades and recognition, including five Lifetime Achievement Awards, induction into the American Advertising Federation’s Advertising Hall of Fame, and more than 14 community awards. I have also been privileged to sit on board seats across the industry, including with the International Radio and Television Society (IRTS), the Advertising Council, and the American Advertising Federation (AAF). Additionally, I am proud to be the longest-serving board member of the Video Advertising Bureau (VAB).

Thank you for your partnership, your commitment, and, most importantly, for allowing me to be part of your journey. It has been one of the greatest honors of my life.

I am proud of what we have built and proud of what we have accomplished.
