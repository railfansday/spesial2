NFC Playoff picture: There’s a new No. 2 behind the 49ers

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A couple of NFC games have already influenced the playoff picture. One team dropped out, while another climbed to the No. 2 spot. The San Francisco 49ers haven't played yet, but a win over the Seattle Seahawks could leave them in an uphill battle for a postseason spot moving forward.

Let's look at the updated standings with Week 12 underway:

There are no tiebreakers worth noting in the standings. Not for the playoff teams, anyway.

The Bears' last-second win over the Lions catapulted them to the 2-seed. It's not the end of the world for Detroit, which fell to the fifth seed. But a head-to-head, divisional, and conference loss could come back to bite the Lions at the end of the season.

A Rams win puts them in the final playoff spot, despite being 1-2 in the NFC West. Advanced stats and power ratings still love the Rams and rank them as a top team in the league, despite their record. They may not pass the eyeball test. If anything, the Packers let Matthew Stafford and company off the hook.

The Vikings will look to get back into the win column against the Falcons. They'd still be behind the Lions due to head-to-head.

The Giants should maintain the lead in their division. They're on the road against the Colts. If not, it'd open the door for the Commanders, who face the Cardinals.

Tampa Bay gets Carolina in primetime. The NFC South race could come down to the very end.
