UIL state cross country: Tompkins' Bailey Pipes wins Class 6A individual championship

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Tompkins freshman Bailey Pipes won the Class 6A girls individual championship Friday at Old Settlers Park in Round Rock, leading a strong showing for Houston-area runners at the UIL state cross country meet.

Pipes crossed the finish line of the 5,000-meter course at 17:20.5, beating out the runner-up, Bridgeland senior Hope Smith, by 14.2 seconds. Smith finished at 17:34.7, and Cypress Ranch freshman Renee Warren placed fifth at 17:47.0, giving the Houston area three of the top five finishers.

The Woodlands earned the area's highest team finish, taking second in the girls standings with 125 points. Flower Mound won the championship with 68 points, and Southlake Carroll (127) was third. Senior Naomi Weller led The Woodlands with a 12th-place at 18:17.2.

Bridgeland finished fourth in both the girls and boys team standings. Smith paced the Bears' girls team, which scored 133 points and finished six behind Southlake Carroll. Tompkins finished eighth with 196 points, College Park was 10th with 218, Katy took 13th with 305 and Tomball Memorial placed 15th with 388.

Atascocita senior Brielle Hernandez finished 15th individually at 18:21.7, while College Park sophomore Isabella Camiro took 19th at 18:26.3. Memorial senior Sophie Peterson placed 25th at 18:32.2.

In the boys race, The Woodlands senior Elton Martin led Houston-area runners with a fourth-place finish at 14:40.8. Martin finished 4.5 seconds behind champion Ruel Newberry of Denton Guyer and four-tenths of a second behind third-place finisher Noah Garcia of El Paso Eastwood. College Park senior Ryan Beken placed 11th in 15:14.2.

Bridgeland led the area's boys teams with 142 points, finishing behind champion El Paso Eastwood (56), Southlake Carroll (68) and Keller (109).

Seniors Patricio Navarro and Dean Rosales led the Bears, placing 18th and 20th at 15:31.0 and 15:31.8, respectively. Atascocita senior Eli Mugambi finished 21st at 15:34.6.

The Woodlands placed seventh with 185 points, followed by College Park in ninth with 233, Atascocita in 11th with 276, Strake Jesuit in 14th with 337 and Conroe in 15th with 363.
