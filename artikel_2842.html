Detroit Lions rule three players out vs. New England Patriots

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Detroit Lions (6-2) released their Thursday injury report ahead of Sunday morning's game against the New England Patriots (5-4) from FC Bayern Munich Stadium in Munich, Germany.

The Lions ruled safety Kerby Joseph, center Cade Mays, and cornerback D.J. Reed out of the contest against the Patriots. That trio isn't traveling to Munich, and are therefore listed as out. However, Joseph was also listed as a full participant for the first time since opening his 21-day practice window on Oct. 28.

Joseph needs to be placed on the active roster by Nov. 18 by 4 p.m. ET or he will remain on the physically unable to perform list for the remainder of the year and his 2026 season will officially be over.

However, Mays and Reed didn't practice on Thursday. Running back Sione Vaki (ankle) also didn't on Thursday.

Receiver Amon-Ra St. Brown (rib) was a limited participant. Safety Brian Branch (hip), quarterback Jared Goff (chest), running back Jacob Saylors (neck / hamstring), and EDGE DJ Wonnum (concussion) were all listed as full participants.

The Patriots ruled out guard / center Ben Brown (knee), cornerback Carlton Davis III (knee), and running back TreVeyon Henderson (illness) from Sunday's game against the Lions.

Receiver A.J. Brown, safeties Kevin Byard III and Brenden Schooler, fullback Reggie Gilliam, tight ends Hunter Henry and Cameron Latu, tackle Morgan Moses, offensive linemen Walter Rouse and Greg Van Roten, running back Rhamondre Stevenson, and cornerback Charles Woods were all limited participants.

Patriots starting quarterback Drake Maye (right shoulder) was listed as a full participant.
