Lions mailbag: Detroit’s defensive adjustments without Hutchinson, with Kerby Joseph

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Detroit Lions defense is going through some serious changes this week, whether they want to or not.

Losing Aidan Hutchinson for the season will create a ripple effect across the team's defensive roster. Not only will that mean new opportunities for new players—see: Ahmed Hassanein—but Detroit will undoubtedly have to adjust how they approach their entire defensive scheme. Will they lean more on blitzing to make up for losses in the pass rush? Will they devote more resources to coverage, hoping that if receivers are blanketed a little longer, it can help the weakened pass rush get home?

Luckily for Detroit, Hutchinson's exit coincides with the return of a key defensive piece: All-Pro safety Kerby Joseph. The Lions activated Joseph this week, and coach Dan Campbell indicated he'll have a pretty significant role in his debut against the Buccaneers on Sunday. With Chuck Clark and Brian Branch playing well right now, could some three-safety looks help Detroit mitigate some of the damage left by Hutchinson's injury?

That's the big topic on this week's Midweek Mailbag.

Note: This week's episode was filmed before Campbell spilled the beans and told the media that Hassanein would make his debut this week. So some of our thoughts on him are outdated.

Other topics of discussion during this week's show are:

How much of the defensive improvement over the last month is sustainable vs. how much was just poor offensive opponents?

Has Juice Scruggs officially Wally Pipp'd Cade Mays? And what should the Lions do with Scruggs this offseason?

Should there be concern about Sam LaPorta's lack of production—and will that impact contract discussions?

Jahmyr Gibbs and Jaxon Smith-Njigba are trending towards record-breaking years: who will win Offensive Player of the Year if they both tear down the record books?
