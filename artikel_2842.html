Brock Bowers returns to practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Las Vegas Raiders began the practice week to prepare for Sunday night's crucial game at the Cleveland Browns with some good news.

Superstar tight end Brock Bowers was present at the start of practice according to media reports. That is a good sign Bowers, barring a setback should be in line to play at the Browns.

Bowers missed Sunday's loss at the Denver Broncos with an ankle injury that kept him to 11 snaps in a home loss to the Seattle Seahawks. He practiced on a limited basis Thursday and Friday last week before being ruled out. Raiders' head coach Klint Kubiak said after the Denver game that the team was hopeful Bowers could play against the Browns.

Wednesday, Kubiak said Bowers was limited in practice and the team remains optimistic he will play at Cleveland.

Meanwhile, defensive tackle Tonka Hemingway (stinger) was back at practice Wednesday after missing the Denver game.

The only two Las Vegas players reportedly not present to start practice Wednesday were defensive tackles Tim Keenan (knee) and Adam Butler (concussion). They were both hurt at Denver. Also, defensive end Patrick Johnson was at practice, which begins his 21-day practice window from being removed from the injured reserve.

This post will be updated after the injury report is released.
