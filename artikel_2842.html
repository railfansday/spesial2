Elic Ayomanor disappoints in Week 11 with three catches for 27 yards

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Tennessee Titans WR Elic Ayomanor caught all three targets for 27 yards Week 11 against the Dallas Cowboys.

Why It Matters: Ayomanor set career highs in catches (five) and yards (79) last week against the Jacksonville Jaguars, which made him the odds-on favorite to take advantage of Wan'Dale Robinson's season-ending injury. In what should have been a great spot for him to become a low-end fantasy starter, he failed to take advantage. As such, he should be considered a WR5 option at best heading into another contest against Jacksonville. (His 10 targets versus the Jaguars also tied a career high - although it should be noted that Carnell Tate was not available). Fantasy managers should be able to do better with all 32 teams back in action.
