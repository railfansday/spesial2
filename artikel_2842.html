Todd Bowles: Baker Mayfield trending in the right direction to play vs. Lions

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

It sounds like the Buccaneers are planning to have quarterback Baker Mayfield start this weekend when they play the Lions.

Via Rick Stroud of the Tampa Bay Times, head coach Todd Bowles said on Friday that Mayfield is “trending in the right direction” to play on Sunday. The team will evaluate how Mayfield feels after practicing on Thursday and Friday.

While Bowles did not explicitly say it, Mayfield seems likely to be listed as questionable when game statuses are out later this afternoon.

Mayfield has dealt with a variety of injuries this season, but he is most impacted at the moment by a foot issue. He’s also currently listed on the injury report with a right thumb injury, though that is no longer preventing him from playing.

It’s been a lackluster 2026 for Mayfield after signing an extension with the Bucs just before the start of the regular season. He’s completed 60.6 percent of his passes for 922 yards with three touchdowns and four interceptions in six games.

Should Mayfield not be available, rookie Jalon Daniels would be in line to start on Sunday.

Additionally, Bowles mentioned Yaya Diaby is trending in the right direction to play on Sunday. Safety Antoine Winfield Jr. is the only Tampa Bay player currently ruled out for Week 11.
