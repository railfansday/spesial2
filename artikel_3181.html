Hollywood Music In Media Awards Winners Include Ludwig Göransson, ‘La Bola Negra,’ Taylor Swift, Diane Warren & Jaafar Jackson

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

La Bola Negra and Ludwig Göransson were among the big winners at the 17th annual Hollywood Music in Media Awards, which were handed out Wednesday night at the Avalon in Hollywood. See the full list below.

La Bola Negra composer Raül Refree was a double winner for Independent Film Score and Song, the latter being “La Nieve,” which he wrote for the Netflix pic with Guitarricadelafuente and Lucas Duran. Göransson — hot off his Best Score Oscar for Sinners, the third of his career — won his eighth HMMA, this one for his score for Christopher Nolan’s global smash The Odyssey.

Elsewhere, Diane Warren won for her song “One Right Here,” performed by Andrea Bocelli and Jennifer Hudson for Maserati: The Brothers. It’s her third HMMA triumph. Taylor Swift took her first Hollywood Music in Media Award, sharing the Animated Film Song prize with co-writer Jack Antonoff for “I Knew It, I Knew You” from Toy Story 5. They megastar performed the track in the film.

Behemoth! won Music-Themed Film, Biopic or Musical, and its director Tony Gilroy was honored for his commitment to the art of film music in Los Angeles. He employed more than 300 local musicians, along with composers, conductors and scoring stages, for the Searchlight Pictures film.

Other winners on the night included Michael star Jaafar Jackson, Project Hail Mary composer Daniel Pemberton, Widow’s Bay David Fleming, Linda Perry, Dario Marianelli, Rock Burwell, Magdalena Sowul, Laura Karpman and Kris Bowers

The HMMAs honor composers, songwriters and music supervisors around the world for their contributions over the previous year in music for film, TV, video games and more.

As for performances at the 2026 HMMAs, Warren played “One Right Here,” Perry did “What Lies With You” from Linda Perry: Let It Die Here, The Isley Brothers and David Kushner played the title track from By Any Means, Colin Meloy of The Decemberists performed “The Better Part of Bravery” from Wildwood, and Ethan & Ari Gold delivered “Brothers Keep Goin’ Anyway” from Brother Verses Brother.

The HMMAs come three days after the 2026 Grammy nominations were revealed, and there is some repetition in the albums up for Best Score Soundtrack for Visual Media, which includes film and TV. Göransson scored Grammy noms for both The Odyssey and Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu, and he’ll go up against Pemberton and Fleming for the Grammy.
