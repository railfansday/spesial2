VisionQuest's Lauren Morais Addresses The MCU's Potential Debut Of Villain Team After Finale

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Lauren Morais, who brought Marvel's Lisa Molinari to life in VisionQuest, breaks down her future with the MCU after the finale of the Disney+ series.

WARNING: This interview contains MAJOR spoilers from VisionQuest season 1, episode 8, "Forever."The Marvel Cinematic Universe continues a key theme through one of its most beloved trilogies.

While WandaVision may have begun as one of the many new shows to debut in the MCU timeline after the launch of Disney+, the Elizabeth Olsen and Paul Bettany-starring adventure became the start of a popular era in the franchise. Not only did it lead to the Agatha All Along spinoff, but also one final project that brought everything full circle.

As viewers got to see VisionQuest, which picked up on the events of the 2021 series, bring the long-time hero back, it also served as a massive payoff for the Maximoff twins, with Tommy's story being properly explored. While his brother was off with Agatha Harkness, the Phase 6 drama showed how his life became a bit different, once his soul was placed in Thomas Shepherd.

I was honored to speak with Lauren Morais, who became one of the latest additions to the MCU franchise, as she plays Marvel's Lisa Molinari, a player from the comics who has gone from villain to hero throughout her time in the lore. Throughout the interview, the Welsh star opened up about bringing Lisa's history to life in live-action, and what may be in store for the character after the finale.

As she landed the role of Lisa, Morais broke down her audition process, which, similar to a lot of other Marvel projects, was kept heavily under wraps. The actress also opened up about the kind of research she did once boarding the show.

Lauren Morais: Well, when I first auditioned, I had no idea who she was. It was just a fake name, fake everything, so I had no idea it was Lisa until my last audition, which [was] my third or fourth audition, so that was fun! Then I read the comics, and I dove into her, and I thought she was such a complicated but such a strong woman, which [is the type of] characters that I love to play. But I really loved the aspect of her being an artist and questioning superheroes, and becoming a villain with the Young Masters, itself. I thought it was a real treat to be able to play this character.

One of the key factors in Lisa's arc in VisionQuest is the deep dynamic she shares with Tommy. However, unlike other TV shows and films where a female character's whole purpose becomes only about serving the male character, the series really explores how Lisa is Tommy's whole world prior to him reconnecting with Vision, while letting her shine outside of their relationship.

When asked if that was something that she enjoyed portraying throughout the season, Morais explained how she cares for Tommy, and how she was able to use her own experience to guide him:

Lauren Morais: "It was so fun to develop that and see that in the script. You meet them both on the bus, and he's about to have a panic attack, and she talks him through it, and she talks through her own survival modes of it, and she got him into the scamming and the pickpocket life, and I thought that was a nice juxtaposition to maybe what we've seen before, but I think all in all, they just really look after each other, and I love that.

Like you said, it's not like Lisa's hanging on to him. It's more like we share a similar past of orphanhood and not belonging, so we're going to stick with each other and look after each other because there's this weird, random connection that instant that you can't really explain. But it's there, and I'm ready to go through this wild journey of finding out that your dad's like a robot. What Lisa thinks, she's still figuring it out, as you can see. But she accepts it because she accepts Tommy in any form he is."

The MCU did a variation of Lisa's Coat of Arms, but with a more grounded approach. While it's not fully Tiboro's Coat from the comics, Morais was excited to see the VisionQuest series honor it in its own way, as she shared how she learned that they were taking this direction with it.

Lauren Morais: "I saw it when I was reading the scripts, it was in one of the stages, like the directions, Lisa has this blazer and has fake arms, and she's pickpocketing. And I thought that was such a fun taste for the audience of Lisa, and also it made sense with her character of being a pickpocket and surviving on the streets.

I thought that was very, very clever, and also as an actor, it was so funny on set, having those arms and that blazer, going to shake people's hands. I gave some people a high five, and some people on the crew hadn't seen the script, so they were very confused when I suddenly had four arms. And I was like, "I'm in Marvel now, I have four arms. This is how it goes, guys!"

As Lisa and Tommy take off with Paladin in the end, Morais was asked if that was perhaps leading to the MCU's Young Masters, or perhaps a variation of the darker team from the comics, as the group is traditionally a group of villains. In terms of what the future has in store for Lisa, she is hoping to see more for her character after the finale:

Lauren Morais: That's up to the Marvel gods, but I think what I would personally love to explore is Lisa's villainy and the Young Masters and her relationship with Tommy going forward. I think that would be so exciting, and taking bits from the comics, and also seeing what she can do with the drone and the forearms and with DUM-E and U. That's what I would love to explore is her and her powers and what she's going to do with them. Is she gonna go for the superhero vibe or the villain vibe? I don't know, but I want to see that.

VisionQuest episode 8 sees the Maximoff brothers finally reunited, as Joe Locke's Billy returned and shared the screen with Ruardih Mollica's Tommy. When asked if there was a version of finale where Lisa and Wiccan got to interact more, Morais responded with the following:

Lauren Morais: I think those are conversations that, when I was thinking about that, is what Tommy and Lisa would have. When they go to the motel, and she's doing the drowning. To me, that was all encompassed in that moment. And as we build up to the finale, those [are] definitely things that Tommy and Lisa have considered. But also, as you can see, I think it was episode four, Tommy says about Vision being his dad, and she just kind of accepts it. She's kind. She trusts Tommy with her whole heart that I think as long as Tommy thinks what he's doing is right, and she'll go with him anywhere. She'll defend him, and if he's in trouble, she knows who to call.

ScreenRant: It seemed like everyone around them was just accusing him of being crazy. But she just kind of accepted it.

Lauren Morais: I think she's gone through so much herself, and she has so much innate trust in him. She's like, 'Yeah, of course, whatever. I'll be by your side because we have each other. We only have each other in this crazy world,' and I don't think she'll [ever] abandon [Tommy] because [they have] gone [a lot.] They've done the [pick-]pocketing, they've been living together in this crazy world without much family - [but] they have each other, I think she's an easygoing girl as well.

While the VisionQuest show is really the end of this trilogy, there could always be more spinoffs, as Bettany's drama was another offshoot. When sharing that the MCU has hopefully not seen the last of Lisa, Morais stressed how she had the best time:

Lauren Morais: "Oh, thank you! I mean, that would be so fun. I've had the best time, the absolute best time, and it's been so cool to see the fan reactions and the theories and people picking up the Easter eggs, oh, it's been a joy."

ScreenRant: "How much have you been paying attention to the theories online? Have you been following any conversations online that you're kind of, 'Oh, some of them are right, some of them are wrong!'"

Lauren Morais: "Definitely, who the Client is. I think that's so fun. There's been so many different theories and theories that I didn't see coming. I've been really enjoying that. Yeah, who the mysterious Client is. I've had friends in my own life ask me, and I was like, "No, you have to watch."
