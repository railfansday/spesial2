Julia Garner's New 8-Part Netflix Series Debuts To Rare Rotten Tomatoes Score

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Julia Garner stars in a new Netflix series that is off to a solid start.

In The Altruists, Garner gives life to Caroline Ellison, the co-CEO of Alameda Research, who, alongside her boyfriend, Anthony Boyle's Sam Bankman-Fried, the CEO of FTX and co-founder of both that company and Alameda Research, violated FTX's terms of service and used customer funds however they saw fit. All eight episodes of the Netflix drama series were released on the streaming service on November 19, with the show chronicling FTX's bankruptcy and what led to both it and Alameda collapsing. The Altruists also stars names like Alex Lawther (Black Mirror), comedian Matt Rife, Hudson Williams (Heated Rivalry), Karan Soni (Deadpool), and more.

In ScreenRant's The Altruists review, writer Ben Gibbons gave the new Netflix series a 7 out of 10 score. According to Gibbons, the show does a phenomenal job with its unreliable narrators and both Boyle and Garner deliver magnetic turns in the lead roles of Sam Bankman-Fried and Caroline Ellison. The review also notes how the show follows a misguided love story at its center. As far as negatives go, the writer comments on how the fact that The Altruists comes with a non-linear story, jumping across different years, and its fast pace can end up making it a challenge for some to follow.

After The Altruists, Garner has two projects on the horizon. Despite the fact that Marvel's Fantastic Four will return in December's Avengers: Doomsday, the actress' Silver Surfer is not expected to appear in the movie. Instead, her next cinematic adventure will be the thriller Tyrant, with her The Altruists' co-star, Hudson Williams, also set to appear in the film. As for the small screen, Garner will be seen in Guilty Creatures, an Apple TV thriller series based on a true-crime book. The Altruists' solid start should build momentum for Garner's next TV show.
