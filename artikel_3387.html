Robert Pattinson’s James Bond Is Officially Breaking a 70+ Year Tradition

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

James Bond has survived everything from exploding volcanoes, laser beams, a man metal teeth trying to bite his head off, and a butler with a lethal hat. He's been around the world on more than one occasion, saved it just as many times, and always found a way to have a drink, all in the name of protecting the country for which he serves, and the monarch at the head of it.

Six different actors have played 007 since Sean Connery first slipped into the tuxedo in 1962, and while the face has changed, certain things have always stayed the same. Now, with Robert Pattinson preparing to take over the role, one of the franchise's longest-running traditions is coming to an end, and it's something that has absolutely nothing to do with the casting.

Upon donning the tuxedo for the first time, R-Patz will become the first 007 in the franchise's history who will serve under His Majesty's Secret Service rather than Her Majesty's. Since Dr. No arrived in theaters in 1962, every actor to play 007 has done so during the reign of Queen Elizabeth II, who ascended to the throne in 1952. Following her death in September 2022, King Charles III became Britain's monarch. Indeed, the link between the monarchy and Bond even went as far as Queen Elizabeth starring in a short film alongside Daniel Craig's Bond as part of the 2012 Olympics.

Buckingham Palace couldn't help but notice, either. In case you weren't aware of just how big a cultural icon Bond is in the UK, the Palace's official Instagram page was one of the first to comment on Pattinson's casting.

“Welcome to His Majesty’s Secret Service, Mr Bond.”

The royal connection is particularly amusing given that one of the franchise's most famous movies, On Her Majesty's Secret Service, takes its title directly from the monarch's reign. Pattinson follows Connery, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton, Pierce Brosnan, and Craig, whose five-film run ended with No Time to Die in 2021. The new movie will be directed by Denis Villeneuve (Dune), with a screenplay by Steven Knight (Peaky Blinders). Amy Pascal (Spider-Man: No Way Home) and David Heyman (Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone) are producing.

“James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime,” said Pattinson in a statement. “I’m deeply grateful to Denis, Amy, David, Tanya, and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me. To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.”
