Sylvester Stallone's Rocky Officially Returns With Exciting New Release Next Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Sylvester Stallone's iconic film Rocky is getting a brand-new release just in time for the gift-giving season.

The underdog classic came out in 1976 and stars Stallone as club fighter Rocky Balboa, who takes on Apollo Creed for the world heavyweight championship. The movie was a box office hit and has been universally praised by critics, earning a Certified Fresh score of 95% on Rotten Tomatoes. The success of Rocky spawned an entire franchise that continues to this day.

To mark the sports drama's 50th anniversary this year, Alliance Home Entertainment and Amazon MGM are releasing Rocky as a two-disc 4K UHD SteelBook on December 15. This new release will include three commentaries with Stallone, his co-stars, and the filmmakers; a three-part making-of documentary; featurettes discussing various elements of the film, including directing, cinematography, and music; and a souvenir mini-poster.

Stallone not only starred in Rocky, but he also wrote the script for the film, which John G. Avildsen directed. His co-stars were Talia Shire, Burt Young, Carl Weathers, Burgess Meredith, Thayer David, Joe Spinell and Tony Burton.

During awards season, Stallone received two Academy Award nominations for Best Actor and Best Supporting Actor. Shire, Meredith and Young were all nominated in the acting categories as well. The film itself won Best Picture, Best Director and Best Film Editing, and was also in the running for Best Original Song and Best Sound.

Three years after Rocky came out, Stallone reprised his role for Rocky II and served as writer and director. Two more sequels were released in the 1980s, with Rocky V following in 1990. Reviews became more critical with each passing sequel, with Rocky IV and Rocky V currently holding 38% and 36%, respectively, on Rotten Tomatoes.

The franchise took a hiatus after Rocky V but was eventually revived 16 years later with the release of Rocky Balboa. Then Ryan Coogler launched the spinoff series Creed, with Michael B. Jordan playing Apollo Creed's son, Donnie Creed. Stallone also returned as Rocky to train Donnie.

The film was a hit, grossing $174 million, and brought the franchise back to its former glory with critics. Stallone once again earned an Oscar nomination, this time for Creed. Two sequels followed in 2018 and 2023.

There are plans to continue the franchise with more Rocky and Creed films and may even branch out into TV. Rocky keeps expanding, further solidifying the original movie's pop culture legacy.

Stallone has worked on many other projects since then, like the Rambo franchise, Staying Alive, the Expendables franchise, and the Guardians of the Galaxy trilogy, but Rocky's staying power is unmatched ahead of its 50th anniversary.
