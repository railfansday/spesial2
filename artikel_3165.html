Upfront capital falls $500 million as NOAA certification approaches.

TMC the metals company Inc. (TMC -6.31%) reported a strategic transition toward a capital-light offshore production model designed to reduce upfront capital requirements by $500 million. Management stated that the company will initially deploy an upgraded version of its proven pilot collection system to target 1 million tonnes of annual production, rather than starting with a full-scale 3 million tonne system. This phased approach is intended to protect the production schedule while reducing engineering and integration complexity. Onshore, the company is pursuing a dual-track strategy by utilizing a tolling arrangement with PAMCO in Japan as a bridge to a permanent U.S. processing facility at the Port of Brownsville, Texas. Management indicated that this approach allows for positive EBITDA potential during the first two years of operations while deferring significant capital expenditures until higher production scales are reached.
