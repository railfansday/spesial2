Hey Barbie! PowerWash Simulator 2's Next DLC Gets Pretty In Pink On Switch 2 Next Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Pink really does go with everything, it seems, as developer FuturLab has announced that PowerWash Simulator 2 will be getting some Dream House DLC next month.

The Barbie Pack DLC launches on Switch 2 on 3rd December for $7.99 / €7.99 / £6.50, and just as you might expect, it looks very, very pink.

This one includes five new Barbie-themed levels in need of a scrubbing (Barbie's plane, campervan, pet park, beach and Dream House), a text-message narrative, and a particularly snazzy pink outfit for your power washing professional.
