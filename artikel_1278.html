Leeds United could face a setback in their bid to sign Melvin Bard permanently, with the Nice loanee reportedly weighing his options amid concerns about a lack of regular first-team football at Elland Road.

The French defender has shown glimpses of his quality since arriving in Yorkshire, but limited opportunities under Daniel Farke could influence his decision when the current campaign ends.

According to a report from 90maat, Bard could turn down the chance to make his temporary spell at Leeds permanent if he cannot secure more playing time for the rest of the season.

The Whites can sign the 25-year-old for around £4.3 million next summer, with his versatility making him an attractive option for Farke’s defensive setup.

Bard can operate at left-back and as a left-sided centre-back, offering valuable depth across multiple positions. His performance against Tottenham Hotspur also demonstrated that he could make a meaningful contribution when given the opportunity.

However, Gabriel Gudmundsson remains the preferred option on the left, leaving the Frenchman with limited opportunities to establish himself in the starting lineup.

The former Lyon defender arrived at Leeds hoping to play an important role, but his involvement has fallen short of expectations.

He has made just three Premier League appearances since joining the club, accumulating approximately 60 minutes of league football.

Gudmundsson’s impressive performances have made it difficult for Bard to break into Farke’s plans, while his brief appearance against Chelsea after impressing against Tottenham offered little encouragement.

Leeds could certainly benefit from retaining an experienced defender for a relatively modest transfer fee. However, Bard will also consider his long-term development when deciding his next move.

If his situation does not improve, the Frenchman could favour a club where he has a better chance of playing regularly rather than committing his future to Leeds.
