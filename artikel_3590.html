Cameron Mathison Details His ‘Relatively New’ Romance After Divorce

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Cameron Mathison is ready to open up about his second chance at love with Taelyr Robinson after his divorce from Vanessa Mathison.

“Taelyr is just such an incredibly kind and sweet and smart and fun, adventurous and supportive and encouraging woman,” Cameron, 57, said about his new girlfriend on the Saturday, November 7, episode of iHeartRadio’s “I Do, Part 2” podcast. “It’s just incredible. We have tons in common, and I never really thought that I would have that much in common again with somebody after being with Vanessa for 20 years.”

While chatting with podcast hosts Daisy Fuentes and Richard Marx, Cameron confirmed he met his new girlfriend on the dating app Raya, and they started dating around April.

Cameron went Instagram official with the model in September when they attended a taping of Dancing With the Stars.

“It’s still relatively new and the Instagram official aspect, I’m usually more private and she’s younger than I am,” Cameron explained. “Being Instagram official is a big deal, I

think more so to potentially younger generations. I’m learning [to] compromise and stuff, but I’m very open and happy to put it out there and share it with people.”

Last month, Cameron celebrated Robinson’s birthday by sharing a carousel of photo memories, including a date night at the Dream Center Foundation’s annual gala.

“Beautiful inside and out, and somehow even more so on the inside,” he wrote via Instagram on October 29. “Happy birthday beauty 🎂✨ The world’s lucky to have you. And I am too:).”

Months before confirming his romance with Robinson, Cameron opened up to Us about what it was like to date again after his 22-year marriage with Vanessa came to an end.

When he first joined the dating apps, the former All My Children and General Hospital star described it as a “whole new world.”

“I met a lot of amazing people, one in particular that became, like, a more committed sort of situation,” he said while attending Jennie Garth and iHeartPodcasts’ “I Choose Me” live summit in April.

While Cameron wasn’t ready to reveal too much at the time, he was proud to say he remained in a cordial space with Vanessa as they coparent their two children.

“I have no place for anger and resentment,” he said. “I try to do whatever I can to be supportive of her, and I really feel that from her, too.”
