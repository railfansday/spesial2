Man Sentenced After Killing Friend, His GF and Leaving Baby to Die in Hot Car

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A Florida man found out his fate after he fatally shot his friend’s girlfriend and left the couple’s 1-year-old daughter to die in a hot car .

Doujon Griffiths was sentenced to life in prison on Monday, November 16, according to the Orlando Sentinel.

The sentencing came weeks after a jury found Griffiths, 26, guilty of two counts of first-degree murder on October 1 in the deaths of Massania Malcom and her daughter, Jordania, as well as attempted first-degree murder and three counts of shooting at, within or into a building.

The deadly incident took place five years before his conviction on September 7, 2021. At the time, Griffiths was with Malcom, 20, her boyfriend and Jordania at the couple’s home in Orlando, Florida, according to an affidavit viewed by Law & Crime.

Griffiths had been living with the couple for about one month because he “was homeless” and the boyfriend “wanted to help him due to them being friends,” according to the affidavit.

The boyfriend said that Griffiths texted him that day “asking him to take him to the bus station” after he and Malcom asked Griffiths to move out of their home because he was not contributing, according to the Orlando Sentinel.

Authorities said that Malcom saw the text about the bus and said, “Let us take him.”

The three family members then got in the boyfriend’s Volkswagen Jetta with Griffiths, and the boyfriend told police that he “heard a gunshot” and he reversed out of the driveway. The boyfriend then turned around to see the defendant “holding a dark colored pistol.” Authorities said that Griffiths “continued shooting,” and the boyfriend got out of the car and ran away as soon as he realized he had been shot.

Griffiths began to chase him, but the boyfriend found bushes to hide behind and he called for help.

Deputies responded to the scene, where they found the boyfriend with “multiple gunshot wounds to his chest.” He told the officers that “a friend shot him and left in a Volkswagen.” He was then taken to the hospital, where he was unable to speak or be interviewed by police due to being in critical condition.

Two days after the shooting, Malcom’s mom contacted the sheriff’s office to report she had not heard from her daughter.

Malcom’s mother was then informed about the boyfriend and went to the hospital, but she did not find her daughter or granddaughter there. She then went to the couple’s apartment and found that nothing seemed out of place.

The mother once again went to police to say it was unusual for her daughter to disappear, and she added that the couple had a man “living with them at the apartment, who is from Jamaica.”

The boyfriend was eventually stable enough to speak to officers, and he admitted he didn’t know what happened to Malcolm or their daughter once he got out of the car.

Authorities eventually found the victims on September 9, 2021, in a car that had been abandoned about one mile away from where the shooting took place.

Investigators said that Malcom had been shot, while Jordania died from being left in the car in “extreme heat” after temperatures surpassed 120 degrees.

The search for Griffiths lasted years, and the sheriff’s office eventually arrested him in March 2024.
