Frida Kahlo Musical In Development With Diane Paulus Directing, Latin Grammy Winner Silvana Estrada Composing Score & Georgina Escobar Writing Book

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: A new, as-yet-untitled stage musical inspired by the life of groundbreaking artist Frida Kahlo is in development, with director Diane Paulus (Jagged Little Pill, Waitress, the upcoming Rhinoceros) leading a creative team that also includes Mexican playwright Georgina Escobar (book) and Latin Grammy Award-winning Mexican singer-songwriter Silvana Estrada (score).

The musical will be produced by American Repertory Theater (A.R.T.), the Tony-winning Cambridge regional theater at Harvard University that has launched such Broadway musical transfers as Pippin (2014), Finding Neverland (2015), Waitress (2016) and Real Women Have Curves (2023), among numerous others. In a statement, Paulus, who is the Terrie and Bradley Bloom Artistic Director of A.R.T., said, “I am deeply inspired by Frida Kahlo’s resilience, passion, and creativity and I am thrilled to be working with Georgina and Silvana to explore the life of this extraordinary artist as a new musical. As we develop this project in the US and Mexico, I am especially grateful to the Kahlo Estate for their partnership in giving us access to primary sources that have inspired our creative process.”

Speaking on behalf of the Kahlo Family, Mara Romeo Kahlo, universal heiress to the Frida Kahlo legacy, said, “Frida has been mythologized since she first entered the public consciousness, and my daughters and I have made it our mission to present the world with the Frida known only to her intimates. That is why we created the Museo Casa Kahlo in my childhood home, and it’s why I am so excited by Diane, Georgina and Silvana’s vision for musicalizing her story.”

Escobar’s book will be a bilingual, impressionistic take on the seminal artist.

Just last week, composer Estrada won the Latin Grammy Award for Best Singer-Songwriter Song for her song “Dime,” from her 2025 album Vendrán Suaves Lluvias (There Will Come Soft Rains).

In addition to the Kahlo estate, the musical is also supported by Corazón Kahlo, the new OS platform that creates “an intimate window into the world of Frida,” and The Fundación Kahlo for Mexican Art and Culture “in furtherance of its mission to celebrate and share Mexican art and culture.”

Paulus’ numerous Broadway credits include 1776, Waitress and Tony winners Jagged Little Pill, Pippin, Hair and The Gershwins’ Porgy and Bess. In May 2027 she will direct A.R.T’s Life on Mars, a world-premiere immersive production featuring the music of David Bowie.

Escobar’s work has been produced and developed Off Broadway at INTAR, The New Group, ART NY & Lincoln Center, downtown at Dixon Place & Clubbed Thumb, Brooklyn at The Brick, among others.

Estrada, who will write the new musical’s lyrics and music, won a 2022 Latin Grammy Award for for Best New Artist for her debut album Marchita. Her songs include the acclaimed “Si Me Matan,” a song addressing gender-based violence, and her second album, Vendrán Suaves Lluvias, was named the No. 3 album on Rolling Stone’s Best Latin Albums of 2025 list. Its lead single, “Como un Pájaro,” has surpassed 30 million streams.
