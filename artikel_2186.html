Middlesbrough should be optimistic about 21-year-old’s future amid bright loan stint for struggling League One side

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Middlesbrough have built a squad that is readily challenging for promotion to the Premier League. Boro currently sit top of the Championship and have responded well to their play-off final defeat last season.

As a result of their big spending in the summer, several loans were sanctioned for players whose game time would be questionable. Out of the three departing for the campaign, Finley Munroe is the player for Kim Hellberg to monitor closely.

Middlesbrough signed the 21-year-old from Aston Villa in January 2026 after impressing on loan at Swindon Town. Now with his move to Blackpool almost three months in, Boro’s prospect is finding some success at Bloomfield Road.

Munroe has made a step-up this season despite being sent out on loan by Middlesbrough. Last campaign, the 21-year-old was playing in League Two and now he is playing in the third tier.

Despite just five starts in the league for Blackpool so far, the left-back has made a good account of himself. The youngster has made eight successful dribbles and won 57 duels. He has just featured at left-back in League One action, and his tenacity is evident with 31 tackles made.

He has been on the bench for the Tangerines’ last three outings, but he has done well to compete with Ilmari Niskanen for a starting XI spot. Munroe’s best display for his loan club came in a 2-2 draw with Bromley back in September.

The 21-year-old grabbed an assist and won 86% of his ground duels. While the Middlesbrough prospect may not have featured as much as they would’ve liked, there has certainly been many positives to take from his stint away from the Riverside.

Middlesbrough currently have two senior left-backs at the club with Neto Borges and Alex Bangura. The former has been Hellberg’s go-to option as the Brazilian has appeared in all 13 league games this season.

His performances have been quality too, with 131 line-breaking passes made and 16 chances created. Defensively, the 30-year-old has been astute too. He has made 50 recoveries and blocked seven shots.

Munroe’s first-team chances are slimmer next season if Borges remains and certainly so if they achieve promotion to the Premier League. That being said, the Brazilian could be a perfect role model for the 21-year-old to learn from, and a position off the bench could be necessary for the youngster.

With the youngster’s ability to play further forward on the left wing too, it does improve his chances of breaking into the first team. However, for his development’s sake, Boro must identify his natural position and work on strengthening him in that specific area.
