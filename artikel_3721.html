Kanye West Poses With His 4 Kids in Rare Family Outing at Yeezy Runway Show

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Kanye West made his latest Yeezy runway show a family affair.

The rapper, 49, was flanked by all four of his children with ex-wife Kim Kardashian — daughters North, 13, and Chicago, 8, and sons Saint, 10, and Psalm, 7 — at his latest fashion show in Los Angeles on Wednesday, November 4.

North, 13, styled her long blue hair, which reached her waist, straight and wore matching blue contact lenses for the occasion. West’s eldest daughter wore an oversized black coat and black boots, while his other three children each wore monochrome-colored outfits.

North also posed for photos with her stepmom, Bianca Censori. The Australian architect, 31, wore a one-piece jumpsuit with a plunging neckline that reached down to her navel.

West was married to Kardashian, 46, from 2014 to 2022. He wed Censori shortly after finalizing their divorce.

Kardashian recently opened up about her coparenting dynamic with the Grammy winner in a new authorized biography, Citizen Kim: The Woman Who Created the Future, written by journalist Jonathan Van Meter.

“Oh, I worry about [Kanye] all day,” Kardashian said in the book. “All the time. That’s the father of my kids, I’ll never not worry about him. And that’s a big burden of having kids with someone who you don’t necessarily get along with. My kids would never know that. I do everything in my power to forget about all the negative things that happened. I have to be realistic, and my therapist definitely tells me, ‘Your boundaries have to go up,’ but we manage to coparent without too much conflict.”

She continued, “It’s been pretty smooth for a while. I guess because we don’t talk that much. But when we see each other, we give each other a hug. I’m really close with his family. I welcome anyone. I tell them all, open-door policy. Anytime you’re in town, come for dinner with us, see the kids as much as you want, take them out, come over and hang out. That’s their family. So I encourage the relationship with their dad so much.”

Kardashian said she mostly communicates with her ex through Censori or his manager.

“I’m always like, ‘Oh, Dad’s traveling,’” she said. “I still make up stories and protect [the kids] because I want them to think their dad is the greatest. But I invite him over all the time. ‘Do you want to come to dinner? I’ll leave the house. Come and have dinner with the kids in their house so that they can feel comfy. Sleep over for a few nights?’ Last Christmas, I said, ‘Sleep over on Christmas Eve. Bring your girlfriend.’ I’m easy! ‘Let’s all do Christmas together.’ And I’ll recommend it again this year. I will sweep everything under the rug, all the horrible things he has said about me, so that the kids can see their father.”
