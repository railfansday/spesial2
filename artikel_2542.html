IPL 2027: Delhi Capitals retention list, released players, trades and remaining purse

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Two-time IPL finalists Delhi Capitals (DC) have announced their list of retained and released players ahead of the IPL 2027 auction, with the franchise making several changes to its squad. Among the biggest departures is Australian fast bowler Mitchell Starc, who informed the franchise of his unavailability for the upcoming season. India all-rounder Nitish Rana, wicketkeeper-batter Abhishek Porel and Sri Lankan batter Pathum Nissanka are also among the notable names released by Delhi.Delhi Capitals, who are still searching for their maiden IPL title, will enter the December 16 auction in Goa with a purse of Rs 39.50 crore and nine slots to fill, including four overseas positions.The franchise have retained a core featuring captain Axar Patel, KL Rahul, David Miller, Tristan Stubbs and Sameer Rizvi. However, whether Axar will continue as captain in IPL 2027 remains unclear.Delhi have also undergone a significant change through a high-profile trade with Lucknow Super Giants (LSG), bringing back former captain Rishabh Pant to their squad. Pant returns to his original franchise at a revised fee of Rs 15 crore, having been acquired by LSG for Rs 27 crore at the 2025 mega auction.In the corresponding player swap, left-arm wrist-spinner Kuldeep Yadav has moved to LSG at his existing fee of Rs 13.50 crore after spending five seasons with Delhi.Starc's departure is another major development for the Capitals. The Australian pacer informed both the franchise and the BCCI that he would not be available for the 2027 season, leading to his release.

Ashutosh Sharma, Auqib Nabi, Axar Patel, David Miller, KL Rahul, Kyle Jamieson, Lungi Ngidi, Madhav Tiwari, Mukesh Kumar, Prithvi Shaw, Rishabh Pant, Sameer Rizvi, Tripurana Vijay, Tristan Stubbs, Vipraj Nigam and Zeeshan Ansari.

Mitchell Starc, Nitish Rana, Abhishek Porel, Pathum Nissanka, Ben Duckett, Karun Nair, Sahil Parakh, Dushmantha Chameera, Rehan Ahmed and Ajay Mandal.
