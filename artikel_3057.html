Nine Energy (NINE) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Cementing growth outpaced rig counts as management pivots toward higher returns.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Nine Energy Service, Inc. (NINE +8.85%) reported third quarter results that highlighted a strategic transition from liquidity preservation to returns-focused operational management. Management reported that while consolidated revenue remained relatively flat, the cementing business delivered double-digit growth and outperformed the U.S. rig count through market share gains and lab-driven slurry designs. The company stated that it is leveraging its improved balance sheet following a restructuring to exit low-return wireline operations in West Texas and North Dakota, refocusing resources on high-temperature gas basins like the Haynesville. Management indicated that it expects fourth quarter profitability to rise despite seasonal trends, supported by higher-margin product mixes and the anticipated return of out-of-service coiled tubing capacity by Dec. 2026.

This added capacity should enable both new product research and development as well as increased manufacturing throughput, including production pressure testing when needed. These capabilities will also enhance the continued development and qualification of new technologies across our Completion Tools portfolio.

The early-stage commercialization of several new products, including the Scorpion 2.0 and Stinger 2.0 for the Permian and Marcellus-Utica basins and the 4.5-inch Scorpion Frac Dart the Bakken Shale and various Canadian markets, provides Nine with opportunities to increase market share across multiple basins. While these products remain in varying stages of commercialization and ongoing field trials, we are encouraged by the early results. We remain focused on internal processes under our control and are making targeted investments to improve the structural profitability of Nine.

During the quarter, we engaged external resources to assess and improve our centralized procurement function that will give us greater visibility into our spend, improve purchasing discipline, and allow us to leverage our scale more effectively across the organization. This is one of several internal initiatives we believe can drive sustainable cost efficiencies and margin expansion over time, while enhancing the operating leverage inherent in our business as activity improves and we seek to scale the business. That same discipline extends to our footprint. We recently exited our wireline operations in West Texas and North Dakota as those locations were not generating acceptable returns.

Before our restructuring, every dollar of revenue was important from a liquidity standpoint, which limited our ability to make decisions like this. Today, we can make operational decisions with a greater focus on returns and long-term value creation. Turning to fourth quarter expectations. Our results will continue to be influenced by the pace of customer activity, the timing of the coiled tubing units returned to service, and the mix of products and services sold. Based on these factors, we currently expect flat revenue and increased profitability compared with the third quarter, and we are projecting fourth quarter revenue in the range of $137 million to $147 million.

The increase in profitability reflects a shift in revenue towards our higher-margin cementing and international tools business. As we move toward 2027, we believe Nine has multiple avenues for growth. These include improving U.S. activity, the return of our full large-diameter coiled tubing capacity, the continued commercialization of our new completion tools products, and the addition of our new testing facility. Our business remains diversified across service lines, basins, commodities, and domestic and international markets. We believe that diversification, together with our technology portfolio and asset-light operating model, provides a strong foundation as we navigate near-term volatility and pursue profitable growth.
