Dogecoin (DOGE) is once again testing a major support zone that has historically led to rallies between 180% and 760%. Meanwhile, a DOGE-focused DeFi ecosystem is about to launch on mainnet, potentially triggering additional utility and demand for the memecoin.

At press time, Dogecoin was trading at $0.05890, having dipped 27.34% in the past month. This price is just above $0.054 – $0.056, which the market has established as a crucial support zone for the memecoin since 2022, and from which DOGE has surged by roughly 3x to 9x from its previous value.

The first instance was between October 13, 2022, and November 1 of the same year. As seen in the chart below, DOGE bounced off the support zone following Elon Musk’s Twitter acquisition and topped off at $0.1588, representing a 184.6% gain.

The second instance was between June 10, 2023, and December 8, 2024. US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) lawsuits against Coinbase and Binance triggered initial price lows. Following the US general election and Musk’s announcement of the Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE), the memecoin skyrocketed by 760.2%, topping off at $0.4843.

It is therefore likely that Dogecoin will once again experience a significant rally if prices remain above the aforementioned support zone.

Meanwhile, sharks have been continuously accumulating since September despite flat DOGE ETF flows and negative price movements.

Another development that could potentially create new organic utility, demand, and a resultant positive sentiment around the memecoin is the development of DogeOS.

Designed by the team behind MyDoge wallet, DogeOS is a Layer-2 network built on top of Dogecoin while running on an Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM). Within it, DOGE is used to make payments for gas fees, lending protocols, prediction markets, and blockchain games.

Following a public testnet launch in September, DogeOS completed version one of its development and security audits a week ago. Developers are now building projects on the public testnet, while the mainnet launch is slated for a later, unspecified time.

Nonetheless, Dogecoin still has several hurdles to jump before the proposed comeback.

The first is the broader market sentiment, which has been extremely fearful due to aggressive sell-offs, ETF outflows, and macroeconomic pressures.

And while DogeOS creates new utility, the network still has to attract a considerable number of users and liquidity. The platform is also under pressure to prove decentralization by replacing permissioned validators with native ZK proofs.

Even more, Dogecoin itself has to rise above an inbuilt inflationary model (5 billion coins annual issuance) through sustained demand.
