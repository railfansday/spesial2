Opinion: Sensational playmaker who plays like Coutinho is coming to join Liverpool's firs-team

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Liverpool have agreed a deal and have secured their new Philippe Coutinho in the process.

The Brazilian was simply a sensation footballer.

His Liverpool exit left a bitter taste there is no denying that, but looking back, it's difficult to begrudge him for that decision.

The money the Reds received from Barcelona also helped fund the arrivals of Virgil van Dijk and Alisson Becker, so it's fair to say Liverpool got a pretty good deal out of it. Barcelona, perhaps, not so much.

Still, with a bit of hindsight, you can really appreciate just how special Coutinho was. The Brazilian was an absolute magician, a natural no.10 who made the game look effortless.

He could glide past defenders, pick a pass that nobody else saw and unlock even the tightest of defences. His vision and technical ability were right up there with the best, and he always seemed to know exactly where his teammates were. When Coutinho was at his best, there weren't many players who could match the kind of creativity and magic he brought to the pitch.
