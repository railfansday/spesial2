RM of BTS Made This Change to His Hair — Now He’s Trending on Social Media

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

RM of BTS just made the subtlest change to his hair, and ARMY thinks it’s absolutely dynamite.

The boy band’s leader shared a carousel of photos on Instagram on Tuesday (Nov. 17), showing off his new look. Keeping the same dark auburn-ish color he had before, RM added fluffy waves to his tresses, possibly through a perm.

“Hello,” he simply wrote in the caption of the post, which included snaps of him on an autumnal walk outdoors and a picture of him wearing his glasses while smiling alongside Korean artist Kim Yun Shin.

The performer kept his comments turned off to the public, but over on X, ARMY immediately started freaking out over his distinguished new look.

“EVERYONE PACK IT UP NAMJOON PERMED HIS HAIR,” one fan wrote excitedly, while another person gushed, “NAMJOON’S HAIR, I’M NOT OKAY.”

“Namjoon looks so cute with his fluffy hair,” a third fan posted.

“MY HONEST REACTION WITH NAMJOON’S NEW HAIRSTYLE,” another person joked alongside a clip of V pretending to give Jin CPR chest compressions.

The volume of reactions to his post made it so his real name, “Namjoon,” was trending on X in the first few hours after he shared it.

RM’s mild makeover came while on break from BTS’ ARIRANG World Tour supporting three-week Billboard 200-topping album ARIRANG. After playing stadiums across the globe since April, the Bangtan Boys paused after their late-October concerts in Brazil.

Now, RM, his new hairdo and bandmates Suga, Jin, Jimin, Jung Kook, j-hope and V are gearing up to head back out, with dates scheduled for Nov. 19, 21 and 22 at Kaohsiung National Stadium in Taiwan. BTS will stay on the road through late December before taking another break, after which the band will resume in 2027 with a series of shows in Indonesia, Australia, Hong Kong and the Philippines.

And — as recently revealed by HYBE — the guys have even more tour dates in the works to extend the trek through the first half of next year.
