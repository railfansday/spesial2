Sources: Rangers in battle with rival clubs over James Penrice deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

His “Deep-Lens” analysis connects the dots between boardroom strategy and pitch-level recruitment, providing a level of transfer clarity that goes beyond surface-level rumours.

Rangers have been joined by a host of European clubs looking to sign James Penrice permanently, sources have told Football Insider.

The 27-year-old's performances since his loan move to Ibrox are believed to have caught the eye of a number of potential suitors, who could be poised to swoop.

Should his permanent move to Ibrox fall through for any reason, there are now clubs across Europe lining up to capitalise, although that is unlikely given Derek McInnes' admiration.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Rangers still hold the cards over Penrice's future thanks to their option to buy.

It has been suggested that AEK Athens are looking to renogtiate the deal as a result of his performances at Ibrox, in an attempt to raise more funds from his permanent exit.

There were, as a result, questions asked about whether a deal would be done with Rangers or whether a door could be opened for their rivals, but the Ibrox club remain favourites.

Despite claims that AEK Athens could look to renegotiate, Football Insider revealed back in September that Rangers had agreed a "bargain" option to buy in Penrice's deal.

The total cost of his signing will land at around £1.2million, with £400,000 having been spent on his loan fee, and an £800,000 option to buy which is likely to be triggered.

That agreement has placed Rangers in a strong position, while sources also suggest Penrice is happy to stay at Ibrox and is not thinking about a move elsewhere at this stage.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Yeah, a permanent move to Rangers is still the most likely outcome despite the speculation otherwise.

"It wouldn't be a surprise that other clubs are sitting up and taking notice of Penrice following his performances for Rangers.

"He's been a standout for Derek McInnes' side since his move from AEK Athens in the summer, and I don't think there's much weight to the speculation about that deal.

"Rangers, they're pretty relaxed on this whole situation. There's a willingness from Penrice as well to secure that permanent move to Rangers.

"So there's a willingness from all parties to get a deal done regardless of whether there is interest from elsewhere at this stage."

Penrice has become a crucial part of McInnes' side since his arrival on loan in the summer, leading to all of this speculation about his long-term future as a Rangers player.

Despite that speculation, though, Football Insider revealed on 15 November that Rangers are planning to trigger Penrice's option to buy sooner rather than later.

The Ibrox club are determined to ensure he remains part of their plans as well as their ambitions for the current season as they go in search of major silverware.

Having made himself a fans' favourite at Ibrox, as well as a favourite of McInnes', Penrice is now increasingly likely to make his stay in Glasgow a permanent one.
