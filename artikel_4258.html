Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Love 'em or hate 'em, gacha RPGs are everywhere - so much so that they dominate our mailboxes on most days since they're just filled to the brim with all sorts of shiny new things (and I do love shiny new things). Whether it's a fancy SSR character joining the lineup or a cool new region to explore, we've got a nifty list of updates here where we go through what's caught our eye so far!  Seven Deadly Sins: Origin I'm kicking off this roundup with the first of three Netmarble entries, starting with The Seven Deadly Sins: Origin! The open-world adventure is adding Estia to the lineup, who's an original hero for the RPG alongside some timely Halloween events.

The exclusive Seven Deadly Sins: Origin character can switch between different weapons to make for a more dynamic moveset, whether that's a Rapier, Wand, or Grimoire. You can put those skills to the test yourself in the Hero Estia Trial Event until November 18th, or have a go at the Mighty Freaky Circus to complete quests and get SSR Magic Crystal Shard and more as a reward.

In any case, this is headlined by the 1.5th Anniversary Re:BIRTH Day Eve Ruby Festa Event, where a whopping 15,000 Rubies will be given away over two weeks. If you're looking for a new narrative to sink your teeth into, the “Revelation of the Forsaken” will run pretty much the entire month until November 26th. Plus, the new hero Judas is joining the fray too - and the best part is that he's available to boost from the Judas Power Up Event!
