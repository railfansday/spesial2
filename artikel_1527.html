Incredible news, Liverpool reportedly in talks right now with world-class star

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Liverpool are reportedly in talks with a magnificent player right now. We're praying a deal gets done.

Liverpool are currently in the midst of their preparations for 2027. It's another massive year for the Reds - one in which Julian Ward truly returns to the fold as sporting director.

We can't imagine he'll do too much different to Richard Hughes, but there will unquestionably be new ways of doing things. Liverpool have different plans now, even if they're minor.

And Ward is forced into major decisions from the get-go. Some high-profile players are out of contract, while other will enter the final year of their deals - he's got to figure out who's staying and who's going.

That's far from easy and Liverpool need to do the bulk of the leg work on that right now. Which, according to reports, is exactly what's happening.

The Daily Mail reports that Liverpool are indeed locked in talks with Alisson Becker over a new contract. Alisson's deal will expire at the end of the season as things stand.

But his form this season has been the perfect reminder of just how good he is. The Brazilian remains one of the world's best goalkeepers and Liverpool will struggle to replace him when he's gone.

Alisson's injury record, however, makes it difficult for the club to go all-in on an expensive new contract. It's a difficult spot and we imagine there will be a compromise at some stage if a deal is to go through.

Maybe that's Alisson taking less money or fewer years. Maybe that's Liverpool going for a shorter contract to limit the risk.

All we know for sure is that Alisson is still playing well enough to justify a contract. It's highly unlikely that the Reds have a better goalkeeper than him within the next three or four years, given the level he's playing at.

If it weren't for the injuries, it would feel like a no-brainer, in truth. And maybe it's just worth taking the risk because of that. In terms of quality, Alisson is undoubtedly the best available option to Liverpool.
