Yum China Q3 revenue misses estimates as comparable sales decline

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Robert Way/iStock Editorial via Getty Images

Yum China Holdings (YUMC) shares were trading about 4.5% lower in the premarket trading on Tuesday after the KFC and Pizza Hut operator reported an unexpected 3% decline in third-quarter same-store sales, missing the Bloomberg consensus estimate for 1.1% growth.
