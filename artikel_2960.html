Cavs’ Kenny Atkinson is honored to coach ‘top-10 of all time’ player James Harden

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Cavs' Kenny Atkinson is honored to coach 'top-10 of all time' player James Harden appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Cleveland Cavs head coach Kenny Atkinson reacted to James Harden scoring 51 points against the Indiana Pacers by reminding reporters of the future Hall of Fame guard's greatness, following a 128-119 NBA Cup win. Donovan Mitchell finished with 20 points, and Cavs' Peyton Watson was cleared to play Friday night. However, it was Harden's incredible scoring performance that led Cleveland to a nine-point victory.

After the game, Atkinson discussed Harden's greatness, calling him a top-10 all-time player during his postgame media availability.

"Guy's special. We've got to appreciate this guy. This guy is top-10 all time," Atkinson said. "I know I appreciate every day coaching him — it's an honor. I really mean that. It doesn't happen to a lot of coaches to get to coach a guy like that."

James Harden led the Cavs with 51 points on 15-of-26 shooting, including 7-for-11 from deep, and a perfect 14-of-14 from the charity stripe. He also finished with six rebounds, six assists, and one steal, as Cavs head coach Kenny Atkinson stood in awe of Harden's season-high scoring performance.

Harden surpassed Hall of Fame guard Kobe Bryant for third-most 50-point games in NBA history (26). Cleveland outscored the Pacers 43-22 in the third quarter, including a dominant 21-4 run after grabbing a 74-73 lead after halftime. Harden's 51 points is the most he's scored in a single game since joining the Cavs ahead of last season's trade deadline. Cleveland earned its third win in four tries.

The Cavs will take the weekend off before hitting the road to face the Chicago Bulls on Sunday.
