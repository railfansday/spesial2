Hermoso becomes most prolific defender in Serie A

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Mario Hermoso looked PSG in the eye, never fearing the team that has won the last two Champions Leagues, and was Roma’s man of the match as they drew 2-2 at the Parc des Princes.

The Spanish center-back, who scored the Giallorossi’s first goal after just five minutes, delivered a performance worthy of a total leader: technical and charismatic.

The goal that made it 0-1 was the epitome of Gasperini’s style: recovering high and running into the French penalty area to fire the ball into the back of the net following an assist from Soulé.

Mario Hermoso, after the match, was partially satisfied with the draw at PSG: “It’s a shame we didn’t take home three points, but one is good.”

Gian Piero Gasperini praised his player: “Today he had three chances to score, he scored a goal: it was extraordinary.”

The Spanish center-back, as reported by La Gazzetta dello Sport, scored his fourth goal of the season yesterday, becoming the Serie A defender who has beaten opposing goalkeepers the most times, equaling his compatriot Ramon of Como and Carlos Augusto of Inter.

Hermoso also equaled the personal records set in a single season in the top five European leagues. The former Atletico Madrid player had scored four goals last year, but in 35 appearances, and the same number in 34 games in 2022-23.
