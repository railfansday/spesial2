Jane Lynch On ‘Glee’ Reboot & If She’d Return: “I Would Love To Do Sue Sylvester For The Rest Of My Life”

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Glee is getting a reboot, and Jane Lynch is open to reprising her role as Sue Sylvester.

In a new interview, the Weakest Link host said she would like to play the McKinley High School coach “for the rest of my life.”

“If I am asked to partake, the answer will be yes,” Lynch said on The Jennifer Hudson Show about reprising her role. “I cannot wait.”

Lynch added, “I would love to do Sue Sylvester for the rest of my life. You know, I wonder, though, how far we can take her now. Because if you look out in the world, there are a lot of Sue Sylvesters out there.”

On October 21, Deadline reported that Disney+ and Hulu ordered a Glee reboot, but made no mention on which actors would reprise their roles. Watch on Deadline

Deadline recently reported that OG Glee stars Heather Morris and Chord Overstreet were in talks to return to the Glee reboot, after previously reporting that Lea Michele was also in talks to reprise her role as Rachel Berry.

Chris Colfer, who played Kurt Hummel, recently teased that he had a “nice conversation” about reprising his role in the revival.

“Honestly, at this point, I’m just curious. I don’t have much information about it,” he said on Daily Beast’s Obsessed podcast. “So, I haven’t had a chance to talk to anybody about it, but I recently had a nice conversation with someone, and I’m gonna have more conversations soon.”

Colfer continued, “But from what I’ve heard, it sounded very funny, and it sounded like a good idea. So, we’ll see.”
