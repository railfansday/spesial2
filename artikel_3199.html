Is Brookfield Corporation Stock a Buy, Sell, or Hold 20% Below Its 52-Week High?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Brookfield's earnings and share price are going in opposite directions.

By Matt DiLallo – Nov 20, 2026 at 11:45AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Brookfield Corporation ›

Brookfield Corporation's (BN -2.81%) stock price and financial results have diverged over the past year. The company's distributable earnings before realizations have risen almost 9% per share. Despite that, its stock price currently sits more than 20% below its 52-week high.

Here's a look at whether the sell-off makes this financial stock a buy, sell, or hold at its current price.

Brookfield Corporation currently trades below $40 a share. That has it selling for less than 16x earnings. For perspective, the S&P 500 currently trades at about 23x earnings.

The company's management team believes its stock is worth $67 a share. They base that on the market value of their publicly traded investments and their estimate of the value of their private holdings, including their wealth solutions business, real estate holdings, and their share of the carried interest in the funds they manage. That's nearly 70% above the current price.

Brookfield's management team believes they can grow their distributable earnings before realizations at a 17% compound annual rate over the next five years. They see multiple growth drivers, including the continued rapid expansion of their asset management and wealth solutions platforms. That drives their view that the stock will be worth $140 a share in five years.

The company's assumptions require it to achieve robust earnings growth and an expansion in the valuation multiple. I think the company could more than double in value by 2035 at even more conservative assumptions. That's why I think the stock is a buy at 20% below its 52-week high.
