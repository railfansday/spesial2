Gadget’s (unofficial) Gear Guide

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Back in April, I wrote out every single item I was taking on the PCT noting “I will definitely follow up with an updated gear list at the end of my hike”. After hiking over 2,500 miles this year on the PCT, here it is.

I was quite happy with my initial list and how my gear held up. If I were to hike the trail again, I’d be happy starting with the same exact setup with only a few minor swaps I made as my hike went on. You don’t need this exact setup to have a successful thru-hike, but I felt like my gear was very, very dialed in for my personal wants and needs. In fact, having such a fine-tuned kit from the start was one of the origins behind my trail name, Gadget!
