De Bruyne tribute, referee slammed: Four things you may have missed from Man City v Napoli

Man City were held to a 2-2 draw by Napoli at the Etihad Stadium in their latest disappointing performance under Enzo Maresca.

City were forced to come from behind twice against Serie A opposition as they slipped to sixth in the Champions League standings.

Former Man United man Rasmus Hojlund put the visitors ahead shortly before the hour mark as he slipped the ball under Gianluigi Donnarumma from close range.

Ayyoub Bouaddi levelled with his first City goal in the 67th minute before Noa Lang put the visitors ahead.

Rayan Cherki's late strike rescued a point for City as several surprise flashpoints occurred throughout.

Football Insider have picked out four moments that City fans might have missed during the Etihad Stadium clash.

Man City icon De Bruyne returned to the Etihad for a second time as a player since leaving the club for Napoli in 2025.

The Belgian's legendary status was reflected before the game, as his chant rang out among home supporters shortly before kick-off.

Man City fans unveiled a tifo to honor Kevin De Bruyne 👑 pic.twitter.com/myMvwM7cca

The City fans also unveiled a tifo to honour 'King Kev' before the game started.

De Bruyne ultimately shone on his latest return to the club before being taken off in the 75th minute.

Napoli were always a threat from set pieces and that looked to be the work of one of their coaches in particular, who constantly stood by the touchline every time they had a dead ball.

The member of away staff was pointed out by City's The Athletic reporter, Sam Lee, who also happened to catch him slipping on his backside before posting the video to X.

Oh my god https://t.co/L5pkUXO9DO pic.twitter.com/BFwD3AB6ik

It was a lighthearted moment during a game full of tense moments in Manchester.

Hojlund has mostly impressed in Naples since the last time he played for United in the 2024-25 season, with 16 goals in 44 games on loan last term and a further four in 16 this season.

His goal will have pleased Red Devils fans, even in their current predicament, and it did look as if he mouthed the words "Manchester" while he held up one finger during the celebration.

The 23-year-old admitted after the game that he had done so to insinuate that his former employers remain the best in their city, saying: "Yeah, it was some good banter, you know. There's the rivalry between Manchester City and Man United, and obviously I have a past at United.

"So that's my club, and nothing against Man City. But yeah, United is my club. It was good to score here."

Referee Kovacs had already angered City fans when he only added two minutes on at the end of the game despite numerous goals and substitutions.

He then blew the whistle around 20 seconds before the 92nd minute passed, according to the stadium clock, which drew strong reactions from players as they surrounded him, and fans, who booed.

The TNT Sports clock was consistent with his timing, however, so it was unclear who was truly in the wrong.
