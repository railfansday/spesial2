Ex-PGMOL chief drops Mohammed Kudus 'red card' verdict after analysing Tottenham vs Ipswich footage

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Mohammed Kudus was unfortunate to be cautioned against Ipswich Town - never mind the calls for a red card.

That is according to former PGMOL chief and ex-FIFA referee, Keith Hackett, who exclusively told Football Insider that the Tottenham star's collision with Leif Davis was simply an accident.

Luca Williams-Barnett stole the show at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium on Saturday, 21 November, scoring the opener before assisting Spurs' second.

Then, 10 minutes from the end, Ipswich fans were adamant that VAR should have given referee Rob Jones a second look at an incident involving Kudus.

The 26-year-old was shown a yellow card after appearing to stamp on Davis' ankle, leaving the full-back in a world of pain on the turf.

Replays showed that there was nothing malicious about the incident, though, rubbishing any calls for the attacker to be given his marching orders.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Hackett had sympathy for Spurs' winger, who was completely innocent despite the coming-together.

"We're seeing a few of these, aren't we? In fairness, he's planting his foot on the ground when he makes contact, rather than actually going horizontal towards the Achilles," he said.

"So on that one... We have seen players, haven't we, this season, some getting red cards and some not. But on this one, I think it's reckless.

"It's in that category of it doesn't fulfil serious foul play. I think that is more of a free-kick than it is a free-kick and yellow card.

"Under the banner of safety refereeing, that's why the referees issued the yellow card, so there's no appeal. The player, to some degree, is unfortunate. It's certainly not a red card."

Supporters have been crying out to see Williams-Barnett given more time, and Roberto De Zerbi finally caved by starting him on the left wing.

The 18-year-old only needed 15 minutes to make a key impact, firing past Christian Walton in between the Ipswich sticks.

His performances so far, it is believed, could force Mikey Moore to seek yet another loan move next summer, despite some impressive games this season.

Fans are desperate for more youngsters to force their way into the first team, and Williams-Barnett already looks to be the next big thing in North London.
