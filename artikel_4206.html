Should You Go After The Woodsman or Tweedle Dee in The Wolf Among Us Remastered: Episode 1?

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

In The Wolf Among Us Remastered, you follow Bigby Wolf as he works his job as sheriff of the Fables, fairytale characters living hidden in plain sight in the city. As with all Telltale games, the choices you make throughout your playthrough will affect the ending you get.

In the opening episode of the game, Episode One: Faith, we meet Bigby, the notorious Big Bad Wolf of fairytale legend. Though he's the sheriff of Fabletown, many of his fellow Fables are still afraid of him thanks to his dark past. While he'd like to gain their trust and maybe even their friendship, thanks to his work, Bigby is dragged into a mysterious murder case.

This walkthrough will contain major spoilers for the first episode of The Wolf Among Us Remastered and may contain spoilers for later episodes.

In the opening chapter, you follow Bigby as he goes to talk to Toad, who's called him in to deal with The Woodsman. The Woodsman is drunk, yelling and throwing things upstairs, and it's upsetting his neighbors. Bigby follows the noise to discover that The Woodsman is violently attacking a woman in his apartment. After a brief struggle that results in Bigby and The Woodsman going through the window and landing atop Toad's car, Bigby chats with the woman briefly, letting The Woodsman disappear into the night to hopefully sleep off his problems.

In Chapter 2, we follow Bigby back to his apartment complex, running briefly into Beauty and Beast on the way up. Beauty is out late, and asks Bigby not to tell Beast he's seen her. Back in his apartment, Bigby chats with Colin the pig before Snow arrives, escorting Bigby to a crime scene: the woman Bigby just saved from The Woodsman has been murdered. Bigby investigates the scene with Snow.

In Chapter 3, we follow Bigby the next morning as he heads over to the Business Office to work with Snow to find out more about the murder. He stumbles into an argument between Snow and her boss, Ichabod Crane. After Crane leaves, Bigby and Snow work together with the flying monkey assistant Bufkin to discover who the victim is, and find out that her name was Faith, the Donkey Skin Woman. They consult the magic mirror to discover Faith's husband, Lawrence, alone in his apartment, and set off to inform him about Faith's death and question him. On their way out of the office, Snow and Bigby get a call from Toad, who tells them in a panic that someone is looking through The Woodsman's things, and begs Bigby to get there quickly.

Chapter Four has the most impactful consequences yet, even if it's the shortest chapter. Here, you choose whether to investigate Toad's Apartment first, or Lawrence's Apartment. This will impact how Chapter Five progresses. For the purposes of this walkthrough, I chose to go to Toad's Apartment first, which is the current most popular pathway.

If you choose to go to Toad's Apartment first, Bigby and Snow see someone rummaging around through the opening where the window was, but when they talk to Toad, he insists it was a mistake and that no one is there. His son TJ is crying, so Snow goes to look at his bug collection with him while Bigby looks around. Bigby eventually discovers that Toad was attacked by one of the Tweedles while they were looking for something belonging to The Woodsman. The Tweedle (Toad is unclear if it was Dee or Dum) beat Toad up and threatened to kill his son if he snitched to Bigby. Toad hands over Faith's donkey skin coat, which contains a letter for Lawrence.

From there, Bigby and Snow head over to Lawrence's Apartment, where they find Lawrence has shot himself after doing something he regrets to Faith, though they're not sure what that is yet. They investigate the apartment, and in the process, find a Tweedle hiding in the closet. Bigby gives chase as the Tweedle runs, following him through the apartments and into a back alley. When he's finally cornered, Bigby learns that this is Tweedle Dee, and that he's been employed to discover something about The Woodsman and Faith, though he won't say by who. Tweedle Dee's brother Tweedle Dum knocks Bigby out, and the Tweedles escape.

After the scuffle with the Tweedles, Snow drops Bigby off at the Trip Trap bar, where he talks to the bartender Holly and her only patron, Grendel, asking after The Woodsman. Though they try at first to deny that he's there at all, eventually The Woodsman appears. When questioned, The Woodsman insists that he didn't kill Faith, and in a panic, begs Bigby to let him go. Still furious with how Bigby and the establishment treat Fables like him, Grendel starts a fight with Bigby. This fight is important for achievement hunters; in order to get all of the Book of Fables entries for Episode One, you'll have to replay the barfight and choose both options — ripping off Grendel's arm, or walking away.

Regardless of how the fight ends, Tweedle Dee walks in as the action dies down, leaving you to choose whether to follow and arrest him or The Woodsman, who takes the opportunity to flee. The suspect you choose here will be the person you interrogate in Episode Two, Chapter Two: Wrath.
