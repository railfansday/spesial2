Everpure: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Enterprises moving AI workloads on-premises are raising the performance bar for storage, the demand shift Everpure is positioned to capture.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 19, 2026 at 2:05PM EST

On its third-quarter fiscal year 2027 earnings call, held on Nov. 18, 2026, Everpure (P +6.01%) raised its full-year revenue growth guidance to 42%. Operating profit grew 76% year over year to $345 million during the quarter. These results demonstrate expanding operating leverage as enterprise data workloads scale. Three takeaways from management highlight how the business is navigating high component costs and capturing market share.

Everpure engineered its hardware designs to lower production costs and passed those savings to customers through average price reductions across most product models in the fourth quarter. Management maintained product gross margin targets at the lower end of its 65% to 70% range to gain market share while high semiconductor prices persist.

Enterprises are increasingly deploying artificial intelligence models on-premises using graphics processing units (GPUs), specialized chips designed for parallel processing. This shift increases performance demands on data infrastructure, positioning Everpure's GPU-connected storage products to win enterprise upgrades.

Elevated component costs encouraged customers to adopt flexible subscription options like Evergreen//One, the company's storage-as-a-service offering. Total contract value (TCV), representing the total dollar value of signed subscription contracts, grew 57% year over year to $188 million, expanding contracted future revenue.

Third-quarter revenue expanded 51% year over year, and operating margins reached a record 24%. High-density flash storage sales to hyperscalers, which are massive cloud service providers, and disciplined expense management continue to drive cash generation. Management is maintaining pricing discipline while tracking component supply costs and subscription adoption.

Proprietary software architecture allows Everpure to consume less power and offer higher reliability than legacy storage rivals. These operational advantages support continued expansion across enterprise and cloud data center environments.
