Why Al Horford returned to Warriors after injury-riddled first season with team

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Why Al Horford returned to Warriors after injury-riddled first season with team originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area

Al Horford had more left in the tank ahead of his 20th NBA season.

Horford surprised some fans by re-signing with the Warriors on a two-year, $13.9 million contract that includes a player option for the 2027-28 NBA season.

After all, Horford seemingly contemplated retiring from the NBA following an injury-riddled 2025-26 season that saw the 40-year-old miss 37 games.

Speaking to NBC Sports Bay Area's Bonta Hill and Dalton Johnson on "Dubs Talk," Horford explained why he ultimately decided to return to Golden State.

"I feel like last year wasn't what we are about as the Warriors," Horford said. "I felt like Steph [Curry] couldn't do what he could do. He had injuries. Coach [Steve Kerr] was dealing with a lot, playing different guys all over the place. I was like, 'I want to give it another shot with the group that we have, and I think if we are healthy at the right time and we're playing the right way, I want to see what that looks like.' So for me, after we lost to [the] Phoenix [Suns], that was what was going through my mind, and I was ready to hit start again, so that's where that commitment comes from, and I want to see it through."

Horford mentioned he had "high expectations" for the Warriors when he originally arrived from the Boston Celtics, but pointed to season-ending injuries to Jimmy Butler and Moses Moody as incidents that changed the team's dynamic.

Butler and Moody's presumed return around the All-Star break leaves the Warriors in what Horford believes will be a "good position" by season's end.

Horford's original decision to leave Boston for Golden State was similarly unexpected, partly because of the Celtics' history with the Warriors during Horford's seven seasons with them.

The five-time All-Star selection was a part of the 2021-22 Celtics team that lost to the Warriors in the NBA Finals in six games before finally winning his first championship in the 2023-24 NBA season against the Dallas Mavericks.

Horford added that before signing with Golden State on his initial two-year, $11.65 million deal last offseason, he did not even look at the Warriors as an "option."

"I was a Celtic through and through, and just committed to it," Horford said. "When I decided that I wasn't going to go back to Boston, and I'm looking at the possibilities, I'm like, 'Really?' I had to have some long conversations with my wife, and we were kind of really talking it through and things like that.

"But I've always respected and been intrigued with the Warrior way and the way that they play here and the things that they do. Playing with Steph, it really puzzled me, and a guy like Draymond [Green] and how he impacts the game. So that got my attention and then looking at how Jimmy came in and had an impact. So I feel like it kind of all came together."

Horford is averaging 3.8 points and 3.6 rebounds in 16.9 minutes per game for the 8-8 Warriors as a backup center, and figures to be an important veteran presence as the season progresses.
