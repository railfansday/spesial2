Carrier: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Price increases of up to 10% are shielding earnings from $550M-$600M in tariff and commodity headwinds.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 29, 2026, Carrier (CARR -4.67%) announced organic sales growth (which excludes acquisitions, divestitures, and currency fluctuations) of 15%. Surging demand for data center cooling systems is reshaping the portfolio. That growth offsets severe tariff and commodity cost headwinds. Three things management said stand out.

Carrier is doubling its Americas production capacity by building a new facility in Fort Worth, Texas. Rapid growth in data center cooling demand and a record backlog (unfulfilled customer orders) of $8.5 billion require immediate factory expansion.

Unexpected input cost increases of $550 million to $600 million from tariffs and commodities squeezed profit margins this year. To protect earnings per share (EPS), management announced price hikes of up to 10% in its Climate Solutions Americas (CSA) segment.

After taking an impairment charge on its Climate Solutions Europe (CSE) acquisition, Carrier sees demand recovering across Germany and greater Europe. European heat pump and air conditioning sales each grew by 35% during the quarter.

Commercial HVAC sales and data center cooling anchor growth, supported by global technician networks and pricing discipline.

Expanding manufacturing capacity positions the company for multiyear execution across core markets.
