Redwire: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Internal research spending of $14.4 million produced the ELSA solar array line, which already drew $27.8 million in contract awards in its first year.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 5, 2026, Redwire (RDW -0.24%) announced third-quarter revenue of $109 million, up 5.4% year over year. Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 revenue guidance range of $450 million to $500 million. Expanding profit margins and record order backlog indicate improving commercial momentum for long-term shareholders. Three things management said stand out.

Gross margin, the share of revenue left after direct production costs, expanded from 16.3% to 32.3% year over year during the third quarter. Management focused on pricing discipline and completing lower-margin legacy space programs to double profit margins on modest top-line revenue growth.

Contracted backlog, representing the estimated dollar value of remaining work on executed customer contracts, grew 14.3% sequentially and nearly 75% year over year. Customer orders across space power systems and defense tech (defense technology) generated a book-to-bill ratio of 1.73, indicating that incoming orders outpaced current sales.

Internal research and development, or IRAD, spending increased 87.1% year over year to $14.4 million in the third quarter. That investment produced the ELSA solar array product line, which captured $27.8 million in total contract awards during its first year on the market.

Inventory of work in process and finished goods rose $10.6 million sequentially to support fourth-quarter shipments. Redwire leverages proprietary space power technology, 40% international revenue diversification, and $538.9 million in liquidity while management tracks federal defense spending.

Capital allocation discipline continues to guide internal investments and contract bidding across global space and defense markets.
