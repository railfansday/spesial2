West Ham 1 – 3 West Bromwich Albion | The morning after

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

This was supposed to be the season in which West Ham were reborn. The slide towards relegation was a couple of years in the making and a year in the Championship felt as if it would allow the club to clear the decks of the m malcontented players and reboot. Fans had spent far too long enduring their club being mired within the bottom three and a chance to start winning again seemed a price worth paying if it got the pride back at London Stadium.

Setting up for home games without a striker worthy of the name, no ability or even a plan to play the ball through the middle and a defence that can’t keep goals out to save their lives (or their jobs) is reminiscent of last season’s ‘siege’ mentality.

Nuno failed to keep the side within the Premier League just as he’s failing to keep them in the reckoning for automatic promotion, with the same, cautious, one dimensional game.

Nuno can’t – or won’t – abandon the cautious, slow ponderous style that is drowning the club and frustrating paying supporters.

Which is not what the 55,000 who signed up were expecting this season.

Record transfer fees for the division suggested that we’d be ‘going for it‘ but the absolute lack of ambition, at home, in the last couple of weeks has been the worst part to witness.

Starting without a plan to attack an opponent is something West Ham fans have seen through, as easily as West Brom and Southampton the week before played through the Iron’s token, disorganised defence. Change the players but the style and result remains the same, it seems. And we know where that buck stops.

Much like every season ticket holder, I’ll bet Jarrod Bowen is wondering what on earth he’s signed up for: It certainly wasn’t this disorganised, toothless, spineless procession of mediocrity and the same excuses trotted out week after week. Spare us.
