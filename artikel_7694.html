Two Individuals Charged In $600 Million Ponzi Scheme

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

United States Attorney for the Southern District of New York, Jamie McDonald, Special Agent in Charge of the New York Office of the United States Department of Homeland Security (“DHS”) Homeland Security Investigations (“HSI”), Pete Gizas, Special Agent in Charge of the Indianapolis Field Office of the Federal Bureau of Investigation (“FBI”), Timothy J. O’Malley, and Acting Special Agent in Charge of the Chicago Field Office of the Internal Revenue Service - Criminal Investigation (“IRS-CI”), Robert J. Kuszynski, announced today the unsealing of a Complaint charging PHILLIP PATRICK WILLIAMS and DEVIN ALLEN JOHNSON with securities fraud and wire fraud in connection with a scheme to defraud thousands of investors in Backward Hat Capital (“Backward Hat”) out of hundreds of millions of dollars.
