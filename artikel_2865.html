Brewers' manager accepts Manager of the Year from hospital

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Milwaukee Brewers finally got over the hump, defeating the Tampa Bay Rays 4-1 to win their first World Series in franchise history. It was a dominant year from start to finish for the Brewers, who finished with the best record in baseball and swept the reigning back-to-back World Series champions, the Los Angeles Dodgers, in the NLCS.

Two weeks after winning the World Series, Brewers manager Pat Murphy made more history, winning NL Manager of the Year for the third consecutive year, the first time anyone has been named Manager of the Year three straight times. Only three other managers in the big leagues, Bobby Cox (2004-05), Kevin Cash (2020-21), and Stephen Vogt (2024-25), have even brought the award home twice in a row.

Murphy's celebrations were paused for hip replacement surgery, but not even that could completely deter him, as he accepted the award from his hospital bed via a video call with MLB Network.

After being asked if he was calling from the hospital because he'd been knocked on his butt from the phone call he got from his hero, Bruce Springsteen, who called him after the World Series win, Murphy conceded that he went under the knife to have his hip replaced.

"I'll tell you, that was pretty special, but no, I had the hip replaced," Murphy responded. "Very grateful."

Murphy, who led the Brewers to a franchise record 103 wins, received 20 of 30 first-place votes from the BBWAA, beating the Philadelphia Phillies' Don Mattingly and the Atlanta Braves' Walt Weiss. Weiss finished second, with seven first-place votes, while Mattingly got one first-place nod. Murphy became the first manager in Brewers history to win the award in 2024.

Since then, he has received 74 of 90 first-place Manager of the Year votes in the NL.
