NFL predictions: Steelers vs. Eagles

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Pittsburgh Steelers take on the Philadelphia Eagles on Sunday afternoon in an interstate battle. The Steelers are coming off a tough loss on SNF at the hands of the Cincinnati Bengals, and the Eagles have their eyes squarely on draft season. With the AFC North up in the air, Pittsburgh can ill afford to let this winnable game slip through their fingers.

Our friends at FanDuel Sportsbook have the Steelers as 4.5-point favorites on the road in Philadelphia. The over/under is set at 37.5, and the Steelers are moneyline favorites at -205.

Here are three prop bets to target on Sunday.

Yes, I get it. Bernard threw up a goose egg on SNF, but hear me out. The Steelers will look to get their passing game back on track altogether, and Philadelphia is a great place to start. Bernard has a total of 57 receiving yards in his last three games after his big game against Tampa Bay. The Eagles allow the ninth-highest EPA per target to slot receivers.

Remember, Bernard will need just one ten-yard catch. You can feel safe with this prop.

Watt was shut out against Cincinnati, but nobody had a good game on Sunday night football. Plus, the Bengals offensive line is much improved. Joe Milton was sacked seven times against the New York Giants. In fact, Philadelphia has allowed 14 sacks over their last three games. Big bounce-back week for the Steelers defensive line, and you can bet Watt will join the party.

Warren hasn't scored since Week 8, and Rico Dowdle has taken more and more snaps from the running back, including last week, in which he out-snapped Warren for the first time this season. The Steelers figure to be playing with a lead and the ability to possess the football makes me believe this streak is snapped on Sunday. The line may say 4.5, but I don't see how the Steelers don't win this one wire-to-wire.
