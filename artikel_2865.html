Packers Week 12 Opponent Preview: Who are this year’s Los Angeles Rams?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Green Bay Packers and Los Angeles Rams head into Wednesday's Week 12 matchup with matching 6-4 records, but they arrive headed in opposite directions. The Rams have won four straight, and their defense has allowed three touchdowns in five games, the fewest in the NFL. Green Bay lost 30-10 at home to Minnesota in their last game. Both teams are still trying to find some identity and could best be described as playoff bubble teams at this stage of the season.

The Packers have a chance to close the gap on Minnesota in the division with the Vikings' loss to San Francisco last Sunday night, whereas the Rams likely won't even catch their division rivals in that regard as the 49ers continue to widen the gap on them.

The Rams rebuilt their pass rush around Myles Garrett and a returning Aaron Donald and rank 2nd in defensive EPA/play. On offense they are missing Puka Nacua (out for the season) and Davante Adams (IR). Micah Parsons is cleared to make his 2026 debut for Green Bay.

Los Angeles went 12-5 and reached the NFC Championship Game, where they lost in a shootout to Seattle. The offense was the league's best by EPA/play (0.11, 1st), with 0.21 EPA per dropback and a 50.7% success rate. The defense ranked 11th in EPA/play allowed (-0.08). Then the Rams spent the offseason going all in on defense.

Edge Jared Verse was traded to Cleveland in the Myles Garrett deal.

OC Mike LaFleur left to become Arizona's head coach.

Assistant head coach Aubrey Pleasant was not retained.

Senior offensive assistant Alex Van Pelt left for Atlanta.

CB Cobie Durant and CB Derion Kendrick signed with Dallas.

OT Rob Havenstein and CB Darious Williams retired.

CB Roger McCreary, LB Troy Reeder, CB Ahkello Witherspoon, T D.J. Humphries and TE Nick Vannett also left.

TE Mark Redman was traded to Green Bay for a 2028 seventh-round pick.

Edge Myles Garrett was acquired from Cleveland for Verse, a 2027 first, a 2028 second and a conditional 2029 third.

CB Trent McDuffie was acquired from Kansas City for picks and signed a 4-year, $124M extension.

DT Aaron Donald came out of retirement on a 1-year, $20M deal.

CB Jaylen Watson signed for 3 years, $51M.

LB Grant Stuard signed for 2 years, $4.45M.

WR Tutu Atwell was reacquired from Miami.

QB Matthew Stafford re-signed for 1 year, $55M.

S Kamren Curl re-signed for 3 years, $36M.

TE Tyler Higbee re-signed for 2 years, $6M.

OC Nathan Scheelhaase was promoted from pass game coordinator.

Assistant head coach Kliff Kingsbury joined the staff.

Special teams coordinator Bubba Ventrone joined the staff.

QB Ty Simpson was a 1st-round pick (No. 13).

Chris Shula remains defensive coordinator, and Sean McVay still calls plays.

Touchdowns allowed, last five games: 3 (fewest in NFL)

W 34-6 at ARI: the line had its best game despite losing guard Steve Avila, center Coleman Shelton and backup Dylan McMahon. McVay used 20 personnel 17 times with a 59% success rate.

Protect Love against the best front he'll see this season. Garrett, Donald, Kobie Turner (back from a groin injury), Byron Young and Braden Fiske give the Rams five real pass rushers. Love throws about 36 times a game, and the Packers can't run the ball, so they have to win with quick throws and help for the tackles.

Keep taking deep shots. Love leads the NFL in 20-plus-yard completions, and Christian Watson (916 yards) and Matthew Golden (853 yards) have combined for over 1,700 yards. A secondary built around McDuffie and Watson will make those throws hard, but the big plays are Green Bay's offense.

Make Stafford beat you without his best receiver. With Nacua and Adams out, Higbee is the Rams' leading receiver with only 304 yards. Stafford has thrown 10 interceptions. Parsons has 3 sacks in two career games against Stafford, and Jonathan Gannon should test a patched-up offensive line.

Don't get out-schemed in the run game. The Rams rank about 6th in EPA per rush and succeed on 49% of plays, 3rd in the league. McVay is now leaning on two-back looks, so stopping the run on early downs keeps Stafford in obvious passing downs.

Win the margins. At 44, this is the lowest total of any Packers preview this season. Against a defense this good, field position, takeaways and red-zone touchdowns instead of field goals will decide it.

Injuries to watch: For Los Angeles, Puka Nacua and TE Terrance Ferguson are out for the season, Davante Adams is on IR, and guards Steve Avila and Coleman Shelton are out. For Green Bay, Micah Parsons is expected to make his season debut.
