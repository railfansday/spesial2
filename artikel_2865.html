WWE Crown Jewel 2026 live results: Updates, grades for today's Roman Reigns vs. Sami Zayn showdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Uncrowned has WWE Crown Jewel 2026 live results, match-by-match updates, highlights, grades and start time for Saturday's champion vs. champion showcase at the ANB Arena in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. In the night's main event, World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns takes on his his old stablemate-turned-rival, Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn, with the men's Crown Jewel Championship on the line.

With the 946 lurking in the background after a Monday night beatdown of Reigns, it's certainly safe to say that, indeed, nobody is safe in this one.

In the women's Crown Jewel championship match, Women's World Champion Liv Morgan faces off against Women's Champion Chelsea Green. Tiffany Stratton has continuously teased — and failed — to cash in her Money in the Bank title shot since she won her briefcase in early October, but the champion vs. champion setup makes for a perfect opportunity for Stratton to potentially capitalize on whoever loses.

Also on the four-match card, The Vision — Bron Breakker and Austin Theory — make their first World Tag Team Championship defense since winning the belts back in July, looking to fend off Penta and Je'Von Evans. Last but not least, Cody Rhodes and Randy Orton go to war in what should surely be a chaotic Last Man Standing match.

Check out the full WWE Crown Jewel 2026 lineup below, which starts at 12 p.m. ET on ESPN Unlimited and Netflix internationally:

WWE Crown Jewel Championship: Roman Reigns vs. Sami Zayn

WWE Women's Crown Jewel Championship: Liv Morgan vs. Chelsea Green

World Tag Team Championship: The Vision (Bron Breakker and Austin Theory) (c.) vs. Penta and Je'Von Evans

Last Man Standing match: Cody Rhodes vs. Randy Orton
