Man United 3-2 Roma: Cunha Inspires Remarkable Champions League Comeback

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Manchester United have spent much of this season searching for evidence that their revival under Michael Carrick possesses genuine substance. On a remarkable Champions League evening at Old Trafford, they found something to believe in.

A dramatic 3-2 victory over Roma ended Man United's nine-match winless run, with Matheus Cunha scoring twice late on to complete a comeback that had seemed improbable during a dreadful opening period.

For Carrick, the result offered both encouragement and considerable cause for concern. His side demonstrated resilience, attacking ambition and renewed confidence after the interval. Yet the defensive vulnerabilities that threatened to undermine their European campaign remain painfully obvious.

Roma required just 11 minutes to establish control. Kobbie Mainoo surrendered possession in midfield, allowing Matias Soule to exploit the space behind Diogo Dalot and finish confidently beyond Senne Lammens.

United's defensive organisation deteriorated further when Soule again found himself unmarked. His subsequent effort struck Ayden Heaven's arm, presenting Donyell Malen with the opportunity to double Roma's advantage from the penalty spot.

Never before had Man United conceded twice so quickly in a European fixture at Old Trafford.

Dalot's positioning was particularly troubling. His repeated ventures forward left Heaven covering considerable ground, while Harry Maguire struggled to organise a defensive unit increasingly vulnerable to Roma's movement.

Bruno Fernandes eventually provided some relief. Having initially seen his penalty saved, the Portuguese midfielder benefited from defensive encroachment and converted the retake.

Even so, the boos accompanying United's departure at half-time reflected the frustration surrounding another disjointed performance.

Carrick's players emerged with greater purpose after the interval, pressing Roma more aggressively and moving possession with considerably greater urgency.

Carlos Baleba threatened following an impressive individual run, although an apparent hamstring injury subsequently forced his withdrawal. Youri Tielemans entered and helped establish greater control through midfield.

Cunha became increasingly influential as United committed more players forward. His equaliser followed a disastrous Roma defensive mistake, rewarding the Brazilian's anticipation and willingness to occupy dangerous positions.

His second demonstrated altogether different qualities.

Driving into the penalty area, Cunha unleashed a powerful finish that completed an extraordinary turnaround and transformed Old Trafford's atmosphere.

There was still time for controversy, with the forward receiving a stoppage-time red card after producing the decisive contribution.

United's victory offered Carrick a potentially significant psychological breakthrough. However, their Champions League ambitions will ultimately depend upon developing the defensive consistency absent throughout the opening half.

Tielemans brought composure, Martinez improved distribution and Lacey offered encouraging attacking invention.

For Man United, this extraordinary result could represent an important turning point. Confidence has been fragile, results disappointing and the atmosphere increasingly restless.

Cunha's brilliance provided the decisive intervention, giving Carrick a victory capable of restoring belief within his dressing room.

Roma will reflect upon a commanding position squandered through defensive errors and an inability to contain United's renewed attacking momentum.

Carrick, meanwhile, must ensure that the intensity and conviction displayed after half-time become consistent features of his team's performances.

Old Trafford has witnessed enough false dawns in recent years. This Champions League comeback offers genuine encouragement, provided United can build upon it.
