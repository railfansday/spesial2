Lyte Fiber Acquires MidSouth Fiber Internet Expanding Its Texas Footprint to 6 Southeastern Counties

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

HOUSTON, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Lyte Fiber, LLC ("Lyte"), a rapidly growing Houston-based fiber internet provider backed by SDC Capital Partners, today announced it has completed its acquisition of MSEC Communications, doing business as MidSouth Fiber Internet ("MidSouth Fiber"), from MidSouth Electric Cooperative ("MidSouth").

"MidSouth spent years building a first-rate fiber network that its members and customers trust, and we know what that trust is worth," said Lyte CEO and founder Carter Old. "MidSouth's rich legacy and deep local roots and Lyte's unmatched commitment to serving Texans make this combination a natural fit. We thank MidSouth Electric Cooperative for choosing Lyte to partner in this new season of serving its members with best-in-class internet connectivity."

Kerry Kelton, general manager and CEO of MidSouth Electric Cooperative, said, "Lyte earned our trust with its commitment to customer service, and we believe our members will benefit from the resources and expertise it brings."

Lyte offers residential and business internet plans for a range of usage needs. Plans run on an XGS-PON network that delivers symmetrical speeds up to 10 Gbps, along with modern Wi-Fi equipment, giving customers enough capacity for everything from browsing and streaming to telehealth and remote work. Residential plans come with a lifetime reliability guarantee and no-nonsense, month-to-month pricing. Lyte's customer service team and network operations center are both based in Houston.

Lyte Fiber is also committed to giving back. The company has pledged a 1 percent giveback each year to local charities and causes in communities throughout its service areas, and this commitment will be extended throughout MidSouth's footprint.

About Lyte Fiber Founded in 2023 in Houston, Texas, Lyte Fiber provides home and business fiber optic internet to dozens of communities across Texas and beyond. Formed in partnership with SDC Capital Partners, a leading digital infrastructure investment firm, Lyte was started by internet industry veterans who wanted to offer a better customer experience, build a good workplace, and connect Texas communities. Lyte also pledges a 1 percent giveback to local charities and causes each year. For more information, visit www.lytefiber.com.

About SDC Capital PartnersSDC Capital Partners, LLC is a global digital infrastructure investment firm with approximately $13.1 billion of assets under management. SDC invests in data centers, fiber networks, wireless infrastructure, and related businesses, and works with management teams to build value using its operating experience. For more information, visit www.sdccapitalpartners.com.

About MidSouth FiberMSEC Communications was established in 2019 as the fiber internet subsidiary of MidSouth Electric Cooperative. The fiber network was originally developed to strengthen the cooperative's electric infrastructure, improve system reliability, and help reduce outage restoration times during major weather events. That investment also created an opportunity to bring reliable, high-speed fiber internet service to homes and businesses throughout the six counties served by MidSouth.

Since its launch, MSEC Communications has built a strong reputation for quality, reliability, and customer service. The strength of the fiber network and the value it provides to the communities we serve have generated significant interest from established internet service providers.
