GTA 6 Players Have Already Discovered an Infinite Money Glitch

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

These get rich quick schemes have the fanbase divided

Many players have a habit of optimizing the fun out of video games, and that tendency has unsurprisingly found its way into Grand Theft Auto 6. Apparently, the game has many ways to “get rich quick,” and these exploitive strategies are going viral online.

In one method, the player drives to the Apollo Aggregates warehouse in South Vice-Dale County, where a drug deal went wrong. The building is full of narcotics, spare bills, and dead bodies, letting you easily pillage close to $8,000 worth of loot. After grabbing the goods, the player stores the contraband in their car, then saves and loads to teleport back to their house. At this point, they bring the contraband to Brian’s place to cash out. A YouTube video outlining the method claims you can make around $4,000 every five minutes, which is more efficient than nearly everything else in the early game.

In another strategy that falls more into the "glitch" category, the player robs the stores near Jason’s house, loses the cops, and then enters any story mission. After the story mission cutscene begins, the player selects Restart Mission in the menu and then pauses and quits. This causes all the stores the player just robbed to instantly reopen with new loot, allowing them to rob them again.

However, despite how efficient these early-game earning strategies are, many players are swearing off the temptation. “Nah I’m good! Didn’t wait 13 years just to glitch my way through the game,” one user wrote on X. “Top tip, if you just play the game the amount of money you can make is unlimited, no special trick needed,” a sardonic YouTube comment reads.

These users point out that earning so much money early undermines the game’s economy, ruins the gradual progression of unlocking better gear, and invalidates many of the game’s cash-earning side missions. On top of this, these methods directly counter the narrative flow of GTA 6, which is about Lucia and Jason attempting to scrape together some semblance of the fabled “American Dream.”

While some are baffled by why anyone would use such mind-numbing strategies, players are obviously entitled to play the game however they want. Still, it seems likely that Rockstar will patch the game soon to remove these methods, especially the second one, which seems to be a bug.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is now available on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X.
