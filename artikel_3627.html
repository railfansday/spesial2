Hila Klein Shares More Details About Husband Ethan Klein's Alleged Affair

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

“H3 Podcast” host Hila Klein is getting into the details of her husband and cohost Ethan Klein’s alleged affair and their ongoing divorce.

“As I’m sure you all know by now, about a month ago, I found out that my husband, Ethan Klein, has been having an affair for the last six months that I know of, possibly longer,” Hila 38, said in a lengthy YouTube video on Wednesday, November 18.

She said, “This is a person that I’ve been in a relationship with for 20 years, since I was 19, and I thought I knew him more than anybody. … When I found out, it was like my whole entire life just shattered in front of me. And it was just like the floor just disappeared. All of a sudden, I realized that this person has been lying to me every single day.”

According to Hila, “Now I just don’t even know him at all. I don’t know who is this person? I’m now divorcing Ethan. I need to figure out what is my life going to look like? How do we coparent when I can’t look at him or talk to him? Where am I going to live? What is going to happen to my career, my job, my employees who I’m responsible for?”

“And while I’m dealing with shock and trauma, I also need to clean up his mess,” Hila said before going into “the timeline of events” that led to her October 23 Instagram post accusing Ethan, 41, of having a months-long affair and announcing their separation.

Hila said she started “getting more and more suspicious of Ethan’s behavior” earlier in 2026, including late-night gaming and “chatting with people and texting late at night.”

At the same time, she said, “In our relationship, I started to feel very alone. It’s like he was there, but he wasn’t there. Especially when I would tell him about my feelings about some of these things that bothered me. I could tell like he would just tell me something like, ‘Oh, I didn’t notice that,’ or ‘I didn’t realize you felt that way,’ or ‘I’m going to try to not do that again,’ but then he would go on and do it again. The same stuff that bothered me, he would keep doing.”

“So, I was really trying to talk about my feelings and share what was bothering me and I just felt like it just wasn’t computing,” she continued. “I was just talking to a wall. I’ve also been very exhausted in our relationship. The entire responsibility of basically everything would fall on me.”

Hila said her suspicions came to a head in October when she asked to see Ethan’s phone. She claimed that he had told her he kissed someone in May. “At that point, I knew my whole life was destroyed ‘cause even though he was saying it was just a kiss, he’s been lying to me the whole time, every day,” she shared. “So, I just knew I knew that wasn’t going to be the end of it. The kiss wasn’t going to be all.”

Hila claimed Ethan eventually came clean to her. “For the first few days, he was sticking to the story that it was just a kiss until eventually I messaged him, ‘Just so you know, I don’t believe it was just a kiss.’ And then he replied, ‘It wasn’t. I’m so sorry.’”

“Eventually, I told him, ‘This is done. We are going to divorce. Whenever you’re ready, just tell me the truth because it’s done. You don’t need to keep lying. Like, this is not happening between us anymore.’ And then he finally said, ‘Yes, it was a full affair. We met regularly for sex.’”

After detailing more of the situation that led to her October 23 statement and the “H3 Podcast” going on hiatus, Hila concluded her video by declaring, “Ethan is the one that ruined everything. He ruined the company, the show, our life, our family, our future. All I did was stood by him through everything.”

“I’ve been his ride or die through so many ups and downs,” she said. “I’ve sacrificed myself for my husband and for my family and for our companies and for our future. … There’s no guide on how to deal with this. I’ve never been publicly cheated on before.”

Hila filed for divorce from Ethan earlier this month. She is asking for joint custody of the couple’s three sons — Theodore, 7, Bruce, 4, and Sunny, 2 — as well as seeking spousal support, per reports.
