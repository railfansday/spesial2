Nobody Wants This' Justine Lupe, Husband Tyson Mason Are Expecting Baby No. 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Nobody Wants This star Justine Lupe revealed she’s pregnant with baby No. 2.

“Grateful for all things cookin and fully baked today ♥️,” Lupe, 37, wrote via Instagram on Thursday, November 26, alongside a Thanksgiving photo of her, husband Tyson Mason and their 2-year-old daughter, Ellis, in which the actress’ baby bump was on full display with a turkey painted on it.

Lupe and Mason, who have been together since at least 2023 and married in February 2025, started their family in August 2024. The Succession alum later recalled filming season 1 of Nobody Wants This while pregnant with Ellis.

“I auditioned knowing I was pregnant and that I was not going to be telling anyone I was pregnant until I was on the set and into the show,” she told Today in December 2024. “The more evolved, feminist version of me knew it was not my problem, and that I’m going to be private about this, and it’s my prerogative. I need to care most about my mental health and the baby.”

She added at the time: “Erin [Foster], the showrunner, was pregnant, and two other women on set were pregnant, so four of us altogether. And I’m so glad I just tried to surrender and not freak out.”

“I love my house, and I love my home, and I love my animals, and my baby, my husband, all of it,” she exclusively told Us in January 2025.

Lupe opened up about Mason’s best qualities, adding, “I think it’s like the day-to-day little things that he does. Like, for instance, he makes me a bath every night and puts out candles and some water with electrolytes and ice. Just little things where he’s just trying to make my day a little nicer. It adds up, and he’s just an amazing partner.”

Nobody Wants This, which has been renewed for season 4, features Lupe as Morgan, the sister of Kristen Bell’s Joanne. In the most recent season, Morgan’s love life took an unexpected turn when she explored her feelings for Joanne’s future brother-in-law, Sasha (Timothy Simons), to disastrous results.

Before the next season premiered on Netflix, Lupe hinted she isn’t giving up hope.

“I have faith in Morgan’s happy ending,” she told Us in December 2025. “I don’t know if it will happen on camera, but I have faith in her as a person who’s figuring her stuff out. I do think she will get her happy ending.”

She concluded: “I feel so trusting in these showrunners. I’m just excited to see what they come up with. I think they are just so talented, and I love each of these characters so much that I’m kind of looking forward to the surprise.”
