Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Lower middle market bets and a collateral sale headline this quarter's update.

Advanced Flower Capital Inc. (AFCG +5.84%) reported an increase in net asset value to $8.55 per share and noted that its non-cannabis lower middle market portfolio now accounts for 32% of total investments. The company stated that it repurchased approximately $1 million in common stock and $19 million in senior unsecured notes to enhance shareholder value and manage the balance sheet ahead of 2027 debt maturities. Management indicated it is focusing on lower middle market opportunities where capital imbalances between large and small lenders create favorable pricing and structural protections.

The Federal Reserve raised rates by 25 basis points in September and held the target range steady at 3.75% to 4% on October 28, keeping debt service costs elevated for many floating rate borrowers. As private credit managers raise larger funds, they increasingly focus on larger transactions. The average direct lending deal was roughly $220 million in the second quarter according to PitchBook LCD. Meanwhile, smaller businesses continue to need capital for acquisitions, growth and refinancing. In our view, the combination of large fund concentration and lenders moving upmarket is creating attractive opportunities to negotiate pricing and lender protections in the lower middle market. We believe the competitive dynamics also differ by market segment. Larger borrowers can often compare private credit financing with broadly syndicated loans, increasing pressure on pricing and structure. By contrast, many transactions we target involve a small group of lenders and are negotiated directly with borrowers and sponsors.
