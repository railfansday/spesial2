Virgin Galactic (SPCE) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Software testing delays push the commercial launch schedule back two months.

Virgin Galactic Holdings, Inc. (SPCE -6.97%) reported the relocation of its lead Delta class spaceship to Spaceport America, shifting company focus from manufacturing to spaceline operations ahead of 2027 commercial service. Management reported that the company is executing a $250 million capital program to strengthen the balance sheet, fund operations through the commercial ramp-up, and retire approximately two-thirds of its outstanding debt. The company stated that strategic investments in a new high-cadence launch hangar and fueling infrastructure will structurally eliminate weather-related delays, increasing the annual flight capacity of the initial two-ship fleet. Positive free cash flow is forecasted to begin in early 2028 as the company moves through its reservation backlog and realizes higher ticket prices from new bookings.

The third factor impacting flight capacity is exposure to overnight weather conditions. We have traditionally conducted our fueling operations outdoors, which exposes our operation to overnight squalls that can also cause flights to be canceled. We have estimated an efficiency loss of approximately 25 flights per year from exposure to these overnight weather conditions. In combination, these 3 factors are expected to reduce our 2-spaceship operational capacity to 125 flights per year, which is the annualized rate we have been projecting for some time.

I am pleased to share our teams have identified a way to structurally eliminate the overnight weather impacts and increase our operational capacity by 20% from 125 to 150 spaceflights per year.
