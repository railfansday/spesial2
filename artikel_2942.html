Another invisible performance from Brian Thomas Jr.

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Jacksonville Jaguars WR Brian Thomas Jr. was not targeted in Week 9 against the Baltimore Ravens.

Why It Matters: On a rainy night when Trevor Lawrence threw 26 passes, none of them went in the direction of Thomas. For the former first-round pick, it marked the third time in four weeks that he's gone without a catch. Over the last six games, he has just 37 receiving yards combined. It's hard to reconcile that this is the same player who posted an 87-1,282-10 line as a rookie in 2024. At this point, the only reason to carry him on your roster is in the hope that he's traded before the deadline to a better situation.
