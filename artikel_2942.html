‘(Victor Wembanyama) Wins Stats and Film,’ ‘Pound for Pound’ – Scottie Barnes’ ‘Best Defender in the World’ Take Divides NBA Fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Scottie Barnes believes he's the best defender in the NBA, even with Victor Wembanyama dominating the conversation. The San Antonio Spurs star was the unanimous 2025-26 NBA Defensive Player of the Year (DPOY) winner, but the Toronto Raptors forward is ready to challenge that status.

Barnes' comments have since sparked a debate among NBA fans.

The 2022 NBA Rookie of the Year made his case in a Sportsnet interview published Friday, pointing to both statistics and film to support his confidence. He is averaging 1.9 steals and 0.9 blocks per game this season.

His claim immediately drew comparisons with Wembanyama. "Wemby wins stats and film lol idk what Barnes saying," one fan responded.

The reigning DPOY is averaging 3.9 bpg and 1.1 spg, giving him a substantial statistical advantage in those categories. Wembanyama has made his presence felt as one of the league's most imposing rim protectors.

Another fan also weighed in on Barnes' defensive standing in Toronto, writing, "Not even the best defender on the Raptors this season."

That comment could invite comparisons with Kawhi Leonard, whom Toronto acquired from the Los Angeles Clippers in September to begin his second stint with the franchise. The two-time Defensive Player of the Year has played just two games for the Raptors so far, making it too early to draw firm conclusions about the team's defensive hierarchy.

Barnes also has competition from other teammates, including rookie Allen Graves and Alijah Martin. However, the fan did not identify whom they considered Toronto's best defender.

Not everyone agreed with the criticism. One supporter offered a simple defense of the 25-year-old, posting, "Pound for pound."

Another fan pointed to his versatility, arguing that his ability to guard multiple positions makes him especially valuable:

"Besides Wemby, actually even despite Wemby, I don't think this is a crazy thing to say I got Scottie over Amen and Ausar because of his positional size and willingness to guard anyone on the court 1-5 truly the most valuable and impressive defensive feat any player could have and Wemby doesn't have nowhere near the foot speed to keep up with guards."

Somebody acknowledged the Spurs star's standing but still gave Barnes credit for his confidence.

"I mean it's obviously Wemby, but he's up there for sure; it's not a crazy thing to say, you love to see confidence."

Others brought Detroit Pistons guard Ausar Thompson into the discussion. "Ausar exists," one fan remarked.

Thompson is averaging 1.7 spg and 0.8 bpg. He also earned All-Defensive First Team honors last season, while Barnes made the Second Team. Thompson finished third in DPOY voting, with Barnes placing fifth.

Barnes has been an important part of Toronto's defensive identity since arriving in the NBA. The Raptors finished fifth in the Eastern Conference last season but have struggled to maintain that level early in the current campaign.

The Raptors' two-way star has a strong résumé to support his latest claim. However, Wembanyama and Thompson have also earned major defensive recognition, giving Barnes plenty of competition as he looks to establish himself as the league's top defender.
