Bill Belichick, Robert Kraft one step closer to Pro Football Hall of Fame induction

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Two key members of the New England Patriots dynasty are a step closer to Pro Football Hall of Fame induction.

Former head coach Bill Belichick and team owner Robert Kraft have been selected by the Hall of Fame's respective coach and contributor subcommittees as candidates for induction in 2027.

They are now among 15 total candidates, who are up for the final voting rounds in February. They will be joined by 13 as-of-yet-unannounced players at a later point.

Belichick, 74, emerged from a group of five coaching nominees, beating out the likes of Tom Coughlin, Mike Holmgren, Dan Reeves and Mike Shanahan.

A six-time Super Bowl champion as New England's head coach, who also earned two rings as defensive coordinator with the New York Giants, he won a combined 69% 0f regular season and playoff games between 2000 and 2023 and earned nine conference championships and 17 division titles. Including a previous head-coaching tenure with the Browns, he ranks second all-time in NFL coaching wins with 333 and first with 31 postseason victories; he also is a member of the NFL's 100th Anniversary Team

Seemingly a lock to make the Hall of Fame based on his unrivaled résumé, Belichick was denied the honor of first-ballot induction last year in what is arguably the most controversial decision in the Hall of Fame's history. His exclusion from the 2026 class drew heavy criticism and set the stage for the implementation of sweeping changes to the selection process.

Kraft, 85, garnered the subcommittee's support over fellow contributor nominees Ralph Hay, Virginia McCaskey, Doug Williams and John Wooten.

His ownership saw the Patriots turn from a relative afterthought into the NFL's most successful franchise. Besides winning six Super Bowls and reaching two others, he also oversaw 20 AFC East titles, 23 playoff berths, and only six losing seasons. The club has sold out every home game since he bought it in 1994, and won a combined 66% of games along the way.

To make it into the Hall of Fame, Belichick and Kraft will need to garner at least 80% of votes from the full selection committee at its next meeting ahead of the Super Bowl. The group will first be reduced from 15 to 10, with between three and seven finalists ultimately receiving the honor.

The expectation is that former Patriots tight end Rob Gronkowski will join them among the 15 candidates.
