Texas women’s sports review-preview: Longhorns basketball begins season on winning note

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Texas Longhorns women's athletics opened November with a volleyball sweep of the rival Texas A&M Aggies, several postseason soccer honors, and a season-opening basketball victory for head coach Vic Schaefer. The next week brings a busy road trip for volleyball, the SEC Soccer Tournament, and the final fall competition dates for several other Longhorn programs.

No. 14 Texas swept No. 17 Texas A&M on Nov. 1 at Gregory Gymnasium, winning 25-18, 25-19, and 25-21. Torrey Stafford led the Longhorns with 13 kills, while Henley Anderson added 10. Texas improved to 12-7 overall and 8-3 in SEC play. Anderson subsequently earned her third SEC Freshman of the Week award.

The Longhorns finished the regular season with a scoreless draw against No. 24 Auburn on Nov. 1. The program ends the year 8-4-5 overall and 3-3-4 in SEC play, good enough to earn the No. 9 seed in the SEC Tournament. Ava McDonald and Amalia Villarreal were named to the All-SEC First Team, Caroline Swann earned All-Freshman Team recognition, and senior Arianna Brothers landed on the SEC Community Service Team.

No. 6 Texas opened its regular season with an 85-65 win over Fairleigh Dickinson on Nov. 4. Madison Booker led the Longhorns with 24 points, seven rebounds, and five assists, while Breya Cunningham recorded 21 points and 13 rebounds. Booker also moved into fourth place on Texas' career scoring list and earned a spot on the Wade Trophy Watch List.

Piper Enge won gold in the 100-meter breaststroke and silver in the 50-meter breaststroke at the U.S. Short Course World Championship Trials. She also qualified for both events at the December world championships. Mena Boardman was named SEC Freshman of the Week, while Alejandra Estudillo earned SEC Diver of the Week honors.

Texas began its fall postseason tournament schedule with the ITA Central Sectional Championships on Nov. 5. Junior Carmen Herea entered as the No. 3 seed and was scheduled to face Oklahoma's Gloriana Nahum in the opening round.
