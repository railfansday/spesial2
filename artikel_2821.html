Ebnoutalib expected to stay at Frankfurt despite rumours of a winter exit

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

It's becoming apparently clear that Younes Ebnoutalib will not be leaving Eintracht Frankfurt during the winter transfer window.

Contrary to other sources, Sky Germany understands that a move for the 23-year-old in January is simply out of the question.

Earlier in the week, Sport BILD reported that a quick departure was possible, with the striker said to be feeling undervalued at Frankfurt.

Whether there will be any further developments regarding this player remains to be seen, but an exit in early 2027 seems off the cards.

Borussia Dortmund is fully informed about all the details of a potential summer deal. A transfer fee of €35million is being discussed.

On Wednesday, BVB were listed as potential suitors for Ebnoutalib, with Bayern Munich, Juventus and Tottenham Hotspur also keen.
