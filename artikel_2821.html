Warriors’ Draymond Green teaching Yaxel Lendeborg ‘tricks’ of winning without Stephen Curry

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The mentality-messed-up Golden State Warriors have relied on Stephen Curry's gravity for more than a decade to make practically everything else work. Now, with their franchise cornerstone sidelined until at least Thanksgiving, Steve Kerr is leaning on Yaxel Lendeborg's unique skill set and Draymond Green's willingness to help develop the Bay Area's next Big Star.

Thrust into an expanded role just weeks into his NBA career, the 11th overall pick has rapidly become a crucial part of the present and the biggest piece to build on after Curry's eventual retirement. The Warriors are leaning into that reality and are encouraged by the rookie's ability to soak up knowledge despite some up-and-down results so far.

"Yaxel has got the size and speed to be a very versatile defender," Kerr told ClutchPoints. "Draymond is doing a great job of mentoring him and teaching him the tricks of the trade and the patterns that he is going to see. In terms of playing off of one another, it's not easy to come into the NBA, see all these actions, see all the different talent and be able to defend all that, but (Lendeborg) is doing a really good job so far. Draymond has really mentored him in that way too."

Kerr will need every old tool in the box plus some new tricks to tread water in the Western Conference. The Warriors (8-8) fell to 10th after dropping every game of a five-game road trip. Thankfully, four of the next five games are at home in the Chase Center, including dates with the lowly Sacramento Kings (1-13), LA Clippers (4-10), and Utah Jazz (3-13).

Green should be able to craft a solid defense against those lottery teams. The 36-year-old future Hall of Famer's value has always extended beyond conventional statistics. A 15-year veteran, his ability to communicate assignments and anticipate opposing offenses helped the Warriors build a championship identity. Those qualities become even more valuable when Kerr can no longer count on Curry to erase mistakes with another barrage of three-pointers.

Lendeborg's offense (averaging 19.7 points, 7.3 rebounds and 3.7 assists in the first 16 games) will have room to shine over the next five games as well. Golden State just needs a few more shots to fall while Curry is out, and they'll be fine. Beyond that, there are many lessons to learn in between the bricks, boards, and blocks for the 24-year-old shooting 45.5% from the field (37.2% 3PA).

Whether the lessons translate into victories, only time will tell. Still, if the Warriors can turn Green's experience and Lendeborg's versatility into a reliable defensive combination now, they may discover something valuable during Curry's absence that will pay long-term dividends well after the best shooter on the planet is done playing.
