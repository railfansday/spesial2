Aaron Rodgers released by Steelers after benching, scathing criticism

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Aaron Rodgers has been released by the Pittsburgh Steelers, according to multiple reports.

Earlier Wednesday, Rodgers went on "The Pat McAfee" show and blasted Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy for "habits" he has displayed dating back to their time together with the Green Bay Packers (2008-18).

The Steelers announced late Tuesday night that Rodgers would be inactive for the team's game Friday, Nov. 27, against the Denver Broncos. Mason Rudolph will start at quarterback.

Rodgers, who will turn 43 next week, was in his second season with the franchise. He won four MVPs and Super Bowl 45, along with McCarthy, with the Packers and spent two seasons with the New York Jets.

"At this point, I'm not going back to Pittsburgh," Rodgers told McAfee. "That ship has definitely sailed."

Rodgers will now be subject to the waiver system and can be claimed anytime until 4 p.m. ET on Thursday, Nov. 26. If he clears waivers, he will be a free agent and can sign with any team.

Rodgers has thrown 16 touchdowns and six interceptions this season. He was 27th in success rate and 29th in expected points added per play, however.

The Steelers currently maintain the seventh and final playoff spot in the AFC postseason race. Drew Allar and Will Howard are the other quarterbacks on Pittsburgh's roster in addition to Rudolph.

Rodgers has said the 2026 season, his 22nd in the league, is his final go.

"I didn't want this to be the way that it ended," Rodgers said. "I was very clear with the ultimatum. And it wasn't a veiled ultimatum. It wasn't posturing."

He also thanked the Steelers' faithful fans for embracing him. Rodgers led the Steelers to the 2025 AFC North title in former head coach Mike Tomlin's final season.

"It was special," Rodgers said. "There's nothing like being on the sidelines listening to 'Renegade' in the fourth quarter."

Rodgers currently ranks fifth on the all-time passing yards list and third on the all-time passing touchdowns list. Only five players have thrown more passes than him in NFL history. He has the league's lowest interception rate (1.4%) ever and no quarterback has taken more sacks than Rodgers.
