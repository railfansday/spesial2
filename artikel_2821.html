What Clippers’ Darius Garland said about making Intuit Dome history with rare start time vs. Hornets

The Los Angeles Clippers are preparing for an unusual challenge when they host the Charlotte Hornets on Sunday. The Clippers' recent victory over Oklahoma City has given them momentum heading into a historic afternoon at Intuit Dome.

Sunday's game tips off at 1:30 p.m. PT, marking the earliest start in the arena's history. The unusual schedule presents another test for Darius Garland and his teammates as they attempt to extend their winning streak.

Garland was asked about preparing for the early matchup following the Clippers' dramatic 113-112 victory over the Thunder. His response emphasized maintaining the same approach regardless of the unusual schedule.

"Same intensity," Garland said. "Same intensity for 48 minutes. Same mindset, same minute. Come in ready to compete."

The Clippers have won consecutive games after defeating New Orleans and Oklahoma City. Their latest victory featured an impressive comeback against the Thunder, capped by Garland's decisive three-point play.

Garland finished with 24 points and seven assists against Oklahoma City. Brook Lopez added 26 points, providing another major contribution from the veteran center.

The Hornets matchup also brings an unfamiliar routine for Los Angeles. Players must adjust their usual preparations to accommodate a considerably earlier tipoff than their typical evening home games.

Garland also acknowledged Charlotte's energy and competitiveness, emphasizing the importance of matching its effort.

"Charlotte's gonna come in ready," Garland said. "They're young, eager, a lot of energy, ready to play. So we gotta come up with the same intensity."

For Garland, the priority remains helping Los Angeles build consistency regardless of the schedule. Sunday's historic start offers the Clippers another opportunity to turn their recent improvement into a longer winning streak.
