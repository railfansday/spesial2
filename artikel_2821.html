Darren Agu Earns Second Mountain West Defensive Player of the Week Honor

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

ALBUQUERQUE, N.M. (KRQE) – The New Mexico Lobos continue to make noise on the defensive side of the football, and Darren Agu is earning national attention for his performance.

For the second time this season, the UNM defensive end has been named the Mountain West Defensive Player of the Week.

Agu's latest honor comes after another productive outing in the Lobos' 21-16 victory over San José State, where he helped lead a defensive effort that limited the Spartans to just 238 total yards.

Agu finished the game with six tackles, including four solo stops. He also recorded three tackles for loss for 20 yards, along with two sacks that pushed San José State back 15 yards.

The defensive end added a quarterback hurry as the Lobos held the Spartans to just 48 rushing yards.

This marks the third time this season a Lobo has received the Mountain West Defensive Player of the Week award. Safety Austin Brawley earned the honor last week following his performance against San José State.

New Mexico enters Friday's matchup ranked No. 22 nationally with a 7-1 overall record and a perfect 4-0 mark in Mountain West play.

The Lobos are riding a five-game winning streak and will look to keep that momentum going when they travel to face Nevada.

The Wolf Pack enters the matchup at 3-5 overall and 2-2 in conference play.

New Mexico will also be looking to overcome its history in Reno. The Lobos are 0-4-1 all-time on the road against Nevada, with their only tie coming in 1942.

Kickoff is scheduled for 7 p.m. Friday, with the game airing on CBS Sports Network.
