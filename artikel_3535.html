How to Watch the 'DWTS' Season 35 Finale After Surprise Episode Change

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Dancing With the Stars is wrapping up season 35 with its upcoming finale — and fans can expect a major scheduling change.

ABC announced during the semifinals that the season finale will air live in every U.S. time zone for the first time in the show’s history. The three-hour special will premiere Tuesday, November 24, at 8 p.m. ET and 5 p.m. PT.

The finale will air on ABC while streaming live on Disney+. It will re-air at 8:00 p.m. PT and stream the next day on Hulu. Viewers across every coast can watch and vote live as the final five couples — Tatyana Ali and Jan Ravnik, Ezra Frech and Daniella Karagach, Amber Glenn and Pasha Pashkov, Maura Higgins and Mark Ballas and Connor Wood and Rylee Arnold — compete for the mirrorball trophy.

Each pair will perform three routines: a judges’ choice dance, the instant dance challenge and a freestyle.

The finale comes after Harry Shum Jr. and Jenna Johnson were sent home in the semifinals despite receiving perfect scores for both of their dances that night.

“Sometimes when people are doing so well in the competition, people don’t necessarily vote,” cohost Julianne Hough told the duo after they were eliminated. “Because they think you’re doing so well with scores.”

Johnson, 32, later addressed the shocking result while speaking to Us on Wednesday, November 18.

“You know what? It’s very bittersweet,” Johnson said. “I think this season — you never know what’s going to happen, I think we’ve seen that from the very beginning. It’s so exciting that it’s so celebrated. Everyone’s getting to watch dance, but you never know what’s going to happen, and so last night, obviously, I think I was just like a little taken back. But I am so incredibly grateful for my relationship and partnership with Harry, and that goes far beyond these just three months of Dancing With the Stars.”

Johnson shared that she would “cherish” her connection with Shum, 44, “forever.”

“That far outweighs any of the heartache I’m feeling right now of not being in the finals and getting to do a freestyle,” she added.

One week before Johnson and Shum’s exit, Jenna Dewan and Val Chmerkovskiy‘s elimination took fans by surprise. (Chmerkovskiy, 40, made headlines earlier in the season when he was benched from the show for controversial comments directed toward judge Carrie Ann Inaba.)

“Every week is a shocker,” Chmerkovskiy told Extra after what became his and Dewan’s final performance. “It’s very competitive. A lot of great couples out there. Wishing everybody the best, and I’m gonna be rooting from home. I thought we had a great season.”

Dancing With the Stars’ season 35 finale airs live coast-to-coast on ABC Tuesday, November 24, at 8 p.m. ET/5 p.m. PT.
