What’s Going on With Alix Earle and Zack Bia? Inside Their 'Fun' Relationship

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Alix Earle and DJ and music producer Zack Bia are just friends despite their flirty Formula 1 outing, Us can exclusively confirm.

“Alix and Zack are really close friends, and they share the same group of friends in L.A.,” a source tells Us. “It’s not going to develop into a deeper thing.”

Earle, 25, and Bia, 30, were spotted together at the Sports Illustrated Circuit Series party on Friday, November 20, during the F1 Grand Prix Las Vegas. DeuxMoi shared footage of Earle and Bia dancing together, and an eyewitness claimed to the gossip site that they were also seen making out at the bash.

“They are just having fun but not exploring it further than that,” the insider tells Us.

Neither Earle nor Bia has further addressed their relationship status. Us has reached out for comment.

Bia was previously linked to Olivia Rodrigo and Charli D’Amelio, while Earle dated MLB player Tyler Wade for three months in 2022 before moving on with NFL wide receiver Braxton Berrios the next year. After the influencer and Berrios, 31, split in late 2025, she briefly dated Tom Brady earlier this year.

Now that Earle is single once more, it’s admittedly been challenging to find The One.

“I mean, my life is always out there,” the Earle Meets World star exclusively told Us in August. “I think when it comes to dating and stuff, there’s moments where things are put out there before I necessarily would want them out there — and not that I’m dating anyone, but you’re standing next to someone and all of a sudden it’s a big story, and I think that’s hard to navigate.”

“That’s been tricky,” she acknowledged. “But, I mean, well, I guess we’ll see what happens.”

While navigating the dating scene, Earle holds no ill will against her exes like Berrios. They were even cordial after running into one another at the 2026 Miami Grand Prix.

“It wasn’t awkward between them at all,” a second source told Us in May. “Alix was genuinely happy to see Braxton and say hello. There is no bad blood between them, and they have both moved on.”

Per the second insider, the reunion brought up “mixed emotions” for Earle.

“Like anyone, of course it brings up mixed emotions for Alix, but she feels like she is in a good place mentally right now,” the source noted at the time. “There are no thoughts on rekindling with Braxton. He was super friendly toward her and not cold in the slightest.”
