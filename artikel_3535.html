Meet the 'Small Town Trouble: Texas' Cast Amid 'Hunting Wives' Comparisons

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Bravo is expanding its slate with Small Town Trouble: Texas — which has already been compared to Netflix’s The Hunting Wives — but who makes up the cast?

The show, which premieres on November 18, is described as an introduction to Boerne’s “most-talked-about social circle,” which includes a “charming yet unconventional group of wives, mothers, and social insiders.”

Small Town Trouble: Texas will offer a glimpse at how “conservative values of small-town life collide with forbidden attraction, messy friendships, and shocking secrets,” according to the official synopsis. “Here, perception is everything, temptation is everywhere, and the truth is never as buttoned-up as it looks.”

While the unscripted series follows housewives in Texas, there appear to be numerous comparisons to The Hunting Wives, which is based on May Cobb‘s book of the same name. The scripted series, which will return for season 2 on November 26, is centered around Sophie (Brittany Snow), who moved from Boston to an East Texas town and found herself drawn to prominent socialite Margo (Malin Åkerman).

Small Town Trouble: Texas has way less murder mystery than The Hunting Wives — (more like none at all) — but there appears to be just as much partying, hookups and messiness. Keep scrolling to meet the cast who will make up Bravo’s Small Town Trouble: Texas:

Her official bio described Aaryn as “polarizing, magnetic and often at the center of the drama.”

“A content creator who was publicly outed as bisexual, she’s built a new life on her own terms with her incredibly supportive husband, Nick, and four daughters whom she homeschools,” the description continued. “Having openly reckoned with her complicated past, Aaryn is focused on personal growth, owning her mistakes, and becoming a better version of herself.”

Before joining the Bravo show, Aaryn competed on Big Brother 15 in 2013. She made headlines after making derogatory statements about fellow housemate Helen Kim‘s Asian heritage and using a homophobic slur toward contestant Andy Herren.

“After breaking away from an ultra-religious upbringing in apartheid-era South Africa, Annari Uys is embracing what she calls the Big Defrost while raising three sons with her husband, Duan,” read a bio for Annari. “Now a free spirit with a growing connection to a close friend, she’s pulled into the group’s emotional and romantic center.”

According to a press release, Hailey is “wealthy, glamorous and full of personality.” She is “just beginning to explore life outside the home she shares with her longtime partner, Josh, and their two kids.”

“Spunky and unapologetically herself, Hailey is ready to have some fun, even as Josh keeps a close eye on her social life,” the description continued. “As one of the few in the group navigating a longtime partnership without a marriage certificate, Hailey brings her own perspective on love, family, and relationships.”

Alli is a “third-generation local with rodeo grit, platinum small-town status and firmly traditional views in her own marriage with Blake.” While “in a state of self-discovery,” Alli stepped away from her career to spend more time with her three kids.

“Alli knows everyone, hears everything, and stays unfazed when the group drama comes her way as the self-appointed Queen of Boerne,” her bio read.

Raised as a preacher’s daughter, Gabby “is unpacking a repressed evangelical past while boldly exploring who and what she wants.”

“After meeting her now-husband on a sugar-dating site, what began as an unconventional arrangement ultimately turned into a marriage,” her bio read. “Today, Gabby and her husband are raising a beautiful little girl and continue to play by their own rules.”
