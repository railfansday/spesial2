Neutrons, death metal and a tiny prehistoric dragon: Scans reveal new horned reptile species from 156 million years ago

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

An unassuming fossil discovery in Wyoming caused quite a stir: Was it a baby dinosaur, a prehistoric snake or perhaps an entirely new species? Neutron imaging at the Paul Scherrer Institute PSI and the trained eye of a paleontologist eventually provided the answer: the 156-million-year-old fossil is that of a previously unknown species of reptile.
