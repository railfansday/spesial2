Prediction: Olympiacos vs AC Milan

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

In what could prove to be an action-packed Europa League showdown on Thursday evening, Olympiacos will welcome AC Milan to Greece.

With a stacked midweek schedule of European action, there are plenty of attractive Europa League betting promotions available.

Ahead of Thursday’s battle from Piraeus, you can find all of the key Olympiacos vs AC Milan H2H stats here.

Starting with the hosts, while Olympiacos might have a real tussle of their hands in the race to reclaim their domestic crown this season, the Greek icons have continued to shine on either side of November’s international break. Marking their return from the fortnight interval with a dramatic 6-2 victory at home against Iraklis 1908, Imanol Alguacil’s side have their sights set on mounting a genuine Europa League charge this year. Getting their European adventure underway with a 2-1 win against Polish outfit Jagiellonia on September 16th, Olympiacos are unbeaten in all of their previous 12 consecutive appearances across all competitions. Sat in the driving seat to punch a lucrative top-eight finish, Thursday’s hosts will also be excited by their extended dominance in Piraeus. In fact, Alguacil’s men have suffered just a single competitive defeat in front of their own supporters since the start of April.

Yet to feature since suffering a broken leg back in September, Olympiacos will once again be without attacking talisman Ayoub El Kaabi on Thursday evening. Likewise, former Lille midfielder Yusuf Yazici remains on the sidelines with a long-term knee issue.

Not included in Olympiacos’ Europa League squad this season, summer recruit and ex-Girona starlet Joel Roca will not feature here. Bagging a domestic brace against Iraklis 1908 over the weekend, former Aston Villa star Leon Bailey will be hoping to keep his spot from the start.

As for the visitors, while there might have been plenty of raised eyebrows over the continent following Ruben Amorim’s arrival at the San Siro, AC Milan have enjoyed a sensational domestic romp under the former Manchester United boss. Marking their return from November’s fortnight interval with a routine 5-1 romp at home against Frosinone on Sunday, the Italian icons have won five of their previous six Serie A appearances. However, despite enjoying a real domestic flurry, Amorim’s camp have drastically failed to find their feet on the European stage. Including a nightmare 3-0 drumming against Bournemouth on October 22nd, AC Milan have lost three of their first four Europa League showdowns and the find themselves sat outside the knockout stage spots. Looking to kickstart their European charge in midweek, the Rossoneri have suffered just a sole competitive defeat on their travels so far this season.

All still dealing with respective injury issues, AC Milan will travel to Greece on Thursday evening without the likes of Alexis Saelemaekers, Matteo Gabbia and former Chelsea midfielder Ruben Loftus-Cheek. However, missing out against Ferencvaros earlier in the month after serving a one-match European suspension, French international Mike Maignan will return between the sticks.

Playing the full 90 minutes against Frosinone over the weekend, 41-year-old Luka Modric could be handed a deserved rest in Greece. Completing a season-long loan switch from Nottingham Forest over the summer, Omari Hutchinson will feature in the final third.

While AC Milan might be enjoying a major domestic flurry, the Italian icons are desperate to kickstart their Europa League charge here. Remarkably losing three of their first four group stage battles, Thursday’s guests look set for what could be another tricky test on foreign soil. Watching Olympiacos mark their return from November’s international break with a thrilling 6-2 victory against Iraklis 1908, neutrals will be hoping for another lively battle from Piraeus in midweek. It should also be highlighted, Alguacil’s side have seen their last three matchups at the Georgios Karaiskakis Stadium produce 13 goals at both ends of the pitch.
