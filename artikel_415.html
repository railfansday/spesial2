France Blames Iran for Cyberattack on Refrigeration Systems

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The French foreign minister said he had summoned the Iranian ambassador to France after a cyberattack on hundreds of supermarkets last month. Iran did not immediately respond.

Ségolène Le Stradic reported from Paris, and Dustin Volz from Washington.

French officials on Monday accused Iran of orchestrating a cyberattack that hit hundreds of refrigeration systems in France last month.

Paris’s chief prosecutor, Laure Beccuau, said in a statement that her office had issued international arrest warrants for three Iranians “affiliated with the Electronic and Cyber ​​Center of the Islamic Revolutionary Guards Corps.” She said their attacks had affected 700 supermarkets across France.

The French foreign minister, Jean-Noël Barrot, also blamed the Revolutionary Guards for the attack and said at a press briefing that the Iranian ambassador to France had been summoned to “strongly protest against this extremely serious act” that would “not go without repercussions.”

“This is the first large-scale cyberattack aimed at sabotage on national territory,” Mr. Barrot added, “and it constitutes a major assault on the nation’s interests and the security of the French people.” His ministry also issued a statement pledging to impose new sanctions on Iranian institutions and individuals.

The Iranian government did not immediately respond, and a spokesman for the Iranian mission to the United Nations did not immediately answer a request for comment.

Ms. Beccuau said that the attack had led to “significant food spoilage” and “led to roughly 20 fires.”
