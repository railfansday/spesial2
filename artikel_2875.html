New Zealand vs. India prediction, team news, pitch report and betting tips for 3rd ODI

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Somewhere above Hamilton, the clouds are gathering, and New Zealand would quite like to hide behind them. They have won 11 home ODI series in a row, a run that has felt like a fortress, yet India, the side who last breached it in 2019, are back at the gates with a 2-0 lead and an attack that keeps rattling their top order.

Wellington was a scrap, the hosts fighting hard before India squeezed home by three wickets on DLS. Mitchell Santner has won only one toss in seven on this tour, and India's call of tails has landed every single time.

Now come rain, possible hail and a seaming surface at Seddon Park, plus Shubman Gill's last game in charge before Shreyas Iyer takes over.

Indian bowlers have found a rhythm, with Auqib Nabi and Arshdeep Singh hunting in tandem and Anshul Kamboj and Gurnoor Brar offering sturdy support, which suits any pitch with a bit of life.

New Zealand's top order, meanwhile, is full of batters desperate for a score to silence the doubters, and desperation rarely pairs well with a new ball.

Daryl Mitchell and Glenn Phillips can drag them to something respectable, but they need help. Should India bat second, Rohit Sharma and Virat Kohli have the experience to settle any early wobble.
