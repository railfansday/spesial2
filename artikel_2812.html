Predictions for the 2026 CIAC high school girls soccer tournaments

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The CIAC girls soccer tournaments begin on Friday and concluded Nov. 21-22 at Trinity Health Stadium in Hartford.

GametimeCT makes its selections on which teams will be advancing to the championship games in two weeks. We also select some seeded upsets that could happen. You can see our preseason and midseason picks on these same brackets. For the latest CIAC girls soccer scores and schedules, follow along on our scoreboard.

2025 champion: Greenwich defeated Amity2026 preseason championship prediction: Amity over St. Joseph2026 midseason championship prediction: Amity over St. Joseph

No. 18 Fairfield Warde over No. 15 Fairfield Ludlowe.Final Four: No. 1 St. Joseph, No. 4 Greenwich, No. 7 Amity, No. 2 Glastonbury.Championship prediction: St. Joseph over Glastonbury.

Going to the power points system where strength of schedule is factored in has made the seedings much better than just based off of a team's winning percentage. It also makes it harder to select seeded upsets. A lot of the top seeds have balanced offenses and great defenses, so I expect to see several games go to penalty kicks. Amity has what it takes get back to the LL final, but beating Ridgefield and Glastonbury, which handed Amity its only loss, back to back is a very tall order. Beating St. Joseph will be a tall order as well, seeing how the Cadets played all season, allowing just two goals. St. Joseph vs. Glastonbury has the makings of a classic.

2025 champion: Masuk defeated North Haven2026 preseason championship prediction: Wethersfield over East Catholic2026 midseason championship prediction: Simsbury over Wethersfield.

Potential seeded upsets: No. 18 Killingly over No. 15 Bethel.

No. 1 Simsbury, No. 4 North Haven, No. 7 Masuk and No. 3 Wethersfield.Championship prediction: Simsbury over Wethersfield.

Here's why: Simsbury's undefeated season got derailed in the CCC tournament semifinals by Farmington. That might take some of the pressure off the Trojans here. Wethersfield got hot in the second half of the regular season and lost to undefeated Glastonbury in the CCC semifinals. Last year's finalists, North Haven and defending champion Masuk, are more than capable of making deep runs here, but it will be an All-CCC championship game.

2025 champion: Foran defeated Northwest Catholic.2026 preseason championship prediction: Foran defeats East Hampton.2026 midseason championship prediction: Lauralton Hall defeats East Hampton .

Potential seeded upsets: No. 10 Foran over No. 7 RHAM in the second round.Final Four: No. 1 East Hampton, No. 5 New Fairfield, No. 10 Foran, No. 3 Lauralton HallChampionship prediction: Lauralton Hall defeats East Hampton.

Lauralton Hall has been up and down this season and didn't qualify for the SCC tournament. But the Crusaders should benefit by playing in that league and put together a nice run here. Foran is the defending champion. East Hampton played a solid non-conference schedule in addition to dominating the Shoreline Conference. The Bellringers will reach its first state final and can win it all. But it will be a Milford program for the second-straight season taking the title.

2025 champion: Morgan defeated Old Saybrook.2026 preseason championship prediction: Lewis Mills over Valley Regional.2026 midseason championship prediction: Lewis Mills over Old Saybrook.

Potential seeded upsets: No. 18 Montville over No. 15 Oxford.Final Four: No. 1 Stonington, No. 5 Coginchaug, No. 2 Lewis Mills, No. 3 Old Saybrook.

Here's why: Lewis Mills has been the pick here all along. Playing in the huge CCC conference is fine preparation. Lewis Mills has long had success in Class M, including a state championship in 2024. Now the Spartans are in Class S. Stonington is quite good, clearly the best team in the ECC. The Bears' only loss came to undefeated East Hampton, the top seed in Class M. Regulation won't be long enough to decide this game.
