Antonio Brown critical of Steelers WR DK Metcalf amidst struggles

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

It's no secret at this point that DK Metcalf has been a massive disappointment ever since he was traded to the Pittsburgh Steelers. His second season in Pittsburgh has been just as disastrous as the first, as he is second in the NFL in drops with 6, while leading all receivers in penalties.

Metcalf leads the Steelers in yards with 432, but has hauled in just 35 of his 71 targets through nine games this season. The receiver admitted to the media that this was the first time he's dealt with these kinds of struggles in his career, but a former Steeler had advice for Metcalf.

Appearing on "The Matchup" podcast with former Steelers teammate Emmanuel Sanders and Chris Harris Jr., former Steelers standout wide receiver Antonio Brown told Metcalf he simply needs to come back to the ball more.

"I see DK Metcalf trying to box a guy out," Brown said. "Yo bro, you gotta go back to the ball. Take your nose to the ball. I feel like guys gotta be more confident taking their nose to the ball. You gotta take your nose to the ball first, and your eyes gotta catch it. It's eyes, nose, hands. Most of these guys, they just use hands, no eyes. You gotta have the concentration. You gotta be focused to be a good pass catcher."

This sounds like shockingly decent advice coming from Brown, who has gained a reputation for being an internet troll at best over the course of the last few seasons. The concentration quote makes a lot of sense given the sheer number of drops Metcalf has had this season.

Simply put, something has to give with Metcalf. Perhaps with the return of Michael Pittman Jr. and Roman Wilson to the Steelers' offense, things will look smoother for Metcalf and the offense as a whole. Expect Aaron Rodgers to continue to rely on Metcalf down the stretch as the Steelers sit firmly in the playoff picture.
