Bournemouth insists on keeping Arsenal target

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Arsenal remain interested in Bournemouth striker Eli Junior Kroupi and are reportedly considering making an approach for him during the January transfer window. The Gunners see the Frenchman as one of the most exciting young attacking talents in the Premier League and believe he could strengthen their forward options.

Kroupi has impressed with his performances, attracting attention from several clubs looking to secure his signature. Arsenal reportedly rate him highly and believe he could offer more in the striker role than Viktor Gyokeres, whose position at the Emirates has come under increasing scrutiny.

Gyokeres is fighting to establish himself as Arsenal's leading forward, but the emergence of Kroupi as a potential transfer target could create further competition for his place. The Gunners view the Bournemouth attacker as a player with considerable potential who could become an important part of their team.

Several clubs are reportedly interested in signing the Frenchman, with Arsenal among those considering a move. However, Bournemouth are determined to retain their young attacker and insist that he will not be allowed to leave during the January transfer window.

That represents a significant obstacle for the Gunners, who would have to reconsider their plans if the Cherries refuse to negotiate. Arsenal may see Kroupi as a long-term investment, but their interest does not guarantee that they can secure his services.

There is also uncertainty about the player's preferred destination, with other major European clubs reportedly monitoring his situation.

According to Team Talk, Kroupi has chosen Paris Saint-Germain ahead of Arsenal among the clubs interested in signing him. The report adds to the difficulties facing the Gunners as they consider whether to pursue the striker.

Kroupi is reportedly interested in leaving the Premier League, and Paris could be his preferred destination. Barcelona is also mentioned as an alternative, with the Frenchman potentially favouring a move to the Spanish club rather than joining Arsenal.

That would be disappointing for the Gunners, who regard him as a player capable of strengthening their attack for years to come. If the reports about his preferences are accurate, Arsenal could struggle to convince him to move to the Emirates, even if they manage to reach an agreement with Bournemouth.
