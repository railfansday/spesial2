Jayden Daniels Overcomes Adversity to Lead Commanders Over Giants

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

With two minutes left at the Meadowlands, Jayden Daniels walked off the field after throwing his second straight fourth-quarter interception— a mistake that would have buried Washington a week ago. Instead, it set the stage for the exact trait the Commanders have lacked all season: grit.

Washington escaped New York with a 24-21 win in a nip-and-tuck divisional battle that kept viewers on the edge of their seats. Daniels shook off his worst stretch of the year to find Stefon Diggs for a late go-ahead touchdown, capping a come-from-behind victory that keeps the Commanders alive in the NFC East. He finished the night with 249 passing yards and three touchdowns — his fourth multi-touchdown performance of the year and a new season high in scoring passes.

Things looked bleak midway through the final frame. With 8:24 left on the clock, defensive coordinator Daronte Jones dialed up an aggressive safety blitz on 3rd-and-7 from the Giants' 34-yard line. Safety Nick Cross came clean with a direct shot at quarterback J.J. McCarthy, but hesitated for a split second within a few steps of the pocket. That brief window was all McCarthy needed to hit tight end Isaiah Likely down the middle for a 66-yard touchdown.

Luke McCaffrey, who has capitalized on the chance at an expanded role, immediately sparked life into the response with a heady special teams play, intentionally fielding the ensuing kickoff with both feet out of bounds to hand Washington optimal field position at its own 40. The Commanders marched down the field with a sharp balance of runs and passes, including three straight uptempo snaps out of the shotgun. On the ninth play of the drive, however, Daniels took a deep shot to Chig Okonkwo that drifted slightly inside, allowing Jevon Holland to haul in Daniels' first interception of the entire season.

Two possessions, two chances to seize control, and two uncharacteristic turnovers. After the defense forced a quick punt, Daniels got the ball right back — only to force a quick out pattern to his left on 4th-and-2 that safety Ar'Darius Washington easily picked off.

In a recurring theme all night, Jones' unit refused to fold. New York managed just 14 total yards across its two possessions following Daniels' turnovers, keeping Washington within striking distance. On the decisive defensive stand, linebacker Sonny Styles shot through the gap on a seek-and-destroy pressure, chasing McCarthy down from behind to help force the punt and hand the offense one final lifeline.

Taking over at their own 29 with 1:46 remaining, Daniels finally had his chance to prove he could overcome adversity — and he delivered. A field-flipping pass interference penalty on Deonte Banks against Treylon Burks pushed Washington to the Giants' 38. Two plays later, facing 3rd-and-4, Diggs caught a short pass over the middle and split two converging defenders, causing them to collide. He scampered the remaining 32 yards untouched, tumbling gracefully across the goal line for the lead.

New York still had 57 seconds left to steal the game back, but Jones made McCarthy earn every inch. Rather than sitting back in soft prevent coverage, Jones kept calling aggressive blitzes to rattle the young quarterback. It worked. On a deep heave intended for Malik Nabers, cornerback Rasul Douglas won the jump ball for a game-sealing interception. Game over.

After calling it a 'must win' game, the Commanders (3-5-1) now sit just two games behind the Giants (5-3-1) in the division with eight games left on the slate. More importantly, Washington owns the head-to-head tiebreaker and extended its unbeaten streak against New York, having not lost to the Giants since 2023. Daniels remains undefeated against New York in his career at 4-0-1.

Washington finally has a tangible reason to believe in its season. With the 18th-toughest remaining schedule in the league — slightly more favorable than the Giants' 15th-ranked slate — what looked pitch black a week ago now carries a real glimmer of hope.
