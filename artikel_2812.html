David Pollack makes Georgia-Ole Miss prediction

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Former Georgia Bulldogs defensive end and On3 college football analyst David Pollack points out that Ole Miss Rebels quarterback Trinidad Chambliss is undefeated as a starter at home. On his podcast, "See Ball Get Ball", Pollack explained how he thinks the No. 2 Georgia at No. 10 Ole Miss game will unfold.

Pollack emphasizes that Georgia will try to establish the run to keep Chambliss off the field. He thinks Chambliss' legs will play a big role in the red zone.

"I don't think this game will be like it was in Athens last year," Pollack said. Georgia beat Ole Miss 43-35 at home and consistently moved the ball. "I don't think it's going to be where you score every possession. I do think there'll be a lot more stops."

Pollack thinks Georgia will have to run Stockton a good amount. He thinks Georgia learned their lesson from last season and do a better job of keeping Chambliss in the pocket. Pollack trusts the team of Georgia.

"I do trust the team of Georgia to to to overcome to be better," Pollack said. He predicts that Georgia will win 28-24, which would be a major step for Georgia in the SEC championship and College Football Playoff race.

"Hasn't mattered," Pollack said on Ole Miss running back Kewan Lacy being out for the Georgia game. "They've been good without Kewan."
