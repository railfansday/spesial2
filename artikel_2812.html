Dan Orlovsky names the trade the Bengals should ‘think long and hard’ about making before the NFL deadline

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Dan Orlovsky believes the Cincinnati Bengals must consider trading for Indianapolis Colts running back Jonathan Taylor before the November 10 trade deadline.

The 27-year-old has been subject to trade discussions for weeks now, but the Colts have been firm on their demand and are only likely to engage in trade talks if a first-round pick is involved.

The Bengals have been impressive so far this season, with Joe Burrow putting on some brilliant performances, including the most recent one against the Atlanta Falcons, where he scored 4 touchdowns (3 passing).

With him leading the way, the Bengals are currently ranked second in terms of points scored per game (30.0). Burrow, Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins have all contributed significantly, but there is one area where the Bengals have lacked production and Orlovsky has a player in mind who he thinks will help improve that.

"I truly think that the Bengals should think long and hard about calling the Colts and trading for Jonathan Taylor. They're that good as an offence. They're doing it their own way. I agree with you. Brown's a nice back. You add Jonathan Taylor to this offence, watch out," said Orlovsky on ESPN's First Take.

The Bengals are ranked near the bottom of the league in rushing yards, with only the Green Bay Packers and Las Vegas Raiders ranked lower than them. Chase Brown, who is the best RB on the team, has recorded 103 carries for 397 yards with just one touchdown.

The team has stayed consistent in their passing game, but Orlovsky believes there will come a point where the team might have to focus their attention on the running game.

"I just don't believe you're going to go into February and you're going to chuck and duck as great as they do it. You're an offence that can say Ja'Marr is here, Tee is here, you can't play them 1-on-1, you'll die if you do. Okay, you're going to have to play a little bit more coverage, the best thing you can do to help create those one-on-ones is to put the ball into a true bell cow back's belly and say, 'Okay, now we got Jonathan Taylor.' You literally can't stop this offence if you get Jonathan Taylor," Orlovsky added.

If the Bengals want to join the Taylor sweepstakes, they have exactly what the Colts want, which is a first-round pick. They can attach another pick or a player to it and bring in a three-time Pro Bowler to help elevate an already brilliant offence. Whether they do that or try and resolve their run defence, which has been a major problem for them this season, will be interesting to see.
