Bills Week 11 Power Rankings: Do power rankings matter heading into a divisional game?

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Despite a convincing win over the New York Jets, national analysts remain divided on whether Buffalo deserves the NFL's top spot. The Buffalo Bills walked out of MetLife Stadium with a 31-12 victory over the New York Jets, but apparently a 19-point divisional win isn't enough to impress everyone.

Heading into Week 11, Buffalo remains firmly entrenched among the NFL's elite, with four of our eight tracked national outlets ranking the Bills No. 1 and the remaining four placing them at No. 2.

Interestingly, two outlets moved Buffalo in opposite directions following Sunday's victory. ESPN dropped the Bills from first to second, while Sports Illustrated bumped them from third to second.

The result? Buffalo's average ranking remained exactly where it was last week: 1.50.

While national analysts might disagree about which team deserves the top spot, there's little disagreement that the Bills belong in the conversation.

ESPN drops Buffalo despite another convincing victory

Perhaps the most interesting development this week came from ESPN, which moved Buffalo from No. 1 to No. 2 following its victory over New York. It's not as though the Bills struggled to put points on the board. Buffalo scored 31 points, generated 389 yards of offense, and kept the Jets out of the end zone entirely.

Was it a flawless performance? Absolutely not. The Bills converted just two of their 10 third-down opportunities, and the defense allowed New York to sustain several extended possessions.

However, Buffalo also produced three second-half touchdown drives totaling just seven plays and 2:49 of possession.

James Cook rushed for 157 yards, including a 74-yard touchdown, while DJ Moore contributed 90 receiving yards and a score. Buffalo found ways to win comfortably without needing everything to go perfectly.

Power rankings aren't simply about the previous week's final score. Analysts consider the overall body of work, strength of opponents, and how teams stack up against other contenders.

Still, dropping a team after a 19-point divisional victory certainly raises some eyebrows.

USA Today FINALLY showing Buffalo some respect

Now here's an outlet we've been watching closely. USA Today hasn't exactly been Buffalo's biggest supporter throughout the season. In fact, the Bills sat at No. 9 entering Week 8 before beginning a steady climb.

The Bills moved from No. 9 to No. 6 entering Week 9, then made an impressive four-spot jump from No. 6 to No. 2 heading into Week 10. FINALLY, some respect!

Following Buffalo's victory over the Jets, USA Today kept the Bills at No. 2 for Week 11.

While USA Today isn't quite ready to crown the Bills the NFL's best team, the steady climb suggests Buffalo has done enough to change the perception of its overall performance.

And considering how long we've been watching USA Today rank Buffalo lower than several other national outlets, seeing the Bills maintain their place near the top is certainly worth acknowledging.

While Buffalo continues battling for the top spot in the national rankings, its next opponent finds itself at the opposite end of the conversation.

The Miami Dolphins enter Week 11 with considerably less confidence from national analysts.

USA Today dropped Miami five positions, from No. 27 to No. 32, while FOX Sports also placed the Dolphins last in its rankings. ESPN, NFL.com, and CBS Sports each ranked Miami No. 30.

It's a striking contrast between two AFC East rivals heading into their Week 11 matchup. Buffalo is receiving serious consideration as the best team in football. Miami is being evaluated among the league's worst.

However, divisional games have a way of making power rankings feel irrelevant once the ball is kicked off. The Bills cannot afford to overlook Miami simply because national analysts have little confidence in the Dolphins' current trajectory.

Buffalo has an opportunity to continue building momentum, and another convincing divisional victory would only strengthen its case among the NFL's top contenders.

The biggest remaining question isn't whether Buffalo belongs among the NFL's elite. The national rankings have largely settled that debate. It's whether the Bills can continue proving they deserve to be considered the very best.

With the struggling Dolphins coming to Buffalo, the Bills have another opportunity to make their case.
