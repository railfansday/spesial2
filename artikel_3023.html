ServiceNow vs. UiPath: Which Technology Stock Is a Better Buy for 2027?

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

ServiceNow's revenue top $15 billion while UiPath trades at a lower forward multiple, but the gap between the two growth stories may matter more than price.

By Brendan Coffey – Updated Nov 19, 2026 at 1:43PM EST

10 stocks we like better than ServiceNow ›

The race to automate corporate work has created massive opportunities for software companies. Investors are currently weighing ServiceNow (NOW -2.43%) against UiPath (PATH -3.62%) to determine which better captures this trend.

ServiceNow acts as a platform of platforms that unifies IT and employee experiences. UiPath focuses on robotic process automation to handle repetitive manual tasks. Both companies are now essential players in the shift toward AI-driven efficiency.

ServiceNow provides an AI platform that connects data and workflows to automate work across IT, customer service, and security. The company sells to approximately 8,700 enterprise customers and maintains deep relationships with partners like Amazon.com Inc. (AMZN -3.92%), Microsoft Corp. (MSFT -1.51%), and Nvidia Corp. (NVDA -2.38%) among tech stocks. It also collaborates with global integrators like Accenture (ACN -4.08%) and Infosys (INFY -1.27%) to help large organizations adopt its technology.

In the 12 months ended Sept. 30, 2026, revenue reached about $15.4 billion. This represents revenue growth of approximately 21.8% compared to the prior twelve-month period. During this time, the company generated a net income of roughly $1.4 billion.

As of its December 2025 balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio was nearly 0.2x, which compares total debt to shareholder equity. The current ratio, which measures a company's ability to cover short-term liabilities with liquid assets, was approximately 1.0x. In the fiscal year ended Dec. 31, 2025, free cash flow reached about $4.6 billion. Note that stock-based compensation represented roughly 35.9% of operating cash flow, which inflates reported cash generation since stock-based compensation is a non-cash expense added back in the cash flow statement.

UiPath offers a business orchestration platform that uses AI agents and software robots to automate complex business processes. It serves over 10,000 customers and leverages a network of alliances including Snowflake (SNOW -2.64%) and Accenture. By focusing on areas like finance and legal compliance, UiPath helps businesses reduce the time spent on manual data entry and routine tasks.

In the 12 months ended July 31, 2026, revenue was around $1.7 billion. This reflects revenue growth of nearly 15% over the previous twelve-month period. Net income for this time frame reached approximately $361.9 million.

UiPath reported a debt-to-equity ratio of nearly 0.0x as of its January 2026 balance sheet, indicating no significant debt relative to shareholder equity. Its current ratio was approximately 2.5x, which shows a strong ability to pay off short-term debts with liquid assets. In the fiscal year ended Jan. 31, 2026, free cash flow was about $352.2 million. Note that stock-based compensation (SBC) represented roughly 78.3% of operating cash flow, which inflates reported cash generation since SBC is a non-cash expense added back in the cash flow statement.

ServiceNow faces intense competition from established enterprise vendors like Oracle Corp. (ORCL -2.88%), SAP (SAP -3.54%), Salesforce.com Inc. (CRM -3.51%), and Workday Inc. (WDAY -2.33%). The company relies significantly on its partner ecosystem for revenue generation and faces operational risks from integrating AI technology. These challenges include legal and ethical hurdles associated with AI and complexities in navigating government procurement rules.

UiPath is cheaper using its Forward P/E for future earnings estimates and its P/S ratio, which covers sales over the past twelve months.

ServiceNow management sees a unique opportunity to leverage its core information technology strength in operations applications through rolling out new GenAI tools, especially its new agentic AI services. ServiceNow expects GenAI to be an accelerator for business, increasing its total available market and boosting average selling prices.

For the current fiscal year, consensus estimates project the business will grow sales by 22% to $16.2 billion, while net income is expected to decline by nearly $300 million to $1.47 billion.

ServiceNow shares have been volatile in 2026 amid fears that generative AI will replace Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) businesses. That's a vague worry right now and probably overstated in the long run. But it's one that has also affected UiPath, as bears see concerns that what AI can provide to its business prospects could eventually erode its business by increasing competition.

UiPath says that in the age of AI, its platform is about orchestration of enterprisewide software and systems to enable companies to find the efficiencies that AI promises while providing guardrails around AI and also working to ensure exactness--the idea being that even if AI is accurate 99.9% of the time, businesses like banks and medical providers need 100% accuracy. UiPath management says many businesses are deploying AI agents, but few have the connective tissue to link these single-task agents to the larger enterprise and to the multiple other software platforms they have deployed. UiPath says its software provides all that in a vendor-agnostic manner.

AI is becoming a dominant theme in UiPath's business, with 18 of its 20 largest deals in its most recently reported quarter involving AI. The business has raised its annual recurring revenue to more than $1.9 billion in its second quarter of fiscal 2027, up 13% year over year. Revenue in the quarter rose 13% to $410 million. For the full-year fiscal 2027, Wall Street analysts project revenue to rise 11% to $1.79 billion. Net income is expected to drop 30% to $200 million, while free cash flow is set to rise about 16% to $425 million, according to consensus estimates.

The choice here is between two companies for which AI may be a double-edged sword: providing impetus for clients to use their platforms while potentially laying the groundwork to erode their competitive moats. While ServiceNow provides faster top-line growth, UiPath shares offer a more forgiving entry point for investors seeking long-term appreciation, with a forward P/E ratio that is below the average S&P 500 stock.
