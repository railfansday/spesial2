Eaton: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Boyd Thermal extends Eaton's reach from power distribution into chip cooling, a move that is already outperforming expectations.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 3, 2026, Eaton (ETN +7.00%) raised its full-year 2026 organic revenue growth guidance, which excludes acquisitions and currency shifts, to a midpoint of 14%.

Management also raised its full-year adjusted earnings per share guidance, a profit measure that excludes one-time items and non-cash expenses, to a midpoint of $13.75. Expanding factory capacity allows the company to convert a record backlog, which represents unfilled customer orders, into long-term margin expansion.

Eaton restructured its international operations early and expanded its product lineup in high-demand markets like data centers. Electrical Global, the division serving international power markets, sharply accelerated its organic growth by gaining share with the same global customers that Eaton serves in North America.

Electrical Americas, the division handling North and South American power equipment, turned new factory capacity into higher output as manufacturing expansions came online. Completing these facilities helps Eaton fulfill record customer orders and expand segment profit margins over time.

Data center operators increasingly require advanced liquid cooling systems to support high-density computing clusters. The $9.55 billion buyout of Boyd Thermal, a thermal management business that makes liquid-cooling systems for data centers, expands Eaton's product lineup from electrical distribution to chip-level cooling. Eaton's debt roughly doubled in the first half of 2026, largely to fund Boyd

Integrated power and cooling technologies, deep customer relationships, and multiyear equipment backlogs give the company structural advantages in high-density data centers. The planned separation of the Mobility segment, which merges with Dana (DAN +2.97%) around the first quarter of 2027, will tilt the portfolio even further toward growth markets.

With major capacity ramps nearing completion and primary end markets expanding, Eaton enters 2027 with strong operational momentum. Management expects double-digit end-market growth to drive continued top-line outperformance across its global power management businesses.
