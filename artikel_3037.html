Energizer (ENR) Q4 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Free cash flow tripled as Energizer eyes a bigger step-up next fiscal year.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Energizer Holdings, Inc. (ENR +12.04%) reported its fiscal fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 results, reporting growth in free cash flow and a reduction in total debt. Management stated that Project Momentum played a central role in creating a more efficient operating model, which is expected to support future margin stability and cash conversion. The company is currently exiting specific low-margin private label segments within the Auto Care business to prioritize investments in higher-growth categories. Management projected a structural step-up in free cash flow for the coming fiscal year due to normalized capital expenditures and reduced restructuring costs.
