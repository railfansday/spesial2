Melissa’s Yellowjackets Season 4 Plan Breaks The Biggest Character Rule

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Warning: There are spoilers ahead for Yellowjackets season 4, episode 1, "Hivemind."After one episode of Yellowjackets season 4, the biggest character rule has already been broken by Melissa Kaplan (Hilary Swank). In season 3, Melissa was confirmed as another survivor of the Yellowjackets in the 2020s. After faking her death, she rebooted her life under the name of Kelly, got married to Alex (Jaylee Hamidi), became a mother, and built a new life.

The show's final season opens with Melissa on the run from her old teammates after the violent reunion in season 3 culminated in her killing Van Palmer (Lauren Ambrose). Initially, Melissa and Alex keep a low profile and avoid being found for four weeks, despite a private investigator being hired to track them down.

Melissa then takes a different approach by moving into the house next door to Shauna Sadecki (Melanie Lynskey) in Wiskayok, New Jersey. At first, Shauna believes this was a foolish move, only to see that her ex has called the media to their neighborhood, which goes against everything agreed upon in Yellowjackets' 1990s timeline.

A promise that the Yellowjackets made was to not speak to the media about what happened in the wilderness and to stick to a single lie about the story of their survival. This is seen in the season 4 premiere by the survivors having a uniform response to the investigator's questions and not speaking to any of the media bombarding them with questions when they board the plane that will take them back to the United States.

Season 1 also set a precedent for this when Shauna and Misty Quigley (Christina Ricci) refuse to speak to Jessica Roberts (Rekha Sharma), who claims to be a reporter, about the true events of what occurred in the wilderness. Jessica is actually a private detective hired by Taissa Turner (Tawny Cypress) to make sure her fellow survivors won't talk while she is running for political office. While she has her fair share of differences with Shauna and Misty, they do stick to their original promise.

This makes it all the more notable that Melissa is now breaking the rule by inviting the media to her home, presumably speaking with them, and one of the Yellowjackets season 4 trailers even shows her and Alex being interviewed on camera. Doing this is yet another way in which Melissa is breaking the trust of the other survivors, which she has already shattered by lying about her death and killing one of them. She is on the cusp of delivering an even worse blow, though.

The other part of the survivors' promise is to keep what happened in the wilderness a secret. By speaking to the media, Melissa has the potential to be the one to finally share the truth with the entire world. Since Yellowjackets season 5 is not happening and this is the conclusion of the series, the truth going public is a viable development for the story's endgame, and it would place the adult characters in a different kind of predicament than they have ever faced before during the final episodes.

That being said, Melissa is acting out of self-preservation for herself and her family. Introducing herself to the world and telling everyone that she is living next door to Shauna now lets everyone know where they are. It will make it more difficult for the remaining survivors to hurt or kill Melissa and her family in retaliation as a result of this.

While she is about to have a platform to reveal the events that followed the plane crash in the wilderness, it probably is not in her best interest. From cutting the Achilles tendon of Coach Ben Scott (Steven Krueger) to eating him and her dead teammates, Melissa did her fair share of things in the wilderness that society would condemn her for, not to mention the consequences of having recently murdered Van.

At this point, divulging the truth is seemingly not in Melissa's best interest. As Yellowjackets season 4 continues, though, what she believes is best for her and her family could change depending on what Shauna, Misty, and Taissa do.
