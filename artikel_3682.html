Here’s Why You Can Get A Haircut At 3 AM In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Grand Theft Auto 6Grand Theft Auto VIGTA 6

Have you ever been chilling at home, watching Weazel News, run your hand through your hair and thought, “Man, I’m getting a little shaggy. Time for a haircut!”?

Has it also been, like, 3 in the morning when you did that? I bet your barber wasn’t open then! Tragic!

Not so in GTA VI, where every store, no matter what it sells or what service it offers, is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week. At any time, you can get your hair and nails done, buy a coffee or an energy drink or a croissant, or pick up a sick new hat to hide your identity after committing crimes. The only limit is having the money to do it.

This might seem a little odd in a virtual world where everything else is so committed to being immersive. Constantly open stores might be a feature of many video games, but when I think about how GTA VI also has buses and trains that you actively have to wait for, that can show up late if a crime or crash slows them down, it surprises me a bit more that the realism doesn’t extend to shop hours.
