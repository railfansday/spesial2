Stokes registers for 2027 IPL auction

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Ben Stokes has entered his name for the 2027 IPL auction; former England captain is among 30 players listed at the maximum base price of INR 2 crore [£165,000]

Former England captain Ben ‌Stokes has registered for the 2027 Indian ‌Premier League auction, Cricinfo reported ​on Tuesday, with the all-rounder among the long list of names potentially set to go under the hammer in ‌Goa on December 16.

IPL rules barred players ​who skipped the mega auction in 2025 from registering for the ​following mini auction, ⁠meaning Stokes could ⁠not return to the auction pool until ‌2027.

Indian media reported, however, that the 35-year-old has been ‌granted permission by the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) to enter this ⁠year's auction.

The 35-year-old has been listed at the maximum base price of INR 2 crore [£165,000] with the players' auction held next month, a day before England's first Test on their tour of South Africa, live on Sky Sports.

England internationals Jonny Bairstow, Sam Billings, Sonny Baker and Tom Curran are also in the 30-player group with the maximum base price.

Stokes, who retired ​from international cricket ​this year, last played ​for Chennai Super Kings in ⁠2023 and has scored 935 runs and taken ⁠28 wickets in ​45 IPL matches across five ​seasons.
