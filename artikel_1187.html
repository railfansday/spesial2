Champions League: Supercomputer Predicts Final League Phase Table

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

With the 2026/27 Champions League season now underway, a supercomputer has tried its hand at predicting how the opening stage of the competition will pan out.

French giants Paris Saint-Germain are, once again, the favourites for glory as they pursue a third successive triumph in Europe's premier competition. But they will face stiff opposition from the likes of Premier League champions Arsenal, as well as Bayern Munich, Barcelona and Real Madrid.

For many clubs, however, the league phase could make or break their Champions League campaign. Now in its third iteration following UEFA's overhaul of the old group-stage format ahead of the 2024/25 season, every result could have a significant bearing on a team's route through the competition.

While some sides have been handed a tougher set of league phase opponents than others, Opta's data analysts have put together a predicted final table, with statistics, fixture difficulty and thousands of simulations helping to paint as accurate a picture as possible of how the first stage could unfold.

The wooden spoon is reserved for Slovan Bratislava, who have never reached the knockout stages and are predicted to collect just 2.51 points. Slavia Prague are expected to finish just ahead of them.

Unfortunately for Azerbaijani side Sabah FK, there is little reason to take issue with their predicted position just off the bottom of the table. Not only are they competing in the Champions League for the first time in their history, but they have also been handed the toughest set of opponents of any club in the competition. As a result, they have had a very tough time getting points on the board, but they aren't tipped to come last.

Lens, Feyenoord and AEK Athens are all in serious danger of finishing in the bottom segment of the table.

Shakhtar Donetsk, who have agreed to play their home games at Chelsea's Stamford Bridge, are currently on course to finish on just 6.61 points after one win, one draw and three losses.

Viking FK will be hoping Norway's growing reputation in European football can help them punch above their weight and replicate Bodo/Glimt's surprise run to the knockout stages last season. They are predicted to bow out at the League Phase, though.

Lille lost their opening three games (3-2 at home to Real Betis, 3-0 away to Arsenal and 4-2 at home to Galatasaray), before drawing against Bodo/Glimt. A shock win over Bayern Munich in their fifth match significantly improved their hopes of progressing.

PSV, LASK and Napoli are all tipped to only hit just over eight points, but that's not expected to be enough to advance.

Porto, Roma, Villarreal and RB Leipzig are all expected to do just enough to qualify for a playoff berth.

Bodo/Glimt were a breath of fresh air last season, as they got wins over Man City, Atletico Madrid and Inter before falling at the Round of 16. After picking up seven points from their opening five matches, the Norwegian side should get another shot at the knockouts.

Manchester United started their campaign off with a convincing win over Sabah, but then suffered defeats to Atletico Madrid and Como. A stunning comeback against Roma on matchday four, and a draw against Sporting CP, has seen their chances of progressing increase.

Bundesliga side VfB Stuttgart, Diego Simeone's Atletico Madrid and Turkish giants Fenerbahce are predicted to finish 18th, 17th and 16th respectively.

They are followed by Sporting CP, who enjoyed an impressive run to the quarter-finals in last season's edition, and Galatasaray, who are currently on a three-game winning run after opening up their campaign with back-to-back defeats.

Europa League holders Aston Villa, are expected to finish 13th. Their hopes of finishing in the top eight and qualifying automatically for the last 16 took a huge blow in matchday five when they were beaten by Galatasaray.

Club Brugge could be this season's surprise package, especially if they match the supercomputer's prediction and finish in the top 12. The Belgian outfit have claimed nine points from a possible 15 so far.

Just above them are Inter Milan. The reigning Serie A champions will hope to recover and have another shot at the big-eared trophy after reaching the final twice over the last five seasons, where they were left heartbroken at the last hurdle against Man City in 2023 and PSG in 2025.

Bayern Munich, with 2026 Ballon d'Or winner Harry Kane leading the line, will look to translate their domestic dominance into continental superiority. However, after picking up just eight points from their opening five games, they are now expected to miss out on a top eight finish.

Spanish side Real Betis have surprisingly won three of their opening four matches, beating Lille, Porto and Feyenoord. The Supercomputer thinks they'll make the top eight and qualify directly to the last 16, with Borussia Dortmund just missing out.

Manchester City are being tipped to finish seventh. Enzo Maresca's team's tally of 14.85 expected points suggests a continuation of the impressive Pep Guardiola era.

Como have one of the most inspirational stories in European football in recent memory, with their meteoric rise under the management of former Arsenal and Chelsea midfielder Cesc Fabregas making them very difficult not to love. They are now predicted to finish 6th after big wins over RB Leipzig, Manchester United, Lens and AEK Athens.

Arsenal are tipped to come fifth. The reigning Premier League champions have beaten Napoli, Lille and Slavia Prague, as well as playing out draws with German duo Bayern Munich and Dortmund.

Liverpool had a tricky tie against Atletico but were able to come from a goal behind to win 2-1 at Anfield. They then thrashed LASK 6-3 in Austria and scraped past Villarreal. A dissapointing collapse against Fenerbahce harmed their automatic progession chances, but they are now expected to finish fourth after a 3-0 victory over Club Brugge.

Man Utd feature, while PSG still aren't top despite retaining their Champions League title.

Spanish giants Barcelona are expected to finish third. Their only hiccup in their opening five matches was a defeat to Paris-Saint-Germain, who are expected to finish a place above them. The French giants are looking to win the Champions League for a third time in a row this season and have shown that they are the team to beat once again.

Finally, Real Madrid are predicted to come first. Jose Mourinho's side are the only team with a perfect record, having beaten Inter, Roma, RB Leipzig, AEK Athens and Roma.
