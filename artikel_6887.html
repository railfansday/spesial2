Fall 2026’s Biggest Pedicure Trends Are Here—Shop the Shades

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

From dark reds and chocolate browns to milky pinks and French tips.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Fall has officially arrived at our fingertips. First, Dakota Johnson’s “brown sugar espresso” nails brought a warm, creamy brown into the conversation; then Anne Hathaway’s “cherry noir” manicure made a convincing case for the season’s deep, glossy reds. If the autumnal manicures so far are any indication, this season is shaping up to be deliciously moody polish-wise. But what about your toes?

While they may be spending more time tucked inside boots and sneakers than on display in your go-to summer sandals, there’s no need to abandon them. A fresh coat of polish may be hidden from everyone else, but “nothing is going to feel better curled up next to the fireplace or snuggled inside your slippers than freshly painted toes,” celebrity manicurist Stephanie Stone says on behalf of ORLY.

The color trends are particularly good this season. Thea Green MBE, founder of Nails.INC, looks to the richer, more textured feel of fall wardrobes for inspiration, pointing to “deep burgundies, chocolate browns, sophisticated grays, and jewel tones.” However, she also loves the contrast of a barely-there, milky pedicure against heavier knits, leather, and suede. Plus, you should prepare to keep your nails nice and short this season. As Stone notes, “you’re going to want to keep your toenails short and naturally shaped so they don’t keep bumping up against your shoes.”

So while your favorite fall manicures might be getting all the attention, don’t overlook what’s happening below. Ahead, experts share the fall 2026 pedicure trends predicted to take over—and the shades you can shop to try them yourself.

“The color of the moment is a deep vampy red,” says Stone. Green concurs, sharing, “Deep, delicious, almost-black reds are going to be one of the most sophisticated pedicure looks for fall 2026.” The shade is perfect for fall because it’s dark, moody, fashion-forward, and timeless. “I particularly love a rich burgundy on short, softly squared toenails with a beautiful high-gloss finish,” says Green.

“Chocolate brown is absolutely having its moment,” says Green. From deep espressos to lighter caramels, “we’re moving towards beautiful, edible-looking tones.” A chocolate pedicure feels warmer and softer than black, but has the same sophisticated impact.

Not every Fall pedicure needs to be dark. In fact, Green loves a delicate, barely-there pink against chunky knits and dark denim. “Soft, milky pinks are subtle, elegant, and give that lovely ‘your nails but better’ effect,” she says. The key is choosing a shade that looks softly polished rather than overly opaque, with a beautiful, glossy finish. “I love it on shorter, naturally rounded toenails for that perfectly groomed, effortless finish.”

“French tips are always in style, but can make you feel extra chic when worn on your toes in the fall,” says Stone. Since wearing the design on your feet can feel like such a statement, autumn is the perfect time to try it since your pedicure is more for you than anyone else.

“We’re seeing a move towards finishes that are a little more interesting than a traditional crème,” says Green. “Whether that’s chrome, pearl, or iridescent is completely up to you.” Stone makes a strong case for cat eye. “Cat eye nail art is still all the rage, and it makes for a great pedicure design too.” Green adds, “ I absolutely love the idea of a delicate iridescent pedicure for fall. The effect should be almost translucent and beautifully luminous, catching the light as you move.” Any fun finish makes a pedicure feel special without relying on elaborate nail art.

”One of my favorite emerging color directions is what I call the new neutrals: mushroom, greige, taupe and soft, earthy grey,” says Green. These shades are perfect for a minimalist, incredibly chic look. These warmer grey tones make them much more wearable than traditional cold grey shades, particularly for fall. “I love this look with short, neatly shaped toenails and a glossy finish.”
