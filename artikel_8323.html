To compute the amount that counts toward the $25k limit, you multiply the number of shares purchased at the end of the quarter by the price of the stock at the beginning of the quarter. So it's not measured against the amount of your contributions, or the amount that you're paying, or the value of the stock when you purchase it. It's odd.
Is this true even with no lookback? The plan says "the purchase dates are the first business day after the close of an offering period" and the purchase price is defined as "15% discount off the fair market value (closing price) of the share price on the purchase date".
Yes, still true. Don't try to make any sense of it: it's just the way the rules work.

Is it reasonable for me to:
-Front load my 401K (should be fully funded by Feb or March)
-Then front load the after tax bucket (hopefully fully funded by May or June, ROTH conversion once fully funded)
-Then purchase all $25K ESPP during the third offering period (July 1 to Sept 30)?
That all sounds reasonable. Plans often limit the amount you can contribute to ESPP per paycheck (like maybe 10% or 15% of your gross pay), and there are often constraints on when you're allowed to change contribution amounts. So if you want to wait until Q3 to start ESPP, make sure you'll still have time to max it out.
