As a lululemon stan since my tween years (I’ve been rocking my Aligns for over a decade), I know that the brand’s workout gear and loungewear are worth the money—but that doesn’t necessarily mean I want to pay full price. Thankfully, lululemon’s Black Friday Early Access sale just landed, bringing savings on the brand’s most popular products.

Head Straight to lululemon’s Black Friday Early Access Sale

If you're as lululemon-obsessed as I am, you know that the brand pretty much never hosts sales (aside from Black Friday and New Year’s)—meaning now is the time to finally invest in the viral Define jacket or stock up on Align leggings (a Cosmo editor favorite) while they're as low as $49. I also found a handful of gift-worthy stocking stuffers for the lululemon fanatic on your list, like plush fuzzy socks and an adorable belt bag keychain.

Leggings are always a coveted find during lululemon’s sales, and this year didn’t disappoint. Need help deciding between styles? Aligns are ideal for yoga and low-impact workouts with their stretchy, whisper-soft fabric, while the Wunder Trains are durable enough for high-impact activities. Plus, the cult-favorite ABC pants are on sale in select colors right now, and they make a great gift for the man in your life (wink, wink).

Looking to spoil your giftee (or yourself) this season? Rest assured, I spotted plenty of splurge-worthy finds, including coats and bags in lululemon's We Made Too Much section. And don’t sleep on lululemon’s sneaker lineup—the Wildfeel has a durable, grippy outsole that’s ideal for trail running.
