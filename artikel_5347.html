Mahindra Last Mile Mobility Wins The Earthshot Prize 2026 for Transforming Last-Mile Mobility in India

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

MUMBAI, India, Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Mahindra Last Mile Mobility Limited (MLMML) has been named the winner of The Earthshot Prize 2026 in the 'Clean Our Air' category. MLMML is recognised for demonstrating how electric last-mile mobility can reduce emissions while creating economic opportunity. The award honours MLMML's efforts to accelerate the adoption of electric small commercial vehicles and make sustainable mobility accessible to drivers and small businesses across India.
