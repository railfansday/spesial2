Chiefs QB Patrick Mahomes reveals the secret behind his four-game no INT streak before Bills clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Kansas City Chiefs QB Patrick Mahomes revealed the real reason behind his incredible streak of going four games without throwing an interception.

Mahomes went 27/39 on pass attempts for 324 yards and three TDs for a 120.0 passer rating in the Chiefs' 34-7 win on November 22 over the Arizona Cardinals. The 33-year-old QB has been in a rich vein of form over the last few weeks, leading the franchise to an 8-2 record.

While most analysts haven't named Mahomes an MVP contender, his current performances are pushing him toward that point, especially with a marquee clash next week against Josh Allen's Buffalo Bills.

After the win over the Cardinals, Mahomes spoke to CBS Sports and revealed the secret behind his streak of four games without interceptions, crediting his teammates on offence for simplifying his life.

"I am playing good football. I think it helps when you run the football like we have been running it, and the receivers and tight ends are making plays for me. But we have got to keep it going. We have gotten to where we have gotten to because we keep getting better. We'll try to continue that. Big game coming up next week."

Mahomes has gone 226/337 on pass attempts this season (67.1%) for a total of 2,709 yards and 22 TDs with just four INTs and a 108.3 passer rating. As a rusher, Mahomes has tallied 100 yards and a TD.

He has had more impressive stretches of going multiple games without throwing INTs, but after his apparent regression as a QB last season before his ACL tear, many believed he would not be able to return to this level of week-to-week consistency.

While he has had some tough games, Mahomes is proving that he is as lethal as ever on the field. With the weapons the Chiefs have on offence around Mahomes, he could make the push for another Super Bowl win with the weapons at his disposal.

Their November 26 clash against division rivals, the Bills, will be a defining test in confirming the Chiefs' and Mahomes' status as legitimate contenders this season.
