'Power Slap world title would change my life'

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The next 24 hours could change Darren Godfrey's life.

The 38-year-old is aiming for Power Slap glory by becoming middleweight world champion in Doha, Qatar.

Godfrey, of Church Stretton, Shropshire, will face Isaiah Quinones, from California, who is reigning middleweight world champion, and boasts the most knockouts in Power Slap middleweight history.

"I know I've got a job to do, and he's the man that's stood in my way, I'll show him no mercy on the night but I'll shake his hand when I pick him up," Godfrey, known in the sport as Tarantula, told the BBC.

Power slapping is a popular but controversial sport involving competitors slapping each other as hard as they can across the face, and saw medics in 2024 warn that it could cause serious brain damage.

He said he believed he would be a world champion from the beginning of his career.

"The first time I ever slapped someone, I sent them right across the stage - so from that moment on, I knew I'd got some crazy power in this game," he continued.

He said a win would change everything - but not just for him.

"The life that the champions are living is totally different to the life that I'm living - I want that life for me and my family," he said.

"The sponsors and endorsements that some of these guys are getting is absolutely crazy, it's changing their lives.

"The night that I win that belt, I'm going to push the wife out of the bed and the belt is going to take her place."

Godfrey's tattoo, on his slapping hand, is a play on words relating to his surname [BBC]

Godfrey started out kickboxing, boxing and bare knuckle fighting, but was scouted to change career by the assistant to Ultimate Fighting Championship boss and promoter Dana White, who leads the Power Slap.

"They must've just thought I was a crazy dude with an iron chin," he said.

"I absolutely love it, compared to the bare knuckle where I was taking a lot of damage, broken bones.

"Power slaps is a good way for me to be able to put out there my power, without the risk of getting my face cut open and losing my teeth.

"I'm confident in my chin and I've learnt a lot of tricks over the years to generate more than most."

Unlike boxing, no head gear is allowed and defenders cannot duck to avoid blows. Even flinching is banned.

Opponents are scored based on the amount of damage they inflict and how well they cope with getting slapped themselves.

Looking ahead to the fight on Thursday, Godfrey said there was "no crowd like a power slap crowd".

"As soon as I see that man hit the canvas, as soon as I know I've connected, I know he's gone, some kind of feeling comes over you.

"If this is where my career ends, that's good enough for me, that's a career well spent for me."

In 2024, the medical experts screened videos of tournaments and wrote a warning letter in a leading journal, JAMA Surgery, about their findings.
