In-form Bournemouth could join elite company in trip to Celta Vigo

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

When Bournemouth fans booked their plane tickets to Spain following the Europa League draw back in August, few would have expected their club to be in the position they are in now.

The clash with Celta Vigo on Thursday gives Bournemouth the opportunity to secure their 10th successive win in all competitions. Their current run of nine wins in a row is already a club record as a Football League team (since 1923-24).

Even the most ardent Bournemouth fans would have laughed at the idea of their club winning 10 successive games in all competitions if you had put it to them a couple of months ago. But maybe we should not be so shocked.

The Cherries ended last season with an 18-game unbeaten run in the Premier League – the strongest finish by any side in the competition – and that was extended to 19 in all competitions, because despite exiting the FA Cup on penalties to Newcastle in January, the third-round match ended in a 3-3 draw at St. James’ Park.

That sequence of results under former manager Andoni Iraola was impressive, but Marco Rose’s achievement since replacing the Spaniard is arguably even better.

"So far, I can just praise the locker room," said Rose about his Bournemouth side.

"We are here to play football and to win the game. Everyone is ready, we have no new injuries ahead of [Thursday's] game. We are glad to have Alex [Scott] and Junior [Kroupi] back and available.

"Celta Vigo are able to run, they are able to press high, they are quite compact and it is hard to score against them.

"They are just lacking goals, but they had the chances [to score]. They are a good team."

Bournemouth's current nine-game winning streak follows an inauspicious start by the new German boss as well. They won just two of their first eight competitive matches of 2026-27 (L3 D3), with both of those victories coming in cup competitions.

Following a 5-1 thrashing at Chelsea on MD6 of the Premier League season, Bournemouth sat in the relegation zone with just three points.

The 3-0 Europa League win over Sturm Graz five days after that loss at Stamford Bridge was the turning point. Since then, Bournemouth have won every game they have played and astonishingly only conceded two goals, despite the injury absence of influential midfielder Alex Scott.

Evanilson has been the star of the winning run, scoring seven goals and assisting two more in his eight appearances.

The Brazilian has scored in each of his last five appearances in all competitions for the club, while his run of six consecutive Premier League games scored in is a club record. He might just have one eye on Jamie Vardy’s all-time record of 11 in a row set in 2015.

Thursday’s European clash in Spain offers Bournemouth the chance of making it 10 wins in a row, but how impressive will that run be compared to previous efforts by other Premier League clubs?

Winning so many consecutive games is rare for any Premier League club, let alone one which is not known for being one of English football's powerhouses.

Only seven clubs have managed to go on a winning streak of 10+ competitive games while in the Premier League – all of those have won at least three top-flight English league titles. Bournemouth are only in their 10th season as a top-flight club.

Bournemouth will have to win another 13 competitive games in a row to eclipse the sensational 21-match winning streak by Pep Guardiola’s Manchester City side between December 2020 and March 2021, though.

Starting with a victory at Southampton in the Premier League on 19 December 2020, City enjoyed 21 successive wins in all competitions before a surprise 2-0 home defeat to rivals Manchester United on 7 March 2021.

Arsenal won their final 11 competitive games of the 2001-02 season before adding two more victories at the start of 2002-03 campaign, with that 13-game winning streak the second longest by a Premier League club in all competitions.

Bournemouth should be confident of securing a 10th win in succession against a Celta Vigo side who have won just four of 18 matches in 2026-27, the most recent coming in the Copa del Rey against fourth-tier club Salamanca UDS.

Beat Celta, then Bournemouth's next challenge will be to secure an 11th win in a row in their home game against Fulham, who sit 15th in the Premier League and have won seven points fewer than Rose’s men.

There have only been eight instances of a Premier League team winning 11+ consecutive games. Can Bournemouth become the ninth?
