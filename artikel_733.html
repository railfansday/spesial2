At a Glance: How to Watch Notre Dame vs. Boston College Football

Jason Benetti will serve as the play-by-play announcer for today’s NCAA football game at Notre Dame Stadium in South Bend, Indiana. Best known as the play-by-play guy for Major League Baseball’s Detroit Tigers, Benetti will be joined by Jason Garrett in the TV booth for college football, with Caroline Pineda as the sideline reporter and Reggie Smith as rules analyst.
