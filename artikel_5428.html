HomeServices of America names Maria Latushkin as CTO

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

HomeServices of America appointed Maria Latushkin as chief technology officer, a newly created role reporting to CEO Chris Kelly. She will lead technology across affiliates as the company begins a Nov. 30 rollout of Maestro and OnePoint Digital.  AI Summary

HomeServices of America announced on Wednesday that it has appointed Maria Latushkin as its chief technology officer, a newly created position overseeing technology strategy, development, engineering, infrastructure and IT operations across the company and its affiliates.

Latushkin will report to Chris Kelly, president and CEO of HomeServices of America, and will oversee technology initiatives across HomeServices, HSF Affiliates, Prosperity Home Mortgage, HomeServices Insurance and HomeServices Title.

The appointment is intended to create a more coordinated approach to technology investments and development across the company’s businesses, with a focus on improving platforms and delivering new capabilities to real estate agents and consumers.

“Technology is central to how real estate professionals win business and serve their clients, and our agents deserve the strongest tools in the industry,” Kelly said in a statement. He added that Latushkin’s experience building technology for consumer brands would help the company turn its investments into improved products and services.

Latushkin most recently served as group vice president of technology and engineering at Albertsons Companies, where she helped oversee technology and product strategy across retail, payments, customer service and supply chain operations. She led a global technology organization of about 1,000 employees and managed more than $400 million in annual operating expenses.

Earlier in her career, Latushkin served as chief technology officer at Omada Health, Narvar and One Kings Lane.

HomeServices said Latushkin will focus on improving the company’s technology platforms and increasing adoption among agents and employees as it continues rolling out Maestro, OnePoint Digital and its broader technology ecosystem. The rollout is scheduled to begin Nov. 30.
