‘Namaslay’ Sets Streaming Release Date on Digital Platforms (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The horror satire “Namaslay” will be available to stream on Apple TV, Amazon, Fandango at Home and Google Pay on Dec. 15.

Directed by brothers Kanish Arhant-Sudhir and Rish Arhant-Sudhir, their feature film debut follows a grieving woman, Gayatri (Kirunthuja Srikanth-Talim), who is invited by her friend Judy (Angela Sant’Albano) to an upscale yoga studio to to reconnect with the yoga traditions she inherited from her late grandmother. The result is a fiery conversation about the appropriation of yoga in the West, proving culture is the blood that keeps the past alive. “We’re thrilled to bring ‘Namaslay’ to an even wider audience through its digital release,” Rish and Kanish said in a statement. “We made this film to be experienced, shared and talked about and we’re excited that audiences across the country will have the opportunity to discover the world we created. As we continue our awards campaign, we’re grateful to everyone who has supported the film and look forward to its next chapter with audiences wherever they watch.”

To develop the meaningful story they wanted to share with audiences, the brothers turned inward to their real relationships and families’ lived experiences.

“There’s something that’s truly slapstick almost about the whole experience, but then there’s also something beneath that’s kind of sinister,” Rish said during the Variety Screening Series panel. “It’s a lot of strains of colonialism, there’s commoditization that’s happening, there is an extraction and then a resale at a premium.”

Kanish echoed, “An identity is not a story. It’s a part of a character that affects how they interact with a story, and there are a lot of movies that have characters who have various identities that are not palpable at all, but the identity of a character in and of itself is not enough to undergird a narrative.”

Alongside Srikanth-Talim and Sant’Albano, “Namaslay” stars Alicia Mitchell-Mangual, Usha Krishnan and Christina Moore. The film is produced by Venk Potula via Khrisp Entertainment, alongside producers Rish and Kanish through their independent film studio Junghal Studios.
