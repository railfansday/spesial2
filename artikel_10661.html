Woman arrested for blocking Uber delivery robot’s path as part of anti-robot protest

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

A woman who repeatedly blocks self-driving delivery robots was arrested for refusing to let an Uber Eats machine pass.

Uber Eats introduced autonomous delivery robots in Philadelphia in March 2026, partnering with robotics company Avride to transport food orders through the city’s streets.

On October 14, Marvin encountered an Uber Eats robot near Philadelphia’s Reading Terminal Market, while returning home from a flu shot appointment. She decided to stand in its path until a crowd gathered and police were called to the scene.

Officers from the Philadelphia Police Department were made aware of a criminal mischief incident involving a robot. They ordered Marvin to move and she repeatedly refused, leading them to issue her with a disorderly conduct citation.

As per The Independent, Marvin said: “I was under the impression that I had more rights than the robot did.” Pennsylvania law classifies autonomous delivery robots as pedestrians, granting them certain rights to use sidewalks, although they are required to yield to human pedestrians.

Marvin maintains that her demonstrations cause little disruption, explaining that she only obstructs delivery robots when she has the opportunity and enough spare time.

She estimates that she typically spends around 10 to 15 minutes preventing a robot from continuing its journey. “I have a life, so I don’t have tons of time to stand in front of robots,” she said.
