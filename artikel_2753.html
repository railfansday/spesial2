Vikings won't be leaving Minnesota for altitude acclimation

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

With quarterback Kirk Cousins back and the Minnesota defense playing at a championship level, the sky is the limit for the Vikings.

On Sunday, the extent to which they’ll be literally in the sky could limit the ability to get an eighth win.

The Week 11 prime-time showdown between the 7-2 Vikings and 8-1 49ers will happen in Mexico City, where the altitude is 7,280 feet above sea level. The Vikings won’t be relocating this week to acclimate to the thinner air.

The 49ers, in contrast, have gone from Dallas to Colorado, where they’ll train in the far lower distance of 5,280 feet above sea level.

The Vikings will have a plan to get their players ready to perform on an elevated field. Details will emerge this week.

Whatever the plan is, it won’t entail the same kind of direct acclimation in which the 49ers are engaging.

It sets up another apples-to-apples comparison regarding the plans for international games. In Week 1, the 49ers went to Australia early. The Rams went late. And the 49ers dominated L.A., winning 27-7.

This time around, the stakes are extremely high. The winner takes over the de facto driver’s seat for the top seed in the NFC.

A loss means more to the Vikings than the 49ers, as it relates to their respective divisions. San Francisco has a solid cushion over the Seahawks and Rams in the NFC West. The Vikings are caught in a three-way tie for first place in the NFC North.
