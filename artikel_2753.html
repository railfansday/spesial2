NFL predictions: Travis Hunter will shut down Malik Nabers

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Here are some predictions for what we'll see today when the 7-2 Jacksonville Jaguars take on the 5-3-1 New York Giants at MetLife Stadium.

According to FanDuel Sportsbook, the good guys are favored by 5.5 points.

Travis Hunter has been excelling as a cornerback this season. Through his first nine games of the season, he has 3 interceptions, 3 pass breakups, a 77.4 PFF coverage grade (ninth in the NFL), and a 0.0 passer rating allowed in man coverage (first in the NFL).

He's probably on pace to earn All-Pro recognition.

That will be especially true if Hunter can shut down New York superstar Malik Nabers today. The Giants' top wideout has been up and down this year as he works his way back from an ACL injury.

According to NFL Pro, "Nabers has matched up with nine individual defenders at least 10 times each this season and has not gained 35 or more receiving yards against any of them."

In other words, when Nabers has been shadowed this season, he's been clamped up.

The prediction: Travis Hunter limits Malik Nabers to under 30 receiving yards

Nabers' receiving prop is set at 67.5 yards on FanDuel.

Only five teams have fewer sacks than Jacksonville this season (17). It's not a significant issue — at least not yet — because the Jaguars rank 12th in pressure rate (37.5%) and first in average time to pressure (2.42 seconds).

Anthony Campanile is one of the league's best defensive coordinators at manufacturing pressure. So long as he's in town, the pass rush shouldn't be a potential pitfall.

But man. It sure would be nice to see Josh Hines-Allen and Travon Walker take the quarterback to the ground a little more often! The expensive EDGE duo has combined for 5 sacks so far this season. Maybe Jordan Davis will give them more room to operate?

So I'll go ahead and speak it into existence for us all.

The prediction: Josh Hines-Allen and Travon Walker each record a sack

Hines-Allen and Walker each have +134 odds to record a sack on FanDuel.

The NFL is great because it's not about any one individual. It's very much a team sport.

A lot of games in this league come down to which team has the better player under center.

Jaguars vs. Giants is an intriguing matchup, especially with poor conditions expected at MetLife Stadium… but is Trevor Lawrence vs. J.J. McCarthy really going to be that close?

The prediction: Trevor Lawrence passes for twice as many yards as J.J. McCarthy

Lawrence's passing prop is set at 240.5 yards on FanDuel; McCarthy's is set at 214.5 yards.
