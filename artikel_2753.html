Elk Point-Jefferson gets revenge, wins 11B state title

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Vermillion, S.D. (KELO) -- Smash-mouth football was the name of the game for top-ranked Elk Point-Jefferson as the Huskies ran all over St. Thomas More to win the Class 11B state championship, getting revenge for last year with a 61-13 victory.

This is Elk Point-Jefferson's second 11B title in school history.

The Huskies attempted just one pass in the game, which fell incomplete, but tallied an incredible 390 rushing yards on 40 carries and eight touchdowns.

"You can't really explain how excited we are. We were pumped up coming into this game. We knew what we wanted to do and we came out and executed," Senior running back Brody Van Roekel said.

Van Roekel was named the Joe Robbie MVP, putting the Huskies on his back and accounting for 178 rushing yards on 12 carries and three touchdowns. He also returned an interception for a touchdown in the final 1:20 of the first half to make it 48-6 at the break.

"He's awesome. He's one of the coaches kids, he's our offensive lines coach, so he's been around this since he was a baby. It's fun to see him do all that," Huskies Head Coach Jake Terry said of Van Roekel.

Fellow halfback Aidan Hammitt also carried the ball 11 times for 78 yards and three rushing touchdowns.

Elk Point-Jefferson's play in the trenches proved to be a major advantage on both offense and defense. The offensive linemen consistently won their blocks, creating opportunities for the Wing-T offense to operate.

The defensive line gave Cavaliers quarterback Noah Wald little time to throw on nearly every dropback. The Huskies also recorded three interceptions, including Van Roekel's pick-six.

Elk Point-Jefferson joins Wall, Freeman and Parkston as the fourth team to win a state football championship in 2026 with an undefeated record.
