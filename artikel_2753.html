Tempers flare! Pakistan player gives Harshit Rana a fiery send-off, teammate steps in - WATCH

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

India A pacer Harshit Rana was at the centre of a heated exchange with Pakistan A players during their Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026 match at the West End Park International Cricket Stadium in Doha on Sunday. The tension continued even after Rana was dismissed, with Pakistan spinner Sufyan Moqim appearing to exchange words with the Indian pacer as he walked back to the dressing room.Rana was dismissed for a duck in the 45th over. As he made his way off the field, Moqim appeared to say something to him. The Pakistan spinner then had to be held back by one of his teammates as tempers threatened to boil over.

The tension between the two sides had started much earlier in the match.During Pakistan A's innings, Rana was involved in a couple of heated exchanges with the opposition's opening batters. Rana also gave Pakistan batter Shamyl Hussain a fiery send-off after taking his catch off Ayush Badoni's bowling.With emotions running high between the arch-rivals, the exchanges continued when Rana came out to bat. His dismissal only added to the tension, with Moqim appearing to give him an aggressive send-off.

Meanwhile, India A suffered a 48-run defeat against Pakistan A in their Group B match of the Asia Cup Rising Stars tournament.

Skipper Tilak Varma's measured hundred and Abhishek Sharma's attacking 70 were the positives for India A in their chase of 314 on a challenging surface.Abhishek kept India's hopes alive with a 42-ball knock that included six fours and six sixes. However, once he was dismissed, the other Indian batters struggled to score quickly against Pakistan's bowlers.India A were eventually bowled out for 265.Earlier, middle-order batter Nawaz scored a quick 79 to help Pakistan A post 313 all out. A regular member of Pakistan's senior white-ball teams, Nawaz brought up his half-century off 82 balls, hitting five fours and four sixes.Openers Maaz Sadaqat (59 off 63 balls) and Shamyl Hussain (33 off 23 balls) also gave Pakistan a strong start. The duo added 66 runs in just 7.5 overs.
