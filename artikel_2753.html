Phoenix Suns vs Sacramento Kings. Live updates, NBA analysis

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

SACRAMENTO – The Phoenix Suns continue a five-game road trip Nov. 18 against the Sacramento Kings at Golden 1 Center.

The 8 p.m. game is being televised on 3TV and Arizona Family Sports.

The Suns (6-7) rebounded from Sunday's loss to the Los Angeles Lakers to end a season-long, seven-game homestand by upending the Denver Nuggets, 117-105, Nov. 16, to begin the road trip.

The Kings (1-12) have the worst record in the Western Conference. They've lost their last six games and are 1-9 in their last 10.

The Suns just have Mark Williams (left shoulder surgery) ruled out on their injury as he hasn't played this season.

Precious Achiuwa (left lower leg contusion) and De'Andre Hunter (left hip soreness/tightness) are listed as questionable for the Kings while Jonathan Mogbo (G League - two-way), Sean Pedulla (G League - two-way), Maxime Raynaud (right patella subluxation), and Ben Simmons (right Achilles tendon repair/surgery) have been ruled out.
