Vodacom Group Limited (VDMCY) Q2 2027 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Vodacom Group Limited (VDMCY) Q2 2027 Earnings Call November 10, 2026 9:00 AM EST

Mohamed Shameel Joosub - CEO & Executive DirectorJohn-Paul Davids - Head of Investor RelationsRaisibe Morathi - Group CFO & Executive Director

Mohamed Shameel JoosubCEO & Executive Director

Good afternoon, and good morning to those joining us from the U.S. Welcome to the Vodacom results call for the 6 months ended 30th of September 2026. I'm joined by our Group CFO, Raisibe Morathi; as well as our Head of Investor Relations, JP Davids. I hope you found the video presentation ahead of the call useful. It covers our performance for the period and the progress we continue to make against Vision 2030.

For those not able to watch our presentation, I will take you through some brief highlights. We will then move into a Q&A session. This was one of Vodacom's strongest first half performances with broad-based growth across the group and good progress against our strategic ambitions. We delivered above target growth in service revenue, EBITDA and operating free cash flow, while our customer base reached 215 million. This is 0.25 billion customers across our Africa footprint and a significant milestone that speaks to the scale that Vodacom has built.

The Safaricom transaction was another defining element of the 6-month period. It remains subject to legal processes in Kenya, and we are set to return to the appeals court on the 19th of November to set a hearing date. As a reminder, we paid ZAR 35 billion to increase our shareholding in Safaricom from 35% to a controlling 55% and consolidated Safaricom results from the second quarter. The ZAR 35 billion was comprised of ZAR 26 billion to the government of Kenya for 15% stake in Safaricom and ZAR 9 billion to Vodafone for an additional 5% stake.
