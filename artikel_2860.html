Vikings Week 11 Stock Market Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Vikings lost the most important game of their season so far in a game in Mexico City that was never close. The Vikings defense was sliced and diced by the 49ers playmakers, mostly with quick game passing, but with some longer completions to Mike Evans as well. It was really a disappointing effort across the board in prime time that made the Vikings look like they don't belong among Super Bowl contenders. Final score was 35-10, but it was really over at halftime when the 49ers were up 28-10.

The 49ers seemed to put more value in winning this game than the Vikings did, and it showed. They were ready for the game of the season and the Vikings seemed just happy to be there. One player not lacking in intensity was Kirk Cousins, but not enough of his teammates- on either side of the ball- shared that intensity.

With that, here is the stock market report.

Kirk Cousins. He threw a pick six, and that was a big negative to be sure, but outside of that he was the driving force of the offense all night. He didn't have much help from his supporting cast, including the defense, but he played relatively well under the circumstances and appeared ready for the big game atmosphere when most of his teammates and coaching staff were not. In many ways he seemed more prepared than his teammates despite having been with the team only two weeks.

Myles Price. He didn't play much as a wide receiver, but was the most productive with the snaps he had. He really deserves to be WR3 at this point. Jauan Jennings was okay, but Price is better and deserves more reps than he got.

James Pierre. He replaced Isaiah Rodgers and allowed basically nothing in his coverage.

T.J. Hockenson. He caught all six of his targets and was able to move the chains on a couple key plays in his first game with Kirk Cousins at quarterback.

Justin Jefferson. What might have been a game changing play that may have gone for a touchdown or at least the deep red zone was blown with him holding the ball in one hand away from his body and sure enough, it resulted in a fumble and turnover. A stupid mistake and a costly one.

Christian Darrisaw. This isn't as much about Darrisaw's performance as his durability. It's been an issue ever since he came back from his knee injury. He lacks intensity and just seems more concerned about injuries than anything else and once again missed a lot of a big game with a shoulder injury. He just doesn't seem like as much of a competitor at this point in his career. And when he's as highly paid as he is, that's not ideal. He's needs to step up or not be the future at left tackle.

Brian Flores. He didn't have an answer for the 49ers quick passing game nor Brock Purdy's focus on Mike Evans. That had to be an expected counter to his defense but there wasn't much of a response or game plan for it.

Kevin O'Connell. It's O'Connell's job to get his team ready for what was essentially a playoff-type game. And he didn't. It was simply a poor effort and the game was quickly lost. This is where O'Connell needs to improve if the Vikings are to win a playoff game or make a deep run in the playoffs. Both Flores and O'Connell were outcoached by Kyle Shanahan in this game.

Defensive Coverage. Outside of James Pierre, it was really a poor night for coverage, whether safeties, cornerbacks, or linebackers. Defending a quick passing game means being able to win more in coverage than the Vikings were able to do and that, more than anything else, allowed the 49ers and Brock Purdy to move the ball down the field and score on multiple drives.

The Vikings preparation for this game. The altitude wasn't an issue or a disadvantage- the Vikings didn't appear to be more fatigued than the 49ers- but the game planning and preparation was lackluster and showed from the first drive to garbage time. This was the biggest of four big games in a row for the Vikings and the second one on the road. I suspect they were tired mentally and perhaps emotionally as well going into this game and it showed.

The Vikings' ability to rebound from this game. It's been a tough four-game stretch for the most part for the Vikings and really one they weren't well prepared for. Not having a decent quarterback and offense for the first half of this four-game stretch and trying to acclimate a new quarterback two weeks ago and then having two road games- the last against the best team they've faced this season- is tough to do midway through the season. But a couple home games against mediocre opponents in Atlanta and Carolina should help, followed by a road game against the Patriots. The goal of this three game stretch should be first to go 3-0- or 1-0 for three straight weeks- and rebuild the intensity and focus of the team in preparation for the next more important game against the Lions on December 20th. It's been a tough month for the team to battle through but hopefully an easier schedule the next few weeks will allow them to rebuild stronger in preparation for the postseason- which will feature the same caliber of opponents as this past month.
