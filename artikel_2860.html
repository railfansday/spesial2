And for the final race of the year, NASCAR is heading down to Miami. The 2026 Cup Series Championship will crown a winner on Sunday.

This weekend's race has a very likely outcome, and it ends with Kyle Larson becoming a Cup Series Champion for the third time in his career. Larson has been unstoppable this postseason, and the odds are heavily in his favor with a 67-point lead.

Larson needs just nine points to clinch at Homestead, regardless of any other driver's placement. Of course, Christopher Bell and Denny Hamlin are still distant possibilities, though they would have to score and place exceptionally to pull it off.

Will Kyle Larson return to the throne in South Florida?

Here's everything you need to know about the 2026 Cup Series Championship, including start time and TV channel for the title race.

The 2026 Cup Series Championship will get started at 3 p.m. ET on Saturday, Nov. 8. The race will take place at Homestead-Miami Speedway in Florida.

The 2026 Cup Series Championship will be broadcast nationally on NBC.

Cord-cutters and fans without cable can also stream the action live on fubo, which offers a free trial for all new users.
