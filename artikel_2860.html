‘Dolly Hated Kiffin’: Tennessee fans take Lane Kiffin trolling to new heights

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Lane Kiffin sent a strong message to Tennessee fans on Wednesday's SEC coaches' teleconference saying he didn't believe they could top the reception he received upon returning to Mississippi in Week 3.

Judging by the response on Rocky Top ahead of Saturday afternoon's kickoff, Volunteers fans took that as a challenge.

A plane was spotted flying over Knoxville and Neyland Stadium with a banner that read "Dolly Hated Kiffin," a gesture that showcases that the late "Queen of Country Music" didn't like the former Tennessee coach during his time in Knoxville.

Kiffin's spent just one season in Knoxville leading the Vols program in 2009. He led the Vols to a 7-6 record and a Chick-fil-A Bowl appearance before bolting to the West Coast to coach at Southern California. In 2021 when Kiffin returned to Neyland as Ole Miss' coach, he was struck by a golf ball while Tennessee fans threw trash onto the field, prompting a stoppage in the game

"I'm not challenging Tennessee," Kiffin said on Wednesday's SEC coaches teleconference. "But I just don't think you can top that (Ole Miss atmosphere) — just the feeling of what that was like and the anger and hatred because it was so recent."

He added: "Again, I'm not challenging Tennessee. It sounds like I am. But I think once we've gone through (the Ole Miss game), I don't think anything is going to (match that). I think this (game at Tennessee) — and it was last time (in 2021) — more fun. OK, they're mad. They're yelling things. They're saying things. It's kind of fun."

The Volunteers are honoring Parton, who died back in September following a brief battle with cancer, with a commemorative helmet sticker throughout the season. The decal features "Dolly" above a butterfly. As noted by the Knoxville News Sentinel, part of the USA TODAY Network, the butterfly has long been associated with Parton and is prominently featured throughout Dollywood. For Parton, the symbol represents freedom, transformation and joy.

Saturday's game between LSU and Tennessee is the lone top 25 matchup in Week 12 amongst teams ranked the US LBM Coaches Poll. There are no top-25 matchups amongst teams in the AP poll, marking it the first time since November 2009 where the first Saturday of November no AP Top 25 matchups in a non-COVID-19 condensed season, according to Iron College Football Stats.
