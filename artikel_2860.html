Iwobi second on creativity list, just needs help

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Tom Masters explains Alex Iwobi's predicament as an unlucky winger for Fulham.

Alex Iwobi must feel like the unluckiest player alive right now, as somehow, despite his incredible efforts, he has zero assists to his name.

When looking at the statistics for most chances created and most assists, there is certainly a pretty obvious outlier.

And that outlier is without a doubt the Nigerian midfielder.

Only Pascal Groß of Brighton has more Premier League big chances created than Iwobi, who has created 24 chances, the second most alongside Nottingham Forest midfielder Morgan Gibbs-White.

But if you look at assist numbers, Groß has four to his name, while Gibbs-White has three and Iwobi has zero.

Why is that? Well, of course, Fulham have struggled all season to convert their chances, and that is not anything new.

We spoke in pre-season about the need for there to be someone experienced to take over the mantle of the Fulham attack, and that it was almost certainly going to be Iwobi.

In pre-season, Iwobi was comfortably the best attacker alongside the wingbacks, but this season, on paper at least, it has probably been Josh King.

However, if you actually look at the statistics and at the way Fulham play it is clear that Iwobi is in fact doing the job that he was asked to do when replacing Harry Wilson as the experienced man in the Fulham front three.

Iwobi has created the chances; he simply hasn't had the help from his teammates in converting them.

There have been several chances from corners which have not been put away, and also of course, there is the issue of Gonzalo Garcia, who has scored just once from an expected goals of over four.

Garcia has now got nine matches in the Premier League without a goal, with Rodrigo Muniz scoring the equaliser against Newcastle last weekend; it feels as though there is a chance the Brazilian may take Garcia's spot up front for the Cottagers.

Iwobi will be desperate for someone new to come into the side or his luck to change, according to expected goals and assists. He should be racking up about four assists by now and be right up there in a conversation with Gibbs- White and Groß as two of the more exciting attacking players this season.

Fulham, unfortunately, have let him down, and as a result, the Nigerian is not being spoken about in conversations that he should be.
