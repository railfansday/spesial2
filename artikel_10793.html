Restaurant employee fired after being caught taking almost entire bowl of Halloween candy

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

An Alabama restaurant has fired an employee who was caught taking almost an entire bowl of Halloween candy after discovering she had lied about apologizing to the homeowner.

The incident was captured on video and shared on Facebook, showing two women taking handfuls of candy from a bucket left outside a home for trick-or-treaters.

One of the women was wearing a shirt bearing the logo of Sakana Sushi Bar & Grill in Hoover, Alabama.

In the footage, the two women approach the bucket and proceed to take almost all of its contents, leaving very little behind.

The person who shared the video criticized their actions, writing: “Greedy adults stole almost the entire bowl of candy!!! literally left almost nothing for the children!! absolutely pathetic”

They also tagged Sakana Sushi Bar & Grill, asking whether one of the women was employed there.

The restaurant subsequently addressed the incident in a Facebook statement, saying it was aware of the footage and was looking into the matter.

“We take this matter seriously and want to make it clear that we do not condone any behavior that takes away from children or negatively impacts our community,” the statement read.

Sakana added that it would address the situation appropriately if anyone associated with the business was involved.

According to AL.com, the restaurant later confirmed that the woman taking the candy was an off-duty employee.

A spokesperson initially claimed that the employee had returned the candy and apologized to the homeowner, Taylor Wilson, on Sunday morning.

However, Wilson disputed that account, explaining that the apology had actually come from the other woman in the video.

After learning this, Sakana said it had confirmed that the employee lied about apologizing and decided to terminate her employment.

“Lying to us after knowing how important everything is and how important we told her this is to us and to the entire community and we basically told her that she can no longer be working with us anymore,” a restaurant spokesperson said.
