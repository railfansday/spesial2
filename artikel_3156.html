BAFTA Loses Longtime Film Awards Sponsor EE

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

BAFTA is losing the longtime headline sponsor of its film awards, EE.

The Brit mobile and internet provider has sponsored the UK’s leading film awards event for almost 30 years but the upcoming 2027 edition will be its last.

The decision was driven by EE’s “business strategy,” according to BAFTA, which said it was first told about the move at the end of 2025. The news was first reported in UK trade publication Campaign.

Not a great few days for BAFTA. Over the weekend it emerged that high-profile musical Michael would sidestep the awards. It’s an unusual move. But it follows producer GK Films’ condemnation of the organization and its host broadcaster BBC following the I, Swear debacle earlier this year. Another possible reason speculated online is the BAFTA TV Award previously bestowed on doc Leaving Neverland. No official reason has been offered for the move. Now it emerges the organization has lost its main sponsor. Watch on Deadline

The latest film edition became mired in controversy after Tourette’s syndrome campaigner John Davidson involuntarily shouted the N-word while Sinners actors Michael B. Jordan and Delroy Lindo presented an award, and the BBC aired the slur despite a two-hour delay.

EE first became title sponsor of the BAFTA film awards in 2013, taking over from its sister company Orange, which started the collaboration in 1998. EE adds its name to the Rising Star Award. P&O Cruises is the headline sponsor of the BAFTA TV Awards.

A BAFTA spokesperson told Deadline today: “2027 will mark the 30th year of the EE BAFTA Film Awards. After a long-standing, successful and trusted partnership, EE advised us more than a year ago that it would not be renewing its support of the Film Awards beyond the end of the current contract which runs to the end of 2027, confirming that this decision is entirely driven by EE’s business strategy. We are incredibly proud of what we have achieved together over three decades and are fully focused on delivering an exceptional EE BAFTA Film Awards in February, where we look forward to celebrating the very best in film with EE and audiences throughout the UK and world-wide.”
