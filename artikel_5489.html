U.S. private sector added 86,000 jobs in October, beating forecasts, ADP said

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Professional and business services lost jobs for a third straight month as base pay rose 3.2% from a year earlier

A sign at a NYS Department Of Labor job fair at the Downtown Central Library in Buffalo, New York, US, on Wednesday, Aug. 27, 2025. (Lauren Petracca/Bloomberg via Getty Images)

ADP reported that private-sector employment rose by 86,000 jobs in October, according to its National Employment Report produced with the Stanford Digital Economy Lab.

The September total was revised to 86,000 from 90,000, ADP said. The October figure came in above the 75,000 jobs that Wall Street had forecast, according to MarketWatch.

Service-providing industries added 82,000 jobs, and goods-producing industries added 4,000. Trade, transportation and utilities led with 39,000 jobs, followed by education and health services with 28,000 and leisure and hospitality with 19,000. Financial activities added 15,000, construction added 13,000 and other services added 10,000.

Professional and business services lost 27,000 jobs, its third straight monthly decline. Natural resources and mining shed 8,000 jobs, information lost 2,000 and manufacturing lost 1,000.

Base pay for private-sector workers rose 3.2% from a year earlier, and gross pay rose 4.7%, according to ADP Pay Insights. Job-changers saw base pay climb 4.8% and gross pay climb 7.6%. Job-stayers saw base pay rise 3.0% and gross pay rise 4.4%.

“Employers of all sizes were hiring last month, and our data suggests that job-changers were lured by a combination of bonuses, overtime, and more hours in addition to higher base pay,” ADP Chief Economist Nela Richardson said in a statement. “Taken together, earnings growth for these workers hit its fastest pace since March 2024.”

Medium-sized establishments added 33,000 jobs, small establishments added 28,000 and large establishments added 24,000. By region, the South added 47,000 jobs, the West added 28,000, the Midwest added 9,000 and the Northeast added 3,000.

ADP had reported 90,000 jobs for September, up from a revised 36,000 in August. The Bureau of Labor Statistics reported that nonfarm payrolls rose 29,000 in September, below the forecast of 84,000.

The Bureau of Labor Statistics is scheduled to release its October nonfarm payrolls report on Friday, according to CNBC. ADP will release its November report on Dec. 2 at 8:15 a.m. ET.
