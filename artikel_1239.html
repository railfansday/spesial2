Arsenal Make Contact to Sign Ivan Fresneda to Replace Ben White

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Arsenal have made contact about a January deal to sign a rapid Champions League star as a replacement for Ben White, according to a report.

The Gunners have had a brilliant season so far and sit six points clear at the top of the Premier League, having won nine of their opening 11 games, while they have won three of their five Champions League games and remain undefeated in Europe at this stage too.

But as Mikel Arteta looks to bolster his squad and retain their league title for the first time in their history, it was now emerged that a new addition at full-back is being considered for the January transfer window.

Arsenal have been looking at the addition of a right-sided defender for 2027 for a while now, and with Ben White now 29, it's believed that the position requires future-proofing.

Now, according to Guillermo Rai, the north London side have made contact with the representatives of Sporting CP defender Ivan Fresneda over a potential January switch.

The 22-year-old, who made his debut for the Spain national team in October, has been a regular for the Portuguese side for a number of seasons, has 18 months remaining on his contract in Lisbon, and is someone that Arsenal have been tracking for a while.

The Gunners view the former Valladolid star as a long-term replacement for White, who is out of contract at the Emirates Stadium in 2028, with the option of a further 12 months.

It's unclear how much a deal is likely to cost, but the Spaniard has £69m release clause in his contract. Sporting would likely be willing to accept offers below that value though, considering the length on his deal and clubs interested in his services.

Arsenal's greatest strength at the moment is their defensive solidity, with the physical prowess and technical quality within their backline making them the best in Europe.

But while Fresneda is a solid defender, he is more forward thinking in his style of play, and would provide a greater attacking threat than White or Jurrien Timber.

The youngster would also bring incredible pace to the side, having been recorded as one of the fastest players in the Champions League in the 2025/26 season, and that will certainly help Arteta to take the team to the next level.

Whether the talks being held leads to an actual move happening in January remains to be seen, with a summer move also possible, but it's clear that Arteta has identified Fresneda as the man he wants as the next right-back in his squad.

The Spaniard could face a lengthy spell on the sidelines.
