Liverpool have taken an important step towards securing Alexis Mac Allister’s long-term future, with the Argentina international eager to extend his stay at Anfield.

The 27-year-old had previously been disappointed after being told that a renewal was not immediately planned, but his excellent form this season appears to have changed the club’s thinking.

With Real Madrid also monitoring his situation, Liverpool now have good reason to resolve the matter before uncertainty grows.

According to Football Insider, Liverpool have opened preliminary discussions with Mac Allister’s representatives over a new contract.

His current deal runs until 2028, but the midfielder is understood to be extremely keen to extend his stay.

That represents a significant change from earlier in the season, when Mac Allister admitted he had been hurt by Liverpool’s previous decision not to prioritise a renewal.

His performances have strengthened his case considerably. Mac Allister has scored five goals already this season and has re-established himself as one of Andoni Iraola’s most important midfielders.

Liverpool’s decision also comes amid continued interest from Real Madrid, who explored a possible deal earlier this year.

There is little reason for Liverpool to allow this situation to drift.

A CaughtOffside report confirmed through Fabrizio Romano that talks have started, while also revealing Real Madrid explored signing Mac Allister towards the end of May.

That interest should sharpen Liverpool’s focus.

Mac Allister is entering his prime, understands the Premier League and offers a rare combination of creativity, intelligence and defensive discipline. Replacing him with a player of similar quality would likely cost considerably more than improving his current terms.

Liverpool have already seen Trent Alexander-Arnold leave for Real Madrid, so allowing another important player to move closer to the final year of his contract would create unnecessary risk.

The player’s stance also makes negotiations easier. Mac Allister wants to remain at Anfield, meaning Liverpool are not trying to convince an unhappy player to stay. They simply need to show that his importance is reflected in the contract they offer.

If both sides genuinely want the same outcome, this should be one of Liverpool’s simplest major decisions of the season.
