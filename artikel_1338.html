Alan Shearer's Telling Comment When Daughter Married 26-Cap England International

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Those of a certain generation will remember Alan Shearer as a relentless goalscorer in his pomp. Although these days he is best-known for his punditry, the Newcastle United legend still holds the record for the most Premier League goals scored (260).

He also bagged 30 goals for England in 63 caps, a fine record which leaves him joint-seventh on the list of all-time men's goalscorers for the Three Lions. Having done so well for his country, it's interesting to learn that his daughter is married to another international sportsman.

Indeed, Hollie tied the knot with her new husband in July 2026. However, the athlete in question is known for playing a different sport.

Shearer highlighted the Englishman's all-round game while naming him the best.

Shearer was on co-commentary duties as Spain beat Argentina in the World Cup final in New Jersey in the summer 2026, but quickly rushed back to England to watch his daughter Hollie marry Joe Marchant. Rugby fans will be familiar with that name, as he has represented England 28 times in the sport.

Marchant currently also plays as a centre for PREM Rugby club Sale Sharks, having left Stade Francais earlier in 2026. Born in Winchester, the 30-year-old made his debut for England in 2019, having played for his country at U18 and U20 level.

Shearer's daughter confirmed her romance with the former Harlequins star back in April 2022, as she later moved to France with him. They then got engaged in 2024, before getting married this summer in Winchester.

They took to social media to share photos outside local pub The Royal Oak, which has stood, in part, for more than a thousand years after being built in 1002.
