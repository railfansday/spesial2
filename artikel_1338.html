Mauricio Pochettino Must Unleash Marcus Rashford for Man Utd vs Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Manchester United are set to enter a new era under Mauricio Pochettino on Sunday, and the new manager is preparing for a baptism of fire.

The Red Devils will travel to Anfield to take on arch-rivals Liverpool in the Argentine's first game in charge since replacing Michael Carrick in the dugout, having had just three days to prepare the team after arriving from the USMNT on Wednesday afternoon.

United head into the game in the relegation zone, having won just one of their opening ten games this season, but the 54-year-old is confident that he can turn things around and revolutionise Man Utd, getting the team back up the table where they feel they belong.

To do that, he must address their defensive issues. Man Utd have conceded 30 goals in all competitions this season, which is the most of any team in Europe's top five leagues before a ball was kicked this weekend.

But he must also find a way to make the team more of a threat going forward, and the best way for him to do that is to add pace. Amad Diallo has failed to score for the club since a 4-4 draw with Bournemouth in December 2025, while Matheus Cunha has scored in just three of his 15 appearances this season. Bryan Mbeumo has scored in four of his 14 appearances, while Bruno Fernandes has scored in four of his 14 appearances, netting six times.

Those numbers are nowhere near good enough for a team who want to be competing at the top end of the table, and that means changes are needed.

One of the biggest issues has been a lack of pace and running in behind. That entire attack prefers to receive the ball to feet and then work combinations, but that allows the opposition to get back in their positions and then shut attacks down. But United could be set to welcome back Marcus Rashford into the fold following injury, and he would be the perfect remedy for that.

Pochettino didn't give any injury updates during his pre-match press conference for the game, but there have been reports that Rashford could be fit to return after missing the last month of action with hip and groin problems.

The England international started the season sharply, and while he didn't register a goal or assist, that was more down to his struggling teammates than his own performance. But now he should get the chance to put that right, while coming up against some of his favourite opposition in a game that means more to him than most others in the squad.

Rashford came through the academy at Man Utd, and there is nobody in the first-team squad who has scored more goals than him for the club.

His relationship with the board and with fans has been up in the air in recent seasons, but the hope is that is now behind them, and he can get his head down and get back to his best, just as he did with Barcelona last season.

Pochettino is understood to be a huge fan of the winger, even being described as being 'obsessed' with him, and Rashford loves playing against Liverpool more than most. In fact, he has only ever scored more goals against Leicester (8), than he has Liverpool (7).

He would also provide great pace and the willingness to run in behind, either from the left wing or a central position, allowing United to be able to counter-attack more effectively. His quality is undeniable too, having been described as 'frightening' by former teammate Ollie Watkins.

“I’m on the sideline and I’m thinking, I shouldn’t come on here really. I’m honest like that. I knew Marcus’s talent and I knew the manager would get the best out of him and to be fair he did. He was unbelievable at Villa.

Considering the short amount of time to prepare for the game, Pochettino must look for the best and most effective outlets he can to make the team a threat, and including Rashford in the starting lineup is something he simply must do if he wants the best chance of getting off to a winning start.
