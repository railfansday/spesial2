Why Anterix Stock Climbed Higher Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The company exceeded expectations for its fiscal second quarter.

Niche telecom stock Anterix (ATEX +1.41%) was something of a sector star on Wednesday, thanks to a quarterly earnings report that was better than many investors and analysts expected. With that tailwind at its back, Anterix's equity closed the day more than 1% higher.

After the market close on Tuesday, Anterix unveiled its fiscal 2027 second-quarter figures. These showed that revenue was just under $1.96 million, representing 26% year-over-year growth. The company flipped to a net loss under generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP); this was a shade under $5 million ($0.25 per share), against the year-ago profit of $53.5 million. However, in the second quarter of 2026, it benefited from a gain of more than $70 million on asset sales and non-cash exchanges of intangible assets.

This meant a mixed quarter for Anterix, as it missed the consensus analyst revenue estimate of $2.05 million but crushed the average projection of a $0.39-per-share net loss.

The company's top-line growth was driven by the recognition of deferred revenue from the wireless spectrum it leases to its clients, which are large American utilities. Its contracts with such customers mandate large upfront payments, which sit on the balance sheet and only move to the profit and loss statement when operational milestones are achieved.

This is likely the chief reason the market's reaction to the earnings report was guarded optimism. During the quarter, Anterix signed no new lease agreements with clients and posted flat quarter-over-quarter revenue -- in contrast to that impressive-looking year-over-year growth.

Anterix has over $33 million in remaining, uncollected contract proceeds, however, and it's sitting on more than $107 million in cash with no debt. Yes, it could use some fresh business, but its financial position is enviable. While the company's fundamentals can be lumpy, it operates a future-forward business with plenty of potential. I'd be more optimistic about its future than those cautious investors on Wednesday.
