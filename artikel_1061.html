Boro ease past Bristol City to go top of Championship

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Middlesbrough vs Bristol City. Sky Bet Championship.

Report and free match highlights from the Sky Bet Championship match between Middlesbrough and Bristol City at the Riverside Stadium on Saturday; Adilson Malanda and David Strelec score as Boro return to winning ways and go top of the table.

Please use Chrome browser for a more accessible video player

Middlesbrough scored a goal either side of half-time to climb top of the Sky Bet Championship with a 2-0 win that piled further pressure on Bristol City boss Michael Skubala.

Adilson Malanda's header five minutes before half-time was followed by David Strelec's finish in the 54th minute to secure a Boro victory.

The three points were effectively secured before Middlesbrough head coach Kim Hellberg introduced the division's top scorer Will Lankshear for the final 14 minutes from the bench after injury.

Defeat heightened the frustrations being felt by the Robins, who have won just one of their last eight matches, as they dropped down to 18th.

Hellberg resisted the temptation to include 13-goal Lankshear in his starting line-up after his recovery from a hamstring problem - and it was a decision that paid off.

Boro had not scored or won either of the two games without the former Tottenham man building up to the international break, but the option was there to bring him on earlier if needed.

There was a touching tribute to Boro favourite Tony Mowbray - who this week announced he was stepping away from his head coach role at Blackburn due to health issues - from the fans early in the game.

City were denied a penalty when striker Lorent Tolaj went down in the area under a clumsy challenge from Ashley Phillips after defensive hesitancy.

Beyond that there was very little to trouble either goalkeeper, with Middlesbrough's larger share of the possession not reflected in goalscoring chances before the opening goal.

The breakthrough arrived when Malanda was allowed to move into space and duck to head Sebastian Berhalter's corner, which floated toward the near post, inside the far corner.

The nearest Bristol City came to scoring in the first period was a harmless Rio Cardines free-kick that curled high over Radek Vitek's crossbar seconds after the opener.

There was still time before the break for Strelec to curl an effort into the arms of visiting goalkeeper Bradley Collins, who then made a stunning save to turn over another Malanda header from close range.

Middlesbrough continued where they left off after the restart.

Strelec had already headed a cross from Amario Cozier-Duberry wide before the two combined again for the Slovakia striker's first of the season, which was helped in by Collins.

That led to a few negative chants from the travelling fans aimed at Skubala and Middlesbrough went on to claim victory from there - even though Bristol City had the better of the chances after that.

Tolaj found the post just after the hour, while Vitek had to be alert to deny Yu Hirakawa and Gibson Yah side-footed an effort a yard wide before Tomi Horvat crashed the woodwork in stoppage time.

"It was brilliant; breaking down a team we hadn't beaten in nine games and a team which had lost only one of their last seven away games, I am happy.

"Breaking down the low block and scoring from a set-piece and from David makes me overall happy, with a clean sheet too.

"It was a massive moment for David. He has been very good since coming back, he's shown how good he is in these two games. That goal was a beautiful team goal and he is a top player - you could see how happy he was for it.

"He is a brilliant human being, it's easy for me to back him. I can see what he does every day. It is about having more quality players being ready to play.

"For Will we wanted to get him on, it's important for him to play and have that feeling. Everyone knows how good he is, I am happy to have him back."

"This was a disappointing afternoon. We tried to nullify all their strengths so to concede from a set-piece when we did made for a difficult start, and the second goal killed us.

"And when you assess our finishing, it hurt us. We hit the post three times, and if we get one of those it would be a different end to the game.

"It is very difficult to take because if that first goal doesn't happen then it is a different game.

"We have something to look forward to and that is a game against Wrexham, and football always brings you another game around the corner. The fans stuck with the lads really well and I thank them for that."
