Buffalo Bills vs. Miami Dolphins, NFL Week 11 live game thread

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

It's finally time, Rumblers! The Buffalo Bills get their first crack at the Miami Dolphins today, and the two teams couldn't be much further apart if they tried. Today's game is an important one for both clubs, but the Bills have very different goals.

Buffalo will look to improve on its 1-1 record versus the AFC East, and to maintain sole possession of first place in the AFC. Miami is looking to build upon its first victory last Sunday, and winning against the conference-leading Bills would go a long way in building confidence for the rebuilding team.

Buffalo will have to rely on quite a few backups today based on the Bills' Week 11 inactive list. Starters in cornerback Christian Benford and inside linebacker Dorian Williams were ruled out Friday afternoon. The Bills will also be without starting tight end Dalton Kincaid, whose prior "doubtful" game-day designation now turns into "out" for Week 11.

Despite showing up as "questionable" due to a late injury on Saturday, defensive tackle Ed Oliver is not among Buffalo's inactive players. We'll see how much he's able to contribute this afternoon.

Make no mistake, this is a very important game for the Bills despite the circumstances of their opponent. Buffalo will have a short week of rest and to prepare for their Week 12 opponents, the Kansas City Chiefs, who they host in primetime on Thanksgiving night. A win today would also give Buffalo its first victory in their NFL/Nike Rivals uniforms.

Will head coach Joe Brady and the Bills' offense look to feature the ground-and-pound rushing attack today, in an effort to shorten the game and wear down Miami? It's work well in recent weeks against other opponents, and running back James Cook III has a goal to hit 2,000 yards on the ground this season.

With that all in mind, here's your open thread for Bills-Dolphins in Week 11. As always, be kind to one another — and in all things… Go Bills!
