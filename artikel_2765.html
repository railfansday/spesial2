Arkansas basketball vs Virginia score, live updates, odds, how to watch

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

WASHINGTON — A grueling nonconference schedule for Arkansas basketball hits its first major road block this weekend in the nation's capital.

The No. 13 Razorbacks (1-0) face No. 10 Virginia (2-0) Sunday, Nov. 8. It will be a homecoming for Jordan Smith Jr. and Cooper Bowser, who both grew up in the DMV area.

This will also be a first test for a young Hogs roster. A rotation filled with seven freshmen will play its first game on national television and meet an opponent with Final Four aspirations. The Cavaliers won 30 games last year in Ryan Odom's first season as head coach. They lost to Tennessee in the Round of 32 of the NCAA Tournament.

Both teams are looking to take a step forward in this season's edition of March Madness. Sunday will be the first measuring stick for the Hogs and Hoos.

The Southwest Times Record is providing live updates throughout the Arkansas vs. Virginia game. Follow along for real-time analysis from Washington.
