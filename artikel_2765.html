Christian Watson won't return against Rams

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Green Bay Packers WR Christian Watson (concussion) exited the Week 12 game against the Los Angeles Rams and will not return after being placed in concussion protocol. Prior to exiting, Watson had four catches for 65 yards and a touchdown.

Why it Matters: Watson was having a strong first half, but a brutal hit that resulted in an ejection will sideline him for the remainder of the contest. At this point, Watson owners can only hope that he doesn't miss additional time. Look for Matthew Golden and Tucker Kraft to pick up the slack for as long as Watson is out.
