Matt O'Riley moves step closer to Brighton exit after what happened vs Universitatea

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Brighton midfielder Matt O'Riley has surely moved a step closer to the exit door after his poor display in his side's 4-0 win over Universitatea Craiova on Thursday night.

The Seagulls kept up their unbeaten record in the UEFA Conference League with a convincing victory over their visitors from Romania.

An early strike from Ibrahim Osman in the third minute set the Premier League side on their way, with Maxim de Cuyper adding to the lead, shortly before the visitors were reduced to 10 men in the 39th minute.

Charalampos Kostoulas got in on the act just after the hour mark, before Osman later added his second of the night to complete the rout.

However, it was O'Riley who was undoubtedly the biggest loser from the evening, as he with withdrawn by Fabian Hurzeler at half-time.

The Danish international has had to fight for minutes at the Amex Stadium so far this term, and he did his chances no favours with his latest performance.

O'Riley struggled to make an impact on the game from the right wing, and it will surely have sealed his fate ahead of the upcoming January transfer window.

O'Riley's long-term future at Brighton has looked bleak throughout the last 12 months, as he was loaned out to Marseille last season, and then hardly featured for his parent club upon his return.

Hurzeler opted to keep the 26-year-old around in the summer as part of his efforts to bolster squad depth for the European campaign.

However, he will surely be frustrated by what he saw from O'Riley on Thursday, despite the victory.

The midfielder completed just 13 passes during his 45 minutes of action, at a poor success rate of 72 per cent, as well as enjoying a success rate of just 50 per cent in his duels.

O'Riley was picked to provide creativity and devilment for the hosts in the final third, but he failed to create a single chance, and completed just one successful dribble.

The fact that Brighton were up against far inferior opposition only served to underline the lack of quality in O'Riley's display.

Hurzeler clearly wasn't happy with his contribution, and given that he has played just 10 minutes of Premier League football so far this term, he is surely a clear candidate for a winter exit.

Former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness recently told Football Insider that Brighton are planning to sell goalkeeper Bart Verbruggen next summer.

The Dutchman is out of contract in 2028, so will have just one year remaining on his contract by the time next summer comes around.

Wyness expects Brighton to create an auction for the 24-year-old, who is already attracting interest from both Liverpool and Real Madrid.

He feels that the Seagulls could potentially secure £60million for the gloveman, who has established himself as one of the Premier League's leading shot-stoppers.
