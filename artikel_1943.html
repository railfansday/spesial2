Man United 'making the same mistakes' as Mauricio Pochettino adds to catalogue of errors

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Mauricio Pochettino's appointment at Manchester United has highlighted their poor decision-making.

The Red Devils have been forced to shell out around £7.5million to buy the manager out of his contract with the USA national team, after being made aware of the release clause.

However, they could have landed him for a significantly cheaper fee should they have made the call before Michael Carrick landed the permanent job during the summer.

As we revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Man United could have easily avoided having to splash out on yet another new manager.

The situation has echoes of Erik ten Hag's tenure, when the boss was handed a bumper new contract just to be sacked months later, costing the Red Devils millions in compensation.

Man United's reluctance to make big decisions has ultimiately cost them hugely financially, but despite having been burnt in the past, they have not learned from their mistakes.

Former Everton CEO Keith Wyness told Football Insider on 11 November that Man United will need more than a change of manager to reverse their recent fortunes.

Man United believe Pochettino will be the man to get them winning games again, despite their miserable start to the season, and were willing to pay big money to get their man.

However, the appointment is reflective of the poor decision-making behind the scenes at Old Trafford, with the board still under scruinty for the job they have done.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, we explained: "It's weird, Man United have got a really poor record when it comes to paying off managers.

"They had the situation with Erik ten Hag where they handed him a new contract and sacked him a few months later, that cost them a big fee.

"They obviously had to pay Ruben Amorim out of his contract. They made a similar mistake with Carrick, handing him a contract then sacking him.

"Now they've had to buy Mauricio Pochettino out of his contract with the USA, that's not been a cheap deal for them to do. It's just poor planning."

Before we cotintued, "It's definitely poor planning, Man United keep making the same mistakes with the managerial turnaround at Old Trafford.

"They're hoping Pochettino can be more of a long-term answer now, they've given him a two-and-a-half-year deal until 2029, and they're hoping he can steady the ship now."

Man United's poor planning in recent years has now forced them to take extra measures to ensure they do not suffer the same issues should Pochettino struggle.

Former Man City financial adviser Stefan Borson told Football Insider that Man United are already trying to reduce the impact of having to sack the manager earlier than expected.

United have now spent over £100million on managers since Sir Alex Ferguson stepped down, and that could increase again in the next couple of years.

Pochettino's contract is understood to be two-and-a-half years long, taking him to the end of the 2028-29 campaign, unless he signs an extension.

That has been flagged as an immediate concern, with a shorter contract meaning his possible dismissal will be more cost-effective than previous managers they've let go.
