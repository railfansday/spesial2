Taekwondo Instructor Allegedly Sexually Assaulted 13-Year-Old, Other Children

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A California taekwondo instructor stands accused of sexually assaulting multiple children, including minors he allegedly abused for several years.

Luis Menjivar Chicas, of Petaluma, was taken into custody during a traffic stop on Saturday, November 14, after a warrant was issued for his arrest, according to a news release from the Petaluma Police Department.

Chicas, 48, is charged with two counts of lewd acts with children, assault with intent to commit a felony upon a juvenile, two counts of continual sexual abuse of a child under age 14, lewd acts with a child by force or fear and five counts of lewd acts with a child under age 14.

The charges stem from an investigation into an alleged sexual assault involving Chicas and a 13-year-old that was reported to Petaluma police in January, according to authorities.

Investigators learned Chicas had been similarly investigated by the San Rosa Police Department in 2024 in connection with alleged sexual assaults of two victims.

The victims told police Chicas had sexually assaulted them for several years when they were minors living in Santa Rosa, according to authorities.

However, police said “there was not enough evidence to proceed with criminal prosecution” at the time.

Then, in April, a different family came forward to report to police that Chicas had allegedly sexually assaulted two additional minors for multiple years, according to police.

Chicas is accused of assaulting the two juveniles while renting a room in their family’s home.

“The three related investigations were completed and forwarded to the Sonoma County District Attorney’s Office for prosecution,” police said.

Investigators learned Chicas was a taekwondo instructor, according to police, who said they are looking into whether he is actively teaching martial arts to students.

Authorities didn’t say where he worked as a taekwondo instructor or for how long.

Information on whether Chicas retained legal counsel wasn’t immediately available.

Following his arrest, Chicas was booked into the Sonoma County jail and had his bail set at $250,000, according to police.

The Petaluma Police Department is continuing to investigate.

The department asks anyone who may have direct knowledge of the alleged sexual assaults to contact Detective Carlos Ramirez at 707-234-4540 or via email at cramirez@cityofpetaluma.org.

Petaluma is about a 40-mile drive northwest from San Francisco.

If you or someone you know has been sexually assaulted, contact the National Sexual Assault Hotline at 1-800-656-HOPE (4673).
