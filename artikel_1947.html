Liverpool 'have already decided' Joe Gomez's future after talks - transfer expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Joe Gomez is facing uncertainty over his future at Liverpool as he edges towards the end of his contract at Anfield.

As things stand, the 29-year-old will be allowed to leave the club on a free transfer come the end of the season, with less than a year remaining on his current deal.

The situation is still up in the air, and there have been suggestions that the versatile defender would be willing to stay if he was offered the opportunity to pen a new deal.

However, former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Liverpool have "already decided" that Gomez will not be offered a contract extension.

The former England international has started just three games so far this season, and is unlikely to play a part in Andoni Iraola's long-term plans.

As a result, there is a growing likelihood that he will be allowed to explore his options upon the expiry of his contract, with Liverpool not planning to keep him around.

Football Insider reported during the summer that Gomez could have been allowed to leave Liverpool in a last-gasp effort to rasie funds from his permanent sale.

However, it was decided that the defender would remain at Anfield for the season given their lack of depth at the back, with his versatility seen as a key asset.

That is unlikely to swing a decision about his long-term future, however, with plans in place to bolster their back line which would force Gomez down the pecking order.

"Liverpool have alreaedy decided they're going to let Joe Gomez leave," Brown told Football Insider.

"He doesn't have much of a role to play at the moment, he's not starting games at right-back even despite their injuries, and he isn't competing for a centre-back position.

"His injuries are always a concern as well, they don't think he can start games consistently, and all of these issues stack up eventually.

"It would be a surprise to me if they were to offer him a new contract, especially when you consider they're going to be looking to bring in new defenders at some stage.

"If he hasn't played much this season, that's not going to change with new players coming in, so I expect he'll be allowed to look at what other options might be available."

Speculation about Gomez's long-term future at Liverpool is also likely to spark rumours of potential interest, with multiple clubs having been long-term admirers of his.

Over the past couple of years, clubs have shown an interest in the defender, with the likes of AC Milan, Brighton and Crystal Palace having been linked at different stages.

Meanwhile, Football Insider reported during the summer that West Ham had been keeping a close eye on Gomez's situation in case he was allowed to leave Liverpool.

The decision was ultimately made to keep him at Anfield, but that is unlikely to have deterred the Hammers' long-term interest should he leave Liverpool for free.

His availability as a free agent is likely to spark further interest, with clubs across Europe keeping tabs on his situation.
