Ex-refs chief drops Bafode Biakite red card verdict after analysing Bournemouth vs Celta Vigo footage

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Referee Harm Osmers shouldn't have needed the help of VAR to issue Bournemouth defender Bafode Diakite with a red card during their 2-1 win away at Celta Vigo on Thursday night.

That is according to ex-PGMOL chief and former FIFA referee Keith Hackett, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the 25-year-old's challenge was an obvious example of serious foul play.

Bournemouth ran out 2-1 winners in Spain despite Diakite's early dismissal, as goals from Alvaro Rodriguez and Rayan saw the visitors come back from Iago Aspas' early strike.

The victory leaves the Cherries sitting top of the Europa League table, having taken maximum points from their opening five games in the competition.

Osmers initially decided that Diakite's lunging challenge on Couhaib Driouech was only worthy of a yellow card, but Hackett has outlined that he was fortunate to have VAR there to intervene.

The German official was sent to the pitch-side monitor to review his decision, where replays showed Diakite planted his studs into the base of Driouech's shin.

Osmers upgraded his decision to a straight red card, and Hackett feels that the incident highlighted the value of VAR once again.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Hackett made it clear that Osmers' initial decision of a yellow card was a "clear and obvious error."

The former official indicated that he would expect a referee of European level to able to detect a poor challenge of that nature from the Bournemouth man in real time.

"It's really good to see the match officials deliver the correct outcome with the intervention of VAR," he exclusively told Football Insider.

"This is a serious foul play challenge, which you would expect the referee to witness on first viewing and show a red card rather than a yellow.

"These challenges should be detected by the on-field referee. Fortunately, VAR rightly determined that the referee's yellow card was a clear and obvious error."

Football Insider revealed earlier in November that Bournemouth have won the race to sign highly-rated Cliftonville starlet Cillian Murray in January.

The Cherries have agreed a deal to sign the 16-year-old, and the move is set to go through once the winter window opens.

The winger recently made his senior debut for Cliftonville, and has previously starred for them in the UEFA Youth League.

Murray is held in extremely high regard behind the scenes in Northern Ireland, and it is Bournemouth who have won the race to handle the next stage of his development.
