Everton could use new commercial agreement to trigger big-money deal - Keith Wyness

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Everton could use their women's team's partnership with JSYK as a test run for a big-money future sponsorship of their men's team.

That is according to former Toffees chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that clubs could potentially share sponsors across men's and women's teams in future.

Everton women confirmed their new partnership with JYSK earlier this week, with the Danish company becoming their new Home Furnishing Retail Partner.

Wyness believes that the deal could potentially be used as a "trial period" for JYSK to strike a similar deal with the men's team in the coming years.

Everton have become a far more attractive proposition since moving to the Hill Dickinson Stadium, and are looking to expand more commercially in the coming years.

Several clubs have had sponsors that have since ceased to exist in recent years, but Wyness has no doubt that JYSK are a far more steady partner.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that Premier League clubs could start using their women's teams as test runs for future sponsorship deals.

He suggested that cheaper, short-term deals with the women's teams would allow clubs and companies to get a feel for each other and their methods before committing to a longer-term agreement.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness outlined that it will be "interesting" to see whether JYSK branch out to Everton's men's team in future.

"It made me think about this in the sense that we're now seeing women's teams sort of being used as a trial for a sponsor and seeing how they get on with the club executive marketing team," he said.

"Now that's an interesting way to look at things that, if you're a sponsor, you're looking at almost a trial period with women's team at a much smaller amount, but then you can see how well the club functions on a marketing level as a partner.

"I think it could be great for Everton, the fact they've got this, you know, we're going to be talking about some sponsors that have gone bust recently.

"Of course, they could expand into the men's team. So I've never looked at it this way before, but perhaps it is time to think about the women's side as a nursery for sponsors going forward."

Football Insider revealed earlier this week that Everton are unlikely to be able to afford the wages needed to tempt former Manchester City midfielder Kevin De Bruyne into signing for them.

Recent reports in Italy have tipped the Merseysiders to make a surprise move for the Napoli midfielder, who is out of contract next summer.

However, sources say that De Bruyne is on a "big deal" in Naples, and Everton are unlikely to be able to get close enough to that figure to sign him.

The Italian giants also have the option to extend the 35-year-old's contract by a further year, creating another obstacle for Everton to overcome.
