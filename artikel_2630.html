Travis Kelce heads to locker room after tackle on Chiefs' first drive vs. Falcons

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Travis Kelce headed back to the Chiefs' locker room at the end of Kansas City's first drive against the Atlanta Falcons. Kelce had caught Patrick Mahomes' first pass of the afternoon for 23 yards, but landed hard on his hip after a diving tackle from Atlanta safety DeMarcco Hellams. Kelce appeared frustrated with the tackle, but no flags were thrown.

On the sideline, cameras caught Kelce grimacing in pain as the Chiefs moved downfield.

The Chiefs later scored on the drive when Mahomes found Emmett Johnson for a 12-yard touchdown pass. Kelce left the field soon after and was listed as questionable to return with an ankle injury.
