Match Report & Player Ratings: Manchester City 2-2 Napoli (UEFA Champions League)

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Following a first-half wherein the home side had dominated possession and created several chances, former Manchester United striker Rasmus Hojlund opened the scoring after 55 minutes before Ayyoub Bouaddi netted his first City goal to restore parity 11 minutes later.

Elliot Anderson had a left-footed strike from close range saved but, from the resulting corner, Jeremy Doku supplied Bouaddi in the centre of the box, with the Morocco international converting his left-footed strike into the net.

Similar defensive frailties were on show for Napoli's second goal, a stunning volley by Noa Lang on the counter-attack, though Manchester City again managed to fight back and earn the point through Rayan Cherki, who made it 2-2 after 86 minutes.

For nearly an hour here, there was hardly any expectation that Napoli were going to break the deadlock, given the tactical changes that Maresca had orchestrated before and during this game. City were direct, powerful and optimistic.

But Hojlund, who had failed to score in four games against City when he was a Manchester United player, made it 1-0 through a goal that will frustrate Maresca, his team and the home supporters immensely.

In an attempt to work the ball out from the back, Marc Guehi, Matheus Nunes and Cherki were all placed under overwhelming pressure before Hojlund, slipped in behind by Billy Gilmour, finished beyond Gianluigi Donnarumma.

It painted a considerably bleak picture for Manchester City due to the chances that they had created throughout this fairly one-sided game up to that point, and even more so because it is the same problems that are proving impossible for City to extinguish.

For the acute decline of the Blues over the past month or so, there have been so many questions around squad selection and tactical stubbornness that have put great pressure on Maresca, who is tirelessly trying to find solutions.

Part of the problem was the lack of intensity and relentlessness at the top of the pitch but, even at the start of this game, it was apparent that Maresca had ordered his players to be more direct; to shoot more and find ways to apply pressure onto Napoli by sending balls into the box as often as possible.

Only 59 seconds into the game, Anderson struck from 24 yards and tested Vanka Milinkovic-Savic, who appeared fairly comfortable in pushing the ball away at his bottom right-hand post.

Anderson, Enzo Fernandez and Cherki were all warming to the game and provided creativity in attacking, sending several crosses into the box, while Nunes delivered a sublime cross to Nico O'Reilly, the left-back's header going over the bar shortly in the opening stages.

In between those chances, McTominay, paired up with Billy Gilmour and Kevin De Bruyne in midfield, wriggled around Anderson and Nunes before having a promising strike deflected out for a corner.
