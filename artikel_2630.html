Knicks pull off Toronto sweep thanks to Jalen Brunson’s late 4-point play

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

TORONTO — Jalen Brunson snatched another victory in Canada with a pivotal sequence.

With 1:25 remaining Tuesday night in a tie game, Brunson pulled up for a 3-pointer that he buried while flagrantly fouled because defender Ja'Kobe Walter set foot in his landing space.

The result was a 4-point play for Brunson and possession retained by the Knicks, who were fouled and quickly made two more free throws to basically put away their 111-102 victory.

Jalen Brunson, who scored a game-high 31 points, shoots over Alijah Martin during the Knicks' 111-102 win over the Raptors on Nov. 17, 2026, in Toronto. Dan Hamilton-Imagn Images

For Brunson, it represented the second time in three nights that he hit the biggest shot in a win over the Raptors.

He finished with 31 points in 34 minutes on 10-for-22 shooting.

The Knicks (8-6) have won 15 straight against the Raptors, a streak dating to 2023.

But Toronto entered Tuesday confident about its chances after stretching the Knicks into overtime Sunday.

Karl-Anthony Towns, who had 16 points and 16 rebounds, slams home a dunk during the Knicks' road win over the Raptors. Dan Hamilton-Imagn Images

"After rewatching and rewatching and rewatching film, we played against the NBA champions and, in my 3 ¹/₂ years here as coach, that's when we came the closest to beating them," Raptors coach Darko Rajaković said. "We're getting better. Just one free throw and the game is completely different."

OG Anunoby, who scored 16 points, slams home a dunk during the Knicks' road win over the Raptors. Getty Images

Once again, though, the Raptors weren't good enough. Karl-Anthony Towns recorded his 14th straight double-double to start the season — a franchise record — with 16 points and 16 rebounds.

Mikal Bridges secured his third straight positive performance with 17 points in 30 minutes.

The Knicks used a 13-0 run at the end of the second quarter to climb back into the game, then reasserted their dominance over the Raptors after Brunson's weighty shot.
