Liverpool hit with concerning injury blow as star forced off early in Champions League clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Liverpool have been hit with a major injury blow as Victor Munoz was forced off in the first half of their Champions League meeting with Club Brugge.

The young Spaniard threw himself into an aggressive challenge on Jorne Spileers shortly before half-time - but came off far worse after appearing to knock knees with the Belgian.

Munoz looked to be in considerable pain as he stayed down and Liverpool’s medical staff were waved onto the pitch. There was a lengthy delay as he received treatment.

He was surrounded by concerned-looking team-mates as a stretcher was brought on to carry him off, but it was waved away and Munoz was able to leave the pitch under his own steam.

The fact he was able to do so suggests his injury may not be too severe, though Munoz still appeared to be in serious discomfort as he made his way to the bench with a pronounced limp.

Rio Ngumoha was brought on in his place, and Andoni Iraola will issue an update on his condition at full-time.

Liverpool were one to the good when Munoz left the pitch, after Dominik Szoboszlai teed Alexander Isak up to score from the edge of the six-yard box with 13 minutes on the clock.
