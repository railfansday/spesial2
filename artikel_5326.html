T2 Mobile Partners with Ribbon to Secure and Manage Enterprise Zoom Communications

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Delivering secure cloud connectivity, end-to-end visibility, and operational control

PLANO, Texas, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Ribbon Communications Inc. (Nasdaq: RBBN), a global leader in real-time communications technology, IP routing, and optical networking solutions, today announced that Nigeria's T2 Mobile has selected Ribbon's voice security and analytics solutions to support secure connectivity for its suite of enterprise Zoom services.

"Ribbon's expertise is a tremendous asset as we work to deliver reliable, secure digital experiences for our customers and build the infrastructure to support the next phase of our transformation and expanded use of cloud communications," said John Vasikaran, COO, T2Mobile. "Ribbon offered the combination of capabilities and expertise we were looking for."

T2 Mobile is deploying Ribbon's virtualized, Zoom-certified SBC SWe in a high-availability configuration, both on-premises and in Microsoft Azure. The state-of-the-art solution provides connectivity to Zoom Workplace, giving T2 Mobile's customers a market-leading offering for unified communications and omnichannel Contact Center.

Ribbon Analytics complements this deployment by providing T2 Mobile with a centralized, end-to-end view of network traffic, status, and call flows. This visibility enables operations teams to identify and diagnose QoS issues, detect potential threats, troubleshoot problems, and manage security policies.

"We're proud to support T2 Mobile as it builds a world-class communications environment. Together, we're making it easy for T2 Mobile customers to adopt Zoom Workplace, giving these organizations the tools they need to increase productivity and compete more effectively," said Lior Tourgeman, VP Sales, MEA, from Ribbon. "We look forward to expanding this relationship."

About RibbonRibbon Communications (Nasdaq: RBBN) is a global provider of voice communications software, IP routing, and optical networking to mobile and wireline service providers, enterprises, critical infrastructure and defense sectors. We support our customers' Path to Autonomous Networks by leveraging the latest AIOps automation platforms and Agentic AI technologies, helping them deliver better customer experiences, reduce operational costs, and achieve sustainable growth.
