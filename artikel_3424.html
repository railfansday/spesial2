'GTA VI' Launch Plagued by Bugs as Gamers Share Wild Glitch Videos

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

After 13 years of waiting, "Grand Theft Auto VI" is finally here ... but gamers are already finding out the biggest heist might've been their money, with the highly anticipated game crawling with bugs and glitches.

Rockstar Games released the long-awaited title Thursday ... and it didn't take long for players to start flooding social media with videos of some pretty wild technical problems.

One gamer posted a clip claiming he'd stumbled upon a "ground breaking glitch" less than 15 minutes into playing ... showing his character apparently trapped inside a fenced-off area with no way out.

And that's just the beginning ... another player shared footage of a character swimming sideways through the water, giving a whole new meaning to going with the flow.

Other players have reported characters falling through the map, walking through walls, and vehicles behaving in ways that definitely weren't in the trailers.

The rocky rollout comes after fans spent more than a decade waiting for the follow-up to 2013's "Grand Theft Auto V" ... with the new game taking players back to Vice City in the fictional state of Leonida.

Of course, glitches aren't exactly unheard of when a massive video game launches ... but after 13 years of anticipation, some players probably expected a smoother getaway.

Looks like Rockstar has a few things to clean up in Vice City.
