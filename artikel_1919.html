Stefan Borson 'amused' by Mauricio Pochettino update ahead of Liverpool vs Man United

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Mauricio Pochettino taking charge of two more USA games before joining Manchester United is a rather "amusing" update.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that the new manager won't be able to have a key impact either way.

Pochettino has now been confirmed as Michael Carrick's replacement at Old Trafford, but fans will have to wait for him to be unveiled.

That is because USA are facing Haiti twice in CONCACAF play-off matches in the next five days, and as part of the deal, he will remain in charge for them.

The Red Devils, on the other hand, face Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday, 22 November. Man United have been on the winning side more recently.

Pochettino will be thrown straight into the deep end here, a game which could already have an impact on how the fanbase feel about his appointment.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson has admitted that he finds the timing of Pochettino's appointment "amusing".

"It's amusing that you wait for an international break to change the manager and then choose a manager that's not actually available until the end of the international break," he said.

"But then again, most of the players... You would think most of the players are not there anyway. So it probably makes very little difference.

"With the Liverpool game, it's not going to be about Pochettino's tactics. It's going to be about whether they get that new manager bounce with the players trying to impress him.

"They're almost playing for themselves. And tactically, he's not going to be able to impose anything in particular. The reality is that he's been focused, I'm sure, on how Haiti play, rather than Liverpool."

Back at Old Trafford, all eyes will be on getting a positive result against Liverpool, and Pochettino won't need to be told the importance of doing so.

Football Insider understands that the 54-year-old will take charge of his first training session on Thursday, 19 October, once USA's games are over.

Carlos Baleba, Marcus Rashford and Luke Shaw are all recovering from injury, so Pochettino may be able to work with them once he arrives.

But as for the bulk of the squad, he will be waiting for most of them to return from international duty before really getting to work at Carrington.
