‘Saturday Night Live’: Robert De Niro & Timothée Chalamet Will Close Out The Year As Hosts

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Saturday Night Live has set its final hosts for 2026. Robert De Niro will return for his fourth stint as host of SNL on December 12. He’ll be joined by musical guest Slayyyter. And Timothée Chalamet also will return for his fourth time as host on December 19, the final episode of 2026, with musical guest Charli xcx.

As previously announced, Amanda Seyfried will make her Saturday Night Live hosting debut in the December 5 episode, joined by musical guest Don Toliver. Seyfried will be promoting her new movie, Netflix’s animated Steps and also stars as Elizabeth Holmes in Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim’s hit documentary You Can See Everything. Oscar winner De Niro currently stars in Focker-in-Law, alongside Ben Stiller and Ariana Grande, the fourth installment of the Meet the Parents franchise, which hits theaters on November 25. Timothée Chalamet will make his fourth appearance hosting SNL on December 19. The Golden Globe winner stars in Dune: Part Three, which releases December 18.

Charli xcx will return as musical guest for the fourth time. The artist is currently nominated for three Grammys, including her work for Wuthering Heights, and is set to launch the European leg of her “Music, Fashion, Film” tour next year.

In addition to the broadcast on NBC (11:30 p.m. ET / 8:30 p.m. PT), SNL streams live on Peacock.

Saturday Night Live is produced in association with Broadway Video. The creator and executive producer is Lorne Michaels.
