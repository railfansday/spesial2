Why Intuit Stock Crashed Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Investors are selling Intuit -- but the stock looks cheap.

By Rich Smith – Nov 20, 2026 at 12:44PM EST

Intuit (INTU -10.75%) stock tumbled 8.3% through 12:15 p.m. ET Friday despite beating earnings estimates last night.

Analysts forecast that Intuit would earn only $2.70 per share on $4.31 billion in sales in its fiscal Q1 2027. Intuit actually earned $2.91 per share (pro forma) on $4.35 billion in sales.

CEO Sasan Goodarzi called the results "solid," with revenue rising 12% year over year, led by the Global Business Solutions division with 15% growth. Consumer revenue underperformed at 7% growth, and the company's Mailchimp marketing platform saw revenue decline 1%.

(Don't worry about that last bit, though, because Intuit -- perhaps disappointed by the business's performance -- now says it is selling Mailchimp to Bending Spoons (BSP +0.84%) for approximately $3.7 billion, paid $3.3 billion in cash plus $368 million in Bending Spoons stock.)

As regards earnings, the "$2.91" was only non-GAAP. Actual earnings, calculated under generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP), for the quarter were only $2.59 -- but still up 63% year over year.

Intuit did give investors GAAP guidance on what to expect for the rest of its fiscal 2027, but the news wasn't quite as good. Revenue is forecast between $23.1 billion and $23.4 billion -- no more than 9% growth, again led by Global Business Solutions. Earnings per share are expected to grow less quickly than in Q1 -- but still pretty quick, rising about 23% to $20.24 per share.

Assuming Intuit hits that mark, at its current $284 share price, the stock would be trading at only 14 times current-year earnings -- quite cheap for a 23% grower.

The bad news is that most analysts doubt Intuit can keep growing at this rate long-term. Average analyst forecasts are closer to 13.5% growth. Between the growth rate and the 1.8% dividend yield, though, I still see Intuit stock as at worst fairly priced -- and probably a bit of a bargain.
