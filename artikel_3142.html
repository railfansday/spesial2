Mativ Holdings, Inc. (MATV -1.90%) reported a third quarter characterized by significant growth in its advanced materials segment alongside external operational disruptions. Management reported that the Filtration & Advanced Materials segment achieved its strongest organic sales growth in history, supported by the commercialization of specialty films for low Earth orbit satellite constellations and demand for automotive paint protection films. These gains were offset by a tornado in Wisconsin that destroyed inventory and necessitated a transition to an inefficient interim distribution network, as well as new 50% tariffs on Canadian tape exports. The company stated that despite these temporary headwinds, underlying demand remains stable and the business continues to prioritize debt reduction and working capital efficiency to reach its long-term leverage targets.
