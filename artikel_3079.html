Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Guidance was cut and the CEO is leaving, but orders are surging.

Vistance Networks, Inc. (VISN -15.14%) reported third quarter results that included a reduction in full-year guidance as management navigates supply chain constraints and shifting customer deployment schedules. The company announced that President and CEO Chuck Treadway will step down on Feb. 1, 2027, following a multiyear transformation that resulted in a debt-free balance sheet and the return of $4 billion to shareholders in 2026. Management stated that while near-term results are impacted by cyclicality and memory chip pricing, the business is focused on the DOCSIS 4.0 upgrade cycle and expanding into security and PON solutions. The company ended the quarter with a strong liquidity position of $842 million to support ongoing organic investments.
