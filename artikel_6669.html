11 Pumpkin-Free Recipes That Still Taste Like Fall

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Say gourd-bye to your least favorite ingredient.

Fall is by far my favorite season. It means a reprieve from the summer heat, so I get to pull out my cutest sweaters and jackets for the cooler days. Best of all, I get to cuddle up in my warm blanket with some cozy and comforting fall dishes. The one problem? I’m constantly trying to avoid pumpkins.

I am not a fan of the gourd, but it’s in nearly every fall recipe I want to try out. If you’re a pumpkin hater like me, here are 11 great fall recipes with no pumpkin in sight. We have got great options for easy, scrumptious weeknight dinners, plus pumpkin-free desserts to satisfy your family's sweet tooth, so pumpkin haters can still get the cozy fall vibes all season long.

It’s so appealing to prepare a meal that comes together in one pan, not just because it’s easy and a flavorful way to cook, but you can customize what goes in depending on the season. This skillet combines chicken thighs with apples and cranberries—the perfect duo of sweet and tangy for this fall dinner.

You’ll love this pecan pie french toast casserole for its layers of soft, pillowy bread topped with a crunchy, brown sugar-glossed pecan topping. Whether you’re a pecan pie lover who needs an excuse for a morning hit, or you need a holiday breakfast with a little seasonal flair, this French toast casserole should be on your list.

Pumpkin may get the majority of the spotlight when it comes to fall flavors, but I'm here to declare that sweet potato deserves its chance to shine. My favorite example? These crispy sweet potato bites. They're the appetizer of the season—perfect for any occasion when you want to make something that’s show-stopping but don’t wanna deal with cutlery.

If you love stovetop mac 'n' cheese, you’ve got to try this creamy butternut squash orzo. Butternut squash and Parmesan join forces with orzo to create a cheesy pasta skillet that hits every nostalgic note.

Filled with tender apples and drizzled with a creamy brown butter frosting, these brown butter blondies are the definition of an irresistible, packable fall treat. Plus, if you’re not already on the brown butter train, these blondies will have you on board in two seconds flat.

Not all sheet-pan dinners are created equal. Sheet-pan pork chops with apples falls into the camp of excellence, packing in flavor with a short ingredient list onto one pan, all of which comes together in just over a half an hour. The pork chops pair perfectly with apples and onions, a savory sweet combination that balances the rich meat.

If you're looking for the coziest fall dinner, look no further than this butternut squash ravioli. With a creamy butternut squash filling and a brown butter sauce, this ravioli is the perfect solution when you're in need of a comforting dinner that will truly wow.

The first sight of apple cider donuts at farmers’ markets signals the arrival of fall and the best time of year. A warm, freshly baked donut, sweet and well spiced, and coated in cinnamon sugar will soon be at the top of your list of favorite fall things. These apple cider donuts are baked, which yields a soft, dense donut, and once brushed in butter, it’s just as good as any fried donut.

We’re so eager for apple cheddar season that we made a casserole version of our bundles. If you love an apple cheddar pastry, we assure you that you’ll enjoy an eggy breakfast version too. When it comes to this casserole, think breakfast strata with buttery, caramelized apples, and lots of sharp cheddar.

These irresistible cheesecake bars are like traditional cheesecake’s cool older sister, and she’s invited to your holiday party this year. These bars perfectly balance creamy, rich cheesecake with the tartness of fresh cranberries and the nuttiness and warmth of a pecan streusel topping, with none of the intensive labor required for traditional cheesecake.
