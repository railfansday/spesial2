Official ‘KPop Demon Hunters’ Advent Calendars Arrive for Christmas, With 24 Days of Gifts to Uncover

The official holiday releases include features accessories, jewelry, stationery and toys inspired by the hit animated film.

The KPop Demon Hunters merch train is rolling into the holidays with the release of an official advent calendar inspired by the hit Netflix animated film.

From collectibles brand, Get Trend, the 2026 KPop Demon Hunters Advent Calendar features 24 days of movie-themed gifts to unbox, from hair accessories to fashion jewelry to small toys. Everything comes hidden in a box measuring approximately 8 x 8 inches, with 24 slots to open — one for each day leading up to Christmas.

Among the items to collect: beaded bracelets, hair clips and a compact mirror featuring the HUNTR/X trio of Rumi, Mira and Zoey; cozy socks with in movie colors and motifs; and keychains, rings and necklaces in a — yes — golden colorway. There’s even a KPop Demon Hunters-themed tote bag to hold all your collectibles in.

You can also pick up this advent calendar, with stationery, stickers, toys and other accessories.

Open one door daily to unveil gifts like a KPop Demon Hunters-themed ruler, pencil sharpener, paper clips and tape, along with accessories like a Derby-themed sleep mask, socks and a cord friendship bracelet. There’s even a mini puzzle featuring an image of the HUNTR/X trio eating their favorite cup noodles.

Nordstrom also has a KPop Demon Hunters Advent Calendar that celebrates the 12 Days of Christmas.

This jewelry-themed release has a different accessory tucked behind each of the 12 doors, with gifts including a gold-tone bracelet, necklace and 10 colorful charms inspired by the characters and motifs from the film. Act fast: this one is 70% off at Nordstrom.com right now.

All of the above advent calendars are officially licensed by Netflix and arrive in time for holiday delivery. The Christmas calendars come on the heels of KPop Demon Hunters dolls, action figures and plush toy releases.

While the movie was originally a Netflix streaming exclusive, you can now pick up a physical copy of the film on Blu-ray/DVD with a special Criterion Collection 4K release, available on Amazon.

It’s hard to believe it’s been over a year since KPop Demon Hunters arrived on the small screen. The animated musical premiered on Netflix in June 2025 and went on to become a global phenomenon, including winning the Academy Award for Best Animated Film.
