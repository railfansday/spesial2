Why Giants might be in store for underwhelming NFL trade deadline

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Why Giants might be in store for underwhelming NFL trade deadline originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The New York Giants are heading into NFL trade deadline day with a few needs worth exploring. Running back is an obvious need, as is their defensive tackle position.

They could look to upgrade their wide receiver room, or other areas of the roster, but DT and RB are the more likely spots John Harbaugh's team will try to upgrade. However, the question is how big of a move this team will make.

According to ESPN's Jordan Raanan, the Giants might be in for an underwhelming NFL trade deadline. Thanks to the sky-high price tag of good players, the Giants might not make a big trade by the 4:00 p.m. trade deadline.

"The trade deadline is 4 p.m. this afternoon. If Monday showed us anything, prices are high. Jaguars had to really pay for deals," Raanan writes. "The Giants have been active making calls and are trying to add to the roster. DT and RB remain the most likely options."

That first part of Raanan's report is the main takeaway for the Giants heading into deadline day. The price tag of elite players is incredibly high, maybe too high for the Giants.

"But as John Harbaugh said Sunday, they know where they are as a team. Don't want to overpay. Already used their 4th and 5th-round picks in deals," Raanan writes. "Making a big trade by this afternoon is going to be difficult and seems unlikely."

The Giants making a blockbuster trade, whether it's for someone like RB Jonathan Taylor or DT Derrick Brown, is not looking very likely right now.

It's too soon to completely rule the Giants out from making a big move, especially if they don't have to mortgage the future to make a deal. But coming into deadline day, that's the price tag of these elite players.

The Philadelphia Eagles traded Jordan Davis to the Jacksonville Jaguars for a 2027 first-round pick and a 2029 second-round pick. That's a massive package this Giants team is not likely to spend on someone, even if that player is great.

While the Giants might make a trade or two, potentially getting some extra depth at running back and defensive tackle, a blockbuster addition is not looking likely.

At 5-2-1, the Giants are going to be buyers. But their actual deadline additions might not be nearly as exciting as those of other contenders, like what the Chicago Bears and Jaguars have done recently.
