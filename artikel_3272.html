Easterly Government Properties: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The company is simultaneously growing earnings and shrinking debt, a rare combination that positions it for investment-grade status.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings conference call, held on Oct. 26, 2026, Easterly Government Properties (DEA +1.52%) reported 2.6% year-over-year growth in core funds from operations per share, a metric that tracks repeatable real estate performance. The real estate investment trust relies on long-term leases backed by U.S. government agencies, providing stable cash flows across economic cycles. Three takeaways from the call highlight what matters for long-term investors.

Easterly raised the low end of its 2026 core funds from operations guidance from $3.08 to $3.13 per share. Management expects full-year growth of roughly 4%, outpacing its historical target range of 2% to 3%.

The company kept its portfolio 98% leased, with a weighted-average lease term of 9.2 years. Management actively reduced soft-term leases, which give government tenants early exit rights, to protect long-term rental revenue.

Easterly lowered its net debt to annualized quarterly earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization ratio to 7.1 times. Reducing leverage while expanding earnings strengthens the balance sheet and supports the pursuit of an investment-grade credit rating.

Government demand remains resilient through market shifts. Easterly relies on mission-critical properties to protect cash flow while evaluating a $1.5 billion acquisition pipeline and pursuing an investment-grade rating.

Initial 2027 guidance assumes $50 million in acquisitions and $25 million to $50 million in development spending.
