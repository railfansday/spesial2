OG Anunoby Drops ‘NYC Blue’ Skechers Sneakers, Inspired by Pair He Wore for Knicks Championship Run

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Rolling Stone may receive an affiliate commission.

New York Knicks star OG Anunoby made headlines during the team’s NBA championship run last season when it was revealed that he wears Skechers basketball shoes on the court. Now, the Knicks guard and power forward is teaming up with the footwear brand on a new pair of sneakers that pay tribute to the city and team he helped bring a trophy to.

Skechers Basketball and Anunoby have just dropped the SKX Nexus “NYC Blue,” a limited-edition pair of sneakers released in the same colorway worn by OG during his now-legendary game-winning tip-in that helped propel New York to an NBA Championship.

The “NYC Blue” colorway pays tribute to the shade of blue seen in the Knicks’ uniforms, while orange laces and accents complete the official team colors.

The shoes themselves feature a breathable and flexible mesh upper, enhanced ankle support, 5GEN midsole cushioning with torsion plate, and a grippy Goodyear outsole for traction on the court. OG’s logo is seen on the sockliner and embroidered on the tongue, with his signature on the heel for an extra personalized touch.

Skechers says these are shoes designed to boost performance, with lightweight materials, a supportive silhouette, an cushy insole for added comfort, and a “strategically-designed forefoot traction pattern” for precise quick cuts.

The limited-edition sneakers ship in a custom-designed box and come with a set of collectible enamel pins with Knicks motifs. Skechers has the unisex basketball shoes available in men’s sizes 4 to 17.

While Anunoby’s Player Edition basketball shoe is now available to shop online at Skechers.com, it’s been a long time coming — the SKX Nexus NYC Blue was first announced for pre-orders in June.

In press materials, Skechers says “This release marks a historic milestone, representing the brand’s first-ever championship win and a defining moment in Skechers Basketball’s rapid rise in the performance space.”

Anunoby signed a multi-year footwear and apparel endorsement deal with Skechers in July 2025.
