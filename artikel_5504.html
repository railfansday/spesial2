InventHelp Inventor Develops Self Operating Confined Space Attendant (SGM-765)

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

PITTSBURGH, Nov. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- "I have been in the agriculture field for 5 years. This time has allowed me to witness many violations to OSHA rules. I thought there should be an autonomous confined space attendant," said an inventor from Newtown, Indiana. As a result, he developed the patent-pending SOCSA SELF OPERATING CONFINED SPACE ATTENDANT, designed specifically to replace the human factors such as lack of attention, poor decision making, inaction or lack of training, and failure to recognize hazards.

InventHelp Invention Spotlight: SOCSA SELF OPERATING CONFINED SPACE ATTENDANT Solves Safety Issues

InventHelp Client's Invention for All Industries

With more industries embracing added safety measures, there may be a demand for a partially autonomous confined space attendant. The SOCSA SELF OPERATING CONFINED SPACE ATTENDANT offers an innovative design that is easy to employ.
