Why Britney Spears' Biopic Team Is 'Taking Their Time' Adapting Memoir

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

More than two years after Universal Pictures secured the highly coveted rights to Britney Spears‘ memoir, The Woman in Me, for a reported eight-figure sum, the film adaptation remains in its early stages.

“Britney’s biopic is still being written, but it’s coming together,” a source exclusively tells Us. “Everything is moving in the right direction.”

Wicked‘s Jon M. Chu is directing the movie based on Spears’ bestselling 2023 memoir while New Girl creator Elizabeth Meriwether is writing the screenplay and La La Land‘s Marc Platt is producing. The “Toxic” singer, 44, will have creative input.

“The team behind the film wants to do it justice, so they’re taking their time,” the insider shares. “They know all eyes will be on it when it comes out, so they don’t want to get anything wrong.”

In the meantime, Meriwether, 45, has been doing her homework. She told The Hollywood Reporter in August that she has read The Woman in Me “multiple times” — both a physical copy and the audiobook narrated by Michelle Williams, with whom she worked on Hulu’s Dying for Sex.

“That audiobook, if people haven’t listened to it, is one of the best audiobooks that’s ever been made,” Meriwether raved. “Michelle is unbelievable. It’s like a monologue. I told her that her performance of the audiobook broke my heart in so many ways. She’s being true to Britney and also telling a story in the way she’s reading it.”

In addition to rereading the source material, Meriwether shared that she has been listening to Spears’ music “a lot” while writing the script.

“I have been going back into her whole catalog, and I’ve been making a playlist for myself of my favorites,” she told the outlet. “I don’t have one. But I really love ‘Perfume’ and ‘If I’m Dancing.’ She’s made a lot of albums. Her hits have been such big hits that I think there are a lot of songs that people don’t think of.”

Chu, 47, later teased to Variety that he has already read some of Meriwether’s screenplay and thought it was “incredible, profound, beautiful.” He added that he has also started going through Spears’ discography “from the very beginning,” naming her early songs “…Baby One More Time” and “Soda Pop” as some of his favorites so far.

As for who will portray the legendary Princess of Pop, the source tells Us, “There have been very preliminary, informal conversations about casting, but they won’t officially start the search until the script is done.”

Us has reached out to Spears’ reps for comment.
