Jaissle Must Be Licking His Lips: Euro Club Eye Swap Deal to Sign Newcastle Star

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Juventus are considering offering Francisco Conceicao to Newcastle United in a potential swap deal for Nick Woltemade.

Woltemade, 24, joined Juventus on loan for a £3.5million fee in September after struggling during his first season at the Magpies.

Yoane Wissa started the campaign as Newcastle's starting centre forward, and with William Osula acting as the direct back-up, Woltemade dropped down to third-choice in the pecking order. Matias Fernandez-Pardo then signed in a £51million deal from Lille, leaving Woltemade's future at St James' Park under serious threat.

An exit route opened up soon after, with Woltemade moving to Juve on a season-long loan. Following a difficult start to life at the Allianz Stadium, the ex-Stuttgart man has hit some form in recent weeks, scoring five times and grabbing three assists in 12 games.

Woltemade found the net and assisted Teun Koopmeiners in Juve's thumping 4-0 win against Venezia on Sunday afternoon, and it's safe to suggest he is well appreciated at the Italian club.

With goals in back-to-back Serie A matches and three in four in the Europa League, Luciano Spalletti is delighted with Woltemade's progress right now and would like to keep him there long-term.

Indeed, according to Gazetta dello Sport, Juventus are preparing to offer Conceicao as part of a player-plus-cash swap deal for Woltemade.

Offering Conceicao in a potential move would take a sizeable chunk off the fee Newcastle would demand for Woltemade, who cost £69m just 15 months ago.

Due to his age and potential, Newcastle would want to recoup as much of that fee as possible, but with Conceicao, 23, added to the mix, Juve may be able to bring the total cost right down.

Alongside that option, Juve could extend his loan for a further season with a buy option for the following summer, should he continue to be out of Matthias Jaissle's plans at Newcastle.

This is a deal that makes sense for all parties involved. With Wissa, Osula and Fernandez-Pardo, Newcastle don't need Woltemade. As well as that, Jaissle's fast-paced and high-press style of football doesn't suit the big German, so it would be better for him to move on.

When it comes to Conceicao, do Newcastle need him? Well, the Magpies are desperate for a new right-winger, so from that perspective they would take him.

Jacob Murphy started on the right in Saturday's 2-2 draw against Arsenal, but he was substituted at half-time for Anthony Elanga after struggling in the opening 45 minutes.

Elanga made an impact, but Newcastle could do with another option and an upgrade on the right side with Murphy, who attracted interest from Nottingham Forest in the summer, moving on.

Conceicao's numbers are far from impressive, having scored just once and grabbed one assist in nine matches this season, but he has shown he has potential and quality in the final third.

A technical winger with outstanding close control and an eye for a pass, Conceicao has talent and Jaissle could be the one to get that out of him. But, due to his form for Juventus, Newcastle shouldn't be spending more than £25m on him, with his injury record a huge concern as well.

If Newcastle could sell Woltemade and receive Conceicao plus £30m then they would have done well, and got the better hand of the deal. Any less then the Magpies should reconsider and either sell Woltemade for a straight fee or negotiate and let him go on loan again.

From Juventus' perspective, they want to keep Woltemade but do have some financial restrictions up against them, meaning they are unable to pay over the odds. A player-plus-cash transfer would be perfect for Spalletti's side in that case, and they should push to get that done.

Newcastle have wasted a fortune on one star this summer, with Matthias Jaissle clearly not a fan of an Eddie Howe signing.
