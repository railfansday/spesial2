Suki Waterhouse Just Revealed a Major Update About Her Super-Private Relationship With Robert Pattinson

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

After years of rumors, Suki finally set the record straight.

Robert Pattinson and Zendaya have a lot in common: They’re both Earth signs, they’ve both starred in superhero movies, they both appear in three of this year’s biggest movies (The Drama, The Odyssey, and Dune: Part Three), and—most importantly—no one could figure out whether either one of them was married...until pretty recently, that is.

It’s been a minute since Robert and his longtime love, Suki Waterhouse, welcomed their first child together (and nearly three years since they got engaged), but Suki FINALLY cleared up her relationship status with Robert—and unlike Zendaya and Tom Holland, they’re not actually married!

During a new interview with the Sunday Times, Suki confirmed that she and Robert haven’t yet tied the knot and aren’t ruling out a “massive” wedding. “It would either be all or nothing,” she said of their upcoming nuptials. “If we were going to go for it, we’d have a massive party, blow it out.”

In light of this new development, I think the time has come for a handy journey through their relationship timeline, starting at the very beginning.

Robert and Suki are spotted for the first time, and the PDA is On. Another. Level. By which I mean they’re seen kissing after a showing of Mamma Mia! Here We Go Again (wow, hot) in some pics published by E! News.

“Along the way, they were very close and cuddled and kissed many times,” an eyewitness says at the time. “Robert was very affectionate to her and had his arm around her the whole way and Suki reciprocated by holding his hand and kissing it.”

Cute, but FYI: This same month, another source downplays the relationship to E! News, saying Suki and Robert are simply “casually dating.”

The Daily Mail out of nowhere, randomly reports that Robert and Suki have broken up. Which, nope! But before long, Lainey Gossip disputes the claim, pointing out that on top of being seen together at a Miguel show in L.A., Robert and Suki both attended TIFF at the same time:

The couple is spotted leaving a Christmas party at 5 a.m. in photos published by the good ol’ Daily Mail.

At this point, Robert and Suki are getting papped a ton and are seen enjoying a stroll through London in photos published by People.

Robert is interviewed by the Sunday Times and makes it clear that he will not be talking about Suki:

“Do I have to [talk about her]? If you let people in, it devalues what love is,” he says. “If a stranger on the street asked you about your relationship, you’d think it extremely rude. If you put up a wall, it ends up better...I can’t understand how someone can walk down the street holding hands, and it’s the same as when I do it and a hundred people are taking your photo.”

He concludes with, “The line between when you’re performing and when you’re not will eventually get washed away and you’ll go completely mad.” Fair!!

Robert and Suki get papped while celebrating his birthday in photos published by People. In other words, they are Still! Going! Strong!

The pair is spotted on a double date with, um, Taylor Swift and Joe Alwyn (?!?!?!) at San Vicente Bungalows in Hollywood, where a source tells Us Weekly they “were laughing and talking” all night and “were at a table, just the four of them.” Oh, and apparently, “Rob and Taylor were across from each other,” in case you were curious about the seating arrangement for whatever reason.

Robert and Suki make a suuuper-rare public appearance together at a Dior dinner during Paris Fashion Week.

And naturally, engagement rumors are sparked thanks to that ring ^^^ on Suki’s finger.

While chatting with GQ, Robert lets it slip that he’s staying “with his girlfriend” in a London apartment during quarantine.

Suki and Robert are spotted making out on a bench, as ya do!

A source tells E! News that Robert and Suki have “definitely discussed” getting engaged but that “Suki is not pressuring him and they are both very committed to each other.”

Fans are convinced there’s a picture of Robert and Suki kissing in the background of this solo Instagram she posts:

In a since-deleted tweet, Suki criticizes the Gossip Girl reboot for using this line: “He’s R-Patz and you’re Suki Nobody.”

“Another day to be reminded that women can also be the patriarchy. Seeing critiques of the patriarchy and sexism, then I get name-checked as somebody’s nobody girlfriend,” she says at the time. “Make it make sense.”

Robert once again refers to Suki as his girlfriend in a GQ interview, saying she couldn’t stop telling a man who had come to fix the boiler about his Batman role. “I’m sitting there facing the other direction, and my girlfriend just keeps continuing the conversation with him,” he recalls. “And I’m looking at her like, ‘Shut the fuck up. Why are you doing this to me?’ She was very entertaining. Just talking to an obsessive fan.”

He also talks about Suki’s reaction to the movie during an interview with Jimmy Kimmel, saying, “It was really her reaction that kind of changed the entire thing because I’m pretty sure she’s not normally into watching superhero movies. And just seeing that it was capturing her attention the entire time and then she held my hand and touched it [to her face] and I could feel a little tear. And I was like, ‘No way!’”

After more than four years of coupledom, Robert and Suki (*fiiiiinally*) make their red carpet debut at the Dior Men Fall 2023 show in Giza, Egypt, where they are the epitome of Fashion Goals™️ (she wears a mauve dress, he wears a silver-toned jacket over a v chill turtleneck):

In addition to making their ’ship red carpet official, Robert and Suki pose for several loved-up pics at the event.

Suki makes a rare comment about Robert during an interview with The Times, saying, “I’m shocked that I’m so happy with someone for nearly five years. I had two days off the other day and it was, like, no question I’m going back to see him. Then he’ll come out for a couple of days.”

She also says, “Rob definitely isn’t getting advice from me about acting, but of course I’ll try and get him to help me with an audition before he falls asleep on the sofa.” Oh, and elsewhere in the interview, the model *also* muses on how they both grew up in Southwest London and ended up together after having such similar journeys in the entertainment industry: “We always say that I never ever would have thought I’d go out with a boy from Barnes and he didn’t think he’d go out with a girl from Chiswick.”

Suki and Robert make their Met Gala debut!

These two step out at the GO Campaign’s annual GO Gala in Los Angeles—a pretty rare appearance!

Suki confirms she's pregnant while on stage at the Corona Capital Festival in Mexico and gushes, “I’m extra sparkly today because I thought it might distract you from something else that’s going on. I'm not sure if it’s working.”

Robert and Suki spark engagement rumors yet again in pics published by TMZ of the couple walking around London and the model specifically wearing a ring on *that* finger.

But it doesn’t take long for fans to find out the truth because, not long after, a source F-I-N-A-L-L-Y confirms that the duo is engaged! “They are engaged. They both want to be married. It’s important for them,” the insider dishes to People at the time.

The celebs officially welcome their first baby!!! And according to pics published by the Daily Mail, they’re already comfortable being out and about and walking their baby in a stroller (which btw is pink 🥺).

Suki posts the first photo of her newborn baby to Instagram, writing, “Welcome to the world angel ❤️.”

That same month, the new parents show up to Coachella!

And Suki confirms on stage that they welcomed a baby girl:

During an interview with British Vogue for the outlet’s August cover, Suki reveals that she and Robert met six years ago at a game night in L.A. and notes that the activity is “one of the things people do here to congregate.”

“I was sure that I’d met him a long time ago, but he didn’t think that we had,” she begins before detailing the events of the night.

On the docket was Werewolf (“think the The Traitors, and if you haven’t seen that, then wink murder”). “It was very, very intense...There were lots of ‘big’ characters, real heavy hitters...Al Pacino was there. Javier [Bardem] and Penélope [Cruz] were there…and, you know, everyone was really acting," she adds.

Except for Suki and Rob, obvi, who found themselves drawn to each other that night. “I think we both sort of have the same slight uncomfortable-ness,” the model reveals before adding that she and her now-fiancé “started giggling at the absurdity of the whole thing.”

The two didn't end up exchanging numbers at the get-together; however, they *did* run into each other nearly a year later. And the rest is history, people!

Oh, and during another part of the interview, Suki confesses that she and Rob fully planned on having their baby: “No, we really planned it...One day we looked at each other and said, ‘Well, this is as ready as we’re going to be.’” “I was like, ‘What can make more chaos?’” she hilariously adds.

Rob and Suki attend the Veuve Clicquot Polo Classic—making their first red carpet appearance as a couple since the birth of their daughter—and ofc, they look very, very chic.

Rob gives his own take on the night he and Suki met—where, apparently, he and Suki didn’t take Werewolf as seriously as the other celeb guests would have liked. “She was sitting opposite me,” Rob says of Suki during an interview with The New York Times before confirming that Javier Bardem, Penélope Cruz, and Al Pacino were indeed all present.

He continues, “Suki and I kept making each other laugh, to the point where someone told us we weren’t taking the game seriously enough. That was a very, very sweet moment.”

A tipster writes in to gossip site DeuxMoi, claiming Rob and Suki tied the knot in the Caribbean on New Year’s Eve.

“Robert Pattinson and Suki Waterhouse got married at NYE at the Caribbean resort I work at!!!” the source claims. “They were so cute and sweet, their baby was there in a gorgeous little bridesmaids dress.”

The insider adds that “their family was there as well” and it was “such a cute wedding”—plus, Rob and Suki were reportedly “so nice.”

Soon after, Sharon Stone hosts the American Heart Association Red Dress Collection Concert, where she introduces Suki to the stage to perform and mentions that Suki gave up time with her “10-month-old baby” and her “husband” to be there, per In Touch—and apparently, Suki didn’t correct her.

Rob and Suki hit the red carpet for the Die My Love premiere and look so, so chic.

A chic night out at the Vanity Fair Oscars after-party…

…followed by date night at the L.A. premiere of The Drama! I’m obsesssssed.

Oh! And during an interview with Extra, Rob and Zendaya are asked about their “biggest secret.” Zendaya playfully teases that there’s “nothing that comes to mind,” while Rob says his big secret is “the same one that you have” while grinning at Zendaya.

Robert and Suki roll up to the Venice International Film Festival looking 10/10.

After almost two years of speculation, Suki FINALLY addresses those marriage rumors, telling the Sunday Times that she and Robert are not, in fact, husband and wife...at least, not yet.

“It would either be all or nothing,” she says of their future wedding. “If we were going to go for it we’d have a massive party, blow it out.”

Of her relationship, she also gushes, “It is almost a decade and that is a beautiful thing. It’s a thing that I go, ‘I can’t believe I have this in my life and I can’t believe that I’m so happy.’”
