Spain vs England LIVE: Nations League match stream, confirmed team news, lineups and fan reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

England travel to Spain this evening in the final game of their Nations League group campaign. The Three Lions are second in Group A3, three points behind tonight’s opponents. Thomas Tuchel’s men suffered their only defeat of the group stages against the world champions, who edged a 3-2 thriller at Wembley Stadium in the last international break in September.

Now, the World Cup semi-finalists could leapfrog Luis de la Fuente’s men, should they defeat them by three clear goals at the Estadio Metropolitano. England converge on Madrid fresh off their 2-1 victory over Croatia last time out, as Harry Kane moved past Peter Shilton to break the former goalkeeper’s record for most England caps.

The Ballon d’Or winner will be keen to prove his worth as the world’s best player against the world’s best team here, and Tuchel is expected to go strong with eyes on the knockout rounds of this competition next year. Keep up to date with all the latest from Madrid with our live blog below.

As for the hosts, a surprise that Spurs’ Pedro Porro is omitted in favour of Pubill, who will be hoping his La Liga rival Gordon has an off day.

Marc Cucurella will be up against Rogers in a battle of Chelsea past and present, while Aymeric Laporte’s experience complements the youthful exuberance and silky ball skills of Barcelona’s Pau Cubarsi.

Fabian will be the ball player in centre-field with Merino and Zubimendi the terrier-like ball-winners, while Baena and Oyarzabal are likely to frequently swap positions with their crafty movement when Spain have the ball.

Starboy Lamine Yamal will be up against Lewis Hall in a mouth-watering face-off down the Spanish right.

A big call from Tuchel to select Trafford in net, but the Leeds goalkeeper has impressed this term for his new club.

Ben White adds a bit more defensive solidity to the backline that Trent Alexander-Arnold does, and he will be up against Alex Baena down the Spain left.

Jarell Quansah has a pacey partner in Ezri Konsa alongside him, but that pace will likely not be needed when dealing with the tricky Mikel Oyarzabal, whose strength comes in his movement and deft lay-offs.

In the engine room, it’s the go-to trio with Elliot Anderson and Declan Rice up against a workhorse Spanish midfield of Martin Zubimendi, Fabian Ruiz and Mikel Merino, while Jude Bellingham will add that box-to-box physicality as well from his more advanced No10 role.

Harry Kane leads the line, flanked by Tuchel’s favoured wide men, Morgan Rogers to the right, and Anthony Gordon to the left, looking to terrorise Marc Pubill.

BBC Sport report that Ben White is set to start at right-back for England this evening, replacing Trent Alexander-Arnold.

Ezri Konsa is also expected to come in, while Dan Burn is also thought to have dropped out.
