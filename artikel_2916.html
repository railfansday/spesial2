Antonelli tops Las Vegas qualifying but starts 6th after grid penalty

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Andrea Kimi Antonelli came from nowhere to take pole position for the Las Vegas Grand Prix – although the world champion will start Saturday's Sin City race from sixth following a grid penalty.

Antonelli never appeared at the races on the famous Nevada Strip, only to blow away his opposition and qualify 0.258 seconds clear of Red Bull's Max Verstappen.

However, Verstappen will lead off the pack as he takes advantage of Antonelli's sanction for exceeding engine parts.

George Russell, who finished three tenths adrift of his Mercedes team-mate, will move up from third to join Verstappen on the front row.

Lando Norris will start third as Lewis Hamilton suffered a night to forget after he finished only 12th.

Russell might have hoped to start Saturday's fixture at the front of the pack after topping final practice.

He called on Mercedes for Antonelli to hand him with a tow on his final throw of the dice for pole. Russell's request fell on deaf ears and although he improved with his last lap, he was unable to trouble the top of the order.

It hands Verstappen, who has won three of the last five races and delivered a mesmerizing drive from the back of the grid to second in São Paulo a fortnight ago, the advantage, not just on Saturday, but in the race to finish second in the standings.

"It was a very strange qualifying for me," said Antonelli. "I couldn't get any feeling in the car. So, it was unexpected. I was able to gain from a tow in front and take pole. I don't know how I did it."

Russell said: "Overall it has been a solid day. I wanted to be on pole. Kimi did a great job and it worked out very well for him.

"But I start second because of his penalty and it is all to play for."

Russell and Verstappen were earlier both let off the hook by the stewards after they banged wheels in final practice.

The two drivers – who have a chequered history – exchanged tyre rubber as they bizarrely duelled for track position, with Verstappen accusing his British rival of pushing him off the track.

Both Russell and Verstappen were hauled before the stewards before being ticked off with a warning and avoiding the punishment of a grid drop.

Hamilton is fighting both Russell and Verstappen for 'best of the rest' in the championship standings but suffered a miserable evening on the Strip.

Hamilton qualified 20th and last here last year, and although his performance on Friday wasn't quite to that sobering standard, the seven-time world champion struggled throughout.

Even with girlfriend Kim Kardashian's support at the back of his garage, Hamilton painfully failed to get his Ferrari up to speed in the cold Nevada air.

"I just don't have any grip for some reason," he complained after earlier claiming he could not extract any more pace from his unruly machine.

Hamilton heads into Saturday's fixture 18 points behind second-placed Verstappen and a point back from Russell, and now looks set to lose further ground with just two rounds to go after this one.

Hamilton's performance marked a dismal showing for Ferrari, with Charles Leclerc only one place better off in 11th.

"Neither of us got through?" a disbelieving Hamilton said on the radio. "Sorry, guys, I gave it everything."

The Ferrari duo will both move up a place with Oscar Piastri, who qualified seventh, bumped back 10 positions following an engine penalty.
