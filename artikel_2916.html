Opera Limited: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Opera's cash-light model funds new AI platforms without heavy capital spending, so shareholders may see returns from its growth plans.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 25, 2026 at 3:10AM EST

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 17, 2026, Opera (OPRA +0.82%) projected full-year 2026 revenue of $735 million to $742 million. The web browser developer is expanding its advertising reach and adding artificial intelligence features while generating cash.

That balance gives the business room to fund new platforms without relying on heavy capital expenditures. Three takeaways from management explain how this strategy builds long-term value.

Management is integrating artificial intelligence directly into its software architecture rather than building a simple chat sidebar. By launching WebAI, the company's platform for running open-weight artificial intelligence models locally on a user's device, it lets developers add features without paying for remote cloud infrastructure.

The company doesn't need to fund massive capital expenditures (CapEx), or long-term investments in physical property and equipment, to add artificial intelligence tools to its browsers. That lean structure leaves plenty of cash for dividends and share buybacks.

Advertising revenue grew 22% year over year in the third quarter as management expanded into new commercial sectors. E-commerce remains the largest market for Opera Ads, the company's advertising platform, but video gaming promotions now contribute a double-digit percentage of ad revenue.

Revenue momentum remains solid as the company enters the fourth quarter, supported by no debt and cash conversion. Management is expanding third-party ad channels, like connected television (CTV), while maintaining cost discipline across headcount and marketing.

Browser engagement reached new milestones post-summer, including 100 million monthly active users on personal computers in October. That expanding user base gives management a strong foundation to pursue growth in 2027.
