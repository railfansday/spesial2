Lakers get huge starting injury boost for clash against Heat

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Lakers are getting one of their starting wings back on Monday night.

Ziaire Williams was taken off the injury report for the Lakers' home game against the Heat after being sidelined for three games because of left foot soreness/injury management.

Ziaire Williams will make his return after being sidelined for nearly a week. Getty Images

The 6-foot-10 wing last played in the Oct. 27 loss to the Trail Blazers, recording 11 points, four assists and two steals in 29 minutes.

"The images have been clean," coach JJ Redick said of Williams' foot on Sunday. "I would describe it [as] a soreness that just pops up in the morning. He's feeling a lot better. Just being cautious with the back-to-back."

Ziaire Williams has been dealing with foot soreness to start the season. NBAE via Getty Images

The Lakers are coming off beating the Raptors on Sunday night, the first night of the back-to-back set.

Williams started in the four regular-season games he's played in so far, plus the two preseason games he suited up for.

Ziaire Williams had a strong start for the Lakers before missing the previous three games. AP Photo/Caroline Brehman

He's averaging 11.3 points on 57.1% shooting (46.7% on 3-pointers), three rebounds, 2.3 assists and 1.5 steals per game.

Adou Thiero (left knee injury management) was the lone player on the injury report, being listed as out.
