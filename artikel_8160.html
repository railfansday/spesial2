I’m Convinced We’re Not Alone

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

What’s on the outside of outer space? Nobody knows, not even a guy who spent 36 years at NASA.

Paddy heads to the Reset Hotel outside Joshua Tree National Park to stargaze with Dr. Gary Blackwood, aerospace engineer, former NASA Exoplanet Exploration Program leader, and (yes) alien hunter. We get into why he’s convinced we’re not alone, how telescopes can sniff out breathing planets from light years away, the Habitable Worlds Observatory, the Belt of Venus, a Hurricane Hilary backpacking mishap, a lightning round (quasars vs. pulsars vs. Jar Jar), and what Blackwood would ask visiting aliens first.

The Reset Hotel, part of Outdoor Collection by Marriott Bonvoy, is a modern desert escape. It’s the perfect place to recalibrate, reconnect, and reset between adventures. It also makes an ideal retreat for your own dark sky explorations, with onsite telescope viewing and guided stargazing experiences.
