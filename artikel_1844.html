Ref Watch: Chelsea star's screaming and cheating among three Andy Madley blunders in Nottingham Forest clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Correspondent AUTHORITY Football journalist with experience at Reach PLC and PA Media; University of Central Lancashire graduate. FOCUS Fan-engagement content and match analysis across the Premier League and Championship. THE INSIGHT Oliver utilises a network of club and industry contacts to deliver verified reporting and fan-first content. He provides the human stories behind the results to ensure fans get the full picture as it happens.

Andy Madley made some debatable decisions as Nottingham Forest inflicted a 3-2 defeat on Chelsea at the City Ground.

Forest went ahead inside the first five minutes courtesy of an incredible volley from Igor Jesus that left Emiliano Martinez with no chance.

Their lead was doubled shortly after half-time as Morgan Gibbs-White bagged from the spot, and he notched his brace late on with Emmanuel Emegha's strike sandwiched in between both goals.

Emegha pulled another back, but Chelsea have now lost three consecutive Premier League games and sit seventh, while the Reds are 14th after back-to-back home wins.

Referee Madley had some big decisions to make on the night, and there were some he got clearly wrong, as well as some correct.

Football Insider have picked out his three worst calls from the City Ground clash.

Caicedo was playing completely out of position at centre-back as Alonso gambled to try something different amid Chelsea's recent struggles.

The Ecuadorian was not one of his side's worst players by any means, but deserved a booking as he screamed at the assistant in the first half following what he perceived to be the wrong call.

He had been chasing down Igor Jesus for the ball after the Forest striker appeared to be played through in an offside position, then took to berating the official when the ball ultimately went out of play seconds later.

It was not a good look for Caicedo, who made a persistent gesture to insist that the flag should have gone up.

Delap was the man to win Forest a spot kick against his old club in the second half, but he should have also been given one in the first period.

The former Blues striker was racing into the visitors' penalty area when Romeo Lavia appeared to catch him as he went to shoot.

Replays showed that Lavia's foot had planted in front of Delap's before he kicked through the Chelsea midfielder.

Those challenges are often seen as fouls in today's game, yet Madley was unmoved.

Reds full-back Williams was very fortunate not to pick up his second yellow card of the league campaign as Chelsea pushed for goals late on.

The Wales star clearly fouled Lavia in a dangerous position in the 83rd minute, as Madley pointed for a free-kick to the visitors.

It was a definite booking, given the proximity of the foul to the area and the ferocity with which he made the challenge, yet Williams somehow escaped without the yellow.
