Airbnb Recreates Iconic ‘Kabhi Khushi Kabhie Gham’ Mansion for Film’s 25th Anniversary, With Kareena Kapoor Khan and Hrithik Roshan Hosting

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Variety may receive an affiliate commission.

Airbnb is bringing one of Bollywood’s most recognizable homes to life in celebration of the 25th anniversary of Karan Johar’s “Kabhi Khushi Kabhie Gham.”

The company has recreated the Raichand family mansion from the 2001 film for a limited-time immersive experience in Mumbai, inviting fans to explore its grand staircase, family study and Poo’s extravagant closet. The two-hour experience, titled “At Home with the Raichands,” will run from Dec. 1-6, with just 180 guest spots available. The experience is being presented by Johar alongside Hrithik Roshan and Kareena Kapoor Khan, who played Rohan Raichand and Poo, respectively, in the film. The three stars will not appear in person

Released in December 2001, “Kabhi Khushi Kabhie Gham,” commonly known as “K3G,” featured an ensemble cast including Amitabh Bachchan, Jaya Bachchan, Shah Rukh Khan and Kajol. The family drama, which follows a wealthy Indian family divided by a son’s marriage against his father’s wishes, became one of the defining Bollywood films of its era, with its elaborate production design, musical numbers and fashion continuing to resonate with younger audiences.

The Airbnb recreation will allow guests to explore the mansion through an immersive audio tour featuring memorable moments from the film. Visitors can also step inside a recreation of Poo’s wardrobe, participate in a character-inspired entry challenge and write a personal note inspired by the movie’s themes of love and family. Each guest will receive a commemorative keepsake.

“Poo has been a part of my life for 25 years, and I love that she’s still having her moment with a whole new generation,” Kapoor Khan said in a statement. “She had her own sense of style, her own vocabulary and, most importantly, she never really needed anyone’s approval.”

Roshan, meanwhile, described the experience as an opportunity to revisit one of his earliest major film roles.

“To see the Raichand House recreated by Airbnb and to know that fans will get to walk down the memory lane themselves is the best way to celebrate the film,” he said.

The activation is part of Airbnb’s “Only on Airbnb” experiences, which have increasingly tapped into entertainment properties and fan culture to create real-world versions of fictional settings.

Booking requests open Nov. 23 at 12 p.m. IST through Airbnb’s dedicated K3G page. The experience is free, with each booking covering two guests, although visitors must arrange their own travel to Mumbai.
