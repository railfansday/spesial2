The Ultimate Thanksgiving Detox? Mexico’s Most Over-the-Top, Celeb-Approved Longevity Clinic

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A new wellness resort is attracting a glittery crowd with outré longevity offerings. Rectal ozone therapy, anyone?

Pampering spa getaways are so passé. The reinvented power trip? Longevity-centered five-star resorts that zero in on preventive medicine and rigorous cutting-edge health-optimization treatments. Think Benjamin Button-like cellular reprogramming and biotech breakthroughs that promise to help rewind biological aging in the elite quest to amp up health span.

Once confined largely to Switzerland, top-tier medi-wellness destinations are moving closer to Hollywood. Most notable among them is SHA Mexico, which opened in 2024 on a beachfront stretch of Costa Mujeres, north of Cancún. The flagship avant-garde longevity clinic, SHA Spain, launched in 2008 in the coastal town of L’Albir, has welcomed pro athletes, royalty, supermodels and such stars as Barbra Streisand (for repeat visits), Demi Moore, Monica Bellucci, Javier Bardem and Penélope Cruz, Lily James, Madonna and Marisa Tomei. Now names like Dakota Johnson, Kate Moss, Jack Black, Pink, model Candice Swanepoel and Lake Bell are on the guest list at the Mexican outpost. Calling to mind The Jetsons’ Googie-style home in Orbit City, the futuristic design, by Mexican architecture studio Sordo Madaleno, was aptly inspired by the human genome. Exclusive to this location is a bank of stem cells, unveiled in June, ethically sourced from embryonic tissues for medical and aesthetic cellular regeneration therapies that target autoimmune disease, muscle injuries, sleep apnea, aging skin and more. (At SHA Spain, therapies use the patient’s own stem cells; these donor-derived regenerative treatments are not yet approved in the U.S. or Europe for the uses described.) Treatments are administered through intravenous infusions, nebulizer inhalation and targeted injection; exosome facials inject the cell-derived donor particles directly into the face.

“These therapies reprogram the body’s natural mechanisms to heal and rejuvenate from the inside out,” says Dr. Andrea Márquez, head of SHA’s regenerative medicine, explaining that aging or inflamed cellular systems need a reboot.

A Cell Regeneration Therapy bed with a “human regenerator device” is said to boost energy, support physical recovery and promote anti-aging benefits by surrounding the body with a field of electrically charged particles.

A popular European beauty secret, not yet available in the U.S. and offered at SHA Mexico, is Profhilo, the Italian hyaluronic acid-based skin remodeling injectable that improves elasticity.

More than 200 SHA offerings range from neurocognitive, hormonal and sleep health to genomic medicine, dermatology and physiotherapy. Each guest is prescribed teas and an anti-inflammatory vegan or pescatarian diet, with add-ons like seaweed or shots of apple cider vinegar. SHA’s Japanese-influenced dietary rules discourage liquid drinks during meals (said to “hinder digestion”), and breakfast kicks off with a bowl of miso soup (probiotic-rich to support gut health).

One gut-health package popular with the Hollywood crowd promises to detox and optimize the digestive system. Included are an intestinal dysbiosis analysis, visceral osteopathy (hands-on manipulation of the abdomen intended to improve function of internal organs) and intestinal ozone therapy. Not FDA-approved as a medical treatment in the U.S., the latter pumps an oxygen/ozone gas mixture into the rectum to purportedly “reduce oxidative stress, support immune balance and promote tissue repair,” according to the SHA website.

Gwyneth Paltrow and self-proclaimed “father of biohacking” Dave Asprey have both fessed to using rectal ozone therapy. Vaginal ozone therapy and ozone IV infusions (also used by Asprey) are additionally on the service menu, as is a dental cleaning that incorporates the agent.

Hollywood dermatologist Ava Shamban (whose clients include Moore, Angela Bassett, Nicole Kidman and Michelle Yeoh) weighs in on donor stem cells and ozone treatments: “I say proceed with caution. The science is soft. The good news is that we are on the cusp of truly groundbreaking longevity sciences that will come with DNA optimization and literal reversal of aging, using the newest technologies.”

As over-the-top as some of this may seem, SHA’s owners are firmly at the forefront of longevity research: “It’s not just about trendy biohacking and optimization; we have built a legacy approaching longevity through the lens of both Western functional medicine and Eastern holistic practices,” says SHA managing partner Alejandro Bataller, whose father, Alfredo Bataller, was inspired to found the company after integrative medicine and nutritional shifts helped him overcome early stage colon cancer.

All-inclusive rooms at SHA Mexico run $600 to $9,000 a night, plus program rates starting at $4,800 for a four-night stay. In late June, SHA’s umbrella company, AB Living Group, opened St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort & Residences next door with a luxe spa, 24-hour butler service, daily champagne sabrage and guest access to SHA. For those who want to truly live the lifestyle, private SHA Residences start at $2.5 million. Next up is SHA Emirates (billed as “the world’s first longevity island”), slated to open in AlJurf, along the coast between Dubai and Abu Dhabi, in early 2028. Residences there will cost $27 million.
