‘The Mummy’: Top Artifact Props From 1999 Film Heading to Auction Ahead of Franchise Revival (Exclusive)

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Also hitting the block from Propstore is the Batpod, with the vehicle from Christopher Nolan's Batman movies having received an opening bid of $598,000.

Two signature props from 1999’s franchise-launching The Mummy are set for auction ahead of the film series’ forthcoming return to cinemas.

Propstore’s Winter Entertainment Memorabilia Live Auction runs Dec. 4-6 and features a number of items from the film that starred Brendan Fraser and Rachel Weisz, The Hollywood Reporter has learned. These include the mechanical Hamunaptra Key, the complicated puzzle box protecting the map to the City of the Dead, and the mechanical Book of the Dead, which Rick (Fraser) and Evelyn (Weisz) inadvertently use to resurrect Imhotep. The props include Egyptian-inspired detailing and functioning mechanical features, with both holding a pre-sale estimate of $26,400–$52,800. Seen below, the Hamunaptra Key is an octagonal item that, when its upper half is twisted, features eight panels that pop open in a sunburst design. In director Stephen Sommers’ film, it was used to lock and unlock the Book of the Dead and the Book of Amun-Ra.

The Book of the Dead, also pictured below, is one of the defining Egyptian artifact props from the movie. It features an ornate mechanical lock and 12 tablet pages detailed with hieroglyphs, with the book’s clasps capable of being released by twisting a circular emblem on the cover.

Registration and online bidding have opened for the auction that spans more than 1,400 lots. Among the highlights is Batman’s Batpod from Christopher Nolan’s The Dark Knight and The Dark Knight Rises, with the motorbike receiving an opening bid of $598,000. Also included among the lots is a stunt fedora and stunt shirt from the Indiana Jones franchise that hails from the personal collection of Harrison Ford’s stunt double Vic Armstrong and looks to potentially sell for upwards of $1 million.

The sale of the props from The Mummy come as the franchise revs up for a theatrical revival. Universal is set to release directors Matt Bettinelli-Olpin and Tyler Gillett’s The Mummy: Book of the Dead in theaters on Oct. 15, 2027. Brendan Fraser, Rachel Weisz and John Hannah will reprise their franchise roles in the movie that is a sequel to the 1999 film and its 2001 follow-up, The Mummy Returns.

During a conversation with THR timed to the first film’s 25th anniversary, Sommers noted his appreciation that The Mummy has continued to draw fans. “It’s so funny how it just never went away,” he said at the time. “I hate to say it, but somehow it’s endeared itself to a lot of people.”
