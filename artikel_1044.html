Sheff Utd thump four past sorry Watford

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Sheffield United vs Watford. Sky Bet Championship.

Report and free match highlights from the Sky Bet Championship match between Sheffield United and Watford at Bramall Lane on Saturday; Ben Brereton Diaz nets twice in comfortable Blades victory over struggling Hornets.

Sheffield United got back to winning ways as they breezed past struggling Watford with a 4-0 victory at Bramall Lane.

Two early Ben Brereton Diaz goals paired with second-half strikes from Jordan Ayew and Sydie Peck sealed the deal in a win that was all too easy for the hosts.

It was a feisty affair, with referee Tim Robinson handing out 13 yellow cards - much to the disapproval of both sets of fans.

Defender Liam Kitching was one of those to be booked after just two minutes but his early caution was soon forgotten as the Blades took the lead.

His searching long ball came off the back of Romelle Donovan, Brereton Diaz picked up the pieces and arrowed into the corner from distance to put his side in front on five minutes.

United had their tails up and doubled their lead in the eighth minute. Peck was brought down by Martin Payero in the box and Robinson pointed to the spot. Brereton Diaz stepped up and slotted down the middle to give the Blades a quick-fire two-goal lead.

Watford managed to keep the hosts quiet enough for the remainder of the first half but showed very little themselves going forward, with keeper Michael Cooper having nothing to do in the first 45.

Tahith Chong thought he had made it three 10 minutes into the second half but was in an offside position after bending in from Peck's neat pass.

Donovan was keeping the Watford defence busy on the left, with his cross nearly being flicked home by Ayew.

Ayew was not to be denied for much longer though; Chong drove down the right-hand side and curled in an inch-perfect cross for the striker who could not miss with his diving header to get United's third after 61 minutes.

Substitute Edoardo Bove could have given the Hornets some hope with just under 20 minutes to play when he finished past Cooper but the linesman incorrectly flagged for offside.

But the game truly was put to bed when substitute Patrick Bamford was played in down the left and squared for Peck to tap into an open goal and make it 4-0 on 84 minutes.

The victory sees the Blades move up to just one place outside of the play-off positions in ninth, while the Hornets hang just above the relegation zone in 21st.

"It's very difficult to control a whole game in this division, more often than not control comes in spells, but I feel that was the case today.

"We wanted to make a really fast start and we did, it set us up for the whole afternoon. Getting those two early goals ignited the support and everyone got behind us from the off.

"I thought our attitude was really good and we created lots of opportunities. You have to go after every game as if your life depends on it. We've been a bit light on bodies but we're trying not to let levels drop.

"It's a long two weeks when you lose just before an international break like we did at Charlton. You spend a lot of time reviewing the game and we knew we had to show a response. I'm really pleased we did.

"We can give any team a game in this league. We can play with so much intensity, for us now we just need to show a higher level of consistency across the season.

"We know there's going to be bigger challenges out there but you just have to beat whoever is in front of you and we were really professional in the way we did that.

"I've seen some really good performances all over the pitch today and it has made for a really enjoyable afternoon for both fans and players."

"Firstly, I want to say sorry to our supporters. When you play away at Sheffield United and show no courage to play football it becomes impossible to get a good result.

"If you come to a place like this and go two goals behind after eight minutes it is always going to be hard for you.

"Things did not get much better in the second half and we conceded two more goals. Edoardo (Bove) was onside when he scored at 3-0 but I don't like to make excuses.

"It is also disappointing for me because all of the goals are from our own mistakes. I think this is normal because of the situation we are in, some of the players do not have very much experience but we still have to fight and believe in ourselves.

"Before the match we spoke about being brave but we showed no personality or aggression. It is really difficult to find the positives here today.

"It is difficult for us because we do not have the quality we need in this moment. This is the big difference between us and teams like Sheffield United with quality all over the pitch.

"No matter the result, losing or winning, you always have to look to the next day. This was a bad result for us but we will move on and show full focus for the next game."
