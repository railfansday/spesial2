Matt Smith’s Doctor Who Era Is Officially Returning in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Matt Smith's era of Doctor Who is one that fans still love very dearly. The actor played the Eleventh Doctor from 2010 to 2013, working with Karen Gillan's Amy Pond and Jenna Coleman's Clara Oswald as his companions during his time. Now though, fans can experience more of the Doctor's adventures with the audio adventures of the Doctor (Miles Taylor) and his companion, Eleanor Fong (Jasmine Bayes). In January, fans can experience the exciting new adventures of the Doctor and Eleanor in a box set release called Doctor Who - The Eleventh Doctor Adventures: Into the Shadows.

The audio stories are set between series six and seven of Doctor Who, right after Amy Pond and Rory Williams (Arthur Darvill) get trapped in Manhattan thanks to the Weeping Angels and before the Doctor and Clara become a "not your boyfriend" pair of friends. In Into the Shadows, the duo will face off against a lot of issues like a sinking ship, encountering a man who is mistakenly known as the Last of the Time Lords, and more. Eleanor and the Doctor are not being fully honest with each other.

Doctor Who - The Eleventh Doctor Adventures: Into the Shadows is available for pre-order for £19.99 to download to own or £29.99 to get the collector's edition CD box set here. The description of Into the Shadows is as follows: "Still intent on keeping a low profile, the Doctor attempts to fulfill Eleanor Fong’s wish to return to Hong Kong in 1990, for some ‘normal’. Perhaps unsurprisingly, things go wrong, and they find themselves trapped on a crashing, sinking spaceship, caught up in a despotic ex-president’s fiendish attempt to regain power, and coming face-to-face with a plan to bend reality."

Smith's era as the Doctor is one that changed how a lot of fans engaged with Doctor Who. It was when the series became a hit in America and so much of the love for the show is rooted in what writer Steven Moffat did with Smith's Doctor. From episodes with Amy and Vincent van Gogh (Tony Curran) to fighting off dinosaurs, it was a fun run of episodes and a joyous time. When Into the Shadows is set is when the Doctor is at his lowest, having just lost Amy and Rory. So it will be exciting to see how the Doctor and Eleanor get through it all.

You can pre-order your collection now and watch Smith's era of Doctor Who!
