Crystal Palace downfall traced back to two players by ex-chief scout

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Crystal Palace have a major weakness when it comes to leadership and set pieces which must be resolved in their upcoming games.

Pressure is building on Pierre Sage's future after a turbulent start to the season at Selhurst Park, and their weakness at defending set plays has contributed heavily.

The Eagles have conceded 21 goals injust nine Premier League games so far this season, with four of those coming via set pieces, the second-worse of any team.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Sage must resolve his team's frailties, but the Crystal Palace players must also take accountability.

The South London club have lost leaders including Marc Guehi and Maxence Lacroix in the last 12 months, which has weakened them on the pitch but also mentally.

Lacking those key influences and players who are willing to take responsibility is now costing Palace, particularly from set pieces where they are especially weak.

Football Insider revealed on 25 October that Crystal Palace's failure to adequately replace the likes of Guehi, Lacroix and Daniel Munoz came as a significant blow to Sage.

The lack of leadership in the back line following Lacroix's exit has become a major concern, with loanees Axel Disasi and Honest Ahanor having struggled so far.

That lack of accountability and leadership has been evident not just in Palace's shocking defensive record, but also on set pieces, where they have been urged to step up.

"Crystal Palace have been terribly defensively, and now they're struggling from set pieces too," Brown told Football Insider.

"Obviously the finger will be pointed at the manager, but the players have got to step up and take some accountability in these situations to deal with the issue themselves.

"The big thing for Crystal Palace, when you look at it, is they've lost players like Marc Guehi and Maxence Lacroix who were real leaders at the back.

"They would have no problem taking control of a situation, taking responsibility, and making sure they dealt with conrners, free kicks, anything like that.

"Looking at this Palace squad now, which of those players are going to step up and take control, take accountability, and deal with things? It's becoming a major weakness.

"Like I said, the blame will come back on the manager and maybe they could be better prepared for these situations, but it's the players who have been letting him down.

"They're in desperate need of leaders at Crystal Palace, and my big worry for them is that these players don't seem to be showing up when they are so badly needed."

Demands for Crystal Palace defenders and leaders to step up have come alongside ongoing speculation about Sage's future in the job, with his position under threat.

The Frenchman has come under fire in recent weeks following a string of disappointing results which have left the Eagles in the bottom three in the Premier League table.

Brown told Football Insider on 2 November that Crystal Palace's star names are still in support of the manager but must start to show that support on the pitch.

Reports have recently claimed that senior players have sent messages of support to the manager as they reaffirmed their backing despite recent results going against them.

However, those players have now been called upon to step up on the pitch, with many having let Sage down and contributed directly to the pressure via their performances.
