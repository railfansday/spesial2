Transfer news LIVE: Arsenal eye Brazil star; Man Utd in Barcelona swap; Chelsea, Liverpool latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Arsenal, Chelsea, Manchester United, Liverpool and Tottenham must start stepping up their January plans, with less than two months to go into the winter window opens. The Gunners are expected to explore signing a new striker in light of Viktor Gyokeres and Kai Havertz continuing to struggle in attack, with Bournemouth forward Eli Junior Kroupi of particular interest, and the likes of Endrick and Julian Alvarez linked.

Man Utd are decided to keep hold of beleagured manager Michael Carrick for now, but a change is still expected this month should results not improve, with Roma and Aston Villa up next. They too will look to strengthen in January either way, with Ivan Toney an attacking target and Jarrad Brathwaite linked to bolster the defence.

One deal which looks very likely to happen is Alex Scott’s move from Bournemouth to Chelsea, while the Blues have cooled interest in Lamine Camara who could be joining Liverpool. Tottenham have also been linked with Scott and Junior Kroupi, and a new striker is likely to be of interest when the window reopens. Follow all the latest transfer news, rumours and gossip live below!

Victor Ozhianvuna has bid an emotional farewell to Shamrock Rovers ahead of his move to Arsenal.

The highly-rated midfielder will head to north London after turning 18 in January, following a deal that was first agreed in October 2025.

Ozhianvuna is viewed as one of the brightest young talents in Irish football and has already made 37 senior appearances for reigning League of Ireland Premier Division champions Shamrock Rovers.

He also became the second-youngest player ever to feature in the UEFA Conference League last year.

His performances quickly caught the attention of Arsenal, who will add the Republic of Ireland Under-17 international to their academy ranks in the New Year.

Arsenal have Eli Junior Kroupi and Kenan Yildiz firmly on their transfer radar ahead of the January window, new reports claim.

Bournemouth forward Kroupi was understood to have been a prominent target for the Gunners during the summer, only to suffer a foot fracture during pre-season that required surgery.

The France Under-21 international was sidelined for three months, halting his significant momentum after a fantastic debut season in English football following a move from Lorient in which he netted 13 goals in 35 appearances across all competitions.

Tottenham Hotspur value Pedro Porro at €70million–€80m amid links with Barcelona and Real Madrid.

Sport claims that the 27-year-old right-back has expressed his desire to return to Spain but says neither club has shown firm interest.

The World Cup winner’s contract runs until June 2031 in north London.

Teamtalk claims that Manchester United have been offered the chance to sign AS Monaco striker Folarin Balogun in January.

The 25-year-old has made just four Ligue 1 substitute appearances, with Monaco willing to let him leave.

Everton, Leeds United, Newcastle United, Nottingham Forest and Juventus have also been contacted, after the Toffees saw a Deadline Day move collapse at the 11th hour for the American.

Tottenham have joined Man City and Man United in tracking Villarreal defender Alex Freeman, Football Insider has revealed.

The 22-year-old has scored once in 14 appearances, while Pedro Porro's uncertain future could influence Tottenham's plans.

Arsenal, Barcelona and Chelsea are monitoring AS Monaco forward Paris Brunner ahead of the January transfer window.

El Nacional claims that the 20-year-old German has impressed this season, attracting interest from several major European clubs.

Monaco's financial problems could force a winter sale, with the French club demanding at least €50millon.

Manchester United view Nottingham Forest midfielder Morgan Gibbs-White as a realistic transfer target.

Ekrem Konur claims that Liverpool and Tottenham are also monitoring the 26-year-old England international's situation.

United consider Gibbs-White a more achievable signing than several other attacking midfield options.

Arsenal are interested in signing Atletico Madrid midfielder Alex Baena, with Mikel Arteta keen on the Spaniard, El Nacional claims.

The Gunners reportedly plan to open talks soon and could offer the winger a higher salary and a prominent role. Atletico have no intention of negotiating his departure unless they receive an irrefutable offer.

Barcelona are considering Paris Saint-Germain defender Willian Pacho as a potential January signing, El Nacional reports.

The move could depend on them selling Jules Kounde and Ronald Araujo to fund defensive reinforcements.

Reports in Spain claim that Chelsea have made Barcelona’s Jules Kounde their top priority for the January transfer window.

The Blues plans to contact the defender’s representatives, with the Frenchman open to leaving Barcelona.

The La Liga outfit could consider selling him if he requests a move, but no agreement has been reached.
