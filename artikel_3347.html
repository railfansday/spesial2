Pokémon Gen 11 Sounds Like A Region-Spanning Dream Come True

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

There's no doubt that 2026 has been a really exciting year for Pokémon fans all over the world, as the franchise we have all grown up adoring finally celebrates its 30th birthday. That meant all kinds of exciting announcements from those continue to chart its course, and we got plenty of fascinating release designed to mark the occasion too.

Arguably the biggest of those was confirmation of the first games of the tenth generation, Winds and Waves, which are set to release in the latter half of 2027. The new region featured in those is based on the archipelagos of South-East Asia, and it looks like a major departure from anything else we've seen to date, something which the series has arguably needed after treading water for a while.

With that in mind, the fact that we haven't reached the tenth generation yet should mean that any info about Gen 11 is far out of the question. That might not be the case though, as recent information seems to touch on exactly what players can expect next.

Over on Reddit, one user shared a post featuring information gained during the infamous Teraleak, which contained thousands of internal files from developer Game Freak. Within that, there were timelines explaining exactly what they were planning to do next (at the time, at the very least), and it could be the most monumental shift we have seen in years.

Specifically, the developer is seemingly planning a new kind of RPG set across all the regions that are based on Japan, namely Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, and Sinnoh. That would be a huge project, and something which fans have been putting together for themselves for a while now, via recompilation projects based on older games.

An official release in that ilk would be huge though, and there is supposedly also a plan to introduce the 11th generation at around the same time, which would make it one of the most fundamentally changing moments in the franchise's history. That could mean a four year cycle for generations is going to become the norm, though Winds and Waves is coming later than expected to make room for the 30th anniversary.

Fans in the responses speculated on what a project that big could look like, with one saying, "It would be interesting to see what sort of story they went with. The criminal organizations include thieves who report to their mafia boss, two extremist groups attempting to terraform the planet, and a nihilistic man who wants to delete the planet entirely. I can’t see any of them working together well."

"Remake of the first 4 regions, in a similar graphics style to black and white, with things like Mega Evolution and modern quality of life features, with a proper battle frontier. I'd love this kind of game," another added.

Whatever does end up happening, we are likely still more than a few years away from seeing it for ourselves, but with Pokémon in a great place right now, it's probably best to just sit back and enjoy it anyway.

Pokémon is a Japanese multimedia franchise created by Satoshi Tajiri and Ken Sugimori, launched by Nintendo, Game Freak, and Creatures in 1996. Centered on fictional creatures called Pokémon, the series spans video games, trading cards, animation, films, merchandise, and competitive events. Players assume the role of Trainers who capture, battle, and trade Pokémon. It is one of the highest-grossing entertainment franchises in history.
