Dolly Parton Competes Against Herself in One of the 2027 Grammys’ Country Categories

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Dolly Parton nostalgia sure seemed to be running high with Grammy voters. The country music icon, who died in August at 80, received three posthumous nods when nominees for the 69th awards were announced Monday morning. And since Parton scored two nominations in the category of Best Country/Duo Group Performance, that Dolly glow also helped secure nods for artists like Zac Brown, Lainey Wilson, and even Queen Latifah.

To celebrate her 80th birthday, Parton assembled a cast of all-stars to rerecord her song “Light of a Clear Blue Morning,” which originally appeared on her 1977 LP, New Harvest … First Gathering. Wilson, Parton’s goddaughter Miley Cyrus, Reba McEntire, and Queen Latifah (scoring her first country-music Grammy nomination) all sing with the Country Music Hall of Famer on the recording, released as a charity single to raise funds for pediatric cancer research at Monroe Carell Jr. Children’s Hospital at Vanderbilt in Nashville.

Brown and his eponymous Zac Brown Band, meanwhile, are nominated for Best Country/Duo Group Performance for their Parton collab “Butterfly.” An inspirational ballad meant to guide Brown’s children through life, the song was featured as a duet with Parton on his 2025 album, Love & Fear. Parton’s final Grammy nomination this year is all her: She’s in contention for Best Audio Book, Narration, and Storytelling Recording for the reading of her 2025 memoir, Star of the Show: My Life On Stage.

In total, Parton has 58 Grammy nominations over the course of her career and 10 wins. Her most recent came in 2021, when “There Was Jesus” was named Best Contemporary Christian Music Performance/Song. This is a particularly exciting year for country music at the Grammy Awards, as Ella Langley secured seven nominations, including in the Big Four categories; Miranda Lambert earned a Producer of the Year, Non-Classical nod; and women dominated the Best Contemporary Country Album field.
