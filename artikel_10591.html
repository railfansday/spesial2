Even your abuelita will love Even Realities’ new smart glasses styles

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Grab a cup of hot cocoa and a sweater, because Even Realities’ new styles and glasses accessories are warming up for the fall.

Just in time for the first anniversary, we’re getting a brand new Even Realities G2 colorway, in addition to two new accessories that have been requested since launch.

The new Even Realities G2 colorway is simply called “Brown 2,” and uses a new material to combine the structural nature and balance of G2 glasses with the classy look of tortoiseshell frames. Brown 2 maintains all the great camera-free features of G2 smart glasses, and even ships in both Frame A and Frame B styles (that’s round or square, basically) for $649. The previous grey, brown, and green colors are also still available.

To go along with the new Even Realities G2 colorway, buyers can also select the new Even G2 Chain to keep your glasses hanging on your neck when not worn. Since Even Realities G2 weighs between 38-42 grams, depending on the style, they won’t feel heavy on your neck (or your face). G2 Chain is made of stainless steel with a sandblasted gunmetal finish and retails for $79.

Lastly, G2 users can now finally choose a polarized glasses clip (pictured above in Frame B style) that pops onto the frames. This is great for all-day outdoor wear since it tints the frames (and, by proxy, also makes the in-lens display easier to read in bright outdoor lighting). G2 Clip Polarized comes with a special case and retails for $149.

The new Even Realities G2 colorway and accessories come alongside a big new software update, including a new Conversate Pro mode that changes the way the glasses remember information you tell them. If you’re not familiar with the glasses, check out our Even Realities G2 review.
