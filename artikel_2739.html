Player Grades: Lakers vs. Heat

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

This is far from a finished product. Head coach JJ Redick has repeatedly mentioned across the first two weeks of the season that patience is needed as a host of new faces get acclimated to the Lakers' system.

What they've already proven, though, is that this is a potent offensive team. Led by Luka Dončić, this team is everything everyone imagined it would be when it comes to scoring the ball. And that comes with, again, kinks still needing to be worked out.

But through their first eight games of the season, the Lakers' 121.9 offensive rating leads the league. They lead the league in field goal percentage, are third in 3-point percentage and second in points per game.

It's been a light schedule, yes, but they've come sprinting out of the gate as one of the league's best offenses. And that's pretty encouraging with work still to be done.

So, let's dive into the win. As always, grades are based on expectations for each player. A "B" grade represents the average performance for that player.

15 minutes, 6 points, 1 rebound, 5 assists, 1 foul, 3-4 FG, 0-1 3PT, -2

Dare I say a much more controlled game from Sexton? That's still relative as he is still a bit too wild at times. But, still, only one missed shot, no turnovers and five assists? Yes please.

Interestingly, Vando got his normal first half shift but did not see the floor in the second half. With the Heat not really having traditional big men and smaller lineups on the floor, the Lakers could get away with Mamu at the five surrounded by wings and guards.

As for the bench group, that JJ waited until there were 82 seconds left in a game they led by more than 20 to put them in felt like a point being made.

Keeping LaRavia in the starting lineup was the right call. Redick is also still experimenting a bit with the rotations, including having Kessler on the floor with Reaves and Sexton to start the second quarter. This is all a work in progress to find the right combinations and he's still doing some experimenting, too.

Using a challenge less than 30 seconds into a game is hilarious. He technically lost the second challenge but I'm not going to dock him for that as Giannis bowling over two defenders while giving them a shove and getting away with it was bonkers.
