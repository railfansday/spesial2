FPL Gameweek 11 Players to Avoid: Enzri Konsa and 4 Others to Sell

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

As the 2026/27 Fantasy Premier League season resumes after the November international break, smart transfer decisions are crucial to protect your rank.

While chasing recent points hauls or bandwagon picks can be tempting, several popular assets carry massive rotation risks, fitness doubts, and looming fixture swings.

Before finalizing your transfers ahead of the deadline, here is our breakdown of the top players to avoid in FPL GW11 so you do not waste valuable free transfers.

Over 160,000 managers have already transferred in Ezri Konsa ahead of Gameweek 11, but priced at £4.9m, the optimal window to buy him has passed.

With William Saliba back in full training and closing in on a return to the starting lineup, Konsa's starting minutes are under immediate threat.

Arsenal enters a congested November and December schedule across multiple competitions, meaning Mikel Arteta will heavily rotate his center-backs.

Furthermore, Arsenal travels to face Manchester City in Gameweek 12, lowering their clean-sheet odds. If you do not already own Konsa, he is a clear player to avoid in gameweek 11 FPL—focus instead on nailed defenders like James Hill, Luka Vušković, Jan Paul van Hecke, or Bogel.

Despite carrying an injury flag in the game, roughly 72,000 managers have brought Johan Manzambi into their squads. Aside from the very real possibility that he misses Gameweek 11 altogether, his long-term minutes are deeply concerning.

Manzambi has played over 80 minutes in a league match only once this season, and Aston Villa is entering a grueling stretch of domestic and European fixtures.

For a midfielder who rarely completes 90 minutes, heavy rotation is inevitable, making him a risky player to avoid in your FPL for GW 11 of the 2026/27 season.

Managers should prioritize nailed 90-minute midfielders from clubs with lighter schedules, such as Chelsea Everton and others.

While Rayan Cherki rewarded owners with a 9-point haul in Gameweek 10, owning any Manchester City asset outside of Erling Haaland remains a massive gamble.

Managers who jump on Cherki after a good week often suffer the same frustration seen in Gameweeks 7, 8, and 9: limited substitute appearances, early withdrawals, or unused benchings.

With City navigating a demanding winter fixture pileup, Pep Guardiola's rotation will be ruthless, cementing Cherki as one of the biggest traps ahead of the deadline.

Avoiding short-term traps like Konsa, Manzambi, and Cherki can save you from painful benchings and wasted transfers over the busy winter period. Prioritizing nailed starters with secure minutes is always the smartest foundation for consistent green arrows.
