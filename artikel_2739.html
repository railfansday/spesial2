Lions HC Dan Campbell’s questioned decision on rare play makes him look like a genius

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

On Thursday afternoon, the Detroit Lions took the field at home for their annual Thanksgiving Day matchup, this time against the rival Chicago Bears. Both teams entered this matchup at 8-2, meaning first place in the NFC North was on the line in this one.

A wild play occurred toward the end of the first half when the Bears fumbled the ball in the end zone and intentionally batted it back out, resulting in a safety. Lions head coach Dan Campbell had the opportunity to decline the two points and instead give his team the ball, where they ended up recovering it near the end zone with an opportunity to score a touchdown, or instead take the safety and the ensuing punt, giving themselves a longer field to work with to score again.

Campbell ended up going with the former option, taking the safety, and it paid off in a big way, as the Lions took the ball and drove it all the way down the field anyway to get nine points out of the fiasco.

Campbell has long been known for going all-in on his decision-making during his stint as the Lions' head coach, frequently going for it on questionable fourth downs and taking other aggressive maneuvers, and the decision to take the two free points from the safety and bet on his offense to be able to orchestrate a late-half drive was no exception, with the team proving him right in his belief.

In any case, the Lions will next hit the field next week vs the Atlanta Falcons on the road.
