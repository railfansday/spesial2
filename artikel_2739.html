Falcons Fans Should Skip Thanksgiving Football For Wu-Tang And U2

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Atlanta Falcons will not play on Thanksgiving Day, a fact that leaves a quiet spot in the holiday schedule for local fans. According to Audacy, the holiday weekend will feature five special NFL games and a star-studded lineup of halftime performers that rivals any concert tour. The Falcons return to action on Sunday, November 29, when they travel to Minnesota to face the Vikings at 1 p.m. ET.

Football fans in Metro Atlanta can start the celebration early on Wednesday, November 25, with a game that breaks tradition. The Green Bay Packers take on the Los Angeles Rams in the first-ever Thanksgiving Eve game, a move that shifts the focus from the dinner table to the screen. Audacy reports that U2, celebrating 50 years since forming in 1976, will play during halftime. Netflix streams the game and halftime show live. A historic band will perform football music before Thursday's main event.

Thursday, November 26, brings the biggest entertainment spectacle of the weekend when the Chicago Bears visit the Detroit Lions. Per Audacy, the legendary Wu-Tang Clan will headline Ford Field's 87th annual Thanksgiving Day Classic for the Detroit Lions. The iconic hip-hop collective delivers its unmistakable sound to a top NFL holiday tradition with classics like "C.R.E.A.M.," "Protect Ya Neck" and "Triumph,". Within the Lions' ongoing entertainment partnership, Detroit rapper Eminem and his longtime manager Paul Rosenberg act as executive producers for this halftime show. For '90s hip-hop fans, this performance could rank among the most anticipated Thanksgiving halftime shows in years.

Later that day, the Philadelphia Eagles face the Dallas Cowboys in the traditional afternoon slot. Miranda Lambert, a country superstar and three-time Grammy winner, will headline the halftime show for this NFC East rivalry game. The performance is part of the Salvation Army's 30th annual Red Kettle Kickoff Halftime Show, which raises awareness for the organization's holiday giving campaign. Destiny's Child, Dolly Parton, Post Malone and Car have graced the Cowboys' Thanksgiving halftime stage over the years.
