T-mobile is no stranger to a crazy-good promo, but the carrier is raising the bar even higher with its lineup of 2026 Black Friday savings. Joining its current iPhone 18 Pro “On Us” and Samsung Galaxy S26+ “On Us” offers, T-Mobile has now introduced the option of three Apple devices or three Samsung devices “All On Us.” Here’s exactly how it works.

Starting off with Apple, for a limited time, customers can now get the iPhone 18 Pro, Apple Watch SE 3, and iPad (A16) “On Us” when you transfer your number, activate a new line on a qualifying T-Mixplan and add smartwatch and tablet data plans. Just looking for the free iPhone 18 Pro? Get yours when you switch to T-Mobile and bring your number on a qualifying T-Mix plan — no trade-in needed.
