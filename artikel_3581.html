Anne Hathaway Gives Birth to 3rd Baby With Adam Shulman, Spotted With Newborn

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Anne Hathaway and her husband, Adam Shulman, are officially outnumbered following the birth of their third baby.

The Princess Diaries alum, 44, was spotted out and about in New York City on Tuesday, November 17, with a newborn strapped into a brown carrier on her chest, as seen in photos obtained by TMZ. While the infant’s face wasn’t visible, a tiny purple pom-pom hat could be seen peeking out of the carrier.

Hathaway looked casual for the outing in a tan coat, white pants, brown boots, a matching baseball cap and sunglasses.

Us has reached out to Hathaway’s rep for comment.

Hathaway and Shulman, 45 — who have been married since September 2012 — previously welcomed sons Jonathan in March 2016 and Jack in November 2019.

In June, Hathaway confirmed that she was pregnant and expecting her third baby with Shulman.

“X Baby I’m yours x,” she captioned an Instagram post at the time.

In the social media video, which was set to Barbara Lewis’ 1961 song “Baby, I’m Yours,” Hathaway covered her midsection with her arms when she walked into the frame. She subsequently removed her arms to debut her baby bump as it peeked out from her white cropped shirt and long flowy skirt. The Oscar winner excitedly cradled her growing belly with a big smile on her face before walking off screen.

Hathaway’s pregnancy came amid an especially busy time in her career as she juggled starring roles in the 2026 films Mother Mary, The Devil Wears Prada 2, The Odyssey, The End of Oak Street and Verity.

In April, Hathaway offered rare insight into her family dynamic amid her packed schedule, praising Shulman for “the way he stepped up” for their kids.

“He supports me completely. This year in particular was unusual,” she told People at the time. “He and I both know that it’s probably never going to happen like this again.”

“In every possible way, he’s the most extraordinary person I’ve ever met,” she added. “I’m so lucky that he’s my partner that I spend my life with. If I didn’t know that before this past year, I think I really know it now because with absolutely everything he’s just, he’s on it. He holds it down.”

While Hathaway joked that she didn’t want to seem like she was “bragging,” she described Shulman as her “dream partner” and noted that the couple “work well” together as a team.

“It’s one thing to have dreams,” she shared. “It’s another thing to have somebody who helps you achieve them. I absolutely would not be able to have achieved what I’ve done without my husband.”

Despite having Shulman’s support, Hathaway has become more discerning about the projects she takes on since becoming a mother.

“That’s kind of reprioritized everything, so it’s actually made me a bit choosier because something really has to be so excellent to spend time away from them because they’re so precious to me,” she said on SiriusXM’s The Jess Cagle Show in October 2022.

As for her decision to distance her children from the public eye, Hathaway told Porter in November 2023, “It’s something I feel is not just essential for my health — I’m on a team, it’s my family, and it’s not just about me.”

She continued, “My family has needs, and one of the needs of children is that they need to be able to define their own lives. It doesn’t even occur to me to link the two up, except through gratitude that they serve each other so beautifully. But they serve each other through me and not through a space that’s outside of myself.”
