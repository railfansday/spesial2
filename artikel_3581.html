49ers’ Kyle Juszczyk Opens Up About Mother-in-Law’s Death: ‘A Strong Woman’

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

San Francisco 49ers fullback Kyle Juszczyk will have his late mother-in-law on his mind when he hits the field against the Seattle Seahawks on Sunday, November 29.

Kyle, 35, will honor his wife Kristin’s late mom, Gini, with his custom cleats on Sunday, in partnership with Novartis’ “My Cause My Cleats” campaign. Gini died of breast cancer when Kristin, 32, was in high school.

“I didn’t have the chance to meet her,” Kyle exclusively told Us. “When Kristin is speaking about breast cancer awareness and all the partnerships that she’s done, it brings up a time for Kristin to talk about her and to tell me about her memories with her mother and for me to see what a strong woman she was.”

Kyle continued, “As a teenager, Kristin wasn’t even aware of all her mother was going through. I know as she gets older, she gets more and more information from her father about all of these different doctors she was seeing, all these different things she was trying. For Kristin and I to be aware of it just shows the strength Gini had.”

Kristin and Kyle got married in July 2019. The NFL star’s cleats will support the Lynne Cohen Foundation, which prevents breast and ovarian cancer through early detection, genetic counseling and community-based care.

Kyle further explained that the cause is “near and dear to his heart” because one of his aunts is a breast cancer survivor.

“I just know what this can do to a family,” Kyle said. “I’ve seen the effect it’s had on my wife. Any little bit of help I can throw out there — any bit of awareness about getting early screening, early detection, how you can save lives — I’m really excited to.”

This holiday season, the 49ers schedule allowed for Kyle and Kristin to host Thanksgiving at their home in California, which included all of Kyle’s family flying in from his home state of Ohio.

“It’s become a tradition that we’ve done for a number of years now because it’s tough in the NFL,” Kyle explained. “You miss so many holidays. The amount of Christmases and Thanksgivings that I’ve missed is too much to count.”

He continued, “But even if our schedule doesn’t line up for the actual week of Thanksgiving, we’ll always celebrate it. Whether it’s a week or two before, a week or two after. We make it a priority because Thanksgiving is my favorite holiday.”

The 49ers host the Seattle Seahawks on Sunday, November 29, at 4:25 p.m. ET.
