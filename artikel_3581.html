Jordan Rodgers Calls Brother Aaron's Steelers Departure 'Sad to See'

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Jordan Rodgers is admittedly shocked by his brother Aaron Rodgers’ departure from the Pittsburgh Steelers.

“That was something we weren’t all preparing for,” Jordan, 38, quipped on the Friday, November 27, broadcast of Good Morning America. “It was a crazy couple days for the Steelers and Aaron. I think that first and foremost, it’s just kind of sad to see it end this way in Pittsburgh.”

He continued, “There was so much good vibes and hope about the reuniting of [Mike] McCarthy and Rodgers and the potential to make a playoff run one last dance. Unfortunately, it didn’t happen that way.”

Aaron, 42, signed with the Steelers in 2025. The team benched him from Friday’s game, shortly before the quarterback claimed that head coach McCarthy, 63, was “unhealthy” in a bombshell interview. While McCarthy has refuted the claims, the Steelers have since parted ways with Aaron.

“I think what’s getting lost in this, though, is just how unacceptable and unprecedented some of the behavior that McCarthy has displayed and how unique that is,” Jordan added on Friday. “I think I would be surprised if he continued to be the head coach moving forward, whether that’s short-term, long-term, whatever it is. I think, having been in locker rooms … there’s too many jobs at stake for people to put up with that behavior.”

He speculated, “I think the Steelers will look into this, but seemingly, the accusations and allegations are confirmed in a lot of ways, and he didn’t refute them. Crazy world that we’re living in, but [it’s a] weird way to see it end.”

Jordan and Aaron recently reunited following a longtime estrangement. Jordan first revealed their family drama on a 2016 episode of his now-wife JoJo Fletcher’s season of The Bachelorette, which his brother later criticized for publicizing their family issues.

Aaron slowly started mending fences with his parents and brothers, Luke and Jordan, following his 2025 wedding to wife Brittani.

“There was some stuff there and then I could tell I was coming to a time where I needed to make it right,” Aaron said on a September episode of the “Not Just Football” podcast, detailing the “tough” conversations the now-reunited relatives had. “I’m in a much better headspace, and I said it before, but my wife deserves a lot of the credit for that just to be even able to to get to that point where I could think about having the conversation.”
