New John Carpenter Meets The Terminator Thriller Confirms HBO Max Release Date

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The new action thriller Onslaught is coming to HBO Max next month, and the release date has just been announced.

Onslaught was released in theaters this September and grossed $3.5 million at the box office. In the film, an Army sniper springs into action after genetically engineered super soldiers go rogue. Celeste Cross, the sniper, has a lot at stake as she has a young child to protect, and she will do whatever it takes to ensure her daughter's safety.

December will also see several other films be added to HBO Max, including Bad Times at the El Royale, BlacKkKlansman, the Rocky and Creed franchise, Free Willy, The Goonies, Mamma Mia! Here We Go Again, Matilda, Meet the Parents, Mrs. Doubtfire and Alien: Romulus, along with the original series My Favorite Murder, C.B. Strike and Harry Potter.

Adria Arjona plays Celeste Cross in Onslaught, which also stars Dan Stevens, Eric Wareheim, Reginald VelJohnson, Michael Biehn, Alex Pereira, Drew Starkey and Rebecca Hall.

Simon Barrett wrote the script for the film, which takes place in the same universe as Wingard's 2014 thriller The Guest. The director and screenwriter also served as producers, alongside Aaron Ryder, Andrew Swett, Jeremy Platt, and Alexander Black. Aside from an underwhelming box office return, Onslaught received mixed-to-positive reviews, landing a 59% on Rotten Tomatoes. The audience score is virtually the same at 60%.

Reviewers claimed the film relied on too many clichés with an incoherent premise. On the other hand, some of the more positive reviews called Onslaught intriguing, entertaining, and intense, and praised Arjona's performance. ScreenRant's Brandon Zachary reviewed Onslaught and gave it a 7 out of 10.

Onslaught is one of A24's more recent films, alongside other 2025 releases like The Drama, Mother Mary, Backrooms, Tony, Primetime and the hit documentary You Can See Everything about Elizabeth Holmes.

Before Onslaught, Wingard had directed the blockbusters Godzilla vs. Kong and Godzilla x Kong: The New Empire, which grossed $470 million and $572 million, respectively. He decided not to return for the upcoming Godzilla x Kong: Supernova in order to direct Onslaught. Wingard has also helmed Blair Witch, Death Note, and You're Next, among other projects.

Onslaught will be released on HBO Max on Tuesday, December 22, 2026.
