These Doctors Are on the Ballot

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

— Here's a look at physicians running for federal and statewide office in Tuesday's election

As the nation gears up for Tuesday's midterms, healthcare is on many voters' minds -- and some voters will see physicians on their ballots this time around, including in congressional and statewide races.

In addition to one senator and 15 representatives -- all physicians -- who are running for re-election, there are also quite a few challengers in the mix.

Here is a list, compiled from various online sources, of physicians to keep an eye on come Tuesday. MedPage Today has interviewed some of these candidates, and those stories are linked below. For a list of nurses running for office, click here.
