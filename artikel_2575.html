Broncos designate running back to return from injured reserve

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Denver Broncos running back Tyler Badie (thumb) returned to practice on Tuesday, opening his 21-day window to be activated from injured reserve. If Badie is not activated to the 53-man roster within three weeks, he will revert to season-ending IR.

The Broncos seem likely to activate Badie ahead of Friday's game against the Pittsburgh Steelers after the club handed down a one-game suspension to J.K. Dobbins for "conduct detrimental to the team." That suspension leaves rookie Jonah Coleman as the only running back on the active roster, and Cody Schrader has maxed out his three practice squad elevations.

In addition to potentially activating Badie, Denver could also promote Schrader to the 53-man roster for Friday's game against the Steelers. Alternatively, Jaret Patterson still has up to three practice squad elevations remaining.

The Broncos could get more reinforcements at running back next week when RJ Harvey (knee) is eligible to return from IR. This week, Badie is eligible to return.
