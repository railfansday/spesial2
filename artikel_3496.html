Matt Rife Denies Using Clip of Lisa Ann Getting Kicked Out of Radio City Music Hall Before Shows

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Matt Rife is firing back at adult star Lisa Ann's claims that he used video of her getting kicked out of his Radio City Music Hall show in cuffs before every one of his shows ... claiming it's never happened!

We got Matt in NYC ... and the comedian says he's never used the clip in question and that she's the one who resurfaced the video.

We reported earlier ... Lisa claimed in a recent podcast appearance that Matt had shown video on his current tour of her getting removed from his 2023 show with a police escort.

She even claimed that she received multiple texts a week about it!

During the incident, Lisa had been detained for 45 minutes before cops released her ... the New York Post reported.

And while Lisa has denied being on her phone before getting kicked out, a source told the publication that the NYPD had footage of her doing so.

We've reached back out to Lisa for comment ... so far, no word back.
