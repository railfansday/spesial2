Real Madrid: the squad almost complete for the Clasico

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

On the eve of the highly anticipated Clásico against FC Barcelona, ​​Real Madrid revealed its squad for this La Liga clash. José Mourinho will be able to count on almost his entire squad for this particularly important meeting. As expected, Raúl Asencio, Ferland Mendy and Rodrygo are still absent, while Vinícius Júnior will also miss out due to suspension. Four absences already known which should therefore not upset the plans of the Portuguese technician.

For the rest, the Madrid group is complete and has serious arguments to challenge the Blaugrana. Kylian Mbappé, Jude Bellingham, Federico Valverde and Arda Güler appear on the list, as do Bernardo Silva, Yan Diomandé and Ibrahima Konaté. José Mourinho will also be able to rely on Thibaut Courtois in the cages and on several offensive solutions, notably Endrick, Brahim Díaz and Carlos Espi. Enough to approach this Clasico with a particularly competitive squad.
