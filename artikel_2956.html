Nvidia Looks for a New Era After Huang

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Bloomberg’s Emily Chang tells Bloomberg This Weekend what her recent interview with Jensen Huang reveals about Nvidia’s succession challenge after the longtime CEO’s abrupt departure. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Chang examines who could succeed Huang, the questions surrounding Nvidia’s China strategy and whether the AI giant can preserve the influence and relationships Huang built over more than three decades.
