AMC Entertainment vs. Roblox: Comparing Volatile and Steady Trajectories in Revenue

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Roblox and AMC have split the quarterly revenue lead almost evenly, but one may soon pull decisively ahead for good.

AMC Entertainment (AMC -1.55%) primarily generates its revenue by owning, managing, and operating an extensive global network of movie theaters and cinema screens located throughout the United States and various European markets to serve a wide audience of everyday moviegoers.

It recently finalized a comprehensive debt refinancing process to better structure its financial obligations, and it simultaneously launched a new internal film distribution division designed to increase the volume of movies shown at its physical locations.

Roblox (RBLX -4.60%) primarily earns its revenue by developing and operating a widespread digital entertainment ecosystem that equips independent creators with a suite of tools to build interactive 3D environments, primarily video games, for a global base of users.

It introduced new strategic initiatives to let developers distribute stand-alone applications across multiple gaming devices, while simultaneously navigating increased regulatory scrutiny and civil legal actions regarding minor safety and parental controls on its platform.

Revenue is a baseline financial figure that helps individual investors track the total volume of money a business brings in before subtracting any underlying operational expenses or taxes. This metric reveals whether an organization is successfully attracting customers and growing its overall business volume over time.

Recent revenue trends reveal a sharp contrast between AMC's cyclical, film-slate-driven recovery after the COVID-19 pandemic kept consumers away for years, and Roblox's steady, compounding digital growth. AMC's sales were always reliant on movie release schedules. However, its spectacular box office receipts in 2026 point not only to a slate of hit films, but also to expanded premium-format screens, particularly IMAX, which drive up ticket prices, and higher per-guest spending on concessions.

As a result, AMC's third quarter revenue of $1.7 billion was an all-time high, surpassing the previous record set in Q2. Before this year, the company's prior record was set back before the pandemic in 2019. This was a long dry spell for the theater chain.

Roblox's revenue trajectory shows consistent year-over-year increases, but that growth is decelerating in 2026. For instance, its Q3 sales of $1.4 billion in 2025 represented a 48% year-over-year jump, but in 2026, Q3 revenue rose 16%. To combat this slowdown, the company introduced Roblox Everywhere, an initiative that seeks to push its content beyond the walled garden of its platform in order to reach more users.
