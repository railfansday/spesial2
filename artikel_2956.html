Browns' Shedeur Sanders to start vs. Saints as Deshaun Watson remains in concussion protocol

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Shedeur Sanders will start at quarterback for the Cleveland Browns in Week 9 against the New Orleans Saints, head coach Todd Monken announced.

Deshaun Watson returned to practice in a limited capacity on Friday, but remains in concussion protocol. Monken said it's unclear if Watson will serve as the backup this week, though he will be listed as questionable.

The Browns' No. 1 quarterback took a helmet-to-helmet hit during the first quarter of Sunday's loss to the Pittsburgh Steelers. Watson missed only four snaps, but later reported experiencing concussion symptoms on Monday night.

Sanders, who will be making his first start since Week 18 last season, made his second appearance of 2026 coming in briefly for Watson against the Steelers. He went 2-for-2 with 2 passing yards before being replaced. He previously entered in Week 3 and threw one pass for a completion against the Carolina Panthers.

During his rookie season in 2025, Sanders made seven starts, throwing for 1,400 yards, 7 touchdowns and 10 interceptions. The Browns went 3-4 over that stretch with Sanders under center.

"Last year I say I was able to see what I needed to see, feel different things during the games and understand what I need to get better at," Sanders said earlier this week. "So then going to this offseason, we worked on that and training camp of course and this offseason and overall I'm just excited for the opportunity."

After the 5-3 Browns play the Saints, they will return home to take on the Houston Texans in Week 10 before going on bye in Week 11.
