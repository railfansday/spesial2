Moderna swung to profit and raised its revenue growth forecast to 15% to 25%

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Third-quarter revenue rose 29% from a year earlier as international COVID vaccine sales climbed; the prior outlook was up to 10% growth

Moderna $MRNA reported net income of $162 million on Friday for the July-September period, reversing a $200 million loss from a year earlier. The company raised its 2026 revenue growth forecast to 15% to 25% from up to 10%.

Diluted earnings per share were $0.38, compared with a loss of $0.51 in the third quarter of 2025. Revenue rose 29% to $1.3 billion from $1.0 billion. U.S. revenue was $780 million, and international revenue was $533 million. The company attributed the increase to stronger COVID vaccine demand abroad, along with shipments made through long-term agreements with governments.

Analysts surveyed by LSEG had expected a loss of $1.25 per share and revenue of $885.1 million, according to CNBC.

"Building on this momentum, we increased our full-year revenue growth outlook to 15%-25%, while continuing to reduce costs and strengthen our balance sheet," Chief Executive Officer Stéphane Bancel said in a statement. Bancel told CNBC, "It's the sales that are stronger and the costs that are also lower."

Research and development expenses for the quarter were $583 million, down 27% from a year earlier. Moderna cited lower clinical development costs after the wind-down of certain late-stage infectious disease studies. For the full year, it expects research and development spending of about $2.7 billion, down from its prior estimate of about $2.9 billion.

Moderna raised its year-end cash and investments outlook to $8.1 billion to $8.3 billion from $4.7 billion to $5.2 billion. It cited improved operational execution and proceeds from a convertible notes financing. In September, the company completed an offering of about $3.0 billion in convertible senior notes, which raised about $2.6 billion in net proceeds after capped call purchases and fees. Moderna continues to expect about half of its 2026 revenue to come from the U.S. and half from international markets.

Moderna stock rose about 3% in premarket trading Friday and was up 2% at 9:36 a.m. ET. The stock had risen more than 600% this year by mid-October and has since climbed more than 700% for the year through Thursday.

In October, Moderna and Merck $MRK presented Phase 3 results at the European Society for Medical Oncology Congress for their personalized cancer vaccine, intismeran autogene. Combined with Merck's Keytruda, it reduced the risk of recurrence or death by 42% compared with Keytruda alone in resected high-risk melanoma. Investors sold off Moderna shares when social media posts exposed some of the results days before the scheduled presentation, according to CNBC.

Moderna has scheduled an analyst day for Nov. 12.
