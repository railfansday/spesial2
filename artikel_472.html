Bessent, Lutnick Clash Over Nvidia China Curbs

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Axios AI policy reporter Maria Curi tells Bloomberg This Weekend about tensions inside the Trump administration over efforts to restrict the flow of advanced Nvidia chips to China and scrutiny of Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick’s handling of Megaspeed. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Curi explains the reported disagreements between Lutnick and other senior officials, including Scott Bessent, and the national security questions surrounding US technology exports to China. (Source: Bloomberg)
