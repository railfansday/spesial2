Now’s the Best Time to Buy Fragrance—Our Favorite Perfumes Are Up to 40% Off for Black Friday

You don’t need me to tell you that perfume is expensive. Just one bottle can run you upwards of $400. I’ve always thought of it as a little luxury (a bottle of designer fragrance sure does cost a lot less than a leather handbag with the same logo all over it). But if you want to smell good without spending all that money, you’ve gotta know where to look. And Black Friday has a surprising lot of really good perfume deals up to 40 percent off.

From our favorite floral perfume to the iconic Glossier You to a Baccarat Rouge 540 dupe that’ll save you hundreds of dollars, there are a plethora of fragrance Black Friday deals to shop right now. Don’t worry—you don’t have to wait until Friday to add to your cart. All of the deals I’ve gathered below (just a sampling of my favorites) are available to buy now on discount. Don’t see your favorite? Nordstrom, Sephora, Ulta, and Macy’s all have tons of fragrance deals right now to shop, so I’ve linked where to find them for you too. Below, though, are a few Cosmo editor picks if you’re in the market to try something new.

Cosmo editor review: “This syrupy, cherry take on the classic La Vie Est Belle fragrance is so sexy and sophisticated at the same time. The note is specifically black cherry, so it’s a bit deeper and more succulent than a standard juicy cherry note. I’ve worn it a bunch of times for nights out this fall.”—BETH GILLETTE, BEAUTY EDITOR

Cosmo editor review: “Although Dossier now creates many of its own original fragrances, the brand started with creating affordable dupes to expensive, iconic perfumes. So it's no surprise to me that Ambery Saffron, its counterpart to Baccarat Rouge 540, smells the most similar of every dupe I've sniffed. It's not exact when you place them side by side, but on skin, I can't really tell that it's two different perfumes. With my beauty editor hat on, I'd say Ambery Saffron is a bit more woodsy than Baccarat, which dries down extremely sweet on my skin. But that's likely because it doesn't have the ambroxan and sugary notes that give BR that syrupy quality. However, I really do love the addition of orange blossom in this Dossier perfume. It creates a teensy hint of citrus that works well with jasmine and saffron.”—BG

Cosmo editor review: “I own a lot of perfumes (ahem, upwards of 200+), and You is one of the longest-lasting scents in my collection. I swear, I can spritz this on a my clothes and still smell it after multiple washes. It's like magic. It's the perfume that kickstarted the whole "your skin but better" movement."—BETH GILLETTE, BEAUTY EDITOR
