Liz Meriwether To Receive ATX TV Festival’s 2027 Showrunner Award

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

ATX TV Festival will present its 2027 Showrunner Award to Emmy-nominated writer, creator, executive producer and showrunner Elizabeth Meriwether.

The Showrunner Award recognizes individuals who foster an environment of collaboration and creativity in their role as showrunner, and whose creative voice has a singular and expansive influence on TV. Previous recipients include creator/showrunners David E. Kelley (Presumed Innocent, Big Little Lies, Ally McBeal) and Bill Lawrence (Rooster, Shrinking, Scrubs).

Meriwether is best known for her work as the creator and showrunner of Fox’s hit comedy New Girl. Her credits also include the FX limited series Dying For Sex, which was nominated for nine Emmys in 2025 and won Michelle Williams the Golden Globe for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Limited Series. Meriwether also created and served as showrunner for Hulu docudrama The Dropout, which won Amanda Seyfried the Emmy and Golden Globe for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Limited Series and was awarded a Critics Choice and Producers Guild Award for Outstanding Limited Series. Most recently, Meriwether created Hulu’s critically acclaimed psychological thriller series Furious, which has been renewed for a second season. Meriwether remains under an overall deal at 20th Television.

The 2027 festival will run from June 3-6 in Austin, TX. Watch on Deadline
