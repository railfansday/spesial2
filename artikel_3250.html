‘Emily In Paris’ Season 6 Trailer Traipses Down Memory Lane From France & Rome To “One Last Story” In Greece

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The first trailer for the final season of Emily in Paris takes a trip all the way back to Season 1 when Lily Collins’ plucky heroine first arrived in the French capital city.

As written in the clip, what started as a detour for the Chicagoan became the adventure of a lifetime, with loves like Gabriel (Lucas Bravo) complicated by Camille (Camille Razat) and other romances with Alfie (Lucien Laviscount) and Marcello Muratori (Eugenio Ranc. Through it all — hot air balloon ads with Alfie, bangs and more — Mindy Chen (Ashley Park) has been there for Emily.

“Stepping into a new era of ambition and romance, Emily faces defining choices that test her ultimate loyalties,” Season 6’s logline reads. “But as unexpected career conflicts and shifting relationships force her to reevaluate where her true future lies, she must make one final choice between her career, her heart, and the life she’s built in Paris.”

The trailer opens with Emily’s arrival in Paris as both herself and the “eyes and ears” around the beautiful city on her social media for her followers. Another flashback shown is her connecting the dots between Chef Gabriel and Camille. Her boss Sylvie’s (Philippine Leroy-Beaulieu) advice to “fall in love” and “make mistakes” also plays across a montage of her riding on a vespa with Marcello and the black and white masquerade ball. Watch on Deadline

It all leads to Greece, the “best for last,” which was hinted at the end of Season 5 when Gabriel sent Emily a postcard from the island nation, where he ended up after working on a yacht. The pair bump into each other looking at flowers while Mindy stars at Alfie stretching before he jumps in the pool, her fiancé Nico just behind her. That was a cliffhanger love triangle ending moment for Emily’s bestie in Season 5.

Executive Producers include Tony Hernandez, Lilly Burns, Andrew Fleming, Alison Brown, Robin Schiff, Grant Sloss, Joe Murphy, Stephen Brown, Ryan McCormick, and Raphaël Benoliel. The series is also produced by Paramount Television Studios, Darren Star Productions, and Jax Media.
