Dental Expert Dr. Theresa Trapp, DDS Guides Patients Through Expectations For Clear Aligner Treatment

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Dr. Theresa Trapp explains how dental health, alignment needs, and consistent daily wear influence clear aligner treatment and long-term results.

EASLEY, S.C., Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ --

Are clear aligners the right choice for straightening your teeth?

Dr. Theresa Trapp, DDS, Dental Expert of Advanced Family Dentistry in Easley, SC, explains what determines treatment eligibility in a HelloNation article. Understanding dental health requirements, treatment expectations, and daily responsibilities helps patients evaluate their options.

Clear aligners are custom-made, removable trays that gradually reposition teeth through controlled pressure. Patients switch between sets according to their dentist's instructions.

Unlike traditional braces, the trays have no metal brackets or wires. They can also be removed for meals, brushing, and flossing, offering greater convenience during treatment.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Clear Aligners?

A good candidate for clear aligners generally has alignment concerns that can be addressed predictably with removable trays.

The article explains that crowded teeth, gaps, and certain bite problems may respond well. However, more complex cases may require braces or another form of orthodontic treatment.

A dental evaluation helps determine whether clear aligner treatment is appropriate. Dentists examine tooth positioning, bite alignment, gum health, and existing dental conditions before recommending treatment.

Dr. Theresa Trapp, DDS, Dental Expert of Easley, SC, emphasizes the importance of following the prescribed wear schedule.

Patients typically wear aligners for 20 to 22 hours daily. Removing them too frequently can delay progress and interfere with planned tooth movement.

Treatment duration varies from several months to a year or longer. Regular appointments help dentists monitor progress and determine whether additional adjustments or refinements are necessary.

What Happens After Orthodontic Treatment?

Completing orthodontic treatment does not guarantee teeth will remain in their new positions without maintenance.

Retainers after clear aligners help prevent gradual shifting. Consistent retainer use is important for preserving results.

The article also notes that treatment costs and insurance benefits vary based on individual circumstances.

How Many Hours Should Aligners Be Worn Daily?

Patients are generally instructed to wear their trays for approximately 20 to 22 hours daily, removing them for meals and oral hygiene.

Can Patients With Gum Disease Get Aligners?

Dr. Theresa Trapp, DDS, Dental Expert of Easley, SC, explains that untreated gum disease or cavities may need treatment before tooth movement begins.
