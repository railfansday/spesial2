Fatbeam Fiber Welcomes Kerry Conn as Chief Operating Officer

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Seasoned broadband executive Kerry Conn joins Fatbeam to strengthen operations, scale business performance, and support the company's next phase of growth.

COEUR D'ALENE, Idaho, Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Fatbeam Fiber, a leading provider of fiber optic broadband in the western United States, is thrilled to announce the addition of Kerry Conn as Chief Operating Officer (COO). Bringing 25 years of leadership experience across broadband, operations, technology, and finance, Kerry will help guide Fatbeam's operational strategy and support the company's continued growth and commitment to delivering reliable connectivity to the communities it serves.

Kerry brings a strong track record of scaling broadband organizations, optimizing operational execution, and bridging the gap between technology and financial strategy to drive sustainable growth. Most recently, she served at Rise Internet, an internet service provider serving residential customers across 16 states, where she led Operations, IT, and Strategic Initiatives.
