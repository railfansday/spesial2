BayCare Proton Center Treats First Patient on Mevion S250 FIT™ with Leo Cancer Care's Marie® Solution

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

BayCare Health System in Florida becomes the second site to offer upright proton therapy using Mevion's compact system, Leo Cancer Care's upright positioning and imaging technology, and RaySearch's treatment planning software.

TAMPA, Fla., Nov. 5, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The BayCare Proton Center has treated its first patient with upright proton therapy, marking an important milestone in its mission to bring advanced cancer care closer to the communities it serves.

BayCare's proton therapy solution is designed around an ultra-compact, fixed-beam proton accelerator combined with a rotational, upright, patient chair which fit inside a typical linear accelerator vault, offering hospitals a different approach to introducing proton therapy without the infrastructure traditionally associated with large, gantry-based proton installations.

This approach is designed to create a more patient-centered treatment experience, while also improving the working environment for radiation therapists by enabling face-to-face interaction with patients during positioning and treatment.
