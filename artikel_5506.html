SuperHero Fire Protection Launches Enterprise Division for Multi-Location Organizations

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

New division gives facility, property, and procurement teams one accountable fire and life safety relationship across supported markets.

LAWRENCEVILLE, Ga., Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- SuperHero Fire Protection recently announced the launch of its new Enterprise Division, created to support organizations managing fire and life safety across multiple properties and markets.

Led by Kim Glasgow, Vice President of Enterprise, the division gives regional facility teams, property portfolio operators, and procurement organizations a dedicated account structure for coordinating inspections, service, repairs, documentation, and ongoing fire and life safety needs across locations.

SuperHero will formally introduce the Enterprise Division to facility management leaders at IFMA World Workplace 2026, taking place Nov. 18â20 at the Anaheim Convention Center in Anaheim, California. Glasgow and Kaycee Carus, Director of Enterprise Sales, will represent SuperHero at booth 449.

"Organizations managing multiple locations are looking for a better way to handle fire and life safety across their portfolios," said Robert Blake-Ward, Chief Executive Officer of SuperHero Fire Protection. "They want the responsiveness and accountability of a local partner, but they also need the reach, technical depth, and operating structure to support them across markets. That combination is where SuperHero is different, and the response we're already seeing to the Enterprise Division tells us there is real demand for it."

SuperHero currently operates through 17 branches across the Southeast and is expanding along the East Coast. More than 900 employees and 600 service vehicles work for customers every day, and the Enterprise model pairs that local field presence with dedicated account ownership at the portfolio level, allowing customers to establish service expectations, approval processes, reporting requirements, and billing structure through one relationship.

Enterprise programs can begin with a defined region or group of locations and expand through the same account structure as customer needs grow. SuperHero teams work across the system brands already installed in customer properties, without requiring proprietary equipment or vendor lock-in.

"For enterprise customers, the challenge usually isn't one inspection or one property," said Glasgow. "It's keeping service, documentation, open items and communication consistent across an entire portfolio. Our job is to give customers clear accountability at the enterprise level while keeping the work close to the properties through local teams."

The division supports recurring inspection and testing, fire sprinkler and fire alarm systems, extinguishers, hydrants, backflow prevention, emergency responder radio coverage, low-voltage systems, and special hazard systems. Enterprise customers can also work with SuperHero on vendor registration, compliance documentation, service standards, and master service agreements structured around their operating requirements.

The company supports inspections, testing, service, and repair across fire sprinkler, fire alarm, extinguisher, hydrant, backflow, ERRC, low-voltage, and other fire and life safety systems. SuperHero combines local accountability with self-performing teams, system-agnostic expertise, and broader regional capabilities for customers managing individual properties and multi-location portfolios.
