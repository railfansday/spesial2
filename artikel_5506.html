PepsiCo is cutting about 1,000 jobs in North America as sales lag

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Free daily briefing on global business news.

PepsiCo's North America beverage volume fell 2% in the latest quarter, and the company cut its earnings outlook in October

PepsiCo $PEP is shedding roughly 1,000 positions in North America in an effort to fix a sales slump in the region, according to CNBC.

Ram Krishnan, CEO of PepsiCo North America, laid out the goals in a Monday memo to employees obtained by CNBC. "We need to be faster, more connected, and more capable, while continuing to invest in growth," Krishnan wrote.

The memo said "too much of how we work today remains complex and fragmented," according to Bloomberg. Krishnan told employees that PepsiCo will "make changes across parts of our organization" over the coming week, according to Bloomberg.

PepsiCo had roughly 306,000 employees globally at the end of last year, some 125,000 of them in the U.S., according to a company filing.

PepsiCo stock rose in afternoon trading on Monday. The stock is down less than 1% this year, while the S&P 500 is up more than 10%.

PepsiCo has also had trouble keeping its share of the salty snack market, according to Bloomberg, with shoppers being choosier about how they spend.

The company cut its full-year core earnings forecast last month. It now expects core earnings per share to grow 2.5% to 3.5% for fiscal 2026, down from prior guidance of growth at the low end of a 5% to 7% range.

North American beverage volume shrank 2% in the third quarter, while volume in the North American convenient foods division was flat. Chairman and CEO Ramon Laguarta said PepsiCo is pursuing structural cost reductions to finance growth-oriented investments.

PepsiCo cut prices on brands such as Lay's and Doritos by as much as 15% in February to win back consumers. Chief Financial Officer Steve Schmitt said on the company's earnings call that the domestic turnaround is moving more slowly than expected.
