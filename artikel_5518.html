My Hero Academia Online 24-Player Battle App 'MY HERO ULTRA RUMBLE Mobile' Release Confirmed & Pre-Registration Now Open!

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Plus, a pre-registration campaign is underway, with launch rewards based on total sign-ups!

IRVINE, Calif., Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Bandai Namco Entertainment Inc. is pleased to announce that MY HERO ULTRA RUMBLE Mobile, a smartphone app for the App Store and Google Play, has been confirmed for release with pre-registration open today. To celebrate, a new teaser PV has been released along with a pre-registration campaign in which the rewards players receive at launch increase with the total number of pre-registrations. Pre-register here!

MY HERO ULTRA RUMBLE Mobile: Release confirmed and pre-registration now open!Reach 300,000 pre-registrations and get all 4 special costumes!The pre-registration campaign is now live, and the rewards you receive at game launch grow with the number of pre-registrations! When pre-registrations reach 300,000, everyone gets the "100 Million Copies Commemorative Costume" for Izuku Midoriya, Katsuki Bakugo, Shoto Todoroki, and All Might! Plus, up to 200 Roll Tickets. We look forward to your registration!

Campaign period: Now through service launch (planned)*The period and content are subject to change without notice.*Rewards are distributed to MY HERO ULTRA RUMBLE Mobile users only.

What is MY HERO ULTRA RUMBLE Mobile?MY HERO ULTRA RUMBLE, the first online multiplayer battle game from My Hero Academia with over 20 million downloads worldwide, coming to mobile so you can play anytime, anywhere! Use intuitive controls to combine the Quirks of fan-favorite Heroes and Villains, and battle your way to victory as a team in a 24-player brawl!

Point 1: Survive the 24-player brawl!Matches are played in a battle royale format with 8 teams of 3. After dropping onto a vast stage, players fight as the playable area shrinks over time, with the goal of being the last team standing. Work with your teammates to claim victory.

Point 2: Make the most of each Quirk!Every Hero and Villain has different strengths depending on their Quirk. Control My Hero Academia characters from a third-person view, lock onto opponents, and attack. Use the unique traits of each Quirk and work together to survive fierce battles. Each character's Quirk can be leveled up with the "Quirk Skill Cards" scattered across the field, and leveled-up Quirk skills become dramatically stronger in both look and effect.

Point 3: Play like a true Hero or Villain!Defeating enemy teams isn't the only way to contribute to your team. Civilians left behind on the stage are another key factor that can decide the outcome. Heroes rescue them and Villains threaten them; by acting the part, you can obtain powerful items that can turn the tide of battle and help your team win.
