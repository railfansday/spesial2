Atomic Mobile unveils multi-carrier IoT service connecting devices across the globe

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

SPRING LAKE, N.J., Nov. 3, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Atomic Mobile today introduced Atomic Orbit , a cellular internet service designed to maintain connectivity for IoT devices by utilizing multiple carrier networks. The service addresses the critical need for reliable data access in scanners, tablets, POS terminals, and smart meters that frequently move across varying signal environments. Atomic Orbit offers global reach across hundreds of operators worldwide.

Relying on a single carrier often leads to coverage gaps, especially for mobile assets or devices deployed in diverse geographic regions. Atomic Orbit solves this by providing access to three national carriers and 18 regional carriers within the United States, alongside global multi-carrier support. This architecture ensures that devices automatically switch to the strongest available signal, maintaining uptime for essential business operations. By eliminating the limitations of a single-provider model, the platform enables enterprises to deploy devices globally without the risk of localized dead zones.

Aaron Ogden, VP of of Customer Success, says "Over the years, I've worked across finance, energy, education, logistics, and healthcare, and I've seen how much day-to-day operations depend on reliable connectivity. Whether it's a payment terminal processing a sale, a remote utility sensor reporting a reading, a fleet tracker sending its location, a field technician pulling up a work order on a tablet, or a backup solution, the connection matters wherever the work happens. A multi-carrier SIM gives organizations access to more than one network, helping keep devices connected as they move between locations or when coverage varies or fails."

The implementation of eSIM technology further simplifies logistics, allowing enterprises to manage profits by reducing the operational complexity of physical hardware swaps when changing service plans or carriers. This flexibility is critical for organizations looking to scale their IoT footprint without increasing administrative overhead.

Learn more about Atomic Orbit at https://atomicmobile.com/products/orbit .

Atomic Mobile developed the product in response to direct requests from customers seeking a reliable multi-carrier service for connected devices both in domestic and international markets. The service is available now for enterprises requiring high-availability mobile connectivity.
