Onity Group returns to profitability on servicing gains

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Onity Group returned to profitability in Q3 2026, reporting $3.5 million in net income as servicing profitability improved. The company added $24.7 billion of servicing and ended the quarter with $343 billion in servicing UPB, up 9% year over year.  AI Summary

Onity Group, the parent company of Onity Mortgage — formerly known as PHH Mortgage Corp., and its subsidiary, Liberty Reverse Mortgage — returned to profitability in the third quarter of 2026 as stronger servicing results offset declining income from its originations business.

The company on Thursday morning reported net income attributable to common stockholders of $3.5 million, or 41 cents per diluted share, compared with a net loss of $12.9 million, or $1.53 per diluted share, in the second quarter.

A year earlier, Onity reported net income of $17.7 million, or $2.03 per diluted share.

The company reported adjusted pretax income of $30 million in the third quarter, up from $14 million in the second quarter. Its annualized adjusted return on equity rose to 19%, compared with 9% in the second quarter.

Onity said market volatility increased hedge costs and affected agency loan sales, creating an unfavorable pretax impact of $11 million, or 95 cents per share after taxes, compared with the second quarter.

In Q2, Onity reported a $13 million net loss that included $9 million in pretax costs associated with its reverse mortgage asset sale and legacy subservicing transfer to Finance of America, as well as $24 million in unfavorable asset fair value changes.

“Our third quarter results demonstrate the resilience of our balanced business and the progress we made to improve returns on a sequential quarter basis,” Glen Messina, Onity’s chair, president and CEO, said in a statement.

Servicing profitability improved sequentially, more than offsetting weaker performance in originations, Messina said. The company also reported a record operating efficiency ratio of 51% for the quarter and 52% over the past 12 months.

Onity’s servicing segment generated $7 million in pretax income and $29 million in adjusted pretax income, up from $12 million in adjusted pretax income in the second quarter.

The originations segment reported $24 million in both pretax and adjusted pretax income, down 15% from $28 million in adjusted pretax income in the prior quarter.

The company’s servicing portfolio continued to expand. Onity reported $24.7 billion in total servicing additions during the quarter, bringing its unpaid principal balance (UPB) to $343 billion as of Sept. 30, up 9% from the same period in 2025.

Subservicing additions reached $53 billion as of Sept. 30, exceeding the company’s full-year target. Onity also reported a 57% refinance recapture rate, an increase of 18 percentage points from the third quarter of 2025.

Servicing advances declined 28% from the third quarter of 2025 to $375 million, while owned forward servicing UPB increased 49% over the same period to $179 billion.

The company repurchased 457,971 shares through Sept. 30, representing 6% of its outstanding shares. The repurchases increased book value per share by $1.82. Book value per share stood at $74 at quarter’s end, up $12 from a year earlier.

Messina said the company expects to navigate the current interest rate cycle by relying on its servicing and originations businesses, which respond differently to market conditions.

In a note published Thursday, analysts at Keefe, Bruyette & Woods (KBW) graded Onity as an outperformer with a target share price of $55.00. Onity shares were trading near $30.85 on Thursday, up less than 1% from the prior day.

“The company reaffirmed its 2026 outlook, targeting the low end of its 10-15% adjusted ROE range, total servicing UPB growth of 5-15%, continued high MSR hedge effectiveness, and maintenance of its operating efficiency ratio,” the analysts wrote.
