Man City plot ‘severe’ Jeremy Monga ‘action’ amid Swansea loan latest – Club adviser

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Manchester City are likely to recall young winger Jeremy Monga from his loan spell with Swansea City and find another solution for him in January.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the Citizens need to take “severe action” to help their struggling starlet.

The 17-year-old joined Championship outfit Swansea on a season-long loan in the summer, but is still yet to play a single minute for them so far this season.

Wyness believes that City will be left with no choice but to recall Monga in January, and find another club who are willing to take him on loan for the second half of the campaign.

The Manchester giants fought off heavy competition to sign the youngster from Leicester City in a £10million deal in the summer, but he has since seen his progress stall.

Monga was seen standing on his own on the pitch after Swansea’s recent draw with West Brom and is clearly unhappy with his situation at the moment.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy business advising elite clubs – believes that City will likely recall Monga and try to find a new loan for him with “guaranteed minutes” this winter.

Swansea are currently searching for a new manager after Vitor Matos’ surprise departure to join Strasbourg, and that could potentially change Monga’s fate.

However, speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider’s Inside Track podcast, Wyness outlined that if the starlet’s game time doesn’t dramatically improve in the coming weeks, then City will cut his loan spell short.

“City can no longer afford to have valuable assets dormant right now. They've got to make everything work hard,” he said.

“While they may have been a little bit more content to let things ride for a while, now I think you're going to see severe action from City. I think it's a failure of a loan agreement, definitely.

“I think he'll be recalled and he'll either go elsewhere with guaranteed minutes, or else he'd be integrated somehow into the City system.

“But I think it'll be a loan with a guarantee of minutes because there's no doubt the kid's a talent, but it’s just not working at Swansea.”

Wyness previously told Football Insider last week that City won’t entertain any idea of selling young goalkeeper Pierce Charles until at least the summer of 2028.

The 21-year-old is currently out on loan in the Championship with QPR and excelled during the recent international break to help Northern Ireland secure a shock promotion to the top tier of the Nations League.

Wyness is adamant that City must “safeguard” their long-term goalkeeping options at the club, given the financial charges that they are currently facing.

Charles is seen as a future number one at the Etihad Stadium, and his time may come sooner rather than later depending on the punishment that the club receives.
