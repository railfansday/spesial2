Fourth Alien Arrested in North Georgia for Voting in Federal Elections

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Benito Covarrubias Iniguez, an illegal alien from Mexico residing in Winder, Georgia, appeared today before United States Magistrate Judge Anna W. Howard on federal charges of making a false claim of citizenship in order to vote after allegedly obtaining a fraudulent passport.
