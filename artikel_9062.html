Have you been partaking in Inktober? We might have a little tip to help you out. Our artist, Phoebe Shore put together an easy-peasy video tutorial for creating your own spook-inspired inky brushes in Procreate.

You'll need one of the best drawing tablets to get started, but more specifically, it has to be one of the best iPads for drawing, since Procreate is not available for Android tablets, Windows PCs, or standard desktop computers.
