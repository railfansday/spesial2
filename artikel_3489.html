DMZ key and locations in Modern Warfare 4

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

There are loads of DMZ keys in Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4. Used to open both locked containers and locked doors, keys are your fast-track to getting your hands on some of the best loot the extraction mode has to offer. However, when you receive a key, you're only given a brief description of where to use it, so we're going to outline where you can use every key the game has to offer.

Here are all the keys we've found in Modern Warfare 4's DMZ mode and where to use them, listed in alphabetical order by the name of the key.

Note that this guide is a work-in-progress, so if the key you're looking for isn't listed here, check back soon.

Update (Nov. 18): We've added directions for where to find 14 more keys in DMZ.

There are two locked doors for the same building with slightly different names. Whether you have the Art Complex Gate Key or the Hajin Art Complex Key, you want to head to the Hanwoori Design Complex in The Grid, which is the southeasternmost building inside the red-shaded area on the map.

The Hajin Art Complex Key can be used on the west side of the building to enter through the back way, while the Art Complex Gate key is used on the eastern side. You can get into this building without a key by going through the front, but that's sure to bring the attention of a lot of enemies.

The Bank Branch Vault Access Key can open four different doors, and you'll always find the key near one of them. One can be found in northern Songdo Wharf, another is west of the Wolves Stadium, the third is in Cheongun Village, south of the casino, and the final one is inside the main bank headquarters in The Grid.

Using the key is a little complicated, though: you need to search nearby for a note that will reveal three numbers of a four-digit code, and you'll need to find a car battery to restore power to the door. There will usually be a car battery in the vicinity, but if not, head to your nearest gas station to search for one, as they're more commonly found there. Once you have the battery, search for the nearby electrical breaker — it'll never be too far from the location of the door — then place it in the breaker.

Once the breaker has power again, return to the door and brute force the code, since you'll know three of the four digits from the note. Succeed, and you'll be in the bank vault.

The Blackmarket Weapons Locker Key can be used in two different locations in Hajin. Look for the blackmarket weapons store, identifiable by its iron wrought gates and red advert on the roof. One of them is in southern Deadtown, while the other is in eastern Hanrin Village. Go to the basement to find two lockers at each location, so if you have a second Blackmarket Weapons Locker Key, it's worth bringing with you.

Along with finding it as loot in Hajin, the Coin Mining Den Key can also be obtained by completing the "Operating Shopping Hacks" story mission. It opens a door on the third floor of an otherwise unassuming building directly north of the Netshin Tech Complex and east of the gas station found to the west of the NuriGO Mall. Inside, you'll find a number of lootable containers, including plenty of server racks, which are handy for electronic sensors.

The Chang-san Air Base is one of the most fortified areas in all of Hajin, which means you're going to have a constant swarm of enemies on you. To use the Commandant Office Key, head to the eastern side of the second floor of the main building in the air base, and you'll find the locked door here.

Completing the "Operation Silent Ledger" story mission will net you the Counting Room Vault Key, but to use it, you'll need to go back into the casino. Our dedicated guide explains exactly where to go, but briefly, you'll want to take an immediate right-hand turn as you enter through the main entrance then go through the brown door in the following corridor. You'll find a bunch of safes, loose cash, and containers inside.

Head to the southeasternmost building in The Grid to find the Hajin art complex, also known as the Hanwoori Design Complex, according to the map. You'll find the locked door to the director's office in the center of the building on the seventh floor.

The Executive Arms Auction Key can be used inside the DBN Headquarters building, which is the tallest skyscraper inside The Grid. If you choose the "Operation Shopping Hacks" mission, you'll spawn nearby. This place is highly fortified, so there are loads of enemies around, and to find the locked room, you'll need to use the various elevators and ziplines dotted around to go to the very top floor (but not the roof). Here, you'll find the locked door pictured above.

Another door inside the Chang-San Air Base, this room is a little easier to get to. It's on the ground floor in the building immediately on the right-hand side of the main entrance, which also contains ammo and armor caches. It's on the eastern side of the building, in a corridor with lots of shelving units.

There are six fire stations dotted across Hajin, and the Fire Station Storage Key can be used at any one of them. You will also usually find a key as floor loot in each one. When you have the key, go upstairs and there will be a locked door to open with the key, usually on the left at the top of the staircase.

The Gangbyeon Apartment A Key can be used on an apartment block at the northeastern end of Hajin River Heights. The building is next to the compound, and is the one that is perpendicular to the river to the south. If you need to get inside Gangbyeon apartment A without the key, then you can descend into the parking lot below and search for the staircase that will take you up inside the apartment block.

The key for Gangbyeon apartment B can usually be found inside Gangbyeon apartment A, which is quite handy. I found it on top of a table in one of the apartments. Gangbyeon apartment B is the building right next to apartment A, running parallel to the river. The key can be used to get inside either the first floor, the basement, or the roof, and if you have the militia arms depot key, you'll need to get inside apartment B first. Apartment B has several apartments to explore, full of decent loot.

There are plenty of gas stations across Hajin, but this one is special because it has a rather large basement. The gas station in question is the one located southeast of The Grid, south of the road cutting horizontally across the big construction site. Go into the basement and the first door on your left is the electrical room.

Southeast of Imjin Farmland is where you'll find the mill stronghold, and in the very center of this point of interest is a tower. Enter via the door at the bottom to find a parkour section, where you must clamber from ledge to ledge in order to ascend. At the very top are two doors next to each other, both of which require Gorani's Scout Overlook Key.

Much like the Blackmarket Weapons Locker Key, there are two locations for the Gun Store Weapons Locker Key, and each location has multiple lockers inside. The gun stores can be found in western Cheongun Village and northern Songdo Wharf. Each of them has an arched red brick roof and a sign above the entrance depicting a cartoon character. In the basement, you'll find multiple lockers that the keys can be used on.

If you've got your hands on the Haejeok Radio Station Key, you're going to want to head to an isolated building directly north of Imjin Farmland and west of Cheonwang Peak Array, in grid square D6. The locked door to the radio station itself is upstairs in the main building.

Directly north of the re-education center in Deadtown is where you'll find the school, which has three doors facing west on the exterior of the building. The middle door is the Haksan school armory.

The Haneul Nuclear Reactor is a dangerous place at the best of times, but if there's anywhere in the location that could be considered relatively safe, it's the Haneul Boat House. If you take a boat around the outside of the reactor, you can park it quietly on the northern tip, where you'll find the boat house guarded by just a few enemies.

The Hanex Guard Station Key is used inside the bank within The Grid, which is the slightly smaller building next to the DBN Headquarters, which is the tallest building there. Go inside and take a zipline to the roof, then go down one floor to find the bank's vault. The locked door inside this room, pictured above, is where you can use the Hanex Guard Station Key.

The Hanwoori Design Complex is a large building in the southeast corner of The Grid, the large, high-threat zone south of NuriGO Mall. The only unlocked entrance to the Design Complex is through the front, which has a number of enemies guarding it, but thankfully, this room isn't too far from the entrance. Go inside, go straight through the double doors on the other side of the room, then look to your left. The locked door to the Hanwoori Art Records room is under a bright light, next to some half-closed shutters.

The Haneul Nuclear Reactor is one of the most dangerous parts of Hajin, not just because of the enemies, but also due to the radiation. Just inside the radiation, in the southernmost part of the area, you can find a locked warehouse which can be opened with the Hazard Storehouse Key.

At Imjin Mill, southeast of Imjin Farmland, there's a rectangular building on the other side of the barbed wire fence to the road. On the second floor of this building, there's a locked door leading to the Hoesik supply room.

In Mirae General Hospital, which is northwest of The Grid and southeast of Cheongun Village, there's a basement that contains a large generator, more electrical equipment, and a morgue. As you come to the bottom of the stairs, go through the doors on your right, and there will be a locked door to the Hospital Chemical Lab on your left.

If you're looking for the Hospital Chemical Lab Key, I found it on a desk on the seventh floor, inside room 703.

To use the Hospital Chemical Locker Key, you need to also have the Hospital Chemical Lab Key, because the lockers are found inside the lab. So follow the instructions above, then you can use up to three Hospital Chemical Locker Keys at once to open the yellow lockers in front of you.

Inside Imjin Mill, southeast of Imjin Farmland, the foreman's office can be found on the second floor. It's right next to the short walkway with a window that you can jump through if you climb up the crates, and is marked with a sign that says "office" next to the wooden door.

The Cheonwang Peak Array is one of the lesser-visited named locations in Hajin, found directly north of the Imjin Farmland area and west of the Wolves Stadium. To find Iskra's Control Room, go inside the long rectangular building found at the bottom of the array, then climb the stairs to the top floor. Iskra's Control Room is behind an inconspicuous locked brown door next to a green sofa.

Jang-ui's Converted Power Room Key can be used in NuriGO Mall, which means you're going to want to bring night-vision goggles, because it is dark down there. Directly below the "NuriGO Mall" text on the map, and right next to the stronghold icon, you'll find a zipline descending underground. Use this, then turn left and aim straight for the corner of the room in the distance. This is Jang-ui's Converted Power Room, and you can get the key (sometimes, because loot isn't guaranteed in DMZ) by killing him.

Jin-Tae's Private Lounge can only be accessed by looting the key off Jin-Tae, the lieutenant who patrols the Heavenly Luck Casino in Cheongun Village. When you get the key, his private lounge is the penthouse suite, but to get up there, you need to go into the parking garage and turn on the power by using the generator. Once the power is on, use the lift from the lobby to go all the way up. You'll find the door to his private lounge on the western side of the top floor.

Inside the 14th Political Prison, the main building is known as The Citadel. At the northern tip of the three-pronged layout, you'll find two separate locked doors leading to the same room, both of which require Kang's Arms Incinerator Key.

Hotel Lucera can be found on the eastern side of The Grid, directly north of the Hajin art complex building. You may think suite 703 can be found on the seventh floor, but you'd be incorrect. Take the zipline all the way up the elevator shaft to the 12th floor, then look for one of two open suites. If you've played through the campaign, you'll be familiar with this area. This key is specifically for the bedroom, so if the bedroom is open in the suite you're in, check the other one down the corridor.

The Mechanic's Shack Key can be used to unlock a small building in southern Cheongun Village, northwest of Mirae General Hospital. It's directly south of the smaller hospital inside the village, with a slanted blue roof, next to a larger residential building.

The militia arms depot is located inside Gangbyeon apartment B, which is northeast of Hajin River Heights. Once you've got the key for apartment B, which can usually be found inside apartment A (details on those keys above, including how to get into apartment A without a key), you'll find the locked door for the militia arms depot on the first floor. Inside is an apartment with loads of locked containers and high-tier loot.

You'll receive the Mirae Electrical Room Key upon completing "Operation Critical Extraction," which is the story mission where you must escort a high-value target out of the general hospital. This key unlocks a room in the underground parking lot for the hospital, which can be found by going to the northwestern corner. Enter through a door at the back and follow the signs for parking, which will lead you to a parking lot with purple walls. At the back is a room with iron bars over the windows and "danger" above the door, which can be unlocked with the key.

The Netshin Tech Complex is a huge building on the western side of The Grid, which means it's heavily defended. It's the one shaped like a trapezoid, with windows covering almost every part of it on the shorter side. If you make your way to the 14th floor, you'll find a locked door leading to the Netshin IT room on the flat southern edge of the building, in the corner of the server room.

The Old Barber Shop is inside a building at the southernmost tip of Songdo Wharf, which is the easternmost region in Hajin. Inside the curve of the road as it bends around due to the coastline, there's a building with two white doors on the ground floor. On the rightmost side of these is the Old Barber Shop.

The Police Station Armory Key is one of the most common keys in DMZ, as it opens armories in all five police stations in Hajin. The map above shows all the locations. In each police station, the armory can be found in the basement, where you'll find weapon crates and other locked containers.

Go north of the police station on the east side of Wolves Stadium, and you'll find the police weapon depository, a tiny building where you can use the key to open a small room.

On the eastern side of the main 14th Political Prison complex, you'll find smaller buildings made up of living quarters, offices, and storage units. One of the easternmost buildings is a corrugated metal shed, closest to the road that goes around the whole named location. This is where you can use the Prison Encampment Storage Key.

In the very southwest corner of Cheongun Village lies a fire station and a gas station. Between them is a rail depot. Go inside here and head down to the basement, where you'll likely find a couple of enemies, along with two doors leading into the Rail Depot Storage room, where you can use the key.

Reactor 02 is the smaller of the two main buildings at Haneul Nuclear Reactor, and you can get inside by climbing one of the many ladders or pipes on the exterior of the whole facility, then dropping into the destroyed part. The control room is in the very northwest corner, at the top of a staircase.

Inside Reactor 02 at the Haneul Nuclear Reactor is a large pool of murky water. To find the Reactor DRC Locker, you've got to dive into the water and find it at the bottom. Pictured above is the pool you need to find. If you're struggling to get inside Reactor 02 in the first place, use a ladder or pipe to climb onto the rooftop then drop into the destroyed building.

In Deadtown, the re-education center is the most notable landmark, as it's the most central building and it has a sprawling underground section. On its south side, you'll see it has a small building attached. The door for this is on the first floor, aka ground level, near the stairs leading down into the basement.

The rice mill is in the southeast of Imjin Farmland, and the Rice Mill Bunker Area Key can be used on a door found in the central building, beneath the stronghold icon on the map. Near the middle of this building is a concrete staircase descending with a locked green door at the bottom.

Roze's Highrise Armory is another locked door that can be found in the Netshin Tech Complex, which is the colossal glass-walled building found on the western side of The Grid. This one is also on the 14th floor, but since this building is split into two sections, you want to make sure you're on the eastern side. If you're struggling to find it, go up to the 15th floor, where you'll find a bridge across to the other half of the building, then descend a floor. The door is on the other side of the armory itself, which has gated windows, and you can get this key as a random drop when you kill Roze.

Head to the two-story building south of the hospital in the northwest corner of the Wolves Stadium location. Go up to the second floor, and you'll find a locked door to the shop records room at the top of the stairs.

The Chemist is a lieutenant always found inside the Haneul Nuclear Reactor, so if you've got his key, chances are, you're nearby. You can use The Chemist's Secure Armory Key to the north of the main compound, just over the fence from the Haneul Boat House.

The Citadel is the main building within the 14th Political Prison, found in northwestern Hajin. To get inside, you must enter through one of the smaller adjoining buildings. The east wing office can be found right next to the storehouse on ground level, at the end of the long section containing all the cells, indicated with a red door.

The east wing is the long section heading eastwards. It looks identical to the south wing, with dozens of cells. The storehouse is found at the very end on the first floor.

Mirae General Hospital can be found northwest of The Grid and southeast of Cheongun Village. It's a rather large building with plenty of enemies inside, and it has multiple entry points. If you make your way to the seventh floor via either the stairs or the elevator, you can find The Doctor's Command Office immediately left after you exit the stairwell, marked as room 704.

The Re-education Center in Deadtown is the main point of interest here, and it has a sprawling basement that connects the main building to another building over the road. No matter which side you enter from, descend as deep as possible until you find the area with the cells, and you'll find a blue door in a corner, just along from where you'll also find the Underground Propaganda Store Room.

The Re-education Center Annex in Deadtown has a big underground section that connects it with the building to the northwest, and in the center is a long corridor with a series of blue doors. The central one can be opened with the Underground Propaganda Store Room Key, and while there isn't much to loot in here, you can pull a lever to unlock another locked door nearby.

The Utility Maintenance Key can be used at the Haneul Nuclear Reactor, which is the irradiated zone in the northeast of Hajin. Directly south of the main reactor building is an area full of electrical transformers. There are three staircases descending underground. Follow the path underground to the west (unless you go down the westernmost staircase, in which case look east) and you'll find a single locked door that the Utility Maintenance Key can be used on.

The Vault Armory is in the underground parking garage at the Heavenly Luck Casino, near the western exit. You're looking for a locked gate inside an area with bars on the window. Even if you don't have a key for the door, you can still loot two containers through the holes in the windows.

Another locked door inside the Chang-San Air Base, you can find the weapons mod workshop on the second floor of a building northwest of the main stronghold. The room is on the northern side of the building, next to a cork board and red sign on a white wall, at the top of a staircase.
