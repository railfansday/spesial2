Man Charged With Murder in Shooting of Texas Mom of 4 on Her 35th Birthday

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

A man was arrested in the killing of a Texas woman who was fatally shot along a freeway while driving home from work on her 35th birthday.

Juan Gonzalez-Orduna was booked in jail on a murder charge on Sunday, November 22, in connection with the November 15 death of Stephanie Gonzalez, the Dallas Police Department said in a new statement.

Gonzalez was shot after she had been involved in a crash on the C.F. Hawn Freeway in Dallas the evening of November 15, police said in an initial statement. She died of her injuries at a hospital.

Before Gonzalez-Orduna’s arrest, Gonzalez’s family said they believed she had been targeted because of the timing of the killing, which occurred on her birthday as she headed home to her four children.

Gonzalez’s cousin Vivian Britton told NBC DFW that Gonzalez had been about 10 minutes away from her house when she was killed after a day of work at Quality Sausage Company in West Dallas, where she was a company supervisor.

“She was on her way home to see her babies,” Britton said of Gonzalez’s children, which includes a baby who is just a few months old.

Gonzalez-Orduna, 29, has been detained at the Lew Sterrett Jail, where he’s also being held on an immigration hold over his alleged illegal immigration status, records show.

It wasn’t immediately clear whether Gonzalez-Orduna had an attorney who could comment on his behalf.

Dallas police haven’t specified a potential motive for Gonzalez’s killing.

Gonzalez “was a loving daughter, wife, mother, sister, aunt, and friend whose warm spirit touched the hearts of everyone who knew her,” reads her online obituary.

She is survived by her mother, brother, sister and nephew, in addition to her four children and husband of more than two years, Luis Enriquez Gonzalez, according to her obituary.

The obituary describes Gonzalez as a devoted mother who deeply loved her children and a person who had a great love of animals, including those she cared for on her farm.

“Whether she was spending time with family, enjoying music, or tending to her farm, she embraced life with warmth and appreciation,” the obituary states.

A woman who identified herself as Gonzalez’s cousin created a GoFundMe to support Gonzalez’s funeral expenses as well as her family.

“Our family is heartbroken by this loss, and it is hard to put into words how deeply Stephanie will be missed,” the woman, Julissa Clemente, wrote.

Clemente also wrote that Gonzalez’s husband and four children “are now facing an unimaginable loss without her.”

Dallas police are asking anyone with information related to Gonzalez’s death to contact Dallas Police Detective R. Kramer at 214-671-3608.
