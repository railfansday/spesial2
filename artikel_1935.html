Sources: Arsenal well placed to leverage ‘good relations’ for Carlos Forbs deal

Arsenal will be one of the leading contenders to sign Club Brugge winger Carlos Forbs next summer, sources have told Football Insider.

Brugge have already indicated that the 22-year-old is not for sale in the middle of the campaign, but it is likely to be a different story ahead of next season.

The Belgian side are “not surprised” by the interest in their star man, and are braced for more clubs to come to the table for him if his strong form continues.

Sources say that Arsenal are prepared to leverage their “good relations” with Brugge to try and sign Forbs next summer, having done business together ahead of this term.

The North London outfit splashed out to sign Christos Tzolis from Brugge for £34million, and set a new record transfer for the Pro League in the process.

Forbs has been in impressive form so far this season, and already has 12 goal involvements to his name, including four in the Champions League.

Arsenal are in the market for a new left-sided attacker in the upcoming windows, having identified the position as an area of key priority moving forward.

They have already been linked with several candidates for the role in recent months, including the likes of RB Leipzig’s Antonio Nusa.

However, there are major doubts as to whether Arsenal would be able to afford the Norwegian’s lofty price tag in the middle of the campaign.

As such, their search for a new wideman could yet progress into next summer’s window, when Forbs will come onto the table as a genuine option.

He is not yet proven in one of Europe’s elite-level leagues, but he is continuing to catch the eye as one of Brugge’s main men.

Football Insider revealed last week that Nusa would be keen on the idea of moving to Arsenal next summer.

The 21-year-old has excelled in the Bundesliga with 12 goal involvements from just 10 league games so far this term.

Sources say that Nusa views Arsenal as a “very attractive project” at the moment, given the success they have enjoyed in recent years.

He is very much open to the idea of a move to the Emirates Stadium, but it may take a huge financial commitment from the club to make it happen.
