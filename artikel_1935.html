Evangelos Marinakis ‘very happy’ after Nottingham Forest latest - ‘there's a big grin on his face'

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Nottingham Forest owner Evangelos Marinakis is starting to believe that his club have turned a corner after their surprise 3-1 win over Manchester City last weekend.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that things are slowly “starting to click” at the City Ground.

Oliver Glasner’s side had won just one of their opening nine Premier League games this season, but their recent victory over City has lifted them out of the relegation zone.

Wyness believes that there is a newfound “confidence” coursing through the Forest group, and expects their season to kick-start after the international break.

Glasner has been under huge scrutiny from the fanbase in recent weeks, but last weekend’s win appears to have eased the pressure surrounding his job.

The mood at Forest has been dramatically changed by the win, with even Marinakis now in high spirits despite the poor start to the campaign.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that Forest are slowly starting to escape the “turbulence” that surrounded them at the start of the season.

Glasner’s side have been putting in strong performances in recent weeks, but have perhaps not had the correct rewards for their displays.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider’s Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained how the win over City has come at the perfect time for the Reds.

“Now of course, Marinakis will have been very happy to have beaten Man City; you can imagine that will be a big grin on his face and you know all of a sudden yes, Glasner has got a chance now,” he said.

“It's also, as you say, about the players taking ownership of the problem, so the players took ownership and they believed in Glasner's leadership.

“They knew that things were almost but not quite, and now they're starting to click. So he's not out of the woods yet, but if they can make it two wins at home at the City Ground, I think, then he's turned the corner. And I can see that happening now.

“There's a confidence in the squad. The break's come at a decent time for them, and they're going to regroup now with a very positive way going in after Man City. So I think the turbulence at the City Ground is seeming to smooth over slightly.”

Former Manchester United chief scout Mick Brown recently told Football Insider that his former club may be able to prise centre-back Murillo away from Forest in January.

The Brazilian is building a growing reputation for himself at the City Ground, and United are long-term admirers of his quality.

Brown believes that the defender could potentially be tempted away from Forest if their results don't improve, with a "huge" offer also likely to tempt the club into a sale.

The ex-recruitment man feels that Forest are vulnerable to potentially losing their best players, unless they can fight their way back towards the European places.
