Cadence Education Celebrates $2.7 Million Raised in Multi-Year Partnership Benefiting St. Jude Children's Research Hospital®

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Early Childhood Education Leader Renews Commitment to Lifesaving Mission, Highlighting Psychosocial Services This Fall

SCOTTSDALE, Ariz., Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Cadence Education, one of the nation's premier early childhood education providers operating more than 350 private preschools across the United States, is proud to celebrate a remarkable milestone in its ongoing partnership with St. Jude Children's Research Hospital â raising $2.7 million to support the lifesaving mission of St. Jude since the partnership began in 2018.

What began eight years ago as a shared commitment to the well-being of children has become one of the most meaningful chapters in Cadence Education's history. Earlier this year, the company's name was added to the Donor Dream Wall at St. Jude, an honor reserved for those who have made a lasting and significant impact on the hospital's mission. Cadence Education is proud to stand as the only preschool company featured on the Donor Dream Wall â a distinction that reflects the extraordinary commitment of every family, staff, teacher, and school leader who have come together year after year to make a profound difference in the lives of children and families served by St. Jude.

This fall, Cadence Education schools across the country came together once again, raising funds through school-wide trike-a-thons, bake sales, and family-giving campaigns. The new total reflects the generosity of every school community, the families who gave and cheered on their children, and the Cadence Education team members who supported the effort from start to finish.

Cadence Education is excited to focus its fundraising on supporting the psychosocial services available to St. Jude families as part of their comprehensive treatment. This reflects a shared St. Jude and Cadence Education belief that social-emotional support is a critical factor in a child's health, well-being, and development. St. Jude specialists provide support through age-specialized education and child life therapists, emotional support such as spiritual care and social work, and therapeutic programs such as music therapy and psychologists, establishing a nurturing environment for the entire treatment and healing process.

Cadence Education is similarly committed to the development and support of the whole child, as reflected in its cornerstone character education and social-emotional learning program, Kind Child. The program is designed to help children develop empathy, kindness, responsibility, and respect for others. Through integrated classroom experiences, family engagement, and community service opportunities, Kind Child nurtures the whole child and empowers young learners to build positive relationships, contribute to their communities, and develop the social-emotional skills that support lifelong success.

"At Cadence Education, our mission is to care for children and their families," said Leigh-Ellen Louie, CEO. "Partnering with St. Jude Children's Research Hospital is a natural extension of that mission â because every child deserves the chance to grow, learn, and thrive. Reaching $2.7 million in donations is a testament to the incredible generosity of our school communities and the belief that we can make a difference in the lives of children far beyond our own classrooms. As the earliest major supporting company from the early childhood education industry, we are proud to see others join this cause."

"Cadence Education has built a remarkable culture of generosity that continues to make a meaningful difference for the children and families served by St. Jude," said Lyles Eddins, senior vice president of advancement for ALSAC, the fundraising and awareness organization for St. Jude. "We are deeply grateful to the educators, staff, families and students whose passion and dedication have helped make this partnership so impactful. Partnerships like this help make cures possible for children with cancer and other life-threatening diseases, giving more children the chance to live their best lives and celebrate every moment."
