Iraola: Liverpool improvement not only down to Wirtz and Isak

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Andoni Iraola insisted Liverpool's recent turnaround in form was "not about Florian Wirtz or Alexander Isak".

The Reds have won each of their last three games in all competitions, following up a 4-0 win over Manchester United in the Premier League with a 3-0 Champions League victory at Club Brugge.

Isak scored twice in the win against Brugge, while also netting in their league victory over Crystal Palace before the international break.

Wirtz also found the back of the net in midweek in the Champions League, while he has both scored and assisted a goal in his last two Premier League appearances.

The only Liverpool player to do so three times in a row is Mohamed Salah, who did it twice, in December 2018 and October 2021.

However, Iraola believes his whole Liverpool team have made improvements on the ball in recent weeks.

"For me, it is not about Florian or Alex," said Iraola.

"If we play well, someone will score a goal, give an assist, make the numbers. It's about how those players help us play well.

"If we have a good offensive volume, someone will score the goals. We have been good in possession in the last few games, I think we are improving a lot on the ball."

“It’s a big game for everyone involved and I’m very happy to be part of it.”  https://t.co/rYdhYlcTv7

Liverpool face Everton this weekend in Iraola's first Merseyside derby since taking over from Arne Slot.

Liverpool have lost just one of their last 15 Premier League away games against Everton (W4 D10), with 10 of their last 14 visits finishing level.

"I haven't lived this derby," said the Spaniard. "But it doesn't take a lot when you are here to decide it's a big game for everybody involved.

"I am very happy to be part of it and I really hope I enjoy it. To enjoy it, there is only one way, to win the game.

"Derbies are always different. It doesn't matter where you are in the standings. Normally, the type of game is different and you can feel it from the beginning.

"We have to do our part, our duels, be at a good level. We have to know it's a derby and also be good on the ball, quality wise."
