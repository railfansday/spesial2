Eagles vs. Cowboys live updates, inactives, where to watch: Dak Prescott, Dallas look to end Thanksgiving jinx against Philly

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

For the middle course of today's Thanksgiving slate, the NFL is serving up a rivalry, with the Dallas Cowboys hosting the Philadelphia Eagles.

The NFL's fiercest rivalry has a little less pizzazz this season: The Eagles are 3-7, the Cowboys are 5-6 and both have glaring weaknesses (Dallas' defense, Philadelphia's quarterback situation) that have hampered their play heading into the final stretch.

The Eagles' latest solution under center, Joe Milton III, follows up his surprising win against the Steelers with a return to Dallas, after starting the 2026 season on the Cowboys' practice squad. Though the Dallas offense is likely to keep up their strong pace, the issues in the Cowboys' defense might be enough to give Milton a revenge game to remember.

Follow along with Yahoo Sports for live updates, highlights and more as the Eagles and Cowboys face off on Thanksgiving, airing on Fox.

Cowboys fans may have been surprised to see OLB Jaishawn Barham listed as inactive since he was not on the injury report heading into game day.

Barham did not reportedly suffer an injury during pre-game warmups and was a healthy scratch for the first time this season, per the Dallas Morning News' Joseph Hoyt.

The outside linebacker has played in 10 games this year, including six starts. Barham has 37 combined tackles, one quarterback hit, and one forced fumble this season.

Moneyline odds: Cowboys -490, Eagles +380

With less than 90 minutes until kickoff between the Philadelphia Eagles and Dallas Cowboys on Thanksgiving, each team has announced its gameday inactives for Week 12.
