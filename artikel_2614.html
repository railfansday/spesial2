‘He Did What He Had To Do’ — NFL Analysts Dissect How Much MNF Loss to Commanders Will Hurt Joe Burrow’s Push For MVP

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Joe Burrow had a strong night Monday. However, it just wasn't enough. The Cincinnati Bengals quarterback threw for 300 yards and two touchdowns, but the Washington Commanders escaped with a 22-20 win on a late Drew Stevens field goal.

The loss drops AFC North-leading Cincinnati to 7-3 and raises a fair question about Burrow's MVP case. Pro Football Network's NFL analysts had their say.

Most analysts feel the damage is minimal. "I don't think this game will hurt Joe Burrow's MVP candidacy all that much," Jacob Infante said.

Infante also put the blame elsewhere. "He did what he had to do and got the Bengals into field goal range; it was the defense that didn't get the job done."

Kyle Soppe agreed. Burrow completed 70% of his passes for the ninth time in 10 games, and Soppe sees a quarterback who is still in the thick of it. "Burrow could have done more to win, but he's not the reason they lost and that still has him as a frontrunner," Soppe said.

He even thinks the game could help Burrow down the line. If the Bengals run the table, that would include a win over Kansas City, and Soppe believes "a 14-win season is well within the range of possible outcomes."

Brandon Astreicher called it a small bump, not a setback. Burrow wasn't as sharp as he'd been for over a month, but he still put up numbers and got Cincinnati the lead with under a minute left.

"I don't think Joe Burrow's MVP case took that big of a hit tonight," Astreicher said. Burrow remains his pick, though he'd understand if someone slotted Brock Purdy just ahead.

Speaking of Purdy, he looms over this whole conversation. The San Francisco quarterback leads PFN's QB Impact grades at 94.1, with Burrow right behind at 91.3. Purdy has 2,624 yards, 23 touchdowns, and five interceptions. Burrow has 3,095 yards, 29 touchdowns and six picks.

Brandon Austin already had Purdy as his MVP before this week, and nothing changed his mind. He liked what he saw from Burrow, saying, "Even in a loss, it was a solid showing for Joe Burrow." But Purdy's performance against the vaunted Vikings defense sealed it for Austin, who believes Purdy "should be the frontrunner at this stage."

Jason Katz was the most blunt of the group. "Does this impact Joe Burrow's MVP odds? Absolutely," he said. Katz called the voting process fickle, and one subpar game can swing opinions, especially when Purdy just dismantled a Vikings defense that entered Sunday allowing the fewest points in the NFL.

Still, Katz urged some perspective. Great teams lose to lesser ones every year, and Purdy himself dropped a game to the Falcons not long ago. "Football happens," Katz said. He still believes Burrow is the MVP, but the outcome rests on the stretch run. "Whether he wins it will depend on how he closes the season," he said.

That might be the cleanest takeaway. One loss did not sink Burrow, and one win did not crown Purdy. The race is tight, and the final weeks will decide it.
