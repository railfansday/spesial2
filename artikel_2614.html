3 things Rams learned about Chiefs in their loss to Bills

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Los Angeles Rams look to continue their five-game winning streak against the Kansas City Chiefs next week as both teams come off games on Thanksgiving and Thanksgiving Eve. Here are three lessons the Rams learned from the Chiefs' Thanksgiving night loss to the Buffalo Bills.

Because of the rainy, cold conditions in Buffalo, the Bills gave James Cook 32 carries, and he turned them into 117 yards and a rushing touchdown on the night. Their constant use of the run game kept Buffalo ahead of the chains and negated the effectiveness of Kansas City's blitz, as the yardage needed for a first down was often managable, allowing Josh Allen to get the ball out quickly instead of having a play develop downfield.

Allen's own rushing ability kept Kansas City's defense at bay too, an element the Rams do not have with Stafford. That is something to consider, but with Blake Corum taking 32 carries for 184 yards and a touchdown against the Green Bay Packers, perhaps the team will find similar ways to emulate Buffalo's offensive success.

Backup running back Emmett Johnson is the next Chiefs' great rookie sensation in the backfield, as he has stunned in a larger role filling in for the injured Kenneth Walker. Rams fans remember Walker destroying Los Angeles in the NFC Championship Game, and Johnson has that same ability.

Johnson plays with a ruthlessness that is driven by his fifth-round selection. Feeling disrespected, Johnson likes to show the NFL why they were wrong to wait on him, putting up 278 rushing yards, over 400 total yards, and four total touchdowns in the last three games.

He'll get between 17-23 carries at least if he's starting, and if he can get rolling, Kansas City's play-action offense becomes nearly impossible to watch. However, there's a chance Walker will return from injury next week.

A magician at avoiding pressures, escaping collapsing pockets, and making off-schedule plays, Patrick Mahomes truly can do it all. One of his most underrated traits is his navigation of the pocket and how he uses slower movements to avoid overpursuing defenders to slip out of arm tackles, extending plays and hitting targets downfield.

If the Rams' pass rush is to be successful, maintaining contain while in their rush lanes and anticipating when Mahomes starts to move will be the difference between a rushed throw and a deep bomb downfield towards a receiver that was being covered for too long.

Pursuit tempo will be critical in this contest.
