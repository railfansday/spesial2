Davante Adams or Torry Holt? The Hall of Fame must choose

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Los Angeles Rams are hoping to get back Davante Adams when they return from their bye week to play against his former team, the Green Bay Packers, next Wednesday. Adams will have an opportunity to produce at a high level for the rest of the year, as the Rams wait for the return of Puka Nacua from his own set of injuries. The last time Adams played without Nacua, he had 195 yards and two touchdowns.

With a handful of games as Matthew Stafford's number one receiver, imagine how much more Adams could add to his Hall of Fame resume. Nothing would make him feel more at ease than adding a Super Bowl championship — or even just an appearance — to his resume at this stage of his career.

Unfortunately, the Rams know what it's like to watch an elite receiver go year after year without induction to the Pro Football Hall of Fame. Torry Holt has been denied entry to the Hall of Fame 12 times, including seven times as a finalist. Imagine being that close every year and not getting in.

19th all-time in receiving yards (13,382)

If you had to pick one — and I know many of you don't want to pick one — but if you HAD to pick one…who has the better case? After all, that's how the Hall of Fame works. You don't pick all. You have a limited number of selections every year, which is why Holt has never gotten in.
