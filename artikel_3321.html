3 Best Prime Video Movies To Watch This Week (Nov 23-27)

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The number one movie on Prime Video this week is David O. Russell’s Madden. Starring Nicolas Cage as the iconic NFL coach and commentator, the sports biopic explores John Madden’s colorful life and career, with Christian Bale, Kathryn Hahn, John Mulaney, and more in supporting roles. Though critically panned, the movie has received a lot of attention since its arrival on the platform last week, but it’s hardly the only thing worth seeing on the streaming service. Here’s a look at three great movies that we think you should watch on Prime Video this week.

For more recommendations, check out our list of the best shows and movies on Prime Video.

A quintessential coming-of-age film of the 21st century, The Perks of Being a Wallflower is Stephen Chbosky’s film adaptation of his 1999 novel, following Charlie, a teenager who shares his experiences from his freshman year of high school in a series of letters. The film follows his trials and triumphs as an adolescent who also suffers from severe trauma, which he remains largely unaware of. Logan Lerman plays Charlie, with Emma Watson and Ezra Miller as his closest friends.

Heartfelt and sincere, The Perks of Being a Wallflower is considered among the most mature of teen films. Chbosky compellingly translates the charm of his modern YA classic onto the screen, with award-winning performances by its leading trio infusing life into the beloved characters. The film has since become a cult hit and remains a defining element of 2010s pop culture.

Every one of these four started as a cast recording people played until the disc wore out, and every one of them eventually turned into a film. Here is yours, what it says about you, and the case for it being the best of the four.

You work like the clock is against you, because in your head it always is. Hamilton is three hours about a man who cannot stop producing, cannot stop arguing and cannot leave a single thing alone, and you recognised yourself in it immediately.

People made up their minds about you early and they were wrong. Wicked takes the most famous villain in American children's fiction and asks what the story looks like from her side, which is a question you have asked about yourself more than once.

You cannot walk past something unfair. Les Mis is a show about a man who spends his entire life trying to make one wrong right, and about everybody around him deciding what they are prepared to lose for other people.

You are not interested in saving it all for later. Rent is a year in the life of people with no money, no plan and a very clear sense that the time they have is the only thing they genuinely own.

In her second feature directorial, Maggie Gyllenhaal reimagines the 1935 film by James Whale, which was inspired by Mary Shelley’s Frankenstein; or, The Modern Prometheus. Set in 1930s Chicago, The Bride follows Frankenstein’s monster, aka Frank, as he enlists the help of a revolutionary medical genius named Dr. Euphronius (Annette Bening) to give him a companion and discovers that his new bride has a past and future beyond his control. Jessie Buckley stars as the titular counterpart to Christian Bale's Frank, with Peter Sarsgaard, Jake Gyllenhaal, and Penélope Cruz as other important characters.

The Bride is not just a modernist look at the 19th-century novel but a feminist revision of a Hollywood classic that thrives primarily on Jessie Buckley’s electrifying, if chaotic, performance. The film received massively polarized reviews but earned universal praise for the lead performances, set design, and costumes. Criticisms aside, the film does add a new perspective to a classic legend: a dark Bonnie-and-Clyde-style mid-century Gothic drama.

Directed and choreographed by Adam Shankman and written by Leslie Dixon, 2007’s Hairspray is a film adaptation of the 2002 musical by Mark O'Donnell and Thomas Meehan, which in turn is a stage adaptation of the 1998 film of the same name. Set in 1962 Baltimore, Maryland, the romantic comedy musical follows the pursuits of a “pleasantly plump” teenager named Tracy (Nikki Blonsky) who strives to become a dancing star on a local TV show and rallies against racial segregation. The film also boasts a stellar ensemble featuring John Travolta, Michelle Pfeiffer, and Christopher Walken, among others.

Adam Shankman turned a cult classic into a mainstream genre hit of its time, making Hairspray a worldwide critical and commercial success. Energetic and fun, with foot-tapping musical numbers, the film is a well-rounded entertainer that successfully retains the spirit of the original while also being refreshingly new. John Travolta’s drag performance in the movie may have drawn mixed reactions, but Hairspray easily ranks among the best musicals of the 21st century.
