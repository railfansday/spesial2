‘VisionQuest’ Teases Surprise ‘Avengers: Doomsday’ Character Return In Post-Credits Scene

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

SPOILERS: This post contains details about the VisionQuest Season 1 finale episode “Forever”

A major player appears to be returning to the MCU following the Season 1 finale of VisionQuest.

In a post-credits scene from Tuesday’s finale episode “Forever”, a hooded figure that appears to be Robert Downey Jr.’s Doctor Doom raises the rubble of Wundagore off Wanda Maximoff/Scarlet Witch (Elizabeth Olsen) after she seemingly killed herself under the collapsing mountain in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness (2022).

“Scarlet Witch, awaken. Your power will be mine,” says the hooded figure. Watch on Deadline

The post-credits scene seems to hint at Olsen’s return for Avengers: Doomsday, premiering Dec. 18 in theaters, after Vision (Paul Bettany) was reunited with both their sons, Tommy (Ruaridh Mollica) and Billy (Joe Locke), in the finale, which follows the events of WandaVision (2021) and Agatha All Along (2024).

Marvel Studios hinted at the family potentially being fully reunited on social media ahead of Thursday’s finale, sharing a message in binary code that appears to translate to: “We could never truly leave each other, even if we tried.” Wanda previously said that to Tommy and Billy in the finale of WandaVision.

Meanwhile, Olsen has said “I know nothing” when asked about her character’s potential return to the MCU. “I don’t know what’s going on. It’s safer for me to not know anything because then I can’t make a mistake in a situation like this. I rather know nothing,” she said in March.
