We’re looking for a Director of Sales Talent Acquisition to lead our global team of sales recruiters and make sales hiring a genuine competitive advantage for Grafana Labs. You will set the direction for how we attract, assess and hire the sellers who drive our growth, and you will do it as a core member of the sales organization, not a service function that sits beside it.

This is a leader who is energized by data, spots risks to hiring goals before they land, and turns insight into innovative, AI-powered tools that help the business make faster, better decisions. If you have built and rolled out ICP-based hiring and want to do it at global scale in a fast-growing, remote-first company, this is your role.
