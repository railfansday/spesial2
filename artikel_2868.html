Ravens, Cardinals announce Jacoby Brissett trade

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Ravens and Cardinals were said to be finalizing a deal that would send the veteran quarterback to Baltimore earlier this week. The trade was officially announced on Friday morning.

Neither team specified the draft compensation that will go to Arizona in exchange for Brissett, but Adam Schefter of ESPN reports that the Ravens will send a 2027 fourth-round pick and a 2028 seventh-round pick to the Cardinals for Brissett and a 2027 5th-round pick.

Brissett started every game for the Cardinals this season before being demoted to the third team this week. Gardner Minshew is now the Cardinals starter with rookie Carson Beck in the No. 2 spot on the depth chart.

With Lamar Jackson and Tyler Huntley on injured reserve, the Ravens turned to undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano for Thursday night’s loss to the Jaguars. Brissett will have over a week to familiarize himself with the team’s offense before a Monday night game against the Chargers in Week 10.
