Omar Cooper Jr. has busy day in Week 9 vs. Kansas City

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

New York Jets WR Omar Cooper Jr. caught eight of 11 targets for 58 yards Week 9 against the Kansas City Chiefs.

Why It Matters: It was another big effort for Cooper, who was not part of the offense against Las Vegas last week (zero targets). It's hard to get a read on Cooper, who posted 100-yard efforts in each of the previous two weeks before last week's shocking bagel. He obviously benefited from Garrett Wilson's absence in this one, but three stellar efforts in his first four full NFL games are enough to trust him as a borderline WR3/4 option. Consider him a strong flex option at the very least in Week 10 against the Bills.
