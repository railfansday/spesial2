Gary Woodland Announces Knee Surgery, Withdraws From Hero World Challenge

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Gary Woodland will miss the 2026 Hero World Challenge after undergoing arthroscopic knee surgery in New York. The American golfer announced the news in an Instagram post on Friday, November 20, explaining that he had been experiencing discomfort in his knee before consulting with doctors.

Woodland underwent the procedure at the Hospital for Special Surgery with Dr. Riley Williams. However, he also provided a positive update on his recovery, revealing that everything went well and he expects to return to hitting golf balls in a few weeks.

The surgery means Woodland will miss the tournament hosted by Tiger Woods, but he is already looking ahead to the 2027 PGA Tour season. He also thanked Woods and the team at TGR for the invitation and expressed his hope of returning to the event in the future.

The procedure took place on Thursday night, and the five-time PGA Tour winner was pleased with how everything went.

The American does not expect to spend too long away from the golf course, as he believes he will be back hitting balls within a few weeks.

"Everything went great and I should be back hitting balls in a few weeks and ready to start the 27 PGA Tour season better than ever," Woodland wrote.

His latest update comes after a successful 2026 campaign, which included a victory at the Texas Children's Houston Open in March. Woodland will now focus on his recovery before returning to competition.

Woodland's surgery has forced him to withdraw from the Hero World Challenge, which will take place at Albany in the Bahamas from December 3-6. He had recently received an invitation to compete in the 20-player event hosted by Woods.

The golfer expressed his disappointment at missing the tournament but made it clear that he appreciated the opportunity to be part of the field.

"Unfortunately, that does mean I have to withdraw from the Hero World Challenge but I am so appreciative of Tiger and the team at TGR for offering me the invite and I look forward to making a return there in the future," he added.

Woodland will now have a few weeks to recover before turning his attention to the new PGA Tour season. His expected return to hitting balls should give him time to prepare for 2027.
