Milwaukee Bucks vs. Miami Heat Preview & Game Thread: Visiting an old friend

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Well, here we are. The Milwaukee Bucks are set to face off against the Miami Heat in a game that's more than a Grand Theft Auto advertisement for me and many Bucks fans. This will be the first time the Bucks match up with franchise GOAT Giannis Antetokounmpo since he was traded to Miami, and while fans have reckoned with this new era in many ways, it will still feel strange watching the Greek Freak throw one down over—not for—the Bucks. If you want a quick stroll down memory lane, check out this list of his biggest moments in Milwaukee that just went up on NBA.com (thanks again for everything, Giannis). Otherwise, there is no way to speed run this level in Vice City, no cheat codes to fall back on, and no real way to lay low and let the Heat meter (okay, that one is from Need for Speed) cool off tonight as the Bucks again try to capture their second win of the year.

A couple of weeks ago, when both teams sat on one win over the first six games, it seemed possible this matchup could be a real competition. Then, as expected, Spoelstra figured it out. Instead of sending Giannis barreling down the court and creating chaos, the Heat have leaned into a more pick-and-roll-heavy style. This is largely due to Davion Mitchell running an incredibly effective half-court offence: the Heat currently rank fourth on Cleaning the Glass in half-court offensive efficiency. Spoelstra has also been staggering Giannis and Bam more, which generally means that one of them is always attacking the rim or any number of shooters—including Bucks legend Bobby Portis—waiting for the pass. Even when you know what the Heat will do, actually stopping them is an incredibly tall order for any team, let alone the Bucks' starting frontcourt.

So, is there any hope, or should you sit around and stare at your PlayStation until midnight? If Miami has any cracks in its armor, they're in its defense. Over the last two weeks, the Heat's defense has allowed 113.1 points per 100 possessions, which is good for ninth in the NBA over that span; however, that includes games against the Jazz and Kings, and over the last three games, opponents have averaged 116 PPG against Miami. Further, the Heat currently rank 23rd overall in putbacks (per Cleaning the Glass), and 25th in overall second-chance points. The Bucks are coming off a game where they dominated the Hawks in second-chance points, 24-8, and beat them on the offensive glass, 17-14. If the Bucks can find ways to secure more offensive rebounds than the Heat, then that gives them a ray of hope. Of course, if there are more instances of Turner looking lost on defense or Kuzma making Kuzma-like decisions, that ray of hope may dim quickly. Jenkins will have his hands full with rotations, but he has obviously shown he isn't afraid to try many different lineups, no matter what point in the game.

Bogoljub Markovic: Out (G League assignment)

Unequivocally, all eyes will be on Kasparas Jakucionis. While attention is on Giannis and his return, there is no doubt Jakucionis will build on the energy and intensity he brought against the Hawks to show his old club what they passed up. I wrote about Jakucionis being the crown jewel for the Bucks in the Giannis trade, and on Sunday he had a career-best game, going for 25 points, five assists, five rebounds, and three steals; the Bucks' offence ran a lot smoother than it has most of this season with KJ at the helm, instead of simply rising and falling on an individual hot streak. If Jakucionis can show out against his old club, it will also be a major reassurance that the Bucks may have found their point guard of the future.

*This is based on the current depth chart, but don't be shocked if Ware or Sims find their way to starting tomorrow night.

Who: Milwaukee Bucks (1-13) vs. Miami Heat (8-6).

Kasparas Jakucionis won't be the only former Heat player looking to send a message: Kel'el Ware likely had this game circled on his calendar, so I will look for the Kryptonian to post a 20-point double-double!
