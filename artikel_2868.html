Mario Cristobal reveals how Miami rallied to beat Virginia Tech

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Miami (FL) football needed a late surge to prevent the Virginia Tech upset on Saturday. Coach Mario Cristobal managed to keep the Hurricanes composed as their pursuit of the College Football Playoff nearly got rattled.

So how did the veteran coach keep his guys focused during moments the Hokies tested Miami?

"Seemed like our defense really buckled down. I believe the next eight possessions, they had just two field goals. Just a great job by them. And you know what, the best thing we did was when we got our opportunities offensively," Cristobal said. "They really started to change."

Yet Cristobal became blunt about Miami's performance.

"We didn't play well in a lot of aspects. But we played tough and gritty…and sometimes that's what you need in conference play," Cristobal said.

He even mentioned how some Hurricane players played through injury during this crucial Atlantic Coast Conference showdown that brought out ESPN College GameDay. Which also included a special guest appearance from famed Hurricane alumnus, WWE legend and Hollywood actor Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson.

But the Hokies applied the scare inside Hard Rock Stadium right away, jumping to a 14-0 lead through nearly the first 10 minutes of the game. Running back Mark Fletcher managed to cut the lead to 14-11 off his one-yard touchdown run.

The upset-minded Hokies later took a 20-14 lead into the fourth quarter before Cristobal turned to the grit he explained in the postgame presser. Fletcher scored two more times to pull off the hat trick, propelling Miami to a 10-1 record.

Now Miami is a lock to play for the ACC title, regardless of what happens in the Hurricanes' Nov. 28 game against Boston College. And Wake Forest will await Miami on Dec. 5.
