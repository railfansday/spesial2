Russell leads second Las Vegas GP practice in wet conditions

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Mercedes' George Russell topped the times at Thursday's second practice for the Las Vegas Grand Prix, pipping Alpine's Pierre Gasly in damp and slippery conditions.

British racer Russell -- who sits third in the championship on 262 points, trailing Red Bull's Max Verstappen by 17 with three races to go -- clocked one minute and 47.272 seconds, just 0.071 seconds ahead of Frenchman Gasly.

Charles Leclerc of Ferrari, who is also in the hunt for this year's runner-up spot on 249 points, was third-fastest, followed by Red Bull's Isack Hadjar and McLaren's Oscar Piastri.

Kimi Antonelli, already confirmed as the youngest world champion in F1 history, was eighth-fastest, behind Lewis Hamilton and Lando Norris.

The first practice session earlier on Thursday had been severely limited by torrential rain, with no driver completing a timed lap.

Five of the 22 racers did not even leave the pits for that session as rain lashed the Las Vegas Strip street circuit, creating extremely slippery conditions.

But the thousands of fans who braved desert night temperatures of around 52 F (11 C) to remain in the grandstands were treated to late-night action, as conditions improved and lap times quickened through the session.

The race takes place Saturday. The Las Vegas Grand Prix is the 21st of 23 rounds scheduled this season.
