Ramp is seeking to raise $1 billion or more at a valuation above $70 billion

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The corporate-card startup is in early talks, with Thrive Capital in discussions to lead what would be its third funding round in a year

Ramp is holding preliminary discussions about a funding round of at least $1 billion that would value the company at over $70 billion, according to The Wall Street Journal. The round would be the third for the company in a year.

Josh Kushner's Thrive Capital, which already backs Ramp, is negotiating to head the round, according to The Wall Street Journal. No deal has been announced.

Ramp intends to set aside some of the new money for possible acquisitions, according to The Wall Street Journal. The company has not yet picked a date for going public.

Ramp disclosed that it had secured $1.85 billion in a financing that put its valuation at $60 billion, according to The Wall Street Journal. Earlier investors include Thrive, D1 Capital Partners, Coatue Management, Founders Fund and Redpoint Ventures.

Ramp announced in October that its annualized revenue had passed the $2 billion mark, twice what it was the previous fall. The company attributed part of the growth to a surge in customer spending on artificial intelligence.

Founded in 2019, Ramp is a credit-card issuer that additionally sells AI-driven software for managing business finances. Its corporate-card business generates most of its revenue, according to The Wall Street Journal. Other business lines, such as bill payments and banking, are expected to overtake the card business in revenue within a year.

Among Ramp's newer products is Router, which helps businesses manage their AI spending, according to The Wall Street Journal. Those products are growing quickly.
