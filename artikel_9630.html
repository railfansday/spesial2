Ukrainian scans, invoices, Word and Excel to Markdown - 5 Apify Actors on 7 files, only one got every table right

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Posted on Nov 15 • Originally published at andriyvk.github.io

Search the Apify Store for "PDF to Markdown" and you get dozens of Actors with almost the same promise: text, tables, OCR, ready for RAG. None of them tells you what happens with a Ukrainian scan, an Excel file or a scanned invoice whose table has no lines. So I ran five of them on the same seven files and compared every output with the text the files were made from. All results below are from real runs on 15 November 2026.

Disclosure: one of the five is mine, Document to Markdown for AI. Every number below comes from a real run, the test files are public and the raw outputs are kept, so you can repeat and check all of it.

"Right" means the text, the heading and the table with the correct rows and with every number exactly as in the document.

The last two are the hard case and the most common one: most invoices, waybills and bank statements are printed without table lines. The files are public: https://api.apify.com/v2/key-value-stores/FloomuI2DrQCJazY8/records/.

The Actors are the most used ones for this search plus one that lists Office formats. Settings: Ukrainian OCR wherever the Actor has a language option (ukr); nidamindllc has no language option.
