Well, it's official: there are two new kids on the block. The new MacBook Pro M6 and the MacBook Pro with an OLED touchscreen are finally here. If you've been waiting to upgrade, or are looking to pick up your first-ever MacBook, now's the time to do it. Pre-orders are live, and we've even found a trick to help you save up to $1,400 on the OLED model.

As someone who owns a MacBook and uses one for work, and coincidentally tests the latest and greatest peripherals, I know that your setup isn't complete until you buy the right accessories. I couldn't get on with my work without my trusty mouse, keyboard, monitor, and other gadgets.

Whether you're after a new mechanical keyboard or even a touch-sensitive one whose keycaps double as a trackpad, a pair of earbuds, or need a USB-C dock for more connectivity, I've got you covered. I've rounded up what I believe are the best accessories (and gadgets) to make the most of your new MacBook Pro M6 or OLED.

The MacBook Pro OLED is one of the biggest MacBook releases in recent years. It can be configured with an M5 Pro (from $2,899) or M5 Max (from $4,499) CPU and packs a 14.2-inch PROLED XDR touchscreen display with 3024 x 1986 resolution, 24GB of RAM, and 1TB SSD. You also get three Thunderbolt 5 (USB-C) ports, HDMI port, and an SDXC slot. The laptop will be available on November 6.

Power users looking for a boost in performance should opt for the new 14-inch M6 MacBook Pro. This powerful laptop sports Apple's M6 chip for blazing-fast performance at home or on the go.

There's no better monitor for your new MacBook Pro than the first-party Apple Studio Display, thanks to its excellent 12MP ultra-wide camera, fantastic six-speaker setup and gorgeous 27-inch 5K screen. It's bright and delivers accurate (and stunning) colors, too.

Read our full Apple Studio Display review.

If you don't mind a third-party display, I can't recommend the BenQ MA270S enough. It's cheaper than the Apple Studio Display and boasts wide color coverage and excellent accuracy, making it a great choice for creatives, and it comes with an Apple-like app for customization. There’s no shortage of ports, and the Nano Gloss 5K panel is a sight to behold.

If you want more screen real estate, the Alogic Edge 40-inch 5K is an excellent choice. Boasting great color coverage and accuracy, and hosting a plethora of ports for Mac users, this monitor offers everything you could ask for. Available in Silver and Space Gray, it’ll fit right into your existing Mac-first setup, too.

The Clevetura S is an outstanding travel-friendly keyboard with touch-sensitive keycaps that turn it into a highly effective and responsive trackpad with MacBook gestures, negating the need for a separate mouse. Its scissor switches are great to type on, too.

The K10 Max is a fantastic iteration of the tried-and-true Keychron formula, offering additional sound dampening, a refined mounting system, and high-performance Super Switches. A deep, thick sound and responsive keystrokes make this a great gaming option, too. It comes with macOS keycaps, making it a solid choice for Apple users.

Want a keyboard that looks a bit different? The typewriter-like Epomaker Glyph (featuring macOS keycaps) is a wonderful, whimsical addition to any setup. It puts the joy back into typing. The keyboard sports a charming typewriter-like design with dual screens and customizable RGB lighting, and build quality is solid.

There's no better mouse for the MacBook other than the Apple Magic Mouse (as long as you don't mind the very flat design). It features a Multi-Touch surface that allows you to perform gestures, and the mouse can last up to a month between charges. This model features a USB-C port. On the bottom.

If you want an ergonomic mouse with buttons that toggle macOS shortcuts, try the BenQ INFTYLAB M Mouse PS01. It features dual scrolling wheels, smooth tracking, and you can assign macOS gestures to its buttons. User-friendly companion software makes customization easy, too.

Read my full BenQ INFTYLAB M Mouse PS01 review.

Given how powerful both the MacBook Pro M6 and OLED sound, I won't be surprised if they're great for gaming. The Corsair M75 (Mac) is a superb gaming mouse designed for macOS. This minimalist-looking mouse features 26,000 DPI sensitivity that you can change on the fly, making it suitable for all kinds of gaming titles. It’s very comfortable to use for long hours.

The UGREEN 8-in-1 Thunderbolt 4 Docking Station is a must-have for those who have a desk setup. It's equipped with three Thunderbolt 4 ports (40Gbps, 15W), three USB-A ports (10Gbps), a gigabit ethernet port, and a 140W DC power port. It's great for creatives and professionals who need high transfer speeds.

I couldn't live without my portable USB-C hub. The Anker 8-in-1 USB-C Hub is ideal for those on the move. It features multiple USB-C and USB-A ports, as well as HDMI 4K/60Hz ports for streaming and mirroring content. There are also two SD and microSD card slots — perfect for photographers and videographers.

The Twelve South Curve Stand sports a modern and minimal design that complements and enhances your MacBook and workspace. It raises your screen off the desk for improved ergonomics and airflow.

The Twelve South Curve Flex Stand elevates your MacBook up to 22 inches and adjusts your keyboard angle from zero to 45°, resulting in a more ergonomic and comfortable viewing and typing experience. It folds flat so you can take it with you when you travel.

If you have an existing Apple ecosystem (iPhone, iPad, MacBook, etc.), the Apple AirPods 5 are an excellent choice. Starting from just $124, these affordable earbuds are the ideal model those on a budget. While the battery life is abysmal, the sound quality, comfort, fit, smart features, and ANC make up for it for casual users.

If you need AirPods that don't skimp on features, the AirPods Pro 3 are the ones for you. They pack useful features like heart rate monitor and Live Translation. The fit is fantastic and comfortable, and there's plenty of battery to keep them going for long listening sessions.

Read our full Apple AirPods Pro 3 review.

Stuffed full of AirPods Pro-esque features like Head Gestures, Live Translation, Camera Remote, and improved Siri integration, the AirPods Max 2 pack lots of smart tech features alongside sound that's rich, complex, and most importantly, fun.

Read our full Apple AirPods Max 2 review.

The TomToc 360 Protective Sleeve is a must-have for those who want to protect their new MacBook Pro M6 or OLED when they're traveling. With four corner protectors, resilient edges, high-density padding, and water-resistant fabric, it protects your laptop from inevitable bumps, scratches and water spills. It also has two separate compartments with several small pockets and a strap for an AirTag or key.

If you want a more low-key sleeve, the Rains Laptop Portfolio waterproof sleeve is sleek and good-looking. It features a flap closure fastened with concealed magnetic straps, and it's made of durable and smooth PU fabric.
