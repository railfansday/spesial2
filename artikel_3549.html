Man Killed Wife and 2 Kids in Murder-Suicide That’s Left Family ‘Heartbroken’

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Authorities in New Mexico said a man fatally shot his wife and their two young children before taking his own life in an apparent murder-suicide.

Police responded to a home in Las Cruces, New Mexico, on Thursday, November 19, around 10 a.m. after a relative reported that she couldn’t get in touch with anyone who lived at the home.

Once officers got to the residence, they discovered the bodies of four family members who had all suffered gunshot wounds.

Following a preliminary investigation, authorities determined that Marcus Reyes Sr. fatally shot his wife, Alayna Reyes, their son, Marcus Jr., and their daughter, Anastacia.

After he killed his family members, Marcus Sr., 28, seemingly died by suicide.

It’s not currently clear whether authorities believe Marcus Sr. had a motive in the killings or if the family had a history of domestic violence issues.

In light of the tragedy, Alayna’s aunt, Kim Vallejos, launched a GoFundMe to financially support Alayna’s mother and the kids’ grandmother.

“I’m raising funds to help my sister with family support and relief fund, immediate family needs, and other expenses arising from the tragedy after the deaths of her daughter Alayna, grandchildren Marcus and Anastacia,” Vallejos wrote on the fundraiser’s page. “This unimaginable loss has left us heartbroken. They were so loved and will be missed.”

Vallejos concluded, “If you are not in a position to donate, sharing this fundraiser and keeping our family in your thoughts and prayers would mean more than you know.”

The fundraiser has earned $25,496 out of its $28K goal as of time of publication.

In addition to the fundraising information, Vallejos shared several photos of the victims. One photo captured Alayna, 28, and Marcus Jr., 8, spending quality time together in a park, while another featured the mother and Anastacia, 6, during a day at the beach. Another photo showed the siblings donning large grins as they enjoyed ice cream.

The murder-suicide has left the family’s neighbors in shock, with some of those who lived nearby speaking to ABC 7 about the killings.

Neighbor Erika Sorensen said that the suburban community where the family lived is “a very calm, quiet area.” She added, “It’s very unnerving that it’s just right there.”

“Nothing like this has ever happened. It’s heartbreaking. It’s so sad,” neighbor Katie Albright told the outlet.

Albright added that “everybody’s talking about how we’re processing it with our kids, and how we’re processing it with each other.”

“And, you know, looking for ways that we can hopefully support the family,” she said.
