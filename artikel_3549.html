Texas Mom of 4 Wanted a Divorce Before Relatives Allegedly Killed Her: Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A Texas mother of four was fatally shot along a freeway on her 35th birthday after she reportedly shared her desire to leave her husband.

An arrest warrant affidavit obtained by Fox 4 News reveals Stephanie Gonzalez’s brother-in-law, Juan Gonzalez-Orduna, and his son were allegedly behind the deadly November 15 attack against Gonzalez after she asked her husband for a divorce.

Gonzalez-Orduna, 29, was arrested on a murder charge on Sunday, November 22, in connection to Gonzalez’s death, the Dallas Police Department announced in a Sunday statement.

His son, Gonzalez’s nephew, is still wanted by authorities, according to Fox 4 News.

The Dallas Police Department didn’t provide further details on the case when reached by Us via email on Monday, November 23.

According to police, Gonzalez died at a hospital after she was shot following a car crash on the C.F. Hawn Freeway in Dallas the evening of November 15.

Her family said they believed Gonzalez had been targeted as she drove home from work to see her four children, including a baby who is just a few months old, per NBC DFW. She worked at Quality Sausage Company in West Dallas as a company supervisor.

Gonzalez’s friends told detectives she was allegedly involved in an affair with a coworker and wanted to leave her husband, according to a Gonzalez-Orduna’s arrest warrant affidavit obtained by Fox 4 News. They allegedly argued with each other when she mentioned wanting a divorce, just hours before she was killed on November 15.

That evening, Gonzalez was on the phone with 911, reporting she was involved in a minor car crash, when a dispatcher heard her screams along with the sound of gunfire, per the affidavit.

Based on a review of the 911 call and traffic cameras, as well as a witness interview, detectives suspected Gonzalez-Orduna and his son Jose Gonzalez of allegedly killing Stephanie, according to Fox 4 News.

They are accused of purposefully crashing into Stephanie’s car in order to force her to pull over on the freeway, according to police, the TV station reported. Then they allegedly ambushed Stephanie.

According to the affidavit, Juan is suspected of driving the vehicle, which belonged to a relative and had its license plates removed. Jose is suspected of opening fire on Stephanie, killing her.

Gonzalez-Orduna is in custody at the Lew Sterrett Jail, where he’s also detained on an immigration hold over his alleged illegal immigration status, records show.

Stephanie’s husband is not suspected to be involved in the killing, per Fox 4 News.

She had been married to her husband, Luis Enriquez Gonzalez, for more than two years ahead of her death, according to her obituary.

“Together they shared a life built on care, companionship, and mutual support,” the obituary reads.

The obituary also describes Stephanie as a devoted mother who deeply loved her children.

A GoFundMe created in support of Stephanie’s family has been created by Julissa Clemente, who identified herself as Stephanie’s cousin.

“Stephanie was loving, caring, hardworking, and above all a great mother,” Clemente wrote on the GoFundMe page. “She leaves behind her husband and four beautiful children, who are now facing an unimaginable loss without her.”
