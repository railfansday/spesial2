Tripo AI Gives A First Look at Its Upcoming Print-Ready Generative Model with Intricate, True-to-Life 3D-Printed Creations at Formnext

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The company also unveils an AI agent tailor-made for additive manufacturing, bridging the gap between AI generated content and 3D printers.

FRANKFURT AM MAIN, Germany, Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- At Formnext, Tripo AI offers a glimpse of its upcoming generative model, capable of faithfully reproducing intricate details and visual nuances with exceptional precision. The company also unveils an AI agent tailor-made for additive manufacturing, bridging the gap between generated content and 3D printers.

During a press conference, Tripo AI's branding team displays intricate, true-to-life 3D-printed pieces generated by Tripo's upcoming high detail model of the H series. The powerful H3.5 3D-native foundation model, to be released soon, is a major upgrade from H3.1, with a significant leap in capturing the finest details and retaining visual exactness.The model generates print-ready 3D content with minimal needs for editing.

On the stage, Tripo AI Research Lead Yuan-Chen Guo unveils Polipop, an AI agent that helps people turn their ideas into bespoke 3D-printed items. Feed Polipop with ideas and it handles everything from 3D modelling to print preparation to sending the finished creation to a 3D printer.

"We are excited to be at Formnext to share our latest advances in generative 3D technology to additive manufacturing," says Guo. "Our upcoming H3.5 3D-native model represents a new milestone in generative 3D technology, bringing print-ready generated 3D content directly to a 3D printer, while Polipop, the AI agent specially developed for 3D printing, helps mold people's ideas into 3D-printed objects, making 3D printing more accessible and easier to navigate."

Tripo's branding team also sheds light on how Tripo has been pushing the frontier of 3D-native foundation models for 3D printing and additive manufacturing. Using Tripo's generative 3D model of the H series, the team demonstrates how one can turn an idea into a 3D-printed object within minutes.
