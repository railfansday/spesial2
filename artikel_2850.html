Rams S Kamren Kinchens ejected for hit on Christian Watson

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Rams safety Kamren Kinchens was ejected from Wednesday night's game against the Packers after a hit on wide receiver Christian Watson.

Per the Netflix broadcast, the league office said that Kinchens was ejected for leaving his feet and making helmet-to-helmet contact with Watson and NFL executive vice president Troy Vincent made the final call on disqualifying him from the game.

Watson held onto the ball, but went to the locker room with members of the medical staff and the catch did not count because Packers wideout Matthew Golden was flagged for offensive pass interference to offset the Kinchens foul.

The play came on a fourth-and-1 and the Packers ultimately opted to kick a field goal rather than go for it again. It is now 13-13 with under two minutes to play in the half.
