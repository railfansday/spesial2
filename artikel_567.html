Sanaa Lathan and Terence Blanchard Join Sundance Winner ‘Jazz Infernal’ as Executive Producers (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Sanaa Lathan, who starred in the 2000 film, and Terence Blanchard, who composed the score, have teamed up on Will Niava’s short film “Jazz Infernal” as executive producers.

The short film explores migration, African identity and the bond between father and son as music becomes their language for grief and self-expression, with jazz shaping the film’s rhythm, tension and exploration of belonging in a new country. Directed by Niava and co-written with Kristelle LaRoche, “Jazz Infernal” premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival before winning the Sundance Short Film Jury Award for International Fiction and earning a place in TIFF’s Canada’s Top Ten.

Lathan made her feature directorial debut with “On the Come Up,” which premiered at TIFF in 2022.

In a statement, she said, “I’m excited for the world to experience this edge-of-your-seat emotional ride, masterfully directed and brought to life by captivating performances.”

Blanchard, an eight-time Grammy winner, is an accomplished composer who has earned Academy Award nominations for his scores for Spike Lee’s BlacKkKlansman” and “Da 5 Bloods.” He has also worked on “The Woman King,” “One Night in Miami” and “Eve’s Bayou.”

He said, “‘Jazz Infernal’ is a moving and inspiring illustration of how powerful music can connect us and maintain the soul and spirit of our loved ones.”

Elegance Bratton and Chester Algernal Gordon have also joined as executive producers. Together, the filmmakers co-founded Freedom Principle, the production company behind work exploring identity, humanity and belonging.

Gordon added, “‘Jazz Infernal’ is a bold reminder that cinema, like jazz, is at its most powerful when it refuses to play by the rules. It’s daring, hypnotic and unapologetically alive.”

The film is produced by Zion Lipstein-Saffer and Samuel Caron.
