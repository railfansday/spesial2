Rams DB Kamren Kinchens ejected from win vs. Packers for hit on WR Christian Watson, who was ruled out with a concussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Los Angeles Rams safety Kamren Kinchens was ejected from Wednesday night's 26-13 win against the Green Bay Packers.

Green Bay quarterback Jordan Love was trying to float a pass to the sideline, targeting Christian Watson.

The Packers wideout looked over his shoulder to track the ball, and Kinchens came out of nowhere and laid a massive, unexpected hit on Watson.

He was ejected for a helmet-to-helmet hit on Watson, and the wide receiver remained down on the field, needing to be tended to by trainers. Watson was ruled out with a concussion.

Kinchens finished the first Thanksgiving eve game with just one tackle and two pass deflections. He will look to return next week when Los Angeles takes on the Kansas City Chiefs for "Thursday Night Football" next week.

This was the second injury Watson has dealt with in this game. On his first reception for 6 yards on the Packers' opening drive, he left the field and was questionable to return with a thigh injury. He returned on Green Bay's next possession.

Watson scored the Packers' first touchdown of the game in the second quarter. Love found him for the 6-yard score to help bring Green Bay within three points.

Watson has been no stranger to injuries this season. He left the team's Week 8 game against the Carolina Panthers with a broken rib. Watson was taken to a local hospital during the game, but returned to the stadium in an ambulance, still wearing his gear, and ran back onto the field to return to the game.

The Packers wide receiver finished that game with 113 yards on six receptions.
