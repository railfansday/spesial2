Stavros Halkias hilariously drops F-Bomb during ESPN 2 ManningCast after Ravens WR Zay Flowers’ fumble

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Die-hard Baltimore Ravens fan and comedian Stavros Halkias walked into ESPN2's ManningCast expecting a routine Monday night win over a winless opponent. Instead, Peyton Manning and Eli Manning watched him suffer a live on-air meltdown at the expense of the Los Angeles Chargers.

Trailing 23-20 with just over two minutes left in the fourth quarter at M&T Bank Stadium, Ravens quarterback Jacoby Brissett connected with wide receiver Zay Flowers for a 15-yard gain near midfield. Rather than going down, Flowers fought for extra yardage, and Chargers cornerback Tarheeb Still punched the ball loose. Safety Elijah Molden then recovered with 2:01 remaining.

Halkias immediately leaped out of his chair and buried his head in his hands. Moments later, Peyton and Eli Manning rolled the replay of his live agony, prompting Halkias to laugh at himself before dropping an unfiltered F-bomb on the broadcast.

"Brutal. I can't believe I agreed to do this bullsht. This fcking sucks," Halkias said.

Eli Manning tried to soften the blow by pointing out that the turnover happened before the two-minute warning, leaving Baltimore a slim chance. The Ravens did get the ball back with 11 seconds left, but they ran out of time, fell to 4-6 on a five-game losing streak and handed the previously 0-8 Chargers their first victory of the season.

The turnover spoiled an otherwise productive night for Flowers, who caught six passes on seven targets for 74 yards. Tight end Mark Andrews led Baltimore with eight receptions for 82 yards. Making his first start for the Ravens with Lamar Jackson sidelined, Brissett completed 33 of 49 passes for 340 yards and one interception, while running back Derrick Henry scored two rushing touchdowns despite gaining just 10 yards on 10 carries.

Los Angeles leaned on running back Omarion Hampton, who churned out 105 yards and a touchdown on 22 carries, while quarterback Justin Herbert added 129 passing yards plus 51 rushing yards and a score.
