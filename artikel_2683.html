Bears’ D’Andre Swift stands alone through 8 weeks with bonkers MNF feat vs. Seahawks

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Chicago Bears delivered a long 17-play march to set the tone against the Seattle Seahawks. Running back D'Andre Swift then wore down the Super Bowl champs from there. Including hitting a bonkers Monday Night Football feat in the 34-17 rout at Lumen Field.

Swift crossed the end zone not once, but three times. And now he owns this title per Stat Muse: The first player to deliver multiple three-touchdown games.

The veteran running back delivered his first 2026 hat trick on the Carolina Panthers. Swift tallied 124 yards with his three touchdowns. But he scored all of his MNF touchdowns during the second half.

Swift busted through the left side of the Seahawks' defense for his first TD with under 5:45 left.

The 5-foot-8 back then cut to his right and created this short foot race to the end zone for touchdown No. 2.

Then with under 4:45 to go, quarterback Caleb Williams busted this power toss to Swift and his determination/power did the rest.

Swift has now scored at least one touchdown in his last four games. He previously snapped a three-game touchdown-less streak before Oct. 11 against the Green Bay Packers.

Swift officially has hit 116 carries, 476 yards and scored seven touchdowns this season. Quarterback Josh Allen of the Buffalo Bills leads the league with nine touchdowns through the ground game.

But Swift is now two trips past the end zone away from tying his previous best mark of nine touchdowns of 2025. That campaign also saw Swift put together a career-best 1,087 yards in playing for coach Ben Johnson.

The Bears are now 6-2 overall after their 17-point trouncing.
