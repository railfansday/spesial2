UCF Knights Go Bowling After Securing Sixth Win

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The UCF Knights finally secured bowl eligibility after winning their sixth game of the season. This achievement makes UCF bowl-eligible for the first time since 2023. Coach Scott Frost and his team earned this right with a dominant 48-17 victory over Iowa State on Friday night. The win ended a long drought for a program that has faced many tough challenges in recent years.

While the city of Orlando might not throw a massive parade, the progress inside the program is clear. Frost knows that fans and critics judge teams by their final record rather than only by how hard they try. The team needs to finish the season strong to show their true growth. They have one regular-season game left against Colorado before heading to the bowl matchup.

The path to six wins was not easy, but the team found a key advantage late in the season. Frost pointed to the team's improved health as a major factor in the performance. He said UCF was healthier than it had been since Week 2, allowing the Knights to play closer to the level he expected. "Well, we were healthy again," Frost said.

Quarterback Alonza Barnett III delivered the performance the team needed to seal the bowl bid. After missing five games with a hamstring injury, Barnett looked like the dynamic playmaker who led James Madison to the College Football Playoff last season. Barnett threw four touchdown passes and completed 17 of 27 throws for 255 yards. He connected with tight end Josh Derry for three of those scores, including a massive 65-yard strike. Frost joked about one specific play during the postgame news conference. "It's a special day when a fat guy gets a touchdown," Frost cracked, referring to offensive lineman Jacob Maiava, who caught his first career touchdown pass.

The coaching staff sees this season as a major step forward from the previous year. Frost highlighted the improved relationships and effort shown by the players inside the building. He believes the culture is much better than it was just twelve months ago, even if the win-loss record did not always reflect that truth. "In the building here, we know how much progress we've made," Frost said, according to a report. The team has shown it can compete in the Big 12, but it needs to close out games to prove it on the scoreboard.

UCF will face Colorado on the road next, with a chance to finish its first winning season since joining the Big 12. Frost emphasized the need to finish the season the right way so the progress is clear to everyone. "We've got another game, we've got a chance to have a winning record in the Big 12 for the first time in program history, we've got a chance to finish this thing the right way," Frost said.
