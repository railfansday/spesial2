WWE Star Bronson Reed Dies at 38 Weeks After He Was Thrown Off a Stage Through Tables

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

WWE star Bronson Reed, whose real name was Jermaine Haley, has died. He was 38.

WWE announced that he died on Friday, November 27, due to "medical complications," but did not provide further details.

"Jermaine was a larger-than-life figure in WWE, but more importantly, he was a phenomenal person, a devoted family man, and a beloved colleague," the company said in a statement. "Our thoughts and deepest condolences are with Jermaine's entire family, including his pregnant wife, Paige, their daughter, as well as all his friends, co-workers and fans."

His sudden death comes just three weeks after he was badly hurt in the ring. During WWE's Crown Jewel event in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, on November 7, Reed was part of an angle during the World Tag Team Championship match in which Oba Femi slammed him off the stage and through a stack of tables, Uncrowned reported.

Reed reportedly suffered multiple broken ribs and a lacerated kidney. He remained in a hospital in Saudi Arabia for more than a week, then underwent surgery and returned home to continue his recovery.

The Crown Jewel event turned out to be his final WWE appearance.

WWE has not said whether his injuries were connected to his death.

Born in 1988 in Adelaide, South Australia, Reed grew up playing rugby and basketball but was drawn to professional wrestling from a young age. He made his in-ring debut in 2007 and spent years competing on the independent circuit in Australia and abroad, often under the name Jonah Rock.

He first signed with WWE in 2019, reporting to its developmental brand, NXT, where he won the NXT North American Championship. After being released in 2021 amid budget cuts, he returned to the company and made his main roster debut in 2022.

Reed went on to win the Andre the Giant Memorial Battle Royal in 2024 and most recently was part of the stable The Vision.
