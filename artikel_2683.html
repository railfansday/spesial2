FPL GW12 Tips: Best Transfers, Captain Picks and Wildcard

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Fantasy Premier League Gameweek 12 is almost here, and the decisions are getting tougher. With the busy December schedule approaching, FPL managers must balance immediate fixtures, player form, rotation risks, and the long-term value of their transfers.

Friday's early deadline also leaves less time to make those decisions. Whether you are planning a transfer, choosing your captain, or considering a Wildcard, it is worth getting your squad in order before the deadline.

Our complete FPL Gameweek 12 guide brings together the key fixtures, transfer targets, captain picks, differential options, and rotation concerns to help you prepare for the weekend.

The FPL Gameweek 12 deadline is Friday, November 27, at 18:30 GMT, with Chelsea's trip to Nottingham Forest opening the round.

Here are the fixtures to consider when planning your team:

There are several attractive opportunities for FPL managers this week, but not every favorable fixture will produce the expected returns.

Bournemouth's attacking assets travel to Fulham, while Leeds host Coventry City. Aston Villa also have an appealing away fixture against Ipswich Town.

The standout heavyweight clash sees Manchester City travel to Arsenal. That creates an interesting dilemma for managers who own premium players from either side, particularly when it comes to captaincy.

Before making any decisions, consider not just the immediate fixture but also the schedule beyond Gameweek 12.

The transfer market has been busy ahead of Gameweek 12, with managers moving quickly to bring in players who have delivered impressive recent returns.

Liverpool midfielder Florian Wirtz has emerged as the standout target, attracting more than 250,000 transfers in. His recent performances, creative output, and upcoming fixtures make him one of the most appealing midfield options.

Bournemouth striker Evanilson is another popular purchase after scoring in six consecutive matches. His form makes him an attractive alternative to the more expensive forwards.

Other players attracting significant attention include Marcus Tavernier, Jan Paul van Hecke, Nicolas Jackson, Noah Okafor, and James Trafford.

However, popularity alone should not dictate your transfer decisions. Some players are being bought because of their recent points, while others offer value over a longer run of fixtures.

Captaincy is one of the most important decisions of the Gameweek, particularly with Erling Haaland facing a difficult away fixture against Arsenal.

Haaland remains a reliable long-term FPL asset, but his immediate fixture makes this a week when looking beyond the obvious choice could be worthwhile.

Several alternatives deserve consideration:

Evanilson: Bournemouth's in-form striker has scored in six consecutive matches and faces Fulham away. His recent points and attacking momentum make him a strong captaincy candidate.

Florian Wirtz: The Liverpool midfielder has delivered consecutive double-digit returns and offers both goal and assist potential against Everton.

Nicolas Jackson: Aston Villa's forward has a favorable trip to Ipswich Town, making him an interesting option for managers prioritizing fixture difficulty.

Alexander Isak: Liverpool's striker offers a strong attacking ceiling, although his midweek involvement makes team news important.

Erling Haaland: Despite the difficult fixture, Haaland remains a viable captain for managers who prefer to back an established premium asset.

Our preference is to consider Evanilson's current form against the long-term reliability of Haaland and the creative potential of Wirtz.

With European fixtures and a congested domestic schedule, rotation is a major concern heading into Gameweek 12.

Several Premier League clubs have played in Europe this week, and the short turnaround could influence team selection.

The most important thing for FPL managers is to distinguish between a player who is likely to start and one whose minutes are uncertain.

Ezri Konsa, Arsenal: Defensive competition and recent European minutes could influence his starting prospects.

Riccardo Calafiori, Arsenal: Competition for the left-back position makes his minutes worth monitoring.

Rayan Cherki, Manchester City: Strong attacking returns do not necessarily guarantee a starting place.

Bryan Mbeumo, Manchester United: Midweek travel and a short turnaround could affect his selection.

Alexander Isak, Liverpool: His European involvement makes the latest team news particularly important.

Joško Gvardiol, Manchester City: Defensive competition and fixture congestion could influence his minutes.

Kepa Arrizabalaga, Arsenal: His starting prospects depend partly on David Raya's availability.

These are rotation risks rather than confirmed absences. A player being mentioned does not mean he will definitely miss the weekend match.

If you own any of the players on the list, check the latest team news before deciding whether to start them or make a transfer.

The challenge is building a squad that can navigate the busy festive schedule without requiring major changes every week.

Before finalizing your team, work through these important checks:

Check the latest injury and team news. A player being a doubt can completely change the value of a transfer, particularly with the busy December schedule approaching.

Look beyond Gameweek 12. A favorable fixture is useful, but it should not be the only reason to buy a player. Consider the next few matches and whether the player has a secure role.

Review your captaincy options. Evanilson, Wirtz, Jackson, and Haaland all offer different combinations of form, fixture difficulty, and potential returns.

Check your bench. Rotation is a genuine concern, so having a reliable substitute could save you from an unexpected no-show.

Avoid unnecessary transfers. If your squad is already well positioned for the upcoming fixtures, rolling a transfer could be more valuable than making a short-term move.

Think carefully about your Wildcard. If you are considering using it this week, prioritize a balanced squad that can handle the December schedule rather than focusing exclusively on the next round.

Gameweek 12 is an important opportunity to position your squad for the busy weeks ahead.

Wirtz and Evanilson are among the standout transfer targets, while the Scout Picks provide a useful starting point for managers deciding on their best XI.

There are also several differential options worth considering, particularly for managers looking to gain ground in their mini-leagues.

However, the most important decisions this week may be the ones that prevent unnecessary problems. Rotation, injuries, and a difficult fixture for Haaland all add an extra layer of complexity to the captaincy and transfer discussions.
