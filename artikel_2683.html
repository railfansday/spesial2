Jeremiah Smith becomes an even bigger Heisman favorite with 2 first-half TD catches against Nebraska

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Did Jeremiah Smith all but end the race for the Heisman Trophy in Ohio State's game against Nebraska on Saturday?

Smith entered the day as the Heisman favorite and became an even bigger one with two first-half touchdown catches. After a TD catch on Ohio State's third offensive play of the game, Smith added a toe-dragging catch in the back of the end zone late in the second quarter.

Look at how he got his feet inbounds on this 31-yard catch.

That grab was Smith's 17th TD catch of the season. His 16th came on a 67-yard catch in the first quarter as Nebraska inexplicably decided to play man coverage on a third-down play. As you can imagine, Smith just absolutely dusted his defender on a deep post.

Before Saturday's games began, Smith's odds to win the Heisman were -200 at BetMGM. They quickly became -500 over the course of the first half. Miami QB Darian Mensah, the only other player with odds better than 10-1 to win the award, is the No. 2 favorite at +700.

With his two touchdown catches in the first half, Smith is also just two receiving TDs away from tying Desmond Howard's longtime Big Ten record for TD catches in a season. Howard had 19 TD receptions in his Heisman-winning season in 1991.

Despite Smith's catches, however, Ohio State trailed at halftime. Nebraska attempted to control the ball as much as possible as it led 21-14 at the break.
