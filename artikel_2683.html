SMAT: Musheer Khan's 65 gives Mumbai sixth victory

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Mumbai skipper Suryakumar Yadav endured his first failure of the Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy but Musheer Khan's 65 off 40 balls and Atharva Ankolekar's 3/17 powered the defending champions to an 86-run win over Uttarakhand in Kolkata on Thursday.Mumbai, who had one match washed out, finished the league stage unbeaten with six wins in seven matches to top Elite Group D and enter the Super League stage, beginning in Nagpur on Nov. 30.Batting at No. 6, Suryakumar was bowled by Jagadeesha Suchith for one. However, Mumbai piled up a formidable total thanks to contributions from Musheer, opener Angkrish Raghuvanshi (51 off 34), Hardik Tamore (39 off 14) and Sarfaraz Khan (31 off 15).Uttarakhand slumped to 58/5 in their chase before Shashwat Dangwal (37 off 20) and Suchith (19) offered some resistance.Ankolekar, Maxwell Swaminathan (2/35) and Musheer (1/1) then shared six wickets to bowl them out for 123.The eight teams through to the Super League are Baroda and Rajasthan from Group A, Delhi and Punjab from Group B, Andhra and Kerala from Group C, and Mumbai and Vidarbha from Group D.Arya continues to fire in Delhi winFollowing his record-breaking 181 in the previous match, Delhi skipper Priyansh Arya struck an unbeaten 56 off 30 balls to guide his side to an eight-wicket win over Railways in Indore. Tejasvi Dahiya chipped in with an unbeaten 41 off 16 balls as Delhi chased down 123 in just 11.1 overs.Chahar's 3/13 takes Rajasthan throughDeepak Chahar gave IPL franchises a timely reminder of his ability ahead of the Dec. 16 auction, claiming 3/13 in four overs to guide Rajasthan to a sixwicket win over Bengal at the Wankhede.Puducherry stun Madhya PradeshPuducherry edged out Madhya Pradesh by two wickets, chasing down 158 with two balls to spare.
