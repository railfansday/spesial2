Vikings-Packers Second Injury Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Minnesota Vikings and Green Bay Packers released their second injury reports in advance of their matchup on Sunday at Lambeau Field. Here they are:

PUP = Physically unable to participate list.

IR = Injured reserve. Note: only starters on IR are listed.

Probable = the league defines a Probable injury designation as a 75% or greater chance of playing. Historically, 95% of players designated probable are active and played on game day.

Questionable = the league defines a Questionable injury designation as being 'uncertain' of whether the player will play or not. This replaces the former definition of having a 50% chance of playing. Historically, 70-75% of players with a Questionable injury designation were active and played on game day.

Doubtful = The league defines a Doubtful injury designation to mean the player is unlikely to participate in the game. In practical terms, teams use the designation when a player has less than a 50% chance to suit up, and active game appearances from this designation remain rare. Historically only around 6% of players with a Doubtful designation are active and played on game day.

Out = will not play. Players with this designation are not active on game day and league rules prohibit them from playing.

For the Vikings, no change in injury status for anybody compared to Wednesday's estimates. That doesn't bode well for any of the players that have yet to practice this week being available on Sunday. We'll see if there are any upgrades tomorrow, but otherwise it looks like in addition to Blake Cashman, Hockenson, Ingram-Dawkins, Jennings, and Van Noy may not be available on Sunday.

For the Packers, only kicker Trey Smack has been upgraded, so in addition to Josh Jacobs- who is having surgery on his broken finger tomorrow, the Packers may be without starters Lukas Van Ness, Javon Bullard, Sean Rhyan, and Zaire Franklin.

We'll have updates tomorrow with injury designations.
