Ciryl Gane defeats Josh Hokit in UFC 334 main event war

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Saturday's UFC 334 fight card at Madison Square Garden was headlined by a heavyweight title bout between champion Ciryl Gane and undefeated Josh Hokit.

Gane capture the interim title by defeating Alex Pereira at UFC Freedom 250 at the White House in June. He was later elevated to the undisputed titleholder after Tom Aspinall vacated the championship due to ongoing complications from eye injuries.

Hokit entered the bout unbeaten and coming off wins over Derrick Lewis and Curtis Blaydes. "The Incredible Hok" was looking to complete his meteoric rise in the heavyweight division with a championship.
