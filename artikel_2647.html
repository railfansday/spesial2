Tre Tucker held to one catch in Week 10 loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Las Vegas Raiders WR Tre Tucker caught one of his two targets for nine yards against the Seattle Seahawks in Week 10.

Why it Matters: Tucker was held in check in Week 10, but to be fair, the Raiders were facing a stout Seattle defense and had Fernando Mendoza under center in his NFL debut. Tucker has had a few strong performances this year, but it could be a rough few weeks while Mendoza gets his feet wet. In the meantime, Tucker is probably best viewed as a WR4 heading into a Week 11 matchup with Denver.
