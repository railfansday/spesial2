Iman Shumpert questions Tom Brady’s ‘winner’ status despite seven Super Bowl titles

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Former NBA champion Iman Shumpert has questioned why Tom Brady is considered one of sport's greatest winners despite not winning a Super Bowl in most of his NFL seasons.

The former Cleveland Cavaliers guard made the comments during an appearance on the Gil's Arena podcast, discussing how athletes are judged across different sports.

Brady won seven Super Bowls during his 23-year NFL career, including six with the New England Patriots and another with the Tampa Bay Buccaneers.

Shumpert, who won the 2016 NBA championship alongside LeBron James, questioned the standards used to celebrate athletes in different leagues.

He pointed to NBA players receiving praise for shooting 40% from the field while Brady is regularly compared with basketball legends Michael Jordan and Bill Russell.

Shumpert also suggested Brady won just 20% of his career games, a figure that does not match the quarterback's actual record.

According to Pro Football Reference, Brady finished his career with a 251-82 regular-season record as a starting quarterback.

That gives him a winning percentage of 75.4%, rather than the 20% figure raised in the discussion. His postseason record was also impressive, with 35 wins and 13 defeats.

Combined, Brady finished with a 286-95 record as a starter across the regular season and playoffs.

The former Patriots quarterback's championship record is just as difficult to overlook. He won six Super Bowls in New England before adding a seventh with Tampa Bay in 2020.

Brady also collected five Super Bowl MVP awards and three NFL MVP honours during his career.

Shumpert knows what it takes to win at the highest level himself. He spent 10 seasons in the NBA after being selected by the New York Knicks in the first round of the 2011 Draft.

His defining achievement came in 2016, when Cleveland overturned a 3-1 deficit against the Golden State Warriors to win the NBA Finals.

The wider debate is about how much weight championships should carry when judging an athlete's legacy. Brady did not win a title every season, but his record makes it difficult to question his status as one of the NFL's most successful players.
