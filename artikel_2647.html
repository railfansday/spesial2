Broncos’ have one of the best safety duos in the NFL… when healthy

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

There were a couple of tweets on safety play in the NFL dropped and despite a rough few weeks with missed tackles, the Denver Broncos have arguably the best or second best duo in the whole league in Talanoa Hufanga and Brandon Jones.

The first came from Gridiron Grading on what they call "Havoc plays" that include a variety of impactful defensive plays like turnovers, tackles for losses, and pass breakups.

Hufanga is off up there in the stratosphere, but Brandon Jones is also in there among the elite to in that upper left box area. So when it comes to those kinds of Havoc plays, both Broncos' starting safeties are balling out in 2026.

There was another on separation and on ball grades for all qualifying safeties. Denver actually had four make the chart, with Taylor Rapp having a lot of struggles. Devon Key has been really good on the on ball grade, but is lacking on separation percentage. However, both Jones and Hufanga are up there in the quality starter range. Hufanga is among the best on ball.

The caveat to all of these stats, of course, is neither guy has been able to stay on the field. Nothing major on the injury front, but the minor nagging type has caused both player to miss time. Hopefully we see them both back out there after the bye and locking down the back end of the defense.

11 true Super Bowl contenders, and their biggest question

Looking at Denver through an EPA lens, you see a team that is more "league average" than Super Bowl contender. Denver's talented defense is actually a below-average unit against the pass (when examining EPA/dropback) and the offense is right in the middle of the pack.
