FSU's recruiting class ranking after Za'Kari Johnson's decommitment

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Florida State Seminoles have suffered another blow to their 2027 recruiting class, with four-star athlete Za'Kari Johnson decommitting on Friday. He is their third decommitment since they fired Mike Norvell.

Johnson committed to FSU on July 14, reclassifying from the 2028 recruiting class as well. Despite his commitment, it seemed unlikely he would stick in the class, especially after they moved on from Norvell. He made unofficial visits during the season to see the Miami Hurricanes, Texas A&M Aggies, and Auburn Tigers, before ultimately decommitting.

He is ranked as the No. 327 overall player and No. 21 athlete in the 247Sports composite. He was expected to play in the secondary at FSU. Four-star Ta'Shawn Poole and three-star Jemari Foreman are now FSU's only defensive back commits, with both expected to play safety in Tallahassee.
