Building Bakodo: High-Performance Browser-Based Barcode & QR Code Engine with Astro, bwip-js, and zxing-wasm

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Barcodes and QR codes are ubiquitous across logistics, retail, warehousing, and event ticketing. However, most online generators either require server-side rendering roundtrips, impose strict rate limits, or plaster invasive ads over the interface.

When engineering bakodo.jp, our goal was simple: create a completely free, 100% client-side barcode suite capable of rendering 111 barcode formats (including JAN/EAN/UPC/ISBN, Code128, Data Matrix, PDF417), generating custom QR codes, handling thermal label printing (ZPL/TSPL/EPL2), and scanning codes directly from device cameras—all running locally in the browser with zero data leakage.

Transmitting inventory or product data to third-party APIs introduces security and latency bottlenecks. By packaging bwip-js (Barcode Writer in Pure JavaScript) into web workers, bakodo.jp computes linear and 2D matrix symbologies purely on the client CPU.

Because everything is calculated on-canvas or exported directly to vector SVG, users can batch-render thousands of SKU labels in seconds without sending a single byte over the wire.

Real-time browser barcode scanning has historically suffered from sluggish frame rates. Traditional pure JavaScript ports struggle when decoding dense 2D matrices or damaged codes from high-resolution mobile camera feeds.

We integrated zxing-wasm—compiling the battle-tested C++ ZXing-C++ library to WebAssembly with SIMD acceleration. With a lightweight requestAnimationFrame loop, the WebAssembly module scans live video streams at 60 FPS while keeping thread utilization minimal.

Industrial operations depend heavily on dedicated thermal barcode printers (Zebra, TSC, Citizen, etc.). Rendering high-resolution bitmaps and sending them via the standard OS print dialog often results in fuzzy anti-aliased barcodes that scanners fail to read.
