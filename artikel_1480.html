Gabbia ‘amazed’ by Cisse’s hunger and desire: “He just needs to keep it up”

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

On the sidelines of the Fondazione Milan event last night, Matteo Gabbia heaped praise on Alphadjo Cisse, who has been very important despite his young age.

Together with the Milan legend Daniele Massaro and the Milan Women player Julie Piga, Gabbia was present at a Fondazione Milan event yesterday. An interview was published with Sky Italia afterwards, in which he confirmed that a return could happen in January.

The defender also spoke to some other media outlets that were present, among other things sharing his thoughts on Cisse. The 20-year-old has an impressive eight goals across all competitions for Milan so far, certainly playing a big role in the points collected.

“He’s at a club that always has to win, so it’s right that he knows where he stands, that he always trains well, and that he doesn’t lose his hunger and desire to improve every day.

“These are beautiful things to see and they have amazed me. I’m really happy that he has been called up for the national team. He just has to keep it up because he’s getting some great results and helping us in return,” Gabbia said as relayed by MilanNews.

Cisse and Mattia Liberali were both left out of Italy’s squad for the 3-1 win against France the other night, but there’s another opportunity against Belgium tomorrow evening.
