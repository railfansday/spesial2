It looks like Geno Smith will start at QB for the Jets in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Jets quarterback Geno Smith twice left the team's Week 10 loss to the Buffalo Bills with an injury. His status has been up in the air through the week. While head coach Aaron Glenn did not guarantee Smith will start at quarterback, the news on Friday certainly makes it look probable.

With the Jets sporting a 2-8 record and the season effectively lost, many fans are calling on the team to get rookie Cade Klubnik into the lineup. Klubnik, a fourth round pick out of Clemson is unlikely to be the Jets' quarterback of the future. The team is likely to select another quarterback high in the 2027 NFL Draft, but Klubnik will remain on the team next year and likely beyond. The theory goes that getting reps for the rookie will be valuable.

However, with Smith having a solid season the Jets are going to hold off on a quarterback change at least for one more week.
