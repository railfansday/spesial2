The Raptors showed they can win tonight: even if it’s just the Nets

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

To borrow words from Bret Easton Ellis' American Psycho (1991), there is an idea of a Toronto Raptors, some kind of abstraction, but there is no real team, only what they look like on the floor that night. But against the Nets, the Raptors looked like the type of team that they've billed themselves as since last season. Big, dangerous, able to guard well, while hitting their shots inside and outside of the arc.

And tonight, with Collin Murray-Boyles, Scottie Barnes, and Allen Graves all playing solid minutes, it looked like Vision 6'9" come to life, like a monster animated with the sole purpose of clobbering the Brooklyn Nets.

The first quarter had the Raptors shooting — and more importantly, making — more threes than usual. They outshot the Nets by both accuracy and volume, with Barnes and Graves looking like a real one-two punch. Julius Randle, while delivering thundering dunks, went 0-3 from three in the first quarter, as the Raptors ran a solid transition offence. The Raptors left the first quarter up four points on the Nets, despite Brooklyn dominating in the painted area, scoring 16 of its 27 points there.

Raptors fought themselves into a double digit lead over the Nets with an Allen Graves three, and continued to build momentum as the second quarter wound down. Scottie Barnes took it to the rack with a spin move into a filthy left hander that left the room begging for more. The Nets heated up from three by the end of the second quarter, but the Raptors maintained their lead into halftime.

The third quarter saw Immanuel Quickley rain in treys, as the Raptors' lead continued to grow. They cracked 100 points as the seconds rolled down, up more than 20 points. At the end of the quarter, Barnes drove to the basket and laid it in, cracking 30 points with 12 minutes left in the game.

By then, Barnes had not grabbed a single rebound, with his teammates doing a lot of the dirty work down low. Further, while Scottie generated great looks, it was Quickley who picked up double digit points and assists in the contest. Against a bad team, Barnes was able to focus on scoring, anchoring the Raptors' offence as they blazed past the Nets. The idea central to the Raptors' building around Scottie had, for the past two seasons, been to pair the young forward with a more veteran scorer.

However, this season has allowed Barnes to prove that on a nightly basis, he can take the top defensive or offensive assignment for the team. Barnes' ability to switch has long been lauded, but the offensive versatility he has displayed is something that the Raptors would benefit from remembering when Kawhi Leonard returns.

The Brooklyn Nets are the Brooklyn Nets, and using a hot knife to cleave through butter isn't a good test to see if your knife is actually sharp. But, it's nice to see after the Milwaukee debacle that the Raptors still have it in them to stomp the NBA's bad teams. More than that, however, tonight showed a version of the Raptors with high highs and low lows, with much to be emulated, and some things to be firmly rejected.

The shooting, especially from those who aren't usually snipers, was great. Allen Graves getting the minutes he needs to develop, and shining in them, produced some great highlights. But, moments of awkwardness, especially the Nets powering through the Raptors in the paint, were something that did not befit a team that projects a defensive mindset as outwardly as Toronto does.

While Ja'Kobe Walter was forced to leave the game with an injury, and did not return, he was quiet from the field, scoring his only points from the charity stripe. Similarly, Jaminson Battle ended the game with 1-6 from three. Sometimes, the shots just don't fall, but the Raptors shouldn't need to compromise between their shooters having good games from range.

Tonight's numbers from the Raptors looked great, but as much as the Raptors won this game, the Nets also lost it, giving up victory to the Raptors rather than having it taken from them forcibly. The rebounding difference between both teams is not something the Raptors will likely be able to sustain against more poised teams, nor the three point volume, but tonight's efficiency is what the team should model, and strive for, especially in harder matchups.
