Panthers designate LB Thomas Incoom for return from IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Panthers have opened the practice window for linebacker Thomas Incoom.

Incoom has been designated for return from injured reserve and will be able to practice for the next 21 days as a result. The Panthers can activate him at any point in that window.

Incoom was placed on injured reserve ahead of Week 7 due to a calf injury. He had six tackles and a fumble recovery in five games before his injury.

The Panthers have now designated seven players for return this season. They will be able to do it one more time during the regular season and that spot is expected to be filled by cornerback Jaycee Horn once he is ready to get back on the field.
