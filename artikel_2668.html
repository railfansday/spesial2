Broncos vs. Steelers first quarter recap

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Denver Broncos won the opening coin toss and deferred to bring out Mason Rudolph and the Pittsburgh Steelers offense to start this game.

Early on, the Steelers offense looked efficient with two runs for 10 yards and a five yard completion to DK Metcalf to the roar of the crowd. Two runs later they would be crossing midfield into Broncos' territory. Rudolph avoided a sack from Que Robinson and got the dump off for no gain to bring a third and six. From the shotgun, Rudolph handed off inside for a four yard gain where it looked like they would go for it. Instead, they decided to punt it away.

The punt was fantastic and pinned the Broncos to their own four yard line. Bo Nix and the offense would start their first drive from the shadow of their own end zone.

Jonah Coleman was stuffed on first down, then Nix threw it away on a well-covered route outside to Jaylen Waddle. On third and 9, Nix tossed it short to Evan Engram who dropped it. The play wouldn't have gained anywhere near enough for the first down anyway.

A poor punt set the Steelers up at the Broncos' 40 yard line on their next drive. Denver's run defense stuffed Jaylen Warren on back to back runs to force Rudolph into a third and nine where he would panic and refuse to the ball away despite pass rushers all over him multiple times and was finally sacked by Nik Bonitto and Malcolm Roach for a huge sack of 12 yards to push them well out of field goal range.

The Steelers would trade bad punts with one of their own to give Denver the ball back with decent field position at their own 24 yard line compared to the first drive.

TJ Watt took control from there dominating Mike McGlinchey on three straight plays culminating in a fumble recovery in the end zone for a touchdown for the Steelers.

Bo Nix finally completed a pass on the next series. A short three yard completion to Waddle while being pressured again. A screen pass outside to Jonah Coleman gained a big chunk but was stuffed just shy of the first down where the next run was blown up. Denver's offensive line has been very bad to start the game.

On the Steelers first play after the punt, Alex Singleton was burned badly for a huge 58 yard gain on a short quick crosser.

The first quarter began about the worst it could possibly begin for the Denver Broncos. They are being dominated in every phase of the game.

Two plays later DK Metcalf beat Pat Surtain II for a 30 yard touchdown to put Pittsburgh up huge early.

An excellent return by Tyler Badie set Denver up at their own 42-yard line. They desperately needed to get things going in this game and needed to take advantage of that field position. They would get across midfield as the first quarter would wind down to a close.
