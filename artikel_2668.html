'It shouldn't happen to us!' – Denzel Dumfries slams Netherlands after 5-0 Germany thrashing

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Netherlands captain Denzel Dumfries has described his team's 5-0 defeat to Germany as a collective failure. Dumfries pointed to poor duels and excessive spaces as key factors in the heavy Nations League loss.

The Netherlands wrapped up their UEFA Nations League group stage campaign with a sobering 5-0 defeat against Germany at the Olympiastadion. Having joined Spanish giants Real Madrid in July 2026 following successful spells with PSV and Inter Milan, the experienced right-back captained his country in Berlin as the Oranje were completely overrun in the second half.

Despite this heavy 5-0 reverse—marking their biggest defeat of the century and only their second-highest loss against Germany after 1959—Xavi Hernández's side still finished second in Group A2, securing their passage through to the March quarter-finals alongside group winners Germany.

Reflecting on the match in an initial reaction to NOS, Dumfries did not sugar-coat the reality of the performance, identifying glaring structural and physical shortcomings across the pitch.

"It was a poor performance. Too weak in the duels and the spaces were too big," Dumfries admitted.

"We lost a lot of second balls, we were weak in the duels and they could easily strike on the counter. It was simply a collective failure. That shouldn't happen to us."

Dumfries also addressed the mounting tension within the squad during the match, noting that frustration and anger hampered their ability to stay calm and execute their game plan against a ruthless German press.

"There is anger and frustration, but in a match like this you have to stay focused," Dumfries noted.

"There was also mutual frustration and that is not good."

The Dutch inability to close down space allowed players like Florian Wirtz and Brajan Gruda to dictate the tempo, with the opening goal specifically arriving after Dumfries failed to intervene as Derry Scherhant cut inside from the left flank.

While the manner of the defeat stings deeply, the Netherlands must quickly put this disappointment behind them as they look ahead to the Nations League quarter-finals scheduled for March 2027.

Dumfries and his teammates will use the winter break to recalibrate, recognising that a repeat of such a collective collapse will not be tolerated when they face Europe's best in the knockout stages.
