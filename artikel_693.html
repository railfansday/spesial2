‘Dancing With the Stars’ Sets First-Ever Live Finale for Season 35

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

For the first time in “Dancing With the Stars” history, the ballroom competition show will air its finale live.

The three-hour finale will air on Nov. 24 from 8:00-11:00 p.m. ET, 7:00-10:00 p.m. CT, 6:00-9:00 p.m. MT and 5:00-8:00 p.m. PT on ABC and Disney+. The finale will re-air from 8:00-11:00 p.m. PT and stream the next day on Hulu.

Currently in its 35th season, six celebrities remain in the competition heading into Tuesday night’s semi-finals: Tatyana Ali, Ezra Frech, Amber Glenn, Maura Higgins, Harry Shum Jr. and Connor Wood. The following week, the remaining couples will compete for the Len Goodman Mirrorball Trophy, performing three routines each: a Judges’ Choice Dance, the return of the Instant Dance Challenge and a Freestyle. The season’s September premiere reached a strong 6.7 million viewers, a 24% increase from the performance of the Season 34 premiere. The episode also marked the series’ best opener in six years, since Season 29 launched with 8.1 million viewers in 2020.

And the audience is still watching along. “Dancing with the Stars” hit a season high with 6.91 million same-day viewers during last week’s Grammy Night.

Season 35 cast members also included now-eliminated celebrities Jenna Dewan, Jackson Olson, Julia Stiles, Ciara Miller, Conner Leavitt, Guillermo Rodriguez, Taylor Hanson, Tyler Cameron, Sarah Jane Nader and Giada De Laurentiis. The reigning champions are Robert Irwin and pro Whitney Carson, who took home the Mirrorball Trophy in Season 34.

“Dancing with the Stars” is produced in front of a live audience by BBC Studios Los Angeles Productions.
