Pure Cycle (PCYO +0.00%) reported fiscal 2026 net income of $14.2 million, marking its ninth consecutive year of profitability. Management stated that top-line growth was supported by record activity in land development and a tripling of water deliveries to industrial operators. The company is initiating a strategic shift to exit its single-family rental segment to concentrate capital on core water and land assets. Management also highlighted milestones in its infrastructure development, including progress on the I-70 interchange permit and the completion of a K-12 campus at Sky Ranch.
