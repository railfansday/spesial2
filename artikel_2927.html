Neon jerseys and a drone show: Miami Heat's Vice City night celebrates &quot;GTA6&quot;

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Miami Heat are celebrating "Grand Theft Auto 6" on Wednesday, Nov. 18, the eve before the highly-anticipated video game is released. They are transforming their basketball matchup against the Milwaukee Bucks into "Vice City" to mirror the world of the game. It will be the first time Giannis Antetokounmpo faces the team that drafted him and guided him to superstardom.

Antetokounmpo joined the Heat this summer in a mega trade that sent Tyler Herro, Kel'el Ware, Jaime Jaquez Jr. and Kasparas Jakučionis to Milwaukee. The No. 15 overall pick in the 2013 NBA draft won two league MVPs, was named an All-Star 10 times and won the 2020-21 NBA finals in Milwaukee, when he was named Finals MVP.

But the Heat have been heavily promoting "A Night in Vice City" since announcing the event back in October. The hype for "Grand Theft Auto 6" has been building for a decade. Now that the game is a day away from release, a $3.9 million marketing campaign is in full force.

The Heat's involvement started with a neon sign that said "Welcome to Vice City" on the roof of the Kaseya Center and has included a series of reveals that culminate in an immersive experience signature to South Beach.

Here's what to know about the Heat's "Night in Vice City:"

The Heat is partnering with Rockstar Games, the creator of "Grand Theft Auto" to host "A Night in Vice City." The event is a promotion for the latest installment of the video game series, "Grand Theft Auto 6," which has been ten years in the making.

For the matchup against the Bucks, the Kaseya Center will be turned into an immersive experience based on Vice City. "Grand Theft Auto 6" is set in the fictional location described as "the darkest side of the sunniest place in America." In previews for the video game, there's palm trees, alligators, exotic cars, gold chains, dark alleys and art deco buildings on the beach with neon lights. Basically, Miami.

For the basketball matchup, there will be special edition uniforms, a "GTA6"-themed court design and a halftime show performance from a surprise guest. Around the arena, there will be music from the "GTA 6" soundtrack being played, photo opps, merchandise, and concessions from the game's fast food restaurants "Burger Shot" and "Cluckin' Bell." The night will conclude with a drone show overlooking Biscayne Bay.

Fans are encouraged to get to the Kaseya Center early to soak in the entire experience. Doors open at 5 p.m. ET ahead of the 7 p.m. tipoff.

Miami partnered with Travis Scott's Cactus Jack brand for their Vice City uniforms celebrating "Grand Theft Auto 6." The jerseys have a black base and pink lines that look like neon lights. The players will be wearing the uniforms during the game and the team said there will be "limited quantities" of jerseys for fans to purchase.

The Heat previously had a Miami Vice-themed jersey series from 2018 to 2021 that utilized pink and blue, the signature colors of South Beach. The concept came about because, when the NBA switched uniform partners from adidas to Nike, Nike introduced the City Edition program. The first uniform, called "Vice," paid tribute to the 1980s crime drama starring Don Johnson and celebrated the vibrant culture of the city.

Per Andscape, the Heat's jersey was purchased more than the total of the other NBA teams' inaugural City Edition jerseys combined. The other iterations of the jersey created a buzz for four more seasons.

The Heat unveiled the court for their Vice City night a day before the matchup against the Bucks. Unlike the intense all-over design of the NBA Cup courts, this one retains a natural wood base. A pink "Vice City" word logo is featured at center court.

The three-point arcs are filled with cotton candy pink with a royal blue box filling in the paint area under the basket. There is a faint palm tree design in the pink zone, which also features the "Grand Theft Auto 6" Roman Numerals logo.

The court has a black border with "I (heart) Vice City" written on the baselines.

Fans can purchase limited edition Vice City merchandise at various locations throughout the Kaseya Center and at the Heat's online team store.

For the Vice City collection, there is a variety of T-shirts priced at $55 with designs reflecting the city's culture, including an alligator playing basketball in a pool and an image of teeth with a gold grill that says "Welcome to Vice City." There are a handful of snapback hats for $50 and baseball caps for $48 that have "Vice City" written on them. There's also a black satin bomber jacket for $168 that says "Vice City" in pink and a "Welcome to Vice City" neon sign that's going for $120.

At the arena, there will be a pop-up for a collection from Travis Scott's Cactus Jack brand and Vice City jerseys for sale.
