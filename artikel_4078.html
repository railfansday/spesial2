Square Enix's 'Harvest Sale' Includes Huge Discounts On Final Fantasy VII Remake Intergrade And Rebirth

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Final Fantasy VII Remake trilogy will wrap next year with the release of Revelation and if you're wanting to catch up on the series, now is the perfect chance.

Square Enix is currently hosting its "Harvest Sale", which includes discounts on digital games and DLC on the eShop. Remake Intergrade is currently reduced to $7.99 / £7.99 and the sequel Rebirth will set you back just $7.49 / £7.49 instead of the usual price of $39.99 / £39.99 & $49.99 / £49.99 (or your regional equivalent).

Apart from this, there are also discounts on the Dragon Quest games, Octopath Traveler series, other Final Fantasy titles, and much more RPG goodness. In the US, this sale runs until 17th November, and in the UK it ends on the 16th November. Here are just some of the titles currently on sale:

For those wondering, part of the reason about the crazy low Remake and Rebirth prices is probably because Switch 2 doesn't get the trilogy bundle which PS5/PC/Series X|S gets.

That bundle which went live after the September State of Play is £80 for the whole trilogy (Revelation standalone is £70) so you get Remake + Rebirth for £10. With the other platforms your preorder money is taken straight away so you can play the other 2 games while you wait for Revelation.

Nintendo however has an annoying setup in place caused by lawsuits where preorder money is only taken a week before launch. So if the bundle existed on Switch 2, getting that bundle would mean you wouldn't be able to play Remake and Rebirth in time for Revelation's launch.

Square Enix seems to have done deeper discounts on Switch 2 to make up for that issue.
