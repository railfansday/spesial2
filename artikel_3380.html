New Doctor Doom Appearance Solves A Major Avengers: Doomsday Mystery (& Raises 2 Huge Questions)

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Warning! This post contains SPOILERS for VisionQuest episode 8

Doctor Doom's latest MCU appearances essentially confirm one of the biggest Avengers: Doomsday theories. However, there are still two fairly big question marks that are well worth discussing in the aftermath.

Between the multiverse's growing instability and the return of several familiar heroes from across realities, there are plenty of unanswered questions surrounding RDJ's Doctor Doom and his full plans for the multiverse. To that end, every new Doctor Doom appearance before Doomsday makes his plan all the more intriguing.

Following Doctor Doom's incredibly exciting appearance at the end of VisionQuest, it now seems more than likely that the ruler of Latveria will have a major ally by his side: Wanda Maximoff's Scarlet Witch. That being said, one still has to wonder who else might be supporting Doom's bid for power in Avengers: Doomsday.

As seen in VisionQuest's major post-credits scene, it was revealed that Doctor Doom revived and took Wanda Maximoff from Mount Wundagore after she brought the mountain down on herself at the end of Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, sacrificing herself to destroy every copy of the Darkhold in existence across realities.

Commanding Wanda to awaken in VisionQuest's second post-credits scene, Doctor Doom says that Wanda's power will become his before reviving her. By all accounts, this is a pretty important scene across the board before Doomsday. Not only does it confirm that Doom is amassing all kinds of power (like claiming the Hulk in one of Avengers: Endgame Encore post-credits scenes), it suggests quite a bit, considering what we already know about Doctor Doom and witches. After all, various promotional materials have shown Doctor Doom backed by a trio of mysterious masked women known only as the "Latverian Witches" throughout Avengers: Doomsday's marketing.

It stands to reason after VisionQuest that one of these Latverian Witches is Wanda herself, especially when you factor in the comics precedent with the "Children's Crusade" storyline, where Wanda went missing following her major House of M breakdown, only to be taken in by Doctor Doom who planned to marry the amnesiac Scarlet Witch in a bid to claim her power. Keeping that in mind, perhaps Wanda will similarly find herself serving Doom in Avengers: Doomsday, willingly or otherwise. However, there are still some pretty big questions about whom the other two Latverian witches might be.

Even before VisionQuest, Elizabeth Olsen's Scarlet Witch was a major candidate for one of Doctor Doom's masked Latverian Witches alongside Charlize Theron's Clea and Sophia Di Martino's Sylvie, especially with their differently colored (perhaps corresponding?) red, purple, and green robes. While Wanda is practically a foregone conclusion at this point, neither Clea nor Sylvie has been officially confirmed as a Latverian Witch.

Then again, an even wilder possibility is that all the Latverian Witches could be variants of Wanda Maximoff from across various realities. Perhaps Doom is collecting more than one Scarlet Witch in an effort to amplify his own power to absolutely massive levels.

That said, the distinct costume colors and the possibility of recruiting even more established magic users in the MCU make Clea and Sylvie the far more exciting/compelling answer.

Ultimately, Marvel Studios has yet to reveal who's beneath the other masks. Even so, with Wanda Maximoff's Scarlet Witch seemingly confirmed as one of Doom's witches, the mystery behind the other two mysterious magic wielders in Avengers: Doomsday just got way more exciting. All in all, it's going to be absolutely fascinating to learn what Robert Downey Jr.'s Doctor Doom intends for the collapsing multiverse, and how these witches in his service will factor into those plans. Thankfully, we won't have to wait much longer, with Avengers: Doomsday being just a few weeks away.

Avengers: Doomsday releases in theaters on December 18th from Marvel Studios.

Avengers: Doomsday is the fifth Avengers film and kicks off Phase 6 of the Marvel Cinematic Universe. A stacked cast of heroes both new and old to the MCU — including members of the X-Men — will face off against Victor von Doom, revealed at San Diego Comic-Con in 2024 to be played by a returning Robert Downey Jr.
