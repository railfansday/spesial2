Guillermo Del Toro's New Netflix Horror Adaptation Gets Major Release Timeline Update

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Guillermo del Toro's next film just received a major new release update from one of its stars.

Del Toro is coming off of the success of his hit movie Frankenstein, which starred Oscar Isaac as the titular doctor and Jacob Elordi as the Creature. Just over a year ago, the horror flick was released on Netflix and went on to win three Oscars and nine nominations in total. Critics also praised Frankenstein, giving it a Certified Fresh score of 85% on Rotten Tomatoes, and audiences were even more favorable at 94%.

Now the acclaimed filmmaker is producing an adaptation of his and Chuck Hogan's serialized novel The Boy in the Iron Box, which came out in 2024. During an interview on The Regular Joes Podcast at Rhode Island Comic Con, Kevin Durand mentioned that he's starring in the film and revealed that he believes it will be released next April on Netflix.

Durand added that he previously worked with de Toro on the FX series The Strain, playing a Ukrainian Brooklynite. "And then he was like, 'You know what? We've really got to stretch Kevin's wings and cast him as a Russian guy in this movie, so I'm playing both sides of the conflict in separate narratives," he explained. Durand went on to say that his character this time around is a "pretty interesting" person, and he believes The Boy in the Iron Box is a "good movie."

Aside from The Strain, Durand has also appeared in TV shows like Lost, Vikings, Swamp Thing, Locke & Key and the upcoming fifth season of Reacher, as well as the movies 3:10 to Yuma, X-Men Origins: Wolverine, Real Steel, Resident Evil: Retribution, Kingdom of the Planet of the Apes, The Naked Gun and Ready or Not 2: Here I Come.

His next project, The Boy in the Iron Box, was helmed and written by David Prior. Del Toro is serving as a producer with J. Miles Dale. Durand is starring alongside Rupert Friend, Jaeden Martell, Chris Petrovski, Aksel Hennie, Arnas Fedaravicius, Eugene Prokofiev, Julian Kostov and Jóhannes Haukur Jóhannesson.

The film tells the story of a group of mercenaries who find themselves trapped on the summit of a mountain after their plane crashes. They quickly realize that was the least of their worries, as a stone fortress reveals horrors they never expected to cross paths with.

Del Toro is best known for directing movies like Hellboy, Pan's Labyrinth, Pacific Rim and The Shape of Water. Frankenstein and The Boy in the Iron Box aren't the only projects that he has collaborated with Netflix on. He's also worked on Pinocchio, the Tales of Arcadia trilogy series and Cabinet of Curiosities. Now del Toro is coming back to Netflix for The Boy in the Iron Box as part of his multi-year deal with the popular streamer.
