Xenoblade Chronicles 3 Version 3.0.0 Is Now Live, Here Are The Patch Notes

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Ahead of the launch of Xenoblade Chronicles 3's Switch 2 Edition on 3rd December 2026, Nintendo and Monolith Soft have this week rolled out a new game update.

This patch officially bumps the title up to Version 3.0.0. Nothing else is mentioned and it's further noted how "no changes" have been made when playing the game on the Switch. Here's the rundown from Nintendo's official support page:

As mentioned by Nintendo, the Switch 2 Edition adds the mythical new hero, Shimmer. There's also the new mode 'Heroes' Vault', exclusive to the Switch 2 Edition.

Apart from this, the upgrade will come with support for 4K resolution in TV mode, 60fps performance, and event scenes have been updated to be "fully voiced in English and Japanese".

If you're planning on upgrading from the Switch version, the upgrade will cost $9.99 / £7.99 or your regional equivalent.
