NeuroPace, Inc. (NPCE -3.99%) reported third-quarter revenue results that fell below internal expectations, leading to a downward revision of full-year 2026 financial guidance. Management attributed the performance shortfall to localized operational issues at high-volume accounts, sales force restructuring, and the residual effects of exiting the SEEG distribution business. The company is increasing investments in its commercial organization to address execution challenges and prepare for potential regulatory approvals in 2027. Despite the quarterly performance, management indicated that underlying clinician interest remains strong as the company advances its clinical programs and product pipeline.
