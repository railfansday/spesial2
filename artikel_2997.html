Dell Technologies: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Proprietary storage keeps gaining share, and Dell screens every AI order before production, a discipline the next few quarters will test.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 25, 2026 at 12:09AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Dell Technologies ›

On its third-quarter fiscal 2027 earnings call, held on Nov. 24, 2026, Dell Technologies (DELL -11.83%) announced record quarterly revenue of $54.9 billion. For long-term investors, the expansion of enterprise hardware operations into high-margin server and storage systems signals structural profit growth. Three key takeaways from the call stand out.

Internal efficiency efforts continue to reshape profitability across the business. Lower operational overhead allows the company to absorb lower-margin server shipments without sacrificing overall profitability.

Rapid order growth in hardware can create customer cancellation risk if funding dries up. Dell actively screens every order in its backlog, or unfulfilled customer orders, for financing, power availability, and deployment site readiness before entering production.

High-margin Dell proprietary intellectual property, or IP, storage products continue to gain market share. Shifting sales toward proprietary storage expands margins inside the Infrastructure Solutions Group, the company's enterprise server and storage division.

Customer demand for computing power outstrips component supply. Custom engineering and global supply chain scale help Dell capture the expanding total addressable market, or total customer revenue opportunity for its products.

Tightly managing component constraints while expanding server capacity positions the business to finish the fiscal year.
