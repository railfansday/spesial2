Personal Consumer Issues • Re: Driveway basketball hoop

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

I had one for myself for a number of years. I play senior ball, so I need to practice. I thought I was going to spend the money and get a fancy, in-ground one, but then a friend offered me a cheap one for free (and even set it up for me), and…it was absolutely fine. I had no need for anything fancier. I just leveled it with leftover tiles, filled the base with water, and got on with my life. When we moved, we left it, and the family’s kids are either using it, or not. I play at the park down the street now.

Like someone else said, do pay attention to placement. The direction of the sun, where the ball is going to go when you miss badly. The guy who worked nights wasn’t super thrilled when I hit the side of his house with the ball several times one morning.
