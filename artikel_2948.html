Why PDD Holdings Stock Jumped Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Shares of the Pinduoduo parent gained on earnings even as revenue was flat.

By Jeremy Bowman – Nov 19, 2026 at 2:53PM EST

10 stocks we like better than PDD Holdings ›

Shares of PDD Holdings (PDD +11.00%), the parent of Chinese e-commerce companies Pinduoduo and Temu, were soaring today after it delivered better-than-expected results in its third-quarter earnings report on Thursday.

The stock was up 11.3% as of 2:06 p.m. ET.

Revenue in the quarter was flat at $16.2 billion, but that actually missed estimates at $17.3 billion.

Transaction revenues fell 12% to $7.2 billion, while revenue from online marketing, including search advertising, rose 12% to $8.9 billion, showing it's doing a better job at monetizing its platform, even as sales are falling.

PDD also cut costs in the quarter, with sales and marketing expenses falling 17% to $3.8 billion and general and administrative expenses down 11% to $236 million, helping boost its operating profit by 25% to $4.6 billion.

The company earned less investment income and faced higher taxes, and as a result, adjusted earnings per share were flat at $3.14, though that topped estimates at $2.71.

CEO Jiazhen Zhao said, "During the past quarter, we continued to deepen our support for the merchant ecosystem, helping merchants develop better products and reach new consumers. We see significant potential to build on this progress and will continue investing in the tools and services that help our merchants grow."

Earlier in its history, PDD regularly reported triple-digit growth, driven by PDD's social commerce design and then Temu's rapid international growth.

However, more recently, the company has struggled as Temu's user base has shrunk due in part to weak consumer sentiment at home and tighter regulations abroad.

While the profit growth through cost-cutting was encouraging, the company will need to return to growth for the stock to keep gaining.
