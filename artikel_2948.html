Baha Mar Championship Tournament: Game #2 – Seton Hall Defeats the Wolfpack 87-81

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

This was going to be a game of Seton Hall's defense (KP ranked #31) versus the Wolfpack's offense (KP ranked #29), their strength versus our strength. Defense won.

The first half was a tight battle with the pace much more in the Pirates' favor than the Pack. The largest Wolfpack lead was 6. The Pack was cold from the arc, 2-10 but happily The Hall was even worse 1-8. Edmead got his second foul early resulting in Adams playing point more than expected. He matched his 5 assists with 5 of the Pack's 11 turnovers. McNeil was 0-3 from the floor and Lubin was 1-5. Hammond led the Wolfpack scoring with 9. Seton Hall up by one at the half, 30-29.

In the second half, Ven Lubin came alive and did not miss a shot, 7-7 from the field and 9-9 at the line. He scored 23 points to keep the Pack in the game. No other player on the Wolfpack made more than 2 baskets. With 7 seconds left, Lubin hit two free throws to tie the game and send it to overtime 73-73.

The overtime was tough to watch. Seton Hall did not miss a shot or a free throw. The Wolfpack managed to make only one field goal and six free throws. They missed 6 3PT attempts. Final score, 87-81.

The Pack could not get the 3PT shot to fall (4-22), and without it struggled to match Seton Hall's scoring.

Seton Hall shot better than NC State from both the field and the 3PT line

NC State did match The Hall in rebounding, 29-29

The Wolfpack shot 92% from the free throw line

Ven Lubin hit his career high, leading the team with 27 points and also led the team in rebounding with 8

Kyle Evans tweaked his knee and was limited in the second half
