Themis Intelligence Introduces Technology, Collaborative Ecosystem for Integrating Trusted AI into Utility Operations

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Official Themis launch convened government, technology, utility leaders to discuss the power and responsibility of trusted, Human-Guided Intelligence in mission-critical utility environments

MISSISSAUGA, ON, Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Themis Intelligence, a Canadian AI company wholly owned by Survalent, held its official launch on November 5, 2026, at Survalent's headquarters in Mississauga, Ontario. The exclusive event brought together senior leaders from utilities, government, technology, AI, and critical infrastructure for an evening of knowledge sharing and discussion around responsible AI usage in critical infrastructure.

Responsibility and human decision making are cornerstone principles of Themis Intelligence, a technology vendor established to bring trusted, explainable AI to utility operations. Themis Human-Guided Intelligence (HGI) solutions combine utility knowledge, operational data, and human expertise. With a mission to enable safer, faster, and better-informed decisions while preserving human authority, accountability, and judgment, Themis helps advance the future of utility operations

Building on a journey that began in 2021 and the commercial release of its first agent in 2025, Themis is bringing an expanding portfolio of HGI solutions to market. Shaped through years of development and practical evaluation with utility customers, these solutions span forecasting, operational intelligence, utility knowledge, and grid and asset insights. Over the coming months, Themis will introduce additional solutions that help utilities make more informed decisions across increasingly complex grid operations.

"With utilities, AI can't just be powerful, it must be trustworthy. Our responsibility is to demonstrate what's possible and earn that trust, one practical application, one result at a time," said Young Ngo, President of Themis Intelligence and CTO of Survalent Technology. "Our utility partners help us understand what needs to be built, and our technology partners help us build it responsibly, effectively, and efficiently."

The launch introduced the broader ecosystem Themis is convening to advance trusted AI for utilities, bringing together utility executives, technology and AI innovators, governance specialists, and government stakeholders to build a collaborative model for integrating trusted AI into mission-critical utility environments. Themis' technology platform is engineered AWS-first, combining cloud infrastructure with purpose-built Human-Guided Intelligence to deliver trusted AI for mission-critical utility operations. Its architecture is designed with enterprise-grade security, resilience, and scalability to support utilities as their operational intelligence requirements evolve.

Dawn Gallagher Murphy, Parliamentary Assistant to the Minister of Artificial Intelligence Adoption, delivered the keynote address, underscoring the strategic importance of applying AI responsibly in critical infrastructure environments.

Jeff Mocha, Chief Operations and Electrification Officer of Oakville Hydro and Amy Grice, CTO of Peninsula Light Company shared utility perspectives and strategies for AI adoption and discussed the critical role of human-guided AI in utility operations. Underscoring the importance of the ecosystem, Alastair Taylor, Chief Executive Officer of Invitas; Noor Hassan, Worldwide Partner Solutions Architect of Amazon Web Services; and Shabnam Haghzare, National Artificial Intelligence Lead of Mitacs, provided valuable insights into the future of AI and the importance of a thoughtful approach to AI rollout in mission-critical operations.

"Themis Intelligence represents an important step for utility technology. With access to Survalent's community of 800+ utilities in 40 countries and a strong and growing partner ecosystem, Themis has a solid foundation from which to introduce Human-Guided Intelligence to the international utility community," said Steve Mueller, President and CEO of Survalent Technology and CEO of Themis Intelligence.

To learn more about Themis Intelligence, visit www.themisintelligence.ai.

About Themis IntelligenceThemis Intelligence, a wholly owned subsidiary of Survalent Technology, is advancing the future of utility operations with Human-Guided Intelligence, designed specifically for the power sector. Leveraging Survalent's trusted operational technologies foundation, Themis structures, contextualizes, and grounds OT and IT data into decision-relevant operational intelligence. Themis supports utilities as they evolve into Distribution System Operators (DSOs) and navigate the challenges of an increasingly decentralized grid.

Trust is achieved through governance, grounding, and traceability, not by assuming a model is "right." Themis delivers solutions purpose-built for utility operations and planning, spanning decision support, forecasting, and governed knowledge management.
