Compare two MLflow runs without leaving PyCharm: the Compare tab in ML Experiment Cockpit

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Most MLflow workflows turn into tab-switching. To decide whether a new hyper-parameter helped, you open the tracking UI, read a metric, go back, open the other run, and compare from memory. The comparison is the part that takes the longest and is the easiest to get wrong.

ML Experiment Cockpit is a read-only JetBrains plugin that puts your MLflow experiments, Optuna trials, registered models and Weights & Biases sweeps in a tool window inside PyCharm, IntelliJ IDEA, WebStorm, GoLand and CLion.

The Compare tab answers the question "which run was actually better?".

Right-click a run in the Experiments tree, pick "Compare with…", and choose the second run. The two runs are shown side by side:

The comparison can be exported as Markdown, which saves rebuilding the table by hand for a ticket, a pull request description or a message to your team.

One thing to be clear about: Compare is the Pro feature. Pro comes with a 30-day free trial, and the pricing page of the plugin shows the current price (local VAT applies depending on your country). Without a Pro licence the tab stays visible but locked, and the four other tabs are completely unchanged.
