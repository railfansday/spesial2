Here’s what happened in crypto today

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Need to know what happened in crypto today? Here is the latest news on daily trends and events impacting Bitcoin price, blockchain, DeFi, Web3 and crypto regulation.

Today in crypto: Zcash developers are seeking another reduction in block times to speed up transaction confirmations, just days after the NU7 upgrade cut the interval from 75 seconds to 25 seconds. Binance France is closing up shop in the country, as all accounts will be closed on Dec. 31, while the US Treasury imposed financial sanctions on eight Canadian money-service companies it claims have done business with sanctioned ‌Iranian entities.

Zcash developers have proposed reducing the blockchain’s target block interval from 25 seconds to 20 seconds, seeking faster transaction confirmations days after activating the NU7 upgrade.

On Sunday, developers ValarDragon and Evan Forbes submitted a draft Zcash Improvement Proposal (ZIP) on GitHub, saying that they want the change to be included in the next community poll and considered for the upcoming NU7.1 network upgrade.

The reduction would shorten the expected wait for initial transaction confirmations. According to the proposal, this would improve the user experience for point-of-sale payments, exchange deposits and cross-chain swaps.

The developers said the change would not increase the network’s overall daily capacity for private transactions. The proposal would also preserve Zcash’s existing issuance schedule, despite blocks being produced more frequently.

The proposal comes just days after Zcash’s NU7 upgrade went live. On Friday, the NU7 upgrade was activated on the mainnet, cutting the network’s target block interval from 75 seconds to 25 seconds.

The upgrade also introduced a Network Sustainability Mechanism designed to recycle a portion of transaction fees into future mining rewards while preserving Zcash’s 21 million ZEC supply cap.

Binance France is closing up shop in the country. Customers have been notified by email during early November, according to media reports.

All accounts will be closed on Dec. 31, and any assets left on the exchange will be charged monthly fees starting on Nov. 29, Cryptoast, Crypto Briefing and other media reported on Saturday, citing emails sent to users by Binance.

On that date, accounts still maintaining a balance will pay a monthly maintenance fee of 0.025% of the balance, or a minimum of 10 USDC, a stablecoin tracking the US dollar.

To avoid fees, withdrawals must be completed by Nov. 29, the exchange said.

The US Treasury imposed financial sanctions on eight Canadian money-service companies it claims have done business with sanctioned ‌Iranian entities.

These companies facilitated the exchange of Iranian rials and other currencies for digital assets, enabling international transactions, a US Treasury Department statement said on Friday.

The sanctions, which serve to isolate the companies from the dollar-based financial system, are the latest in a series of Treasury moves against firms outside the US.

These businesses have facilitated transactions with sanctioned Iranian digital asset exchanges, as well as with digital asset addresses associated with Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC), according to the Treasury.
