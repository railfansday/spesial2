SMU's Rhett Lashlee Facing Delicate Roster Decision Ahead Of Stanford Season Finale

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The SMU Mustangs will travel to take on Stanford for their regular season finale as Rhett Lashlee's team aims to end what was a once-promising year with a win.

After back-to-back defeats to Wake Forest and Notre Dame, the Mustangs won't be playing for anything substantial again, with many experts predicting they'll play in the TaxSlayer Gator Bowl against Florida.

So that brings us to Saturday, when the Mustangs play their final regular-season game against a resurgent Stanford team.

Will Lashlee go all out to try and win a game that doesn't have much riding on it, or will he rotate the roster and allow some players who haven't seen the field much in 2026 a chance to play and show what they are made of?

"If the opportunity is there, yes," Lashlee said. "The number one thing we want to do is win. We want to win, and there's plenty of young guys that have gotten more and more opportunities as the year's gone on. But this is an opportunity for us to win.

"We don't have a lot of experience with back-to-back losses here. It's not acceptable. It's not what we have become accustomed to. So, that's the biggest thing. We want to go win. Whatever that looks like, if that's play a bunch of young guys, sure. If it's play a bunch of old guys or somewhere in the middle, probably is what it's going to be. But we just got to win."

Don't expect the starters to play a full four quarters in this one, which means players we haven't seen much of this season might get some snaps.

Stanford is on a roll right now, having won four of their last five games and scored 30+ in those four wins, so the Mustangs' defense, which has struggled to keep things tight over the past couple of weeks, might have its hands full.

Tavita Pritchard's team has nothing to lose, which is another element we have to think about here in terms of which players Lashlee sends out, because they can throw caution to the wind, because there's nothing on the line. You don't want your young players to get torched.

But winning is a good habit to be in, and if the Mustangs can halt their two-game skid to end the year, at least that is something positive ahead of a Bowl game in the coming weeks.
