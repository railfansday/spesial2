Question of the Day: How many wins will it take to win the NFC North?

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Chicago Bears sit at 7-2, but unfortunately, the Detroit Lions and Minnesota Vikings also sit at 7-2, making it a 3-way tie for first. The Bears currently are losing the tiebreakers to both Detroit and Minnesota, so the Bears technically sit in 3rd place in the division, with the 6-4 Packers in last.

The North is a gauntlet and a half and with 6 divisional games left to be played (4 featuring the Bears), it's going to be the divisional games that are truly going to decide who is going to win the crown and have home field advantage in the playoffs through at least the first two rounds of the playoffs.

I think for the Bears, it's going to come down to which teams they beat down the stretch. With 8 games to go, however unlikely, if the Bears win their 4 games out of the division and go 1-3 in the games in the division, that's going to put them at 12 wins, but they will probably not have the tiebreaker against any of the teams in the division if that happens, which means that won't be enough. But if the Bears go 2-2 outside of the division and 3-1 in the divisional games, that's also a 12-5 record, but they would be in much better shape in the tiebreaker situation.

Answer. With the 0-2 divisional record, even if the Bears went 3-1 in the division and finished with 12 wins, they probably wouldn't have the tiebreaker against everyone. If the 1 loss comes to the Packers or Vikings, that team will have the tiebreaker. If Minnesota wins 1 more divisional game, they'll most likely have the tiebreaker against the Bears. It gets tricky for Chicago and tiebreakers within the division. Unless the Bears go 4-0 in divisional games down the stretch. However, if they do that, with New Orleans and Miami on the schedule, the Bears would be looking at, most likely, at least 6 more wins, giving them 13.

13 is the number. With 3 teams at 7-2, and with how good all four teams are, I just think it's more likely than not that one of them reaches the 13-win total. I find it hard to believe that two of them will, given they all have divisional games remaining against each other. I think one of these teams finds a way to 13, and that's the number that wins the North. I think if the Bears or any of the other teams reach 12 wins, that will guarantee them a wild-card playoff spot. If any of them only get to 11 wins, I think that leaves them vulnerable to missing the playoffs. An 11-6 team missing the playoffs would be wild, but if the last-place team in the North finishes 11-6, and the Seahawks or Rams can get to 11-6, that means one of them is going to be sitting home in January with 11 wins but missing the playoffs.

What do you say, Bears fans? Do you think they need to win 13 games to win the division, or do you think a smaller number can get it done? Or do they need to reach an even bigger total?
