Cowboys DC Christian Parker calls losing play-calling duties 'not an ideal situation,' says he'll 'cross that bridge' on future with team

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

A day after Dallas Cowboys defensive coordinator Christian Parker was stripped of defensive play-calling duties, the young coordinator spoke to reporters about what he called a "significant" personal disappointment. In a press conference on Thursday, Parker said he initially pushed back on losing the responsibility but accepted that the decision had been made.

"Not an ideal situation for me personally, but it's what's best for the team," Parker said, via multiple reporters.

Parker told reporters that the new arrangement with senior defensive assistant Mike Pettine, who will now take on the play-calling, is "only awkward if you make it that way." The young defensive coordinator added that he considered not staying with Dallas, but decided to stay on.

"In my opinion, that would have been running from it," Parker said of leaving.

However, when asked about his future with the Cowboys, Parker left more up in the air: "We'll cross that bridge when we come to it," he said.

Parker, who is in his first year as the Cowboys' DC and his first year as an NFL play-caller in general, saw his short tenure calling the shots come to an end on Wednesday after the team's early struggles. Play-calling duties for the Dallas defense has gone to Pettine, who was brought on as a senior defensive assistant last month to give the 34-year-old Parker some support.

The move came after the latest dismal showing from the Cowboys' defense, a 46-40 loss to the San Francisco 49ers where Dallas allowed 584 offensive yards — the most the team has given up at home in the franchise's long and storied history.

Postgame, Cowboys head coach Brian Schottenheimer ripped the defense's effort as "atrocious", the latest sign of Schottenheimer's frustration with his young DC. Owner Jerry Jones said Tuesday that he was discussing Parker's role after the performance, before the team ultimately settled on keeping him in the position but reducing his duties.

Part of the problem is that Sunday's loss was just the latest winnable game ruined by poor defense. The Cowboys started the season by failing to get a fourth-quarter stop in a loss to the New York Giants, which Parker admitted was "unacceptable." Dallas then allowed the Baltimore Ravens to get a seven-second game-winning drive in Week 3, before a defensive collapse in Week 8 led the Arizona Cardinals to score four touchdowns in a row to eventually win in overtime. In Week 9, the Cowboys blew another big lead against the Indianapolis Colts and only narrowly avoided disaster.

Schottenheimer and Jones will now hope that Pettine's guidance can salvage the season, with Dallas currently holding a 4-6 record.
