Give Claude live access to UK Companies House, government tenders and more with MCP

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

LLMs are great at reasoning about companies and markets, and terrible at knowing what happened at Companies House this morning. The fix is the Model Context Protocol (MCP): give the model tools that fetch live, official data, and let it decide when to call them.

This post shows how to connect seven business-data tools to your AI assistant in about two minutes, with no code and nothing to install.

Disclosure: I built these. They run as Apify Actors, all on official APIs and open data, with personal data stripped.

Only want one or two tools? Trim the tools= list. Fewer tools also means less context used.

The model picks the tool, fills in the input, waits for the run and reads back the dataset, so you can chain questions ("now check which of those companies have overdue accounts").

Each tool call runs on your Apify account and is billed per result, from $1.50 to $5 per 1,000 rows. A typical question costs well under a cent, and Apify's free plan includes monthly credit.

Setup details and the full tool list are on GitHub.
