Having spent nearly a month in theaters, a sleeper hit fantasy romance film starring Olivia Colman and Alexander Skarsgård is all set to begin a new chapter in its journey at home. This week, the movie will be released on the PVOD market, along with Aaron Sorkin's box-office underperformer The Social Reckoning. No one would have seen this coming, but the fantasy romance movie has outperformed The Social Reckoning by a wide margin, having nearly quadrupled its production budget in theaters. The movie will no doubt have a solid run at home, given its positive reviews and stellar audience reception.

That said, the movie's box-office run appears to be drawing to a close. It has witnessed a significant drop in revenue over the last couple of weeks, as a result of a fast-declining theater count. In its fifth week of release, with a digital debut approaching, the movie is expected to see further declines in both revenue and its theatrical footprint. Also featuring Elizabeth Debicki and Peter Dinklage, the film has emerged as the first major box-office hit distributed domestically by Black Bear Pictures.

The firm had previously released the Jason Statham-led action movie Shelter and Guy Ritchie's costly misfire In the Grey this year. Shelter grossed around $13 million domestically, while In the Grey emerged as Ritchie's lowest-grossing film in over two decades, with a final haul of under $6 million.

Black Bear's new movie, the fantasy romance starring Colman and Skarsgård, is Wicker. Directed by Alex Huston Fischer and Eleanor Wilson, the film holds a "Certified Fresh" 94% critics' score and a "Verified Hot" 92% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes. The aggregator website's consensus reads, "Wicker is a charming fable that strikes the right balance between naughty and nice, drawing much of its appeal from deftly deployed salty humor, seamless creature effects, and Olivia Colman's eminently lovable performance."

The movie exceeded box-office expectations in its opening weekend of release and showed solid resilience until its theater count began to fall by week three. Its global haul now stands at nearly $40 million against a reported budget of $10 million. At its peak, Wicker was playing in 2,374 locations nationwide. But it lost more than 1,200 theaters over the last couple of weeks. By the time it enters its fifth week in theaters, it will have lost more than 1,500 locations overall. The movie is expected to debut on digital platforms on November 24, alongside The Social Reckoning and A24's sensational You Can See Everything. Stay tuned to Collider for more updates.
