Tom Hardy’s 20-Part Gangster Masterpiece Is Officially The Perfect Binge This Weekend

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Tom Hardy's 20-part gangster masterpiece is officially the perfect weekend binge, with two complete seasons of gripping crime drama now streaming on Paramount+. This series is currently the #2 most-watched on Paramount+ in the world heading into the holiday weekend and has been one of the most consistently popular series on the platform throughout all of Fall 2026. Led by Hardy, Pierce Brosnan, Helen Mirren, and one of the best casts on any modern streaming series, there has never been a better time to binge this crime drama than now.

With MobLand season 2 coming to an end last week, it's officially the best show on Paramount+ to binge from start to finish this weekend. Tom Hardy gives one of his career-best performances in the series, which is deeply entrenched in calculated gang warfare across the UK and features fantastic action, tension, and character development throughout. The newest season of MobLand has featured some of the highest-rated episodes in the show's history, and it has already been renewed for a third season, making it well worth the investment.

Tom Hardy leads the exceptional cast in MobLand, which is arguably the very best crime series available to stream on Paramount+. Rather than being the head of the main crime family, the Harrigans, that the series chronicles, Hardy plays their key fixer and enforcer, Harry Da Souza, who is constantly tasked with maintaining order within the family while also doing their bidding, all with the intention of maintaining the Harrigans' position as the most feared criminal organization in London.

Harry carries out tasks primarily for Brosnan's Conrad but also works closely with the eldest son in the family, Kevin (Paddy Considine), while also tending to the needs of the hot-headed Maeve (Mirren), Conrad's wife and the matriarch of the Harrigan family. Conrad and Maeve are often on the same page in MobLand, but as pressures and threats rise from a variety of sources in MobLand season 2, a new complex power structure forms within the Harrigans that complicates the established order.

The rich character development in MobLand that also rests on delicate power dynamics is one of the most fascinating sources of tension throughout the series and is a significant reason why the crime drama exists in such an elite category. As with any gangster series, there is plenty of action, strategy, and standoffs that captivate viewers and make it hard to look away, but the primary emotional core is told through the lens of Harry and his immediate family. With so many layers of drama and intrigue, MobLand operates on a prestigious level of its own.

This weekend is the perfect opportunity to binge all 20 episodes of MobLand following its explosive season 2 finale, which just landed on Paramount+ on Friday, November 20. One of the most exciting aspects of MobLand season 2 is its increase in scale and stakes, both of which were expanded on thanks to new cast member Johnny Flynn, who plays the maniacal villain and fierce rival to the entire Harrigan family, Frankie Cooper. The multitude of showdowns between Harry and Frankie throughout MobLand season 2 become a thrilling vehicle for most of the action and suspense in the latest season of the gangster series.

All 32 episodes of Netflix's gangster thriller masterpiece deliver gripping crime drama, unforgettable characters, and relentless suspense.

Critics contend that MobLand season 2 is a notable improvement on the already acclaimed debut season and helped establish the series as a bona fide gangster franchise. ScreenRant gave MobLand season 2 9/10 stars, calling it "an electrifying triumph that pulls no punches from start to finish." If you enjoy well-crafted gangster series such as The Sopranos, Boardwalk Empire, and Top Boy, the latter of which was created by MobLand creator Ronan Bennett, then consider Tom Hardy's MobLand the best new show to binge this weekend not only on Paramount+, but on any major streaming platform.
