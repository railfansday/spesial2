7 Upcoming Prime Video Movies, Ranked by Hype

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

2026 has been a strong year for Prime Video in the TV section, but the movies this year have been pretty all over the place. The streaming service started the year strong with the buddy action comedy The Wrecking Crew, and has since released a steady cadence of movies that largely missed the mark with critics but fared well with audiences. Some of the more acclaimed releases this year include the romance films The Love Hypothesis and Love of Your Life. The rest have mostly been forgettable, but that might be about to change.

In the final months of 2026, Prime Video is preparing to release some of its hottest new movies of the year, including a star-studded sports movie and some hilarious holiday films. And that’s not all! The streaming service has some rather exciting titles lined up for 2027 and beyond as well that are worth putting on your watchlist. So, without further ado, here’s a look at the biggest upcoming Prime Video movies, ranked by hype.

A French Prime Video original film, Storm is directed by Edouard Salier and written by Matthieu Rumani and Nicolas Slomka, who previously created the French Prime Video series Culte. Set on the night of December 25, 1999, when the storm of the century caused massive devastation across France, the movie follows two teenagers, a blind girl named Chloé (Clara Pacini) and popular boy Erwan (Bach Buquen), as they find themselves caught in the torrential floods and depend on each other to survive. Other cast members include Roxane Mesquida, Félix Moati, and Artemisia Toussaint.

One part coming-of-age drama and one part disaster movie, Storm is set to be Prime Video’s first new original movie of 2027, and it sounds like an interesting watch for disaster movie fans. The film has built some anticipation online so far, but it’s not the most hotly anticipated upcoming movie of the bunch.

A Spanish-language thriller film, Perfect Liars is directed by Jesús Colmenar, who also co-wrote the movie with Ángela Armero — an adaptation of Alex Mírez's eponymous Wattpad novel. A revenge thriller set at an elite university, the movie follows Jude Derry (Irene Balmes) as she infiltrates the inner circle of the popular, wealthy, and charismatic Cash brothers, hoping to uncover the truth about her brother’s death, only to find herself at the center of a complex web of seduction and deceit. The film also stars Gleb Abrosimov, Pau Simon, Pablo Scapigliati, Asier Etxeandia, and Irene Ferreiro in notable roles.

Somewhat reminiscent of the Netflix teen drama show Elite, Perfect Liars promises to be a captivating mystery thriller with plenty of spice, which is exactly what the novel’s millions of fans would want. With a sleek, tense atmosphere, the show looks like it could prove to be quite an addictive watch, and with Money Heist alum Jesús Colmenar behind the camera, expectations are high for Prime Video’s newest Spanish Original. Overall, Perfect Liars looks perfectly poised to join Prime Video’s collection of YA thriller dramas as an exciting new addition for genre fans.

Directed by Carlson Young, who also directed the 2026 romantic drama The Last Sunrise, Clashing Through the Snow is an upcoming Christmas comedy written by Daniel Mackey and Rebecca Ewing. The film stars Christopher Briney and Michelle Randolph as Ryan and Hannah, rival publishing editors whose jobs are on the line unless they can land a lucrative deal. When a severe snowstorm grounds all flights, the two rivals are forced to team up for a cross-country road trip. Lukas Gage, Paris Hilton, Julia Fox, and PJ Byrne star in supporting roles.

Clashing Through the Snow may not be Prime Video’s biggest holiday movie, but it’s certainly generated a lot of buzz online, especially with the release of its trailer on November 10, featuring Olivia Rodrigo’s first-ever Christmas single, "Take Me Home (4 Christmas)." The ensemble cast is also quite promising, with Paris Hilton notably playing a fictionalized version of herself. Everything we’ve seen of the movie so far suggests a classic holiday rom-com that could be perfect for a cozy winter night.

Directed by Adam Shankman and written by Allan Rice, The Man with the Bag is an upcoming Christmas action comedy starring Arnold Schwarzenegger as Santa Claus. After disgruntled elf Nadia Nyce (Awkwafina) steals his sack of presents, Santa is forced to seek the help of criminal Vance (Alan Ritchson) to pull off a dangerous heist to save Christmas. The film also features Liza Koshy, Kyle Mooney, Adrian Martinez, Jane Krakowski, and Ken Jeong in notable roles.

The Man with the Bag is easily one of Prime Video’s most anticipated holiday releases of 2026, and that’s largely thanks to the sheer hilarity of its set-up. From the trailer, Arnold Schwarzenegger’s Santa looks like the perfect blend of cheesiness and charm, and it’s certainly exciting to see the iconic actor in a Christmas comedy again, 30 years after his 1996 cult classic Jingle All the Way. With the added star power of the ensemble supporting cast and a heist movie narrative, The Man with the Bag certainly deserves a place on everyone’s holiday watchlist.

Directed and co-produced by David O. Russell, Madden is a sports biopic chronicling the life and career of American football coach and commentator John Madden. The biographical sports drama stars Nicolas Cage as the titular legend and explores Madden’s journey from a Super Bowl-winning partnership with Al Davis and the Oakland Raiders to becoming one of the sport’s biggest icons. Besides Cage, the movie also stars Christian Bale, Kathryn Hahn, John Mulaney, Sienna Miller, and Shane Gillis as other real-life personalities.

Madden is easily one of the most exciting sports biopics of the year, and with the stacked cast involved, it’s no wonder the movie is one of Prime Video’s most anticipated releases of November 2026. Officially described as “a celebration of a brilliant mind whose once-in-a-century spirit and personality, partnership with his daring wife Virginia, lifelong friendships, and infectious love of people and football transcended the world of sports,” the film promises a journey through Madden’s legendary career both on and off the field, from his record of never having lost a season as coach to his multiple Emmy-winning career as a commentator.

Directed by Jamie Babbit, Red, White & Royal Wedding is the upcoming sequel to the 2023 rom-com Red, White & Royal Blue, which was based on Casey McQuiston’s 2019 novel. The first movie followed the love story between Alex Claremont-Diaz (Taylor Zakhar Perez), the first son of the United States, and Prince Henry of England (Nicholas Galitzine). As the title suggests, the sequel will bring their story to its next chapter. Uma Thurman, Sarah Shahi, Rachel Hilson, Clifton Collins Jr., and Stephen Fry have been announced to reprise their roles from the first movie, and the film adds Henry Ashton, Alex Høgh Andersen, Lena Headey, and Chloe Fineman to the ensemble.

First, a bit of necessary clarification: despite the title suggesting a historic gay royal wedding, the movie will actually revolve around the wedding of Henry’s younger sister, Princess Beatrice, and all the chaos that it unleashes. Even though we don't have a release date or much concrete info about the upcoming movie, it certainly ranks among Prime Video’s most buzzworthy projects currently in the pipeline.

After the massive success of The Summer I Turned Pretty, it was pretty much inevitable that Prime Video would want to extend the franchise beyond the hit series. And that expansion is going to come in the form of an as-yet-untitled upcoming film directed and co-written by Jenny Han, who also wrote the original novel trilogy and created the series adaptation. The new romantic drama will, of course, see Lola Tung, Christopher Briney, and Gavin Casalegno reprise their roles from the series.

The Summer I Turned Pretty has easily been Jenny Han’s biggest success yet, and that’s saying something considering Han’s also the author of the hit To All the Boys series. The upcoming follow-up film has a lot of fans very excited, and the movie is easily one of Prime Video’s most anticipated upcoming projects. While we don’t have an official title or release date yet, the film wrapped filming in July 2026, so it shouldn’t be long before we see this eagerly awaited romance drama arrive on the streaming service.
