Joni Mitchell Has Some News

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

In a collection of political paintings, the famed musician and artist lambastes MAGA, the Epstein class, and Trump's attacks on immigrants.

In her song “A Case of You,” Joni Mitchell described herself as “a lonely painter / I live in a box of paints.” She has never been a particularly shy one. The famously elusive artist, who has pronounced painting her "first love" before music, makes plain statements in NEWS, a collection of political paintings.

Conceived and designed by Mitchell, the book unfurls a scathing rebuke of recent Republican administrations in the United States. Twenty-three paintings are presented chronologically, with a handful created between 2001 and 2004 and the majority made in the last 18 months. Works from the Bush years show the Twin Towers aflame with a sinister face in the smoke or a bare-chested blonde woman who sits astride Osama Bin Laden’s shoulders.

More recent works feature Adolf Hitler with the letters "MGGA" ("Make Germany Great Again"), former DHS Secretary Kristi Noem on horseback labeled “grifter,” and a triptych of Jeffrey Epstein, Ghislaine Maxwell, and President Trump as disgraced royalty playing cards. A spread of all the works becomes a Thunderdome-like arena of modern US politics — ending with a self-portrait of Mitchell receiving a Kennedy Center Honor. As if the answer to all that chaos is elevating impactful art.
