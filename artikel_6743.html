All About the ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Holiday Special—and How to Tune In

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Feeling woeful about Dancing With the Stars Season 35 being over? Well, we’re not quite done. The show is returning for its annual holiday special, and we have all the details on what to expect, where to watch, and whether this thing is live.

Here’s the logline from ABC: “The ballroom comes alive with holiday magic as ‘Dancing with the Stars’ celebrates the season with 12 all-new festive-themed routines, dazzling holiday decor and heartwarming moments with the cast.”

And judging from photos, it looks like all the pros and several celebs from Season 35 will be in attendance. Plus we’re getting a performance from host Julianne Hough!

The special airs during Dancing With the Stars’ usual Tuesday night time slot. In other words, tune in at 8 p.m. ET on Disney+ and ABC!

Nope! The special was prerecorded. Which we can confirm since…ya know…photos are already available.

That would be The Wonderful World of Disney: Holiday Spectacular, which has a Dancing With the Stars link due to being hosted by Derek Hough. Plus, there’s a performance of “It’s the Most Wonderful Time of the Year” featuring the show’s pros and troupe dancers. This special airs Monday, November 30, on ABC at 8 p.m. ET, not to be confused with the DWTS special the next night!
