Commanders’ Jacory Croskey-Merritt ‘expected’ to return from injury for Bengals matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Commanders' Jacory Croskey-Merritt 'expected' to return from injury for Bengals matchup appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Washington Commanders could get a hopeful name in their backfield Monday night, with Jacory Croskey-Merritt expected to return against the Cincinnati Bengals after missing four games with a groin injury.

CBS Sports reporter Matt Zenitz reported that Croskey-Merritt is currently expected to play Monday. The running back has been sidelined since landing on injured reserve but recently had his 21-day practice window opened as Washington evaluated his recovery.

Croskey-Merritt was a full participant in practice throughout the week and received a questionable designation for the matchup. Washington will still need to activate him from injured reserve before he can take the field.

The 25-year-old had a promising start to the season before his injury. Through five games, he recorded 239 rushing yards and two touchdowns on 67 carries, giving Washington another option in its ground game.

Head coach Dan Quinn also offered an encouraging assessment of Croskey-Merritt's progress earlier in the week, saying the running back had a "great week" as he worked toward returning.

His potential availability comes at an important time for Washington, which is also monitoring Rachaad White's health. White was added to the injury report Friday with a heel issue and did not practice Saturday. He is also listed as questionable for Monday's game.

If both running backs are available, Washington could split the workload between them. However, Croskey-Merritt's return would provide additional flexibility for the Commanders as they prepare for the Bengals.

The Commanders will need to make their final roster decisions before Monday night's kickoff, but Croskey-Merritt's full week of practice offers encouraging signs that he could finally return to the field.
