Giants target Jonathan Taylor talks trade speculation as deadline nears: 'At this point, it just becomes noise'

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

As the NFL trade deadline inches closer, Colts running back Jonathan Taylor is looking forward to being on the other side of it, no matter what happens.

Taylor, who is at the top of the rumor mill ahead of the Nov. 10 deadline, spoke with the media following Thursday's practice and was asked about his feelings regarding his name being thrown around in speculation.

"It's been dragging for a while now," Taylor said of the rumors. "At this point, it just becomes noise."

"No one wants uncertainty," Taylor added. "Until the past couple weeks, it's kind of been the same thing. But it won't be until Tuesday where you'll know exactly what's going on, you'll know exactly what the verdict is."

The Giants are one of a few teams interested in the Pro Bowl back, but as of Thursday, no trade has been agreed upon.

SNY's Connor Hughes reports that various NFL sources are mixed when it comes to whether the Colts will actually trade Taylor. Taylor is under contract past this season, making Indianapolis' asking price so steep that SNY sources believe the Colts aren't actually interested in making a deal.

Whether that turns out to be true is anyone's guess. With less than a week before the deadline, the Colts and Taylor are handling Sunday's game against the Cowboys as any other week. That doesn't mean Taylor can stay away from the speculation and rumors, but the 27-year-old is taking it all in stride.

"You can't avoid it," Taylor said. "Internet, all of that stuff. You can't avoid it. But it's more about trying to block that out. Let's say you're getting online. Whatever you're getting online to do, go get it done. And whether you have some type of marketing obligation, get it done, and then you're off of it. But you're not going to avoid it, not until it's all over."

Taylor confirmed he hasn't requested a trade and is in lockstep with Colts head coach Shane Steichen about his availability, preparing to play despite the risk of injury.

"At this point, the hay is in the barn now. We're preparing for the Dallas Cowboys," Taylor said. "After last week, nothing else matters. We have to give these fans a win. We have to put out a great premium product."
