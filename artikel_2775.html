Eagles 2027 first-round pick tracker

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Philadelphia Eagles currently have two first-round picks in the 2027 NFL Draft: their own and the Jacksonville Jaguars' via the Jordan Davis trade. As we've done here at Bleeding Green Nation in the past when the Birds held multiple first-round picks, we're going to log weekly updates on the status of their selections.

For non-playoff teams, since the postseason will determine the order for the teams in the dance.

WEEK 10: The Eagles entered their bye with a 2-7 record after dropping seven games in a row. The Eagles are 4.5-point underdogs to the Pittsburgh Steelers in Week 11, so, the losing is expected to continue.

WEEK 10: The Jags beat the lowly Tennessee Titans to advance to 7-2 on the season. They also got big-time help from the Houston Texans blowing a 17-point lead in a loss to the Cleveland Browns. With the Texans dropping to 3-6, the Jags are now -30000 (via FanDuel) to make the playoffs. Barring a shocking collapse, their pick owed to the Eagles will not be in the top 18.
