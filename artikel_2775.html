NFL playoff picture Week 11: Texans mounting late wild-card charge?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Every week for the duration of the 2026 regular season, USA TODAY Sports will provide timely updates to the NFL's ever-evolving playoff picture − typically starting Sunday afternoon and then moving forward for the remainder of the week (through Monday's and Thursday's games or Saturday's, if applicable. And, when the holidays roll around, we'll be watching then, too).

What just happened? What does it mean? What are the pertinent factors (and, perhaps, tiebreakers) prominently in play as each conference's seven-team bracket begins to crystallize? All will be explained and analyzed up to the point when the postseason field is finalized on Sunday, Jan. 10.

Here's where things stand with Week 11 underway:

1. San Francisco 49ers (8-1), NFC West leaders: A surprisingly comfortable margin in what was the league's best division last year could be tested in the coming weeks − and especially so against relentless Minnesota in Estadio Azteca's thin air. Remaining schedule: vs. Vikings [at Mexico City], vs. Seahawks, at Giants, vs. Rams, at Chargers, at Chiefs, vs. Eagles, at Cardinals

2. Detroit Lions (7-2), NFC North leaders: They're actually in a three-way tie with Minnesota and Chicago. But the Lions, who haven't played the Bears, currently have a 1-0 record against those teams, putting them in first. For now. Remaining schedule: vs. Buccaneers, vs. Bears, at Falcons, vs. Titans, at Vikings, vs. Giants, at Bears, at Packers

3. New York Giants (5-3-1), NFC East leaders: They have the most difficult remaining schedule in their division, though the Cowboys have yet to prove they can take advantage of their opportunities to gain ground this season. Remaining schedule: vs. Jaguars, at Colts, vs. 49ers, at Seahawks, vs. Browns, at Lions, at Cowboys, vs. Eagles

4. Carolina Panthers (4-5), NFC South leaders: New Orleans and Tampa Bay remain just a game off the lead of what's bound to remain a one-playoff-team division. Remaining schedule: vs. Ravens, at Buccaneers, at Vikings, vs. Saints, vs. Bengals, at Steelers, vs. Seahawks, at Falcons

5. Minnesota Vikings (7-2), wild card No. 1: Kirk Cousins' return was a smashing success ... through one game. A Week 2 victory at Chicago currently puts Minnesota into the fifth seed. Remaining schedule: vs. 49ers [at Mexico City], vs. Falcons, vs. Panthers, at Patriots, vs. Lions, vs. Commanders, at Jets, vs. Bears

6. Chicago Bears (7-2), wild card No. 2: They lost some ground during their bye week, but they're rested and ready to make it up in Week 11. Remaining schedule: vs. Saints, at Lions, vs. Jaguars, at Dolphins, at Bills, vs. Packers, vs. Lions, at Vikings

7. Green Bay Packers (6-4), wild card No. 3: Is this really going to be the fourth straight season they finish as the NFC's final wild-card entry? A 4-4 record in NFC games currently confers the tiebreaker over Seattle, which is 2-3 in that department. Remaining schedule: BYE, at Rams, at Saints, vs. Bills, vs. Dolphins, at Bears, vs. Texans, vs. Lions

8. Seattle Seahawks (6-4), in the hunt: The defending champs have been beset by various issues − a new offensive coordinator, a series of QB injuries, having to use their entire running back depth chart. If most of those resolve, maybe they can ultimately break through the NFC North logjam ahead of them. Remaining schedule: BYE, at 49ers, vs. Cowboys, vs. Giants, at Eagles, vs. Rams, at Panthers, at Rams

9. Los Angeles Rams (6-4), in the hunt: Losing their first two NFC West games further hinders a team that's failed to live up to its own hype this season. The Rams also own the league's second-toughest remaining schedule ... though at least they won't be impacted much by WR Puka Nacua's suspension. Remaining schedule: BYE, vs. Packers, vs. Chiefs, at 49ers, vs. Cowboys, at Seahawks, at Buccaneers, vs. Seahawks

10. Dallas Cowboys (4-6), in the hunt: They're two games back of the Giants but winning the NFC East is probably Dallas' only hope of making the field. Remaining schedule: vs. Titans, vs. Eagles, at Seahawks, BYE, at Rams, vs. Jaguars, vs. Giants, at Commanders

1. Buffalo Bills (8-1), AFC East leaders: They've never had home-field advantage during the Josh Allen era. May as well get it in the first year they're in their glorious new stadium. Remaining schedule: vs. Dolphins, vs. Chiefs, at Patriots, at Packers, vs. Bears, at Broncos, at Dolphins, vs. Jets

2. Kansas City Chiefs (7-2), AFC West leaders: After splitting with the Broncos, their 3-1 record in division games and 6-1 mark in intraconference games give them a strong start in the tiebreaker department − vaulting them back into first place and ahead of Jacksonville. Remaining schedule: vs. Cardinals, at Bills, at Rams, at Bengals, vs. Patriots, vs. 49ers, at Chargers, vs. Raiders

3. Jacksonville Jaguars (7-2), AFC South leaders: A roster reloaded ahead of the trade deadline and a weak division should keep the Jags on the fast track to a second straight divisional crown. At minimum. Remaining schedule: at Giants, vs. Titans, at Bears, vs. Steelers, at Texans, at Cowboys, vs. Commanders, at Colts

4. Cincinnati Bengals (7-2), AFC North leaders: They moved atop the AFC North by blowing out the Steelers in Week 10 and earning a season split with them. Remaining schedule: at Commanders, vs. Saints, at Browns, vs. Chiefs, at Panthers, at Colts, vs. Ravens, vs. Browns

5. Denver Broncos (7-2), wild card No. 1: Statistically, they have the league's easiest remaining schedule, which could mean a second straight No. 1 seed as things unfold. Remaining schedule: vs. Raiders, at Steelers, vs. Dolphins, at Jets, at Raiders, vs. Bills, at Patriots, vs. Chargers

6. Pittsburgh Steelers (6-3), wild card No. 2: They dropped out of the AFC North lead but will try to get healthy on the other end of Pennsylvania this weekend. Remaining schedule: at Eagles, vs. Broncos, vs. Texans, at Jaguars, vs. Ravens, vs. Panthers, at Titans, at Ravens

7. Cleveland Browns (6-4), wild card No. 3: QB Deshaun Watson continues to pull wins out of the fire ... and pulled the Dawg Pound back into a postseason position with last weekend's comeback against Houston. And given they don't play on the road again until Dec. 20, this is a stretch to take advantage of. Remaining schedule: BYE, vs. Raiders, vs. Bengals, vs. Falcons, at Giants, at Ravens, vs. Colts, vs. Bengals

8. New England Patriots (5-5), in the hunt: They're without QB Drake Maye and running out of time to make a run, especially with the opponents they're about to face. Remaining schedule: BYE, at Chargers, vs. Bills, vs. Vikings, at Chiefs, at Jets, vs. Broncos, vs. Dolphins

9. Houston Texans (4-6), in the hunt: A strength-of-victory tiebreaker over Las Vegas moved them up from 11th place to ninth after Thursday night's rollover of the Colts. On the outer rim of the playoff fringe for weeks, the schedule should give them a pathway to relevance ... maybe more. Remaining schedule: vs. Ravens, at Steelers, at Commanders, vs. Jaguars, at Eagles, at Packers, vs. Titans

10. Las Vegas Raiders (4-6), in the hunt: Rookie QB Fernando Mendoza didn't look ready on Sunday. Whether he's ready to play in Denver remains to be seen. A superior conference record keeps them ahead of Baltimore. Remaining schedule: at Broncos, at Browns, BYE, vs. Chargers, vs. Broncos, vs. Titans, at Cardinals, at Chiefs

11. Baltimore Ravens (4-6), in the hunt: New QB1 Jacoby Brissett couldn't keep them afloat Monday, when the Ravens fell to the previously winless Los Angeles Chargers. And now idle Baltimore has slipped behind the Texans and Raiders. The chance QB Lamar Jackson returns this season looks increasingly remote. Remaining schedule: at Panthers, at Texans, BYE, vs. Buccaneers, at Steelers, vs. Browns, at Bengals, vs. Steelers
