Tennessee football fans eager to see the Faizon Brandon package, CFP climb past Alabama

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Tennessee football fans want to see more Faizon Brandon in the offense, DeSean Bishop's assault on the record book and the Vols climb the College Football Playoff rankings.

They should get all three wishes granted as the No. 9 Vols head into the final three-game stretch of the regular season. They also want an explanation for a controversial call that they thought was a UT touchdown by DaSaahn Brame.

Tennessee (7-2, 4-2 SEC) plays at Texas A&M (4-5, 1-5) on Nov. 14 (3 p.m. ET, ESPN). But first, let's look back to UT's 34-6 win over No. 13 Kentucky by answering fan questions in an overflowing Vols mailbag.

Supposedly, the CFP rankings reset each week. But judging by the initial CFP Top 25, Tennessee should move up four spots because four teams ahead of the Vols lost. No. 8 BYU lost to No. 3 Utah. No. 10 Ole Miss lost to No. 2 Georgia. No. 12 Ohio State lost to No. 23 Oregon. Plus, No. 13 Tennessee beat No. 6 Kentucky – that is, according to the CFP rankings.

Georgia, Utah and Alabama might be the top three. There'll be an ongoing debate about the weight of UT's head-to-head 26-10 win over Alabama. But the important thing for the Vols is that they keep winning to secure a playoff spot.

Heupel said he challenged the running backs at halftime because they had missed some assignments and running lanes set up by blocks. They refocused and reconfigured, and it showed in the results. The Vols rushed for 134 yards in the second half.

In the third quarter, Brame caught a 20-yard pass and fell backward over the goal line as he tried to secure the ball. He didn't lose possession until he landed in the end zone. However, ESPN rules analyst Steve Shaw, the former SEC head of officials, explained during the broadcast why it was an incomplete pass.

When a receiver is going to the ground as they make a catch – diving or falling, for example – they must maintain possession through contact with the ground. So it looked like a catch but wasn't one, by rule.
