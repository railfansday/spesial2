Celtics bench player gets rare start against Orlando Magic

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

BOSTON — The Celtics will start Luka Garza in place of Neemias Queta when they face the Orlando Magic on Monday night. Queta, who has started at center for the past 8 games, gets a rest on the second night of a back-to-back.

Derrick White, Jordan Walsh, Paul George, and Jayson Tatum will round out the starting lineup. Queta is the only player on the roster to get the night off.

Garza is averaging 6.1 points, 3.2 rebounds, and 1.5 assists per game in 12.5 minutes off the bench. He's shooting 45.2% from beyond the arc. This will be Garza's second start of the season; he also started alongside Neemias Queta in Toronto as the Celtics employed a double-big lineup.

This will be Garza's 7th start as a Celtic; he started six games last season and one this season.

Mitchell Robinson, who is averaging 5.2 points and 5.5 rebounds in 22 minutes off the bench, will also likely play significant center minutes. Rookie Chris Cenac Jr., who finished with 6 points and 3 rebounds in 7 minutes off the bench on Sunday, could also see some run.
