Tennessee Titans Players to Be Thankful For This Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Families around the country are celebrating Thanksgiving today.

Although the Tennessee Titans haven't given fans a lot to be thankful for this year, there are a few players who should be recognized for their contributions.

After an offseason of hope, the Titans are actually worse than they were in 2025, which is hard to imagine. The Titans are 1-9 heading into Week 12 after a 51-30 loss to the Dallas Cowboys.

They are trending toward having the No. 1 overall pick for the second time in the last three drafts. The Titans were bad last year, but they weren't this bad.

The Titans were 3-14 last year, and with how tough the remaining schedule is, it's hard to find a game where they actually have a chance of winning.

This season has been a disappointment and one that fans probably want to forget. We can still highlight players who should have fans excited about the future of this team.

I thought of a few players who should be giving Titans fans a reason to feel thankful this year.

Anthony Hill Jr. was a third-round pick out of Texas.

The Titans didn't expect him to start right away. He got his opportunity after Cedric Gray went down with a concussion ahead of the season opener. Hill started instead and had a stellar debut.

He finished with 16 total tackles, leading the team. He's been in the starting lineup ever since.

It has been hard to keep him off the field, which is great to see this early into his NFL career.

We saw the potential that Hill had coming out of the draft. He has the right build for a middle linebacker in Robert Saleh's system. There was just no telling when he would be on the field consistently.

He proved that he was ready early on in the season and is looking like a solid pick by General Manager Mike Borgonzi.

Tony Pollard continues to prove that he still has what it takes to be a reliable running back in this league.

He got off to a slow start, but then really came to life in Weeks 7-8. He had back-to-back 100-yard performances against the Cleveland Browns and Cincinnati Bengals.

He came back down to Earth against the Jacksonville Jaguars, but then finished with 110 rushing yards in the Cowboys game on Sunday.

It's unfortunate that his contract is set to run out next year. However, he is making a good case to be re-signed by the Titans.

We'll have to see if it's a good enough case in the next few months.

Jacob Martin was a player whose stats didn't wow you initially.

Heading into the season, the most sacks he had in a single season was 5.5 last year with the Washington Commanders. I figured he would be a depth piece on the defensive line, especially since he is 30.

He has proven that he can be a valuable weapon on this Titans' pass rush time and time again. He had his breakout game against the Bengals, which was shortly after the Jeffery Simmons trade.

He now has 7.5 sacks and is leading the Titans. Martin has one more year on his deal after this season, but could earn himself quite a nice payday.

The Titans have needed a player to step up to the plate after trading Simmons. Martin has answered the bell so far.

With how bad the Titans have been at certain points, it was hard to think of another player.

However, Joey Slye has been everything the Titans have needed him to be as a kicker. You would like the Titans to score more touchdowns. However, Slye has been solid when the offense has been inconsistent and has settled for field goals.

He is perfect on extra points and has only missed one field goal this season.

He has the consistency to make kicks from inside 30 or 40 yards but has also proven that he has the range to take on kicks of up to 60 yards. His longest kick last season was 58 yards, and he hasn't dropped off by much.

It has to feel good knowing that when the Titans need a tough kick to be made, Slye is there.

Slye also showed us that he isn't afraid to lay the boom. He drilled a Jaguars returner in Week 10, forcing a fumble. The hit from Slye made the crowd at Nissan Stadium let out a collective gasp.

With how unreliable the Titans' kickers have been in the past, Slye has been a breath of fresh air.
