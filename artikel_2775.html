Texas vs Texas A&M Prediction: Can the Longhorns Lock This Up?

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It's all right there for the taking for a Texas Longhorn team that just has to beat the second-worst team in the SEC to have everything it wants.No. 3 in the latest College Football Playoff rankings, Texas will go to the SEC Championship with a win over Texas A&M, and more importantly, it will be a mortal lock for a CFP spot no matter what happens against Georgia next week. But that assumes a win in College Station.Lose, and then things get dicey. There's still a chance to get to the SEC title game, but making it in the College Football Playoff as a three-loss team with a defeat to a 4-7 squad would be a big ask.Texas A&M lost last year's rivalry showdown to Texas, but it was just the first loss of the season. It cost the Aggies a shot at the SEC Championship, but they made it into the CFP without a problem. Win this against the hated program from up the road, and it would almost excuse this stunning clunker of a campaign. Almost.

Texas A&M started the season No. 8 in both the AP and Coaches polls, but it only managed one win over an FBS team – 34-7 over Arkansas – since mid-September. It's on a four-game losing streak.

Texas has won five straight since losing back-to-back 27-24 games to Oklahoma and Florida.

This is the third meeting between the two since Texas joined the SEC, with the Longhorns winning both games by ten points.

Texas A&M just doesn't know how to score.The Aggie passing game is wildly inefficient, there are too many stalled drives, and it's hard to win when you don't put points on the board. The Aggies have failed to hit ten points in five of the last seven games, and they scored a total of 23 points combined in the last three games.Even when the offense does move a bit, turnovers (Kentucky) and red zone collapses (South Carolina) get in the way. In this, the Texas pass rush and run defense should be the reason the Aggies don't move.

Can the Aggies start forcing more takeaways? They don't have a pass rush, and the defense breaks in too many key moments when it needs to bend, but the problem is on the offensive side.The Aggies hold up well against the run, but the takeaways have stalled after coming up with six in the first two games of the year. The two in last week's 28-7 loss to Oklahoma helped, but they weren't enough.Texas turned it over multiple times in six of the last ten games, and it's not above having penalty issues. Texas A&M can't shove anyone around, but if it can win the field position battle and catch/make a couple of breaks – see, the Texas loss to Oklahoma – it'll have a shot.

The rivalry aspect matters, and Texas A&M will play like a team with nothing to lose, but after a rocky start, the Longhorns will play like they're ready for what's next. The 45-3 Longhorn win over Arkansas last week was focused, and that will continue.There's still talent on Texas A&M, but the lines aren't playing well enough and there's no pop to the attack to keep up the pace.
