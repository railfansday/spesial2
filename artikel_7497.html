Indian National Living in San Bernardino County Sentenced to 2½ Years in Federal Prison for Role in $7.2 Million Cargo Theft Ring

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

An illegal alien from India was sentenced today to 30 months in federal prison for conspiring with others to steal more than $7 million worth of cargo from warehouses across Southern California by buying or using their own trucking companies to bid on authentic shipping contracts, picking up the cargo loads, and never delivering the loads to the agreed upon destinations.
