Troubling stats highlight Warriors' struggles during disappointing road trip

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Troubling stats highlight Warriors' struggles during disappointing road trip originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area

Without a nucleus, a team can't function properly.

Warriors superstar Steph Curry is at the center of everything the Warriors are trying to accomplish this season. The four-time NBA champion is currently sidelined with a right oblique abdominal tear, and Golden State's supporting cast has yet to show any fight against adversity.

Golden State's offense has clearly been stagnant since their best player was injured, and signs don't point to a resolution anytime soon.

The first quarter woes continued throughout the road trip. The Warriors only led in one of the five games, which was early on against the Dallas Mavericks.

The start of the Warriors season has been extremely streaky. They lost the first three out of the gate, then went on an incredible eight-game win streak. Since Curry went down against the Houston Rockets, they haven't won another game.

The defense has a lot of work to do, but the offense is the biggest concern. Unable to break 100 points in four straight games is unacceptable in today's NBA.

Curry will be reevaluated on Thanksgiving, but more help could arrive sooner than that, as guard Brandon Williams, sidelined with a hamstring injury, will be reevaluated this weekend.

The hope is to get Williams back on the court next week, but as long as Curry is out, wins will be hard to come by for Golden State.

Rookie Yaxel Lendeborg has his work cut out for him early into his NBA career. Trying to keep a historic franchise afloat while waiting for Curry to return is a tough task for a 24-year-old.

One positive is that the Warriors return home on Sunday after a disastrous road trip. The bad news is that they'll be facing the 11-4 Boston Celtics.
