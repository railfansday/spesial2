Taylor Swift’s CMA Performance Was Filled With Easter Eggs From the Early Days of Her Career

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Taylor Swift made a triumphant return to the Country Music Association Awards last night, where she performed one of her newest singles, “I Knew It, I Knew You,” and one of her oldest, “Our Song.” The performance marked yet another example of the pop star’s return to country music, and — in vintage Swift fashion — it was filled with easter eggs that might just indicate there’s something more on the horizon.

Swift’s dedicated fans and sleuths have been assiduously dissecting every frame of her performance for clues, with many starting to suspect the long-awaited “Taylor’s version” of her self-titled debut might finally be on the way. “LOOKS LIKE I HAVEN’T YEE’D MY LAST HAW THANK YOU CMA!!!!” she quipped on her Instagram Stories after the show.

While Swift regained the rights to her masters before dropping the re-record, she said she planned on releasing it “when the time is right.” And though the album’s 20th anniversary did pass on Oct. 24, there’s arguably no wrong time to put it out.

As such, her performance at the CMAs was filled with nods to the beginning of her career. Most obvious was her decision to pair “I Knew It, I Knew You” with “Our Song.” The third single off of Taylor Swift, “Our Song” was Swift’s first Number One hit on the Hot Country Songs chart, and she even performed it during her first performance at the CMA Awards in 2007.

But while “Our Song” is one of Swift’s most cherished tunes, it’s also one she hasn’t performed a lot on tour in recent years. The last time it was a key part of her setlist was when she was supporting Speak Now. Since then, she’s only dusted it off a few times, including a handful of performances during the “surprise song” slot of the Eras Tour.

Beyond that, some of Swift’s most eagle-eyed fans zeroed in on a very particular staging choice. Swift’s performance was also peppered with cameos from big-name country stars, as she strolled from her dressing room to the stage. First, she greeted Brad Paisley, with whom she toured in 2007 and was now dusting off some of her old CMA trophies. (Perhaps penance for the prank he pulled when he filled her tour bus with live crickets.) Elsewhere, she shared moments with Little Big Town and Sugarland, two artists she wrote songs for and with; and gave a fist bump to Eric Church, whom Swift famously replaced as the opener on a 2006 Rascal Flatts tour. When Swift finally got on stage, she was joined by Keith Urban, another artist she supported on tour in the late 2000s.

And in case all those allusions in the performance weren’t enough to make you think the Taylor Swift re-record might finally be on the way, consider this: The whole night, Swift was sat next to Tim McGraw.
