CBS’ ‘Matlock’ Officially Sets Season 3 Release Date

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

For the first time since its 2024 premiere, Matlock has not been on CBS's fall schedule, marking an intentional strategic shift rather than a cancellation. Series creator Jennie Snyder Urman requested the move to the network's midseason rollout after the central Wellbrexa investigation was resolved in the Season 2 finale. This extended nine-month break gives the writing team extra breathing room to craft a fresh mystery without compromising quality.

Production is already underway, with Kathy Bates returning as Madeline Matlock alongside Skye P. Marshall, who is also directing an episode this season. When Matlock Season 3 finally premieres on Thursday, January 21, 2027, it will also feature a slight schedule adjustment. The hit legal drama is moving from its previous 9:00 pm slot to 10:00 pm ET, airing directly after Elsbeth to anchor CBS's Thursday primetime lineup.

Matlock is one of several CBS shows with shorter seasons, with the network ordering just 13 episodes for Season 3, down from the usual 20-episode season. Fire Country and NCIS: Origins have also seen this change. As for where the story goes next, Urman confirmed it will remain in the healthcare industry and build on the Wellbrexa mystery, even though it won't be a direct continuation. The new arc involves insurance and healthcare. "We had a lot of pieces going in, so we really had to create this new piece of it. I feel lucky that the network’s giving us time to get it right because I don't want the quality to drop," she said, adding, "The quality is everything to me."

You let people think you are not a threat and then you simply never go away. Columbo is the only major detective show that tells you who did it in the first ten minutes, because the pleasure was never the puzzle, it was watching a rich man slowly realise he is in trouble.

You are the most interesting person in the room and you know exactly how to use it. Rian Johnson built Blanc as a gentleman detective who is also an outsider everywhere he goes, which lets him be charming at people who would never otherwise talk to him.

Everybody tells you everything, and you have never once asked them to. Jessica Fletcher is a crime novelist from a small town in Maine who is so warm and so obviously trustworthy that suspects confess to her essentially as a social courtesy.

You expect to be lied to and you are almost never disappointed. Chandler wrote Marlowe as a man with a rigid private code in a city that has none, which is why he takes the case, solves it, gets nothing out of it and takes the next one.

CBS has made some significant changes to its winter schedule as shows that had been delayed finally get slotted in. The new hit legal drama Cupertino is moving to Sundays at 10 pm beginning January 17, while the new comedy Eternally Yours is also moving to Mondays at 8:30 pm after a new episode of Ghosts. NCIS: Sydney takes over the NCIS: Origins slot beginning Tuesday, January 26, while Elsbeth moves to 9 pm and Matlock to 10 pm beginning Thursday, January 21. Ghosts airs the second all-new episode of the week at 8:30 pm after Georgie & Mandy's First Marriage to be able to fit its 22-episode sixth season into the schedule. Matthew Gray Gubler's new procedural Einstein airs on Fridays at 8 pm, while Sheriff Country moves to 9 pm beginning February 12.
