Better Home & Finance: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

HELOCs earn about the same revenue per loan as mortgages on much smaller balances, a shift that could cushion earnings while originations stay under pressure.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 9, 2026, Better Home & Finance (BETR -1.53%) outlined an operational overhaul to lower fixed costs and push toward core profitability. Investors focused on long-term value need to evaluate whether aggressive expense reductions can stabilize cash flow while high mortgage rates depress loan originations. Three takeaways from the call stand out.

By replacing traditional management layers with software tools, the company aims to reduce quarterly adjusted operating costs to between $45 million and $47 million in the fourth quarter. Cutting corporate overhead helps the business stabilize cash flow while mortgage volumes remain volatile.

Home equity lines of credit, or HELOCs, generate six to eight points on gain on sale compared to two to three points for traditional mortgages. Expanding this product lets the lender earn equivalent revenue on smaller loan balances.

The company's proprietary loan platform, Tinman, originated 57% of third-quarter loan volume, up from 40% a year ago. Shifting volume toward automated platform partners can lower customer acquisition costs, or CAC, by allowing the business to scale without spending heavily on direct advertising.

Mortgage market headwinds continue to depress refinancing activity, but automated underwriting tools expand broker distribution. The business relies on software automation and third-party partner integration to capture market share while working toward breakeven in adjusted EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization).

Strategic execution now centers on maintaining lower fixed overhead while expanding loan originations through external partners.
