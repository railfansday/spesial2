Prediction: These 2 Stocks Will Be Worth More Than SpaceX 5 Years From Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The market caps of Broadcom and Meta Platforms are only a few hundred billion dollars behind SpaceX's.

SpaceX (SPCX +3.12%) has become one of the biggest names on Wall Street since its June debut, and currently has a market cap of almost $19 trillion, but two other tech giants could be worth more within the next five years. Specifically, Broadcom (AVGO +1.78%) and Meta Platforms (META -0.53%) are both well-positioned to benefit from the artificial intelligence (AI) boom, potentially pushing both companies' market caps past SpaceX's by 2031.

The world's largest tech companies are increasingly designing custom AI chips and networks tailored to their own computing needs, and Broadcom has become a key partner in that effort. The results are already showing up in the numbers.

In fact, Broadcom reported strong growth in its AI semiconductor business, driven by steadily rising demand for custom AI accelerators and networking technology. In its fiscal third quarter, AI semiconductor revenue grew 221% year over year to $16.7 billion. Management also indicated that momentum continued into the current quarter as the AI build-out accelerated.

Broadcom's expanding position selling the picks and shovels to multiple companies in the AI race could potentially make it more valuable than SpaceX in the next few years.

Looking ahead, if hyperscalers and AI labs continue shifting more of their workloads to custom chips, Broadcom will benefit from industrywide spending. That could be a more sustainable model than relying on the success of any specific consumer-facing platform.

Still, Broadcom faces a number of risks. In particular, any slowdown in AI infrastructure spending by its largest customers could impact its revenue growth. There are also concerns about its high degree of customer concentration and whether investments in AI will ever generate attractive returns.

The case for owning Meta has changed considerably over the past year. Instead of its AI-related spending being viewed as an expensive long-term investment, AI has become a part of the company's core business. And it's turning into a major advantage.

Meta already has billions of people regularly using Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and Messenger, along with millions of businesses spending money on its advertising platforms. As a result, Meta can deploy AI directly across that massive ecosystem.

On top of that, Meta reported that AI-powered content recommendations are gaining traction. Plus, revenue grew 26% year over year in the third quarter to $64.6 billion.

Meta could eventually surpass SpaceX in market cap by combining a powerful cash-generating platform with its ability to deploy AI across billions of users. In other words, if AI improves advertising, engagement, and monetization, while also creating successful new products, Meta could benefit from several growth engines all at once.

However, the biggest risk now is that Meta's AI spending has become enormous, and investors are demanding to see returns on those investments. Advanced AI products can also be expensive to operate; meanwhile, the company's Reality Labs segment continues to lose money.

Surpassing SpaceX in size won't be an easy feat, but Broadcom and Meta have two things going for them: strong existing businesses and enormous opportunities to capitalize on AI.

These days, SpaceX's market cap is a bit under $1.9 trillion, and it trades at a price-to-sales ratio of about 59. That's well above Broadcom's P/S of 19 or Meta's P/S of 6.8.

To pass SpaceX, Broadcom would need to grow by about 17% from its current market cap of just under $1.6 trillion, while Meta, today at just over $1.6 trillion, would have to grow by about 16%.

But looking ahead, SpaceX is expected to grow as well. If its market cap grew 10% annually, to about $3 trillion by 2031, Broadcom and Meta would each need to increase their market caps by 13% per year. They could each get there by growing earnings just 6% per year through 2031, assuming their forward P/E ratios rise to 25.

Ultimately, Meta looks like the safer choice, given its powerful cash-generating platform. However, Broadcom could be the higher-risk, higher-reward bet to top SpaceX by 2031.
