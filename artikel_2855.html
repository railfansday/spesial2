Enzo Maresca Defends £125 Million Enzo Fernandez - Price Tag Does Not Stop 'Human' Mistakes

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

How does Enzo Maresca fix Manchester City's midfield?

Enzo Maresca has been under intense scrutiny following a 3-1 defeat to Nottingham Forest before the recent November international break, which many feel was largely related to his squad selection and use of Enzo Fernandez in the number six role.

Operating best as a more advanced midfielder, the Argentina international's attacking eagerness has often left Elliot Anderson stranded just ahead of the defensive line, opening huge gaps for teams to attack into.

As was best seen against Nottingham Forest, all three of Oliver Glasner's side's goals came through the middle, where Fernandez should have been covering, with the result now drawing concerns over Enzo Maresca's decision-making and calls for Ayyoub Bouaddi to be utilised more often.

For what seemed a partnership destined for immediate greatness when they signed for the club in the summer, Elliot Anderson and Enzo Fernandez have struggled when playing alongside one another, despite costing £116 million and £125 million respectively.

The England international has arguably been Manchester City's best player so far this season alongside Rayan Cherki, and while Fernandez has also shown strengths across the pitch, a lack of balance in midfield has caused too many problems, according to Maresca.

"Again, we won games with Enzo inside the pitch, and I know that when there is a defeat we are all looking for the reason why and sometimes we think that this is the reason why or that," Enzo Maresca said.

"Sometimes probably we need more balance, probably need different kinds of players inside the pitch; it depends a little bit on the game. But again, we won games with Enzo, we lost games with Enzo, and it's not just Enzo - more players.

"But we are going to find solutions also for more balance in defensive lines, midfielders, or up-front."

Manchester City host Fulham at the Etihad Stadium on Saturday afternoon and, having won their last 20 games against the Cottagers, it makes for the perfect opportunity for Enzo Maresca to regain some backing from frustrated supporters.

The Italian kicked off his Premier League tenure in the North-West with a seven-game winning run across competitions, and will hope to prove himself against Alvaro Arbeloa's side - who are currently 15th in the Premier League table.

It seems a simple fix to the majority of Enzo Maresca's problems coming from choices made in midfield, but admitted on Friday afternoon that the exorbitant fees paid for these players don't help when mistakes are made.

"Probably, yes. But it's something I cannot control. Just because we paid some money for players, people think he's not going to make a mistake, ever. He's a human, a player, and he's going to make some mistakes," he said.

It is not yet known whether Enzo Maresca will start the game with Elliot Anderson and Enzo Fernandez occupying the deeper midfielder positions once again.

However, it is hard to imagine the Italian will make a similar mistake in such a significant game, where Manchester City must win if they want to keep up the chase with Arsenal and, somewhat surprisingly, Brighton & Hove Albion, in the early stages of the Premier League title race.
