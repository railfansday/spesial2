‘You don’t ever see’: Steph Curry says what he finds really ‘incredible’ about Yaxel Lendeborg

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Steph Curry was the star of the show for the Golden State Warriors against the Orlando Magic, but Yaxel Lendeborg did more than play his part.

The Warriors were made to work hard despite a dominant first half. They got battered in the third quarter and forced overtime in the fourth before winning the game.

Curry's 41 points made the headlines, but Lendeborg scored 23 and was again very impressive for large parts of the game.

Lendeborg has been one of the biggest success stories of the season.

The rookie has started his NBA career in tremendous fashion and has performed magnificently in almost every single game so far.

Against the Magic, Lendeborg scored 23 points and recorded eight rebounds and three assists. He had his tough moments at times, but overall, it was another very impressive game.

Curry was asked about his young teammate after the game, and he branded the 24-year-old's composure as 'incredible'.

He said in his press conference: "It's more of the same that we've been able to see, that consistency of effort and aggressiveness, and then the composure to just make solid plays. It's incredible to watch.

"We all kind of turned the ball over too much tonight, and there were a couple times where he got in a bad position offensively, where he was just attacking the wrong lanes and all that.

"But those are the types that you don't mind because he's trying to make plays, and then it starts to slow down for him in those moments of the game that matter. And you saw him float in the lane, hit a big three, running in transition…

"Like, you don't ever see him kind of dial it back because things aren't really going his way. And you love that about a rookie."

Lendeborg is all action, and even when things are not working out for him, he shows incredible courage whenever he receives the ball.

That is the sign of a great player, and if he keeps developing at this rapid rate, he will become a superstar for the Warriors very soon.
