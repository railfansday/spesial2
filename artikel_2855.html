Christian Watson injury update: Packers WR hurt on massive hit

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Thanksgiving Eve hasn't gone according to plan for Christian Watson.

The Green Bay Packers receiver exited with a thigh injury after making his first catch of the game. He returned on the Packers' second drive. Watson appeared to be in the clear, until taking a huge hit late in the first half from the Los Angeles Rams' Kamren Kinchens.

Watson remained down after the play, where his head hit the turf.

Kinchens was ejected from the game. Officials determined that the defender left his feet and delivered a forceable blow to the head and/or neck area.

The 27-year-old Watson has been no stranger to injuries in his career, but has remained healthy in what has been a career-year for him.
