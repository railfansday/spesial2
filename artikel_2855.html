Dan Campbell honors Jack Campbell with game ball after Lions' Miami win

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The Detroit Lions' celebration in Miami after their 19-9 win over the Dolphins quickly turned to injured captain Jack Campbell.

After Sunday's win, coach Dan Campbell gave an emotional locker-room speech on Sunday, Nov. 8 about the team's resilience before announcing a game ball for Jack Campbell when the room erupted.

"Yes, sir," one player could be heard yelling.

It was one of several emotional moments in the postgame celebration, which also included a game ball for defensive coordinator Kelvin Sheppard and a visit from owner Sheila Ford Hamp.

Dan Campbell's postgame speech was longer than usual and centered on perseverance after an emotional week.

"When you're a champion and you just [expletive] keep goin' after it. You just do," Campbell said. "And you'll find your way through it as long as you believe and you just keep goin', man, and good things happen."

Campbell pointed to three third-quarter plays that helped swing the game in Detroit's favor.

After a Chuck Clark interception, Jared Goff connected with Jameson Williams for 31 yards and Amon-Ra St. Brown for 17. Jahmyr Gibbs followed with an 8-yard touchdown run, Detroit's only touchdown of the game.

"Bam, bam, bam. Three plays, man. Just like that," Campbell said. "That's what we are capable of."

Campbell also praised his players for responding after a difficult week.

"As the person, man, and your freaking teammate, I am so proud of you guys," he said. "I love this team. It was built for a reason."

Earlier in the day when players and staff were arriving to Hard Rock Stadium, many donned a No. 46 jersey to honor their captain, placed on injured reserve Saturday, Nov. 7.

And many fans at the Miami stadium showed their love for Jack Campbell as well.

Campbell had another game ball to give out.

After praising a defense that held Miami to 77 rushing yards and no touchdowns, Campbell presented the second game ball to Kelvin Sheppard, who leads the defensive unit.

Sheppard appeared emotional as he addressed the team.

"Listen, it's about the players, man," Sheppard said. "I just want to say I appreciate y'all. Seriously."

He thanked his defensive players and talked about how the team helped him through a difficult season.

"Nothing about this year has been easy, but leaning on y'all, man, is why I wake up every day," Sheppard said. "It's why I enjoy that drive to work every day. Serving y'all."

Lions owner Sheila Ford Hamp also joined the postgame celebration, telling the team she was proud of how it handled an emotional week.

"You all hung together, stuck together, leaned on each other, and look what we did? We won," Hamp said.

She also brought the conversation back to Jack Campbell.

"I saw Jack a couple of days ago and he was worried about all of you," Hamp said. "Well, I know he's not now. I know he's just as proud of you as I am."

The tribute continued as the Lions gathered for their final team huddle, with Jack Campbell's No. 46 jersey being held up after linebacker Derrick Barnes' urging. Barnes was another player who wore No. 46 ahead of the game.

Jared Goff delivered a fitting final message: "Stick together. Keep leanin' on each other. Love you, boys."

The Lions next "host" the New England Patriots in Week 10 at FC Bayern Munich Arena in Germany. Kickoff is scheduled for 9:30 a.m. Sunday on Fox.
