Kourtney Kardashian Makes Rare Comment About Travis Barker Split Rumors

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Kourtney Kardashian is addressing rumors claiming her marriage to Travis Barker is over.

“[My publicist] wrote to me this morning. She is like, ‘The magazine is saying that you and Travis don’t live together anymore and you are splitting up.’ Any truth?” Kardashian, 47, said during the Thursday, November 5, episode of The Kardashians.

Kardashian urged her spokesperson to let the narrative play out, adding, “I go, ‘Tell them to print it.’ Just see what happens.”

The reality star clarified that the claims had no credibility.

“They know it is not true. They know they saw a dumb TikTok of someone who makes up fabricated stories,” Kardashian fired back to her friend Simon Huck, who slammed the “conspiracy theories” about the famous family.

Huck, 42, also called out the “insane” AI videos on social media. “And people believe it,” Kardashian replied. “It is so bizarre.”

Kardashian’s personal life has been front and center since her days on Keeping Up With the Kardashians. Her nearly 10-year relationship with Scott Disick was documented on the hit E! series as they expanded their family three times before calling it quits in 2015.

After the breakup, Kardashian found love with Barker, 50. They got engaged in October 2021 before tying the knot in May 2022. The couple, who candidly discussed their difficulties expanding their blended familyr, surprised the public when Kardashian announced in June 2023 that she was expecting a baby with Barker by holding up a handwritten sign at a Blink-182 concert that read, “Travis, I’m pregnant.”

Three months later, Kardashian revealed she had been rushed to the hospital for emergency surgery ahead of her fourth child’s arrival. (The reality star also shares kids Mason, 16, Penelope, 14, and Reign, 11, with Disick, 43.)

“I will be forever grateful to my incredible doctors for saving our baby’s life. I am eternally grateful to my husband who rushed to my side from tour to be with me in the hospital and take care of me afterwards, my rock,” she wrote via Instagram at the time. “And to my mom, thank you for holding my hand through this. As someone who has had three really easy pregnancies in the past, I wasn’t prepared for the fear of rushing into urgent fetal surgery.”

She continued, “I don’t think anyone who hasn’t been through a similar situation can begin to understand that feeling of fear. I have a whole new understanding and respect for the mamas who have had to fight for their babies while pregnant. Praise be to God. Walking out of the hospital with my baby boy in my tummy and safe was the truest blessing.”

Kardashian and Barker welcomed their son, Rocky, in November 2023. While being asked about the pregnancy for Barker’s documentary, Louder Than Fear, which was released in August, the duo revealed they previously went through a miscarriage.

“When we lost the baby, we were devastated,” Kardashian said about how she found out she was pregnant six months into dating Barker in 2022. “We cried for days. All we wanted was to have a baby together.”
