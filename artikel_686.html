Trent Reznor and Atticus Ross to Score David Fincher’s ‘The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth’

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Nine Inch Nails principals and veteran film composers Trent Reznor and Atticus Ross are once again teaming up to provide the score for David Fincher‘s new film “The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth,” marking their sixth collaboration with Fincher on one of his movies.

News of Reznor and Ross’ score was revealed on the poster for the film, which will be released exclusively to IMAX on November 25 for two weeks before it becomes available to stream on Netflix beginning on December 23. The duo first worked with Fincher on 2010’s “The Social Network,” winning the Academy Award and Golden Globe for best original score. Reznor and Ross continued to provide music for all of Fincher’s subsequent films including 2011’s “The Girl With the Dragon Tattoo,” 2014’s “Gone Girl,” 2020’s “Mank” and 2023’s “The Killer.” In 2013, they won the Grammy for best score soundtrack for visual media for “Girl With the Dragon Tattoo.”

Outside of their work with Fincher, Reznor and Ross have composed the scores for numerous films including “Patriots Day,” “Soul,” “Challengers,” “Bird Box” and “Queer.” Ross has been collaborating with Reznor as far back as 2002, when they worked on the project Tapeworm, and has contributed to numerous Nine Inch Nails releases.

“The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth” is a spin-off of 2019’s “Once Upon a Time in Hollywood,” focusing on Brad Pitt’s title character. The film, written by Quentin Tarantino, is set eight years after the events of “Hollywood” and features an ensemble cast that includes Elizabeth Debicki, Scott Caan, Carla Gugino, Timothy Olyphant and Yahya Abdul-Mateen II.
