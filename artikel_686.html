Emma Chamberlain, Wesley Wang, Aryna Sabalenka and More Unveil New Projects for YouTube

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Streaming giant hosts Creator Premieres event to promote its stars and help brands navigate through the tonnage on the platform

Sean Downey of Google sounded a lot like a network TV executive on Tuesday evening as he opened the second annual YouTube Creator Premieres content showcase in Hollywood.

The event, held at the Hollywood Roosevelt hotel, showed off upcoming titles from such disparate stars as influencer Emma Chamberlain, director Wesley Wang, tennis superstar Aryna Sabalenka, musician and comedian Morgan Jay, chefs Nick DiGiovanni and Ina Garten and comedians Lilly Singh and Grace Reiter. Downey opened the hourlong presentation by stating what was obvious from the scene and from the trends in media, marketing and advertising for the past year: The definition of television has changed. He noted that the first Academy Awards was held in the same ballroom where a few hundred creators, influencers and journalists gathered. The studio system that defined who got on the air and into the multiplexes is under reconstruction.

“Today, the studio are the creators who are in this room. They own the studio. The creators decide what gets to be shown and they have an unbreakable connection with the fans and people that want to watch their content,” Downey, who is president of Americas and global partners for Google.

Of course, the sales pitch wasn’t far behind. The event was designed to warm up deals for big stars such as Chamberlain, Sabalenka and Wang as well as rising stars. “Brands and creators were built to be together,” Downey said. “They speak a language of trust and resonance.”

The streaming pipeline controlled by YouTube also facilitates all the new dimensions of the digital economy, from second screen activity and community building to supporting vertical media, microdramas, e-commerce and livestream events.

The event was hosted by Scott Evans, the former “Access Hollywood” co-host who has seen his YouTube business blossom with the success of his “House Guest” lifestyle series. YouTube unleashed a slew of trailers and teasers for shows rolling out through the end of the year and into the first quarter. Evans is getting a “House Guest” spinoff series that will find him traveling to visit homes in exotic locations, starting in January with a trek to Lagos, Nigeria.

“This platform changed my life,” Evans told the crowd. In a different era, his TV career might have ended with the sunset of the NBC-produced daily entertainment newsmagazine series “Access Hollywood,” which ended this past fall. Instead, thanks to the opportunities afforded by YouTube, “I marched out of the mud and said ‘Finally, I’ve got more time.’ ” Evans said.

Evans hosted short talk-show style interviews with Sabalenka, Wang and Chamberlain. Sabalenka, who already has an enormously popular YouTube channel, explained why YouTube is her favorite social media platform.

“I tried bigger documentaries with Netflix” but the fit wasn’t right, Sabalenka said. “I wanted to have more control to tell the story the way I wanted to tell it. And I didn’t want to want one year for it to come out.”

Wang told Evans that he has launched a new production banner designed to help showcase budding directors. A few years ago, one of Wang’s shorts caught the attention of executives at Sony Pictures. But Wang’s path has kept him close to YouTube. His list of projects includes a filmmaking competition series “Absolute Cinema,” which puts contestants through a seven-day filmmaking boot camp with Wang in the hopes of winning $100,000 to fund their films. He’s also spearheading the ambitious “Extraction 14″ which is a murder mystery that challenges viewers to be the first to solve the case.

Wang emphasized that YouTube is the platform of the future because it allows for directors to build a fan base that moves with them.

“We want to build experiences, not just films,” Wang said. “We want people to watch the film and then talk about it on Reddit, on Discord. There’s a lot more opportunities for audience engagement.”

Among the trailers showcased was the latest movie parody from multihyphenate comedian Grace Reiter. “Twilight (but better),” coming in February, is a follow on the success of her “Hunger Games (but better)” parody released in 2025.

Here is a rundown of all of the projects unveiled and content descriptions provided by YouTube:

Chef Nick DiGiovanni, the creator behind the world’s largest food YouTube channel, launches Chef (@Chef) — a new series from his production company, Knife Drop Studios. After reaching billions of viewers through his global culinary adventures (@NickDiGiovanni) and step-by-step recipes (@NicksKitchen), DiGiovanni is now turning the camera solely toward fellow professional chefs, offering an unfiltered look inside kitchens around the globe. From Michelin-starred restaurants and hidden street food gems showcased in full-length episodes, to POV recipes in short-form episodes, Chef puts viewers in the shoes of the people behind the scenes, offering a unique, captivating perspective for everyone from the culinary obsessed to the simply curious. You can view the first full-length episode, “Inside the World’s #1 Pizza Restaurant” now, only on YouTube, along with the first short-form POV episode, “Butter Chicken Shawarma.” New short-form POV episodes will debut daily across Nick’s newly-launched @Chef channel with full-length episodes rolling out throughout 2026-27 exclusively on YouTube.

Morgan Jay | “Music Therapy with Morgan Jay” | New series premieres November 18

Music Therapy is an original YouTube series developed and produced by viral comedian and musician Morgan Jay and Ty Stafford of Austin-based creative agency Hello Party, financed by Live Nation Studios. In each episode, Jay plays musical therapist to a celebrity guest, blending unscripted conversation and improv with live songwriting before the pair close with an original song they create together. The result lands somewhere between a talk show, a comedy special and a live concert. The first season features Kam Patterson, Ron Funches, Mara Wilson, Brennan Lee Mulligan, Yung Gravy, Matt Friend, Abbey Romeo, Mark Smalls, Tony Jefferson, ASHBY, Josh Johnson and Mandal Mann. Music Therapy was created by Morgan Jay and Ty Stafford (Hello Party) and is directed by Stafford. Brooke Schley serves as showrunner and co-writes the series with Jay. Executive producers are Graham Sicalowski, Robin Rapino, Stella Bulochnikov, Brooke Schley and Matt Sadeghian. Watch the series trailer here.

Emma Chamberlain | “What Are You Waiting For?” | New series premieres early 2027

Emma Chamberlain doesn’t like waiting in line — and wants to understand more about people who do. In this new ongoing series, what starts as an investigation of New York City’s most frequented lines unspools into a thoughtful and expansive meditation on subcultures, fandoms, and what makes our modern world tick. At once whimsical, profound and provocative, and underpinned by Emma’s singular POV, What Are You Waiting For? is a hilarious cultural commentary that not only asks what people are waiting for — but why. The series is executive produced by two-time Emmy and Golden Globe winner Joe Lewis, with Amplify Pictures (Fleabag, 100 Foot Wave) serving as the co-studio and production company.

Grace Reiter | “Twilight (but better)” | New parody film premieres February 18

Comedian, writer, and actor Grace Reiter (Saturday Night Live) is expanding her comedic universe with Twilight (but better), her second unscripted parody film. Reiter first made waves with her viral sketches and The Hunger Games (but better), which launched on YouTube in November 2025 and has garnered more than 18 million views. Now, alongside her work on SNL and her Netflix series Minimum Wage, Reiter brings her signature blend of spontaneous comedy, sharp satire, and irreverent humor to one of the most iconic franchises of a generation. Twilight (but better) is produced by But Better Productions. Grace Reiter serves as director with Andie Schlenker and Anderson Appleby as producers, and Cindy Schlenker as co-producer. Watch the teaser trailer here.

“Response” | New short film premieres early 2027 in collaboration across the Wesley Wang and Jason RodeloYouTube channels

In this new sci-fi horror short film, a routine welfare check at a remote desert preserve turns terrifying when four park rangers discover an otherworldly presence inside a woman’s tent, leaving the youngest ranger separated from his own body. Response is produced by Wesley Wang Media with Jason Rodelo serving as director and writer.

“Extract 14” | New interactive series premieres spring 2027

A new six-episode, interactive murder-mystery series that puts viewers in the role of detective, with $100,000 on the line for the first person to crack the case. Told through found footage, digital breadcrumbs and hidden clues woven throughout each episode, the mystery unfolds over six weeks on YouTube, challenging viewers to pause, rewind, decode and collaborate to uncover the truth. The first viewer to correctly solve the case wins $100,000! Extract 14 is directed and written by Wesley Wang and produced by Wesley Wang Media.

“Absolute Cinema” | New competition series premieres spring 2027

A new series where ten aspiring filmmakers, chosen from thousands of internet strangers, are brought to New York for an immersive, seven-day filmmaking bootcamp where they’ll compete for $100,000 to bring their dream movie to life. Guided by filmmaker Wesley Wang and a panel of Hollywood industry experts, contestants will take on every stage of the filmmaking process, culminating in a red carpet premiere where the viewers get the final say in choosing the grand prize winner. Absolute Cinema is produced by Wesley Wang Media.

Aryna Sabalenka | “Aryna’s Arena: Unfiltered” | New series premiering spring 2027

She’s the most dominant tennis player on the planet; and the world can’t look away. Aryna Sabalenka has spent years letting us watch her win. Now cameras follow her everywhere press passes don’t go. A Gucci ambassador designing her own $250K diamond necklace, a bride-to-be planning a wedding while living out of a suitcase for 300+ days a year, a daughter flying home to Belarus between slams, Aryna is building an empire in real time with the whole world watching. From UltraBoom Media, the creators of OFF DAY, a team led by Producers and Creatives from Anthony Bourdain: Parts Unknown, Aryna’s Arena: Unfiltered is an evolution and expansion of Aryna Sabalenka’s YouTube series (Aryna’s Arena). It is a docu-reality series that lives inside her world, on the court, on the road, and everywhere in between, capturing the brand meetings and dress fittings, the late nights with her fiance, and the quiet hotel room moments where she finally gets to just be herself. Raw and glamorous with the playfulness that is uniquely Aryna, this is an unfiltered voyeuristic portrait of power, identity, chaos, and the chosen family around her that makes it all possible.

Track Star* | “Track Star Tonight” | New series now streaming, with new episodes rolling out this winter

Track Star* is a music-driven digital show hosted by Jack Coyne that brings artists and fans together. Expanding on its popular on-the-street game and Track Star Podcast, Track Star Tonight is a new, intimate live performance series featuring emerging and established artists in front of a small audience, blending live music, storytelling, and artist discovery. Following performances from artists including Remi Wolf, Kings of Leon, The Womack Sisters, Medium Build, and Florence Road, the series will continue to release new performances and collaborations through the end of 2026 and into 2027. Track Star* is produced by Public Opinion Corporation and created by Jack Coyne, Kieran Coyne and Henry Kornaros.

On November 25, Happy Hour will air a special Thanksgiving episode featuring past guests and friends of Ina, including Bobby Flay, Seth Meyers, Julia Louis-Dreyfus, Jennifer Garner and, of course, Jeffrey. Ina and her friends will answer listener questions, share Thanksgiving memories, and offer hosting tips to get you inspired before the big day. Happy Hour is part of the Vox Media Podcast Network. Ina Garten serves as host with Jenna Weiss Berman, Ali Jaffe Ramis and Deborah Davis as executive producers. Sam Eckholm | Access Granted: “Flying with the Royal Air Force Red Arrows” | New episode premieres November 27

Access Granted is a cinematic documentary series that takes cameras to the world’s most secretive places, putting audiences inside demanding missions that have never been seen before. In an upcoming episode, host Sam Eckholm joins the Royal Air Force Red Arrows as Britain’s legendary aerial display team crosses the Atlantic to celebrate America’s 250th birthday, going behind the scenes with the pilots, the engineers, and the crew. Then he straps into the back seat of the lead jet and flies the full display, flying hundreds of miles per hour just a few feet from six other aircraft. Sam Eckholm is the creator and host of Access Granted with JoJo Phillips serving as executive producer and Swayne Martin as producer.

Created with Jon Youshaei is the award-winning docuseries where Jon goes behind the scenes with today’s biggest stars during their biggest moments. Scott Evans | “House Guest Vacay” | New series premieres January 8

Scott Evans is packing his bags and taking House Guest on the road with House Guest Vacay. His first stop in Lagos, Nigeria premieres January 8 exclusively on YouTube. House Guest Vacay is produced by Summer Break Studios and created and hosted by Scott Evans.

Phil Rosenthal | “Somebody Feed Phil” | Season 9 premieres spring 2027

Opa! Season 9 of Somebody Feed Phil kicks off in a destination long at the top of Phil’s wish list: Greece. On the island of Sifnos, globetrotting foodie Phil Rosenthal finally gets his first taste of the country, with sun-soaked tavernas, fresh-caught seafood and a warm welcome from new friends. Lilly Singh | “Eligible Aunties” | New series premieres 2027

Eligible Aunties is a warm, funny, character-driven unscripted docu-follow series hosted by Lilly Singh that trades shallow villa romances for mature women with real emotional intelligence and emotional baggage. With Singh acting as the ultimate cheerleader and trusted confidante, the show follows a diverse cast of older single women navigating modern dating apps, new romance rules, and their meddling families. Directly serving a rapidly growing demographic of older unmarried adults craving real connections, the series highlights the joy and chaos of putting yourself back on the market. Ultimately, it proves that while finding love doesn’t get easier with age, it definitely gets more interesting. Eligible Aunties is produced by Unicorn Island Productions and created and hosted by Lilly Singh.
