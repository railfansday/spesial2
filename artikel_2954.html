The Billionaire Winklevoss Twins Predict That Zcash Could Hit $5,000 Within the Next 5 Years. Here's Why They're Wrong.

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

By Lyle Daly – Nov 19, 2026 at 2:45PM EST

Zcash has been one of the crypto market's biggest success stories in a turbulent 2026, but it's best to take this prediction with a healthy dose of skepticism.

Zcash trades at about $930 as of Nov. 18, meaning it would need to grow roughly 40% per year to reach $5,000 within five years. For a cryptocurrency, that's certainly within the realm of possibility, but it faces some serious obstacles.

In May, a security researcher found a vulnerability in Zcash's Orchard shielded pool that could have allowed attackers to create undetectable counterfeit ZEC. The vulnerability has been fixed, but it went undetected for four years. Since the pool shields transactions by design, it's impossible to determine if anyone exploited the flaw.
