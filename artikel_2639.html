Deion Sanders' press conference takeaways before Cincinnati matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Colorado is riding high following its biggest victory of the Deion Sanders era. However, the Buffaloes still have their back against the wall as they prepare for Saturday's road matchup against Cincinnati.

The Buffs (4-6, 2-5 Big 12) kept their bowl hopes alive with an impressive 42-32 upset of No. 14 Houston, fueled by an explosive rushing attack and timely defensive plays. Colorado now needs to win its final two regular-season games to reach bowl eligibility.

At his weekly press conference Tuesday, Sanders expressed confidence in his team's mentality and said the performance against Houston was what they expected all season.

Sanders also provided updates on several injured players, confirmed Richard Young and JaQuail Smith as Colorado's top two running backs, and discussed his recent approach at practice, which has included taking reps at defensive back.

Here are the biggest takeaways from Sanders' press conference ahead of Colorado's matchup with Cincinnati.

Scudero has been out with a foot injury since September, and Mitchell has missed the past two games. Sanders said that he "wouldn't bet on them being available."

Colorado is coming off a performance in which they rushed for nearly 250 yards, thanks in large part to explosive runs from Richard Young and JaQuail Smith. Sanders confirmed Young and Smith are the top two backs heading into this week's game against Cincinnati.

"Those two have the hot hand right now," Sanders said.

The performance against Houston was clearly the best of the season, and when asked Tuesday whether it was expected, he said it was.

"For the kids, they've been expecting that type of performance from one another all year long. Offensively, defensively and special teams, as well as coaching. It all came together, and I'm glad the true fans got an opportunity to see the possibilities of this group."

After the biggest win in the Sanders era, Sanders is loving what he is seeing from this week in terms of mentality.

"I love waht I see on a daily basis," Sanders said. "These are some really good kids, and they're fighting towards the end. We harp on: how do you respond to adversity?...We really try to prepare them on how to get through the trials and tribulations."

Sanders came into the press conference dressed in a No. 21 practice jersey decked out with gloves and sweats. For the second week in a row, Sanders took reps at defensive back, which he says gives his team a big boost.

"I worked today. Back hurts, thighs hurt, eveything's hurting," Sanders said. "I may go in cleats sometime this week. The kids like it when I am out there because Im coming out there with energy, I make sound effects when I make tackles. They love the sound effects."
