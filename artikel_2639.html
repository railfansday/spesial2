Lady Stars stay unbeaten

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Western Boone girls basketball team improved to 3-0 on Tuesday night with a 67-45 win at Covington.

Western Boone shot 51% from the field, while forcing 31 Covington turnovers.

Ady Nicley led Western Boone, scoring 23 points and adding five steals and three assists.

Ally Lewis had 16 points, 12 rebounds and seven steals. C.J. Paris added a career-high 14 points, three rebounds and two assists.

Mickey Burns contributed 6 points on an efficient 3-of-4 shooting performance while grabbing six rebounds, four assists and two steals.

Makenna Harrison added six points and three steals..

WeBo heads to Crawfordsville on Friday to play in the Sugar Creek Classic.
