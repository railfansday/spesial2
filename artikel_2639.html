Late touchdown salvages forgettable night for DK Metcalf

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Pittsburgh Steelers WR DK Metcalf had two receptions, 54 yards, and a touchdown against the Cincinnati Bengals in Week 10. He was targeted six times.

Why It Matters: Metcalf couldn't get on the same page with Aaron Rodgers until the outcome of the game was decided with a 32-yard touchdown grab that saved Pittsburgh's top target for an abysmal night statistically. Seeing the Steelers' passing game utterly fail to get anything done for the first 45 minutes of the game was discouraging to say the least, however. Over his last three games, Metcalf has just five combined catches despite being targeted 18 times. That last number provides hope as it shows he's still a focal point of the offense, but right now Metcalf is barely keeping his head above water as a dicey WR3.
