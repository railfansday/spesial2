Travis Kelce injury update as Chiefs TE exits game vs Falcons

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Kansas City Chiefs tight end Travis Kelce suffered an apparent injury on his first catch of his team's Week 10 game against the Atlanta Falcons.

Kelce's injury occurred as he was tackled by DeMarcco Hellams at the end of a 23-yard catch-and-run. The safety rolled up the 37-year-old tight end, and Kelce was slow getting up after the play.

Kelce was able to get off the field under his own power, but he walked toward the sideline with a limp.

Kelce did not return across the remainder of the Chiefs' first drive, which ended when Patrick Mahomes threw a touchdown to running back Emmett Johnson. He was later seen walking to the locker room with members of Kansas City's training staff.

Here's more to know about Kelce's injury.

The Chiefs announced Kelce is questionable to return to Sunday's game against the Falcons because of an ankle injury.

Kelce played two snaps and recorded one catch for 23 yards before exiting the game. It marked his 200th consecutive game with at least one reception.

Noah Gray is entrenched as Kelce's top backup. The six-year veteran has spent his entire career with the Chiefs since being a fifth-round pick in the 2021 NFL Draft.

Gray entered Week 10 having totaled 15 catches for 184 yards and a touchdown working as the Chiefs' No. 2 tight end this season. He has played in 91 career games with 40 starts and has 139 catches for 1,439 yards and 10 touchdowns.
