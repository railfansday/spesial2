Patriots fans’ confidence in team nears season low

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Everything seemingly went right for the New England Patriots in 2025.

In Mike Vrabel's first season as head coach, Drake Maye emerged as an MVP candidate with a significant Year 2 leap, while nearly every major offseason addition, such as Milton Williams and Stefon Diggs, played a key role in the Patriots' run to Super Bowl LX.

This season, however, has been a different story.

With plenty of new faces on both sides of the ball and a league-high number of injuries, the 2026 Patriots season has felt more like a grind. The season has been defined by plenty of ups and downs, from Maye's slow start highlighted by a blowout loss in Jacksonville to an upset win in Buffalo that kickstarted a winning streak.

That win streak was followed by the Patriots dropping two games against NFC North opponents, including a troubling 45-27 loss at home to the Green Bay Packers.

Those peaks and valleys have been reflected in Patriots fans' confidence in the team, which nearly hit a season low this week following the loss to Green Bay, which marked the worst defeat of the Vrabel era.

Despite fan confidence taking a major dip this past week, the 5-4 Patriots still find themselves in the AFC playoff picture and as contenders.

"We're 5-4. I think there's going to be a lot of contending teams that are 5-4. I think that's the nature of the National Football League," Vrabel said. "We've got to draw a line here. We've got to think that we're 0-0, and we've got to start putting ourselves in position to play meaningful games in January or in the middle of January."

According to Patriots fans, the team's ceiling is viewed best just as a playoff contender. In the latest SB Nation Reacts survey, 54% of participants said that is how they see the team compared to just 3% who still view them as Super Bowl contenders. The remaining 43% of voters don't view New England as a contender at all.
