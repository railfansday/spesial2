Among rivalry games, Territorial Cup hits differently for Dillingham

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Arizona State football coach Kenny Dillingham has been part of a lot of rivalries as a coach. When he was an assistant at Oregon under Dan Lanning, there was the "Cival War" game against Oregon State. At Florida State working with Mike Norvell, there were games against Florida and Miami. He was at Auburn, an SEC school with no shortgage of rivals.

The Territorial Cup showdown between Arizona and Arizona State is different. Of course, being a native of the Valley, that alone makes it hit differently for Dillingham.

"Both sides live next to each other," Dillingham said. "This rivalry, more than most that I have been a part of, have houses where you have a family where your whole family went to school A, or your whole family went to school B."

"We have a lot of that, but here in Phoenix we also have a lot of this person went here, this person went here. Or my neighbor went here, and I went here. Other places I worked at, it was almost like pockets of different, like this town roots for school A, this town roots for school B. Boom. If you drive through this town, make sure you have nothing that shows your logo, they may pull you over."

Dillingham said in Phoenix, seemingly everyone went to one school or the other.

"I think that's what makes the rivalry unique is that everybody co-mingles, and it's on you every day because you're next door neighbor is on the other side of it," he said. "Or somebody in your house is on the other side of it."

The 100th edition of the Territorial Cup will take place on Nov. 28 at 7 p.m. at Casino Del Sol Stadium. Both schools went to overtime in their last outing, with ASU (6-5, 4-4) falling to Oklahoma State while No. 19 Arizona (7-3, 5-3) defeated Kansas State 35-28.

This will be the fifth game this season in which the Sun Devils have faced a nationally ranked opponent. The game will air on FOX.

There has been a big turnover on the ASU roster, so this will be the first Territorial Cup for many Sun Devils, including the backfield tandem of quarterback Jake Fette and running back Cardae Mack, both true freshmen, as well as highly touted wide receivers Omarion Miller and Reed Harris, both transfer portal acquisitions.

Dillingham said he will have assistant defensive line coach Vince Amey address the team. Amey has been part of the rivalry as both a player and a coach.

Dillingham said he doesn't recall a particularly memorial Territorial Cup game he watched as a youngster. For him, it was always more about the time of year.

"I don't have a memory of when I was 9, or 10 or 8. I just remember flags flying," he said. "I remember people talking crap. I remember Thanksgiving. Football. Thursday morning everybody talking about the game. I remember people wearing their jerseys, I remember all that. I remember it being a thing. I remember it coinciding with Thanksgiving.

"So that's what I remember the most. It was Thanksgiving, and it was the Territorial Cup. It's what you did at that time. It didn't matter where I traveled or what school I coached at, I didn't get to watch every game when I was going around the country coaching but I always watched this game. Doesn't matter what my schedule was, I was going to watch it."

This game will also mark the ninth game in as many weeks for the Sun Devils, whose last Saturday off was Sept. 26, the week after the game against Kansas in London.

The Sun Devils had several players injured against Oklahoma State, who won't be available this week. The back end of the secondary was hit particularly hard, which was apparent went Adrian "Boogie" Wilson went out due to a targeting call.

Dillingham said he will adjust practice this week to help his team get to the finish line, although a bowl game will come later on.

"We're going to practice a bit different this week. Nine in a row," he said. "Maybe I didn't pull off enough, fast enough, definitely the stretch caught us there, like whoa, whoa, whoa. We gotta tailor back a little bit. This is the biggest game of the year. We've got to have our guys as fresh as possible to be able to go play it."
