Cowboys stretch lead over Titans to 20-3

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Cowboys have gotten their act together, which was hardly a guarantee despite facing the one-win Titans.

Dallas has scored three touchdowns in three drives. The latest — an eight-yard pass from quarterback Dak Prescott to receiver CeeDee Lamb on third and goal — stretched the margin to 20-3.

Lamb has six catches for 79 yards. That puts him above 1,000 yard for the season. It also makes him the first player in franchise history to have six straight 1,000-yard seasons.

The score was also Lamb's 50th career touchdown catch. He got there in his 106th career game. Per the Cowboys, Lreached 50 career touchdown receptions in his 106th game. Lamb is the third-fastest player in team history to reach 50 touchdown catches, behind Bob Hayes (66 games) and Dez Bryant (70 games).

The Titans have lost safety Tony Adams for the game with an illness. Titans fans watching the game can relate.
