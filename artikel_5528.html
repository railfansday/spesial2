Mitchell Expands Intelligent Estimating to Collision Repair Market

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Crash Champions becomes the first multi-site operator (MSO) to make the new technology available across its U.S. network

SAN DIEGO, Nov. 3, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Mitchell, a leader in the development of innovative auto physical damage technology solutions, today announced the release of Mitchell Intelligent Estimating for U.S. and Canadian MSOs and independent collision centers. Already relied upon by North America's leading auto insurers, the solution uses vehicle-damage photos to automatically generate a preliminary estimate in minutes, helping to streamline the appraisal process and improve estimator productivity and consistency.

Crash Champions, one of the nation's fastest-growing, founder-led collision repair providers, is the first MSO to make Mitchell Intelligent Estimating available across its U.S. network of more than 650 collision centers in 38 states and Washington, D.C. The successful deployment builds on the two companies' continued collaboration to reimagine the vehicle claims process and deliver a premier customer experience using the latest technology.

"Mitchell Intelligent Estimating helps to improve the customer experience by connecting our Service Advisors to the technology they need to help them build best-in-class estimates and repair plans," said Mark Miller, chief information officer of Crash Champions. "After testing the solution and receiving real-world feedback from our team members, we decided to quickly expand its use across our entire network. As vehicle technology continues to innovate, we're committed to investing in the latest solutions like Mitchell's, which assist our team members in delivering a consistent, high-quality repair experience to every Crash Champions customer and business partner."

Available as an add-on to Mitchell Cloud Estimating, Mitchell Intelligent Estimating combines AI with Mitchell's cloud ecosystem, open platform, intellectual property and data. From uploaded photos of vehicle damage, it makes it possible for an initial appraisal to be generated automatically that estimators can then review, edit and submit. "MSOs and independent collision centers are under increasing pressure to improve productivity while delivering a proper and safe repair," said Debbie Day, executive vice president and general manager of Mitchell. "Mitchell Intelligent Estimating was designed to address that challenge by automating and optimizing a key step of the repair process. Crash Champions has long been at the forefront of innovation in collision repair, and we are proud to support its continued commitment to advancing the industry through this nationwide deployment."
