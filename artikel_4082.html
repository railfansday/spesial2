Team Cherry Announces A Silksong Presentation Showing Off Sea Of Sorrow

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

While the long-awaited release of Hollow Knight: Silksong received glowing reviews and record-breaking player counts at launch, the success has only added even more excitement over when the officially announced DLC would be released. While fans have had nothing but speculation to go off of since the free Sea of Sorrow expansion was teased last year, it seems to be coming sooner than expected thanks to its developer's latest reveal.

Shared on the official Team Cherry YouTube channel, Silksong's developers have scheduled a stream premiering on November 27 at 7:00 AM PST, which promises to provide "A special presentation with a bunch of exciting announcements, and an update on Hollow Knight: Silksong - Sea of Sorrow!" as noted by Wario64 on X, who helped share the news.

While there have been plenty of rumors that a Silksong Sea of Sorrow release would be shadowdropped sometime this winter, the latest official announcement is the first time Team Cherry has posted about the game since the DLC was announced back in December of last year; it's not hard to see why fans are already losing their minds over what the stream could entail.

Combined with the fact that the original Hollow Knight: Silksong had its release date announced merely two weeks before the game actually dropped, the presentation also has several fans convinced that we could very well get a full Sea of Sorrow launch within the next few weeks, after most had given up hope we'd get an announcement before the end of 2026.

Silksong fans have joined No Man's Sky fans in wanting to give their favorite studios more money.

Although it's hard to say whether or not we'll actually get to play Silksong's highly-anticipated story expansion so soon, the amount of hype in both the live chat and reactions online shows that there is still plenty of love for Team Cherry's Hollow Knight follow-up to help bring it back into mainstream popularity again.
