Ex-West Brom boss rushed to hospital

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Former West Brom boss Pepe Mel was rushed to hospital in his native Spain during the weekend after he suffered a stroke.

The Baggies have released a statement on social media sending their sympathies with the 63-year-old, who was in charge at the Hawthorns from January 2014 to May 2014.

The experienced manager is currently out of work, but has worked in Spain, Greece, England and Morocco.

His most recent managerial stint came with IR Tangier, where he oversaw seven games in charge before departing in March 2026.

Mel began his coaching career in 1999 with Coslada, and has worked with clubs such as Getafe, Alaves, Real Betis and Deportivo de La Coruña.

West Brom were his only foray into English football, taking over the team after Steve Clarke’s dismissal midway through the 2013/14 campaign, ultimately overseeing a 17th place finish in the Premier League table.

West Brom have posted a message on their X account wishing Mel a speedy recovery after it was reported that he was rushed to hospital at the weekend with a stroke.

The 63-year-old underwent successful emergency surgery, and the club have told him that they will be with him every step of the way in his process of returning to good health.

“Everyone at West Bromwich Albion wishes former boss Pepe Mel a speedy recovery following surgery, after suffering a stroke,” wrote the club.

Mel is believed to be in intensive care in the aftermath of his emergency surgery, where he will begin his road to recovery.

The Spaniard was appointed at West Brom in January 2014 after Clarke oversaw a challenging first half of the campaign.

However, he led the Baggies to just three wins from 17 fixtures, which led to his departure at the end of that season after keeping the club in the Premier League for another year.

He was succeeded by Alan Irvine, with Mel going on to return to his former club Betis in December 2014.

James Morrison’s West Brom side are currently fourth in the Championship table after the first 13 games of the campaign.

Albion have won seven, drawn three and lost three, leaving them four points adrift of leaders Middlesbrough.

The Midlands outfit are also four points clear of ninth place QPR, and are level with rivals Wolves but below them in the standings due to an inferior goal difference.

Next up for Morrison’s side is a home clash against Southampton on 3 November in an 8pm kick-off.

It was a nice message from West Brom to wish Mel well in his recovery from a stroke, particularly as he only spent a few months at the club.

The football world has joined them in hoping that he can get back to full health in the near future.

Mel is particularly beloved at Betis, where he not only enjoyed two managerial stints but he also played as a forward before going into a coaching career.

Everyone will be hoping that a positive update on Mel’s progress follows soon, and that he could even potentially return to management in the future, if he chooses.
