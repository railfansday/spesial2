Tenerife Boss Reacts After Jesus Owono Hit With Maximum Ban Following Jesus de Miguel Death

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Tenerife manager Alvaro Cervera has responded to the sanction given to FC Andorra goalkeeper Jesus Owono, who was sent off in an incident that eventually led to Jesus de Miguel's death earlier this month.

On November 2, Tenerife met Andorra in a Spanish Segunda Division match in the Canary Islands. With the game poised at 2-2 heading into stoppage time, Owono and De Miguel were involved in a collision which caused the latter to suffer a serious injury.

The Spanish striker was attempting to latch onto a long ball when he was met with Owono's imposing frame. The Tenerife forward was rushed off the pitch and quickly transported to hospital, but was unable to be saved, with his death confirmed the following morning. The match was abandoned.

Owono is expected to avoid legal action, despite the Spanish police opening an investigation into involuntary manslaughter. However, the shot-stopper has been handed a 12-match suspension for his role in the tragic event.

Whether the number one actually serves this suspension remains to be seen, as some reports suggest that Owono is considering hanging up his boots after being involved in the traumatic incident, with the player said to be struggling to come to terms with what happened.

The footballing world paid tribute to De Miguel in the week that followed, with stadiums across Europe holding a minute's silence to pay their respects.

Meanwhile, the clubs, players and coaches involved in the incident have had to continue with their normal routines. Tenerife's game against Granada last Saturday was postponed, but their encounter at home to AD Ceuta FC on Sunday will go ahead as planned.

Jesus Owono has broken his silence after the tragic passing of CD Tenerife forward Jesus de Miguel.

For FC Andorra, manager Carles Manso will be without his first-choice goalkeeper for at least 12 matches.

Many have questioned whether this punishment for Owono is sufficient given what happened, and Tenerife head coach Alvaro Cervera has now had his say on the matter.

Cevera told the media during his press conference ahead of the clash with Ceuta:

"I think there are rules they have to follow, and what has happened isn’t covered by those rules.

“The rules really don’t give them any other option, and we can’t say any more."

While many have called for a harsher penalty for Owono, many more have come to the Equatorial Guinea international's defence, arguing that while his challenge was reckless, there was no malice or intention to cause De Miguel harm.
