Sources: Liverpool 'have the money' to pull off £100m signing in January

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Liverpool have got the funds available to go out and spend big in the January transfer window, sources have told Football Insider.

The Reds have not been shy to spend big in recent windows, with the likes of Florian Wirtz, Alexander Isak and Bradley Barcola all signed for fees in excess of £100million.

Those players have had varying levels of success at Anfield, but their inconsistencies may not be enough to put the Reds off of spending big on another huge transfer.

As we revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Liverpool would be more than capable of spending over £100million if the right player emerged.

This comes as Iraola targets major improvements in his squad ahead of the January transfer window, with key areas of the squad still in need of strengthening.

While is it doubtful that Liverpool will make a £100million signing mid-way through the season given the nature of those deals, plans could be put in place for a long-term target.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider on 13 November that concerns about Barcola's slow start to his Liverpool career could change Liverpool's plans.

However, with some big-name targets on their radar including Bayern Munich star Jamal Musiala, Liverpool have not ruled out another big-spending spell next summer.

Should they finish in a high Premier League position, and go deep into this season's Champions League, it will only serve to boost the budget available to Iraola.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, Callum O'Connell suggested: "Liverpool are going to be interesting to look at financially, because they've spent over £100million on a few different players.

"They went and got Isak, Wirtz, Barcola and they've all had differing degrees of success, so that's something they must be aware of.

"It's not that they can't afford to spend that again, but I doubt they're going to be willing to go out and spend that sort of money again on another big-name player."

Pete O'Rourke then explained: "I would be surprised if they did go and spend that sort of money, but they do have the money to do that if they wanted to.

"The revenues are good for Liverpool, the Champions League will be key to that as well this season if they continue to do well there.

"If there was a player who came up for that sort of price, who they thought would be good enough to improve their squad, there's no reason why they couldn't pay the money."

With the news that Liverpool would be financially ready to spend big on another new addition in 2027, recent transfer rumours are starting to make more sense.

Links with a move for Bayern Munich's Musiala are the latest which could force the Reds to spend big, while there are key areas which they may be tempted to spend on.

Brown told Football Insider last month that Liverpool's desire to sign a new central midfielder could see them move for Real Madrid star Federico Valverde before long.

The Uruguay international is a crucial part of Jose Mourinho's plans in Madrid, and is largely viewed as unattainable, but a £100million fee could change that stance.

The 28-year-old has established himself as one of the most impressive, consistent and reliable midfielders in world football, and is firmly on the radar at Anfield.
