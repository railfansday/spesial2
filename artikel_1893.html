Crystal Palace 'will be relegated' if Thomas Frank does not make overhaul - ex-chief scout

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Thomas Frank has inheirted a Crystal Palace side in freefall and must make major changes if he is to save them from relegation.

The boss got off to the worst possible start as his side suffered a late 3-2 defeat away at Coventry City, which once again exposed the weaknesses in their defence.

While Frank cannot be entirely blamed for the defeat, having had less than a week to train with his squad, it echoed the same issues that Palace have faced all season.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Frank has to make drastic changes if he is to turn Crystal Palace's miserable start to the season around.

The Eagles have now conceded the second-most goals of any club in Europe's top five league, second only to Man United, and leaked three again at the weekend.

That back line must immediately be addressed, whether that is with tactical tweaks on the pitch, a change of formation, or through recruitment in January.

Brown warned Football Insider on 17 November that Frank may have been set up to fail from the start at Crystal Palace after their failures in the transfer window.

The new boss has been presented with a squad which, particularly in defence, many beleive is not up to scratch for the level they are aiming to compete at.

Frank has praised his side's attacking profficiency, but that has never been an issue for Palace this season, and there are other areas which are in desperate need of attention.

"Crystal Palace are in a desperate situation and it's very hard to get out of it," Brown told Football Insider.

"For any club, when you're bottom of the table after this many games, it can feel impossible to turn things around no matter how well you play or how hard you work.

"If things continue the way they have been in defence, Crystal Palace will be relegated, and that shouldn't be a difficult thing to figure out.

"So Thomas Frank is going to have to make some major changes in that back line, he needs to figure out who his best players are, and maybe change the shape as well.

"It's not just a case of being a bit more organised or cutting out mistakes, because their problems are deeper than that, and the manager has got to do something about it.

"So like I say, a change of shape or extra work on the training ground, even bringing in new players in January, he has got to try everything to make them more solid.

"Because Palace are good going forward, they have plenty of goals in them and Frank has seen that, but if they keep leaking goals, there's no way they're going to start winning games."

Frank quickly emerged as the leading contender for the Crystal Palace job after Pierre Sage's dismissal, and the club hope he will be the man to reverse their fortunes.

Having sunk to the bottom of the Premier League table, the new boss is expected to turn things around, and they hope progress will be made in the coming weeks.

Football Insider revealed on 15 November that Frank's Premier League experience and remarkable work with Brentford made him the top choice.

The boss is also viewed as a good motivator, which is something Palace are searching for to improve their morale having dropped to the bottom of the Premier League table.

Crystal Palace hope Frank's reign will reflect the work he did with Brentford rather than with Tottenham, where he struggled, and believe he is the right man for the job.
