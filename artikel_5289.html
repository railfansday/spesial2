Cardano Gains a Perpetual Futures DEX as Atlas Goes Live

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Cardano is showing strong momentum in its DeFi ecosystem. This comes as a recognized trading platform, Atlas, launched its decentralized perpetual DEX on Cardano today, with analyst Cheeky Crypto pointing to ADA’s resilient on-chain network.

Atlas is a perpetual trading DEX platform that allows traders to open leveraged short or long positions using stablecoin collateral. Its debut on Cardano signals the ADA network’s continued ability to attract serious users into its on-chain ecosystem.

Reported today, Nov. 2, 2026, via official sources, Atlas announced the launch of its stablecoin perpetual DEX platform on the Cardano DeFi ecosystem. The rollout redefines how users interact with Atlas and Cardano’s huge interoperable DeFi ecosystem.

The debut marks a crucial advancement from Atlas’ specialized decentralized perpetual futures avenue into a broader trading hub where users can access various DeFi applications on the Cardano ecosystem. The expansion offers tangible benefits to Atlas users. This means Atlas traders can use stablecoin holdings as collateral for perpetual positions while optimizing capital use on Cardano’s DeFi.

The launch is a hardcore bullish signal for ADA, marking another institutional addition to the Cardano DeFi ecosystem. It signals user demand on Cardano DeFi as more projects expand on-chain and expose their users to innovative DeFi utilities.

In his podcast analysis on YouTube, analyst Cheeky Crypto further explained the drivers behind Cardano’s DeFi growth, including ADA price appreciation from 2017 to 2025. The analyst showed ADA’s incredible journey from its launch, when it traded at $0.02, to November 2025, when its value averaged $0.4154.

Cardano, launched on Sep. 29, 2017, saw one of the most outstanding monthly rises in November, according to the analyst’s revelation. With an upward trend from the first day of the month, the price historically rose by 58% from 2017 to 2025, as reported by the analyst.

Another milestone for ADA is the recent celebration of its ninth anniversary on Sep. 29, with an optimistic trend continuing this month, according to the analyst. This shows strong institutional demand and market confidence in the Cardano network.
