Aston Villa could see Unai Emery deal broken by ‘blockbuster offer’ – Keith Wyness

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Aston Villa will still not be protected from a potential approach from Real Madrid once manager Unai Emery signs his new contract.

That is according to former Villa chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the imminent agreement only serves to protect the compensation fee that his former club could secure.

Recent reports have suggested that Emery is on the verge of sealing a new contract in the second city, after another bright run of form of late.

Wyness believes that the boss is ready to commit to the club for another cycle of three years, but that could still change if the Real Madrid job becomes available.

The 55-year-old has been linked with the Spanish giants of late, while there has also been reported interest from Barcelona in recent months.

The Real Madrid job is widely regarded as one of the biggest in world football, and there may ultimately be nothing Villa could do if Los Blancos came calling.

Villa’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Villa Park between 2016 and 2018 and now runs a consultancy business advising elite clubs – believes that Emery’s new deal will still contain “clauses” that could allow him to leave the club.

The Scottish businessman has no doubt that the imminent deal is a hugely positive move for Villa, but he is under no illusions that it may not protect Emery’s future that much.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider’s Inside Track podcast, Wyness outlined that Real Madrid will still provide a long-term concern.

“Look, all it does with a manager is protect the fee that you're going to get for him. If there is a decision made by the manager he wants to go, then there'll be clauses in the contract that will allow them to move,” he said.

“However, it does seem very positive that he has agreed to another cycle with Villa, and that's what it looks like, at least another cycle, so another two or three years.

“So it's good news, but as I say, look, we know in the game these days, managers can move. If Real Madrid were to come in with some blockbuster offer, who knows what could happen.”

Football Insider revealed earlier this week that Villa remain relaxed over the future of goalkeeper Zion Suzuki, despite interest in him emerging from both Real Madrid and PSG.

The Japan international only moved to Villa Park in the summer, but is already being tracked by almost all of Europe’s elite clubs.

Sources say that Villa are not prepared to sell Suzuki any time soon, and believe he has the potential to develop into one of the best goalkeepers in the world.

The former Parma ace has endured a mixed start to the life in the Premier League, with a few recent errors threatening to overshadow some eye-catching early displays.
