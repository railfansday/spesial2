Nottingham Forest forward Dan Ndoye was among few bright sparks for Switzerland as they drew 0-0 with Slovenia in the Nations League on Friday.

The visitors enjoyed much of the action in the second biggest city of Slovenia, Maribor, but could not find the breakthrough.

The winger enjoyed perhaps the biggest chances in a game that was devoid of real action and excitement.

Ndoye is always in strong form for Switzerland, scoring 12 goals and providing seven assists in the 42 caps since his debut in 2022.

But the 26-year-old has never managed to produce the same form for Nottingham Forest, and has got off to another slow start to the campaign.

With just two goals in his opening 11 appearances so far this season, Oliver Glasner will hope to see him return from international duty with some renewed confidence.

In a game that so desperately lacked spark and invention, Ndoye tried his best to provide it.

The winger took three shots and created two chances for his teammates - the joint-most of anybody on the pitch in both stats.

He also took more touches in the opposition box than anybody else, with seven, as he looked to find the all-important breakthrough.

Ndoye attempted four crosses into the box, was fouled once and won half his duels, getting his defensive work done as well.

The 26-year-old made a tackle, a clearance and four recoveries, in what will go down as a bright display.

Swiss outlet Watson rated Ndoye's showing as a four out of five, describing him as "largely inconspicuous", but adding that "the lack of support was noticeable".

For SRF, the winger scored four out of six stars from around 3800 user ratings.

Ndoye will play no part in Switzerland's second international game of the break, against Scotland on Monday.

Manager Murat Yakin has decided to release the winger back to Nottingham Forest as he experiments with other options.

Glasner will hope to use the extra time to work individually with the winger, who can be so dangerous if he finds his feet.

In Ndoye's form for Switzerland, Nottingham Forest have an example of what he can do, but they must find a way to translate that form to the City Ground.
