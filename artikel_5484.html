Metal fabricator NIC Global to cut 103 jobs near Seattle, move production to Eastern Washington

Tanggal: 2026-11-05


NIC Global plans to close its Seattle-area manufacturing plant and cut 103 jobs, saying work from nearby customers has declined and it hasn’t been able to replace it. Most production will move to Spokane, Wash., as the company sees increased demand for products supporting data centers growth in Eastern Washington. Read More
