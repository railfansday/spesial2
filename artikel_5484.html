Shopify stock sinks after earnings report as revenue missed expectations

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Shopify reported 32% revenue growth and $119 billion in gross merchandise volume for the quarter ended Sept. 30

Shopify $SHOP stock was headed for a sharp decline after the company reported 32% revenue growth for the quarter ended Sept. 30. Revenue missed expectations, according to MarketWatch.

Adjusted earnings per share came in at 49 cents, compared with 34 cents in the same quarter of the prior year. That topped the 47-cent consensus estimate from FactSet, according to MarketWatch.

Shopify said revenue grew 32% in constant currency and its free cash flow margin was 22%. Gross merchandise volume, or GMV, which measures the total dollar value of orders facilitated through the Shopify platform, reached $119 billion.

"We powered $119 billion in GMV, while continuing to build commerce into every surface, every agent, and increasingly, everywhere," Harley Finkelstein, president of Shopify, said in a statement.

For the fourth quarter, Shopify said it expects revenue to grow at a low-thirties percentage rate year over year. It expects gross profit dollars to grow at a high-twenties percentage rate and operating expenses to equal 29% to 30% of revenue. Stock-based compensation is projected at $155 million, and the free cash flow margin is expected to match the fourth quarter of 2025.

Shopify's management team will hold a conference call on Tuesday at 8:30 a.m. ET to discuss the results. A webcast will be available on the company's investor relations website.

Shopify in September partnered with Meta $META Platforms to let Meta's Muse AI agent browse Shopify-powered stores and complete purchases for users. The integration is to be built on Shop Pay, Shopify's one-tap checkout service.
