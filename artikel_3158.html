Why AMD Stock Is Down Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

AMD stock is getting hammered in a chip sell-off after Democrats' midterm wins, even though the company just reported a record quarter.

Advanced Micro Devices (AMD -15.27%) stock is down 15.1% as of 2:38 a.m. ET on Thursday, Nov. 5, after a midterm victory for Democrats set off a sell-off in chip stocks.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC -3.95%) and the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -5.06%) are down 3.9% and 5.0%, respectively.

Two days after the momentous election, investors are dumping chipmakers, worried that a Democratic Congress will clamp down on the AI industry. The risk to the data center build-out is actually greater at the state level: new governors in Texas, Ohio, and Georgia campaigned partly on data center moratoriums. These are key states for AI construction.

If these moratoriums are put in place, the AI capex flows that have fueled the extreme growth of AMD and other chipmakers could slow.

The iShares Semiconductor ETF (SOXX -10.34%) is down 7.5% so far today.

AMD reported its Q3 results on Tuesday afternoon, with sales up 45%, hitting $13.4 billion. Data Center revenue nearly doubled. The question investors are asking is whether the data centers that buy AMD's chips get built as fast as everyone planned. And if that happens, how does AMD keep growing at the pace investors have come to expect?
