<em>Only Murders in the Building</em> Season 6: Everything We Know So Far

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Though Only Murders in the Building wrapped its fifth season in October 2025, the Hulu hit is hardly slowing down. The series is heading toward its next chapter with awards-season momentum after earning Golden Globe nominations for Best Television Series – Musical or Comedy, as well as best performance nods for stars Selena Gomez, Steve Martin, and Martin Short.

The final episode of season 5, which ended with the suspicious death of Cinda Canning, left fans on a major cliffhanger that begs for a deeper investigation. Here’s everything we know so far about the future of the show.

Yes! The news came shortly after the season 5 finale premiered. According to The Hollywood Reporter, the forthcoming season will officially switch locations and be filmed in London. The previous season’s finale ended with the trio listening to a new Cinda Canning podcast set across the pond. That, along with Canning’s mysterious death at the end of the episode, may hint at why the group could travel to the U.K.

Selena Gomez previously stated that she hopes Only Murders continues for many seasons to come. “I really hope that I can just keep evolving Mabel as I get older, because I want to do this for a long time,” she told USA Today in September 2025.

Steve Martin also felt optimistic about a sixth season. “We believe we’re doing another season,” he said. “We think so. But you have to live season by season. And even if it was canceled tomorrow, we would have to say, ‘We had a great, great run. We loved what we were doing. It was a great way to spend five, six years.’”

In May 2026, Gomez shared that filming had officially begun when she reposted a photo from the show’s Instagram account to her Instagram Story. The image, showing Gomez alongside some of the cast members together in London, was captioned, “Season 6 🇬🇧 Day 1!”

Just like previous seasons, season 6 is confirmed to have 10 episodes.

Only Murders in the Building season 6 is taking place in London, as well as a select few countryside locations in England. In an August 2026 interview with Vulture, showrunner Josh Hoffman teased, “one of the more signature episodes is called ‘The Country House,’ and that sits in a very specific vibe and look, and quite a collection of characters.” He added, “We got about an hour outside of London…and into some of the stately homes.”

Season 5 concluded with Cinda Canning (Tina Fey) dropping dead in front of the Arconia, the same night that her new U.K.-set podcast The Girl With the Curls aired. Given the proximity, Charles, Oliver, and Mabel set out to solve her murder. “It was technically a murder in the building because she did it with her last gasp,” Hoffman told Vulture. “But the question is, what was going on with her? That is what our trio is off to investigate.”

Specific plot points will remain under wraps until its premiere, but Hoffman revealed that Charles’s Brazzos television show is getting a reboot in the U.K. “Martin Freeman will go toe-to-toe at some point with Charles as to who’s the better Brazzos,” he shared.

The show will also be taking place during the holiday season. “With the show airing at the top of December, it was impossible to avoid,” Hoffman added. “Within the story, we are moving towards the end of the year. The end of last season was fall, so we had to carry through on that.” The sixth season was shot in an uncharacteristically hot summer, but the show did its best to capture an English winter “that feels a bit rainy and a bit cloudy. There will be a bit of a lean into the holiday season by the end of it all.”

Selena Gomez, Steve Martin, and Martin Short have returned for another season. Season 5 featured newcomers Renée Zellweger and Logan Lerman, while Keegan-Michael Key was elevated to a recurring role. It’s not yet clear whether these supporting characters, along with Meryl Streep and Da’Vine Joy Randolph, will return for season 6, but we’ll stay tuned.

Season 6 has also added several notable new faces. In June 2026, Deadline reported that David Tennant, Nicola Coughlan, Jodie Whittaker, Jim Broadbent, Richard Ayoade, Adrian Lukis, and Kathryn Hunter had joined the cast in recurring roles. By August 2026, Deadline reported that Doctor Who star Peter Capaldi will also be joining the upcoming season—Whittaker, Tennant, and Broadbent have all had roles within the Doctor Who universe.

Olivia Colman was added to the cast days later, with Gomez, Streep, and Short announcing the news via an Instagram video. “We have had so many amazing guest stars for Only Murders in the Building,” Gomez said in the short clip, with Streep adding, “We’re just missing one.”

Short jokingly pops into the frame before making way for Colman to appear alongside the trio.

Colman’s character will be a writer, Hoffman revealed in the Vulture interview. “The U.K. is the home of Agatha Christie. This is where Cinda Canning might have met her maker in some way,” he said. “And all of them are following along in the tradition of the cozy murder mystery. Olivia is in that line as well as a memoirist.”

Jennifer Saunders will also have a role in the sixth season, with Hoffman telling Vulture that her character takes significant inspiration from Vivienne Westwood. “I had an incredible time many years ago working on a screenplay with Brian Grazer and Ron Howard on the life of Vivienne Westwood,” he said. “So I spent a lot of time here in London developing that screenplay with her.” Though that movie never materialized, Hoffman used his research to inform Saunders’s character, which will “lean into the fashion design world in great British style, with a tip of the hat to the great Vivienne.”

Queen Camilla was extremely close to having a role in Only Murders in the Building season 6, with Hoffman telling Vulture, “We almost got Camilla...I know it’s insane, but yes, there was a moment.”

The Queen Camilla cameo never materialized because of logistics, but Hoffman “did pursue it a little bit, until I realized it’s going to be a whole thing.” Hoffman isn’t ruling out Prince Harry and Meghan Markle cameos, though—“When is she traveling? We’re leaving in three days.” He added, “I wouldn’t rule anything out.”

Season 6 of Only Murders in the Building will arrive on December 8. Gomez and the rest of her castmates revealed that filming wrapped in an August 28 video shared to the show’s social media platforms.

Yes. First look photos arrived on August 25, and feature Gomez, Martin, and Short back in costume as Mabel, Charles, and Oliver in winter-approved wardrobe staples.

The trailer for Only Murders in the Building season 6 arrived on November 17. In the trailer, Mabel, Oliver, and Charles try to find out the truth about Cinda’s death after seeing her leave her hand on the gates of The Arconia in her final moments. “Cinda died dropping a hand on our gate,” Mabel says, to which Oliver adds, “She clearly wants our help.” After discovering that Cinda was in London just before her death, the trio travels across the pond to London with the hopes of finding out who did it.

The trio struggles with getting used to how life works in England. Simple things like drinking tea prove to be a challenge when Mabel suggests that Cinda could have died from a poisonous pot of tea. The suspects—aside from Gemma Joyce, Coughlan’s character—aren’t immediately clear, but there are a host of individuals who could be guilty of the crime as the first glimpses of Streep, Ashley, Colman, and more characters are revealed in the trailer.
